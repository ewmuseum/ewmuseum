Muffled cheers imply that the show is still going on, but
4M will not be around for the end of it. 4M, who's visible breath seems to provide a window to the steaming inside of him, stalks to his car with duffel bag in hand. As he slams the bag down on top of the trunk, he notices a presense, and addresses it:]
4M: Good, there's a camera here! Somehow 4M knew there would be! Let's start this right now by addressing what you are all thinking. The impossible has happened. Yes, "Mr. Majestyk", Mr. Heart and Soul of Wrestling himself, mailed it in tonight! Well, technically we all mail it in, but you know what 4M is talking about, so there's no need to break keyfabe on something so trivial. But the real question to be asked here, is [shouting]
CAN ANYBODY [BLEEP]ING BLAME HIM??? First, Derek Mota loses his damn smile. What is it with Canucks who can't come through in the clutch anyway? Anyway fine, delay us all two weeks, force 4M to make donuts in the freakin" Tim Hortons to make ends meet for a little while, that's still cool, so long as we're all equal.
Then they do the unthinkable: hold another mini card and keep me, 4M, scrap 4M from the whole damn thing altogether! And who's fault do you think that is? Anderson? Mota? Yeah, they share some blame for the ultimate decision, but what it ultimately comes down to is one prima donna forcing 4M off the show so he don't have to share the spotlight with him.
Yeah, I'm talking about you, Moxy Blue. I know the insane professional jealousy you have of me. I know you can't even stand to be in the same arena as me, which is why you didn't even make a token appearance tonight. But when you start taking money out of my pocket, when you start hurting MY career, then maybe it's time to start fighting fire with fire. Cuz you damned well KNOW that if the truth of what you're really like backstage got out, you ain't got no future in this business NOWHERE!
Now, 4M went out there tonight and stunk up the joint. Granted, 4M had nothing to work with, but making idiots like Watts and that fanboy look good, that's my job. 4M didn't do it well tonight, and he apologizes for it. But soon, 4M has a world tag team title shot coming up, and it's pretty much a lock he's gonna win. You know why? Because in the tag ranks, 4M can carry three guys instead of just one, and they need a good excuse for him to keep doing that. So, until Mike Justice stops ducking 4M like he has for the past year, cause he doesn't like The Mac
Daddy's sitff working style, 4M will continue to carry his silent wonder partner up the tag ranks, and the Gimp and Wimp Connection will continue to roll along for better or worse.
Moxy, maybe you wanna take a closer look at the action next time? Bring that ice skater girlfriend of yours too. At least, I think she must be a skater, because she does such an excellent impersonation of a Triple
Sow Cow. Anyway, you come on down, and 4M will carry three and a half guys that night. Strike that, you're not even half a man. Just cower away in your corner like always, and suffer in the reality that, despite your best intentions, you will never, EVER, be like 4M!
[McArthur opens the door, slams his duffel bag inside, slams the door shut, cranks the ignition, and peels off into the night.]
------------------------ "Flatlander" Duke Putrid ------------------------ [The camera fades in on a park bench. A pair of dusty work boots trail massive legs that slump over the side. Pigeons swarm around the feet.
but no food is dropped. As the camera pans out, the size and ebony skin of the man reveal him to be none other than NLWP newcomer
'Flatlander' Duke Putrid.] [Duke is wearing old jeans and a yellow and black flannel overshirt. Next to him on the bench is a heavy leather overcoat, which the Wyoming native has apparently removed. The big man sighs as the camera focuses on his face, looking a bit annoyed. The camera shy 34 year old pushes the lens back a bit, and begins to speak in his rich, Western barritone.]
DP - "Arthur Gybertson is my opponent this week. I only have one question. Who is Arthur Gybertson?" [Duke pauses for a second, rubbing the salt and pepper stuble on his head as he thinks. His brow furrows and relaxes, and finally he decides to continue.]
DP - "I mean, maybe I've just been looking in all the wrong places. I spent some time in Chicago, some time in Japan, but I've never heard of this guy. But be someone new. Then again, I guess I'm 'someone new'
myself, eh?" [The rancher smiles, seeming to recall fonder days before he became a travelling performer.]
DP - "But regardless, I guess I don't have a right to complain. After all, my contract said as long as I was on the sidelines, I got no paycheck, right? And a man's gotta earn his salt somehow. So, yeah,
I'm glad I can fight this Arthur fella.
Even if I _don't_ know who he is. I do know this. I'm rusty. No denying that. I've spent several months trying to get a job somewhere. not exactly sure how I got into a place with as small and exclusive a roster as this, but I did. Only one thing bothers me. I'm pushing 35, boys.
I step into the ring with college kids every day who have more experience between the ropes than I do. Thankfully, God still sees fit to keep my back strong . . . "
[Duke puts a hand behind his lower back, stretching.] DP - "And my fists ready." [The 'Flatlander' raises both of his enormous hands in balls the size of grapefruits. He cradles one fist in the other soon enough, and begins to silently work on popping his knuckles.]
DP - "God sees fit to do a lot of things. I may not know much about how to look pretty in the ring, but I sure know how to kick the hell out of one of these pretty boys, like your Van Strifes or whoever the flavor of the week is.
And Arthur Gybertson, you're getting just the same treatment from me. Nothing special, I'll just help you choke on your teeth, boyo. I don't suppose I need to say anymore, do I? No?
Good." [Duke sighs and cracks his neck, now ready to turn his attention to something else it seems.]
DP - "Lessee . . . I see Ken Curtis week in a week out on NLWP programming, and I must say
. . . I'm damned impressed.
No nonsense. I like the guy. To be perfectly honest, I'm not too certain he knows who I am. No matter. Ken, Setz, Mike, and all of the
'stars' will know me soon enough. I think I like being annonymous for now. Why?
I can show up, hand some poor fool his ass, and leave without the benefit or some wetback calling me out; as I'm sure I must look like now. 'Cept I'll be honest with you boys. I don't wrestle. If anything, I fight. But beyond that, my goal is short and simple:
Get paid. I want my ranch back; I want my life back. And I'm doing it then best way I know how. The trail of bodies begins this week at Madness,
Arthur Gybertson is the first in line to get knocked down."
[Duke stands up and slowly grabs his coat as the pigeons scatter. He tosses it over one arm and takes a step forward.]
DP - "I've said it before. Look to Justice or Curtis if you want a hero, kids. If you just want to see a man who works for a living instead of being marketed, you've come to the right place.
And Arthur, I'm taking you to Devil's Tower at Madness. And it's gonna be a long fall." [With that, Devil's Tower's own Duke Putrid steps farther ahead, walking past the camera entirely. The image pauses on the empty bench before it fades out.]
------------------- "Cowboy" Ken Curtis ------------------- [The camera opens on "Cowboy" Ken Curtis, clad in street clothes, standing in front of a large Texas flag. His black hat is pulled low over his eyes, and the glowing tip of his cigarillo juts from beneath the brim. He removes the cigar and begins to speak...]
"Ya know, my ol' man was a hard man t' love. He was hard on us kids when we were comin' up. Maybe a little too hard. But as I got older an'
a bit wiser, I came t' understand why that man was as hard on us as he was." [Curtis takes a drag from his smoke...] "Ya see, the world is a cold, cruel place. [Curtis raises his right hand to reveal a long, black leather strap...] "... he taught me how t' take a beatin'." [Curtis takes a drag from his smoke and drops the butt to the ground and grinds it out with his boot heel.]
"So LOCO? Come next Madness, I'm gonna take this here strap an' I'm gonna teach you the lesson that my daddy taught me a long damned time ago, a lesson that you've been askin' for since you stepped foot here in the Cowboy's backyard. a very good place t' be."
