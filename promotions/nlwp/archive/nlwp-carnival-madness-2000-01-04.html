NLWP Carnival Madness, 4 January 2000

4 January 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The camera opens on "Cowboy" Ken Curtis, clad in street clothes, standing in front of a large NLWP banner. He removes the smoldering cigarillo from his teeth and begins to speak...]
"A man with a whole lot o' sense once tol' me, 'be damned careful what you wish for, son, 'cause ya just might get it.'"
[Curtis takes a drag from his smoke...] "Ya see, I was the big dog here once b'fore. An' if it wasn't for that mama's boy Nicky Doom pullin' the biggest break of his pathetic excuse for a career, the Cowboy'd prob'ly still be wearin' that strap. An' I guess the thought o' the Cowboy wearin' the gold appeals t' somebody upstairs here, 'cause they've t' give the Cowboy a shot at the right t' face that coward Justice at the next big throwdown. All I can say is this...
... they best be careful what they wish for, 'cause the Cowboy ain' nobody's lap-dog. I don' step an' fetch for nobody, not even the sumbitch that's signin' my paychecks."
[Curtis drops the cigar butt and grinds it out with his boot heel...] "So Cadaver? LOCO? Moxy? You boys had best listen up an' listen good. The Cowboy is in this thang t' win this thang. An' as far as I'm concerned, you three curtain-jerkers ain' nothin' but stones in my passway, an' when the time comes, you can either step out o' my way or
I'll step over ya. We'll play it your way. But make no mistake about it, I _will_ so whatever it damned well takes t' win this match, an'
that's how the cow eats the cabbage." The cameras follow a young family walking down the street, the two children holding their parents' hands, and huge bundles of cotton candy in their other hand.
As the family turns the corner, the children gasp in amazement as the cameras begin to pick up the reason for this amazement ...
... one of the biggest carnivals that Toronto has ever seen. And no, not a winter carnival. Today is a beautiful day, and there's rides everywhere, a carousel, and game booths lining the walls for a seemingless endless path.
One of the children looks up at his father, asking him a question.] C: Daddy, what's that? F: Get back, Colin! The smiles disappear. The hubbub subsides. The crowds seem to part involuntarily as the huge, hulking frame of a masked man, dressed from head to toe in black, save for a red skull emblazoned on his barrel chest, his face obscured by a featureless mask, steps out from the shadows behind the stall.
He picks up a stick of cotton candy and examines it quizically, then drops it to the ground behind him. Children cower behind their parents as the man moves closer, close enough for the rasping sound of his breathing to be heard.
This is Cadaver. He gestures to his surroundings with a dismissive wave of one trunk-like arm.]
CADAVER: A meaningless display. A meaningless display... rather like the meaningless display of weakness and cowardice shown by Setzer van Strife and his Lynch Mob some time ago.
You may have won that match, Strife... but nobody can say that they have truly defeated Cadaver until he doesn't get up again.
But get up again I did, and get up again I shall. You shall not deflect me from my path. You shall not deflect me from my quarry. And that man is you, Mike Justice. Of your many faults, perhaps your greatest is that you talk too much. Cadaver, as you will find, is a man of action. Never a meaningless display. And when I take three men, and pound them into submission, here in this mecca of frivolity, you will see.
You will see that there is meaning in every blow, in every drop of blood that I squeeze from every wound, in every crack of bone and tearing of sinew.
It will not be a pretty display. But you will know, Justice. You will know what Cadaver has in store for you. You will know that the man who put that belt around your waist can take it from you at any time.
And take it I shall. Take it... I shall. [Cadaver clenches his two gloved fists, the silence that has descended around him so complete that the creak of his leather gloves is clearly audible. The monstrous figure retreats into the shadows beside the cotton candy stall... and is gone.
Cut to center of the carnival, right by the carousel, where Jim Vasquez and Tony Mastino stand. The two are bundled up in warm clothing, but the atmosphere outside apparently has made this clothing redundant.
Mastino begins stripping as the sound turns on...] TM: Dammit, it's hot like one of my bitches today! JV: It's one of the warmest January days we've ever seen in Toronto, now up to ten degrees celsius, and what a beautiful day to celebrate the NLWP's special event! Tonight, four men will square off in this carnival match, with the winner receiving a title shot at the NLWP
World Champion "Apostle" Mike Justice at the PPV on February 14th!
TM: Yeah, who's the moron who keeps changing the date of the PPV anyways? They're gonna screw up the buy rate on this puppy!
JV: I don't think we have to worry about that, Tony. With the lineup being made as we speak, this one will keep the phones at the cable company ringing off the hook!
TM: We're still a month away, Jimmy. No need to schill yourself out for a few extra bucks. The clause in your contract only kicks in two weeks before the PPV, remember?
JV: Oh yeah. Well let's get to the match then ... four wrestlers. "Cowboy" Ken Curtis, one of the favorites tonight due to the fact that he's held the NLWP World Title before, and has had several other World
Titles in his legendary career. Then there's LOCO, the king of the independant leagues. Unpredictable. But when you're talking unpredictable, there's Richard "Moxy" Blue. One of the true icons of the sport, the Mox can pull a win out of his ... well, he can scrape out a win anytime. And last but not least, the man who's been aiming for Mike Justice for some time now, the massive Cadaver.
TM: We saw him already tonight. He is a scary man. Not that I ever get scared, of course, but if I did, he'd be the man that would scare me.
JV: Well, they're slowly coming into the area right now, where we'll get started ... wait a second, something's going on off to the side!
[The cameras pull away from Vasquez and Mastino, as the technicians quickly run down the path to the outskirts of the carnival, where the
Lynch Mob, LOCO and "2 Smooth" Setzer Van Strife beat on a fallen
"Cowboy" Ken Curtis.]
JV: What the hell is going on here? This match hasn't even started yet, and Setzer Van Strife isn't supposed to be anywhere near here!
TM: Well, it looks like he's breaking the rules, Lynch Mob style! JV: Oh no, don't tell me you're becoming a Lynch Mob mark. TM: They're giving the Lynch Mob sign to the cameras, this is hilarious! JV: What's so funny! This is a terrible attack by a bunch of degenerates, and you're just laughing!
TM: Well what did you expect? Setzer to play a nice guy and kindly help Ken Curtis pin his buddy? I don't think so, Jimmy!
JV: Well the Lynch Mob has just left Ken Curtis amidst the rubble of a hoops game, and are now walking to the carousel!
TM: I guess that's the starting point, huh? JV: Well the other two wrestlers, Richard "Moxy" Blue and Cadaver are there waiting, barely keeping from ripping each other apart, waiting for the other wrestlers to show up!
TM: But Curtis is still buried under a pile of stuffed animals, Jimbo! JV: The Lynch Mob runs out and are attacking both Cadaver and Moxy! This is the weirdest combo we've seen in a long time, even if it may be short lived! TM: Well they'd better work together, or else LOCO and Setzer are gonna tear through them!
JV: Tony, Setzer Van Strife is not even in this match! I'm surprised that security hasn't dragged him out yet.
TM: Oh, like the carnival security is gonna work. Some old fat chick and her 15 year old son are gonna drag out "2 Smooth". Yeah, tell me another one.
JV: Well it somehow looks like they're letting Setzer Van Strife fight this match! I guess due to the fact that "Cowboy" Ken Curtis has been injured, they're letting these four men fight for the title match!
TM: Good move. JV: It's a travesty! Ken Curtis should be one of the favorites, and instead they left him out to dry!
TM: And Mox ain't doing much better now, is he? JV: Of course not! The momentum is against him! It's a well oiled machine against two individuals, as the Lynch Mob gains the quick advantage!
TM: Setzer Van Strife has Cadaver's number once again, just like last week when he got the three count on the mammoth. He grabs the bigger man and just tosses him into the wall!
JV: These makeshift buildings aren't exactly the most sturdy things around! The whole thing just collapsed on both of them!
TM: LOCO and Moxy getting it on here, LOCO picking up the Mox ... Press Slamming him over his head! He runs over and drops him onto the remains of the wooden building!
JV: Owch! Right on top of Setzer and Cadaver! Setzer won't exactly be the happiest guy on earth when he finds out that his partner just dumped him!
TM: LOCO ain't giving it away yet, Jimmy. He's got Moxy by the hair, dragging him over to the ... oh yeah!
JV: Bumper cars! TM: He just tosses Moxy into the round, all the bumper cars off... the operator is just standing here dumbfounded.
JV: Oh no! LOCO now has Richard Blue on top of one of the bumper cars, and he Piledrives him onto the car!
TM: They're turning the bumper cars on! The stupid pimply faced kid musta got all excited and just turned the cars on!
JV: LOCO is stepping into one of the bumper cars! He's going to ... he's going to try and run over Moxy with the car! TM: Mox is too stunned from the Piledriver to realize what's going on! Hit him, LOCO, hit him! Kill him! JV: He's got the bumper car going and is driving straight at Moxy... but Moxy dives out of the way! TM: Damn! I was hoping he'd kill Moxy there! JV: We didn't notice. But LOCO just went speeding by a diving Moxy and crashed into the wall! And Blue just coming up from behind, dropkicking LOCO in the back of the head!
TM: Oh man, down around the corner, Cadaver and Setzer Van Strife getting up from that weird building collapse we just saw.
JV: Cadaver looking like he has the advantage here, a much bigger man so he took less damage from the fall. But he's not looking happy here, taking a glance around for the other wrestlers before dragging Setzer out of the debris.
TM: Where are they brawling to? JV: They're going to the groundhog game! Hit the groundhog with the hammer! I loved that one when I was a kid!
TM: I think maybe you hit YOURSELF with the hammer, Jimmy. JV: Funny. But Setzer Van Strife trying to get the upper hand, and he grabs one of the hammers and knocks Cadaver over the head with it!
TM: Cadaver's head is made out of stone, and he feels no pain! Hit him in the bag, Setzer!
JV: Just like the last time, right Tony? TM: Hey, you do what it takes. JV: The crowd is accumulated near these two wrestlers, and they're laughing at the feeble attempt by Setzer to hurt Cadaver. And frankly,
I can't blame them there. You should use the weapons at your disposal, but that was pretty pathetic.
TM: Well Cadaver just throws Setzer down the path, right through the pretzel machine! Pretzels flying everywhere! Five second rule! Five second rule!
JV: No Tony, they're not going to bring you pretzels, even if they were on the ground for less than five seconds.
TM: Can't blame me for trying. JV: Cadaver rubbing the pretzels into Setzer's face, and the salt is blinding him! Setzer has been incapacitated by a pretzel!
TM: Shut up! JV: And he's dragging Setzer down through Exhibition Place in Toronto, all the way to one of the tallest machines in the section... what is it?
TM: It's like a scale of some sort ... JV: Oh no, it's the test of strength! And that huge thing right beside the scale ... TM: It's a hammer! A giant sledgehammer! He's gonna kill Setzer! JV: Cadaver with the hammer now, he's arranging it so Setzer's head is directly into the swing ... he lifts the hammer over his head ... good god, somebody stops this before someone gets killed ...
TM: Setzer moved out of the way! He barely got his head of of the way of the sledgehammer shot, but his arm was still in the way! Van Strife screaming in pain as he clutches his hand!
JV: Cadaver just nailed him on the hand with the sledgehammer! That was just ugly! TM: LOCO and Setz are gonna have matching casts, Jimbo! JV: Van Strife is screaming in pain, as Cadaver looks rather pleased with himself. I think he won the "test of strength" game this time around, god only knows what could've happened if he had hit Setzer in the head like he was aiming!
TM: Moxy and LOCO now brawling back towards the center of the park, the crowd clearing away to give them some room.
JV: They're meeting up with Setzer Van Strife and Cadaver now, almost in the same area. And ... oh no.
TM: Oh man, they're heading towards the roller coaster! JV: All four men brawling their way towards the roller coaster! This one could be very ugly, folks! But Setzer is obviously in much pain from that sledgehammer blow earlier, Cadaver is just ripping this young man apart right now!
TM: Where's Moxy??? JV: I have no idea! Richard "Moxy" Blue has apparently disapeared, his opponents are looking for him but he seems to have snuck away in the whole confusion of things! That's not like Moxy at all!
TM: I always knew he was a coward, Jimmy. This just proves it. JV: There must be a reason. Well LOCO is assaulting Cadaver now, with
Setzer Van Strife producing a few kicks to the head to add to it.
They're walking him over to the roller coaster, god only knows what they plan on doing to him there!
TM: Well they're gonna take a sidetrip to the carousel, Jimmy! Setzer just threw Cadaver into the spinning platform! That one just about took Cadaver's head off there, Jimmy!
JV: Just about! It's bad enough when you ram someone's head into a solid object, and even worse when that object is flying by you!
Cadaver's been busted open here, you can see the blood flowing through the mask that he wears.
TM: Wait a second ... JV: This isn't something that little children should see, Tony! There's small kids riding the carousel, and to see two men beating on a single man might leave scars!
TM: Yeah, on Cadaver! But ... that ... riding the carousel ... JV: I have no idea what you're talking about, Tony. Oh no... LOCO has just ripped apart one of the stands and is using a 2x4, smashing it down on the back of Cadaver! This is not good!
TM: Umm... that was ... JV: Oh my ... [The announcers are left in silence as the carousel spins around, and we see none other than Richard "Moxy" Blue riding one of the horses!
Moxy waves to the cameras as he spins past the cameras towards ...] JV: It's Moxy! He's riding the carousel! Moxy's standing and ... TM: He's standing on the horse! And ... and he's diving off the platform! JV: Plancha onto LOCO and Setzer Van Strife!!!!!!!! The referee is making the count!!! 1!!!! 2!!!!! NO!!!!! JV: LOCO kicked out of it! Really that one was impossible, trying to cover two wrestlers at the same time, but one of the most unexpected
surprises of the evening here! We thought Moxy had run away! TM: He still should! Damn him! JV: And Cadaver is getting a chance to recover! Moxy is going crazy on the Lynch Mob, taking turns on each one of them! The fans are going crazy here, taking a break from their rides and games to cheer for
Richard Blue!
TM: The morons! Gimme a mouthful of cotton candy, a 40 oz. of vodka and a few loose ho's and that'll make me happy, not that loser Moxy!
JV: Moxy's got the 2x4 that LOCO was using on Cadaver, and is now turning it against the Lynch Mob! And this one isn't made out of foam either, it's hard wood!
TM: Hey, that's what all the women tell me too! JV: Cadaver now reaching into the debris of the stand that LOCO just broke down, grabbing a bunch of stuffed animals and throwing it down on them!
TM: That had no effect whatsoever. JV: No, but it's pretty funny to see Setzer Van Strife covered in mounds of teletubbies! TM: I gotta admit ... that ain't so bad... JV: Okay, maybe that wasn't the whole point of it all. Cadaver now is through the stuffed animals, and has found the crate that they were being stored in! Smooth in the crate, and is rolling him down the aisle! Watch out kids!

TM: This time the wall stands! And it's Setzer who goes flying! Oh man, I think that 2 Smooth is pretty much out of it after that one.

JV: "Cowboy" Ken Curtis was waiting for them at the duck shoot ... and he's got one of the air rifles! He's got it loaded ... and shoots at
LOCO! TM: Bullseye! JV: He got him right between the eyes! He could've blinded LOCO with that shot, but instead hit him right in the forehead, knocking LOCO right down to the ground!
TM: He's shooting one more ... JV: Right in the ass! LOCO may have been anally penetrated by "Cowboy" Ken Curtis here! Uhh... but not in that way, in case you were wondering! TM: Moxy is having a love fest here! The guy's laughing so hard at all this that he's on the ground laughing his guts off!
JV: Wouldn't you? "Cowboy" Ken Curtis has made his way back in to counter all the teamwork that the Lynch Mob did on Moxy and Cadaver the rest of the match! And Cadaver is now dragging Setzer Van Strife to the roller coaster!
TM: He's lying him down on the tracks! And 2 Smooth still doesn't know where he is, he's so stunned from getting rammed into the wall just a minute ago!
JV: Oh my god ... Cadaver is not trying to pin Setzer ... he's trying to kill him! TM: The train is moving pretty fast towards them ... Setzer may have

TM: He's locking him in! And he's getting in himself! This is hilarious, Ken Curtis and LOCO are going for a ride on the roller coaster!

NLWP, Tony! And Cadaver will be facing "Apostle" Mike Justice at the
NLWP's first PPV back on February 14th!

TM: Ken Curtis is looking pretty damn happy here, Jimbo. Maybe he was to weak to win the match after that vicious attack, but he played the role of the spoiler pretty damn well!
JV: And lets give some props to Richard "Moxy" Blue, who put on a great fight tonight as well! But tonight Cadaver was the best man, and he's going to the main event of our first PPV!
TM: Mike Justice has quite the challenge ahead of him, Jimmy. JV: He certainly does. He certainly does. We're out of here for tonight, thanks for watching this very special Madness broadcast, we'll be back next Monday with our regular show! See you then!
[Credits begin rolling as we see Cadaver standing back on the ramp, his arms raised in victory as the blood flows from his mask. Fade.]
