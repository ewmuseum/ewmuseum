NLWP Backstage Pass, 19 January 2000

Chairs and clothes are strewn out almost everywhere, some covered in the thick foam of "Mooselips" Beer, as "Mr. Majestyk" Maurice McArthur and Valtharius the Mad celebrate becoming the new NLWP World Tag Team Champions. Valtharius sits on the locker room floor chewing the fringes of his newly won leather belt.
Valtharius is completely oblivious to the fact he has won a championship while 4M has his tag belt in one hand and a Mooselips in another, almost frantically searching for some way to release his emotion, before finally addressing the camera:]
4M: Is 4M the [BLEEP] or what!? Damned bookers and a lame partner put 4M in an almost impossible position, and what does he do? The Mac Daddy goes out and turns the tables on all of them! The man they said couldn't even be carried is now doing the carrying! David and Goliath, new NLWP Tag Team Champs! [name withheld], 4M hopes you're watching this, because 4M has got two words for you...
VTM: EAT WEASEL!!! 4M: No, that ain't it! "Visionless Coward!" This is the same Maurice McArthur he was four yours ago, but you couldn't see that! McArthur trouncing Genesis, that's the way you SHOULD have booked it three years ago, but you were blind to the greatness right in front of you! You couldn't see the courage and creativity that's finally bringing 4M to the top, you only saw a guy without a million dollar body or a pretty boy face! So you jobbed 4M out to stiffs night after night! Speaking of stiffs, Cadaver, don't get too attached to that North American title. Now that 4M has established himself as the premier member of the New Team Sychosys to become TWO TIME! TWO TIME! TWO TIME tag team champion of the world, 4M thinks it's about time to get some singles gold around his fat waist. And 4M remembers when you cost me my chance at gold in the battle royal. So in a way, you're just like Spreadbury, holding 4M back from his rightful place on the top. You better not get any designs on the world title meat boy, because that North American title belongs to 4M, and he's taking it from you!
[Valtharius leans to the side and lets out a loud fart and continues to chew on the tag team belt. He bites into one the gold plates and reacts violently as it is much like chewing on tin foil.]
4M: And what about the world title, huh? The big guy here has finally got a taste of the gold...literally! You think that's gonna satisfy him? A tag belt and piece of fluff like Sean Watts!? Hey, Mad Boy, tell the people what you think about Mike Justice!
VTM: WHO MIKE PUS-LICE?!? ME VALTHARIUS NOT KNOW IT WHAT IS!!! [Valtharius turns his belt over and sniffs the back then turns it back over and begins peeling the gold plates from the belt to get at the fresh leather underneath. Fortunately, 4M has been well-trained in dealing with "unusual personalities", and snatches the belt away from
Val:] 4M: HEY HEY HEY! Listen here, this belt ain't something that's supposed to be wolfed down in one night! You're supposed to savor it, maybe with a bit of white wine, or a parsley garnish, or...
VTM: GIMME VALTHARIUS BELT!!! ME NOT LIKE EAT WHINE!!! 40-WATT PERSON WHINE WHEN ME HURT HIM AND NOW HIM UPSET POOR, POOR VALTHARIUS TUMMY!!! ***BURP*** 4M: Yeah, whatever [fumbles around in his locker, and pulls out a slip of paper] Here's ten bucks, go over to the Kwik-E-Mart next door and pick up a bottle of that new JR Barbeque Sauce. DON'T EAT THE MONEY!
You got that? [Valtharius takes the money, sniffs it, then stuffs it down the front of his dirty loin cloth. 4M grimaces at the thought of where the money went then mutters something about Valtharius giving a whole new meaning to "dirty money."]
VTM: ME GO GET BARBIE DOLL SAUCE NOW!!! NOW GIMME VALTHARIUS BELT!!! [He hands the Mad Italian one half of the NLWP Tag Team Championship. Valtharius stands up and promptly plods over to the door and leaves with belt in hand and money down his loin cloth.]
4M: [yelling as Val slams the door shut] AND PICK A TOOTHBRUSH WHILE YER AT IT, YOU GOT A BAD CASE OF WATTS BREATH! [4M lets out a heavy sigh] Well, it beats the other program 4M was working. Anyway,
Abracadaver, there'll be time enough for you, but for now, we got a pay per view coming up, and we're all dressed up with nowhere to go! So we're throwing out an open challenge to anyone out there to meet us for these belts! And 4M means anyone, be it the former champs, the
Imperialists, the freaking Down Syndrome Boys or any other overhyped spammers in the EMWC! Heck, Moxy Blue, bring your sow cow and your cat dog on down for a whupping for all 4M cares! Bottom line is, it
DOESN'T MATTER WHO WE FIGHT, because this is the 4M Millenium, this is 4M's time, and if you like it, you can kiss 4M's increasingly huge ass! Whooo! [With that, 4M goes off in search of some grub.] ---------------- Jarrod O'Lachlan ---------------- [Scene opens backstage at the Copps Coliseum in Hamilton, Ontario. To be precise, it is inside Jarrod O'Lachlan's dressing room. Jarrod is sitting on a bench with his back against the wall. He hasn't bothered changing from his wrestling attire. His hair is ruffled and sweat pours down his reddened cheeks. He looks up at the camera with a less than happy look in his face. He then closes his eyes and looks down to the ground]
JO: You know, one tries ones' best. I bust my ass fighting Ken Curtis in an attempt to be taken seriously, and for a moment there it looked like it was about to happen...
[Jarrod looks up at the camera] JO: ...but alas, it was not to be. [Jarrod takes a deep breath]All I wanted was to capitalise on the great match I had against him and have a good, clean match tonight and earn a pinfall win to show the fans that I am great wrestler, but it was ruined...
[O'Lachlan stands up and looks as if he's about to burst with anger] JO: [Jarrod points at the camera]...RUINED BECAUSE YOU LITTLE NLWP TV TITLE GEEKS HAVE SOME STUPID LITTLE FEUD WITH EACH OTHER. BOLTZ, MCCORD, JORGENSEN, BE WARNED; YOU ARE ALL NOW IN MY HIT LIST... [Jarrod begins walking away. He notices a water cooler on his way. He stops, looks at it, and knocks it down...]
*SMASH* [Jarrod resumes walking as the scene fades] ------------------- "Cowboy" Ken Curtis ------------------- [The camera opens on the nattily-clad Dean Duncan, mic in hand, standing in front of the locker-room door.]
DD: "Hello, NLWP fans! I'm Dean Duncan and I'm here outside the NLWP locker room to try to catch a few words with the man himself, "Cowboy"
Ken Curtis. He should be..." [Suddenly, the door swings violently open and "Cowboy" Ken Curtis, clad in street clothes and clutching a black leather gym bag, storms out of the room. He sees the camera and slows a bit, allowing Duncan to approach him.]
CKC: "Ya got two minutes, Duncan. I ain' in no kind o' mood t' be runnin' off at the mouth back here with the likes o' you."
DD: "Fair enough. We saw the unlikely duo of Justice and Cadaver coming to your aid tonight. Did we witness the beginning of an alliance..."
CKC: "Damned if you so-called 'broadcast journalists' don't look for a story where there ain' one. First thangs first, Duncan. The Cowboy don't need, and damned sure don't want, anybody's help. Justice 'as got a helluva damned fight on 'is hands with that sideshow freak Cadaver, so if the holy-roller knows what's good for 'im, he'll stay out o' the
Cowboy's backyard and tend t' 'is own damned fences. And as far as the freak himself is concerned, I ain' got no idea why he showed up out there an' I really don' give a dog-damn. But if he knows what's good for 'im, he'll stay outta the Cowboy's fight too, or he just might not make it to that big fight for the big strap. Like I said b'fore an'
I'll damned well say again, I didn' ask for no help an' I don't need no help." DD: "Then how, may I ask, do you plan on evening the odds in your fight with the Lynch Mob?"
CKC: "Duncan, you an' ever' sumbitch walkin' these halls knows damned well that the Cowboy can stand up with LOCO an' Van Strife any night o'
the week. It ain' a matter o' evenin' up the odds... it's a matter o' dividin' an' conquerin'. An' you know as well as I do that the Cowboy is pretty damned good at doin' that. LOCO got a taste o' what the
Cowboy can dish out t'night when I took the tire iron to 'is ass out in the parkin' lot. If I gotta jump those bastards in the locker room, in the parkin' lot, or at the front counter o' Dunkin' Donuts t' square the debt, then that's damned well what the Cowboy's gonna do."
DD: "Do you have any idea who may have been behind the voice that interrupted the card tonight before yours and LOCO's match?"
CKC: "If I had t' guess, I'd think some o' LOCO's chickens are comin' home t' roost. But t' tell the truth, I don't know who it was, an' frankly, I really don' give a rat's ass. 'Till the man shows 'is face, he don't mean a damned thang t' the Cowboy. "
DD: "Any last words for the Lynch Mob before you leave us?" CKC: "You damned right I got some words for those yella-dog sumbitches..." [Curtis turns and points to the camera, a wild, angry look in his eyes...] CKC: "You bastards are diggin' yerselves deeper an' deeper in the hole ever' time you pull a stunt like the one you pulled t'night! An'
anybody that knows the Cowboy knows that I ain' gonna let y'all get away with that kind o' crap week in an' week out! So enjoy yer little ride while it lasts, ladies, 'cause it ain' gonna be too damned long b'fore the wheels come off!"
[Curtis turns and walks away as Duncan turns to face the camera.] DD: "There you have it, NLWP fans! The Cowboy is obviously focused on settling his score with the Lynch Mob, and as far as this 'broadcast jounalist' is concerned, that can't be good news for LOCO and Setzer
Van Strife. I'm Dean Duncan for Backstage Pass!" [Fade to black.] ------------------------- Erik "Ragnarok" Jorgensen ------------------------- [The scene opens inside a locker room... Where the NLWP TV Champion, Erik "Ragnarok" Jorgensen is... eating? Yep, he is eating some fried chicken. And watching a video of past Madness.]
EJ: [Munch, munch] I gotta say, by Volstagg's stomach... These guys know how to make a wild night. Crashes, bumps, shots... [Munch] Oh, there are those two trolls I will be fighting soon. McCord and Boltz. I gotta say, they are uglier than Ooger's face! And Ooger, for those don't know, was a big troll I killed some years ago, with the help of my Mjolnir.
[Erik pats the sledgehammer at his side, and gets a chicken wing.] EJ: Boltz... McCord... We're fated to meet. The Nornes decided that... And even a God must respect their decisions. So be it. I will be glad to meet you both again... And by Loki's smartness, I will turn you both into a pile of dead meat... Just like this!
[Erik throws the wing on the camera... Static.] ------- Cadaver ------- [The shot opens up on the gleaming gold plate of the NLWP North American Championship belt. As the camera pulls back slowly, a mesh comes into focus -- and it becomes apparent that the belt lies in the bottom of a wastebasket in a dimly-lit locker room, the gold glinting in the subdued lighting.
The shot continues to pull out until it is filled with the hulking frame of Cadaver, clad from head to foot in black, save for the blood-red skull emblazoned on his huge chest. He speaks from under his featureless mask, a low, rasping voice that is none the less menacing for its quietness:]
CADAVER: Meaningless. A meaningless trinket. A gold belt, a title. But there was no glory in victory tonight. Kid Canuck is crushed... but what manner of man was he before he stepped into the ring with Cadaver? And what manner of man will he be now? This title means nothing to me. The belt lies where it belongs; and yet I am ordered to defend this title. Defend it
I will; who will be strong enough, determined enough... stupid enough to try and take it from me?
[The shot closes in on Cadaver's eerily featureless mask. Not even the whites of his eyes break the black visage.]
But some titles _are_ worth the taking. "Apostle" Mike Justice, you proved tonight that you are a strong man. A man worthy of facing Cadaver. The question is: are you strong enough? I know it will trouble you on the cold winter nights that lie ahead. For all the trappings of your feted position, deep down you question your worthiness.
As well you might. [Cadaver... sneers? It's hard to tell through his featureless mask.] You will yourself get a crushing lesson in your own inadequacy in just a few short weeks. I will see to it that you make it to meet your destiny, Justice. I will see to it that you learn that lesson. Learn you will. But will you understand? [The shot pulls out as Cadaver exits the locker room without another word, leaving the NLWP North American Championship belt in the wastebin as he goes. Fade.]
------------------------------------ Scott Fear & "Naturalist" Rob Jenner ------------------------------------ [The scene before the camera is that of the Imperialist locker room. A mini bar is set up with a bar tender. The Naturalist Rob Jennerand
Scott Fear are pacing back and forth with beverages just behind Tony Mastino and their manager Cam McMillions. Behind them their press crew of Buisness-man Bo Blue and Bad-boy Gus Johnson are talking away on cellular phones sitting on soft seats with beutiful ladies]
TM: I'm here in the locker room of the Empire where there seems to be a great deal of tension. Do you have any comments on that Mr.
McMillions CMc: First of all Tony, you can call me Cam. Secondly, don't confuse tension with a bit of frustration. The boys have just witnessed two titles change hands tonight, titles they have been in line for.
TM: I see, do you guys have something to say on your own behalf? SF: You better believe it Tony, I'm here, the number one contender for the North American belt and do I get the title shot? No, they give it to the number one contender for the world belt. He won, now they'll probably have a return bout and I'll have to keep waiting, despite having been the number one contender.
TM: That is frustrating, are you smelling forces at work against you? SF: Possibly Tony, I would have found it fair had they given an even exchange between contenders and given me a shot at the world belt.
TM: That would have been quite the match! SF: But it didn't happen Tony, because nobody wants a piece of me, they fear Scott Fear. The bookers know it and they don't want a title around the waist of a man who only the stupid would face. But that's going to have to change here Tony, for good reason.
TM: Why don't you elaborate on that... For those fans who are a little slow. SF: Certainly. Lets just think about the standings. I am, or was the number one contender for the North American title, I should have a title shot. Secondly, myself and my main man Rob Jenner were the number two contenders for the tag team belts before monday night. During
Madness both teams above us fell apart, but somehow a team comprised of a man who wasn't even on the ladder and another man who I've soundly destroyed in one on one compitition, walked away with the belts.
TM: The same belts that you and your partner have claimed a right to since you came back to the NLWP.
RJ: Well Tony my man, "Claimed a right to" doesn't quite cover it. With all the break ups we should be officially the number one contenders for the belts. However, the fans out their in our empire know who the real tag team champions are and that's us. The monster
Scott Fear, and me, the real full meal deal, the Naturalist, Rob Jenner. TM: Speaking of yourself Rob, we haven't seen to much of you as of late. RJ: Yes, don't think I haven't noticed! I've wrestled what, twice since the league restarted, only once solo, of course a victory. The
NLWP is forth in the ratings! You'd think they'd want a show stopper like myself on every show! Somebody is letting my prime main event material go to waste!
TM: That's so true, I can't agree with you more. So we have two wrestlers in this locker room, itching for some serious action.
CMc: That about sums it up Tony, and if the Imperialists don't get the action they deserve soon I don't think we'll be able to keep them passive in this locker room, they can only be distracted for so long.
TM: And you said the "action they deserve", I'm assuming certain compitition won't make your cut.
CMc: Tony, with my boys here trying to drive home the point they've been making I pitty any unfit punks who step into the ring with them.
TM: As do I... Well fans you've heard it here first, the Empire is heating up like a pressure cooker set to explode on the NLWP. This is
Tony Mastino signing out to have drinks in the Empire locker room. -------------------------- "Blizzard" Johnathan Frost -------------------------- [The camera opens backstage, mere moments after the conclusion of the tag team title matchup. We can still hear the roar of the crowd celebrating the new champions...and the roar of the new champions somehow echoing over the crowd noise.
A few seconds pass, until the curtains leading out to the ring part, and "The Blizzard" Johnathan Frost walks backstage, looking very much worse for wear from being thrown around by Valtharius the Mad. Covered in sweat, breathing heavy, the International Falls native takes a few seconds to catch his breath...
...before noticing the camera, and letting loose an audible sigh] JF: You can't leave me alone, can you? Here I am, moments after being tossed around by that...thing...out there, trying to recover from almost becoming that thing's midnight snack, and here you are shoving a damn camera in my face. Can't get any privacy around this place...you're lucky I'm not shoving that damn thing up your nose.
So everyone's probably wondering "Why?" Why I turned on Genesis and threw away the World belts to that embarrassment and the retard. Hey,
I told everyone at the beginning I didn't want the tag team belts, and I was going to dump them at my first chance. And that's what I just did. Genesis was nothing but a weight dragging me down, and I ditched him the first chance I got. He can go do whatever the hell he wants, because I am done with him.
Yeah, that's right, done. No match, no revenge, no blow-off, NOTHING. He can go flounder in curtain-jerker hell, and maybe down the road I'll kick your ass just for fun.
So the question is...what now? What can Johnathan Frost do here in the NLWP? [Frost nods to the camera, a curt sharp nod] JF: Stay tuned, and maybe you'll find out. Rest assured, my ass isn't buried like Genesis' is.
[Fade]
