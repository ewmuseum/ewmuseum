Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The Maximum Wrestling Circuit reached the newsgroup on 15 June 1996 through someone else's plug: the operator of a different e-fed posted its creation sheet on a friend's behalf, promising “REAL extreme and hardcore action every card” and describing it as “a regional organization for the Dawg Pound of Wrestling (DPW),” with a standing monthly crossover shot at that fed's own World titles built into its own creation sheet. Its own planned title board ran a sanctioned MWC Heavyweight Title and Heavyweight Tag Team Title alongside an unsanctioned “Texas Wildman” belt, outcomes decided by roleplay quality with a points tiebreaker, under a five-show weekly slate — Monday Morning Power Hour, Tuesday Night Spectacular, Thursday Evening Showcase, Saturday Sunrise Crunch Brunch, and Saturday Nightmare — plus a supercard called In the Park After Dark and a monthly pay-per-view, Maximum Pandemonium.

Its only other surviving document, a weekly schedule dated 4 July 1996, names roughly two dozen wrestlers already booked and shows the promotion already running: a live card, Midnight Slaughterhouse, out of St. Louis, Missouri, opening on Splatter the Clown against “Big Bear” Brian Deverlough, a randomly-drawn tag team gauntlet, a DPW World Title defense by “Sadistic” Steve Stone against “Devestator” Mick Morton, and a four-team, five-man-a-side Survivor Series tournament for an American Commonwealth Championship. On the same card, Terry “Shotgun” Storm was booked to defend the MWC Heavyweight Title against Magus in a submission match — already champion by the time this schedule survives, with no earlier document recording how he won it.

No result of that card, or of any later one, survives; the promotion's own record ends on the schedule that set it up, and no closure notice was ever posted.
