************************* Maximum Wrestling Circuit Creation ************************** Have you ever wanted to be in an e-wrestling league that brought REAL extreme and hardcore action every card? Well, this could be the fed for you! The MWC will be dedicated to the most entertaining and original events ever in e-wrestling.
We will not be a World federation but a regional organization for the Dawg Pound of Wrestling(DPW). So that means we will have crossovers with them. Including, a MWC wrestler will get a shot at their World titles once a month. This will hopefully be the ultimate in all e-federations you have ever joined. Titles? Yeah we have them.....
