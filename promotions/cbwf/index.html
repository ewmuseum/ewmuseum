Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

CBWF ran a "Crazy Bill's Wrestling Ring" webring member site under a persona known only as White Knight, its own home page dating the fed to August 1998 and "still going" as of its capture. A rebuild announcement on the fed's own main page points toward "the 2002 SESSION," the corpus's own latest dated content, warning that applications submitted during a stated window would see a delayed reply while the operator was "on deployment in Egypt."

The fed's own served roster mixes the real Radicalz stable — Chris Benoit, Dean Malenko, Eddie Guerrero, Perry Saturn — and The Undertaker with original characters (D-Tour, Inferno, Phantom Lord, Pimp Dawg, Freak). No title history, card, or result survives in the held corpus; the fed's own rules page permits "real and original wrestlers" and caps each roleplayer at four wrestlers.
