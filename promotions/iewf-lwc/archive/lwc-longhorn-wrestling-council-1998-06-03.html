[LWC] Longhorn Wrestling Council

3 June 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hey everyone! I'm the LWC's founder (Ryan)'s co-prez Greg of course. Ryan and his wife just moved and dropped his internet provider until he could find one in his new town. Apparently a few people didn't quite have faith or couldn't wait and have found a new fed to reside in. Which is of course fine.
To restock a few empty spots in the roster, we'd love for some _new_ blood. "Gimmickless" wrestlers are all the rage. Well, we'd actually enjoy a gimmick or two. An original personality and not the same old "Dark and Mysterious" or the same old "Bad Ass Texan".
Before we get into applications and things like that, e-mail me your idea of what a cool and new personality would be, or a cool or new gimmick would be.
If you can only describe it in roleplay, send me that or a portion of one and we could work with stuff like applications from there.
Thanks, Greg
