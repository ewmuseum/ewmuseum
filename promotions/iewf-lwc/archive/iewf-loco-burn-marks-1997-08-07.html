As for EP and JA, well, EP isn't using that name anymore, and is in NLWP and some farm league for NPWA. As for Adonis, he was set to retire, but now I've decided to make it semi-retirement. I like to play many wrestlers, and I've used Adonis on and off since 1991, and I have newer wrestlers that are more interesting. Besides, I brought back Soultaker, my best pbm wrestler into IEWF, and unlike what Tonnage says, Soultaker has been around since 1989, while Undertaker has been around 6 years or so.
