Wrestlegeddon '97! Check this out!, 2-3 September 1997

2-3 September 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Dear e-wrestler, For those of you who haven't met me before, my name is [name withheld], co-president of Prime Time Wrestling League along with [name withheld], and I'm here to tell you a little about an event we're putting together. It's going to be _VERY_ big and might actually be a turning point in e-wrestling. I just thought I'd inform you that the Prime Time Wrestling League is going to be hosting one of the biggest e-wrestling Pay Per Views ever, bringing together LOOP, RSPWF and AOL feds alike. Some of the biggest organizations ever will be there, and I'm giving you the chance to get on the mailing list to receive updates, roleplays and the actual Pay Per View down the road. A date is not set yet but these are the feds that will be or most likely will be involved, check it out. * PTWL: Prime Time Wrestling League * SOW: Superstars of Wrestling NOW: Network Online Wrestling * GFWA: Grapplemaster's Fantasy Wrestling Association * PCW: Psychotic Championship Wrestling * SJPW: Super Japan Pro Wrestling * CMLL: Championship Mexican Lucha Libre EEF: European E-Wrestling Federation IEWF\LWC: Longhorn Wrestling Council SCW: Southern Championship Wrestling * RDW: Red Dice Wrestling * ECWA: East Coast Wrestling Association * EWA: Extreme Wrestling Assiciation * GEWA: Global E-Wrestling Alliance The organizations with a star to the left of their name have already agreed to participate in this once in a life time Pay Per View. The rest of them, well, are either maybes or haven't been contacted yet. Now, if you'll notice, 16 feds are set to part in this interfed card, but only 14 are listed there. That's where you come in. We're looking for either a European, Japanese or Mexican e-fed to take part in this event. If you know of one, or run one, please contact me at [detail withheld] and CC: the message to [detail withheld] . If you'd like to get on the mailing list to this Pay Per View, please just send me an e-mail stating so to [detail withheld] . And as always, have a nice night. Thanks for reading and listening. Later, Briggs [PTWL Co-Prez] Dear fifthm20, I appreciate the kind words, man, it's nice to have some encouragement. I have put you on the mailing list and I regret to inform you that we're completely full for the Pay Per View. We're overloaded with feds, we're up to 14, now. Recently, we've signed the EWA into the mix. This is the complete list so far ... * PTWL: Prime Time Wrestling League * SOW: Superstars of Wrestling * NOW: Network Online Wrestling * GFWA: Grapplemaster's Fantasy Wrestling Association * PCW: Psychotic Championship Wrestling * SJPW: Super Japan Pro Wrestling * CMLL: Championship Mexican Lucha Libre EEF: European E-Wrestling Federation IEWF\LWC: Longhorn Wrestling Council SCW: Southern Championship Wrestling * RDW: Red Dice Wrestling * ECWA: East Coast Wrestling Association * EWA: Extreme Wrestling Assiciation * GEWA: Global E-Wrestling Alliance * feds are confirmed By the way, please keep your eyes pealed for a Euro, Mexico or Japanese fed for me. It's pretty important. Thanks, Briggs
