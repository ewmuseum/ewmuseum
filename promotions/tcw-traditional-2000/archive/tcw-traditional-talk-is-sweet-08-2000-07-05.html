+-+ TALK IS SWEET +-+ Issue Number Eight July 5, 2000 Traditional Championship Wrestling Webpage: [detail withheld] -=- Table of Contents -=- [1] Rampage [2] Lance Manyon [3] Jon "Gambit" Lightning [4] Jack "the Ripper" Williams [5] Sovereign (Debut) [~]~[~] RAMPAGE [~]~[~] [The scene is set in a little resteraunt in what looks to be Glawscow,
Scotland. Three men are sitting at a table, one is recognized as
Rampage. He looks at the camera and excuses himself.] R: Excuse me for a second, please. I have an interview. [Rampage stands up, and he is wearing a t-shirt that reads "And God said
"Let there be Rage..." on the front. He is also wearing his usual kilt.
He walks out of the resteraunt, and begins to talk, while people walk by.] R: Deuce, I got to admit, you sure scared the itty bitty camera man. Oh, he really looked like he was gonna crap his trousers for a second there.
Like hell. See, Deuce, I don't care if your quotes are the "Quote of the
Week" for TCW. I don't care if you read articles on me. Hell, I don't really even care if you know my damn theme Music word for word, all I care about is getting in that ring, wrestling, and, win or lose, showing you why I am called "The man of Rage". See, you can pick on my style, you can pick on my past, and hell, you can pick on my kilt, which I wear because it is tradition based, and I'm a tradition based person. Because you can drive your 2000 cars, live in the rich Buffalo, New York (by the way, there is a rich part of), or you can yell at little camera people, it doesn't matter to me. I will show you why no one has EVER been able say that their match with me wasn't a challenge. Hell, I may not be your greatest challenge, but I will sufice, I garentee. Now, on to that
Costal Title Tourney.
[Rampage stops for a minute to take a drink of a soda he brought out with him. He looks at the camera, and smiles a seemingly harmless smile.]
R: After I show Deuce why I am called Rampage, I have a title to focus on. The Costal Title will be around my waist, maybe not off the bat, but it will be. Not even The Ninja, Jade, Willams, Malice, ANYBODY can tell me different. And that's all I have to say about that.
[Rampage then smiles, and seemingly mocking Deuce starts to yell.] R: (Mocking Deuce) Get out of my car, because I gotta ice my hemroids, and I'm big and bad, and deep down inside I'm scared sh*tless because
I'm hurt and my hemroid itch.
[Rampage starts to laugh, and walks back into the resteraunt.] FTB [~]~[~] LANCE MANYON [~]~[~] [The scene opens in an empty arena, hours after TCW's last televised program. The crowds have long left and a single figure wanders aimlessly about the base of the ring....Lance Manyon. He is adorned in a black tee which reads "TCW..Feel The Tradition" on the front and "100% MANyon" on the back in white letters. With his hands in the pockets of his blue jeans, Manyon offers a reflective expression and begins to speak as the camera slowly move in....]
LM: "Lance Manyon and TCW...the road has not been a simple one. On one hand we have the world's hottest up and coming wrestling promotion and on the other, the one of the most driven competitors to be seen in ages...seems like a natural blend doesn't it? Well the road here in Traditional
Championship Wrestling hasn't been an easy one to traverse thus far with me suffering embarassing losses to some of the weakest talents that
President Uss had the misfortune to sign. Blame it on my recovery from the bottle, blame it on a lack of focus, blame it on anything but know one thing....LANCE MANYON IS BACK."
[Manyon slowly makes his way over to a steel grey guardrail with sparkles weakly under the dim lights overhead. Manyon then looks dead into the camera and with a flaring intensity his words thunder with power...]
LM: "I've already taken a loss to Malice in this heavyweight shingdig, and Chris, I give you all the credit in the world. You were able to do what the rare few have, and thats beat me at my own game. Buy son, the war is just started. If you thinking pinning me gives you a bigger Johnson than the rest of us, I pray to the good lord himself that you take up a little hobby called facing reality. If it weren't for Serena mis-stepping in attempts to--ahem!--"help" me, your ass would've been layed out, staring into the heavens with visions of Grandma. That was only one battle, boy, and believe me when I say this, the next strike, the deathblow, will be MINE. I don't know when--it could be tomorrow, hell, later tonight---I don't know where---could be at the hotel, backstage, or when you're out on the town with one of your boyfriends--but payback is coming your way, and that my friend, is a
PROMISE. [Manyon steps even closer to the camera with eyes of fire...] LM: "And speaking of the Heavyweight Tournament, Jade, a haven't forgotten about you, not by a longshot. You are just a stepping stone in my plans to become the rightful heir to TCW's crown. It's been a long time...4 long months I've sat out, playing the sidelines, looking for a home to demonstarte my skills in. Now that I've found one there's no way in HELL
I'm letting you roadblock by destiny. See, what seperates the Manyons from the Jades of the world is the pure determination, and irrefutable desire to be the best that i can be because I know I'm just that damn good. You..heh..if it was a perfect world, you'd be pumping gas for five-fifty an hour. But alas the world--and you--are far from perfect.
I've seen punks like you before..so eager to step into the limelight that you forget your proper position in the pecking order and overstepping your boundaries, resulting in my size twelve and a half being firmly placed in your ass. Think of me if you will as a blowhard, a cocky sonuvabitch, whatever. The simple fact of the matter is this:
The Traditional Championship Wrestling World Heavyweight Title will make this man ten pounds heavier when Serena straps that sweet leather about my waist and Uss proclaims me the top dog. Jade, my time has come."
LM: "On a final note, I'd like to address all of the other Traditional
Championship Wrestling "Superstars" who are participating in the World
Title Tourney. I laugh at the Sam Marchetti's...I scoff at the Jack
Williams' and Jon Lightnings. Why? Because the future of this company has arrived...and the future is Lance Manyon. With my eyes on the prize,
I'm steamrolling through the competition and nothing---and I mean
NOTHING--is gonna stop this train!" [With the image of two searing eyes burning a hole into the screeen, the camera fades to black with the sound of a mischievious laughter...]
[~]~[~] JON "Gambit" LIGHTNING [~]~[~] [The camera pans through an empty arena, it stops at the ring, where we see Jon Lightning sitting atop a ladder in the middle of the ring. He is wearing a pair of jeans, TCW t-shirt, and black boots. He is staring down at the ring mat. He slowly raises his head towards the camera and begins to speak in quiet but determined tone...]
Jon: I'm sitting atop the ladder of success right now, climbing over all those before me to reach the most prized thing in TCW, the heavyweight title. Now this week I face you Candice in the tourny as well for the money you stole from me. You see Candice I wanted this match very badly for this is a match that will push us to our limits. We will see who can truly go beyond the standard and show TCW what this business is all about. Do you think you can set the standard Candice, I don't think you can, but we will see this saturday with $10000 on the line as well. Can you feel all the energy around this match, the boys in the back will be watching and waiting to see which one of us will climb this ladder for the win. Have you heard their saying in the back that whoever wins this match will most likely be the first champ of TCW? Can you handle all this attention or is your inner demon taking control now and the fear of being a failure setting in do you know whats going on in your head
Candice? If not I can help you figure it out after I beat you into the mat this week.
[Jon climbs down the ladder to the ring level.] Jon: I've been around the ring for a short time now but this has to be my favorite match of them all. Two men enter with one goal to climb the ladder and retrieve the prize that hangs above them, it's an epic battle. I can't wait to see you go down Candice, for then you will know my HELL! You talk of gimmicks and character but this me I spent years digging my way out of the emotional hell my family put me in do you know what it's like to walk the line of insanity? The Doctors told me that
I'm fine now so keep your two bits about my mental health to yourself you walking freak who walks in the dark playing with himself. When you wanna talk about your ability or soon to be lack their of you let me know!
[Jon is starting to shake a little he turns around towards the ladder and screams.] Jon: GoOD GOd CanDice I can feLL the PAin that I'm going TO PUT yOU throUGH On IMpaCT! [Jon starts to mumble to himself in an almost child like voice.] Jon: Gonna have to put the Candyman through the mat off the ladder. He needs to be punished for being a bad man. I uh think he's gonna be mad but bad people almost always are mad they don't know how to be happy.
[Jon starts to laugh with an almost craziness to his voice like he's they only person who understands the joke.]
Jon: Impact is coming Candice and you can no longer hide in your darkness you will have to come into the light! So be ready to go back to school. For after I hit you with the Jonton Bomb I'm going up the ladder for the cash. Later Candice you freak.
[Scene fades to black as Jon continues to talk to himself.] [~]~[~] JACK "the Ripper" WILLIAMS [~]~[~] **Questions and Answers all week long. Jack Williams has been raking his brain and he just about lost it last time we saw him. He knows he needs to prove that he is worthy of winning the title, but everyone else seems to think otherwise. Saturday on Max Impact Jack Williams hopes to answer all the questions silence all the critics and get back on track for a title** (The scene opens on an empty arena. TCW crewmembers scamper about setting up the ramp and TWC-tron. The ring is complete but the rest of the set is in shambles. From underneath the stage someone flips up a curtain and approaches the ring. It is clear that it is Williams.
He walks up to the apron and runs his hand along the mat and then jumps up. He climbs through the ropes and goes to the corner. He sits and holds his head in his hands) Williams-Well here it is...less than 48 hours away from Maximum Impact...All the questions will be answered...I have always said that questions are for the weak minded...and a few people have a lot of them...I mean do I deserve to be in the
Championship Tournament...will I win...Everyone else are wondering where there next promo appearance's will be and I am concerned about my next match...you guys have all the pressure on you from outside of the ring...and me...well I have all the pressure on me inside the ring...I am the one who has to prove to the boys in the back that I deserve this title...I am the one who has to perform...I am the one who will be called a loser and a fake if I lose...you guys lose and you'll probably get another shot...
(Williams runs his hands through his hair) Williams-You know Talamaur I applaud you, you showed who was the better of us two and I respect that...just hope we meet again and give the fans what they pay for which is the best matches around, and I can honestly say that was one of the
Best if not the best match I have ever fought in, I've watched that match over ten times now and going over and over in my mind what I could have done to make the break. But to you Talamaur I respect you.
(Williams stands up) Williams-It's ok though...I now have the chance to get my respect in the
Match against "Franchise" Chris Malice, you can talk the talk but in two days you won't walk the walk Chris Malice...I'll make sure you can't walk...You know you have been pretty impressive when I've watched you, you haven't seen me watching you but I'm there "Franchise" I'm there...and with my in ring ability I should see you over in lets say 5 minutes but no "Franchise" I will entertain the Crowd and I will make this match special. Not only is there a win at stake in the Tournament it means a whole lot more to me than that. Sure I've lost in the past but the new "Ripper" is here now and I plan to be tougher than ever before, I have only fought one wrestler in TCW and that hasn't given me all the experience I had hoped for but be ready "Franchise" to be washed away by "The Ripper" experience. (Williams gets up and walks along the ropes) Williams- You see Malice not only will this get me a win in the
Tournament but it will also get me the respect I need off the fans I have not encountered much push behind me from the crowd and I need to do so soon or they will think I am a no-hoper, and Malice that is one thing
I am not! (Williams walks to the middle of the ring)
Williams-And If you win "Franchise" then that will not be it, I will continue to haunt you and keep on coming for you until I beat you that is the Williams way never stop until your job is done, saying this there won't be need for that because the job will be done and dusted on
Saturday in front of a maximum crowd and at the end they will be cheering my name not yours you poor little rich boy.
    (Williams jumps up and sits on the turnbuckle) Williams-Saturday will be the night that I prove not only to the boys...to the crowd...to myself...that I am for real...and I am here to stay...I will not leave the stadium without a win...it's just not gonna happen... (Williams jumps down and walks off to the back. The scene fades) [~]~[~] SOVEREIGN (Debut) [~]~[~] **The lights fade and a pyro explosion echoes throughout the arena, signifying the entrance of Sovereign**   Sovereign - Are you ready for the silence?   **Deadly silence befalls the arena, the crowd eager to hear what the Dark one has to say**   Sovereign - I am what you have all been waiting for, the messiah of TCW if you will, I make my debut on this weeks 'Impact' and as the name implies i am going to make an immediate impact on all of you.....An impact you are gonna like!
  **Sovereign paces the stage area as if visualising his upcoming match in his mind**   Sovereign - I have thought long and hard about my opening match with
Switch and beleive it will be a good launch pad into the roster, ending a guys career before it begins although harsh seems the prudent course of action as it means the S.O.B cant cross my path in future broadcasts.
  **The long cape Sovereign always wears, which was originally wrapped around him is aloud to drop down showing off his ring attire**   Sovereign - I dont care much for speaking, the only reason i am here now is to inform you all of my arrival, i will do my speaking in the ring at
'Impact' when i destroy Switch launching myself into the running for the
Coastal title. So to all my newly made fans i say thank you have a safe journey home, and to switch..... MAY WHATEVER GOD YOU BELEIVE IN HAVE
