After Candyman sucessfully defeated Jade in a Heavyweight Title Tournament match on Maximum Impact, Candyman proceeded to punish Jade. Candyman went to the top rope to perform his "Candy Surprize" legdrop finisher when Jon "Gambit" Lightning jumped over the railing and pushed him off. Security had to seperate the two men from coming in contact with each other. However, this week on Maximum Impact these two men will colide one on one in the main event! !~ TALAMAUR UPSET WITH LOSS ~! Word has it that Talamaur isn't too happy about his debut loss to Jack "the Ripper" Williams on Maximum Impact. Talamaur has challenged Williams to a rematch on this weeks edition of the show, but this time it will be a Heavyweight Title Tournament match! A lot is at stake when these two colide in six days! !~ MARCHETTI SUFFERS INJURY ~! Sammy "the Bear" Marchetti suffered a dislocated shoulder after Lance Manyon moonsaulted on him on Saturday. Marchetti tried to catch the smaller Manyon but the force was too great, the move costed Marchetti the match and a week of action. Sammy will return to action in two weeks time. ×-×-×-×-×-×-×-×-×-×-× WEEKLY RECAP ×-×-×-×-×-×-×-×-×-×-× Maximum Impact 6.17.00 TCW Arena, Queens NY 4,000 Fans (Sold out) - Jack Williams d.
