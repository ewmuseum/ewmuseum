King: "Welcome to the show, great to have you." LM: "Great to be here, Lar." King: "My pleasure, Lance. First of all who is this lovely lady to you?" LM:: "This is Serena, my manager and the closest friend that i have. She handles all of my personal needs. Now before you run away with some freeky story, i mean my BUSINESS needs. I'm far from it to be honest, but my kid loves the stuff. He tells me that you struggled early on, losing to the likes of a fellow named Pash
Crackers, whom I gather isn't exactly the creme de la crem. However, the footage given me from the TCW offices show I rather sharp looking, crisp
Lance Manyon. What is the cause of this change?"
LM[shifting in his chair]: "There have been many factors which have contributed to my rebirth...better conditioning..a more focused vision..but the big change had to come by me putting down the bottle. It is well documented that I have taken 'a sip or two" every now and then.
Hell, let's keep it real, i was nothing more than a drunk, which directly led to the embarrassment suffered at the hands of Pash
Crackers. If I hadn't brushed the drinks to the side, right now I'd porbably be face down, unconscious, inhaling a pool of my own vomit. Not exactly the way I'd like to be remembered." *laughs*
King: "And what motivated you to get that mokey off of your back?" [Lance looks into serena's eyes warmly and she blushes over the fact that Manyon is about to show respect by acknowledging her..]
LM: "Wrestling." [Serena's expression instantly transforms from jubilation to one of disappoint ment mixed with a bit of rage...]
King: "Wrestling?" LM: "That's right. Wrestling. The business that MADE Manyon also SAVED
Manyon. You see Larry, after Pash walked away with prehaps the biggest upset EVER, I knew right then and there that I had hit rock bottom. The man that had headlined cards worldwide had suddenly become the laughing stick of pro wrestling. Do you think that I was immune to the whispers?
That I turned a deaf ear to the chuckles of how Manyon fell off? How Mr
PPV wasn't fit to even work dark matches? I took it all in and reality hit like a spiked bat. I had a legacy, a memory touphold. One of discipline and hardwork, but I couldn't live up to that standard being alcohol's bitch. My self dignity was at stake and I had to alter that crash course...fast."
King[reading from his papers]: "Certainly the business may have saved you, but let's not forget your contributions either. I've discovered that you've been the proud owner of no less than twelve championships in your year and a half wrestling. Definitely nothing to sneeze at. I would say that make you an odds on favorite to win TCW's current Heavyweight
Title Tournament. Do you agree?"
Serena[nonchalantly]: "Yawwwwwwwwwnnnnn." [Lance looks at her curiously and continues...] LM: "I don't mean to come across as cocky or arrogant, but theres no better man to represent this company as top dog than myself. And speaking of green, I must comment on Jade, my opponent."
[Manyon looks directly into the camera with a stern expression....] "Jade, the crime that I'm about to perpetrate, the sound trashing I'm about to deliver, it's business, nothing personal. MY event..MY time to shine..MY destiny. The future has arrived...and it's name is MANYON."
