+-+ TALK IS SWEET +-+ Issue Number Six June 29, 2000 Traditional Championship Wrestling Webpage: [detail withheld] -=- Table of Contents -=- [1] Deuce [2] "World Turner" Kristoff St. Livingstone (1) [3] Candyman [4] Lance Manyon [5] "World Turner" Kristoff St. Livingstone (2) [~]~[~] DEUCE [~]~[~] (An alarm clock sitting on a desk fades into view. The clock reads 12:42
P.M. Suddenly, a figure arises next to the clock, apparently just waking up. The man stands up, wearing a pair of old shorts and no shirt. He is pretty well built, and, from the looks of things, about 6'2, 225. He has light skin and short brown hair. As the man stretches to get woke up, he notices the camera he borrowed from the TCW for just this very occasion.)
Man: What? This damn thing's on? Oh well, I hope it caught some of the party last night, it was >SOME< party. I guess we got a little wild and made a film or something, oh well. For those of you that don't know me, and I'm sure most of you don't, being that I'm not from around where this thing's broadcasted, I'm Deuce. You may know me from past wrestling engagements I've been involved in that are now being put into syndication. Well, I'm here to say that I'm completely dedicated to this fed, at night, just don't ask me to wake up too early, cuz that just doesn't fit well with my lifestyle.
(Deuce points to the alarm clock.) This is about the normal time I wake up. I've heard some bad things about people like myself. For instance, the other day I heard on MTV
News that the President says slackers that have no education and sleep all day are killing our nation. And to the President I say...Hey, Mr.
Reagan, just because I sleep all day doesn't mean I'm killing the rest of our beloved country of El Salvador. He also said that 55% of today's youth can't even locate Colorado on the map, and to that I say...Hey, just because 55% can't locate Colorado on the map, what about the other
85%? I know this country like the back of my hand.
(Deuce holds up his hand to the camera.) Ahhhh! What the hell is that on my hand? How long has >THAT< been there? Well, anyways, on to other topics, like Traditional Championship Wrestling. I think this is the perfect fed to showcase my in-ring wrestling ability, which, by my own account, rivals that of any man alive. Bring on the Hardy Boyz, Edge Slash Christian, even the great
Chris Benoit, and I'll show them all up. I can pull off >ANY< move imaginable at any and all times. How's this for an open challenge? I dare anybody to outentertain me in a one on one, mano-a-mano match.
ANYBODY. Anybody from that fatass Sammy Marchetti to the toothpick thin
Ninja, by the way, Ninja, that's quite an original name you've got there. Don't change that name...you've really got something special there.
(Deuce reaches for the remote, turning on the TV.) Well, fans, fellow wrestlers, and even upper management of Traditional
Championship Wrestling, I'm Deuce, get used to my face, cuz you're gonna be seeing a >WHOLE< lot more of it in the future.
(Deuce reaches for an unseen button on the camera, followed by an instant fade to black.) [~]~[~] "World Turner" KRISTOFF ST. LIVINGSTONE (1) [~]~[~] [Scene opens at "Diaglo's", the finest italian bistro in all of Sistareton County, MA. Sitting by himself at a small table, is none other than TCW Superstar "The World Turner" Kristoff St. Livingstone.
Kristoff sips on a vintage red wine(I have no idea what I'm talking about), as he looks over his menu. As Kristoff decides between the
Eggplant Parmasian, or the Linguni with Clam Sauce, he's interrupted by his ringing cell phone. He picks it up, flips it open, and pulls up the antenna.]
KStL: Yes? Voice on phone: Hi, Kristoff..it's mom. KStL: Ugh...hello, mother. Can I help you with something? Kristoff's Mom: Well, can't a mother just call her son without a reason? KStL: ..... hold, please, I have another call. [Kristoff hangs up his phone, and goes back to reading his menu. His phone rings again. He answers, again.]
KStL: *sigh* Look, mother, unless my father died, or you have some OTHER good news, I really don't wish to speak to-
Voice: It's Sara, Kris. KStL: Ah...well...finally someone worth talking to. Why, may I ask, are you calling me, instead of sitting across from me? Reservations at "Diaglo's", my dear, don't come cheap. Sara: Yeah, I know. I got tied up at the dry cleaners. They overstarched your shirts. KStL: Again? UGH! Honestly, they could just bring people in from some third world country, to do a better job. Sara: Uhh...Kris? This was "Tru Foo's Happy Dry Cleaners". These >ARE< third world employees. KStL: Ah...point taken. Sara: Oh, I also got word from TCW. You make your debut next week. KStL: Ah, well about time. So, tell me, is it a heavyweight tournament match? Sara: Uh...actually..... KStL: Hmmm? Sara: They went with that OTHER privledged wrestler...Malice. KStL: What?! Oh...honest to God...why do I even bother. *sigh* So, what wonderful dissapointment will I be facing, then?
Sara: ......Ninja. KStL: He's a ninja? What's his name? Something terribly cliched, I'm sure. "Moon Shadow" perhaps? Or "Shadow Warrior". Sara: It's just "Ninja". KStL: ..........You MUST be joking. Sara: No, sadly. KStL: Ninja? Ugh...I can't even fathom the brainstorming session that took place to come up with THAT name. Sara: Kristoff, I'm gonna get moving now. I'll be there in about 10 minutes. KStL: Alright. Goodbye. [Kristoff hangs up the phone.] KStL[shaking his head]: This Malice dolt gets >MY< spot in the tournament, meanwhile I'm forced to associate with someone named
"Ninja". Ugh, let the backstage politics begin. [Scene fades out as Kristoff again resumes looking at his menu.] [~]~[~] CANDYMAN [~]~[~] [Surrounded by darkness, we come to reveal a scene in the woods. Compelled by some interior ambition, a lone cameraman has set out in the midst of a stormy night in hopes of attaining a few words from a single man. Now, with the wind rushing through the woods around us, we are held inside the playground for the TCW's most vicious mind. the Candyman's.
As the camera swivels from side to side trying desperately to find something worth capturing on television, thunder is heard in the background. An ominous cloud can be scene barely through the lucious greens above. This deep dark cloud is moving in closer and closer by each drawing second, and finally, it sets in above us and seems to almost stop. As the camera takes its attention away from the sky, and directs it more towards its immediate accompanyment. Large trees , extending from the ground to hundreds of feet in the air, are set almost side by side creating what seems to be an impenetrable wall of force against this cameraman. How did he get here? Why did he come here? Then, out of the darkness, a man appears. Barely visible with a small amount of light coming from the moonlit sky above, this man comes alone.
Isolated from the world, these two men stand together, however, set apart by different traits. Looking, perhaps ten feet into the distance, we are not able to tell any distinct features, but it is known that this is the Candyman. He comes out at night only to be ushered into the depths of darkness when the sun rises each morning. But now, he stands here, ready for what the world, or beyond, may throw at him. In his hand, is a bag...A bag that one might find at a bank, holding money....Maybe 10,000?]
---The Candyman---     Compelled by what seems to be a single notion.... Each soul upon this mortal coil we have so guiltlessly named earth, struggles, day in and day out, to merely survive. Everyone.... Whether it be myself..... Or perhaps Noferatu. Each man has weaknesses that cause he or she to fall at any given time. These failures cause the struggle to survive.... For if no one striuggled and everyone thrived..... Life, would be meaningless.
[Still held behind closed doors by the darkness, he stands motionless, while the wind can be heard in the background.]
---The Candyman--- For the years I have lived my life, I have all too often been held subject to hate.... lies..... betrayal..... fear...... and pain......
This holds true for all other men as well.... But what sets me apart from the rest of my species is the fact that I endure it all, and top it off with something more.
  I cause each and every emotion of pain you all feel. I bring forth the hatred that burns deep within each mortal soul that steps into and out of each and every TCW arena. And without me..... There is no Jon
'Gambit' Lightning.
[The man of the darkness can now be seen moving.... Closer..... Closer until he is nearly face to face with the camera, and then, as quicky as he came upon the cameraman, he moves past him, still talking.]
---The Candyman--- Virtue....... By definition itself, means the upmost importance and gift you possess. The ability to stay true to one's causes and hold upon them in all circumstances..... It says nothing about doing what is right in the eyes of the public. It says nothing about doing what others believe you should do. It tells you to stay true to your cause...... Jesus
Christ, a myth, perhaps, that stayed true to his beliefs, and is remembered in most hearts for it.... Was he really a soul who walked this earth, who moved as we do, talked as we do?   That subject is not up for discussion..... But the fact remains.... Jesus stayed with himself, and his beliefs and was persecuted for it not being the popular thing...... You all, those non-symbolic fans that I put in the seats, and I take your money.... You all are persecuting me..... And the only, only difference between myself.... And the man many of you call your savior is the one uncontrollable fact that haunts each and every one of you.....So is that why I took your money, Jon 'Gambit' Lightning?....
[The Candyman turns around and the only distinct feature that appears on his face is a smile.]
---The Candyman--- I love the persecution you set upon me.... I love the hatred that fills in your eyes when you see me.. I love the fire that consumes your heart each time you talk about me. I love the thirst for blood every time your mind sets upon me. I cause it, I thrive off of it, I live it. Day after day, you set me upon that cross and drive those painful thoughts into me.... But I endure it, because that is what I live off of. You feed me the fuel that I need for the fire that's burning and burns, and fills with desire to slit your throat and watch your blood spill onto the floor in an enormous amount..... For I have returned. Jesus once came to save this hell-hole. He was a fool for doing so.... I have come to ensure each and every soul alive falls fast and arrives in hell. Your fate rests upon many decisions in your life. Judgement has come.
[He stops, turns his head back around to face the clouds.] ---The Candyman---   However, recently, it has come to my attention, that in the next few days, I have been set against my rival, yet another man sits in front of me come Saturday..... Noferatu. Now Noferatu, your soul escaped a hellish position a couple of weeks ago, and you shall not do so again.
In my first match in months, I proved that I could go with the tops in this sport, and keep it a sport..... However, now, failure is no longer an option..... Your life does not hang in the balance, for I care not about you.... But rather, about Jon Lightning. The man who did do well against me last Saturday. While when I reversed his hold, it wasn't me that slid over too far, as I am a wrestler from Penn State, I know exactly how far to turn with a guy of your weight, Gambit, but you, and you alone were responsible for turning over a little further, so that we were both down and entagnled in one another to the point of not letting go, fearing the other would gain an advantage. I thought that, and you did to. We played a deadly game of chicken, Lightning, waiting for each other to break the hold, roll over and continue onwards. But we both wanted to win so we stayed like we did waiting for the other to move first...1...No movement.....2......Further yet we would try to hold it out to the last possible moment....Right before the referee hit three, you did attempt to break the hold...And so did I. But, we were just a little too late, and before we could unlock the other the ref had already rung our fates in..........3.....It looks so fast from the video replay, but yet when I was theere, in that moment in Trenton, New
Jersey, it went by so slowly..... [All of a sudden, he kneels down, much like a catcher in baseball, and runs his fingers through the dirt below. Now, not too far away, lightning strikes and thunder is heard only a few seconds afterward.]
---The Candyman--- But now the next opponent is Noferatu...come Saturday.... It shall only be a preview to what occurs in the following months for you and the rest of this place..... The battleground for us.... For the never-ending battle between Heaven and Hell........ Who shall claim victory in our one epic event? Time shall tell the world soon enough, but come
Saturday, your soul shall rest in another grave.... The grave the fans have layed for you, as well as the same one you have layed for yourself.....
[All of a sudden...] (--(Crack)--) [Lightning strikes, and rain begins to fall... But through it all, the
Candyman continues to talk....]
---The Candyman--- The gods above have grown angry with me.... For I plan to show the truth.... The truth that haunts them, the truth they refuse to show the world..... Satan knows the truth and sent me, his apostle, as well as a specified group of other men to ensure truth be told..... Your life
Noferatu, shall be put in waiting for the God's above..... For come to......
[All of a sudden, sparks fly in front of the camera, and it cuts to black immediately.] [~]~[~] LANCE MANYON [~]~[~] [The scene opens in an arena packed with 15,000 sedated TCW fans at a house show in Fayetteville , North Carolina. Various signs are scattered among them which read "TCW—Wrestling at it's Finest!", "Sam Marchetti
Sux" and "Malice is Da Man!" and a chant of BOORRR-RINGGGGGG begins to echo throughout as two jobbers, El Tigre and Loco, battle exchange armbars for seven minutes. Suddenly, without warning, the opening strands of "Cracking the Surface" blares and the people in attendance rise to their feet in jubilation! Lance Manyon emerges from the entranceway in a black TCW tee shirt and black jeans, and the ever-lovely Serena stands proudly next to him in a matching one piece mini. The pair look out over their screaming fans as Manyon removes a mic from his back pocket...]
LM: "Fayetteville, North Carolina...make a lil noise in this bitch!" [Huge POP! from the fans] LM: "That's the kind of ovation that the TCW crowds should be giving, not chants of boredom inspired by these two 'bronies in the ring. I'd probably be fending the pair off of they understood a lick of English, but hey, thats on them! Now many of you by now know that Serena here has entered me into the TCW Heavyweight Title tournament devised by
President Uss to determine a champion. Well seeing as how I'm the only
MANyon amongst boys, word on the stret is that I'm the odds on favorite to blow thru that baby and walk out with an extra 10 lbs of pure gold strapped about my waist..and i couldnt agree more! Look at this roster..where are the challenges? Half of these guys should be working in indy bingo halls much less battling me in a tournament to crown a champion...and speaking of which, lets move onto Chris Malice. Malice,
BOY, I don't know what hole you crawled out of, but i suggest crawling back to avoid me putting one of my gators firmly and securely in your ass! Ever since I tossed the bottle aside, I have been the MAN with no help from anyone at all!"
[Serena looks at Lance oddly, eyebrow raised..] LM: "I overcame my demons all by myself.....I am responsible for mowing thru opponent after opponent in TCW....so when you see me standing alone in the ring at only 6'2, 227lbs, don't believe that you can easily roll over me! I'm a one man wrecking crew, and you--heh--if it werent for this tourny, i wouldnt even know your ass was on the roster! And remember that nothing--and i mean NOTHING-- is gonna stop this train!"
[~]~[~] "World Turner" KRISTOFF ST. LIVINGSTONE (2) [~]~[~] [Scene opens along the side of a desolate road. Pulled off onto the shoulder is a large, black luxary car. The hood is propped up, and smoke is bellowing out of it. A finely dressed man, in standard chaffeur threads, stands off to the side, puzzled look on his face. Leaned back against the rear of the car, is "The World Turner" Kristoff St.
Livingstone. Kristoff, as usual, wears a look of disgust. He holds his cell phone close to his ear.]
KStL: I don't know where the hell I am! Send some.....what? 3 hours? Oh, you most certainly better be kidding! No, I refuse to be in this state for one moment longer than I have to. I'll simply call a ca...hello?
[Kristoff twiddles with his phone. He tries to get it to work, to no avail.] KStL: Excuse me.....driver? Driver: Yes...sir? KStL: Didn't I specifically ask you to make sure my cell phone was charged? Driver: Actually, no. No you didn't, sir. KStL: Oh.....well, that's because I figured it would be a waste of breath. It SHOULD be common knowledge to charge my cell phone before such a long trip to such a God awful place.
Driver: Sir, I'm most definately- KStL: Fired? Yeah, you are. Find your own ride home. [Kristoff walks down the road, leaving the Driver alone with a broken down car, and no means of communication.]
KStL: They just hire people off the streets nowadays, I swear.... [Kristoff walks for a while, before a large mass transit bus approachs him. It stops, and the double doors flip open, revealing a smiling bus driver.]
Bus Driver: Howdy! Care for a lift? KStL: In THAT death trap? Thanks, I'd rather take my chances with the wild....smog monsters...or whatever lurks in this dreary place.
Bus Driver: Hmmm, well I suggest you hop aboard. There's nothing but nothing for miles ahead.
KStL: ......... Bus Driver: C'mon! No charge even. KStL: Ugh! You would actually EXPECT me to pay for a ride on this infernal machine? I was thinking the exact opposite. Bus Driver: You coming, or not? KStL: *sigh* FINE! [Cut to Kristoff sitting rather miserably near the back of the bus. He's surrounded by several locals. They're not exactly Kristoff's kind of people.]
KStL: This is horrible. I'm going to have to bathe for hours to get rid of the MEMORY alone, of riding this God forsaken, dullard-mobile. [A rather slovenly, obviously mentally-ill man sits across from Kristoff, and sets his gaze upon him, unbroken.] KStL: You're going to eat me aren't you, you big dumb animal? [The man says nothing, just keeps his gaze fixed on Kristoff.] KStL: Ugh...people like you should NOT be allowed out of cages, let alone on [shudder] public transportation. Do me a favor, and choke on a chicken bone. Or a cat bone. Or whatever a moron like you eats.
[The bus ride continues. The bus actually becomes quite occupied. So occupied that people have to stand and hold onto hand rails, until a seat becomes available. Proper busriding ettiquette says if an elderly person has no where to sit, a gentlemen offers her or him his seat.]
KStL: *sigh* [An elderly woman, stands next to Kristoff, holding on to the hand rail with one hand, and tightly grasping her cane with the other.]
KStL: Excuse me...ma'am? Elderly Woman: Oh....thank y- KStL: I'd appreciate it if you stood somewhere else, please. No offense intended of course, but I think you need to change your adult diaper.
Elderly Woman: Wha-?! KStL: You heard me, poodle head....go stick your soiled "undergarment" in the face of someone less.....[looks around at the other bus riders]....like me. Take your choice, you have a bus full of dim-wits and feebs, like this one [points at the large, gazing man] right here.
[The old lady limps over across the aisle to someone less....like Kristoff.] KStL: I hope my parents don't live to be that age. Ah, I'll just put them in a home anyway. Or the streets. Either way, fine by me. Random bus rider: Excuse me, but you're terribly rude. Who do you think you are? KStL: Eh? Let me pose that same question to you. Who do you think YOU are, addressing me in such a manner? I'm Kristoff St. Livingstone. It would be wise of you to remember that.
[An awkward silence falls over the section of the bus. It's broken up by a complaining Kristoff.]
KStL: Are we there yet? If I have to spend one more moment on this thing with [said derisively] these people, I'm afraid I'm going to start losing teeth, and gaining weight....[looks at the heavy-set gazer] and becoming feebish. Ugh...
[Fade out as the bus continues on it's journey.] © 2000 Traditional Championship Wrestling
