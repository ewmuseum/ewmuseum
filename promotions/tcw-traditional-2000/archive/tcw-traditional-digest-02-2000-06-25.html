/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\ TCW TRADITIONAL DIGEST \/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/ +-+ Traditional Digest Issue #2 +-+ TCW Website: [detail withheld] =-= Table of Contents =-= [1] President's Piece [2] News [3] Weekly Recap [4] Maximum Impact 6.24.00 Line-up [5] Current Roster [6] Coming & Going ×-×-×-×-×-×-×-×-×-×-×-×-× PRESIDENT'S PIECE ×-×-×-×-×-×-×-×-×-×-×-×-× I hope you all enjoyed last nights Maximum Impact, and by the looks of the survey results, you did. But now, another installment of this fine fine program in upon us. This week we have two big Heavyweight Title
Tournament matches on the show as well as a double debut match from some of TCW's younger talent.
I would like to point out that there is an on-line strat sheet available for your convience on the TCW homepage. Two of you took advantage of this for last nights card. Remember, you don't have to send in a strat but they are recomended! I'm hoping we can get more people to start using them. And while we are on the subject, you can send in-rings to me as well. These could be the difference between a win and a loss. If
Candyman or Lightning would have sent me an in-ring, the result of their match may have been a little different. Do you see what I am saying?
Well, thats enough ranting for this week... ~ President Uss ×-×-×-×-× NEWS ×-×-×-×-× !~ MALICE ATTACKS ~! Moments after Lance Manyon defeated Life in the Maximum Impact opening match, "Franchise" Chris Malice blindsided the master of the Lance
Crusher. Chris Malice has been added as the final man in the Heavyweight
Title tournament, and a match has been signed this week between the two for Maximum Impact!
!~ WILLIAMS/TALAMAUR PART TRES?! ~! Jack "the Ripper" Williams and Talamaur will meet once more this week on
Maximum Impact. Neither man has been able to get rid of the other. One week ago, Williams pinned Talamaur in the opening match. Last night,
Talamaur returned the favor by pinning Williams in a tournament match.
This week is the rubber match, but will it be their final match? !~ CANYMAN TAKES MONEY AND RUNS ~! There was $10,000 at stake in last nights main event. Neither man was able to win the match, but Candyman still left with the money. Candyman is going to have to face Lightning again in the near future as both men are in the Heavyweight Title tournament, and don't be surprised if the money comes into play!
