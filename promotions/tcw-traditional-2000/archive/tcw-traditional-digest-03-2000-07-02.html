I would like to thank [name withheld] once again for writing the
Candyman/Noferatu match. He will be helping out every week with the matches. If you would like to write also, drop me a line! I could always use the extra help! You would only have to write one match a week or every other depending on how you want to. It would also be interesting to see who's matches get what ratings when the surveys are sent out.
Also, a little match writing couldn't hurt your career in TCW :-) One other thing about the card, I noticed that people want to see more interviews. What do you want me to do about it? Writing in-rings for the cards could be very important and could determine who wins and who loses. On the flip side, I did get a lot of strats in this week. I think at least one for every match, so keep that up! In-rings are your friends
too :-P This weeks card is STACKED! Everybody currently on the roster is in a match! Six huge matches plus our first dark match! For those of you who don't know, a dark match is a match that takes place before the show goes on the air. The match will be in recaped form (not written out) but it will still count on their records, unlike house shows.
~ President Uss ×-×-×-×-× NEWS ×-×-×-×-× !~ MAXIMUM IMPACT GETS BIGGER ~! TCW had a one month experimental deal with Maximum Impact to see how it would go over. Now that the one month has passed, and Impact is sucessful it will stay on syndicated programming. However, for the first time this week, Maximum Impact is going from one to two hours! Thats a whole another hour!
!~ MARCHETTI UPSET WITH RECORD ~! Returning from a shoulder injury this week, Sammy "the Bear" Marchetti suffered a loss to Jon Lightning in a Heavyweight Title Tournament match on Impact. Marchetti is a great talent but has yet to win a match here in TCW. He has promised that things are going to start turning around.
What could he have up his sleave? !~ LADDER MATCH SIGNED THIS WEEK ~! The first gimmick match in TCW will take place this week on Maximum Impact as Jon "Gambit" Lightning takes on Candyman in a $10,000 ladder match. Not only does the winner get the money, but he gets a win in the
Heavyweight Title Tournament! All the marbles are on the line this week! ×-×-×-×-×-×-×-×-×-×-× WEEKLY RECAP ×-×-×-×-×-×-×-×-×-×-× Maximum Impact 7.1.00 Nickel Lounge - Dover Deleware 764 Fans - Candyman d.
