The yells of the man selling cotton candy can be heard because it is so quiet in the arena. Then suddenly all the lights go out in the arena and it is pitch black. Blue spotlights hit the crowd and roam the arena until "Spleen" by Staind begins to blast over the
P.A....~
"CAN'T BREEEEEEEEEEATH.... SHUT UP!..... SHUT UP!!" ~The spotlights go directly to the entranceway as Jonny "Stellar" Samson steps out for his first appearance at a TCW show. The crowd doesnt recognize him but cant seem to help but to be bedazzled by his entrance.
He walks down to the ring and steps in while his music keeps blasting...~ "I dont look like YOU, do the things you DO, but im F*CKED UP TOO! I dont Need YOU!" ~He gets into the ring and signals for his music to be cut and it stops while he grabs a mic from the ring announcer. He paces around the ring and begins to speak....~
"Hello loyal fans of TCW how are you all doing on this fine evening? I'm sure you must be having a good time I mean.. this is TCW!"
~The crowd goes nuts and cheers in agreement~ "But anyways, I am Jonny "Stellar" Samson. I just signed a temporary contract to have a match or two to decide if I'm good enough for the big boys. Ha! good enough? I am better. And once I beat the living crap out of this Carnage fella saturday at Maximum Impact in a dark match, you will all see that I am not lying when I say I am the best, I am the
Stellar One! I think all of you hard working fans deserve to finally see a good old fashion beating which is what I plan to give you this saturday."
~The crowd, happy with what they have heard, cheer louder and begin chanting "Stellar, Stellar"~
"Let me tell all of you about myself. I am from the great state of
Illinois and the great city... well....the city of Calumet City. I dont like to talk too much about my past. All I will tell you that it has given me the wonderful gift of being able to not care about myself and put my own body on the line, do whatever it takes to get what I want.
And what I want, long term, is the TCW World Heavyweight Championship! And if I have to risk my life to get it, well then so be it. I will go through any kind of matches. You name it. Barb Wire matches, Japanese
Death matches, even a Carnage's Fat Momma Match! Anyway you want it I will do it! That is my long term goal. As far as my short term goal, kicking Carnage's teeth in, well I will just have to wait till Saturday for that. But I can asure you that Carnage will leave there wondering......well......actually he won't be wondering. It's kinda hard to do any kind of thinking at all when you are laying unconscious on the cold cement floor after getting the Stellar Driver! Thank you for your time ladies and gentleman. Have a wonderful evening!"
~The crowd cheers very loudly as "Spleen" plays over the P.A. again and
Samson  leaves the ring, slapping the hands with fans as he makes his way back to the lockerroom.~
[~]~[~] CANDYMAN [~]~[~] [The camera opens inside the basement of the Candyman's house. And, predictably, there is no light....The Candyman turns one on though, just enough to give a background for what he has to say. There is no outside noise, just a TCW cameraman, fumbling around in the dark and the
Candyman.] [The Candyman, from what we can see, is wearing a dark T-shirt. Darkness surrounds his lower body. His short brown hair is on his head and there is no facial hair to be seen, unlike the last segment. His eyes are dark. His mouth is very stern, and ready to talk...]
---The Candyman--- You know, once in every person's lifetime, not just the brave, and the valiant, there comes a time when the life from their very bodies is exterminated...Their lease on life is done. Once, not twice...Until now.
---The Candyman--- Jon Lightning, you have escaped the very pits of darkness and a reality of pain and suffering...You see, in my previous three victories, I had never encountered much until you came along. And somehow, someway, you found it, a way to postpone the meeting with your maker...Clint Steel, where is he now? Probably in one of those old people's homes where they stick vegetables, no longer good for anything. I ended his career all by myself and can and will do it to anyone.....Including you.
[There is a brief pause. The Candyman stops to let everything sink in. In the death of darkness, a bird is heard chirping from the outside. The
Candyman smiles vaguely and gives a chuckle at the resilience of the feathered animal.]
---The Candyman--- Because, Jon Lightning, even someone so 'great' as you can only escape for so long. Your new time for Armageddon is in the year of our Lord,
2000, July the 8th....For that night, in the main event of TCW's Maximum
Impact, you are committing suicide....Career suicide...After I beat someone, look how much good is done to their career, Jade hasn't been heard of since I destroyed him a couple of weeks ago, Clint Steel is gone, Noferatu isn't even in the second hour of the show!
[A laugh by the Candyman, laughing at the misfortunes that he has caused to other people's lives. But then, as soon as his face lightens up, it falls back, like the darkness that surrounds him, into a quiet, serious tone.]
---The Candyman--- No one is ever the same after I am through with them....No one except you, Lightning. And trust me, you were the lone one to get away, everyone slips now and again, but let me assure you that the Candyman has slipped for the last time....Just look what's at stake. First and foremost the Heavyweight Title Tournament. The winner of this match picks up two more points and takes atleast a tie for first place overall, while the loser falls into a dead heat with three or four other mid-carders...10,000 dollars. Five grand is mine, five K is yours...There is no better motivation than money...And gold...Also, unofficially, the winner of this match, which should be the last one in our feud will be counted on to lead the TCW, and be the favorite to win the tourney itself....From Day One, I was in this tournament to win it, and it was created for me to take home the gold plated belt. Jon
Lightning, I want to know this, do you have what it takes to beat me? This is not a question that I , or anyone else can answer, you must search for this yourself....Because I have seen your total effort, that
'thing,' whatever it is that separates the good from the truly great...You thought you had it, but you couldn't beat me. I wasn't trying as hard as I could have, no maybe I should restate that, I wasn't using everything I had, like you were. I could have gone ten, twenty, hell, even an hour longer if needed. You, haha, Jon, you and I both know it, you were spent. I had you right where I wanted you but I miscalculated you and Jon you made it a double pin.
[The Candyman stops, as if it were a crime to think of his shoulders being down on the mat. Then, he begins to talk again.]
---The Candyman--- So you postponed it....But not even you can run forever, GambitMan. There is something looming heavy on your mind....A secret, so deep, so intense that it is literally driving you crazy! I can see it. It is very obvious, Jon, that you are suffering from a nervous condition. But please, don't worry just yet, it is well documented...Unfortunately there is no cure for being scared of the Candyman. So stop searching so long and so hard to find it, would you Mr. Lightning?
[And suddenly, a beam of light enters the scene...Just a single strand of yellow that dives down at an angle onto one side of the Candyman's face. He tries to act oblivious to it, but does know it's presence as he continues.]
---The Candyman--- There is so much at stake Saturday night. This bout has been called such things as 'the most important match in TCW history' and the winner of this match has a very good chance of winning the Heavyweight title, in everyone's mind. Certainly, whoever wins, me or Lightning will be the man with a target on, the man that others want to seek and attempt to destroy. There is no way that the Candyman and Jon Lightning can live up to the expactations that have been given to them....They are too big.
And it's just gonna be an old fashioned beating. Nothing about it. Just the Candyman taking another crony to the woodshed. Just as a beam of light sometimes penetrates through darkened defenses, a pesky wrestler sometimes pulls of a double pin....But sooner or later....
[The Candyman stops in mid sentence and stands up. In his hand is a roll of duct tape, black. There is a small crack in his 'defense' and he quickly tapes it up. the sound of the adhesive snaps through the surronding area. It can be heard distinctively. Then, once more is semi-darkness, the Candyman sits back down and turns his attention back to the cameraman.]
---The Candyman--- Sooner or later it is all taped back into place....And that's what I am planning on doing to you, Lightning, this Saturday night. Putting you back into place, and putting the TCW, The Candyman's Wrestling, back where it belongs......In the dark...
---The Candyman--- Two men. One ladder. Something has got to give and you must get the feeling that there will be no backing down from anyone...And that leads to blood.....Lots of it, spilled throughout the ring. Plan on that.
Then, that stairway to hell that will seemingly never end. It is the longest fifteen feet in the world, trying to climb up a ladder, when wrestling a match with an opponent under you, trying to grab that title....Or money. This match though, will determine the title. Whoever wins this one will win the TCW Heavyweight title. And I guarantee to you, my people, that I will be the first TCW Heavyweight champion. That is a promise......So I guess that means I have to win the ladder match this Saturday night, doesn't it? So it is set....
[A brief pause.] ---The Candyman--- It is set...Haha. [A mad laugh by the Candyman.] ---The Candyman--- Everything comes to he who waits....And I have waited for such a very long time....That is all that I have to say to you now, please turn the camera off....
[The scene, already in half-darkness, fades to a more complete black.] [~]~[~] CARNAGE (Debut) [~]~[~] [Carnages theme music hits the arena and the man that everyone has been waiting for walks to the ring. He steps inside and takes the microphone off the ring announcer.]
  "My name is Carnage, for those of you who don't know me, you will soon because I am gonna kick everyone in this whole federations ass. Starting with Stellar Samson, with a name like that he belongs on the street corner not in the ring with me.
   I've come to this federation for one reason, and thats to be this best damn fighter this federation has ever seen seen. This quest begins with Stellar Samson in a dark match at Impact. I have read that I must prove my worthiness to earn a spot in the coastal title tournament. Well once I have completely decimated the rookie Samson I shall take the spot that is rightfully mine and then go on to win the title.
  Each and every one of you shall soon feel what the Massacre does before
I pin your sorry asses.So all of you, prepare because CARNAGE HAS
ARRIVED!!!!!"   [Carnage then leaves the ring and walks to the backstage as the fans chant his name.] [~]~[~] JADE [~]~[~] ~~ The Scene opens up back stage outside of Jades locker room. Deuce: Yeah, I got to do a little shopping, feel free to follow me around, and record everything I say. Yep, gotta have these.

Deuce: SNAP INTO A SLIM...ah, screw it! I never liked that commercial anyway. Now Rampage, you seem to not be very bright.
Let's review some of the things you said about me in your last interview. And I quote, "Because you can drive your 2000 cars, live in the rich Buffalo, New York" -Rampage. Deuce: I can't find these ANYWHERE! And Rampage, if I lived in Buffalo, why would I have a hotel room? Who lives in hotels?
