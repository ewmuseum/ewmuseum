TCW — Card

Opening Festivities — Day One

9 June 2000
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
+-+ TCW Opening Festivities - Day One +-+ June 9, 2000 Traditional Championship Wrestling Webpage: [detail withheld] [It is prime time on a Friday night, you are passing through the channels when you hear...]
Voice Over: Lance Manyon .. Sammy Marchetti .. Noferatu .. Jack Williams
. Candyman .. Talamaur .. Clint Steel .. Jade .. These are the superstars of Traditional Championship Wrestling and tonight the festivities will begin!
[We are now taken inside a fairly sized room, headed by a podium with about 50 chairs to boot. The room is empty accept for two men who are donning suits and mics, obviously the announcers for todays events. The older man on the left begins to speak.]
"Explosive" Rex Dynamo: Welcome ladies and gentlemen to your first ever taste of Traditonal Championship Wrestling! I'm "Explosive" Rex Dynamo and with me tonight and always "Smashin'" Vance Cracken!
"Smashin'" Vance Cracken: [sarcastically] Thanks for that great introduction, Rexy! RD: As I'm sure you can see behind us, in about twenty minutes time
President Jon Uss will make his first appearance and address any issues that may come up in a very special press conference!
VC: And you know that the press has a lot of things to question on TCW! RD: Right now however, we are heading out to the local beach not far away from this location where we understand Lance Manyon has something to say!
[..Cut..] [The scene cuts to a hot, sun-drenched beach where crowds of tight, tanned bodies absorb rays, play valleyball, and dive into the ocean waters. Sitting in a beach chair with a pair of Oakley shades, a
Hawaiian print shirt, cargo shorts, and sandals is crack TCW reporter,
"Supreme" Stan Smooth. Next to Stan in his own beach chair is Lance
Manyon, wearing a black and aqua WFWA logo shirt [which is blurred out by the TCW cameracrew], black jogging pants, and Nikes. In Manyon's hand is a brown paper bag from which he sips from occasionally. As the camera slowly zoom in on the pair, Stan Smooth begins the interview with a smirk and some sarcasm....]
SS: "Here we are boys and girls live from Traditional Championship
Wrestling's Opening Weekend Festival with a man who's looking to make a big splash in the federation, Lance Manyon. Lance, I would say that it's good to finally meet you but that oh-so-lovely 40 ounce of yours makes me *so* jealous."
LM[ bit tipsy w/ a goofy smile]: "If ya allz could me be me, ya would." SS: "Right. I'm curious...about the tee shirt... whats up with that? I mean, what are you thinking?"
LM[pointing at shirt]: "This? Well, Rena says dat I should focus on the glory days, y'know,when I was on top of the world, n' stuff. I don't know what she means tho....I think I'm still pretty much the man, don't you? Uh...by the way, did I offer you some of this? I maybe alotta
---URP-- things, but rude aint one of em." SS: "No thanks, maybe later. I've already had my share of malt liquor for the day." LM[guzzling the beer]: "To each his own." SS: "So tell me about these "glory days." I'm positive that the TCW audience is wondering about your wrestling background."
LM: "Like I said, when i was in dat federation out west, I was DA MAN. I was the Ultimate champion....3 time world champion, US champ, and I also took home that dinky lil' TV strap. I purty much ran the joint and everyone there knew their roles, to use a overused catchphrase."
SS: "I'm glad to see that you're so humble." LM[grinning]: "Keeps me grounded." SS: "This fed out west, was that your only professional gig?" LM: "Naw, worked a few shows on the indy circuit, but when I saw the amount of moolah that the big boys toss around, I jumped ship with the quickness."
SS: "Well, things certainly won't be a cakewalk here in TCW. President
Uss runs a tight ship and believes in showcasing real wrestling not the sports entertainment crap you see crammed down America's throats on
Monday Nights. Do you beleive that you are capable of performing at such a high level week after week?"
LM: "Lemme tell ya something, Stan---" SS: "Excuse me, that's SUPREME Stan." LM: "Well, STAN, I have been in Iron Man matches...I can drop a pinpoint bridging suplex on a dime...I can do it all. As long as it aint hardcore."
SS: "Ah, a chink in the armor." LM[slightly irritated]: "No BLEEPhole, I just find no joy in knockin some poor joker out with a cookie sheet. Where's the art in dat? Where's the beauty in dat? Where's the skill in dat?"
SS: "Lance, with that attitude you should fit in well with this organization...once you put the bottle down. "
LM: "Whaddaya sayin'? You callin' me a drunk????" SS: "Look at yourself,do I really need to say anything?" [Lance leans back into his beachchair with a smile and takes a sip of the brew, thinking that he's just received a compliment. But after a few seconds he wisens up...]
LM: "HEY! What the hell is dat supposed to mean? Listen here "Stan".....get me upset one more time and I'll superkick you back to calling matches in barnyards for ya five bucks a hour! Your career'll be over just that fast! In fact this interview is over!"
[Manyon covers the camera lens with is hands as the scene fades to black...] [Cut back to Dynamo and Cracken who have made their way into the main hallway of the building.]
VC: Look at that drunk! Is he in any condition to wrestle at all? RD: He is actually set for action today... lets just hope he can clean himself up by match time.
VC: Do you actually think for one second that he could actually sober up within the hour? RD: Of course not, but lets hope he wises up. He could have a real bright future here in TCW if he doesn't mess it up!
VC: No doubt, but I think he already has. And what is with this WFWA crap? RD: I guess he's paying his respects to past glory! VC: This _IS_ TCW, right? RD: Now fans, its time for some wrestling action! We are going to head back out to the beach where there has been a ring constructed for TCW!
[..Cut..] [Seemingly center on the beach is indeed the TCW ring; with a blue mat, black ropes and turnbuckles, and the TCW logo streaming down each side of the ring covering up whatever may be underneath.]
[The voice of Rex Dynamo can now be heard as the announce team will be calling this match from back at the building.]
RD: This will be the debut match for Jack "the Ripper" Williams as he takes on local wrestler, Kake!
VC: Our first taste of TCW, yummy! RD: Take it away ring announcer "Lord" Richard O'Riley! [We can see Richard stepping into the ring with a couple of 3 x 5 cards in hand. In the near background we can see what looks to be Kake warming up.]
RO: Welcome ladies and gentlemen to day one of the opening festivities! This contest is set for one fall with a fifteen minute time limit and is the inaguaral TCW match-up!
[The small crowd that has gathered begins to clap.] RO: Introducing first, currently in the ring to my left.. he is from
Brooklyn, New York [mild pop from the hometown] and he weighs in at two hundred and three pounds, K A K E!
[Kake raises both arms in the air and receives a miminal reaction. He is wearing a purple singlet with yellow lightning bolts all around it.]
RO: His opponent... [Tubular Bells can now be heard playing over the sound system that has been set up on the beach as O'Riley continues.]
RO: He comes to us from London, England and he weighs in at two hundred ninety pounds, here is J A C K "THE RIPPER" W I L L I A M S!
[Williams begins to make his way through the sand to the ring area as he receives about an even ammount of boos and cheers from the crowd.
Williams is sporting black spikey hair as well as a long black trenchcoat and sunglasses.] VC: Man, this Williams looks like the Terminator! [Williams climbs up the ring steps and steps between the middle and top ropes before he takes his sunglasses revealing black eyes. He proceeds to take off his trenchcoat also showing his wrestling gear which consists of black pants with "THE RIPPER" written down each side of the legs and black boots.]
RD: Kake is giving up almost ninety pounds and 10 inches on Williams! [The bell rings signaling for the start of the match.] RD: And we are underway with the very first match in TCW history! VC: This is going to be one to remember! [Kake charges at the bigger Williams only to meet a big boot to the bridge of the nose.] RD: Kake made a major mistake by going right into Williams and paid for it with a boot to the face!
VC: And that isn't a wrestling boot either, that was a Lugz right in the face of Kake! [Kake holds his nose in pain as Williams bends over to pick up the fallen wrestler. Williams backs Kake up into the corner and repeatedly elbows him in the back of the head. Kake, not being able to take the punishment slumps down in the corner.]
RD: Kake looks really out of this match! VC: What did you expect? He's in there with a TCW wrestler, the best in the world! [Williams grabs the left arm of Kake and begins to shoulderblock it three or four times. He thens spins it around and drops all of his weight on it.]
RD: Williams with an arm breaker right into the mat! ["The Ripper" drops a leg across the back of the head of Kake and makes a cover.] Ref: One.. Two.. Thr VC: Williams picked Kake up! It would have been all over... I love it! [Williams whips Kake into the ropes and attempt a clothsline on the rebound but Kake ducks and comes back the other way. He leaps at a stunned Williams and catches him with a cross body block.]
Ref: One.. Two.. RD: Kake barely gets a two but he sure did surprise Williams with that move! [Williams and Kake both get to their feet at the same time and Kake slides under the legs of Williams. Kake jumps on the shoulders of
Williams and attempts a victory roll but Williams simply falls back and
Kake crashes into the mat.]
RD: The experience of Williams paid off on that one, he knew where he was and stoped a possible pin attempt.
[Williams scoops the lifeless Kake off the mat and powerslams him over.] VC: Kake's second wind has come and gone, he doesn't stand a chance in there anymore. RD: I might have to agree with you on this one! [Williams once again scrapes Kake off the mat and kicks him in the stomache causing Kake to bend over in pain. Williams sets him up for a suplex...]
RD: Suplex coming up! [Williams holds Kake up in the air for about five seconds before spinning him around and connecting with a DDT.]
RD: THE BIG RIPPER! What an awesome move! That was his finisher! Ref: One.. Two.. Three! RO: Here is your winner, in 4:06, JACK "THE RIPPER" WILLIAMS! RD: Williams proves that he is going to be a force to be rekoned with here in TCW! VC: We'll see, I'm still kind of tossed up on whether I like this guy or not. RD: Let's head back inside where the press conference is about to start! [The scene cuts back to the conference room that was shown at the top of the hour. The only difference now is that all of the chairs have been filled with media and "Supreme" Stan Smooth has made his way to the podium.]
SS: First off, I want to thank you all for coming here today to the grand opening of Traditional Championship Wrestling. With that said, I would like to introduce the CEO and President of this fine company, Jon
Uss!
[Uss stands up from a long stretched table aside of the podium and shakes the hand of Stan Smooth before Smooth sits down.]
JU: Thank you Stan, welcome to the TCW press conference. Who wants to start? [A young woman in her late twentys raises her hand.] JU: Yes mam... Reporter #1: Hello, my name is Martha Silvia from the Washington Times. Many times in the past we have seen the presidents of organizations appear on television and get involved in the action. Will you be taking any part in this?
JU: I will never appear on camera for anything besides events such as these. I will never be involved in any of the action. The spotlight will always be on the wrestlers, and not the staff. [pointing to another woman] Yes...
Reporter #2: I'm Amy Durnst from the Deleware New Era. You have stated that TCW will be a straight foward wrestling promotion with no "extreme"
or "hardcore" edge. How do you think you will fare against the ever growing "garbage" promotions?
JU: Hardcore wrestling hit its high point a year or two ago but since then, it has really lost its novelty. Its only a matter of time before garbage feds run out of ideas.. and fans. I grew up on the traditional style of wrestling, and that style is going to continue with TCW.
[Uss points to a man in his early fourties.] Reporter #3: Ted Jones from the New York News. What seperates TCW from the rest? JU: Without a doubt TCW has the hardest workers in the business. Right now we are suffering from a small roster but in due time that number will grow and we will become a major player. We never said we were the best, but we are going to try our hardest to be the best! Next...
[Another man begins to speak.] Reporter #4: James Martin from the Dover Post. Are you worried about the health conditions of your wrestlers? Already, it looks like Lance Manyon should be in AA instead of TCW.
JU: I think in any profession, you have got to be concerned about the conditions of your employees, so yes of course. About Lance Manyon, as soon as he becomes a threat or puts anyone else in that position, then I will deal with him.
[Yet another man raises his hand, given permission to speak.] Reporter #5: William Jordan from the Maryland Press. Do you have any personal favorites as to the roster of TCW?
JU: None at all, the ones that work the hardest will get the most from
TCW. One more question...
[A black haired woman begins to ask her question.] Reporter #6: What is the current title situation here in TCW? JU: Currently TCW has three titles; the Heavyweight, Coastal, and Tag
Team Championships. The later two will be put on hold until we get a bigger roster but the Heavyweight Champion will be crowned soon. Next week on Maximum Impact, the start of an eight man round robin style tournament will begin. That means each wrestler will have to face the seven other contenders and the two with the best record will face each other in a final match for the championship. More on this next week! And now, I want to thank all of you who came here and I'm sorry I couldn't answer all your questions. Have a good evening...
[The scene cuts to the two announcers now in the dining hall of the building.] VC: Wow! Next week marks the start of the Heavyweight Title round robin tournament! RD: And we will be right there calling the action! VC: Well right now we have another competition that I'm going to love calling the action, the swimsuit competition!
RD: Give me a break... [Cut back to the beach where a crowd has gathered along side of a stage.] VC: Oh this is going to be good! ["Lord" Richard O'Riley appears on stage decked out in a hawaiin shirt and Khaki shorts with sandles.]
RO: It is now time for the SWIMSUIT COMPETITION! [The male audiance goes crazy as the loudest pop of the day can be heard.] RO: First, here is TCW's own Hannah O. Taylor! [Taylor comes out wearing a two piece blue suit. She is looking really annoyed.] RO: Give it up! Crowd: Wooooooooooo! RO: Next we have the star behind Clint Steel, Dallas Star! [Dallas Star comes out wearing a pink two piece with stars on the front of each side.] RO: How about it? Crowd: Wooooooooooo! Some guy: Take it off! RO: Finally we have the brains behind Lance Manyon, Serena MacDonald! [Serena comes out in a business suit with a mic in hand, giving her lots of heat.] SM: I refuse to take part in such an event like this. All you men are dogs! [Serena leaves to a chorus of boos.] RO: Well that leaves two then! And its up to you to see who wins! [O'Riley puts his hand flat of the top of Taylor's head.] RO: Will it be Hannah O. Taylor? Crowd: *clap* *clap* *clap* [He does the same for Dallas.] RO: Or will it be Dallas Star? Crowd: *CLAP* *CLAP* *CLAP* RO: It's official, the winner of the swimsuit competition is DALLAS STAR! [Dallas is handed a trophy as Clint Steel comes on stage to congratulate
Dallas.] VC: Aw, isn't that nice? RD: Look from behind, its the Candyman! [Sure enough, Candyman leaps on stage and locks a full nelson on Steel before taking him back into a suplex.]
RD: Candyman just full nelson suplex'ed Clint Steel on the wooden stage! Steel may be seriously hurt. CUT! Get away from this! [We cut back to the building where Rex and Vance still have their headphones on in disbelieve of what just happened.]
RD: Sorry, we were unprepared for this fans! Right now we are going to go to our final segment of the night as we will see if Lance Manyon is in any condition to wrestle!
[The scene cuts to the ring once more where "Lord" Richard O'Riley has managed to make his way to.]
RO: This contest is set for one fall and has a fifteen minute time limit! VC: What shape is Manyon in? RO: Introducing first, he is currently in the ring and weighs in at two hundred twenty pounds, PASH C R A C K E R S!
VC: Who? RD: Don't ask.. VC: More local scum, huh? RO: Uh huh. RO: And his opponent... ["Cracking The Surfice" strikes up on the sound system as two figures begin making their way to ringside.]
RO: Being accompanied by Serena MacDonald, he hails from East Side, New
York City [semi-pop] and weighs in at two hundred and twenty seven pounds, LANCE M A N Y O N!
[Manyon comes out shirtless, with white trunks with navy blue designs, white boots, with navy shinguards, navy elbowpads, and black fingerless gloves.]
[The bell rings.] RD: And now is the time to see if Manyon is ready to go! [Both men lock up and Manyon backs Crackers into the corner and proceeds to chop him repeatedly getting a "whoooooo" from the crowd. Crackers slides to the mat where Manyon begins to put the boots to him.]
RD: Lance Manyon looks to be in great shape! VC: Well he's in the ring with a nobody! [Manyon whips Crackers into the turnbuckle, but Pash leaps up and nails
Manyon with a twisting shouldertacker getting a "ahhh" from the crowwd.
VC: What a move by Crackers! [Crackers charged at Manyon but he back body drops him over the top rope. Pash lands on the apron and pulls Manyon down to the mat. Pash jumps to the top turnbuckle and drops a leg across the face of Manyon.]
RD: This Pash Crackers is giving Manyon a real run for his money! [Pash bodyslams Manyon and heads to the top turnbuckle. Crackers atempts a 450° splash but Manyon moves out the way.]
RD: Oh! Crackers looks like he imjured his ankle on that one! [Manyon whips Pash into the ropes and knees him center in the stomache.] VC: Manyon is gaining some major offense! [Lance scoops Pash up and turns him upside down in the corner.] RD: Tree of woe! [Lance begins to stomp away at Pash.] RD: The alcohol seems to have no effect on Manyon when he wrestles! VC: Not yet at least! [Manyon whips Pash into the ropes once more and goes for a tilt-a-whirl but Pash reverses it into a flying headscissors.]
VC: And Manyon is down once again! [Both men get up at the same time and Pash attempts a German suplex but
Manyon hangs onto the top rope which sends Pash flying into the mat.]
RD: Great ring presence by Lance Manyon! [Manyon walks over to Pash to pick him up but Pash surprises him with a small package.] RD: Small package! Ref: One.. Two.. Three!! VC: WHAT!? RD: Local wrestler Pash Crackers has just pinned Lance Manyon! [Crackers looks around in amazement, and then slides out of the ring and runs before anything else can happen.]
RD: Look at Manyon! He is pushing the ref around and here comes Serena
MacDonald! We are out of time, see you tomorrow fans!
[The scene fades to black as the copyright image appears.] © 2000 Traditional Championship Wrestling
