HOT: Are you OK? R: I'm fine. [takes deep breath] Sorry. HOT: I won't ask anymore question for today. R: No, go ahead. Please do. HOT: OK, one more question. Do you have anything big planned for Saturday that you can let us in on? R: Yep. I plan on staying in that ring, and helping my team make it to the Max Impact on the 22nd.
[Rampage walks away, in obvious emotional pain. Hannah O. Taylor walks away also, with a really weird look on her face.]
FTB [~]~[~] DEUCE [~]~[~] (Flashback to last Saturday night's Maximum Impact.) [With Deuce still on the mat, Rampage leaps up in the air but Deuce somehow manages to spring to his feet and superkick Rampage on his way down.]
RD: Deuce just hopped up on his leg with no problem! VC: And used it to kick the teeth of Rampage down his throat! [Deuces reaches down and tears the tape on his knee off and throws it into the crowd.] RD: It was all a setup! Deuce was fine from the beginning! [Deuces whips Rampage into the turnbuckle and follows it up with a hurricanrana.] RD: Hurricanrana attempted by Deuce! VC: What a master plan! Deuce is the REAL master of surprise! (Zoom out from a TV set in the center of a large room, which is adorned with plenty of nice things. Deuce is sitting in a recliner with a remote in his right hand and his left hand somehow balancing a Coke and a handful of Doritos. He looks pleased...very pleased.)
Deuce: Oh how great it was! To see the look of fright on your face,
Rampage, when I sprung up and caught you with that superkick to the chin, OOOOOH! It gives me chills, Rampage, chills.
But it seems fate has a sense of irony, for not only are you obviously feeling hate towards me for the prank, if you can call it that, I pulled on you, we're also partners this Saturday in a Coastal Title match. Any little mistake made out there by either one of us, and we could both be gone, our title hopes vanquished.
(Deuce, sensing that he's out of Doritos in his left hand, reaches back into the bag to get yet another handful.)
Rampage, I personally have no problem with you. You were simply a pawn in my proverbial chess game. You were just in the wrong place at the wrong time. I'm sure you may feel a little anger towards me, but that can be dealt with at another time. This Saturday, our team must band together to come out victorious.
(Deuce flips the channel until he sees ESPN2. I think we all know what's on right now, hell what's always on ESPN2. World's Strongest Man.)
Damn, talk about syndication. This thing's on my TV more than that little Pepsi girl with the everchanging voice. To get a little off-topic, as if I haven't already, I want to share some of my views on some of the things that have been going on lately.
(Deuce pulls, what looks to be a cue card, off the ground next to his recliner. He holds it up to read...HEAVYWEIGHT TITLE TOURNAMENT.)
Now this, my friends, is a joke. Hmmmm...let me see, we're having a tournament to crown our first heavyweight champ, and the top draw in the fed isn't IN the tourney. Makes great sense. WAIT! I've got it. Jon Uss is trying to make the Coastal Title the number one belt around here. It makes more sense now, Jon. I was starting to worry about being left out of the Heavyweight Title Tournament, but now I feel better.
And even if you aren't planning the Coastal Title to be above the Heavyweight Title in stature, I'll MAKE it better, I'll MAKE it tougher to get, I'll MAKE it the most prestigious belt ever seen in these parts.
Because I'm just...that...damn...good. (Deuce pulls out another cue card. It reads "DANGEROUS" DAN DIXON.) 3-D, by the way, that names a little overused, no? But anyways, Danny
Boy, I just wanted you to know that you don't frighten me at all, if that's what you're going for. Fear. Going for fear were ya? Buddy, your nickname might be Dangerous, but my MIDDLE NAME is Danger.
(Deuce gulps down the rest of the Coke.) OK, so I lied, my middle name's not Danger, but I'm still not afraid of you. Come this Saturday, Dan, I'm gonna personally make sure your candyass doesn't come anywhere close to that title. Cuz, Dan, when I play, I always play...DEUCES WILD.

Tower: Now I don't personally see any reason why we shouldn't be handed the tag titles right now, no questions asked, right
Eric?
March: You damn right. Who's gonna stop us from winning the belts? Tower: Nobody in THIS fed, that's for damn sure. But, onto my match this weekend against Ninja and "Sane Ninja Clown" Giggles

Tower: Eric, it's not me that needs to be watching The Learning Channel, it's my ninja counterparts. They should focus their watching on the medical shows, cuz after this Saturday night, they're gonna want to know what exactly all that doctor lingo means as they're being pulled away on a stretcher.

It is midnight, just a few hours after Maximum Impact went off the air, and we open up to a wide angle shot of a strip club somewhere in Manhattan. The club, called "Hired Gunns", is obviously doing some good business. There's a line of people leading up to the door, loud music and whistling can be heard coming from inside. After a few seconds the scene changes to an interior shot. The place is packed with drunken men waving around dollar bills at girls dancing on tables and a stage.
Since this is a family show the girls bodies are tiled out so that their nude features are indistinguishable to the viewer. Rob Zombie's
"Dragula" - a strip club favorite - is playing in the background.] #DIG through the ditches and# #BURN through the witches and# #SLAM in the back of my...DRAG - U - LAAAA# [The camera pans upward to a plate glass window looking out upon the club. Dan Dixon can be seen standing on the other side observing the people below, apparently from some kind of office. Abruptly, the scene changes again, this time to an interior shot of the office. The music is muffled, and Dixon's back is to the camera. A quick look around the office reveals a desk, a sofa and chair set with a coffee table in front, and a ficus tree in the corner. There's various plaques and trophies around the room, most of them seem to be wrestling related. A knock at the door causes Dixon to turn slightly towards the camera.]
3D: Yeah? Man's voice: (muffled by the door) Mr. Dixon? There's a Mr. Sammy Marchetti here to see you. 3D: (to himself) 'Bout time...(to the man) Send him in, junior. [The door opens and in walks Marchetti, carrying a briefcase. He extends his hand and smiles.]
Marchetti: Glad to see ya, Dixon. 3D: (shakes his hand) Its good to see you, Marchetti. Its even better to see that briefcase. Marchetti: You did a great job tonight, and since you held up your end of the deal, I'll hold up mine. (Opens briefcase with $100,000 inside) This is what we agreed on, right?
[Dixon picks up a stack of bills and studies it for a moment. Happy with what he sees, he looks up at Marchetti and smiles.]
3D: This'll do just fine.... Just fine. (motions towards the sofa) Want to stay a while, maybe have a drink? Marchetti: Sure. (Sits on the sofa) Perhaps we could discuss some, uh...(Clears throat) future business arrangements. I like your work.
[Dixon holds down a button on a speakerphone that's sitting on the desk and speaks into it.]
3D: Donnie, bring a couple of whiskeys in here......better yet, have one of the girls bring 'em. [Dixon takes a seat on a chair opposite Marchetti and smiles.] 3D: So..... Who's next? Marchetti: Well, I'll have to get word from the "President" about that. As soon as they let me know, I'll let you know. [The door opens and in walks a scantilly clad blonde carrying a tray with two drinks. She smiles and offers the drinks to Dixon and
Marchetti, who take them. Dixon returns the smile and stuffs a bill in her g-string. The girl exits and the two men lean back and continue their conversation.]
Marchetti: You run a nice business here. 3D: Heh...thanks. (sips his drink) I'm not exactly the business man type, but I've got a certain fondness for strippers. They remind me of.....me. [Marchetti looks puzzled by this remark, so Dixon explains.] 3D: You see, strippers do what they do for the money they get. They have a specialty, they excel in sex appeal, and they take advantage of it. I have a specialty, I excel in hurting people....(smiles)..and I'm sure as hell taking advantage of it.
Marchetti: So I see. (Marchetti's cell phone rings) Um, hold on. (Opens phone) Yeah... Uh-huh... Ok, fine. Thanks. (Closes phone) Well, that was
Uss, and it appears that Chris Malice is my opponent next week. (With disgust) "The Franchise".
3D: Malice.... [Dixon ponders this bit of information for a moment, as if remembering something from the distant past.]
3D: You know, I've had a couple of run-ins with "The Franchise." Marchetti: And who came out on top? 3D: (smiles) Heh...take a guess. [The two of them chuckle and sip their drinks.] 3D: Seriously, I'm pretty familiar with Malice. I've wrestled him before, and we were even "running buddies" once upon a time. I can have you so ready for this match, Malice won't know what hit him.
Marchetti: (nods) Sounds good... (cell phone rings again) Damn, hold on... (opens phone) Hello?.......yeah........what?......alright, I'll take care of it. Goodbye. [Marchetti puts his cell phone in his pocket, sets his drink down on the table and stands up.]
Marchetti: Mr. Dixon, I'm afraid I have to leave. Something's come up. [Dixon stands as well.] 3D: That's too bad. I was gonna' have one of my girls give you a private show. Marchetti: (smiles) That's tempting, but I really need to get out of here. (extends his hand) Take care of yourself. 3D: (shakes his hand) You too. I'll see you later. Marchetti: Yeah, later. [Marchetti leaves the office, and Dixon closes the door behind him. He walks over to the desk and takes a seat, pulling the briefcase close. He opens it, smiling, and begins to count the money.]
3D: Heh... Nice doing business with you, Mr. Marchetti. [Fade to black.] [~]~[~] SOVEREIGN [~]~[~] **Shot opens on Sovereign sitting in a large leather chair reading a book, 'The rediscovery of man' he looks up from the book and begins to address the audience**
