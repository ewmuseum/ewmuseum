+-+ TALK IS SWEET +-+ Issue Number Thirteen July 12, 2000 Traditional Championship Wrestling Webpage: [detail withheld] -=- Table of Contents -=- [1] "Sane Ninja Clown" Giggles Shirow [2] "Dangerous" Dan Dixon [3] Jon "Gambit" Lightning [4] Jack "the Ripper" Williams [5] James "A**hole" O'Brien [~]~[~] "Sane Ninja Clown" GIGGLES SHIROW [~]~[~] (The scene opens to the inside of a small plane.  There's some equipment for skydiving, and three people.  One is the instructor, wearing a gray bodysuit, goggles, and a parachute.  The other two are
"Sane Ninja Clown" Giggles Shirow and Mister Bozo Hayashi.  Giggles wears a orange and green striped pair of karate pants and an orange ninja hood, along with a parachute.  The rest of him is painted green, along with the orange kanji tatoos on his front and back.  Mister Bozo has green, curly hair and a large, red, squeeky nose.  The rest of his attire consists of a black ninja robe and a parachute.  They're sitting in silence until...)
  Shirow: Ah, hello.  'Tis a greeting, but what is a greeting really?  It started and remains to be a trivial rite of passage that completes the sociological scale of 1999.  You know what I am saying?
  (Mister Bozo nods.)   Shirow: But to greet is to say farewell, something that would end the balance on which we stand.  That, being in direct correlation with the evils of the modern nuclear age.  And what do we have to show for this...this project of Manhattan?  Cheetos, the snack food of champions, but still, snack food.  I wish that I could take a Cheeto in my hand, hold it between my fingers, and watch the heads of the non-believers fall from the shoulders of the clouds of the heavens and then say, "Yes, yes I will."  Boot to the head!  To the head!  To the head again!  And for what?  For Ninja and for Gabriel Tower. 
Whoop me some ASS!   (Giggles begins to juggle some flaming knives.)   Instructor: Um....Mister Hayashi?  Could you ask him not to do that?   (Mister Bozo nods, stands and pushes Giggles out of the plane.  Hayashi proceeds to draw a sword and jumps out himself.  The instructor goes over to the door, looks out, shakes his head, and closes it.)
  [Fade to Black] [~]~[~] "Dangerous" DAN DIXON [~]~[~] [Fade in. We are back inside the "Hired Gunns" club, apparently during the slow daytime hours. There are no windows to give away the time of day, but as the camera pans around, we can see that there are few men in the club and even fewer girls dancing. Abruptly, the scene changes to a shot of Dan Dixon in his office. He is leaning back in his chair with his feet propped up on the desk, dressed in jeans, snakeskin boots, and a plain black t-shirt. His casual dress, along with his size, makes him look more like a bouncer than the owner of the club. Dixon's moment of relaxation is disturbed by a man's voice coming over the speakerphone.]
Voice: Mr. Dixon? [Dixon sighs and removes his feet from the desk. He leans forward and pushes a button on the phone.]
3D: What is it, Donnie? Donnie: There's a lady here to see you. 3D: A lady? Is she hot? Donnie: Well...yes, I guess she is. 3D: (smiles) Send her in, junior. [After a few moments the door opens, and in walks Hannah O. Taylor, intrepid reporter and TCW's resident diva. She smiles as she walks towards Dixon, extending her hand.]
HOT: Nice to meet you, Mr. Dixon. I'm... 3D: Hold that thought. [Dixon stands up and looks her over, smiling and rubbing his chin.] 3D: Oh yeah. Donnie wasn't kidding, was he? [Hannah looks puzzled.] 3D: I think we've got a place for you. When can you start? [Hannah seems confused for a second, then she suddenly realizes what
Dixon is thinking. Her face turns red, and she shakes her head.]
HOT: I'm not looking for work, Mr. Dixon. I already have a job. 3D: Eh? Then what do you want? HOT: My name is Hannah O. Taylor, I'm a reporter for TCW. I'm here to interview you. [Dixon stares at her for a moment then laughs softly.] 3D: Oh...heh...my mistake. You've got to understand, I get a lot of pretty girls in here everyday, and I'm usually the one doing the "interviewing". Hope I didn't offend you. HOT: Its alright, Mr. Dixon. 3D: Could you drop the "Mr. Dixon", please? I'm not a schoolteacher. HOT: Well, okay. What would you like me to call you? 3D: You can call me Dan...or Danny...3D, the Dangerous One... HOT: (interrupting) Um, why don't I just call you Dan? 3D: (shrugs) Suit yourself. (motions towards the sofa) Have a seat,
Hannah O. Taylor. Make yourself at home. HOT: Thank you. [Hannah seats herself on the sofa and produces a mini cassette recorder from her pocket, which she places on the coffee table in front of her.
Dixon takes a seat at the other end of the sofa.] 3D: Ready when you are. HOT: (smiles) Well, first of all I'd like to welcome you to TCW. 3D: Its good to be here, Hannah. I've had my eye on TCW for a while, but
I was sort of...(makes the quotation mark sign with his fingers)..."retired". I did a little tour of duty on the west coast, but it wasn't for me. I came back here and opened this place, and that's when TCW caught my eye. I was enjoying running my club, but I was itching to get back in the ring. When Sammy Marchetti came to me with a business proposal, well...to use an old gangster cliché...it was an
"offer I couldn't refuse".
HOT: There's been a lot of speculation as to what, exactly, your relationship with Mr. Marchetti is. Care to elaborate on it?
3D: Well, let's just say that I'm his..."insurance policy". He was having a little trouble, things weren't going his way, so he came to me. He wanted me to come in here and swing the odds in his favor, and that's just what I did this weekend during his match with that Tallywacker guy.
HOT: You mean Talamaur. 3D: Whatever. HOT: And what do you get out of this arrangement? 3D: (smiles) Heh... A very large amount of money. HOT: And what about this weekend? Will you be "swinging the odds in his favor" during his match with Chris Malice?
3D: I'll be around, Hannah. I may not be at ringside, but I'll definitely be watching that match closely. I'll make sure Sammy gets what he wants. HOT: I see... Why don't we talk about your match for a moment... 3D: My match? HOT: Yes, you're booked in a eight-man, elimination style tag match. The surviving team goes on to fight for the vacant Coastal Title a week later.
3D: Is that a fact? (he thinks about it) Who else is in this match? HOT: Well, I'm not sure of the exact lineup, but off the top of my head, there's men such as Rampage, Sovereign, Deuce...
3D: (chuckles) Heh...Deuce. Kinda' sounds like "douche"...heh heh... [The look on Hannah's face tells Dixon that she doesn't really appreciate the joke, and his laughter quickly subsides.]
3D: Ah...nevermind. [Hannah decides to ignore it and press onward.] HOT: About the match, do you have any comments for your opponents or your teammates? 3D: Um...to be honest, this comes as a surprise to me. Like I say, I'm here to make sure Sammy gets what he wants, that's what he's paying me for. President Uss insisted that I sign a contract before he would even let me in the building, he wanted me on the roster. I figured I'd be back in the ring soon, just didn't expect it to be this soon. But, you know...(smiles and rubs his chin)...this could be a good situation.
There's no reason why I can't help Sammy and put a little gold around my waist at the same time. I'll have to do a little homework on these guys, then maybe I can comment on it.
HOT: Understood. I'd like to thank you for your time. 3D: No problem. [The two of them stand up and shake hands, and Dixon smiles again.] 3D: Sure I can't interest you in a little "night job"? You could make a hell of a lot of money. HOT: (shakes her head and smiles) No thank you. 3D: (shrugs) Your loss. HOT: I'm sure it is. Goodbye, Dan. 3D: Later. [Hannah leaves the office, and Dixon watches her go. As the door closes behind her, he sits down at his desk and pushes a button on the speakerphone.]
3D: Hey, Donnie. Donnie: Yes, Mr. Dixon? 3D: Go down to the TCW building and get me a copy of this weeks card, junior. Donnie: I'm on my way. [Dixon leans back and relaxes once again, propping his feet up on the desk. Fade to black.]
