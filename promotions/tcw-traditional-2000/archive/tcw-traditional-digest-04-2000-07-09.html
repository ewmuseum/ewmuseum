You'll know what I'm talking about when you check out the card for this week. ~ President Uss ×-×-×-×-× NEWS ×-×-×-×-× !~ DIXON DEBUTS - MARCHETTI WINS ~! "Dangerous" Dan Dixon made his TCW debut last night on Maximum Impact when he attacked Talamaur leading Marchetti to pick up the victory. We now know that the mysterious video package that was sent in earlier in the week, was indeed by Dan Dixon. !~ DEUCE FAKES INJURY ~! After Rampage he is the king of surprise, Deuce surprised everyone when he shed his injury near the end of their match last week. Deuce had been complaining about the injury all week long and Rampage was shocked when Deuce shot up. Deuce went on to win the match with the Deuces Wild and make a sucessful debut in TCW. !~ USS MAKES ANNOUNCEMENT ~! After the announcement about the announcement one week ago, TCW has been a buzz about what it would be. Uss announced that the TWO men with the highest total points in the tournament will go on to meet each other at the supercard in August. ×-×-×-×-×-×-×-×-×-×-× WEEKLY RECAP ×-×-×-×-×-×-×-×-×-×-× Maximum Impact 7.8.00 Party Palace - Buffalo, NY 803 Fans - Lance Manyon d. Once an entire team of four men have been eliminated, the match is over. A man can lose by pinfall, countout, submission, and disqualification. The winning team will face each other on the 7.22.00 edition of Maximum Impact for the COASTAL TITLE! If all four men survive on a single team, then the match will be a four corners bout. If three men survive, it will be a triangle match. If it comes down to two men, then they will fight each other in a regular singles match.
