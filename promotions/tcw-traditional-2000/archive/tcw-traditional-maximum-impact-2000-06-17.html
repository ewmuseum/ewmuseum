+-+ TCW MAXIMUM IMPACT +-+ LIVE! From the TCW Arena Queens, New York June 17, 2000 Traditional Championship Wrestling Webpage: [detail withheld] [The scene opens up on a blank screen. Nothing but darkness can be seen besides some "snow" from the bad reception on your television set.
Before anything can be viewed on the screen; what will come to be known as the Maximum Impact theme song, Debonaire by Dope, begins to play with the opening video package closly following...]
### I don't need diamond rings or high priced suits that shine ### ### Limousines and flashy things or ancient bottled wine ### [The TCW logo appears, swirls around, and disapears.] ### Designer names and lavish things and million dollar hair ### ### Bonny dames without a brain ### ### I never really cared ### [Lance Manyon is shown with Serena MacDonald on a beach.] ### I don't care nothing about it ### ### whoa yeah ### [Jack Williams delivers a text-book suplex.] ### I don't care for glitzy things or a fancy neighborhood ### ### Glamour games or plastic fame or the king of Hollywood ### [Sammy Marchetti steps out of his limousine.] ### I don't need flamboyant things or a top hat on my head ### ### Modeling for magazines or to be a debonaire ### [Candyman posing on the top turnbuckle.] ### I don't care nothing about it ### ### whoa yeah ### [The video package now ends and the scene cuts inside the TCW Arena where a couple hundred fans have begun cheering. The song hasn't stopped playing yet as the final thirty seconds or so blasts through the arena.
The camera pans across the small but packed arena until it reaches the announce position located near the ring where our announcers await.]
Rex Dynamo: Hello, fans and welcome to the debut edition of Traditional

"Smashin'" Vance Cracken!

I: Well folks, it looks like Talamaur has arrived in the CPW Arena ready for tonight's event of Maximum Impact. Talamaur what is running through your mind after Jack "The Ripper" Willams' threating comments?
T: That's easy, my mind is filled of visions with him being brutally massacred, and the look on his face is a look in anguish, sorrow, and death. Tonight I will give him a beating in which he won't forget, a beating which will last an eternity.

Richard O'Riley: Ladies and gentlemen, welcome to TCW Maximum Impact! [cheer] This opening contest will be the first official TCW match! [cheer] It is set for one fall with a twenty minute time limit... [The arena turns dark as "Gravedigger" by the New York Rock and Rockk

RD: The fans are booing Talamaur here tonight! They really don't like this guy! Richard O'Riley: And his opponent... [The lights raise back up as "Tubular Bells" begins to play.] Ricard O'Riley: He hails from London, England and weighs in at two hundred and ninety pounds, JACK "THE R I P P E R" W I L L I A M S!

*DING* *DING* *DING* RD: And here we go with our opening contest! [Both men circle each other, and Williams goes behind with a full nelson. Talamaur is able to reverse it into a full nelson of his own but
Williams comes right back with a side headlock.]
VC: Great chain wrestling to start this match off! RD: Thats what Traditional Championship Wrestling is all about! [Talamaur elbows Williams in the side of the head breaking the headlock then connects with a slap to the chest which backs Williams up against the near ropes and gets a small "whoo" from the crowd.]
RD: A knife edge chop by Talamaur! VC: I heard that one from over here! [Talamaur whips Williams into the ropes but Williams reverses it and nails Talamaur with an inverted atomic drop on the rebound.]
RD: I FELT that one from over here! [Williams quickly jumps on the second rope and comes back with a cross body block into the cover.]
Referee: ONE.. TWO.. KICKOUT! RD: Williams connects with a beautiful springboard cross body! [Talamaur gets up and attempts a martial arts kick to the head but
Williams catches it and brings him down with an ankle lock submission.
Talamaur quickly reaches the ropes.] RD: Williams showing great presences in there! [Both men get to their feet and Talamaur whips Williams into the far ropes, Williams comes back with a Thesz press into the cover.]
Referee: ONE.. TWO.. THR.. KICKOUT! RD: Williams surprised Talamaur with that move! [Talamaur trips Williams up and wraps his left leg up in a scissors type move. Williams grabs for the ropes and breaks the hold.]
RD: Talamaur with some submission offensive of his own, but Jack Williams made it to the ropes! [Both men get to their feet and Talamaur nails a standing side kick to
Williams which knocks him right out of the ring.]
VC: What a kick! That could have knocked Williams right out! RD: He fell to the outside! This could be a countout win for Talamaur! Referee: ONE.. TWO.. THREE.. FOUR.. [Williams gets back to his feet.] FIVE.. SIX.. SEVEN.. [Williams climbs up on the apron only to be met with another kick.] RD: And Jack Williams tumbles to the outside once again! [Talamaur gets a running start and leaps over the top rope and onto
Williams.] RD: Jack Williams was just crushed by Talamaur! VC: I'm really liking his style! [Talamaur rolls Williams back into the ring and follows him in. He picks him up and tosses him against the ropes nailing a lariat.]
RD: And Williams is turned inside out! VC: Talamaur is beating on him relentlessly... I love it! [Williams is whipped into the ropes one more time but he reverses it into a bridge suplex.]
Referee: ONE.. TWO.. THREE.. no, KICKOUT! RD: And Williams was so close on the pinfall that time! [Williams gets up but Talamaur kicks him in the back of the knee, and
Williams buckles to the mat.]
VC: Talamaur with the ol' charlie horse! [Talamaur keeps Williams down with a cross arm breaker.] RD: Talamaur has this submission locked in right in the center of the ring, where he wants him!
[Williams slowly kicks his feet over to the ropes and breaks the hold.] RD: Williams is out but the damage was done! [Talamaur takes Williams over with an overhead suplex. Williams slides out to the floor and Talamaur follows.]
VC: And they are back outside! RD: What a match this has been! [Williams surprises Talamaur by ramming him into the ring post and bodyslamming him on the concrete.]
RD: And just like that Williams is back in control! [Williams head to the top turnbuckle and comes off with a big splash on
Talamaur on the outside.]
VC: WHAT A MOVE! RD: Williams didn't need a table or a chair like you might see on another wrestling program! He just used his body as a weapon!
[Williams rolls Talamaur back in the ring and follows right in with him.] RD: I think this match is all but over! [Williams picks Talamaur up for a suplex but turns it into a DDT in mid-move.] RD: There it is, the BIG RIPPER! Referee: ONE.. TWO.. THREE! RD: WHAT A MATCH! Richard O'Riley: Here is your winner, JACK W I L L I A M S! RD: Williams earned that one! VC: We'll be right back after these commercials! [Commercial break.] RD: We are back fans and it is now time for our elimination-style triangle match! VC: This one looks to be really promising! [The scene cuts to the ring where Richard O'Riley is once again positioned.] Richard O'Riley: The following contest is a elimanation triangle match with a thirty minute time limit!
[The official theme of Lance Manyon, "Cracking the Surface", strikes up over the PA system.]
Richard O'Riley: The first competitor... from Upper East Side, New York
City [cheer], he weighs in at two hundred and twenty seven pounds and is accompanied by Serena MacDonald... LANCE M A N Y O N!
RD: Manyon is receiving a huge ovation from the hometown crowd here tonight! VC: And having Serena MacDonald with him doesn't hurt matters. RD: Not at all. ["Grave Digger" by the New York Rock and Roll Ensemble starts up once again tonight as another dark figure makes his way down to ringside.]
RD: And Talamaur's regular tag team partner is going to be in action here next. Richard O'Riley: From Birmingham, England... he weighs in at one hundred ninety seven pounds, he is N O F E R A T U!
RD: Two thirds of the match are now in the ring, we are awaiting the third! ["Woke Up This Morning" by A3 can now be heard playing over the PA system as an arena full of jeers and boos is sounded.]
Richard O'Riley: And finally, from Rome, Italy and weighing in at three hundred forty seven pounds. He is being accompanied by Johnny "The Rat", here is SAMMY "THE B E A R" M A R C H E T T I!
RD: Marchetti making a huge entrance here in the TCW Arena here tonight! *DING* *DING* *DING* VC: Look at the variety in this match, one is from England, another from
Italy, and Manyon is from right here in scum New York!
RD: You better watch it, the boss won't like that! VC: [In a horrible New York/Italian accent] Fo'getta bout it! RD: Getting to the match, all three men will be in the ring at the same time and the match will not end until two of them have been elimanted.
[Noferatu dropkicks Marchetti as he enters the ring, sending the big man to the mat. He quickly jumps on top making the cover.]
Referee: ONE.. TWO.. KICKOUT! RD: Noferatu went for the early win there! [Lance Manyon jumps over the top rope and lands a legdrop on Marchetti and he makes the cover.]
Referee: ONE.. TWO.. KICKOUT! VC: Both men are trying to eliminate the bigger Marchetti early! [Both men begin stomping away at Sammy until Noferatu turns around and puches Manyon square in the face.]
RD: Alliances aren't going to last long in this one! [Manyon rallies back by bouncing off the ropes and nailing Noferatu with a jumping lariat.]
RD: Pinning combination! Referee: ONE.. TWO.. KICKOUT! RD: And Noferatu was the one almost pinned that time! [Marchetti comes over and rakes the eyes of Manyon before smashing his head into the top turnbuckle.]

[Noferatu slaps a headlock on Manyon, before Manyon pushes him off against the ropes.] RD: All three men are capitalizing on each others moves! [Noferatu comes back off the ropes and Marchetti steps in with a spinebuster.] Referee: ONE.. TWO.. THR.. KICKOUT! VC: That was the biggest spinebuster I've ever seen! VC: Obviously Noferatu has more guts than brains. RD: Good night Noferatu! [Marchetti locks in a headlock on the fallen flyer. Manyon comes over and gets Marchetti in a sleeper.]
RD: Look at this! Marchetti has a headlock on Noferatu, and Manyon has a sleeper on Marchetti! [Noferatu elbows Marchetti in the gut, causing his head to jerk up which in turn busts Manyon right in the jaw breaking both moves.]

VC: Wow! Nobody saw that one coming! [Marchetti big splashes him to make sure he is down and makes the cover.] RD: That is all she wrote for Noferatu! Referee: ONE.. TWO.. THREE! Richard O'Riley: Noferatu has been eliminated! RD: Noferatu was wore down too much during the course of this match to withstand that! VC: Which leaves us with Lance Manyon and Sammy Marchetti. RD: What a move by Manyon and Sammy almost goes down! [Manyon whips Marchetti into the far turnbuckle and he goes back first into it.] RD: Manyon has Marchetti reeling! [Johnny "the Rat" Marchetti begins to scream at Manyon from ringside. Manyon goes over to the ropes and begins to yell back.] RD: This is not a smart move on Manyons part at all. VC: Look at Sammy waiting in the wings! [Manyon turns around quick enough to see Marchetti charging at him with the Cosa Nostra, and ducks. Marchetti bounces off the ropes and Manyon goes for a back body drop but Marchetti counters with a piledriver.]
RD: Manyon ducked the Cosa Nostra but was nailed with a piledriver! Referee: ONE.. TWO.. THR.. KICKOUT! [Marchetti backs Manyon up in the corner and begins to stomp away at him.] VC: Marchetti is stomping a mud hole in him! [Marchetti brings Lance back out of the corner and nails him with a scoop slam.] RD: I think its all academic from here. RD: That may have knocked all the wind out of Manyon! Referee: ONE.. TWO.. THRE.. KICKOUT! VC: How does Manyon keep coming back? RD: The longer this match goes, the better a chance Manyon has of winning. Marchetti is too big to go extended ammounts of time.
[Sammy begins climbing the turnbuckles, reaching the second one.] RD: What is he doing?! VC: If he hits this, its all over! [Marchetti jumps off with a big splash but Manyon rolls away in the nick of time.] RD: Manyon moves! [Lance rolls him over into a pinning position.] Referee: ONE.. TWO.. THR.. KICKOUT! [Manyon picks Marchetti up and backs him against the ropes, puts his head down and Marchetti catches him with a swinging neck breaker.]
RD: Manyon makes the same mistake again! [Marchetti picks Manyon up for a powerbomb, but Manyon counters with a facebuster in the air.]
VC: Marchetti had his face jammed into the mat by Manyon! Referee: ONE.. TWO.. THRE.. KICKOUT! VC: These two have put on such a great show, I almost forgot this was originally a triangle match!
[Manyon slowly climbs the turnbuckle from inside the ring as Marchetti tries to get to his feet.]
RD: Marchetti is up, but what is Manyon doing here? [Manyon backflips off the top into a moonsault right onto Marchetti. Sammy tries to catch him but the impact is so great that Manyon takes him over and hooks both legs in a pinning move.]
RD: And Manyon takes him over! Referee: ONE.. TWO.. THREE! RD: Manyon wins the triangle match! VC: That is real surprising considering he lost to that nobody last week on the openng festivities!
RD: Manyon sure surpised me! Richard O'Riley: Your winner, LANCE M A N Y O N! [Manyon climbs out of the ring and leaves with Serena.] RD: Its time for another commercial break and then we will be back with our main event! [Commercial break.] RD: And we are back and ready for our main event! This will be a Heavyweight Title tournament match. The winner will be the first person to pick up any points so far.
VC: This tournament is going to be good! RD: Take it away Richard O'Riley! [We head back up to the ring one more time as O'Riley is standing by with mic in hand as usual.]
Richard O'Riley: This is the TCW Maximum Impact MAIN EVENT! It is set for one fall and is a Heavyweight Title tournament match!

Richard O'Riley: Hailing from Portland, Oregan... here is J A D E! [Jade begins to run down to the ring but Candyman is waiting behind the ring steps and he blindsides him.]
*DING* *DING* *DING* RD: And Candyman attacks Jade before the bell! [Candyman whips Jade into the railing but Jade is able to swing around and Candyman crashes into the gate.]
RD: Jade reversed the Irish whip into the gaurd rail. [Jade rolls Candyman into the ring and follows him in but Candyman turns around and begins stomping on him as he is entering.]
RD: And just like that Candyman takes control! VC: What a smart move! [Candyman drapes Jade over the top rope backwards and nails him with a clubbing shot to the chest. And again he does it. He attempts a third but Jade grabs his arm and hiptosses him over the top rope.]
RD: And Candyman is once again on the floor. [Jade comes off the apron with a double axe handle into the head of

Candyman with a cover.]

RD: A great counter move by Jade! Referee: ONE.. TWO.. KICKOUT! [Candyman rakes Jade in the eyes which stops his momentum. He sets him up but a suplex but Jade blocks it and turns it into a small package.]
Referee: ONE.. TWO.. TH.. KICKOUT! RD: Jade is pulling out all the stops in this one! VC: It's only a matter of time before Candyman takes control. RD: And all the air has just been sucked out of Jade! VC: I told you Candyman would take control! [Candyman takes one of Jades legs and drops an elbow across it.] RD: That brings us back to an earlier match, trying to charlie horse that leg! RD: Candyman blocked that pin attempt with a nice fist. [Candyman begins to gawk with the fans and Jade comes back with the sunset flip.] Referee: ONE.. TWO.. TH.. KICKOUT! [Candyman props his feet up on the middle rope behind the refs back adding leverage to the hold. Jade nails Candyman wih three stiff shots to gut releasing the hold..]

RD: Jade with a second wind here late in this match! VC: Get up Candyman! Don't blow it now! [Jade heads up to the top turnbuckle and waits for Candyman to stand up.
