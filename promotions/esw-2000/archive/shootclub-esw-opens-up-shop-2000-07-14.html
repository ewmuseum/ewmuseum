Shootclub: ESW Opens up Shop!

14 July 2000
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
July 14, 2000 - Shootclub.com has official begun! The first month of Empire State Wrestling action is up to be viewed by all. New results are posted each Monday, Wednesday, Friday, and Saturday.
Be sure to check it out. You can get to the reults and profiles from the main page. FEEDBACK Shootclub.com is a VERY complicated endeavour and there ARE going to be bugs. Please be sure to point them out to us. Also, if you have ideas for making the game better... let us know. And of course, don't be afraid to send us compliments... you can't get enough of those :) We look forward to hearing from you.
WHAT'S NEXT? Go to http://shootclub.com/first.html and read up on what is expected next from all the players. Learn how to modify your wrestler, submit promos, and be sure to read the "How to Win" section.
The next cycle is the August cycle. The deadline for getting NEW wrestlers and MODIFYING current wrestlers is Saturday July 29th, 2000 by midnight. If there is enough response, then we will be opening our second league Georgia All-Star Wrestling (GAW). New York? Atlanta? Hmmm?
At the beginning of the August cycle, there will be a pay-per-view. Rumor has it, that Adelphia cable will be offering it for .95 in some parts of Westchester County. *VERY* Exciting. There will be a battle royal for the TV title and maybe even some booking-on-the-fly...
something never tried before on a pay-per-view. FRIDAY NIGHT FURY At the bottom of this page is the first TV Report, Friday Night Fury, for ESW. In it, the league promoter is announced and he has some very unkind words for both the wrestlers and promotion in general. The main motivation behind this report is kick things off and show how ESW is in fact a regional promotion without the frills of the big leagues.
Don't sweat that you are in a "small time" league, chances are ESW will continue to grow as Shootclub grows and will one day become a full-fledged worldwide promotion with many of the wrestlers in it now being it's biggest stars. This is your chance to start something great. Also, don't be offended that you are not in this "initial"
story line. There are 38 wrestlers and not everyone could be placed. In the future, there will handfuls of stories going on with room for everybody. Just remember, the best roleplayers and best performers will get the most coverage in the story lines.
A very important note, be sure to send in some general ideas for story lines that you would like to see your wrestler(s) in. The final story lines will be culled from these suggestions, the promos, and the feuds/allies selected by each wrestler.
And YES, matches will be more tightly integrated into upcoming show reports. This storyline just took up all of the available time. You can expect show reports at least twice a month and we may ask for assistance from all of the players to pull this off. Someday a player will take over the reigns as promoter of ESW.
Below is the start of the Friday Night Fury report. Be sure to go to the website, to get THE REST OF THE STORY:
http://shootclub.com/cgi-bin/update.pl?leagueId=2&reportId=126 Good Luck in August, Shootclub Webmaster [detail withheld] http://shootclub.com Friday Night Fury: http://shootclub.com/cgi-bin/update.pl?leagueId=2&reportId=126 New Players Guide: http://shootclub.com/first.html *-*-*-*-*-*-*-*-*-*-*-*-*-*-*-*-*-*-*-*-*-*-*-*-*-*-*-*-*-*-*-*-*-*-*-* Friday Night Fury: ESW Starts with a "Bang" July 14, 2000 - Portchester High School - Portchester, NY The show opens with no pyro, no music, no laser lights. That wrestling cliche may be a little too costly for the new upstart. However, in an all too familiar scene, almost all of the wrestlers on the ESW roster have made their way down the aisle and are now standing around the ring. The wrestlers are sizing each other up, and what the TV audience at home doesn't know, is that they have been for nearly 15 minutes.
The crowd is getting really restless and they start chanting "ECW,
ECW!!" That rascal of medical remedy DR. LOVE tries to convince some of the fans that it should be "ESW, ESW!!" ... but the unconvinced audience continues with... "ECW, ECW!!"
Then, without warning, the house lights go down. After a lingering moment of darkness, a modest pyrotechnic display lights up the ringside area momentarily, and an indistinguishable song plays on the crackling P-A system. A fan in a faded Bushwhackers t-shirt hops onto his chair and starts to pump his fist, but he soon slithers back onto the floor when he realizes that he is alone in his enthusiasm. Welcome to the independants, Mark.
Suddenly, the music stops. A hush falls over the sweltering hall. A nervous young man in a wrinkled suit stands up from the ringside table and enters the ring, microphone in hand. He wipes his brow with the sleeve of his jacket and raises the mike to his lips. "SHUT UP!"
yells a smartass fan before the ring announcer can utter a word. This promotion will obviously have to earn the respect of the hard-to- please "sheet-smart" crowd. As the announcer makes another attempt to speak, his attention is abruptly diverted by a commotion at the entrance curtain.
>From behind the cloth curtains emerge THREE MEN who glow like platinum. Four hundred heads turn at exactly the same time as these figures glide towards the dusty ring. It's inexplicable; they just simply look different than everybody else in the arena. Their eye-catching shine is quite distinct from the pale skin of the largely male, teenage audience.
The man of the moment, PAUL PRATT, makes his way towards the ring. Paul is dressed to the nines in a beautiful Armani suit, which had to cost 10Gs easy. He's tall, handsome, but looks meek when compared to the men who stand beside him.
Flanking Pratt are two visually striking specimens. On the left is veteran superstar and flamboyant prettyboy "LUCIOUS" LARRY LAWRENCE, and on the right, SSW's freakishly muscular fast-rising star, "LETHAL"
LENNOX LANE. Larry has the cocky swagger that only a wrestler who has been in the ring with the very best could pull off. The well-travelled
Lane is a monster of a man who shows no fear. Paul, the brash loudmouth, was a manager back in the days when your IQ was more valuable than your cup size. His new role, however, is as part-owner of Shootclub International and promoter of its first league, Empire
State Wrestling.
He has entered the squared circle, flanked by his "bodyguards", and takes a walk around the ring to inspect his patrons. Apparently unimpressed, he approaches the center of the ring and bounces momentarily to test the frame, then rolls his eyes to Lawrence. Pratt unceremoniously dismisses the ring announcer as he takes the microphone from him.
PAUL PRATT: "Thank you everybody! Thank you!" You could hear a pin drop. PRATT: "I am so glad all of you could make it down to ringside, so you could hear what I had to say. As you know, I, Paul Pratt, have had many monikers through the years... 'Playboy', 'Prettyboy',
'Highroller', etcetera, etcetera. But now, I add a new title to the list... 'PROMOTER'!! And I'll even up that... 'EVIL PROMOTER'!"
Paul smiles, Larry smiles, and Lennox looks straight ahead. PRATT: "That's right. I have decided that I will not just 'mimic' or
'imitate' other promotions, I am going to just blatently rip them off.
So from now on, please refer to me as 'Evil Promoter' Paul Pratt." Some fans laugh, but most just look confused. PRATT: "I'm sure that you are all aware that Shootclub International, of which I am a one-half owner, has scoured the planet looking for the best talent available to bring to the viewing public, during this era of wrestling's greatest popularity ever!"
The crowd cheers. PRATT: "And I am happy to say, we HAVE succeeded in our quest!!!" The crowd is actually starting to get into it. PRATT: "We have secured to long-term, guaranteed contracts...the BEST
DAMN GROUP of wrestlers the world has ever seen in ONE PROMOTION!"
The house is rocking! Unbelieveable! PRATT: "And that promotion IS...." The crowd in unison leans forward with their eyes and ears open... PRATT: "...SHOOTING STAR WRESTLING!!!" The collective jaws of the whole crowd dropped to the floor once they realized that he was speaking about Empire State Wrestling's sister league, SSW!
PRATT: "Yes, Yes." Paul nods. The fans look confused and "200 PROOF" ADAM EDWARDS tosses a bottle in the ring, since the fans failed to act appropriately...
according to him. PRATT: "You best all watch that behavior, or your short-term contracts will get THAAAAAAAT much shorter."
As Paul warns the talent, "COMMANDER" CODY holds back Edwards to his chagrin. PRATT: "You see folks, ESW is the LEFTOVERS. Sloppy seconds, if you will. You wrestlers are the sorriest bunch of talents I have seen in a long time... Wrestling ability? None. Gimmicks? Pathetic. I really don't see much hope for you at all. None at all."
Larry is nodding and Lennox keeps staring. Paul then abruptly turns his attention to Lawrence.
PRATT: "What are you nodding at Lawrence?" The luscious one looks stunned. Pratt continues. PRATT: "Thank you for getting my coffee, shining my shoes, and for making sure my Wheaties stay crispy in milk. NOW, Larry Larry, GET OUT
THERE WITH THE REST OF THE CATTLE!!!" Larry looks like a deer in the headlights, staring in disbelief! Pratt talks out of the side of his mouth to Lane. PRATT: "Aww, look Len, he's like the stray cat you make the mistake of feeding a couple of times, and they just won't leave you alone. Well,
I'm cutting the cord, and you're back with the rest of these alleycats out here! You're a has-been, Lare, and if I have anything to say about it... YOU WILL NEVER WIN A MATCH AGAIN! And you KNOW I have the clout to pull it off!"
At that moment, it became all too clear to Lawrence that it was Pratt that was behind the referees screwing him out of every match he's had since he came to ESW 3 weeks ago. He had thought that once his good buddy Paul Pratt showed up, that things would be straightened out, oh how wrong he was. Lawrence charges Pratt, but Lane gets in between the two and Lawrence stops right in his tracks.
LARRY LAWRENCE: "You SOB, I'll kick your a-- and this oversized gorilla's a--. Come on!" Paul laughs at him and tells him to go down to ringside, while simulating tearing up a contract and tossing it in the air with his hands. Lawrence complies but shouts out a few more expletives.
PRATT: "Like I was saying, you are ALL second-rate wrestlers and I am truly disgusted that I am going to have to deal with the lot of you.
And NO, I will not fall into the same traps that all my fellow 'Evil
Promoters' fall into. I WILL NOT.. sign a 5-minutes in a cage if so- and-so beats so-and-so match... that is WAY too tired. I WILL NOT...
attempt to get my top star out of my company... you actually have to have a top star to do that. And finally... I WILL NOT... put the heavyweight title on myself... I like putting myself over but I have my limits. But I do have one promise..."
The crowd starts the A-HOLE chants almost on cue. PRATT: "My promise is to you is this... I WILL step down as being promoter of ESW in six-months time! GUARANTEED!"
The fans comes unglued, on cue. PRATT: "HOWEVER... It is up to you whether I step down because this league folds just like I expect it to and I can claim the mother-of- all tax write offs..."
Crowd boos. The fans are getting behind the wrestlers of Empire State
Wrestling! PRATT: "...OR whether it's because you all prove me wrong and become a tolerable, hell maybe even an entertaining, promotion to watch...
that's up to you! I win either way!" The crowd wanted to cheer but it turned quickly into a chorus of boos. PRATT: "That's it. Class dismissed." Paul and Lennox Lane leave the ring and head to the back, with their heads held high. The crowd and the wrestlers are all shocked with what just happened.
