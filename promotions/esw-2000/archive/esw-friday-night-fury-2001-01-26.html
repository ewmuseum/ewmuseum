Empire State Wrestling: Friday Night Fury

26 January 2001

http://shootclub.com [detail withheld] FRIDAY NIGHT FURY Empire State Wrestling - Manhattan Center, NYC The production quality of Friday Night Fury has improved dramatically from the early days of ESW, but it's still a small time promotion, anyway you cut it. It does however show dramatic promise. The crowd is primed after an electric dark match between Chris Cannon and Jesse
Smith. The crowd erupts when Jim Bossy announces that they are "live"!
Over the PA system, which is much better than the boom box on the timekeepers table that still accompanies them on the 'C' card house shows, blares the new music of the man in ESW. THE man is not Mat
Hellion, but rather.... (music kicks in) No Payday... that's what ya get Have your chain pulled too long, Dopey workers selling bodies for us are SPROCKETS Interchangable, replaceable, and expendable. Find your place in the unemployment line 'cause it's, it's just a matter of time, cause you get... NO Payday... The crowd stands up and boos the one person that the Promoter of this league thinks is it's only true star... himself. Paul Pratt, the self- proclaimed 'evil' promoter of ESW confidently struts his way to the ring. He is smiling like the cat that just ate the canary. He swaggers into the ring, and proceeds to address the crowd with the only wireless mic that ESW owns.
PRATT: "Thank you, thank you! Welcome everybody to FRIDAY NIGHT FURY!" It's sounds like he is about to rip his larynx to shreds. PRATT: "I am in a good mood tonight! And why, you ask? Why? Because we made it to the city! New York City! The Big Apple! And once we have New
York under our belt, there is no stopping the ESW from becoming the
BIGGEST damn thing in the wrestling business! And Jake Collossal..."
Paul's expression changes for the worse... PRATT: "I may be part of your "team" but I am not your crony. The ESW is primed and ready to be the top dog in your little project, the
Shootclub Wrestling Alliance. But you listen to me BOY, you try to push us to the side... you try to put us in the corner... and it will be the
BIGGEST mistake you'll ever make. I will jerk the rug out from under you... I will take my promotion out of the SWA... and I will put you
OUT OF BUSINESS and don't you foget it!"
The crowd cheers the Highroller due to the pride they have in their promotion. They even start an "ESW, ESW, ESW" chant!
PRATT: "Now back to the business at hand. Farm-hands get out here, it's time to work the crops..."
The crowd is confused until they see that star after star in the league come out. The first two are the Sharp Dressed Men, Douglas and Golden, followed by Larry Greenbrawler, Dr. Love, the Hornet, Psycho BOB, ZoSo,
Weedman, and trailed by Zero Tolerance, Adam Edwards and ESW
Heavyweight Champion Mat Hellion. They now understand, who the farm hands are, and most don't look too pleased... except, for Golden and
Douglas who "know" they aren't being talked to.
PRATT: "Well boys, it's been a long time since I have addressed you in public but things are all in disarray. Hell, most of the fans out there don't even know who to boo and who to cheer for anymore."
The 10 men in the ring look indifferent to that statement. They are here to "make it", not to worry about whether they are loved or cheered for... or at least that's what the majority of the men say they believe.
PRATT: "Well, I am here to clear things up. I am the good guy here. I put money in your pockets, food in your bellies, and plates for your barbells. But the problem I have here is that some of you do not see it that way. You don't see it that way at all! You think I am here to chew you up and spit you out. Not true, not true! I am your father... I help you, I protect you, and I TEACH you. And tonight I am going to TEACH a few lessons."
The wrestlers look at each other and the pain is visible in all their faces... other than the S.D.M. that is.
PRATT: "Dr. Love. My first acquisition for the ESW. You have been a great performer in and out of the ring. Publically, we don't see eye to eye. But privately, you have come to see me to solve your problems time and time again. But my good Doctor, that time has ended. Tonight, I am giving you your chance to solve your OWN problems, Tonight, in a ring that looks remarkably like the one we are standing in, you will be facing this punk, here... Weedman. Weedman, buckle up buddy boy, you have been talking a lot of trash it is time for you to back up those words."
Doctor Love, who is standing beside his Nurse Candy Hart, is glaring directly at the Weedman who has glassy-eyed smile across his face and is saying "No problemo" to Pratt, off-mic.
PRATT: "However... there is one stipulation, due to the issues regarding that misfire of a story line a couple weeks ago, Nurse Candy
Hart will not be allowed at ringside."
Nurse Candy Hart looks upset, but the two men are not distracted in the least. PRATT: "This match is about wrestling and not who-is-who's nurse. That story line has been overdone in nursing homes all across the country...
and I apologize for coming up with it in the first place!" Pratt then moves over to Greenbrawler and looks him dead in the eye. Greenbrawler is considered the toughest wrestler in the league by many, and he doesn't back down an inch when approached by the small (by comparison) Pratt. In fact, Pratt reverses himself a little before speaking.
PRATT: "Book yourself in many main-events lately? It looks like you and
Weedman are trying to weasle your way into the Pay-Per-View title picture. The championship committee might not take kindly to your forwardness. But I am here to do you a favor, my burly friend. I will give you an opportunity to prove yourself in Triple Threat matches against two very worthy opponents... 99 time-TV champion ZoSo!"
Larry looks confident even though he knows the large ZoSo, is about as tough as they get. PRATT: "And facing Larry and ZoSo is... the Hornet!" The announcers wonder if the Hornet's face is dropping behind the mask. Hornet has held his own with the toughest of the league... but two at the same time may be a little too much for him!
PRATT: "Some of you may be wondering where Triple-L, Larry Lawrence is... well... I am giving him the biggest punishment I can think of for this ego-maniac... no AIR-TIME! That's right, when he showed up to the arena, I had security send him home. And this is not some overdone angle, where he shows up at the end to mess up the main event. He has been sent home, and maybe, just maybe he can prove himself and get back on TELEVISION AGAIN!! But until that time... no TV for Larry! His DADDY said so!"
Paul is circling the ring looking at his hired hands. Most of them smile when they hear of Larry's bad fortune. Paul comes to a sudden stop and looks directly at the champion, Mat Hellion, and he loses all the amusement that he had. He tells all the boys to leave the ring.
They all hesitate and cautiously exit. The boys all know not to start beating on each other now... because it is the boss's time in the spotlight and the worse thing you can do is steal spotlight from the real egomaniac in the ESW. All leave quietly except the Sharp Dressed
Men, who know their role is Pratt's plans, and Edwards who would never leave the side of his best friend.
PRATT: "Tisk, tisk, tisk. Mat Hellion. ESW HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPION, Mat
Hellion. It sounds so good, doesn't it? Almost poetic. Poetic!"
Paul's lips curl up in an awkward smirk and he shakes his head in disappointment. PRATT: "YOU, my friend, have a great gift. YOU, my friend, are my top dog. I know, I know. Your image, your image. You're a loner. A wild- man. You don't NEED this title. You don't NEED these fans. You don't
NEED me."
Mat agrees with Pratt and Paul fills with rage... but takes a minute to breathe, before he addresses his franchise wrestler.
PRATT: "You're DAMN RIGHT! You don't NEED this title, or these fans... and you sure as hell don't NEED me!" Mat is shocked, something that never happens to the man who makes his living by shocking people.
PRATT: "You DO NOT... NEED ME... AS AN ENEMY! THAT, my friend would be the biggest mistake of what should be a long career! So that is why I am telling you now... DO NOT screw this up! Do not disrepect me. Do not forget that I am person who put you where your now... You like your parties, you like your friends...."
Paul looks directly at Adam Edwards. PRATT: "And I even think, there is a growing part of you that likes these fans!" The fans cheer and Hellion doesn't change his expression. PRATT: "Well you my friend, best get your priorities straight. And to you, I would like to teach the hardest lesson. BUT, unfortunately, the championship committee has determined that you will be facing the rising star of Psycho BOB tonight. It should be a great, two nuts going at the highest prize in the game... can't wait."
Paul seems very disappointed and seems almost defeated. But then he perks up. PRATT: "But yes, there issss.... SISTER. Adam Edwards, your tag-along booze buddy may have to pay for your mistakes. Tonight, Mr. Edwards, you will be facing the team of Don Douglas and Johnny Golden, the Sharp
Dressed Men in a handicap match. Maybe then, Hellion, you will learn where your bread is buttered. Once Edwards is up in the ICU, you will see what a loser he is and come to your senses. And DON'T EVEN THINK about interfering, or I will strip that belt off of you so fast it will make your head spin! So, Hellion, off to the back with you... let's get this night started."
