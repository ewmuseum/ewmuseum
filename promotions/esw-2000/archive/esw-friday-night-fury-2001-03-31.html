Empire State Wrestling: Friday Night Fury

31 March 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
SHOOTCLUB: http://shootclub.com FRIDAY NIGHT FURY: http://shootclub.com/cgi-bin/web.py?pg=rp&id=68 Below is an excerpt from today's FRIDAY NIGHT FURY... to read the rest go to the above link.....
SHOOTCLUB INTERNATIONAL and PRATT ENTERPRISES present... LIVE FROM THE BIG APPLE... NEW YORK CITY!!! THIS IS ESW FRIDAY NIGHT FURY!!! TROLL: "Coming to you from the heart of Manhattan, in NEW YORK CITY...welcome to ESW FURY!!! I'm MICHAEL TROLL, and alongside me in the broadcast booth is none other than an old favorite up here in New York, the legendary 'MR. DEFECT' HURT LEMMING!"
LEMMING: "Let me tell you something Troll, I'm no OLD favorite! I'm telling you, if it wasn't for my back, neck, hip, knee, ankle, pancreas, and pinky toe, I'd be in that wrestling ring RIGHT NOW showing all those PUNKS what being a DEFECT is all about!"
TROLL: "But for NOW, you're here with ME, calling the greatest action from the greatest superstars in Empire State Wrestling, and WHAT A SHOW we have tonight! We're going to start things off in a BIG WAY, with a BIG TV TITLE matchup! 'LUSCIOUS' LARRY LAWRENCE of Hot Commodities Incorporated puts his title on the line against a former ESW heavyweight champion, and one of the most dominant champions in HISTORY, Hurt... LARRY GREENBRAWLER!"
LEMMING: "Larry Lawrence is good, DAMN good, Troll... but he's no DEFECT. Then again, neither is Larry Greenbrawler. Hell, NEITHER of them are as good a LARRY as my dad, *LARRY* 'THE BATTLEAXE' LEMMING, but few are, my friend, few are! Anyway... it's gonna be a helluva match!"
TROLL: "It's gonna be OFF THE PAGE, Mr. D! And later on, THE HORNET defends the ESW title against another crowd favorite... the man that beat him for the title earlier this month, the fire-breathing ZOSO!!! That and SO MUCH
MORE in the hour, so let's go to the ring and get it started HERE on FRIDAY
NIGHT FURY!!!" We go backstage, where "EVIL PROMOTER" PAUL PRATT is standing on the other side of the curtain with TV champ, and participant in the opening match,
"LUSCIOUS" LARRY LAWRENCE, as well as JOHNNY GOLDEN and "DIAMOND" DON
DOUGLAS, former tag champs THE SHARP DRESSED MEN. PRATT: (pointing at Lawrence) "Okay TRIPP, TV title, you and the Green
Machine. Let's go out there and show Shootclub that HOT COMMODITIES
INCORPORATED is for REAL. The HEAT is ON, Larry, let's GO!" As Larry goes through the curtain and into the arena, Pratt remains behind to have a word with the Sharp Dressed Men.
PRATT: "What's going on with you, 'John-John'? 'DEE-DEE'? I thought we were
TIGHT? LISTEN... you've had the contract in your hands for a WHILE now, but you STILL haven't officially signed on to be a part of HCI!"
GOLDEN: (puts his arm around Pratt) "We're TIGHTER than tight, man, but there's just a FEW things we have to work out, Paul... you know, the FINE
PRINT." PRATT: "Yeah, so I hear. My lawyers are telling me that you're dragging your feet! What, you think I'm gonna rake YOU GUYS over the coals? After all I've
DONE for you?"
DOUGLAS: (slaps Pratt on the back) "We've had your back from the VERY beginning, buddy. We just want to be sure that WE'RE getting what WE need out of the deal! Hey, why are we dealing with your LAWYERS anyway? I mean, we haven't even SEEN you since Crowded House!"
PRATT: (sighs) "I'm VERY BUSY, Don... do you FORGET that I have a LEAGUE to run here? And this whole SWA thing is..."
The audio mysteriously goes dead for a moment (a work of Shootclub?)... PRATT: "...in my side. Look, I've invested A LOT in you guys, and I NEED to know that you're on THIS cruise for the LONG HAUL! This is RIDICULOUS.
(points over his shoulder) Okay, I gotta get out there... let's discuss this
AFTER the show. Let's think about TONIGHT now... REMEMBER?"
The SDMs shake their heads simultaneously, and Pratt goes through the curtain to join his man at ringside. Golden and Douglas shoot each other a curious look as we cut to the arena.
ESW Television Championship: "LUSCIOUS" LARRY LAWRENCE w/ "Evil Promoter" Paul Pratt vs. "CRUSHER" LARRY GREENBRAWLER The TV champ is in the ring giving the fans hell, and here comes Pratt jogging down the aisle to join his charge. Then, the booming theme of the challenger kicks in and LARRY GREENBRAWLER makes his entrance! Greenie enters the ring and stares across at Lawrence... then to Pratt. Pratt takes a step forward... and GREENBRAWLER LUNGES!!! WAIT! LAWRENCE RUNS
INTERFERENCE! He dove right between them with a forearm to the cheek to
Greenbrawler! The TV champ lays in the stomps as Pratt high-tails it to the floor!
One stomp after another as Lawrence starts in with the smack talk to Greenbrawler that he is so famous for! On one stomp, Greenbrawler CATCHES
HIS FOOT and holds it there! He sits up and twists the ankle around, tripping LLL up and dropping him to the mat face-first! STF by GREENIE! And the SLAPS to the back of Lawrence's head! VICIOUS! The Crusher gets up and picks up Lawrence... off the ropes, LLL ducks a clothesline... back again,
SIDESTEPS A BIG BOOT... off again, SPEAR BY GREENBRAWLER takes him down!
WOAH! BIG RIGHTS BY THE CRUSHER TO THE TEMPLE! LOOK... HERE ARE THE SHARP DRESSED MEN! What are they doing here? They walk down the aisle slowly as Greenbrawler continues the punishment! The two stop at the apron, on the opposite side that Paul is on. Pratt is SCREAMING instructions across the ring, but I don't know that the Men hear him. Pratt stomps around the ringside area to the SDMs and asks them what is going on!
Pratt is yelling and pointing at the ring... the referee comes over to that side of the ring to see what the problem is.
Greenbrawler has LLL up... gutwrench suplex! Up again... vertical suplex... NO! Lawrence slips down and lands behind him! Forearm to the Crusher's back,
LLL turns him around...
Pratt is motioning for Johnny Golden to TRIP someone in the ring! Golden is nodding his head, and glaces at the ring, as Lawrence sends Greenbrawler towards them with an irish whip! The ref jumps out fo the way! Golden turns back to Pratt to make sure he's watching... CRUSHER REVERSES THE IRISH WHIP!
Lawrence into the ropes... TRIPPED UP BY GOLDEN! Golden didn't see the reversal, and Lawrence went into the mat RIGHT ON HIS NOSE!! He comes up
BLOODIED! PRATT IS GOING NUTS!!! Lawrence gets up and turns towards the SDMs on the floor! But here's Larry G! Behind Larry L... BELLY TO BACK SUPLEX by
Greenie! Greenbrawler yells a few words at Golden and Douglas and turns back to LLL on the mat. DOUGLAS REACHES IN AND TRIPS UP GREENBRAWLER! He falls...
WITH A HEADBUTT TO LAWRENCE'S GROIN! OH MAN, it's falling apart! Greenbrawler laughs and gets up... and REACHES over the ropes, pulling up
Douglas by the hair! Up on the apron! Golden follows him up! Golden yells
"DUCK!" to "Diamond" Don and comes across the apron with a DIVING clothesline to Greenbralwer in the ring, send him over INTO THE TOP
TURNBUCKLE as Golden falls over Douglas to the concrete... and ONTO PRATT! MY GOD! FINALLY, the ref calls for the bell... WHO KNOWS what the call is though. Lawrence is on his knees now, wiping his bloody nose as Greenbralwer staggers back... UPPERCUT LOW BLOW! He gets up and HOLDS THE CRUSHER for
Douglas on the apron! Did LLL see how that low-blow headbutt went down?
Douglas pulls a ROLL OF QUARTERS from his jacket pocket! DON SWINGS... GREENBRAWLER TAKES TEN BUCKS IN THE EAR!!! QUARTERS FLY EVERYWHERE!!! Golden gets up off Pratt and climbs into the ring, as does Douglas, and they start in with the boots on Larry G! A dazed Pratt soon joins them, and it's 3 on
1! FINALLY, these three are on the same page!
TAG TEAM CHAMPS ZERO TOLERANCE JUMP TROUGH THE CURTAIN!!! Mat Hellion and
Adam Edwards dive into the ring to the cheers of the crowd! ZT and the SDMs have had a LONG rivalry on the road, not to mention Hellion's former allegiance with Pratt! Pratt slides out to the floor to avoid the onslaught!
Golden is there to meet Edwards... punches back and forth! Hellion meets
"Diamond" Don... down to the mat they go! Lawrence is still on top of
Greenbrawler, but gets up! DROPKICK TO EDWARDS' BACK! Edwards goes into
Golden, into the corner they go! Lawrence pulls Hellion off Douglas by the hair... and they engage in a momentary stare-down before THEY come to blows!
But NO, HERE COMES THE CAVALRY! This is NOT going to happen now! The other refs and members of the office are down there to calm things down. We have NO winner in this match, but this is NOT over!
