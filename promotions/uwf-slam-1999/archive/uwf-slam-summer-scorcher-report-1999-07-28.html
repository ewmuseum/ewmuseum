******************************* Ultimate Wrestling Federation http://members.xoom.com/RGano/UWF2 ******************************* "Summer Scorcher" Report July 28th, 1999 The UWF is less than 2 weeks away from their 3rd PPV entitled "Summer
Scorcher" 5 title matches will take place at the UWF's annual August
PPV! In this report, I will concentrate on a few matches that are guaranteed to take place at the PPV.
In the Main Event, The UWF's Ultimate Champion, Archangel, will face off against 2 of his biggest challenges in a "Triple Threat" match for the
UWF's greatest prize! This is by far the UWF's greatest PPV Main Event as it is a combination of the first 2 Main Events from "Ocean Wave" &
"Championship Mania" Archangel hopes to continue his 1 1/2 month title reign at the PPV. Unforgiven, who recently lost the UWF Intercontinental Championship to
Rockin' Rebel, will attempt to win back the gold at the PPV. Of course, it is unknown who will be the champion at "Summer Scorcher" because
Rockin' Rebel is defending the title against Deathstalker this saturday on UWF Slam. Regardless of who the champion is, Unforgiven will get the title shot!
Priest will be holding a "special" match at the PPV for a 2nd month in a row! This time, Priest has challenged Mighty Tak to a flaming steel cage match that he refers to as a "Burn in Hell" match. We have seen the destructive power of Priest in the past. It will take alot from the former DoD member to survive this encounter!
