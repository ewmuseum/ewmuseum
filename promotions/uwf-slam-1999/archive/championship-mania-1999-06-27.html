Championship Mania

27 June 1999 (posted 12 July 1999)

The UWF recently brought it's first PPV to the fans! This sunday, the UWF is about to do it again when it presents "Ocean Wave" You can catch the UWF action at http://members.xoom.com/RGano/UWF2 Here is what happened at "Championship Mania", the UWF's first PPV! ---------------------------------------- "Championship Mania" June 27th, 1999 As "Championship Mania" comes on the air, the camera scans over the 25,000 fans that have attended this milestone event in the UWF! Several fans can be seen holding signs which say "DoD 4-Life", "Archangel
Rules!", & "Unmask the Unknown!" Many fans can be seen yelling throughout the arena, ready for the action to start!
UWF PRESIDENT INTERVIEW The UWF President, [name withheld], came down to ringside at the start of the PPV. The President stated that Deathstalker would not be able to compete because of a dislocated left shoulder. Since Deathstalker was supposed to compete in the UWF Ultimate Championship tournament, it was
decided that the winner of the Unforgiven Vs. Chaple match would take Deathstalker's place & face Rockin' Rebel in the opening round of the tournament. CHAPLE Vs. UNFORGIVEN The first match of the PPV got underway as Unforgiven prepared to face Chaple (With Priest). For those that are new to the UWF, Chaple was the one who hit Unforgiven over the head at UWF Fever, allowing him to be defeated for the first time. It also ended the winning streak of his friend, Archangel. As the match started, Chaple considered his options of getting the big man off of his feet. Since he was outweighed by over
150 pounds, there was a huge disadvantage for Chaple. Unforgiven was seemingly not hurt as Chaple began hitting dropkicks & other aerial moves against Unforgiven. Finally, realizing that something had to be done, Chaple gave Unforgiven a kick between the legs! The referee warned Chaple, but the Puerto Rican Assasin kept on kicking him.
Bearing alot of pain, Unforgiven went down to the mat. Chaple then used alot of mat moves against Unforgiven. As Unforgiven began to get worn out, Chaple picked-up Unforgiven & tried to choke slam him. Realizing he was too big to choke slam, Chaple paniced. Unforgiven the reversed things & executed a choke slam of his own on Chaple. The referee ran over & made the 3 count. Unforgiven won the right to face Rockin' Rebel in the first round of the tournament. After the match, Chaple ran to the outside & grabbed a steel chair. When he returned to the ring with the chair, he nailed Unforgiven over the head with the chair. The big man staggered over the top rope. Chaple then battled with Unforgiven to the backstage area.
PRINCES OF DARKNESS Vs. ZORAN & FIRESTALKER This match was scheduled to determine who would face the UWF Tag Team Champions at UWF Fever this wednesday. Since this was an important match, both teams knew that they had to give 100%. The Princes of
Darkness (PoD) had already wrestled together in a couple of tag team matches in the past weeks. As for their opponents, they had a tough time just going down the aisle towards the ring, let alone prepare as a tag team. As the opening bell sounded, Cobra & Death Venom immediately attackes their foes. As they knocked Zoran outside of the ring, they concentrated on Firestalker. The former Stalkers member was hit with a double clothesline as he rebounded from the ropes. Zoran, realizing that he had the opportunity to leave Firestalker against 2 people to get beat up, left the ring area. As Firestalker watched his tag team partner head back to the locker room, Cobra knocked him to the mat & applied the "Cobra Clutch", forcing Firestalker to submit. The PoD
earned the right to face the winner of the Hoodlam Vs. Rhinestone Cowboys match at UWF Fever for the UWF Tag Team Championship! FIRST ROUND MATCH ROCKIN' REBEL Vs. UNFORGIVEN Unforgiven made his way to the ring with his forehead bleeding from the chair shot he recieved via Chaple. Rockin' Rebel headed down the aisle with a microphone in his hand. As he headed towards the ring, he kept telling the crowd that this "Jolly Giant" could never beat him after already wrestling one match. Rockin' Rebel's mouth seemed to get him in a bit of trouble though as Unforgiven immediately placed Rockin' Rebel in a claw hold. The big man then lifted him up & threw him into the ring. Rockin' Rebel, in a panic frenzy, crawled out from the ring. As he regrouped, Unforgiven waited patiently. Rockin' Rebel re-entered the ring & decided to keep his mouth shut. Unforgiven went to hit Rockin'
Rebel with an overhead smash, but Rebel ducked the move & delivered a right fist. The same pattern repeated itself a couple of times as
Rockin' Rebel played a defensive role against his opponent. But Unforgiven could not be tricked all night! The huge monster got tired of playing these games & leveled Rebel with a clothesline. Unforgiven gave the signal for the four corners choke slam. Unforgiven then picked up his foe & repeatedly slamed him into each of the 4 corners before executing a choke slam in the middle of the ring. With the referee registering the pin, Unforgiven would advance in the tournament, facing his friend Archangel or Unknown Wrestler.
FIRST ROUND MATCH ARCHANGEL Vs. UNKNOWN WRESTLER Unknown Wrestler made his way to the ring with his manager, Mr G. The crowd eagerly awaited the entrace of Archangel as his music played over the speakers. As Archangel appeared through the curtain, he began running towards the ring. He slid under the ropes and began to hit both
Unknown Wrestler & Mr. G with closed fists. Archangel threw the manager over the top rope. Unknown Wrestler ran over to Archangel and hit him with a double-ax handle to the back. Archangel went down to the mat after Unknown Wrestler hit a bulldog. As Archangel lay face down on the mat, Unknown Wrestler ran over to his manager for advice. The masked wrestler went over to Archangel to pick him up, but Archangel beat him to it & picked up Unknown Wrestler for a body slam. Archangel tried to follow it up with an elbow drop, but Unknown Wrestler moved out of the way. As both men made it to their feet, Artchangel ran up to Unknown
Wrestler & nailed a running clothesline. As Unknown Wrestler stumbled around the ring, Archangel picked him up & prepared for a powerslam.
Unknown Wrestler tried shifting his weight onto Archangel, but could never change things. Archangel still hit the powerslam. A close cover by Archangel followed with a vicious DDT. With Unknown Wrestler dazed,
Archangel seized the opportunity to hit the "Elevator to Hell" & the referee made the pinfall. Archangel would officially go on to face his friend Unforgiven in the second round of the tournament.
FIRST ROUND MATCH "BIG D" DEVESTATION Vs. GOLDEN ONE Golden One, a former #1 ranked wrestler here in the UWF, made his way to the ring first. When "Morphine" by Michael Jackson played over the speakers, the crowd knew that "Big D" was on his way to the ring.
Devestation made his way out with Z Gangsta & Master Rapper, the other members of the DoD. Golden One seemed to be a bit worried about the 3 members of the DoD being at ringside, so he began talking to the referee. This talk continued for about 15 seconds. The referee talked to the ring announcer & an announcement was made stating that Hoodlam would not be allowed at ringside for this match. Z Gangsta & Master
Rapper protested, but finally went back to the locker room. The match between these 2 UWF wrestlers was a classic. Golden One tried for several aerial moves that helped him in past matches. "Big D" just kept swatting Golden One down like a fly during each of his moves. During the match, Devestation kept flashing the crowd the DoD hand symbol.
Golden One blocked a clothesline attempt and nailed "Big D" with a closed fist. Golden One enjoyed a brief moment of offense, but
Devestation quickly reversed things with a shoulder block. Knowing that that one move like that hurt Golden One, "Big D" prepared Golden One for a "Razor's Edge" Golden One seemed to be almost knocked out when
Devestation executed the move. Finally, "Big D" hit Golden One with his
"Devestator" running powerbomb for the pinfall. "Big D" would now face
the winner of the Mighty Tak Vs. American Made match-up. FIRST ROUND MATCH AMERICAN MADE Vs. MIGHTY TAK American Made, a man that has a few months of ring experience, was scheduled to face the man in the UWF with the least ring experience,
Mighty Tak. "Mr. USA" made his way to the ring followed by his opponent. The former soldier was hoping that his quick self-taught wrestling lessons would help him advance in the tournaent. American
Made with his power advantage, began the match fist by tossing Tak into a corner. Tak replied by running out of the corner & tackling American
Made in the middle of the ring. Mighty Tak then repeatedly hit American
Made in the head with several closed fists. American Made pushed of
Mighty Tak from on top of him. Mighty Tak watched as American Made hit the ropes & leap at him with a shoulder block. The force of the move knocked Mighty Tak out of the ring. The crowd turned towards the aisle as "Big D" Devestation made he way down the aisle with a steel chair.
The referee watched as "Big D" hit Mighty Tak on the back with a steel chair, causing Mighty Tak to win via DQ for outside interfierence. As
"Big D" walked back down the aisle towards the locker room, American Made confronted him, upset because he ruined his chances to become the UWF Ultimate Champion. Devestation responded by nailing American Made with the chair as well.
SEMIFINALS ARCHANGEL Vs. UNFORGIVEN The second round of the tournament started with a match between friends as Archangel faced off against the man he helped bring into the UWF,
Unforgiven. The corwd wondered if these 2 friends would wrestle against each other. Unforgiven walked down to the ring with a bandage on his forehead from the earlier chair shot by Chaple. Archangel came to the ring and shook hands with his friend before the bell rang. Once the bell sounded, both men went at it with a vengence. With Unforgiven shoving Archangel against the ropes, the referee tried to break things up. Realizing that he could not seperate these mad men, he decided to just stand back & watch. Archangel managed to crawl between
Unforgiven's legs & then began pressing him up against the ropes. Unforgiven got out by nailing Archangel with an elbow in the side of the head. Archangel stumbled back & ran into the referee. The referee was knocked out on the mat. Archangel & Unforgiven kept fighting against each other as Chaple made his way out and walked to ringside. Neither man saw Chaple as he stood there. Archangel & Unforgiven went to the ropes & executed a double clothesline. Both men laid down on the mat in the middle of the ring. Chaple took this opportunity to pick-up a steel chair from ringside & walked into the ring. As Unforgiven began to sit up, Chaple hit him over the head with the chair. Chaple exited the ring with the chair. By this time, the referee was waking up along with
Archangel. Not knowing that Chaple hit his friend with the chair, Archangel made a pin against Unforgiven, advancing to the finals of the tournament. SEMIFINALS "BIG D" DEVESTATION Vs. MIGHTY TAK Prior to the start of this match, Devestation said in an interview that he intentionally wanted Mighty Tak to face him in the semifinals of the tournament to get revenge on him for tricking the DoD just a couple of weeks earlier. Mighty Tak made his way to the ring first followed by
"Big D" When Devestation walked into the ring. Mighty Tak told Devestation that this would be the final time they ever faced each other. As Devestation came up to Mighty Tak, he punched him in the face, knocking him down. Mighty Tak was not moving as he laid on the mat. Devestation got in pinning position as the referee counted to 3.
As the referee raised Devestation's hand, Mighty Tak got up & ran over to "Big D" & gave him a hug. The fans were confused about the events until Devestation took the microphone and announced Mighty Tak as the newest member of the Dungeon of Devestation! Even though "Big D" had hardly wrestled the entire night, he earned the right to face Archangel for the UWF Ultimate Championship in the Main Event!
UWF TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP HOODLAM (With "Big D") Vs. RHINESTONE COWBOYS The top rated tag team in the UWF, Hoodlam faced off against a team that just got their first win ever on UWF Slam the day before, the Rhinestone
Cowboys. Cowboy Curtis & Cowboy Jack both got wins on UWF Slam by winning their triple threat matches, but they have never had a tag team victory. Z Gangsta & Master Rapper came to the ring escorted by "Big D"
and the DoD's newest member Mighty Tak. The Rhinestone Cowboys were hoping that they could get an upset in this title match so they could be the owners of the Tag Team Championship. Before the match, Mighty Tak managed to sneak up behind Cowboy Jack, who was on the ring apron, and trip him from behind. He managed to fall off & smash his face against the ring apron. Cowboy Curtis, who was in the ring, was met by both members of Hoodlam who tossed him outside the ring. From there,
Devestation moved over to where Cowboy Curtis laid on the outside & gave him a massive splash. "Big D" rolled him back into the ring. Cowboy seemed to be at a huge disadvantage against what seemed to be 4 men since his tag team partner hit his face on the ring apron & was half conscious. Cowboy Curtis put up an admirable fight against Hoodlam.
During the final moments of the match, Z Gangsta was thrown into the corner. Cowboy Curtis tried to hit a "Bronco Buster" against Z Gangsta, but the youngster caught him in mid-air & reversed his move into a spinebuster slam. The referee made the 3-count & Hoodlam walked out of the ring with the UWF Tag Team Championship!
UWF ULTIMATE CHAMPIONSHIP ARCHANGEL Vs. "BIG D" DEVESTATION It was no surprise to many of the fans in attendance that the finals of the tournament would be Archangel Vs. Devestation. After all, these men had fought off the best UWF wrestlers since the day they arrived on the scene. Now these 2 men would collide in the middle of the ring for the UWF's greatest prize... The UWF Ultimate Championship!
Archangel is escorted to the ring by Unforgiven. The huge monster, now with a couple of bandages on his head is Archangel's insurance policy to make sure the DoD doesn't get involved in the match.
The Dungeon of Devestation escorts their leader, "Big D" Devestation down the aisle. The 6'7, 450 pound former UWA champion was determined to win the title for the DoD. As "Big D" stepped into the ring, the tention between the 2 opponents could be felt.
The match started out with a traditional "feeling out" moves like arm & collar tie-ups, running to the ropes, & closed fists. "Big D", who has not wrestled as much as his opponent during the PPV, quickly gained the advantage by hitting Archangel in the head with closed fists.
Devestation sent Archangel to the ropes & hit him with a running shoulder block. Archangel would not stay down though. The "extreme"
UWF wrestler leaped up from the mat & went after Devestation with a frenzy! Archangel hit Devestation with closed fists, elbows, & clotheslines as "Big D" began to stumble. Mighty Tak tried to climb into the ring, but Unforgiven quickly ran over and pulled him down. In the ring Archangel continued to destroy with several moves which could only be called "extreme". Archangel gathered a large amount of strength
& managed to body slam his much larger opponent. With "Big D" on the mat, Archangel attempted to go to the top rope for a move, but Z Gangsta reached up & made the ropes shake, causing Archangel to fall off. Both men were down on the mat as the referee made a count.
1..2..3..4..5..6..7.. Finally, both men started getting back up to their feet. Archangel went over to the ropes, but Master Rapper pulled the rope down & Archangel fell out the the floor. The DoD attempted to interfier, but Unforgiven ran over again & started hammering on the DoD, letting his friend re-enter the ring. Devestation wanted to capitolize on his much weaker foe & gave the signal for the "Devestator".
Devestation put his foe into position, but as he went for the move, Archangel escaped behind him & pushed "Big D" into the turnbuckle in front of him. Devestation started walking backwards in pain & Archangel rolled him up from behind. The referee counted 1... 2... 3!!! The
25,000 fans in the arena jumped to their feet as Archangel overcame many odds enroute to becoming the UWF's first ever Ultimate Champion!
