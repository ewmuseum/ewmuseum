UWF Fever

18 November 1999 (posted 19 November 1999)

Ultimate Wrestling Federation http://www.geocities.com/uwfslam ================= UWF LIVE! November 18th, 1999 (Several still shots are displayed from the action at "Strive 2 Survive" last sunday night.) Announcer:  Welcome everyone to "UWF Live!"... The UWF's weekly news-talk program!  This week, the UWF welcomes Great Numpty & the current UWF Intercontinental Champion, Mike "Showtime" Bradshaw!  We'll also take some calls from the fans!  And now... Here are the announcers for UWF Live, Jake Stevenson & Chris Armstrong! (Jake & Chris make their way out to the stage area to a tremendous amount of applause.) Jake:  Thank you all so much for joining us for this edition of the UWF's News-Talk show, "UWF Live!"    Tonight, we are going to have a knockout show as we have both Great Numpty & the UWF Intercontinental Champion, Mike "Showtime" Bradshaw on the show!  But first, we need to take some time out for a commercial! (Commercial) (Scene is shown of Santa's elves working hard in their toy workshop on the North Pole.  The elves are busy putting together some wooden toys & whistling "We wish you a Merry Christmas".) What happens when the spirit of Christmas invades the UWF? (The doors of the workshop bust open & 2 wrestlers begin to brawl throughout the workshop.  Toys are picked up & used as weapons against one another as they continue to brawl.) It becomes "Season's Beatings"! The UWF Presents "Season's Beatings" Only on PPV --- December 12th, 1999 Jake:  "Season's Beatings is sure to be a great PPV for the UWF!  It's the start of the show & I'm here to bring out our first guest!  He has a guaranteed shot at the UWF European Championship this saturday night on UWF Slam, let's bring out Great Numpty! (Fans in the audience begin to cheer for Great Numpty!) Jake:  Numpty, this past sunday night on "Strive 2 Survive", you got to compete in a special "Elimination Match" with 7 other wrestlers.  The winner of the match would earn a title shot against the UWF European Champion, but you managed to win that match with Saint when you both went over the top rope at the same time!  What was going on in your mind during that match? Great Numpty:  Winning!  Plain & simple!  I didn't come here to the UWF to lose, did I? Jake:  Obviously not, but now you have your title shot against the current European Champion, Shogun Assassin.  Not only willyou be facing
Shogun Assassin, but you will also have the pleasure of facing off against
Saint in the same match-up! Great Numpty:  Saint...  I remember the way that he came at me with a vengence!  I will return the favor this saturday night when I beat you and Shogun in the middle of the ring with the "Numpty Drop"! Jake:  That's a tough challenge ahead of you! Great Numpty:  Tough?  I had a tough match at "Strive 2 Survive", but I came out on top there as well!  I will have gold around on my waist here in the UWF & it will happen at Shogun Assassin's expense! Jake:  That's all the time we have for our interview session.  I'm sure that fans around the world will want to ask you questions during the call-in portion of the program.  Ladies & Gentlemen, let's hear it for the man that is aiming for the European Championship, Great Numpty! (Crowd cheers as the cameras fade to a commercial.) (Commercial) (Video clips are shown of the UWF Ultimate Champion, "Big D" Devastation, using his patented "Devastator" on different wrestlers.) "Big D" has been running wild over the UWF competition... (More scenes are shown of "Big D" battering his opponents.) Can anyone stand up to the challenge? The UWF Presents "Season's Beatings" Only on PPV --- December 12th, 1999 Chris:  We're back again!  Our second guest of the evening is the reigning UWF Intercontinental Champion!  Here is Mike "Showtime" Bradshaw! (Crowd gives a loud cheer for Bradshaw as he enters the studio.) Chris:  Mike, you are one of the few wrestlers in the UWF to ever win a UWF Championship belt within the first month of competition!  That is a truly amazing feat! Mike "Showtime" Bradshaw:  I've got to be honest & say that I am privilaged!  I took out a respectable champion! Chris:  I'm sure that all of your fans around the world would like to get the inside scoop on your match this saturday night on Slam!  If your team wins, then Kurt-Pac gets a shot at "Big D" for the Ultimate Championship, but if your team loses, Max Pain gets a shot at the Intercontinental Title. Mike "Showtime" Bradshaw:  That's the stipulation for the match.  The UWF is putting myself against some of the biggest talent in the UWF & when I say that, I'm obviously refering to Devastation.  Max Pain is good, but first of all, his team has to win to get a title shot against me & that just isn't going to happen!  If anything will happen, then Kurt-Pac will get a shot to become the new Ultimate Champion! Chris:  it's going to be quite a match! Mike "Showtime" Bradshaw:  It certainly will!  I'm looking forward to getting a chance to compete against "Big D" in the ring! Chris:  I hate to dwindle on the past, but I have to ask you about Deathstalker.  This guy had a chance to make UWF history by becomeing a 3-time Intercontinental Champion.  Do you think he will ever earn another shot at your title? Mike "Showtime" Bradshaw:  If he does, then I'm sure that he will deserve it!  Only time can tell! Chris:  Do you see yourself ever challenging "Big D" for the Ultimate Championship? Mike "Showtime" Bradshaw:  I certainly do!  I figure that if I can get the Intercontinental Championship after a month, then with a little more effort, I can earn a Championship match! Chris:  OK!  Thanks alot Mike!  We're going to go to another comercial break & when we come back, we'll bring Great Numpty back out here so that the 2 of you can answer questions from the fans! (Commercial) (Scene is shown of Santa's elves working hard in their toy workshop on the North Pole.  The elves are busy putting together some wooden toys & whistling "We wish you a Merry Christmas".) What happens when the spirit of Christmas invades the UWF? (The doors of the workshop bust open & 2 wrestlers begin to brawl throughout the workshop.  Toys are picked up & used as weapons against one another as they continue to brawl.) It becomes "Season's Beatings"! The UWF Presents "Season's Beatings" Only on PPV --- December 12th, 1999 Chris:  Welcome back everyone to "UWF Live"!  We already have one guest out here, so let's bring back out Great Numpty! (Crowd cheers as Great Numpty comes back on the set.) Jake:  Here is how this last segment is going to work...  We are going to take calls from all over the world to ask these wrestlers questions!  Give us a call at 1-800-UWF-LIVE! (*Fake Number!!!*) Chris:  Let's go to the phone lines where "Carlos" is standing by! "Carlos":  Hey guys!  I just want to say that you guys have had some excellent matches!  What I really want to know is who would win if the two of you were to square off in the ring? Great Numpty:  That's an interesting question Carlos, but I think that after a wild match, the "Numpty Drop" would be the deciding factor that will guide me to victory! Mike "Showtime" Bradshaw:  Oh yeah?  Well, the "Showstoppa" has guided me to the Intercontinental Championship & I think it could take you out as well! Chris:  Guys!  Calm down please!  This is supposed to be a non-violent show! Great Numpty:  With all due respect for Mike, I think that maybe sometime down the road, we should go ahead & square off! Mike "Showtime" Bradshaw:  I can handle that! Jake:  (Wiping the sweat off of his brow) Whew!  That settles that!  Our next caller is "Martha"...  Go ahead!  You're live on the air! "Martha":  This question is for Numpty.  I saw that you want a match with Kurt-Pac!  How do you think you can beat him? Great Numpty:  I beat him in the Elimination match, didn't I?  I did it one & I'll do it again! Chris:  Our last caller is "Billy".  Go ahead with your question! "Billy":  Hey there!  My question is for the champ!  Hey Mike!  When are you going to make a serious run for some real titles in the UWF? Mike "Showtime" Bradshaw:  (Chuckles) like I said earlier, I'll be the champion someday, but right now, my main priority is to defend the Intercontinental Championship against any challengers! Chris:  That's it for tonight!  I'd like to thank our guests for taking time out to be on the show!  Be sure to catch our show next week when our guests will be Unforgiven, Saint, & Masta Killa! Jake:  And don't forget that the show will be on wednesday next week because of the Thanksgiving holiday! (Fade to black.)
