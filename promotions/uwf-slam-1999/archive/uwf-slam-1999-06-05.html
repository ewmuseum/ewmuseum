UWF Slam

5 June 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The UWF (Ultimate Wrestling Federation) hosts 2 weekly cards. Below is the UWF Slam card for June 5th. If you are interested in joining the
UWF, check out our site at
http://www.geocities.com:80/Colosseum/Arena/4720/UWF/UWF_HQx.html Thanks! UWF President [name withheld] ----------------------------------------- June 5th, 1999 UWF Slam opens up this week with much anticipation for the Royal Rumble match-up where the winner would get the first spot in the Ultimate
Championship tournament that would be taking place at "Championship
Mania"
Stalkers Vs. Rhinestone Cowboys In a blow-away style match, Firestalker & Deathstalker used several double teaming tactics to defeat the Rhinestone Cowboys in this opening match. Firestalker got the win after hitting Cowboy Curtis with an inverted power bomb.
Winners: Stalkers (6:55) Archangel Vs. Golden One Both of these men were undereated going into the ring. Golden One had defeated American Made twice & got a win over Rockin' Rebel wednesday night on UWF Fever. Archangel got his first & only win against Zoran at the same UWF Fever card. The action was fast & furious as Golden One tried his best to counter the "extreme" style of Archangel. Golden One at one point went for an atomic drop on Archangel, but somehow injured his knee in the process. Archangel placed Golden One in a Boston Crab &
Golden One immediately submitted. Golden One limped back to the locker room as fans cheered on Archangel's victory in the ring.
Winner: Archangel (13:16) NO HOLDS BARRED Unknown Wrestler (With Mr. G) Vs. "Big D" Devestation This match re-defined the word "brutal"! Devestation & Unknown Wrestler brought every weapon imaginable to the ring! There were chairs, baseball bats, garbage cans... Even a shock stick! The goal of this no
DQ, no countout match was to knock your opponent out for a 10 count.
Unknown Wrestler got the early advantage when Mr. G kept distracting
"Big D". Unknown Wrestler resorted to a lot of brawling in this match.
Devestation managed to get on the offensive when he took the baseball bat Unknown Wrestler was swinging & broke it over Unknown's head! With
Unknown Wrestler dripping blood early in the match, Devestation tried to make him bleed more! Devestation hit the masked man with several chair shots & hit a massive powerslam onto a steel chair. When Mr. G tried to help out Unknown Wrestler, Devestation simply gave him powerslam onto the chair as well. Sensing victory, "Big D" used his "Devestator"
running powerbomb on Unknown Wrestler & let the referee count to ten for the win! Winner: Devestation (11:13) 13-MAN ROYAL RUMBLE With a spot in the UWF Ultimate Championship tournament at "Championship
Mania" on the line, Deathstalker & Unknown Wrestler were the first 2 men that were announced. Deathstalker managed to have his way with the still groggy Unknown Wrestler for the first 2 minutes. The buzzer in the arena went off & the fans watched Golden One make his way to the ring. Devestation threw Unknown Wrestler in the corner while he concentrated on attacking the formerly undefeated Golden One. Unknown
Wrestler crawled over to a corner during these 2 minutes to heal his injuries. Golden One had Deathstalker hanging on the ropes when the buzzer went off again & Zoran made his way into the match. Zoran also had his sights set on Golden One. Golden One had to fight off many
2-on-1 attacks from Deathstalker & Zoran. After a few moments of double teaming, Zoran went after Unknown Wrestler, who was still over in the corner. When the 6 minute mark arrived, Unknown Wrestler was on the brink of elimination by Zoran, Golden One was duking it out with
Deathstalker, & Archangel was announced as the 5th entry. Archangel immediately went after Golden One's leg, remembering how it was injured. Deathstalker helped Archangel in his attack on Golden One.
Over in the corner, Zoran was still close to elimating Unknown Wrestler. No elimations had occured when the 8 minute mark had arrived
& Cowboy Jack made his way down. Deathstalker changed his mode of attack as he went after Cowboy Jack. Again, for the next 2 minutes, there were no eliminations! Zoran was still hurting Unknown Wrestler,
Archangel was still beating on Golden One's leg, & Cowboty Jack was receiving a beating by Deathstalker. When the 10 minute mark was reached, the other member of the Stalkers, Firestalker came to the ring. Both members of the Stalkers continued to beat on Coyboy Jack.
Archangel had Golden One against the ropes and attempted to try knocking him out with a clothesline, but Golden One ducked & knocked Archangel out of the match with a back drop. Golden One then went to help out
Zoran attacking Unknown Wrestler. The 12th minute passed & Master
Rapper came to the ring. He immediately went after the Stalkers.
American Made was the next entry into the Royal Rumble match. American
Made sought after Golden One. American Made had been scouting out the match in the back & realized Golden One had an injured leg. For the next 2 minutes, Golden One suffered extreme pain as American Made locked
Golden One in a series of leg submission moves, hoping Golden One would submit. Meanwhile, Deathstalker had busted Cowboy Jack open,
Firestalker was battling with Master Rapper, & Unknown Wrestler was making a comeback against Zoran. American Made went to attack Golden
One, but he missed. Golden One stumbled away and inadvertantly bumped into Firestalker & Master Rapper, who both fell out of the ring. With all of the distraction, Unknown Wrestler made his first offense of the
Rumble by charging American Made & clotheslining him out of the match.
At the 16 minute mark, Cowboy Curtis entered the Rumble & assisted his tag team partner, Cowboy Jack, against Deathstalker. The Rhinestone
Cowboys continued their assault against Devestation for the next 2 minutes as Unknown Wrestler, Golden One, & Zoran battled against one another. Rockin' Rebel was the 11th wrestler to enter the Rumble.
Rockin' Rebel went after Zoran as Unknown Wrestler attempted throwing
Golden One over the top rope. 2 minutes went by. Z Gangsta came out next. The Hoodlam member went after Cowboy Curtis, leaving Cowboy Jack to face off against Deathstalker. As the croud counted down for the final entry, "Big D" Devestation came out. The big massive man was attacked by every one in the ring, hoping a combined effort could get him over the top rope. Devestation unbelievably held back every attack. Zoran went in to attack a still groggy Unknown Wrestler. Zoran eliminated him with a spinning mule kick that sent him over the top rope. Z Gangsta kept up his attack against Cowboy Curtis & tossing him over the top next. At this point, 7 men remained... Cowboy Jack,
Deathstalker, Devestation, Golden One, Rockin' Rebel, Z Gangsta, &
Zoran. These 7 men continued to battle each other. The croud turned their attention from the match to the entranceway where Archangel was making his way back down to ringside. Zoran was the first to be distracted by Archangel. Cowboy Jack took advantage of Zoran being distracted and connected with a dropkick that sent him to the arena floor. Golden One then caught sight of the man that gave him his first loss earlier in the night. Golden One was yelling at Archangel from in the ring. Z Gangsta came up behind Golden One & clotheslined him to the floor. Archangel & Golden One began brawling all the way to the locker room. Devestation, realizing that the rest of the wrestlers were getting tired, decided that this would be the time to begin eliminating everyone. He began to pound on each man with clotheslines & closed fists, making each man to go to his knees. Devestation gave Z Gangsta a
"Devestator" running powerbomb. "Big D" then walked over to Cowboy Jack to give him one as well. As Devestation moved Cowboy Jack into position, Cowboy Jack gave "Big D" a closed fist to the groin. As
Devestation yelled out in pain, Cowboy Jack went to the ropes & delivered a shoulder block that sent "Big D" over the ropes. Cowboy
Jack saw Z Gangsta and eliminated him as well with another shoulder block. The final 3 men... Cowboy Jack, Deathstalker, & Rockin' Rebel...
paused for a moment in the ring. Each man looked at each other & realized that one of them would be going on the "Championship Mania"
with the chance to win the UWF Ultimate Championship. The croud began chanting "Rockin... Rockin..." as they were cheering for Rockin' Rebel.
Rebel was the first of the 3 men to make a move. He went against the ropes & attempted a double clothesline on Cowboy Jack & Deathstalker, but they ducked & hit a double clothesline of their own. Seeing that
Rockin' Rebel was dazed, Deathstalker gave him a push over the rope to the floor. Deathstalker turned around to see Cowboy Jack running towards him. Deathstalker ducked down & Cowboy Jack fell over the top to the outside. Deathstalker stood alone in the ring... proud of the fact that he was going to "Championship Mania"... Proud of the fact he had the chance to win the UWF Ultimate Championship!
