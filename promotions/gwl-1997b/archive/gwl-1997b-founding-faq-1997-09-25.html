GLOBAL WRESTLING LEAGUE IS OPEN TO WRESTLERS IF INTERESTED!!!

25 September 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
GLOBAL WRESTLING LEAGUE Welcome to the GWL! ROLE-PLAYING This is a key factor to winning matches. Basically, all you have to do is participate by sending in flashes, strategies, interviews, sneak attacks, etc. It's simple: You don't roleplay, you don't win. This is a booked league, it's not a sim where you just put it the stats and get a winner. All factors are taken into consideration which is why you must roleplay, I can't stress it enough. STRATEGIES: Strategies are a must. You must send in a strategy for each match you are in or you will lose, I gurantee it! Strats tell me how you want your wrestler to wrestle in a certain match and if I don't have a strat then I won't be able to write an offensive or defensive for your wrestler. In all sports you need a gameplan, as needed in e-wrestling in order to succeed. Strats don't have to be a big deal, just a few lines describing what you want your wrestler to do in a match or before and after a match. FLASHES/INTERVIEWS Flashes: It's just a wrestler talking into a camera speaking his thoughts about what's on his mind. I will not accept little one-liners like: "I'll kick your butt, you're dead" type of flashes like those will cause you to lose. Nice creative, imaginative flashes will give your wrestler a good advantage. So remember all these things when doing your flashes. Interviews: Someone asking your wrestler a series of questions. This is pretty much self explanatory. When doing interviews, you make up your own questions and have your wrestlers answer them to a interviewer. SNEAK ATTACKS This is also considered role-playing but you can't rely soley on this only. In other words, you can't just go around jumping people and beating them up week in and week out. Too many sneak attacks get annoying for the other handlers and ruins the fun. Also, I have a right to decline sneak attacks if it doesn't make sense to have the attack and if it doesn't fit in with the fued or storyline. FEUDS/STORYLINES/ANGLES Obviously this is what it's all about, what makes wrestling fun. In order to have great feuds and storylines, you must put effort and imagination into it. Interact with other wrestlers handlers, form alliances, create situations, just as you see how wrestling is on TV. By creating feuds and storylines, it also counts as roleplaying and it shows the fedhead that you really care about the league and want to make it as best as can be and it looks good in the feadheads eyes.
