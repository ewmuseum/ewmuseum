XWC/Cheap-shots and Press Confrence, 15 March 1997

15 March 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
================================================================ XWC World Heavyweight Champion: Mojoman XWC World Tag Champions: TET XWC Int'l Heavyweight Champion: Alex Williams XWC Cruiserweight Champion: Simon Lebec ================================================================ President : Sean Gilbert ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- -------- Commentators: Eric Rudlinger Matt Totaro ================================================================ 1/ K-SOLO 'demands' a Title shot!!!!!! 2/ Pain-a-Maniac spells trouble?????? 3/ 4/ 5/ 6/ 7/ 8/ 9/ 10/ ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- - 1/ K-SOLO--You know, I sit back and think about this past edition of Powerslam,and what do I see?I'm ranked 6th in singles and Possessed and I are the 2nd ranked tag team here!(SOLO starts to laugh out loud)YOU KNOW I THOUGHT IF YOU WERE RANKED YOU WERE SUPPOSED TO GET TITLE SHOTS AND ALL THAT OTHER GOOD STUFF!! HEY ALEX, MOJO! I'VE BEEN ASKING FOR A SHOT FOR A LONG TIME. You know what? My problem may not be with you,but with Gilbert. HEY MR. PRESIDENT?!?!! WHERE'S MY SHOT?!After I was cheated out of MY T.V.title, I didn't get a rematch or any other title shots! That's okay,because Possessed and I WILL get the tag titles!!But before we meet the tag CHUMPS, I have some scores to settle. P.A.M., I would love to kick your ass one more time.XWC CHUMPS,WATCH OUT BECAUSE THE SOLO ONE IS COMING FOR ALL THE GOLD! FOOOO! YOU CAN'T HOLD ME BACK!!! ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- 2/<Fade into Pain-A-Maniac walking the streets of New York. He's decked in an overcoat> I'm back. And that's trouble for some souls. K-Solo, anytime foo. Once you step in the ring with the master. It will be de ja vu. Except Red Raven won't interfere. Trust me. I hear that a lot of people are rebelling to the BAHW, well, not me. XWC has always and will always be my home. If I can remember right, the Killer Farm Animals, Snuggleblat and Heaterleg won the tag belts. What's up with TET. You get a bunch of bogus belts? Mojoman, I don't really care if you turned against anyone. Sooner or later, you'll have to defend against Pain-A-Maniac. I was once caled one of the hottest young stars in the XWC. Well, If I beat you, I will be the hottest. <Fade out> ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- - 3/ ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- 4/ ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- - 5/ ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- - 6/ ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- - 7/ ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- - 8/ ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- - 9/ ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- - 10/ ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- Anyone interested in writing a few matches for the PPV? Thanx Jason [detail withheld] ============================================================================ (The scene opens up to a noisy, crowded press room it New York's Kennedy Airport...Sean Gilbert is seen approaching thr podium, the reporters are in a frenzy) Gilbert: Thank you all...thank you all for coming. (Reporters silence) Gilbert: Well, I won't hold this off any longer, the XWC's next Pay Per View Supercard which will take place in the Louisiana Superdome...will be MAYHEM '97 and will take place on March 23rd 1997!!!! (reporters once again go into a frenzy) Reporter: Any idea on what the MAIN EVENT will be? Gilbert: In light of the current happenings here in the XWC, I have come to a decision involving the International Title and the World Title...for the first time EVER in the XWC...the International and World Championships will be defended against each other!!!! (crowd grows even louder) Gilbert: Thats right...Alex "The Specialist" Williams and Mojoman will be squaring off for all of the glory!! Reporter: Mr. Gilbert..!! Gilbert: Yes? Reporter: Aren't you afriad that there will be outside involvement for Mojoman's behalf?? We have seen it before! Gilbert: Taking that under consideration, I have decided that this match will be a no holds barred, no weapons illegal, CAGE MATCH!!! But...not even that may be enough, so it is now sanctioned that both belts will be raised high above the Louisiana Superdome, and the only way a man can win the match, is to reach those belts, and possess them! Reporter: A CAGED LADDER MATCH??? Gilbert: That is correct. (crowd starts chattering loudly) Gilbert: The cruiserweight title will be also be defended...."Showstopper" Simon Lebec will defend the title against El Jaguar!! Reporters: Mr. Gilbert...!!! Gilbert: Sorry...I've got a plane to catch!! (Gilbert quickly leaves the press room, camera fade)
