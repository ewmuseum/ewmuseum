The way it is....by Matt Totaro Hello again XWC fans and we are just 6 days away from Mayhem '97, which will bring in a record of over 76,000 fans. I'd like to take an in depth look at the matches that will be taking place.... The Cruiserweight title match is expected to be a classic, with Simon Lebec taking on the Mexican Sensation El Jagaur. Lebec himself is more familiar with mat wrestling and Jaguar with aerial manuvers, it will be interesting to see how these two match up. The Tag Team title match which will have The European Technicians defend against The Brown Brothers in a no disqualification match. With the type of men the Brown Brothers are, I expect this match to be violent in every sense of the word. I expect The Technicians will be very unfamiliar to this type of a match, and The Brown Brothers could very well defeat the fan favorates to become the new champions. K-Solo, in his return to singles competition here in the XWC, will be taking on the 7'5, mountain of a man, Pain-A-Maniac. Somehow, I do not see Solo defeating this monster on his own. Solo is a great competitor, but PAM's size seems just too massive for one man to take down. Also Utter Domination Mercinary Ajax will be taking on "Amazing" Aaron Aikman. As many may recall, Ajax may have cost Aikman the title in his match against Mojoman. And finally the main event, the double Championship match. World Champion Mojoman will be taking on International Champion Alex "The Specialist" Williams in a caged ladder match. Never in my career have I ever witness a "caged" ladder match. This match is going to have two men, who are former best friends, who have never faced each other before, locked in a cage, and have both their championship belts raised high above the arena!! I have absolutely no idea what is going to happen in this match. All I know is that the rulebook will be thrown out of the window, and we will witness two men literally fight for everything they've got. See you in Louisiana folks!! ________________________________________________________________________ 4. UNCENSORED...by Eric Rudlinger Hello punks!! Special interview with the wrestler of the month International Champion Alex "The Specialist" Williams has been in the XWC since almost the beginning. He has been with us through thick and thin, and he remains one of the most respected and popular wrestlers in the world. We are proud to bring you this in depth interview with The Specialist: Totaro: Alex, first of all, before all the glitz and the glamour...how did the dream called "The Specialist" first come to be. Williams: I can remember, when I was around 9-10 years old, begging my parents to let me stay up late to watch "Prime Time Wrestling," which ran until around 11:00 at night. Whenever I saw Bret "Hitman" Hart, my heart just sank. When he was wrestling, I was wrestling. When he got pinned, it felt like I got pinned too. I wanted to be just like him. It was kind of like a dream. And this was before I has even started wrestling amateurly. I started that in high school, and was pretty successfull at it. Around this time I was probably 13 or 14 years old, and I was already winning state championships, a gold medal in the Junior Olympics, and I remained undefeated in my high school wrestling team. They even put me in a double higher weight class. My friends nicknamed me "The Specialist," and it stuck through all these years. Totaro: When did you first realize that "The Specialist" could not be a dream anymore...that it would be your destiny? Williams: Well...when I was about 14 years old, a group of friends and I went to a WWF live event. This was the first time I had ever been to a live event before, and it was a wonderful experience for me. As soon as I saw Bret Hart come through those curtains, the second he slapped my hand, I knew exactly what my destiny was. Totaro: How do you see your future in wrestling? Williams: As everyone knows, I am with the XWC 100%, but just like I felt when I was in college, I yearn for more competition. I thrive on it. As soon as the day comes, where I feel that I have accomplished all that can be accomplished in the XWC, that is the day I will move on to bigger things. I am only 24 years old, and my whole career is definetely ahead of me. Totaro: In closing, do you think that your second generation style of wrestling will compete against the new generation of young wrestlers that are piling on the XWC doorstep? Williams: I feel that with technical wrestling, I can adapt to any style anyone can throw in my way. I can take a bigger man down, I can outsmart a brawler, I can get a clean win, and can please the fans. That is all that matters. In closing I would like to add that I am very honored to be chosen XWC Powerslam's wrestler of the month, and as always...prepare to feel unbearable pain!!! _______________________________________________________________________ 6.
