XWC\Cheap-shots, 17 March 1997

17 March 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
================================================================ XWC World Heavyweight Champion: Mojoman XWC World Tag Champions: TET XWC Int'l Heavyweight Champion: Alex Williams XWC Cruiserweight Champion: Simon Lebec ================================================================ President : Sean Gilbert --------------------------------------------------------------- Commentators: Eric Rudlinger Matt Totaro ================================================================ 1/ K-solo speaks the truth???????? 2/ Brown Brothers on their upcoming Tag Title shot. 3/ TET respond to the Title challenge. 4/ Alex interviewd by a couple of kids ??????? 5/ Utter Domination issue their challenge! 6/ El jaguar makes a promise to his fans! 7/ Bordeau and HIS Title??????????????? 8/ 9/ 10/ --------------------------------------------------------------- 1/ You know I was going to come out here and rant and RAVE about evrything, but I'm not. I'm here to tell all of you out there, the FANS, the PRESIDENT and the other "so called" wrestlers here that,I AM THE FUTURE. I AM THE MAN THAT WILL TAKE THIS FED INTO THE NEXT MILLINEUM. WHETHER I'M A WORLD CHAMP OR A TAG CHAMP, LET IT BE KNOWN ON THIS DAY, .....MARCH 14,1997, I,K-SOLO, THE MAN OF THE HOUR,TOWER OF POWER, HAS OFFICIALLY ANNOUNCED HIS TOUR OF DESTRUCTION. EVERYONE WILL BE DESTROYED. AND AS A GOOD FRIEND OF MINE ALWAYS SAYS....."the streets will flow,with the BLOOD OF THE NON-BELIEVERS!" FOOOOOOO!! YOU CAN'T HOLD ME BACK!!!!! -------------------------------------------------------------- 2/THE BROWN BROTHERS finish watching the card announcement for meyhem. After the announcement of the tag title match, the two brothers high five each other. LARRY BROWN: This is what it is all about. In just a few short days, we will be the new World Tag team Champions. The promoters could deny us no longer. Everyone said we had to prove ourselves--that we can't ride on past glories. Well that's just fine. We have held the Tag Championship in 3 seperate federations and now we will prove to eveyone in TV land that those titles were well deserved. European technicians, how technical will a no holds barred, no DQ match be? Well, it's our kind of match and I can tell you it will be technical. You'll see flying elbowdrops, northern lights suplexes, and tilt a whilrl slams. Unfortunately for you chumps, it will be on the concrete floor or accross the steel security barricade. SEAN BROWN SAYS: And don't think your european style will scare us because my brother and I have seen it before, losers. We've fought the best from that sorry continent and beaten the best. So don't get your hopes up about dazzling us with some great moves. The only dazzling thing you'll see is the ring lights spinning round and round as you're laying on your back watching the tag titles getting wrapped around our waists. So bring all you got and we'll be more than happy to knock it down. --------------------------------------------------------------- 3/ Scene opens up on Achilles walking down the street to the local newsagents. He picks up his latest copy of Powerslam flicks through it, then turns to face the camera. "you know, the best thing about being in XWC is that every wrestler gets his chance to win something more than a few fans. 'PDQ' and myself are gratefull for that and we really apreciate the fact that we are allready regarded by someas one o the best teams ever seen. Now to be XWC champs you have to be the best, WE are the best. Why 'cause we hold the titles. Brown Bro's you can harp on all you want about your past glorys, we dont give a damn. We have one intention for the PPV and the Louisiana Superdome with those titles still around our waists. You know you be very grateful, grateful that XWC has some of the best medical staff available in the Wrestling business, and we sure hope your insurance payements are up to date because believe me, you will sure get to like the taste of that hospital food........" Achilles saunters off down the road, chuckling to himself as he reads Powerslam................ --------------------------------------------------------------- 4/ (the scene, Daytona Beach Florida....the boardwalk can be seen, with many people gathered on the beach for Spring Break, the camera closes up at a lone surfer, seen surfing on the outside break alone) Kid#1: Hey, you see that guy out there? He is surfing some pretty dangerous water with the undercurrent picking up! Kid#2: The waves are getting up to 9 foot overhead out there! He must be nuts! (the man swims closer to shore...at a battle with nature, he can be seen struggling, but slowly he makes it to the beach) Kid#1: Hey man, are you OK?....wait...you're..y-you're...THE SPECIALIST!!! Kid#2: Omigod! Its really you!! Alex Williams! Williams: Hey guys...you shouldn't head out today, the conditions are really dangerous. Kid#2: Yeah, we know! We saw you surfing out there...why did you head out? Williams: Well, I just got a lot of stuff on my mind. Kid#2: Alex, c-can we have your au... (Williams takes out a pen from his surfing trunks and signs both surfboards) Both kids: Thanks!! Williams: No problem guys. Kid#1: Hey Alex, this Sunday, we're gonna watch you beat the crap out of that loser Mojoman!! Good luck! Bye Alex! (both kids leave the beach, and Williams gets on a motorcycle, and rides to a secluded area of the beach, there he stands alone, looking at the tides) Williams: Justin...just 6 days. 6 days until you and I go down into the pits of hell together. One of us will not leave. Its never been this personal before Justin. Never. This may seem like an interesting form of training. I am doing my best to regain peace with myself. But it has become very, very clear to me, that the only way that I can regain my inner peace, is when you are annihilated. You and your band of evil you call Utter Domination. (Williams puts on a pair of specs...) Williams: Then, after I have destroyed you, I will fullfill my destiny. I will climb to the top of the ladder, and grab my International belt, and I will grab The World Heavyweight Championship. My dreams will become reality, as I hold them high above my head, then they will be draped over each shoulder. Rest assured, my old friend, you will prepare to feel unbearable pain... (Williams then rides off on mis motorcycle into the horizon...camera fades..) --------------------------------------------------------------- 5/ ( the dark dungeon can be seen, with torches on the sides of the room....Utter Domination, lined up in 2 single files, lead up to the source of all their evil...) (lightning stikes!!) Mojoman: Alex...I have waited so long....so very long...TO DESTROYYY YOUU!!! (THUNDER!!) Mojoman: When you attacked me....you went over the line...you hit me with that belt that will soon be mine...and you call it bravery? Is it bravery to attack 5 men that are bigger and badder than yourself? No it is NOT!! Alex..When I climb that ladder, and when I grab those belts...I WILL FINALLY RULE!!! You cannot stop me. Maybe you have not realized yet...I CANNOT BE STOPPED!! I CANNOT BE HARMED!! WATCH ME AND TREMBLE!!...for I bring the purity of oblivion..... (Ajax now appears..) Ajax: Aikman...you should have learned you lesson the first time...now, since Mojoman does not see fit to waste his time with you...I will take care of you MYSELF!!! You will fall to me...to Utter Domination!! (The Hellspawn now appear...) "Evil" Eric Chambers: Utter Domination will rule all!! We are going to start be challenging the PUNKS that call themselves..."The Outsiders!!" You call yourselves "Legends!!" You don't even wrestle anymore!! HA!! "Dirty" Dick Prosser: Thats right, you old has-beens can not even lick the crap off our boots!! Mojoman: By the end of the night...The Louisiana Superdome will be knee deep in the corpses of ALL of the XWC's fan loving f(BEEP)ots, and on top of the large grave, will be my dear old friend...Alex "The Specialist" Williams. hhe...hehe..heh..hehe... Utter Domination: HAHAHA!! HA HA!!!!! HAAA!!! (camera fades as the evil laughter of all of the men can be heard) --------------------------------------------------------------- 6/ (camera cuts to El Jaguar in a ring training) El Jaguar: This Sunday at Mayhem '97 I will be having de match of my life!! I will be getting a Cruiserweight Title shot at "De Showstopper" Simon Lebec...and I promise to all of my fans, I will not dissapoint them!! I will train very hard to win the Cruiserweight title for my counrty!! VIVA MEXICO!!!! (camera fades out) --------------------------------------------------------------- 7/ Bordeau- Well...looks like Lebec has returned to the XWC...and he took my belt. NO ONE TAKES MY BELT!! NO ONE!!!...ahem...anyways, I feel that it is time to move on...so I will be challenging the one who calls himself...."Snapblat." Disgusting name for a disgusting man!! Bunch of filthy REPTILES!! eh..eh.he...YOU ALL WILL FALL TO THE POWER OF THE PARALYZER!!!..eh....Snapblat, you think that you are a worthy cruiserweight for MY title!! Yes, thats right, its MY title!! I was the first man to hold it!!! ITS MINE!!! Filthy swine...why don't you bring your idiot friends as well!! They could use a lesson from the Paralyzer!!! YOU ALL COULD USE A LESSON FROM ME!!! YOU WILL FALLLL!!!!!!!!!.... (camera fades abruptly) --------------------------------------------------------------- 8/ --------------------------------------------------------------- 9/ -------------------------------------------------------------- 10/ --------------------------------------------------------------- Specials: ********Mayhem '97********* 3/23 Title Matches: World Champion Mojoman vs. International Champion Alex "The Specialist" Williams is a no holds barred caged ladder match!
