[XWC] Interim President Sean Gilbert's press conference

17 July 1996
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Scene is a packed hotel ballroom. XWC Interim President Sean Gilbert enters the room and reporters chatter and photobulbs flash. Gilbert steps up to the podium as the din gets louder.] Gilbert: I've called this press conference for two reasons. One is to address some issues going on in the XWC. The other is to address the upcoming Upheaval pay-per view. It goes without saying that the XWC is one of the hottest feds right now. We have many feuds and action. However, several issues have taken a front burner, in my opinion. These are the XWC offices' problems with Viper and his acquisition of the Canadian Crippler, Chris Benoit, as an advisor; our ongoing saga with Substance Chad O'Donnell; and what appears to be malfeasance on the part of Shining Path in offering a tag title shot to enemies May 19 for interference, then turning around around and announcing the contract is loser leaves town. I will address questions on these three issues first, then to other XWC business. [Reporters call out. Gilbert points to one.] Reporter: How did you react to what Shining Path did in regard to the tag titles? Gilbert. I was angry. Our titles are awards for wrestling, not bribes for help. Shining Path has, in effect, made this entire federation a laughingstock because they have to get help to beat the champions, and they use the tag belts to do it. For that reason, I am tonight officially stripping Shining Path of the tag team titles and ruling their contract with May 19 void. The titles are now vacant. [Reporters look shocked. Room is noisier] Gilbert: Since Shining Path seems so into games around here, I have decided to make our World tag team title showdown interested. Four teams will be in one ring in an elimination four way dance. Anyone can tag anyone, so long as there's contact. Teams are eliminated until we have champions. You all understand the concept, but there will be four, which will make it interesting. Reporter: Who are the four teams? Gilbert: Shining Path, May 19, The Xtreme Gangstas and The Outsiders. And, since all parties seem so inclined to attack each other before matches, anyone attacking another team before or after the match or elimination will get an automatic and minimum one week suspension from all XWC activities, including Cheap Shots and appearances. If any team not involved in the match decides to attack one of the teams competing, they face a minimum two week suspension. Periods of suspension will increase if the attack warrants it. I will not tolerate an attack on these teams, because we're all fed up with it. Reporter: Can you comment on the status of Wolverine, who is part of the Outsiders, after that fight with James Havok? Gilbert: Actually, I had lunch with Wolverine and Colossus today. Wolverine suffered some bruised ribs. Fortunately, he's in incredible condition and is alright. Both are young, idealistic guys breaking into the sport against people like Shining Path and Shakur, who clearly don't care what lengths they go to to win or get over. Both were disturbed by the Tuesday match and indicated they want to take their wrestling up a notch against opponents. I am confident they'll be ready for Upheaval. Reporter: How did you feel about Kuwasi Shakur's comments about his third match against Colossus? Gilbert: I respect Black Fist a great deal. He was the first XWC World champion and guys like him, Wug and Simon Lebec have been with the XWC through thick and thin. However, I felt Colossus deserved the matches. Both are good competitors. With Colossus' defeat, we're looking at James Havok to face Shakur at Upheaval. Reporter: Is that a definite? Gilbert: Yes. James Havok will face Kuwasi "Black Fist" Shakur for the International title at Upheaval in a falls count anywhere Street War starting outside the stadium and going in. Both guys will be armed with baseball bats. If they want to war, they can do it at Upheaval. Reporter: What is your response to Viper and his advisor, Chris Benoit? Gilbert: First, the XWC has initiated a suit against Benoit and WCW for Benoit showing up here. It's an unfair business practice for WCW to allow Benoit over here as a walking billboard for their organization. As for hanging Viper with a gimmick he hated, Viper can tell whatever story he wants. Obviously, he forgot coming up with the idea in our offices. If he puts his hands on me again, he'll have made the biggest mistake of his life, besides coming up with a bad gimmick. Reporter: Do you have word on rumors more wrestlers ar joining his alliance? Gilbert: I've heard it, but it won't keep me up nights, rest assured. Reporter: On the subject of invaders, how do you feel about Substance Chad O'Donnell's recent statements regarding a stretcher match with Bullywug? Gilbert: I've kept my mouth shut about SCO"D for awhile. Now I'm going to speak up. SCO'D can say all he wants about the XWC being nothing to him. The cameras weren't in contract negotiations when SCO'D was first in the XWC and SCO'D grovelled like a dog as well as took a significant pay cut to join. I'm tired of SCO'D's crap. He talks about the current XWC talent? Whatever revisionist history he pukes, SCO'D was always a minor star in the XWC during his time here and now talks like he was the top dog. I'm sorry, but a onetime TV champ who won on a fluke isn't top talent in my book. His first departure had a lot more to do with friction between SCO'D and [name withheld] than an exclusive GIWF contract. I've always maintained SCO'D was a good interviewer, an average wrestler and a passable tactician -- this is why he was paid a good deal less when he was first in the XWC. Any fairy tale he chooses to weave is his problem. Bullywug has been awarded a new title belt and SCO'D is still what has always been -- a midcarder too big for his britches. [Reporters look shocked. Room is noisier] Gilbert: Substance Chad O'Donnell will face Bullywug in a stretcher match at Upheaval. It is Bullywug's option if it's a title match. SCO'D isn't even ranked. What's more, even if Wug says it's a title match, SCO'D will have to ask me pubicly to be allowed into the XWC. I may allow him in and, even then, he'll get a standard pay rate. Reporter: What is the status of the Shootfight tournament? Sunset Rider has removed himself, as have others. Gilbert: Sunset Rider was concerned about injury in the Shootfight; indded, it takes a special kind of athlete to join a shootfight. Mega-Warrior and Yul B. Hurt have also opted out to join together for the tag battle royal. One spot has been filled by Possessed, who will face Mojoman in the tournament. Two spots are open. The first two wrestlers who send a message labelled "Shoot tourney" or the like and ask admittance will be let in. Remember, a wrestler cannot be in the shoot tourney and the tag team batle royal too. Reporter: Can you confirm reports from Pro Wrestling Illustrated that the XWC is in negotiations with legend "Kingly" Edgar Figaro to join the XWC? Gilbert: At this time, I can only say we've talked with Mr. Figaro, had a good meeting and have a good relationship. Mr. Figaro is, of course, a major star and him joining the XWC would be a big coup. However, big star or not. Figaro would not be given prefential treatment either. Reporter: You were quoted in The Wrestling Informer saying Upheaval will be moved up a few days. Can you confirm or deny this? Gilbert: Upheaval has been moved up to Wednesday, July 31. We wanted to have a July PPV and decided to move it up. The card will be a combination of summary, play-by-play and commentary. Expect the quality you have come to expect from the XWC. Listen folks, I've got to make a plane. Only two more questions. [Reporters call for attention. Gilbert chooses one, then another] Reporter: Mr. Gilbert, how do you feel about the number of disqualifications and interferences that seem to affect XWC outcomes? Of the title changes lately, two of three were affected by interference. Gilbert: Interference is part of wrestling. However, the number is alarming. I will not permit this fed to become some sort of streetfight league. We may have 'extreme' in our name, but we are a wrestling organization. Lots of smaller leagues offer the lowest common denominator -- the XWC offers the best of hardcore and technical matches. Our strength is wrestling, and I am acting more harshly against particularly vicious instances of interference or attack. You'll be seeing more fines and penalties in the future. Reporter: Rumors are flying that there are two tag triangle matches in the works for Upheaval, with the winners of both getting a third triangle match against the International tag team champions. Is this true? Gilbert [looks annoyed]: Alright, I had wanted to wait to announce it, but evidently you have an inside source. Upheaval will feature what we call "The REAL Triple Threat." Other feds have triangle matches -- the XWC will have just what you described -- two triangle matches, with the winners of each getting a final triangle match against the International champions, who are currently Alex "The Specialist" Williams and "Lightning" Eric Johnson. It's set up to give the new teams recruited a chance to roleplay enough to win and make the established teams work harder. Our first triangle match has Demolition Alpha, Sex Pistols and Team Canada. The second features Hopscotch Heroes of the Apocalypse, Midnight Riders and Dr. Death and Leviathan. We have not gotten all contracts complete, so there may e changes. Before people start exposing the rest of Upheaval, I have to go. [Gilbert leaves the podium as questions are shouted.]
