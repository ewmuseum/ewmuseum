XWC S H O W C A S E ! =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= Booker's note: Showcase is an occasional card of jobber matches for new people. I tried to include as many people as i could, but if i missed you, let me know and we'll schedule you elsewhere. you now have your first match and win, so you have no excuse to not roleplay. check out others and their talent and make challenges. that's all! =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= Substance Chad O'Donnell vs. Frustrated Bob O'Bibbie (jobber) SCO'D showed up again at the show and slammed the XWC. SCO'D ain't new but he challenged anyone to come out. None of the regulars felt like taking on SCO'D, so a really dumb jobber did, and got a Flying Piledriver for his trouble. SCO'D wins! =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= El Toro Negro vs. El Pollo Frio (jobber) Toro hits the ring to a big pop and puts on a great show of good power and technical moves. Pollo tries to match Toro's explosive luchador style with no luck. He wins with a torture rack-style hold. =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= The Dukes vs. Banana Splits (jobbers) A much-discussed tag team, the Dukes enter with supreme confidence. Their teamwork is the most impressive of the night and they have no slowdown, winning this match in under five minutes! Evil Enos with the One Way Ticket (powerbomb off the top rope)! =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= Marvelous Maurice Salizar vs. "Hunka Burnin' Love" Rick Studd (jobber) Salizar seems pretty exciting to watch as a technician, and the crowd seemed into his style too. He wins with the Annihilator (sleeper). =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= Pain-A-Maniac vs. Peace Man (jobber) Pain goes nuts -- pounding and throwing the jobber. He no-sells every move, which prompts the hardcores to start throwing cups of beer into the ring and boo. Maniac with the chokeslam and the pin. =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= K-Solo vs. "Lickable" Louie Love (jobber) K gets one of the night's bigger pops as he heads to the ring. Fans seem to like his persona and aggressiveness. He scores a pin with an awesome dragon suplex! =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= Verderer vs. Ace Ventura (jobber) Verderer does not seemed fazed by his debut loss and makes the jobber suffer. He is on fire and slags the jobber hard. He does a rack-style submission and gets the win! =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= Iron Man vs. The Tin Man (jobber) Wrestling later with the team Heavy Metal, Iron Man shows off his strength with many power moves. He throws the jobber with a ring-shaking powerbomb for the pin! =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= Chance Martin vs. Aerial Assault (jobber) Martin is a smart technician and gets some great moves in. He does well and gets a pin wwith a flying guillotine legdrop! =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= Savage vs. Porky Da Cop (jobber) Savage is savage in the ring, using mostly brawling to win. He sends Porky to the mat with an rattling flying shoulderblock for the victory. =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= Apache Assassin and Cherokee Chief vs. Sideshow Bob and The Cowboy (jobbes) Great teamwork for these two. They beat the jobbers in no time, scoring a win with a moonsault. =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= "Marvelous" Matthew Hayes vs. Beef Jerky (jobber) Hayes gets a fat pop tonight, and he eats up the fan attention. In the ring, he's all business. He scores an easy pin with 2Cruel (Diamond Cutter) =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= Thrill Killer vs. Natural Born Killer (jobber) TK is a masked man with a great presence in the ring. He shows his skills then lands a Heart Attack (savate kick to the head) for the win! =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= Malice vs. Happy the Clown (jobber) The big Malice beats Happy without mercy, only to leave him screaming for mercy with an Iron Claw and the victory for Malice. =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= TNT vs. Pinky (jobber) A jumpy wrestler, TNT is all over the jobber like no one else tonight. He goes with a jumping DDT and a win. =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= Alexei Egarova vs. Ivan Smirnoff (jobber) The big Russian is loved by the crowd for some reason. Egarova gets the submission with an inverted bear hug. =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= Centurion Actus Vapus vs. Brickhouse (jobber) The Ancient Roman is a great mover in the ring and impresses the fans. Cent with the Torture Device (DDT) and the victory. =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= Ruckus vs. Poster Boy (jobber) An exciting technician, Rukus gets a huge pop! Plenty of good moves and Ruckus wins with a Flash Kick (spinwheel kick). =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= Psychosis vs. Godhead (jobber) Not relation to the Mexican luchador, Psychosis was a monster who wasted no time ripping apart the jobber, ending it with Utter Damnation (chokeslam). Intimidating. =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= Darklord vs. Baskin Robbins (jobber) Daeklord is a total madman, but is methodical in his madness. He takes out the jobber's leg, then arm. Darklord does an unusual surfboard submission for the win! =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= Blades vs. Disgruntled Postal Workers (jobbers) Shane and Dane Blade are twins who work well together. Lots of high-flying spots. Dane with the Spiked Power Bomb (top rope powerbomb) for the victory! =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= Ape vs. Azreal the Supercat (jobber) Some heat but not much for the primitive Ape, who gets a submission with a vicious reverse chinlock. =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= Shen Long vs. Dan "The Beast" Severed (jobber) Long is a martial arts master with a 100-0 reord coming into the XWC. He is methodical and a concummate showman in the martial arts. He gets a painful hammerlock on and the jobber submits! Make it 101-0! =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= Heat vs. Tag Monsters (jobbers) Da Bomb and TNT (not the singles wrestler) get a pop coming out and keep getting a pop as they make some aerial moves, as well as technical holds. They win with a top rope suplex. =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= Jimmy Cougar vs. Ronald McDonald (jobber) The exciting Cougar is booed by the fans, but he doesn't seem to mind. Lots of technical moves, and the win for Cougar with the Cougar Clutch (sleeper) for the submission. =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= Thunder, Dr. Death and Leviathan vs. The Brady Brothers (jobbers) The only 6-man match was interesting more for the Temple of Darkness' impressive display of holds and more. The crowd boos these guys heartily. Dr. Death with the Death Drop (powerslam) for the victory! =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= Animal vs. Liontamer (jobber) A psycho in the squared circle! Animal destroys the jobber in no time and executes a powerbomb for the pin. =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= Mysterioso vs. The Riddler (jobber) Mysterioso gets a big response as he comes out to a good show of high spots and fast grappling. Somersaults, frogsplashes and more. He wins with a big moonsault! =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= Lethal Weapon vs. Cobra 4000 In one of the more anticipated matches tonight, LW upsets former TV champ Cobra 4000 with a crescent kick to the jaw! Lethal Weapon thrilled! =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= Dante the Ice Cream Man vs. Nicolas Bradford (jobber) Everyone screamed for the Ice Cream Man! Sadista with the State of Confusion (brainbuster suplex off the top rope) for the victory. =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= [fade into a casual-looking rec room, XWC Interim Pres. Sean Gilbert sits on a lounger, beside a wrestler] Sean Gilbert: Welcome to Up Close, an occasional segment hosted by me where we take XWC stars out of the ring, away from the gimmicks and on a personal level to see them for the people they are. I'm Sean Gilbert. When we talk about legends in the XWC, one can't help but mention the man who was the XWC's second world champion. Prince of Darkness impressed many people with his skill and power. When he defeated Kuwasi "Black Fist" Shakur for the World title, it seemed like his star had reached its peak. Then, almost as quickly, POD lost the World title to Bullywug and disappeared. Over the past few weeks, POD has announced his return to the ring, and he's here with us tonight. Welcome, Prince of Darkness. POD: Thanks for having me. Gilbert: The big question seems to be where you've been the past month or so. After losing the world title to Bullywug at Uproar in June, you seemed to disappear. What was going on with POD during the week before Uproar and what happened that caused your sabbatical? POD: When you have evrything you've strived for, it's real easy to lose focus. Movie offers, T.V. interviews.....I knew what I was, but had forgotten what it took to get there. When you put your hand in too many cookie jars, you slow down. After losing to Bullywug at Uproar, I needed time to be with myself. I needed to re-evaluate my goals, as well as set new ones. I even considered retiring from wrestling alltogether. I quickly realized that I couldn't just quit. I cleared my head and found peace of mind. I'm focused again. Gilbert: What brought you back? POD: I missed it. I missed being at the top. I missed wrestling. It's the only thing I've ever been good at. It wasn't for the money or the fans. I came back for myself. There is nothing on this earth as gratifying to me personally as getting my hand raised in victory. Gilbert: Where did you grow up?? POD: Parts Unknown!! (POD smiles) That's the truth! I honestly don't really know. The best I can tell you is Everywhere and Nowhere!! My father was a fighter pilot, and my mother was also in the Forces. We always moved around a lot. It's hard making friends when you're living out of a suitcase. I guess that's how I came to look out for myself. Gilbert: How did you become a wrestler? POD: My old man died in 'Nam when I was five. Of course I tried to follow in his footsteps. I enlisted when I was of age. It didn't work out very well. I hated taking orders. I was discharged after puching out one of my superiors in a bar one night. As fate would have it, a booker for a small indie fed happened to witness the whole thing. He told me I should be a wrestler. At least then I'd be paid to beat people up. Since I was jobless, and I had always been a wrestling fan, I looked into it. Six months later, I was ready to wrestle. Gilbert: Do you remember your first match and how did you do? POD: My first was hard to forget. Jim Crockett signed me straight out of wrestling school to a tryout contract. My first match was scheduled for a house show against none other than the man himself....."The Nature Boy" Ric Flair!! I was scheduled to do the job to Flair. Due to bad weather, not many people showed for the card........500 ticket holders at the most. About five minutes into the match, something happened to me inside that made me say to myself,"Hey!! I can beat this guy!! There's no way I'm jobbing to him!!" I snapped after that. I beat Flair form pillar to post that night, almost killing him. The look on the ref's face was priceless!! After smashing the greatest wrestler in history into the mat, the ref had no choice but to count the three and raise my hand. Crockett fired me the next day, thus, ending my career in the NWA. The fed exec's fed the public some bull about Flair getting injured in a plane accident. There was also a tape of the house show. I guess it "mysteriously" disappeared. I've never seen it. Guess the only people who will ever know the truth are the people in that empty arena on the night in question. Gilbert: What has been the greatest moment for POD, personally and professionally, and what has been the lowest moment? POD: Greatest momment would have to be beating Christopher Fantasy to become the first FeWS Heavyweight Champion. I really don't have a lowest particular momment, though I do have a lowest week. That was not too long ago, when I lost my XWC title to Bullywug and lost another title in another fed a couple days later. it was at this time that I decided that I needed to get away. Gilbert: You were one of the first wrestlers to use a gimmick of a man who sold his soul. Today, there are a lot of wrestlers who emulate a darkside gimmick. How do you feel about that? POD: Well, I've always said that I really don't try to emulate the "Undertaker" gimmick that is so common these days. My feelings?? Well, with the popularity of Mark Callous' WWF gimmick, it's bound to be ripped off. That's why I was cautious when developing my gimmick. I read a lot of Renaissance drama. After reading Marlowe's "Dr. Faustus", I knew the gimmick I'd work. The story of a man who sells his soul in order to become the most powerful man in the world. I can't say there aren't any aspects of Mark's gimmick that I've never used. As they say..... "If it ain't broke then don't try to fix it." However, I've always considered myself to be the "Boogie Man" rather than the "Undead Zombie Soul". Gilbert: What wrestlers have inspired or entertained you over the years? POD: Jake Roberts, Randy Savage, Rick Flair, Roddy Piper. I watched these guys. Gilbert: Being a heel, you'll get into arguments with fans. has there ever been a situation you felt genuinely worried about? POD: Not really. I'm a big guy. I can fend for myself. However, there was one night that I felt like there could be trouble. I was doing a house card in upstate New York about a year ago. After the card, I decided to head down to the bigger arena in the city. We weren't booked there. Some bigger fed had it that night. I decided to check it out. As I'm passing a nearby bar, I see these nine thugs beating the snot out of this one, puny, wimpy, little guy with earrings and long hair. For a second, they look up at me and say," Don't worry Boogie Man!! We only beat up weenie prettyboy wrestlers!!" With that I laughed and went on. Too bad I missed the ending of the show. Gilbert: One of wrestling's great debates these days seems to be the WWF vs. WCW. When kicking back at home, which do you tune in? POD: I try to watch both, but with my schedule, I don't have time to watch every show anymore. Gilbert: How do you feel about the state of wrestling today? POD: It could be a lot better. I'll admit, WCW has come up with some great angles as of late. Vince has to do something other than sex angles to win that big war. Gilbert : Who do you find entertaining or good in the XWC? POD: I like to watch my tag partner "Showstopper" Simon Lebec do his thing. He can work a crowd like nobody else I've ever seen. As much as I'd hate to admit it, I have respect for Bullywug too. He's the only man to have beaten me twice in my career. I respect him. Doesn't mean I like him. As of late, I've been watching a lot of tag matches, since Lebec and I are preparing to reap havok on that division real soon. Gilbert : What are your immediate goals now that you're back?? POD: To reclaim my glory. I don't think anything else has to be said.
