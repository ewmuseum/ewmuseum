Mike Houston: Hello everyone, welcome to the first House Show Results program of the AWF. Alot of great things happend tonight in Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania leading to more exctiment tomorrow night in Philadelphia at the First Union
Spectrum but first, let's get on with the happenings from tonight at the AWF
House Show.
"The Professional" Shawn Page -vs- Tony Lattanzio Mike Houston: The first match was to kick off the return of the AWF and the 16
Man AWF World Title Tournament. The first match is a First Round Tournament match up pitting "The Professional" Shawn Page against Tony Lattanzio. Tony
Lattanzio came out to his theme music "Money" by Pink Floyd as pyros burst to add life to the crowd. He came out flashing money around and counting it on his way to the ring just like he does at the casinos in his home of Las Vegas,
Nevada. He gets into the ring and tucks his money into his tights as he awaits his opponent. "Come with Me" by Puff Daddy plays over the PA System as "The
Professional" Shawn Page walks down the aisle a laser emits a scope on the ground that follows him to the ring. After he is several feet out of the curtains, pyrotechnics go off behind him. He makes his way to the ring and soaks up a loud positive pop from the audience that were in attendance tonight.
He then climbs the ropes in the corner of the ring and works the crowd getting more fanfare. He gets down and the match was underway.
Greg Styles: The match started off quite fast with Tony Lattanzio getting an early advantage. He whips Shawn Page into the ropes and on his way back, Tony goes for a clothesline and misses. He turns around and gets a clothesline in return. The crowd was getting into it as Shawn Page was working the crowd even more. Shawn Page took advantage the moment started using a series of moves on
Tony Lattanzio. At around the six minute mark, Shawn Page power bombed Tony
Lattanzio in the corner then pointed to the turnbuckle implying what he was about to do next. He ascended the turnbuckle and did a knee drop off the top rope. The crowd cheered for Shawn Page to finish off Tony Lattanzio and so
Shawn Page picked up Tony to his feet by his hair. Just then, out from the locker room curtains, none other than that savage drunken indian, Eddie Chavez, comes walking out. Chavez got a big pop from the Pittsburgh fans as he was wearing his infamous "F*** U" T-Shirt. A fan from the crowd who acted as if he was Chavez' number one fan started chanting "Eddie, Eddie, Eddie". The deranged indian looked at the fan with drunken eyes of rage and put the Navajo Death
Grip on him. Chavez yelled to the fan "NOBODY CALLS ME EDDIE, NOBODY"!!! Not just that but he then continued to ask another fan for some of his beer. The fan generously handed him a beer ball. How the fan smuggled the beer ball into the arena, we don't know but Chavez chugged and chugged not giving a damn how it got in. The crowd cheered "Chug, Chug, Chug" as Chavez started to distract
Shawn Page.
Mike Houston: As Tony Lattanzio started to come up behind Shawn Page, he quickly turned around and kicked Tony in the gutt and delivered a DDT. With
Chavez looking on, Shawn Page signalled for his finisher, "The Professional
Hit". He grabbed Tony Lattanzio and picked him up followed by dropping him in a
Michinoku Driver in the corner of the ring. This led to Shawn Page climbing the turnbuckle and jumping off the top turnbuckle with such elevation dropping the huge leg drop from the top rope which Shawn Page calls "The Professional Hit".
He immediately went for the cover with no time wasted as the ref counted the one, two, three as "The Professional" Shawn Page gets the win over Tony
Lattanzio advancing in the tournament. Greg Styles: That was a great match to start off the tournament and the entire return of the AWF. Eddie Chavez is proving that he isn't scared of Shawn Page as them two been giving each other heat since the return of AWF. Before we continue on, we have to pay some bills. We'll be back right after these messages.

[Commercial ends] Greg Styles: Okay we're back in the AWF Studios. We now would like to continue on with the results from tonights house show in Pittsburgh. The winner of the next match will go on to face Shawn page in the Second Round of the Tournament.
Python -vs- Michael Spears Mike Houston: The lights slowley dimmed as "N.O.R.E" by Noreaga started to play throughout the arena. Out came Michael Spears bobbing his head to the music with a pretty good fan reaction. He gets in the ring as the lights come back on and he awaits his opponent. Suddenly, "Devil Without a Cause" by KidRock plays.
The fans await the former AWF World Heavyweight Champion, Python in his first match to regain what was once his. After about 15 seconds, the fans are wondering where he is. Michael Spears is walking around the ring in anticipation. He starts throwing his hands up in the air yelling "Where is he"??!! Not knowing that Python likes to wait about 30 seconds after his song starts to come out, the crowd is wondering if he will show up. Finally, at around the 30 second mark like always, Python comes out from the locker room curtains. He has a mixture of fan reactions as some are booing him and some are cheering "Welcome back Python". He gets in the ring and immediately attacks
Michael Spears.
Greg Styles: Python taking it to Michael Spears early in the match. He whips
Spears into the turnbuckle and runs after him connecting with a flying clothesline. He continues with a series of chops to the chest of Michael Spears as the crowd reacts with the infamous Ric Flair "WOOOO" chants. He goes for another one but Michael Spears blocks it, reverses the positions and starts chopping the chest of Python and then hitting a Frankenstiener or a... a... a
FrankenSpearer. Whatever you want to call it. The crowd started to show their appreciation of the match up. The match went on back and forth maintaining a
50/50 effort. It was practically a stale mate all the way through as both man gave it their all. Finally, around the 12 minute mark as both men seemed to be exhausted as the match started to slow down a slight bit, Python who at the moment was on the floor started to get up. He gets up and brings Michael Spears to his feet, whips him into the ropes. On the way back, Python hits a devastating superkick that rocks the jaw of Michael Spears. Python calls for the "PYTHONdriver" as the crowd knows exactly what that is from his past in
AWF. He sets up Michael Spears for a piledriver but Spears reverses into a backdrop but NO!! Python lands on his feet, turns Spears around, kicks him in the gutt, sets him up for a piledriver. He jumps and hits his version of the piledriver known as the "PYTHONdriver". He goes for the cover and it's a one, two, three.
Mike Houston: A pretty good win on the part of Python as he advances to the second round of the tournament to face "The Professional" Shawn Page. I'm sure that Michael Spears has alot of frustration to take out due to him not advancing but expect him to bounce right back in no time. But next up we have the results for ya from another 1st Round Match Up.
NaiLZ -vs- "Showtime" Scott Harrison Mike Houston: To begin things off, "Killing Time" by Metallica plays over the
PA System as "Showtime" Scott Harrison came out to a decent pop from the fans.
He gets in the ring anxious than ever to wrestle. Suddenly, "For Whom the Bell
Tolls" by No Limit Soldiers started to play over the PA System. The fans were wondering why the theme music of NaiLZ was playing if he was fired from AWF. He came out through the crowd and got into the ring. He grabbed a microphone and began to spea....
Greg Styles: (interupting) Waaaaait a second here. I thought NaiLZ was fired from the AWF for his recent actions. He shouldn't be wrestling in this match up at all. I did something similar in my career and I was fired and because of it,
I couldn't get a job anywhere. I had to peel potatoes for a nearby resturante till some indy fed decided to use my managerical talents rather than my wrestling talents. What's he doing in AWF??
Mike Houston: Well, what happend was NaiLZ said that he is back in AWF to a strange but true pop from the crowd. NaiLZ went on to say that the AWF
President CAN'T fire him because it has to be approved by BOTH the AWF President and the AWF Vice President so due to the Vice President not being around at the time to ask his decision, only the AWF President made his so now we are back to square one. NaiLZ is STILL a wrestler for the AWF and after the announcement, the match was underway.
Greg Styles: They locked up with Nailz coming out on top early on in the match. Nailz uh.. he uh.. uh.. nailed Scott Harrison, no pun intended, with a back breaker following up by running to the ropes, bouncing off, and on the way back delivering a solid leg drop. The crowd is cheering for Nailz as he picks Scott
Harrison up by his hair but he hits a low blow on Nailz. He gets up and while
Nailz is bent over, Scott Harrison hits a backspin DDT. That's his finisher folks!!! The Showtime DDT!!! The ref counted a one, two, but no three due to
Nailz getting his leg on the ropes in time for the break. At around the nine minute mark, both men were battling back and forth. Wrestling holds were no more as a basic all out brawl ensued. Nailz signalled for his finisher called
"The Death Spike". He picks up Scott Harrison and hits the finisher for the one, two, thr.. oh but no. No three count as Scott Harrison's manager Jason
Cage came in to break the fall. Nailz got pissed off and caught Jason Cage before he escaped the ring. He set up Jason Cage and hit a jack-knife powerbomb on him as the fans scream in excitement. Suddenly, Scott Harrison came up from behind Nailz and turned him around, kicked him in the stomach and hit the
Showtime DDT. He covers immediately as it's a one, two, three.
Mike Houston: Well there you have it. "Showtime" Scott Harrison advances in the tournament as Nailz will have to sit back and watch as the others advance in the tournament, but expect him to cause alot of havoc in the AWF and try to wreck the results of the tournament due to his loss tonight. Gentlemen, we will be right back with more results of the house show after this. [Commercial plays] "Ever have an itch in the oddist places during the most critical of times? "The Icon" Shane Grey -vs- JR Kidd Mike Houston: The lights go out and strobes start to flash and laser light beams flash all over the arena as "Hell's Bells" by AC/DC plays over the PA
System. The crowd is waiting for the wrestler to come out. Suddenly, JR Kidd ermerges from the curtains as some pyrotechnics go off. He flirts with a few women on his way to the ring. Already in the ring is "The Icon" Shane Grey.
They tangle up and JR Kidd gets the upper hand. He has Shane in a headlock and is squeezing tight. Shane Grey tosses JR Kidd into the ropes but on the way back, JR Kidd hits a spin kick taking Shane Grey down. He quickly runs to the ropes, bounces off flying backward hitting a springboard elbow drop to the back of Shane Grey. He picks up Shane and hits a Michinoku Driver near the corner.
He leaves the ring going outside on the ring apron. He points up signalling he is going to ascend the ropes. The ladies in the crowd cheer him on as he goes up. He stands on the top turnbuckle and maintains his balance. JR Kidd does a
450 Degree Splash on Shane Grey and pulls him to the center of the ring. He goes for the cover but Shane Grey kicks out just before the third count. He seemed to be very exhausted and beat up at this point. JR Kidd picks him up and sets him up for a piledriver. He picks and his a spinning piledriver which he calls the "Sexydriver". He goes for the cover and gets the three count for the win in his first match up here in AWF.
Greg Styles: HAHAHA!!! YES!! JR Kidd beats Shane Grey proving that he is no icon that he claims he is. JR Kidd is every women's dream and every man's nightmare. I mean, look at him. The women love him, the men hate his guts. I would expect for JR Kidd to shake up the ranks of AWF in no time.
Mike Houston: Well, that's your opinion Greg. I agree that he is a pretty good wrestler though with that quick win. He proved himself by earning that win.
Anyhow, moving on to another match up. This was also a non-tournament match up. It was Spydy against that insane drunken indian, Eddie Chavez. Spydy -vs- Eddie Chavez Mike Houston: Spydy came to the ring with the fans booing him for some reason. "Another one bites the dust" by Queen plays as he gets in the ring and puts his paintball gun in the corner of the ring and getting ready for battle with
Chavez. "Symphony of Destruction" by Megadeth plays as Eddie Chavez makes his way to the ring. Fans gave him a huge pop. Some had signs that said "DRUNK
24/7" and some had signs that said "Chavez: 100% Proof". He made his way to the ring and as he got in, he was attacked right away by Spydy. He tried to take it to Chavez early by ripping a page out of the book of WCW's Raven by hitting an
Evenflow DDT. He turned around and started to celebrate too early as Chavez quickly got up and ran after Spydy, taking him down and beating up on him. He tried to apply the Navajo Death Grip but Spydy tried to stop his him by holding
Chavez' hand away from his mouth. Chavez got up and kicked the head of Spydy and brought him to his feet. He whipped Spydy into the ropes and ran after him.
On the way back from the ropes Spydy met a devistating clothesline from Chavez that knocked him down to the mat and hard. Chavez then went to the corner of the ring and grabbed Spydy's paintball gun and started playing around with it acting like he is shooting american soldiers kind of like the way his ancestors did way back. Anyhow, he started to toy around with it and accidently shot a blue ball square in the nuts of the AWF Interviewer James Monroe. He fell to his knees holding himself as his hands were blue from blue paint ball. The medical crew went an assisted him while Chavez looked on laughing.
Greg Styles: Spydy got up and tried to sneak up behind Chavez but with the instincts that Chavez had, he turned around and hit Spydy with the paintball gun square in the jaw. Chavez through it down and continued the assault by stomping on Spydy. Later on, at around the seven minute mark, Chavez was having a good time beating up on Spydy. None other than "The Professinal" Shawn Page, came walking out from the curtains to look on. This distracted Eddie Chavez causing Spydy to get an advantage. He rolled up Chavez for a two count but wasted no time at all to whip Chavez into the ropes and follow up with a drop kick. Shawn Page not having a loss for words kept blabbing on the microphone which started to drive Spydy nuts. Spydy told Shawn Page to shut up but Shawn
Page just kept on going. Eddie Chavez saw the advantage and came up from behind
Spydy, turning him around and hitting his Lone Wolf DDT. Eddie Chavez got down on his knees and applied a madible claw hold which he calls the Navajo Death
Grip. Spydy was trying to take the pain but there was nothing he could do but submit. Spydy started to tap out which allowed Eddie Chavez to get the win via submission.

The Bounty Hunter -vs- Jack Strange Mike Houston: The lights dimmed as red and green lights flashed around the arena. "Twist" by KoRn played as Jack Strange came out and made his way to the ring holding that bag of marshmellows he bought just the other day. He ate them on his way to the ring as he tossed a few at the crowd. Jack Strange got in the ring and got ready to face his opponent. Although we here at AWF can't remember who the hell made this song, the theme "The Good, The Bad, The Ugly" started to play as The Bounty Hunter and his manager/employer Mr. Rockefeller came to the ring. The Bounty Hunter looked to the side of him and seen a fan booing him holding a Jack Strange poster. The Bounty Hunter took it and tore it in half as he continued his way to the ring. He got up on the ring apron and with his big legs, entered the ring stepping over the top rope. Both men were ready to fight it out as the tension started to thicken. The bell rang and things got underway. Jack Strange started it out by tossing The Bounty Hunter to the ropes and following off with a power slam on the return.
Mike Houston: Jack Strange paused to pick his nose to add to his deranged ways. He followed it up by applying a chokehold on him. The ref told him to break the hold so Jack Strange went for an arm bar instead. The Bounty Hunter reached his long arms out to the ropes to break the hold. Both men back up to a vertical base as Jack Strange asked for the microphone. Jack Strange said into the microphone, "I'm the marshmellow man!!! KOO-KOO-KACHOOOOO!!!" The crowd went nuts and started to laugh as The Bounty Hunter wasted no time seeing the opportunity to turn the tables around. The Bounty Hunter now took advantage executing a power slam on Strange and dropping a knee to his forehead. He then picks up Jack Strange and whips him into the ropes and on the way back, The
Bounty Hunter grabs Strange by the throat and executes a chokeslam. He then acts like he has a gun in his hand and pretends to "shoot the enemy" with it signalling for his finisher. The Bouny Hunter then picks up strange and sets him up for a Tombstone Piledriver which The Bounty Hunter calls "Dead or
Alive". He jumps and hits the move on Jack Strange and goes for the cover. It's a one, two, three as The Bounty Hunter pins Jack Strange to advance in the tournament. Believe me, he will make sure that the AWF gets nuttier and crazier as long as he is around. Anyhow, after the match Mr. Rockefeller gave
Ten-Thousand Dollars to The Bounty Hunter. He pockets the money as Oliver
Closeoff appears from the curtains. He points at The Bounty Hunter while holding a huge wad of One Hundred Dollar Bills in his hand. He then smiles and turns his back, leaving through the curtains. It seems as if he was taunting
The Bounty Hunter. There is a grin on the face of Mr. Rockefeller and The
Bounty Hunter as they leave the ring concluding the night.
