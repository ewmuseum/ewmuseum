AWF (American Wrestling Federation, AOL-hosted, 1999) — Card

AWF Sunday Night Revolution

11 January 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The AWF logo appears on the screen, then explodes as "Trust" by Megadeth begins to play. The music continues to play as the scene changes to show thousands of screaming fans. As the camera focuses on different areas of the arena, some signs from the fans can be seen. One fan has a sign saying
"Hiroshima.............BOOM!" One lovely young lady has a sign saying "All Of
My Clothes Off, In No Time!" The camera then goes to ringside to the announcer's table to show AWF's two color commentators, Mike Houston and Greg
Styles.)
Mike Houston: Hello everyone, and welcome to the American Wrestling Federation's first ever Sunday Night Revolution! We are LIVE at the sold out
First Union Spectrum in the city of brotherly love, Philadelphia, PA!
Greg Styles: Brotherly love? HA! Are you kidding me? I parked my car down the street and went into the corner store, and when I got back, the only thing left of my car was the car hood ornament! First I get the Navajo Death Grip from that psycho Chavez, and now this? How am I going to get home?
Mike Houston: Well, if you hadn't called Chavez by his first name, that wouldn't have happened. As far as your ride goes.....Don't miss the bus!
Greg Styles: Oh very funny. Very funny. Hiroshima vs. Steven Sweet Matt Hampton: Ladies and gentlemen, our first match is scheduled for one fall. In the ring at this time, from Las Vegas, Nevada. He weighs in at 223 lbs......Steven Sweet!
(Fans begin to boo as Steven Sweet climbs to the top turnbuckle and begins to jaw jack with some fans)
Greg Styles: Doesn't seem like Steven Sweet is very popular here. These people have no respect!
(The lights go out and "It's the End of the World as we know it (and I feel fine)" by REM begins to play as a red spot light reveals Hiroshima coming to the ring. He enters the ring and raises his hands as fireworks burst out from the ring posts. The fans boo loudly, but a few cheers can be heard throughout the arena)
Matt Hampton: From Tokyo, Japan, weighing in at 287 lbs...........Hiroshima! Mike Houston: It seems there are a few Japanese fans in the arena tonight. Well, there's the bell and this match is underway. Greg Styles: Hiroshima wasted no time and just blasted Sweet with a vicious
Mafia kick. Hiroshima now stomping the hell out of Sweet. He picks him up and whips him into the corner, and follows up with a thunderous clothesline. Damn,
I think the ring moved!
Mike Houston: You may be right on that one, Greg. Hiroshima gorilla presses
Sweet up in the air, and drops him hard to the mat. Hiroshima drops a knee onto the face of Sweet, then begins to pound on his face. Hiroshima just destroying Sweet!
Greg Styles: Hiroshima picks up Sweet and lifts him into the air. Hiroshima showing off his strength by holding Sweet. All the blood rushing to his head.
Oh my! Brainbuster by Hiroshima spikes Sweet in the middle of the ring! He picks up Sweet and picks him up, runs across the ring, and plants Sweet with a
Running Liger Bomb.
Mike Houston: Hiroshima picks up Steven Sweet and heaves him into the corner. He begins to blast Johnson with vicious open hand chops that can be heard up in the rafters. Hiroshima whips Johnson hard into the opposite corner, and he hits hard sternum first.
Greg Styles: Wow! What a kick! After Sweet stumbled backwards from that
Irish whip into the corner, Hiroshima leveled Sweet with one hell of Mafia kick to the back of the head! Steven Sweet is out!
Mike Houston: Hiroshima picks up Steven Sweet, and drops him in The Vertabreaker, that's his backdrop driver finish. There's the cover, 1....2....3. Matt Hampton: Ladies and gentlemen, here is your winner, Hiroshima! (Fans boo loudly, but Hiroshima just laughs and leaves the ring area) Mike Houston:: Steven Sweet is hurt and trainers and medical personnel have brought out a stretcher to haul Steven Sweet to the back.
Greg Styles: Hiroshima basically just laid an atomic bomb of destruction all over Steven Sweet.
Mike Houston: It's time for our next match. Dexter vs. Troll ("Summer of '69" by MxPx begins to play as Dexter makes his way to the ring) Matt Hampton: Ladies and gentlemen, this match is a first round tournament match. Coming down the aisle from Orange County, California. He weighs in at
220 lbs.......Dexter!
(Dexter walks over to some fans to exchange high fives) Mike Houston: Dexter getting a nice reaction from the fans as he is high fiving many of the youngsters at ringside.
Greg Styles: Are you kidding me? This kid is nothing but a punk, just like the rest of those brats that support him.
Mike Houston: Stop that! Dexter now in the ring awaiting his opponent. ("Chant Rituals" by Elliot del Borgo begins to play as Troll runs into the ring and slides under the bottom rope)
Matt Hampton: Ladies and gentlemen from the Swamps, he weighs in at 232 lbs.....Troll! (Fans give Troll a mixture of boos and cheers but pays them no mind) Greg Styles: Look at this guy, he is too strange. Someone needs to get him to a good tailor.
Mike Houston: Strange but also very dangerous. There's the bell and they lock up. Troll a knee to the gut followed by a vicious knife edge chop. Troll whips Dexter into the ropes and hits a beautiful drop-kick. Dexter rolls to the outside.
Greg Styles: That is isn't a very safe place when fighting Troll.....Wow! Somersault plancha over the top rope by Troll and Dexter is down. This guy is nuts! Mike Houston: Troll with a chair in hand, and he plants it across the head of
Dexter. Dexter is dazed but he is still up. Oh my! Troll blasts Dexter with the chair again and Dexter goes down in a heap! Troll now setting up a table.
He grabs Dexter, but Dexter rakes the eyes of Troll. Greg Styles: Dexter rolls Troll back in and throws the table in as well. Troll cuts Dexter off while he is coming in and whips him into the ropes, but
Troll puts his head down to soon and Dexter grabs him and spikes him with the piledriver. He's going for a cover!
Mike Houston: 1....2..Troll kicks out. Dexter now setting up the table vertically in the corner. Troll still seems dazed as Dexter picks up Troll and goes for the Irish whip into the corner, but Troll reverses it and Dexter goes head first through the table! That was brutal!
Greg Styles: Dexter is out cold as Troll drags him away from the broken table. Troll now scaling the turnbuckles. Troll now on the top turnbuckle and delivers the Nightmare, his back flip leg drop right across the throat of
Dexter.
Mike Houston: Dexter is hurt, but Troll isn't going for the cover. Troll is signaling to the crowd then he locks on that armbar submission hold of his, The
Revenger. Dexter immediately taps out.
Matt Hampton: Ladies and gentlemen, here is your winner.......Troll! (Fans give him a mixture of boos and cheers again but he still pays them no mind) Greg Styles: Troll was just relentless this whole match and Dexter just got ran over. The referee now raising the hand of Troll, but Troll just decked the referee with a clothesline and locked The Revenger on the referee! He snapped!
Mike Houston: Security on their way to the ring, but Troll releases the hold and escapes through the crowd. The referee looks hurt as our trainers are at ringside checking on his condition. So Troll advances in the tournament for the World Title.
Greg Styles: The ring is cleared and they are helping the referee to the back, but now we have to pay some bills so stay tuned as Sunday Night Revolution continues after this
[Commercial Plays] Has your child been talking back?? Has your child been bringing home bad grades? Have you heard dirty words come out of your children's mouth? Well if so, then buy the new and improved SmackDown 2000 DELUXE. This new tool will straighten your children out in no time. No more misbehaving for the babysitter, no more running around in the supermarket. Buy the SmackDown 2000
DELUXE available at a store near you and teach your kids to know their role!
[Commercial Ends] Mike Houston: Okay we're back and ready for our next match up right away. Swift vs. Titan (Fallin' up-Black eyed peas begins to play as Swift makes his way to the ring. The fans cheer as Swift enters the ring) Matt Hampton: Ladies and gentlemen, this next match is a first round tournament match. From Chicago, IL, weighing in at 260 lbs.....Swift!
(Fans cheer loudly) Mike Houston: It seems like these fans are showing their support for Swift, and he is going to need all the support he can get.
("We're All Together Now" by WWF SlamJam begins to play as Titan walks intensely to the ring under a chorus of boos)
Matt Hampton: From Baltimore, MD, he weighs in at 385 lbs....Titan! (Some fans boo a little but Titan just stands in the corner, staring at Swift) Greg Styles: So we stole his theme song from the WWF, so what?! Anyhow, this guy Titan is a monster! He stands at 7' 3" and weighs in at 385 lbs. This guy is just an awesome human specimen. There's the bell and Swift runs across the ring and hits Titan with a hard clothesline. Titan just stares Swift. Titan telling Swift to try again. Swift hits the ropes and lands another clothesline and Titan still won't budge. Swift hits the ropes again and Titan attempts a clothesline, but Swift ducks and jumps onto the back of Titan and slaps on a sleeper hold. Titan grabs Swift by the back of the neck and just plants him with an earth shaking power slam!
Mike Houston: Titan is just so powerful. Titan picks up Swift, and hits him with a hard forearm shot to the back that floors Swift. Titan bounces off the ropes and drops a massive leg across the back of the head of Swift. Titan picks up Swift and slaps on a bear hug, but Swift counters with a clap on the ears that stuns Titan. Swift runs to the ropes and clips the knee of Titan, bringing him down to one knee. Swift immediately follows up with a running clothesline and the big man goes down. Swift locks on an armbar, but Titan begins to get back to his feet. Titan gets back to his feet, but Swift keeps the armbar locked on.
Greg Styles: Swift trying to keep the big man neutralized, but Titan just pushes Swift into the ropes and attempts a clothesline, but Swift ducks. Swift comes back and gets floored with a big boot to the face. Haha! I think Swift went down swiftly! Wouldn't you agree?
Mike Houston: That joke sucked. Greg Styles: Drop dead you moron. Titan picks up Swift and delivers a massive power bomb. We may need to pick Swift up with a spatula after that one! Titan picks up Swift by the throat and lifts him into air, then drops him into a backbreaker. That's his finisher, The Armageddon! There's the cover,
1....2....3. Matt Hampton: Ladies and gentlemen, here is your winner.....Titan! (Some fans cheer but mostly boos are heard as Titan ignores them and walks back to the dressing room.)
Mike Houston:: So big Titan advances in the tournament to decide who will be the next AWF World Champion. Let's go back to ring announcer, Matt Hampton, to find out who the participants of the next match will be.
"The G-Man" Granston Gunner vs. Morpheus Matt Hampton: Ladies and gentlemen, this next match is scheduled for one fall and is a first round tournament match.
("Pass Me By" by the Insane Clown Posse begins to play as the lights go out. A blue spotlight hits the entranceway and Morpheus walks out to a mixed reaction
from the crowd. He rolls into the ring and raises and arm while pyres shoot out from the ring posts) Matt Hampton: From Milwaukee, WI, he weighs in at an even 300 lbs.....Morpheus! (The fans give him and equal amount of cheers and boos, but he pays no mind as he stands in the corner)
Mike Houston: Morpheus looks a big impressive young man, let's see how he fairs in this first round tournament match.
("X-Raided" by Psycho Ward begins to play as Granston Gunner makes his way to the ring. The crowd boos him, but he just laughs and enters the ring)
Matt Hampton: From Pittsburgh, PA, he weighs in at 313 lbs...."The G-Man" Granston Gunner! (The crowd boos but he simply laughs) Greg Styles: Wow, look at this. These two big men are now in the center of the ring, staring each other eye to eye. Morpheus and Gunner hit opposite ropes and ram into each other hard, but no body even budges. It's like seeing two enraged bulls going at it.
Mike Houston: Morpheus hits the ropes again and Gunner attempts a clothesline, but Morpheus ducks the clothesline and nails Gunner with a superkick to the jaw that takes the big man down. Morpheus begins to stomp on the head of Gunner.
Morpheus picks up Gunner and whips him into the corner. Morpheus follows in with the clothesline, but Gunner gets the boot up just in time. Morpheus stumbles back and Gunner knocks him down with a forearm to the temple.
Greg Styles: Gunner drops an elbow onto Morpheus, then he picks him and slams him down to the mat. Gunner attempts a leg drop, but Morpheus moves out of the way. Morpheus picks up Gunner and whips him into the ropes and goes for an elbow, but Gunner ducks and hits a clothesline that sends both men over the top rope to the floor. The referee is laying the count as the two big men are exchanging punches and kicks.
Mike Houston: Morpheus rakes the eyes of Gunner and goes to ram him into the steel ring post, but Gunner stops and counters by ramming the head of Morpheus onto the ring post. Gunner kicks Morpheus and kicks him in the gut, then spikes him with the DDT. Gunner now rolling back into the ring as the referee is signaling for the bell. It seems that Morpheus has been counted out. Let's go to Matt Hampton for the official word.
Matt Hampton: Ladies and gentlemen, your winner by count out....."The G-Man" Granston Gunner! (The crowd boos loudly, but Gunner leaves the ring laughing with his hand raised in the air)
Greg Styles: Well, Gunner advances to the next round of the tournament. Our producers are telling us we need to take a break so stay tuned as the first ever Sunday Night Revolution will continue after this
[Commercial Plays] "It's here!! The new Meatcutter 560!! Use it to gutt fish, chop ham, and even to cut hot dogs. It cuts through cans, it cuts thew screws. And yes, it slices, it dices and we're sorry, it doesn't make Jullian Fries. But that's not all, if you order now, you'll recieve a free handbook on illegal things you can do with the Meatcutter 560 that we can't tell you now. All of this and more for the low price of $35.95. ORDER NOW!!
[Commercial Ends] Mike Houston: Boy, do we have strange commercials on this station or what? Anyhow, let's get on with another match. The Eliminator vs. Alex McCoist ("Dragula" by Rob Zombie begins to play as The Eliminator walks intensely to the ring) Matt Hampton: Ladies and gentlemen from New Orleans, LA, he weighs in at 268 lbs....The Eliminator!
(The fans give off somewhat of a mixed reaction, but The Eliminator is getting quite a few cheers from the fans)
Greg Styles: Well, well, these fans are actually showing some respect to
Eliminator. Miracles do happen.
Mike Houston: Be nice, or some of these fans might mug you outside. Greg Styles: Shut up, that could happen. ("Come with Me" by Puff Daddy and Jimi Page begins to play, but instead of Alex
McCoist, a young man with a head set on comes out and whispers something into the ring announcer's ear)
Matt Hampton: Ladies and gentlemen, your attention please. Alex McCoist is not here tonight so therefore, your winner by forfeit, The....
(Steven Sweet, with a neckbrace and a microphone in hand appears in the entrance way.) Steven Sweet: Now wait one second here. Nobody isn't going to get some cheap forfeit win as long as the Sweet One is around!
(The crowd starts a Sweet sucks dick chant that echoes throughout the arena) Steven Sweet: Shut the hell up you bastards! Shut up! Eliminator, if you have the balls, I'm going to kick you in them!
Mike Houston: Well, it seems even though Steven Sweet got decimated in his last match, he still wants to wrestle tonight as he charges toward the ring.
He dives under the ropes and gets stomped by The Eliminator who picks him up and whips toward the ropes. Sweet bounces off and gets his head nearly torn off with a vicious lariat. Eliminator picks up Sweet and hits The
Eliminationbomb. Sweet lands on the back of his neck and gets folded like a piece of paper. Cover by Eliminator. 1....2....3. Goodnight, Sweet!
Matt Hampton: Ladies and gentlemen, your winner........The Eliminator! (Fans give off a nice pop as The Eliminator makes his way back to the ring) Greg Styles: Well it seems The Eliminator has advanced in the tournament. Somebody better get a spatula and scrape up what's left of Steven Sweet. Dumb bastard must be from Philadelphia.
Mike Houston: If you had been paying attention, you would know that he is from
Las Vegas, not Philadelphia.
Greg Styles: Philly, Vegas, doesn't matter. Both places are filled with uneducated jackasses.
Mike Houston: I give up, you're hopeless. Anyhow, it's time for our main event on this very first Sunday Night Revolution!
"Sexy" Scott Johnson vs. Oliver Closeoff Matt Hampton: This next first round tournament match is scheduled for one fall and is the main event for the evening!
("I'm Too Sexy" by Right Said Fred begins to play as "Sexy" Scott Johnson makes his way to the ring)
Matt Hampton: From Tallahassee, FL, he weighs in at 226lbs...."Sexy" Scott
Johnson! (Fans give off a mixed reaction as Johnson awaits his opponent) ("Whatta' Man" by Salt-n-Pepa begins to play as Oliver Closeoff makes his way to the ring under a heart shaped spotlight)
Matt Hampton: From Malibu, CA, he weighs in at 276 lbs....Oliver Closeoff! (There are mostly boos from the fans, but quite a few young ladies are cheering for Oliver Closeoff)
Greg Styles: Finally, main event time. Both athletes lock up. Side headlock by Closeoff. Johnson pushes Closeoff into the ropes, but misses with the clothesline. Closeoff comes back with a stiff elbow and a vicious knife edge chop. Johnson returns fire with his own knife edge chop. Now the two athletes are exchanging chops as the crowd is screaming "Woooo!" with each hit.
Closeoff gouges the eyes of Johnson and breaks the exchange. Mike Houston: Irish whip by Closeoff sends Johnson into the ropes and Closeoff catches Johnson in a beautiful power slam! Cover! 1...2..Johnson kicks out.
Closeoff now stomping on Johnson. Closeoff bounces off the ropes and drops and elbow onto the chest of Johnson. Closeoff now flexing his muscles. He doesn't see Johnson getting up behind him. Closeoff turns around and ducks a wild haymaker by Johnson, then lands on low blow that send Johnson down.
Greg Styles: Ha ha, classic wrestling if you ask me. Closeoff hooks Johnson for what looks like a DDT, but Johnson counters by suplexing Closeoff over the top rope to the outside. Johnson bounces off the ropes and goes for a somersault plancha, but Closeoff counters with a vicious power bomb! The fans are on their feet! Both men look hurt. Closeoff grabs Johnson and goes for the Irish whip, but Johnson reverses it and Closeoff is sent into the guard rail upside at an incredible speed.
Mike Houston: Scott Johnson now rolls into the ring to break the referee's count. Closeoff looks dazed and rolls back into the ring, only to get kicked in the ribs by Johnson. Johnson goes for the Irish whip, but Closeoff hangs on to Johnson's arm and again gouges the eyes of Johnson. Closeoff hooks Johnson and hits a beautiful vertical suplex, then floats over and goes for the cover.
1....2...Johnson gets the shoulder up! Closeoff drives Johnson into the corner and a forearm shot to the temple. He goes for an Irish whip, but Johnson reverses it and Closeoff hits the corner and hard. Closeoff stumbles forward, but Johnson put his head down too soon and gets caught by Closeoff. There it is! The PassionPlex! What an amazing bridge! 1....2....3. It's over!
Matt Hampton: Ladies and gentlemen, here is your winner....Oliver Closeoff! Greg Styles: So Oliver Closeoff gets the win here......hold on, we have trouble! (Mr. Rockefeller and the Bounty Hunter make their way to the ring) Mike Houston: This doesn't look good for Closeoff. Hunter now in the ring. He approaches Closeoff and raises his hand to hit him........no wait! Closeoff just gave Bounty Hunter a high five. They're in cahoots!
Greg Styles: Ha ha! I love it! They had everybody here fooled! (Oliver Closeoff, Bounty Hunter, and Mr. Rockefeller make their way back to the dressing room. Closeoff stays to flirt with some young ladies at ringside as
Bounty Hunter and Mr. Rockefeller disappear behind the curtain)
Greg Styles: Ha ha, look, he's talking to some lovely young ladies at ringside. Remember, all of their clothes off, in no time!
Matt Houston: Something is happening over at ringside. Who is that? Holy cow! It's Akira "Kamikaze" Stone! He was hiding under the ring the whole time! Closeoff doesn't see him. Oh! Akira just leveled him with a vicious clothesline. Now he is just pummeling Closeoff repeatedly.
Greg Styles: Oh my gosh! Akira Stone just pearl harbored Oliver Closeoff! JR
Kidd now coming from the back to make the save. What's JR Kidd doing here?!
He has no business being here. Uh Oh!!...... Wow! Hiroshima out of no where just blasted the JR Kidd with a steel chair to the back. Now all four athletes are battling it out in the aisle. It's total chaos! I love it!
Mike Houston: Hiroshima and Akira Stone are battling out with JR Kidd and
Oliver Closeoff and here comes the authorities to break it up. This is complete chaos!!
Greg Styles: Oh No!! We gotta go!! We're running out of time but wait here comes The Bounty Hunter out from the back to rescue Oliver Closeoff and JR
Kidd. Security is holding him back... We gotta go!! We gotta go!! See ya next week right here for Sunday Night Revolution!! For Mike Houston, this is Greg
Styles.
(The camera zooms out as the crowd is chanting "Let them go, Let them go!!". Still-clips of the show play as "Trust" by Megadeth plays. After about 15 seconds, a black screen with the AWF Logo appears and then explodes as the screen fades to black )
