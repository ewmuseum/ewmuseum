A Baltimore Usenet fantasy wrestling federation of 1996 to 1998, voted the newsgroup's top federation in December 1997, which spent the autumn of 1996 under the banner of the VPWF in a takeover angle before returning to its own name, and marked each May with an anniversary pay-per-view, May Madness. Written by AI, 2026

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The Major League Wrestling Organization opened on rec.sport.pro-wrestling.fantasy on 16 May 1996 with a call for hardcore fans and their TNM wrestlers, and ran its cards as television: weekly shows called Wednesday Washout, Saturday SLAM and later Thursday Night Titans, quarterly Battle Brawls, and pay-per-views, out of a home arena in Baltimore, Maryland and the Smarkdome in Pittsburgh.

Its first autumn was a takeover. On 14 September 1996 Vic Patrinne pinned the federation's president in a cage match to take it over, renamed it the Vic Patrinne Wrestling Federation, banned the letters MLWO, and split the roster between his own alliance and an MLWO Alliance that wanted the fed back. By January 1997 the MLWO name had returned, and its president with it.

It shared its wrestlers with the federations around it. Its cards booked DPW, PCW and EWA names into its own rings and carried other feds' matches on its pay-per-view, and its president and one of its own stars wrestled in PCW as the tag team Bigger Than Hollywood. Its first anniversary card, May Madness at Disney World on 25 May 1997, ran a Disney Death Match fought across the theme park and a falls count anywhere match of fifty-six minutes between Caleb Temple and Matt Harris.

In 1998 its five singles championships (World Heavyweight, North American, United States, Television, and Lightweight) and a tag team title built a recognized “Quadruple Crown” of their own: Matt Harris became the first wrestler to hold four of them at once when he added the Television title to his Lightweight, North American, and World reigns at a January 1998 Saturday Slam in Pittsburgh. “Armitage” Steve Spector, a two-time and later three-time World Heavyweight Champion whose finisher was called the Cherry Blossom Bomber, was most often matched with Captain Midnight. At the second anniversary May Madness, on 25 May 1998, Kevin Legend won the World Heavyweight Championship in a ladder match and Team LWC took the Unified Tag Team Championship in a three-team elimination match.

How it ended is not known. Its former operator wrote in October 1998, founding another promotion, that he had been replaced at the head of the Major League Wrestling Organization about six weeks earlier.
