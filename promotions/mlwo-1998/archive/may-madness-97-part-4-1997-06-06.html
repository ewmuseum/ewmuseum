T H E R O C K ======================================= Guest: CALEB TEMPLE ======================================= [The wrestling ring is darkened, and the podium back near the entrance lights up. There is a glowing white sign on a jail cell like background that flashes "THE ROCK". There is a black and white marble couch below the sign, where Shawn Adams sits. Shawn is wearing a white PCW t-shirt, blue jeans, and a pair of Nike Air Max 2s. On the couch is a facsimile of the
MLWO World Title. An MLWO logo now hangs over "THE ROCK" sign. Adams sits up straight and picks up the mic. He waits for a second, then speaks.]
SHAWN ADAMS: Welcome...to...THE ROCK! [Fans boo, lots of smarts in attendance pop] SHAWN ADAMS: Well, what a night it's been so far. Jak Martin came out here, we talked about the fake Jak Martin, the hatred for Justin B.
Chris D came out, we talked...I don't care what any of it was about. Now, we're up to the third time you all have to put up with me tonight. My simple answer is this: tough shit. My third guest...well, third time's the charm.
He kicks ass. This is the one guy I have respect for because I think he can kick my ass. He is the single toughest man I've ever seen in the sport...oh, and he's a good friend of Insanity...that makes him better.
Ladies and gentlemen, I give you at this time the man who's job tonight is to destroy Matt Harris like he destroyed Steve Sampson. The EWA
HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPION OF THE WORRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRLD...CALEB TEMPLE! [The lights go out and the arena is in total darkness. The sound of
'Carmina Burana' crashes over the PA system, and a stream of blue light shoots out. A figure emerges, silhouetted by the light. He begins to walk slowly down the aisle. As he reaches the podium, a spotlight goes on, and we see Shawn
Adams
standing next to the man... it is Caleb Temple. He is wearing black jeans and boots, and his silver crucifix is laid against his bare, tattooed chest.
His hair hangs in straggly rat tails over his face. Temple shakes Shawn's hand] SHAWN ADAMS: Caleb...a big night, not only for you personally, but for MLWO. TEMPLE: This night, Shawn, people will be MADE to understand. This will be the night when the millions of people who watch MLWO will KNOW that
Caleb
Temple is FOR REAL. SHAWN ADAMS: Before we talk about tonight's match, you're currently involved in the MLWO's 'Sultan of Slam' tournament, the winner of which WILL get a shot at the World Champion, whoever that may be. Think you can add the
MLWO World title to your collection? TEMPLE: Does it matter, really? SHAWN ADAMS: I don't follow, man. Of COURSE it matters. If not, then why do it? TEMPLE: You're not seeing the big picture yet, Shawn, but you will soon enough. See, all of the titles I've won have been incidental. They've been a side-effect of my achieving a higher ambition. All the titles in the world don't mean a thing in the grand scheme of thing. What's more important is getting the job done effectively in order to win a title.
SHAWN ADAMS: Still not with ya. TEMPLE: Let me dumb it down. Titles are not why I do this. It has NEVER been about that. I do this, I put my body on the line night after night like this, because I need to expel my own burning rage. To do that, I need to project my own twenty-seven years of horror onto a focal point. That focal point is my opponent. If I take a title in the course of destroying my opponent, it's a bonus. But the titles are still of secondary importance.
SHAWN ADAMS: Back to the tournament, though. You think you have a shot at winning it? TEMPLE: I have FAITH, Shawn. That's all I NEED. Harris, Spector, Stone... those three fools will NOT be allowed to stand in my path. My destiny awaits me. If I have to break all THREE of them, I WILL. Let there be no confusion about that. I don't care about any of them. I don't care what I have to do to destroy them. I don't give a good God damn.
SHAWN ADAMS: Let's talk a little about the place where you made your name, the Extreme Wrestling Association. Just two weeks ago, you finally won the
World championship over there... not that it means anything. [He laughs distastefully] TEMPLE: Whoa, just hold on there, boy. Let me just cut you short there, because there's something I want to bring up regarding the EWA.
SHAWN ADAMS: You're finally going to renounce it for the festering shitheap it is? TEMPLE: Not quite. I want to talk about the attitudes of certain long-time MLWO superstars. More and more recently, I've been hearing people talk about Caleb Temple competing in the MLWO after making his name in the EWA. Now, these people who've been running their dumb mouths off, they have this idea that I'm winning so convincingly in MLWO because of my past reputation, and the things I've achieved in the EWA.
SHAWN ADAMS: Since when do _you_ care what people say about you? TEMPLE: Get it clear, Shawn, I DON'T. But I don't think people should be misled like that. Since I arrived in MLWO, I've worked hard. I've taken on
EVERYONE your buddy Cutter could throw at me. And I've beaten them BADLY. The EWA reputation, that is meaningless here. I know that. It would be the easiest thing in the world for me to come out here and talk about my record in EWA.
[He runs his hand through his dark, stringy hair] It wouldn't prove a damn thing, though, and I know that, so I don't do it. I't walk out here with my World title around my waist. I don't push the
EWA at ALL. See, people need to understand this one thing about Caleb
Temple. He represents NO organisation. No stable. He represents Caleb
Temple. And ONLY Caleb Temple.
SHAWN ADAMS: Then how do explain your success so far here? TEMPLE: [chuckling] God's will, son. God's will. SHAWN ADAMS: Now, while we're talking EWA, what do you make of tonight's little bombshell with 'Slick' Rick?
[Temple reflects for a moment] TEMPLE: Ahh, yes... Baptist. The man who gave me my break in the EWA. See, people don't understand the history between Caleb Temple and Rick Baptist. Sometimes I don't agree with the man or what he stands for. But I _AM_ indebted to the man. Last August, when no professional wrestling organisation in America would touch Caleb Temple with a bargepole,
Baptist
took a MAJOR gamble and signed me for the EWA. See, for years, I wrestled in places like India, Brazil, Puerto Rico, Taiwan and Burkina Faso. Why?
Because no wrestling promotion in America would risk everything on me. They said I was 'too extreme'... 'a bad risk'... [He chuckles] But Baptist... he saw something in me. SHAWN ADAMS: Now HE has some major problems with the EWA, it would seem. Kind of ironic, since you're now the Champion of that very promotion. TEMPLE: See, that's business, Shawn. It's all deals and talks and offices, and none of that stuff holds even the slightest fascination for Caleb
Temple. I'm not a businessman. I take care of business. There's a big difference.
SHAWN ADAMS: Now, as we draw ever closer to the big moment, onto tonight's match. 'Hardcore' Matt Harris. The first time you guys have ever fought, right?
[Temple chuckles again. It sounds sickening] TEMPLE: HARRIS!!! Come out, come out, wherever you are! I've waited a LONG time for this moment. See, all of you folks who think Matt Harris really
_IS_ hardcore, you just sit back there and watch a little show. You just relax and watch Caleb Temple go to work. In a few minutes, I'm going to rip your little hardcore icon to SHREDS.
SHAWN ADAMS: You sound confident for a man who's about to go up against one of the wiliest veterans in the sport.
TEMPLE: A veteran he may be, Shawn. But 'Hardcore'... he's NOT. _I'LL_ show the world what 'hardcore' _really_ means. Watch and learn.
SHAWN ADAMS: And final words? TEMPLE: No more talking now, Shawn. It's time. Trust me.
