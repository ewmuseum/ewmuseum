[RSPWF 200] Congratulations/A Little Acceptance Speech

15 December 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Well, it's been a whirlwind of a year, hasn't it? We wind closer and closer to the end of the damned year, and I still can't put out a late MLWO card no matter how hard I try not to be on time <grin>. This year's Top 200 feels especially good for me, because MLWO had a very high representation in it, and somehow, my lucky self got this big number 2 for Shawn Adams. As for how the MLWO did... 192. Paradox - MLWO United States Champion 181. Super Scott - Died in MLWO :) 150. Chris Deugau - Hilarious roleplayer, all around cool guy 143. Mr. Bungle - newest entry, so I'm able to put him here :) 112. "Dreamlover" Trey Porter - Retirement tour stopped long enough to wrestle the match of the year. 73. Dark Destroyer - Former MLWOer. One of three who were in the Legends Match. I'm not trying to take them as my own, but they're here for the hell of it. 57. "Jumbo" Jim Mongol - Successful wherever he goes 37. Marcus Renge - Triple crowner in the big M. 28. Captain Midnight - MLWO Rookie of the Year 24. Jak Martin - His description said it all. My current Champ 21. Steven "The Edge" Spector - Joe, SS's handler, is a great guy, and Spector is the most over face in the history of e-wrestling. 18. Jeff "Madfox" Matthews - Pretty new to MLWO, but already working his way up. 16. Mr. Robinson - Undefeated. Period. 15. Requiem - Uhh, he wrestled once in MLWO, he's listed as the most dreaded adjective of all, (IIWF, MLWO), shame on you for having the guts to perform in two feds feuding for #1. :) 10. Matt Harris - A lot you can say about this guy. It's already been said, though, if it's positive. 9. Otto "The Butcher" Verhoeven - Guilty of Requiem's crime. 6. Caleb Temple - Currently AWOL from the 'net, period. And while I'm at it, since [name withheld] was right, my ego can't get any bigger <g>, the big plug: 2. "Superstar" Shawn Adams - 35-4 lifetime. That period is an end of statement. Congrats to all who made the list, a special congrats to the 17 MLWO guys on that list, and a special congrats to Shawn Adams, who's newest job in LWC is to hunt down Brody Thunder, tear his head and shoulders away from each other, and pin him just so I can say I'm #1. Oh, by the way, big congratulations Rod, you earned #1.
