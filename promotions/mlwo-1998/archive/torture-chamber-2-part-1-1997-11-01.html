[MLWO Torture Chamber 2] 26 Oct. 97 - One

1 November 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Scene fades in on Mark Cutter's hospital bed. Mark is laying in it, but is in his suit, etc., as if he were going to be at the Tokyo Dome.
A little scrolling time-lapse announcement states that the occurrence was five hours ago, at Tokyo National Hospital.]
MARK CUTTER - "Sit back, might not be a fun ride..." [Mark frowns] MARK CUTTER - "Maybe Owen Curtis was right...maybe wrestling IS boring...maybe Barry Hart was right...wrestling isn't WRESTLING anymore. I've been sitting here in this hospital since the eleventh, and I'm seeing BORING, POINTLESS working. Two events, and I'll I've seen is blood. What we have tonight is a PPV...something that should be different. If I see something I expect to happen...if I see the same thing over and over again...it might just be too much. I might just [BLEEP]ing leave, because when I say we're the best..."
[Mark sits up in the bed] "I'm not feeding the ego of a man who will leave on the first loss." [Mark laughs] "I'm not feeding a main eventer who wants lips on his [BLEEP] at all times. And you know what?"
[Mark smiles] "I don't like Gunnar Gaines, either." [Mark laughs] "To tell the truth, if I see Torture Chamber 2 tonight, and I hear
Slick Rick act like the man who's name he stole one more time, or refer to a half-cooked wrestling show one more time, these crutches aren't going to be able to hold me back. Rick Baptist has stole every thing there is in wrestling, and now he's trying to steal fame he used to have. You can steal words all you want, Rick, but in time you will realize that hype isn't always _true_. When you came to the MLWO at
May Madness as my vice president, you spoke the truth when you said
MLWO was great. However, I used you as a weapon against the EWA. And now that you're running the EWA, I have no choice..."
[Mark points to the TV] "A few weeks ago you called a glorified RARE a pay-per-view. The two hundred people with balls enough to buy it out of sheer blindness have refunds in the mail, thanks to me. People should get that shit FREE.
I don't want to see glorified SLAMs in the MLWO. I don't want to see
Jak Martin v. Marcus Renge headlining a card in a few months with
J-Dog against Big Cheese for the North American Title because my talent base is too small. Rick..."
[Mark frowns, and hangs his head for a moment.] "You're fired." [Mark just merely spits] "It hurt to say that, Rick...but it's the truth. You're getting too old...and stale. It's time for you to retire. Goodbye, you can make your own paychecks now that you run the Nelson-Ringmaster Wrestling
Federation."
[Mark counts on his fingers...a "one"] MARK CUTTER - Owen Curtis, I'll get to you later in the night... [Mark holds up a "two", as in "two down".] MARK CUTTER - And as for Barry Hart...your ass sucks. Oh, I'll talk to you later too. [Mark holds up a fourth finger, and a small frown comes across his face.] MARK CUTTER - As for the quitter...Marcus Renge...the fourth agitator, so to speak...he may have dug deepest into my nerves on this last waining hour. He didn't have the BALLS to call Owen Curtis...or even the BRASS BALLS to call the real President, Mark Cutter. Hell, he had no balls period. He got that little b[BLEEP] Gwynne Soleil to come down here and hand the note to the secretary of the hospital, who in
TURN handed it to me. I read it, called Owen...and he handled the broadcasting of a photo of the letter. That's what the wrestling world saw last night.
[Mark smiles] MARK CUTTER - Marcus, your shoots were not pathetic, they just weren't real enough. You want the definition of a shoot? Read stuff by
Kendel Jones...THERE is a real man, son.
[Mark feels a cold shiver down his own spine.] MARK CUTTER - You want to realize you're not good enough anymore? You shoulda just asked me. Or, wait...maybe you should have asked Shawn
Adams. You and he were on the borderline of shooting each other here in the MLWO, so I got him out of here so you could focus on my
"booking" for you. So, what do you do? You follow me to GFWA...where he kicks your ass. Shoulda realized it then when one half of Bigger than Hollywood kicked your ass, you're gone.
[Mark laughs] MARK CUTTER - You wanna know how I feel right now, Marc? I feel like reminding you of something. When you signed your contract, it was signed with that prick Vic Patrinne. Sure, you got a new contract when I came back, but it was signed originally with that prick. There was a no compete clause in it, saying that if you left the MLWO...or wrestled somewhere other than the MLWO...within ninety days of the end of your contract...you're my property. I will OWN YOU, Marcus
Renge...and guess what...now I do. [Mark stands up, his body looks weak.] MARK CUTTER - But do I want a washed-up has been? That's an easy answer. Hell no. [Mark starts to walk out of the room.] MARK CUTTER - You're been given enough of my presence for one day,
Marcus Renge. Have a nice life.
[Fade] MARK CUTTER - For those watching Torture Chamber...consider yourselves privileged. [Silence...then the silence is broken by the visual of the disclaimer.] !!! WARNING!!! The following program contains scenes of an explicit, overly violent nature. It is a graphic program, and viewer discretion is advised.
The surgeon general has stated that MLWO is not plausible for those pregnant, with a heart problem, or unable to jump up and down and yell real loud, for the symptoms of spontaneous excitement abound in all
MLWO fans.
[The screen is lit up by an MLWO logo, which swirls around amidst a stormy backdrop. A singular crackle of lightning is heard.]
VOICE - The Major League Wrestling Organization, the overall standard in wrestling action, and a good attempt at violent, bloodthirsty entertainment.
[Screen: Black. Suddenly, the MLWO Torture Chamber logo is on the screen. It slowly fades out.]
[Camera fades in on Tokyo, and the Tokyo Dome. The Dome is filled to capacity, and the white ramp has been replaced with a black and orange ramp, with the orange letters "TORTURE CHAMBER 2" running down it vertically. In the background are five tombstones, those of Alex
Adams, Matt Harris, Steve Spector, Jak Martin, and Marcus Renge. A slow, dry, icy fog rolls from the entranceway. Black streamers fly from the roof, which is focused upon by the camera. In the rafters is the humongous Torture Cage, in which the main event will take place.
The smaller Torture Cage will be constructed later in the card. Four long poles hang from the corners of one of the two rings, which both have orange aprons, with the MLWO logo on the sides. Torture Chamber
2's logo appears on the video wall, and the crowd starts to pop. The arena is slowly shrouded in darkness, as fire explodes from the four corners of the ring. A shocked crowd pops madly.]
["Would I Lie to You?" by the Eurhythmics plays, and a man in a tailored tweed suit steps out and stops at the top of the ramp. From his blond hair and 238-pound frame, we can see that he is MLWO President Owen "The
Truth"
Curtis.] OWEN "THE TRUTH" CURTIS: Stop the music. [Music stops.] Good. I'm not even going to go all the way down to the ring, because what I have to say here is short and sweet.
Now, it has been brought to my attention that one Dark Destroyer may want to appear here tonight, in person, in the MLWO.
[Crowd boos. Several chant "Spector! Spector! Spector!"] Well, as president of the MLWO, I am here to do three things. One is to tell The Truth, the Whole Truth and Nothing But The Truth, so help you all
... [he smirks] ... the SECOND is to put the WRESTLING back into the Major League
Wrestling Organization, because right now MLWO could stand for Moronic Losers
Waffling with Objects ...
[he grins] ... and the third thing I'm here to do is to do the things that Mark
Cutter WANTED to do, but didn't have the GUTS to do. Now, every president has WANTED to ban the Dark Destroyer. Every time his Pampers get out of joint, the guy walks out. Well I am doing exactly that. Effective NOW, the Dark Destroyer is BANNED from the MLWO ... and I have directed security to make sure the man isn't even allowed in the building.
A single sperm cell would have an easier time swimming through a tube of Nonoxynol-9 and surviving it, than Casper will have finding his way to an MLWO ring. That's The Truth tonight, or any time from this point forward. I'm sure you'll see this is the BEST move for the MLWO from a fan's standpoint, as well as a stockholder's standpoint. It's DEFINITELY the best move for those prone to the crippling nausea brought on by Casper's big, fat, worthless ego. Think about it. Why should we spend money to hype someone that no one respects and who won't keep his word? Why give him exposure? Why even risk it by letting him even buy a ticket? I may be the first president smart enough to realize this, and definitely the only one with the balls to put that realization into action, but we _shouldn't_ allow him back. EVER.
And we won't, as long as the president here is Owen Curtis. This is the right thing to do, and everyone knows it. It's been a gut feeling for everyone for nearly a year now. I just put it into words and into action. All of you should consider me a hero. And _that_, gentle audience ...
... is The Truth. ["Would I Lie to You" plays again as Owen leaves the way he entered...but then stops, sitting ringside. Vinnie Styles comes up into the ring holding a mic.]
VINNIE STYLES - Ladies and gentlemen...your President... MARK CUTTER! ["Hail to the Cheif starts up, Mark Cutter enters the arena, wearing his tux. The arm is in a cast, and he still has the neckbrace on. He has now picked up a limp. He starts to step into the ring, and then sees Owen Curtis sitting at ringside, wearing formal attire. He does not really stand out in the crowd at first glance, as the floor seats are mostly attired by the rich on this occasion. Mark, however, sees him right away, and asks Vinnie to toss him the microphone.]
MARK CUTTER - Well, well, well...if it isn't Phillip "The Truth" Muschnik. [Owen remains calm.] MARK CUTTER - Owen, son...I've got QUITE a few things to say to you after that little tirade...
[Mark mutters 'truth my [BLEEP]'] MARK CUTTER - First of all... [Suddenly, Barry Hart comes out, and Mark notices him...and is immediately cut off, as the Ass has a live mic.]
BH: Hey slot machine!! One armed bandit!!! Bet your mistress has a strong forearm now. Looking pretty desparate. Lose the favor of the boys in the back. Being the outsider is making you look a little crazy. Strange how the guys who make the money have a lot of the pull.
You're the one who got me into this stupid match and see what happened to you, unemployment. I thought I saw you outside begging for some money. I can't believe you thought that washer I gave you was actually money. You looked like a little kid on Christmas morning. (Barry speaks like Butthead) DUMBASS!!!!! Uhh....huh....huh
[Barry then looks toward the crowd. His arms spread wide looking for some appreciation.] BH: Asso! Asso! [Barry then bows] BH: Don't know if you speak any English but I would like to say "Herro!" It's nice to see that breeding isn't limited to the southern
U.S. The least you could do is put your women in dresses so I could tell you apart.
[Mark Cutter stops his stare, and walks towards the apron, going up the steps and into the ring. He smiles, and then points at Barry with his good arm.]
BH: Unemployed, ex-president, COME ON DOWN!!! I think I can help you out. Now that I have ten minutes to kill waiting for his beaten up ass to get down here, I saw that there was no kennel here. No dinner and a card tonight. But seriously, thanks for comping here and supporting my cause. This whole card makes me sick and I am only here to show my disapproval of it. I also have a huge announcement.
[Barry then climbs to the middle rope.] BH: GOD DAMN!!! My ass is looking great. Even though you nips can't see it, you can trust me. Just move your..................
[Cutter then taps Barry on the shoulder and Barry jumps back in fear.] BH: Hey, ex-prez. If I pull on your good arm, will you spit out money??? HAHAHAHAHAHA!!!!!! If you try to hit me, Slabuu will kick your handicapped ass within seconds. You just listen. That's why I have the mic and you don't. Shouldn't you be contemplating on how you are getting home??? I bet your excited about your job as a paper boy starting.
[Cutter then rips the microphone out of Barry's hand.] MARK CUTTER - You've seen the interview...I know you were watching. Barry, everything I did say and could say about you is correct. You know it, too. All the mutilation of your ass people want to perform is NOT out of jealousy, Ass Master of Pain. It's out of hate for the fact that you flaunt something they can't help but think looks so feminine.
[Barry and Slabuu mock Mark.] MARK CUTTER - Mock all you want, maybe you can hit the road like the
Slick Has-Been. BH: Boy, I am really inspired now. I think I will start wrestling
"dysfunctional" style now. Slabuu, go get a table. I am gonna let you break it.
[Slabuu starts jumping up and down all excited.] BH: I was just kidding, you DUMB FUCK!!! [Barry then pushes Cutter back.] BH: It's time to leave handy-man. Here's the big announcement. Yes, I am going to work the weapons match tonight just to show that I can beat any style with my "traditional" style. Our great president
Curtis, by the way I hope you love your watch, has granted me a title match that if my opponent breaks any rules then he not only loses the match but the title as well. The name of this match will be the
"Softcore Life Match". Also, if I beat Superstes and his painless heart, which I will, the whole card November 8th, in the Omni in
Atlanta, will be under Softcore rules. I've titled the card, "Softcore
Coming Home". How perfect. Use the ropes for leverage and you could lose the match. See what you have started, Cutter??
MARK CUTTER - Softcore eh? Softcore? Fine...the deal is yours. [Mark turns his back to the crowd] MARK CUTTER - Cain will kill this guy...this should be a sin... [Slabuu taps Cutter, and an alert cripple he is, Mark delivers a
Forward jawbreaker. When he gets up, Barry grabs the arm and delivers a single arm DDT before locking on a big armbar. Mark screams out in pain, the sling ripped from him.]
BH: Bounty collected. I told you not to mess with me and now it is even worse. I hope you've got severence pay. Insurance won't cover all of this damage.
[Barry acts like he is going to faint.] BH: All of this hard work has made me exhausted. GET THE STRETCHER!!!!! WE NEED HELP!!! [The EMT then comes down to get Cutter. After the EMT puts Cutter on the stretcher Barry shoves him off of the stretcher, over the top rope down to the floor landing on his already injured shoulder. Barry then gets on the stretcher.]
BH: Hold my hand Slabuu. Slabuu: STAY AWAKE!!!! TALK TO ME!!!! BH: I can see the light. Lou Thesz, I am coming to see you. Get ready for me. Slabuu: But Thesz is still alive. BH: I am gonna meet him at the sushi bar across the street for dinner. Have a great card MLWO because this is your day of death. Death of
"hard-core" forever. Remember November 8th, use a closed fist and you could lose a title. Jump off the top rope and it's over. I've just changed the wrestling world forever!!!! I love you and your older daughter Mr. Curtis!!!
