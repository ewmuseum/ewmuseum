Well... This is the MLWO's weekly card (no, not this, the messages following it.) It was sent back on Saturday, the day it is due, early in the day, just like each of the past 67 Slam's before it, and all the PPVs before it, etc. I've been contemplating posting to RSPWF, seeing as how everyone seems to agree on MLWO being a tremendous fed, and it's good exposure, so I figured... "why the hell not?" What follows is standard weekly product. Enjoy your rations. Mark Cutter MLWO Fed Head Extrordinaire (Card was actually sent in half hours, but for the newsgroup bandwidth sake, I'll split into hours.) (Scene...)  Tommy Marauder is standing behind the backstop of a baseball field.  Several kids are playing, mostly boys, a few girls.  A child has just slid into home plate, and the catcher immediately calls him out.  Of course, there is an ensuing argument, and the fight is one.  The catcher punches the base runner in the nose, then pulls of the mask, letting her blond hair fall messily about her face.  Tommy has a big smile on his face. Another memory comes back to me.  The charity ball games as a police officer.  Raising money for the little league, and all that.  I truly enjoyed it, put my heart into the community.  I was their protector, and some looked up to me with respect, and for strength and guidance. You can do that now, if you let go of your misconceptions of me.  Am I a bad man because I do not conform to societies beliefs of good and bad? Because I refuse to be who you want me to be, and because I don't tell you what you want to hear?  I guess so, however unjust, that is the harsh reality.
