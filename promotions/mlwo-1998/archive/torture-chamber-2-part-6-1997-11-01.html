[MLWO Torture Chamber 2] 26 Oct. 97 - Six

1 November 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[THE SCENE: The inside of a penitentiary. Standing behind bars is the
Dark Destroyer. Behind him is a single cot with no sheets and a small, thin pillow. He's wearing a black prison jumpsuit, black weight lifting gloves, and his customary face paint. Around his neck is a chain necklace.
His hair dangles over his face as usual. He drifts his attention toward the camera and smiles fiendishly.]
DARK DESTROYER - Welcome to Death Row, where _nothing_ is "4 Life". We measure by eternity in here. What exactly _is_ Death Row? This isn't some sort of persecution we were sentenced to. This is where those of us who aren't civilized enough for your society come to _belong_. This is where the cannibals line up for _cuisines_. It's where the mentally unbalanced can stand on level ground. It's no circus, and there's no space for a ringmaster or a grizzly bear to be the baddest thang ridin' a tricycle in loops! Or maybe, Gunnar Gaines, you could pass as the bearded lady. [grin] But then people might accuse you of infringing on
Cheryl's look. It should be painfully visible by now that I don't like you, Gunnar. As a brother I love you. As an opponent, as a competitor against my right to the EWA World Title, I can't tolerate you, and I _won't_! I heard you talking about last year's conflict. The one that I "dropped out of"?
Well, the last you time you sat your fat ass down on the toilet your brain must have dropped out of that Grand Canyon sized crack, because I
_beat
you_ in your own Alaskan Death Match! I beat you in another of your favorites, the Coal Miner's Glove Match, and I _knocked you out_ to do it!
I beat you until you couldn't continue in the Barbed Wire-Baseball Bat
Match! It took the combined forces of you and two others to finally wrangle that Extremist title from my hands! And what did I do after that?
I became the EWA World Heavyweight Champion for the _fourth_ time! So take your revisionist history, stick it up your ass, and set fire to it!
[He backpeddles and sits down on the cot.] DARK DESTROYER - You can believe what you like about the past. Get a good look at your number one ranked plaque from the Top 200 and reinforce your belief that you were chosen number one because you deserved it. I'll tell you _exactly_ why you were awarded that slot! Because _I_ deserved it and no one could stand it! I'm quite obviously the most despised man in this industry and everyone can feel the vomit escalating in their throats when they realize that I'm every bit as supreme as I say I am! It even sickens
_you_! It's the only thing that you've ever swallowed that can open your eyes to a hangover the next morning!
I don't care about your damn contract. I don't care if it's full-proof, iron-clad, water resistant, squeezably Charmin or two-ply! I'm _still_ gonna wipe my ass with it unless my demands are settled! So crack your whip, Charlie Cheesecake! Twirl your mustache and pluck your pubic hairs for all I care! _You_ don't rattle a nerve in my body! I hope the same is evident to you, Pooh Bear. I may be a Gaines for life, but Death Row is for _eternity_, and the Gaines' family may not last that long. [sneer]
Bet your soul on it.
