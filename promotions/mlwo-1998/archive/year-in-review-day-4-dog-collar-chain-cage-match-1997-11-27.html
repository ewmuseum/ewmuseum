The ring announcer enters the ring as strains from the music bring the crowd into a frenzt, with "SPECTOR" signs and "HARRIS" signs competing for supremacy!) RA: The following contest, set for one fall, is a special DOG COLLAR CHAIN MATCH!! (HUGE POP!) And it is for the MLWO Lightweight Championship!!! (BIG POP!) Introducing first, coming to us from Carteret, New Jersey, weighing in at 224 pounds, in his first dog collar chain match since apparent DEATH! (HUGE POP!) Introducing... SSSSSTTTTTTTTTTEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEVVVVVVVVVEEEEEEEEEEENNNNNNNNNN "THE EEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEDDDDDDDDDDDDDDDGGGGGGGGEEEEEEEE" SSSSSSSSSPPPPEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEECCCCCCTTTTTOOOOOOOOORRRRRRRRRRR!!! (The crowd absolutely erupts as confetti falls from the ceiling of the arena, and at the entranceway, Spector, in all glory and with a dog-collan chain dragging behind him, makes his way down the aisle!
Fans lunge over the barrier in attempts to touch the superstar, and one person gives Spector a "The EDGE is the GREATEST!" poster, and
Spector holds it high to the fans, who erupt again! The fan gets his poster back, and Spector, surveying the steel cage, promptly starts climbing over it with dog-collar chain in hand!)
VS: Have you ever seen anything quite like this? We are about to see a man who we all thought _DIED_ in this kind of match wrestle in the
VERY SAME match against the Lightweight Champion and master sneak
Matt Harris! This is unreal!
DS: Leave it to the MLWO to bring us something no one else could ever dream of! Spector and Harris! You gotta love it! Spector looks absolutely RIPPED! he doesn't seemed fazed at all by the cage or the collar! My god, it almost looks like a dominatrix party, if you added a few females and...
VS: We get your point, Derrick! Spector is in the ring just waiting for Harris... ("Control" by Juno Reactor starts to pump from the loudspeakers, causing the hardcore fans in the crowd to go absolutely nuts...)
VS: And he's about to GET Harris, as well! Listen to these fans! They want this macth BADLY! DS: Jesus, they want it worse than sex! Only in the MLWO! (Before the announcer has a chance to make the intro, out comes Sydney, wearing a Steven Spector t-shirt! The crowd begins to slowly rise in volume as behind Sydney, wearing a noose pulled by Sydney, is
Matt Harris, in all his glory. Harris points a baseball bat right at
Spector, and then points it at himself, repeating the gesture over and over again as the crowd is going ballistic!)
VS: You think Harris has a plan for this match? DS: Any time Harris brings a noose down to the ring, it can only mean one thing... get your orders for pints of blood now, folks. They aren't going to last for long!
(The announcer, standing outside the cage, begins...) RA: And his opponent! From New Youk City, he weighed in at a powerful
250 pounds. He is the CURRENT MLWO Lightweight Champion! Accompanied by the LOVELY Sydney Smythe....
"HHHAAAAAAAAAARRRRDDDDDDDDDDDDDDDDDDDDDDCCCCCOOOOOOOOOOORRRRREEEEE!!!" MMMMMMMAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAATTTTTTTTTTTTTTTTTTTTTTT
HHHAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAARRRRRRRRIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIISSSSSSSSSSSSSSS!!!!!
(Fireworks go off in the rafters as Harris, noose and all, walks up to the cage! He points at Spector, saying "YOUR ASS IS MINE!", and immediately after Sydney takes the noose off, bulls through the cage door...)
VS: OH MY! HARRIS COULDN'T WAIT ANY LONGER! DS: Right hand, left hand, kick to the gut, UPPERCUT! The refs are trying to get the other end of that dog collar on Harris, and HE
THROWS THEM THROUGH THE DOOR!!! Harris picks up the dazed Spector,
REVERSE DDT! And now Harris is going to put the dog collar on
HIMSELF! LISTEN TO THE CROWD! VS: The choker is on Harris now, and SPECTOR SURPRISED HIM WITH A KIPUP LARIAT!!! Harris is dazed, and look at Spector trash talk him... OH!!! DS: A lugee just got Harris right between the eyes, and WE'VE GOT A
BRAWL IN THE CENTER OF THE CAGE AS HARRIS JUST LIFTED SPECTOR RIGHT
OFF HIS FEET! VS: Harris is pounding away on Spector as much as he can, and now Spector shifts his weight and HE STARTS POUNDING! My god, these two men seem to want to annialate each other!
DS: It's a dog collar age match, Vinnie. That's a pretty good indication of that, don't you think?
VS: Spector brought Harris to his feet, KNIFE-EDGE CHOP WITH THE CHAIN! HARRIS IS BUSTED WIDE OPEN! Spector sees this, and leapfrogs over Harris, and runs the other way! OH MY! The chain reached its full length and snapped Harris's neck! That looked terrible!
DS: That absolutely RULED! Harris is in a heap on the mat, and
Spector has CLIMBED THE CAGE!
VS: But Harris still has his wits, and quickly gets up... HE'S CLIMBING THE CAGE AS WELL! Harris is behind Spector and he tries to smash an elbow back into Harris... GERMAN SUPLEX OFF THE CAGE TO THE
CENTER OF THE RING!!! 1!...... 2!...... KICKOUT! Harris just pulled out a TREMENDOUS MOVE for a two! Spector is fighting back to his feet, Harris... kick to the gut... RELEASE
POWERBOMB!!! DS: Spector is now feeling the full effects of the ruthlessness possessed by Matt Harris! Harris brings him to his feet, scopps him up into a slam position... HOLY SHIT, HE FLUNG HIM LIKE A DART INTO
THE CAGE WALL!! BOTH EN ARE BLEEDING NOW! VS: Spector is in a heap besides the cage wall, and Harris stomps away... He points to Sydney... THE BAT IS IN THE RING! IT'S HOME RUN DERBY TIME!!! DS: Harris is winding up as these fans are ON THEIR FEET! hey want to see something happen here, as Spector is slowly rising... Harris winds up, and [CLANG!!!] SPECTOR MOVED!!! HARRIS WRUNG THE BAT ON THE
CAGE! THE BAT IS LYING IN THE RING! VS: Harris is trying to get it back... Spector kicked it away from him! Harris with a right hand BLOCKED! Spector... right hand, left hand, BACK EDGE CHOP! Harris is dazed... uppercut! Spector off the ropes... BULLDOG!!! HARRIS IS IN TROUBLE!
DS: Spector walks up the cage wall again! Harris is still down in the ring... MOONSALUT OFF OF THE CAGE!!!
1!...... 2!...... SHOULDER UP! How in the world was Harris able to roll a shoulder??? Spector thought he was the new Lightweight champion right there, and he's rolling the dog-collar chain in his hand!!! VS: Harris is getting slowly to his feet, and Spector waits... RIGHT HAND! THE CHAIN SENDS HARRIS DOWN HARD! HE'S BLEEDING PROFUSELY!!! Spector jumps on him... REPEATED RIGHT HANDS! Harris can't withstand this onslaught... He's spouting like a faucet now, and HE KICKED SPECTOR RIGHT IN THE BALLS!!! DS: That stopped Spector right in his tracks! He is kneeling down in INTENSE PAIN! Harris tries to stand, but he wobbles and falls again from all the blood he's losing! Spector is shaking off his manly hurt, and he approaches Harris... OH MY GOD!!! VS: HARRIS GOT THE BAT! HARRIS GOT THE BAT! SPECTOR WAS JUST CRUSHED!!! HARRIS HAS TATTOED SPECTOR WITH THAT BAT, AND HE'S NOT GETTING UP!!! DS: Harris smirks, then covers... this has to be the end... 1!...... 2!...... HARRIS PULLED HIM UP! Is he NUTS??? He has this match won! Spector is out cold after that
LETHAL shot to the head from the bat! What does Harris have in mind... Sydney is up on the ring, holding a chair! SHE'S HOLDING IT
OVER THE CAGE!!! VS: This doesn't look good for Spector! Harris has dragged him up by the hair, and Sydney is motioning Harris on! Harris with the whip across... WHAT THE FUCK???
DS: HE REVERSED IT! HE REVERSED IT! SPECTOR JUST SENT HARRIS HURDLING
INTO THE CHAIR! SYDNEY GOES FLYING! HARRIS IS OUT! SPECTOR ISN'T DEAD
JUST YET!!! VS: Spector is still dazed though! He is on his back trying to breathe, while blood comtinues to pour off of his beaten face! Harris is now busted wide as well, and the mat looks like a Civil War battleground! SPECTOR IS ROLLING TOWARDS HARRIS!
DS: He's agonizing over it, but he's trying to cover the Lightweight
Champion! Harris is STILL NOT MOVING! Sydney is just recovering from her trip to the concrete... SPECTOR IS ALMOST THERE!!!
VS: Not a dog-collar could keep Spector down! Not a chain could keep Spector down! Not even an aluminum bat could keep Spector down, as HE COVERS HARRIS!!! 1!...... 2!...... KICKOUT! KICKOUT! KICKOUT! HOLY SHIT!!! DS: Harris isn't human! He can't be! But he's still alive! Can you believe this? Both men are still alive in this match, but NEITHER can even stand right now! Wait a minute... Harris rolls over, and now covers Spector!
1!...... 2!...... KICKOUT! How much longer can these two men go? Both of them are busted wide! Both of them are exhausted beyond belief! But NEITHER will surrender here tonight! NEITHER MAN HAS ANYTHING LEFT!
[Mist is coming up from under the ring. The camera gets blurry and then it suddenly focuses up to the ceiling. Standing on top of the rafter is N. He is wearing his long brown cloak, green mask and black body suit. He has a black bungie cord. He latches his hand into the cord and drops straights down into the ring, he unlatches the cord and runs straight Harris and Spector. He lariats both of them and then lifts the dog collars up, while lifting Spector and Harris up at the same time. He latches the dog collars on both their necks and whips them both into the ropes. Harris comes first, meeting the boot on N, head on. Spector comes next, getting back dropped hard into the mat.
N grabs Spector by the head and tosses him over the top rope. He lifts Harris up and places him on the top rope, creating a choke and pull between Spector and Harris. N climbs the ropes…spins Harris around and DDTs him onto the cement floor making Spector to spontaneously crash hard into the floor, bleeding from the mouth and unmoving as is Harris. N stops Spector in the head and rolls into the ring, grabbing a microphone and a chair as he goes. He plants the chair in the middle of the ring and sits down.]
N: This won't take long…I just want to sit here for a second and look at my handiwork. So..You know who I am Steven? GOOD!!!! Yet, you feeble minded dead man, you know not what my grievance is? Too bad, because if your brain just clocked a little you could possibly end this thing easily, but no. I have decided I don't want you to ever figure it out. I want to send you back to grave and spit on your burial site and piss on your tombstone, laughing. Once you are completely gone, I can become myself once again. Not until then, but know this…this won't end for a long time and I give you greetings…
WELCOME TO HELL……AGAIN!!!!!!
[N lifts the chair, tosses it onto Spector and leaps down smashing the microphone on the chair and over Spector and leaves the area, laughing.]
DS: Both men are...out. HERE...N HAS BEEN CUT OFF BY STEVEN SPECTOR...wait...did I just? VS: THERE IS ANOTHER STEVE SPECTOR! STEVE SPECTOR NUMBER TWO IS ATTACKING N! THEY ARE FIGHTING AMONGST THEMSELVES! BOTH OTHER MEN ARE STILL OUT IN THE RING! DS: They're starting to stir though...and N has basically kept a clean ending from possibly happening.
VS: Spector is the first to his feet, and HE IS GOING TO END THE MATCH!!! HE'S CLIMBING THE CAGE, DOG COLLAR, CHAIN AND ALL! IT'S TIME FOR HARRIS TO KISS HIS BELT GOODBYE!!! DS: You may be right as... WAIT A MINUTE!!! VS: SYDNEY! HOLY SHIT! WHAT THE HELL IS SHE DOING??? DS: The crowd is roaring... SYDNEY PUT THE NOOSE AROUND SPECTOR'S
HEAD!!! Sydney is literally PULLING on Spector, trying to choke him out! Spector is simply too... OH MY GOD, SOMEBODY HELP HIM!!!
VS: HARRIS IS PULLING ON THE DOG COLLAR, AND WITH THE COLLAR AND THE NOOSE FROM DIFFERENT DIRECTIONS, SPECTOR IS BEING CHOKED OUT OVER TOP
OF THE RING! HIS FEET ARE OFF THE GROUND! THIS IS HORRIBLE! NEITHER
SYDNEY OR HARRIS WILL RELIEVE THE PRESSURE! DS: Spector has gone limp! He may not even be breathing! Sydney has let go of the noose and has pulled it back outside! This crowd is in shock... indeed, the REF is in SHOCK! But Harris pulls him back up... HARDCORE DRIVER!
1!...... 2!...... 3!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! (DING DING DING!) VS: SPECTOR HAS BEEN CHOKED OUT, AND HARRIS RETAINS THE CHAMPIONSHIP! WHAT A WILD MATCH! STEVE SPECTOR MAY NEVER RECOVER! DS: What else can happen tonight? Absolutely incredible! VS: Harris has a mic... [Matt grabs a microphone handed to him by Syd through the cage. He turns and looks at Spector for a moment, then walks over. He falls to his knees beside the fallen Spector.]
Matt: I really don't know what to say to you...you stupid, stubborn man. You stubborn, self-righteous, I-refuse-to-face-the-facts man.
You came here tonight and just thought wrong. this fight didn't have to happen. You beat yourself when you signed the contract.
[Matt rolls his eyes.] Matt: You asked for it. I sat around and told you I could beat you...I said that a long time ago, Steven...but you asked for this.
Every punch, kick...every ounce of pain I just inflicted on you was at your own request... [Matt points down at him.] Matt: Maybe, in some alternate universe you neved "died." Maybe you were never hung and injected with drugs...and that way there this whole scene could have been avoided, because in that world...Steven
Spector isn't a fool. You were a fool when you accepted that match with Wrath, and you were a fool to accept this match with me.
[Matt gets to his feet and walks to a corner.] Matt: Sure, try to BS me with this talk of new energy coarsing through you...you still went down. Your new energy didn't do a god damn thing to Matt Harris. You're still slow, you're still old, and you still turned your back on all these people...I say again...you deserved this...you asked for this.
[Matt turns back again, walks over and gets right in Spector's face.] Matt: You're lucky there's still breath in your lungs, you're lucky you'll live another day...you're lucky I'm not the bad guy here,
Steven. Because if I were Wrath, you wouldn't be lying here...you wouldn't be all comfy...you'd be strung up...again. Maybe this beating you just took is what we call in this business "a wake-up call." Hang it up, Steve. As I was once a respected colleague of yours, I advise you to go home and never come back...before you really get killed.
[Matt gets up and takes one final look at Spector.] Matt: Told you so...you're a name on a list. End of sermon. [Matt tosses the microphone down, unlocks his collar, and starts to climb over the cage to the floor.]
VS: Oh...my...god. DS: I'm shocked. VS: Well Derrick, we do have to move on however, so we'll see you later. DS: So long. ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ Winner: Matt Harris ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
