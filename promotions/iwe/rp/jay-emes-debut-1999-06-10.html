Jay Emes Debut Roleplay

10 June 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Scene fades into a mild late afternoon in the outskirts of the Windy City, Chicago, Illinois. The sun is still out, but it is slowly fading into the horizon, causing bright red, orange, purple and blue colors to hover over the city. Down on a small street, "TicketMaster" is open for business. An extremely long line starts at the front door and continues down the entire street where people suddenly disappear behind a brick wall. This line is filled with "America's Youth" wearing obscene clothing and body piercings in places most people don't even have places. As the camera slowly glides by, the young teenagers start to goof off and flash their middle fingers.] [Without warning, a middle aged man steps into the scene. This man stands out in this sea of youth as he is wearing a blue blazer with a white dress shirt hiding underneath. His thinning brown hair is combed over the side of his exposing dome in an attempt to make people believe he in fact has a full head of hair. In this man's hand is a microphone. He walks over to a slightly overweight young female wearing a black Marilyn Manson T-shirt and wearing green lipstick.] Man: Welcome everyone. I'm John Kosac and I am here in Chicago, Illinois, on behalf of the IWE. Today I wish to talk with some of the IWE fans waiting here in line to purchase tickets for the July 12th edition of Explosion here in Chicago, Illinois. This is the first event since "Survival of the Fittest" and it will take place in The IWE Coliseum. Anyway, I am about to get a few words from these youngsters. First, we have . . . [John Kosac holds the microphone up to the young woman's mouth, who seems excited to be on television.] Young woman: Veronica. JK: Ah, Veronica. So, are you a big IWE fan? Veronica: Yup. Ever since it started, I was a huge fan! [Kosac smiles.] JK: Well, Veronica, I have a question for you. What do you think of this new fellow who has just arrived on the scene, Jay Emes? Veronica: "So Damn Cool"?! Why, I think he's great! I love his cocky attitude. He's one of those athletes that knows what he can do and flaunts it. And well he should. JK: So, are you looking forward to his debut in the IWE and pro wrestling? Veronica: S'why I'm buyin' my ticket! [Veronica giggles and walks ahead in line.] JK: Well, next in line is . . . [Kosac holds the microphone up to a short, lanky kid. He has greasy black hair and a nose ring with a chain that goes up to his ear.] Boy: Yo, s'up? I'm Mike! Yeah! IWE! JK: Alrighty then, Mike. Same question. What do you think of Jay Emes? [Upon asking the question, Kosac holds the microphone up to Mike's mouth, but he hesitates as he looks into the camera with a blank expression on his face.] Mike: Oh, sorry dude. Emes? Well, I don't know the guy and I've never seen him wrestle, but if he's as good as he says he is, then I can't wait to see him. Plus, anyone is better than General America! That guy's a fag! He should go back to the 80's or where ever the hell he came from! [A group of kids begin to cheer and jump in an attempt to be seen on camera.] JK: Thank you very much! Now ba-- [A deep voice suddenly interrupts. This voice comes from behind the camera. As the camera shifts and turns around, none other than Jay Emes himself is standing on the sidewalk, with that haughty grin on his face. The fans waiting in line suddenly break out in cheers, even though they've never seen him wrestle or really know who he is. All they know is that he was on TV and is more known than they are. Emes smiles as his ego is boosted just a little more.] [Emes is caucasian with slightly tanned skin. He has a well built, well conditioned physique and has an athletic muscular body. He has long, chestnut brown hair that is tied up in a ponytail. He also has dark, brown eyes. On his left shoulder, he has a tattoo of a topless woman bending over. He is wearing a black "N.E.W." T-shirt with cut off sleeves and black Nike shorts.] ["So Damn Cool" walks towards John Kosac and grabs the microphone out of his hands.] JE: Ha Ha Ha! What a damn trip! Now, this is the Windy f'n City! [Line crowd pop.] JE: I'm sure that the anticipation is at an all time high here in Chi-Town. Why? You ask. 'Cause the greatest thing to hit wrestling is about to make his debut, that's why! Now, for those of you who don't know, I'm Jay Emes . . . "So Damn Cool" Jay Emes! And I'm here in the IWE for one reason and one reason only, warm-ups. That's right. I'm here to prepare for my big debut in the National Extreme Wrestling. Now, don't get me wrong, IWE is great, but the ink is already dry on my N.E.W. contract. I know that's upsetting since you IWE fans only get a taste of Emes! [The infamous, arrogant grin cracks once again.] JE: Before I go off yappin' about how great I am, which I am, let's talk about my first opponent here in the IWE and professional wrestling itself, General America. Heh, those were the good ol' days, weren't they? The days when little kids took their vitamins, not their virginity's. And the days that teens stood up and saluted the flag. Well, General, those times have changed. They have changed into this . . . [Emes waves his hand over the line of young teenagers waiting for their tickets.] JE: Oh, yes! This is America now, General. The villains you once defeated, the days you once saved . . . no more! The bad guys have overwhelmed you. You can no longer defeat the villains of today's world. Just as you cannot defeat me on July the 12th. Not only because I am the "Unbeatable" Jay Emes, but because I am the villain of this battle you will have to fight. And because of the fact that I am . . . [Points to himself with each word.] . . . SO . . . DAMN . . . COOL! [Emes chuckles.] JE: Is that enough for you, General America? Or do you need more reasons? Hell, I think Explosion will be enough proof. 'Cause after I show off my amazing talent and skill to you and the world, you will be forced into retirement, no no, make that a hospital. G.E. . . . keep singin' the "Star Spangle Banner", because that's _all_ you'll be able to do after I'm done with you. Why? Because I am, you guessed it . . . . . . So Damn Cool! [Fade.] --[name withheld]-- Handler of Jay Emes [N.E.W.] *Can be seen in IWE* [0-0-0] "Welcome to PHB, Pimpin' Hoes Baby!" "Oh, hey cops, welcome to PHB, Pasta House, Boy!"
