Real Staff =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= INTRODUCTION =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= The big arenas ... the big names ... the _new_ sheriff in town. The organization that has come out of nowhere to reach the pinnacle of excellence. Stellar action every Saturday night, stellar Pay Per Views. Stellar wrestlers. The ball is in our court, and we're gonna run with it until don't want to anymore. Welcome to DYNASTY. The true organization in which wrestlers _wrestle_, scrap their way towards the main prize ... the ICON Heavyweight Championship. If you acheive that goal here, you are a talented athlete, also not to mention, the top dog. Can you survive in this one of a kind promotion? Well step into our ring, and show us what you have. =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= ALL ABOUT DYNASTY =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= --[DYNASTY Brodcasts]-- DYNASTY _three_ main brodcasts. All are hosted by Jean Lamont and Trevor Hall, the two main commentators here in DYNASTY. They're chemistry on the microphone is fantastic, and you'll love to hear them each and every week.
LEGENDS OF WRESTLING: DYNASTY's main brodcast. Live, every Saturday night, on the UPN network. There are six-eight matches a week on this program, not to mention interviews with DYNASTY's top stars. Defended weekly is the DYNASTY Cruiserweight championship against a worthy contender. Also weekly, the North
American Championship is on the line. Rarely is the Icon Heavyweight
Championship & Icon Tag Team Championship.
DYNASTY RING REPORT: Every Wednesday, DYNASTY produces a clip show of the happenings of DYNASTY. News stories are broken, matches are announced for the upcoming card, etc.
One informative program. It also features two pre-recorded interviews from two top stars in DYNASTY. Be sure not to miss it!
UNDER THE MIC: If you wanna say something, you do it here at Under the Mic. Every Monday, Tuesday, Thursday, Friday, and Saturday prior to Legends of Wrestling, Under the Mic is on the air. So if you wanna run your mouth, do it here. --[DYNASTY Championships]-- Here at DYNASTY, we have three singles championships and one tag championship. They are the titles that makes what DYNASTY is today.Can you become a champion? Well it takes hard work and dedication to do so, and here they are ...
ICON HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPIONSHIP: The main prize. The big kahuna. The most presteigous prize in all of DYNASTY. Every wrestler over 227 pounds dreams to have this belt wrapped around their waist.The man who can walk away with this belt draped across their shoulder is not just a champion, but and _ICON_.
NORTH-AMERICAN CHAMPIONSHIP: Second best is not bad. Basically this belt is defended weekly. Sometimes called "the endurance" title. Can you hold on to this belt for a long period time? Maybe, maybe not.
CRUISERWEIGHT CHAMPIONSHIP: Are you 227 pounds and under? Do you want to have the chance of being the _top_ cruiserweight in all of wrestling? Sure you do. Unlike other promotions, we give the cruiserweights the spotlight, they get the respect they deserve, and most of the time, not on the undercard.
Do you have the talent to be a top cruiserweight? Then show your skills in DYNASTY. ICON TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP: See Icon Heavyweight Championship. --[DYNASTY Pay Per Views]-- No, we don't have monthly pay per views, even though ... DYNASTY has the budget to afford it. DYNASTY puts on bi-monthly pay-per-views so that the card itself has more build up, more action, and more anticipation, and we don't want _you_ the viewer to shell out $30 bucks a month for crap.
DYNASTY Pay Per Views are our special cards. Every title on the line,feuds end, feuds begin, special apprarances, and suprises.
=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= MEET THE FACES =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= --[Terrance Goldsworthy]-- The kingpin. The man who makes DYNASTY what it is. The esteemed owner. Young, knows the wrestling and money business as well as anyoneout there. Makes sure that weekly,
DYNASTY puts on a decent product, and all the talent signings, press releases, and the public relations happenings include him.
If somethings so out of line that the commissioner can't handle, he steps in and takes charge.
--[Commissioner Albert Vasquez]-- The man who decides on what matches takes place, how vacant titles are decided, how suspensions and finings goes, the lawmaker in DYNASTY.Don't follow his orders, you're suspended.
Can become a hated man sometimes, and sometimes, he can be a nice person. The real arm of the law here in DYNASTY.
--[Trevor Hall]-- The main voice of DYNASTY. The man who calls the action here in DYNASTY. He's liked, one of the nicest commentators out there. Some say he's the best play by play man in the business.
--[Jean Lamont]-- From Montreal, Quebec, Canada, he's a real asshole. The guy who would laugh when an old lady falls down a flight of stairs.
Has _no_ common decency whatsoever. Rarely positive, and is hated by 75 % of the fans in the arena and television.
--[Wayne Vengeance]-- DYNASTY correspondant. Always backstage trying to find the scoop. Conducts most of the backstage interviews and reporting everything that's going on behind the scenes.
--[Paul Martin]-- See Wayne Vengeance, but he has a lower paycheck. =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= DYNASTY APPLICATION(s) =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= --[Singles Application]-- NOTE - Please include a sample roleplay along with your application.
