Poolside

Here is the last RP I did for DYNASTY. It was much better than the first, but if any of you have tips, or if there is anything you'd like to say, please do.
Thanx. [...Fades in...] [Scene] [...The scene open and you have a pool side view of a large mansion. It is about mid day, and the sun sparkles off the water in the pool.
There are large bushes the surround the yard, some are cut in certain shapes. Like a dollar symbol, and other such things. At the base of the pool lies a man wearing long silver sorts, and a pair of silver sunglasses...]
[...He is stretched out in a long, lounge chair, with a small table beside him, in which has a small glass of wine on it. He slowly sits up, and it is now clear that the man is "Sweet as Silver" Midas Runt. Runt grabs the drink and takes a sip, and return it softy back on the table. He gives a smile, as he sits back in the chair, and begins to speak...]
[SSMR] "Fury decides to speak, huh? He seemed almost as confident as myself. And that, in it's own way, is a rather good thing. Now you say Terrance Goldsworthy knows this, and Terrance Goldsworthy knows that. And you seem to think you can walk right in here, and take everything this great organization has to offer. But see, you have done, and seen nothing yet. You may think you are the greatest thing that ever happened, but the truth is your not. _I_ am the greatest thing that ever happened!"
[...Chuckles...] [SSMR] "But even I, the great Midas Runt know, you can't just walk in and expect everything to be handed to you. You must word for it, and prove yourself. Now, you say that I am nothing more than garbage, and you should be wrestling better. But let me inform you, that I am not garbage, and as a matter of fact, I am the farthest thing from it. Would garbage have all of this?"
[...Runt raises his arms, as if to show off his mansion, pool, cars and everything else that can be seen. He then flexes his muscle, and proceeds to kiss it...]
[SSMR] "And I am sure, it doesn't have arms, and the body, quite like this. And let me make you aware of this as well. I may be going into the ring with a
_superstar,_ which is fine with me. But you are going into the ring with an
_ICON,_ a _legend,_ a _god._ And you will be the one looking up to the ceiling, wondering what went wrong, and how you could have lost. As I leave, with my hand raised, and one step closer to the North American title."
[...Pauses...] [SSMR] "Fury, a word of advise. Don't count your pay checks, before they are cashed. 'Cause there is always someone out there to screw ya'. And you are about to get your taxes taken out."
[...A wink...] [SSMR] "And you can take that, to the bank!" [Scene] [...Runt leans up, and takes another sip of wine, then lays back down. He adjusts the way he is laying, until he find a comfortable position. Then all is silent...]
