DYNASTY (1999) — Roleplays

Beachside

8 April 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Here is the first Net RP I have done in over 5-6 months. So it is not all that great, but pretty good. I know I made a lot of mistakes, so if you guys would all help me out, I would greatly appreciate it.
[...Fades in...] [Scene] [...The scene opens up on a beach, in down state California. It's a nice, hot Thursday afternoon, and there are very few people at the beach. A few people walking there dogs down by the water, and a few women in the water. As the camera pans right, you see two people laying down on towels. And a cat, laying on a silver pillow. You see that it is "Sweet as Silver" Midas Runt and Gold. Runt is wearing silver shorts that go down to his knees, and silver "Oakleys." Gold is wearing a gold two piece bathing suit, and black sunglasses...]
[...Midas Runt sits up, and begins to pet his cat, Princess. She starts to pur, as Runt turns his head to the camera, then back out to the sea.
You hear some seagulls fly over head, as a crisp wind blows in from the
North. Runt then begins to speak...]
[SSMR] "What a nice day. So perfect. The sun will soon be setting over the water, everything is quiet in peaceful. Just like I like it. And what less than this, for a man like myself? Although, I have the times where I like it loud, and fun. Because, just like everyone else in this world, I have my wild side. That only few have seen. [...pauses...] Yet..."
[...A Smirk...] [SSMR] "But soon, you will all see my wild side. You will see what a calm, cocky, rich boy can do. It begins in San Antonio, Texas. On
DYNASTY's, Legends of Wrestling. My first match here, but one I am sure few will soon forget."
[...Smile...] [SSMR] "And the first man I have to go threw, 'The Diabolical Bastard' Rip
Fury. [...Chuckles...] You almost frighten me Fury.
See, it is men like you that are a disgrace to men, like me!" [Scene] [...He stops petting his cat, Princess, who is still purring. And very loudly now. Gold then rolls over onto her stomach, and you see her backside, which seems to perfect to be true. As if it were carved from stone. Runt smiles, and continues talking...]
[SSMR] "Don't you all wish you had, what I have? [...Flexes his muscle...] [SSMR] "I know you do. [...sarcastic laugh...] But you can't. But that's the way the cookie crumbles. Some people have it, others don't. Perhaps one day I will meet a man that can live up to me. No, no, wait. Maybe I won't, cause no one can! So Fury, try as you might, nothing is going to come of this for you. Because this man, is going to take you out bit by bit, and piece by piece.
[...A big smile...] [SSMR] "And that, you can take to the bank!" [Scene] [...The sun is now setting out over the water, and it causes a nice yellow glow. You hear some more birds over hear, and the low hum of a plane that flied high above the earth. Runt lays back down, with the smile still on his face...]
[...Fades out...]
