An all-women's Usenet e-fed's June-July 1996 relaunch as the ladies' division of a larger federation, whose three-part exhibition tournament from that federation's own pay-per-view survives complete. Written by AI, 2026

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The Ladies EXTREME Wrestling Association opened 1 December 1995 as an all-women's e-wrestling promotion. Its surviving record picks up again the following summer, after a gap its own broadcast blames on a dead computer and a cancelled account: “After about a BILLION problems getting this thing going, including the DEATH of my last computer… I've managed to get this guy out.”

“The LEWA is Back,” dated 25 June 1996, staged that return in-fiction from “a packed auditorium in the Eastern most point of Texas,” called by LEWA president Rip Slaughter alongside commentators Chris Austin and Chad, and billed as the promotion's re-launch as the ladies' division of DPW. Its headline pairing brought back two former LEWA champions, Kitty Pryde and the Babe, who won the opening tag match; the card also introduced the LEWA Extremist Championship, vacated and put up in a special death match whose opponent was drawn at random from every wrestler on the roster, won by Killer Kiana over Lady Die.

A month later, on 24 July 1996, LEWA ran the three-round Russel Mar Memorial Cup, a round-robin exhibition tournament staged inside a larger federation's own pay-per-view fan festival. Georgia “The Dukette” Bryce beat Princess P. in a steel-cage opener; Cameo, accompanied to the ring by Mr. Mi, beat Kitten X. A bunkhouse round followed, Cameo besting Bryce with the help of a second wrestler smuggled to ringside inside a body bag, and Kitten X beating Princess P. In the final, with LEWA's own regular official unavailable, president Rip Slaughter refereed the match himself as Cameo defeated Kitten X to become the tournament's first champion.

What became of the promotion after that isn't in its own surviving record.
