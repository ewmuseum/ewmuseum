LEWA — Card

"LEWA/DPW Russel Mar Memorial Cup, Part A"

24 July 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Rip: This is the very first match in the Russel Mar Memorial Cup, a round robin tourney that will crown our NEW RMMC Champion!
Chris: And what a match it will be! Pitting two exciting newcomers to the LEWA in a steel cage, to see who has the heart, the strength...and the insanity neccesary to be the very FIRST RMMC champion!
Rip: Only time will tell which of these four women will be able to win this matchup...but I can tell you one thing, it'll be well worth the ride. Anyways...without further ado, the steel cage is up...so LET'S GET READY to
Rumble! Chad: This match is scheduled for one fall, making her way down the aisle she is one of the hottest newcomers in the LEWA, Georgia "The Dukette" Bryce!
Georgia makes her way down to ringside, cool, calm and composed, and quickly steps into the ring.
Chad: And her opponent, making her debut in the LEWA. Very little is known about this woman...but the
LEWA is certainly excited to have her amongst it's ranks. Princess P.! Chris: Reportedly the P is for Pain...personally I think that remains to be seen, but who knows?
Rip: She'll also struggle with wrestling as a singles wrestler in this match, as normally she teams with another wrestler to form one of the more devestating new teams in the LEWA, but tonight she's flying solo. Only time will tell if she's up to the challenge.
Rip: They're in the cage, and The Dukette's immediately on the offensive, Charging in with a clothesline, but Princess ducks, throwing Georgia face first into the cage.
Chris: Kicking Georgia in the gut, Princess sets her up for the piledriver, only to find herself on her back after a back body drop from Georgia.
Rip: Locking in a headlock now, and Georgia has Princess on the mat. Adding additional pressure Georgia continues to tighten her biceps, increasing the pain greatly on Princess's pressure points. Chris: But Princess is already up, and throwing an elbow into the gut of Georgia. Whipping her into the ropes, and side stepping her, Princess grabs Georgia by the hair, tossing her body into the cage.
Rip: Princess has her from behind, and sends Georgia to the mat with a dragon suplex. The official's in position. 1...2...no.
Chris: Georgia rolls back, and is up. Blocking a kick from Princess and throwing her to the ground.
Georgia turns, and immediately locks Georgia into her pattented figure four backbreaker! Rip: Princess is refusing to...she submitted. Chad: Your winner, via submission, Georgia "The Dukette" Bryce! Rip: Tough luck for Princess...but now she's off to the losers bracket. She'll face the loser of the Kitten X/Cameo match for the right to fight for the title.
Chad: The second match of the RMMC is scheduled for one fall. Making her way down the aisle, and accompanied to the ring by the infamous Mr. Mi, she is one of the most mysterious women in this sport's great history. CAMEO!
Rip: Of all the wrestlers in this match Cameo by far has the edge on experience, but who knows...anything's possible in the LEWA!
Chad: And her opponent, making her debut in the LEWA she is one of the most exciting women in the sport today, we are glad to have her so let's extend a strong ovation to...KITTEN X!
Rip: They're in the ring and Cameo's on the offensive immediately, catching Kitten X in the corner with an avalanche, Cameo turns, whipping Kitten
X into
the corner. Chris: Kitten X comes off the ropes, and hits Cameo with a powerful clothesline sending her to the mat. Coming in Kitten nails her with an elbowdrop and makes the cover.
1...2..no. Kitten turns, grabbing Cameo around the neck in a tight headlock. Time passes and life goes on, finally... Rip: Cameo's starting to move! Up to her feet now, and whipping Kitten
X
into the ropes. Throwing a low dropkick at Kitten's knee, Cameo moves in, holding
Kitten's
legs in her arms,
and falls to the mat, throwing Kitten into the cage with a slingshot.
Chris: And Cameo wraps her up in a small package! 1...2...3! Our next round is set! Later in the card we'll have Georgia Bryce take on Cameo and Kitten X take on Princess P in the second round of the RMMC tourney.
