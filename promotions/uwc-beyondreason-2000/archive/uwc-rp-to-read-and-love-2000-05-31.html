UWC — Roleplay

RP to Read and Love

31 May 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Or hate, whatever... Here's the deal -- they're coming. We all know it. And we are all interested in them, despite our putting on of cool facades where we pretend not to care. Still, deep down we know we like it when our characters end up on the top 200...whether we're mature enough to not get worked up about which spot or not. (meaning we do get worked up) The RSPWF mid year 200 is coming, and I guess I might as well be the first person (in a while, anyway) to (re)open that pandora's box for yet another year of flame wars and "spam" accusations. So here we go. In intrest of pretty much my ego and my ego alone, I will officially begin my ~EVIL! campaign to brand the names of my characters on your brains so that when mid-years come around, you will be compelled beyond your will to vote for them. Or something. I suppose you could vote for them because you think they're worthy, and not just because I've imprinted them on your memory... So what am I looking for? Ass-kissing, basically. If you want to criticize constructively...fuck you. No wait! I mean, thank you. (-: I'm not going to say "Don't send me any apps for feds" because I know people are going to anyway, regardless of whether I tell them not to do it. Instead, I'll just say I don't have time for another fed right now, so go ahead and send me those apps...just don't be surprised when I don't fill them out. I just don't have the time. Don't call us, we'll call you. Here goes...one of my personal favorites from the Burt Clifton catalogue. I'd explain the character, but I've rambled enough as it is. Ask me aboot it, and I'll reply. Otherwise, enjoy the ride, and get used to this shit... ****************************** [Give me your poor, your tired, your huddled masses...HEY! HEY! I said give me them, I didn't say plunk them down on my front yard and have them camp out there for days like they were deadheads or something!] [But that's what we've got now, at 13876 Carlisle Street, Racine, Wisconsin. Out of left field...hell, out of the fucking bullpen, a new World Champion was recently crowned, and he happens to be an utter surprise. The resident of this house - well, one of them, anyway - is the new heavyweight champion of the world, and his name is Burt Clifton. And of course, whenever a local kid makes good, his hometown goes nuts and won't leave him alone for the world. On the lawn and spilling into the street, hundreds upon hundreds upon thousands upon thousands of Racinians are crowding around the home of their neighbor, their champion, the world's champion. You'd think he played quarterback for the Packers or something, the way they're going nuts out there, clapping and screaming and cheering and chanting his name...] [Inside the house, however, things could not be more different. There is but one person looking out her kitchen window, mad as hell and not willing to take anymore.] Momma: "What the hell is all this about? Burt...what the hell did you do?" [Clifton enters, dressed like it's 1989 (in other words, normally). He's looking kinda downcast for a guy who just did what most people can only dream of doing.] Clifton: "Huh?" Momma: "The whole city is camping out on my lawn, screaming your name!" Clifton: "What?" [Clifton opens the window so he can pop his head out to look, and sure enough, he sees the whole city camped out on Mom's lawn, chanting his name.] Momma: "What the hell did you do?" Clifton: "Nothing..." [He's shy and downward looking as he says this...typical "lie to your mother" behavior. Momma, being a mom, sees right through it more or less immediately.] Momma: "Don't you lie to your mother! Burtford Harrington Clifton, what have you done?" [Don't you hate it when your parents use your full name like that?] Clifton: "I...I...uh...you don't want to hear it. It's wrestling related. You never want me to talk about my career..." Momma: "Don't give me that crap, I never said anything like that! Don't play games, tell me!" [Fucking mothers...changing the rules as they go so they don't have to admit when they've contradicted themselves.] Clifton: "I...I...won. I won it all. I'm the world champion." Momma: "WHAT?" Clifton: "I'm the World Heavyweight Champion. In theory, the best wrestler in the world." [Momma is silent.] Clifton: "I ammounted to something, Mom! I'm really successful. I did good." Momma: "You became Heavyweight Champion of the World?" Clifton: [smiling, anticipating praise] "Yeah." Momma: "And that's why everybody's going nuts? Because it's a hero's welcome for my boy?" Clifton: [modesty slowly ebbing, as he senses his mother's praise and pride to show any moment now.] "Yeah, I guess." Momma: "Well what the hell did you go and do that for? Look at what you've done! I've got the whole sitting smooshing up my lawn and probably tramping all over my flower bed! Look! The chamber of commerce is crushing my dahlias!" Clifton: "But...it was an accident...I didn't mean to win..." Momma: "Well that hardly changes things, does it? Make them go away!" Clifton: "Okay." [Clifton goes outside in order to attempt to get the crowd to disperse. His plan, as you should have expected, backfires. The mob rushes him, forcing him to run back into the house and close the door behind him.] Momma: "Well, that was sure stupid, wasn't it? I'm going to call the cops and get them to break up this crowd." Clifton: "Shouldn't take long...the police force is out there, too." Momma: "Oh for the love of Lombardi!" [Momma calls the cops anyway.] Momma: "Yes, there's a crowd of people on my lawn and its disturbing me...well, you're the cops! Break it up! Oh, your units are already here? Well then it should be really easy to disperse them! Come on, hustle! Thank you!" [Outside, the cops reluctantly start breaking up the crowd. Reluctantly because Sgt. Harris was the one leading the Clifton chant. Momma and her son look on from the kitchen window.] Clifton: "Well, that's over, huh? We can all go back to normal lives now." Momma: "The hell we can! They'll be back tommorrow. They came back today, didn't they? They came back yesterday, didn't they?" Clifton: "What are we gonna do?" Momma: "What you're gonna do...is stop being champion." Clifton: "It's not that easy, mom..." Momma: "Shut up when I'm talking! I can't have a city on my lawn every morning just because they want to go all crazy over my boy because they think he's special or something!" Clifton: "What do you mean?" Momma: "What I mean is, there's no room for this in my house. You can't stay here anymore. Pack your stuff." [Clifton's jaw drops, and tries it's best to shatter itself on the floor. Doesn't quite make it.] Momma: "I'm serious! I'm not having a crowd out here every day! I need to have some privacy! You can't stay here if you're going to be drawing this kind of attention!" Clifton: "But mommy..." Momma: "DON'T BUT MOMMY ME, MISTER! Get packing! Now! So you can get in your car and get out of here before they come back again!" [Clifton slinks away, utterly disheartened, and sounds like he might be crying again. There's no arguing with momma, and he knows it. Dammit, it's not supposed to be this way. Winning the world championship is supposed to be a dream come true, and here it is getting Clifton kicked out of his house. As he sadly shuffles off to his room to pack up his stuff in preparation to leave the only place he's ever called home, we fade out to nothing in particular.] -- Bobby Who Handler of: Mike Masters [UWC, LVPWA], Burt Clifton [UWC], and one bland as hell RSPWF sig.
