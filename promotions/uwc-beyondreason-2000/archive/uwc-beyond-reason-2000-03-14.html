UWC Beyond Reason, March 14, 2000 Dalton Dome, Chicago, Illinois LAST WEEK: Mike Masters gets revenge in the worst way, and reveals a deceitful swerve. He’s back in action, and tonight, he’s getting back what he feels was robbed from him by the Outpatients, the World Tag Team Titles, now snugly on the waists of Super Cool. Will Masters gets what’s coming to him? Two of the All- Time Greatest Tag Teams in the UWC collide in the Main Event as Masters teams with reluctant World Champion Burt Clifton against Ray Hagan and Tommy Kain! Speaking of Super Cool, they won the World Tag Team Championship last week with a convincing win over the Nas and Lash Connection. Hagan went at it alone while Tommy Kain led the audience in a new group dancing record for the Guinness books. There was controversy, and now there will be a three-way dance for the #1 contendership of the tag team titles between the newly re-christened Nas and the Whip and the improbable team of Phantom and Kujo, otherwise known as SLAM EVIL! Tom Collins won Courtney Blackwell’s freedom from Axel Reed for a week, but unfortunately, it didn’t lead to a romantic reunion with her man, Burt Clifton.
