UWC community notices, 2000

10 February – 26 July 2000 (compiled)
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

10 February 2000 — "The UWC needs new bodies!"

Hello. I'm not going to bore you with saying that this fed is the best or anything. If anything, we probably have one of the worst looking web sites. We don't have the biggest roster. We don't have 3-D pictures of our wrestlers doing their finishing moves. I'll tell you what we have. We have a fantastic core of guys that know the drill, go with the flow, and create some of the most original and exciting rps around. We have a crew of guys running the show that knows that winning and losing may be fun, but being part of the bigger picture when it comes to angles is even better. And, we have the best damn cards on the Internet, with attention to detail, psychology, and most importantly of all, angles. Also, we do things the easiest way possible: Your rps are the sole determinant of the winners and losers. Of course, you can take my word for it, or you could see for yourself: http://members.xoom.com/UWC_online/ or you could see our last card: http://members.xoom.com/UWC_online/current.html And our cards are ALWAYS! on time. drop us a line and give us a chance. 19 April 2000 — "UWC is Number One and the Best!"

UWC is the best thing going hands down because: 1. Competitiveness - EVERYONE can win and everyone tries. Claims of favoritism or bias on the part of UWC is ludicrous, Kurt and the crew gives anyone heat that works for it. 2. Work Ethic - We are all different and have different writing styles, but UWC has less slackers than any fed I've ever been in. Guys like Nas who get ripped on, never give up, they just try harder. What fed heads are more dedicated than ours? They set the standard and we follow suit, kudos to everyone. 3. Damn Good Cards - What's wrestling without a solid storyline to base it upon? Everyone has an angle going on in some way or another and it's always been like that. We are not just a fed, but a huge dysfunctional family. -- Marcus Payne, 04.19.2000 Dead Ed ~blushing! we need new bodies to job. http://members.xoom.com/UWC_online/ * Sent from RemarQ http://www.remarq.com The Internet's Discussion Network * The fastest and easiest way to search and participate in Usenet - Free!
