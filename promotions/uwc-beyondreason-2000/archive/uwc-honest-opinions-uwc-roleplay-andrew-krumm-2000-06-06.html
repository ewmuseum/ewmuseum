"[Honest Opinions] UWC Roleplay: Andrew Krumm" — a newsgroup thread

6–7 June 2000

--- part 1 of 8: Jun 6, 2000, msg_id P193gaSf1nAJ ---
Okay guys, I'm about to drop my latest roleplay on here... Pay no mind to the bad spacing, cutting and pasting will do that sometimes. Thoughts, constructive criticisms, and all types of other crap will be read and appreciated. With that said.... OH! A little background. Andrew Krumm is leader of The Riot Squad, and is managed by his now wife, Nicole Masaki. Syko is his best friend, and tag team partner, when he can get booked. Lately Syko can't, thus the Save Our Syko campaign has begun. This week Krumm and Nastradamus (another member) are to face Overkill (Gabriel Ragnarok, a crazed viking freak of a man, and Marcus Payne, Michigan U.'s favorite son). _______ << A 3/4 view of dartboard on a wall. That's all we see. Deal with it. >> Off camera voice: "Ragnarok, Ragnarok, Ragnarok. You attempt to make a lot of valid points from what I've said. But alas, great viking..." << A dart flys erratically, missing the target COMPLETELY. At this point, we turn to see who's just thrown the dart away like that, most likely on purpose, and it's none other than the Hardest Worker in the UWC himself, Andrew Krumm. He's in a bar, which is empty, except for the barkeep, Syko, who's waiting for his turn on the darts, and Nicole, sitting on a nearby barstool, eating some steak fries. >> Andrew: "It appears you're a bit off." Nicole: "I hope that throw was on purpose, dear.." Andrew: "It was, Nicki... Because just like my skills in the ring..." << Another dart thrown. Bullseye. Dead center of it at that. >> Andrew: "I'm always on point." << Nicole has a good light giggle at that. >> Nicole: "Ragnarok really screwed up, didn't he?" Andrew: "He sure did. Maybe his viking helmet is 2 sizes too small this week. You brought the tape recorder I gather?" Nicole: "Shit, I left it back at the hotel. Looks like we'll be quoting by ear." Andrew: "Whatever. Let's start from the top, shall we?" << And awaaaaaaay we go. >> Andrew: "Well, lets see... Besides leading off with something that I stated a few days ago about little things that can be exploited in a competitor, you DARE insinuate that my mindset is the same as my partners? Do you think exactly the same as anyone else? If you do, let me be the first one to offer my condolences to the shell of a man you are. Everyone thinks for themselves Ragnarok. As far as getting in the ring, and beating the piss out of you, Nas and I are on the same page. We know what's gotta be done, and we'll do it. Don't believe me? Watch and see. Or rather, Watch and feel. Second... just because we have FANS, and people who CHEER OUR NAME, does not mean we're not focused. We don't step in the ring until Tuesday. If someone walks up to me on the street and says 'Hi!', then BY GOD, I'm gonna say hi back! See, unlike you, I'm generally a nice guy. I never fuck anyone over, I never give people a reason to hate me. That's just who I am, outside the ring. Inside the ring, in the heat of battle is another thing. Once i'm in between those ropes, and that bell is rung, my concern is one thing. The man standing a few feet in front of me, ready to rip my throat out. I train during the week, like the madman you claim to be. Ask Nicole. She's there, every step of the damn way with me, egging me on to do another rep, another mile, another lift, another 10 minutes in the ring... She pushes me as much as I push myself to be the BEST FUCKING WRESTLER THE UWC HAS EVER SEEN. I know the fall is long and hard, and when you hit rock bottom, there's nothing worse in the world. I've been there before. But I'm HERE NOW. I'm READY for the challenge you can claim to bring. And I'll be here, with you, for every punch, choke, chair, sword, or whatever you wanna throw at me. I know I talk big game, Ragnarok... But i'm MORE THAN CAPABLE of backing it up when it's time." Syko: "And lets not forget his little 'history lesson' about Jenn & Violet. I mean shit, we're saying someone, ME, is gonna be around to protect Nicole this week... and you go and point out the only reason Violet & Jenn got fucked up was because Fang and Briceland were busy watching each other's backs, instead of taking care of their loved ones... Jeez Raggy, if you'd have paid attention, I don't think you'd have made such a stupid oversight... Pay attention when we speak, Gabriel. You never know when our words will come around to bite you in the ass. You think I wouldn't stand a chance against you? I'm the psycho here, as much as you claim to be. Any Puerto Rican wouldn't back down from you, especially me. You think I'm easy pickins? Then you better lay off the Black Lotus, because it's impairing your thinking to the nth degree. Wake up, Ragnarok. Otherwise you'll get eaten alive by the Riot Squad." Nicole: "And there was the part where he still refers to me as bait. If he was listening to what was said, he'd know that I was already used as bait to get him to bring his attention to us, so that we could show the world the Ragnarok isn't the mean, green, killing machine everyone makes him out to be. He can be dealt with like anyone else on this planet. As far as we're concerned, with all these gaping holes in your VERBAL defense, IT'S NO WONDER we're showing the world how over-rated you are! We're as ready as ever to face Overkill. And that may just be too much for you to HANDLE." << The phone rings in the bar, as Krumm throws his last dart. A little high, but pretty damn close to a bullseye. Syko steps up and throws a dart, nailing a nice 20. >> Nicole: "And what about Marcus Payne? I mean shit, did you see him with that FAKE Japanese guy? What bullshit. To think that anyone would just turn tail like a coward like that is ludicrous. Especially the fact that he was a cabbie.... Tell me, how many japanese cabbies have you seen in Chicago... " Barkeep: "Not a one, and I live here." Nicole: "Exactly! I mean jeez, it had to be a plant... do you think someone from Chicago would just happen to have a Wolverines shirt underneath a Yellow FUBU shirt in Chicago? It's alright, as long as he's alive and kickin', he'll get his. Us Japanese are very proud people you know. Any more sake back there?" Barkeep: "Nope." Nicole: "Crap. Ah well... Guys, you ready to go? Up at 7 tomorrow. Big day." Andrew: "Yeah, I think I've beat Syko bad enough at darts for one night." Syko: "Hey, at least I'm getting better." Barkeep: "Hey, thanks for the business tonight, you guys. Anytime you come back to Chicago, stop by." Andrew: "Hey, it's our pleasure... How much we owe ya?" Barkeep: "$20." Syko: "I got this one guys... you go on ahead, i'll be up to the room in a minute." Andrew: "Alright. Have a good one, man." Barkeep: "You too!" << Exit Krumm & Masaki. >> Syko: "Hey listen.... I got a petition going around.... I was wondering if you can keep this in your bar, and help get some signatures. It's the Save Our Syko campaign, started by a fan... He wants to see more of me on UWC tv.... And I know he's not the only one, so if you could?" Barkeep: "No problem. I wouldn't mind seeing you do that Syko Spin myself." Syko: "You're a class act, Tony. See ya 'round." << Syko leaves a copy of the petition, and a crisp $100 on the counter as he leaves... Fade out to the 'Save Our Syko' commercials. >>
