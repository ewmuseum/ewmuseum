UWC — Roleplay

Critiquable RP (Burt Clifton)

3 January 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Questions, comments, whatever...just as long as you reply in some way. Not looking for a fed really, just some pats on (or knives in) the back... ************************************************************************ [Camera opens in poorly-lit environs: a narrow hallway in the middle of a ticky-tacky suburban house, one of many that look pretty much the same. This one, however, is much more important to us, because it happens to be the home of Burt Clifton, recently ranked #2 on the "Top 50 wrestlers who still live in their parents' houses" poll conducted by WHOO! Magazine. And, speak of the devil, here comes Burt Clifton, dressed normally (well, normal for him, anyway: most people don't wear pre-torn jeans and a faded Live Aid T-shirt these days), muttering about something to himself, something he seems to have a hard time believing...] Clifton: "Cooler than Ferris Bueller? COOLER THAN FERRIS BUELLER? How can ANYONE say that with a straight face?" [Clifton shakes his head in disbelief as he opens the door that leads to the staircase that descends into his not-so-swinging bachelor pad. He says the following words in an incredulous fashion:] Clifton: "Cooler than Ferris Bueller..." [Bang! Crash! A distracted Clifton stumbles down the stairs and, as he attempts to maintaing his balance, his forarm smashes hard against a bookcase on the near wall, sending a scale-model Voltron plummeting to the floor. Which wouldn't be so bad, except that Clifton lost his battle to regain his balance and his knee landed on and crushed the scale model that was the product of weeks of frustrating, tedious, mind-numbing work many years ago. The kind of work that, when it is undone as it has been here, really upsets you.] Clifton: [looking down in horrific disbelief at what has just happened] "OH NO! NO NO NO NO NO NO NO NO NO NO NO!" [Clifton scrapes up the mangled pieces of the object that he cares more deeply about than even the puppy he got for his sixth birthday and cradles them in his trembling hands.] Clifton: "Look at this! Look at what happened!" [Clifton bows his head as he sets about delicately placing the broken pieces on the shelf where they stood united in a glorious replication of a glorious cartoon hero less than a minute ago. Clifton seems almost on the verge of tears (he worked hard on that model, dammit!)] Clifton: "It's your fault Tommy Kain. You got me all worked up with that lie about you being cooler than Ferris Bueller..." [Clifton looks up at the camera, enraged] Clifton: "NOBODY IS COOLER THAN FERRIS BUELLER! It's sacrilege to say otherwise! And now Voltron is broken because you made God angry by saying that, Tommy. You were bad and Voltron suffered because of it. And it was all done through me..." [Suddenly, the thundering voice of a middle-aged woman bursts out from an unseen source, a voice with such an overwhelming presence that it seems as if the walls themselves are speaking. The kind of vocal effect only a mother can produce.] Mama Clifton: "Burt! What the heck are you doing down there? Are you masturbating again?" Clifton: "SHUT UP MOM! Nothing's going on! I just fell down the stairs!" Mama Clifton: "Serves you right for not fixing that third step!" Clifton: "BUT I'M BUSY MOM! AND I'M NOT A CARPENTER!" Mama Clifton: "Well keep it down! I'm watching The Rose Bowl! WOO HOO! GO BADGERS!" [After this interruption by Clifton's oh-so-supportive mother, Clifton sits directly on the floor of his room, sulking.] Clifton: "Should never have set up the VCR to tape that game...she hasn't stopped watching that thing since the live showing...its just that game, on tape, on a loop, over and over again." [But Clifton, momentarily distracted from his suffering, looks at his bookcase and sees the painful reminder of what has happened...that Voltron is in pieces. All because of the outlandish commentary of Tommy Kain.] Clifton: "Sorry Voltron...I'm sorry I got distracted. This is your moment." [Clifton continues to stare at the remnants of the Voltron model as he slowly rises back to his feet.] Clifton: "I swear I'll make it up to you, Voltron..." [Clifton turns back to the camera with uncharacteristic focus and intensity reflecting in his eyeballs.] Clifton: "And there's only one way to do that. Tommy Kain, you wanna be the U.S. champ? Well, now, because of this, because of what you caused, I can't let you do that anymore. In the name of Voltron, WHO YOU BROKE, I'm gonna beat you to that U.S. Title. I'm gonna get there before you. But in order to do that, I have to go through Billy Bob and Pyro. Therefore...Billy Bob, Pyro, this is bad news for you. I just got real motivated real quick for this match. I already had a heckuva lot of motivation what with this being my chance to shine on my own and make a name for myself...but now..." [Clifton pauses, and almost starts laughing as he shakes his head slightly.] Clifton: "...Now...well, Pyro, I've heard of you before. I saw my share of SEWFG2, and BTW. I remember how how much that Spanking Monkey God meant to you back then. Maybe he still does, maybe he doesn't. I dunno. But I do know this: Think about how much it meant to you. And then multiply it by 10...and you'll begin to get an idea about how much Voltron and that model of Voltron -- that I worked hard on for weeks with clumsy little 8 year old hands to put together -- meant and still means to me. I've spent my whole career in someone else's shadow. I've got a broken icon on my hands." [Clifton laughs, partly at himself] Clifton: "And to top it all off, my mom just accused me of masturbating on national TV for everyone to hear. So Billy Bob, Pyro, if you think I'm going to give anything less than my absolute best in that match, then you've got just what Rob Halford said you got: another think coming. I'm coming to get a win, so I can lay legitimate claim to a U.S. title shot, so I can get there before Tommy Kain gets there, so I can get vindication. Those are The Facts Of Life, guys, and I'm not talking about my third-favorite show." [Clifton looks one final time at what is left of his model Voltron, and is clearly affected. Again, he looks almost fit to cry, but he doesn't quite tear up.] Clifton: "January 4th...is for Voltron." [As Clifton continues to stare at his motivation -- a pile of broken plastic -- the camera operator tactfully decides to cease filming and (unseen to you) leave the premesis. Fade out to a commercial for a compilation of Power Ballads that is not available in stores. Call the number on your screen to place your order now, because every bad boy has his soft side.] ************************************************************************ Aaron The One Named Star (Like Goldberg and Madonna) Handler Of: Mike Masters [UWC, IWC] Burt Clifton [UWC] and many failed other projects that I'd like to sweep under the rug and forget about if you don't mind.
