Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The Computerized Wrestling Association was created 18 January 1997, running matches on the TNM 6.2+ simulator with a spot built into its own application form for a sample roleplay, since it was, in its own words, only looking for good ones.

By that April it was running roughly four cards a week and at least one pay-per-view a month, its roster “SQUIRMING with wrestlers,” in its own phrase, after combining with another e-fed it named only as “our friends at the CWA.”

Its one surviving card, Tuesday Crash, aired 29 August 1997 with President Williams opening the broadcast — “welcome to Fried. Friday Fried. Friday Fried Deluxe... well, almost” — before a National Title match pitting Mike Tyson, with Mr. Fuji in his corner, against Devil Worshiper, backed by Brother Death. Tyson won by disqualification after Brother Death pulled down the top rope mid-fall.

Its last surviving document, a recruitment post dated 26 October 1997, called the promotion “almost an 'Established E-Fed,'” still running cards Tuesday and Friday, a booked card on Mondays, and further cards on weekends. No further record survives.
