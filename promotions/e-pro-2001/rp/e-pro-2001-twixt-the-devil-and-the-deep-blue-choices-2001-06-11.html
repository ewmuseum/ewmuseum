'Twixt the Devil and the Deep Blue: Choices

Title: 'Twixt the Devil and the Deep Blue: Choices The rain lashed from the heavens, falling like crystal tears to the ground. The wind, whipped up by the storm, threw leaves and rain around like playthings.
Caleb knew it rained. From inside the large, four story building, he could hear the comforting thrum of the rain pelting the roof. The hallway he walked through was silent, preternaturally silent if you asked him, with the calming, soothing watercolor walls that are a trademark of hospitals everywhere.
The soft sound of boots followed him, as Draeger stood three paces behind him, his briefcase thumping gently against his leg as he walked. Draeger, surprisingly, was dressed sharply in a solid white suit, offset by small, dark stylish glasses on his hooked nose. Surprising things, the things fate made people do. Look at him, Caleb thought with a chuckle and a look down at the solid black suit that he was dressed in. Silver cuff links glittered in the phospherent light that shone from above.
He never thought he'd've been dressed like this again, of his own free will. Strange thing, fate.
A Trans-Atlantic flight. Japan to Baltimore, MD. Back to memories and sorrow. Back to the scene of the crime, so to speak. On a search for 'Angel,' a girl he once knew. Something he let slip through his fingers.
She wouldn't slip through this time. She had something he wanted, no, something he -needed-, some secret, some knowledge of him that even he didn't know. That was why she was special-knowledge is power. And, in that respect, she Was powerful indeed. Not to say she isn't powerful in any other way, he thought with a wry smirk at the memories that bubbled up.
He needed her, not only the knowledge that lay within the delicate skull, beneath golden tresses. But her, her presence, her warmth- Her. He was headed into something he almost couldn't fathom, a fight against the two men in E-Pro who could take him out.
Very dangerous men. November, of EXW fame, where Caleb met him. And Ran Jishu. The only man to beat him in a long, long time. Loss was a thing he refused to accept. And that loss had come clean, come without tarnish. And that galled. It stuck like something distasteful in the back of his throat, lodged there. The only way to get it out was to beat it out.
"Ravenell," Draeger finally spoke, cutting short Caleb's thoughts. He heard Draeger's feet stop moving, in front of a hallway that looked like many others. "This is the hallway."
The Fourth Floor of Sheppard and Enoch Pratt. So many memories, he thought with a chuckle. None his wished to remember.
The lowest points in his life- depression, anger, hatred, suicide. That's what he associated this place with. Pain.
And it was run by the 'honorable' Doctor James Alecard. A beast in unassuming skin, that was Caleb's assessment of the man. And, the man of the hour arrived. "Ah, Mr. Ravenell," Alecard's voice was just like it was so long ago, soft and slick like a car salesman's'.
Caleb grimaced as Alecard continued. "I trust you do not come for a prolonged stay, judging from your attire."
"Cut the bullshit, Alecard," Caleb hissed. "I'll be quite blunt with you. I do not like you. I do not like the thought of Angeline in your care. I find you to be a detestable excuse of a man without a redeeming quality to be found. You are a man who, through the misfortunes of people like myself and Angeline, you
make -your- fortune." "Ah, so very refreshing, as normal," the always imperturbable Alecard spoke as Caleb finished. "It is so good, seeing a man who does not take the circuitous route. Commendable, in fact."
As Caleb began to speak, Alecard continued. "But, my good man, I trust you understand that I cannot remand the redoubtable Ms Angeline into your care. To be quite blunt with -you-," the first hint of anger entered Alecards voice. "She is demented. She is quite unhinged. I do not trust her in my care,
unattended and un-medicated, much less -your- less than desired, let us say, care." Caleb grinned, if that was at all possible, and Draeger stepped up. "Ah, we knew you would say that, James." Dr Alecard flushed at the impersonal use of his name. "See, while normally I'm a rather strange man," he grinned at that. Reinhardt had visited Caleb
during several of his prolonged visits. And he'd always had the feeling that Alecard wanted HIM to have one of those visits. "But, every man must make a living, no? And I, unbelievably as it may be, make my way as a lawyer." Reinhardt Draeger grinned at that, as he saw realization flash on Alecards face. "And
I'm oh-so-sure that I could find something in this less-than-desirable hospital to make your life . . .
miserable, if you don't release Angeline into our care." "Are you THREATENING me?" Alecard said in a shocked voice. Patience wearing thin, Caleb hissed in anger as he hand lashed out. He gripped Alecard by the tie, pulling the other man close to him. "No, this," he hissed, "is threatening you. "Now, continuing along that line of thought, get her. Now."
"Now now, my very violent friend," Draeger laid an arm on Caleb's, disengaging it from Alecards' tie.
"Calm down," he whispered into Caleb's' ear and, surprisingly enough, he did. "Now, Alecard. We know of certain . . . peccadilloes, let us call them . . .
that you have," Draeger smirked as he opened his brief case. Several sets of five by eight glossy pictures slid across the table. Alecard blanched, his face paling so that it was almost as white as the doctor's uniform he wore.
"H-how did you g-g-get these?" He whispered, staring down at the pictures, which had him engaged in acts best left to the imagination with certain younger patients of his.
"Tsk, tsk, tsk, James. Think what your darling wife, and daughters, would think of this. Oh my,"
Draeger said, feigning shock. "That young lady can -not- be much older than your own darling daughter, isn't she?" "YOU CONNIVING BASTARD!" Alecard roared, flinging himself at Draeger. His forward momentum abruptly stopped as Shadow Fire's fist slammed into his jaw, rocking his head back.
"God, I've wanted to do that for a long time," Shadow Fire murmured, grinning as Alecard spat out a wad of blood. "Now that that is out of the way, lead us to Angeline," Draeger's voice was firm as he spoke. Alecard reluctantly turned, leading the way. |------*-------| Standing at the foot of the bed, staring down at the glassy-eyed figure with the blonde hair that had haunted his dreams for so long, Caleb struggled within himself.
He struggled to reconcile this figure, so thin and wasted, with the vibrantly dark girl he had once known. He struggled to find what -he- could have done to avoid this.
Her clothing alone was a shock. Gone were the tattered and torn jeans, the pleather pants with thin slits running up her thigh, the form-fitting shirt that had hugged her figure. Instead there was a plain white hospital gown, cinched at her waist. On her wrists and forearms old and new scars like twisted, some a pale tracery like a spiders web, several others were hard and knotted tissue. Above the marks, an IV protruded.
"Fragile, she doesn't see her beauty, she tries, to get away," Caleb's voice was a soft, strained whisper. He couldn't help it. "Sometimes it seems that nothing is worth saving. I won't watch you slip away," a tear trickled down his cheek.
He stared down at his hand as a crystal tear shattered against it. He raised it to his face, staring at it. He almost didn't know what it was it had been long, oh so long, since he had last shed a tear.
Like the tears that trickled down his cheeks, a drip could be heard from the IV in her arm. She looked so frail, so fragile, lying in the hospital bed.
"Get these out of her," he whispered in a pained voice. Alecard, with a worried glance at Caleb, did as he was told, carefully pulling the needle out. A drop of blood swelled up, but was quickly dabbed away.
"I'll take care of this, just get her files. She's going to be transferred," Draeger's voice was calm as he spoke. With a last glance over his shoulder, Alecard rushed off to get the file.
Caleb's voice was hard. "Now that he's gone, you get the other files. I have a few suspicions.
And keep hold of those pictures," he smirked as he looked over his shoulder. Draeger simply nodded, hurrying off.
|------*-------| Without a hitch, it all came off. Several hours later, many of which were logged in the air over the
Pacific Ocean, with the semi-stuporous Angeline being wheeled in on a stretcher, and they found themselves at the end of part of their journey.
Not the end, the journey was over but just beginning. Now the hard part was to begin... Caleb helped push the stretcher up the inclined ramp, leaning in slightly against the incline, but it was easy. Too easy; she was too light.
"Mmm," the voice was a bare whisper, but it grabbed his attention like a scream. Hurriedly he got the stretcher to the top of the incline and went to
Angeline's side. "Hey, Angel, how you feeling?" Caleb's voice was surprisingly tender as he spoke. "Mmm, w-whos there?" She whispered. Her eyes fluttered, revealing the startling emerald green eyes.
"I-it's me," Caleb surprised himself, stuttering. "Caleb," he whispered. The look of recognition that flashed across Angeline's face brought warmth to his long cold heart. Only to shatter it as the look on her face transformed into one of rage. "You bastard!" She hissed in anger. "Five years, you idiot! Five years you were gone," her eyes were suddenly clear and lucid, livid with long suppressed rage. "You were out of my life for five years!"
"Y-you know why, Angel," he whispered. "I was in jail. I-I never meant to hurt you," a tear trickled down his cheek.
Pain slammed its rusty knife into his heart as she continued. "You stupid bastard! Get out of here, get away from me. I told you five years ago, and I'll tell you again, get-out-of-my-life!"
His heart felt like it was torn, shredded beyond redemption. He tried to speak, but only spluttered and stuttered.
"I don't CARE about what you need," she tried to sit up, but the restraints on the stretcher held her down. "I don't care about you, I don't want you in my life! Go back, go back wherever you came from, just get out of my life!" She screamed with her eyes wide and red. She struggled against the restraints holding her down, her muscles flexing and striving, but she couldn't break them.
She began to scream, high pitched sounds full of anger and rage, thrashing like a wild animal on the stretcher.
A nurse, a Japanese lady with a nametag that Caleb didn't understand, rushed up, rattling off a few words in Japanese. Quickly two men, both large, with short cut black hair, came to her side and held the thrashing Angeline to the stretcher.
"YOU BASTARD, I'LL KILL YOU!" she screamed at Caleb, her voice piercing through him like the needle that pierced her arm as she spoke. Her voice began to trail off under the effects of the drug, and her eyes to flutter. Slowly they closed, not allowing Caleb to see those emerald green depths.
But, the look in her eyes, pure fury, pure hatred, would never leave him. |------*-------| "You didn't expect her to welcome you with open arms, did you, Az?" Draegers voice was once again the desultory whisper that Shadow Fire was used to. Both of the men stalked the streets of Tokyo, ties undone, shirts open at the neck.
Throwing his coat over his shoulder, Shadow Fire searched through his pockets. "Want one?" he asked in a whisper soft voice, as he held out a cigarette.
"Sure." A click of a lighter, a small spurt of flame, and soon Shadow Fire inhaled the harsh yet welcoming bite of the cigarette.
"I don't know what I expected, I just know I need those answers, god damn it!" He hissed in anger, smoke flowing from his mouth. "I don't give a damn about her. I try to help her, and she insults me. Screw her," his breathing became harsher as he gave into the anger that always bubbled close to the surface.
"Yeah, well, either way, you don't need this, Az," Drager spoke softly. "Focus this shit on what you need to do. Who care's 'bout her, all you need to do is win in the ring, and it won't matter anymore." "The ring doesn't mean anything to me, Rein, and you know it. But, yeah," Caleb nodded in acknowledgement. "Baggage will weigh me down. I'll pay her stay at the hospital, give her time."
"God, Az, you do remember how much hell she put you through? She played you, Az, played you like a fine instrument. And here you go, offerin' to let her do it again. You are a fool, Az, a love-struck and blinded fool," the last was said with surprising venom.
Shadow Fire turned to him. "Shut up, Reinhardt. You -don't- know what this is about. You think I still have feelings for that b-tch?! She's a tool,
Reinhardt, only a tool. I need what she has inside her skull, the knowledge. Without that, I'm not whole. Once I have it? Well, I intend to hurt HER just like she hurt ME five years ago."
"You're an asshole, Az, you know that? A manipulative little bastard," Reinhardt Draeger shook his head in disgust. "You were crying back there,
Caleb. Tell me you don't feel anything for her." "Do not," Shadow Fire's voice was a soft whisper, "ever again call me that." "What, Caleb? An Asshole? Or a bastard? Or was it manipulative?" Reinhardt knew what he was doing, and Shadow Fire flushed a deep crimson.
"No, you stupid sh-t! Never call me Caleb. Caleb died years ago, he is dead and gone," Shadow Fire barely held his rage in check as he spat out the words. "You're like a little kid, -Caleb-," the emphasis was unmistakable. "Petty," Reinhardt spat the word out in distaste.
"Yeah, well you like my money enough, don't you?" "You enjoy this shit, though. I don't. You're disgusting," with that last word, Reinhardt walked to the curb. Raising a hand to hail a cab, he grinned as one stopped.
Flicking the cigarette off the tip of his finger, he turned to Shadow Fire as he opened the cab door. "You never answered my question, Caleb. Do you still care for her?"
In lieu of an answer, Shadow Fire spat on the sidewalk. Shaking his head sadly, Reinhardt Draeger got into the cab.
[But I do,] the words were whispered in the depths of Shadow Fire's skull. He repressed the source of that voice with relish as he lit up a second cigarette.
