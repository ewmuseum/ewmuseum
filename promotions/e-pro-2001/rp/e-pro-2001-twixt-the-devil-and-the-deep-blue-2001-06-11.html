'Twixt the Devil and the Deep Blue

11 June 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
This is a part of a series I'm doing, this is the third in the 'Twixt the Devil and the Deep Blue series. It's going in depth into Shadow
Fire's, yes, he's a goth, past.
Note: The emphasis on Caleb and Shadow Fire is purposeful. I do not just switch names arbitrarily (did I spell that right?). He has four seperate and distinct personalities: Caleb, the Kid, Azrael, and
Shadow Fire. His full name is Caleb Azrael Ravenell. This is a dream like-hallucinatory RP, he's also a Schizophrenic medicated with Fluanxol (I researched it... was REALLy bored) who
HASN'T had his meds in a long time. He also got lit the fuck up by
Ran Jishu.
And please, I don't care how harsh the review is... but be helpful, not "You SuCK!!!~" :) (i'll bury my ego and take it :) )
‘Twixt the Devil and the Deep Blue: The Choice. -=Hello. . .is there anybody in there? Just nod if you can. . .hear me. Is there anyone. . .home? Come on, now. I hear you’re feeling. . .down.=- Blood, crimson fury, coated his vision. Everything was red. Blood-shot eyes gave a cast to the world. The fury came bubbling back.
His fist lashed out, striking the pinkish, at least to him, walls. He knew, dimly, that they should be white. Maybe he was a little more injured than he thought.
A concave depression appeared magically, at least to him, in the wall. His fist hurt. He didn’t know why.
Again, a hiss of displaced air, a crack and patter as plaster rained down. The concave deepened.
He couldn’t figure out why his fist hurt. He was sitting down. SLAM! Deeper depression. SLAM!! Even deeper. SLAM-CRUNCH! Pain shot through his hand like coke through his veins. Thinking of coke, he could do with some right now.
The whisper-voice, dim in his skull, of his conscience spoke. Something about probation. Something about violating that. His fist slammed again into the wall, slamming through the hole into the wall. Damn his fist hurt.
His hand tangled in the wires of the wall. Colours corruscated through his eyes, shimmering hues of white and black and gray and red, red predominatly. Varying shades, varying degrees. But all were like fire, the fires of some bottomless, unimaginable pit, fires that were hued in black and gray.
He chuckled; they were like his name: Shadow Fire. His head jerked of it own accord, whipping to the side. Shadow Fire? His name? What… his fist slammed into the wall again, widening the hole.
He didn’t care. His fist hurt. His head hurt. His back hurt. His whole body roared in agony. What was a name, compared to the rest? He screamed, a unvoiced yet deafening sound that filled his world. A sound that echoed in the spaces between his thoughts. The dim voice whispered in his skull. He roared again.
The voice shut up. [God damn about time.] Did he say that? His fist struck the wall again. Who cares? I, Azrael. I, Shadow Fire. Silence consumed him. -=I can ease your pain. Get you on your feet again. Relax. I need some information first. Just the basic facts. Can you show me where it hurts?=-
. . . -=Now I’ve got that feeling once again. I can’t explain, you would not understand. This is not how I am=- A voice in the dark, whispering softly. Soft spoken and calm. Soothing, like a balm on a burnt limb.
He thrashed, his body twisting and twining of it’s own accord. “Calm down, Caleb, calm down,” the whisper was back. He lashed out. The voice danced out of his reach. “Damn it, Caleb,” the voice made a motion. Or at least he thought it did. Oh well. -=Okay… it’s just a little pinprick …. Now we don’t go,=- Shadow Fire screamed as the pain laced through his arm like wildfire, like a volcano, searing his veins. “AAAHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHH!!”
-=But you may feel a little sick,=- roiling like a sea, naseua in it’s purest form, agony unbearable, pain unlivable, torture unimaginable. He scream-retched again, his scream gagging in his throat as bile and blood came up.
“Give him more, I can’t bear to see him like this!” The whisper said. He tried to deny it, tried to stop them.
He failed. Angeline . . . The world was ice and he walked upon it. The ice emcompassed him, soothing his battered soul. The ice was within and without. The pain was a bare memory, a fleeting thought. He could feel it. It burnt in his gut and his mind like liquid fire. It was within, yet he couldn’t feel it. Looking around himself, Shadow Fire noted his surroundings. Cold and gray, the sky was a forboding mass of gray-black storm clouds. The ground was solid ice, stretching into infinity in every direction, a frozen lake. Kind of like his frozen soul. Shadow
Fire chuckled. He didn’t know where he was. He found, to his surprise, that he didn’t care. -=Can you stand up? I do believe it's working, good. That'll keep you going through the shows. Come on it's time to go.=- He stepped confidently forward. He didn’t know where he was, why he was there, or anything. He might as well walk.
The ice crunched lightly beneath his feet, a sound that echoed preternaturally in the silence that
was the landscape. The fleeting thought came again, something about his name. The whisper was forgotten as the crack shattered the silence. Veins of rising ice, shattering beneath the weight of two hundred plus pounds, resounded like a gunshot. The ice rose at odd angles, edges sinking and rising in pandemonioum. He spun wildly as the cracks spread like wild fire. What he thought was the ground, but was in reality ice, began to rock.
Arms pinwheeling wildly, he overbalanced. His body slipped, his head struck the ice, his vision was blinded by the strike.
-=When I was a child I caught a fleeting glimpse Out of the corner of my eye. I turned to look but it was gone I cannot put my finger on it now=-
Flashes of light flared behind his vision, flood lights sparkling in his mind. Images coalesced out of the ether.
FLASH: A cruel, haggard face. Eyes sunk in their sockets, cheeks desiccated like a corpses’, skin worn like old, fragile parchment. Donovan Ravenell:
father; demon. FLASH: A glint of steel flaring in a candle-lit room, reflecting and refracting the orange glow.
FLASH: A sound, a horrific scream, a woman’s scream of pure, tortured pain. FLASH: A knife slicing into pale flesh, a woman’s face, twisted in a rictus of pain. Elizabeth Calysa Ravenell: mother; angel. FLASH: A blood splattered image of Donovan, knife in hand. FLASH: A scream, the utter denial of a young child forced to stare into the face of cruel insanity. A scream, the utter denial of a grown man blanking out his past, denying what he knows to be true. Denying his past. Denying his present. Denying his future. Succumbing to nothingness.
A whimper, the acceptance of a grown man. A moan, the pained remembrance of times he should have forgotten coming back in waves, shattering over his soul and his mind.
“GOD!” He screamed-thought, denying his past. The cold, old, familiar weight settled on his mind. A burning flame, a searing fire, a darkness out of the depts of a tormented soul, an evil he once denied, an evil he once accepted. An evil he welcomed once again. -=The child is grown, The dream is gone. I have become comfortably numb.=-
