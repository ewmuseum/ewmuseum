Universal Championship Wrestling Federation — Rules

WCWF is looking for new members

2 October 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
This league is called the Universal Championship Wrestling Federation or UCWF. It is a booked league. Here is everything that you need to know about it... ***SHOWS*** There will be three house shows a week done summary style and three arena shows with full commentary. The three arena shows will be called Monday Night Live, Thursday Night Rumble, and Saturday Night Stampede. There will also be a pay-per-view held about every six weeks and maybe a Saturday Night Main Event or Clash Of The Champions style card. ***TITLES*** There will be seven titles in the UCWF: World heavyweight, U.S. heavyweight, TV, light heavyweight, World tag team, U.S. tag team, and women's singles. The titles will be handled differently than almost every other league, real or not. The title reigns' lengths will be that of WCW or WWF style of which people will have five or six month reigns and not 15 day reigns. There are also some rules about title matches that are different. The TV title will only be defended on television. If a tag team champions wins a singles title and vacates the tag belts, the remaining partner will then be given a new partner and a bye in the tournament to decide new champions. If a champion is intentenionally DQ'ed or counted out, he/she will lose their title and a tournament will be held to determine a new champion. If a wrestler forfeits a title shot, he will not recieve another one for six months unless he has a viable excuse and did not just not show up. Also, there will be no "tripleheader" tag team matches. Only tag team titles will be on the line in tag team matches. All titles must be defended within 30 days. ***WRESTLERS*** There won't be any real wrestlers or real names. In other words, be original. I don't want a whole lot of wrestlers with names like Goldust, Diesel, or Yokozuna; these are fine, but we still need some Bret Hart's and Shawn Michaels'. I also don't want a whole lot of wrestlers of 300 lbs. If I do accept a wrestler over 300 lbs. and I have a choice between two, I'll pick the one will the more experience and more titles one usually. The wrestlers in this fed will go on losing streaks and winning streaks the size of those wrestlers in the WWF or WCW. A tag team takes up to male wrestler spots. Tag teams usually don't wrestle a lot of singles matches. While stables are needed, I don't want too many and no stable shall be bigger than eight members and only two or three of them may be allowed. There will also be injuries and I will determine when they happen (you can too if you want to). I will reserve the right to turn down all wrestlers, angles, and interference. ***FLASHES & CHALLENGES*** I would like for everyone to flash at least once a week. However, you will not be punished unless you do not flash for a month or more. Flashes must also be at least a paragraph long. Send all flashes and challenges to me. There won't be any cussing in the league unless it's an occassional ass, hell, damn, or something to that effect, like Steve Austin uses. I want this to be a more family-entertainment style league along the lines of the WWF or WCW, not like ECW, although there will be some hard-hitting action and "hardcore" matches. There also will not be any personal attacks to other owners. If you do this, you'll be kicked out immediately. You can use an announcer in your flashes, but if you're going to attack them please e-mail me and ask me first. Also, please use the announcer as they act on the shows. The announcers are John Jacobs, Eric Gibson, "Nasty" Nate Oliver, Ted Tolliver, Rick Hill, Hank Hayes, and Drew Collins. Gibson and Oliver will act as heel announcers. Also, the commisioner for the league is Carter Scott and the executive vice president is Ron Adams. I will make some matches for cards and I will make some from challenges. I do have the right to turn down all matches, but I will try to ablige the owners in the UCWF. ***MAGAZINES & UPDATES*** A magazine will be sent out once a month and updates will be sent out once a week. The magazine will contain rankings, Most Popular and Most Hated rankings, articles, a profile of a wrestler, an "X-Ray" where a panel determines the status of a wrestler's career, a wrestler of the month, a match of the month, Thumbs Up & Thumbs Down, an article introuducing a new wrestler, an interview with a wrestler, scouting reports, ratings analysis, and a roll call of champions. The update will include a wrestler of the week, match of the week, rumors, rankings, and title changes. Both of these could add or lose features at any time. ***RULE CHANGES*** Any of the rules may be changed if not to the liking of the owners in the UCWF. ***CREATION SHEET*** Name: Height: Weight: Hometown: Age: Hair: Eyes: Skin: (African-American, Caucasian, Chinese, etc.): Special: (scars, tattoos, etc.): Allegiance: (face/heel/neutral) Entrance Music: Description/Appeareance: Personality: Background/Experience/Titles Won: Style: Finisher (describe if needed): Set-Up Move To Finisher: Ring Entrance: 31 Moves: Three Strengths: Three Weaknesses: Does He/She Cheat?
