UCWF Changing And Looking For Help & Wrestlers

12 October 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The new fed, the Universal Championship Wrestling Federation, is going to be having a little renovation. Now instead of being a booked league, it will be split into two division/leagues, one booked with no name so far, and one simulated called the World Cyber Wrestling Federation. As you can guess, this will be a lot of work, so I'm looking for a board of directors type of thing. And although this is a very long shot, I'm looking for two people who have access to a homepage and can make a good one and would be willing to keep up a homepage for the leagues/divisions. I can't do homepages very well, so I was hoping someone would help me with that. So, if you're interested in the above or just in entering a wrestler in either league/division, we're still plenty open for business, so drop me a line if you want in. Thanks.
