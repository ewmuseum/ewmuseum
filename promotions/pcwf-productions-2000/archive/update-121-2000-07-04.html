Hopefully [name withheld] won't mind too much. :) * Have you ever read two MOV's so action packed with angles, intriegue, storyline, and packed with the new talent as great as the past two? * Please email [detail withheld] with your strats/roleplays. * Please prebook all matches when possible and co-write them to keep [name withheld] from having to book too many matches. :) Cooperation to this will be rewarded when I return. * Please observe deadlines as [name withheld] shouldn't have to write any matches but he may have to so give him all the time in a world. Give preference to strats. * Please email [detail withheld] and volunteer to write matches. Volunteers will be given big hugs when I return, and Twinkies and Donuts for all. * Email me if you have any complaints/questions while I'm gone, NOT [detail withheld] , I will email you when possible. * As you may or may not know, I am taking a three week trip to Alaska. I had arranged for [name withheld] (Twizted/Hagadorn handler) to take over the fed, but from what rumors say around the area, he has sold out to another federation. This is unconfirmed, but considering he arrives one day before I leave and I may not get to sort things out, it's better to be overly cautious than to be unprepared. I don't know if any of you remember [name withheld] .. he's an old school e-wrestler .. the realistic handler of Madcap back when Anderson/Madcap were a top team wreaking havoc on regional feds in Michigan. [name withheld] has told me he'll handle the fed if I need him to -- so email and everything may need to be adapted.. I may just let him have use of my Yahoo account. I will be on while I'm gone, but not in ICQ/AOL or IRC. I will be PHYSICALLY CONNECTED at home so do not get mad if I do not respond. This is because I have business things that come in while I'm gone or not. I am aware the president leaving for 3 weekends isn't very endearing but I assure you the fed will be in good hands. I'll still be answering emails and probably even writing a few matches to the duration that I will be gone. If you want to check out my itinerary, perhaps I'm coming to a city near you -- if so, you can let me come over and answer email. :) [detail withheld] -- then click on Alaskan Roadtrip 2000 for details and if you want to follow me on my trip. I leave next Friday morning. *IF* everyone can get everything into me on time (yeah right) I'll stay up as long as it takes Friday morning to get SNM out before I leave to at least deal with that, but you'll have to work with me. I'll need writers and everything ASAP and strats in Wednesday night to make it realistic.. so if you want to help out, although I won't move the deadline on you guys, but feel free to work ahead. * You know, for Chris being on vacation, a few other guys being out .. SNM was really, really nothing less than spectacular. Great matches. Great angles. Some storylines everyone can take part in .. Really had something for everyone in there. Nothing less than stellar. * Wow. Can you believe how great the PPV was? With as little matches as there were? The quality was great..
