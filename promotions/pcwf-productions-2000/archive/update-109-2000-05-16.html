Over 200k? Largest MOV ever .. keep labeling, keep up the hard work -- and we may be having a temporary third show for awhile starting after the PPV I may let some over acheiving staff member run. :) * Getting tired of me building this thing up, but SNM was great again as well. Let's keep the positive momentum going! * WHOOOOOOOOOOO! What can I say about MOV? Action packed from opening to close. Lots of surprises, lots of good angles.. and rumor has it SNM may top it. Let's try to pack SNM with action and full work from a lot of people. * SNM was out again early and very well done .. once again, thanks to [name withheld], [name withheld], and Russell who did a lot of hard work for the card, and all the new guys that pitched in. A special recognition to the handlers of Mr. Nice Guy and Slam Dunk Samson for their great work. Welcome back Ryan Holmes and High Times, even if Cronic and Blunt were in a guest starring role for the Vancouver crowd. Also, thumbs up to Alex Plant, Lightning, and Outkast for some consistent work and good SNM coverage. * I have to give credit where credit is due. Although there was not much work for MOV, it was really good, and surprisingly long. I have to give all credit to Mr. [name withheld], the handler of Spike, who has said he wants to help out and make an impression, well, everything you saw on that card, he did. From opening commentary to assembling to writing dark matches and making it flow, he was the man on this card. It's great to see someone being such a team player and giving me a well deserved Tuesday off. (Now, to get a couple Saturdays off) :) Great job [name withheld] .. the whole fed commends you on your hard work and dedication. Even at 3 in the morning. * You know, with 5 booked matches and all things considering, the PPV really did not disappoint at all. Special thanks go out to [name withheld], the handler of Spike for a lot of great and funny work on hour 1, [name withheld], to writing two hellaciously good matches for hour 3, [name withheld], a new guy who has a ton of potential for doing an impromptu match, and everyone who strated and helped out .. [name withheld] helped out a little bit as well and all of it is mucho appreciated. May's PPV is called Courage Inspection and no doubt it'll be greatness as we head to the summer. * If you know of anyone that's looking for a federation, let them know to email me..
