Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The federation announced its own return on a Saturday in March 2000, a recruitment post promising a laid-back, fake-wrestlers-only roleplaying board under a new web address and a new co-president, and looking to "repeat history" after what it called a prior run that had broken into a fan poll's top fifty. A second post the same week widened the pitch, offering free email accounts, a hall of fame, and a message-board application process to anyone willing to give the letters a second life.

By May the federation had settled into a weekly rhythm: Saturday Night Mayhem posted in three hour-long parts, a numbered president's newsletter that had already passed its hundredth issue, and a secondary brand, Ground Zero, carrying developmental cards on its own separate night. Five titles ran through the roster at once — a World Heavyweight, an International Heavyweight, a World Cruiserweight, a Hardcore championship shared by two co-holders, and a Tag Team title — contested across two monthly pay-per-views, Courage Inspection in May and Summer Inferno in June, each posted whole across three surviving hours.

The summer brought an inter-promotional feud with a rival online federation, traded across dueling newsgroup columns each side called the real last word, and an in-house Action Heroes Tournament of Champions run alongside the regular card cycle. When its president left for a three-week trip that July, the newsletter named a veteran handler as steward of the federation in his absence, promising the weekly show would go on without him.

The four letters had already named two earlier, unconnected federations earlier in the same run of years, neither sharing this one's own web address, production credit, or roster, and this federation's own claim to an earlier "1998 to 1999" run matches the record of neither.

No closing statement survives. The last document held is a backstage segment dated the middle of July, framed as footage almost cut from an earlier pay-per-view's tape, promising a return that the surviving record does not carry any further.
