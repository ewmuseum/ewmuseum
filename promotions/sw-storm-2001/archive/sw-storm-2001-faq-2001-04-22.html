Storm Wrestling [SW] http://ewrestling2001.nsproductions-network.com/main.html -=[ About Us ]=- ************************************************************************** ******************* Storm Wrestling is an umbrella fed within the eXtreme Wrestling Alliance [XW]. The eXtreme Wrestling Alliance is home to many different regional feds. Each region will send competitors every 2-3 months to compete in an eXtreme Alliance pay-per-view event. The eXtreme Wrestling Alliance has it's own World Heavyweight and World Tag Team Championships for them to compete for. Storm Wrestling is one of these regional feds based in the central northern states. These states include: Illinois, Indiana, Iowa, Michigan, Minnesota, Missouri, Ohio, and Wisconsin. The Storm Wrestling federation headquarters is located in Toledo, Ohio. All wrestling events, such as Monday Night's Eye of the Storm and Pay-Per-Views will be held within the region. Storm Wrestling CEO Ike Rogers, was contacted by the American Broadcasting Association [ABC]. ABC was interested in signing Storm Wrestling to a television deal. Although practically all the prime time slots are taken, ABC and Ike Rogers were able to come to a deal. On Monday Night's, Storm Wrestling will air it's Eye of the Storm event from 10:00 PM Eastern time until 12:00 PM Eastern time. Pay-Per-Views will air normally on cable access television. Various Information: Storm Wrestling is run by [detail withheld] also known as [name withheld]. To join, go to the site and click the application link in the navigation section. If accepted, a member of the SW Executive Office will contact you via mail. Roleplaying takes place on the Interview Board. On Air Personalities: Play-by-Play Commentator: Ray Tilly Color Commentator: John Jinks Backstage Interviewer: Sherlie Gothan Backstage Interviewer: Tyler McMertal Ring Announcer: Michael Wirick Referees: Jim Deshane, Patrick Cosack, and Peter Stockton Wrestling Titles: SW Platinum Heavyweight Championship - Our version of a World Title. This title is open to all wrestlers, there is no weight restriction. Highest title in the federation. SW Great Lakes Championship - This title is the second highest in the federation. Much like a United States Title, also no weight restrictions. SW North Star Championship - This title is much like a television title. It will be defended every card. Also no weight restrictions. SW Tag Team Championship - Top titles for a tag team. -=[ Rules & Regulations ]=- ************************************************************************** ***************** All wrestlers/handlers need to roleplay at least once a week. It's not to much to ask that you roleplay once a week to keep Storm Wrestling active. If you are unable to roleplay please let someone in the executive office know. All wrestlers/handlers have the opportunity to send in an interview for each weekly card. By doing the pre-match interview/ post-match interview, it helps bring the card or results to more of a reality. All wrestlers/handlers must understand that when you join the Storm Wrestling we hold the right to control your character if you leave prematurely. All wrestlers/handlers must have all roleplay's for each card in by 12:00 PM Eastern on Saturday. This gives us time to get the winners decided and the matches written so that results are up on time. All wrestlers/handlers must understand that we, the staff within the SW are trying our best to please everyone but this will not happen, of course there will be the lone duck that won't like something and make a big deal out of it. There is a 0% tolerance policy with every wrestler in the oWa. And honestly there is not one wrestler/player that is worth keeping if he is treating everyone without respect. SW grades roleplay's on 5 things, 1. Creativeness 2. Quality (such as, does the roleplay attract us, does it keep our attention and etc.), 3. Quantity (oh yes, length does matter), 4. Subject (does it have a point and does it concern the match the wrestler is in), 5. Approach (does the wreslter/player approach and thicken his angle and/or plot in the roleplay.) All roleplay's are to be placed on the Interview Board. HTML is allowed but Do not use table formatting, or linking roleplays. They will be deleted and will not count towards your match. Do not use other wrestlers in your roleplays without permission from the perspective wrestler/handler. Please do not ask when results will be posted, or when your poser images will be made. SW is a roleplay/angle based fed, which means, that winners are decided by the best roleplayer of the match. But if a loss would help to further a fued or help create an angle, you will be notified before hand, so that you don't get angry after the results come out. -=[ To Join ]=- ************************************************************************** **************** Go to the site ( http://ewrestling2001.nsproductions-network.com/main.html ) and fill out the application. If accepted you will be contacted within one week. Storm Wrestling, [name withheld] President & CEO
