Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

American Power Wrestling Federation announced itself on 8 November 1997: “The APWF is looking to open up soon.” It was built as a real-wrestler roleplay league, open only to talent drawn from the WWF, WCW, ECW, and the wrestling scenes of Japan and Europe, each handler allowed up to four wrestlers and one manager, matches decided by the quality of a roleplay rather than its length or frequency.

Its title ladder copied Extreme Championship Wrestling's own directly — a World Heavyweight, World Television, and World Tag Team Championship — running one weekly card, billed inconsistently across its own two surviving posts as both “Warriors of Saturday Wrestling” and “Saturday Showdown of Stardom,” and a pay-per-view planned every three months starting that January.

Its founding post already claimed one stable, the Awesome Powers, managed by Bobby Heenan and built around Owen Hart, The Giant, Sid, and Kevin Nash.

No further document survives past 9 November 1997. Whether the league ever opened, ran a card, or crowned a champion isn't in the surviving record.
