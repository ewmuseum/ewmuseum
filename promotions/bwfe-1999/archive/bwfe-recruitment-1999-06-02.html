BWFE Recruitment Notice

2 June 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Are you looking to join an established, competitive e-fed that's been around for 3 solid years, then the BWFE may be for you... The BWFE, that's the BEST WRESTLING FEDERATION EVER, is looking for new talent!
The BWFE is an email based fed with 50 members. We have 3 weekly cards. The top show is Sunday Showdown, featuring 6-8 matches every week, along with the freshest angles going today. Wednesday Warfare is a great 4-5 match show, with top angles as well. And BWFE Rush Hour is the equivalent of a televised house show...
Our Pay Per Views happen once a month, on Wednesday nights, in place of
Warfare. The June Pay per view, hardcore Hell, is shaping up to be a banner show.
Our members are expected to RP (flash) at least 2 times a week, but not more than 3 please. We want the BWFE to be a place where creativity and
QUALITY are as important as quantity. Flashes are sent out every weeknight at 10 PM CDT, and some Sundays, as demand necessitates...
All of our shows are hand written by established writers, all most angles are written by the writers themselves, and all booking is done by co-owners Rich Boles and Shane Kelley. Neither Shane or myself run any wrestlers, so there is no conflict of interest.
The BWFE features a registered chatroom on the Undernet, #BWFE. Several of our handlers can be found in there every day, and are more than willing to answer any questions you may have. Both Shane Kelley and
Rich Boles are regular fixtures there as well.
The BWFE also has a strong website at http://bwfe.hypermart.net . The site contains the BWFE rules, list of members, member profiles, the BWFE
Control Center, and a members only area with the Challenge Board, the
BWFE Calendar, and the Flash of the Week.
To apply for membership in the BWFE, all you have to do is fill out the application at bwfe/
hypermart.net . If you have any questions, feel free to email Shane Kelley at [detail withheld] , and Rich Boles at [detail withheld] .
