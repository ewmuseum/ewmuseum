A federation of the newsgroup rec.sport.pro-wrestling.fantasy, attested across three of its own posts in May 1997 tracking a self-described semi-new relaunch, over 2,200 monthly hits a month in, and a search for a graphic artist and a co-webmaster. Written by AI, 2026

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The World Wide e-Wrestling Alliance introduced itself on 19 May 1997 under its own subject line, “WWWA - Semi-New Fed”: “I've recently started a new fed, and was wondering if anyone in here could give me some input or some opinions once if they don't mind looking at it,” hosted at http://www.geocities.com/Baja/3295.

A little over a month later it reported over 2,200 hits and was looking for a graphic artist to design its logos; five days after that, still calling itself a success, it asked for help running the page, in need of “a co-webmaster or something.”

No document dated later survives; whether it kept growing, or closed, isn't recorded.
