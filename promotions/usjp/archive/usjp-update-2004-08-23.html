With the recent additions of established Juniorweights Nemo and Catastrophe, the front office is expecting many big matches between the two. Nemo recently of BSCW fame, while Catastrophe returning to the ring ... a former UWF and NEO Juniorweight Champion. While there are likely no plans for a Juniorweight division in the near future, the daredevil high flying style fits right in with the USJP style. B. Management is apparently pleased with first week DVD sales, confident the numbers will continue to climb. At this time no official number has been released. C. While Stephen Lords has been named the Commissioner, the man to keep the boys in line. Former IWE General manager Tony Capri has been hired as well by the USJP to serve in a similar role. Another step in owner [name withheld] stepping aside to spend more time at home with his wife D. Tetsuo Kimura is on the bubble with USJP officials. His lackluster effort in the opening contest didn't sit well with management. Giving him the next show off in Philadelphia, Tony Capri is still expecting an appearance from Tetsuo Kimura. When asked about the consequences of a no show, Capri replied "We'll cross that road if it comes". [INJURY REPORT] 1. Marcus Hayden - Strained neck suffered at the hands of Vile Vince Viper. He'll miss action on the next show but is expected to be at one hundred percent for the following taping. 2. Blackout - Injured ribs during the main event cage match with Rally Jackson. Will compete against Eric Vanguard at the next taping, however not at one hundred percent. 3. Ashley Brookes - Suffered a severe concussion during the aftermath of the main event. Not to mention the fifteen stitches on her forehead.Her status for the next taping is day to day. 4. Mad Dog Maddox - Bruised shoulder. No time off. 5. Bernie Cox- Stitches for a gash caused by Tetsuo Kimura smashing a beer bottle over his head. No time off. [ROSTER] 1. Bernie Cox 2. Blackout 3. Caliban 4. Catastrophe 5. Takeshi Tanisaki/Sabbath [LINEUP] The USJP returns to action... Thursday September 09, 2004 From the Murphy Rec Center in Philadelphia, PA The opening bout will pit two men in need of a win after tough losses last week. A banged up "WHITE TRASH WARRIOR" BERNIE COX will step in to the ring with a bitter RALLY JACKSON. Can the underdog COX pull off the upset or will the TIGERHEART rebound? At the Beginning WEREWOLF GREGORSON was victorious in impressive fashion. Can he keep his winning ways going when he welcomes in three newcomers to the USJP roster in a Four Way Elimination Match. His opponents? NEMO. The daredevil veteran CATASTROPHE. And the much hyped JOHN WAYNE EVANGELISTA. Eliminations will occur by pinfall, submission, or via over the top rope. After a disappointing outing last week, a banged up and pissed off MAD DOG MADDOX will be out to prove a point when he steps in to action with the "WALKING CONTRADICTION" SABBATH! CALIBAN will be out for revenge when he meets VILE VINCE VIPER one on one. It was VIPER who nearly caused the Alaskan Enigma to be eliminated from the six man tag last week when he came to the aide of SABBATH. The Freak was none to pleased and immediately following his victory, it was reported that he demanded to Commissioner Stephen Lords that he be placed in a match with VIPER. The Semi-Main Event will feature two men who couldn't keep there hands off of each other in the six man tag last week. The sadistic clown VOODOO bested one "DEVIL DOG" TAKESHI TANISAKI, eliminating him. Will the outcome be the same when they meet one on one? Oh yea... it will be a DOG COLLAR MATCH! And in the Main EVent Blackout will be looking for payback when he and "EXCESSIVE FORCE" ERIC VANGUARD meet in a WEAPONS MATCH. Use what you bring ... bring what you like. Vanguard did a number on Blackout's manager Ashley Brookes, busting her open with a steel chair. You better believe that the ten year veteran is out for Vanguard's blood! Plus we'll hear from rookie MARCUS HAYDEN. What does he have to say about VINCE VIPER? Also, TETSUO KIMURA has been ordered by STEPHEN LORDS to be in Philadelphia or face termination. All of this and much much more. Deadlines for strats and on cards is Monday August 30th at Midnight whatever fricking time zone. I scheduled more matches so I will need help.
