Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

USJP survives in the record only mid-run, through a roster update dated 23 August 2004; no founding document survives, and "USJP" is the only name the promotion's own text ever gives itself. That update reports two recent signings, Nemo and Catastrophe — the latter a former UWF juniorweight champion returning to the ring — and frames both as a fit for “the daredevil high flying style” the promotion had already made its own.

Its front office was in transition at the same time: Commissioner Stephen Lords kept the roster in line, and a newly hired general manager, Tony Capri, formerly of another promotion, was brought aboard as the owner stepped back to spend more time at home. Capri's first real test was Tetsuo Kimura, benched for a lackluster effort and warned to show up for the next Philadelphia taping or face termination.

The update carried a sixteen-name roster, a weekly Top Five led by Caliban, and an injury report naming five hurt wrestlers, including a manager, Ashley Brookes, left with fifteen stitches and a concussion after a chair shot at ringside.

It previewed the promotion's next taping, set for 9 September 2004 at the Murphy Rec Center in Philadelphia: six matches including a four-way elimination bout introducing three newcomers to the roster, a Dog Collar match settling a grudge from the prior card, and a Weapons match main event between Blackout and “Excessive Force” Eric Vanguard. No further document survives, and whether that card ran isn't in the surviving record.
