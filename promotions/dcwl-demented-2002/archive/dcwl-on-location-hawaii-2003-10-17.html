Press Release: On Location — Hawaii

17 October 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
DCWL PRESS RELEASE RE: TOTAL DEMENTIA - ON LOCATION : HAWAII Upcoming on Halloween night, is a very special episode of Total Dementia, under the banner of "ON LOCATION : HAWAII". Before presenting the facts of the upcoming event, let us grasp an understanding of the On Location concept and its deep history within our gaming community. ON LOCATION made its first e-wrestling appearance in March of 1999, as a full-blown Supercard in the WWA. The event was based inside of the live arena, with several featured matches taking place in various locations throughout the United States, such as a "Disneyland Brawl", and a "Shopping Spree Match". Two other ON LOCATION Supercards took place in the WWA, before the promotion closed its doors. However, when two former WWA fedheads came together to form the DCWL resurrection, the ON LOCATION concept was brought back to life in the 2003 Spring Season, as a special episode of Total Dementia. The event was a huge success, claiming the DCWL Arena as its home base, with several special attraction matches taking place in and around Toronto, including a Monkey Cage Match between Dale Stanwycz and The Vindicator inside the Toronto Zoo, and a bloody Hardcore Honeymoon Match pitting Skye Ashner against Tawny Blake! However, the highlight of the night remained inside the DCWL Arena, as Jack Ketch defeated Guido the Great to claim the DCWL Grand Championship inside of a UFC Octagon! On October 31st, the DCWL once again revisits the ON LOCATION concept, and once again, the event is using the DCWL Arena in Toronto as its home base. However, the attraction matches will be taking place in and around the Island of Hawaii, and the Grand Championship match itself will be contested on the Island. Below are the details of each ON LOCATION match, and the stipulations to obtain victory: <> PEARL HARBOUR MATCH FOR THE DCWL GRAND CHAMPIONSHIP TAWNY BLAKE (c) -vs- THE VINDICATOR Taking place on top of the USS Arizona Memorial (Photo Gallery available at http://www.nps.gov/usar/photos2/indexa.htm ), the war between The Vindicator and Tawny Blake will be waged under most unusual circumstances. A smaller replica of the standard DCWL wrestling ring will be secured on top of the Memorial, where the competitors will begin their battle. The first fall of the match (for both wrestlers) requires them to score a pinfall or submission inside the ring. Once the fall has been acquired, the winning wrestler must forcibly remove their opponent from the Memorial, and send them into the waters below. The match rules call for No Countouts, No Disqualifications, and No Time Limit. <> UNHOLY DEATH MATCH IN THE HAWAII SATANIST CHURCH JACK KETCH -vs- BEDROCK In a match that has been building up steam for nearly nine months, Bedrock and Jack Ketch will finally battle one-on-one, with no pesky security officers to pose as a distraction. The fitting location of the Hawaii Satanist Church (information available at http://www.satanist.net/hawaii/ ) serves as a battlefield, with the winner being the first man to score a 10-count Knockout in an Anything Goes fight to the finish! <> PU'UHONUA TEMPLE BRAWL THE CAPITOL -vs- MARK HALEY Dating back to their years in the WWA, the intense hatred between Mark Haley and The Capitol has never been truly resolved, despite the bloody wars engaged in by the two. The next chapter of their violent saga takes place in Honaunau, Hawaii, on the grounds of the Pu'uhonua Temple (Panoramic View available at http://www.letsgo-hawaii.com/honaunau/panorama.html ). The 'Temple of Refuge' will be anything but when inhabited by these two warriors, in a match that can only end in a 5-count Pinfall! <> BROKEN COCONUT MATCH FOR THE PLUNDERWEIGHT CHAMPIONSHIP KRACKERJAK (c) -vs- SKYE ASHNER In the first of two matches taking place inside the DCWL Arena, KrackerJak and The New Classic will revisit two-thirds of the memorable Plunderdome Experience, for the Plunderweight Championship! Four large coconuts will each be suspended over the four corners of the DCWL wrestling ring, and each coconut holds inside of it a mystery weapon to be used in the match once the coconut is opened by force! Pinfall is the only way to win this match, and like the previous matches, there is No Time Limit for this Plunderweight Championship Match! <> HALL OF FAME LEGENDS MATCH JOSE GARCES -vs- DALE 'THE MIRROR' STANWYCZ In the only straight-up wrestling match of the evening, the first and only inductee to the DCWL Hall of Fame meets a man who is unquestionably destined to be a future inductee, as Dale Stanwycz accepts the challenge of Jose Garces! Pride and bragging rights are on the line, as Garces was never able to score a victory over the current Number One Contender to the Grand Championship, and finally seeks to remove that blemish from his illustrious record! Five matches that could serve as legitimate Supercard Main Events anywhere on the planet, make up the lineup of an event that promises to eclipse any previous incarnation of ON LOCATION! It's an event that you simply cannot afford to miss! © 2003 - Demented Creations, Inc. http://www.geocities.com/dcwlwrestling/
