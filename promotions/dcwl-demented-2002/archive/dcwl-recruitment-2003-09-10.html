DCWL (Demented Creations, 2002–2003) — News

Recruitment Drive + Sample Card

10 September 2003
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Dear RSPWFites: Currently, the Demented Creations Wrestling League is seeking for a few good men (or women) to step into the squared circle of the DCWL. The promotion recently celebrated its first anniversary of operations, with a Supercard Extravaganza, "Cornerstone Revolution", which is available for viewing at: http://www.geocities.com/dcwlwrestling/cornerstone_2003.txt Please accept my invitation to explore the event, and consider making the DCWL a home for one of your characters. The story of the DCWL is that it is owned by a Toy Corporation, and operated with the plan of marketing its product line to the young fans. As an efed, we operate on a self-professed "part time schedule", with one flash packet sent weekly, a "live" event produced every other week, and Supercards/PPVs take place once every three months. Basically, our philosophy is quality over quantity, allowing plenty of time for angle/storyline planning, with a light schedule truly befitting of a hobby. In order to provide a full and entertaining experience, the DCWL maintains a limited roster, and every new arrival to the DCWL receives plenty of fedhead attention, to ensure they are properly integrated into the roster and the game as a whole. As a fedhead, I have been involved in our game since 1996, operating in only three promotions over those seven years. The DCWL opened its doors in June of 2002, and has been going strong ever since. I maintain a staff of at least three members at any given time, both to ensure the timely release of events, and to make sure one person's ego is not dominating the booking of the fed. Feel free to contact me with any questions you may have about the DCWL at [detail withheld], and I sincerely hope you will consider the DCWL as a potential ewrestling home! Sincerely, [name withheld] DCWL Kingfish http://www.geocities.com/dcwlwrestling/
