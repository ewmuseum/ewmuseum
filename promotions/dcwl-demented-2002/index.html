Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Demented Creations Wrestling League opened its doors in June 2002, founded by two former WWA fedheads once that promotion had closed. Its own recruitment post, marking its first anniversary that September with a supercard called Cornerstone Revolution, described a deliberately light workload — one flash packet a week, a “live” event every other week, and a supercard or pay-per-view once a quarter — run, in its own in-fiction premise, as a promotion owned by a toy corporation marketing its product line to young fans. Its own Kingfish kept a staff of at least three so that, in his own words, “one person's ego” would never dominate the booking.

Its surviving record centers on a Halloween 2003 special, On Location: Hawaii, reviving a concept the promotion traced back to the WWA's own touring supercards of 1999. Held out of its own DCWL Arena in Toronto with matches staged across the island of Hawaii, the card put the Grand Championship on the line atop the USS Arizona Memorial: Tawny Blake defending against The Vindicator in a match that required a pinfall inside a scaled-down ring first, then the winner physically forcing the loser off the Memorial and into the water below, no countouts, no disqualifications, no time limit.

The rest of the card matched it for spectacle: Jack Ketch and Bedrock settling a nine-month feud in an Unholy Death Match staged inside a Hawaii Satanist church, decided only by a ten-count knockout; The Capitol and Mark Haley carrying a years-old grudge into a five-count-pinfall brawl on the grounds of the Pu'uhonua Temple; KrackerJak defending the Plunderweight Championship against Skye Ashner in a Broken Coconut Match, four coconuts rigged with hidden weapons hanging over the ring's own corners; and Jose Garces, the DCWL Hall of Fame's first inductee, meeting Dale “The Mirror” Stanwycz in the one straight wrestling match of the night.

No result from On Location: Hawaii survives, and no further document carries the promotion's own record past this card; how it turned out, or how long Demented Creations Wrestling League continued afterward, isn't in the surviving record.
