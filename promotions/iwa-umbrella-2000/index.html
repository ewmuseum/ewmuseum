Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

On 29 January 2000, a body calling itself the IWA pitched itself not as a single federation but as an umbrella for e-wrestling federations “with a twist,” describing itself as newly established.

Its own plan named three federations it said it had already created to run under its banner — the XWO, the AJWA, and an AWA covering Japan and America — and sought people willing to run each one, with a shared roster of wrestlers meant to be subcontracted out across all three.

The same day it opened an application form twice, in two separately worded notices, promising to assign approved wrestlers to one of its member federations.

No further document survives. Whether any of the three federations the IWA announced were ever actually run, or a single wrestler assigned to one, is not recorded.
