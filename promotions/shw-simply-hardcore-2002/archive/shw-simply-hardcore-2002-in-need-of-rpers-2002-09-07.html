SHW In Need of a Few Good Rpers

7 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hello everybody. I am [name withheld], Co-Owner of Simply Hardcore Wrestling. Since late July, we have housed both established Rpers and ones just starting out.
However, recently, we have had a few of our main components leave for personal reasons. School has started, and the summer is over so it seems like many people have more important things to do. What we are looking for, is a few good men (or women) to have fun in our fed, not turn it into their lives.
I will now let you know what kind of fed we run... This is for original characters only, and you role-play by email. We look for both quality and quantity, and will accept applications from beginners as well as established and successful Rpers. The SHW has room for everyone with owners who help in making you better.
There are TWO shows a week. One on Saturday and one on Monday. Both shows are equal in terms of talent. A wrestler will no be booked for a match on both shows. That's two shows a week plus on Pay-Per-View event a month.
We have three owners. Myself, [name withheld], [name withheld], and [name withheld]. We are all former RPers who are now completely loyal to SHW.
We desperately need to have a bigger roster, as many of the men we have spoke to have found more important things to do. Please contact me at the following email address:
[detail withheld] . Send an email or IM me whenever you get a chance. We have a great website you can look at while you're deciding what to do. Here is the URL: www.angelfire.com/empire/SHW Please act now. Fill out an application, I'll look it over, and if you got what it takes we'll fit you into next Saturday's card! Thanks!
