IFWF — Card

Battleground, Part Seven

16 May 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
POLLARD: LADIES AND GENTLEMEN, THIS MATCH IS SCHEDULED FOR ONE FALL!!! AND IT IS FOR THE IFWF WORLD HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPIONSHIP!!!!!! FIRST, MAKING HIS WAY TO THE RING…..HAILING FROM THE CANADIAN ROCKIES….WEIGHING 239 POUNDS….CHRIS “HITMAN” THOMAS!!!! [“Cold Contagious” starts playing, and the Jumbotron starts playing various clips of Thomas in action. Thomas emerges from behind the curtain, wearing a black vest over his ring attire. The vest has the word “Hit” down one side and “Man” down the other. On the back, in silver, are the words “Lone
Wolf,” with a picture of a wolf’s head. He is also wearing two piece black wrestling tights. There are small, silver targets all over the outfit, with a huge, jagged, red one on his chest. The words “Canada’s Greatest Athlete” are on the seat of his pants in read and white, and the words, “Hit Man,” in blue, run down each leg…..]
REGENT: And here is Canada’s greatest athlete! BARLOW: So he believes…but everyone is entitled to their own opinions…. [While walking down the aisle, Thomas argues with a couple of fans at ringside. Thomas climbs into the ring, climbs the turnbuckle an throws his arms in the air, soaking in the boos from the crowd….]
POLLARD: AND HIS OPPONENT, FROM “THE HEART OF AMERICA,” WEIGHING 254 POUNDS….THE CURRENT IFWF WORLD HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPION….. ”FLAGMAN” JOE MELILLO!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! [“You Get What You Give” by the New Radicals begins to play….] BARLOW: And here comes the Heavyweight Champion! REGENT: He’s a chump! BARLOW: He is not! He is one of the more respectable champions we have had! REGENT: Yeah, more respectable than Engel! BARLOW: Yeah, maybe I should tell him you said that…. REGENT: What? Mark? He is one of the most respectable champs we have had! BARLOW: But you said… REGENT: What? BARLOW: Nevermind! [On the way to the ring, Flagman wears a leather jacket with “USA” written down the sleeves and a an American flag on the back. He also wears a star-spangled headband, and red, white, and blue wristbands and sunglasses.
He also wears a tunic-type trunk which are white on top, red on the bottom, and a blue chevron with stars in the middle. His boots have the American flag on the side…..]
[Melillo high-fives the fans as he jogs down the aisle. He carries a full-size American flag to the ring…..] BARLOW: And both with now…with a collar, elbow tie up. And Melillo with a beautiful armdrag! Thomas smiling…shaking his head…giving the champ some credit for that move. Melillo offering his hand to help Thomas up…but
Thomas grabs the ropes and helps himself up instead. Both men circling each other now…..both men lock up….and it’s Thomas this time with an armdrag!
Melillo gets up quickly….another armdrag and turns it into an armbar! Melillo is trying to stay at a vertical base…trying not to give Thomas any more leverage. REGENT: Looks like Thomas already has control of the match…. BARLOW: So far, he does. Melillo reverses the armbar….reversed again by
Thomas! Melillo scoops up Thomas and plants him on the mat with a bodyslam!
And he slaps on a Fugiwara armbar! He is going for the submission! And
Thomas quickly makes his way to the ropes and slides out of the ring!
REGENT: Smart move…don’t let Melillo get any momentum here…take a walk on the outside. Break the
Champs’ concentration…brilliant strategy. BARLOW: Are you done? REGENT: Yes, I am. BARLOW: Melillo holding the ropes open for Thomas…but Thomas wants no part of it. He walks up the steel steps and enters the ring. Thomas charges
Melillo….he pushes Melillo into the corner as they are locked up. The referee asking for the break. Thomas backing off…and he nails Melillo in the face with a right hand. And then a kick to the mid-section finds the mark! Follows that up with a series of European uppercuts! Thomas whips
Melillo into the turnbuckles on the opposite side…and follows in with a clothesline!
REGENT: He caught him with that one! BARLOW: And Thomas whips Melillo into the turnbuckles again….Melillo bounces off the turnbuckles…Thomas picks him up…BACKBREAKER!!!! And he is holding Melillo on the knee….pushing down on his legs and face.
REGENT: You just don’t bend that way, Tommy. BARLOW: The referee asking Melillo if he wants to give up. But the heavyweight champion is shaking his head! Thomas pushes Melillo off his knee and covers him!
ONE! TWO! BARLOW: No! The champion kicks out! Thomas picks up Melillo…hooks the tights…vertical suplex…and he is holding him in the air…letting the blood rush to his head…and he drops him! Thomas with another cover!
ONE! TWO! BARLOW: Melillo kicks out at two! Thomas setting Melilllo for a piledriver now….BUT MELILLO BACK DROPS HIM INSTEAD! Melillo grabs Thomas…put him between his legs…AND EXECUTES A PILEDRIVER OF HIS OWN!!!! He covers Thomas!
ONE! TWO! BARLOW: Thomas kicks out! Melillo picks up Thomas…whips him into the ropes…goes for a clothesline…Thomas ducks…he grabs Melillo…whips him into
the ropes now. Thomas goes for a clothesline…Mellilo ducks…comes off the other side…FLYING BODY PRESS…NO THOMAS POWERSLAMMED HIM!!! Great counter move! REGENT: That is what makes Thomas a great wrestler. He is always thinking in the ring. BARLOW: Thomas picks up Melillo by the hair….hooks the leg…RUSSIAN LEGSWEEP! NO! Melillo with an elbow! Melillo wraps his arms around Thomas’ waist….GUTWRENCH SUPLEX! And that move may buy the champ some time.
REGENT: He will need all the time he can get…. BARLOW: Both men to their feet! Melillo with a right hand! Another one! He whips Thomas into the ropes….BACK BODY DROP! He picks up Thomas…whips him into the ropes again…dropkick! Melillo covers Thomas!
ONE! TWO! REGENT: Thomas kicks out! BARLOW: Melillo whips Thomas into the turnbuckles….and Melillo spring off the ropes with A BULLDOG AS THOMAS COMES OFF THE TURNBUCKLES!!! We have a cover!
ONE! TWO! REGENT: Come on Thomas! BARLOW: We see you have your pick. Thomas signaling to the top…..he climbs the turnbuckles…Thomas is getting up…he nails Melillo in the gut…he climbs the turnbuckles….
…hooks his arm….ROCK BOTTOM FROM THE TOP!!!! OH MY! REGENT: What a move, Tommy! Now pin him, Chris! BARLOW: Thomas slowly crawls over for the cover! ONE! TWO! THRE… BARLOW: NO! MELILLO GETS HIS SHOULDER UP AT THE LAST SECOND! And Thomas thought he had him and he is letting the referee know about it. Thomas picks up Melillo…slams him to the mat! Now Thomas is going up top…he climbs the turnbuckles. He is perched up top now….ELBOW DROP FROM THE TOP!
REGENT: Perfectly executed! I thought the “Macho Man” was up there for a second! BARLOW: WE HAVE A COVER! ONE! TWO! THREE! BARLOW: NO! NO! MELILLO KICKED OUT! Thomas can’t believe it! Thomas picks up Melillo…he whips him into the ropes….goes for a clothesline…Melillo ducks…goes around the back of Thomas….GERMAN SUPLEX! NO! Thomas stops it…he goes around the back of Melillo! NO! Blocked by Melillo! GERMAN SUPLEX
FINALLY EXECUTED BY THE CHAMPION! ONE! TWO! THR.. BARLOW: NO! THOMAS KICKS OUT! Melillo picks up Thomas now…he whips Thomas into the ropes…reversed by Thomas….Thomas puts his head down…Melillo comes off the ropes…he catches Thomas…hooks the arms…BUTTERFLY SULPEX!!! He cover
Thomas!
ONE! TWO! TH… REGENT: Thomas kicks out again! BARLOW: Melillo picks up Thomas…SMALL PACKAGE! ONE! TWO! BARLOW: But a kickout by the challenger! Both men up…Thomas charges with a clothesline….Melillo ducks! Melillo with a right hand…swings and misses as
Thomas ducks that one! Thomas grabs Melillo by the hair…pulls his head back…DRAGON SLEEPER!!! DRAGON SLEEPER! And the champion is going down!
REGENT: Ring the bell…this one is over! BARLOW: Thomas has Melillo down on the mat in the center of the ring! The referee is checking on Melillo…he lifts his arm!
REGENT: IT FALLS! BARLOW: The referee lifts his arms again…. REGENT: IT FALLS AGAIN! ONE MORE CHRIS! BARLOW: THE REFEREE LIFTS HIS ARM AGAIN…. REGENT: IT FALLS AGAIN! BALOW: NO! IT DOESN’T! MELILLO IS STILL IN THIS ONE! Melillo rising to this feet! Elbow to the midsection! Another one! Melillo picks up
Thomas…BACK SUPLEX!!!! And both men are down…the referee laying in the count! ONE! TWO! THREE! FOUR! FIVE! SIX! SEVEN! BARLOW: Melillo starting to move… REGENT: And so is Thomas…come on Chris…get up! BARLOW: Both men fighting on their way to their feet…exchanging lefts and rights! Melillo locks on a side headlock….Thomas whips him into the ropes….Melillo off the ropes…kick to the mid-section by Thomas…he grabs the leg…FISHERMAN BUSTER! He nailed him! Thomas gets up…he is calling for the
Sharpshooter! He grabs Melillo’s legs….he wraps one leg around….Melillo kicks him off! And that sent Thomas through the ropes and to the outside!
REGENT: And look at Melillo he is following him…some champion he is. He is going for a count out win!
BARLOW: You don’t know that! REGENT: Sure I do…Melillo is a coward! BARLOW: Will you stop! Melillo smashes Thomas’ head into the steel ring barrier on the outside! Nails Thomas with a right hand….and now he is taking Thomas by us! The referee is counting both men…
ONE! TWO! THREE! REGENT: This could be good… BARLOW: Thomas caught Melillo with a shot to the gut….and HE SMASHES MELILLO’S FACE INTO OUR TABLE HERE AT RINGSIDE! Thomas grabs some cable cord and now he is choking Melillo! Come on, Chris! Stop that! And the referee has stopped the count and he is outside trying to get Thomas to release the choke! REGENT: No! Let them fight! When it comes to the title…if you really want it…you have to win it at any means necessary!
BARLOW: And I suppose you are going to join the Nation now? Thomas grabbed one of our monitors now! No! HE CHARGES MELILLO WITH THE MONITOR! MELILLO
KICKS THOMAS AND STOPS HIM! Melillo picks up the monitor….HE NAILS THOMAS
WITH IT!!! OH MY! He picks up Thomas…drags him on our table! I am getting out of here!
REGENT: Come on, Tommy! Now it’s getting good! BARLOW: Melillo is picks up Thomas…setting him up….PILEDRIVER!!! PILEDRIVER!!! And our table is wrecked into pieces! REGENT: What action, Tommy! BARLOW: Melillo crawls out of the wreck….Thomas is barely moving. Melillo grabs Thomas by the arm and drags him out! He picks him up….rolls him into the ring. Melillo climbs in the ring and slowly covers Thomas!
ONE! TWO! THREE! BARLOW: He got him! REGENT: NO! THOMAS GOT HIS FOOT ON THE BOTTOM ROPE! BARLOW: Unbelieveable! Melillo is stunned! I thought he had him! Melillo drags Thomas to the center of the ring….he grabs his leg….FIGURE FOUR LEG
LOCK! He has him in the center of the ring! And look at the pain on
Thomas’ face! He is trying to fight it! Thomas’ shoulders are down! The referee is in position!
ONE! TWO! REGENT: Thomas gets his shoulder up! BARLOW: Thomas fighting it again….Melillo nails him! Thomas’ shoulders are down again! ONE! TWO! REGENT: Thomas gets his shoulder up again! BARLOW: Thomas fighting it….trying to turn Melillo around…..and…he…DOES!!!! He turned the figure four around! And this time Melillo is the one in pain! REGENT: Wait! Thomas is unhooked Melillo’s legs…. BARLOW: THOMAS IS GOING FOR THE SHARPSHOOTER! REGENT: HE HAS HIM! HE HAS MELILLO IN THE SHARPSHOOTER!!!!! WE HAVE A NEW CHAMPION! BARLOW: The title could slowly be slipping away from Melillo’s waist! He is trying to make his way to the ropes! Melillo is getting closer! He is almost there!
REGENT: NO! THOMAS PULLED MELILLO BACK IN THE CENTER OF THE RING! BARLOW: The referee is asking Melillo if he wants to give it up! Melillo is shaking his head! But you can see the painful expression on his face.
The fans are going nuts here! Melillo turning himself toward the ropes….he is making his way to the ropes!
REGENT: Come on, Melillo! Give up! BARLOW: Melillo grabbed the bottom rope! The referee taps Thomas on the shoulder! Thomas thinks he won the belt! But the referee is telling him, no! Thomas is not paying attention to Melillo and Melillo is trying to get up. Thomas is irate! Thomas runs at Melillo….MELILLO PICKS HIM
UP….SPINEBUSTER!!!!! Melillo clutching his leg…still feeling the affect of that Sharpshooter by Thomas. Melillo slides out of the ring…he grabs Thomas by the leg and pulls him to the corner of the ring….
REGENT: What is Melillo doing? BARLOW: Melillo has the steel ring post between Thomas’ legs….Melillo grabs him leg…RINGPOST FIGURE FOUR!!!!
REGENT: Hey, that is Chris’ move! BARLOW: And Melillo is working over Thomas’ leg! The referee is outside, trying to break the hold!
REGENT: Count Melillo out, ref! BARLOW: Melillo breaks the hold! Thomas is clutching his leg now! Melillo slides back in the ring….he crawls over to Thomas…grabs his leg….KNEEBAR
SUBMISSION! And Thomas is screaming in pain! Thomas trying to get to the ropes! REGENT: He got the bottom rope! Now break the hold, ref! BARLOW: The referee is laying on the five count….but Melillo won’t let go! REGENT: That cheater! BARLOW: Melillo breaks the hold at four! Melillo picks up Thomas…slams him to the mat! And now Melillo is going up top! Thomas is pulling himself up by the ring ropes…Melillo climbing the turnbuckles with his back turned to
Thomas…Thomas is on his feet…he sneaks up behind Melillo….picks Melillo up on his shoulders….maybe going for a flapjack now….NO MELILLO WITH A VICTORY
ROLL! ONE! TWO! THR.. BARLOW: NO! THOMAS KICKED OUT! Both men scramble to their feet! Melillo picks up Thomas…Thomas falls behind Melillo! Melillo turns around….
REGENT: LASERLIGHT!!! BARLOW: NO! Melillo pushes him into the ropes….FLAGMAN FOREARM!!!! HE NAILED THOMAS WITH THE FLAGMAN FOREARM!!!! REGENT: I heard you the first time! BARLOW: Thomas slowly getting up….Melillo LOCKS IN THE FLAGSTAFF!!!!! THOMAS IN TROUBLE!!!! REGENT: NO!! BARLOW: THE REFEREE CALLS FOR THE BELL! THOMAS GAVE UP!!! [The bell rings….] BARLOW: MELILLO RETAINS THE BELT!!!! POLLARD: LADIES AND GENTLEMEN, THE WINNER OF THE MATCH….AND SSTTTTIILLLL
THE IFWF HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPION…..”FLAGMAN” JOE
MELILLO!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! [“You Get What You Give” by the New Radicals starts to play…the fans cheer…] BARLOW: What a match! Thomas gave it his all….but Melillo had more heart tonight and he walks away STILL Heavyweight Champion.
REGENT: Oh…he got lucky. BARLOW: Wait…Thomas is walking over to Melillo…extending his hand for a show of respect. What a display of sportsmanship!
REGENT: Awww….Thomas is becoming a softie now, huh? BARLOW: Will you stop it. Melillo looking to the crowd…..and they shake hands! And Thomas is leaving….
REGENT: He is probably going back to wash his hand….who knows where Melillo’s hand was before the match! BARLOW: You never stop do you? REGENT: Nope…. BARLOW: Well we have had a handful of great matches tonight... REGENT: But the biggest one is up next!! BARLOW: Fans, we thought the day would never get here, but we're about to make wrestling history! For the first time ever, one on one, Brian Knight will face
Joe Petrow in a non-sanctioned, no holds barred, grudge match to end all grudge matches!
REGENT: These two guys have vowed to try to kill each other in that ring, and
I'm not so sure that it's just idle threats.....These two hate each other like no two athletes ever have!
BARLOW: I just wonder how such a match can even end? Will it be one fall? Last man standing? I Quit? How can it be over while one of these two is still able to move?
REGENT: Hopefully Commissioner Stewart will shed some light on that. He just entered the ring and accepted the microphone from Michael Pollard. I guess it's time to make history!
BARLOW: Let's go to the ring!
