New Fed: The Hardcore Wrestling Alliance

12 August 2000
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I have run a few feds, and I took a rest from running them a few months ago, but I have decided to run once once again, and I have made the website.
Http://members.aol.com/[detail withheld]/main.html
It has only been open a good couple of hours, and already we have a Lita, a
Jeff Hardy, and Triple H, but don't let that fool you...We also accept Fake wrestlers, and in fact we are looking for a few good Real and a few good Fake
Wrestlers. The page will be updated daily, and I will Role Play, as the President to further angles, also I will be on hand to help in case you have any questions or you would like advice to help further your Role Playing and have a better time winning.
Also, I will feature hand written cards. I also am the player of James Storm, and perhaps you saw my free agent post a little while back. That can show you my writing abilities, and hopefully it will be good enough writing to please you, but it isn't Zeus Pro, or TNM7, so you don't have to worry.
There will be four championships, The World Heavyweight, The USA-Canadian, The
World Tag Team, and The Hardcore.
If there are any other questions, just ask me at the site. James Storm -Canadian Hero- [detail withheld]
