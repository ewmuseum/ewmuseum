Both men return from retirement to settle the score once and for all, with long time rival of both men, FANG acting as the special guest referee. Anything can happen as TOMMY
RIFLES and ERIC COOPER go head to head for the _last_ _time._ PEOPLES' CHOICE MATCH "Iron Man" Eddie Jacobs versus Andrew "Flash" Tucker The fans, the critics, the suits, and even the wrestlers who make this business great came together and voted for the match they wanted to see most. Here it is... it's happened before, but I'm sure EDDIE JACOBS and ANDREW TUCKER will have no trouble tearing each other apart one more time for the enjoyment of everyone in attendance.
GLASS TABLE MATCH "Sadistic" Shane Alexander versus "The Handler" Liam Rogan SHANE ALEXANDER signed an open contract to face anyone in a Glass Table Match. Whether it was for the money, the sheer brutality of the match, or just the desire to kick some ass we do not know... but LIAM ROGAN accepted the contract and is prepared for violence!
SCIENTIFIC RULES MATCH "Queen of Extreme" Heidi Leick versus "Tornado" Tara Smith A rivalry that seems to never end. These two have fought many wars with HEIDI LEICK usually edging here nemesis out. Well twice before they've fought under Heidi's rules, but now the tables have been turned. These two are going at it under TARA SMITH'S terms, and it should be one for the record books.
IRON MAN MATCH "Screaming Drillbit" Jason Keening versus Mystery Opponent In an hour long battle to see who can score the most falls, we have the most enigmatic contest of the evening. [name withheld] contacted JASON KEENING telling him he'd face the challenge of a lifetime. Rumor has it that
[name withheld] has yet to find an opponent for the "Screaming Drillbit", but the
CEO of the event claims otherwise... he says the MYSTERY OPPONENT will shock the world.
DOUBLE MAIN EVENT Part 1: 3-WAY ELIMINATION STYLE DREAM TAG TEAM MATCH "Top Dog" Rick Styles and Nina Grimsson versus Damage Inc. versus Alex Extreme and Luke Kinsey Somehow this wacky combination of tag teams was assembled, as one mixed tag team, one established tag team, and one team of high flyers attempt to out-duel each other. Will the two un-established tag teams be able to work together? Will Nina be able to keep up with two teams of the most competitive men in wrestling? Will the arena still be standing after this one?
Part 2: ULTIMATE CAGE MATCH "Last American Badass" Alex Martinez versus "Crimson" Joe Reed Two of wrestling's greats go at it in the most classic, dangerous, and exciting match ever designed... The STEEL CAGE MATCH! ALEX MARTINEZ, a man who's consider the best in the business these days takes on the man who's been labeled a has-been of late. JOE REED attempts to prove the world wrong.
