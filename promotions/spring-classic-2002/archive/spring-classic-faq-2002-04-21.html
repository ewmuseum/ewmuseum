An Independent Supercard (FAQ version 1)

21 April 2002

Hello all, I will keep my opening comments brief because the FAQ does a good job of explaining everything to you, but I'd like to invite you all to fill out an application and also take special notice to the
DREAM/GRUDGE/PEOPLES' CHOICE Match section. The more ideas and requests I get, the better the chances of an extremely exciting event.
I don't think it wold be a bad idea for people to post match suggestions on the newsgroup. That way handlers can see what matches other people want them to participate in, and decide if that's something they'd be interested in. Thanks, and enjoy the FAQ.
[name withheld] ---------- Spring Classic '02 : LEGEND! FAQ version 1 I. Who? II. Why? III. What? IV. How? V. Apply! *SECTION I: Who? [name withheld] and YOU! That's who. For those of you saying to yourselves: "Who the hell is this [name withheld] dude?", let me enlighten you. I, am [name withheld] :-)
I've been active as a handler in e-wrestling since 1994 (wow, I need a _real_ life), and as a card/match writer for almost as long. My most well-known creations include the character Fang who's my longest standing character. He's competed in UEW, WWO, GSWP, and many others.
He's currently active in GWC/MPW and is a participant in Wrestlebowl IV. My most recent other character is Skylar Dalton who competed in EMWC and was runner up in the Wicked Voodoo: Urban Legends tournament. I've also been a fed-head for a few different feds, most noted TAW (Totally Awesome Wresting 95-97) and TSC (The Squared Circle 2000). And as for you, well, you know who you are, and I humbly invite you to work with me on what can be a whole lot of fun.
*SECTION II: Why? There are several reasons why. 1. I enjoy booking and running angles as the primary reason why I participate in this hobby. I also love handling my character, which prevents me from becoming a full-time fed-head. This gives me an outlet to run a major event, a set of angles beginning to end, and no obligation to keep going once the event is complete.
2. Wrestlebowl IV is coming out soon, and all roleplaying and stratting is done. I figured it'd be a perfect chance for either those who missed out in Wrestlebowl to participate, or for those who enjoyed Wrestlebowl and are looking for another supercard experience.
3. The card also serves as an outlet to work with people I've never worked with before. I am sure I will also be working with some I've worked with in the past, and I'm looking forward to that, but I am extremely excited to have the opportunity to work with new handlers and see how people out of my current loop get things done.
4. One section of the card is the DREAM/GRUDGE Match section. I am VERY excited about it. You'll read exactly what it is later on, but again it allows characters who may not be in any feds together to run a feud or just have a dream match. OR, it allows two handlers who may not like each other to square off in an unbias arena. One where there is no fed allegiance or favoratism.
5. Why not? *SECTION III: What? Spring Classic '02 Depending on the success if this event, there will be a Spring Classic every year. It's a two-night supercard that takes place in early June featuring the best e-wrestling has to offer from the widest variety of loops. This years' theme is "LEGEND!"
The Card - LIVE from WEMBLEY STADIUM, Wembley, England JUNE 6 and JUNE 7 !!!NIGHT ONE!!! The LEGEND! Cup (L-Cup) tournament: This is an 8-man tournament to name wrestling's first _real_ _undisputed_ legend. ROUND 1 Participant #1 vs. Participant #8 Participant #4 vs, Participant #5 Participant #2 vs. Participant #7 Participant #3 vs, Participant #6 ROUND 2 Participant #1/#8 vs. Participant #4/#5 Participant #2/#7 vs. Participant #3/#6 FINALS - STEEL CAGE MATCH Semi-final winner #1 vs. Semi-final winner #2 !!!NIGHT TWO!!! DREAM/GRUDGE/PEOPLES' CHOICE Matches! 8 Action packed mathces decided by the fans or the wrestlers. Stipulations are always welcome. Have a handler whose character you want to destroy, but can't 'cause the feds you're in are both bias towards the respective characters?
Bring it here! Know a character you love and have always wanted to face off with? Fight him here! Have a feud that seems like it'll never end? End it here! Want a feud? Start it here! Have a match that you'd LOVE to see? Suggest it here! Suggest it on the newsgroup... suggest it to the handlers. Let's make it happen!
That's what NIGHT TWO is all about! *SECTION IV: How? It all gets started with a few applications and a few match suggestions and requests. How are results determined? There will be 3 Flash Packets. The first will be released three weeks before the event. The second two weeks before the event, and the last on the day prior to the event (but will be due a week before the event). These flashes will be a major factor in determining results.
A strategy (strat) will be due with the final RP, a week before the event. A creative strat goes a LONG way.
Angles - Angles may be the most important of the three. A solid angle in the tournament or your dream match is extremely ehlpful. These are what will drive the card and make it most fun for me to write.
Therefore, if you write have a good one, I will like you and you will win :-) The card will be put together and written by myself and a staff of writers. *SECTION V: Apply!
