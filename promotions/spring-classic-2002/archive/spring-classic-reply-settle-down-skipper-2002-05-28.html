Whoa, whoa, whoa.. settle down there, skipper. First of all, I don't play God.. I leave that to Mat. All I said was, it's fucking pompous to think that your supercard is so important that it'll have the first ever, undisputed legend in e-wrestling. I figure.. to have your character become a legend, you gotta do X number of things over X number of years and well, not just win some "fun" or "shitty" or whatever adjective will be placed in front of "tournament". If there is such a thing as a legend in this hobby, there already are quite a few.. and they've worked hard for it. I named a few, there are many more.. yadda yadda. So stop cryin'.. do your card, have fun and the such.. but please don't get all wah wah when somebody points out a travesty in the making.
