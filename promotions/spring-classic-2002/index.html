Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Spring Classic ’02: LEGEND! introduced itself in April 2002 as an independent supercard: two nights in early June, no federation behind it, and an open call to handlers from every loop. Its FAQ promised a Spring Classic every year if the first one worked, and gave its reasons plainly — a chance to book a major event from beginning to end with no obligation to keep going once it was over, and a neutral arena where characters from feds that never crossed could finally meet.

The first night belonged to the LEGEND! Cup, an eight-man single-elimination tournament billed as naming wrestling’s first real undisputed legend, its final set inside a steel cage. The second night was left to the audience: Dream, Grudge and Peoples’ Choice matches suggested on the newsgroup and agreed by the handlers involved. The first-legend billing drew scorn on the newsgroup within days of the FAQ’s repost; the event’s answer was that it was a promotional tactic.

The lineup of 2 June 2002 set the show at the SkyDome in Toronto rather than the FAQ’s Wembley Stadium, which a reader had noted was closed for redevelopment, and filled the L-Cup bracket with entrants billed from GWC/MPW, the NIWF, the ICW, NEO, IWF/WoW and BSCW. Night of Stars stacked seven matches alongside it: two retired rivals returning for a Battle of the Ages, a glass table match, a scientific rules match, an hour-long Iron Man match against an opponent nobody named, a three-way dream tag match, and an Ultimate Cage Match between Alex Martinez and “Crimson” Joe Reed.

Results were to be decided by three flash packets, a final strategy and the angles each handler brought, and at least one entrant’s first flash reached the newsgroup: twelve silent minutes of a veteran at his desk, ending in a single line. On 16 June 2002, before either night was held, the event was called off. The L-Cup was never contested, and no match on the card took place.
