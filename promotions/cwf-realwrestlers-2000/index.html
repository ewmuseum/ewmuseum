Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

A real-and-created-wrestler promotion calling itself CWF was advertised across the year 2000 by the same recruiter each time, who framed a June relaunch as the return of “the original founder of these small feds,” run over e-mail before that history began.

That June 2000 relaunch pitch asked members to nominate their own top ten wrestlers from “the Big Three real life feds” for open roster slots, promising a 1 July start with no favoritism since neither the recruiter nor his writing staff would wrestle themselves; three names — Rikishi, British Bulldog and Chris Jericho — are recorded as already claimed.

By November the promotion had relaunched again, message-board based this time and mixing real and created wrestlers, advertised across a November ad and a December repost; a further relaunch notice went out again at the very end of December, describing itself as barely a month old and unlikely to close “anytime soon.”

No card, roster confirmation, or result survives from any of the year's relaunches. How many times this CWF actually opened its doors, and how or when it finally closed, is not known.
