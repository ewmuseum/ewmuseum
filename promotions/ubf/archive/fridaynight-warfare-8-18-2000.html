FRIDAYNIGHT WARFARE Friday August 5, 2000 — 7:00 p.m. Central Time (the fed's own announced lineup; posted results below are dated August 18, 2000) 1st Match: Heywood Vs. Mean Green 2nd Match: Crisis Vs. Iceman (Isolation match with Carnage in cage) 3rd Match: Eric Truth Vs. Iron Warrior 4th Match: D-Strange Vs. Chaos 5th Match: Stone Cold Vs. Eric Truth 6th Match: Phrog Vs. Grand Puba 7th Match: Kurt Angle Vs. Inferno 8th Match: Mr. Ass Vs. Anarchist RESULTS — Friday Night Warfare Results-Aug.18,2000 Match 1: Heywood Vs. Mean Green Winner=Heywood Via Chairshot Heywood ties Mean Greens wrist and ankles to the ring post with barbe wire and delivers the "Gag Order". Match 2: Cris Crisis Vs. Iceman (Isolation match w/ Carnage and Eric Truth in cage) Winner=Crisis Via DQ Crisis is just about to unlock the cage to release Carnage as Iceman lay on the floor. Suddenly, the ladder is kicked over by Kyle Kash sending Crisis to the floor, causing a DQ. ***Commercials*** (camera comes into focuse on Ring Announcer Cameron Spayze who is walking around backstage with Jett Pearson. As they pass numerous dressing room doors, they stop at the sound of MonstaMac's voice. Surprised at how clearly his vioce can be heard from inside the room, they eavsdrop on his conversation. Overheard: Yes, DeRose Florist? I'd like a dozen roses delivered to the UBF Plaza as soon as possible...yes, a dozen roses...(long pause)...No, nothing more. O.k., thank you. Bye-bye. Cameron Spayze: Aw, how sweet. He's ordering me flowers. Dick Meyers: What?! Those aren't for her! Those are for Mrs. Pearson! Cameron: Come on Jett, I gotta get ready. Dick: What the h^ll is she thinking?! And more importantly, what will be the end result if she gets her hands on those roses? Match 3: Eric Truth Vs. Iron Warrior Winner=Iron Warrior Via Pin Match 4: D-Strange Vs. Chaos Winner=Chaos Via "Knock-out" Chaos wins due to a "knock-out" after placing Strange on a table outside the ring and delivering a diving headbutt from the top rope. Match 5: Stone Cold Vs. Eric Truth Winner=Stone Cold Via "Stuuner & Pin" ***Commercials*** (camera comes into focus backstage where Donna Pearson and Cameron Spayze are fighting in a dressing room. After a few minutes, Donna is thrown onto a couch, next to the couch is a table with a vase on it. As Cameron is about to Jump on her, Donna smacks her in the head, knocking her out cold. It doesn't stop there, however as now Donna has placed the stem of a rose across Cameron's throat. And that stem is covered in thorns! After being pulled apart by security, Cameron lay bleeding on the floor unconcious. Peramedics rush in to stop the bleeding on Cameron's throat. After placing Cameron on a stretcher, peramedics are broken up by D-Strange. D-Strange: It' Okay! It's okay! I know CPR! (D-Strange rushes to Cameron's side and begins kissing her franticly. Scene cuts back to areana.) Match 6: Phrog Vs. Grand Puba Winner=Grand Puba Via "Pin" Match 7: Kurt Angle Vs. Inferno Winner=Inferno Via "Pin" Match 8: Mr. Ass Vs. Anarchist Winner=Anarchist Via "Interance by Ty Rannt and a spiarl bomb onto announcer's table.
