Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

UBF billed itself under a bare three-letter name its own held corpus never expands, while branding all four of its titles "Bloodfest" throughout. Its home page describes it as "a brand new federation" as of December 2000, run by a president known on the site only as MonstaMac, with two weekly shows -- Fridaynight Warfare and Saturday Night Showcase.

Heywood Jablowme held the fed's own Bloodfest Heavyweight Title -- the only reign the held corpus records, with no earlier history surviving. Fridaynight Warfare (18 August 2000) stages a roster mixing original characters against a scattering of real WWF-era names -- Kurt Angle and Mr. Ass both wrestle undercard matches, alongside a backstage vignette that tangles the fed's own president up in a mistaken-flowers subplot between two of the show's own on-air personalities.
