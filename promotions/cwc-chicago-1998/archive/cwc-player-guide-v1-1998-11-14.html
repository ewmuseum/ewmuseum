The CWC in General CWC stands for Chicago Wrestling Corporation, and is one of the many imaginary fantasy wrestling leagues in cyberspace, in other words, the internet. The CWC exists to provide wrestling fans with a creative and a enjoyable gaming format. Ever dream of putting on tights and walking down that long aisle into the squared circle? Well, CWC is a fed where you create your own wrestling character and guide him to the World Title ... Or to nowhere. :) CWC was created by [name withheld]. Brett's first organzation was the Elite Wrestling Corporation. Brett founded it on June 19, 1998, and during the month of September, Brett began Co-Running it with [name withheld]. However, Brett on November 14, 1998 handed it over the EWC to Matt and Matt only to start up his own promotion. That very same day, the 14th of November, the CWC was born. [name withheld] has also roleplayed in such feds as the IWF/WOW, AWMC, MVW, N.E.W., the INWI, NOW, and plus many others. So, it's safe to say your President has experience in the front office. II. Rules The CWC will not allow any "Out of the World" gimmicks. Meaning, the President will not tolerate wrestlers with gimmicks that are too far fetched. For example, I don't want any plumbers or clowns in our league. _NO_ comic book gimmicks, just keep it plain and simple. As a general rule of the thumb, applicants whose wrestlers are similar characters in the league that are already established in the CWC, are not admitted, even if they are the top roleplayers. I mean, would you want to have five undertakers running around? _ALL_ wrestlers must be original and not taken off any "real" wrestlers. (i.e. No "Stone Cold's", "Nature Boy's", or "Canadian Crippler's".) You can only submit either One Singles Wrestler or One Tag Team in the CWC. People tend to enter four or five characters into one league and only pay attention to one. This is a problem in the CWC we have eliminated. Also keep in mind that if your character is in inactive for two straight weeks, he will be removed from the CWC Roster. In order to ensure each player recieves sizable activity and particpation in the CWC, the roster must be kept relatively small. CWC allows 24 singles and 6 tag teams. This allows each wrestler a match once a week and the attention the player deserves in a league. Upon being admitted to the CWC, the President will ask for a sample roleplay. Here is an idea of what is expected of in your sample roleplay. Vitrone [HHWO, INWI, N.E.W.] III. Particpation Particpation in the CWC is essential and the key to success. Roleplaying (See Roleplaying Regulations Below) takes up much of that particpation. You are required to submit a minimum of one solid quality flash a week. This is your major flash, meaning it's your big time flash, that developes your character. Read Roleplaying Regulations below for full understanding of your roleplaying standards. Other ways of particpation is sending in strats, sneak attacks, and getting involved in your current angles and storylines. We'll get into more detail as the league progresses. IV. In addition, there are three features that come out during the week: - CWC Update (Sat.) Home of next weeks lineup, news & reviews. Other exciting things. - CWC "First Come, First Serve" (Mon.) Some roleplays from your favorite CWC superstars. - CWC "Statin' the Facts" - Countdown to Saturday Night Live (Fri.) A televised show featuring the roleplays of the CWC. CWC Roleplay Regulations CWC, emphasis on, good writing=good push. A long roleplay doesn't neccessarliy mean that is a good roleplay. A well written quality 6k roleplay can beat out a long, dull, boring, and dragging 12k roleplay any day of the week. Though, the longer the roleplay, the better. Remember the three most important things when creating a roleplay. Content, Length, and Detail. Your flashes will be sent out every Monday in the feature "First Come, First Serve" or put into the flash show "Statin' the Facts" - Countdown to Saturday Night Live, depending on arrival in my mailbox. Failure to produce roleplays of any sort, whether they be interviews, strats, or whatever, in a short period of time, will result in a cautionary warning from the President, and if the inactivity continues, your wrestler may be suspend or fired by CWC altogether. VI. Ground Rules Nobody is stopping you from having fun, and that is the main reason you come to CWC, is to have fun. But like all good things, there must be rules that each player must abide by, in consideration of CWC Council and your fellow players. Please follow these regulations: DON'T use another player's wrestler, unless there has been an OK by the other party and by the President. Furthermore, don't ask for your wrestler to hosptialize another wrestler unless there has been consent from the other party and the President. DON'T attack announcers or any of the CWC staff, unless there has been express permission by the President. You can attack as many no name jobbers as you want. DON'T use excessive profanity. Often when you go into realization of your character, obscene language is all part of the game when it is hot and heavy that your wrestler is going through. However, dirty and very vulgar words will be edited out in your flashes. Words such as "ass" and "damn" will be allowed, though stronger words than it will not. You have to keep in mind that such language is not acceptable in television programmings. It is very possible to express your anger and extreme emotions without using a string of four letter words. Any character handled by [name withheld], or [name withheld], may have nothing to do with this company! Since [name withheld] is a egotistical assmonkey and the epitome of ignorance, and [name withheld] is the 'lamest' piece of garbage lying around, I say, they may have nothing to do with this chicago-based company. And if they don't want to anyway [which I am guessing] Rock on! VII. How does the CWC operate? CWC is a ''booked'' league. Meaning the match results are predetermined by the CWC council. Results are not made up of, with that single match in mind. Generally, it accounts for the bigger picture, meaning the angles, storylines, and so forth. And your basis of roleplaying helps a tremendous way in helping the match results. However, for instance, two good roleplayers have a match against each other, both have been flashing all week, and now it comes to conclusion that the President will take it upon themselves to determine results with a coherent storyline in mind. VIII. Titles The Chicago Wrestling Corporation currently sanctions three singles titles: - CWC Skyweight Championship Current Holder: Vacant. (The Cruiserweight Title of the CWC. Weight limit 225 pounds.) - CWC Cook County Championship Current Holder: Vacant. (Universal Weight. The #2 Title in the EWC.) - CWC Chicago Championship Current Holder: Vacant. (Universal Weight. The #1 Title in the CWC. If you are the Chicago Champion you are the greatest wrestler by far in the CWC.) The CWC also sanctions one Tag Team Title: - CWC Chicago Tag Championship Current Holder: Vacant (Universal Weight. #1 Tag Team Title in the CWC.) Titles can only change hands by pinfall or submission, unless specified before the match. IX. Rankings CWC uses a point system for its rankings. Once getting admitted to CWC, the wrestler starts off with zero points and works his way up by getting victories. A wrestler is not deducted any points if he loses. Point System are as follows: [Credit to [name withheld], the first man to give points for winning titles.] +5 for winning the Chicago Championship +5 for winning the Cook County Championship +3 for winning the Skyweight Championship +1 for a win +0 for a draw -1 for a loss -3 for losing the Skyweight Championship -5 for losing the Cook County Championship -5 for winning the Chicago Championship X.
