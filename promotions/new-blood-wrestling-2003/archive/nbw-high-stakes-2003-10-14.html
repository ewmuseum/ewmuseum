NBW High Stakes, 9-30-03

14 October 2003

Hey folks, As most of you know, NBW has been running strong for about three months now. On the advice of a friend, we never posted to RSPWF during our first cycle, because we didn't want to be one of those feds that came out like gangbusters and broke down in the first few weeks... so now that our second supercard is done, I've decided to post it here on RSPWF. http://nbw.[name withheld].com/NBWHighStakes.txt If you go to nbw.[name withheld].com -- you can see all of our past cards, and it'll be updated to reflect the ongoings of High Stakes within the week. If you're interested in getting on the mailing list, it's at www.yahoogroups.com/group/nbwgroup and just add yourself to the list. Enjoy the show. Ameet
