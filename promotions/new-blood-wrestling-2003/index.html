Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

New Blood Wrestling self-hosted its own full card archive online, and by October 2003 described itself as “running strong for about three months now,” placing its own start near the middle of the year.

Its own supercard, High Stakes, was announced on 14 October 2003 as “the defination of all things NBW,” a card “stretch[ing] from the purely insane to some of the best techincal wresltign ever,” naming five gimmick matches on the bill: a hardcore match between two veteran Japanese wrestlers, a Stairway to Hell match, a Last Man Standing match, a Falls Count Anywhere match, and a Crown of Thorns match, alongside a companion roleplay show, Flashpoint.

The card itself is not reproduced here; its own results lived on the promotion's self-hosted site. The newsgroup's own community poll still carried New Blood Wrestling's roster as late as 27 January 2004, the last trace of it in the surviving record.
