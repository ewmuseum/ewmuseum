N.E.W. — News

Memorial Cup Tournament Participants

28 March 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Gentlemen, First off, I would like to thank everyone for applying. I received a good amount of applications, numbering somewhere in between 25-30 last time I checked. It was a difficult process, but I believe J.R. Hollis and I have done the best job possible selecting the participants. Second, I would like to announce that the tournament will be moving back in its date. It will take May 1st, live from the USF Sundome in Tampa, Florida. This is to allow more time for RP's, and will allow more time to generate heat between the contestants. Third, on Wednesday night at 5 pm, I will release the matches and brackets for the tournament via email. And finally, I would like to say that RPing by the contestants may begin now, if you so desire. RP shows will be released every Saturday in a show entitled "On the Spot," starting with this Saturday. If we get a surplus of RP's, we will release more shows during the week. And now, the contestants. They are arranged in alphabetical order by last name: #1 Creed #2 Nick Demola #3 Fang #4 Eddie Jacobs #5 Jeff Matthews #6 Mystery Man #1 #7 Mystery Man #2 #8 Steve Owens #9 James "Simply the Best" Pillow #10 Alex Reaver #11 "Money Driven" Mike Sebastian #12 Rick Styles #13 Stonebreaker #14 Andrew "Flash" Tucker #15 Jack Vars #16 V.D. Vitrone Thanks to all who applied. Remember, the event will take place May 1st, and RPing may begin now. GF
