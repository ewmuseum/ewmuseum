And when you throw all of this together, you definitely don't need more...well, there's more anyway! The stipulation for this match is a SUBMISSION MATCH, first man to get a submission wins. This matchup promises to be highly anticipated and watched, as it is the first
N.E.W. sanctioned match in almost FIFTEEN MONTHS!
Promoted by: Jeremy Baltimore NOTHINGFACE, STEPHEN C. BOURDO, and LAWRENCE WILLIAMS will clash in another mat classic that the fans will definitely be ready for. Will the sociopathic
Nothingface be able to tackle the caniving Bourdo? Or will ring veteran
Williams mechanically dismember them all? Regardless, these questions will all be answered, as they try their luck in a TEXAS DEATH MATCH, where two men have to be unable to answer the ten count before a winner is declared!
Promoted by: Brandon Bloom In a match that just screams "violence", ALEX HAWTHORNE, LIAM ROGAN, and VANDAL
GOMEZ will lock horns in what promises to be the first bloodbath N.E.W. fans have seen in ages. And there's a special stipulation too, to make sure it lives up to the hype. A special Triangle First Blood match. FIRST BLOOD
MATCH? Not talking about a little scrape on the knee here. When we say blood,
WE MEAN BLOOD! Last man not bleeding, wins.
Promoted by: Steve Miller CHRISTIAN MADISON and TYRONE PARKER, two Cruiserweight Greats known in federations across the globe, will draw a line in the sand to see who the man really is. The only catch is, they have a special opponent in this match, a
MYSTERY MAN yet to be announced. And to make matters even more confusing, a
FALLS COUNT ANYWHERE stipulation has been stamped on this match, making it nearly impossible to control!
Promoted by: James Pillow And in the final Triangle Match of the evening, we'll have a pair of giants,
JACK CODY and TONY PASENO ready to rip each others throats out. Throw in the third challenger, the crafty MADISON J. VALENTINE, and you have what has the makings for a wrestling classic. However, when you lock these three beasts inside a STEEL CAGE and let them fly, you know the action can only be contained for so long!
Promoted by: [name withheld] MAIN EVENT One wrestler from each camp will square off in a special Five-Way Elimination
Style match, winner is sure to be a top contender for the N.E.W. Heavyweight
Title when it is decided.
From Camp Baltimore - "Candyman" Damian Rose From Camp Bloom - "Homeless" Joe Boxcar From Camp Miller - "Cold Blooded" Chris Courtade From Camp Pillow - Rick Stevens From Camp Russell - "Incomparable" Dylan Rapture The lineup says it all. These five men will be desperately trying to make an impression on the fans by being in the first Main Event on N.E.W.'s
"Resurrection" card. Wrestlers are eliminated by pinfall, submission, disqualification, or count-out.
Promoted by: The N.E.W. Committee iii. Deadlines The deadline to get your flashes in for N.E.W.'s flash show, ON THE SPOT, will be Tuesday, June 27 at 11:59p.m. PST.

and can cut a full inring with *their* promoter on hand if they're in the Main
Event) will be Thursday, June 29 at 11:59p.m. PST.
