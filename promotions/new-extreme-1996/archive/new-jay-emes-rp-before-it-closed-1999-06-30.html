N.E.W. — Roleplay

A Roleplay Written Before It Closed

30 June 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
This roleplay was made for N.E.W. before it closed down. ========================================== [The camera fades into black. A short buzz is heard in the background as the darkness remains. While the buzz continues to pierce through the speakers, an old black and white picture of former N.E.W. CEO GoodFella emerges onto the screen. The mid-50 year old man is standing in the ring he made famous, smiling all the while. The picture slowly fades and the old intro to N.E.W. Global
Warfare starts up.]
["Victory" by Sean "Puffy" Combs begins playing and the opening scene begins as the previous narration ends. You can hear the sound of a beating heartbeat behind a black background. It then fades, and shows highlights of various
N.E.W. superstars doing particular moves. Such as "Kodiac" Dan Harris falling of the ladder, over the top rope and through the table outside the ring.
"Desire" Brandon Bloom pinning "Hollywood" Hogan in front of 100,000 fans in the Pontiac Silverdome.]
[The pulsating heartbeat is shown once again, and it fades into a clip of
"Simply the Best" James Pillow turning on the Fellas. "Vindicator" Vinny
Vitrone breaking a crutch over the head of a masked member of the CRACK Connection. It shows Brass Knuckles and Jim Whitten battling on top of a scaffold. GlacierIce nailing a Northern Lights Suplex on Cactus Jack from the top rope and through the table, crushing Destroyer who was underneath the table. Poison Kiss blowing a kiss to the camera. Eddie Jacobs leaping off the
Slam Dance turn table, and landing right on top of GlacierIce. Eddie Jacobs winning the Intercontinental Title from Balial as the crucified Balial slowly rises to the rafters.]
[The pulsating heartbeat is shown once more, beating faster and more rapidly. It fades once again, this time into "Stylish" Steve Milla, Balial, and Tank in a three way side headlock. It shows James Pillow holding up the Heavyweight
Championship Belt. It shows Ice & Ice raising the Tag Team titles over their heads and celebrating with "Sweet" Sandy. Another highlight of Ian Walsh pinning Steve Milla is shown. "Dynasty" Drew Snyder applying the Cowboy Clutch on an unfortunate opponent. Ian Walsh cracks a chair over President GoodFella's head. It then shows a shot of Drew Snyder turning against Assaf Levavy and putting him in the Cowboy Clutch. And finally, it shows James Pillow pinning
Vinny Vitrone to win the Heavyweight Title for his second time, and it shows
Vinny Vitrone holding up the N.E.W. Championship after winning it back at
Desert Storm 2.]
[The images finally fade, and darkness returns to the screen. Then, suddenly a voice is heard. The voice of "The Promoter" Aaron Kozak.]
AK [V/O]: What happened in the past of the National Extreme Wrestling is over
... [The word "finished" flashes across the black screen.] AK [V/O]: We should not cry about what has happened, but look forward to what is to happen in the future ...
[The word "future" flashes across the back screen.] AK [V/O]: The future being ... "The Unbeatable" ... "The New Mr. N.E.W." ... "The Rookie of the Year" ... the future is ... "So Damn Cool" Jay Emes ... [The words "So Damn Cool" emerge onto the screen in big bold letters.] [Suddenly, "Thunderkiss '65" by White Zombie begins to play. The screen remains black, but the blackness fades, and a small room is shown. A large paper backdrop hangs from the ceiling. "So Damn Cool" is spray painted on this backdrop.]
[The music grows louder, and some light begins to shine on the subject. Then, a man busts through the paper backdrop with authority. This man is young, cunning,
and good looking. His face is familiar and young, and he is fresh meat in the sport of wrestling. A smile slaps on his face, and his pearly whites are revealed. This young man is wearing black Nike versible shorts that hang down around his knees and a black Timberland windbreaker that is zipped up half-way. You can see he has no T-shirt underneath the breaker, and his muscles stick out. He has chestnut brown hair that is tied up in a ponytail and dark, brown eyes that gleam in the lights above.]
["Thunderkiss '65" slowly fades and the young man takes a standing position as he looks into the camera lens. Once again, that haughty, arrogant, chesty smile cracks upon the face of this man, the man known as Jay Emes.]
JE: National Extreme Wrestling, I have arrived. The next sensation in this sport and in your organization. I may be new, I may be young, but I'm the future of this damn company. I have many years ahead of me, and in those years, I am going to turn this organization upside down and inside out.
[Emes looks down and cracks a half-smile. He then looks back into the lens, more pompous than ever.]
JE: I know what this place is about. I did my homework. I know N.E.W.'s past. I am aware of all the so-called "greats" this company once produced.
Brandon Bloom, "Mr. eWrestling" as you like to say, I know what you're capable of. You're a limelight lover. At one point in time you attempted to be the entire show with your little "nod, wink, and a smile" BS and the DBBWF.
[Emes chuckles.] JE: James "Simply the Best" Pillow. Catchy name. I like it. I think it's very creative of you. And I'm sure you will be known by that name for the rest of your life and career here in N.E.W.. But, how long do you think you can be "Mr. N.E.W"? I know you didn't give yourself that name, the media did, and I know you own this organization now, but how long do you think you will have the title of "Mr. NEW"? Stars come and stars go in this business, and
I think it's "Mr. N.E.W.'s" time to step aside and leave a little room for the new spotlights. Spotlights like Jay Emes, "The New Mr. N.E.W."! You may run the show now, but you are not _THE SHOW_! That job now belongs to me, Jay Emes.
[As the words leave his mouth, Emes basks in his pride, and is taken away from life and brought to his own egotistical world.]
JE: Speaking of "spotlights", how could I forget about "El Spotlight"? How could I forget about the Vindicator, The True Hero, The Littl' Brah'... V.D. Vitrone. This man is a legend in his own right. He's held the N.E.W. World Heavyweight title several times. Vitrone has been there, he has done that. It's almost as if there is nothing left for this man to accomplish, yet he still comes back to the organization that made him what he is today,
The Spotlight. Well, Vinnie, it's time you lose your spotlight, and give it to your new pal, Jay Emes. Why you ask? Because I am "So Damn Cool"!
I am the next big thing, but I'm here to stay. [Emes points to himself.] JE: Now, who did I leave out? I could of sworn there was another N.E.W. "star" that I did not mention. Hmm... is it Assaf Levavy? Nah, he's back in
Israel looking for his yomikka. Is it Patrick Carter? Nope, last I heard he was trying to reform The Beatles. Is it "Brass Knux" Jason Williams? No way, he's "supposed to be" dead. Hmm... who is it? Oh, I know! It's the Ironman!
That's right, it's "Snake Eyes" Eddie Jacobs! How could I have forgotten your redneck ass? Eddie Jacobs, man oh man. The Tennessee native himself. The man who turned down the N.E.W. World Championship, the single most important thing in his life. The one thing on this Earth you cared most about, something you've fought for your entire career in wrestling. Hmm... I don't know if that was smart, redneck. I mean, what do you have now? Nothing! But, I guess you can't really expect much more from a hick such as yourself.
[Emes sarcastically nods.] JE: But, in all fairness, you're alright. You're a swell guy, Jacobs. To tell you the truth, you and I have something in common. That is, we both want the same thing here... the N.E.W. World Title. The difference though, it ain't gonna take me a year and a half to get it. Why?
Cause I'm so... damn... cool! [Yet another grin cracks, and Emes points to himself with great pride.] JE: The time is nearing. July 14, N.E.W.'s first show back. This is where I will prove to the world that the "Unbeatable" Jay Emes is just that...
unbeatable! I have shown you what I can do in my warm-up match in the IWE, and as you can see I remain undefeated as I defeated General America in my _DEBUT_ match in this sport. That flawless record will stand and stand strong from now on. The IWE wasn't easy, and neither will the N.E.W. be, but when you're
"Unbeatable", you're unbeatable for lif-- wait, that's too over used. Let's just make it simple, I am the "Unbeatable Man"! There, that's better.
[All smiles once again is Jay Emes.] JE: Although my first N.E.W. match will _NOT_ go on my win-lose record, I'm still going in with one purpose. _NO ONE_ will be able to toss me up and over the top rope, but if someone does it will be because I slipped. But, get thrown over a top rope means _NOTHING_! I doesn't count. Why? Cause wrestlers get thrown over the top rope every match. Getting pinned is what counts! And getting pinned is something that will _NEVER_, ever happen to Jay Emes, that's a promise. But, Vendetta, "America's Last Chance" Johnny Washington,
"Magnificent" Mike Douglas, Ogre, Maurice Martin, Mitch "Sure Fire" Gatlin, and
"The Iceman" Chris Steele better all beware. 'Cause I'm lookin' to toss all of you craps over the damn top rope! I'm gonna be looking forward to it, I hope you are.
This has been "So Damn Cool" ... [Fade.] --Jason MS-- Handler of Jay Emes [WET] *Can be seen in IWE* [1-0-0]
