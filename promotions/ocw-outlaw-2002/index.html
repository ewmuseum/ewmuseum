Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Outlaw Championship Wrestling opened in March 2002 as what RAW had evolved into. In its own fiction RAW had been bought and dismantled by 'The Outlaw' Travis LaGrange, who kept much of its roster and brought it home to Texas under a new name.

Its first card, Dead or Alive, came from the Reunion Arena in Dallas. RAW's old board had made five guaranteed contracts a condition of the sale. The returning Doug McCray held one of them, and LaGrange made his place in the tournament for the first OCW World Heavyweight Championship depend on a no-disqualification match against the owner himself, which McCray won with the Devil's Deal.

The same night opened a round-robin World Tag League for new tag team champions, two points a win and one a draw: the Men in Black beat Debonair, and the Kings of Hardcore fought Tommy Bedford and Knox Roberts to a time-limit draw. In the first round of the thirty-man tournament Taylor Green, Randy Love, Tony Quint, Simon Underwood, Despair and Spikyjim went through.

Each match was written by its own writer, and the card came with the promos its members posted to the federation's mailing list. That card and one digest of those promos survive; how the tournament and the tag league ended is not in the surviving record.
