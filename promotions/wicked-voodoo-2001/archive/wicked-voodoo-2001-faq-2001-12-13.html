History... In May of 2001, wrestler Simon Bach dreamed up the idea of running an eight-man tournament showcasing the best and brightest in the sport of wrestling.
However, this tournament would be given one of the biggest platforms in wrestling to showcase itself, Master of the Ring V. Bach, a participant in the very controversial MotR III was said to have some negative feelings towards Nick
Demola, however, that was pushed aside when Bach came to him with the idea for this event. The first Wicked Voodoo took place in Baton Rouge, LA. Eight men that thought they'd be headed for France and the grand MotR were redirected by Bach and
Company and brought to the Riverside Centroplex, a converted ice arena. The participants were as follows; Vandal Gomez, Matt Saunders, Eric Emerson, Jake Davids, Alex
Extreme, The Messiah, Eugene Robinson, and Ketiak. In the end, after a few injuries and adjustments in the interest of fairness, the fabled 'Death in
Darkness' was used in a match pitting: Gomez, Robinson and Ketiak. After a great match, which ranged all over the Centroplex, Gomez was able to close the lid on
Eugene Robinson for the victory. 'Urban Legends' is the second installment of WV and promises to top the first one. b.) Real History... I, [name withheld], came up with an idea for a small tournament to more or less leech off of the large MotR mailing list. (Insert evil laugh here) The first round saw
8/8 out of roleplays which had me pumped up to write those dark matches. As much as I feel guilty about giving such good roleplays a dark match, I was the only match writer and wanted to save myself for the last three matches... but that's a different story, and thus why I went with just one big match against the three that flashed.
Now, without the help of the MotR mailing list. I am pretty much taking WV II out onto its own supercard, but I am keeping the 8-Man format to it. Well, I wouldn't really call it a supercard... it'd be more like a "king of indies" event. But that's not really important.
If I have more matchwriters *cough* this time, I will have all eight matches 'live'. In ABS format. ---- 2. Who Runs It? a.) Crew of Voodoo Simon Bach - The man in charge of the whole deal. You want answers, you talk to Simon Bach... just don't expect to get a straight answer from him. He still likes to act as a heel even though his wrestling career is pretty much over. He is also the color commentator of Wicked Voodoo. This saves him money!
Johnny Marks - Also called "Screaming" Johnny Marks. Marks is the play by play announcer for Wicked Voodoo. And is also currently looking for a job for a major wrestling promotion. Marks is a young (25) and hungry announcer and tends to really sell the action in the ring. Marks is a close friend of Bach, in real life.
Josh Davis - Ring announcer for Wicked Voodoo, he also worked for Atlantic Coast Wrestling as their color commentator. Mike Frye - Head Referee. Well, he's the only ref... so, he's in charge. Don't push him, cause he'll push back.

---- 3. How is it ran? a.) Tournament Like any tourney, the field will be set up randomly and placed into brackets. And since it's an eight-man, you will have to win three matches to win the whole sucker. Now, in the first one, I played around with the brackets as far as next match-ups. But, I don't think I will go through mixing it up again. So expect it to be more cutthroat than before.
I'm going to got with a two flash format here. Pretty much, you've got two weeks to pump out two flashes that you could be proud of. It could be a regular flash and then a character development flash, it could be two regular flashes bashing your first round opponent, or you don't even have to talk about anyone and just develop your character twice. Anything you come up with is cool. But just remember to do two of them :D
Also, on the second week a strat is due. It is vital that you do one or I'm not going to have any clue on how to write your character, or I won't have anything interesting to send to motivate my matchwriters - if there are any. Are there any? Please?
b.) Dates. Week 0: Card is announced, brackets are set up. Week 1: Flash Packet #1 Week 2: Flash Packet #2/strats due.
