Another eVo star in talks with VW?
The media has been buzzing since the closing of eVolution Wrestling, a federation under the Tyler Davidson Corporation. Such starts as Matt Hunt, James Kimson, and VW's own Damon O'Halloran were all apart of that federation until it's closing. Many have had talked with different federations about their free agency, and a few have gotten to talk with VW at one time or another. Most of them, however, ended all talks early in their progressions. VW kept pursuing certain wrestlers, but the telephone calls were never returned and the scouting continued.

Rumors speculate though that Damon O'Halloran will not be the only eVolution veteran to join the ranks in VW. Thug roC, a multiple time champion across the country has been said to be in talks with VW's front office and scouting agents. Thug roC, a very well documented Cruiserweight legend and a very charismatic speaker, has been sought after by the VW staff. Although talks and meetings are not frequent, there's a chance that he will come to VW. There's no telling on a timeframe for his arrival though. Thug roC is known for dragging out deals and meetings to make sure that not only does he get what he wants on the deal, but makes everyone anticipate his arrival/joining.

Neither party, VW nor Thug roC, were available to comment on the situation. Many believe that the fact that there are a few wrestlers that know Thug roC, that they can draw him to VW. Thug roC is scheduled for another meeting at the VW headquarters in Sacramento, California later this week. Some think that this could be the meeting where VW either picks up the ball and run with it, or mistakenly drop it.

-John Clancy
