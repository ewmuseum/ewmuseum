The Forsaken — a Sunday Night Strife segment draft

2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
6 The Forsaken Add one matchup hThe scene cuts backstage to the locker room where we find those wacky middle-aged guys, The Forsaken, conversing amongst themselves quietly in the corner. Possibly discussing strategy as to go about their current situation with Invincibleness and Sweet Illusion. No #1 Contenders have been decided yet, but we know this much…hell will break loose at any given time…sorry for damn near using a J.R. quote, but you know what? It fits the bill, so kiss my ass.

Their conversation is cut short as Farwell reaches into a black bag near his feet and procures two items that haven’t been seen since the final days of OSW…

The OSW Tag Team Titles.

“Check ‘em out,” Farwell said. “We busted our asses off to get these belts and we’ll do the same when we beat up those weird lesbian champs.”

“Dude! Why are we beating up lesbians?” Walkins asked.

“Because Sweet Illusion are a constant thorn in everybody’s side…and they won’t kiss on-camera…whores.”

“Ah.” Walkins picked up his belt and remembered that fateful match where they defeated Politically Incorrect and P.W.A. in the fabled No Ladders II match. Yeah, that was some cool shit. Seriously. People were all “SMASHCRASHBASHBITCHTHATSMYBELT…”

But enough of that rhetoric. Farwell set down his belt next to Walkins as he stood up.

“Watch those belts, man. I’m gonna go get a soda. Teasing that short-shit, Gabe, really makes a guy parched. Be back in a few.”

“Gotcha,” Walkins replied.

Farwell got up and left the locker room. A few minutes went by as Walkins sat back and adored both tag team title belts. Two straps of gold were his eye candy right now and not a thing in the world could ruin it for him…that is until The Almighty Master of Your Mom entered the room. Walkins looked up and gave Gabe a weird look as Gabe only grinned from ear-to-ear.

“You’re not a hockey player, are you?” He asked.

“Me? A hockey player?” Gabe replied. “Bull-fucking-dick-ass-shit! I SMASHED fucking Wayne Gretzky in the face with SMASHYOURFUCKINGFACEINWITHTHEHEELOFMYBOOTIFIER! That’s why he retired, but I have something to tell you, my mortal.”

“Um…what?”

Invincible Gabe looked around the room, obviously coming up with some sort of lie to construe. He just threw caution to the wind and glared at Walkins.

“That one guy…balding guy…he told me he needs help…catching…a rabbit. Fuck whatever he said before. He needs you, man. Now! Now! Go, go! Your life depends on it! Do it for your country…or some shit.”

Walkins ran out of the room in a panic while screaming about a rabbit profusely. Retard. Anywho, Invincible Gabe was left all alone as he smiled a sinister grin…what’s this? Something Invincible Gabe did…worked? The fuck?!?!?!

He glared at the OSW Tag Team Titles. They practically called out to him, “take me.” The only time somebody would ever say that to him. Ever. He placed both titles belts across his shoulders and smiled as he walked off…

…With the OSW Tag Titles in tow. Fear it.
