Vintage Westling Rulebook


The Following is a small set of rules to follow while competing in Vintage Wrestling 2.0

Enjoy Yourself

This whole thing of 'e-feds' is all a big game. If things doesn't go right, it's not going to change your pay. You get no pay regardless where you compete. If you find that a time of e-fedding is boring, is because you are makeing it boring, not because of the federation you are in. Enjoy yourself, but in the same since, make your work worth something.

Be Patient

Everyone will get their turn to shine in the light. But you must understand, everyone can't be fighting for the World Title or the Tag Titles at one time. Take your time, build your name up in other angles and your time will come soon.

Be On Time

Everyone should have their angle pretty much planned out and knows what shows they will do what on. You should always have all of your segments turned in a day or two before the show to ensure that it gets up on time.

Be Active

Writing Segments is one and Roleplaying is another, but it won't take much for you to stop by the forums and participate in some discussions. That keeps the OOC atmosphere active and lively. We will all be able to work together.

Your Job and Your Help

Your job in Vintage Wrestling is to find a partner and work out a complete angle. Following that you are to write as many segments as you would need to promote your angle. Your goal is to make your angle one of the hottest things in the federation. You can say you are competing for the spotlight. You are trying to get the fans to want to know more and more about your angle. Roleplaying, you can do on your own time, and you can also do it to furthur your angles. It is not required, but if you would like your name personally to be known more, then Roleplaying is your thing. Posting News and Rumors for your angle is also required. Sometimes, depending on who is writing, you may need to write your match, the way you want it for your angle. Anything else we can possibly ask from you is extra help.
