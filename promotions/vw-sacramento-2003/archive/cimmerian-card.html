Cimmerian

Real Name: Juan Alvarez II Birthdate: March 24, 1977 Hometown: Tepatitlan, Mexico Height: 5'9 Weight: 168 lbs. Nicknames: Status: Face Entrance Music: 'Go to Sleep' by Eminem, DMX, and Obie Trice

Wrestling Style: Luchador Set Up Move: Fatal Retribution (Stepladder tornado DDT) Finisher: 1. The Stygian Press, 2. The Iron Spike, 3. The Purge (1. 630 Splash from the top ropes. He does this rarely, most often at Pay-Per View events. 2. Cimmerian lifts his opponent up in suplex position, and slingshots him off the top rope.
