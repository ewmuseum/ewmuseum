League of Lusers — Return

"The LOL reopens! Are you funny enough to join?"

4 April 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Five months after it closed, the number #555 parody fed on the net, the LOL (League Of Lusers), has reopened! Do you feel you have what it takes to follow in the footsteps of men like Van Halent, Snidely Q. Pidmore III and "Diamond Cold" Disco Disciple? Do you think you could make a fedhead collapse into paroxysms of laughter? If so, then don't delay... join the LOL today! The LOL is a booked parody fed which aims, quite simply, to be a good laugh for all involved. We'll be having three cards a fortnight (just to be original...), occaisional PPVs, lots daft matches, and (with your help) an avalanche of hilarious roleplays! We want to hire the most rip-roaringly funny characters in the world; no wage demands too low! No perks attainable! For more information, check out the LOL website at: http://fly.to/lol/ Or alternatively, there should be a handbook floating around somewhere on this newsgroup... Look forward to been tickled by you! Jim. (formerly JJF)
