Introduction ********************************************************* *************************************************************************** Allow me if you will to let you into a little secret: professional wrestling is not an entirely serious sport. Remember Kevin Sullivan going to the Himalayas and finding Yeti in an iceberg? That made me giggle. Remember the Honky Tonk Man singing? Remember Hulk Hogan's bandana? Nobody could say these were serious things. And yet e-wrestling as a whole struggles to reflect this; most electronic federations (with some notable exceptions) seem to have names like "Super-Hardcore Mega-Extreme Death Death Death Killing Wrestling Federation"; they tend to concentrate on the brutality of wrestling. The violence, the blood, the gore. And they forget about the fun. LOL has been created as a celebration of the innate silliness of pro-wrestling; a place where handlers can bathe themselves in an endless river of F..U..N! Or, to put it another way, LOL (which stands for "League of Lusers") is a parody fed. So we don't want SERIOUS wrestlers. We don't want SENSIBLE angles. We want the funniest wrestlers you can create, with the most brain-dead finishers your minds can conjure, participating in the most outlandish angles ever conceived. But most of all, we want you to join the LOL and just plain have fun. *************************************************************************** * 2. How Things Work ****************************************************** *************************************************************************** a. HOW TO GET MATCHES In short... CHALLENGE! If there's a match you want, mail the President and I'll see about scheduling it. If you want a match but don't have a specific opponent in mind, just say you want an open contract. If you see an open contract and you want to fill it, just mail me. If you want a match with certain stipulations, just say so! I will fill up free slots in a card by scheduling matches of my own choice, but I'd really rather if I didn't have to. b. PARTICIPATE PARTICIPATE PARTICIPATE! If you want to win matches, you've gotta roleplay! A roleplay is where you write a scene with your wrestler doing something and/or saying something... the wrestler with the better RPs (short for "roleplays") will generally win a match! You can send your RPs to the President and he will make sure that they feature on the every next LOL show. If you'd like your wrestler to be interviewed by one of the LOL announcers, just write write the interview and send it to me, and I'll see what I can do. Note that there is NO limit on how much you can participate each week, although in practical terms you won't want to RP more than once on each show very often. c. IF YOU WANT SOMETHING COOL TO HAPPEN... STRAT! "Strat" is short for "Strategy" - if you want something specific to happen (e.g. a sneak attack, a run-in, or if you want your wrestler to adopt a certain tactic in his match) then just mail the President about it and, although we can't always follow your suggestions to a 'T', we'll do our best! d. BE KIND TO THE PRESIDENT! When sending the president LOL-related mail, please start the subject line with [LOL] and then describe what it is, e.g.: [LOL] Flare RP [LOL] Seamus the Leprechaun in-ring for Wampage This helps me not to lose anything important. And believe me, you'd HATE it if that happened! :-) e. HAVE FUN FUN FUN, BABY! And remember... don't take this too seriously, the purpose of it all is just to have FUN! *************************************************************************** * 3. The Booking ********************************************************** *************************************************************************** For the most part, LOL matches are booked according to which wrestler I think is being the funniest; this takes into account roleplays, strats and angles. The emphasis is more on quality than quantity; so roleplaying 20 times a week more than your opponent will not guarantee you victory! (In actual fact you'll probably lose, since unless you have a very furtile imagination indeed you'll be repeating the same stuff time after time and that quickly gets boring.) On the other hand, if you don't roleplay for a match at all you're very unlikely to win it. *************************************************************************** * 4. The Shows ************************************************************ *************************************************************************** The LOL works on a fortnightly rotation, with one main card and two minor cards every two weeks. These cards are: --> Sunday Morning Wrestling Show <-- A show that tours the country; nay, the world, to bring the biggest and bestest competitors in the LOL to the fans. This is our main card, consisting of around six live matches every other week, plus interviews & roleplays. --> Chucklemasters <-- Held the Friday after every Sunday Morning Wrestling Show, Chucklemasters aims to recap recent action as well as showing roleplays, a couple of jobber squashings, and usually a live main event. --> Wednesday Wampage <-- A house card from the Ronnie D. Memorial Dome that is similar in many ways to Chucklemasters. Held the Wednesday before every Sunday Morning Wrestling Show, Wampage offers previews of forthcoming matches as well as the latest roleplays & interviews, a couple of dark matches and a live main event. So, every two weeks will pan out like this: Week 1 Sat Sun - Sunday Morning Wrestling Show Mon Tue Wed Thu Fri - Chucklemasters Week 2 Sat Sun Mon Tue Wed - Wednesday Wampage Thu Fri Week 3 Sat Sun - Sunday Morning Wrestling Show Mon Tue Wed Thu Fri - Chucklemasters Week 4 Sat Sun Mon Tue Wed - Wednesday Wampage Thu Fri ...and so forth, ad infinitum. In addition to all this, watch out for: --> Pay-Per-Views <-- There will be pay-per-views, probably every few months or so, bringing you the biggest and most bizarre matches in LOL history. More details will come closer to the events. *************************************************************************** * 5. The Titles *********************************************************** *************************************************************************** The LOL sanctions the following three titles: --> The LOL Galactic Title <-- The big one; the most prestigious title offered by the LOL. The one every single's wrestler would drool to get his hands on. This belt will be held only by the very best. --> The LOL Orange Grove Texas Title <-- The secondary title for singles competitors; less prestigious than the Galactic title but a whole lot more prestigious than nothing at all! Named after the birthplace of LOL boss Ronald Adonis. --> The LOL World Tag Teem Titles <-- The only title for tag teams in the LOL; intented for the best of the best. A third title (either a Luserweight title or a Radio title) will be added at a later date, should we develop a big enough roster. *************************************************************************** * 6. Will do whatever it takes to make LOL a success. Oh, and he's a hunchback with a limp, a withered arm and a deformed face, btw. Makes Quasimodo look like Leonardo diCaprio. --> Richard Chopper <-- LOL's senior ring announcer; nothing special about him. Situations Vacant (minds too!) *************************************** *************************************************************************** There's always room for people who want to be a part of the LOL, in whatever roles they prefer. However, I am currently actively looking for the following: WRESTLERS - Where would a fed be without wrestlers? Well, up the creek without an inflatable life-raft of course! :) So feel free to enter your most ridiculous, outrageous, satirical or just plain FUNNY wrestlers into the LOL! Be as imaginative as you like, we'll lap them all up! There's an application form in the next section. WRITERS - There is an old saying that you can never have enough writers - and since the President is a lazy bastard he wants as many as he can get! :-) So if you feel you could write matches for any of our cards mail me and earn my eternal gratitude. Just indicate how much you'd be willing to write on a scale ranging from one match every four weeks to four matches every one week, and experience that warm glow that comes from helping a sad fed to keep its head above water! ASSISTANT BOOKERS - have good ideas but don't have the time to write full-blown matches? Then this could be a job for you... basically, the job of an Assistant Booker is to work with a writer in suggesting "spots" - jokes that could be used in a match. Should help to take the strain off tired minds! ARTISTS - I've created a website myself, but unfortunately I can't draw any better than your Aunt Mabel's pet goldfish can! So I'm on the lookout for someone who could create some _good_ graphics for the LOL website; if you think this could be you, please get in touch. So if any or all of these interest you, don't delay! Email [detail withheld] today! :-) (Actually, take your time if you want to. I only said "today" because it rhymes.) *************************************************************************** * 9. A Little Bit of History ********************************************* *************************************************************************** The LOL was started in July/August 1998, and continued for several months of relative success with stars like The Dodo, Van Halent, "Red Hot" Cobra Commander, The Ham N Egger Express and "Diamond Cold" Disco Disciple before the strain of running the fed, coupled with my generally knackered state at that point in time and some personal problems, caused its closure. At the time, I vowed I would never go back. However... By March 1999 I had vastly recovered and had time on my hands, so I thought why not revisit the thing I most enjoyed in e-wrestling - fedheadery? I toyed with the idea of starting a serious fed, even going as far as to write a handbook for a prospective fed named the SEWA, but deep down I knew that parody wrestling was what I really loved. And so, after much soul-searching, my heart was made up: the LOL would be reborn. But it wouldn't be the LOL that had closed the previous November; no, this LOL would be a low-stress version, but still taylored to one aim, and one aim only. Allowing people to have fun. *************************************************************************** * 12.
