Introduction ************************************************************ ****************************************************************************** Allow me if you will to let you into a little secret: professional wrestling is not an entirely serious sport. Remember Kevin Sullivan going to the Himalayas and finding Yeti in an iceberg? That made me giggle. Remember the Honky Tonk Man singing? Remember Hulk Hogan's bandana? Nobody could say these were serious things. And yet e-wrestling as a whole struggles to reflect this; most electronic federations (with some notable exceptions) seem to have names like "Super-Hardcore Mega-Extreme Death Death Death Killing Wrestling Federation"; they tend to concentrate on the brutality of wrestling. The violence, the blood, the gore. And they forget about the fun. LOL has been created as a celebration of the innate silliness of pro-wrestling; a place where handlers can bathe themselves in an endless river of F..U..N! Or, to put it another way, LOL (which stands for "League of Lusers") is a parody fed. So we don't want SERIOUS wrestlers. We don't want SENSIBLE angles. We want the funniest wrestlers you can create, with the most brain-dead finishers your minds can conjure, participating in the most outlandish angles ever conceived. But most of all, we want you to join the LOL and just plain have fun. ****************************************************************************** * 2. The Rules *************************************************************** ****************************************************************************** a. HOW TO GET MATCHES In short... CHALLENGE! If there's a match you want, mail the President and I'll see about scheduling it. If you want a match but don't have a specific opponent, just say you want an open contract. If you see an open contract and you want to fill it, just mail me. If you want a match with certain stipulations, just say so! I may fill up free slots in a card by scheduling matches of my own choice, but I'd really rather if I didn't have to. b. PARTICIPATE PARTICIPATE PARTICIPATE! If you want to win matches, you've gotta roleplay! A roleplay is where you write a scene with your wrestler doing something and/or saying something... the wrestler with the better RPs will generally win a match! You can send your RPs to the President and he will forward them to the rest of the federation in "packets" released around three times a week. Alternatively, if you want a roleplay to be shown or even take place (ie in-rings) on one of the cards, just mention this when you send it in. You can also do special features, like interviews on our weekend show "Wampage"... just say what questions you'd like to be asked, write the answers and send it all in. c. IF YOU WANT SOMETHING COOL TO HAPPEN... STRAT! "Strat" is short for "Strategy" - if you want something specific to happen (e.g. a sneak attack, a run-in, or if you want your wrestler to adopt a certain tactic in his match) then just mail the President about it and, although we can't always follow your suggestions to a 'T', we'll do our best! d. BE KIND TO THE PRESIDENT! When sending the president LOL-related mail, please start the subject line with [LOL] and then describe what it is, e.g.: [LOL] Flare RP [LOL] Seamus the Leprechaun in-ring for Wampage This helps me not to lose anything important. And believe me, you'd HATE it if that happened! :-) e. HAVE FUN FUN FUN, BABY! And remember... don't take this too seriously, the purpose of it all is just to have FUN! ******************************************************************************* * 3. Cards ******************************************************************** ******************************************************************************* --> Tuesday Wrestling Event <-- A show that tours the country; nay, the world, to bring the biggest and bestest competitors in the LOL to the fans. This is our main card, consisting of around six live matches every week. --> Weekend Wampage <-- A house card, with summaries of two or three dark matches (generally against jobbers) held in the Ronnie D. Memorial Dome, and maybe a live main event. Also on Wampage are reviews of the past week's action, a preview of the forthcoming Saturday Night Wrestling Event, plus interviews, special features, and so forth. --> Pay-Per-Views <-- There will be pay-per-views, probably every couple of months or so, bringing you the biggest and most bizarre matches in LOL history. More information as we get nearer the time. ******************************************************************************* * 4. The Titles *************************************************************** ******************************************************************************* The LOL sanctions the following three titles: --> The LOL Galactic Title <-- The big one; the most prestigious title offered by the LOL. The one every single's wrestler would drool to get his hands on. This belt will be held only by the very best. --> The LOL Orange Grove Texas Title <-- The secondary title for singles competitors; less prestigious than the Galactic title but a whole lot more prestigious than nothing at all! Named after the birthplace of LOL boss Ronald Adonis. --> The LOL World Tag Teem Titties <-- The only title for tag teams in the LOL; intented for the best of the best. A third title may be added at a later date, should we develop a big enough roster. ****************************************************************************** * 5. Who's who in the LOL **************************************************** ****************************************************************************** --> Ronald Adonis <-- The Chancellor (ie supremo) of the LOL; a comitted wrestling fan, he founded the LOL and still owns a majority shareholding. Will do whatever it takes to make LOL a success. Oh, and he's a hunchback with a limp, a withered arm and a deformed face, btw. Makes Quasimodo look like Leonardo diCaprio. --> Richard Chopper <-- LOL's senior ring announcer; nothing special about him. Yet. --> Zarg the Unmercible <-- An alien warlord bent on world domination. I shouldn't worry about him too much, though. ****************************************************************************** * 6. Who REALLY runs the LOL? ************************************************ ****************************************************************************** --> President JJF <-- JJF's been around e-wrestling for a couple of years now, but has only recently hit the (reasonably) big time with The Amazing Dave. He's also handled a ridiculous number of parody wrestlers, including Blubber Mountain and "Schoolgirl" Kathy Briggs. He's also a raging ecomaniac on the quiet. ****************************************************************************** * 7. Situations Vacant (minds too!) ****************************************** ****************************************************************************** There's always room for people who want to be a part of the LOL, in whatever roles they prefer. However, I am currently actively looking for the following: WRESTLERS - Where would a fed be without wrestlers? Well, up the creek without an inflatable life-raft of course! :) So feel free to enter your most ridiculous, outrageous, satirical or just plain FUNNY wrestlers into the LOL! Be as imaginative as you like, we'll lap them all up! There's an application form in the next section. WRITERS - I doubt I'd have it within me to write every single match for this fed - lazy bastard, aren't I? ;-) So if you feel you could write matches for our Tuesday card, mail me and earn my eternal gratitude! Just indicate how much you'd be willing to write on a scale ranging from one match every three weeks to three matches every one week, and experience that warm glow that comes from helping a sad fed to keep its head above water! WEBMASTER - I unfortunately can't design webpages to save my life, and can't draw any better than your Aunt Mabel's pet goldfish can. So I'm looking for someone who could create and maintain a LOL website; if you think this could be you, please get in touch. So if any or all of these interest you, don't delay! Email [detail withheld] today! :-) (Actually, take your time if you want to. I only said "today" because it rhymes.) ****************************************************************************** * 8.
