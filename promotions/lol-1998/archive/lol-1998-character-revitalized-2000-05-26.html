[opening post — 26 May 2000] Any former LOLers up in this hizouse?!! Well, if you are one of the lucky few who participated in that goofy fed, you may recall a character of mine by the name of... [dramatic pause.] 'THE REVOLUTION' REVEREND PHILBERT MAGWEED THE THIRD [..and the ominous music sounds.] While I've decided to change him from a goofy parody fed wrestler to a "real" character, who I use in "real" feds. Hmmm..that's a bit ironic. A real e-wrestling federation. After all, it's all fake...or is it...perhaps in another strange, far off dimension, all our characters are REAL wrestlers in that dimensions WWF or WCW. "Homeless" Joe could be the WWF champion in the 90,000th dimension or something. Anyway, it's really something to think about. So my point is, do you think it's possible that our wrestlers compete for REAL in another dimension? Wait a second...that's not my point at all. My point is, I would like to enter the good reverend into a fed, but his character will be much different. Instead of being crazy in a funny way, he's going to be crazy in a more heelish misanthropic way...lemme know what you think..
