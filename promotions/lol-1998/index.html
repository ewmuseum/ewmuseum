Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The League of Lusers introduced itself on 21 July 1998 as “the #559 parody federation,” its own Frequently Asked Questions pitching it against fed names like “Super-Hardcore Mega-Extreme Death Death Death Killing Wrestling Federation”: “LOL has been created as a celebration of the innate silliness of pro-wrestling; a place where handlers can bathe themselves in an endless river of F..U..N!” Its own fiction put Ronald Adonis in charge as Chancellor, introduced as “a comitted wrestling fan” who “founded the LOL and still owns a majority shareholding” — and, in the same breath, as a hunchback with a withered arm and a limp who “makes Quasimodo look like Leonardo diCaprio.” Richard Chopper served as its ring announcer (“nothing special about him. Yet”) and Zarg the Unmercible as a wrestling alien warlord “bent on world domination.”

It sanctioned three titles from the start — the LOL Galactic Title, “the big one,” reserved for the very best; the LOL Orange Grove Texas Title, named for Adonis's own in-fiction birthplace; and the LOL World Tag Teem Titties, left unclaimed for lack of a roster deep enough to fill it — run across a weekly touring main card (Tuesday Wrestling Event) and a house show (Weekend Wampage), with occasional pay-per-views. It ran as an open, roleplay-decided fed: wrestlers wrote their own matches and the better roleplay generally won.

Its first run closed after a six-part SantaSlam '98 season finale that November, its own Galactic Title changing hands to Van Halent on the way out. Its operator's own farewell post, 10 November 1998, left no room for a comeback: “that was the LOL's final card... ever. Full stop; no last-minute reprieves, no miraculous saves, no living to fight another day.”

Five months later, on 4 April 1999, it reopened under the same operator and the same web address, with the same who's-who of Adonis, Chopper and Zarg still billed and a promise of “three cards a fortnight.” Ten more cards ran between 16 April and 20 June 1999. Van Halent carried the Galactic Title across the gap between eras and defended it successfully through the whole return; G.I. Slow, the Orange Grove Texas Champion coming in, lost the belt to “The Loose Screw” Dmitri Damian Maku Stalin on 6 June 1999. The long-empty World Tag Teem Titties finally changed hands by tournament on 3 June 1999 to the Real American Zeroes, who lost them eleven days later to the pairing of Bubbolicious Bob and Van Halent — only for the Chancellor to hold the belts up days after that when the new champions' own partnership broke up, with a rematch announced for 28 June 1999.

That rematch falls after the record's own end. The League of Lusers closed a second and final time on 22 June 1999, its operator writing “this is the second time the LOL has died; there will not be a third coming,” and thanking the roughly ten people a day who had kept visiting its website.
