Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The TWL started life on its own operator's BBS a couple of weeks before its earliest surviving newsgroup post, on 4 May 1997, run by a co-president and booker pair.

It was a real-wrestler simulation fed: each handler picked six wrestlers from a pool spanning NWA, WCW and WWF stars of the 1980s through the then-present, two of the six forming a mandatory tag team, with an optional manager and stable name. Weekly cards were decided by the TNM simulator; pay-per-views mixed simulation with booked finishes for match types, like four-way tag bouts, the software couldn't yet handle on its own.

By mid-May, with a new GeoCities homepage up, its recruitment post was tracking dozens of real wrestlers already claimed — among them Sable, Chyna, Vader, and the Undertaker — against a further list still open, including Ric Flair, Hulk Hogan and Eddie Guerrero.

Its last surviving posts, dated 10 June 1997, were still recruiting. No card, roster confirmation, or result of its own survives.
