TWL NEEDS YOU!!!!

10 May 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The TWL is opened and looking for people to join. It doesn't matter if you are an vetern or an rookie, all are welcome to join. This is a real wrestler fed. For weekly cards its more sim but for ppv's its part sim and booked. On ppv's matches that TNM can't deliver just yet, like a four way tag team match. You pick 6 wrestlers. 2 of the 6 have to be a tag team. If you want you can pick a manager and a stable name. Matches are played on Tnm 6.2+. So if you want to join pick from the wrestlers that are not taken.
