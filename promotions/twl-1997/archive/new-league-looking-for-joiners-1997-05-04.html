This league started a couple weeks ago on my bbs and im looking for people to join. here is some info on the league and if your interested in joining email one of the two email address at the bottom and they well tell you who is gone. there are a lot of good real wrestlers left, also if you have any questions please ask How things work I will be honest and impartial. I have a large amount of wrestlers in this Fed. As I said earlier, the software generates the outcome of each match based on the wrestler's attributes. I will consider and respond to the handlers if they have any concerns, and then my decision will be FINAL. I don't think there will be many problems, but I will follow procedures I wrote above (no one gets special treatment). Basically, things are running smooth. I trust that this could be a fun Fed with lots of great wrestling and enough stuff provided for a handlers and their wrestlers if they're champs or just an average ranked wrestler. Each person should be active with trash talk and interview flashes. This helps build plots and feuds in the TWL. Any person who doesn't reply over 2 weeks time will have deletion of their wrestler(s) so that new members may join the fed. So once again, if you don't send flashes and actively participate your wrestlers become free agents. There is nothing worse then when a handler has all kinds of talent in his fed and doesn't send flashes. Trash Talk, sneak attacks, interviews, and mandatory match requests should be submitted to Mike, your Co-President and booker.
