The TWL is looking for people to join. It doesn't matter if you are an vetern or an rookie, all are welcome to join. This is a real wrestler fed. For weekly cards its more sim but for ppv's its part sim and booked. On ppv's matches that TNM can't deliver just yet, like a four way tag team match. You pick 6 wrestlers. 2 of the 6 have to be a tag team. If you want you can pick a manager and a stable name. Matches are played on Tnm 6.2+. So if you want to join pick from the wrestlers that are not taken. We can't be the best without your help.
