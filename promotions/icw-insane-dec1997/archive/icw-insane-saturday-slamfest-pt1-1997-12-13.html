(Fire works blast off as clips from the Title tournements are shown. Then it flashes through the winners in order from least to greatest. Scott Hall holds the Gimmick Match title in the air. It switches to the Outsiders finishing Hell's Servants to get the National Tag belts. Then Hall is shown winning the National Title, and then clips from the Nash/Big Daddy Cool match. Then Earthquake, and Gargoyle are shown as they dominate the 3 teams they had to battle. Then the camera focuses on the announce desk where Mr. Insanity, and Jack Hammer sit. Then as the fire works stop blasting off. The Wizard by Black Sabbath plays, and The Boys In The Ring make their way to the interview area.) Greg: Well, well, well, it seems to me that the Boys in The Ring don't have any gold because they were ripped off. Now I don't care who the champs are I think its Earthquake, and Gargoyle, but me, and my boy Rich we are callin' Clan Leader, and Alein Predator out right out for a match tonight. Rich: Hey, if they don't wanna accept everyone here knows why, but we are ready to talk you down with a couple of towers. 'Cuz Boys In The Ring we joke around alot, but when you get down to the bottom line we are the greatest athletes in this fed, and everybody knows it.
