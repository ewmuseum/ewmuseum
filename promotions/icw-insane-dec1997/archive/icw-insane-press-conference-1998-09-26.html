ICW — 1998 restart

Press Conference & Information

26 September 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
This is a 2 part message one is an in character event, and the other part is a link and a bit of information first the in-character part. [The scene opens to the back of the ICW arena where a press conference type area has been set up there are approximately 12 men on the stand, and a whole sea of news reporters. One of the man steps up to the stand, he is ICW President & CEO Joseph Gregory Allocca. He takes the stand and looks around for a minute.] JGA: Welcome everyone, as you know, the ICW is beginning to pull together for a possible surgence into e-wrestling. We are currently at a low level of talent, and our looking for more, but the current staff is nothing short of top notch. As for those of you who don't know me, my name is Joseph Gregory Allocca, but you can all call me Greg Allocca. I have been in this business for almost a decade, and am proud to announce that the rumors of the ICW's re-opening are in fact true. And as soon as we gain enough talent we will hold our first card. We were originally hoping for an October 7th opening with our Wednesday night event, Knightmare On Wednesday, but that will most likely not take place due to the lack of talent. I will now answer any questions, you may have. [Most of the reporters raise their hand. Allocca picks a good looking woman from the back.] Woman: Mr. Allocca, exactly what are the talents that you have signed? And what wrestlers are currently under negotiations? Greg Allocca: Well, right now we currently have 3 wrestlers under contract, and 1 possible wrestler under contract, as well as our new commisioner who has a history of getting involved with the matches. The 3 wrestlers our from the far east in Japan, Tecate, as well as former ICW Superstar, the masked sensation known as Squala. As well as Wilfredo Allocca. [A reporter gets out of his seat with a raised eyebrow.[ Reporter: Wilfredo Allocca? Greg Allocca: You probably know him As The Almighty Prey or Freddy Allocca. Reporter: But isn't Allocca your last name? Greg Allocca: Yes indeed it is, and we are brothers, but I can assure you that he will recieve no preferential treatment, as I will take no part in my brothers matches at all, I will even step out of the announce booth during his matches if need be. [The woman Greg called on earlier raises again.] Woman: And who are The Commissioner, and Tentative Wrestler? And what wrestlers are you currently under negotiations with? Greg Allocca: The Commisioner has been named he is Adam Solomon, who is right over here. [Adam stands up, he is the 3rd chair on the right of GReg Allocca.] Greg Allocca: As well as our Vice-President, Edward Glide. [Edward Glide stands up, he is the first chair on the right of Greg Allocca.] Greg Allocca: And the rest of the staff. The tentative wrestlers is Josh Cagle who is currently suffering from some injuries, and isn't certain if he would be willing to take the risk that is involved with Josh Cagle. As far as the wrestlers I am currently speaking with the most notable in e- wrestling world are Brad Brown, as well as Markus King who has been one of the top indies wrestlers for almost a year now. [A reporter in the lower left hand corner stands up.] Reporter: And who are your primary targets as far as getting contracts signed? Greg Allocca: Well, we have taken notice in the e-wrestling world, and we know that the ICW has a lax chance at these stars for now. However we do have our sights set on signing Brass Knuckles, whether it be a short term or long term deal, Ronnie Deschenes, who we know will most likely turn down all our offers, J.W. Hardin, and of course The Siberian Express. [A man way in the bask of the room stands up.] Reporter: So you are basically targetting the more established stars? Greg Allocca: Don't misinterpret what I'm saying, I do hope to get my hands on a handfull of the established stars, because they will indeed help the ICW get off the ground. [Another Reporter in the center of the room stands up.] Reporter: So you are open to just about anyone? Greg Allocca: Anyone who is willing to put in the time, and effort it takes, and who understands that they are not the ruler of the world, or the owner of the organization. I have time for only 2 more questions so please hurry this along. [The woman from earlier stands again.] Woman: Most of these wrestlers, we haven't heard of before, are you mainly targetting the indy circuit? Greg Allocca: In a way yes, but only because the indie have more young talent, I want this to be a young league, I hope to constantly get newer, better, and younger wrestlers. I know that there are poor wrestlers in the indies as well, but I hope that myself, and my staff will be able to meld many of them into great superstars. [A small man stands up in the upper right hand corner of the screen.] Greg Allocca: Yes? Reporter: Are the rumors true of Meanstreak, and OZ entering the ICW, and even Derek Mota, and what of your relations in the DCWL are you going to try and gain some of their talent. Greg Allocca: Meanstreak and OZ are personal acquaintances of mine, and I do not want to surround myself with more controversy as to preferential treatment as of now they are not being negotiated with, I will first see how the public reacts to my brother being in an ICW Ring. I have never seen Derek Mota wrestle, although if he's interested like any other talent in wrestling he can talk to me. As for the DCWL, the ICW, and DCWL have no current allegiance whatsoever, although, I may try to sign Jose Garces, or Dale Stanwycz from that organization. But I will let them approach me, since I do not went any rifts between myself, and the general management of the DCWL. And as of now, I'm sorry, but I am out of time, another press conference will be held next week at this time, to answer any further questions you may have. Thank you, and good day. [The camera fades to black.] Ok, we are still looking for a lot of talent, if you were mentioned in this that means I am very interested in having you join, or perhaps a possible 1 match deal for a quick feud down the line. the web page address is http://www.geocities.com/Colosseum/Court/6486/ICW.html There is an application there as well as more information, if you have any questions, I will either answer them in an e-mail, or if they are related to the business-like side of the fed, put them into next weeks press conference, I hope you all enjoyed, this, there will be many more features added to the ICW including an EXCLUSIVE monthly interview in which a wrestler will be interviewed in-depth about his past [made-up, and/or real], present [whatever] and future, what his direction in the fed will be. We have one weekly card, and a house show, as well as a lot of cool side things, all to make things fun, thanks people. -----== Posted via Deja News, The Leader in Internet Discussion ==----- http://www.dejanews.com/rg_mkgrp.xp Create Your Own Free Member Forum
