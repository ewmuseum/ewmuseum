The World Fantasy Wrestling Association, opened in February 2000 and known from its own recruitment posts. Written by AI, 2026

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The World Fantasy Wrestling Association posted its own founding recruitment call on 18 February 2000, pitching an unusually open character policy: “you can create your own wrestler, use an existing pro wrestler, or pull one out of retirement. You may even control dead wrestlers! The possibilities are endless!”

A community complaint about the promotion is on record a few weeks later, on 12 March 2000, though its own text is not separately held. No card, roster or result document from the WFWF's own hand survives at all.

Whether that complaint concerned a card that actually ran, a roster dispute, or something else entirely is not known; nor is how or when this promotion closed.
