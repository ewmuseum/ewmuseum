Empire Pro Wrestling — Roleplay

"Kayfabe: From The Desk of Lindsay Troy"

11 March 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
DBrunkGXW — 03-11-09, 09:23 AM For immediate release:

Empire Pro Wrestling has been contacted by the representatives of Craig Miles regarding the events that took place on Aggression 42. Specifically, there is a pending legal claim regarding the attack upon his person by Anarky during the match. According to his representatives, Mr. Miles has sustained serious injuries and will not be competing in the forseeable future. Mr. Miles has, however requested that Anarky and Marcus Westcott be forced to team together on Aggression 43. In addition, Mr. Miles stipulates that he will present some demands at said show wherein he will outline his offer to forgo legal proceedings.

Therefore, it is my decision that Anarky will team up with Marcus Westcott at Aggression 43 against Stalker and the EPW Television Champion, Fusenshoff.

Lindsay Troy
Owner, Empire Pro Wrestling
