Y'all are going to laugh your asses off when you see this. Me? I cringe, and cry. I cringe because it's VERY apparent that y'all didn't like me so much back then, hence the Double Q(XXX rip-off, in a pink jWo shirt), Kendall Codine attack(for the one millionth time), and Eduardo's Nocturnal kicking my character's ass(for the one millionth time).

Add to the fact that my stable turned on me(and this was BEFORE we worked together, planning things like that out so everybody looks good), Eli Flair whipped Sean's - *ahem* - XXXstasy's ass(AGAIN!), I get the feeling after reading these two events that those attacks were probably designed to get XXX out of the game.

And, that's not even why I cry. I cry because all of this happened in '99. Y'know? Back when we thought the world would end in 2000, and we wanted to get a world title reign in before we all died(I know that was *my* motivation). It's now '08, the world didn't end(thank GOD, didn't win my first world title 'til '01), and I realize that in a year, it'll have been a decade of knowing you losers.

We have all come a LONG way.

Enjoy!

Number two probably was a better match, but one was more special for me. For those that don't know the story, Dave came to CSWA as a part of an 'Invasion' storyline.

That was ON the surface.

Beneath it, a couple of CSWA people came to me and told me about how the GXW tried to tarnish the CSWA title, by having their champ(Ryan) beat up our champ(Evan Aho) on their show, putting heavy emphasis on his being the CSWA champ(correct me if I'm wrong, Brunk, but was the CSWA title on the show as well?). The people that told me were pissed, and I was told that it was a shoot, and that we(CSWA) were taken advantage of, so I, in turn, became pissed.

So, Dan Ryan comes to the CSWA. And, I take any and every opportunity that I could to talk as much trash as possible, leading to a match and a pretty intense back and forth between Jonathan David and I. The match ended up being a countout win for Trip(hah!) on a house show card, and afterwards, I get an IM from Kent saying that Dan Ryan's handler wanted to chat(or something of that nature).

My first reaction was: "Hell no! He can kiss my ass!", but Kent somehow got me to talk to him, and he ended up not being so bad of a guy. Dave doesn't tell people this very much, but he and I became pretty close, we talked very often, and once the whole Invasion thing simmered, our characters became cool. But, Ryan winning the CSWA World, to me, was like Sean winning it, because Dave was my buddy, and he deserved it.
