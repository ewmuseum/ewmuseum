"The End..."

24 October 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Cross — 10-24-05, 12:02 AM To my fellow comrades in e-fedding,

I can honestly say that I really didn’t think the day would come when I could admit to myself that I was done. I guess it’s been denying it for the past couple years, but with hiatuses here and there I’ve been able to prolong the inevitable. I have nothing, absolutely nothing, left in the tank to continue the fantasy wrestling career of Cross. The PPV will be the last event for Cross. I am officially retiring.

My personal life comes first and I have too much on my plate. Even if I had some spare time, my mind is occupied with so many other things, as much as I wish I could roleplay or promo, it just doesn't flow out of me. You all deserve the 100% best and I can't even give 10%. It's best I go out going for the top title. Nevertheless, it's been fun.

Cross has been an expression of my spiritual journey that I was graced to put in written form and share with all you great writers. My faith has been challenged because of you guys and I have challenged you guys to think more seriously about your own faith system. Awesome fun.

I won’t say goodbye because just like in real professional wrestling, never say never. I apologize to the leaders of the promotion for leaving the vacancy, but there are many great up and comers that will fill the void and Cross will just fade into history. Thank you to all the handlers that I got to work with, in particular one person and you know who you are. (Check your inbox) Cross wouldn’t have been the character he was without you all.

May the Lord bless you and keep you all. Til’ we meet again.

Sincerely,

-Eric Xandor Phenomenal — 10-24-05, 03:19 AM wow...Nice speech. I come back from retirement and you leave. I'm sorry that we won't have the chance to go up against each other or even team up. Sounds like it would've been a nice war of words ...even better... a great team.



Even though I don't know you, I'm sorry to see you go. When you feel that need to roleplay again. I'll be right here waiting for you. :)



Good Luck in your future and God Bless.



Scott Franklin
