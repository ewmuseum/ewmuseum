"EPW Roll Call"

26–28 March 2014

The Great Eye — Mar 26, 2014 at 4:56 AM OK Folks, just wanted to get back into the swing of things as I’ve been out of the loop way more than I should have been the past few weeks. My mom had a torn rotator cuff that had been bothering her for years and finally she broke down and had the surgery for it and since then my days have been been spent helping her around the house and taking her to the various and sundry doctors that she needs to be seeing. That’s squared away for the most part and I’m back to try to right the good ship EPW as best I can. So first and foremost going into WrestleVerse I need to know who all I can count on to show up for that show. So please post here so I know who the roster is as we head to the big dance. Also I know a few people who talked to Brunk about signing up to EPW, and I’d like to get ahold of them as well and see what their plans are.

As for Dave, his plate is full as of right now, hopefully things will get right for him sooner rather than later and he’ll be around to talk to everyone.

And as for Pete, he just did me and Dave a solid and got the show up while we were not in a good spot to get things done. He has in no way shape or form been promised a 5 year reign as EPW World Champion for his efforts and all rumors to that point are nothing but lies, at most I’ve offered him a 2 year run.
RStrawsma — Mar 26, 2014 at 11:04 AM Strawsma is still here.
