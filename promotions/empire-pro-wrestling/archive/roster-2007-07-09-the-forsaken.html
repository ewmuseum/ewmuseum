The Forsaken

9 July 2007 – 7 February 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Team Name: The Forsaken Members: The First & Dope (Felix Red) Name: The First Height: 5' 11" Weight: Legit 190, announced at 210 Finisher: "Cut The Threat" a Harlem Hangover, or "Inescapeable Torment" Which is an Oklahoma Roll. (It's a out of nowhere roll-up type of move, done by the First to an opponent who's prone on the mat) Move List: Jeff Hardy Look: Jet black died hair with random colored streaks in it down to half way down his back. He'll wear Manson, Korn, Disturbed, My Chemical Romance, HIM, Green Day T-Shirts or random **** like the "I do my own Stunts" T-Shirt. He may wear a home made "Snape killed Dumbledore" Shirt. Face/Heel: He's a whiny goth, so the goth/emo crowd would dig him. He'd he over for his high spots, I'd call him a face for the time being. Music: "Never wanted to Dance (The Birthday Massacre remix)" by Mindless Self Indulgence Entrance: The First generally walks towards the ring with his head down brooding...Ignoring the crowd, he walks up the ring steps and then walks to the corner that allows him to see the entrance and sits down in the corner like Raven waiting for his opponent. If he enters second he'll walk out, get about half way to the ring, then do the UT Hair Flip and then hop around a bit to get himself pumped and then enter the ring. - Name: Dope (Felix Red) Height: 6’2 Weight: 225 From - The primordial ooze Face/Heel: Heel (I guess) Appearance: I usually describe him as resembling Regi, from Bloody Roar 4, except with dread locks. and he’s going to have to wear a black and white, simplified Juventud Guerrera-esp mask, with the hair (dyed black) coming out the back. Trunks are just black trunks with “DOPE” written down the sides of the legs. Black boots….Um…Let’s have him wear a black denim trench coat to the ring. Music: “Agenda Suicide (Fake Agenda Mix)” by the Faint. History: Hardly anything is known about the personal history of the masked wrestler known only as Dope. Seriously. No one knows squat. It’s all types of mysterious. Style: Probably some combination of Tajiri, AJ Stlyes, and RVD. He’s been accused of being a spot machine, although his ring psychology has improved over the years. Move set: Dropkick to the knee Spinning heel kick (his version is meant to mimic the hurricane kick from Street Fighter 2, so it sometimes serves as a false finish. Like a “I can’t believe he kicked out of that!” sort of deal) Moonsault into a DDT Tornado DDT Various shotokan kick combos. Springboard bicycle kick Backflip Soccer kick Arm wrining/hook kick combo (booker T) Shining wizard Rolling Russian leg sweeps… Standing 450 Splash Twisting leg drop Tree of woe/baseball slide Any of these with tables and/or chairs involved (Arabian facebusters, triple jump style ****, Van-Daminators, ect…) Finishers – Dope Slam (Fireman’s carry into a sitout dominator. Doesn’t work or larger opponents. Maybe a cutoff at 260 lbs, or something) Dope Dive (180 Swan-ton bomb. Although he gets lazy and just does a regular swanton a lot. Like how Justin Credible used to do a spinning tombstone, then started doing a regular tombstone? Same idea. ) Dope Stretch (Regal stretch) Tandem finisher w/the First - “Art of Despair” - Total Elimination. Entrance: By Himself - Lurks out from behind the curtain right around the time the bass rift kick in on the song, and wanders down to the ring, occasionally grinning and/or yelling things at the fans. Slides in under the bottom rope, and sits himself down on the top turnbuckle, awaiting glorious combat… W/The First - From what I gather, The First, Dope’s tag team partner, has sort of a Raven esq. walk down routine. Figure First does the sitting in the corner thing, and Dope kind of hops up the second turnbuckle from the otherside of the ropes, and stares at people in the audience….Not that they can tell he’s staring, ‘cause he’s wearing a mask… Forsaken Entrance music is "Never wanted to Dance (The Birthday Massacre remix)" by Mindless Self Indulgence. updated felix info... proper names for finishers! - Ecstasy of the agony (Fireman’s carry into a sitout dominator. Doesn’t work or larger opponents. Maybe a cutoff at 260 lbs, or something) - kind of looks like Super Dragon's Psycho Driver III only a lot quicker, and more of a suplex than a piledriver. opponent's supposed to land on his stomach, not head. A Shattered Horizon - (180 Swan-ton bomb. basically the same idea as the Spiral Tap. Although he gets lazy and just does a regular swanton a lot. Like how Justin Credible used to do a spinning tombstone, then started doing a regular tombstone? Same idea. ) Anti-Life Equation (Regal stretch) And I think I'm gonna stick with "Never Wanted to Dance (Birthday Massacre Remix)" by MSI for his solo music, as well as the Forsaken's theme, if that's all cool....why nobody that I know of is using that song for walkdown music in the real world, I'll never understand... The First's solo music is "Happy Birthday" by The Birthday Massacre, EPW edit.
