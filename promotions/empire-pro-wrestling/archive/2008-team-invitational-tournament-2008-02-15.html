TH — 02-15-08, 10:02 PM So, did you ever want the chance to beat up on the boss? You did? Then have I got the opportunity for you! Brunk's entered Dan Ryan into the TEAM Invitational Tournament. OF course, he won last year, so giving him a beatdown is easier said than done, but the chance is there, right? Besides, last year was his year. This year is going to be your year. Who am I talking to? You, that's right you. Enter this tournament, and have a chance at eW immortality. And maybe you'll get a main event push, or at the very least, a chance to wrestle Jomo and Beastlet in a handicap tag match on the next Aggression :p
