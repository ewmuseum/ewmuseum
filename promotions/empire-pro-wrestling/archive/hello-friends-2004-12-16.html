SteelCitySon — 12-16-04, 02:09 PM Hello everybody.

Some of you might know me, some might not. My name's John Miller and I used to handle The Steel City Icon John Miller (not very original, i know). Anyways, I used to be a part of FW a while ago, but decided to take a break after a couple years. Actually, I was the man that founded EPW. It's great to see where it's come under the great leadership of Jeff and Dave. I know that I put this place in good hands. Anyways, I just wanted to announce that I would be coming back in a wrestler-only capacity and I look forward to working with you guys. Some for the first time, others yet again. I'd like to say thank you to Jeff for allowing me to come back. I'd also like to say "hi" to the handlers I haven't been able to talk to in a LONG time. I really look forward to having some fun with you guys.

Sincerely,

John Miller
