RStrawsma — 01-03-08, 03:36 PM Wrestlers
"Triple X" Sean Stevens - Really solidified himself this year.

JA - Perhaps Empire Pro's most consistent competitor in recent years, and I'm glad to see him finally breaking into the main event scene.

Kin Hiroshi - Undoubtedly the fastest rising star on the roster.

Sarge - Underdog Award of 2007

Matches
I have to say, the past two World Title matches were unbelievably memorable. From Joey Melton's twin brother emerging to Sean Stevens winning the gold. I've also been quite stoked about the past couple IC Title matches. Brown/Foxx was brutal on a lot of levels, and Sarge ending a long, seemingly endless reign was truly monumental.

Events
I think Wrestlestock caps all.

Debuts
Ice Tre, Stalker, and Felix Red

Returns
Gotta go with Sarge here.

Departures
Karl Brown and Frankie Scott.

Not sure what's become of Lindsay Troy, Joey Melton, and Troy Windham, but I'd probably put them here too.

Surprises
The Melton twin was a big surprise.
Dope being Felix Red was also a great surprise. That came totally out of left field.

Feuds
Dan Ryan vs. IrishRed
Lindsay Troy and Joey Melton
Sean Stevens and Ice Tre

Angles
The legit injury of Foxx.
Blitz walking out.
Everything circling the Troy/Melton scenario.
The Stevens' "retirement" swerve right after winning the belt.
Anything involving Ice Tre.

Moments
For me, the biggest moment was the second night of Wrestlestock, as Lindsay Troy got on the mic and totally ripped Melton a new asshole. One of the best mic spots I've seen in a long while.

I feel like putting Stevens' ascension to World Champion here as well, though it goes hand in hand with Troy's defeat on the previous event.

Ice Tre's ominous "Dag Yo."

Oh, and Beau Michaels with a jock strap in his teeth... can't beat that.

Most Improved
Nakita Dahaka and Sarge

Managers
Hasn't been a big year for manager, but I give credit to Leonard Johnson for his final farewell and f*ck you to Dan Ryan as Blitz made their final appearance on Empire Pro.

Staff Members
Mojo Massey and Mike Neely. I think it'd make an excellent sitcom.

Outlook on 2008
I get this feeling we might actually see some untapped talent appear in Ice Tre. If that happens, then damn...

I predict Sean Stevens being very dominant in the World Title scene for a long time to come. His battles with Tre, Daymon, Hiroshi, and JA are only just beginning... and I wouldn't be surprised if Stalker was added into the mix somewhere down the line.

I can imagine things will get interesting when the invasion angle over in A1E starts having repercussions on Empire Pro programming. Beast and Cross are planning something big, I feel.

With any hope, we'll get a conclusion to the drama between Lindsay Troy and Joey Melton.

Sarge will do well as the IC champ, and hopefully supersede that status with some time and effort.

I know I've got a lot of big things planned for my characters, so anything is possible at this point.
