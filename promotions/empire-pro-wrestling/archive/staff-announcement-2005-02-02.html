DBrunkGXW — 02-02-05, 10:23 AM We have a new member of the "staff" so to speak.

Tom Holzer will be taking on the role of Matchwriting Assistant (if ya need an official name for it). Basically, Tom will be responsible for sending out my booking to the matchwriters, communicating with them throughout the matchwriting cycle and organizing that side of card production so that I can be free to work on outlining each show, on reading and judging roleplay and making sure the information I receive back flows well into a solid product that we all enjoy.

Tom will have no booking say whatsoever, if that concerns anyone here (since he handles a character in the fed).

That sets our current structure as follows:

Dave Brunk - Owner
Jeff Bolichowski - Webmaster
Tom Holzer - Matchwriting Assistant

Tom will also be in charge of making sure I have my coffee when I arrive for work.

Thanks Tom.
