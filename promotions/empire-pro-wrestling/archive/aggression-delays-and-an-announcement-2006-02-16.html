"Aggression delays and an Announcement"

16 February 2006

DBrunkGXW — 02-16-06, 06:39 PM First of all everyone, the delays on the Onslaught and Aggression are squarely on me. Right now I'm battling a nasty case of some back spasms and am on some rather nice muscle relaxers as a result. While it allows me to be a little more comfortable, it doesn't do much for my ability to write coherently in an e-fed sense.

Regardless, I want the card up this weekend and I'm determined to make that happen.

Now for some promising news. Many of you may have heard or read that [name withheld] has retired from e-fedding after a long and extremely heralded career. That's not the promising news, of course. LOL. We'll miss Troy Windham and his Entourage, definitely.

At Unleashed, our upcoming PPV there was the be a five way dance for the World Heavyweight Title. This had not been announced yet officially, although a few of the participants are already aware of their involvement. Now then, Troy Windham was to be one of the participants and with his retirement will no longer be a part of the show.

The easy way would be to drop him and make it a four-way dance instead. However, in lieu of that I've decided to have a battle royal on our next Aggression, the last before Unleashed where the winner will take Troy Windham's spot in the main event at Unleashed.

I want to make this clear. This will be a roleplayed for only one time battle royal event. It doesn't matter how high or how low you are in the pecking order, bring your best stuff, put out your A game and you will be considered. If a lower card competitor puts out the best showing, he will be in the main event at Unleashed. No angles will get in the way of it. Furthermore, no angles will be determining the winner at Unleashed either so this is truly up for grabs.

I'll be posting a sign up thread in the OOC forums shortly. Please sign up there if you're interested.

Thanks all.

- Dave
