Empire Pro Wrestling — Title History

EPW Television Championship — Title History

started 21 November 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
* Black Dawn 2005 - November 17, 2005 - "Yours Truly" Adam Benjamin won a tournament to crown the first ever EPW TV Champio when he pinned Foxx after a powerbomb into a flipover bridge * Unleashed 2006 - July 15, 2006 - "Da Bomb" Mike Evers defeated "Yours Truly" Adam Benjamin when he got the decision during a double pin where Evers shoulder came up a split second before the three count * Aggression 27 - November 15, 2006 - "The Phenom" Shawn Hart defeated "Da Bomb" Mike Evers after a victory roll * Russian Roulette 2007 - September 18, 2007 - James Irish defeated "The Phenom" Shawn Hart via a Funk #49 * Black Dawn 2008 - April 21, 2008 - Fusenshoff defeated James Irish by pinfall in a four way dance also involving Cameron Cruise and Shawn Hart following a Whiskey Bomb. * Aggression 39 - October 21, 2008 - Ice Tre defeated Fusenshoff by pinfall after a rollup. * November 7, 2008 - Ice Tre was stripped of the Television Title prior to Unleashed 2008 due to suspension, leaving the title VACANT. * Unleashed 2008 - December 9, 2008 - Fusenshoff (2) won the vacant Television Title in a Stalker's Rules Match after repeated chairshots to the head of Stalker. * Aggression 45 - July 17, 2009 - Layne Winters defeated Fusenshoff after a Green River Justice. * Black Dawn 2010 - June 8, 2010 - Karl "The Dragon" Brown defeated Layne Winters & Erik Black in a triple threat match after a Dragon's Bite and pinfall on Winters. * Unleashed 2011 - February 9, 2011 - Donovan Astros defeated Karl "The Dragon" Brown by pinfall after The Slingshot Effect. * Wrestleverse IV - August 3, 2011 - Cameron Cruise defeated Donovan Astros via submission when Astros tapped out to the Cruise Control. * Aggression 62 - November 19, 2011 - Adrian Willard defeated Cameron Cruise by pinfall after Higher Vision * Russian Roulette - February 3, 2012 - Rich Mahogany defeated Adrian Willard by pinfall after a rollup and a handful of tights * Aggression 65 - April 5, 2012 - Larry Tact defeated Rich Mahogany by pinfall after inside cradle * Aggression 67 - June 26, 2012 - Anarky defeated Larry Tact after interference from Teddy Alexander and a Chaos Breaker * Unleashed 2013 - May 10, 2013 - Malcolm Joseph-Jones defeated Anarky after a Greatness Buster Title suspended due to hiatus.
