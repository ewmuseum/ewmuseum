EPW Intercontinental Championship — Title History

started 21 November 2006

* Black Dawn - March 18, 2004 - "Yours Truly" Adam Benjamin d. Karl "The Dragon" Brown by pinfall when Benjamin countered a superplex into the Shining Wizard * Unleashed - May 31, 2004 - "Gentleman" Jonathan Marx d. "Yours Truly" Adam Benjamin by pinfall after countering the Shining Wizard into a belly-to-belly suplex * Aggression - September 7, 2004 - "The Anglo Luchador" JA d. "Gentleman" Jonathan Marx when Sebastian Dodd interfered, accidentally hitting Marx with a steel chair * Aggression - December 26, 2004 - "The Messiah" Sebastian Dodd d. "The Anglo Luchador" JA by pinfall after hitting a low blow and the D-O-DDT * Aggression - July 8, 2005 - Cameron Cruise d. "The Messiah" Sebastian Dodd by pinfall after hitting the Shipwreck * Aggression - September 19, 2005 - Cameron Cruise was stripped of the title in light of false accusations tying him to the Ku Klux Klan * Black Dawn 2005 - November 17, 2005 - Karl "The Dragon" Brown d. "The Anglo Luchador" JA to claim the vacated title * Aggression - October 15, 2007 - The Sergeant pinned Karl "The Dragon" Brown in a triple threat match that also included Troy Douglas after a Flying Elbow Drop from the top rope. * Black Dawn - April 21, 2008 - Troy Douglas defeated The Sergeant by submission when The Sergeant fell unconscious due to a Scorpion Deathlock. * Aggression 42 - March 6, 2009 - Cameron Cruise (2) defeated Troy Douglas by pinfall after a Reality Check. * Russian Roulette III - October 28, 2009 - "The Phenom" Shawn Hart defeated Cameron Cruise after interference from both HOPE and Anthology members when he was pinned following to a shot to the head with the Intercontinental Belt. * Unleashed 2011 - February 9, 2011 - Michael Bastard defeated "The Phenom" Shawn Hart by pinfall after the Bastard Driver. * Wrestleverse IV - August 3, 2011 - Impulse defeated Michael bastard by pinfall after a Sudden Impact. * Black Dawn 2012 - August 13, 2012 - Stalker defeated Impulse by escaping the cage in a grudge match after Impulse was shocked via taser by Caitlyn Daymon *** Black Dawn 2012 *** - Stalker was stripped of the title by Dan Ryan and fired, making the championship VACANT * Unleashed 2013 - May 10, 2013 - Boogie Smallz became the champion in a five way elimination match that also included Rezin, Christian Light, Rich Mahogany and Adrian Willard when he pinned Willard after a punt to the skull Title suspended due to hiatus.
