"A thank you"

7–8 February 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Stalker — 02-07-08, 10:57 PM Basically.... here it is.

Thanks for making my start here so wonderful. After returning to e-wrestling after, well damn, like a 6 year break, I didn't imagine i'd have so much fun. This place is perfect for my schedule and even though i've only churned out about one rp a match since the epic Hiroshi encounter it's mainly because i've worked and tried to put so much effort into quality of what happens on the show.

The show is most important to me and hopefully no one feels like i've stepped on their toes by doing what i've been doing. Dave is great to me even though we don't converse that much I think he knows what I want to bring to the table. Ryan has done nothing but make my character look good. As for Jamar and Paul well they are the roots of what I left behind in SCW. Not saying that I had anything to do with how good they are they just simply remind me of how good this game can be.

At the end of the year simple thank yous aren't enough, but they must suffice. So to Dave thank you for EPW and to everyone else thank you for doing what you do.

Peace,
Justin

http://www.fwrestling.com/fwc/forumdisplay.php?f=426 jayshort — 02-08-08, 01:36 AM for me, the Jason Reeves character brought back so many memories of about five-or-six years ago, when we were much younger doing the same thing we're doing now, with a different energy level, at different places in our lives. Now, we're adults, some of us have had children, in my case, 2 beautiful little girls, in Dave's ... an entire village. :) i always love to look back at the characters from the past and see if I can spot change in them through our own personal changes, and I think it's a testament to the writer if he can evolve personally, but keep the character true to what made him/her special. You do a great job at this, Justin. it's good to have you back, and I know I've done a horrible job of keeping in touch these past few weeks(my computer sucks, and I work crazy hours), but your character coming back was a refreshing change for me, and I personally appreciate you FOR it. Keep up the good work.
