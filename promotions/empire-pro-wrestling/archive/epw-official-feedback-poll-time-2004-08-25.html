:D MarcusWestcott — 08-28-04, 05:43 PM 1. What are we doing in running the fed that you particularly like?

I like it that there always seems to be a great vibe about a show - I can't put my finger on it, but I get the impression that everyone's having a good time, so that's always a plus.

I also think that you guys are great in listening to the handlers, and also in that you're able to take segments/ideas that we have an make them work *somehow* so that everyone is a part of things.

And it looks like some real storylines are starting to fall into place, and that's always a plus.

2. What would you change about the way we do things?

It's been said, but get the cards out quicker. If you need to put out a call for cardwriters, do it at the start of the week, not after the promo period has ended, so there's just time wasted there. I don't want to open up a whole can of A1E vs. EPW worms, but we run under the same system - head booker and group of writers, and our cards are out in four days tops. Not saying it has to be that way here, but there's certainly room for improvement there.

3. What have you enjoyed in EPW the most so far?

My obvious answer is winning the World Title, as I've come so close everywhere else and not quite done it. I had a blast in my very first fued here with Sands, so that was a real nice way to open things up.

I also enjoy that so much effort goes into the writeups - you can tell just by reading them. May be a symptom of taking too long, but when the cards come out, they're sure worth it.

I also thoroughly enjoy the fact that JA~! has made his efed return. Miss ya, man.

4. What is your opinion of the website/Is it time for a re-design?

Web site's not bad. A little vanilla, but I've seen a lot worse. My biggest concern on the site is getting all the profiles looking uniform, and maybe get some more detailed titles done. You guys should talk to Josh and Brian, and get some slick ones done up - see the AWA title for a reference.
