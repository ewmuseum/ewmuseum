TH — Aug 3, 2011 at 8:55 PM I have a confession to make. My heart really hasn’t been in this for awhile now. The match you just read was Michael Bastard’s last for the foreseeable future. It’s also my last e-fed match for at least awhile now. I’d like to say that if this the last time I’m here as an active handler, it’s been fun. EPW (and A1E and NFW and NEW and AWC and ACW and MBE and LVW and CSWA and UXW and TEAM) have all been indelible parts of my life, and e-fedding in general has provided me with some good memories and better friends.

The last thing I wanna do though, is get all schmaltzy, because who knows, I may be back some day. And if I am? It’ll more than likely be here or in A1E, which a bunch of you have been in or are in now anyway. But if not, I just wanna say it’s been a hell of a ride. Thanks to Brunk for thinking I had some talent and giving me a big stage to shine, and thanks to everyone who’s pushed me over the years to be a better writer and handler.

And I’m not really going anywhere far. I’m still on Facebook, Twitter ([detail withheld]) and I got that wrestling blog that I write. But I just won’t be handling in an e-fed for the time being.

So, yeah, so long, farewell, auf wiedersehen, adieu and such. Thanks for everything and keep the ship running as smoothly as it is now. It’s not one of the best feds, if not the best, I’ve ever been in for nothing, y’know?

That said, Tom’s one of the best, and has done a hell of a lot for the hobby in and out of this particular e-fedding community. Sorry to see him go, but glad he was part of EPW history. His characters are all over the EPW record books.

Thanks, Tommy.
