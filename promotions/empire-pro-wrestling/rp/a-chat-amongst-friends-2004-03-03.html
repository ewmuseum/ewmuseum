Empire Pro Wrestling — Roleplay

A chat amongst friends

3 March 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
OoC: This is actually being filmed with a hidden camera. More will become clear later.
------------------------------------------------------
[FADE IN. Karl "The Dragon" is talking to a small group of old training partners. They're standing off to one side of a gym, well lit. One of the other people in the gym, a Japanese man, glares momentarily at Karl as the camera passes, moving closer. There is some general merriment]

Man#1: Yeah, yeah. So, Karl, you seen that punk Rocko's letter to ya?

Karl: Yep. Not really interested in what the guy has to say, personally. He seems to think he's important.

Man#2: Wait, wait, wait. You mean you aren't mad at him?

Karl: How can I be? He's entitled to his opinions. Now, I could have sworn I'd apologised to him over my comments about his lifestyle, but he obviously hasn't forgiven me. Ah well. His loss I guess, eh?

Man#1: Yeah, I remember that. Man, that guy sure holds a grudge.

Man#3: Don't you keep sayin' you don't care about titles?

Karl: Yes. It's true. Dunno why he can't get it through his head that fame and remembrance aren't the be all and end all of life. I don't care if I win a title. I don't care if I go down in history, or if I get forgotten. I just want to push myself further and further and better myself. I don't think I've ever held myself to be that high. I don't even compare myself to others in this business because what's the point? I mean, what use is there comparing me to, say, Derek Graham? The guys huge and a different style. He isn't me. Maelstrom? Not me. Rocko? Not me. Sands? Marx? Not me. Benjamin? Still not me, is he? So how can I compare myself to anyone in this industry? I can't even compare myself with any of you guys because that's unfair; we're not the same people, are we? I can only compare myself against myself.

Man#4: He seems to think that gold is everything. He doesn't know that it's not a battle against everyone else, but battle against yourself. There will always be someone who can beat you. Yourself. If you let defeat at the hands of an opponent deflate you, then you truly have lost. If you can pick yourself straight up and move on, then you still the victor. Maybe gold never comes your way, but if you give it your all and can tell yourself honestly that you have done your best, then you the true man and victor.

Karl: True, Shinji, true. Rocko, though, I doubt he'll ever see that. My fear for him is he'll go about, win all the titles he can, and still be looked down on by people. He's talented, but he really seems to think too much about winning for the sake of winning. It wouldn't surprise me if he wakes up one day, and hopefully realises that his entire outlook has been false. I mean, I don't give a sh*t if I win the title at Black Dawn or not. I don't care if I win the NWL heavyweight title or not. Yeah, it'd be nice, but the biggest prize is going beyond my own abilities.

Man#3: I heard he said you must be eatin' your words now. Boy, is he an idiot or what?

Karl: Hey, less of that mate. He's not an idiot, he's just confused. I'm man enough to say yes, I won the match because of his interference. Yes, he's sunk to the level Sands stooped when he cost me my match against Rocko. Yes, Rocko proved he isn't a man of honour when he decided he was too good to go for the IC title and work his way up to the top. And yes, I do realise that he maybe hasn't grasped the concept that maybe, just maybe, I'd have won anyway. But he's not an idiot. Dishonourable, maybe, but no idiot.

Man#3: What do you mean? If he really cared about holding you down, he'd have been in the match himself and taken the fall, or helped you beat Troy like a man.

Shinji: That make him coward, not idiot. Idiot not know what they are doing. Coward hit like Rocko, from behind, running from problem rather than facing it head on. And coward know about it. Rocko should have begun climb up the mountain again like a true man. It takes strength to begin again. It takes cowardice and dishonour to run from commitment and try to elevate oneself on ones path through underhanded deeds. If Rocko ever win Empire title, or any title, it taints its honour. He thinks he deserves respect for what he has done in career. He does not understand that respect is earned in each new company, not given. Respect, he has just lost.

Man#2: Yeah. One thing, though, he says he wants to see ya humiliated by Benjamin. What's up with that?

Karl: Dunno. Maybe he thinks that if I lose, I'll be sad and sorry for myself like he is. I don't really care what he wants or thinks though. How can I be humiliated when I don't care either way if I win or lose, so long as I do my best and surpass my own abilities? I always know I can lose, I always work on the basis I'm gonna get well and truly destroyed, so I'm more than ready for defeat. He seems to think winning is all that matters. I'll let him think that. He's seen more, so he knows more? Yeah, right. Just because I don't talk about my history he thinks he's seen more. That's why I stopped judging people.

Man#5: Yeah, and he said he gives more. He gives more BS than anyone I've ever seen, that's for sure. I mean, he's taking out his frustrations for getting screwed over on you. Who gives a f**k? Yeah, he shoulda been treated a little better, it's Ryan's fault for pullin' outta the first match, but come on. You work with what ya've got.

Man#1: He doesn't. He picks up his ball and goes home. [The group laughs, except Shinji, who merely smiles]

Man#2: Yeah. I like how he stereotypes you Brits too. Never seen Brits with teeth as bad as Rocko's are, let's put it that way. Yours are fine anyway. You're accent? At least it ain't the drawl I hear from him. I mean, come on, I can at least understand you when you talk, Karl. Rocko, I really have trouble. Maybe he should think more 'bout himself before he tries to take the low blow and criticise you for being, I dunno, HUMAN?

Man#6: [off camera] I love how he says he has a right to be justly rewarded. Talk about ego.

Karl: Yeah. He's done a lot, sure, but this is a new fed. New fed, new beginning, everyone starts at the bottom of the pile. I haven't got a problem with it. He seems to have this opinion though that because he's been around, seen a bit, and got respect and titles in some feds he has a right to come in and head straight to the top here. Come on, I came out of this place, have a first class honours degree to boot, but I know I have to start at the bottom each and every time I sign a contract with a company. I'm probably peoples favourite to lose in the WLS round robin brackets in NWL. I don't care. Rocko would have a fit if someone were to think that of him though. [More laughter from the group]

Shinji: But this is discussion for another day. Karl, I would like you to meet someone.

[Shinji leads Karl across the gym, towards a full-size wrestling ring. In it, stands the man who earlier glared at Brown. He is a Japanese man, early twenties, wearing a red tracksuit]

Shinji: You remember Uematsu? He wishes to talk to you.

Karl: Yeah. How've you been?

[Shinji notices the man with the hidden camera, seizing the bag on his shoulder and throwing it to the floor, breaking the camera, as we hit white noise. FADE OUT]
