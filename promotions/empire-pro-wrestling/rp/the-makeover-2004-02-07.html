Empire Pro Wrestling — Roleplay

The Makeover

7 February 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
JABolich — 02-07-04, 10:00 PM (FADEIN: The back of a Rolls-Royce. Sitting there with a wine flute in his right hand is a well-built blond man with a face reminiscent of a teen-heartthrob boy-band member. He wears a ridiculously expensive white silk suit and tie over a black dress shirt. Even his Italian-leather shoes are white. A carved ivory cane leans on the seat next to him. Those familiar with New Attitude Wrestling, Global Xtreme Wrestling, and mostly Major Championship Wrestling will recognize him as "THE SHOW STEALER" DEAN MATTHEWS.)

Matthews: Hello, hello, hello, peons and plebians. I come before you to day with a proposal for every rising star in the back who wants to rise out of the gutter and BE somebody. Well, this is your lucky day! As of now, I'm accepting applications to be my pal. As my pal, you'll benefit from the finest managerial services in this sport and earn yourself a free makeover in the interest of making people love you.

Come now, I see all of you kids out there with either no gimmick or a bad gimmick, trying desperately to make people take interest in you. Unfortunately, you're all failing miserably. None of you have the attitude! When I wrestled in Virginia people KNEW that when I came out, the show'd be interesting, because I KNOW how to manipulate the shallow and fickle minds of the fans. And now you too can have millions of dumb marks worldwide worshipping the ground you walk on - or cursing it, depending on what milks your Guernsey - simply by becoming my pal and receiving a complimentary makeover.

Still skeptical? Well, alright. I'll show you.

(CUTTO: A blank white screen with elevator music playing in the background. After a few seconds, there is a soft *POP* as an animated semblance of ROCKO DAYMON appears in the center of the screen.)

Matthews (v/o): Here, we see Generic Wrestle-Man Number One. You may recognize him as Rocko Daymon. Now, Rocko has a problem. You see, he just doesn't have a gimmick, so people just don't care about him.

(With a *POP*, a text bubble appears next to ROCKO's head: "Please Be My Pal, Dean!" The bubble vanishes after a moment.)

Matthews (v/o): Sure, Rocko! I'll be your pal. Now, let's get people interested in you. Here, try these on!

(There is a soft rustling sound as ROCKO is suddenly clothed in a pink leisure suit.)

Matthews (v/o): Add a couple of these... Maybe a pair of these... Ooh, can't go wrong with the bling-bling, either...

(As Dean says the last bit, there are three *POP*s. With the first one, gold chains appear around ROCKO's neck. The second pop heralds the arrival of a pair of outrageously huge star-shaped sunglasses. On the third pop, several rings appear on ROCKO's hands alongside some brass knuckle-like handgear - one on each hand, each one reading in bold letters: B L I N G)

Matthews (v/o): Hell yeah, bro, you're big pimpin' now, dawg! Now let's round it all off...

(With a giggle, several cardboard standees of bikini-clad women appear to either side of ROCKO as "Big Pimpin'" by Jay-Z plays.)

Matthews (v/o): And voila! Meet "Ravishing" Rocko Daymon, the biggest pimp this side of Compton! See? Not only do you now care about Rocko, HE'S now getting himself a whole lot of lovin'!

(CUTTO: The Rolls-Royce again.)

Matthews: There you have it. The Show Stealer gets results, kiddies, and it shows! Now NONE of you can resist being my pal! Not that you ever could anyway - it's a once-in-a-lifetime opportunity! Better act now, or you'll miss out on the opportunity to say that you're pals with the coolest man on the planet! So call me now at 1-800-BLINGX2 and apply to become my pal today! Don't delay, call today, because I'm selling like hot cakes! The Show Stealer's in limited supply, so call before I'm goooooone!

(FADEOUT)




All applicants for the Be-My-Pal Contest must enclose a $1000 service fee. Offer valid in all American states except Hawaii and Alaska and all of Canada except Quebec. First five callers win a free Rocko "The Yellow Dart" Daymon plush doll.
