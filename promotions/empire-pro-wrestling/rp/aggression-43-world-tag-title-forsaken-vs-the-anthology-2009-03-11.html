The Great Eye — 03-13-09,03:27 AM (FADEIN: The First sitting on the steps of Front Street in downtown, Salem, this time he's wearing a black hoodie and black pants, his face is painted white. Some kids try to skateboard on the steps near-by)

FIRST: First...First...First...To do something before others do it...To be the best...First times...Yes, all of this...Yes...But that is not me...

See, I was The First...Am The First...Because many eons ago...I was born, most likely somewhere in Africa, maybe the middle east, I can't be sure...But I was born...I grew...I became a man in my tribe...But...There was something...Off...About everyone else...

One day I looked into a pond and saw a reflection of myself...I noticed it right away...The spark in my eyes...It was there, but everyone else in the tribe...They did not have it...

They were alive, but they were feral, merely animal intelligence...They did not seek beyond eating and screwing, beyond sustaining life...I had to help them...

I made drums and played music...I danced, I did what I could to show them something more then merely the growing of food, the killing of animals, or the urge to breed...Some turned away from me, some watched, but nothing changed...It was as it was before...I felt defeated, hurt by my inability to reach them...

I decided that the only way to pass the spark in my eyes would be to start breeding, to make others who could be born as I was...So I began mating with the unspoken for women of the tribe...

But then something happened...One day one of the other men of the tribe looked at me...And the spark was in his eyes...And we knew what it meant, we saw it...We laughed together...The first time such noise every entered into this world...Joyful, joyful noise...And so we worked...Song, dance, Art, communication, the ways our souls express themselves, we poured it out into them, they began to see what we saw, the spark in their eyes turned on...The tribe over the years slowly changed, and all now become one with the spark...

We'd run into other tribes, feral as we used to be, sometimes they would join with us, and over time they would find the spark as well...Other times they would not...It could be violent...One day we had a fight, two of our tribe were killed, the first to die since the spark had visited us.

We mourned

We cried the first tears of humanity...

We grieved...We felt pain no being on this earth had felt before...

The worst part of the dead was to look at them...To see their eyes, to see the spark had left them...

Over the eons...The spark grew...Soon, all were touched by it...Soon all knew what it was...

Our souls...Where do they come from? Why do they exist? What gives us a claim to them? What does it mean to call somebody spiritual, do they have more of a soul in them? Do they have more souls? What happens when you don't have a soul? How does one get one? When somebody is described as a lost soul, what happened to them, how did a soul get lost?

I am the story of humanity....I was The First to have the spark in my eyes...I shall be the last of us when we are no longer...I have lived through famine and war...Prosperity and peace...Been man and woman, straight and gay...I've seen about all I can imagine I could have seen over the journey of our race from that tribe to this moment where I sit on some steps and talk to a video camera...

This is who I am...This is my legacy, my gift, my burden...And it is truly out of all of this...I figure you will take...Nothing...

You'll insult me, degrade me, talk about how you'll destroy me...When I can not be destroyed...

This flesh is nothing, this body, but a shell...You may thwart my desires, you may batter and beat me...But in the end...I'll move on, as will you...

We are but travelers having an adventure....In this situation we're just going to beat each other silly, maim and injury each other over pride, and symbols...It's a wonderfully detached way to view things, but sometimes I just can't keep that perspective...

Of course, in the here and now I want to stomp Jared Wells and Larry Tact's guts out, to beat them half dead and make them pay for the crime of even setting foot in a ring with Felix and I...

But what is our battle, but a spectacle...A play told through violence and athletic talent...Of brutality and beauty...What are we but the avatars of a arena full of fans projecting their passions upon us...

All we are, all we do in that ring, feeds the spark...Makes us more alive, more then what we were before...Such is the nature of all competition...Such is the nature of all interactions...

As I punch you in the jaw...I reaffirm life

As you smash me into the mat...You embrace the energy that makes us all more then mere beasts

The ref counts three...catharsis, finality, this event concludes, and allows for us to move to the next one, and the next...Next match, next show, next PPV

This is what we truly are...But the font of the energy from which the masses drink, drawn to the spark as moths to a flame...

Let us make it a truly wonderful, magical night...One that will echo through the generations...

Rest assured...This is the nicest I'm going to be to you for the foreseeable future...

(Smiles)

Pax vobiscum
