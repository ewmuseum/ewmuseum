Empire Pro Wrestling — Roleplay

Presenting... The Entourage!

14 March 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(CUT TO: A black backdrop with the EPW logo behind it. Standing in the background, muscled, oiled and shirtless is THE MYSTERIOUS ZOLTAN -- flexing his chest muscles up and down, standing 6-foot-nine, making gutteral animal noises, Greek-style chains around his neck, wearing fur boots. In front, smug, reading a copy of the latest NEW REPUBLIC, with multi-colored dreads and a "NO WAR FOR OIL" homemade T is AUGUST DE LA ROSSI, holding a rolled up poster.)

AUGUST: Did you SEE that? Huh? Well... (August gestures at his head.) I KNOW YOU DID! I know that you, at home and in the arena, witnessed not just the debut of The Entourage, the greatest alliance in this sport today... you witnessed not just the debut of The Mysterious Zoltan, the UNCHAINED BEAST (Zoltan pulls apart the chains and screams) bent on nothing more than YOUR destruction... but you also saw the in-ring debut of YOURS TRULY... August De La Rossi, the sport's next great superstar, the man who will teach you how to raise your children, how to appreciate art and how to vote in national and state wrestling... and the right hand man of THIS MAN (August unfolds the poster, it's a CSWA 2001 ANNIVERSARY poster of Troy Windham, in a bowtie and a speedo, on a beach, flashing double guns reading TROY 2 K underneath), the greatest star this sport has ever produced, Hollywood's Greatest Icon, The Epitome Troy Windham. Now, Troy sends his regards -- he really wishes he could be here today, but he's too busy getting a hand release from Alyssa Milano at his beachfront property in South F-L-A, but he did want you, his great EPW fans to get a look at him. (August looks at the poster in awe, and then rolls it back up.)

You see, WE, Troy Windham's Entourage, are here for a purpose. And that is to procure the EPW World Tag Team Championships. The reasons, you illiterate morons, part of the AmeriKKKA bourgeios, ask? ONE... for the unchained beast Zoltan to PROVE to the world he is this industry's strongest physical force. TWO... so I can use my world title to influence world trade politics and help balance the inequalities between RICH and POOR. THREE... so I can use my world title to influence you morons to go out and purchase The Postal Service album and to educate yourselves by purchasing subscriptions to Maximum Rock'n'Roll and to shop at Hot Topc. FOUR... soI can use my world title to also show the world of my artistic brilliance and integrity, a cold unfeeling world which, like my father, does NOT understand me. And FIVE... so our benefactor Troy Windham can increase the legacy of his already Hall-of-Fame enshrined career, so he can continue to DICTATE the terms in this industry, so he can rightfully serve as the fabulous KING of this industry.

We've already stepped on some toes. We now plan on BREAKING a few more. The Entourage, on behalf of Troy Windham... YOUR NEW HEROES. YOUR NEXT WORLD TAG TEAM CHAMPIONS! THE BEAST IS UNCHAINED! FREE LEONARD PELITER! MAKE TRADE FAIR! (FTB)
