AGGRESSION 41: TV Title Match: Fusenshoff (c) vs. Shawn Hart

5 – 14 January 2009

ShawnHartXXX — 01-11-09,04:50 PM FADE IN: Philly, PA - 2:08 P.M.

SJH, PhD. is kicking it old school with the Empire Pro Wrestling logo on the wall behind him and an interviewer that just so happens to bear a striking resemblance to him (or more accurately his half-step sister twice removed Felicia) standing at his side with microphone in hand.

INTERVIEWER: "SJH, after all the hoopla surrounding the explosion of the Anthology onto the wrestling scene and all the success it has had thus far, how does it feel to see that success rewarded with a shot at Fusenshoff and his television title, and what do you expect from the match?"

Hart smiles.

SJH: "Well, Miss Interviewer lady that is TOTALLY unbiased and in no way related to yours truly, to me... it feels a lot like sex. And when SNOOZENSHOFF and I lock horns at Aggression, you can bet your bottom dollar that JAM MASTER JIGGY is gonna get his CLIMAX!!"

INTERVIWER: "Well Shawn, I don't know you... and like you said, I'm totally impartial where this match is concerned, but I have to say - listening to you speak with such passion, such... INTENSITY, about sex and climaxing... it's kind of turning me on!"

The Phenom lets out a hearty chuckle.

SJH: "Now, now... let us dispense with your blatant swooning at my very visage. For us to enter into any kind of liaison would be TOTALLY illegal... Not because we share the same DNA or anything, but because as a member of the media, you have sworn an oath to see both sides of any issue and report only the facts. But yeah, if Snoozenshoff hasn't put the entire viewing world to sleep with his utter asininity and mind numbing irrelevance, the whole lot of you are gonna see a beat down SO FURIOUS that slammin' chicas such as yourself are gonna be havin' romance explosions that will top the charts and rock the Richter scale."

INTERVIEWER: "Well, violence usually eeks me out... but not when its YOU dishing it out."

SJH: "Hey now - if I didn't know any better, I'd say somebody was paying you to say these things."

INTERVIEWER: "I know, right? But that would be tooooootally wrong."

SJH: "TOTALLY!!"

INTERVIEWER: "I'm sorry... I just can't help myself!"

SJH: "Hey, it's totally cool. Jus' goes to show the effect the Anthology has on any and all that stray onto our path. That's just how we do, I suppose. We come in and the impact is immediate! With you, the impact is that your stew pot starts to simmer, and you YEARN for me to bust out my big SPOON and take a bite. With someone like, say.. Snoozenshoff, or the Crimson Calling for that matter, the impact is that they get their faces TOTALLY smashed beyond the point of recognizability and the Anthology marches on to bigger and better things. Call it an outrage, call it a MOCKERY, you know what I call it?"

INTERVIEWER: "The truth?!"

SJH: "TOTALLY! And sometimes, the TRUTH HURTS!!"

INTERVIEWER: "Yeeeeouch."

SJH: "Heh... Raaaaaawrr!!"

INTERVIEWER: "Heh... heh... grrrrrrr..

Hart playfully pounces at the woman.

SJH: "Heh... watch out!!"

The girl begins to blush.

INTERVIEWER: "Whew... oh my..."

SJH: "Totally."

INTERVIEWER: "So after this week, what's next for you heart throbs?"

SJH: "Well, after the Television title rests, ONCE AGAIN, around the waist it called home for a solid YEAR, me n' the boys are gonna keep on goin' 'til ALL THE GOLD is on our side. After all, championship gold is supposed to be indicative the best this business has to offer. And like it or not, the Anthology is exactly that! Snoozenshoff, you're about to find out just what that means."

INTERVIEWER: "You tell 'em bro!! And by bro, I mean famous wrestler from TV land that I can totally relate to, almost like we're bros or something."

SJH: "Totally."

INTERVIEWER: "Shawn Hart, thank you for this tremendous opportunity."

SJH: "Hey, that's me babe... I'm all about the....uhhh.... what're they called?"

The interviewer looks blankly at Hart.

SJH: "Ohhh, you know... the people who watch these things, and come to the shows and stuff?"

INTERVIEWER: "The fans?"

SJH: "TOTALLY! Yeah... any chance I get to share a few words with the..uhh... with..."

INTERVIEWER: "The fans?"

SJH: "Right! Always glad to give these fan people more to talk about. And at Aggression, you can count on old SJH doing just that! See you in the ring, Snoozenshoff... the PHENOM has left the building!"

As the interviewer gets weak in the knees, we FADE OUT.

Fusenshoff — 01-11-09,11:17 PM Fade in to Fusenshoff sitting behind an EPW backdrop. Hes rocking the Television Title with its recently added flask holder.

Fusenshoff: Well if it isnt Jess back from wherever the hell you were a few months ago, moping in solitude about getting your ass kicked night in and night out.

Shawn Hart new look, new stable same ridiculous sh*t spouted off in a befuddling and cringe-worthy manner.

Let me define for you what entertainment is NOT Jess. Its not building enough sexual chemistry to make ladies swoon and men heat up like a hot pocket between brother and sister. Innuendos and playful flirting is meant for those of us that didnt come from the same C-section scar. You may think its slapstick comedy to pass longing glances and reverberate rising body temperatures between siblings, but the rest of us just end up running head-first to the nearest stall.

Theres also the fact that between your incest-laden sexual tension and spur-of-the-moment half-assed wordplay, you forgot to say anything remotely relevant to the fact that Ive been the thorn through your entire midsection for the last year in EPW.

I beat you while you bickered with a ref just before Black Dawn and then extended your losing streak at said pay-per-view. Back then you were trying the same name-calling as now (worked well then didnt it?). It was STROGANOFF back then, or did you forget? Remember, you declared that forever I will be deemed that moniker? Time sure is a foe to the fickle Jess. You really think the same old craps gonna fly this time around? Do you really think Snoozenshoff is that much more inventive? Worn out jive with a fresh, new polish didnt work for Jay-Z and it makes you look worse than just ridiculous...

You look washed-up

Washed back up onto the shore like the corpse of a sea-beaten sailboat and trying to pick up whats left of your career. At least you choose to pick yourself up after falling into another downward spiral, though sometimes it takes longer than others. We were paired up in the King of Kings tournament, but you tucked-tail after a first round defeat, not to be heard from for months. Thats the fighters way of handling a loss Jess. I was more at fault than you for that one Hart, but you didnt see me exiting stage left with my pants around my ankles.

You left without saying a word. Then I went ahead and kicked your teeth in last week for good measure. I figured youd at least hint at why you had a fat lip in your interview, which was compliments of my boot, but still you leave everyone in the dark. How am I supposed to believe youre prepared to meet me in the ring with any intensity? What do I have to do to get your head back in the game? Youre so damn complacent it makes your supposed promotional segment even more unbearable.

Whatever Shawn. Continue to prove you could care less about me having your number throughout 2008 and waste another title shot. It makes sense that youd do this again- its not like you earned it. Usually a guy has to win at least one singles match in the last year to be worthy of fighting for gold. I think Lindz booked you in this match as a sympathy offering- like a Christmas present for Oliver Twist.

Too bad youre getting a gag gift in disguise. The Television Title isnt going to be gift-wrapped like your title shot. By the end of the night all youll get is yet another beating at the hands of yours truly.

Fade out as Fusenshoff stares into the camera with utmost concentration. He takes a swig of his flask and waits patiently for the red light on the camera to go off.

ShawnHartXXX — 01-13-09,12:25 AM FADE IN: Philly, PA - 9:40 P.M.

Once again, SJH channels the old school as he stands solo before the camera with the Empire Pro logo on the wall at his rear.

Heh.... rear......

SJH: "Heeeeeeeeyoooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooo ooooooooo!!"

The over-exertion of Hart's vocal chords cause him to cough.

SJH: "Ahem.... So this is it, huh? I'm being forced for about the eight-millionth time to defend my vivacious verbiage, my PHENOMENAL frolicking, my splendoriffic stylings to an unworthy, uneducated, UNAMAZING opponent?! I mean, I know you've never been one to venture outside the box, Snoozenshoff, but COME ON... you're better than that. Resorting to the same tried and tired tactics that have failed just about every guy on this circuit over the course of the last ten years and expecting that SOMEHOW it will shepherd the sheep into reaching the same sickeningly inaccurate conclusions that you've decided to leap to about le Phenom?"

He shakes his head in disgust.

SJH: "Shame, shame - I know your name! I mean, what more can a guy say? You try to assassinate me and my style, you mock me and everything I stand for - you woefully attempt to turn SJH into the butt of everyone's joke. The whole exercise is old hat, my friend... and despite the fact that old hat is your bread and butter, surely you can see that such a strategem is not the formula for success. It's exactly that kind of thinking that allowed me to carry that shiny strap you've been posing with for the past couple weeks for an entire freaking year! Each and every man I faced during my record-setting reign came in with the same horrendous game plan... and one by one, I knocked 'em down like I knock back Coronas. Speaking of which..."

Hart procures a frosty brew from some one off-camera and takes a big gulp.

SJH: "But y'know what, that's ancient history, and unlike you - I live in the now. I don't care about your so-called domination of me in '08... if you call squeaking by in a couple matches, half of which had other people in them and other people getting pinned, domination... but i digress. What I care about is whats recent, and in our most recent in-ring foray, I did everything in my power to carry your ass in the first round of the Kings of the Cage tourney only to have you puss out, get waxed, and single-handedly cost us the contest. So really, you can holler to the hills about how you did this and you did that a year ago, but the meat n' potatoes of the situation is the only thing that counts is the here and now... And right here, right now, this business is abuzz over the utter domination that's being dished out by yours truly and the rest of the Anthology! Call it an outrage, call it a mockery, call it whatever the hell you like. Deep down, you KNOW it's the truth!"

SJH takes another quick swig from his Corona.

SJH: "Gift-wrapped though this title shot may be, I know that I can't rest on my laurels here. All my former glory ain't gonna do me a bit of good in our bout. I've got to come out, just like I do EVERY time I take to the ring, and prove myself aaaaaaaaaall ooooover again. So you g'head and ride that history we have and see how far it gets ya. Me? I'll be happy to bring you back to reality with a BOOT to the back of your skull. Then we'll see if you want to keep living in the past. The PHENOM has left the building!"

FADE OUT.

Fusenshoff — 01-14-09,12:37 AM Fusenshoff: I couldnt have said it better myself Jess former glory.

Fade in to Fusenshoff standing on the lawn in front of Independence Hall in Philadelphia, PA. The TV Title is nowhere to be found as he pulls his flask out from the inside of his leather jacket.

Fusenshoff: I guess I missed your point. You decide it would be funny instead of painful and gag-inducing to hit on your sister. Do you realize how stupid, not to mention monumentally perverted, you sound by arguing that Im just repeating what everyone else did back when you used to win matches? Yeah, the old verbally-grope-your-sister routine works like a charm, huh? Your opponents are put-off, they think youre bat sh*t crazy and it gives you the edge you need to finish them off.

But theres a difference this time Hart, I know youre just a glorified clown.

I see through the shock gimmick and it doesnt phase me. Im not trying to make you the butt of everyones joke Shawn, you do that on your own.

Ill give you an example. You argue that whats important is the recent past. Who the hell are you kidding? Okay, you joined The Anthology whoop dee doo! You havent won a singles match since I joined EPW. You havent even been in a singles since I wasted you the week before Black Dawn. When I was getting my ass beat in the middle of the ring against Crimson Calling, you were getting your ass beat outside the ring. So yeah, I sucked worse than you sucked that night. You just said last night And right here, right now, this business is abuzz over the utter domination that's being dished out by yours truly. Did I miss something? You havent even been back long enough to scratch your ass let alone do something noteworthy here.

Then you proceed to shoot yourself in the foot by clamoring on about your year-*cough* ten months and three defenses *cough*-long title reign. That ended a year and a half ago Jess. Thats about as recent as an Osama Bin Laden sighting.

Go ahead and say I pussed out during the King of Kings tournament. I showed up the next week. Lets just hope your buddies can soothe your ego enough to keep you around after I teach you what rusty means at Agression.

Fade out as Fusenshoff turns around to stare up at Independence Hall. Despite him being turned around, we can see him pull his flask out of his jacket and take a swig.
