OUTSIDE OF THE RING
TOO MODEST OR TO HONEST?
An interview with: "Hollywood Star" Vince Von Trap.

In the six years that this author has served as a member of the journalist team that contributes to the great publication, Fighting Spirit Illustrated, never has he encountered an interview with the likes of a man like Vince Von Trap. Excuse me, Hollywood Star, Vince Von Trap. Yes, Hollywood Star must come before the utterance or writing of Von Traps name, as he will so often point out. Never in the six years has this writer found a more difficult, more self absorbed subject for such a piece than on that fateful friday evening. I almost didn't pack enough audio tape to record this mans rants and I almost past my deadline eight different times before this was completely editted. HOLLYWOOD STAR, Vince Von Trap is a name that may or may not be familiar to you, and if you're a wrestling fan, his name probably means about as much to you as the name of your neighbors pet turtle. Vince Von Trap is not a marquee pro wrestler, no, not yet and he may never be but he has what very few have, that biological x-factor. The thing that seperates men from boys in this industry and the greats from the okays to the fakes. Von Traps name may carry some weight with others. Particularly with the fans of action films. More specifically, the absolutely terrible B-Rated action films that are often seen on cable movie channels at ungodly hours in the mornings. These films are seldom seen in even the widest of rental stores and are a hard entity to find anywhere. Von Trap has been in countless movies such as these, thus the reason for his nickname, Hollywood Star.
This publication is of the Fighting Spirit Illustrated North American branch and finds most all of its distribution in North America, however, Von Traps name may have additional meaning if youre an avid fan of Mixed Martial Arts or have imported MMA tapes from overseas. Von Trap, has, infact, competed in mixed martial arts on one prolific occassion. That would be a 45 second fiasco some time in the year 2003. You see, Von Trap was coming off his biggest break in B-Movies when his battle scene with B rate action movie king Dan "The Tiger" Lee (another name most people have no clue about) began getting some good reviews from internet critics. It seemed as though Von Traps stock was rising up. The film was SUPER FIGHTERS 8, it came out in the states in 2001. With his stock rising, so did his intake of drugs and amphetamines. He was out of films for several years and almost lost his life twice. Von Traps agent finally got him something, in 2003 but it wasnt a film appearance. Von Traps agent was friendly with some Japanese producers and those friends of Von Traps agent desperately needed somebody to fill in for their real fighting competition event in Japan, a MMA show. You see, there was a scheduled bout out there but one of the two men couldn't make it and they were hard pressed for somebody to show up in one days notice. The Japanese could've grabbed any old judoteka from Japan but they didnt want some plain character. They talked to Von Traps agent to see if he knew any colorful American martial artists and Von Traps agent knew just the man. Von Trap arrived in Japan drunk and disorderly 5 hours before his MMA debut and his only shot at retribution. Von Trap, a skilled martial artist, but nothing too great, was in way over his head. He stumbled into the octagon and was blatantly drunk as anybody could point out if they viewed the tape. Infact, this writer can still recall the laughing of the Japanese commentators calling the action as they watched Von Trap walk right into the octagon cage several times before finding the door. Von Trap was utterly abused for 25 seconds of the match and blood was getting everywhere. To this day I do not know why the referee did not call the match on account of a TKO. To his credit, Von Trap started to awaken when he realized blood was pouring out of his face. He dodged a few moves from his opponent but at the 33 second mark, the unthinkable happened. His opponent took a long swinging punch that Von Trap somehow ducked. His opponents fist swung around and hammered himself in the face, knocking himself unconscious. The referee and the crowd watied until the 45 second mark just to see if this whole thing was serious and, low and behold, it was. Von Trap won the fight when his opponent knocked himself out. It was a rebirth of sorts for Von Trap. It was several hours after the event at a Japanese post fight press conference when Von Trap dubbed himself "Hollywood Star" when the media asked who he was and where he came from. His only MMA win was later changed to a no contest when officials found him testing postitive for marijuana in the mandatory pre fight drug test. However, Von Trap had made connections backstage at the event. In what he describes as a conversation that went from politics to women to baseball, Von Trap was talked into training for pro wrestling by these Japanese men. 6 months of training and Von Trap left Japan to continue his acting career and to begin his pro wrestling career. Von Trap has worked a few independent shows in his hometown of Hollywood California, wrestling under the name Vinny Von T for the first few appearances until just using his regular name. Von Trap has also continued his acting career and that is where this writer found him.
It took me a half a dozen calls until I could finally reach Von Trap and several more calls to finalize an interview. I should've given up, I know, but I am a determined writer and the challenge of getting this thing done intrigued me. It was some thirty miles away from Von Traps Hollywood estate on the set of his latest movie, The Attack of the Zombies 27, that I was finally given the chance to sit down and talk to him. Von Trap seemed surprisingly awake and surprisingly up for the interview. I had heard stories of his incredible wardrobe but Von Trap wasn't in his typical suit, no, he was sporting a pair of sunglasses, his hair tied back in a ponytail. He had a shirt that said and I recall it perfectly well and the picture will even verify it, "Yeah ladies, it's true." He was wearing beaten up jeans and oddly, what appeared to be very expensive aligator skin shoes.


Chad Spacely (CS): Hey Vince, thanks alot for taking time out of your work today to talk to us. It is a real pleasure.

Vince Von Trap (VVT): Yeah, I know.

[Authors note: yeah, we're getting off on the right foot.]

CS: Right, so, Mr. Von Trap, you've been called alot of things in your day, some say you're an up and coming actor, an ambitious actor, but others say you're a typical martial arts jock with some good looks whose gotten lucky. What do you think about this?

VVT: Yeah, I've also been called the african giant by a couple of the ladies, bro... hahaha, you know what I mean?

[Authors note: I dont think he knew that my recorder was on at that point.]

VVT: Oh, is that thing on over there, oh really? Oh, sorry. Okay, right. Well you know dude alot of people don't like me because I'm probably their father in reality so it's like you know man, you know what I mean, it's like I knocked up their mom or something and they're frustrated cause they don't have a father figure. You know?

CS: Seriously, Mr. Trap.

VVT: Whoa, whoa.. Hang on a second dude. Lets get something straight turkey toucher. It's not Mr. Trap, it's Hollywood Star Vince Von Trap, okay? That's what it is. You call me anything short or anything different than that and this interview is over buddy. Got it? Another thing, you want me to be serious? Well come on bro, look at me, I have a million dollar look, people are gonna be jealous. As far as that other stuff, ambitious? Up and comer? Dude I am a freaking oscar award winning actor, the academy just hasnt given me any awards yet because well, its political.

CS: Okay, I apologize Mr. Hollywood Star Vince Von Trap. I apologize. Next question. Why dont you talk a little bit about this movie your doing right now, that would be Attack of the Zombies 27.

VVT: Okay, cool. You don't gotta call me Mr. but whatever. Yeah, well this is Attack of the Zombies 27 just another flick I'm workin' on but I've got a lot of projects going on right now. It's cool, you know? I'm actually in talks with Lee (authors note: He's obviously referring to B rate action movie king Dan The Tiger Lee) about doing something with him, thats what Im really focusing on. This gig here isn't that big of a deal, Im not really in this flick for too long. No big deal if I say it because its better that the ladies hear it from me than when they see it in the film, but I do die in this flick. Theres a scene where the Zombies get me and yadda yadda yadda but whatever. Yeah, I play a role thats really hard for me to play. I'm a deep thinker bro, I am a really introverted guy man, I ain't really big into the money the looks and all dude I really ain't. Thats why this film is kinda important man 'cause it puts me out of my comfort zone. I play the role of Jimmy Vega in this flick, I'm a martial artist guy whose big into sports and in this film I like fighting and I like the women and its my gung-ho attitude and rebel nature that gets me into hot water with the zombies and they get me. So, if you knew anything about me as a person you'd know that is so far from who I am deep in my soul so it's been a challenge but I think the academy and the critics will appreciate my broadening my horizons as an actor.

CS: Uhm, Mr. Hollywood Star Vince Von Trap, arent most of your movies about you being a martial artist fighting people?

VVT: Huh?

CS: Anyway. Well this is Fighting Spirit Illustrated and we wont get too caught up in things not pertaining to professional fighting. Its been a couple of years since your one and only mixed martial arts fight in Japan. You won the fight but it was very controversial, in fact, several days later, the decision was reveresed, Mr. Hollywood Star Vince Von Trap, what could you say about this?

VVT: Well dude let me just say that I won the fight and I dont think the pot helped me. Infact, it hindered me a little bit because if you watch that tape you can see that I was touched a little bit in the beginning.

CS: Touched? Mr. Hollywood Star Vince Von Trap, you were a little more than touched. Your opponent had a 98% connect ratio and you had some pretty severe bruises and there was alot of blood on your face.

VVT: What? I dont remember that and that isn't on my tape. What were you watching? You sure you were checking out my fight? Hey, what kind of publication is this anyway? You guys print the truth in your magazine? I hope you guys didnt write that. Thats not the way the world saw it!

CS: Please, continue

VVT: Well yeah man I mean I won the fight. Everybody knows I'm undefeated in MMA. Nobody out in Japan wants a piece of Hollywood Star Vince Von Trap. Thats why I was never invited back to participate. They know what I can do and they dont want a foreigner that looks this good to be the star of their organization.

CS: Well your opponent from that evening has issued countless offers of a rematch, care to comment?

VVT: Dude, I would kick his ass any day he wants. I already beat him once, what the hell more is there to prove? If I can kick his ass faster? That's such a dopey jock sort of thing. Like I said dude, I am a deep thinker. I'm not into that.

CS: So you're denying the challenge?

VVT: Dude, I ain't denying anything. Yeah, I'll fight him, but talk to the promoters of that MMA group. Dude, they don't want any part of VVT. I made alot of money and good friends in Japan, I don't have any reason not to fight. I love fighting, I'm the greatest martial artist of all time. Everybody knows this stuff though man. They dont want me. I'm interested in beating up people for money so I'd take his challenge or anybody elses.

CS: Very interesting. So yeah, you met up with some pro wrestlers in Japan and thats where you trained, any comments?

VVT: Yeah man, about half way on the way to my first day I learned how to wrestle like the greatest wrestler ever. Really, the whole 6 months was me teaching them how to wrestle, how to use martial arts and how to play baseball.

CS: I see. Care to tell the truth?

VVT: Yeah, the truth is that. If you got something to say, then get the hell out of my face!

CS: Excuse me, excuse me. I'm really sorry. Please, lets continue.

VVT: Lets.

CS: Okay, so... You've come back to America, you've been making more films, what do you expect in your future Mr. Hollywood Star Vince Von Trap?

VVT: Okay man. I am the biggest free agent in pro wrestling. Thats what the deal is honey. I am the biggest free agent this sport has ever known but I wont let my ego get in the way and I am willing to negotiate a logical figure. I've got alot of interest in me by several companies here, in Mexico, Canada and Europe. It's only a matter of time.

CS: So Vince, what do you-

VVT: Vince? Whoa! WHOA! Dude..what the hell is your deal man? Why all the negative energy? Why the hostility? I haven't been with your mom in eight weeks, whats your problem? I told you man, it's Hollywood Star Vince Von Trap, why is that so hard?

CS: I'm sorry, Holly-

VVT: No. No no no. This is over dude. It's over. I'm a busy guy. I don't have time for this. You have no respect man. Look, I ain't trying to be negative brah, but you gotta learn how to conduct an interview better. I'm out of here man. You got a real problem.

CS: Mr. Holly

VVT: Nope, Nope. I'm out of here.


That was the end of my interview with Von Trap. He just got up and walked away. I didn't know how sensitive he was and I called him later and left several messages with his voice mail. He eventually called back and said he didn't remember me and thought I was a stalker. The interview that was just presented was editted from several hours of conversation, most of which was about Vince Von Trap talkin about Vince Von Trap. Hes a man, Outside of the Ring, and although I hate to admit it, whenever he gets into the ring, it may mean serious trouble for any opponent.*
