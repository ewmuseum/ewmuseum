Empire Pro Wrestling — Roleplay

Destiny

3 March 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
SCENE BEGINS

(Dark room. No light. Can't see a thing. But we can hear. In the background, there is the faint sound of what might be a fan spinning slowly; perhaps a regular ceiling fan, or maybe from the ventilation. Somewhat less distinct is the quiet scratching sound, like a pencil scrawling itself on paper. After a moment, the sound stops... and is replaced with the squeaking of chair legs against the floor as someone pushes back and stands up. Retreating footsteps, followed by a sudden flash of light which is a door opening.)

(Through the light, we see the sillhouette of a man. A moment later, he steps out, and closes the door behind him. A small light comes on from overhead, illuminating the spot in front of the camera. Next to a steel chair is a small decoratory table, holding several sheets of paper and a pencil. The camera tracks in and pans down, allowing the paper to fill the frame. It appears to be a letter, written with unremorseful legibility.)

(The droning atmosphere sounds fade out. In comes Beethoven's "Moonlight Sonata". A clip of Karl Brown's voice is played.)

Karl Brown
"Let me begin by addressing Troy.

You are right in studying your opponents. And I’m glad that you have been able to get such thorough information about myself and Rocko. It gives you an approximate idea of what to expect..."

"Expect..."

"Expect..."

(As it echoes out, Troy Douglas' voice can be heard.)

Troy Douglas
"Rocko, though we've never faced each other, or even really met, I feel you and I have something in common. A past of loss, pain, and sh*t neither of us would rather talk about. The difference between the two of us is, that right now I'm the one whose got his attention focused on the ring. And hey, that ain't your fault. In three weeks with this company, you've gone from a bye in the world title tournament, to facing one of the most dangerous men in the industry, to finding out that a couple hours after you lose to him that this very same man had quit the company. I mean, that's a lot to go through..."

"to go through..."

"to go through..."

(Fade to a shot of Rocko Daymon, in his last match in Superior Championship Wrestling. Laying face down on the stage, the entity known as Clapper applies the Black Noose submission. Daymon, having suffered countless trials through all the pain and agony of that epical battle against the mysterious man from his past, and fighting off a slipped disk in his neck that would maim his ability to walk for at least another year, sinks into the black abyss and passes out.)

Karl Brown
"I’m not overly interested right now in winning the IC title. I’m here to test my skills against you and Troy. If I get to face Adam Benjamin at Black Dawn, then I’ll have interest in winning that title, because it’ll be a show that my skills have improved..."

"improved..."

"improved..."

(Fade to somewhat similar footage of Troy Douglas passing out from the Black Noose, applied by Clapper, in the final moments of the titanic X-Treme Title match.)

Troy Douglas
"THAT folks, is leaving it all on the line. That match took me to places I never thought I'd go, physically and psychologically. I nearly broke a man's neck, twice. I nearly broke my back against a sink, fell through a table and had 274 thumbtacks sticking out my back, and got dragged around on concrete by a speeding car. Finally, I withstood the most devastating hold in wrestling today and PASSED OUT COLD before I would utter the words "I Quit". Because I will never quit..."

"never quit..."

"never quit..."

(Fade to footage of Rocko Daymon's assault on the ring at Aggression. First he lamblasts Karl Brown with the chair, then follows it with a direct hit on Troy Douglas.)

Karl Brown
"It’s about who you choose now is it? Sorry, Rocko, but I’ve never bowed down to the whim of an opponent. And I don’t intend to start for someone as pathetic as you. You won’t choose who wins this match. That’s still to be decided. In the ring. There are three of us. It’s not in your hands. It’s in our hands."

"in our hands..."

"in our hands..."

(Fade back to the letter on the table. A recorded message of Rocko's voice is played back.)

Rocko Daymon
"Four men in the running for a second-rate belt... myself included, yet I'm hardly interested. Three of these men must compete among eachother...

In the end, what it comes down to is not who is the most determined, or talented, or focused, or non-hypocritical..."

(Dramatic pause.)

Rocko Daymon
"It's all about who I choose..."

(Slow zoom into the letter, and Rocko's voice begins to dictate.)

Rocko Daymon
To my esteemed opponents,

There must be a lot on your minds now, and I can understand why. But I want the both of you to know that before I explain my actions, I've been down the same road many times before. Unless you can handle walking it, perhaps you should think twice before ever signing a match against me a second time...

From the very moment it was announced that Karl Brown, Troy Douglas, and myself would meet vying for a chance to fight Adam Benjamin for the Intercontinental Title... I never had any intention to win. Rather, I would observe what either of you had to say... and in the end, I would make my decision of who would be given another opportunity, and who would be damned.

Karl Brown... you were damned.

Troy Douglas... I admire you. As you said yourself, though we never formally met, we have a lot in common. I can't help but to feel humble in your presence, after what you've been through... to obtain the title you already have.

Shall I tell you a story?

The same man who handed you the X-Treme Title at Battleground Britain... was once an opponent of my own. He was my last opponent during my tenure at SCW. Like your match, ours was long and brutal... something that no average second-rate wrestler like Karl Brown could survive in one run. At the end of that match, I was so broken and beaten, that I was forced to leave wrestling for a year...

You've had that time on the bench yourself... haven't you? Then you know where I'm coming from. You know what it's like to be standing outside the circle, looking... wishing you could be back in that ring, wrestling up a storm, wowing those fans and using your God given talent to its fullest. You KNOW that sort of pain, Douglas, and for that, you gained my respect...

But the fact that you went through the same hellacious match against Clapper... and SURVIVED, is what convinced me to give you this opportunity. You did what I could not, and for that reason, you deserve MORE than the Intercontinental Title...

I only ask, Troy... that you won't be deceived by my actions. I apologize for having to get physical, and for costing you the match... but please understand that there is more to gain from this than what has been lost. You see, Troy... you are BETTER than the Intercontinental Title. You don't deserve to be held down at the level of Karl Brown and Adam Benjamin; you have worked to hard to settle with that.

Again, I am sorry for what I did to you... but I didn't think you'd understand if I told you. I had to take matters into my own hands. I hope you make every use of this opportunity... and use it as a second win for what will hopefully be your future attainment of Empire Pro's World Title...

But for Karl Brown, there will not be that sort of blessing. There will only be the... "honor" of being the Intercontinental Champion, or at least the runner up. For someone who thinks so highly of himself, Karl... you certainly have no qualms about being as Empire Pro's "second best".

For as long as I’ve known you, you’ve looked down your nose to me… openly admitting that you are “disgusted” with how I live my life. Maybe so, Karl… but my life is none of your business, and you have YET to prove you are better than me.

I watched your promos… and I must say, I was disappointed to hear such shallow, simple-minded words and phrases. I have to ask, Karl… what makes you think you’re better than me? Is it the fact that you win your matches by putting your feet on the ropes, or by the aid of interference? Or is it simply because you find a flaw in your opponent, and focus on that as opposed to yourself? It’s for these reasons why you’ve always been a step behind your fellow Englishman, Adam Benjamin. Since your debut in MCW, you have always lacked the spirit, pep, and tenacity to be something more than the average muscle-head in the ring. But you fail to be anything unique.

To suggest that my composed piece of footage was a feeble attempt to draw “pity” is a laughable statement. Do you think I was really trying to get sympathy, Karl? No… now it’s obvious, that I was warning you. I played the part of destiny, and decided to give you this small victory, which would be the first step in your dull and boring mid-card career in Empire Pro.

Do you remember when you said that it WOULDN’T be based on my decision? I suppose you’re eating those words now; shame on you for never expecting the unexpected. I am full of surprises, Karl… as I proved when I consciously pulled you over the fallen body of Troy Douglas, based on my CHOICE! Obvious evidence that on a mental level, I’m one step ahead…

You speak too much, Karl… you pick away and criticize, but fail to look at yourself. How could you know anything, Karl? This isn’t about what happened in the past… but the present didn’t just appear on itself, did it? I am who I am because of what I was; for the same reason, you are nothing more than a mid-card clown because you couldn’t do anything to make yourself better in the past few months, in MCW and here in Empire Pro. How could you understand what I’m going through, when you haven’t been in my shoes?

I’ve been at the top of the game, and right now I’m at the bottom. Losing must be so simple for you, but you’re young; you have a long career ahead of you, roughing it in the mid-card leagues without anybody ever being able to remember your name. Losing is something that you’ll get used to.

(The writing becomes fairly illegible.)

Rocko Daymon
But me? I’ve been in this business longer than you’ve had hair on your dick. I have crawled on my belly through BROKEN GLASS to get where I am today. I fought off countless opponents, defended numerous talents, suffered more damage to the head than I can REMEMBER, and how am I rewarded? I get f*cked in the ass with a pineapple by that b*tch Paul Freeman! I have every RIGHT to be pissed off, Brown! I have BLED and SWEAT professional wrestling! But because of one executive’s decision, I have been DENIED my right of being justly rewarded! I have been EXCLUDED from the World Title tournament, just to fulfill HIS vision of… what he thinks is a better match…

So next time you decide to hire a cameraman and flap your lips, exposing your horribly disfigured teeth and affecting my demeanor with your excruciatingly painful accent, remember that the past makes the present; I have seen more, therefore I know more. In addition, I give more, and therefore I AM more than you.

I decided to give you the win against Douglas… because I want to see Adam Benjamin humiliate you in the middle of the ring at Black Dawn. The Pay Per View event… your BIG night, against your old nemesis, who you could never prove yourself better than. And if you win? Then hey, good job, “bloke”. But you still have a long way to go before your reach my level.

In the meantime, Troy Douglas and I will be looking to get ourselves back into the main event matches, vying for that World Title, or at least to thing considered MORE worthy than the second rate IC Title. We will go on to greater things, and you will be left behind… struggling to fend off Adam Benjamin and other mid-carders and newcomers to put food on the table. You will be forgotten, Brown… like I almost was.

I hope the both of you gentlemen understand where I’m coming from. If not, then perhaps it will take some time. At this point, I can only promise you that I will not have a hand in any of your future plans, just as long as they don’t interfere with my own. Good luck to the both of you in your prospective paths.

I leave you with this: In this industry, you are either the best, or you are nothing. Regardless of where I stand, I will not rest until every last man and woman involved in Empire Pro feels my pain.

Sincerely,
Rochester Vincent Daymon

(Fade to black.)
