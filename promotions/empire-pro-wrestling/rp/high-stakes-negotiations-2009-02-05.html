Empire Pro Wrestling — Roleplay

High Stakes Negotiations

5 February 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(FADEIN: An office. On one side of the room is The First, Felix Red, and a man who's most likely their lawyer. First is wearing his black three piece suit with a white tie, and horn rimmed-glasses, his hair pulled back into a pony tail. Felix is wearing a purple business suit with his dreads pulled back into a goofy pony tail. Ryoko on the other side of the room, wearing a grey business suit with a shirk cut below the knee, sits beside another man in a suit, most likely her lawyer. We see The Forsaken's lawyer talking for a moment, and then he hands over a piece of paper to Ryoko who looks at it.)

RYOKO: Look, I have no recollection of signing this thing what-so-ever.

FELIX: Take some personal responsibility. Did I tell you to drink a wine glass of liquid GHB to balance out the meth you were tweeking out on?

RYOKO: Probably.

FELIX:....actually, yeah, i did. Sorry

RYOKO: So yeah, there's no way you can argue that I still have to be your manager for life.

FELIX: Oh, but there is....and I'd be wrong, but it would take a long time and cost a lot of money to prove it.

FIRST: We've had our lawyer work out what we think is a fair compromise that will prevent this from getting into a messy and protracted legal battle...

(The First nods at his lawyer who then pulls out another sheet of paper and hands it across the table to Ryoko's side...Ryoko's lawyer looks over it for a moment before handing it to her to look at.)

RYOKO: (reads over contract)..........(sigh) Fine. But you're still an a$$hole, Felix.

FIRST: What about me?

RYOKO: You're okay, I guess. Who are you again?

FIRST: I'm the First, I'm fighting for the EPW World Title on the next show.

RYOKO: Really? I've watched EPW off and on...Triple X is gonna kill you, that guy is a beast.

FIRST: Man I can't buy a break...Even my new manager is betting against me.

RYOKO: Oh right, you're the goth guy with the make up and stuff, I you looked different all cleaned up...Oh geez, that means you two hang out with that fat freak cross dresser, I want a stipulation in this contract I don't have to be ringside when he's out there...(Shudders)

FELIX: No Dice

RYOKO: Ugh...Fine...I do this against my will...(Ryoko takes out a pen and signs the contract.)

FIRST: No you didn't...

RYOKO: What?

FIRST: You didn't do that against your will...You have free will, we all do, and you made that decision willingly, you may not have liked it, but you did it anyhow, I hate when people claim things are against their will.

RYOKO: You're becoming an real jerk, First.

FIRST: I guess, but I'm still far down the line from being as horrible to you as Felix was...

RYOKO: Yeah...But I've had fun with Felix before...You're not fun...


(Awkward pause)

FELIX:......We're totally going to do it again, you know.

(Ryoko glares at Felix)

RYOKO: I'll see you at the arena, and that's it...

(Ryoko gets up and walks out of the room quickly, her lawyer gets up and follows her out the door.)

FELIX: Yeah, I'm gonna hit that again...(glances at First) Ever Eiffel Towered a chick?

(First shakes his head.)

FIRST: I thought you had a kid on the way with someone else...

FELIX: Uh...she miscarried, actually. It was a bummer.

FIRST: Oh...Geez...Sorry...(Exhales as an awkward silence hits)...

I think she'll warm up to Gotho in time...

(CUEUP: "Hot and Cold" by Katy Perry...)

FELIX: What the? Where's that music coming from?!

(Gotho bursts into the room)

GOTHO: I'm young and I'm blonde and I've done a lot of cocaine tonight! Let's dance!!!

FELIX: Oh sure, why not?

FIRST: This just gets more surreal by the moment.

(First, Felix, and their lawyer all shrug and start a dance party)

(FADEOUT)
