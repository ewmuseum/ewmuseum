Empire Pro Wrestling — Roleplay

Let's get this out of the way...

9 January 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Scene fades in to the parking lot of the MCI Center in Washington. A black Lexus SUV swings into the parking lot and approaches the rear entrance, before stopping near the door. The passenger door of the SUV opens, and a big man, 6' 6", almost 300 pounds and in amazing shape, gets out of the car, his long blond hair flowing over his black leather jacket, and he reaches back into the SUV to grab a gear bag. After slinging it over his shoulder, he shuts the door and taps his hand on the car, and waves as the Lexus drives off, leaving the man to walk into the arena. As he opens the door and walks in, he's immediately greeted by some of the arena staff.

Staff #1: Beast?!? Good to see you!

Beast: Been a long time, Stan.

The two men shake hands.

Stan: But what are you doing here? Isn't A1E in England this week for the big PPV? Don't you have a tag title match?

Beast: Yup, we sure are. As a matter of fact, I gotta catch a plane in... (checks his watch) ... about 4 hours. The staff at Empire Pro asked me stop by on the way through and shoot a promo spot.

Stan: Empire Pro...? Wait just a darn minute... you're working for Empire Pro now too?

Beast: (laughs as he claps Stan on the shoulder) Nothing gets by you, does it? I just signed with them a few days ago. Going to be a huge launch, and it's great to be part of something new, and have a chance to shape something right from the beginning.

Stan: (shakes his head) Man, I don't know how you guys do it. And now you're doing it twice as much.

Beast: It takes a special breed, Stan, it really does. Just say it's something I love to do, and maybe I'm out to prove something to myself.

Stan: Oh man, you got that twinkle in your eye again... the boys in Empire Pro better look out. They don't know what they're getting themselves into.

Beast: (laughs again) I'll tell 'em you said that. Look, I gotta get running and get this spot done. You take care, alright? Maybe I'll see you at the first show.

Stan: I'll be there with bells on, Beast! It was good to see you again!

Beast: You too.

Beast turns and continues down the hall. A few moments later, he is met by a member of the Empire Pro staff, and they have a short conversation while the cameras are set up. Everyone scurries about, preparations are made, and soon, the spot starts...

Beast: You know, I've been in a lot of arenas across North America and in Europe, performing in front of a lot of sold out crowds... I've been in this very arena, competing as a member of A1E. I've had some good times in this arena, but now, there's something about stepping foot in here as a member of a new organization... there's a different vibe, a different rush about it. It's new.

As I sit here and look up and down the roster sheets, there are some very talented individuals that have joined Empire Pro... Dan Ryan... Rob Sampson... Jonathan Marx, who was part of A1E a long time ago... I know the quality of superstar that's going to be part of this promotion, and that's what's going to drive me here. The thrill of competing against new faces, in front of new fans... I have a feeling it's going to be great.

And I'll be damned if I don't take every opportunity I get and run with it.

Now, I know what some people are going to say... "Beast, you're a member of A1E first. You're just in Empire Pro for the paycheck. You're going to bail at the first sign of trouble, because you have A1E to fall back on."

All I'm going to say to those people right now, is...

Shove it up your (bleep).

I am here because I believe that I can still do it. I am here because I have the desire to do it. I am here because I want to be the very best - not only in A1E, but in professional wrestling.

Now, I'm not going to be like some of those others out there who walk into a new promotion and claim immediate dominance over everything they see within five minutes of walking in the door. I know I'm going to get there. But it will take time. It will take time for me to prove to the Empire Pro staff, the other guys in the back, and to the fans that are going to watch us night in and night out. But when I met with Mr. Freeman and signed on the dotted line, I made him a promise that he was going to get everything that the Beast had to offer, and I wasn't going to let him down.

I'm here, Empire Pro, and I plan on being here for an awful long time to come. I'm ready to compete.

But the question is...

Are you ready for me?

Pleasure meeting you.

Beast turns and walks off camera, and the scene fades to black.
