[NAWF] My new federation

13 August 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I *REALLY* need good RPers, as well as tag teams. Tag Teams are very much needed for my federation right now, as I only have one as of yet. NORTH AMERICAN WRESTLING FEDERATION INFO Last Updated: Thursday, August 7th * Sections 1. About your President 2. About the Federation 3. Rules 4. Other Info 5. Sheet ------------------------------------- Section 1. : About your President ------------------------------------- Hello. I am the president of the NAWF, and my name is [name withheld]. This first section is about myself as the handler, not the wrestler. Most of you probably know me as the handler of most of the wrestlers in the AC. However, some of you may not know this is not my first stab at running an e-federation. A while ago, I helped [name withheld] run the WIWF, which had a great following. However, due to lack of time (among other things), I decided to step down as co-prez of the WIWF. Following that, the federation closed down. I then headed to other federations, and did well, winning title with several of my characters. Then, in June of 1996, I started up the ACWF. It was a federation with wrestlers who were in the AC, and it was a lot of fun. However, on Tuesday, August 5th 1997, I closed down the ACWF. I had been debating whether or not to start up a federation that was open to everyone for sometime before that, but wanted to get a feel of some of the other federations on the 'net first. And now, I have done that. So, I have opened up the NAWF, to try and make a fun, popular federation, that will not be a so-called "loop" fed (term used to describe a fed that is connected with other feds on the NewNet IRC Channel #e-wrestling). I also will guarantee you no bias in my decisions. This federation will be based on roleplaying, angles, and also hype. Main Event matches are going to have more bearing on rankings then midcard matches, and jobber matches etc. ------------------------------------- Section 2. : About the Federation ------------------------------------- As of right now, not much has been decided as far as characters go. As far as I've decided, only a couple things have been decided. #1: Federation will be based in North America (hence the name) #2: To begin with, there will be only 1 card a week, taking place on Monday night. Name of the card TBA. #3: The main commontators for the NAWF will be Raul B. Dangerous (AC/Heel bias) and Boni Chivilranti (no-bias). The ring announcer will be Jeremy Singer. #4: The interviewers will be Rocky Roades, and Evan "Fatso" Belly. Both of these people will have no-bias. #5: Head of officals will be former manager Eddie Boulder. He'll be in charge of hiring a staff of officals. Among the things left to be determined are the titles (probably will be, in order of importance, the NAWF Title (NOTE: Not called a world title, called the NAWF Title), NAWF TV Title, NAWF Tag Team Title, and depending on number of lightweights, a NAWF Light Heavyweight title.) Also, there will be a limit of 3 roleplay's a week. These INCLUDE in-ring's (where your opponent goes to the ring and gets interviewed during a card, or comes out, grabs a mic, and starts ranting and raving at everyone). Flashes are to be sent to [detail withheld] , and make the subject Flash or something like that. In-Ring's are to be sent to [detail withheld] , and please make the subject In-Ring, or say somewhere in the letter that it's an in-ring. There will be 1 card a week, as I mentioned. The NAWF Title, the NAWF Tag Title, and the NAWF Light Heavyweight Title will be defended once a month. The TV Title will probably be defended every show, depending on some things. However, for the TV Title, it WILL be defended 2 times a month. Also, most titles will be on the line on PPV's, which will take place once every two months, or more depending on if we need an inter-fed PPV. Rankings will be decided on roleplaying, angles, and win-loss records. So, having a great win-loss record will be good, but not saying much to a champion might put you lower then someone who is feuding with the champ. Angles will have to go through me. I don't want to see someone who doesn't RP enough come out and attack the NAWF Champion, just to try and get a title shot. That won't work to become a contender, because win-loss will still matter in rankings, and I won't put somebody who's 1-5 in the #2 contender spot. Flash packets will be sent out whenever I get enough, or just prior to a card. Update's will be sent out probably on Tuesday's and Friday's, and will include rankings, schedule's, news, rumors, and anything else I fit in. ------------------------------------- Section 3. : Rules ------------------------------------- I have only a couple of rules, and if they aren't followed, you can expect to be fired. #1. Roleplay WELL, or lose/get fired.. WELL means proper spelling, puncuation, and meaning. I don't want to have to read something that doesn't make sense, and if I have to, the commontators and fans might turn on you for doing bad roleplays. Also, if you go 2 weeks without roleplaying, expect to get jobbed out inside of 6 or 7 minutes, and possibly injured for a week or two. If you go one more week past that point, then expect to find a pink slip. #2. Keep in touch with me. If you're going on vacation, let me know. If you're sick, or have some deadly illness, or are paralyzed, at least try and let me know. If I don't know, I'll figure you are just not roleplaying, and you'll be come an un-official JTTS. #3. Don't complain TOO much. You'll lose if I decide you should lose. If someone out RP's you, or out strat's you, you'll lose. If somebody has an angle they want to run, and I like it, that angle might take effect over a match. And, if you lose to someone you don't think you deserved to lose too, then why not try and get revenge. Ask me for a Falls Count Anywhere match, or another such match, and really get into it. #4. If you don't like something, let me know, and I'll try to fix it. As simple as that rule sounds. ------------------------------------- Section 4. : Other ------------------------------------- Match results based on RPs, angles, and strats. If it gets into a dead head in RP's, or if it's close either way, a strat can help push you over the top. "A strat?" you may ask? Well, quite simply, a strat is a plan for the match. If the guy's a really big guy, mail me and tell me how you'll try and get him down. If the guy's quick, tell me how you'll try and slow him down. Tell me what move your guy will use, what he'll do in the ring, etc. MAKE YOUR CHARACTER REASONABLE! I don't want 175 pound guys who do gut-wrench powerbombs or tiger drivers. You can put that on, but I'll use my mind to decide if the move is possible. IE: Don't expect your light heavyweight to be doing too many top-rope powerbombs on 300 pound opponents. NO RIP OFFS. The moment I see somebody calling their guys "Stone Cold" Steve Boston, who hails from Texas, weighs 240 pounds, and uses the Stone Cold Stunning as a finisher, I might accidently hit Ctrl+D or delete on my computer...and it would be a shame to see those sheets go to waste, now wouldn't it? ------------------------------------- Section 5. : Sheet ------------------------------------- Here you go. Fill this out, and mail it back to me at [detail withheld] . I'll mail you back if it's accepted, and if I don't accept it, I'll mail you and tell you why. *** IMPORTANT: Limit of 1 singles wrestlers, or 1 tag team. Only accepting the first 16 wrestlers and 8 tag teams to start with. -------------------------------------==== Posted via Deja News ====----------------------- http://www.dejanews.com/ Search, Read, Post to Usenet
