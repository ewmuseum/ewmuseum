It's THE Column - Trends in eW

20 September 2005
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

Trend - Current style; vogue

Trends hit us everywhere, whether it’s the clothes you wear, the way you talk, or the latest in MP3 technology. We’re all susceptible. The trend is most prevalent in our hobby with upgrades and interactivity. Although that’s where some of us would like it go, most of us don’t. Now, before you close your browser and move on to another columnist’s article I’m going to my very best to leave the 3D character creation program, Poser out of the equation. With that said, our hobby has left it’s roots of great writing behind, and succumbed to flashy design and graphics overall, rather than what most of us claim to be here for – the writing.

The trend, for me, started with the 88X31 graphic link. The little buttons popularized by EWN in it’s first run and perfected, and slung over the top by the emerging [name withheld]-owned Wrestlecell. These link options were popular first from blogs, and adapted as eWrestling’s most favored way to give your pals a little props. Thus, the trend of getting that one ‘big fed’ to give you a link-back to put your promotion over started. Soon, and still, we found/find these little graphic links plastered all over some page with the extension ‘.links.html’. Someplace far down on the navigation menu of almost every eWrestling promotion you can find.

The 88X31 was where I started to see the proliferation of eW trends. Promotions seeing, and copying what they thought would put their promotion on the map. Also note, this had nothing to do with writing.

The second trend was self-hosted CGI. Common Gateway Interface revolutionized our hobby, and made it possible for our hobby to ‘trend’ (there’s that word) away from BBS and email to roleplay boards and out of character forums. Typically Fed Heads found a free site and had their boards hosted there. Then came the opportunity to have your own CGI, on your site. Now, CGI was around before eWrestling, and it was being used before the 88X31 was popularized, but it didn’t get it’s full stride until communities like EWN and Wrestlecell showed us it’s power. CGI, hosted on your site went from simple message boards to fully functioning backstage management tools to make an ePromotion run smoother, and faster for owners and handlers alike. CGI has given way to PHP and even some database managed ASP. Now, while the latter are tougher to find, they’re out there, and they’re pushing yet another trend in eWrestling; interactivity at little to no time cost to the owner/webmaster. This is probably the most productive trend eWrestling has seen, albeit again, having nothing to do with writing.

Professional design came into being a couple years ago. Efed owners saw the power of CGI, and realized the meaning of having a professional site with CGI, and all the bells and whistles. In this same category is of course Poser. This topic of ‘the trend’ has been beat to death on every forum in eW. So to spare this dead horse another beating from yet another eW columnist I’ll stick to the design overall. Solid ‘branding’ of a promotion with a customized logo, and various other areas was important, and still is. So important it’s overwhelmed the writing side of our hobby. Handlers look for the flashy design to draw visitors in, and worry more about their bio page than what takes place with their character weekly. This can partly be blamed on money, which brings me to the next trend…

Money. Money in eW is a fabled thing. Some old-school fogeys like myself have heard the tales of [name withheld] and the deal with EGN. If you haven’t, you’re too new, or you don’t care. I hope you’re of the latter category, if not mail me and I’ll enlighten you. In any case, money is probably the root source problem. We have designers whoring themselves out to make that flashy design mainly for feds that don’t emphasize writing. The design hauls the page views in, and management convinces the handlers that they’re popular. All the while the owner paid cash for a solid design for a mediocre fed. Regardless, these feds are out there. Running right now. You know who they are, and if you don’t, you’re probably enamored by the design and don’t care, or you’re in one and believe the hype.

There’s certainly trends I haven’t touched on, some you have noticed, some that you hate, either way eW is trend based. With all the bad, it brings good. The eWrestling community has been in a slight decline with pro wrestling at the core of the problem. However, flashy designs, and Poser work still do reel some rookies in. For that, old losers like myself are thankful; it’s the byproduct we hate.

What’s next? Well for the last five years people have been talking about ‘live’ shows, fully animated in Poser. Other steps forward are certainly too far advanced for me to even imagine. I hope and pray the next trend is writing. I hope we see email feds return and revolutionize our hobby into one where all that is required are communities like these to keep us connected. Promotions with shows that mean something. Products that make us really want to read what happens next week, not so we can seem cool on EWN’s forums by being knowledgeable about Sylo’s current angle, but because we’re truly interested in the quality writing that our hobby offers.

Keep writing, keep trying…

- k y l e
