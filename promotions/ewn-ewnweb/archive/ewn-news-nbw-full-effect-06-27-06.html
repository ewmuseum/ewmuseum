nbW: Full Effect 06-27-06

29 June 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

After the last episode of nbW's Full Effect, it seemed the newly debuted tag team of Heaven and Hell was caput after Heaven's outburst. But after Hell did some spin doctoring to Xander Napoli the team has been reinstated, what else is in store is anyone's guess, but Hell pulled off a nice singles victory in his debut single match.

Apparently the golden boy of nbW has now turned into its prodigal son as Alan Helms has defected to LoC. What this means for the Keystone/Dynasty unification match at Twilight against Cameron Cage is unknown, though Napoli's muttering's of Plan B are intriguing.

Hardcore heavyweight macWICKED is still on the rise and now looks to be going for a 4peat victory next week against everyone's favorite nutjob Lunatic. However, he and Zatch Rollins don't seem to be finished either, as the ninja, fresh off a victory over Harvey Grimm, confronted mac backstage demanding a rematch.

Austin Advent appealed to the Full Effect fans saying he was turning over a new leaf. Honestly. Whether he's serious or not remains to be seen. J-Funk in a seeming turmoil after being placed in the newly formed hardcore division, Blitzkrieg, seemed to have a lightbulb go off after a comment from his female companion Glitter Shimmers, what the pimp of funk has in store is anyone's guess.

Andrew Martin made his return after his controversial hiatus and definitely made his presence felt to all of nbW. Determine to show why he was King of the Mountain, he was prepared to deliver an ass-kicking in Full Effect's Main Event against mysterious newcomer Nemo. Xander Napoli however, seemed to be one step ahead of Martin and set a shocking stipulation to the match. The ending to Martin v. Nemo shocked everyone in nbW and it appears that Martin's run-in with Xander isn't about to be over.

Also be sure to check out the latest Star Front for nbW's Superstars for Full Effect.
