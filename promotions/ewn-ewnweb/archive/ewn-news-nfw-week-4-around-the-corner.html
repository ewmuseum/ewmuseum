NFW: Week 4 Around The Corner

3 October 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

With the first quarter stretch in the books, New Frontier Wrestling moves into Week 4 with its first interconference matches of the season containing several marquee matches, grudge matches as well as some dream matches that you'd have to find on an interfed supercard anywhere else in the world.

Over 48 hours, two shows will broadcast from Tokyo, Japan and Las Vegas, Nevada and there's certainly some tension rising in the locker rooms as some wresters find themselves in must-win situations to stay with the leaderboard pack, while other wrestlers have the opportunity to create some distance between themselves and competitors.

In Tokyo, two interconference matches have alot of critics whispering in anticipation as Rook Black, the only West Conference wrestler with 30 points takes on 'The Boy Who Destroyed the World' Felix Red. Black, the last tSC Championship title holder faces the odd, yet highly decorated fwrestling.com stalwart in Red. The other interconference match on everyone's lips is the all-star cruiserweight affair between Lindsay Troy and Seymour Almasy. Craig Miles and Troy Windham will make the trip to Tokyo as well from the Western Conference. Miles in tag team competition against Doc Silver and Kazou Shizaki, his partner being last year's Ultratitle winner; Michael Manson. Troy Windham, the current CSWA World Champion will be facing hot new NFW sensation, Maggot.

In the Western Conference, Dan Ryan and Eddie Mayfield is on everyone's lips as the perennial interconference match on the card, but that's only part of the stellar lineup including Scott Riktor's official Ultratitile competition debut as well as the highly anticipated Triple Threat match involving Jeff Garvin, Frank Heimdal and Mike Randalls.
