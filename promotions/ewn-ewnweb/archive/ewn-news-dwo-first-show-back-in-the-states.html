DWO - First show back in the States!

12 March 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

On March 10th the DWO returned to the states with a bang. The city of choice for the return was Los Angeles, California and it was simply awe inspiring to see just how packed the arena truly was. The show went off without a hitch, everything going as planned until.. Vandalism struck. At some point near the beginning of the show three masked delinquents made their way inside of the DWO custom built underground arena and spray-painted several messages along the walls, the floors, and even across the janitors. It was quite odd really, but annoying nonetheless.

Ignoring the vandalism for the time being the scheduled matches were greeted with much success, the card of choice causing the LA fans to hit their feet on more than a few occasions during the span of the two hour show. Highlights were of course the backstage brawl between Luke Justice and rising star, Krys Branden. Brutal and quick paced the match was Branden’s from the get go, he came out with a win over Luke in just under a ten minutes.

Even though the backstage brawl was quite the fan shocker we cannot leave out the main event which featured the DWO’s Anarchism title being put on the line. The week before last at Insurrection, The Butcher made it very clear to current title holder, Arsenic Hail that he would be coming for her and her title. This week he got his wish when Hail accepted and Owner Callisto Bane threw down the stipulations.

The match would be a TLC (tables, ladders, and chairs) match, but not the normal kind you see everywhere else. Oh no, this is the DWO and thus a very serious addition had to be added to heighten the danger. This particular TLC match would be taking place in a DWO custom built cage made entirely out of razor wire. No cage meshing here kiddies, straight wire on all four sides with no top to keep the competitors inside.

The object was to win or retain the title, but there was yet another stipulation in addition to the razor wire cage and the Tables, ladders, and chairs. There would be no pin falls, there would be no submissions, and there would be no knock outs. Needless to say the fans were waiting in anticipation the entire night for this insane event. The main event came quickly enough and both Arsenic Hail and The Butcher were securely locked inside of the cage.

From the very beginning the match was brutal, each wrestler trying their hardest to take the other out without causing injury to their own selves in the process. During the span of the match both wrestlers kicked things into high gear with Arsenic going for the highflying, dangerously suicidal moves to slow down the much larger Butcher. While she was doing this The Butcher was combating her with hardcore brawling maneuvers to try and keep her grounded.

Several lacerations, much blood loss, and lots of gory goodness later Butcher took Arsenic down from the top of the cage with a massive Crucifix sit out powerbomb through THREE tables that had been lit on fire. Arsenic was thought to be down for the count but amazingly she sat up, yeah, she sat up and managed to stomp a mud hole into Butcher before setting him on fire. She was nice though, she did put him out.. With several chair shots to various parts of his body as well as his head!

In the end it took a very well calculated DDT from the top of the cage, landing Butcher on the top of his head, to take him out. Without much waiting Arsenic headed over the top of the cage and managed to get down to the other side in order to win the match and retain her Anarchism Championship Title. She’ll more than likely be on the shelf for a couple weeks, but after speaking to her backstage she sounded confident she would soon be back in the ring to defend her belt yet again.

Oh, about the vandalism backstage at the beginning of the show.. It continued throughout the show with the culprits still not being caught. A surveillance tape was shown to Owner Callisto Bane, depicting the three delinquents writing backstage, and a close up of just –what- they were writing. The tape showed Circle of Rage in large letters down the hallway, which could only mean one thing in the DWO.. Adam Stryker would soon be showing up. Without guessing one could imagine how angry this made the DWO Owner. Is Adam really coming back? What could that mean for the sixth slot for Lord of the Asylum that’s still open? Patience.. Time will tell.
