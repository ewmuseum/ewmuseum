nbW: Full Effect 10-23-05 - High Octane Fun

25 October 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

With one week left until 25 to Life, nbW’s Full Effect has it’s work cut out for it. After a huge show last week, Full Effect is showing to a sold out crowd and will further push the stakes going into next week’s pay per view.

Newcomer Delacroix, hot off a win from his debut last week decides to up the game by taking on William Arthur Regal this week. But with WAR being an experienced ring veteran, bent on winning at any cost, has the rookie bit off more than he can chew?

Rising star Ali Amore, recovering from his defeat at the hands of Jeff Garvin squares off against another newcomer to the federation, One Spot. Has Amore learned from last week, or will the past repeat itself?

Alan Helms teamed up with fan favorite Matt Haddon last week to take on Tepemkau and Wosret aka Torment. After fighting the entire match Helms tagged in a battered Haddon for the pin. But to Helm’s and the crowd’s dismay, Xander Napoli declared the winner of the tag match to be the number one contender for the Dynasty Title. What will go down between the two men this week and will Helms succeed in securing a shot for the belt?

After his brutal assault on Sean Hayes last week, Jack Owyns is back and determined to make the Heavyweight title his once again. Who will fall before the obsessed man and what price will Owyns be willing to pay to win the belt?

The Four Horsemen egged out a win over Keystone champion Callie Urban and her partner Dunkirk last week in a highly controversial match. How will Urban respond this week and what do the Horsemen have for an answer?

Spike Saunders and former friend Andrew Martin battled it out last week with Martin taking the victory. But as his nephew Mat Walton got in the ring a surprising face showed in Spike’s favor. Now that Saunder’s has reteamed with good friend O’Dell, will it be enough to defeat uncle and nephew in a tag match this week?

More eliminations are to be made before the pay per view and there’s trouble brewing backstage as well. Conflict arises and fists fly in the high octane lead in to 25 to Life.
