AWC: Fresh! on form

24 June 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

Tuesday's Fresh! broadcast was heralded by many as the best AWC weekly show yet, rivalled only perhaps by the 7th June show which culminated in a gripping and bloody Relentless title three-way match.

Just as on that show, Christopher Ashton was up to his usual antics, this time not turning up at the original time scheduled for his Relentless title shot, and then demanding his opportunity right at the end of the show. Ashton's ploy meant that Crimson O'Malec had to contest two tough matches in a row - but remarkably, the 5'3", 165-pound Irish-American won out in both, first combining with his partner Paddy O'Shea to nullify the threat of Kris Krimzon and The Educator, and then coming from behind to defend his Relentless championship in a thrilling finale against Ashton. O'Malec must surely now be considered AWC's one genuine blue-chipper.

Contesting that observation might be the cocky Transatlantic champion Alexander Strider. Forced to, against his will, take on the female Ellis Nash, with the threat of a title defence against her immediately after Solarized if she were to beat him, Strider managed to do the job - just about. Unwilling to directly strike Nash, Strider limited his offense to throws and suplexes, figuring that this would minimise the brutality on a night when "The Mongoose" was under close scrutiny. The details of Strider's ongoing trial are not known, but for obvious reasons, the AWC superstar will not want to give the opposition any ammunition with his actions on national television.

Elsewhere on the card, debutant Steven Xandrous, and Void, in his second match, both picked up victories. The Alliance championship league continued as The Full House won for the first time. Due to Ashton's actions, timings went out of sync slightly, and AWC ended up over-running their allocated time slot. This meant that there was no room for the customary Fresh!man announcement at the tail-end of the show; instead, Entertainment Manager David Harber specified that the winner of the weekly MVP-style award should be revealed via the official AWC website. He told us:

"Crimson O'Malec had another great night, and really, I can't look past him for Fresh!man. However, the logistics of giving him the prize don't quite add up. Crimson is already the holder of the Relentless title, and competing in both the Alliance and Frontier divisions. It would be unfair on both him and the competition to potentially push him into a fourth title division, and as such, I am instead choosing to reward the promising newcomer Steven Xandrous with the Fresh!man accolade."

Pearl went on to add: "James Brunt conducted the draw for us immediately after the show, and I'm pleased to announce that Xandrous' prize will be entry into the first Livewire championship match, due to take place when a third contestant has been found." Pact was the first contender drawn for this match after he won one of his two Fresh!man accolades.
