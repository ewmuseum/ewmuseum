Anarchy rules as rioting "bursts" (ha, ha) AWC's bubble

9 November 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

Sunday's taped edition of Fresh!burst showed the United States of America that the lawlessness in New Orleans following Hurricane Katrina could be a symptom of a far larger problem.

Unprecedented crowd rioting turned the memorial service at the end of the show into a heartless farce following its interruption by Adam Dick and Mike Wade, and the ensuing problem got so out of hand that AWC staff and fans alike thought their lives might be in danger.

David Harber has banned all AWC employees from commenting on the events, but it is surely certain that tough sanctions will be taken to prevent recurrences of such a shocking sequence of events.

After much consideration, Harber persuaded the majority of AWC's broadcast partners to run the show regardless. Video feeds of the primary riot flashpoints were spliced into the Sunday night show in place of some other planned segments to ensure that viewers took on board the full horrors of what happened at the Metro Center in Rockford, Illinois - the venue has now indefinitely banned the Atlantic Wrestling Club and indeed all wrestling promotions from using the venue. It is hoped that seeing the damage they caused will enforce a sense of responsibility on those who were present, and serve as a warning to other AWC fans of just how badly things can spiral out of control if they don't conduct themselves appropriately.

In the wake of these events, AWC is looking to double its security staff. Applications should be sent to David Harber at the Atlantic City, New Jersey headquarters. Harber is also looking to take on two additional referees after Senior Referee Michael Ryan expressed his horror at the extent of what his section will have to undertake this Friday at Triangles. The new employees will not be chosen in time to take any work off the current referees' hands for Triangles, though.
