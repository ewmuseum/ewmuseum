Fed Review: fWo

31 August 2004
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
  So, what's all the big fuss here? Well, ladies and gentleman this is the first Fed Review for the 'just returned' eWNweb. So, how should I exactly start this out is a good question? I mean, Jon came to me and asked me to write a review for fWo and all the changes that have happened to the site and the roster and all that recently. Whoa, I know what your thinking; an fWo review? Yes, I said a review for the fWo. Fans Wrestling Organization, a site ran, owned, and operated by not only [name withheld], but by every roster member. Many consider the fWo to be the best efederation on the internet. Well, we'll just see about that. Alright, let me give you a little taste of the things I'll be ranking the efederation on in these little 'fed reviews'. Graphic Design, Writing, OOC Atmosphere, and Overall Content. Alright, so there's the specifics but for this first review, I think I'm going to do it a little different. It's obvious to most that fWo doesn't have a working design up right now. And, it's kind of hard to see what their OOC atmosphere feels like since the fan boards aren't readily available. I'm going to give this review simply based on their writing, the story lines and the characters. The developments that have taken place in fWo over the past few weeks basically. So with that, it's time to get down to business. fWo members, and the owner Travis all promised us big things from the fWo site. There was promises of ground breaking scripting, new design, all the works. Everything that made an efed stand out 'eye candy' wise, they promised us. So, on the day that this was all suppose to happen, what did we get? Nothing. Nothing visually that is. We got a splash page that was uploaded late, and nothing more. But if you read and follow the fWo story, you got a lot more then that. We got some of the best writing we've seen out of any fWo complete roster in it's history. First they started dishing out results for their two 'new' efeds. That's right, fWo has developed a minor league's of the efederation world. OCW and the cWL. Most fans had already heard about OCW, but not many of them really new what to expect. If you had ever browsed the fWo site and even thought about joining you might have seen the OCW initials on the 'development' link that the site has. But, most fans didn't know it was ever going to be a reality. Well, OCW was more than a reality. OCW became the home to most of the Survivor 4 entrants, and a few fWo members as well. Originally dubbed Orlando Championship Wrestling, most fans were expecting it to be a glorified development territory. So, you'd apply to the fWo and get rejected; then they'd move you to the OCW? You couldn't be more wrong. The OCW is an efed of its own. They have got one of the best assembled rosters of any efed today. What should you expect from OCW? Nothing less then you expect out of the fWo, only a much more different product. Alright, so they also had a cWL card, and announced that they would be re-opening the cWL. Anyone who has followed this community or angle feds for anytime at all should already know what the cWL is, but incase you don't I shall enlighten you. The cWL or Championship Wrestling League was started by Ed Wilson (1976-2001) and at the time was considered by many to be the top efed on the internet. Many of the fWo stars of today got their start in Ed's cWL. Travis the owner of fWo even handled his own character in the cWL. So, when Ed passed away there were some things that didn't get finished. Well, upon fWo's rebirth of the cWL they decided to finish the card that was never released. Travis alone worked on this unfinished piece of work, and molded it into a story. Along with the first OCW card being posted on the fWo website, the cWL's show was posted as well. So, we had this new fWo. There was no site, there was no possible way of seeing the roster, but we had two cards. We had results for the first OCW show, and we had results for a cWL show. Where did the fWo fit in all of this? Not very much longer at all, the fWo would release it's first card since the 'mini' hiatus. Now it was complete, we had a story to try and piece together. What was next for the fWo? When was the OCW going to begin running as it's own promotion, and what should we expect from the cWL? Things in [name withheld] land have gotten a little confusing lately, but one thing is for sure; fWo will not be the same. They've lived through a deal with Disney, four separate survivor tournaments, and the abandonment of certain roster members. Those certain roster members have formed an alliance and created OCW. What will the fWo do about this? Will There be friendly competition between both, or will it be a heated rivalry? Only time will tell, and at this point that's all we have. fWo has nothing but time on their hands, and they are using it to their advantage. fWo is in the midst of telling an amazing story, and they are taking us all along for a ride. Oh yea, and if I were to grade fWo on their story telling, they'd get no less then an A+ - [name withheld]
