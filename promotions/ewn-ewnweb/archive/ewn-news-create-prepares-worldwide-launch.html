CREATE Prepares Worldwide Launch

21 September 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

The world's first worldwide wrestling promotion, CREATE, is making the final preperations for its worldwide launch on Wednesday, October 5th, 2005. The first-ever event will take place in Manchester, England, with a worldwide live broadcast throughout North America and Europe, as well as a live-to-tape broadcast on Japan's GAEA Sports.

CREATE's concept revolves around two armies, the Rudo and Tecnico armies, competing over three-month seasons for supremacy, but the first two shows will feature the wrestlers of the individual armies taking part in one-night competitions to crown the "Strongest", or leader of their army. The first show will be dedicated to the Rudos, while the second show (from Madison Square Garden in the US) will see the Strongest Tecnico crowned. Finally, the Strongest Rudo and Strongest Tecnico will go one-on-one on Wednesday, October 19th in Tokyo, Japan, with the winner becoming known as the first-ever CREATOR, or top wrestler in all of CREATE.

CREATE's website is still accepting applications from wrestlers from around the globe looking to compete on its worldwide stage. There's little time left, though, as Sunday night the rosters will freeze and the cards for the first two shows will go up. Already the biggest star of CREATE's roster is undoubtfully former fWo United States and Internet Champion and former IOW World Heavyweight Champion Xias, who will be among the frontrunners to take on the mantle of Strongest Rudo. One of his top rivals from Japan, former Jolt Ambassador Champion Kenjiro Ito, is looking to prevent that from happening.

Meanwhile, on the Tecnico side, the roster is full of some of the most hungry young talent in the world. Major American heavyweight prospect Juggernaut is looking to make his name in CREATE, but he'll have to contend with such young talent as masked Korean junior-heavyweight Psycho Mantis and the electrifying Chinese future superstar Ekin Lau.

Yes the first two shows of CREATE should provide the world with some incredible competition, as the two armies battle amongst themselves to determine who will lead. But the real war will be fought as the two armies will have to begin working together to attain victory over the other, and win the reward that comes with it- twice their normal pay for the entire duration of the season! With that kind of prize at stake, you know the competition will be fierce.
