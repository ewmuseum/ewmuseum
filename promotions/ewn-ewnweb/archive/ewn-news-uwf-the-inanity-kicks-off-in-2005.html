UWF: The Inanity Kicks off in 2005

19 January 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

It's been a long wait for fans of the Universal Wrestling Federation, but everyone's favorite wrestling promotion (voted most-likely-to-ride-the-short-bus in its 1977 Yearbook from Wrestling Promotion High School) finally kicked back into action this weekend ... and truly, what better way could there have been than passing out one-foot tall little Golden Imps (complete with little Golden Imp Penises) made by grade school age Laotian kids we keep in a warehouse with no ventilation to the best (... and not-so-best) in the promotion?

The seventh annual awards ceremony was an event for the ages. The best, brightest, and least likely to survive in the wild gathered together to reward their own, and they did so in style. UWF Owner Richard Hayes was in hand, giving out an award along with the head of the referee staff, Jerry "Pops" Kohler (who, surprisingly, was the only person on the show to make children cry, traumatizing a passing group of girl scouts at one point during his presentation). Spiff the Magic Monkey appeared with his legal counsel, Johnny Cockroach, to simultaneously present an award and continue his demand for justice after his role in the first (and likely last) "Flaming Monkey Match" at the UWF's FANdemonium PPV. Not only was World Champion Tsunami Oni in attendance, but his defective clone Bizarro Oni appeared with fan-favorite Big Ted's Arm to honor a few of their colleagues. Commissioner Jack Union made quite a showing, paired with a random eastern-European hooker brought by (*EDITOR'S REMOVAL), and boy was that star hoping his wife wasn't watching. There was even a tear-jerking moment as the Local NVA Company, the most successful tag team of the last three years, came close to blows (or rather a fork duel) before realizing they'd created a delicious desert and embracing on stage.

But the weekend certainly didn't end there, as the Official UWF Impy Awards was only the precursor to a night of debauchery and unbridled violence as only the UWF can provide! In a one-night, single elimination tournament, the very best of the UWF clashed: Multi-time Champion Malcolm Kennedy; PCW Hall of Famer "The Malice Man" Duke Williams; multi-time World Champion, UWF Hall of Famer, co-PTC Wrestler of the Year, and reigning PTC Global Champion "The Final Fantasy" Seymour Almasy; one-half of the reigning World Tag Team Champions Kenny Mercury; the reigning North American Champion, the behometh from the Siberian arctic, the Communist; one of the hottest stars coming into the event, a man with only one loss to his name, "The Savior" Wes Diable; reigning Intercontinental Champion and purveyor of Dutch Man-Love, "The Dutch Destroyer" Nick Persia; a man with an uncanny resemblance to a troll doll, the Dreammaker; and reigning Television Champion and undefeated Hot Mask.

Nine of the best qualified for the event, but at the end of the night only one man outlasted them all, as reigning World Heavyweight Champion Tsunami Oni was on hand to witness. But the night certainly had more to it than just that, as the votes finally came in to decide once and for all! ... the leader of the Bunny Army of Doom: Laguna, creator of the Army, who had spent over a month campaiging for the job ... or perhaps the most talented technical artist alive, Chris Storm, homeless after every intelligent wrestling promotion in the world had refused to touch him after Storm oversaw yet another promotion's collapse with RPW. Duke Williams came face to face with his fellow Primetime Championship Wrestling Hall of Famer, the eternal demon Clyde ... or he WOULD have if Duke had actually been standing at that point. A wrestling legend returned ... only to come face to face with what happens when you try to get by without paying your production crew. And a contingent of fellow PTCers from Lights Out Wrestling even stopped by for a nice little chat ... something about infidel mortals or what not. It was silly.

All that and a bag of chips ... because the UWF is back in action for one more year of traditional, old fashioned, testicle-removing wrestling.

"I'm a mama kangaroo! Hoppy hoppy hoppy!" ... The Universal Wrestling Federation
