EWN (eWrestling Network) — New Frontier Wrestling's own season report, on a third party's desk

KVC, Youngblood Among Week 5 NFW West Surprises

12 December 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

In front of a sold-out PIT in Albequerque, New Mexico, New Frontier Wrestling's Western Conference went through a night of swerves, revelations, upsets, hosejobs and stunning debuts.

From the formation of Calvin Carlton and Troy Windham's New South Enterprises with Jeff Garvin, to the main event heard 'round the world featured the wrestling debut of former fWo World Champion 'Kodiak' Vic Creed against 'Cocky' Craig Miles, as well as the stunning...'victory' for Eddie Mayfield over Tom Adler for the once-defunct, but now active UWA World Championship?

A four hour event that had 19,000 people screaming till their throats had nothing left, New Frontier Wrestling brought down the house with 10 conference matches.

In the early going, Seymour Almasy def. Uriel to become the first West wrestler that night to 40 points in the conference and with the MANY roster changes, especially in his group...'The Final Fantasy' pulling off the win certainly gives him a clear path to a possible bye in the playoffs.

Next, in a shocking twist, Ghost's debut match in the West against Gabriel Brown...never even started. Brown, surprisingly quit in anger of the dangers and violence expected to work under in the West, walked out and left the arena. Later that night, New Frontier Wrestling fans were treated to his replacement Manpower, whom this reporter actually finds more entertaining than Brown...which wasn't hard.

Following that 'match,' Guy Boudreaux...did not endear himself to fans or insiders, or probably Frank Heimdal. "The Rajun Cajun" apparently mocked Shane Southern, then pulled out every cheap play in the book and managed to win his first match over Heimdal.

That's when Season Pass subscribers and ESEN TV network viewers alike were treated to a three hour live boradcast of Midnight Madness that needed a scoresheet, box score and matrix flow chart to explain completely.

Eddie Mayfield def. Jeff Garvin in a wild, Mid-South infused donneybrook that saw Anne Mystic take a Piledriver from Calvin Carlton through a table! Mayfield recovered from the Memphis Death Certificate, winning with a sunset flip.

Next, the shockers started. Troy Windham and The Entourage ran down to the ring, and put the hurt on Mayfield! Carlton, apparently invoking the spirit of Dr. King (and offending America), launched into a speech announcing the fomation of the New South Enterprises. Just when things looked bleak for Mayfield...

Tom Adler SAVES him, asking Eddie if his allegiances are to himself, or the women by his side.

Next up, in another upset, Norman Quiry, a mysterious, but LARGE man that likes to be called N. Quiry (like inquiry, hah!) not only defeated Marcus Cameron, but 'The Last Angry Man' tapped! Watch out for this newcomer...apparently the humor is only in the name.

The newcomer charge continued next when Scott Riktor def. Rook Black in a dream match classic that you could only ever see on ESEN. Riktor remains undefeated since joining the fed, while Black has lost two in a row...with Tom Adler coming up next.

...if you thought it was crazy so far...then get this.

Joey Melton def. Eli Flair. There's not much to say, but a few things...there was a horse. The lasso of truth. Did we mention a horse? And some jelly commercials. Its also being touted as one of the best matches of the week, so quite a big return for Joey...while Eli Flair fails to gain any ground...after a very frustrating few weeks.

Next in a classic, Mike Randalls def. Alias for the first time in his career. A brutal, straight-up match with no interference, the crowd watched two of the best duke it out. But there was one surprising spectator than most...as Brandon Youngblood showed up with some beers, showering the crowd and getting the attention of his past rivals.

The conference confirmed that night that Youngblood IS back, even after backing out on his previous contract. And checkit, next week...he's facing Mike Randalls. Looks like NFW is jumping at the chance for the former PRIME 5-Star Champion to bring his 5-Star game. Only time will tell if Youngblood stays, or like so many other times in so many places...goes.

Still have that scoresheet? Good, there's one more hour to recap. Enough swerves and shocks to knock anyone's lights out, it all started when Tom Adler def. Troy Windham via DQ and OFFICIALLY became UWA World Champion once more.

Officially is the stance that ESEN and NFW are giving right now, because there are rumours that this next result will greatly affect forthcoming TV scheduling as apparently Anne Mystic, Craig Miles and Quentin Sullivan approved the rules and regulations surrounding the title...and are now putting money into the title's legitamacy.

Adler's won the match, but Mystic plays her ace in the sleeve card. Apparently, having the power to do so, she books an "I Quit" Match on the spot with Adler defending the title against EDDIE MAYFIELD.

Mayfield already out from saving Adler from Troy and the Entourage gets ready, but Adler grabs a mic and QUITS on the spot and leaves the title with Mayfield. After tauntings from Mayfield, Adler comes back with the shocking secret that Adler's never challenged Mayfield because Anne Mystic, begged him not to and his prior relationship and feelings...made him agree and promise.

Is it true at all? Who knows. Either Tom Adler and Anne Mystic are still in bed together, either Adler is still pining, or Adler is playing some serious mind games on a relationship already showing some cracks in the seams.

However, this is the result of Adler's actions: Eddie Mayfield is the UWA World Champion, a championship under Anne Mystic's power possibly...or rather, apparently. Who knows what that means...but I'm guessing we'll find out very soon.

So that was the table setting, the stuffing, the potatoes...but the turkey hadn't come. Not yet. As Craig Miles would have to face a hand-selected opponent signed to the Western Conference...from the hands of his mortal enemy Eddie Mayfield and of course, Anne Mystic.

Miles came out...a visibly angry Mystic with some nasty chants came next...

...and then the music hit...

...and the rest was history.

Kodiak Vic Creed def. Craig Miles in a brutal 29-minute brawl that lasted into the final minute, where Eddie Mayfield and a half-drunk bottle of champagne collided with Craig Miles' head allowing KVC to hit his murderous Press Slam/High Angle DDT -- the Powertrip for the pinfall.

The night closed on that note, Miles out cold, KVC celebraing...

...leaving many questions to be answered soon hopefully.

Don't lose that scorecard.
