Core Wrestling: PRIDE Returns in the Mile High City!

3 January 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

The first official Core Wrestling edition of PRIDE for 2005 will be live this Wednesday, January 5. An explosive card has been signed to kick off the new year from the Mile High City -- Denver, Colorado.

A fatal-four-way battle has been signed by order of Robert Maxwell which will involve four athletes who are rumored to be in the "dog house" with Core Wrestling. Word has it this week could be the last time Fathom, British Bomber, Ned Jennings, or Corey Marshall could be on Core television programming.

Tony Aliso will return to the ring and take on a new Core prospect, Hiroshi Ryusaki. This is a big opportunity for Ryusaki and a win over Aliso in his television debut might give this new face some respect from the locker-room.

Coming off losing in the Tag Team Championship Ladder match two weeks ago, Jake Summers is rested and ready to re-enter the ring. This week, he returns to single's action and takes on a veteran Core athlete by the name of Young Gunz Banks.

Speaking of the Tag Team Championships, newly crowned champions Pat Dwyer and Chris Collins will try for two straight wins as they team up against William Curr and another new Core athlete, Adam Dick. The titles are not scheduled to be on the line, as long as the executives refrains from any last minute decisions.

As reported earlier last week, a big Six-Person Tag Team main event has been signed by Robert Maxwell for the first PRIDE of 2005. It will be three different Core champions -- Classic Champion Veronica Paige, Pride Champion William Gwynn, and Legacy Champion Jason Hughes -- teaming up against Munson, Nick Worrell, and the returning Smiley. Exactly what will go down in this huge match is open to speculation, but be sure that Fred Fusion will be in the corner of Worrell's team as it all unfolds.

Do not forget that Robert Maxwell has promised to deliver exclusive news concerning the next Core Wrestling Pay Per View event. Maxwell has promised to make the announcement of the main event at the Pay Per View, so you can only imagine what it might be.

Of course, to find out what will go down this week on PRIDE, you have to tune in Wednesday at 9 pm EST on the WB!

For more information on Core, visit: http://www.corewrestling.net
