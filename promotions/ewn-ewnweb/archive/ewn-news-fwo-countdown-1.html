fWo Countdown 1

15 January 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

It's been eight years in the making, and in one night the world of the Fans Wrestling Organization will be flipped upside down. Storylines, schemes and plans that date back to the first night the fWo opened their doors in Cyberslam I come to fruition as the stage is set for the end.

The Wrestler of the Year tournament comes to a close as Graphic Violence and Keith Scott Zimmerman wrestle for an honor that comes but once a year... and put on a battle that show why both men made it to the final match. The fWo World Heavyweight Championship goes on the line as High Flyer takes on not only Sonny Silver, but a returning former World Champion in the form of Deacon... who wants his title back. Black Quicksilver, Aimz and Halo wrestle in what may be their final match of their fWo careers... and much more.

But it is the backstage manipulations of John Smith, the former Chameleon that will finally come center stage. From his alliance to Neo, to his relationship with Silver Fox, Jim Dudley and the Walt Disney Company itself... the long-running Smith storyline finally comes to a tipping point as his plans, and how others fit into them, are finally well and fully revealed.

It is a night of tragedy, of consequence, and of endings as the fWo puts on the show that means the end of everything... for the Countdown has finally hit zero.
