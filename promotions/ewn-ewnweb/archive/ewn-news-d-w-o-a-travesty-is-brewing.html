d.W.o - A Travesty is Brewing...

13 March 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

March 17th, 2005 the state of California will again have the breath knocked out of them as the DWO takes their tour onward to San Francisco. This upcoming Thursday night fans of the debaucheries that the DWO airs will be in for a special treat. The night will be kicking off with a singles match between newcomer Chris Bones facing veteran of the federation, Sean Quinn. Chris Bones is fresh off a loss against fellow newcomer, Jonathon Winters and will take the opportunity of being pitted against someone like Quinn to make a name for himself. Sean is coming off a brutal ongoing battle against nemesis, Damian who took the life of his confidant and personal student, Ryo. It is sure to be quite the battle between these two as they duke it out in the DWO ring to see who can overcome who.

The second match of the night will showcase two of the aforementioned men. You’ll have the pleasure of seeing the murderous monster, Damian in the ring against newcomer to the DWO, Jonathon Winters. While Jonathon bested Chris Bones last week, many are saying he won’t be as lucky when he stands toe to toe with the killer known as Damian. It’s going to be a tough call for whatever unlucky referee warrants the honor of officiating this match. Speaking of Damian, rumors have been circling as of late that owner, Callisto Bane has been speaking much of him (And his partner, Samantha) since his inception into one of the six Lord of the Asylum slots. What could this mean as far as alliances go? Well if Callisto still has the attitude about these two unclean beings as she did before.. Not much.

Moving onto the third match set up for your viewing pleasure, you’re going to be seeing the undefeatable Digital Mortality facing rising star Krys Branden. The “Half Pint” Krys Branden will be looking to knock Mr. Mortality off of his pedestal and put the first mar on his record since his arrival in the DWO. A lot of fans are saying he could probably do it if he could keep his wits about him the entire match, he did put out Christian Darke and Luke Justice not too long ago, but die hard Mortality fans are simply laughing at that idea. Digital managed to get himself into a slot at Lord of the Asylum as well, he’s going to be going up against some of the best in the business, and could his streak end there should Krys Branden not be successful with his claims? Fans say this is a possibility, but they’re not giving up on Digital, win or lose.

Speaking of someone as high caliber as Christian Darke.. He’s been on a rampage as of late due to his manager, Lexis Bane, “abandoning” him to flee to North Carolina for six months. He’s been taking people out left and right, and has lost one match since returning to the DWO.. And that loss was to newly rising star, Krys Branden. This week Darke will be facing the newly crowned Blood Feud Champion, The Real Deal in a non-title bout that is sure to be a crowd pleaser. Both men are crowd favorites so the audience will have a tough time deciding who to cheer for. Recently Real Deal has been seen having his own backstage troubles with newcomer, Jonathon Winters, what will come of that is still not clear but thus far we can’t say if it will be good or bad. Christian Darke is another of the six entering Lord of the Asylum this month, he’s a strong runner for the World Dominance title that is up for grabs.

Now is the part of the story where we explain just why fans in San Francisco are going to be in for such a treat. We’ve already told you of the problems between Psymon and Draven, both of them claiming to be “THE” Monster of the DWO. For two weeks now both men have challenged the other without anything coming of it besides The Butcher being given the Last Rites from Draven after his hellacious match against Arsenic Hail for her Anarchism title last week. Well folks, this week the petty name calling has been put aside because both of these men are going to be meeting inside of the ring in a Hells Pit match, Draven’s specialty… Why does that figure? We understand some of you, well a lot of you, probably do not know exactly what a Hells Pit match is all about. Good thing for all of you that we’re prepared to explain everything down to the very last detail. Take a breath,..

-Hell's Pit-

An octagonal pit made of solid steel, with no turnbuckles or ropes. A ramp leads down into the pit, and once both competitors are inside, the pit itself is enclosed by flames, assuring no escape or count outs. On opposite sides of the octagon, are two poles, one with a sledgehammer, the other with a bat. Those are the only weapons available other than the person themselves in the match. There are no ledges to climb... Once you’re in the octagon; you stay there until the match is over.

Personally we think that the ticket sales to see this match alone will be enough to power another show in California before the tour is over and the DWO takes its shows elsewhere. Quite the match it’s going to be, and the prize is not only gaining closure to who is the Monster of the DWO, but also the pleasure of silencing an opponent for good. In addition to all of the action ready to explode on this edition of Thursday Night Travesty you have to keep in mind the escalating situation with Adam Stryker possibly making a return to the federation that made him a star. Not only that, but what is with the entire situation between Arsenic Hail and Callisto Bane? Things are sure heating up in that department. Explanations on that and more on the show, be sure to tune in!
