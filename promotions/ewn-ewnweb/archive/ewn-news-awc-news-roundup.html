AWC: News roundup

27 May 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

Pearl Shows His Hand
26th May 2005

AWC Transatlantic champion Alexander Strider's plans came to a sudden halt on this week's edition of Fresh! after Entertainment Manager David "Pearl" Harber made a ground-breaking announcement concerning the upcoming edition of Fresh!, and more specifically, its main event. Let's just say Strider has a BIG problem on his hands.

In what was in most people's eyes a well-deserved victory, Pact defeated Kris Krimzon. His performance impressed AWC officials immensely, and for the second consecutive week, Pact was crowned Fresh!man. This honour allows Pact to challenge for two championships, but there is word that there is even more in store for the crafty veteran.

Fresh! also featured the debut of such superstars as Brittany O'Kelly and another glance at the feisty cruiserweight, Crimson O'Malec.

In two weeks' time, Fresh! will be live from the Mohegan Sun Arena. What kind of developments will there be? Who will be Fresh!man? Will we hear from the champion concerning his massive match? Keep a close eye on the AWC website for more information.

Blame Fred!
24th May 2005

David "Pearl" Harber, AWC Entertainment Manager, today confirmed that the Fresh! event previously scheduled for next Tuesday, May 31st, had been cancelled. He also revealed that the source of the misdemeanour was none other than janitor FREDROCK~!, who allegedly posed as Harber in a phone call to the Wachovia Arena in Wilkes-Barre, PA, last week. Harber did not confirm whether disciplinary action had been taken.

Pearl also announced that Zack Johnson and Joshua Curtis, who briefly made up the duo The Old School Connection in AWC, had been released from the company. No reason was given.

Protest breaks out in local mall
23rd May 2005

(Assoc. Press) Fayetteville, North Carolina – At the local mall in Fayetteville, North Carolina, the line to see the new "Star Wars" movie involved an hour wait for a ticket. Many people ended up waiting an hour just to find out that the night’s last midnight showing had sold out. However, today, the line to get a signature from AWC star, Crimson O’Malec, who also goes by the nickname “The Poet”, was much longer. Many fans who also waited in line to get tickets to this Tuesday’s Fresh! Event at the Cumberland Crown Coliseum faces queues that were even longer. Down the street at the town square was a Women’s Rights Forum, gaining an even larger crowd of feminists.

Crimson O’Malec, who faces Brittany Kelly, a female AWC wrestler, at the Fresh! event, was showing a promotional video of the match, including footage of other male wrestlers fighting female wrestlers. No footage, however, was shown of O’Malec himself wrestling a woman. O’Malec, who weighs less and is shorter than his opponent Brittany Kelly, made no public comment at the event, but one fan who had gotten his autograph stated, “Crimson told me he was ready to get into the ring and expected a great show. He told me that he was expecting to show the people backstage that he was meant for much more than fighting women despite his tiny size.” O’Malec was unable to authorize this information.

After a seemingly calm autograph session, while Crimson O’Malec and his entourage were cleaning up the area, a group of about thirty-five feminists began protesting against O’Malec’s presence in the mall, claiming that a wrestling match against a woman was a complete debauchery. The feminists caused quite a disturbance, keeping O’Malec and his crew from packing up their equipment.

To calm the crowd, Jessie Marsh, a friend of O’Malec's, grabbed a microphone set up for the event and stated, “You should be happy that female wrestlers have gained the status to wrestle men. This is one of the steps necessary to show that women are just as able as men.”

North Carolina State Police were called to the scene to break up the incident. Atlantic Wrestling Club was unable for comment at the time this article was published, other than to say that Jessica Marsh is not an employee of, nor does she represent, Atlantic Wrestling Club or its enterprises. AWC's Fresh! event will be held at the Cumberland Crown Coliseum tomorrow (Tuesday, May 24th).

Two absences
20th May 2005

In light of last week’s show, Fresh!, wrestler Pierce Lavelle, who suffered a brutal beating at the hands of Alexander Strider, will be undergoing a series of tests and examinations as recommended by his medical staff. A rumour is circulating that Lavelle has suffered a minor concussion and might miss two weeks of wrestling action, but this could be just a vicious rumour.

However, it has come to our knowledge that an internal memo was leaked to an Atlantic City newspaper on Sunday morning. The memo states that this up-and-coming superstar will be unable to make the next show of Fresh!.

For fans this is a disappointment, yet Fresh! this week has sold out with some great matches booked. We note that our very own champion has been demoted to mid-card for his inconceivable actions against Lavelle. This punishment is sure to affect Alex Strider’s mentality going into the show.

On another note, AWC-rajah.net reports that AWC employee “The New Age Star” Christopher Ashton left the arena during Fresh! after a call he received. Ashton seems to be somewhat of a mystery backstage, as he has made no friends nor has he spoken to anyone bar Alex Strider. This seems to have confused many individuals, seeing as Ashton, sometimes known as Chris-Ash, was one of the more sociable members backstage in Neon Wrestling last summer. Ashton has also managed to tick off many of the staff with his nonchalant attitude, most notably David Harber, who has refused to comment on the Chris Ashton situation more than once. However, most sources think that Harber took Ashton off the card last week because it has not sat well with Pearl that Ashton puts his personal life before his professional one, and reportedly rushes out of meetings with the AWC head at the first phone call he gets. As it stands now, Christopher Ashton has not called in sick or to explain why he ran off on Fresh!.

Staff expect Ashton to show up to the Fresh! live show this week even though he hasn’t been booked. That at this point seems highly unlikely, and it appears that Harber may have only two realistic choices: book Chris-Ash, and lure him out of hiding, or fire the youngest Ashton brother. At this point, the latter seems much more likely.
