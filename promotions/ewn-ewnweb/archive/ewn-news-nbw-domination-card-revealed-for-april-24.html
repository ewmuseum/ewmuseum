nbW: "Domination" Card Revealed for April 24

22 April 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

Two days separate no brand Wrestling from presenting "Domination" live on Pay Per View from the Stabler Arena in Bethlehem, Pennsylvania on April 24, 2005. Featured is a massive eight-match card which includes a miniature title tournament with four of nbW's rising talents to debut the nbW Dynasty Championship. Two grudge matches, a Keystone Championship rematch, a triple threat elimination match, and a speculated nbW Heavyweight Championship main event will round out the card.

As the fifth Pay Per View event from nbW since December's relative beginning, events surely seem to become bigger in terms of quality wrestling entertainment with each passing show. If the previous four weeks of Infamy are any indication to the massive excitement and energy that will be on display at Domination this Sunday, wrestling fans and critics are in for quite the treat.

Rejection, Blake Gray, Callie Urban, and Lunatic have all managed to secure places in the Dynasty Championship Tournament at Domination. Starting the action at Domination, Callie Urban will face Lunatic. Immediately following that, Blake Gray and Rejection will face off in a rematch from three weeks ago on Infamy; this time advancement in the tournament will be at stake. The winners of both matches will move on to fight for the Dynasty Championship in the semi-main-event.

All four of those athletes will be subjected to "double-duty" at Domination as Rejection will face Pat Dwyer and Mat Walton in a Triple Threat Elimination Match immediately following his first tournament match. Blake Gray will take on Callie Urban in a grudge match to settle their differences from the past two months after the triple threat match. Lunatic and Rey Campbell will meet following both those as the Keystone Championship is on the line in a Straight-Jacket Match.

In the second grudge match on the evening, Jason Kain will meet against The Don in an "I Quit" Match. Don vows vengeance on Kain for the incident involving his nephew just over a month ago while Kain maintains his innocence. It is irrelevant now as they will collide to settle the score once and for all.

The main event of the April 24 Pay Per View will feature Jack Owyns defending his nbW Heavyweight Championship against Spike Saunders, who became the Number One Contender just a week ago on Infamy. Saunders is on a mission to dethrone Owyns who took the belt off of Uncensored just last month at Relentless. But where does Uncensored fit into all of this? He will most likely be live in the arena for the event and could definitely play a factor in the outcome of the main event.

An absence of lower card athletes is evident as Kerry Millano, Silverstreak, Dilly Dawg, Ryan Billows, Torment, and Wreck are no where to be found on this card. Many of them are still in search of their big break in nbW. Will they find that break at Domination on Sunday, April 24 live from Bethlehem, Pennsylvania?
