Zero To Hero just keeps rolling...

18 April 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

Hyde's famed uberhype machine is running at full capacity this week, as the AWC randomiser~! spits out daily additions to the eight-man Zero To Hero tournament for the Transatlantic title due to take place on May 6th.

Today's entrant was the respected indy worker Alexander Strider; he takes his place at the top of the brackets opposite the 48-year-old veteran Pact. It will be interesting to see the outcome when these two collide in the very first match in the Atlantic Wrestling Club's history, opening the show in two and a half weeks' time.

In the meantime, keep checking the website daily at awc.supremepixels.net. Additions to the brackets are made daily, and you can read a different biography of a Zero To Hero competitor every day!

On a related note, tickets are selling fast for the 10,400-capacity event in Tampa's USF Sun Dome. The event is predicted to be a sell-out, so we would advise you to book your place at the event as soon as possible to avoid disappointment. Tickets for the first four editions of Fresh!, AWC's flagship weekly show, will go on sale from this Saturday, April 23rd, for events every Tuesday from May 10th up until the end of the month.
