Retribution Fallout

29 April 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

With just a few short days until Thrill 181, Cyber Extreme Wrestling is experiencing a bit of post-Retribution fallout. Among the top stories:

For full Retribution results, please visit http://ppv.cewentertainment.com

Employees Leaving:
In a Press Conference on Wednesday, CEW Chief of Operations Mark Knight announced that two men will be leaving the company as a result of the chaos at Retribution. Muzzolini, a former CEW World Champion, has requested time off until further notice. Also, Da*Link*Went recieved career-ending injuries in his match with Nick Alexander and will not be returning to the company.

CEW News has also learned that Lord Skywolf, Dru Jericho, Phoenix Murdoch, and Drake Star may all be leaving the company in short order as well. Jericho and Murdoch were all but run down by new North American champion Scott Slade at the pay-per-view and their status for Thrill 181 is currently unknown. Star has requested to be let go from the company, though he may be requried to perform this Sunday at Thrill. Finally, Skywolf has been in dicussions with CEW officials about his departure from the federation, though a time table has not been set.

New Hires:
The cuts from the CEW roster following Retirbution may well be the best for the company, as a number of new entrants to the CEW ranks will be coming in the next few weeks. Both Hoody and Blaine Carington are expected to debut this Sunday at Thrill. A new addition to the roster is Malik Roland, formerly of All-Star Wrestling and no brand Wrestling. Roland's debut has not yet been set, though unnamed CEW officials have confirmed his contract signing.

Thrill 181:
The 181st episode of CEW Thrill (that we've counted, at least) will be live this Sunday night from the Charlotte Colesium in Charlotte, North Carolina, and airs only on HBO.
