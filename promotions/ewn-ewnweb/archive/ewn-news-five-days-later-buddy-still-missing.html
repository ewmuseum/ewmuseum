Five Days Later, Buddy Still Missing

20 September 2005
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

BEAUMONT (AP) – September 20, 2005 – It has been five days since the last episode of PRIDE aired on the WB. The Main Event during the show featured Buddy Kingfisher defending his Classic Championship against former EPW Intercontinental Champion, Khaos. At the conclusion of the match Kingfisher’s nemesis, Hiroshi Ryusaki, made his presence known by helping Khaos win the Classic Championship. At the end of the match, Ryusaki continued with a brutal attack on Kingfisher that would send him to the hospital after the show to be treated for broken ribs among other things.

Kingfisher was patched up and given a room to recuperate in, but he chose to check himself out of the hospital. He left know word with any of the hospital staff as to where he was going or why he was leaving. The nurse at the reception desk said Kingfisher’s persona when checking out was normal.

“He didn’t seem angry or upset or even sad,” she said, “He seemed almost content.”

Kingfisher’s hospital bills were of course paid by Core Wrestling, so therefore the only information the hospital has on Buddy Kingfisher is the basic information like his name, age, and so on. The hospital staff was reported to be excited to see the cW superstars in their hospital, and was confused why he did not take the time to rest from his injuries.

“One would assume, he’s heading off to get some revenge,” another nurse said.

Core Wrestling officials thought the same but the local authorities checked out the home of Hiroshi Ryusaki and no sign of Kingfisher could be found. Core Wrestling officials also sent contacts to Kingfisher’s home as well as the home of a few family members and friends. No one had seen Buddy Kingfisher since Thursday, but all were beginning to get scared. As reported earlier, cW got in contact with Kingfisher’s close friend, [name withheld]. [name withheld]’s comments as stated earlier were:

"I really wish I could be of more help to you guys. Buddy loves Core Wrestling, but I honestly have no idea where he is or why he chose to go off to where ever he is. I sure do hope that sometime soon, Buddy does show up or at least contacts either cW or myself."

The normal time limit before one can file a Missing Person’s Report is a standard forty-eight hours. That time frame was crossed on Sunday, two days after Kingfisher was treated in the hospital. Core Wrestling along with the family and friends of Buddy Kingfisher have filed the Missing Person’s Report for Kingfisher. His picture and information has already been faxed all over the country and is beginning to spread to other countries as well. The whole nation has begun a search for Buddy Kingfisher.

“I just hope nothing happened to him,” a loyal fan stated.

Information regarding the status of Kingfisher and any leads will be posted as soon as possible on the Core Wrestling web site Newswire so that the fans of Buddy Kingfisher cane be kept up-to-date on the situation.

If anyone has any leads or any information that could help find Buddy Kingfisher, please e-mail [name withheld]
