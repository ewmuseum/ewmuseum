Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is The news column of the EWNWeb.net front page in February 2003, an iteration of the hub earlier than the one the wing’s own files record (August 2004 to August 2006), as the Wayback Machine captured it on 11 February 2003: its dated posts from 5 to 10 February 2003 with their time lines. A full name in a byline reads as a first name and last initial; e-mail addresses and messenger names closing the posts are replaced by the marker; the page’s navigation, its tag line and its menus are not carried.

Earlier today word came out of the Terra Sports Entertainment Headquarters that after months of bidding the wrestling company known as NTICW was finally sold.
