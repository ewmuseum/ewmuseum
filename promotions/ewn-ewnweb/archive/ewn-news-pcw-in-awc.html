PCW in AWC!

20 November 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

A surprise announcement today by David "Pearl" Harber has sent shockwaves through the industry: the famed Primetime Championship Wrestling announce team of Dave Kern, Jeff Marx and Steven Smith will return to television as a trio from their professional exile... and in AWC!

The threesome are probably the most well-known commentators in the business, and this is a real coup for AWC. They will replace the East Atlantic announce team of DOOOOOBLIN DAVE~!, Miguel Rodriguez and Hans Heinemann, all of whom were released yesterday.

Last seen calling the shots at PTC Super Series, it is rumoured that this is where the team met AWC's Entertainment Manager for the first time. Once he realised the trio were available Pearl wasted no time in snapping them up.

"Recently I've been watching plenty of tapes from when the business was at its peak, from 2001-3, trying to grab some ideas which would help make AWC reach those lofty heights today. I've particularly been enjoying the PCW events, and finding Kern, Marx and Smith available for work was almost too good to be true," Harber revealed this morning. "We've got some real 'old-school' appeal in these three, not to mention their undeniable charisma. They work particularly well as a trio so I won't be splitting them up."

In other AWC news today, the injured Mickey Fitz-McCarthy has surrendered his Alliance title belt. Expect an announcement on Fresh! from either Harber or the enduring Alliance champion Paddy O'Shea as to the future of the championship.
