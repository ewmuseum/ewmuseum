RAW Material

20 September 2005
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

An article written to bring forth new ideas and promotions to the minds of this community. Each week I will do a search on the internet and make my best attempt to come across something breath taking.

Link: nfw.fwrestling.com
Name: New Frontier Wrestling

Unlike the inaugural article, this is a fed that many in this community may know of but have never really looked into. Raw Material won’t only cover what has not been seen or heard of but what is in our own backyard but maybe forgot.

While talking to [name withheld] (ProFNDub) on this forum about another fed we both handle in he mentioned his fed, NFW. Being out of the loop for over a year I was eager to see what I was in store for. I had looked at it before but just grazed threw the pages with nothing really to do, out of sheer boredom. This time around, my eyes saw something new and fresh. One of the highlights of my visit was seeing a “main” page that reminded me of a real wrestling promotion, it gave the appeal that I’ve always wanted to see in a fed.

I ask myself, “How can this community say eWrestling is dying when feds like this exist?” NFW is raw material, no pun intended. It baffles me now that I’m actually getting back into the hobby how so many people can have a negative outlook on a rather strong hobby. It’s starting to make me realize that maybe there is no decline, but ignorance passed down from past communities.

[name withheld]’ NFW has a very interesting concept to it. Not based off dice, cards, long arcing storylines, or role-plays. The fed has been based off the same infrastructure as the NFL. The NFW is currently in it’s 2nd season since this concepts inception. During the season the characters compete, then go into a playoff scenario to win NFW’s (in the past CSWA) Ultra Title at their final PPV “Wrestlebowl”.
