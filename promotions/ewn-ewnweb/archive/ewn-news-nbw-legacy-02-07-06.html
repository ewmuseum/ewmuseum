nbW: Legacy 02-07-06

9 February 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

With all the commotion running wild going into the pay per view, hopes were high, and dreams were set to be realized. New comer to the no brand Wrestling, Cameron Cage, ended up the third finalist of the 25 To Life tournament, achieving the title shot remaining, the Dynasty Championship. Cage would have to face the current Dynasty Champion, Torment; and it was clear, the battle would be a big one for Cage's career. Mind Games would ensue leading into Legacy, corrupting the man's train of thought and leaving the fans to wonder if he had what it takes. Many men had faced Torment in the past; few achieved victory and left with their own two feet. Cage's destiny could very well be rewritten at Legacy.

Having won the 25 To Life tournament, William Arthur Reagan latched onto his titleshot at Legacy, but shocked the world when he made his decision clear... He would challenge Callie Urban for the Keystone Championship. Urban had defeated his four horsemen of apocalypse one by one, and it was nerve wrecking to WAR. His goal wasn't about the top prize in the business, but rather the Keystone Championship and wipeing away the soiled prestige of the title by taking it off the waist of nbW's top woman, Callie Urban.

Ali Amore was the second runner up of the tournament, and that granted him a title shot of the Keystone or Dynasty title, in which his choice was the Keystone as well. Having been a lackey for Keegan for sometime, Ali Amore saw his oppurtunity to shine away from the Mercenaries, and gain a little gold in the process. Legacy will be his chance for change.

A triple Threat TLC match for Legacy was announced prior to the December break of nbW; However, with the Championship being vaccated by Callie Urban, who would up and quit nbW, the triple threat remains in turmoil. Xander Napoli is expected to make an announcement regarding the third contender live on the pay per view.

A rematch of sorts from 25 To Life when Andrew Martin 'stole' the championship belt out from under Spike Saunders's waiting grasps. For weeks the two would battle it out in the ring and backstage, neither giving in to the other. It would be announced that the two would face one another once more at nbw's grand stage, Legacy. Before Legacy could role around however, Martin made an open challenge on Full Effect, to anyone in the locker room that wanted a title shot. Spike Saunders returned to the astonishment of the fans, having been gone for quite sometime.

Saunders would then go on to beat Andrew Martin for the pinfal victory until a interfering Hayes turned the match into a two out of three falls matchup. Andrew Martin was already up one victory from his previous win over Haddon that same night, and Saunders was tied with him then. Team work from Martin and his goons, O'Dell and Walton, would result in a quick victory for Martin, to retain his championship belt.

Saunders walked away a battered man, but his night wouldn't end there. The lights in the arena would fade as an echoing laugh could be heard while a broken clock flashed on the nbW tron. Bruises, battered bones, and pain set aside, Spike Saunders heads into Legacy with one goal in mind - To finally become the World Champion.
