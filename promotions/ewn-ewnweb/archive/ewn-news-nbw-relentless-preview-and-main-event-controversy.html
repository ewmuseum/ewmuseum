nbW: Relentless Preview and Main Event Controversy

15 March 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

As much of the world knows, the nbW champion, Uncensored has deemed the entire federation unsuitable for him to defend his championship against. The Maximizer and Jason Kain were out to prove him wrong. At Relentless, Uncensored will STILL face an opponent, even though the true number one contender has been forced to retire due to injuries suffered at this past week's Infamy.

Who will Uncensored face? Not only does the federation want to know... The world wants to know.

Alex Styles set out to see if the federation could impress him. But instead of being impressed, he was way less than so. He found his title match in the least likely of areas. Lunatic has been pestering Rey Campbell about his "misuse" of equipment, and Rey is willing to go the extra length to finally rid himself of the loser once and for all... and if it means taking a table down... and a taking a belt home?

That's just icing on the cake.

What the world needs is people who can throw matches like these two. But what we get is screwed endings and unclear victory. Many factors have gotten in the way of these two resolving their problems. Does it finally come out clear? Or does it go with a victory that is as dirty as the show it's on?

Pat Dwyer came into nbW to be successful. Unfortunately, his first match proved him wrong. Not only did he lose, but he lost badly. He wanted revenge on Gray, and he'll do whatever it takes to exact that revenge. But how? By being relentless.

Reports have said that several superstars will be in attendance at this show. Not only members of the federation: Jack Owyns, Alan Helms, Jason Kain; but also some surprises. Maybe a debut? Maybe an interference? We have no clue at this point. But it is damn sure that no one on the roster is going to sit around and do just watch. Each is going to get as involved as they can in what is sure to be a major no brand Wrestling Pay Per View event.

"Relentless" airs Sunday, March 20, live from The Bryce-Jordan Center at State College in Pennsylvania. With a four-match card lined up in the two-plus hours of confirmed air-time, no brand Wrestling must have something lined up to shock the independent wrestling world and continue to impress wrestling fans around the world.
