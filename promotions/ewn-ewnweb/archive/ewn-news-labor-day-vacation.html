Labor Day Vacation

11 September 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

Sup, bitch? I don't know about you cats, by my weekend was ill, man. I peeped this hot broad at the bar the other night. She was all, "You too old for me." Ain't nuthin wrong wit bein' fifty three, baby. My game was feelin' kinda hurt. No lie, no lie. But then I got mah mack on, threw some sick lines at shorty. After getting her drunk as a muh fug first though, of course. Needless to say, GTD (Got Tha Draws), playa!

Anyways, this hick lookin' mofo next to me sayin' "You should utilize proper language and stop acting like your black." I told that punk sucka to go to hell. There's somethin' I supposed to tell ya'll fools. Oh yea... (Credit to the albino hick weirdo next to me) Explicit Violence was canceled for this week. That funny looking Bruce punk gave us the day off.

He all tryin' to say it's due to the holiday! Screw that, mang. I see the conspiracy foo'. You corporate tools tryin' to deny my God (Seth Anson) of his glory and disciples. I see through your lies like I got X-Ray Vision, suckas. Hey, get of my goddamn mouse, you ghostly lookin' virgin! Don't you dare hit enter or I'll beat the whiteness off yah bitch ass muh fu
