The Road to A-Town: Gold and Glory 2005

13 March 2005
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

Two weeks ago, Core Wrestling Chairman, Matthew Lanier, shocked the world by bringing in the xCw World Championship and giving it to William Gwynn. And then Gwynn tried to make the world think that the xCw World Championship was a more prestigious belt than the Core Wrestling Legacy Championship. Lanier introduced a new kind of corruption into Core Wrestling last week and it almost seemed like he turned his back on his loyal Core Wrestling fans by bringing in another federation’s championship belt…

But that just proves…

We’re on the Road to Gold and Glory.

Conflicts between superstars are heating up as preparations for the biggest event in Core Wrestling history are beginning. A week after Tides of Change, Matthew Lanier came out and promised a Main Event for Gold and Glory 2005 that would surpass any Main Event Core Wrestling has ever scene. And then just a mere two weeks later, Jake Finale re-entered Core Wrestling and showed his intent on gaining the title he was destined to hold before. The former GCWF superstars can very well be in the Main Event at Gold and Glory.

On top of Jake Finale eyeing Munson’s Legacy Championship, the xCw World Champion, William Gwynn has made it very known that he wants to knock Munson off the Legacy pedestal like he knocked Munson off the PRIDE pedestal. William Gwynn didn’t hold the PRIDE Championship, but losing it to Nick Worrell at Tides of Change kick started his second run at a championship held by Munson. Gwynn and Munson’s hatred for one other runs deep in their blood, and neither one of them are going to go down without a fight.

The Pioneers, Tim Shipley and Tony Aliso, feel they should have won the match with their last encounter with the Core Wrestling Tag Team Champions, Shades of Gray, but it just wasn’t meant to be on that night for Shipley and Aliso. Hiroshi Ryusaki and [name withheld] proved why they were a great team together and are planning to continue to do so no matter who they face at Gold and Glory. But you can rest assured that The Pioneers are looking for a rematch and are looking to become Core Wrestling Tag Team Champions.

This week the action continues to heat up as some superstars are still making their last minute impressions for their card placement on Gold and Glory. This is the biggest event for Core Wrestling and everyone wants a chance to be in the spotlight on this very night. For the next two weeks, the superstars are going to be pulling out all the stops. But even though Core Wrestling is going into its biggest PPV yet, the question on everyone’s mind lately is:

What in the world is Lanier doing that involves the xCw World Championship?
