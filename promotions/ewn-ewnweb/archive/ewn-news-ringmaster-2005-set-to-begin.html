Ringmaster 2005 Set to Begin

5 November 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

Every year Core Wrestling's winter is surrounded by the rumors, expectations and mystique of a single event. The Ringmaster Tournament is the pinnacle that a member of Core Wrestling can receive, a single trophy given to them for being simply the best competitor in a field consisting of the promotion's best. Every year the aspects of how you win Ringmaster are changed so that even each Ringmaster has achieved a unique feat that keeps their name on our lips for a full year and often beyond.

2003 - Core Wrestling's inaugural Ringmaster was home to a series of qualifying bouts in the first round, a gauntlet match for both semi-finals and the main event at the Ringmaster PPV was an Iron Man Match lasting 60-minutes between two future Legacy Champions in Jason Hughes and Skill Willie. In the end Jason Hughes walked away as the 2003 Ringmaster and would go on to continue a career that would lead to 3 Legacy Titles, a Tag Team Title, a Hall-of-Fame nomination and recognition as arguably the most dominant force in cW history.

2004 - Core's second edition of Ringmaster was a standard 16-person tournament style event which saw the main event of Ringmaster be a 2 of 3 Falls Match between Smiley (fresh off of a huge upset over Jason Hughes) and the eventual winner, Nick Worrell. Worrell's Ringmaster victory was the high point of a two year career in Core Wrestling and he would continue with a nice reign as PRIDE Champion in early 2005, breaking into the Legacy division before disappearing in the summer of 2005.

2005 - There has been much speculation as to the style of contest that will be awaiting the combatants at this year's Ringmaster and here, exclusive to www.corewrestling.net, we have the finalized brackets for the 3rd annual Ringmaster Tournament which will consist of 15 of Core Wrestling brightest competitors.

---

Round 1 - A series of intra-group bouts featuring four groups randomly selected by cW Chairman Matt Lanier and cW Commissioner Jason Hughes. This round will occur from PRIDE 47 thru PRIDE 49 with a possible tiebreaker for groups 2, 3 and 4 scheduled for PRIDE 50 (Pride-X).

Group 1: Buddy Kingfisher (legacy champion), Peter Vetra, Jackson Clermont and John Combs.
- This four person, single elimination tournament will see the Legacy Title on defense throughout the tournament and the winner after three weeks will not only advance to the semi-finals but will be the Legacy Champion.

Group 2: Hiroshi Ryusaki (pride champion), Artemis and Smiley
- A three-person round robin series will occur between the members of this group, with two participants facing each other each week for three weeks. The winner is the one who gains 2 victories in this event and will not only advance in the tournament but will be the PRIDE Champion as the belt will be defended in any match that the champion is involved in. Should it end with each member getting one victory a tiebreaking, title bout will occur on PRIDE 50 to see who moves on to the semi-finals.

Group 3: John Adams (classic champion), William Curr and Robert Michael Kai
- A three-person round robin series will occur between the members of this group, with two participants facing each other each week for three weeks. The winner is the one who gains 2 victories in this event and will not only advance in the tournament but will be the Classic Champion as the belt will be defended in any match that the champion is involved in. Should it end with each member getting one victory a tiebreaking, title bout will occur on PRIDE 50 to see who moves on to the semi-finals.

Group 4: Munson, William Gwynn and Mark Blackwolf
- A three-person round robin series will occur between the members of this group, with two participants facing each other each week for three weeks. The winner is the one who gains 2 victories in this event; should it end with each member getting one victory a tiebreaking bout will occur on PRIDE 50 to see who moves on to the semi-finals. As this is the sole bracket without a title at stake each match will have a gimmick of some type behind it and will be the main event on PRIDE across each of the three weeks that the round is run.

---

Round 2 - Two triple threat bouts on PRIDE 51 between the four winners of the first round, group matches and the two roster members who garnered byes into the second round [2004 Ringmaster Nick Worrell and Veronica Paige]. Two randomly chosen group winners will be pitted against Worrell, while the other two will be pitted against Paige. No title will be on the line in the semi-final matches and the two winners will advance to the Ringmaster PPV which is scheduled two weeks after PRIDE 51.

---

Round 3 - December 15, 2005 "Ringmaster" Pay-Per-View
The two finalists will be facing off in the main event of this year's Pay-Per-View in a yet to be named match and the winner will walk away as the 2005 Ringmaster for Core Wrestling.

"In 2003 I walked away with this event's prize and with it I held the future of Core Wrestling in my hand. I made 2004 the year of Old School and this year's event is no different. The winner of this tournament will be given not simply a trophy but the key to any success that he or she chooses to embrace in 2006. This event MAKES legends in Core Wrestling and there is a legend in this field just waiting to be born."
- Commissioner Jason Hughes (2003 Ringmaster)
