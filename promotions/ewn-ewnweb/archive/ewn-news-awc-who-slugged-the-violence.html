AWC: Who slugged the Violence?

16 June 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

The mystery of who knocked out "The Violence" Vince Jones with his own baseball bat this past Tuesday on Fresh! remains unsolved as AWC moves inexorably towards Divide And Conquer, the pay-per-view a week today after which the roster will be split into two tours until Coast To Coast, the biggest event of the calendar, on 1st September. Divide And Conquer is still without an announced main event, but that is expected to be rectified this Sunday on Fresh!burst now that Adam Dick is once again secure in his Transatlantic championship reign, having successfully defended against his girlfriend Ellis Nash and both members of his "bogey opponents" The Furious Fists Of God, finally laying to rest the demons of three losses to the Martin brothers, in this week's Fresh! main event. His win was overshadowed by a thrilling end to the show; Dick was attacked and left prone in the ring by a returning Jack "The Bull" Murphy. Could Murphy, a former Transatlantic champion, be laying a claim to the Face-Eater's Crown ahead of DAC, or will Mike Wade finally get the shot fans have been clamouring for since his turn on former co-Unfuckable Dick before Zero 2 Hero?

Also on the show, David "Pearl" Harber announced two big pay-per-view matches featuring the Alliance titles and the newly-introduced Countdown trophy. While every one of AWC's permanent duos has confirmed their entry into the Sudden-Death Invitational Alliance Rumble, there have as yet been no entries by the one-off duos Pearl promised. Since AWC wrestlers have been given the all-clear to enter the Invitational even if they have another match on the night, could we be seeing such mouthwatering pairings as Garbage Bag Johnny and Adam Dick, Darcy Crisis and Aimz, or even Psymon and Zsasz? Anything is possible.

Pearl promised that he would reveal the four competitors in the Four Way Fury match for the Countdown trophy via the website; a memo was sent across just minutes ago and we can now exclusively tell you that the match will be: Captain Suleimon vs Kip Brown vs AgentDash vs Teresa Tomas. The winner will then go on to defend the trophy on every AWC show, East and West Atlantic, until Coast To Coast, when the holder will receive a "very special prize".

We also have confirmation for you of another Divide And Conquer match: Ellis Nash is set to defend her AWC Frontier belt against Anton Assault, who won the opportunity by forcing Aimz to submit in a closely-contested Four Way Fury on Fresh!. This means that Mikey O'Reilly's challenge of a Bare-Knuckle Boxing match is now an open challenge; it remains to be seen whether anyone will step up and accept.

Sunday's Fresh!burst card throws up primarily newcomers; the main event will feature the return of fan favourite Kris Krimzon against an opponent yet to be announced.
