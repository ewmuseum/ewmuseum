AWC: So... where to now?

3 March 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

There was a definite aura of change about Fresh! this past week, the first since Bloodlust. Modifications are most certainly afoot - as AWC prepared to move forward without one of its most familiar faces in Paddy O'Shea, who lost his right to compete in the promotion of his choice at Bloodlust when Mike Wade defeated him, our new champion Jack Murphy was showing that he himself had changed. Wade then sensationally chose to give up the Relentless title belt he has coveted so much, in order to effect more readily his own personal change as he aims higher in AWC, towards the Transatlantic championship that so recently adorned the crown of his duo partner's head. And the stars of the night, of course, weren't the same old, same old we might have expected in 2005: Celestial Fury, Mikey O'Reilly, Darcy Crisis and Teresa Tomas were some of those lesser-known names who all got over in their own way on a night when AWC's fans were begging for someone to go out and make him or herself a hero.

Darcy Crisis had perhaps the best opportunity to do that, in his main event slot with the champion, but despite asserting himself well was distracted at the last by a fleeting appearance from Aimz, with whom Crisis has very significant history, and Jack Murphy was able to recover to hold his credibility on night one as top man. O'Reilly, who was revealed on the night as the mystery client of the himself myserious Zsasz, pulled off a valuable victory over big-name newcomer Dagoth Kinslayer to put himself forward as one of AWC's brightest prospects. Celestial Fury, the Alliance champions, took another step towards being mentioned in the same breath as the greats; their backstage interviews get better by the week and The Green Grappler pulled off a highly creditable win over Heroic Henry. And Teresa Tomas' unlikely befriending of Chainz was the talk of the town long after the show was over.

This Tuesday, AWC travels to New Jersey. Boardwalk Hall is but a jaunt from AWC headquarters in Atlantic City, and the line-up is more than worthy of its close proximity to the higher-ups: of the six matches scheduled, three would all have been potential main events. Jack Murphy makes his first defence of the Transatlantic championship against Frontier champion and Grand Slam hopeful Mike Wade. Adam Dick and Ellis Nash face off in a battle of the mercurials. And six tough-as-nails men battle it out on the unique boardwalk itself, with the first to find the hidden title belt becoming the new Relentless champion. This promises to be an unmissable edition of Fresh!
