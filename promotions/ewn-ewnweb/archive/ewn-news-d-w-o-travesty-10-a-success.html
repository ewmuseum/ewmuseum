d.W.o - Travesty (#10) a Success!

18 March 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

March 17, 2005 - The DWO’s tenth show since returning from its hiatus has aired in San Francisco, California and what a brutal event it was! The show kicked off with the cameras following one: Christian Darke backstage. Routine enough, until Christian took a detour into Digital Mortality’s locker room, tricking Digi’s friend into believing he was security. Needless to say Christian took out Digital’s friend with ease and left him there for Digital to find later on in the night. Oh boy, when Digital did find him it was not a pretty scene. Christian had truly messed this poor man up, leaving him near coma. Digital managed to scare information out of a nearby janitor who had seen the entire thing, and that’s when he found out it was Christian Darke behind the attack.. They’ll be meeting at Lord of the Asylum at the end of the month for the main event match up. We say not a moment too soon!

The nights matches began with newcomer Chris Bones taking on veteran of the DWO Sean Quinn in a singles match up. The match began normal enough but it was soon apparent that Chris Bones meant to win tonight in order to make up for his debut loss last week against Jonathan Winters. Throughout the match Quinn put up one hell of a fight, managed to keep Bones at bay for quite a while however toward the end of the match Bones edged in with his set up move Methods of Immortality. The move was executed perfectly, but the end result was Sean Quinn left on the mat with a broken shoulder. The referee was quick to react to the situation but Chris wasn’t going to let Quinn weasel his way out of the match with a broken bone. After shoving the official aside Chris Bones leveled Sean Quinn with his finisher, Thunder Struck, and pinned him for the final three count thus giving him the win and Quinn only some hospital time.

After this match was over the Megatron came to life to reveal a strange being who simply referred to himself as being called ‘Seven’. He said he had a message to give and proceeding to explain the deadly sin of greed. He even went as far as to give the sin of Greed a face, and it was the face of DWO Owner and founder, Callisto Bane. The crowd didn’t take kindly to this slander and began booing loudly before Seven could even finish his spiel. He basically let it be known that this type of greed could not go unpunished and that soon, probably at LOA, he would begin picking off those associated with each deadly sin he was prepared to reveal. Before the Megatron went dead he made sure to repeat his name, the sin, and the person associated with the particular sin. It was quite annoying to say the very least, and we think the fans would agree to that statement.. It sure seemed that way in the stands!

The evening kept right on rolling with the second match up of the night featuring Jonathon Winters and the most sadistic man in the DWO... Damian. The match didn’t remain a singles bout for long, with Damian it always seems to turn into something bloody; people have simply come to expect it. We suppose Winters was left in the dark about this little fact because he was not expecting what he got in the ring with Damian. The match began with Damian simply smoking a cigarette and waiting for Winters to attack. Winters went for it, and the match was on. Before long Damian let it be known just why he’s nicknamed the “Macabre Murderer”. Between nailing Winters with chairs, barbed wire, tables, and the like he found time to take some of the drawn blood to his sadist cannibal girlfriend and allow her to lick it from his fingers. Sick, yes. Disgusting, yes? What do you expect? It’s the DWO. The match was ended with Damian delivering a crucifix piledriver onto a chair to Winters, otherwise known as the Garroting Chair.

After this match the cameras went backstage to find Psymon and The Butcher having a little confrontation. It seemed that The Butcher had made it clear to Psymon that he planned to interfere with the main event match which would be between Psymon and Draven. Psymon didn’t like this fact too much and had Butcher pinned against the wall, threatening him and basically telling him that he didn’t want, nor did he need Butcher’s help in his match against Draven. The Butcher wasn’t satisfied with that, he wanted revenge on Draven for attacking him last week after he lost his match for the Anarchism title against current champion, Arsenic Hail. Before things got too out of hand between Psymon and Richard “The Butcher” Kayson, common ground was reached and Butcher gave his word he wouldn’t show his ugly mug in the main event. Psymon kept him pinned to that wall for a moment longer but eventually things cooled down and both men separated.

With the ring being cleaned up after the Damian/Winters match over commercial break we headed into the third match which would showcase rising star Krys Branden and his attempts to put a black mark on the spotless loss free record of one: Digital Mortality. The match began quickly with Krys drop kicking Digital from behind as he stood on the turnbuckle showing appreciation to his ecstatic fans. Digital ate concrete on the outside, before battling back against Krys and eventually leading to the both of them back into the ring. The match was filled with busted mouths and noses, a bit of biting into barricades, and enough high flying to make anyone dizzy. Toward the middle of the match it seemed Krys might be losing his beginning match advantage. Digital was tiring of the child’s play and began taking it to Krys Branden harder than before. With a figure four threatening to ruin Branden’s chance of beating Digital, Krys had to rearrange his game plan.

He managed to get out of the leg lock and landed a massive X-Factor to Digital only to scale the turnbuckle and hit his finisher which Digital kicked out of. That didn’t matter to Branden; he kept the speed going and pulled out a bit of trickery on Digital at the very end. He scaled the turnbuckle with Digital ready to grab him as soon as he jumped, but rather than jump to Digital he jumped over him and grabbed hold from behind, performing a sun set flip and pinning combo to take Digital out. There goes that one black mark on an otherwise spotless record! Krys left the ring battered and badly bruised, it was a very intense match up with Digital but he’d pulled off a win which was quickly giving him the nick name: Streak Ender because let us not forget he ended Christian Darke’s winning streak not that long ago. Better luck at Lord of the Asylum Digital.

Backstage after this match the cameras caught up with Callisto Bane in her office waiting for Arsenic Hail to show up with her medical documents. Show showed up alright, not in a good mood either. You see, sometime last week Callisto got Arsenic on the telephone and managed to get under her skin a bit about certain duties her friends were being used for. Without going much deeper into the situation… Arsenic still held a grudge about it this week. She gave Callisto her medical papers alright and a side dish of attitude. Playing her cards the smart way Callisto simply kept a fake smile plastered across her face. This would prove to rub Arsenic wrong better than anything else Callisto could have done. The meeting ended with Arsenic landed a hard punch to Callisto’s mouth, but to the young girls surprise Callisto’s head didn’t even move an inch in any direction.. It just remained where it’d been before the hit. All she had now was a throbbing hand, no satisfaction so she stormed off out the door, slamming it behind her. It was only after Arsenic was gone that Callisto realized her mouth was bleeding..


Speaking of Christian Darke in the recap of the Digital Mortality match… His match up against current Blood Feud Champion, The Real Deal was next on the docket. This match was very interesting as Darke strayed away from his Capoeira style and going for more fighting against The Real Deal who simply stuck to his powerhouse and submission stylings. The fight was evenly matched in the beginning, but without too much trouble the front runner for the World Dominance Title, Darke edged out The Real Deal and put the hurting on him. At one point both men ended up outside, Christian “accidentally” throwing TRD into the barbed wire on the outside and getting himself into trouble with the official of the match, James Kilpatrick. A while after being back inside of the ring Christian put TRD in the Dragon Sleeper hold and managed to knock him out with it. After it was official he’d won he slid out of the ring and took possession of TRD’s Blood Feud title belt. He proceeded to take it straight to the trash can near the commentary table and toss it inside. The crowd was outraged by this act. It wouldn’t stop there.. He flicked out a lighter and actually set the trash can and the title on fire! Oh yeah, bet your bottom dollar Callisto Bane will have a word with him about that little stunt.

After this episode the show went right on with the main event being the next on the line up. This was no normal main event for Travesty; of course as of late what has been a normal main event? This was the long awaited Hells Pit match between Draven and Psymon. The match began with both men entering the pit and from there the chaos began quickly. Draven was the first to make a move, leveling Psymon but Psymon wasn’t planning on being an easy target. He was quick to his feet and the match raged on with these two monsters beating the living daylights out of one another. At one point a sledgehammer was wielded by Draven and used to dig a crater right into the ribs and stomach of Psymon and then of course to check the alignment of his spine when he was choke slammed onto it. Hey! We never said this was going to be a –nice- match. Throughout the middle of the match both men continued to duke it out, Psymon managing to get in some hard hits to The Walking Nightmare but for the most part he was just delaying the inevitable because he really pissed Draven off when he swung that sledgehammer into his skull and tried to pin him. Yeah, Draven handed him his ass for that little stunt.

After the first initial pin Psymon took Draven down again, trying once more for a pin but Draven surprisingly powered out, frustrating Psymon who laid him out again but this time went to one of the posts and grabbed a base ball bat instead of trying for a third pin. Psymon actually snapped the bat over Draven after beating him with it for several minutes, hitting any point he could without Draven blocking the swings. Draven was pinned yet again, but yes, he powered out and showed just why he was the true Monster of the DWO. At some point during the match though, Psymon’s buddy The Butcher showed up in the Pit and took Draven down, both The Butcher and Psymon double teaming Draven with nothing being able to be done about it.. No disqualifications in the Pit my friend. Finally sick of being double teamed Draven grabbed hold of The Butcher and took him out with a massive spine buster after almost tossing Psymon into the flames surrounding the pit. He wasn’t done with Butcher just yet though; he snatched him up and tossed him completely out of the pit, through the fire surrounding and onto the ramp above where the match was taking place.

With Butcher now out of the picture for good Draven made his way to Psymon, jerked him up and leveled him into the steel of the mat with a face down choke slam! Needless to say blood was spraying everywhere. That wasn’t the end of it though; Draven lifted Psymon up yet again and took him out for good with his patented finisher, Last Rites. With Psymon thoroughly down Draven placed a massive boot on the mans chest and pinned him for the three count. Draven was crowned the true Monster of the DWO, a title that had rightfully been his in the first place. With the main event out of the way the show was pretty much over, but before the camera could cut out our good ‘ole friend Seven decided to show up on the Megatron for the second time. This time he was calling out the deadly sin of Sloth, and gave it a face too.. The face of Lexis Bane. That made his two primary targets of the moment Callisto and Lexis Bane. With Callisto already dealing with Chryste and Arsenic, can she handle another person stalking her? And what about Lexis? She’s out of commission for the time being while handling family issues in North Carolina. What does Seven want? What are his plans? Find out next week on the eleventh edition of Travesty!
