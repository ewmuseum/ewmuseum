AWC announces this week's card

28 August 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

AWC has announced the six matches booked for this week's Fresh! show that will broadcast live from Nottingham's National Ice Centre.

The big match is the main event, in which The Educator will defend the last of the three titles he won at The Battle Of Britain in a Three Way Elimination match against former champion Pierce Lavelle and fan favourite Paddy O'Shea. David "Pearl" Harber is reported as considering The Educator's Streets Of London victory somewhat fortuitous, and the match he has booked is sure to test the abilities of the new champion to the maximum. It is, however, somewhat surprising that Hate was not chosen for the match rather than Lavelle or O'Shea, seeing as he was the dominant force in the epic match at The Battle Of Britain eight days ago. Harber, however, has stated that he will only be booking The Fifth Horseman in curtain-jerkers until he sees fit to apologise to Pearl for his associate Azagtoth's brutal attack on him which left the Entertainment Manager unconscious. This week, Hate takes on Void, a man who has never quite got started in AWC since his debut in mid-June.

Another match of interest is the potentially combustible Four Way Fury match between Amy Silveira, Mike Wade, Tim Shipley and The Illustrious Face-Eater. Face-Eater has been at Shipley's throat ever since he entered AWC, and it was a shame that we didn't get to see them go one on one at The Battle Of Britain; instead the two were forced into a Three Way Elimination match which was won by The Educator, so capturing the Frontier title. The belt was then lost to Andy Murray last week on Fresh!; Murray is not booked in Nottingham but is expected to make an appearance.

Wade has also been in the news this week after his actions against Hate in the Birmingham NEC, when His Swerviness appeared to ally himself to rival Irishman Paddy O'Shea. The implications of this for Eire Og and Emerald Isle remain to be seen.

This will also be Silveira's return match after some minor neck surgery following an injury sustained at the hands of The Farmer - who imposed himself on last week's show by coming out to ringside and brawling with Steven Xandrous during the main event. This week, neither has a match, but we can expect tension between the two.

Grady, a new signing who lost very quickly to Jason Locke last week, takes on another newcomer who made a big impact by winning the Best Of British match (despite not being Welsh), Adalia Delorian. Other new faces on the show will be John Kross and Juri Hyobanshi, who will face off against each other, and the duo known as T.T.S. who take on Rainbow Flag in a debut delayed from last week when they were going to take on Bloodlines, but Joshua Curtis and Ami Mizuno, having already been given their marching orders by Pearl, refused to show up for the match.
