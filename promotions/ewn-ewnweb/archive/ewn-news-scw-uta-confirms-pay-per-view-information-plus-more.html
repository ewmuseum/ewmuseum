SCW/UTA confirms pay per view information. Plus more!

13 February 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

The SCW/UTA announced yesterday that their upcoming pay per view, entitled, Resurrection, will in fact be held on March 6th, 2005. A location is still yet to be set, but the semi main event has been signed. Along with this, the following rumors have been buzzing around backstage this week.
- James Matthew Wingate Jr better known to UTA fans as Matt "The Hitman" Fury has been in talks recently to acquire the Fury name from Eddie Peterson so he can use it, instead of his present alias Matt Storm, when he begins active competition in Apex Wrestling. However, it looks as if Eddie is not releasing the rights anytime soon.

- There has been a LOT of talk backstage about the merger already being split, or Greg and Eddie having problems. Folks, this is confirmed to all be just rumors. Do not threat, SCW/UTA will be around for a long time. It's in papers and ink. As for Greg and Eddie fighting, I caught up with Mr. Peterson himself and he says the other then love spats he and Greg are 100% happy with the situation and have no personal problems.

-Fury will most likely be returning before or at the next PPV which has released the date of March 6th and titled Resurrection. This has, however, caused a situation backstage. Slayer will most likely be slated in the world title match. His opponent? Loki Navarro. But if Fury does return. Loki will most likely be sent to feud with him as the ultimate heel meets the hopeful ultimate face. Where does that leave Slayer? Possibly being pitted against The Spectre, which up until now has been out of the question. Management does NOT want to place Slayer vs. The Spectre unless one of them is to turn face. But the problem of both being very deserving arises here. There is a very SMALL chance that the main event will be a fatal four way with The Spectre, Slayer, Loki, and Fury. But that again post the problem of three major heels, the three biggest heels in the SCW/UTA, and one face. But isn't it funny, that even though he is a heel, fans still chant for The Spectre...

- People are being signed left and right folks. Jigsaw, Razor, Troy Adams, Wolfman, Moto psycho. When will they all finally be debuted? Soon hopefully. Razor already has entered into the mix, a and will soon followed by Troy Adams and Jigsaw, hen the rest.

-Ron Hall was at an autograph signing this week in Canada for the UTA camp. Does that mean he DOES have a contract?

- Justin Konrad is reportedly very displeased with his current angle with Greg Manix, but due to contractual obligations can not back out of it.

- Look for Wild Fire to be scheduled for the PPV in a high impact match with a new comer.

- Interested in the past? Well so is Eddie Peterson apparently as he has been in talks with Seth Payne aka formerly known as Klash's brother who goes under the moniker Kor. Talks with former UTA champion AC Smooth HAVE finally began. But it doesn't look likely that AC will be entering anytime soon.

- Eron, the biggest asshole in wrestling, has been reportedly eyeing the SCW/UTA for a run.

- Look for Troy Adams to possibly enter under the UTA loyalist, which currently consist of Seth Payne and Scott Daze. God, three henchmen to do Eddie's bidding. Yuck.

- Look for the Greg Manix vs. Justin Konrad Iron man Last Man Standing PPV match to be changed, due to the match itself being booked already sounds boring as hell.
