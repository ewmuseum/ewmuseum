Durian David Orthane Comments about Joining HATE

7 July 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

After refusing contract offer after contract offer, a chairman from the Hate corporation stated, "When we don't have what we want, we get it. Everything has a price and that includes every man. Lets just say we're willing to give Mr. Orthane an offer he can't refuse."

Days later, Durian David Orthane signed his signature acrossed the dotted line in a six month contract with Hate. The amount is undisclosed but it looks as if Hate closed this agreement.

We caught up with Mr. Orthane for an interview and an interview is what we got..

---

HATE Correspondant: Mr. Orthane, how long have you signed a contract with for Hate?

Durian David Orthane: Six months, not one day more or less. From then on, we'll see how things go.

HATE: Can you disclose how much money you were offered?

DDO: Not in your wildest dreams. My financial issues are personal and I won't get into them with anybody.

HATE: Now that you are officially Hate material, what are your plans for the future.

DDO: Simple. Do what I get paid to do. There are two kinds of people in life. There are people who are employed under a certain company. They meet the minimum and walk away happy. They just want a paycheck. And after that week is over, they forget about it and move onto the next. And then, there are those who are good at what they do.

HATE: And you are saying that you are one of those that are good at what they do?

DDO: [chuckles] .. how about I let my actions speak louder than words.

---
