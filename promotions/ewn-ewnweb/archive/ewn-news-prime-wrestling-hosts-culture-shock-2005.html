PRIME wrestling hosts Culture Shock 2005

22 February 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

Once a year PRIME wrestling holds a wrestling event that easily surpasses the year befores. At Culture Shock 2005 it was no different. We had a night full of bumps bruises and even ring collapsings. Here is a little preview of what the night held.

To start the evening we had the Five Star Title match. The stipulations? You ask. Well they were simple the current champion Nova was able to pick his opponent. Who did he pick you may ask but the real question is did God make this pick? That is right he choose God's Champion Hoyt Williams. This match up was one of the night. So go and see the results for to see if Hoyt can become a two title champion or if he is shut down by Nova.

Next we had the finale of a great fued we have seen jump from federation to federation. HIT and Karina Wolfenden have been fueding for a long time ever since she broke his neck and almost ruined his career. Tonight the fued is ended... or so we thought...

One of the biggest singles matches of the night was up next we had the curent Universal Champion Black Angel square off against Boda, a man who earned his shot at ReVolution 50 and expected a fair shot until the third competitor was thrown into the mix, Killean Sirrajin. Killean who deserved the shot, was looked down upon by Boda who felt he deserved the shot all alone. Will the third person stop Boda from becoming a champion or will it not even matter, or will the reigning champion come out and be able to stop them both? You will have to read to find out.

The final match before the great Duel Halo was a match that was scheduled to take place on ReVolution 55 before Culture Shock but due to some time restraints and such the match did not happen so the bookers decided to throw this match onto this card and it deserved every minute of it. Shane Wethers and Jackson Darilek were looking to see who the better wrestler of the two really are. During their first match Jackson Darilek took out Wethers and you will have to read to see if Darilek can make it 2-0 over Wethers or if Wethers was able to hold of Darilek and make the score even at 1-1

Finally we have the match of the night everone has been waiting for. We have the Duel Halo, a match which was created by a side lined owner while sitting in his hospital bed. This match is a something you could refer to as a big cluster but it was the read of the night. We saw 30 Superstars enter the ring at one time or another, well not all made it to the ring, to try and out last the other 29 superstars and take home the win. Their prize? Well the prize was whatever the winner wanted, last year the winner, Ian English, took over 10% of the company what would happen again this year? You will have to read to find out!
