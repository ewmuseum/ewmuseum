AWC: Zero 2 Hero unveiled!

20 March 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

Immediately after the conclusion of last night's Fresh!burst (a transcript of the show is due later today), Sasha Volkyeva and David Harber, the Entertainment Co-Managers of AWC, issued the long-awaited release detailing the arrangements for the first event of Year Two of AWC: the second instalment of the annual Zero To Hero tournament.

Zero To Hero II, or Zero 2 Hero as it will be officially known, will follow the same format as last year's original in that eight wrestlers will compete in a standard one-night knock-out tournament. The prize on the line, however, is different. When the late Alexander Strider won the original Zero To Hero on May 6th last year, he walked away the first ever AWC Transatlantic champion. This time, the person crowned Hero of 2006 will be rewarded with a shot at that same belt, to be taken four months later at AWC's planned showpiece pay-per-view, Coast To Coast, which is being called "the biggest event of the calendar" by those in the know.

Zero To Hero 2005 was the first ever AWC event, and thus featured eight wrestlers entirely new to the promotion. This time around, AWC is well established as a top organisation globally, and so the arrangements will be slightly different. The tournament will be split into two brackets. Bracket One features current AWC employees yet to really break through the talent-heavy midcard to establish themselves as star names. The four chosen will battle it out in two first round singles matches, with the winners of those facing off in a semi final, whose winner will go through to take on the winner of Bracket Two in the Zero 2 Hero final. Bracket Two is also made up of four wrestlers, but these four are entirely new to AWC and will not be known to AWC television beforehand. This bracket will work in the same way as the first.

Just like last year, the brackets will not be known to anyone - not even the wrestlers themselves - until they are filled, one wrestler per bracket per day, on this very website from April 16th. The AWC server is already in the process of being upgraded in anticipation of the traffic surge on the 16th, when everyone from friends, relatives and fans of hopeful competitors to fans all over the world will be logging on to see which eight privileged men and women will be given the chance to throw their name up with the AWC elite in 2006.

Competition for places is already said to be fierce, despite applications only having opened today. Numerous AWC personalities are on recruiting runs infiltrating all the top wrestling circles to extend the opportunity to become a star to every man or woman who might be interested, but only four of potentially thousands of applicants can make it into the outside entrants' bracket. Internet-based bookmakers are already offering odds on which big names might enter Zero 2 Hero, with the decorated but notorious "fed-whore" Seymour Almasy odds-on favourite.

Hopefuls are reminded that to have a chance of entering this elite and highly exclusive tournament, they should get their applications in as soon as possible, and certainly before the March 29th deadline. Since Pearl is insisting on talking face-to-face to all applicants before making any decisions about who to give the opportunity to skip the queues and become a main eventer in a night, the earlier applications are received, the better, so that the Entertainment Co-Manager can take time out of his busy schedule to meet with each wrestler individually before his (somewhat ill-timed, if you ask us) break to spend time with family in California in early April.

Because of the inordinate amount of time-consuming administration involved in setting up what is touted as the biggest one-night tournament in all wrestling, and to give the Entertainment Co-Managers time to assess prospective entrants to the AWC bracket outside of the melodrama of weekly TV, there will be no standard Fresh! shows between this Friday's Twilight Of The Gods and Zero 2 Hero, which will come live from Manchester, England's Evening News Arena on April 28th. Negotiations with networks have, however, begun over alternative AWC shows that could be put on to feed the addiction of fans worldwide during this month-long quiet spell; documentaries, recap shows and talk shows have been mooted but nothing concrete has come from the front office.

Another exciting announcement that was somewhat overshadowed by the plethora of Zero 2 Hero news was the surprise announcement of an AWC anniversary show on May 6th, one year to the day since the first AWC show. The special Saturday show will come from the tiny Millennium Forum in Derry, Ireland. The venue was decided by a fan vote on the AWC website in March. Only 400 tickets will be available for the show, which will bring together AWC stars past and present for a once-in-a-lifetime nostalgia trip - and only 300 of those will be available via general sale. Keep an eye on the AWC website for instructions on how you can get your hands on one of the hundred other tickets for this unique and special show!
