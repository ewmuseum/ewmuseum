AWC: Soak up the sun {{SPOILERS}}

3 July 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

'Amazing' is not a strong enough superlative for just how mindblowing AWC's second pay-per-view event, Solarized, was.

The event took place at New York's Madison Square Garden on Friday, and despite early doubts, was clearly deserving of the best-respected wrestling arena in the world. While the first half of the show was perhaps uninspiring, it had always been nothing more than a setup for the three huge matches booked for later in the evening - and none of those three disappointed.

Those rooting for favourite Pact to win the Solar Power match had reason to be disappointed, however. After winning the contenders' battle royal at Fresh!burst, Pact was allowed to choose the assignment of planets to the contenders. He opted to give himself Pluto, meaning that he could enter the ring last, on the assumption that champion Crimson O'Malec would be sufficiently worn down by that time for Pact to take victory, despite O'Malec being allowed to use weapons. However, it was plucky newcomer Steven Xandrous, entering seventh out of nine, who managed to fell the sun, becoming the new Relentless champion in the process. Because of this, Pact never even got to enter the ring.

Krimzon Karnage Inc. and Emerald Isle were the two duos to qualify for the Alliance championship league final, after the events of the two Four Way Fury matches earlier in the night. The final took the form of a No-DQ Ladder match, and despite the understandable exhaustion of Crimson O'Malec after his exploits just moments earlier against seven men in a row in the Solar Power match, he and his partner Paddy O'Shea managed to clinch the Alliance titles. Even though the match was two-on-one for large parts, O'Shea managed to hold his own, with Kris Krimzon and The Educator unable to work together well enough - perhaps due to Educator's promise to Krimzon earlier in the evening that after this event, their partnership would be over.

And then it came to the big one - the Inferno match. The blaze was bigger than expected, and even though ten firemen were presence, the flames frequently threatened to get out of control. Fortunately, the fire surrounding the ring didn't do any unexpected damage, though the stifling heat did elicit several complaints after the show from first row ticket-holders. There are also rumours of lawsuits over the risk of respiratory diseases. Commendably, Pierce Lavelle and Transatlantic champion Alexander Strider fought for over a quarter of an hour in intense heat before Strider finally succumbed to the power of the devouring inferno, allowing Lavelle to execute a spectacular variant on his finisher, Whiplash, into the fire itself. "The Mongoose" managed to roll through to the safety of the outside with the impact, but his tights did catch fire on the way, and this was enough for Senior Referee Michael Ryan to call for the bell.

All in all, then, four new champions to take AWC into a new era: Pierce Lavelle, Crimson O'Malec, Paddy O'Shea and Steven Xandrous. But, after the show's cliffhanger climax, the one person on everybody's lips this weekend is not Lavelle, not Xandrous, not even FREDROCK~!... but a former Core Wrestling superstar who was supposed to have retired.
