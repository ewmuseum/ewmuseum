Core Wrestling: Preparing For 'Tides of Change'

23 January 2005
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

The time is growing near for Core Wrestling to bring 'Tides of Change' live on Pay Per View this coming Wednesday, January 26. Airing from the Sullivan Sports Arena in Anchorage, Alaska, this event will feature one rather intriguing line-up of wrestling matches.

For what is being considered his first true opportunity, Munson will challenge Jason Hughes for the Legacy Championship. The only other time these two have met one-on-one was December 15 when Hughes escaped with the win through interference from Core Wrestling Director of Operations Robert Maxwell. It is being called "The Ultimate Test of Endurance" by order of Robert Maxwell and will take place inside of a Lion's Den under 'I Quit' rules. The only way to win is to do whatever it takes inside that Lion's Den to force your opponent to quit.

Another main event atmosphere match involves William Gwynn and Nick Worrell battling out with the PRIDE Championship hanging fifteen feet above the ring from a cable. It will be a Ladder Match to determine the champion as Gwynn's title has theoretically been stripped from him. Much like the main event, these two have only met once before and William Gwynn escaped with the victory inside of a Cage Match on 'Extreme PRIDE' on December 22, 2004.

Two other Core Wrestling Championships will be on the line. Veronica Paige will put the Classic Championship which she won back at Ringmaster 2004 up against Smiley and Young Gunz Banks under 'Triple Threat' rules. The first pin fall or submission grants the winner the Classic Championship with Veronica not even having to be involved in the decision to lose the championship. Former rivals turned partners and Tag Team Champions, Pat Dwyer and Chris Collins will put their belts on the line against Adam Dick and William Curr. Three weeks ago, Chris Collins suffered a handicap match loss to Curr and Dick because Dwyer was unable to wrestle due to an injury from the Tag Team Championship victory the week prior.

Finally, two more matches round out the kick-off to Core Wrestling's Pay Per View calendar year. Tim Shipley and Jake Summers will wrestle to decide who will be partners with Summers' current tag team partner, Tony Aliso. Things have become personal between Shipley and Summers since Summers debuted in Core under two months ago. Four new faces to Core will be involved in an opening tag team bout as Bane and Austin Reeves take on [name withheld] and Hiroshi Ryusaki. The Director of Operations went on record to say this match would be a "test to show if these young athletes really want to be here."

Rumors are running rampant and circulating around the internet concerning 'Tides of Change.' People are speculating that Jason Hughes may be retiring after his title defense at the event Wednesday. Some people are questioning the integrity of the Tag Team Champions, citing they will crack under the pressure against two young upstarts.

Only one thing holds true no matter what is said before 'Tides of Change' this Wednesday night. The only way to know what actually happens is to order and watch this historic event for yourself.

For more information concerning 'Tides of Change' and Core Wrestling visit: http://www.corewrestling.net
