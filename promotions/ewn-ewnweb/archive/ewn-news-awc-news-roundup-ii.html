AWC: News roundup II

9 June 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

Dwarf wins stuff = RATINGS!
9th June 2005

Fresh! hit its highest TV ratings yet this week, as Tuesday's show from Uncasville, Connecticut was absolutely packed with action. From Pearl's 'surprise' announcement of the Alliance titles, along with their championship league, right through to the four-way (make that three-way) Weapon of Choice match in which Crimson O'Malec beat off the ferocious challenges of Pact and Kris Krimzon to become the first AWC Relentless champion, it was all good harmless fun. Well... harmless? O'Malec might disagree, having required a blood transfusion after being stretchered out of the arena. Bruce Lomond might disagree; his testicles have swollen to four times their usual size following Ellis Nash's repeated assault. Ethan Phoenix might disagree - do you know how much that jacket cost?

Hate squandered his chance at a Solarized Transatlantic title shot, faltering as the 'team' of Pierce Lavelle and Alex Strider managed to work together against him for at least part of the handicap match. Next week, he is relegated to a makeweight in what is a 'test' match for Christopher Ashton, who didn't show up to this week's main event: Pearl has booked the two in a weapons match, with the stipulation that if Ashton does not show up, his AWC contract will be terminated. It's a lose-lose situation for the former Neon Wrestling Syndicate member, who will either be torn limb from limb by the even-more-angered Hate, or be fired from the Club.

Elsewhere on next week's bumper card, we see the start of the Alliance championship league. There are still some surprises to come there, as on inspection of the completed sign-up sheet after Fresh!, David Harber realised that Ethan Phoenix had failed to enter his partner's name. This was surely just an oversight on the part of the new signing, but AWC is milking it for all its worth, as next week's bill features Phoenix teaming with "a mystery partner" against the interesting duo of Kris Krimzon and The Educator.

An impressive showing this week has earned Ellis Nash a surprise main event billing. She will face the raw Pierce Lavelle in a match whose result could have a big impact on her position in AWC.

If next week's Fresh!, which broadcasts live from Buffalo, New York on June 14th, can live up to the awesome show put on on Tuesday night, we are in for something special.

AWC star injured in gang attack
8th June 2005

After last night's Fresh! taping AWC Superstar Pact was attacked and seriously injured in his own home after an apparently motiveless assault that has the Yonkers' Sheriff's Department baffled. "Yeah, we think the attack happened around 2AM - according to eyewitnesses the victim was set upon on his front porch by a gang of four or five men in black," said Deputy Eddie Scott, "Anyone with any information should come forward, and any information given will be kept in strictest confidence."

Pact is currently in a critical but stable condition at St. John's Riverside Hospital. His longtime friend, Eiji Ohayo, and his agent, Alexi Kalashnikov, are keeping a vigil at his bedside and have promised to provide us with regular updates on his condition.

ANOTHER protest?!
5th June 2005

This time Kris Krimzon is the one facing the picket lines, and a possible lawsuit. A few nights ago, a woman claims that she saw Kris Krimzon chase her pet dog, which she confirms to be a poodle, away and that she saw Kris Krimzon throw a large trashcan at her dog, hitting it on top of the head. The Krimzon residence has been swarming with protests due to the AWC star’s actions. AWC Entertainment Manager David Harber is said to be "dismayed" at the news.

It has also been rumoured that hot prospect Samuel Livingston will soon be making his return to AWC TV, after a bitter contractual dispute led to his departure. Harber is said to have patched up relations with the IwE champion, though there is no offiical confirmation of this story.
