XRW announces rules for main event match at Reborn!

27 April 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

Well, it is no secret that Xtreme Rampage Wrestling has announced that at Reborn they will be having a prison break match for the XRW Heavyweight Champion. THe on thing everyone was asking was..."What the hell is a prison break match". XRW Owner William T. has released the official rules for the match yesterday. Here it is:

Prison Break Match Rules-

any size team .vs. any size team

Hardcore elimination style team brawl match. One participant from each team starts the match. Then the other participants are isolated inside of two jail cells on the sides of the cage they are surrounded by. Note: This cage has a roof.

The two participants begin to fight and after two minutes if there is no winner then the cells open and it is an all out brawl. After five minutes, the men go back to their cells. This happens every five minutes after that. If at any time the representative for either team pins the other teams representative then the team of the representative tha was pinned is released for a full minute. They are then free to attack but not pin the victor. After this two minute interval, the victor may choose a member of his team to release and the match then becomes handicap. The match continues in the same manner until there are three match participants left (even one team). These members then fight in an all out elimination match against each other. The winner of the match is the last man left!

The participants for the match have been announced. They are: Jet Harnson, The Great Muso, Johnny Speede, Y2RR, Daemon Natas, and El Brujo.

Do not despair though. There are still great matches left to participate in at reborn, including the Lethal Championship Match and the Tag Team Championship. THere will also be a match where the winner will recieve a CWF contract. So sign up fast before all of the jobs are filled! There are staff positions available too!
