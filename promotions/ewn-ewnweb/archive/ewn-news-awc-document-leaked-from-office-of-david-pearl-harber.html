AWC: Document leaked from office of David "Pearl" Harber

29 October 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

A mysterious document has made its way onto Atlantic Wrestling Club fansite InsideAWC.com.

Purportedly leaked from the front office, it lists the names of the majority of AWC's current active roster arranged into six teams of three for two qualifying matches. Notes on the document indicate that the two teams that win the matches will thereby qualify for spots in the main event at AWC's upcoming pay-per-view, Triangles, about which little is known.

The document is reproduced in its entirety below:

QUALIFYING MATCHES, AWC TRIANGLES

ALREADY QUALIFIED - ALCAEUS (red), PIERCE LAVELLE (blue)

MATCH ONE - THREE WAY TRIO TAG
WINNING TEAM QUALIFIES
Jack Murphy (red), The British Bomber (blue) and ??? (green)
vs
Johnny Noble (red), ??? (blue) and Butterfly Hamada (green)
vs
??? (red), Norman Quiry (blue) and Hate (green)

MATCH TWO - THREE WAY TRIO TAG
WINNING TEAM QUALIFIES
Patrick Mapleleaf (red), Adam Dick (blue) and Tim Shipley (green)
vs
Harry (red), John (blue) and Tony Aliso (green)
vs
Red Rock (red), Paddy O'Shea (blue) and Crimson O'Malec (green)

The sharp-eyed amongst you will have noticed that at the conclusion of the qualifiers - dates for which are not set; speculation is that they could take place at the event itself - there will be three entrants with a red tag, three with a blue tag but only two with green. Is this an oversight by AWC's Entertainment Manager David Harber, or an indication that the entire document is false after all?

It is unclear what the colours mean, and also whether the "???" spots are intended for hyped "mystery opponents" or are simply "TBA". Everything, however, seems to be working in threes - three-way matches between three teams for entry into a match in which competitors will fight it out for three prizes - as revealed on this week's Fresh!, the prizes are (1) the Transatlantic title, (2) contracts for a shot at each AWC championship, and (3) a mystery prize.

If this document is genuine, it indicates that simply the right to compete in the Triangles match itself will be a hard-earned honour. Triangles, with no officially announced matches despite being due to take place two weeks today, is possibly the most exciting AWC pay-per-view event to date.
