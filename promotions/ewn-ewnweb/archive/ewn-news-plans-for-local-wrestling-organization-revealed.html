Los Angeles Times Newspaper

Plans For Local Wrestling Organization Revealed
After purchasing a former popular skating rink, Adam Davis has gone public with his plans for the building.

"After looking at my options, I've decided to go with a wrestling company," Davis says. "The building is perfect in size to fit about one-to-two thousand people, a nice ring, and a walkway." Davis says that he's planned to name the organization 'Los Angeles Wrestling Club.'

Davis also told LA Times who he's hired as the president of the company. Veteran wrestling promoter Eric Hall has signed a one-year contract with LAWC and is excited about the company's opportunities.

"We have our own building, we can set everything up instead of having to move from building to building with a boring atmosphere, we want it to look as though we're a global organization," Hall tells us.

Hall and Davis have told us that the LAWC will hold a weekly event which they've named "Crossfire". Crossfire will take place every Monday at six o'clock in the evening. Hall and Davis are going to try to get a television contract as soon as possible.

"Things for the LAWC," Davis says, "are only going up."
