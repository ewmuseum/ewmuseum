The End of the Road To Retribution?

24 April 2005

Have you ever seen those signs while you're driving? You know, the ones that say "Road Closed, 1000 ft ahead." Sometimes you freak out, because you don't know what you're going to do when the road closes. Sometimes you're cool with it, because you're either supposed to be going to the end of the road, or you're turning off somewhere near there. Sometimes ... you're not sure if you're going to find where you're going ... or go flying off the cliff. The above predicament is exactly what Cyber Extreme Wrestling faces this weekend. The road to Retribution is coming to an end. And exactly what will face the CEW after the show is a complete unknown. CEW Champion Da*Link*Went, the longest reigning champion in the seven year history of the federation, will face "Showtime" Nick Alexander, the man who held the reign record until a few short weeks ago. Longtime CEW superstars Muzzolini and AC will step into the ring in a match unsanctioned by CEW officials. The best of five series between Descendent and Jakob Mayhem will conclude with a Fucked Up Ill Shit match inside a steel cage ... never before seen in CEW! The CEW Television, North American, and Tag Team belts will all be on the line as well, at the biggest CEW show of the year! The road to Retribution ends tonight! Live, and only on PPV. www.cewentertainment.com
