COUNTDOWN TO DYNASTYS DESTINY BEGINS

9 March 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

With less than a week till we find out just who will win Destiny 2006, the anticipation is growing as Dynasty Wrestling heads towards the MCI Center in Washington D.C for one of the most popular PPV’s of the year. If the Destiny match itself wasn’t enough, sparks are bound to fly as the Immortal Championship is decided for the first time in DW history as two certifiable Dynasty legends battle it out inside the career ending triple cage structure in the form of ‘The Enigma’ Chris Cage and ‘Superstar’ Shawn Storm, both former two Time World Champions. The World Championship is also decided as new Champion Jet defends for the first time against KoRe to put an end to the long running rivalry once and for all! A member of the SOA and the X-iles face off in the form of International Champion Killjoy and Famine of the Vile, and a new Cruiserweight Champion is crowned as Sabrina Wilson, Ice and Wildcard meet in triple threat action. With the Dynasty Wrestling roster all gunning for a place in the Destiny match, just who will make it into the match its self, with Jon Page promising a few surprises in the match despite having to enter the ring second himself, with long term rival and current DW Tag Team Champion Steve Jason entering the ring first! Just what will Amy Page have in store after the bombshell she dropped at the last PPV and what other surprises will Destiny throw up? Tune in on Sunday night, only on PPV to find out!
