nbW: Full Effect 11-13-05

17 November 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

Just a mere week following 25 To Life's conclusion, and sparks continue to fly. Full Effect started off with a amazing debut match for Zatch Rollins, when he took on Alyx Norwood. The match wasn't meant to finish as a familiar face within the ranks of no brand Wrestling, made his return.

Delacroix took one one of the four horsemen, in attempt to settle his problems with that group. Keegan and cthe Mercenaries revealed their plans for Uncensored and made it clear that he would need a couple friends in order to come out in one piece, when the five of them face him and his 'friends' in a few weeks.

The new World Champion made his presence known, and his actions as well. And Callie Urban wrestled the four horsemen with her partner, daughter of Xander Napoli, Krista. A few phone call's, debut's, and returns of omnious superstars. All this and more on this week's edition of Full Effect.
