Core Wrestling's "Tides of Change" Stuns Wrestling Fans!

27 January 2005
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

The tide has come and it swept one man on the Core Wrestling roster away. The rumors of Jason Hughes' retirement have been put the rest. On the 'Final Countdown: Preview to Change' Jason Hughes spoke openly about his pending retirement, win or lose against Munson. Hughes made it clear he wanted to leave and retire as the Core Wrestling Legacy Champion. The question is: Would Munson let him?

It was a long and grueling near one-hour main event between Jason Hughes and Munson inside the Lion's Den for the first ever "Ultimate Test of Endurance" in Core Wrestling history. Both men put their bodies on the line and bled a little, sweat a lot, and suffered even more. In the end, only one man could walk away with the championship, though he could barely walk at all. To find out who was able to hold the Legacy Championship over their head and soak in the glory, visit the online transcript to 'Tides of Change' which is now available.

Though it was the biggest event on the night, 'Tides of Change' featured so much more. Packed with a Pre-Show and exclusive match previews, the event was by far the biggest and best offered by Core over the past two years. Five other amazing matches were packed onto the card and all of them lived up to their expectations and more.

Kicking things off was tag team action as [name withheld] and Hiroshi Ryusaki took on Bane and Austin Reeves. These four young upstarts on the Core Wrestling roster had the opportunity to make a name for themselves and open the Pay Per View festivities. Which team would succeed? The Classic Championship was on the line in a Triple Threat match as Veronica Paige put her belt up against both Smiley and Young Gunz Banks. Smiley has been very angry lately and determined to undo the injustice of his loss to Nick Worrell at ‘Ringmaster 2004’ in November. Will Smiley be able to defeat Paige and Banks, or will someone else prevail?

Tim Shipley and Jake Summers squared off one-on-one after a long two months of disputing over who would be partners with Tony Aliso. Things would be settled, but with a real twist. Director of Operations Robert Maxwell signed Tony Aliso to be the special referee for the match. Would Aliso call it down the middle or would he have a preference to which man walked away with the win?

The Tag Team Championships would be defended by Pat Dwyer and Chris Collins as they are challenged by Adam Dick and William Curr. The last time any of these men met, William Curr pinned Chris Collins. The time before that, Dick and Curr defeated Collins in a two on one handicap match. Would the Tag Team Champions finally right the wrong put upon them?

In the Semi-main event, Nick Worrell gets his PRIDE Championship rematch against William Gwynn in a Ladder Match with the belt hanging fifteen feet in the air. The last time these two men met, William Gwynn defeated Nick Worrell in a Cage Match. Can Gwynn go “two-up” on the young man who won Ringmaster 2004? Or, can Nick Worrell defeat the PRIDE Champion who ended Munson’s 165 day title run?

All this and more happens on the FIRST Core Wrestling Pay Per View of 2005! ‘Tides of Change’ has been broadcast live from the Sullivan Sports Arena in Anchorage, Alaska.

For full results from 'Tides of Change' visit: http://cgi.corewrestling.net
