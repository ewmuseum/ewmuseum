AWC: Pact to hang up his boots

31 July 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

Persistent rumours were confirmed today as the Atlantic Wrestling Club issued a release stating that the veteran hardcore wrestler Pact is to retire after the upcoming The Battle Of Britain pay-per-view event.

Back problems have dogged the ageing grappler throughout his time in AWC, and on more than one occasion he has wrestled despite medical reports recommending to the contrary. An examination this week, however, revealed that spinal damage has moved into the 'major' category, and AWC's Entertainment Manager David Harber, acting in the interests of Pact's long-term health, is refusing to let Pact wrestle on weekly television.

"I've allowed Pact to wrestle in the past, even though the doctors were telling me it was wrong, but enough's enough. I've been told that one bad bump could paralyse or kill him, and I can't in good faith allow him to wrestle," the man known as Pearl told this website exclusively. "He's been pleading his case - he wants to fight week in, week out - but I can't let him. I'm afraid he doesn't know what's good for him," he went on.

Pact will, however, participate in one final match on 19th August as part of The Battle Of Britain. His opponent(s) and the match stipulations have not been released.

Harber followed this release with the news that there has, in light of Pact's condition, been a change to this week's Fresh! card. Pact had been due to participate in the main event, a Three Way Fury match against Tim Shipley and Crimson O'Malec for the Frontier title that is currently being unlawfully held by The Educator. His replacement will be the impressive new starlet Amy Silveira, whose reward for becoming Fresh!man eleven days ago has been bumped up from a Livewire title shot to a spot in this match. Unfortunately, unless Kris Krimzon comes up with a way to outwit The Educator - unlikely - before the final match in Raimundo Saporta on Tuesday, the contest may not even take place.

In other AWC news, it has emerged that Juri Hyobanshi, who took part in Primetime Central's CW2 tournament earlier this year, has signed a contract with the promotion.
