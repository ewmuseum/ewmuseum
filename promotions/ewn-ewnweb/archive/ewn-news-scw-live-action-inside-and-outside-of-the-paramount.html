SCW :: LIVE! Action Inside and Outside of the Paramount

20 August 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

Cincinnati (Event News) ~ Superior Championship Wrestling produces action inside and outside of the Paramount Arena this week on LIVE! The pre-PPV event, SCW's second ever, was quite the buildup.

As promised the number one contenders were decided in a set of semi-final matches and two men have advanced to the SCW Global Championship match at The Main Event pay-per-view on Sunday, August 28! We were supposed to see a match for the SCW Television Championship, but due to "outside interference", a double title, triple-threat elimination match has been booked! The first fall will decide the SCW Television and second fall will decide the SCW United States Champion!

Johnny Vivacious, who was barred from the building, had his own ring setup outside and even competed. The heat between his PPV opponent, Quinn Masters, has been turned to HIGH as the two inevitably locked up by the events close in a brutal and bloody battle. Their match on Sunday is non-sanctioned by SCW, there are no holds barred and falls will count anywhere!

We also saw the onscreen debut of MiSfIT and BUD as they destroyed the backstage area. They have been booked in a "Dueling Debut" match this Sunday. With the likelihood the Lucianos, Stirellis and SCW General Manager Stace Matthews making their own impact on the show, The Main Event is shaping up to be a crowning achievement for all involved!

It has been confirmed that SCW sponsors have picked up SCW superstars for advertising and promotion of their products. Johnny Vivacious will be walking advert for tshirthell.com. Ice has signed a deal with Pepsi to be their spokesperson in the Midwest. Vivacious and Ice will appear in numerous commercials that will appear on SCW broadcasts.
