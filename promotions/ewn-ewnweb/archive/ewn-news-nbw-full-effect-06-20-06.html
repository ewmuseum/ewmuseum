nbW: Full Effect 06-20-06

20 June 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

Starting things off on the right foot following Ardent, nbW took to FX for Full Effect. Immediately upon airing, the dark match from nbW's Supercard Ardent, was shown to those live in attendance and watching around the world.

Leonard Antioch had a shocking announcement for the nbW locker room, presenting a new reason for the wrestlers to take to the ring and team up. 3Peat macWICKED faced off against the Jedi of Funk J-Funk in a wicked hardcore match.

The Four Horsemen had a few issues of their own during Full Effect, when El Asasino's past seemed to be haunting him. While a duo of 'different' personalities took their step into the nbW arena, after posing as another tag team; Heaven and Hell.

Following Ardent, it was clear that the colossus of nbW, Spike Saunders, was in a upset mood, and during Full Effect he took it up a notch by calling out Logan, aka the Lone Wolf. Things didn't quite work out the way he had planned, and after a match which was more of a beating and then a shocking save, the look on his face said it all; It wasn't over.

nbW CEO Xander Napoli had another meeting with his 'ally' in the Hayes situation, and finally the fans found out who was the mystery individual at Full Effect prior to Ardent. And in the Main event, after a 'misfire' by one individual, WAR faced off against the newest tag team of nbW, Heaven and Hell in a handicap match. The result came with some outside interference, and further staked WAR's claim to gold in the coming weeks.

Just prior to the closure of nbW's edition of Full Effect, a secret meeting in the parking garage, where a handoff between three people occurred. Whatever the situation was, changed when two Wolves interrupted the proceedings and obtained whatever was being traded. All this and more, when nbW presents Full Effect 18, live only on FX. Also be sure to check out the nbW Star Front, where the Superstars are evaluated on their preformance's.

Source: nbW Full Effect - June 20th, 2006
