SCW::Open Invitation to WrestleFest

12 September 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

Cincinnati (Event News) ~ Superior Championship Wrestling, in association with Paramount Parks, presents WrestleFest on Sunday, September 25 from the sold-out US Bank Arena in Cincinnati, Ohio.

The pay-per-view event is just the culmination of a week’s long tribute and thanks to wrestling fans. Throughout the week, on the grounds of SCW’s Paramount Arena fans are welcomed to celebrate the sport’s rich history, eat and enjoy their time reminiscing and mingling with SCW superstars!

On Thursday, September 22, Paul Brown Stadium opens to the fans for a four-day festival and carnival where several SCW and independent superstars will make an appearance (FEPO). Fans are invited to dunk their favorite (or least favorite) superstar, get autographs and meet the Miss WrestleFest contestants. The Paul Brown Stadium will shut down an hour before bell time on Sunday to allow ticket holders a chance to get to the US Bank Arena for the pay-per-view event like none other!

Independent wrestlers are invited to participate in an open invitational battle royal where the winner will get a shot at the Global Championship! The match will feature fifteen SCW superstars and fifteen independent wrestlers from around the globe. The match is pin, submit or over the top rope elimination and another competitor will enter every two minutes! From what I’ve been told sign-ups are open until 12:00AM (EST) Friday, September 16, but will lock as soon as all of the spots are filled. SIGN UP HERE!

Any independent agent who signs up is free and clear of any further obligations to SCW after the WrestleFest event. We wish them nothing but the best in all of their future endeavors. SCW talent reps with contracts may approach some, but it is the choice of the independent agent on whether they sign or not. If an independent agent wins the battle royal they will automatically be offered a contract which they can accept or decline. If the contract is accepted, they will get a shot at the Global Championship on Thursday, September 30! If the contract is declined, they are free and clear of any obligation after WrestleFest.

With so much to gain, being a part of this event will bring huge exposure to all involved! I will keep you updated on other matches as they are signed and the further development of SCW’s WrestleFest 2005!
