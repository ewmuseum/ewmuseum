[Lights Out Wrestling] New Strides

5 March 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

After coming off of a week of many twists and turns, where not only newcomers were pushed, but the wings of LOW spread out a bit, causing for some new strides to be taken...

This past week on Realm, The Killer Pirates of the Outer Caribbean came in and beat the Flying Vergomovs for a shot at the tag team titles at the next PPV. After thus, CJ Tyler, a new guy whose' not so shy to the camera, especially with his surfer tan, defeated Tony Morrison for the #1 Contender ship of the Realm Title, which is held by The Demond!

Speaking of the Demond, it was one for the ages as The Demond and Donny Diamond duke it out hardcore style in the Pittsburgh Dump for the Realm Title. However, to the fans dislike, and surely to the dislike of the wrestlers themselves, they ended the match in a draw, causing the Demond to retain his title...

Last but not least, John Adams faced off against Dark Haze, in what was drastically the only match in LOW History to make two non-World Title Contenders go at it, with no title on the line, in the Main Event of an LOW Realm! Was it disappointing? HELL NO! Haze and Adams gave it their all, but in the end, it was the Haze's willingness to do anything to win that gave him the edge.

Aside from the matches a few things happened backstage. Richard Rush (LOW's current owner) had been called by someone who appears to be apart of the LOW company, or is going to be soon. The details are unsure, but the fact remains that something is going down, and the story is only but beginning.

S.I.N. has marked it's territory in LOW as it's leader John Adams sent S.I.N. to attack Dark Haze, but to no avail, the Haze of LOW kept on agoing, and defeated the odds, even when attacked from behind.

Then there was one... extreme title belt that is! After the vicious fight between Adams and Haze, the former Extreme Title was ignited and then scorched black as John Adams lit it on fire. He may not have won, but he left his name all over the title for all to see.

.... That and more happened on Realm 71, but for the full happenings, go read it yourself! Click here to visit www.lowonline.net. Now onto next week, the show in which will be the heater for the Pay-Per-View, and will definitely lead some of LOW's newer talent into gold-deserving opportunities. Make sure not to miss it.
