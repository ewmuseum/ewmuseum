[CEW] Retribution

25 April 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

The road to Retribution has ended for Cyber Extreme Wrestling. The journey to the biggest pay-per-view event of the year is complete.

Da*Link*Went is the current CEW World Champion. After many previous attempts, he finally won gold last May at Retribution V. He is now the record holder for the longest title reign in company history. Link has risen above his height, his youth, and his critics to leave one Retribution champion, and return the next year as Champion again.

"Showtime" Nick Alexander is the CEW's most infamous champion. A four-time World Champion, Alexander has been held down for months by CEW Operational Manager Mark Knight, with whom Alexander has had a bitter rivalry throughout his career. Alexander beat Knight last month at Unleashed to earn this title shot, and then went through the first CEW champion in history, Sledge, to prove his worthiness.

Aaron Crabill, otherwise known as AC, has been the franchise of CEW since its inception. CEW fans have watched as a botched AC Bomb paralyzed AC's mentor Bloodbath. They have seen AC ask the love of his life, Taylor, to marry him, only to have the relationship soiled by a drunken Taylor's transgression with Alexander. They have sat by as their hero was consumed by a greed for the World Championship, turning his back on everyone close to him. Most recently, they have watched as Muzzolini manipulated Taylor into divorcing AC and going after half of everything he owns.

Muzzolini is larger than life. Cold-hearted, calculating ... a maniac on the very edge of control. But always in control. Muzzolini had his title shot at Unleashed interfered with by AC, in return for his interference of an AC title shot. Muzzolini responded by convincing Taylor to divorce AC, then found himself in jail after AC talked to some members of the FBI. Muzzolini and AC were last seen fighting backstage, with Muzzolini having hold of a Glock.

These are the players ... their Retribution ends tonight ...
