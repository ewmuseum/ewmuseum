d.W.o - Travesty #11 AIRS!

27 March 2005

March 24th – Travesty: A night of surprises!

The last Travesty for this month has went off of the air with many fans jaws on the floor. Yes folks it was that action packed, but it was also chalk full of surprises. The night began with a little backstage visage of Digital Mortality making his way into a locker room that soon became known to belong to none other than Christian Darke. Over the past week or so these two haven’t been on the best of terms, especially after Darke was responsible for putting the beating on one of Digital’s close and personal friends. Though the man is pulling through quite nicely Digital had nothing but revenge on his mind, we weren’t allowed to see what happened when Christian went into his locker room and thus far no word has been given on the situation.

After that small backstage glimpse we went straight to the ring for the first round excitement which boasted to be good action. It lived up to the boast. From the moment Chris Bones and Zane Ozz stepped into the ring the fight was on. Zane was trying to be a gentleman about the whole match by extending his hand which Chris Bones took but as it seems he had different plans. Zane was taken down by Chris, not a very sportsman like thing to do, but hey this is the DWO. The match went back and forth but for the most part Chris Bones was asserting his dominance against Zane. By the time Zane got the upper hand it was already too late. Chris finished Zane off with his finisher, Thunder Struck to snatch the win.

Moving right along the fans were greeted with the backstage presence of highly respected and very loveable reporter Rain Riley. She was trying her hardest to get star Jonathon Winters to agree to an interview before his match with former Degenerate Champion, Nick Crowe. After chasing him down the back hallways she was answered with a simple “No” and then a door being slammed in her face. Without hesitating Rain let herself right into Winters locker room and began badgering him once more. It was easy to see that Jonathon was amused by this woman but he wasn’t giving her an interview, it was plain as that. The two argued about it for a good three minutes before Rain threw her hands up in defeat and stormed out of the locker room to leave Winters to prepare for his match that was coming up.

Good thing Rain didn’t follow him to the ring because Jonathon had his hands full with Nick Crowe from the beginning. With Crowe getting on his injured kidneys, Winters was coughing up blood before the middle of the match. However, by the mid point Winters was dominating Crowe with move after move and plenty of counters to make the former champion plenty frustrated. Crowe took back the advantage toward the end by ramming Winters into the ring posts a good number of times to injure his back and kidneys even more so then they already were. Needless to say Crowe was out to permanently hurt Winters and not just to claim another victory, but hey he’s a hit man that’s what they do for a living! Well in Crowe’s case a side living. Onward toward the immediate end of the match Crowe got creative and decided to grab a chair. Too bad for him because Winters saw it coming and planted the chair back into his face with the Indifference Maker (super kick) and pinned Nick Crowe for the three.

After the match we’re sure Jonathon headed to his hotel room for some recuperation time. With the cameras backstage they caught rising star and fan deemed streak ender, Krys Branden heading into the arena. Funny enough Rain wasn’t around because we’re sure he would have given her an interview. He seemed a little down, not really in the good spirits but then again he’s been down as of late. We didn’t stay with Branden long as he soon retired to his locker room. Probably for a bit of last minute preparations, he was facing Kuff McSlade after all and that was coming up rather soon. It was for the Degenerate title, something Kuff had managed to hold onto since the Insurrection pay-per-view event. Streak Ender had quite the challenge on his hands, would he live up to the title given to him by the fans?

The match started out badly for Krys with Kuff beating on him from the beginning. As soon as Krys slid into the ring Kuff grabbed him up and threw him into the ropes then took him down and his head almost off with a massive clothesline. It didn’t stop there, Kuff was making sure to keep the match face paced so Krys wouldn’t be able to get up top and take the advantage. After a shoulder block, a DDT and several other hard hits from Kuff, Krys managed to take Kuff down a notch with a severe drop kick straight to the bigger mans mouth. Both men hit the mat but it would be Krys to take the lead with a senton off the top rope that kept Kuff down for a brief moment. It was only a -brief- moment too because Kuff was quickly up and beating down Krys yet again, it was clear Kuff did not want to lose his Degenerate title to someone like Krys Branden. Krys powered out of Kuff’s hold on him yet again and took it to Kuff with several moves one of which being a somersault top rope drop kick that literally leveled Kuff. With the pace set Krys nailed Kuff with his move Wretched Euphoria into The Crux and then landed his finisher Paroxysm to finish the match. Ladies and gentlemen we have a new Degenerate champion.

After the match the cameras caught up with Krys Branden and so did reporter Rain Riley. He was wearing his newly acquired title over his shoulder and grinning like a pig in shit. Rain was quick to congratulate the new champion on a job well done but was even quicker to get into questioning. Krys admitted he was proud to have defeated Kuff but Kuff was a hard opponent and a well deserving champion. Did he mean it, or was it just the general spew of bullshit? We don’t know. Rain was happy about getting her interview though, we do know that. At the end of the interview Rain was even propositioned by the young champ who said she could stop by his locker room any time should she have more questions or want to get into a bit of deep conversation. Uh huh, conversation our asses we know what Branden was thinking! After that remark he headed off leaving Ms. Riley to swoon all alone with the camera guy.

After that high octane match the main event would really have to be a spectacle to beat out the Degenerate title bout. It was going to be Arsenic Hail, Inphino Blitz, and current Blood Feud Champion, The Real Deal all in the ring at the same time. To make matters far more dangerous this was going to be a three way hardcore battle to determine who would go into the sixth and final slot at Lord of the Asylum. Are we out of our minds!? Well that remains to be determined but the several shrinks we’ve spoken too seem to have a unanimous decision about that one. The match began with Real Deal and Blitz going at it, Blitz delivering a couple of those deadly kicks while Deal forced him back with a few haymakers. Blitz ended up being taken down out of the blue with a kick to his temple from Arsenic who was then charged by Real Deal. A spear landed her in the corner but she got out of it by kneeing Deal in the face and in turn he was taken down from behind by Blitz who grabbed hold and took off into a bulldog.

Blitz however was again taken down by Arsenic who drop kicked him in the face. She was on Blitz in an instance, grabbing him up and throwing him into the ropes. She was side swiped by an awaiting Real Deal who then grabbed the rebounding Blitz and tried for an arm drag but was taken back by a kick to the midsection and then tossed onto the mat like a rag doll by Blitz and his Japanese arm drag! The match was growing more and more intense by the second; any of these three competitors could walk out the winner and be heading into LOA. Soon the hardcore stipulation was brought into the lime light as Real Deal is slammed head first into a chair that had been brought into the ring earlier by Inphino Blitz. Arsenic was responsible for that little accident after countering a suplex Deal had intended and dropped him with a DDT instead. The match raged on as soon more weapons were thrown into the ring, tables to be exact. Real Deal was looking to throw Arsenic down into one of the set up tables but she ended that idea by kneeing him right in the groin, yeah that took him down quickly.

She was going after Blitz next but he managed to get his Hamada submission hold on her, quite a devastating a painful move to have to try and endure. Lucky for Arsenic Real Deal slammed a boot into Inphino to break the hold. However he wasn’t on her side because soon he was driving a chair down into her shoulder! Real Deal turned to toss the chair but when he did he was caught by Inphino and driven through the table with a powerful reverse tiger suplex! Real Deal was out of it for the moment and Inphino spins around to go after Arsenic but when he did he was leveled with a chair and body press to the head that took him down. Arsenic covered but Real Deal broke it up easily. He managed to nail her several times in the face and then simply catapulted her out of the ring and damn near half way up the ramp with a gorilla press! Real Deal would have no more satisfaction for a time though because Inphino was now laying into him with kick after kick to his face.

Inphino finally relented and allowed Real Deal to fall to the mat where a cover was made but Deal kicked out at the last possible second. Inphino stood but had no time to worry about Deal at the moment; Arsenic was getting up on the outside. With some quick thinking Inphino climbed the turnbuckle and flew backwards out of the ring smacking against Arsenic back to chest, the both of them flying to the spiked ramp painfully, neither showing signs of consciousness. In the ring Deal was the first to stand and he slid out only to roll back in with a ladder and several dangerous weapons which included glass tubes, barbed wire, tacks and everything else that could be used to cause pain. Deal rolled out and grabbed up Arsenic, rolling her into the ring with ease. He went for the pin but was pulled out by Blitz and heaved into a nearby guard rail. Inphino had the advantage over Deal but Arsenic flew from the ladder in the ring right into him, the both of them landing hard on the concrete.

Arsenic was quick to stand and began beating the living hell out of Blitz, but soon turned her attention to Real Deal whom she throws into the ring and locks a submission in on. However, Blitz rolled in and broke the hold only to put Arsenic in nearly the same hold! Deal breaks the hold eventually and all three competitors are back in the ring fighting it out. Deal soon took the spot light as he demolished both Arsenic and Blitz, having an aluminum base ball bat never hurt anyone, did it? He made his way to Arsenic, about to bash her head in but grabbing the first thing she could she rammed her hands forward right into Deal’s face, shoving whatever she had into his flesh. Deal screamed. When we say screamed, we mean screamed like someone was killing him. Blood was pouring down his face like nothing we’d ever seen before and the fans as well as Arsenic were just stunned to say the least. It wasn’t until Deal fell over lifeless on the mat that we were able to see what happened. There, sticking out of his eye was a rather large piece of glass. He was down for good and we think everyone knew it from that point.

Inphino was making his way up, unaware of what had just happened and due to Arsenic ramming into him there was probably no way he would hear about it until after the match. Arsenic wanted to win and suddenly Real Deal was forgotten, simply left to bleed out on the mat. Arsenic locked Blitz into her devastating move, Scream for Me and he was left helpless on the mat in this submission hold of doom. He wasn’t going to give up though, or at least he was fighting to not give up. At this point the camera cut backstage to show Callisto Bane making her way down a flight of stairs. The camera came back to the ring showing the raving fans as Inphino sweated the submission out. Suddenly the crowd’s attention shifted as referee Eddie Manneddi was leveled across the back of the head with a steel chair! He was out cold, no doubt about it and Arsenic was ripped off of Inphino and soon staring in the eyes of Callisto Bane. Callisto grabbed her at the temples and then leapt up through her own extended arms and nailed Arsenic right under the chin with her boots. Arsenic flew back and was knocked out cold.. Inphino now being drug over and on top of her.

Callisto had her chair and was out of the ring after that, sneaking off down the side of the ramp and out of sight just as the referee began to wake. His eyes widened at the pin and he began to count, the crowd booing and crying out loudly. The three count was made and EMT’s flooded the scene to take care of all three competitors. The fans were NOT happy about this situation and actually began forcing their way down to the barricades, trying to bust them open. Security had to come out and keep them back, and even that wasn’t enough, MORE security had to be called out. Commentators Mike Cerrone and Jay Stevens stayed on the scene, explaining the show was over and reminding fans to be sure to watch for Lord of the Asylum March 31st. It was quite the show, amazing and maddening all at the same time. With Arsenic being cheated out of the chance to compete in Lord of the Asylum what will she do for revenge? So many questions, you’ll just have to tune into Lord of the Asylum: Night of 1,000 Scars to get your answers. You dirty little druggies, you know we have your fix.. Don’t miss out!
