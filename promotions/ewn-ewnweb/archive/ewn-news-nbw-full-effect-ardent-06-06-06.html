nbW: Full Effect Ardent 06-06-06

8 June 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

The highly anticipated supercard event that was ARDENT left fans and wrestlers alike speechless when the dust finally settled. In a night where records were broken and debuts were made, carnage reigned supreme as nbW pulled out the stops to once again, wow the fans.

The cocky Scotsman macWICKED squared off in a hardcore match against Zatch Rollins after a heated confrontation in the halls backstage, and even after the results, it seems the heat between these two is far from over.

NbW’s first Heavyweight Champion, Uncensored, squared off against the Horsemen and after an upset, gave a scathing shoot to the fans before making a shocking announcement. Lunatic battled and appears to be getting some action, though the female in question might not be as willing as Looney thinks.

In what was to be the match for the first holder of the Dynasty/Keystone Merged title, chaos reigned. Neither contender ever made it to the ring after the brutal attack of Torment. What this means for the belt is anyone’s guess. And who was the shadowy figure with Torment? What will Napoli do for retribution?

In the first mystery opponent matchup of the night, Napoli announced Andrew Martin’s opponent would be none other than Spike Saunders. With the bad blood brewing between the former friends, the match was brutal. A surprise showing from LoneWolf however had ramifications way after the match was over.

And in the Main Event of the show, WAR got a surprise when Napoli locked the Horsemen in their own locker room to block interference. More of a surprise was WAR’s opponent, the mysterious Nemo. The two men went all out and the match basically turned into mayhem. With surprise figures playing a part, the outcome of this matched shocked everyone at nbW.

But once the dust has settled, how will the fans and wrestlers react? What will happen to Cage and Helms? What age is Torment ushering in? And just who the f*ck is Nemo?
