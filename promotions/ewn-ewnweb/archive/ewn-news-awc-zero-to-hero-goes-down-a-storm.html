AWC: Zero To Hero goes down a storm!

7 May 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

Last night, Atlantic Wrestling Club presented its first ever show, Zero To Hero, live on pay-per-view across the world. It was a night of high-octane thrills, spellbinding technical action and nail-biting tension, but after seven awe-inspiring matches, only one man could come out on top. Would it be the hardcore veteran, Pact? The pre-occupied Alexander Strider? The college expellee Pierce Lavelle? The experienced and fun-loving Sektor? The goofball Kris Krimzon? The man trying to escape from Tony Stecchino's shadow, Christopher Ashton? The frankly terrifying Hate? Or the hotly-tipped Danny Polar? These questions were answered last night, as a paying audience of 10,000 and the many more watching at home on television lived every second of the action with the wrestlers on show.

There is no question that one of the biggest winners of the night was Entertainment Manager David "Pearl" Harber. Harber immediately established a strong rapport with the fans, and when he came to the ring just prior to the main event to book two matches for the first edition of Fresh!, which will take place in Kissimmee's Silver Spurs Arena this Tuesday, he received the biggest pop of the night. This certainly bodes well for AWC's continuing appeal to those bored by the stale, repetitive booking of other long-established wrestling promotions.
