Dynasty Wrestling's Evolution 2005 Main Event?

15 February 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

With main event match confirmed as of yet for DW’s next PPV ‘Evolution 2005’, fans are still left wondering who will step up to take a shot at Psyko Stevo’s crown as DW World Champion. Many fans feel that Jet will take his place in the main event after making his feelings known that he wants a shot at the biggest prize in Dynasty Wrestling, whilst claiming that he deserves it after surviving the ‘Organised Chaos 2’ match at Original Sinners 2005. However after Darkhan managed to score a rare victory over the champion albeit by count out, many claim that the International Champion will be the one with the shot at the ‘big one.’ However with T-Money, Shawn Storm & others all staking a claim to the throne, DW Destruction #104 should be an interesting show as DW Owner Jon P. and new manager Gillesp work out who will headline Evolution against the World Champion.
