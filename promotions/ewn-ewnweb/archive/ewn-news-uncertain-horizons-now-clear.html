Uncertain Horizons now clear...

1 December 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

As the end of 2005 rapidly approaches, the superstars of True Talent Wrestling are faced with a new year ahead of them, but what is in store is completely uncertain. On November 27th, 2005 TTW brings the masses Uncertain Horizons LIVE from the MCI Center in Washington D.C. ONLY on Pay Per View.

Last year the event saw the first ever Cylinder of Chains Match go down for the TTW Tag Team Titles, when Stellar Insanity held the belts... and their opponents were the tandem of Paul Jarvis and Justin Moreno. "The Ladies Man" Tim Jones was being cheered as he fought Damien Black in a gasoline can match... Stephen Rawlings, Devastation, and Jonny Johnson went two falls for two titles, while John Thomas and Vincent Mallows fought in separate matches, not even involved with one another. Two faces no longer a part of TTW saw an end to their bitter rivalry, and the event was headlined by then TTW World Champion, Christopher Davis, as he fought against then TTW Monster, Chaos.

A lot has changed since then, but one simple fact remains: a new year lies ahead and where the TTW superstars go from here is up in the air. The answer to that question lies on the horizon, however uncertain said horizon may be.

We managed to catch up with one TTW superstar after the event to get a comment.

"It was kind of a surreal night for a lot of us" Said TTW superstar Justin Moreno. "Things worked out, and a lot of that "uncertainty" heading into this event... like heading into ANY event really, fixed itself or whatever, but I don't know."

Action and suspence awaits all fans eager to see what happened and for those with the cash TTW24-7 is showing the entire event 100% FREE for the entire month of December.
