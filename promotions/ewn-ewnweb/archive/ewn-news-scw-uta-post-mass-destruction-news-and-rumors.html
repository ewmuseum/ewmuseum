SCW/UTA Post Mass Destruction News And Rumors

16 February 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

The brain is a terrible thing to waste, that's why straight from the online site of the SCW/UTA I decided to bring to you the post Mass Destruction rumors and news to keep your mind growing.

- The SCW/UTA is currently very unhappy with Jigsaw's character. His performance on the last Mass Destruction was less then perfect. Even Jigsaw was unhappy afterwards. Look for him to leave after next week's show when he faces World Title contender, The Spectre. Jigsaw is considering a character/gimmick change before the PPV.

- Long time friend of Jay "Jigsaw" Harper, Jermaine "Razor" Thompson is reportedly happy with his current status in the SCW/UTA even if he is 0-3. When I caught up with him after the show he told me that he's just paying his dues, and knows his future in the SCW/UTA are bright. Razor is also under consideration for a gimmick adjustment.

- The Spectre, reportedly, was considering leaving the SCW/UTA to finally retire before talking to the creative team about one more big push. As Mass Destruction showed, we will be seeing a LOT more of the sadistic Spectre in the next few weeks. Will Resurrection be the last we see of him? Or is there one more title run in the eyes of a monster?

- Loki Nevarro and Slayer both where seen backstage after mass Destruction talking about a re-match. Fans really enjoyed their match which ended in a time draw. IF Fury does not return by the time for Resurrection, Look for Loki and Slayer to headline the PPV for the world title.

- Ron Hall HAS signed a deal with Eddie Peterson! You hear it first here folks. Although details ARE sketchy right now, he is still unable to participate in the ring due to his recent surgeries, but may take an on air role for Eddie Peterson after Resurrection. Peterson hopes to step out of the spotlight and manage the UTA side as well as he can after the PPV.

- Management is VERY disappointed in David Blade after Mass Destruction. Although not everyone caught it, a few did. After a few mishaps by Blade Seth Payne got the word to shoot on David and end the match. The original ending was planned for Blade to come out victorious, but that changed quickly. David Blade is now on a suspension that may lead to his contract being voided.

- Greg Manix's plane was delayed and he wasn't able to make it to Mass Destruction, placing a lot of stress on Eddie Peterson and the UTA camp. But Eddie's creative heads quickly got to work on changing the show around to flow evenly. Word has it Justin Konrad was agitated that his television time was cut because of Manix no showing.

- MotoPsycho and Superstar Shane Sullivan debut next week in singles competition against Seth Payne and Scott Daze. This match was set to see how well the four can work together, so that a tag team feud may be worked into the storylines leading up to Resurrection.

- Crimson Lord to return? Scott Vicious IS in contractual talks with SCW/UTA heads. As for him actually returning to the ring, we must wait and see.

- Although still not signed, look for WildFire to have a strange gimmick match taking place in an odd location at the PPV.

- Troy Adams debuts officially next week, but his on screen performance at Mass Destruction was accepted VERY well. Although not apart of Eddie Peterson's 'Clique' Troy did fit in very well on screen with Peterson. A future angle MAY be worked off of that.
