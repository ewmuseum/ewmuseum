AWC: Solarized has come into our solar system

29 June 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

With special thanks to Pierce Lavelle

AWC wrestlers and fans are hyping up for the next pay-per-view, Solarized, due to broadcast this Friday from Madison Square Garden in New York. The AWC staff and fans have seen some amazing wrestlers join the company in the last two months. Bitter rivalries, new championship belts and great matches are all coming together at Solarized. One fan had this to say:

“It is going to be the biggest pay-per view so far in the AWC. I can’t wait! I have been queuing outside Madison Square Garden ticket office for two days now, waiting to buy front row tickets to the madness."

David “Pearl” Harber has made some spectacular announcements on the last few Fresh! shows in regards to the event. Crimson O’Malec will be defending his Relentless title in a match against nine other men, representing what Harber referred to as the nine planets of the solar system, tying in with the event's gimmick. O'Malec himself will represent the Sun, and will be allowed to use weapons in the match. He will take on each of the "planets" in turn, in the order of distance from the Sun with Mercury first, until he loses. If he wins against all nine opponents, O'Malec retains his title. This announcement was made on the most recent Fresh!.

In the main event Alexander Strider will be facing Pierce Lavelle in an Inferno match. It is unclear as yet quite what rules AWC will be employing for their version of this rarely-performed match type. Some fans think that there will be a circle of fire surrounding the ring. Others believe that the ring itself will be set ablaze and both men will have to compete under these circumstances. One thing is certain, however: the goal of the match is for the opponent to be set on fire. This feud is one of AWC's most bitter rivalries; will we see Strider retain and continue his winning streak (he is still undefeated in AWC) or will we see the bright, young Pierce Lavelle come out on top of this match? Either way this match is set to be a “blaze”. (Oh, that's funny - Ed.)

David Harber has himself come "under fire" (Right, you're fired - Ed.) for making this match in the first place - it has been argued that such a dangerous match should be reserved for only the most heated of feuds, and many regard the entire Strider/Lavelle angle as more of a childish squabble. Pearl himself refused to comment on this issue; a source close to him, choosing to remain anonymous, said that the entire AWC staff is worried about Harber. "The man is under intense pressure, and the rule-breaking of Chris Ashton and Alex Strider has hardly helped." Our source even suggested that Harber may be thinking about stepping down from his role as Entertainment Manager.

Newcomer Ellis Nash will be taking on Eddie Sanders in the evening's curtain-jerker. Ellis Nash has proven she is one tough woman, beating Pierce Lavelle in her second week in the AWC. She proved her money's worth in completing FREDROCK~!'s janitor rules and regulations, despite Strider’s efforts to stop her, so rising to the challenge set by Pearl after she became the Fresh!man two editions of Fresh! back. Perhaps we will see another victory for this New Yorker.

We will also be seeing the conclusion of the Alliance championship league. Two Four Way Fury matches will be taking place in one night. First is Crimson O’Malec, in his first of three possible matches at Solarized; he’ll be against Kris Krimzon, HIV Joey and Daniel Kiki Von Tannerinos III. The other match is between Paddy O’Shea, Lamar, Joey Dave Gladstone and The Educator. These two matches should prove to be quite entertaining, to say the least.

Then we have the final for the Alliance championship; the winners and runners up of the table-format league will compete for the Alliance gold, two new belts added to the shelf recently by Harber.

David “Pearl” Harber has come up with the most spectacular pay-per-view in sports entertainment for the month of July. Solarized will be taking place at the prestigious Madison Square Garden arena on the 1st July. Viewing charges vary dependent on country; the large-audience countries have their individual prices for the event listed on the Solarized mini-site.
