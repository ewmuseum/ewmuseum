AWC: King Dick and Queen Ellis-abeth

28 March 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

Twilight Of The Gods on Friday night was a fitting end to AWC's first year in business. There was viscera, there was excitement, there was blood and there was controversy. Adam Dick will grab the headlines, winning back his Transatlantic championship in a match characterised by the over-involvement of both commentators, Truth Waters, who took over the referee's role, and George Cassidy, whose timely intervention may have cost his former idol Jack Murphy dear. Ellis Nash produced a fine performance to quickly see off a jaded-looking Mike Wade and capture her first AWC title belt, the Frontier championship, while Red Rock and "The Violence" Vince Jones truly rocked the schoolhouse in an epic Relentless title match which Red Rock managed to win.

Anton Assault decimated Hate, who was a shadow of his former self in the Shoot Cage match, after the previous match within the same steel structure had seen Garbage Bag Johnny pull off a massive upset over Butterfly Hamada and Aimz. Darcy Crisis bested Captain Suleimon in an excellently unique Trivial Punishment match after AgentDash had begun proceedings with a convincing win over Mr. Marshall. But the real story that is keeping the internet buzzing even four days after the show is that of Chainz.

The behemoth fought out a tough battle with Mikey O'Reilly, winning the sick Sadist match to end his recent losing streak. But that wasn't enough to placate the unending desires of Mike Sloan, who wanted more than anything a reconciliation with his long-term girlfriend Tracy. After a bloody altercation with AWC Legend Pierce Lavelle, Chainz didn't get that, and seeing no other way forward, dived through a hotel window, crashing to the concrete below as the show was abruptly pulled from the air.

Was it a dangerous stunt or a suicide attempt? Nobody seems to know. But more worryingly, nobody seems to know anything AT ALL about the condition of Michael Sloan. Fans, journalists and wrestlers alike were herded away from the scene on Friday night as Chainz was lifted into an ambulance... and we've heard nothing since. Not a word. This isn't due to alarming ineptitude on the part of hospital staff... they've told us they're not allowed to say a word. But who, pray, is giving them their orders?

In the meantime, we pray for Michael Sloan, knowing nothing of the condition of the man-monster who took a horrible fall.
