AWC: Winter Warfare and winter break

31 December 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

"Even Winter Warfare can end in happiness"

AWC's Winter Warfare over-ran its scheduled three hours due to two unscheduled events - a match between Adam Dick and former Core Wrestling Legacy champion Buddy Kingfisher, who is new to AWC; and a horrible kidnapping sequence that thankfully ended the eventual arrest of former AWC wrestler Tony Aliso - but thanks to Pierce Lavelle and Paddy O'Shea, still managed to bring all 18,000 fans packed into the Kolnarena and the many more watching at home together in a unifying and emotional climax as Lavelle pinned the rampant Irishman to retain his Transatlantic championship despite bullet wounds to one shoulder meaning he could barely use it for much of the match. At the conclusion, Lavelle and O'Shea collapsed into each other's arms, equally exhausted after a testing and impressive match, which could have gone either way and ended when O'Shea, attempting an impromptu acrobatic move, was caught in mid-air by the reigning champion and slammed into the turnbuckle with the Whiplash finishing move. Fans commented that it was probably the most powerful execution of the Whiplash that they had ever seen, and O'Shea has been flown to London for emergency treatment on his upper back in a specialist hospital.

In the same hospital is Tim Shipley, who is undergoing surgery on his own back after his confrontation of Aliso at the Sonnenberg winery led to being slammed against a sharp helix of steel used for blending wines. Although at the time, Shipley was able to continue a brawl with the man once known as The Breaker, surgeons have told us that his vertebrae were partially severed in two places and the plucky young Englishman is lucky the damage wasn't far more serious. Shipley, who successfully defended his Frontier title against Red Rock earlier in the night, announced from his hospital bed that he would not be returning to active duty in AWC or any promotion in the foreseeable future, not just due to his injury, which could have kept him out for two months regardless, but because of a creeping disillusionment with everything surrounding the squared circle. One suspects that with Tony Aliso now behind bars the likeable college student may go back on this somewhere down the line, but he insisted his sole desire now was to finish his Mathematics degree at Harbrook University in Albany, New York.

Jack Murphy and Butterfly Hamada contested an exhausting Two Straight Falls match, which had fans of technical wrestling glued to their television screens. The two put on a strong showing and it was The Bull who eventually came away with the victory to prove to Hamada that she doesn't belong in the ring and end their little feud.

AWC has received numerous complaints from parents following the Cup Of Blood match which preceded that encounter. Chainz and Relentless champion Mike Wade went at it in a title match under Chainz' own stipulation; the controversial wrestler was allowed to choose whatever type of match he wanted after he came out on top in the Pyramid Scramble at Triangles in November. The aim of the match was to fill a one-quart cup with the opponent's blood, and after a technical difficulty with the chain holding the two men together breaking apart, it was Mike Wade who managed to squeeze enough of the red liquid from Chainz into his cup to retain the Relentless championship belt. Both were rushed to a private hospital and received immediate blood transfusions, raising questions about the sensibility of such a booking when stored blood is, as always, scarce. Medical campaigners have issued their disgust at a "waste" of blood that "could have been used to save lives". Chainz also toed the line earlier in the card when he leapt into the shower with his obsession Alexa Kendericks, and against her will things almost became X-rated before Tracy intervened to save her long-term boyfriend from a lengthy prison sentence.

Elsewhere, Pierce Lavelle and Sarah Kennedy finally got somewhere with their courtship, a frankly strange video made us wonder what relevance "ASSAULT" has to AWC, Aimz defeated Andy Murray, The Furious Fists Of God enforced their superiority over the enforcers, and Sasha Volkyeva introduced Buddy Kingfisher for his first appearance in AWC. Of course, Adam Dick had to come out and steal his heat, and the two got it on in a high quality impromptu match culminating in victory for The Illustrious Face-Eater.

Winter break

AWC is now on a winter break, with events suspended until Fresh! on January 17th, from which date the weekly show will be back on the West Atlantic with commentary coming from the much-loved duo of Truth Waters and George Cassidy. In this usually-quiet post-Christmas lull, there is a fair amount of news to get through before the website goes quiet for a couple of weeks.

Possibly the biggest event of this week is the surprise disbanding of the AWC Livewire championship. The midcard title belt had been vacant since Jack Murphy dropped it in favour of his own Platinum title and David Harber and Sasha Volkyeva made a joint decision to get rid of a belt whose image has been tarnished by its reputation as a "jobber title". One suspects that this may be little more than a publicity convenience, however, with the accompanying press release placing much emphasis on the cooperation between the usually at-odds Entertainment Co-Managers in this decision. Making everything out to be rosy at the head of AWC is according to some a flagrant attempt to gloss over the cracks that have recently been appearing in and around the company, not least due to a significant shareholder questioning the "mindless spending" on some aspects of business. This shareholder's report, submitted to majority shareholder Sam Newman on Christmas Day, highlights in particular the Triangles event, whose "arrogant flashiness" led to "spending above sustainable levels", particularly on the Triangles match itself and on the Pyramid Scramble match which, "dismissed as a circus by purists", was "a complete waste of time and money". Increasing staffing costs are also slated by the shareholder, who criticises the "unnecessary addition" of two extra referees in Richie Travis and Selena Sumner, and the "extravagance" in hiring three former PCW commentators on lucrative contracts. He also calls the hiring of Sasha Volkyeva an "unnecessary doubling of the spending on wages for the Entertainment Manager position" since Volkyeva is receiving the same salary as Harber, who has not seen his earnings drop since her appointment which has in theory halved his workload. The shareholder's report concludes with the observation with "AWC must cut its runaway spending or it will run itself into the ground". Sam Newman dismissed it as "uninformed", but we must wonder how the only slight increase in market share over the past few months has justified the additional costs which do not appear to be building towards a future as Mr Newman claims. Could AWC be in trouble?

Tim Shipley, two-time Frontier champion, is recovering in hospital after spinal surgery following his extended brawl with Tony Aliso at Weingut Sonnenberg in Germany. Aliso has been extradited back to his home state of California and awaits trial for a multitude of crimes that could lead to lengthy imprisonment.

Another former AWC Frontier champion Andy Murray has parted with the promotion by mutual consent. The Scottish King of Cool's contract expired at Winter Warfare, where he lost a grudge match against rising star Aimz, and it is believed Murray intends to take a break from wrestling to focus on other interests. Long-time training camp wrestler Jayden Gi-Kyon has also been released.

Replacing them on the roster are new signings Shawn Harris, the son of "All F**king That" Jeff Harris, a veteran of wrestling; and husband-and-wife duo Pleasure and Pain. Pearl and Ms Volkyeva intend to make further hirings during the break, adding to recent successes such as the double-signing of HSW stars Jason Warr and Teresa Tomas and the enterprising move of bringing in former cW Legacy champion Buddy Kingfisher, who lost his first AWC match against Adam Dick at Winter Warfare.
