nbW: Twilight PPV 07-11-06

14 July 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

nbW rolled into the Sovereign Center, in Reading, Pa for the Twilight PPV, and left the building with a glare of success in nearly everyone's eyes. The event left the fans and even the staff impressed, and that is saying a lot.

Xander Napoli made his first 'State of the nbW Address' live for the PPV. The new divisions were discussed as was the announcement of nbW's plans for the next two months; the keyword, Global.

The first ever nbW Blitzkrieg Champion, North Atlantic Champion and Tag Team Champions were crowned at Twilight. But just because they won the Blitzkrieg Championship match, didn't mean they were guaranteed to walk out of the arena still Champion. The belt received quite the few holders in the course of the night, building the prestige of such a championship.

Placed together inside the square circle, the tag teams of nbW put on a great showcase of their talents, proving to nbW that they too, deserve the spotlight. Even one of the tag teams weren't whom they appeared to be.

Feuds were put to a rest when Spike Saunders faced his nemeses, Lone Wolf, in a hard fought fight within the confines of 'The Halo'; the end coming from outside help for both men, including a certain pink-haired former nbW champion. And also Jason Rhodes and Troy Adams settled their differences in a brutal hardcore battle, seeing only one man walking out at its end.

Cameron Cage went to the ring not knowing who or what to expect in the case of his opponent for the Dynasty/Keystone Championship, and by the time the bell tolled, he knew, just like the rest; nbW's all about Talent.

After months of wait, WAR finally got his chance at the World Championship against Andrew Martin. The end result, may not have benefited either man; thanks to the arrival of one "retired" superstar and a duo of mystery. One thing is for sure, the Age is Here.

Twilight had it all. From Midgets to missing members of Management. From Returns to Unprovoked Attacks. Even the World Champion had a word or three for his fans. All this and more with nbW's presentation of Twilight, Live only on PPV. Also be sure to check out our column 'Star Front' for the latest standings of your favorite superstars based on their performance at Twilight.
