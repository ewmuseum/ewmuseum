AWC: Testimony on the horizon

23 September 2005
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

Fresh! was a night of revelations for some and mystery for others. While the full details of The Educator's dalliances with the Prometheus Serum were revealed by Kasidy Drake, a guest from Core Wrestling, the plot thickened for those following the intricate story of Tim Shipley and The Illustrious Face-Eater - the website can exclusively reveal that the Face-Eater has sensationally distanced himself from allegations that he was behind the damage to Shipley's locker room. If (though this is highly unlikely) the man behind the mask is telling the truth, the question we now have to answer is: who or what is Omniscient?

A title changed hands, matches were made for Testimony and Super Series, and David Harber spent most of the show unconscious. Check out the transcript for full details.

AWC has also announced the cancellation by mutual consent of various workers' contracts.

Of these losses, the most notable is that of Adalia Delorian, who made a big impact when he debuted by winning the Best Of British match at Solarized on 1st July - despite not actually being of Welsh heritage as he was supposed to.

Other departures from the roster are: Crucifix and Ghost (jointly known as Deliverance), Tamura and Gakuto (jointly known as Team Super Evil) and Katerina Zagarovsky. The Educator has also officially had his contract terminated after the Prometheus Serum scandal.

This leaves a gaping hole in AWC's Alliance division, and David Harber is actively recruiting new faces. We are told that one or two new names may also come in to bolster the singles roster.

The AWC website was today updated with the Testimony card as we know it. More matches will be added to the schedule before the event one week today, but the revelation of the previously-unannounced table match between Red Rock and The British Bomber is enough to whet the fans' appetites. There is also confirmation of Pierce Lavelle versus Hate in a "No Escape" match (inside a roofed cage) for the Transatlantic title; the showdown between Tim Shipley and The Illustrious Face-Eater in a "10-Count" match (this will NOT be for the Face-Eater's newly-won Frontier title as it makes no sense to give Shipley a title opportunity in his last AWC match); the Alliance championship face-off between Emerald Isle II and T.T.S.; and FREDROCK~!'s "Janitor's Closet" match with both [name withheld].
