AWC: Birch the brief

12 May 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

Ralph Richards has been licking his wounds after his brutal assault by FREDROCK~!. Literally. Today, however, he took time out from his busy schedule of eating custard and playing Pac Man to address the state of his health to a jam-packed conference room with his lawyer Birch by his side. Here follows the transcript...

-

"Excuse me… testing… One, Two, Twenty!” Birch pauses and looks at a statement he had prepared before hand on a napkin.

“My client, Sir Ralph Rupert Richards, has sustained a severe injury. He has consulted the medical advice of a nurse.” Birch pauses and takes a sip of water. “...who you can see in the next issue of ‘House Of Anal’. She has diagnosed Mr. Richards with an osteitis pubis injury, resulting in Mr. Richards complaining about lost usage of his legs and loss of ability to pleasure himself.”

Ralph Richards puts his head in his hands and begins to weep.

“Therefore, it is my deepest regret to inform you all that Sir Ralph Regis Richards shall undoubtedly be unable to wrestle. Due to this, and the loss of income he shall suffer during the weeks of his recovery, he has filed a lawsuit against this hideous assassination attempt to end a innocent life by none other than FREDROCK~!”

Richards has pulled himself together as he takes the floor. “Thank you so much Birch. I have some final words… I hate to say this sooo much but with the loss of my legs...”

Ralph begins to rub his hand down his arm.

“See I can’t feel them. FREDROCK~!: your ass is gonna be sued.”

Birch gets up from his seat. He begins to wheel Richards, who is sat in a wheelchair only wearing a pair of underpants, down a small ramp. They get to the doorway. As Birch pushes Richards down a step, he falls out onto the floor. Richards gets up onto his feet and sits himself back in the wheelchair as the conference comes to an end, with the stunned stuffed teddy bears and Barbie dolls taking furious notes.

In related news, the first episode of Fresh! aired live worldwide on Tuesday night. Fans were treated to a spectacular display ranging from Hate's vicious and systematic decimation of Kris Krimzon to the glimmers of brilliance among the petulance of Pierce Lavelle. Pearl had some big announcements to make, most notably that Alexander Strider will be forced to defend his Transatlantic championship for the very first time this coming Tuesday. But who was to get the title shot?

Of course, no AWC show is complete without FREDROCK~!, and he was resplendent in his full glory. Encounters with Ralph Richards and a dating service proved amusing distractions from the serious, focused atmosphere of tense, technical wrestling action... oh, who are we kidding. But really, it was a great card. I promise!
