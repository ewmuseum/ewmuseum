AWC: A bloody mess, that's what!

18 May 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

Many people's expectations for this week's main event differed. Some expected just a simple title defence for Alexander Strider, adding some credence to his Transatlantic championship reign. Others willed his challenger on, hoping for a surprise victory for Pierce Lavelle. But no one could have predicted what really happened...

Let's put it this way. The crimson mask made its first appearance before the bell had even rung, and by the end of the match, the one man left standing was not Strider or Lavelle. We won't give any more away.

Unfortunately, AWC's plans for the next few weeks have been dealt a hefty blow. A phone call to AWC headquarters in Atlantic City today revealed that the Wachovia Arena in Wilkes-Barre, PA, was operating under the impression that the Fresh! show scheduled for May 31st had been cancelled. Entertainment Manager David Harber denies any such plans: "There wasn't even an issue regarding that show. I have no idea what the people up in Pennsylvania are playing at."

Pennsylvania-based attorney Eric Halloran has been called in to investigate the matter on the behalf of the Atlantic Wrestling Club. Right now, chances of a show on May 31st look slim, with the Wachovia Arena already having booked a new event in Fresh!'s place. The real question is, where did the arena get the idea that Fresh! had been cancelled? For updates as they come, keep your eyes fixed to the website.

In related news, wrestlers CMack, Danny Polar and Taylor Troublesome have all been released from their short-term contracts. Another AWC employee, Sektor, has parted from the promotion following a more extensive survey of his back revealing injuries more grave than originally thought, contracted after taking the Whiplash powerbomb into the turnbuckle from Pierce Lavelle at Zero To Hero.
