AWC: Garbage Crisis for DAC

21 June 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

A piece of footage detailing a meeting between Garbage Bag Johnny and Darcy Crisis had to be removed from last night's telecast of Fresh!burst, due to graphic and downright confusing content. However, it was learned that in that meeting the two agreed to make their contest at Divide And Conquer this Friday a Landfill match. Both combatants were later interviewed to receive their thoughts.

According to Garbage Bag Johnny, “Darcy Crisis and his henchmen surrounded me in the back, hypnotizing me with their black and white stripes. It’s a god damned conspiracy, I tell you! They’re out to get me! I want all referees barred from ringside at Divide And Conquer. That’s why I came up with the Landfill match; no referees needed, only a mound of garbage and a shovel!”

Darcy's response: "I can't believe management is so inept as to allow unfettered ON AIR drug use by their talent... but luckily I was able to use the situation to my advantage. That hobo will think twice about dropping a dose when he's raised the ire of the Darcinator. He thinks he got what he wanted with this Landfill match, barring all referees from ringside... but in fact, everyone will be barred from ringside. No Aimz, no referees, no zombies and no Riverside Motherfuckers. It's just him, and it's just me, and we're going to settle this Royal Court nonsense once and for all."

The object of a landfill match is similar to the concept of a buried alive match, only instead of a grave and a pile of dirt, there’s a hole on the entrance ramp next to a heaping pile of garbage. There are no pins, submissions, count outs, or disqualifications. The only way to win is to knock your opponent into the hole long enough to completely submerge them in rubbish.
