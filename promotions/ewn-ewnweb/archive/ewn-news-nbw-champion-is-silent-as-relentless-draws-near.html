nbW: Champion is Silent as 'Relentless' Draws Near

11 March 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

The big news around no brand Wrestling lately has been the silent Heavyweight Champion, Uncensored. With 'Relentless' just a little over a week away on Pay Per View (the third nbW Pay Per View), people are beginning to believe the Champion is a little worried about his pending title defense on March 19.

The Maximizer and Jason Kain have fought for the past month over the rightful claim to be the number one contender to the nbW Heavyweight Championship. Each of them wants to secure a spot in the main event of 'Relentless' against the Champion, but only one of them can. This coming week on 'Infamy,' Maximizer and Kain square off in a Ladder match to decide who will face Uncensored. The two weeks prior have tested Kain and Max as neither of them has scored a decisive victory over the other to officially challenge for Uncensored's belt. There must be a winner this Saturday night in the ladder match as the contract for the main event looms above their heads.

A number of other situations are heating up around nbW as young rookies like Blake Gray, Callie Urban, and Alan Helms have been turning heads of fans and management. Gray rolls into 'Infamy' this week coming off a huge victory over a five-year veteran Pat Dwyer who is said to be fuming after that loss. Going head-to-head in a rematch from two weeks ago, Blake Gray faces Rey Campbell, the only man to defeat Gray in the three matches he has had with the company.

Spike Saunders confuses fans as he teams with Mat Walton against Rejection and a partner of his choosing on 'Infamy.' The mysterious Torment will be in action as he takes on Alan Helms and Jim Thompson in a handicap match. Pat Dwyer will also see single's action as he takes on Erick Davies.

Expect to see a lot of things happen on the last 'Infamy' before next Saturday night's Pay Per View 'Relentless' presented by nbW! A whole card should be lined up by the time 'Infamy' goes off the air Saturday night on FSN!
