SCW :: Shooting from the Hip

4 September 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

A commentary by Paul Pryend

So, the Superior Championship Wrestling staff has given me yet another duty; to step away from signing megastars and autographs to bring it to you, the fans, in a direct and to the point manner. Actually, that’s not the case at all, in fact I’m doing this of my own free will because there are just some things you people really need to know. Right when you were convinced that the SCW Insider had all the scoops.

One of my reliable sources within the medical offices of SCW has informed me that the Global Champion wasn’t booked last week because he was injured in his match against Jack Griffith at The Main Event. I was even led to believe by this source that Robert Williamson requested his title not be on the line this week at LIVE! in his match against the National Champion, Dean Naylor.

Speaking of Naylor, did anyone else read where he was arrested? Me neither, but he most certainly was. I’m so glad he represents the European nations and not the United States. Some of the numerous charges being pressed against him by, what I heard was, four individuals that our National Champion pummeled in a bar brawl were dropped after Naylor agreed to community service. Until the rest of the charges are settled, Naylor still faces possible jail time.

Our Pepsi Cola representative and current Television Champion, Ice, was rumored to have a big problem with the most recent development of Shane Johnson. Supposedly, according to what a source in the booking department told me, Ice was to make the turn at LIVE! and go heel; however, Luciano showed his authority and not only gave the turn and push to Johnson, but further infuriated the situation by removing two losses on Johnson’s match record.

Some say the man of the night last Thursday was Jack Griffith. I agree to an extent. Anyone willing to come to the ring during the champion’s address to his fellow Buckeyes must have no worries in the world. Not only did he put the champion away for the evening, but he and Naylor gave another knock-down, drag out for the fans’ amusement. This is a man willing to hurt people if he has to in order to obtain what he wants. Not a man that I would rush to go head to head with.

Were the Tag Team Champions a little overexposed on LIVE! or what? I mean, if there were any segments within that show that didn’t feature them, I must have blinked and missed them. Quinn Masters was in TWO of the commercials during the event. A source in the executive offices let me know that due to their seven-digit contracts that they will most likely devour every show until the end of time. Speight, once an outspoken icon in the business, now just seems to sit to the side of Masters and chuckle and shake his head. Much like Masters' puppet, Speight speaks when Masters allows it. Must be nice to know the General Manager personally.

Speaking of our General Manager, a rumor running rampant in the locker room is that she may be pregnant! To enlighten those that are convinced otherwise, this is a show produced for your entertainment. If this rumor were true, Johnny Vivacious would have to be the father, wouldn’t he?

Vivacious will have an open contract with SCW as it’s going around he may be returning to one of his previous employers. He will continue to host Club Vivacious on LIVE! and that segment will take on more of a night club effect as the three piece office set is dumped and Vivacious will interview his guests in a bar setting, while being served most likely, in the ring. The hearsay has yet to be confirmed, but given his rocky past with Cyber Wrestling Revolution I’d say he plans on returning to Total Knockout Wrestling where he was allowed more of a hands on creative control over his character. It is rumored he still holds Christian Montiero in high regard.

Watch for the debut of Town and Country, though they were pushed out of the Deuces are Wild Tag Team Championship tournament last Thursday with the promise of a title shot, their debut will be something worth waiting for I’m told. Davyd North continues to impress the brass with his skills and in-ring ability. Flash Lightning, the transitional Television Champion, needs to pull his personalities into focus and get back in the chase.

Until next time, if I didn’t print it, it’s hearsay.
