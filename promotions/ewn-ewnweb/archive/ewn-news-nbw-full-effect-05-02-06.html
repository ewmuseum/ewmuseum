nbW: Full Effect 05-02-06

2 May 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

Nearly a month has gone by since nbW last graced the live television circuit (The Trials PPV). May 2nd marks the return of the nbW after four weeks of Spring Cleaning within the ranks. FX is proud to bring nbW Full Effect back on their schedule.

We at nbW take pride in being able to support the fresh blood in the industry, and tonight's no exception. Three wrestlers make their in ring debut tonight; Haywire and Jason Rhodes. In addition to that, it's been speculated that at least one of the championships will be on the line when either William Arthur Reagan or Cameron Cage defends their strap.

In addition to the matches, Xander Napoli has two shocking announcements to deliver. We've heard rumors that there is a new championship belt about to be thrown in the mix, but no confirmation has been available.

All this and more when nbW and FX bring you the Full Effect!
