Rules: Welcome to IWS!

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Rules Recreation: The one thing that every person in the world enjoys doing. How do we do it? We let ourselves go. Escape normal life and set out to make or minds and bodies free once again.
Us, we like to fight... Why do we fight? If you understood, you'd do it too. Our lives are driven into monotonous tradition. Work, Eat, Sleep. Is this what our lives our reduced to? Being forced to slave all day so someone else can reap the benefits of your blood, sweat and tears? How does this make you feel? Good? I didn't think so.
So how do you change all this? We're not offering an answer, we're offering a solution. When you fight, you can feel the change instantaneously. That tense, anxious feeling in the pit of your stomach, that is your hatred for being a person who is forced to be a nobody. The blood on your knuckles is the blood of your boss, your father, your wife. The broken teeth that fall out of your mouth are the very things that are holding you back in your ever pressing struggle for life. You spit them out and throw another punch.
In many cultures and religions there are rules to obey. Rules to make you some sort of disciplined student of society. Rules to upset you when you want to do something that is said to be wrong.
Rules are made to be broken. This is true, but if you want to regain your purpose in life with us, you're going to have to obey our rules, obey our regulations. We are not asking for much, just ten simple rules. If you can obey these, you're in...
#1 You do not speak for Faith or Hope. #2 YOU DO NOT SPEAK FOR FAITH OR HOPE!!!! #3 You do not complain about the results (If you lose a fight, it is your fault...not ours! We do not play favorites and if we say you lost then you lost.... you may think you did enough to win but if you didn't make it obvious to us then you must not have. We will not put up with bitching...)
#4 You DO NOT use other IWS superstars in your promos without their permission! #5 Stay in contact, if you cannot roleplay for a scheduled match then contact the staff on the out of character board, by instant messenger or through email.
#6 Fighting on the OOC Forums... Ok listen... We're not going to give you people an ok to fight on the Forums but if you're going to... do it like mature adults.
If we see that you can't solve your problems yourselves, we will have to step in and settle it for you. We can be a very, very vocal people... so we somewhat understand.... arguing will sometimes happen, but when you start acting like a 10 year old kid... that's when we will draw the line. You can take your shots at each other but we will not be giving you people very many bullets so say what you have to say and get over it. We don't want the Forums covered with shit.
#7 Originality is a must, if you can't be original just leave now. #8 DO NOT give out your password to anyone for any reason. This is grounds for immediate termination. #9 The Role-play deadline is based on the double deadline ratio. The Final deadline is Monday, 12 am IWS board Time, the first deadline is Saterday at 12am board time.
This means no posting your first rp on the day of the deadline. You must have your first rp up by the first deadline or you will lose your match. You can post as many as you want but keep in mind, holding rps is very much frowned upon, it is unfair to your opponents.
#10 Don't waste our time...... We're going to tell you now..... You people have the choice of making this fed great, or making this fed suck because we're going to work as hard as you do..... You slack...
We slack.... You do as good as you can, and We'll do the same. Have fun damn it..... that's what this fed is for. We want to have a good ass time, and most of that depends on you. As long as you do your job.... We'll do our job... YOUR JOB + OUR JOB= FUN!! Remember that....
