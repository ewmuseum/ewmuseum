Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

No F'N Limits IWS announced itself on 1 December 2007 as “back and better than ever”, the fed that “made you love underground wrestling”, promising violence, boobs and blood. Its hub-hosted site gave the rules (ten, the first two being not to speak for Faith or Hope, two of the handles that sign its news posts), a short FAQ that rules out real wrestlers, rolls the deadline on a double schedule and names its weekly show, Desire, and its pay-per-view line, Straight to DVD, and a page of the hard-edged matches the fed kept for feuds and big nights: Go to Hell, Flaming Warfare, Runaway Train, Pits of Hell and the rest.

The record is a single week. The posts of 1 to 3 December 2007 welcome the first four wrestlers, Glenn Legend, Ryan Havoc (called a former IWS Champion), Brandon Gateman and Ginger Snapps, whose profile pages are held, and say the fed would have its first card once it reached a signing target the post leaves blank. No card, results page or roleplay survives; the roleplay board the site links to was on an Invision Free forum that this collection does not hold. Two of the profile pages carry 1986 birth dates, which are the wrestlers' own and not the fed's.
