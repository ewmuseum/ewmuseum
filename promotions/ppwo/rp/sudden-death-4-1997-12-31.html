PPWO Sudden Death {4}

31 December 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=<> JD EAGLE- "Wow, Pillow & Colby, not to mention Simelia are forces to watch out for. Ladies and Gentleman, it's time for our main event, the 4 way dance featuring Angel Of Mercy, Alain Fiscus, Casey Stanfield & Shane MacMurchy. Take it away Kris..." =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=<> PPWO Ring ANNOUNCER Kris Shaw: This next match is the 4-Way Dance for the last spot in the World Title Tournament! (Crowd Pops) PPWO ANNOUNCER Kris Shaw: And Introducing first... From New York, New York.. weighing in at 245 lbs... here is "TOO NYCE" CASEY STANFIELD!!!!! ("Firm All Stars" by the Firm begins to play as Casey Stanfield walks out. The crowd is fairly behind him, as he makes his way to the ring.) PPWO ANNOUNCER Kris Shaw: The second competitor for this match... From Calgary, Alberta.. weighing in at 268 lbs.. here is... SHANE MACMURCHY!!!!! ("Phoenix" by Daft Punk begins to play as Shane MacMurchy walks out. The crowds initial response are boos, but Shane doesn't seem to care. He makes his way to the ring, standing at the opposite corner from Stanfield.) PPWO ANNOUNCER Kris Shaw: From Parts, Unknown.. weighing in at 357 lbs.... THE ANGEL OF MERCY!!!!! ("Ina-Gadda-Da-Vida" by Iron Butterfly begins to play, as the massive Angel of Mercy makes his entrance. The crowd erupts. He makes his way to the ring slowly.) PPWO ANNOUNCER Kris Shaw: And the final participant in this matchup... From Amarillo, TX... weighing in at 246 lbs... "THE ONE MAN RIOT" ALAIN FISCUS!!! ("Malpractice" By Faith No More begins to play. The crowd is at a mix of cheers and boos. Alain Fiscus doesn't make his entrance.) (The men in the ring look on, as Fiscus' music still plays. The Referee looks worried, as Fiscus still does not make his entrance.) (JDE): Well, it looks like Fiscus might be a no-show. Wait! What's this! Fiscus is coming from the Crowd! The Crowd is erupting! Nobody in the ring sees him! (Fiscus climbs over the guard-rail, jumps in the ring, and begins attacking Casey Stanfield. As soon as this happens, Shane and Angel begin going at it.) *ding* *ding* *ding* (JDE): All four men are going tooth and nail! Angel and Shane are in one side of the ring, Fiscus and Too Nyce in the other.. (All 4 men are brawling. Angel is pounding down on Shane! He hits a Headbutt! Shane goes down! He walks over to Fiscus and Stanfield, still brawling, grabs both of their heads and slams them together! The crowd erupts) (JDE): Angel of Mercy is the only man standing! Oh! MacMurchy just clipped his right leg! Angel is down! Shane puts a Figure Four on the Angel! (Fiscus and Stanfield get back up, pounding on each other. Fiscus hits a Low Blow! Casey is down! Fiscus runs over and Flying Legdrops Shane! Shane lets go. Fiscus starts pounding on The Angel, but the Angel feels nothing.. He grabs Fiscus and sends him to the ropes... Double Axe-Handle Chop! Fiscus goes flying! Casey and Shane are both working on the Angel now! He's down! Casey hits Shane with a Russian Legsweep!) (JDE): Fiscus just ran right into Casey with a Running Lariat! Fiscus picks Shane up.. Shane hits a Northern Lights Suplex! Shane gets back up and begins kicking Casey! The Angel picks Alain up.. Fiscus hit another Low Blow! The Angel is down! Fiscus grabs Casey and gives him a German Suplex! But Shane knocks Fiscus right back down with a Flying Clothesline! (The Angel slowly gets up.. Shane and Fiscus are pounding on each other.. The Angel picks up Casey.. Casey hits a DDT! He then runs at Fiscus and MacMurchy.. Roundhouse Kicks them both down! Casey then runs to the top-turnbuckle.. He jumps at the Angel.. The Angel catches him! The Angel has Stanfield in a Bear Hug! The ref asking Casey if he wants to submit) (JDE): You can just feel the pain of the Bear Hug by looking at Stanfield's face! But he's not giving up.. Shane and Alain are both brawling with each other.. Alain runs at the Angel... Flying Dropkick! Angel lets go of Casey as they both fall down! (Fiscus picks the Angel up... Belly-to-Belly Overhead Suplex! But Casey catches Alain with an Axe Kick! Shane then Flying Clotheslines Casey!) (JDE): These men are going at it with no stops! They all must realize how important it is to win this match.. (Shane picks up Casey.. throws him to the ropes... Casey ducks a clothesline.. comes back and hits Shane with a Front Kick! Then then picks up Shane and hits a Snap Suplex! The Angel and Fiscus are duking it out.. The Angel picks up Fiscus.. CHOKE SLAM! He hits a Leg Drop! he picks Fiscus up... Delay Suplex!) (JDE): Just look at the Angel's strength! He's held Fiscus up there for a good 15 seconds.. comes down.. SLAM! Fiscus is in pain! The Angel then Clotheslines Casey! Then Shane! The Angel of Mercy is on a roll! (Shane and Alain give the Angel of Mercy a DOUBLE Low Blow! The Angel goes down! Alain picks up the Angel.. another vicious German Suplex! Shane and Casey are brawling.. Casey kicks Shane in the gut.. Piledriver! Casey smiles as Shane grabs his neck.. Casey picks Shane up.. sets him on the turnbuckle... Belly-to-Back Superplex!! He goes for the pin... 1.... 2..... Fiscus kicks Casey in the head! he picks him up... a Chop! Casey comes right back with a Chop! They continuely chop each other! They chop each other at the same time.. Both men fall down!) (JDE): They are too much for each other! The Angel runs over to Shane.. picks him up... No! Shane hits a Side Suplex! Shane runs to the ropes as The Angel makes his way... Shane hits the Angel with a High Cross Body! NO! The Angel catches him! BACKBREAKER! (The Angel then makes his way to Casey and hits him with an Elbowdrop. He tries to Elbowdrop Fiscus, but Fiscus moves.. Fiscus runs to the ropes... Baseball Slides right into the Angel! Another time! He goes for a third.. But Casey meets him with a kick to the midsection! He then delivers a massive Powerbomb! He goes for the cover... 1... 2... Shane with the save! Shane picks up Casey.. Reverse DDT! Shane goes for the cover... 1.... 2..... kick out! The Angel and Fiscus are brawling.. Fiscus kicks him in the gut.. runs to the ropes... Dropkick to the Knee! he runs to the ropes again.. But the Angel catches him in a Spinebuster! he goes for the count... 1.... 2.... kick out! Shane picks up Casey... But Casey hits a Back Heel Kick! He then puts Shane in a Figure Four! The ref is asking Shane..) (JDE): Shane is in pain! Too Nyce has this Figure Four on solid... Fiscus just hit the Angel with a Double-Arm DDT! he runs over to the top-turnbuckle... Top-Rope Legdrop on Stanfield! Casey lets go.. Fiscus picks him up... has him in a bulldog headlock.. runs to the ropes... SPRINGBOARD BULLDOG! Fiscus goes for the cover... 1..... 2.... Shane makes the save. Shane then runs to The Angel and clotheslines him out of the ring! Both men fall to the outside. They begin brawling. The Angel knocks him down! He begins to get back into the ring, but Fiscus Baseball Slides into him! Casey catches Fiscus in a German Suplex of his own! (Shane picks up a chair... *SMACK* right on the back of the Angel! Again! The Angel's looking at him! It didn't effect the Angel! He hits him with a kick to the midsection.. Piledriver onto the Chair! The Angel makes his way back into the ring.. grabs both Casey and Alain! DOUBLE CHOKE SLAM! He goes for the cover on both men.... 1..... 2.... Both men kick out at the same time! Shane is still lying on the floor... but he slowly makes his way back into the ring. The Angel picks up Casey and throws him to the ropes... the collision.. POWERSLAM by The Angel! the cover... 1.... 2.... Fiscus makes the save. Shane then Flying Clotheslines Fiscus! Both Men fall out of the ring! They begin to brawl.. Fiscus with a kick to the midsection... He Powerbombs MacMurchy through the timekeeper's table! *SMASH*) (JDE): Shane has got to be out from that! Fiscus makes his way back to the ring where The Angel and Stanfield are duking it out.. Fiscus runs at them.. but they hit Fiscus with a Double Clothesline! Fiscus did a 360 in midair! he lands on his face! Casey then hits a Back Heel Kick on The Angel! He grabs him... Foot Sweep! Casey goes to the top... hits The Angel with a Missile Dropkick! He goes for the cover.... 1.... 2.... Fiscus again with the save! (Shane is slowly making his way up... He gets in the ring... walks over to Fiscus from behind... hits him with a Low Blow! he then Legdrops the back of the One Man Riot's head! Casey and the Angel make their way up.. Casey hits The Angel with a Spinning Heel Kick! he runs at Shane.. Shane ducks! Casey lands on his side! Shane picks him and throws him to the ropes... Flying Dropkick by Shane! Fiscus slowly makes his way up.. runs over to the Angel and puts him in a Texas Cloverleaf! The ref is asking him if he wants to submit..) (JDE): The Angel of Mercy doesn't even look to be in that much pain! he's shaking his head no... Shane just hit Fiscus with his patented Superkick! But Casey catches him in a roll up! The count... 1...... 2..... kick out. The Angel picks up Fiscus... Another Backbreaker! the count... 1..... 2..... kick out. Casey throws Shane to the ropes... Lariat! He hits a Kneedrop! he goes for the pin.... 1..... 2.... The Angel makes the save! Fiscus runs to the ropes... Catches the Angel in a Spinning Heel Kick! He then picks up Stanfield... Inverted Powerbomb! (Fiscus picks up Stanfield again, but Stanfield hits a Small Package! 1........... 2............... kick out! Casey hits a Chop! Fiscus back with a Chop! Casey knocks Fiscus down with his third Chop! he picks him up... Double-Underhook Suplex! Shane then slams his knee into the small of Stanfield's back! He hits him with a Back Suplex! He goes to the top... he looks to be attempting a Splash... but the Angel is covering Casey! Shane hits a Splash on both men! Fiscus then runs over and pins both the Angel and Shane! The count..... 1....... 2...... both men kick out! Alain then runs over to Stanfield and picks him up.. throws to the ropes..... Fiscus lands a Hurricurana! The Angel and Shane are brawling once again.... The edge they way to the ropes... They both fall out! They are still brawling... Fiscus and Stanfield are both lying on the mat.... The ref is counting out MacMurchy and the Angel) (JDE): If they're not careful, they're both gonna get counted out! They are attacking each other at full extent! The ref is on 6........ 7......... 8......... 9................................ 10! Both Shane MacMurchy and the Angel of Mercy have been counted out! But they don't seem to care! They are dishing out and taking in all sorts of damage! Security from the back are coming to seperate these two! (Fiscus and Stanfield both slowly get up.... Fiscus hits Stanfield with a Chop... takes him and sets him facing outward on the top turnbuckle... FISCUS HITS AN INVERTED SUPERPLEX! But he's too exhausted to make the cover.... Both men gradually get up... Fiscus to the ropes... Stanfield hits a Superkick of his own! The cover.... 1......... 2....... kick out! Stanfield picks him up... Front Face Piledriver! He again picks him up... He goes for another Front Face Piledriver! NO! Fiscus sweeps his legs from under him! Fiscus locks on a Texas Cloverleaf! The ref is asking Stanfield...) (JDE): The Angel of Mercy and Shane MacMurchy are finally back in the locker-room... The ref asking Stanfield if he's gonna submit... he's shaking his head no.... Fiscus lets go! He stands there looking down at Stanfield... He picks him up... Stanfield punches him square in the face! (Fiscus is bleeding from the mouth! Stanfield with a Chop... hits Fiscus with a Kneelift! Another one! Another one! He's saying this is over! He's going for the New York Suplex! Fiscus blocks it! Fiscus blocks it again! Casey drops Fiscus and gives a couple of kicks to Fiscus' head. He picks him up again... NO! Fiscus hits a Low Blow! Fiscus picks up Stanfield in a Fireman's Carry....) (JDE): This is it! This is what he's been looking for! FISCUS HITS IT! HE HITS THE DEATH VALLEY DRIVER!!! He Goes for the pin! (The ref counts...... 1....... Fiscus pulls the tights......2...........................3!!!!!!) (JDE): Fiscus has won! The ref raises Fiscus' hand up! Fiscus takes his hand away and makes his way out of the ring and into the dressing room. (Fiscus has a smirk on his face as he makes his way to the locker room. He flips off a member of the crowd who is laying the words on him. Stanfield is sitting in the ring pondering on what happened. He looks disgusted, as he stands up, and leaves the ring. While he makes his way to the back, the crowd cheer him on.)
