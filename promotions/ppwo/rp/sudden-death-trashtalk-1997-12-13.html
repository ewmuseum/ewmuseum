PPWO Sudden Death: John "Brain" Smith TrashTalk

13 December 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Camera shot 1: A room without a window and John Smith sitting at a table together with an unknown man. [The walls of the room are painted black, blue, green and red and each wall has a different colour. In opposite of the door - where the cameraman is standing - there are two men sitting on wooden chairs in front of a steel table.] Camera shot 2: Zoom of the table [On the table different things are lying. First of all there is a medical encyclopedia, then there is a notebook and a yellow pen and finally there is a radio with "Breed to Breathe" by Napalm Death playing.] Camera shot 3: John Smith sitting at the table talking with the other person [He is wearing a Cradle of Filth t-shirt, wore down jeans and black boots. The man sitting next to him is wearing a black suit and a black tie. Beside this he`s wearing glasses. When the cameraman enters both men are discussing something and they don`t stop.] Man: And you are sure that everything you described to me is right? John Smith: Of course I am. We wouldn`t be here if I didn`t. Man: And it is impossible that you suffer from schizophrenia? John Smith: Why the hell should I know? You are the psycho analyst, aren`t you? Man: I am, but your case is more complicated than anything I have seen so far. [After a few seconds of thinking:] Well, judging on what I have heard so far and based on my observation of your behavior in the last hours I would say that you are definitely NOT schizophrenic, but at the moment I can`t tell you yet what you are suffering from. John Smith: But you have to know at least something about my sickness? Man: I do. I know its symptoms and if I work at you for a while I could even find out what caused this strange sickness. But that requires a lot of time and I am not sure how well I will manage. John Smith: I am sure you will do a great job and I am ready to answer all of your... [Finally John Smith notices the camera and turns around.] John Smith: Welcome everybody, I have invited all of you here into one of my rooms, because there is something very important I have to tell you. As some of you know I was waiting for my psycho analyst [John points to the man sitting beside him who nodds] two days ago, but because of a tragic accident he wasn`t able to make it by then. Today he has finally found the time to join me here in this room to discuss the problem I have with my mind. I don`t know how much you have heard from our conversation, so I`ll just repeat the most important things. And I have good news for you - my psycho analyst has confirmed me that the theory I came up with two days ago was right. I am NOT a schizophrenic, but still I have a very serious sickness in my mind and it seems that it`s impossible to heal. It is still unclear what caused this problem, but the symptoms are clear: I am living in the illusion that I am suffering from schizophrenia and this causes me to change my behaviour all the time. It`s not for real, but my sickness makes me think that I am a different man every few minutes. I have no idea yet what influence this will have on my future, but I don`t think that it will change very much, since there is really nothing you could do against it. No matter what happens I will make sure that I`ll stay in the wrestling business and continue my job to go out every night and give my best and I`ll try to win as often as possible, with a little bit of luck even winning some titles. But I wouldn`t like to name too big targets because I am still a newcomer and I will know more about my future in a few months` time. [Suddenly John Smith gets a strange look on his face and is quiet for a few seconds. When he starts talking again you have the feeling that he`s a completely different man. You can see that the psycho analyst look on his watch and makes a few notes. John Smith turns around and sitches the side of the playing tape. After a moment "The End" by the Doors blasts through the room.] What was I talking about? Oh yeah, the Zielinski Cup 1998. Well, first of all I would like to thank Milosz and [name withheld] who gave me the opportunity to be part of this tournament, although they didn`t know anything about me and my wrestling ability before. I am sure that I won`t disappoint them in my performance, because this tournament has been one of the main factors which caused me to start wrestling. I knew exactly that for a rookie there are only a few ways to become well-known and popular in the wrestling business. One of them is to start right in one of the very few major federations outthere - and I have to admit that this is also a possibility I DO have, since I was able to sign a contract with the NeCW - then as a second possibility you can try to get your hands on one of the BIG superstars in wrestling, although this is NOT a method I would recommend, because a clear win for the superstar is almost sure without taking care of the wrestling skills, and the third possibility is the one I had the most faith in - to sign in for a major tournament and win it. I know that my comments will appear definitely too self-sure for most of you, but I don`t care about it, because I know that everything will change once you have seen me in the ring. I am not here to job for anyone nor to make so called superstars like "Playboy" Ronnie D, Vampyro, Rick Styles or Alex Extreme look good. The only target I`ve got in this tournament is to win it and then to move onto bigger things like finally winning the PPWO Arizona title, or kicking some asses in the NeCW. Camera shot 4: Radio ["The End" by the Doors fades away and "Fuel" by Metallica blasts through the room. John Smith gets a puzzled look on his face and doesn`t seem to know what is going on when he starts talking again. One more time the psycho analyst looks on the watch and makes some notes.] Life. A word used daily by everyone of us. But who does really understand what it means to live? Can anyone of you definitely say that he has fully understood the meaning of this complex notion? Is it a life if you are paralised and tied to a bed without the slightest possibility of improvement? Is it a life if you are living outthere in the streets, fighting for your right to "live" everyday? Is it a life if you have never had a chance to live it, being born in a ghetto or a slum? I am not here to answer this questions, only to draw your attention on problems of everyday life, noone of us deals with, living in a "perfect" world. Be honest, when was the last time one of you rich assholes did something for the poor, sick and weak of this world? When was the last time you did at least think about them and the problems they have in their "life"? I`m leaving this topic for the moment, but I`ll make sure to come back to it in near future when everyone of you has had the chance to think about his behaviour. Back to the Zielinski Cup. My first opponent on the 24 December will be Joe Mama. Joe, I have heard a bit about you and it seems that you are quite a good wrestler. Also you have to have a lot of endurance if he wants to step in one ring with Chris Quigley and fight him 60 minutes long. I wish you all the best for this match, but unfortunately for you this will not help you in your match against me. Wanna know why? Because each time you step in one ring with John Smith you have to be ready to fight an opponent with three or four different styles of wrestling. And this counts for everyone of you outthere. Nobody is good enough to be prepared for a match against me and I will prove it everyone who is man enough to accept my challenge. Yes, this is an open challenge to the whole wrestling world. If anyone of you is ready for a match against the unpredictible John Smith, just contact the organizators of the Zielinski Cup at [detail withheld] and they will make sure I get your message. But before I try to conquer the whole world, I will concetrate on my future opponents in the Zielinski Cup. In the second round I will have to face either Professor Payne or Rick Styles and I have big respect for both of `em. They are two great wrestlers and Rick Styles is almost a wrestling legend. Nevertheless there will be no way one of them could beat me in the ring, no matter what kind of trick they will try. I am ready for every surprise you can have prepared for me and I have a lot of surprises for you myself. But for the moment I am saying goodbye and see you tomorrow.
