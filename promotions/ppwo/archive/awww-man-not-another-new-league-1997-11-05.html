I know we're all thinking 'Damn, that's just what we need, another league that won't last two weeks'. Well, just give us half a chance, we'll all just have to see what happens. _________________________________________ |_________________________________________| _____ _____ __ __ ____ | \ | \ \ \ _ / / / \ | O | | O | \ \ / \ / / / \ | __/ | __/ \ \/ \/ / | O | | | | | \ _ / \ / |___| |___| \__/ \__/ \_____/ __________________________________________ |__________________________________________| -*-PPWO HANDBOOK VERSION 1.0-*- ======================================================<> Introduction... PPWO(PHOENIX Pro) is an E-Fed that made it's appearence first in June of 1997, then known simply as PPW. After a few wrong turns, it was given a full face lift, becoming PHOENIX Pro. At that point, PPWO got it's first measure of sucess, luring in several big names, and holding top notch matches for slightly over 2 monthes before closing down due to personal conflicts among staff members. Now, in early October '97, just as the mythical Firebird where PPWO draws it's name from, we are ready to be reborn and to rise to new hights from the ashes of the past... =========<> *PPWO Info... *Sucess in the PPWO(How to get it)... I'm sure you're wondering by this point, "How do I suceed in PHOENIX Pro?", well it's simple. There are several ways to reach the top of the PPWO, and here they are... *FLASH or ROLEPLAY-- A flash or a roleplay, is a way your wrestler may speak his mind. If your wrestler prides himself on his work on the mic, then this is your ticket to sucess. If you aren't sure what a RP(roleplay)is, well I'll fill you in now. RPing is easy. The main goals of it are to stay true to charecter, be descriptive, and to get your point across with out babbling. In PPWO, Your Flashes are judged by quality, not quantity. I know, alot of feds say this, but PHOENIX Pro means it. You want to try and RP so that you keep your audience interested. No one wants to read endless flashes that go on and on. *WINNING ON WRESTLING ABILITY-- In real life, there are wrestlers who don't give extensive interviews, yet still suceed. Take Dean Malenko for instance. Malenko did not gain his reputation by working the mic. He did so with his moves in the ring. So, in PHOENIX Pro, we offer you the option of doing this. How so? Well it's simple. If you aren't the best RPer in the land, you can do things such as writing "Showcase" matches for yourself(See Section On Showcase Matches), sending in Match Strats(See Section On Match Strats), and plenty of Challenges(See Section On Challenges). *SHOWCASE MATCHES-- A showcase match is a match against a low level wrestler or as much as I hate to say it, a "jobber". The PPWO has several "Showcase" wrestler, you can find a list of them at the PPWO Home Page. Matches against Showcase wrestlers don't count as much in the rankings as matches against Superstars, but they are an undeniable part of the game. PPWO encourages, and in some cases requires handlers to write one Showcase match a month. There are several benefits to writing Showcase matches for your wrestler. It shows us how your wrestler acts in a match, it gives you points in the rankings, and gives your wrestler more exposure. It's not hard to do, just write what happens, and we'll edit it, putting it on the card. *STRATS-- Strat is short for strategy, which is basically just what you want your wrestler to do in a match. It's an extra simple way to participate. All you have to do is to send a mail to myself saying something such as-- "Mr.President, I know my opponent is stronger, so I want to use a "hit & run" strategy in our match on tuesday. If at all possible, I'd like to take him outside the ring and use a chair to take him out for the 10 count. Thank you.". It could be even more simple, or more complex, it's up to you! You can even send in a strat saying you want your friend to interfere for you. Your options are limitless. *CHALLENGES-- If you would like to challenge a wrestler, you could do it in a few ways. Either do it in an interview(Flash or InRing), or simply send in a 'contract' to the president. All this is, is a message saying "My wrestler(whatever his name is), would like a match(specify on what kind of match)with this wrestler(the guy you're challengeing) on Tuesday." I will then forward the 'contract' to the man your challening, and if he agrees, the match is signed. *TITLES(What they are & order of importance)... PPWO Heavyweight Title(Universal Weight)- The PPWO is the leagues biggest prize. It must be defended once a month minimum(You can be sure it'll be defended MUCH MORE than once a month). PPWO Arizona State Title(Universal Weight)- This is the secondary title(Today Arizona, tommorrow the world! ;)) It can be challenged for by anyone, and must be defended every week. Defitnently a great stepping stone to world title shots. PPWO Tucson Heavyweight Tag Team Titles- This is self explanatory, and more or less goes by the same guidelines as the heavyweight singles title. *NOTE* More titles may be added once we pick up momentum. *CARDS- As of this moment, the PPWO will only have one televised card a week(until we pick up momentum & some writers that is). We will also put out house shows 1-3 times a week. On the big show we'll have 8-10 matches, 5-6 live and 2-3 summarized. Of course, this all depends on the reaction of this federation, and the number of writers we have. *PPWO NEWSLETTER- Every once in a while (At LEAST once a month.) PPWO will put out a special newsletter keeping newcomers updated on the happenings in the PPWO. It will also feature rumors, and anything else that would enlighten you in the knowledge of e-wrestling. (Damn, sometimes I sound gay.) *PAY PER VIEW- Grand Canyon COLLISION! First off, on our weekly shows and house shows, we'll have initiation matches, the winners get into our special 8 man tournament for the PPWO Heavyweight Title, it'll all happen on one night, GRAND CANYON COLLISION! We'll also feature many other matches on the card. Hopefully, (It's in our plans.) The Tucson Tag Team Titles and The Arizona State Title will be claimed by then. *THE APPLICATION- Now duh.
