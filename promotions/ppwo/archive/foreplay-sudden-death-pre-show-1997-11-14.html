PPWO Foreplay [Sudden Death Pre-Show]

14 November 1997

From the ashes of the past... The journey begins a new... The PHOENIX flys again! _________________________________________ |_________________________________________| _____ _____ __ __ ____ | \ | \ \ \ _ / / / \ | O | | O | \ \ / \ / / / \ | __/ | __/ \ \/ \/ / | O | | | | | \ _ / \ / |___| |___| \__/ \__/ \_____/ __________________________________________ |__________________________________________| PPWO...Please Sir...May I Have Another?!? [SCENE- The PPWO news room, a low budget closet sized room with nothing more than an old desk with a PPWO banner hanging off of it. Sitting behind the old desk is none other than PPWO Commentator James Dean "J.D." Eagle. He is wearing a relaxed looking outfit of simple jeans and a PPWO T-Shirt. He smiles wide at the camera and begins to speak...] J.D.EAGLE- "Welcome fans to 'PPWO: Foreplay', the official pre-game show of PHOENIX Pro! We've got tons of things to do to get ready for the PPWO's first ever show, so lets get to a run down of the matches..." K.T.Kult VS. Killer Katamount(JD EAGLE's View)-- "This should be a good one folks, though I've heard rumors of a possible injury sustained during training by Katamount. Kult is considered by most people to be one of, if not the up & coming superstar in our sport, and I personally know that he is anxious to get in there and do some work. Another factor that could help him, is his friendship with one 'Vile' Vic Studd, a veteran wrestler, making his return to the ring here in Phoenix Pro. Katamount could surprise you, but I wouldn't count on it. EAGLE's Prediction: K.T.Kult. "No Gimmicks Needed" Aaron Sykes VS. Eric 'The Livin' Legend' Cooper(EAGLE's View)-- "This could be one of our most interesting contest, pitting Eric the veteran against the youngster with extremely little pro experiece in Sykes, that bit of info is enough for me to give the advantage to Eric Cooper. Sykes could pull off an upset though, and defeating a name like Cooper could sky rocket this kid's career here in the PPWO. I for one don't believe in miracle's so look for Cooper to take this one. EAGLE's Prediction: Eric Cooper. The Showstoppers(John Dominate & "Main Event" Barry Cole) VS. Enforcement & Elimination(Jerald & Dominic Pezzino)-- "This match is probably the most evenly pitted match in the tag team title tournament, with both teams without a doubt being amongst the favorites to take the titles, of course, only one is going to walk away from this one with their tournament run still in tact. As much as I admire the work the Showstoppers have done, I've got to say that The Pezzinos, being a father & son duo and having spent so much time together get the advantage. EAGLE's Prediction: Enforcement & Elimination. Kaine VS. Jason Roth(EAGLE's View)-- "This one is a time filler more or less. We put a doninate force like that of Kaine in there, and when he drew Jason Roth, this thing just was over. Kaine's dominate power moves, coupled with the fact that his intimidating presence is enough to scare most opponents before the bell even rings. Look for Kaine to win in a BIG way. EAGLE's Prediction: Kaine. Crackwh*re Bob VS. EMT-- "This match probably shouldn't have even been sanctioned. EMT is a solid wrestler, who's been doing decently all over the wrestling world. CWBob, well, is a skanky young man with virtually no wrestling skills and even less interest in being a wrestler. I personally can't see a man the likes of Bobby Bradstreet going any futher in this tournament, this sport, or this world as it is. Look for EMT to win this thing. EAGLE's Prediction: EMT. Bringer's Of Havok(Walter Shaker & Dan Durante) VS. Pain Company-- The PPWO hasn't heard a thing from the Pain Company since they signed their contracts, and we can't even confirm that they'll be there this Sunday. Shaker & Durante, although a good team, may have problems seeing as they haven't teamed together much. There's not much more we can say about this one, I'm picking BoH, but it could go either way. EAGLE's Prediction: Bringer's Of Havok. "Simply The Best" James Pillow VS. Magnus Colby-- "Here is battle of two young men, looking to take their careers to new levels. I can't understand it, but Pillow seems to have a fixation with some low level Showcase by the name of Andru Edwards. Come on Pillow, call you call yourself "The Franchise" you call yourself "Simply The Best"? Seriously man, if you were gonna steal someone's gimmick, don't take Edwards. Based on wrestling ability, I'd still pick Pillow to take this one home. EAGLE's Prediction: James Pillow. Alain "The One Man Riot" Fiscus VS. Shane MacMurchy VS. "Too Nyce" Casey Stanfield VS. Angel Of Mercy-- "When the good men in the PPWO offices booked this thing, they expected little out of 4 unknowns. Now it would seem that this thing could steal the whole show, with all these youngsters looking ripe for some sort of title reighn here in the PPWO. Fiscus has been preparing for an all out 'Riot', no pun intended, and is expecting a brawl to end it all. Stanfield has more or less ignored MacMurchy & AoM, concentrating his efforts mostly on Alain. MacMurchy, I believe, is ready for anyone, and though odds makers are proclaiming him a dark horse, I wouldn't be surprised one bit of he took this thing. AoM is coming in as the massive underdog, but again, nothing would surprise in this big contest. Who do I take though, I'll go out on a limb and pick the dark horse here in Shane. EAGLE's Prediction: Shane MacMurchy. J.D.Eagle- "Fans, if I were you, I woudn't dare miss the action at the PPWO Convention Center in Phoenix, Arizona this Sunday, Nov. 16th. Thanks for joining us today folks, and don't forget, PPWO Sudden Death is this Sunday, DON'T miss it.
