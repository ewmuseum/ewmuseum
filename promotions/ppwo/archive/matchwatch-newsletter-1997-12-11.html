It's NOT the end of the world if you DON'T but it IS nice if you DO! :-) WHAT TO SEND? v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^ - TO GET MATCHES: Challenge, Challenge, Challenge! - TO WIN: Roleplay, Roleplay, and (did I mention?) Roleplay! - ALSO IT HELPS TO: Do in ring interviews! (Sunday Sudden Death) Just leave space for the announcers to ask questions, or write them yourself. You fill in the answers or whatever and we'll do the rest! These are due the Friday before the card if the questions are to be filled in by me, otherwise it's OK to have them in for Saturday. - AND IT NEVER HURTS TO: Send in a STRAT! That's short for STRATEGY. A strategy would include how you want your guy to go about wrestling the match, or any sneak attacks/double crosses that you want to see happen. This is not required every time ... just when you want something special to happen. It REALLY helps improve the angles and plotlines. We can't always follow your suggestion to a "T" - for instance, say that another person has a strat that conflicts with your strat. Then, we have to compromise - but we will try. - THE BOOKING SYSTEM: This is fairly simple and laid out. Your flashes and in- rings count for three quarters of winning/losing. Strats and the heat you build count for 5%. And angles count for 20%. What this means is that flashes/in-ring are the most important, and most of the time if your flashes are better, well, you win. _BUT_ if there is an angle that I like, you MAY job to fulfill the angle, ESPECIALLY if the flashing is close. In this fed, there is an "everybody jobs at one time or another" clause. None of us are omnipotent, therefore, you can and will job at times. Now, if you don't like it, there's the door. We're not forcing you to stay. __BUT__, these are fair rules and shouldn't cause too many problems. Let's have some fun! I can't have inactive flashers. - RP'ING RULE #2: You are allowed the following _PER WEEK_, as in number of flashes: * _4_ roleplay per week (no more) * _1_ in-ring (wrestler gets in the ring on card and speaks) * _X_ character development flashes (do not count towards matches) Don't feel that you absolutely need to do the rps _and_ the in-ring to win. But you'd better do one or the other, at the least, unless you wanna job.
