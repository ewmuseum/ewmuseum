(crowd cheers) Bill Eadie and Barry Darsow, better known as Ax and Smash of Demolition, formed one of the most explosive and exciting tag teams of the 1980s and were the first team to ever win three WWF World Tag Team championships. Tonight, we celebrate her career. Scott Hall and Kevin Nash, the Outsiders, hit their stride when they joined the World Wrestling Federation in the mid 1990s as Razor Ramon and Diesel respectively. Both would go on to revolutionize the WWF and then the WCW, forming the New World Order with Hulk Hogan and changing the very face of wrestling forever. Eric Bischoff went toe to toe with the greatest wrestling promoter of all time and nearly toppled him. He changed the way wrestling was promoted, delivering main event matches each week and brought the entire industry to a whole new plateau and tonight, we honour that achievement and welcome him to the UWOW Hall of Fame. Dick the Bruiser and the Crusher were well known for boozing, smoking cigars, and kicking ass in the ring in the 60s, 70s and 80s. They knew what it meant to be tough, smash-mouth wrestlers. The late, great Andy Kaufman knew what sports entertainment was back in 1983 when he feuded with UWOW Hall of Fame legend Jerry Lawler. He is the first celebrity and the only celebrity to be inducted into the UWOW Hall of Fame and there is no question that while Andy Kaufman did not fit well with Hollywood, he sure as heck fit well with wrestling and we celebrate his life tonight. Greg "The Hammer" Valentine is a second generation star, son of the great Johnny Valentine, tag team partner of Ric Flair and former WWF Intercontinental and Tag Team Champion as well as NWA United States and Tag Team Champion among other accomplishments. Nikolai Volkoff first appeared as a Mongol but soon developed in to one of the most hated... and, as a result, most loved... bad guys of the 1980s. He would achieve the zenith of his career at Wrestlemania I in 1985, winning the world tag team championship with the Iron Sheik, who also goes in to the Hall of Fame tonight as part of the Class of 2008. The Iron Sheik, former WWF Champion, brought bad guy wrestling to a whole other level when he defeated Bob Backlund for the WWF Title in 1983 and he didn't stop there. He went on to form a successful team with Nikolai Volkoff and joined Sgt. Slaughter to terrorize Hulk Hogan in the early 1990s as well. Killer Kowalski is now best known for training Triple-H, but he should also be remembered for classic battles with the likes of Bruno Sammartino, Bob Backlund and Pedro Morales. He was the quintessential villain in the ring but is now beloved for years of service. Finally, Beautiful Bobby and Sweet Stan, the Midnight Express. These two men revolutionized tag team wrestling, brought high impact, aerial maneuvers to the matches and, with Hall of Famer Jim Cornette, raised the bar substantially and permanently in the ranks of tag team wrestling.
