Wrestler Tribute: Ric Flair

16 June 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Whoooo!!! The Nature Boy Ric Flair!!! What can you say about a man that has done everything you can do in this sport of ours?? Ric Flair, the 14 time Heavyweight Champion in the WCW, NWA and WWF. Ric Flair started his career in the 1970's and was a h it from the start as he won Tag Team titles with Greg Valentine and other partners before moving on to the United States Title status and eventually to World Title status beating men like Handsome Harley Race, Dusty Rhodes and Ricky Steamboat to mention a few. Flair was the king of the NWA and WCW before a contractual dispute sent Flair packing to Stamford and the World Wrestling Federation were he wrestled from the Summer of 1991 to Winter of 1993. Flair instantly made the WWF his home by coming out on national television with Boby the Brain Heenan proclaiming to be the REAL WORLD'S CHAMPION as he insulted Hulk Hogan, then WWF World Champion. Flair even had the NWA World Title belt with him which was video distorted by the WWF after WCW made complaints about "their" title being used, when it was in fact the NWA World Title. Flair did so much in the WWF from his great feud with Hulk Hogan to his feud with the Macho Man Randy Savage over the love of Miss Elizabeth, who Flari claimed was really HIS behind the scenes. Flair finally fell from grace in the end of 1992 when he lost his second WWF World title to an up and coming Bret the Hitman Hart who went on to become a legendary star in the WWF! Flair made Bret what he was by doing the infamous job!! Flair then had his final feud in the WWF by feuding with his former manager and what he called his "Executive Consultant", Mr.Perfect Curt Hennig. Flair lost a CAREER MATCH to Perfect the night after the 1993 Royal Rumble on Monday Night Raw. Flair leaves the WWF and... HELLO WCW!! Flair gets a great homecoming as he shines like a blazing star as he takes the NWA Title from BARRY WINDHAM to become the new NWA World Champion, a title victory WCW did not recognize! Flair went on to feud with the late Rick Rude as he lost the NWA Title to Rude, which was later renamed the International Heavyweight Title. Flair then went on to defeat Vader for the WCW World Title at Starrcade 1993 and went on from there to have memorable matches with men like Vader and Ricky Steamboat in 1994 until.... STING won the International Title and the UNIFICATION BOUT was signed for a Clash of Champions card on TBS. Sherri Martel amde her WCW debut by announcing she would PICK HER MAN in WCW and she came out dressed in STING MAKE UP!! Sherri go tnailed by Sting by accident allowing Flair to knock down Sting and get the win and after the match, Sherri and Flair showed they were together all along and Flair is now the UNDISPUTED World Champion of WCW. Then, HULK HGOAN came to save Sting from the attack setting up Hogan .vs. Flair at BASH AT THE BEACH 1994 when Hogan pinned Flair 1,2,3. A few short months later at Hallowe'en Havoc 1994, Hogan beat Flair in a CAGE MATCH in which Flair said he would retire if he lost so... FLAIR LEAVES or so we think!! No less than a few months later Flair resurfaces to manage Vader against Macho Man and Hogan. Flair was allowed t owrestle again at the request of Hgoan so the big tag match would take place. Flair then reformed the Horsemen with Arn ANderson and feuded with Vader for a few short months before Flair went on another feud with Savage, again with Elizabeth in the middle of it all. Liz then turned on Savage to manage Flair and hte HORSEMEN were really born with Flair, Steve McMichael, Benoit, Woman, Liz, Debra and Anderson. Flair went for months with thsi winning combination taking the WCW Title at Starrcade 1995, his event. Flair lost the gold to the Giant Paul WIght ending his real WCW career in essence. Flair went on to win the US Title from Konnan before injuring his shoulder and leaving WCW for many many months. Flair returned to feud breifly with the NWO before being injured AGAIn and then, a contract dispute caused Ric Flair to leave WCW indefinitely. Flair returned with teary eyes to Nitro to reform the Horsemen with Benoit, Mongo McMichael and Anderson but this was short lived as Flair turned heel and became WCW President on camera, joining his son David Flair and new valet Aysia. Flair was on top of the world until his back went screwy and he left the ring AGAIN and now sits at home, trying to negotiate his release from WCW. Why does a man like RIC FLAIR leave such a legacy of love and hope?? He wins, he styles and he profiles and he does it with class! He has been screwed around by WCW management many times but always, he was on top of them and always came out with his head held up high. I expect this time to be no different. There is rumblings that Vince Mcmahon, Ric Flair's real life friend and the owner of the WWF, has been hoping Flair would return to the WWF in some capacity when his contract in WCW ends. Cna you say COMMISSIONER FLAIR or commentator Flair?? No doubt, Flair is the best on the mic in this business and the WWF could use a LEGEND like Flair to round out their roster. Flair, though, continues to wrestle to the top of WCW in an epic feud against the Immortal Hulk Hogan along side Lex Luger and Miss Elizabeth. Flair is THE MAN!! Whoooo!!!
