"AFWP-new E-mail-sim league"

16 December 1995

Hello, I would like to invite you to a new REAL wrestler fantasy league. This league is both a Roleplay(via E-mail) and a simulation league) Each match is done on a basis of points. The breakdown is below:
50 points: 5 simulated matches based on WLS 5.1(don't worry about wreslter availability, I have a database of over 200 real wrestlers) 10 points each!
30 points: Quality of Roleplay. Self explanitory. The 30 points is divisible, RP is done via E-mail to
[detail withheld] I WILL mail ALL roleplay to ALL members! 10 points: streak, OK here how that works: lets say guy A is facing guy B. Guy A has won his last 3 and Guy B lost is last match. the formula is
5+(A-B) a winning streak is a plus and a losing streak is a minus. so in the above example guy A would get 9 points and guy B would get 1 point.
It IS possible to get a minus in this catogory, but highly unlikly. 10 points: push, a push is the amount you want to "push" your wrestler. It is on a basis of 1-10. The average for all of your wrestlers push,
MUST be 7.5 or less. If it is not then I will deduct points from one of your wrestlers.
BONUS 5 points: being a defending champion. Note: Attacks do have an impact on the rp grade. Keep this in mind, make your attacks creative. BTW, I'm NOT send plans for attacks to ANYONE except the person who sent it to me!
Other notes: If you would like to join, send a list of wrestlers you would like. Send as many as you want with there pushes. You will get a max of 5. Remember this is a REAL wrestler league, not a generated one.
THe AFWP stands for the Alliance of Fantasy Wrestling Professionals. Thank you, [name withheld] [detail withheld]
