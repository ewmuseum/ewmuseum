Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The Alliance of Fantasy Wrestling Professionals announced itself on 16 December 1995 as “a new REAL wrestler fantasy league,” pairing e-mail roleplay with a simulated ruleset drawn from a database of over two hundred real wrestlers — invented characters were barred outright.

Its own scoring formula graded every match in detail: fifty points from five simulated results, thirty from the quality of a member's roleplay, ten from a win-or-loss streak run through its own plus-or-minus formula, and ten more from a “push” rating a member set for each wrestler, capped by house rule at an average of 7.5 across a whole roster so nobody could push every wrestler to the maximum at once; a defending champion earned five bonus points on top. It ran on a hosted Prodigy homepage and staged two title tournaments in its short life.

No closure statement survives. Its last surviving document, dated 15 January 1996, is still an active operational update proposing a new world-title format, with nothing beyond it to say how, or whether, that plan was ever carried out.
