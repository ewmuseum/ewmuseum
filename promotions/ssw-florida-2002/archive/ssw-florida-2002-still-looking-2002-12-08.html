SSW still looking

8 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
SSW(Sunshine State Wrestling) has just put out it's first show, but is still looking for a few more good players.
A little background: SSW is being run by two people who have been in ewrestling since 1996 we've both had characters that were World Champions in different feds(No none of the "big" RSPWF feds, but still respectable feds on the next "lower" tier I guess you could say.) We ran one other successful promotion a few years ago that had to be shut down due to lack of time, as all of you know real life happens sometimes :). Well, after being in another fed together for the last 6 months or so, myself and Craig decided it was time to start up another promotion and see where we can go with it. Thus, the SSW.
SSW is an Indy fed based in Florida. Basically we have no World Title, we have no presence on a National or International stage, all we have is a bunch of guys(we hope) trying to make it in the business. We are going to start off with just one title, the SSW title and then if we get larger we'll introduce another title, and Tag-Titles if we get enough good tag teams.
Our basic focus here is on action in the ring, and less of the "sports entertainment" side of wrestling. We are really going for a grassroots feel with this, wrestling cards will take place in gyms, local YMCA's, Military bases, and we may have a home building where we do most of our shows when not touring. People will be limited to one wrestler(2 if a tag team) and these will be all new characters, no old characters who have been in a big fed, we want fresh ideas and fresh young talent.
We will have two roleplay shows a week, with 1 roleplay allowed per character per show. We are looking more for two well thought out roleplays rather than 7 shoddy quick roleplays, thus the limitations. On show interviews(in-rings) will be limited to just champions, as this is an Indy fed we don't have enough production dollars to have everyone and their mother doing interviews and taking time away from the wrestling. That being said, even champions may not be able to do an in-ring depending on our time constraints that week, we will inform the champions of this week by week as needed. As for backstage "skits" and angles we will allow 2-3 of these per show on a first come, first serve basis. The reason for the limitation is the same as for the limitation on in-ring interviews, production money and the fact that we want to focus on the in-ring product more here and don't want it to get watered down by constant DQ endings and losses due to interference.
Again due to the Indy feel, there will be no supercards nor PPV's. If in time we get large enough and want to try to elevate the SSW to a higher level then we may start those types of cards up as needed. We will have one show per week, 2 roleplay packets per week, and a newsletter type of show
2-3 times a month(maybe more, maybe less, we haven't fully decided on this yet).
Both myself and Craig consider ourselves to be good match writers, we may not be the best you've ever read but we can both tell a good story in the ring and we both have a good grasp of psychology and a vast arsenal of move names and how they're performed floating around in our heads. Our reason for doing this is basically because we've seen what wrestling(and ewrestling) has become. With the emergence of WWE as the only real wrestling promotion on a National level, wrestling has become watered down and has started to focus less and less on "wrestling" and more and more on
"entertainment"(though Smackdown definitely is getting back to the right idea). With that happening, Efeds have followed that lead and are getting more and more outrageous with the angles and the entertainment being more important than the wrestling. Well, me and Craig are old school, and we like wrestling, so we want to bring some of that back, even if it is just in a small little ewrestling promotion :).
Now that I've typed all of that out, our web page is up and running. We are going to be an email based fed, though updates and stuff will be on our web page as quick as my tired old self can get them up ;). We have a message board that's strictly OOC, all in character stuff that needs to be said can be said in roleplays or during matches as needed. If this has piqued your interest at all, or if you feel like maybe you'd like to get back to a fed that's more down to earth give us a look at:
