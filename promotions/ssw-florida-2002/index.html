Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Two handlers who had wrestled in e-fed rings since 1996 opened a small Florida promotion in November 2002, after their previous outfit folded for lack of time. Its own cards ran from a Sarasota high school gymnasium to YMCAs in Venice and Tampa, true to a founding pitch that rejected arenas for gyms and grassroots venues.

Its first card crowned a debut winner in a rising New York high-flyer and closed with a two-man main event, no other titles or angles yet in play. Two more weekly cards built a small roster of names before an eight-man tournament began, its own first round fought out over an entire card.

The promotion's own commentary team, an aging play-by-play voice and a brash, wandering color commentator, carried nearly as much of the show's own voice as its wrestling did, needling each other between falls.

Its own most chaotic finish came in a tournament main event restarted by an on-screen SSW authority figure after a referee missed a wrestler's foot on the ropes -- the reversed decision then went the other way before the night was through.
