Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Supreme Championchip Wrestling opened on 18 June 1997 — its own spelling of “Championship” stays consistent across every self-reference it left behind, this one included — hosted on GeoCities with a tiny opening roster and an open call for more.

Its own advertising became the running story. One recruitment post offered two versions of itself side by side: “for those of you who like the used car salesman pitch,” a cowboy-voiced routine (“COWBOY BOB: COME JOIN THE SCW NOW… COWBOY BILL: BUT YOUR THE ONLY E-FED IN MERCER PA. COWBOY BOB: SHUT UP COWBOY BILL”), and beneath it “a sane regular pitch” for anyone who preferred it. The daily repetition of ads like it drew a multi-day flame war and parody thread from the rest of the newsgroup.

Roster control was enforced in practice, not just promised: within its first days a wrestler named Mr. Payne was expelled outright to keep the numbers in line.

Its first card crowned DJ its World Champion in a Triangle Match; its second retained him against David Disaster by way of a chair shot landed while the referee was down. A third card was set to pit him against the Sniper, fresh off beating Vito Rizzotto for the top contender's spot, in a match its own preview floated turning into a steel cage bout — no result of it survives.

Its own record runs out after a 4 July 1997 recruitment post describing itself as entering “our third week of existence.” Nothing more survives until a single-line post, 8 March 1998, announcing it “once again open.” Nothing follows that either.
