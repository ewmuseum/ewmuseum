I'm gonna TRY to decypher this... > First Off Who Are You To Tell Me How To Punctuate When You Are Too Lazy > To Put A Space Between Your And Cards That's what's known in layman's terms as a 'typo'. It has nothing to do with punctuation. > Second You Advert Is Not A Word > Used To In Short Describe Advertisement Maybe If You Were Not So Lazy > You Would Type Out The Entire Word 'advert' is slang used in Britain to mean adverisement. You don't flame a potential member, because then that ex-potential member (especially one with a small amount of influence) can go off and tell everyone that your fed is not worth joining. Just a bit of friendly advice. Something you should learn to listen to. > So Get > Lost You "EXTREME" Piece Of Garbage. I am not extreme, nor have I ever claimed to be extreme.
