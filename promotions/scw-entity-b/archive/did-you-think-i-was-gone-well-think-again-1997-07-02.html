DID YOU THINK I WAS GONE WELL THINK AGAIN

2 July 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I am back with a preview for the next SCW (Supreme Championchip Wrestling) Card/ The news in SCW is that David Disaster is getting ready to square off against the American Ninja in what should be a great match. Disaster has a balanced offense and a temper and the Ninja well we dont know too much aboout him his last match was not very impressive he has something to prove. Hoser has a match with Vitto Rizzotto coming up Vito was humiliated by the Sniper Vito's debeut was ruined and he wants to take it out on someone who had a successfull debeut. To add more importance to this match if Hoser wins hoser will receive a shot at the SCW title next week if Vito wins Vito will have a non-title match next week with whoever the champion may be. And our Main Event will be a match for the SCW CHAMPIONCHIP between the Sniper who won a top contenders match against Vito Rizzotto last week against the UNBEATEN SCW CHAMPION DJ. DJ is a dominating wrestler (although he screwed David Disaster last week when he nailed him with a chair while the ref was out) he has an arsenal that could end a match in seconds he is one tough dude. But his opponent is no slouch the Sniper is more than a match for DJ and if DJ lets his guard down for one second he will be locked in the BULLSEYE and we will have a new SCW CHAMPION CROWNED. Rumor has it the main event might become a Steel Cage Match. if you want to join SCW send an e - mailto: [detail withheld] and ask for a signup form. visit SCW on the web http://www.geocities.com/colosseum/loge/3622/main.html
