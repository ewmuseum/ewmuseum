Wrestling in your own fed

22 June 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The Original ( [detail withheld] ) wrote: : I think it's okay to have your own characters in your fed, at the : beginning it may be the only thing you've got... Well..following up on what everyone else says here...(in fact, I'm in the situation where I have that with myself)...only it sort of evolved into the situation... Christopher Stonebreaker was a major player in the WWO when he first came in, and I was a consistent proven RP'er at the time, and earned a title. Well, eventually our fed head was getting burnt out, and being the lead (and only other) writer at the time, I volunteered to take the position... After some time, I realized first, I wasn't able to actively roleplay and keep the federation quality up (i tried and i got some serious complaints), so I sent Stonebreaker on a "promotional tour". I dont' want him to get forgotten in the federation, so he stays as a "star" midcarder, but he will almost always lose to pushes, strats, etc, and better roleplayers. If I have him go against a weaker roleplayer, he comes out on top, but I like to think I have good judgement in that department. But as for titles? He'll probably never get another shot at one in the federation unless the co-prez of mine returns and says "Okay, Mark you did quite a bit for us, here's your reward...." But until that day happens, he stays a "star" midcarder. And if someone could beat him it's a great boost to their ratings, because he does have a great reputation in that federation as well as one or two others. But I try to keep him only semi-active. [name withheld] Co-President of hte World Wrestling Organization Handler of Christopher Stonebreaker, new NOW World Champion SCW Television Champion and who knows what other federations he might just invade! ::)
