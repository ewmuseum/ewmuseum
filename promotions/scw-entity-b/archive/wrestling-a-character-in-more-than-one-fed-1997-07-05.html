Wrestling a Character in more than one Fed!

5 July 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Mitchell \"Moxy\" J. ( [detail withheld] ) wrote: : [name withheld] wrote: : > : > Hi all E-Wrestlers, : > the theme today is the flood of multi-used characters, I think it's just : > boring to see one character to be represented in more than one Fed at a : > Time, do you think that would happen in Real-Wrestling, isn"t the target : > of Fantasy Wrestling to be cooler than ProWrestling, and in my opinion, : > a Handler should therefore only use a character in one Fed at a time, : > cause it sounds crazy to have 6 Belts in 5 different Feds at the same : > Time, or? Well....I had been thinking about this topic quite a bit lately.... I will be one of those handlers, who at first, had no idea what i was doing. So I joined as many federations as I possibly could, most of which died on me with in a couple of months. After a while, various members of those federations took notice of me before the fed's death, and invited me over to other federations. I slowly weeded out the federations that had no interest for me, while keeping myself actively roleplaying. After narrowing it down to the 4 current federations I"m listed in...(only active in 3 of those -- Belt holders in 2, and a contender in a third ::) ), I look back and think that if i hadn't spread myself out originally, I wouldn't have been able to define my character or mature him as an individual. When I invented Chris Stonebreaker, he was a hot shot rookie, who thought he could go in whereever he wanted and kick some arse for the fans, and get away with it. After I ended up jobbering in a number of feds.. (one of which I was only upset about), I paid attention to the federations that were interesting to me... (And no, not any of the title holding feds..those always seemed to close -- I wonder if it was a sign? ::) ) Stonebreaker has matured in the 10 months I have run his character (Wow!! That long???). Where he would go in and just fight any body and anything...(which is where he faced his first humiliation...after getting through Robski's stable in teh HWL....he got his his butt kicked by the man himself.), I have matured him enough to know exactly what the character of Stonebreaker is, and I could literally pick any path to follow now, without any question of is this where i want him to go? So was I wrong for goign in multiple federations? Nope..I learned (sometimes the hard way) what to look for in good and bad federations. It played well into my character, and suited Stonebreaker just right. Would it work for any of my other characters I want to create? NO telling...probably not, but if that's what it took to generate him where i like him, then I'd probably do it again.
