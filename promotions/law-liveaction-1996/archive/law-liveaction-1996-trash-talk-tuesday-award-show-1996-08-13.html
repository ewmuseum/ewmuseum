Janus: Greetings LAW, and welcome to the first showing of the Professors new interviewing technique. It will be appearing regularly in my normal interview spot, but will be debuted here tonight at the LAW Awards Show. The title of this segment is
'Under the Spotlight with the Professor.' Here in the first edition of it is the LAW wrestler James Havoc.
[a polite applause can heard coming from the audience.] Janus: Mr Havoc, who is your favourite real life wrestler and Why? Havoc: Hmmm, good question. There are a few competitors that I admire... Taz is a great technician, and Sabu is an awesome aerialist...but I would have to say that I admire Tommy Dreamer.
The guy can whoop a little ass, and if he started to WRESTLE like he used to do, he would be an incredible force in the wrestling world.
Janus: Your favourite sports personality and Why? Havoc: Well, other than this sport, I don't follow much else... I'd say Mikey Whipwreck. He has a lot of heart and is very down to earth with the fans... Janus: If you were on a 10 hour plane trip. Who would you like to sit next to and Why? [Havoc pauses and thinks for a few moments before answering] Havoc: Asia Carrera, the pornographic film star. She's cute, she seems intelligent, and I never met a porn star before. [Havoc smirks]
Janus: If you were down to your last $10 how would you spend it and Why? Havoc: Lunch at BK, because I like to eat. Janus: The wrestler you admire the most in the UEA and Why? Havoc: Wrestler I admire most? Honestly, I don't admire no one, however I do have respect for guys like Sub-Zero and Fast
Fighter... little guys with a lot of heart. Janus: The wrestler you dislike the most in the UEA and Why? Havoc: Whoa, pal, that would have to be a toss up between the
"pals" Peterbilt and NBK... I have NO respect for these guys, and they make me feel ill whenever I see them.
Janus: Describe yourself in three words. Havoc: Big, ruthless, fun Janus: And finally, what can you see yourself doing after you have finished your wrestling career?
Havoc: I see myself retiring and going into a career of writing. You know, I may be big and all, but I've got a knack for tellin' stories too!
