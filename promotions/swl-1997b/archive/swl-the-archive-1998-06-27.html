************************ Wrestler's Bio *************************** Name::Gravedigger Weight: 256 Height: 6' 6" Hometown::Miami,Florida Finisher:Lights Out Finisher Description:Death Valley Driver Managed by: Miss Demeanor -------------------------------------------------------------------------- ------------------------------------------ *********************** Interview w/Gravedigger************************* David Campbell: So Grave Digger can you tell us your feelings about Mass Destruction turning on you during the Pay Per View and joining the kAo? Have you ever spoke to him since? If you did what did he say? Gravedigger:Well,as far as I am concerned,Mass Destruction belongs in the kAo because he is just as spineless as the rest of those ingrates!! I have spoken to him once and he said he wanted to join the kAo,but he didn't want to attack me,but we all know that it is a bunch os BS.he attacked me because he is mad because I am the only one who has beaten him! DC: Grave Digger besides Mass Destruction who in the SWL do you want to get your hands on the most? Why? GD: Right now I am not worried with anyone in particular because I am just looking to dismantle the kAo. DC: In the next Pay Per View who would you like to see fight and who would you like to fight yourself? GD: I want to see Gravedigger against Mass Destruction in a Steel Cage Casket Match for the SWL title. DC: How long do you think you be able to hold on to the U.S. T.V. title? GD: I can hold the belt as long as I want,I am unbeatable. DC: If you do lose it who do you think you will lose it to? GD: ABSOLUTELY NO ONE!! DC: Who in the SWL do you look up to the most? Why? GD: I really don't look up to anyone,but I only "respect" Hardcore,Jim Hart,and Ryan Shaffer because we have traveled the same roads and been through the same things. DC: Ever since the Time Bombs lost Mass Destruction to the kAo do you think the time Bombs could compete with the other stables? Could they when he was in? GD: I think the Time Bombs can compete if they got some talent in their stable. The Time Bombs could really never compete with other stables because as I said earlier,they lack the big name talent!! DC: Gravedigger,we are out of time,thanks for the interview. GD: The pleasure was all yours!! -------------------------------------------------------------------------- -------------------------------------------------------------------------- ------ **********************************Past Results****************************** Warzone Hyper Man defeated Asics by D/Q Hardcore defeated Rob Bansin by D/Q Prime Time defeated Harry Hender by pinfall Anthony Mislan deafeated Kraze by D/Q Basic Killer and The Remedy defeated Gravedigger and Mass Destruction by pinfall Monday Madness Gravedigger defeated Jerry Mack by pinfall The Hater defeated Brad Winfield by pinfall The Titan defeated Jason Jones by pinfall Wednesday Mayhem The Stallion defeated The Women Lover by D/Q Slick Styles defeated Brandon Robbins by pinfall Harry Hender defeated Sam Oliver Barnes by D/Q Anthony Mislan defeated Prime Time by D/Q Friday Night Raw The Hater defeated Mad Man Matt by pinfall Gene Hoffman defeated Hyper Man by pinfall to win the lightweight title!!
