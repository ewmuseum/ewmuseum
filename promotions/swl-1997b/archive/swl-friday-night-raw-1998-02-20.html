SWL Friday Night Raw

20 February 1998

************************************************************************** ******************** Friday Night Raw February 20,1998 Live From San Diego,California ************************************************************************** ******************** -=(Fireworks go off)=- -=(Camera scans around the arena and gets a great shot of the sold out crowd)=- -=(Camera scans to Jim Ross and Jack Fancy who are at the announcers table)=- Jack=The hottest show on TV.We are live and we have 3 fulls hours of great action.Boy do we have a huge night in store.So much to talk about.Especially the return of Mr. Justice!
-=(Some clips of Mr. Justice are shown)=- -=(Camera scans backstage into the parking lot.Mr. Justice just got out of his car and Sunny is there also)=- Sunny=Mr. Justice.Can we get some words on why you have returned to the SWL....Mr. Justice?Apparently he doesn't want to talk guys.Back to you. -=(Camera scans back to the announcers table)=- Jim=On to other SWL news.The SWL has invited anyone from any federation to be in the crowd for the SWL February Pay Per View.This PPV was suppose to feature the "Greatest Man in SWL History" match.But Sniper refused to face American
Heartthrob.So the SWL Officials have came up with a new idea.Not much is known about except that it is the 1st match of it's kind.
Jack=Well Jim.I am just as curious as you are.I have no idea what kind of match this will be but I cant wait till next Saturday.We will be right back!
-=(Commercial advertising the new kAo T-Shirt)=- -=(kAo's music starts as the entire kAo walks out)=- -=(Crowd goes wild)=- Jim=Who gave these guys air time.They are not scheduled to fight. -=(Basic Killer grabs a mike)=- BasicKiller=Well well well.Havent seen the SWL for a while.I guess you can say we have been busy with other "operations".Since I have been back I have had a few talks with the SWL President.But before I get into that I would really like to discuss the things leading up to this talk we had.See,it all started a few mopnths ago.When I ran Mr. Excellent out of the SWL.I was supposed to face him at a Friday Night Raw.Well,he was fired,or shall I say he got himself fired.So,that little chicken is off in the SWL underleague.Never to be seen again.
-=(Crowd chants 'kAo..kAo..")=- BasicKiller=Well it seems the Prez was been with how the kAo was acting.So I threatened to leave.You see the SWL can not live with out the kAo.We are they're oxygen.They can not breathe with out us.So,it came down that I had to give them my walking papers.But then I recieved a phone call a few weeks ago.The president wanted to make me an offer.That offer was to be in the main event at the February Pay Per View.Under 1 condition.That the kAo would return to the SWL.And my answer was yes!
-=(Crowd goes wild)=- -=(The kAo's music starts as they walk backstage)=- Jack=A huge announcement just made by Basic Killer.We now know more about the
February Main Event.Folks,we will be right back!
-=(Commercial advertising the February Pay Per View)=- -=(Mr. Mainevent's music starts as he walks out with Jenny Anderson)=- -=(Prodigy's music starts as he walks out with the SWL World Lightweight Title)=- DING DING DING!!! Jim=The opening bout for the SWL World Lightweight Title is underway.Both men lock up.Mainevent twists the arm of Prodigy.He drops an elbow on it.Down goes
Prodigy but not for long as he kicks to his feet.Both men are at the top of the
Lightweight scene not only in the SWL.But in the world.
Jack=Your absolutly right Jim.Both these men lock up again.Mainevent slaps on a headlock.Mainevent gots that one locked on tight.Oh no.Wait.Prodigy picked him up and dropped him on his back.Great move as he breaks free.Both men up at the same time but Prodigy with a dropkick.Mr. Mainevent roles out of the ring.Watch out.Prodigy off the ropes and he comes off with a big corscrew plancha.Both men down on the arena floor!
-=(Crowd goes wild)=- Jim=Prodigy the first one to get to his feet.He is going in the ring and to the top turnbuckle.He is really pumping up the crowd.
-=(Crowd screaming at the top of their lungs)=- Jack=Mr. Mainevent just getting to his feet.Mainevent rolls in the ring and
Prodigy gets off the turnbuckles.Prodigy walks right over to Mainevent.Nice high angle body slam.This leads us to a commercial break.We will be right back.
-=(Commercial advertising Monday Madness)=- Jack=We are back.Mr. Mainevent just turned the match around with a DDT.Both men are down.The ref count has reached 4.Mainevent the first man to his feet and he can barely stand.
Jim=Mainevent walking over to Prodigy and grabs his hair.Wait.Prodigy just reversed it into a small cradle..1...2...3...Prodigy has pulled it off!
-=(Crowd goes wild)=- -=(Replay shows how Prodigy won)=- -=(Prodigy grabs his back and celebrates as he walks backstage)=- Jim=Folks.This has been a great start in what should be a huge night in SWL
History.We still have a great show in store.But first.Lets recap.Mr. Justice pulled up to the arena at about 2 minutes after we went on air.He didn't say nothing to Sunny when she tried to interview him.
Jack=Also,Basic Killer and the kAo came out and pumped up the crowd.Also,Basic
Killer announced he was going to compete in the main event at the February Pay
Per View.
Jim=And you just saw Prodigy defeat Mr. Mainevent as he retains his belt.Folks,we will be right back!
-=(Commercial advertising Wednesday Mayhem)=- Jim=We are back.Our next match was suppose to have Gangsta facing The Beast but
The Beast has left the SWL so Gangsta will take home a win VIA DQ.Lets go to ringside for our next match.
-=(The Reaper's music starts as he walks out)=- -=(Kold Krush's music starts as he walks out)=- DING DING DING!!! Jim=Here we go.The Reaper approaches Kold Krush but walks right into a huge boot.Kold Krush now drops a huge forearm and The Reaper is sent down to one knee.Kold Krush with another forearm.Wow.Look at the power.
Jack=The Reaper fires back with a shot to the kidneys.The Reaper back up and he makes a huge tackle.Kold Krush is down.Nice comeback.
Jim=The Reaper walking over to Kold Krush and brings him to his feet.Kold Krush sent to the ropes.Kold Krush comes off into a boot to the midsection.There it is.The Reaper Stunner...1...2...3...
-=(Crowd cheers)=- Jack=The Reaper with a win but he was not competing at 100%.This man has alot of talent but he dissapointed me with that match.
Jim=Your absolutly right.Kold Krush and The Reaper can make a huge impact on the SWL but that match looked a little sloppy.We will be right back.
-=(Commercial advertising the SWL Homepage)=- -=(Simon Prestwell's music starts as he walks out)=- -=(Mysterious' music starts as he walks out)=- DING DING DING!!! Jim=-Mysterious was 1 and 0 when he competed in the EWA.He defeated Gravedigger then handed in his walking papers.He comes out blazing in this match as he throws left and rights.Oh my.Prestwell fired back with a huge blow and
Mysterious went done in a hurry.Prestwell now approaches Mysterious but gets a bout to his face for his troubles.
Jack=Mysterious kicks to his feet.Prestwell just getting back up.Beautiful spinning heel kick by the 6 foot 210 pound Mysterious.Mysterious now placing
Prestwell against the turnbuckle.He gets to the second rope and delivers the punches..1...2...3...4...5...6...Oh no.Prestwell grabbed Mysterious.Here comes the powerbomb.No.Turned it into a hurracaranna..1...2...Kickout by Prestwell.
-=(Crowd in shock)=- Jim=That almost cost Mysterious the match. Jack=Mysterious the first to his feet.Prestwell now back.Prestwell whipped to the ropes.He come off into a sidewalk slam.How did he pick up Prestwell.He outeighs him by 45 pounds.Amazing power by the little guy.
Jim=Well it was a sloppy move but none the less it worked.We will be right back! -=(Commercial advertising Monday Madness)=- Jack=We are back and Mysterious still dominating the match.He has Prestwell dazed in the ring.He is calling for it folks.He is up top.Top Rope
Killer..1...2...3...He got it. -=(Crowd in shock)=- Jim=What a move by the man known as Mysterious.Wow.Prestwell was just dominated.Huge move and it put away Prestwell.
-=(Replay shows how Mysterious won)=- Jim=We will be right back with our main event! -=(Commercial advertising the February Pay Per View)=- -=(Camera scans to Preston Walsh who is in the ring)=- Preston=San Diego.It is time for the main event! -=(Crowd goes wild)=- Preston=Coming out first..Standing 6 feet 11 inches..Weighing 305 pounds..Being brought to the ring by The Kid..The master of the Splash Mountain..He is..Biggy!
-=(Biggy's music starts as he walks out with The Kid)=- Preston=And now..Standing 5 feet 8 inches..Weighing 214 pounds..He has a devestating powerbomb..He is..Dead Alien!
-=(Dead Alien's music starts as he walks out)=- Preston=And last but not least..Standing 7 feet 1 inch..Weighing 305 pounds..He finishes off his opponents with his massive chokeslam..Being brought to the ring by Demona..He is..Dark Knight!
-=(Dark Knight's music starts as he walks out with Demona)=- DING DING DING!!! Jim=Dead Alien in a bad spot folks.He is near the ropes.Only one way to be eliminated and that is to be thrown over the top.And watch out.Dark Knight charged Dead Alien.Dead Alien was hit with a huge lariat.He is eliminated.
-=(Crowd cheers)=- Jack=Dark Knight better turn around.He does and gets a drop kick for his troubles.He has been eliminated.Biggy has already pulled it off!
-=(Crowd cheers)=- -=(Light go off)=- -=(Crowd goes wild)=- Jim=What is up with this? -=(Fireworks go off)=- -=(Crowd goes wild)=- -=(Mr. Justice walks out)=- Jim=Here he comes.He is walking to ringside.He has got in the ring.Oh no.He just attacked Biggy.Dead Alien got in the ring and got a whooping.Who's next?Mr. Justice is destroying everyone.
-=(Basic Killer runs out to ringside)=- -=(Crowd going wild)=- Jim=We now have a brawl between two top stars.They are going at it.Oh my.Everyone is on their feet.This place is going wild!
-=(Lights dim again)=- Jim=Who can this be? -=(A fireworks lights up the arena)=- -=(Lights go back on)=- -=(Chaos Bill runs to ringside)=- Jim=Chaos Bill is back.Oh what a night.This place is going wilf folks!Everyone is on their feet.Holy moly.Chaos Bill,Basic Killer,and Mr. Justice are going at it.Folks,we are out of time.See you Monday!
