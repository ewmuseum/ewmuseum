SWL Supremer Awards

20 June 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
-=(Supremer Awards)=- -=(June 20,1998)=- -=(Live From San Antonio, Texas)=- -=(The Supreme Wrestling League theme starts)=- -=(Camera scans around room. Such wrestlers as Hardcore, Kraze, Mass Destruction, and many others are dressed up for the event)=- -=(Jack Fancy and Anita Somers walk out)=- Jack Fancy= Welcome to the Supremer Awards. Every December and June the Supreme Wrestling League holds this special event to congratulate the top wrestlers of the past six months. This, is the third Supremer Awards. And the show gets better each and every time. Anita Somers= Tonight The Supreme Wrestling League puts the fueds and hatred behind them, and they congratulate each other for a job well done. To present our first award, we send it over to Supreme Wrestling League Supremer Strap Champion, Kraze. -=(Camera scans to Kraze)=- -=(Camera scans to Hardcore who throws Kraze a look)=- Kraze= Better wipe that look of your face Hardcore before I come down there and knock it off. -=(Hardcore stands up)=- -=(SWL Officials quickly come out and have Hardcore sit down)=- Kraze= Moving on. Our first award goes to the person that risks his or her life by walking to the danger zone with the wrestler. This person risks life and limb just to get a win. This person is known as the manager. The Lovely Jen has been dubbed the manager of the Supreme Wrestling League. But today the Supreme Wrestling League will crown a new Manager of the Year. The Lovely Jen won this award in June of 1997 and December of 1997. The nominee's are... -=(Scary Mary for Harry Hender)=- -=(Andy Sowle for Andy Carter)=- -=(Luscious Lisa for Anthony Mislan)=- -=(Jenny Anderson for Mr. Mainevent)=- Kraze= And the winner.... With eleven of the nineteen votes... Jenny Anderson! -=(Crowd cheers)=- -=(Jenny Anderson and Mr. Mainevent walk up and are presented the Supremer Award)=- Jenny Anderson= Thank you. As most of you know I manage Mr. Mainevent and Mr. Pay Per View and Mr. Headliner. All three have held Supreme Wrestling Titles on more then one occassion. Its an honor to win this award. I thank you all. -=(Crowd cheers)=- Announcer= When we get back, Supreme Wrestling League Lightweight Champion, Hyper Man. Will present Newcomer of the Year. -=(Commercial advertising Warzone)=- -=(Camera scans to Hyper Man)=- Hyper Man= The Supreme Wrestling League gave me the honor to present Newcomer of the Year. Alot of people come and go from the Supreme Wrestling League every year. And it is especially hard for the Newcomers coming in and knowing what to and what not to expect. Past winners include Sniper from June of 1997 and The Executioner from December of 1997. The nominee's are... -=(Ecstasy)=- -=(Prime Time)=- -=(Gene Hoffman)=- -=(Wonderful Welbe)=- Hyper Man= And the winner... With eight of the nineteen votes... Ecstasy! -=(Crowd cheers)=- -=(Ecstasy walks up and is presented the Supremer Award)=- Ecstasy= Even though I am no longer with the Supreme Wrestling League I would like to thank everyone of you. Especially everyone who voted for me. This award has a special place in my collection. Thank you. -=(Crowd cheers)=- Announcer= When we get back, Jim Ross will present Finisher of the Year. -=(Commercial advertising the Supreme Wrestling League t-shirt)=- -=(Camera scans to Jim Ross)=- Jim Ross= Tonight, members of the Supreme Wrestling League have put away their boots and tights and have slapped on the suits and ties. Tonight is a very special occasion for many. Tonight could be a turning point for someones career. I have the honor of presenting Finisher of the Year. American Hearttrob took home the award in June of 1997 with his Heartbreaker Heartpunch and Hardcore Harley Robinson won in December of 1997 with his Moonsault. The nominee's are... -=(The Executioner's Be-Heading DDT)=- -=(Anthony Mislan's Ego-Cutter)=- -=(Hardcore's Straight Jacket)=- -=(Cassaday's Equalizer Tombstone)=- Jim Ross= And the winner... With eight of the nineteen votes... The Straight Jacket by Hardcore! -=(Crowd cheers)=- -=(Hardcore walks up and is presented the Supremer Award)=- Hardcore= Hardcore one, Kraze zero. -=(Crowd cheers)=- Announcer= When we get back, Mass Destruction will present Announcer of the Year. -=(Commercial advertising the Supreme Wrestling League Voting Booth)=- -=(Camera scans to Mass Destruction)=- Mass Destruction= Announcers give life to Supreme Wrestling League shows. They give a whole new look on whats going on. Thats why the Supreme Wrestling League congratulates the best of the best with this award. Jerry Lawler won this award in December of 1997. The nominee's are... -=(Jim Ross)=- -=(Jack Fancy)=- -=(Jerry Lawler)=- -=(Anita Somers)=- Mass Destruction= And the winner... With nine of the nineteen votes... Jerry Lawler! -=(Crowd cheers)=- -=(Jerry Lawler walks up and is presented the Supremer Award)=- Jerry Lawler= I thank none of you. I got this award on my own. I deserve a raise. Ha ha. -=(Crowd cheers)=- Announcer= When we get back, Anthony Mislan will present Match of the Year. -=(Commercial advertising the Supreme Wrestling League webpage)=- -=(Camera scans to Anthony Mislan)=- Anthony Mislan= Each week the Supreme Wrestling League dishes out some of the best wrestling matches around. Some are worth mentioning, and some are not. Yet every once in a while one match goes down that no one will forget. The June 1997 winner of Match of the Year was Rodman versus Dynamite Dana while the December 1997 winner was American Heartthrob versus Sniper. The nominee's are... -=(Cassaday versus Sniper)=- -=(King Snake versus The Violater)=- -=(Kraze versus Insanity,second match)=- -=(The Bat versus Basic Killer,first match)=- Anthony Mislan= And the winner... with twelve on the nineteen votes... Insanity, now known as Hardcore versus Kraze! -=(Crowd cheers)=- -=(Both men walk up and are presented one Supremer Award each)=- Hardcore= Hardcore two, Kraze one. Kraze= The show aint over yet. -=(Crowd cheers)=- Announcer= When we get back, Hank Jackson will present Tag Team of the Year. -=(Commercial advertising Monday Madness)=- -=(Camera scans to Hank Jackson)=- Hank Jackson= There are many great Tag Teams in wrestling today. Tag Teams provide twice the action then what some singles matches provide. The Tag team division is on the rebound in the Supreme Wrestling League. This making this years winner, the main target. Soultaker and Deathmaster, also known as The Dead Guys, won in June of 1997 while the Basic Killer and Mean Man won in December of 1997. The nominee's are... -=(Basic Killer and Mean Man)=- -=(Soultaker and Deathmaster)=- -=(Canadian Connection)=- -=(Mr. Pay Per View and Mr. Headliner)=- Hank Jackson= And the winners... With eight of the nineteen votes... The kick Ass order! -=(Crowd cheers)=- -=(Basic Killer and Mean Man walk up and our presented the Supremer Awards)=- Basic Killer= If you thought we were good then.... Mean Man= Wait till you see us tomorrow. The kick Ass order is OOUUTT!! -=(Crowd cheers)=- Announcer= When we get back, Supreme Wrestling League United States Television Champion Gravedigger will present the Lightweight of the Year. -=(Commercial advertising Warzone)=- -=(Camera scans to Gravedigger)=- Gravedigger= The Supreme Wrestling League has one of the top Lightweight Divisions in the world. The division started a day after the second Supremer Awards on December 14 at the In Your Face Pay Per View. From there on the top Lightweights in the world have blessed the Supreme Wrestling League ring. The nominee's are... -=(Prodigy)=- -=(Andy Carter)=- -=(Mr. Excellent)=- -=(Mr. Mainevent)=- -=(The Executioner)=- Gravedigger= And the winner... With seven of the nineteen votes... The Executioner! -=(Crowd cheers)=- -=(The Executioner walks up and is presented the Supremer Awards)=- The Executioner= I would like to thank everyone who voted for me. I would also like to congratulate all of the other Lightweights for putting up such a challenge. I look forward to beating you all again in the ring. -=(Crowd cheers)=- Announcer= When we get back Preston Walsh will present Stable of the Year. -=(Commercial advertising Warzone)=- -=(Camera scans to Preston Walsh)=- Preston Walsh= San Antonio, Texas. It is time for the..... Just kidding. Stables are a big part of the Supreme Wrestling League. With Kraze changing his stable name every week it is hard for the fans at home to keep up. -=(Crowd laughs)=- Preston Walsh= The nominee's for Stable of the Year are... -=(Timb Bombs)=- -=(Loose Cannons)=- -=(Hostile Takeover,now known as The R.I.O.T. Squad)=- -=(S.W.O, now known as The Pain Killers)=- Preston Walsh= And the winner... with nine of the nineteen votes... The Pain Killers! -=(Kraze walks up and is presented a Supremer Award)=- Kraze= Kraze two, Hardcore two. -=(Crowd cheers)=- Announcer= The moment everyone has been waiting for. Tony Arnold presents Wrestler of the year, when we get back. -=(Commercial advertising the Supreme Wrestling League webpage)=- -=(Camera scans to Tony Arnold)=- Tony Arnold= This is without a doubt the biggest award of the night. Every wrestler would love to call themselves the Wrestler of the Year. Yet in a few moments, only one person will be able to do that. Sniper took home the award in June of 1997 while Mr. Excellent and American heartthrob both took home the award in December of 1997. The nominee's are... -=(Kraze)=- -=(Hardcore)=- -=(Cassaday)=- -=(Anthony Mislan)=- Tony Arnold= And the winner... Hold on... We have another tie... -=(Crowd cheers)=- Tony Arnold= With 6 votes each out of the nineteen... The winners... Kraze and Hardcore! -=(Crowd stands and and cheers)=- -=(Kraze and Hardcore walk up, both are presented a Supremer Award)=- Kraze= Kraze three... Hardcore= Hardcore three... -=(Hardcore and Kraze go afce to face)=- -=(Wrestlers come up on stage and break things up)=- Jack Fancy= Folks, mayhem breaking out. We will see you tomorrow for Warzone. Goodnight!
