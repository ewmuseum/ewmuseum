**** 4 Star Show ============================================== Biography of Anthony Mislan ============================================== Real Name: Anthony Mislan Age: 16 Hometown: Linden, NJ Wrestler's Name: "Mr. eXtreme" Anthony Mislan Height: 6'5" Weight: 254lbs Finisher Name: Apocolypse Drop Finisher Description: Death Valley Driver ============================================== Interview with Anthony Mislan ============================================== Bill Jerry: Welcome Anthony Mislan...It is a pleasure to have you hear... Anthony: Its a pleasure to be hear. BJ: Well Anthony...I was wondering...along with the entire wrestling business...Why did you turn your back on Elite X and join the Pain Killerz?
Anthony: Well technically, I didn't turn on Elite X, when I was with GraveDigger, we are the Natural Born Killaz. That is tradition right there, that is why I went back. But GraveDigger began to become jealous of me, and he started to make bad decisions, so I left. I joined the PaiN KiLLeRz because I needed backing and they could give it to me. Now that I look back at it, I am glad I left Elite X.
BJ: Okay...Well what are your thoughts on loosing the belt to Mass Destruction? Anthony: Well, I am still really alittle pissed off about that. Because of the circumstances that happened during the match and after. I know and everyone else knows that I should have that World Title around my waist.
BJ: What is your opinion about the stable wars that are happening here in the SWL? Anthony: I think it is great, because it adds alot of excitement to the SWL shows and PPVs. BEcause you never know what is going to happen. And with people turning on one stable and joining another, it is really awsome.
BJ: Well what belt do you wish to go after now that you have none? Anthony: I am setting my sites on the US TV Title. And eventually I want to retain my World Title. Right now I just want to get some revenge from Mass
Destruction.
BJ: What do you think about the president of the SWL being on the side of the stable that you hate in the kAo?
Anthony: Quite frankly, I think it sucks. Because how can the president of the
SWL join a stable, especially the kAo, and expect us, the wrestlers of the SWL to believe that he will be an unbiased president. I think that he is going to be biased towards people in the kAo itself.
BJ: I wanna thank you for your time! Anthony: Have a nice day. ============================================== Behind the Scenes with Jason Oliver ============================================== [ The camera zooms into a shot of Jason Oliver backstage of Monday Madness...he is wearing a black suit with a white tie...he has a SWL microphone in his hand and starts to talk ]

Sir: Why yes...It is pretty simple...this device right here is called a Trank 2000...it is connected to these wires which in turn are connected to the bases that you see on the ramps out front...when the lever gets pushed down...it sends an electric current to the bases which are then ignited and you see the fireworks that you do...
