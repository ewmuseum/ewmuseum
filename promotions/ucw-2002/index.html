Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Universal Championship Wrestling ran from its own bare-root site on fwrestling.com in 2002, crowning its first Total Champion, Klaus, on 23 March. Its title history page records five championships in all — the Total, South Pacific, North Atlantic and Tag Team titles, and a King Of HaRdCoRe crown — though only three of them show a reign on the page that survives.

Klaus lost the Total Championship to Darryn Lee a month later at Last Rights, in a match billed Luck Of The Draw. The South Pacific title changed hands twice in the same stretch, Bryan Bryn Bryson beating Max Pain for it at Blackout before losing it to Damien Black at Havoc, the same card where Exile won an eponymous tournament to become the promotion's first King Of HaRdCoRe.

Two roster cards survive from the promotion's own roster page. Klaus von Knorre, out of Ramstein, Germany, walked to the ring smoking a cigar and carrying a broom wrapped in barbed wire with four fluorescent light tubes duct-taped to the handle, finishing opponents with a Michinoku Driver he called the Exterminator or a guillotine leg drop off the top rope with a stop sign, the Teutonic Facebuster. A second wrestler, billed simply as Shawn out of Canton, Georgia, entered to Tupac's “Hit 'Em Up” under green pyro, finishing with a Verte-Breaker he called the Sensational Finale or a five-star frog splash, the Sensational Impact.

No card, result, or further document survives beyond these three pages; how Universal Championship Wrestling's title picture continued after May 2002, or how the promotion itself ended, isn't in the surviving record.
