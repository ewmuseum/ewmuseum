UCW Roster Card: Klaus Von Knorre

captured 24 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Roster Age: 30 Height: 6'2 Weight: 228 pounds Style: All-Rounder Hometown: Ramstein, Germany Finisher #1: Exterminator Finisher #1 Description: Michinoku Driver Finisher #2 : Teutonic Facebuster Finisher #2 Description: Guillotine legdrop off the top turnbuckle with a stop sign. Theme: "Born of a Broken Man" by Rage Against the Machine Entrance: The lights go out, and the arena is thrown into total inky blackness. A large fire begins to burn on the entrance ramp, and "Born of a Broken Man" by Rage Against the Machine blares. Red pyros shoot up towards the ceiling, the curtain parts, and Klaus walks out. In his hand, he holds a broom with four florescent light tubes duct taped to the handle, and the whole thing wrapped up in barbed wire. Klaus stops on the entranceway, puffing from a cigar and looking around at the crowd. More pyros shoot off, and Klaus flicks the lit cigar butt at a fan, who flips him off and boos. Klaus makes his way to the ring, climbs up onto the ring apron, and through the ropes.
Appearance: Klaus is lean, yet muscular. He has short, black hair and a little goatee. His ring attire consists of  jean shorts, a black sleeveless Rammstein tee-shirt, heavy black leather boots, black motorcycle gloves, black leather wristbands with metal rivets. Black elbow and knee pads. Knee brace on right knee. Silver-framed sunglasses as entrance attire.
Favourite Moves: Top hope spinning heel kick Snap German suplex Tiger Bomb DDT Brainbuster Irish whip into a powerslam T-bone suplex Overhead belly-to-belly suplex Powerbomb Standing Dropkick Praying Rope Walk (an homage to one of his idols, Jinsei Shinzaki) Diving Moonsault Asai Moonsault (from ring apron to the arena floor) Ankle-grab body splash History: Klaus Von Knorre was born in Ramstein, Germany on September 29th, 1971. The son of wealthy German industrialists, Klaus lived a life of luxury until the night of December 12th, 1977. On that cold, windy night, Klaus' parents were headed home from an early Christmas party. The roads were slick with ice that night, and Herr Von Knorre tried to swerve to avoid hitting the other car, but it was too late.
After the funeral, 6-year-old Klaus was shipped off to the United States to live with his aunt, who resided in Orlando, Florida.
Growing up in a strange country with nobody around who loved him, shunned by his peers and ignored by his aunt, Klaus grew into a bitter, hateful young man. He began to hate everyone, and ostracized himself from the rest of society, which he deemed inferior to himself.
On his 18th birthday, Klaus recieved his inheritance of his parents' wealth, which was quite a substantial amount of money. With his newfound wealth, Klaus decided to make the lives of others as miserable as his own, since that was the only thing that brought him any real happiness or pleasure. He traveled to Tampa, FLorida, and enrolled in one of the many professional wrestling schools that are located there. A favorite of the instructors at the school because of his athletic talent and no-nonsense approach, Klaus graduated as the most promising student.
It wasn't long before Klaus caught the eye of Jeremy Chaplin, who ran a promotion called CWA. Impressed by Klaus' potential,  Chaplin inked the young man to a deal with the company. Klaus completed a very successful rookie year, which culminated when he won the CWA Hardcore Title. As a matter of fact, Klaus was still the CWA Hardcore Champion when that company went bankrupt and closed its doors forever.
Klaus almost immediately signed a contract with the prestegious GCW, and continued his path of destruction, including a reign as GCW Hardcore Champion. But GCW never really gave Klaus a fair opportunity to shine, and so the young star wasn't too upset when he was locked out and fired with the rest of the GCW roster several months ago.
Now that Klaus has signed with the upstart UCW, he hopes that he will finally be given the opportunity to shine. Now is his time. Now it is his turn
