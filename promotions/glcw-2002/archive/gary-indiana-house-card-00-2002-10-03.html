Mister Dread — 4 October 2002 ::FADEIN on a 50's style railroad car diner. THE WOLF occupies a booth in the back, slouching in his seat. A cup of coffee steams at his left, and a cigarette smolders in an ashtray at his right::
THE WOLF: So, let me get this straight. I'm wrestling the Jobber ... but he's not a jobber? His entire gimmick is based on guys who get pinned for a paycheck and yet he's a serious competitor, huh? Well, to each his own, I guess.
::THE WOLF sips from his coffee cup, wincing slightly at the hot liquid:: THE WOLF: Well, Jobber, what can I say? This is hard for me, because the usual formula doesn't apply. I can't really rant about all the wrongs you've done me, because there hasn't been any. I can't go on about our epic feud, because there isn't one. Normally I would talk about how I'm going to hurt you, maim you when we step into the ring. But why? What reason do I have? I'm not going to hurt you because I hate you, Jobber. I'm going to hurt you because I can. There's no buildup for this match, no real reason for it. But it's going to happen anyway. There's also no real reason for me to maul you in the ring, but it's going to happen anyway.
::THE WOLF drags deep from his cigarette before stubbing it out forcefully in the ashtray. Exhaling twin plumes of smoke from his nose, he continues::
THE WOLF: I'm not really an athelete, Jobber. I'm not a former NCAA champion, or a professional weightlifter, or an ex football player or anything like that. The only thing that keeps me in this is that I get a chance to prove myself. Over and over and over again I'm thrown in the ring with men that are two or three times my size. And not only do I win, but I dominate. Because I can.

(FTB) Manson — 6 October 2002 ((FADEIN: Michael Manson in black leather jacket and pants watching classic, old school Saved by the Bell on a monitor. His face never leaves the screen as he talks.))
MANSON: So inadequate and mediocre. I am about to win the GLCW title and yet, they give me a match with Jarod Poe again, who will end up with a title shot by default. Or I'm sure Krusher will make up some excuse for it. Poe already failed, already lost. Why is he rewarded for being a failure?
Attempted murderers aren't put to death. There is no margin for failure here, no moral victories. Poe was outfought and outsmarted. He doesn't deserve anything close to a title, let alone the chance to wrestle his obvious idol. Even though he can't seem to get that right. I wonder if he's figured out by now that no one cares what happened to his girlfriend or mother or whatever. Let me give give you some advice about women, leave their money on the nightstand and they can let themselves out.
Just another in an endless line of gothic characters which the GLCW seemed infested with. So I'm going to have to put up with of this self tormented and supernatural talk for a while now. First its Poe, probably the only man alive stupid enough to believe I would go to a church for anything other than stealing the wine, and Golem who seemingly vanished on the way to the studio. There was a probably a mountain in his way and he had to get out of his car and move it.
As for billing this a grudge match, I can imagine him having a grudge against me. Then again, give it a few weeks and the whole promotion will for one reason or another. It doesn't make him special, significant, or even interesting. Despite his rantings that he has a plan for me, I remain indifferent. I don't care if Poe lives, dies, or marries and has many obese children. Just another faceless victim, not unlike a postal worker. Maybe thats why they all go mad.
