TBirdSCIL — 2 August 2003 All members, I just realized that Great Lakes Championship Wrestling has passed its first anniversary! Congratulations to all for making this such a great league and thank you all for sticking with it and RPing your asses off every time out. Here's to another year!
President [name withheld] Mister Dread — 2 August 2003 That's right! Wow, a whole year already. ::raises a beer in toast:: Right, here's to another year!
JLevinson — 3 August 2003 Wow... has it been that long already? Congrats Scott, you've got a great league with a ton of great RPers and talent. Here's to many more.
-Josh scwhat82 — 3 August 2003 Congrats to everyone in this fed for making it what it is, and seeing that we've seen quite a few other feds closing during the past year, it's good to see this has survived.
Here's to many more years and on a personal note, i want to thank scott and any other rp'ers who have helped me raise my skill level, which i'll admit has to raise a lot more. coming into this fed i was definitely the rookie and although i still am i've learned a lot so thanks to everyone.
Showtime Steven James JLebron — 3 August 2003 Scott, there was never a doubt in mind that when you're focused on running a league, it would become one of the premiere leagues out there. Congrats on year #1 for the GLCW! I'm looking forward to posting a similar note this same time next year.
P.S., I'd also like to acknowledge all the other rpers, cardwriters and anyone else who contributed in helping to make the GLCW one of the best leagues of its time......
[name withheld] [name withheld] — 3 August 2003 I'm in love with the GLCW. Everybody in the league is great, and it is alot of fun. Alright, I'm ready for another shot dammit....
-Barry PhantomZ — 4 August 2003 [updated:LAST EDITED ON Aug-03-03 AT 08:35 PM (EST)]My name is [name withheld], and this is the only federation I can think of Rping for. Thanks Scott, and everybody else, for making this all still so much F*CKIN fun.
And btw... I'm still looking for my dad... Manson — 4 August 2003 And I keep telling you, David, I'm right here.
