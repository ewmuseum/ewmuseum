GLCW — Card

Kalamazoo Riptide 05

30 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
TBirdSCIL — 30 December 2002 (FADEIN: The cameras come in Mike Manson, as he remains locked in the room from earlier in the show, he sits in the middle of the room, quite calmly, as if he is without a care in the world, he is looking up at the monitor, the camera quickly pans to show Lisa Turtle shoving French Fries into the mouth of Screech Powers, showing that Manson has somehow rigged one of the monitors to show a Saved by the Bell rerun)
TONY ROSS: "Now what is Michael Manson doing?" RICK WISEMAN: "I’m not really sure Tony, but it seems as though our Great Lakes Champion is a bit of a Saved by the Bell fan."
T.R.: "But.... wouldn’t you think he would be a slight bit upset after the way had happened tonight?"
R.W.: "Well, that’s Manson for you, nothing really seems to bother him, and this is all apart of his elaborate mind games he likes to play with people. He seemed to be upset before but now he is making the most of his situation."
T.R.: "Well, folks, this situation is bound to get more interesting as the night goes on. In the mean time, let’s get up to the ring for our next match."
MATT FALEY: "This match is scheduled for one fall.... (CUEUP: "Pure Trauma" by Downset) Coming to the ring, accompanied by Sly Sterling, from Miami, Florida, weighing in at 234 lbs., he is 'EL DIABLO' ABGEL CASTILLO!!"
(crowd gives both of these men pretty good heat, Castillo basically shrugs it off, but Sterling truly soaks up the spotlight, the two enter the ring, and play for the crowd a bit, Sterling more than Castillo, who is mostly down to business)
M.F.: "And his opponent... (CUEUP: "Enter Sandman" by Metallica) From Pandora Island, weighing in at an even 300 lbs, MAELSTROM!!"
(crowd pops HUGE for Maelstrom as he walks slowly towards the ring, never taking his gaze from Castillo, as he steps over the top rope)
T.R.: "And we’re off right away here as Castillo goes flying into the legs of Maelstrom. That dropkick takes the big man down to a knee. Castillo now hits several roundhouse kicks connecting squarely to the head of Maelstrom. Maelstrom shakes those off a bit, and catches the leg of Castillo as he tried another kick and he shoves Castillo flying into the opposite corner. The crowd really responds to that, as Castillo is up quickly, he runs towards Maelstrom, ducks a clothesline and connects with a nice dropkick that staggers the big man."
R.W.: "Smart strategy by Castillo early, using his quickness advantage against Maelstrom. If Mael can get his arms around Castillo, he could really struggle. Castillo quickly hits a nice spinning heel kick sending Maelstrom down to the ground. Mael gets back to his feet quickly, Castillo comes around for a DDT, and Mael blocks that and takes Castillo down with a slam. Nice reversal by Maelstrom.
T.R.: "Maelstrom now is going to slow down the pace here a bit. He takes Castillo and lifts him above his head. Maelstrom is now military pressing Castillo above his head and he drops him unceremoniously to the ground. What power by Maelstrom!"
R.W.: "Mael now comes up and just pounds away on the back of Castillo with those huge right arms."
T.R.: "He now applies a headlock, Maelstrom has really slowed this match down. Castillo needs a quicker pace to have a chance. The referee is now checking on Castillo to see how he’s doing, as he makes to the ropes to break the hold."
R.W.: "Mael stays on the attack. He picks up Castillo, SHOULDER BREAKER! Here’s a cover, 1-2-and Castillo manages to kick out."
T.R.: "Maelstrom is relentless here, he whips Castillo into the ropes, but ducked his head too early for the back body drop. Castillo flips over Mael’s back and hits another dropkick to Maelstrom’s leg. Castillo now hits a swinging neckbreaker! Cover, 1-2- and Mael kicks out emphatically!"
R.W.: "That one might have angered Mael, he jumps to his feet, and reaches out to grab Castillo, but Castillo ducks and hits a European uppercut. He bounces off the rope, AXE KICK! Castillo to the top, HURRICANRANA! Here’s a cover, 1-2- and Mael just kicks out! Maelstrom looks a little dazed and the crowd is stunned!"
T.R.: "And Castillo seems to be playing to the crowd here a bit, and I’m not sure that is the smartest idea. He turns around and meets a vicious clothesline by Maelstrom. Mael is back up now, he picks up Castillo and lands a textbook vertical suplex. Look at Castillo scampering to the outside now, and Sterling is there to council him."
R.W.: "Can’t say that that is necessarily a bad move by Castillo. Maelstrom was starting to gain a little momentum on him, and if that happens, the match normally isn’t far from over."
T.R.: "Castillo slowly reenters the ring, and Maelstrom charges, Castillo ducks again, and goes for the same dropkick from earlier, but this time Maelstrom was waiting for it, and was able to move out of the way. Maelstrom has Castillo again, picks him up, CHOKE SLAM! Cover, 1-2-2 and a half! Somehow Castillo was able to kick out of that! I don’t know how but Castillo got out of a brutal Maelstrom choke slam!"
R.W.: "And now what is Sly Sterling doing up on the ring apron! He’s yelling at the referee, and now Maelstrom is going over to confront Sterling. And listen to the crowd as Maelstrom grabs Sly Sterling, but wait, SMALL PACKAGE BY CASTILLO! 1-2-3! NO!! Maelstrom just kicked out of that one! Holy cow! We almost had one of the biggest upsets in the history of the GLCW!"
T.R.: "That’s right Rick, and Castillo is still on the attack of the stunned Maelstrom. Spinning back kick! Another cover, 1-2-and this time Maelstrom is able to kick out with a little more ease!"
R.W.: "Maelstrom has regained his senses a bit here. Castillo continues to come at him, but he just can’t put the man away. Castillo now hits a nice clothesline off the ropes, here’s another cover, 1-2-and Maelstrom kicks out!"
T.R.: "Castillo off the ropes again and once again he went to the well once too often as Maelstrom catches him and reverses it into a powerslam, cover! 1-2-and Castillo kicks out. Maelstrom again takes Castillo, and BACK BREAKER!"
R.W.: "I think Maelstrom is starting to smell blood in this match. He could be getting ready to finish him off. He grabs Castillo, lifts him up, JACKKNIFE POWERBOMB! This over, 1-2-and no, Sterling grabs the leg of the referee! Maelstrom had this match won! And now Maelstrom grabs Sterling and throws him in the ring. Mael picks up Sterling and hits a jackknife powerbomb on Sterling as well!!"
T.R.: "But Castillo is back up, flying clothesline!! And here’s the bionic elbow, Maelstrom is again dazed! Can Castillo again capitalize on this? It appears he might going for the Salsa Picante, but can he do it Maelstrom?!"
R.W.: "No he can’t Tony!!! Maelstrom blocks it, elbow, and another!! MORTAL SIN!!!! THE MORTAL SIN IS LOCKED!!! Can Maelstrom fight off two men here?? Castillo has no place to go!!! HE’S TAPPING!!!! Maelstrom wins the match!!!"
MATT FALEY: "Here is your winner.....(CUEUP: "Enter Sandman" by Metallica) MAELSTROM!!" (the crowd pops as Maelstrom raises a hand and shoots a disdaining look towards Sly Sterling, and then steps over the top rope and leaves the ring)
T.R.: "A great win for Maelstrom here over Castillo who put up a hell of a fight, and with Sly Sterling’s help, I thought he might be able to pull out a win."
R.W.: "But Sly definitely felt the impact of his involvement, as he is still down after that powerbomb by Maelstrom. We’ll be back right after this."
(fadeout to a commercial for the GLCW’s next house card in Saginaw, Michigan)
