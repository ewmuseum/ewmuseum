Racine Riptide 04

21 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
TBirdSCIL — 21 September 2002 (FADE-IN: Cut back to the action in Racine, the camera quickly pans the crowd, displaying signs such as "Maelstrom is God," "Kraven is my Father," and "Get a Life Savage," but in general everyone is going crazy as the cameras come back in on Tony and Rick)
TONY ROSS: "What a show we have had to this point here in Racine ladies and gentlemen. We’ve seen ‘The Lost Cause’ pick up a huge win, and then let out a very enigmatic warning."
RICK WISEMAN: "Very true Tony. I’m not exactly sure who O’Neill is talking about, but it’s blatantly obvious that he has someone very specific in mind as a target."
T.R.: "Also, we had Jon Savage attacking Maelstrom before the match even got going. Savage claimed that since his previous two opponents had cheated, he had lost his first two matches, I guess he saw this as somehow attempting to even out the score."
R.W.: "Well, of course we know how that turned out. On basically one leg, Maelstrom was able to completely obliterate Savage. And how about that scene at the concession stand?"
T.R.: "Absolute carnage is what it was, but it proves to show how much of a monster Maelstrom really is. But for now, let’s get back up to the ring for our next match."
(CUTTO: Matt Faley standing in the ring) MATT FALEY: "This is match is scheduled for one fall. (CUEUP: "Another One Bites the Dust" by Queen) Coming to the ring, from Warwick, Rhode Island, weighing in at 240 lbs., he is 'SHOWTIME' STEVEN JAMES!!"
(James receives a good pop from the crowd as he enters the ring, dressed in his jagged blue and yellow spandex, he slaps a bunch of hands on his way to the ring, and he enters the ring and ascends to the middle rope and extends his arms to the fans, and gets a good pop.
M.F.: "And his opponent.. (CUEUP: "Chop Suey" by System of a Down) From Parts Unknown, weighing in at 213 lbs., he is REDEMPTION!! "
(Redemption also receives a rather nice pop from the crowd, as he enters the ring dressed in his completely black gear)
T.R.: "OK, looks like we’re about ready to go here. We have a definite contrast in styles. The tall, more grounded James, against the high-flyer in Redemption."
R.W.: "And James immediately uses his size to his advantage, as he starts the match with a quick knee to the stomach of Redemption, and he starts working him over with several quick punches to the back of the neck."
T.R.: "Very good early strategy there for James, trying to keep the high flyer grounded. James gets several more shots in, and Redemption staggers down to the ground. Redemption fights back, and hits a few shots of his own."
R.W.: "For a smaller guy, Redemption can definitely pack a punch. Now he bounces in off the ropes, and hits a nice running dropkick on James, which can’t take him down, Redemption bounces off the ropes, and runs into a huge clothesline from Steven James!!!"
T.R.: "Redemption is going to have to be quicker than that. He doesn’t look as crisp in the early parts of this match. Not nearly as much as I have seen him in the past."
R.W.: "James now picks Redemption up, and hits him with a very nice overhead suplex, and Redemption staggers to the ropes. He is giving up a great deal of size, especially height, and a move like that could really take him out of it."
T.R.: "Redemption is back to his feet, and he catches James with a kick to the stomach as James charged!! Redemption now bounces off the rope, and hits a spear on James! That came out of nowhere!!"
R.W.: "But Redemption is not following up on it. He is playing to the crowd, and giving James an opportunity to quickly get back up to his feet. Redemption just seems totally distracted by something today."
T.R.: "And it cost him this time, as he just turned around into a snap suplex by James. He’s a cover by James, 1-2-and Redemption gets a shoulder up."
R.W.: "James thought he should have had three on that, but he continues the assault on Redemption, it seems as though the crowd is starting to get behind Redemption a bit, trying to urge him out of whatever comatose state he might be in now."
T.R.: "It’s seeming to work, as Redemption fights out. James charges, and Redemption moves and throws James running into the corner, ROLL-UP, 1-2-and James just kicked out of that one. James seems to have taken that as a wake-up call and gets right back in on Redemption. Now the two are trading blows standing in the middle of the ring."
R.W.: "Uh-oh, we have interference." T.R.: "It’s Angel Castillo! Castillo is making his way towards the ring, he is coming after James obviously."
R.W.: "But wait! It’s Chris McMillan, Castillo’s opponent for tonight! He is coming out and attacking Castillo, and now these two are brawling in the aisleway!"
T.R. "Meanwhile, Redemption and James continue to fight it out in the ring, and Redemption staggers James. He climbs the top rope and hits a beautiful missile dropkick! Redemption now has the advantage! But instead, he is now yelling at McMillan and Castillo, who are brawling their way back to the dressing room. Redemption needs to stay focused on James."
R.W.: "And it’s going to prove to cost him! Redemption turns around, RIGHT INTO THE LIGHTS OUT! James had come behind Redemption, and just like that, he hits his devastating maneuver. The cover’s academic.... 1-2-3!!!!"
M.F.: "Here is your winner..... "SHOWTIME" STEVEN JAMES!!" (CUEUP: "Another One Bites the Dust")
T.R.: "Redemption just didn’t seem focused in this match, and he made one very bad mistake, and it cost him dearly."
R.W.: "Also, it will be very interesting to see how the events that transpired between Castillo and McMillan affects the match tonight.
T.R.: "But for now, let’s go to the backstage area, where Lady V is standing by." (FADE-IN on the GLCW banner backstage. A angry and sweaty Chris McMillan stands there alongside Lady Veronica, fresh from his brawl with Angel Castillo. McMillan is attired as usual in cuffed jeans, engineer boots, a plain white t-shirt, and a worn motorcycle jacket)
LADY VERONICA: "I'm here with 'The Wolf' Chris McMillan, fresh off of what had to be a disappointing loss to Golem last week. Wolf, tonight you're slated to go head-to-head with 'El Diablo' Angel Castillo, whom you just had an impromptu brawl with. Your thoughts?"
THE WOLF: <takes some deep breaths and speaks> "Tonight I wrestle a man who calls himself 'The Devil'. I'm not all that impressed, though. He names himself after the Father of Lies, the Prince of Darkness, the Fallen Angel ... but he doesn't quite live up to the name, I think. He's not scary or evil. Hell, he's not even mildly foreboding. He's a deluded, braindead husk that likes to pretend he's a little bit better than the rest of us. I'm not buying it. Now, I've heard that Castillo is good in the ring. Well, that
might be so. But I think I'm a little bit better. We're getting ready to find out." LADY V: "And what about what happened tonight during the match between Steven James and Redemption?"
THE WOLF: "I didn’t want Castillo getting involved in their business. Plus, I saw an opportunity to get a bit of a jump on tonight. I want Angel Castillo to know exactly who he is dealing with. And if he doesn’t know yet, tonight he will definitely find out for sure."
(THE WOLF brushes past LADY V on his way to the ring) (Fade to commercial)
