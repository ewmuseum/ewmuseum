xXJay StrokeXx — 11 August 2002 (Camera fades in on the almost akward looking team of Dalton Parker and Kurt Rogers, The High Plains Drifters. Parker looks a little more comfortable with the camera being near him, but Kurt shifts nervously, a little intimidated.)
DALTON PARKER: Ok, so, our quest to redeem GLCW begins in Madison, Wisconsin. Against.... (Kurt gets up on his tip-toes and whispers the name into Parker's ear) Lithuanian Lightning and Rick Morgan. Wait, Lithuanian Lightning and Rick Morgan? Oh, Kurt, say it ain't so!
KURT ROGERS: (with some very odd inflection) It, ain't so! (Dalton looks down at Kurt with a mix of amusement and confusion. He continues) DALTON PARKER: I am not an easily frightened man, but whoa, when we, The High Plains Drifters, are challenged by terrifying athletes as Lithuanian Lightning and Rick Morgan, well, you know. It's freaky! I mean, ok, Kurt... (They look at each other) what are you scared of?
KURT ROGERS: Oh. Um, Scaffold Matches (Dalton nods), stuff that's been in the fridge for more than a few days (Dalton nods), names that have alliteration, like Lithuanian Lightning, or Poseable Prosthetic Paul E. Paul (Dalton looks down in confusion at the last name, but still nods). Oh, and those people who go to a fast food place and order a ton of crap, and then get a DIET soda! What's their deal? Oh..
(Dalton interrupts) DALTON PARKER: Kurt, thanks for proving the point. But, that'll be enough. Good job. That point, loyal listeners...
KURT ROGERS: ALLITERATION!!!! (Runs off screaming) DALTON PARKER: That point, is that we have to overcome our fears eventually. That means that Kurt and myself must overcome Lithuanian Lighting (Far in the background, "AHHHHH!!!!") and Rick Morgan, at the next GLCW Riptide Event. We shall... overcome... I know it's a song. Shut up.
(Dalton turns to run after Kurt. He shouts, "Kurt, come back! I'm sure he's not nearly that scary!" Fade to black)
