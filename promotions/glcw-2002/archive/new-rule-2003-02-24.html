TBirdSCIL — 24 February 2003 All members, I just had to say this because it was bothering me. From now on, I don't want anyone complaining even in character about big matches given away on house cards. Throughout the history of wrestling, house cards have been hugely important in drawing money and keeping leagues in the black. Both WWF and WCW pretty much abandoned that concept and the WCW is no longer with us and the WWE is making all the same mistakes. Let's put it this way, if you are Joe Schmo from Hammond, Indiana and you want to pay money to go to a non-televised wrestling show, wouldn't you want all the big stars to be there for your entertainment? So I don't want anyone complaining about huge matches on house cards because it makes the league look bad. Thanks.
Pres. [name withheld] TheOriginalSE — 25 February 2003 and there have been huge complaints about wwf and wcw putting huge matches right after pay per views on television that fans can watch for free whereas they just paid $30 to watch it the night before ...
its annoying and fans in real life are annoyed with it too .. and if i were a big name wrestler who gets injured on a house show because the match was a big match and a lot was expected out of it, then i'd be annoyed too.
