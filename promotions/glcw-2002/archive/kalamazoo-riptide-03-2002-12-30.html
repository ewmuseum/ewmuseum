GLCW — Card

Kalamazoo Riptide 03

30 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
TBirdSCIL — 30 December 2002 ((FADE-IN: Michael Manson is sitting in the room he was locked up in, looking annoyed and as if he wants to destroy one of the cameras that is looking at him. CUT-TO: Ringside.))
RICK WISEMAN: "And it looks as if Michael Manson is getting more and more frustrated with what Commissioner Krusher has done."
ROSS: "Well, coming up next, fans, is one of the matches that I've been waiting for since it has been announced. Jean Rabesque will be taking on the Unholy Alliance's Flatliner!"
WISEMAN: "We've already seen Rabesque plenty tonight.. first as one of the four men to help lock up Manson, then as 'Simply Sensational' Sean Edmunds blindsided him as he was going to get ready for this match, and then again when he finally got fed up with Edmunds' antics, and attacked him after his victory over Nick Kurtel."
ROSS: "Lets go up to Matt Faley in the ring!" ((CUT-TO: Faley, in the ring.)) FALEY: "Ladies and gentlemen, the next match is for one fall. Introducing first.. (CUE-UP: "Shapes of Things" by the Jeff Healy Band. The crowd pops as Rabesque starts to walk down the rampway, with a slight limp..) from MONTREAL, QUEBEC, CANADA .... weighing in at 245 pounds .... JEAN RABESQUE!"
ROSS: "Rabesque slightly limping here, Rick.. as Edmunds attacked him earlier and put him in that figure four leglock.."
WISEMAN: "But nonetheless, he is in the ring and looking at the curtain awaiting the arrival of Flatliner!"
FALEY: "And his opponent... (CUE-UP: "For Whom the Bell Tolls" by Metallica. Flatliner and Kraven start to walk down to ringside. Flatliner is also limping.) Being led down to the ring by fellow 'Unholy Alliance' partner Kraven... From PANDORA ISLAND, he is one half of the Great Lakes Tag Team Champions..... FLATLIIIIIIIINERRRRRRR!"
(Crowd erupts in boos) WISEMAN: "And Kraven has come down here with Flatliner.." ROSS: "Flatliner is also limping, but his seems to be much more pronounced than Jean Rabesque, and Rabesque's eyes already are lighting up."
WISEMAN: "But they're not lighting up at Flatliner's knee, Tony. Rabesque is enraged that Kraven is down at ringside.. and now he's yelling at the referee to get Kraven into the back. Flatliner gingerly getting into the ring and here comes Kraven into the ring."
ROSS: "But the ref is quickly over there telling Kraven he's got to go to the back and Kraven is arguing with him! Now the ref is yelling at Kraven to get himself back! And Flatliner is limping around behind Rabesque who is yelling at Kraven!"
WISEMAN: "WAIT A MINUTE! Flatliner just ran up to Rabesque and KNEED HIM IN THE BACK WITH THAT BAD KNEE!! Rabesque is on the ground stunned! Now Flatliner is taking off his knee brace... and HE'S USING TO BASH RABESQUE IN THE HEAD WITH!!"
ROSS: "Flatliner was FAKING the knee injury just to lure Jean Rabesque into a lull! I cannot believe this!"
WISEMAN: "And now Kraven, seeing as his work is done hops off the apron and is walking backwards towards the ramp as Flatliner continues to beat on Rabesque with that knee brace!"
((CUT-TO: The top of the rampway .. a figure in the darkness appears. His face is covered with a black hooded sweatshirt and his face is illuminated by the lights in the background. Kraven turns around and stops dead in his tracks .. he looks up at the figure, who is massive in size. The figure just shakes his head and then is gone ... Kraven slowly starts to ascend the rampway, making sure this figure isn't there.))
ROSS: "Who was that?!" WISEMAN: "I don't know, Tony, but whoever it was was MASSIVE .. and even Kraven was stopped in his path when he saw that."
ROSS: "Flatliner has gotten rid of that knee brace and is still pounding on the back of Jean Rabesque who hasn't gotten off the mat yet."
WISEMAN: "Finally the ref cuts in and allows Rabesque time to get up. And now the bell has rung.."
ROSS: "Flatliner charges in and places a knee into the gut of Rabesque to double him over. Hand full of hair now as he throws Rabesque SHOULDER FIRST into the turnbuckle. Rabesque in the corner, here comes Flatliner charging in.... and he just SQUASHED him in the corner, placing an elbow in the jaw for good measure."
WISEMAN: "Flatliner is vicious, he won't stop at any cost.." ROSS: "Flatliner brings Rabesque out of the corner .. and is now slapping him around in the face.. Hooks the tights into a nice suplex. Flatliner is up and is being greeted by a chorus of boos from the fans."
WISEMAN: "The fans don't like the way this match started out, with Flatliner attacking Rabesque like he did. Flatliner walks over to Rabesque.. and Rabesque with a punch to the gut.. another one! And Flatliner is doubled over... DDT! Rabesque back up to his feet waiting for Flatliner... he's up .. and RABESQUE with a HUGE CLOTHESLINE that sends Flatliner over the top rope and to the arena floor!"
ROSS: "And now they're both on the outside! Flatliner slow to his feet.. and RABESQUE SPEARS HIM ON THE ARENA FLOOR.. And now Rabesque is letting him have it with lefts and rights into the face of Flatliner!"
WISEMAN: "And now Rabesque picks him up.. irish whip sends Flatliner CRASHING into the ring steps!"
ROSS: "Flatliner is holding his knee ... did he really injure it this time or is he faking it like he did before?"
WISEMAN: "I don't know, Tony.. but Rabesque rolls him into the ring and he's still holding that knee."
ROSS: "Now Rabesque goes after the knee with a few stomps .. picks Flatliner up, and takes him down with a leg sweep aimed right at that hurt knee. Rabesque drops an elbow on that knee and he's not going to relent anytime soon."
WISEMAN: "Rabesque now picking up Flatliner ... but Flatliner with a small package... ONE ..... TW -- no, Jean got out at one and a half.. Flatliner surprised Rabesque there and Rabesque is back up and he drops the elbow on the knee.... but Flatliner moved and Rabesque hit the mat!"
ROSS: "Flatliner quickly got himself out of the way and now Rabesque is up checking his elbow .. he doesn't see Flatliner coming from behind... GERMAN SUPLEX WITH A BRIDGE... ONE .... TWO .... KICKOUT by Rabesque."
WISEMAN: "Flatliner is proving to Rabesque that he's got more technical finesse than Rabesque might have thought..."
ROSS: "Flatliner picks up Rabesque.. and takes him down with a short arm clothesline which nearly took off his head! Now Flatliner is kicking Rabesque in the midsection until he kicks him right into the corner.."
WISEMAN: "The ref pushes him out of the way and separates the two... but here comes Flatliner CHARGING IN!"
ROSS: "And the ref JUST gets out of the way as Flatliner ran SHOULDER FIRST into Jean Rabesque's midsection! Rabesque stumbling out of the corner... Flatliner catches him.. BELLY to BELLY SUPLEX! He covers him... ONE ... TWO ... KICKOUT by Rabesque!"
WISEMAN: "And Rabesque kicking out just in the nick of time there, Tony.." ROSS: "Yes, Flatliner almost stole the match right there. Rabesque is on his knees.. here comes Flatliner.. and Rabesque pulls on the tights and Flatliner has gone careening into the turnbuckle face first!"
WISEMAN: "Rabesque yanking the tights to get the edge on Flatliner, it seems the flash of anger and annoyance that was displayed earlier when he finally responded to attack after attack by Sean Edmunds has returned in this match.."
ROSS: "Rabesque back up, slowly, but up, as Flatliner is still in the corner. Rabesque walks in and knees him in the head.. he picks him up... running bulldog out of the corner! Rabesque now grabbing the leg of Flatliner.. he's GOING FOR THE FIGURE FOUR!"
WISEMAN: "But Flatliner quickly kicks him in the face for all his troubles. Flatliner into the corner .. but here comes Rabesque... BASEBALL SLIDE INTO THE HEAD OF FLATLINER!"
ROSS: "Flatliner trying to get some time to recuperate and he got two of Rabesque's boots in his jaw.."
WISEMAN: "Rabesque picking up Flatliner and has him into the corner.. irish whip .. no REVERSAL by Flatliner!! REVERSAL by Rabesque and Flatliner goes chest first into the turnbuckle! He comes stumbling backwards.. DROPKICK to the back by Rabesque and FLATLINER GOES OVER THE TOP ROPE!!"
ROSS: "And the crowd loved that! Rabesque is climbing out of the ring and walks over to Flatliner whose still on the floor. Rabesque jawing at Flatliner telling him to get up. Flatliner starts to stir.. and Rabesque is waiting for him to get back up. Flatliner finally up. Throws a punch at Rabesque, but Rabesque blocks it! Rabesque with a right of his own and Flatliner goes down!"
WISEMAN: "Rabesque picks him up.. Irish whip into the ringpost and Flatliner hits his shoulder and goes down! The ref at 6, as Rabesque rolls in and out again. Rabesque walks over to Flatliner and finally rolls him into the ring."
ROSS: "Rick, Rabesque could have gotten the count out victory there, but decided to go out and bring Flatliner in for some more."
WISEMAN: "Rabesque now applying an arm bar on Flatliner's hurt shoulder. But Flatliner slowly backs him into the corner. And Rabesque keeps putting more pressure on the arm.. but finally Flatliner has gotten them into the corner and the ref goes in to break it up."
ROSS: "LOOK AT THAT! As the ref was trying to separate them, Flatliner jabbed his fingers into the eye of Rabesque and Rabesque is walking around in the ring aimlessly as he cannot see!"
WISEMAN: "Well, that gives Flatliner some time to try to shake off his shoulder problems.."
ROSS: "He didn't get much time as Rabesque starts closing in.. AND FLATLINER JUST UNLOADED ON RABESQUE WITH A HUGE CLOTHESLINE! He covers him.. ONE ... T.. KICKOUT by Rabesque."
WISEMAN: "Flatliner couldn't put enough pressure on his shoulder to make a good cover, so Rabesque easily escapes that pinfall. Flatliner waiting for Rabesque to get up.. picks him up!! And SLAMS him down with a BACKBREAKER POWERSLAM..."
ROSS: "Flatliner now choking Rabesque.. and the ref counting, one, two three.. and Flatliner releases before four ... Flatliner on top of Rabesque and wailing away.."
WISEMAN: "The ref gets involved and tells Flatliner to get off and he complies... JUST TO GET RIGHT BACK ON HIM AND START CHOKING RABESQUE!"
ROSS: "And the ref quickly counts, and Flatliner has to release before four again." WISEMAN: "Flatliner testing the ref's patience with his choking ... and now he hooks Jean Rabesque up.. NORTHERN LIGHTS SUPLEX! No bridge however and Rabesque is on the mat."
ROSS: "Flatliner showing Jean Rabesque that not only can he be physically brutal, but he does have some technical skills as well. I would have liked so have seen the bridge there with the Northern Lights Suplex, but Flatliner has impressed me enough already."
WISEMAN: "Flatliner picks Rabesque by the head ... RABESQUE WITH A SMALL PACKAGE... ONE .... TWO.... THRE-- KICKOUT BY FLATLINER!"
ROSS: "Rabesque almost snuck a quick victory there and Flatliner is back up and stomping the living hell out of Rabesque."
WISEMAN: "Flatliner kicking Rabesque in the stomach and now the head.. and he drops the knee on the throat of Rabesque and Rabesque is rolling on the mat."
ROSS: "Flatliner now slowly stalking Rabesque.. Rabesque resting on the ropes now... and FLATLINER CHOKING HIM WITH THE ROPES!!"
WISEMAN: "The ref quickly gets him to back off as he approaches.. now the ref checking on Rabesque. Flatliner against the opposite ropes... leaps and LANDS ON THE BACK OF RABESQUE .. choking him again!!"
ROSS: "Finally the ref tells Flatliner that he's thisclose to being disqualified and Flatliner assures him that he won't choke Rabesque again."
((CUT-TO: The rampway as Kraven starts walking down to ringside. The crowd responds to his presence.))
WISEMAN: "What is he doing back?" ROSS: "Just when you thought things couldn't get worse for Rabesque, they do." WISEMAN: "Flatliner looks over at Kraven .. and as he does he doesn't even see Jean Rabesque climbing the ropes.. he turns around.. AND HERE COMES RABESQUE WITH A FLYING CROSSBODY... ONE ..... TWO .... THREE-- nooooo! Flatliner got out right before three!"
ROSS: "Rabesque put EVERYTHING into that move, and now is lying on the mat barely breathing. Flatliner gets up and plants an elbow into his chest. And now he drags him over to the ropes again.."
WISEMAN: "And Flatliner breaks his promise to the ref and starts choking Rabesque with the ROPES!! The ref is LIVID and gets in there to break it up.. Rabesque hung over the ropes as the ref backs up Flatliner giving him a piece of his mind.."
((Kraven goes and gets the ring steps. He nails Rabesque's head with them and Rabesque goes flying on his back in the middle of the ring. The crowd starts booing incredibly loud.))
ROSS: "I CANNOT BELIEVE THIS! KRAVEN JUST NAILED JEAN RABESQUE IN THE HEAD WITH THE RING STEPS WHILE THE REF WAS WARNING FLATLINER!!"
WISEMAN: "The ref stops talking to Flatliner to see whats happening and all he sees is Jean Rabesque lying unconcious in the ring! Flatliner takes this opportunity to move around the referee and he picks up Rabesque...... FLATLINE HEART PUNCH!!!! INTO THE DEVIL's DROP POWERBOMB!!! Flatliner covers..... ONE ...... TWO ...... THREEE!!! FLATLINER HAS DEFEATED JEAN RABESQUE HERE ON RIPTIDE!"
ROSS: "Kraven now getting in the ring. This is disgusting! KRAVEN NOW STARTS BEATING ON RABESQUE!!"
WISEMAN: "And now the GLCW Tag Team Champions are beating on Jean Rabesque. Flatliner holds him up as Kraven is giving him enormous body blows...."
((The crowd pops as a man comes from the crowd, grabs a chair and enters the ring. Kraven and Flatliner do not see him as they are busy putting the boots to Rabesque.))
ROSS: "And now some man wearing a hooded sweatshirt has come into the ring and saved Jean Rabesque from the vicious beat down that the Unholy Alliance were giving to Jean Rabesque.. by CLOCKING FLATLINER and KRAVEN WITH A CHAIRSHOT TO THE HEAD!"
((The man takes off his hood and sunglasses to reveal...)) WISEMAN: "BRIAN LAWLER HAS ENTERED GREAT LAKES CHAMPIONSHIP WRESTLING!!!!" ROSS: "And the crowd is going nuts as Lawler is helping Jean Rabesque out of the ring and up the rampway! A terrific match ruined tonight as Flatliner STEALS a victory from Jean Rabesque here with help from his tag team partner Kraven! But the big story here, is BRIAN LAWLER has joined the GLCW!!!"
WISEMAN: "I cannot believe it, Tony! Brian LAWLER!" ROSS: "We will be right back!" ((CUT-TO: Another shot of the cannon that was shown earlier. This time the wick is three quarters burnt.... the wick keeps getting smaller and smaller as the flame burns towards the end.. FADE-TO: Commercial)
