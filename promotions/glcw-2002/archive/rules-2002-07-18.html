TBirdSCIL — 18 July 2002 All members, Here is a quick overview of the rules and regulations, I don't want to make this as long as the US Constitution so I'll make it brief:
1. There will not be a stacking rule, since RP will be completely judged by quality. The amount of RPs will be judged evenly against each other based on the quality of each. Quantity will not matter. so if an RPer throws up 5 short crappy RPs to one really good and well thought out RP, the latter will more than likely win.
2. Constructive criticism will be welcome but excessive bitching is not. Paul and I will welcome any ideas about the league or any ideas about storylines and angles, but excessive whining about losing a match will not be tolerated. This league is more about fun than competition, so if you're all about getting nothing but victories, this league is not for you. Enjoyment and fun is the main goal of GLCW.
3. If you fail to RP for one card without letting us know you will not be able to RP, you will be suspended for one card. If you fail to RP again without letting us know, you will be terminated from the league.
4. Flaming and personal attacks will not be tolerated. Even though the OORP Board is open for debate and discussion, I will not let any personal attacks or flaming to continue and those posts will be deleted. If you have a problem, take it to email.
5. Swearing will not be tolerated. I'm not going to go into specifics but pretty much everyone is aware of the buzzwords that would not be allowed, the f-word and the s-word among them. I expect everyone to be able to communicate and cut promos without sounding like Andrew Dice Clay on acid. You can use a (beep) once in a while, but that's about it. And please don't email me about what swear words you can use, you're just telling me right off the bat that you can't communicate intelligently.
6. Expect cards to have a one week turnaround or slightly longer depending on how busy we are with real life. We might be experimenting with the idea of frequent house cards with short RP times during TV show downtime, so keep that in mind. House cards will be extremely short form, about a paragraph per match. If you have feedback about our house card idea, please email me and let me know what you think.
That's it for now, keep an eye out for further development... GLCW President [name withheld]
