GLCW — Card

Indianapolis Riptide 07

28 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
TBirdSCIL — 28 April 2003 (FADE-IN: The camera pans the Pepsi Coliseum in Indianapolis, showing the fans in varying states of hysteria. The shot focuses on one of the better looking women in the audience, who smiles flirtaciously at the camera and does a little dance as all the men around her hoot and holler. CUT-TO: Matt Faley Standing at mid-ring)
M.F.: "Ladies and gentlemen, our next match is the first of the double main event, part of tonight's tag team turmoil!! (crowd cheers) First, coming to the ring," (CUE-UP: "Man That You Fear" by Marilyn Manson) "from Hartford, Connecticut, weighing in at 239lbs., ANARKY!!"
(The crowd boos loudly as Anarky steps out, wearing his beat-up old leather jacket with the anarchy symbol on the back. He doesn't pay any attention to the crowd as they heckle him and he rolls into the ring)
M.F.: "And his partner," (CUE-UP: "Mechanical Animals" by Marilyn Manson) "From Chicago, Illinois, weighing in at 230lbs., MICHAEL MANSON!!"
(The crowd maintains the rain of boos as Manson comes out, and he is as indifferent to the crowd as Anarky is. Manson wears long black rights with strange red designs on them along with a black T-shirt. He climbs into the ring and talks to Anarky as the fans continue to boo)
M.F.: "And their opponents," (CUE-UP: "Shapes of Things" by the Jeff Healy Band) "From Montreal, Quebec, Canada, weighing in at 245lbs., JEAN RABESQUE!!"
(The crowd cheers loudly as Rabesque emerges from the locker room, wearing blue trunks with a towel around his neck. He slaps a few hands along the way, but keeps his eye on Manson and Anarky in the ring. When he gets to ringside, he points over at them and has a few choice words)
M.F.: "And his partner," (CUE-UP: "Shadow Man" by W.A.S.P) "From Hell, Michigan, weighing in at 265lbs., the GLCW Television Champion, JAROD POE!!"
(The crowd continues to cheer loudly as Poe comes out, wearing the TV belt around his waist, along with black leather pants and black combat boots. When he gets to the ring, he raises his arms to acknowledge the crowd and then stares in at Manson and Anarky)
TONY ROSS: "I've been looking forward to this one, guys! Four of the best in GLCW are going to go at it right here in tag action! We have the former Great Lakes Champion Michael Manson and his partner, multi-time World Champion Anarky taking on current GLCW Champion Jarod Poe, and his partner Jean Rabesque who has held many titles himself!"
RICK WISEMAN: "Manson and Anarky do have a history of teaming together and being allied in the past, while Rabesque and Poe are an odd couple of sorts. Poe has made some disparaging remarks about Rabesque in the past, but apparently they have made amends to team tonight and as well as at Wargames."
T.R.: "These are four of the men who will be inv0lved in that huge main event Wargames match at Ringlords 1! All four men are in the ring staring at each other and we have some psyching out going on here!"
JAKE SHADES: "Poe is already out of it and Rabesque was psyched out before he even got to the arena! That girly man is wildly intimidated by Manson and Anarky, and do you realize that Manson and Rabesque have not faced off one on one yet? Rabesque is ducking him yet he is the number one contender which is absolutely ridiculous!"
T.R.: "Whoah, all four men begin brawling in the middle of the ring at the drop of a hat! Rabesque and Manson are pounding away at each other and Poe and Anarky are squaring off! Anarky and Poe both go tumbling over the ropes and Rabesque and Manson will be the legal men in the ring! Manson rakes Rabesque's eyes and kicks him in the shin to get the advantage! Manson grabs a side headlock and begins punching him in the head!"
R.W.: "It is true what Shades said about Manson and Rabesque never facing off, but I just think because it would be such a big match that Krusher is saving it."
J.S.: "Yeah, he's saving Rabesque's life! We all know that Krusher is buddies with Rabesque, and I believe more than a friendship is going on there!"
T.R.: "Enough! Manson rams Rabesque's head off the turnbuckle and then body slams him down! Manson mounts the second turnbuckle and comes down with a forearm across the throat! He drops down and begins choking Rabesque but the referee is busy yelling at Poe and Anarky to break it up and get to their corners! Oh my!! Poe just posted Anarky viciously! That took care of that as Poe gets to his corner!"
R.W.: "Poe may very well be one of the few men in GLCW who can stand toe-to-toe with Anarky."
T.R.: "Anarky is indeed double tough, but so is every man in this match! Manson looks for the tag and sees Anarky isn't there and just shrugs and goes back to work on Rabesque! He pulls Rabesque up and whips him in and hits a nice running clothesline! Anarky slowly makes his way to the corner and climbs the apron and he looks shaken up! Manson may want to hold off on tagging him for a while! Manson picks him up and hooks him in a full nelson, possibly going for the dragon suplex, but Rabesque reverse out of it with a go-behind and pulls him back with a throw suplex! Rabesque starts to crawl for his corner but Manson is able to collect himself and grab his leg!"
J.S.: "Manson is not going to give Rabesque any breathing room and he knows that Rabesque is the weak link! Him and Anarky and gonna grind this clown into hamburger meat!"
T.R.: "Manson pulls Rabesque back and then rams his knee off the mat! Keep in mind that Rabesque's main submission move is a figure four and if you weaken his legs, it makes that hold less effective! Manson twists on that leg some more and he reaches over and tags in Anarky! Anarky takes hold of the leg and drops a big elbow onto the knee!"
J.S.: "Listen to that fruitcake cry out in pain! Ha ha!" T.R.: "Anarky twists that knee after the elbow and now he's slapping Rabesque in the face, trying to humiliate him! Anarky has that leg bent in an awkward position and there's not a lot Rabesque can do about it! Anarky slaps him again and now Rabesque is angry! He breaks the hold and kicks Anarky away with the free leg! They get up and Anarky walks right into a hip toss! Anarky right back up again and Rabesque downs him with a shoulderblock! Rabesque runs off the opposite ropes and connects with a stiff clothesline! Rabesque dives for the tag and in comes Poe!"
R.W.: "It looked like Poe worked himself into a frenzy over in the corner." T.R.: "Poe looks to be psyched up and the crowd is 100% behind him! He comes in swinging away at Anarky! He connects with some huge rights and Poe gorilla presses Anarky!! He presses him a few times then deposits him to the mat, showing off his impressive strength! Poe waits for Anarky to get up and then hits a running knee that sends Anarky into the corner! Poe goes in after him with forearms to the head!"
R.W.: "Poe is just absolutely relentless when he's in that ring, and that's one of the reasons why he is the Television champion, he's like a machine."
J.S.: "Yeah and he also doesn't pay attention!" T.R.: "Manson knees Poe from behind! Poe almost falls out of the ring but he balances himself and goes after Manson! These two have a history and Manson is able to avoid Poe and the referee steps in between! Big Al Marinaro wants Manson out of there as Poe tries to get at him! Anarky from behind rolls Poe up!! 1.....2.....Kickout!! Almost got him! Poe looks quite angry as he quickly gets to his feet and kicks Anarky in the stomach and hits a big kneelift!! Anarky staggers back and Poe grabs him and lifts him up!! Press slam into the Falcon Arrow!! The Poison Dart!! A cover!! 1........2.......No!! Anarky got a shoulder up!"
R.W.: "That's one of Poe's favorite moves and he's put away more than a few men with that move, even putting The Jobber through a table with it."
T.R.: "Poe drags Anarky to his feet and whips him in! Poe powerslams him with authority!! Poe gets Anarky to his feet and tags in Rabesque! Rabesque grabs Anarky's arm and hits a stiff short clothesline! Rabesque drops down for a cover!! 1.......2....No!! They can't keep Anarky down!"
J.S.: "They're gonna have to do a lot more than that to get a pin on Anarky, anything short of using a gun isn't going to work!"
T.R.: "Rabesque begins stomping on the knee of Anarky, possibly wearing him down for the figure four! He does a series of step over toe holds to really work on that knee! Anarky tries to crawl for the corner but Rabesque has a firm hold! Rabesque drops an elbow onto the knee of Anarky! He pulls him up and picks him up for an atomic drop but the impact hits Anarky's knee! Anarky tumbles to the mat and Rabesque quickly slaps on the figure four!! He's got it in the middle of the ring but Manson runs and stomps on Rabesque to break the hold!!"
R.W.: "And here comes Poe! Poe chases Manson out of the ring!" T.R.: "Marinaro starting to lose control here as Anarky gets up with a limp and he avoids a Rabesque knee clip! Rabesque runs into the turnbuckle and Anarky catches him on the rebound for a rollup but no referee! Rabesque kicks out and both men get up and Anarky kicks him in the gut and nails him with the Chaos Breaker!! That snap reverse neckbreaker put a tremendous amount of torque on Rabesque's neck!! Anarky covers but the ref is occupied!!"
J.S.: "Come on Marinaro, get your fat ass over there and count!" T.R.: "Poe finally goes back to his corner and Marinaro sees the pin but Anarky is already up and yelling at him! Anarky and Marinaro are arguing now and look at this!! Poe is dragging Rabesque closer to the corner!! He hops back on the apron and waits for Marinaro to turn his attention back and he tags Rabesque!! Brilliant!!"
J.S.: "Brilliant!? That delinquent just blatantly cheated and you call that brilliant!? If that was Manson and Anarky you would have had an epileptic fit!"
T.R.: "Poe comes in firing away at Anarky! He gets a few shots in but Anarky stumbles back and tags in Manson!! Manson climbs in and he's immediately bludgeoned by Poe! Poe kicks him in the ribs and whips him in! He gets Manson in an airplane spin on the rebound!! He drops him with a diamond cutter and that is the move he calls the Black Circle of Death and this is it!! 1........2........Ohhhhhh!!! Anarky is barely able to make a save!! Unbelievable!!"
R.W.: "That match was over right there! Anarky had his sense about him enough to get in there and break that up!"
T.R.: "Poe tries to get at Anarky but he drops off the apron! Now Poe is screaming down at him!"
R.W.: "Poe has got to keep his attention on Manson!" T.R.: "Manson rushes Poe but Poe knew he was coming and stepped aside!! Manson rebounds and Poe absolutely levels him with a clothesline!! Wow!! 1........2......No!! Manson barely kicks out!! Poe can't believe it!! Poe drags Manson up by the hair and brings him to the corner!! He puts Manson up on the top rope and he's going for the Sudden Impact!! Anarky is there and he pulls Poe's feet out from under him!! He saved Manson's life there and Rabesque has seen enough!!! Rabesque comes charging around the ring but Anarky is already back in his corner!!"
R.W.: "Marinaro is out there yelling at Rabesque to get back to his corner and he's trying desperately to get control here but this gives Manson and Anarky time to do their dirty work!"
J.S.: "Look at that idiot Rabesque! He doesn't know how bad he's screwing Poe!! Go back to your corner you big dummy!"
T.R.: "Anarky is in the ring and he DDTs Poe and goes back to the corner quickly!! Manson is up and he grabs Poe from behind and nails him with a dragon suplex!! Devastating impact!! Manson cannot cover here! He crawls to his corner and tags in Anarky as Rabesque has finally gone back to his corner after arguing with Marinaro on the outside! Anarky comes in and pulls Poe up and goes for the Chaos Breaker but Poe blocks!! Poe kicks him in the gut and whips him in!! Anarky ducks a clothesline, and kicks Poe in the groin!! Chaos Breaker!! A pin!! 1.......2........3!!! He got him!! Anarky gets the pinfall on Poe but not without a kick to the groin to set that move up!!"
J.S.: "Ha ha ha, I don't expect anything less from these guys, they always find a way to win and that's why they will always be better than two jerks like Poe and Rabesque! This is just a prelude to Wargames and I can't decide who I think is gonna tap first, Rabesque, Poe, Maelstrom or Justice!"
R.W.: "And Jean Rabesque is not happy! Look out!" T.R.: "Rabesque plows into Anarky and sends him flying out of the ring!! Manson charges him and gets flipped high and out of the ring for his troubles and he lands hard on the floor!! Ouch! But the damage is done and Manson and Anarky are the winners as Rabesque checks on Poe!"
R.W.; "That was a great match and I'm not going to take anything away from Rabesque and Poe, if Marinaro had a little more control over the match they may have won."
J.S.: "Yeah well tell that to idiot Rabesque who couldn't keep his mouth shut! He cost them the match and if I was Poe I'd slap him upside the head!"
T.R.: "Ok folks, as Manson and Anarky leave the ringside area being pelted with garbage, let's take a break! We will be back with the main event of Riptide right after this! Maelstrom and Jared Justice vs. The Unholy Alliance!"
(fade to a commercial for the new GLCW Magazine)
