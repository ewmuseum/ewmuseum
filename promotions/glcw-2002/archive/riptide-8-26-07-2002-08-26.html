GLCW — Card

Riptide 8/26 07

26 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
TBirdSCIL — 26 August 2002 (FADE-IN: The locker room area. Golem is walking by and Lady V runs up beside him. She gently grabs his arm to slow him and he spins around quickly. He scans her up and down and then begins to walk away again.)
LADY VERONICA: "Golem, wait! I was wondering if I could get a word with you? About your upcoming match with 'Mister Dread', Chris McMillan?"
GOLEM: "And what is it exactly that you want me to say? Nothing I say has ever or will ever be correctly interpreted by the people watching this. McMillan will realize what I have been saying all too soon, though."
LADY V: "Do you plan on extracting revenge on McMillan for attacking you at the end of First Wave only a few weeks ago?"
GOLEM: "He didn't attack me. That implies that he did some sort of damage, accomplished something. You and I both know that McMillan could never slow a man such as Golem down. I shall wear on and I shall blow past 'Mister Dread.'"
LADY V: "I think you may be taking him a little too lightly. This isn't the first round anymore. He wasn't handed his first victory."
GOLEM: "And I was? Last time I checked, it was Golem who won the main event last show. I didn't beat Hawk McDaniel. I beat Troy Martinez, with relative ease no less. I have always before and will always from here on out make good on my promises. And I promise you, my precious pet, that I will defeat Chris McMillan tonight."
(Lady V steps back in a combination of fear and disgust as Golem slowly smiles.) LADY V: "The same could be said about McMillan. He, too, has claimed victory tonight and last time he made such a proclamation, it came true."
GOLEM: "I believe I said before...I am *NOT* Hawk McDaniel. I will not get frustrated with McMillan because I will have NOTHING to get frustrated about it. I will go in and I will win. You know what? I am going to go now. And win."
(Golem begins to walk off as Lady V turns to the camera.) LADY V: "Well, bold claims by Golem and we will get a chance to see if he can follow through on those claims."
(CUT-TO: Matt Faley standing at mid-ring) MATT FALEY: "Ladies and gentlemen, in our next Quarterfinal match, coming to the ring," (CUE-UP: "Dogs of War" by Pink Floyd) "From Cadillac, Michigan, weighing in at 180lbs., CHRIS 'MR. DREAD' MCMILLAN!!"
(McMillan gets a good pop as he comes to the ring wearing black jeans and a "Dogs of War" T-shirt. He leapfrogs over the ropes into the ring and begins to loosen up. The mounting number of stitches on his body is easily noticed)
M.F.: "And his opponent," (CUE-UP: "The Day the World Went Away" by Nine Inch Nails) "From Death Valley, California, weighing in at 238lbs. GOLEM!!"
(The crowd erupts with boos as Golem stalks to the ring with his trademark sneer. He stares evilly at several fans at ringside and even makes a few youngsters flinch. He climbs into the ring yelling at several fans as the boos rain down. Golem does the throat-slice gesture at McMillan before stretching a few times by the ropes)
TONY ROSS: "Golem endearing himself to everybody here in Madison tonight, and McMillan doesn't look impressed! McMillan is still not 100% so it will be interesting to see how he handles this match with Golem! Golem is smaller than McMillan's last opponent Hawk McDaniel, but Golem is one of the most evil and despicable men in wrestling, as well as incredibly sadistic!"
RICK WISEMAN: "McMillan needs to play catch-as-catch-can here and stay away from Golem's offense, because we don't know how much more McMillan can take. He took quite a beating last week but came out on top."
T.R.: "And Golem rushes him for the attack! Big rights and lefts and some clubbing forearms tothe back! Golem knees McMillan in the face and then rakes his eyes! He pushes McMillan into the corner and starts biting his forehead! Al Marinaro wants a break pronto but Golem keeps gnashing away! Marinaro pushes Golem off and Golem pushes back, and Marinaro pushes Golem right down!! The fans erupt in approval and Golem is right in his face! MCMillan rolls him up from behind!! 1....2....Kicked out!!"
R.W.: "These guys have got to understand that these referees aren't going to take any of their crap! Scott Malec hired these larger and tougher referees to avoid any of them being pushovers or getting hurt during matches, and Golem found out the hard way, almost costing him the match!"
T.R.: "Golem right back at McMillan with some kicks to the stomach and he runs McMillan head first into the turnbuckles! Golem sits on the top turnbuckle and was going to leap off but Macmillan grabs him off out of nowhere and hits a nice sit-out powerbomb!! McMillan is a bit too shaken up to capitalize!"
R.W.: "We saw this at First Wave where McMillan would just hit moves out of nowhere! He is very unpredictable as well as very resilient for a smaller man!"
T.R.: "Golem by no means is a large wrestler but he has a 50 pound advantage over McMillan as well as big experience edge! Mcmillan pulls Golem up and hits an implant slam! He's going up top! Corkscrew splash!! He nails it!! 1....2....No!! Golem escapes! McMillan hurt himself a little bit with that move and he gets up slowly!"
R.W.: "Every part of McMillan's body has to be sore from all the beatings he's taken recently, not just here in GLCW! McMillan holds a very busy scehdule and it's going to be hard for him to absorb a lot of punishment!"
T.R.: "Whoever wins this match will go on to face the winner of the main event, Jobber vs. Angel Castillo! McMillan pulls Golem up, climbs out on the apron, vaults and hits a springboard missle dropkick!! Golem does a full flip backwards from the impact! McMillan is over and he pulls Golem up and Golem pokes him right in the eye to buy himself a few moments!"
R.W.: "As much as I disagree with it, its a very effective way to stop an oncoming opponent and Golem will not hesitate to do it!"
T.R.: "Golem hooks him and hits a full nelson face slam!! Golem gets on top and begins pounding away at the head of McMillan! McMillan tries to protect himself as Marinaro warns Golem about the closed fists! Golem pulls McMillan up by the hair and whips him in! McMillan runs right into a discus elbow! Golem grabs him and pulls him up for a powerbomb but instead he throws him back onto the ropes into a stun gun type move!! Wow!!"
R.W.: "That was quite an original and effective move by Golem as McMillan is gagging for air here after having his neck snapped on the ropes!"
T.R.: "Golem slowly stalks him and kicks him in the ribs a few times to knock more wind out of McMillan! Golem drags McMillan to his feet and hits a half nelson suplex!! He rolls over and covers McMillan!! 1.....2.....Kickout!! He almost had him!"
R.W.: "Golem didn't hook the leg there and that might have been the difference in getting a pin there, because McMillan by no means kicked out with authority! He is having trouble breathing here!"
T.R.: "Golem jaws with Marinaro a bit about the count before pulling McMillan up and he whips him into the corner! Golem rushes in but McMillan barely gets out of the way and Golem goes crashing shoulder-first into the ring post!! McMillan shakes off the cobwebs and hits Golem with a shoulderbreaker and he slaps on a quick armbar just like that! And now Golem is in trouble as he yells out in pain!"
R.W.: "Golem hit that ring post with tremendous velocity and McMillan has the presence of mind to go right to the shoulder like a shark smelling blood! McMillan turns the tide yet again!"
T.R.: "These fans in Madison are fully behind McMillan here as he really applies the pressure on Golem's arm and shoulder! McMillan holds the arm as he stands up and kicks Golem's shoulder a few times and then he drops an elbow right onto the bicep before wrenching that armbar back on! But Golem is able to throw a leg over the ropes as he worked himself close enough to reach! McMillan is forced to break the hold! Golem rolls out of the ring!"
R.W.: "Smart move by Golem to regain some composure and catch a breather for a few moments! But McMillan may not allow it!! Look out!!"
T.R.: "McMillan just leapt over the ropes with a plancha but Golem got out of the way and McMillan hit the floor with a sickening thud!! Golem staggers over and drags McMillan to his feet and oh no!!! He just DDT Chris McMillan on the floor!! McMillans head smacks the concrete and he has got to be knocked out from that vicious move! Golem just doesn't care!"
R.W.: "We may need some EMTs after that one!" T.R.: "Golem rolls McMillan into the ring and let's hope he puts an end to it without furthering the damage! He picks McMillan up and hits the Backdrop Driver and this is academic!! 1....2....3!! Golem gets the win after that brutal DDT on the floor!!"
R.W.: "And now he's got the Claw in McMillan's mouth!! Golem is trying to claim another victim here! Golem shoves Marinaro back as he won't let go"
T.R.: "The fans erupt as Troy Martinez comes charging to the ring and he pulls Golem off and they begin exchanging rights and lefts!! Martinez gets the advantage with some tremendous shots and he hits the "Rabble Rouser" Death Valley Driver!! Martinez gets some revenger on Golem after Golem applied the Claw to him last week! The fans are on their feet here!"
R.W.: "We already saw Martinez on the warpath earlier tonight against Dean McQueen, and now he continues his rampage here as he leaves Golem laying in the middle of the ring! This could also be Martinez paying McMillan back for running out last week and stopping the Claw!"
T.R.: "Martinez exits the ring to the cheers as several EMTs check on McMillan, who's neck might be broken for all we know after that vicious DDT on the floor! He is moving so that is a very good sign! They lift McMillan up to a standing position and it looks like he is OK as the fans applaud, but he is really hurting! The EMTs help him out of the ring!"
R.W.: "Even though Troy Martinez already hit the 'Rabble Rouser' on Golem, McMillan I'm sure will want his own revenger eventually!"
T.R.: "Let's take it back to Lady Veronica!" (CUT-TO: Lady Veronica with "El Diablo" Angel Castillo, Lady veronica looking into the camera but El Diablo's stare fizated on Lady V's behind)
LADY VERONICA: "So, We are here with prospective competitor "El Diablo" Angel Castillo. He's booked in tonight's main event against tried and true veteran The Jobber. What are your feelings going into this match, Angel?"
ANGEL CASTILLO: (Still looking at Lady V's behind, but then snaps back into attention) "Huh? What? Oh, yeah, The Jobber. Well, The Jobber, he likes to believe that he is someone who is untouchable. He seems to believe I am nothing to be concerned about, and he..."
(Sly Sterling walks in and steps between the microphone and Castillo.) SLY STERLING: "Allllowwww me to introduce myself, my darling. I am Sly Sterling, manager of this here fine specimen of a man standing behind me. Now, to The Jobber, I have only one thing to say, if you want to mess with "El Diablo", The GENERALISSIMO of the latin explosion and the future of all that is GLCW, then just prepare to be royally trounced by the huracán-like power of the man they call "El Diablo". You're only lucky, by god you are lucky, to be in the main event with a star of this caliber, of this SHEER MAAAAAGNITUDE. So if you can keep your eyes open long enough during the match, despite the sheer FEAR that overcomes you, to see who it is that's beating the anglo-saxon out of you, I guarantee you will see the face...of Angel Castillo."
(Sterling walks away almost like nothing happened, El Diablo left somewhat surprised.) Castillo: Ummm...yeah!" LADY V: "Fans, we will be back after this message with the Main Event here on GLCW Riptide!"
(Lady V pushes Castillo away who is still staring at her rear-end. Fade to commercial)
