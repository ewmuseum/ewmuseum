TV Title Match: Jarod Poe vs. Sean Edmunds

25 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
TBirdSCIL — 25 February 2003 Please keep all RP for this match in this folder... TheOriginalSE — 1 March 2003 ((FADEIN: 'Simply Sensational' Sean Edmunds is standing on the side of a road, apparently waiting for his ride. He stands there, with a few bags in one hand, and a duffle bag strapped on his back.))
EDMUNDS: "And so its finally happened. I finally get the rematch for the Television title. But unfortunately its not against the man whom I really wanted a go at. It's not against the Jobber. Jarod Poe. (smiles) I remember our last encounter. It must have been really infuriating to get counted out .. especially when you had such a weak victim at the time. You had me, Jarod. I was still recuperating from by far the biggest beating that I've ever taken.. and you still could not put me away. How did that make you feel, Jarod? (pauses) I know the answer already, you don't need to say it. Because after you're loss to me you went on and won the GLCW Television title. I bet you pretty much forgot about how lame you were in our match. (smiles) That is, until you looked at your title defense this upcoming card and saw that 'Simply Sensational' will be staring back at you. And now you've got something to prove. Not to the fans, not to the management, but to yourself. And to me. You see, Jarod, this isn't just another match for me. I was knocked down off my pedastal at Massive Assault, I was kicked back down just the other night in that huge four way elimination match. But now I've got a glimmer of hope again. I know what I can do to start to restore, at least in my own mind, the beliefs I once held. And that all begins with you. And that Television title."
((A Lexus pulls up. A guy in a chauffer suit gets out and pops the trunk. He takes Edmunds' bags and puts them in the trunk. He opens the door for Edmunds and Edmunds gets in. CUTTO: Inside the car as its driving.))
EDMUNDS: "I'm waiting for this, Jarod. I'm waiting with great anticipation. The Television title will be a nice addition to my collection. Great Lakes Championship Wrestling needs someone like 'Simply Sensational' to be their Television champion. I mean, just look at who we have as the Heavyweight champion for crying out loud! Maelstrom?! I think that what the fans need is a REAL champion. And 'Simply Sensational' is going to give them just that champion. Jarod, you can have your sensei tell you everything he knows about me. You can ask him for ways to block whatever I might throw at you. But the one thing you won't have in that ring is experience. Sure we've been in one match together, but that's not enough to map out ways to get around me. And sure, your sensei has plenty of ways, but all of his ways have come in the ring, because he has plenty of experience with me. He can spot moves before I do them. You don't have that experience, Jarod. You can watch all the tape you can get. You can study the lists all you want, but the fact is, once I get in that ring, you're not going to be observing anymore. You'll be right in the thick of things, and you're going to be over your head. I admire you, Jarod, I really do. I admire the way that you handle your matches, and I admire the ruthlessness that you have. But thats about all you'll get from me, Jarod. Admiration. And you certaintly won't get anything more. Especially not my shoulders on the mat for the three count, or me submitting in the ring. Those days are over, Jarod."
((Edmunds looks out the window.)) EDMUNDS: "You had your chance when I was feeble and ready to be picked off, Jarod. What are you going to do now that I'm stronger, better, and more ready to handle whatever you throw at me? (turns to camera) What are you going to do?"
((FADEOUT)) Devil666 — 2 March 2003 (Outside fogged-up windows the world passes by. The snowy landscape was the same when the bus left Michigan and not much has changed on the way to Milwaukeee. Miles have gone by, but the white snow continues. From the window Jarod Poe looks out into the bleak winter night. His thoughts focused on his upcoming match and mouth watering for a bar with good sausagenot too mnetion a cold beer. It's a little past eight and if everything stays on schedule the bus should reach it's destination in fifeteen to twenty minutes. Jarod will be in town a few days early which he usually hates, however Milwaukee offers it's own set of distractions and he'll find a way to suffer through it. looking around heand the camera crew make up hald of the passengers on board. Not liking crowds Jarod is relaxed more as he turns sideways and faces the camera)
Poe: Ah, Sean. So we meet again. Two wrestlers stepping into the ring heading on completly different paths. After all I've been on quite a hot streak as of late. I've put the Masked Blazer out of his misery, beating the Jobber...something you could not do and I even showed Larry Tact just what kind of wrestler I'm capable of. Hell I even proved how big of a hypocrite the man is. ah, but the one small blemish as of late is the track meet the two of us had. that night in {pauses} what was it Cleveland when you proved your head was broken, but your legs worked just fine. A night you ran like a child, but are almost proud to claim a mighty victory. {laughs} I guess if I had been on the same path as you are as of late...well I'd take what little glory I could. {laughs} After all Sean...you've far from liven up to the claim of being "Simply Sensational" as of lare. {grins} Much more along the lines of simple then remotley being sensational. After all you quit against {cringes} Jean Rebesque and then you followed it up with a less then steallar performance in that four-way. Ah, but you can at least admit that. Now Sean you get a shot...you get a shot at the GLCW TV Title. It's a belt that I own amd quite honestly Sean {laughs} it's a belt you badly need to win.
(Jarod reaches down tot eh bag at his feet and slowly unzips it. He slowly pulls out the TV title is displays it before the camera. He taps it with his hand and smiles)
Poe: Is this what you need Sean?...this what you want?...this your glimmer of hope? {grins} Well good Sean. I'm glad we have that made clear. You see Sean I'm not happy about our last meeting. I'm not happy you got a cheap win. Ah, but Sean something is going to have to be different this time around. See last time Sean I got my hands on you and sent you into panic mode when that bandage came off. You ran...didn't live up to that false promise you made about giving me a fight. Well you going to have to this time Sean. Your going to have to give me a fight {taps the belts} if you want to walk away with this. My only question is Sean...why should I beleive you this time? Why should I shake in fear at a threat from Sean Edmunds when you quite frankly have no ground to stand on. The past two few months speak for themselves Sean and you the last man who should be making threats. {laughs] Ah, but Sean I have a question for you. this time..this time when we're in the ring. When I have you in my grasp and the pain is starting to mount. Well Sean what will you do...will you stand and fight...or Sean just like last time. Will you turn tail and run. {taps the belt again} If you want this Sean yuot going to have to FIGHT for it and Sean...well I just don't think your up for that.
(The bus comes to a stop having reached it's destination. Jarod tosses the strap over his shoulder and makes his way outside. The cold winter air is like a hard slap in the face. Jarod turns back to the camera a smile on his face as he taps the the belt once more. As the words come from his mouth they form a cloud in the air)
Poe: Cold out here Sean...cold like me. Admiration...Admiration from Sean Edmunds. Well I tihnk that's the last thing I would get if you were "Simply Sensational" as you calim to be. The truth is Sean...your not...and haven't been. It's all just a false claim your making...I'm half expecting you to point your thumbs to yuor shoulders when you say it. The real truth is Sean Edmunds has falling from his perch. On a downfall he's nto quite sure when it will stop. Well Sean it won't stop when you get in the ring with me. See Sean I didn't need my Sensei to defeat the Jobber...I didn't need him to defeat the Masked Blazer...i didn't need him to get Larry Tact's attention. I did this all on my own and I'll do the same thing when I defeat {points to the camera} You!!! Save your threats for someone who will listen...save your promises for the only person who hears them...yourself. Sean you need to be very careful You've fallen quite far and this time when yuo stand and fight. Which you will HAVE to do if you want the GLCW TV Title. Well Sean just like the others have I'll send you down in flames. I'll take whatever you've been given out the past few months and giving back like all the others have upon you. I'm going to defeat "Simple" Sean Edmunds and use you as a lauching pad for my shot at the GLCW World Title. See Sean you had your turn and now your desperate for what I have. Well you can't have it Sean...you can't. Your going to have to fight, claw and scratch for what I have and Sean {spits} I jsut don't think you have it. {pats the title on his shoulder} You want this Sean...FIGHT ME this time. However I warn you Sean...if all your going to do is run and hide. Then pray Sean...PRAY that I don't catch up with you. Now if you don't mind Sean...I'm getting cold out here. I'll see you in the ring. Bring your admiration Sean...I'll turn it into desperation.
(Jarod turns his back tot eh camera and walks towards the bus station. He knows a hot meal and a cold beer are not far away. He also knows the warm blood of Sean Edmunds is ready to be spilled and the thought warms him to the core)
