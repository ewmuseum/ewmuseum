TBirdSCIL — 1 February 2003 All RP for this match goes here... ChrisHorowitz — 6 February 2003 (Cue up "Dogs" by Pink Floyd) (Fade into the Whitelandia Compound of the Jobber, cut to the living room, where guess what, he's sitting on his couch with his television title.)
The Jobber: So here we are Jared Poe, once again scheduled as each others dance partner. I would have thought you would have learned your lesson after our last encounter. You were carried from the ring a charred corpse, looking like you had been in an explosion instead of a wrestling match. Yet once again you are back to challenge me. I'm not sure if this is bravery or stupidity, or perhaps a bit of both.
I had hoped to hear from you, honestly. After Maelstrom it's almost interesting to listen to you. But I guess you couldn't find another tatoo parlor who's needles didn't contain HIV to film in and show how hard and tough you are. At this point, will any of your words really make a difference? Is there anything you can say to me to make you appear as a threat to my title?
I'm sure you've ramped up your intensity, and doubled your efforts to push the limit, and all that jazz, but come on man you are the laughing stock of GLCW. I can't even go out there and tell my opponents how I destroyed you in two straight matches without being laughed at. Last time we fought for the Television title, this time you're fighting to try and reclaim your honor. Sadly that will not be happening Poe, and you will have to return to the dirty tatoo parlors and have Gus give you a brand new Kermit the Frog on your lower back.
But is it all darkness for you here in GLCW? Is this failure and suffering just like all the pain and suffering you've endured in your life, being sent to bed without supper, your parents buying you the GI Joe base instead of the aircraft carrier, are your losses, your humiliations just more of what you claim makes you stronger? You'd think after so many failures, it'd actually show up in your record. But yet after such a miserable stint here, you are still shoved down our throats as I said you would be. Those in the championship committee still see value in a big tough tatooed freak, even if he can't beat any of the top tier opponents he has faced.
It can't be fun to listen to the truth Poe, it must be making you angry, I'm sure it'll be evident in your claims of how angry and intense you'll be in the ring, how you will overcome an opponent who has embarassed you so many times, and I'm sure even you won't believe a word of it at this point.
So Poe, please do come on the air and tell me why you won't choke once again. Please tell me why you are any different than the others who have come after my title. Tell me why I won't vanquish you as I have all the others, when you haven't been able to deal with half of them. But while you are telling me all of this, just stop and explain one thing to me, because it's been weighing on my mind.
What have you done to deserve this shot? Most people in this sport don't get a second chance, yet here you are with a third try to become Television champion. This all might sound oh so arrogant, but Poe I've proven that I can beat you, so I think that I deserve the chance to sound arrogant while talking about you. All I ask is that you somehow explain to me why you deserve this, and why you deserve to be something more than an injoke between all of your opponents. Let all these questions fill your mind my lad, and they'll remove any of that confidence or intensity you may have left.

I could care less about the TV Title Jobber, but I'm more then willing to add your name to the resume. You look in the mirrior tonight Jobber...see all the cracks in your logic. I know the truth and this time Jobber...this time you won't be able to handle the truth.

(Fade into the Jobber's Whitelandia compound. Cut to the living room where the Jobber as usual sits on his couch, GLCW Television title by his side.)
The Jobber: Jared Poe, I grow weary of this seemingly endless dance. I'm tired. I'm sick and tired of listening to you, sick and tired of fighting you, and sick and tired of saying why I'm oh so much better than you.
I'm sick of the "I do not feel pain and I do not know fear" attitude. I'm tired of you acting like you are a threat for the World title, let alone my Television title. Since the GLCW Championship committee thinks that all I am good for is defending this title against the same peons, I'm going to have to do something I'd rather not, because I will not have my name and my reputation undermined by a bunch of suits who think that if they give Jared Poe 50,000 shots at my title he'll eventually win and become some kind of a tatooed painless star. Poe, if I do not leave the arena as GLCW Television Champion after our match I will not return to this federation. You will not see my face again. If I triumph, I do not expect you to do the same as you obviously do not abide by the same code of honor. In the past you have shown you will accept a win any way you can get it. Look how the championship committee has railroaded me int he past, having me disqualifed for any little thing just so you can save face.
You brag about that little video clip you showed? You act as if that farce is some proof that I was bettered by you?
Then you tell me to prepare? For what I'm not sure. You've already been proven to be a liar, a fake. We've seen you are just like everyone else.
Yet you keep claiming that no one knows you. You claim you are misunderstood. Poor baby. You're just so deep, your lifestyle is so complicated that no one can understand you. I guess me saying this in a mocking tone puts you one step closer to the edge, and about to break... Or something out of some angsty teenage rock music.
So many times you've said you are pushing yourself to the limit. You're pushing the envelope, and each time it isn't enough. Each time you fail. You can tell me how I will eat the Television title, or how you will destroy me in some other manner that doesn't involve deep throating a gold belt. But Poe, does anyone take you seriously?
Of course not! They all want you to go away, like I do. We're tired of the hype. We're tired of the talk, we simply want you to leave because it's obvious you will never amount to anything on your own. You'll just keep getting shoved down our throats in hope that eventually you'll get lucky and the committee will have their tatooed angsty monster.
But of course you just can't understand. The reason I'm sick of you is I don't like being the second banana. I'm sick of facing people like you, Castillo, Golem and Edmunds. Golem is the same as you, getting more shots he doesn't deserve except on the World title scale.
I crushed Golem, and now Poe, I frankly don't see you as a threat. And if somehow, somehow you beat me, well then no one in this federation will see my face again.
