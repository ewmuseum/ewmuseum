GLCW — Roleplay

Jared Justice vs. Nikolai Ash

15 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
TBirdSCIL — 15 February 2003 Please keep all RP for this match in this folder... [name withheld] — 18 February 2003 (fade in: Nikolai ash is sitting in his darkly lit library. the aura coming off of him is rank, and violent, the anger oozes out of his pours and sends a fowl mood throughout the air. Ash looks grimy, but not affected by this matter, looking depressed and volatile he stares blankly into the camera.)
...alas, I am back... back from the time I spent with another, in the midst of a conflict between another pair of men that were a debauchery amongst the human race and didn't deserve to see the light of day anymore. but what can I do? I am but a simple man, with no liability.. no faux ideals, only true goals and accomplishments under my belt...
::Ash sighs:: So here I am again in the middle of another match, another card.. on another cold Midwest day.. awaiting my so-called "destruction" by another opponent, who thinks he's worthy... yet worthy of what? worthy of beating a battered and swollen man into submission only to make themselves believe they are bringing JUSTICE to the world?! JUSTICE? Ha.. isn't that just a fallacy... in the most unoriginal way. What kind of justice does more pain bring? This world is based solely around the anger of others.. small men with supposed big hearts ready to take on the world and battle against the foes that have been set in front of them... for JUSTICE TO PREVAIL THEY MUST?!!
..justice...for all... but none for me. I have no contempt for you, so have none for me. What happens to you, can happen to me.. but what is to happen no one knows, nor cares if your body is the end up in the Ohio river swelling, filling with hemoglobin, and as frigid and stale as a piece of meat left to thaw in the kitchen sink.
(fade out) YinYangGuy — 20 February 2003 (FADE IN: Jared Justice is sitting in a darkened room lit only by candles. CUE UP: "Firestarter (Empirion Mix)" by The Prodigy.)
JARED JUSTICE: It is rather comical how some people hear my name and then suddenly jump to conclusions. Nikolai Ash is under the false impression that I am a man seeking Justice.
(Jared Justice's eyes grow big and he looks even more maniacal than usual.) JARED JUSTICE: Do I look like a judge....jury?? I'm neither. I'm merely the EXECUTIONER. I do not stand for TRUTH, JUSTICE, nor the American Way. My mission in the GLCW does not involve bringing the guilty to some idealistic justice.....However, they will repent. Ash, have you not been paying attention these last several weeks? I'M THE FIRESTARTER.....TWISTED FIRESTARTER!!!! There are no faux ideals here. You spew all of these big words trying to impress people. Perhaps you want to set yourself apart from the ordinary riff-raff that frequents the GLCW. Whatever the case may be is of little consequence to me. I'm glad your aren't going to be one to preach to me about the fine art of technical wrestling, I have heard enough of that from Larry Tact. Now, I gave him to choices going into our match. I suggested to him that he shouldn't make the mistake of making the match into something personal....he chose to talk about Parkinson's Disease for some odd reason. I have yet to make any suggestions to you, but you've apparently already decided. You stated that you harbor no contempt for me. That's nice. Really it is. Then you referred to the Ohio River and myself swimming in it in a rather unflattering manner. Well, I'm not sure whether to take that as a threat or a promise. I do know however, that I hold no contempt for you. You will be set ablaze though. Nothing personal, just BUSINESS. See, burning people is my business and right about now business is good. I'm getting paid a hefty amount to make sure that Michael Manson's career goes up in smoke and it will!! However, I have the opportunity to take a few extra victims along the way. It's kind of like an added bonus. You hold no contempt for me??? I've already decided that I don't like you. Maybe it's your demeanor....maybe it's because you've made assumptions about me that just aren't true. Maybe it's just because you are ugly and ignorant.....a buffoon!! I hate buffoons!!! Your existence seems to be a pathetic one, so let me end it for you. JUSTICE??? Do you want JUSTICE???? I'll bring you fire.....FIRE!! You won't be judged. I'm just going to BURN you for the hell of it!!!
(FADE OUT) [name withheld] — 23 February 2003 (fade in: Ash stands facing the camera with a smile upon his face, but a smile of a grim, lonely man.)
...whom is one to cast the first stone? not I, not I says the strong willed man, for is it you? I bet it is, for it isn't me.. dont you see? your name was not layed upon this tongue in vain, it was merly used to perplex the senses into thinking a word was used to accentuate your wrongdoings... no such idea is farther from the truth, do you believe? Well I suppose you do not for you are from another linear universe, looking to find the parallel relay to jump to another and destory the other.
A judge, a jury, an executioner? Far be it for me to lay a name upon you, it isn't me who is meant to do so. You are the character in my life... the play is out there, you are merely portaying a role you want. Did I pick it for you? Most definently... Not, the one who did it.. but did the outside world, possibly. But you do refer to yourself as the executioner.. so hip hip.. hooray is it? Or will I be thrown out for contempt.. and set down on the guiltine for my ultimate demise? Tell me. I mean, in your world I have the chance to repent before my skull is crushed in the cold sharp steel grasp of the blade and strewn out on the pavement. Do you agree in your statement?
..justice, if I may refer to you in your surname. I have not been paying attention the last few weeks, my mind has been else where but is here now on you for the time being. Anyways, you tell me not to guess you or judge you.. yet you do the same to me? My words aren't meant to impress just accentuate my rhetoric. As deeply sorry as I am that you are unable to do the same, I am not as forgiving as you'd think. But I do forgive you for your inability to stay on the topic of me for more than a few moments, but I suppose "ADD" is a disease rampant in the populous. But what do I know.. I'm quite ugly, ignorant, and a buffoon.
::sighs:: Yet this buffoon seems to have a high bid upon his head for all the work he's done so far, yet the only price upon your head is for you to be removed due to your lack of sense. But... what do I know? I'm not as "HOT" as you.
(fades)
