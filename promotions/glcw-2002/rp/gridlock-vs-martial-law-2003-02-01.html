Gridlock vs. Martial Law

1 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
TBirdSCIL — 1 February 2003 All RP for this match goes here... RYoungblood — 1 February 2003 (Fade in to a taxi cab in Tower City, the Center of Cleveland, Ohio. The cab is in a traffic jam. Horns are echoing throughout Public Square.)
Ryan Youngblood: Goddamn it. I hate this freaking Gridlock! Cabbie:(with an Indian Accent) Sorry Ser. I can not control de trafik! Steve Slayer: Dude, calm down. He's not blaming you. RYB: Steve, Abbul.. I'm not talking about the traffic!! (camera zooms in to see that he's looking at the GLCW Promo Photo of the Tag Team "Gridlock".
Abbul: How deh hell do you know mai name? RYB: I looked at your license. Abbul: Oh. SS: God, I love this town. It's so great we've finally come back here. RYB: Yea.. We haven't partied here since October 23rd Ninteen-- (Both of them say in unison) RYB & SS: NINTY NINE! RYB: But, lets thank the Pres. for hookin us up with a great hotel too.. Man, I feel like a king here at the Renaissance.
(They finally get out of the cab, and are met by a fairly large african american gentleman)
Bellboy: Hey Buddies! Welcome to the Renaissance Cleveland! Let me take your bags and let you get settled.
(They walk into the hotel and immediately get swarmed by fans wanting autographs and pictures..)
SS: Hey guys. We're just checking in, you mind waiting fo... RYB: What the hell you talking about? Refusing to sign for CLEVELAND FANS? They're the greatest of all fans. Go Tribe!
(Fans go crazy. Ryan opens his jacket to reveal a C.C. Sabathia Jersey. Steve does the same, except he's wearing an outdated Jim Thome jersey.. The fans boo for a little bit.)
SS: Hey, we're originally from Philly. I like the guy. Fan: Ah, you're alright! I just wouldn't walk around Tower City with that on, especially near Jacobs..
RYB: Alrighty guys, lets go to the Lobby so we're out of everyones way. (They sign for a little while, after they get tired they realize they've signed for everyone, and finally check in.)
SS: Phew, I didn't expect that warm of a welcome on such a cold day. RYB: Pff, yea tell me about it. I love this place to death but everytime I come back it seems to get colder and colder.
(They go back to the Lobby and have a seat near the giant marble fountain. In the distance a piano player is playing some Beethoven music.)
SS: This whole time, we haven't really said much about Gridlock.. RYB: Gridlock? What's to be said. They're just a stepping stone to us. Before we know it we'll be moving on to bigger and better things. But for now, I guess we'll be big fish in a small pond..
SS: It's not going to take long for the pres and company to realize how bright of a future we have here.
RYB: This will be our second match here in GLCW. We're so fortunate to be in Cleveland for it.
SS: Listen to us, we love this place- it sounds like we were born here. RYB: Well lets put it this way- Cuyahoga County has some new Comrades. SS: Yeah, Gridlock.. With the city of the Tribe behind us, we're unstopable. And when I hit either one of you with the license to kill- there'll be no comin back for ya.
RYB: And for the one that is fortunate(laughs) enough to not be on the receiveing end of a License to Kill.. There's going to a blood drive with your name on it. When this match is over, Gridlock, the word pain is going to have a whole new meaning..
(Both men laugh as the camera fades out) ShawnHartXXX — 6 February 2003 [updated:LAST EDITED ON Feb-06-03 AT 02:01&nbsp;PM (EST)](Fade In: To a state of the art gymnaseum somewhere in the suburbs of Cleveland. The pool area to be exact. In that pool area, relaxing in the hot tub, is none other than GLCW managerial success story, Sly Sterling. As he sits there, alone in the spa, he runs a hand through his hair, lays back, and smiles approvingly. He then glances up, towards the camera, and begisn to speak.)
Sterling: "Déjà Vu... the illusion of remembering an event that is ACTUALLY being experienced for the first time. A feeling that you've heard or seen something once before. And ya know, in watching that opening bit from the guys that like to call themselves Martial Law, I can't help but wonder about this whole déjà vu concept. Here we have, yet ANOTHER pair of overconfident, overachieving hot shots labeling my boys, Raze and Nuke of the Gridlock crew, a stepping stone. And what else? After Gridlock you'll be on to bigger and better things? YOU TWO are the big fish in a small pond? I could continue to dissect your grossly ignorant misconceptions, but for the sake of conducting things in a business-like manner, how 'bout I cut to the chase? Because the way I see it, Mr. Youngblood... Mr. Slayer, if somebody doesn't present you with that sorely needed reality check, and do so in a timely fashion, the both of you are going to drown in this so-called 'small pond'..courtesy of Da Grid!"
(As Sly finishes his sentence, the scream, "Cannonbaaaaaaaaaall!!" is heard in the background, followed by a thunderous splash. Suddenly, Sterling is drenched by the after-effects of an overweight diver in the nearby pool. He coughs up some water and rubs his eyes as he grimaces, then looks up towards the man in the pool.)
Sterling: "DON'T splash me AGAIN!! I HATE being splashed!! You do that just ONCE MORE and I'll cannonball my FIST down your THROAT!!"
(Sly closes his eyes, and counts to ten under his breath. He then licks his lips, opens his eyes, smiles, then looks back into the camera.)
Sterling: "Geeeeeeeetting back to the point at hand, Martial Law, the two of you might be asking yourselves, "If Gridlock's so tough, why are you doing all the talking for them?" And while, in your simple, hazy minds, that may seem like the proper thing to ask... rest assured, we have our reasons. You see, while you two are trouncing about with a cabby, reciting Prince lyrics, and playing around with the fans in hotel lobbies, GRIDLOCK continues their training regime. Because that's what these guys are all about. The matches, the fights, physcially DOMINATING each and every windbag that comes their way. And so, each and every day, all day long, they prepare. I mean really, why the hell do you think I'm at the gym!! I obviously don't need to tone down.."
(As Sly rests in the hot tub, the water jets cause his semi-flabby, bat-wing arms to jiggle.)
Sterling: "So, as you parade around like a couple of tourists, just remember... Raze and Nuke are here. Fine-tuning the well-oiled machine called Gridlock! I'd like to hope you're taking this in, so when the time comes for Riptide, my guys can actually have a decent match, but from the looks of things, it's just gonna be another squash. Ah well, such is the life of the BIG FISH!!"
(Suddenly, Raze and Nuke dash onto the scene in swim gear. Before Sly even realizes what's happening, both man plunge wildly into the hot tub, drenching their manager!)
Raze: "Sly! You're still here!! It's a good thing too, because GRIDLOCK has something to say to MARTIAL LAW!! *he looks into the cam* That's right, I've seen you guys around, CHUMPING OUT for the fans, giving out plugs for this crappy town and it's PATHETIC baseball team! How many games under .500 were they?! But then again, who else would a couple of LOSERS cheer than a LOSER team from a LOSER town. But hey, I guess that's just your style. It will be at Riptide, I cann tell you that much.."
(A furious Mr. Sterling interjects...) Sterling: "You idiots splashed me!! Dammit!! *he turns his attention back to the task at hand* Ryan Youngblood, Steve Slayer, the bottom line is this... The both of you are tossing out garbage when you talk about running over Gridlock. Stepping stone?? STEPPING STONE you say?! Raze and Nuke are more like an avalanche, my friends. And at Riptide, this avalanche will CRUSH YOU!! The only thing the two of you have to look forward to is the long, dusty road home to lick your wounds. Heh, in other words... it's like déjà vu, aaaaaaaaaall over again. 'Til next time boys..."
Fade Out RYoungblood — 11 February 2003 (Camera fades into the Tower City mall, where you see a movie theatre that isn't very busy, it's clear through the window that its late- around ten o'clock. Most of the stores are closed or closing and the only lights on in the mall are the lights in the fountain which Ryan Youngblood and Steve Slayer are positioned in front of.)
Steve Slayer: <laughs> Nice to hear from you Sterling. And I'm glad that Gridlock has representation. Because after your match with Martial Law, you're not going to be able to act for yourselves-- <laughing> Let alone wrestle again!
Ryan Youngblood: It's good to see you're getting some training too.. Because, from these match tapes I've been watching-- you guys give this place a bad name. You don't always need to be in the gym to be a good wrestler. We were in the hotel weight room for over two hours today-- and yesterday, hell everyday. But we don't brag about that-- because we don't have to. Come Riptide you two will know why.
SS: And you, Sly, I recommend you stay backstage for this massacre. And any children coming-- this match won't be for the squeamish. I'd suggest you turn your heads for the 2 minutes we make waste of this Gridlock.
RYB: Anyway, it's been a pretty busy week for Cleveland's favorite tag-team! It feels good to just rest though. This mall is great too! On the third floor theres a Hard Rock cafe. Real great times. Lets hope the GLCW returns to Cleveland soon.
SS: I can't believe we actually heard from an actual wrestler. Raze, I'm glad you took out your "English For Dummies" book and learned how to piece together a few sentences. It also means a lot that you took some valuable time out of your "grueling workout." Sadly, your work won't be good enough. No matter how long or how hard you train- neither of you will be able to measure up to the magnitude of Martial Law.
RYB: Train if you want, but it's futile. Gridlock, when this match ends, and all the smoke is cleared- the word "pain" will have a whole new meaning.. Until Riptide..
[b](Camera Fades Out) ShawnHartXXX — 13 February 2003 (Fade In: To the Great Lakes Championship Wrestling logo as the scene opens at a television studio in downtown Cleveland. As the camera locks in on the logo, one half of the tag team sensation Gridlock, the man known as Raze, steps out onto the shot and stands imposingly in front of the logo. He then tugs on his beard, cracks his neck from side to side, and begins...)
Raze: "Y'know... normally when me n' my main man Nuke are booked in these fights, we let Sly do all the talking. That's just the way it goes. He likes to vocalize, and we like to pulverize! But after watching those two ham n' eggers Martial Law shoot their traps, I feel inclined to step up to the plate. Ryan Youngblood... Stevie Slayer, I saw you boys laughin' at us. What was it that you said? We can't wrestle? When you're through with us we won't be able to act for ourselves? Oh, and my personal favorite... yer gonna make waste of us in two minutes, as well!? Mighty ambitious of you to make those kinds of claims, but I've gotta admit... I admire your courage. You damned right! Hell, to get ahead in any walk of life, you have to have that will to succeed, and from the looks of it, you guys've got it. Hooooowever, there comes a point where ambition stops, and losing touch with reality starts, and you, my friends, have crossed that line! And, unfortunately for the two of you, yer now in hostile territory!
Mr. Slayer, I bet you think you're real clever comin' at me with that English for Dummies crack. But let me tell you, if you don't know anything else... know this... you won't be laughin' when I've got these hands around your neck! You know, it's almost funny that you call yourselves Martial Law. Cuz ever since day one here in GLCW, Raze, Nuke, and Sly Sterling have had the run of this place! Setting the standard through brute force and unbridled agression! You can toss out that 2 hours a day bull crap all ya want... this stuff is our whole life!! We're at it 24/7! Gridlock LIVES to bring two-bit PUNKS like you to their knees! And come Riptide, the two of YOU.. will know why! I hope you really enjoyed the mall and the Hard Rock Cafe. Cuz when Gridlock is through with you... you probably won't even remember it! I'm sick to death of little b(FCC)stards like you writing us off when yer nothin'!! So when Riptide rolls around, I'm making it my personal mission to make sure that each and every one of you start gettin' the message!"
(Raze turns around, grabs the GLCW logo which hangs behind him, and pulls it violently off it's wires! He then lets lout a beast-like cry and hurls it angrily to the ground.)
Raze: "Make no mistake about it, Gridlock is here.. and we're gonna SMASH each and every pair of Ken dolls that comes our way! Martial Law, you're no different! You talk about the meaning of the word pain? You haven't FELT pain until you've gone toe to toe with da Grid! I may not be Bill Shakespeare on the stick, but I think my cannon arm knockin' you on your ass will speak for itself. Uuuuuuntil Riptide..."
(Raze smiles as he steps off and we fade to black.)
