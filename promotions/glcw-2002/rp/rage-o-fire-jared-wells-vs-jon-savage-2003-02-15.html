GLCW — Roleplay

"Rage o' Fire" Jared Wells vs. Jon Savage

15 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
TBirdSCIL — 15 February 2003 Please keep all RP for this match in this folder... [name withheld] — 15 February 2003 (...Camera opens up to a front shot of the Resch Center in Green Bay Wisconsin. Camera circles around the building then gets shots of the crowded streets. From a distances, 'Rage 'o Fire' Jared Wells walks towards the camera wearing blue jeans, tucked in polo shirt, black leather jacket with his hands in his pockets. He stops walking and looks up at the building with a smile...)
"You know they say in order to find your roots you have to go back where you started. I remember selling out crowds of 40,000 people. I remember the epic battle of Wells versus Copycat for the WWL Tri-American championship. I remember so much that I did............but now it's all in the past. I'm in a different state of mind now these days. Some people tell me I matured a lot in the past few months. Most people still believe I'm still the manipulative devious guy who only cares about himself. I'll admit my past is bad, but I'm ready to turn it all around. I might not win the guys respect in the back, but I sure as hell won my fans back. I'm so pumped up and ready to get back into the ring. Many superstars come down the line and claim they are the PEOPLE'S CHAMPION but let's face it.........I set the standards for the real PEOPLE'S CHAMPION. Blood, sweat, and tears is what I produced over the years and it's finally paying off. Whether you HATE ME or LOVE ME......You have to RESPECT ME! I love this business man and I would do anything to get back on top. I'll go off the record to say my time in the G-L-C-W, I will bust my ass to become the champion. Of course starting at the very very bottom and working my way to the top. I will chop down every tree in my way, and as long as the people are behind me.......THERE'S NO STOPPING ME!"
(...Wells begins to walk again around the Resch Center as a couple kids come up to him and ask for an autograph. Wells signs and tells them to take care as the kids run off excited...)
"That right there just made my day! But let's get onto the better part of the subject. My return at the Wisconsin show against Jon Savage. Whether there is ten people in the crowd or a thousand I will give it 110% to show why I am a great entertainer. Jon Savage I've heard of you but I don't know to much about you. My in ring work speaks for itself. Right now you have a little pain in your stomach, sweat starting to drip off your eyebrow, and you have the urge to vomit. That is called FEAR DUDE! Jon Savage, it's a normal thing before a match with me. The question I have for you buddy is, ARE YOU READY? I'm gonna be honest with you man, this is my first match back. I have over a million people waiting for this moment to happen and I can't let it slip. I've heard people tell me my time is way over. That the fire of RAGE is burnt out! MAH-MAN, I hate to tell you this but you have to be BEAT one way or the other. This old dog has plenty of new tricks BRAAHH! The RAGE-BOMB is where it all lies big man! I will hook the head, drive you through the mat six feet under, and I will put the final nail in your own coffin. And if there is anything left I'll throw you out to my fans.....because they can save you man! Oh yeah! They will give you another breath of life into your body. GREEN BAY here we are baby! My run in the GLCW will be for a long time. JON SAVAGE........Your time will come.........TIME HAS COME TO RAGGGGGEEEEEEEEE"!
(...cameras fadeout to Wells walking away...) [name withheld] — 16 February 2003 Cam fades into the home of Dr. Johnny Wildside. We see Wildside sitting in his living room, sipping on cognac. Jon Savage is also there, reading "The Wall Street Journal." They both see the camera. Wildside puts down the cognac and Savage is seen moving next to Wildside.
WILDSIDE: I have never heard a promo quiet like that before. "Rage O' Fire," eh? Well, my friend, Jon Savage is the FIRE EXTINGUISHER. And the fans? Who needs those bunch of Green Bay Bunglers. Ham'n'Eggers, that's all they are. They don't mean a thing to us...never have, never will. Tell 'em Savage....
SAVAGE: See Wells, you wanna talk about rage. You want to write checks we both know your a$$ can't cash. I too am returning to Great Lakes, but I am different than before. I am focused. I am intense. I...AM...SAVAGE! Now get that damn camera out of here!
Cam backs off quickly as Savage slams the living room door. [name withheld] — 16 February 2003 February 16th, 2003 5:54am [ - camera opens up to an empty BWI airport in Baltimore Maryland. The entire airport is shutdown due to bad snow conditions. You see 'Rage 'o Fire' walking around the empty airport waiting for a flight. The camera zooms over to his face for a few comments - ]
JARED WELLS: "Jon Savage........Mister Wall Street? Of course I don't want to forget Dr. Johnny Wildside. Kinda funny seeing you guys lay back and enjoy some drinks.........To me it's almost like your celebrating just a TAD-BIT early DON'T-CHA say? In a way I understand what your going through. When I was young and stupid, I did the same thing. But.........I'm not here to tell you what to do. You must learn from your own mistakes. You dare to question my fans? Well son, you must pay. I told everybody I was on a mission...........to become the heavyweight champion. Your are my first victim in the path of RAGE. You claim to be focused and intense? (LAUGHS) My friend, you have no idea what your getting yourself into. You see once my flight leaves here in Baltimore, I'm heading straight to the gym for some intense training. The people asked for me, the people believe in me and dammit, JON SAVAGE you will be mowed down. And for your buddy Johnny Wildside......I've seen people like you! (LAUGHS and GRINS) You come to the ring bro? (WELLS WAVES FISTS IN THE AIR) I got the graveyard.....or the hospital..........PICK ONE! John Savage......PAL.....how are you gonna put the fire out with an empty fire extinguisher? (LAUGHS THEN GETS SERIOUS)
...TIME HAS COME TO RAGE....... ( - fadeout - ) [name withheld] — 20 February 2003 Cam fades into a GLCW interview set in the GLCW studio at WKYA-TV in suburban Detroit. Jon Savage is seen walking in. He takes off his leather jacket, picks up the GLCW microphone, and starts talking while staring directly into the camera.
SAVAGE: Rage, now you've gone and done it. Oh boy, are you in some SERIOUS trouble. Not only have you awakened the RAGE in me, but you've also upset Wildside. Now I know that you're big mouth has overloaded your fat a$$ and has caused you to write checks that you can't cash, BRAH! When I get you in that ring, I'm gonna tear you limb from limb. I'm gonna knock you out so fast you're head won't have time to spin. I'm then gonna rip your friggin head off from your neck and crap down your scrwany neck. Then I'm gonna stomp you into a mudhole and walk you dry! (begins mimicking Rage "you talked about the fans. you talked about the fans. and now I'm gonna punch you. blah, blah, blah.
Save the whinin' and b****in' for the nurses that take care of you when you're in the hospital, punk. I've seen youngsters like you over the years, and you know what? They don't last. They're here one minute, and just like that (snaps fingers) they're gone the next. And why, you ask, are they gone so fast? Because they mess around with veterans that they shouldn't. Things tend to happen to these babies that seem to think they can just take over the whole world. Legs, arms, and/or necks tend to get broken. Maybe a shoulder could get dislocated or ribs could get fractured. Hell, worse, someone...could...get....burned severely.
Cam shows Savage producing a large fireball in front of the camera.... You underestimate the power I truly posess, Rage. You also underestimate the abilities of my esteemed manager, Dr. Johnny Wildside. Cock and arrogant, that's what you are, son. Being that way is gonna cost you a helluva lot in our match....
Cam shows Savage producing another large fireball into the lense of the camera. [name withheld] — 21 February 2003 [updated:LAST EDITED ON Feb-21-03 AT 12:10&nbsp;PM (EST)]( - Camera opens up to local gym in Baltimore Maryland as you see Jared Wells along with some lifting buddies around a television. Wells is wearing black adidas warmups with a GLCW tank top. Everybody is glued to the television watching events unfold in Rhode Island with the nightclub and Staten Island explosion in New York. Camera pans up to Wells off guard as he begins to comment on his match with Jon Savage - )
JARED WELLS: Time is ticking away my friend. Jon Savage, your days are numbered. You keep talking 'bout the CHECKS 'n the CASH. I'm going to repeat myself one last time........DO YOU KNOW WHAT YOUR DEALIN' with? I could go on and on about my history but I'm not. I'm going to step into the ring and let you learn first hand who I am. Your talkin' to me like I'm some rookie. (LAUGHS) If there is one thing in this business I'm good at.......MIND GAMES. Mah-MAN I have you so frustrated right now you don't know what to say or do. And if you think thats bad, wait 'till we get in the ring. But go ahead bring the fireballs, the flames, Mr. Wildside, the United States Army.....they won't save you. Like I said before, the only way you'll be saved is by my fans. But after the trash coming out of your mouth, I don't think they'll spare it. Have I awakened the RAGE inside you? (LAUGHS) I have that effect on people dude. Just as the music hit, the lights go out, and your standing in the ring scared out of your mind......just remember one thing......YOUR FINISHED. As I make my way to the ring and your lookin' over the crowd as if your lookin' over a mountain........BE PREPARED because this is one fire you won't be able to put out! You say the time and talking is through? You ready to dance BRO? Well RAGE........he leads. Oh yeah one more thing.........TIME HAS COME TO RAGE.............
( - fadeout to Wells turning to the tv again - ) [name withheld] — 24 February 2003 A camera fades into Jon Savage walking down a tree-lined street in the Detroit suburb of Auburn Hills. He is vocally thinking out loud about upcoming events in GLCW....
SAVAGE: Lets see...Rage O' Fire out of the way, who's next.....maybe....Cruise??? sees camera Yes, I'm contemplating the next move after I defeat Jarod Wells, and yes, I'm looking at Cameron Cruise. We had a match here previously, and I think there's some unfinished business. Anyhow, Wildside's tying up loose ends in travel plans, accomodations, and transportation, so I thought I'd take a walk and enjoy the sunshine. pauses Jarod, you better enjoy the sunshine during the day and the star-filled nights, because my friend, it is you, sir, that doesn't know what your getting yourself in to. Not me. See, Wells, what you fail to realize is that I have the braun and Dr, Johnny Wildside has the brains to make us the most lethal pair in GLCW. What you're not taking into consideration is the fact wrestling me is just like wrestling a tag team of sorts. See, Wildside is the second man at the ring. scoffs Not that there's going to be very much of you for Wildside to finish off. Frankly, you know that your days are numbered and that you are scared of the uncertainy that is...wrestling me. You don't know what I'm capable of. You have no idea what my in-ring style's like, and you have no idea what I can truly do inside...or for that matter outside...the ring. Wildside, you see, has been coaching me for the last few weeks as I was on hiatus from Great Lakes. We were reviewing new techniques that will shock crowds and cripple opponents. And that is the name of the game, Wells, isn't it? Who can cripple who the fastest and do the most damage? Of course, listening to your promos is damaging in a way...it makes one long for the excitement of watching paint peel or sorting laundry.....yawns.....what you've had to say previously has bored the hell out of me, and I know it's bored those idiots that are the fans. Well, Jarod, you're not going bore us any longer after I get through with you.
Camera shows Savage producing a fireball out of nowhere SAVAGE: Can you handle the heat, Jarod? We'll see..... Camera shows Savage walking away.....
