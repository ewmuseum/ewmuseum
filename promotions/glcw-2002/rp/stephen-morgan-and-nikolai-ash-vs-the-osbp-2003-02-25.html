[name withheld] — 26 February 2003 (fade in: our familiar library of Nikolai Ash, the light begins to seep through a curtain in the far corner. Ash doesn’t seem affected by the light, his eyes are cracked open and he seems less than ready, but the aura in the ring would say differently, even after first glance. Ash’s head comes to face perpendicular with the camera.)
..a tag partner, a partner in crime, someone to help me in my climb, my ascent to a higher assessment of life. Is that what one would like to think when they see that Stephen Morgan and me aligned in the ring? Or is simply the image of a person like me grasping for the last breath on a cold evening as I drown in a pond in the middle of Minnesota.. the land of lakes.
But far be it for me to say that a land that cold is any place for the men of the beach, the men in the shorts with the tan and the rippling muscles ready more for a placid day on the brisk, warm waters… rather than face the harshness of the cold… the pain, the tingle in their arms when the pain is unbearable. Hmm… what is it that I speak of? Are my rants as hard as oak, let me knock on wood to make sure.. See, I walk down streets in my life hoping to take the rights paths not always achieving that goal, but.. what can I do? I am only human, and human beings need help… and maybe, at this time.. Morgan is my help, he is my savior.. .or at least sees the same as me. Knowing my life goals, and at least pretending to show remorse, and to care about my view. The unity amongst team members, the cohesion of two men…two vibrant men with the ideas to change the future.
::taking a sip of water from a glass placed on a table top:: …I, am less of a partner… and more of a man with needs. A thirst for knowledge, and solidarity.
(fade) SouthernBoy — 26 February 2003 {{...FADE-IN: "Stupendous" Stephen Morgan is sitting behind a large, oak desk dressed in a smoking jacket and slacks. A half smoked cigar sits in the ash try beside him...}}
SM: " I've been getting calls all day long...movie producers, directors, actors and actresses...all wanting to know the same thing. Stephen, WHY are you aligning yourself with Nikolai Ash? He's too dark for your persona. You're the good looking, tough, sensitive leading man...putting yourself in this situation with Ash could be career suicide! "
" I've been listening to that all day. And each and every time, I have the same answer for them. You see, Stephen Morgan isn't just some one dimensional talent that always focuses on doing the same thing, day in, and day out. I'm looking to broaden my horizons, TEST my limits. I'm on the path to greatness already, why not venture off somewhat and make my OWN road. All the greats have done it. Robin Williams...a great comedic actor, has stared in dark pictures, as well as dramas, and done very well. I am taking this concept and brining it to Great Lakes Championship Wrestling. I am bringing the skill, the charm, the charisma of "STUPENDOUS" Stephen Morgan and combining it with the cunning, the viciousness, the "hardness" of Nikolai Ash. "
{{...Morgan picks up some papers on his desk and straightens them. He then sets them down next to the ash tray...perhaps a little too close...}}
" It would be a hard combination for ANY team to overcome...much less two beach bums from Chicago. Listen up....Moon Doggie....Frito Pie...you two jack idiots are nothing compared to two of the most highly tuned, highly trained wrestlers in the world. You're scabs on the backs of dead rats...NOTHING to me, and I venture to say, NOTHING to my partner. You've got a nice little gimmick, a cute little catch phrase, and a good look. But jumping around and yelling "HOOT" in everybody's ear will get old REAL quick....not to mention, it doesn't WIN wrestling matches. "
{{...The cigar next to Morgan has caught the papers on fire and the begin to slowly start smoking. Morgan does not notice....}}
" So until you boys actually can lay claim to being in the ring with two men of the caliber of "STUPENDOUS" Stephen Morgan and Nikolai Ash, I'd keep the hooting to myself and the start preparing yourselves for life AFTER wrestling. And if it should enter you rather THICK heads that you actually have a CHANCE against us...well, you, just like ALL the rest...can KEEP DREAMING. "
{{...Morgan notices the papers burning and jumps up quickly knocking over his desk chair. He grabs a fire extinguisher from under the desk and zaps the ash try. He looks sheepishly into the camera as we FADE OUT....}}

(FTB) [name withheld] — 2 March 2003 (fade in: the posthumous face of Ash as he sits in the dark with his head resting ever so gently upon his hands which are propped up on his knees. Depression seems to have set in, but with Ash no one is to truly know with the smug atmosphere he possesses.)
…ten more paces until I reach the center of the universe, spin on my heel a quarter, and face the eminent doom that stares down my proverbial barrel. I grab my self and get ready for the stroll down the oak street, the wind blowing gently, and my hair matted down with sweat and grime. The sun burning my face as its degree reaches an infallible number. But who knows, what shall happen at the one moment when the planets align and I lay face to face with my maker, or a pseudo-architect of human nature. Take your pick and make it your own observation..
It seems, that Harold and Alex have at this point made an observation about me and my partner in crime, if you want to be so broad and vague with the term itself.. Mr. Morgan. See, we may seem like an incomparable pair.. but it may just be a ploy on the senses … I mean, there have been many odd partners in history that seemed to work out just right, I wont bore you with them because I am not in the right mind set to… to do, to do. To do, what phoneme of the water world… ::deep breath:: Kevin Costner fantasy land, seem to wish they can do… do what, what? Well I’m not sure what their goal really is… for I’m lost amongst my own thoughts, and their lack of verbiage … or hip lingo. But hey.. we’re cocky, and smug I guess…. I guess you can be right, but most likely wrong like your parents may have been when they dropped you on the doorstep of life and made you realize that your life was not full of strong points.. strong jaws, but not strong attributes.
..but intelligence isn’t always an asset when it comes to professional wrestling, nor is research in your case… but some take it upon themselves to wring it, some take the time to study up and learn the intricacies of life and the aspect of wrestling.
::sighs:: Sad state of the world today.. two by two we march… one brain donated by a chimpanzee at a time.
(fade)
