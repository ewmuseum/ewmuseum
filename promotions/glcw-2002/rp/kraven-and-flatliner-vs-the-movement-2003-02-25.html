GLCW — Roleplay

Kraven and Flatliner vs. The Movement

25 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
TBirdSCIL — 25 February 2003 Please keep all RP for this match in this folder... JC — 26 February 2003 (Camera starts to show a collage of the battles between the Unholy Alliance against the Movement, from there it fades into a collage of their battles against the likes of Rabesque, Maelstrom, Golem and the vicious beat down they administered to Xanatos and Kurtel... From there it fades into their last battle that pitted them along with Manson going against Maelstrom, Golem and Jean Rabesque and as it starts to end, the last scenes we see are the vicious assault that they along with Anarky give to Rabesque and Maelstrom as Golem walks away… Fade out… When camera comes back into focus, we are face to face with first Flatliner and then Kraven… The GLCW tag team belts on their shoulders, both dressed in all black MC leather outfits and each wearing their own personalized tee-shirts…. Behind them is that usual brick wall, the NEH/UA spray painted on it as well as the GLCW logo… Kraven is the first to speak…)
KRAVEN: Another Riptide, another title match… It’s been a while Movement…. Or should I say the new and improved Movement… Tell me something Movement, do you really think that change will favor you this time around!? Do you seriously think that the time has come for you to grab the brass ring from us!? If you are thinking that way, Shane, Rocky; then you are two fools who have learned NOTHING from our past encounters… Think about it Movement, think about it real hard and deep; what is it that makes Kraven and Flatliner so hard to beat!? What is the key ingredient in their mixture!? And most important of all, how have they been able to become a team of one, when all around them have faltered… (Pauses) Don’t know do you!? Well let me refresh and jar your memory cells a bit here… It’s quite simple Movement; but I’m not going to tell you what the formula is… You’re going to have to figure that one out all by yourselves… Maybe this hiatus you two took may have or may have not done wonders for your morale, and that’s a good thing in a matter of speaking… I see that you have a new mindset, one with a purpose; and that purpose is to take what is rightfully ours… Many before you have tried Movement, teams like Maelstrom and Rabesque, Golem and Rabesque, and even teams that Malec has slapped together in the hopes of finding the right combination… I am however glad that this time around it will be you that we will be facing; it was getting mundane, fighting the same old slugs time and again… I know that you two have set your minds to become the tag champs, your agendas and goals are so near, yet they will be the furthest things from your grasp… I bet that even your training has been elevated to a new level and now you feel that you are ready to take the bull by its horns; have to warn you Movement, it wasn’t easy then and it certainly won’t be any easier now…
FLATLINER: The simple fact of the matter is this…. We have our own agendas; our own goals and they do not include you two… We intend on keeping these belts for as long as we want to…. By any means necessary, by hook or by crook… You may be able to run roughshod around those other peons that think they are tag team extreme, but when you confront us, you’ll be slamming into a freaking brick wall Movement… While in your minds, you’re of that mindset that you will prevail, that you will supercede us, no such luck, that’s all wishful thinking on your part… With each passing day, we get better, we get stronger and most important of all, we meld into one all that much more… They say that there’s strength in numbers, but what they as well as you two have failed to understand and realize is that we don’t follow any typical rules, we go by the moment, that moment in which we are facing each other, our glares trying to burn holes into each other and in the end we always prevail, we always come on top… Am I taking anything away from you two!? Of course not, we know what you two can and cannot do, we know that once in that ring you will fight as if your lives depended on it, and believe me when I say that it will… Just ask those who have tried before you, ask Xanatos, ask Kurtel, they know, they felt the brunt of our aggression, they were at the receiving end of a vicious ass whipping and in short they became just two more victims in the endless flowing stream of desire… They like everyone else were found lacking and you two will be no different…. Sure you’ll come down and give us a hell of a fight, but in the end as you’re laying on your backs, trying to figure out what went wrong, don’t blame yourselves… Blame circumstances, blame Malec, blame KK and anyone else that comes to mind… But just realize that you once again came up short and were disposed of by us, the team that rules the tag team realm here at the GLCW….
KRAVEN: I will admit Shane, you and Rockwell, that you both possess the talent, the will and eventually will come into your own…. Eventually… But this is our time and we don’t intend on letting you or anyone else get in our way, no one and I do mean NO ONE will interrupt what we have set in motion…. We plan on seeing it through Movement and all your training, all your preparations will prevail to NOTHING!!!
FLATLINER: Nothing more to be said Movement, you have your agenda, we have ours… Your mindset tells you that, this is it!!! This is the one…. (Shakes his head…) You believe that if you want to; I know otherwise… But come anyway, come and try your luck again Movement… Determination is a very strong weapon, but alas there are too many chinks in its armor and we know each and every single one…
(Slowly the camera fades out into yet another collage, this one promoting Riptide… “Great Lakes Championship is proud to announce its first trip to Milwaukee, Wisconsin for the newest edition of Riptide coming from the Bradley Center!” flashes across the screen, it then shows two swirling fireballs as the cover the screen, they explode into the teams of Kraven, Flatliner and the Movement; their previous encounters fast forwarded until they are face to face, nose to nose… The camera then fades to black….)
JC — 6 March 2003 (We find Rico Suave reading a wrestling magazine; the “wrestler’s observation“ to be exact, the camera gives us an overhead view over his shoulder, “JC SUCKS” by Dick Rapaport, smirking as he reads on, finishing he turns the page to find an article on the UA… His expression becomes serious as he begins to read the article; we then get a face view of him as he starts to peer into the camera…)
RS: Evening folks, just finished reading two articles from the Wrestler’s Observation and I must say, very interesting stuff… But I have to contradict Mr. Rapaport on most of his article, well really all of it, and judging from what I’ve read you can tell that’s he doesn’t know squat about JC… These types of losers are what give wrestling a bad name, but such is life I guess… (Smirks) But tonight is not about JC, tonight I will be bringing yet another mundane interview; this time I have the Unholy Alliance, I was hoping to get an interview with the Movement, (Grins) especially with Britni, Man she’s HOT!!! (His grin turns into a frown) But NOooooo!!!! No such luck, I get stuck with the tag team champions, the UA… Very friendly individuals, real sociable fellas… The UA vs. the Movement… Two teams that are not strangers to each other, one holds the gold, the other wants it… As the ranks of GLCW tag team wrestling start to swell, by far the Movement have shown their superiority over the rest and that’s a hard thing to do as of late, lots of talent in the tag team arena, new and fresh talent among several old school veterans and legends… Many clawing their way to the mountaintop, and leading the pack is the Movement as they once again go for the gold… Will the third time around be the charm or will it be strike three!? Hard to say, the Movement has shown improvement after returning to the GLCW following their short hiatus… And yea, it can be said that the UA have appeared to not really be on their “A” game; hard to say folks just how this one will turn out… I was able to get a few words out of the UA the other day, and I do mean a few words… So without further ado, let’s watch this 5 minute interview….
(Camera zooms in on a screen in which we see the UA, no brick wall this time around, just an empty parking lot, with the exception of a black Hum Vee… With a cocky grin, Kraven begins to speak…)
KRAVEN: Hey Movement, sure hope you’re out there listening to this crap…. Just wanted you to know that even though many agree that silence is golden, it doesn’t cut any ice with us… Your reasons for remaining mute are of no consequence to us, but if we have to do this promo crap, so will you…. (Smirks) No, I’m not trying to dictate what you need or need not do, personally I don’t care… You don’t want to talk, well then to hell with ya dudes; your reasons may hold water with you, but it will not hold any with us… Now if I may, you two have stated that you are the new and improved Movement, not in so many words but the message was quite clear; so tell me Movement, do you really think that you stand a snowball’s chance in hell to come into Riptide and defeat us!? In what world would that be!? In YOURS!? (Bursts out laughing cynically, Flatliner just continues to glare…) Your world is non-existence, your thoughts and wishful thinking mean nothing to us, and as far as we are concerned you two are just two more sheep Malec has decided to throw into the slaughterhouse… You fail to see the real picture here Movement, Malec and KK has duped you into actually believing that you stand a chance against us, probably has told you that the third time around is the charm… But what he failed to tell you is that he has ran that same package over and over again to each and every single team he has pitted against us, he has tried with every single combination known to man, singles, doubles, new guy, old guy, he has even gone as far as to team up the likes of Mael, Golem and even Jean to no avail guys… But I take it that you feel that you do actually stand a chance in taking these away from us, (Waves the belts up in the air…)
FLATLINER: So Malec’s new prima donnas think that they are exempt from doing these damn stupid promos huh!? That all they have to do is show up and the titles will be theirs… (Glares cynically) What we have here is a failure to communicate, what we have here is two individuals who think they can come and run roughshod over us and take what is ours… No such luck Movement, what makes you think that you are special!? No one here is special Movement, except for us… And in case you’re wondering why, wonder no more and just look at the record, look at how many piss pots Malec has assembled to take us on and look at their track record when it concerns us, they have all FAILED!!! And so WILL YOU!!!
(Camera rocks back and forth as Flatliner pushes it away, he then walks away leaving Kraven who stares into camera with a malicious expression, shakes his head and heads in the same direction Flatliner went off… Camera then zooms back to Rico, who continues thumbing through the magazine as he sees the camera and starts to speak again…)
RS: Like I said a very short interview… Now if we look at the statistics we will see that the odds are really against the Movement, in my opinion they will fall once again… But stranger things have happened, in the blink of an eye the tides can quickly change and it’s a whole new ballgame… And whether you like them or not, they are the tag team champs, and they rule that arena with an iron fist… Nothing is beneath them, they do whatever it takes to stay on top, you the fans mean nothing to them, and authority is a non-existent entity as far as they’re concern… JC wasn’t able to contain them, and as you can see Malec and KK sort of keep their distance from them; now don’t take and twist that around, like you always do with everything else I say, but it’s true folks; just look at all the crap the UA has gotten away with, not long after winning the titles, they dumped JC and while they may have had their reasons, it didn’t get any better where they were concerned, it got worst folks… Their union with Manson brought about chaos, superstars like Maelstrom, Golem, Jean Rabesque, just to name a few have experienced constant beat downs at the hands of these three, and now it would seem that Anarky has joined the ranks, maybe so or maybe not, but they seem to be on the same wave length… They have locked in their sights on several top GLCW stars, and it seems like that is just the beginning… And having said that, what about this hooded figure that keeps attacking the UA!? Whoever the hell he is, is massive and has gotten the best of the UA… And it’s strange how they really don’t mention him and when I have tried to ask or inquire about him, I have run into a brick wall; a lot of rumors going around pertaining to that, but that’s all they are just rumors… On a lighter note, I would like to welcome Anarky, Suicide and Minion to the GLCW and on behalf of myself and the fans, give them hell guys… The GLCW is fast becoming the fed to be in and anyone with half a brain can see why…
(Looks at his watch…) RS: But I can see by my Mickey Mouse watch that the time has come to say good bye to all my fans, so as always the privilege has been all YOURS!!!
(Camera fades off as Rico laughs… Fade to black…)
