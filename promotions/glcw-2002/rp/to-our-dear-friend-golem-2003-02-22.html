To Our Dear Friend Golem...

22 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Manson — 22 February 2003 After all this time and with my new found sensitivity, I find myself feeling for you Golem. I wonder if your life wouldn't be the wreck it is now, if not for my thorough drubbing of you. Will you ever get that coveted GLCW title shot against Maelstrom over Anarky, Rabesque, me and the rest of the cast? Sadly, it seem not, though you seem to have an issue. But I told you guys it wouldn't end well, now did I?
And looking at my team, my victorious team, I might add, we don't need to like each othe, we even insult each other at times, but we're not a family. It's business. Even Anarky understands this and that's why he and I have been able to business over the years. And Golem, who wouldn't like to see tis match you two have end with a handshake? Well, that's not likely, but the thought is nice.
But to the point, Golem, obviously something is lacking, missing shall we say? Which is why I extend the courtesy to you to come on over to the good guys. Be taken under my proverbial wing. Then that TV title, a tag title down the road perhaps? Maelstrom himself? Infinity abounds. And most of you, at least you know that even though every partner is merely a means to an end, at least I wouldn't turn on them in the middle of a match and strike them. That's not practical afterall, do it after the match to preserve your victory. But did your former teammates consider this? Did they evn strategize with you, if they bothered at all?
This can change though, Golem. Because above all,I am a visionary and can offer you a life of wonder. Who wouldn't want to see the photos of Lady V that Jakes Shades couldn't get? The inner workings of my mind..and of course people who work alongsideyou for a goal, instead of letting self-righteous morality and selfish needs destroy any working relationship.
I understand this isn't an easy decision. These things never are. I'l give you some time, but not forever. Salvation waits for no man. If you have ever really wanted to move mountains though, you'll at least consider the offer...
-Michael Manson
