ShawnHartXXX — 4 January 2003 [updated:LAST EDITED ON Jan-04-03 AT 01:16&nbsp;AM (EST)](FADEIN: Press conference setting, lots of people, lots of lights, lots of talk. At the podium stands the one and only manager to the best, Sly Sterling. Behind him in three chais sits his usual, all to familiar clientele, being "El Diablo" Angel Castillo, and the duo of the men respectively known as Raze and Nuke, collectively known as "Gridlock")
Sterling: "Ladies and gentlemen of the press, good afternoon. As I'm sure you all know, I... am Sly Sterling, and the men sitting next to me are Raze, Nuke, and 'El Diablo' Angel Castillo, and we've called this press conference today to drop a little announcement. Before we begin, however, I regret to inform you that we WON'T be taking any questions, because... quite frankly, we know what needs to be said and to allow any of you to just jump in would only numb and digress from anything we have to say.."
(As the press in the room mumble amongst themselves over the no question ruling, Sterling takes a sip from his water bottle, smiles confidently, then continues on.)
Sterling: : "NOW, getting right down to business, it is common knowledge that I represent both Mr. Castillo, as well as the tag team sensation Gridlock... And up to this point, I think it's pretty safe to say that both parties have had quite the share of success. Mr. Castillo has gone to the limits with the likes of the Jobber and Maelstrom, and throughout each contest, taken his opponent to the limit... both in his numerous victories AND during the occasional defeat. Gridlock, though reasonably tame from a verbal aspect, has endured much of the same success in physically dominating each and every ram-shackle duo tossed their way. Now, with all that being said, I think it's pretty safe to say that El Diablo Angel Castillo, along with Gridlock, have more than held there own with the country's top talent, RIGHT HERE…in GLCW..."
(He pauses briefly, glances over at his clients, nods approvingly, then turns back to the members of the press.)
Sterling: " HOWEVER.................. ...After several weeks of simply, testing the water... come Massive Assault, LIVE from Grand Rapids, I think it's high time that my clients hopped right into the pool! You see, like a predator stalks its prey.... gauging the situation, measuring that which lies in front of it, Angel Castillo and Gridlock have hung back... Waiting for juuuust the right moment…to move in for the kill! Bottom line, the prey that stands before us appears in the form of Steven James, Chris McMillan, and Cameron Cruise.... And when my clients step into that ring, united together for the VERY FIRST TIME in competition, the three of them are going in... for the death strike!!! El Diablo.... tell 'em how it is!!"
(Castillo steps up to the podium and grabs the mike, clears his throat, and begins.) Castillo: "Ah, yes, well, as succinctly as my manager has said it for all of you, I implore all of you...
(Camera pans back so you can see Raze and Nuke in their chairs) Castillo: "...intelligent people of the press..." (Raze starts jacking the air off and Nuke appears to be making a raspberry, but you can't hear it, shot pans back to just Castillo at the podium)
Castillo: "Don't think that our words are merely just words. Time and time again I have been I have had to face such “incredible, unstoppable, phenomenal” opponents who were told to have such incredible in-ring abilities like (mocking tone) “The Wolf” Chris McMillain, and “Showtime” Steven James, two self-professed “superstars” who have nothing super about them except, say, super-mediocrity. I hold victories over both, and the third man in this assembled band of the damned is a new-comer who was thrown in there just for looks, I guess. Does anyone really believe, HONESTLY, that even if they put for all the effort they have in their poor, poor bodies combined, that they could overcome the impossibly talented combination of myself, a skilled in-ring technician, and these two BASTIONS OF BEASTLY BRUTE FORCE? If me and my companions' past demonstrations of strength and ability are not enough to show that we can overcome any opponent that stands on the other side of that ring, inside the ropes, on the mat, looking us in the eye, then I have nothing more to say than simply...seeing (pauses and flashes devilish grin) is believing."
(El Diablo stands down, as Sly meets him pats him on the shoulder, than takes his place at the podium.)
Sterling: "What most of you fail to realize here is that apart, Angel Castillo and Gridlock are forces to be reckoned with, but together.... heh... (An evil grin covers his face).... they’re a force to be FEARED!! It may not happen today, it may not happen tomorrow... but for these three men, the SKY is the limit... and GREAT THINGS lie in their cross hairs. And it aaaaaaaall begins RIGHT NOW!!! The infusion of Latino rhythm and cold, hard, good ol' fashion CHAOS, has created an alchemy that is matched by none. And with GOD as my witness, consider all that you've seen to this point nothing more than a sneak peak... of the GREATNESS that lies ahead!! Thank you, aaaand good..."
(Suddenly Raze stands up, locks eyes with his manager, then motions him to the side as he steps to the podium)
Raze: "Listen up, cuz I'm only gonna say this once. El Diablo has had a couple of down moments, and the same goes for me and my brother here, but as of THIS moment.... all of that means NOTHING! You can criticize us because we don't bare all on a mic, you can knock us on our... unique style.... WHATEVER. Because the simple fact remains that us three guys here are the FUTURE of GLCW, and we WILL NOT BE DENIED in obtaining that which is RIGHTFULLY OURS!!"
(Nuke gets up and leans in towards the podium) Nuke: "Forget all that!! I can sum it up for you in FOUR WORDS, people!! Prepare.... to get.... GRIDLOCKED!!!!!!"
(Sterling steps back up, puts his arms around Raze and Nuke, then lowers his head towards the microphone.)
Sterling: "The writing is on the wall, people. Heed the warnings, or get lost in the shuffle!! Good afternoon!"
