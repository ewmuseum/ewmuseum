GLCW — Roleplay

Anarky vs. "Rage o' Fire" Jared Wells

11 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

[name withheld] — 12 March 2003 (scene opens up to Jared Wells leaving a GLCW show carrying his jacket and pulling his luggage on wheels to his yellow 2001 cobra mustang - very wore out from his match he throws his stuff in the trunk and turns towards the camera for some comments)
'RAGE 'o FIRE': I've waited over eight long years to say what I have to say right now! The contract has been signed and we are headed to the first ever WIRED! Rage 'o Fire versus Anarky baby in Flint Michigan. This match means more than anything right now to me. It's kinda like a second ounce of life in my career. Finally Anarky, I will prove to you why I'm just as big of a star as you. I remember back when you were the TOP GUY in this business. I'm not just talking about one company.....but the whole world. YOU WERE THE MAN TO BEAT YEARS AGO MAN! I was carrying the WWL heavyweight championship while you were beating everybody. Not only are you a household name but your an ICON. The only problem is.......the wrestling business would not accept JARED WELLS as a TOP GUY in the business. They said that I was better off staying in the WWL for the rest of my career. The UNIFIED TITLE tournament went on without me. And still in the WWL wanting a piece of that spotlight. I took a run in the CSWA but nobody took me seriously. But there was one guy doing it all in wrestling.....Anarky. Man, you were on every cover of wrestling magazines.........on every damn show! You did it all dude! I had to sit back.....suck it all in and watch because nobody gave me a chance. I'll admit man, I was damn jealous of you back in the day. I might have been the WWL heavyweight champion, but I was never the REAL WORLDS CHAMPION. And a lot of people around here might not understand what I'm talking about. The GLCW is totally different than the past. The IMA Sports Arena will be SOLD-OUT for sure, and the millions watching on the GLCW.com. HISTORY will be made, and a new ERA will begin.
(Wells then shuts the trunk and sits in the car with the music low and the windows down as the camera zooms to his face)
All due respect to Maelstrom and Ash, but Wells 'n Anarky should be the MAIN EVENT. This is more than just a match.....this is about the past, pride, and RESPECT. I'm not in it for the money or the spotlight. The dream match has happened.....everybody across the globe will be watching and when the bell rings ANARKY.........all hell will break lose. As you've noticed, I've won my fans back. They will stick with me each and every way in this match. They've waited too long for me to blow this match. I'll go off the record right now and say this will be my biggest match ever! No world titles, no number one contender match......nothing but PRIDE 'n RESPECT. After I hook the head big man......drive you through the mat with the RAGE DROP............ONE....TWO.....THREE.........it will finally be over. The dream will come true. While your laying there on the mat wondering what happened.....I will lend my hand out and give you the option to shake it. As far as the young guys in the back locker room......lay back and take notes because this will burn the house down. Anarky.....I will put up a fight. I will stop at nothing to defeat you. I HAVE TO BEAT YOU DUDE! RAGE 'o FIRE is better than ever....and the RULE of ANARKY will finally be stopped........
(Wells turns the car on as you hear it roar as he is tapping on the gas) ANARKY.......time is ticking away my friend..........and there is one more thing that I've always wanted to tell you..........TIME HAS COME TO RRRRRRRAAAAAAAGGGGGGGEEEEEE!!!!!!!!!!!!!
[i](Wells then stepped on the gas and zooms out of the parking garage) (f/o) JLevinson — 13 March 2003 (FADEIN to an empty gym, save for one man in the back corner lifting weights. As the camera pans around all of the unused equipment, the repetetive groans and thuds of the weights hitting the ground stands out. Anarky puts the weights down and sits down on a bench, wiping his face off with a towel. He breathes heavily for a few seconds before speaking.)
ANARKY: "Over the course of my career, I have gone through many... evolutions. From a starstruck jobber... to a glorified jobber... to a reigning God of Wrestling... to nothing. And back again. Rage... Jared... whatever you want me to call you... I remember you. Don't think that just because I had the belts and the glory that I didn't see what was going on around me. That I was too busy trying to be THE MAN, as you say, that I forgot everything.
"You see, Jared... of all the lessons I've learned... of all the trials and tribulations I've had to go through just to get here, just to be on the undercard of a house show for a regional promotion with YOU... the one that stuck with me with the most... was the one I learned, not while I was Champion, but while I was nobody.
"You may or may not be right about things, Jared. Certainly there was a time when I held more titles than anyone. Nobody can argue that. But one thing you may not realize is that despite all of my championships, I didn't receive one OUNCE of RESPECT from ANYONE that mattered. Sure, nobodies like you worshipped me while you touched yourself in the dark, lonely nights... but the ones that mattered... they never cared.
"I could never win enough for them. I could never hold enough titles. I could never inflict enough pain on them to make them see that I was, indeed, THE BEST WRESTLER IN THE WORLD. And then I finally realized... and I retired. It didn't matter. Jared, people only see what they want to see. I realize that now. Guys like Doc Silver, [name withheld], GUNS, Mark Windham... supposed legends... do you know what they really are, Jared? Petty, insecure children. They were so INCAPABLE of understanding that somebody could come out of NOWHERE and do what they COULDN'T... that they simply refused to believe. And when enough people deny the truth, Jared... well, it doesn't exist for them at all, does it?
"I found myself, Jared. Not in the glory of the gold. Not in the impressive victories. Not even with the fans cheering my name. No, Jared, I found something REAL in a sport full of PHONIES. I found something BEAUTIFUL in a world of DISGUST. Do you know what I found, Jared? I found that it is only when there is blood dripping from our wounds... when every muscle in your body screams for release... when every part of your soul begs for mercy... when there is simply nothing left to give... that is when we find ourselves. Only when we have nowhere to go, and nowhere to hide, and nothing to prove, that we can truly become something better.
"Jared, this might be the most important night of your life. Your fans may have waited years for this. You might think that you're FINALLY getting a chance to prove yourself. Well I hate to disappoint, Jared, but I've got NOTHING TO PROVE. I don't have to beat YOU or ANYONE ELSE... because I've already proven everything there is to prove. I've slayed the unslayable. I've held the titles. I've given the fans more entertainment then they will understand, and they hate me for it. Fine.
"You can call it a Dream Match, or you can call it the most important match of your life. You can call it your final chance to prove yourself, or you can call it a match of respect and honor. But it doesn't matter, Jared. Win or lose, nothing changes. Everyone is too consumed with their own glory to admit that maybe, just MAYBE, somebody out there could be BETTER.
"The REAL question, Jared, is why I'm wasting my time on YOU. You see, I don't have ANYTHING TO PROVE to ANYONE. I don't give a damn if Maelstrom has the belt. I don't give a damn if there's 10 guys ahead of me in the rankings. And I care even LESS if you think that beating ME is going to make you any more of a man. Why are guys like Stephen Morgan and Nemesis getting shots at the TV Title while I'm stuck here, fighting guys like you? I haven't lost yet, but Nemesis is somehow ahead of me?
"It's the same old story, same old song and dance, Jared. Malec knows it. Maelstrom knows it. Hell, even a fool like Jobber probably knows it. Do you know what I'm talking about, Rage? I'm talking about the fact that EVERYONE in the WORLD knows that the ONLY man in this WHOLE PATHETIC LEAGUE who can even CHALLENGE our Champion... is ME. And if YOU THINK... for ONE GODDAMNED SECOND... that you're going to get in the way of THAT... well, Jared, then I think it's about time that I show you what you called the "RULE OF ANARKY" is ALL ABOUT.
"Chaos rules all, Jared... whether you like it or not. And at Wired, I WILL be victorious. And you will still be nobody. And the earth will still spin towards exctinction. And nobody will care."
(FADEOUT.) [name withheld] — 14 March 2003 (scene opens up to JARED WELLS sitting a seat looking at some wrestling magazine from 1997 with Anarky on the cover. He is rested, relax, and laid back in the chair)
JARED WELLS: You know I could come out here and bore you guys with some meaningless promo but I'm not. I'm going to tell it like it is to all my fans.....especially ANARKY. It seems Anarky, you and I are a lot alike in some ways. Then again, we don't see eye to eye on most things. I'll agree with you on some issues concerning the GLCW........I'm surprised your not the world heavyweight champion right now. But let's not talk about the titles, the money, or whatever corrupts the common man. The great 'ol sayin' in wrestling is TO BE THE MAN, you gotta BEAT THAT MAN! You know how many times I was told that years ago?
Our match coming up might not mean anything to you but it will to me. Easy for you to say all of that because you've done it all in a short period of time man. You say you entertain more than the fans will understand.....but they hate you for it? That's the beauty 'bout this journey we call wrestling. I thrive off of fans who hate me.....most of all love me. But regardless of what they think, deep down inside they respect you.
And I'm a bit thrown off of some of your remarks like NOTHING CHANGES........NOTHING CHANGES!!! Come on man, you know damn well you can make some serious changes around here if you wanted to. But yet you stand there and just suck it all in as if nothing happened. I'm all about the CHANGE in my life....I know it will be for the better. Things are going to change dude, and we'll start at WIRED. Then when the path of RAGE is laid, my fans and I are heading to the top. This won't be just some wrestling match.....I will FIGHT YOU tooth 'n nail. If I have to pull some old tricks out of my book.....so be it. The GLCW just so happens to be where we stand now....I'm ready for some changes and I'm sure you are as well.
We've been around forever so I say lets do this. We will have millions watching us as we prepare for war. It's my turn to come out of nowhere and do something. I've retired too many times for simple stupid reasons. All the way from creative control to who I was facing. It hurt my damn career and I'm still paying for it. I can't change anything I've done in the past but I sure as hell can CHANGE! ANARKY you will be stopped, and the your right about the CHAOS. But the question is.....who can start more CHAOS? Anarky versus Rage......what dominates.....But remember this Anarky.......I might be some no name piece of crap to you........but I do RESPECT you for what you've done for this business that is sometimes DARK. I'm going to earn your respect......even if I have to beat your ass for it. Your an ICON in this business......and I believe I'm and ICON as well.
Nothing to prove in this match huh? (SMILES) I got no problem with that. As long as your the SAME ANARKY that I once watched. You say the earth will spin towards extinction? The only thing that will be extinct is ANARKY that flows through the GLCW and the rest of the wrestling WORLD......RAGE WILL RULE..........your time is up pal.......TIME HAS COME TO RAGE....
'nuff said....... [i](f/o) JLevinson — 15 March 2003 (FADEIN to a dark nightclub, strobe lights flashing and video walls playing strange images. People dance on a video floor that also flashes strange symbols and imagery as "Down In It" by Nine Inch Nails plays in the background. The camera pans up to the catwalk above, where Anarky is crouched down, his eyes gazing down at the people below. After ten seconds or so, he stands up and walks across the catwalk and into a small office. The camera follows. Anarky sits in a leather chair and puts his feet up on the giant oak desk.)
ANARKY: "Welcome to my home away from home. I purchased this club in 1998. I thought it would be a wise investment, and it has been. There have been plenty of wise investments, in fact. I can't lie... wrestling has been good to me. I guess tremendous success can reap astounding financial benefits.
"But it doesn't change anything, Jared. I know you can't see it. It doesn't matter. The money, the fame, the fortune, the GODDAMNED RESPECT."
(He stands up and clears off a huge piles of papers and office supplies off the desk. He empties out the contents of his drawers and throws them against the wall. He stops and breathes heavily, sitting back down after a few seconds.)
ANARKY: "No, Jared... you want to be THE MAN? Well you can have THE MAN, and you can have THE TITLES, and you can have everything, because I DON'T WANT IT ANYMORE. Because you all make me sick. Doing things for the damned FANS. The FANS DON'T CARE ABOUT YOU. They only care about is the flavor of the week. As soon as they find something better, they'll leave you to die in some backwater league nobody's ever heard of.
"You think there's some great honor to what we do. As if we were gladiators fighting over something truly worthy. But we aren't. We're just pitiful little souls spilling blood for the masses.
"The only thing I need, Jared... is for you to open up your goddamned eyes and realize... that this isn't a game. You pull out old magazines with me on the cover. You talk about what an HONOR it will be to beat me. You spend the whole time kissing my ass and telling me how GREAT I AM right before you tell me that you're going to BEAT ME. You think I care about your or anyone else's respect?
(He stands up again, flipping over the huge desk which lands with an enormous thud. He stands there, breathing wildly, his hands balled into fists, his eyes closed. He opens his eyes suddenly and stares not into the camera, but THROUGH the camera, as if it was not there.)
ANARKY: "No... no... no... no Jared... you don't get it at all. You think it's going to be a match for the ages... when really... it's just another chance for me to silence the very things about myself which I hate... the unceasing voices that beg me to strike, to destroy, to violate...
"YOU NEED TO OPEN UP YOUR GODDAMNED EARS. I am not THE MAN. I am THE MOST DANGEROUS MAN ALIVE. And it isn't because I BEAT SOMEBODY or HELD SOME TITLE or even because I was on some GODDAMNED MAGAZINE COVER. It was all... because... I can't stop... and you can't stop me... and at the very first Wired, the Gods of War will be appeased with your sacrifice.
"Your rage... your fire... will be extinguished." (FADEOUT.) [name withheld] — 17 March 2003 ( - scene opens up to downtown at Anarky's nightclub that is getting ready to close up for the night. Wells is standing outside in the dark watching everybody leave - )
JARED WELLS: 'Ya know I'm getting a gut feeling that your a 'LIL-BIT scared? I'm smelling the FEAR in the air already. (SHOUTS) YOU KNOW IT.....I KNOW IT! (CALMS DOWN) For once somebody got up in your face and gave you a reality check about LIFE itself. Sweat begins to drip from your head....and you have a burning sensation in your stomach.
'ol RAGE gotcha thinkin' all of the sudden. I back down from NOBODY. YOU CLAIM to be the most DANGEROUS MAN ALIVE? (LAUGHS) USED TO BE my friend. USED TO BE! What happened to you? I'm starting to think you've lost your DAMN MIND. I'm willing to throw all the RESPECT out the window. And I'm gonna shoot with 'ya here man.....I'll be damned if I'm going to let you tell me I don't see anything going on around here. MAN I've been through too much crap to hear this.
But after our match maybe you'll come out of the black cloud you've been living and realize REALITY. All of the sudden you've done everything possible and now you hate everybody for it. Give me a break dude. You walk your ass down that aisle....jump into the ring and look me eye to eye. I want your ass in full gear, and I want the REAL ANARKY. You may not agree with alot of what I have to say but you will RESPECT RAGE at the end of the road. I have nothing more to say man.........besides.....TIME HAS COME TO RAGE........not a week from now......BUT NOW! Oh yeah, how 'ya gonna put the fire out when your extinguisher is empty? Think 'bout it.
(f/o) JLevinson — 18 March 2003 (FADEIN to the city of Flint, Michigan, a few days before GLCW's first Wired broadcast. Anarky sits on a bench overlooking Stepping Stone Falls at Lake Mott. Many tourists are standing around, taking pictures with their families as Anarky simply sits, watching quietly. Finally he speaks, his voice once again composed.)
ANARKY: "Fear. Many claim to know it, or understand it, but are ruled by it nonetheless. The more I look around, the more I realize that we are ALL ruled by fear. Not just you or I, Jared... but all of mankind. Our great country is about to wage a war, Jared, based on fear. Fear of terrorist attacks. Just that is enough to cause a country to kill thousands of Iraqis every day... to make the world a safer place.
"We all have our fears. But you have gone too far, Jared, in assuming that I am afraid of you. For what would I have to fear, Jared? Loss? I have lost before, and I will lose again. Certainly, the prospect of losing to such a... courageous young man such as yourself... is not enough to instill fear in me.
"Is it fear of pain? Now if you believe that, Jared, you certainly haven't even begun to understand me. You've got all the magazines. Why not open to the page where GUNS hit me in the head with a scepter? Why not show me the pictures of [name withheld] having me locked into the Devastator... until I lost consiousness, and then, even THEN, Jared, after I was beyond pain, he still kept it locked in until he was disqualified. Or maybe it was my grueling, nearly hour-long match with Stone Wolf in the FWF just a year or two ago where I became the FWF Frontier Champion... ?
"Am I saying all this to impress you, Jared? No. I don't need to impress you. You're obviously far too stupid to be impressed. Even fear is beyond you, isn't it? You make a lot of big claims that I've lost a step.. that I USED to be the most dangerous man in the world.
"You think because I don't carry around five belts that I'm not dangerous? You think that because I'm not on the cover of magazines that I'm no threat? That my time has come and gone?"
(He turns and smiles at the camera, and then stands up and walks along the edge of the lake. After a minute he finds solitude and leans over edge and touches his hand to the water... )
ANARKY: "A man will tell himself whatever it takes to move beyond fear. You have clearly told yourself that I USED to be dangerous... that I USED to be The Man... but now I'm just a washed-up has-been. Well whatever it takes to get you to sleep at night, Jared.
"I presented you with nothing but the truth, but you didn't want it. I told you that all the titles... and the glory... and the fame... was worth nothing. And you couldn't believe it. You couldn't believe that something you've dreamed of for so long could be so fake... so illusory.
"You want the real Anarky, Jared? The one who was the King of Wrestling only five or six years ago? He's dead, Jared. He died a long time ago. And I am what's left.
"And if you think that makes me LESS dangerous... well... " (FADEOUT as he smiles into the camera... ) [name withheld] — 19 March 2003 (Camera opens up to the IMA Sports arena as the crew is putting together the ring and setting up props. Then the camera pans over to the empty seats in the back as you see Jared Wells sitting in the dark looking around. JARED WELLS: The United States is on the verge of war possibly in a few hours from now. Another war that is about to happen will take place in here. All my fans out there know that ANARKY will no longer exist here in the GLCW. The path of RAGE has been created and it leads us up to WIRED. My fans believe in me, and I can't let them down. 'Rage 'o Fire' lives on forever. With all that said, ANARKY, time is running out.
There are things that I have said that were a little bit over board. Frustration is a killer man. You claim to be the most dangerous man......I think not. People on the streets are coming up to me and telling me that I'm starting to lose it again with the ego. I said NAH......I'm holding my ground. There are things in this world that people would not understand. Like I said over and over ANARKY......the money, the belts don't mean anything. The only thing on the contract is your hand writing and mine....that's it. You can think what you want........I have no plans on being stopped.
You say the REAL ANARKY died awhile ago? Well what's left will be killed off. I WILL put you out of your misery. Everybody from Flint, Michigan all the way to the internet will be glued to us. We are the WARRIORS of this business ANARKY. All of this bad mouthing back and fourth with each other will do us no good. It's too bad people have to see the evil side of GOOD things. JLevinson — 21 March 2003 [updated:LAST EDITED ON Mar-20-03 AT 11:41&nbsp;PM (EST)](FADEIN to the locker room at Wired. Anarky sits in a plain metal stool, his back to the camera and face staring at the mirror, finishing up the last touches of his facepaint, depicting an almost entirely white skull with dark eyes and mouth. He stares into the screen almost vacantly and finishes. He pulls on his shirt quietly, covering up a plethora of scars. Long, black tattoos covers his arms, mimmicking the bones in his arms except highly stylized and sharp. He pulls on his black leather gloves and balls his hands into tight fists. Then he begins to wrap tape around his wrists as he speaks quietly... almost vulnerably... )
ANARKY: "Rage... in all of your mindless ramblings... in all your shallow hypocrisy... in all of your tainted lies... you found something true. Something real.
"Time is up, Rage. The warpaint is on. Talk... is simply that. Talk. You say you're going to win. I tell you you aren't. That's it. No more. No less.
"... I can almost hear the bell ringing, Rage... and do you know for whom the bell tolls... ?
(He smiles briefly at the mirror and stands up, cracking his neck. He turns slowly towards and camera and seems lost in thought. He speaks again, quietly but surely.)
ANARKY: "... I almost enjoy this moment the most... the moment right before... the anticipation that rushes through me like a wave... and I forget everything... and I know that soon, I will be home again...
"... Rage... I hope you intend to keep your promises. Because I'm going to keep mine. "I just wanted to tell you... that this is going to hurt me... more than it hurts you. But I don't want to lie."
(His mouth stretches into an enormous, psychotic grin. He puts his hand over the camera and shoves it back as he walks out of the room. FADEOUT.)
