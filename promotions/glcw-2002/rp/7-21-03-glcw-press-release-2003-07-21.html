Yuji Yamashita — 21 July 2003 Excerpt from Fight 1 Magazine pg. 23 SAIKYOU to the States? FROM JAPAN - After participating in the annual Rainbow Festival show in Osaka, Japanese wrestling star Yuji "SAIKYOU!!" Yamashita spoke to reporters from NewsAsia about returning to his hometown of Nigata and prepping for a trip to the United States.
"I've been thinking about this for the longest time," said Yamashita, "and I believe it's best for me to go back overseas on yet another learning excursion. I am disappointed with the results so far and will not be returning to my home country until I am satisfied with my performance."
Early last year, Yuji flew home after a lengthy stay in Canada, Mexico, and the U.S. Under the impression that he wasn't ready to compete, Yamashita had forced himself away from his friends and family in a feverish quest to improve his skill and learn as much as he possibly could in his journey to become one of the top wrestlers in the world.
"I am a perfectionist. I am cocky, arrogant, and oftentimes reckless. I know no humility and will not back down from anyone. Power and aggression only take you so far. My last trip saw that I formed a base of skill. Now I'm going to solidify it. Power, aggression, and skill... I'm going to excel at my craft or die trying."
Several offers have been made for Yamashita's services. Most of these opportunities are in promotions he's worked with before. However, Yuji shows no interest in treading on old ground and looks to test his skill on the less beaten path. One organization he expressed interest in was Scott Malec's Great Lakes Championship Wrestling. Upon hearing this, NewsAsia asked if there was a certain reason behind the favoritism of GLCW over the others.
"I want to find someplace that I've never been to before. I want to accomplish my fourth navigation(ed note: "4th year of wrestling") in uncharted waters. I don't care who I face. Give me someone, anyone, and I will defeat them. Bring me their name and I will bring you their head. All I ask for is competition. I've weighed this choice against all others and have finally chosen a path to follow. The journey begins now. Do not disappoint me." ***
