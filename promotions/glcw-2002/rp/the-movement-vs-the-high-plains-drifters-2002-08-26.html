TBirdSCIL — 26 August 2002 All RP for this match goes in this folder... [name withheld] — 28 August 2002 (Our scene opens on a hot and sunny day in the heart of the desert. We're in the middle of a boomtown from the 1800's, or so it would appear. There are all sorts of townsfolk strolling along on this hot afternoon. However one lovely lady stands apart from the others. A young brunette, just over 19 years old walks down the center of the dirt street. She's got on a small black cowboy hat, a black leather vest - just barely covering her breasts. Her long legs disappear into a very small leather mini-skirt. She stops short of the camera and puts her hands on her hips and rests her small frame on her left leg. The camera quickly spins to the left towards the Bullhorn Saloon, where a straggly cowboy is in midair after crashing through a window. Through the swinging doors comes a mountain of a man, better known as Shane Rockwell. Rockwell is decked out in the 1800's cowboy garb, complete with a six shooter stuck to his side. Rockwell exits the saloon and tips his hat to the gorgeous young lady. The straggly cowboy gets up to his feet and runs at Rockwell. Rockwell levels him with one punch.)
ROCKWELL: (Spoken with a mock southern accent) "Some of ya'll never learn do ya?" (Rockwell picks up the straggly cowboy and hoists him over his shoulder. Rockwell carries him to the outhouse and kicks open the door. There sitting on the john is another man decked out in a Charlie Chaplin outfit, but he's better known as the British sensation, Rocky Wellington.)
WELLINGTON: (Spoken with a mild British accent) "Oh bloody hell. Can't a man relieve himself in private?" (Wellington pulls his britches up) "Well, have at it chap."
(Rockwell dumps the poor cowboy headfirst into the hole.) ROCKWELL: "Well, that takes care of that." WELLINGTON: "I do say, I love these outfits." ROCKWELL: "Yeah, don't think things look great on us?" WELLINGTON: "Couldn't have spoken words more true." ROCKWELL: "But JESUS, look at Britni. Is she not the epitomy of SEXY?" WELLINGTON: "Aye." ROCKWELL: "Aye? That's all that your British trap could muster? Aye? By God, she's the sexiest thing to hit the world since..."
WELLINGTON: "Queen Elizabeth?" ROCKWELL: "No!" WELLINGTON: "Madonna?" ROCKWELL: "No!" WELLINGTON: "Cyndi Lauper?" ROCKWELL: "No!" WELLINGTON: "What about Jewel?" ROCKWELL: "For the LOVE of GOD, NO!" WELLINGTON: "Bloody hell, I've got it, Nicole Kidman." ROCKWELL: (Shaking his head in disgust) "Rocky, HOW in the HELL can you compare that beast of a woman, Nicole Kidman to this lovely damsel Britni?"
WELLINGTON: "Okay, sorry. You're right. She's as beautiful as Julia Roberts?" ROCKWELL: "Listen you damn Brit. Britni cannot be compared to those. She's is the measuring stick that all of those women try to become."
BRITNI: "Save it for later boys." ROCKWELL: (Staring at her breasts) "Right. Will do." WELLINGTON: (Smacks Rockwell in the arm) "Good God, just grab the squibblies if you have to, but we must get on with this, I'm hotter than a hoon in heat."
ROCKWELL: "A hoon?" WELLINGTON: "Don't ask!" ROCKWELL: "Right. So anyway, in case all of you people watching this haven't figured out, there's a new game in town. The Movement. The British sensation, Rocky Wellington and of course me, the powerhouse of the North, Shane Rockwell. And always at our sides, the lovely and the VERY talented, and I'm not JUST talking about between the sheets, BRITNI."
WELLINGTON: "Ah bloody hell, I hate these damn cowboy boots." ROCKWELL: "Yeah, so here we are now, in the Great Lakes Championship Wrestling. Seems as if we're drawn ourselves the dubious honor of debuting against the High Plains Drifters. Interesting enough. Me n Rocky been doing some research boys. Seems to us that you were once some sort of modern day cowboy? Well, we couldn't find any ranches where mating with the cattle was outlawed, so we did the next best thing to prepare for you. See, we're down here at the 'How the West was Won!" interactive park to see what motivates you."
WELLINGTON: "So far, we must admit we've found only two things." ROCKWELL: "Sweat and big fat hairy apelike women." WELLINGTON: "Seems to me that it would fit two blokes like them." ROCKWELL: "Indeed it would. So listen, we're not going to spout out the rhetoric lines like, 'Be prepared a storm is brewing', or 'We're going to take you where you've never been before', or even the famous 'You'll never knew what hit ya', 'cause boys, we're preparing ourselves for you. We're playing the poker games. We're drinking the beer. Hell, we're even thinking of going to pan for gold (winks at Britni)."
WELLINGTON: "Now where are those damned horses so I can get these bloody boots off?" (A stagehand brings two horses from off camera. Rockwell hoists himself onto one of the horses and pulls Brinti up as well. Wellington climbs onto the other.)
ROCKWELL: "We need a fitting end to this. Roll the music." (The theme to Bonanza plays as they ride off into the sunset, slowly fading to black) xXJay StrokeXx — 2 September 2002 (Camera fades in two rather confused looking guys. We don't roll over for anybody, especially a couple of no good do-er's like yourself. I know that phrase isn't real, but i don't care. You know why? Because you made me angry! And you wouldn't like me when I'm angry. (Kurt starts tapping on Parker's shoulder, rapidly, and then whispers something into his ear) Oh, right, um... wrong phrase again. Damn copyright laws. You would be displeased with me when i'm unhappy with you. That's better. You're gonna get beat, the three count, by either one of us, and man, oh man, it's not gonna be pretty.
Goodnight, go back to your Wells. (Camera fades to black) [name withheld] — 3 September 2002 (Our scene opens with a close up of Rocky Wellington's face. As the camera pans back, we see Rocky's uppper torso moving up and down. A beautiful blue sky background is behind him. As the camera continues to pull away, we see Rocky Wellington dressed in a cowboy outfit again. He's riding a horse. Well, a horse that you stick quarters into. Behind him is not a majestic landscape, but a painted poster paper behind him. A small children's horse is moving up and down and Rocky is pretending to be a true to life cowboy. Britni enters the picture dressed in a VERY, VERY small pair of shorts and a belly shirt and a black cowboy hat. From the other side of the scene enters Shane Rockwell. Rockwell sports authentic cowboy apparel as well.)
ROCKWELL: "Hmmm, having fun Rocky?" WELLINGTON: "I'd be bloody happier if I was off this damned horse." ROCKWELL: "So we heard from those two yeehaw boys didn't we Rocky? (Wellington nods and then pretends to lasso a steer.) I believe those two talked someting about respect. (Wellington, Britni and Rockwell laugh hysterically, obviously forced.) Lemme tell yall somethin'. You two ain't proved nothing to us yet that we should be respectin' ya. In fact, how in th' hell do ya expect us to respect guys who are out on the range."
WELLINGTON: "Where the deer and antelope play?" ROCKWELL: "That's right Rocky. Glad you're catching on. But seriously, here's these two cowfolks pretending that they are superior athletes. Listen boys, do I really need to spell this out for ya? I mean, I know yall don't have anything other than the first grade from whereever in the hell yall are from. But really, listen. I'll try to talk slow and dumb it down for ya. You see Rocky Wellington and Shane Rockwell ain't two noobs who've been walkin' the street and then one day decided, 'Hey, let's drop the spurs and get ta' wrestlin.' Nah, see, hate us or love us, we're two of the youngest superstars to hit this nation, and it's just unfortunate that the High Plains Drifters will be the first notch on our way to glory."
BRITNI: "You know, those two said that women are respected for what they are: WOMEN. Not sex objects. They even said sure, you may get a couple of horny teenagers to cheer for you by having a girl ride her skirt up a little, or having her take down the buttons on her blouse, but where's it gonna get you in the long run? Lemme tell ya somethin' boys. I don't do this for the benefit of anyone but myself. You see cowboys, I know I'm sexy. I know I'm the hottest piece of ass that you two men have ever seen. As a matter of fact, (she starts to pull her belly shirt up) I'm sure you two are just salivating over this, aren't you? Yeah. (She stops the tease and pulls it back down.) Don't doubt the power of a woman boys. Keep your mind on Rocky and Shane."
ROCKWELL: "Right babycakes. Listen boys, it's simple. Get momma to fix you some nice homemade vegetable soup, you know the kind with the big hunks of meat and potatoes? Then I wantcha to saddle up the horsies and put on your Sunday best. Hell boys, we want ya to look good when we lasso you cowboys up and drop you down to the ground. Then and only then, you'll realize that there was nothing that you could do to stop it. It's like fate, hicks. You ain't gotta roll over for us, but hell boys, it'll be easier for ya. In fact, maybe if ya just roll over you'll be able to go back home to Mom and the kids and have some family time before you have to go round up the herd. Otherwise, I'm just not sure what kind of shape yall'll be in when we're finished."
BRITNI: "Not that it matters Shane." ROCKWELL: "Exactly. Once we've decimated you two hicks we're going to continue looking towards the top. After all, we WILL be the most dominant TAG TEAM in GLCW. Now boys go stick some straw between your teeth and let it all sink in. We'll be waitin' for ya. Roll the music."
(The theme to Bonanza plays as we fade out with Wellington still rocking on his horse.)
