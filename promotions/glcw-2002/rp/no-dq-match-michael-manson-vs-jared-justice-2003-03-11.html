No DQ Match: Michael Manson vs. Jared Justice

11 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
TBirdSCIL — 11 March 2003 Please keep all RP for this match in this folder... Manson — 12 March 2003 ((FADEIN: Michael Manson, wearing the "Have You Need of a Messiah?" t-shirt and black leather pants in an undisclosed backstage area.He has a zippo lighter aflame waving left to right, right to left without even looking at it.))
MANSON: I imagine this is what you wanted, Jared. After all, you ramble on and on about it. I know you're probably the biggest Mike Manson fan this side of Jean Rabesque and King Krusher, but I'm wondering about why you want this.
Let's review,I have a match with Troy Martinez and him being Troy Martinez I beat him like a POW in a Cuban prison. From his hospital room where he struggles to drink orange juice and watches Passions religiously, him and his girlfriend decide to hire someone to take me down since obviously both of them lack the competancy to do it themselves. That's where this whole master plan of theirs breaks down.
Because as strange as this sounds, they hired someone who is actually stupider than they are. First, it's supposed to be about money, a straight-up business deal. Something to be dealt with in a manner of prfessionalism and detachment. Instead they hire a quivering retard who sounds like he'd rather have sex with me than burn me. Let's face it, he talks about me all day long and hes built it into this strangely climatic orgasm for himself. In the process, he makes himself the horse's ass of the promotion by doing nothing about ranting about something he'll never actually do and constantly getting himself dq'ed. He must have spent hours telling Anarky he's going to burn me, drown me in the lake of fire, etc. Except of course no one but you, Troy, and his bag lady care. You just can't be taken seriously, shaking and taking like a spastic mental patient without his medication.
And you will never actually burn or torch me. I don't have to mention the frequent attempts on my life ranging from fire to poison. Suffice to say, even if I did gt burned, it wouldn't stop me. And wouldn't take just all the fun out of it? Even so, I doubt you're capable of it. You're simply too freaking stupid to pull something like this off.
Your first attempt for instance to try to barge on my dressing room during one of the biggest cards of the year when everyone is watching. You blatantly carry a blowtorch around and walk right into security. Now of course I was eating pez and wondering what the racket was and, really, you have no idea how disappointed I was that it was just another pyromaniac. One who really should have known that obviously my dressing room and person are going to be the most watched and guarded.
Yet, you learn nothing. Still carrying around that blowtorch, the new phallic symbol of 2003, and ranting about what you're going to do without any idea of how. You have your chance now, I suppose, but a match? No, too many logistics, to many ways for things to go wrong for you, and even with no dq..still too any rules. And, King Krusher, I will laugh my ass off once someone in the audience has their face seared and torched because you allowed this idiot to on his wreckless meaningless quest. I did warn you and after all, you should even be able to see that a 12 year old from a charity at ringside is more likely to burn than me.
In closing, Justice, some advice for the next time you try something like this. To start, don't tell anyone you're going to do it. Secondly, don't carry around your weapon like a jackass. Thirdly, forego the public forums and matches. Try an alley, bar, or even handcuffing and stripping a loved one of the victim in the basement. That always works.
And finally,ask for much more money. Because what they're paying you now, whatever deviant sexual amusement you'll get out of it.....it's not really worth me doing to you what I did to Martinez..or worse.
YinYangGuy — 17 March 2003 (FADE IN: Jared Justice is standing in a dimly candlelit room. He is holding his blowtorch in his right hand. CUE UP: "Firestarter (Empirion Mix)" by the Prodigy.)
JARED JUSTICE: Manson, the day of reckoning has come for you. You are taking things rather lightly for someone who is about to meet their miserable end. Is it about the money? It was at first. It isn't anymore. As the weeks have passed on, I'll admit that you've become a bit of an obsession. They say that you are one of the baddest....the most sadistic men on the planet. I want to see you prove it. People say your name and shudder most times after doing so.
(He mockingly shakes.) JARED JUSTICE: I don't think you are all that scary. I think that maybe we shouldn't even fight in a no DQ match. This ceremonial battle will serve no other purpose other than to line the GLCW's pockets with more greenbacks. This isn't about some commercial product. Are you a salesman?? Are you hoping to sell a few more "I am Manson - I Eat Pez like a nitwit" t-shirts as a result of this battle???
(He pauses as if he really expects an answer.) JARED JUSTICE: I have an idea, why don't they hang us both upside down and slit our wrists?? The person who passes out first will be declared the loser. That kind of thing is more your style, isn't it?? Or would you rather that we have a contest to see who's better at gratifying a goat? Would you like that??
(Again he pauses waiting for an answer. He seems a bit upset when he doesn't get one.) JARED JUSTICE: Maybe we can even snort pez afterwards!!! You aren't taking me seriously up until this point. Maybe you should. If I was someone who wasn't serious, I wouldn't have stuck around. I would have left after fighting the likes of Larry Tact and Anarky. They are so mundane. They were so dull, that I would have left out of boredom. This is all about me and you, it always has been don't you see that?
(He pauses slightly and defiantly continues as if he felt he was stopping Manson from answering him this time.)
JARED JUSTICE: We've been on a collision course since day one and it isn't about the money. It's about two guys who just don't care. We just don't care. Do you care? I don't. I don't even care if you care. I don't care if children in the crowd get burned. What kind of place is a wrestling match for kids anyway? Shouldn't they be home eating pez and watching Sesame Street??
(He grins knowing that Manson would love to answer that but Justice won't let him.) JARED JUSTICE: You like digging up dead children and parading them around as your puppets! Well, the dead children called me and they said that they are tired of you. They want me to bash your brains in so they can rest in peace!!! They want me to kill you and feed your remains to sheep. Do they have Pepto Bismol for sheep? I bet eating someone like you would repeat on the poor sheep for days!!! Manson, I am coming for you. You have not days, but hours....minutes even. Your charade is over, done. Countless others have tried, but I will be the one to succeed. Count your blessings on one finger and then kiss your ass good bye!!
(FADE OUT) Manson — 17 March 2003 ((FADEIN: Michael Manson wearing his "Manson is Love" t-shirt and black leather pants with a bucket of water in front of him, only a few inches deep.))
MANSON: So another self-righteous sycophant who really wishes he was me, once more tells me my time is over, I'll go down, etc etc. Apparently we have to settle this in the ring since Jared obviously isn't smart enough to anonymously burn my house down or shoot me in an alley. Then again, maybe it's just the attention he craves. After all, he burns me to death and then maybe people will like him an he'll finally hve that craved father figure he's been looking for sinc his mother's boyfriend used to put out cigarettes on his arm.
There's really no point in settlng this in goat gratifying or pez snorting either since I'm probably better at that than you anyway. You might want to come across uncaring in an attempt to be as inhuman as I naturally am, but already your actions say otherwise. Accepting a task for money, coming after me at all, that shows that you do care about something. That you want to do it in a public forum confined with so many rules shows you care...you want the attention...the mystique..and whatnot.
In fact, it's even better that you don't care about burning the children front row. That way if and when they can, they can buy the new Michael Manson artificial skin and burn wear. After all, they're there for that sort of thing, if that answers your question. They want to see me crucify the likes of you, mock society and all norms, so that they can go to school the next day and get suspended trying to. See, just like you, they want to be me.
It's fairly obvious, since all you do is rant like a maniac an try to use that devilish rapier wit I am known for. Of course you miss the point completely and end up sounding more like Jarod Poe, but he wants to be me too. Hey, maybe you guys can be friends. But you'd hav to fight it out with Jean Rabesque over who's the biggest Mike Manson fan this side of the Atlantic.
But since you're transparent, unoriginal, saying and threatening me with all the same things I've heard before, I've decided to try to help you out. If only to make this somewhat more interesting for the sake of my own attention span.
See, your problem is you've already told me your master plan. In fact,you've basically told everyone. Now, what better thing could you do other than not try to burn me, but disfigure me in some other way? Now, let's be creative. I mean there's suffocation with plastic...cutting with various objects..poison.....hanging....crucifixion,though that's another thing you probably couldn't do as well I can.
My first choice would be drowning though. Here. ((Manson kicks the bucket in front of him and circles form in the water.)) The complete opposite of fire, no one will see it coming. It only takes a few inches of water too. Simple, even for you. And then if you really want to impress people, you'll find a way to drown all he children in the front row. You know, just to show how uncaring you are. Then they can sell t-shirts that say "I was drowned by Jared Justice and I Got Was This Lousy T-Shirt". See? You don't need to be a pyromaniac to be loved, accepted, and attended to. Just be that Jared Justice we all know you are inside and go for drowning. The kids will love it.
