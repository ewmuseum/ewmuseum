GLCW — Roleplay

entering upon a new day...

24 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
invisible — 24 September 2002 ((FADEIN: The camera starts in slow motion. Its in a small time club somewhere in Michigan. The lights, which normally would be flashing away, are coming in slowly, flashing bright, then slowly fading out. The camera moves, jerkingly, closer and closer to a figure at the bar. The camera stops, and the lights still flash in slow motion. The blue light flashes and puts a blue shadow on the man's face. All of a sudden, the camera switches back into real time and the lights start flashing faster, making the shadows cover the man's identity except every so often when a white light illuminates half his face.))
MAN: "Fall is finally here. My favorite time of the year, perhaps. Everything that has bloomed, everything that has thrived will soon, slowly, but surely, begin to fade. All of those with stars in their eyes, all of those with bounces in their steps, begin to falter. Its at this point in the year where my senses start to sharpen. When I see those around me expose their weaknesses. I can see people already beginning to unconciously expose themselves. And soon, very soon, I will swoop down into Great Lakes Championship Wrestling and exploit every last one of those weaknesses. That's what I do best, you know. I exploit for my gain."
((The camera does a 180 degree turn onto the other side of the man's face. As it does this the white light flashes. This, combined with the slower motion shot of the camera, illuminates the man's face almost to full recognition. The camera shows short bleached blonde hair, with piercing blue eyes that wink at the camera almost to say "Do you know me? Because I know you." As it reaches its focus point, the light changes to purple, and the camera resumes to real time.))
MAN: "For now I'm just sitting here. Not just in this run down club, but clubs like this all around the state. It helps me recollect what it once felt like to have no cares in the world. Many burdens have fallen on my shoulders the past few months. Many burdens clogged my mind and took their tolls on my body. But now that I have finally broken free from the shackles of these burdens I can concentrate on finally pursuing my goals in life. To come into the GLCW and face those who I've avoided for months upon months is one of these goals. The wait is hard to endure. To have come so close, but yet remain in the shadows like I have is one of the harder things Ive had to do lately. I want so very bad to jump right into the thick of things. But I know I must wait. I know that I have not done enough evaluating .. that I haven't finished analyzing the GLCW. But pretty soon all will be well."
((The flashing lights in the club go out and the crowd on the dance floor groans. The silhouette of the man soon blends in with the blackness of the room. When the lights come back on, the man has left. But a small card is left standing on the spot of the table where he sat. The camera closes up on it and it reads...))
"On sunny days im alright and i walk in the light .. but as fall consumes the calender and the light slowly fades quicker and quicker, the true self comes out ... see you soon. -ose"
((FADEOUT: As the strobe lights start flashing again. The camera cuts back to slow motion and the shot cuts out as the screen turns a reddish pink.))
