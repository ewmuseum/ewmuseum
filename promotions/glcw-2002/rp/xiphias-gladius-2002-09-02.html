GLCW — Roleplay

Xiphias gladius....

2 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
JC — 2 September 2002 (“It was several days removed from Riptide, and we saw not only Nevada Smith and the Unholy Alliance but Maelstrom as well… On that faithful night, we learned just how deceitful JC can be, as he introduced the Unholy Alliance to the GLCW… A lesson that has yet to begin… My first show interrupted by JC and his lil posse in which they actually TOOK OVER MY SHOW! And after several days of thinking about it, I decided to head to Malec’s office… As I was about to enter it, the door flies open and lo and behold, who do I see on the other side of the door!? NEVADA SMITH! But that wouldn’t have surprised me but what did,was who was behind him… It was CZAR folks! Yes, Czar, the man who has been a thorn in JC’s side for quite some time and in his hands was what appeared to be a contract… My quick thinking mind put one and one together and realized what I had just witnessed! What might that be you ask!? Well let me spell it out for you folks, Nevada Smith, Czar, Malec and KK, all in the same room with Czar holding what appeared to be a contract!? It’s either one of two things… But I’m not going to even go there, no folks, this one I am going to let you figure out… So there I was face to face with Nevada and Czar… While Nevada’s smirk made me nervous, I was preoccupied with Czar’s empty blank stare, it’s no secret that our paths have crossed and 98% of the time I was at the tail end of an (bleep) whipping, and here I was face to face with my nemesis… So I took a few steps back and braced myself for whatever was to come only nothing came, no fist in my face, no foot up my (bleep)… And THAT folks made me even more nervous! But I took the chance; I asked Nevada and Czar for an interview! And guess what folks!? I GOT IT! The following are excerpts from that interview, an interview that even shocked me”….)
(We then see the intros to Rico Sauvé’s show “Burning Questions” and after several minutes RS is shown… Next to him sits Nevada Smith, with Czar in the third chair burning holes into RS…)
RS: Good evening folks and welcome to Burning Questions! And as promised I have tonight as my very exclusive guests, none other than the controversial Nevada Smith and Czar! So tell me Nevada, what was all the false hype and deception about you coming to the GLCW about!?
NEVADA: THAT amigo you need to ask JC, I came on my own accord! My agenda is much different from what JC has up his sleeves! Just remember this; the greatest deception was perpetrated by the devil when he made the masses believe he didn’t exist!
RS: I see… So am I to take it that the deception still continues that what I saw a few days ago in Malec’s office was part of that deception!?
NEVADA: Take it for whatever you want Rico! What transpired in Malec’s office is strictly between us (points to Czar and then himself) and Malec!
RS: Okay… Are we ever going to see Nevada Smith in the GLCW!? NEVADA: Hard to say Rico, it all depends on circumstances… RS: Well speaking of circumstances, Golem, Jobber and O’Neill have made their feelings known where it concerns you…. Care to comment!?
NEVADA: (Smirks) Do I care to comment!? Do you think my feelings were hurt that I need to comment!?
RS: Err, no just thought you may have had something to say about their comments… NEVADA: Their comments mean crap to me… Golem is an individual that is hell bent on destroying everything that is placed in front of him…. The man is completely spaced out, all that gothical hoola, hoola crap; kiss my hairy (bleep)… I like Golem though, that’s why I keep him around… Golem is one hard nut to crack, very unpredictable, an intelligent freak of nature and that is part of what makes him dangerous… Our battles have been written in the annuals of wrestling…
RS: As was your last battle with Maelstrom! (There is an eerie silence as Nevada glares at RS… Czar is about to get up, but is stopped by Nevada…)
NEVADA: And your point being!? RS: Nothing! That battle between you and Maelstrom was one of the most violent and brutal matches I have EVER witnessed! I have to wonder though, that it is strange that you all show up on the same night, at the same GLCW event… Any comments on that Nevada!?
NEVADA: Don’t burn that lil pea brain of yours wondering Rico! Mael doesn’t sweat me, if it’s an instant replay that he’s looking for… Look no further Mael! Back yard, front yard, parking lot, alley way anywhere you want Mael!
RS: Are you issuing Maelstrom a challenge!? NEVADA: (Cynically smirks) There you go again Rico, putting damn words in my mouth! I am not under contract with The GLCW, Mael is! So knowing Malec as I do, he would NEVER allow it! You see ya ignorant bastard, Malec cannot afford to have Mael hurt yet again! Mael is going to be his money maker! So why would he risk that by sanctioning a match between myself and Mael! But anytime Mael wants to jump the fence, I’ll be waiting on the other side!
RS: So if I could ask, what did transpired in Malec’s office!? CZAR: NONE OF YOUR DAMN BUSINESS! RS: Hey calm down dude! No need to get all pissed off at me, I just happened along! CZAR: How about I just happen to kick your (bleep)!? (Without warning he jumps up and rushes towards Rico, but Nevada heads him off and restraints him… Whispers something in his ear which seems to have a calming down effect on Czar…)
RS: Sorry dude didn’t mean to piss you off! Let me change the question… Nevada, you mentioned Golem and Maelstrom… What about O’Neill and Jobber!?
NEVADA: What about them!? All feds have those types Rico, you should know that by now… Disgruntled individuals who blame all their short comings on others! O’Neill wants to believe that I have “STROKE” (Smirks as he makes the quotation marks…) if I had any stroke son, I’d be running the GLCW, not no has been wrestler turned commissioner or a washed up promoter grasping at straws! And as for “The Jobber’s" insinuations, go stroke yourself Jobber! Your opinions are just that… Opinions! And you’re entitled to them… No matter how asinine they may be! We all see the world in different colors amigo, I see black, you see white; I see white, you see black… That and the fact that I give a rat’s (bleep) about you or your opinions, basically sum up my opinion of ya, comprende amigo!? And as for the rest of the GLCW peons, Malec and company included, remember be careful for what you wish, because with me you will most certainly get it!
(“At this point we went to a GLCW Racine Riptide promo and when we returned I was sitting there by myself… Pondering and wondering what the hell is going on here… Two words linger in the back of my mind… “DECEPTION” and “PERCEPTION”! That and the burning questions, what did transpired behind the closed doors of Malec’s office and what are Nevada and Czar up to… And let’s not forget about Maelstrom and JC and the Unholy Alliance… Yet another triad of chaos… With Racine Riptide just a few days away, what can we expect to explode!? Well time has about run out, so I will leave you all with just two words…“DECEPTION” and “PERCEPTION”! Good night everyone”…)
(Promo cuts for the Racine Riptide are shown…. Fade to black….)
