GLCW — Roleplay

Yuji Yamashita vs. Jared Justice

16 August 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
TBirdSCIL — 16 August 2003 Please keep all RP for this match in this folder... YinYangGuy — 28 August 2003 Ready for a Fight (FADE IN: Jared Justice is standing in a back alley. CUE UP: "Just Barely Breathing" by Killswitch Engage. Justice is leaning against a brick wall.)
JARED JUSTICE: You know, I'll be the first to admit that I just haven't been myself since War Games. I'm not exactly sure that I can put my finger on why that is either. I guess I'm just bored. I'm bored with all of the cookie cutter personas running around here. I see the likes of Michael Manson and his knock off Madonna Wayne Grossard and they make the weird just ordinary. Being weird, being a freak....it's been done to death already!! In my next match, perhaps I'll fight someone who's different. I'm scheduled to fight Yuji Yamahita. His name certainly sounds different. He certainly looks different than the run of the mill guy around here. So, I have high hopes that our match will in the least bit be mildly exciting.
(Justice folds his arms across his chest.) JARED JUSTICE: Now, Yamashita has yet to address me. I hope that he doesn't approach me with either the dull and boring "I'm going to rip off your head and puke down your throat" attitude or the "I'm the newest gift from heaven to wrestling and you must bow before me you sniveling swine" bit. Yamashita, do you know me? Do you know anything about me?? Here's a novel thought, let's not waste our time with some long drawn out war of words....let's just fight!! What do ya say?
(FADE OUT)
