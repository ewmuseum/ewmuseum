GLCW — Roleplay

"Rage o' Fire" Jared Wells vs. The Cannonball Kidd

25 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
TBirdSCIL — 25 February 2003 Please keep all RP for this match in this folder... GARTHIsTheLaw — 1 March 2003 (Cueup: "Burn Baby Burn" by Ash) (Things start out with a black screen. After a few seconds, the words "CANNONBALL KIDD" appear, grow seemingly closer to the camera, and then fade out. They are followed by "A MAN" and "WITH A MESSAGE". After the text is a close-up on Cannonball Kidd's eyes, covered as always by his signature flight goggles)
Cannonball Kidd: As I'm sure everyone knows well by now, I am a self-actualized man. And although that makes me the absolute best person I can be, sometimes, even a self-actualized person can feel a little unhappy. I mean, first, I had trouble sending my Message to Nemesis, since he used boredom as a weapon against me and impaired my ability to help by being so monotonous that I couldn't concentrate. And then, I was left off the latest house card, depriving me of another chance to spread my Message. I was starting to think that maybe I was doing something wrong.
(Camera pans back. Cannonball appears unhappy, but then grins in an inspirational fashion)
Cannonball Kidd: But no self-actualized man would let such doubts plague him! So first, I arranged to appear at that very house show that I was left off of, since GLCW needs my Message as much as I need GLCW as a medium to spread it. And now I'm in an even better mood, because I recently found out that my opponent at the next edition of GLCW Riptide will be none other than "Rage 'o Fire" Jared Wells. I mean, sure, since my GLCW debut I've already faced a few legends in our business, but Jared Wells is more than just another legend. Back when I was first breaking into the wrestling business around 1996, 1997, he was THE legend - the man who had done it all. Anything you wanted to do in the business, chances were that Rage 'o Fire had done it first.
(The camera pans back to show Cannonball from the waist up. His eyes go wide and he puts both hands out in front of him in a "Stop!" gesture)
Cannonball Kidd: But that was long ago - back in the mid-90's. And now what was once a legend in the wrestling business has degenerated into a joke. After countless retirements and returns, the man I once had the utmost respect for has become a shell of his former self. And that, Jared Wells, is why I approached you at the Green Bay house show. I knew that the only way you could possibly return to your legendary status is with my proven program for self-actualization. Now, unfortunately, you chose to reject my program.
(The camera zooms back on Cannonball's face. He shakes his head, then looks back up at the camera)
Cannonball Kidd: I was disappointed, but because I have the deepest respect for the legend of Rage 'o Fire, I've decided not to abandon the cause. And that's why I'm glad GLCW decided to give me a match with you at Riptide. You've rejected my program once, but I think that once you've had a chance to really listen to what I have to offer, you'll begin to see how truly beneficial my program will be for you. Think about it, man! Instead of being Jared Wells, the multiple retiree and all-around joke as far as wrestling careers go, you could go back to being Rage 'o Fire, the absolute LEGEND of the business!
(Another inspirational grin) Cannonball Kidd: All you have to do is say the words, Wells, and I can start helping you build towards self-actualization today! I think we can both agree that you need it. But the question is, are you willing to admit it? I certainly hope so, because there's nothing I'd enjoy more than the full return of Rage 'o Fire! But before I can acknowledge you as the Rage 'o Fire of old, you must make the first move and acknowledge me as a Man...
(Zoom back in on Cannonball's eyes) Cannonball Kidd: ...with a Message. (Fade out) [name withheld] — 2 March 2003 2:23pm ET(Bel Camp, Maryland) (camera opens up to 'Rage 'o Fire' Jared Wells wearing baggy blue jeans, GLCW polo shirt, and a black leather jacket over. A cold day in Maryland as Wells approaches a pizza shop in some shopping center. The shop is called Riverside Pizzeria. The cameraman follows Wells, then opens the door as all the guys in the place greet Wells. He walks up to the counter and starts to slap five with everybody. There's a few people in the dining room noticing him. Camera pans around the place as you see old WWL pictures of Wells wearing the world title, a few pictures of Wells/Edmunds crotch chopping with autographs. The manager named Rich in his early fifty's walks up and shakes Wells hand)
RICH: Hey man what brings you here today? WELLS: Oh just stop by to get something to eat, and to show the GLCW where I came from. RICH: Whatever you want Jared, it's on the house buddy! WELLS: Thanks Mr. Rich! (one of the cooks then yells towards the camera) COOK: Hey RAGE.....BEAT THAT CANNONBALL KIDD'S ASS FOR ME!!!! WELLS: (Smiles) Now.....now! In due time my friend. RICH: Yeah we were just talking about your match coming up wit 'da KIDD... WELLS: I know you have. And I remember when I worked here before my wrestling career that's all we talk about.....wrestling. Wrestling this, wrestling that. Who'da ever thought I would be wrestling today?
RICH: I'll have to say JARED, you've been lookin' good as of late. I mean your like a nice guy now! (LAUGHS)
WELLS: I'm changing for the better Mr. Rich. I think it's time get serious and actually go out and win without any B-S or politics. Pretty much that's why I'm rolling this film here. To show where I came from. I'm legit now baby. Now my match up with the CANNONBALL KIDD should a barn burner. He's been 'round for awhile now and finally we will meet one on one.
RICH: Correct me if I'm wrong Jared, but it seems like your getting revenge on all veterans?
WELLS: No, I just have something to prove to all the veterans. The KIDD has been around for awhile. I remember when he first came up. I say RESPECT is the key issue here in this match up. KIDD will put up a fight but I need to take advantage of his flaws. And if you are out there KIDD......LISTEN UP! I need this win more than anybody. As I said to everybody a few weeks ago, this might be my only shot! We'll lay all the jokes and rumors aside and get serious. RAGE-BOMB is where it all lays dude! ONE, TWO, THREE it's over, your out, DONE! I respect you man, but I gotta do what I gotta do. Remember one thing..........
(WELLS points to all the cooks) COOKS: TIME HAS COME TO RAAAAAGGGGEEEE!!!!! WELLS: See 'ya in the ring BRRRAAHHHHH! JARED WELLS: LOOK DUDE....I can't wait any much longer man. I've asked the camera man to tape this segment. CANNONBALL KIDDY, you want to play games? (Takes deep breath) Bro, I don't have time for games. Your little encounter with me was pretty cute. Self-Actualization? Common man, what happened to you? Your head is out on cloud nine some where. Yes I did reject your program. You think it would help my career out of the gutter? (Laughs then waves fists in the air) No way pal.......THESE WILL. Like I said over and over I RESPECT YOU.......but I need to knock some sence into your head. WAKE UP, and realize your not god. I know what your going through man. In your mind nothing can happen to you....But once you step into the ring dude, LIGHTS OUT JACK! Maybe you'll wake up from this fairytale. But one thing is for sure man.........RAGE IS APON YOU........
TIME HAS COME TO RAGE....... (fadeout) GARTHIsTheLaw — 7 March 2003 (Cueup: "Innocent" by Our Lady Peace) (Things start out with a black screen. After a few seconds, the words "CANNONBALL KIDD" appear, grow seemingly closer to the camera, and then fade out. They are followed by "A MAN" and "WITH A MESSAGE". After the text is a close-up on Cannonball Kidd's eyes, covered as always by his signature flight goggles)
Cannonball Kidd: It seems, Mr. Wells, as though you have reacted to my attempts to introduce you to my program unfavorably. I would be lying if I told you I'm not just a LITTLE disappointed. Surely you, of all people, must realize how valuable my program would be for your career right now. I mean, if people like "Showtime" Steven James and "Lost Cause" Chris O'Neill can't figure out what my program could do for them, that's fine. These are guys who've never really been successful in the wrestling business. They'd LIKE to be, I'm sure, but for the most part they don't know what they're missing. But YOU, Rage...YOU are quite different.
(Camera zooms out to show Cannonball from the shoulders up) Cannonball Kidd: You've lived the life of a true champion in this business. You know what it's really like to be on top. But that's not where you are right now, is it? I mean, sure, you're not exactly down at the bottom, but you're not too far away from it. Believe me, Wells, I know what it's like - I know how much it can hurt when you realize that you were once at the top, and that you've since sunk to the bottom. I was at rock bottom while I was in the crushing grip of my painkiller addiction years ago...I'd gone from being a top contender to being a nothing in the business, and a nothing to everyone I knew.
(Camera zooms out to show Cannonball from the waist up. He strikes a heroic pose) Cannonball Kidd: I know you've spent years struggling with YOUR inner demons too, you know. I know you think that your dedication can bring you past all obstacles to your success now, and you may be right. All I did was offer you a surefire way to get back on track, instead of sitting idly by while you take your chances with more of the same old, same old. I've watched your promos, and you haven't changed a bit from the old days, it seems. Nostalgia will only get you so far.
(Cannonball slowly shakes his head) Cannonball Kidd: Maybe the way I tried to get you to accept my program was a little extreme. Extreme...but necessary. I knew you just blew me off when I told you about my program in the ring, and I wanted you to take it as seriously as it deserves to be taken. Don't you see, Rage? I had to HURT you in order to HELP you. And I can still help you.
(Camera zooms back in on Cannonball's face) Cannonball Kidd: And I can start right now. I know you have your heart set on doing this by yourself, Rage, but if you find out that doesn't work, I want you to know that there is another option. If you really want to get your career back on track, doing the same old routine won't help - but self-actualization will make ALL the difference. This opportunity doesn't come around every day, Rage, and if you pass it up, it could be a long time before you get another one anything like it. All you have to do is tell me, and I can start helping you on the way to self-actualization today. The time has come, Jared Wells...not to rage...but to accept me as a Man...
(Zoom in on Cannonball's eyes) Cannonball Kidd: ...with a Message. (Fade out) [name withheld] — 8 March 2003 (CUEUP: Cochise by AudioSlave) (fast flashing pictures of Wells appearing from GLCW signing to winning his debut match against Jon Savage. music slowly fades as the camera zooms out to Wells walking down a dark street wearing blue jeans, black leather jacket, and his hands are in his pockets. he slowly walks down this dirty street kicking rocks as you see poor ruined houses that people live in)
JARED WELLS: The MAN with a MESSAGE! The MAN that will not only save me but all of my fans as well. (Smiles) I see you enjoy taking cheap shots at me not only in the business, but as my personal life as well. Let me tell you one thing CANNONBALL KIDD.......I know what your trying to do and I don't like it, nor will I accept it. I know my career is at the bottom. But they say in order to find your roots you have to go back to the beginning and that is where I am today. When I signed with the GLCW I told all the staff that I wanted to start from the bottom. KIDD, you just so happen to be in the way. New time, new era, NEW RAGE. I can't rewrite the past but I can predict the future. 'RAGE 'o FIRE is runnin' stronger than ever. My millions of fans might have turned into the hundreds......but slowly.......AND SURELY we will win 'em all back. After I defeat you ONE, TWO, THREE.........I'm not going to decide if I let you go on with the after life.....my fans will. I could come out here and say I hate your guts KIDD......but I won't......I enjoy watching you with your mouth. We'll see what is stronger.......your in ring work......or your mouth. You and I have been around for a long time.........in a way I respect you......but then again your lost in your head......Not only are you lost, but you feel FEAR runnin' through your blood.
(Wells approaches an old house that looks like its been vacant for years) WELLS: This is where I used to live man. I remember when I was young I would dress up like a wrestler and pose in the mirror. I wanted to have it all dude. I did have it all at one time......then lost it all......and you know the funny thing? I don't want any of it back. There's something I want more than all........it's so deep I can't even explain. Hopefully after my long run as 'RAGE 'o FIRE', I'll be able to show the whole world. Self-Actualization is nothing that I want to be apart of. You say you have to hurt me to help me? (Smiles) Wait 'till we get in the ring son, and I 'll show you what PAIN is all about. You've faced many in your past but you've never had a challenge like this. This might be your gravest challenge to date. I will walk that aisle.......I will look you in the eyes........while the sweat begins to drip from your brow and your lips begin to quiver............millions behind me, breathing down your neck.............I WILL TELL YOU............TIME HAS COME TO RAGE........not days or weeks........but NOW!!!!!!! You say you can help me? HELP YOURSELF.........your gonna need it KIDD! Self-actualization will fail........due to a virus called RAGE. The journey of RAGE continues..........KIDD.......PAL........YOUR DONE.............enough said..............And oh yeah......WITH A MESSAGE!
