"The Future" Brett Irvine vs. Nick Kurtel

26 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
TBirdSCIL — 26 August 2002 All RP for this match goes in this folder... RaskalRogue — 3 September 2002 (Fade in to the darkness of woods. Only small slivers of light are visible, wavering in the night wind and adding to the mysteriousness of the scene. The scene embraces that of the past. All around are trees, huge in their might next to the small scrub that are bushes. The camera pans in search of finding its purpose here. It finally rests on the outline of a diminutive boulder. On this rock sits a figure, clad in khakis and leather jacket. He motions to the camera with a subtle nod of the head, then moves not more than an inch, and begins to speak.)
Nick: Nighttime... in the comfort of the forest. Nothing is greater in this world. You're in touch with nature at its finest. And no other person is here to disrupt the perfection. Breathe it, cherish it, for it will not always be here. The past held more, the future holds less. Sadly, these woods don't bring tales of magik and special creatures. Nay, they now bring a simple escape from our lives of X-box, cubicles, and political fiascos. One day in the future, there will be no more of this comfort. The future brings happiness, no doubt, but it brings far less than is wanted or needed.
(He glances around, then breathes in deeply and slowly shakes his head back and forth.) Now I'm sure you're all probably thinking I had a little too much to smoke, but this is not so. Here's my thing. Yea, I've been a little busy this past Labor Day weekend, but I come back to cut a promo in response to my opponent's, and there is none! What's a man to do? I sit here in the comfort of these woods to lose myself in another world, because apparently the one I'm in right now just isn't up to par. Brett, take a moment out of your busy day and realize you have a job. I am your job. Your goal is to come to me and try ever-so-hard to beat me. Though, I must tell you, this job, this quest won't end in your favor. We will meet in the ring, we will fight in the ring. But the whole time it is only you searching for my weakness, searching endlessly in this forest for a better future, a future where you are champion and I lie at your feet.
There was a time here where people believed in a better future, they looked forward to it. There was magik and intrigue. The darkness brought less fear and more mystery. Now, it brings one thing. It brings me to you. And all of your character creations and talk of how you are the one true icon won't help you in the ring. You say you can predict the future. Well, fortune teller, perhaps you should watch your step, because the future is changing before your eyes. It clouds your vision, this arrogance of yours. Watch your step, appearances can deceive. When it all comes down, your muscles and your arrogance will get you somewhere, to the mat at the 3-count. Brett, you speak of being the future...well, the future doesn't look so bright... welcome to my world.
(He backs down off the boulder, slides his hands into his pants pockets, and turns off into the darkness. The wind picks up by the most miniscule amount, scattering what little light was noticed earlier.)
Fade out
