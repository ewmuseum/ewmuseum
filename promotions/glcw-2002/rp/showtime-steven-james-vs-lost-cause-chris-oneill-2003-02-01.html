GLCW — Roleplay

"Showtime" Steven James vs. "Lost Cause" Chris O'Neill

1 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
TBirdSCIL — 1 February 2003 All RP for this match goes here... scwhat82 — 2 February 2003 Fade in to Showtime Steven James sitting at a bar drinking a tall Sam Adams, after a few sips Lady V sits down and joins him and orders martini, she looks at Showtime and begins speaking.
LV: How's it going Steve? SJ: Pretty good, how bout u? LV: Good, depressed about your loss? SJ: Nah, as usual i learned from my mistakes and will hopefully not make them again. LV: Well you know Chris O'Neill hasn't been racking up the victories as well so i guess both of you have a lot to prove.
the bartender brings the martini and sets it down, she thanks him. SJ:laughing Are you here to drink or to give an interview? LV: Maybe a little bit of both. SJ: You know O'Neill will probably say something about me hitting on you or something, they always do.
LV: Hey, if they can't handle our friendship then that's there problem. So, what are you planning on doing in that match?
SJ: The usual, I'm gonna wrestle like only Showtime can and i'm gonna bring that boy to school by beating him pillar to post.
holds up his glass of beer, to which Lady V holds up her martini SJ: Here's to my victory. Salute. They clang glasses scwhat82 — 12 February 2003 Fade in to Showtime Steven James standing in the middle of a ring as he always does near the night of an event. He leans against the ropes and glances around the empty arena. He looks at the camera and begins.
Chris, your response...or lack of for that matter, is noted. Now, you may be waiting until the last moment or what not but the bottom line I don't care because no matter how little you respond the outcome will be the same. You see there is this bullseye on me as an "up and comer" and i do not see you coming anywhere close proving it wrong. So you can keep quiet all you want son because i have one goal in mind and that goal is to take you to school.
Lady V works down the aisle. LV: Hey Steve. SJ: Hey Veronica, what's up? You look a little stressed. LV: Because I am...it's just this whole thing with those pictures... SJ: Hey, don't you worry, i'm sure Malec has a plan. LV: Yeah. SJ: You wanna grab a Corona? LV: Sure, i need one. SJ: Ok, lets go. looks at camera Oh, by the way Chris, it's Showtime.
