"Showtime" Steven James vs. Jon Savage

25 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
TBirdSCIL — 25 February 2003 Please keep all RP for this match in this folder... scwhat82 — 26 February 2003 [updated:LAST EDITED ON Feb-26-03 AT 10:31&nbsp;PM (EST)] Fade in to Showtime Steven James walking around an aquarium, he stops by the Great White Shark tank, he watches with amusement for a while and begins talking
Beautiful aren't they? Magnificent really. You see what we're looking at is the best hunter on earth, they're strength is unparallel, and their senses unmatched. They are the perfect killing machine. Did you know they can sense a drop of blood in billions of gallons of water? And when they get their target within sight they circle it 3 times and attack without a moments hesitation. You see they are the ancestors of an ancient shark believed to be extinct named Megaladon. The Cliff notes version of them is basically they were the size of a Greyhound bus and if alive today, no one would be safe in the water no matter what.
So Jon Savage, it is apparent to me that we will now face off and you will learn that like the sharks that i have spent countless of hours studying i am one hell of a hunter. Oh sure we'll wrestle a little bit and go back and forth, but when i see the moment, i will seize it and strike without hesitation. And before you know it will be on your back looking at the lights, wondering what the hell just happened to you.
And for you Cannonball Kid, you're on borrowed time son, and if i were you, i'd be looking over my back.
So Savage, get ready because it's Showtime. [name withheld] — 6 March 2003 Camera fades into Wildside and Savage in a motorboat, fishing on a relaxing weekend just before Milwaukke Riptide...
SAVAGE: I want to talk to you about what happened with the Jarod Wells match..... WILDSIDE: yes??? SAVAGE: What the hell were you thinking? WILDSIDE: Look. I know you're upset. But we can't look in the past. We've got to look to the future.....
SAVAGE: "Showtime" Steven James..... WILDSIDE: Right, we faced him before, didn't we? SAVAGE: Could have, possibly. Although it could've been another scrub named Showtime. So what's your plan, genius?
WILDSIDE: We've got to use his preoccupation with The Cannonball Kid against him. You know, keep that feud fresh in his mind.....
SAVAGE: (smiling) Oh yes, I see. He's the shark, and we're the shark hunter...... WILDSIDE: (chuckling) Yes, that's right. It will be very interesting to see what happens at Milwaukee Riptide.....
Cam shows Savage's line going crazy WILDSIDE: Looks like you've got a bite.... SAVAGE: Yeah, I do.... Savage "wrestles" with the fish for a few moments before pulling the fish into the boat, holding it up for Wildside and the camera to see
WILDSIDE: Look. It's a suckerhead..... SAVAGE: You know, boss, this is symbolic in more ways than one. I've got a suckerhead here, and well, Steven James, you could be a suckerhead, too. See, if you're "the shark," as you like to call yourself, then that makes me the shark hunter. Now, James, it's better to be the hunter because the hunter is the one that's in control. The hunter stealthly encircles his prey....unbenownst to the prey.....until the prey shows it's perverbial achilles heal. Than boom! The hunter strikes! That's how our match is going to be at Riptide. You'll know I'm there but you won't know when I'll strike.
Cam shows Savage turning back to his fishing.... WILDSIDE: You gonna cokk that fish and eat it all at once? SAVAGE: No. I'm going to enjoy torturing him slowly and enjoy every bite. I think I'll name him......Showtime.
Cam shows both of them laughing menacingly.... scwhat82 — 7 March 2003 [updated:LAST EDITED ON Mar-07-03 AT 06:04&nbsp;PM (EST)][/i] Fade in to Showtime Steven James sitting in a hotel room watching a tape of the Savage interview. When it ends he turns off the tv, thinks for a couple of seconds and then bursts out laughing. He finally calms down, regains his composure, looks at the camera,and begins.[/i]
Savage, Savage, Savage...what's up with you son? I mean come on, like i didn't see that one coming a mile away? I knew that when i started talking about sharks you would come out with what you did, i know that sharks are hunted, i watch the discovery channel. But what you fail to realize is that you, by no means, are a hunter. You're just a little fish in a big ocean. You're nothing, you're nobody, you're just someone i've been asked to take care of in a match that's not even being televised. I've been asked to demote myself so you can have an opponent.
James gets up walks over to his bar, has a sip of his martini, and looks at the camera with a slightly more serious look on his face.
Savage let me give you a little information about being a hunter seeing that you think you are one. First of all, don't hunt what you can't catch. Second, don't hunt what you can't see. And Third, don't hunt what you can't kill. You see Savage when we get in that ring you will not be about to to catch me, you won't be able to see me or what it is i will be doing to you, and you sure as hell be able to kill me. You see i'm not just your normal prey, you may think that you have me in your sights and that you, as the hunter, has the advantage. But what you fail to realize is that i know where you are at all times, i know what you're doing, i know what you're thinking and i know what you're planning.
voice drops And when you think i'm ripe for the killing i turn around and attack, and you will never see it coming. You go from being the hunter, to being the hunted in no time flat and before you know it, you're on your back for a three count, and you have to look in the eyes of your manager wildside as he looks on hopelessly knowing that his client has just lost another match and both of you will know at that moment just how much of a lost cause you are and that your rank in this fed is not going up at any time.
So get ready Savage, because hunting season's over for you. [name withheld] — 9 March 2003 We see Jon Savage angrily pacing in front of Johnny Wildside's desk after seeing the latest "promo" from Showtime. Wildside tries to calm Savage, but to no avail. Letting his emotions get the best of him, Savage picks up a chair and sends it sailing out the office window. A few office workers come into the office, and Savage hollers at them to leave. He then turns to the camera.....
SAVAGE: Showtime, if bulls--- was worth anything, then son, you'd be a millionaire. The rage within me is very powerful. Sometimes, it boils over. That chair that I just flung out a fourth floor office window is symbolic. That's exactly what I'm gonna do to you when we meet inside the ring. I'm gonna toss you around the ring like a little cheerleader that's in the backseat of the team captain's car. Then when you're lying on the mat, wondering how you got there in such bad shape, then I'm gonna finish you off in quick fashion. There ain't gonna be nothin' left of your punk a$$ except a little grease spot in the middle of [President] Malec's canvas. They're gonna have to scrape his ring off with a q-tip in order to identify who you are. Right now, you're breathin. I can't do anything about that. But when I get you in the ring.....oooh boy.....I'm gonna walk a mudhole in you and stomp it dry.....
Showtime, you've made a deadly mistake by raisin' my ire. I was rasslin' all the greats while you were still diapered, boy. It's gonna feel like a trip to the woodshed when I get finished with you. The time for talkin' is over, Showtime. It's time to meet your maker.......
THE SHOWSTOPPA With that, Savage storms out of Wildside's office......
