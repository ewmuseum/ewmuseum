GLCW — Roleplay

Aftermath of PPV...

11 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[name withheld] — 11 June 2003 (Camera opens up to Tony Ross & Jared Wells backstage after the Ring Lords I PPV. Wells just in his adidas warmups is sweating and seems to be very tired)
TONY ROSS: Hello fans, I'm back stage after a historic night in the GLCW. The first ever Pay-Per-View, Ring-Lords I. One of those matches included 'Rage 'o Fire' Jared Wells against Larry Tact in a Lights Out Match. Jared, congratulations for your victory tonight.
JARED WELLS: Thanks Tony. TONY ROSS: The Lights Out match up was one everybody was talking about tonight. After months of aggression between Wells/Tact, is it over between you two?
JARED WELLS: I doubt it Tony. It was Lights Out for sure and 'ol Rage came out on top. After he was dumped on his head with the Rage Bomb, and then the count of ten, do you really think its over? Maybe for now. Later down the road Tact, we'll meet again.
TONY ROSS: This is certainly a victory you needed here in the GLCW. But I want to comment on your attitude tonight. You seemed to be very focused, and you didn't have your normal ring attire.
JARED WELLS: I had something to prove tonight man. I came to the XCel Energy Center to fight. Larry Tact is tough man and I couldn't let him win. After they cranked up Here To Stay by Korn, and I walk down that aisle, I was in complete control dude. No fancy attire, no pyro, just JARED WELLS. And the fans we're awesome!
TONY ROSS: What's next for 'RAGE 'o FIRE'? JARED WELLS: Well I have a big Number One Contender Battle Royal coming up. Eight guys will see who really wants it more. Slowly but surely we are rising to the top man! My fans know it, I know it.
TONY ROSS: You have been a force here in the GLCW for sure. But there is speculation going around that you've been on your cell phone a lot talking to somebody. Word going around that your talking to somebody from the past? Trying to bring somebody back into wrestling?
(Wells gets a funny look on his face and just walks off) TONY ROSS: JARED! JARED! WHO WAS IT?! (Camera pans to the back of Wells as is fades off)
