PhantomZ — 19 February 2003 FADE IN... (Rolling hills of sparkling white. Drifts and dunes as far the camera will let the eye see. The sun is shining, and the sky is an empty blue. A sunny, summer blue. But alas, it's still winter. The camera zooms out, revealing the sparkling vastness that once was a lake, and still is under some few feet of fresh powder. The shoreline is indiscernible from the far off land, as it all just snowed together. Way, way out lies the high cliffs, jutting out from the frozen desert like a tectonic fault line. They too, are topped high, and frozen waterfalls dot their walls.
The trees are not so dry and brown as they once were. Now, once again caked with ice and snow, they hang, waiting for the spring sun and air to lift the weight off their tired branches. Just when you think it's all over, just when you think the worst has been survived... wham You're hit when you least expect it.
The camera zooms in on something moving in along the cliff's edge... way, way out. A man, walking...)
CUT TO... (Water, in its attempt to find it's natural course back into the earth, has instead been frozen on its path. Still-shot, liquid free-fall, stretching out away from the rock, bubbling over the ice, and freezing itself along its predecessors. With as much success. Now, only a small trickling "drip drip" makes its escape. Down, through the snow, through the frosted ground, back to its destined watershed. How long will it take a "drip drip" to get through the feet of powder? And through the tundra-ed ground... or will it jot its course through the bit of space between earth and snow, hoping to find its way back? How long will it take for this "drip drip" to join its frozen sisters in the lake, to return to how it once was? A droplet is as unique as a snowflake, as they have much more in common than solely their chemical composition. And this "drip drip"... she's even more special. She's been rain... she's been sleet... she's been snow... she's even been hail. Not many of her sisters have ever been hail before, not around here anyway. She's been in bottles, been in jugs, run through faucets and sunk in tubs. She's been added to milk, added to meats, put in a sauna where she became steam. Even less of her sisters have been real-live steam before... so much more interesting than mere evaporation. The "drip drip" drips, its future not yet decided... and never to be known. Oh yeah... many more times than once... she's been tasted. She's been... refreshing. She's been a lot of things, but right now... she aches to return to her sisters... aches to be back home. Still special, still unique... but not so alone...)

His steps are careful, for the high snow camouflages many obstacles. Sometimes he must step over a rock, or move beside a high stump. He continues, towards a rock in the distance, which appears as though it was once atop the cliffs, but fell off. The rock is large, with a steep face towards the sun. The snow has taken leave off that face, and now is only damp in memory of precipitation thawed and melted away.
The man gets adjusted, tucks his long coat underneath him to prevent getting wet. He stomps his feet to get the crust of snow off his boots and pant legs. He unbuttons his jacket, and exposes his buttoned-shirt to the air. Around his neckline, we see a silver chain, and it peeks through his unbuttoned top button... but is generally unseen. He adjusts his collar, so that it isn't so restricted by the collar of his jacket, and he sits... elbows rested on out-bent knees, his face turned up towards the bright sun. The cliffs and a bright blue sky behind him...
