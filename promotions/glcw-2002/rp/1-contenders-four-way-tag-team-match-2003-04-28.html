JON SAVAGE: I think we've got a winner, here..... Suddenly, out of nowhere, a courier pulls up next to the threesome. Jon Savage makes a threatening motion towards the courier at first, but Wildside is seen retaraining Savage.....

JON SAVAGE: What do you mean? WILDSIDE: We're going to be in a four-way dance to determine the top contenders to the GLCW Tag Team Title!
JON SAVAGE: Well it's about time that the GLCW front office got smart about us. I mean, with my identical twin brother here, they probably realize that we are unstoppable.....

I'd actually compare them to a couple of squash wrestlers peeing all over themselves because they know they're going to be treated like the couple of prison shower pieces that they are. What we need to do is go over strategies that will cripple our opponents and give us the top contendership that we so richly deserve. Then it's on to the gold, the richest prize in this game. The Great Lakes Tag Team Titles.
Cam shows the trio arriving at a local restaurant to eat.... JON: Aren't you worried about Martial Law and Dogs of War? WILDSIDE: Are you kidding? Martial Law, well, we'll send em' packing faster than Iraqi soldiers in Baghdad. And the Dogs of War? They'll be howling in pain when we get through with them. Heck, they might be...put to sleep.
We have the ultimate control. I mean, think about it. Identical twins in a tag team! The first two teams didn't know what hit them.....these other six goons won't know either. C'mon guys, dinner's on me!!!!!

As the camera pans we see a card table, a mini fridge, and some floor lamps set up APA-style in one corner of the room. A boom box sits perched on the edge of the table, playing Led Zeppelin’s “Gallows Pole”. Jason Payne and “The Wolf” Chris McMillan sit on folding chairs at opposite ends of the table. Payne’s feet are propped up on the table as McMillan leans forward, adjusting his chair. Payne nods at whatever McMillan is saying as the shot comes in closer::
THE WOLF: … so I hand her back the panties, and she says to me “Well, at least you didn’t bring the chicken!” Get it? The chicken? AHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAAA! Heh … ha … mmm … oh.
PAYNE: … THE WOLF: Oh come on, that was FUNNY! I mean, the c-c-c-chickennnn … AHHHHHH HA HA HA HA HAAAAAAAA …
::Payne leans back, folding his arms and waiting as McMillan calms himself. Payne’s face breaks into a scowl, and a disgusted sigh escapes him::
PAYNE: Come on, man. Knock it off, will ya? We’ve got a big match coming up, an IMPORTANT match, and we’ve gotta get ready. We don’t have time for you to sit there giggling like a little girl.
THE WOLF: Jeez, Jason. Mellow out a little, will you? We’ve got plenty of time to get ready for this thing. I ain’t sweating it.
PAYNE: I know you’re not, and that’s what’s got me worried. What happened to last week, when all you could talk about was drive and hunger and being focused? You were the one getting on me last week for screwing around, and look where that got us. You were focused. You were driven, and collected. We went out there hard, and we kicked Martial Law’s collective ass. Now all of a sudden, you think we can just ride that, let it slide, screw around when we’re about to go into our biggest GLCW match to date? What in the hell is wrong with you?
THE WOLF: I just don’t see why you’re making such a big fuss over it. There’s four teams in this thing, Jay. We’ve been through two of them already without a sweat. And uh … we’re the third, in case you hadn’t noticed. That only leaves one unknown, as far as I’m concerned, and if they wrestle like the rest of the meatsacks around here, we’ve got nothing to worry about.
PAYNE: Oh come on, Chris. If we’ve learned ANYTHING in all the years we’ve been wrestling together it’s that there’s always something to worry about. Always someone to watch out for, and it’s your complacency that’s gonna have our asses in a sling going into this. This is a difficult match regardless of who’s in it, man.
THE WOLF: I suppose you’re right. I guess I just don’t see the point. I mean, after last week we’re already next in the rankings for the tag titles. Why should we have to have a match for a prize that should be ours by rights already?
PAYNE: Look, we made it in the rankings, but we haven’t been named the number one contenders yet. As far as I’m concerned, if this is what it takes for us to get our shot at those damn belts, then that’s what we’re gonna do, and we’re gonna do it like we always do. Full throttle, baby, right down their friggin’ throats.
::Payne drops his feet to the floor and stands up suddenly, coming around the table to stand over the Wolf. Payne grabs McMillan by the collar of his motorcycle jacket and yanks him unceremoniously to his feet::
PAYNE: But YOU. You sit there making stupid jokes like nothing at all is happening. Acting like a fool, when we should be getting ready for this match, getting ready to claim our shot at the tag straps. Are you stupid, or just lazy?
THE WOLF: (snarling) Watch it, Jason … PAYNE: No, YOU watch it. Shut up and listen for once, will you? We’re being given the opportunity to claim what belongs to us. More importantly, though, we’re being given the chance to go out there and PROVE that we’re not just blowing smoke. The chance to prove that the Dogs of War are exactly what we claim to be. The unstoppable force, a walking natural disaster. We’re taking on the WHOLE DAMN tag team division here, Chris, and I WILL NOT let you sit here on your ass and throw it all away. I’ve worked too hard for this. WE’VE worked too hard for this. You asked me last week if I questioned your drive, your desire, your hunger … well, maybe it’s time somebody did. You sit here week after week and listen to Ross and Wiseman talk about how you’re one of the most resiliant, resourceful, talented, and toughest men in the business, and you’ve let it go to your head. You’re getting complacent, Chris. You wanna know if I question your drive? You’ve LOST your drive. LOST your commitment and hunger, and everything that it takes to be a champion in this sport, and I’ll be DAMNED if I’m gonna carry your ass through this.
::The Wolf’s eyes flash with anger, and he shoves Payne back, jabbing a warning finger into his chest::
THE WOLF: Dammit, Jason, if there’s one thing you don’t do it’s question my drive. You’re lucky I don’t kick your ass right here. Nobody gives more to this sport than me, Jay. NOBODY. You and me, we’re the FUTURE of professional wrestling, and nobody can say otherwise. You wanna see hunger? You wanna see drive? You wanna see determination? Then let’s hit the gym, we got work to do.
::The Wolf spins around and storms off camera. Payne stands there, a grin spreading across his face as we FADE TO BLACK::
[name withheld] — 5 May 2003 Johnny Wildside is at the GLCW television studios in front of the camera in studio 1a, the studio where promotional interviews are done. Two people from makeup are getting Wildside ready when he suddenly pushes them away. He grabs the microphone from the production assistant and starts talking.....
WILDSIDE: First of all, I'd like to make an announcement. I no longer want to be known as "Dr. Johnny Wildside." While I am a doctor, I feel that to better exemplify my current position here in Great Lakes Championship Wrestling, I want to be known now as The General.....
Next, I wish to address the current competition for the top contendership for the GLCW Tag Team Championship. We recently heard from three of the participating teams. The Oak Street Beach Patrol, The Dogs of War, and my team, Major Impact. To show you that I'm not worried about the competion, I can't even remember the other two jobbers that are involved. Nonetheless, let's look at how things are currently shaping up for this history-making and career-breaking match.....
First, we have Major Impact. Two of the meanest and leanest sumsabeeches in all of the GLCW. And the beauty of the whole deal is, they're identical twins. Nobody -- not even me, really -- can tell who is who for sure. We're on a hot streak and on our ways to the top. As their commander-in-chief, their ring General, I am plotting a course that will lead Major Impact to the richest prize; the Great Lakes Championship Wrestling Tag Team Title.
Then, we have the Oak Street Beach Patrol. They're so busy telling themselves how great they are, that they're not even paying attention to the fact that they're nothing more than losers with anybody over the age of 18, whether male or female. Boys, and I do mean boys, what you need to realize is that you're in the ring with ADULTS and not the teeny-bopper training-bra pimpil generation. If you want to lower yourself to catering to that crowd, then go someplace else. Of course, it's hard to do anything when you're in full body casts eating through a straw.....
Pauses momentarily THE GENERAL: Then you have the Dogs of War. It's Jerry Lewis and Dean Martin. Jerry tells the joke and Dean, well, since Dean couldn't get laid in a morgue, then nothing probably happens. This team is desperate, you see, because their time here in GLCW has passed. They were once regarded as one of the greatest teams in GLCW, around the time they and OSBP were all going through puberty together, but now the time has come and gone. This is the wrestling business gentlemen, and Jon and Ric Savage are here to make sure that business...is...good. Believe me, it will be after our match.....
The General takes a drink of water and looks at the GLCW banner THE GENERAL: As far as the other jobbers are concerned, while the days of having "squash" matches are long over, I'm sure that after you two get out of the hospital, you could probably head to an organization that's just starting or one that is so far gone that nobody really cares. No matter what, though, your days of competing here will be over. pauses Oh yeah, it's Martial Law. Martial Law, OSBP, and Dogs of War....all of you are going feel the most extreme pain and you all will be humiliated beyond your wildest belief.....
The General pauses again for a moment THE GENERAL: And most of all, you...will...feel...the Major Impact of facing us. Yes, my friends, it's like shooting fish in a barrell....and we're armed and ready. No matter how hard the six of you work out, no matter how hard you party with your boyfriends, and no matter what you think you might do in our match, nobody can prepare you girls for Major Impact. Is it going to be easy for us? I'm planning on it not to be. Remember, ladies, we're hungry for the gold and we'll do whatever it takes to get there. Eisenhower once said "desperation is a great motivator." Well, sweethearts, hunger...hunger for the gold...is our ultimate motivator.
With that, The General throws the mic down in front of him. He finishes his water, tosses the glass over his shoulder, and walks off the set.

McMillan slumps down in the corner, resting against a turnbuckle. Payne rests his forearms on the ring apron, a towel draped around his neck. McMillan looks up, noticing the camera crew. He motions to Payne, who begins to speak::
PAYNE: Well well well, it looks like Minor Impact has finally decided to rear their heads around here after all. We were beginning to think you had done the smart thing, boys, and decided to stay at home for this one. In retrospect, though, I guess expecting the smart thing from the two of you is something of a stretch. And I see that Wildside has gone for a change of image. “The General”, huh? General what? I’m surprised you can even find your way to the ring, much less get in it.
::Payne mops his forehead with the towel before tossing it to the floor:: PAYNE: I heard you, Wildside. I heard you discount the Dogs of War, blow us off. That’s one of the worst mistakes you could possibly have made going into this match. It’s gonna be a hard enough match as it is, but you’re going into this thing half-assed, and it’s gonna cost you. You think that the Dogs of War are through here? That our time is over in the GLCW? Not even close, Chester. We’re just getting started. OUR time has just begun.

PAYNE: Now get the hell out of my gym. We’re through talking. ::FADE TO BLACK:: [name withheld] — 6 May 2003 Camera fades into the offices of The General, where he is seen discussing something with a producer....

Mister Dread — 8 May 2003 ::FADEIN on a dimly lit, dingy, generic looking motel room. Sitting in the room's sole chair is "The Wolf" Chris McMillan. A cup of coffee sits steaming nearby, while a cigarette smolders close at hand. The Wolf sits mostly in shadow, but his eyes gleam through the darkness::
THE WOLF: You know, maybe you're right, Wildside. I think the time for making jokes is over. Let's talk seriously for a minute. I'm glad to hear that you and your partner are training hard for this match. I'm glad you're taking it seriously. It makes me happy, because it means that you don't have an out. You've got no excuses. You'll fall just like the others, and there's nothing you can say to redeem yourself afterwards. There's no time out, no do-over, no 'I wasn't ready' ... nothing at all to fall back on except for the fact that you just weren't good enough. You can train, say your prayers, and take your vitamins, and it isn't gonna do you or anyone else one bit of good when you step into the ring with the Dogs of War.
::The Wolf pauses, taking a drag of his cigarette and shifting in his seat. Smoke swirls around his face, obscuring it from the camera lens::
THE WOLF: You seem to think I'm not taking this seriously, Doctor Johnny. You couldn't be more wrong about that. Oh, I may crack a joke or two to relieve the tension, but I'm always on track. Always focused, always ready to fight. That's the way it's always been with me, no matter where I wrestled or what gimmick I was using at the time. That's the one thing you'll always get from Chris McMillan ... the best. Nothing less, ever. I've been in the ring and gone toe-to-toe with some of the biggest and the best in the business. Do you know why that is? Because I AM one of the best in the business. One of the best ever, in fact. It might sound like nothing more than idle boasting to you, but take it as you will. The truth is always just around the corner waiting to be discovered, and you're about to discover mine ... and your own.
THE WOLF: You're talking about destroying the Dogs of War, breaking the Dogs of War ... well, it's never going to happen. Not by you, and not by anyone. My body has been broken more times than I can remember. I've spent more nights rotting in a hospital bed than I care to think about. Waiting, anticipating the results of an MRI or a CAT scan, always afraid that this is going to be the last straw, the injury that ends my career ... and more times than once, secretly HOPING that it would be the one to end me. But it hasn't happened yet, because I just don't have it in me to give up. But all those times ... after all I've given ... who came to me in the hospital? Who sat down beside me and said 'Thank you, Chris, for what you've given the sport ... given us'? NOBODY, that's who. Not you, not Krusher, not Jean Rabesque or Larry Tact or Angel Castillo or any of those stinking idiots that sit in the stands week after week swilling beer and killing brain cells. Wasn't that enough for you? I gave you my body, my dedication, my love ... I BROKE MY MIND FOR YOU PEOPLE! WHAT ELSE CAN I GIVE? WHAT MORE DO YOU WANT?
::The Wolf takes a moment to compose himself, dragging deep on his cigarette before continuing::
THE WOLF: The only thing that you people haven't been able to break is my spirit ... the only thing that I haven't given to this sport is my soul. You want it? Come and get it.
