Minion vs. Suicide

11 March 2003

Suicide — 12 March 2003 (Camera fades in to a beautiful and sunny day at the Bay City Recreation Area in Michigan, on the shores of Saginaw Bay. It's a weekend, so many kids and their parents are having fun spending time, playing games, having picnics, and so forth. Tents are set up over by the camping grounds, while some fathers and sons are over by the docks fishing in the Saginaw Bay. Everything looks peaceful and serene. The camera then fades over to the side, as a dark figure, sticking out like a sore thumb, sits silently on a bench, watching all the activity. As the camera pans around to the front of this dark figure, a Hispanic man, wearing an eye patch over his right eye is revealed. Seeing the familiar insignia on the front of this eye patch, a white futuristic looking S, it is now certain that this man is none other than Suicide. Suicide seems to be relaxed, as he is somewhat slumped against the bench, not moving anything except his eye, watching and gazing about.)
Suicide: "A dawn of a new day. Nothing is more invigorating than starting each day anew. No one knows what each day will bring us, but one thing is for certain: we must live each day like it's our last. I remember my time off from wrestling made me enjoy other pleasures in life. But even then I had to live it like I may never see tomorrow because not even I knew what tomorrow would bring. It's a rush knowing and feeling this, especially since it's been enhanced as of late with my admittance into GLCW. So far, I'm enjoying each day wrestling for GLCW. I've so far had a few challenges thrown at me, to which I am pleased and grateful for. But one day, I got a phone call and was told that my next challenge would be a man that I respect and one of those few I've been looking forward to facing in the squared circle."
(Suicide reaches for the Fedora on top of his head and takes it off, laying it gently on his lap.)
Suicide: "Just like myself, you Minion know what it's like to start anew. We both have been on the shelf for quite some time, so I can sense you feel the same way when I say it's good to be back in this great sport of ours. However, while we share this similarity, that's where it completely ends. From what I've seen thus far from you, it seems you're out looking for blood. Those eyes of yours are showing emotion of angst, anger, and frustration. You don't care who you face, what challenge is thrown at you, because in your mind, the result is always the same; pain and suffering. Well, while I admire your aspiration for violence, I would like to clarify on a few things if you don't mind."
(Suicide sits up in the bench, tossing his long black hair behind his back, as he seriously looks into the camera.)
Suicide: "First of all, if you're looking for ways to commit bodily harm on and to me, then bring it on. Nothing entices me more than a worthy adversary bent on the destruction of others by his own means. It shows you have tenacity, willing to make any sacrifice to achieve your goal. But if you really think for one moment I'm going to take your threats and like them, well sir, you are mistaken. I don't care what kind of grudges you have, whether they are inner grudges or ones against your maker. The fact of the matter is I'm going to come right out once that bell rings and wrestle you with everything I have. You're someone I know that can't be taken lightly at all. I respect the years you've put yourself into this sport and respect how such a fine athlete you are. Second, if you are going to be forthcoming and just going to spew your garbage on what exactly you're going to do to me in our match, then you've probably forgotten who you're dealing with or heard some bad advice on who you're dealing with. While others take your words to heart, I could care less what you say to me. You want to make me bleed? That's nice. You want to make me feel pain beyond pain? That's swell. You want me experience the living hell that you call your life first hand? Super duper."
(Suicide leans forward, resting his elbows on his knees as he clasps his hands together.) Suicide: "You see Minion, it doesn't matter what you say you are capable of. Anyone can come to me, stand face to face, and lie. A lot of wrestlers here in the GLCW love to brag about their accomplishments, but some have failed to live up to those. You can tell intricate stories about your life in Babylon Ancient Rome, Babylon Long Island New York, or Babylon Five. Save it for someone who gives a damn. The only thing I'm interested in is you, in that ring, Flint, Michigan, having the challenge of my life. I want you to push me to my limits Minion, because I want to see what yours is. I will do my best to give you a challenge, all you have to do is just show up that night. Whatever meaningful we have to say to one another can be done in our match. But if you are going to make that mistake in threatening me with your bravado time and time again like you do to others, well, to be quite honest....you'll be buried deeper than Tom Parker himself. Make no mistake about it, when it's all said and done, I just want you knowing that, in the end, you weren't dealing with some zealous, timid creature....you were dealing with none other than....The Man....The Myth....The Legend....Suicide. So enjoy each day like it's your last Minion, because if you underestimate me like most....it might just be your last. 'Nuff said."
(Camera pans away towards the activity in the recreation area once more. As the kids are laughing and having fun, the camera fades out.)
DavidMc — 16 March 2003 (screen fades in as we see ourselves in Minions apartment. The camera looks out the window and we see the fading sun and the emerging darkness. The camera swings around and we can see the apartment. The place is pretty bare. There is a refrigerator in one corner of the room with a card table set up with a slightly burned candle. The rest of the place resembles a Spartans home very bare and very desolate just enough to get by on nothing more and nothing less. In the most desolate area there is a lounge chair set up in the middle of the room facing a television which is projecting snow. The camera gets closer to the chair and we can barely see a figures head emerging from the top. The camera gets closer and rounds the side of the couch to reveal Minion. His hair is front of his face and he has a cigarette in his mouth. Around the chair are several cig butts and a couple burn marks in the floor. The Minion takes a couple of drags from his cig and then exhales and begins.)
Minion: An old day ends and a new night begins. The night is my kingdom it is the time in which Babylon truly strives. Night is when the creatures come out to play. Play with the people who don’t belong or think they do, but they don’t no one belongs in Babylon except me. It is always nice to feel the first cool breeze of the night after the sun goes down. There is something majestic to the whole electric glow that the city, especially Babylon gives off. The mystery of night is so thrilling. You never know what or who may be around the next corner. You never know if you are taking your last step or the next step to getting that special lady into the sack. But I can assure you good folks that Babylon is the safest city in the world. Night time is the time when I can truly collect my thoughts. It is the time when the world just seems to shut up and I am alone with myself like I have always been.
(Minion brushes his hair out of his face and then takes another drag off his cigarette before flicking it to the ground)
Minion: Suicide you are right about one thing and that is by the tone of my voice the look in my eye and my demeanor. I am in the GLCW for blood and revenge but if you think that is the only thing I am out for in the GLCW then you are mistaken and if you think I am going to tell you why I am in the GLCW then you are dumber than I thought. Suicide I would like nothing better than to rip you limb from limb and make you scream out in pain, but honestly I am going to deny you that privilege. You see I have no bone to pick with you. You are just another pay check and a free trip to some crappy town in middle America I would never go to unless I was on the tour. Suicide you are a just person in the wrong place at the wrong time saying words you don’t mean. You are just saying words that are going to set me off and you know it. I think you want the Minion experience but I am not going to do it. I will not spew my garbage as you so elegantly put it, but I will say that I will not give you my all because you don’t deserve it. You don’t deserve the Minion experience you deserve maybe half of my effort and that is all I plan to give because that is all that you will be able to stand. This is not about ego Suicide it is just plain and simple that you don’t deserve to fight me.
(Minion grabs the remote control that is sitting by his thigh and turns off the Television. The apartment goes silent and the only light we can see is from the full moon outside. The eerie white glow gives Minion an eerie deathly look. We can hear Minions neighbors fighting and on the other side of Minions apartment we can hear loud sexual noises. Minion doesn’t notices and lights up again. He takes a couple of drags and then begins.)
Minion: Do you still respect me now Suicide? Do you still respect what you have never fought and have only watched from the shadows only hoping to be as good as me. Hoping one day Suicide that you would have your name in lights against the greatest the sport has to offer, but you haven’t and you never will. You see you are just cannon fodder to me. You are just meat the dog and as you are fed into the meat grinder your career even though it is having resurgence will slowly slip and fad into oblivion where it belongs. Do you still respect me now Suicide? Suicide I have no problem with ending your career in fact I rather end it so I never have to fight you again and it would just be one less person in my way of reaching my goal. It would make me feel good knowing that there will be one less person when I look over my back to see all my enemies ready to stab me in the back at the first moment of weakness. Do you still respect me now Suicide? You are weak Suicide. You have always been. Like you I have watched you from the shadows when I have gotten the chance. I see nothing special. I see wasted years in the gym of striving to be good. I see wasted years of fighting in the ring of wasting blood and tears for no good reason. You are weak Suicide and the weak shall never conquer the world only the strong like me move on in this world feeding off the weak such as you. Do you still respect me now Suicide? I don’t brag about anything I have ever done Suicide. You see bragging indicates I have pride. But I have no pride just the desire to strive and be greater than the person across the ring from me. I have desire which feeds me day in and day out and without it I would kill myself because I would have no purpose for living. Perhaps life would be easier if I did kill myself. The voices in my head would stop. The violent tendencies would cease to exist. I would be out of this mortal realm for good, but if I killed myself there would be no evil in the GLCW and we can’t have that can we Suicide.
(Minion takes another drag off his cigarette it has grown quiet again as both of Minions neighbors have stopped making noise. Minion sighs a deep sigh and then takes another drag of his cigarette and begins again)
Minion: Life is weird Suicide. In the world there are people like you who are stepped on by people like me at every chance people like me get. People like you are used and abused every day, every minute, and every second. What makes you think you can break out of the mold so many have tried to break out of? You say that you believe I am no push over but deep in your head you think I will be just an easy victory for you are mistaken. In life people like you should step over people like me, but it doesn’t happen that way. People like you fail to realize that in the real world people like me will do anything to achieve victory and people like you will just sit there and be content with following the regular path to victory, but it seldom works. Suicide when your music hits and you walk out to that ring and see me don’t be scared of me. You are just filling your role in society. You are not expected to beat me. You are just expected to sit there and hope you can last a few minutes so you are not humiliated. You told me Suicide to enjoy every day like it was the last. I issue the same statement to you, but with a twist. Enjoy every day in your wrestling career as it was your last because more than likely it will be. You are dealing with the real thing Suicide realize it now or later it makes no difference to me, but you will realize that I am not gimmick and what I say is the truth. You will fall from grace and I will move on to bigger and better things than you.
(Silence fills the room as we begin to fade to black we can hear the Minion taking drags off his cigarette and then exhaling. Then silence. Then black)

A plethora of fruits are shown in wooden tables, made into their own individual little boxes. The camera cuts to a close-up shot of the fruits. A variety of nature's goodness is shown, such as oranges, bananas, pears, grapefruits, grapes, et cetera. As the camera pans around slowly, a finger-cut gloved hand moves into view, browsing the corner store's wares.)
???????: "Variety is the spice of life. Nothing is more invigorating in life than making choices. I guess that's the positive about human life; we are able to shape our own destiny by making choices. By making such decisions, be it selecting what piece of fruit to eat today, what kind of career one wants to have, or should one take a shower, you help shape one's self, molding them into something grand. Most of the time, a person with a conscience, makes wise decisions. They have this feeling deep within themselves that they know what they want to achieve and how to get there. Their choices make such consequences rewarding. However, if one acts in brash judgment, they could regret the consequences due to the wrong choices they make. But no matter what choice a person makes in their life, in order to make it valid, to make it become reality...."
(The finger-cut gloved hand stops over by the Granny Smith apples and grasps it in its hand. As the hand pulls the apple away, the camera pans back, revealing none other than Suicide, wearing black jeans, black wife-beater, with his long black hair tied in a ponytail. Suicide brings the apple to his lips and takes a bite. As he chews, he looks towards the camera, staring intently.)
Suicide: "They have to believe in it. When I was told I was a fool to become such a creature as wrestling, I took it in stride. For many years people have doubted my ambition and skills, thinking I was nothing more than a pushover. Sure, I could have regretted my decision, but I have never to this day. I've made my decision and it's a decision I have to live with for the rest of my life. Nothing you or anyone else can say or does that will make me think otherwise. As for you Minion, it seems you too have made a decision. You apparently decided to underestimate me and overlook me. You sit in your chair, comfortable with judging me as you see fit, thinking I'm nothing more than a stepping stone....a 'paycheck' as you so eloquently put it. I admire your blunt personality; the way you just say what's on your mind and give it to me straight. Unfortunately there is something about making choices that you shouldn't do; you shouldn't choose one way while thinking another. Contradicting yourself is very unbecoming of you Minion."
(Suicide takes another bite of the apple as he reaches into his jeans pocket and pulls out a silver dollar. He tosses it to the store clerk and goes his way down the street.)
Suicide: "You must feel proud of yourself that you can belittle me because you think you're better than me. That's fine, I can respect that. You say you have no qualms with me, that I'm not worth the trouble of maiming. And yet sir, you go on and on....and on about what you will do to me in our match; how you'll end my career, thus doing me a favor. Well Minion....what is your choice going to be? Are you going to destroy me or are you going to look past me like you're doing now? You seem a bit confused on that part. Do you think it's a wise decision to think I'm like these unlearned rookies who come in as a ball of fire, but yet gets their light extinguished? How about many of these veterans, like yourself, who believe they are untouchable and invincible? Apparently, since you have some sort of time machine to go into the past and live in Babylon amongst the Spartans, you can use that contraption to go to the past when I first started in the professional leagues. There were many wrestlers just like you who thought I was nobody that wasn't worth their time. Oh how they wished they could have penciled me in their schedules sooner! But that was then and this is now. Let's come back to the present for one moment and say that I was someone that couldn't be defeated unless you gave me your all. Would you take me seriously then?"
(Suicide continues eating his apple, crossing the street as the light turns green for pedestrian walk.)
Suicide: "A lot of wrestlers like yourself tried to step on me. Unfortunately for them, they either ended up with s(FCC)t on the bottom of their sole, stepping on a nail, or ripping their fleshy soles across a jagged piece of glass. I'm the disease that's starting to spread in GLCW Minion. Apparently, even though you're in a state of denial, you're taking notice of this and that's why you act like you have your back against a wall. You also choose to believe that I am looking for victory (shakes his head). Quite the contrary…. those who go looking just for victory will end up failing eventually. Those who look only to survive because they have nothing left in their lives....do. I probably am not expected to beat you.....in fact, I believe many people are riding on you to defeat me. It's called deviancy. People are afraid of something different and that's why the hostile attitude you so righteously possess. Unfortunately Minion, people who have voices in their head, who threaten to decimate their opponents, who want to hear them scream as they shiver in delight....are a dime a dozen. What you are saying is nothing new to me. If you really want to surprise the hell out of me Minion, choose to take me seriously. It's the only way you may survive and, since that's all you care about, win. I'm not going to back down from you because of hollow threats. I'm going to get in your face and piss you off.....because I can and I will. I'm going to Flint, Michigan looking for a fight, the fight of my life. If you continue to choose to not give me your all.....you better be prepared to get acquainted with the canvas; you'll be kissing it. All I want is to experience the real thing Minion, nothing more, and nothing less. I hope the voices in your head can give you a clear cut decision that you can stick with, otherwise....."
(Suicide finishes eating his apple, as he conveniently stops next to a trash receptacle. The camera cuts to a close up shot of his head, as he looks at the apple core.)
Suicide: "It'll be the pits for you, as I toss one more ignorant bastard....." (Suicide throws the apple core into the trash as the camera cuts to it falling in.) Suicide: "In the trash, continuing onward towards my destiny. 'Nuff said." (Suicide walks away as the camera gets a close up shot of the apple core in trash receptacle. The camera then fades out.)

He reaches into his coat pocket and pulls out a cigarette and lights up but continues walking. The camera eventually catches up and Minion begins)

It would have been better if you had just remained silent but no you had to open your mouth and reveal to the world just how ignorant you can be. Suicide the time has come for you to fall. I will not lie to you it will hurt and it will hurt bad but fortunate you I will not come back to do it again.

The camera pans back, revealing Suicide standing in front of it. He stands there wearing a black "Suicide: Enemy" t-shirt with his long black hair flowing down his back. Suicide stares intently into the camera, not wandering off view at all.)
Suicide: "I'm going to make this as simple as possible. I am a man of honor. I honor this sport that I partake in and in a way, worship it to an extent. If this makes me proud because I love this sport, then label me as such. As far as my career is concern, I don't care how it's gone down. It wouldn't matter whether I was a main-eventer or down in my dumps, losing night after night in front of a rabid wrestling crowd. Fame never mattered to me, titles never mattered to me, and accolades never mattered to me. Just the fact that I have been granted the honor, nay, privilege to wrestle against the best in the best in GLCW, is what keeps me striving and going in this great sport of ours. I live in reality because let's face it; one day everyone could be chanting my name in big venues around this country and the next, I could be wrestling in some teenager's parent's basement. But I press on because I never know what's going to happen. No matter what anyone has told me, it was I who was in control and I who had the power over my own fate."
(Suicide scratches his chin as he leans back a bit, still keeping his gaze keened on the camera.)
Suicide: "With all that being said, let me make this clear for you Minion. I don't give a damn what kind of nightmares you are having, who's telling you what in your head, or how much you keep threatening me with bodily harm. That last part is what I expect out of every opponent I face in the squared circle. Most people would let what you're saying get to them, but all you're doing is helping my cause: to see to it I survive against you even more. You can hype yourself up to be something huge, but if I come a-knockin' and you can't answer, I'm kicking that damn door down with force. You are like a puppet to me Minion, as I control your strings. You allowing yourself to let me anger you is foolish on your part. Though you may have intensity and a raging fire deep within your soul, it'll only hinder your intelligence and concentration as I gracefully and strategically achieve my survival. I could care less what kind of level you are walking on; I will cross whatever boarders, run through any walls, and climb over any mountains.....just to bring you to mine."
(Suicide adjusts his finger-cut gloves, never even keeping his eye off the camera for a moment.)
Suicide: "You don't believe I am a cancer to GLCW? It's not a literal meaning mind you, but figuratively speaking. You see, I thought you were different Minion. I thought you were someone who would understand me, respect me for what I represent, and be eagerly waiting the day we face off against one another. But, just like my last opponent Mr. Roberts, you failed this....all of this. You're just like him and that doesn't help your cause in what you're trying to achieve. To get people to take notice and appreciate you? To finally get the recognition that you're one of the top wrestlers in this sport? Well my friend, it takes hard work and dedication to do that; not idle and empty threats. Although I'm glad you're now going to give me your all, unfortunately you're doing it just to amuse yourself and to prove your theory I'm a sham. Come Flint, Michigan, at Wired, I'm going to show you why you should have played it safe and came up with a hypothesis rather than claiming what you're babbling about is the truth. Bring everything you have, because I know I will. This is serious to me. This sport means more to me than you, the voices in your head, Babylonians, and basically life itself. My destiny will not be hindered by a rhetorical buffoon. The only thing that might fall on that night Minion, besides maybe yourself slamming onto the canvas.....is your illucid dreams. When it's all said and done, win or lose Minion, I will come to you like a man of honor, a man of respect, and will shake your hand because I know you'll give me one hell of a match. But hopefully, when you come out of this match, either unscathed or embarrassed by the fact you underestimated me, you will come to know the mysterious wanderer simply known as....The Man....The Myth....The Legend....Suicide....'Nuff said...."
(Suicide promptly exits out of the camera's view. It then zooms in close to the GLCW banner before it fades to black.)
