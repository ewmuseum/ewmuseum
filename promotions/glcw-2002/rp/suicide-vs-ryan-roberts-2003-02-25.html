GLCW — Roleplay

Suicide vs. Ryan Roberts

25 February 2003

Suicide — 25 February 2003 [updated:LAST EDITED ON Feb-25-03 AT 04:47&nbsp;PM (EST)](Camera fades in to a close up shot of a pair of hands. The hands are in tannish color, with many scars on them. A black fingernail can be seen, due to probably to stubbing and such. The hands are outspread, looking like they are being gazed upon by someone.)
???????: "These hands can do amazing things...." (The camera zooms back a bit, revealing none other than Suicide standing in front of a brick wall with the GLCW backdrop behind him. He is staring down at his hands, looking them over with his one good eye as an eyepatch with his symbol covers his right.)
Suicide: "They say that the hands are the real tools of the job. One can have many dreams, many great ideas. But they can sometimes never come to fruition without these wonderful instruments. It doesn't matter how intelligent one person can speak to the masses. It doesn't matter how inducing one person's words can be to soothe those masses. It doesn't even matter what kind of dreams you can have at night, like little inner plans that one wishes comes to life. In the end, your hands produce the effect to the causes in our lives."
(Suicide turns his attention to the camera, looking sternly into it, with his hands still spread out.)
Suicide: "Most of the wrestlers in GLCW don't care what they can do with their hands. They only want things to happen for them, but never want to make them happen. They just want to be handed unearned rewards rather than snatching the fruits of their labor. I don't really care what I have to do to survive, I just know that my hands will get me through it one way or another. These hands are the real weapons of mass destruction. They can begin and end life. They can also determine how much a wrestler really wants it. Yes I know, there are other things that come into play, but on the physical spectrum of wrestling, hands are all you've got."
(Suicide pulls back his hands and places them in his trench coat pockets. He turns his head ever so slightly to the right but still keeps his eye towards the camera.)
Suicide: "The first thing my hands and I will be doing in the GLCW is debuting at Riptide in Milwaukee, Wisconsin. Now I know some of you in the GLCW would be pissed to hell knowing you had to open a wrestling show but to be honest, I'm not. I like to start my way from the bottom and claw my way upward. That's how becoming a wrestler should be. Nevertheless, even though I will be starting things off at Riptide, I'm not alone in this. What's more important is the fact I will be wrestling against a man I've never wrestled against nor to my recollection never faced before. Mr. 'Twisted Prodigy' Ryan Roberts, allow me to introduce myself. The name is Ulysis Solian, but to the wrestling world, I'm simply known as Suicide. I'm a wrestler just like yourself, anticipating the experience of wrestling in GLCW and getting things going. I'm also one of the most underrated and underestimated wrestlers in ths sport today. Now I've been out of the loop for a good six months or so, so there's an off chance I might have a little ring rust.....but I doubt it. I've been training for this day and let's just say that I'll keep on doing so right before I face you and even after I face you. I'm preparing for you Mr. Roberts because to be quite honest, I don't know who and what to expect. But that makes this match even the more challenging....and all the more fun for me."
(Suicide takes out his right hand and adjusts the brim of his Fedora that's on his head, as he turns his attention back straight to the camera.)
Suicide: "So I tell you what Mr. Roberts, I'm giving you a decision on how you want this to progress. Now on one hand (extends his right hand out in view), you can comply and take me seriously and hopefully we'll have one hell of a match. Or on the other hand (extends his left hand out in view), you can be like most of the self-centered, egotistical, narcisstic bastards I face and just completely disregard me as a challenge to your wrestling skills. But let me say that in the end, no matter if I win or lose, I will extend my hand to you (extends his right hand as if he was to give someone a handshake) and offer my respect to you. Why? Because I'd like to believe that respect is earned in the ring, not out. Also, I believe the best way to show respect is by handshake. Again, if you want to take the low road and treat me like I'm some second-rate fluke, you'll get my hand extended to you alright (extends his hand again in a handshake gesture)....right down your throat (turns the handshake gesture into a fist). It's your call Mr. Roberts, I'm leaving that choice up to you. I've made my choice....and that choice is to wrestle you. Just know that when that bell rings, we both facing one another in the squared circle, you'll be looking right at me eye to eye, knowing that your destiny awaits with....The Man....The Myth....The Legend....Suicide...."
(Suicide begins to walk off-camera, but stops himself short and turns his attention to it one last time.)
Suicide: "Oh and my hands will be there at Milwaukee too. 'Nuff said...." (Suicide now walks off screen, but his arm apparently extends itself in view. The camera zooms in on his hand, as it gives a thumbs up, followed by an OK sign, and finally waving. Suicide's hand waves as it moves off-screen, as the camera fades out.)
LMccarver — 2 March 2003 [updated:LAST EDITED ON Mar-03-03 AT 08:16&nbsp;AM (EST)]The camera fades into the Agora Theatre, The GLCW crew is busily going setting up the theatre for the upcoming GLCW show. A lone figure sits alone in the fifth row, the GLCW camera crew moves closer to show one of the newest GLCW stars, the Twisted Prodigy Ryan Roberts. Ryan sits in a black trench coat, black tank top and black jeans. A huge smile is on Ryan's face.
ROBERTS: I can picture it. Suicide can you? You see I sit here and I play the match out in my head. I see two of the GLCW's young stars putting on the match of the card. I get to open the show and I get to set the level that the rest of the roster must follow. Do you have what it takes to step up to that level? I guess we'll find out now won't we?
Ryan stands up and folds the chair and tucks it under his arm. ROBERTS: You talk about how your hands can do amazing things? Well this here pats the steel folding chairthis thing can do amazing things to one's body. It can break ribs, indent a skull, even break a hand. I look forward to seeing you at Riptide, Ulysis. I do however want to mention one thing. When you jump on the plane, hop on the bus, get in your car. Realize that you're heading to a match with a man that works hard for what he gets.
Ryan starts to shake his head and then looks past the camera ROBERTS: You have all trained me well, you have been with me through the think in the thin and you helped me get to where I am. You want Suicide to feel pain? I was actually beginning to like Ulysis. He believes the same thing that we do. Hard work gets us to the top. Yes, I know if I want to be taken seriously, I need to make my opponent suffer. Yes, I understand, Ulysis will suffer.
Ryan's eyes close and then when they reopen, Ryan stares into the camera. ROBERTS: Suicide, My mentors have spoken and you must feel the punishment that we have felt through our past. Personally I have a respect for you comments and I guess we'll see if in the end if you can live up to your side of match. You bring your hands of mass destruction and I'll bring mine.
Ryan pats the chair and then drops chair on the floor. Ryan turns and walks off. Camera fades to black
Suicide — 2 March 2003 (Camera fades in to the front of the Bradley Center, in Milwaukee, Wisconsin sometime around 1AM at night. Litter is spread on the ground, showing signs of busy activity a few hours ago. A maintenance crew member is shown, dressed in navy blue slacks, cleaning up the rubbish. As he busily pushes his broom back and forth on the ground, the camera pans to the right. A figure, dressed in a brown trench coat and Fedora hat, while smoking a cigar, is shown leaning against one of the sides of the arena. He watches the maintenance crew member working diligently, earning his keep. The camera then pans around the front of the man, wearing an eye patch with a very familiar symbol, that of a futuristic looking S. The man is identified as none other than Suicide. As he continues to watch the gentleman, he pulls the cigar out of his mouth and exhales into the brisk air.)
Suicide: "He works so hard, not caring what people think of him, how they look upon him, but to survive every day. You see Mr. Roberts, I'm like that gentleman. I'm a hard working person. I take nothing for granted. When it's time for me to go into battle, survival is only on my mind. I'm not worrying about the win, because that will come with hard work and patience. That man represents those virtues. While most people, like you Mr. Roberts, have their heads in the clouds, wishing for things to happen for them, like this guy, I'm making them happen. I'm not wondering and dreaming beforehand about how my match will enact itself or the outcome that will appear when it's all said and done. I'm not even wondering if I'll hurt you or not, because to be quite honest, trying to hurt you means nothing to me. Why? Because I don't need to try...."
(Suicide takes another puff of the cigar, as the maintenance member picks up the trash that he swept together in a pile.)
Suicide: "You can sit there Mr. Roberts and proclaim how you're going to open the show and how you're going to set the example to the rest of the GLCW. I wonder if the voices in your head reminded you that in a wrestling match, it takes two to tango. Need I remind you that while you're foolishly looking at outcome, probably a notch on your belt no less, there is something more important you need to focus on? You see, you've made several mistakes and misjudgments. First off, I'm not new to this sport. This 'level' that you speak of that you're going to set and that I need to step up to is nothing more than a fallacy. You and I are equal. I'm not better than you, you're not better than me. Unfortunately, according to GLCW and everyone else watching our match that night, one of us will end up being better than the other. While you can summon the 'mentors' in your mind to tell you you're going to win, I'm going to let my wrestling skills show you."
(Suicide watches the worker hauling a trash receptacle around, placing the trash off the ground in it.)
Suicide: "The next mistake you've made was the fact that you were wielding a steel chair in the air like you were He-Man, summoning the power of Greyskull. I'm sure every time you stand in front of a mirror, with a steel chair in hand, telling yourself....sorry, your 'mentors' telling you are the chosen one while you scream from the top of your lungs 'I've got the power!', you actually believe that. But back here on planet Earth, you look like nothing more than a chump wielding a toothpick. I'm sorry Mr. Roberts; do you actually believe you're going to cause bodily harm to me with a steel chair? Let's be honest for a second; if I've been electrocuted, thrown into barbed wire, slammed into flaming chairs, and even stabbed by someone I used to call a best friend, do you actually believe that steel chair's going to save you from me? You see, I know you're scared already. You're telling yourself, against your 'mentors' wishes, that I'm probably someone you shouldn't mess around with, and the only thing you think will save you is a steel chair...."
(Suicide shakes his head as he takes the Fedora off his head. He takes one last puff of his cigar, before putting it out on the bottom of his shoe. Suicide then tosses it in the trash receptacle that the maintenance worker is dragging behind him as he passes him by. Suicide steps off the side of the arena and starts to walk around the front courtyard.)
Suicide: "Listen Mr. Roberts, if you're only goal in this match is to make me suffer; well you've achieved that so far with your bantering. I'm sure you're having a good, hearty chuckle at my expense but to be quite honest.....I'm not laughing. When I said I'd give you a decision on how you want this progress, I wasn't lying. If you really want to do things the hard way, then sir, you're in for a rude awakening. Don't worry if I'm going to live up to your expectations in this match. What you should be worrying about if I exceed your expectations. Your steel chair isn't going to scare me away nor will your sideshow freak antics. While you live in your little fantasy world, I'll be living in reality, where I will be awaiting you in the ring....at Milwaukee. I think those voices in your head are screwing up more than just your sanity. But maybe my hands, my tools of survival, will help you set you on the right path. Because if you won't take me seriously like I'm trying with you, then you'll be like the rest of the litter that plagues this ground; tossed into the trash can by GLCW as I continue on my journey....never bothering to look back. I'll see you in Milwaukee Mr. Roberts....and hopefully in reality as well....'nuff said...."
(As Suicide walks out of view, the camera zooms in on a piece of trash that lies on the grass. The camera stays with it for a few seconds before a pitch stick impales through it. As the pitch stick lifts out of the ground, the piece of trash goes with it. The camera zooms out a bit, showing another maintenance worker placing the garbage in a trash bag. The camera then fades out.)
LMccarver — 3 March 2003 The sound of flesh being slapped FADE IN: Ryan Roberts stands in front of mirror, using a leather strap to flog his back with. Huge red welts begin to form on his back and Ryan continues to look into the mirror, a emotionless look on his face. Finally a small trickle of blood begins to flow from some of the welts and it is then that Ryan finally stops the abuse on his own body. A glassy looked Ryan Roberts turns to the camera.
ROBERTS: I have questioned my superiors and for this I will pay the ultimate punishment, twenty lashes with a leather strap! Suicide questions my motives? Why is that Suicide? What is there to question? Do you look at me and see a man that is not on even ground with you? You speak as if I’m going to walk into the ring and I’m going to roll over and play dead for you. Well you are mistaken friend. You see, I know what I have to do and I don’t care what your past has put you through, for it is nothing compared to the Twisted Prodigy.
Ryan’s sweat soaked hair falls into his eyes. Ryan continues, without brushing it away. Roberts: Your past is nothing compared to what I’ve had to deal with. Abandoned before I remember. I was thrust in and out of foster homes. I had no friends, no history. I just floated from place to place. It was then that my true friends came to me and they told me what I needed to do to make it. I latched onto amateur wrestling and I was state champion my Junior year of high school, But then my only friends told me how the others laughed at me behind my back and that I should fight back. It was not my fault that I broke that boys arm, he shouldn’t have told my friends those awful things about me.
Ryan hangs his head and continues ROBERTS: Now you’ve come out and made fun of one of my friends. Saying that he can’t hurt you? Let’s out a evil laugh, reaches down and grabs the steel chair from the arenaMy friend thinks that you will be well acquainted after Riptide. He says that your misconception of what you’ve faced in the past, will be nothing compared to the punishment and pain that you will feel. He’s grown found of your hands, Ulysis. He’d like to get an up close meeting with them. I think that it can be arranged, don’t you?
Ryan sets the chair down and pushes the hair out of his eyes. Ryan looks into the camera ROBERTS: Ulysis, You’ve given me a decision? A decision if I want to shake your hand or get it shoved down my throat? I guess we’ll wait until Riptide, Ulysis, to see where I’m leaning, but if you think that shoving your fist down my throat is an easy task. You’re in for a rude awakening come Riptide. With or with out my friend Ulysis, I know I can beat you at Riptide.
A grin comes to Ryan’s face ROBERTS: You question my desire to claw my up the roster? Yet at Riptide, I will stand nose to nose with you and I will lay it all on the line. I don't issue mindless challenges to champions of the GLCW, I don’t complain to ownership, looking for a quick rise up the ladder. No, I plan to roll up my sleeves and not only beat the hell out of you, but to out wrestle you. The way you jack your jaw, you think that you’re in for a easy night. If that’s that thought process running through your head, then you have already lost this battle. Keep talking about your hands, Ulysis. When you stand across from me in the ring and you look me dead in my eyes and realize that I’m not going to look past you. Then you’ll realize that I’m not some lackey to pad your record with. I’ll see you in Milwaukee, Suicide.
Ryan puts on a GLCW T-shirt and then turns and walks away. FADE TO BLACK Suicide — 6 March 2003 (Camera fades in to the Briggs & Stratton Milwaukee Muscle exhibit at the Discovery World Museum in Milwaukee, Wisconsin. Many people are shown interacting with the exhibits. A family is pulling on a rope to lift up a building, while a kid is pushing a train on a set of tracks. As the camera pans around this exhibit, none other than Suicide is shown standing next to a model of a mountain. There is a kid pushing on the mountain, really getting into it. Suicide watches with pique interest as the kid can't budge the mountain.)
Suicide: "I had an epiphany the other night and I've come to a conclusion. Before I came to any conclusion I had to have some kind of question that needed to be answered. Well Mr. Roberts, after hearing you talk about yourself over and over again, the question appeared in a dream. The question was, 'Why do people feel the need explain their actions?' I've noticed a trend since first starting in this sport and that is, no matter who I was up against, be it the rookie or the veteran; they all felt the need to explain what they went through in their lives and what they are achieving in doing. What I find ironic about all of this that nearly every single time someone opens their mouths with promises such as these, they never come true."
(Suicide turns his attention to the little kid for a brief moment, still trying to move that mountain model. Suicide turns his attention back to the camera.)
Suicide: "Now I know you're probably asking yourself Mr. Roberts what does this have to do with you. It's real simple, even this kid behind me can catch on quick. First off, I don't give a damn about your past or how you were scarred and hurt in that past. Do you actually believe for one moment that I will be intimidated by all of this? Is it really necessary to compare my past with yours when I never even made mention of my own history? Why so imitated by my words? You actually believe I think of you as nothing more than crap that I step on with my shoe? I think you judge me wrong Mr. Roberts. Every wrestler I face in the squared circle I consider my equal. It does not matter whether I love or hate who they are or what they represent. But I respect each man I go into battle with. You Mr. Roberts will not be the exception. Just because you are a (makes quotation marks with his fingers) 'special' person with (makes quotation marks with his fingers again) 'special' needs doesn't equate to you getting specific (makes quotation marks with his fingers once more) 'special' attention. I pay a unique and close attention to detail to every opponent I face. When I get in that ring, what was exchanged verbally between you and I takes a backseat to what will really do my talking: wrestling."
(Suicide checks back on the kid again, still finding him having trouble moving that mountain model. He shakes his head and turns back his attention to the camera once more.)
Suicide: "I anticipate the fact that you believe wrestling your or shoving my fist down your throat will be no easy task. But again, does conveying this notion that slapping yourself with leather straps and once again wielding the Chair of Greyskull will really deter me from my ultimate goal? I'm glad you have desire to move up in the ranks of GLCW. But it seems that personality of yours is really getting in the way of that. It's because of that personality that leads you to believe I'm just like the others in GLCW; who gloat about themselves, their accomplishments, everything they've worked hard for. Oh I have busted my ass in this sport for many years, but why should I bore you with the excruciating details? Why should I be like the others and swing my years in this sport like it was a two foot golden phallus? I'm not in the GLCW to compare Mr. Roberts. I'm here to just wrestle. And I hope that in the end, when it's all said and done, win or lose, you come out of our match knowing that. I can't wait when we stand nose to nose and look each other dead in the eye. I can't wait for the fact that you won't look past me. I want a challenge Mr. Roberts. I want you to fight me with your heart and soul, because I will be doing the exact same thing. But please, for the love of God, screw the macho-psycho crap and come at me like a man; face to face. Because if you end up losing because of such, then there's just one little piece of advice I'd like for you to remember. And that Mr. Roberts is...."
(Suicide walks over to the mountain model and the young boy, as he is still struggling with moving it. Suicide taps the boy on his shoulder and motions for him to step aside. He stands looking at the model, in deep thought. Suicide then extends a finger towards the mountain and somehow pushes the mountain about four inches forward. The young boy looks to be in amazement over this feat, as Suicide shrugs it off like it was no feat at all. He then turns his attention to camera once more.)
Suicide: "It's easier to move a mountain than it is to change one's personality....'Nuff said...."
(Suicide walks away from the mountain model and out of the camera's view. The little boy goes and continues to try to push the mountain, but still doesn't budge. He then stops and thinks, almost emulating Suicide himself, as he extends a finger. He places it on the mountain and begins to push it a couple of inches. The little boy jumps up and down and cheers, before running out of the camera's view, yelling for his parents. The camera then fades out.)
LMccarver — 9 March 2003 [updated:LAST EDITED ON Mar-09-03 AT 02:04&nbsp;AM (EST)]FADE IN a light rain falls and Ryan Roberts sits on a rooftop, overlooking the streets of downtown Milwaukee. Ryan wears a black trench coat and flips a coin in the air and catches it in his hand. The camera man moves closer from behind and Ryan never turns to look but just begins to speak
Roberts: Tick Tock, Tick Tock. That Suicide is the sound of the time as it passes before our match. You’ve made a great opponent in this battle of words, but the battle of words is about to be a mere after thought. You see, Suicide, I’ve been sitting here and been thinking about all that you’ve said. I couldn’t make up my mind if wanted to shake your hand or dare you to shove your fist down my throat. That question has been burning in my head for days now. Yet, I think I have come up with the perfect solution to my problems.
Ryan turns and looks into the camera and holds up a quarter. Roberts: It’s a simple game of chance now isn’t it, Ulysis? It could be heads I shake your hand and tails I dare you to try and shove that fist down my throat, or I could say tails I shake your hand and heads I dare you to shove the fist down my throat. What do you think Ulysis? Do you think that’s fair? Do you think letting a coin decide is going to make a difference?
Ryan studies the coin and then continues. Roberts: My past has to do with my present, Ulysis. You may choose not to hear what I say, but it is the road that have brought me here. Remember that it was you that opened up that Pandora’s Box. Was it not you that made mention of your friend stabbing you? Maybe you should take off those blinders that you’ve been wearing and take a real look at things. You ask how am I different than the others here? I could start a long list, but it would probably fall on deaf ears. I guess at Riptide, I’ll have to be the one that shows you what makes me different than the rest of the GLCW roster.
Ryan stands up and pushes the wet hair from his face. Roberts: You talk about me being a( makes quotation marks with his fingers)special person. I guess we’re all special in our own ways aren’t we, Ulysis? We all fight with our inner demons. You are no different than the rest. You just catalogue me as a lost cause. It’s a stereotype that I’ve battled my whole life. You look at me and see a ( makes quotations marks with his fingers special person. Well this special person will be waiting, Ulysis. I suppose you want me to flip the coin and let you know what to do.
Ryan places the quarter on his thumb and flips it high into the air. You can see it shine in the street lights, Ryan moves his hand at the last minute and the quarter falls to the street below.
Roberts: You don’t want me to be the one that tells you what’s going to happen? You just wait until time runs out, Ulysis and then you’ll know what I have in store. Tick Tock, Tick Tock, Tick Tock….
Ryan pushes past the cameraman and jumps on a fire escape and descends to the street below.
FADE TO BLACK Suicide — 9 March 2003 (Camera fades in to a brick wall with a GLCW backdrop on it. Standing in the foreground of all of this is Suicide, looking a bit intense as he stares into the camera with calm, yet dangerous eyes.)
Suicide: "I'm going to make this as painless for not just me, but for everyone else, including you Mr. Roberts. I don't like to judge anyone, anyone at all. But sometimes you have to call a spade a spade and well Mr. Roberts, but you're are just another drop in the hat. You can criticize me of all the comments I made towards, but the fact of it all is I tend to be blunt with my honesty. And I'm being seriously honest when I say....I don't give a flying f(FCC) what you claim you are. Flip your coins, count the minutes and seconds, talk to your inner demons or 'friends' or 'mentors' as you put it....whatever makes you sleep soundly at night. I guess I could also say whatever makes you think you will get by me any easier in our match. Play whatever little games you want to play Mr. Roberts, but playtime's over. I don't play games but when I do, I play for keeps. Your fixation of my shoving my fist down your throat or any other orifice you find exciting is clouding your perception and twisting my words to comform to your liking. My ultimate goal isn't to hurt you....hell, my ultimate goal has nothing to do with you. What it is none of your concern. Your only concern is me and let me tell you, make no heads and tails about it, I'm coming not only to survive....but to give you the beating you now so rightfully deserve. You can throw that rhetoric 'Pandora's Box' statement around like a frisbee, because believe you and me, most of the wrestlers in GLCW have used it to 'outsmart' their opponents, when in fact, just makes them look like a complete jackass. But you don't seem to have any trouble looking like one to me as of late. You can go around and claim how different you are, but what makes stand out my friend isn't what comes out of my mouth.....but what comes out in the ring. And I'm coming out like I just busted out from the gates of Hell. Once again I anticipate my match against you and don't really care when my time's running out, because Mr. Roberts, it seems......your time has already ran out. See you at Riptide....'nuff said....."
(Camera fades out as Suicide continues to stare into the camera intensely.)
