Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
tony101882 — 15 June 2003 (Open up to a dark gym, it appears to be late at night. Silence fills the room, except for the clanking of weights. The camera moves in closer, showing Scotty Sanders racking a 300+ pound bench press.)
Scotty Sanders: Competition, it fuels us all. That's why I'm here. There's a fire inside me, to be the best this sport has seen. Actually it not just a fire, ITS AN OBSESSION. I can't eat without thinking of wrestling, I can't sleep without dreaming of wrestling. I left my home, my family, my girlfriend, all to chase this dream and cure this obsession that burns inside me. I want to be the best and I'm willing to go up against anybody. I have nothing more to loose. My LIFE is here. I can't turn back.
My name is Scotty Sanders and I'm probably the biggest underdog this company has ever seen, but I have something to prove, not only to my family and friends who said I was crazy for persuing this dream. NO, NO, more importantly I have to prove this to myself, that I didn't throw my life away for this dream, instead I gave up my life to make my dream a reality.
