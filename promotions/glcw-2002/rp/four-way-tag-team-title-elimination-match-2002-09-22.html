TBirdSCIL — 22 September 2002 All RP for this match goes in this folder... JC — 27 September 2002 [updated:LAST EDITED ON Sep-27-02 AT 06:15&nbsp;PM (EDT)](“WOW! What a PPV! Riptide was not only exciting, it was full of surprises! The night started out with the UA coming out for the tag team match and just when we were all expecting them to go ballistic, they walked away… But hold on folks, it doesn’t end there, cause they did come down for the Golem vs. Jobber match and tried to take out Golem, and what happened next was really surprising, Maelstrom came down and actually rescued Golem, chased the UA away and even shook hands with Golem… Question there is, was that the birth of an alliance between the two!? They both have issues with the UA, can it be that the UA created a catalyst with their actions!? And what of Jean Rabesque!? Is he also trying to pay the UA back for past deeds!? No hand shakes there folks, but I see a union forming against the UA, it is true what they say, “the enemy of my enemy is not my foe”… BUT! Knowing the UA like I know them, they will not forget the fact that both Maelstrom and Jean Rabesque spoiled their little party… And if past history holds true, folks I have to say, get the bandages and splinters because it’s going to be a bloodbath! But I guess that saga will have to take a backseat for now, as they are in the Great Lakes Tag Team Title Four-Way Elimination Match against the Movement, Gridlock and the High Plains Drifters… I had a chance to catch up to the UA, the other day and asked them about Riptide and their upcoming match and folks, part of the interview had to be deleted due to the explicit language spewed out by these two monsters… The following are excerpts on that interview”….)
KRAVEN: (Face distorted in anger) MAEL! You just don’t listen do you!? You want to mettle into our affairs!? You and that French tootsie pop want to stick your noses into what doesn’t concern you! Well check this out dude, once we’re done with this dumb (bleep) 4 way match! We’re going take care of you and Rabesque! BUT! First things first! Chicago, Illinois, get ready to see greatness! Get ready to see a 4 way match that will crown tag team champions! 8 men will enter but ONLY TWO will EMERGE! Chi-town’s very own, THUNDERDOME! Not two but SIX against two! Are we worried, do we look worried!? (Smirks) It doesn’t matter whether it’s 2, it doesn’t matter whether it’s 4, and it doesn’t even matter if it’s 6! The key factor here is that me and Flats are the most cohesive team around! We are 2 that work as 1! We are the …. (Just then JC comes out of nowhere and cuts Kraven off, both he and Flatliner glare at JC who with a devious smirk says…)
JC: Don’t crowd me guys, I have great news! I just struck a deal with one of the other teams; you and them are going to eliminate the other 2 teams! And afterwards…
(Flatliner angrily cuts him off…) FLATLINER: And what POSSESSED you to do that! Who the hell asked you to interfere in our affairs!? HUH, WHO!?
JC: Chill out dude! I know that you don’t need any help from me, I just wanted to even the field for you, I just wanted to make it fair for yo….
(This time Kraven cuts him off…) KRAVEN: So you take it upon yourself to cut a deal huh!? Well what makes you think that we will honor it!? What makes you think that we’re going to play ball!? Just who the hell do YOU think you are JC!? Did we tell you to stick your damn nose in our business!?
JC: I was just trying to… (Gets cuts off) KRAVEN: Shut the hell up JC! Movement, Gridlock and High Plains Drifters, from here on end we’re gonna call you the “magnificent six”… And from where I stand, it’s one hell of a Mexican standoff! Just don’t blink guys, that’s all it’s going to take, one blink and you’re GONE, HISTORY! Now you may be good, of course you have to be good, otherwise Malec and double K wouldn’t have put you in such a hazardous position… Malec, must have in his infinite wisdom realized the danger we actually pose, so he decides to make our uphill climb just that much steeper… But it doesn’t matter how many teams he throws into the pit, he’ll never break our spirit! When we are face to face, “mag 6” I want ya to look deep into our eyes and realize that you’re in for the fight of your life, cause you will be! You see guys, these types of matches, are all too familiar to us… Now you got to start really asking yourself, what is the true equation here… Is it like JC claims that one of you has sold out!? Or is it just JC spewing crap, and then in the back of your mind lingers the thoughts, what about Kraven and Flatliner,!? What are they really all about!? (Smirks cynically) What we did to Jobber and Golem, was nothing, that was just our way of saying, “HEY, what up guys”! And that was just the beginning, cause now the game gets more serious… You see mag 6, we are just as hungry as you, maybe even more hungrier! We aren’t taking anything away from you, we are sure that you guys believe that you have it all figured out, (Smirking, he shakes his head, Flatliner just stares… JC!? Well let’s just say that JC is just standing there nodding his head…) At Chi-town showdown, it will all come to a head… It will be there where your fortitude, your endurance, as well as your beliefs will meet its most truest test! From afar we have watch, we watched the tapes many times over, have watched you workout, train etc, etc… We have found the chinks in your armor and you better believe that we are going to capitalize on them!
(Smacks Flatliner on his chest hard, Flatliner doesn’t even flinch… Looks at JC and grins, JC slowly starts to smile…)
KRAVEN: Come on big man, let’s go work out… (“With that said, he turns and starts to walk away followed by Flatliner and then JC… As I stood there, I couldn’t help but sort of feel sorry for JC… Why you asked!? Why should I feel sorry for that sneaky SOB!? Quite simple folks, JC is not the man in control here, or so it seems… But from where I stand it would seem as if Kraven is heading this group… Now I want to take a few minutes and direct my conversation to the other three teams, in this elimination match… Don’t take the UA for granted, do not underestimate them… They are very cunning, very devious and above all, while they love to brawl and just plain slug it out, they are very accomplished wrestlers… Technical as they come, but as brutal as barbarians! Is what JC said true!? Or is it just another deception!? Hard to say guys, one thing I do know about JC and that is that the man is always one or two steps ahead of the game… Don’t take anything he says for what he makes it out to be, and then again can it be that’s what he wants you to do!? Interesting predicament, you guys find yourself in, but hey you’re big boys, you’ll figure it out… Now the UA, that’s a whole different ballgame, devious and dangerous… They will hurt you bad, if you aren’t careful… Ever seen that new Army commercial!? You know, the one where they say, “ARMY OF ONE”…. Hehehe, Kraven and Flatliner aren’t an army of one… No, no guys… They are ONE of ONE! Very cohesive, very alert and most important very brutal yet technical when they want to be…. Two parts of a triad… The third part being Nevada… Hmmm, forgot about him, wonder if he’ll be there… Well guys, enough of me flapping my gums and making noise… Time to hit the weights, to plan, to strategize and try to come up with a plan to withstand showdown, believe me when I say, be afraid, be very afraid…. HAHAHAHA! Just jerking your chains guys… I am sure that you are all more than prepared to take on the UA and each other… Well this about wraps up this segment of yet another great show… Good night folks, guys… The pleasure has been all yours”….

Dalton approaches the camera, looking quite confused.)
DALTON: Kurt, we lost. We, the HIGH PLAINS DRIFTERS! You got much too distracted by the boobies, my friend. Sure, I was looking too, but yeah. And so what if it WAS me who got pinned, but i got hit in a very sensitive area!! But that doesn't bother us, does it? (Kurt shakes his head "no") Ok, now, pardon my vulgar language, but where in the heck is this competition? Really... Kurt? Where are they? (Kurt raises his arms so as to say "I dunno!") Exactly! We've been waiting for the opportune time to spring our Drift-fu on them, but nothing''s been happening. Sure, we've got the vets, Kraven and Flatliner,but not a single other team. You know what that means, Kurt?
KURT: What does it mean, Dalton? DALTON: It means that we just can't find them! We should go try to find that Kraven guy, but he's kinda scary, isn't he?
KURT: Yeah, he is. I don't wanna fight him. He'd probably hurt me. DALTON: KURT! We are not cowards! We're crusaders for truth! And you know what crusaders for truth do?
KURT: Back away quickly and let the other teams beat each other bloody? DALTON: You're darn right! And then, we swoop in for the kill. Or the pin, as the case may be.
KURT: Can we do that? DALTON: Sure, Kurt. We'll do that. And we'll do that good. Real good like. Like with a rock or something. Well, not a rock, but maybe i'll throw you, like at that Midg- er, Vertically Challenged Person Toss, and then BAM! Down they'll go. Like bowling with VCPs, as I like to call 'em.
KURT: I'm just stocky. I'm NOT VERTICALLY CHALLENGED! DALTON: Ok, ok... fine, stocky. And i'm stringy. Alright, let the search continue. To the halls!

(Our camera pulls back and Rocky Wellington enters the picture from the left side, and Britni and Shane Rockwell enter from the right side. The Movement, a tag team that no one knew about before Racine Riptide, now stands victorious over the High Plains Drifters. Rockwell's chisled physique is masked by blue jeans and a black leather jacket. Wellington wears a blue turtleneck sweater and Britni is dressed in a sun dress.)
ROCKWELL: (Smacking vigerously on a piece of gum) Yee-haw!! The Movement gets just one step closer to the GLCW tag team titles. See boys, I told ya I had the secret weapon. (Rockwell puts his arm around Britni's waist and draws her close) This here boys is the best weapon that money don't hafta buy! (He releases Britni who puts her arms on his shoulder and stares at him.) Ya know we've been cruisin' the West Coast, hittin' the beaches, riding the surf and all of the sudden we get this letter from Malec stating that we've been entered into some four-way tag team elimination match. Well, I had to make Wellington do his best Sherlock Holmes impersonation and frankly, my dear Watson, he discovered who our opponents would be.
WELLINGTON: I do thank you for that gesture of kindness Shane. At any rate, I did in fact discover who our opponents would be. First and foremost, the (makes quotes with his fingers) "The Unholy Alliance" of Kraven and Flatliner.
ROCKWELL: Yeah, the (all three of them make quotes with their fingers) "UNHOLY ALLIANCE". Well, seems to me that I did some research of my own. Seems that this Kraven character and Flatliner guy are legitimate. Now, I'm sure you two guys have been roughshed over the entire world of wrestling. But honestly guys, the 1980's are over and now it's the new breed that's around. I know you two were getting tired of sitting around at St. Matthews Retirement home picking the guacamole cassarole out of your dentures, but are you two really ready to step back into the ring with the younger stars of today? (He pauses to smack his gum more.) But listen old-timers, we're willing to step into the ring with you. I just pray that you've got your life insurance policies on hand, and maybe even your family doctor, 'cause as much as you've seen in your lives, NOTHING will prepare you for the FINAL APPROACH. Unholy Alliance, it doesn't matter to us where you've been, or hell, where you've come from home. What matter to us is that you lay it easy on the Bengay. I know yall old folks don't know the word sparingly when using Bengay. Go 'head and bring your arthritic asses to the ring so we can stop 'em. After that we'll grab you two wheelchairs, an express ticket to the emergency room and a week's supply of Jell-O for you to eat after we beat the living hell out of you two.
WELLINGTON: Well said chap! But after I found out about Kraven and Flatliner, I found out about another tag team. Gridlock.
ROCKWELL: WHAT IN THE HELL IS A GRIDLOCK? Who are they? (Both Britni and Wellington shrug their shoulders.) Okay, listen you two, you're able to escape the scrutiny of the Movement. We'll just chalk it up that you two guys haven't laced your boots up yet. (Wellington whispers into his ear) Well, ol' Rocky says you two are Raze and Nuke. (Rockwell, Britni and Wellington begin laughing uncontrollably) WHO IN THE NAME OF ALL THAT IS GOOD NAMED YOU TWO? Raze the nukes Rocky!! I mean, HONESTLY guys, is that the BEST you could come up with? Hell, I'm more intimidated by the names Kraven and Flatliner. Raze and Nuke... blow it out yer asses 'cause we'll be sticking our feet down your throats.
WELLINGTON: Finally I concluded that we'd be facing the High Plains Drifters. ROCKWELL: (Sighs) Again? Didn't we just beat those two yee-hooten cattlefolk? I'm not gonna get into the lives of the High Plains Drifters again. We beat their leather-laden asses last week, and quite frankly ain't nothing that I can imagine that would change the outcome again. Listen boys, it's all real simple. The Movement is a runaway freight train. Try to stop us if you can. But be prepared to find out that you just simply can't hang with the tightest tag team in the worrrrrrld, the MOVEMENT! Now stick that up your ass and think about it.
