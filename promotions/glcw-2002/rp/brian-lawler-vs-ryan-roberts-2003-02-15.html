GLCW — Roleplay

Brian Lawler vs. Ryan Roberts

15 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
TBirdSCIL — 15 February 2003 Please keep all RP for this match in this folder... LMccarver — 17 February 2003 Hell's Bell's by AC/DC plays on the scene. FADE IN: A apartment and from the looks of things, the room is a mess. books act as a leg on a couch and the room is filled with pizza boxes and beer cans. You hear a toilet flush and out steps The Twisted Prodigy, Ryan Roberts. Ryan's hair, like normal, hangs in face. He pushes it back out of his face and looks into the camera. Ryan walks over to the stero and turns it off. Ryan tosses a couple of pizza boxes off a chair and takes a seat.
ROBERTS: Brian Lawler I don't even know you and I already don't like ya. You see, you are the first victim, excuse me, opponent that I will face in the GLCW. The funny thing is you don't even realize what your about to get yourself into.
Ryan let's out a laugh and then looks up above his head. ROBERTS: He doesn't know the pain that you have taught me. The hours of training, the days of torture I went though. None of that will sink in, until I stand across the ring from him and I will make you proud. I will make Lawler scream out in pain, He will feel the agony that I went through.
Ryan laughs loudly again and then focuses on the camera. ROBERTS: My master wants you to feel the torture that I felt while I was training. I will not fail him, for your pain will bring me much pleasure. Many say that there's a fine line between pain and pleasure, but I've learned that to be false. Pain is pleasure. Do what you can, Brian Lawler. For you're about to step into the ring with a man that loves to feel pain and loves to dish it out. The doctors have set me free and it is you that will fall into defeat. The sands fall through the hour glass, Brian. It's only a matter of time until you feel real pain.
Ryan glares at the camera man Roberts: Get out of here, NOW!!! The screen quickly fades to black
