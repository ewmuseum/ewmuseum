GLCW — Roleplay

Anarky vs. Jared Justice

25 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
TBirdSCIL — 25 February 2003 Please keep all RP for this match in this folder... JLevinson — 1 March 2003 (FADEIN to the inside of Anarky's stark apartment. The camera pans past the nearly empty living room and down a white hallway. The camera takes a sharp left into the private gym, where Anarky sits, his back to the camera, having just finished doing a set of lat pull-downs. Sweat runs down his shirtless back and a plethora of scars. In fact, there is hardly any part of his back that isn't covered in some kind of scar or bruise.)
ANARKY: "Long I have waited, Jared... if I may call you that. Patiently, as always, hoping that, for once, I would not have to make the first move. Bring the pawn into the vulnerable position. But yet your patience... or is it apathy... far outweighed mine. I suppose I shouldn't be surprised. Your whole career has kind of been a joke, hasn't it, Jared? Always playing second fiddle to someone else.
"Well here's you big chance, Jared. You and me, one on one. I've made my move, and now you can either make yours... or simply resign. Either way it's the same to me. You're just another target. You're just another way to vent my frustration. You see, Jared, they think that I'm so consumed with getting a title shot that I'm not willing to EARN it. But of course, they lack any understanding whatsoever. They think that I covet their gold... their worthless little belt. But I assure YOU, Jared... that my interests far exceed any glory to be found here.
"For me, the best part, no, THE ONLY PART... are those precious moments we share together in that ring. And try as I might, Jared, I just can't seem to find someone who truly wants to share in this experience with me. Someone willing to fine tune themselves into that perfect weapon... in order to bludgeon me. So I await my battles with Maelstrom... or Rabesque... or Edmunds. And they give me you. A has-been. No... a never-was. I've watched your mediocrity for years now, Justice, and I can't say that I'm much impressed. But when you have no dedication... when you have no true BURNING FOR THIS, Jared, I guess I can't really EXPECT you to have any understanding.
"Well you don't have to understand for it to hurt. What you DON'T KNOW, Jared, is GOING TO HURT YOU. Of that I promise. It's your move now, Jared... what're you gonna do?"
(FADEOUT.) YinYangGuy — 9 March 2003 ((OORP: My apologies for being so late, I have had an unbelievably busy week.)) (FADE IN: Jared Justice is sitting in a dimly lit room. Two candles flicker in the background. Justice's looks shadowy as he speaks. "Firestarter (Empirion Mix)" by the Prodigy plays in the background.)
JARED JUSTICE: It is written that Anarky and I are to do battle this week. Anarky, you feel that you have the upperhand as you see me as being mediocre at best. This coming from a man who has past in and out of the wrestling scene for years. I really don't care about your perception of me. I really don't care what your goals are in the GLCW. I am not consumed with such petty things such as shiny metal belts. I have made it quite cleear what my goals are. Michael Manson will burn. Why is the management of this promotion wasting my time with the likes of you and Larry Tact? Wins and losses are of no consequence. Pain, torture and demise are all that matters. I'm not here to impress you or anyone else with my wrestling prowess. I want Manson to burn....plain and simple. Is this a hard concept to grasp? Is it Anarky?? Are you going to try to stop me from accomplishing my goal?
(Justice seems to grow a bit agitated. Justice clenches his fist and cracks his knuckles.)
JARED JUSTICE: Are you going to try to SAVE HIM???? HUH??? Go get your jollies fighting Maelstrom, Edmunds, and Rabesque. They don't matter. NONE OF THEM MATTERS TO ME. Manson MATTERS. His time grows short. All the delay tactics won't prevent the inevitable. It is his destiny. I am steadfast.....HE WILL BURN!!!! BURN!!!!! Anarky, if you wish to burn as well, just say the word and it shall be done!!!
(Justice grabs a candle and he stares at its flame. He seemingly becomes mesmerized by it. He then starts to laugh sadistically.)
(FADE OUT) JLevinson — 9 March 2003 (FADEIN to the empty rooftops of a building in Hartford, Connecticut. Steam rises from a vent into the clear night sky. Anarky stands near the edge of the building, looking up at the cloudless sky, the moonlight reflecting off of the painted white skull on his face.)
ANARKY: "Salvation, Mr. Justice, comes to those who need it. Manson doesn't need to be saved. He's more than capable of dealing with the likes of you. What I find AMUSING, however, is that you are so quick to BRUSH ME OFF. So consumed with Manson that you've lost all sight of everything outside of your own... petty... goals. Now, I know you think going after a piece of tin is petty, Jared, and you're right, it is. But it's no more or less petty than doing after Manson with a blowtorch.
"To answer your question, Jared... NO, I am not going to keep you from preventing you goal. Manson can protect himself. You were right about one thing, though. This IS about pain, torture and demise. But not Manson's. That's a story for another day, Jared, and YOU need to keep focused on the TASK AT HAND.
"If you think you can take me lightly, you're wrong. If you think my goals aren't important, you're doubly wrong. Because my goals... my INTENTIONS... are just as clear as yours. And that involves paving a way right to the GLCW Heavyweight Title, and more importantly, Maelstrom. And you, my GRACIOUS FRIEND, are IN THAT PATH. So whether or not you CARE ABOUT IT, you better DEAL WITH IT. I don't have time for your pyromania, Justice.
"You want to burn me? Add me to that short list of yours? Well it's all set, Justice, and all YOU have to do is SHOW UP."
(Anarky steps up onto the ledge of the building and extends his arms outwards. He sways for a moment and regainst his composure.)
ANARKY: "I know more than you realize, Jared. I know all about hate... and even more about pain. And if you think burning Manson, or me, or anyone else alive.. is going to make YOU feel more alive... then you're going to be sadly mistaken. But there's something to learn in all of this. Something to be gained.
"I once heard that it isn't until you've lost everything... that you're free to do anything. Well I'm not sure that's true, Justice, but I'm sure you're going to be able to tell me. Because at Riptide, you've got more than you bargained for. You've got ME, you've got MANSON, and you've got the UNHOLLY ALLIANCE. And if you think a blank stare and a blowtorch are going to save you from us...
"Well... you better start praying to a new God." (FADEOUT as Anarky laughs quietly to himself.) YinYangGuy — 10 March 2003 (FADE IN: Jared Justice is sitting a candlelit dressing room preparing for Riptide. CUE UP: "Firestarter (Empirion Mix)" by the Prodigy. Jared Justice appears to be in a foul mood.)
JARED JUSTICE: Anarky.....you speak of this task at hand. You think I've brushed you off as a nobody....a person of little consequence. You did the same to me. The truth is, we have no business together....no grudge.....no ill will. You appear to be looking to change that though. I am standing in your path to Maelstrom. When then consider me the inpenatrable force....an unmovable object obstructing your path. The voice in my head and I have decided that we don't like you. You're like Larry Tact....with your holier than thou attitude. You want me to pray to a new god. Who says that I even believe in a god? Perhaps pain is my god. Perhaps watching you smolder in an open fire is my god. I want to hear you scream Anarky. I want you to scream my name until your lungs burn. I want you to know true pain. Accept it as your friend. Allow it to devour you and spit you up again. Then you can speak to me about pain, torture and demise. I may be consumed with thoughts of getting to Manson. However, you've become my fantasy now. At Riptide, my fantasy will turn to reality. Bring Manson....let him watch. Let him see what destiny awaits him. You dismiss me. Bring the Unholy Alliance as well for I will show even them a new meaning to the word "Unholy!!!" You want all this for a belt????? I pity you, Anarky. I pity you for being consumed with such trivial things. I pity you for you have nothing better to do, no better aspirations. Win the belt from Maelstrom....in the end you'll lose it to him or someone else and be back to square one.....where you are and who you are now.....NOTHING.
(FADE OUT) JLevinson — 10 March 2003 (FADEIN to the dark locker room before Riptide. Anarky sits on the floor, his back up against the wall. The room is completely empty. His head hangs down and his snarled dirty blonde hair hangs over his face.)
ANARKY: "Jared... I applaud you. Once again, you realize that the GLCW Heavyweight Title is, in itself, worthless. However, what you, and everyone else around here fail to realize, is that that BELT... comes with something. It brings the meanest... the baddest... the most talented. Maelstrom knows it. Manson knows it. And I know it.
"You like to point fingers, Jared... you like to incinuate a lot of things. You think I've got a holier than thou attitude? You dare to compare me to Larry Tact?! No, Jared, I see that you've had your head up your ass so long that you can't tell the <BLEEP> coming out of your mouth from REALITY. I don't NEED Manson. I don't NEED the Unholy Alliance. I've got MORE THAN ENOUGH to BRING.
"I ain't BRAGGING, Jared. I'm just good at TELLING IT LIKE IT IS. And it ain't MY FAULT that you're a FRAUD and that you'll always BE A FRAUD. You think you've got what it takes? Well so does everyone else, but it never ends up mattering, does it?
"Jared... I beg of you... I plead of you... to make good on your word. You want me to scream your name in pain? Sounds exquisite. Smolder in an open fire? Oh, you entice me... but will you? Will you truly do these things? For I never know... so many... so very many have made those same promises.
"Pain... sharp, brutal, constant pain... beautiful, numbing, nullifying pain... yes, Jared... I know all about it... and I'm going to show you... just how painful this can be. And when I'm done with you, you will beg me for mercy. And then... and only then... will you know pain.
"Fin." (FADEOUT.
