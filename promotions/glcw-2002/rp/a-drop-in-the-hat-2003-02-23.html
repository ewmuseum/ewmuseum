A Drop In The Hat

23 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Suicide — 23 February 2003 (Camera fades in to a shot of a white Fedora hat, as it sits on gravel upside down, the crown open for all. After a few seconds go by, the sound of metal oscillating in the air is heard off camera. A few seconds later after this sound, a shiny object quickly falls into the Fedora's opening, feeling like it appeared there at the speed of light. The camera still focuses on the hat as yet again the sound of oscillating metal is heard in the background. Once again, after the sound, another shiny object falls into the hat. The camera pans closer to the hat as the sound and sight of whatever's flying into the hat quickly happens again. The camera shows several silver dollars at the bottom of the Fedora hat. Another silver dollar flies into the hat as the camera now zooms away from the hat. The camera as it zooms out shows a silver dollar flying through the air in an arc, aimed perfectly right into the Fedora. Every minute or so, another silver dollar is thrown into the hat with perfect accuracy.)
???????: "A drop in the hat...." (The camera pans towards the sound of the mysterious, yet familiar voice. Shown as the source of this voice is a man dressed in a white trench coat, his long black hair flowing down the back and sides of it, as he sits in a ripped apart Lay-Z-Boy recliner with plaid decor, looking quite comfortable. Although he isn't wearing his usual mask, the eyepatch covering his right eye with the familiar S symbol gives the man's identity away as none other than Suicide. He sits there staring at across his way, almost perfectly motionless, as he is the source of all the silver dollars flying into the Fedora.)
Suicide: "A drop in the hat. Yes the old, even correct, idiom is "at the drop of a hat". But what I'm talking about isn't something you'd foolishly give up to do whatever one's heart desires. The phrase "a drop in the hat" is more like replay....being rhetoric. I've been watching the GLCW and that's what the theme has become as of late: rhetoric. Constant rehashing of the past, reliving accolades of old, praising accomplishments that happened a year, two years, ten years ago. Doesn't matter if it's the old guard or the new blood; the end results are perfectly the same. Spitting the rhetoric because no one can't obviously look into the future, to progress, to move on. It seems to me many wrestlers are getting a bit too comfortable for their own good in the GLCW. Many of you have become content with the status quo. Perfectly fine. Some people don't wish to see what limits they can break with their own selves."
(The camera cuts to watching silver dollars fall into the Fedora, filling it more and more every minute.)
Suicide: "Your warrior chants of success and achievement, screaming to the entire wrestling world you will someday win the World Title or become famous.....and yet, no one cares. The more you do this, the more you're filling the proverbial hat until you can't fill it no more...."
(The camera shows silver dollars now bouncing off the tall heap of silver dollars in the Fedora.)
Suicide: "Now let's switch it up for moment. What if you were given the chance to stray from the norm? What if you were given the chance to actually show your worth and show who you're really about? Now what if you didn't need anyone to give you a chance and instead....you had the power to just do it?"
(The camera shows Suicide chucking a silver dollar in the air, completely sailing over the Fedora, not even aimed at the hat at all.)
Suicide: "This is why I'm here in the GLCW. I'm not here to conform to what everyone's doing. I could care less what little factions are building in the background or in front of the fans every week. I don't have the willpower to be consumed by the politics that everyone's getting so worked up here. I only have one desire, one comfort that allows me to sleep at night; and that's wrestling. This sport is my life. Some people are destined to do amazing things; to run countries, to lead an army into victory, to cure diseases. Then there are those who are destined to fail ultimately, to live in a pool of their own despair. I don't know what my destiny entails but I do know one thing: It's wrestling. You've got your legends, you've got your veterans, you've got your superstars, and you've got your wrestling's future. And you know what I think about the top names and soon-to-bes in GLCW? They are all the same. They are all human. They are all wrestlers. Wrestlers that I will wrestle, that I may defeat, and hopefully, will put them in their rightful place; back to reality.
(Suicide continues flicking silver dollars at the hat, never keeping his eye of the Fedora.)
Suicide: "So what am I doing back in this sport? No one's for sure and to be honest, I'm not sure. But let's just say this time around, it's different for ol' Suicide. Instead of being distracted by friends, instead of trying to be distracted by made up on the fly enemies, it's just me, the wrestling ring, and whoever President Malec decides to throw at me at that given night. And I'm going to do it, not for pride, not for prestige, not for titles, not for fame; I'm going to do it for wrestling. What I've seen has made me sick in the stomach and boys, it's time to get back to basics. And what makes me even more dangerous isn't this focus, but the one thing I can admit that I haven't seen anyone else in the GLCW that has the balls to do....and that's admit I'm human. It doesn't matter who you are, what you've done, and how long you can talk and put me to sleep. What only matters to me is not just if I can survive against you.....but if you can survive against me. Because if you can't do that, then all you'll be in the end is....."
(Suicide flicks a silver dollar towards the Fedora. The camera follows it in the air and as it lands on top of the heap in the hat. He then finally acknowledges the camera as he stares into it calmly yet intensely with his one good eye.)
Suicide: "....another drop in the hat.....'nuff said." (Suicide turns his attention back to the hat as he starts up the coin flipping once again. The camera pans away watching the mysterious wanderer flipping coins into the Fedora as his surroundings appear to be a city junk yard. The camera then fades out.)
