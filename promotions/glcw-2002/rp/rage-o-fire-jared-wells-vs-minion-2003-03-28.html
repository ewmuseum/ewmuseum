"Rage o' Fire" Jared Wells vs. Minion

28 March 2003

[name withheld] — 3 April 2003 (Camera opens up to the locker room as 'RAGE 'o FIRE' is sitting taking the tape off his wrist as he got done his match with Cannonball Kidd. Wells looks disappointed because of his outcome in the match. Camera zooms into Wells sweaty face)
JARED WELLS: 'Ya know tonight didn't really go as planned. But I should have known better. Kidd caught me off guard.....WHAM, both feet on the ropes, ONE, TWO, THREE. You haven't heard the last of RAGE my friend. Now onto the next stop at the Pepsi Coliseum. PATH of RAGE continues regardless of the loss. Minion, so you've signed the dotted line huh? I've known you a long long time pal. I remember your glory days. And I remember never ever facing you in the ring. As long as you and I have been around.....NEVER. Usually I would pick my bags up and leave after a loss like this but I'm not going to. Because if I quit, then the GLCW company wins. The suits 'n ties don't want to see me shine. They don't want me to rank at all because they know my history in the past. And sometimes I feel like I'm paying for my actions right now. So in order to change the way of thinking around here I must jump into the ring with the ALMIGHTY MINION. I guess you could say this is a match that has been waiting for a long time. To be honest with you I really don't care who I face anymore. One way or the other you will be stopped. And I know you just signed the long term contract with the GLCW but after our match, you'll be looking for a new job. My fans all around the world know that RAGE 'o FIRE will dominate. My fans will be breathing down your neck through the match. You'll start to get a nasty feeling in your stomach. Do you know what that feeling is? FEAR! PAIN! Name it and your thinking it right now. I'm sure your wondering what I'm talking about right? Well it's simple......come to the Pepsi Coliseum and look me in my eyes. Mind games? Nah....just the straight up reality. I've come off a BIG LOSS tonight and I can't afford losing another one. Whatever it takes DUDE, I will beat you. My fans are very forgiving and they allow me to do whatever. I know what your capable of and it won't be enough to stop RAGE. Nothing personal, just all business. RAGE is gonna roll right into Riptide with victory on his mind. What do you plan on doing? The choice is your BRAH! There is one thing on my mind and that is to get on cloud nine and become the GLCW HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPION. I will chop you down like a tree dude, and when you fall over very slow, my fans will take that extra breath to blow you down.
(Wells starts to take off his boots as he has a little pain in his face) JARED WELLS: We are only days away MINION. I have made my case to you, now I wait 'till you speak. I RESPECT you for what you've done for the business over the years.....but your time has come.
...TIME HAS COME TO RAGE.... ...RAGE IS GONNA GIVE TO 'YA.... (f/o) DavidMc — 10 April 2003 (Screen fades in as we see ourselves in Minions apartment. The lights are very dim perhaps either from the types of bulbs in the lights or just due the building being old and not allowing much power too much of anything. The camera pans around and the place is a mess. Over in the area where the television is cassette tapes litter the floor, boxes of food days old lies half eaten with some of the boxes turned over oozing out the remains of what was once something edible. The camera keeps panning and we can see newspapers littering the floor and various other things. The camera moves around the house very stealthily looking for Minion. When the camera rounds one corner we see a silhouette of a finger standing by a window looking out. Lightening races across the sky illuminating the room and we can tell it’s the Minion without even having to see his face. We can hear the rain outside start to come down. The Minion speaks without even turning around)
Minion: I love the rain. I love how the symbolism attached to it; how it is supposed to wash away the sins of any man and you can start anew. While most common people can have their sins washed away by a little acid rain, but not me. My sins committed upon this world are far too numerous and the truth of the matter is…I like my sins. I like to be envious of myself. I love my arrogance. I love the dirty thoughts that race through my mind every second of everyday. While everyone can start over I cannot. I am doomed to face myself in an endless cycle of repeats and more repeats. Though I have accepted my cruel fate; in fact I have learned to embrace my fate to the best of my ability. The world works in mysterious ways. What I call a curse brings me money. What I call a non lethal virus brings so much joy to my life even though that joy is ripping people a part but still joy is joy no matter where it comes from right? Think if I had never had a fascination with a washed up wrestler I would not be here. I would not be able to entertain the thousands and thousands of GLCW fans ever week. Think how bad a world that would be. Think of all the souls that would be so crushed that I didn’t come out from the locker room to entertain you worthless cows. But that is fine because as they cheer me on I get my pay check from them so it doesn’t bother me one bit. But come next riptide I get to trash one of their hero’s the man whose dream match I am about to make come true. One Mr. Rage of Fire Jared Wells.
(Minion reaches into his coat pocket and we can hear him flick his lighter and light up his cigarette. He takes some deep drags and exhales then begins)
Minion: I remember when I was young and watching you on the television. Your time has passed you by Wells; the time for the young super stars of the wrestling world to step up the plate. You had your time in the sun now it is time for people like me to emerge from the shadows and take your place in the sun. You are not just fighting for rankings. You are not just fighting for your paycheck you are fighting for you spot on the pecking order. You know that the youngsters especially me are hungry and have been for along time. You know that I will do anything to take your spot. You know how that would make me feel to beat someone I watched on television before I even entered the sport. You are right the suits don’t want you to shine. There is no more money left you in you anymore. The money lies within me. The brass know this or else they wouldn’t of booked you against me. They know that when the sheep turn on their TV for their weekly Riptide they will mainly tuning in to watch me. To see what I will do to the poor helpless individual across the ring from me. Perhaps they tune in to watch me lose but every week I keep disappointing them. Perhaps I will develop a cult following. Wells don’t get any wild idea running through your head about how you will beat me and make all the idiotic fans you love so dearly happy. See I think there is one thing you need learn about fans if you haven’t already and that is they are fickle. You see they will turn on you in a heart beat they will hate you if you do the slightest thing wrong in their eye. They are worthless. They have no clue what it takes to be a professional wrestler that is why I loathe them.
(Minion takes another drag off his cigarette and then drops it to the floor, stomps it out, and then continues.)
Minion: In reality Wells you know I will win. You know that this match you have dreaming of always turns out negative in your dreams. In reality Wells I don’t think you have what it takes anymore to hang with someone like me. This isn’t mind games I am playing with you Wells. Oh no no no this is the cold hard truth. It is a bitter pill to swallow but one you will have to swallow once the bell rings. I plan to take you from pillar to post beating your body senseless as the idiotic fans boo me. The boo’s only feed my hunger for blood; their boo’s only enrage me more to keep mashing your body into the ground over and over. It brings such joy to me to make at least one person’s life hell it makes me ecstatic to make thousands of people lives a living hell. Perhaps you should try it one day. When the time comes for Riptide just show up Wells and take the easy way out. Just lay down and take the 1-2-3 so we can both move on. Because we all know where you are going and we all know where I am going so let’s stop kidding ourselves that you could possibly have anymore future left in you. The time is at hand Wells. I want to feel your Rage. I want to experience the Rage I want you to unleash yourself upon me so that I can just beat you down like the dog that you are. You will fall from grace as I rise up to the top where I belong. Enjoy the fall it’s a long way down.
