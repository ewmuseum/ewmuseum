The Oak Street Beach Patrol vs. The Dogs of War

11 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
TBirdSCIL — 11 March 2003 Please keep all RP for this match in this folder... Mister Dread — 15 March 2003 ::FADEIN on the rickety, sagging ring at the Basham-Schultz Wrestling Academy, the dim and unsavory haunt of the Dogs of War. "THE WOLF" CHRIS MCMILLAN and JASON PAYNE are running drills in the center of the ring, trading holds and running the ropes. After a while, PAYNE calls a break and grabs a towel. THE WOLF turns, catching sight of the camera crew. He nudges PAYNE, and they turn to face the camera::
THE WOLF: So, the Dogs of War make their debut soon, facing off against ... The Oak Street Beach Patrol. That's some scary stuff, man. You've gotta fear and respect two guys that wear Ocean Pacific clothes and spend their days playing T&C Surf Designs on the NES.
PAYNE: (chuckling) Hey, show a little respect. You've got to stay on your game, Chris. We're facing off against one of the PREMIER GLCW tag-teams in Hepatitis Harry and Anal Cavity Alex ...
THE WOLF: Uhhhhh, I think that's "Hang Ten Harry" and "Aloha Alex". But I could be wrong ...
PAYNE: Whatever. Doesn't matter what these snapperheads call themselves. They're not ready for the Dogs of War. Not even close. For that matter, neither is anybody else in the whole damn federation. See, not only do the Dogs of War have more ambition, hunger, and drive than any other tag team here, each of us is a virtual walking encyclopedia of wrestling. Any hold you can think of, we can counter. Any move you can pull out, we can reverse.
THE WOLF: Now, I know we're not the biggest dogs in the yard. But we're DAMN sure the meanest. We're here, ready to win at any cost. If that means that we have to hurt you, so be it. If that means we have to MAIM you, well, that's the way it goes. I will not hesitate to end a man's career if I think he's in my way, and neither will Jason Payne. Put quite simply, the Dogs of War are the most lethal force to ever, EVER step into the ring. Our currency is flesh and bone, and we've come to collect.
PAYNE: Oak Street Beach Patrol ... Welcome to Purgatory. ::FADEOUT:: Greggulator — 20 March 2003 (CUT TO: The GLCW banner hangs in front of a black backdrop. Clad in black wet-suits, with the GLCW logo air-brushed on in day-glo pastels, is ALOHA ALEX and HANG TEN HAROLD-- the Oak Street Beach Patrol.)
10: That's right, the OHHH ESS BEE PEE is back in front of the GLCW cameras, after a few days of surf... heheheh... and turf.
HAND: HOOT! HOOT! HOOT! 10: Y'see, me and my partner in Rhyme'n'Crime here love doing what we do. Since we were little kids, we've been best of friends-- tagging up to win multiple Tandem Titles on the prestigious and ultra-competitive Great Lakes Surfing Circut. After conquering the tubular waves of Huron, Ontario, Michigan, Erie and Superior, we decided we needed another rush-- and that brought us here to the world of professional wrestling.
HAND: HOOT! HOOT! HOOOOOOT! 10: Now, our next foes await. Dogs of War-- you are new, we are new. But that's where the similarities end. You claim to be able to destroy my career? Well, Dogs, we've gone down the Pipeline down the St. Lawrence Seaway. That's a ten-foot break... and me and my main muchaho here surfed it to the max and LIVED to tell about it. You think we're scared of two cats that hang out in dimly-lit parlors who use words like snapperheads? No way-- not when we have the combined powers of our fans behind us... the Oak Street Beach Patrol Party Posse.
HAND: HOOT! HOOOT! HOOOT! HOOOOOT! 10: So, McMillan and Payne... when we face off... you're going to find-out first hand while WE are the only new tag team that matters here in the GLCW. And the world is going to see why you're called the Dogs of War. And that's because... YOWZA... you guys defnitely bite! Let's go surf!
HAND: HOOT! HOOT! HOOT! HOOT! HOOOOT! HOOOT! (FTB) Mister Dread — 20 March 2003 ::FADEIN on a stretch of highway. US 115 just outside of Cadillac, Michigan, in fact. We see a lone vehicle on the side of the road, a metallic mint green 1969 Plymouth Fury II. Perhaps the ugliest car ever made, it rests on the shoulder with the hood up. "The Wolf" Chris McMillan and Jason Payne stand there, staring at the engine in bewilderment::
PAYNE: I'm telling you, there's something wrong with the alternator. (pointing) THE WOLF: ... That's the distributor cap. Idiot. PAYNE: (insulted) Whatever, man. I only took auto shop for three years, y'know. Anyway, did you see the Oak Street Beah Patrol on TV the other day. Man, what a joke.
THE WOLF: Tell me about it. I mean, HOOT? Jeebus. PAYNE: We're gonna violate those guys in Flint! THE WOLF: ... PAYNE: HEY! That's not what I meant. I was trying to say that it's gonna be an easy win for us.
THE WOLF: Provided we MAKE it to Flint. Stupid car. ::Payne kicks the side of the car, hard. We FADEOUT to censored profanity and the sight of Payne hopping around like a retard::
