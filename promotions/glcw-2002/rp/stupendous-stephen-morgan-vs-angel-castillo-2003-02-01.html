GLCW — Roleplay

"Stupendous" Stephen Morgan vs. Angel Castillo

1 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
TBirdSCIL — 1 February 2003 All RP for this match goes here... SouthernBoy — 6 February 2003 {{...FADE-IN: "Stupendous" Stephen Morgan is standing on pier overlooking Lake Erie in Cleveland. He's dressed in a black leather jacket over a grey roll collar sweater, wood flannel pants, and brown hiking boots. Morgan shades his eyes from the sun as he looks out over the water. He moves his line of vision back and forth as if he's looking for something...}}
"STUPENDOUS" STEPHEN MORGAN: " Well, I've been standing here for about 20 minutes now and still no sign of the next wave of Cubans coming in on their little wooden boats. Shame really, I was looking forward to getting a look at the struggling masses looking to make a name for themselves in the land of the free. "
" I guess I'll just have to settle for meeting mister Angel Castillo IN PERSON. A real Cuban, LIVE and in living color...man, I truly can not wait. For you see Mister Castillo, it's always been a dream of mine to meet an immigrant such as yourself and hear your stories of hardship and suffering....stories of how much your sacrificed to come to America and live our your dream. At Riptide, I get my chance to listen to YOUR story. And when you're done, I'm going to BEAT THE LIVING HELL OUT OF YOU and send you back to your island with a TRUE Vision of what America is all about. You see when you're rich, you MAKE the rules and ..."
{{...Suddenly a Lake Erie patrolman comes up to Morgan and taps him on the shoulder. Morgan is startled and almost falls into the water, the patrolman just catching him...}}
PATROL OFFICER: " Son, didn't you see the sign out front. THIS pier is bein' renovated right now. You're libel to fall into the water if you ain't careful. "
SM: " Sorry officer, I just wanted to catch a glimpse of America, you know, trying to catch some Cubans swimming in. "
PO: " Uh, son...Cubans come into Florida, you're on the wrong end of the country. " {{...Morgan gets red-faced and walks up close to the patrolmen and whispers something into his ear. We hear the last little whisper Morgan tells the officer...}}
SM: " ...just go with me here. The GLCW refused to pay for my ticket down to the Keys to film this. "
{{...Morgan the pulls out a wad of cash and hands it to the officer who just nods...}} PO: " Pardon me MISTER Morgan. I didn't realize exactly what you said. Yes sir, that is a beautiful view of the...uh...Atlantic? "
SM: " Yes officer, it sure is. Will there be anything else? " PO: " No sir. You have a good day. " SM: " Adios amigo. " {{...The patrolman just shakes his head and walks away leaving Morgan alone once again. He again looks out over the water...}}
SM: " As I was saying Angel, mind if I call you Angel? As I was saying, it's going to be a real honor to beat down one of Castro's own and send him running back with his pinko tail between his legs. ... I tell you what Angel, I'll make you a DEAL huh? When you get back to Cuba, look Castro up for me and see if you can't get me a few of those cigars. Do that for me, and I'll send you back enough "denero" for you to feed your family corn or beans, or whatever it is you grown down there...FOR LIFE. Deal? "
" Anyway, I look forward to our meeting Angel...it's going to be a good time for one and all. Sorry it'll end so badly for you. "
{{...FADE OUT as Morgan looks out over the lake one more time, then FADE TO BLACK...}} SouthernBoy — 12 February 2003 {{...FADE-IN: "Stupendous" Stephen Morgan is standing in front of a American Flag backdrop with the letters "GLCW" in script gold letters on it. He's dressed in an "Uncle Sam" outfit complete with white beard and top hat. He's starting at the camera intently, then points his finger directly at the lens...))
SM: " UNCLE MORGAN WANTS YOU! That's right Angel Castillo, Uncle Morgan has taken time out of his busy schedule promoting the upcoming Oil War with Iraq to give you a little HISTORY lesson. Now don't worry, this lesson doesn't involve any "book learnin'", since you probably can't read anyway, all you have to do is get your translator, kick back with a good Cuban CEEGAR and LISTEN. Now before you go to sleep on me, this isn't just ANY ol' history lesson about the US of A, no sir, THIS history lesson is about a GREAT American HERO, a patriotic man who over-came the odds and became one of the greatest wrestling superstars the sport has ever seen. I'm talking, of course, about STUPENDOUS Stephen Morgan. "
" He came from humble beginnings, the son of a Fortune 500 Company VICE-president, not president, he grew up just OFF of Beverly Hills, California. Sure, he had it rough growing up...the older kids on the polo team hated him because he was so much better. The bigger wrestlers used to team up on him and beat him up because, as a FRESHMAN, he was doing more than they could ever dream of doing as seniors. And yes, he had to overcome his father only being a MILLIONAIRE instead of a BILLIONAIRE, but he managed. He worked hard every day after school going searching the helps rooms to make sure they weren’t stealing...yes, he put in his five hour work week with pride. His father pushed him hard to follow in his footsteps, but this man had a DREAM...a dream of becoming what every woman wanted, and what every man wanted to BE. He began getting offers to star in major motion pictures, record CD's, and even star in his own drama on NBC. But this guy turned them ALL down to do the ONE thing that he KNEW that was the best in the WORLD at...wrestling. "
" So he went to a now defunct league I've been told I can't mention...a rookie, a man just starting out. Nobody gave him a CHANCE. But do YOU know what this guy did Angel? He only won the WORLD TITLE in a TWENTY man gauntlet match that included some of the best wrestlers in the WORLD...and not only that Angel, NOT only did he beat out TWENTY other men, but he DREW NUMBER ONE. He showed the WORLD what he was made of that night...he showed the world just WHO he WAS. "
" Now he's in GLCW Angel...he's here to face what he's been told is the greatest competition this side of the MISSI SIP. He's HERE to show the world, ONCE AGAIN, what he's made of. But there's a problem Angel. He's being held BACK. He's being put in meaningless NON TV matches against guys that don't bother to cut promos...he's being BURIRED at the BOTTOM of the card because the BRASS of the front office want to push some PSYCHO all the way to the MOON. Well Angel, they're going to have to DEAL with this guy soon enough. "
" Do YOU know who he is Angel? Have you heard the story? Well, if you're still a little confused, which is to be expected given your questionable heritage, then ... "
{{...Morgan winks...}} " ...you'll find out at Riptide. " {{...FADE OUT...}}
