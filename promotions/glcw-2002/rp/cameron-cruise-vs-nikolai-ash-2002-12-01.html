GLCW — Roleplay

Cameron Cruise vs. Nikolai Ash

1 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
TBirdSCIL — 1 December 2002 All RP for this match goes in this folder. zeroxxzero — 8 December 2002 (fade in: close up a roaring fire, the embers fly out at a rapid pace. Camera begins to make a controlled pan and zoom out to see Nikolai Ash sitting in a antique cedar rocking chair. Wearing a black sweater and dark blue stonewashed jeans, and black loafers. His hair is neatly combed. Ash is taking very deep breaths, but exhaling slowly. His hands are raised in front of his face and slowly coming down across it, he squints his eyes and looks groggily into the camera.)
…so another day is upon us, and another ..ahem..wonderful show. As I sat in my bed last week, I awoke to the phone call from the GLCW head offices ..in some Midwest city… god if I know the name, or care.. I was informed that “You’re going to have to face Cameron Cruise at the next Riptide event..yada yada..” Wonderful I thought to myself, another way for me to ..quote un quote prove myself… hmm, what a larf? How amusing is it… that I have to keep proving myself to a bunch of rednecks and washed up car sales man… who cant make this months mortgage, so they piss what they do have away at the GLCW..they bring their children and try to show them they care about them, because after he and his wife divorce they’ll want to pick him over her. But that’s what America has come to.. money grubbing, attention starved, homeless, heroin addicted lovers of sacrilege. ::chuckles:: shame? I think not. Just a matter of evolution, something a lot of the fans … and at least three quarters of the locker room have not gone through since their monkey ancestors first picked a banana off the tree. Then again most of the locker room and fans.. cant comprehend three quarters.. nor do they even have that much in their savings account.
::Ash begins to rub his temples with the tips of his pointer and ring fingers:: But that is hardly the point I am trying to make now.. or is it? Who even knows anymore? I must sound so carnivorous.. biting, shredding, tearing my way through the public like a pack of ravenous wolves. Ahh.. who is to care? For.. I do not see it mattering, because no matter how I portray myself to the public.. they will always look at me different.. even after I dispose of their flimsy completion.. such as… Cameron Cruise. Who would have ever thought I… would be here.. or better yet, who would have ever thought… Cruise stood a chance..
(fade out) TBirdSCIL — 8 December 2002 :::NOTE- I will be posting the RPs for Cameron Cruise until further notice. He has been having problems posting so I will do it for him until the problem is solved:::
(fade in to an afternoon sky panning lower to a mansion-ish house. The camera moves forward as if you were the one walking up the walkway, up to the front door. Just as a hand reaches to knock, a door opens revealing Cameron Cruise looking very Robin Leach-ish, with the exception of Cruise's trademark shades replacing the pipe.) Cruise: Ahh, welcome, please come in, come in. What is it that brings you here? (a muffled voice much like the peanuts school teacher is heard, and Cruise smiles) CC: Promo time then is it? Well when isn't it a good time for promos? (Cruise sits down, in a lounge chair). Let's to it then. Now, from what I understand the young man I face is named Nikolai Ash....and he seem's abit bored. Well now, Mister Ash...I have some advice for you, to your comments made toward me. You're LATE. I've been proving myself time and time again for years, against new crowds and in different organizations, not just THIS ONE. What makes *YOU* any different? What makes your testimony any different from mine? What gives you the RIGHT to think that you can declare yourself better when you have never even seen, heard, talked, anything about me at all....just because I haven't gotten the chance to say my piece before you. That's right, kid. I'm a busy man. I don't just sit around sleeping things off just because I'm too bored to do anything else. I told Savage the same thing that Ill tell you right now. Knock it off. Don't assume, just because I haven't said anything yet, that I still won't kick the insolence out of your hide. So before you even decide to comeback with some self-rightuous comments about this or that or Sylvester the cat....take a big chunk out of what I say, and sit back awhile and chew on that. (From the right side of the screen comes two men that approaches Cruise, and whisper something in his ear.) CC: Yes, yes, okay. He can have it back. (Cruise then takes off robe and gives it back to the two men and walks out of view. Fade out.) zeroxxzero — 9 December 2002 (fade in: a close up of Nikolai Ash’s face, his hair is combed, and he is sporting a five o’clock shadow on his square jaw. You are so close you can almost feel his exhales. As the camera begins to zoom out we see Ash sitting in the same ancestral rocking chair he was once before.)
hmmm… Mr. Cruise, it is wonderful that after such a long wait, you’ve finally come to talk to me.. or at least take yourself away from watching A Charlie Brown Christmas. Hopefully, Snoopy and the gang will not miss you? ::chuckles:: It is yes..promo time, time for you to spray your incredulous saliva onto the television monitors. You seem very skeptical, about me, and my talents, that may be why I am so bored.. that and the wait I had to endure this past week. Like most wrestlers, you will sit and make accusations ..or claims ..or whatever about how wonderful you are, and how I will “perish” in the ring with you.. that’s so very comical. Most wrestlers never take the time to watch others in the ring, scope out their charisma, their stamina, their ability… most will sit and babble on about nonsense, making claims that are false.. that have no baking what-so-ever. How is it possible? No one really knows… but you are just the same. You are another pompous, run-of-the-mill, jackass.
::takes a sip of water and clears his throat:: see, what you do not understand is.. I never claimed I was better… I just happened to play on your weaknesses, you just happen to believe it was bashing… but it was quite the other way around my friend. Your mind may have played tricks on you, your subconscious and conscious conspiring against your cerebellum. Now you may have been proving yourself, but you’ve only been proving something to yourself, not the fans… or anyone else of importance… why is that? Well.. possibly because you’ve never been able to grab the crowds attention because of your lack of flare and charisma.
Sir…or sirs, however many multi-facetted personalities you poses.. you can tell me all you wish, that does not prove any more to me, than does your inadequate silence.. For you may wish for me to knock off my incessant guffawing and slander towards you and any other GLCW wrestler… but it is my job here… possibly a duty sent from the hand of God himself to exasperate the feelings of all the humans I can. Simply put… for all you imbeciles, I am here to annoy you… and bother you, and let you see you for the mistakes and scum’s of the Earth you truly are. ::pauses to collect his thoughts:: I’ve taken a few hours, to ponder your thoughts and have laid to rest any suspicion of a challenge.
Or… who knows.. I could be wrong and you could turn out to be the greatest challenge of my short career, but.. on second though, I’m hardly ever wrong.. see you on a fortnight sir.
(fade)
