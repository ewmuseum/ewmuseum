TBirdSCIL — 22 September 2002 All RP for this match goes in this folder... ChrisHorowitz — 25 September 2002 (Cue up "A Whiter Shade of Pale" by Procal Harum.) (Fade in to the Whitelandia compound of the Jobber. Cut to the living room of the estate, where the Jobber sits, looking more intent, more angry than he has been seen in the GLCW. He is not intently focused, or filled with rage, but he is not the passive commentator that we have seen in the past.)
The Jobber: For three years I have come to the ring, and one thing was certain. For three years I would enter the ring, and know that when I left I would not have been pinned, or submitted. For three years I never lost. For three years only wins were placed on my record, and the losses went to others in the match. For three years never to be pinned, never surrendering. But now what do I do? The streak is dead. The thing that set me above all others has come to an end, finally it is proven that I am mortal. No one since Stone Wolf has shown me or the fans my mortality, my limits. Since then my limits have not even been tested, it is only others who have failed me, and had their limits found out during the course of battle. So what now? I have been relegated to the side show. I have to enter the ring for another prize than what I came here for, with a long winded whiney fork tounge wind bag in Jared Poe, a less whiney windbag who has already fallen to me in the past in Nick Kurtel, an off tempo character who has fallen to me recently in Castillo, someone I couldn't care less about in Steven James, and Troy Martinez, someone who must rely on past accomplishments to even achieve some resemblence of respectability these days. No one knows what these accomplishments are, or where they were accomplished, but none the less Martinez is presented as an accomplished veteran who is a master of the squared circle. Martinez, I know the truth, you are a washed up probable never was, something that everyone else in this match will probably live up to as well.
The Jobber: These are the opponents I have to look foward to, not Michael Manson. My ultimate opponent has been denied, by Golem. Of all people, why must if have been one I brushed aside so many years ago? The loss is one thing, but to him? To Manson I could live with it, but Golem, one who is only a legend in his own mind? That is what I will have to carry with me in this match, and those to follow. But it will be fresh for this one, and ring truer here than in any other, because those here can only hope to reach the fairly weak status of Golem, and most of you will only dream that you have reached the status of Golem. Golem is still a notable grappler, while none of you are. A loss to him is one thing, but do not think that this has somehow sidetracked me. Three years of winning lead to that loss, and that loss stings, and it will not be repeated. Not here, with this prize. Those others in this match know the sting of defeat all to well. To them it is common, they accept it and just move on to another loss. But to a person who hasn't lost in three years, the sting of defeat is so potent that as it happens you realize, as I have realized that it cannot be allowed to repeat. This sting cannot become happenstance in my career, because I will not allow it. I did not come this far to allow myself to be taken down to this level by Golem only to lose! I did not come this far to fail to capture a television title. I did not miss my chance to face my ultimate opponent only to go down in defeat to some nobody like Angel Castillo, or Showtime Steven James. None of you deserve to be taken seriously. One deserves to be a carnival side show, another the host of a Cuban cooking show on the Food Network, and the rest just the dregs of the wrestling world, none deserve to hold a belt, not even a television title. None deserve to be taken seriously, but in this match, at this point I take EVERYTHING seriously. I will not be embarassed again! I will not go fall, I will not be defeated by those who are inferior to me! I will triumph in this match, because that sting of defeat, its foul taste must never, NEVER pass my lips again!
(The Jobber leans foward, becoming more animated, more into the interview as he does.) The Jobber: These are not threats, they are simply the rantings of a lunatic. A madness has swept over me, one that I cannot control. I cannot let myself fall Castillo, James, Poe, Kurtel, and Martinez. I cannot let my mistakes against Golem repeat themselves. The teachers in elementary schools tell us we learn from our mistakes and our losses, and perhaps this is true, because I have learned one thing, that I cannot let it happen again, that I know the mistakes, and I will not repeat them. You all will not prevent my climb back up the slope I fell down against Golem. I will return to the top to avenge this loss, and to claim the ultimate prize. For now I will settle for this prize, this television title, and will lay to waste any opponents I may have to deal with in this gauntlet. This loss has only served to awaken me, and as Castillo found out, even as I slept I was more than a match for him. Even asleep I was more of a match for almost any man here, simply not Golem on that day. Now that I have been awaken, now that I have a mission, a calling to prevent another embarassment, I will be that much more of a man, that much more than what Castillo couldn't defeat, that Kurtel couldn't defeat, that Nevada Smith couldn't defeat, and that EVERYONE for the last three years has been unable to defeat. If a sleeping giant could do that to the wrestling world, what will I do now that I have been filled with renewed passion for this sport? What will happen to any of you who have to enter the ring opposite me in this gauntlet? Just think to yourselves, think what I have done for so long as only a mere shadow of my greatness, and now think that the shadow is gone, and only the real man behind that shadow remains. You will not be facing my shade in this match, you will be facing me, at my peak. There will be no overlooking, no ignoring, none of that. I will simply deal with the challengers I am paired up against, and make them realize that I have awakened.
