GLCW — Roleplay

"The Future" Brett Irvine vs. Jon Savage

5 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
TBirdSCIL — 5 August 2002 All RP for this match goes here. jgoods 21 — 8 August 2002 (Scene opens in a locker room somewhere in the arena, the cameraman goes through a dressin room door with "THE NATURE BOY" written on it. The camera pans around the room to show a man standing with his back to the camera wearing a white cotton bath robe with 49-time World Heavyweight Intercontinental Television Champion spray paitned on it. The man seems to be wearing a very bad bleach blonde wig, he then turns to the camera to reveal, it's "THE FUTURE")
FUTURE: WHOOOOOOOOOOOO!!!!!!!!!!!!! Ladies and females, I wanna let you know that "The Nature Boy" has arrived. Oh yes! Now that "THE FUTURE" is in GLCW things will start to look up. Cause when you have a former 49-time World Heavyweight Intercontinental Television Champion like myself you dont need anyone else. WHOOOOOOOOOO!!!!!!!!!! Heck, I could just come out and roll around in the ring for 30 minutes droppin elbows and knee drops on the mat. Well, at least that would be more entertaining than say, Ric Fla.........oh wait, sorry, I meant Jon Savage.
(He begins to strut back and forth around the locker room, screaming WHOOOOOOOOOO!!!!!!!!!!)
FUTURE: Now Savage you know your not as big of a kiss stealin, wheelin dealin, limo drivin, camel ridin, son of a gun, as the former 49-time World Heavyweight Intercontinental Television Champion, but then again who is. Nobody that's who. Now unlike the real Nature Boy, no not Buddy Rogers, wait he was the real one, but anyway Ric Flair. I've got a few more than 3 good moves, I can do more than a knee drop, a chop, and a figure four, oh no. I've got 4 good moves, nah, I've got a lot more than that, because I am the Best Technical Wrestler in the World.
(He takes off the robe and the wig and throws it down.) FUTURE: I came to GLCW to be the man and to be the man I gotta beat the man, but Savage you ain't the man, that don't mean I'm not gonna whip your a**. So Savage when ya step into the ring to go one on one with the One True Icon in this Business, boy you'd better be ready for a lesson. A lesson from the Personification of Perfection. And that's a fact.
(FUTURE walks over and looks into the camera.) FUTURE: So gang, "THE FUTURE" is here and things are about to change, big time. But here are some words for all of ya to live by during my era as, the man, and those very important words are, "THE FUTURE IS NOW!!!!!!!!!!"
(He walks out of the locker rooms as the camera fades to black.) JonSavageESQ — 10 August 2002 [updated:LAST EDITED ON Aug-10-02 AT 10:16&nbsp;AM (EDT)](Camera fades into Jon Savage entering the Palace of Auburn Hills when world-renowned wrestling announcer Dallas Austin stops him to ask a couple of questions).....
DALLAS: How do you feel about your loss to Redemption in the first round of the GLCW Title Tournament???
SAVAGE: How do I feel? You ask me "how do I feel?" (mimicking) "How do I feel....how do I feel?" Tell me something, how does THIS feel?
(Savage grabs the microphone with one hand and shoves Austin out of camera view with the other. A loud crash is heard followed by moaning)
SAVAGE: Jackass. Redemption, we WILL meet again VERY soon. Now I'm out of the GLCW title tournament, I have more important things to focus on. Who is this youngster that the GLCW matchmakers have pitted me against? What's his name, Prime Time or The Real Deal or something? Oh yeah, I remember, "The Future" Bret Irvine. Well let me tell you something, kid, and I want to be sure you get this the first time so listen REAL close, K?
(pause) SAVAGE: Be sure your Prudential Life Insurance policy is paid up. Kiss your wife/girlfriend/signficant other goodbye before you go to the ring. Ask a chaplain to pray with you while you're going to the ring. Enjoy all the pyro, sights, and sounds of your introduction and heck, even strut around a little. 'Cause when that bell RINGS, it's MY TIME. You will feel pain.....you will feel agony.....and you will....WILL.....feel defeat....at....the.....hands.....of.....me! After I pin you and get my first GLCW victory, then "The Future" will be....THE PAST!!!!!
(Savage drops mic and heads to dressing room as camera pans over to security still aiding Dallas Austin. FTB)
jgoods 21 — 11 August 2002 (Scene opens with "THE FUTURE" lacing up his boots and getting a drink of water. He pours a little water on his head and looks up to see a camera.)
FUTURE: Now, Jonny Boy. You know better than to pretend you don't know me. I mean, I am the One True Icon in GLCW. I'm the reason this company is going to suceed, I'm the reason this company has a prayer, I'm the reason this company has a "FUTURE" No pun intended. Wait, yes there was. But anyway, see my first order of business here in GLCW is to dispose of the 10 time Michigan State Pig Wrestling champion or whatever he calls himself. Savage you may think it's cool to push annouuncers around and repeat the same line 5 times, but me and the fans of GLCW don't, ya know what we think is cool. That's right, me, "THE FUTURE"
(He takes another drink of water and gives a little flex to the camera.) FUTURE: (laughs) Savage I know muscles don't help you win titles, and in your case, God knows they don't help you become a pro wrestler. But, I had to work hard to get these and I'm doubting I'll have to work to hard in my match against you. But, what the Personification of Perfection will have to work hard to do is to show everyone in GLCW that I am just what I say I am the Best Technical Wrestler in the Business.
(He looks down at the floor trying to think of something to say.) FUTURE: Oh yeah! I almost forgot, it's time for me to introduce a new segment in my already breathtaking interviews. And that segment is called, Let Me Predict "THE FUTURE" So, Savage here is exactly what is gonna happen when we step in the ring to go one on one. You will slowly walk down to the ring to a frightening silence I mean you could here a fat guy in the 300th row drop his hot dog. Then, when my music hits, you'll have to pull out earplugs, but that still won't help to quiet the deafening 1 million decible cheers, ringing throughtout the arena. We'll step into the ring stare down face to face, then in fear you will wet your pants and pass out. The ref will just start the match anyway, because nobody really cares about you. I will walk over and place my foot across your face and the ref will count the 1,2,3. I will play to my millions of fans and you will wake up three weeks later in a trash bin in New Mexico, wearing nothing but your matching red and blue jacket and a smile. A small Mexican man will tell you that you stumbled in like that 4 days ago, you will then remember why your there. Because, you messed with "THE FUTURE" And that's not a prediction, That's A Fact.
(He laughs and looks into the camera.) FUTURE: Savage, your gonna find out soon enough, that, "THE FUTURE IS NOW!!!!!!!!" (Fade to black.) JonSavageESQ — 13 August 2002 (Scene opens with Savage holding a microphone in front of a GLCW banner) SAVAGE: In just a few days, "The future" will be "The PAST." It's a fact, Bret Irvine, that I will.....WILL.....be the victor when we meet in the ring. Now from what I've seen, you have a hard time with that fact.....
(Takes full water bottle and smashes it over his head. Blood and water drip down as he continues)
SAVAGE: See, Irvine, I don't have a problem sweating, bleeding, or playing dirty. VERY DIRTY. I'm gonna give you the fight of your life, and in the end, MY hand will be raised as the victor. Get used to it because it's a fact. I AM THE FUTURE!!!!!
(ftb)
