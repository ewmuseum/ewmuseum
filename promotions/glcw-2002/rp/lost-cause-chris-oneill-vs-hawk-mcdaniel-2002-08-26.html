"Lost Cause" Chris O'Neill vs. Hawk McDaniel

TBirdSCIL — 26 August 2002 All RP for this match goes in this folder... KING — 29 August 2002 FadeIn: A black screen with four small white squares, in each coner. The squares slowly moves into the middle and expands and gets framed by the black. Its a still shot of Chris O'Neill hitting Nick Kurtel with the 450, the picture freezes for five seconds, then it breaks into four again and returns to the corners. The four other squares turn up, and come to the middle, and there is the red headed, shirtless, white bandanna wearing, Chris O'Neill standing with his arms folded, rolling his neck.
V/O:Who is Chris O'Neill? O'Neill:Chris O'Neill is not the punk that has graced the RING in GLCW. He is the focused, and abusive man that you saw in the locker room, getting to know Hawk McDaniel, and Mr. McMillian
V/O:Who is Chris O'Neill? O'Neill: Chris O'Neill is about to become the biggest menace in wrestling! Chris O'Neill is a guy, with a major upside, and a sick mind!
V/O:Who is Chris O'Neill? O'Neill: Chris O'Neill is not one to back down from anyone, an the guys that really annoy me...are the ones that talk alot of nonsense! And obviously Hawk McDaniel is one of those guys! And now I need to handle my business, and Hawk your my business! You bad mouthed a guy, you did'nt know. You claim I weird, hinted I was a drug addict. Big mistake, huge mistake there, BIG TEXAN!
V/O:Who is Chris O'Neill? O'Neill:Chris O'Neill will not get lost in shuffle, in now what seems to be a realitivly small pond with HUGE fish! Nevada Smith, King Krusher, Maelstrom, this list is long, an the oppertunities limited. Like I sai before I will become the menace every promotion dreads!
V/O:Who is Chris O'Neill? O'Neill: Chris O'Neill is DEVISTATION, remember that HAWK, Chris O'Neill is DEVASTATION!!!!!!!!!!
(ftb) :7 KING — 1 September 2002 Fadein: The door of a bar opens, and the camera enters. And quickly pans the bar. The bar appears to be filed with middle age people, but the loud DJ, strobe lights, an overall decore, make it seem like the bar is trying to be more hip then it truely is. Guys are prowling around the bar trying to pick up, women on the mend. And some women are teasing to get free drinks. The camera heads toward one dark coner of the bar, where three preppy dressed young men sit, they are whispering amongst themselves with there heads down. They notice the camera, and as they look up its Chris O'Neill with his red hair under a black bandanna. He nods at the guys he is sitting next to, they get up and head towards the DJ. The music goes off, and O'Neill just smiles. No one else seems to notice.
O'Neill:"Hawk, listen Hawk, this very very simple! You have bored me with your sudden silence! I am so bored, that I have lowered myself to come to a place like this to keep me entertained. But me being bored is about to change as we head towards RIPTIDE, I will have you to entertain me! Because you obviously know that I don't like people with BIG MOUTHS like I said before, and now your going to be quite! It does'nt cut it, you already have insulted me, already got under my skin!"
(O'Neill puts his watch up to his ear, and nods in agreement to it.) "Tick, Tick, Tick....TIMES UP! Hawk, I am not in a pleasent mood, between losing my first two matches, and the sight of NEVADA SMITH and MAELSTROM showing up, not mention the UNHOLY ALLIANCE! All these things have really added up to me being wound pretty tight right now, I am rigt at the point where I wanna snap! Unless I prove to this federation, that I am something worth puttng an effort into I am going on the back burner and will be forgotten about! Cause its all about who has the STROKE, and right now I am only STROKING one thing! Now don't get me wrong HAWK, I will hurt you, but I won't cripple you, I will make you remember me, and then when you get better I will leave my calling card for you again! Because when you cross me once, you cross me forever! I am goingto make THE TRAIL OF TEARS seem, like a blip on the rader screen of history, compared to what I do to you! Cause Hawk, SICK TO THE CORE, ain't just to sell t-shirts!
