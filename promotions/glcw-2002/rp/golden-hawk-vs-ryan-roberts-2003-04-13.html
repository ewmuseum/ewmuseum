GLCW — Roleplay

Golden Hawk vs. Ryan Roberts

13 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
TBirdSCIL — 13 April 2003 Please keep all RP for this match in this folder... jediPREZ — 16 April 2003 The Hawk's Arrival FADEIN: The downtown Duluth, Minnesota bus station. Greyhound Bus #237 from Austin, Texas has just arrived at the station. The doors open and a few passengers walk off before GOLDEN HAWK, recent GLCW signee strolls off with a large duffel bag. HAWK is wearing a long brown overcoat, blue jeans and black steel-toed cowboy boots. HAWK looks around for a few moments before seeing the cameraman and smiling - he walks straight to the camera and drops down his bag...
HAWK (w/ a thick Texan accent): "I wasn't expectin' no grand pickup, thanks gentlemen. Hey - is that thing on?"
(The camera nods up and down) HAWK: "Well, alright then. No time like now to introduce myself to GLCW, huh? I bet some of those boys have been waitin' to hear a word from me."
(The camera nods up and down) HAWK: "Well, let's do this. For those of you that don't know me, standin' right before your very eyes is (thumbs his chest) GOLDEN HAWK. For those of you that DO know me - you now realize that comin' to the Great Lakes area is nothin' less than a big 'n bad TEXAN just lookin' for a little trouble and a WHOLE LOTTA fun. Now, I see some familiar faces like Manson and Stephen Morgan around here - I've seen what they're all about, I've seen what they can do. But what's callin' the cows home for me is the amount of faces I ain't ever seen before. And just one of those names happens to be 'TWISTED PRODIGY' RYAN ROBERTS. Now, I've done a little research on the kid - he likes to take chances and high-risks. GOLDEN HAWK ain't one to complain about that - I like it when someone is puttin' their body on the line night in and night out. It shows they got some heart and dedication, something a few of YOU might need. And Roberts, it's all fine and dandy that you're one of the new (quotes the air) HARDCORE SENSATIONS coming up through the ranks. It's all water under the bridge that when we step into that squared circle, any move you try may end up putting the BOTH of us in the hospital. There's nothin' more that this big 'ol Texan doesn't love than a bloody, backyard-styled brawl. I grew up on 'em, I feed on 'em and I live for 'em. Now, you ain't about to see this 6'6 PLUS, TWO SEVENTY PLUS - hard like TEXAN STEEL about to leap off no light rafters. But Roberts, you better get it to your head right now - that EVERYTHIN' about (thumbs his chest) THIS TEXAN is OLD SCHOOL. You can bring all the canes from Japan, all the tables and chairs needed to seat a HAWK FAMILY PIT BARBEQUE, if that's what yer about. All I need is one of THESE (raises his left hand) followed by one of THESE (raises his right hand) and I got all I need to bloody 'ya up. And don't think I ain't been in no HARDCORE fights, boy. Just remember, before there was (quotes the air) HARDCORE - there was Barbed Wire, Cowbells, Branding Irons, Indian Strap Matches and of course MY sentimental favorite out of the BIG T...the TEXAS DEATH MATCH. TEXAS was the breedin' ground for the new style you're developin' kid...and TEXAS was the breedin' ground for GOLDEN HAWK, the OLD SCHOOL graduate that's about to give you the lesson of yer LIFE. 'Twisted Prodigy' Ryan Roberts - my name is GOLDEN HAWK and in Duluth, Minnesota...I'm takin' 'ya to school."
(FTB)
