Martial Law vs. The Spider Murphy Experience

25 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
TBirdSCIL — 25 February 2003 Please keep all RP for this match in this folder... RYoungblood — 27 February 2003 (Camera fades into a building with a wrestling ring. Ryan Youngblood and Steve Slayer are practicing against each other.. Youngblood ducks from a big boot from slayer and returns with a unbelievably powerful clothesline. He goes for the cover, 1...2..kickout. Slayer gets up and takes something out of his pocket, tries to hit RYB with it but the ref notices it and DQ's Slayer.)
RYB: What the hell are you doing? SS: Using a foreign object.. It's legal right? RYB: What are you talking about, we've been wrestling for 8 years, I'd hope you know that using that stuff is illegal.
SS: It seemed legal, maybe because the referee was so dumb that he couldn't see that object being used. GLCW needs new referees, the stupid ones we got here now couldn't detect a gun, let alone any other foreign object. Well, I mean if the Spider Murphy Experience can get a cheap-ass win off of us undetected by cheating like that, why can't we?
RYB: See, thats an easy one. We have honor, they don't. They know that they're a run-of-the-mill tag team and have to resort to cheap methods to win a match against us.
SS: Two losses, one was deserved though, they outfought us.. But, not the one against Spider Murphy Experience- we had that one in the bag. Its pretty pathetic you two need to do that kinda crap just to notch a win.
RYB: We have pride. SS: Yeah, pride is fine. But that was a win they TOOK away from us. And they'll pay dearly for that.
RYB: <laughs> The time has come. It's time to show these guys we aren't playing games. And if they pull a stunt this time around like they did last time, we're going to be prepared?
SS: What do you mean? RYB: Well, lets just say we have ourselves a backup plan. (Fade to black) Showtime — 9 March 2003 Scene opens in Spider Murphy's study. "Bollywood" Rahul Seth is reading a book on Karl Marx, Spider is watching an E! True Hollywood Story about Groucho Marx on TV.
Spider: Haha! A club that would have him as a member! Clicks TV off. Hello, fans. I'm Spider Murphy. You may know me better s TV's Spider Murphy. Today, I'm here to talk to you all about a subject very important to me. That is: WHY MARTIAL LAW SUCKS. Because they are incompetant in battle and weak in bed. They would know how to throw a ##### punch if I threw one at them! Which I will, repeatedly! When we fight! That'll teach you what from what!
Bollywood: Oy... Spider: ##### YOU! ALL YOU CAN JUST SOD OFF FOR ALL I CARE! YOU-- I... I'm sorry, now I'm just too mad to even finish a thought, so I'll just mumble incoherently for a while... hmm mm bmmm nuh... uhm... boh luhhh... buh. Jeez, I hate you.
Fade out. [hr] I am Showtime... all who look upon me go "Hi, Showtime!"
