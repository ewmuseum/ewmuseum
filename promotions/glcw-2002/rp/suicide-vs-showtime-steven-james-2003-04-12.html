GLCW — Roleplay

Suicide vs. "Showtime" Steven James

12 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
TBirdSCIL — 12 April 2003 Please keep all RP for this match in this folder... scwhat82 — 14 April 2003 Fade in to Showtime Steven James playing the card game Gin with Lady Veronica. LV: So Stephen, how do you feel about your match with Suicide? SJ: I was wondering when the interview would start. LV: laughing Hey, I've gotta do my job just like anybody else. SJ: Good point, well, in that case I feel like beating somebody up to be honest with you. And suicide can say this, and he can say that, but the bottom line is that i'm looking to kick someone's teeth in and if it happens to be his then fine.
LV: How do you feel about your loss in that gauntlet match for a shot at the tv title. SJ: I lost my concentration for a second, nothing more, nothing less. A mistake that I will not make again, and will definitely not make against Suicide.
LV: For your sake I hope you don't. SJ: i won't...but you know what? I'm gonna end this promo short because our beers are getting low and we need a refill. So, we'll finish this another time so for the time being we can drink and play cards.
LV: Sounds good to me. Suicide — 20 April 2003 (Camera fades in to a brick wall with the GLCW backdrop over it. Standing in the foreground is Suicide, looking calm and yet, nonchalant. He takes a puff of a cigar in his hand as he looks away from the camera.)
Suicide: "You know....I thought that this sport was taken seriously. I thought that maybe every wrestler in the GLCW can agree on one thing....and that was no matter how different we view life and such, we all know that this is a sport to be taken seriously. But apparently my fine fellow wrestler Mr. James doesn't really care much for it. Mr. James could care less about this sport so much in fact that he completely ignores who he is up against. I understand I'm nothing much, nothing but trash, but at least pay attention to someone who could very well hand you your ass on a silver platter. Mr. James, I'm not here to brag about what I've done in my career nor relive past memories. I'm seriously dedicated to giving back to this great sport of ours what it's given me. But if all you're going to do is take from the free change tray and not give back, then you're a glutton for punishment. So much in fact that since no one's taking me seriously around here, well.....I'm going to have to make an example out of you Mr. James. It's not going to be pretty, but when it comes to be 'showtime', that's your cue to either fight me like a man or get the hell out of my way. Trust me when I say all of this.....I seriously mean it....'Nuff said...."
(As Suicide walks off screen, the camera fades out.) scwhat82 — 22 April 2003 Fade in to Showtime Steven James standing in the middle of the ring. There are crew members setting everything up for the show. He looks around the empty arena, looks at the camera and begins.
Suicide, you better watch your G*d Damned Tongue son, don't you EVER question my passion for this business. Every Damn match i come in here like everybody else who has a love for this business, and i give it my ALL! I GIVE IT MY SWEAT! MY BLOOD! MY BODY! MY LIFE! MY EVERYTHING! AND YOU GO AHEAD AND QUESTION MY DESIRE?!
takes a few minutes to calm down, then begins again You know i am what you see, i am a guy who's mostly laid back, i like to have a few beers, i like the company of women, and i enjoy the beach and the weather that comes with it. And it takes A LOT to get me angry, but you, have succeeded. Now don't worry, i'm not talking about you getting underneath my skin to the point where i'm going to be acting on blind emotion. You see what you have done is put blood in the water with a great white shark swimming near by, what you have done, is sealed your fate. The match is over, the winner is known, you have questioned the wrong person's desire. So get ready Suicide, because when it's Showtime, it'll be time for you to learn your next lesson, which is be careful who you piss off, because you may just get your ass kicked.
Suicide — 22 April 2003 [updated:LAST EDITED ON Apr-22-03 AT 09:59&nbsp;PM (EST)](Camera fades in to the Entertainment and Convocation Center in Duluth, Minnesota at night. Standing in front of it is none other than Suicide, as the wind blows his long black hair, sort of covering his face. Only one eye can be made seen as he looks at the camera.)
Suicide: "I'm a complete (FCC)hole. I know I am, people tell me this everyday and it's something I have accepted in life. I think it's because of my blunt honesty, how I present things in a truthful matter. I don't sugarcoat anything I say so I don't hurt anyone's feelings. It seems to me a lot of people in GLCW get touchy when you speak the truth to them because no one has the balls to tell them to their face how it is."
(Suicide takes off the Fedora on his head, which allows his long black hair to flow more freely in the wind.)
Suicide: "With that being said Mr. James, let me make one thing clear. Everything I have said is how I feel and I will not apologize nor take back anything. For someone who claims it takes a lot to get them angry, I hardly lifted a finger and got the type of reaction I was expecting to get. I think a lot of wrestlers here are a bit to relaxed for their own good. No one's questioned their integrity in this sport but when some lowlife bastard like myself does it, everyone's on the defense. Mr. James, I'm sure you love this sport very much. But it's not the fact that you don't take this sport seriously for what it's worth, but also the fact you don't take me seriously."
(Suicide brushes the hair from his face, revealing an eyepatch over his right eye with his symbol emblazed on it. He has a slight smirk on his face as he does this.)
Suicide: "I've had many people warn me not to piss them off, because I will pay for it. So what am I paying for Mr. James? So I pissed you off, big deal. And now you want to kick my ass. Do I really have to be afraid now? You take that anger and that desire you have for this sport, channel it, harness it, and direct it right towards me. I want to experience everything you have. I want to know exactly what Mr. Steven James stands for. If you fail when we face each other on Wired, consider yourself made an example of. The example you will represent is what everyone in the GLCW stands for. Hardly anyone cares about what this sport means. They just want to fame, fortune, championship titles. They don't care how much hard work and dedication it takes to achieve that. What you're looking for Mr. James is something that is empty and that will disappear once you leave this world. I'm looking for something of higher meaning and believe you and me, I'll take that beyond my grave. Because when it's all said and done, win or lose, you'll know what I represent and if you give me your all, I'll shake your hand in respect. But make no mistake about it, I don't fear you. I just hope in due time you'll come to understand me; not just as someone who told you like it is to your face, but someone who's simply known as The Man....The Myth....The Legend....Suicide. Fall out of line with me and 'showtime' will simply be changed to.....'show's over'.....'Nuff said...."
(Suicide places the Fedora back on his head and walks off into the night. The camera gets a nice picture of the Center where Wired will be held before fading out.)
