GLCW — Roleplay

the lights are shining ...

22 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
invisible — 22 September 2002 [updated:LAST EDITED ON Sep-22-02 AT 00:48&nbsp;AM (EDT)] ((FADEIN: On a sunny day where kids are running in and out of houses, in the streets, and with their friends. CUTTO: A side view of the bottom half of a man walking. We cannot see his face, but only the bottom half of his walking body.))
MAN: "It's been a time since I last wrestled professionally. Almost seven months now. And yet everywhere I look in the GLCW I see people who I cannot wait to jump back into things with. I see 'the Eradicator' Troy Martinez. I see Jean Rabesque. I see the Pandorian sobster, Maelstrom. There is so much here in the GLCW that I'm surprised it took me this long to finally open up my eyes and play."
((As soon as he says "play" a little boy stops and stares at him. The figure stops in front of the little boy, whose mouth has dropped open.))
BOY: "Hey! Aren't you that S..." ((As the little boy was talking the figure puts his hand over the mouth. Using the other hand he shakes his finger at the boy.))
MAN: "Didn't your mother ever tell you not to tell someone's secret? Good things come to those who wait, my little friend. And the GLCW will have to wait a little bit longer before they get the full experience."
((The little boy runs off, looking over his right shoulder back at the figure who has turned 180 degrees to watch the kid run away.))
MAN: "The lights are shining bright on Great Lakes Championship Wrestling. Yet the beacon of light, that which outshines all others, has yet to appear. But soon, (pauses) very soon, GLCW will be blessed ... just remember though, good things (pauses) happen to those who wait."
((FADEOUT: as the figure starts walking away. The camera slowly fades to black just as the man's upper body / head starts to become visible.))
