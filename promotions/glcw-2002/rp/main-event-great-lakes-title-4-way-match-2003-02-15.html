I figured this day would come. I really wasn’t sure what else I had to do. Months after months, match after match, and still Jean Rabesque was not granted a single title shot. And even know, I can’t get the one on one match the world deserves to see.
Maelstrom, you know exactly what I’m talking about. There’s lots of crap going on right now in the GLCW, and you’re currently the closest thing I have to an ally.
