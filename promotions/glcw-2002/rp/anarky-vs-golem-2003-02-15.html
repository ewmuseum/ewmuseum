Anarky vs. Golem

15 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

JLevinson — 15 February 2003 [updated:LAST EDITED ON Feb-15-03 AT 04:44&nbsp;PM (EST)](FADEIN to ANARKY'S locker room after the house show in Hammind, Indiana. The room is stripped of most common luxuries, and ANARKY sits in a folding chair, staring at the chipped facepaint in the mirror. His heavy breathing comes to a slow as he notices the camera and nods.)
ANARKY: "I find it most unfortunate that Mr. Malec feels it so necessary for me to... go through the ranks, so to speak. Not to take anything away from my darling opponent tonight, Showtime, but let's face the facts: everyone outside of Showtime's own simple mind knew how that would end. Just like everyone knows that Rychard Isaac's fate lies in my terrible hands now. Just like everyone knows, Golem, that YOU... are once again... in my way.
"Yes, Golem, it is most unfortunate for your own career that you are constantly pitted against me. Men such as Maelstrom and Jean Rabesque and Michael Manson go prancing around, praising their own idiocy as if it was something to flaunt, while you... and I... have already danced several times. And I don't think I need to play this tune for you to remember how it goes, Golem. It's something like one, two, three strikes you're out. And you're on your last pitch.
"Golem, I have no doubts in my mind of what you're capable of. Surely, inside of you is something that resembles the inside of me. Something both afraid and terrifying. Something beautiful and disgusting. Something merciful and merciless. Something that drives through all of the streams of bullshit into the unknown truths: that the only things that matter are those that take place in that ring. For the moments we have away from the hypocrisy and judgment that is always thrust upon us. But you are but a pale shadow of me... a well of untapped resources that may or may not ever show themselves.
"For you, my cursed friend, the third time won't be the charm. You will come down to the ring and sacrifice yourself as you always have, AS YOU WERE BORN TO DO, and when it's over, the only thing they'll remember you as... is the first one to put up a fight.
"Until we meet, I will bide my time, and wait patiently for your most eloquent responses. And when you have made them, as you always do, with your dark poetry, and you come to the ring, looking like you do, scaring the little kids like only you can... well, you know the routine, right?
"I'm still waiting for you to show me something." (FADEOUT.) JLevinson — 20 February 2003 [updated:LAST EDITED ON Feb-20-03 AT 12:45&nbsp;PM (EST)](FADEIN to the inside of an exquisite church. Enormous stained-glass windows depicting various religious figures stretch up towards the cathedral ceiling, which has a stirring picture of angels watching from above. The camera pans around the empty church, and standing in the middle of the red carpted aisle is ANARKY, standing with his back to the camera, his black leather jacket and scrawled white anarchy symbol barely visible in the darkness.)
ANARKY: "They say that patience is a virtue. They say that good things come to those who wait. I don't know who the hell they are, Golem, but quite frankly, you're beginning to bother me. And I would think, that of EVERYONE HERE, that YOU, above all else, would know... that bothering me is never a smart idea."
(He walks forward to the front of the church, kneeling down at the altar and lowering his head.)
ANARKY: "I am not a religious man, Golem. But quite frankly, you've taken away all of my options. I have waited. I have watched. I have taken this all in with an impressive amount of patience. But my PATIENCE IS WEARING THIN, Golem. I thought that maybe, just MAYBE, you might take it all seriously this time. I thought that being carried away by EMT's with your neck practically broken would've made you realize that you CANNOT simply brush me aside.
"But you REFUSE TO LEARN, Golem. Even the lessons of PAIN are far too complex for you to understand. You and your claw... and your idiotic appearance... and the way that you preach on about things that people like Trent Reznor and Maynard James Keenan would have trouble understanding. No, Golem, for far too long, you have wasted your potential. Worse... you have wasted my time.
"All I ever asked, Golem, was for some competition. All I ever WANTED... was someone to come to me... and FIGHT LIKE A MAN. But Golem, time and time again, all I have met with is disappointment. Empty promises made about what people would do to me.
"So I came here. Not to wash myself clean of these sins... or to pray to a God that doesn't exist... or to be struck by the beauty of art. I came here, Golem, to REMIND MYSELF... of all of the BROKEN PROMISES. Of all of the EMPTY LIES spewed forth by every damned soul in the GLCW, and every other place I've ever had the pleasure of overrunning.
"For you, Golem, I am going to do something... most exquisite. I am going to make an example of you. I am going to show the entire world... what happens to pretenders. I'm going to put on a show... for every idiot fan sitting there, their bloodlust flowing wildly... and I will give them what they want. I will give them the destruction of Golem. And when it's done, you'll still be an empty shell of a man. You'll still be a pathetic nobody who can't live up to his potential.
"And maybe, just maybe, I can feel a little bit more alive. Maybe your pain can wake me from my slumber. But I doubt it, Golem. I need something more now. I need someone who can inflict pain, as well as take it. And you are no one to be fulfilling such needs."
(He begins to tremble, clasping his hands together, a sight that would normally look like praying, but instead, has the look of lunacy as his whole body shakes violently.)
ANARKY: "Please... make... it... stop... " (FADEOUT.) Vertigo — 21 February 2003 (Cue Up: "Hurt" by Nine Inch Nails. Fade into a gray snapshot of Anarky dropping Golem over the top rope. It is frozen right where Golem's neck impacted the ring apron. The camera zooms out and shows that it is a photograph in Golem's clawed grasp.)
Golem: It's a painful memory. Or at least it would be, if it weren't buried beneath a million other more painful ones. Anarky, I have heard stories a million times over that strike me very similar to the one you are currently pushing. Big, mean, loves to fight. Same old, same old. Of course, 'Nark, you've had the uncanny ability to deliver on such promises where others aren't as capable. But this match isn't about your capability. It's about your culpability. It's time that body pay the ransom for what you bestowed upon my body. I am not going to lie to you, I don't get up any single morning without the tinge of what you did to my neck. Hell, I am not sure if I will ever be the wrestler I was before it. But, I am a survivor. I have persevered throughout all kinds of things. That was but one more example. You come here and you cry to me about broken promises. You want to hear about broken promises, Anarky? Broken promises is what my good friend and yours Maelstrom has given over and over again. It ended with cutting my ties with him and Rabesque, but that is the last gift I will give you. I left them to you and Manson and the UA to teach them a lesson. A lesson they will hopefully experience many times over. But I am not them, Anarky. I do not stumble mindlessly through these things with my humble hands over my eyes. I see where others are simply afraid to look. I recall the past, embrace the present, and singlehandedly shape my own future and yours, 'Nark. You say that I am incapable of inflicting pain on you? Oh, tsk tsk. Don't let the past be anything more than a memory. It's a painful one for me, to be true, but don't trick yourself into believing that I have nothing to offer you. More men have left this business at my hands than yours, Anarky. You may be a perennial around here, but age has worn thin your ability. Me? A shell of a man? Pot...kettle...black. (Golem chuckles to himself) You can hurt me. I am not some untouchable force. But you couldn't finish the job then and you aren't going to be able to do it now. I have heard a million men tell me they are going to make an example of Golem. None of them have been able to. Golem will not brush you aside, Anarky. Golem shall embrace the battle, the hunt, the kill. Golem shall savor the end as much as the beginning, and all middles in between. The dance, you see, is the flow and sway of these things. Are we but puppets, Anarky? Or are we the puppetmasters? I know that one of us are...but I will allow time to reveal who that man is. You want a fight? Oh, I'll give you a fight. I'm give you a damn good fight. You want someone to make it stop? Oh, Golem shall make it stop. The Claw will make everyone stop.
(Golem cracks his knuckles as the camera fades to black.) JLevinson — 23 February 2003 (FADEIN to a darkened hotel room. Anarky sits in a chair, staring out the massive windows at the bright full moon, which is the room's only source of light, reflecting off of the luxurious silver candlesticks and various decorum. He sighs slowly before speaking.)
ANARKY: "Golem... what is it that they say? You can lead a horse to water, but you can't make him realize that he's just a dumb <BLEEP>ing horse? That kinda reminds me of you, Golem. You've got all the pieces to the puzzle, but you just can't make any sense of it. You sit here and you tell me that I have had the UNCANNY ability to do what others have only dreamed of. That I have forever effected your wrestler career with one fell swoop. That you GAVE ME Maelstrom to do with as I please.
"Golem, I know that many of us harbor delusions of grandeur, but I think that you need a slight dose of reality. You want vengeance, Golem? I don't blame you. You lost a lot of ring time for that injury. But it doesn't work like that, hombre. Just because you want something really bad doesn't mean you can do anything about it. Just because you want retribution from me doesn't mean you have the ABILITY TO DO SO, Golem. In FACT, I'm having trouble figuring out where in the hell you get the idea that you COULD, in fact, beat me. Was it the way I manhandled you in the ring? Or, let me guess: it's all of the careers you've ended. All of those huge names, like who? Troy Martinez? Give me a break, Golem. Yeah, you've managed to injure a few nobodies and even manage an impressive win once or twice a year. But what in God's name makes you think you can do the same to me? DO I LOOK LIKE TROY MARTINEZ, GOLEM?!
"No, Golem, I'm not like them. They were little fish in a big pond. And I'm the <BLEEP>ing great white around here, Golem, and despite what you might think, I don't NEED you to believe that. I don't care if you sit home at night jacking off onto pictures of my bloodied and worn body. If that's what gets you to sleep at night, Golem, then you can believe whatever the hell you want. You think my career has worn thin my abilities? Well people used to tell me the same thing, Golem: FIVE <BLEEP>ING YEARS AGO. And you know what? They were wrong. So that means that you just might be wrong, too. Did I look like I didn't know what I was doing against Steven James? Or Rychard Isaac? Or YOU, just a year or two ago? You think I've lost my abilities since then? Tell me, Golem: what do you think is worse... taking a very serious stance and realizing that I AM the MOST DANGEROUS MAN IN THE WORLD... and training for that... OR... believing that in one short year I've lost all of my abiliites and will succumb to that Claw of yours? I don't need to hold your hand and walk you through this, Golem. I don't have time to play games. I've got plans and you aren't in them.
"Call me what you want, Golem. Tell me that I'm old and decrepid. Tell me that just because I whipped you from pillar to post last time doesn't mean I will again. I already know that, Golem. You see, unlike you, who CLAIMS to acknowledge the past, I have a VERY real idea of where I've come from and where I'm going. And like I have said before... you are very much in my way. I never said I was going to end your career, Golem. I'm not going to kill you. I may not even permanently injure you. After all, I've already done that, and you obviously didn't learn a damned thing. I said I was going to make an example of you -- JUST LIKE LAST TIME -- and I intend to keep that promise.
"Chaos reigns supreme, Golem... and all... will fall before me." (FADEOUT.)
