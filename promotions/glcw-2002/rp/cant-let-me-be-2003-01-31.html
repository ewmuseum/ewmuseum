Can't Let Me Be?

31 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[name withheld] — 31 January 2003 [updated:LAST EDITED ON Jan-31-03 AT 11:20&nbsp;AM (EST)]<FADEIN: Camera opens up to a still shot of an empty office room as the sun beems into the room. You see papers sitting on the table along with a few pens as if a contract was signed. All of the sudden somebody begins to speak as the camera still rolls the empty office>
...All I wanted to do is retire man. I just wanted to pick up my bags and go home. I had nothing more to prove. I've done it all and seen it all. I wanted to retire and play golf in my spare time. But then a problem occured. People kept coming up to me and share stories on how they used to dress like me, act like me, and wrestle like me. Little kids came up to me and asked for my autograph. After everything I did to the business. I lied, I cheated, I quit, and most of all I turned on my fans. I told them I did it for the money. I told them I did it for life style. I went through drug addiction, I thought for sure would ruin my career. We went through everything man. Three-Time WWL World Heavyweight Champion, Two-Time WWL Television Champion, WWL Tri-American Champion. Quit wrestling so many times that nobody wanted to sign me again. All of the sudden this beautiful thing that was called 'RAGE 'o FIRE' started to rumble again. Fans are forgiving, and they've asked for me back. I'm back alright.....what's OLD is NEW again. We will re-write the pages of 'RAGE 'o FIRE' in 2003. RAGE says it shall be written, IT SHALL BE DONE. Just maybe they guy named 'RAGE 'o FIRE' can turn everything around. Turn the bad into the good once again. Once again be the heavyweight champion of the world. I don't care about the money anymore. This time I do it for the sheer thrill of entertainment and the sport of wrestling. This might be my only shot to get back where I belong and I'm gonna make it my best. It's either DO or DIE dude. Right now I have alot of things to fix and one of them is my fans. I need to show them that I can be trusted again. That RAGE will live forever! I must start at the very bottom and work to the top of the ladder. Just maybe, JUST MAYBE.........'RAGE 'o FIRE' Jared Wells......can become the hero he used to be.........just maybe.....
<camera finally moves over to the voice in the background and you see 'RAGE 'o FIRE' JARED WELLS walking away>
