Tact looks around for a bit, then faces front again. Tact: Hm...wonder what the price is on this. Never a damn worker here when you need one. Eh..I'll get them later. Speaking of empty threats, though, it brings to mind when you brought up a question to me, Jared. To answer that question, no, I don't know how it is for 90 percent of my body to be burned. But I know some guy who can help you with that. He wears this stupid looking mask, and thinks he's all 'hip' now, and that he can talk with any amount of entertainment....sort of like you. Except you've probably accepted that you aren't very amusing...at least one of you, up in there, has had to by now! This guy, though, he got burned, and now he's just feeding off of what he once thought was this huge disaster. He's a wrestling 'superstar' now. It's funny how people have the stupidest reasons for getting into wrestling...."I have most of my body burned, I must avenge this...and then start a wrestling career!" I mean, it's ridiculous! And then there are all the hyped up 'legends' of our time...but I'll get into that somewhere down the line.
Tact looks over the television again, nodding his head approvingly. He mutters a few things before walking away from the television, the camera following and showing a side profile of Tact.
Tact: Make all the small talk about what the braintrust of Jared Justice has concluded on the issue of Jean Rabesque and myself, but know that it doesn't mean a thing. Whether anyone thinks highly or lowly of me, it doesn't mean a thing. Because I don't believe any of it. All I believe, is that people will see me person, and sooner or later, they will not be able to deny my superior ability. And after what I've gone through, there's no more satisfying way I can think of for everyone to be enlightened to that blissful fact. Jared Justice, you will find this out very shortly, and then...you too, will be tactilized.
Tact reaches the back of the store, where there is an empty customer service counter. Tact mutters some more things, and then smacks the counter a few times.
Tact: A LITTLE HELP HERE? Geez... An employee comes out from the back area, a hot dog and cheese fries in his hands. Tact's face drops like a weight, to disgust. Fade out.
