GLCW — Roleplay

Jon Savage vs. "Stupendous" Stephen Morgan

30 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
TBirdSCIL — 30 December 2002 Please keep all RP for this match in this folder... SouthernBoy — 7 January 2003 {{...FADE-IN: "Stupendous" Stephen Morgan is sitting behind a large, oak desk dressed in Khakis, and a polo shirt. Morgan quickly flips through pages bound together to look like a script. He finally gets to the end and looks up with an almost disgusted look....}}
STEPHEN MORGAN: " If I told him ONCE, I've told him a THOUSAND times...ACTION roles...ACTION. Listen Tarantino, if you're watching...and I know you are...if these scripts don't get better, you can LOOSE my number. Got it? "
" Now, on to other things. {{...Morgan tosses the "script" in the trash...}} And that's my upcoming MAIN EVENT match with Jon Savage. Now Savage, I realize the GLCW made a mistake and put this match at the bottom of the card, but don't worry...I'm going to carry your sorry ASS to a WORLD CLASS wrestling event that will have everybody from Spielberg to Ron Howard talking about my acting ability. "
" Yes, I said acting because for me to make this look like it's even going to be CLOSE will require a skill not many have. I'm willing, for the good of the company, to make you look good Savage. I'm not willing to just SQUASH you and send the peons running for there large buttered popcorn and diet coke. The GLCW NEEDS me to KEEP the fat asses in there seats and talking about just what Stephen Morgan is going to do NEXT. "
" You see Savage, if you think about it, you're actually QUITE lucky. YOU get to look good without doing much. I mean sure, you're going to have to take a beating...but your wounds will heal...eventually. But look at it this way, when you are ready to step back into the ring around Christmas...you can hold THIS match up and say look, look what I did against "STUPENDOUS" Stephen Morgan...which by that time, no doubt, will have been the GLCW World Champion for months. "
" Yes, you're quite a lucky man Savage. I don't envy you...but I'm sure most of the other pieces of excrement in the locker room do. So shine up your pretty wrestling boots, get a haircut and shave all that excess body hair. SMILE BIG for the cameras...you're about to be made FAMOUS. "
{{...The phone rings and Morgan picks it up...}} " Morgan ...... Listen Cameron, I said NO. Titanic part TWO can't work! Don't you get it...the SHIP SANK! "
{{...Morgan SLAMS down the phone and gets up quickly from his desk tripping on the phone card and almost spilling the bottle of water on his desk...}}
" God da...this...piece....TURN THE CAMERA OFF " {{...The camera shakes from side to side signaling that the camera man has no intention of stopping. Morgan, fuming comes toward the camera and puts his hand in the lens as we CUT TO BLACK..}}
SouthernBoy — 10 January 2003 {{...FADE-IN: The eloquently decorated living room of "Stupendous" Stephen Morgan. Morgan is running on his treadmill decked out in a purple sweat suit and white headband....}}
"STUPENDOUS" STEPHEN MORGAN: " OK Jack, give it to me. " "VOICE FROM SPEAKER PHONE": " Well Stephen, I talked to Malec, and well, you're match will NOT be the main event...in fact, it's not even going to be televised. "
SM: "WHAT!" {{...The force of the yell causes him to almost fall off the treadmill. He barely catches himself on the handles, then pulls himself up and shuts off the machine....}}
SM: " Listen Jack, you get that no account Malec on the phone NOW and tell him that I didn't sign a contract to be wrestling DARK matches. If he wants to make MONEY, he better get Stephen Morgan on TEE VEE, otherwise, he may be seeing me on the OTHER channel in ANOTHER promotion.
JACK: " I've told him that Stephen, but he thinks Mike Manson will be a better draw and he wants to make sure you can deliver the goods you SAY you can deliver. "
SM: " Is he serious? Does he WATCH wrestling outside of his own rinky dink promotion? Does he KNOW that EYE, "STUPENDOUS" Stephen Morgan, am UNDEFEATED in a wrestling ring? Does he know that there are directors and producers knocking down my door to try to get me into THIER movie. You know WHY they do that JACK? Because STEPHEN MORGAN SELLS! That's right Jack, you call Malec back and you tell him, STEPHEN MORGAN SELLS.
JACK: " OK Stephen I'll tell him, but I don't think it's going to make any difference. " SM: " Call me back when you're done. " JACK: " Later. " {{...Morgan pushes the hang-up button on the phone and walks over to the table and grabs a bottle of water. After taking a long sip, he sits down on the table and begins to rub his temples...}}
SM: " Ok Malec...I think I get it. You want to try and deflate my ego somewhat, right? You send these cameras down here, and I think it's to film the announcement of MY MATCH with Jon Savage as the NEW Main event for the show. But instead, you catch me off guard, you TRY to humiliate me by not even putting my match on TV. Well you made a BIG mistake Malec, because NOW I'm going to have to do something to get by dignity back. I'm going to send this JON SAVAGE into an early retirement. You messed with me, so NOW I'm going to send home one of your so called "talents". Just remember as you watch me DESTROY this peon that YOU brought it on him...and if you don't want it to happen to your "draws" like Manson and Maelstrom, then you better get it through your THICK head that STEPHEN MORGAN does not respond well to DIS-RESPECT. "
" As far as you go Savage, you can thank Malec personally for what I'm about to do to you. You were going to get off easy, you were GOING to be able to survive...now, I'm going to beat you within an INCH of your very LIFE...that is, IF You even decide to show your face. "
" GLCW, you may think Stephen Morgan is some unknown, undercard PEON, but in reality I am your WORST NIGHTMARE. Sleep well. "
{{...FADE OUT...}}
