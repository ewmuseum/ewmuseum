Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

WAR! Wrestling introduced itself on 28 April 2004 with a modest promise: “a fantasy wrestling league that's all about having fun and being creative... sure there is a competitive edge to it but above all else we are here to have a good time.” It billed itself as touring the United States behind a weekly Monday night show, Mandatory Mayhem, hosted by Ric Michaels and Chris Adonis, with a pay-per-view planned roughly every 45 days.

Its founding FAQ staged president and owner Brian Tomlin as an experienced wrestling executive finally given the chance to run a promotion of his own, backed by an interviewer, Stacy, and ring announcer Matt Wetterson. Four championships were on offer from the start — a World Championship, a United States Championship, a Television Title for newcomers on the way up, and a World Tag Team Championship — and the promotion's own application called for “original and realistic characters only.”

No further document survives. Whether Mandatory Mayhem ever aired, or any of its four championships were contested, isn't in the surviving record.
