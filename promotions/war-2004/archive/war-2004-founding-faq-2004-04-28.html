[WAR!] Founding FAQ

28 April 2004
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[WAR!] Wrestling is a fantasy wrestling league that's all about having fun and being creative. I mean sure there is a competitive edge to it but above all else we are here to have a good time. THE FED [WAR!] Wrestling tours the United States broadcasting a weekly Monday night show called "Mandatory Mayhem" which is hosted by Ric Michaels and Chris Adonis. [WAR!] will also host a major Pay Per View event every 45 days or so, the names and locations of these events vary. There are three singles titles and 1 tag title that the wrestlers of [WAR!] will be competing for... [WAR!] World Championship - The MAIN title in [WAR!]. For a wrestler to hold this title, he will have to be the best of the best. [WAR!] United States Championship - While this is the secondary singles title, it still takes a talented and determined wrestler to strap this belt around his waist. [WAR!] Television Title - This title is a popular title amongst newcomers and those in the wings for bigger and better title shots. But don't get it twisted, to hold onto this title will take hard work and determination. [WAR!] World Tag Team Titles - If two wrestlers can work together well enough, they can become the Tag Team Champions of [WAR!]! The Personnel PRESIDENT/OWNER: Brian Tomlin - An experienced man in the wrestling business, having had executive positions in various independent promotions. He has aspired to own and operate his own wrestling promotion and has finally been given the opportunity. PLAY BY PLAY ANNOUNCER: Ric Michaels - A virtual rookie in the wrestling announcing game. A former host of a late night wrestling radio talk show, Ric is the extremely knowledgeable. His straight laced demeanor often makes him an easy target for his broadcast partner, Chris Adonis. COLOR COMMENTATOR: Chris Adonis - A former wrestler, Adonis brings experience and charisma to the [WAR!] announce team. INTERVIEW: Stacy - Stacy has been in the business for a long time and is known to do what it takes to get a scoop. She is the woman who gets all the answers from the wrestlers of [WAR!], be it in the ring or backstage in the dressing room. RING ANNOUNCER: Matt Wetterson - A veteran ring announcer Wetterson is the friendly and familiar voice of [WAR!] who introduces each wrestler as they make their way to the ring. The Rules Participation in [WAR!] is pretty relaxed. Basically, when you have a match ... role-play! Role-plays don't need to be expositions of great length. All we ask is that you take your time and let the creative juices flow. I'm sure we can all agree that we'd prefer and shorter entertaining RP to a rambling, incoherent 10 page diatribe. You are, of course, free to role-play even if you don't have a match that week. Which brings us to the other point when it comes to role-playing ... if you have a match and you don't submit a RP - well, let's face it, the odds are pretty damn good you are going to lose that match. Finally, the only other thing to talk about is this... remember, this is just a game. If you have a concern about how your wrestler was portrayed, or how a match unfolded, please, just email us and we can discuss it. Pretty much any problem can be worked out via a dialogue. Immature or inappropriate behavior will not be tolerated. We're all in this to have fun, let's keep it that way.
