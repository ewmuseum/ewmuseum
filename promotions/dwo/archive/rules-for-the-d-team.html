Rules for the D-Team

2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Rules for the D-Team At the posting of each card a group will be assigned to handle the results for that week. We have finally gotten the help that we need so I will be expecting you all to act responsible when your group is assigned for results. Members of each group are responsible for making the show the best it can be. I will not tolerate slackers or arguers inside of a group. If you slack off on your duties without a legitimate reason then you better be prepared to be publicly suspended for a term of two weeks, during this time if you have a title you will be striped of it for reasons of not performing your duties to the staff in which you signed up for. Members from each groups are allowed to work together if they wish. An assigned group may seek help from the group that is not assigned only if they seriously need to. If you cannot work, or do your matches, or complete them before the deadline then you better tell us about it on the Developmental forum before Thursday or you are looking at a suspension. I will be making an account on Hotmail strictly for D-Team members to use. I will be forwarding all applications that come in to that address so when you need information you will have it at your disposal. Also, I will be changing the request forums address to the hotmail account for the D-Team so that when someone sends in a strategy , in-card, or interview you will have it immediately when you need it The members of the group that have no assignments for DWO shows that week will be in charge of posting news on Primetime Central in favor of the DWO. This news would include recaps from the show, and any news and rumors resulting from that show. I will be handling the regular news posting dealing with federation activity, but the show information is solely that groups responsibility. If you want to write up a column for the DWO then that would be great, an entire group could even get together and work something out that way. It is all up to you guys on anything extra you come up with, BUT remember.. You best get your assigned work done before the extra or suspension will result. Lastly.. All assignments that are complete must be posted and titled with the name of the match under the assignment post made by me. If not I will end up having to search for your match and that will not be fun. All assignments are to be posted Wednesday no later than 5:00 p.m. central time. I can see when you posted your matches, and I will personally add a mark to your name for each time your assignment is late. Three marks will result in suspension, any more and you are fired from the D-Team, and considering for jobbing and potential firing. This is a serious position in the DWO, take it as such.
