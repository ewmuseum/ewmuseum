DWO Superstars — Adam Stryker

2002

Out of Ring Stats Gender:Male Nickname: Mr. Submission, Master of the Submission Height: 6'5" Weight: 269 Age: 27 Birthday: 4/17/75 Hometown:Los Angelos, CA In Ring Stats Theme: When Worlds Collide by PM5k Face/Heel/Neutral: Heel, Heel Finisher: The Restraining Order Description: The Crippler Crossface Favorite Moves: Standing Side Kick, DVD, Fall Away Slam, T-Bone Suplex, Northern Lites Bomb Bio: Stryker was born Adam Lukas Dyas. He was born into a very rich family. His father was a succesful lawyer as was his grandfather. Born in LA Adam grew up in the nice neighborhoods and went to the better schools of the area. He began his wrestling career at the age of 10. He used it as a means to vent his frustrations. He would never fight anyone on the playground or after school but he would always make the kid he was wrestling feel the pain. Adam was a very good and successful wrestler. He had won numerous tournaments in the LA area and was considered one of the top wrestlers in the state upon entering HS. But his parents had other plans for him. They pulled him from the public school system and placed him in a very prestigous private school. He was crushed and he thought that his wrestling career was over before it even had the chance to begin. Adam soon got his chance as the wrestling coach saw him around the campus. Being only 100 pounds it made him very useful for the struggling team. He agreed to wrestle and was very good. He wrestled all four years he was in the school and put up a very impressive 159-2 record. He won four consecutive state championships at 103, 119, 145 and 189 while repeatingly beating charges of steriods from other wrestlers in his league on more than one occasion. When his HS career came to end Adam received a letter from the coach of the UCLA team. He was offered a full scholarship to the school and happily accepted. But before his first match for UCLA he was involved in a fight with an upper classman. Adam suffered a cut that wen down the middle of his right eye causing a scar which was removed by surgery. The other kid landed himself in the hospital. The coach siding with the upper classman kicked Adam off of the team and he was eventually expelled from the school. His dreams were crushed. This is when he met JL Knight. The two became friends and Knight convinced Adam to try his hand at professional wrestling. It was also Knight that convinced him to drop his real last name and pick up Stryker.JL got Stryker his first real break in the MEWO. But shortly after that fed closed down and Stryker was left with no where to go. He dropped Knight and decided to go it alone. He first took a tour of Japan and watched and learned from the hardcore wrestlers of the Japanese federations. He spent a couple of years there and came back here with a new drive and a new desire. His first major federation was the EHWF. He spent the better part of a year there and left to join the AWR. But while in the EHWF he became the champion by defeating the unbeatable Jaxx in a classic title match. The next day Stryker told the federation that he was jumping ship and he joined the AWR. He spent two years in the AWR and was their first World Champion after the brief closing was over. He held the US and World Titles at the same time and had been a Tag Team Champion before. The AWR made him famous because of some of the battles he had. Most known of them all is his battle with Calixto Baptista and with Kurt 'the Wolf' Keizmen. It was his performence in the Descent into Hell match at one of the last AWR PPVs that made him big. Fans loved to hate him and always knew they would get a good show from him. Since the AWR closed its doors Adam has been wrestling in smaller feds but nothing too serious and now wants for the chance to make it big again.
