The DWO Head Quarters

2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Recent Updates: None as of right now. Before your eyes stands a massive steel structure in the heart of Providence, RI. This hulking man made structure was none other than the DWO Head Quarters. The building as always was looking beautiful in the dimming light of the sun that was glinting off of the glass frames. A camera crew takes its place on the side street, moving toward the building to the two massive double glass doors. They are opened and once inside a man in a black suit meets them, greeting them. Marcus Steel- Welcome, glad to see you could make it to the showing of the DWO H.Q., As you can see we are busy today so you should get to see a lot of action. Follow me if you please.. Marcus leads the camera crew down a long corridor and to an elevator at the end. Marcus then proceeds to press the up button and step inside when the doors open. The camera crew follows inside. The doors close and the elevator begins its rise upward. Marcus Steel- Today I will be your tour guide. During this little trip you will see just what our recruits go through before they are able to say they are Elite. Some of what you will see will not be pretty, but it's a long grueling process to joining the DWO ranks. The elevator stops on floor twenty-three and the doors open to a whole new world. Inside were rings, modified rings with four feet of structured mesh and steel around them. No ropes..just wire and steel. Men were inside of these rings with trainers, most of the men were bloody and beaten raw. The voices of the trainers could be heard yelling at the men who were failing to stay on their feet inside of the training rings. Marcus steel makes his way over to one of these rings and looks inside. The camera settles on one of the trainers who is working with a bloodied and limping man. Brian McGrath- What's wrong boy can't hack it? Up on guard now! You think I'm joking son? Up now! The man gets up on his sore leg and begins sparring again. Running and rebounding the caged walls only to train himself with the needed flip and fall knowledge. The man stumbles on a second attempt and crashes to the hard mat surface. There was no tension break with the hit to the ring floor. The man tries to get up, but falls back on his face. The trainer growls and walks to the man, grabbing him up and slamming him face first in to the mesh of the ring wall. Brian McGrath- You must not want this bad enough! You think this is some game!? Get your ass out! Now! Brian, the Trainer, hooks the mans arm in an arm bar lock and sends him out of the ring by tossing him over the steel and caging of the walls. The man hits and crumples in to a heap on the concrete floor. Brian McGrath then looks to Marcus and smirks as Marcus simply guides the camera men on down the line of training rings. Inside of some of the rings were women, but unlike most Federations, these women were being trained by men in the same fashion as all the rest of the recruits. One of the women in the rings was bruised up, bloody with a broken right arm and still yet she was up on her feet doing all that her trainer was barking at her. Marcus looks to that woman and then to the camera. Marcus Steel- You see her? That is what we are looking for here in the DWO. People who will fight with broken bones, broken spirits..People who no matter what will take a beating and keep on fighting until they pass out from sheer pain. We don't take pussies in to the ranks of the DWO. Here you either make it or you don't. The camera focuses on the woman once more as Marcus Steel walks on down the line. The next thing they were to view was a large metal platform. Lined up were pairs of men and women. Each woman and man were paired with someone of the opposite sex. People in the DWO were taught early that gender makes no God Damn difference. A trainer was at the head of each line up of recruits barking commands. Inbetween each line was another trainer, grabbing up people who couldn't do right and tossing them aside. The camera catches all of the action as men and women who are paired up together train for a common goal. The head trainers yell out moves and the men and women comply, doing the best they can. Bodies slam in to and against the metal platform, bodies snap, moans and groans can be heard echoing off of the walls. One woman grunts as she tries to lift her male partner for a suplex. She can't do it. A trainer beside her pulls her over and sets her up, suplexing her backward on to the metal surface. The woman cries out from the pain but she stands and limps back over to her opponent, this time completeing the move correctly. The trainer moves on down the line. Marcus Steel- No one here in the DWO head quarters is treated differently. Here in the DWO gender, race, disabilities, make no difference. Everyone is treated the same no matter what. You either work as one machine or you walk. No inbetweens, and no easy way out. Here it is Pass or Fail. In the corner of the lens a man can be seen getting repremanded for doing a move wrong and seriously injuring his opponent inside of one of the circular structures. The trainer bashes the man over the head with a bamboo rod and the man goes down with a busted forehead. The medics crew can be seen picking the hurt man up, and the hurt opponent up and chartering them away. The trainer can be heard screaming out to them as they go. Trainer- Do NOT Bother coming back you chicken shits! Marcus laughs softly and shakes his head while walking on toward some office junctures. He enters in to one of the offices where a large table full of men around it in chairs speak among themselves. Marcus Steel leans against the door frame and the sound man with the camera crew turns up the volume so the people watching can hear the conversation. The men were obviously talking about who was to make it to the DWO and who was to be sent packing. Marcus looks to the camera and speaks in a whisper. Marcus Steel- Even though the ones being chosen today make it out of this hell they will be in for the ride of their lives inside of the true DWO ring. Trust me..You will know you are Nothing. For Now you are the lucky few getting to see just what it is like to try out for the DWO. Expect no mercy..Now get out of my damn face! Marcus Steel pushes the camera lens backward and the man holding it falls on his ass. The other two men help him up and they scurry out of the office building and back in to the sparring area. The three crew men walk along the corridor and get in to the elevator as the scene fades to black. Staff Members Marcus Steel, Brian McGrath, Jennah Peters, Laura Jefferson, Micheal Calhoun Recent Victims None as of Yet
