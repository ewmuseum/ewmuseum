Lord of the Asylum: Christmas Chaos, continued

31 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
“Nazi Halo” begins to play over the PA System as Shawna Blaze emerges from the backstage area. She ignores the boos and the catcalls from the horny men near the ramp as she continues to walk to the ring, eyes straight ahead. She rolls underneath the bottom rope, standing to turn her face back to the entranceway, crossing her arms over her chest as she looks impatient to get this match going. Mike: “Think maybe attacking Jade has upped her aggression level?” Jay: “No, I think Draven took care of that. The Jade thing just upped her attitude level.” Mike: “Good point.” Jacobs: “Introducing first, from Sidney, Australia, standing 5’8 and weighing 143lbs., SHAWNA BLAZE!” Mike: “Let’s not forget how Blaze got her, thanks to that win over Diamond in one screwed up match.” Jay: “Screwed up or not, she has a chance to get her hands on the World Title against Kaiowas later tonight if she can get past Corbin.” The lights slowly dim down to black as an eerie silence falls over the crowd. The sounds of far-off footsteps are heard for a moment -- all of a sudden an image of three blood soaked claws tears through the big screen, as the lights rush back on. 'Bad Religion' by Godsmack hits and immediately rocks the crowd as twin white pyros shoot up on either side of the stage. In the midst of the fireworks and music, Julius Corbin walks out onto the stage to the crowd, getting a rather large chorus of jeers. Corbin walks out onto the top of the ramp and looks out to the ring as he is taunting for the crowd, before solemnly walking down the ramp as red fireworks race up either side of the ramp, and back down again. Julius stops at the end of the ramp as the crowd keeps jeering, and looks both ways to the crowd before raising his arms in the air and letting out a roar. Corbin beats himself in the head to psyche himself up, before sprinting into the ring. He rolls in, and uses the ropes to pull himself to his feet. Mike: “Speak of the devil.” Jay: “I think you mean, speak of the Jesus wanna-be.” Mike: “Either way, the guy is psycho.” Jay: “This is the DWO…psycho is good.” Jacobs: “And her opponent, from Glasgow, Scotland, standing 5’11” and weighing in at 190lbs., JULIUS CORBIN!!!” The bell rings, and Shawna wastes not time, using her speed to sprint across the ring at Corbin. Corbin smiles, expecting a shoulder tackle he knows will fail, only to have Shawna slide between his legs, and lift his feet off the mat with a hellacious nut punch! The crowd ‘OHHS!’ in pain as Corbin drops to his knees, Shawna now standing behind him. Grabbing a handful of his hair, she wrenches his head back and begins pummeling him with lefts and rights across the jaw and forehead, before using his hair to slam him face first into the canvas. Shawna then straddles his back, grabbing Corbin underneath the chin and wrenching upwards into a chin lock. Corbin yells, reaching up to try and pry Shawna’s hands loose as referee Eddie Maneddi drops down in front of him, asking if he quits. Mike: “I don’t think Corbin is gonna submit to a chin lock.” Jay: “Not likely, especially with a shot at the World Title on the line!” Corbin continues to struggle, finally managing to pry Shawna’s hands away from his chin. Still grasping her hands, Corbin rolls, forcing Shawna underneath him as Corbin attempts a modified pin. Shawna gets her shoulders up before Maneddi can even begin to count, but Corbin uses his position to drive a couple of elbows into the ribs of Shawna. Finally releasing her hands, Corbin hops up to his feet, catching Shawna with an elbow to the stomach as she grabs her ribs in pain. With the wind knocked out of her, Corbin hooks her leg as Maneddi begins a count. 1… 2… Shawna kicks out, and Corbin stands again, forcing Shawna to her feet. He slings her hard into the corner, running past her as she bounces off the turnbuckles, to hit the ropes and clothesline her off her feet. Corbin then proceeds to stomp down hard on Shawna’s arms, shoulders, and face…wherever he can find an open spot to kick! Jay: “Corbin’s playing kickball with Shawna’s head!” Mike: “That’s one game I KNOW I don’t want to play!” Finally Corbin stops kicking her, reaching down to get a handful of her hair and force her back to her feet. He slams her back into the corner, then chops her hard across the chest as the crowd “OHHS!” along with the sound of slapped flesh! Again he chops her across the chest, but this time Shawna responds, reaching up to grab a handful of Corbin’s hair, sidestepping, and slamming his head into the turnbuckle! Corbin stumbles backward a bit, and Shawna leaps into the air, kicking Corbin smack in the face and knocking him to the mat! Shawna stomps down on Corbin now, returning his earlier favor, snarling as she does so. Maneddi tries to force her away, but she shoves him to the mat before continuing her stomping of Corbin! Mike: “Better watch it, she’ll get herself Dqed!” Jay: “That would suck to lose your shot at the World Title because you’re stupid!” Maneddi gets back to his feet, pushing Shawna back, who looks at him in bewilderment. Maneddi berates her and warns her that the next time she’ll be disqualified! Shawna simply nods, then reaches down to lift Corbin to his feet. Corbin slaps her arms away, kicking her hard in the gut, and then slamming her down to the mat with a flowing DDT!! Holding on to her head, he turns the move into a chokehold, forcing the air out of Shawna as she struggles to get free! Mike: “It’s the Enigma by Corbin!” Jay: “Oh…look who has been doing their homework all of a sudden!” Mike: “Oh…shut up.” Maneddi kneels beside Corbin and Shawna, asking if Shawna wants to quit. She says no, but she is fading fast from the lack of oxygen. Corbin tightens the hold, as Maneddi grabs one of Shawna’s arms and lifts it into the air. One… The arm drops limply to the mat, the crowd chanting along as Maneddi again raises the arm into the air… Two… Again the arm falls flat to the mat. Maneddi lifts the arm again and lets it go, but before Shawna’s arm can hit the mat, Corbin suddenly breaks the hold, shaking his head and smiling at Maneddi. Corbin stands and forces Shawna to her feet. He then slings her across the ring to land face first in the turnbuckle. As she leans against it, Corbin runs the length of the ring, swinging himself out between the top and middle rope as if he were performing a 619, but instead of kicking Shawna, he hooks her head with his legs, forcing Shawna to front flip over the top rope and out onto the floor! Corbin stands on the apron looking down upon Shawna as the crowd breaks out into chants of “Holy Shit! Holy Shit!”. Mike: “My sentiments exactly! That move never ceases to amaze me!” Jay: “Yeah…but what is it called, genius?” Mike: “Um…um…” Jay: “The Switchback! Ha!” Mike: “I knew that…I did!” Corbin still stands on the apron, and then leaps off, landing an elbow across Shawna’s chest! Shawna lies dazed from the elbow and the fall to the floor, as Corbin stands again and grabs her by the feet. He then catapults her head first into the steel ring post, Shawna’s skull bouncing off it with a sickening thud as the crowd “OHHS!” loudly! Shawna falls limply to the floor as Corbin stands again, smiling broadly as he picks her up and forces her into the ring. Corbin then climbs up the turnbuckle, looking down at Shawna who lies upon her stomach, raising his arms into the air to taunt the crowd! Mike: “What does he think he’s doing up there? This isn’t one of Corbin’s specialties!” Jay: “Hey, you’re allowed to try something new!” Corbin wishes he hadn’t, as his posturing has bought precious time for Shawna. As he leaps into the air, intending on a frogsplash, Shawna finds the strength the move, Corbin getting nothing but a face and gut full of mat! Corbin rolls around in pain, before a bleeding Shawna can force him to his back and hook his leg! 1… 2… Corbin kicks out roughly, tossing Shawna bodily off of him. Shawna lands on her back, blood dripping from an open wound on her head, as she tries to use the ropes to get to her feet. Just as she makes it, Corbin, who had himself gotten to his feet, charges her full speed. Shawna grabs the top rope and pulls it down, Corbin launching himself out on the cold floor outside! The crowd laughs hysterically as Shawna rolls out of the ring, stumbling and leaning against the apron, before kneeling down and reaching underneath the ring. She brings out a steel chair, and stands with it, a smirk on her face as Corbin gets to a knee. He gets no further as Shawna tees one off his head with the chair, knocking him backwards to the floor as the crowd chants “UWAHH!”!! Mike: “This isn’t a hardcore match…is it?” Jay: “It is now!” Shawna tosses the chair aside, stumbling over to Corbin to lift him to his feet. She slams his back into the ring apron for good measure as a trickle of his own blood runs off his nose, and then she rolls him into the ring, quickly following. She gets him on his back and hooks the leg as Maneddi drops for the count! 1… 2… Corbin kicks out! Just before Maneddi’s hand touched the mat, Corbin somehow manages to kick out. Shawna looks disgusted as she works her way back to her feet, walking to the closest turnbuckle and climbing to the second one. Once there, she leaps off, landing a leg drop across Corbin’s head and face before immediately rolling into another pin attempt! 1… 2… Mike: “Corbin kicks out again!” Jay: “With the World Title at stake, she’s gonna have to come up with more than that to keep Corbin down! After all, he’s on a religious mission!” Mike: “He is? I thought he was just looking for a good psycho ward.” Shawna is now back on her feet and forcing Corbin to his. She backs him up into the corner, then takes a few steps back and comes at him full steam. Corbin moves and shoves her face first into the turnbuckle, Shawna bouncing backwards from the impact before Corbin nails her in the chin with a superkick! Shawna and Corbin both fall to the mat, Maneddi standing over them for a long heartbeat, and then starting the standing 10 count! 1… 2… 3… Shawna rolls over onto her stomach, but makes no other effort to move. Corbin is working his way towards the ropes slowly as Maneddi continues to count. 4… 5… 6… Mike: “Come on! One of you two get up!” Jay: “Yeah, what he said damn it!” Corbin gets to his feet, stumbling about for a moment before spotting Shawna still flat on her stomach. Corbin walks to her, straddling her back and locking her into a Camel Clutch! He wrenches back hard on it, ignoring Shawna’s blood dripping onto his hands as she screams out in pain! Maneddi is right there, asking her if she wants to quit, as she violently says no over and over again between screams of pain! Mike: “Ad Criticos!” Jay: “Nah, just a camel clutch dude.” Mike: “That’s what Corbin calls it, Mr. Know It All.” Jay: “Oh…” Corbin finally releases the hold once he realizes she isn’t giving up, then grabs a handful of her hair and slams her face down violently into the mat! Corbin stands again, then forces Shawna to her feet once more, hooking her up and suplexing her back first to the mat! With Shawna prone, Corbin once again climbs the turnbuckle as once before. Mike: “Here he goes again!” Jay: “And for your info, he had done aerial moves before. See…you don’t study well.” Corbin reaches the top, and without hesitation leaps high into the air, this time connecting with the frog splash! Corbin rolls off Shawna, grabbing his ribs, before returning and laying atop her for the pin! 1… 2… ….KICK OUT!!! Mike: “Shawna kicked out of the Golden Dawn!!!” Jay: “How did you know the name?! Huh? You’re cheating over there!” Mike: “ Am not!” Corbin has a look of sheer surprise on his face at Shawna’s kick out, and he snarls, and then lifts her back to her feet again. He slams her back first into the turnbuckle, then climbs it and begins punching away at her already bloody head! The crowd chants along, but their counts suddenly change into a loud “UWAHH!” as Shawna low blows Corbin! Corbin grabs himself and falls backward off the turnbuckle and hard to the mat, Shawna falling face first out of it to land an inadvertent head butt into the same area! Mike: “WOW! Double low blow to Corbin!” Jay: “Yuck…my stomach hurts now!” Shawna manages to roll away from Corbin as he continues to yelp in pain, struggling to the ropes and finding her way to her feet. She stumbles over to the turnbuckle again, forcing herself upwards to the top one. She leaps off, driving an elbow into the chest of Corbin from the top rope! She simply lays an arm over his chest as Maneddi drops for another count! 1… 2… Mike: “And Corbin KICKS OUT!” Jay: “It’s gonna take one hell of a move to put these two away for good! Neither one wants to quit!” Shawna finds her feet again, lifting Corbin up and slinging him into the ropes. As Corbin comes back, she attempts a clothesline. Corbin ducks it, bouncing off the other ropes looking for one of his own. Shawna ducks his, and as Corbin spins, she kicks him hard in the gut! She grabs his head and jumps straight up, making look as if Corbin is about to suplex her. She then swings her legs back down and twists his head, slamming him down to the mat with a wicked modified neck breaker! Mike: “She calls that Convicted, and we can call this one over!” Shawna rolls atop Corbin as Maneddi drops down for the count! 1… 2… Suddenly Maneddi stops counting, patting Shawna on the back and pointing to Corbin’s foot on the ropes! Shawna snarls in anger, standing to her feet and forcing Corbin to his once more. Again, she slings him into the ropes, but Corbin reverses it! Shawna ducks the big boot attempt by Corbin, bouncing off the opposite ropes and running back towards him! But Corbin is ready for her, twirling her around in mid-air before dropping her head first to the mat with a cradle tombstone piledriver!!! Mike: “They’re in the center of the ring, and Corbin just nailed Shawna with the ATONEMENT!” Jay: “She can’t get a foot on the ropes like he did!” Maneddi drops for the count as Corbin hooks Shawna’s leg! 1… 2… …3!!! Maneddi calls for the bell as Corbin makes his way to his feet, staring down at Shawna. Maneddi walks back to him and lifts his arm in the air, but Corbin snatches it away from him, turning his eyes back to Shawna as he smiles wickedly, then rolls out of the ring. Jacobs: “Here is your winner, JULIUS CORBIN!” Mike: “Corbin will face Kaiowas later tonight to determine the new DWO World Champion!” Jay: “Give credit to Shawna, she showed a lot guts here tonight, but Corbin was just too much to handle!” Mike: “All this action, and we still have the double main events to go!” ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ Callisto vs. Lexis The electricity inside of the arena was intense. You could feel the excitement building inside of the fans, for they knew what match was about to take place.. It was time for the bloodcell match between Callisto Bane and Lexis Bane. The audience was alive with bickering, chattering, and noise of all sorts, as were the announcers, commentators and even the security guards. This match was to be brutal, and everyone knew it.. One more reason why security had been increased and the medics had been placed down by the ring instead of pending call backstage. All were in awe of the ring set up, the apron black with the DWO logo as norm, but the side banners flapping with blood, broken hearts, and the owners name along with Callisto‘s. While the excitement in the audience and around the ring grows a lone figure comes through the curtain and walks across the stage to the side of the right leg of the Mega Tron. A switch is grabbed and the person pulls down, cutting the lights off in the arena. After that a spot light hits on the curtain and from the speakers pours Bon Jovi’s "Always".. Which was quite odd considering the circumstances. Mike and Jay exchange looks as the lyrics catch everyone off guard. "And I will love you baby, ALWAYS, and I’ll be there forever and a day... ALWAYS,.. I’ll be there til the stars don’t shine, til the heavens burst and the words don’t rhyme. I know when I die, you’ll be on my mind.. ALWAYS." Mike: What the hell..? Jay: Perhaps this is Lexis on reform? After that last word of the verse is said the system goes scratchy and Madison Avenue’s "Who the Hell are you?" chimes in cutting over the love song which was playing a moment ago. "Now who the hell are you to treat me like that? I don't care where you've been, what you've done, or where you're at. Now who the hell are you, to act the way you do? You won't be smiling by the time I'm through with you. Now who the hell are you?" Jay: That‘s just wrong! Having those lyrics right after that love song. Talk about a slap in the face for Callisto! Mike: And you do know that Bon Jovi song was the song that Callisto dedicated to Lexis on the night they got married.. And they even danced to it many, MANY times in this very ring. That has to hurt Bane. As the music continues playing Lexis appears on the stage with Venus, and a huge grin on her lips as the audience goes nuts with boos and shouts. Purple and white strobes flash, as white, and purple smoke fills the entrance and ramp way. She doesn’t hesitate to flip most of them off as she makes her way through the smoke to the ring. Lexis climbs into the ring, careful of those barbed wire ropes which she was sure she would feel later. Finally inside of the ring her theme stops and the lights return to normal, and there stands Lexis watching as Venus pads off backstage... and Awaiting Callisto’s arrival. Jay: Callisto hasn’t come to this ring since the first show.. Her first appearance since the reopening of the DWO.. Here tonight and only on PPV! Mike: Well.. Where is she? The crowd, along with Lexis, stand in anticipation, all wondering where Callisto Bane was and when she was coming out. Suddenly twelve sets of red concussion bombs explode around Lexis, sending her into a hopping fury while the crowd covers their ears. The arena cuts dark.. "Wolves" by Kittie blares and strobes are flickering wildly around the stage area as a line of fire spreads from backstage down to the stairs of the ring, all eyes remain on that fire line before attention is drawn elsewhere. Callisto Bane lets loose with her loud, ear shattering battle cry while back flipping down the ramp way! Mike: It’s Callisto Bane! Callisto continues back flipping all the way down that line of fire and to the end of it where she stands upright, one arm crossing her stomach and the other wrapping around the back of her neck in a seducing pose that captures the eyes of all, including Lexis Bane. Callisto was looking around her, eyeing all of the fans in attendance before allowing her cold silver-blue metallic orbs to land on Lexis Bane. The look from there on was that of a mad woman, she had her prey in sight and it would be hell from then on out. Jay: What a look.. Jesus. Mike: Gave me the shivers.. Lexis steps back from the barbed wire ropes, looking down to Callisto who was climbing those steps, taking them one by one in order to carefully watch every move Lexis makes. Lexis shakes off the feeling of fear and grins, egging Callisto on with her hands outstretched, fingers wiggling. Callisto slips between those ‘ropes’ with ease, standing inside of the ring now and still staring down Lexis as hard as before. Mike: No doubt the song had an effect on her.. This is going to be one hellish match here tonight folks. Batten down because the storm is about to hit. Jay: Here we go.. Callisto steps before Lexis, standing her entire height of six foot eight inches.. Poor Lexis was only five ten, but she hid her fear easily behind that mocking glare. The two now stand toe to toe, eyes boring into each others. Callisto speaks, her words falling on deaf ears for the crowd was too loud to care what words were thrown. Lexis fires back with her own, also unheard to anyone but the two inside of the ring. Around them the cage is lowered.. The crowd staring on in awe while their mouths cry out the want for blood. Callisto falls forward to those knees, her blonde locks bouncing messily around her face which was pure hate laced at the moment. Inside of the ring Lexis was still romping around, tagging Callisto every now and then with a low shot opened handed to a part of the body. Callisto Bane: "I loved you." And after that was spoken Lexis is dropped down into a massive choke slam! The crowd erupts after a moment, trying to get Callisto to continue with the attack.
