DWO Terms

2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
GENERAL 1. The Dominant Wrestling On-Line Federation and its other promotions are all ran via role-plays and angles. Victories and losses are subject to the quality, and quanity of role-plays done. 2. You are permitted to control as many wrestlers in the DWO as you like. You are permitted one automatically, but have the option to have as many as you like, if you have shown or you can demonstrate to us that you can keep up with the amount chosen. 3. All decisions made by the DWO President and Owner are final and binding. 4. We have one card a week. and a monthly PPV. There is also a Side Show where all interviews are conducted. 5. Do NOT come into the DWO and act like a child. all it will get you is a pink slip. ROLEPLAYING 1. Do NOT IM either the Owner or the President asking these following questions or any that would apply: "when are results going to be posted?", "Why did I lose my match?", "Why is the card not up?","when will the card be up". And so on and so forth. We do our part, you do yours. 2. You MUST do at least one RP a week per event. 3. While we tolerate adult language, we ask that you please limit how much you use it, and only in an appropriate context. In other words, do not curse just for the purpose of cursing. When we see a profanity laced diatribe, it tells us two things. One is that you have no creativity. Two is that you are too immature to be kept on the roster. 4. Do not refer to any 'real-life' wrestling organizations or wrestlers in your RPs. 5. Do not impersonate any of the talent of the DWO or its promotions, or any member of the Front Office, without their expressed WRITTEN permission. You will be expected to show this written permission on request should any member of the Front Office ask for it. Failure to comply with this rule WILL lead to IMMEDIATE terminaton. Do not test us on this one. 6. The use of established pro wrestling catchphrases is strictly prohibited. 7. If you are not going to be able to roleplay for a determined amount of time, please contact the Front Office. We are more than happy to work with you in circumstances just like this. 8. Attacks (Physical) are not permitted in RP's unless you have requested. This request is to be presented to the Owner, or President before any attack is made. 9. Roleplays that attack the following items are strictly prohibited : Religion, Race, or Gender. CHALLENGES/ATTACKS 1. Please check the Future Cards page, in order to ensure that the person you are challenging does not have a match already scheduled. If we receive a challenge like this, it will be thrown out. 2. We reserve the right to modify (slightly) or refuse any attack sent to us. We usually receive a certain amount per card, and we simply can't use all of them. If we feel they are worthy, we will try to fit them into News, but there is no guarantee. 3. We will not use attacks that simply say 'I want to jump this guy and beat him up.' Show some creativity. 4. When sending in attacks, you are not permitted to dictate how a match will end. For instance, you can't submit an application that says 'I'm going to attack this guy, and then his opponent will beat him for the title'. The only time you are allowed to dictate the end of the match is if you tell us you want your guy to lose for some reason or another. 5. When sending in attacks, you are not permitted to put words into the mouths of other wrestlers. That is all for now.
