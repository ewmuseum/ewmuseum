Thursday Night Travesty — the card, 13 June 2002

13 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
As the sun goes down, The moon shall rise, The clouds will hide all other light. In the darkness of the full moon, We will know what terror comes soon. They shall all die on that night, And from the ashes we shall rise. We shall bring back to earth what belongs. Hell shall rise from it's grave, along with all it's demons. And once again their world shall be ours. [ Datum ]5-9-02[ Hauptleitung ]-DWO- Travesty Card Thursday Night Travesty 6.13.02 LIVE from Slaughter, Louisiana! Low Card Matches Match One Will Brown vs. Chris Kronic Singles Match These two newcomers will battle it out in hopes of striking star status in the DWO. Match Two Reuben Sikes vs. Darkice Singles Match These two men will also fight to pinfall in order to catch the eye of the high ups. Match Three Don Rossi vs. Cody Scott Singles Match Don Rossi, not so new to the DWO will take on newcomer Cody Scott in a test with new blood and old blood. Middle Card Matches Match Four Jonan vs. Switchblade Extreme glass hands match Extreme title is on the line in this match up. The hands of both men will be taped and douced in glass! Match Five Adam Stryker and Sean Campbell vs. Gina Muniz and Angelus Tag Team match up Tag titles will be ON THE LINE! Only one partner in the ring at a time. Match Six Jamie von Eriche vs. Kristi Kiss Table Match These two DWO Superstars will battle it out to see just who will come out with a victory. High Card Matches Main Event Jake Storm vs. Da Mab vs. Lana Day Three way Brawl Three of the DWO's top stars will face off inside of the ring to see who will become number one contender for the IC Title! Other Appearences: Mr. Wolf, Da Thug, Ketsueki Karasu, Angelus, and Amira! Card and appearences subject to change. Click for Results
