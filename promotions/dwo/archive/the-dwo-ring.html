The DWO Ring

2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Recent Updates: None as of right now. Ah,..The DWO Ring. Such a complex structure that once you are in there is no way out unless you win or lose. We will begin with the basic structure of the ring. The DWO ring is fifty feet around, the inside is stretched mat and underneath is steel sheets. No spring is used in the making of this ring, so the fals are very hard on the body. The turnbuckles are steel posts which connect the ropes that enclose this structure. The ramp which leads to the ring is an eleven inch glass rampway leading from the stage down to the ring area. Under this ramp is a pit leading one hundred feet down to a rock bottom. Falling off would mean instant death. All in all the DWO ring is a deadly place to enter into. Each time you go out you face a chance of great injury and even possibly death. All the workers and fighters show great caution when setting up the ring, this is why the shows are always located below ground at a specific site inside of a city. Never will you catch the DWO above ground. Much like the ring, the arena in which all the action takes place is just as dangerous. The risk of mass break out such as fire, riot, or any other threat is great. The arena's at which the DWO preform are all below ground in the gladitorial pits and colleseums. Here the arena's consist of a huge circular pit in which the ring is placed. Up above the ring are the bleechers in which the blood thirsty paying customers reside. Behind all of the openings of the ring and the arena there are hidden tunnels with which hold the back rooms such as the locker rooms, the computer rooms, and the gym. Many other rooms are located in the back as well. Others such as the main exits and so forth. Out of all of this..the fans still flock to see the live shows that are preformed in such harsh conditions. Even though all is underground the DWO still go the length to keep their fans comfortable. There are coke venders as well as beer, food, and air, and heat units all located within the more accessable parts of the arena back area Above- A screen shot of the tunnel leading down to the arena. Some needed rules for the ring. 1. Three basic ways to win: Pin, Submission, and Knock out. 2. If an opponent is rendered out cold you automatically win. 3. If you are out of the ring for more than 15 seconds you lose. 4. If both wrestlers are out at the call of 15 seconds then both men/women lose and the match is a draw. 5. Show caution for youself as well as your opponent, it is easy to become seriously injured by just the ring itself.
