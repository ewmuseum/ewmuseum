Chryste — the DWO Legend montage

2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Name: Chryste Gender: Female -Our montage opens to the brutal power bomb Chryste delivered in her debut to Amira grunge, setting off the C-4 explosion than ultimately ended her career. It carries on to Chryste battling outside in the parking lot with several robed figures, before fading into her hitting a dragonrana pin on Gina Muniz to win the Intercontinental title. It progresses through her fights with Jade, to the final burning tables match. Several more clips are shown, of Chryste seeking comfort in the arms of Callisto and Draven. Spanning past several more events, from Serpente to a clip from her match against Inphino Blitz, the clip freezes on a shot of her distraught face as she stares up at a deceased Kazuyin, her bloodied face wracked with pain. After ghosting to the cover of Playboy magazine, the montage slowly ends…- -Voice Over- "Chrystal Felinni was and will always be the most loved DWO athlete.. With unrivaled skill and a body to match she ruled the ring. Holding title after title 'Chryste' quickly moved up the ranks of the DWO, breaking out and even ruling the ring of Primetime Central by defeating opponent after opponent in the Golden Turnbuckle Tournament. She went as far as to nearly overcome fellow Legend.. Inphino Blitz. Time and time again Chryste has taken fans, opponents, and allies by surprise with her uncanny ring ways and quick witted antics. Forever will Chrystal "Chryste" Felinni remain in our hearts, cherished superstar, beloved friend, and first ever DWO Legend."
