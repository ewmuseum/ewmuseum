DWO News, December 2002 to January 2003

Jan. 25, 2003 Draven: One More Time? - staff @ 11:33 pm PST If you watched this last Travesty edition you would have noticed that the DWO's superstar Draven has pulled himself from the federation in a retire attempt. However, he attacked Donny Diamond at the show and said some things that could hint to his possible return for one more go at attaining the DWO Extreme title. We're unsure if this is true but we do know that the match has been set, but Draven has yet to comment on weather he will participate or pass this opportunity up. We're awaiting his word of confirmation before we begin to comment more so. We hope to hear from him sooner rather than later, and we will keep you posted. Crimson Dance - staff @ 11:33 pm PST Lord of the Asylum: Crimson Dance This months wild ppv promises to blow ratings out of the water. We have matches tipped to the tops with superstars you are dying to see. The show will be taking place in Chicago, IL in the custom underground arena. However, the main event and such we have in store for you will shock you into watching. Below is the card, check it out and make sure to order! Lord of the Asylum: Crimson Dance LIVE! From Chicago, IL January 31, 2003 Main event: Rainer vs. Kaiowas Crimson Steel Cage match. HW title on the line. Description: These two will meet inside of a spinning enclosed steel box. At random points during the match parts of the box will slide open while closing other previously open parts. The trick is this, when the sides open they release acid from pore pits at the top portions of the sliding hooks. Yes, hooks.. Sharp, dangling, steel hooks. The acid can leave a very nasty burn. All in all the cage will create a puzzle box for the wrestlers to over come while overcoming one another. A true test of skill and wit while also testing their brute muscle. Match Seven: Donny Diamond vs. Draven{?} Extreme Title on the line. Glass Tables Match Description: DWO Glass tables are not ordinary at all.. Under the five inch glass are supporting steel plates, and inbetween those two lairs are barbedwire. In order to win you must punish your opponent by putting them through three of these randomly set up glass tables, and you have to put them through with style. Meaning, no accidental pushes, all out moves through the glass. Match Six: Indy Cred vs. Laguna and Josh Starwind Tag Team Match Match Five: Adam Stryker vs. Syko Arsonist Singles Match To settle the disput that flared between them this past week. Match Four: The Event vs. Noah White IC title on the line Brawl for All Match Three: Kuff Mcslade vs. Tyrone Grace RD number one contenders match Match Two: Julius Corbin vs. Timothy Marcus Singles Match Match One: Alyssa vs. Darkice Singles Match Potential Second shot at the RD title at a date to be announced. A Legend Returns.. - staff @ 11:32 pm PST A Legend returns.. Weeks ago we warned you of a return of a man who would once again change the way you look at the DWO.. This man has made his return. This past week on Travesty Alyssa Sanders took to the ring to see just what awaited her inside. When she turned, she was greeted by an unfamiliar theme with the blackening of the arena. Minutes passed and nothing, but once he made his debut the world held its breath. Who was he? Adam Stryker You heard it people, the former DWO Heavyweight Champion, former ring leader of the Circle of Rage, and one of the DWO's highest respected wrestlers.. Has returned. He took a win over Alyssa and went on to argue with Kaiowas and then end the night in a fight with him which resulted in him and Kaiowas being ousted from the premises. What does the future hold for Adam? Only time will tell. Kaiowas vs. Rainer - staff @ 11:32 pm PST Kaiowas vs. Rainer! For months it has been awaited.. Someone appropriate in talent to challenge our current DWO Heavyweight Champion. Finally, Rainer has come into play and has stepped up to take on Champion, Kaiowas. This month at the DWO's upcoming PPV event, Lord of the Asylum: Crimson Dance. The match has been set and a dangerous match it is indeed. These two men will step into quite the hell hole in order to either attain or retain the most coveted title in the DWO. As for the match itself, you will have to wait for further information from our creative department before we blurt out all of the details. If you know anything of the DWO, you will know you are in for the match of your life. Rainer, Kaiowas, and a death trap like no other, only one can come out a champion.. Who do you think will be left standing in the end? More as it becomes available Jan. 19, 2003 The [ Shooting Star ] Press - staff @ 6:13 pm PST Can't start a show without the Hell's Heartbreakers, and that's exactly how Travesty was kicked off this past week. After another tongue wagging performance, Mike and Jay were able to pull themselves together and get the first match underway, a one on one contest between Vash and DWO newcomer Wicked. Wicked and Vash put on a wrestling clinic, showing alot of basic wrestling skills that had the crowd in silence and Mike and Jay scratching their heads. But the violence finally kicked in, and it looked like Wicked would have the advantage when he spit the Black Mist, but thanks to his foot making contact with Vash's groin, Vash stumbled out of the way, recovered, and nailed Wicked with a superkick to the jaw he calls the Silent Violence, and gianed the pinfall. Before the commercial break, Lexis Bane arrived to the arena to a chorus of boos from the DWO crowd. Mike and Jay speculated as to why she was there early, as the announcement she planned to make wasn't supposed to happen until later in the show, but with no answers available, they moved on to the next match, which pitted Noah White against a mystery opponent. That mystery opponent turned out to be another DWO newcomer by the name of Tyrone Grace. Tyrone showed off his high flying style early, nailing a missle dropkick from the top rope to Noah on the outside! Noah gained the advantage with good mat skills, but Tyrone kept coming back with his speed and high impact moves. But Noah is no slouch, landing some high impact moves of his own. But just when it looked like Noah could pull it out, Tyrone gained the final advantage and nailed Noah with the Image of Perfection, and gained the win over Noah! Once again the camera's caught up with Lexis Bane backstage as she walked and entered into her office. But they didn't stay long, as Lexis forced the camera crew out of her office. The third match of the night...never took place. Syko Arsonist was set to take on another DWO newcomer, Shawn Diemond, but Diemond never showed, prompting a tyrade from Arsonist on the mike. Syko complained about his training for low card matches, only to have his opponents no show or be complete pushovers, so he demanded a better status within the DWO...or he'll walk! Fourth match of the night was for the number one contendership to the IC Title, and pitted The Real Deal against Julius Corbin. Both men attempted to end the match quickly, Corbin even hitting a half nelson/german suplex on Real Deal, but unable to keep his shoulders on the mat. Next both men went into submission mode, Real Deal getting a bow and arrow lock only to have Corbin reach the ropes, then Corbin nailling a DDT into a chokehold on Real Deal, who used his physical strength to get out. The match became even more violent from there, with both men doing various high flying and sickening impact moves. At one point, Corbin's knees give out on him, as he and Rainer fall to the mat, Donny and Ron Diamond take advantage of the situation and attack Real Deal, shocking him with a tazer gun and Donny Diamond hitting the Sinner's End, not just once, but twice, the second time upon a flaming chair! EMTs finally were able to get to Real Deal, but you can bet Real Deal will be looking for major revenge against the two men! Mike and Jay announced that DWO's newest show, Saturday Night Relentless, would debut soon, then off they went into the 5th match of the evening, an endurance match between Shawna Blaze and Alyssa Sanders. Alyssa dominated this match early, getting the first knockout attempt on Shawna Blaze with a nice bulldog, only to have Shawna get back to her feet (rules of the match were wins by knockout only). Shawna was able to come back however, and the match went back and forth, with Shawna still trying to win the match even after Alyssa injured her arm. But in the end, Alyssa knocked out Shawna Blaze with the Sweet Sensation to gain the victory. After the match, Mike and Jay recieve information that Callisto Bane has come up missing from the hospital, prompting Lexis to leave the arena to return to that very hospital. Before the next match could get underway, Donny and Ron Diamond made their way back to the ring. Ron took the mike and explained to the crowd that his brother Donny was the epitomy of darkness and violence, and that sooner or later the DWO World Champion, Kaiowas, would fall to the might of Donny Diamond, who would take his place as the leader and ruler of the DWO. Ron said that no one could defeat Donny Diamond, but that remains to be seen. The Main Event of the night pitted Rainer against Apocalypse in a no holds barred, exposed floor, steel rope pit brawl! Both men brought the pain early, Rainer using Apoc's head for batting practice with a steel chair, and Apoc showing Rainer what a table is used for, hitting a chokeslam that put both men through the wood and on the floor. The match was bloody for both men, as their bodies were tossed about on the cold concrete floor and their flesh ripped to shreds thanks to the steel ropes. Just when it looked like Rainer would have the win, Apoc was able to fight back, and once again both men went back and forth, Rainer even landing a back breaker upon a steel chair! But it wasn't enough to keep Apoc down, as he set up Rainer for the chokeslam once more, but Rainer was able to get out of it, and lock on his finishing submission on Apoc! Apoc tried and tried to break the hold, but eventually passed out from the pain! Rainer gained the victory, but Apoc has yet to tap out to a finishing submission! Before the show came to a close, the megatron was taken over by the symbol of a dragon around a cross, and the sound of a heartbeat thumping through the loudspeakers. As the DWO crowd set their eyes upon that symbol, a dark voice opened up in place of the heartbeat, saying that a soul within the DWO was being called home, but would refuse to leave with a fight, with causing violence and bloodshed once more. The voice claimed that one man inside the DWO had something that belonged to him, and that the voice wanted it back. No one knows just who this voice belonged to, but hopefully next Travesty will reveal some answers, both on the owner of the voice and the whereabouts of the DWO Owner, Callisto Bane! With all of the applications turn aways are a must, but among these applications who have been allowed in? Below is a small list of those who you will be seeing in the DWO from now on out. -Indy Cred -Kuff McSlade -Apocalypse -Tyrone Grace -Wicked You should get to know these names, they will soon be active in the Rising Dominance divisions and other divisions seperated in the DWO. Welcome them, and make them feel at home. Rainer stepping up. - Staff @ 9:39 pm PST This past Travesty we had the pleasure of seeing a true star step up and grasp what he believed to be his. Rainer, a major fan favorite in the DWO recently took it upon himself to state he was done with low-mid carding and was taking up a position in the higher divisions. With this statement Rainer stormed off backstage only to be struck back out onto the stage with a hard right to the jaw from none other than Apocalypse! You heard right folks, the former AW Heavyweight Champion is now signed to the DWO and has challenged Rainer to a bout this upcoming Travesty. What does this mean? Rainer got what he wanted.. He is main eventing this week in a hellish match against the man who challenged him. Can Rainer accomplish his goal of remaning in the top brass of the DWO, or will be be knocked down by the ego of Apocalypse? We'll have to wait to see this week! Jan. 02, 2003 Callisto and Lexis In A Bloody Brawl! - staff @ 2:17 am PST In one of the most burtal matches ever witnessed in DWO PPV history, Callisto Bane battled her wife Lexis Bane in a BloodCell match to determine the one true owner of the DWO!! These two women did major damage to each other, destroying the ring and the announce table in the process! But when it was all said and done, a bleeding and weary Callisto pinned Lexis to regain her ownership of the DWO! But it wasn't over yet. From the rafters a figure appeared, dropping a steel cable down and hanging DWO CEO Draven! Using Draven's body as a counterweight, this figure dropped to the mat. As Callisto attempted to help Draven, the figure nailed her twice with a sledgehammer!!! Lexis, having witnessed all this, shocked everyone by attacking the figure and helping Draven back to the mat! The figure disappeared as this happened, and Lexis checked on Draven before making her way to the hospital at her wife's side. Who is this mysterious figure that attacked Draven and Callisto? Only time will tell... Serpente!! - staff @ 2:11 am PST In the first match of Christmas Chaos, UWF's former star Serpente made her presence felt when she attacked Brian Gains and Makalya Manzu in their bout! Serpente and her cloaked companion did major damage to the two, but they had one more surprise up their sleeve... Serpente unmasked in the ring...to reveal that Serpente is Chryste!! Chryste and her new friend reveled in their surprise attack to the shocked DWO audience, before Chryste muttered one single word: GOTCHA! What does this mean for the DWO? We'll find out soon enough! World Title Tourney Down To Two! - staff @ 2:07 am PST At LOA: Christmas Chaos, the World Title Tourney came two matches closer to a final winner. Kaiowas defeated Vash in solid fashion to advance and meet Julius Corbin, who defeated Shawna Blaze, in order to determine the new DWO World Champion, to be crowned at the next Travesty. Both of these men have worked hard to get to where they are, and a match between Kaiowas and Corbin promises to provide one hell of a show for the DWO faithful. One of these two men will walk out of Travesty the DWO World Champion...but which one? Stay tuned to find out! Dec. 27, 2002 The Whispered Word - staff @ 5:26 am PST Issue One - Hot on Top Good evening, this special edition of the DWO newly developed column will bring you exclusive over-all news of the happenings in the most dominant federation to date. This column today will cover the most recent events taking place since the rise of the Lexis Bane era, the good times, the bad times, and the power hungry antics of this new owner. Shall we begin? Part One: New Beginnings, New Ownerships. In the beginning Callisto Bane brought about Dominant Wrestling On-Line, a slow moving yet up and coming federation which showed promise through rough times. In her struggle she managed to bring the DWO up and up until landing a spot in Primetime Central, where the DWO was allowed to grow and prosper. Prosper it did, gaining publicity and a more digified roster while expanding wildly and becoming a fan favorite. Since that fateful sign to PTC the DWO has continued to show it is indeed a federation to heed, but major things have occured which would to some cause a federation to crumble. It began with the death of a very prominant player in the DWO, a powerful wrestler by the name of Chryste. With Callisto tending to Chryste under secret terms a jealous wife became grave and grim. With the death of Chryste came the perfect crime being put into play. Callisto was sent to jail on the charges of murder.. The culprit? Lexis Bane. You may ask yourselves just what would drive Lexis to do such a thing, could Callisto have honestly caused the death of Chryste? Accusations were thrown around and many things were pinned on Callisto, but in the end it was Lexis admitted to Callisto that she was behind the entire situation. A very enraged Callisto Bane was then hauled off to the State Prison without hope for escape. It wouldn't be fit until Bane was then sent to a mental asylum on charges of an unstable mental condition. Lexis meanwhile was sitting high and proud on her current throne of dominance, also going as far as to close the DWO down simply to mock Callisto while she was 'helpless'. During this Callisto could do nothing but sit and rot in prison, no hope of a repeal or any type of pending freedom. With her senses back to her, Lexis reopened the DWO under new ownership, herself.. Of course things would change drastically and change they did. The DWO was brought back with a powerful first show, play carding most all of their best talent while showing how they could still be dominant without the need for a gimmick laden card. Lexis succeeded with her plans by bringing DWO from a nasty last place to an amazing first place finish in the PTC ratings. All this was with one show back which proved the DWO was far from failing and simply not going any where but on top where it belonged. However, Lexis is being doubted with her ability to keep DWO up in the top three of PTC, can she pull it off? Perhaps. But with Callisto Bane now free from her asylum due to Chryste making an appearence.. Can she even keep her hands on ownership of the company? We'll see. Part Two: Talent Showing Talent. With the reopening of the DWO, Lexis had no choice but to reopen the application departments. With the chance to get into the DWO back again many flocked toward those newly inked applications and signed away their lives. With so many applicants Lexis Bane appointed a few of her most trusted advisors at the head of the department which over sees who signs and who enters. With this department now in full swing the new comers came in high numbers. From the beginning things were as should be, many newcomers showing face, most making early names and then of course those who chicken out on their first booking. With half of the applicants turned down, the other half entered, and then of course another half no showing.. The DWO was looking at a small number of those who were willing to work and do all they could to become stars in the company. Lexis was rewarded by her tough booking and her equally harsh entering rules. With her decisions marking improvement in the ranks and a stable number of stars the stage was set for nothing but showering attention. Lexis made sure to keep her eyes peeled for slackers and once all was weeded and everything was set things continued to flow smooth. She had done a great job creating an amazing roster for the DWO, now all she had to do was wield all of that talent and make sure to keep things going according to plan. The stars she had signed did indeed show mark improvement from their first showing in the DWO training camp. Once in front of millions and before a camera they seemed right at home. With names like Logan Miller, Darkice, Jade, and many more showing good face who else can we expect to see? It seems Lexis has her eyes on the bigger names to fill a few remaning spots on the roster, the talent she provided the DWO with already is proving to be a challenge even for the most seasoned of DWO wrestlers. Thus far she has done a great job in the talent department, but again, can she continue this trend? Part Three: Regaining Power As it seems all good things must come to an end, could the same be said about Lexis' reign as owner of the DWO? Perhaps.. With Callisto Bane out and about she has proven herself to be a threat to Lexis' control. With already showing up at the first DWO show with her secret weapon [Jade] on the beginning credits. With all of this talk circling Callisto's return Lexis has taken action by heightening security at both her home and the arenas where the DWO is set to preform. But will this be enough? As it seems Lexis is already slipping in power with her CEO Draven turning and revealing himself to actually be sent by Callisto Bane. Of course this was only revealed after Draven had tricked Lexis into signing papers for an event to end her reign. Those papers were to cause a match for Lexis' ownership to take place at the upcoming PPV event, Christmas Chaos. It was said that after Draven was seen leaving Lexis' office with those papers the owner darted out of the arena in a mighty rush toward the parking lot. With all of this set to take place at the PPV it will no doubt be the most amazing match up of the night. The tickets for this event have already began to sell like crack on the market and we can only trust that in no time they will be sold out as usual for a DWO event. With the card looking hot, and this match being on the top ticket, you will not be disappointed.. This will indeed be a show not to miss, so catch it, and more December 31, 2002 only on In-Demand Pay-Per-View. I leave you know with a little incit on the happenings, don't miss out on more at the show.. Dec. 22, 2002 The (Shooting Star) Press - staff @ 2:20 pm PST Here I am again…Medulla Oblongata reporting for The (Shooting Star) Press as I am paid to do. Anyway, I don’t have much time for fun and games this issue, as I am on a strict deadline, so I’ll do a basic overview of the happenings in DWO. Things have been heating up in the DWO as of late. Feuds and rivalries have been building with a foundation of hatred. This whole Callisto/Lexis/Draven/Jade business has been firing up to say the least; finally coming down to Callisto and Lexis facing off in a Bloodcell match! Anyway, back to the latest on DWO Travesty. Let’s see…the opener of the night was LOX going against Logan Miller. Needless to say, the match went back and forth as the two men displayed technical abilities in the ring, coming down to LOX hitting his infamous LOX Buster, winning the match over Miller. The next match of the night was Kaanen Thorne going against James Van Allen in a singles match up. The match started off with JVA getting the early advantage, tossing Kaanen about and around the DWO ring – but Thorne soon stopped that, and quickly gained the momentum he needed to hit his 6-6 Crush on JVA, and get the three count. Moving along quickly, the next match was another preliminary match to see who advances into the Final Four of the World Title tournament. It was Vash going against Xander Payne. Vash immediately started off, nailing Payne with random martial arts kicks to get things going. Xander quickly fought back though, and turned the affair into a bloody mess outside of the ring. When the two men got back into the ring, Vash scored with his Hard Target finishing maneuver, causing Payne to tap out! Quickly moving on…we goes to The Event squaring off against Kaiowas in another preliminary match. As Kaiowas was showered in jeers, he used that as fuel to quickly dish out punishment to The Event. Slam after slam, the Event kicked out of everything Kaiowas was giving to him; and then some. The Event began to fight back midway through the match, but was cut short by Kaiowas’s Tribal Punisher finishing maneuver, securing a spot in the Final Four. The next match is Donny Diamond versus Shawna Blaze. Before the match, Jade assaulted Shawna, which lead to Donny picking her apart for the most part of the match. Though, Luke Lawless came out, and laid out Donny after Donny laid out Blaze with a pair of brass knuckledusters. Luke then quickly pulled the out cold Blaze over Diamond, giving her the win! The final match of the evening was Julius Corbin going against Nick Crowe. The match went back and forth so many times, I’d need several extra hands to count it. Crowe and Corbin fought each other until they were blue in the face, but in the end, one mistake by Nick Crowe lead to the downfall, as Corbin quickly hit his Atonement finisher for the pin. Well, that’s it for me! Stay tuned next week for the rundown of the first Pay Per View! DWO presents Christmas Chaos! Dec. 21, 2002 Callisto vs. Lexis!!! - staff @ 12:09 am PST Just when Lexis thought she had it under control...this past Thursday night on Travesty, her control came in jeapordy. Earlier in the night, Lexis had an impromptu meeting with DWO CEO Draven, concerning last weeks embarassment thanks to Jade. Draven produced a stack of papers, claiming they were the legal documents for jade's release from the company. Without even bothering to read it, Lexis signed her name on the dotted lines, handing the papers back to Draven to be processed. However, later that evening, Draven was caught by DWO cameras meeting with a mysterious figure outside the arena. It was there that these same papers were delivered to the figure, which signed it's name on them as well. Mike and Jay, the announcer team of the DWO, speculated that the figure could have been Callisto Bane herself, but they had no concrete eveidence to prove this...but the last part of Travesty put all doubts to rest. After seeing the meeting with the mysterious figure on her in-house monitor, Lexis stormed Draven's office and confronted him, threatening to fire him if he didn't tell who he had met with and what she had signed! Draven informed her that she couldn't fire him, until one certain event took place. When she asked what that was and what exactly she had signed, he told informed her that she was now part of a double main event at the next PPV!!! Lexis was shocked, and then angered beyond belief when she found out that according to the contract she had signed, if she failed to appear, all ownership and legal rights to the company would go to her opponent! It was then that Lexis asked who exactly her opponent would be. With an evil smile felt for miles, Draven dropped a bomb that completely leveled Lexis...she would be facing her own wife, Callisto Bane!!! You heard right...at the upcoming DWO PPV, LOA: Christmas Chaos...Callisto Bane will take on Lexis Bane for complete ownership of the DWO! The first major war since DWO's return is about to take place...with the DWO itself hanging in the balance! How will this powerplay turn out? Stay tuned to the DWO to find out!
