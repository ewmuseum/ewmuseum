Pyrotechnics explode out of the stage and down the ramp way as a mad guitar riff begins to spasm throughout the speakers of the P.A. system! BOOM! The fans are on their feet as Marilyn Manson's "Seizure of Power" erupts around the arena, sending everyone in to a frenzy! BOOM! Pyrotechnics again erupt from the ring corners, bright silver followed by the down pour of red embers from the rafters. BOOM! Scene's from last weeks Travesty begin to flood the Mega Tron. Kinsley being watched and then attacked by Erryk Zython in the DWO upper ground parking lot. The abduction of Amira from the backstage area on the part of Callisto Bane. AJ Trinidad winning his match and the women in general supporting the scene of the DWO. THURSDAY NIGHT TRAVESTY Slams in to the screen as the fans are interrupted by the sounds of "Untitled" by Fitch blaring over the DWO P.A. systems that were placed around the arena. The banging music sets the already excited crowd in to a rage. One the stage the two new members of the DWO, Drone and Ender now stand. Down the ramp way they walk and then after circling the ring once they climb inside of the massive structure. Once inside the man known as Ender retrieves a microphone from Bethany and walks to the side of his partner, Drone. Mike: "Now what the hell is this all about?" Jay: "You got me,..But the show has started so I suppose they want some of the action." Ender: "As you all know, or as some of you Texans might not know seeing as most of you are illiterate anyway.." Massive boos erupt from the crowd as trash becomes a problem inside of the ring. Ender: "Calm down! Rednecks.. Jesus Christ. Like I was saying before being so rudely interrupted.. We have a match booked against Stryker and Gina at the upcoming PPV: Blackshock. Now what you may not know is that we’re going to walk out of that match with titles around our waists, because face it. We are the best the DWO has to offer hands down. My partner here, Drone has some things of his own to say." Ender hands the microphone over to Drone as the crowd begins to get antsy. Drone: "No more week tag teams holding those titles.. Now we are here to take everyone out of the running. Starting with Gina and Stryker, but ending with everyone who dares to cross us. Now.." Drones speech is ended with the appearance of The Fury on the stage. His hands are on his hips and his eyes are staring down upon Ender and Drone as they watch him, all wondering what the newcomer is doing on the stage. The Fury pulls a microphone from his back pocket and begins to speak while walking down the ramp slowly. The Fury: "You know, the both of you have some really big mouths. Just like myself you have only just began in the DWO and you are already trying to become top dogs. Don’t the two of you know anything?" The Fury was becoming awfully close to the ring apron but still he walks and talks, gesturing every now and then. The Fury: "Seriously you two are looking to get yourselves in to some major trouble. I mean, someone from the back could just walk down to this ring.." The Fury climbs up the steps slowly and slips through the second set of ropes before standing fully inside of the ring. The Fury: "Get in to the ring with you two.." The Fury steps close to the two men but Ender and Drone remain still. The Fury suddenly lands the microphone butt against the head of Ender, sending him to the mat! The Fury then counters a blow from Drone and lands a solid kick to the mans gut, putting him over! The Fury takes advantage of this bent over position and hits a scissor kick to the back of Drones head! The Fury: "I mean hell! Someone could just walk out and kick your asses!" Jay: "The Fury just took out Ender and Drone!!" Mike: "Oh man! Are they going to be pissed!" Untitled, the name of the tag team making up Drone and Ender are out on the mat, rolling for a moment before standing. The Fury smirks and leaps over the top rope on to the apron and then climbing down. He takes off in a dead run to the back with Ender and Drone hot on his tail. Mike: "Hah! Look at The Fury high tail it! Ender and Drone are right behind him." Jay: "Part of the action..-Shakes his head- Man what a way to start off the show. Which reminds me, welcome to TRAVESTY!" Mike: "That’s right folks, welcome to the show.. And from the looks of it you are already getting your monies worth here tonight. Jay: "Well if you think that is hot, wait till the PPV airs! We have an amazing card lined up for that event, but for tonight we have one hell of a show booked up. We want to get straight in to it here tonight with the first match which will be Lana Day taking on Chris Johnson in a singles match." Mike: "Now do you really think Chris has anything to worry about? I mean.. Lana is a bit smaller than he is." Mike Johnson was actually secretly making his way to the ring at the moment. He had no entrance music playing and no pyro’s exploding. The crowd almost didn’t even catch his arrival until he got in to the ring. Mike: "Speaking of Chris Johnson.." Mike points to the man in the ring and Jay looks, smirking before finishing his sentence. Jay: "Come on Mike, you have seen more take place in this ring than a smaller opponent beat the hell out of a larger one. I am sure Lana Can hold her own. Look at.." Jay is interrupted by a loud burst of pyrotechnics as "Paperdoll" by Kittie blares out over the P.A. systems and the lights in the arena darken. Lana Day makes her way through the curtains… The crowd sounds off a loud reaction as she makes her way down the ramp, red lighting slowly beginning the circle the aisle. She reaches the ring, and hops to the apron, staring across at Chris Johnson as he waves her in! She smiles, leapfrogging over the ropes, wasting no time to go to the attack! Mike: "And Lana wasting no time to get this one going! She tackles CJ hard with a spear, and begins wailing away on his face with a barrage of punches! She’s gonna take his head off!" Jay: "Literally?" Mike: "Shut up!" Jay snickers as CJ reaches out, grabbing the rope by his head. The referee warns Lana, before forcing her to break away from him. She stands to her feet, and stomps his side several times before he rolls from the ring, holding his side as he stares up to her, as the crowd howls wildly! Mike: "And Johnson with a smart move to get out of there and rethink his game plan!" 1...2... Jay: "He’s yelling back and forth at Lana as she beckons him back into the ring! CJ turns to the crowd, and he’s telling them to shut their mouths!" 3...4...5... Lana smiles, and runs forward, baseball sliding out of the ring towards CJ! He quickly turns and sidesteps as the kick goes past him! He levels Lana with a short sprint Yakuza kick as she stands to her feet! Jay: "Ha! What a setup! The count is restarted now, and CJ grabs Lana by her hair, pulling her to her feet." Mike: "He grabs her in a bear hug and runs, slamming her back first into the apron! Obviously painful by the sound of that yell!" CJ repeats the maneuver a couple more times, before dropping Lana to the mat. He rolls back in the ring, leaning on the ropes and laughing as she slowly rolls to her feet, holding her back. She starts to climb into the ring, but CJ grabs her by the hair, pulling her up to the apron. Mike: "CJ lets go of Lana and swings a wide right hand, but she ducks it!" Lana takes advantage of CJ’s back turned, kneeing him in the small of his back! He cries out, stumbling a few steps out into the ring. Lana hops up to the top rope, and dives off, hitting a reverse hurricanrana on Johnson that slams the back of his head into the mat! Jay: "Whoa! Great display of skill from Lana Day!" Mike: "Yes it was! She’s a little slow to her feet, but she quickly covers and hooks the leg!" ONE!!! TWO!!! TH--KICKOUT! Mike: "And so close! Were she just a little quicker to her feet, she could have made short work of CJ here tonight!" Jay: "Close only counts in grenades and condoms! What Lana needs is to put down the donuts and put her ass on the treadmill and she might get something done!" Mike: "Lana brings Johnson to a sitting position now, and hits him with a hard kick to the back of his head! I’m sure she’d let you get in there and show her how yourself, Jay!" Jay: "Err… Just do your job and worry about the match at hand!" Lana brings CJ to his feet, and Irish whips him, but he reverses it! He goes back to the opposite ropes, rebounding and catching her with a heel kick! He slowly rolls to his feet, and jumps, dropping a leg across her throat! He gets up, bringing her up by her head, and scoops her up, dropping her down head first with a cradle suplex! He quickly rolls to his feet, heading over to the ropes, where he starts to climb them. Mike: "And CJ is heading up top! He’s looking to put a finish to this one and get himself going in the DWO!" Jay: "Lana is a little ways out there… I hope eh has a plan for getting that far out! Last thing I wanna see is some show off getting scraped off the mat for overdoing himself!" CJ stands on the top turnbuckle, and raises his arms high! He crouches and dives from the ropes head first, coming down onto Lana with a massive flying Headbutt! Mike: "SHE MOVED!!!" Lana barely musters the strength to move from the attack just in time as CJ’s head bounces off the mat and he slumps unconscious and doesn’t move! The referee checks both wrestlers, lifting Lana’s hand, as it falls, as well as Johnson’s, which also falls. Jay: "Neither one of the two are moving!" Mike: "No, really? I thought we had some real action going down in there! And the ref is starting the count!" One... Two... Three… Four… Five… Six… Jay: "I think I see Lana coming to!" Seven… Eight… Mike: "CJ begins to stir as well! One of them could make it!" Nine… Ten… Mike: "And this one is going to end in a double count out! Both of these competitors fighting hard tonight, but between that cradle suplex and missed head butt, neither was able to get back to their feet when it mattered! And one has to ask how either of these two will do going into Blackshock, both with big match ups!" Jay: "Yeah but too bad they couldn’t pull off wins here tonight. I mean geeze..A doubt count out? How sorry was that?" Mike: "Not that sorry, They did put on a great show of action for us." Jay: "Yeah I suppose you are right.." Mike: "Aren’t I always?" Winner of Match One: Double Count out. On the Phone. The office was lush and cool inside, the occupant the same. Callisto Bane was behind her desk, speaking in to the receiver to someone on the other end. Callisto: "I know that would be a great idea.. I just am not sure how she will take it." Callisto: "Yes, I do care about her and her career. Look I will talk to her about it, but she is a stubborn one so I’m not sure how far I will get." Callisto draws patterns on her note pad before her with her index finger while she listens and then replies. Callisto: "Like I said, I will try. Though in the end it might be a bad answer you get on this end." Callisto looks toward her door and then down to the card for tonight. She nods once more. Callisto: "Alright, thanks and Good bye." The phone is hung up and the camera fades to commercial as Callisto runs her finger down to the second match of the nigh, tapping against the names. Commercial Break: SPEED - The newest game for Sony Playstation 2. Get it today and feel the adrenaline as you are seated behind the wheel of one of these 12 intense hot rods! Only 24.95 at gaming stores everywhere! Mike: "AJ will win dammit!" Jay: "No..He won’t." Mike: "How do you know and why do you doubt AJ so much?" Jay: "Because he has become a real ass as of late." Mike: "So?" Jay: "So asses don’t win. Plain and simple." Mike: "We’ll see! Welcome back folks for the second match of the night. As we saw last week the newcomer Mike Pizaz almost took AJ Trinidad’s head off with a baseball bat. Well tonight AJ is looking to get a little revenge on Pizaz. This match should be an intense WIN for AJ." Jay simply rolls his eyes and shakes his head. Jay: "Whatever.." "Hectic" by Mobb Deep blares throughout the arena, as A.J. Trinidad’s video flashes across the tron, prompting him to walk out onto the stage, Amira beside him as he does, much to the fan’s delight! He looks from his left to his right and smirks, before holding the DWO World Title high over his head, causing the fans to explode as he heads for the ring! Once to the floor, he paces back and forth a few moment’s exchanging friendly words with Amira before rolling into the ring. Mike: "A.J. and Amira still together after backstage speculation that the two were having problems involving Callisto Bane… No real information to if it’s true or not, other than Amira was released of her assistant duties earlier this week… And one moment, I am being told now, that we’re going backstage with our cameras for a moment." The scene switches to backstage, where we see Mike Pizaz walking through the hall on his way to the ring. He is wearing his T-Shirt reading "Too Damn Sexy"… He rounds the corner, quickly stopping as he nearly runs over Chryste, who is making her way towards the locker rooms, a lost look in her eyes. She’s wearing a large "Dominant is Extreme for the Standard" sweater, and has her IC title over her shoulder and her hands behind her back, as if hiding something. She stops and looks up at Pizaz, as he looks back to her, "checking" her out with a nod and a smile. Mike Pizaz: "Hey there sexy! Lost fan looking for every woman’s dream!? Nice belt, those toys get more and more real every day!" Chryste tilts her head slightly, a smirk on her face as she speaks, her voice light, almost unheard. Chryste: "Yep… Just a fan… A wannabe Chryste… So you like the belt?" Mike Pizaz laughs, patting her on the shoulder as he cockily looks down in her eyes. After freezing for a moment, he starts to laugh, and begins to talk. Mike Pizaz: "Ha! You are Chryste huh? Damn, never thought a rumored nutcase would look so… Damn!" He throws his hands up as a frown droops upon her lips. "Whoa whoa! Just kidding doll! Don’t kill me! Ha! I’ll take your number though!" Chryste smirks, and then looks around, almost as if disinterested as she peeks past him down the hallway, tilting her head to the side. A few moments pass as Pizaz continues to "check" her out, and she focuses her attention back on him, a slight smile on her face. Chryste; "Oh, sure… [detail withheld], but anyways… I see you’re about to wrestle the big guy, World Champ and Leader of the Antis… Or am I mixing you up with some other unbelievably gorgeous, rightful egomaniac? Mike quickly goes over the numbers in his head, smirking as he looks down at Chryste, tilting his head and chuckling egotistically as he speaks. Mike Pizaz: "Nope! That’s me babe! But anyways, enough about that chump, how abo--" Chryste cuts him off, her voice still low, and depressive feeling as she holds up an oak baseball bat, a soft smirk forming on her lips as she speaks. Chryste: "Good then, you’re the guy I was looking for… You lost your bat last week, and I was just gonna give it back to ya in case you needed to protect yourself from those Anti’s… they’re terrible, terrible people… Later guy, remember the number…" She slaps the bat into his hands, her bandaged hand gripping it tightly before letting it go, staring plain faced up at Pizaz… She walks past him and turns for the hall as he watches, smirking before mumbling to himself and heading for the ring. We are now taken back out to the ring, where AJ awaits his opponent. Mike: "And for some reason or another, Chryste either has eyes for the newcomer, or is stirring up some kind of trouble! Who knows with that youngster though, she’s a proverbial whirlwind of emotion!" Jay: "She’s a freakin lunatic! Plain and simple!" Mike sighs, as the lights in the arena dim… Mike Pizaz makes his way out onto the stage as his theme explodes over the PA to a loud chorus of boos from the crowd! He smirks cockily as he looks out over the crowd! He walks half down the ramp, and stops to flex his muscles as fireworks explode on the stage! As he starts for the ring, AJ quickly slides out and starts up the ramp for him! Mike: "And Trinidad is ready to really tear the new guy apart!" Jay: "Pizaz is the smarter child though1 He rears back with the bat and swings! But Trinidad ducks it and grabs his arm, kneeing him in the gut!" Pizaz heaves, and slumps over as AJ grabs the bat from his hands and slings it down the ramp where it lands beside the ring! He delivers an axe handle to Pizaz, who falls to his knees as the referee starts yelling for them to take it into the ring! Jay: "Those two best get in the ring or there won’t be a match tonight!" Mike: "And that kind of knowledge is exactly what makes our jobs look so glorious! Pfft! AJ grabbing Pizaz by his head now and dragging him for the ring. Pizaz breaks loose though at ringside with an elbow to Trinidad’s gut! He grabs him by the hair and BOUNCES his head off the steps before rolling him into the ring!" The bell rings as Pizaz quickly slides in, after grabbing the bat and resting it on the steps beside the apron, dropping an elbow to the back of Trinidad’s head! He hops to his feet, pulling Trinidad up before rocking him with a stiff European uppercut, which sends AJ stumbling back! Pizaz takes off towards him, but Trinidad recovers in time to grab him around the arms and flip him over! Jay: "Trinidad looked to be I trouble, but he seems to have fixed things with a belly to belly suplex!" Mike: "A very well executed counter by the champ! Pizaz is quickly getting got his feet however, with no intentions of making his claims look shallow!" Jay: "Trinidad with his title on the line against Jackson rust at Blackshock however, will not allow things to look bad for himself tonight either!" Trinidad and Pizaz quickly lock up… They push each other back and forth before Trinidad draws him in and arm drags him to the mat! He applies an arm bar as Pizaz screams out, and fights the hold! The ref checks on Pizaz, who refuses to submit, and continues to fight the lock! Mike: "Pizaz showing some resiliency here as he refuses to let the lock get the best of him! AJ finally releases the hold and the two get to their feet… they lock up again, this time with Pizaz pulling a surprise knee off on AJ!" Jay: "If he woulda hit any harder, we would saw where Trinidad took Amira for lunch! Haha!" Pizaz reacts quickly, tucking AJ’s head under his arm, and hooking the tights for a quick snap suplex! He holds onto him, rolling over and bringing him to his feet. He lifts him high this time, holding the World Champ up in the air… … … … … Mike: "And Pizaz finally drops AJ from that stalling brain buster after about fifteen seconds of airtime! So far he’s really showing that he does indeed have the talent to back up his words!" Jay: "Imagine the press if he was to actually upset the World Champ here tonight! I don’t think a debuting superstar has ever done that!" Pizaz hops to his feet, stepping on AJ’s chest and flexing to the loudly booing crowd as the ref goes down to count! ON--KICKOUT! AJ wastes no time to get the shoulder up, nearly knocking Pizaz over in the process! Pizaz growls and reaches down, grabbing AJ by the neck and dragging him to his feet! AJ surprises him, knocking his hands away and punching him in the face! Pizaz stumbles back, taking another hit, and then a knife edge chop which sounds through the arena as the fans wince! AJ grabs his hands and whips him into the ropes, catching him on the way back and quickly hoisting him up into a jumping Death Valley Driver! Mike: "HE KILLED HIM!!! Oh my god! That had to be the most brutal move I’ve ever saw! No way is Pizaz walking away from that! AJ Trinidad, using the momentum of his running, just planted Mike Pizaz with a jumping DVD!" Jay: "Ha! Pizaz just got knocked outta the ball park! And Trinidad had best get to the cover quickly before this kid comes to!" Trinidad gets to his feet, rolling Pizaz over. As he drops to his knees to ready the cover, "Mouth full of Poison" by Kittie blasts up over the PA and Callisto Bane steps out onto the stage! The crowd goes silent as AJ hops to his feet, approaching the ropes and watching as she slowly approaches the ring! Mike: "And the boss lady has some sort of business to attend to tonight! Let’s pray she’s happy with our performance!" Jay: "Dear Lord… May you bless us with Chryste against Jade being a nudity rules match so that I can die a happy man for my poor perform--!" A loud hit is heard as Jay cries out! Callisto’s music stops as she ignores AJ, and walks around to the opposite side of the ring, confronting Amira… The two begin to speak as AJ turns to Pizaz, his eyes half fixed on the two at ringside. Mike: "And Trinidad seemingly more focused on everything but the match at hand! He best not take Pizaz to lightly and finish this while he can!" Jay: "Shh! I’m eavesdropping here damn it! Can’t hear much, something about missing someone, needing her back? OSW!? What’s she saying about OSW!?" SLAP! "OWWWWWW!" Mike: "Amira lost her job over all this, wanna lose yours too!? Commentate!" The three all start to exchange words, with Amira between the ring and Callisto! Out in the audience, we see a fan jump the barrier, holding a sign that reads "The Anti, Anti!" Jay: "Look! Mike Pizaz slowly awakens, sitting up as he looks around the ring… slowly crawling over to the corner, he grabs up the ball bat as Chryste grabs the ref by his collar, half pulling him over the ropes! Pizaz stands to his feet, quickly making his way across the ring, to where he nails AJ Trinidad in the back of the head with the bat, causing the World Champ to slump and fall to the mat! Trinidad got ripped! Regardless however, Pizaz will go down in the books as the big winner tonight, and it’s a big one at that!" Pizaz hops up, heading to the backstage with his arms raised to a massive booing! AJ slowly gets to his feet, looking around confused before turning to Amira and Callisto! She wasn’t leaving AJ as he would eventually think, but instead she had to calm Callisto down. Unlike AJ this woman was more of a threat. Winner of Match Two: Mike Pizaz Mike: "Dear God what just happened!?" Jay: "Geeze..I dunno but if AJ Trinidad can be suspended than so can we so I am staying out of this one."
