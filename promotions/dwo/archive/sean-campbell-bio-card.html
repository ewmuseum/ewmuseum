DWO Superstars — Sean Campbell

Out of Ring Stats Nickname: "The Rising Star" Height: 5'11" Weight: 235 Age: 19 Birthday: 1982 Hometown: Toronto, Canada In Ring Stats Theme: "Renegades of Funk" by Rage Against The Machine Face/Heel/Neutral: Popular, Popular Face Finisher: Rising Star Press Description: A frontward backflip from the top rope. Sean lands on his opponent with a huge body splash. Favorite Moves: Diving reverse DDT, capture suplex, twisting hurricrana, corkscrew elbow drop Bio: "The Rising Star" Sean Campbell grew up in the depths of Toronto, Canada, with his parents and two sisters. His father was an abusive drunk. "The Rising Star" had no other alternative but to take a baseball bat to his father after hitting his mother continueously throughout the years. The assault with a deadly weapon caused "The Rising Star" to be locked up in juvenille hall for 13 months. After getting out, Sean Campbell quickly quit school and got a well paying job, raising his mother and two sisters throughout his teenage years. Sean lost the job for being caught with "herbal refreshments". He soon found another job setting up rings for a small promotion called Elite Wrestling Alliance. EWA owner Ron Irvine saw the young 18 year old performing twisting planchas and various other high flying tactics off the top rope, and saw that he discovered a star. Sean Campbell debuted as "The Rising Star", and was defeated by Johnny Idaho, a very good friend of Sean Campbell's. Sean moved onto Extreme Hardcore Wrestling, where he rised to popularity with his cousin Jerry Campbell, and became one of the most enjoyable promos in the business. In only his second week, "The Rising Star" captured the International Title from Nick Dangerous and Chris Christ in a three way match. Sean still wrestles for Extreme Hardcore Wrestling, and is still the International Champion. He's also a very close friend of Prez Thug's, and is an important part of the EHW Creative Team.
