Damian — History

2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
History Personality Saying Damian is not a 'people person' would be the understatement of the century. He trusts, with the exception of Samantha, absolutely no one. Damian isn't very talkative either. In fact, it's a rarity to hear Damian speak. Samantha pretty much says everything for him. On the rare occasion Damian does speak it's very likely that someone will be killed, maimed, or destroyed mentally. Damian, as well as Samantha's, intelligence is WELL above average and they have both spent long, tedious amounts of time studying phychology so that combined, they can put a human being through mental hell. Damian's senses (smell, sight, hearing, and touch) were unusually high when he was born and once he was old enough to understand the advantage to this he put nearly endless hours of work into further developing them. He can now recognize someone whom he has met's scent, his sight is slightly under that of a cat, and his hearing is slightly below that of a dog. This allows Damian to have a huge success at avoiding attacks and ,while wrestling, reversing moves. Damian is a serial killer and has nearly mastered it's art. He loves to put humans through agony. His weapon of choice is a hammer. He uses it well and brings it everywhere, keeping it in his trench coat. History Damian Allen Wesley was born in Philadelphia, Pennsylvania on October 31st, 1981 to Jonathan and Mary Wesley. He was the middle aged of three children. His younger sister Maria was two years younger and his elder brother Andrew was two years older. Damian's young life was a happy one. He was shown love and kindness by his parents and grandparents, whom lived with Damian and his family. He loved his grandparents above all others and learned so much from them both. They loved him as he loved them. He loved life. But his life took a drastic turn for the worse when Damian was eight. On January 22, 1990 his grandfather died of a heart attack and two weeks later, overcome by grief his grandmother slit her own throat. The two had a funeral next to each other. Damian's life was never the same. Damian's father fell into a deep depression, which quickly lead to alcoholism. He lost his job within two months of his parents' demise and did not look for a new one. He began to beat on Damian, as he blamed him for his parents' death, because of how close he was to them. Damian, for the first time in his short life felt what it was like to be truly hopeless. None of his family would help him and he did nothing for four years. But on April 22, 1994 he had taken all he could. After a particularly brutal beating from his father involving a belt and empty bottle of vodka Damian took a hammer from his father's toolbox and bashed his family's brains in, one after another. In an attempt to keep himself out of trouble, Damian opened a deep gash in his hand, rubbed his blood all over the house, front door, and sidewalk, faking his own death. Remarkably, it worked. But Damian had nothing, he lived on the streets for two years, stealing all the meals he could. But when he was 14, Damian, who looked quite a bit older than he really was, got a job at a local liquor store. The owner let him sleep in the back. By this time Damian had discovered a dark lifestyle. One that he fitted into perfectly. That of the Goth's. He embraced bands like Bauhaus, Sisters of Mercy, Alien Sex Fiend, and Bile. He went to every club and show he could afford, which was actually quite a few. But he made few friends in the Gothic Subculture. That was okay, the three he did make were well enough. Especially, one girl, a girl named Samantha... Damian worked in the liquor store for two years. He and Samantha became best friends, spending every moment they could together, as they found one another fascinating, in ways neither of them could understand. Quickly the relationship turned into a romantic one and the two fell deeply in love. Damian was fired and kicked out of the liquor store when the owner discovered him stealing candy, booze, and smokes. Luckily Samantha had a home that she shared with two other ladies. She insisted that Damian stayed there. He did, begrudgingly, as he hated her roommates. He would pretty much stay in Samantha's room coming out only to go to the bathroom and leave. Damian's murderous feelings did anything but die with his family. He killed many, many people, with the same hammer he used on his family. He was calculating and a master at the art of homicide. The police have never been able to catch Damian. Or even target him as a suspect. But Samantha did not know Damian's secret. She found out one night. But in doing so, she revealed to Damian her secret. On September 22, 1999 Damian awoke to a blinding pain in his neck. He quickly realized what was going on, Samantha was trying to eat him. He pushed her away, evidently awaking her from her 'sleep cannibalism'. She began to sob, he began to laugh, telling her HIS secret, the to became even closer, and she became his partner. Eating all of his victims, further confusing the police. Damian's hatred grew for the whores he was forced to live with. He begged Samantha to let him kill them but she forbade it. So Damian needed a job. Because of his appearance it was difficult to get a high paying job. But Samantha found a place Damian might fit into. The world of pro wrestling.... Damian had never watched wrestling in his life but, needing money he did indeed join the PWA. He graduated in no time and moved on to NTICW. It closed before he could have a match. So onto CHZ. It closed too. To PURE. Closed. Oh well, Damian had made a nice amount of cash and took a break. Now that cash is more or less gone. So after a long hiatus Damian returns to wrestling. And enters the world of the DWO.
