Thursday Night Travesty — the card, 9 October 2003

LIVE from Wilmington, Delaware! October 9, 2003 After a one week break due to coming off of a highly violent pay-per-view event the DWO is now back on track and coming full force to Wilmington, Delaware! The line up we have for this crowd is sure to be heart stopping, and full of high octane, pulse racing action! With the injury list high for the end of the month leading into October you're going to be seeing many more debuts than you normally would in one show. But hey, things happen.. The list of debuting wrestlers isn't exactly shit material! We've got names such as Seymour Almasy, Jason Warrior, Reality/Mayhem, and Michelle Raines coming to you live this upcoming week. Should be entertaining to say the least, this will not be a show to miss. With so many grudges coming off of the pay-per-view anything is likely to happen! The DWO is thrilled to say the least at being able to house two of the recently deceased LOW's main stars on our show this week. We did a recent poll among fans and it seems that they wanted to see two LOW stars battle it out on DWO television for the first time ever. Well this week the fans are getting their wish! We've got Jason Warrior and Reality/Mayhem taking it to the ring in their debut on DWO T.V.! For two weeks now the fans of this establishment has been plagued by the being known as Seymour Almasy, for these two weeks he has sent messages to the DWO about ending our rein of sin on this earth. This week he faces off against a member of Fates Calling and a possible member of Fates Calling. Christian Darke and Gual look to over throw Almasy and prove to him that the DWO is not something to fuck around with.. Michelle Raines, a new addition to the DWO looking to make his debut this week on Travesty. Kaos, the new number one contender for the DWO Pride Title will look to take out Michelle in the early goings of this match no doubt. But with the current Pride Champion commentating during the match Kaos will have to keep his eyes watching his back as much as watching his front. Regin could easily want to get in on the action and prove to Kaos just why he is the Pride Champion! Hecate, Arsenic.. Both competitors looking to up their ranks by winning the DWO Tag Team Titles. In order to do this they must defeat Damian and Roulette in a fair match. Can it be done? This week we see these two teams meet one more time, tag team titles on the line in what could turn into an all out brawl inside of the ring. Can Hecate and Arsenic keep their cool long enough to pin legally? Who knows.. Over the past weeks Timothy Marcus has become a huge thorn in the sides of Callisto and Lexis Bane. Starting out as an outside informant for a suspecting Lexis, Timothy fed her lie after lie about Callisto's fiascos with former DWO star, now missing, Chryste. Timothy has already been punished by being made to face Hecate.. Now he will be further punished by facing an opponent he does not even know! Bane has informed all staff Timothy is not to know who his opponent is until he is in the ring and the bell has rung. This could prove a breaking point for Timothy, will he crumble.. Will he overcome Bane's challenges? Tune in to see! October 9, 2003 Results
