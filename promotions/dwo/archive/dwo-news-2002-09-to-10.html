DWO Updates at Monday 7th October, 2002 by Callisto The DWO has undergone some updates in the past two days that you might want to check out. The links have been updated, some taken off and some added. Some of the ones added are the D-Team information page as well as the shopzone page which is still under construction. The roster has been updated, some cuts have taken place, but some additions have also risen. The DWO would like to welcome all of its new members, along with its newest staff members. We are still looking for DWO results writers so if you can spare time to write on a regular basis then please post a reply to the topic on the OOC forum. Even if you are a passer by to the DWO and would like to be a writer, sign up we would appreciate it. If you have any ideas for merchandise for the shopzone, or ideas, comments about anything in general just post it on the OOC forum. Thanks!
