DWO Champions, June 2002

8 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Recent Updates: None as of right now. Heavyweight Championship John Miro Won- 4.21.02 Heavyweight Champion..Sure it may sound nice to you. But making it to the top to be in the running for this title is brutal. The few that have had the honor of owning this Title have had their fair share of beatings. In order to obtain this title you have to prove to the Owners, the roster, and the fans that you have the talent, the courage, and the know how to uphold the meaning of the Heavyweight Title. Extreme Championship Switchblade Won- 5.23.02 So you think that a shot at the Extreme Title is a walk in the park? Well you have another thing coming. Imagine your perfect body being slammed through eight inches of glass table. Or your body being dropped off a thirty foot cage. Or even your body being tossed in to a pit of acid. Still think this title is a walk in the park? Didn't think so. Intercontiental Championship Angelus Won- 3.25.02 So you want a shot at the IC title? Just because it's the third title down the line doesn't mean shit to the gold hungry men and women out there. You try taking the beating delivered from the top of a cage. Try taking on all the men and women inside of the royal rumble, and Try to come out on top in a match where the odds are stacked against you. Still want a shot? Tag Team Championships Gina and Angelus Won- 5.16.02 The Tag Team titles are the symbol of determination on the part of a team of fighters. To win these titles you must prove that you can work with a partner inside the ring to defeat the opposition. Women's Championship Kristi Kiss Won- 5.23.02 The Women's Championship, last but not least. Some federations look down on this title, but until you have been inside of the ring with the women of the DWO you have no idea how hardcore this title really is. You want a shot? Think you can handle stepping in to the ring with one of the DWO's women? Think again. Number One Contenders Heavyweight Championship- Lethal Extreme Championship- TBA Intercontiental Championship- TBA Tag Team Championships- TBA Women's Championship- Lana Day
