DWO News, May to June 2002

2002
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
50% Owner? With Callisto Bane away on personal business, she hasn't had much time to deal with federation matters..Or has she? As it seems Callisto Bane has struck a deal with FAW's British Bomber that leaves her as a fifty percent owner of Revolution Wrestling. As we speak a contract is being drawn up that will seal the deal between these two Federation owners. When Callisto settles in as half the company owner the DWO will be using RW as a feeder fed to get more talent in to the DWO. And from what we hear, RW talent is elite. Look for more on that topic. Amira Medical Update As we all know, Amira was injured in her match up with Chryste last Thursday when a bomb exploded under her head! What we do not know as of right now is the condition of Amira thus far. Here in the DWO news room we have reporters all over the place looking to get an update on her. So far there has been no luck, but we hope to get some information soon. Stay tuned to find out more. Angelus Gone? Well recently one of our superstars has dropped DWO and seemingly moved on. The man known as Angelus left the ranks for unknown, and what Stryker would call..Unreasonable reasons. We have yet to speak with the departed Angelus but we have news reporters headed to his home to get some answers. More as it becomes availble. Callisto in OSW? Callisto Bane has been receiving many invitations as of late to supply her talent elsewhere and not just as Owner of the DWO. Her wrestling abilities are being called upon to be used for a federation widely known as Old School Wrestling, aka OSW. This Federation is one of the elite, and ranked accordingly in WT. We will give you more news on her decisions, moves, and any plans as they come about. FAW-DWO Show? As many of you have noticed, the DWO is not getting along well with FAW. Now it is not all of FAW, nor all of DWO but merely a few rowdy members of each federation looking to butt heads. So far the DWO will be facing two FAW members, Stryker and Syphon X and then of course our newer female, Chryste taking on the Owner of FAW, British Bomber. Callisto Bane is in works with British Bomber to come to an agreement on where to host both matches. Callisto Bane has suggested a FAW-DWO Show, a sort of one night only deal. Nothing more has been mentioned so we will keep you posted on breaking news. Major Changes The DWO heads have met together and all agree that some major changes will be taking place soon. Roster cuts , news designs, more elite fighters, and of course better ratings. The heads of the DWO had this meeting of sorts last week at the monthly get together they call 'business friendly'. Callisto Bane was of course present, taking time away from her family to deal with Federation matters. She too thought changes were in order, she was caught stating that the changes would ensure the well being of the DWO and its future. More will be touched upon soon. PTC Ratings and how to help Now we all know about the DWO making it in to the PTC Networks, but how many of us really know how to help us get great ratings? Now you will, because the DWO news team is about to break it down for you. To help the DWO get rating high you must become active on the PTC forums, this doesn't mean just the trash talk forums, this means ALL of the forums. Mainly the General Conversation forum, and those like it. Phantom Booker looks for people in Federations who are active all around in PTC as well as in the DWO. This is how you make it to the PTC top ten lists and such. Now also you can submit news to Callisto Bane at [detail withheld] to be posted on the main PTC page. If you see something happening in the DWO, or could possibly happen in the DWO, send it to her! This means if you have some secret that is going to take place that could take people by suprise then submit it to Callisto and she will stick it up as rumors. If you have some news concerning you or someone else you are affiliated with then submit it to Callisto and she will post it under the news. Be active and we will become one of the best PTC has to offer. Callisto cannot do it by herself! Possible Partnership Most of the DWO should know already that British Bomber, the Owner of FAW is not liked around the locker rooms of some of the DWO fighters. Be this as it may, Callisto Bane and British Bomber have been meeting often about business matters concerning RW, DWO, and FAW. This may come to a shock to most, but they could possibly be forming a partnership that could rock PTC to its very core! Nothing more is known on this topic at the moment, but more will be announced as we get the breaks. PTC Network Spot! June 11, 2002 - Rain Riley Finally another PTC Network spot has opened up and the DWO is in the top running. As a matter of fact, the DWO is TOP in the running. DWO needs YOUR help to keep us in the top and to help us grasp the network and make it ours. All of you go to prime time central and post on the forums under the topic about who should get GW's network spot. The address there is.. http://www.primetimecentral.net Make sure to support us! Doppelganger caught? June 11, 2002 - Rain Riley If you haven't kept up with the recent happenings in the DWO, then you should be informed that an Amira look-a-like has been on the rampage. This woman has been in the locker rooms of all the DWO women and men, trying to soil the reputation of our true Grunge Queen Amira! The true Amira did her research and as it seems, put this woman 'out of action'. Will any further legal action take place? We'll see. AJ Trinidad coming home. June 10, 2002 - Kerry Sims Our heavyweight champion, AJ Trinidad, is making his way home back to the DWO after a recent split with his significant other. The DWO would like to express their heartfelt concern for the current heartache in AJ's life, but wish him the best of luck with his return to the DWO. Callisto and Lexis.. June 10, 2002 - Rain Riley Love on the rocks? Perhaps..But we're not jumping to conclusions here on the DWO staff. We haven't seen these two together in quite some time and well it is beginning to puzzle our fans as to why. We have yet to get a comment from Callisto Bane or Lexis on this topic, but the current status between Callisto and her 'others' may say all that needs to be said. More as it develops. Twin Amira's? June 6, 2002 - Rain Riley The possibility of this is higher now than we suspected before. We new something was up when Amira made the move on Gina Muniz at the DWO House Party, but as of now the rumor of a twin amira is proving to be true. We saw the stand off between the pair on Lord of the Asylum, the DWO ppv, but the doppelganger Amira knocked the real Amira out. She then proceeded to start chaos when she publically raised a hand to the DWO Owner, Callisto Bane. This doppelganger has got to be stopped, and the real Amira,..Well she has some explaining to do to the Owner. But to make it more trouble on Amira, Callisto has sent a formal invitation to Amira for a meeting to be held between them in her office. What could come of this 'meeting'? And will Amira have to suffer the consequences of her Doppelgangers trouble causing habits? Triple champ? June 6, 2002 - Kerry Sims The DWO now has a problem with a certain title exchange. It seems that three women now own the right to call themselves the DWO Women's Champion. Here is the break down.. At LOA this past week Kristi Kiss[C] was taking on Lana Day for the women's title. Now Lana pinned Kristi Kiss to win the title, BUT then Jaslene Myst came down and won the title from Lana Day. And then! Kristi Kiss won the title back, but there was no pin. The officials of the DWO are stumped with what to do, but we hope to sort out this misunderstanding soon enough. Development June 6, 2002 - Kerry Sims The DWO has undergone some changes in the past week. Is has been rumored, and now finalized that the DWO would be changing up the D-Team. As of late we have seen more organization among their ranks, and we can only hope to see more in the weeks to come. I suppose we will see if they can all work together at the upcoming Travesty. We on the reporting staff wish them the best of luck. House Show? June 5, 2002 - Rain Riley Well we are finally seeing the development of the DWO House Show: Relentless come about. It has been anticipated for a long time, and it is great to see it about to go in to full effect. We have heard that Callisto is settling a few more legal matters with networks and such before full broadcasting will begin. More to come on the topic when it develops. Kristi Kiss and Duke Williams? May 26, 2002 - Rain Riley What is up with Kristi Kiss and Duke Williams? That is a question that is circling around the mind of many DWO'ers as of late. Kristi Kiss, the new Women's Champion, kissed Duke Williams in the ring Thursday night, could that have been just pure happiness about her lead over the other women? But then again we did see them at the party over at Callisto's house, and things seemed to be heating up in the mud match. But then! We see the pair dart off upstairs toward the showers..More steamy news as follows. Armandus' Return to the Ring. May 24, 2002 - Rain Riley Put out of action at the hands of Callisto Bane, there has been talk that the giant Armandus Paraxades may be making his return to the ring soon. This event has been much anticipated by fans of the golden eye beast. We have been trying frantically to catch a word with Armandus but he is nowhere to be found! Look for more as it develops. Callisto in the street fight? May 24, 2002 - Rain Riley Rumors have spred like wild fire that the owner of the DWO will be stepping in to a street fight hosted by another Federation. Well the rumors are true! Callisto Bane has signed her name on a contract that enters her in the GW Street Fight for the Street Fight Championship. She will be face to face with many of the greats around PTC. Good luck to her.. Possible Alliance... May 23, 2002 - Kerry Sims Around the backstage area of the DWO as well as the fan area many people have been talking about two men..AJ Trinidad and Switchblade. These two men have become the top of DWO chatter simply because there is a possibility of an Alliance being born. Kristi Kiss has also been joined in to the possibile members ranks as many of the fans believe she will side with them after what happened on Travesty with Seth Valentine. More shall follow. Tag team on the horizon May 23, 2002 - Kerry Sims Around the DWO and in Callisto Banes office we reporters have heard talk of a tag team coming around in the near future. We haven't yet gotten the scoop on just who will be the participants in this tag team forming, but we hope to find more as news develops.
