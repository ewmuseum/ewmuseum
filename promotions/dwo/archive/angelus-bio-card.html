Out of Ring Stats Gender: Male Nickname: The Guilty One Height: 6'7" Weight: 273 Age: 23 Birthday: 28.06.1978 Hometown: Bel Air Out of Ring Stats Theme: 'Bodies' by Drowning Pools Face/Heel/Neutral: Face Finisher: Dead Center Description: Double Moonsault (Flips over backwards twice before hitting the opponent) Favorite Moves: Spinning heel kick, Top rope DDT, Sudden Death(Edge-O-Matic), Spear, Stadning Dropkick, Reverse DDT(like Christians), Twisting Body attack, VanTerminator, Rolling Thunder, Single leg drop kick (like RVD), Dangerous DDT, Multiple Chokeslams (more and more chokeslams one after another), Cartwheel to standing Moonsault, Spinning Belly to Belly suplexes, Multiple Belly to belly suplexes Bio: When he found the perfect career for his level of fitness he decided to join a wrestling training camp called the NCAA. Where such brutes as Brock Lesnar worked. He was trained in a new and unusual style to use the best of his martial arts and high flying skills. Then He made his shocking debut under the name of Dark Angel in The Dynasty wrestling. He never actually won a championship there and he was then made redundant when the company collapsed. So he decided to look elsewhere. This is how he found himself inside the DWO.
