About the DWO

2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Recent Updates: None as of right now. The Dominant Wrestling On-Line Federation was established January twenty-fifth, 2001 with the intent of bringing unrivaled Entertainment to the masses. Thus forward it has brought such entertainment to people around the globe. This brought about the term "Global Domination". Anyway, back to the task at hand which is explaining about the DWO. The DWO was founded by Callisto Bane, a very intelligent athlete, and a very rich business owner. At the age of Twenty-Four Callisto Bane has successfully risen up and taken the reigns of control. With a dedicated staff and a working roster the DWO is proving to be one of the Elite. With the many Wrestling Federations popping up as of late one may wonder just what makes the DWO so different. One word will explain all. Originality. This is something everyone in the DWO fold strives for. Callisto Bane taught from day one that this simple task was the one way to maintain true star status inside of the DWO. With this task set before the roster the writers, and the staff a hard journey was ahead. But unlike all of the other Federations sprouting up, the DWO showed perfected skill and is still one of the top Wrestling Federations to date. With the daunting task of running such a skilled Federation one might wonder just how everything works. The idea is that the entire Federation works together just as a well oiled machine would. Everyone works together to perfect what the DWO does best, and that is put on one hell of a show. Each man, woman, and staff member gives one-hundred percent effort and the machine remains running at top standards. Now down to the basics about the DWO. The Dominant Wrestling On-Line Federation Official Head-Quarters is located in the heart of Providence Rhode Island. With it's massive steel structure and floors, it is the definition of dominant stature in the small state. The DWO building is separated in to four main sections. Each section contains four stories. Below is a small overview of all four sections. Computer Tech Sanction: This is the very first floor of the DWO official Head-Quarters. In this specific part of the Building skilled DWO technicians are hard at work designing, graphing, and other important tasks given by the front office. Without the work of these skilled few the DWO would be a bit on the bleak side of the Wrestling World. They audit the themes, Video's, and PPV logo's. Main Office Sanction: The second section of the DWO Official Head-Quarters building is the place where the staff members/office members are stationed. The staff members of the DWO range from ring workers all the way up to the CEO of the company. Here they each have their own private offices equipped with the best luxuries money can possibly buy. Each staff member has a certain job inside of the DWO and they all work together to complete those jobs with minimum set backs. Presidential Sanction: Here the President of the DWO resides when doing things such as paper work and other tedious tasks. As the president of such an established company as the DWO their job can be very stressful. For this reason the President is equipped with personal secretaries, staff members, and workers of their very own. It's sweet being the President. Founder/Owner Sanction: Now this is the very top floor of the DWO official Building. In this top section of the building resides the very founder and owner of the DWO company. Yes, Callisto Bane. Behind double doors reaching to the ceiling and made of beautiful oak, finished to a shine sits in a leather chair behind an oak desk the maker of the DWO. Here she conducts various meetings and corresponds with many of the DWO affiliates. It is here that most of the creative juices flow. And there you have it, the DWO building mainly simplified in to a moderate explanation. Now that you have a little information on the Company known as the DWO you should consider joining our ranks. Now on to the more basic ideals of the DWO. Here in the DWO we have one show a week for purposes that the work put in to the federation and the shows should all be enough for only one show a week. It is named Travesty, and it is held on Thursday nights at six p.m. central standard time. Any and every promo for Travesty must be in before six p.m. central standard time or it does not count for shit inside of the ring. Next we have a pay-per-view every month named Lord of the Asylum. Each time it has the same name, but a different idea to keep it original and interesting to the masses. Here in the DWO interviews are always conducted. Fighters have access to interviews any time of the day or night. That is really the production side of the DWO so there isn't much more to explain as basic. But if you have the skill, the determination, and the passion for stardom then sign an application today and be on your way to "Global Domination".
