What really happend? — 30 September 2002

30 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What really happend? at Monday 30th September, 2002 by Callisto In that sponsor break? This is what happend.. This is what happened.. We go back to the ring area where the lights dim down, and again the same video plays just like the one from three weeks ago. This time the video is alot shorter, instead it just shows the images of the three people stood together, then instead of "They Are Coming" flashing on the tron we have "They Are Here!"......the lights go pitch black, then Red, White and Blue pyros explode.. Faith by Limp Bizkit hits and the fans go wild, the image re-appears on the tron to show Courtney Charisma Carter, Kathryn Velmont-Thomas and The British Bomber. The three burst out onto the stage to the fans delight, Bomber stands at the top of the ramp in the middle and looks down at the floor, he raises one arm followed by the other. Kathryn and Courtney stand either side of Bomber with their hands on their hips. Bomber starts jumping around pumping the fans even more, they start making their way to the ring and Bomber rolls in, he holds the ropes open for Kathryn and Courtney to get in then after they do he signals for a mic from the ring announcer and gets handed one, he looks around the fans then nods smiling. British Bomber: Yo, Whats up Las Vegas! The fans cheer again for Bomber, then chants of "Bomber" start to come from the crowd. Bomber again stands in the middle of the ring as they quieten down. British Bomber: You know, a few months ago I said a few things that some feel I shouldn't have, well I thought about that and I think at the time I was defending the place I wrestled in, and I had to but now I have left that steaming pile of crap they call FAW and joined the best federation there is out there. But nearly a month after signing the DWO contract Callisto Bane gave to me I have made an appearance, but you guys didnt know that obviously because it would have given it all away. He climbs out the ring and looks underneath it, he pulls out a sledgehammer then rolls back in with it. British Bomber: Now three weeks ago you may have seen one of these just lying around next to someone. Well when I signed with the DWO I decided to bring my toy with me, and after sorting a couple of problems out I sort of forgot it and left it on the scene.....didn't I Chryste? The crowd give Bomber a mixed response, more cheers than boo's for him. He gives a sadistic smile. British Bomber: Why did I do it? I wanted to prove to people like Chryste that I am not in the DWO to make up the numbers, but that I am here to make a example out of such 'big time players' as Chryste, Jackson Rust, Draven and people like that. Bottom line is that the time is now, its time to revolutionise the DWO. Bomber walks over to the turnbuckle and looks at the fans, he looks at the top balcony then all the way to the front row. British Bomber: These people havent come here to see assholes like Draven and Chryste, they have come here to see TRUE athletes like myself, Kathryn and Courtney. See we chose the DWO for a reason, we didnt go to AW, NGW, XCWF or some other old peoples home like that, we chose the DWO because of the chance to preform for the great fans night in night out. And now that we are here, we will do so because we love you all! Bomber hands Kathryn the mic and gives her a kiss on the cheek then walks over to the turnbuckle and sits on the top one. But before Kathryn can say anything Courtney snatches the microphone from her, Bomber and Kathryn look >on confused as Coutrney begins to talk in a harsh tone. Courtney: Lana, Lana Lana. I can't believe you could do such a thing. Attacking me on Travesty. How could somebody like you have the audecity to attack a beautiful, courageous, charismatic, and a hell of a better wrestler then you. But listing my qualities on my fingers isn't going to get me anywhere. How could you after I set you up with.... She pauses, hesitantly at Bomber before turning to Kathryn. Courtney laughs sadistically, and looks to the floors as she begins to talk again. Courtney: Well you know. So what's up with that? But hey, doesn't bother me. It should bother you however Lana. Let's just say that when your all alone in the dark. I will be right there breathing down your neck, making your hair stand on end, but when you turn around. I won't be there. She walks to the ropes opposite the mega tron and leans against them... Whispering she talks once more. Courtney: So Lana, Watch Out. Cause I'll be watching you, and that little thing with you and you know who, don't worry that'll be our little secret. She laughs sadistically as Faith by Limp Bizkit hits as Bomber rolls out the ring pissed off, he looks up at Courtney as Kathryn looks on confused as we fade out. But why wasn't it aired? Because Callisto Bane hadn't been sure if all lines of contract previous to that of the DWO was cleared. But upon speaking with Bomber and his mates, it has been found that he and them are officially in the DWO and under contract. Which is why this clip is now being alowed to air. Back to Main
