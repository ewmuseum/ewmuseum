DWO Superstars — John Miro

Out of Ring Stats Gender: Male Nickname: The Eagle Height: 6'3" Weight: 231 lbs. Age: 26 Birthdate: March 3rd, 1976 Hometown: Phoenix, Arizona In Ring Stats Theme: "The Unforgiven" by Metallica Alignment: Neutral, edging on face Finisher: The Final Chapter Description: John first swings his opponent into the ropes. When he comes back, John then kicks him in the gut, to stun him, and in one fluid motion, John rolls over his back as his opponent stands and wraps his right arm around his opponents head. The momentum slingshots John downward, smashing his opponents nose in a modified Running DDT. Five Favorite moves: German Suplex, Dragon Suplex, Northern Lights Suplex, Superkick, Guillotine leg drop. Bio: John Miro was born Pierce Jonathan Delmiro in Phoenix, Arizona. Has two older sisters, and one younger brother. Anna Delmiro, Alison Delmiro, and Jacob Delmiro. His mother, Lynn Delmiro, and father, Paul Delmiro. They lived in a middle-classed, typical home. Their income was not small, not large. Enough to keep the family in tact. At the age of eighteen, John suprised his parents, telling them his dream was to be in a ring, wrestling. His father was outraged, his mother more than this. John, sick of the actions at home, went off, as well as his brother. Jacob and John soon learning the game of becoming a wrestler, and joined together to form a tag team. They lasted in the AWWF for 35 plus days, until Jacob's knee was severly injured. John soon quit the AWWF, not wanting to pursue a singles career. But now, he has landed in the DWO, and starting to fufill that dream once more.
