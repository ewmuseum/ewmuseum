DWO Gimmick Matches

2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Recent Updates: None as of right now. Normal Match- Two wrestlers. Normal wrestling rules apply. Tag Team Match- Two teams of two wrestlers. Normal tag rules apply. Six Man Tag Match- Two teams of three wrestlers. Normal tag rules apply. Three Way Dance- Three men in the ring at the same time. Elimination rules apply. Cage Match- Extreme match. A steel cage with no roof encases the ring. The first wrestler to climb to the top of the cage and climb down to the floor with both feet on the floor is the winner. Barbed Wire Match- Extreme match. Ropes are replaced with barbed wire. First wrestler to secure the pinfall or submission wins. Strap/Chain Match- Wrestlers are connected by a strap or chain at the wrist. Extreme rules apply. The first wrestler to secure the pinall or submission wins. Tag Team Match- Two teams of two wrestlers. Normal tag rules apply. Tornado Match- Two teams of 2, 3, or 4 wrestlers. All men are in the ring at the same time. Elimination rules apply. Four Corners Match- Four single wrestlers, or teams of wrestlers, occupy the four corners of the ring. Anyone in the ring can tag anyone outside the ring. Elimination rules apply. For teams, when one member of team is pinned or submits, the entire team is eliminated. Extreme Match- No rules. The match can go anywhere inside, or outside, the building. The wrestlers can use any weapon they can find. Pinfalls and submissions count anywhere. Table Match-Extreme match. The winner is the wrestler (or team) that can put ONE opponent through a table. If more than one wrestler (or team) is involved, it will be elimination. Hell in a Cell- Extreme match. A cage with a roof encases the ring. The cage is not connected to the ring apron, but extends several feet beyond the edges of the ring and touches the concrete floor. Winner is the first person to get a pin or submission. Ladder Match- Extrme rules. A title, prize, or weapon will be placed on a cable hanging from the ceiling of the arena. The first wrestler to climb a ladder and take the object from the cable will be the winner. If it is a weapon, then the first wrestler to secure a pinfall or submission will be the winner. Hardcore Match-Anything goes in this match. No DQ, interferance allowed in this type of match. Weapons are allowed. The only way to win is by pin or submission. Ladder Match-A Ladder is needed to pull off a win here. The ladder can be brought to the ring, or anywhere in the arena. Weapons can be used here. This type of match is mostly for championship matches. The belts are hung above the ring and the person to climb the ladder and get the belt wins. Strap Match-The two, or sometimes three, wrestlers are connected by a leather strap. Weapons can be used. Way to win is by pin or submission. Table Match- A match where are must put your opponent through a table to win, and the ref must see it also. So if the ref doesn't see you put him/her through a table, guess what, you have to do it again. Weapons allowed. Casket Match- A match where you must put your opponent in a casket and shut the lid to pick up the victory. I Quit Match-A match where you must make him/her say I quit. Weapons are allowed. Last Man Standing-A match where if you are knocked down and not up by the count of ten, by the ref, you loose! Weapons and interfereance is allowed. Stairway to Hell Match-A ladder is needed. A singapore cane is hung above the ring and the only way to get it is by climbing the ladder. The first to hit the other with a singapore cane wins. First Blood Match-The opponent to draw first blood looses. Weapons allowed. Steel Cage Match-A cage surrounds the ring. Weapons are allowed. First to climb out of the cage wins. Anywhere Fall-Falls count anywhere, outside, under the ring, in the crowds, in the bathroom, anywhere! Inferno Match- Fire surroudns the ring. First to catch on fire looses the match and some skin. Caged Stretcher match- This match involves an open cage around the ring and there is a stretcher outside the ring. There can be any number of wepons in the ring but the referee has the right to get rid of wepons he feels are to violent. To win you must have your opponent stretcherd out of the arena. Note if your opponent is able to get off the stretcher before he leaves the arena then the match must continue the cage is not pad locked! Barbed Wire Table Match-Good match for the Extreme guys. The ring is surrounded and covered in barbed wire.Ropes in barbed wire, turnbuckles, and the wrestlers can even put some around a chair or 2x4. Tables are inside the ring, and outside the ring. The only way to win is by putting your opponent through a table. Hardcore Flamethrower Match- Very high risking match. Hardcore rules, and the only way to win is by setting your opponent on fire! Barbed Wire Strap Match-Same rules and win modifiers as a strap match, only the strap is barbed wire! Gateway to Hell match-A gateway to hell match is a match where there is a cage(the type of cage that is used in a Steel Cage Match with no top) around the ring. The opponets are in the ring of course and there is weapons inside the ring. Outside of the cage, there is flaming tables everywhere. The only way to win is by actually putting your opponet through the flaming tables. That means both men up on the top of the cage at the same time. But they will start at the bottom. Unforgiven match-No rules, interferance allowed. Three tables are in the ring. One on fire, one covered in broken glass and the other in barbed wire! Put your opponent through 2 of the 3 and pick up the win. Electric Shock Match-Wires that spark and shock on impact are around the ring. First to electrify the opponent in to submission wins. Broken Glass arbed Wire Match-Barbed wire and broken glass surround the ring and cover the turnbuckles and ropes. First to pin wins. Barbed Wire fist match- Both wrestlers arms are covered in barbed wire. Pinfall victory. Boiler Room Brawl-Takes place in the boiler room. Use whatever is in there. First to escape wins. Explosion Match- Hardcore rules accept that landmines are hidden inside the ring. Barbed Wire Bat match- Baseball bats are in the ring, and they are covered in barbed wire. Hell in a Cell- A huge cage surrounds the ring, top and bottom. Cover is on the cage. Way to win is by pin. Barbed Wire Hell in a cell-Same as before, only the cage is covered in barbed wire. Fire and Broken Glass-The ring is set on fire and broken glass is in the ring. Pinfall wins. Highway Match-Crazy and insane match. Takes place on the highway! Cars passing by might hit a wrestler. Pinfall wins the match. Backstage Flamethrower Match-Taken place backstage, behind the DWO Mega Tron . Fire is set around and forms a circle. Wrestlers are inside with a ref. Pinfall victory. Limit Hell Match-A fire circle is set around the two wrestlers. Every 2 minutes, the circel of fire gets smaller. First wrestler to become a wuss and says I quit before he is set on fire looses. 20 story ladder match-8 ladders are set up 20 stories above the ring. On top are tables. Wrestlers must stand on the tables to battle. First to throw the opponent off of the ladder and down 20 stories wins. 4 way Taser Death Match-Four tasers are set on each turnbuckle. They shock every 3 minutes. Any wrestler in their way gets a painful shock! Barbwire Broken Glass Scaffold match-a scallfold match with barbwire and broken glass covering ground below! Fury Death Match- Super Scaffold(just a really big scaffold) with a 8 foot circular hole in the center. Under the circle(in the ring below the SuperScaffold) is a pile a broken glass and barbwire. Under the pile is C4 explosives. On the Super Scaffold is a cage surrounding it, just to prevent excape. The way to win is to throw your opponent through the hole onto the lethal pile 20 feet below. Vancouver Death Match-Ring canvas ripped out, and tables are put in its place. These tables are covered in thumbtacks. The ropes are completely bound in barbed wire, and the turnbuckle pads are replaced with C4 explosives. Surrounding it all is a cell. On top of the cell are 3 20-foot ladders arranged in a pyramid, and 10 feet above the top ladder is the belt. The objective is to obviously grab the belt, but you've got to put it around your waist. If you drop it, then the match continues. One Way Out Match-A huge cage surrounds the ring, top and bottom made out of barbed wire. there is a Flamethrower and wire cutters on the roof and hand cuffs on the side to lock the other player up. petrol is under the ring, and on the side is a small hole that Weapons will be put through . to win you must lock your opponent up on the wall, then put the petrol on them, then get the Flamethrower and light them up, then you must get the wire clippers and cut a hole to get out. Dungeon Death Match-3 rooms in a building are step up for war. Each room has a different theme. To win you have to PIN your opponent(s) in EVERY room. The last room is always the most dangerous. Walls, ceiling, and floor are covered with white padded cushions. Inside the cushions are strategically place land mines. Weapons in the room include: tables wrapped in barbwire and C4, boards with TNT, baseball bats covered in explosives, and shock grenades. Inferno Hell in the Cell- Hell in the cell on fire object to win is either set your opponent on fire or pin.
