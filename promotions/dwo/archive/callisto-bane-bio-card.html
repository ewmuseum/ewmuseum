DWO Superstars — Callisto Bane

2002

Out of Ring Stats Gender: Female Nicknames: The Dark Angel, The Olden Beast Height: 6'4" Weight: 285 lbs Age: 25 Birthdate: Unknown Hometown: Found in Liverpool, England but no true Hometown known Out of Ring Stats Theme: "What I've Always Wanted" by Kittie Alignment: Neutral Finisher: Sweet Illusion Finisher Description: Opponent is caught in an armbar and drug to the turnbuckle. He/She is then lifted up on to it and Callisto Leaps off behind the opponent. While in the air her opponents arm is lifted, causing them to arch and her boot heel is slammed in to the back of their neck. It creates a nasty face first slam in to the mat surface and results in istant black out or even death. Bio: Callisto Bane was found inside of Liverpool, England by a man named Jeremy Duepche. She was an orphan, and feeling sorry for her he took her in. She was trained in the arts of battle, instructed by the best to become the best. At the age of 15 she was inside of the underground fight clubs and gladiatorial pits. She has learned to kill when needed to survive, she was taught that in her last gladiator pit brawl with a man by the name of Omega. She defeated him with all odds against her to obtain the Gladiatorial Heavyweight Championship. With which she retired with. At the age of 20 she broke in to the world of Sports Entertainment, the wrestling business was booming and what better place for her to gain experience. She worked various slums and finally made it in to the big times, winning title after title until finally gaining enough publicity and money to open her own Federation. Thus DWO was born...
