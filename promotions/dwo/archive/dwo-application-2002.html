Recent Updates: None as of right now. So You want to win some DWO Gold ? Not until you're signed up. Fill out this application, and send it to us. We ask that you do not roleplay until you have been accepted. Please keep this in mind while you are filling out your application... Your application will be reviewed by Owner Callisto. If you have any questions, corrections, or comments, contact the President or Owner using the email links on the front page. Do not contact anyone else in the Front Office, as they do not review the applications, and will not answer emails dealing with them. Please be sure to read the rules, and take a look around the site to make sure that you will be able to contribute here. We do not want you to waste your time or ours. Remember, No Real Wrestlers !!! This cannot be stressed enough, as people still ignore this. We do not except real wrestlers, or real wrestler names. Be sure to give us a valid and working email address. If you do not, we have no way of contacting you and in turn your application will be disregarded. Email providers that work well are Hotmail, Lycos and Yahoo. There are many others and we do not require you to use these but it does make things easier. We ask that you give your real name on the application. We are not criminals, so there is no need for an alias. If you are new to e-fedding, or are totally new to the DWO in general, we ask that you please look through all of the pages on this site. Read the posts on the board and get familiar. Be Patient,as it does take us some time to review the application and to make a decision on it. Most decisions are made 1-2 days after getting the application. Your application is appreciated! Just complete this form. Click on Submit when ready to send. Once your application has been reviewed, the owner and president will contact you. Please be patient as it can take a few weeks before we contact you. Notice, we do not send rejections out because it would take far too much time. Thank You.
