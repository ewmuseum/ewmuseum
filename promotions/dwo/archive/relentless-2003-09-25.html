Relentless, 25 September 2003

25 September 2003

Los Angeles, California.. September 25, 2003 Pitter.. Patter.. The rain just keeps falling down from the heavens, as if the angels themselves can't cease their crying. Accompanying the tears of above is the subtle roar of thunder across the horizon, dark clouds rolling along and painting the sky with their dark colors. Tonight was the night, the setting to bring forth the havoc that would take place in a little over two days time. Descent into Darkness, the DWO's end of the month pay-per-view event was drawing considerably close and tonight would seal the nail in the coffin for the fates of many. The busy city of Los Angeles was already feeling the demonic pull of the DWO's presence, a line over seven city blocks was already forming and continuing to grow by the minute. But unknown to the ticket holding fans outside things were already beginning to pick up down below the warehouse.. Inside of the DWO's custom underground arena. A luminescent glow of moonlight shines in through the blinds and back against the wall; reflecting from her creepily white hair. There’s a soft whistling of the wind and rain as it lashes down against the double glazed window. Little sound comes from the inside of the room apart from the soft hum of concentration from the oddly muscled lady. Two strange beams flash into the darkness as her eyes flutter open. Her thick muscled arms open up to her side as the white semi transparent blouse slowly follows down to her wrists. Her muscled balls of fists clench tightly as her humming becomes louder, almost taking over the rattling of the weather outside the door. Her humming is interrupted by a repeated thudding at the door; her voice reacts in a raspy and cold hearted way. Zahri: “What the fuck?!” Booker: “Ummm, Zahri?” Zahri: “What?” Booker: “We have your booking for tonight.” Zahri’s white pools close up as the eerie feeling of the grin on her face is enough to send shivers dancing up and down your spine. Her leather coated legs straighten with a loud crack as she rises to her feet; slowly her boots beat against the ground as she makes her way towards the door. Her frying pan sized hands grasp the golden handle of the door and it fails to move from her grasp. She grunts slightly and tries to twist the ancient knob; which in turn makes the small door accessory come off in her hand. Her voice releases her anger in a small whisper. Zahri: “Son of a bitch.” Zahri puts her hand through the hole where the door handle once was. The pine door rips from its hinges across her hand. The fat balding reporter looking man wipes a bead of sweat from his brow and pushes the blue bridge of his glasses further up his nose. Zahri blows the hair from her face and delivers a grunt at the man who clutches the notepad lifts a few pages from his board. The man looks up in time to see Zahri blow a white streak of hair from her face. Zahri: “Would you please hurry up?” The man stutters a little which leaves Zahri a little more than red in the face. Booker: “OK! You’re facing Rick Velocity tonight. I guess I’ll have to go and give him the bad news.” Zahri snaps the door from her wrist and tosses its two pieces across the room. The Booker man takes a few steps back without taking his hazel eyes from the almost male form of Zahri. Zahri takes next to no time to make her way out of the door and to the new target of her anger. Her speed picks up a fast pace as she makes her way around the corner. Her gigantic body nearly scrapes on the walls as she makes her way towards the locker room of the target. Zahri bangs her fists down against the thick pine door and grins as Velocity opens the door. His face screws up when he sees the intimidating Amazon like woman standing within the shadows of his door. Rick Velocity: “Who the hell are you? Get out of my face!” Rick Velocity shakes the walls with the strength he slams the door shut with. Zahri flickers her tongue along her lips before she presses her palms against the door. The door shakes before Zahri’s hands dive into the solid pine door splintering outwards. The thudding of Velocity’s head against the door bangs into the room. Zahri presses her huge muscular body against the door and the door breaks in half. Zahri pulls her hands away from the bloody face of Rick Velocity and the broken door before kicking the remaining bottom half of the door away from the resting place. Zahri: “Zahri’s the name. I’ve been sent to help you clear out your room. Apparently there’s going to be a muscled Aphrodite moving in here and well…that leaves no room for you? I hope you understand?” Zahri leans down and grips Velocity by the ears before lifting him a couple of feet off of the floor. Her upper body tenses as she tosses Velocity against the lockers. The red steel of the lockers bends behind the pressure his two hundred and sixty pound frame. Zahri walks over to where Rick Velocity is; he coughs up blood before kicking the large woman’s feet from beneath her. Zahri rolls backwards and gets back up in time to catch a devastating TKO right around the jaw. Zahri’s face slams to the side; a hardened look of shock is spread across her face before it in turn gets slammed into Rick’s face hitting a devastating head butt. Rick is forced back against the locker and again Zahri goes for a head butt; fortunately for Rick he can no longer stand and Zahri slams her face right into the steel. There’s a very cartoon-esque imprint left of her face in the steel door. Zahri: “Slippy little buggar aren’t you?” Zahri grips the bloodied face of Rick and picks him up by it just before she drags him face first along the wall leaving a probable lasting effect on them. Zahri gets to the door and tosses him through an opposite window. Zahri: “I like this place…it has a nice atmosphere.” Zahri looks around at her devastation and giggles before wiping her hands. Blood coats the walls, lockers are left broken on the floor as glass from the opposite window scatters onto the carpet. The small bald booker man from before runs around the corner and looks on at the mess. He once again wipes the sweat from his head as he sees the woman in front of him. Booker: “Err…Zahri is it?” The white haired Amazon-esque woman looks down at him and nods her head and grunts. Booker: “I’ve just got news that Rick Velocity is fired. So, you get to face Skorp.” Zahri slams her fists into the frame which used to hold the pine door and screams. Zahri: “Mother BOOKER!” -End Inside of the now packed custom arena in Los Angeles, California the show prepares to begin.. The lights begin to lower and zips of silver and blue lights begin to flash around the arena. While this begins to happen the fans grow tense, about to burst with anticipation. From backstage Mike Cerrone and Jay Stevens make their early accent down to their commentary table. The crowd gives the two renowned men a warm welcome as they take their seats behind the large table. Head sets are donned as the lights cut completely, the arena being immediately shaken up by exploding missiles from above, flying down and hitting either side of the staging area which causes a line of explosions to go off down the ramp and around the entire ring. Once those explosions finish one massive explosion occurs inside of the ring, causes it to burst into flames which quickly eat at the matting.. Seeming to write a message. Indeed it was a message, a clearly seen one once the flames subside.. "Suffer Relentlessly" No doubt about it, those words would show to become actions here tonight. The fans were in a frenzy, the camera's capturing them as they wave their signs and cheer their favorite stars. Many signs bared names such as Callisto, Roulette, Lithium, and Tyrone.. Even a few without names, just logos and trademark sayings. Both Mike and Jay grin as the fans subside into semi-silence and they can actually begin the show. Mike: Good evening! The bell rings and here comes Zahri with an immediate clothesline, which she follows up with an immediate elbow drop. Skorp manages to move out of the way. Skorp returns to his feet as Zahri bounces back up to hers. Skorp goes for a clothesline but Zahri bends him back as the clothesline narrowly misses her. Zahri gets back up and meets the turning around Skorp with a high leg lariat. Skorp falls to the mat; Zahri lifts him up and pushes him into the ropes. Skorp returns from the ropes and Zahri delivers a devastating uppercut. Skorp falls to the match holding his nose in pain. Zahri grabs Skorp up to all fours and bends over him. Zahri begins delivering knee after knee to the shoulders of Skorp. Skorp falls to the mat now trying to hold both his shoulders. Zahri smacks Skorp across his face and then lifts him up. Jay: Skorp needs to wake up out there! A female for crying out loud is beating him! Mike: Is that really that surprising here in DWO? Jay: Well...no I guess not. Skorp lies on the mat for a moment, pondering what just happened. He snaps to his feet just in time for Zahri to leap off of the ropes, and land a splash down onto Skorp. He falls, and Zahri rolls to the turnbuckle, pulling herself up. Skorp gets back to his feet again, but as Zahri leaps off for a second dive, he sidesteps it, and she lands on the mat. Skorp drops down with an elbow to her chest, then stand for a second, only to land on the mat. He scrambles to his feet, and grabs hold of Zahri's oncoming arm. Skorp ducks the clothesline, and she rebounds off of the opposite ropes. Upon her running back, Skorp ducks down, and lifts up once she hits his back. Zahri flies in the air, nearly over the ropes, and as she falls, Skorp kicks his foot out, sending it hard into her ribs! Mike: HOLY SHIT!!! Jay: I hope nothing's broken! That looked PAINFUL! Zahri curls up from the kick as she lies on the mat, and Skorp towers over her with a smirk. He leans down, grabbing a handful of her hair. It is twisted up in his fist, as he lifts the girl to her feet. She stands due to the force, and is pushed towards the turnbuckle. Her chest hits hard against it, and as Skorp approaches her; she jumps onto the bottom ropes, and kicks out with all her might. Her feet slam into Skorp's stomach, sending him flying backwards. Zahri stands, holding her ribs for a moment, before she turns and walks to Skorp. Once over him, she towers, then stomps down onto the spot her feet already kicked.
