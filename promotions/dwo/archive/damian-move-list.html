Damian — Move List

2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Move List Wrestling Style: Heel Set Up Move: The Macabre Murderer, The Man Without a Conscience, The Razorblade Smile ( ) Finisher: 235 () Other Moves: Deeper into Damian's fighting style Damian is a very well rounded and dangerous competitor. He is very strong, able to throw opponents up to his own size around the ring with great ease. He is also very agile, and very meticulous, always intent on his opponent's condition and maneuvering. Damian is a master of countering and reversing attacks, also quite formidable with submissions, as well as striking, high flying, hardcore tactics, and brawling. His wrestling training was not as long and arduous as many wrestler's were. He has a limited amount of wrestling capability but he has mastered a large amount of wrestling moves, with which he likes to be innovative and inventive at applying. Damian is incredibly patient, as he is a serial killer, and he will never lose his temper. he makes very few mistakes. His senses are incredible. His hearing, sight, smell, and touch are far above average and he utilizes this to the best of his ability. Strategies Smaller Opponents: Damian will manhandle a smaller opponent, working on their neck, back and legs throughout the match with vicious submissions and grappling moves in an attempt to keep them using their agility. Damian will not usually use his high flying skills against a more quickly based opponent. He can however, spar and reverse with the best of lightweights and is not at all afriad tp show this ability. He will usually try to use a strong wrestling move to take them down to the mat, at which point he will usually brawl and lock on submissions. Large Opponent: Against a larger opponent Damian will utilize his speed, agility, flexibilty, and superhuman senses to baffel his opponent. Although strong, he will not attempt too many power moves against a larger opponent. He will work on the limbs of the opponent to keep them off their feet and from using the usually arm and hand based powerhouse style. He will use many strikes and when needed his high flying wrestling abilities. He will use some what of an in and out attack style. Heels: He will use their aggresion against them. Face: He will be quite aggresive but not in an over-zealous manner. Moves The Pit and the Pendulum:Damian climbs to the top rope and hits a high altitude Shooting Star Press into a Guillotine Leg Drop on his layed out opponent. Pestilence: Damian lifts his opponent into a Fireman's Carry. He stands at one side of the ring and runs, leaping over the top rope and somersaulting in the air landing the opponent through a table with a huge Samoan Drop. This move takes alot out of Damian but more or less ends the match for the opponent. Hell On Earth: Damian lifts the opponent onto his shoulders in a Fireman's Carry. Throwing them over as if going for a DVD but landing them onto their knees and dropping into a Neckbreaker. Helter Skelter: Damian goes for a Snapmare, landing a Reverse DDT. He then stands up and flips forward into a Neck Wrench. Into The Abyss: Damian stands on the top rope and flips into a Moonsault with a 360 Degree Twist landing of course into a Moonsault. The Mannequin Maker: Damian lifts the opponent into a Crucifix Powerbomb position spinning 180 Degrees and landing them in a DDT. Blazing Burial:Damian locks on an STF/Full Nelson Hybrid onto the victim. The Mindfuck:Damian hits a quick roundhouse kick to the victim's gut, leaps into an enziguri like kick to the forehead and swings his free leg around the victim's head hitting them in the back of the neck causing a scissors like effect. The Bad Trip: Damian hits a huge leaping uppercut to the chin of the opponent with his left handfollowed by a viscious chop to the back of the victim's neck with his right. Descent into the Maelstrom: Damian locks the victim in a standard camel clutch with a twist. He uses his hammer to choke the victim out. Capital Punishment: (Rarely used) Damian lays the victim under the bottom rope, their head hanging off the apron, and places a chair, in a sitting position, under their throat. Samantha gives him his hammer and Damian pounds on the back of the victim's neck with it. Godless Savage Garden:Damian plants the victim with a reverse DVD before locking on an Bridging Indian Death Lock. Abra-Cadaver: A series of moves starting with a simple Gut-Wrench Powerbomb, immediately followed by a standing Front-Flip Senton Splash, followed by lifting the victim up into a DVD, lifting them again into a Hangman's Neckbreaker, lifting them up again in a Gory Special to a Gory Bomb, and finally rolling back into a Boston Crab. Let Chaos Entwine:With the opponent on a table outside of the ring Damian performs a Springboard 360 Degree Corkscrew Shooting Star Splash Suicida with insanely high altitude. Deathwish:Damian lifts the victim above his head in a Gorilla Press dropping them over the top turnbuckle stomach first before catapulting over the top rope and hitting a Diamond Cutter landing the victim head first into the ground.
