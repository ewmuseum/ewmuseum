The end

6 November 2003

The Aclincans of the round table show all Date/Time: Unknown Place: Main Chamber of Aclincan People: Sheera, Edmund and Dr Pudkuf [Sheera, Edmund and Dr Pudkuf sat around the table side by side in a dim very dim light. The only source of light was from two candles, which sat in the centre of the table. Sheera sat back in her chair looking towards the ceiling. Edmund perched his head on the up turned palms of his hands and he had an annoyed look on his face. Dr Pudkuf sat normally with his arms set from his elbow onwards onto the table. He turned to Sheera and then to Edmund and they both looked back. Edmund was the first to break the silence, which had engulfed the room since Equinox had left. Emily was still taking him to his room so the meeting began] Edmund (To Pudkuf): Doctor…why did you not tell the outsider of the truth about the swamp? There is nothing he could really say if we were to tell him what actually happens. Dr Pudkuf (To Edmund): I do not want to alarm him Edmund. He is a guest, not someone who is going to turn on us like our people did. He will never be told of what happened, we will go on with our lives and help him become one of our community, we need as many people as possible to help win our next battle. It is very important. [Sheera looked towards Edmund with a look as if she had a plan. Edmund returned the look without Pudkuf noticing. Sheera then turned to Pudkuf] Sheera (To Pudkuf): Doctor…I feel it is necessary that we tell this man of what the swamp is for. Therefore there are no secrets being kept between each of the parties. He would then feel that we could actually trust him because we have let him know of our darkness. [Pudkuf’s fist slammed against the table] Dr Pudkuf (To Sheera): Have you not been listening to me?! You need to understand that he will never be told! And if anyone ever tries to tell him then they will be the next one’s to be taken to the swamp and sent to their end. If we tell him he will try and escape. He will be afraid that we will do it to him too. Sheera (To Edmund): Edmund…let’s sort this fool out. [Pudkuf raised from his chair as fast as lightning and moved back against the wall of the main chamber. Sheera and Edmund both stood slowly and stalked towards him] Edmund (To Pudkuf): We’ve had enough of this Pudkuf. We’ve had enough of you running things around here. Look where it has got us. Sheera (To Pudkuf): Nowhere! Absolutely nowhere! You bring all these different people into our community and say that they will lead us to victory. Every time someone new comes here you say that they will be our salvation. Well I’m sick of salvation and I’m sick of you. Edmund (To Pudkuf): Stand down as the leader of this community Pudkuf and we will let you go without a mark on you. [Edmund was cut off when he heard the door behind him creak which made him jump. He turned to look and saw that the door had completely opened but there was no one there] Edmund (To Sheera): Wait here. [Edmund proceeded towards the door and just before he was able to walk through it Emily blocked his way. She looked extremely agitated with Edmund] Emily (To Edmund): What exactly is going on here Edmund? Edmund (To Emily): We’re taking over this place Emily. It’s none of your concern. Emily (To Edmund): It is my concern though Edmund. It is all my concern as I am part of this community and so is my friend. Edmund (To Emily): Who’s this friend of yours? [Emily laughed and turned to her left] Emily (To Edmund): Let’s just say that the Doctor let me know that you two were up to something so I came over here as soon as I could. I know you don’t like this person Edmund but why don’t you tell him what you’ve wanted to tell him all along. [She then turned her attentions to Sheera who was looking on] Emily (To Sheera): And you Sheera. Why don’t you come over and let my friend know what you want him to know. If you don’t let him know then I will. I know the doctor doesn’t want him to know but I do so that you two can be banished from this land forever. [Emily put our her right arm and motioned with her index finger for the person to walk towards the door. Equinox was the friend she was talking about and he entered from the direction she was facing. Edmund was not impressed at all] Edmund (To Emily): You’ve made friends with this fiend? You would rather be with him than help us to bring our community to a better light? You would turn on our years and years of friendship and loyalty for scum like this who had only been in our community for two days? [Emily and Equinox both smiled and looked toward Edmund] Emily (To Edmund): Just because I have made friends with Equinox doesn’t mean that I am not a friend of yours. You may not believe in this man that stands beside us but I do. I believe that he can bring us out of the troubles we have been in for all these years. Unlike you, I spoke with him. I talked to him about things that happened before him came here. He is the same as you and does not believe that he is the way. He believes there is more to this than meets the eye. [Edmund looked towards Equinox who quickly stared back] Equinox (To Edmund): Why must you have a problem with me Edmund? Do you feel threatened by my presence here? You being the biggest of the big around here must be a great accomplishment. You being the leader of the warriors of this community must make you feel proud but when someone arrives that could take your place in an instant then you cower behind false words and pretences. When we all spoke last night you automatically jumped to put me down. To make me look like a nothing, an idiot and someone who didn’t belong here. I know in my own mind that I do not belong here; I do not need people to tell me what I already know. [Edmund moved closer to Equinox and went head to head with him] Edmund (To Equinox): I have a problem with you Equinox because you are a liability to our community. While everyone is worried about your welfare and how we’re going to keep you here in good health, I am the only one worrying about the attack which is about to be launched onto us within the next six hours. [He turned and looked around the room in a full circle] Edmund (To everyone): Are you all forgetting that our lives are about to be put on the line? Are you all forgetting that tonight may be our last night on this world? I am sick and tired of waiting to be executed. We have not prepared anything. We have not prepared defences, attacking strategies or even where we are going to go in the event of a retreat. [Equinox’s hand moved over the mouth of Edmund and gripped it tightly] Equinox (Quietly to Edmund): Keep quiet for just one second. [Equinox let Edmund go and walked towards the middle of the room. Edmund watched on surprise that Equinox had actually grabbed him like that] Equinox (To everyone): Tonight it is simple what you all must do! Tonight when the invaders come and try to attack the community. You must all hide away. Find a place to do this and do not come out until they have gone. If you try to face the invaders then you will be destroyed. My only suggestion to you is for you to hide in the swamp. [Sheera looked up in shock at this comment] Sheera (To Equinox) Maybe it’s time we told you what the purpose of the swamp is. It was only a few days before your arrival that the swamp was formed. It was originally a clear water lake to which we could go and cleanse ourselves of all that was wrong in us. As you know, your community is very small but what you didn’t know was that we were the one’s who made it like that. We used to be the most peaceful people ever but then something happened. Dr Pudkuf (To Equinox): We lost a good friend, the father of our community. He fell into the lake one day and was never seen again. We couldn’t even recover his body to give him a proper goodbye. People started to revolt against us as the new leaders of Aclincan. They tried to overthrow myself, Sheera, Edmund and Emily. Those who joined with us in defence would help us defend against these forces of evil which had taken over half of us. Some of us did not survive the battle before we eliminated the evil threat. This time I do not feel we will be as lucky. Equinox (To Pudkuf): I can feel a presence approaching from the north. In my mind all I can see is a mass of black. I warn you Doctor that you must gotto the swamp; it is the only way that you will survive this attack. [FLASH] [Equinox felt a large chill flow down his spine and he looked around the room. Everyone had disappeared and the room was thrust into silence] Equinox (All around): Hello? [His voice echoed through the windy chamber as he looked around for any sign of like. He turned to the door to check outside for anything but froze at the sight he saw in front of him. To him he was looking at his father, or some type of representation of him] Equinox (To father): What are you doing here father? Michael (To Equinox): I am here to warn you my son. You are the greatest danger that you have ever faced. If you do not make it to the swamp of the community within the next couple of minutes you will be taken into every lasting darkness…they are coming for you my son…run! [FLASH] [Once again Equinox was back in the chamber with the Aclincans. Without saying a word he ran out the door and looked towards the path, which lead towards the swamp. He closed his eyes and started to sprint the hundreds of metres of path in front of him. He could hear a large wind growing behind him. He looked around to see something he didn’t expect at all. A large curtain of black glided across the sky and started to descend towards his position. He only had a couple of hundred metres to go and it started to gain on him. As he reached the edge of the swamp he launched himself into the air and started to fall directly towards the bile. Before he got a chance to be enveloped the black curtain. His vision turned to black and he woke up back in the Funeral Directors] Equinox (To Erika): Mum? [Erika embraced her son] Erika (To Equinox): What happened Lori? [Equinox just looked at her, his eyes began to swell violently and a tear rushed down his cheek] Equinox (To Erika): The evilness took me away… [Erika put her son’s head to her shoulder and held him tight] Erika (To Equinox): We’ll come and see you father tomorrow son. We have to go see the man I was talking to a few days ago about helping you with your anger. [Equinox looked up from his mother’s shoulder and somehow gave a smile] Equinox (To Erika): O.K… [Fade to black] Date: 24th November 1997 Location: McCelland Mental Help People: Dr Steve McCelland, Erika and Lori [Dr McCelland, Erika and Equinox sat in the large office filled with models of skeletons, brains and various other parts of the body. Steve sat behind his large wooden desk and was dressed in a very formal suit. He turned to Erika and reached out to her] Steve (To Erika): First of all I must say I’m very sorry for your loss Erika. [He then turned to Equinox] Steve (To Equinox): I know that it is going to be very hard for you too Lori and I know that you’ve been experiencing problems since his death. What has been happening to you? [Equinox looked at him with pure hatred in his eyes. He didn’t feel hate for Steve but at this point in time he was just angry at everything that existed] Equinox (To Steve): I see things in my head. I go to other realms where I have tasks to do. My mother says that my eyes and I just black out roll up into the back of my head. After a while I wake up and I’m fine again. [Suddenly his voice became very evil and he stood up and pushed the desk towards Steve. It connected with Steve’s ribs, which knocked him back a little] Equinox (To Steve): I’m turning to the dark side Steve and there’s nothing that I can do about it. There is nothing that anybody can do about it. Since my father’s death I have went through a transition. I have learned to accept the fact that my father isn’t coming back and I am able to accept the fact that I can still live my life without him! [Steve pushed the desk back away from his ribs only to have it forced back on by Equinox] Steve (To Equinox finding it hard to get his words out): What are you doing Lori? I can’t breathe! [Erika stood up and pulled back on Equinox. Finally he relented to his mother and took a seat. Steve pushed the desk off himself and held his ribs for a moment before turning to Erika] Steve (To Erika): Your son is corrupted. Erika (To Steve): What do you mean corrupted? Steve (To Erika): The death of Michael has affected the chemical balance in his mind. He is losing control over his emotions. Soon he will not know the difference between right and wrong or else he will just know what is wrong. You just saw him push a desk into me for no reason. [He turned to Equinox slowly and looked at him afraid] Steve (To Equinox): Why did you push my desk into me Lori? [Equinox smiled and his eyes opened widely] Equinox (To Steve): Because it felt like the right thing to do! Steve (To Erika): Do you see what I mean. I recommend that we place him in containment at an asylum for the time being until he can sort himself out. I’ll make all the arrangements. Are you O.K with this? Erika (To Steve): If you feel it’s the best thing to do. Steve (To Equinox): Will you be O.K with going to an Asylum for a while Lori? It’s for your own good. [Equinox gave a completely smarmy response] Equinox (To Steve): Whatever man… Steve (To Both): Well then it’s settled. I shall contact you Erika when everything has been sorted out. [Steve stood up and extended his hand for a shake. Erika shook his hand then moved towards the exit of the room. When Steve joined hands with Equinox he couldn’t get out of it as he was being held tightly. Equinox stared into his eyes while squeezing tighter and tighter] Equinox (To Steve): Thank you for your help Steve. [Equinox let go of the tight grip he had on Steve had and turned to follow his mother out the door. Steve sat down again in his chair and felt his ribs and his hand. He shook his head then looked down at the file in front of him] [Fade to black] [End]
