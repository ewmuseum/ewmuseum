Meet The Family (2nd DWO)

17 October 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
It wasn’t as inconspicuous as they would have liked, but they had only received the notice a day in advance and were ad-libbing the best they could. Nine o’clock was the exact time the first car, a jet-black Mercedes Benz C32 AMG, rolled out the front gate of Brian Arson’s home. The car itself was a mysterious beauty, shiny and sleek, with tinted windows outlined by silver trim. When the car finally disappeared from view another Mercedes, identical to the previous one, also rolled through the steel gates, following the same path as the first and eventually vanished. A few more cars, a silver Ferrari, a black Audi, and a white Mercedes, all exited the Arson property on what seemed to be equally spaced intervals. A bit suspicious? Only to those that really paid much attention, but it was mid morning and most of Brian’s neighbors had already gone off to work. Just when it seemed that the last of the cars had come through, there came the unmistakable grumble of Brian’s Viper. Seconds later the speedster pulled out of the black paved driveway, the sun gleaming off the royal blue that had just recently been waxed. The car was a symbol of wealth; a symbol of power. No other automobile that Arson owned fit his personality as well as this awesome machine. Brian lived his life on a knife edge, he didn’t give a fuck about what anyone else thought, and the car seemed to engulf that attitude. It was his life, and the only way you could take it away was from his cold dead hands. The convoy of cars had moved slowly at first through the main streets of Ventura, but they were in a hurry, and once they hit the highway only a cloud of dust was left in their wake. While the other cars sped off, the Dodge Viper maintained a constant speed. Brian could deal with being late, but the last thing he needed was to be pulled over. And so he crept on, heading in a southeasterly direction toward the more desolate and desert-like regions of California. It was nearing eleven o’clock when the cars arrived at the large estate on the outskirts of San Bernardino. The blue Viper was the last car to pull through the front gate, slowly rolling up the drive toward the back where the rest of the vehicles were parked. The house was enormous, much larger than Brian’s, and it stood on countless acres of land stretching in every direction. Lush green grass and beautiful deciduous trees covered the entire property. In the distance, one could see the outline of the remarkable mountain landscape. Adjacent to the house lay another large building only one story in height. This was the garage, and it was where Brian was heading at this very moment. Outside the building were two dignified looking butlers that were there to enter the key codes that opened the doors. They were quite familiar with the blue Viper, and when they saw it, immediately opened the doors allowing Brian to pull in. The inside was astounding. If not knowing any better, one would think he had just entered a top notch European car dealership. Brian’s Viper along with a couple Corvettes stood out amongst the Ferraris, Porches, Lamborghinis, and Audis, as being the only American made cars in the complex. Brian parked the Viper in a far corner next to a green Porsche. The rest of the cars that had left his home earlier were scattered about the garage. Brian waited for everyone else in the place to leave before he stepped out of the car. From top to bottom, Brian looked like ‘the man’. His long blonde hair ran down his back over the custom Italian designer suit. The infamous dark sunglasses hid his innermost thoughts from the rest of the world. The tailored pants rested just above the ground on the top of his shining black dress shoes. In his right hand was a black briefcase holding incredibly important information as well as his ever so lovely protection. You could say that Brian looked like a million bucks, but you’d be sadly mistaken, for he was so much more. Arson walked over to an elevator that was close to the garage doors. He pulled a key card from his pocket and slipped it into the slot and then punched in a code. A minute later Brian was stepping off the elevator into the corridors underneath the property. The underground halls were made entirely of metal giving off blurry and distorted reflections. The halls led to the main house and were built nearly ten years ago, initially designed as an easy escape route. Now, it was an easy entrance and exit from the garage without the risk of being seen on the surface. Brian came to the end of the hall and stopped in front of a large steel door. Once again he used the key card to get through, a different one perhaps, and on the other side were two elevators and a locked steel door. He stepped into the elevator on the right and took it up to the main floor. The door opened out into the extravagant main hall. At the opposite end from where Brian was standing was a magnificent marble double staircase. The entire hall was laden with blue and white marble squares beautifully polished and looking untouched. Giant white pillars lined both sides with guards standing in front of every other one, equipped with AK’s and dressed in white uniforms. Brian stepped off the elevator and made his way through the hall, nodding with a smirk at the guardsmen who stood there expressionless. At the other end of the great room a man donned in butler’s attire came walking toward him with a smile on his face. Brian grinned as the man approached. “Mr. Arson, it’s so good to see you again!” The butler took hold of Brian’s hand and gave it a firm shake. “Likewise, Philip, how are you doing?” Brian replied with much charm. “Oh, I’m fantastic, sir. I wish we could chat a little more, but the meeting’s almost started. Maybe later?” The man smiled again and then turned toward the direction he had come. “You can follow me this way, Mr. Arson. I will escort you to the room.” He then took off with Brian right behind him. They went through a door just to the right of the staircase and down a long hallway. “We’ve had to increase the security these days what with everything that’s gone on down in Los Angeles. I’m sure you won’t have much trouble with any of the guards, but just in case, make sure you let me know.” Philip was quite fidgety and seemed to absolutely love all the little things that went on around him. The energy was surprising for a man of his profession. The two men were getting closer to the end of the hallway where a set of double doors stood. Brian adjusted his tie and took a deep breath. This was it. A meeting like this had not been called in nearly two years. Behind those doors were some of the most dangerous, wealthy, and infamous criminals that the world had never known. Along with these men was another name, one that was actually very well known: Armando Vazquel. When the two men got to the door, they both stopped. The hall was empty. Everyone else had already arrived and were waiting on Brian. The butler opened the double doors and stepped inside, Brian swallowed deeply and followed. “Sir, El Padrino, I present… Mr. Brian Arson.” Brian carefully made his way into the room with a convincing smile on his face, slipping into his relaxed and charming self. The room was very large and in the middle was a large square table in which nearly twenty men sat. All of them turned to the door upon Brian’s entrance. A few of them smiled while most just gave him a slight nod of approval. At the opposite end of the table, a gallant looking man stood up with a big smile on his face. He moved away from his chair, walking up to Brian with his arms outstretched, and the two men embraced. “Es bueno verlo, Brian!” Armando Vazquel… he was it. The biggest man in the business in the western half of the United States. Over 70% percent of the cocaine smuggled into the U.S. from Colombia came in through him. He was one of the richest men in the United States but of course you’d never find him on any Top 100 Wealthiest American list. And it wasn’t the fact that he was rich that made him so brilliant. He was brilliant because unlike most famous drug lords, Vazquel worked from within the United States and not the base country. You’d think that this would make him much more vulnerable than if he were outside the country. Well, this would be true, but only if the CIA or FBI had any account on the man. The story was quite complex but in the end, Armando, by some miracle, managed to erase any records of himself ever existing in the United States. To them, he was nothing but a rich businessman who kept himself secluded from the rest of the world. After the heartfelt hug, Vazquel led Brian back to a chair reserved right next to his own at the large table. To his left was none other than his good friend, Chris Griffey. Brian smiled and nodded at him and Chris returned the gesture. Everyone at the table seemed to be a bit uneasy and keeping to himself. Not everyone was friends here, but they were in the business together, so they had to act in a respectful manner. If tempers arose then there was no telling what might happen. “Gentlemen… welcome to my humble palace,” Vazquel spoke fluent English but had a pretty thick Spanish accent. “I hope none of you had too much trouble getting here. A lovely day outside, yes?” He paused and lifted his hand toward the window. “Most of you are probably wondering why I called you here today, and a few of you I was able to speak to before more or less. You see, we have a bit of a problem on our hands that you may, or may not, know of.” A few whispers went around the table, a few of the men eyeing one another. “Hmm, how should I say this,” he pondered before going on, “when you control a business so great and so successful as I have done, there will always be people trying to take it away from you. This is to be expected of course whether it be the FBI, the DEA, or some other national authority who have the balls to think they can tell another human being what to do.” Armando grew a little heated but then calmed back down. “You see, I do not have a problem with this. It’s their job, they have to. But… what I do have a problem with, is when other businesses or organizations think they can impede on my own network and steal money from me! Money that I have spent so much time in attaining and have worked so hard for! No, that is not right...” He paused a moment, and there was no longer the smile upon his face. This was the real Armando Vazquel, when he meant business, and when he was angry. You didn’t mess with this man, oh no, not if enjoyed your life. He was cruel, heartless, and dangerous, he’d do anything for money and power, even if it meant murder. Well, let’s rephrase that… especially… if it meant murder. “Pablo Escobar had a dream… a dream that he nearly accomplished. He created an entire empire from almost nothing, and he worked through the ups and downs to maintain it. But his life was tragically cut short, and the goal he had set for himself was not nearly completed. So I am here now, to maintain his legacy, to continue on with what he was destined to do. And I ask you now, why must outsiders feel the need to interfere?” He stopped at this rhetorical question. The rest of the men either shook their head, agreeing in some way or another. Brian just sat there, resting his chin on his hand, soaking in every word that this great man was saying. “Marcus Marcanicci. This is a man that decided to flex his muscles at me. You all know him, the owner of many casinos in Nevada and down into Mexico. Word is he wants to try and take me out. Cut me off from my own business. Take me away from my own people and buy my associates out from under me.” Armando paused for a moment with a serious look on his face but then turned it into a smile. “What, is he nuts? Ahaha,” he laughed wholeheartedly, a booming laugh that echoed throughout the chamber. The rest of the men laughed as well, even Brian, but there was one man, an intelligent looking Spanish man, nearly five chairs down to the right of Vazquel, whose expression never changed. He simply sat there with a pitying look on his face. “Well, I’m sure your friend, Mr. Arson, could answer that for you, couldn’t he?” The man spoke up, his voice rising above the laughter in the room. He looked evilly at Brian. Vazquel watched with a bit of amusement at what was about to transpire. “Or on second thought, why don’t we ask Pablo what he thinks of the situation?” “Oh, don’t even try to play that card against me! I had no control over his murder, and I think that’s quite obvious.” Brian shrugged off the accusations against him. The man’s name was Fernando Marinez, and Pablo Marinez, the man who had been found dead outside Los Angeles the week before, was his younger brother. They had been incredibly close and when he had been informed of his untimely death, it was a devastating blow to him. “If you hadn’t sent him on that trip. You knew how risky it was! You ordered him to go!” Fernando was getting extremely upset and tears rolled down his face. “Yes, I told him to go, but he never once showed that he was the slightest bit nervous!” Brian was starting to get angry. “No! You’re wrong, it’s your fuckin’ fault, you son of a bitch!” The man yelled and jumped from his chair, drawing a gun from his pants. Across the table, Griffey shot up pulling out a gun of his own, aiming it at the head of Marinez. “Whoa, whoa, gentlemen, gentlemen… let’s calm down,” Vazquel spoke up in a soothing voice, although he too was beginning to get a little nervous. But no matter how much anyone pleaded with Fernando, he would not drop his gun. “I swear to God, I’ll smother your fuckin’ brains across the wall, you bastard!” Fernando yelled, but Brian knew now that if he hadn’t done it already he would most likely not do it at all. “Ok, fine… go ahead… shoot me,” Brian put his hands behind his head and rested back in his chair. “You got me, I can’t do anything, so shoot me.” Chris looked at Brian as if he were crazy. He believed that Fernando would actually shoot him. “Fuck you, man!” He pulled the trigger, but no bullet came out. Brian twitched, freaked out at the fact that he would have actually done it had the gun been loaded. Fernando laughed like a madman, “ha, you’re not even worth it, man! You’re not even worth it... no, fuck you, fuck you Brian Arson, and FUCK YOU Armando Vazquel!” The man took the gun, turned it to his own head, and pulled the trigger. This time there was no little ‘click’ and the window behind him lay coated in a thin layer of crimson. The men sitting beside him jumped up as the body of Fernando Marinez hit the side of the table and fell to the ground in a pool of blood. Brian sat there his head in his hands. To his side, Vazquel sat with an annoyed looked on his face. He didn’t care about Marinez; he didn’t give a damn about either one of them. They were just annoyances that he was now rid of. “Jesus Christ,” he muttered angrily to himself. “This was stupid, I don’t know what I was thinking. Half of you don’t even need to be here, especially that piece of shit that just ruined the meeting.” He nodded toward the lifeless body lying on the floor. “Forget it, everyone out! Now!” All of the men left from the room quickly, some having to step over the dead body of Fernando. “Brian… hold on a moment.” Brian stopped and turned toward his friend. “It wasn’t your fault. You know that. I’m hearing things about you having troubles back in Ventura. You don’t need to worry about anything, just go about your life. You’re family to me, and family look out for one another. Now, we’ll talk about what’s going on sometime later. I want you to stay at my house tonight. Whatever you need, just tell me, I’ll get it for you,” Vazquel smiled at Brian. “Thank you, Armando,” Brian smiled and looked down. Armando patted him on the back, and Brian left the room. Just outside the doors Chris was waiting for him. “That was a close one, eh?” Chris was still a bit jittery. “It’s all right now though, you’ve got some other things to worry about, right? Like DWO?” He smiled and shot him a sarcastic little wink. “Yeah, I guess so,” Brian agreed, and the two of them walked off down the hallway. “Oh, by the way, did I tell ya you’re facing Belmont…” Chris grinned, and Brian turned to him and just shook his head. He opened the wooden door at the end of the hallway and slammed it behind him.
