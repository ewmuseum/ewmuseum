WWWF WrestlePania, 27 March 1997

27 March 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
************************WrestlePania************************** ************************************************************ JS: Welcome to the first ever WresltPania. I am your host, Joey Styles. Today, we will find out who will become to first ever World Wide Wrestling Federation World Champion, and the first ever World Wide Wrestling Federation World Tag Team Champions. All the matches will be Best of Three Falls Matches. First we start off with the WWWF Tag Team Tournament. We have in this bowl all the names of the tag teams. The Eliminators, The Outsiders, The Headhunters, The Nation of Domination, The Horsemen, and The Macho Men. Since there are only six teams, President [name withheld] has decided to have the winner of the last match advance directly to the finals. Right now, I am going to pick the first two teams out of the bowl. The first team is……….the Nation of Domination! There opponents will be………the Headhunters! The Nation comes out with Clarence Mason, and then the Headhunters come out with the lovely Kimberly Page. JS: The first fall goes to the Headhunters when Headhunter #1 comes off the top ropes with a devastating moonsault on defenseless Savio Vega. The fall came at the 1 minute 57 second mark. The Headhunters came out attacking, and the Nation was not prepared by the savage Headhunters. JS: The action continues as Crush is setting up Headhunter #2 for the Heart Punch, but Headhunter #2 moves out of the way. JS: Headhunter #1 is once again going for the Moonsault on a prone Vega, but wait! Clarence Mason has just pushed Headhunter #1 off the top rope, and the ref sees it! He has disqualified the Nation of Domination at the 18 minute mark. Clarence and the Nation are infuriated. Clarence has promised legal action against the WWWF. The first card, and the WWWF already has legal troubles. JS: I am now going to pick the next two tag teams. They are……….the Eliminators!… ……..and the Horsemen. Saturn and Kronus walk down the aisle with Francine, and Benoit and Malenko arrive with Alistar Marvelous. JS: Benoit and Malenko are in control, and Malenko just hit Saturn with a spinebuster! He then does a Victory Roll, and goes for the pin. 1……….2………3! The Horsemen win the first fall at the 17 minute mark. JS: Saturn and Kronus are setting Malenko up for the Total Eliminatio, but no! Benoit just hit Kronus with a drop kick. Saturn comes over, and then Benoit gets him in a belly-to-belly suplex. He goes for the pin.1……..2………3! Benoit and Malenko will advance to the semis and face the Headhunters. JS: The two last teams left, the Outsiders and the Macho Men will be battling it out to see who gets an automatic trip the WWWF World Tag Tournament Final. Outsiders arrive with DiBiase, and Macho Man comes out by himself. Where’s Sting? JS: The first fall went to the Outsiders when Nash hit Savage with the Outsider Bomb. Can Savage survive if his partner doesn’t show up? The Outsiders are still beating on Savage. No! Wait! Here comes Sting from the rafters with his baseball bat and he starts beating on Hall and Nash. The ref is letting this go! DiBiase is irate. He is yelling at the ref. OH MY GOD! He just punched the ref! The ref has disqualified the Outsiders, and has awarded the second fall to the Macho Men. JS: The third fall goes to the Macho Men, when Hall was trying to bodyslam Savage, but Savage countered it with an inside cradle. 1-2-3. Savage and Sting advance to the finals, and await the winner of the Horsemen-Headhunters match. JS: We are letting the tag teams get a rest, and we are now starting the WWWF World Title Tournament. These matches will also be best of three falls matches. The first match is…….Hollywood Hogan vs Ahmed Johnson! JS: Hogan has just landed the Leg Drop O’ Doom on Johnson. He covers him, and gets the pin. Hogan wins the first fall at the 20 minute mark. JS: Second fall to Ahmed Johnson with the Pearl River Plunge. Hogan is having a hard time getting up. The match is now tied at one a piece. JS: Four minutes after the Ahmed put the Plunge on Hogan, he did it again. It looks like Hogan’s back is really hurt. He had to be carried out on a stretcher. Update on Hogan’s condition later during the show. Ahmed advances to the quarter finals. JS: Next match is………Steve Austin vs huh? Brian Pillman! They are stablemates! Pillman: I refuse to fight my own stablemate! (Pillman leaves) JS: I guess Austin wins by default, and will face Ahmed Johnson in the second round. JS: Next match is………Raven vs Arn Anderson! A battle of new school vs old school. JS: Old school wins the first fall when Arn Anderson wins with the Gourdbuster. Raven slumps back in the corner. JS: New school wins the next fall when Raven gets his patented DDT on Double A. JS: The new school defeats the old school in this battle of generations when Raven lands another one of his patented DDT’s on Anderson only three minutes after he landed the first one. Anderson is seriously hurt, and his manager, Alistar Marvelous, is checking up on him. JS: Our next match is………….Undertaker vs Faarooq! JS: Undertaker is going for the Tombstone when Ahmed Johnson comes in and knocks the Undertaker out with a chair. Faarooq is disqualified, but he doesn’t care! Johnson gets his 2X4 and they start working on the lower back of the Undertakers. Faarooq and Ahmed start celebrating when……..the Undertaker mysteriously gets back up. Double Chokeslame on Faarooq and Ahmed. Ahmed falls over the top ropes, but Faarooq isn’t that lucky. Tombstone on Faarooq, and the Undertaker advances. JS: Next match is………….Mr. Perfect vs Ric Flair! JS: Perfect just caught Flair in the abdominal stretch. Can Flair reverse it? Guess not, because he just submitted after being in the hold for seven seconds! JS: Perfect just put got the Jumping Neck Snap on Flair, and is signaling for the Perfect Plex. Perfect lands it! 1-2-3! Perfect wins. Flair is complaining about a fast count. Both of them shake hands after. JS: Next match is…….Bret Hart vs Sid! JS: Sid raises Bret up for the Powerbomb, but Bret gets out of it! He drop kicks the back of Sid, and goes to work on the knees. JS: Elbow from the top. He lands it. Bret slaps on the Sharpshooter. Sid won’t give up. He’s been in it for over 7 minutes. JS: The ref has stopped the match because Sid is unable to continue. He was in the Sharpshooter for over 12 minutes. He fell into unconsciousness and the ref had to give the match to Bret. Sid is being stretchered out, as Bret celebrates and is lauging at Sid. JS: Last match of the first round is Diamond Dallas Page vs The Giant. This look likes a mismatch! JS: Oh my god! DDP just brought the Giant with a flying clothesline. He goes for the pin. 1……….2…………3! DDP causes a major upset, but he has to pin the Giant one more time to advance. JS: DIAMOND CUTTER! DDP just delivered the Diamond Cutter on the Giant from the top ropes, and has just pinned him. This was the upset of the round! DDP shows that the bigger you are, the harder you fall. JS: Another match was Vader losing to Shawn Michaels. Michaels will face Mr. Perfect in the second round. Note: These matches will not affect the rankings and the win loss record. JS: The next match in the WWWF Tag Tournament will occur now. The Horsemen will be facing the Headhunters. The Horsemen look to be outsized by the Headhunters by over 300 pounds! JS: This match was a no contest as the Headhunters used their weight advantage. Benoit tried to bodyslam Headhunter #2, but collapsed, hurting his back. The Headhunters strategically worked on the back on Benoit. The first pinfall came when Headhunter #2 landed a splash. Benoit was pinned, and could take no more. He had to be stretchered out, leaving Malenko by himself. Malenko fought bravely, but could not fight the Headhunters by himself. Flair and Anderson tried to come out, but the Undertaker and Raven stopped them. Headhunter #2 landed a moonsault, and the Headhunters advanced to the final, where they will face a rested team of Randy Savage and Sting. JS: The next match in the WWWF World Title Tournament was between Steve Austin and Ahmed Johnson. Austin, who was fresh, picked his spots, and hurt Ahmed, who had a grueling match with Hollywood Hogan earlier in the card. Austin won the first fall with a small package. Ahmed was complaining to the ref that the count was fast when out of nowhere, Austing put the Stone Cold Stunner on Ahmed. 1-2-3. Austin wins. Austin: Just another chapter in Austin 3:16! JS: Our next match is between the Heart Break Kid and Mr Perfect. Both came off tough victories. Michaels defeating Perfect’s stablemate, Vader. Perfect beat Nature Boy Ric Flair. These two technicians batlled for thirty minutes before the first pin. Jumping neck snap, Perfect Plex combo from Perfect, as he got the first fall. Michaels cam roaring back and worked on the lower back of Perfect. Michaels got the second pin when he threw Perfect into the turnbuckle and followed it up with the Superkick O’ Doom! But the third and deciding fall went to Mr. Perfect, as he once again got the Neck Snap-Perfect Plex combo on Michaels. Michaels and Perfect shook hands after the match. JS: The last match of the quarter finals is between Diamond Dallas Page and Bret Hart. Bret Hart was not found any where in the building. Maybe he was too much of a chicken to show up? Who knows? DDP wins by default. JS: I forgot to mention that Raven also no-showed against the Undertaker. Rumor has it that Kimberly Page forced her wrestler Raven to no show. JS: The Semi-Final Round of the WWWF World Title Tournament. Matches are Austin vs Undertaker, and Mr. Perfect vs Diamond Dallas Page. JS: Undertaker was well rested for the match, but Austin had just defeated Ahmed Johnson. Every time the Undertaker amounted an offensive combo, Austin would cheat and rake the eyes of the Undertaker. Austin couldn’t do much either, because the Undertaker would use his super human abilities to withstand all the punishment he received. The first pin fall came when Austin caught Undertaker in a belly-to-belly suplex and pinned the Man from the Darkside. The crowd was stunned as the crowd favorite to win the WWWF World Title was down 1 fall to nothing in the semi finals. The crowd was stunned even more when the Undertaker did his famous sit up, and Austin nailed him with the Stone Cold Stunner. Austin advances to the Final and faces the winner of the DDP/Mr Perfect match. JS: Perfect had come off two straight victories off two of the best wrestlers in the world. DDP beat the Giant in the first round and received a bye when Bret Hart no showed. The first fall went to Perfect as DiBiase tried to sneak brass knuckles to Page when the ref saw it. Ref called a dq and awarded the fall to Perfect. DiBiase caused another loss for his stable. If I were the nWo, I would fire Ted! JS: Perfect was working on the back of DDP the whole match, and his work paid off when Perfect landed the Jumping Neck Snap on him, and pinned him. Page’s neck s really hurt, and doctors are checking him out now. JS: The Headhunters defeated the Macho Men in a Death Match to become the first ever WWWF World Tag Team Champions. The rules for a death match are as follows. The only way to win was to pin your opponent three straight times. Headhunter #1 landed three straight moonsaults and three straight pins in a two minute span on Macho Man Randy Savage. Savage earlier had pinned Headhunter #1 with the Patented Flying Elbow Drop, but that was all the offense the Macho Men could come up with, as they too were killed by the Headhunters because of the massive weight advantage. JS: In the finals of the WWWF World Title Tournament, Mr. Perfect faced off against Stone Cold Steve Austin in an Iron Man Match. Austin took the first two falls with the Stone Cold Stunner, but Perfect came back with the Perfect Plex to get a pin. Then Austin did an amazing feat by putting winning three straight falls, all ending with the Stone Cold Stunner. Perfect broke the streak by getting another pin with the Perfect Plex. Austin then put two more pins on Perfect with the Stunner, but Perfect mounted a comeback with 15 minutes left in the match. He pinned ustin once with a DDT, another time with the jumping Neck Snap, and another time with the Perfect Plex. All of this was not enough though, as time ran out on Perfect. The first ever WWWF World Champion is Stone Cold Steve Austin! JS: The First Ever WRESLEPANIA HAS ENDED. Hope you enjoyed it. I am Joey Styles saying, Stone Cold Stunner! ************************************************************************ Updates: Diamond Dallas Page will be out one card due to a neck injury. Hulk Hogan is out 3 cards due to a back injury
