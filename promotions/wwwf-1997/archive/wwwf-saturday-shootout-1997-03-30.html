WWWF Saturday ShootOut, 30 March 1997

30 March 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
******************************Saturday ShootOut********************** ********************************************************************* JS: Welcome everybody to the first ever Saturday ShootOut! We are at a sold out Philadelphia Spectrum and I am your host, Joey Styles. We have an exciting card in store for you today. We have one title match, and the co-main event is a Over the Top Rope Battle Royal for the Vacant WWWF US Title. The other main event is a no disqualification, no count out, no time limit I Quit match between Pitbull #2, and Brian Pillman. Our first match is between the Hart Breakers, and the Outsiders. The winner of this match will face the Head Hunters later in the card for the WWWF World Tag Team Titles. Here comes the Hart Breakers led by Sunny. Then comes out Ted DiBiase with the Outsiders. Ted DiBiase has already cost the Outsiders a loss in one match when he punched the ref at WrestlePania. Let’s get to the action. Bret Hart and Shawn Michaels beat The Outsiders when B. Hart defeated Nash by disqualification in 0:29:49. JS: Ted DiBiase once again causes his team to lose by disqualification when he pushed Shawn Michaels off the top rope. The Outsiders are irate at their manger. Well, the Hart Breakers win, and they will be facing the WWWF Tag Team Champions, the Head Hunters later in the show. Our next matchup is between Raven and the Sandman. Sandman comes out with the exotic Beulah McGillicutty, and Raven comes out with Kimberly Page. Raven pinned The Sandman after a power bomb in 0:10:11. JS: Raven put a devastating power bomb on Sandman. Our next match is between Brian Pillman and Nature Boy Ric Flair. This will be Pillman’s first match of the card. Later on, he will be facing Pitbull #2 in the main event. This match is very important for Flair, because if he wins, he will get a title shot at Stone Cold Steve Austin. Brian Pillman pinned Ric Flair with the Air Pillman in 0:25:03. JS: Pillman wins with the Air Pillman. Austin came out to distract Flair, and when Flair turned around, Pillman landed the Air Pillman. Pillman can really fly! Next match is Mr. Perfect and Double A, Arn Anderson. Mr. Perfect and Arn Anderson battled to a double countout in 0:02:12. JS: This was a knock down drag out affair. Anderson attacked Perfect before he even reached the ring. When the finally got to the ring, Perfect threw Double A out, and they brawled from there on. The ref could not control them, so he made the decision and counted both men out. The next match is between Diamond Dallas Page and Ahmed Johnson. Diamond Dallas Page pinned Ahmed Johnson with the Diamond Cutter in 0:12:34. JS: Page came out of the crowd and took it to Johnson. The NOD tried to interfere, but the nWo wouldn’t let them. Page won it with a kick to the midsection followed by the Diamond Cutter. The next match also features the Nation of Domination as they battle with the Eliminators. Kronus and Saturn beat Crush and Savio Vega when Kronus defeated Crush by disqualification in 0:12:12. JS: The Eliminators were setting up Savio for the Total Elimination when Ahmed Johnson ran in and stopped it. The Eliminators fought back and ended up giving Savio, Crush, and Ahmed the Total Elimination before Faarooq came out and cleared the ring. Next match is a special challenge match. Scott Hall had an open contract, and Vader challenged him. Vader pinned Scott Hall after an inside cradle in 0:11:42. JS: This was Hall’s second match of the night as Vader beat him with an inside cradle. It’s unbelievable that such a huge man like Vader can do an inside cradle. Next match is Taz vs Sid. Taz asked for this match and got it. Sid should be favored to win since he has a height advantage of about a foot. Taz made Sid submit to the Tazmission in 0:16:26. JS: Taz did not back down despite the huge height differential. When Taz slapped on the Tazmission, Sid gave up instantly. The next match is for the right to face the WWWF World Tag Team Champions at Monday Night Misery. The Horsemen come down with Woman, and the Macho Men come out by themselves. Dean Malenko and Chris Benoit beat Sting and Randy Savage when D. Malenko made Sting submit to the Texas Cloverleaf in 0:20:29. JS: The Shooter, put the Texas Cloverleaf on Sting. Sting tried to escape, but was unable to. He submitted after being in it for 42 seconds. Next match is the Head Hunter-Hart Breakers match. Winner will face the Horsemen at Monday Night Misery. The Head Hunters beat Bret Hart and Shawn Michaels when Head Hunter #1 pinned B. Hart after a moonsault in 0:22:30. JS: It’s amazing that such an big man can perform that kind of manuever. Head Hunter #1’s moonsault is absolutely devastating. The next match is the co-main event. It is an I Quit match between Brian Pillman and Pitbull #2. One has to wonder how much Pilman’s match earlier took out of him. No-Time-Limit-No-Countout-No-DQ-I-Quit-Match Brian Pillman made Pitbull #2 submit to a sleeperhold in 0:33:43. JS: Pit Bull #2 never said I Quit, but he was in the sleeper hold for 11 minutes! The ref had to make a decision, and since Pitbull #2 was unconscious, the ref gave the match to Pillman. Now the other main event. A 16 man Battle Royal to determine the first ever WWWF US Champion. Participants are: Vader, Mr. Perfect, Kevin Nash, DDP, Arn Anderson, Ric Flair, Undertaker, Head Hunter #1, the Giant, Sting, Bret Hart, Shawn Michaels, Ahmed Johnson, Faarooq, Taz! 16-Man Battle Royal Head Hunter #1 won a 16-man Battle Royal: x Anderson threw out Sandman in 0:08:27 x Ahmed Johnson threw out B. Hart in 0:11:30 x Giant threw out Diamond Dallas in 0:14:17 x Giant threw out Taz in 0:21:34 x Ahmed Johnson threw out Nash in 0:23:07 x Head Hunter #1 threw out Undertaker in 0:41:21 x Head Hunter #1 threw out Anderson in 0:47:28 x Perfect threw out Flair after the Perfect Plex in 0:49:25 x Head Hunter #1 threw out Vader in 0:50:02 x Head Hunter #1 threw out Faarooq after a belly-to-belly suplex in 0:54:55 x Head Hunter #1 threw out Perfect after a DDT in 1:14:06 x Ahmed Johnson threw out Giant in 1:21:50 x Head Hunter #1 threw out Sting in 1:29:56 x Head Hunter #1 threw out Ahmed Johnson after a stomp in 1:37:25 x Head Hunter #1 threw out Michaels after a facerake in 1:37:53 JS: Head Hunter #1 wins after he rakes the face of Michaels, and throws him over the top ropes. Head Hunter #1 is the first multi-belt holder in the WWWF. This has been the World Wide Wrestling Federation, and I am Joey Styles, saying I Am Outta Here! ************************************************************************ President’s notes: Send in challenges for Monday Night Misery. The Horsemen will face the Head Hunters. Stone Cold Steve Austin will make a mandatory title defense. Hope you enjoyed, and keep sending in interviews!
