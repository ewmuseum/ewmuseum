CWC — Rules & Regulations

CWC Rulebook

21 August 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
http://members.tripod.com/~CWCo/index.htm How the CWC is run... The CWC is a roleplaying federation. Originality Only original wrestlers will be accepted into the CWC. You can name your wrestlers after real wrestlers. You can call your wrestler "Big Daddy Cool" Stevie Kick, but if he starts saying that he's too SWEEEEEET, then he won't be accepting in the league. When naming your wrestler, be original. We want wrestlers who have, not only, catchy names, but good names. I'm not looking for any superheros, just good wrestlers. Limits The reason that you can only have one wrestler or tag team is simple. I want wrestlers to join together by themselves and each have a different personality. I've seen feds where a stable is controlled by one man. In result, everybody has their on personality and it shines in your wrestler. Jobber Matches Jobber matches are simple. You can either use one of the jobbers hired by the league, or create your own!!! Then just send me what happens during the match and the moves that you use. Make it as real as possible. Have you ever seen WCW Saturday Night? Well, have you ever seen somebody such as Diamond Dallas Page fight some guy you've never seen before, and he still gives DDP a little run for his money? Well, that's how I want you to write your jobber matches, unless it doesn't fit your wrestler's personality. If you have any other comments, please just mail me. Interviews This is what makes and breaks a lot of wrestlers. Write your interviews as descriptive and as interesting as possible. Unlike some feds, I will send out all the interviews in a bundle instead one by one. I only want you to send out one, maybe two, interviews a week. I know, at least I know I do, you have lives outside of the internet and e-wrestling, so I don't want this to be too time consuming. Please do not use other wrestlers in your interviews without getting their consent first. I don't mean that you can't say their name and address them, but don't attack them during an interview. You will be suspended for 2 weeks for this. Please do not use explicit words in your interviews. I really don't care if you do it from time to time, but please do not use them in every other word you speak, like Steve Austin. Just like a real interview, I will edit out some, but not all of the four letter words in your interviews. I know some of you can't be here all the time. Heck, I'm not here all the time : ) But if you do go out of town for a week of so, please mail me and tell me so!! How matches are decided... Matches are decided on four things: Interviews, Strategies, Wrestler's Stats, and Outside Interference Interviews: You need to interview. This is a great way to get a push for your wrestler by yourself!! Strategies: You don't have to write very long strategies. All you need to do is send in something explaining what you will do in the match, like say you were Chris Benoit taking on Kevin Nash. You would take out his knees would you not? Well, just tell me all that you plan to do in the match!! Wrestler's Stats: Strengths and weaknesses really do matter!! Choose carefully!! Outside Interference: You never know when some nut will nail you with a wicked chair shot from behinid. Well, theirs not much you can do about this. But can you? Money System We here at the CWC have what lots of people want, MONEY!! We woke up one morning and decided that you deserve the money!! Well, you don't actually get the money, so don't come after me. I'll explain the money system right here. For signing a contract, you get a signing bonus, depending on the length of your contract. If you win a jobber match, you get $100. If you win a match against a superstar, you get $200 If you battle to a draw against a superstar, you get $50 If you lose to a superstar, you get $25 In PPVs, the money is doubled!! You can spend it in many ways. 1) You can go to the CWC training facility. To get in to training camp, you have to pay $800. In training camp, you can add a new strength, and if you really want, a weakness?? Also, you can add a new fav move to your arsenal. If you have a big match, you could send out an extra interview for the week, but it will cost you $400. Or you can spend in any other fashion. Buy yourself a house or a car or a pet. Whatever. It's your thing, do you what you wanna do. If you get a fine, you'll have to pay it off with your money. Titles The CWC will have 3 single titles and 1 set of tag titles. The only way for belts to change hands are by pinfall or submission. The World Heavyweight Champion must defend his belt every 30 days. The United States Heavyweight Champion must defend his belt every 25 days. The Regional Heavyweight Champion must defend his belt every 20 days. The Heavyweight Tag Team Champions must defend their belts every 30 days.
