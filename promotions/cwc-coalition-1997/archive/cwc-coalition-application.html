CWC Application

21 August 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
http://members.tripod.com/~CWCo/index.htm Do you think you have what it takes to join the Classical Wrestling Coalition? Well, first, you have to meet the following.......... Criteria You are required one wrestler or one tag team. You must send in an interview every week. You will write your own jobber matches (Read Rules and Regulations For More Information on Jobber Matches.) Your wrestler must be an original wrestler. Your wrestler cannot be participating in any other leagues. Your wrestler must be realistic. I don't want any 8 foot 1000 pounds monsters!! Now, if you want to continue, please cut and paste the following, then send it to me here Your Name: E-Mail Address: Your Past E-Wrestling Experience Wrestler's Name Hometown Age Height Weight Face/Heel/Popular Heel/Neutral What does your wrestler look like? Please Be As Descriptive As Possible Ring Attire Entrance Music In order of Artist and then Title. I don't want Bret Hart's Theme Music. Be Original. Wrestling Style What kind of wrestler is he? Example: Kevin Nash is a Top Notch Brawler while Rey Misterio Jr is a Daredevil Flyer Please tell me 15 moves that your wrestler uses more than others. Please tell me your wrestlers three strengths Example: Strength, Agility, Work Ethic, Mind Games. Anything that has to deal with wrestling applies Please tell me your wrestlers three weaknesses (Be honest, everybody has them.) Finishing Maneuver Is the move Submission or Regular? Finisher Description Please be descriptive as possible if it is a new move. If is another move, please tell me the technical name for the hold. Don't say that an Inverted DDT is a Scorpion DeathDrop. What is the upper limit that your wrestler can apply his finisher? 3 counters for your finisher What moves that your wrestler that leads to an easy pin besides your finisher? If so, what foriegn object does your wrestler like to you? Does your wrestler have a manager? Manager's Name Will he or she interfere in matches Description of Manager Please give me a full description of your manager: Looks and Personality Describe your wrestler's personality This is where you build your wrestler's character. Tell me about his life, goals, etc. Please write a sample interview that I will send out to all wrestlers if you make the grade A little tip: This is where you mostly control your wrestler. Be creative and original. Write it like a movie script. Describe where you are and what you are doing. I like good interviews. Please make me happy :) Tell me what titles he has held in other federations, if any How long do you want a contract for? 1-5 months? I realize that during my contract period that my wrestler will not be allowed to compete in any other wrestling federation. If he does and gets caught, he will be banned and released from the CWC forever Sign your name and sign your wrestler's name. Side note: This means that your wrestler will not compete in other federations, this doesn't mean that YOU cannot. Feel free to join as many feds as you want, but not with your CWC wrestler. Tag Team Name (If you are creating one, fill out another app his tag partner?) Tag Entrance Music Tag Team Partners Name Tag Team Finisher Description of Finisher 3 counters moves for your finisher Titles that the team have won
