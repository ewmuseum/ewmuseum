Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

A federation also calling itself the FWA posted two recruitment notices to rec.sport.pro-wrestling.fantasy on the same day, 18 June 2000 — one a general call for staff, the other a writer’s application form.

The first post drew a hard line on its own roster policy, in its own words: “FAKE WRESTLERS ONLY!!!” It was recruiting writers first and wrestlers second, and the second post laid out its own writer’s tryout — a name, a sample match, and a sample magazine article.

No roster, card, or closure notice survives past those two posts; the pair of ads is the whole of what’s known about the promotion.
