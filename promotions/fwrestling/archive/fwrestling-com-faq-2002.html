Need to know more about we offer? Get to know FWrestling.com's features below. News and Directory Features FW News: Includes in-roleplay news from leagues on FW Central and other major leagues around the Web. This news is provided by 'reporters' affiliated with the leagues and/or FW.com. Circuit News: Includes in-roleplay news from leagues and wrestlers around the Web. Circuit News is currently open to all posters while we beta-test the setup. Get more information and post news here. Features: Includes both in- and out-of-roleplay content including general interest columns, in-roleplay columns by fantasy wrestling characters, special announcements for events, etc. Directory: The FW.com Directory allows users to link sites for their favorite leagues, wrestlers, resources sites and even personal pages. It's easy to add links and even easier to search for them. The Directory is here. Menu Features FW Central: Active since 1998, FW Central provides a stable forum setup for leagues to host their roleplay and general discussion. League fedheads can get more information about being setup on FW Central here. CSWA: The reason the site was originally created, the CSWA moved to FWrestling.com in 1998 after spending six years on the PRODIGY online service. The CSWA has its own domain at CSWAwrestling.com. Forums: In conjunction with Ringcrew.com, FWrestling suggests that you join us in visiting the Ringcrew forums for a different kind of discussion forum. Mature debate, frank discussion; it's about playing games, not bashing people. What Is FW?: Need some insight into what Fantasy Wrestling is? Get the info you need on roleplay, heading up a league, and even a brief history by checking out whatis.fwrestling.com. Hosting: Need space for your league or wrestler website? Look no further. FWrestling.com offer free hosting for FW-related sites. Take a look at the Hosting page for more information. Ringcrew: FWrestling.com's premiere partner is the top resource site led by [name withheld]. Ringcrew.com provides tutorials on FW, HTML, Poser, CGI and more, as well as editorials and hosting!
