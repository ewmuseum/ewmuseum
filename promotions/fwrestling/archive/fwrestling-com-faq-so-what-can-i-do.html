So What Can I Do on FWrestling.com?

1 January 2008 (migration timestamp; not necessarily first written then)
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Shock of all shocks — FWrestling.com isn't just a message board. As the new FWrestling.com rolls out, so do some of the "good ol'" features along with some new ones. Here's a partial list of what you can do so far: All Fedheads and Handlers Can: Post a "League Profile" where FWrestling.com viewers (over 20 million so far) can get all the basic info they need about your league and how to join, as well as check out your latest events, news and rankings. Include your league in a huge league index where users can search based on criteria about the type of league they want to join. Post in-character news about your league, character or current angle. Post event links and info to a broader audience and receive feedback through a voting process. Post your league's rankings Request a ballot as part of the FW Top 20 (league fedheads only) Upload your own league graphics for use in your league profile, news and events posts. Include your league's top wrestlers as nominees in the FW Top 20 Rankings. Receive free or premium hosting for your league, wrestler site or personal site. Affiliate with FWrestling and receive a special link on the league index. FW Central FedHeads Can: Receive highlighted coverage of their league news. Upload larger graphics on their league profiles and include more information. Vote as part of the FW Top 20 Post "Upcoming Event" information to hype coming cards. Receive premium hosting services for free. Special Contributors Can: Post features and columns bi-weekly to contribute to the site Post "how-to" and resource articles as part of the "What Is FW?" section Receive a ballot as part of the FW Top 20 rankings Receive premium hosting for free (based on the length of their contribution, etc.) More coming soon, assuming folks jump in and use the current functionality. If there's anything you think is missing, or if you have ideas, please post a note on FW Central or e-mail here. Copyright CS Enterprises 1998-2008. All rights reserved.
