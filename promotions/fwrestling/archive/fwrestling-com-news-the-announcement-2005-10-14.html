The Announcement

14 October 2005 (record-derived page)
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

It's certainly not as earth-shattering as say, a new Supreme Court justice, or even as mind-blowing as Tom Cruise actually allegedly fathering a child, but here it is.

Beginning shortly (see more below), I'll begin posting what I'm currently referring to as the “FW Daily.” (If you've got a better name, e-mail me and I'll give you the prize of my undying gratitude.) Just to make things confusing, I don't plan on posting it daily, although the intent is to eventually get there with your help.

So what is it? Well, first, realize that unless there's public outcry (which I don't expect), I'm removing the “Latest Circuit Headlines” from the front page. They may make a return down the line in some other form, but considering few folks were putting in the effort to use them anyway, I don't think it's a great lost. In their stead, FW Daily will serve as part-blog and part-news-hype-machine. Think of the old “Daily Lariat” on that site Steve and I once helped start.

So why should you care? Well, for one, FWrestling.com, despite needing a content update, is still, by far, the number one visited fantasy wrestling site on the web – meaning that what's posted here is seen by an average of just under 200,000 visitors per month accessing over 3,000,000 pages on the site. For two (like that?), because you love me deeply. I know… you can finally admit it. Even you, [name withheld].

So what I am asking of you? To get in the habit of e-mailing [detail withheld] with any or all of the following:

— in-character news about your league or your character that you'd like to highlight — out-of-character news (new leagues, accepting apps, want-ads, etc.) — basic spotlights on hot characters or feuds that you're following in your favorite leagues
— info on upcoming league events (cards, tournaments, special events, etc.) — questions (inane, evil, or otherwise to put me on the spot) that you would like to have me answer or give an opinion on (or that I might poll other folks with).
— commentary on just about any FW-related subject (that might even get you a request to be a regular contributor)

Note, of course, that I maintain editorial control, which means that there's a chance that not everything will make it in, but I'll try and let you know if that happens and why.

If and when I start receiving some responses, I'll kick things off (hopefully beginning next week).

On a personal note, as life gets busier and busier for me (as it has and does for many of you), I find that it's too easy to shove things that seem ‘less important' (like this hobby) to the wayside. But I also realize, through experience and through the company that I work for, that it's important to maintain balance – meaning that there's a time to work and a time to play, and that one can't completely overtake the other. All that said, this is an attempt to keep myself involved on a more regular, scheduled level – to do that, I need your help. I need your input on what you'd like to see, I need your news/questions/commentary/reviews to keep my interest level up…and honestly, to keep me feeling accountable to you guys to post your content.

I'm hopeful that this will make is easier to send content in, since you don't have to log-in or be given access to a posting system, all you have to do is e-mail [detail withheld]. [name withheld] also has access to that e-mail, so be sure that I'll be enlisting his help (as available), just to annoy him.
