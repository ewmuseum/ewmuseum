Submitting League Top Ten Rankings

1 January 2008 (migration timestamp; not necessarily first written then)
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Submitting League Top Ten Rankings is intended to allow leagues to highlight their top characters (and hard-working handlers), as well as display the league's champions. By entering rankings each month, leagues also gain a ballot in the overall "FW Top 20" and have their ranked wrestlers automatically added to the ballot nominations. 1. Log into the Content Manager and select “Submit League Top Ten” from the main menu. 2. Fill out the form using the following tips: a. “League”: Choose the league you are submitting an event for. Note: the league must have filled out a league profile in order to be included in the list. b. “Subject”: This is the rankings name. For example: EPW Top 10. c. “Top Title Name”: The name of the top league title. For example: NEW World Champion. d. “Champion's Name”: The name of the character holding the 'top title.' If the title is vacant, simply put Vacant. e. “Top Ten Listing”: List your rankings here, one rank on each line. For example: 1. Dan Ryan 2. Eli Flair 3. Deacon 4. And so on 5. And so on 6. Until you get to 10. 3. Frequency: You may enter rankings on an as-needed basis, but they should be no more than weekly. 4. Wrestler Mini-Profiles: Ideally, those in charge of rankings for a league are asked to enter a VERY brief wrestler profile for those on the rankings. This allows users to see more info on the character, as well as a link to their full profile on your league site. See "Entering Wrestler Profiles for Rankings" in the FAQ. 5.
