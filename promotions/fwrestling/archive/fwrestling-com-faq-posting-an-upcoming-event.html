How do I post an &quot;upcoming&quot; event?

1 January 2008 (migration timestamp; not necessarily first written then)
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Adding upcoming events is limited to FW Central leagues and major affiliates. If you would like access or have a major upcoming event you would like to add, simply post a note on FW Central and we'll do our best to help. If you do post an upcoming event, please remember to go back in and EDIT the event once it has occurred to make it appear in the "Latest Events" area. Simply click the "Upcoming Event" checkbox OFF and put in a link to the event. Copyright CS Enterprises 1998-2008. All rights reserved. Graphics done by [name withheld], MonkeyMaster, Esq.
