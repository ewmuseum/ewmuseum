So maybe this should be the “Monthly Dose,” huh? The good news is that I'm back from over two weeks of training and traveling. The bad news is that I'M BACK!

A limited little blog this time, since what news I did receive in my [detail withheld] mailbox is just about outdated. I'll reference it below, though.

The Hype: TEAM

So [name withheld] has figured out that the easiest way to get me to hype something is to name the trophy after me. I encourage the rest of you to follow his example: trophies, awards, cash prizes, children… whatever has meaning for you.

TEAM originally started as a concept for a new league based on team wrestling, featuring wrestlers thrown together randomly or brought in as a league ‘team.' Now, instead, it has become a 32-man invitational tournament, featuring a number of wrestlers from FW Central, Primetime Central and A1Wrestling (A1E/MBE). Keep in mind that all three of those communities have traditionally been havens for roleplay leaguers – those who like the tet-a-tet of the back-and-forth, the competitive aspect of the promo. That's not to say there isn't storyline, but in a tournament like this, the characters have to get to know each other first as they create feuds and stories throughout the run. Some angle fedders could learn from this – sometimes it pays just to throw some guys against each other and see what chemistry develops, as opposed to angling out a year's worth of a feud that may not work from day one.

So a quick sidenote – exactly how much is [name withheld] paying me for the use of the CSWA Auditorium that he's holding the “Greensboro Regional” in? I mean… it must be something, right? Tom, you can use that little Paypal button on the main page.

While freely admitting that I don't know all the names involved, and that I'm not up on the roleplay (given my latest trek into wilderness that is New York City), I thought I'd give a couple of early favorites for you. But first, one more sidenote…

A quick snippet from the TEAM Tournament Preview show shows that [name withheld] owes me even more cash.

SJ: I disagree though. Merritt is under a federal investigation for fraud, has killed midgets, hell, he's even cut off someone's arm with a lightsaber.

SM: That was a fantasy sequence. [name withheld] still has both arms intact.

SJ: Well, my point stands. I actually think that it takes away from the prestige of the tournament to have his name attached to it.

So a very brief look at the brackets and my comments:

Philadelphia Regional Hida Yakamo vs. Saint Irish Red vs. Timmy Windham Karl Brown vs. Trevor Hawke Steven Shane vs. Steve Johnson

Most interesting match in this bracket just from the title has to be Irish Red vs. Timmy Windham. Having had a significant role in developing Timmy over the years (though I don't run him now and haven't for years), my heart goes with everyone's favorite Muppet Kid, even if we do now know that he is only a Windham by marriage and that he's in thrall to the evil Mickey Benedict. Everybody should be allowed some mistakes, huh?

I think the sleeper match in this one is a toss-up between Yakamo/Saint and Brown/Hawke, with my edge going to the latter (for what it's worth).

Calgary Regional Wildstar vs. Logan Caine Cameron Cruise vs. Beau Michaels Promo vs. Mr. Entertainment Larry Tact vs. Phenomenal Frankie Scott

Again, Wildstar brings back fond memories of the early 90s. Hornet's original protégé who turned on him to escape his shadow. Since then he's become the Five-Star Wonder, in the ring and on the stick. But I think his run through this tournament is pretty limited.

Most intriguing match here should be Cruise/Michaels, but I'm better it turns into the year's biggest “Match That We Are All Worse Off For Having Seen.”

Larry Tact and Promo are fun names that have made their limited runs at various times. It would be interesting to see who wins if they both reach the second round.

Greensboro Regional Dan Ryan vs. "The Prophet" John Adams Rocko Daymon vs. "Yours Truly" Adam Benjamin Andrew Gilkison vs. Shiek Nasty James Irish vs. The Sargeant

Ah…Greensboro.

Dan Ryan and James Irish certainly have the names in this one. Ryan has proved he can work as a character in both the FW leagues and A1 – and if it means anything, he's got “home field” advantage. Gilkison is another strong A1E name.

But the match of interest in my mind is Rocko Daymon vs. Adam Benjamin. Why? Hopefully you'll see.

St. Louis Regional Boogie Smallz vs. WildChild Victor Molotov vs. The Mighty Impala Ken Cloverleaf vs. Joe Average Beast vs. Alcaeus

And last but not least, St. Louis. Home of the mighty UNC Tar Heels NCAA Basketball Tournament victory last year. Hark the sound of Tar Heel voices…

Whoops, I think that was sidenote #12 already. I haven't done my research to find out if this WildChild is [name withheld]'s or another. My guess is it's another, which gives me the chance to lean towards one of the most underrated characters of the last several years, Boogie Smallz.

The Mighty Impala should probably be gone in round 1, and the other two matches seem straightforward, assuming Ken Cloverleaf and Beast show up. But I've noticed a bit of hype behind PTC's Joe Average, and I know nothing about Alcaeus.

So there you have it – a thinly-veiled hypefest for the TEAM Invitational Tournament. You too can have hype – you just either have to name something after me or pay me… it's really quite simple.

Circuit News and Want Ads

As I said, not much in the mailbox considering the delay, but here's what I've got. Remember, more info can be found on the main page under “Take Two: News From The Dose.”

NWF Blackout went up recently… and don't you have to love any event that features a “Sadist Match?” I mean, really… how much over-the-top can you get then a domed cage and an electrified cage with not only scaffolds and weapons inside, but lined with C-4 explosives. I'm assuming the lower seats in the arena were blocked off – I hope the view from the Georgia Dome's upper deck row YY was good.

XWF opened up as of last week and is looking for signees. The first card has been booked and is available on their website.

Ultimate Championship Wrestling held their first PPV event from the MCI Center in Washington, DC on November 12th.

The show included a main event that pitted Dan Ryan going up against The First and Irishred for the very first UCW Championship crowning. Others on the show included Cameron Cruise, Johnathan Marx, and Cross.

fWo announced their Wrestler of the Year Tournament participants. Full list is available in the “Take Two” news items.

XWF opened up as of last week and is looking for signees. The first card has been booked and is available on their website.

Empire Pro's Black Dawn PPV is in the middle of being posted as this goes up. If you've never seen Beast or the trainwreck that is a Joey Melton vs. Cameron Cruise match, then you need to take a look. And in a cheap personal aside and homage… HORNET WINZ!!!!~ Not only does he win, of course, but he makes an out-of-CSWA appearance thanks to Dan Ryan.
