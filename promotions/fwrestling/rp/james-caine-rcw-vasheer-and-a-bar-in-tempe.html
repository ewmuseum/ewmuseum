==================================================================== ~~~VaSheer, Surround by the mists, and brimming with life. Alastaire looked to the King's castle from high atop his dark keep. His last assault on the castle was a success at first, that was, until the King and his first wizard found that infernal spellbook deep beneath the castle. At that point the king had learned his true power, the ability to use both positive and negative magick. While he himself could only conjure positive magicks, much like the first wizard that he had once been a long time ago for the first king. Alastaire still believed this king was different, while everyone else claimed he was the king who vanished years ago. If only Alastaire could prove they were all wrong...but how? How could he...
Alastaire quickly turned and proceeded down the stairs of the tower, his dark robes flowing behind him as he moved briskly. It was so simple, he thought, he could use his positive magick to create a phantom image of the former king all he needed was the spell with which to do it, and he knew, for better or worse, right where to find such a spell.~~~
==================================================================== |---July 26th---| |----- 2K8 -----| |-Tempe,Arizona-| The lights flickered as the beats pounded throughout Caine's skull. He stood at the end of the bar, watching as all the silly little people danced away their cares of the previous week, alcoholic toxins running through their bodies, distorting their senses. The smells of sweat and pheromones, mixed with alcohol and puke hung in the air like stink over a cow pen. It was all rather humorous to James who was never really the social type. This wasn't his type of place, but he was here with a friend, not to mention it gave him the chance to think about things. James turned to see that his attractive female bartender had brouoght him his second drink. He never had a problem being social enouogh with the ladies, but he wasn't the type of man to approach most women, unless he got that itch. As of late he had lost the itch and he wasn't quite sure why, then again the drive hadn't been the same since Starr had left him with his dick in his hand, and forty dollars to his name.
"HEY...JEN...JEN! Can I get....UGH!" the red head said as she stepped up to the crowded bar, squeezing between Caine and his friend.
"What's your poison?" Caine's friend asked her. she replied motioned him to lean closer so that he could hear her a little better over the blasting music. "Bacardi, Pineapple juice, and Malibu...they call it Gorilla piss in vegas."
James' friend turned to the bartender, Jen and motioned with one finger, she came to him almost instantly and asked what he'd be having. He told her the order, and to put it on his tab, and that he'd go ahead and have a couple more for him and James.
"You didn't have to buy it." the red head said with a smile. "I wanted to, would you care to dance?" "Mikey, you sure you can afford to be buying drinks for everyone and their mom?" James asked his friend.
"I wouldn't mind at all, just let me get-" was as far as she got as Jen brought their drinks right over. Mike, and the redhead, who ironically turned out to also be named Jennifer, took their drinks and disappeared to the dancefloor.
James watched the taller geeky white boy dance a bit, chuckling to himself, but Jennifer was smiling so apparently he wasn't doing to bad. James turned back to the bar, and called over Jen.
"What ya need sweetie?" she asked. "Lemme get two straight shots of Seagrams, and a Jaeger bomber." "Looking for a hangover? You haven't even finished your bacardi." "Actually I hadn't even touched it yet... gimme a second here." James said as he grabbed the glass and looked for Mikey and Jennifer. He spotted them quickly and turned back to Jen. "You just get my drink's I'll be back in a minute." James said as he grabbed his glass and headed over to Mike. He took Mike's empty glass and replaced it with his full one.
Mike stopped dancing for a moment to look at James, slightly confused, "Not drinking?" he asked.
"Meh, I placed a different order. Have fun." caine said with a smile to him and the cute little redhead, before going back to the bar just in time to recieve his shots. "Thank-you cher." he said to Jen, as he grabbed the two shotglasses. He tapped them on the table one time before downing the shots with a straight face.
"Now there's a man who can drink. How ya feelin?" Jen asked him with a smile ignoring the customers screaming their orders at her.
"The same as when I came in." "Don't I know you from somewhere?" "Yea, here." Caine replied and Jen laughed before heading off to grab some other orders. James just turned his eyes back to the dance area. He was glad most of the people were inebriated and it was fairly dark, so almost no onoe recognized him. He began to slowly nod his head in tempo with the beat as he felt the alcohol slowly start to enter his thought process. 'What was he doing here, James Caine doesn't club. James Caine sits at a bar where the jukebox plays classic songs from his youth, and the sounds of balls cracking on the pool table mixes with the sound of laughter.' he thought to himself.
James was a quiet bar type, he preferred the smell of booze and friendliness as opposed to all this action and excitement, but then again he was here with two goals. Get Mike a piece, and take his mind off the epic ass whooping he was gooing to hand to little Willy Mccainish. Lately James had found that was all he really cared about anymore, and maybe that's where his itch ran away to. Maybe the fact of the matter was he preferred violence to sex. James quietly lost himself in his thoughts as he downed his Jaeger, and watched the night go by.
