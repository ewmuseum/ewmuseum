Book XI: Chapter XVI Interview with a Madman [Part 3] "Each of the four men got exactly what was coming to them. In the morning I awoke early to make the preparations. You see in Alice's basement, the cells are not cells, but merely large steel boxes,a door on each side, that can be moved. I arranged the boxes in the shape of a cross, locked each of the inside doors save for one, and kept four other doors open. I then had the lights fixed to the center of the cross, keeping the rest of the area dark."
"And you did all of this because someone raped a girl, you didn't know?" Stone said from his place at the table.
"The story doesn't end there. I had each of the four men bound and gagged in their sleep. I entered the center of the cross, waiting as each of the men were beaten to without reason before being tossed in the cells. They slept as I just stood there, lost in my own meditations. After a couple of hours the sleeping agents wore off, and they began to awaken. I smiled and cracked my taped fingers. The first door slowly opened, and Taka stepped forward. The others began peering through the small barred windows of their cells."
"Taka... You and the other three men in this basement have been found guilty of rape, and violating MY RULES." I said to Taka as he stood there confused. "How do you plead?"
"H.. Plead Insanity..." Came the beast like remark from another of Taka's Pesonalities. "Insanity won't save you here... " Was all I said as I lunged foreward. "I wrapped a hand beneath his chin, and twisted it around as I moved swiftly beside him. His neck made a sickening crunch that echoed throughout the basement. Taka crumpled, and the next door swung open..." I paused in my story to take a drink of my beer, and light another cigarette. The reporter behind me typing furiously, as could be notd by the sound of furious clicks and taps of keys.
"I rose from a knee to look directly into the eyes of Lax. He apparently knew what was going on because I had just reached a standing point when his knuckles conected with my Jaw. Now I do ask you, Mr. Stone, to take into consideration that it has only been three weeks since I was taken out of action with multiple bodily injuries. Not to mention the hole in my chest still burns."
"How can you get away with this?" Stone asked, the tapping of keys pausing as he awaits my response.
"Alice's Asylum has no laws... Mister Stone... That is all you or anyone will ever need to know..." Was all I said as I stared at the static of the screen, recalling how my palm felt as it made the smashing contact with Lax's nasal pasage. The force compacting the small fragments of cartilege into the fore front of his brain. He fell face first to the floor. They all did, every damn one of em...

She watched the face of death, and vengence as she stared at the screen. biting her nails nervously, knowing this man who was taking vengence in her name was eisking his very life. But why? He barely knew, or uderstood her.
Cheri thought quietly to herself, as she rubbed the itching marks along the inside of her right elbow, and watched this almost total stranger she laid with a night before, kill without mercy the four men who had taken her without her sober consent. She wondered if this man knew the truth, or could ever find out. what would he say, or worse... what would he do?

Rugal's door swung open and the large agile man stepped into the circle, I ran towards him, and lept flying through the air, aiming a kick towards his temple.
He ducked, grabbing my ankle and tossing me into the steel. I crashed hard landing on my shoulder. It stung but only for a moment, I winced briefly and when I opened my eyes a large boot was moving quickly towards me.
I grabbed the foot, and twisted it. The large man spun to the ground allowing me to roll backwwards and to my feet. I waited only long enough for him to get to one knee, his other leg extended and balancing him, before I cracked my foot into his face.
"And that was the end of him, I gather." Stone said pausing to take a breath before the tapping of keys returned.
"Not quite..." It was going to take more than that for him. Rugal was an animal, and a very violent one. He did what he wanted, when he wanted. He was back to his feet quickly and his hand wrapped itself in my hair.
He yanked me sideways, throwing me to the ground, before placing a kick to my chin. The kick spun me to my back, and I managed to roll away from another driving heel. This time I rolled and swept his knee out.
I was quick to capitalize and straight DDT'd him into the hard concrete ground. As I started to roll from the drop his arm wrapped my waist and he carried me with him as he stood. He dropped me with three russian suplexes into the ground.
"He stood there then. Just growling at me." As I regained my self, he grabbed me by the back of my neck, and pulled me to my feet before thrusting me towards the metal wall of Sutter's cell.
I threw my feet up and ran up the wall, flipping myself over the beast. I landed in time to recieve a kick square to my gut. The opening was far too obvious.
Teeth flew from Rugal's mouth as my foot impacted the side of his face. A superkick, hard, fast, and clean to his jaw. That was all it took. He fell to the ground, and I mounted myself above his back, grabbed a fistful of hair and began to smash his face into the ground.
"I smashed his face until it collapsed, until the skull couldn't handle the impact any longer.Blood was everywhere."
Creak... Sutter's door opened slowly. |Interview with a Madman... Pt. IV|
