D.o.M._2K8 presents: Psychotic Perfection -->Episode II<-- -->Ka-BLAAAAAMM!!!<-- ------------------------------------------------------------------ ::Black..:: ::Black empty Space...:: ::There is nothing...:: ::Where have all the flowers gone?:: ::Where are the Stars?:: ::They've all gone away:: ::No more do they still come out at night:: ::But I do...:: *Tick* *Tock* *Tick* {With the sound of ticks a clock swings to the right of the screen, and at the tock swings back the left. Following the third effect as it swings back to a second tock it fast wash out fades and Yasunori Mitsuda and Matthew Valente's 'Corridor's of Time' begins to play as the scene shows the black and white video of James Caine stepping onto numerous wrestling stages.}
::James Caine:: I've been in this business a long time... {James speaks solemnly over the music just enough for it to provide a melodic ambient background to the greyscaled video of James Caine prepping in an empty outdoor ring, apealing to the crowd at massive events, and warming up at other various live events}
::James Caine:: I've seen a lot of faces, and been in in so many places... {As the song continues in the back scenes of James in pain, with blood showing in color through the grey. }
::James Caine:: I've made a lot of friends...but for every friend... {James is seen with his former cliques, highlighting days with Tombstone, Friday, Vic Powers, Kain, Freddy Ryder, and Star Caine.}
::James Caine:: I've made twice the enemies... {James is seen in classic feuds with Plague, Kyle Neverwinter, Eliminator, Rob Evil, Chuck King, Scorpio, the DoS, Kaige, Evan Michaels, Gothic Angel, Slayer, Xavier Caige, Logan Filth, Firestarter, Evan Michaels, Juggernaut, King Kill, and his lone battle with Trent Steel.}
::James Caine:: But it's true what they say, pain is temporary... {Shots of Caine enduring horrible punishment} ::James Caine:: Chicks dig scars.... {Caine showing off his back to numerous women, and just being with many women.} ::James Caine:: Glory... lasts forever. {Video footage of Caine raisin numerous titles, awards, and trophies.} ::James Caine:: And Legends...Never Die... {The song fades with Caine being shown laying on his back looking up at the lights as various ring announcers are overheard behind the song.}
