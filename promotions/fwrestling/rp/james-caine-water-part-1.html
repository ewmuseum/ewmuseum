Book XI: Chapter X2 [Part One]Water... <...The Ego Jason Davis... The white halls traveled endlessly, as the mists surrounding Wonderland slowly replenshied and became the illusion that is. a splendiferous thing it was, when one looked past some of the more disturbing parts of Wonderland. Now my only dilemma had become how to cross an ocean.
As the cat and I continued our steady walk through the Wonderland halls, the cat decided to strike up a conversation. "You know... How is it Wonderland is like one big castle... I mean I never really paid attention before I just poofed here and there... but now I notice it's one big castle with lands through it's doorways. Strage really."
"Yes, that's what wonderland is... Strange. And it is a giant castle with each of it's doorways leading to another section of Wonderland. One to the Hatters house. One to the Diamond King's castle. One to the forest, and to the rabbit hole... Well that lies behind us." I answer him. "Really however, it all depends on what the fairies want you to see. and what they want you to see is based on your thoughts."
"Well that answers that part of the question... what about the fact that, according to your map, we still have an ocean to cross?"
"Yea, I'm workin on that one Cat..." I answer but was more or less kinda hoping the Luminae would lend us a hand.
As we came to the final door I paused. I held my breath, a moment, hoping to see something familiar, something from Va'Sheer on the other side. I grasped the handle firmly, and turned, hearing it click, before swinging it wide open.
======================================================================== The screen opens to a sounds loop with the introduction of Motorhead's 'Orgasmatron', and I see James Caine sitting in his promotion room. The walls a white around him as he is seated on a stool in the center wearing a Black shirt that reads 'Piss On Imperium' that hangs lossely over his faded dark blue jeans, as the song continues its loop in the background.
"You know... OWFWrestling.com has a poll located on it's Main Page for fans to vote on. The poll asks who has been more dominant over the past few weeks. The choices are between, Imperium. OWF United, and Scarred. As of this morning, Thursday the 31st... imperium is leading massively. And yet as I have done my part week in and week out. I find that I can actually agree with that. I mean, if we measure up the amount of blood lost by each of the three factions I would have to say that Clearly Imperium is dominating United and Scarred. However there are those who doubt what I say."
James lights the cigarette hanging limply from his lips and inhales deeply, and exhales before continuing.
"And my response to those of you who so choose to disagree, I urge you to watch this classic video promotion that clearly supports my statement.
As the introduction finishes out, I see footage of James Caine's fantasticly charismatic entrance.
'I am the one, Orgasmatron. The outstretched grasping hand. My image is of Agony, My servants Rape the land. Obsequious and Arrogant, Clandestine, and vain. Two thousand years of misery, Of torture in my name. Hypocrisy made paramount. Paranoia the law. My name is cold religion... Sadistic, sacred whore.' As the lyrics begin I watch the footage of James Caine bitch-slapping Trent Steel across the face in the middle of the ring. Knocking Plague in the chin with brass knuckles, Jim Morris bashing steels face, and Caine magically appearing behind him. I am then shown footage of James 'Psycho-crushing' Steel through a table, and lastly flaunting with a gorgeous red headed female in the audience who is wearing one of his tee-shirts cut short.
'I march before a martyred world, an army for the fight. I speak of great, heroic days. Of victory and might. I hold a banner Drenched in blood, I urge you to be brave. I lead you to your destiny, I'll lead you to your grave. Your bones will build my palaces, your eyes will stud my crown. For I am Mars, the god of War, and I will cut you down.' I realize it is the shortened version of 'Orgasmatron' that has become James's entrance theme, as the second verse kicks in. The video montage behind shows Scarred's first all-out attack on Imperium, in which Caine is seen hammering away on Angel. The montage transtitions to Caine standing on the stage holding an unconcious Chris Green, and then fades to him grabbing Dk's throat shortly before transitioning to the stage collapsing. The last shot is of Jesse Williams holding a handful of nuts, bolts, and screws with a smile on his face. The songe ends with footage of Imperium's small attacks on Scarred that left no real impact other than a minor flesh wound to the group.
