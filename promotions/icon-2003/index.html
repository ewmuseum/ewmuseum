Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

ICON introduced itself on 8 July 2003 with “Meet the Icons,” a roster bulletin naming most of its own characters — among them Alex Jones, Brady Lawson, Dark Bishop, Handsome Henry, Lark Fenriz, Magnus Zahn, Rudy Ensasserol, the Scarecrow, Sean Clayton, Xavier N. Vain, and Zodiac — and naming its own first card, Episode ZERO, as close at hand.

A follow-up post of 1 August 2003 announces the promotion's own founders poster, showing the ten men who actually competed on Episode ZERO: Handsome Henry, Zodiac, Magnus Zahn, Dark Bishop, Rudy Ensasserol, Brady Lawson, Sean Clayton, the Scarecrow, Lance Dewlock, and Xavier N. Vain.

No transcript of Episode ZERO, and no later roster or results document, survives here. How ICON's own run played out isn't in the surviving record.
