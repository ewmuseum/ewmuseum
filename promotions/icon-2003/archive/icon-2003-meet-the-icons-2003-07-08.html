Very short-tempered, talk smack to Brady and you're liable to get smacked or worse. Finisher: Gravel Grinder - Crossed Arms Brainbuster 'THE NEW ERA' BRIAN JORDAN Height:6'5 Weight:275 Wrestling Style:Technicial brawler Stereotype: Power house Personality: Imagine HHH after his quad as a face Finisher: Crossing Jordan - A Fisherman suplex off the top rope combined into a pin DARK BISHOP Height: 6’1 Weight: 227 Wrestling Style: technician, high flyer, shooter Stereotype: risk taker who has his moves backfire sometimes Personality: comedic with aggression Finisher: Bishop Bomb '03 - corkscrew burning hammer DARREN BLADE Height: 6'2" Weight: 243 and 1/2 pounds Wrestling Style: Technician/High-Flyer Stereotype: Cocky, pretty boy, rich kid, jerk. Personality: Blade is a cocky, arrogant rich kid. He will go to any extremes, even to make himself look somewhat foolish in order to repay that favour three fold to his opponent. Blade has been known to use his rich schemes in his plans as well. Finisher: Guillotine Death Drop - Sleeper Hold held into a reverse DDT. 'HANDSOME' HENRY Height: 6’1’ Weight: 210lbs Wrestling Style: All Rounder (specifically technician/brawler) Stereotype your character falls into: Prettiest boy or Bastard w/body of Greek god. Personality: Handsome Henry is god’s gift to women. When he steps out of the backstage area he makes more than one male audience member question their sexuality. He isn’t like every other pretty boy wrestler, he’s prettier. The only thing to surpass his beauty is his wrestling ability. Finisher: Beautiful Dreamer - Locks on a sleeper hold, then hits a reverse Russian legsweep so his opponent is face down on the mat, then turns the sleeper into a choke. ICHIRO 'ICHIBAN' HAKISAKI Height: 6'1" Weight: 233 lbs Wrestling Style: A technical brawler, similar to Hakushi. Stereotype your character falls into: A Japanese Movie Star/Game Show Host/Wrestler Wrestler's: A Japanese Movie Star/Game Show Host/Wrestler. Ichiro is a lovable character, albeit, incredibly corny and full of bad stereotypes. Finisher: Japan Finisher Number One - A Spinning Double Arm Piledriver 'THE WORLD'S MOST FAMOUS WRESTLER' LANCE DEWLOCK Height: 6’2 Weight: 232 pounds Personality: English goofball Wrestling Style: Technician, with a little heel-esqe brawling thrown in to boot. Finisher: The Final - Steve Corrino’s Old Skool Expulsion, basically a Twist of fate applied starting from a reverse DDT position rather than a regular DDT position 'THE WOLF' LARK FENRIZ Height: 6'5" Weight: 250 lb Wrestling Style: Power/Brawler Stereotype: "I hate the world!" gothic loser with a penchant for Satan. Personality: He's a mystery man. No one really knows anything about him, other than he can really fight in the ring. He's attitude is unique, especially considering his extremist views. He takes a more serious attitude towards life but is still a pure trouble maker in every way. A extreme loner. Rarely talks to a soul. He's... just evil personified times ten. Finisher: Sammael ("venom" of god) - A sitdown reverse powerbomb (victim lands on stomach, not back) combo. The other part to the move is at the end Larks locks on a bearhug, wrenching the ] back further. He adds wraps his legs around the victim's body, pushing the victim's head into the ground. 'HELL'S GUARDIAN' MAGNUS ZAHN Height: 6'7" Weight: 319 lbs Wrestling Style: Technically skilled powerhouse Stereotype: Destructive monster Personality: His natural disposition is pissed off... Even when he wins he's pissed off... Magnus doesn't play well with other's, he is the classic loner... Keep away from him and you won't get hurt... Alright he'll still probably hurt you but not as much... He'll maim his own Mother is he's in the right mood... Finisher: "Oh Yea.....It's ZAHN!!!" - Burning Hammer 'THE ULTIMATE HUMAN WEAPON' PSYCHROBAT Height: 6'3" Weight: 243 pounds Wrestling Style: He's an insane, fearless high flyer who also is very technically adept. Stereotype: Silent but deadly assassin. Personality: He will take an insane risk. Other than that, he's almost robotic with precision, never questioning the next move he should take. Ringmaster does all the talking and is a brash, cocky and shameless promoter of his young protege. Finisher: Big Top Suplex Technical name / description: German suplex off the top, into a bridge. 'THE ROCKSTAR' RAWK ROCKINGTON Height: 5’11 Weight: 210 lbs Wrestling Style: high flying, brawler Stereotype: over the top comedy character Personality: Rawk is his own biggest fan. Unfortunately, he’s oblivious to the fact that no one else likes him. The fans, despite his face-like demeanor totally hate him. Finisher: The Head banger - Top rope DDT RUDY ENSASSEROL Height: 5’10 Weight: 180lbs Wrestling Style: high flyer. Stereotype: 80’s baby face. Personality: because of his very young age, Rudy is pretty stupid and not too "worldly." He is easily manipulated and trusts people too easily. Finisher: Teenage Suicide - corkscrew shooting star press from the top rope THE SCARECROW Height: 6’10 (but appears taller due to thin frame) Weight: 195lbs Wrestling Style: Submission Stereotype: Psychotic, but a new breed, as he lacks the power and motivation most "monsters" have. Personality: Desires the chance to cause pain and discomfort, in any form. He’s cunning as a fox, cowardly when needed, and never misses an opportunity to strike. Finisher: Silent Scream - Stands opponent in corner facing center ring and climbs behind him. Applies a chokehold and leaps outward. Opponent will land face first with all of The Scarecrows weight on his neck and throat. Jumping Bulldog you could say. 'THE NEW DISORDER' SEAN CLAYTON Height: 6'5" Weight: 289 Lbs. Wrestling Style: Brawler Stereotype: Destructive powerhouse Personality: This wild man has emerged into the professional scene after his dominance of the amateur leagues. His incredible size, along with his breathtaking strength, has seen him quickly rise to the top, but as far as Clayton is concerned, the rising isn't over. Clayton, like most others, strives upon being the best. But in his case, he has to be the best. There is no other alternative. He is not your everyday guy who will go out and bully anyone; in fact, he is completely the opposite. Clayton will carefully choose his victims, and once they are chosen, he will not rest until he has inflicted some kind of damage. Finisher: Disordered Drive/Double D - Spear 'THE MOMENT MAN' TODD MORROW Height: 6"3 Weight: 290 lbs. Wrestling Style: blue-collar brawler Personality? Loveable, hard-working, passionate guy. A wrestling fan. Finisher: Final Moment Description: Todd behind the opponent. He grabs the arms and executes a spinebuster motion, driving the opponent face first onto the mat. XAVIER N. VAIN Height: 6'1" Weight: 234 Wrestling Style: technician Stereotype: cocky rich boy heel Personality: arrogant. cocky. self-assured. Xavier N. Vain has always had everything handed to him, and he expects the wrestling world to do the same. He has the utmost confidence in his natural athletic gifts, and is always willing to tell anybody who listens all about it. With Vain, it isn't bombast-- he thinks it is candor. Finisher: X-Plex Description: fisherman's suplex ZODIAC Height: 6'9" Weight: 385 Wrestling Style: power shooter Stereotype: destructive monster Personality: The man is a killing machine, simple and plain, no one knows where he came from, who trained him, or anything of that nature, only that he enjoys inflicting pain and making people uneasy with his dark outlook on all things. Finisher: The Evil Eye - Zodiac gets double nerve hold on either side of the opponents neck, and then lifts them high into the air, peering into the opponents eyes with his evil red eyes, usually getting the submission. If not, then he drops it into a falling slam and will go for the pin. HADES Height:  6'3" Weight:  273lbs. Wrestling Style:  Technical wrestler, is also highly skilled at submission and mat wrestling, and can brawl with the best of them. Personality:  Mix the attitude of a Stone Cold with the wrestling knowledge of a Bret Hart. Hades is a wrestling purist, and absolutely despises the glitz and spectacle of it.  He doesn't believe in things such as gaudy costumes, pyro, valets (in fact he is very masoginistic and believe that women have no place being in the sport). He is a bad-ass who won't back down from a challenge.  He has a bit of a God complex (obviously), and believes everyone to be inferior to him in the ring.  Hades is constantly trash-talking, any time that he has an advantage in the match. He has a vice with alcohol though, and is known to break character and get a little goofy at times if he's on a bender.
