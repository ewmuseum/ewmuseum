OvWL — Rules

Ohio Valley Wrestling League Information Packet

10 June 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
OvWL - Ohio Valley Wrestling League Information Packet Version 1.0 -------------------------------------------------- Contents - - Mission Charter - Concept - How It'll Work - Guidelines - Cards - Titles - In-Ring Rules - Application - Short Poll (2 Questions) -------------------------------------------------- Mission Charter: Ohio Valley Wrestling League- Founded by Brad Congelio in June 1999 Brad Congelio was a e-fed wrestler in many different federations, but the politics of each stopped him from competition. He stopped even watching wrestling on television with disgust. One day, he was offered an invitation to view a wrestling federation. These men were unknowns to the world, and what they did was merely wrestle for the joy of the sport. He saw men such as Tony the Train wrestling in matches where they would put their lives on the line, all for their own appreciation, and to entertain the crowd. The crowd at this event was the best he had ever seen, and he saw that this is where it should be. It was no longer just small time to him, it was what it should be. He then went into the ring, and many of the fans no longer took his side. They saw him as a former superstar in wrestling that was only there to show what he could do, as sort of a cocky individual. He knew that that was hardly the case. He showed up weekly, and gave the best battles he had ever done, even having a slightly
harder time in the ring. The fans though, unanimously wanted him out, but the owner saw him as a wrestler, no better, no worse than any of the other competitors. Brad Congelio stood on the sidelines a while, and watched the action. He saw none of the screw-job endings that made the big companies what they were. He saw only people holding grudges until they were settled in the ring. He didn't see the owner intervening to make one person more of a star than anyone else. Jacob then saw what he was to do.
Thus, the Ohio Valley Wresting League was born. Congelio sold all his earthly possessions to buy a warehouse, and a ring. And he restored the building with a few friends, and just plain manual labor. The OvWL Arena is in Wellsburg,
West Virginia, in an area of town where few travel, but hopefully more will.
Congelio is not interested in making himself big, he has already proven what he can do. He is more interested in giving the people, and the wrestlers what they want.
-------------------------------------------------- The Concept: Okay, this is the deal. You may think your wrestler is a superstar, but they all have to start off somewhere. And I think this fed is a good place for them to start out. This is a "rookie" fed. Where wrestlers will gain a fan base, and someday expand. There are no "real" wrestlers in this federation. Look at it this way, you can't put someone who's made it big in a smalltime fed. The fans won't appreciate it. I hope to make this fed an "underground" type fed.
Where fans bring video recorders, and send away their tapes to other fans of wrestling around the country, so to speak. The wrestling matches will start off in the warehouse, probably for about a month or so, that is where every card will be held, until a demand from the public builds us up. Then the fed will begin to spread out, into neighboring cities and what not... then to other states, then to smaller cities in further away states... until the federation has a lot of fan support, and THEN, then there will be a weekly televised event... before that it will be on public access. The crowds will then start filling the buildings more and more, until there would finally be a full house in the OvWL Arena, and a new building is erect to serve the same purpose. Then maybe a "real" wrestler or two would come in, but none of the ones you can see on television every week with WWF or WCW or ECW... only ones that aren't wrestling in the big productions... so it can seem more realistic.
The idea behind this fed is so that it can act in cooperation with real life, rather than conflict it with wrestlers from all over being involved. Like, right now, people like Jake "The Snake" Roberts would be able to come in on occasion to spice things up a bit, but that wouldn't be until the fed hits syndication. After it hits syndication, the work load will seemingly be the same. Hopefully the fed will boost to a point where it's one of the big ones... what with having numerous television shows, and Pay Per Views...
-------------------------------------------------- How It'll Work: Here's how I want to work this out a bit. For the first month, it will be as if someone sent you a recording of the event, from the crowd. There won't be any announcers of any sort, except for the ring announcer. All segments that you send out, I ask that none of them been in a crowded arena, with tons of people chanting your name, or anything like that. Your actions rather than your words in the OvWL will determine your fan base at first. You'll have to read the card to determine how you should have your fans reacting to you in your segments, at first, your segments should be done in the OvWL Warehouse.
We won't have camera men, we won't have announcers, we won't have interviews, aside from the ring announcer. After a month, we will get a public access show, where commentary will come into play. This will be when variations as to the place of your segments can come in. You won't have to constantly do your segments in the OvWL Warehouse any longer, you can do them, basically anywhere
BUT the crowded arenas will people chanting your name. There won't be tons of fans just yet. At this point, events will be held anywhere from the arena, with more fans in the seats, to high school gymnasiums, to bingo halls, pool halls, to bars, etc. etc. etc. This stage will probably last about 2 to 3 months, before we start going to smaller cities' Civic Centers and Coliseums.
That's when the OvWL Warehouse will start selling out each time we are there. The show will start going into syndication, and being played late night on lesser companies networks. (i. e. ECW is late night on Sunshine Network and
America One) It will probably be some station that no one has ever heard of, but that's OK, that will increase fan base. It will become a possibility then that a Pay Per View could be held one every 3 or 4 months... until it the fans start loving it. As you can tell, there is a long term plan for the federation. I hope it stays around long enough for it to become one of the bigs in wrestling. That's the entire idea. An evolving federation, that will bring the people to superstardom. With faces and heels... and what not.
-------------------------------------------------- Guidelines: 1. Read the cards to see how fans will react to you. 2. Tell me how you want your character to come out of things (i. e. Heel/Face), and I can help make it happen. 3. Tell me if you want to attack people during their matches or afterward, it will help if you want to become a heel. 4. If you want to become a face, I might have you come out on rescue missions, that can set up abnormal tag teams, alliances, what not... but it will all work out. Don't worry, I won't do anything that you wouldn't want me to do.
5. At first... do not have the fans chanting your name, or overly cheering you. A cheer here and a cheer there, or a boo here and a boo there is all that's necessary.
6. Be sure to change as the fed changes. 7. Don't be afraid to experiment. That's what is really needed in the federation is people willing to take risks. 8. Don't try to challenge someone to something that the federation wouldn't be able to support. (i. e. Don't challenge to ladder matches when there is no way to suspend items from the roof. Don't challenge to boiler room matches when there isn't a boiler room. Weapons matches, and things to that extent can be supported in the federation's beginnings, but not huge gimmick matches. In fact, a cage match wouldn't be able to be supported at first, maybe a fence match.)
9. If you want to have a few words for you opponent before the match starts, go ahead and send them in to me, and I'll put them in. 10a. The fed will not be sent by way of BCC, so keep lude comments that can get you in trouble to yourself. It's too much of a hassle to send out things
BCC for me, so it's just better if I didn't. Please put a disclaimer on anything that would be offensive.
10b. No TOSing of other's roleplays. Yes, I know that I just said put disclaimers on offensive things, and to keep comments to yourself, but I wouldn't have to say that if you would abide by this rule. It may sound like a given, but 10a. would not be a necessity if it were.
11. Try not to hold personal grudges with other members of the federation. I can't stop you if you do, but please bare in mind that it is based on fantasy. It's all in good clean fun. 12. I'm going to try to run the show myself, but if I need help, I don't think I'd ask, so I'd appreciate if you'd offer a helping hand. I hope to have everything under control before that happens.
13. I'm going to try to have the roleplaying win out over an angle. Sometimes it's a given that an angle will come out, but PLEASE, I beg of you, understand when that happens that it is not an insult to the person on the bad side of it.
It's only one match, and if it's an angle, I'm sure that one match is easily affordable. 14. A champion will not be crowned until we hit television (Public Access). Then there will most likely be a one-night 8 man tournament to crown the
Champion. It will be a while until a lesser title is born, probably right before we reach Pay Per View-able status.
15. At first, you will be allowed either one character or one tag team (per screen name). If, after you have proven your worth to the federation with the one character limit, you wish to take on a higher responsibility, with another wrestler, you may be able to do so, however. If you begin to slack off with the usage of one of the characters, please either withdraw one of the characters, or I may end up taking them away.
16. Everyone wants to make it big, with your help in RPing and if I can make the shows great, I really think this could happen. -------------------------------------------------- Cards: Month One: There will be plenty of house shows, matches chosen randomly, winners based on both my decision, drawn out of a hat, or how you've been RPing thus far that week. Results will be sent out as if reading the indy-fed results from a newsletter.One card a week will be sent out in full match format, these will be the matches you roleplay for. Most likely there will be
8-10 matches on these cards. But even if you don't have a match for this card, you might still want to roleplay. I might send out an agenda of the week, (i.
e. the highlighted matches for each houseshow) as if they were promotions to get people in the seats. These matches will be sent out like houseshows, but they will have buildup with the fans, and will be in the later moments of the card. None of the events will be named in Month 1, and all will be held in OvWL
Warehouse.
Month 2: There will again be plenty of house shows, in surrounding cities. Minor cities mainly, within the Follansbee-Wellsburg area, given in results format.There will be one show, on public access, with one announcer: Stan
Homage. The matches will be in full match format, with color and match commentary from Stan, every other week the matches will be in OvWL Warehouse.
8-10 matches.The agenda idea will probably keep rolling like that, so that if you don't have a match on that card, you'll probably be in one or more of the highlighted matches of the week.The P. A. show will have a name, but that is undecided at this point.
Month of Syndication (Probably 4 or 5):The house shows are a definite thing. They will be held throughout Wellsburg, West Virginia and surrounding states. In minor cities mainly, Follansbee, Weirton, Wheeling, etc.The syndication show will have two announcers, Stan Homage and Louis Anderson. The matches will be in full match format, color commentary from Stan, match commentary from Louis.
They will all be in upscale cities, with every 3rd week, they would be back in the OvWL Warehouse. Again, 8-10 matches.Again, and agenda to keep the seats filled.The syndication's show name will be the same as the Public Access show.
One of the other shows in the week will also have a name. In the later months of syndication, the television show will be given in result format, but there will be a special event instead that is in full match form. (this is like the
House Party events or Clash of Champions or what not, where they are PPV caliber, but on regular television 10 or 11 matches each).
Month of Pay Per Views (starting most likely about month 8 or 9):The past events will go as normal. The named house show will be put on television, but remain in the same style. The special events that are put out in full match format will be put on Pay Per View once every 3 months or so, being a little larger scale than before with matches. 12+ matches per Pay Per View card.
Everything else will go as normal. -------------------------------------------------- Titles: The Ohio Valley Champion will not be crowned until we hit Television, as previously stated.
On the Public Access show, one week, there will be a one-night-8-man tournament to determine the champion. Fatigue will come into play. Afterward, the champion will defend the title once every other week on television. He may fight other matches, and he may put it on the line at other times, but he is only forced to defend it once every other week on television.
When special shows come out, he must defend there. That does count of the once every other week. If the champion decides to put the belt on the line two weeks in a row, he is still required to put the title on the line after a week passes.
The Ohio Valley Tag Team Champions will be crowned after the Ohio Valley Champion is crowned, and when we have a large enough base for a competitive Tag Team Division. They will be forced to defend their titles in the same manor of the
Ohio Valley Champion. The Syndication Championship:Will come after syindication... (hmmm.) This title will be defended on every syndicated television show. It isn't a nessecity to defend otherwise, but can be.
Other titles will be decided upon later on, but I've been using a lot of ECW concepts in the creation of this federation, and this is what they are working with right now. I don't think there will ever be a hardcore championship of any type in the OvWL.
-------------------------------------------------- In-ring Rules: 1. A count-out will be reached if the wrestlers do not make an attempt to reach the ring, or are unable to reach the ring at a 10 count. Two men can only be counted out if they never try to make it back into the ring. If they try, but are stopped, the count is broken.
2. A disqualification will be decided by the referee of that match-up. Most of the time, if it happens on the outside of the ring, it will go. If something is brought into the ring, but from the ring-side area, it may be allowable. Weapons brought from the back by wrestlers are disqualifyable upon the the referee's discretion. (i. e. it may be allowable to bring in a chair from the outside, but if a chracter were to bring a customized weapon (like
Jarrett's guitar), they are more likely to be disqualified.)
3. Titles can change hands in the following ways: Pin-fall Submission Count-out Knockout Titles cannot change hands by disqualification, therefore most title matches will be no-DQ. This is designed so that if the Champion wishes to maintain their title by losing their matches purposely, it cannot happen. If they walk out of a match to retain a title, they will lose it. If they try to get theirself DQ-ed, it can't happen. If they are counted out of the ring, based on how the count-out rule is set up, it will be because they cannot attempt to answer the 10-count on the outside of the ring. The count-out victory will therefore be deserved, not a hoax.
4. Pin-falls are made when a combatant has his shoulders pinned to the mat for the referee's three count 5. A submission is aquired when either the person answers to the ref that he gives up, says the words, "I quit," or taps the mat three times in a manner where is doesn't appear he is reaching for the ropes.
6. A knockout is when a wrestler is on the mat for a referee's 10 count without being touched in the process by their opponent. Also, if a wrestler is in a submission move, and quits fighting, if the referee raises the man's hand
3 times, and it falls either to the mat, or down limp each time, that is considered a knock-out.
