Wrestler Application [8]. Closing Statements --------------------------------------------------------- * Word from the President... The Ohio Valley Wrestling League follows the trend laid down by the original National Wrestling Alliance... Truly hardcore, traditional wrestling. In it's glory days, the National Wrestling Allaince was a better 'traditional'
wrestling federation than World Championship Wrestling, and a better 'hardcore' wrestling federation than both the World Wrestling Federation and Extreme Championship Wrestling. The difference lies in the fact that to the National Wrestling Alliance, their traditional wrestling was more hardcore than anything found today. We will have the specialty matches similar to the World Wrestling
Federation, but ofcourse we will have many more traditional matches without bloodloss or anything like that. However, every time you step into the ring in the Ohio Valley Wrestling League, you run the risk of serious injury. As for championships, the competitors in the Ohio Valley Wrestling League understand that these titles are something to be honored and fought for with every fiber of their being. Here, the champion does not give credibility to the title, the title gives credibility to the champion! You are not walking into this federation and getting a title without a fight. You are not going to become the number one contender without a little old-fashioned blood, sweat, tears, and dedication... but that's what 'real' professional wrestling is all about, isn't it?
Brad Congelio. President, OvWL --------------------------------------------------------- * The People behind the Scenes... The OvWL is operated by _ONE_ person. His name is [name withheld], or usually "[name withheld]" if you get to know him well enough. He started the planning of the Ohio
Valley Wrestling League nearly 4 months ago, and it is just now starting to take shape. Brad knows e-wrestling inside and out, considering the fact that he has been in it for over 3 years now. He is very qualified to run a federation and _WON'T_ let it die before _HE_ dies himself.
--------------------------------------------------------- * Codes of Conduct... There are several 'no-brainer' rules in e-wrestling, and I am hoping you are atleast somewhat aware of what I mean when I say that. Therefore, I will only cover those rules which the OvWL stresses more than any others. However, these
'no brainers' should be recognized as unwritten rules, and should be followed. [1]. This is number one, because it is the most important... have some fun! If you come into the Ohio Valley Wrestling League looking for nothing but fun and excitement from roleplaying , as well as good angles and cards, then that is exactly what you will get.
[2]. This promotion is run through a closed-system format. Meaning that all flashes will be sent to myself, and compiled into the OvWL flash show: 'OvWL:
War of Words'. The maximum number of flashes you are allowed on a single show is one, therefore if you feel like writing quite a bit, make it into one flash.
If you have any questions about this, you can contact me. [3]. If you have a match, you _must_ roleplay to win. It does not matter to me, if you are the biggest e-wrestling 'name' on the internet. If you do not flash, then I guarantee you will not win. Simple as that. I do not say this to be mean, but roleplays are the threads that keep a federation together. I can only do so much to keep this place running, flashing is what you guys can do to keep us from closing down.
[4]. There is no specific length your roleplay has to be, as long as it is quality. If I were going to guess, I'd say 20-35 lines is a good average. This gives you enough dialogue to get your point across, but not too much to where you repeat yourself over and over... These are not official minimums and maximums, they are just suggested guidelines.
If you have any questions about these e-mail myself. The OvWL, primarily, does not go around suspending everyone. We will definitely be quite lenient, but if someone disobeys to an extreme extent, they will be punished... See, now the rules werent that bad were they?
--------------------------------------------------------- * OvWL Events The Ohio Valley Wrestling League has signed an exclusive deal with the United States Satellite Broadcasting network, to show all weekly cards on the Home Box Office. We pride ourselves on delivering quality professional wrestling to homes nation-wide once a week. Our mix of red hot wrestling action, with twisting and turning plots leaves the viewer on the edge of their seat!!
[a]. The OvWL's main brodcast is known as 'OvWL: Breakdown'. Hosted by Matt Canistraro and Kristen Bioux, two of the top colour commentators in the business today. Their chemistry on the microphone is fantastic, and you will love to hear them argue with each other every week. There are normally seven to ten matches a week on this program, with the possibility of interviews from some of the federation's top stars. Title defenses are usually of the
Television, as well as the Lightheavyweight or Tag Team Titles. Only during a non-Pay Per View month is the World Heavyweight Title defended here.
[b]. In the OvWL, our pay-per-views are second to none. Everyother month, the OvWL holds a Pay Per View... this is where all feuds will end, begin, or become even more personal!! We gurantee that when you witness an OvWL pay-per-view, that you are seeing the best professional wrestling possible. Try getting that kind of a gurantee from some other federation!!
[c]. To go along with the weekly television shows, and bi-monthly Pay Per Views, the OvWL also puts on several house show events throughout the month... During these shows, you will always see the best action in thw world today, which can only be brought to you by the Ohio Valley Wrestling League. While major title matches are a rarity, and title changes are even moreso, they do occasionally happen... and since not all of our fans can attend every house, we give you the full results during 'OvWL: War of Words' every Sunday and Thursday night .
--------------------------------------------------------- * Roleplays and Angles... I mentioned roleplays already in the rules, but let me touch upon them once again. Roleplays should be done entirely in character. They should not be for the most part shoot comments. I personally dont mind a shoot roleplay every once in a while if it is in good fashion, but let's not get too carried away. I give an inch, let's not try to take a mile. ACWA has a one roleplay per show limit. I am not forcing anyone to do a roleplay, but trust me, if you have a match and do not roleplay for it, you will _not_ win! Now if you don't have a match, I would still ask you to do a roleplay, just because it surely can not hurt you, but can only help you. I see you trying even though you dont have to and I smile and your name just might get a nice push. Everything you get out of the OvWL revolves around what you put in.
[a]. 'OvWL: War of Words' is a program that airs on Sunday and Thursday nights, and features comments from OvWL superstars as well as a rundown of recent house show results, and hypes upcoming weekly shows and|or Pay Per Views.
Now, I can honestly say angles are my favorite things in any federation. I am going to say right now, sometimes an angle, if it is good may make you lose a match even if you did have the better roleplay. Sometimes that is just the way the cookie crumbles. But hey, it is all in good fun. Now on angles ... if you are a wrestler in this fed and feel you are going nowhere and need a good angle or feud with someone let either me know. I would be more than happy to help you. Heck, that is one of the main reasons I am here. I want our wrestlers to succed as well as this promotion.
--------------------------------------------------------- * Championships... Each title has permanent stat bonuses. Television and Lightheavyweight are lesser, while Heavyweight is larger. Tag Teams have their own bonuses, seperate from the singles division... If you lose a title, a rematch will be signed for two to three weeks after the time you lose it. The rematch will be against whomever holds the title, not the person you lost it to, some times in a Triple
Threat Match, but usually in a one on one situation... Title holders do not have to accept anyone's challenges, however it would be in your best interest to accept reasonable ones. As advice, it isn't a good idea to go straight for the Heavyweight Title. Start with the Television Title, then work up to the higher belt. The more you work, the better you get. That is a huge rule here...
World Heavyweight Title: Quite possibly the most prestigious title in professional wrestling today. Such men as 'Iceman' Shawn Hayes, Angel Reaper, and Mark Andrews have all held this gold around their waists at one time or another. To capture this belt, is to state you you are among the elite in the industry today. It is defended atleast once a month, usually during a pay-per-view. If there is not a pay-per-view that month, then the title must be defended on a broadcast of Breakdown, usually the last one of the month.
World Tag Team Titles: May be the most prestigious title in tag team wrestling. To capture this belt, is to state you are among the best of the best in tag team wrestling that the industry has to offer. The belts are defended twice a month, including pay-per-view defenses. If there is not a pay-per-view that month, then the title will be defended on a broadcast of Breakdown.
Television Title: This is definitely an endurance title. The holder of this title definitely has the most grueling schedule of all other champions, as the gold must be defended once a week, during OvWL Breakdown. Although the schedule is a hard one to keep up with, winning this gold throws you into the driver's seat... as you become the number one contender for the World Heavyweight Title.
Lightheavyweight Title: The most prestigious title in professional wrestling, for anyone under 225lbs. To capture this belt, is to state you are among the best of the best cruiserweights in the industry today. It is defended twice a month, not including super card defenses. If there is not a pay-per-view that month, then the title will be defended on a broadcast of Breakdown sometime during the month.
