NEWF Looking for New Stars

2 September 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
NEWF New England Wrestling Federation http://newfonline.esmartdesign.com -------------------------------------------------------------------------- -------------------------------------------- The New England Wrestling Federation is gearing up for a revival of one of the top independant federations in New England but we need you to reclaim the glory that we once had. The NEWF is based in Concord NH and runs a weekly show on Friday Nights titled "NEWF Ground Zero". The card will come in commentary style with backstage happenings and interviews just as you would see on RAW or Smackdown every week. The NEWF attempts to make fueds and storylines that are interesting and have the Wrestlers involved in all aspects of the storyline. We feel that if the handler is not comfortable doing an angle or has ideas for the angle then we don't want to dictate the whole thing and we allow all wrestlers input into their characters. In The NEWF your character development and advancement is based totally on your performance. If you are a dedicated role-player, show effort and put effort into storylines, Your dedication will pay off for you. We try and make the NEWF a fun place for everyone involved and make it your game. We are here just to write and watch what you guys come up with. Since the NEWF is a regional promotion and we are trying to make this as real as possible we will be starting out in small arenas, just like any other independant promotion does. We also groom our own stars. We don't care if you are some big time guy in another fed. If you come into the NEWF you will start out at the bottom and work your way up. You rise depends on your participation. If you are dedicated, It will be quick. If you don't show dedication. Then it will be slow or non-exsistant. However we do occasionally bend the rules and if you have a history with a top tier player then we might work that into a storyline and bring you in and have you guys fued. However most of the time that is not the case and you do start out at the bottom. The NEWF has in recent weeks begun to upload all their old results to their homepage and we urge you to check them out. If you like what you see please sign up and join along for the ride on the adventure that is the NEWF. If you have any questions please e-mail me.
