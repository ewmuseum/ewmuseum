NEWF Looking for Handlers

11 July 2001
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The NEWF is again looking for handlers. We are a small regional promotion that stresses fun and interaction between wrestlers and the Staff. We are planning on running one show a week and as we grow have more shows added. The Belts are as follows: NEWF Heavyweight Championship- The Top position for the Best in the NEWF. NEWF New England Title- The secondary title defended only in New England. NEWF Cruiserweight Title- For the best Cruiserweight in the NEWF. NEWF New England Tag Team Championship- For the best Tag Team in the NEWF. NEWF TV Title- Defended on TV only. We enjoy "building" our own talent and enjoy helping and watching beginners grow. So if you are new to the e-Wrestling community, please give us a try. At the NEWF we reward for dedication and effort. You might not be the best role-player in the promotion but if you show us you are dedicated to it, You will be able to hold a championship. So if you are looking for a new challenge or are just starting out, give us a look. It won't hurt [name withheld] President, NEWF Wrestling Inc http://newfonline.esmartdesign.com
