NEWF New England Wrestling Federation http://newfonline.esmartdesign.com -------------------------------------------------------------------------- -------------------------------------------- 1. What is the NEWF? The NEWF stands for the New England Wrestling Federation. The NEWF was founded in December 99 but closed in January. The second incarnation lasted the longest from April 2000-May 2001. Now I am preparing to restart the promotion with a different spin to organization. This time I want to run the NEWF as a regional promotion that would run shows in small venues like YMCA's, High School gyms, etc. just like the last two incarnations but this time I am going to attempt to fill the roster like Zero-One does over in Japan. 2. How would this work? In e-feds most make you sign a contract for x amount of months, however I want to run mine by appearance. You sign up your wrestler for x amount of shows. Instead of when you sign up and guarentee x amount of months, You would sign up for one show. After that you can tell me you want to be on the next show and then you would be booked for that show as well. I would also like to get a few under long term deals (months) so I could build my organization around them. 3. Why fill the roster like this? I am hoping that by doing it this way, people will be able to check out the fed for an event and if they like it sign for a long term deal or if they don't they can leave without having to quit. It would also interject some new life into the roster every so often, and I am hoping it would allow some big name e-wrestlers to tour a small indy. It would be like Stone Cold fighting some no name on an OVW card. 4.Sounds good. How do I sign up.
