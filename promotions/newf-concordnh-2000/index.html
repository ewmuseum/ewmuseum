Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

A promotion calling itself NEWF advertised its own opening on 26 March 2000, describing itself as a league “just trying to open” and pointing recruits toward a hypermart-hosted website. Its own later account of itself, posted that November, placed its true start around May — describing the November post as arriving in its “7th month of operations.”

A summer recruiting call, posted from Concord, New Hampshire in July, described the small promotion losing both of its main-event handlers at once and clearing out its own roster's dead weight to make room for replacements. A further pair of notices that September and November asked for writers to help carry a second weekly card, the November post noting that its own operator had written every show alone up to then.

A rival league advertising under the same bare initials the day before this one's own opening call drew a public reply distinguishing the two: the other promotion's first show, its own owner wrote, was already booked for the very next night.

No card, roster, or result of any kind survives in the held record. Four recruiting notices are all that remain of a promotion that, by its own account, was still standing seven months after it began.
