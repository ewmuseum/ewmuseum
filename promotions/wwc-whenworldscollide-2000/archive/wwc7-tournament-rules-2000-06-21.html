WWC7 Tournament Rules

21 June 2000
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
http://www.geocities.com/~iwa/WWC/ Yup. It's that time again. Time for all the biggest, baddest, meanest and savviest role players on the Internet to give it their all and see who the best ewrestling role player out there really is! For those of you who are unaware, the "When World's Collide" tournaments have been going on for almost 3 years now and we've see TONS of the top talents from about 200 different federations go at! The WWC tournaments started out as a simple get together of the top talent of several of the top federations on the Net and they have become a quarterly event that draws ewrestlers from all over the web who are looking to prove themselves on a TRULY global scale! There are no "Fed Favorites" or "Prez's Pets" or "Big Name Shoe-in's" here! The WWC tourney's were built on one premise. To make sure that the best ROLE PLAYER'
S get the glory. Not the "Golden Boys" and the "In Crowd." With that all said and done, let's get on with the rules.
There are 10 rules to this next tournament, WWC7. Obey the rules and everything will be hunky-dory. Break ANY of the rules and face being immediately kicked out of the tourney and banned from all future WWC events.
Here they are: Any and all handlers are more than welcome to sign up ONE and ONLY one wrestler for the tournament. The tourney brackets as usual will feature 128 of the best singles wrestlers in the game. Once there have been 128 applications accepted, that's it. Tourney is full until next quarter. IF YOU
ARE GOING TO SEND IN AN APPLICATIONS PLEASE MAKE SURE YOU CAN AND WILL
ACTUALLY PARTICIPATE. I WILL KICK OUT ANYONE WHO SIGNS UP AND THEN NEVER
ROLE PLAYS! In order to apply, email an application to
[detail withheld] listing your real name, your wrestler's name (ONLY original names! We won't accept any "Stone Cold's" or "Undertakers" or "Original Bad Asses" or stupid crap like "Mr. Death" or "The Unbelievable" Jon Doe, etc.), his height and weight, your finisher and what federations you're CURRENTLY (Or most recently.) in. Applications MUST be received NO LATER than midnight, June
25. Final brackets and role playing for the tourney will begin on July 1! The first round of role playing will end July 7 at midnight. Each wrestler will be RANDOMLY paired up against an opponent. YOU CAN OR CAN NOT ROLE PLAY
AGAINST THAT OPPONENT. IT'S UP TO YOU. The Judges will pick the top 50% of role players to progress to the next round. (This is very important because the best HALF of handlers progresses every round. You AND your opponent for that week may both move on, assuming you both are in the top 50%.) People can begin role playing for the next round Saturday morning at 12:01 AM on the next role playing board and the next round brackets of 64 handlers
(Exactly 50% of the original participants.) will come out on Sunday. The same process will continue for the next week and that Sunday the number will be whittled down to 32 for the third week and then the following week the same thing whittling the number down to 16 for the fourth week. The fourth week is the final week with all role playing finally ending at midnight on
Saturday, July 29. The 5 title winners and the top 16 in ranked order will be released on Sunday, July 30.
Concerning role playing, you CAN NOT use another wrestler in your role plays without his permission. You can TALK about other wrestlers and you CAN insinuate things about other wrestlers, but you CAN NOT have another wrestler take part in one of your role plays. Role plays need to be IN
CHARACTER. Please, no Out of Character (OCC) bull crap! (I know there is a fine line here, so use your common sense.) There can be NO CURSING OR SEXUAL
CONTENT IN YOUR ROLE PLAYS!!! I mean it on this one! If you have to curse, put in something like f-k or s##t or $%^#& or something. Flagrant cursing is a no-go. Likewise, no sex. You can't INSINUATE your having sex with someone and you can't describe it or talk about it. If you want to make general sexual commentary, that's fine, but if it's border line or worse, I WILL delete it without hesitation! Like wise, NO graphic or sexually explicit pictures or music will be allowed in ANY role plays! NO HTML will be allowed in the SUBJECT line of a role play either! DO NOT change the HTML of your
Subject line or your role play WILL be deleted. Lastly, AND MOST
IMPORTANTLY, each handler is ONLY allowed THREE role plays PER WEEK in the first two rounds and FIVE role plays PER WEEK in the last two weeks! Make your role plays good and long! YOU CAN NOT SPAM THE BOARD WITH ROLE PLAYS!
DO NOT respond to anyone else's role play. If you have something to say, make your own role play about it. DIRECT RESPONSES WILL BE DELETED ON THE
SPOT! That's about it with regards to role playing. PLEASE ABIDE BY ALL OF
THESE RULES! If you don't, your role plays will be delete, you'll be kicked out of the tournament and you're IP address will be banned from the boards!
There will be 5 Judges, all of whom have been past participants in the WWC tourney's and all of who are among the most respected names in the ewrestling business. (Don Jones, Jim Harper, myself, etc.) Majority rules in the voting and trust me, NO members of one federation and/or "the big names"
will be favored over the other participants. I hand picked these other 4 guys to help because they all know a good role play when they read one and they are all unbiased. We five like to see the best ROLE PLAYERS win. Not just our buddies or guys we know. The judging will be COMPLETELY impartial and unbiased. Trust me on that one.
That's about it. Those are all the rules. IF YOU CAN'T DEAL WITH THEM,
PLEASE DON'T EVEN BOTHER APPLYING FOR THE TOURNEY! If they all work for you and you want to prove yourself on the most recognized, efed independent forum, feel free to send in your application. Send in your application before the June 25 cut off and tell all of your friends and collogues to feel free to sign up as well. If you ask anyone who is familiar with the WWC tournaments, you'll find that it's one of the most tried and trued tourneys out there and to even place in it is not only widely respected, but can help your ewrestling career immensely! Ask any of the former winners, not the least of whom includes such names as Heartbreak Meyers, Jackylfan, Sheep
Mangler, Jimmy, The Polish French Fry, Interrogator, Deviator, Dutch Daon,
Dan Sweeny, Dickie Wreenkle, Jimmy Jailbird, Ranma Saotome, Frank "The
Postman" Letters, Mike Haggar, Destructo, Special K, "Tiger" Terrence
McGregger, Ryan Johnson, "Too Sexy" Steven McKnight, Undertow and Raymond
Faith. See you at the tourney! WWC Webmaster PS If you have any questions please POST THEM UNDER HERE! DO NOT EMAIL THEM
TO ME! Thanks! PSS For more information about "When World's Collide" or about the WWC tournaments or to sign up for the WWC7 tourney, please feel free to visit the WWC home page at
http://www.geocities.com/~iwa/WWC/
