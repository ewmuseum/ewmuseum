Career history

Lee Smith was born on 1st December 1975 to a Mrs Smith. Unknown to Lee, the father was not documented. His mum was a cleaner to a Hollywood star and had had a one night stand with him precisely 9 months before. His Mum confronted the Hollywood star who bribed her not to tell the media. He bought her a mansion in a Hollywood suburb. Lee was a fairly average child until at the age of 7 he learnt some useful moves in self defence classes. He also attempted Judo, but quit before his vocabulary of moves extended beyond belief. It was when he was 12 that he first became interested in the showbiz industry. He watched his favourite movie star regularly, and had been told he resembled him. Of course, the relation was a lot more than resemblance. When he was 18 he left school and enrolled in a wrestling school, the Keening School of Grappling Arts. He studied for years until he became good enough. He passed with flying colours - one of the most talented graduates the school had ever had. His ego began to rise slowly. In 1994 (at 19yrs of age) Lee decided he would become a stunt man. It turned out a movie producer had been keeping a close eye on him - and so he accepted the role to be a stuntman to his favourite Hollywood superstar, the one he had been told he resembled. This Hollywood star became uncomfortable with Lee’s presence (he realised it was his son) and upon his orders, the director fired Lee. Lee Smith became extremely bitter and interrogated the director until he discovered the real reason he had been fired. The star of the show, the biggest star in the movie industry….did not like him. Later that day he confronted the movie star. At first he wouldn’t budge but then the truth came pouring out. He explained how he had been paying his Mother monthly allowances until she had fled the country three years earlier. Lee was livid and asked how many people knew. It was their secret, and so the Hollywood star paid Lee double the amount he had been paying his mother. The money was rolling in as Lee also had two other jobs, a night time shift at a supermarket and a daytime job at the Keening School of Grappling Arts. Lee’s father died in suspicious circumstances a year later. His Hollywood career had by then plummeted and so he put himself in debt paying Lee. It is suspected the death may have been some kind of scam as his fathers body was never recovered for post mortem. In April 1997 Lee began work at an amateur wrestling federation called Golden State Wrestling. He named himself Lee The Lion .He had much success in GSW, and had the highest salary on the roster. By this time Lee’s ego had rocketed. He had had several relationships with women, many didn’t make the one month mark - depending on how quick Lee got bored of them. Lee scooped every single title in GSW by 2000 when his contract ran out. He left on a high, with a sellout crowd at his last match. Lee took a break after that and was scouted by Dave Nelson, owner of International Wrestling Federation. Lee immediately signed up as “Legendary” Lee Smith, the name which would soon be spoken by millions. He made his debut in a triple threat match. Unfortunately Lee fell to the knees of Washdogg, which increased the heat between the two. Eventually after many hard fought battles Lee became involved in a rivalry with Norwegian Viking Thor. The rivalry began as the two locked up for the number one contendership of the hardcore title, which was currently held by Devastation. After two draws Lee finally fought against Thor. Thor emerged victorious after cheating. Lee was furious and crashed into President Dave Nelsons office. Thor claimed that he had been threatened with the sack if he did not screw Lee out of the #1 spot. The match at the PPV against Devastation which was at stake had already been recorded two weeks before as it was a boiler room brawl - which is why Dave wanted Thor to win although Lee was clearly the better wrestler. Lee was absolutely astounded and announced to the IWF fans that the match was pre-recorded. Lee was suspended after this outburst and soon found himself living in luxury. IWF Closed shortly after, and Lee was paid off with a huge sum. In the summer of 2001 Lee was approached by a man who claimed to be Bill Clinton - but clearly wasn’t. Bill explained that he was the President of a global federation called FAW and that he was looking to sell his shares. The federation was in crisis as the Co-President, Tom The Bomb, had been kidnapped by a member of the roster. The FBI had been called in to investigate and there were rumours of unsettled members - who were everyday being offered multimillion contracts with the likes of WWE (Then WWF). Lee used the money he had gained from his biological father and GSW as well as his stint in IWF to buy 50% of the shares in FAW. Lee immediately became a liked figure as he helped to uncover the kidnapping of Tom The Bomb which was carried out by the Omens of Death, Spawn and Crimson Lord. Over the next few months Lee became a hated figure, a liked figure….and once again a hated figure. In January 2002, as the war with xCw was threatening to come to a close Lee Smith clinched Tony Vigilante - locking him into a contract with the federation in a move which would prove to be absolutely crucial to FAWs survival as a dominant federation. Tony signed after a bust up with xCw owner and FAW member The British Bomber. The upcoming pay per view was When Worlds Collide. It was an xCw/FAW Production. The main event was a royal rumble, and the rules were clear. 30 men were chosen, 15 from FAW, 15 from xCw. Lee made a last minute switch and placed the man he had so much faith in- Tony Vigilante, who replaced a wrestler who was holding an injury. And so the stage was set - Vigilante making his debut in the biggest Pay per view in the entire World. Millions bought it, billions watched it - and the tension rose to an almighty high. With 10 minutes left in the rumble, xCw looked like they could be closed FAW down. The rules stated that if xCw won FAW would be permanently closed and if FAW won Lee Smith and Tom The Bomb would gain control of the federation and could do whatever they wanted with it. Tony Vigilante was looked upon as no more than an accessory, a wrestler used to fill the gaps. The Great One had stayed in from almost the very beginning and was by now dominating. But no - a great shock occurred as he was tossed out of the ring. We were left with 3 xCw wrestlers and 3 FAW Wrestlers. 2 xCw Wrestlers, 2 FAW Wrestlers. 1 xCw wrestler and 2 FAW Wrestlers. 1 xCw wrestler and 1 FAW Wrestler. And then finally, one Furiously Addictive Wrestling superstar triumphed and brought the event to an end. His name? None other than Mr Vigilante himself. Celebrations continued through the night as a memorable pay per view was capped off. As the months wore on we saw less and less of Lee on our screens. It was in June 2002, 13 months after he bought shares of Bill Clinton….that he finally departed on a very emotional note. But then, on 24th November 2002 Lee Smith was signed once again to Furiously Addictive Wrestling. The Legend was about to get bigger.
