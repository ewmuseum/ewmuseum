FAW Panic, 27 April 2002

27 April 2002

The camera zooms into the eye, and zooms out to see that the eye is in the middle of the ring and the crowd are screaming, Pyro's go off everywhere. The scene then cuts to the announce table where Floyd Rotterdamn, Shane Riot and Mario Ciaralli are seated. Mario Ciaralli: Welcome to FAW Panic! Floyd Rotterdamn: What a show is in store for you to night. Shane Riot: Were only days away from Breaking Point where Tony Vigilante kicks three others asses to become FAW champion.! Floyd Rotterdamn: we've got four action packed match for you this evening plus the Tom, JLC contact signing, so lets get down to busisness. VIP Visit We see Prodigy, Engima, Dandude and Curtis Ambrose are all walking around the backstage area. Shane Riot: There are a few members of FAWA. Floyd Rotterdamn: Oh Yeah, they're on a VIP visit. They bump into Baz. Prodigy: Hi Baz. Baz: Uh… who are you. Dandude: We're from the FAWA. Baz: Oh-- Okay. The four men begin to walk off until Baz assaults all four with a steel chair. Mario Ciarilli: MY GOD STOP THIS! Floyd Rotterdamn: This is sick. Baz destroys them then walks off. 9th Wonder Of Foam vs "The Polish Powerhouse" Stephon Michowski (Sorry match isn't written I had to do main event.) Winner: Foamy. Sweet Revenge The titentron flickers on and Overweighter is seen leaving EDH's locker room with a big smile on his face. Shane Riot: Why's he so happy after Tuesday i'm surprised he even showed up here tonight. The camera enters EDH's dressing room n the far end of the room EDH is seen tied and gagged to a chair as water from the shower pours down on EDH. Mario Ciaralli: After weeks of humiliation at the hands of EDH, Overwighter finally got some revenge. Shane Riot: But EDH will finish what he started this Sunday. Floyd Rotterdamn: We'll anything goes this Sunday when EDH takes on Overweighter in a Hardcore Rules match for the International Title. FWO locker room. We see Jim "JLC" Phoenix, 9th Wonder of Foam, Sabatash, Hardcore Scotty and Neo are all in their locker room having a chat. "Yeah" Jim states. "I agree." "Isn't it your contract signing tonight Jim?" Sabatash queries "Yes." Jim answers hatefully. "You want back-up?" Neo offers. "No, but thanks." Jim thanks them. "This is my business!" On that Jim leaves the room. Trent Hunter vs Sabatash Mario Ciarailli: Next up at this short addition of Panic is Sabatash against Trent Hunter in a normal rules match. Shane Riot: This aught to be a pretty decent match. Mario Ciarailli: Decent? What do you mean by decent? I think this match will be a great one. Shane Riot: I just think that this is going to be a quick match. I don't know exactly who will win but I think it will be a quick match. Floyd Rotterdamn You are confusing. Shane Riot: Ok, so what? Floyd Rotterdamn: Never mind! Shane Riot: Well here comes the first man out to the ring. Trent Hunter walks out from behind the curtain as his music blares over the PA. The crowd cheers for him as he walks down to the ring. Trent gets in the ring and stands in the middle of the ring awaiting his opponent. Then, "Nothingness" by Obscure Youth hits the PA and Sabatash comes walking out into the arena. Sabatash darts down to the ring and hopes to catch Trent by surprise by sliding into the ring but doesn't. Trent meets Sabatash with stomps as Sabatash slides into the ring. Adam Hutton calls for the bell and the match officially begins. Mario Ciaralli: Here we go, the match is on. Shane Riot: Quick one aren't ya? Mario Ciaralli: Huh? Shane Riot-- Idiot. Back in the ring, both men are up and they go for the tie up. Sabatash whips Trent into the ropes and then goes for the clothesline but Trent ducks and grabs him from the back and hits a German suplex on Sabatash. Sabatash gets up, but is soon meet with shots to the face and neck from Trent's elbow and then Trent finishes it off with a punch to the chest with both fists at the same time. Floyd Rotterdamn: Wonderful moves by Trent Hunter. Shane Riot: Yeah, if we are doing Ty-Kuan-Doe Trent picks up Sabatash off the mat and sets him up for a piledriver. He goes to lift up Sabatash, but Sabatash resists. Trent backs up a bit and knees him in the gut and then goes and hits the piledriver and goes for the pin. 1......2....kick out! Floyd Rotterdamn: Near win for Trent. Shane Riot: Told you this may be a quick one. Mario Ciarailli: It ain't over yet. Trent whips Sabatash into the ropes and when he comes back, Trent hits a sidewalk slam. Trent wastes no time and picks Sabatash right back up and hits a cradle DDT and then goes for the pin again. 1.....2......3 Adam Hutton calls for the bell and then raises Trent's hand in victory. Shane Riot: It is over and it was quick. Floyd Rotterdamn: Great match. JLC and Skipster.. Skipster is shadow boxing in his locker room with his FAW European title over his shoulder. There is a knock on the door. Skipster suddenly stops then turns to look at the door. "Come in" he calls. The door creeks open then out steps Jim "JLC" Phoenix. "We meet again. Grandslam Champion's." Skipster points out. "Indeed, but I just want to make sure we're on good terms. I didn't want to fight you. Tom used you as a puppet to take me ou--" Jim is suddenly interrupted. "Look here!" Skipster yells in fury. "I was no Puppet, that's why I turned on Tom because he was trying to use me as a puppet! I don't like you and I'm guessing you don't like me. But I hate Tom and I know you do too. So I will oblige… for now." Jim grins. "For now." He says then leaves the room. "The Tru One" Joshua Crowe vs Crimson Lord Winner: Crimson Lord The Omega Fro vs Morte The ring crew begin to set up the inferno pipes around the ring. Shane Riot: We'll everything will be set in just a few minutes. Mario Ciaralli: Yeah but with only two days until Breaking Point should Fro be in a match with this much risk involved. Shane Riot: Is Fro the World Champion? Mario Ciaralli: We both know he is. Shane Riot: Then as Champion he should fight whenever and in what ever type of match he is placed in. Floyd Rotterdamn: Mario, Shane has a point. Mario Ciaralli: Shane with a point? Never. Floyd Rotterdamn: We'll were all set to go here. "Brackish" by Kittie hits and Morte makes his way to the ring slowly. The crowd boo vigorously. Morte slides into the ring and awaits his opponent. Shane Riot: This is a great opportunity for Morte. Can you imagine a win over the World Champion. "Freedom Fighter" by Creed hits and Omega Fro makes his way to the ring. He slides into the ring and stairs down Morte. Fro hands the World Championship belt to the official. Floyd Rotterdamn: Were all set for this non title match. The inferno starts up and both men stare around the ring. The referee calls for the bell. Mario Ciaralli: Here we go. Shane Riot: So let me get this straight. There's no pinfalls, no disqualifications, the only way to win is to set your opponent on fire. Mario Ciaralli: Got it in one. Fro and Morte lock up and Fro sends Morte into the ropes. Morte bounces off the ropes and the flames around the ring jump. Fro hits Morte with a Samoan drop, Morte lands inches from the flames. Shane Riot: Move Morte. Move! Morte rolls away from the flames and into the feet of Fro. Fro lays a few kicks into the midsection of Morte. Fro raises Mort to his feet and nails a clothesline. Mario Ciaralli: Fro's in control right now. Shane Riot: We'll Morte's back to his feet Fro will be in danger now. Morte charges at Fro with a spear and begins to lay into Fro with a series of punches. Fro kicks off Morte and begins to nail Morte with a series of punches of his own. Morte again reverse and lays into Fro once more with punches. Shane Riot: Who's punching who? Fro kicks off Morte and sends him towards the flames. Morte springs to his feet and Fro nails a dropkick sending Morte over the top rope over the flames to the outside. Floyd Rotterdamn: We have a problem. Shane Riot: What problem? Mario Ciaralli: Morte is on the outside. Fro's on the inside and there separated by a wall of fire. That's the problem. Morte begins to pace up the ramp. Fro begins to shout at Morte. Morte turns and walks back to the ring. Fro catapults himself to the outside. Mario Ciaralli: Holy Shit! Shane Riot: You can say that on tv. Mario Ciaralli: Oh sorry. Both men are down on the outside. Floyd Rotterdamn: Is either man moving? Mario Ciaralli: Not what I can see. Slowly Morte begins to get to his feet. Shane Riot: Morte's first up. Morte gets to his feet and stumbles into the guard rail. He grabs a chair from a fan and walks towards Fro who is now to his feet. Morte swings the chair but Fro ducks and pushes Morte into the flames. Mario Ciaralli: Fro's done it. Morte runs backstage as Fro looks at the carnage. Shane Riot: Some get Morte a fire extinguisher. Tom The Bomb/ JLC Contract Signing "In Too Deep" by Sum 41 begins to pound out of the speaker system and is quickly drowned out by boos and jeers. As Tom the Bomb steps out. "Ladies and Gentlemen please welcome the president of Furiously Addictive Wrestling, Tom the Bomb." Hutton announces. Tom jaunts down in his fancy white suit with a black tie and shiny black leather shoes. He struts cockily up the steps then steps under the top rope into the ring. He then approaches the table that's in the ring on opposite ends is a black leather swivel chair. In the middle is a few sheets of paper on a clipboard. Tom takes a seat. Then the lights turn a familiar shade of navy blue getting a standing ovation. "The World Is a Vampire" are the words sang out of the speaker system as "Bullet With Butterfly Wings" by Smashing Pumpkins beats out. "Ladies and Gentlemen, welcome FAW Hall Of Famer, first ever grandslam champion and Commissioner Jim "JLC" Phoenix." Adam Hutton announces again. Jim "JLC" Phoenix steps out from the back wearing blue jeans, silver trainers, a black "Breaking Point" T-shirt and a black leather trench coat that's loose like a cape. He strides out slapping hands with various fans that are by the barrier. He glides down to the steps and gets to the middle steps then bows his head and stays motionless for a few seconds then swings his arms up in the air and his head back making pyros go off all throughout the arena. He glides across the apron then steps in the ring. He stares down Tom - dead in the eye. Then heads over to the opposite seat, not letting his eyes off Tom. He parks himself down then Tom asks Adam Hutton for a microphone. "Hi Jim." Tom greets surprisingly smugly as he's about to sign a contract putting his job on-the-line against a trained and successful fighter. "What's he so happy about?" Ciaralli asks. "He's the boss, he has the right to be happy." Riot adds. "But still…" Ciaralli moans. "Lets just get it done with." Phoenix growls. "Okay, Okay. Just to make sure this is the exact same copy of the contract your attorneys looked through." Tom informs JLC. "Yeah, Yeah. Tell me where to sign." Jim demands. "Here." Tom shows Jim and hands him a black pen. Jim quickly signs. Then he hands to Tom, Tom just flicks through with the grin not departing from his face. He signs the contract. "There--" "We done?" Jim asks impatiently. "Yes" Tom yells standing up then heads over to the ropes and points to the furytron. "Jim 'JLC' Phoenix versus Tom 'the bomb' with Skipster as special guest referee in an 'I'm your bitch' match. But I'd like to divert your attention to the furytron." Jim stands up and leans on the rope nearest the furytron as the seen shows FWO in their locker room trying to get out - but the door is locked then the same scene but with Skipster in part of the FWO. Then Jim turns round astonished and Tom shoots ink out of the pen into the face of JLC. This blinds him for a second then Tom hits Jim over the head with a clipboard. Then runs off laughing, as Jim lies on the floor unconscious with his eyes covered in black ink and his forehead covered in crimson blood. "Mother of Peal!" Floyd Rotterdamn gasps "Tom's just got one over on JLC."
