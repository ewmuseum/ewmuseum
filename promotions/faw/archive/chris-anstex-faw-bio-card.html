Career history

N/a right now but I just thought I should explain everything that has happened to me since I joined FAW as Chimera leading to Chris Anstex.May 23rd 2001: Filled in an app and wrote my first rp.May 24th till about 27th: On one of those days I got told that Bayne had replied to my rp. So I replied to him and we started an argument from there. Spike replied and instead of me and Bayne fighting we teamed. (Members of Krush were Chimera, Spike British Bomber and Bayne.)1 of the next 2 or 3 shows I had my first match which was a tag-team match which me and Bayne won.Then I had laptop problems and wasn't seen for ages. June 22nd 2001: I become 15 years old and got a computer for my birthday. Last week of June: I contact Jon Fox (the guy that got me into FAW) and he told me FAW's link so I could go back to rping.July: Went on a holiday for 2 weeks. Came back and put Spike through a table and beat Bomber twice. Then asked for a European title-shot.August: Won the hardcore title and lost it at the ppv to Crimson Lord. Joined Dark Gen 1 and became its last member. Joined the FAW corp.September 2001: Can't remember what happened.October: Lost internet and phone for 10 weeks.November: Had no internet.December: Got internet and phones working again but this was a bad month for me.Jan 2002: A rise for Chimera. Finally got a European title-shot after waiting 6 months. Won the title and held it until Feb.Feb: Lost the European title and the next day I quit FAW because of an argument. The next day of course I got hacked. March: Lost a 3 way match at the PPV and then got sent to FAWA. Couldn't rp much because I was mostly studing for exams this month.April: A really bad month because I was busy getting ready for exams.May: Had exams couldn't rp that much. Went one year in FAW as Chimera. Four days later I got fired from FAW and re-joined as Chris Anstex who is Chimera but trying to forget about him.June: Started having problems rping as Chris Anstex so I decided to try and add my Bone Smasher character into my rps and that helped.July: Went on holiday for 2 weeks and couldn't rp that much. In fact I don't think I rped once that month.August: Started having comp problems so this turned out as a bad month for me. I was only booked once this month but couldn't really do much.September: Got my comp fixed but took time off because of school work.October: Chris Anstex decided to go back to being Chimera.
