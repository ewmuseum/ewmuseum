(grandslam) Handler Information Handler Name: [name withheld] (Offer me a spot in your fed and die) AIM Name: [detail withheld] MSN Name: [detail withheld] Bio The special Hot Stuff dedicated to Jim 'JLC' Phoenix.May 30th, 1966. Amarillo, Texas is the place. When Mrs. JLC gave birth to 9 pound baby boy, who we shall call J…L…C. JLC has been a fighter since the age of 1-week-old, as he had bad asthma and nearly died at a young age. But he overcame. Lets skip a few years to when JLC was 5, JLC had just started school, when a big kid three years older than JLC was picking on JLC. JLC's parents didn't really care, so JLC and a group of friends in his same year tried to beat him up but the boy hurt them all. They all fell and cried except JLC, he fell but he didn't cry he knew one day he would make him fall, he knew one day he would topple him. JLC Was ten, and this boy was thirteen. JLC approached the boy while the boy was alone, the boy pushed JLC over and laughed JLC got up and picked a up a large stone and threw it at him hitting his temple, blinding him in his left eye. JLC was then thought of as a hero in Primary School. But that was JLC's first goal, he thought if he could do that, he could do anything. Then when he was 12 he had just started to watch Professional Wrestling when he was watching a Pay-Per-View, When he saw his hero win the World Title, As his hero celebrated JLC got inspired, a twinkle came into his eye, like it did the day he was five years old. No he didn't want just to become a professional wrestler, no he didn't just want to become a world champion in an Indy fed that wouldn't last 10 seconds. But he wanted to be the greatest FAW Champion of all time. He wanted to go down in the history books. His friends thought he was foolish, they mocked him making him an outcast. And as he was mocked, over kids in his position would think it was all over. But not JLC, he continued to go on, trying to re-live his boyhood dream. Trying to claim what he had wanted. When he was young he signed up for semi-professional feds. When he was scouted by EWF President Baz, JLC went on to become Grand Slam Champion at EWF, leaving nothing to be accomplished. When a friend at FAW [name withheld], gave him his card, to join FAW. He went for tryouts and was accepted. JLC was meant to be pushed as a face, but the fans did not warm to him. JLC was a dis-respected jobber. With nothing to his name except being the person who kicked crap out of kids in EWF, he was proving to bring down ratings when he went in the ring. He was put down by everyone. But did he quit? No! He kept at it, with visions of being FAW Champion still in his eyes. He slowly worked his way up to the top and when he was nearly there, He got fired. JLC had nothing, but months later JLC returned, shocking the world. But he started all over again. JLC fought his way to the top, JLC tried and tried to get to the top he became International Champion, and he dropped it at Taking Hardcore to the Extreme. Was this a blessing in disguise. JLC's humours antics suddenly got deadly serious and he was granted a FAW World title shot on 25th November, 2001... This was without a shout of a doubt JLC's hardest moutnain to climb. It took one hour, for the climb that would give him Legend Status, though there was rocks JLC climbed over everyone as he got too it! One-by-one untill he got to the top. The match was over, the biggest night of JLC's carrer was decided that JLC was the best in the industry beating Spawn in an Iron Man Match, 7 falls to 6. JLC won the World Title making him FAW's First Grandslam Champion. He went on to successfully defend the title, and as before he was scheduled to fight in the 9 man hell in a cell match at Evil Intentions he was took out by a mysterious person sidelining him for months. But JLC didn't keep his name out of the show. Oh No, President Lee Smith and Vice President Ben Preston hired JLC to help them fight off the xCw invasion Lead by British Bomber. JLC met up with his good friend Spider and is backing him through his trials to claim the gold that was held by the legends, HaHa Klown, Sean Mason, Deadman, Pyro, Spawn and of Course J...L…C! But at the night he helped Spider the mystery attacker struck again.Just a few months later, it happened. Tom revealed he was in cahoots with FAWs mystery attacked. Who then JLC appeared unmasking the attacker as JLC's long time friend Skipster! They fought for one month until Tom booked them in a hellhole match.After a bloody battle on the top of the cage Skipster knocked JLC off to the outside giving Skipster the win. This angered Jim, he wasn't seen for a week - then it happened. Jim had found new friends in the foam world order. They were intent on making Tom's life a living hell. So they did, Jim challenged Tom and his presidency at April Avalance: The Breaking Point. Jim succeeded becoming president of FAW. Quotes "Bad Things Happen to good people!" "Cometh the man; Cometh the hour." "C'mon chump! Jump! Jump!" "Bad things happen to good people"
