FAW Sunday Night Distortion Live from St. Louis, Missouri, Kiel Center LIVE Sunday 14th July 2002 WHAT THE HELL! (INTRO) Mario: Welcome to Distortion number 7! What a night we have in store for you, The final two Diabolical Day qualifying matches with the tournament proper starting this Wednesday on Panic. A limo pulls up. Mario: Who the hell is this? Shane: It must be Tom, I know he isnt here yet. Floyd: Well I guess we are about to find out.... The door of the limo opens up but no-one gets out, we move in a bit more then out of the limo gets The British Bomber! The fans go crazy as Bomber looks at the camera then limps off. Mario: OH MY GOD HE IS HERE! Floyd: But if you noticed he isnt at 100% which makes me believe Bomber isnt competing tonight. Mario: Maybe your right. Well, not that I'm overlooking him tonight - but X-Rodd better be ready for defeat... Now let me ask you something... What takes place one week from today? James Harris: Diabolical Day 2002... Chris Thunder: Yes, and one week from today, I become a World Heavyweight Champion for the second time... Because whoever tuned into Panic this past Wednesday, will know that I advanced in the Diabolical Day tournament - with ease... And tonight, I have a little warm-up match before taking part in this interpromotional match-up with that slimy XCWF "superstar", Crimson Lord on Panic... James Harris: Of course, if you become champion next Sunday, you will be a double champion, do you think you can handle defending two belts at the one time? Chris Thunder: James... You have to go back about 9 months, and you'll discover that I was once a triple champion here in FAW... During the invasion of xCw, I held the xCw Intercontinental Title, along the xCw and FAW Tag Team Titles. So its out of the question whether I can handle it... I've set so many high standards here in FAW, that the competition can only look on with envy... I go out there and entertain every night, for the fans, and they lap it up... With me as the World Heavyweight Champion, surely I will be the top man in the company for many months to come... I will prove that last time was no fluke, and I will lift that title belt next Sunday... Why? BECAUSE I CAN! Thunder just smiles and enters the Dark Generation locker room, where he joins Triple C, Nina and Spider around the monitor for Prodigy's upcoming match... Prodigy vs ??? Etan Cross will defend against EDH and Kryptonite... Shane Riot: Yes, they knew that Mario - they ain't that stupid... "Jay-Z" by Renegade hits as Kryptonite steps onto the entrance ramp and begins his walk to the ring... He steps up onto the apron, staring down EDH... Etan Cross’s voice booms in “It’s time to Hook’em to the Cross” as the sound of white noise and Radio Static come blasting across the arena. Mexican Radio starts to Play, as the titantron buzzes to life, flashing images of fighter, after fighter being dropped on their head’s, by Etan Cross’s various suplex’s. The startled fans jump to their feet, and turn to look down the rampway. At first sight of the 'Blood Angel' Blaze, and the Princess of Pain, Tragedy Ann, the arena explodes and the fans start going wild. Out walks the Anti-hero, the Machine Etan Cross towards the ring,. He grabs a microphone, and turns towards the fans, as chants of ETAN!! ETAN!!! rock the arena... EDH and Kryptonite slowly work away on Cross, who makes the mistake of sliding into the ring... Both men takes shots at Cross, who fights back, knocking Kryptonite to the mat, before being taken down by a stiff right from EDH... Cross then slides out of the ring again... Floyd Rotterdamn: Etan Cross, already retreating from the mat action... Remember, he does not need to be pinned to lose the title! Shane Riot: Which means he must get back into the ring! Get back in and defend the title! Mario Ciarilli: I think he has sometung up his sleeve that will give him the win... Keep your eyes peeled... EDH slaps on a boring headlock, Kryptonite reverses and overpowers EDH, who is whipped into the ropes... Cross pulls the top rope down, and EDH flies onto the outside... Kryptonite then baseball slides Cross, and follows the other two men out of the ring - as the referee begins the ten count... Floyd Rotterdamn: Love him or hate him, Etan Cross is talented, and has a wide range of moves, which he can slap on at any time... Shane Riot: He doesn't have to worry about moves just now, he's getting away from the brawl as much as he can! He's the damn champion! Cross slides back into the ring, posing to the fans for a moment - before EDH steps up onto the apron and begins to exchange rights and lefts with the European Champion... Kryptonite attempts to pull EDH down from the apron, but is kicked away. This allows time for Etan to grab both of EDH's arms from behind and bend his back, into a Dragon Sleeper position... However, EDH springs over Cross, catapulting back into the ring, rolling up Etan... 1.... 2... Kickout after two... Etan sweeps EDH to the floor, before lifting him and delivering a basic powerbomb... Kryptonite, by this time, is back in the ring, and DDT's Cross to the floor - then going for the pin - 1... 2... Kickout... Floyd Rotterdamn: That was a close one, definately! Mario Ciarilli: But some great ability by EDH to scoop Etan over for the roll up just seconds ago... Both Kryptonite and EDH almost capturing the European Title with those two pin attempts! Both Cross and Kryptonite battle in a tie-up, with Cross getting the upper hand, delivering a German Suplex from behind... Now in control of the contest, Cross positions both EDH and Kryptonite beside each other, before stepping up onto the top turnbuckle... He looks behind for a second, before executing a moonsault on Kryptonite, as EDH rolls out of the way in time... Cross looks to have got the worst of the move, as Kryptonite suddenly rolls on top of Cross! 1.... 2... 3! Kryptonite has won his first bit of FAW gold! Floyd Rotterdamn: Kryptonite - the NEW European Champion! Shane Riot: What?! Oh my God... That prick is the European Champion! Damn! This is the worst day of my life! Mario Ciarilli: I knew he could do it... Honest! "Jay-Z" by Renegade hits and Kryptonite rolls out the ring holding the title, he looks up to the ceiling in disbelief and clutches the title to him tight. He holds the title up high as EDH and Cross look on from the ring as he goes up the ramp. Hey Hey!
