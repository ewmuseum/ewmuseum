Roster card

Real Name: Robert Jaxom Birthdate: 10-2-74 Hometown: L.A, California Height: 6'7 Nicknames: Fro Status: Face Current Teams: none Entrance Music: 'Freedom Fighter'- Creed Wrestling Style: All-Rounder Signature Moves: Armageddon, Giveaway Other Federations: RMWF,UWF,UWA,UCW,ECWF Other Federation Awards: PTC- #5,#7,#8 Rankings in PTC Top Ten Handler Information Handler Name: [name withheld] AIM Name: [detail withheld] MSN Name: [detail withheld] Bio Robert Jaxom was born on October 2nd of 1974 unto his mother, Sherri and his father, Bill. The only child of a mid-low class family, Robert worked as hard as he could for everything he did. Robert grew rather fast for his age and by the time he was in 10th grade he was already 6'6 and also an exceptionally bright student. Robert went on to become Valedictorian of his class and got a full scholarship to the University of Michigan. While there he was a starting linebacker. He started all but one of his games that he played missing the one due to a twisted ankle. Robert double majored in Psychology and Writing. When Robert graduated in 1997 he was unsure of what to do with his life. He mainly wondered for a while before meeting a fellow friend, in early 1999, from Michigan University who had gotten into the wrestling business and deciding to give it a try. Robert adopted the name 'The Jaxomator' for the first fed he joined up with which was called Central America Wrestling Federation. After about 6 months here and learning the basics he decided he was ready for bigger things. While in between federations 'The Omega Fro' was born. Robert liked the ring to the name and already had planned how he would play out the name. His next federation, the Real Man's Wrestling Federation, became the first and only federation to see The Omega Fro as a gothic/prophet type person. This style lasted about 3 months before he dropped out of the public eye. While out Jaxom though about how he should change his personality and the true and current nature of The Omega Fro was born at last. When The Omega Fro returned to the ring of RMWF he was a changed person and his results showed as he went on a 30 match, 7 month, winning streak before deciding that he was getting to big for RMWF. When Fro left the DRWF he searched for a long time to find a federation. He joined many but did not like them and so quit. In November of 2000 he found the ECWF, a fed that looked promising and had a good atmosphere. When the fed opened, Fro was instantly one of the top 3 competitors in federation among other such as T-Bone and Pyro (yes FAW's Pyro). Unfortunately the federation closed down soon after opening and thus Fro could not make a well-known name for himself. Fro decided to let fate to the wind and went back home to Los Angeles to train. Within a few weeks a recruiter from a federation called the United Wrestling Association asked him o join and so he did. Fro was an instant success here and captured the #1 Contendership to the World Title within 3 weeks. As his championship match came near and so far not a piece of gold to his name from any fed, an unfortunate turn of event caused the federation to close. His friend that he had met in the ECWF, Pyro, talked fro into joining the fed that he owned called Underground Championship Wrestling. Fro did semi-well here but didn't really feel the click as he had in the UwA. He got his first shot of gold in the UCW in a three-way match for the Hardcore Title against two unknowns to him by the names of Shockwave and Skipster. Two days before the match he twisted his ankle but decided to continue with the match anyways which would Skipster would gone to win to the dismal of Fro. Soon after however the UCW closed, but the owner of the federation, Pyro told of the young talent about a federation he was in called the FAW, Furiously Addictive Wrestling. Fro signed a contract with the FAW on June 10th. After messing around for about a week he learned that his former employer/owner of the UwA, American Psycho was also in the federation this put high hopes into the head of Fro. Within two weeks of his arrival he was giving his first match, which happened to be at the ppv, and to his surprise, was for the #1 Contendership to the World Title. He unfortunately re-injured his ankle once again and once an again lost his match against old rival Skipster from the UCW. Fro then kind of slacked around until the beginning of July and then decided to make a name for himself. Fro went through the ranks of the FAW like a rocket and soon gained a shot at the Hardcore Title. Facing the current Champion, Paranoia, Fro felt confident going into the match and came out the victor and with his first piece of gold ever. At the next PPV however Fro felt he hunger for something more. After beating Minion for a successful defense of his title he staged an attack on himself that resulted in him losing the hardcore title as a result of the 24/7 rule. Fro went on had a few shots for the International Title but were screwed out of every last one them by the current Vice President Gee Monie who was also the International Champion. Though with the backing of his newly formed stable, the Furious Alliance, that contained such members as Skipster, Mortician, JC Ice and Richard Storm and also Sadam Hussein himself, the current co-president. After a turn of events Fro got a World Title shot and beat Pyro, Skipster, Sean Johnson and Mortician all in one match to gain the World Title for the first time. As fate would have it however family problems caused him to give up the title and the title as leader of the Furious Alliance to go back home. Fro was away for 1 1/2 months but when he came back he came back in a furry as in his first match back he defeated the then current World Champion Spawn, which sparked a hatred and rivalry of monstrous proportions that has lasted until this very day. Fro would then think that he was not quite in shape and then leave for 3 weeks before coming back. When he returned he beat British Bomber, Skipster and a few others before suffering a leg injury, which caused him two weeks to heal. After fro returned he returned with a vengeance. Once again defeating Spawn in spectacular form he went on a destructive road and continued on it for some time. Eventually he and his best friend Skipster formed a tag team and soon there after captured the FAW World Tag Titles on January 17th of 2002. They held the title until March 3rd where they let a new tem beat them and decided to go out onto their singles careers. Fro became on fire and when 'When World Collide' came around he was ready to claim his right as the #1 contender but was cut short by a new guy in the fed called Tony Vigilante. Despaired Fro slacked off for a month before getting his act together. The next pay-per-view was the one where Skipster and he lost their tag titles and Fro became more determined when he saw that the man who was to go to EBII as the #1 contender lost his spot to British Bomber. Then Fro was thrilled when he got to face Bomber for the #1 Contendership on Panic and capitalized. Fro was heading to Extreme Beating to fight his rival Spawn for the FAW World Title. Extreme Beating II came and went and left Fro with the FAW World Title. Then came a fateful day of Break Point where he was left title less. Not knowing what to do Robert disappeared for a month before coming back. When he came back it was announced that he was an inductee to the FAW Hall of Fame. Now it is time for Robert to prove that he deserves to be there, but only time will tell if this will come true. Quotes "That's the way it is, that's the way it was, and that IS the way it always will be!!!"
