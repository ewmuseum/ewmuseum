Panic, 24 May 2001

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The lights flash wildly as the crowd erupt into cheers and the World Title appears on the fury-tron . The Name Deadman comes up in a stamp and covers the title . The crowd go silent as this continues . Letters come up on the screen one by one . L E T T H E P A N I C K I N G B E G I N ! The crowd go ballistic as one of the most entertaining Thursday Panics in FAW History begins with this brilliant card storming up to the top of the wrestling world and the TV Ratings . We go to our commentary team , as always , President Bill Clinton and President K , Also known as Former 3-time World Champion of FAW , HaHaKlown . Bill Clinton : Hello and Welcome to Thursday Panic ! HaHaKlown : As always , I think we should let the Panicking Begin Bill . Bill Clinton : Yes FAW is approaching its 7 month anniversary and I can tell you straight out there will be fireworks on June 18th [excuse me if I am not correct .] HaHaKlown : Yes there will be a lot to see and Im told there is a possibility of a Mini-PPV taking place on that day . Bill Clinton : Yes , it could happen but we are not currently sure . The first match for tonights event is for the Tag Team T itles which are currently held by X-Factor who will of course be stricken from faw on June 11th . What a sad day that will be . Its X-Factor vs New Error [Death & Insane Pain] vs British Bomber and Spike . HaHaKlown : Entertainment as its best . But first , lets have a look at this brief tape regarding CEO Richard and his current FAW Status . Tape Starts Sunday : 3pm : CEO Richard causes great confusion and "fu###" up email FAW Info goes to . This was not spotted until Monday and was the main reason why FA W IS WAR Results were late . Richard goes on strike but soon stops . Tuesday : 7pm : CEO Richard is FIRED from FAW By President Hussain . He demanded to be asked back but Clinton , whom he was talking to , refused . Richard announces he will be back with revenge up his sleeve . 8pm - Tuesday CEO Crusher appointed . Tape ends HaHaKlown : What could this mean for New Error ? Clinton : Who knows but one things for certain Death and Insane Pain will not be at full strength tonight . X-Pacs music blasts into the arena . He enters via the ramp along with his fellow partner Justin Credible . New Error music plays into the arena . CEO Richard has been mysteriously wiped out from the video and replaced with Insane Pain , of who we can only assume is the new leader . The Krusherz music blasts into the arena . British Bomber runs down to the ring and climbs the apron . He raises his hand and the pyros explode as Spike walks down to the ring with the crowd cheering . The odd Max X fans are booing oddly , A Couple of weeks ago it would have been Max and Spike in this matchup if it wasn't for Amy . HHK : DING DING DING . This match gets too a start . Its tornado tag . Death grapples up with Justin Credible . Justin nails a backbreaker . Clinton : X-Pac and Spike are fighting outside the ring already . Its No-DQ In this match . Spike grabs a ladder from underneath the ring . He climbs up it as X-Pac watches on , bewildered . And then he realises Spike is planning to jump on him . X-Pac climbs up the ladder as well as Justin gets triple teamed by Bomber Insane Pain and Death . Death whips Justin into the ropes . While this is happening X-Pac and Spike fight up the ladder . HHK : Finally they both reach the top and continue pummeling away at each other . X-Pac gains an advantage as Spike is knocked , just hanging on to the ladders side . Clinton : British Bomber takes out Insane Pain with a kneedrop . Death then hits a clothesline as Bomber falls to the ground . Death and Justin fight it out . Death hits a neckbreaker . Spike finally hits a brainbuster over the ladder. Spike has fallen down to the ground where the sun doesn't shine aswell ! HHK : This match is a thriller ! Clinton : Ronny Biggs is soon to get out of jail . HHK : What the fxck has this got to do with the match ? Is he Prison Guard or something ? Clinton : Don't ask me . HHK : Spike is moving ! Death tries for the DDT on British Bomber but amazingly fails as Bomber drops back to the ground and hits a big kick . That kick was huge ! Clinton : Even I have got to agree with you there . And believe me its not often us two agree on something . HHK : Your damn right . British Bomber , Spike and X-Pac are the only people up at the moment . All the rest are completely and utterly unconcious . Spike signals and Bomber runs for a chair . He put the chair next to Spike . Clinton : What are they planning to do ? HHK : Only time will tell . Clinton : Man Oh Man you are lame . HHK : Fxck you commoner . Clinton : At least I am not a freakin' Clown ! HHK : You might as well be one I mean just look at you screwed up tosser . Clinton : Spike nails a Spike Driver on X-Pac onto the chair ! HHK : Bomber grabs the titles from the ring apron and raises them high as we hear the sound of the referee counting the pinfall . Clinton : The Winners and New Furiously Addictive Wrestling Tag Team Champions , British Bomber and Spike , also known as .THE KRUSHERZ . HHK : What a brilliant match . Clinton : Next match up is Mortician and Gordone Junior taking on Mystic X In a handicap match . Mystic X has been preparing well for this match backstage . HHK : Yes I saw him in the gym earlier on . Clinton : You in the gym ? I thought you were the Ice Cream type of guy . HHK : Yes I was just walking past on my way to the Ice Cream van actually ! Clinton : Ha ! [Tier - Rammenstein plays through the speakers and into the arena . Mystic X stands at the ramp . He looks up as dozens of pyros explode but much to his disgrace the crowd boo .He looks disgusted as he walks down to the ring . ] [New Errors music blasts through the FAW Sound Speakers . This one however includes CEO Richard . Mortician and Gordone Jnr walk down to the ring in full stride . ] Clinton : This match is going to be amazing . Mystic being double-teamed !? I never thought Id see it until now . Believe it or not I reckon he will triumph in this match . HHK : I always thought he'd get handicapped someday , hes just a rotten bastard , everyone hates this guy except his beloved girlfriend . DING DING DING . Clinton : Mystic X stands in the centre of the ring . He is unaware of what to do next . Or else he simply doesn't care ? Who knows but I think Mortician is going to make the first move . HHK : Yes he just moved his leg ! Clinton : Do you know that you are incredibly lame ? HHK : And what are you , insane ? Clinton : No that's Insane Pain . Lame Damn Clown ! LDC ..Rats I didn't get it . Jeering Lame Castle ! Got it HHK : Look whos Lame now . Mortician throws Mystic X ontop of his back . Mystic punches Mortician over and over again . It seems Gordone Jnr is to become in-active in this match . Clinton : Yes certainly . Oh Look Gordone hits a inverted DDT On Mystic . Mortician tries for the cover..ONE TWO THRE.HE HAS HIS HAND ON THE ROPES . HHK : That was lucky . Clinton : Very very lucky . HHK : Mystic X gets up and lifts up Mortician . He cannot decide what to do . He lifts him up and hits a Push N Pull Piledriver . Oh What a move ! Clinton : Mystic is truly a brilliant wrestler . HHK : I Totally agr.disagree . Clinton : Incorrect . Mortician is lying on the ground . Mystic and Jnr stare each other down . Gordone tries for a powerbomb but fails as Mystic counters it into a hurricarana . I didn't know he was capable of that ! HHK : We could and should have a real hardcore match here next up . Clinton : Yes this could give a new meaning to The Word Hardcore as FAW Seeks out its next Hardcore Champion. HHK : Im hoping that son of a bitch Damo wont win this one . But then again Im not exactly hoping anyone WILL ! Clinton : Referee Louis Savva rings the bell . Damo gets a garbage can from his dumpster . He throws weapons into the ring as Bayne hits Minion with a steel chair . That must have been a hard shot ! HHK : Very Very Hard . Clinton : Damo climbs up the apron with an iron pipe in his hand . He smacks Bayne around the neck with it . Bayne does not look too hurt suprisingly . Minion grabs a straight jacket from underneath the ring ! We could see one of these men being strapped in tonight . HHK : That would be something to see . Imagine Pizzler tied up . Ha . Clinton : Bayne grabs the straight-jacket off Minion and begins strapping Minion in . On His Debut we could see Bayne make a big 'thing' on the FAW Fans , we could have the chance to cause Great embaressment to our other debutant for tonight , who is of course Minion . HHK : Bayne is roaring the arena into cheers , I don't think I have ever heard such a big effort from the fans to make some noise . Clinton : Bayne locks on the first strap ..the second.the third..the fourth and finally the fifth . Minion is tied up . Damo just watches on in surprise at the events taking place . Bayne asks Muzzler a question . The International Champion comes out of a trance and jumps out of the ring . HHK : He grabs a table and slides it into the ring . I think I know whats coming next.. . Bayne sets up the table . He grins and chuckles as he lifts up Minion and hits Death from Above right straight directly through that solid wood table . Minion bounces off the table and hits the hard ground again . Blood pours down from his temple . Clinton : Bayne.that vicious.horrible...horrible person . The Devil has taken control and its taken its toll . These ugly scenes could cast a shadow forever on the rest of Minions career in Furiously Addictive Wrestling . HHK : Its not a question of could , it WILL . Damo and Bayne slap each others hands at laugh . Damo lets Bayne step infront of him and his laughing stops . Bayne notices and turns around . MUZZLER BOMB . Damo just Muzzler Bombed Bayne . Bayne is knocked unconcious by this uncontrollable force . Clinton : Damo goes for the pin Cover , ONE TWO THREKICK OUT . Amazing Stuff there . Absolutely amazing . Bayne must be superhuman If he can get out of the strongest move in Sports Entertainment today . Both men have hit their finishers on each other . Oh No Look behind Damo ! Minion is up . HHK : He swings from side to side with his hand holding his head . He takes away his hand and looks at the large lumps of blood covering it . He grabs a chair . He is obviously focused on hitting Bayne . But wait a moment he hit Damo ????! Clinton : I really do not have a clue why he did that . Not a clue . Bayne got him in this condition . I think the fans are starting to like this guy ! HHK : My God you can sometimes be so funny . HHK : Wait a minute.The crowd are chanting Minions name . Minion sees Bayne getting the Cover . He tries to stop Bayne but just faints . 1..2......3 . A Slow count from Louis Savva . And we finally have a New Hardcore Champion crowned . And if I am not mistaken the last one was infact Damo Muzzler . And now it is BAYNE!!!! HHK: And now here we go, the main event, the three men are out to the ring *DING, DING, DING* Clinton: And here we go, Deaman and PyRo fight it out as Max-X goes to the outside and kisses Amy, his new girlfriend.... HHK: And then PyRo lands heavy onto the outside and Maz begins to kick him! Clinton: But wait a minute, Deadman with a chair shot to PyRo and Max! HHK: He puts them into the ring and has the cover..1....2.....!!!!KICK OUT! PyRo gets up and BAHM! A chair shot! 1...2.....3!!!!!! PYRO WON THE MATCH!
