These Results are in brief , they were very rushes as the match writer wasnt anywhere to be found , and neither was the other Prez on Tuesday . Sean Mason def. Olympian via pinfall ... Bill Clinton - The bell rings as we start the match . Olympian nails a suplex . Sean hits the neckbreaker ..... 5 mins later ... Sadam Hussain - Wait a moment , its Lex Luger ! Hes coming down to the ring with a chair , Olympian has the cover, but Lugers broken it up while the ref is distracted , PIN 1 2 3 , Sean Mason Wins . Backstage we see Jon the bomb pull up and the mystery woman gets out of the car .... Majin Buu defeats British Bulldog by DQ Bill Clinton - Majin Buu nails a front leg drop .... 2 mins later Sadam Hussain - What the hell.....Its HaHaKlown coming down to the ring , hes got the world title belt , Hes gonna hit Majin with it , go Klown , go Klown (he chants) , NO Majins ducked , he turns , Piledriver on Klown by Buu ! But wait , here comes Sean Mason coming down to the ring , he hit the Inferior Compliment ! Majin has won by DQ , ive gotta sort this one out . (Hussain gets in the ring with a microphone) Hussain - Right , im fed up of the brawling , sort it out this Sunday , Sean Mason vs HaHaKlown vs Majin Buu (Laughing Scaffolding match for World Title ) , its a change of plan , ALRIGHT !!!!! (Sadam walks out of the ring and goes back to the announcers table . ) The Allied Forces defeat surge via pinfall Surge Sadam - Here we go ! Regal hits the english bulldog followed up to a Regal stretch , Raven gets out of it . 15 Mins Later ... Bill - Show just got tagged in ,THE FINAL CUT ! Pin 1 2 3 ! Next up we have Jon the bomb vs Austin vs ??who is it ..i forgot to tell someone to play it , holy cow . After Jon and Austin are out...... Bill - The ref rang the bell ! But here comes Taker down on his scooter , he hit Jon with a chair , the bellhas gone to Takers head, carnage in the ring . Look ! Lector wants to join in , hes came down to attack Austin , hes biting him , the ref is braking it up . (Bill stands up and speaks ) Bill - Another match for Extreme Beating , Undertaker vs Jon the bomb is a hell in a cell ! And while im at it , Hannibal vs Austin in a singles match ! Lex Luger is coming down.....hes beating the hell out of Austin , HEY , LUGER , STOP THAT ! Oh...Hannibal vs Austin vs Lex Luger vs ....British Bulldog sounds better , in a battle royal for the UNIVERSAL TITLE . HaHaKlown & hhh vs Dogg & Brahma Bull 5 mins Later HaHaKlown gets the pin on Dogg , after taking out Sean Mason who attacked him . The Undertaker vs SiCoKlown 2 mins Later , Jon the bomb comes down to the ring and watches Taker pin SiCoKlown , Jon walks off and the mystery woman sneaks behind Taker and hits him with the pipe as Jon grins and gives the thumbs up to the woman . Extreme Vs DDP Extreme wins after hitting DDP with the Smackdown ! Know your role Cd Case . Matches Announced for Extreme Beating ..Sean Mason vs Majin Buu vs HaHaKlown in a LSD Match for HaHaKlowns World Title Jon the bomb vs Undertaker (Hell in a cell for International Title) Hannibal Lector vs Steve Austin vs Lex Luger vs British Bulldog William Regal vs HHH (Euro title match, stip yet to be announced) Yet to be confirmed... Brahma Bull vs Extreme vs DDP (Hardcore title) P.S. WE NEED A MATCH WRITER !!!! THESE WERE INCREDIBLY RUSHED
