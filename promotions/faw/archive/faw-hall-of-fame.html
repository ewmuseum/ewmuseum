FAW Hall of Fame

Furiously Addictive Wrestling Hall Of Fame {Contrary to beliefs, wrestlers who are currently on the roster can still be hall of fame members} HaHaKlown HaHaKlown is a class act. He was a god send from day one in FAW, and brought with him several enticing gimmick matches and angles. He had several feuds, including one which ended up with a young JLC having to leave FAW because of the Klown. HaHa will forever be remembered as one of the most unique wrestlers ever to step in a Furiously Addictive Wrestling ring. His most memorable feud has to be with Mojo, a feud which turned into Dark Carnival vs. Mojo & Co. This feud ended up with HaHaKlown winning once again. He held the title for approximately 3 months, before handing it over to sidekick Deadman. HaHa became President of FAW, and stepped out of the spotlight. When he stepped down he went missing, with no source of where he was many assumed he had took his own life until early 2002 when he returned and took out the man he took out once before, sending JLC out of FAW once again, re-forming Dark Carnival with JLC could be the biggest idea he ever had… or the worst, stable members get jealous, but whatever happens HaHa will come out on top, he always does. Pyro Months after HaHaKlown’s departure, Pyro joined FAW. First impressions with all the road agents and fans were that this boy had big talent. These expectations were fulfilled to the maximum. Pyro was moved straight up with the big guns, in the world title division. He won the World title at Breakaway 2001, defeating Axle Mystic X and Spike. That reign lasted for only 1 month, although it seemed a lot longer as there were 2 PPV’s until he lost it to American Psycho. He also had a 15 day reign which was short-lived, totalling his time as world champion to around 2 months. It is arguable to say Pyro is the reason FAW is here today, he rocketed the role-play quality in here. Like all legends there are rumours of a return one day, but whether his return will remain a myth or the legend will be back only one man knows and that’s Pyro himself. American Psycho American Psycho was the one who took the gold off Pyro and held it for around a month. He was plagued with problems in his time but only ever lost one match out of 10 against up and comer Spider who would also join the Hall of fame. He also shot the role-play quality to a high and gob smacked everyone by producing the biggest role-play in FAW History on the eve of Disaster Down Under [when he defeated Pyro] with a cracking 55kb role-play. He returned briefly in a little less successful stint but has since disappeared once again leaving FAW’s fans looking to an unlikely return. British Bomber British Bomber joined FAW in April 2001, and had long feuds with JLC. He holds the record for the most title wins, with 9 FAW Titles and 2 unclassified titles from invading federations [xCw & XWW]. He is also the most hated man in FAW, after invading with xCw and XWW not in respective order. His most famous feuds are with JLC, Chris Thunder and Tom ‘The Bomb’, no matter what happens, Bomber will forever be remembered as the guy you love to hate or the guy you hate to love. After winning the FAW World title and XWW World title at Diabolical day 2002 Bomber can perhaps be named as the best in the business today, and while he’s in FAW, the corporate members can watch the money roll in. Jim ‘JLC’ Phoenix JLC joined FAW in February. He was probably the worst on the roster at that time. Let’s just say ten lines were above average. He was a hard working wrestler and won all the lower titles in what seemed like no time. He slowly won them over and over again. But then the breakthrough came. With the help of several top e-wrestlers JLC improved dramatically and moved up the ranks after 8 months in FAW. He won the International title and then battled warrior Spawn and won in a tremendous bout setting the standard for perhaps all future role-plays. JLC is the first out of FAW’s two grand slam champions, the other being Skipster. He returned at Diabolical day 2002 alongside old rival HaHaKlown forming Dark Carnival Evolution looking to cause havoc among FAW, the heel from hell is back and ready for carnage. Spawn Friend of Pyro, who formed The Omens of Death, Spawn is an ex-euro and ex-tag champion. He is also an ex-World champion, after losing the title to JLC after a fairly long reign. Spawn is a legend, and will not be forgotten in a hurry. He and JLC made up a perfect main event at Deceit, producing an absolutely shocking amount of role-plays and giving it all they could. Unfortunately, Spawn was outperformed by JLC. However, about three months later Spawn got a title shot against Spider, after a lengthy bout Spawn won it, taking the title into the pay per view where he faced Crimson Lord and won, since then Spawn has laid low a little, but you can be sure he will soon be back in the spot-light chasing for the top gold in sports entertainment, the FAW Heavyweight title. Spider/Vincent The man who became known as Vincent one year after he joined FAW in the first place as Spider. One year tenure filled with injuries, titles, torture and pleasure, Spider’s ride is perhaps one of the bumpiest in all the FAW Hall of fame. It all started in July 2001 when he formed Dark generation with Jim ‘JLC’ Phoenix and Gee Monie, he and JLC became best of friends very quickly and stormed the newly re-instated tag team division by storm, being stripped of the titles after he and JLC suffered injuries after holding the titles all summer. He then returned and so began the biggest boom in his career, he became the first man to defeat the FAW World champ of the time American Psycho in a non-title match, he injured Psycho for a long time, something which he would enjoy doing many-a-time in his career. After disappearing he came back and won the International title, the Hardcore title and a month later received the biggest screw job in all FAW when Tom ‘The Bomb’ and The British Bomber teamed up and screwed him. For the pay per view he had produced the biggest role-play in FAW history at the time with a 104KB role-play. A month later he won the World title. Defending it all nights he even defended it against all-comers in a 24 hour period and also winning a 4 on 1 gauntlet all in the same night, after losing the title to Spawn Spider left FAW. He returned soon after and got vengeance for JLC by beating HaHaKlown and re-forming Dark generation, at Diabolical day 2002 he and the British Bomber screwed Dark Gen though and Spider became Vincent and looks to the future, and the rest, as they say, is history. Skipster Skipster joined FAW in June of 2001. He wasn't the best on the roster but he also wasn't the worse. Skipster worked hard throughout the months, gaining the Hardcore title. Soon Skipster got a world title shot and won the title, however he soon lost it to Pyro in a street fight. Skipster soon tagged up with the Omega Fro and as a tag team, the two won the Tag Titles. Then the two men split up, but they soon formed the ultimate stable which consisted of Richard Storm, The Mortician, JC Ice, and of course, Skipster and The Omega Fro. Together, they held almost every single title. But then Storm and Mortician left, Skipster and The Omega Fro were on their own once again. Skipster has held every title and is FAW's Second Grand Slam winner next to JLC. A tremendous athlete who is perhaps the most deserved of a Hall of fame place Chris Thunder One month after signing, on the June 1st edition of Panic, Thunder debuted in a ladder match against Damo Muzzler with the International Title on the line. Unfortunately, Thunder didn't come out victorious, but in the next few months, "The Man with All the Talent" would hold high hopes for the future of FAW. Wrestlers have come and gone since the debut of Thunder, but Chris has refused to turn his back on Furiously Addictive Wrestling, and since May 2001, has became the first FAW Light-Heavyweight Champion, before picking up two Tag Team Title reigns with Gee Monie in early September, and arch-nemesis, The British Bomber in late November, along with becoming the International Champion and heading into a big money match-up with Spider at the November pay-per-view Deceit... Thunder competed in the eight-man hell in a cell match for the World Heavyweight Title, and was screwed over by Tom the Bomb, who was revealed as the new owner of the invading xCw. However, Chris would defeat new champion, Bomber on the 19th January edition of Panic, and become the new World Heavyweight Champion, before dropping the title nine days later at the January pay-per-view to Spider. Since then, Thunder has became an FAW Tag Team Champion for a third time with Prodigy, and the two have became firm favourites with the fans, what the future holds in singles and tag team for Thunder remains to be seen, but you gotta’ know it won’t be easy…
