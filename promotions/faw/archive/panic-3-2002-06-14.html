FAW Wednesday Night Panic (Delayed Viewing) Live from Baltimore, Maryland; Baltimore Arena Aired Friday 14th June 2002 - Taped Wednesday 12th June 2002 Shifty's Gone (INTRO) As reported a few nights ago by co-president Tom The Bomb who now works behind the scenes in Furiously Addictive Wrestling, he released this press release to PrimeTime Central's EEL Network. "Furiously Addictive Wrestling's International Champion, Shifty, was arrested by Balitmore Police late this afternoon. The reason is unknown to this reporter, but it is said that Shifty used his one phone call, to call FAW's main man, Tom The Bomb. The founder and Co-President of FAW. He said he would come down and see what he could get done. It is said that he was going down to post bail for Shifty but instead once he got there he asked to enter Shifty's cell to speak with him. The police where hesitant, but let Tom in with Shifty. Apparently Tom beat Shifty and left him lying in his own blood. Once out of the cell Tom turned back to say something to Shifty. Shifty, with all his might looked up at Tom, just as Tom told him he was fired. Apparently fans were not happy, as many have been seen grouping together at tonight’s Panic. Panic starts here in a few hours, and this reporter just hopes that it does not get canceled like Distortion did. Panic starts in just over an hour, and they have let very fans in so far. All the wrestlers have arrived and were briefed about Shifty and his firing. Xander, who holds Shifty’s IN title, has not made a statement. I have not been able to get in contact with either Co-President. But it does look like Shifty is gone from FAW forever. And fans sure are not happy with Co-President Tom The Bomb."
