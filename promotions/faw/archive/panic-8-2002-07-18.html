FAW Wednesday Night Panic (Delayed Viewing) Live from Cedar Rapids, Iowa, Five Seasons Center Aired Thursday 18th July 2002 WTF!? (INTRO) This week's Panic opens with the usual montage, followed by the massive pyro explosion at the top of the ramp... Blue and white lights flash around the arena, as Floyd Rotterdamn, Mario Ciarilli and Shane Riot welcome us inside the ring... Floyd Rotterdamn: Hello everyone, and welcome to FAW Panic! I'm Floyd Rotterdamn, alongside Mario Ciarilli and Shane Riot! Tonight, we have Spawn taking on Crimson Lord in an interpromotional match-up, and more Diabolical Day action, as the quarter final matches take place! The crowd cheer for Rotterdamn, but the cheers quickly turn to jeers, as Shane Riot takes the microphone, leaning against the ring ropes... Shane Riot: You know, tonight will be an interesting night, Floyd Rotterdamn... We have our quarter final matches, but tonight is also one of the worst nights on the FAW tour... You see, Mario Ciarilli, the typical New Yorker he is, actually drove in from New York, to avoid being mobbed by fat, sweaty and dirty Iowans... I mean, tonight, he's actually got more of that rather cheap aftershave he has on to avoid becoming "one of the gang". Isn't that right Mario? Mario grabs the mic from Riot to a huge pop... He poses to the crowd before holding the mic to his mouth... Mario Ciarilli: Shane Riot... Don't lie... Little white lies get you nowhere with the fans right here in Cedar Rapids, Iowa... We all know that you prefer perfume and that you infact drove in from Toronto to avoid getting any heat from the Iowan fans... Isn't that right? Floyd looks on, as Mario hands the mic back to Riot... However, the lights go out in the arena, and come back on again as a blue explosion erupts at the top of the ramp... The crowd get to their feet as "Back In Black" by AC/DC hits the arena speakers... Chris Thunder steps out onto the entrance ramp, clad in the official Thunder and Prodigy t-shirt, jeans, and black boots, with his FAW Tag Team Title perched on his right shoulder... Riot looks on in disgust, as Thunder spits out some water, throwing the bottle into the crowd and stepping into the ring, taking a mic from the timekeeper... Chris Thunder: You know, I couldn't resist coming out here and addressing the commentary team... This is the first time in a while that I've managed to catch up with you three... And, Shane... I've sat in the back, time after time, and I've heard you blabble on about this, that and the next thing... But the people watching at home do not deserve to listen to stupid dumbfuck commentary from you... So, why don't you get your ass outta this ring while I use my air time? Shane Riot: Your air time? Chris Thunder: Yeah, my air time... Get the hell outta this ring before I remove you myself! Riot flees the ring, much to the delight of the fans, Floyd and Mario... Chris Thunder: I'm gonna make this quick... Bomber... You came down to the ring, and gave me a stunner this past Sunday night on Distortion... But tonight, you're gonna need to watch your back... You never know who will come down after you... You just never... know! Just then, Triple C runs down to the ring... Triple C: Chris... Please... Don't start arguing again, please! Chris Thunder: Courtney, stay out of this, he's made a big mistake, and its gonna cost him! Thunder begins to leave the ring and starts walking to the back... Triple C: Chris... Don't do this... Chris... CHRIS! Thunder turns around for a second, before walking off stage... Courtney is visibly emotional, and runs to the back, as Floyd and Mario take their places at ringside for the opening match... Xander vs Syd Diabolical Day Quarter Final ~The first match was about to get under way as it would pin the man that took out Apocalypse about a week or so ago in Syd going up again the Ratings man in Xander. The first to come down was Syd to a wasteful crowd as the fans screamed away as he came out in his usual clothing. He got in the ring and did a few poses as Xander's music hit. he came out to the fans in a mixed reaction. So screaming and some yelling for him. He got in the ring and went right at Syd. Syd ducked under the closeline as Xander came off the ropes and got hit with a hip toss. Xander got up and again got hit with a hipp toss. He then rolled out of the ring as Syd took chase. Syd grabbed a hold of the top rope and launched himself over the top rope into a missile dropkick that knocked Xander to the floor. Syd got right up and toss Xander in the ring where he went for the top rope. Xander got to his feet and as Syd came of he caught him and hit a counter into a powerbomb. Xander crawled to the feet of Syd and rolls himover to his stomach as he twisted his foot around to the back of Xander. He then puts his right arm around the head of Syd like a Inverted DDT position. Syd cry's out in pain as the ref looked on. After about three minutes, Xander tell everyone that it is over. He grabs up Syd and tosses him into the ropes. Syd goes flying across as Xander does the unthinkable. Syd comes back and as he does he gets grabed around the throat by Xander and lifted in the air and down with an Inverted Chokeslam in a powerslam. The ref goes down to count as Xander makes the pin. One, Two, Three. Xander edvances in the DD tourney.~ Who Was It? Whats The Secret? "In Too Deep" by Sum 41 hits and Tom The Bomb comes out with Kathryn to chants of "asshole" from the fans, Tom struts down to the ring with Kathryn and climbs onto the apron holding the rope for Kathryn to get through. They get in the ring and Tom comes onto the mic. Tom The Bomb: After what happened on Sunday at Distortion with someone costing me the match I have decided that I know how he is, I know who did it. The crowd start chants of "Bomber, Bomber" Floyd Rotterdam: Do you think he means Bomber? Shane Riot: Bomber is a little punk and deserves to be fired! Tom The Bomb: I know your watching, and right now I would like to say to the man who cost me the match....YOUR FIRED! "Highway To Hell" by ACDC hits and Tom stands in the middle of the ring waiting for Bomber to appear but Bomber doesnt, the music stops and Tom smiles. Tom The Bomb: Looks like the great almighty British Bomber is more of a pussy than I thought! British Bomber: Hey Tom! Tom looks up the ramp but doesnt see Bomber, everyone in the arena is confused. British Bomber: You know Tom they wasnt very nice words to say about me, especially when you dont know where I am but hell I am going to show you where I am. He sounds the car horn and we go to the furytron where the British Bomber isnt sat in a car at all. We are looking up at a monster truck which Bomber is in. British Bomber: Where am I Tom? I only know that but you know what, I am nearly 100% and I promise I will be ready for the PPV but for all these weeks you have kept my mouth shut about this secret......until now. Kathryn you have kept me quiet and did a ok job of it in the process but you have not finished my career have you? I am still here, so I guess now would be a good time to reveal the secret! Tom The Bomb: CUT THE FEED NOW! Floyd Rotterdam: At last! The secret! British Bomber: The secret? Well we all know that Kathryn is married to Jeff Harris and pregnant, about 7 months now aint it Kathryn? Jeff was over the moon, he was happy.... but its not his baby is it Kathryn? I know who's baby it is! Tom The Bomb: CUT THE GOD DAMN FEED! British Bomber: Why have Kathryn and Tom been so ruthless to try and end my career? Take a look in the ring right now and there you see the father of Kathryn baby! TOM THE BOMB! Thats right, Tom is the father! Shane Riot: WHAT! Floyd Rotterdam: WHAT THE HELL! British Bomber: Thats why they have been so intent on keeping my mouth shut, thats why all the attacks, and Tom if I am fired for costing you your match then so be it but I'm going to leave you a present. Bomber starts up the monster truck as the camera moves away, it is revealed he is in the car park. He revs the truck and drives it straight over Tom's car! Bomber backs it up and his eyes lock on Kathryn's 1957 Shelby Mustang. He drives straight on and over her prized car! He stops and climbs out smiling with the fans going nuts. British Bomber: Tom, Kathryn. You screw with me then...... KVT: YOUR GONNA PAY FOR THAT! Bomber just smiles. British Bomber: You know how it feels having something you loved taken away from you in a instant, just like your trying to do with me Kathryn. This Sunday I will go on to win the title, this Sunday I will win you! KVT: Oh no your not. Im out. British Bomber: What? KVT: You think you can ruin my life by spreading some BS, That Tom is the father of this baby? You destroy my precious car. THE ONE MY FATHER GAVE ME FOR MY SWEET SIXTEENTH BEFORE HE DIED? There is no way in hell, you are gonna win me. British Bomber: You know, tonight when I beat Prodigy and advance to the semi finals in my first ever Diabolical Day tournament, I will prove to the world that I am the best, that I will win you can destroy Tom! KVT: That is not gonna happen & if it does my god. Bomber you ever read the bible? British Bomber: Yeah, I believe it is the book named Bomber 7:11. KVT: No, ya see There's a passage I got memorized, seems appropriate for this situation: Ezekiel 25:17. "The path of the righteous man is beset on all sides by the inequities of the selfish and the tyranny of evil men. Blessed is he who, in the name of charity and good will, shepherds the weak through the valley of darkness, for he is truly his brother's keeper and the finder of lost children. And I will strike down upon thee with great vengeance and furious anger those who attempt to poison and destroy my brothers. And you will know my name is the Lord when I lay my vengeance upon thee." I never really questioned what that passage meant. I thought it was just a cold-blooded thing to say to a motherfucker 'fore you popped a cap in his ass. But I saw some shit this mornin' made me think twice. Now I'm thinkin', it could mean you're the evil man. And I'm the righteous man. And Mr.Sledgehammer sat in my locker room, he's the shepherd protecting my righteous ass in the valley of darkness. Or is could by you're the righteous man and I'm the shepherd and it's the world that's evil and selfish. I'd like that. But that shit ain't the truth. The truth is you're the weak. And I'm the tyranny of evil men. But I'm tryin'. I'm tryin' real hard to be a shepherd and bust that sledgehammer into your knee repeatedly, but you see if you win on Sunday, like the passage said I will strike down upon thee with great vengeance & furious anger.. British Bomber: Well thats good for you, but I have my own little passage, and that is the passage where it says "Thou has commited a crime, and thats fucked Tom The Bomb. Thou shall pay for the crime Tom The Bomb by meeting one's sledgehammer in one's face!" KVT: You know I could stand here all fkn day & play your lil mind games but screw that, I gotta things to do then have a slagging match. British Bomber: Then in that case..... I will see you on Sunday...... SLUT! KVT: Oh cause thats really mature. Bomber climbs back into the moster truck and runs over her car one more time. British Bomber: SEE YOU IN HELL KATHRYN! Etan Cross vs Jeff Harris Diabolical Day Quarter Final ~The next match up would bring up the red hot under rated Etan Cross again the some what legend in Jeff Harris. Jeff Harris came out to a great massive booing corwd as al sorts of stuff was tossed at him. From food to toys and to under clothing. Harris got in the ring and gave the fans waht he felt about them as he awaited the arrival of Cross. Cross came out in a loud booing of his own. Not as bad as Harris but Cross came running straight for the ring. He slide in but right as he did Harris slide out of the ring walking over and grabbing a steel chair and started tossing it into the ring. Harris then toss back the apron and grabbed out a table. As he brought it out, Cross came out of the ring and took off to the right. As Harris looked around the ring, Cross came runing towards Harris and started hitting rights to the jaw of Harris. Cross grabbed the right arm of Harris but as he did Harris reversed the wipe and Cross hit the cross bar. Cross came back a little and Harris picked him up in a belly to back suplex. The ref got the count to five before Harris tossed Cross back into the ring. Harris to to his feet and hit a big leg drop to Cross. He went for a cover but only got a two count. Cross got to his feet and Harris went for a foot to the gut but Cross blocked it and swung Harris around. As Harris came around Cross gave his own kick to the gut but Harris hit a leg lock take down. Harris went for the submission but Cross grabbed the ropes. Harris got angry as he then lifted Cross and sent Cross into the ropes. As he came back Haric kicked him in the guit and then lifted him up in to a powerbomb position. As Cross was coming down, he reversed the move and grabbed the right arm of harris as he locked on Harris's own finished. The ref came down to look as Harris started to tap out right in the middle of the ring. After the match Etan left and The British Bomber ran down and started attacking Jeff Harris. He hit Jeff's shoulder with a sledgehammer then followed up with chair shots leaving Jeff Harris clutching his shoulder.~ Chris Thunder vs 9th Wonder Of Foam Diabolical Day Quarter Final ~The next match was up as the all talent man of FAW was to come to the ring. As his music hit, Chris Thunder came down to the ring was a mic and told the whole world that tonight he would defeat Foamy and go on to face the next challenge in the tourney to become the next World Champion. Before he could get to the ring his music started again and out of the corwd came Foamy as he ran right to the back of Thunder and hit a spear knocking him to the floor. Foamy brought him up to his feet as he locked in and hit a vertical suplex on to the floor again. Foamy rolled over on to the body of Thunder and started laying in the right to the face as Thunder tried to toss him away. Foamy then grabbed up Thuinder and tossed him into the ring as he followed. Thunder got to his feet and backed away from Foamy as he came runing at him with a hard closeline. Thunder ducked the move as Foamy hit the ropes and then was laid out with a superkick from Thunder. Thunder then grabbed up Foamy and tossed him into the corner. He started to hit him with hard kicks to the stamach as he stepped back and ran right in with a knee to the face. Foamy started to bleded from the nose from that shot. Thunder then pulled him to his feet as he whiped him in to the far ropes. Foamy came back but as he did Thunder jumped into the air and went for a leap frog but Foamy caught him in the air as he slammed him down with a powerbomb. He then hit The Stormer as he picked up the win and advanced to the next round.~ The British Bomber vs Prodigy Diabolical Day Quarter Final ~The next match up was to face the shocking returning to the ring British Bomber and one half of the tag team champs in Prodigy. He then took a set of the stairs and put Bombers leg in the middle of them as he came down with a hard foot on the top making them sandwich his knee in the middle. Bomber rolled away holding it in pain as Prodigy pulled him up and tossed him in the ring. Bomber fought and fought but as he did he reversed the hold and locked on a reverse Englishlock as Prodigy started to tap away.~ Interfed Match Spawn [FAW] vs Crimson Lord [XCWF] The match between former tag team partners and friends began with Crimson attacking Spawn as he walked down to the ring... From there, Crimson dominated, and at one point, dropped Spawn on his back, onto the hard concrete floor... Once both men were back in the ring, the bell sounded, and we were underway with some stiff ring work from Crimson. Spawn responded with many countering moves, and at one point executed the "Last Kill" for a two count... It was going down to the wire, as Crimson began to slap on many submission moves, which Spawn countered time-after-time... Crimson then placed Spawn on the top turnbuckle, and proceeded to superplex Spawn down to the canvas. However, the referee hade counted to five, and was attempting to pull Crimson's left leg... The XCWF star booted the referee down, and executed the superplex, but there was no-one to officiate the count... Crimson then went onto the outside and took the timekeeper and ring announcer's steel chairs, throwing both into the ring... He placed the two chairs on their legs, side-by-side, and attempted to suplex the former World Champion through the chairs. However, Spawn managed to land on his feet, low-blowing Crimson, and executing a neckbreaker through both chairs! The referee began to come round, and officiated the count... 1.... 2... 3!
