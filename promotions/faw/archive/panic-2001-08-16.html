FAW Card Archive

Panic, 16 August 2001

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
OOC: I am so sorry about results but see my post on roleplay board to find out more. I have done as much as possible. But I had 2 results writers say they could write and not do them (no hard feelings to them) and then I had to get 2 people to do a match each. And I have been writing since 9:30pm till 10:30pm to get it this far and I ain't had a break! I am sorry that there are like loads of matches not written but please forgive me! I'm gonna be ready for an explosive Entrapment though! :::Panic Intro The Panic music hits and the cameras come on with pyros exploding in the Mississippi Coliseum. The cameras span the crowd a few times and look at various signs then to the announce table. Michael Cole: Welcome to Panic! We are here in the Mississippi Coliseum, I am Michael Cole alongside Gee Monie tonight and we see a great night here tonight, XWW with FAW Title shots just 3 days away from Entrapment live on Sunday 19th this week! Gee Monie: A night where FAW will kick some serious monkey ass! It will be one hell of a night I promise you! And yours truly still not booked but we will see about that come the end of the night, you see I had a chat with the prez of this business and my rival, Sadam Hussain, well I will show you the footag later but I will expect a reply from him! Michael Cole: And you deserve one! But tonight we see a light-heavyweight title match and there will be 10 men surrounding this very ring! But the 10 men have not yet been named! Gee Monie: And of course tonight it is up to Spawn to keep that title away from XWW ranks and keep it with the almighty FAW! :::The Main Event Promo The Omega Fro comes down to the ring. The Omega Fro: Finally The Omega Fro has become the world champion. I said that I was going to win this title and I did. I beat Shawn Johnson, I beat my good friend Skipster, and I beat a hall of famer, in Pyro, all at the same time. But my celebrations were spoiled when that, Misfit-X came down and got into my business. Apparently, Mystic-X has been given a shot at the world title.Well, Mystic, tonight's the night that you get your shot! With that out comes Shawn Johnson Shawn : Listen Fro, first of all, I haven't come out here to ask for a rematch or anything, there'll be plenty of time for that. But tonight, its 4 days away from Entrapment. We can get X another time, but tonight, lets take out XWW! Tonight, You should be challenging the XWW World champion The Great One to a match. This brings out Highwayman. Highwayman: Sorry but that just aint gonna happen. The Great One doesn't want to beat you two events in a row so he aint gonna come down and do that because he's a good guy. But I'll be happy to kick both your asses right here right now. Fro: You can shut your mouth now or I'll come up there and shut it for you. This brings out The Great One. Great One: Sorry about that Omega Fro. What Highwayman was meant to say was that I didn't want to beat, embarrass and humiliate you two events in a row. Fro: Well that aint gonna happen if you don't face me tonight is it. Highwayman: Ok I've got an idea. How about, The Great One and Highwayman, against The Omega Fro and that other dork standing next to you. Johnson : Dork!? Allright then, this match will happen, and FAW will reign supreme here tonight. The Great One: Allright then it's settled, but until that match, The Great One and Highwayman, are going back, to greatness. Great One's music hits, and they all leave. :::Commercial Break :::Cut Backstage The cameras cut backstage to where Sadam Hussain is standing with Tony Blair. Tony Blair: So, Sadam we heard what Gee said in his commentary, do you remember the chat he is showing us straight after this next match? Sadam Hussain: Definitely do! Tony Blair: BUT will you reply to it? Sadam Hussain: Damn right I will, that Dark Generation has earnt a way into the PPV this Sunday and we will see him win for FAW! And when he has, it is back to FAW and showing me whether he has it or not! Tony Blair: We all await it! :::Shadow vs Skipster - Hardcore Rules - Hardcore Title WINNER: Skipster via chair shot to Shadow with Skipster having bin over head, getting the cover and winning 1...2....3! At the end of the match Sam Macaroni ran down and beat down on Skipster. :::Footage From Earlier Gee Monie: And now as promised the footage of the conversation between me and Sadam... The footage rolls on the Furytron. Sadam Hussain: What can I do for YOU, Gee? Gee Monie: Look, I ain't on the Entrapment card? You don't think I can handle it or something? If you ain't careful it will be you I come after there! Sadam Hussain: Look, I know you can do it but I'll have to see where the hell I can fit you in, you can't just demand things! Gee Monie: OH YES I CAN! And I always get what I want! Gee leaves as the scene fades. :::Minion vs Bone Crusher WINNER: Bone Crusher - at end of match Mortician ran down and they started brawling. Gee Monie: Oh my god! The 2 men who have to team are fighting here! Michael Cole: This is madness! They better work this out! :::Commercial Break :::PyRo Interview The camera cuts to PyRo standing outside his locker room with Tony Blair. Tony Blair: You called me here PyRo, I don't have alot of questions for you, but I wanna know, what have you been doing while you have been off from FAW? PyRo: Not alot TB, Just preparin' for a FAW return really, I left and loved this place, I wasn't gonna stay off! The only reason I am back is because I love this place and I want my gold back! And speaking of the World Title gold, the man who screwed me out of that title...MYSTIC X! And I want his ass in a match, I want revenge, and I am not willing to wait till SummerSlaughter in however many weeks, I want my match TONIGHT! That's right Mystic X, I am challenging you to a match, I'm calling you out tonight! Accept the damn match and go one on one with the PyRo! And The Great One wants to talk about me and say how bad I am, well he is next! Tony Blair: Thanks PyRo. The scene fades out. :::Spawn (FAW)(c) vs Butch Dingle (XWW) - European Title Michael Cole: And now, we are ready for our first installment of Inter-Promotional action!! Spawn, the FAW European Champion against XWW Championship challenger, "Butch" Dingle!! Gee Monie: We'll see how this pair's up, with 1 big XWW star against the FAW star who actually has gold!! "Livin' It Up" by Limp Bizkit hits as XWW legend "Butch" Dingle walks to the ring, much to the dismay of the FAW fans... Michael Cole: "Butch" Dingle, headlined the main event at XWW Ultimatum a few weeks back where he lost the hell in a cell match to Highwayman... Gee Monie: You can't doubt Dingle's athletic ability... A great performer, but who is actually proud to be an XWW star? No-one!! Michael Cole: He has been a very loyal XWW superstar, and has even notched up the now defunct EIPW, then EWF Television Title... Spawn's music hits as he celebrates on the entrance ramp... "FAW" chants begin, as Spawn stares down Dingle... Gee Monie: Dingle can match himself to Spawn, THE FAW European Champion. And you would find Spawn would come out on top!! In the case of strength, and overall ability!! Spawn steps into the ring, and gives his title belt to the referee to show off to the fans... Dingle runs at Spawn and connects with a clothesline as the bell rings... Whip by Dingle, reversed by Spawn, into the turnbuckle goes "Butch", who then athletically swings over the top rope onto the apron. He then begins to illegallly choke Spawn as the ref begins the five count... 1... 2... 3... 4... Dingle lets go and takes more advantage with kicks to Spawn. Spawn recovers by pulling down the top rope, which Dingle topples over back into the ring... Kicks by Spawn... And then a massive leg-drop... Cover... 1....2... Kickout... Dingle counters Spawn's chokehold, by using the ropes and following up with a small package... 1 ... 2... Kickout... Michael Cole: Yet another kickout... Gee Monie: I'm not gonna go on about this all night... As far as I'm concerned... XWW sucks... FAW rules!! Goodnight!! Michael Cole: Not so fast, Gee... You're gonna see this match out!! Gee Monie: Damn!! Nearly got away with it!! Clothesline by Dingle, Spawn gets right back up, and uses a cross body press... 1... 2... 3!! Michael Cole: Spawn defeats Dingle!! I don't believe it!! Gee Monie: Yes!! FAW come out on top!! Easy stuff!! :::Backstage The cameras cut to the Commish's office where he has called Mystic X to his office. X: You called for me, and this had better be about my title shot tonight. Commish: Well not exactly, but it is about a match. You see The Omega Fro is already in a match tonight, and someone has just called you out about a match, because he wants his revenge. X: Who? Commish: I'm afraid you will face Pyro tonight. Good luck. X: Pyro, yeah and. What's to be afraid of. I made him tap out at DDU, I'll make him tap tonight! Commish: But the match is a TABLE MATCH! X: I'll make him tap and THEN put him through the damn table then! Mystic X leaves. :::The Mortician vs Spike Monie: Next up, a great match, where 2 FAW veterans i suppose you could call them, will battle it out in singles competition! Cole: That's right, Spike is one of the longest serving superstars here in FAW, and Mortician has been here a long time aswell! Monie: I been looking forward to this match for a while now, and can't wait for it to get going! Cole: Well, its a shame that no stipulations have been added, but oh well, here we go! Spikes music hits and Spike appears at the top of the ramp. He looks pretty confident, and sets off on the reletviley short walk to the ring. He slides into the ring, and simply bounces on the ropes, and watches the FAWtron for The Mortician. Monie: Now all we need now is the Mortician. Cole: Well, i dont think this guy will just not bother turning up! Suddenly, Spike is pulled out of the ring, by The Mortician. The bell doesnt ring, and the Mortician knocks Spike down. Mortician walks over to the announce table, and pushes one of the technical crew out of the way. He then picks up the chair they were sitting on, and hits Spike right over the head! Monie: A DQ surley! Cole: The bell hasn't rung, a goos strategy there by The Mortician! Mortician then throws Spike into the ring, and slides slowly in after him, and the match offically gets going. The bell rings. Mortician places Spike in the corner and delivers kicks to the midsection. He then whips Spike into the opposite corner, and charges at him and hits a Stinger Splash. Cole: Spike looks hurt here! Monie: Mortician on top here. Spike falls onto the mat, and The Mortician goes for the cover... Pin: 1....2....3! Gee Monie: And he won it! Furious Alliance prevail! :::Commercial Break :::PyRo vs Mystic X - Table Match WINNER: PyRo - The 2 men made each other tap but PyRo got in a powerbomb to put Mystic X through a table. :::Sadam Hussain Promo Rollin' hits and Sadam Hussain gets into the ring with a mic. Sadam Hussain: Okay, for a start I would like to address Entrapment, the event where FAW are going to destroy the XWW/PSW alliance and take control of the world of them! You all saw the footage of Gee Monie demanding his match and I have decided to grant him the match! You see he will be involved in the 6 man tag match, the match will be Suicidal J/Minion/Gee Monie vs Pheonix/Hacksaw/SexyRob....that match will be an easy FAW win of course! And also on that night we see the Total Freeze Over team fighting The Goodfellas, and with Skipster defending his Hardcore Title successfully, we have discussed this over and that match will now be a HARDCORE RULES, HARDCORE TITLE MATCH! What a match that will be, the Hardcore Title will be on the line and if one of The Goodfellas pins either JC Ice or Skipster the Hardcore Title goes to them 2 as joint champions! Sadam Hussain: Now onto tonight, the next match is a lumberjack match for that light-heavyweight title! And no-one currently knows the 10 men who will surround this very ring, well I am out here to tell you the men! You see after long discussions we have decided to make it an even match, we have decided to put out 5 FAW men and 5 XWW men! And they are to be, starting with XWW, Hacksaw, Crisis, SexyRob, The Goodfellas! Now for FAW's 5 elitist, The Mortician, Skipster, JC Ice, Minion, Shadow! So, see you at Entrapment! Sadam walks off and backstage. :::Suicidal J (c) vs Chris Thunder - Light-Heavyweight Title - Lumberjack Match Match continues... Suicidal J then gets thrown to the outside to the FAW stars and Thunder gets out to the XWW side and all 12 men get into the ring. The all out brawl then continues with the 6 vs 6 continuing. The referee rings the bell and Suicidal J remains the champion. The scene goes to a commercial break as the 12 men continue to fight. :::Commercial Break :::The Omega Fro & Shawn Johnson (FAW) vs The Great One & Highwayman (XWW) WINNER: The XWW team of The Great One and Highwayman! As the match comes to an end Highwayman puts Shawn through a table and The Great One puts The Omega Fro through a table. Gee Monie: Exactly the opposite to what you will see at the main event of Entrapment! :::After The Match The Camera goes backstage. The XWW/PSW are fighting with FAW all over the place. Sadam is chasing after Thunder. He runs to the parking lot and rides away in his limo. Sadam turns around and Clinton is standing there. Bill: So Sadam, how ya doing. O don't worry, I'm not going to take you out, oh no I'll leave that until Sunday. But right now, why don't you look behind you. He looks around and Thunder nails him with a chair. Thunder: After Sunday, FAW, will stand for Forgotten American Wrestling, cus it's wrestling, it's American, and it'll be forgotten! Commish then comes running onto the scene and Thunder and Clinton high tail it in the limo. Commish kneels down to Sadam and checks on him while looking on at the limo driving off. The scene fades as the FAW logo comes up.
