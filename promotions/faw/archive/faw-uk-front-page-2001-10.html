Or click HERE for the card. 28th September 2001 | FiW Card/PANIC:UK Results The card is up for FAW IS WAR, and more importantly, the PANIC:UK results are up! And what an event it was! A great event all round and we saw many things take place, titles on the line, main event changes, a car accident, a main event change and much, much more! 27th September 2001 | PANIC:UK PANIC:UK is now just a short 24 hours away and the deadline is tonight at midnight EVERYWHERE (UK, US). So get those roleplays in and win your damn matches and get the matches written as you deserve! Thankyou and goodnight. 25th September 2001 | Staff I have just put up a staff page and added EWCentral (we now host our site with them @ http://www.ewcentral.com/faw/) and Prime Time Ratings (soon to be our interfed) to the links section in the bottom left. The Staff page is HERE and also under HQ to the left. 25th September 2001 | Roster/Applications The roster is updated and once more no bios! Sorry for this but they will be up within 2 weeks of now. I promise that. And also the applications are still open BUT we have heard from the back office that FAW is looking for major tag teams. To sign up as a tag team there is now a new tag team application form up, and it is to be found under the Singles one. Thankyou. 24th September 2001 | FiW Results/PANIC:UK Card They are both up and can be found by clicking the links and you know where to find the links, to the right! Also Superstar X link added under LINKS. 23rd September 2001 | Summer Slaughter Results/Press Release The Summer Slaughter results are up, and they are great! I enjoyed them and I hope you will too! They are to be found either under the PPV banner to the right or by clicking HERE. Also, there is a press release done straight after Summer Slaughter but I urge you to read SS first as it is revealed in there what it is about. PLEASE READ SS FIRST! The press release can be found to the left under NEWS (under HQ) or by clicking HERE. Thankyou and enjoy WWF Unforgiven AFTER Summer Slaughter! Oh and Champions to the right updated tommorrow so as not to spoil Summer Slaughter for you all... Bone Crusher This guy has worked hard lately and for that reason he will be getting a title shot at Summer Slaughter. He has worked hard and impressed me and the staff! A great guy and we look forward to seeing more of him...
