Front Office Home Rules Appliaction Members Roster Champions Forums Sunday, June 01, 2003 Breakaway PPV - @ 11:27 PST Unfortunately, we have to announce that todays PPV will be pushed back to Wednesday night. This past week we have installed the backstage script and perhaps upon reflection we chose the wrong time to make such a radical change to the infrastructure of FAW. It has come to the staffs attention that alot of people have had problems accessing this backstage area and due to Jamie having computer troubles this past week he has been unable to correct the issues in a manner which would allow everyone to have a fair chance going into this major event in terms of their ability to RP. After much thought we have decided to postpone the event allowing everyone to have until TUESDAY MIDNIGHT GMT (6pm CST) to roleplay for an event which could well shape the coming months here in FAW since so much is at stake. In another announcement, FAW has taken the decision to put the FAW Continental Title on the line in the forthcoming Turmoil Match scheduled for the PPV. This decision means the likes of Sniper, Costella, Bomber, Juvian and The President of the Altered States now have the chance to capture one of FAW's most prestigous honours. Once again apoligies for the inconvenience, but I hope you can appreciate that FAW prides itself on being fair to all and doing anything but postponing this event would hinder the chances of some through no fault of their own. Log In Television PANIC card PANIC results Past PANICs Other Shows Pay Per View PPV Card Latest PPV PPV History PPV Headliners Fans Hall of Fame Title History FAW Past FAW Rules Character Creation 1. One character is permitted per person unless they are in a tag team together, in which case should they split up one of the wrestlers shall be taken off of the roster. 2. No real wrestlers allowed, although we do accept things related to real life if your name is “The Rock” you won’t be accepted. 3. Unless the owner(s) say otherwise you must enter a sample RP. 4. After being accepted it is down to you to contact others and begin feuds and hype yourself around PTC also. 5. A history is not necessarily needed but the more detailed the application the more chance of entering FAW’s roster. Roleplaying 1. You are expected to roleplay at least twice per card, if you fail to roleplay what-so-ever without reason you will be suspended for one card. If you roleplay once but not a second time then you will merely receive a warning, should it occur a second time within the next four cards you will then be suspended. After a first suspension should you have to be suspended for a second time for this reason your contract will be terminated. 2. If no roleplay or sign is seen of you within 2 weeks then your contract can be terminated and you can be fired, this does not apply if we know about it or we have kept your wrestler after you leave. 3. Roleplays are not expected to have an overdose of swearing in, an occasional word is accepted but on the whole, we expect them to be clean OR blocked out. 4. It is important to mention the match that you are in for your role-play, this does not apply if you are introducing yourself or sparking off a feud. Even if it’s just once or twice mentioning your opponents that is acceptable (Although it doesn’t do much for your chances during your match). 5. The wrestler must always act as though he is really fighting someone and not use terms like "roleplay", "angle", "handler", and never call another wrestler by their REAL name. 6. Roleplays must not include members of the front office, handlers, staff, managers of other wrestlers without permission. This includes the President(s)/Owner(s). 7. You may use Melissa Farrow, Adam Hutton or James Harris as an interviewer in Roleplays. 8. Roleplays are due in at midnight GMT the night before the event. 9. There is a limit of 2 Roleplays per day for any form of card. 10. The roleplay limit’s for each card are as follows: Televised Shows - Three per show Pay-Per-Views - Five per show Challenges and Matches 1. Gimmick matches are handed out by the FAW staff, so please don't expect to get one automatically if you request it, they are normally handed out for angles and feuds. Should you request a gimmick match and get it you are expected to roleplay extremely strongly for the match. 2. Despite all our best attempts we cannot fit all interference and angles into the results, however we will try to fit it in on a future card. 3. Sneak Attacks and interferences must always include a reason so we know why and how. 4. If you interfere in a match it should be for a feud purpose, no other reason unless it is to help someone else’s feud. 5. Do not complain about not receiving title shots or high profile matches, the owners decide who gets what and if that is not clear we will make it so. Championships 1. All titles can only change hands with a submission or pin fall unless otherwise stated, e.g. Ladder matches, you grab the belt off the roof or Triple Cage is the same. 2. Title holder(s) can hand title shots out to anyone, but the front office can hand out title shots to number 1 contenders if they are being avoided. 3. The World Title is the top belt in FAW. It must be defended at least 1 time a month and at EVERY PPV. The holder is normally the best Roleplayer in the federation at the time. 4. The Continental Title is the second best belt in FAW, if you hold this belt you are on your way to World Title quality. If you need any tips then please don’t hesitate to ask any of the staff as we are always welcoming people who want to work to the top, it shows dedication. 5. The push belt in FAW is the Hardcore belt, you are on your way up if you hold it. 6. The Tag Team Championship is a hard title to win, only the best team wins the belts. It must be defended at least 1 time a month. Although you don’t have to be in a tag team to win the title it is recommended if you know your ally and work together to produce a pair of top quality series of Roleplays. 7. If a title is lost then the former champion reserves the right to get a rematch at the card of his choice, if the title is won back the 2nd person does not receive a rematch. General Rules 1. Matches are 95% decided by Roleplays although we will tell you if you will lose due to angle reasons, however we still expect you to roleplay to keep up your form. 2. FAW is a roleplay federation, you must roleplay. 3. FAW is a family fed with a hint of non-family! We aim to provide no crimes being committed and no blood and violence. 4. Act your alignment, heels hate fans, fans hate heels, faces love fans, fans love faces, Neutrals/Tweeners don't mind either way! 5. All deadlines are GMT Time unless otherwise noted! 6. Do not complain about favouritism, the winners of matches are carefully decided, unless you have a very strong case then you will be punished. 7. All wrestlers upon contract acceptance become the intellectual property of the FAW. Whereas if your character leaves the federation, we observe the right to retain the character to be used to finish an angle and or a farewell match, if you will. In addition to this rule, there is something called the Showtime clause: Essentially if you leave, these things happen, and good luck. But if you insist on making a rant prior to your leaving, your character will be seized and paraded around like a complete moron. Shows and Match Rules 1. Panic airs every Sunday night and you can expect at least 1 title to be on the line and 4-8 matches every event! It is 1 and a half hours long and you get the best action ever in the wrestling world! 2. FAW has a monthly PPV where there are up to 11 matches and every title is on the line, feuds are settled and you can never predict what will happen! 3. We try to get the results up on the day of the event and the card is posted at the same time as the results go up for the next show. 4. Do not moan and ask when the results will be, we play our part and you play yours, we can only post the card when we receive all the segments and matches. Please Note: Due to PTC Rules, if you quit, we reserve the right to NOT release you - and due to PTC Rules, you will not be allowed to join another PTC Fed if this is the case. If you want a release then contact the Head of Recruitment or the owner(s).
