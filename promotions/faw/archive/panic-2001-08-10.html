"Highway To Hell" by AC/DC hits and down the ramp comes the former FAW International and XWW Intercontinental Champion, British Bomber... The crowd are booing like hell after last weeks attack on Chimera... Bomber gets in the ring and grabs a mic... Bomber: Now, I know how much you fans... Just then Bomber is cut off by the more popular VP of XWW, 9 Inch Sean... Who has said to have already been fired by the staff... Bomber wastes no time in rushing to 9 Inch, and he immediately meets 9 Inch with a clothesline... Bomber then grabs the back of 9 Inch's neck and throws the VP over the steel steps... Bomber unloads on 9 Inch, who replies with a low-blow, followed up by a bulldog... Michael Cole: These two men really are getting it on here tonight!! Gee Monie: Well, you can't blame them for not trying to show off their ability... The two men finally enter the ring and Bomber strikes 9 Inch with his elbow... Sean slides under Bomber's legs but is met by a powerbomb!! Cover... 1... 2... Kickout... Bomber begins to once again unload on the VP... But Sean takes advantage with a small package... 1... 2... Kickout... 9 Inch with a clothesline and then the 9 Inch Stretch to top it off!! Deadly J runs down to the ring and distracts the referee while Bomber taps out!! Michael Cole: Bomber is tapping!! It's over!! SURELY!! Chris Thunder then appears through the crowd and grabs a steel chair. He makes a gesture at Gee along the way... Gee Monie: That's it, this one is my duty!! Michael Cole: Where the hell are you goin'? Gee Monie takes his headset off and paces around the ring for a second... At that moment, Thunder and 9 Inch stand apart, and Thunder cracks the steel chair over his head!! Gee meanwhile, pulls Deadly J from the apron, and then attacks Chris in the ring!! Gee and Thunder fight onto the outside as the ref sees Bomber making the cover... Thunder overpowers Gee and tries to break up Bomber's cover, but it's too late... 1... 2... 3!! Michael Cole: Bomber's career saved!! 9 Inch is sensationally retired!! Bomber stands up and celebrates, while Thunder hits him with the chair too!! Thunder stomps away on Bomber as Spike comes to the ring, and clears Chris Thunder out!! Thunder grabs a mic... Chris Thunder: Spike... See you shortly, when you get your ass whooped!! Michael Cole: The XWW VP is sensationally retired!! Chris Thunder has left mayhem behind him!! We have two bodies down in the ring, and two on the outside!! Including my commentrary partner, Gee Monie!! And when we come back, Thunder is out here again, to take on Spike!! Spike vs. Chris Thunder (Non-Title Match)(Anything Goes Match) Spike is seen walking around backstage, when from behind him comes the FAW Light-Heavyweight Champion, Chris Thunder with a chair... He clocks Spike with the chair, and places him on his back... Chris Thunder: Spike!! I've chosen the stipulation for our match... ANYTHING GOES!! The referee calls for the bell and the match begins right there and then... Michael Cole: That's not fair!! Thunder taking advantage of Spike!! Gee Monie: I suppose that's the way it goes. I don't like neither of these guys, so I'm hoping for a real brawl!! Thunder is a real jackass... Saving British Bomber's career at the hands of my XWW counterpart... Michael Cole: Do you think 9 Inch has a future here in FAW? Gee Monie: I wouldn't say so... He would get his ass beat every week!! Thunder grabs Spike's ear and rams him into a nearby steel door which bursts open to reveal mops and hoovers.... Thunder then arm drags Spike onto one of the hoovers... Spike lies in pain as Thunder makes the cover... 1... 2... Kickout Spike counters Thunder's next move attempt with a low-blow followed up by a swinging neckbreaker right onto the concrete floor... With both men down the ref begins the ten count... 1..... 2..... 3..... 4..... 5..... 6..... 7...... Both men scramble back to their feet exchanging blows... Thunder ducks Spike's attempt of a forearm, and gives Spike a snap suplex!! Thunder then struggles up the steel door onto the balcony after placing a mop on the stomach of Spike... Michael Cole: Where the hell is he goin'? Gee Monie: Don't tell me he's gonna jump... Michael Cole: Maybe he is too extreme for any FAW superstar!! Thunder celebrates and jumps!! Spike moves out the way, and Thunder's face rattles off the mop!! Thunder screams in pain as Spike gets to his feet and grabs a steel chair... Thunder suddenly arm drags Spike again as he bleeds... And follows up with The Stormer through the merchandise table!! Cover... 1... 2... 3!! Michael Cole: Chris Thunder wins it!! Gee Monie: I'm really surprised with that win!! Thunder makes a gesture in the face of the dazed Spike and walks off as EMT's arrive to attend to Spike... Michael Cole: Spike wasn't 100% though... Gee Monie: And the attack from behind didn't help either!!
