Real Name: Jason Jeffrey Jacobson Birthdate: November 8, 1975 Hometown: Boston, MA Height: 6'6' Nicknames: The Master of Destiny Status: Neutral Current Teams: None Entrance Music: 'Across the Nation' by Union Underground Wrestling Style: Technical, Powerhouse, Submissionist, Hardcore and Aerial (All-Around Technician) Signature Moves: Spinning Spinebuster, Front Suplex w/Armbreaker (holds opponent’s arm across opponent’s chest while executing the move), Death Valley Driver, Military Press into a Piledriver, Tilt-a-Whirl Sideslam, Dropping Facebuster (X-Factor), the Fall-Away Slam, Spinning Belly to Belly Suplex, Snap Suplex, Belly to Belly Suplex, T-bone Suplex, Jump Swinging DDT, Side Russian Legsweep, DDT, Shooting Star Press, Top Rope Headbutt, Top Rope Crossbody, Sharpshooter, Double Arm Stretch (Rings of Saturn) and the Cobra Clutch Other Federations: 4-Time USWF United States Champion, USWF Cruiserweight Champion, UWA Cruiserweight Champion and UWA IC Champion Handler Information Handler Name: [name withheld] MSN Name: [detail withheld] Bio HistoryJ.J. Dynasty was born as Jason Jeffrey Jacobson in Boston, Massachusetts in 1975 and grew up watching the professional wrestlers of the WWF. This included such characters as Pedro Morales, Tito Santana, Ricky Steamboat, the British Bulldogs, Rick Rude, Randy Savage and Sergeant Slaughter. When Jacobson turned 21on November 8, 1996 he signed onto a wrestling school taught by Killer Kowalski and spent the better part of two years in the school, learning traits that he would later use in his professional wrestling career. In November of 1998 Jacobson entered his first federation, the Central Wrestling Alliance, as J.J. Jacobson. However as most young federations go, this one did not last long as it shut down in early January, leaving Jacobson with only twelve professional matches under his belt and no paycheck coming in. J.J. spent the better part of three months refining his skills as well as his outer personality before returning to the world of Pro Wrestling in late April of 1999 as part of the newly founded United States Wrestling Federation where he legally changed his name to J.J Dynasty. He spent a good amount of time feuding over that federation's United States Championship, a belt he wore four times between June of 1999 and May of 2000, also claiming the USWF Cruiserweight Title on one occasion in late 1999. Then, seeing as the USWF was slowly dying, J.J. jumped ship to the United Wrestling Association in early August of 2000 to seek success in 'greener pastures' as he called it. He entered the UWA and earned almost instant success by winning the UWA Cruiserweight Title in early September 2000 and then winning the UWA Intercontinental Title in October 2000, holding both belts until January 7 of 2001. On that date he competed in a match against Nova and suffered a Novacaine of the top rope, allowing Nova to pick up the win. However, J.J. suffered a fracture of his L-4, L-5 and L-6 vertebrate after the move and required surgery, causing him to resign from UWA two weeks later. He spent the better part of fourteen months recuperating from his injuries before stepping back into the ring in April of 2002. He proceeded to train his body back to the status it once was at and awaiting a company to sign on with. Though he is still capable of high-flying aerial maneuvers, he is no longer a true cruiserweight. He has built his body into a more massive, yet muscular 260-pound powerhouse (up from his old weight of 225 pounds) and is truly a force to be reckoned with. He signed on with the CCW in May of 2002 and was shown as more sadistic than ever before as he began playing headgames with all of the CCW stars until CCW abruptly ended and Dynasty was once again without work.He routinely refers to his horrible accident as his 'reawakening' and has seemingly grown stronger physically as well as mentally. Dynasty has a very strange outlook on the wrestling business since becoming active again. He believes it is his duty to 'save' those who put their bodies and lives on the line by ending their careers so that they might be able to live a better life. He created the Road to Hell brainbuster upon his debut into the business and has made it one of the most dangerous finishers ever seen. He has recently added the Eternal Damnation and the Destiny Driver to his repertoire, as well as modifying his submission hold, the Dynasty Dreamer, to make it even more painful. He believes that he is above the standards that he sets for the rest of the world and is not against showing his opponents the error of their ways by putting them out of commission for period of time.There is something almost inhuman about J.J. in that when he is in the ring he carries himself with an almost God-like confidence and rarely moves away from that look. There have been numerous times where he has shown an aggressive, sadistic and damn-near satanic side to him, causing many to wonder if perhaps in exchange for the wrestling skills that he has he may have needed to sell his soul to the devil himself. His main catchphrase has always been 'You cannot change your destiny and you sure as hell can't beat Dynasty!' and so far in J.J.'s career that sentence has proven to be very, very true. GimmickA 'reborn' wrestler who after breaking his neck gained a new meaning for life. He believes that his purpose is to show other wrestlers that they are nothing and that they should be simply taken out. He is trained in the art of 'mind games' and is always willing to use his psychological skills to mess with his opponents. He is ruthless and will stop at nothing to gain whatever power he feels he deserves.AppearanceJ.J. Dynasty stands 6'6' and weighs 260 pounds. He has blue eyes and short black hair, in an Army-style haircut. He also has a well-trimmed black goatee, which highlights his near-perfect smile. He has a well-chiseled body, best compared to that of Brock Lesner, including a six-pack, 24-inch pythons, muscular legs and a very large upper body. He has a good tan, which he still works on and only about 6% body fat. He wears a size 13 boot and has well-defined calves, triceps, abdominals and pectorals. He has one tattoo: that of barbed wire around his upper left bicep, and no piercings. Ring AttireDynasty's apparel is rather bland compared to that of most wrestlers. He wears dark blue wrestling shorts and a tight, dark blue tanktop. He wears traditional black wrestling boots and black wristbands. His shorts, tanktop, boots and wristbands all have a 'J.J.' insignia on them to identify them. He also wears his black leather jacket and black tear-away pants to the ring, both of which he removes before he begins a match. Both of those items are also emblazoned with his 'J.J.' insignia.Enterance (Pyros, Music, etc)The lights dim and a voice plays over the Public Address system, saying the words 'You cannot change your destiny, and you sure as hell can't beat Dynasty' as the same words flash across the screen and a white spotlight shines down on the entrance ramp. Rob Zombie's 'Never Gonna Stop' begins to play as Dynasty walks out from behind the curtain and makes his way down the ramp as dark blue flames shoot up from the sides of the ramp. The pyros shoot off, matching Dynasty step for step as he walks down the ramp. When he reaches the bottom of the ramp he raises his arms into the air and all the pyros on the ramp, as well as all the pyros on the stage shoot off in one huge burst of flames.
