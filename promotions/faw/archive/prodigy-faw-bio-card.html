Roster card

Real Name: Scott Costella Birthdate: 12th June 1976 Hometown: Detroit, Michigan Height: 6'5'' Status: Neutral Current Teams: with Chris Thunder Entrance Music: 'Never Gonna Stop Me' By Rob Zombie Wrestling Style: Powerhouse/Brawler/Hardcore Signature Moves: Fracture (Sharp Shooter/Arm Bar), Triple Powerbomb with sitdown Pin, T-Bone DDT, Backslam, Swinging DDT Other Federations: FWF, UCW, UWF Other Federation Awards: UCW TV Champ, UCW Hardcore Champ (3), UCW Tag Champ (4), UCW Intercontinental Champ (5), UWF Hardcore Champion (2), UWF World Champion, UWF Hall of Famer Handler Information Handler Name: [name withheld] MSN Name: [detail withheld] Bio Scott Costellas career has been short compared to such greats as The Undertaker, The Rock, Ric Flair and Hulk Hogan but already he has been compared to them. At the age of 26 Scott has held 16 titles, over 3 federations in 3 years and 9 months including The UWF World Title. Scott has even gone down the same same road as Hulk Hogan and The Rock and had an acting career.Scott is not well known but when you hear his name it is normally connected with the amazing bumps he is so willing to take. He has been powerbombed through the roof of a Hell In A Cell, cut open with flaming barb wire, put through numerous tables, hit with ladders and chairs, been powerbombed off the stage of the UWF arena not to mention the the brutal events that took place in the Street Fight between himself and his ex-best friend. He is a brawler through and through and after taking such dangerous bumps he can still manage to get up and continue.Although Scott is not well known, whenever he makes a debut in a fed he makes an imediate impact such as his debut in UWF when he won the Hardcore Championship in his first week and went on to win the World Title shortly after.Since making his pro-wrestling debut in June 1999 Scott has been wowing fans with a mix of power, charisma and ability. ----On 12th May, 1976 Scott Costella was born to John and Sarah Costella. Only a year before him, on 6th March 1975, his brother, Steve Costella was born. Scott and Steve grew up in Detroit, Michigan and from the age of 5 and 6 they both grew up watching wrestling. They developed a love for wrestling and it wasn't long before they both dreamed of becoming wrestlers just like their idols Hulk Hogan and Ultimate Warrior... By the age of 12 and 13 they had made a make-shift wrestling ring in their backyard and practiced wrestling on each other. Scott even broke Steves' wrist trying out a wrestling move. Several years later at the age of 16 Steve graduated from school and managed to secure an office job and a year later Scott graduated and took an assistants job at the local bank. By the age of 19 and 18 they had saved up quite a bit of money and with the money their parents gave them, they moved to Boston and enroled in The Killer Kowalski School Of Wrestling, which was run by Walter 'Killer' Kowalski. Not many people can say they were trained by a legand. After training for 3 years they were skilled in the sport of wrestling and set out to start their career and fulfill their dream. In 1997 they started out in the indy circuit, in NWA (National Wrestling Aliance). Steve used his childhood gimmick of Kid Blaze and Scott took on the character of Voltage. They tagged together and made quite an impression. They were even labled as the two who were most likely to make it big. They stayed at NWA and became quite popular with its audience but Indy status wasn't enough. They wanted to be in the big time and become idols to kids like their idols was to them. Scott first made his pro-wrestling debut in FWF (Fantasy Wrestling Federation) under the guise of Kid Blaze. His career in the FWF was short lived and winless. From the start in June, 1999 to August, 1999 - when FWF closed down - Kid Blaze never won a title nor a single match. During his FWF career he feuded with Nikidemis 'The Darkstar' Fitzgerald. His most dangerous match (to date) was with this man. It was the culmination of their fued and at the 3rd FWF PPV they faced each other in a Devils Playground-Blazers Yard match (Inferno/Barbwire Match within Hell in a Cell including Tables, Ladders, Chairs and other hardcore equipment). Kid Blaze was totally slaughtered by The Darkstar and was even powerbombed through the roof of the cell into a table below. He was also cut open with flaming barb wire and nearly set on fire twice. Scott suffered a broken arm for his troubles and was out for 3 months.For 3 months Scott wasn't booked anywhere because of his injury until his brother contacted him about signing up with him in his and his partners fed, Universal Championship Wrestling. Moving to UCW, at the start of 2000, Scott dropped the 'Kid' to become Blazer. Blazer also brought along his stable, Forces Of Nature, containing Twister and Tidal. Shortly after he turned on his team and a short Blazer/FON feud began. Within weeks of dropping from FON Blazer became TV Champ - his first ever championship - after defeating Elmo and Enigma in a 3 Way Ladder Match. Blazer lost it shortly after to Elmo and became Scott. A few weeks would pass and the Commissioner - Steve Costella (Scotts Brother), made Scott and Elmo team together. Scott and Elmo proved to be a good team and Scott became 'The Show' and his team, Elmo and himself, became The Showstars - dancing and singing they became the most popular and successful tag team in UCW history. The Showstars won the Tag Titles from Forces Of Nature. Loosing the tag titles to The Hardcore Revolution they won them back 3 weeks later at the Capitol Punishment PPV in a Tables Match. The 2nd tag title reign was longer than the first. This 3 way tag feud went back and forth for a couple of months until the Mayhem PPV, where in a 3 way match FON won the tag titles back but also put Elmo out of action for several months with a devastating submission manoeuvre. Scott, now more commonly known as 'The Show', set out on his solo career again. Within weeks, Scott took the Hardcore Title, from the most successful Hardcore Titleist in UCW, Reject- also ending Rejects 3 month long reign. The Show learned to become hardcore but lost the title to Damage. He won the Hardcore Championship once more, winning it back from Damage. The Show left the hardcore scene and moved up to the Intercontinental ranks. He won his first Intercontinental Championship against Reject, under the guide of his new manager, Nina. Nina became Scotts manager after Scott saved her from Reject and Dixky. This sparked off a short Show vs. Reject fued. For 4 months Scott stayed in the Intercontinental ranks, becoming a 4 time Intercontinental Champ. During this period The Red Blazer, who believed he was superhero, joined UCW. He would join forces with FON to take on Nitemares Ministry. The Red Blazer-Nitemare feud would end with a Darkside Rules Casket Match, in which The Ministry would help Nitemare retain the Intercontinental Title. After this brutal match Nitemare took off The Red Blazers mask to reveal Scott. In the last two months a masked man started to run-in on Scotts matches, costing him the match - several times, these were title matches. Scott would also loose out in The Ring Masters 2000 tournament due to his masked attacker. Show beat Nitemare at Bloodshed for his 4th Intercontinental victory but lost it at UCW: Warfare, 2 weeks later. Elmo was at ringside, still on crutches and Elmo tried to aid Show by swingin at Nitemare with the crutches but missed and hit Show, allowing Nitemare to pick up the win. This lead to A Night To Remember;A Night To Remember would mark Elmos return to UCW, refereeing The Show vs. Nitemare Iron Man match for the Intercontinental title. This was probably one the most important matchs of his career - He would also set a new record with the most Intercontinental Championships captured (if he won). The match was going good with 5 minutes left, and the score 4 all. In the dying moments of the match, Show looked to go 1 up on Nitemare, by trying land Da' Bomb however Nina climbed upon the apron and pushed Show off the turnbuckle, she would then climb on herself and land Show with an Ellison-Bomb. Elmo, who until now had remained fair, got Show and El-Iminatered him, placed Nitemare over him and made the pin. He would then stand over the fallen Show and take the masked attackers mask from his back pocket and trash talk Show. Nina turned out to be Elmos sister.This would begin a deeply emotional and rage fuelled rivalry. Upon the turn on his parnter, Elmo became Renegade - a more darker and hate filled version of Elmo. After this transformation Show became Overdrive - a more hardcore version of Show. Renegade said his transformation and turn on his good friend and tag partner was because he was tired of been part of the 'show' and tired of the fans. Scott returned to his first instinct in been Hardcore but would not go for the Hardcore Title Instead he would fight Renegade for the Intercontinental title (with a few appearances from Nitemare) with the Hardcore insights involved. The first Overdrive vs. Renegade match was a month later at The Nightmare Before Christmas PPV with Nitemare as the special guest referee. The Match was for a shot at Nitemares Intercontinental gold and was also a Street Fight. This match would see Renegade walk away Number 1 Contender. The new year swung around and it was time for the Rumble. The Overdrive-Renegade-Nitemare feud was well heated by now and neither one of these men would walk away Rumble winner due to them eliminating each other and continuing to fight on backstage. Steve Costella would make Overdrive apart of the Renegade-Nitemare Intercontinental Match at the Karnage PPV. Karnage would see Nitemare drop from the feud and the Overdrive-Renegade one get even more heated as Overdrive would become a 5 time Intercontinental Champion in a 3 Way TLC match. A couple weeks later and UCW had to shut down. Overdrive would retire a 1 time TV Champ, 4 time Tag Champ, 3 time Hardcore Champ, the current Intercontinental Champ, and a record 5 time Intercontinental Champ. 6 months passed and Scott was again not booked in any major fed. He moved from wrestling school to wrestling school doing training sessions for a month. In June of 2001 Scott Costella entered the show business. It was director Robert Dicamillo, who saw him at the Karnage PPV, who wanted Scott for his wrestling movie. The film was shot in Las Vegas and was called Prodigy. It was about a man who was the best in the business but would get screwed at the most important match of his career. It would then see him make a come back, one year later, from the drunken slob he would be into the best in the business once again. It was not only meant to show the entertainment but the reality behind wrestling. 3 months into filming and it had to close down due to financial reasons - They always said if they could get the money to re-film then they would call Scott back up. October 2001 saw Scotts return to wrestling in the Ultimate Wrestling Federation, only as Jake 'The Silencer' Bullet. He became Hardcore Champion within a week. Jake Bullet lost the Hardcore Championship to the guy he won it off a week later - Double X. 2 weeks later and Jake 'The Silencer' Bullet won the Hardcore Title back but this time kept it. The Silencer then went onto win the UWF World Title after beating Napalm, Omega and the so called 'Living Legend' Chris Powers in an 4 Way Anywhere Falls Match at The Final Four - a knock-out competition with 16 contestants who Scott beat to get to The Final Four. He was dubbed a 'Wrestling Prodigy' and he then dropped 'The Silencer' to adopt 'The Prodigy'. This was further used after some fans learned off his brief Hollywood stint. He was double champ - UWF Hardcore and World Champ. He held both titles for a little just over 2 months where he lost the World Title to Chris Powers with help from his run-in assailant at the UWF December PPV. He then lost the Hardcore Title to Double X due to the 24/7 rule 2 weeks later in mid January. Prodigy was placed in the UWF Hall of Fame for having the longest double title reign and most successful debut and it still stands today.Scott went back to live with his parents for a month before setting back off out where he joined SCW in mid January 2002. Scott Joined SCW (Steel Chair Wrestling) as Prodigy. He never got round to his first match but he did spark a comical friendship with the SCW interviewer - Billy Robinson. Though he was destined for greatness in SCW, SCW was forced to close down when the past owner came back and destroyed his creation.Nearing the end of February, Scott was contacted by Stanley Greer, otherwise known as Freedom. They met in UCW and they have quite a good backstage friendship but never showed it in the ring and they had one or two matchs in UCW. Scott says Freedom was his inspiration to become who he is today. Stan invited Scott along to SCW and also invited him to join XWA (Xtreme Wrestling Aliance). Stan then became the XWA Commissioner. Prodigy had his first XWA Match at XWA: Live on March 9th 2002 against 'The dark one of XWA' Inferno. This was a Hardcore Rules match which Prodigy lost. Prodigy then had several more matchs in XWA before moving on again. He made XWA history by having the quickest match ever - at 6 seconds - by hittin Jack Phillips with The Fury Arrow. He then went onto to draw the Xtreme Title No.1 Contendorship match with Inferno. This then lead to the Prodigy vs. Inferno vs. Black Panther (c) Xtreme Title at Reincarnation. Shortly after Prodigys brother - Steve Costella rang him up about once again wrestling as a team. Looking to make it big as a tag team they set in hope of been contracted by FAW. By Friday 5th April they had secured a contract and were sent to FAWA (Furiously Addictive Wrestling Accademy) - A developmental fed for FAW. They have been there, since, working on the tag team skills...After several matchs, loosing all of them Prodigy decided he had had enough. He accepted Chris Thunders open challenge to prove himself but Enigma ran in and go to Thunder first. Prodigy turned away while Chris Thunder beat down Enigma but ran down to help out at the end. The match was set - Prodigy vs. Chris Thunder on Distorion 5... however... the match took a sudden twist. Chris Thunder start to beat the ref. down which prompted Prodigy to go up to Chris Thunder... Were they shook hands. Enigma getting in the ring to check out whats going on was only greeted with a beating from both stars. Prodigy had turned his back on his brother and his best friend to team with Chris Thunder.. Prodigy said his turn was because he had 'found the perfect friend and partner in Chris Thunder... so much better than his retard brother, Steve..' Prodigy and Chris Thunder are now looking to take the FAW Tag Division by storm....Homepage: http://prodigy117.tripod.com/ Quotes "Be Silenced!" "Who's More Extreme?"

'The Masked Foreigner' then used Prodigies own finisher to win the match. After the match, 'The Masked Foreigner' then spoke perfect english and revealed that Prodigy was a fake... He would then take of his mask to reveal Prodigy!!! - 'THE REAL PRODIGY' Scott Costella...
