(Many children of dreams of walking down the isle, being cheered by 20, 000+ rabid fans wanting to see you do what you do best... Many dream, but never do. People need money, thats a fact, and most of the time dreams take a backseat to taking a dead end job just to make ends meat. For most people, thats their whole life, sit in front of a desk in your little cudbical and check the clock every five minutes aching to get out of the hell whole you call your life. And what kind of life is it anyway? Not one people aspire to have. One man, amoung the many thousands, joined the elite group of guys who shit on 9 to 5 jobs and risk there lives every week in the squared circle, their battle field, just to say they are doing what they always wanted to do. This man's name is Sean Miller, or as the fans of the world of wrestling know him... Saviour. The 6'5", 275lbs electric second generation fan favourite, son of the great Mitch Miller... And he was here... The FAW. The big leagues. He knew that this was his time, it was fate that lead him to this day, the day he would finallyshow all the people in his child hood who said he could never reach that level, that they were wrong. Dead wrong! But he knew, not until he riased the World Heaveyweight Title above his head would he ever be satisfied and content in his quest to be at the top. That feat alone would take every last drop of sweat, every last pint of blood, and every last bit of energy in his body to get the job done. And there were still obstacles in the path to the world championship. Sure ennough they wouldn't just hand over their most important title belt to some rookie to the promotion. That would be ludacris, and Saviour didn't want it that why. He didn't mind climbing the ladder, and he sure as hell didn't care what the FAW bookers had to throw at him, cause no one on the face of the face of the place earth would stop him. The scene fades in to the interview room at the Furiously Addictive Wrestling headquarters. In the background is a blue filt drape of some kind with FAW on it. There are two chairs in the middle of the room; black directors chairs. In one of them, to the far right in the interviewer that FAW asigned to do the interview. He is a small lanky man, only about 5'7 and have black well trimmed hair. Then, the camera pans over abit. You can tell that every fan at home watching leaped to there feet at the site of... Saviour. He sat there in his chair, calm, cool and collected. In his usual black tear-aways with white trime and white t-shirt with his famous nick name, "Intensity Personified" in big black Grunge letters with a blue haze around it. Saviour tilts his black sunglasses down a bit and stares into the camera.) Interviewer: Hello, and welcome Furiously Addictive Wrestling fans, to an exclusive interview with the newest signie to FAW, Saviour! This guy has had huge success in various promotions and has won a ton of awards. Finally, after a few weeks of discussing his contract with us we finally reached an aggreement and here we are! Thank you, Saviour, for taking time out of your scheduel to come in here and do an interview for the FAWrestling fans. I'm sure it means alot to them... (Sits back in his chair and waits for Saviours response to his flattering remarks.) Saviour: Well... He sits up and brushes his light brown hair back with his hand.) Thanks for the warm welcome. Its truely my pleasure to be sitting in this room to do an interview that will be seen by millions upon millions of fans. Its very exciting to know that many people are watching you, taking the time to hear what you have to say. I'm gratefull that people would do that for me, and I promise them, when they turn on FAW is WAR or any other weekly show, and I'm on there, they will get one hell of a show. (That was one rule Sean Miller, Saviour, lived by... Give the fans something that will stick with them at any cost. He wasn't any high flyer, but if the fans wanted it, look out cause he'd go a flying. If they wanted blood, take your children to bed, cause out comes the weapons, baby! Saviour had a ton of loyal fans in the idependent circut, they loved his lust to be the best and to be the one stand out. And even though his time in his only other international federation with a TV deal was a short one, many of his indy fans followed him where ever he went.) IInterviewer: I can't argue with that. After reviewing your tapes, you seem to be the type of sports entertainer that only comes around every decade. It would be an under statement to say that I'm not excited about your in-ring debut, where ever and whenever that maybe. Okay, on to some more questions, first off, what plans or goals do you have for your run in FAW? Saviour: That's good question actually... Like most guys, I want to be World Champion. Which is pretty general, everyone wants to be the big star that dawns the gold. But to go a bit deeper that that, I want to make an impact. When Saviour's boots are no longer laced up, I still want people to remember my name whenever someone mentions the word "Legend". Quite simply, I want to be a Legend in this game. And come hell or high waters, I will be a legend... Interviewer: As you may or may not know, there are a few big factions, or stables, in FAW that pretty much rule the place just because they have strength in numbers. Alot of the guys who decide to well- piss them off usually end up in a body bag so to speak. One would think it would be wise to associate themselves with one of these groups. Atleast, that's what I think the mart thing to do is. But then again... I'm no pro wrestler, am I? (Factions... Stables... They can sometimes rule over a wrestling federation with an iron fist. No one can stop them, and if you try, it may result it a gang beating or an ambush in the back. Best not to cross them and stay out of their way. The only thing that can stop them, other factions. An endless cycle of a bunch of groups battling over power in the federation. And no one ever wins...) Saviour: Factions, they may think they will push The Saviour around. They may try and intimedate me. But the fact of the matter is this! I will not be some groups b###h! Starting today a new era has arrived, and FAW will finally be 'Saved' by the Saviour. One by one! Piece by bloody peice! Those damn factions will fall to the group at the hand of yours truely! And just like my finishing move... when I'm done... The Show will be Over! I'm finished here...
