Hot Stuff, 1 September 2001

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
:::Hot Stuff Intro The cameras switch on as the pyros explode and the Hot Stuff music plays and the cameras then zoom into the announcers table where Stew Byse and Rob Dale are sitting side by side for a great night. Stew Byse: Tonight is a special night and the first night ever in which a title will be defended at Hot Stuff! We will see Shadow take on JC Ice in tonight's main event, and yes, the Light-Heavyweight Title WILL be defended! Rob Dale: And also tonight we see debuts of Kemosabi and J and a return match for Paranoia! And also here tonight will be Michael Cole interviewing the one, the only, SPIDER! The crowd boo. Stew Byse: Yes, and this night is about to begin. :::Cut To Presidential Lounge The cameras cut backstage to where Bill Clinton is sitting doing some paperwork. In walks Tom The Bomb and the 2 presidents begin to speak. Tom The Bomb: No Bill, you cannot do that! The commissioner will not get fired and if you do there will be hell to pay! Bill Clinton: I don't wanna fire him just you know demote him to a lower division in the federation that WE made what it is! Tom The Bomb: I do not care what you say Bill! It ain't gonna happen, no way, no how! Tom storms out of the room and doesn't look back as Bill sits back and breathes heavily in his chair. :::"Disco Manaic" Gibby Taylor vs Path WINNER: Path :::Commercial Break :::Cut Backstage The cameras cut backstage where Skipster is standing with a microphone backstage talking. Skipster: Now Pyro, I have an offer for you. You go back inside the locker room, stopping at every room begging forgiveness for torment and trash talking and I won't embarass you MUCH. You don't and you will be embarresed to no end by me quickly beating you. And before our match is over... you are going to have to walk across the ring... put you head between you knees... and kiss you own ass! :::J vs Enigma WINNER: Enigma :::Cut Backstage Backstage, JC Ice and Shadow are walking through the corridor and they bump into each other. They look at each other and exchange a look. Shadow then puts his hand out and the 2 men shake hands. Shadow: I will still support your stable but I had to fly solo, no doubt about it! JC Ice: Well tonight, that belt, *he taps it*, is coming to Furious Alliance! Shadow: I'll see you out there! :::Kemosabi vs Paranoia WINNER: Paranoia :::Spider Interview Michael Cole: Thankyou for joining me here tonight at Hot Stuff, Spider, last Thursday night, we saw you advance to the Summer Slaughter World Title match, what was going through your head during the match? Spider: I was convinced that I would get revenge on Mystic X for screwing with my mind about my parents, unluckily I only came out with a draw, right now? I am thinking I'll need to get revenge at the Pay Per View. Michael Cole: Well what were you thinking when your hand was raised in victory? After all, you will be going to Summer Slaughter and challenge for the World Title. Spider: Well how do you think I felt? I was ecstatic! I am about to reach the pinnacle of my career, the point where no-one and I mean no-one could claim I was a jobber! I could finnally prove all my critics wrong, especially certain jealous FAW wrestlers, but I am not going to say any names because that's the kinda' friendly guy I am, wait no I ain't I am an evil SOB! Michael Cole: Well what about Mystic X, what do you want to do to him and what do you think of his chances in the World Titlerace? Spider: I think his chances are about as slim as a supermodel! I am going to tear him into pieces, you don't mess with an already crazed mind just to win a match, that would make you an idiot! X doesn't know what his ass is in for! And I am going to venomise his ass with the Hellians Virus! Michael Cole: This Monday night you will take on Continental Connection member, "The Idol" and Furious Alliance member, JC Ice, what do you think to that match? Will you be able to overcome that test? Spider: You god damn right I will! I have beaten that butt puppet JC Ice many a time, and as for being an idol, his name should be Doli, because he belongs to himself, a little girl who doesn't want to get her ass kicked by the great Spider! They are both members of rival stables so that gives me even more incentive to kick serious ass on Monday! Michael Cole: Well, we look forward to seeing you in action Spider, do you have any final comments? Spider: Yeah I do, people have underestimated me, and now they are viewing the next World champion of FAW! You want some? You can come and get some because I like nothing more than beating your ass 'cause I am the venom! :::Commercial Break :::"Homicidal Madman" Shadow (c) vs JC Ice - Light-Heavyweight Title Match WINNER: Shadow
