FAW Panic, 21 December 2001

21 December 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Gund Arena, Cleveland, Ohio - 20,562 - 07/12/2001 Earlier Today We fade in with two voices being heard on the sound system. ?: Right, ready to put the plan into action? ?2: Yes. Lets go. A door slams as we head back to the arena. --Transmission Starts-- Panic Intro "Never Gonna Stop" by Rob Zombie hits and the transmission begins. The cameras switch on to huge pyros exploding and then the cameras zooming round the arena and focusing in on the ring and then the cameras zoom in on the announce table. Michael McGiggins: Welcome to Panic! I am Michael McGiggins alongside Ryan Stroke and what a night we got tonight. Ryan Stroke: Damn right there Mikey! Michael McGiggins: Do not call me Mikey! Ryan Stroke: Sorry, but tonight's main event sees Bomber take on SpAwN in an I quit match with 2 special guest referees. Michael McGiggins: And we are just 48 hours away from EVIL INTENTIONS! What a night that will be. Ryan Stroke: Damn right. Evil Intentions Main Event Promo "Sex Bomb" hits by Tom Jones and down to the ring walks Lisa. She gets into the ring. Lisa: Welcome to another edition of FAW IS WAR! And, tonight we are going to give you all one hell of a show, but this Sunday there is the pay-per-view. And involved will be a 9 man main event! Now, I just want to assure you all that the rumours of a mystery man interfering are false and the man who will interfere is none other than...no....I will make you all wait. But the man will be coming to FAW, there are no doubts, he was in FAW for a maximum of 3 weeks....he was involved in one pay-per-view and he is now returning to make sure the FAW World Title comes home to FAW again. And tonight I just wanted to tell you all that whoever wins that match on Sunday night should be very proud, I do not care who it is, I have my thoughts on who will win, but I will not share them with you right now. Then suddenly "Faith" hits and down to the ring, and into it, gets British Bomber. British Bomber: Lisa, Lisa, Lisa...why don't we just talk about this a little. You see, you come out here and say that someone is going to bring the title back to FAW and someone is going to beat xCw down and all that crap, but let me see, that will not happen, I don't care what you say, but it won't because I am the FAW Living Legend, and that's the way it is gonna stay, and this Sunday I will take the FAW World Title with me to xCw and I will make sure that your time as FAW owner are over. And by the way Lisa, no matter who you think will win from your precious company...YOU ARE WRONG! "Bullet With Butterfly Wings" then hits and down to the ring walks the FAW World Champion with his World Title belt. He, too, gets into the ring. JLC: xCw...FAW.....XWW.....hell PCW....I do not care who the hell is in the match, how good they are....how crappy they are, because you can all run your mouths on how you will win the match, take the title, drive each and every other person into retirement....because the fact of the matter is that I am the FAW World Champion, I worked for this belt and I do not intend losing it to any one of those other 8 men on Sunday night! Chris Thunder then walks out to the bottom of the ramp without getting into the ring. Chris Thunder: JLC, you say you worked for this title? Well, let me just tell you Jim, I am also a long serving FAW member and I am not going to standby and let you have that title without a fight, I too have been through my phases without FAW, like my XWW venture, which Bomber, failed, as will yours to this federation, mainly because it has me, but I will be fighting for all I am worth and taking that FAW World Title! "New Disease" by Spineshank hits and then down to the halfway part of the ramp walks Spider. He stops and begins to speak. Spider: You know, I disagree with each and EVERY one of you...FAW has never seen a World Champion with dignity and respect for the company...the only man I have seen like that is J..L..C! The only ever World Champion! And now it is time for someone to take over from him, and that man? Well, who else can it be? Spider! So, I suggest you all just step aside and let me into the swing of things while I kick some serious ass this Sunday night... "Disturbed" plays and then out to the left hand side of the ramp walks the xCw World Champion, STUNNING STAN! Stunning Stan: Now seeing as all of you have come out here to plea as to why you are so good, I may aswell be out here and prove to you how damn good I am! Tonight I will be beating your world champion one on one. Tonight I will be showing each and everyone of you other 8 guys that I am good enough to win your World Title! And then, I will get your respect and then you will know that this Sunday that title...IS MINE! Then Skipster walks out to the right hand side of the ramp. Skipster: Maybe it's just me, but none of you are taking into account guys who have been here along time aswell as being top main eventers for nearly their whole time...and who could that be? Oh yes...ME! So, on Sunday night I will show you why when I take that World Title from you! But, until then you get your little moments to think you will win. Then suddenly "The Way I Am" hits and out to the middle of the top of the ramp walks SpAwN. SpAwN: You know, it makes me laugh listening to all of you talk about how great you are. I have the advantage, I do not need to prove it, you all know that I am damn good, the World Champion for months, the hellmaster, you all cower in fear of me, and tonight Bomber, you are mine, I Will take yo ass out and make you scream 3 small words. But until then, y'all should have a nice day! "The Way I Am" hits and SpAwN leaves to the backstage area. The other 6 men stay stood in position as then Bomber gets out of the ring and starts to make his way up the ramp to leave and Spider pounces on him from behind as the crowd go wild with joy for Spider, who continues to pound on Bomber as Stan starts to make his way to save his boss, but Skipster attacks him to the crowds delight and then JLC gets out of the ring and starts to beat on Thunder to the crowds delight as the cameras fade out to backstage. Cut To Corridor SpAwN: What did I say? They are all a bunch of jackasses....! Jamie Shock vs Alex Storm WINNER: Jamie Shock Commercial Break Shifty vs The DT Down to the ring walks VP Ben Preston. VP Ben Preston: Just so that y'all know, this match will not be taking place due to neither man showing up, thanks. Ben Preston leaves. Cut To British Bomber's Locker Room The cameras cut to British Bomber's locker room. Bomber is sitting on the phone and the man on the other side can just be made out but his voice cannot be recognised. British Bomber: Hey man. We gotta speak about Sunday, how will it all work out? Unknown: Look, we will have to meet up, I cannot do it now, I am on the way into the arena, I will meet you tonight in my limosene after Deas' match and we will discuss things. No worries, we will sort it out. British Bomber: Look, I want that World Title and you know what I have promised you if I get it, don't you? Unknown: Your stock in xCw is on it's way to me! British Bomber: No, joint stock. Unknown: I apologise, that is it. Meet me in 2 matches Bomber. British Bomber: Yes sir. The scene fades out. Chris Thunder vs British Bomber WINNER: Chris Thunder The match was a physical contest with Thunder eventually coming through it the winner. Skipster vs BBD (xCw) Deas' music hits hits and down to the ring walks BBD. He gets into the ring and then awaits his opponent's entrance! Ryan Stroke: The man who wants respect, is it just me or do ALL the xCw guys want respect? Michael McGiggins: Yep, and that is coz they SUCK! Ryan Stroke: Well said Mikey. Michael McGiggins: DO NOT call me Mikey.........please! Skipster then runs down to the ring and jumps under the bottom rope and goes straight to work on BBD. BBD falls to the ground from the beating and Skipster then starts to laugh, he turns his back and then BBD takes out Skipster's knee from behind. Ryan Stroke: Ouch. Not good for Skip there. Michael McGiggins: He will beat this guy though. BBD then gets up and whips Skipster and delivers a samoan drop when he bounces back off the ropes, and pins Skipster. Pin: 1...KICK OUT! Ryan Stroke: There we go, only got a 1. Skipster gets up slowly and BBD kicks his face back down to the ground before he can get up and then Skipster rolls underneath the bottom rope and walks right round the ring and then rolls back into the ring and starts to deliver some right hands to BBD. He then delivers a huge powerbomb which goes into a pin. Pin: 1...2...KICK OUT! BBD then gets up slowly and starts to exchange blows with the man who will main event this Sunday and he then ducks a punch from Skipster and then Skipster ducks a big boot and delivers a huge piledriver to BBD! Pin: 1......2.....3! Ryan Stroke: FAW prevail! Michael McGiggins: As they should, now we should be seeing what is going on with Bomber and that mysterious man in the limo remember... Cut To Parking Lot The cameras cut to the parking lot and a limosene is in the shot and into the parking lot walks British Bomber, the limo door flies open and a black-gloved hand can be made out, British Bomber gets into the limo and the door closes. Ryan Stroke: Who the hell is that? Michael McGiggins: I don't know, but if they work everything out then we will find out at Evil Intentions! Ryan Stroke: Damn right and that man will be joint owner of xCw!!!! Michael McGiggins: And we will be right back after this... Commercial Break Huh? The scene opens up after the commercial to yet another backstage scene where we join a slightly flustered Britney, the very esteemed partner of "Stunning Stan". Having endured the past few days vicous rumours the focus was now finally back on her husband as he prepared for his match which was to follow tonight. Suddenly though she is stopped in her tracks by the rapid movement of a courier who is seemingly carrying a package for the young diva. Taking the envelope from the young mans hand she opens it up and slides the very expensive looking cartridge paper from its current home and unfolds it. As she does though we see a swift change in facial expression as the smile which was previously present turns to a look of horrer. Tossing the letter to the ground we then see Britney dissapear off into the distance in a fit of tears; whilst in the foreground the FAW camera zooms in on the letter which lays on the floor. In almost caligraphy like handwriting lays a small poem which has seemingly once more been addressed by the same man that performed the stunt on XCW Smash... Roses are red Violets are blue Stan you’re a wannabe me Cos Britney you know I'm da stunning one for you Britney I often look at you standing there tender and fair. Considering just how you'd react to me running my fingers through your hair The time will soon come when Stan will lie dead and buried. And you will walk past without a single care. Everything's relative in this life we lead. Up till now you’ve had no reason to leave. But times are changing and your surroundings too. And on my arrival it will be clear I am the one for you. With that the camera fades to black and we return to our previous scene..... The Omega Fro vs Baz "Now You Know" by Powderburn hits and down to the ring walks the one, the only, The Omega Fro! He gets into the ring. Ryan Stroke: This fan favourite is going to be facing Richard Storm this Sunday in what will be a great match. Michael McGiggins: Before we start on that what just happened? Ryan Stroke: Some dodgy stuff, that is all I know! Michael McGiggins: Well it is xCw apparently, so therefore we will excuse it! Then Baz walks down to the ring and gets into the ring. Ryan Stroke: And the man who will fight Morte this Sunday for the European Title! Michael McGiggins: And it is Baz's first proper shot at FAW gold. Ryan Stroke: and be assured he will try to take advantage of it. The bell rings as Baz gets the upper-hand on The Omega Fro, he gives him repeated right hands and then whips him to the rope and then as Fro runs back off the rope Baz throws him over the top rope. Baz follows him out through the middle rope and throws Fro into the steel steps. He rolls Fro beck into the ring and covers him. Pin: 1...2.KICK OUT! Fro then starts to crawl to the rope after the huge hit from Baz and Baz starts to kick Fro repeatedly in the gut. He then stops and smiles and then picks up Fro and goes for a piledriver but fails that and Fro flips him over his back and quickly covers Baz. Pin: 1...2..KICK OUT! Ryan Stroke: Maybe it's just me but I am a little nervous about the rumours of the interference Michael. Michael McGiggins: You are not the only one coz I am concerned too! Ryan Stroke: But I am looking forward to the main event itself! And the whole night. Michael McGiggins: Hell yes. Baz then starts to get up and Fro clotheslines him down and delivers an elbow drop. He covers again. Pin: 1..KICK OUT! Baz then gets up and Fro kicks him in the gut and picks up Baz and then slams him down for the ARMAGEDDON! He covers Baz. Pin: 1... Michael McGiggins: 2..3! Ryan Stroke: I don't think there is any doubt Mike. Pin: 2.... Ryan Stroke: Here we go, win number 1 for Fro! Number 2 this Sunday maybe. Pin: 3! Cut To Parking Lot The limo door flies open once again and out of it gets Bomber, he shakes the gloved hand and then walks the other way and smiles as the limo door shuts and the limo pulls off. Ryan Stroke: I am still waiting to find out who the hell is in there! Michael McGiggins: Well I cannot wait for this Sunday, 48 hours to go! JLC vs Stunning Stan (xCw) Ryan Stroke: Here we are. Michael McGiggins: We have yet another match between these two young dazzling champions. Ryan Stroke: This is part 2 of the battle. Michael McGiggins: I think Stan could grab the victory. Ryan Stroke: He was unlucky last week and this could be his lucky night. Michael McGiggins: Remember both these men are In the main event at Evil Intentions. Ryan Stroke: Oh Hell Yeah. Michael McGiggins: So it should be good to see the resulting match. Ryan Stroke: Remember, that’s Evil Intentions, LIVE this Sunday 23rd December. Michael McGiggins: And christmas may just come early for the person who wins the main event, Ryan Stroke: Yes, that’s right, EI is just two days before the anniversary of Jesus’s birth, also known as Christmas. Michael McGiggins: And Ryan is looking forward to receiving his barbie doll. Ryan Stroke: GRRR… Michael McGiggins: Anyway, lets go beyond Xmas to this battle. Ryan Stroke: JLC will be entering first. ‘Bullet with butterfly wings’ by Smashing Pumpkins plays over the cheers of the crowd as the FAW World Heavyweight Champion Jim The Loo Cleaner, JLC, walks out to an incredibly standing ovation. Michael McGiggins: It will be his one year anniversary here soon. He walks out with pyros going off here and there and raises his hands as the cheers get louder. Ryan Stroke: This man lost to Spider recently. Michael McGiggins: That was a fluke. He has regained his form. Trust me. He gets into the ring and shakes hands with the ever professional Louis Savva, the referee for the encounter. Ryan Stroke: Now we wait for Stan. ‘Disturbed’ plays. The arena goes dark. Then suddenly we hear a gang of bangs which come after each other and Stunning Stan walks out to a chorus of boo’s. He gets into the ring and stands nose to nose with JLC before the referee separates them and sends them into different corners. Michael McGiggins: Remember on xCw Smash Stans wife, Britney, received a love letter. It is thought she may be cheating on him. Ryan Stroke: That’s interesting to know, I didn’t see Smash. Michael McGiggins: Why? Ryan Stroke: I only watch Quality television. Michael McGiggins: Ur…yeah..same here, just a one off. The referee rings the bell as they go at it exclusively on Next Dimension TV. Stan beats on JLC with punches and kicks and sends him into the turnbuckle. He runs at him and slumps him down. He then kicks him in the nuts and grabs his legs setting up a move similar to the walls of jericho. JLC grabs onto the ropes as they scatter across the ring. Ryan Stroke: Already an enticing match. JLC clotheslines Stan down to the ground. He grabs him and hits a backbreaker. He then continues the violent assault on Stunning Stan by choking him on the ropes. Michael McGiggins: Well Done @ JLC. JLC loses the advantage he has so far gained when Stan irish whips him into the ropes and then gives him a lariat. Stan stands JLC up and tries to give him a piledriver. JLC jumps down and then powerslams Stan. JLC covers a now ‘power-less’ Stan. Pin: 1…2…. Ryan Stroke: JLC should have guessed he wouldn’t have won it from that. Michael McGiggins: I think he did this before, he should learn from his mistakes. But maybe it was worth a go. Ryan Stroke: Yeah…maybe. JLC pulls Stan towards him and gets him in a piledriver position. He then stands up and lifts him above his head nailing a spectacular powerbomb. JLC then kicks Stan over into the centre of the ring where he elbow drops him. Michael McGiggins: Weak move. JLC then quickly rushes for the cover. Pin: 1…2….KICK OUT! Ryan Stroke: Nice from Stan, but I still hate the jackass! Stan then quickly gets up and ducks under a clotheslines and rolls up the FAW champion, JLC... Pin: 1..2......3! Ryan Stroke: He has done it! Michael McGiggins: We go backstage now. Cut To Corridor A man is tip toeing around the corridor. He turns a corner slowly with a creak beneath his feet. Then Lisa Da Sex Bomb comes up from behind, equally as slowly and points at the man. Police run at him and remove his bandana. It is the cleaner, Rob. Commercial Break Cut To Parking Lot Lee Smith stands in the centre of the carpark with the same bandana Rob was wearing. LS: Looking for someone Lisa? Thought so. You see, on FIW you declared that neither me or tom would be allowed within miles of you, or we were to be arrested. So, Lisa, just bring it. Lee waits for 30 seconds as the crowd chant his name. Ryan Stroke: This is ridiculous. Michael McGiggins: He wants to be arrested? Police officers come out from all sides as Lee stands still. They handcuff him and place him in the car. Police Officer: Sir, its time to take you to the station. They lock the car and drive off. Ryan Stroke: It would seem so. But….why? Lisa then runs out and sees the car drive away. She jumps for joy. Michael McGiggins: There is something weird about this….. Cut To Lisa's Office We come in slowly with the door closing shut. It is dark. Suddenly the camera man is pushed into the centre of the room. We hear footsteps as someone escapes on foot. The door slams shut as the tilted view of the room from the camera can be seen. We see some of the cameramans blood on the screen but we can also see all of Lisa’s office, which has now been well and truly destroyed. Cut To Announce Table Ryan Stroke: I think I know whats going on! Michael McGiggins: What? Ryan Stroke: Isn’t it obvious? Michael McGiggins: NO! Ryan Stroke: Lee wanted to get arrested so that he could lure Lisa out of the building so that- Tom could sneak in and get into Lisa’s office, then destroying it. Michael McGiggins: It all makes sense now. Ryan Stroke: And Tom has probably gone now, so that he will not get arrested because they have absolutely no evidence against him. Michael McGiggins: A clever plan. SpAwN vs British Bomber - I Quit Match - Ben Preston and JLC Referees Michael McGiggins : Welcome back to Panic and the main event, a big matchup between SpaWn and British Bomber, booked by Vice President Ben Preston who is also this match's special referee! Ryan Stroke : Not forgetting JLC as Special referee also and the fact that this match will be I quit rules! Michael McGiggins : I am getting word that our 1st referee is ready to come out here! Playing God by Spineshank play's on the FuryTron as FAW Vice President Ben Preston walks out onto the rampway receiving a very mixed reaction, he strolls down to the ring Adam Hutton : Ladies and gentlemen, making his way to the ring in this 2 referee I quit matchup, the 1st referee and Furiously Addictive Wrestling Vice President... BEN PRESTON! Preston rolls into the ring with his shirt on and grabs a microphone Ben Preston : Well boys and girls, here is your main event for the evening, and I must introduce my replacement as referee, I thought, do the fans really want me here? So, in his syndicated hating of me, I introduce.... SPIDER! Spineshanks New Disease plays around the ring and Spider does indeed walk out wearing a referee's shirt! Ryan Stroke : What the hell?! I thought these 2 men hated one another! Spider keeps walking as Preston get's out of the ring, they exchange friendly smiles, Spider doesn't get in the ring but instead walks over to Preston, he offers his hand and Preston slowly goes to shake it, Spider then kicks him in the gut and delivers a massive Hook kick! Michael McGiggins : They still do Ryan... they still do! As Spider celebrates Bullet with Butterfly plays and the 2nd referee for the night, or 3rd depending on opinion, JLC walks out, Spider climbs in the ring and JLC also does, they exchange a strange friendly/rival glare, they talk to each other for a second and both nod as SpaWn comes out with a microphone SpaWn : Hold up, are you telling me, the Hell master must also have 2 bozo's against him? As SpaWn stops talking British Bomber runs out and Bulldogs him! Spider calls for the bell to start the match, the 2 referee's walk over to the 2 men still suspicious of one another. Ryan Stroke : This should be one hell of a match! Bomber jumps on top of SpaWn locking on a half Camel clutch, he uses his free hand to grab a microphone Bomber : Go on crap master, say those 2 words! SpaWn : Never! Bomber laughs and let's go he gives the mic to JLC and drags SpaWn down to the lower ramp and sets up a table, SpaWn slowly get's to his feet and Bomber lifts him with a powerbomb but SpaWn reverses with a DDT through the table! Michael McGiggins : SpaWn with a shocking reversal! SpaWn slowly stands and stomps on Bomber, he climbs back onto the ramp and walks to the ring, Spider checks on Bomber as JLC follows SpaWn, Spider kicks Bomber in the gut! Ryan Stroke : What the?! Is this some kind of XCW beating?! Spider smiles deeply as fans seem to cheer him beating the XCW owner, Bomber stands and Spider looks innocent, Bomber just walks to the ring with SpaWn shouting to bring it on. Bomber rolls into the ring as does Spider after him, Bomber and SpaWn get into a punching battle as Spider stares at JLC who does not notice, sudenlly from nowhere Spider runs at JLC who dodges, Spider stops and they get into a brawl of their own! Michael McGiggins : What the hell is Spider doing? Who's side is he on? Wait, I just thought, what about the rumours of Spider an... Ryan Stroke : THE OMENS! Spider finally uppercuts JLC and pushes him out of the ring, he bounds off the ropes and clotheslines Bomber and SpaWn! Michael McGiggins : So much for that idea! Spider stands tall in the ring as XCW stars come flying down that are in the main event at Evil Intentions, Spider smiles and stays in the ring attempting to fight off the stars but eventually losing the fight until some FAW members come down also in the Evil Intentions main event! Ryan Stroke : What the hell?! Sudenlly Ben Preston stands from the floor as does JLC, FAW members battle back against XCW until 3 men are left, JLC, Bomber and SpaWn, Bomber and SpaWn battle as JLC watches, Spider rolls into the ring bleeding from the head, he stares at JLC and walks over, he puts out his hand Michael McGiggins : THE HELL?! DON'T DO IT JLC! JLC put's out his hand and shakes with Spider, they carry on refereeing as Bomber wins the battle and smashes SpaWn with a European Uppercut, he then snapmares him and locks on an Armbar! He grabs the microphone JLC had Bomber : C'Mon! I knew you'd get FAW idiots down here! Now say those words! At the word idiots JLC and Spider look at each other, they stomp on Bomber and JLC picks him up and delivers a Loo-Plex! Spider climbs the turnbuckle with everyone cheering him on, he points JLC to SpaWn, JLC put's the 2 opponents next to each other and Spider Venom's them both! Michael McGiggins : Huh? I am really confused now, what is this, an unholy alliance again?! Spider grabs a microphone as does JLC JLC : No we ain't back together, but one thing is damned for sure, no-one will EVER, beat FAW's greatest 2 champions! Spider : God... Damn... Both and crowd : RIGHT! Ben Preston rolls into the ring and grabs both their microphones, he talks into one Ben Preston : The winner of this matchup? The winner is nobody, beside FAW, you'll just have to wait and see who is the best of them all... at Evil... Intentions... Then suddenly into the ring gets SpAwN after the VENOM and he knocks down the Vice-President of FAW and then he locks in the third degree burn to Bomber. BOMBER SCREAMS I QUIT AS THE RING ANNOUNCER HEARS HIM AND RINGS THE BELL. SpAwN grabs a microphone. SpAwN: Do not mess with the best..... SpAwN then leaves the ring and walks up the ramp with JLC trash talking him and Spider standing behind looking at the fallen Ben Preston and the rest of the men down on the ground as the crowd do not know what to think and the show ends.... --Transmission Ends--
