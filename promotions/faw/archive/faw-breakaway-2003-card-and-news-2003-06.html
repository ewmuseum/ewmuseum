FAW Breakaway 2003 Card and News, June 2003

Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Sunday, June 01, 2003 Breakaway PPV - @ 11:27 PST Unfortunately, we have to announce that todays PPV will be pushed back to Wednesday night. This past week we have installed the backstage script and perhaps upon reflection we chose the wrong time to make such a radical change to the infrastructure of FAW. It has come to the staffs attention that alot of people have had problems accessing this backstage area and due to Jamie having computer troubles this past week he has been unable to correct the issues in a manner which would allow everyone to have a fair chance going into this major event in terms of their ability to RP. After much thought we have decided to postpone the event allowing everyone to have until TUESDAY MIDNIGHT GMT (6pm CST) to roleplay for an event which could well shape the coming months here in FAW since so much is at stake. In another announcement, FAW has taken the decision to put the FAW Continental Title on the line in the forthcoming Turmoil Match scheduled for the PPV. This decision means the likes of Sniper, Costella, Bomber, Juvian and The President of the Altered States now have the chance to capture one of FAW's most prestigous honours. Once again apoligies for the inconvenience, but I hope you can appreciate that FAW prides itself on being fair to all and doing anything but postponing this event would hinder the chances of some through no fault of their own. Saturday, May 31, 2003 Warning For Non-Rpers In Tag Title Match - @ 15:52 PST I do notice a lack of rps from those featuring in the tag team title match. I don't like complaining, but I will make it clear now that any team which has no activity in terms of RP's will be withdrawn from the contest (unless extreme personal circumstances are made aware to the staff). Its a huge opportunity for alot of newcomers, so don't just let the opportunity pass by nor take it for granted. Friday, May 30, 2003 Forums......... - @ 22:23 PST Just a quick note, I have fixed the forums as of now (the time is now 3:22am GMT so Jamie you BETTER thank me) I spent most the night on them trying to fix the bastards and finally they are done. Backstage Area - @ 10:07 PST If anyone is having problems accessing the backstage area or has not received their password other than (D and Sniper) I suggest you make Jamie aware at the following email address: [detail withheld] I have passed on information regarding those two isolated cases, but from now on it would be more efficient to email Jamie because the CGI script is his domain. Wednesday, May 28, 2003 view new roleplays at - @ 13:40 PST post all rps in backstage section from now on plz.
