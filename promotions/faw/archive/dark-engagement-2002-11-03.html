Dark engagement

3 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Dark engagement Date: 3 Nov 2002 Show: Distortion Floyd Rotterdamn: But wait... Whats happening now? The match is over but Crimson seems to be beckoning to the back... Mario CirallI: Oh boy... Shane Riot: Here comes The Dark Nation... Chimera and Scott Costella come running down the aisle, with chairs, wearing 'Dark Nation' shirts... They slide into the ring and as Damien is rising to his feet, bring the chairs crashing down on him, at the same time. Floyd Rotterdamn: Oh my god! Shane Riot: Brutal... The woman in purple gets into the ring and brings Crimson his Internation aswell as a mic. He raises it to his mouth. The crowd is booing loudly and quickly turn to 'Nation Sucks' chants. The chants continue, getting louder and louder until Crimson speaks out, in a loud voice, Crimson Lord: Damien... You were a good fight, tonight... But let me introduce you and everyone else here, tonight, to someone very special to me... My bride to be... My Lady Jade! The crowd are obviously stunned by this shock announcement. They stop the chants, whilst most remain silent, others carry on a low hiss of boos. Floyd Rotterdamn: What? Have I heard right? Is he going to marry her? Shane Riot: You bet your ass... And a good bride she will be... oooh boy Crimson Lord: Thats right... Me and Jade will tie the knot, in 3 short weeks at Deceit! Crimson Lord and Jade hug and kiss as Chimera and Scott Costella lay into Damien Ryan, with the steel chairs. The crowd begins to boo loudly again. As Crimson and Jade stop kissing, Crimson tells Chimera and Scott to stop the beat down and they retreat back up the ramp. Crimson seems very infactuated with Jade, as they walk up the ramp, paying all his attention to her.
