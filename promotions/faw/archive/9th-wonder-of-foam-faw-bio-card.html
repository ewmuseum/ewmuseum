Real Name: [name withheld] Birthdate: 20/04/71 Hometown: The Foam Factory Height: 6'7 Nicknames: Foamy One, Mr.Foam, Hulk FoamGun Status: Face Current Teams: FoamWorldOrder Entrance Music: 'Rengades Of Funk' - Rage Against The Machine Wrestling Style: Brawler/Technical Signature Moves: EvenFoamDDT, Foamybomb, FoamySault Other Federations: EWFx2, CWR, CWF, TSWF, SHWF, SHWC, UEF, PWA, 720 Wrestling, OAWF, WFW, ICW, UWO Other Federation Awards: TSWF Tag champs (2), SHWF Tag Champs (2), UWO Tag Champs (1), OAWF Tag Champs (1), PWA Tag Champs (1), OAWF European Champ (1), PWA Hardcore Champion (1), SHWC World Champion (1), UEF World Champion (1) Handler Information Handler Name: [name withheld] MSN Name: [detail withheld] Bio In the 18th Century, in darkest Mexico a group of monks created a super-human. His name? The Wonder Of Foam. Created from a 137% batch of tequila, the dying body of an American monkey handler and an antique Roman 'sponge on a stick' arse wiper.The Wonder Of Foam defended Mexico from the Welsh, whose invasion of 1834 failed because their army got called up for Rugby tour of Australia.Soon America tried their luck, even with his awesome strength he was not match for the USA's army. Soon his monks decided that he needed a weapon. So they created a comedy Foamy finger. One touch and it's victim would be mortally wounded. Dubbed 'The Foamy Finger Of EVIL!' It fended off the USA's army with The Wonder Of Foam using it.Sadly The Wonder Of Foam got into an eating competition with Lord Jif, from the Lemon planet. The Wonder Of Foam had severe constipation, and on his deathbed he had a vision.'i see...i see a number, nine, the number is Nine! It is emblazoned in the hearts and minds of millions of people. He is their saviour. But he is growing weak. He has used the Foamy Finger Of Evil to ward off the EVIL's of his world. But he is soon to leave that world. But there is another. I see another number....a Ten, yes a TEN. He has come with the 9th one's blessing, he carries with him the Foamy Finger of EVIL! He will be remembered for even longer and by more people than the 9th. He will become the king of his world. With him is a helper. I see, a dressing gown, and a smell of Danish proportions. Oh...my ass!!!'Then The Wonder Of Foam died. But The Wonder Of Foam had a child in 1859 the 2nd Wonder Of Foam was born, he had a son, and so on. Until 1905 when the 4th Wonder Of Foam decided there was no need for a crime fighter. So he took up wrestling, with Fat Pat he became world champion. He retired in 1910, handing down his mantle to the 5th Wonder Of Foam, sadly he took the Foamy Finger of EVIL on a boat trip in April of 1912.The 5th Wonder Of Foam touched the deck of a boat called the Titanic, it sank and the 5th Wonder with it. History records that accident as an iceberg collision.In 1918 a 17-year old boy, whose father died on the titanic decided to become a wrestler. He calld himself the 6th Wonder Of Foam, as his father had before him. Sadly he went to France to fight the Germans. The Foamy finger of EVIL got sent to Austrailia by accident, after miss-reading the Japanese instructions on his hand grenade he was blown to pieces. Is last, cryptic dying words...'Mitigating Noodles'In 1929 the 6th Wonder's brother decided to take up wrestling to do what his older brother had always wanted to do. A devoute Jew, he mistakenly went on a Jewish power march to the Reichstag in Germany. He never returned.But in 1939 his 19 year old nephew found his wrestling gear. He wrestled for three years as the 7th Wonder Of Foam, he got married and had a girl and a boy. Sadly he died in 1945 in WW2 during operation 'certain death'. His dying numbers were '30th, 2001'. Since WW2 the Foamy Finger of EVIL has been kept in a secure location to stop evil tyrants such as Rupert Murdoch from getting his hands on it and achieving certain world domination & tyrany.His son turned 16 in 1955 and soon became the youngest wonder of Foam. For 5 years he was known as the Foam Wonder of Eigth. In 1960 his dyslexia was discovered and he became known as the 8th Wonder Of Foam. In 1971 he gave birth to his second child, a boy. He followed in his fathers footsteps. And in 1989 he set up a Foam Factory to supplement his wrestling career. In 1998 the 9th Wonder Of Foam hit the big leagues with CWF an American federation.By 1999 he had joined TSWF, soon he became tag team champion with his friend Neo. Since then he has joined countless federations, taking part in backstage activities, even opening up his own fed 'World Foam Wrestling'. On October 19th 2001 he decided to unleash the awesome power of the Foamy Finger of EVIL! on the world, in order to achieve his dream of becoming world Champion and he did, becoming the first ever UEF World Champion. (He did win it in January 2000 with SHWC but it shut the day after) Then he retired after achieving his dream. He appeared in shwf, tswf, oawf, ewf, ewf (a different one), cwf, uwo, uef, cwr and World Foam Wrestling. Over his career he won eight tag team titles (once with Invisible Jim) one European title, one hardcore title and two World Titles.His younger nephew the 10th Wonder Of Foam soon took over the Foamy reigns of madness. When his first fed 720 Wrestling shut down the 10th Wonder decided to travel the world Cheech & Chong style for a couple of months in search of the Rowntree.So now the 9th Wonder Of Foam has come back out of his early retirement in order to make sure that Foam continues to rule the world of wrestling beginning with FAW Roleplays Archives One line is better than none I went nuts on a pencil dammit!
