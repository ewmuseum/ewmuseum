Why FAW (2001)

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
WHY FAW - Unlike all of the other efeds out there on the internet, FAW, like stated in the about section, is an optimized mix of a booked and nonbooked chemistry. It actually works a bit like a real wrestling organization. - The Press Conference is the battlegrounds where you do your special "roleplaying".. This is where you have interviews, backstage talking, videos playing, etc.. The Press Conference is so unique in that, unlike other efeds, all roleplays are on one page. No more clicking the subject to read a roleplay. Also, to save you some time, you do not have to type in your wrestler's name. It is all provided in boxes, you just choose what fits your wrestler out of the box and then make that roleplay. - Also, The Press Conference is HTML friendly.. Now, if you don't know no HTML, thats fine, just roleplay normally, because FAW is also member friendly and can understand that some people are not going to be 'Net "gurus".. - As for the style of results. We will use different styles as we go along.. Video, SHORT text, LONG text, and of course text summary... Right now we are using the shorthand version where each match is just a few lines and interviews are just summarized.. It seems to be the popular choice by members, and until members choose differently, we will probably stick with it.. (FAW is very interactive and member friendly) - ICQ and MSN Messenger are another FAW advantage.. What I mean is.. Unlike most of the other "Presidents" I WANT you to add me to your list so we can discuss your wrestler's development. Remember, FAW is only as successful as the members make it, and character evolvement makes that wrestler special... - There are two types of efeds on the net, as mentioned on the about page.. and of course, we are a good mixture of both.. While some go by roleplays and the other go randomly for winners of matches.. We go for the best roleplayer to win, but, sometimes in order to make the fued even deeper, I may talk the owner of the wrestler into jobbing via a screwover, and maybe pushing the match into a ppv match.. - Also, the amount of time you have been in FAW helps you too. We always appreciate long term members that roleplay good, and it shows. In other words, if someone joined 3 days ago and did a good roleplay everyday to get a match on FEAR against the World Champion and the World Champion has done maybe only one roleplay.. Then chances are, it will be a nontitle match... You have to work your way up into the rankings.. You can't jump from number 20 to number 4... A bit of realism I guess...
