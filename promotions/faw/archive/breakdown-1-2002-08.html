FAW Breakdown #1, August 2002

August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Opening Montage "Girl All The Bad Guys Want" by Bowling For Soup plays as the first ever Breakdown hits the air, a video package plays showing all the great over the past 20 months of FAW, from Sean Mason, to HHK, to JLC, to The British Bomber, to Chris Thunder then finally to Tony Vigilante. We cut to the announcers table which is up on the stage, Floyd brings us in. Floyd Rotterdam: Hello folks! Welcome to the first ever Breakdown! We have a action packed card for you tonight, including JJ Dynasty facing X-Rodd, we also have Cyrus Skar out here tonight as well as the main event! Chris Anstex squaring off against Joe La Fontaine! Shane Riot: An..... James Harris is backstage when Bomber walks past, Harris stops and chases after Bomber. James Harris: BOMBER! Bomber stops and turns round. James Harris: Bomber, tomorrow you go u....... Bomber snatches the mic off Harris and shoves him out the way, Bomber pulls the camera closer and looks into it. British Bomber: James shut up and go back to licking monkeys anus'! Furiously Addictive Wrestling, Distortion is tomorrow, everyone will be on the edge of their seat! Why? Not because Dark Generation is going to go out there and whoop the asses of Spawn, HaHaKlown and JLC.....NO! Its because The British Bomber has a huge surprise for the people, a huge surprise for the fans! Bomber throws the mic to Harris then continues walking off as we go to the announcers table. Sabatash vs Damien Ryan Both men looked at each other before tying up. They tied up with Sab sending Damien off the ropes. Damien went off the ropes and Sab dropkicked him. Sab went for a pin. 1 2 kick out. Damien got up and Sab hip-tossed him. Damien got up and Sab hip-tossed him again. Damien got up a third time and once again got hip-tossed. Damien got up and Sab dropkicked him. 1 2 kick out. Damien gets up and kicks Sab. He then DDT’s Sab and pins him. 1 2 kick out. Damien picks up Sab and neck breakers him. 1 2 shoulder up. Damien tries to pick up Sab but it gets turned into an inside cradle. 1 2 kick out. Both men get up and they both close line each other. Sab gets up first and then Damien gets up. Sab punches Damien three times before Damien falls. Damien gets up and gets close-lined back down. Damien gets up and gets kicked and then gets a DDT. Sab pins him. 1 2 kick out. JLC goes through the crowd while HHK and Spawn make their way down the ramp. The three go into the ring and JLC hits Sab while Spawn and HHK double-team Damien. Spawn goes for his Last Kill on Damien while JLC goes for The Angel's Descend. HHK watches the damage being down. DCV’s music hits the PA. Highlights Package: Panic #10 We cut back to Panic, showing Bomber challenging JLC, and him accepting with the World title on the line. We then go to Bomber being attacked by a masked man. We also see JJ Dynasty make his debut only to be attacked by fellow former CCW'er Cyrus Skar. We look on at all the matches that took place, Xander retaining his International title, Neo defeating Chris Matten but we are interupted by X-Rodd's music as we go to our next match. X-Rodd vs J.J Dynasty X-Rodd keeps up the offense by gettting J.J up and slaming his head against the turnbuckle. J.J falls to one knee after a few shots, but X-Rodd grabs him from behind and plants him with a nice Release German Suplex. X-Rodd then goes for the cover...... 1...... 2......kick out by J.J, as the fans cheer X-Rodd on the outside. X-Rodd then gets J.J up and places him in a sitting position on the top turnbuckle. Rodd then climbs up to met him for a Superplex. Instead of falling X-Rodd lands on his shoulder hurting it, he rolls outside onto the apron. J.J takes advantage and flies off with a Leg Drop to the back of X-Rodd's head. J.J lands on his feet on the outside as Rodd's head smacks hard against the ring apron, then he falls all the way to the floor landing hard on his back.Dynasty congrats himslef for a few seconds then rolls X-Rodd back into the ring and makes a cover...... 1...... 2......shoulder up by X-Rodd. J.J looks a bit shocked, but keeps on the offensive. He gets X-Rodd up and nails him with some stiff european uppercuts that get X-Rodd to fall into the corner. J.J then tries to irish whip durst into the opposite corner, but Rodd reverses it and throws J.J. Dynasty however hangs on and comes back with a Back Elbow to Rodd's jaw. He then lifts X-Rodd up for the Belly To Back Suplex, but it is reversed nicely into Monster Bulldog by X-Rodd. He covers...... 1...... 2....... ......kick out! J.J is back up and hits X-Rodd with a low blow. J.J keeps up the momentum and quickly gets X-Rodd to his feet. J.J then takes a few steps backwards and goes for the Superkick, but X-Rodd reads it and catches J.J by the leg then hooks his head and flips him over with a Fisherman's Suplex, he rolls out though. Picks up J.J Dynasty and then drives him down the the X-Driver......1 ......2 ......3!!!! Introducing Cyrus Skar Cyrus Skar is stood backstage with the European title over his shoulder, James Harris is stood next to him. James: Cyrus you wanted to speak. Cyrus Skar: Europe is a waste of space! James: Is that all? Cyrus Skar: No it isnt, I had a word with the top guys about the European title and I told them I was not proud to be European champion, so I requested something and they accepted. At that point and man walks up and takes the European title throwing it to one side, the man presents Cyrus Skar with a new title, one not seen before in FAW. Cyrus Skar: From this day forward I am the pround US Champion! James: You got it changed? Cyrus Skar: Yes I did, I wanted to be proud and Tom wanted me to be a proud champion so he dumped the European title and got me a US title! Cyrus walks off shining his new US title. Su Estupendo Heroé and Sensei Yogahashi vs Mass Murderers ~ The match started off at a lacklustre standard. The Mass Murderers getting the edge over Su Estupendo Héroe & Sensei Yogahashi. It was only until an explosive set of double team moves knocked the Mass Murderers off course. Sensei Yogahashi hitting a super kick on Nightmare King. The thud of the kick could be heard outside the arena, the fans cowered in a quench. Nightmare King managed to hit a low blow that put Sensei Yogahashi in a world full of pain. Nightmare King tagged out and in came Black Ghost. Black Ghost solemnly was embraced by the crowd who hoisted their signs into the air. Black Ghost began to work on the arm of the Sensei Yogahashi. Good use of the ropes for leverage as he planted Sensei in a abdominal stretch. The referee didn't notice the illegality in the hold, but later realized after the fans chanted to him. Black Ghost hit a succession of chops before dropkicking Sensei Yogahashi to the mat. Black Ghost hooked Sensei's leg as the referee dropped to the floor to administrate the count. A two count was all Black Ghost was awarded. Sensei managed to struggle towards his corner as Black Ghost was attending to the referee. With Black Ghost's back turned, Su Estupendo rolled him up, but only got a one count. Black Ghost got back up to his feet, grabbed a hold of Su Estupendo and threw him straight into the turnbuckles. Black Ghost tagged in Nightmare King and the two began to hit on Su Estupendo in the corner. The referee couldn't stop Sensei Yogashi coming into the ring, but as he came towards The Mass Murderers he was greeted with a commendably well worked hip toss. Nightmare King and Black Ghost began to double team Sensei and Su Estupendo until the referee ordered one of them leave. It was at this point that Nightmare King grabbed a hold of Su Estupendo and hit a brainbuster. Nightmare King made the cover and the referee dropped to the canvas to make the count. 1...2...3... and this one was all over. The Mass Murderers impressive in their feat of Sensei Yogahashi and Su Estupendo Hero.~ Chris Anstex vs Joe La Fontaine ~The match started off at a slow pace with La Fontaine getting the upper hand. Joe hits a huge brainbuster on Anstex and went for the pin but only got a two count, Joe picks Anstex up and kicks him in the gut then running against the ropes and hitting a DDT. Joe goes up to the top rope for a eblow drop but Anstex rolls out the way. Both men are down with the referee counting, on 6 both men get to their feet, Joe uses the ropes to get to his feet but Anstex follows up with a clothesline that takes them both over the top rope. The referee starts the count as Anstex gets to his feet and picks up Joe, he whips him into the ringpost causing Joe to fall flat on his face, Anstex rolls in then back out to break the count. Anstex picks up Joe and hits a suplex onto the floor, then rolling Joe into the ring and going for the pin but only getting a 2 count.~ ~Anstex looked up at the referee but the referee saying only a two count, Joe slowly gets to his feet and hits a low blow on Anstex causing him to fall to his knees, Joe picks him up and whips him off the ropes but going straight into the referee. Suddenly nXn, aka X-Rodd and Etan Cross come running to the ring, Etan rolls in with a chair and smashes Anstex over the head with it, busted open Anstex lays motionless on the canvas as Etan rolls out the ring with X-Rodd looking on, Joe makes the cover as the referee slowly gets back up, he makes a slow 3 count with Joe La Fontaine ending up the winner. X-Rodd and Etan Cross climb into the ring to celebrate with Joe and destroy Anstex with the steel chair in the mean time.~
