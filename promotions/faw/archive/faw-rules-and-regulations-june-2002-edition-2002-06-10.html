FAW Rules and Regulations, June 2002 edition

10 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
RULES & REGULATIONS We do not tolerate the breaking of the Furiously Addictive Wrestling. You will get no more than one chance to rectify any wrongs, and then you are out the door. We get enough applications to fill spots which you have been fired from and will do this, so follow these rules and all will be okay. If you are warned once by any member of the FAW staff then stop doing the wrong and it will be well, but a second warning will likely result in the termination of your contract Roleplaying As the staff, we do not expect swearing to take place in your roelplays. If it is so essential that you must please beep it out, eg. f@#k, s**t, etc. No sexual harassment or racism is allowed in any circumstances. You are expected to rolpleay at least twice a week with quality roleplays, matches or not. If there is no sign of you in 2 weeks then your contract is immediately terminated and your character therefore belongs to Furiously Addictive Wrestling. Wrestlers This federation does not allow real wrestlers from real organizations, original wrestlers only please. If you do apply as a real wrestler your application will be blatantly ignored and you will hear nothing from us, this includes any catchphrases you have, don’t make them from real wrestlers. Please Note: Due to PTC Rules, if you quit, we reserve the right to NOT release you - and due to PTC Rules, you will not be allowed to join another PTC Fed if this is the case. Submissions In-card submissions now play a huge role in Furiously Addictive Wrestling. They are a huge part of your roleplaying process and could mean the difference between winning and losing. We are a roleplay federation and the federation is treated as such but we do take in-card promos, backstage attacks, interviews, and the like into account. If you send in 2-3 quality backstage segments each event/week then it strengthens your chances of winning matches. They could be used to express your characters feelings on a matter, they could progress the portrayal of your wrestler or it could progress an angle. If you like please contact the staff and we will clear up any queries you have with this. This will really help the shows that we put out and also your wrestlers progression in FAW. You can attack wrestlers in these promos but we reserve the right to edit the attacks you make or not use them, depending on the manner you attack your opponent, and of course you may have your opponent beat you up in a submission and then in a later one take it out on something of theirs! Advertising You may not under any circumstances advertise your own eFeds or interfeds using the FAW forums or other resources provided by us. Deadlines/Limits Wednesday Night Panic takes place once a week on a Wednesday. The roleplay limit for the show is a four roleplay maximum per person, the same applies to tag teams (four roleplay per tag team maximum). The deadline for the show is 12:00am EST – 5:00am GMT on the Wednesday morning/Tuesday night. Sunday Night Distortion takes place once a week on a Sunday. The roleplay limit for the show is a four roleplay maximum per person, the same applies to tag teams (four roleplay per tag team maximum). The deadline for the show is 12:00am EST – 5:00am GMT on the Sunday morning/Saturday night.
