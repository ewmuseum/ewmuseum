Backstage controversy.... Date: 10.20.02 Show: Distrotion 19 The camera cuts to backstage again as we see Damien Ryan still walking through the hallways with his weapon opens door, and checking rooms. He even walks in on a women changing but it doesn't phase him. A ring crew member walks the opposite way. Ryan grabs him by the shirt. Ryan: I've checked all over this place, now I know you couldn't of not seen somebody come past here last week to the entrence ramp after my match. Don't lie to me, you know what will happen. Ring crew member: Oh god, Ryan, I honestly don't know. You've got to believe me! Ryan: Look (he glances at the man's name tag) Jim. I have twenty seven stiches in my back and I'm not going to sit here and let you- There's some noise heard in the dressing room next to them. Ryan lets go of Jim to go investigate, Jim runs off down the hallway scared shitless and Ryan checks out the room. He opens the door and walks in. The room is really dark but has candles lit all around. We see Sin kneeling on some sort of pillow type thing and seems to be praying to a small Christian alter. Ryan: What in god's name are you doing? Sin turns around shocked Sin: Damien Ryan, for one do not use the lord's name in vain, and two, do not raise your voice in this room, please. Ryan: I know your pissed about last week Sin! Do you have anything to do with my attack last week?! Sin: Ryan, for the last time, do not raise your voice in this room, we are in the presence of the Lord. Ryan grabs Sin by the hair and drags him out of the room and into the corridor. He pushes him against the wall. Ryan: Listen freak! Tell me! Sin punches Damien Ryan in the nose, grabs him by the hair and tosses him into a rack of steel chairs, the chairs spilling out all of the corridor. Ryan quickly gets up and tackles Sin and the two begin brawling in the corridor, rolling arund and laying huge fists on eachother. Finally backstage members and security makes in to the scene and tries to break them up till the camera cuts back to the arena.
