FAW IS WAR, 25 June 2001

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
:::FAW IS WAR Intro The pyros explode and the cameras pan round the arena and then zoom in onto the announce table where Michael Cole and Bill Clinton are sitting. Bill Clinton: Welcome to FAW IS WAR tonight! We are just 6 days away from Diabolical Day, and tonight we will see 2 more qualifying matches and 1 more qualifying match results! This will take the total up to 5 people through! Michael Cole: I am Michael Cole alongside Bill Clinton and we are looking forward to seeing 2 title matches tonight and a great event, just as this Sunday night at Diabolical Day where we will see the World Title defended as well as 9 other matches! What an explosive night we await! Bill Clinton: But, the other question is, who is Sean Mason's mystery partner for tonight's match? Who will he be challenging for the Tag Team Titles with? We will see tonight! Michael Cole: We will also see the Hardcore Title be defended by Paranoia! :::Backstage The cameras fade backstage on the Furytron and they go into Sean Mason's locker room. Sean Mason is sitting there looking at his International Title belt and starts to speak to who seems to be himself. Sean Mason: This is my first title in FAW! And tonight I add another title to this one here. I will go to Diabolical Day with 2 titles round my waist and I will then win my European Title belt! Looks like it is my lucky streak in FAW! And it is with you that I win the title tonight. Disguised Voice: Yes, we will win tonight! Noone suspects it to be me! But it is, I wonder what they will think when I walk out there with you and we win the titles! Sean Mason: They will be shocked! I can't wait to get out there and win another title. :::Cut To Announce Table The cameras fade to black on the Furytron and back to the announce table. Bill Clinton: The man who will team with Sean Mason is here now, in this arena! I wonder who the hell it will be.... Michael Cole: Well I can't wait for tonight's main event! It will be like a great main event! I wonder if it is a new guy in FAW, a man who nobody knows yet, a man who people will not be expecting, it's really exciting! I'll tell you that! Bill Clinton: Well, we can be sure of one thing, Sadam Hussain is also here tonight! And have you seen the match at the PPV? It is me teaming with VP Hellmaster and we will take on the other President, Sadam Hussain and the other VP, Deadly J! Michael Cole: Well, that will be a great match, I can guarantee that, and it is hard to choose the winner! I think that it is evenly match. Bill Clinton: Get it right, Michael, who will win? Michael Cole: Okay, you will win, I think you are great! :::VP Hellmaster Promo Hellmaster's music hits and the fans cheer as he walks out to the ring and climbs into the ring. VP Hellmaster: Tonight, you will see a great event take place. Tonight, you will see 3 Diabolical Day Qualifying Matches. Tonight, you will see 2 titles being defended! Tonight, you will see Power Of FAW lose their Tag Team Titles! That son of a b[beep]ch Sadam Hussain will see his precious Tag Team Champions no longer be champions. You will witness this great night in FAW and you will be able to tell everyone you know how great it was to see it happen! and this coming Sunday night you will be able to say the same thing, you will be able to say that you saw Bill Clinton and VP Hellmaster kick Sadam Hussain's and VP Deadly J's asses! You will witness a great happening! And when it is all said and done, you will know that you are witnessing a great event. You see, in the past, we have seen many great events. The first thing was me being born, the second was the formation of this great town. The fans cheer. VP Hellmaster: Then you will see this Sunday night, at Diabolical Day, you will see me and Bill kick some dirty ass! And tonight, when Paranoia retains his Hardcore Title, and when Bayne beats PyRo, Tag Team Titles will be lost! Hell, I don't really care what Power Of FAW do tonight BUT the matter is, they will probably not win a single damn match! So, good luck tonight, Power Of FAW, coz Brutal Deluxe, my good friend, Bill Clinton's stable, are gonna win all their matches tonight! Hellmaster's music hits and he walks round the ring twice and then goes to climb out of the ring but then stops as VP Deadly J's music hits. He walks out and stops on the top of the ramp and puts a mic to his mouth. VP Deadly J: Well, well, well, what do we have here? A bonnefide jack ass? That's right, coz tonight you see, Power Of FAW will not only win their matches but they will kick ass over "Brutal Deluxe". And when all is said and done and you people witness tonight's events, well, you have been promised a landmark night, and well, you will get one! You will see Power Of FAW retain their Tag Team Titles! I may not yet be in the most powerful stable FAW will ever see yet but I know that they will win their matches and kick some ass! So, good luck tonight Hellmaster! Deadly J's music hits and both men walk backstage. :::Backstage The Furytron turns on once more where The Mortician is walking through the locker room area when suddenly a figure jumps out from behind him wearing a mask. The figure walks behind him, taps Mortician on the shoulder and when he turns round, hits him over the head repeatedly with a chair. Then when Mortician is down, the man rips off his mask and the face is of CHRIS THUNDER. :::The Mortician vs Chris Thunder - Diabolical Day Qualifying Match WINNER: Chris Thunder due to interference from 9 Inch Sean (XWW Star) :::Paranoia vs T-Rip - Hardcore Title Match WINNER: Paranoia :::JLC vs Skipster - Diabolical Day Qualifying Match Bill Clinton: We have a Diabolical Day Qualifying match coming up. The former 2-Time European Champion of FAW JLC versus the man who will battle PyRo at Diabolical Day , known only as Skipster. Michael Cole: This match will be A hard one to predict don’t you think? Bill Clinton: It could go to Skipster or JLC , infact I’d say this match would be a damn good final. But it is not to be. Crawling by Linkin Park plays supposedly loud over the tron system. The crowd are far too unaware to compliment the music as the booing for the man they actually CHEERED at Breakaway, the heel from hell JLC. His shirt shines in the arena but whether it will stay like that is anyone’s guess. Ride the lightning by Metallica is played throughout the wild arena with the #1 contender for the World Title making his way down and around the ring. The two lock up as the match begins. JLC with the forwards suplex. Russian Leg Sweep by Skipster on JLC. JLC hits a piledriver and continues the beating. Bill Clinton: It is still hard to predict but JLC seems to be ontop. Skipster is caught out by JLC who locks in the sleeper. The referee waits for ten seconds before raising Skipsters hand. He then repeats the action twice waiting for Skipster to make some acknowledgement that he is still concious. Eventually Skipster does and both men exchange blows to the mid-section with Skipster falling. Bill Clinton: The crowd are wild with anger at JLC , he has once again emphasised that bad thingz do happen to good people. Michael Cole: I suppose you never get injured then. Bill Clinton: No I Don….wait a minute you are saying I am a Bad Person? Damn you ! JLC goes for the cover. Pin: 1 2 Kickout. Michael Cole: That was pure luck. Skipster challenges JLC and does the same Pin: 1 2 hand on ropes. Bill Clinton: And so was that! Michael Cole: JLC is just getting warmed up! JLC hits a LooPlex and goes for the cover. Pin: 1 2 3 Michael Cole: JLC ADVANCES :::PyRo vs Bayne WINNER: PyRo :::Power Of Two (British Bomber & High Voltage) vs Sean Mason & Mystery Opponent - Tag Team Titles Match Bill Clinton: Our main event for tonight is coming up in just a few minutes. Michael Cole: Here we are , the climax of the show coming up. Bill Clinton: It’s the new member of Hells Henchmen High Voltage teaming up with British Bomber to battle Sean Mason and his hand picked partner. Highway to hell blasts into the erupting arena which shatters all hope of a slight cheer for the combined team of FAW Debutant High Voltage with the former International Champion British Bomber. Highlights are shown of the feud leading up to this match and of course we saw High Voltage interfere on Panic. Bomber wears his usual wrestling attire and of course High Voltage wears the same clothes as he did on his arena debut. The crowd are expectant of respect , which is not given by the rude team. They cannot be certain whether it will be Mason or his partners music which we hear. Bill Clinton: Immense leadup has finally stopped…..enter the partner of Sean Mason… Tier by Rammenstein explodes into the wild arena as the message is rapidly spread around the arena that Mystic X is the partner of Sean Mason. X looks up to the adoring crowd from the entrance ramp perfected with delight at the response given. He shakes hands with the crowd and even signs Autographs as we see Sean Mason finally follow behind as the word stardom must surely be echoing around the wrestlers minds. Both teams give an evil glare to one another before climbing into the ring and preparing to lockup. The bell rings as Cole and Clinton talk over the ever-lasting chants of the eager crowd. Michael Cole: I think the crowd are a little over excited after that surprise. Bill Clinton: It shocked me as well….I thought it was going to be arch enemy of Mason JLC , Don’t ask me why. Michael Cole: Who would have thought it? Mystic X of all people. Mason and Bomber begin circling each other as the other two men cling onto the ropes. Mason swings his foot at Bomber giving him a surprise on the way. Bomber drops to the ground and Mason locks in the figure four leg lock. Bomber cries out in a mix of anger and pain. He soon stumbles across the rope and grabs hold of it. British Bomber then hits a brainbuster swallowing his worries. He stamps violently on Mason and then tags High Voltage. X screams on the outside aware of the carnage to unfold unless Mason tags. However Sean is too late and Power of Two double team The Icon with High Voltage nailing a flying headbutt. Bomber gets revenge on Mason for losing him his title and continuously batters Mason. Sean Mason reaches out his hand with Power of Two working up the crowd. Mystic X snatches the tag and gets inside the ring with High Voltage the legal man. He throws Bomber out of the ring and signals for his partner to damage Bomber. He rolls up High Voltage. Pin: 1 2 Bill Clinton: He kicks out on two! Michael Cole: With the tag titles at stake there is sure to be some more action. Bill Clinton: I don’t think there has been a time where there HASN’T been action. Mystic X rolls up High Voltage again. Pin: 1 High Voltage kicks out on the first count as we switch to the camera outside of the ring. Bomber whips Mason into the barrier. He throws him back and the cameraman falls over. However the camera stays up, although not for much longer as British Bomber drops it across Mason. Bill Clinton: Cable Viewers Press the Red Button on your remote to see your very own outside of ring view. Michael Cole: Damn I wish I had cable.. Bill Clinton: You are in the arena you don’t need flamin’ TV! Michael Cole: Oh…..I get you now… High Voltage counters a stunner into a submission move. He calls for Bomber who finishes with Mason to climb the top rope. Bill Clinton: Bomber is going for the headbutt now! Sean Mason sneaks up from behind Bomber and knocks him off the turnbuckle. High Voltage is unaware. Mason petrifies High Voltage by hitting a hurricarana in mid-air. The crowd gasp as the athleticism of Mason who gets the pin. Bill Clinton: Mason isn’t the legal man! Pin: None Michael Cole: But Mystic X is! Pin: 1 2 3 Bill Clinton: We have new Tag Team Champions. Michael Cole: Ever since Hells Henchmen was formed Bayne [Hardcore Champion at time] has left, British Bomber lost his IN Title, Spike didn’t win the World Title and NOW THIS ? Is there a curse on these guys? Bill Clinton: Or is it just a fluke? OR MAYBE even the fact that they now have competition in Brutal Deluxe. :::Dark Matches Axle vs Jay Wiked WINNER: Jay Wiked
