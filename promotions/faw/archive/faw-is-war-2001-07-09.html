FAW IS WAR, 9 July 2001

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
:::FAW IS WAR Intro The pyros blow and "Enter The Sandman" by Metallica plays. Then the lights flash and the camera zooms in on the entrance way and then on 1 or 2 members of the crowd and then goes in to the announce table where Bill and Michael are sitting once again. Michael Cole: Welcome to FAW IS WAR, I am Michael Cole alongside Bill Clinton. And tonight we will see a great night with the World Title on the line and that match looks set to be a great match with the match for Disaster Down Under, between Jay Wiked, the winner of Diabolical Day, and PyRo...the man who remains undefeated in singles competition. Bill Clinton: and of course, the European Title is on the line in that match between Dimitri Duncan, Double D and the champion, The Mortician, that is one of the most eagerly awaited matches this FAW IS WAR. We have a PPV quality card coming up for you, so get ready. Michael Cole: And don't forget XWW boss, Chris Thunder was suspended at Panic last week. Footage is shown of Thunder being suspended. Bill Clinton: And that is all coz of the interferences from XWW in recent shows. So I don't se XWW getting in here tonight. And with Sadam hiring double security it looks set to be a hell of a night tonight! No XWW and a pure FAW show, through and through! Michael Cole: Well, let's get this show on the road right now. The first match is Lightning taking on Pennywise. :::Lightning vs Pennywise Michael Cole: Now It Looks as if were gonna see FAW’s latest newcomer Pennywise, In action now! Bill Clinton: Yeah FAW IS WAR this week is set to be great Michael Cole: That’s Linkin Park and that means here comes Lightning Bill Clinton: But look he’s being jumped from behind by Pennywise with a kendo stick Michael Cole: this is gonna be brutal, because Pennywise levels Lightning with a stiff right hand and another and quickly followed by a dropkick Bill Clinton: And now he’s taken him back to the ring Bill Clinton: And look at all them tools he’s throwing into the ring and Lightning oh my god, what a splash over the top rope Michael Cole: And Lightning looks to be setting up that table and he’s got PennyWise up but pennywise counters with a low blow Bill Clinton: And you can’t forget everythings legal in a hardcore match Michael Cole: Pennywise with the steel chair, and he levelled him with it Bill Clinton: Now Pennywise trying the pin on the floor Pin: 1….2 and a kick out by Lightning Michael Cole: Near fall there, Pennywise straight back up though, as is Lightning Bill Clinton: Huge spinning DDT by Lightning Michael Cole: What a comeback by Lightning, who’s on spaghetti legs at the moment and Lightning is setting that table up in the corner of the ring leant up against the ring post Bill Clinton: He’s gonna try the spear to Pennywise through the table Michael Cole: And he connected with it! Bill Clinton: Geez, Pennywise must be out cold, and why isn’t Lightning trying to get the pin? Michael Cole: Pennywise is getting to his feet, How the hell is he doing that? Pennywise Falls back down Bill Clinton: Easy he isn’t! Michael Cole: Lightning! Stop posing and cover the man! Bill Clinton: Look Pennywise is up Michael Cole: Powerbomb on Lightning by Pennywise Bill Clinton: But Pennywise doesn’t know where he is Michael Cole: But still he’s going under the ring Bill Clinton: He’s pulled out two more tables he’s setting one on top of two Michael Cole: What could he be up to? Pennywise walks backwards and picks up a chair and nails Lightning with the chair. Pin: 1….2….3 Michael Cole: That’s it its over Pennywise just beat Lightning in a great match here tonight. :::Cut To Announce Table The camera cuts to the announce table after a great match, Pennywise overcoming his match. Bill Clinton: WOW! What a match that one was and no-one was expecting a Hardcore Match, not even me! I just thought that it was a normal match! Michael Cole: Yes, me too! I think it surprised everyone! Bill Clinton: Well, what is gonna happen next here on FAW IS WAR? :::Cut To Furytron The lights suddenly go out and the cameras go to the Furytron, the whole arena is in pitch black and suddenly some words flash up on the screen GET READY ------- YOU WILL WISH YOU DIDN'T DO IT.... The lights then come back on and the fans are talking about who it was as the cameras go to a commercial break. :::Commercial Break :::The Mortician vs Dimitri Duncan - European Title Match Cole: European title will be on the line in this next match, which will pitch the current champion, The Mortician against DD, who has been granted his title shot, which he definitely deserves! Clinton: Yes, he's been working very hard in FAW over the last couple of weeks, and this shot is long in coming! Cole: This should be a great battle, and there is definitely a strong chance of a title change! Clinton: This one could do either way. "Diary Of A Madman" - Ozzy Osborne hits and the current champion, The Mortician, appears at the top of the ramp, holding his belt in the air. The fans aren’t happy with this, and want a definite title change! Cole: The fans hate Mortician! Clinton: Hang on….doesn’t the current champion always come out second? Cole: Well, this is FAW, anything can happen! The Morticians tall figure slides into the ring, and leans on the corner post, as DD appears at the top of the ramp, moving down to the ring with his unknown music. The fans hold there arms out, hoping to touch DD, but DD is focused on the match, as he knows this is a huge opportunity for him. Cole: I was talking to DD earlier on, and he said that he has been preparing all week, and is ready for this match tonight! Clinton: Facing The Mortician is definitely something that needs a lot of preparation; this is going to be a tough test for him! Cole: [Bell rings] Here we go, this is going to be a roller coaster ride that you don’t want to miss! Both men tie up, and DD gets the advantage as he hiptosses Mort. Mort is right back up though and DD knocks him back down again with a clothesline to the chest. He goes for the cover. Refs Count: 1..Kickout Cole: Quick kickout! DD picks up Mort, and attempts the whip. Mort reverses and fires DD into the ropes. Mort hits a big back body drop. He picks up DD, and hits a body slam. Clinton: The Mortician will definitely have the edge for power here! Refs Count: 1..Kickout Cole: Another quick kickout! Mort picks up DD again, and whips him into the corner. He goes into the opposite corner, and charges at DD, and runs right into him! Clinton: DD squashed under the weight there! Cole: I couldn’t see him for a moment! Clinton: This guy isn’t that heavy, just under 300 pounds, but he is 7’1” Cole: You cant really say that just under 300 pounds isn’t much! Mort picks DD up by the throat, and throws him to the ground. Mort taunts the crowd, and fails to see DD, who is slowly rising. DD hits a low blow! DD then hits a big DDT! DD gets up, and climbs the ropes. He waits for Mort to rise, and when he does, flys at him with a missile dropkick! Clinton: That move knocks the big man down! Cole: DD coming back into this now! DD goes against the cover, and decides to go for a submission. He goes to the head, and locks in a choke hold. He quickly releases the hold, and hits a leg drop! Refs Count: 1..2..kickout Clinton: That’s not going to be enough for beat the Euro champ Mortician! Cole: DD already showing signs of frustration, as he goes after the ramp, saying that it was a slow count! Clinton: It was a fine count! DD jumps out of the ring, and picks up a chair, and throws it into the ring. Mort is still down, and DD slides into the ring, picking up his chair. He waits for Mort to rise. The crowd are trying to get Mort of the ground, and see the chair shot coming! Mort rises, and DD swings, but moves out of the way, and DD hits the referee! Clinton: Oh no! He just took out referee John Savva! Cole: Oh well, the referee is well out of it! DD throws the chair into the crowd, and turns around, to be greeted by Mort! DD stops, as Mort has one of his sadistic smiles on his face! DD pleads to him, but Mort ignores him, and picks him up into a sit down shoulder postion! He hits it!! Cole: That move is particularly painful! Clinton: But he cant go for the pin, The ref is down! Cole: And Mort knows it! Mort tries to get the referee up, but is unsuccessful. Mort picks DD up, and hits a big powerbomb! Clinton: Did you see the ring shake then!? Cole: Mort is a tough guy, great ability, that’s why he's the European champion! Mort is very annoyed, and gets out of the ring. A fan is taunting him, so he spits in their face! Cole: Well that’s not very nice! Clinton: Yes it is, that’s a souvenir for the fan! Not every person can say that the FAW Euro champ spat in there face! Cole: That’s ridiculous! [Spits in Clintons face] How did you like that? Clinton: Maybe it’s a little worse that I thought. Mort picks up DD, and hits the Manditory Suicide!! Mort goes for the cover, but of course there is no ref to make the count! Mort cant believe it, but another referee comes running down to the ring. Mort goes for the cover. Refs Count: 1..2.. VP Flare appears, and pulls the referee out of the ring, and he nails him with a chair shot!! He slides into the ring, and before Mort can turn, flies at him with another huge chair shot!! The crowd go crazy, as VP Flare, picks up DD and DD hits Total Domination! The crowd don’t believe it! DD is out of it, but VP Flare puts him on top of Mortician. The original referee slowly makes his wave over for the count! Refs CountL 1…2…3!! Cole: NEW EURO CHAMP! NEW EURO CHAMP! Clinton: I don’t believe what happened there! Unbelievable ending to that match! :::Cut To Announce Table Michael Cole: Well, already tonight we have a new European champion, BUT will the main title tonight, the World Title change hands? Well, stay with us through the night to find out! Bill Clinton: And what was going on on the tron earlier tonight? Who is taking us over by use of the Furytron? Only one person I can think of, but you think for yourself! Maybe we will find out tonight, maybe not! Michael Cole: Well I damn well hope so.....we deserve to know along with Sadam and all the fans here! :::Sadam Hussain Promo "Rollin" hits and Sadam Hussain walks slowly to the ring to the boos of the crowd. He gets into the ring with a mic. Sadam Hussain: You know, I am the co-owner of this great federation and I deserve a little more respect then you people give me. I mean, I am President Sadam Hussain...I am the man that ran Power Of FAW and we were the best damn stable EVER in FAW and always will be! Well, although a few of us still are together, we have lost British Bomber....but, we will get on without him.....now on to tonight....firstly, earlier on tonight we saw something perculiar happen on the Furytron, now, I am not pointing any fingers but I think we ALL know who it was, and I say, good luck coz no-one other than FAW members are getting into the Gardens tonight! Madison Square Gardens tonight is FAW's territory and only FAW members will walk around here and not be escorted out by security! The fans cheer. Sadam Hussain: Now, later on tonight that main event World Title match, I was sitting backstage thinking about it. We are in the greatest arena in sports entertainment and we are going to have a World Title match? Well, that's all well and good, but a World Title normal match? Well that sucks basically! I thought long and hard about how we could get the best main event....then I thought, TLC MATCH! No.....then I thought STREET FIGHT.....no.....then I finally came to the match I am out here to announce.....tonight right here in this very ring the World Title will be defended by PyRo against the Diabolical Day King, Jay Wiked in a..... The crowd eagerly await. Sadam Hussain: IN A CAGE!!!! "Rollin" hits and Sadam walks back smiling. :::Commercial Break :::Skipster vs JLC MATCH WRITER ILL WINNER: Skipster :::Cut To Furytron The Furytron switches on again and the lights go out again. "YOU SHOULD NEVER HAVE DONE IT --- WE ARE HERE NOW!!!" Suddenly Xavier Christopher runs in from backstage and begins to beat Dimitri Duncan from behind and Dimitri falls to the ground. Xavier puts a mic to his mouth. Xavier Christopher: You cannot hold back XWW! We own FAW...get ready for the BIG ONE FAW! With that security escort Xavier out to the parking lot and watch him drive off. :::Cut To Announce Table Bill Clinton: NO! Damn them, they got in here TONIGHT! Damn.... Michael Cole: Another XWW invasion takes place here tonight....how long can this continue? Who knows? Bill Clinton: Well, I think that we handle this backstage! Let's get on with this great 3 way debut match! :::Aaron Slater vs The Rage vs The Machine - Hardcore Match Bill Clinton: Coming right up every single person in this arena will witness Aaron Slater , The Machine and Rage battle it out right in the square circle that is the FAW Ring! Michael Cole: Not only that but it will be a hardcore match. Hardcore rules can only mean ratings Bill as everyone knows. Bill Clinton: I have a slight feeling they may not draw what we hope as I have been told this match will contain a lot of blood which may cause the viewer to change channel. Michael Cole: Tragedy. Bill Clinton: My views exactly. Rage’s theme tune is played aloud around the arena. The debutant comes from behind the curtains holding up his hands as several hundred cheers are heard. He then drops his hands to his side and goes almost backstage. Someone shoves a shopping trolley towards him and he grabs it. Inside it is several weapons. This sets alight the burning desire which the crowd craves for, war. Rage climbs into the ring stunned by the reaction he is getting. Suddenly Aaron Slaters theme tune blasts throughout the arena. The crowd boo him , but only slightly. His face turns a comforting pale as he excites himself with a dumpster full of hardcore weapons. He climbs the apron and poses for the unwanting crowd. Out of nowhere a sudden bass eruption explodes onto the circuit. The Machine walks down the ramp and to the crowds surprise carries no weapons at all! His head continuously nods. All three men are in the ring as we prepare to start. Michael Cole: So who are you betting on tonight Bill? Bill Clinton: Lets just say $50 promises The Machine a pint of beer after the match. Michael Cole: Ha! You are choosing the guy with no hardcore weapons? The whole meaning of hardcore is pain and quite honestly Cinderella scares me more than this guy. Bill Clinton: We know Michael. Just come to terms with the fact that she was driven by a pumpkin. Michael Cole: What the ….you freak! Bill Clinton: I think the match will start shortly. Michael Cole: Correct. The bell rings. Aaron Slater charges at The Machine with a chair. He takes him out instantly. The Machine takes a bump and gets straight up , heading for a collision course with the chair once again. Michael Cole: CRASH. Rage takes out an iron bar. He steps into the ring and clobbers both men with it. The crowd begin to chant for The Machine, who is dealt yet one more blow as the piledriver damages him more. Rage and Aaron Slater fight as Slater is whipped into the ropes. Upon his return a fire extinguisher lays out some unacceptable damage. Bill Clinton: Holy s[beep]t! Michael Cole: Shit? Holy? What is wrong with you? Bill Clinton: I’m over exaggerating Cole , this is the part where we start to argue…. Michael Cole: Oh yes…Umm….you are so skinny you can do press-ups under a door! Bill Clinton: You are bad. How about….you do not have a Mum, just two dad’s and a chemistry set which costs £5. Michael Cole: £4.99 actually. Bill Clinton: You admit it?! Michael Cole: No but one of my friends has been in a position… Bill Clinton: With you I guess. Michael Cole: Damn you. The camera turns to the action as we see Rage littered with blood on the ground. As Aaron Slater and The Machine battle it out we see a replay of what got Rage in this state. [Replay] Aaron Slater hits a powerbomb onto a chair. The Machine bulldogs Slater onto the mat. The Machine reaches into Slaters dumpster after climbing out of the ring. He picks out a tennis ball and racket. He climbs back into the ring. Bill Clinton: Speaking of freaks. The Machine serves the ball into the back of Rage. Michael Cole: Second serve. Bill Clinton: What are you talking about, that was in! Michael Cole: I am not serious. Bill Clinton: Well I am. The Machine causes yet more damage as he misses the ball and slams Rage with the racket. Some members of the crowd laugh as they hold up their banners. Michael Cole: I never noticed that. We go back to the live action where Rage is still sprawled across the floor. The Machine chokeslams Aaron Slater. Bill Clinton: Use the racket USE IT! Michael Cole: I heard you were at Wimbledon, the tennis in England on Saturday. I suppose that is why you are so obsessed. Bill Clinton: I’m thirsty, I need some deuce. Michael Cole: Its worse than I thought…. The Machine lands the pinfall. Pin: 1 2 3 :::Cut Backstage The cameras cut backstage where Jay Wiked is walking through the corridor for his match. British Bomber suddenly jumps him with a lead pipe and repeatedly beats him with it. The cameras then cut to a commercial break before the main event. :::Commercial Break :::PyRo (c) vs Jay Wiked - World Title Match - Cage Match Michael Cole: The main event for tonight is coming up! Bill Clinton: Yes after the cancellation of Jay Wiked versus Pyro at Disaster Down Under the match was soon scheduled for TONIGHT! Michael Cole: Speaking of Jay Wiked just before the commercial break he was viciously attacked by British Bomber. Bill Clinton: Lets have a look at that again… Tape rolls on the tron of Jay being beaten up. We then go back live where the paramedics are taking Jay away. Michael Cole: Bomber…what an evil soul he carries. Bill Clinton: It’s a pity really. Michael Cole: I don’t think these fans are too pleased about that attack. Bill Clinton: I think I have something that will cheer them up. Bill smiles as we hear a creak. A Cage is lowered down to the ring as the fans look on mystified by this apparent mistake. Michael Cole: It isn’t….you didn’t……. Bill Clinton: Jay Wiked versus Pyro for the World Title in , you guessed it, a CAGE MATCH! The crowd go wild and forget all about Jays attack. Michael Cole: Jay will never by able to compete at this rate. The tron switches on. Jay Wiked lays on the couch as the paramedics stitch him up. He shoves past them and gives many of them the Wicked Bomb. He sprints down the corridor as we fade out. Bill Clinton: Where is he going? Michael Cole: Well…it is kind of obvious…he wants to compete tonight. Bill Clinton: I never anticipated this. I was hoping for a cancellation and an early night in bed. Michael Cole: You of all people should know these guys NEVER give up. Bill Clinton: Oh Well I didn’t think of that. As the cage is lowered Highway to hell by ACDC plays. Out comes someone. Who could it be? As darkness plagues the arena we see a small glint. The camera continues slowly panning around the arena but then goes back. It stops at the position. We see more shining. Then the lights dim more. We see two eyes. They are both different colours. All of a sudden dozens of pyros explode and confetti is spread around the arena. Pyro stands at the ramp. We see his two eyes, one orange and one green. We then see the World Title belt tightened around his waist. He looks up at the setting of possible demolition, the cage in instant delight. The crowd are shocked by this reaction as they considered Pyro a cautious person up till now. Pyro breathes in and out shaking his legs from side to side. He does some stretches. He then is let into the cage by the referee. He looks up staring around. Suddenly Conflict plays and Pyro is jolted by a surprised reaction. Jay catches him by surprise as he runs at him. Bill Clinton: What a start! Michael Cole: BRAWL! BRAWL! BRAWL! As Clinton and Cole continue acting outrageously beyond their adult content Pyro whips Jay into the cage. Jays head is hurt. He falls to the ground not letting go of it. Pyro stomps on Jay who bounces off the mat and lands once again. Michael Cole: Woah…. Bill Clinton: I knew Pyro was strong but not this strong. Michael Cole: Oh , that’s why you made him your World Champion! Bill Clinton: I didn’t…umm…fate took place…. Michael Cole: Come off it Clinton, all these people know the truth. Bill Clinton: I did not sleep with Miss Monica Lewinski. Michael Cole: Here we are again changing the subject! Bill Clinton: Damn…you didn’t fall for it. The action continues with Jay attacking Pyro. He bulldogs him onto the floor. The King stands up and receives an ovation from the crowd. Suddenly Highway to hell by AC/DC plays. Michael Cole: Perhaps this is Pyro’s twin sister? Bill Clinton: Oh no he is back. I am afraid you are wrong Cole he is not Pyro’s twin sister, he is Satans Evil Sister! Cole begins to laugh at British Bomber continues his walk down to the ring. His impression to the crowd seems to be improving as this time he gets a mixed reaction even though he attacked Jay earlier tonight. Jay Wiked is distracted By British Bomber. Bomber smiles as if everything is going according to plan. Jay turns his attention to Pyro as Bomber gets angry. He smashes open the cage door and walks to Jay. He scruffs up his neck and lifts him up high. Michael Cole: I Think he is jealous of the King. As Bomber is about to lock on the English Lock Rollin’ plays. The speakers vibrate as British Bombers eyes are set alight. He drops Jay and looks for a way out. As Sadam Hussain begins to pick up speed Bomber crushes the cage door open. It falls as the crowd pick up the noise considerably. Sadam grabs Bomber and locks in a headlock. Bomber easily gets out of it. He suddenly shoves Sadam directly through the Iraqi Announcers Table. Bill Clinton: He just ruined their broadcasting! As Bomber is flattened Pyro calls out from inside the cage. Spike is awkwardly forced to confront British Bomber on what his injuries are. Meanwhile JLC and Gee Monie throw Sadam around a little to get a little bit of a piece of him. Clinton follows the two and they brawl down the tiers. Inside the ring Jay and Pyro are completely astounded by the outcome of this match so far. Spike Bomber and Hussain , formerly known as Power Of FAW until just recently are forced to speak to each other. Spike tells Sadam what has gone on. Sadam Hussain’s face lights up in an exclamation of fear. As the cameras switch to where JLC Gee Monie and Clinton are we see all three lying on the ground. The camera is very close up. Now in the ring Jay Wiked and Pyro continue fighting. Jay hits a DDT. He bounces against the ropes and nails a leg drop. Jay climbs the cage. Michael Cole: He is near the top now…..we could have a new World Champion…. As Jay tries to climb over the top his foot slips. He hangs on the edge of the cage as a gasp is created by the crowd. Pyro shakes the cage and Jay goes plummeting down to what could be a horrible death. A bang Is heard as he lands. He is definitely unconscious according to Pyro who shows this by looking very amused. Pyro climbs the cage. Michael Cole: Oh Well Pyro is going to retain As Pyro reaches the top he stands up. Michael Cole: That suicidal …b…b… bitch! He hits a diving headbutt of the top of the cage. Jay looks restless but then is once again sent back to his unconscious status. Both men begin to move. Pyro gets the cover over Jay Wiked. Pin: 1…..2…………3….. The show ends swiftly tonight with the cameras focusing on Bill Clinton.
