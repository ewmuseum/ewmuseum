Can I become an affiliate with you? v. Credits I. GENERAL INFORMATION i. What is Furiously Addictive Wrestling? Ans) Furiously Addictive Wrestling is an 'underground' fed , which operates illegally , tickets are hard to come by , you have to have a good reputation in organized crime before we can trust you ;-) , and provides you with every single type of illegal fighting you could ever imagine. The locations are undisclosed until the very last second , to throw the cops of the track. We are a 'worldwide' fed , arriving in various locations across the world , mostly doing tours , but not too long tours (we don't need the cops to know where we are) of different countries. ii. What type of federation is Furiously Addictive Wrestling? Ans) Furiously Addictive Wrestling is e-mail flash packet system based federation with handwritten results. But, the winners aren't decided on roleplays alone , but rather many factors which can be viewed below. Roleplays - 55% And remember , we strictly follow Quality Over Quantity. Quantity is still a factor , as one must put effort to win his match , but NEVER , I mean NEVER compromise on quantity. In-Ring Interviews as oncards (see Part IV. Question v.) are accepted but please DON'T just rant on how badly you are gonna kick your opponents a** coz it is SO boring to read. Strats - 15% Go to Part. IV , Question iii of the FAQ to find out what a strat is. Strats play an important role in this efed. Strats help the matchwriters to put in more material in the matches and generally make it more interesting. A Good Strat = A Good Match = A Good Chance Of Winning (especially if you and your opponents flashes are very close to call!) Angle - 15% Do you just want to see Wrestler No.1 win the match. Don't you want to why does Wrestler No.1 suddenly goes into a rage when he hears Wrestler No.2 name? Don't you want to know , why Wrestler No.1 goes crazy when someone tries to work on his neck? Don't you want know why he doesn't like to talk about his past. Don't you want to know why is he so much against heels? Don't you want to know why he wears a leg brace to the ring? Get Instant Messenger tools like AOL IM and ICQ and chat with fellow handlers and discuss. If you are out of ideas , contact us at our official email , [detail withheld] , and we will try to come up with an angle for you. We shall determine how well you are playing your part of the angle , how well you are co-operating with your handlers , and other factors. Participation - 10% Participation doesn't just simply mean you roleplay every week and sit back. You come forward , discuss angles and other stuff with the fedheads , staff , fellow handlers etc . You send in ideas for matches , you send in strats etc etc On Cards , Heat & Character Development - 5% On Cards (see Part IV. Question .v) are a great way to get noticed . Also the make the cards more interesting to read. 'Heat' means how to crowd reacts to your wrestler , Long Term Consistent Roleplaying = Lots Of Heat. Character Development is another important part. Would you except a true babyface to use swearwords every second? Or a monster heel who actually does things for charity? You get the idea? iii. How is it different from other federations? Ans) Furiously Addictive Wrestling isn't just a federation of a 20,000 people strong fanbase. Ferrociously Addictive Wrestling is a great fed to be in...we have all features that most feds can't handle, eg. the Money System... iv. Who runs Furiously Addictive Wrestling? Ans) Furiously Addictive Wrestling is run by two Prez's or fedheads, Bill Clinton and Sadam Hussain , the one who usually takes care of putting together matches. We have many staff members and have a writing team of 3-4 experienced guys , and may have more , provided the handlers chip in. v. How do I join the Furiously Addictive Wrestling? Ans) Please finish reading this FAQ , so that you can decide whether this is the fed for you or not. Then you can apply for joining by going to the Join page from the homepage. We shall get back to you , probably within seventy two [72] hours ,if you have been selected. We don't have time to reply back to you if you have not been selected. If you have been rejected , feel free to reapply when you feel that your talents have improved! vi. How many wrestlers can I handle? Ans) You can handle one tag team OR one singles wrestler. Also , we prefer tag teams made of two handlers instead of one , as they are more interesting. vii. Hey, I will join Furiously Addictive Wrestling , but I need a World Title shot as my first match. Ans) Well , then go to hell then. We don't care whether you are a former IWO , fWo or CWL World Champion , you'll have to work your way to the top. II. EVENTS i. How many weekly cards are there? Ans) Currently , we have 2 weekly cards that are our main cards, Thursday Night Panic and FAW IS WAR...we also have a house show named Hot Stuff. ii. What type are they? Ans) We hand-write all cards UNLESS there are dire circumstances which do not allow us to do so. iii. We do not have a flash packet but we will soon start up a newsletter service iv. Ans) The default schedule , unless otherwise stated in the Tour Dates section of the website , is as follows Monday - The card for Thursday Night Panic and Hot Stuff is decided and FAW IS WAR results are finalized Tuesday - The cards are finalized Wednesday - Results for Thursday are written Thursday - Results are finished Friday - Card for FAW IS WAR are up and Hot Stuff matches are written Saturday - Results are finalized and the card for FAW IS WAR is finalized Sunday - FAW IS WAR results are written vi. Any other shows? Ans) VERY occasionally we hold one time shows and they are like SUPERCARDS.... vii. How do I get all these? Ans) By doing whatever it says on our homepage III. CHAMPIONSHIP i. What are the belts? Ans) Currently, we have 7 belts: Universal, World, International, European, Hardcore, Women's, Tag We could increase it if we get more tags and stables and more women wrestlers... ii. How do I get a shot at the titles? Ans) Just coming out and demanding a shot at the title won't do. Instead you must be consistent, climb the ladder to the top of Ferrociously Addictive Wrestling and finally arrive at the topmost part of Ferrociously Addictive Wrestling. Then probably you'll be booked in a match for the titles. Also , PLEASE don't demand pushes . Instead of getting pushed , it might go the other way. IV. SUCCEEDING IN THIS FED i. Can you give me a few guidelines on roleplaying? Ans) If any of you handlers have any good tips please send them at the official Ferrociously Addictive Wrestling e-mail. It may be added if it is good. Also , if you are not a member of this site , please give your qualifications. For the meantime , I'll post my own. ---------- First of all , they are various forms of writing flashes , from a movie script type , to past tense type of flashes. Use whatever way you feel comfortable. Also , I have found all successful characters to be very similar to the handler. For example , I am the joker of the class , always cracking up everyone with my jokes , but I created an evil character. I failed miserably , then I tried a humorous character, and I succeded. Plzzzz dont use bad grammer , itz making da flash look sooooo bad , k? Grammar problems are mostly found in powerplant federations. No good grammar is going to get you nowhere. Get RID of the chat language , I really go mad when I see people writing , 'r' instead of 'are' , 'u' instead of you and various others. Always read your flash after you have finished writing it and run it through a spell checker. Please don't try to be like other real wrestlers. Hey yo, Wrestler#1 , YOU'RE NEXT!!! FEAR THE STUNNER!!! ARRRRRGGGGGHHHHH! Wow , that was a world class line , wasn't it? Yep , a world class of line of WHAT NOT TO SAY!!!! I have seen lots of wrestlers that simply combine quotes of different wrestlers , DON'T DO IT! You won't get anywhere if you write these sort of catchphrases. Also , don't write my wrestler looks like' Undertaker' , or he walks to the ring , 'Mankind' style or he comes out to the Rock theme music. Create a in-depth wrestler , some of the most common biographies are as follows 'Wrestler#1 grew up with dreams of becoming a wrestler , one day he found a building marked 'Wrestling School' , so he joined it. He worked in 'indies' for a few months before going up to the higher levels' Another perfect example of a bio? Yep, a PERFECT EXAMPLE OF WHAT NOT TO WRITE! Please , I am sick of these biographies. Please ask yourself the following questions before you create and wrestler. What is your wrestler background story? Was he abused as a child? Was he brought up in riches and that probably the reason for him being a spoilt brat heel ? Why the hell did he become a pro-wrestler? Remember , not all pro-wrestlers have dreams of becoming pro-wrestlers. Why in the world is your wrestler a loner? Why doesn't he want to make friends with anyone? Why does he like the fans? Why does he like breaking rules? Why does he like getting HARDCORE all over the place? What is his gimmick and why does he behave like that? Is his hardcore gimmick due to the fact that he was raised with Pit Fighters and taught to wrestle at a early age? Is his gimmick of trying to eliminate bad guys due to the fact that he was raised in a family of police officers ? 'The Franchise' Axel Rotten , Jim 'The Hitman' Jarret , Rock Cold Steve Johnson , ALL THESE NAMES DON'T WORK! Avoid these names as well as wrestler alias like 'The Peoples Champion' Jim Hart , The Rattlesnake , The Gravedigger etc etc I'm 8 feet and 600 lbs so I'll roll over everyone? Nah mate , fedheads HATE giants. I'm 7'2 feet and 212 lbs / I'm 5'8' and 505 lbs . This one is stupider than the above. Let's think of a guy who is 7'2. Big Show? Well is he 212 lbs? NO , let's think of guy near seven feet , Kane and Undertaker. Are they ANYWHERE near 212 lbs? Let's think of a about 5'8 . Tazz. Is he 505 lbs , which is incidentally somewhat near Big Show's weight? My wrestler's weight is 450 lbs and my move list is as below 1. Swanton Bomb 2. Shooting Star Press 3. 450 Splash 4. Tornado DDT 5. Moonsault Can a 450 lbs person (think Rishiki) do any of these? NO My wrestler's weight is 180lbs and my move list is as below 1. Powerbomb 2. Chokeslam 3. Running Powerslam 4. T-Bone Suplex 5. Body Press Drop Can a 180 lbs person (think Taka) lift up a 300 lbs person ? NO .Except a few like Taz [glares at Wallie (inside joke)] Swearwords. Unless your wrestler is a redneck don't use them. When Hogan was a true face (back in late 80s) did you ever hear him use the four letter word? Also , if you have to use them ,preferably [BEEP] them out , though it is not necessary as it is an 'underground' federation Make your opponent look stupid. NO, I don't mean use him in your flash, didn't you know it was not allowed? You defeated Wrestler#1 but he lost to Wrestler#1 , point it out. Hmmm.... I think that is it all. --------- Let's hear yours , send them at [detail withheld] ii. What are strats? Ans) Strats are short for strategies. Strats help the matchwriters to get the general feel of your wrestlers. In a close contest in roleplaying , strats may be the deciding factor. This makes the cards much more interesting , as well as gives the matchwriters a lot of material to put in Though we can't guarantee that the strat will be used , perhaps a part of it may be used , perhaps the whole of it, if it is super - good , and perhaps none at all. The different types of strats are as follows: a. Match Strategy What you want your wrestler do in a match, like focusing on a part of a body , trying to lay his hands on a weapon etc etc b. Counter Strategy Used to counter the match strategy , like focusing on the wrestler's back so that he cannot do his trademark move , the powerbomb etc c. Post Match Strategy Which includes what would your wrestler do if he loses and what would he do if he wins. d. Run Ins Is when a wrestler not physically in a match disrupts the match. Usually used if you are feuding with one or more of the wrestlers in the match. e. Save Used to counter run-ins . Usually if you have an alliance with one or more wrestler in the match f. Sneak Attacks Can happen anywhere , when wrestler is doing an interview , when he is arriving backstage etc etc iii. How do I go working on an angle? Ans) You can get the wrestler email , ICQ or AIM on request from us , by mailing us , (we don't put contact info as our handlers don't like mail from other federations asking them to join) and can discuss angles with them. If you don't have AOL Instant Messenger (AIM) or/and ICQ , you get them free from the web at ICQ.com and AOL.com . They are great instant messaging tools. If you are out of ideas , you can get in contact with us at our official mail address. [[detail withheld]] iv. What are oncards? Ans) Oncards are flashes/roleplays appearing on the weekly shows We cannot guarantee they will be used , especially if we get way too many oncards , but 90% of the time, they shall be used. They are three different types of oncards a. In Ring Interviews Like The Rock's promos of WWF , Shane Douglas of WCW etc etc . Please don't just simply talk about how badly you are gonna beat them. Rip on them , make them look stupid , draw more heat to yourselfs etc etc. Also please note , that the interview must be done in the ring only , there is no such 'backstage' arena in the temporarily ring setups which can be pulled on within minutes . b. Commercials Have your wrestler to advertise some 'underworld/'illegal' product , like Cigarettes , and drugs ;-) or some fighting academy etc etc . These draw more attention to your wrestlers. V.
