Faw Shopzone provides perfect clothing for you to save and edit . Step 1 - Right Click on the clothing and save it . Step 2 - Go onto Paint and OPEN the picture Step 3 - Edit the piece of clothing and save Step 4 - Send it as an attachment or post it on the OOC Board . Step 5 -Recieve your piece of clothings FAW Rating out of 10 . Step 6 - Look at what you have won ! Rating 1 = Nothing 2 = We will put your site on the links page 3 = A Complimentary on the house Geocities Website for you to continue with 4 = We will make a custom graphic for you of your wrestler 5 = A spoiler for the next Faw show ! 6 = A free message board dedicated to you . 7 = A wrestling game 8 = a wrestling game & special Mentioning on FAW News . 9 = A wrestling game & special mentioning on FAW News & faw Rumours . 10 = tell us Your site of your favourite site , and we will put a link to it on the homepage , and you get as many wrestling games as you want !
