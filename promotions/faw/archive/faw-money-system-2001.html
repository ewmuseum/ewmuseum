FAW Money System (2001)

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
HOME The Money System is run on a VERY basic system: The contract length you sign determines the amount of money you receive: The Roleplays you do (quality and quantity) The Matches you win or lose Lose money by certain requests (see below)! Money is lost in various ways: 1) If you don't roleplay fo 1 week or more you lose money. 2) If you request a certain stipulation in a match it costs you and if it is not granted you lose more money. 3) If you request a title match it costs you and if it is not granted you lose more money. 4) To make a backstage/sneak attack it costs money. .1) If you make 5 roleplays in 1 week of good quality and length you earn money. 2) If you win a match you win money. 3) If you win title matches or number 1 contender matches, you earn even more money. 4) If you win the Royal Rumble or King of the Ring, you win even MORE money.
