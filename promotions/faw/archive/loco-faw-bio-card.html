Move list

Wrestling Style: Powerhouse (Tends to concentrate on the opponents head, neck and back.) Set Up Move: Loco Driver (Rikishi Driver ) Finisher: Loco Death Drop (Test Big Boot in the turnbuckle into a Inverted Brainbuster DDT from the top rope) Other Moves: Loco Falling Neckbreaker - (In a back to back posistion Loco grabs the head of his opponent with both hands like a reverse Stone Cold Stunner and he dig's his right shoulder into the back of the bottom of the back of their neck. He then snaps there head backwards with both his hands and thrusts his shoulder up into the neck even further forcing their neck backwards against his huge right shoulder, as he does that he jumps to gain extra leverage and force. Loco lands on his backside. Often used as a reveral.)Hangmans DDT - (Loco lifts the opponent up so their legs are either side of his head like in a Powerbomb posistion. The opponent hangs down upside down with their head left just above the ground facing away from Loco, Loco then sharply drops to his knees and forces the opponents head and neck into the ground)Choke slamSpiral PowerbombDouble Powerbomb PinCobra Clutch SlamFull Nelson SlamSambo SuplexSpearSpinebusterTilt-A-Whirl SlamPendalum BackbreakerBig BootMudhole StompingHeadbutt To Opponents FaceLow BlowChair Shot To Opponents HeadTaunts & PosesWhen lieing down after being knocked down Loco will often lay down on his back motionless and then suddenly sit up as if nothing has happened. (Like Kane does in WWE.)When a match is coming to an end and Loco is on the recieving end of things he will suddenly 'Go Loco.' Loco builds up strength and a rush of adrenaline whilst refusing to go down. He bursts to life, blows seem to make no effect on him, he then goes on a powerful and brutal attack. (Similar to Hulk Hogans 'Hulking Up')Slow cut throat taunt in a turnbuckle with an opponent nearby. (To signal The Loco Death Drop)Loco will often attempt to spit at an opponent as well as flip them the bird with both of his hands.
