Career history

Jeff Harris was born in 1968 in Las Vegas Nevada the home of the rich. He was the only child in his family which made him quite spoilt and got just about everything he wanted, at the age of three his mother and father, Melissa and Richard Harris started to collect for college funds so Jeff could go out and be the best gambler out there.At the age of 5 Jeff became very interested in wrestling and started attending house shows for the WWF and other promotions watching wrestlers like Hulk Hogan, Macho Man, The Giant. By the time he really took it seriously he was 9 and asked his parents if he could be a wrestler when he grew up, his parents was behind him 100 per cent hoping he would forget about it be time he grew up. At the age of eleven he started up his own back yard wrestling federation and got a few friends to try it out and play on the trampoline.When Jeff was sixteen he started to take wrestling classes in Nevada under the a great superstar named Kenneth Storm, he taught him all sorts of wrestling moves and saw potential in him. When he turned 17 and was ready for college Kenneth Storm asked his parents if he could be taken on a wrestling course in Miami Florida, his parents were to worried to let him go that far away, but Jeff pleaded with them to let him go and wrestle, his parents dis allowed it, Mike Storm was disappointed with the parents for not letting Jeff follow his dream, so after the silent treatment for two weeks his parents finally agreed to let him go and become a superstar like he has seen on the television.After the first year of his course he was already the biggest and brightest student in the class, with the help of his good friend and mentor Kenneth Storm he learnt many amazing wrestling moves used all over the world. As he turned 19 years old he was to appear on his first 'trial' match on a wrestling dark show at MJW which meant Miami Junior Wrestling, but Jeff's first match was indeed a scary one as his first match was a hardcore match which he never tried before. He went against someone called Paul Howorth a.k.a Armageddon, the match was over in a few minutes with Jeff being totally obliterated. He was bleeding from the mouth, nose, head, arm and neck. Jeff had doubts about ever wrestling again. A few weeks later he had another match in MJW against a wrestler that has had allot of accreditation and was 4 years older than Jeff named Peter Howorth the brother of Armageddon, Jeff used his quickness and shiftiness to get in control of the match, as Peter Howorth tried doing a powerbomb Jeff escaped and locked in a move no one has ever seen before Jeff called it the Jazzy Jackhammer, Peter tapped out naming Jeff the winner.With that move in his arsenal Jeff was on an undefeated streak with 13 wins, the people who went to watch Jeff absolutely loved him. By the age of 21 he left the school and got a contract with the Supreme Wrestling Alliance, but only after 1 month of being there his heart was broken when his best friend and mentor Kenneth Storm died in a fatal car crash. Jeff attended his funeral and didn't wrestle for like a year, to add to more pain and suffering his mom and dad split up, his father re-married a few months later to a Mrs. Jane Howorth, mother of two. Jeff was surprised when he saw the two people he had his first two matches against. Peter and Jeff didn't get along all that well but Paul advised Jeff to get back into wrestling and dedicate it to Kenneth Storm so he did, he was in a few indy feds before he got a contract to the XWF in 1993, he was never seen throughout the whole 5 months he was contracted until he faced Peter Howorth who was now called Neu Generation for a heavyweight title match, he beat Neu to become a one time XWF world champion but then lost it to him a few weeks on just before XWF closed.In 1994 Jeff got a contract from a big time wrestling promotion called VWWA, Virtual World Wrestling Alliance, he actually had a hard time there with the staff especially the President. He won the hardcore championship in the first month he was there beating a lot of his opponents with the Chain Reaction, he then lost it to a cruiserweight called Blak n Blu, who told Jeff about his father dieing in a car crash, Jeff asked his name and Blak n Blu said his name was Mike Storm and that his own name was Sean Storm. Jeff was surprised to see the son of his mentor, they became good friends as Jeff beat Angel in a hardcore match to become two time hardcore championship, the Owner/President of the company hated Jeff every day because he had more glory than the world champion him self Sheriff Hunter, When people saw Jeff they screamed with joy but when Sheriff came out he was ignored hoping to see Jeff again. A few months later Jeff lost the hardcore title but won the Internet title the following week, the Owner of the company screwed Jeff out of the title by losing to the former world champion Sheriff Hunter, the crowd started booing like never before at the so called face Sheriff Hunter. The commissioner had enough and was on the side of Jeff giving him two title matches in one week, he won the hardcore title for the third time on Monday and then beat Sheriff Hunter for the Internet title on the Friday, having two titles was not easy especially when the Prez tried screwing him in every match. Jeff then said he would give up both titles if he was given a world title shot against the new world champion Stone Cold Stu (Cousin to the Howorth's) The Owner agreed as he thought he could screw Jeff for the last time, after a long and hard battle Jeff locked in the Jazzy Jackhammer, The Owner ran down the ramp and got into the ring ready to hit Jeff but the Commish ran in also and suplexed the Owner out of the ring, Stu tapped out allowing Jeff to win the world title, The following night the Owner was really pissed off and fired Jeff and the Commissioner from the VWWA, Many superstars found it unfair so 98% of the roster quit including Sheriff Hunter.In July of 1996 Jeff was contracted to the UCWF as their Vice President, this company was only open for a maximum of a month due to money problems, the promotion included wrestlers like, Sheriff Hunter, Neu Generation, Blak n Blu, Angel and Armageddon. After doing a good job at being a vice prez he went to the TUFWF when UCWF closed. Under contract of Nick Tyers he was hired as the Vice Prez and did a good job until complications arose. Nick and Jeff had a lot of arguments until Jeff made the biggest mistake of his career and hit Nick square in the mouth causing him to get fired and was never to step on TUFWF soil again. The TUFWF closed a few months on and Neu Generation opened up his own Promotion called NWWF, the Neu World Wrestling Federation. Quite a few feuds broke out between Neu and Jeff, Jeff and Neu became multiple NWWF champions, Jeff was fired as Vice President but then screwed Neu into giving him a commissioner job, he became the TV and tag team champion in his first ever tag team match winning the NWWF titles with Armageddon, a wrestler was then hired by Jeff called Blaze(Not WFW's Blaze) who Neu feared throughout the XWF, with Jeff, Armageddon and Blaze against Neu he was pretty screwed. The company ended a few months later with Neu becoming TV Champion once again before the NWWF closed and merged with a new XWF. The Owner [name withheld] and Jeff Harris, Jeff didn't take much notice in the XWF but quit when Neu was fired as an Owner. Jeff tried his own hand at running a promotion called Supreme Wrestling Federation, the SWF didn't run for very long as the company ran into money problems and closed with Jeff being the SWF world heavyweight Champion beating The Terminator.This took him to 1998, after all the schooling, teaching and wrestling lessons Jeff was just a floater, jumping to Indy fed to Indy fed. He finally got a contract in a big time promotion, called the XPCW, the president was very fond of Jeff's wrestling talents and read his past. he was given a shot against the world champion Mike Richardson, he actually beat him much to the crowds delight, but it was a non title match. Jeff thought what he could have accomplished if it was a world title match, the President James Thomson saw a lot of skill in Jeff as he won the hardcore title. Sean Storm was then brought in from the ECW. The two teamed up and beat the tag team champions for tag gold. Jeff lost the hardcore title and re won it in one night due to the 24/7 rule, in 2000 Jeff applied for the XCW but was put on their waiting list for six months before it closed. XPCW was in heavy money loss and cancelled half of it's shows, a few weeks later the WFW opened and Jeff saw that Neu Generation and Sean Storm was a part of the roster. Three days later Jeff was released from the XPCW.Jeff entered the WFW as a big surprise to Neu Generation, the two men had a feud and faced each other for the SWF world title, Jeff lost the match making the match a huge upset for him. The two men made up and became friends making a stable called the Extreme Nature with Neu himself and Blak n Blu. It didn't last long as Neu Generation retired and Blak n Blu was involved in a car crash. But before it ended Jeff's first important match was against the former XCW champion Jay Moss for a Canadian title shot, a big surprise for Jeff as he beat the Canadian champion and became the new WFW Canadian Champion. With being left on his own he dominated the singles division beating a lot of former XCW wrestlers. He finally lost his Canadian title to a man named Jon Brewer two months on, but on account to illegal drug abuse Jon was released from the WFW and Jeff had the Canadian title again. The next month he lost it. He then formed a new stable with former XCW superstars, Scott Blaze, Adam Masterson and his new partner Stunning Stan. After losing the Canadian title to Hunnish, the week after at the Pay Per View, That F'n Stunning won the tag team titles, later on in the night Jeff totally destroyed Bryan Deas to win the United States title, but as Jeff found out what a true faggot Stan really was Stan was fired from the WFW and became tag champions with Adam Masterson. After about a month Adam Masterson quit WFW, Jeff then lost the US title to Ravage, but one month later he win the IC title from Ravage and joins Bad Omens!The most recent happenings of Jeff Harris' career have been a long and winding road, after an argument with the staff in November 2001 Jeff Harris left the WFW and had an OOC feud with Jay Moss, Jeff Harris went to FAW and took part in the xCw/FAW invasion angle, Jeff Harris lost a lot of credibility from continous jumps from one side to the other, he patched things up with Jay Moss and all seemed to be well, after the invasion ended Jeff Harris went to xCw and won the Hardcore title, soon after he captured the tag team titles with 'Unforgiven' Thomas Dsousa, xCw ended horribly and Jeff Harris was left with no where else to go, for months on end he had been waiting for offers, he joined the WFW with Jay Moss but it didn't last long as Jeff Harris had numerous OOC fights with staff members and wrestlers, he went BACK to FAW and seemed to be doing quite well until a shot to the shoulder by a chair from The British Bomber changed things for life. Jeff Harris' shoulder was a mess and bone fragments fragmented into his neck/spine and the doctors told him that he'd never wrestle again however Jeff Harris proved them all wrong by spending nearly all of his money on operations. He received his first offer from a man called Ryan Gibson to join a promotion called 'The Warehouse' Jeff Harris who wasn't fully recovered yet jumped at the idea. He was put to the test and passed it on the first event as he beat 7 people in one night to become the Warehouse world champion. This was a huge mental message to Jeff Harris saying that his career wasn't over, he went on to beat Killah Xtreme and ID the next week in a tag match but after that the promotion closed, no one knows why... Perhaps money problems anyway Jeff Harris got a shoulder scan because it was giving him problems in tW, the doctors said that it is more messed up as ever and he shouldn't wrestle anymore. Jeff Harris however decided otherwise, he sold his house in Las Vegas and bought an apartment somewhere in New Jersey, he spent the money he had on a shoulder operation. Jeff Harris came back soon after as '009' Jeff Harris a secret agent ripping off the antics of James Bond and Austin Powers the gimmick was funny to the people who watched him although yet again OOC problems occurred and he was forced to leave the WFW. For five to six months Jeff Harris has had nothing to do with wrestling at all except for the time he tried it out in PWA but The Phantom Pedophile fired him coz Phantom Pedophile is a fucking faggot! Anyway, a new year has passed and The Golden Nine hopes to try out his luck in FAW, so keep watch!
