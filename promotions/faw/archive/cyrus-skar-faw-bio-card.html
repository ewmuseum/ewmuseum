Career history

Cyrus's mother died whilst giving birth to him. His dad committed suicide soon after Cyrus was born. Two brothers who took him in as their own looked after him. They taught him how to fend for himself as they lived on the streets. Cyrus was not educated like normal children, he learnt by watching television through shop windows and reading books in the library.He was told by one of the brothers he was built for fighting. This gave Cyrus the idea of fighting in pubs for money. He fought hard until he had enough money to get to America. Whilst over there he continued to fight until he had enough money to join a wrestling school.He did well learning all there was about wrestling. But he was dismissed, because of his split personalities. One minute he was cool, calm and collected. The next moment he was vicious and angry. One day he snapped whilst he was practicing some moves with his training buddy. He pounded him to within an inch of his life. He was thrown out of the school for not being able to control his twin personalities. Cyrus then wrestled in a few federations getting a few years of experience and ending many careers. He joint FAW and won the European Title, he kept the belt till it was retired. He then won the US Title to become the first ever US Champion. He has never been pinned or made to submit in his entire wrestling career. He has lost one match through DQ.
