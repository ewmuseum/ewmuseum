Skipster 4/14/02 Wrestling Name: Skipster Real Name: [name withheld] Nickname(s): Skippy Age: 23 Height: 6' 4" Weight: 275 pounds From: San Francisco California Alignment: Face Status: Active Manager(s): JC Ice Trademark Move: Powerbomb Finishing Move: Face Slam Description: Powerbomb Into X-Factor Entrance Theme Music: Metallica "Ride the Lightning" Entrance: The arena goes dark and then lightning bolts start to fly across the arena. "Ride the Lightning" by Metallica hits the P.A. and Skipster comes out from behind the curtains holding a nightstick. Skipster makes his way down the entrance ramp and then slides into the ring and awaits his opponent. Video: The screen is black, with flashes of silver lightning bolts fly across the FuryTron. The word "Skipster" is shown in grey lettering in the center and then it goes to clips of when Skipster won the World Title, when he got hit by a car when he was fighting Pyro in a street match and then a clip of the Furious Alliance Stable. FAW Career Highlights FAW World Champion (1) FAW International Champion (1) FAW Hardcore Champion (2) FAW Tag Team Champion (1) (w/ Omega Fro)(1) Biography Skipster grew up in Overland Park, Kansas with 2 younger brothers. Skipster has live in Kansas his entire life, living in Topeka, Lenexa, and Overland Park. Skipster was the oldest child in the family and the oldest grandchild on both sides of the family. Skipster did get a few more privileges than anyone else in his family just because he was the first. Skipster started school when he was about 5. Skipster is a great athlete. He played football, baseball and ran track in high school. His freshman year of high school, he wrestled for the school, but the quit after two weeks because it wasn't any fun. Skipster had a neighbor that would always have backyard wrestling match, about on a week when we were able to. Skipster always wrestled with them. He was the youngest wrestler out there but still won a ton of matches. In High school, Skipster lifted a ton of weights. The weight room was his second home. He wanted to get stronger because he wanted to become a great football star, but it never happened. Skipster didn't start playing football till the 8th grade and so he wasn't as far as people who have been playing for a while. But that didn't stop him, he kept lifting and playing throughout high school. But then high school was over and Skipster had to go to college. Skipster went to The University of Missouri for college and he stayed there for 4 years, playing football with friends. He focused a lot on his school work, he wanted to get a good education because he wasn't for sure of what he was going to do with his life just yet. It was his junior year in college before he finally decided that he wanted to be a wrestler and when he got out of college. He applied for the UwA and was accepted. But then a few months later it closed down. That is when Skipster went to the UCW. Skipster has wrestler for 3 feds now, all of them closing, except for the FAW. In the UCW, Skipster taged up with JC Ice, whom is now his manager here in the FAW. Skipster was the Hardcore Champion and tag team Champs in the UCW. Skipster first heard about the FAW from Pyro, who at the time was in the UCW with him and when it closed down, Pyro suggested that he comes to the FAW, so Skipster did so and has been here for 10 months now and will be here for a while. Skipster has done many great things in FAW. He has held every title except the European. He has been world title material almost the entire time he has been in FAW. FAW is his home now, and Skipster isn't going to be leaving any time soon. Source: FAW
