FAW Deceit 2001 Predictions

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Main Event - SpaWn (c) vs JLC - World Title Match - Iron Man Match The main event, the hardest match to predict, always has been, always will be, but who do the FAW fans think it will be? Well, it could be either of these 2 guys, they fight for 1 whole hour Sunday night. The man with the most pins and/or submissions wins. SpaWn's LAST KILL versus JLC's LOOPLEX. The winner: SpaWn Has held the title for so long the fans think that he will beat their favourite JLC. Crimson Lord (c) vs The Great One - Hardcore Title Match - The Sickness 1st Fall: Cage 2nd Fall: Glass And Thumbtacked Tables 3rd Fall: Ladder. 2 out of 3 falls. The all important match of the night. The match which has been building up for weeks. The Great One looking to win back what he has worked for over all the years, while Crimson Lord wants to show that he can wih it. The winner: The Great One He has showed alot of great skill in the past weeks and the critics, staff and fans think that he will win the big one versus Crimson Lord. Tom The Bomb vs Lisa "Da Sex" Bomb & TBA - Handicap Match - Stipulation TBA Tom and Lisa, they meet in the ring for the first time in eternity. Who will win the match though, who is Lisa's partner? Well, that will be the deciding factor in the match. The winner: Lisa & Partner Lisa has the edge with a 2 on 1 advantage. Chris Thunder (c) vs Spider - International Title Match - Anywhere Fall Match Anywhere Falls matchup. Spider and Thunder, Intercontinental Title. This will be a huge matchup with alot surrounding it. The winner will take the title and the spoils. Everyone seems to have one man in mind for the win. The winner: Spider The xCw killer can only win this one. He has beat champions in the past week but no gold, now gets his shot at gold! Stunning Stan (c) vs The Mortician (c) vs Bryan Deas - xCw World & Intercontinental Titles - Cage Match 3 men ALL in one solid steel cage. Stunning Stan, The Mortician, Bryan Deas. What a match, the winner takes both titles away. The losers take away nothing. The winner: Stunning Stan The current xCw World Champion goes into the match with his title on the line and wants to beat both of these guys, he will be focused and will want to take 2 xCw titles away. Axl Young vs "The Untouchable" Jon Thomas (c) - xCw US Title They walked into FAW and went striaght at each other. Now they meet in the ring. xCw US Title on the line, only held a few days by Jon Thomas and he has to defend it against his enemy at Deceit. The winner:Jon Thomas Because Axl has not had much experience and Jon Thomas is off the back of the US Title win, but can he continue it? Skipster vs sYn - No DQ Skipster and sYn will fight it out one on one, in a no DQ matchup. The winner: Skipster He is a FAW great, World Title shots in his career and no-one can see Skipster losing to sYn. Morte vs Shady-O-Grady - European Title #1 Contenders Match Both rookies are searching for gold, and now, the winner gets to take on the FAW European Champion, Jeff Harris. That match will happen a week on Tuesday at FAW IS WAR. What a match this will be. The winner: Morte A great signing by FAW and a great addition to the roster who is always looking to be the best. British Bomber vs The Omega Fro British Bomber will fight The Omega Fro, the returning Fro. The winner: British Bomber The Omega Fro has had no wrestling since leaving FAW and now in his return match he fights British Bomber, who is after FAW gold.....a good match, but surely only one outcome.
