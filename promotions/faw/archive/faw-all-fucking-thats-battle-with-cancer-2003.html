All Fucking That's Battle With Cancer

Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
All Fucking That’s Battle With Cancer As I sit here and write this I am constantly trying to speak hoping that my mouth will spurt out the words “fuck sake I need a damn cigarette”. The reason I can’t speak, as many of you know is because two weeks ago I was diagnosed with cancer of the larynx. Little did I know though that it’d be the worst two weeks of my entire life! As July was nearing its end and August was just on the horizon I started to get a lump on just by throat, I went to my doctor to get it checked out and after some extensive tests it was revealed that I had gotten larynx cancer. Now I rarely get scared but that news scared the shit out of me, whom wouldn’t it scare? The news that there is a possibility that I could die isn’t something to be joked about; the cancer was the cause of the lump on my throat… Also known as a tumor, I had many an X-Ray at the hospital and they told me it was the size of a golf ball and I hadn’t swallowed any golf balls that week so I was pretty damn worried. The doctor at the hospital also told me that getting larynx cancer at my age was a rarity because it mostly happens to people that have had a history of cancer or over the age of 50, smoking can cause it but it’s not a high risk, most smokers get lung cancer rather than larynx cancer I guess I was just one of those unlucky ones. After several days absent on the Internet many people were getting pretty worried, I was only able to tell one person about my condition no one else was online that day. During those days absent I had been in hospital getting tests done and it was during those days that I lost the ability to speak. If you were like the librarian that works in the Chesterfield Library then losing the ability to speak wouldn’t be that much of that bother but everyone who knows me know I am a very open minded, outspoken person. Losing my speech was like losing my golden nine™ and plus the fact that I run a nightclub in the town centre, I can hardly tell my staff what to do via notepad and paper can I? During that day I was online I posted a message on the DWA forums asking to speak to Heather, Strike, Humus and Jimmy T the four people I am closest to online. I turned off the computer and decided to go downstairs for a while to go sit and think, although as I got downstairs I collapsed, I lost consciousness. My step bro [name withheld] was shit scared because he knew I had cancer, he was the first person I told, he called for an ambulance and off I was to hospital. When I came to I was in a hospital bed connected to an IV and had one of those cool oxygen masks on, I soon found out that the reason I passed out was because the cancer already started to spread to my lungs and it was making it hard for me to breath (duh!), I didn’t notice anything but if you are only able to take in a small amount of air for a long time if you’re going to pass out. You’re going to drop your glass of ribena all over the floor. So while I was laying helpless in a bed with my step bro, step sis, my real brother and sister, my step dad and my mother all close (it felt like a family reunion =/) the doctor told me that he can get rid of the cancer quickly by doing an operation where he takes out my larynx, then the cancer that is spreading to my lungs can be dealt with more easily… At that moment I thought, “I can’t go on without speaking for the rest of my life, I’m not using a fucking voice box machine thing...” So I motioned for a pad and a pen and wrote, “If you take out my larynx, I’ll take out yours.” Meaning there was no chance in hell he was going to take my ability to speak. The doctor and my family didn’t think it was funny but Peter was laughing his ass off! The doctor came up with another solution though and that was chemotherapy, by giving me drugs that contain anticancer cells it could clear up the cancer within a matter of days. I went for that option naturally. I was giving a fact sheet on what chemotherapy can do which said: What Can Chemotherapy Do? Depending on the type of cancer and how advanced it is, chemotherapy can be used for different goals: To cure the cancer. Cancer is considered cured when the patient remains free of evidence of cancer cells. To control the cancer. This is done by keeping the cancer from spreading; slowing the cancer's growth; and killing cancer cells that may have spread to other parts of the body from the original tumor. To relieve symptoms that the cancer may cause. Relieving symptoms such as pain can help patients live more comfortably. It didn’t really relieve the pain because let me tell you, I was in a lot of fucking pain for the best part of two weeks. So as the chemotherapy started I was stuck in hospital until the cancer was cleared luckily they caught it before it got really serious… Or that’s what they said; I’d think losing the ability to speak would be pretty serious. Is Chemotherapy Used With Other Treatments? Sometimes chemotherapy is the only treatment a patient receives. More often, however, chemotherapy is used in addition to surgery, radiation therapy, and/or biological therapy to: Shrink a tumor before surgery or radiation therapy. This is called neo-adjuvant therapy. Help destroy any cancer cells that may remain after surgery and/or radiation therapy. This is called adjuvant chemotherapy. Make radiation therapy and biological therapy work better. Help destroy cancer if it recurs or has spread to other parts of the body from the original tumor. In addition to Chemotherapy, they did operate on me to get rid of the tumor; it felt somewhat better once the tumor was removed. While being in a hospital bed Peter would visit every day to tell me that people from FAW, DWA, PIW all wished me well, also some messages some people gave Peter to tell me, everyone’s comments were much appreciated and it made me smile and it made me feel happy knowing that everyone had my back. After Chemotherapy treatment was going on for days I was finally told that the cancer in my throat had disappeared, they then used radiation therapy to get rid of the cancer that was spreading to my lungs, apparently I had a “psychotic episode” in the hospital too, I don’t remember a thing about it but apparently I was in a state of depression for days, I had a lot to deal with recently, breaking my ankle, my best friend dying, my father dying about a year and a half ago, I guess my mind though I was next, I feel alright now though just extremely exhausted, I don’t stay depressed for very long… While having treatment there was a person in the opposite room to me (I had my private room w00t!) who would always keep banging his door, I came to the conclusion that he was a spasticated retarded version of Bryan Deas. There were many times when I felt like kicking his door in and telling him to shut the fuck up but you know… I had that whole lack of voice thing. It was on Monday when I was told the cancer had been treated, I should be able to get the ability to speak back in about three weeks time as the cells in my body are repairing the damage cancer has done. It’s still hard to breath at times but I can get by, the doctors wanted to keep me in until Saturday for observation but we finally came to an agreement that I’d go home and come in for check ups every morning because let’s face it… I have had enough of hospitals for now, it was great to get online and talk to people online again, it was also great to sleep in my own bed, I am just glad to be home, the recovery process is going to be slow but All Fucking That will make a full recovery, no one should ever go through the shit I’ve gone through I now understand people’s pain when they go through any kind of cancer, if it’s cancer it’s fatal and if you survive, you’re a winner in my book, but one question always bugs me ...Why do people keep asking me if I thank God because I was saved by him from cancer? If there is indeed a God then he was the one who did this to me in the first place, and I overcame such an ordeal meaning All Fucking That is above the Lord. ...No offence to you religious types =) But anyway that is what I have been through over the past two weeks. If you have read the whole thing then I thank you for your time. Let’s hope there won’t be a next time, - [name withheld].
