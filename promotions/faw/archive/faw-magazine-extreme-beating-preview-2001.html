FAW Magazine — Extreme Beating Preview (2001)

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
William Regal will defend his newly won European gold against HHH - This match has been hyped for about two weeks , but it took an amazing turn on Friday when Bill Clinton "threw back" Regals Euro belt at him and told him if he wanted a Universal title shot he would have to beat HHH . If he loses he will not be allowed a Universal title shot until the next ppv ! Expect this match to have a big impact on the night , and with two other matches .........oh i cant tell you any more . Extreme and Brahma Bull and DDP will battle it out for the Hardcore championship - Not much gossip about this one , the only thing that made the fans even RECOGNISE this match is when Extreme defeated DDP (Brahma Bull -ref) on Friday Night Fear .That was a good match , expect this to be much , much better . Jon The Bomb will defend his International Title against his brother and enemy the Undertaker - The biggest match in the history of faw involving the biggest storyline in the whole of FAW . The mystery woman will be revealed , the brothers will go at it 1 on 1 . Ive heard rumours that this match will have the biggest result in FAW and lead to utter carnage at April Avalanche . The 3 bigs will reign supreme , Storyline , Match & Result . Bill Clinton will take on The Big Show in a cage - This match was announced on Fear , it was built up by the Anywhere Fall match on Friday Night Fear , which ended by The Big Show being attacked by Someone . Jon the bomb helped out Bill earlier on the event , rumours speculate it was his Mystery Woman that attacked Show . HaHaKlown will defend the World Title Sean Mason & Majin Buu - HaHaKlown will make this match a dream come true . The Laughing Scaffolding match makes its way to FAW in Extremist style . Sean Mason is expected to make a big impact . Lex Luger will defend his Universal title against Hannibal Lector , Steve Austin and British Bulldog in a battle royal .-The highlight of the show , nothing else to say ...that damn good . Dogg will be in action against JLC -"The match of the Ego's ", according to William Hill . We have Dogg , the man that blasted into faw with a reply to every message , but that is nothing compared to JLC , the thickest wrestlers in sports entertainment today , he thinks hes the whole mile . SicCoKlown will be in a match, and it will be a number 1 contenders match for the European Title and he will be against Kurt Angle and The Defiant - No hype at all , wrestlers are all poor and never speak , apart from the Defiant .
