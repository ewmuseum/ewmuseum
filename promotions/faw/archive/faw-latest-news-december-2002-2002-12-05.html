FAW Latest News, December 2002

5 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
l a t e s t n e w s Wednesday, December 4, 2002 Sunday 8th December Panic Preview Join us on December 8th as FAW hosts 'PANIC' live from the sold out Robarts Arena! After the shocking announcement last week signalling the initiation of the Fatal Alliance- and the hellish brawl that erupted because of it- what will Jason Roberts have to say to William C. Myers, and what is Myers agenda? Also: who are the other group leaders? All will be revealed. In Addition, the first ever Fatal Alliance matches have been decided. The card reads as follows. MAIN EVENT: Scott "TRP" Costella (2) vs Spawn (1) Lee Smith(3) vs Renegade(4) Xing(4) vs Ken Ruslan(1) The Omega Fro (2) vs The Great One (3) XiN(1) vs Sabatash(2) vs Rzarector(3) vs Officer Lawrence McGruff(4) Join us this Sunday, as FAW explodes into your living room with 'PANIC'! ---------------------------- Tuesday, December 3, 2002 Panic Airs, New Look FAW! FAW returned with a bang tonight with the delayed viewing of Panic, who won the first ever RaGE in a CAGE? What happened? Did Bomber return? Take a look by clicking the link to the right of this story! ----------------------------
