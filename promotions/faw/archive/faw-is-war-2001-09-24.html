FAW IS WAR, 24 September 2001

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
:::FAW IS WAR Intro The cameras switch on with the FAW logo on and then the pyros explode and the cameras zoom in on the top of the ramp and then pans round the crowd. The cameras then cut to the announce table. Michael Cole: Welcome to FAW IS WAR. We are just 24 hours off the back of the biggest PPV in FAW history. Let us take you back to the highlights of the night last night. The Furytron switches on, we see the night kick off with the XWW Title being put on the line. We then cut to the Hardcore Match where Kemosabi is screwed outta the title and Crimson Lord is the brand new champion. Then we see Deadly D successfully defend his European Title and then JLC winning his scaffolding match and retaining. Then it shows Thunder retain his XWW World Title but also win the Tag Team Titles with Gee Monie. Then we see highlights from the main event. We then see SpaWn lift the title and the tribute for PyRo, but in the middle of that Great One gives him GREATNESS through the commentary table after the match and then he gets booed by the fans. Tony Blair: What a night that truly was and tonight there have been lots of rumours of something big happening in FAW right here tonight. Michael Cole: Well this night is off right now! :::Rick Bruiser vs Ben Juventud WINNER: Ben Juventud :::Chris Johnson vs "The Ruffneck" Jason McAndrew vs Jay Cannon WINNER: Jay Cannon via chair shots when the referee was down after Cannon knocked him down by accident. :::Commercial Break :::Cut Backstage The cameras cut backstage to where Skipster is walking down the hall and it looks like he is on the way out to the ring when suddenly The Great One runs up behind him and takes him out. The Great One: It is booked Skippy! Me and you tonight's main event, see you out there. The scene fades. :::Rocky 'The 8 ball' Rodriguez vs Johnny Havok WINNER: Double Count Out :::Commercial Break :::Skipster vs The Great One OOC: We join the match at The Great One delivering a DDT to Skipster... TG1 kicks Skipster in the mid-section and follows up with a DDT. Just then, Dark Generation members, JLC runs to the ring, as Great One exits the ring, oblivious that the former tag team champions are behind him... The referee looks on, and things change just as quickly again, when Chris Thunder hits the referee from behind for no reason at all. He then launches himself off the apron onto his fellow Dark Gen member!! Tony Blair: What in the Joel?!? Thunder jumping onto a Dark Generation member?!? A Thunder-Great One alliance?!? Michael Cole: We are witnessing this before our very eyes!! Thunder turning on Dark Generation!! Skipster gets up and looks on, as Gee Monie now hits the ring and turns round Skipster, giving him a sidewalk slam!! Michael Cole: Wait a minute!! Gee Monie having a crushing affect on Skipster!! And look on the outside!! The Great One walking away from Thunder's destruction on JLC and Spider!! Gee waves The Great One into the ring, who turns to Skipster and gives him Greatness!! Spike and British Bomber slowly walk down the ramp... Spike runs on, and pulls Gee out the ring!! Bomber circles the ring as the referee witnesses the cover by The Great One... 1...... 2...... Bomber pulls The Great One out the ring!! Tony Blair: British Bomber stopping the count!! Michael Cole: And now, Thunder getting back into the ring!! Thunder goes under the bottom rope, knocks down the referee for a second time, and begins to gesture at Skipster, when suddenly, The Commish, Dragon, SpaWn and Crimson Lord attack Spike!! Gee recovers and he points to Thunder... The men run to the ring, looking like they're going to attack Thunder, but they begin to stomp on Skipster!! On the outside, TG1 launches Bomber into the steel steps beside JLC... TG1 steps up onto the top rope, and orders the men to continue the assault on Skipster!! Michael Cole: I don't believe this!! Sections of the crowd are now BOOING The Great One!! Who is now showing his sadistic side!! But wait!! The Mortician!! He's here to make the save!! The Mortician fails in his attempt to save Skipster, as the Great One's "servants" immediately jump him... Thunder and Gee collect the broken bodies on the outside and shove them in the ring... The other 8 men line them up in order on the ground... Thunder grabs JLC by the hair and gives him THE STORMER!! The other men look on with big smiles on their faces!! Gee executes another sidewalk slam to Skipster!! The Great One gives GREATNESS to The Mortician!!!! Gee attacks Bomber with KASH MONEY KICK!! SpaWn gives Spike a taste of THE LAST KILL!! Tony Blair: What a master plan by these men!! Look who we have in there!! Pure talent!! Michael Cole: Yes, I have to agree with you, Tony. Enormous talent in there!! But the shocker of the evening is The Great One revealing his REAL intentions towards the "dark" side, if you want to call it that... And I don't think they're stopping there!! Look where Gee's going!! Gee Monie grabs a steel chair from the outside and launches it into the ring... Chris Thunder gives Bomber a STORMER for good measure... The Great One waves Gee back in to witness one of the injured men taking a beating from this newly formed alliance... The match isn't even officially over yet, as the referee still hasn't even signalled for the bell, as he's still out!! TG1 points to Skipster... Dragon picks him up... Gee gives Great One the chair... AND WHAM!! Skipster falls flat!! Four men take a turnbuckle each... One man takes one side of the ring with Thunder and Gee sharing the one facing the announcers desk, gesturing at Bill and Cole... The Great One stands in the ring, goading the boos and jeers he is recieving... Dragon recovers the referee as The Great One places one foot on Skipster... 1........ 2.......... 3!!! Tony Blair: The Great one takes the win!! Look at them!! Who the hell do they think they are?!? Michael Cole: A new alliance has been formed!! The Great One turns his back on the fans!! What does this mean for FAW?!? We'll see you on Panic!! The telecast ends showing The Great One staring, with weary eyes. The other men lift him up onto their shoulders as the show ends....
