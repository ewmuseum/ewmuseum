FAW Panic #5, June 2002

June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Opening Skit Mario: Welcome to Panic! "Highway To Hell" by ACDC hits and the British Bomber comes out to cheers from the fans, he is wearing his usual wrestling attire and he walks down the ramp to huge pops. He climbs onto the apron and poses to cheers from the fans. He climbs into the ring and is handed a mic. British Bomber: A few weeks ago I said this was a funny business, you never knew what could happen......jeez I sure bit myself in the ass there. You know over the weeks I have seen Kathryn return then Tom, and I know she is trying to end my career, finish me off for good because if she doesnt then she will feel she has failed and will not stop till she does do the job but she wont get the chance. The fans give a mixed reaction to this. British Bomber: I suppose what I am trying to say is that the dream is over, the run is over.....my career is over...... The fans boo this. British Bomber: Tonight I will not let KVT and Tom get to me because I am retiring, I have had enough, game over. My contract runs out after Diabolical Day, after the night of the tournament, or PPV itself. I can't do this no more, I have entertained to my full extent and I feel I cant go on no longer. So this is my final goodbye to the fans, the company, the wrestlers and the staff. Goodbye...... "In Too Deep" by Sum 41 hits and Tom walks out onto the stage. Tom The Bomb: Bomber you aint going anywhere! See tonight you have a match against a mystery opponent then on Sunday you go into the main event.......no, not with Fro, Skipster, Spawn and Klown but against ME! British Bomber: Damn Tom, I gave over a year to this company now your taking away my right to retire? Tom The Bomb: Shut up! See you face me Bomber but Kathryn will also be the referee for the match! But you know what, if you lose, then you keep your mouth shut on TV until Diabolical Day after you retire! British Bomber: Thats fine Tom, but see there are going to be stipulations of my own, you name Kathryn as your referee then I have one in mind for me too so there shall be 2 referee's! But Tom, if you lose, and by god you shall then I reveal my secret to the world on Panic three days later. "Highway To Hell" by ACDC hits and Tom looks down at Bomber pissed while Bomber just gives a smug smile. Match 1 - Apoc vs Neo Neo’s music was heard through the PA and Neo went to the ring. Systematic would next blast through the PA system and Apocalypse would go to the ring. When Apocalypse got into the ring the match started. Both men looked at each other. After both men tied up Apoc sent Neo off the ropes. Neo slips out of the ring and Apoc watches. Apoc walks to Neo as Neo looks under the ring. Apoc is just about to grab Neo but Neo uses a tray to keep Apoc away. Neo goes into the ring with the tray in hand. Apoc gets up Neo hits him with the tray again. 1 2 kick out. Neo tries to hit Apoc again but Apoc uses a drop toe hold. Apoc gets up and so does Neo. Apoc picks up the tray and tries to hit Neo with it but he misses. Neo goes for a school boy rollup pin. 1 2 kick out. Apoc gets up and closelines Neo. 1 2 kick out. Apoc picks up the tray and hits Neo with it. 1 2 kick out. Apoc picks up Neo and DDTS him. 1 2 kick out. Apoc looks at the ref. Neo gets up and rolls up Apoc. 1 2 kick out. Both men get up and both men close line each other. When both men get up Neo close lines Apoc once, twice and a third time. Neo picks up the tray and knocks Apoc down. 1 2 kick out. Neo picks up Apoc and triesw to DDT him but Apoc turns it into a Northern Lights suplex. This counts the three. Match 2 - The Omega Fro vs Joe La Fontaine Match Not Written - Both men DQ'ed due to it turning to a Hardcore match Match 3 - Spawn vs HaHaKlown - Ref=Skipster Spawn pummels Ha Ha Klown into the turnbuckle. And then starts throwing fists at his head. He pulls away as Skipster pulls him back. Both men look at each other knowing that at Collision Course both men will face each other. HHK pulls himself up and waits for Spawn to turn around, and when he does HHK throws out a kick straight into his chest and then one straight into his chin. Spawn finally goes down after shots to the head from a few punches, and then HHK whips himself off the ropes and throws himself down with a splash. HHK finally covers and Skipster hits quickly to the floor as he strokes...... 1...... 2...... Spawn gets his shoulder up, but he's still not all there. HHK sits up and gets on a knee still a little winded after his splash. He blows Spawn to the head with a spin kick and smiles at the pain of Spawn. He picks him up and Spawn fires back with punches and does a snapmare to Ha Ha Klown then a neckbreaker. Spawn limps back up and then picks up HHK but The Klown see's him in the side of his eye. And ducks and comes back with one big spear and then picks him again and does a downward ddt and covers.... 1...... 2...... Kickout! Ha Ha Klown grinds his teeth, and quickly shouts at Skippy saying it should have finished. HHK picks up Spawn and fires with an elbow and then a fist to the stomach of him. He whips himself off the ropes and drives an elbow into the back of the head of Spawn and then throws him over the top rope. With Skipster now looking down on Spawn at the entrance ramp and counting up to ten. HHK suddenly rolls out of the ring and grabs the ring bell. Spawn pulls himself back into the ring and Skipster for some reason pulls Spawn up. Skipster smiling with the crowd throws Spawn forward where HHK has the ring bell concealed and smacks Spawn down with it. The crowd ring out boo's and boo's. HHK laughs and Skipster doesn't know what he has done. Ha Ha kLown then picks up Spawn taking him over with a snap suplex, then whiping him straight into the turnbuckle. HHK then lifts Spawn up onto the top turnbuckle and sets him up.... BAMMMMMMMMMMMM! Skipster just looks around and decides to have a little fun with each other, he grabs HHK and takes him by the waist and drives off with a powerbomb. BAMMMMMMMMMMMMM! HHK falls to the floor and Spawn falls in hand and both men collapse in the middle of the ring. Skipster the referee laughs at both competitors and decides this match has gone on to long, and he quickly starts to count. As he gets up to five, both competitors start to move and Skippy decides to slide to the outside, before grabbing a chair and rolling back into the ring. Keeping count and both men get upto their knees at seven. As both men are now up and dazed, Skipster looks at them both and raises the chair above his head. The crowd though seem to get louder and louder as a mysterious figger pulls himself into the ring and grabs the chair from Skipster's grip. The camera turns in view and shows it's Shifty back in FAW! Both men quickly look at each other and smile. Skippy lets go of the chair and Shifty quickly rockets a chair shot to both mens heads. He laughs out loud, and decides he wants it over quickly and tells Skippy to keep counting up to the ten count! BAM! BAM! BAM! BAM! Again Shifty fires a few chair shots into the backs of Spawn and HHK, before finally turning away and exiting the ring. The scene fades with Spawn and HHK laying down in the ring, blood tricking from their head and the paramedics quickly running down to help. And Skipster looking at Shifty knodding his head in approval at what just happend. Match 4 - The British Bomber vs ???? - Refs - KVT & Tom The Bomb Match Not Written - Mystery Man was Chris Thunder, after a series of dodgy referee calls Thunder won the match. Summary Its real simple guys, you dont roleplay then you get fucking shit results. Apoc was the only one that roleplayed for the event, and the rest of you didnt bother, although Fro contacted Dade and gave a explaination for himself. Next time this happens, only 1 roleplay from one person then those that do not roleplay shall be warned, and if it happens again then we fire you and job your character.
