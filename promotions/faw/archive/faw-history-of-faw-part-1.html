The History of FAW, Part 1 Thanks for clicking, this should be a good column to write, thanks again for Einstein for helping to write this column Jan 23rd 2001- Jon Da Bombs fed Bad Ass Wrestling Federation shuts down. He is invited to take part in several feds but opts to start a fed with [name withheld] and ### Martin. Jon is messing about and comes up with the perfect idea, each prez will be a PRESIDENT! Sadam Hussein, Bill Clinton and George Bush. Problem. [name withheld] wants to be his gay ass of a character Mister Cool or the big show. He is removed out of the staff. Tom and Jon look at angelfire, geocities and finally decide upon homestead to be their host. Site designed by Jon. Tom and Jon spend literally days recruiting and get about 6 members in a day. First card held DAY after fed opens, simmed on Zeus Pro. Ive kinda forgotten the rest till a few months later. 4 months later. FAW Roleplay Board is sensationally spammed about 1000000000000000000000000000 times. #1 Suspect, old friend [name withheld]. [More on this later] FAW is on a temporary break, opens 2 hours later [!] now allows real and fake wrestlers. Get 15 members in a day, same site design, a few changes. Tom and Jon meet a nice guy called HaHaKlown on MSN. We get chatting and he is the first rper on the new rp board. He's a good rper and Jon looks up to him as god. FAWs running great. One day an app comes in from a guy called 'The Phantom' or some crap. He has applied before, except he was Ken Shamrock in the old FAW. Tom recognises his email as someone who once attempted and succeeded in hacking him. If my brother wasn't such a jackass FAW wouldn't be alive now. I was talking to the guy and he kept on asking me what his p/w was. I went offline 'cos my brother needed the computer. Went back on and tom told me everything, just seconds and he could have been spamming the FAW Boards. Turns out [name withheld] didn't spam us after all then. Close call. Anyway, somewhere in this, night of disaster. I think it was in March or something. I am talking to this guy called Mat. Go offline. Come back on. Tom and [name withheld] are going absolutely nuts, tom says hes gonna get hacked cos of me, [name withheld] says he is going to hack me, I really DIDN'T have any clue at all then. I became the most hated man in FAW for twenty four hours. I came back 2 days later and permanently blocked [name withheld]. I got rid of MSN [That's why a lot of the oldies, like JLC, Didn't really know me till like May, even though they'd been in FAW since February.] Me and Tom, AMAZINGLY, ran a fed without any kind of communication except email for get this, a month. Then, I got AIM and we continued to talk. I got back MSN about 3 months later, I have never since spoken to the two people who were my best ever friends on the net, A [name withheld] and [name withheld]. FAW joins ICWF Interfed and is proclaimed the worst fed of the lot. ICWF crumbles. Anyway..quite a few weeks later HaHaKlown and JLC feud- OOC. HaHaKlown challenges JLC to a loser leaves town, both IC and OOC. HHK threatens to leave, so the shot is granted. JLC loses. HaHaKlown is appointed as FAW President. HHK lets JLC rejoin weeks later, stopping PWA from snatching up the heel from hell. New ceo, CEO Richard. He asked me 400000 times to be a Prez and even once got into the site and changes the password, so we couldn't get in. I can honestly say, I've never met such scum in my life as low as Richard. You Suck. HHK Is asked to write matches [we aren't using Zeus Pro anymore] and fails to do so every single time. He disappears off the face of the earth. Reappears weeks later saying 'Hey dood its summer in america, im taking a break'. Says its possible of a return. Damn, still waiting .
