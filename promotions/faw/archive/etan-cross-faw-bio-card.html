Handler Information Handler Name: [name withheld] AIM Name: [detail withheld] MSN Name: [detail withheld] Bio ALIGNMENT: Face with hill impulses, very cocky, very arrogant, and brash loves to hear the fans chant his name, and he LOVES to talk the fans love to hear the sound of his voice as much as he does. Hes VERY brutial and loves to hurt his oponentsGIMMICK / PERSONALITY: Etan Cross is a master at mindgames. He likes to talk ALOT and thinks everyone loves the sound of his voice as much as he does. He is considered crazy, and unorthodox, because of the things he says, he is also a true jokester, and loves to try to embarrass and taunt his enemies outside the ring to make them angry, but then when he gets them in the ring it is an all out attack with submission moves, and suplex's. Etan is a Suplex Machine, and love to toss his opponet around the ring like a ragdoll with his different suplex's. He is a true Submissions expert, who is highly skilled in Muay Thai, Judo, karate, kempo and kickboxing, as his stand up assault, but his favorite attacks come form taking his opponents to the mat and using his knowledge of submission wrestling, sambo, shootfighting, and Brazilian jujitsu, to make them submit, another of his favorite sounds is someone tapping out!! STYLE: A TRUE technically sound, fighter, and technician. Etan has been trained for over 17 years to use his head and take apart his opponent, standing up with his amazing striking ability, then use one of his many suplex's to slam his opponent to the mat, and use his knowledge of submissions to take them out, but he has a quick temper and loves to brawl, and beat his opponent's bloody, has 16 years of Pitfighting, Shootfighting, and freestyle wrestling experience. Has competed in Rings, NHB, Gongs, and the World Combat Jujitsu Championships, and has a professional Record of 37-2-2. His two draws were against Rickson Gracie, and Todd Madeina, and his two losses were against Don 'the Predator' Frye, and Kazushi Sakuraba both losses were split judges decisions. Used to fighting in a cage or octagon, but has adapted his style for the wrestling ring. Blaze has a martial arts background that dates back to age 11 when she started taking Karate at a local Dojo in Richmond. After her and Etan Crossed paths he trained her in Judo, Kickboxing, and grappling/shootfighting. When Etan Cross Wrestled for Pride, and NHB, Blaze would help him train for his competition. They later together opened the Cross Roads Training Center in Black Mountain NC. Together with some other trainers they Strive to be the best and help train other up and coming Grapplers and Shootfighters. This is also the Location where Dee Cross was taught to fight. Etan and Blaze over saw his training and made sure he was ready for action when he graduated. Favorite Matches: Street fighting rules, Hardcore rules, Anything goes, and his Signature Match is a Burning Cross Suplex coffen death Match. This is where the ring is wrapped in barbed wire, and thumbtacks are spread around the ring, Two Coffins filled with broken pieces of glass sit in the middle of the ring, and there are 4 burning cross's on the tops of the corners of the ring. The only way to win is to suplex your opponent into one of the coffins. Blaze just loves ANYTHING where there is a lot of Blood!MOVES: T-bone suplex, Dragon suplex, Hardway suplex, Rolling German suplex, Cobra Clutch suplex from the back (that is when you lock up a cobra clutch, and slam your opponent on his head. MAIN FINISHER: The Killer Cross. Fisher 2: The Thrill Kill.
