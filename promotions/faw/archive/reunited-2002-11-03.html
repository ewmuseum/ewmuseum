Reunited? Date: 03/11/02 Show: Distortion 20 (Renegade is seen walking along a corridor backstage at the Adelphia Coliseum, making his way towards his dressing room to pick up his Tag Team title before heading off to the ring. As he opens the door he sees a familiar figure sitting down in front of him. Sitting on one chair and his feet up on another. It is ‘The Real Prodigy’ Scott Costella. Renegade: Hey man. How’s it going? Scott: Yeah I’m doing good thanks mate. Look, I’ve just stopped by to thank you for covering for me over the last couple of weeks. Renegade: Hey no probs man. You know I’d do anything for an old friend. And future tag team partner. (They both laugh) Scott: Thanks again man. I owe you one. Renegade: Hey forget it. You brought me to FAW so now were even. Now if you don’t mind, I’ve got a tag title to defend. (Renegade picks his belt up off a side bench, tosses it over his shoulder and starts to walk out the door) Renegade: Oh and by the way Scott. Scott: Yeah. Renegade: I can’t wait until we tag up again. The era of the extreme will rock! Scott: Same here. I can’t wait. (Renegade walks out the dressing room and heads off back up the corridor. The camera turns round and fixes on Scott. A sly smile creeps across his face)
