CHAMPIONS World Champion The Omega Fro International Champion The Mortician European Champion Spawn Hardcore Champion Skipster Tag Team Champions Dark Generation (Spider & JLC) AS OF 15th AUGUST 2001 The American Psycho The American Psycho deserves to here as a wrestler of the week.. Why you ask.. Well it's simple.. He IS The new World Champion.. He defeated Pyro, who has been the top man in the fed for nice mount of time... Not to menchin that his determination has gotten everything he has today.. So I say good job and congratulations, and also keep up the good work... Skipster The second man this week is Skipster.. As of late Skipster has shown some great hidden talent that most did'nt know about.. He is also on a bit of a streak.. And him joining the newly created Furious Alliance can only propell him even more then has already... Obly time will tell when this talented wrestler will get FAW gold... JLC Our third and final wrestler of the week is JLC.. JLC has shown some tremendous strives in the past couple of weeks.. His current loyalty to the Dark Generation can only help in the future.. He is currently one half of the FAW Tag Team Champions.. And as the fued between the Alliance and Dark Generation heats up. He will undoubtedly see more and more action...
