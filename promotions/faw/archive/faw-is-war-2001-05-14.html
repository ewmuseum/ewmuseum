[Fireworks start up . The FAW IS WAR Tune sang by Charlotte Church silently plays into the arena . Then Limp Bizkit take over with 'rollin' . Suddenly 'Bawitdaba' By Kid Rock blasts into the arena and the crowd get off their feet for the World Champion Deadman . He calls for silence and begins to speak .] Deadman : Spike..Thursday Night . You ruined my day Spike . Im gonna make this pure and simple for you so you can understand . ME AND YOU . BREAKAWAY . On Thursday Night , its me and Zoe vs you and amy . PHHK : Yes I know , the only thing that would make it better was if HaHaKlown was in a match . PC : I have got to agree with you there . And this match is going to be action-packed aswell . [Blades music plays into the arena . The crowd give a mixed reaction to this living legend who comes down to the ring wearing all black . He steps into the ring and prepares to fight British Bomber . ] PC : Who do you think will win this one ? PHHK : British Bomber PC : Me too . Oh they have started . British Bomber hits a big boot . He lifts up Blade and nails a over-head suplex drop . He throws Blade against the ropes . Blade is choking on the ropes . PHHK : Blade fights back with a forearm blow to the chest . Blade nails a neckbreaker . British Bomber reaches for the ropes and pulls himself up using them . British Bomber crawls under the ropes . He grabs a chair . The fans are booing now . Reverse Brainbuster onto a chair by Bomber on Blade ! That was amazing . PC : Yes it was . Blade takes a knock . That was unfortunate Klown . PHHK : All these KlownaHolix didn't come here to see such vile fighting , they came here to see me . Then British Bomber slaughtered Insane Pain with that steel chair . And we are still going on here . Insane Pain is being stretchered off . The two are being separated . Insane Pain will be able to limp through his match tonight but he will then be layed off until after Breakaway unfortunately . It looks like CEO Richard may have something to say about this . [CEO Richard appears on the screen .] CEO Richard : Bomber.you just earned yourself a match at Breakaway . Not against me . Not against anyone else in FAW . But against a little friend of mine . His name ? I will not tell you . But come June 10th ? The games are over . [The cameras follow CEO Richard . He opens a door . ] CEO Richard : I told them about you . Come 10th June , its time for you to make your debut here in FAW . Oh No that's right , have you been here before ? Oh Never mind I will ask you later . PHHK : Woah ! Richard has got a little secret . Lets have a look at the Breakaway Card so far.. ]Promotional tape of Breakaway rolls .] 'YOU CAN TAKE MY MONEY BUT NOT MY GAIN YOU CAN TAKE MY MONEY BUT IM GONNA BEAT YOUR ASS UNTIL YOU GO INSANE' Instrumental 'And when we meet Things are gonna be weird You are gonna be beaten ONE TWO THREE FOUR FIVE AND .BANG' [In that , a video was rolled of the highlights between Spike and Deadman .] 'S-P-I-K-E vs Deadman , a FAW Show , A FAW Wrestler and a madman..In A Glass Tables Hell In A Cell Iron Man Match for the FAW World Title . And then , British Bomber vs a mystery opponent in a match set-up by CEO Richard' PHHK : We could have more matches on the way tonight . [X-Factors music plays across and into the arena . Justin Credible wins . But British Bulldog hits Justin with a chair and lands the running powerslam on him . News Just in , Justin Credible vs British Bulldog in a Dog Kennel Match at Breakaway Hardcore Match Damo Muzzler vs Gordone Junior Clinton: What a match this is going to be! Damo Muzzler has really been turning up the heat these past few weeks, and he's definitely the star of the future! Cole: He certainly is! You Only Get What You Give by the New Radicals hits, and the International Champion Damo Muzzler appears at the top of the ramp, with his International belt. He also has a chair Cole: He certainly looks like he’ll do the business! Clinton: Yeah, Gordone isn’t to experienced here Damo gets into the ring and holds his belt up in the air, as My Generation by Limp Bizkit hits, and Gordone appears, with a trolley full of weapons, ranging from stop signs to lamps! Clinton: Gordone has a lot of weapons! Cole: And I’m sure he wont be afraid to use them! Clinton: Well, Gordone is tossing all the weapons in the ring, and Damo picks up the trash can lid, and hits Gordone! Cole: Gordone looks stunned here, and this 367 pounder Damo, already seems to be in control! He pulls Gordone into the ring, and hoists him up high and hits a big jackhammer suplex. Gordone is down already, and Damo is searching for a weapon! Clinton: And he has found a trashcan now, and sets it up in the corner turnbuckle! Cole: Yeah! Whats he gonna do? Well, Damo is picking up Gordone, and he places him in the corner, with the can behind him! He heads over to the other side of the ring, and runs at Gordone, and Damo delivers a huge splash!! Clinton: Did you see the impact! Cole: Yeah, Gordone just crumbled under that! Damo wants more! He climbs over the top rope. And gets a table! Oh no! Clinton: He throws it in the ring, and it lands on Gordone, who is out of it! Cole: Damo is back in now, and he sets up the table. Now, he's picking Gordone up, and he is climbing the turnbuckle. Damo has Gordone on the top rope in a DDT position. Clinton: And now he's falling! Bang! Through the table! Through the table! Cole: Man! There goes the referee for the count! REF: 1..2..3! Cole: Its over! First attempted pinfall and Damo has won it! A simple walkover for the big man! Clinton: How did such a big guy do that top rope DDT? Cole: I don’t know, it was amazing! We have to take a break now, and we have some interesting news! Commercial Break – FAW Divas- Striping to the bone! Video, priced £18.89 (18) Clinton: And we are back! That video is really great! Cole: Who cares, the news is Big Red Machine has been suspended for lack of roleplaying! We are not sure if it is just suspension, or he has been fired, or just that he hasn’t turned up! But Prez Hussain has told us he wont be fighting because of lack of roleplaying! Clinton: So I guess its just The Ripper VS Mystic X! Cole: Damn right, and here comes Jack The Ripper!
