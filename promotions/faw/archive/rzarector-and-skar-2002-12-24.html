Rzarector and Skar

24 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Rzarector and Skar Date: 24.12.2002 Show: PPV - Evil Intentions ::The camera cuts backstage to show a locker room. The door swings open to show the unlikeliest of pairings. Cyrus Skar is stood talking to Rzarector. They both turn to see the camera. Cyrus smiles a sick smile as the camera pans to show Rzarector smiling as well...:: Rzarector: A long time ago, I used to hate some people for there actions. Everyone in the FAW was the same....all demonic. Yet now I have found a new ally, who has promised to turn from his evil ways and join me in taking towards the light. Me and Cyrus Skar, an unlikely pair, well.........the fact is that we are not that unlikely. For God has intended us to join when he sent me messages in my dreams last night. Cyrus: My friend here may talk as a religious man but our beliefs are both the same. We've formed and alliance if you like to dominate FAW. With us two standing together I don't see any one man who can take us down. We are two of the best wrestlers in FAW and together we shan't be taken down. Rzarector: *Hisses* You see, things have been changing since everyone has been leaving. People by the likes of Spawn, Bomber, and Scott Costella. They all left this great ring for another federation. FAW made them who they are, and once they're gone who will they be? *Hisses* No one. For we made them. Now FAW will make us. Cyrus: FAW has some of the best talent in the world. I found that out when I left last time to try and find better competition. It was impossible. That's also a problem as well for FAW, these people Bomber, Spawn, Costella, they all think they're too good for FAW. Well they're not, there isn't a company better than FAW. Spawn, Bomber and Costella are the exceptions to the rule. They are not 'talented' superstars they are egotistical freaks who think they are superior to everybody else when they are not. We understand we are the best and we know that nobody will be able to beat us now we are aligned as one almighty force. Rzarector: Speaking of this force, tonight I have my shot at becoming the new World Champion. I will go up against the Great One, Spawn, Foamy, and Renegade. Renegade my former tag partner is my favorite in winning this match. Spawn is just a deadbeat. He won’t get anywhere in this match. As for Foamy and The Great One, I'm looking forward to our little fight tonight. And you can't forget about Lee Smith , he is a great competitor, he surprised me when I saw his videos air. He is in really great shape. Yet he has a slight anger problem. That is probably from his demons when he was in his drinking era. God has came to me in all my dreams this past week, and he has told me that I will go into this match the man with the most heart, yet I will not be the man who is going for the World Title. I will just be the man who is there to win the World Title. There is a difference. I will be there just to win it. So when I get commercial offers, they will all hear my message. The Word of God is my message. And now Cyrus Skar is going to be hearing it. For Armageddon is upon us. I feel sorry for anyone who does not hear this warning. Cyrus and me, well I'm going to preach, and he is going to keep it real in the ring. ::The camera fades out.::
