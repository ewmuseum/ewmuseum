Front Office Home Rules Appliaction Members Roster Champions Forums Sunday, June 01, 2003 Breakaway PPV - @ 11:27 PST Unfortunately, we have to announce that todays PPV will be pushed back to Wednesday night. This past week we have installed the backstage script and perhaps upon reflection we chose the wrong time to make such a radical change to the infrastructure of FAW. It has come to the staffs attention that alot of people have had problems accessing this backstage area and due to Jamie having computer troubles this past week he has been unable to correct the issues in a manner which would allow everyone to have a fair chance going into this major event in terms of their ability to RP. After much thought we have decided to postpone the event allowing everyone to have until TUESDAY MIDNIGHT GMT (6pm CST) to roleplay for an event which could well shape the coming months here in FAW since so much is at stake. In another announcement, FAW has taken the decision to put the FAW Continental Title on the line in the forthcoming Turmoil Match scheduled for the PPV. This decision means the likes of Sniper, Costella, Bomber, Juvian and The President of the Altered States now have the chance to capture one of FAW's most prestigous honours. Once again apoligies for the inconvenience, but I hope you can appreciate that FAW prides itself on being fair to all and doing anything but postponing this event would hinder the chances of some through no fault of their own. Wednesday, May 14, 2003 The Commission Is Born... - @ 20:23 PST In the previous few days FAW completed the capture of former OWA and OSW star Syndicate. OWA for those who are not familiar, is a now extinct brand which gave birth to such names as Big Grumpy and Malcolm King who later went on to achieve much success within professional wrestling. What is perhaps most interesting about his signing to FAW is his links with other previous signing Julius Monroe. In recent days the suggestion has been made that these two names could be set to come together to give rise to a stable calling themselves 'The Commission'. With a friendship already apparent during the previous Panic between Juvian Ramorez and Monroe, the news that Monroe has Syndicate also as a friend to call upon will send shockwaves through a FAW locker room they both see as vunerable and ready for the taking. Both Monroe and Syndicate are heavily motivated by their past; whats more certain though is neither man will hold back on their conquest to win respect from the 'hood'. Sunday, May 04, 2003 The FAW Revolving Door - @ 17:52 PST Expect a number of announcements relating to the roster situation in FAW to be made tommorrow morning. Rumours circling the internet suggest two departures, followed by two arrivals. FAW is keen to build a committed roster and these two signings should further add to those who are already apart of the dynamic roster we see today. New Prez Found - @ 08:09 PST A new President may have been found is coming from HQ stay tuned for more infomation. Friday, May 02, 2003 Smith Agrees Settlement with Former Employers - @ 17:33 PST Rumour has it that "Legendary" Lee Smith, previously of Furiously Addictive Wrestling fame, has finally agreed a settlement with the company. Smith caused outrage after April Avalanche when he walked out on the then-ailing federation, which has since seen an almighty revival thanks to the heroics of Jeff Harris and co. Smith is thought to have paid off his contract using money from his backpocket so that he can be free from the federation and have a fresh start. Top of his list is said to be T.W.A.T.S., a federation which Jeff Harris said earlier to today is "Where he belongs..." once again sparking rumours of an off-screen spat between the two.
