FAW Card Archive

Panic, 20 September 2001

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
OOC: Sorry about NO matches being written but when you see Summer Slaughter you will know why! And yes, these results are by no means the greatest and then again not the worst in FAW history, but they will undoubtedly be better by Panic and easily better at SS! See ya at Summer Slaughter... :::Panic Intro The pyros explode at the top of the ramp in the entrance area as the Panic entrance music begins and the fans get to their feet and wave their many signs in the crowd. The cameras go round and then into the ring where a big pyro on each turnbuckle goes off and then the cameras zoom into the announcers table where Tony Blair and Michael Cole are sitting. Tony Blair: Welcome to Panic. I am Tony Blair and alongside me is Michael Cole. We are just 3 days away from Summer Slaughter, the biggest PPV extravaganza ever to hit FAW and no doubt will live up to expectations with a Last Dance match, scaffolding match, TLC match, and of course that amazing main event that no-one thought possible. Michael Cole: Definitely right Tony, we never imagined it, and of course earlier this week, we saw the main event for tonight booked and we will see British Bomber, Chris Thunder and the XWW Champion fight in a 3 way hell in a cell match for that newly resurected XWW world Title belt, and that was of course by the champion, The Great One. Tony Blair: Yes we did Michael, and we never expected it to ever come out again as it has done here tonight. And the main event will be a great one indeed, just as we expect the whole of the show here tonight to be. And do not forget that our hearts and prayers are still with those victims and their families of the New York incident only just over a week ago tonight. Michael Cole: Well said Tony, and it is definitely true. And this night looks set to kick off. :::The Great One Promo "Break Stuff" by Limp Bizkit hits and The Great One walks out to the top of the ramp. He gets to the ring and then climbs up the steps and gets into the ring via the ropes. He climbs 1 turnbuckle then the next and then does all 4 and the fans are taking pictures and cheering the XWW champion. He throws his belt over his shoulder and grabs a mic from the edge of the ring. The Great One: FINALLY GREATNESS HAS COME BACK TO BOSTON, MASSACHUSETTS. The crowd goes wild for The Great One as he stops and looks into the crowd and then continues with his speech. The Great One: Tonight, your XWW champion takes on 2 opponents who call themselves not by their real names, the jackasses, but they call themselves British Bomber and Chris Thunder. Those 2 guys are the 2 whom I chose to be my opponent's tonight and yes I wanted them inside a steel cage as you can see hanging above me right now. The Great One looks up and smiles. The Great One: And them 2 guys will of course face each other once more on Sunday night but in Tag Team competition for those Tag Team Titles. And also at Summer Slaughter I go one on one with Natas the ass! We will go one on one inside the ring in a Great One speciality, A LAST DANCE MATCH! My creation, my speciality, my night! Summer Slaughter will be the night you see The Great One at his best, Sunday will be the night you see an ass-whoopin' like you have never ever seen in your entire lives! When we arrive in the Pontiac Silverdome Stadium you will see a Great One you have never seen, a Great One whom will whip ass over all competitor's and then I will sometime take FAW gold just as I have this XWW gold over my shoulder right now. "Highway To Hell" suddenly blasts out as the fans boo as Bomber walks out and down to the ring and then goes head to head with Great One and then grabs a mic of his own and begins to speak directly to the Great One. British Bomber: Great One, tonight is the night you lose that XWW gold, and you will lose it to the man who put XWW out of business, you will take it and you will shine it up REAL nice and then you will hand it over to me, you will hand it over too British Bomber! FAW will witness tonight a new era, an era of BOMBING! Tonight marks a great night in my career, where I become XWW champion! But Great One, about Summer Slaughter, you see you may face Natas, who will obviously beat your ass, well I face Chris Thund... Before the sentence ends "One Step Closer" hits and Chris Thunder walks out to a chorus of boos and he walks down to the ring and gets into the ring and then puts a mic to his mouth. Chris Thunder: Bomber, Great One, would you both please shut the hell up? You are giving every member of this community here in Boston a headache, well, more like neck cranck coz they are sleeping already! But Chris Thunder is here to save the day and to tell both of you that you will not leave this Sunday with a belt, neither of you, coz tonight I take that title off of you Great One and Sunday I take your title Bomber! The Great One raises one arm and speaks. The Great One: I will see you 2 in the ring later tonight, IF YA...... In mid-sentence Natas jumps over the security railings and gets into the ring, The Great One escapes out of the ring just as Natas hits both Bomber and Thunder over the head with a chair and then swings for Great One overt the ropes as he makes his way up the entrance ramp and then "Papercut" hits and Natas trash talks in the middle of the ring as the fans boo. :::Cut To Dark Generation Locker Room The cameras cut backstage where JLC and Gee are in their locker room. JLC: Did you see what Natas just did to Thunder? He is your tag partner Gee, what are you gonna do about it? I didn;t see you out there helping him, you gotta take control of the situation, you gotta show that you mean business, both to Bomber and to Natas, that you will NOT back down from a fight. Gee Monie: You know what JLC, you are right and I intend to do that and I will show them who is the best tag team in FAW and in FAW history! JLC smiles and nods as Gee leaves the room angrily. :::Commercial Break :::During The Break The during the break logo comes up and the cameras appear to cut to the parking lot where Gee Monie is walking with a mean look on his face and more importantly a sledge hammer in his right hand and a glove on his left hand. He walks up to a truck with the number plate "NATA3". He lifts the sledgehammer and repeatedly brings it down onto the bonnet of the car and then works all over the windscreen and the front and then the back eventually. Natas comes running out to his truck as Gee runs in the other direction, Natas stops chasing him and starts to look agonizingly at his car and then from behind Chris Thunder comes and hits the STORMER on Natas and leaves him in the middle of the parking lot, meets up with Gee and the 2 walk off laughing sinisterly. :::Gee Monie vs Andrew “The Slater” Ferguson WINNER: Gee Monie via the KASH MONEY KICK to the face and then Gee and Thunder celebrated in the ring. :::Cut To Backstage The cameras cut backstage to where Chimera is walking to his locker room with his Hardcore Title over his shoulder. He then looks behind him and from out of no-where runs Kemosabi, one of his Summer Slaughter opponents. He has a referee with him and Kemosabi then throws Chimera into the side of a nearby dumpster and then covers him...1...2...CRIMSON LORD PULLS HIM OFF...the 3 men then brawl and the referee's then come and break up the brawl in the backstage area. :::Jimbo Jnr vs Bone Crusher WINNER: Jimbo Jnr. via Jimbo Jacknife to Bone Crusher and then then as Bone Crusher tries to fight back after the match he gets another for his efforts. :::Commercial Break :::Dragon vs Kemosabi WINNER: Kemosabi via a chair shot to the head after Chimera tried to distract the ref which allowed Kemosabi to use the chair. :::Cut To Backstage The cmaeras cut backstage to where The Mortician is walking through the backstage area when suddenly JLC runs out and attacks him. He hits him repeatedly over the head and then he throws The Mortician through a garage door and then follows him out onto the streets where JLC throws The Mortician into a shop window as he crashes through it. JLC then leaves as The Mortician tries to rise to his feet. :::Saviour vs Richard Storm WINNER: Saviour via Storm DQ after using the L-HW Title belt. :::Commercial Break :::Cut To Power Of Two Locker Room The cameras cut to the Power Of Two locker room where Spike and Bomber are talking. British Bomber: Sunday we will retain those titles Spike. Spike: I just hope you are okay for the match! You took a pretty hard blow earlier and now you are going into a match in a cage and taking on Great One and Thunder in a 3 way match! British Bomber: You know you have no need to worry Spike, I will win the title tonight and we will go on to retain our titles Sunday! Then Thunder and Gee rush into the room and overturn everything in the room and then start throwing Power Of Two around like they are worthless. They throw them into tables, sofas and then they finally leave them lying in a pool of blood in their bathroom section of their locker room. :::Deadly D vs The Mortician vs Crimson Lord WINNER: Crimson Lord after The Mortician was took out by JLC again and then Deadly D got took out by Dragon and then he hit the BloodLust and got the cover on The Mortician as the men brawled on the outside and in the ring and Crimson left delighted by the win. :::SpaWn Interview The cameras cut backstage to where William Hague is standing with SpaWn. William Hague: thanks for joining me SpaWn, now tonight, infact next you face Smoke and then you go into that 4 way in just 3 days, and it is your first sniff at World Title gold, and what do you plan to do in that match? SpaWn: For a start, tonight, I will kick this guys ass tonight, I am the better wrestler and the better guy! I will go out there and do what I do best, but this Sunday I will do even more and give it 200% of my effort! Sunday night you will see SpaWn even clearer than before! SpaWn will emerge the victor and I will give it ALL I have to win the title. William Hague: Well with PyRo gone do you think it will be easier to get yourself noticed here in FAW? SpaWn: Well yes coz it will not be all PyRo, PyRo, PyRo! But I will miss him, he brought me into this great federation and I didn;t want him to leave this way, but he has and we have to deal with it. We will all get through it! And this week will not be an easy task for me but I will take it and I will do my best with it! I can guarentee that on Sunday night a new FAW World Champion will be crowned by the name of SPAWN! SpaWn leaves. :::SpaWn vs Smoke WINNER: SpaWn via a beating and then a clothesline from hell to finish it after Smoke tried to hit a bulldog. :::Commercial Break :::The Great One ( c ) vs Chris Thunder vs British Bomber - XWW World Title Match - Cage Match WINNER AND NEW XWW WORLD CHAMPION: Chris Thunder via Bomber getting beaten into GREATNESS by Great One and then the STORMER getting hit on Great One by Thunder and Thunder escaped the cage and then as Great One chased after him and his belt Great One got hit by Natas from the side and then got put through a snooker table from the very top of the ramp.
