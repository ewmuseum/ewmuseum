Real Name: Dave Hurst Birthdate: 12-1-70 Hometown: Brighton, England Height: 6'2' Nicknames: EDH, The Craziest f*cker there is Status: Heel Entrance Music: Place For My Head by Linkin Park (Reanimation Remix) Wrestling Style: Submission, Technical Signature Moves: Boston Crab Counter, Shooting Star Press Other Federations: XWF, XWE, PWA, EWF, EOW, EHW, TCW, Other Federation Awards: XWF Roleplayer of the month, EWF World title, EWF Tag titles, EOW International title (x2), XWE HardKore title Handler Information Handler Name: Dave Hurst AIM Name: [detail withheld] MSN Name: [detail withheld] Bio Throughout Dave Hurst's life, he has been a victim of bullying. He was a victim from the age of 3 when a boy would beat him up at playschool. The boy followed him to primary school and beat him up there. Then in secondary school a group of bullies would beat him up and steal his money every week. Well, Dave chose not to show emotion at this. Dave chose to keep all of his emotion inside and keep smiling. Which seemed as a good thing at the time. Unfortunately, what Dave didn't realise is that through his emotion, a second entity was created in Dave's psyque. That chose to call itself 'Exceptional' Dave Hurst. Dave went to see psychoanalysts about voices he was hearing in his head. All they could tell him was that it was mild, stress-induced schizophrenia and that Dave should try to calm down with work at school. So Dave tried to get on with his life and it was going okay. Until Dave's 18th birthday, when Dave was having a few beers with a couple of friends and here was a knock at the door. Dave answered it and it was the bullies from his old school. They were saying that they were told there was a party here and they wanted to come in. Dave smiled and told them they must have been mis-informed. But they demanded that they have a party and stormed in. They smashed up the house, played music really load, were starting fights with Dave's friends and basically being complete --[INSERT EXPLETIVE]--. This moment was the point when Dave's anger was let out for the first time since primary school. The problem is that with the extra persona in Dave's psyque, Dave wasn't the persona showing the anger, that was 'Exceptional' Dave Hurst. 'Exceptional' Dave Hurst momentarily took control of Dave Hurst's body and he liked the power it gave him. He ran to one of the bullies and layed a massive right to his face, breaking the guy's nose in the process. The others ran for their lives. The bully that was left was kicked numerous times to the ribs until he stopped moving. The friends of Dave stood there shocked. As EDH stormed out of the house and ran away from Dave's home, Dave's friends and family and Dave's life. The next year a homeless Dave Hurst managed to make his way to America on a cargo plane. EDH looked around needing his fix of violence. Roleplays Archives Title Dependancy (rp#3) Quotes "I'm not an idiot, I'm a human being!"
