Real Name: Joseph La Fontaine Birthdate: 27/3/74 Hometown: Detroit, MI Height: 6' 2' Nicknames: 'The Ice Man' Status: Neutral Current Teams: N/A Entrance Music: 'Coolness Is A State Of Mind' by Nirvana and the Red Hot Chili Peppers Wrestling Style: technical, Roman grecco, suplex and submissions expert Signature Moves: Reverse DDT, Dragon Sleeper, Sharpshooter, Any suplex, Most submissions. Other Federations: DTA, EWA, PWA, PWA2, X-Fed, F7W, 720, wFw, wF'Nw, WWC, ICWF, UKWF, REW. Other Federation Awards: PWA World Champion, PWA2 World Champion, X-Fed World Champion, WWC World Champion, PWA Intercontinental Champion x2, WWC Intercontinental Champion, PWA European Champion x2[held IC and Euro to be 1st Eurocontinetal Champion], DTA Hardcore Champion x2, WWC Hardcore. Handler Information Handler Name: [name withheld] AIM Name: [detail withheld] MSN Name: [detail withheld] Bio Joe grew up on the mean streets of Detroit, his only outlet from his frustrations, and from the gang violence exploding around the city was skateboarding. Joe under achieved at school only because, all he wanted to do was skate. He was crowned 'Michigan State Skater Of The Year 1988', and later he went on to retain the Title four more times. Joe's private life was rocky as his mother a French-Canadian was involved with his Detroit born father, Joe hated his father and spent many a happy childhood summer in Canada where he began to love is Canadian herritage and despise his own filthy American DNA. His best friend John 'The Rocket Man' Williams persuaded him to go to wrestling academy with him. After months of planning, and winning every title a skater could win 3 times over, LaFontaine announced an early retirement from competitive boarding at the age of 22, packed up everything he had, and moved across the country to train at Etan Cross’s Cross-Roads Training Cent! er. Both Joe, and John entered the C.R.T.R. and quickly made their way to the top of the class of 1989. Joe was a NATURAL in the ring, and even managed to beat out Trent Razor, and Johnny Blade, for Honor Grad, and Top Distinguished Fighter awards. Now an accomplished wrestler, skateboarder, and fighter, LaFontaine and Williams toured the Indy circuit for three years tagging under the guise of 'The Alliance Of Defiance'. LaFontaine left his good friend in search of more money, and to prove to everyone he deserved to be fighting against the top fighters in the World. Eventually ending up in the D.T.A were he captured Hardcore Gold. Not content with this he left for the EWA. Management wouldn't take a chance on him, and didn't want to give a young Indy star a chance to rise above mid-card status. Joe had several decisions to make, he was at a crucial time in his life. Joe left the EWA and went on a tear. He held several titles all over the world. He even managed to hold on to! several World titles at the same time, this is what eventually got him noticed, and Turned the young skater from Detroit, into a true legend in the sport of Professional Wrestling, The 'Bad Man' was showing no signs of slowing down, and showing Everyone how bright his star could be. Joe continued to kick ass, and hold titles all over the world. His quest for competition eventually brought him to the REW in 1998 where he captured 'Euro continental Gold' on no less than 2 occasions. In 2000 the promotion lost a television contract with Krazy Broadcasting, leaving LaFontaine no other choice than to leave the REW. He went back to skating professionally for another year, but he longed for the ring. LaFontaine wrestled in the PWA where he captured the World Title twice, unfortunately the Federation folded. LaFontaine has since past many a Federation and won many a Title. La Fontaine now wrestles with Furiously Addictive Wrestling. Quotes "I'm Cooler Than You!"
