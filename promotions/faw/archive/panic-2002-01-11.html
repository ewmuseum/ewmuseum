FAW Panic, 11 January 2002

11 January 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Staples left, Los Angeles, CA - 19,282 - 11/01/2001 --Transmission Starts-- Panic Intro "Never Gonna Stop" by Rob Zombie blasts out and the pyros explode at the top of the ramp and the cameras switch on to a capacity crowd of over 19,000 people in the huge arena for tonight's event. The cameras then zoom in on the announce team of Ryan Stroke and Michael McGiggins. Ryan Stroke: Welcome to Panic! I am Ryan Stroke and alongside me is Michael McGiggins, and Michael, what a night we got tonight! The huge main event already booked as British Bomber, the Furiously Addictive Wrestling World Heavyweight Champion, taking on 3 members of Dark Nation in a 3 on 1 handicap match. Michael McGiggins: What a match that truly will be, and before we get on with the rest of the run-down, the plans have already begun, and apparently it will mark the start of a worldwide tour for FAW! Extreme Beating II will be held in New Dehli, and from there we will go on a worldwide tour, a date is yet to be made for where we will end but I am sure it will be a great tour! Ryan Stroke: It will be, undoubtedly, but back to tonight now....focus Michael. Skipster, The Omega Fro, Chris Thunder, and the greatest spectacle in sports entertainment, THE GREAT ONE will all be in action! Michael McGiggins: It truly looks like one hell of a night and it is about to all kick off right now! Cut To Parking Lot The cameras cut to the parking lot where a limo pulls in and out of it gets Baz! The fans go crazy for the European Champion. He throws his title belt over his right hand shoulder and then looks ahead and then into the camera to the left of him and then walks into the building. Ryan Stroke: Is he coming out here Michael? Michael McGiggins: Damn sure looks that way. Cut To President Tom The Bomb's Lounge The cameras cut to the lounge of the co-owner of xCw and the co-owner of FAW, Tom The Bomb. He is sitting with the co-owner of xCw and FAW World Champion, British Bomber. Tom The Bomb: Main event Bomber. You versus Dark Nation, a night where you get to show how great you are in the ring and prove why the hell you are FAW World Heavyweight Champion. British Bomber: Tom, I know that is true and I know I will do it, coz basically I AM THE MAN! They will not be able to beat me, SpAwN, Morte and Crimson Lord, I will show all 3 of those guys just how great I am and why I am that great! Tom The Bomb: And then at the pay-per-view we will demolish Spider and you will retain that World Title and still be the man. There is a knock at the door. Tom The Bomb: Come in. The door opens and in through the door walks Vice-President of FAW, Ben Preston. The fans cheer him and he walks into the middle of the room. Tom The Bomb: Can we help you? British Bomber: Yeah talk Ben. Ben Preston: I was just listening to you and I heard you talk about your demolition of Spider, but I must beg to differ because in one week's time at Panic Bomber you will defend your precious World Title! Tom The Bomb: WHAT? You can't do that Ben. Ben Preston: I was employed by Lee and I can do as I please Tom. Now shut up and hear this.... The fans cheer. Ben Preston: Next week at Panic you will defend your World Title against a man you do not like, a man you have abandoned, a man who has abandoned you, you will fight Chris Thunder for that FAW World Heavyweight Title. And Tom, you will not book anything in your favour because there will be no stipulations, a straight wrestling match for the title and no special referees or announcers, a straight-up match! British Bomber: Ben, you have just made yourself one hell of an enemy! Ben Preston: You finished Bomber? Good. Ben then leaves the room and Tom and Bomber look worried. The cameras then cut back to ringside. Ryan Stroke: WOW! Huge news from the VP of FAW! World Title on the line in 1 weeks time. Michael McGiggins: Well that will be a great match, Bomber vs Thunder no stipulations, and no special referees or announcers! Ryan Stroke: Well I cannot wait and it is still another week away. Baz Is In Da House! "Bodies" by Drowning Pool hits and the European champion walks out to the top of the ramp with his title belt. He walks down to the ring and the corwd go wild as Baz slaps a few hands on the way down. He gets into the ring and then stops and slings his title belt over his shoulder once more. He then gets a mic off of Adam Hutton. Baz: 2 cards, 0 matches! Did you know that? European champion. FAW European Champion and not been on a card for the last 2 shows. But I can guarentee to you that I will be out here wrestling this Tuesday night. But that is not the reason I am out here. I am out here because there is one man who has caught my eye. That man is Richard Storm. He is very successful here in FAW and will still go along way. And this Tuesday night at FAW IS WAR I want to fight Richard Storm, the longest running Light-Heavyweight champion in history, the last ever Light-Heavyweight champion in FAW. He is currently challenging for Tag and Hardcore gold but why not this gold over my shoulder? It means alot more to anyone than Hardcore gold. So, Richard Storm, I want to make sure that you know that the offer is there and I want the acceptance as soon as possible so that I can kick your ass this Tuesday night at FAW IS WAR. You see I have had 2 events of relaxation and now I want to defend my title, a fresh start, and the way I intend to go on! The Furytron then switches on and JLC appears on the screen. Ryan Stroke: Will the commish make the match? Michael McGiggins: Him and Baz are good friends ain't they? I should think so. JLC: Baz, great to see you back once more, and I want to let you know that you do not need to wait on the reply of Storm because the commish is here to make the match happen. BAz will fight Richard Storm for the European Title this Tuesday night at FAW IS WAR! Baz: Jim, that is great of you, and I would like to personally thank you for the opportunity to take the man out and show why I am FAW European..... Chimera then suddenly runs into the ring through the crowd and behind the European champion with a mic and a steel chair. Baz: Champion! Chimera taps him on the shoulder and Baz jumps round startled. Chimera: You see this chair Baz? It's got your name on it! Chimera whacks Baz with the chair and leaves him laying in the middle of the ring and walks out of the ring, he then stops as JLC looks shocked on-screen. He turns round and slides into the ring and lifts up the chair again and hits Baz with it and Baz rolls over and turns back over and he has blood pouring all over his face. Ryan Stroke: OH MY GOD! Baz is hurt now. Chimera then puts the mic to his mouth. Chimera: Baz, you want to talk people up and say who deserves title shots? Well look at me, you CANNOT say that I do not deserve title shots, because I am that damn good, and it is for that reason that this Tuesday night... JLC: You will fight for a title Chimera... Chimera: The Euro... JLC: Chimera, let me finish you fool. You will fight for the Hardcore Title! That's right, Morte. You versus Morte for the Hardcore Title. Chimera: Jim, you do not know how much his ass is going down! JLC: We will damn well see.... Chimera: too right! "Disposable Teens" hits and Chimera walks backstage leaving Baz laying in a pool of blood. Cut To President Lee Smith's Lounge The dorr bursts open and Lee stands up in his lounge and it is Spider. The men shake hands as the fans cheer for the World title number one contender. Spider: Good to see Bomber is being made to work again. Lee Smith: Ain't it though? Good choice by Preston! Spider: Yeah, but what about this Tuesday, what you got instore for me Lee? Lee Smith: You are going to be fighting in a triple threat 3 way match. Spider: against who? Lee Smith: Black Ghost and CRIMSON LORD! Spider: Sounds good to me Lee, and I cannot wait to kick this guy's ass and I cannot wait to beat on Crimson! The scene fades out. Richard Ricardo vs Kyle Glory - Hardcore Rules Adam Hutton: This match is counted on one fall, it's a Hardcore rules match. There is no title on the line. Kyle Gorey's music starts up. Adam Hutton: First… Kyle Gorey! Kyle walks down to the ring Adam Hutton: And his opponet….. Richard Ricardo's music begins Adam Hutton:…. RICHARD RICARDO! Richard walks down with a Kendo stick. The bell rings and Richard swings straight at his head, Kyle ducks, he counters it into a legsweep and jumps into a school boy …1…kick out! Ryan Stroke: Close there! Kyle gets the kendo stick, swings it at Richard's chest. Then as he bows to nurse it Kyle follows up with a devastating strike to the top of the head. Richard falls down. Michael McGiggins: Ouch! Ryan Stroke: A Kendo shot right to the head, that’s gonna bruise! Kyle climbs up to the top rope of turnbuckle and hits a moonsault! …1…2…Kickout! Ryan Stroke: That was three wasn't it? I'm sure his hand hit the mat for the three. Kyle picks up Richard, but Richard whipes his arms away. Kick him in the stomach and hits a Super Powerbomb! Michael McGiggins: Wow. Ricardo gets out of the ring grabs a sledgehammer from under the ring. Slides back in, and as Kyle gets up he has an almighty swing at his head, Kyle's busted open, his blood splatters as he falls straight to the ground motionless. Ryan Stroke: WOAH! Michael McGiggins: This should be over …1…2…3… Adam Hutton: The winner…. Richard Ricardo Ricardo celebrates as EMT's tend to Kyle. Commercial Break Shifty vs Spike Adam Hutton: The following contest is for one fall. Coming to the ring Spike. Spike’s music hits and Spike goes to the ring. Adam Hutton: And his opponent Shifty. Newborn by Muse hits the PA and Shifty goes to the ring. Ryan Stroke: This should be a good match. Michael McGiggins: Yeah it should be. Ding ding ding. The match starts with both Shifty and Spike looking at each other. They then tie-up and Spike turns this into a head-lock Shifty makes it to the ropes and sends Spike off them. Spike runs to Shifty and Shifty drop-kicks him. Ryan Stroke: I didn’t see that coming. Michael McGiggins: Neither did I. Both men get up and Spike runs to Shifty. Shifty goes for a hip-toss and Spike gets up. Shifty goes for another hip-toss. Spike gets back up and runs to Shifty. Shifty goes for a third hip-toss and goes for an arm-bar after it. Ryan Stroke: Nice moves. Michael McGiggins: Yeah. The ref checks the hold and and Spike gets back up. When Spike gets up he eye-rakes Shifty to get him to break the hold. Spike then suplexes Shifty. Spike goes for a sleeper hold. Ryan Stroke: This match has just turned around. Michael McGiggins: Very quickly. The ref checks the hold and Shifty looks knocked ou. His hand goes up once it falls. Up twice it falls again. Third time it stays up. Ryan Stroke: How is Shifty still in this match. Michael McGiggins: He wants to win. Shifty fights to get back up and he elbows Spike three times. Spike breaks the hold and Shifty ddts him. Shifty goes for a pin. Ref counts. 1 2 kick out. Ryan Stroke: Close. Michael McGiggins: Both men get up and Shifty kicks Spike and goes for a pile-driver. Shifty grabs Spike’s arms and moves Spike to a corner. Shifty climbs the top rope and goes for a Redemption which hits Spike in the chest. Ref counts. 1 2 3 ding ding ding. Adam Hutton: Here is your winner Shifty. Ryan Stroke: Shifty wins this match for FAW. Michael McGiggins: Yeah he did. Rumble Entrants... "Boiler" hits throughout the arena and down to the ring walks President Lee Smith, the co-owner of FAW, he gets into the ring and smiles. Lee Smith: 3 more entrants for When Worlds Collide have been drawn....the next man to be drawn was SPAWN from FAW! He will be looking to go to Extreme Beating II to face the World Champion...the next man was STUNNING STAN from xCw! The former xCw World Champion! And finally for tonight CHIMERA was drawn out to enter the royal rumble! Ryan Stroke: Another great 3 men! Michael McGiggins: Only 2 weeks till that huge event don't foget. Lee Smith: That is business taken care of and we will see you very soon.... "Boiler" hits again and Lee Smith leaves the ring. Jeff Harris vs Chimera - No DQ WINNER: Chimera Commercial Break Skipster vs Chris Thunder Ryan Stroke: Great match right here! Next week at Panic Thunder will fight for the World Title...and Skipster will fight with The Omega Fro to fight Dark Nation for their Tag Team Titles. Michael McGiggins: Well I cannot wait for Panic next week, it will be a great night, no doubts on that one. Ryan Stroke: It will be one hell of a show and only a week before When Worlds Collide aswell! Michael McGiggins: Looking like it will be the biggest night in FAW history up to now. Ryan Stroke: Looking like? When Worlds Collide WILL BE! Michael McGiggins: Okay you are correct. Ryan Stroke: But of course. "Freedom" hits and down to the ring walks Chris Thunder. He gets into the ring and the crowd boo the man. He climbs one turnbuckle and the fans continue to boo and he gets down and walks into the centre of the ring. Michael McGiggins: The man with the World Title show next week. Ryan Stroke: A match which will undoubtedly shock us all, but you can only presume that the champion will retain really. Michael McGiggins: But anything COULD happen Ryan. "Ride The Lightning" then hits and Skipster walks out to the top of the ramp before stopping and then walking down to the ring and getting into it to the fans delights. He drops his International Title belt and the referee gives it to Adam Hutton. Ryan Stroke: Here we go then! 2 men who have a huge future in FAW and it all begins right now. Michael McGiggins: They both got a great time here right now. International champion for a start for Skipster, and then there is title shots coming both man's ways. Ryan Stroke: Yeah, that's very true, I cannot wait to watch these 2 in action. The bell rings as Skipster whips Thunder across to the ropes and then hits a huge Samoan drop on Thunder and then quickly pins. Pin: 1..KICK OUT! Ryan Stroke: Never gonna happen! Michael McGiggins: worth a try though, you gotta try to find out really. Thunder gets quickly up and Skipster then delivers a kick to the gut and then he goes for a bulldog but Thunder moves and hits a DDT onto Skipster! He then goes up to the top rope and goes for a splash off the top tope, but Skipster manages to move out of the way and Thunder lands on the canvas. Michael McGiggins: Nice work from Skipster there. Thunder then starts to get up and he goes for a slothesline on Skipster and Skipster ducks it and delivers the FACE SLAM! Ryan Stroke: It is over! Michael McGiggins: OVER! Pin: 1...2...3! Ryan Stroke: He did it! Skipster beat Thunder! Cut To Backstage We go backstage to The Omega Fro’s locker where The Great One comes walking in. The Great One: Fro, fro wherever you are…. Ryan Stroke: It seems they are playing….hide and seek? The Omega Fro comes in from another room in his locker and sees TG1 standing there. He is stunned. The Omega Fro: What are you doing here? The Great One: We are against each other tonight. The Omega Fro: As if I hadn’t noticed dude, can’t wait to kick your ass though. The Great One: Huh? The Omega Fro: I cannot wait to kick your ass. The Great One turns casually and then turns back to punch The Omega Fro whose lip begins to bleed. The Great One throws him against his locker. The Great One: Just like the royal rumble, this match will just be a warm up for my world title shot which I shall attain. No one shall be as confident as me. When I raise my hand tonight in that very ring just metres away…it will not be full, as shortly when I raise my hand high at the top of each turnbuckle it will be accompanied by a welcome visitor. Gold….The World Title. Fro, prepare to perish. The Great One storms out as The Omega Fro regains his breath. Ryan Stroke: I’ve just realised something, we are here in California which is the home state of both The Omega Fro and The Great One, that means they will be fighting for even more pride tonight! The Great One vs The Omega Fro Ryan Stroke: This is our 5th encounter of the night. Michael McGiggins: I actually think these two are the best two performers in FAW at the moment Ryan Stroke: Yes definitely among the top 5, it will be very close. Michael McGiggins: It has the makings of a ppv main event. Ryan Stroke: Wasn’t it the main event or one of the top matches at Entrapment, the FAW/XWW PPV? Michael McGiggins: You know I am not actually sure, I seem to remember something like that but I’m not certain. Ryan Stroke: Yeah, my thoughts too. Michael McGiggins: Its also glad to know both superstars are called ‘The’ at the beginning. For some reason I think that is more effective. Ryan Stroke: Yeah, like ‘the Michael McGiggins’. Michael McGiggins: Not quite, how about ‘The Superb Two’. Ryan Stroke: Isn’t that a complete rip off of The Great One? Michael McGiggins: He stole it off me first! Ryan Stroke: Yeah and pigs can fly. Michael McGiggins: So Westlife can fly? Ryan Stroke: Yeah, they are flying without wings. Michael McGiggins: Your jokes are so stupid. Ryan Stroke: A pleasure working with you to Mikey. Michael McGiggins: GOD DAMN YOU, HOW MANY TIMES, HOW MANY TIMES? Ryan Stroke: Huh? Michael McGiggins: How many times have I told you I hate that name! Ryan Stroke: Once or twice? ‘Freedom Fighter’ by Creed plays as a shriek can be heard. The arena goes into darkness as the music is lowered, it then comes back full blast with the Omega Fro standing at the ramp. The 6’7 MONSTER , from California makes his way into the ring as the announcer Melissa Farrow introduces him and a large amount of cheers can be heard. Michael McGiggins: I went to California once you know…. Ryan Stroke: Where in Cali? Michael McGiggins: I went into Hollywood into a club and the next thing I woke up tied to a casino in Las Vegas… Ryan Stroke: You woke up in Nevada?! Michael McGiggins: Yes…I could never quite work out what happened…. Ryan Stroke: I’ll warn you in advance of an explosion. An explosion of laughter…HAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHA! Michael McGiggins: Hey, it wasn’t funny at the time! ‘Break Stuff’ hits over the sound system and the crowd responds with a huge pop, definitely the biggest of the night. The Great One walks out to a huge standing ovation from the FAW crowd as they salute their number 1 wrestler. He struts his stuff down to the ring with the pyros blasting through the air at full speed. His hometown fans continue cheering him ahead of The Omega Fro, also born in LA. Ryan Stroke: WOOOOO. He sprints down to the ring as The Omega Fro looks on. He climbs all four turnbuckles separately holding his hand high in the air. Michael McGiggins: Earlier on tonight the Great One said that his hand was not fulfilled, not full. Because soon it would possess the world title in it. Ryan Stroke: And he was deeply serious people, deeply serious. The two men get into the ring and prepare to grapple. The bell rings and the tension splits and then quadruples as The Omega Fro gains the early advantage throwing TG1 into the corner. TG1 slides through his legs and then locks in a full nelson. The referee counts as eventually The Omega Fro elbows TG1 away. The Great One flies into the ropes and is then slammed to the ground by The Omega Fro. Michael McGiggins: Some super action early on. The Omega Fro uses his height advantage and nails a strong samoan drop. The Great One moans in pain and slowly gets to his feet. He then deals out at random a punch and a kick to The Omega Fro. After several of these he nails a spinebuster. Ryan Stroke: It could be all over VERY early. Michael McGiggins: No, not this early. Ryan Stroke: Why not? Michael McGiggins: Put it into perspective, they are both alive and well. Ryan Stroke: Yeah, suppose so. The Omega Fro then nails a sidewalk slam and goes for a cover, hooking TG1’s leg. Pin: 1…..KICKOUT Ryan Stroke: He easily kicked out. Michael McGiggins: He doesn’t give up that easily. Ryan Stroke: Both these people are fighting for the pride and the support of their hometown. The Omega Fro locks The Great One in a headlock as he tumbles to the ground. The Great One then gets up slowly and gives The Omega Fro Greatness in an instant! Ryan Stroke: WOW, WOW WOW WOW WOW AND…WOW! Michael McGiggins: That is one fxcking spectacular move. Ryan Stroke: Too spectacular for my liking. Michael McGiggins: Who gives a rats-ass about your liking? NO ONE. The Great One then stands in the middle of the ring and raises his hands high, both of them. The fans give a massive pop as he humiliates The Omega Fro. The Great One covers The Omega Fro with a slow but successful pinfall. Pin: 1……..2…… Ryan Stroke: Hand up! How’d he manage it? Michael McGiggins: We may never know. The Great One kicks The Omega Fro and goes straight for another cover. Pin: 1…2….3….. Ryan Stroke: It’s all over. Michael McGiggins: Yes it jolly well is. The Great One’s music hits as the crowd cheer and he walks out with The Omega Fro picking up pace. Cut To Backstage We see the Dark Nation in their locker whispering. They then shout ‘winners!’ and exit heading for the ring. Then we see British Bomber and Tom talking and Lee Smith watching them from behind a corner…. Michael McGiggins: Its show time… Commercial Break British Bomber vs Dark Nation - Special Referee: Tom The Bomb Ryan Stroke: We saw the two parties preparing there. Michael McGiggins: Its been a long time since we have had a handicap like this, we’ve had 2 on 1s but I can’t remember a 3 on 1. Ryan Stroke: Neither can I. Michael McGiggins: Did I ask you? Ryan Stroke: No…but… Michael McGiggins: NO BUTS! Now shut up. Ryan Stroke: ….. Michael McGiggins: Not literally… Ryan Stroke: We have a special referee involved here, I am sure that Tom The Bomb will be specially bias towards his loverboy, British Bomber. Michael McGiggins: So basically its 3 on 2, Spawn, Morte and Crimson Lord versus Tom and Bomber. Ryan Stroke: Yes, basically. Michael McGiggins: So, what possible outcomes do you see to this match? Ryan Stroke: I can see Tom and Bomber getting the victory due to Tom not counting a Dark Nation pin AND perhaps some weapon involvement. Michael McGiggins: Yes, same here although I can see an interference from xCw members. Ryan Stroke: Or even FAW Members…. Michael McGiggins: If you have just joined us we are ready for a rollercoaster 3 on 1 main event of which we have come to the conclusion that there will be lots of cheating, and I’ll give you a hint as to whom, this crowd will be booing until the sun don’t shine . Ryan Stroke: And there ain’t gonna be no solar eclipse in USA for another couple of years at least! Michael McGiggins: HAHA! ‘Du Hast’ – Rammenstein plays as the pyros go up and Crimson Lord walks out of the darkness. The crowd give a heel reaction to the New Yorker who gets into the ring and gives the finger to the ground. He then mimics Bloodlust scaring the fans increasingly. Ryan Stroke: Nice entrance from him. Michael McGiggins: He just wants to begin. Ryan Stroke: And here comes Spawn…. ‘The Way I am’ by Eminem, aka Marshall Mathers plays. The confetti shoots up and smoke sprays as we see Spawn in the highlight of his career, winning the FAW World Title and , not suprisingly, we do not see him losing it to JLC [Injured]. Spawn walks down as Crimson Lord greets him and whispers in his ear. Spawn nods approvingly as they both look towards the ramp where we await the third and final member of Dark Nation’s entrance. Michael McGiggins: And now…. Ryan Stroke: Morte! ‘Brackish’ – Kittie plays as the lightest member of Dark Nation makes his way out. Morte, the English wrestler who has found a home in the US of A also greets his fellow team mates. He seems deeply serious about the bout as we wait for the ‘guest of honour’- or the referee to make his way down. Michael McGiggins: Now we have the referee, the traitor…. In Too Deep by Sum41 Plays as Tom The Bomb walks out to a huge triumphant boo. He dances around to the theme tune laughing at the fans booing him and gets into the ring with his referee shirt on. He tries to shake hands with each of the Dark Nation as they hold out their hands and then withdraw, with each one of the 3 members spitting on their hands. Tom walks to the other side of the ring. Ryan Stroke: You know what? I wish Spawn and Crimson killed this dude when they kidnapped him! Michael McGiggins: HAHAHAHAHAHAHA, I don’t think he will appreciate that comment and it was always the plan for Lisa and Crimson to set up. Ryan Stroke: Unfortunately Lisa only makes appearances here and there and is not a permanent member of the FAW Roster, only appearing on special occasions. Michael McGiggins: yes, such as to announce she is suing and taking Tom and Lee Smith to court for murder and depression she believes was caused by them. Ryan Stroke: Yes, she lost her baby because of them two. Lee being the hero and Tom being the villain at the current time. Michael McGiggins: And Tom’s situation deterioating every second. Tom situates himself in the centre of the ring as ‘Faith’ strikes and the Limp Bizkit song plays with xCw banners being held high and a spectacular titan tron video being shown. British Bomber then walks out mocking the fans and laughing at them. Ryan Stroke: Morte and Bomber are both from England. Michael McGiggins: To all you English fans watching with your fish and …..chips in your hands, can you please recite the word french fries over and over, the true name for your so called chips. As all four wrestlers are now in the ring Tom The Bomb calls for the bell. The bell rings as British Bomber immediately gets out of the ring and heads for weapons. He gets a chair as Morte does a suicide dive over the top rope. He swings the chair at Morte hitting him while he is in mid air. Ryan Stroke: He isn’t allowed to do that! Michael McGiggins: Doesn’t tom know this is a normal rules match? Tom allows the action to continue as Crimson Lord gets out of the ring with Spawn. British Bomber swings the chair at him and knocks him down. Morte then gets back up as Spawn goes into the ring giving out his frustration and rage at referee TTB. Ryan Stroke: I think he knows….he’s doing it intentionally. Michael McGiggins: Why? Ryan Stroke: Switch the mind on, he co owns Bombers federation and hates all three of his opponents. Spawn threatens Tom The Bomb with a fist. Tom is prepared to call for the bell if this happens . As Morte and Crimson Lord are slaughtered by the lethal chair in Bomber’s hand Spawn lets go of Tom and goes to the outside. Bomber swings the chair and hits him but he stands still. He swings it and Spawn catches it and throws it and prepares to hit Bomber with it. He drops it as Tom The Bomb makes his way outside. Spawn goes for the last kill on Bomber but before he does Tom swings a chair at Spawn who ducks. Michael McGiggins: HE HIT BOMBER! Ryan Stroke: He just hit British Bomber, his fellow co owner. As Tom looks in distress and disgust at Bomber, with blood trickling down his head he turns right into Crimson Lord and Spawn. Morte pumps up the crowd in the ring as they nail a separated powerbomb, with each carrying a half of Tom’s body. Tom hits the ground and goes unconscious. Michael McGiggins: Please Ryan…. Ryan Stroke: What is it now? Michael McGiggins: Excuse me If I cry with joy…. The fans are in a state of syncopation as the two xCw main co owners are covered in blood on the ground. Crimson Lord throws them both into the ring separately. Ryan Stroke: This is glorious. Michael McGiggins: Truly glorious. Morte stares at their bodies in the ring. He bends down on Bomber and Tom and wipes some of the blood onto his finger. He then gets British Bomber up and nails the Rigor Mortis on top of Tom as both are covered in blood. The fans go wild once again. Ryan Stroke: For the 2nd night running British Bomber and Tom The Bomb have been massacred at the hands of a leading FAW Stable. Michael McGiggins: Whats really good is usually the referees would come down to restore order, but they hate the men being hurt and don’t care! Morte calls his team mates into the ring. Spawn Crimson Lord and Morte raise each others hands in a sign of unity. Then, out of nowhere FAW Co Owner Lee Smith comes down to the ring. Ryan Stroke: What now? He whispers in Crimson Lords ear and Crimson Lord laughs. He grabs Tom The Bomb as the fans are going ecstatic and nails ‘The Demo’! Lee then walks round to Adam Hutton and tells him something.... Adam Hutton: And your winners.....DARK NATION! Michael McGiggins: HAHA, That’s just like Lee, wanting to destroy enemies. We then go off air with Lee pointing at Tom and Bomber and miming ‘ We Mean Business’ He then says ‘FAWck You’. Ryan Stroke: We are going off air in a second folks, it looks like we have a no contest result here, but Dark Nation the clear winners if it weren’t for Tom. Bye. --Transmission Ends--
