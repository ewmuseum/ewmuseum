Distortion #19 segment 1 Date: 20/10/02 Show: Distortion #19 A large cheer is heard as The Great One is seen standing next to James Harris backstage. Great One is wearing his newest piece of Merchandise, showing if off to the viewers. James Harris: Great One, tonight you face Chris Thunder where you have the chance to Go to Australia and Disaster Down Under to wrestle for the FAW World Title against Spawn. You and Thunder have been at each throats to say the least over the past few weeks and it all comes to a shortened head tonight from what we would usually see. The Great One: Tonight, The Great One walks into the ring for his first singles match back in action since February this year. Tonight, marks the day of The Great One's full return and his first victory back in Furiously Addictive Wrestling. Next Sunday then marks the night that The Great One and Spawn go one on one, go head to head, go man to boy in the middle of the ring for the third time but for the very first time on pay per view. Twice already The Great One and Spawn have fought infront of the world for the price of grabbing your remote and clicking to the correct channel to watch it. Spawn has won one battle, The Great One has won another and next Sunday the score will be settled. There will be no ties and the same goes for tonight. All that stands in my way is a damn weather hazard, and seeing as I come from Los Angeles where we get a lot of sun, you can bet your bottom dollar that the weather is going to clear up and dispose of Chris Thunder. James Harris: After everything that has happened since you have returned, well, what do you make of it all? The Great One: I left DCV and thought it was about to die but then they went and brought in two jimima puddleducks called Chris Thunder and Cyrus Raynes. DCV is now bigger, but it's not as strong because there is nobody that can replace The Great One. There's me, Foamy, Bomber and Xander who are ready to take these little bitche's down and I'm going to do Thunder tonight and Spawn next week and Bomber is vice versa. So Thunder should get ready for tonight when I send him packing to Disaster Down Under ready to be finished off by Bomber and then Spawn will have to prepare for the greatest test in his life when The Great One headlines his first ever pay per view after only a month of returning and he takes the FAW World Title. James Harris: And let me just say I believe every word you just said. Thank you Great One. The Great One: It's a pleasure.
