FAW FAW is WAR, 8 January 2002

8 January 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
OOC:- There were not to many rps due to the short notice of this card so therefore the results are not top class, but at Panic be ready for one hell of a ride! :) LA Sports Arena, Los Angeles, CA - 18,000 - 08/01/2001 --Transmission Starts-- FAW IS WAR Intro "Control" by Puddle Of Mud blasts out around the arena, at a jam-packed capacity 18,000 people. The lights switch on as the pyros complete the explosions round the ring and arena. The cameras then fade into the announce table. Jackie Williams: Welcome back to FAW IS WAR! After a 2 week absence from this particular event, with only Panic happening, welcome back! And what a night we got tonight, with alot going on. But before me and James start...let us just take back to xCw Smash yesterday night. It was a shocker to say the least... (All of a sudden the British Bomber runs to the ring carrying a chair, he slides in, Stan sees him and tries to cut him off but as he gets up he runs face first into the chair shot, Stan is busted wide open from the chair. Bomber gets rid of the chair and drags Y2Jay over Stan, he then revives the ref and slides out of the ring. Post runs in to save Stan but Bomber cuts him off and the two men start fighting, the ref crawls over and makes the count, ......1......2......3! The ref calls for the bell and the announcer gets up to make the official call.) Harold Fellows: Your winner by pinfall, Y2Jay. (Post grabs Stan and the two of them start walking to the back carrying their Tag Titles, Bomber helps Jay up and the two start celebrating in the ring as Bomber holds Jay's hand up in victory. Stan and Post look up at the xCw Tron then back at the action in the ring, they look at each other and decide that enough is enough, so they go to break up the celebration. They drop their Titles and run back to the ring, Post comes in behind Bomber and Stan behind Jay, the two of them turn around and each get a kick to the gut, Stan gives Jay another "Stunning" Experience while Post puts Bomber in the Vortex. Post and Stan leave them lying in the ring as they stand over them, Stan is still bleeding profusly and may need medical attention.) Gary Logan: Oh my God, I can't believe what just happened, Stan and Post took out Y2Jay and the British Bomber. But that's all the time we have for now so tune in next week on xCw's special Tuesday Night Smash. Suddenly the cameras cut backstage. Its an impromptu conclusion to the show as many in the arena had expected the credits to fade in. But instead of that we are confronted with the unusual picture of a Corvette which has its back right hand side door open wide. Suddenly screams can be heard in the distance, and without a seconds notice a man appears out of the blue masking his appearance using baseball cap and shades. The man tosses a woman into the backseat before slaming the door shut and as he does so the face of none other than Britney can be made out through the back window. Her arms though are taped up and she has no means of escape. Next thing known to the camera man is the car is speeding off into the distance leaving them in their week. James Harris: Many questions arise anyway! Mainly, who is this "kidnapper" of Britney, could it be a completely obsessed man, or is it a man looking to get through Britney, and he is using Stan's weak point? And that would of course, be the sexiest woman in Xtreme Championship Wrestling, do you remember Evil Intentions when we saw her tits? Now that was an event that will stay on tape at home for a few years yet...I watch it every week! Jackie Williams: Thankyou for telling us that James. Anyway, tonight we see that huge tag team main event, Bomber and Bomb, the xCw co-owners, take on Chimera and Chris Thunder, who came so close to a Tag Team Title shot only to be screwed by Spider. James Harris: That is harsh, he was only being fair. But Spider will face Shifty later tonight and Shift earlier this weekend said that Bomber and Bomb were treating him to this match, but I do not agree with that, they wanna see what he can do and then they will work him and Great One over if they need to! Jackie Williams: And also 3 more competitors for the rumble were announced! Remember last night that JEFF HARRIS from FAW, BRYAN DEAS from xCw and SPIKE from xCw. Wonder who will come out tonight?! Xtreme Nature... "Tainted Love" hits and down to the ring walks the newest tag team in FAW, Xtreme Nature, Jeff Harris and Richard Storm. They walk down to the ring and grab a microphone each. Jackie Williams: Jeff Harris and Richard Storm! James Harris: What are they doing out here? Jeff Harris: Welcome to FAW IS WAR everybody! And tonight me and Storm are out here for some little requests of Tom The Bomb. The co-owner of FAW. You see, at Panic you saw our little interference Tom, and at the pay-per-view book us a match! January 27th Tag Team match! Non-Title or Titles, I do not care which, we do not care which because if the champions are in the match the match will have those titles on the line, and if not then they will not be on the line, that simple. Richard Storm: So Tom why not get your ass out here and book the match..... "In Too Deep" hits and down to the ring walks Tom The Bomb. He gets into the ring and grabs a thrid microphone. James Harris: What will Tom do about this. Tom The Bomb: Richard, Jeff, I will give you the match. But not just any match, now let me see. It will be you and Storm versus a team you are very familiar with, CHRIS THUNDER and CHIMERA and you 2 teams will take on a third team, and that team will be THE OMEGA FRO and SKIPSTER! The crowd go wild. Jackie Williams: This promises to be the greatest FAW PPV in history. Tom The Bomb: But don't think I will stop there because a 4th team will be added....the team of THE SHOWSTERS! Now how do you like that? Richard Storm: You really think that worries us Tom? Because it doesn't, and that is because we are better than all 3 of those other teams. And January 27th, we will prove that and you will not think you can intimidate us again! Jeff Harris: Damn right Tom.... "Tainted Love" hits as the 3 make their way backstage..... Jackie Williams: WHAT A MATCH. James Harris: I cannot wait for January 27th!!!!! Mass Murderers vs The Showsters Melissa Farrow :The following tag-team match is for one fall. coming to the ring The Showsters. Song 2 by Blur hits the PA and Scott 'The Show' Costella and Kyle 'The Blaze' Costella go to the ring. Melissa Farrow : And their opponents the Mass Murderers. Mass Murderers music hits and Black Ghost and Nightmare King go to the ring. James Harris: This should be a good tag-team match. Jackie Williams: Yeah it should be. James Harris: This should be a good tag-team match. Jackie Williams: Yeah it should be. Ding ding ding. The match starts with Black Ghost and Kyle 'The Blaze' Costella looking at each other. Both men then tie-up with Kyle 'The Blaze' Costella turns it into a head-lock. Black Ghost gets to the ropes and sends Kyle 'The Blaze' Costella off them. When Kyle The Blaze' Costella gets back over to Black Ghost. Black Ghost goes for a hip-toss. Kyle 'The Blaze' Costella makes a tag to his partner Scott 'The Show' Costella. James Harris: Good time to tag. Jackie Williams: Will it get past Black Ghost ? Scott runs to Black Ghost and Scott trips up by a drop toe hold. Black Ghost tags in his partner Nightmare King. Nightmare King goes for a lag drop and pins Scott. 1 2 kick out. James Harris: This team is quite good. Jackie Williams: Yeah they are. Nightmare King picks up Scott and bodyslams him and than elbows him. He then goes for a sleeper hold. James Harris: Why is Kyle watching this ? Jackie Williams: Maybe its because he doesn’t want beaten up right now. Kyle goes into the ring and base-ball slides Nightmare King. James Harris:There we go. Jackie Williams:Cheat. The ref tries to get Kyle out of the ring and behind the ref Black Ghost goes into the ring and both members of Mass Murderers hit Scott with a double power-bomb. Nightmare King goes for a pin while Black Ghost goes running across the ring to hit Kyle. The ref counts. 1 2 3 ding ding ding. Melissa Farrow: Here is your winning team the Mass Murderers. James Harris:What a good team. Jackie Williams:Yeah. Commercial Break Crimson Lord vs Richard Storm - Hardcore Title #1 Contenders Match WINNER: Richard Storm.... Cut To Backstage The cameras cut to The Great One's locker room. In walks Adam Hutton. Adam Hutton: Hey Great One, you wanted to say a few words? The Great One: Yes Adam, now gimme da mic and sit down yourself. Tonight, I face Morte, the guy who won the Hardcore Title, and I will beat the man, I will beat the Dark Nation jackass and I will show him just how great I am and teach him not to mess with The Great One, teach the whole of the Dark Nation not to mess with me because I am that great, I am The Great One! Shifty vs Spider - Hardcore Rules Match Melissa Fellow: The following contest is a hardcore rules match. Making his way to the ring Shifty. Newborn by Muse hits the PA and Shifty goes to the ring. Melissa Fellow: And his opponent Spider. New Disease by Spineshank hits the PA and Spider goes to the ring. James Harris: This match should be good. Jackie Williams : Yeah its a hardcore match. When Spider gets into the ring Shifty elbows him. Ding ding ding Shifty than pins Spider. 1 kick out. Shifty picks up Spider and tries to power-bomb him. Shifty goes out of the ring as Spider reverses it. James Harris: That was unexpected. Jackie Williams: Can Spider benefit ? Shifty gets up and Spider goes out of the ring. Shifty kicks him and tries to suplex Spider. Spider reverses this into a suplex and pins Shifty. 1 2 kick out. James Harris: Spider the XCW killer really has been representing us. Jackie Williams: Will he get it done at When Worlds Collide ? Both men get up and Shifty kicks Spider and ddts him. Shifty gets up and looks under the ring and finds a kendo stick. He runs at Spider and tries to hit him with it. Spider grabs it and kicks Shifty. He hits Shifty with it. Ref counts. 1 2 kick out. James Harris: Spider is putting alot of pressure on himself right now. Jackie Williams : He is not. Both men get up and Spider throws Shifty into the ring. Spider climbs the top rope and goes for a Venom however Shifty moves out of the way. Shifty pins Spider 1 2 kick out. James Harris: How did he kick out of that ? Jackie Williams: Don’t know. Both men get up and Shifty ddts Spider. 1 2 kick out. Shifty gets up and tries to power-bomb Spider however Spider keeps falling and he low-blows Shifty. Spider than goes for an inside cradel. 1 2 3 ding ding ding Melissa Fellow: Here is your winner Spider. James Harris:Spider wins. Jackie Williams: I hope he beats some XCW members the same way. Commercial Break The Great One vs Morte WINNER: The Great One Cut To Backstage The cameras cut to Lee's office.... Lee Smith: The entrants for the rumble....number 7 will be THE OMEGA FRO....number 8 will be WOLVERINE and number 9 will be CHRIS THUNDER! Thankyou.... Jeff Harris vs SpAwN Jackie Williams: This is our 5th match of the night folks, just in case you can’t count. James Harris: For everyone who’s interested, contact me to launch the anti-Williams campaign, with such friendly activities as petrol bombing Jackie’s lovely house and kidnapping her children. Jackie Williams: I don’t have any children! James Harris: Who was that the other night in the living room? Jackie Williams: -WHAT-THE….You were watching me. James Harris: Um…no….course not…don’t imagine fantasies Jackie…. Jackie Williams: You sick man. I bet you take photographs of me getting changed every night. James Harris: I can’t because you draw the curtains. Jackie Williams: You SICK man. James Harris: Anyway on to other things, later on in the night we have Dumb & Dumber, also known as Bomb and Bomber versus Chimera and Chris Thunder, who lost to Skipster and Omega Fro to get this match. Jackie Williams: Chimera and Thunder are my favourites for a slaughter, simply because they have the backing of the FAW fans. James Harris: Correct, and the FAW Commentators. Jackie Williams: Of course James…of course… James Harris: But now to this match, Jeff Harris versus Spawn. First Jackie, any thoughts you’d like to share with your enemies watching you live on TV? Jackie Williams: Enemies? I was voted the most popular entertainer in sports entertainment. James Harris: Yeah, in the ‘Bye Bye’ magazine Jackie Williams: Bye bye? It was ‘Hello’ magazine actually. James Harris: HA! Jackie Williams: Now, answering your question this is a tough one. Both men obviously tired, Jeff Harris has been in action over at The Corner- a fellow compatriot of FAW at the top of wrestling. James Harris: Actually I think its called The Edge… Jackie Williams: Whatever, Like I care… anyway, both these men are considerably tired, we saw Spawn make a point of that in a recent promo. James Harris: Yes…it seemed he was almost complaining at tiredness, surely neither man will be at the top of his fitness tonight. Jackie Williams: That goes with the rest of the card, the card went up rather late allowing not much preparation at all. ‘The Way I am’ by Eminem plays as the xCw World Champion is introduced by Melissa Farrow. He gets a heel reaction instantly from both sets of crowds, and probably the only man to hated to a maximum by FAW and xCw fans, which is the only thing they can agree on : ) . James Harris: Introducing the man who orchestrated the kidnapping of Tom The Bomb, JLCs enemy, and JLC is of course the most loved man in the town at the moment. Jackie Williams: OF Course…. Spawn gets into the ring with almost full attention on him, apart from the spotlight which focuses on the entrance ramp. Spawn’s entrance is shadowed by the chants of ‘Jeff Harris’. Then ‘My Sacrifice’ by Creed plays and the Innovation Sensation, the main event, whatever you choose to call him, Jeff Harris makes his way down to the ring with a brilliant pyro display. As the smoke clears he raises his fist in the air and makes an action that intends to say ‘you will feel this Spawn’. He gets into the ring and prepares to fight the demon. Jackie Williams: Its better than I thought, they don’t seem too worn out. James Harris: Yes….but we shall see. The bell rings as Jeff Harris circles the ring with Spawn in close contention. Jackie Williams: This should be interesting, I expect both these men to be contenders in the Rumble at When Worlds Collide. Jeff locks up with Spawn and immediately pushes him away, whipping him into the ropes. On his return Jeff lifts Spawn over the back of his head and nails the move, slamming him down to the ground. Spawn rolls over and gets up as Jeff is weirdly on the mat. James Harris: He could have picked up an injury… Jeff pops back up to his feet as Spawn turns him around and executes a back rake. Jeff lands on his back on the mat. Spawn puts his foot on JH’s face. He then puts more pressure on Jeff Harris’s face. Spawn then spins around on his foot which is on Jeff Harris’s face Jackie Williams: A lovely spinning boot scrape there. James Harris: Yes, performed well by the ex FAW Champ. Jeff Harris gets up eventually and attempts to go straight for a powerbomb. He fails miserably as Spawn attempts a full nelson butt drop. He finishes it off sublimely to cap off his past few weeks of glory. Jackie Williams: Bravo, Bravo! James Harris: Nickelodeon, Nickelodeon! Jackie Williams: Pardon? James Harris: Bravo’s a TV Channel isn’t it? Jackie Williams: You scare me sometimes. James Harris: You think your children might end up like me? Jackie Williams: For the love of god I have no children! James Harris: You want my love? Jackie Williams: WHAT? James Harris: For the love of god you said, And I am god. Jackie Williams: I’m going to hire a bodyguard to sit next to me here next week. James Harris: Go ahead, as long as he doesn’t sit in between us. Spawn nails a spinning DDT and goes for the quick cover. Pin: 1….2…… Spawn pauses and gets up. Jackie Williams: Why did he do that? James Harris: Wait and see. He then lifts Jeff Harris up trying to get him stable. He pulls him towards him and executes the Last Kill! Jackie Williams: I see, nice tactics. James Harris: I guess he is sending out a message to anyone and everyone, don’t mess with the devil and you know what you will get. He then hooks Jeff Harris’s leg for a reasonably quick end to the match. James Harris: It’s all over… Pin: 1….2…. Jackie Williams: It isn’t now! Jeff Harris then reverses the pin Pin: 1…2…3..! Melissa Farrow: Our winner, Jeff Harris! Jeff Harris celebrates as his theme music hits and the fans boo. Spawn comes up from behind and pushes him out of the ring and onto the concrete. My Sacrifice then plays as Spawn scatters away as Jeff attempts to search for him. Jackie Williams: Those two were very evenly matched. James Harris: Well done to both wrestlers. Jackie Williams: I’m forced to agree. James Harris: We could see Extreme Nature vs Dark Nation soon. That’s Jeff and Richard Storm versus Crimson Lord and of course Spawn. Jackie Williams: That would rock my world. Commercial Break Bomb & Bomber vs Chimera & Chris Thunder Jackie Williams: This is our main event of the night. James Harris: It certainly is and is – at the moment, looking to be an absolutely fantastic match up. Jackie Williams: Nice emphasis on the word fantastic their James- James Harris: Well, its my job to please. Jackie Williams: I’m sure you’ve pleased all those people rallying for the word fantastic to be used more on TV. James Harris: Obviously they don’t watch FAW then…a lot of stuff is fantastic here! Jackie Williams: HA!, Well Done there. James Harris: People, I think Jackie has a serious health problem. She just said something good about me Jackie Williams: Yeah. James Harris: What a historic night. Jackie Williams: Not as historic as Evil Intentions…and WWC on Jan 27th… James Harris: Yes people, we had them at EI, and we will have them at WWC….I am proud to announce that on January 27th TITS will return to FAW and xCw viewing. Jackie Williams: That’s what you think – those women might come out in 20 layers of clothing. James Harris: You know what….I doubt that. ‘Faith’ by Limp Bizkit plays as British Bomber walks out to huge boo’s from all over the arena. As the majority make their voice heard he brandishes the FAW World Title bringing a HUGE cheer from the xCw fans who see their native owner with their opponents belt. The 6 foot 231 lbs wrestler and xCw Owner climbs into the ring trashtalking the referee. Melissa Farrow: And now…his tag partner, 50% owner of both xCw and FAW…Tom The Bomb! The fans chant ‘Traitor’ over and over as they wait for Tom to appear. He appears with an xCw shirt on which stirs things up even more. The FAW fans resort to violence as ‘In Too Deep’ by Sum 41 plays. James Harris: He certainly is in too deep. Although the music cannot be heard with the fans almost rioting, he raises his hands high as he looks around at the abusive banners in the FAW Stands. He sees such banners as ‘You Disgrace Cameron, North Carolina, your hometown, you SOB!’ and ‘DIE BITCH DIE’. Guards have to block the FAW fans from invading the ring. Tom gets into the ring with British Bomber as they bow to the FAW fans taking the rip out of them well and truly. They laugh – but the smile is soon wiped off their faces when ‘Disposable Teens’ by Marilyn Manson plays. Jackie Williams: Its Chimera! James Harris: Chimera is in the house people and this isn’t going to be pretty. Chimera appears as the fans give a mixed reaction as Disposable Teens continues to play in the background . The pyros shoot off into the arena sky of all colours as he raises his hands to a huge ovation. He walks down to the ring as Tom The Bomb and Bomber come charging out and attack him before he reaches the ring. They throw him in the ring as the bell rings. Jackie Williams: This is unfair! James Harris: Cheats. Chris Thunders runs down to the ring without his music even beginning. He sprints like his life depends on it – and actually, it seems it does in this case! He turns Tom The Bomb around and gives him a huge fist. Jackie Williams: Go Thunder, WAHOO James Harris: Thunder is punching the man that caused him a great deal of injury at Evil Intentions by pushing him off the cell. Thunder gives around 10 blows to the chest of Tom The Bomb and then headbutts him down to the ground. The fans go nuts as Chimera throws British Bomber into the ring. Jackie Williams: Those two have a great long running rivalry ever since Thunder turned on FAW and started the XWW Revolt. Chimera then whips Bomber into the ropes. Bomber reverses the move and whips Chimera into the ropes who prepares a huge clothesline. Chimera’s leg is held onto by Tom The Bomb who still battles away with Chris Thunder. Chimera loses his balance causing Bomber to pounce for a sidewalk slam which he is successful with. James Harris: But that was ages ago and look who’s not on FAWs side now. Jackie Williams: Never would have guessed. Chris Thunder then turns in a rage at Tom The Bomb and nails The Stormer! James Harris: SUUUUUUUPER. Tom The Bomb collapses… Jackie Williams: He has collapsed like a pile of rubbish. James Harris: Excuse me young, ignorant, annoying, stupid, silly, brainless, spasticated woman what did you just say? Jackie Williams: He has collapsed like a pile of rubbish. James Harris: You leave me speechless. Jackie Williams: Why? James Harris: HE IS RUBBISH! I mean come on Jackie this guy wants to make us redundant, me and you, no job. Jackie Williams: I can certainly see why he wants to get rid of you… Chris Thunder goes into the ring with Tom The Bomb still on the ground and goes up from behind on Bomber. He nails a neckbreaker on Bomber and gives a high five to Chimera. Tom The Bomb then riots through and into the ring with a steel chair. He tries to hit Chimera who ducks. Tom hits Thunder who was behind Chimera. James Harris: Oh No! Jackie Williams: He succeeded…that can only mean one thing….A Bad Thing! Chimera then tussles with Tom The Bomb in his advantage and gets the chair. He then hits Tom The Bomb over the head with the chair knocking him unconscious and sending him out of the ring. James Harris: Two can play at that game. Jackie Williams: Now that IS Simple Fear established. The referee restores order as Tom The Bomb lies outside of the ring and Chris Thunder lies dizzily on the apron. Chimera drags British Bomber towards him and throws him into the roper. British Bomber shoves Chimera onto the ground. Chimera performs a rollover and gets up. He nails an underhook suplex and then bends over Bomber, locking in a leg lock. The referee separates him as Bomber wrestles him off. Chimera then nails a clothesline and then stands back up. Chimera then gets British Bomber in position and sets up the ChimeraDriver… James Harris: YAY! He nails it as the fans are ecstatic. Tom The Bomb attempts to get into the ring but Chris Thunder pushes him off the apron and onto the ground as he cries out in pain. Chris Thunder pushes the referee out of the way and comes into the ring pondering a double team move. Jackie Williams: The referee is down, the referee IS down! Chris then nails a DDT on Bomber. James Harris: Is that all he is going to do? He has the chance to kill Bomber and he….DDT’s him? Chris then goes outside and gets a table. He puts it inside the ring as Chimera grabs it. He sets it out straight. Jackie Williams: I have a feeling they ain’t gonna use that table for food James… James Harris: Yeah, probably for sitting on… Jackie Williams: Now I really DO see why TTB would want to fire your ass to Brazil and back again. James Harris: He told You I could have a vacation? YAY! Jackie Williams: Um…No…he didn’t… Chimera and Chris then shout ‘FAW FOR LIFE’ and double chokeslam British Bomber, FAW World Champion and xCw Owner through the table. James Harris: He’s taking quite a lot of beating recently. Jackie Williams: He deserves it! James Harris: Oh this is glorious…. Chimera then shakes the referee to consciousness as Chris Thunder clears the remains of the table as Chimera – as he is tagged in – pins British Bomber. Pin: 1…2...KICK OUT! The fans are shocked as Bomber then crawls over to his corner and tags in Tom The Bomb who is back up on the apron. Tom runs in and takes out Thunder with a clothesline. He then grabs Chimera and whips him to the ropes as the FAW World Champion gets into the ring and locks in the ENGLISHLOCK on Chimera and Tom then goes up to the top rope and delivers the TOM BOMB! to Chris Thunder and pins him... Pin: 1..2...3! James Harris: He won it! Jackie Williams: Damn Chimera and Thunder were so close! James Harris: And we are out till next week! --Transmission Ends--
