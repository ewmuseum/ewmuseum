FAW Distortion, 2 June 2002

FAW Distortion Live from the Syracuse, New York; OnCenter Attendance: Unknown Sunday, 2nd June 2002 Forgot Something Guys? (INTRO) The cameras are in the parking lot and they see Shifty arrive minus his International Championship. Lance Hunter and Chad Winters are standing across from Shifty. Syd: Shit I forgot my bag. Trent Hunter: Yeah, I forgot my tickets. Shifty looks at Syd and Trent. Shifty: Is that ment to be funny. Shifty walks off as Syd and Trent look confused. "Ballistic" Bryan Deas vs Kevyn Vernon [ Match not handed in. Bryan Deas picks up the victory by a clean pin ] Apocalypse Has Something To Say "Slowburn" by Systematic hits and Apocalypse makes his way to the ring. He slides under the bottom rope and into the ring. Apocalypse stands on the turnbuckle and raises both his above his head. He drops back to the mat and calls for a microphone. Shane Riot: What's he doing out here, he's not scheduled. Mario Ciaralli: Oh let the man speak Shane. Apocalypse: Violator! Violator get your ass down here now! "Square Dance" by Eminem hits and Violator makes his way out onto the ramp. Shane Riot: Things are picking up. Apocalypse: Violator you may stand there now Hardcore Champion but you shouldn't be, as the video evidence shows you pinned me after the buzzer sounded. "Apocalypse points to the titentron and everyone watches as the buzzer sounds as the referee counts three." Apocalypse: You see right there I should not have been stripped of my title. I still should be Hardcore Champion and I will be Champion! The crowd cheer. Violator: So are you going to do anything or just stand there and cry. Apocalypse: Well Violator the reason I called you out here is for my shot right here, right now. The crowd go wild as Apocalypse takes off his jacket. Violator: Well you see boy normally I would but you have to earn it. So I tell you what if you want a piece of me so bad then come Panic you and me in a non-title match, what do you say to that. Apocalypse: I say I'll see you there. Violator walks backstage and Apocalypse taunts to the fans. Chris Thunder & Prodigy vs Mass Murderers [ No match handed in. Chris Thunder and Prodigy pick up the victory ] Tag Team Championship The British Bomber & Mystery Partner vs Foam World Order Mario: And up next is what I have been looking for. We get to find out who is the Mystery Partner for Bomber is. Riot: Yes, and I bet it is Tony. Floyd: Well right now it could be anyone. I have heard rumors of it being lots of wrestlers. {As the two talk about how they know what wrestler it is, Rage against the machine’s Renagades blares through the speakers all the way from the bottom to the very top of the arena. A loud bang goes with the music as both members Neo and the 9th Wonder of Foam comes walking out holding their hands up in the air. The crowd jumps on their feet sending out their big cheers for the two tag team champs. Walking down the aisle with their belts hanging over their shoulder like a baby girl or boy on his mothers shoulder.} Riot: And this should have been for the tag belts. Floyd: Why is that Shane? {As the crowd watches them get to the ring, FWO slides under the bottom rope tossing their titles over to the side as they get to their feet. 9th wonder goes for the first corner he sees as Neo leans on the ropes awaiting the arrival of their challengers.} Mario: So I wonder who will come out first? Floyd: Well it looks like we will get our answer. {With that said, Faith by Limp Bizkit comes on as The crowd comes alive with Boo’s as the traitor comes walking out from the back with his wrestling attire on as he walked in a peaceful manner.} Riot: These people should show him respect. Floyd: He turned on the old school. {Bomber walked down the ramp way never taking his eyes off the standing FWO in the ring as he stopped at the bottom of the steel ramp as he pulled out a microphone from his back pocket. The face of concern as the great Bomber put the microphone up to his lips.} Bomber: Everyone wants to know who is my partner. Is it this guy or this guy? Well here he is. {The fans let out a big heel pop as Coolness is a state of mind by red hot and nirvana comes blasting on the speakers as the crowd looked up the ramp way getting ready for who ever would come out. The music keep on playing as no one came out but the fans let a big scream as the man that was his partner was none other than Joe La Fontaine come in rolling under the ring as FWO both didn’t know what was going on. } Mario: Oh my god! Joe La Fontaine is The British Bombers partner {Bomber rolled in underneath the bottom rope as Joe ran right at Neo. As Bomber rolled in Foamy hit him with a hard axe handle to the back of the head knocking him back down to the ground. Neo on the other hand was hit with a hard closeline to the back that almost took him out of his wrestling boots. Joe turned to Foamy and hit him with a several right hands to the face as he stepped back a little and hit a massive superkick to the jaw that leveled him over the top rope. Bomber slowly got to his feet as he and Joe went to work on the seven footer Neo in the ring. Grabbing both arms, the two fan haters whipped Neo across the ring. On his return, Neo was shot in the air and back down with a giant flapjack.} Riot: And that is the way to go. Floyd: That’s the only way they can win. Mario: And look at them again. {In the ring both members kicked away at Neo before Joe turned and saw Foamy getting to his feet. He then took off at the ropes, launching himself over the top rope like in a royal rumble and hit a cross body press on Foamy as the crowd let out a boo. The ref then finally rang the bell for the match to start as the two legal men in the ring was Bomber and Neo. Bomber slowly picked up Neo by the hair as he pushed him against the ropes. SMACK, that was the sound of a big chop from that size thirteen hand of Bombers. He then sent Neo across the ring and on the big man’s return he was meet with a hard kick to the gut. Bomber with the advantage wrapped that right arm around the head of Neo and hit a big DDT with made Neo lay there motionless. Bomber then rolled hi over and went for the cover.} One........... Two.................... Threeeeeeee {But no, Foamy came in a saved him as Bomber rolled off of Neo.} Mario: We almost had a winner there. {Foamy raised up Bomber by the hair and hit a massive right to the jaw before sending him into the corner. Foamy then took off to the corner as he hit a closeline that could have rocked the nation today. Bomber wiggled out of the corner but to only be hit with a big boot by Foamy. Foamy then grabbed the hand of Neo and pulled him over to their corner as he got out making the tag in.} Riot: That’s cheating there. Floyd: Ya and that crap earlier wasn’t? Riot: No..... {As Foamy came in Bomber got to his feet slowly with the help of the ropes. Foamy went at it hitting big kicked with that left leg to the gut as he wrapped his arm around the head and took a hold of his tights as he hit a high suplex on the 231 pounder. Rolling off of Bomber, Foamy bounced off the ropes and hit a high ankle leg drop onto the neck of Bomber. Getting to his feet he picked up Bomber as he took him and tossed him into his corner tagging in Neo. Neo came in hitting rights and lefts on the former leader of old school. Bomber took every blow as he fell to the bottom. Neo walked back a little as he got a better look at the carnage. As the crowd came to their feet Neo took off as he hit a hard running knee into the face of Bomber with made a big red mark.} Floyd: What a move by Neo. Mario: And he is adding that aggression to his style of wrestling. {Neo grabbed up Bomber and took him along the ropes as he sent him flying across the ring. Bomber bounced off the ropes but on his return he ducked a big closeline from Neo. Stopping in his tracks, Bomber hit a big kick to the stomach as he twisted at that neck hitting a neckbreacker letting Bomber get some rest. The ref came in to count the ten count as Bomber started to roll towards his partner as Neo did also. About at eight Bomber made the tag into Joe as Neo made the tag into Foamy. Both men came running in at eachother as Foamy hit a bigflying spinning heel kick. Hopping back to his feet Foamy hit another spinning heel kick to Joe as Bomber came back into the ring.} Riot: Come on Joe. Step it up now. Floyd: So, you getting a little worried over there? Riot: ..... {Neo with somewhat a little bit of wind hit a big drop kick onto Bomber who rolled out of the ring. Joe, holding his face in pain got hit with a big inverted DDT from Foamy who went to his feet. Foamy then took his right foot and put on the anklelock as the ref went in for the count. Bomber with a pissed off look on the outside grabbed a chair as Neo went over to stop him. Bomber played there holding his face as Neo went to jump in the air to his a big cross body but out of nowhere came that unforgiving steel chair from Bomber as he laid out Neo. Bomber then went to roll back into the ring but the crowd started to get worked up as from the crowd came Sabatash leaped over the rail and went at Bomber with a hard right to the head.} Mario: And what is Sabatash doing here in this match? {Bomber went at it but the fresh man was to much as Bomber got whipped into the ropes by Sabatash as the ref saw from the inside. The ref came over and told Sabatash that he needed to go to the back but he refused. This distraction was all that Bomber needed as he rolled into the ring with that chair and as Foamy got to his feet. Foamy not knowing what is going on turns to only get hit with a hard steel chair in the head sending blood everywhere as Foamy just laid there. Bomber then grabbed a hold of Joe as he pulled him on top of Foamy as the told the ref to turn around. As he did he saw the pin and went down.} One............ Two............... Three.........{And yes the hand came down as the bell rang signaling the end of the match.} Floyd: What a cheat there on that one. Riot: A W is a W in the winner side. {As the bell rang Bomber and Joe rolled out of the ring as Neo and Sabatash got to their feet in the ring. All three men looked out at Bomber and Joe La Fontaine as the came faded to the back on the face of Bomber holding his right arm up in the air.} Shifty vs Sabatash The drums from Korn's "Blind" kick in from over the p.a system, the lights begin to dim and the bass kicks in followed by the guitars. The words "ARE YOU READY" hit and fireworks fill the ramp way before Shifty emerges from the curtians without his International championship belt. Shane Riot: New pyro, new music and new ring attire for the International Champion. Mario Ciaralli: Shut up Shane this is just self indulgence. Shifty slowly walks to the ring and the crowd boo very heavily towards him. Shifty steps between the ropes. Floyd Rotterdamn: Xander stole Shifty's International Championship belt last week on Panic. Mario Ciaralli: Yeah to add to his collection. Shane Riot: Was anyone else freaked out about that? Mario Ciaralli: About what? Shane Riot: The fact Xander had collected all those titles. Floyd Rotterdamn: A hobbies a hobby. Shane Riot: Well he's gonna pay when Shifty gets a hold of him. "" by hits and Sabatash makes his way down the ramp and the fans are chanting out to him. Sabatash slides under the ropes and is met by a boot to the head from Shifty, the referee rings the bell. Shane Riot: It's on. Shifty lays a series of kicks into the mid section of Sabatash before delivering an elbow. Shane Riot: Great work by Shifty beating down on Sabatash. Mario Ciaralli: He attacked him before the bell. Shane Riot: So? Shifty raises Sabatash to his feet and whips him off into the ropes. Sabatash ducks the clothesline and nails Shifty with a spinning kick. Sabatash goes for a pinfall. Pinfall: 1...Kick Out Both men get to there feet and Shifty pushes Sabatash into the ropes. As Sabatash bounces back Shifty hits a samoan drop. Shifty then bounces off the ropes and nails a springboard splash on Sabatash. Shifty climbs the ropes and looks down at Sabatash then the titentron flickers on. Shifty looks up at the titentron. Shane Riot: What's happening. The titentron shows Xander entering the building, he has the home-made television title and the television title on his right hand shoulder and the Universal and Lightweight title on his left hand shoulder with the International Championship around his waist. Sabatash grabs Shifty's head and slams Shifty to the mat. Mario Ciaralli: What a move. Shane Riot: Shifty's gonna be sore in the morning. Sabatash picks Shifty to his feet and German suplexes Shifty with pin. The crowd get distracted as someone makes there way to the ring. Pinfall: 1.....2.........Broken. The referee is pulled to the outside of the ring by Joe La Fontaine. La Font powerbombs the referee and slides into the ring. Mario Ciaralli: Come on disqualify Shifty. Shane Riot: The referee is out cold. Joe and Shifty begin to stomp down on Sabatash and the referee gets to his feet. The referee calls for the bell as Shifty and Joe double Powerbomb Sabatash onto the mat. Joe begins to stomp down on Sabatash as Shifty removes the leather strap from his waist and begins to whip Sabatash with it. Winner: Sabatash by DQ Sabatash lies in a pool of blood and Joe and Shifty walk backstage. Segment 3 - For after Shifty/ Sabatash The titentron flickers on and we see Xander passing by Baz, Xander turns and punches Baz in the shoulder. Xander: I bet you could only wish to hold as many titles as me. Baz: Whatever. Xander: I'm a great champion, a fighting champion so I'll give you a International title shot at Panic. Baz nods and Shifty walks in the double doors. Xander spots him and runs into the women's bathroom. Shifty bangs on the door and waits outside. Shifty: Come out Xander, I will get my title back tonight. Tony Vigilante vs HaHaKlown [ No match handed in. HaHaKlown wins via pinfall ] Hard Knocks Match - Number 1 Contendership Spawn vs Skipster [ No match handed in. Spawn wins via pinfall after New School interferes ] Shifty! Shifty is still seen standing outside the women's bathroom and two women leave with baby strollers. Shifty: Is he still in there. Woman 1: Yeah. Shifty: Thanks. Shifty bursts into the toilet and spears Xander into the cubicle door. Shifty then begins searching the cubicals for his belt. Xander walks out the door and walks towards the woman and strollers. He lifts up a baby and passes him to the mother, Xander then pulls the International Championship from the stroller and places it around his waist, he then picks up all the titles and begins running to the parking lot. Shifty is seen running from the women's bathroom in pursuit of Xander. Xander reaches the parking lot and tosses the belts into his car. He drives off and Shifty falls to his knees. Shifty: Bastard!
