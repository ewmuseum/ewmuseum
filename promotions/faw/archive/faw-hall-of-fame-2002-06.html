FAW Hall Of Fame

{Contrary to beliefs, wrestlers who are currently on the roster can still be hall of fame members} HaHaKlown {Current Member} HaHaKlown was a class act. He starred from day one in FAW, and brought with him several enticing gimmick matches and angles. He had several feuds, including one which ended up with JLC losing a loser leaves town match, but soon returning. He will forever be remembered as one of the most unique wrestlers ever to step into a Furiously Addictive Wrestling ring. His most memorable feud has to be with Mojo though, a feud which turned into Dark Carnival vs Mojo & Co. This feud ended up with HaHaKlown winning once again. My guesses are he held the title for something like 3 months, before handing it over to sidekick Deadman. HaHaKlown became President of FAW, and stepped out of the spotlight. He went missing, some suspect him dead, and some think he decided to go on a 20 yr tour with the circus, but the only thing we do know for sure is {even this we are not 101% sure} he will not be coming back. Pyro Months later, after HaHaKlowns imminent departure, Pyro joined FAW. His first appearance showed that he was an exciting prospect with great expectations. These expectations were fulfilled to the maximum. Pyro was moved straight up with the big guns, in the world title division. He won it at Breakaway 2001, defeating Axle Mystic X and Spike. That reign lasted for only 1 month, although It seemed a lot longer as their were 2 ppvs until he lost it to American Psycho. He also had a 15 day reign which was shortlived, totalling his time as world champion to around 2 months. It is arguable to say Pyro is the reason FAW Is here today, he rocketed the roleplay quality in here. There are rumours of a return, ever since he left, and they may be true, but we may have to stick around for a few more months to discover. American Psycho American Psycho was the one who took the gold off Pyro and held it for around a month. He was plagued with problems in his time but only ever lost one match out of 10. He also rocketed the rp quality, and gobsmacked everyone by producing the biggest roleplay in FAW History on the eve of Disaster Down Under [when he defeated Pyro] with a cracking 55kb roleplay. He has now returned and we can expect him to mount a title challenge. British Bomber {Current Member} British Bomber joined FAW in April, and had long feuds with JLC. He holds the record for the most title wins, with 9 FAW Titles and 2 unclassified titles from invading federations [xcw & xww]. He is also the most hated man in FAW, after invading with xCw and XWW not in respective order. JLC {Current Member} JLC joined FAW in February. He was probably the worst on the roster at that time. Lets just say ten lines was above average. He was a hard working wrestler and won all the lower titles in what seemed like no time. He slowly won them over and over again. But then came the breakthrough. With the help of several top e-wrestlers JLC improved dramatically and moved up the ranks after 8 months in FAW. He won the IN Title and then battled warrior Spawn and won. He was the new world champion! JLC is the first and at the time of press only grandslam champion in FAW. Hell, he even had his own Hot Stuff dedicated to his name! Spawn {Current Member} Friend of Pyro, who formed The Omens Of Death, Spawn is an ex-euro and ex-tag champion. He is also an ex-World champion, after losing the title to JLC after a fairly long reign. Spawn is a legend, and will not be forgotten in a hurry. Him and JLC made up a perfect main event at Deceit, producing an absolutely shocking amount of roleplays and giving it all they could. Unfortunately, SpAwn was outperformed by JLC and still waits for his rematch at the title.
