FAW Rulebook, August 2002 edition

11 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Furiously Addictive Wrestling Rulebook Please read over these rules before joining as they are our rules and we stick by them! You have 3 chances at them, 2 warnings and the third is a firing offence! Please read! · Character Creation · Role Playing · Challenges & Matches · Championships · General Rules · Shows & Match Rules Character Creation 1. Only one character per person, there is this limit so that you can roleplay more with that 1 man. You are allowed to handle 2 men if they are a tag team together, and we are talking tag teams that tag together more than concentrate on singles. If for whatever reason, your tag team splits up, one member is taken off the roster. 2. Original wrestlers ONLY. No real wrestlers at all, no rip-offs of real wrestlers either. This means no people whose catchphrase is JUST BRING IT, please do not make me turn you away for this reason! 3. The sample roleplay when you apply is the thing that is important on the application so make that your best or link to a previous roleplay. 4. You are promised a match within 1 week of roleplaying for the first time, so please make sure you roleplay to your best and after that, you have to feud for a match. 5. It is important to start feuds when you are new, so please use the interference forms, etc. Roleplaying 1. You are expected to produce one roleplay per week, and it is expected to be of good quality and length, please make sure this happens. 2. If no roleplay or sign is seen of you within 2 weeks then your contract is terminated and you are fired, this does not apply if we know about it or we have kept your wrestler after you leave. 3. Roleplays are not expected to have an overdose of swearing in, an occasional word is accepted but on the whole, we expect them to be clean OR blocked out. 4. It is important to mention the match that you are in for your roleplay, this does not apply if you are introducing yourself or sparking off a feud. 5. The wrestler must always act as though he is really fighting someone and not use terms like "roleplay", "angle", "handler", and never call another wrestler by their REAL name. 6. Roleplays must not include members of the front office, handlers, staff, managers of other wrestlers without permission. 7. You may use Melissa Farrow, Adam Hutton or James Harris as an interviewer in roleplays. 8. All roleplays are due in at midnight the night before the event, eg. FAW IS WAR is held every Tuesday so roleplays are due in at midnight Monday [EST] and 5am [GMT], and the same thing applies to Friday Panic deadlines [deadline is at 12am [EST] on Thursday and 5am GMT . Challenges & Matches 1. Gimmick matches are handed out by the FAW staff, so please don't expect to get one automatically if you request it, they are normally handed out for angles and feuds. 2. We do not promise to use all interferences as we get more than 1 for each match and we also have angles to continue, and not too much can happen in one match. 3. Sneak Attacks and interferences must always include a reason so we know why and how. 4. If you interfere in a match, expect the person who you interfered against to interfere in your next match as this is the rule, we allow it in these circumstances. Championships 1. All titles can only change hands with a submission or pinfall unless otherwise stated, eg. ladder matches, you grab the belt off the roof! 2. The title holder can hand title shots out to anyone, but the front office can hand out title shots to number 1 contenders if they are being avoided. 3. The World Title is the top belt in FAW, and it must be defended at least 1 time a month and at EVERY PPV. The holder is normally the best roleplayer in the federation. 4. The International Title is the second best belt in FAW, if you hold this belt you are on your way to World Title quality. 5. The push belts in FAW are the European and Hardcore belts, they are both difficult to get, but they are great to have as you are on your way up if you hold them. 6. The Tag Team Championship is a hard title to win, only the best team wins the belts. It must be defended at least 1 time a month. General Rules 1. FAW is a booked federation and we choose winners on the quality of roleplayers. If you roleplay when you don't have a match, aswell as when you have a match, it is more likely that you will succeed more than someone who just roleplays when they have a match. Rarely, we choose winners for feuds and angles. 2. FAW is a roleplay federation, you must roleplay. 3. FAW is a family fed with a hint of non-family! We aim to provide no killings, guns, knives, racisms, sexisms, and we accomplish this. 4. Act your alignment, heels hate fans, fans hate heels, faces love fans, fans love faces, neutrals don't mind either way! 5. All deadlines are UK Time unless otherwise noted! 6. All wrestlers upon contract acceptance become the intellectual property of the FAW. Whereas if your character leaves the federation, we observe the right to retain the character to be used to finish an angle and or a farewell match, if you will. In addition to this rule, there is something called the Showtime clause: Essentially, If you leave, these things happen, and good luck. But if you insist on making a pissy rant prior to your leaving, your character will be seized and paraded around like a complete moron. Shows & Match Rules 1. FAW IS WAR airs every Tuesday night and you can expect at least 1 title to be on the line and 4-8 matches every event! It is 1 and a half hours long and you get the best action ever in the wrestling world! 2. Friday Night Panic is the same as FAW IS WAR but with 4-7 matches on the card! 3. FAW has a monthly PPV where there are up to 11 matches and every title is on the line, feuds are settled and you can never predict what will happen! 4. We try to get the results up on the day of the event and the card is posted at the same time as the results go up for th next show. Please Note: Due to PTC Rules, if you quit, we reserve the right to NOT release you - and due to PTC Rules, you will not be allowed to join another PTC Fed if this is the case. Also if you apply to FAW please note that there may not be space and you may be moved to Revolution Wrestling.
