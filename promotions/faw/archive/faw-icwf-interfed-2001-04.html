FAW — The ICWF Interfed, April 2001

Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
ICWF is our brand new interfed....we are the newest federation to join ICWF and we hope to thrive in it..... In our first few hours in ICWF the owner of them writ this in the news section: The new federation signed to the ICWF today "FAW" have done the first ever ICWF indy promotion. The prospects for this federation are looking great and they have set an example for all you indy federations out there to follow. Its things like this that help get television deals Then in the next few minutes this was put on the Latest News page: .::: 28.4.01 :::. @FAW looking good@ FAW are the latest federation in the ICWF they are looking extremely good. The owner is said to be delighted at joining the icwf, and was reported to saying " Soon the FAW will be on your television screens mark my words!" There is alos some big news going around in ICWF....UKWF's Television contract with Canon Broadcasting is up in 24 hours from NOW (29th April) and will therefore be either signing them up again or looking for a new fed..... The other big news regarding TV contracts is OSW.....ETV are thinking about the termination of their contract.... The only other TV contract that is available is the Yankee Network and they are happy at the moment with the status of PHWL.... FAW were the FIRST indy fed to produce a promotion page......click HERE to see the promotion page that FAW did and click HERE to see ICWF's promotion page.... We are also in the Top 100 e-feds list for them and the link is shown below: Please keep checking back to this page for updates and we will keep having them so you know our status! Thanx for your time! President Sadam Hussain Some massive news graced the scene in ICWF today, 1st May 2001, we found out that the UKWF's Television contract with Canon Broadcasting has been terminated and the contract is up for grabs and the decision is made on Saturday as to wo gets the contract! Let us all hop it is FAW! If ANYONE has any suggestions for the fed please e-mail us on [detail withheld] and say why it will help us get the TV contract! Please all wish us luck in our attempt to get the contract at the weekend! Thanx for the support in ICWF and for the whole of FAW!
