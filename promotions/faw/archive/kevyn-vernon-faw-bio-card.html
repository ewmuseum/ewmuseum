Real Name: Kevyn Vernon Birthdate: October 13, 1977 Hometown: Hellborn, Germany Height: 6'1' Nicknames: N/A Status: Heel Current Teams: N/A Entrance Music: 'Violent Revolution' by Kreator Wrestling Style: High-flying, Hardcore Signature Moves: Highlighter, Blood Generator Handler Information Handler Name: Andrew AIM Name: [detail withheld] Bio Kevyn Vernon was born in Hellborn Germany on October 13, 1977. He lived a very short time in Germany after hisbirth and moved to the United State of America with his mother and father. They settled in Newark, NJ where Vernon grew up. He started his wrestling career when he entered high school and joined the wrestling team. He was never the top man on the team, but always was one of the best. He wrestled his first 3 years of high school and ended up joining a backyard federation when he was a Senior in highschool. This is where he learned the roots of hardcore wrestling. Vernon was the World Champion after a short time of being in the federation. He combined his newly learned hardcore tecnhiques and high school wrestling manuevers and became a dangerous force. He gave up backyard wrestling after about 2 years when the fed finallyclosed down. Vernon started wrestling in indy feds across the country and is now trying to make his way into the big leagues and become a superstar. Quotes "Something Wicked Is Coming..."
