Roster card

Real Name: Chris Craven Birthdate: 22nd January 1976 Hometown: Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania Height: 6'4' Nicknames: None Status: Neutral Current Teams: Prodigy Entrance Music: 'Back In Black' AC/DC Wrestling Style: Brawler Signature Moves: Belly-to-Back Suplex, Tiger Suplex, Fisherman's Suplex, Triangle Hold, Front Face Full Nelson Other Federations: NMW, IRE, xCw Other Federation Awards: xCw Intercontinental Champion, xCw Tag Team Champion Handler Information Handler Name: [name withheld] AIM Name: [detail withheld] MSN Name: [detail withheld] Bio Chris Thunder is a second generation superstar. As a former FAW World Heavyweight Champion, Thunder uses his ring savvy to dominate his opponents... Chris has had his moments at the top of the card, also becoming an International Champion... Currently one half of the FAW Tag Team Champions, along with Prodigy, Thunder has aligned himself with longtime nemesis, The British Bomber, Spider, Courtney Charisma Carter and Prodigy to reform Dark Generation, the stable which rocked FAW in summer 2001. The status between Thunder and Triple C is unknown, although in recent weeks, Bomber has been more than unhappy with the rumours flying around that Chris and Courtney are a couple. The intense hate between both Thunder and Bomber stems back to July 2001, just a month after the debut of Chris Thunder in Furiously Addictive Wrestling... The two still have a lot of unfinished business, which has been put on hold for the time being, as Dark Generation attempt to bring down the power trip of Tom The Bomb and Kathryn Velmont Thomas...========================================Well, I thought I might as well write up something on my FAW career... I've had many ups and downs here, but its been a great experience... Here's my take on the past 14 months...May 2001 - I began to chatting to Jon Fox in mid-April, after he offered his services to become a columnist for XWW... I signed on the dotted line for FAW on May 1st, and was thrown into the midcard, with my first match against Damo Muzzler on FAW IS WAR a few weeks after signing up for the fed I'd be in for the next fourteen months...June 2001 - Oh yes, the debut of Chris Thunder on FAW pay-per-view at Breakaway, in a triple-threat match for the International Title against the champion, Sean Mason and the wrestler which would develop one of the most bizarre relationship with Chris Thunder for the next year... None other than current ally The British Bomber! Of course, Mason emerged victorious in this battle, but I think I was beginning to make waves as a heel, instead of my runs as a face in both NMW and IRE...July 2001 - The first Diabolical Day was fast approcahing, and I engaged in more than one battle with The Mortician in the next few weeks... In the tournament, I surprisingly made it all the way to the final, before being defeated by Jay Wiked, (who has also been known as Spike, Trent Hunter and Syd) who recieved a title shot the next night on FAW IS WAR... I was so close to a World Title shot after just two short months in FAW, but I would have to wait another five months before I recieved my first title shot. But it didn't matter, I was having fun lingering in the mid-card...August 2001 - Some of the best work of the past 14 months took place at this point... I became the first ever Light-Heavyweight Champion, and picked up my first Tag Team Title reign, with Gee Monie... The time was right to be elevated to the upper-midcard, (well, at the time, I think that was what the staff were thinking) and I was placed into the International Title run again... Now, at this time, JLC and Bomber were the only men in the path of Chris Thunder, and the next few months would be interesting...September 2001 - I finally put XWW to rest in late August, and two days after I had closed the place, I met Matt Henstock through Hussein Fazel, who has disgraced himself in the wrestling sim community with his immature antics along with Aaron Fearon and Liam ' the corporate dumbass fifteen year old who pretends he's rich' Manton, although we'll leave the sim story for another day... Although, trouble was on the horizon... Matt and I were planning to open a new federation which would have a domain and all the trimmings. In the end, it would be known as XWW (which opened once again). Some FAW handlers were gaining interest in this new venture, which I had mentioned to Jon who had met Matt a few days after the fed plan first came to light... Basically, Richard, handler of Chimera/Chris Anstex had asked a few questions too many about this new fed, and had ridiculously rumoured that I was only wanting to team with him so I could keep a hold of the FAW Tag Team Titles, (which were both in my possession after Gee had quit) and 'take them to this new fed of mine'... This was bad timing to have rumours like this floating around, as FAW's site stated that there would be another invasion... As the founder of XWW, I was bombarded with questions about whether 'XWW would be invading again' and all that... I eventually blew up after the same rumour that Richard had began was being thrown in my face, with all this stuff about how 'Tom and Jon wouldn't be happy with you stealing FAW talent.' That was absolutely absurd, and I attacked both Richard and Jon about this... I went so ballistic, I engaged in a heated argument with Richard's brother and spammed the Roleplay Board... I'm certainly not proud of my actions, but I swear right fucking down, I didn't intend to steal ANYONE from FAW... Anyways, as far as roleplaying went, at Taking Hardcore To The Extreme, I won the International Title in a triple-threat match with Bomber and JLC, a matchy which triggered the beginning of the xCw invasion...October 2001 - This month, Thunder defected to xCw, had a short relationship with Kathryn Velmost-Thomas before she went back to Bomber and I defected back to FAW... I also lost the International Title... Hmm... Sounds like an interesting month... << *Note sarcasm* November 2001 - Yet another 'interesting' month...December 2001 - Things began to pick up in December, as I was included in the nine-man Hell In A Cell match at Evil Intentions... Of course, it became eight men when JLC was attacked by a mystery man, who we found out later on was Skipster... I made it down to the last three, before I was screwed by new xCw co-owner, Tom The Bomb, and thrown from the top of the cell through the announcers desk... Nice...January 2002 - 19th January on Panic... BOOYAH! World Heavyweight Champion for the very first time! Kicking The British Bomber's ass for the title, and getting some revenge for last month's attck from Tom The Bomb... Dropped the title to Spider nine days later in a triple-threat match with Bomber and Spider, this time, screwed over by JLC...February 2002 - Everything went downhill... Didn't even appear at Vendetta - one of the worst months in my time in FAW...March 2002 - Fought for the Hardcore Title at Extreme Beating 2, facing off against Sabatash and Xander... Xander came out victorious after I was strapped into a stretcher, and couldn't kick out!April 2002 - Was sent down to FAWA to shake off some ring rust...May 2002 - Returned with new tag team partner in Prodigy, and we began our quest for the tag team titles!June 2002 - Reformed Dark Generation with Bomber, Spider, Triple C and Prodigy at Collision Course... One week before, Prodigy and I became Tag Team Champions, ending the reign of the FWO! Roleplays Archives Feelings... Quotes "How can anyone possibly overcome 'The Man With All The Talent'? Career history

After being involved in a horrific accident at his home in April 1994, Chris Thunder's father, James, attempted to get his son involved in wrestling... James, a former AWA World Heavyweight Champion, wanted his son to follow in his footsteps, and be at the helm of AWA... However, in June 1997, after getting himself in shape, AWA was announced bankrupt just days before Chris' first match... His father attempted to buy the fed and make it his own, but Chris had already made up his mind. He was off to work for James' long-time rival, JT Manning, who was the owner of No Mercy Wrestling, a big-time fed on the west coast...It took Chris about another year and a half of training, and he finally made his debut in March 1999 for NMW, just missing out on the National Championship against Warlock... Thunder had set his sights on World Title contender DJ Drew, and VP Johnny Jarred and Drew rivals, 'Rad' Ronnie Cochran and Red Storm had no problem with Chris' intentions, as he and Drew went at it numerous times, with Chris saving both men from a beating...Just a few weeks before Chris' debut, Manning had been sensationally fired live on network TV, and Jas Reskin quickly replaced him... Reskin was determined to push the new boys, and Thunder was one of his top priorities... However, financial difficulties halted the process, and Chris was out of a job...Chris searched for a new federation, but no-one showed interest. Until Iron Ring Entertainment saved the day in December 2000... Chris had taken almost a year away from the wrestling business to spend time with his family, and when Roy Mann came calling, Chris was once again in the spotlight... He was quickly placed into a feud with young Canadian, Scott Osbourne, who went on to become IRE Heavyweight Champion... It was in IRE that Thunder ran into an old nemesis from NMW - Highwayman... Highwayman was a bitter man, after losing out on the NMW No Mercy Title on the last ever NMW telecast. He, like Thunder, had taken a long absence from the business...In was in February 2001 that Thunder, Highwayman and Simon Seaman were placed in a triple-threat ladder match for the Continental Title that Highwayman made a massive impact on the life of Chris Thunder... Apocalypse (not of FAW fame but Highwayman's brother) knocked Thunder from the ladder and sent him to the announcers desk below. This was where Seaman captured the title, and Highwayman left empty-handed... Thunder, meanwhile, had sustained a horrible ankle injury, which sidelined him for about three months...Soonafter, IRE closed, and Thunder was given a massive pay-off... With the cash, he began to start work on his own promotion, while taking independant bookings and nursing his ankle... It was in March 2001 that Xtreme Warzone Wrestling (XWW) held it's first show... The first few months went well, but Chris, determined to get back into the ring, was hungry for some action in another federation...He recieved an offer from FAW executives in late April, and on May 1st 2001, Thunder signed the official contract, which tie him down to FAW for six months... On the June 1st edition of FAW IS WAR, Thunder made his debut, going up against Damo Muzzler... And just a few weeks later, made his first PPV appearence at Breakaway, where for the first time, he crossed paths with The British Bomber...Meanwhile, over at XWW, Highwayman had signed up to Thunder's federation, and the two had seemingly buried the hatchet. But soonafter, they were at loggerheads again... And when XWW sensationally invaded FAW in late-June, Thunder denied he had anything to do with the invasion... The owners of FAW back then, however, were having none of it, and Thunder was suspended until further notice, just one week after losing the 1st annual Diabolical Day final to Jay Wiked... Chris was looking for revenge, and when The British Bomber searched for a new start in wrestling, Thunder offered him a job... Funnily enough, Thunder had also signed Kathryn Velmont-Thomas, who had a history with Bomber, to their books too... The two were seemingly happy riding along in XWW, until Kathryn was kidnapped just six days before the Ultimatum PPV... She returned, after being freed by her kindappers, but was too shocked to speak about the ordeal...But Kathryn had fooled us all, and when Bomber made the save for her in her match that evening, after the stalker hit the ring, it was where the unthinkable happened... Thunder and his henchman, Jodi Fox, attacked Thunder from behind, and as Kathryn scurried from the ring and grabbed a sledgehammer, Thunder savagely attacked Bomber... When KVT stepped into the ring again, she pounded Bomber with the sledgehammer, and announced she was together with the stalker - XWW star, Blade!A month later, at the interpromotional event, Entrapment, Bomber turned his back on XWW, and defected back to FAW... The no-contest ruling on Bomber's match was the deciding factor, as FAW edged out 6-5... The next night on FAW IS WAR, Bomber managed to get a hold of the XWW documents, and burnt them in the middle of the ring before Thunder's very eyes... XWW died, but it was far from over... The Bomber/Thunder feud was just beginning...At SummerSlaughter in August, Thunder and Gee Monie defeated the team of Bomber and Spike to capture the Tag Team Titles, and at Taking Hardcore To The Extreme in September, Chris defeated JLC and Bomber in a triple threat match to win the International Title... Then, at Evil Intentions in December, in the explosive 8-man Hell In A Cell match for the World Heavyweight Title, Tom The Bomb defected from FAW to become half-owner of Bomber's xCw, which Thunder had defcted back from after a horrific spell during a short-lived 'business alliance' with Bomber... Tom threw Thunder from the top of the cell, sidelining him for three weeks, as Bomber paraded around with his newly-won World Heavyweight Title...But it wasn't over. Thunder and Bomber collided on the January 18th edition of FAW IS WAR, and Thunder sensationally escaped with the title! However, Spider would win the title nine days later, and from there, Thunder experienced a tough time in FAW... Failing to make an appearence at the Vendetta PPV, and only managing to challenge Sabatash for the Hardcore Title at Extreme Beating II, Thunder had dropped from main event status to FAW's 'dog house', in just two months... Things got worse when he was demoted to the FAWA (Furiously Addictive Wrestling Academy). But this was where he met a rising star in Prodigy... And when both he and Thunder made it onto the FAW roster once again, Prodigy turned his back on his brother, Enigma, and joined forces with Thunder...Just one week before Collision Course in May, Thunder and Bomber went at it again, this time in tag team competition, when he and Prodigy picked up the Tag Team Titles from Bomber and Joe La Fontaine, and on the Panic live telecast four days later, defeated Bomber in singles action for the first time in months... Then, at Collision Course, Thunder successfully defended his Tag Team Title against Chris Anstex, while Prodigy successfully defeated Black Ghost... Both Thunder and Prodigy had help from Bomber and Vincent, formerly known as Spider, and rumours were flying around that they would be joining forces... Then, as Bomber and Tom went at it in their match, Courtney Charisma Carter, Bomber's step-sister, helped Bomber defeat Tom, and Dark Generation, the stable which rocked FAW in the summer of 2001, was reborn... Thunder, Prodigy, Bomber, Vincent and Triple C had joined forces to eliminate all evil within FAW...But trouble was on the horizon, as HaHaKlown, the first-ever World Heavyweight Champion, made his presence felt after Bomber defeated Xander in the 2nd Diabolical Day final, defeating Thunder in the semi-final as tension between the two began to rise once again... JLC made his return, but joined forces with HHK, the man who had drove him out of FAW just two months earlier, forming Dark Carnival eVolution!Thunder and HHK began feuding, while Bomber and JLC rekindled their feud... And at SummerSlaughter, Thunder defeated HHK in a Last Man Standing Match, which is billed as one of the best matches in FAW history...Shortly after, HHK, JLC, Bomber, Vincent and CCC departed from FAW, leaving Thunder to pick up the pieces left from the bloody feud between DCV and DG... As Thunder slowly began to move down the card, just like at the start of the year, Bomber made his return, and had an impromtu match with World Heavyweight Champion, Spawn, at Entrapment in September 2002... This was where Thunder blasted Bomber with a chair, causing the DQ, before anhialating him... Thunder claimed that Bomber 'screwed him over', referring to the incident at Entrapment in 2001, before making remarks about his step-sister, Courtney, stating that 'she can go to hell'...And then, on the 6th October edition of Distortion, Thunder caused even more trouble, when he sensationally hooked up with DCV, a stable which he had feuded with just two months before, helping Spawn and Apocalypse take apart Crimson Lord, the returning Great One and the 9th Wonder Of Foam! And then, on the 13th October edition stated:'The heart of DCV is cold and calculated... Just like me...'Thunder was indeed a changed man, having joined the dark side... However, at Disaster Down Under in Melbourne, Australia, Bomber and Thunder went one in a Lumberjack Match. Bomber coming out victorious with the Anklelock... After the match, Thunder's lumberjacks, Rzarector, 9th Wonder Of Foam and SummerSlaughter opponent, HaHaKlown, lay a beating on Thunder. Placing him out of action... Since then, Thunder has vanished from the FAW scene, and it's unknown when he'll return... There has been no updates on his physical condition, and the realisation of missing out on the main event spot at Extreme Beating III must be hard to take. What we do know is, that Chris Thunder will return - but who will he pounce on?
