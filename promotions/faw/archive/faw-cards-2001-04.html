This is the page where ALL FAW cards will go.....and there will also be a preview to each match in that show the day before the event takes place..... We did this because we thought it would be better than a simple page of cards! Non-Title Match Magic vs Austin Referee: Adam Yates Magic makes his debut against the texas rattlesnake . International Title Match Deadman vs Spike Deadman finally gets his shot at gold . Non-Title Match British Bulldog vs Justin Credible Referee: John Savva Bulldog battles one half of the tag team champions . Non title Match Lex Luger vs X-Pac Referee: Adam Yates and luger gets the other half ! Non-Title Match Damo Muzzler vs Max X vs Mortal Maniac Referee: Adam Yates speechless i think is the word ! Non-Title Match HaHaKlown vs JLC Referee: Mojo The loser will leave town ! Non-Title Match JLC vs Dark Legend Referee: Danyal Griffiths JLC battles newcomer Dark Legend . Non-Title Match Deadman vs Dark Lord Referee: Adam Yates A Deadman versus a Dark Lord ? What a match ! Inter-gender Tag B. Bulldog & Debbie vs Lex Luger and ??? Referee: Danyal Griffiths The question remains , who will lex choose ? Tag Team Titles X-Factor vs Dudleyz Referee: John Savva We could have new Tag Team Champions ! Dont you just crave for this match ? I do ! Hardcore Title Match Damo Muzzler vs Rhyno vs Mortal Maniac Referee: Adam Yates Rhyno could have a tough battle here , and Maniac will be panicking as Muzzler defends . Non-Title Match Mojo vs HaHaKlown vs Alex Wolfe Referee: John Savva Alex has shown morale and talent , but with Mojo and HaHaKlown fighting it out perhaps he could sneak a victory ? Card subject to change in the next few days.... Non-Title Match The Undertaker vs Mike vs Jason Jacobs Referee: Danyal Griffiths Non-Title Match Y2J Vs Bloodhound Referee: Adam Yates Non-Title Match Dark Carnival vs Mojo , and The Acolytes Referee: Danyal Griffiths Tag Match E & C vs Dudleyz Referee: John Savva Card subject to change at any time.... 26th April 2001 26th th April 2001 14th April 2001 14th April 2001 International Title Match Bain vs Extreme vs Spike Referee: Danyal Griffiths Spike defends in what could be a very Extreme battle [lol] .
