Farrow speaks with the Hardcore Champ! Date: 10.27.02 Show: Disaster Down Under PPV (right before Hardcore Title match) Melissa Farrow is standing outside a locker room that has Damien Ryan's logo on it. She's looking beautiful tonight in her pretty dress for the PPV. Melissa Farrow: Ok, I'm standing outside the dressing room of the FAW Hardcore Champion, Damien Ryan. We'll see if we can get a couple words. She knocks on the door and opens it, and we see Damien Ryan sitting down tieing his boots, he motions his head for Melissa to come in. Damien is wearing his usual wrestling gear, black boots, blue tights with two red maple leafs on eachside and a black spider web design on the pants. No shirt, tape around the wrists, and his long black wet hair comes down. Melissa Farrow: Damien, any words about your match coming up next with The Duke. Damien Ryan: Tonight is another night where Canada will prosper. Another night where a superstar climbs up another rung on his ladder of success. Another night where Damien Ryan picks up another win and leaves his opponent in serious pain. Duke, I hope you've been training, praying, getting ready to face me. I hope that you've prepared for this Hardcore match, prepared for a wrestler with not only his exteme abilities, but a technica, submission master. Get ready Duke, because Canadian air is lingering, and your about to destroyed by the Toronto Destroyer. Ryan gets up, grabs his Hardcore Title belt and walks out of the dressing room heading towards the arena. Melissa Farrow: Floyd, Mario, Shane, back to you guys.
