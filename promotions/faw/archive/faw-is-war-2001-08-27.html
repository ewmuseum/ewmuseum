Johnson and The Omega Fro still battle it out, with The Omega Fro stunned from the bell-shot earlier on in the match. The Omega Fro tries to hit a full nelson slam but is overpowered by Shawn. Johnson hits a clothesline on Fro. He quickly gets back on his feet and hits a suicide dive over the ropes. He did not try to hit anyone. He runs up the ramp and charges at Pyro. He knocks down Natas and prepares to strike Johnson. Pyro blocks a punch from Johnson however and counters with his own fist. Tony Blair: The fans are pumped. Michael Cole: Pumped with blood Johnson and Pyro fight all the way down to the ramp. Johnson goes for the tombstone piledriver. Pyro flips back onto his feet and hits a stunner. Johnson lies flat on the floor as Natas recovers and walks towards Pyro. He chokes him from behind and Johnson climbs to his feet. Johnson pummels Pyro in the chest.
