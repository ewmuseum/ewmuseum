A Tag Of A Talk...

20 October 2002

A Tag Of A Talk... Date: 20/10/02 Show: Distortion 19 James Harris: I’m here backstage with the tag team champions, Rzarector & Renegade. Firstly I’ve got to ask, how are you feeling Renegade after what happened last week? Are you fit enough to compete tonight? Renegade: Physically I feel like crap. Me ribs are still sore and I don’t have full range of movement in my neck. But mentally I’ve never been better.That match with Apoc opened my eyes a bit. It opened them wide! I now who what has to be done. What has to be done about Apoc, about keeping our titles and about our opponents tonight. Crimson Lord and The Duke. I may not be 100%, but they’ll underestimate me at there cost. James Harris: As you’ve just mentioned Renegade, your opponents tonight are Crimson Lord and The Duke. This will be your first match together since winning the tag belts at Distortion 17. Although your be belts aren’t on the line to night, you must feel very confident about going up against a team who are tagging up for the first time here tonight. *Rzarector hisses at James Harris.* Rzarector: James, you know, when tonight’s match is over, there will be nothing of a laughing matter. I don't feel good about Crimson Lord, considering he has beat me, giving me my first loss here in the FAW. I don't feel good about anything in this match up. Me and Crimson had a major fight. When that was all said and done he defeated me. Now what can we say about this match? Cant he tag team champions hold off long enough where they won't get hurt? *James Harris is about to say something when the green eyes of Rzarector glows under his black hooded face. His fangs shine out as James Harris shakes.* James Harris: So you guys are telling me, that you don't know what will go down at this Distortion? is that right? Rzarector: James that’s exactly right. There is no such thing as fate, God won't allow it. The only thing we can do is rely on our talent, and that is it. Renegade: Ummmm, yeah. What he said!
