Hot Stuff, 25 August 2001

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
HOT STUFF BANNER :::Hot Stuff Intro The camera pans round a capacity crowd for Hot Stuff this week, the first for a few months and then the cameras go to the opening entrance where a big pyro explodes in pitch blackness and then the cameras go to the ring and look at the Hot Stuff banners around and then go to our new Hot Stuff commentary team of Stew Byse & Rob Dale. Stew Byse: Welcome to the first edition of Hot Stuff! Rob Dale: You do not know me but I am Rob Dale and alongside me, Stew Byse. And once more welcome here! And tonight we have a main event including a debuting MxM and 2 Dark Generation members. Stew Byse: And we also have an exclusive Great One interview with Michael Cole conducting it! Rob Dale: Here we go now! :::Cut Backstage The cameras cut backstage where Tom The Bomb is walking down the corridor and then he gets interrupted by Power Of Two members, Spike and Bomber where they stop him and speak. Spike: Look you son of a b[beep]ch I am sick and tired of you and your ways and how you keep screwing us over, but now we are getting payback for that you see, if you screw us on Monday night in our Tag Team Titles match then your ass will be going down man, and I mean DOWN! I mean you will be toasted for good! British Bomber: Tom, you are an idiot to think you can keep screwing us and if you screw US on Monday or ME on Thursday, I guarentee that your ass is dead! Tom The Bomb: I am the prez of this business and will treat you however I want to treat you! British Bomber spits on Tom and then Tom starts beating on both of them and then all of a sudden Justin Extreme runs round the corner and hits Tom from behind and then Bomber hits the British Lock on Tom as he begins to tap after about 20 seconds and the referees run out to break them up. :::Canyon vs "The Idol" Marty Stevens WINNER: "The Idol" Marty Stevens by pin fall :::Cut To EMT Lounge The cameras cut to the EMT lounge where Tom The Bomb is getting treatment on his ankle. Tom The Bomb: Can't you guys hurry up, I think that he deserves a screwing for that personally! He wants to lock in that gay lock and then say do not screw me? Well, he just earnt it himself, but I have decided not to give him that until maybe a few weeks time, trust me I will show him who is the president of this business! The EMT's continue work as Tom holds his ankle in anguish at points. :::Cut To Dark Generation Locker Room The cameras are in the DG locker room where Thunder and Minion are preparing for their match. Chris Thunder: I hate Continental Connection but Tom getting the British Lock layed on his ass is the best thing I have seen in this fed for a while! I have to say that them FA geeks need teaching a lesson, but tonight we go after a team of idiots to be honest, they suck! MxM, I mean who the hell are they? Minion: I don't care who the hell they are coz they still suck! We are Dark Generation and we will one day be the rulers of FAW and show the world who we are and why we are DARK! The new generation will come soon and when it does, FAW won't know where to turn! The scene fades out. :::CrackerJack vs Dimitri Duncan WINNER: Dimtiri Duncan :::Cut To Total Freeze Over Locker Room The cameras cut to the Total Freeze Over locker room where JC Ice and Skipster are sitting with the World and Hardcore titles talking. Skipster: Power Of Two? Who the hell do they think they are? Beating on the prez Tom The Bomb! We will teach those punks a lesson tommorrow night and win those Tag Team Titles! JC Ice: DAMN RIGHT Skipster, you didn't win those belts for nothing! Skipster: Hell no! The scene then fades out. :::Natas vs Sky WINNER: Natas by clean pin fall :::The Great One Interview The camera switches on in a lighted room. The camera walks down several corridors and we come to a door. It bears the words TG1. We enter and see Michael Cole dressed in a black suit. He is wearing a white buttoned shirt and a FAW tie. He holds up his finger and begins to speak. Michael Cole says: Hello my names Michael Cole and I am here today at the Hot Stuff arena interviewing FAW Star The Great One. The camera turns to The Great One who is in casual clothes. He is wearing TG1s merchandise which has only recently been released. His Nike trainers and his gym bag which is flung over his shoulder show that he wants to make the interview quick. Michael Cole says: If you missed Thursday Panic Natas & The Great One invaded the match and eventually ended up fighting each other. Great One, tell me, how did you make that vital decision to attack Natas in a such a brutal way? The Great One says: Well Michael, Back when I was kicking Natas's ass in EIPW, I took a vacation, and came back to find that he had left the federation, so there was some unfinished business that I wanted to take care of. When I saw him enter FAW and get involved in that cage match, I saw that as my chance to finish his off. Michael Cole says: But it seems Natas will want some revenge and indeed he got some at the end of the show when he controversially fought back with that iron pipe. Do you think the iron pipe may have effected your fitness? The Great One says: Well, when I attacked him first off, I didn't use any weapons, that came later, but because Natas is such a wimp, he had to use weapons right from the start. He hits me with an iron pipe and takes me out, what the hell do you think Michael, of course it hurt. But unlike Natas, The Great One is a man, and he can take it, and by Monday, i'll be 100% fit easy, because Natas's shot was so weak that it only took about 5% out of my fitness level. Michael Cole says: You are not involved in Mondays show as of yet, is that a hint that you will be at the arena? The Great One says: You're damn right that The Great One will be in the arena. The Great One has signed a FAW contract, and is now ready to start to kick ass and entertain just like he always does, in every single event, whether The Great One has a match or he doesn't. Michael Cole says: I think the Commissioner will object to you 'kicking ass' in every event, especially when you do not have a match. The Great One says: The Great One couldn't care less what the commissioner thinks, no one can stop The Great One from doing what he does best. I was born to entertain the fans, I was born to bash all of FAW's living brains in, just like I have done in every single fed that I've been in. The Great One brings in the ratings, so if The Great One is kept off FAW programming, you can watch Xtreme TV's contract with FAW The Great One pauses. Michael looks at the cameraman obviously amused. The Great One says: To go bye bye's because the ratings will be lower. Michael Cole says: Now that you mention ratings, rumours have been spreading around FAW that you will indeed team up with Pyro sometime next month,can you confirm these? The Great One says: What the? The Great One hasn't heard anything like that. But, if need be, The Great One would be happy to team with Pyro, to take out Natas and that Shawn Johnson guy. The Great One and Pyro do not like each other, they have never even spoke. I respect him for everything he has done in FAW, and if need be, yeah, you may see The Great One and Pyro team up. Michael Cole says: Back to Panic, we saw you jump off the top of a cage to bring damage upon Natas. Could we see that kind of suicide jump regularly? The Great One says: Hell no. The Great One has never hit a high flying move in his life except that. I've never even tried for a damn drop kick. Why I climbed the cage I don't know. I should have just hit greatness on him and saved my own body as well because, that jump took a little out of me as well. Michael Cole says: Who is your next target? The Great One says: My next target, hmmmmmmm, well, Natas took me out by suprise at the end of Panic, so I think I might continue to beat his ass down. If not him, then The Great One's going for the FAW World Title. Michael Cole says: Critics have slammed your wrestling style and labelled you an 'ego headed fool'. Some people agree and I think with you saying yourself you hope to win the FAW World Title , might just prove to them. Do you think the critics could already be biting through your reputation or is your opinion that they are harmless and the fans will forever love you? The Great One says: All Critics can kiss my ass, plain and simple. I can't say that the fans will love me forever, because I might get obsessed with winning the world title, and do anything to do that, and that could mean beating the living hell out of all of their favourite superstars to get their. But my wrestling style, no one can fault that. The fans will always love to see me wrestle, without question. The Great One says: As for me being an ego headed fool as you call it, The Great One has an ego, The Great One has a huge ego, but a fool, oh no, far from it. Michael Cole says: I think that will be all. The Great One says: Well then I have got to go and check in at my hotel. Nice speaking to you Michelle. Michael Cole says: Its Michael. The Great One says: Okay see you in hell Michelle. The Great One walks off with Michael Cole looking unhappy. As The Great One exits the door a grin appears on his face. The cameraman and the rest of the crew begin to laugh at Michael as we head back to the action. :::Dark Generation (Chris Thunder & Minion) vs MxM (Myth & Xero) WINNER: Dark Generation by disqualification after Power Of Two interfered and beat on Dark Generation and then went for MxM. Then Total Freeze Over run to the ring and take out Dark Generation and then they fight Dark Generation while MxM fight Power Of Two, MxM and Total Freeze Over then clear the ring and celebrate as PoT and Dark Generation leave seperately and the show fades out with MxM and Total Freeze Over staring each other down.
