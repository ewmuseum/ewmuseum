HSBC Arena, Buffalo, New York - 18,595 - 01/02/2002 The When Worlds Collide banner flashes across the screen and the re-cap of one hell of a night in FAW and xCw kicks off. The first scene shown is that of Jim "JLC" Pheonix being told that the job would be done tonight. Then up pops the shot of Crimson Lord defeating Shifty in a buried alive match-up. This is followed by Britney winning the xCw Women's Title. Shots of Harris leaving the ring during his Tag Team Titles match and Fro and Skipster retaining their FAW Tag Team Titles are shown quickly followed by Tom telling Jeff that if he didn't win the royal rumble, he was fired from FAW. This is closely followed by Morte retaining his Hardcore Title and Chimera becoming the number 1 contender to the European Title....then the World Title match rolled around and all the men made their way to the ring...3 men, 3 referees, 1 champion. The cameras show JLC screwing both Thunder and Bomber out of the title as he threw Bomber out of the ring and threw Spider on top of the then World Champion, Chris Thunder as he counted the 1...2...3 for Spider to become the new Furiously Addictive Wrestling World Heavyweight Champion. Then the rumble itself...the first thing it shows is Jeff Harris getting thrown out of the ring and the commentators saying that Jeff Harris is fired from FAW for life! Then it cuts to the very end as Crimson Lord is eliminated from the royal rumble, the final man to be eliminated and Tony Vigilante celebrating in the middle of the ring, as he celebrates his rumble win, and the man who will go to Extreme Beating II to face the World Heavyweight champion in a one on one match for the title. --Transmission Starts-- Earlier This Week The scene goes to a press conference type room where there are alot of press guys there taking photos of the guys sitting in the chairs at the front. They are the 2002 Royal Rumble winner, Tony Vigilante, President and co-owner of FAW, Lee Smith and the xCw co-owner (!) British Bomber. Lee Smith: Thankyou for all attending this little signing of the papers that will confirm that Tony Vigilante, the 2002 royal rumble winner, will get a Furiously Addictive Wrestling World Heavyweight Title shot at the biggest event of the year, Extreme Beating number 2. The night will be huge, and with my signature, Bomber's signature, and Tony's signature it will all go ahead smoothly. The reason we need Bomber's signature is that as we wiped out Xtreme Championship Wrestling under a week ago, he must sign this confirming that Tony did actually win the rumble.
