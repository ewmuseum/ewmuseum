May Day Massacre — Card, 5 May 2001

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
WELCOME TO MAY DAY MASSACRE Date: 5th May 2001 Time: 7:00pm (local time) Arena: Astrodome, Houston, Texas Ticket Information: Tickets are sold out, consult the box office for more information... This will be FAW's FIRST PPV under ICWF involvement and FAW want to make it a great success with ALL titles to be on the line.... PLEASE REMEMBER: The card is likely to change so this is NOT at all the final card for the PPV....MAY DAY MASSACRE!!!! -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Non-Title Match The Undertaker vs Mystery Opponent Referee: Adam Yates --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- #1 Contendership Of Hardcore Title Rhyno vs Y2J Chris Jericho Referee: Danyal Griffiths --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- #1 Contendership of World Title Deadman vs Yet To Be Named Referee: John Savva --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Women's Title Debbie vs Callie Stratus vs Sarah vs Trish Stratus (c) vs Zoe Referee: Adam Yates --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Hardcore and European Titles Damo Muzzler (c) vs JLC (c) Referee: John Savva The first fall being a cage match for the Hardcore Title and the second being a casket match for the European title... --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Universal Title British Bulldog vs Lex Luger (c) Referee: John Savva This match sees two men go for the Universal Title in a Tables, Ladders and Chairs (TLC) match... --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- The card is due to change ALL roleplays are due by Saturday at midnight and all forms are due by Saturday at midnight..... The card will change by Wednesday and the FINAL card is due up Tuesday.... Tag Team Titles X-Factor (c) vs The KrusherZ Referee: Danyal Griffiths --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- World Title Mojo vs HaHaKlown (c) Referee: John Savva This match is a Last Man Standing in a Cage match!!! --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- #1 Contendership of European Title Foolio vs Max - X vs Python vs Mortal Maniac Referee: John Savva --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Non-Title Match Blade vs Magic Referee: Adam Yates
