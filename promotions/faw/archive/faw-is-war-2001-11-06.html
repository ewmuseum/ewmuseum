FAW FAW is WAR, 6 November 2001

6 November 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Nassau Coliseum, Uniondale, New York - 16,297 - 06/11/2001 --Transmission Starts-- The transmission starts with highlights of FAW IS WAR, namely the revealing of the kidnappers...The Omens Of Death! With Lisa as manager! This then brought on the booking of JLC versus British Bomber and the main event with the Tag Team Titles on the line! FAW IS WAR Intro "In The End" by Linkin Park hits and the cameras switch on to alot of pyros hitting. Then the cameras fly round the arena a few times before then zooming in on the announce table. James Harris: Welcome to FAW IS WAR! An explosive night tonight and it will be starting in a moments notice! Jackie Williams: Yes, and that huge main event pitting The Omens against Thunder and Bomber, for the tag team titles will be kicking off tonight! James Harris: And Bomber will have 2 matches tonight, one against JLC! And that match will have his main event opponents, The Omens at ringside! Jackie Williams: What a night, and we have one hell of a night lined up! xCw Promo 'Faith' by Limp Bizkit starts up and the owner of xCw makes his way to the ring with his troops. British Bomber - Now as the owner of xCw and half the FAW tag team champions and half the xCw tag team champions I felt it was my duty to announce xCw's champions.....first off we got the Cruiserweight champion...introducing NATHAN COOL Nathan Cool gets into the ring and recieves his title. Nathan Cool - Thankyou Bomber and I would just like to say to all this poor excuses for fans can go kiss Tom The Bombs ass cause when we take over we will fire his ass and fire all the FAW crap that is present The crowd boo and Nathan Cool gets out the ring British Bomber - Now, I would like to introduce to your our tag team champions...me and Chris Thunder Bomber smiles and throws Thunder the other tag title British Bomber - Next up is the Hardcore title, who is the champ...none other than Kathryn Velmont Thomas She smiles and rolls into the ring, the embrace and share a passionate kiss he then hands her the title British Bomber - Now, onto the US title, see there is one special person that I have my eye on in FAW to hold this title....and in the upcoming weks you will find out who he is....he will be the best damn US champ this company has Crowd boo British Bomber - Now Stan and Bryan come in here, I need to present your titles Stan and Deas get into the ring British Bomber - Now the Intercontinental title, you all ask who, well it was a tough decision and we went with Stunning Stan, now Stan I expect you to beat Chimera on Friday and I expect you and Deas to win tonight....... He hands Stan the IC belt British Bomber - Now onto Bryan Deas, well I guess your the champ, and I expect you to defend against all comers....starting with your opponent next week....your the champ, the main man...the guy that will destroy FAW...... Deas takes the title and all the xCw champions pose with their titles...'Faith' by Limp Bizkit hits and the xCw superstars make their way to the back. Morte vs Jason McAndrew James Harris : Next up we have 2 up and coming superstars ready for battle in Morte & Jason McAndrew! Morte's music starts up and he walks down to the ring with a mean look to his face, he climbs the turnbuckle and taunts to the crowd and then McAndrew's music interrupts him! Morte looks down at the ramp waiting for McAndrew but no-one comes, suddenly someone jumps over the crowd barrier... Jackie Williams: McAndrew! He's gonna' start us off with a sneak attack! McAndrew runs into the ring, spins Morte around and delivers multiple punches, he ends with a kick to the gut and a neck breaker, he hooks Morte's leg James Harris: 1... 2... Nope, Morte managed to reach the ropes. McAndrew grabs Morte with his face in McAndrew's armpit, he delivers an inversion of the DDT! He runs off the opposite ropes and delivers an elbow drop to Morte, he then climbs the turnbuckle and positions himself Jackie Williams: Get up Morte! As McAndrew is preparing Morte jumps to his feet and pushes McAndrew off, McAndrew smashes his head on the ring steps! Morte follows McAndrew out and slams his head into the barrier multiple times before climbing it, he kicks McAndrew into the ring, as McAndrew grabs the apron for balance Morte delivers a diving spear! Morte then places McAndrew over his shoulder and rams his head into the turnbuckle before getting into the ring and letting the ref count McAndrew out Referee : 1... 2... 3... 4... McAndrew stirs... Referee : 5... 6... McAndrew stands and returns into the ring only to be beat on by Morte, nonetheless he gets to his feet and starts brawling back, he eventually takes down Morte with a Lou Thesz Press and punches him in the gut. McAndrew gets the sit down pin on Morte but only gets a 1 count. James Harris: Pretty entertaining, the lead has changed has many times so far McAndrew stomps on Morte and places him in the corner, he goes to an opposite corner and climbs the turnbuckle, Morte stands and run's at McAndrew but McAndrew delivers a huge front dropkick almost taking off Morte's head! Morte falls to the floor and keeps sliding and gets his lower extremities smashed into the turnbuckle! Jackie Williams : Looks like Morte is gonna' have no children in his future! McAndrew put's the pin on Morte but as he is just about to gain the 3 count he stops, he stands and climbs the turnbuckle again... James Harris: Oh my god what's coming this time?! McAndrew jumps off of the turnbuckle and goes for a massive diving headbutt...but Morte rolls out of the way and hits a DDT. Pin: 1...2...3! Cut To Backstage The Omens walk into the arena, and find their locker room door locked, Lisa follows and she goes to fetch the janitor... Thunder and Bomber attack The Omens from behind, and they're blanked out on the floor... The Great One and JLC chase off Thunder and Bomber as The Omens recover... Lisa comes back and finds The Omens getting to their feet with Great One and JLC still standing there... Lisa shouts at them, and The Omens beat down Great One and JLC thinking they were the ones who beat 'em down....but then Great One and JLC rise to their feet and they fight back as as the scene fades. Commercial Break Cut To Backstage We go backstage with Spider sitting in his locker room with brother Rick. Rick is reading a magazine and Spider is packing his bags for the next event. Spider: Do you ever wonder…..who are real parents are? Rick: No Spider: I do. I wonder who gave birth to us, where they are no, are they aware of my new fame ? Rick: Good point. Pause. Rick: Do you think we should try to track them down? Spider: Don’t know. Where could we start? Rick: We could go to the agency of adoption. Spider: Wheres that? Rick: New York somewhere. Spider: I don’t really fancy it. How would we get mum and dads records? Rick: Aha. We could ….. Spider: No. No and No. Rick: We could though couldn’t we….it wont do anyone any harm if we just smash a window and climb it will it. Spider: What if it has an alarm? Rick: What do you think I do in my spare time? Spider: I dunno. Rick: I work for an alarm company. That’s why I know where the agency of adoption is. They have an alarm and I am going to go and fix it on Friday because its broken. Spider: Okay. Rick: And all we have to do is take the batteries out and say its working. Spider: …And then break in later on in the night. Rick: Yes! Spider: I like that idea. The two laugh as we go back to the action. The Mortician vs Skipster Jackie Williams: Who will win this? James Harris: Probably Skipster, hes been on form lately. Jackie Williams: The Mortician beat our very own IN Champion on Panic though. James Harris: Oh yeah, I forgot about that. Jackie Williams: You fool. James Harris: I will take that as a compliment, I don’t think we should be giving compliments on air somehow though. Jackie Williams: Here comes Skipster. ‘Ride The Lightning’ plays. Skipster walks out dressed in ripped jeans and a torn shirt. He places his elbow pads firmly on and walks down. He gets into the ring staring at the ground. He calls for the fireworks and shouts out. His voice breaks and he grabs a microphone not showing any anxiety what so ever. Skipster: RIDE THE LIGHTNING PEOPLE! The crowd cheer for Skipster. The camera pans around him and we see several banners. Some of the most common are ‘I wish I was at XCW Blackout now’ and ‘HI ANDY!’. It continues to pan around it while focusing out a bit. We see three more frequent signs ‘ I KIDNAPPED TOM THE BOMB’, ‘FAWCK YOU’ and a cut out of Dark Vader with the words in felt tip pen ‘Let the FAWce be with you’. James Harris: The crowd are really showing their support nowadays and I think some of the wrestlers appreciate it more than they now. Jackie Williams: Skipster is getting some good cheap pops from the crowd. The lights turn off in one go and we hear a click. Flames rise on a plastic chair. They erupt even more and the lights are raised on a silver platter till they maximise. We see The Mortician sitting in the chair with flames on it. He gets up and kicks it into the crowd. Several screams are heard afterwards. The Mortician walks down to the ring shoving his weight past the crowds. He competes in a pre match staring competition with Skipster which ends with The Mortician taking his eyes away to climb the steelsteps. Skipster attacks him while he is not looking and knocks him off the steelsteps. The bell rings even though they are not in the ring. Skipster throws The Morticians head into the table. Jackie Williams: I heard that crash! James Harris: That made me dizzy Jackie. Jackie Williams: Old age is a soft symbol to follow sometimes. Skipster sets up the DDT onto the steps. The Mortician reverses it and spinebusters Skipster down onto the steps. He drags the steps out and throws Skipster off them Jackie Williams: This is nail biting. James Harris: Damn, you wouldn’t want to ruin your lovely nails would you. Jackie Williams: N….was that sarcasm? James Harris: God calm down. Skipster throws The Mortician back into the ring. He slides in. The Mortician stamps on his back and hits an elbow drop on his stomach. Skipster yelps and wraps his legs around Morticians. He drags his arm down and jumps on top of him. Pin:…. Jackie Williams: The referee is day dreaming! Skipster gets up and pushes the referee. The referee stumbles over the ropes. Skipster gasps and turns. The Mortician throws him up against the turnbuckle and slams into him. The Mortician hits a samoan drop and locks in the sharpshooter. Skipster taps violently in the centre of the ring, unable to reach the ropes. The referee keeps down as Mortician refuses to let go off the lock. Skipster drags himself to the ropes. He holds onto them with the crowd booing at the mortician. Skipster now has a firm grip on the ropes James Harris: GET UP REF, Hes missing what this match is about… Jackie Williams: Cheating and violence! James Harris: As opposed to love and comfort. Jackie Williams: Oh hell yeah! James Harris: You look good when you are angry. The referee gets into the ring and pulls The Mortician off of Skipster. He does nothing else and the match continues. Skipster runs at The Mortician. He hits a spinning neckbreaker. The Mortician collapses. Skipster climbs the turnbuckle and raises his hand in approval. He places his fists together but is victim of a cheap shot to the back. The Mortician climbs the turnbuckle with him and holds his leg. He throws him back. Skipster lands with a thump. The Mortician gets down from the turnbuckle and goes for a sidewalk slam. He nails it firmly and hooks the leg. The referee bends over and taps the mat. Pin: 1…2…..3 Jackie Williams: But the referee said 3 before he hit the mat, in the meantime Skipster kicked out. James Harris: Hes been practising. Skipster pushes The Mortician into the turnbuckle and whips him back into the diagonal one. Skipster hits a belly to belly back suplex. The Mortician lands on his feet and hits a clothesline. The Mortician hits a northern lights suplex and pins. Pin: 1….2…3… Jackie Williams: Clean sweep for The Mortician Jason West. James Harris: That’s two wins on the trot for him. Jackie Williams: He deserved it. Cut To Backstage Its after the 1st match between Morte vs Jason McAndrew the camera cuts to backstage in the V.I.P Lounge and there is Spawn talking to Prayer who is warming up, Spawn is telling Prayer all of TG1's awerness's and to watch out for JLC at ringside, Suddenly we hear Giggles as Crimson Lord and the Bride of Darkness Lisa "Crimson's" Bomb walk into the V.I.P Lounge area, They take a seat on a leather couch as spawn talks to crimson. Spawn: Hey man, aint you going to get ready for our match, we face The xCw boyz later tonight, Plus we are at ringside during JLC and Bombers match! Crimson: Hey man, just chill, i have it all in my mind the only thing more important now is Lisa, not only are we to beat xCw later we have to watch out for Lisa(Crimson and Lisa share a kiss), Prayer! Prayer: Yes Crim? Crimson: Tonight Prayer you are to show the world what the Omens are about, you are going to Beat TG1 Senceless! And if JLC shows up i am shure Spawn will have it coverd, Spawn you wanna take him in the Locker room to train, go! Prayer and Spawn leave and it leaves Lisa and Crimson Lisa: you know Crim, after tonight you will have it all, Two belts and me! Crimson: Your all I need. The Scene goes to the ringside.. The Great One vs Prayer Prayers Music is about to hit and Spawn is to accompany him out to the ring, Crimson with lisa attached walks up to Prayer and begins to talk, Crimson: Go out there Bud and kick his Great Ass all over, If it gets hard tell spawn and he will give the signal ok!, Now go and DOn't Get lost in the dark! Prayer's music hits and Prayer walks down to the ring accompanied by SpaWn. They get into the ring and SpaWn whispers something to PRayer and he smiles and then SpaWn jumps out of the ring. James Harris: Now this will be one hell of a match! Jackie Williams: Definitely! The Great One then walks down to the ring unaccompanied and looks at SpaWn and trash talks him but SpaWn just sniggers at The Great One and he climbs each turnbuckle as the fans go crazy for The Great One. He then jumps off the final turnbuckle and the referee calls for the bell. James Harris: You can already feel the tension in the ring for this match up. The Great One and Prayer tie up and The Great One pulls Prayer's arm round his back and Prayer gets out of it and knocks down The Great One with a short arm clothesline. Prayer then picks up The Great One and sets him up for a sambo suplex but he fails it and The Great One delivers a belly to belly suplex. Jackie Williams: Great suplex by Great One, and this is the man who will be taking on the one and only Crimson Lord, the hardcore champion, at Deceit. James Harris: And Prayer is still yet to be booked! Jackie Williams: But what a great wrestler he is. James Harris: And he is trying to prove that tonight. Prayer then goes for a suplex of his own but once again fails and The Great One hits a DDT. Prayer then signals to SpaWn, and he throws a chair into the ring. Prayer picks up the chair and SpaWn distracts the referee... James Harris: This is not right dammit. Prayer swings at Great One who ducks and hits GREATNESS on Prayer. HE then runs over to SpaWn and hits him off the ring apron, throws the chair out of the ring and covers Prayer. Jackie Williams: He is gonna do it. Pin: 1...2...3! The crowd go wild as The Great One climbs up a turnbuckle and then jumps off it and walks up the ramp backwards trash talking SpaWn and Prayer who are both in the ring. Commercial Break Cut To Presidential Lounge Lee Smith is watching tapes of Panic all over again. Lee Smith: I still cant believe it. The Omens of death kidnapped tom all along. Cleaner: Me either. The cleaner takes out her cigarette and throws it in the bin. There is a knock on the door and the cleaner answers as we hear Lisas voice. Lee runs into the closet and the cleaner opens the door. Lisa: We are here to take over the Presidential lounge. Where is Mr Smith? Cleaner: He went out….. Lisa: Why? Cleaner: To um……find that thing. Lisa: What thing ? Cleaner: It is private. Lisa: Oh well, We will just have to move in now then. The camera fades out with Lees eyes visible in the closet and Lisa throwing the cleaner out and locking the door. Spider vs Chimera Jackie Williams: Spider looks set for a comfortable victory here. James Harris: Perhaps. New Disease By Spineshank plays and the music stops suddenly. Spider walks out and the crowd go silent as we hear footsteps. He stamps once and pyros go off and the music continues. Lasers point around the arena and he approaches the end of the ramp. He jumps forward and more fireworks go off. He gets into the ring receiving a good reaction from the huge amount of crowd. Jackie Williams: He looks on form. James Harris: That could be said yes. Jackie Williams: What would you say? James Harris: He looks on form Jackie Williams: You are weird. James Harris: I pride myself in being different from others. Chimeras theme music hits. He run out from the crowd and is mobbed. A fan reaches out and clings onto his ‘FAW FOR LIFE’ shirt. He elbows the fan and the fan is removed from the arena by security guards prompting all other possible trouble makers to not attempt to distract Chimera James Harris: What a task this man has. Jackie Williams: To defeat Spider, this would be one step up for Chimera. James Harris: Definitely. Chimera gets into the ring. The bell rings. Spider throws Chimera into the ropes. He hits an arm lock. Chimera is taken to the ground. Chimera nails Spider in the chest. Spider falls back but recovers. Spider hits a front suplex. Chimera reverses it and goes for the powerbomb. Spider jumps down and kicks Chimera in the ankle. Chimera is wiped off is feet. Jackie Williams: He chopped his legs off, literally! Spider hits an uneasy DDT. Chimera lands roughly. James Harris: He seems to have twisted something there. Chimera gets up and receives a brainbuster for his hard work. Spider locks in a sleeper. The referee dives down and raises Chimeras hand. Chimeras hand falls. He does it again. Chimeras hand drops. He does it for the third and final time. James Harris: ooooh…. Chimeras hand falls and Spider lets go, celebrating his victory. Jackie Williams: A submission victory for Spider. James Harris: Well done to him. It was darn close though. Cut To Backstage Lisa now has all her stuff unpacked. She lies down on the couch and decides to go for a shower. Lee climbs out of the closet and hears the door handle shuffle. Tom The Bomb enters and we hear Lisa shout ‘darling, Im here’. She assumes it is Crimson Lord making the noise. Tom looks at Lee and thinks that Lee has betrayed him. Tom pushes Lee over onto the floor. Lisa hears the noise and gets out of the shower with a towel on. She screams as she sees Tom punching Lee. Lisa: Calm down. How the hell did you two get In here. CALL SECURITY! She presses the red button and security storm in and drag Tom away. Lee holds onto his ribs which are hurt as Lee puts on a high heeled shoe and kicks him out. She slams the door and sighs. She looks at the table which says ‘Presidents Office’ on a plaque. She laughs as we go back to the action. JLC vs British Bomber - Omens At Ringside Jackie Williams: The eagerly anticipated match comes to our TV screens. James Harris: We have seen so many events take place over the last few weeks, what comes next I wonder? Jackie Williams: You think there might be a dramatic change? Dare I say it. James Harris: Its quite possible and you know it. Jackie Williams: I suppose it is. But onto other things, who are you voting for to be the world champion after Deceit? James Harris: I cant say just right now, I cant tell. Its going to be a really close one, or so I sense. Jackie Williams: Yes, I think we are all feeling that. James Harris: And now that we are on this main event subject, I would just like to say the rumours going around about Lee Smith are false! Jackie Williams: So in other words you are going to buy FAW and are covering it up. James Harris: No. The #1 Contender for the world titles music hits as JLC walks out. He poses for the crowd with them cheering. He makes his way down to the ring as the commentators take notes Jackie Williams: I cannot WAIT for this to start. Surely a rollercoaster for all involved. James Harris: You summed it up. ‘Sex Bomb’ by Tom Jones plays (remix) And Lisa Da Sex Bomb appears at the top of the ramp. She flashes some flesh and walks down to the ring with Spawn and Crimson Lord slightly behind her James Harris: [screams] Guess who is looking hoooot? Lisa gets into the ring under close protection from the omens. She grabs a microphone and speaks at JLC. Lisa: Now Jim, may I call you that? JLC: You can call me whatever you want woman. Lisa: I gather you have heard this is a Non DQ match. JLC: Yes. Lisa: JLC, prepare for death on ice. JLC: Whats that supposed to… A microphone makes a huge sound as we see from behind the British Bomber has attacked JLC over the head. Lisa laughs and walks out of the ring. JLC is flattened as Crimson Lord and Spawn stamp on him. Crimson Lord and Bomber hold him back. Spawn gives right hands left hands , both hands to JLC and causes a great deal of damage. Jackie Williams: This is a bloodbath James, this is a vicious assault! James Harris: Damn, for once your right Jackie, this is not right. The camera zooms on JLC who looks back to the ramp waiting for his allies to come down and help him. He rolls over and gets up with blood dripping from his head. He strikes Bomber in the head and takes him down. He then hits a LooPlex on Crimson Lord and looks at Spawn. He takes Spawns World Title off the belt and brandishes it. He hits Spawn with it and goes to the outside. He grabs Lisa Da Sex Bomb and holds onto her neck. He strangles her Jackie Williams: Someone do something! Lisas face goes shatteringly red and she begins to drop to her knees. Suddenly JLC falls and as he moves we see Crimson Lord with a chair in his hand. Lisa and Crimson hug as Spawn comes out and throws JLC into the ring. JLC shakes. Spawn throws Bomber on top of JLC Pin: 1…2…… Jackie Williams: He kicked out! The fans look in disbelief has JLC gets his hand up. Lisa and Crimson Lord walk up the ramp with Lisa almost reduced to tears. James Harris: JLC scared then off. Looks like Crimson is taking her back to the locker. Jackie Williams: Looks like its nearly over. James Harris: That decisive move could be the turning point of the match. JLC throws Bomber into Spawn. Spawn sits in the turnbuckle knocked out and JLC rolls up Bomber who retrieves back to the centre. Pin: 1..2…3… James Harris: Jesus MARY and JOSEPH, J-L-C WON! VICTORY FOR JLC! JLC WINS! Jackie Williams: I don’t believe it. James Harris: That was absolutely amazing The fans cheer unanimously as Spawn opens his eyes and sighs. He buries his head in his hands and stands up. He drags Bomber off from the floor and pushes him out of the ring. He stands next to JLC and taps him on the back. JLC turns and opens his eyes widely. They go nose to nose as the crowd cheer and chant ‘Fight’ repeatedly. Crimson Lord walks down to the ring obviously having settled Lisa and drags Spawn away telling him its not worth it. As Spawn disappears JLC smiles. Cut To Thunder's Locker Room Chris Thunder: So, you want to become my new manager? Manager: Yes. Chris Thunder: Are you married? Manager: No. Chris Thunder: Single? Manager: Yes. Chris Thunder: That will do for me, you are hired. Manager: When can I start? Chris Thunder: Panic Manager: Okay. David McKay & Scott "The Enforcer" Kruze (FAW) vs Bryan Deas & Stunning Stan (xCw) - Inter-Promo Match Jackie Williams: And next the xCw invasion team will be fighting the FAW team of warriors! In this case it will be Bryan Deas, who we found out to be the xCw World Champion at the top of the show and the Intercontinental Champion, Stunning Stan! James Harris: And as soon as the champions for xCw were announced, matches have been booked by President Lee Smith for Friday night's event! And what a huge event that will be. Jackie Williams: Well, a night on Friday which will be like no other! It only took xCw 2 weeks to get into FAW shows, and now they are in they want some domination. James Harris: And hopefully their titles will come to the FAW ranks sooner than we think! Jackie Williams: And what about FAW titles in xCw ranks, we don't like tot alk about it but it must be a possibility... The invasion team comes out first through Stan and Deas. They get into the ring. James Harris: And they are in the ring and they are awaiting the arrival of their opponents now. Jackie Williams: And that will be the team of McKay and Kruze. McKay and Kruze then walk out to their music over the tanoid system. They also get into the ring. James Harris: The FAW team now out to the ring, they are against the xCw World and IC champions, and Kruze has the light-heavyweight championship! Jackie Williams: Well it should be a food match with 4 great competitors. The bell rings. All 4 men start to pound away on each other. Stan and McKay are then left in the ring and they start to battle. Stan throws McKay out of the ring much to the delight to Deas who gets the tag, Stan goes to the outside and throws McKay back into the ring. Deas then hits a DDT on McKay and goes and tags in Stan as McKay crawls to his corner and tags in Kruze. Kruze then delivers a huge body slam to Stan and parades round the ring and then walks back over to Stan who hits him in the gut and then delivers a snap suplex. James Harris: Nice movement from Stan here tonight. Jackie Williams: Showing what xCw are made of! James Harris: Surely are. Stan then tags in Deas and they double suplex Kruze and McKay jumps into the ring but the referee holds him back and Stan runs over and clotheslines McKay over the top rope. Deas then grabs up Kruze and delivers a huge powerbomb and covers the FAW member. Pin: 1...2...3! James Harris: It's over! xCw have defeated FAW! Jackie Williams: Very nice piece of work from their guys, but FAW need to buck up their ideas! James Harris: They will have to if they wanna be successful against xCw! Commercial Break The Omens At Death vs Team xCw (c) - Tag Team Titles - Special Ref: Lisa - No DQ James Harris: Main event time Jackie, and what a match, a match made by Lisa just last Friday night, and now look, it is here! What a match it will be, Tag Team Titles, FAW vs xCw, xCw in possession of the titles, Lisa as the referee. It all looks good for FAW! Jackie Williams: Except for JLC and Great One, they could easily cost the Omens this match-up. James Harris: Great One and JLC could kick the Omens asses but that doesn't mean that The Omens are out of it, they could also come back, but it isn't too likely! Jackie Williams: Well all we need is for the egotistical maniacs to cost the Omens the match and make sure the titles don't come back from FAW. James Harris: Great One and JLC are FAW for life and I bet they will stay well away from this match here tonight. Jackie Williams: Well that remains to be seen. A re-mix of Tom Jones' "Sex Bomb" hits and Lisa walks down to the ring wearing tight black trousers and a black and white bra-top. She gets into the ring through the bottom rope pausing as she gets half way and then getting into the ring with a mixed reaction, but more boos, and the cheers are only for her body! Jackie Williams: LISA! She makes all of us women proud, she shows us to stand up to men! James Harris: Ahh, shut up! Jackie Williams: NO! See, it works! James Harris: Oh my god..... The challengers music then hits and The Omens Of Death walk down to the ring with their Hardcore and World Title belts over their shoulders. They get into the ring and Crimson hugs the recently pregnantated Lisa and then SpaWn smiles at her. They then await their opponents arrival talking to Lisa. Jackie Williams: Your new tag team champions! James Harris: Can't you just shut the hell up? Jackie Williams: Well they will be after this match, trust me. James Harris: Well that remains to be seen, but the xCw jerks are on their way to the ring now! Jackie Williams: We both agree they are jerks then! WOW! "Faith" by Limp Bizkit hits once more and British Bomber and Chris Thunder, of xCw, walk down to the ring with their Tag Team Title belts. They jump into the ring and Lisa rings the bell and the match is underway with both SpaWn and Crimson Lord in the ring and Lisa not making either team get out onto the ring apron. James Harris: Has Lisa made this match a tornado tag? Lisa looks over at the announce table and nods. Jackie Williams: WOW! An even greater addition to this already great match. SpaWn is beating away on Thunder as Crimson Lord does the same to Bomber. SpaWn then goes for the LAST KILL but Thunder reverses it and throws the world champion over his back and he lands on his back as Thundr picks him up. Thunder then goes for the STORMER but Crimson Lord clotheslines Thunder down as British Bomber walks over and grabs Crimson Lord and throws him over the top rope and to the floor. Lisa then walks infront of Team xCw who walk over to SpaWn. Crimson Lord then jumps in from behind taking down both Bomber and Thunder. James Harris: Pure cheating from us there! FAW is being disgraced by The Omens and Lisa! SpaWn then gets up only to find that JLC jumps into the ring and knocks down Lisa for Tom The Bomb and then he goes for SpaWn, but SpaWn ducks a clothesline and then hits the LAST KILL on JLC. HE then goes for Thunder and grabs him and throws him to Crimson, who hits a BLOOD MIST and covers Thunder as SpaWn delivers a LAST KILL to Bomber. Pin: 1...2...3! Jackie Williams: What a match, and we have new Tag Team champions. As the referee gets out of the ring to get the title belts, JLC hands them to the referee, and the hardcore belt but he walks up the ramp with the World Title belt. The Great One and Tom The Bomb appear at the top of the ramp and they raise JLC's hands. The scene then fades with SpaWn, Lisa and Crimson Lord looking up the ramp at Tom The Bomb, JLC and Crimson Lord all with their hands high. --Transmission Ends--
