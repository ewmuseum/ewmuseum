(Read 10 times) Roger Rabbit Guest Summer Slaughter Rumours. « on: September 11th, 2001, 1:51pm » Summer Slaughter is going to be the biggest and best p.p.v. is FAW history, with some of the greatest matches ever, but some of things that are going to happen will be huge. With Pyro as the new world champion, The psycho representing the red white and blue, the American Psycho, will return and screw Pyro out of the world title. Another man who will be returning will be the devils servant, and he will screw The Great One out of his match with Natas and take away all of Great One's pride because Great One took away his parents. Summer Slaughter will be the night when Furious Alliance dismember, as the world title will come down to The Omega Fro and Skipster, and they will beat the hell out of each other as one of them wins the title. The rest of FA will then come down and beat the nuts out of each other as it has not been going well for them recently with all the titles they have lost. The only title they hve now is the president of the federation. It will also be the night when Continental connection and Dark gen I mean D-generation-X, join together to make one big and nasty stable that will dominate the federation. This will mean all the faces in the federation being in deep trouble, which will mean Great One joining Omens of Death of course with a different name, with Skipster and Omega Fro also helping them out after they put their differences aside.
