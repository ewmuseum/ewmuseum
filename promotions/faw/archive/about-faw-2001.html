About Fantasy Wrestling Don't understand the concept of fantasy wrestling? It's a fairly simple game to play...and a lot of fun to read and interact with. FAW is one of many e-feds on the Internet today. Hopefully, once you read this page, it will give you an idea of what Fantasy Wrestling is about and what some of the terms used in the guide mean.. First, Here are a few terms that you should know the meaning of: Roleplays- RPs for short. Roleplaying is an interview your wrestler makes in an effort to prove he's better than he's oppenant. Cards- Lineup of matches. Our Cards are FAW is War, Blood Is Thicker Than Water and Friday Night Fear. We also have a monthly PPV. Fixed/Booked- Any fantasy fed where roleplays determine the winners. There is little luck involved in a booked fed... most outcomes are determined through roleplaying ability, interactivity from the members, and ideas etc from a member. A good balance with all of these makes a champion NotFixed/NonBooked- Any fantasy fed where the luck of the draw determines winners. The President of the fed either lets a program pick a random winner, or just picks one randomly him/herself. Handler- The creator of the wrestler. I am Mr. X's handler.. In other words, I am the owner/creator of Mr. X. FAW is an optimized mix of a booked and nonbooked chemistry, it works a bit like a real wrestling organization. A wrestler will challenge another wrestler and they will soon be setting a date to step into the ring. Once the date is set, they will have "interviews" that will feature them speaking against each other. One of the more important factors to wrestling, and who gets a high push (high push means they're more likely to win) is if they can do a good interview. Chris Jericho and the Rock would be two perfect examples of this- Both men can get the crowd to cheer, boo, yell, and scream just by speaking. The same concept works in FAW. The only difference is, roleplaying is what determines your push. Roleplaying, essentially, is your voice in fantasy wrestling. In a nonbooked fed, roleplaying would determine the winners completely. You would roleplay, your opponent would roleplay back to you, and you would go back and forth roleplaying until the specific date of the card. The matches would then be simmed, typed, or posted for you to view. The catch for a booked fed is, as long as you roleplay alot and with the best quality, your going to win. The catch with the nonbooked fed is, no matter which wrestler (most usually) does the best roleplaying, the person to win will most generally be random. FAW, as you can tell, is a good mixture of the good parts of both. Not only do we we pick our winners from the outcomes of roleplays, but we also make sure that it stays in the realm of reality.. In other words, you can not join one day, do some hotshot roleplaying, and become World Champion next week. You have to work your way up and prove yourself worthy.. Alot of wrestlers get trampled on when they first enter because they pick on everyone and everyone retaliates, this leads to them giving up and not roleplaying.. Which then leads to them being fired. We have a perfect tandom... Your not going to get screwed either way. I will add more and modify this to include more detail when I get a chance to.
