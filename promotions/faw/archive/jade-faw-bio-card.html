Career history

Things started out well for Jade in life. Good family, great financial background, hell she even had a good education. By the age of 19 she was an overacheiving Personal Assistant. Renoun throughout Philadelphia for being a 'tough nut.' I mean in a professional manner.Jade kept in shape like any professional person, using Judo, Tai Kwan Do and an excessive amount of aerobics. Her parents doted on their one and only child. Giving her anything and everything that she wanted. At most times they seemed a little dismayed at just how focused she was on her job. She had her own home, car and was totally focused on living out life to the full. Although her personal life was somewhat empty. The opnly love in her life was a little bulldog. Until that fateful day...When she met a future DWO star...Switchblade. As he slid in front of her in that quiet little restaraunt. His head a little laccerated from the leg of the table. The wheels on his skates still rolling quickly as he laid on his face. The relationship blossomed from there. Soon Jade gave in her Personal assistant 'career' to follow her lover around America on a series of DWO shows. Promoting each event obsessively.Things went downhill from there. The more relentlessly jade promoted the DWO, the more successful it became. But her relationship slacked. Michael, aka Switchblade, soon became attached elsewhere. In the open arms of Kristi Kiss. As Jade watched tape after tape of the dates and the kisses, goodnees knows what else. Jade just simply faded. Her spirit, her hope of happiness dissapeared. To be replaced with a depression, unhappiness and ANGER. An anger which changed her felings, her strength and supremely her physique. She became focused on one target of these affections...Switchblade and exactly what she would do when she had THAT chance. To tunnel all of her energies into the ring. She knows there is only one way in which she will be able to do this. Inside of a DWO ring.Bare in mind. If I do get in I shall be posting this sample rp as part of her first rp...The BIO will be the first time.jade fought her way through most of the Wo lower card, beating men and women alike. Eventually finding another chance at the big time by means of giving her life away to some Holy force. This being possessed her until the time which Chryste Died. With no meaning nor reason for that posession to remain, it left, aking all jade's memories one by one with her. Now Jade is desperately trying to regain her memory.
