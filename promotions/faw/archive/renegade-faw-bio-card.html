Career history

Renegade made his FAW debut in September of 2002 when he made an unexpected appearance at the end of the Spawn Vs ??? match at Distortion 15.Renegade jumped the guard rail before assaulting Spawn over the back of the head with a steel chair and then ran off up the entrance ramp. Using the excuse of “wanting to get noticed” for the unprovoked attack.Renegade’s first match in FAW came the following week at Distortion 16, were he took part in a Mini Royal Rumble match for the International title number one contenders spot. The match itself was pretty uneventful for Renegade as he was eliminated early on by the eventual winner, Syphon X.Next up for Renegade was a Tag Team Elimination match up at his first FAW Pay Per View, Entrapment. Renegade competed against 5 other men and ended up winning the match along with Rzarector. Earning the unlikely pair a Tag Team title shot at Distortion 17 against then champions Spawn and The Great One.Renegade and Rzarector (Dubbed R+R by some), won the match at Distortion 17 after The Great One turned on his team mate and hit him over the head with a steel chair, allowing Rzarector to pick up the pin fall. Renegade had his first taste of FAW championship gold.AT Distortion 18, Renegade went one-on-one with the monster that was Apocalypse. Towards the end of the match, Renegade set Apocalypse up for his finishing manoeuvre, the ‘Blackout’, when Apocalypse counted which resulted in Renegade being dropped on this head. Apocalypse then picked up the almost unconscious Renegade and gave him the ‘Rise Above’ Apocalypse devastating choke slam finisher. And the match was over. Renegade had to be helped to the back by the EMT’s. He was later taken to a local hospital for observation, and released the following day.Renegade was fit enough to compete the following week at Distortion 19, where he and Rzarector went toe-to-toe with Crimson Lord and The Duke in a none title match up. Renegade was still having problems with his neck from the previous weeks match against Apocalypse and Crimson and The Duke picked up on this and aimed there attacks on Renegade at this weakened area. R+R showed great team work throughout the match and came out on top once again. Renegade this time picking up the three count after giving The Duke the ‘Blackout’. TO BE CONTINUED...
