FAW Rules (2001)

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Home - When you join, make sure to start getting a feel of what roleplaying in FAW is all about.. Then choose your destiny and roleplay. Do not waste your time and mine by joining if you are not going to be an avid roleplayer. - You can create up to 2 wrestlers but that is only if you wish to be a tag team, eg. The Dudleyz. - When you roleplay, please, watch the language. Occasional swearing is acceptable, but not every other word. - When you roleplay, please make sure to spellcheck before submitting it to the Press Conference. This will make you, and FAW in general, look better. - Roleplay AT LEAST 3 times a week.. I prefer to see at least one roleplay before each show and at least one after each show. - Quality of Quantity is my perception.. I love to see roleplays that span out 18 lines, but I prefer reading a good 8 liner before a watered down and drawn out 18 lined one. 6 lines is the minimum because anyone can type up five lines. Remember, using descriptions before and after you speak should be at least a line a piece. - Remember to have fun and don't sign up too many wrestlers if your time is pretty well spent. 1 good roleplaying wrestler is ten times better than 2 wrestlers that roleplay rarely. - Also, ALWAYS READ ALL RESULTS.. I make all the results very interesting and alot of people only read their match.. It is easy to tell and hurts the effectiveness of your wrestlers and their roleplaying due to the lack of knowledge of who is who.... Basic Rules with straight forward meanings. You follow these rules, and optimize your roleplaying ability with the guide I created for you and you are on the way to the top. Remember: FAW is just for fun, but you have alot more fun when your on top of the game..
