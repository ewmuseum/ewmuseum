The Great One Vs The Omega Fro - Ending

8 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The Great One Vs The Omega Fro - Ending Date: December 8th Show: Sunday Night Panic Floyd Rotterdam: Well that's another great victory for The Great One who has certainly been impressing recently to say the least, but now what's he doing? Shane Riot: Well duh, he's taking a chair from underneath the ring, he must be tired and wants to sit down. Mario Ciaralli: Or maybe he's about to do a number on The Omega Fro, we all heard what Great One said earlier, he's tired of facing and beating Fro and maybe he's going to try and make sure he never has to face him again. Shane Riot: No way, The Great One wouldn't do that, he's a role model and he loves his fans, why would be want to disappoint them? Great One takes a chair from underneath the ring and slides it in. He walks up the steps and into the ring and picks up the chair and walks over to the fallen Omega fro. Floyd Rotterdam: I think you might be right Mario, unless he's going to lend Omega Fro a helping hand and give him a seat, which I highly doubt. Great One folds out the chair in the position for someone to sit down on it. Shane Riot: You see, Great One is way to classy to hit someone with a chair. He'll probably get a table out next and put some food out on it for Fro to eat. I'm not being sarcastic, not in the least. Mario Ciaralli: Shut up Shane. Shane Riot: You can't tell me to shut up, no one can tell me to shut up. Floyd Rotterdam: Shut up Shane! Great One helps Fro up and asks him if he's alright. Fro nods but isn't exactly in the best of shape. Great One asks him if he wants to sit down, or at least it looks that way because the camera doesn't pick up what he says. Fro shakes his head to say he's ok. Great One goes to walk away but turns around, kicks him in the gut and DDT's Fro onto the chair. Floyd Rotterdam: OH MY GOD! WHAT THE HELL WAS THAT? Shane Riot: HAH! I was wrong about The Great One, he really does have class! Great One picks Fro up and whips him into the turnbuckle. He follows that up with a clothesline and then throws him to the floor. Mario Ciaralli: This is a completely different side that we are seeing of The Great One, and now where is he going? Great One jumps out of the ring and goes under the ring once again. This time he pulls out a trash can and a table. Floyd Rotterdam: What is he doing with a trash can and a table? Shane Riot: He's being the perfect host. First he offered Fro a chair, now he's getting him a table and trash can to put his leftovers into. What I would do to be able to eat at The Great One's house! Mario Ciaralli: Well if he does to his house guest's at dinner what he is doing to Fro now then I certainly would not like to dine at The Great One's home. Great One slides the table into the ring but leaves the trash can outside. Before returning into the ring he removes one of the mats at ringside, unveiling the concrete floor. He now gets back into the ring and sets up the table as Fro makes his way to his feet. Great One sees Fro making a move and quickly picks up the steel chair and gives him a hard shot right in the head. Floyd Rotterdam: This is ridiculous, what is the meaning of this? This is the Great One that we have all come to know and love, this isn't The Great One that we have seen over the last two months doing everything for his fans and being the good guy. Shane Riot: When you say that you loved him... Floyd Rotterdam: Don't even start Shane. Shane Riot: Sorry. Great One puts the chair on the table and lifts Omega Fro up onto the top rope so that he is facing away from the ring. Great One climbs up to the middle rope and puts Omega Fro in position for a tiger suplex. Mario Ciaralli: Oh God no. I don't remember ever seeing The Great One use a tiger suplex as one of his moves, but if he chooses to use it now then the results will be very, very bad for The Omega Fro. Great One then lunges backwards and hits a tiger suplex onto the chair and through the table. Floyd Rotterdam: I do not believe it! Why Great One, why? The fans reaction is one that is very unfamiliar towards The Great One as they start to boo his actions. Shane Riot: Can you believe it!? These fans are booing The Great One! Great One gets up and lifts his hands in the air proud of what he has done. A big smile emerges on his face as he gives a 'whoo' to the crowd at hand who boo him even more. Shane Riot: Did you ever think you would hear a round of boos for The Great One? Floyd Rotterdam: That I did not and I also did not think that I would ever, ever see The Great One go to these levels. Mario Ciaralli: If it was someone like Spawn in there then I can imagine The Great One doing it, but The Omega Fro? There is no beef between these two? This is The Great One trying to get rid of one of the main men who has a chance of standing in his way of being the FAW World Champion. Great One looks down to the floor and sees the spot where the mat has been removed. Great One lifts Omega Fro up and whips him into the ropes that are on the opposite side of the side that has had the mats removed on the outside. Great One stands by the ropes and as Fro comes back Great One delivers a belly to belly overhead suplex, where The Omega Fro flies over the top rope and his back smacks against the concrete floor. Floyd Rotterdam: OH MY FUCKING GOD! Shane Riot: YOUR GOD!? WHAT ABOUT MINE!? Mario Ciaralli: SON OF A BITCH! Great One clears his ears out with his two index fingers and then puts his hands to his ears to hear what the crowd are shouting. It's no longer a chant of 'Great One, Great One' but 'Great One sucks, Great One sucks.' Great One looks as if he is going to cry from the crowds words, but then his unhappy face turns to anger and he goes to the outside and lifts Fro up again. Floyd Rotterdam: Haven't you done enough? Just leave him alone; we have got to get security out here now! Great One picks Fro up and takes him over the trash can. Great One hooks one arm and then looks around to the crowd. With his free arm he drums his head, thinking of what to do, and then when he finishes thinking, he hooks to the other arm and then gives Fro a double arm DDT onto the trash can! Mario Ciaralli: I'm speechless! Shane Riot: Obviously not otherwise you wouldn't have been able to say, 'I'm speechless.' Floyd Rotterdam: These are the acts of a desperate man if you ask me. Shane Riot: Well nobody did ask you so why don't you shove your comments up your mums dress. Floyd Rotterdam: I've never done that, why don't you give me a demonstration on your mum because you've had enough practice. Mario Ciaralli: Stop arguing, can't you see that something drastic is going on right now? Great One stands up and kicks Fro a few times while he is down. Great One then looks around the crowd to receive more boos. He walks away as if he is finished but as he walks past the steps he kicks the top of the steps off and throws it to one side, leaving just the bottom half. Floyd Rotterdam: Oh come on Great One, just leave will you, what else can you possibly do? Great One lifts Fro up to his feet and stands on the steps. He puts Fro's head between his legs, facing the floor. Mario Ciaralli: He's not going to do what I think he's going to do is he? Shane Riot: Probably not considering the chance of you thinking are about the same chance as you getting laid tonight, and the chances of that are unfeasible. Great One lifts him up into the piledriver position. Floyd Rotterdam: NO! And Great One drops to his ass and drops Fro's head onto the steel steps and finally ending the carnage as they are suddenly bombarded by refs and road agents. Floyd Rotterdam: Thank God someone came out here to stop this. Shane Riot: If you wanted it stopped so damn much then why didn't you get off your fat ass and out of your chair and stop it yourself? Floyd Rotterdam: Because my job is to commentate, ok. Great One is pulled away and pushed up the ramp as everyone surrounds Omega Fro. Great One walks backwards up the ramp with a sick smile on his face and an 'asshole' chant starts to fill the arena. Medics go past The Great One to tend to Omega Fro and one runs in the back of Great One and falls down. As Great One yells at him the medic tries to get to his feet but Great One keeps pushing him back down. Mario Ciaralli: Oh come one, first he destroys Omega Fro, don't tell me he's going to destroy that medic as well. Shane Riot: Mario buddy, I hate to tell you this but, he's going to destroy the medic. Mario: NO! Great One picks up the medic and slaps him around the face. He gives him another slap, and a third, and then puts him in position for greatness, and he hits it on the steel ramp, knocking the medic out. Floyd Rotterdam: The Great One has some serious problems that he should seek medical help for. Shane Riot: Nope, The Great One has seen the light and now knows what he must do to win the FAW World Title. Great One lifts his arms into the air as The Omega Fro is loaded onto a stretcher. Great One walks through the curtains and is slowly followed by the refs, road agents and medics with Omega fro on a stretcher. They help the other medic as they go past him and then everyone returns to the back after that shocking moment in FAW. Floyd Rotterdam: Unbelievable, absolutely unbelievable. I never though I'd see the day when The Great One would do such a thing like that. Shane Riot: I never thought you'd see the day that saw a woman other than your mum's knickers. Mario Ciaralli: How can you joke at a time like this Shane? Shane Riot: Oh, I wasn't joking, I have read Floyd's diaries. Floyd Rotterdam: We don't know what that was all about folks, but I think that The Great One should be mighty ashamed of himself and should leave this arena at this instant!
