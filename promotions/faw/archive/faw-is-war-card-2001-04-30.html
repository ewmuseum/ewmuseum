FAW IS WAR — Card, 30 April 2001

WELCOME TO FAW IS WAR Date: 30th April 2001 Time: 7:30pm (local time) Arena: Allstate Arena, Chicago, Illinois Ticket Information: Tickets are sold out, consult the box office for more information FAW's first card in the ICWF interfed, trying to impress the head of ICWF and also the Television Networks along with the fans worldwide who will tune in to see this stunning FAW IS WAR..... There is going to be a stunning show come your way so make sure you tune in and watch it and enjoy it! This coming Monday night will see FAW go to the Allstate Arena in Chicago and good luck to all involved.... -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Non-Title Match Magic vs Austin Referee: Adam Yates Magic makes his debut against the texas rattlesnake . --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- International Title Match Deadman vs Spike Referee: Adam Yates Deadman finally gets his shot at gold . --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Non-Title Match British Bulldog vs Justin Credible Referee: John Savva Bulldog battles one half of the tag team champions . --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Non title Match Lex Luger vs X-Pac Referee: Adam Yates and Luger gets the other half ! --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Non-Title Match Damo Muzzler vs Max X vs Mortal Maniac Referee: Adam Yates Speechless i think is the word ! --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Non-Title Match HaHaKlown vs JLC Special Guest Referee: Mojo The loser will leave town AFTER the PPV though!!!! --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- The card will be posted Monday evening unless extreme external influences prevent this from occuring. The rp deadline for the card is this coming Sunday evening at 12 AM and the deadline for angles and other non-match submissions is Sunday evening at 10 PM, angles and rps after their deadlines will not be considered for the matchs unless extreme circumstances once more. Any questions or queries regarding the card or any suggestions feel free to contact me by either AIM or email me. Hope you all enjoy the card and rp lots so to make sure the card is the success it has the potential to be.. The results WILL be up early Tuesday evening if not Monday!
