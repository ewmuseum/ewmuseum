FAW Wednesday Night Panic Live from Philadelphia, Pennsylvania, First Union Center Live Wednesday 19th June 2002 Enimies Re-Unite? (INTRO) Mario: Hello and welcome to Panic, where questions are to be answered and faces debut! Shane: The question on everyone's lips is who is the man that came out after Bomber's and Spawn's match and also who are the 3 mystery men in the Diabolical Day tournament. Floyd: Diabolical Day brackets get released on Distortion guys when hopefully we will find out who they are. Shane: Well questions to be answered but James Harris has the 9th Wonder Of Foam backstage right now. (James Harris stands with the 9th Wonder Of Foam) James: Tonight you face the Mass Murderers, but you asked for this space to be devoted to something else. Foamy: Yes, that something is the nXn. James: Yes FAW’s latest recruits, I see that you have a history with them? Foamy: Oh yes. I came to FAW coming off my re-entry into wrestling following a very short-lived retirement. I brought the FWO with me, but now it lies in tatters. Out of all the people I thought I’d never see again the nXn was one group of people I thought I’d never ever see. James: What is your problem with them? Foamy: Where to begin where to begin. Well they killed off my company, World Foam Wrestling. Joe LaFontaine was in that group then. When he turned up in FAW I thought it was merely a co-incidence. Then X-Rodd turns up and now Etan Cross and the two crack fiends that follow him. I chased them around 720 Wrestling until it closed and now it appears that they have found my trail and have landed once more here. James: Do you think they have come for you? Foamy: Probably not. But let me tell you now just like Spiderman I thought I would never have that arch nemesis, but perhaps the one group of people other than Quakers who I cannot get along with at all are the nXn. They hate my jovial ways and I hate their fake ness, the air of mystery that likes to surround them, mixed meanings and metaphors that hide their insecurities. James: Are you out to get them? Foamy: So far every confrontation has been initiated by them, but it’s time that the Foamy One finally ended this chase. I beat X-Rodd for my first World Championship in SHWC, I defeated Joe LaFontaine last week and now the time is coming whether it be next week or next year that the Foamy One will once again step into that ring and defeat Etan Cross. I have done it before but I didn’t finish it off. James: What about the FWO? Foamy: It is all but dead. Perhaps it is time to form new alliances and form stronger partnerships. But it is good to see old faces. I had hoped though that Etan Cross would have stopped this wannabe Vampire shtick and grown as a person. It is also good to see that Blayze has recovered from her vagina narrowing surgery because back in 720 every man in the locker room nearly fell into that cravass. James: Any last thoughts before your match? Foamy: Only that I hope that the nXn are watching out for the new Foamy One. Also I can’t wait to hear that good old crap spew from their mouths about how my silly ways are well silly and that blood shall flow yadda yadda yadda. First there was Foam, then there were Renegades… Foam World Order vs Mass Murderers The next match up came to a brief start, the showing came with the Mass Murders taking on the Foam World Order. The match up started with Black Ghost taking on Neo. As the two went together, Ghost got the upper hand early hitting a bunch of closelines on the bigger Neo. But the twisted started to come back but it came to a crashing holt as Ghost came with some big upper hand chops to Neo. As the match went on Ghost taggee in his partner Nightmare as he went to work on the lower body of Neo.~ Nightmare with the upper hand in the match hit some power house dropkicks to the upper chest and lower back but then the tide turned as Neo hit a big spear on Nightmare. The two men made the taps to their partners as 9th Wonder came in red hot. Closeline here and spinning heel kick there. He even made a cover on to Nightmare but it was stopped by Black Ghost. The match hit it high point as Neo got tagged in and took it right to Nightmare. He hit a big powerbomb right in the middle of the ring but the match ended with Ghost actidently hitting Nightmare in the head with a chair. Neo got the three count as the crowd went wild. As Neo stood up, out of the crowd came Apocalypse nailing a big chair shot to the head as 9th wonder came to the rescue. The scene then faded to the back. Segment #2 The scene faded back to a black screen, suddenly a few words written in fire came over the a picture of the country of spain.... "Me volví a su país para hacer algo con mi vida, porque a 2 años que he estado esperando una oportunidad, y ahora finalmente la he tomado". It was quickly translated into enlighs for everyone to know.... "I came back to his country to do something with my life, for 2 years i've been waiting for an opportunity, and now i have finally taken it". We then went inside the arena where ever kept the eye on the FAW-Tron before it changed into more spanish. "Para me siento en mi lockeroom en el FAW, el mal excesivo de la regla de la voluntad i del wounder de i, o la regla malvada de la voluntad sobre mí. Soy el santo español, y nadie podrá pararme. Haré lo que hago para proteger el inocente, así que cuando usted camina por mí. Recuerde una voluntad de la cosa...."I mirar cada uno, por todas partes yo van!" Again it was translated.... "So as i sit in my lockeroom in the FAW, i wounder will i rule over evil, or will evil rule over me. I am the spanish saint, and no one will be able to stop me. I will do what i do to protect the innocent, so when you walk by me. Remember one thing....'I will watching everyone, everywhere i go!' The screen faded to show a Spanish hero, and his name came up on the screen before it faded.... Su Estupendo Héroe Etan Cross vs Apocalypse The next match pitted a newcomer to FAW, “Anti-Hero” Etan Cross against a 5 time Hardcore Champion, Apocalypse. The match started slowly with Apocalypse getting the upper-hand even attempting his finishing move, the Raise Above, but failing to hit it as Etan managed to come back into the match very swiftly with the changing point in the match as he hit a belly to back suplex. He then hit a series of suplexes, including a rolling German suplex and a dragon suplex. Referee Adam Yates had to stop the Anti-Hero from using a chair to knock out the 7’ monster and this gave Apocalypse the chance to get back into the match which he didn’t take effectively. After Apocalypse attempted and failed to hit his Raise Above again, Etan Cross tried to hit a powerbomb but couldn’t lift Apocalypse and instead hit a dropkick and locked in a sleeper hold to which Apocalypse started to fade. Etan then let go of the hold and the Anti-Hero then hit a T-Bone suplex followed by his finishing move, The Killer Cross making Apocalypse tap out and Etan won the match as the scene faded. Segment #3 Etan Cross stands backstage with the "Dead Angle" Blayze, as he looks around his new surroundings after his lenghty match against Apoc in which he defeated the Ex-5 Time Hardcore Champion. He untapes his wrists as Apoc coems up behind, he turns and they both look at each other and knod. "Good match...." says Etan before he was to turn, Apoc grabs him on the sholder and looks back at him. "It's newbie luck punk! Remember that" he says. He then looks over at Blayze and yes her up and down before licking his lip and turning back to Etan. "Keep hold of her you don't when punks here, will want a peice of her!" he laughed. Etan chuckle as Blayze just looked at them before and tapped her foot, as Apoc was to leave a lockeroom door swung open stubbing him in the foot. Etan looks around and laughs. "Gahhhhhhhhh! Fucking hell!" screams Apoc as he kneels down taking off his boot. Suddenly a big black pair of boots stop infront of him, as we hear a sound from Etan not to far away. "Do we meet again...." he says, before the camera shows standing there is Neo. Neo looks over at Etan and snarls, before pushing the Hardcore Championship over his sholder again. He looks down at Apoc again and smiles. "So poc, what you doing down there. Wanting me to kick your ass again!" he laughs. Apoc stands up again and looks staright into the eyes of Neo. "Pfft!" he says before walking off. The crowd in the arena can be heard cheering Neo's names. Neo can hear and he looks around, and then looks at Etan. "Wait over there for a minute Cross....". Etan just knodded and told Blayze to wait with him. Apoc walked off, but was quickly blasted in the back of the head with the Hardcore Championship. "Ha ha ha ha!" laughed Neo. The fWo member then looked down on the Ex-5 time Hardcore Champion, and laughed he quickly pushed him up against the wall and looked at him. "IF YOU WANT TO BE 6 TIME HARDCORE CHAMPION, THEN YOU HAVE TO GO THROUGH. NOW IF YOU DON'T MIND....". WHACK! Neo quickly kicks Apoc in the gut. "I HAVE SOME OLD PEOPLE I NEED TO MEET AND GREAT! SO IF YOU DON'T MIND...." WHACK! Again Neo smacks Apoc. "I'LL BE ON MY WAY WITH THIS BELT!". And so he was, Neo just smiled at Apoc and watched him slump to the floor before looking over at Etan and walking by. "Nice to see you back Etan, i'll see you more in the next few weeks. And Blayze, you still look as good as you used to!" he laughed and quickly walked by. Etan and Blayze look curously at Neo, but then turned away. Neo walked off whispering a few simple words under their breath. "You still look as you used to....yeah....washed up!" he chuckled before walking down to the fWo's lockeroom, situated on the right. Shawn Boyd vs Joe La Fontaine Non-Title BOOOM! "Vitamin" by Incubus blared out of the speakers of the arena tonight, the crowd didn't die down at all as cheers and people roared up from their seats. Off his first apperance in FAW one that was slated in a loss, Shawn Boyd quickly raced down to the ring. As he did "Coolness In The State Of Mind" by Nirvana and the Red Hot Chilli Peppers blared out of the speakers, with the European Title draped over his sholders Joe La Fontaine and his manager John Doe walked down to the ring. Looking calm and colective Joe rolled in the ring, but suddenly was blinded by the offence of Shawn Boyd throwing punches and kicks to Joe's chest area. Boyd thew a varity of power moves at Joe including a powerslam, then a whip to the ropes and then blinding Joe with a back elbow, and quickly a rake to the eyes blinding the European Champion. As Boyd picked up Joe again and John Doe his manager screaming for his to power he started to. Joe quickly punching Boyd in the stomach then knocking him down with a dropkick to the mat with exstensive force. Joe knowing how to take someone in Boyd's stature, threw down a leg drop and then decided to go with the springboard elbow drop which only got a two count. With Joe rallying on he quickly stepped up a gear and decided to lock on the sharpshooter. Boyd screamed in pain as he pulled himself towards the ropes, Joe quickly pulled him back into the middle of the ring. Knowing that he wasn't going to get his way. Fontaine quickly let go and jamped his foot into Boyd's back before picking him up off the mat. Joe let go with a few punches but Boyd came back and gave Joe a spin kick to the head. Boyd picked up the Euro Champ and went for the suplex, Joe perched above the mat quickly took his time and fell behind Boyd and decided he was going to end right now. Quickly grabbing Boyd's waists Joe took him over with a belly-to-back german suplex and then another one, before throwing Boyd to the mat with a reverse ddt. This got the crowd going as they started to boo louder than they already had. Joe quickly decided this was it ready to finish Boyd off with the "Chill-Out". He quickly picked up Boyd then smacked him in the gut which doubled the ex-XCWF member over. Flinging himself off the ropes Joe was about to collide with the Chill-Out, but suddenly without the ref noticing was smacked from behind with a chair by a crouched Neo of the fWo by the ring apron. Boyd capitalised with a little strenght and going for the "Low Boyd Bomb", and falling straight on top of the Euro Champ. The counted to three and Boyd's hand raised to victory. Suddenly John Doe pulled his comerade out of the ring, as suddenly Neo charged into the ring and took Boyd's head off with the Hardcore Championship as the scene faded. Who Was The Man On Distortion? "Highway To Hell" by ACDC hits and The British Bomber comes out onto the stage to a huge ovation from the fans. Bomber aknowledges this and walks down the ramp and into the ring, he poses for the fans for a few minutes then takes a mic. British Bomber: Last Sunday at Distortion we saw Courtney get taken to hospital, she is fine by the way but we also seen a gorgeous woman return, nah it ain't Kathryn it's the fit girl in row 4. The crowd laugh. British Bomber: But yes, we saw Kathryn looking to finish me off. But to her soon, I faced Spawn in one of the greatest matches of my career and the better man won, Spawn well done man! British Bomber: In that case lets talk about Kathryn and her secret....... The lights dim and Pink pyros explode around the arena.Me Myself and I hits and Kathryn comes out onto the stage, she poses momenterily for the fans, she recieves a mixed response, She continues down to the ring, where she gets in and is given a mic. KVT: Now Bomber, we've spoken about this, Don't say anything! He looks at her and shakes his head. British Bomber: Not you again, let me get on with it please. KVT: Yeah it's me, and I'm back. I told you I would be watching you and every where you go. I'll be there. Your not going to say a thing about this. It has nothing to do with you. So don't make a fool outta yourself and get involved. British Bomber: I am already involved, and stay out of my face, I dont want to see your......... He looks behind her British Bomber: ......Fat ass anymore. KVT: Wow, you actually come up with that yourself? Oh my god, Bomber learnt how to insult, shame you can't back up your words. He walks back and leans against the ropes. British Bomber: Well you know, what I want to know is who brought you back to this place. KVT: I came back on my own terms, I never really left, I was on maternity leave but after seeing what you've done I had to come back to finish what I started. British Bomber: Well sorry but me and you are finished, there is no way back Kathryn. KVT: Yeah I know, I was the one that broke of our little engagjent and found a REAL man. I came back to have you destroyed. British Bomber: Destroyed? KVT: Taken out, put on the shelf on a permanent basis. British Bomber: What the hell are you on about woman? KVT: Just wait and see British Bomber: Well who was that man the other night?........... "The Way I Am" by Eminem hits and Spawn comes out onto the stage with mic in hand. Spawn: Bomber we had a hell of a match on Distortion but we got unfinished business! Spawn walks down the ramp and into the ring, he confronts Bomber......."Freak On A Leash" by Korn hits and the same man as on Distortion appears at the top of the ramp. The crowd look to try and see who it is....he starts walking down the ramp and it is soon known to be TOM THE BOMB! Spawn rolls out the ring and walks up the ramp slowly as the fans boo Tom, he climbs into the ring and smiles. Kathryn smiles sweetly at Tom momenterily then turns to Bomber as the smile turns from Sweet to sadistic. Tom pauses before speaking and the fans begin to give a faint boo to him. He then looks up at Bomber and looks up at Kathryn. He smiles sweetly at Kathryn, who returns the smile and then he looks at Bomber and his face goes very evil. He begins to speak. Tom The Bomb: The British Bomber. The British Bomber. We go back quite a way, huh? Well, since I'm here now, I may aswell explain to you why I am here, seeing as we go back so far. Bomber, I am back for my company and if you got a problem with that then you can kiss my ass! Ever since I left Furiously Addictive Wrestling the ratings on TV went down. I am the rightful owner and always will be, no-one else, just me. And now I'm back I intend to take it back to the heights we were at just a few months previous to my departure. We were on top of the world, remember that Bomber? And why were we on top of the world again? Ah yes, me! Because I owned and ran FAW and not some wannabe owner, it was me wasn't it Bomber? KVT: He is right ya know Bomber. The British Bomber: You know Tom, your right, WE was on top of the world, remember Evil Intentions? Me and you? Me as World Champion and you as co-owner of FAW? But I came to my senses and realised how much of a jackass you really are. I did it for the gold, I didn't care about you and never will, I care about this company and I intend to win the Furiously Addictive Wrestling World Championship on my own this time and not with the help of some pumped up jackass like you. Just stop there though Bomber.....let me just show you something. Look u at the Furytron right now.... He looks up at the Furytron. Blood froths out of Bombers mouth as Spider stares down. Main Event The Omega Fro vs Chris Thunder As the event is drawing to a close it is time for the best part of the evening for the fans and everyone in the arena. It is the Main Event, the FAW ring announcer announces tonight’s main event will be The Omega Fro vs Chris Thunder. A in all his glory Chris Thunder comes down to a mixed amount of boos along with the odd cheer from the crowd. Then the crowd favourite of the match FAWs The Omega Fro makes his way to ringside. The Omega Fro starts the match off trying to keep it down to a slow pace of action but Thunder speeds up the match hitting a few blows to The Omega Fro in a row before going for a DDT. Fro does not allow it hitting a suplex on Thunder. Chris Thunder and The Omega Fro go throw a fast pace section of the bout where both members end up being knocked to the mat for the ref to count them down as he gets as high as 8. Both men get up as they brawl for a good 6 minutes. It ends up with Fro going for his finisher but bring turned into a hurricarna for the two count. This match by this time was well over with the fans standing out of their seat due to the spots being pulled off in this match. As Fro hits his finisher for the first time but is stopped for the pin as Thunder’s tag team partner distracts the ref. Fro attack him and is rolled up by Thunder but the pin does not go past the two count. Fro quickly gets back on top of the match only to get tripped up off the ropes by Prodigy who the ref told to get away from ringside. Well this was all happening Thunder pushed Fro into the ref knocking him over the top rope and out of the ring. The tag team soon took this to their advantage as they gang attacked Fro, Thunder pinned him but as the ref gets up he counts to 2 and Fro kicks out. Thunder has a go at the ref giving Fro time to hit Armageddon for the win 1…2…3
