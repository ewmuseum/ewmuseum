Crimson Lord gets up with the chair and cries out ‘YOU FEEL MY PAIN’. He strikes Spawn over the head with the chair and knocks him down. Spawn gets up and hits him with a fire extinguisher. Spawn collapses as they both lie in the centre of the ring. Ryan Stroke: They both had evil intentions and look how they ended up. Michael McGiggins: This is Michael McGiggins over and out from Friday Night Panic. We go off air with the camera fading out of the scene in the ring with weapons all over and Spawn and Crimson Lord lying on either side. --Transmission Ends--
