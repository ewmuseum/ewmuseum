Rzarector - 8 Panic Preview / 15th December Edition It has been confirmed that on Monday December 15th 2003, the former World Heavyweight Champion, Tony Vigilante, gunned down by an unknown villain at last months Deceit pay-per-view will return! But in what state of mind and condition will Tony Vigilante be in upon his return? Allegations, statements, and rumors have been flying around since the eventful November night when Tony Vigilante was gunned down, but who in his humble opinion, shot him down-- will we find out? Lisa Tien branded Tony Vigilante the greatest man to ever grace a Furiously Addictive Wrestling ring, but after all her mind games and her somber attitude towards the shooting, how will she react to Tony Vigilante's return? Will the Furiously Addictive Wrestling President continue advocating the former World Champion or will she return back to her wicked ways and be-grudge him? Full Card Listing / Information MAIN EVENT Stipulation to be Decided Rzarector vs. Don Corneoli On Tuesday evening, the FAW officials met up for their weekly eventful Panic match making session. This week officials had received a marked envelope from Mafia Incorporated, in which Don Corneoli offered Rzarector a match. After signing it, Lisa Tien didn’t realize the small print Mafia Incorporated had wrote, allowing them to pick a match of their desire. We will have to wait until the 15th December to witness the chaos Mafia Incorporated want the fans and superstars alike to witness. FATAL FOUR WAY Apocalypse vs. 9th Wonder of Foam vs. Chris Thunder vs. Hardcore Hoefel What happens when you place four fantastic superstars together in the ring-- it's fatal, that's what! Apocalypse, the returning 9th Wonder of Foam, Chris Thunder and the renegade Hardcore Hoefel will stand foe-to-foe in the middle of the ring. Apocalypse had been on a losing streak recently, with his partners obsessed with letting him down, but how will he fair in this explosive contest? Chris Thunder, fresh from his feud with Don Corneoli is pitted with some of the best talent, but will his experience in the ring prove invaluable when the 15th Dec episode of Panic is over? And don't forget Hoefel and the 9th Wonder of Foam, both great in their own way but will they have the ability to overcome the other three? FLIP REVERSE Champion vs. Challenger Violator vs. "Lee-gend" Lee Smith Flip reverse is a contest set up by officials for one night only. The World Heavyweight Champion will take on the International Title Number One Contender and the International Champion will take on the World Heavyweight Title number one contender. In this first flip reverse match, the "Lee-gend" Lee Smith has the chance to set his career alight when he takes on the current World Heavyweight Champion, Violator, in a non-title contest. Can Lee Smith rise to the challenge or will Violator add Lee Smith to his already long list of defeated opponents? Only one can wonder! FLIP REVERSE Champion vs. Challenger Sniper vs. Orlando Rivera This isn't the first time these two have met. Orlando Rivera overcame Sniper to win a title shot at Deceit last month, but Sniper has the chance to gain some revenge. In the second flip reverse match of the evening, will Sniper show Orlando Rivera his pedigree and exactly why he is the International Champion, or will Orlando Rivera prove too much for the young, currently uninspiring Sniper? WILDCARD SERIES Triple Threat Tag Team Hank Sharpe & The Meditator vs. Karnage & Shane Tiler vs. Havoc & Lone$tar Two more tag teams are revealed for the When Worlds Collide alliance rumble. Karnage will team with Shane Tiler and Havoc will team with Lone$tar, but will those two new tandoms be able to overcome Hank Sharpe and The Meditator, who got off to a good start as in preparation for WWC with a victory a couple of weeks ago on Panic against Kash Pachino and Sharky De Niro?
