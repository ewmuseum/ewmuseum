Rzarector - 8 CHAMPIONS Furiously Addictive Wrestling has put up for grabs many titles down the years but in 2003 it was decided the World Heavyweight, International and Tag Team titles were the ones worthy of staying in their rightful place. From Continental, European, Hardcore, Extreme, and Women's Titles, we now have three Championship belts with each belt having a healthy supply of competition.
