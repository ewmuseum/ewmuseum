FAW FAW is WAR, 22 January 2002

22 January 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Nationwide Arena, Columbus, Ohio - 20,000 - 22/01/2002 --Transmission Starts-- Press Conference Held At 5pm We are crowded into the room…suddenly a hospital spokesperson walks out ready to issue a statement concerning the welfare of a FAW ‘citizen’, in other words, a wrestler. It is thought the situation is not critical, but the patient is not particularly stable. “Earlier Today at approximately 7am in the morning FAW Wrestler Jim Phoenix, also known as JLC….” [Gasp from on looking media reporters] “Jim’s condition worsened over night, and he was brought to hospital by close friend Spider as they were discussing tactics for wrestling matches at around 4am, and just as a precaution we did some more tests. It is with great delight we can report no virus was found, and no broken bones but we have found something quite suspicious….It seems Mr Phoenix was the victim of food poisoning. He collapsed with Spider and unfortunately his hand joint was fractured. We can also pinpoint the time of the food poisoning, on Friday, that is all I will say. This is being treated as a suspicious case. That is all.” The spokesperson exits with the media frantically asking her questions of which she replies no comment to every single one. FAW IS WAR Intro "Control" by Puddle of Mudd hits and the transmission starts and the lights are out as the music kicks in and the pyros explode still in the dark, camera flashes start to happen as the lights switch on to a capacity crowd of 20,00 people. The cameras then zoom in on the announce table. Jackie Williams: Welcome to FAW IS WAR! I am Chimera vs Alex Storm Melissa Farrow: And our first match, scheduled for one fall. Coming to the ring first weighing 300 pounds ALEK STORM. "This is not" - by static X hits and Alek Storm walks down to the ring. Melissa Farrow: And his opponent weighing 320 pounds CHIMERA!! "Disposable Teens" - By Marilyn Manson hits and Chimera walks down to the ring. He climbs into the ropes and stands up to Alek Storm, he swings a punch at him and Alek Storm throws one back, they continue to exchange punches until Chimera stumbles backwards. Storm starts to give Chimera kicks to the gut, he then sends Chimera for a ride. Chimera runs back and shoulder tackles Alek Storm to the ground. Jackie Williams: Wow!! These 2 both have so much power, this will be great! Chimera picks up Alek Storm and puts him in a side headlock, he squeezes the head of Storm and Storm smacks the side of Chimera as he flails his arms wildly in pain. In pushes Chimera off him and to the ropes, Chimera tries for a clothesline but Storm ducks it spins around and hits Chimera with a clothesline. He picks up Chimera and rests him against the ropes, and backs up a bit and tries to hit him with boot but Chimera rolls out of the way and Alek's foot gets stuck in the ropes. Chimera gets up and then hits Storm with a jumping clothesline and they fall out of the ring. The ref starts his count. James Harris: They should get back in the ring Jackie Williams: Wrestlers are so unpredictabe, they might hurt themselves! The ref is at 5 as Storm and Chimera get up, Storm kicks Chimera in the gut and throws him over the barricade, he climbs over and the 2 fight all the way up the fans. Tom The Bomb: WOAH WOAH WOAH! Lee, why don't you bring yourself out here because when you hit me last Friday and knocked me to the floor, Lee, it really made me feel good, being knocked down by you, I actually enjoyed it because it meant I got the chance to absolutely whoop your ass! And when I did that felt even better and this Sunday night when we are referees along with that crappy commissioner of yours, Jim Pheonix! Jackie Williams: Will he accept? James Harris: Does he have the balls? Thunder and Lee run to the ring and slide under the bottom rope and the fans go wild as they exchange blows and Tom and Bomber get the upperhand and then Tom leaves the ring leaving Bomber to get some cheap shots in and Tom gets 2 chairs. He hands one to Bomber and holds onto one. The fans boo as they repeatedly hit the FAW co-owner and FAW World Champion with the chairs and then they drop them and smile and then leave looking back. Smoking Guns: Now I know you weren’t expecting me to say anything at the beginning of the match but then again I don’t care. He waves away at the ring and walks up the ramp. The crowd start booing. Morte gets out of the ring and goes in hot pursuit, he runs up behind him and hits him with a clothesline, he brings him back into the ring and starts stomping away at him once again. Smoking Guns gets up and nails Morte in the jaw with a tight fisted punch. He has a stressed look on his face and he starts laying into his face with right hands. He busts Morte’s lip open as blood goes everywhere. James Harris: Oh my god he’s snapped. Jackie Williams: I guess he has had enough of the ass kicking but if he doesn’t stop soon he will be disqualified. Morte tries reaching for the rope but he is pulled back in the ring. Smoking Guns runs at Morte but gets whipped over the top rope. The crowd cheer, Smoking Guns gets straight back up and slides in the ring, he stands up and both men exchange blows to the face, Morte whips Smoking Guns against the ropes and goes for a clothesline, Smoking Guns ducks under his arms and bounces off the ropes hitting Morte with a shoulder block to the back. Jackie Williams: Oh my god that could break someone’s back! James Harris: Yeah it could but it didn’t did it you stupid woman. What a match this is turning out to be. Smoking Guns is actually managing to get control in this match. Jackie Williams: And you thought he was a pussy! James Harris: God damn I want a new partner, I can’t be doing with blondes. Morte pushes the ref away from him then picks up Smoking Guns and throws him against the ropes, as Smoking Guns runs back at Morte, Morte locks in a sleeper hold, Smoking Guns struggles to break free and is successful when he hits Morte in the stomach with elbow shots. James Harris: Morte trying to knock out the new comer here. Jackie Williams: Lucky for him he got free. James Harris: Yeah. He runs against the ropes and hits a big boot to the face of Morte, Smoking Guns picks Morte back up and kicks him in the mid-section then goes for a Gorilla Press Slam, Morte escapes and then pulls Smoking Guns to face him then gives him a Gorilla Press Slam, the crowd cheer as Morte goes to the second rope waiting for to rise. Smoking Guns slowly gets to his feet only to get a second rope spear from Morte. James Harris: Morte with a high-risk move! Jackie Williams: It’s hardly high-risk though is it? I mean come on it was only off of the second rope. James Harris: Will you shut up. Jackie Williams: Well excuse me for doing my job! He follows up by grabbing Smoking Guns and slamming him down to the mat before he knows what hit him. Morte backs up and goes for a big splash onto Smoking Guns, but he rolls out of the way! Smoking Guns gets up first. When Morte gets to his feet, Smoking Guns whips him into the ropes, Morte bounces back, Smoking Guns drops to his stomach, Morte hops over him and bounces off the opposite ropes, Smoking Guns gets up and catches him in a power slam. James Harris: Great counter there! Jackie Williams: As we said earlier on this match is turning out brilliantly, not bad for a debut match. James Harris: And not bad for a come back seeing as Smoking Guns was going to walk to the back. Jackie Williams: Yeah. I guess he really was worth the money in hiring him. He quickly gets Morte in an Arm bar, then goes for figure four, but Morte boots him into the corner. Morte gets up and charges at Smoking Guns, dropping him with a splash into the corner. He picks Smoking Guns up and attempts a power bomb, when he gets Smoking Guns up, Smoking Guns counters and grabs Morte’s head and turns it into a face buster! He picks Morte back up and goes towards to turnbuckles for a high noon but Morte recovers and hits a Rigor Mortis! He then goes for the pin, …1 …2 …3!! Melissa Farrow: The winner of this match, Morte! Cut To Backstage We are backstage with Tony Vigilante who has just arrived in his locker room after attending and wrestling at last nights xCw Smash. He puts down his gym bag and walks over to the locker. As he turns the key and opens it Tanya enters. He closes the locker immediately. He walks over to her and they share a passionate kiss.
