PANIC RESULTS In an effort to evade the media, Lisa Tien held the recent Panic show behind closed doors and we have the results here exclusively for faw-wrestling.com. As the results were exclusive, we only have a few matches written to their entirety whilst others take the form of a summary. Violator vs. Apocalypse Violator defeated Apocalypse by count-out and it was a close call as Apocalypse apparently got into the ring in time but the referee Zeeshan Alam controversially gave Violator the victory. Violator had gone close to defeat Apocalypse twice in the match with near pinfalls but his stroke of bad luck ended when Apocalypse was counted out. Horazon vs. Tristian Grayber Horazon defeated Tristian Grayber by a mere whisker. Horazon managed to roll up Tristian Grayber with a small package and the Holier THan Thou superstar was miserable upon defeat on his debut. Tristian Grayber showed some good signs of being a complete wrestler and we expect him to show his worth soon. Meditator has got Smith down on the ground now, hoping to put an end to this match quickly. The Legend is trying to get out but his attempts only cause the Meditator to apply more pressure. Smith begins to relax a little bit and suddenly springs upward sending his head into the Meditator’s jaw! The big man jolts up and then falls to the ground holding his mouth in pain.
