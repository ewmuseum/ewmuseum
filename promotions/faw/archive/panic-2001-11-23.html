FAW Panic, 23 November 2001

23 November 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Arco Arena, Sacramento, California - 17,317 - 23/11/2001 --Transmission Starts-- Panic Intro "Never Gonna Stop Me" by Rob Zombie blasts out as the pyros in the Arco Arena hit out and the fans go wild as the transmission begins. The arena is at a capacity 17,317 people for a huge Panic tonight. Michael McGiggins: Welcome to Panic, tonight we have a shortened show for you, well in matches, but not in entertainment or length for that matter. Ryan Stroke: But with the pay-per-view just 48 hours away it was decided that the whole roster would not be booked tonight and that the top eventers would not have matches, Crimson Lord, SpaWn, JLC, THE GREAT ONE! The crowd go wild. Michael McGiggins: The Great One is back in FAW! What a night it was last Tuesday night. The Great One trashed Lisa's party and kicked some serious ass. Ryan Stroke: He most certainly did, and did it in excellent fashion too. Michael McGiggins: That man is a hell of a guy, he got fired, still returned for payback and we still await to find out if he is re-instated back into Furiously Addictive Wrestling or not? Ryan Stroke: Surely he will be at the PPV? Michael McGiggins: It would appear so, no other match has been booked anyway. Ryan Stroke: Well what a night we'll see this Sunday at Deceit. All of your favourite FAW superstars will be there to take on the rest of the superstars! Michael McGiggins: And that is just 48 hours away. Prez Tom The Bomb Promo "Fat Lip" by sum 41 hits out over the tanoid system. Out to the ring walks President Tom The Bomb to a bundle of cheers, the fans go wild and Tom grabs a mic off the ring announcer and puts it to his mouth. Tom The Bomb: You know, it's funny really, no matter what The Omens try they cannot beat me, they cannot beat JLC, they cannot beat The Great One! Look at this, for a start, Lisa tries to put me out of this federation and into retirement, but I press on and announce a match for the company at "Evil Intentions" on 23rd December this year, the year will be rounded off huge! But she tries to put me out of business, and here I am standing before you today! Then, there is JLC, Lisa and her Omens think that they will be able to take this man to pieces, limb by limb, but guess what? It ain't gonna happen. This guy has a World Title shot in just 2 days time, and he is in the arena tonight. JLC is the greatest guy to ever step into a FAW ring and he will always remain that way. He is up there with the likes of PyRo, Deadman, HaHaKlown, and of course, I have to hand it to him, SpaWn! But you still cannot drive this guy out Lisa! And finally there is the greatest spectacle in sports entertainment, THE GREAT ONE! This guy is something else. He walked into the arena just one week ago for Panic, exactly one week ago. He walked into the arena and expected to beat The Omens. But he lost, he got fired from FAW. Lisa was the happiest woman alive, but then during her party on Tuesday night who turned up? Yes, THE GREAT ONE! The fans go wild for The Great One. Tom The Bomb: He walked in and kicked some ass, he walked into the arena and kicked Crimson's ass, hell, he beat everyone's ass! But the fact is Lisa, you cannot remove any of these men from this federation. No matter who you are, no matter where you're from, you will not be able to take on me, JLC, The Great One! We are a new force to reckon with Lisa, we are not The Omens Of Death but we can sure as hell beat them. And this Sunday at Deceit we will show that. Let's start with the main event. JLC versus SpaWn! 1 hour iron man match. World Title on the line. JLC will come out the victor and put another spanner in the works of the Omens' and your plans Lisa. A force not to mess with we truly are. Then there is The Great One, he will fight, undoubtedly fight, Crimson Lord for the Hardcore Title. Let me see, all the odds are surely stacked against The Great One, only one man has scored a win against Crimson Lord, and that was only on the 24/7 rule, can The Great One break that record and beat the man? YES! The crowd goes wild. Tom The Bomb: And what about me? I fight on Sunday night, I fight Lisa "Da Bitch" and a partner of her choosing, the man that is still to be announced, the man who dare not reveal themselves out of fear. So, who could it be? Any of The Omens, who though? SpaWn? Crimson Lord? Prayer? But no, I know exactly who the man is Lisa "Da Bitch" has chosen. PYRO! PyRo will be the man who will fight in the huge handicap match. PyRo and Lisa will probably be the 2 people to try and take on yours truly in a handicap match! PyRo then walks down to the ring and the fans look shocked as he walks to the ring. He gets into the ring and grabs another microphone. PyRo: Tom The Bomb. Long time no see? What, no call? No messages? Well, guess what, who gives a damn? Not me, that's for sure. You think that I will be the man to team with Lisa this Sunday night. Well, guess what, I will not be the man to partner Lisa this Sunday and take out you, take you to pieces, but if I was that man, you would know it and you would be the man who was down, you would be the man who would run and hide, not me, and you know why? Tom The Bomb: No PyRo, you tell me why.... PyRo: Because I am the TOP DAWG and you would BURN IN HELL! With that PyRo drops his mic and locks in the 3RD DEGREE BURN on President Tom The Bomb. He then leaves Tom lying in the middle of the ring knocked out and leaves to the boos of the crowd. Ryan Stroke: WOW! PyRo returns and makes an impact. PyRo just shocked us all with that 3rd Degree Burn! What a submission move though. Michael McGiggins: But we still don't know who the hell will partner Lisa this Sunday. Ryan Stroke: Well it won't be PyRo! Michael McGiggins: Apparently not. But Tom looks hurt, will he make it to Deceit? In the ring the paramedics roll Tom, who still isn't moving, onto a bed and he lies on it and the paramedics wheel him off up the ramp and the cameras follow them into the parking lot where the put him in an ambulance, the sirens start to go and they take Tom away. Morte vs Kathryn Velmont-Thomas Ryan Stroke: Our opening match is here now. Michael McGiggins: KVT vs Morte. Ryan Stroke: Should be good. Morte will battle Shady o Grady at Deceit, who is of course the person KVT faced earlier this week. Michael McGiggins: Yes, it was a double count out wasn’t it? Ryan Stroke: I think so. Mortes music plays and he walks out with the crowd all rooting for him. The banners are held high in the arena. The crowd chant ‘we want DECEIT!’. Moulin Rouge plays as the hit soundtrack to the hit movie plays. KVT walks out in her usual clothing with an engagement ring on her finger. Ryan Stroke: Here we go, let the battle begin, let Panic begin. Michael McGiggins: If we must [sigh]. Ryan Stroke: Ha! That’s so not funny. Michael McGiggins: Then why did you laugh? Ryan Stroke: I am not completely sure. Michael McGiggins: ….Freak… Morte starts us off with a baseball slide to the outside. He drags KVT out and punches her in the lungs. He then gets into the ring. 1…. 2……. 3…….. 4….. 5……. 6…… 7…… 8…….. KVT gets back into the ring and nails a piledriver. Morte fights back and goes for a powerbomb. He knocks her out cold and pins. Pin: 1…2……..3 Michael McGiggins: Morte wins. Cut To Backstage The cameras cut to backstage where Morte is walking through the corridor of the backstage area and looking pleased after his win. Then suddenly from behind runs Shady-O-Grady, his opponent for Deceit. He turns Morte round and hits LAMBS TO THE SLAUGHTER onto a pile of 5 or 6 chairs! Shady then shouts something about beating him at Deceit, smiles and walks off leaving Morte being attended to by paramedics. Then suddenly Professor Found and Apocalypticus run in and they beat down on Shady-O-Grady and the officials hold back the 2 monsters. They then walk over to Morte to check on him. Cut To Parking Lot The cameras cut to the parking lot where the cameras see PyRo get into a 4x4 truck and drive off. The scene then fades to a commercial. Commercial Break Cut To Backstage Crimson is at a water cooler, when JLC approaches him. JLC: What is it? two days 'til your destroyed? Crimson doesn't look at him, then he slowly turns his head and smiles. Crismon: Ha, pretty funny. But not as funny as the thought of you laying in the middle of ring bleeding after Spawn shows you a new meaning of pain for a whole hour. JLC: Nah do you know what is funnier? Crimson: What? JLC: The fact that you're crap in the sack... I mean that must mean you're pretty bad. After all Lisa's had three quarters of the roster. JLC walks off and Crimson stares at him with hatred, then when he leaves he punches the water cooler and it breaks. Crimson walks off the other way. Chris Thunder vs Axl Young Ryan Stroke: The FAW IN Champion battles the man who by FIW may be the XCW US Champion. Michael McGiggins: That’s only if he beats JT though. Ryan Stroke: That isn’t an impossible task. Michael McGiggins: In my opinion, it is! Ryan Stroke: Keep your opinions to yourself. ‘Smells Like a Teen Spirit’ by Nirvana plays. The FAW IN Champion takes a slow stroll down to the ring. He pauses at the end of the ramp and then continues walking. He gets into the ring and throws his title over the turnbuckle. He climbs the turnbuckle and kisses his belt with the boo’s raising the roof off the arena. Then suddenly Axl Young runs down and attacks Thunder. Ryan Stroke: We come we go. Axl Young nails a spinebuster on the IN Titleholder. He hits him from pillar to post. Michael McGiggins: What will happen now….? Chris Thunder hits the referee and knocks him down. Then Spiders theme music plays. Spider runs down to the ring and gatecrashes the match, both physically and literally. He hits Thunder with a chair. He stamps on Thunders motionless body as Axl hits an axe handle smash off the top rope. Chris Thunder and Spider continue fighting. Spider hits Thunder with another chair. Spider chokes Thunder on the FAW IN Title. Thunder shouts out and cries for a realise from Spider. Spider laughs and delivers venom to Axl Young. Kathy comes rushing out crying. She stands at the apron and peeps through the ropes as Spider slaughters Thunder. Ryan Stroke: What is she going to do? Kathy grabs an iron pipe much to the delight of the spectators. She gets into the ring and lines it up on Spider. Spider ducks and she hits Thunder over the face! Spider hugs her as she tries to escape. She escapes down the ramp and looks back as the referee comes to consciousness and Axl Young reaches over his hand over CTs. Pin: 1…..2….3… Michael McGiggins: Axl Young wins. Ryan Stroke: That was a bit unfair. Cut To Omens Locker Room The cameras cut to The Omens locker room. The cameras zoom in on Lisa who is sitting there and then back off and span round and then zoom back in on Lisa and then back off once more and get into the picture Lisa, Crimson Lord and SpaWn. Lisa: Did you see what happened earlier? That was special! Tom won't even be at Deceit, I don't even have to fight. Crimson Lord: I know, but did you see what JLC just said to me a while ago? Lisa: Yeah, but do not worry about that, you will be kicking ass and SpaWn will be kicking ass. SpaWn: Just like PyRo did earlier tonight ya mean, that rocked, that rocked big time. He showed Tom not to mess with him, and ya know, we should start showing authority we do not respect them, we don't follow orders, we are above them, not sitting back and taking the s[beep]t. We are above them and we need to start showing it and acting like it. Crimson Lord: Well said there SpaWn, but I don't know whether we necessarily want to start acting like PyRo, he's not exactly.... SpaWn: What, the best? Yes he is...what the god? Yes he is....have you ever met that guy in the ring? He is one tough SOB and will show most people just why he is a FAW hall of famer. He is that damn good and will beat most asses from here to the UK and back again and he would have proved it if he had of been here for the UK tour! Crimson Lord: Okay man, calm down. The scene fades out. Justin Sane vs Stunning Stan Stunning Stan walks to the ring and gets into the ring and the crowd boo the xCw invader. Ryan Stroke: Another one of them jackasses. Michael McGiggins: The debutant in Justin Sane tonight. Will he be able to take on the xCw World Champion? Ryan Stroke: This guy is a great wrestler. Justin Sane then walks to the ring with "Eyeless" by Slipknot blaring out. He is met halfway up the ramp by Stunning Stan who instantly swings a chair at him but Justin blocks the chair shot and throws Stunning Stan to the ground off the ramp. He then walks down the ramp and round the corner. He grabs the chair off Stunning Stan and hits it over Stan's head. He then walks to the ring with Stan bleeding from the head and throws Stan into the ring and covers him. Pin: 1...2...KICK OUT! Ryan Stroke: I thought Justin had him beat already! Michael McGiggins: I think everyone in the arena did too. Ryan Stroke: Well it looked like it. Michael McGiggins: Justin looks irate that he didn't take the win right then. Ryan Stroke: Well I think he should have done too. Justin then picks up Stan and kicks him in the gut and then delivers the INSANITY PLEA! He then covers Stan. Pin: 1...2....3! Ryan Stroke: Quick debut match against the champion! Michael McGiggins: But it shows that Justin Sane has great potential. Commercial Break Cut To Backstage Backstage Spider is walking through the corridors. Suddenly he bumps into Kathy. He stares into her eyes and she stares into his. Then Chris Thunder comes walking around the corner. He looks at the two and storms off thinking Kathy has defected back to Spider. Kathy: It wasn’t like that! CHRIS, COME BACK! CHRIS! Spider smiles and walks off as Kathy chases after Chris. British Bomber vs "The Untouchable" Jon Thomas "Faith" by Limp Bizkit hits and down to the ring walks the xCw owner, British Bomber. He gets into the ring to the boos of the crowd. Michael McGiggins: The owner of xCw! Ryan Stroke: The man who will fight The Omega Fro in 2 days, British Bomber, the jackass! Michael McGiggins: All of them idiots from xCw are though. Ryan Stroke: You got me there, I gotta agree! "Put Your Hands Up" by Jadakiss hits and down to the ring walks the xCw United States Champion, Jon Thomas! Ryan Stroke: Bomber booked the match on FAW IS WAR, Jon Thomas took on sYn and won the United States championship! Michael McGiggins: And defends it against FAW member, Axl Young this Sunday at Deceit. Ryan Stroke: And that will be a heated match that both new guys want to win, they both want that gold that Jon has and now they have a chance at it. Michael McGiggins: It will be a great match. The match begins quickly as British Bomber starts to slaughter Jon Thomas and pummel him with his punches. Jon Thomas goes down and Bomber goes for the BRITISH LOCK but Jon Thomas wrestles out of it and grabs Bomber and delivers a body slam followed by an elbow drop while he is down. Ryan Stroke: The US champion seems to have the measure of the owner! Michael McGiggins: Ain't it great? Ryan Stroke: Hell yes! British Bomber then gets up quickly and takes down Thomas and goes for the BRITISH LOCK in the middle of the ring and locks it in this time. Jon Thomas raises his hand to tap just as he is about to tap The Omega Fro pulls the referee out of the ring and gets into the ring unseen and kicks British Bomber off Jon Thomas and hits Bomber with a chair repeatedly over the ribs. The Omega Fro jumps out of the ring and roll the referee back in as Jon Thomas gets an arm over Bomber. Pin: 1..2.....3! Commercial Break Skipster vs Prayer Michael McGiggins: An eventful night tonight. And just 2 days away from the PPV and tonight is almost over. Ryan Stroke: Well I can't wait till the PPV on Sunday. There are just 2 days to go till the huge night and that will be one hell of a night for the fans and wrestlers alike. Michael McGiggins: Well there will be alot going on on Sunday night, with the first match back for The Omega Fro. Ryan Stroke: And no doubt PyRo, the man who locked in the THIRD DEGREE BURN on our President Tom The Bomb at the top of the show, will be there too. And that was a damn wrong thing to do. Michael McGiggins: Yes it was, but we cannot do anything about it, and PyRo will be there I believe. Ryan Stroke: And first out to the ring is a member of The Omens Of Death, Prayer! Prayer walks down to the ring alone as a member of The Omens Of Death. He jumps into the ring and the crowd continually boo Prayer. Michael McGiggins: Certainly not a favourite of the crowd. Ryan Stroke: Well he is a bit of an ass. Michael McGiggins: Took the words right out of my mouth. Skipster then walks down to the ring and the crowd go wild. Skipster slides into the ring and raises his arms and the crowd cheer. Then from behind Prayer hits him and the bell rings. Skipster falls to the ground and Prayer picks him back up and goes for a russian leg sweep but Skipster reverses it into a face crusher. Prayer goes down as the fans cheer and Skipster hits an elbow drop. Ryan Stroke: That's what people came to see, The Omens get beaten! The Omens and xCw! If both them get beaten, everyone goes away happy. Michael McGiggins: Well said, and we will have to wait and see if that comes true this Sunday night. Ryan Stroke: I damn well hope so. If we did then we would see new xCw Intercontinental and World Champions. We'd see a new International Champion, and of course new World and Hardcore champions, The Great One, The Mortician, Spider and J...L...C! Michael McGiggins: That would be a great night! Ryan Stroke: The party would never end! Skipster then picks up Prayer and hits a reverse DDT on The Omens of Death member. Prayer goes back to the mat and Skipster climbs the top turnbuckle and flies through the air and crashes down on PRayer with a big body splash. Prayer then starts to get up using the ropes and the referee starts to hold Skipster back and Prayer hits a low blow on Skipster and the fans boo as Skipster falls to his knees and PRayer gets up and delivers a kick to the face of Skipster who goes down to his back as PRayer covers. Pin: 1...2....KICK OUT! Ryan Stroke: No chabce! Michael McGiggins: Not against this man, no way. Ryan Stroke: I think The Omens are thick aswell as ugly. Michael McGiggins: That was uncalled for Ryan. Ryan Stroke: But true! HA! Prayer then picks up Skipster looking mad and throws him out of the ring and to the floor. Skipster falls to the floor and Prayer follows him out of the ring and then starts to kick Skipster a few times on the back and then he whips Skipster into the ring steps and Skipster falls onto his back looking hurt. Prayer then walks over to him and grabs his arm and puts it on top of the announce table and then elbows it a few times and then grabs his arm and delivers an arm bar. He then roll Skipster onto the announce table, he follows him onto it and goes for a piledriver but Skipster throws him over his back and sends Prayer crashing THROUGH the announce table. Michael McGiggins: Well there goes our announce table. Ryan Stroke: Prayer got what he deserved. The fans go wild as Skipster rolls the lying Prayer into the ring and covers him. Pin: 1....2....3! Ryan Stroke: Skipster is back to his winning form! Michael McGiggins: Skipster leaves with the victory, but look at Prayer, he's lying in the middle of the ring unconcious! Ryan Stroke: It's a damn good thing he ain't booked for Deceit! Michael McGiggins: Damn right. Ryan Stroke: We will see you at Deceit in 48 hours folks. The scene fades with Prayer lying on his back in the middle of the ring not moving and the paramedics running past the victor, Skipster, down to the ring. --Transmission Ends--
