Move list

Wrestling Style: High Flying, leaning towards lucha libre Set Up Move: Burial Rites (Duke will use this as a finisher when he is able to lift the person (I.E. the opponent is roughly his weight 200 to 250 lbs.) He will get the opponent and hit a devastating vertibreaker, which will not be one the opponent will be getting up from. ) Finisher: Fall from Grace (Duke will scale the turnbuckle, keeping his back to his opponent who is lying face up on the mat. Stretching out his arms in the crucifixion pose first he will then leap off the turnbuckle executing a Sky Twister Press (Corkscrew Moonsault Splash)) Other Moves: Other Trademark Moves:Unforgiven- Developing his technique, Duke has found this move to be quite effective when he pulls it off. He manages to get it out almost whenever you think it’s highly unlikely. The base form of the move is a shoulder spin rana, but more often than not the move is tinkered with during the match and it is easy to find him performing it to the outside or from a springboard position. The Infinite Sadness (Melancholy)- This can be a two part move for special occasions or one for normal. During his continuing training Duke has always striven to be the best at high flying. Here he showcases a combination of such skills. If it is for a special occasion he will do both moves consecutively, normal he’ll pick one out of the two.#1 – A springboard somersault body attack.#2 – La Quebrada, the springboard moonsault.Beast with Two Backs - The victim and attacker are side by side on the mat on their backs. The attacker has their leg, which is closest to the victim wrapped around the victim's near leg. The attacker reaches their far leg across the their body and locks it behind the victim's neck, forcing the victim's head into their chest. This hold is often set up with a side Russian leg sweep. The attacker grabs the victim's near arm and applies pressure to it. (Grounded Octopus hold)

Career history

The 16th of May 1978. Not much of a date to some but to the Duke it is his date of birth. All those years ago his mother first cradled him in his arms.How times can change.From the beginning Duke was perceived to be just a run of the mill everyday child. But just like so many of today’s adult population, his life was altered by one defining moment. At the age of 10 he began to watch wrestling, intrigued by the big stars on WWF television. Hulk Hogan, Randy Savage, a young Shawn Michaels, all were his idols of the time but most of all he enjoyed Jimmy Superfly Snuka. While he was tiring out, Duke had an unquenchable thirst to see him leap off the top. From him and Shawn Michaels he found his passion: highflying wrestling. Deciding to train to become a wrestler Duke enrolled in a local school and began his tutelage. The journey was long and arduous but at the age of sixteen Duke had achieved almost everything he could in the school. It was then the trouble started.It had been an everyday thing for Duke, come in, spar and then have matches for the younger aspiring wrestler’s enjoyment. But not that day. He had been tired but couldn’t let down his friends before he left to go off to America to make his way in professional wrestling. He had to go. It all went fine until they gave him his match. With his best friend Killian. They joked and planned a major farewell for the crowd. Only for the crowd they would witness more than one farewell that day. To this day, the memory of that stays with Duke of the day he, through no fault of his own, killed his best friend in the middle of the ring. The crowd who had gathered looked in horror and shock as Duke tried to help but was pushed aside by paramedics. With that people turned against him. Judged as a mutilator his friends began to dry up. Duke felt hurt but took some back in the hope that when he got to America it wouldn’t follow. He was wrong.The scholarship he had earned was taken away for his incident and Duke was left in the cold. Nobody to turn to he tried his own family. They left him shocked when they shut the door in his face and refused his presence. Shattered and broken, Duke took his ticket and left Ireland. The next 5 years are one that is shrouded in mystery and has never been told by Duke publicly. Titbits are known about his wrestling career and he did have a stint in a luchador school in Mexico. Beyond that we know nothing more than his nickname which has stuck throughout the years. The Unforgiven OneIn the summer of 2001, shortly after his 23rd birthday he received news of a soon to be opened new federation backed by a multimillionaire. Eager to join Duke signed up and the rest as they say is history. Duke has enjoyed a long and liked tenure in wCW although there have been times when he questioned himself. From the mists of time however a person rose up and restored his faith in him. That was his one friend who stuck through with him. Shona. Now happily in love they have enjoyed many a time together although recently as a result of a despicable act by Belly, Shona’s father was killed. Since then Duke has drifted from place to place, rarely settling for long. Recently his split personality of sorts has driven Shona away from him and once again his path is a solitary one. Truly he has fallen to the bottom, now he must make his way back up.
