A Challenge To Profusion

9 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
A Challenge To Profusion Date: 9th February 2003 Show: Panic .::The camera switches backstage where Adam Hutton is standing next to 'Golden Nine' Jeff Harris, Adam Hutton is holding a microphone to his mouth as he begins to speak::. Adam Hutton: Jeff Harris, I believe you had something to say to... Profusion? Jeff Harris: Damn right woman! What I had to say is this, Profusion if you somehow get past The Great One on Wednesday and retain your Continental title then I am challenging you to a match at the PPV: Vandetta! I want a rematch and this time there will be no interference, no Atticus Pulsar, no Dan Logan or any other jobbers. I am challenging you to put your Continental title on the line in a ladder match, that is of course if you aren't scared like the little cocky bitch that you are. Then after I trash your ass you can piss off to your little OSW and win titles with no meaning against overrated untalented wrestlers. Adam Hutton: Can I just ask something? Jeff Harris: What? Adam Hutton: Well you didn't beat him last time, what makes you think you can do it this time? Jeff Harris: Boy stop trying to interview me! He won the match last time because of that bitch Atticus Pulsar, he however will not beat me if he decides to take my offer up on this match and that's all I have to say about that. .::Jeff pushes Adam Hutton out of the way and walks off::.
