Panic (May 2001)

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The cameras switch on to a great arena The pyros blow and the crowd erupt here and the cameras span round the crowd... Michael Cole: Welcome to The Pepsi Centre here in Denver, Colorado, we have a great show lined up for you tonight....we have a sell out crowd here and we have a sell out crowd for the massive Pay Per View coming up.... Bill Clinton: Hi, I am Bill Clinton and I am sitting here with Michael Cole....Thursday Night Panic is coming to you live as an Indy fed of the ICWF Interfed..... Michael Cole: Speaking of ICWF....what is going on with that Bill? Bill Clinton: Well.... [Full Nelson hits and Magic runs to the ring] Bill Clinton: I'll tell you when Austin is out.... [The glass breaks and Step Off hits and Stone Cold runs to the ring as the crowd go wild for this man!] *DING, DING, DING* Michael Cole: These 2 are off! Bill Clinton: Well, ICWF, we are currently an indy fed there and we are currently going after a Television contract with Canon Broadcasting as it has come available.....we will see at the weekend if FAW successfully snatches it up....and if we get lucky then we will be one of three TV feds in ICWF..... Michael Cole: that will be great, we will broadcast from better arenas every event and pull better crowds! Bill Clinton: Well, we have already pulled a great crowd for May Day Massacre, we will see a sold out Astrodome in Houston, Texas sold out on May 4th..... Michael Cole: Have you seen Austin and Magic go at it? It is great! Bill Clinton: Who is that at ringside...she just ran down, she looks to be supporting Magic? Michael Cole: And she is distracting the referee......MOCHERY has just been hit on Austin after a low blow.....the ref turns round The referee begins to count....1.....2......3!!!! AND HERE IS YOUR WINNER.....MAGIC!!!! Bill Clinton: So for the second time this week, MAgic wins..... [Magic grabs a mic] Magic: You wanna know who this is? This is my NEW manager and she is beautiful....this is CRYSTAL and she will bring out a NEW Magic!!!!! [Full Nelson plays and Magic and Crystal walk off together......] COMMERCIAL BREAK Bill Clinton: That was great and she is gorgeous! Michael Cole: Yes, she is and we now have another great match between one half of the tag team champions, X-Pac take on Spike.....these two superstars will meet this Sunday at May Day Massacre along with their tag partners for the tag team titles! [X-Factor hits and X-Pac walks to the ring] [Spike then runs to the ring behind him and hits him from behind] Michael Cole: They climb into the ring.... *DING, DING, DING* Bill Clitnon: Remember, both of the partners have been taken out of this match, they can't run to ringside, if Max does, they dont get a shot at the titles and if Justin does, the titles are vacated and the match at the PPV is called off...... Michael Cole: Well, I dont think ANYONE will come to ringside, these two superstars are great and it could be a great match! Bill Clinton: Yep, and if tonight proves to be as good as it looks, May Day Masscare will kick ass big style! Michael Cole: and look at Spike, he is kickin X-Pac's ass....I really do like him and Max, 2 young stars gettin' a tag title shot! It's great if ya ask me! Bill Clinton: Well, we will see if they can actually win those titles off a great team like X-Factor! Michael Cole: Well, I wish them lotsa luck! Bill Clinton: X-Pac has Spike in the X-Factor position.....he runs towards him.....Spike moves and hits the SPIKE DRIVER!!!!!! The ref counts....1.....2......3!!!!! AND HERE IS YOUR WINNER.......SPIKE!!!!! COMMERCIAL BREAK Bill Clinton: Well, he won Michael, can he do it at the PPV though? Michael Cole: I Hope so, he is great and was unlucky to lose the International title..... [Livin' It Up hits and Max-X makes his way to the ring and gets into the ring] [Dark Carnival hits and Deadman runs to the ring] *DING, DING, DING* Bill Clinton: So, now we have the NEW International title holder, ever since beating Spike on FAW IS WAR taking on a great superstar, as you say Michael, Max-X..... Michael Cole: Well, Max looks set to get his first singles victory if he beats the IN title holder here tonight....he will defend that title at May Day Masscare and Max gets that tag team titles shot!!! Bill Clinton: And we just saw Spike beat up X-Pac, so can Max do the same thing to Deadman? Only time will tell....we will see the answer soon tonight! Michael Cole: Well, once again, I hope he does, but Deadman ain't all that bad a competitor, well obviously, he is the IN title holder! Bill Clinton: And look at these two go....WAIT.....MAx just hit the MAX BOMB on his ass! The ref counts...1.....2......3.....!!!!!! AND HERE IS YOUR WINNER.....MAX-X!!!!! COMMERCIAL BREAK Bill Clinton: So, both members of The KrusherZ have won their matches, but can Justin Credible get X-Factor's respect back and win his match against Damo Muzzler?? Find out next here on PANIC! [You Only Get Waht You Give hits and the Hardcore champion, Damo Muzzler makes his way to the ring] [X-Factor plays and Justin Credible, 1 half of the tag team champions makes his way to the ring] *DING, DING, DING* Michael Cole: Champion versus Champion........Hardcore vs Tag Team......the winner? Dunno! Damo Muzzler looks to have an early advantage, but I wouldn't rule out Justin.....he's great! Bill Clinton: He is definitely great Michael, I rate him VERY highly here and I think that he will actually prevail here but we will see nayways! Michael Cole: What are your views on Damo's match at May Day Massacre? Bill Clitnon: Well, |I think that he will beat JLC for both titles coz JLC knows he leaves FAW after the match so he probably won't bother trying too hard! Michael Cole: You are probably right Bill! Bill Clinton: Of course I am, I am Clinton! HA! Michael Cole: WOAH! Damo just got knocked down and Justin picks him up........THAT'S INCREDIBLE to Damo.... The ref counts.....1......2.....3!!!!! AND HERE IS YOUR WINNER........JUSTIN CREDIBLE!!!! Bill Clinton: That was a great match! COMMERCIAL BREAK [Rollin' hits and Sadam Hussain walks to the ring] Sadam Hussain: I am terribly sorry but the match between Y2J Chris Jericho and Mortal MAniac has been called off as Y2J is injured and wants to make sure he is at full fitness for May Day Massacre........ Thanx for your understanding! [Rollin' hits and Sadam goes back again!] COMMERCIAL BREAK Bill Clinton: Well, that is a shame, that looked like it would be a great match too! Michael Cole: Well, Bill, that's life! We now have the main event! A great tag team matchup! [Dark Carnival hits and Bull and HaHaKlown make their way to the ring....] [My Way hits and JLC and Mojo walk out.....Mojo infront of JLC ignoring him!] *DING, DING, DING* Bill Clinton: The main event is off then Michael, but who will win? Michael Cole: Dark Carnival are the most likely to me! The World Title holder.....HaHaKlown will cane these guys! Bill Clinton: You are probably right Michael!!! Michael Cole: And remember Mojo vs HHK is the main event for the PPV this month, this Sunday night! Bill Clinton: I hope you will all join us for that great match and the great event! We have fans around the world who will, so don't let us down! Michael Cole: I agree Bill, please join us! Bill Clinton: Wait a minute, this isn't right, Callie Stratus is distracting the referee and Deadman has come to rinside.....NOOO! Michael Cole: Mojo is hetting triple teamed here, and Deadman has taken JLC out with a chair! Bill Clinton: Callie still has the referee! TOMBSTONE to Mojo followed by the WHOOPIE CUSHION!!!! The ref counts....1......2....3!!!! AND HERE ARE YOUR WINNERS.........DARK CARNIVAL!!!! Bill Clinton: Please join us for MAY DAY MASSACRE!!!
