..::FAW::.. ..::Fedwars::.. Phillips Arena, Atlanta, GA - 19,445 - 05/02/2002 Footage from last weeks Panic is being shown on screen at the start of tonight's huge showing of FAW IS WAR. We start with the scenes of Tony Vigilante being horribly attacked by British Bomber. We then see Lance Sterling booking himself against Spider for a World Title shot where if he loses he leaves FAW! We then see Spider getting the 1 2 3 on Lance and retaining his title forcing Lance out of FAW for life. Then we see Kathryn knocking down Bomber in the middle of the ring and Tom running to the ring and helping Kathryn, and Tony joining in and all 3 persons beating down on British Bomber. Then we see Chimera winning his first European Title in FAW and Jay winning a 4 way match, followed by the Dark Nation winning their 6 man tag match, and finally shots of The Omega Fro and SpAwN ruining the scaffolding match throwing Great One and Thunder off the top of the scaffolding and Tom The Bomb announcing that the man who hit the ground first lost the match, Chris Thunder, giving The Great One a FAW World Heavyweight Title shot tonight at FAW IS WAR. --Transmission Starts-- FAW IS WAR The lights are out and the pyros suddenly explode as the cameras switch on and "Control" by Puddle Of Mudd blasts out over the tanoid system at FAW and the fans go wild as the cameras zoom round the capacity crowd of 19,445 in the Phillips Arena as the cameras then stop on the announce table.
