As The Omega Fro and Minion are the legal men no pins can be made. A tag is now equivalent to a rare animal. The cameraman walks around the ring looking at the carnage. On his way he sees Spider and JLC flat on the floor with blood dripping down their foreheads. He sees Bonafide and Jimbo collapsing in a heap. He sees the most devastating scenario, as he walks past The Omega Fro and Minion. Bill Clinton: Still no sign of movement with those two. Pyro and American Psycho are the only men who are not lumberjacks conscious. Jay Wiked and Shawn Johnson lie plumped in individual turnbuckles. The crowd roar as they see the two men that will fight in a street fight this Sunday go at it. Pyro hits a sidewalk slam. Michael Cole: The effects of that move could be disastrous for Team Psycho,Johnson and Minion. American Psycho obviously hears the crowd chant ‘3rd Degree Burn’ over and over. He gets jealous of their love of the move. He strikes Pyro in the lower section and hits a fast DDT. He locks in the Psycho Submission in the centre of the ring. The crowd go crazy. Psycho smiles. He continues with the move. Nemesis gets off the ground and gets into the ring. He looks around at the crowd who don’t seem to have noticed him. He climbs the turnbuckle. He hits a moonsault and the lock is broken! American Psycho is incredibly mad as he chases Nemesis around the ring. Pyro follows suit, although going after both men. Nemesis takes a turn for the worst as he heads up the ramp. Pyro continues chase as a smirk grows wider on American Psychos face. He turns back to the ring. Pyro continues following Nemesis. Bill Clinton: American Psycho has turned his back on Nemesis. Michael Cole: I thought he hated that guy. Bill Clinton: Maybe Nemesis has some sort of plan and Psycho has seen through it. The camera turns to where Daniel and Mike should be sitting. Daniel has gone. The titan tron switches on. Nemesis has Daniel tied up. Pyro stares in shock as Daniel sobs. Pyro decides he does not stand a chance and backs away. The camera focuses on the cigarette lighter and bottle of petrol in Nemesis’s hands Bill Clinton: Oh My God! We go back to the ring. American Psycho sprints up the ramp. He wants to prove to Daniel that he is sorry for what The DT did to him by saving his life. American Psycho walks past corridors and bumps into people. He does not know where they are. He hears a car screech in the carpark and Pyro shouting. He runs to the parking lot. He cannot see anyone around. He sees Daniel being thrown into the centre of the carpark. Nemesis continues driving round and round. Pyro is frantically trying to save Daniel. Pyro runs off to call 911. This leaves American Psycho and Nemesis, and of course Daniel, on their own. Nemesis drives round and round circling Daniel. American Psycho jumps over to Daniel. He unties Daniel and the two embrace. Is this a happy ending? No. Daniel and Nemesis suprisingly throw American Psycho into the path of a car. Daniel doesn’t seem to object as we see Pyro at the wheel. American Psycho looks to his right obviously dazed. He stands up and then sees the car. He rolls over the bonnet as the car strikes Psycho. Pyro opens the door and gets out. He pulls his hair obviously very upset with Nemesis. Bill Clinton: A set-up? A SET-UP! Michael Cole: They knew Psycho would try and save Daniel. They knew it! If he had done, Psycho would have been hurt and would know how Daniel feels. American Psycho runs off back to ringside astounded that he has been tricked. He is shaking with sweat. Michael Cole: As you can see not much has happened. As American Psycho looks around from inside the ring he is ambushed from behind by Jay Wiked. Jay hits the WickedBomb on Psycho.
