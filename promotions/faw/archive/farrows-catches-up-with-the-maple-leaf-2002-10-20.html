Farrows catches up with The Maple Leaf!

20 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Farrows catches up with The Maple Leaf! Date: 10.20.02 Show: Distorion 19 Backstage we see Damien Ryan walking down a corridor kicking open doors searching around for someone. He walks into the men's restroom. We then hear some rustling going inside and finally the door swings opens and some innocent man gets pushed into the corridor. Damien smacks him a couple times then pins him against the wall. Ryan: Who the hell was it who hit me last week!? Man: Ah! Help! Ah! Ryan: Shut up and tell me! We then see Ryan look down and then let the man go. The camera than zooms in on the yellow stream flowing on the floor because the man pissed his pants. Ryan: Jesus man, you're not worth it. Ryan continues to walk down the corridor and then Melissa Farrows runs up to him. Melissa: Damien! Wait a second! What are you doing? Why are you on this rampage? Ryan: I told everybody already, and I made my promise that I was going to find whoever attacked me last week! Melissa: You plan on just cruising around in here and brutilizing innocent people? Ryan: Yes because in America it seems like nobody around here is honest. Atleast in Canada we can have a honest, truthful life. Now Melissa, unless your going to help me find this asshole then get out of my way! Melissa: But- Ryan: Shut up bitch! Melissa stands there totally shocked and the camera fades back to the arena.
