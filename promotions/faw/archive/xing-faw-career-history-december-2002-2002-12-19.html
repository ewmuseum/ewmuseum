History At a very young age probably around the age of 3-4 he was a very troublesome boy, but the canadien couple put the pen to the paper and took the young mischevious boy from his surroundings of the orphanage play school. Though giving as much as they could the couple could not stop the young Juan from changing his awkward ways and becoming a good boy. He was given dailly meetings with a counciler because of his short fuse of a temper. Later on in his life he met a woman around his age who after 6 years had spent the best days of her life with him but tradjically on her way off to work in a very successful company got rammed in the front of the bonet and smashed through the windscreen of the car and Juan witnessed this incident and was never the same again he is now living on the pure pump he gets of his wifes death. When his parents received the news he was devasted to hear that they were estatic at the news of her death he went balistic and.......... We know no more than that he has now changed his name and is totally resentful at his parents and labelled himself a no go area and branding his name as Xing. He then went to dailly nights underground fights he was drunk and fought to give it a try then a man about his height offered a challenge to Xing, so not knowing that he is the one they down at the underground call 'all that!' my a drunk acceptance to him and they fought. Now Xing was obviously getting his butt kicked he thought he should give up he waved his hand up the crowd booed and 'All that' picked him up and said and i quote 'Going to run home to your mummy and daddy or is it your ugly bird you just luckily happened to pick when you got auctioned in a charity place because she sorry for you.' So Xing responded with a smash in the mouth the crowd roared the fight wasback on Xing screamed out'Don't ever talk about my wife like that again you get me!' He smiled and looked like he had let off All that and walked off. While all that was getting treated Xing came back in and he totally mullered him all that was concused had 12 stitches and got stripped off his name of 'All that'. They labelled Xing 'A no go area' Xing was proud of his performance.Xing went home and considered a life of the underground wrestling which he did for an other six years and never lost afight in about 76 fights when one night thought to himself after being recommended to joing a proper fed and he thought and decided he would join a fed. A few years time after lots of bare hard training was good enough to be wrestler because of his drive and passion for his wife and during every fight thinks up oin the bloke and what he said and then releases a royal whooping on whoever is in front of him. Which brings us to his first week in the fed where he is going to face a tough competitor in the likes of Lee Smith who is a total and utter rich boy.(God i'm jealous!!!!!!!!!)
