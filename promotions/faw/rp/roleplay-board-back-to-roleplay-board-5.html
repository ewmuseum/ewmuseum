Cyrus Skar — FAW Roleplay, August 2002

August 2002

Furiously Addictive Wrestling Roleplay Board Roleplay Board | Back to Roleplay Board Cyrus Skar Entitled: JJ Dynasty and a 10 men battle royal. (RP for Distortion) Written: 2.8.2002 Location: Unknown *The scene opens to show Cyrus Skar walking down a busy street. Cyrus is taller than every person on the road, his head is visible above the crowd. The camera shows him stop outside a restaraunt. He hesitates but then goes in. A small waiter comes over and asks Cyrus where he would like to sit. Cyrus points to a table all on it's own. The waiter takes him over there and Cyrus sits down.* Waiter: Would you like a menu sir? Cyrus: Yes I would, could you hurry please, I am on a tight schedule. Waiter: Erm, yes ok. *Five minutes pass and Cyrus still has not been given a menu. Cyrus spots the waiter and calls him over.* Cyrus: Where is my menu? Waiter: Oh, I'm sorry, I forgot. Cyrus: Don't forget again. Waiter: Ohhh, I'm scared, the big scary man's threatening me. Don't threaten me again or I'll get you thrown out of here. Cyrus: You'll do as I say or I'll knock you flat on your arse sunshine. Waiter: Really? Try it! *The waiter puts his guard up, gingerly waving his fists around in front of his head. Cyrus calmly and slowly walks out of his seat and over to the waiter. Cyrus grabs the waiter by the throat.* Cyrus: I won't hurt you whilst I'm calm like this. You had better hope I don't snap, if I turn into my other persona you're going to be...be...ah. No! *Cyrus lets go of the waiter and starts clutching at his head, he writhes for a few seconds holding his head. Then he stops, the waiter backs up against a wall trembling. The huge 6ft8 figure of Cyrus Skar walks up to the waiter, that strange look in his eyes.* Cyrus: You're going to wish you never met me boy! *Cyrus grabs the waiter by his throat and throws him into the wall, the waiter lands back first. He falls to the floor clutching his back, Cyrus however, feels he has not caused enough pain, he picks the waiter up and lets him stand up by himself. He measures him then punches him in the jaw, knocking a couple of his teeth out. Cyrus quickly leaves before anybody comes over to see what has happened. He is stopped by the manager. He starts holding his head again and shaking. He then looks up, his eyes seem normal again.* Manager: Did you just beat up my waiter? Cyrus: I'm afraid so, I have a second personality that can get a bit out of control. Here take this money, keep some for yourself and give some to the waiter. *Cyrus throws a wad of $50 bills at the manager. The manager smiles and lets Cyrus leave. Now it's getting dark outside and not many people are about. Cyrus walks accross the road and into a park. He walks over to a bench and sits down.* Cyrus: Another eventfull night in the life of Cyrus Skar, oh well. It does not matter, I shall eat when I get home. JJ, I see you are concentrating on me as well as Rodd, well that's your decision, you have an important first match. I have a meaningless battle royal. I'll win it no problems, the only threats I have are Apoc and Skipster, I'll dispose of them like I did the rest. I don't need Spawn saying I'm a rookie, not even taking me into account for this match up. I am the European Champion, I am ranked 6th in this federation, Apoc and Skipster and everybody else in this battle royal are below my level of ability. I shall prove this once and for all at Distortion, maybe when I win this my name might start to get recognised. JJ, maybe you will get a shot at my European Title, maybe you won't. We'll see, you'll have to prove yourself first. You've just entered this federation, I can't really remember you from CCW, from what I remember you weren't that good back then. If you can prove to me you are worthy of a shot at this title, I'll give you one, until then, don't run your mouth. Don't get cocky thinking you can beat me, notch up a few victories like me and then maybe you can start to be a little bit arrogant, don't come into this federation thinking it'll be easy, because it sure as hell won't! I am here to make your life a living hell, I don't want this to be easy for you. I am a good champion, I shall dispose of any worthy challengers. I shall not, however, accept any challenge off anybody. You expect me to give you a shot at my title in your second match in FAW? I don't think so, you don't have much talent, if you can beat Rodd then maybe I will think about giving you a shot. But then again beating Rodd isn't much of an acheivement. Why did I assault you at Panic? Because you were running your mouth, you didn't have the right to run your mouth, especially not about me. Next time I hear you running your mouth about me, you won't be so lucky, I'll maime you so badly you won't know what's hit you. I'm not going to promise to end you career and all that sh!t! What's the point? The reality is most people retire, I have heard of very few people ending peoples careers, that's why I don't claim to be a career killing maching like some of the big names around here. Most big wrestlers think just because they are big and strong the can win every match. They don't realise you have to be mentally strong as well. I am mentally strong, I know how to counter any move thrown at me. I am a ring physcian. I know what to do when I'm in a certain part of the ring. I have great ring awareness, I have incredible ring intelligence, unlike most here in FAW. Now you may try and say that when I snap I lose my intelligence. That is the biggest mistake you make, because I don't lose it. All I lose when I snap, is my remorse, which means I won't hold back after the bells rung and I have won the match. Just hope for everbody elses sake I don't snap oh, and JJ. Beware of the Night Fall... *The camera fades out to black.*
