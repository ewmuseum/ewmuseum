Foamy again aaaahhh shit!!! Spawn Furiously Addictive Wrestling Roleplay #66 Date: 11/2/02 Location: BOSTON (DISTORTIONRP 1) (Scene opens with Spawn sitting on a couch in the Gym watching TV. Foamy's return promo is playing as the phone rings. as if this was planned he hits the speaker phone button.) Spawn: Speak at me. Mike: Yo, Spawn did you catch the promo. The movie producers is in talks with FAW to show the movie trailer at an upcoming Distortion. Spawn: I'm watching the promo now. Taking time off to shoot a movie in a time when the company needs you most what the hell were you thinking? ( There is a beep noise, as Mike goes to speak. Spawn looks at the caller ID.) Spawn: Mike, Prayer's calling on the other line I'm gonna connect him on threeway be quiet for a sec. (Spawn clicks over to the other call) Spawn: Speak to me Prayer: Spawn, this is Prayer. Before you hang up here me out. Spawn: I'm listening Prayer: I know I said something’s about the FAW that were pretty screwed up, and I thought most of them were true, but it seems that the FAW is holding it's own and I was wrong to doubt that it could. Tom gave me a job so I could pay my rent, and feed my Kids and when he left I thought the company was done. Spawn: So when you think things are going bad you abandon ship. What the hell kind of philosophy is that? Prayer: I realized I was wrong and now I want to make up for my mistakes. (There is another beep on the phone line, Spawn looks down at the caller ID again, and has a funny look on his face, he then connects all four people on conference call. All of a sudden the camera zooms in on the Face of a Teddy bear with multiple piercing, and purple hair.) Manny: WHAAAAAAAAAAZZZZUUUPPP? (Camera then flashes to a close up of Spawn) Spawn: WWHHHHAAAAAAZUUUPPPP??? (The camera then flashes to Manny on his Cell phone sitting in a booth in side Taco Bell.) Manny: WWWAAAAAZZZZZUUUUPPP? (The camera then flashes to a picture of Prayer. It appears as someone has drawn a mustache and horns on it. It also has a speech bubble that says Prayer is a gay homosexual.) WWWWHHHHAZUUUPPP? (The camera goes back to Spawn sitting on the couch talking on the phone.) Manny: So what you doing? Spawn: watching the promos drinking a water? Manny: Fighting the battle of who could care less. Prayer: I was trying to have an important conversation. Manny: Boo de frickin hoo, Who let the back stabbing snaggletoothed wall-eyed, pigeon-toed jackalope in on this conversation? Prayer: Damn Care Bear reject, since when do Teddy Bears use the phone? Manny: MMmmm, Tacos. Spawn: Anyway, was their an importance to this conversation? (There is another beep, and Spawn doesn't even bother to look at the caller ID before connecting the caller to the group.) Telemarketor: This is Michele Toler, with the Daily Journal Newspaper we're offering a free week long trial of our Newspaper. Would you be interested? Manny: (crying) Why are you people stalking me I don't want ur paper. Paper hurts like burning ahhhh stop trying to touch me there. Telemarketor: I'm not touching you I'm trying to tell you about our paper. Manny: What is this paper you speak of? What is it's absorbance level? I am trying to house break my New dog fluffy. Telemarketor: Absorbancy? What the hell kind of question is that? Who are you's people? Spawn: We're the people your mom warned you about. (Michele(the telemarketor) bursts into tears than hangs up on our group. After her nervous breakdown she quits her job cuz she can't put up with the torment. She moves back in with her parents, before reevaluating her life, and going back to college, and becoming a successful science teacher at Vineland High School. Back with our conversation.) Prayer: So what I was trying to say is that mike can come back, and train at the gym, and I'd like to come back, and teach if it's all good. Manny: LIES!!! All lies. He's just trying to find away back so he can plunge the knife deeper and put the FAW out of Business. Prayer: Look you Paddington bear rip-off on crack mind your business. Manny: Aww naw hell nah boy you all gone up and done it. Wait until next time I see you I'm gonna f*ck me somebody up. Prayer: The hell with it I don't know why I bother. (Prayer hangs up the phone.) Spawn: That was interesting. i still don't trust him. i think he's plotting. I don't have time to be worrying about him when there's Foamy to take care of? Manny: I gotta go. My friend is giving me a ride to Hot Topics. I'll see you llama's later. Mike: So do you think Prayer might be in on the whole theft thing? Spawn: Not sure, I have my suspicions, but the police haven't turned anything up yet. Mike: It's definitely something to keep an eye on. Spawn: So when you gonna return to action?, you've missed alot. Mike: Soon, but I don't wanna ruin the surprise a big, big surprise. Well Kidd I gotta run. Don't worry the DIWF will see the Freak Show again soon. (Both Spawn and Mike hang up the phone. Spawn looks over at the TV there's some new jobbers wrestling an opening match for one of FAW's recap show's. Spawn stretches out laying down on the couch. The match ends with a a very bad version of the Frog Splash. Well at least the match is over. Foamy promo comes on.) Spawn: Don't worry Devastation I haven't forgot about you. Distortion will be here soon, but the question is can you survive? 'I'm full of piss and vinegar right now. This is a Dogg you never wished you'd meet in a dark alley. But your about to meet me in something worse. And that's a ring. Foamy, this match is definatly going to the Dogg. But I'm glad I'm pissing you off Foamy. Because anger is blindness. And blindness could be deadly. Expecially in our case. Foamy, as you are, I'm going to be fighting for my life in this match because of two things. Your just doing it for the world title. I'm doing it for the belt and the respect. I know your a man Foamy. You are no pussy like the curtain-jerkers that are all of a sudden invading the FAW. I know you'll give respect to where respect is due. Even though it was never important to me before, it's kind of important now. But back to your anger. Your anger is gonna be your weakness in this match. It's gonna cost you your win. See, when I said you weren't mentally ready for the match, that's exactly what I meant. Your blindness is gonna cause you to get disqualified. But wrestle how ever you want to.' Is all going to end up with a victory for me. I'm not gonna whine and cry like you to try to justify any defeat I hand to you. I'm here to show your ignorance. Yes, it is possible for you ignorance to be stretched even more. I know, I was suprised too. But Foamy, each time you speak, it's out of your ass. Like the comment of me not being in your league. Your right, I'm far above your league. And on Sunday at Distortion I will prove just that. (Spawn turns the TV off leans back on the couch, and shuts his eyes as the camera fades to black.)
