A bit Dull..... Like Spawn And Chris Thunder The British Bomber Furiously Addictive Wrestling Roleplay #2 Date: Tuesday 15th October 2002 Location: Rotherham, South Yorkshire, England The Scene: A Cold, Wet Tuesday Morning In England ----- Start Promo ----- As the morning broke in England in the UK it was cold, The British Bomber felt good after Distortion and the payback he achieved. He climbed out of bed and opened the curtains to find a cold, wet, dark morning. He looked over at the clock as it read 9:15am, he then sighed and scratched his head, he then proceeded to get dressed putting on some black jeans and a Manchester Untied football club jersey. He then sat on the edge of the bed and began to think. 'Man its early, I knew I shouldn't have done that much work on Distortion last night..... oh well, it was worth it for a ounce of revenge on Thunder, he is going to get what he deserves off The Great One next week' He got to his feet and picked up the phone, he dialled in a number and it rang, but then went through to the voicemail on the person's phone. Bomber: Damn it, answer phones really annoy me. Anyway listen, Chris accepted the stipulation so I am choosing you as one of my lumberjacks, keep this all hush hush because I want them to be shocked when I name you at Disaster Down Under, its going to be you..... At that point the tape feed cuts out and then we quickly return. Bomber: So remember that cause you three guys will need to be on your toes and I know you can do it but until then keep this to yourself, I dont want anyone to know about it, alright gotta go, later. He hangs up then puts the phone down, he looks over at the wall and looks at posters of David Beckham, Manchester United, and various WWE superstars. Bomber: This Sunday I face Spawn so I must concentrate on the job in hand, my first match back since announcing my return last week on Distortion 17 and its main event, I think this proves to Thunder that I am in no way past it. Bomber starts to walk downstairs and downstairs is a beautiful young lady, she is about 25 with long blonde hair, he smiles at her. Bomber: Morning. Woman: Morning. Bomber: You seen the lineup for Distortion next week? Woman: Yeah....... Steven please becareful, I know your past history with Spawn, Chris Thunder as well as DCV, I have been watching them all week in week out and we both know what they are capable of. Bomber: How long is it till Christmas? Woman: 71 days, why? Bomber: Ok, well I know it isnt Christmas yet and we still got 10 weeks to go but I got a song I have prepared and been itching to sing, and I think now is the time to sing it. Woman: Oh god, you sing? Bomber: I'll sing it to Thunder so he can remember it and take time to memorise it for Disaster Down Under. I want to sing you a little song about Christmas, you know its called The 12 Days Of Christmas. Here we go Chris, this is something you can expect. At Disaster Down Under you can expect. 12 dropkicks droppin' 11 heads are bleedin' 10 spines are busting 9 broken bodies 8 kicks are kickin 7 punches punchin 6 5 star splashes 5 seconds of the fans chantin my name 4 stunners 3 englishlocks on your 2 bent legs and an ass kicking all over Australia! Woman: Not bad I suppose Bomber: Hey it ruled, as far as Spawn goes, next week when I defeat him, after I slap the Englishlock on and snap his ankle then leave the remains to The Great One I will move onto Chris Thunder and totally destroy him like the weaking he is. Woman: Then do it, cause I have to go to work, now I'll see you later. The woman gets up and leaves as Bomber watches her walks off in some tight black trousers and him staring at her ass........
