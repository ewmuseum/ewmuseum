Out of there Xing Furiously Addictive Wrestling Roleplay #217 Date: 22nd JANUARY Location: Recently with Xing “We are arresting you Xing on assault of this man your old school bully teacher you will be held in over night just on suspicion but any more dangerous acts and you will be held in for longer lets say you are on probation for this week because we can be forced enough to ban you from this Sundays PPV event.” Xing spits out at the old school bully teacher and his length in jail is increased to two extra days. He then at the police office tries to escape out of the jail with even worse consequences he is held in for even longer much to the dismay of Xing. In Xings cell nowadays “I can’t get to sleep with all this snoring and the annoyance of not being allowed to go to rumble is really ticking me off big time, roar! I need to get out of here hello look I know you want your sleep at this time, What! Look at the time I have a news conference in an hour please can you let me out of here I will give you money look nice money or I will give you clothes nice very nice gear. Just come here now and let me out! Or I wont shut up you fucking selfish Americans just let me out or are you having your fifth course meal yet for breakfast and you are still going to eat up to your 9th meal then once you have finished you’ll.” Xing stops talking when he sees a very annoyed cop walk up to his cell looking extremely tired he says, “what do you want with me?” Xing replies, “I have a news conference now can you ring them and inform them of my whereabouts because I don’t want to blow this opportunity you see this is very important.” Xing is once again cut off when talking by the cop who said, “Look I will call them in the morning I don’t want to hear your life story all right now get to sleep or you will wake the other cell mates wait a minute you tried that last time look don’t do anything you will soon regret because I will keep my eye on you.” Xing falls asleep gets down into his dream where he is the rumble winner and the fans are up on there seats cheering for Xing when he is woken even more to the dismay for Xing he is awoken by a loud horn Xing feels sick and tired after having less than 15 minutes of rest. He is pulled out of his cell by his ear from a guard on duty and is taken to a concealed room he is given a lawyer he sits opposite the police and the old school bully teacher to make matters worse every word and every movement is caught on tape so he cannot make a mistake. DC Plod: “Right Xing we are going to ask you a series of questions and don’t be influenced by the old school bully teacher ok here is the first question ‘Do you plead guilty or not guilty over assaulting the old school bully teacher the man sitting next to me?’ Xing: “What do you expect me to say look at this beautiful face how can I be guilty? Blob, Plob, Tobe, or whatever your name is I know you told me not to be influenced by the old school dud but look at that face it is so annoying.” DC Plod: “Its Plod and anyway if you were to plead not guilty then you wouldn’t be bothered with his look with have video evidence of a man that matches your details to be ramming this man here into a slot machine look at that injured face.” Xing looks at the old schools face to be so ticked that he gets onto his knees pleading guilty and praying that he can get let out with no repercussions. The interview is broken down when Xing has a visitor he is once again pulled to the next room his visitor the conference men who are ready to set up the interview through the glass. The interview Interviewer: “I know this is nor time or place to be doing but answer please all of these questions first one ‘Why are you in jail?’ second one ‘How do you think you will fare at the rumble?’ and the last one ‘What kind of shape are you in with no training being in jail and all?’ Xing: “Look I will answer all of your questions in a few minutes but please before I do look I am on my knees here look please before I answer your questions can you pay the money to bail me out because at the moment I am not going to the rumble because I will be stuck in here all afternoon on Sunday Please!” Interviewer: “What is in it for us if we do bail you out with our money?” Xing: “Look I will give you the money to pay for my bail but if you don’t you wont get the answers to the questions and you wont have anything to tell your bos on the fed back call.” Interviewer: “Ok we will do it but only if you answer our questions right now and you hand over the money also now.” Xing: “ Ok I agree also to your terms look heres the money don’t tell anyone I gave you it or you and I will be toast because I don’t let anyone out of my area without getting hurt just like I did with Violator and just like I did with Jeff Harris ok here are my answer. Question 1: ‘My old school bully teacher.’ Question 2: ‘I will fare just fine and I will knock every other 19 superstars that look me in the eye over the top rope.’ Question 3: ‘At the moment I am in all right shape but I need a warm up dummy to beef up.’ Ok pleasing that’s it now get me out of here!” Half an hour after they bail him out Xing stands outside his old school is looking through the window of his old teachers office.
