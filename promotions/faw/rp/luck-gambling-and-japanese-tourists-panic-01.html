Luck, Gambling and Japanese Tourists (Panic #01)

26th November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Luck, Gambling and Japanese Tourists (Panic #01) 'Legendary' Lee Smith Furiously Addictive Wrestling Roleplay #80 Date: 26th November 2002 Location: Geneva, Switzerland “I’d like to withdraw some money please” he said, in a broad American accent. Lee Smith stood, with his recently shaven bald head emphasising his menacing eyes. He is wearing an expensive tuxedo as if he is going out. “How’a much’a?” the lady at the counter said in a predominantly French accent. “Thre’a thousand’a dolla” he said, evidently mocking the womans accent. She gave him the money in $10. Lee turned around and looked at his surroundings. He was in a busy bank, bustling with highflying businessmen withdrawing money, and newlyweds starting new accounts as well as the healthy sum of people insisting last week they didn’t withdraw $10,000. Lee places the money inside his jacket and strides confidently across the hall. As he gets to the door he stretches his arms and yawns. He swings open the door and is blinded by the sunlight. By now the sun is setting upon the horizon. Lake Geneva shines with radiance and hospitality as Lee takes out some sunglasses and puts them on. Lee slides his hands into his pockets and walks with his head to the ground; his face expressing no feelings. He comes to a halt as he approaches a horizontal escalator which escorts people to their destination. He hops aboard and watches at the people that are his surroundings. Children hang around the outside of the area, begging to be let in. They scream curse words in French at the security guard who ushers them away. Meanwhile toddlers wait with their mothers who anticipate their husbands arrival from the ship just coming in from Lake Geneva. They greet them and hug them; asking how they’re long day at work has been. The Legend comes out of his fixed stare as the escalator stops. He glances at the inside of the multi-storey complex and enters. Suddenly he is hit with an adrenaline rush of addiction, scams and money laundering. “WELCOME TO THE CASINO” the sign reads, flashing in a Vegas type fashion. He takes his hands out of his pockets and roars with excitement as the cash is airborne. He manages to catch it all in a mad scramble; hands flailing everywhere. He then approaches the customer desk and lands his elbow on the desk. He purches there as the man at the desk continues what seems to be a social talk. The man laughs in hysterics - exactly the opposite of Lee who is frowning like an enraged cheetah. The Living Legend mutters a curse word as he puts his hand on the phone. He “accidentally” hangs up for the man at the counter who now switches identities with Lee, who has a huge grin on his face - the opposite of the man - who now has a rather large frown assembling. “What’s the charge rate for chips?” he says, flicking through his notes with eye contact completely on them. “100 chips iz 28 dollar Monsieur” the middle aged man says, stroking his goatee as Lee rolls his cash and puts it on the desk. “Give me all you got” Lee Smith says to the man who then digs deep into his till and hands over thousands of chips. He then hands Lee $500 and ushers him away. 2 HOURS LATER The sun is now permanently embedded down below in Geneva as black sweeps the street. Meanwhile in the casino the golden rays of the sun still shine - for Lee is slowly improving his skills. We zoom in on the Hero’s Idol who sits with his eye focused on the machine. He continues the short and simple process of placing his chip in the machine and pulling the red plastic stick tilted towards him that’s spins the reels on a collision course with each other. He loses. No emotion shows on his face as he begins to doze off. ZZ…….ZZZZZZZZZZZZZ………..ZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZ.ZZZZZZZZZZ…….. Tap on the shoulder. Eyes open. Yawn. Where’s money? Money in machine. Lee awakes with a jolt and turns with his eyes barely open. He realises he never made it to his hotel room and was asleep. He sees a flash which almost blinds him completely. It is a Japanese tourist who (unusually) speaks very good English. “Are you Lee Smith?” he asks, as the slow interrogation begins. The man seems nervous as he is aware of Lee’s temper. “That’s….the…name” Lee says, with his words separated by yawns at intervals. He rubs his eyes and sees that the Japanese man has bleached hair and brown eyebrows as well as bold brown eyes. Lee looks towards the exit as the Japanese mans speech bores him and sees it is dawn. “LEE SMITH! LEE SMITH! LEE SMITH!” the man shouts as a stampede of tourists burst through the door in peculiar fashion. He hears his name being chanted but curls up in his arms and tries to get back to sleep. He is tapped on the shoulder by a man who is predominantly American. “F##K OFF!” Lee shouts. The echoes can be heard for several seconds as the tourists slowly wear away - scared by Smiths attitude - all having seen tapes of him in action before...and all…generally…..not taking a liking to him. Lee is tapped on the shoulder again and doesn’t even bother to turn around this time. Instead he screams “GO TO HELL” - with emphasis on the word hell. “One question…please……..” the man asks and before Lee can reject his offer he speaks. “Do you think you’ll beat Xing?” he asks….Lee turns and a cheeky smirk appears. “You know, I’ve been gambling all night. One thing I’ve learnt all night is that gambling isn’t a normal game. This game is about luck. So what if your Einstein - the unemployed janitor next door as got just as much chance…..well….unless your loaded with cash like me…in which case the stakes are slightly higher. So - gambling is all about luck. Whereas my day job - kicking the shit out of losers like Xing - is all about talent. And you know, I’ve been studying him closely. What past experience has he got? He went unbeaten in the sewers of Brooklyn. Now, look at my past experience. A GCW European champion - GCW WORLD Champion - IWF Hardcore Champion….you name it I’ve had it. So…I propose to you that our match at Panic, Sunday December 1st (which just happens to be my birthday) is when the talent will really shine. What do I mean by talent? Me of course. I’m talented and skilled - as well as experienced. Xing…is not. Instead he fights on the memory of his wife - who I highly doubt ever existed. I hope you don’t mind me asking…well…actually…I don’t care if you mind me asking - but are you a betting man?” “Now that you ask, I am.” the man says, curiously looking at Lee with his eyebrow raised. “Well - if I were you I’d head down the bookies now. Fill in that slip - and then the money will be rolling in for you.” Lee replies, as the man questions him. “Who shall I place a bet on?” he whispers, making sure no others in the casino hear this tip. “Me. Lee Smith to defeat Xing on Sunday 1st December” Lee mutters. He slowly gets out of the chair which is now almost attached to him and tip toes away. The man comes out of thinking mode and continues socialising with Lee. After a few seconds he realises that Smith is no where to be seen….and continues with his days work.
