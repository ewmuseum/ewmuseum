DDU Roleplay 2 [He's inside my freaking head!]

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
DDU Roleplay 2 [He's inside my freaking head!] The Great One Furiously Addictive Wrestling Roleplay #40 Date: 24/10/02 Location: The Great One's home 'Get out of my house man, I'm not telling you again. You are trespassing for one and secondly you are breaking and entering. I should call the police right now and have you arrested but that would be a waste of time because they wouldn't get here for the next three hours, so screw them. But I'm warning you right here, right now, get the hell out of my house and off my land. Oh you won't really, well then it's your fault then when you wake up tomorrow you'll find yourself in a hospital bed isn't it because I am this close to bashing your living brains in. I mean it bitch, I'll paint the walls with your brains, they could use a fresh coat and since I have a bit of free time I just might think about doing it right now. You're not scared, really, you don't think I’ll go through with my words and I won't do it. Why don't you try me and see just what happens when you screw with me. You screw with me and you're going to get yourself a lot of pain and you will get hurt, so just get out of this house right now or I will speed up the beginning of the end of the your life. I'm not going to sit back and watch you roam throughout my house and do whatever the hell you want without my permission. I own this place and I say what goes on around here and I am telling you that your company and your presence is not wanted now get out before I kick you out. You better put that vase down, that was my mothers vase and she asked me to have it as a family emblem, now put it down.' Great One jumps out of bed as the man in his dream smashes the vase over his head. His face is covered in sweat and he is breathing heavily. He seems to be quite shaken up from the dream he just had and quickly takes the bed covers off of him and gets out of bed wearing nothing but boxer shorts that he sleeps in. He rubs his eyes with both hands as he turns the clock radio around to look at the time. The clock reads 03:30 and Great One gives a sigh and hits himself on the head. He stretches up and then down to touch his toes. He sits down on his bed and opens the top draw and takes some socks out and sits them on the bed. He then goes over to the closet and takes out some Le Coq Sportif tracksuit bottoms and a Pittsburgh Penguins hockey shirt. He takes them over to his bed and sits them down next to his socks. He puts the socks on first, then the tracksuit bottoms and then the hockey shirt. He stands up once again fully dressed and walks over towards the door. He is a bit dizzy and starts to stumble before he loses his balance and falls over banging his head on the floor and knocking himself unconscious. 'What are you up to now? Come on man there is no need for this, you don't need to get all of these guys to help you, why not do this like a man and face me one on one, or have you balls been crunched that much that you can't do it on your own? You are like one of the toughest and strongest men I have ever known and still you have to make the playing field un even. I'm at the bottom of the hill and you're at the top and it's a sixty degree angle, there is no way for me to get on top. I don't stand a chance at the moment because all you do is cheat and get help, you're pathetic. What's the point in cheating to get a victory, what do you prove when you win through cheating? It's defies the whole point of the better man picking up the win. Look at you with your steel chair in your hand, what's wrong with walking up to me and punching me in the face? You know that I can defend myself and I'll fight back and hit you back don't you. It's not wise, it's not clever, it's cowardness. You disgust me. You are the top of the mountain and pushing away everything that opposes you without even breaking into a sweat sometimes, so why are you pulling out all the illegal crap to get one over on me? You've taken me out before without using any methods of breaking the rules, so what's the damn deal with all of this? You hit me with that chair and you're just going to piss me off even more than I already am. You already broke into my house and smashed a very expensive vase over my head that meant a lot to me, and I'm already pissed at you. Don't make me any more angry than I already am because I will not hesitate into kicking your ass this time around, so just put the chair down.' Great One wakes up from his fall, again in a cold sweat, and a bruise on his forehead. He pulls himself up on the bed and shakes the cobwebs off his head before he rubs the bruise on his head. 'Ow,' he moans as he rubs the small yellow mark just above his right eye. He gets up, walks out of the door and enters the bathroom. He looks himself in the mirror and sees the bruise on his head. 'Man I'm tired,' he says to himself as he opens the cabinet and pulls out some soap. He turns the tap on and fills the sink with water. He proceeds to wash his hands and face before pulling the plug and letting the water run out through the pipes. He takes his toothbrush and gives his teeth a clean and then flosses. As he is flossing he goes a bit too far down and cuts into his gum. 'Damn it,' he yells as he withdraws the floss from between his teeth, 'what the hell am I doing up at this time anyway?' He flushes the used floss down the toilet and then goes to leave the room. But before he can leave the room he slips on the soap that had fallen onto the floor and as he falls he bangs his head on the toilet seat, once again knocking himself out. 'Get me a doughnut will you Pete, thanks man. Yo Semore, lets go sit down over here. Carl should be here in a minute, I told him to meet us here. Oh Pete, get us two lemonade's as well please, thanks. I'll leave the money on the desk, keep the change. Oh, Carl's here already. Wait a minute that's not Carl. Who the hell are you and what do you think you're doing sitting down at our table like you're our best friend. Like I care if talking like that isn't going to get me popular, I don't give a shit, I'm not on duty and I ain't signing any autographs all right. I'm sick of all the attention I get, maybe I should just quit wrestling all together. What I shouldn't quit wrestling why not? Because you haven't kicked my ass yet, what? What are you talking about. Hey listen boy, you better settle down, we're in the middle of a cafe and I don't want to get into any fighting in my local cafe all right. If you want to fight somebody then why don't you get lost and go and fight with one of your friends. I'm not in the mood to be fighting, I need to save up all of my energy for my showdown with the World Title on Sunday, now get lost before I tell the get security and they throw you out. Go on, get lost, be gone, go find yourself another table to fiddle with yourself you sick little bastard. Don't you just sit there and ignore me, you'll do what I say right now, and you can shove that mask right up your fat ass which is the same place it came from because it stinks like shit anyway, just like your wrestling and fighting skills do as well I am sure. What, you're going to show me that you don't stink at fighting and wrestling? Prove is bitch, I'd love to see you try because my mind tells me that you absolutely suck and my mind is not wrong very often. Hey, I said fighting and wrestling skills, not how well you can hit someone over the head with a baseball bat. I'll tell you what you can do with that bat shall I, you can take it and make it feel right at home with all of your friends and shove it straight up your own ass and have hours and hours of pleasure, shut up Semore. But if you hit me around the head then you will be sorry.' Great One wakes up once again as he is hit around the head with the baseball bat curtosy of the same person that hit him with the vase and the chair in his previous two dreams. He sighs deeply as he wakes up and puts his hand to his mouth and sees the blood of his lip on his fingers. He stands up, picks up the soap and puts it where it belongs; in the cabinet. He runs the cold water, takes some tissue paper and holds the paper under the water. He dabs at the cut on his upper lip and clears the blood away before running his hand under the tap to remove the blood from his fingers. He throws the toilet paper down the toilet and flushes. 'Oh man,' he sighs, 'what the hell is going on? I keep falling over, knocking myself out and dreaming about the same dude hitting me over the head with some object and that wakes me up. Something is very wrong here. I mean I don't even know who the guy is because his face is either blurred or he wears a mask. What the hell is going on?' He walks down the stairs and into the kitchen where he goes straight over to the fridge. He takes out a glass of milk and drinks it down to half the glass. The glass then falls out of his hand and hits him on the foot and he jumps backwards to avoid the shards of glass that are produced as the glass hits the floor, but as he does so he bangs his head on the cupboards behind him and falls into a sitting position on the floor. 'It's a beautiful isn't it. The sun is out, the birds are singing, the kids are playing having fun. Smell the freshness is the air; it's a wonderful day. Lets just hope it stays like this because I am sick of the rain in the world. I also can't afford to get wet and catch a cold, I need to be at my best for Sunday or else Spawn is going to beat me like a bitch. I guess I sort of lost it at one time but never again. That time he beat me was really bad but I got back up and I beat him and made the score even. I need to make it uneven this Sunday by beating the hell out of him and making an example out of his to the world. I can't afford to sit back and let him run ragged all over me because I'm too good for that. I am The Great One and I should have the FAW World Title, but I guess God is trying to stop that from happening with all the shit that has been going on lately. Not you again. Can't you just get a life and leave me the hell alone. You come here, you find me and say basically nothing. You hit me in my house, you hit me in my local cafe, and now we're in a park. Don't subject all of these children to your violent acts because they need to grow up to be civilized and kids and not think that fighting is how everything should be done. You are an immature little bitch if you are fighting all the time and you have nothing better to do than go looking for trouble. Grow up man and find something constructive to do. You don't need to be hitting out on people all the time, go and do the family thing or whatever man instead of looking for a fight. What are you carrying a shovel around with you for? Have you come to play in the sand with all the other kids or something? It's just like I said earlier, you are an immature little kid who needs to taught a lesson and if you're not careful I'm going to teach you this lesson with my right hand, my left hand and that shovel and I smack it around your fat ugly head and then go digging with it, right up your ass, bitch.' Great One wakes up as he is hit around the head with the shovel. He rubs the back of his head, the part which hit the cupboard a minute ago and then rises to his feet. He looks down and sees the milk and broken glass on the floor. He kneels down beside it and picks up some of big pieces of glass and puts them into the bin. He cuts his right index finger on one of the pieces of glass as he drops them into the bin. 'Shit,' he yells as the blood appears on his finger. He puts his finger in his mouth and sucks up the blood from his finger. He stands up and takes a packet of plasters out from the cupboard and wraps one around his finger. He then goes into the closet and pulls out the vacuum cleaner and hoovers up the smaller pieces of glass on the floor as well as some of the milk. When all the glass is cleared he takes a cloth and scrubs on the floor where all the milk is until it's all soaked up. He places the towel over the sink and rings it out so that all the milk falls out of the towel and goes down the drain. He opens up the washing machine and throws the towel in along with some other whites that were lying near him. He puts some toast in the toaster and starts to prepare his breakfast without any accidents happening to him this time around. 'Man, I better see someone about these dreams,' he says to himself and he walks back into the living room and looks in the cabinet for a phone book. He opens up the book and looks for a psychiatrist. He looks down for the one closest to him and writes down the number on a piece of paper. He picks up his cell phone and dials the number. 'Hello, is that the psychiatry? Oh good. I'm sorry that I am calling this early in the morning, but I'm afraid it's urgent, I'm starting to get paranoid here. You can send someone over right now, thanks. Ok, my address is: one, West Grove Gardens. That's all you need, oh that's ok then. How long will he be? That's pretty quick, ok, I'll see the guy in ten minutes then. Thank you very much. Bye.' He puts the phone down and then puts some beans in the microwave and two eggs on the stove. A few minutes later they are all done and he sits down at the table to eat his breakfast of fried egg and beans on toast. As soon as he finishes his food there is a knock at the door. He takes his plate out and puts it in the kitchen with the pile of dirty dishes that he didn't get round to washing the night before. He wipes his mouth and then opens the door. 'Hello,' says the man at the door. 'Hi,' replies Great One. 'I'm James, and I'm sorry to call you at this time but I think it's urgent.' 'It's no problem sir, I'm always available at any time to help the needy,' says the psychiatrist. 'I'm not the needy, I'm not having spasms and things, just strange dreams that I'm getting a tad worried about. And what should I call you?' asks Great One. 'You can call me David, I guess,' he replies. 'Ok then Dave, come in and have a seat.' 'I said David, not Dave,' answers David as he walks into Great One's house. Great One closes the door and both take a seat and start talking. 'So, what seems to be the problem James?' 'Well it started off when I went to sleep last night. I woke up at half past three this morning in a cold sweat after dreaming that someone was in my house. I kept telling him, I think it was a him, to get out and that if he didn't I would kick his ass and get rid of him, but he just stood there in my house immensely calm. Nothing I said to him got through to him, it was like he couldn't understand what I was saying. He didn't care about my feelings and what certain things meant to me, and he even picked up a very expensive vase that was handed down to me from my mother, and her father gave it to her. That was when I woke up. I woke up on the exact moment that I was hit over the head with the vase. I woke up and couldn't believe what had happened in my dream and was very confused by it.' 'I can see why. Very confusing that is, what line of work are you in?' 'Professional Wrestling. I work for FAW, Furiously Addictive Wrestling.' 'I see, and I suppose you have a match coming up do you?' 'Well of course, and it's the biggest match of my career.' 'Well there you go then. You are simply frightened of your next opponent and the man who was in your dream was your opponent.' 'No but I'm not scared of him, not one bit. I've faced him twice before and beat him in one of those matches. I don't sweat Spawn at all.' 'Well if you don't sweat him as you put it, why are you having dreams of him attacking you?' 'I'm not having dreams of him attacking me, I'm dreaming on some person I don't know attacking me. If it was Spawn then why would his face be blanked out?' Great One starts to get annoyed. 'Maybe you are so afraid of him that you can't dare look into his face.' 'Maybe he's so ugly that I dare not look into his face. But let me carry on. I slipped and knocked myself out right. Then I have another dream. I haven't got a clue where this one is at, but it's the same guy once again only this time he has two buddies with him and I am cornered, out numbered and am about to get my ass kicked. Being the fair guy that I am I try to reason with him and tell him to fight me like a man and just make it one on one. But he doesn't listen to me and it ends with me hitting me in the head with a steel chair.' 'Well then it has to be this Spawn character, who in normal life would hit you with a steel chair? Steel chairs are only ever used for weapons in wrestling. It has to be a wrestler who is in your dreams because no one else would ever think about hitting you with a steel chair, and since your next opponent is Spawn, you are obviously afraid of him and he is the man who is terrorizing your dreams. Just see sense and believe it man, you know it's true.' 'No, it isn't, that's not how it works. Spawn hasn't done anything to make me fear him. Chris Thunder has more of a right to be in my dreams than Spawn; Thunder is the one who has been attacking me ever since I returned last month. If it were any one that I know then why would his face be blanked out anyway? It has to be someone who I haven't got a clue about and wouldn't even think about them crossing my paths. Sure, it may have something to do with FAW, maybe it's someone who is returning. There are rumours flying around backstage that there is someone who is joining or returning to FAW and he wants to put his hands on only one person. I'm not sure if that is meant in a loving sense or a hateful sense but if I'm going to be the next FAW World Champion then I'm going to be at the top of the list aren't I? It could be someone like, oh shit. It could be Pyro.' 'Who is Pyro?' 'Pyro is this little bitch who I put through a table this time last year and he never got a true revenge on me and now that I'm back in FAW and am about to win the biggest prize that it can offer, I am going to be the number one target for many people. Pyro and Spawn have a lot of history together; they were former tag team champions. I'd hate to see the end of DCV and then the reformation of The Omens Of Death, those guys got me fired from FAW in one match and I won't see it happening again.' 'Ok so we've established that is must be someone from the FAW or someone who is joining or returning to the FAW, which was what I said all along. Now even though you don't want to believe it, it is fear that you hold. You are about to get to the top of FAW and you have heard rumours and things and it's making you paranoid that you are going to be targeted to be taken out. You don't know that this person who is returning is coming after you so lets put that aside for a moment. You are also not the main target just yet anyway as you still don't hold the FAW World Title. If this Pyro is coming back you said you had put him through a table so what are you afraid of him for?' Great One starts to frown and bites his tongue to let some of his anger out. 'I never said anything about being afraid all right, now lay off the afraid thing damn it. Pyro is a huge legend in FAW, the second man to be put into the FAW Hall of Fame, he will never be forgotten around this place. Spawn and Pyro make the best team in FAW history, and if they were to reunite as a team then my chances of success are lowered. Spawn can't do it on his own and that's why I am thinking he has been recruiting and gone to get help and the most obvious help would be a returning Pyro as they have been best friends for so long. But screw Pyro all right, I haven't fought Pyro in a match yet and I would love to kick hiss ass so if that jimima wants to come back then he can and I'll happily bash his living brains in. But I'm telling you, this guy in my dreams in not Spawn. I have had more dreams than those two.' 'Well then tell me the next one,' says David as he jots something down. 'So this time I'm in a cafe with my best friend Semore. We're just getting a bite to eat and a quick drink when he walks in and sits down again. We were expecting my friend Carl but Carl doesn't show up and instead we get some guy with a mask on. This time his face isn't blanked out, he is wearing a mask. This adds to your guess of it being Spawn because Spawn wears a mask, but many others wear masks also. The mask is not one that I have seen before. I keep talking to him and telling him to go and find somewhere else to sit but he pays no attention to me at all and just blatantly ignores me. He even tries to order something to be brought to the table we are sitting at. He tries to give me some shit but it's no use and then he hits me around the head with a baseball bat and I wake up. It's the same thing happening every time. I have the dream about this idiot, I give him and shit and tell him where to go, he hits me over the head with something and I wake up. I ain't got a clue what's going.' 'Perhaps these dreams are telling you that you're not ready to be fighting for the FAW World Title. You have only been back in FAW for what, a month, and you have already been gifted a shot at the world title. That is a very fast rise to the top even if you are one of the top guys in the federation and you pull in the biggest crowds. I was saying fear, fear that you are not good enough at the moment from all the time you have had out of action and now you come back and face your arch nemeses Spawn. Spawn is very big, very tough, very strong, be is a very big challenge for you and I thought that you could have been afraid of letting all your fans down and letting yourself down. I have noticed that you are very cocky and somewhat over confident and you are very big headed. You believe you are Gods gift to wrestling and if you don't back up your words you are going to embarrass yourself in front of the world. You have heard rumours of someone returning and have automatically thought that this returning character is going to go straight for you and take you out because you will be the main man after Disaster Down Under if you win the title. You just need to relax and train harder.' 'You might be right, I have no reason to be afraid, I am the best wrestler to ever step into a FAW ring. There isn't no one I haven't fought and beat so I should just forget about the whole thing as if it never happened. I am going to kick the shit out of Spawn at Disaster Down Under simply because I can. Thank you for your help.' Great One stands up, as does David, and Great One leads him over to the door and lets him out. 'Thanks, bye.' He got him out as quick as he could and even forgot to pay him but David must have forgot himself as he didn't ask for any money and walks away after he is pushed out of the door. 'I said I have no reason to be afraid. Maybe I am scared. After all I am one cocky son of a bitch and I can't afford to lose or I'll look like an idiot. It must be a wake up call. I have to train harder for the next few days than I have ever done before in my life.' Great One picks up his sports bag and leaves the house locking the door behind him. He is going to prepare for the biggest night in his life.
