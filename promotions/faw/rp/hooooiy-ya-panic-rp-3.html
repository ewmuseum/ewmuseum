HOOOOIY-YA! (Panic RP #3) Gabriel Hunter Furiously Addictive Wrestling Roleplay #254 Date: 1st February Location: Chinatown -- The anticipation is getting greater and greater, as Sunday Panic draws closer. To satisfy a pretty violent mood, Hunter has agreed to attend a martial arts & Karate course, in a big to take out some anger, and to improve his repertoire. Besides, Karate is damn cool -- -- The camera zooms in, but Im not going to tell you WHAT it zooms into! HAHAHAHA! Oh wait, that would be pointless, wouldn’t it? Why Am I saying this? Meh. Anyway, the scene slowly begins to fade in, inside an oriental-style building. Oriental style in what way? Well, we’re in a room, measuring about 10x10metres, in the centre of the floor, is a 49 sq metre padded mat, a faded grey colour, it looks as strong and sturdy as a freight train. Four large pillars are what we find at each of the corners, each decorated with oriental drawings and inscriptions, probably for decoration more than anything else. The roof is tall and pointed, it’s wood painted in different tones of red and gold, tales of dragons and samurais told through the pieces of Eastern art. The “walls” of the room are typical oriental – wooden rectangular grades, backed by paper, very bright white paper at that. Through the paper, which obviously has several high-intensity lights shining upon it, the silhouettes of several people dressed in Japanese robes can be seen, walking to-and-fro. The only visible entrance to the room is along the far wall, which is also decorated with oriental décor. Soft, Japanese strumming music plays gently in the background – which is quite relaxing. -- -- Suddenly, with the hit of a gong, an oriental grand master steps through the door on the far wall, dressed in a white martial-arts robe, a black belt tied tight around his waist. He looks to be about 50 – shown by the slightly greying black hair, which sits a-top of his gloriously stereotypical mandarin head. He walks across the room towards the edge of the mat, before bowing toward the door. As he bows, FAW Superstar Gabriel Hunter steps through the door, dressed in similar attire – albeit with a white belt. Gabriel, trying his hardest to impress, has the same look of concentration across his face as was seen in the grand master’s features. Gabriel bows in return at the Japanese gentleman, who walks into the centre of the mat, Gabriel following. Again, the grand master bows, as does Gabriel, before the black-belt begins to speak, in a wonderful accent. -- Grand Master: Gweetings glasshoppa, I undahstand that you have come to learn the ahrt of Karate? -- Gabriel bows once more, the palms of his hands placed together. -- Gabriel Hunter: Si Senior … Errr, I mean, Hei! Grand Master: Good, then we can begin. My name is Gwand Mastah Hidetoshi Yakahama, welcome to the combat arenah, mister? -- Gabriel pauses for a second, not realising that Yakahama is indeed asking him a question. -- Gabriel Hunter: Ah yes, my name is Gabriel Hunter, grand master! -- Yakahama again bows. Gabriel looks around curiously, before noticing that Yakahama has bowed, and quickly follows suite. -- Gabriel Hunter: Oh, sh*t, this bowing malarkey is dead confusing -- Yakahama does not response to his, ignoring Hunter’s idiocy. -- Yakahama: We must be started, glasshoppa, you have much to learn. First of all, I am going to stawt you off with a basic Karate chop. Gabriel Hunter: Yes, glasshoppa. Yakahama: Uh uh! You glasshoppa, me gwand mastah! Gabriel Hunter: Sorry, GWAND MASTAH! Yakahama: That bettah. Now, the secret to a good, hard Karate chop, is all in the elbow movement. Any old glasshoppa can TWY and chop, but only true warriors can execute this simple move with pinpoint perfection. All you have to do, glasshoppa, is to set your forearm at a 90 degwee angle with your upper arm, and then … HUUUUH! -- With a suitable grunt, Yakahama performs a stereotypical karate job, just like you’d expect it to look like. Gabriel looks at Yakahama, who looks at him in return, as if to say “your turn”. Without hesitation, Gabriel attempts the chop. -- Gabriel Hunter: HIIIYAA! Yakahama: Vewwy good glasshoppa … You are quick learnah, have you practised the art of combat befoah? Gabriel Hunter: Uhh, well yeah, I guess. I mean, I’m not a karate master or anything, but I’m like, a professional wrestler, if that counts for anything. -- Yakahama nods his head, looking indifferently interested. -- Yakahama: Ahh yes, any experience as a combatant is vewwy useful in the art of Karate! Gabriel Hunter: Well, the only reason I came to this stupid chink-house was to learn how to do some f*cking cool kicks and throws to beat the crap out of Spider, Colby Newsome and Big Baby Jesus with, you know? Yakahama: I see. Gabriel Hunter: Well, I thought I outta put some foots in some mouths, so to speak. I made this “big” return to wrestling, but to be frank, I haven’t done jack crap since I entered, so I’m out to prove my worth on Sunday ... -- Yakahama nods, and bows again to Hunter’s slight surprise. -- Yakahama: Ahhhh, glasshoppa, you are vewwy skilled in the art of pre-battle trash-talk! Gabriel Hunter: Err, well, I haven’t got started yet, but anyway. Firstly, there’s this Spider guy, who’s supposed to be some kind of legend around here. Well, to be honest, I don’t really know a great deal about him, other than he’s a former world champion. I question the guy who made this match, placing a guy like Spider in a match against 4 rookies, but heck, If he wants to get in my why of victory, I’ll be more than happy to squash him! And then there’s this guy, Big Baby Jesus, who thinks he’s some kind of legend or something like that. Jesus, let me tell you this, you can call yourself the “son of God” all you want, but the fact is, you’re just another run-of-the-mill moron. -- Gabriel pauses for breath, perhaps getting a little too carried away with his trash-talk, before catching his breath, and continuing. -- Gabriel Hunter: And then there’s Colby Newsome, who doesn’t even seem to acknowledge my existence. Well, I see he’s new around here like me, and will be looking to prove something also, but to be honest, I don’t see him as a threat … In fact, he’ll probably be beaten to a bloody pulp by the end of the night. Yakahama: Blood, you say? Gabriel Hunter: Uhhh yeah … But anyway, aren’t you supposed to be teaching me Karate or something? Yakahama: AHHH YES! KARATE~! -- Gabriel rolls his eyes. -- Yakahama: Now that you have easily mastered thah arts of thah chop, I must teach you how to use your feet as a weapon. HIIIIYAAAA! -- Yakahama launches into the air, performing a 360 degree spinning kick, his right leg well above his head. He lands down with grace, as if he had never even moved. -- Yakahama: A 360 Spin Kick, although time consuming, is a mastahful attack that, when successful, knocks opponent for six! Now you twy, glasshoppa! -- Gabriel, not fully remembering what Yakahama actually did, shrugs his shoulders, before jumping into the air, not anywhere near as high as Yakahama reached, and performing a dropkick. Gabriel lies flat on his face on the mat below, before rising up to his feet, brushing his suit down. -- Gabriel Hunter: How’s that!? Yakahama: Not exactly what I expect from you, glasshoppa, you have much to learn … Gabriel Hunter: I see, so this is going to take more than 15 minutes then? -- Yakahama smirks, shaking his head. -- Yakahama: You have sense of humour! I like sense of humour! Gabriel Hunter: Dude! It was a serious question. Yakahama: Anyway, now we must twy a basic throw, before we move onto the more advanced methods of Karate! First … -- Yakahama grabs a hold of Gabriel’s arm. -- Yakahama: You must gwab your opponent by the arwm! Then, which your right foot, buckle thair left leg … -- As he said, Yakaham hooks Gabriel’s leg, before flipping him over his shoulder hard down onto the mat. Gabriel, being a wrestler of course, merely shrugs it off, and climbs back to his feet, “dusting” himself off. -- Gabriel Hunter: Ummm, Ok, that’s it? Yakahama: Hehe, relax glasshoppa, that is merely a startah! Gabriel Hunter: When do we get to the beat sh*t out of each other. -- Yakahama chuckles. -- Yakahama: The time for combat will come, glasshoppa … Gabriel Hunter: Ok, but one more thing … Yakahama: What that? Gabriel Hunter: MY NAME IS GABRIEL YOU DAFT TWAT! So stop calling me Glasshoppa, before I kick your ass. -- Again Yakahama chuckles, seemingly irritating Hunter. -- Yakahama: Twust me, Gabriel, you are far from being able to defeat me in the art of combat … Gabriel Hunter: IT’S NOT THE ART OF COMBAT, IT’S F*CKING KARATE! -- At Gabriel’s outburst, Yakahama recoils, a look of some shock across his face. He steps backward, protectively holding his hands out in front of him protectively. -- Yakahama: How DARE you insult the art of combat, glasshoppa! I swear I kill you for this! -- Unwisely, Gabriel responds with venom. -- Gabriel Hunter: Then, motherf*cker, I challenge you to a pure, 100% karate contest! … Even though I know jack sh*t about this crap, but anyway … Yakahama: Don’t make me laugh glasshoppa, I cwush you like a bug! Gabriel Hunter: In 30 minutes then? Oh, and I’d learn to speak some proper English if I were you. -- Without pausing for a reply from his “mentor,” Gabriel rushes out of the room, leaving a fuming Yakahama standing on the mat; a sick grin comes across his face, as we fade black. -- 30 Minutes Later -- Our scene resumes inside the same room that we saw just moments ago, although this time, the room seems to be full of Japanese warriors, all noticeably younger than Yakahama. The warriors surround the outside of the mat, all dressed in identical jet-black silk robes, waiting the inevitable karate contest between Hunter and Yakahama. Suddenly, to a huge ruckus of cheers, Yakahama steps out from the back, dressed in the same attire as last time. With a confident look across his face, Yakahama strolls out onto the mat, amidst chants of “MASTAH!” from the crowd. The reaction suddenly turns sour, as Gabriel jumps out from behind the beaded-door frame, immediately adopting a posture that Bruce Lee would be synonymous with. Gabriel chops the air a few times, looking around at those around him – obviously, he has no clue what he’s doing. The only noticeable difference, is a head-band around his forehead. Gabriel jumps into the centre of the mat, as the ruckus calms down. With one foot balanced off the ground, two hands held protectively, and a look on his face that would make even the sternest of men laugh, Gabriel begins the war of words, complete with terrible Japanese B-Movie lip synching. -- Gabriel Hunter: So Yakahama, we meet again, the question is, which one of us will survive!? Yakahama: You petty wrestlah fool! I will crush you under my shoe, testament to thah bug that you are! Gabriel Hunter: Don’t bet on it Mandarin! I’ve eaten fish bigger than you! Yakahama: Enough of this verbawl diarrhoea! Let the games begin! Gabriel Hunter: BAH~! -- Before Yakahama can do anything, Hunter launches through the air aiming a kick towards Yakahama’s had. Sadly, he misses, as Yakahama doges out of the way, laughing at Hunter’s poor attempt. -- Yakahama: Hahaha … You still have a lot to learn … Now let me show you how it done! -- Yakahama slowly approaches Hunter, who adopts his goofy stance again. Gabriel keeps his eyes focused on Yakahama, but is unable to block a well-placed sharp kick to the sternum. Gabriel is winded, as he bends over clutching his stomach. Yakahama capitalises on this, with a powerful throw to Gabriel. Yakahama stands over his fallen opponent, as the crowd around him laugh. -- Gabriel Hunter: SH*T! You can fairly kick ass for an old dude! Yakahama: You ain’t seen nothin’ yet, glasshoppa! -- Yakahama grabs Hunter’s wrists, pulling him to rise feet, before falling backwards, and throwing Hunter over with his legs. Both men are quick to get up, but Hunter is caught with a roundhouse kick to the face, which surprisingly, doesn’t knock him down. Yakahama nails the throw he taught Hunter on his opponent, as the near-defeated Scotsman slumps down on the ground at the corner of the mat. The crowd, by now, are chanting Yakahama’s name, as he grabs Hunter by the throat, showing amazing strength, and throws him up against the pillar in what could be described as an illegal move. Hunter struggles to break Yakahama’s grip. -- Gabriel Hunter: Ok, you can let me down now … I was just messing, you know? Nothing personal … Why not just end this and go out for a drink, huh? -- Yakahama again laughs in Hunter’s face. -- Yakahama: Not a chance, glasshoppa! You are helpless to stop my wuthless assault! There is nothing within your powah that you can do to defeat me … Why not just submit? You are FINISHED! Gabriel Hunter: F*CK YOU! -- BOINK~! Gabriel nails Yakahama straight in the eyeballs, with a well placed finger poke of death. Yakahama clutches his eyes in pain, as Gabriel gets down from the pillar, and nails Yakahama with a bulldog of all moves. The crowd are now booing the hell out of Gabriel. -- Gabriel Hunter: OH YEAH! WHO THE MAN BABY! -- In the crowd, we focus on a muttering between two man. -- Japanese Guy 1: That cheating! He do wrestlah move on Mastah! Japanese Guy 2: Yah! He must be disqualified! Japanese Guy 1: HEI! -- Meanwhile, Gabriel pulls Yakahama to his feet, before throwing his head under his arm pit, and hoisting him up with a hanging vertical suplex. Gabriel then sends Yakahama crushing down on the mat with a tombstone piledriver. Gabriel springs to his feet, performing a perfect rip-off Ric Flair strut. -- Gabriel Hunter: PERFECT ENDING ON YOUR ASS! BOOYAH! -- As Gabriel celebrates, all of the Japanese warriors that occupied the side area of the mat, rush to the centre, and attend to their fallen master, who is obviously knocked out, Gabriel punches the air a few times, before looking right into the camera’s lens. -- Gabriel Hunter: Spider, Jesus, Newsome … Beware … For I am expert Karate Master! -- As Gabriel punches the air one more time, he looks around, a few of the warriors have started to realise that they want revenge on Hunter. -- Gabriel Hunter: Oh sh*t … OH F*CKING SH*T! … Why do I ALWAYS end up running from EVERYONE!? -- Gabriel shakes his head, hastily looking for a way to escape. He notices the paper wall right next to him, and without hesitation, dives right through it. The paper and fragments of wood fall down before him, startling an attractive Asian woman on the other side, who drops the plates that she was carrying. The shatter into 1000 tiny pieces on the floor below, but Hunter doesn’t care, as he dives through the next paper wall, the warriors starting to give pursuit. The next wall, however, is wood, and Hunter clasps a hand on a door, throwing himself into a dark office room, locking the door behind him. Immediately, the constant knockings from the other side can be heard, as Gabriel starts to panic. He notices a window on the other side of the room, below him lies a busy and bustling street right in the heart of Chinatown. Gabriel pulls the window open, looking down below, before sighing. -- Gabriel Hunter: Im in Chinatown … I just beat the sh*t out of thair “Master” … As soon as they found out, I’m screwed! -- Suddenly, the door behind him bursts open, as the Japanese guys flood in. -- Japanese Guy 1: THAT’S HIM! YOU WILL PAY FOR WHAT YOU DID TO OWAH MASTAH! Gabriel Hunter: Damn … I haven’t seen that many chinks since Rush Hour came out! WARGH! -- Without hesitation, Hunter launches his massive body out of the window, safely landing on the ground below, as the scene fades out. Once again, Hunter’s leisure and relaxation exploits have blown up in his face. Will he EVER get any peace!? --
