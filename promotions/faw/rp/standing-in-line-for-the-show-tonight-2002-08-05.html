Standing in line, for the show tonight...

5 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Xander Entitled: Standing in line, for the show tonight... Written: 8/5/02 Location: A small park, somewhere [The scene opens in a small park. You see Xander standing on a bridge overlooking a very small riverbed, that seems all but dried up] 'Crazy days' Xander starts on his usual path. 'Bomber attacking me, me defending my title left and right, Foamy and his general weirdness. I'm not sure what to make of it all. Could drive a normal man insane, and an insane man to crimes of passion. I just beat one of the tougher guys I've faced in FAW in the Highwayman. Took down Chris Anstex. Now, this coming Wednesday I have to face the infamous Skipster. And he's also going after my belt. Who isn't these days? Huh? 'Whether it's Foamy, Highwayman, Skipster, Shifty, Apocalypse, or Bomber himself. I will take on anyone they throw at me. So far, I've come out on top of each and every challenge.' He says sounding prouder, and prouder as he goes on. 'I have one of the best records in FAW... I'm the current International champion... and the only Quintuple Champion to ever hit the scene... although should have been a Sextuple champion. That will also change though. I got my due shot at the world title.. .as long as Bomber can hold onto it. I wonder if he'll need my help. I know if Phoenix wins it, he'll puss out and not honour the match given to me by Bomber... Decisions, decisions.' [Xander starts to move off of the bridge as the cameras view shifts to a shot of the water below. As the camera turns back in Xander's direction, Xander is already turned around ready to continue] 'So Skippy' he continues. 'What makes you think you're worthy of my belt.. or even a shot at it? Sure, you've been things before.. but what have you done lately? I don't remember seeing you in the Diabolical Day Tournament... or, at least past the first round if you were there at all.... maybe you aren't worth it... or, maybe you were just scared. Scared that if you couldn't win, then everyone would make fun of you.. make you the laughing stock of FAW. Is that what it was? You scared? Too scared to take that risk. I think we all know if I was scared or not. Diabolical Day would have been glorious, if it weren't for those stupid assed clown... or, is it stupid ass-clowns? Either way. Sooner or later DCV will have to come to terms with they sucktitude, and I'll be more then happy to help them discover it... just like I'll show you Wednesday Skip... get ready baby, cause I feel like there'll be a drop in the ratings in your future.' [Xander turns and walks off]
