You better watch out! Xing Furiously Addictive Wrestling Roleplay #124 Date: 27th december Location: California Sea side Beautiful scenary here in California by the beach sweat our asses off in this boiling burning sun not one breeze with in miles. Months and months of training and years of sweat and hell yeah a lot of blood has drove him, Xing to this very fed FAW a brilliant fed at that we have followed Xing here in a local gym to see how he prepares for such big clashes and how he go into a fight the better man. We won’t disturb during his hard work but I will report what he is doing his on the push bars now pulling and stretching above the bar reaching for the ceiling pull up after pull up is strain and more strain trying to get as fit as possible. Now he is climbing down from the bars wiping his sweat and odder off of his head. Approaching two humongous logs of trees approximately 30 kilos each obviously a hard weight to lift but Xing does it with a breeze slinging it on his shoulders and jogging on the spot with penetrating at his shoulders shredding the skin of Xing he takes a roar out loud and chucks them like a baby across the gym. Now he is grabbing me by my throat and taking the mic. out of my hands and reports a rather weird gesture “I am all about choices and the sounds of the voices that run through and out of my mind But I can not be ignored. You may think I’m wack maybe a few brain cells I lack. But tell Lee and Big V to watch there back because their planet is under attack. He then walks with my microphone outside with me dragging along much to my dismay with the floor scratching at my head following behind Xing who is walking up to a house on the street opposite the gym he knocks on a door and asks for a Mr. Lee Smith but they said he knocked on the next door and next and so on with me behind listening to No! No! No! On every single door with out fail. He stops for a minute thinking and then drops me in sheer delight he runs off into a casino with joy in his eyes obviously cracked something thinking he could be gambling or something like that. Running through isle-to-isle no sign of Lee or Big V suddenly anger shook Xing that made Xing turn into a rage destroying everything in his way like a mental man then the owner calls up the nearby mental institution lucky for him Xing did not hear the call as Xing thought for about five more minutes to himself (He is not very bright) came up with another idea he could be in other casinos and walks out only to be stopped by the mental institution cops. That took no time in arresting Xing and taking him away locking him up in his very own cell for night so he could calm. I t looks like everyone is feeling the wrath of Xing these days even Xing himself is feeling it so just a friendly ow my neck still hurts sorry just a friendly warning stay out opf his way or it won’t be pretty.
