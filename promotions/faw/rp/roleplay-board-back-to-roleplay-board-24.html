Apocalypse — FAW Roleplay, August 2002

August 2002

Furiously Addictive Wrestling Roleplay Board Roleplay Board | Back to Roleplay Board Apocalypse Entitled: A time for talking but soon action Written: Location: Apocalypse's house (......A man with nothing to lose goes into a match with both to legends in that ring. What could happen in a instant? Anything in your wildest dreams could happen. A man could get hurt, a man could win a championship, and a man could lose a life long dream. Anything that makes for good showing could happen. But lets see who all in the world of living could get what they want. HaHa Klown could get respect from both guys in that match. He could pin the champion Bomber and then somehow get respect and a match for the gold that he dreams of having again. British Bomber could show why he is the man to beat in this horror match. He could show everyone why he is the man that has no fear and why he is the mat to beat for all the gold in the federation. And then there is Apocalypse. The odd ball of this all. The man that needs nothing, the man that dreams of it all, and the man that wants it all. The man that knows how good he is and then man that will go to nothing to win that world title. Why could happen when these three men step in that ring for a thriple threat non title match up.....) {The scene open with Apocalypse driving up to his local home as he comes to a stop. Red and black 2002 Dodge Ram pick up with a double due sound system. The driver side door opens and out steps the man that will take on both legend in the game on Distortion. Wearing a pair of white shorts and a old shirt that reads, “J2K, it is coming for you” as he shuts the door behind him. He looks around as a little white german puddle comes running up to him as he leans down to pet it. That was a mistake as a women about five eight and one hundred thirty pounds came walking up behind him. She had on a hot looking hot pink dress that went to her knees but very sexy looking. Apocalypse got up as he turned around and saw the women stairing.} Apocalypse: Can I help you miss? {The women didn’t do anything as she just grabbed her dog and started to walk away. Apocalypse shock his head as he started to walk for the front door. As he got there passing the trees that he had planted about a few years ago, he saw a car in the corner of his eye pull up in front of his house. He stopped as he turned aorund to see who it was. yes it was that very person as the camera man came out with his camera in hand.} Apocalypse: God you guys come at very bad times you know. Camera man: Well that is true but we where sent here to talk to you. {Apocalypse motioned for them to come on up to the pourch as he grabbed a seat from the other side. Pulling it across the wood he put it right near the door as if he need to run in he could. The camera man put the camera on roll as he told the interviewer to starts with the questions.} Interviewer: Well hello Mr. Apocalypse. Apocalypse: Well this is great. Just start with the interview because I am about to get ready to go into town and talk with some fans. Interviewer: Well my first question would be, how do you think you will favor in your triple threat match against both HaHa Klown and the British Bomber? Apocalypse: Well this two men have made this federation. Fopr me to be in the same ring let alone the same match up with them is an honor on my part. The fans may holler for Bomber because he is both a legend and a champion but deep down I know it will all change. You see Apocalypse knows he will get his change at that title around the waist of Bomber. I haven’t got it because he thinks I am not ready. Well if he will remember a few weeks ago I beat his ass from post to post and then if it wasn’t for DCVthen I would be getting that world title match soon. So you see Klown I get to beat you down in that ring for screwing me over. You see this will not be a wrestling match for me. No, this will be a respect match for me because I will beat it into you for taking what my mom wanted me to get. You see, not only when you ran in on the match you took the dream but you took the dream off of my mom. Now you must pay for what you did. Klown, I hope you know that this is personal and not business because you will die, me losing the match or not because it will take your littlepansy friends JLC and Spawn to stop me. I promise this son and I always bring them to life. {The Interviewer looked down as he started to laugh a little from what Apocalypse was talking.} Apocalypse: What are you laughing about? You think a young star like me can’t take out a legend like Klown? You see this will be a way to show you how great I am. Now roll with the next question. Interviewer: Well you 9th Wonder and Xander have had some problems of late. How do you feel about this? Apocalypse: How do I feel? Wonder is a great fighter. The cage match was one of my hardest matches I have ever had to wrestler or brawl in. He took my butt to the limit and to places that I did know I could go. I have a new found respect for Wonder but for Xander, well me and him will get in that ring one way or the other. You see, I never got to get him back when he took that chair to my head in the cage match. What was he thinking? Hitting the greatest thing in the hardcore division. Did he get hit to much when he was a kid because if he thinks that getting at me will make him as high as I am then he is crazy. The only thing it will do is get him and ass whooping in that ring. One day he will get it and I promise to all the fans that I will give them what they want. {Apocalypse takes his right arm as he grabs his drink from inside the door. taking a long hard drink he sits it down beside his chair as he looks at the man across asking the question.} Interviewer: Well on August 13 you go one on one with Chris Storm in an interfed match. How do you fair in that match? Apocalypse: Why is this even coming up? That match is for there people. But if you want some info then I will give it to you. On August 13 I do face Storm in a match but not a normal match. A strap match is what we will fight in. Two of the biggest men in there federation going one on one with each other. He talked some crap and then challenged me to a match. It was that simple to the degree. I don’t back down from a good fight and I wasn’t going to start with this no body. So just like Mort when we faced each other. I will get the One, two, three in the middle of the ring. The I will be two and zero in the interfed match’s. Now that will be all on that subject. Interviewer: Well do you have any comments on you and Summerslughter? Apocalypse: Well what is there to say. As of right now I am in no match. The match I want is taken by JLC. Mr. loser himself. But then again like i said I will get my shot one day. When Bomber gets off his scared ass to face me in that ring. But then again I could face both Xander and Wonder for the IN title but then again they are to scared to face me to. Why is this? Am I to great of a man to be fighting anymore? No it is because I am a legend already in this business. Now I must end this because it is 3:30 in the afternoon and i got to get ready to do something. So this means it is over. {With that the camera points the feed downward as the scene then fades to black.}
