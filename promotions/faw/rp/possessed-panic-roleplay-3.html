Possessed [Panic Roleplay 3]

January 4th 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Possessed [Panic Roleplay 3] 'Legendary' Lee Smith Furiously Addictive Wrestling Roleplay #141 Date: January 4th 2003 Location: Hotel in Tampa, Florida “Police, OPEN UP” the voice screamed, banging rapidly on the door. It was a sunny winters morning, the night before Panic. Lee Smith was set to face Rzarector at Panic - and reveal his new mentor to the world. But before that….”THIS IS YOUR LAST CHANCE!” the voice shouted as Lee rose from his bed and performed several forward rolls before making it to the door. He stood up and uncreased the folds in his shirt. He was about to go down to breakfast but in burst a steamy faced police officer, who lacked any respect for a man of Lees intelligence. “Who the hell are you?” ranted Lee at the officer who immediately flashed his ID. The wallet that contained his I.D. had several layers which were full of others Identification cards and it fell - revealing several aliases and pictures of his family. Lee observed at the pictures of the officer with several different wives. “Bigamy ay…that’s a new one” Lee chuckled as the Officer clumsily shoved the ID back in the wallet. “Listen up, I’m Constable Sharp of the Essex Police Department and I have travelled half way across the world to speak to you. Where were you…..on the night of 31st December 2002.…? “I’ve said it once and I’LL SAY it again - just who the hell do you think you are interrogating ME?!” Lee answers as the Officer develops a smug expression. “Answer the question or I’ll lock you up” he mutters under his breath. Lee responds within seconds for fear of missing Panic. “I was staying in a hotel around the area where Hersheys Chocolate is produced” Lee replies as the interrogation comes to a close. “What’s it to you?” “My informant was correct…Did you or did you not see a fat man, wearing a chequered cape and hot pants running with several boxes of chocolate, followed by a taller man wearing a pink suit?' the officer says with a straight face. Lee smiles. “I have to confess to you Officer. I….I…..did NOT see a fat man, wearing a chequered cape and hot pants running with several boxes of chocolate, followed by a taller man wearing a pink suit” Lee answers as the Constable looks curiously. “So….let me just recap….you did not see a fat man wearing a chequered cape and hot pants running with several boxes of chocolate, followed by a taller man wearing a pink suit?” “No….I did not see a fat man wearing a chequered cape and hot pants running with several boxes of chocolate, followed by a taller man wearing a pink suit” Lee answers, yawning in between words. “Do you know anyone at all who may have seen a fat man wearing a chequered cape and hot pants running with several boxes of chocolate, followed by a taller man wearing a pink suit?” The officer asks, pleading with Lee for a positive answer. “Do you know anyone at all who treats a Legend like myself with such contempt and udder rudeness - because I sure don’t!” Lee replies as the officer avoids the spit coming from his mouth. “That’s what us Essex Police are for lad….hang on a sec…Legend?” Constable Sharp replies, dropping the handcuffs that were not spotted by Lee. “My name is Lee Smith. ‘Legendary’ Lee Smith if you like.” Lee explains as a feeling of déjà vu creeps into his body. “Your Lee Smith? My son talks about you all the time! Can you sign this handkerchief?” Sharp replies. “I can” Sharp takes out an ancient handkerchief which has undergone surgery. “What is your sons name?” Lee asks. “Paul. Paul Sharp” “Obviously.” Lee responds. “So, you know anything about my career?” “Yes I hear a lot - all set to face Rzarector tomorrow?” Sharp asks. “I have nothing to prepare for. Rzarector is what us Hollywood guys like to call a loser. A fluke. A one off. Lightning can strike more than once - but in my time as a professional athlete I have learnt that luck never strikes more than once. At the Dome he was possessed. Possessed with luck. He was lucky to come second with myself…what an honour that must have been for him. But he was also lucky in the fact that he escaped with his life that fateful night. Luck never strikes more than once. I have a feeling he won’t be so lucky….” Lee lurches forward and looks deep into Sharps eyes. “Next time.” he quietly mutter in the ear of Sharp.
