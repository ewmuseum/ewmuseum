What actually is the 'distaster' around here? (PPV RP#2) Damien Ryan Furiously Addictive Wrestling Roleplay #41 Date: 10.24.02 Location: FAW Main Headquarters and offices (the screen opens up in front of a giant building, on the side of the building is a huge sign with the FAW logo on it. The building is in the heart of the city and all around the building and street are billboards advertising FAW's upcoming payperview event called 'Disaster Down Under', some of boards have pictures of people like Spawn, The Great One, and the British Bomber. The camera cuts to the front doors of the building and a taxi pulls up to the curb, we see FAW superstar, Damien Ryan get out of the taxi. He pays the the taxi driver and walks across the large sidewalk, full of people, towards the building. He's is everyday clothes, wearing jeans, and longsleeve shirt, boots, and his black is pulled back under his Toronto Maple Leafs cap. He walks to the front doors and walks into a lobby that has a good amount of people walking around in. He walks to the front desk where there is a woman receptionist sitting, she's on the phone and Ryan stands there waiting for her help. She seems to be on a phone call that seems to not at all important. She seems to be gossiping with a friend and Ryan tries to get her attention by clearing his throat. She finally looks up and gets off the phone) Receptionist: Hello, can I help you? Ryan: Yeah, can you tell me where the new head of staff meeting is today? Receptionist: Well its going to be in the Main Board Room #2, but only people who got the memo are supposed to be there, now if you have a memo and you can show me, I'll buzz you through the door into the building. Ryan: Actually no, I didn't get any memo, I found out today that this meeting was going, but I need to speak to staff today. Receptionist: Well, I cannot let you through right now, but the meeting gets over in a couple of hours, I'm sure if you can make a phone call then you can talk to one of them, but it's very unlikely that they will get back to you, considering this is probably the busiest time right now with the while new main staff change, and getting ready for a major event. Now, I can set up an appointment for you to meet with someone, everything is booked in the next three weeks, so I think I can fit you in the second week of November, does that sound good? Ryan: Are you shitting me lady? Just let me in the meeting, they know who I am. Receptionist: I'm sure they do, sir. But I cannot let you in there. Ryan: Lady, open in the door. Receptionist: Sir, if you don't refrain I'm going to ask for security. (Ryan walks away from the desk in disgust and annoyance and walks over to the locked door and begins to pull at it.) Receptionist: Sir, you have to buzzed into that door, sir, I'm going to have to call security! (Ryan pulls the door hard enough to break the buzz lock and he walks into the building, he walks down the corridor and turns left through a door and walks up a few flight of stairs. Once he reaches the floor he wants he get on another corridor to a hallway of board rooms, he reaches the door that says Main Staff Board Room #2, he looks in the side windows and see men in women all dressed up around a square table confrencing. Ryan opens the door and meeting stops and everybody looks at him when he enters. We can see a lot of familiar faces, and FAW President Neil Peters is at the front of the room giving some sort of Powerpoint presentation. Looks like on the screen has some new layouts for some sort of new FAW building. Ryan continues to walk around the table) Neil Peters: Uhmm, Damien...we are kind of in the middle of a meeting here. Damien Ryan: Oh, just act as if I wasn't here.... (Ryan reaches the other side of the table and grabs James Harris who's sitting in a chair, and he pulls him right out of the chair and pushes him towards the door. We see Neil kind of smiling, and James is all pissed off) James: Ryan, what the hell are you doing. Ryan: (Still pushing James towards to the doorway) Come on, I need you for a minute. (Finally they reach the door and Ryan pushes James into the corridor, right before he goes to close the door, Ryan peeks his head in the room) Ryan: Keep working guys, keep us alive here, you provide a good show environment...I'll provide the ratings, alright? (Everyone in the room smiles and Ryan closes the door behind him. The camera cuts to the corridor where James and Damien are. We see James aggrivated, trying to unrinkle his jacket. He seems really agrivated and Ryan walks up to him.) James: Damien, you just can't pull me out of an important meeting like this! Ryan: (sarcastically) Oh really, James? James: Yes! (Ryan just stares at James, and James seems a little uneasy while Ryan's glaring blue eyes stare it him) James: Anyways, I obviously don't have a camera crew or anything around. (Ryan points to behind James, and James turns around noticing the cameraman, and the cameraman gives a little wave to James and James seems more frustrated. He grabs the microphone from the cameraman's hand) James: Good evening everbody, I'm James Harris, here at the FAW Main Offices, next to me is the current FAW World Hardcore Champion, the man to face The Duke at the anticipated FAW payperview spectacular, Disaster Down Under. Ryan: Wow, your good at that. James: You better believe it son. (Ryan laughs) James: Now Damien, why you here today, other than dragging random people from major board meetings? Ryan: Well, why don't you tell me James? Aren't you the one inside the conference room? What are they talking about in there? Will there be an FAW around long enough for me to reach my pinpoint goal, or is FAW going to be tossed in the bottom drawer stored away for a while? James: Well, I really cannot comment on that... Ryan: Bullshit you can't comment. You know whats going on, you know behind the scenes, you've interviewed every single damn wrestler in this company, and you're telling me you can't comment on anything? Jesus Christ, James. For a man who has been around as long as you, who can think up those sweet introductions, I'm dissapointed in you. You're supossed to be the man who spices up these interviews and you can't give a comment about only FAW primary concern right now? Hell, maybe I should give Melissa Farrow a call, maybe then atleast I can get a real interview... James: That whore don't know how to give an interview if her prositute job depended on it! Damien, why did you need to talk so badly that you came all the way here from Toronto, and pulled me out of this meeting. Ryan: Jesus, James, have you been watching my promos? If you'd open up your stupid mind of you, maybe you might catch on to the fact that I want to be number one in this organzization, that I want to be the man who gets the star roles in movies and is names the sexiest man on tv for People magazine. I want to beat every single man in this league to a bloody pulp, by beating them senseless the ole' Canadian way! And starting this Sunday, things change. James: You got the Duke. Ryan: Your right I got the Duke, I have the Duke cooked, and served on a platter in front of me, because I'm going to chew him up and spit him right back out. I mean, you'd think that a wrestler would be a little bit excited about a title shot, but still the Duke remains wordless. I don't know, maybe he lost his voice from sucking on one too many long objects. I guess all that matters is, is that The Duke is going to walk into Disaster Down Under ready for a game of ring around the rosie and he's not going to expect a Maple Leaf to fall from the sky and drill his sorry midieval ass into the ground. I'm sure all your fans are happy Duke that they have been sitting in front of the TV waiting for a word from you, they keep flipping back to PTC for the Duke interview, but the only Duke related thing they see is the Halloween shows on the family channel of little kids puking up their Halloween candy, because puke rhymes with Duke, doesn't it? That's pretty much all you really are to people as of late Duke, nothing but digestival waste that was ejected by the body because it shouldn't be there. Your like the wrestler who is not wanted here, so like the spew, your going to be ejected, by me, because I'm going to beat your ass so bad that your going to want to leave! James: Words, by the Hardcore Champ, well we'll see you this Sund- Ryan: Whoa Whoa Whoa, this interview isn't done yet! James: Damien, I need to get back in there......Jesus...Ok, Damien I have one last question for you! Ryan: Shoot. James: Whether, you win or not at Disaster Down Underm what are your plans for FAW after? Ryan: I've been putting a lot of thought into this James, but I honestly don't want to jump ahead in my game. But I'm definetly thinking about working my way up the rankings to face to higher ranking wrestlers and headline shows. I'm definetly ready to be booked in those matches that people expect to survive, those crazy ass matches that people dare to ask if it's real or not. Then theres the regular, old fashion matches...don't tempt me. James: Ok, Damien thanks a lot. Folks, your Hardcore champion, seeming ready for Disaster Down Under! (the cameraman signals that the camera is shut up and James gives him the mic back) James: OK, I need to get back in there, Ryan, call me next time will you? Ryan: Ok, James, go get us some money in there kid! (James ignores Ryan but he laughs as he goes into the conference room. Ryan walks down the corridor and walks down the staircase down back to lobby where we see some repairmen working on the buzz door that Ryan broke earlier. We also see the receptionist sitting at the front, shes on the phone again, again seeming like its a unimportant phone call, Ryan stops to look at her, she looks at him and doesnt even remember who he is. Ryan smiles and walks out of the building as the camera fades out)
