Persuading Comparisons (Panic Roleplay 1)

31 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Persuading Comparisons (Panic Roleplay 1) 'Legendary' Lee Smith Furiously Addictive Wrestling Roleplay #246 Date: 31/1/03 Location: Unknown We fade in from a commercial break with Lee Smith sitting down on a comfortable chair. His legs are crossed and he is wearing dark shades among other things. He is motionless momentarily as his leather trousers shine in the camera lens. Behind him is a plain ‘FAW’ backdrop, and he seems to be all alone in the room. “When Worlds Collide 2003.…” Lee says as we cut to a highlights package in black and white. It stops as Lee falls over the top rope. “That was the defining moment for me” he says, coolly removing his shades. They remain hanging on his finger. “Evil Intentions 2002.….runner up. When Worlds Collide 2003.…..Third Place. My podium is almost filled…the trophy cabinet waning away with weight. I can only look forward now…no time to dwell on the past.” he pauses and we cut to a highlights package of his past in FAW. “No time to dwell on the past…..My next mission….should I choose to accept it….is to take on Flash at Panic. A match made two weeks ago. Vendetta will be my third PPV since my return. Third time lucky? I am beginning to doubt my ability. You people out there? I know you love me, which is why I ask you this.” he stops and leans forward as his chequered shirt creases. “Urge me on. Shout my name from your seats. Get on your feet. Right now, I need you guys more than ever before.” he leans back and sighs…. “Flash….Mr Stuntman….FAWs New Recruit…everybody’s favourite loser. Call him what you like. Since I returned I have slowly adapted to the conditions. Just like an animal. And the solution to the conditions? Animal behaviour. And boy o’ boy it was needed at When Worlds Collide. I lasted that long only to fail at the penultimate hurdle. You have no idea what that feels like. Flash. I promised my fans I would triumph at When Worlds Collide….and I…..” Lee stops as his eyes begin to water. “Failed” he looks down at the ground and contemplates his speech before continuing. “Flash…me and you have lives which are only different at a second glance. You were brought up in Los Angeles…I was brought up in Hollywood. You hit the big time in movies as a stuntman. You…were…and almost still are…a movie star. I’ve only ever known one other moviestar. My Father. Some of my followers may know my mother worked for him when I was born, I was a secret love child. But here’s something not all my followers will know. My Father is dead. Gone. Forever. Eternal Death.” Lee stops as the watering of his eyes stops and he squints with his eyes becoming bloodshot. “My Father was….a b*st*rd. A selfish manipulative….seducing degrading….ba**ard. I’m glad he’s dead. But -- my heart feels empty without knowing I have had some revenge for his sins. And so….now…I finally get my chance to fill my empty heart with gold. I will never defeat my father for that is beyond someone of my power and dominance. But you Flash…you come a close second. This isn’t just a job anymore for me. This is my life. And if I do not triumph? God forsake me for I will be as bad as him…my father. But I shall triumph - just like a mountain climber I shall climb the mountain. I - like a mountain climber - will slip along the way. But - unlike a mountain climber - once I defeat the menace that is Flash I will teleport myself to within inches of the mountains peak” We slowly fade out with no sound as the FAW logo flashes on the screen momentarily.
