Eye of the Tiger!

7th December 2002

Eye of the Tiger! 'The Real Prodigy' Scott Costella Furiously Addictive Wrestling Roleplay #95 Date: 7th December 2002 Location: Fayetteville, North Carolina. The scene opens on the Crown Coliseum in Fayetteville, North Carolina. The camera switches to the inside, where in one of the many backstage corridors, Scott Costella is heading out of the building. 'It's just great... My first match since the upgrade to FAW and the start of the Fatal Aliances... and i get a fricking match with Spawn...' Scott stops in the middle of the corridor, perhaps thinking to himself. He shakes his head and carries on walking. 'How the hell can I compete with a former World Champion...? How can I compete with someone like Spawn... the Bookers must have suffered temporary insanity... I mean... It's like sending the lamb to the slaughter!' 'Heres me... Scott Costella... 6'5'' 240lbs... All I have over Spawn is the fact that I'm one foot taller than him... Other than that... I'm not gonna have a snow ball's chance in hell of beating his crazy ass... I mean he just doesn't want to beat me... He wants to destory me... and for the fun of it!... This is definately not good... I hope they have medics on standby...' Scott is nearing the exit, when Adam Hutton catches up to him, Adam Hutton: Scott... Can I just get a few words for the show tommorow? Scott Costella: If you must... Adam Hutton: How do you feel about facing Spawn? Scott Costella: How do you think I feel? It's Spawn... He'll rip of my neck and spit down my throat! Scott begins to walk away, when Adam tries to blurt out some confidence building words, Adam Hutton: But you've hung with the best of them... You can do it again... But it isn't enough... Scott keeps on walking til he reachs the exit into the underground carpark and heads to his custom Porche. He flings his bag into the back and gets in and drives off. He enters on to the busy main street and drives down, staring blankly ahead. He turns on his CD player, and 'Eye Of The Tiger' by Survivor blares out of the speakers. As the opening guitar sequence reaches his ears, a change stirs in him. His spirit begins to lift and he begins to feel for the thrill of the fight again... 'Perhaps I can beat Spawn, afterall... I am Scott Costella... 'The Real Prodigy'... I have as good a chance as anyone... Spawn may be a former World Champ... and the best in the business... But there is one thing I have... that he doesn't have... DESIRE!...' 'Where he just wants to beat people up for the fun of it... I want to earn my way to the top and become the best... And I know I can do it... Spawn will just be one of many stepping stones, in the rocky road to the top...' 'It's The Eye Of The Tiger, its the thrill of the fight... Rising up to the challenge of your life...' The spirit lifting song is in full swing and working its magic on Scott Costella. He is no longer, staring blankly ahead... There is that sparkle in his eyes, just like the one that was present in his first ever FAW match. 'I wouldn't even be here if it wasn't for Chris Thunder... He took a chance and helped me find my way into FAW... And now I'm here, I can't let it go to waste... I will probably loose tommorow... I will definately get my ass kicked all over that ring, tommorow... But there is one thing, Spawn will not brake... My spirit...' 'I may not be the best on the FAW roster... I may not ever be FAW World Champion... But I am gonna try my best to be it...' 'Spawn thinks I should try something new... Thinks I shouldn't even both walking to the ring... He says he wants me to make him care....' 'Yeah... i'll try something new.... I will walk to the ring... And I will make him care...!' 'I'll show him new... I'll show him a new me... a confident me... a me with courage... A me that will fight him... And i'll make him care... I'll make him care, when I'm kicking his ass in the cntre of the ring and Silence his ass! and pick up the 1..2..3...' 'I might not be 'The Man With ALL The Talent.... But I can do anything I want... When I put my mind to it...' The scene fades out as Scott's custon porche speeds down the street and pulls in to a fancy hotel car park. Perhaps Scott can beat Spawn... He thinks he has a good chance at it... Only time will tell!
