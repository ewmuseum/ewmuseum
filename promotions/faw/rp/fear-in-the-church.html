Fear in the Church

10/26/02
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Fear in the Church Rzarector Furiously Addictive Wrestling Roleplay #54 Date: 10/26/02 Location: In the Church Fear is something that strikes pleasure into the hearts of several people. To see someone die, to feel someones last breath in your hand as your choking there neck. God has mercy on everyone, well everyone but some that is. There comes a time every now and then when God realizes peoples sins, and will never forgive them. Such as Foamy and his Church, it is the antichrist, the fall of the FAW. (The screen opens to a church session going on. It is a huge church with statues of Jesus all around. Everyone is dressed tuxedos and the priest is up in the front yelling at the top of his lungs. Church ends at 10, the clock now reads 9:50. The preacher is finishing his sermon when the church doors kick open. There a fog rolls in, behind it a dark figure in a black hooded robe. The crowd is stunned as his green eyes span all through them. His fangs shoot out of his mouth like lightning from the sky. The crowd is in a daze. The priest is in shock. The figure walks to the front of the church, whispers to the preacher, and walks to the backroom. Now the preacher in full sweat, stops and says the sermon is over, everyone have a nice day, then he goes to the backroom. There Rzarector waits for him. The priest is shaking and all sweaty, Rzarector hisses out loud, the priest sits down and drinks water.) Rzarector: There is fear growing in the church. Priest: What kind of fear, the fallen one? (Rzarector looks up and hisses again as his fangs spread out onto the bottom of his mouth.) Rzarector: Fear like the one of the antichrist. God told us it would be coming, we steed warning, and continue with your sinful lifes. Yet now another has awoken, my future opponent. The same guy that mocked the church, and made a foolish thing called the Foamy Church. Priest: What is this church son? Rzarector: It is a church where they worhsip my opponent, The 9th Wonder of Foam, or Foamy. Priest: You know, I never sensed a time like this coming to the world, but you my son, it is in your hands. You have to win and destroy his soul. Use your godly abilities and ressurrect him to the lake of fire. You can do it, we all beleive in you. (Rzarector looks up at the priest as he is mesmorized by Rzarectors eyes at once. Rzarector gets up and walks out to the window where a figure of a cross is in the church yard.) Rzarector: You can beleive in me all you want, until that time comes. I may win, I may not, whatever happens I will not let Foamy out of my sights. I know his evil ways, I know how he is like. The fans seem to love his comedy, well this is comedy of a fool. Yet the 9th man relishes in the attention, like a kid smiling for a picture. Well Foamy is a kid, and that is it. Nothing more, he can talk all he wants, act how he wants to, but until I see something good out o fthis man, he will just be another soul burning in my mind. Why did God do this to me? (The priest shocked looks up.) Priest: Do what my son? The fact that he gave you a special ability to judge people on what they are, not what they appear to be? The fact that he gave you a second chance, from being the black demon master, to being the Black Priest. Rzarector, you changed your ways, you should have rotted in hell, but you didn't, and you owe God for that one. (Rzarector appears enraged and walks over to the priest and starts yelling.) Rzarector: I would have burned in hell????? NO! You will burn in hell, you think everything you do is perfect. I remember your past priest. I remember that pregnant girl you left when you were 16, I remember the fact that you killed your dog because you didn't like that. Noone knows that but me, now where will you be headed? God didn't give you a second chance, I blessed you. Priest: Calm down, come to your senses. (Rzarector sighs.) Rzarector: I'm sorry Priest, through God's eyes I blessed you. He blessed you, you just finally admitted to me your sins.
