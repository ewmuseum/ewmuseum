Destiny is a big thing with Xing (Panic #2) Xing Furiously Addictive Wrestling Roleplay #271 Date: 11th February Location: Alabama cemetery The screen opens in front of a cemetery. The environment is very spine chilling and eerie. A six-feet tall fence surrounds the entire square mile of the cemetery. Vines and rust engulf this grey metal fence. Inside the cemetery are small shades of dirt and grass, which the moonlight projects onto the camera. Wind echoes throughout the shadows of tombstones as coyotes are heard barking into the ground. Krik, Krik, Krik is the sound piercing your eardrum. The sound of twigs breaking on the raw and unholy ground. The moon begins to glisten upon Xing and then a shadowy figure appears before him Xing begins to back off in fear of the man who he can not point out the man holds his hand out to Xing, Xing gets up off of the ground in which the unveiled man put him there by his creepy powers. Xing stares into the mans eyes and keeping a little look at his hand in case he did something that would harm Xing, he then margined himself from the man asking, “Who the flaming heck are you? You scared the crap out of now if you are a mugger I would like to warn that you will be given all of my money that I own, which is a lot.” Xing begins to grovel on the floor begging at the feet of the man he thinks is a mugger the man begins to laugh and replies to Xing sounding like he knows him, “Now, Xing I thought you were supposed to be big man on campus but this, this is just pathetic honestly, Xing I know you from a big event in mine and your life’s I am Fred Harrington. Look Xing I mean you know harm I don’t need your money I am rich you see I know your wife from when well me and her got acquainted in bed after the dance. Yeah! The good old days you know we were voted as the most likely to become the best couple from outside our school in Houston you know she was a great actor she had a brilliant Canadian voice she used to use nearly all the time some people even got her fooled for a Canadian, Can you believe that?” Xing looks at the man in unbelievable anger and disappointment and then replied violently, “You dated her, you made love with her, you were in a big event in my life and yours which I don’t know but you will tell wont you, you better because I will hurt you, She was not Canadian but that is why her parents talked all Texan like, and you you’re American aren’t you boy you better start talking or you see this trusty hammer can do me wonders.” The man looks at Xing very worryingly into Xings eyes they made him talk because fire was burning his vision, “Gosh Xing you wont like this but, I was the one that ran over yours and my ex-wife why? Because I hated seeing you two so happy together so I thought that if I could kill you I thought well my problems would be over now Xing don’t take this the wrong way that was along time a go haven’t you done anything bad in your life?” Xing in utter disgust and sickness knowing he could have been killed not his wife and that the man before him killed his wife and could have killed himself the King of the world not Lee Smith Xing pulls out his hammer he was carrying in his pocket and swung it across the man’s head, his skull begins to bleed quickly then Xing began to cry standing over the body, the moving body Xing was shocked in how the guy got up after the shot to the head. The guy got up pointing, “You, you are a murderer wait till the police hear about his!” Xing scared from the threat of the police grabbed hold of him and said, “You are in no shape to go anywhere now come here look here is your ex-wife kneel down say your prayers” Xing then under his voice murmured “because it is the last thing do!” and smash the hammer across his head, Xing then began to dig the grave up when he heard the police sirens so Xing quickly took the man dropped him into the grave, “Stop in the name of the…” Where is he as soon as the cops got there Xing was nowhere to be seen so be ware Violator because you wont know where Xing is going to hurt this Sunday on Panic he is going to jump out from anywhere and I hope you are scared because Xing is resurrected from the past and is coming to get you Violator! Just like Fred and Mrs. Fred you will rot away from this wrestling lifestyle and shown the door into your destiny ‘Your Grave!’
