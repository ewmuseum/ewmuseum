Crimson, the true meaningi of a king, read Rzarector Furiously Addictive Wrestling Roleplay #21 Date: 10/18/02 Location: A village far away ~A king, is one who should rule a great nation under his wise advice. A king is not one who uses punishment and lies to summon a nations anger.~ (The screen opens to be black but with a Narrators voice in the background.) Narrator: People are blind to there mistakes. Several people are blind to there mistakes. Yet certain people, are not really blind at all, instead they brag about there mistakes. In real life these people are called a$$holes, but in after life they are called demons. The following story is going to be a true one about a king, and his several people who thought that they were the largest and greatest country. This country though lacked one thing, a lack of religion. Seldomely has a country ever done what this one has, yet, there king told them to. Now he is eternally damned. Attention beware. (The screen opens up to show a small village in the middle of a huge plain of grass. You hear horses running in the fields and children playing. The camera zooms more into the village to show dogs running around, butterflies flying, and crickets chirping. There is a huge fiest today, to celebrate the birth of a new child into this small village. The weather for the week is supposed to be very sunny, with a slight chance of rain, well thats what the astrologers said. Inside a hut, in the middle of the village, screams are heard. You zoom inside to see a woman, screaming in pain, as her legs are spread, and several people are around her. First, the head pops out, then half the body, and last the feet pop out. The woman slowly stops screaming as blood is everywhere. Everyone in the hut is smiling, as they cut the umbilical cord. The woman starts cheering as she turns around the upside down baby and holds it. There you see it, you wonder what? There is a huge scar on its back shaped in the form of a cross. Everyone cheers as this is the chosen baby. The main doctor, a dark figured man, with a long mustache, grabs the baby by the arms and carries it outside to a crowd of cheering people. There the fiest begins, yet there is a strange sense in the air. As a man, by the name of Edward the Mad Scientist senses it, along with his apprentice Joseph. There we go to there room.) Edward The Mad Scientist: You know Edward, it seems like a day this good is yet to end this good. There is somethign floating in the air that would make a man weep, that would make women fall to there knees, and make children feel the agonizing effect of death. What it is, I don't know. (Edward begins pacing around again.) Edward the Mad Scientist: Joseph, I've taught you how to look into the souls of people, to find there true future. Not something as everyone else would call crazy, but just see what they really want, and just tell them that is what will happen. The mind gets, what the midn wants. Remember that Joseph. (Joseph was a younger boy, about 27, and he still had the young look on his face. Blue eyes, blonde hair, and wearing a robe.) Joseph: I know master, I sense it to. I've heard of the wars, that our surrounding country has started, and this is a new holiday for us. I sense disaster, I know there will be disaster. Edward the Mad Scientist: Only if they could see it coming, but who are they to beleive us. (There Edward looks back out the window as it zooms back into the festivals.) Doctor: EVERYONE FIEST! THIS IS A GREAT HOLIDAY TO US!!! (As the doctor yells out over a crowd of cheers, you then zoom to the forest outside of this little happy village of about 100 people. There in the forest is 30 men on horseback, with swords, there leader a dark man in a black robe. He is yelling things to them! Saying that we must attack and we must attack now. The warriors all smile, wearing nothing but shirts and pants, they draw out there swords. The leader then tells them to go now! The warriors cheer as they all dash out into the village. From up high above in his toward Edward the Mad Scientist sees it all.) Edward the Mad Scientist: What is this? Who is that coming out of the forest, I knew it was to good to be true. (In astonishment.) Joseph: There coming? There coming now? What do we do? WHAT???? Edward the Mad Scientist: (Still shocked.) I don't know son, I don't know. Fate will rule, there is no end to it. (The camera then zooms back out to the once happy village, now all in fear as they see the warriors on horseback fleeing up through the forest towards them. The doctor yells out to everyone, 'EVERYONE INSIDE! KEEP THE CHILD SAFE!!!' Everyone starts fleeing as the warriors are about 1 mile from the village. The doctor then runs inside his hut, and comes out with a sword. He looks with fear in his eyes, as he knows his judgement day is coming. He prays to God as the warriors enter the village. He yells out some final words.) Doctor: I KNOW THE DEMONS WOULD SHOW!!!!!! (Right as he said that he pulled out his sword and stabbed one of the riders. The other warriors flew by him as he fell to the ground. There his head rolled off onto the dirt. Women are heard screaming throughout the village as warriors are pulling everyone, women, men, and children, from the huts and cutting off there heads. The riders on horseback then pull out torches and begin setting every hut on fire. The leader of the group, slowly starts into the village, his face cannot be seen. He stands there and watches the carnage, as his horses hooves are laying on the doctors body. Edward and Joseph are still watching.) Edward the mad Scientist: Evil has won, good has perished. There is no hope for us if they find that child. God knows they will. Joseph: Where did they take him? The newly born, will he survive? Edward the Mad Scientist: They took him to a dugout hill, right inside town, the entrance is covered up by a hill of hay, but if they burn the hay, then they have gotten the best of us. We will lose, one whole nation, all of God's people, will lose. Joseph: I want to go help! Edward the Mad Scientist: Time will come when we can help but for right now, we just stay safe! Trust me it is the best thing to do. Joseph: (in a sad face) Yes master. (Footsteps are heard coming up the towers steps, there Edward swallows hard as him and Joseph pull out there swords. They just sit and listen to the footsteps of that five story tower.) First story..... Second story..... Third story.... Fourth story.... Fifth story.... (There instead of the door getting knocked down, there was just a knock at the door. Joseph and Edward look suprised as they peep out the peephole to see who it is. They see people on foot, in blue uniforms. They open the door. There is another countrys army. They stand there in platemail armor, looking at Joseph and Edward. They ask them if they can use there lookout for there scouts. The two scientists agree as the guy goes over and looks at the carnage. From there he gives a hand shake to the east side of the forest. You hear a whistle go off, as 300 foot soldiers step out, and 200 more on horseback. They begin charging the small village as the evil warriors on horseback looked shocked. The village now almost nearly burnt, everyone dead, but the mother and her child hid safe in the hill. The leader of the demon army yells out to his men, 'WHERE IS THE CHILD??' The men look dazed as the good army is now 1 mile from the village. The leader yells, fight til we die. The warriors pull out one sword in one hand, and a dagger int he other. The 500 man army enters the village, as the 30 on horseback start fighting. The evil army is at once defeated, but not without killing atleast 100 of the good army. The leader of the demons got away. The village was, what you could call it saved, just because the holy child was there. This holy child was going to grow up to be a great prophet. There the camera zooms to a land far away. There you see a pure black castle with fire all around. The leader of the army runs into the castle and up to the king.) King:What news have you brought me? Was the child destroyed? The evil child? Demon Leader: Well, everyone was cheering for it, are you sure it was evil? King: What happened to the child? Demon Leader: Nothin but we did manage to butcher everyone else before the other army came in and killed the troops I took with me. King: So you didn't succeed??? (The King goes into a violent rage and calls in his guards. He points towards the leader and immediately they start stabbing the leader. There he dies, now the king looking worried, goes to his famous, woman.) King: I don't know what to do, the child is evil, there is no such prophet other than God. Woman: Well they said that the child was the prophet of God, everyone thinks your evil. King: I am? I'm trying to help. Woman: Well maybe you shouldn't, killing is not the answer. King:What do I do? I've seen you ressurrect people before. Ressurrect this person, please....the old King. he will tell me. Woman: As you wish. (The woman begins chanting as a king appears before them. He looks not a day over 40 and is in perfect condition. Except for a blood scar on his neck. This king was killed in battle.) King: What do I do King Illshneer? Old King: I've came to give you one word from God himself. You are doomed to hell, you and the demon woman. (The Old king vanishes as the other King begins to cry. He weeps and weeps, as the other owman just walks away.) King: Why, why me? (The demon army was defeated, and the child saved, to eventually become the pope. There he made several thousand people transfer to christianity. The Demon King is probably rotting in hell. The screen then goes black as Rzarector appears.) Rzarector: You see Crimson, you call yourself a King, and even though Kings think they are doing the right thing, they sometimes are not. You are looking at Gods Prophet right here, I see what you do. I will tell you now Crimson, you are doomed. Relive from your sins, and show love to other people, or let me cast you itno the lake of fire with all the other damned souls. It's your choice, now make the connection. One more word, yes if you win, you can get the title shot. (The screen goes blank.)
