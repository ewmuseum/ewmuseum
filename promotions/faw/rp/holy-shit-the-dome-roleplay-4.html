Holy Shit - The Dome Roleplay 4

Holy Shit - The Dome Roleplay 4 'Legendary' Lee Smith Furiously Addictive Wrestling Roleplay #116 Date: 21st December 2002 Location: FAW Arena The car pulled up outside the arena as a sea of fans bashed their hands against the window. Lee Smith sat in the limousine with his coat over his head as he covered himself. The noise filtered through into the limo creating a quagmire of noise. “Get a move on” Lee screams at the driver whose expression doesn’t change. Several minutes later they pull up in the underground carpark. Lee gets out of the car and stretches. “Take my bags and put them in my locker room” Lee orders his chauffeur who doesn’t look too pleased. Lee walks into the arena and up the staircase. He sees the production crew who are all anxious about the success of the event. “Hey Lee” a few of them say as Lee walks past oblivious to his surroundings. He sees the red curtain draped across the entrance and pulls it across. He removes his jacket and reveals a blue buttoned up shirt. Lee first looks at the seating arrangements of the arena. The production crew seem to be placing Event Programmes on each seat. His eyes then turn to the other side of the arena, more seats…same old story. Suddenly something catches his eye. “H…o….l…..y……ssss……HIT!” he shrieks and stares at the end of the ramp. At the end of the ramp is a huge dome which stretches all the way across/along the ringside circle. It doesn’t take Lee long to work out that that is the infamous Dome. The camera closes in on Lee who has sweat pouring down his cheeks. His eyelids seem slightly swollen and watery. He unbuttons the top button on his shirt and pulls the curtain back again. He exit’s the arena and falls flat against a wall. He gasps and struggles to regain his composure before peeping through the curtain once again. The Living Legend walks down the ramp which has had no exposure to the public so far. Lee reaches out as he gets to the end of the ramp and touches the steel on the dome. His hand caresses its bumpy surface. He hears a member of the production team shout at him from across the arena. Smith pulls back. “Please don’t touch that again, get out of here” the man says. “Give me a minute” Lee replies, with his eyes fixed on The Dome. After 30 seconds he spits onto his palm. He rubs the spit on the steel of The Dome. The man escorted him out of the way and backstage. Lee sat against the wall again….He had left his mark…just like he would on Sunday. His chauffeur approaches him as he daydreams. “Sir, your locker room inspection has been completed” the chauffeur says, puzzled by Mr Smiths uncharacteristic. Lee gets up and walks with the chauffeur to the locker room. They both sit down on a bench. Lee sighs; a sentiment echoed by his chauffeur. “You saw it didn’t you” the chauffeur claims, leaning back against the locker. “Yes. Yes I did” Lee says with his head in his hands. “What did you think of it?” the chauffeur asks. Lee sits upright and goes into deep thinking. “It was…..it was….perfect.” Lee replies, still in an isolated stare. “One word summary?” the chauffeur questions…he seems to have had experience at this kind of counselling. “Beautiful”
