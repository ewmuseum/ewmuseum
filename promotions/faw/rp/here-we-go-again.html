Here we go again. The Omega Fro Furiously Addictive Wrestling Roleplay #138 Date: 1-3-02 Location: L.A, California Today Robert Jaxom (The Omega Fro) has been wandering around Los Angeles, his home city. He wondered around town for a while and then decided that he would go and get a hot chocolate from Starbucks™. He only hopes that this Starbucks™ will be better than some he has been to n the past. He walks into the Starbucks and as usual with Starbucks™ there is a line. Robert quietly waits in line so that his turn might be able to come soon enough. As he is waiting in line Rob notices the diversities of the people in the line. There are young and old people, black and white. “I wonder if they have the same problems as I do going on in their lives,” thinks to himself. As Robert is looking around waiting in line, a pimply faced teenager looks at him. The teen takes another look at Robert and then his face brights up into a look of excitement. After a couple of minutes of just staring and Rob not noticing the teen gets up the nerve to speak. Teen- Um sir, would you by any chance happen to be The Omega Fro, the wrestler? Robert Jaxom- Yeah that would be me. The teen gets even more excited from this response. Teen- You are really The Omega Fro. You aren’t joking with me or anything like that, because if you were it would be really cruel and mean. Robert Jaxom- I’m him. I am The Omega Fro in the flesh. The teen just can’t hide his excitement. Teen- you are like the coolest guy ever and you are here in this Starbucks with me talking to me, of all the people you could be talking to you are talking to me. This is like a dream come true. Robert Jaxom- Well you were the one who started the conversation. Teen- Yeah but still, you are The Omega Fro you are like one of the best-known wrestlers among the FAW. Robert Jaxom- Yes I’ve heard that before. Though it doesn’t make any difference overall to me whether I’m the best-known man in my apartment or in the world of wrestling. Robert Jaxom just stands there dumb founded as the teen looks at him in awe. The teen turns to his mom who is standing near not paying any attention do this. Teen- Mom, this guy is The Omega Fro from the wrestling show I like to watch and I’m talking to him. Mom- That’s nice dear. Teen- But mom! This guy is The Omega Fro. Mom- Yes dear you told me already. Just before the teen can speak again Robert intervenes. Robert Jaxom- Hey kid can you do me a favor and not call me 'Robert Jaxom' in public. Teen- Um sure, but then what should I call you? Robert Jaxom- Well you could call me Rob, like most people now do. Teen- Ok, I could do that Mr. Fro, I mean Mr. Rob. Robert Jaxom- That’s better. Robert notices that it is his turn to order and he steps up to the counter. Robert Jaxom- I’d like a hot chocolate please. Starbucks™ Employee- What kind would you like? Robert Jaxom- A hot chocolate. Starbucks™ Employee- What kind The employee says this with some annoyance. Robert Jaxom- I would like a hot chocolate. Robert is now starting to get a bit annoyed. Starbucks™ Employee- Ok sir. I understand that, but what kind. Robert Jaxom- What do you mean what kind? I want hot chocolate. You know it’s a drink that tastes like chocolate and it is hot. Starbucks™ Employee- There is more than one kind sir. There is German chocolate, regular chocolate, Swiss chocolate, French chocolate, Milk chocolate. The list goes on forever. Robert has a bewildered look on his face. Robert Jaxom- I just want some hot chocolate. Give me something. Starbucks™ Employee- I guess I could give you regular chocolate. Would that be all right? Robert Jaxom- That would be just fine. Robert says this with an air of sarcasm and annoyance. Starbucks™ Employee- So you want finesse flavored chocolate? Robert Jaxom looks as if he is about to blow. Robert Jaxom- No! I just want some hot chocolate. Robert Jaxom starts thinking to himself. He knows that there is only one true hot chocolate. Why can’t this kid just give him some hot chocolate? That is all he is asking for, just some simple hot chocolate. Starbucks™ Employee- So you don’t want finesse flavored hot chocolate? Robert Jaxom- No I do not. I just want some regular hot chocolate. Is that too much to ask? Starbucks™ Employee- No sir. I’ll have it in just a moment. Robert Jaxom- Thank you. As these two have been “discussing” what hot chocolate he should get, other in the back of the line have been getting disgruntled. Finally a man decides to speak about it. Man- Will you hurry it up! Several others agree with the man. Man- I mean we are back here waiting for our turn. All we want is something to drink and you have been up there for 5 minutes now. Robert is more then a bit annoyed with this. Robert Jaxom- Well I would hurry up but this dimwit here won’t hurry up and get what I asked for. Man- Who cares make him hurry up anyways. Robert Jaxom- I would but I feel like having my hot chocolate and not getting kicked out of this place! Robert is really staring to look annoyed now. The teen that had just previously been talking tom him decides that he will speak. Teen- Will you people please shut your mouths! Do you know who this guy is? He is The Omega Fro! You people should show some respect! Robert Jaxom- *whispering* Hey listen kid I told you don’t mention me by that name like that, and really it’s no big thing. Teen- What do you mean it’s no big thing! You are The Omega Fro! Robert Jaxom- *whispering* I’m only The Omega Fro when I’m at the events. Kid, you have got to understand that I live a life outside of wrestling. Out here in the real world I am Robert Jaxom. When I’m in wrestling events I’m The Omega Fro, the wrestler. The teen seems a bit hurt and a bit depressed at this. Teen- But, but, You are The Omega Fro. Robert Jaxom- *whispering*That may very well be true. However, that is only when I am wrestler. Out in the real world I am myself. I am Robert Jaxom the person. While Robert and the teen have been talking the now angry “mob” is getting angrier and the same man begins to speak once again. Man- Hey! Will you hurry it up already. Some of us have jobs and such to get back to! Robert Jaxom- Oh shut up already. Can you not see that I am having an intelligent conversation here? Rob is really starting to get angry himself. Finally the Starbucks™ Employee comes back with his hot chocolate. Starbucks™ Employee- Um sir here is your hot chocolate. That will be $2.35 please. Robert reaches into his pocket and grabs his wallet, even though he thinks that the price is outrageous he decides that he should get out of there while he can keep his cool. He pull out two one dollar bills and puts them on the counter and then reaches into his pocket and retrieves a quarter and a dime. He then grabs the hot chocolate and begins whispering into the teen’s ear. Robert Jaxom- *whispering* Listen kid, I’m going to hand this hot chocolate to you. When I leave the store I want you to throw this all over that idiot guy who can’t keep his mouth shut. I’d do it myself but I’ve got a match at Panic, and I can’t participate if I’m in jail. Teen- I don’t know. I could get into some trouble for doing that. Robert Jaxom- *whispering* True you could, but I’ll get you a couple of tickets to wrestling. Teen- Will they be to the FAW. It’s by far my favorite wrestling federation of all. I mean you do have a huge match coming up do you not? Robert Jaxom- Yea, me and that idiot Great One against Xander and The British Bomber. How did you find out? Teen- The Internet, where else. So you that you two can beat them. Robert Jaxom- Without trying. Teen- That’s awesome. You haven’t been doing so good as of late so I’ve been kind of disappointed and everything. I know a lot of my friends who have began to make fun of me because I have you picked as my favorite wrestler. Robert Jaxom- Well we’ll win this one kid, and if those other kids saying anything about you having me as your favorite wrestler just give them a good kick in the ass. Teen- My pleasure. So I’m guessing we have a deal about the tickets then. Robert Jaxom- Fine then, it's a deal. Teen-Oh hell yeah I’ll do it. Robert Jaxom- Ok, just give me your number and I’ll call you with the information. Robert remains calm and steady, as the “mob” steadily gets angrier. The teen gets a napkin and writes his phone number down. Robert then hands the teen his hot chocolate and proceeds to walk out of the store accompanied by cheers from the “mob”. After a few moments after he gets out of the store, Rob hears a scream and recognizes it as the stupid teen that couldn’t keep his mouth shut. Robert smiles to himself and thinks “oops” as he continues walking down the walkway in downtown Los Angeles thinking about his renewed place in the FAW. Hoping he can be as successful as in he has been in the past.
