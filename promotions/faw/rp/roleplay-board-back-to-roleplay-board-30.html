Furiously Addictive Wrestling Roleplay Board Roleplay Board | Back to Roleplay Board Spike Entitled: Out of the darkness . . . I stand Written: August, 11, 2002 Location: Through me After the fall of xCw there was a man who’s’ name couldn’t be heard. He crept somewhere beyond eyesight, and looked upon our unseeing eyes. He hid among the rest of the people, and was complete inconspicuous when seen. He wasn’t seen nor heard. Among people of FAW this man now creeps out of the shadows into the artificial sunlight from the light inside the stadium which holds the FAW event. SpiKe . . . Among the rest now he stands strong, his head once again held high. His fans stand behind him like he never left. Sheltered by a team he stands among them, and runs his mouth. * * * * * * * * * * Among the rest I stand, sheltered by many loved by more. I love my fans, and I draw my strength from their screaming bodies, and swaying movemoents. They love to see me wrestle, kick ass. I rely on them . . . I love them From their chants I rise above . . Take charge . . . I stand above my opponent, take his head, and bash it with a SpiKeDriVer! They love it, I love them for it. * * * * * * * * * * The streets of Ottawa Canada, my hometown. My home The thought warms my mind, and my soul. Where I was raised, cared for, loved, and learned to wrestle. Learned to do what I now do for a living. Hardcore The thought still chills me to the bone. Even from my xCw days being a 3-time Hardcore Champion beating the best of the best. But the beating that Jeff Harris, and Wolverine gave me hurt my hardcore senses. It hurt me. Being hardcore may be the hardest thing to do. It seems easy, but to really hit someone with a chair is hard. It fuckin’ hurts! To mix it with a ladder match is complete brutality. SpaWn, and old foe. From my days both in xCw, and in FAW he still stays, really amazing. Now the FAW Hardcore Champion he stands above all hardcore comers standing true to his title belt. But no matter how brutal it becomes, no matter who wins, I will have put up the best fight I could have. The best man can, and will win. I just have to pray to be living afterwards . . . C’est Fin
