Show Stopper Xing Furiously Addictive Wrestling Roleplay #102 Date: 7th dcember Location: Train station in Japan, Tokyo “Swish goes the passing by train and again as another goes by then one stops then out comes the man the one and only he may be a liar but who cares its Xing hundreds of camera crews surround him asking why he lied but answer from Xing except wait till I come to my senses and they did they backed off him and let Xing walk on.” “We follow Xing as he walks on by the way to people who are going to be watching this I am Sting Bozeman from the number #1 show ‘Kai’en tai get hai’ I am following Xing to find out the reason why he did what he did. He is now going in to a room, which to me is unknown, I can hear murmuring. I am now going under cover as a cleaner so you will have to stay outside but I will tell you what happens when I get back I will tell you all.” Sting turns off the camera and walks in the room with a broom that he picked up from the floor. He comes back behind Xing looking shocked picks up his camera and walks on behind Xing and some person. Sting turns on his camera as Xing walks into another room but know a Press Office and says “I’m back after witnessing the unthinkable Xing and yes Lee Smith together laughing at a video that was Lee Smiths interview in a dark room which had just a TV and a table with chairs in. Xing is in the press office right now and I’m going back as a number #1 announcer for a #1 show.” Xing walks out of the press office after about 10mins to find a camera shoved in his face by Sting who asks a bunch of questions like “Why are you together with Lee? Why did you lie? Why were you in the press office?” and many more but Xing just said, “Read tomorrows paper and you’ll see!” Read all about it 'Xing lied with a friend as Lee Smith'shouted a bus boy on his bike. Monday night live show Kai’en Tai get Hai “Now people I’m the show owner and presenter Ivana Tinkle. Now today we will be asking the question ‘Did Xing lie or did Lee Smith just pay the papers to say what the did?’ Well ladies and gentlemen we have just the person to answer that and he is the one and only that every show wants it’s Xing.” The crowd roar Xing enters the threshold of Kai’ en Tai with his pal that is Lee Smith. “Now Xing I guess the first question that I got to ask is why are you with your biggest rival Lee Smith there?” asks the lady Ivana Tinkle when Xing replies with “He may look like Lee Smith but don’t dis him like that now who would want to look like that tramp any way. Ivana this is my pal Gusher now we both wanted something real bad and would do anything to get it now I wanted to murder Lee Smith and he Gusher wanted to get stardom and so we thought to do a scam, sorry this is your next question isn’t? Any way we thought about a scam when we came up with what we did. You might think it is sick but to all you Xingthings out there I am the freak of all freaks now I had a desire to kill Lee you could say I have turned psychotic or something but if you don’t like it then tough you will have to stay out of my way and the freaks way because I am on a war path with Lee and if your watching this keep your eye out for me because I’m coming at you I maybe coming from up there down here from the left or the right.” Xing stops talking while turning his head around in a circle ending the show with his threat to Lee Smith.
