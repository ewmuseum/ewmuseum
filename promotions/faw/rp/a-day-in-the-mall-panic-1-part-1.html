A day in the mall (Panic 1 part 1) Profusion Furiously Addictive Wrestling Roleplay #128 Date: 30-12-2002 Location: A Mall “ummm this Chicken burger is the shit” I said to myself while eating a McChicken burger from the local McDonalds restaurant. “Too bad all these jackasses are sitting around me” I said to myself softly while staring around at each person sitting in the restaurant. “Can I have your autograph?” A young boy of about twelve years said to me holding out a piece of paper in front of me for me to sign. “Kid, it’ll cost you ten bucks to get an autograph from me. Do you have ten bucks?” I said with a sniggering face. “No, I’m only twelve, please can I have one?” The kid said with a sad face, a face that was full of anticipation was wiped off his face and now had one filled with sorrow. “Scram kid, I ain’t giving you nuttin” I said with a growling voice. The kid ran off to his mum and some man who was extremely well built. The kid pointed towards me and the man who was sitting beside him rose from his chair and stood at about 6 feet 7 inches tall. The man walked towards me keeping his eye locked on my body. I took a bite of my chicken burger and a gulp of my Coke and sat there thinking what was about to unfold. “Whats this asshole going to do to me. I’m a pro wrestler I can take him out.” I thought to myself in a desperate manner as the man continued to walk towards me and was only about twenty paces away. I tried to control my heavy panting and tried to keep it at a minimum. My pores began to seep with sweat. The sweat ran down my forehead soaking my face. “On the count of three get up and run” I said to myself an gushingly. I counted to three in my head and pushed the chair back and tried to run but the big man cut me off and shoved me back down into my seat. “Burger sir?” I said while nervously holding my chicken burger up to the mans big hand. The man bent down and put his head near mine. “You harassing my boy, little man?” The man said with a fouling face as his eyes roamed along my face. “Me harassing someone? Surely you have got the wrong person.” I said while trying to escape round the other side of the seat. “Well I heard you wont sign my boy’s piece of paper. And I don’t like smart asses who disrespect my family, you understand.” The man said while lifting one of my fries and putting it in his mouth chewing it with his mouth open. “All I asked for was ten bucks for an autograph, well you see I am a professional wrestler and I don’t give anything away for free. You see my situation good sir” I said cunningly thinking I had got myself out of this one. “I completely understand. I understand that you’re a sorry son of a bitch who is about to his ass kicked!” The man said clenching his fist. “Ohh shit” I said to myself as I dived across the table knocking all my food on the ground. Luckily I landed on my feet and ran for it out the door of the shop. I ran through the mall but the big man was following behind me. I was a lot more agile and was loosing him but he was still pursuing me. I saw a crowd outside some music shop so I ran in there while watching the man rung right past me. “Thank you Lord” I said to myself while wiping the sweat of my forehead. I looked around at what everyone was queuing at. It was some rock singer. I decided to join the queue and see what the hype was. My eyes glided around the mall and saw a poster, which looked really interesting over beside the Adult shop. “ Shit, maybe that is a sign advertising for a male porn star, I’ll make a fortune. Screw wrestling this is the big time for me” I moved out of the queue and walked swiftly gliding past the other customers. I walked over to the sign and a dull expression came across my face. “Damn just my luck” I said to myself, as the sign was not for what I was hoping for but it read “Karate lessons, only $2.50 an hour.” “Karate lessons. They could come in handy since I am wrestling some ninja guy this Sunday. Actually these lessons will come in very handy as they will allow me to win the Continental Championship for sure.” I said to myself with a smile on my face. I counted the money in my hand and I had $3 in loose change. I walked over to the elevator and waited for it to open. It opened and I entered it. I pressed level one and I saw the man whose son I had annoyed looking at me. He pointed at me as I pressed the button vigorously. He was about ten paces away from the elevator as it closed. “Ahh ya dozy fuck” I shouted down the elevator as a feel of relief came across my whole body. I got to the top of the elevator and exited through the doors as I got to my level. I looked around for a minute trying to see where the karate shop was. I found it but I also noticed a sale in the local Ice Hockey shop. “Damn I hate these decisions. What to do, what to do. On one hand I could have a continental title but on the other I could have a new pair of skates. Wait no I couldn’t I only have three dollars. Screw this I’m away for a karate lesson.” I said while walking towards the door of the karate store. I knocked on the door and some hot Chinese chick opened the door and my mouth dropped at the tight, white martial arts outfit she was wearing. “ How may I help you sir?” The beautiful lady said towards me. “I’m here for a karate lesson, I think” I said nervously while checking out her legs. “Yes sir, we have two more spaces left. Would you like to join us now?” She said in a soft, gentle voice. “Damn right I would. Do you have a boyfriend?” I said as it slipped through my mouth. “Excuse me sir” The lady said with a confused look. “ I said would you………ummmm……like a new friend, yeah that’s it” I said with a deserved look on my face. “ I can always do with a new friend.” The woman said invitingly. “ Just follow me”. She said as she turned round and walked into the shop. I checked out her hips and her rounded ass. “ Maybe this won’t be too bad after all I said to myself” While rubbing my chin checking out her ass once more. “Maybe I will call myself Profusion San from now on. But for now it’s party time” I said to myself as I followed her into the shop with the door closing behind me.
