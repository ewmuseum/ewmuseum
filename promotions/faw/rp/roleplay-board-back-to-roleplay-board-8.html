Spawn — FAW Roleplay, August 2002

August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Furiously Addictive Wrestling Roleplay Board Roleplay Board | Back to Roleplay Board Spawn Entitled: DCV THE CARNAGE BEGINS Written: 8/3/02 Location: The scene slowly opens up into one of the most common settings the FAW has seen...the apartment of Spawn. And there Spawn is again, sitting on the couch that he has made famous. Sitting at his feet is his massive beast of a dog, chewing once again on some stuffing that has popped out of the couch. Spawn stares blankly ahead, directly at the grimy wall. The only sound that can be heard is the wet sounds of droll slapping against the black lips of the dog beneath Spawn. Several seconds elapse of this wet silence before Spawn leans forward, running his hand through his own hair, a smug grin growing over his lips. Spawn shakes his head as his grin turns into a hearty laugh, throwing himself back into the couch. The dog looks up inquisitively at Spawn, who is now trying to contain his laughter. Several more seconds pass before Spawn leans down towards his dog and grabs hold of his face, smushing the skin forward in his hands. Spawn: He’s calling ME out? Spawn leans back, the laughter and enjoyment suddenly void. Spawn: HES calling ME out... Suddenly, Spawn’s face turns very angry. His eyes close slightly and his jaw becomes firmly clenched. Spawn: Who the FUCK does he think he is? WHO THE FUCK DOES HE THINK HES TALKING TO?! Where does he get the right to say anything to me? This kid has dwelled in the lower tier of wrestlers for his whole career, and he thinks that he has the right to say a single fucking word to ANYBODY, let alone me? So that’s how we’re going to play it? In the fucking back? Good...I’m the master at that fucking game... Spawn cracks his neck and looks towards the camera. He motions for the man behind the camera to move closer and the man immediately and obediently follows. After a few feet Spawn holds his hand up, stopping the cameraman dead in his tracks. He leans forward, propping his elbows onto his knees. Spawn: Listen you little FUCK. You better not start shit with people that you know nothing about. You see, I have done nothing but gone easy on you and your fuck buddies. I’ve kept myself quiet, and even refrained myself during shows. But now...NOW you have the BALLS to call ME out? Oh this is about tog et really fun. So allow me to start this whole game of ours by simply destroying everything you had to say to the DCV. First, let me address the issue of you calling YOURSELF a legend in the game of teams. Now, Bombitch, I’m not too sure if you can read or not, so why don't you have your master, Thunder, read the title histories for you. Because in case you don’t know this, I STARTED and MADE this fucking place what it is today you ignorant little shit! You think you’re a legend in wrestling because you can win a belt that you have to rely on someone else to win? See, I won the world title twice on my OWN. I won the hardcore title on my fucking OWN. And if you saw the tag team title match, you would realize that I carried my partner, Crimson, and won the tag team titles on my OWN. You on the other hand feel compelled to take it in the ass by absolutely anybody that can win you something. Well, when I get done with you and your 2 boyfriends, you can have all day and all night to make intricate plans of passion to each other. Until then, you’re stuck facing the meanest sonuvabitch that has ever stepped foot into the ring. And yes, it might be true that I, the meanest sonuvabitch to ever step foot into the ring, has never made a derogatory comment to you despite the fact that you have “stood” in my way for so long. Well Bomber, that reason is pretty fucking simple. You see, you’re like one of those kids on the block that are always getting pushed around by other kids. You make feeble attempts to stand up for yourself, but nothing well enough to actually be considered a contender. And then people start feeling bad for you. “Oh, give the retarded guy a title shot. It’ll make his day!” or the ever popular “Go easy on him! We all know that he can’t ACTUALLY hurt anybody!” Shit Bomber, look at yourself. Think about all the shit that has been handed to you in the FAW. Do you actually believe that you could accomplish any of that on your own? Hell, even your stable buddies only let you step into the ring because they don’t want to hear your retarded rambling once the match is over. Shit, nobody wants to listen to a retard pissed off. Firstly they make no sense. And secondly...well, all you really need to know is the firstly. Shit, do you know what the best part of being me is? It’s when I have never fought, talked, or thought about you in my life, yet you already know so much about me. You know I’m the Hell Master. You know my repertoire. See Bomber, you might call yourself a legend, but only legends have things like that happening to them. Hell, I don't know what belts you own, who your friends are, what your finisher is. But you know all about me, and it’s not because you study films. It’s because I’m a fucking playground legend. So don’t kid yourself into actually believing that you’re a legend in the team rank. Kid, you’re not even my opening act. I save that for low lives and losers like FRO... Spawn leans back once again, pressing his shoulders firmly against the top of the couch. The dog, which has remained quiet for this whole time, stands and hops onto the couch beside him. After several seconds of balancing itself on the cushions, it finally flops down with a heavy thud. Spawn simply stares amused at the massive animal before returning his attention to the camera. Spawn: Oh, now here comes the real fun. We have Bomber’s boyfriends, Thunder and Prodigy. God, this fucking fight is going to be ridiculous. Thunder, you’re just like your friend Bomber. You have this high and mighty impression of yourself and your accomplishments in this fucking business, when the truth of the matter is that you’re just another midcarder getting paid HALF of what I make. For some reason or another, you actually believe that you are the GOD of wrestling. Well, tell me oh wise God, why don’t you have MY hardcore title over YOUR shoulder? Why have you never had a shot at THIS title? Hell, why can’t you even get close enough to my title to fucking smell the blood sweat and tears that REAL Gods like ME have drenched this title WITH? Explain that one to me oh God of wrestling! Oh, I know! I guess that mystery is just like all the other mysteries that God leaves us with. What’s your answer? Because God...oh, excuse me...because YOU will it so? How’s THIS for an answer shit head. How bout you have never gotten close enough to this title or the wrestlers who have owned this title because you wouldn’t be able to last three seconds in the fucking ring with them? How bout the best you could ever wish to achieve is a title that was made famous by a guy like Neo? How bout you AINT shit, you WERENT shit, and you will NEVER be shit in the FAW because you don’t have the talent to actually do something besides play second act to people like Skippy and FRO? Two men who both had to leave the game of wrestling because it was too HARD. Oh, but skippy’s back! skippy is here to rescue you from the big and bad DCV! That still doesn’t take away from the fact that you have never accomplished anything in your career as a FAW wrestler. Kid yourself all you want. Have your whore call you a God, have your parents call you a God, have your stable buddies bend over and call you a God. Because the only person that actually believes that you are a God of wrestling is YOU. You are a walking joke. You talk yourself up and the only thing people can do is laugh because you talk a game that you don’t have. People look at your past and wonder to themselves, “why the fuck is this guy talking? He’s done absolute shit! I wonder if he believes his own gimmick?” Well, I know one thing for sure Thunder...none of the DCV believe it. Everyone of us, and everyone else that steps into that ring knows that in order to be a God or a legend of wrestling that you must PROVE yourself. I think it’s fair to see that I have proven who and what I am. Unfortunately, it’s pussies like you that step into MY ring and use the word “legend” loosely. It started with your anus licking British friend Bomber. The little guys started screaming legend because there was nobody like ME to dethrone the fuck. And now you and your friend Bomber and Prodigy truly believe that you MEAN something to this corporation? Kid, please...we would be better off without you two and your “defected from British” friend. So pack your shit and get the fuck out...because we don’t need you. Spawn: Now, in parting, I have one question that I want to be addressed. Bomber, where the fuck did you go? You felt it necessary to destroy a plan that I had PERSONALLY setup, and then you disappear back into the sands of Mexico? That’s right you little shit, I’m calling you out. I think people get the wrong idea about us two. People think that you’re a “legend” because of how long you held my title for. But the thing is Bomber...I was never around to dethrone you and take it back. I had other issues to attend to. And it figures that the second I get back into the groove of things that you run away like a scolded Dogg. Well I want you to make an appearance and I want you to stick your fucking nose out. That way I have a little bit of fuel for the fire when I burn down your trio come Sunday night. Because I have Prodigy and Thunder running their mouths to me. Heh, shit...I’m basically running on fumes. Come out and say something Bomber...or else... Fuck it...You Have Been Warned... Spawn leans forward and smiles into the camera. After a brief moment, he shoves the camera backwards. A Crash is heard and then everything fades to black.
