Kryptonite — FAW Roleplay, August 2002

August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Furiously Addictive Wrestling Roleplay Board Roleplay Board | Back to Roleplay Board Kryptonite Entitled: General Knowledge Written: 8-3-02 Location: Los Angeles, CA ((((Your regularly scheduled program is interrupted by a screen full of static, and the sounds of Linkin Park's 'Points of Authority'. The static clears, opening across Los Angeles by night. The moon overhead is framed by skyscrapers, illuminated by neon corporate logos and lights from the offices within. The camera draws closer to one tower, constructed of black glass and steel, devoid of any signage identifying it. Through the window on the top floor, a vast room can be seen. Along one wall are several monitors, depicting news programs and sporting events, as well as different areas of the city. A woman, wearing black leather pants and a black leather trenchcoat, sits in a chair in front of the monitors, observing the different goings on. Another man sits in a huge leather chair behind her. Swinging around towards the camera, the man puffs on a cigar. Standing up, he begins to pace the room, his twisted and tangled black hair tucked underneath a top hat, tattoos covering his arms. Clamping the cigar in his teeth, he puts his hands behind his back, and begins to speak while pacing, looking out the window at the city below.)))) Man: Civilization. Today, the term itself is a misnomer. What's civilized about the world outside your window? Throughout history, there's been great ones. The Roman Empire, Ancient Egypt, The Mayans, The Spaniards, The United Kingdom, The Soviet Union, and of course, the United States of America. Look around. Is there anything civilized about this country anymore? Fuck no. People are getting beat up, raped or killed, and it's not going to stop. Throughout history, every civilization, no matter how perfect it seemed to be, has had it's share of violence. More importantly, violence has always been a part of entertainment. Packaged and sold, bought and paid for. Thing is, the shit that they're tryin' to sell you nowadays? Well that just ain't good enough. ::turns to the woman:: Val, bring me up a display. ((((The woman nods, punching at the keyboard, shutting off several of the monitors, save a few, which are displaying football, hockey, wrestling and boxing. The man turns to the camera and puffs on his cigar.)))) Man: Look at this shit. You call this violence? You get penalized for hitting a kicker in football, in hockey they hand you a stick and expect you not to hit anyone with it. Boxing is fixed and is riddled with pussies who are too afraid to really uncork on each other. They dance around, hug a lot and occasionally throw a punch. Wrestling has been sanitized. You have idiots like The Rock, and hitting someone with a trashcan lid is supposedly the epitome of hardcore. Give me a fuckin' break. Now that Rhodes guy had the right idea, but his place shit out. So it appears to me that you people need something different, something better. Your bloodlust needs to be satiated and that's where I come in. ((((The man smiles, twirling his cigar in his fingers, as he sits back down in the chair, swinging it around and leaning into the camera.)))) Man: I'm Kryptonite and that lovely lady over there is my assistant, Valentine. Let's face facts. The world is fucked up and it ain't gettin' better. You slave at your 9 to 5's, you sit in two hours of traffic and society actually expects you to not want to fuck people up. So you look for an outlet and what options to they give you? Football? The WWE? That doesn't satisfy shit. So now, I'm giving you something different. Furiously Addictive. Sure, it sounds like a hundred other things that came before it, but we're gonna do things a little different. Im gonna give you the whole nine at Distortion Vincent. Im gonna give you your technical wrestler, the counter wrestler, all that shit. I want to show you the sick side though. The one who will hit each other with anything and everything and will spill pints of blood, all so you don't have to. Got a set of balls? Then you will come to that ring. Man or woman, everyone get's some when they step into that squared circle. All you have to do is walk to that ring with your A game in your hand. Not much to ask, is it? Your government has failed you and it's up to you Vincent to pick up the the DCV's dirty work. Your working with a wounded animal, and there is no telling what I will do. I will refuse to be censored or legislated. This is my night, and you will feel my fear::smiles and puffs his cigar:: Which I'm sure is making you ask yourself 'How am I seeing this then?' ::smiles and motions to the wall of monitors:: This is where I show myself. FAW Television, the EET Network. No one wants to see you against me in that ring, but fuck 'em. The Dark Carnival started it, and you will be a stepping stone. You stand in my way Vincent and there is no escaping my path. Your life lies in my hands, and there is only one way to surVive. I will give you your fix of violence and determination. People over look you, but I won't. I'll give you what you really want and what you really need and that is a purified ass kicking. You wanna see me live? That can be arranged. Carefully coded details will be placed in some of our transmissions and the smart motherfuckers out there will be able to figure out where to come see Kryptonite at his best. You are walking into a forgoten pit of darkness and there is no return. I am a man of my word and as god as my witness i will walk out of Distortion a winner.....and a SurVivor!! All you have to do is come to the ring, that's all I ask. ::smiles:: We're gonna do things your way, the way YOU want. If this city, if this world is gonna burn, it may as well be us who sparks the motherfucker up. So what I'm doing right now is sending out an open invitation. Vincent, I will give you the first shot. I will give you the first chance at taking out The Violator. You are only hours away from your fate, and it lies within me. Get the connection Vincent. You've got only hours, and Valentine will post your face around the world, when your time comes. If you wanna be the best, you gotta fight for your life. You ain't gonna find that glory elsewhere. If you wanna bleed, I am your man. My time is your time. This is my life, this is my glory. I will take out the baby-face of the FAW. I've fallen, but I have gotten up and I will remain on my feet until I can stand no more. Vincent, I hold no grudges and no anger against you, but you have crossed into a path of a man with only revenge on his mind and there is no way out!!. Bring everything you have. Fight for your life because I will plan to take it. I guarantee you that you'll never be the same. Stay tuned because This shit is about to get really.....really......good. Feel the Fear!! ((((The man places the cigar back in his mark, blowing out thick clouds of smoke, as the camera changes to static, before displaying the Violator logo.))))
