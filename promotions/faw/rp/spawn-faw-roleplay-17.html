Spawn — FAW Roleplay #17

captured 8 August 2003

SpAwN--The Hell Master \~~~~~~~(opening scene)font color="silver"> The scene Spawns house were we see Spawn watching Tv all alone in the living room, he is still hurting from the main event match he just had a few days ago. Then all of a sudden the phone rings Spawn gets up to go answer it. Spawn: Hello who is this. Prayer: Hey Spawn is you old buddy Prayer remember me. Spawn: Oh yes I remember you. Back in the days when we were a hot tag team, so how you been? Prayer: I have been good and you? Spawn: I have been doing pretty good. Good news is I am the new FAW world champion it was a battle but in the end I came out on top of the world. Prayer: Man I hear that I knew you had it in you to become one of the best. So what now? Who is next on your hit list? Spawn: It's a cage match for this Thursday at panic my opponent is some guy name the crimson lord. Prayer: I heard of the Crimson Lord he use to wrestle in other federations am I right? Spawn: Yes you are right. But I really don’t know him all to well. Prayer: So what you got in store for him? Spawn: Oh I got a lot of plan for his ass, he thinks just because I am hurt that he is going to get the best of me well he is wrong and I intend to prove him wrong this Thursday at panic. You know his biggest problem is he got an identity crisis on his hand. Motherfucker doesn’t know his left from his right and inside the steel cage I’m going to tech him a lesson he will never forget. Prayer: Let’s meet somewhere and talk about this crimson clover guy some more hell I’ll even help you with this punk. Spawn: No way man I don’t need help, but we can meet. When do you want to meet and hang out like the good old days? Prayer: How about today I’m not doing anything right now and you? Spawn: I’m not doing anything right now or later on so we can meet and talk some more like the good old days. Prayer: Good how about we meet at the pizza place in Washington st. Spawn: Harry O’s you go there a lot? Prayer: Sometimes I do they cook good food in there so you game we are going to meet? Spawn: Cool I’ll be there in about an hour so I will see you then. Spawn and Prayer meet up at the pizza place they get their order and begin to speak but then they are joined by a strange looking man who does not identify himself and he begins to speak to them. Man: Are you Spawn the wrestler? Spawn: Yes I am who wants to know? Man: That is not important what I need you to do is come with me I have a very important person that I need you to see? Spawn: You want me to go now can it wait till later? Man: No it can’t it is very important because in the long run it will benefit you too. Spawn: Ok but I’m bringing my friend along for the ride just in case. If you can’t deal with that then I’m not going. It’s a deal? Man: That’s fine with me now let us go time is short. Spawn, Prayer and the man get in their car and head out to what looks like a warehouse. They get off the car and go inside the place. Inside they meet another man and they begin to speak. Joel the boss: Hello and welcome this is not really my place and excuse me for this short notice meeting, but don’t worry it will be to your liking. I believe in introduction agree well then my name is Joel The boss and this is my city. Now what I want to ask you is very simple I do not ask for favor usually I’m the one that does the favors, but in this case is a different story now I need a favor. Spawn: what kind of favor are we talking about here? Joel the boss: It’s simple (Joel shows a picture of Crimson lord to Spawn) do you know this guy? Spawn: Yes I do what about him? Joel the boss: Well then you know he is a wrestler and the son of a bitch owes me a lot of money form the time that he began his wrestling career. I made him what he is today and that disgraceful asso does not even thank me for it. Spawn: Ok so what’s the scoop? What’s your favor, and how can I take care of it? Joel the boss: What I want you to do is put him out of wrestling for good plain and simple. Can you do this for me? Spawn: Yes I can but I won’t. Joel the boss: Excuse me! Come again. Spawn: You heard me I won’t do it for you. You do it your self. I do not work for no one, never have and never will. Joel the boss: Boy I guess you don’t know who you are messing with do you. This is no wrestling show this is for real boy when I hurt someone they stay hurt for good there is no return and it seems to me your looking to cross that path. Is it boy? Spawn: No it’s not what I’m trying to do but I’m not scared either all I’m saying is a simple no whether you take it or not. At that moment some more guys come out from one of the backroom and surround Spawn and Prayer, It seem that they are about to hurt Spawn and Prayer but before anything was about to happen Joel the boss begins to speak again. Joel the boss: Now, now, now, now, this is getting out of hand. Prayer: Oh yeah well tell your boys to back off, or it will get out of hand. Joel the boss: Sure sure I will we are all friends here this is all just a misunderstanding on my boys part they are sorry and so am I. This is a simple solution that can be fixed very easy. Agree? Spawn: No I don’t agree you need to learn to take the answer no once in a while it wouldn’t hurt your career or your pride. Joel the boss: Ok then tell me why you wouldn’t do it and will end it at that. You have my words. Spawn: You see I fight this Crimson guy Thursday inside a steel cage, now to me he hasn’t done anything stupid. That is to me in my eyes. So I look at him as another opponent trying to get ahead by defeating me especially now more than ever that I am the world champion so I give him all the respect in the world for that. The problem for him is that I am not going to let that happen, he won’t be able to defeat me I will defeat him that is guarantee and in doing so I will teach this rookie a lesson he will never forget but I will do it for me not for anyone else. And my intentions is to wrestle him not to hurt him and put him out of wrestling because I am not about that. I am all about the competition and the drive and motivation to go on to become a legend among legends. Wrestling is my life and I won’t let you or anybody else take it from no matter what. That is the reason why I will not do this for you. Joel the boss: Ok good enough, I admire a man with balls and you mister got some balls, so I will keep my words and this meeting is over you guys are free to go. But let me remind you this is between us no one else needs to know for example like the authorities, see what I’m saying. So if they come after me, I go after you fair? Spawn: Fair enough. We are out of here. Joel the boss: One more thing when you fight Crinson lord you tell him for me that Joel the Boss wants what’s his and I will get it one way or the other he will pay you make sure you tell him that? Spawn: That is simple I can do that without no problem. Now I am out of here. joel the boss: Listen up (calling one of his boy over to him) I want you to follow him and give them an example. gang: Ok boss. The gang follow Spawn and prayer all the way till the end of the street as Spawn and Prayer were about to turn the corner the gang car gets in front of their car and blocks them, they then get out of the car and ask Spawn and Prayer to get out of the car. gang: Get out of the fucking car right now! Spawn: What's the deal here I though me and your boss were on the level? gang: You are fuckin wrong we are hear to give you a fair warning our boss does not like taking no for an answer. Spawn: Well then what are you guys going to bout? Prayer: yes start something we are waiting? The fight begins, and Prayer and Spawn takes the upper hand. Prayer grabs one of the guys and pushes him to the ground. He sees the other guy coming at him and kicks him with a super kick on the chin. At the same time Spawn is shown grabbing one of the mafia guy and bodyslamming him on top of the car. The guy goes right through the car window he is left there out cold. The fourth guy jumps Spawn from behind but Spawn ducks and he flys by hitting his head on the car door Spawn then picks him up and power bombs him on top of the car. The scene switches to Prayer who has already down one guy and is finishing the other guy with a choke slam just like that the fights end with the bad guys all lying on the floor and Spawn and Prayer looking down at the chaos they have just created. Spawn then begin to speak to them. Spawn: Now listen and listen well, who do you think you guys are fucking with. Take this message to your boss and tell him the next time to keep his word of honor if he is a man of honor. Tell him that if he comes after me, and my friend here, that he will pay dearly with his life. Now you go and tell him this and make sure he gets the message right. The guys all get up still groggy from the beating they have just received. They get into the car and drive off. The scene shows Spawn and Prayer just staring at the car leaving they then look at each other and begin to speak. Spawn: Just like good old time huh. Prayer: Oh yes just like the good old days, maybe we should think about getting together when my contract expires in my fed. Spawn: I'll be waiting for the moment when we shall rule the tag team scene again. Spawn and Prayer get into the car and take off as the scene fades to black.
