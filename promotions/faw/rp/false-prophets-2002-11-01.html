False Prophets

1 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
False Prophets Rzarector Furiously Addictive Wrestling Roleplay #65 Date: 11/01/02 Location: In the middle of nowhere (The screen opens to show pure black, a narrators voice is heard in the background.) Narrator: Through many years, and many centuries several prophets have came out of nowhere and claimed to be the holy one. (The screen starts showing pictures of Hitler, Pope, and Jesse Jackson.) Narrator: Yet they all had a downfall. They made several people beleive them, only to deceive them in the end. How many people were to be killed from these people? Sometimes I sit back and think to myself of all the tragedies and it just tears me apart. There is no greater pain then seeing your fellow humans being tortured to death by a false prophet. Yet this Sunday comes even more false prophets, for the #1 contendership for the hardcore title match is coming up. No where is Rzarector. (The screen stops showing pictures of several false prophets and comes on to show Rzarector, sitting on a chair in his small grey mansion on the outskirts of a small town in Ohio. The house is all black and has weird cross formations for windows. The only light in the room where Rzarector is at is a small beam of light glowing out through a painting of jesus's eyes in the walls. Rzarector hisses.) Rzarector: Tommorrow, I embark on yet another journey. I journey to which I have never traveled before. Without the knowledge only means one thing, the better it will be. I have a shot at that hardcore title, and me being tag champions, I will be both of them. Then the Dark Carnival will reign supreme. With Rzarector holding two of the belts, and the other two belts up for grabs between any of them. Now I'm sure you heard the narrator talkign about false prophets. Well let me tell you a little about this match. (Rzarector sits back and his green eyes glow out of his black robe as he flashes his fangs. He rearranges in his seat.) Rzarector: Renegade, my fellow tag partner, I see that you are pissed at me. About what? Me joining the DcV? Or the fact that I got more allies than you? You know Renegade, you come out there talking about how you won't hold back. Well Ren, I won't hold back either. I see your sins better than anyone else, why? Well that is because I am your tag partner. One half of your life, more than your girlfriend. Yet I see some faults in your motives. You seem to be very cocky, yet I'm not putting you down. You are one half of me to. Yet you seem very aggressive and cocky, well that my hide your self esteem. Maybe your self esteem is so low you just feel like taking it out on other people? It's so decisive for you isn't it Renegade? (Rzarector hisses as his green eyes glow very bright. Rzarector gets up and walks outside to a yard of red and black flowers, a cross in the middle, and a fountain of jesus in the center of a huge pool.) Rzarector: I never swim, I'm repellant to water. God won't let me cleanse my soul until I correct my sins. So I have to stay away fromw ater, I can't swim. I used to know but God took the ability from me. I know why, I know him very well now. A harsh God, but a very reasonable God. Yet Renegade does not understand anything. I am a prophet, I am not a false one, Renegade is a false prophet. (Suddenly out of the flowers another man walks up. A dark man, with a dark figure on him. He is wearing a large trenchcoat, black boots, a black hat, and smoking a cigarette. Rzarector looks in shock as the man walks up.) Man: Hello, Rzarector? Rzarector: Who are you and how do you know me? Man: I was sent to you, some guy was talking in his sleep and told me to visit Prince Paul. So I came quickly, the man is very sick. Can you help? Rzarector: I can try, where is he? Man: He is this way, follow. (The man takes Rzarector through the woods to a small house in the outskirts. There the man takes Rzarector inside the house to a small bedroom where an elderly gentleman lays coughing and his face very pale. Rzarector looks in disgust.) Rzarector: You took me here to save him? Let him die, not one thing would make me try to save him. (Suddenly the voice inside Rzarectors head plays.) *Rzarector, your sins will never be corrected. What if all those people you hurt never forgave you, I would have never gave you this opportunity. Now get on your knees and pray for this man and he shall be healed. Listen to me, I am your master.* (Rzarector hisses as he begins talking.) Rzarector: (talking to the man.) This gentleman laying here, nothing but betrayed me. He has yet to ever help me once. All he ever does is spit on my feet and throw his trash in my yard. He says I am the antichrist. Who does he think he is? I am one with God, not one against God. Yet this man never stops bothering me. Now God has struck him ill because of his sins, and he would never correct them. So who is it of me to save this fool? He is the one that needs to suffer. So let him. (Rzarector starts to leave the room when the voices from inside his head start up again.) *Rzarector, this is your last chance. Don't correct this sin, and you never will have another chance on Earth to correct them. Time for your eternal flames, not the ones of others.* (Rzarector gives a loud hiss and flashes his fangs as he walks back to the guy. there he drops to his knees and begins a silent prayer. In it he wishes the guy back to full health. Like that the guy comes back to conscious. The guy looks at Rzarector and spits on him and yells at him. Rzarector leaves the house very angry as the voice says one last thing.) *You did very well my son, maybe there is aplace for you in the golden walls after all.*
