Match it up... (Distortion RP# 2) Damien Ryan Furiously Addictive Wrestling Roleplay #16 Date: 10.16.02 Location: Miami, Florida, USA (the screen opens up outside the American Airlines Arena where we see a Primtime Central television van with some trucks around it. Looks like there's some guys unloading equipment for this Sunday's FAW show. The pull out board switches, moniters, cameras, and all sorts of technical utilities. We see a navu blue pick-up truck pull up next to the van and the engine turns off. The door opens and Damien Ryan walks out and around the truck. He's wearing windpants, and sweatshirt, and a Maple Leaf's cap. He opens the tailgate of his truck and pulls out a large gym bag, tosses it on his shoulder and starts walking to the garage opening where all the technical utilities are heading. As he continues to walk a security guard stops him) Guard: Hey pal, where the hell you think your going? Ryan: I have a pass. Guard: At this time of night?! I don't think so buddy. Ryan: I don't think you understand- Guard: Wait a second, aren't you Damien Ryan!? Well I'll be damned! Ryan: Yeah that's me man, now I need to work on some things backstage. Guard: (seemed curious and excited) You got something planned or something? Ryan: I guess you can say that, I guess you can say that I already have a clue on who slammed my back with a chair last week, and put stiches in my back! Nice talking to you. (Ryan tries to continue to walk but the security holds his arm up) Guard: Sorry Damien, but I can't let you through. Ryan: What the fuck! Get your nasty, filthy, American trash hands off of me! (Ryan grabs the arm the block him and twists it a certain way, snapping the mans arm causing the bone to go through the man's skin. The man looks at his arm and is it total disbelief) Guard: My....my....my...my arm! Oh my god! (The security guard pulls out his steel rod with his good arm to defend himself and try to arrest Ryan. Ryan puts the large bag down unzips it and pulls out a kindo stick. The guard swings his weapon, and totally misses. Ryan kicks him square in the testicals and slams kindo stick right across the security guards forehead, breaking the man's skin and he begins gushing blood. Ryan throws the broken kindo stick to the ground. Zips his bag back up and continues to walk into the building. A man with a camera tries to catch up, the man seems rather goofy, wearing his PTC uniform and a DOW wrestling hat) Cameraman: Holy shit, that was amazing! Ryan: What do you want man? Cameraman: I just got all that footage on tape, there big bucks on this video tape! This could be my real big break! Ryan: You publicity kid? You want to be noticed? Cameraman: Hell yes! Ryan: Well first take that ridiculous hat off! FAW should be your only fed to work with. What's your name? Cameraman: Eugene. Ryan: Uh....well, it's Pierre now, that's a good Canadian name. Pierre, I promise you with this interview, you will become one of the top reporters on the PTC network, you hearing me out? Cameraman (Pierre): Yes sir! Ryan: Now you just got a preview of what I have in this bag here. This here is 'EXTREME' bag number one, one bag out of man I have to carry in. I also have some more suprises associated with this. Foamy's promo really hit a raw nerve today, he pretty much said I sucked. He put it in his own words, but the way I took it was, that I am not worth anything, not main event potentional, and I shouldn't even be Hardcore Champion. Ok, you are entitled to your own opnions Foamy, but so weren't the columnists and reviwers when they ranked me as a top five crapper, and someone who was expected to fail. But look where I am now, a month into the business and I'm the FAW Hardcore Champion, and not to mention undefeated! Foamy, you're just going to be another to add on my victory list. I'm on a short fuse as you can see and I'm definetly ready to step into the ring with you. Pierre: Damien can you- Ryan: Shut up! Pierre: Sorry. Ryan: You know what, you probably could be Hardcore Champion, no doubt about that. I mean, you're definetly one of those main eventer wrestlers fighting for the World Championship. But can you really beat me Foamy? You sound just like Crimson Lord, and Sin, and Neo, and all the rest. But they didn't beat me. You talk about how you were an extremist in other federations, and how you were champions, I give you respect for that, but do expect a pat on the back from me? I dont think so Foamy. You really want me to put the title on line....hell I'm not afraid to. I'll put the Hardcore Championship belt strap around Britney Spears' waist and said, 'If you have sex with her, you win the title'. The point I'm trying to make is, the Hardcore title means shit to me Foamy, if I lose, who gives a shit. I'm just saying I probably won't because you are definetly underestimating me, just like everyone else is! You will learn the hard way Foamy, and you will learn the Canadian way. Pierre: What about- Ryan: Jesus Christ Pierre, do you know how to fucking listen? (Pierre lowers his head) Ryan: As I was saying...Foamy, I'll wait for a response from you, whether you want this match to have stipulations or not, doesn't matter to me, I'll beat you anyways. You could throw in the ladder and make this a ladder match for all I care, I mean, you certainly have experience with ladders considering you're too much of a fucking nitwit to reach the top of a ladder, even a half-retard could make it to the top! You could do anything Foamy, I'm waiting... Oh yeah, and for those three scrubs fighting for the number one contender's spot. I'll be watching you three go at it, but I can tell from now, none of you seem able to step up to the potential of a Canadian. (Ryan grabs his bag and walks off into the building) Pierre: Damien! You wan't my card? (Ryan keeps on walking without saying a word, and in the background we the see flashing lights from the ambulance showing up for the security guard. Pierre turns the camera around so its on his face. Revealing his acne and nosehairs) Pierre: This Egen- I mean Pierre from PTC signing off! (he searches for the button that fades the camera out and he struggles making very unattractive faces and showing us his nasty yellow teeth and mucased tongue, finally to our pleasure the camera fades to black)
