The Future Is Bright. The Future Is Hardcore! Renegade Furiously Addictive Wrestling Roleplay #63 Date: 31/10/02 Location: Outside Freedom Hall, Kentucky (It’s 10.46pm on a Thursday night in Louisville, Kentucky and Renegade is sat on the steps leading up the main entrance of the Freedom Hall. It is quite a still night, the nice of heavy traffic from the near by roads can be heard as well as the footsteps of people out for a late night stroll. The lights from the Freedom Hall mean that it is very light were Renegade is sitting and you can see him very clearly. He sits on the steps, arms resting on his knees with his hands together. He has his head down looking at the floor, his long black hair is tied back out the way and hidden away under a dark blue NY baseball cap. He is wearing his long black leather trench coat which follows down either side of his body, as well as his black leather pants. Under his trench coat he is wearing a black t-shirt with the letters –XF- on the front in red. What does –XF- stand for? He lifts his head slowly, looks towards the camera and begins to speak) Renegade: In three days time I’ll be competing here (gestures towards the Freedom Hall) in a Battle Royal match for a shot at the Hardcore title. My opponents in the match are the new boy Joey Johnson, the so far silent Shane Billz, The Duke who I fought in tag team competition only two weeks ago and the final person in the puzzle is none other then my tag team partner, Rzarector. Now, quite why me and Rza were booked in a match against each other is beyond me. But anyway we are and thee aint nothing I can do about. Even though I tried to! Anyway, back my opponents for this Sunday. First off, The Duke. Well having been in the ring with him only just recently his style and abilities are still fresh in my mind so I think I can cope with anything that he can through at me. Shane Billz, has this guy ever had a match here yet? This guy isn’t even worth talking about, pass. So now we come to the new boy Joey Johnson. Now I know that ya new around here and want to make a name for yaself and get noticed and everything, hell I did, and having seen your performance at the Pay Per View, I’ll be watching my step around you. You got skills, I’ll give you that, even if you did lose to Costella. (Renegade pauses for a long time, the look on his face changes to one of anger and frustration, then, just as quick as this expression came, it went, and his face returned to how it was before) Renegade: Ar I missed out Rza. Well, what can I say? I’ve been in the ring with him probably more than anyone else in FAW, so I know exactly what this man is capable of. And he’s capable of a hell of a lot I can tell you. But just let me say this, if it comes down to it and me and Rza face off against each other during the match, I’m just gonna let you know now Rza that I wont hold back, just as I know you wont hold back against me either. We’re both very competitive people and give 110% out there every night. That’s how much we love this business. That’s why we’re tag team champions. But Sunday night isn’t about tag team gold, it’s about hardcore gold. I title I’ve had me eye on since I joined FAW. I love to get hardcore! As you might have guessed from watching me so far. Even though I haven’t been in a hardcore match yet in FAW, I’ve used a steel chair more the a few times. So ask yourself this. If I will use a steel chair without thinking twice in a regular match, just imagine what I can do in a match where anything goes. I could cave your skull in with a dust bin lid, and it would be legal. I could drive your sorry ass through a table, and it would all be legal. I can do anything I wish to. (smiles) This is the kind of environment I thrive in. Remember, I was born to hurt people! (a sly smile creeps across Renegades face) Your Downfall will once again lead to a Blackout. (Renegade gets up off the steps, brushes himself down and walks slowly off across the Freedom Hall parking lot. His trench coat swings playfully from side to side with each step. The camera the spins around away from Renegade and focuses on the main entrance of the Freedom Hall, before fading out to black)
