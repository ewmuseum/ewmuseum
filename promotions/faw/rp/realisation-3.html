Realisation 3 'Golden Nine' Jeff Harris Furiously Addictive Wrestling Roleplay #264 Date: 8th February Location: Unknown :.:Last time I was in FAW I left in disgrace with the owner, ownership changes from time to time though and I am pretty known for my history with authority figures around here and other places. Maybe it is indeed time I do concentrate on the match at hand, why I chose to stay in FAW is beyond me… I joined when I was under the influence of Jean Deas; I guess I stay because I need to prove something to the people that I can still wrestle and put on beautiful matches. I need to prove that I can still beat the best and that is exactly what I am going to do tomorrow night at Panic!:.: .::The cry of seagulls carries across the bay shattering the calm lapping of the water on the cold February evening. As the blackness fades from the camera lens the scene of a jet-black BMW comes into focus parked at the edge of a bay with a grand view of the bright moon in the night of February the 8th. It's been one week since Jeff Harris was last in a wrestling, one week since he was deprived of the win against the Continental champion. Also a week where Jeff Harris discovered his true identity and what he can accomplish. And now, as he leans back on the hood of his BMW he reflects on the past 6 months. The six months in which he was abducted, brainwashed and sent back in the world by Jean Deas and Pete Harris, then getting out of WFW and going to FAW then beating 5 other people to win the hardcore championship. Also becoming someone who was feared by many and respected by many more although maybe it will take time for the respect to return and fear to be demanded, Jeff hasn’t been much of a loser but over the past couple of weeks he has been one, losing to Profusion and getting eliminated two times in a row at the rumble of When Worlds Collide, hopefully Jeff Harris will go a step higher after his match on Sunday. As Jeff curves his attention back to his accomplishments over the past month in FAW, the haunting fact that the title that he need, and the title that he wants is still not his, being world champion for 2 hours on a show dedicated to himself just doesn’t cut it, he needs to be world champion now and not later but it’s very doubtful that 9th Wonder of Foam will give him a shot. A way Jeff Harris can get to the title is if he defeats Skipster a FAW Hall of Famer and Rzarector the number one contender for the world title then he will prove that he can still beat the best instead of pissing around with people like Profusion, Pulsar and other no-name jackasses with no future. A car pulls up aside of Jeff’s BMW, the door opens and out gets Adam Hutton… With a camera crew they all walk up to Jeff who continues to stare blankly into the night sky::. Adam Hutton: H-Hello, Jeff? .::Jeff turns his head towards Hutton giving an evil glaire::. Adam Hutton: Have I come at a bad time? Jeff Harris: Anywhere where you are, it’s always a bad time. What the hell do you want? Adam Hutton: Well… I want an interview. Jeff Harris: With whom? Adam Hutton: With you. Jeff Harris: With me? Adam Hutton: Yes you. Jeff Harris: Holy Jesus Christ! I haven’t been interviewed for a long time and I don’t intend to break that streak either, so I will just tell you what needs to be said then you and your camera crew can get lost. Adam Hutton: …That’s fine. Jeff Harris: Good, as you probably know my mind has been through a lot over the past few weeks, but now people will recognize me for who I really am and that is a big time player in FAW. I don’t think you or anyone else can imagine what winning this match would mean to me, I have already beaten The British Bomber, Chris Thunder, Spider and SpaWn all FAW Hall of Famer’s and winning this match tomorrow would mean I have beaten yet another Hall of Famer that being Skipster… I deserve to be at the top of this business and that will happen one way or another because if I beat Rzarector and he goes on to beat 9th Wonder of Foam at Extreme Beating 3 then that will mean I would deserve a world title shot. I have been world title material since The British Bomber and his little band of bitches screwed me last year, it’s been hard trying to get through the pain I’ve been through but it’s okay now, the pain in me has gone and the pain for everyone else will begin. People will learn to respect me for who I am and what I have done. Now I know that Skipster and Rzarector have been somewhat feuding but know I am in the main event, Skipster and me have only ever clashed once and that was well over a year ago in a 4-way tag team match. A match where Skipster and The Omega Fro cheated to win, this is a match I probably wanted for a long time, a chance to get my revenge on him as for Rzarector I don’t really have any problems with him except he won the rumble and I didn’t but hey it’s not like he eliminated me but I will eliminate him tomorrow night, but enough with this talking. Talking does nothing, it’s the ass kicking that does something, so that’s all I have to say to you, now fuck off. Adam Hutton: But what about the… Jeff Harris: GET LOST! .::And just like that Adam Hutton gets knocked out by running into the camera, Jeff Harris turns his head back to the moon just staring at it as the scene fades::.
