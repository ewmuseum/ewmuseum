Frontlines (3/3) Roleplay #3 for WWC Rzarector Furiously Addictive Wrestling Roleplay #172 Date: Jan 24 Location: Heaven *And that was it, Rzarector stood in the face of 5 demons. They were hissing and growling as there red eyes flashed, but still Rzarector stood still. Rzarector flashed his fangs at them as they inched closer and closer towards Rzarector. They all formed pentagram lines with Rzarector in the center. The demonic Lee Smith steps up.* Demon Lee Smith: So this is what we have come to expect from you? Nothing, you promised us that you would be more than this. You told everyone in this whole place that you would do things differently. That you wouldn't fell like your two older teachers did. But were you right? No you failed one more time. All those times you got a title shot, all those times you failed once more. You see Rzarector, it aint about us. If you ever want to make it far in life, you got to beleive in yourself. If you are always worried about losing, or having a hoe mess up your life. Then you will find yourself in a closed casket. See it all makes sense. *Rzarector stood there as he stopped flashing his fangs, he thought deep down inside for a second and came up with a weird idea.* ^Rzarector talking in his mind^: You know, I'm starting to see through this. There is something more to these demons looking like the wrestlers at When Worlds Collide than I thought they were. God is behind all of this, maybe I won't find out right now, but I soon will. I promise......see God, you think you can tease me, well you can't. I will play along with this. I won't even fight, and I will just see what they do. *Rzarector laughs wickedly in his mind as he thinks about God looking on as Rzarector got the beat down. Then God coming out and telling these fake demons to stop fighting him. Then the Black Priest would jump up and tease God saying that it didn't get him. Rzarector snapped back into reality as the demons are still looking at Rzarector. Demon Gabriel hisses.* Demon Gabriel: You know Rzarector, just like Lee was saying. maybe if you had more time to think. Maybe if you came to hell with us, we could help you.
