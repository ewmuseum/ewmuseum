WWC Roleplay 2 - The thoughts of a mad man Spider Furiously Addictive Wrestling Roleplay #241 Date: 25-01-03 Location: N/A Prelogue This Roleplay is a brief thought diary of Spider of his FAW career; it is not an actual promo… Pre-FAW Many years ago, back when I was a rookie still, a jobber if you like, I didn’t have many friends, I was very much introverted, many people just thought of me as some dumb kid who didn’t deserve shit. Then Gee Monie came along, then JLC came along, finally, I was ready to leave my shell, finally the world was in my hands. 2001 I started FAW, August, after a month I and the man I know as Jim gained tag gold, we were FAW’s best tag team of that year… ever. No-one would ever beat us, and Gee Monie lead us, him as the International champion, us as the tag team, we kicked serious ass. Dark Generation died, I died, I had the chance at the World title and failed, miserably I might add. A month or two later the shell was broken, I entered into a feud for Gee’s old title, the International title against Chris Thunder, the price? My sister, I learned on that night I beat him, nothing comes for free, and I know it better than most. The fans out there, they may learn more often, and they may learn soon. I went on, beat Jeff Harris for the Hardcore title, brought it back to FAW, and then, came so close, until British Jobber got a little help from Tom The Condom… Tom came back recently… as shit is ever, he can be commissioner for all I care, he can book me in some shit against every man in the fed, but on Sunday I am doing that anyway… 2002 It started so great, beating Thunder again but this time also getting revenge on Bomber, the memory, after that Great One fell to my form of greatness, but Spawn, that man cost me my lifestyle. Spawn injured me, the hell master gave me a punishment, I tried to come back, I really did, but I couldn’t, so I took time off, lived my life, learned more about me. But while I was away from the ring I could not learn. Instead I taught Lazlo… then I learned another lesson… don’t send a boy to do a man’s job… At Christmas I got married, to a beautiful woman, the woman known as Nina, the woman who was my onscreen girlfriend in November 2001, the irony that she was my nurses flatmate, we met at the hospital in June, damn she was so sexy, perfect tanned skin, beautiful brown hair, the lovingly inserted blonde highlights, the blue eyes I got lost in. Nina is the reason I came back, to provide for her, and she knows I’m only happy in two places, in her arms, and in that ring, and someday soon, I want her to have another type of ring on her finger… 2003 This year, is the year of the Spider. I’m going to start as I mean to go on, I may not win the damn rumble but I’ll try as hard as I can… does that sound corny or what? So many others, I may not win, but if I do, then that World title is as good as mine… still the win must come though. Just to think any jobber like Gabriel Hunter, Profusion or Cyrus Skar could win that title makes my blood boil and makes my lunch come up, if I lose it’s going to be against someone I respect… oh wait JLC isn’t in the rumble, guess I better win this thing…
