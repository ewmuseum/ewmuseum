Spawn — FAW Roleplay #44

captured 23 July 2003

SpAwN--The Hell Master \~~~~~~~(opening scene)The scene FIW after math were we see Spawn and Pyro in the locker room and we see Spawn in a rage he has just lost a match with skipster and it has aggravated him very much. Spawn: Man this is fuckin bullshit Pyro, how can they, it is bullshit man I tell you, in front of all these fans, the big high ups they insulted my skills, makes me want to walk from this fucking fed man , give this shit up once and for all. Pyro: I have always told you us legends, do not get a fair shake in this place, they have always screwed me here and there, that is why I can’t make up my mind about leaving or not and I return every time, and then I am out again. Spawn: Man I could beat skippy with my hand tied behind my back, my fucking promos were good man you know that yet the fucking high ups, tell me to take a fall just to give him a fucking push since he has been losing big time, I say bullshit. Pyro: I feel you man they have done that to me on a lot of occasion trust me they fuckin suck like that man. Spawn: Man what this was here tonight was unrealistic, hell even the fans knew skippy can’t beat me unless it is giving to him. Which is was you know that, I tell you I am so fucking pissed off I feel like breaking this locker apart. Pyro: Man do not give them the satisfaction, that is what they want. Spawn: I can take a defeat, because I have taken some cheap defeats in this place but enough is enough, when are they going to beat me fair and square but not as a set up. Or because of a push up the latter. I feel to me the only real defeat in my career was the title defeat now to me that was a war and it was JLC’s night, a very good battle indeed, other than that look at the rest of my defeats unrealistic, they always use me to push someone up the latter for example look at my match with Fro, you tell me how the hell a man that has been gone for quite sometime comes back and beat a fucking champion that’s hot and almost unbeatable it is fucking unrealistic is what it is, man Pyro I tell you the day they made me lose to Bomber for the European title when you know for a fact I can beat him easily was the day they screwed me. And you know the shit about is I never get my revenge that’s what pisses me off the most. I have four defeats under my belt Fro never got a rematch, the grea1 never got a rematch, JLC haven’t seen a rematch there, and now skippy the goody two shoe who not only beats me but I believe in less than five minutes fucking unrealistic. Is a good thing I keep records of my career here in FAW so that when the time comes for me to take a walk I will never come back. I guess that’s how they repay me for all of my efforts in this place. Pyro: Well what’s done is done now is time to go forward and you have Crimson this Friday at Panic. Spawn: You know what Pyro I am so fuckin mad that right now I feel like doing a no show just like the great 1 did. I mean come on what is the purpose of Fighting Crimson again the first two meetings we had were no contest, why does it get so hard for me to beat him it doesn’t make sense you saw our first match I destroyed him inside the steel cage yet I didn’t win amazing I tell you fucking amazing. The sad thing is Crimson is a good friend what can I say about him he is a good if not one of the best wrestlers in FAW, I don’t know if I want to wrestle him you know what I mean Pyro, it is almost the same as me fighting you, it isn’t going to happen and if it does then I take a walk simple as that. Pyro: Yeah I would take a walk too, seeing as how they would probably screw us both. Spawn: Man Pyro I have been thinking and I will not take this bullshit, lying down so you know what I am going to do? Pyro: What's up what are you thinking? Spawn: This is what I have been thinking, when I first arrived to FAW, I have always been quiet keeping it to myself I would these massive promos but I would not go out there and do challenges, yes I installed fear into a lot of my opponents, but I have always stay quiet in voicing out my opinions, which to me I feel led to me getting screwed a few times, well from this day forward that is all going to change, from now on Pyro you will the ruthless son of a bitch Spawn you have ever seen, I will be so ruthless that I would even betray my mother. NO! more mr nice guy, no more is guys JLC and skippy , bomber, Omega Fro, great 1 if he is till here are going to slip by me with cheap ass victories, from now on they are really going to have to earn them. Because I will literally fight tooth and nail and slap their bitch ass around making them think twice why they became wrestlers. No more stupid interviews, no more fan fares, Spawn is a fucking wrecking machine and it is time I show that side of me, the real, real dark side of Spawn. Pyro: If that is the case then you must have started early when you came into my match and literally put Bayne out of commission. Spawn: That son of a bitch had to be put in his place, who the fuck that idiot think he is, coming back after a long absence and thinking he can beat the Omens of Death, well he got proven wrong. The only thing that son of a bitch can beat is the great1's little shift stick beat it till it comes is what he can do. He is lucky all he got was a few broken bones it could have turned out permanent for him. Pyro: Oh shit man! (looking to the side, and seeing a reporter coming down their way) Spawn: What, what's happening? Pyro: Look at what's coming down this way a stupid reporter, you know that fool is going to ask you all kinds of questions about your matches with Skipster and Crmson. Spawn: I hope he does, he will be the first step I take in the real dark side of Spawn. The reporter who has just come in looks at Spawn and Pyro and approach them, they look at each other and then the reporter starts to ask questions to Spawn. reporter: Hey Spawn can I ask you a few questions? it won't take nothing but a few minutes. Pyro: Yo Spawn you don't have to answer none of these bullshit questions this piece of shit want to ask you, let's just get out of here. Spawn: No it's ok I can handle this guy, so go ahead ask your questions but mind you, you get out of line and you are going to pay dearly. reporter: How do you feel after that shameful perfoermance you put up against skipster a wrestler you have beaten a couple of time in the past. Spawn: What are you a stupid moron didn't I tell you not to ask stupid questions? reporter: Sorry didn't mean to make it sound like that I apologize. Spawn: Much better now to answer your question let's just say that skippy was handed by big bosses upstairs an early x-mas gift. reporter: What do you mean handed over to him who handed him what? Spawn: I got screwed that's the bottom line to this deal. Skippy should have never won but he did and now he will be running for his life, when I get him on the rematch. reporter: And when is this rematch supose to take place on what day is going to happen? Spawn: If I knew I would tell you wouldn't I. reporter: What about tomorrow you have a big match against the Crimson Lord are you going to be ready for this match mentally. Spawn: Crimson Lord, the former Omen of Death, a good friend in and out of the ring and perhaps the only wrestler I have yet to defeat. He has a lot of things going for him and I give him all of the credit in the world, but Crimson you got in my face at the wrong time my friend, and I do not know what to do with you, should I even show up for our match tomorrow it is something that I have yet to decided, but all of the world knows that Spawn never backs down from any challenge, all of the world knows that I will rumble with any one at any given time, there is no stopping me, what you see is what you get total domination. I have been thinking what makes wrestlers fear me what makes them put out 120% in order to battle me and I know what it is. It is because I am the best FAW has to offer right now and you smart asses want to make a name out of yourselves by getting a victory over me. Is that what you want to do Crimson my friend, get in line because it is a very long, and a long list of losers on this line and you know what I think I will add your name to this list. . Is that what you want to do Crimson my friend, get in line because it is a very long, and a long list of losers on this line and you know what I think I will add your name to this list. You tend to forget Crimson that the first time we battle I destroy you from pillar to post and the second time was no different is true I didn't beat you but I punished you and that was very satisfying for me. All I need now to complete my hunger is a victory on an Omen that got away. Tomorrow it will be a night of hell a night when we shall fire and brimstones light up the sky and the fans will be screaming as the heat reaches them telling them that hell is in war. And there two most feared dark lords are in a total complete battle, who will come out on top, who will rise to the occasion, who will walk the walk, and talk the talk, is it you Crimson or me the answer lies tomorrow when we square off in the middle of the ring and beat each other senseless with no care for our friendship, our body, our spirit and our mind. I will tell you this Crimson I will come out on top this Friday it is a match that I must have a match that I must take a match that I will put you out to pasture and send you back from whence you came hell. I will be declare the winner and Spawn shall reign supreme. reporter: Are you sure you will be able to defeat Crimson tomorrow after your poor showing at the hands of skipster? Spawn: You know what little man I have had enough of your stupid questions so this is what I am going to do for you( Spawn turns to the side and with out the reporter knowing what is coming Spawn nails him with a hard fist to the face.) You see what you made me do (go into his pocket and takes out some cash) here you go that will take care of the bloody nose and the court fees now get the fuck out of here before I really get evil on you. Pyro: Damn Spawn you really put that son of a bitch out, let's get out of here before this really turns into a bad deal. Spawn and Pyro begin to walk away, as the reporter in laying on the floor bleeding from the nose. Down the hallway Spawn looks at Tom's office and goes to knock at the door. Pyro: Yo what are you doing man, we do not have time to talk to him. Spawn: This is something that I must do it has been long in the waiting plus it will only take a few minutes. Pyro: What are you going to talk to him about? Spawn: You will see it is good for me but is going to be shocking for him. (Spawn goes into his pocket and takes out a list of names, he then knocks at the door again and goes in with Pyro Inside Tom ask them to take a seat and if they want something to drink they both say no and the conversation begins. Prez Tom: So what can I do for you two guys? Spawn: Don't fucking act stupid Tom you know the deal, you know what happened out there to me you changed the story line and jobbed me to that bitch skippy, I want some explanation and I want them now. Prez Tom: I don't think you should be hollering at me like that it could get you into a lot of trouble? Spawn: What are you st….( just as Spawn was about to say something a man walks into Tom's office and whispers something into Tom's ear and then walks out of the office) what was that about? Prez Tom: That was from around the corner it seems there was an incident were a reporter got struck by a wrestler and hurt real good. Do you know anything about that Spawn or Pyro? Spawn: No we don't, but it sounds real good one of those idiots get a beat down all they know what to do is harassed people. Prez Tom: They are just doing there job, but just a reminder we all know the rules you hit a reporter you get fined and suspended those are the rules. Spawn: We know the rules Tom but that is not why we are here you know that we have a beef to pick with you and you we do not see eye to eye. Prez Tom: Yeah I know and if you two guys even think about touching me in here you will get fired. And I don't care what type rating you both bring. Pyro: Tommy boy you shouldn't be threatening us you just may never know what type of accident can happen to you. Prez Tom: Let us get on with this now what is it that you guys need? Spawn: You see this list in my hand, this is what I want you to do….first of all go ahead take the list and read the names on it I will tell you the rest (Spawn hands over the list to Tom who begins to read it) Prez Tom: Stunning Stan, Omega Fro, JLC, Skipster, Mortician, Bayne, Spider, Morte, Bomber, what do you want me to do with all of these names on the list? Spawn: Aaahhh good question you see since the moment you guys screwed out of my match, out of my victory with skippy I have been in a rage and I want some payback, so what I want you to do is go to the arena in front of all of the fans and introduce that list to the fans, and then from The FAW Tron I will do the rest. Prez Tom: But why what is it that you are going to do with these names? Spawn: Just do what I ask of you and everything will work out just fine now me and Pyro must go and we will be waiting. Pyro and Spawn step out of the office and start to speak to one another. Pyro: What is up with that list Spawn? What are you up to and how come the Crimson Lord is not in the list? Spawn: It is simple Pyro this is my revenge list all of those on the list will fall to me tonight I will challenge each and every single one of them to a fight and we are going to find out who is a man and who is a chicken. And the reason Crimson is not there is because he is still my friend and I do not fight friends out of revenge. Pyro: Well then if you are going to do thids we better do it now so let's go get ready. The scene shows Tom the Bomb inside the ring with the list and a microphone in his hand as he begins to speak to the fans. Prez Tom: Hello everybody how is the show thus far ( fans cheer loudly) well today is special here in my hand I have a list of names, names that must respond to the man that will speak upon them when he appears I will not tell you who he is but he told me to metion the names to all of you. We begin, Stunning Stan, Omega Fro, JLC, Skipster, Mortician, Bayne, Spider, Morte, and Bomber. These are the names that appear in this list, and these are the names this man will talk about now I leave you all with him. The arena goes dark and the FAW Tron comes on. Spawn appears on it and receives mixed reactions from the fans. He then begins to speak. Spawn: I will not say welcome for I do not care, and I will not say how good this place is or how good this show has been because I do not care. What I do care is this list that Tom that son of a bitch has read to all of you and the names that are on this list are the names that I will talk about here today. Let us see who is first on my list oh yes Stunning Stan it must be an honor for you to have me mention your name… not only that but to be first in Spawn's list, but its not because you are the best, no way it isn't because you are the first that I have to stop no way, it is because I just happen to write your name first and you just happen to be lucky to be the one that will get your ass kicked first that's the reason I challenge you to a fight any day of the week, are you man enough Stan can you heart answer the call will your heart let you answer, a lot of questions give me an answer. Spawn: Next on this list is the Omega Fro now Fro you owe me big time you are one up on me a gift given to you no less by the big boys upstairs so I challenge you to a fight so that I can get my revenge upon you as I know how to do now I heard news that you have left FAW for a while so I will be patient and wait for you. You see my career is far from over and yours is too. When you come to FAW answer the call and Spawn will wait to give you your demise. Spawn: Next that I want to challenge is the man that beat me legit and that is the world champion JLC I have not forgotten my belt I will get it back and this time you will no back up your great friend is greatly gone and you are all by your self what are you going to do now, who are you going to turn to now no one that is. Mark my words JLC hell has risen again the sky has turned pitch black and your reign is coming to an end just as soon as it began, answer my challenge JLC accept your faith because if you don’t I will find you somehow. Spawn: Skippy boy mr goody two shoe Santa Claus came mighty early for you this past Tuesday, and you know what enjoy because you are set up to take me in a rematch this Tuesday coming up at FIW there will not be any given gifts to you this time you are going to have to earn a real victory from me, you are going to have to earn the fight so be ready mr. goody two shoe now it is time to become a man no more cheap given victory if you win this time I will bow down gracefully, but then again maybe not. Spawn: Next on the list as we continue is none other than the Mortician wise up man accept my chanllenge to a fight let us see if you are from the dark side like you say you are… do not be a chicken, be a man take the challenge you are a champion, you know how it is, just imagine how good it would feel it you beat me in a fight your popularity would grow very high. Take the challenge Mortician and be a man. What do you have to lose, oh wait a minute but an ass kicked by the hell master the true ruler of FAW. Spawn: Ha, ha, ha, ha, what can I say about this stupid monster that calls himself Bayne how is your leg feeling better I hope, all I can say is when you come out of the hospital give me a call and we shall battle hellish style… for now I am done with your sorry ass. Spawn: Spider the eight legged creature what a bug, a tough one I might say ready to spin his web on his victim all of the time but not on all of the victims especially not on me but I will tell you what I will be doing to you bug if you accept my challenge. I will send to insect cemetery and you will be remembered as the insect that got taken out by the hell master. Accept my challenge become a tarantula not an eight legged bug. Spawn: Next on the list the rookie Morte now how did his name get on my list I don’t know but since your on then what the hell why not I don’t have nothing else to do let’s see if you are as good as you think you are I will make you a known superstar if you can beat me then you will be pretty much set for a long time to come if you can beat me which is bullshit, so let me know Morte if would be interesting to give an unknown a shot at my career. Spawn: Last but not least is Bomber what can I say I about you bomber you owe me and you owe me big and you need to stop running and answer my challenge stop being a crying bitch and be a fighting man. It is time to put up or shut up. Spawn: That is all of them now if they would all grow some balls we would have the fight of our lives if they grow some balls we shall wait and see. Also I challenge any tag team out there to face the Omens of Death any time any place any where. As for you Crimson once again you are not on the list because I feel that you will get your just deserve tomorrow when I fight you at Panic and Beat you from here to there, I feel tomorrow is a night for redemptions my redemptions and you sorrows and defeat, get ready to get pummel to the ground, get ready to feel the hell of Spawn all over you and get ready to be defeated as I pin you to the mat 1, 2, 3, now Crimson can you take the defeat can you take the loss will it bring you down to your knees, yes it will and I will be the man that will do it tomorrow night. Now this interview is over. Take over you fake ass president (talking toTom the Bomb) Prez Tom: Before you go Spawn this is exactly what you want to fight all of these wrestlers is this correct? Spawn: You are a smart man Tom yes it is and I want you to be the man to set it all up, you think you can handle that or is it to much for you? Prez Tom: I will handle it but suppose I decide not to what then? Spawn: Then I believe it is your funeral not mine so I would think twice before I would do a move like that. Prez Tom: Oh don’t worry I will Spawn but just remember one thing you do anything to me and you are as good as gone just like I told you before. Spawn: Do not make any threats Tom it is not you. To me you are just a punk I am letting run the show, remember whenever I want to I can take your power from you got that now I bid you farewell. Spawn and Pyro leave the arena and go to the parking lot were Spawn runs into Mike, they all greet and Mike ask Spawn a question. Mike: Hey Spawn are you ready for tomorrows Devil’s Pit man it will be a hot one the best yet? Spawn: No way Mike no more pits it is all over it is time for the new Spawn and the Devils pit is no place for the new Spawn. Mike: What are you saying are you out of your mind? Spawn: No way Mike I am totally serious no place what so ever now let us leave it at that ok. Mike: Come on Spawn hey Pyro speak to him get some sense into him will you? Pyro and Spawn do not answer and keep walking as the scene fades to black.
