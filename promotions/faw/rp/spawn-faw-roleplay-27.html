Spawn — FAW Roleplay #27

captured 8 August 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
SpAwN--The Hell Master \~~~~~~~(opening scene)The scene starts out in the aftermath of FAW Thursday panic were we see Spawn in his locker room putting his wrestling gear inside his bag after a very successful victory against Juvenile the #1 Stunna a match that Spawn controlled and took advantage all the way. The scene shows Spawn walking out of the locker room and being stopped by a few of the wrestlers, they begin to talk to Spawn and congratulate him on his victory. Wrestler 1: Hey Spawn man that was a hell of a match it was yours all the way. Wrestler 2: Yeah finally someone takes a misery out of FAW hopefully that will be the last time we will hear from that scum bag Juvenile. I Spawn: t was worth every minute of the match I really wanted to shut his mouth up for good, whether I did that or not remains to be seen. But if I haven’t then there is always a chance to finish it. Wrestler 1: Man everybody in the locker room appreciates what you have done, you deserve to be called a champion man. Spawn: Thank you all for those nice comments they are all greatly appreciated it, and I intend to do what I have always done since my arrival here in FAW fight till the end and never giving up. Honor is all what it is about and I am full of it and I am still hungry for more victories. Wrestler 1: Well we have to get going but before we leave I like to offer you an invitation to a get together in my house later on at around 8:00 pm there will be music girls and food, can you be there. Spawn: Let me see what I am doing later on and if I’m not that busy yes I will be there. The wrestlers leave and Spawn starts out for the exit himself, he then bumps into Prez Clinton and they begin to talk. Prez Clinton: Hey Spawn, great match Juvenile never had a chance, never saw what hit him. Spawn: Yeah thanks I went out there and kicked ass just like I said I would. Prez Clinton: I see now you have a match against JLC the international champion for FIW this Monday? Spawn: Never a rest for the champion, I guess it must come with the gold. Prez Clinton: Sorry about that Spawn but you are one of our top stars and you my friend bring high ratings. Plus your fight against JLC will be an incredible battle this Monday. Just look at the picture, the International champion vs. the world champion sounds like a monster movie. JLC is ready are you? Spawn: I am always ready for war it is what I do best, and you are right JLC better be ready because if he is still sleeping then he will miss the entire beating he will receive. Prez Clinton: You are so sure of yourself? Spawn: I am always sure of my self it is something that I learn to do, because it is the way that I gain the advantage on my opponents the edge if you can call it that. Prez Clinton: You know JLC is a good wrestler one of the best in FAW and by him holding the international belt this long he has really made a name for himself an accomplishment worth getting credit for I think. Spawn: You are correct on that but I also have made an accomplishment you see I have the world title not him so my accomplishment is far bigger than his. I give him credit and respect he deserves but it stops there that is all I give him and it is very unfortunate for him to run into me because nothing will keep me from getting my hands on him and breaking him in half limb by limb inch by inch. Prez Clinton: I see. But you know what is amazing? The fact that he was in the tournament, for the world title and he lost to Mortician. Only to find himself fighting, the world champion this Monday at FIW. Now I know the belt is not up for grab but in any case it is a main event and after months of waiting we are finally going to see JLC vs. Spawn. Spawn:Yeah you will see JLC vs. Spawn and I guarantee you it is going to be a slobber knocker. But I will also guarantee you that the outcome will be very decisive in my favor, you see I will go in there with my mind focus on the game my body prepare for the physical phase it will go through. My instincts will be as sharp as ever, nothing JLC can do or throw at me will get me of my game plan. The fact that he has held the international belt for quite sometime do not impress me, what would impress me is if he wins this belt that I have draped across my shoulders now that would be impressing, but it's not going to happen. Not now, not then, and not ever, because I will not let it happen. It's just that simple, a lot of wrestlers can go around saying this Spawn guy just because he won the world title thinks he is all high and mighty talking bullshit all of the time. Well then I guess they are right because I talk the talk, and walk the walk, I can back it up 100%. You see JLC I was not handed this belt it was not given to me not by Prez Tom or by you Prez clinton. I had to earn it win all of my matches before me without losing a single one, I had to prove my self that I was worthy of this belt the most precious belt in FAW, and I fought hard for it, sweat and blood came from me for this gold in order for him or anybody else to try and come take it from me they would have to kill me or get rid of me from the face of this planet and that Mr. Prez will never happen. Prez Clinton: Well there is one thing you do not have to worry about in your match this Monday at FIW. Spawn: Oh yeah and what is that? Prez Clinton: Your belt or his belt will not be on the line. Spawn: I see, now I ask you this by you saying my belt will not be on the line is this suppose to make me feel a relief to my match, make feel better that I do not have to defend my belt just go out there and defeat my opponent is that what you are trying to say? Or are you trying to say that I am scared of JLC? Prez Clinton: No it is not what I am trying to say at all. All I am saying that you guys….(Gets cut off) Spawn: I do not really care what you are trying to say belt on the line or not JLC is going to get spanked baby style if you know what I mean, if he never got spanked before by his mom then it pleases me to be the first to put respect on this little boy's ass. Now I ask you another question ? are you a gambler, do you make bets Mr. Prez do you. Prez Clinton: Some times I do on special occasions here and there, what about it? Spawn: This occasions is a good one and you should bet on it. Prez Clinton: I do not make bets when it come to wrestling matches it is unprofessional in my part. Spawn: That is a shame then because if you did bet you make sure you pick the right winner on your bet. Pick the man that will give you money in your pocket, not the man that will send you to the poor house and picking your welfare checks on those long lines. And do you know who this man is this man is me, I will be the winner so bet on me and you will have a lot of money for the month, but bet on him and you will be in the poor house for a very long time. Face the deal Clinton the truth is that I Spawn do not back away from any challenge, it is in my nature to fight wars all of the time, because it is what I do the best in life and there is no stopping me and JLC will find out because he will not be able to stop me. I know he doesn't like it, and he will probably throw some complaints here and there about me talking all of this bullshit but you know what I have to say to that fuck you JLC, fuck you and the horse you rode in, because whether you like me or not I am the best going around today so you can just kiss my ass. Prez Clinton: I see you are full of life and energy but I hope you do not put to much into this battle as you know it could take its toll on you for the PPV. Match. Spawn: No way Clinton, I know the scoop, I know what must be done, and what I must do. And losing isn’t one of them and yes I will be ready for the PPV, nothing not you not JLC and not even the four last contenders in that match will make me lose my concentration I am ready all the way. Prez Clinton: Changing the subject from JLC for a minute, what will you do if your partner the Crimson Lord wins the tournament this Monday will you fight him after all he is your partner. Spawn: Of course I will fight him this is my title and I will defend it against anyone that steps up to the plate and if the Crimson lord wins so be it then me and him will be going at it in the PPV and may the better man win it. And like I said before I always consider myself coming out on top all of the time. But that is for another day right now JLC is all that is in my mind and he is going to fall I will beat him till there is nothing left to scrape of the floor my mind will be focused on destroying him inch by inch. I will toss him from one side of the ring to the next, and he will never know what hit him. And when I am done with him he will think twice to ever get in the ring with me again. So when you ask me if I am prepared yes I am but the question is is he prepared, I doubt it. Prez Clinton: Oh Jesus is getting late I better be leaving you know how the wife gets. Spawn: No I don’t I am not married but it seems to me that someone has been timed. Prez Clinton: It is not about that just got to get home early is all. Spawn: Ok then I will let you go don’t want to be held responsible if some one is found knocked out in the corner somewhere.(smiling) I will see you later. Prez Clinton: Ok I will see you later Spawn. Clinton and Spawn head there separate ways and Spawn bump into Mike and they begin to Speak. Spawn: Hey Mike what are you doing here? Mike: Hey Spawn, I am here on a commercial I am doing for FAW. Spawn: What type of commercial is it? Mike: Is a pepsi commercial and is a long one so they ask me if I can produce it. Since they like the way I produce your Devil pit. Spawn: That’s good for you Mike now listen tomorrow I have something I would like to show you and an Interview I would like you to conduct for me. Can you do this for me? Mike: Does it has to be tomorrow can it wait till Thursday or something. Spawn: No Mike I need this done for tomorrow I would appreciate it greatly. So can you do it? Mike: Ok I will do it for you, at what time you want me to be at your house? Spawn: Six o’clock and do not be late, so I guess I will see you tomorrow then? Mike: See you then Spawn, bye, bye. Mike and Spawn go their separate way as the scene fades to black
