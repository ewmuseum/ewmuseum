Aggravation Jade Furiously Addictive Wrestling Roleplay #74 Date: 10112002 Location: Here, duh […She brushes the white streaked, coal hair out of her face. A little sweaty from the bright glow of the Californian sun at midday, it stays together, perched perfectly behind her ear. Her black tinted glasses get pushed up her nose, Jade appears nervous as she rubs her nose, keeping her hands no more than three inches from her face. She purses her lips as the annoying voice of the chubby salesman fades into the scene…] “Huh?” […The confused words squeak a little as they pass through the front door of the well lit apartment into the hall. This was the fifth apartment she had viewed today. None of them suiting her criteria, and this was becoming little more than an aggravation to the young FAW star. Her first instalment of her yearly salary had come through only this morning, after her appearance on her first FAW show titled ‘Distortion.’ This greasy little man wanted to get a piece of that pay check, thus the reason they had been here since 6:30 am this morning…] Salesman: I said, this is an ideal position for an apartment for a young lady as yourself. The sea is within ten minutes walking distance, Grocery stores are right around the corner of 45th st. Nightclubs, scenery and historical monuments are all within half of an hours drive. I see you are getting a little tired. It is 2:00pm, you want to give it a rest for today? “Actually..” […That was the second time the salesman had asked that question. It was also the second time that the greasy little midget had taken it upon himself to ignore his customer. Now Jade was getting very, very frustrated…] Salesman: I didn’t think so Honey! You just don’t like it right? It is fine, we can find somewhere else, I have a few other properties in mind. […Jade grunted and followed the small, peculiar man out of the door, once again. Crimson came to Jade’s mind, filling her with a hot, pleasurable feeling. The only other thing she felt this kind of warmth from was slamming someone’s head through a chair, hitting her patented move the Fallen Warrior. Aaaagh such delighted feelings Jade sometimes thinks she doesn’t deserve. But the goodness Jade has delivered in such a small life span would allow even Satan himself to deserve a little happiness…] Jade (Thinks): I swear if I have to view one more damned property today I am gonna snap that little oily runt. Ignorance is not something, which I enjoy and well I could be doing more important things than walking around with this fat little jackass. I could be training for FAW, arranging little catfights between Lacuna and myself, or I could even be with my beloved Crimson. But no I am stuck here with Chubby. Heh. 'I'll tell you what you fat little...' [...Jade pokes the sweaty man in the chest while backing him in the corner...] '...runt, I didn't ay i wanted to carry on, and to be frank if I have to look at one more damned house today I am going to throttle someone, namely you! Now get out of my way before I squash you!' [...Jade pushes the man against the glass door creating a little crack where his back hit. Right before she leaps through the hole where the door should be. [...Jade stomps down the concrete steps. Her heavy black army boots making dust as she makes her way to the salesman's BMW! The keys are still in the door of the house. Some how the engine starts and the car hurtles away in reverse, gravel flicking up at the screen of the $30,000 convertible machine. Jade's silver tinted glasses remain on the floor as the car makes its way out of view...] -[Fade]- 'Bastards like that should rot in hell!' [...Jade sighed to herself as she sips on a cup of Mocha in her white mug...] 'People like that give me the fuel I need to keep this fire burning, don't misinterpret that but...' [...Jade looks away, around the room, nobody is there. Jade is clearly talking to herself, quite loudly too...] [...Jade gets up off her humble black leather two seater and wanders around her living room. She wander to her magnificent fireplace and moves an ornament backwards and forwards a few times, very obsessively she stands in front of the 14 by 14 inch, golden framed, and autographed, picture of the Crimson Lord. Jade's violet lips leaving their mark on the head of her Crimson. Jade once again sighing as she looks at a gym bag resting on the dining table...] 'Now what goodies did Lacuna leave for me? I mean she practically left her gym bag there for a little light fencing, makes for an easy couple of hundred at least. FAW wages can’t cover for everything in my expensive lifestyle. Even Crimson cannot do that I suspect. However much of a masterful animal he is in and out of the ring.' [...Jade blushes a little over the thoughts of the other night. The hours on end spent, having fun...] […The first item Jade pulls out of her failure of a tag partner’s gym bag is a set of delicate diamond encrusted cutlery. Jade eyes glow, sparkling in the preciousness of the items she holds in front of her…] “They are beautiful. These could be worth an easy 2 thousand if you go to the right store.” […Jade suddenly loses interest and staggers back to the sofa, she falls there backwards onto the sofa and blacks out…] -[Fade]- The only woman to grace FAW’s roster seems a little distracted to say the least. This is probably the third time Jade has had a blackout since before the TTT at the PTC started. She lost her match up when she stood besides Lacuna Coil, as her blackedoutness started to ware in about three quarters of the way in. Unable to get anywhere or do anything she was left t lose the match for their team. Trying to keep the blackouts from the few people who dare care for Jade was a hard task indeed. She nearly took a turn while she was shopping only yesterday, a quick sit down and something to eat, managed to keep her awake, on her feet. All Jade knows now is she needs to get around this problem before she takes another lucky shot in the Tag Team Tournament. Otherwise things are going to become messy. [… Jade rolls over in her sleep down to the crimson carpeted floor. Her head barely misses the table but gives her enough for her to awake herself again after about an hours sleep. Her hair matted to her forehead with sweat…] “Aw shit!” […Jade’s frustrated tone rings out as she bangs her fists down on the table in anger, she couldn’t believe she managed to do it again! Jade stands up, her knees failing a little but she steadies herself on the arm of the sofa…] […Darkness now fills the room, Jade walks over to a drawer in her cupboard and takes out a few thick white candles and scatters them with no particular pattern around the room. There hasn’t been electricity in this festering crap hole since Thursday and well Jade was tired with it. All these personal problems blended with the fact that Jade was to face to other cruiserweights in one match up in the form of the Triple threat. No wonder she was late with things! Unable to keep a top on these things Jade has been left without sleep, until her body can take no more, and one hell of a headache 24 hours a day and seven days a week…] “I guess I will just plod along. Not always the way to do things but I shall take days as they come to me. Right now I feel there is no other way to tackle problems but take them as they come. Then you can squash them, demoralise them and publicly shame them. The kind of shame the red Dragon will experience when the time comes at Distortion. The kind of pressure that will snap off Shensui Yusuki’s head when she hits her career killer. I guess I should speak to crimson about my worries but not now, later perhaps. First she needs to get out of this crap heap.”
