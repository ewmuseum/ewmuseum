Spawn — FAW Roleplay #29

captured 23 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
SpAwN--The Hell Master \~~~~~~~(opening scene)The scene Boston, in a little town call the south end it is the home town of the wrestler known to the world as Spawn. The wrestler who is also the FAW world champion, and the wrestler, who his next opponent is his tag team partner the Crimson Lord. The scene shows Mike just arriving in his car he parks and goes towards Spawn’s house, he knocks and is showed in. Inside we see Mike and Spawn talking. Spawn: So Mike what brings you here today? Mike: I have the scripts for the new episode for your show. Spawn: Oh yeah! that is good news it’s about time. So tell me about it, show them to me. Mike: I can show them to you but I won’t and there is a very good reason for it you see it is abig show for tomorrow and a very big surprise and I do not want to ruin it. But don’t you worry you will have enough time to practice your lines and everything. Spawn: Alright Mike I will go with your decision now since you are here let’s take a trip I want to show you something? Mike: Oh no! I hate your trips you are always up to no good, do I have to go with you? Can’t you go by your self please? Spawn: Mike, Mike settle down what good is a trip if you can’t share it with someone, plus you are good company and besides I have been laying back somewhat and a lot of wrestlers have been taking shots at me from a far so it is time for me to get back in the picture of things the big spotlight you know what I mean you see there are too many wanna be hotshots going around opening their big mouth about me and I aim to shut all of them up. Are you ready to leave? Mike: Yes now that you put it that way let’s go? Mike and Spawn get in the car and drive off. The scene shows Mike and Spawn getting off the car and walking into an abandon warehouse, in the warehouse we see chains, a hammer, knives, clubs, a 4x4 wood, and a table with ankle locks on it. Mike eyes get bloodshot and he ask Spawn what is all of this. Mike: Spawn now you have done it, you have gone of the deep end….may I ask you what the fuck is all of this? Spawn: I will tell you Mike you see this Thursday at panic I fight my tag team partner the Crimson Lord and of course you know what he holds, he holds the hardcore belt, so I said to my self. Self is time you become hardcore is time you became the hardcore champion and how am I going to accomplish this. I will begin here in my asylum were hardcore is all that exist inside these walls were blood sweat and tears are but a drop of a dime were no one cares if you die or live the only thing that matters is who will come out on top who will go that extra mile and do what is necessary to win at all cost throwing friendship and brotherly love out the window,This is what I must do to show Crimson what hardcore is all about. Mike: You are taking this to the heart remember he is your tag team partner and all? Spawn: Yes he is and he is after what is mine the world title, and when someone wants what I own they become my enemy even my own tag team partner. And I will do what ever is necessary to defeat him, even if it means sending him home in a body bag, and that is exactly what I intend to do. Mike: Why? you are not defending your title this Thursday you are actually going for his belt. Spawn: That is true but you see if he wins then he will automatically be the number 1 contender for the world belt. That is too easy for him and I am not going to let that happen, I am going to make it real hard for him you will see. Mike: Is that why you have all of these gadgets on this table? Spawn: Let me define all of these gadgets with a little story behind them and what they are for. First we have a set of knives, what are knives for? to cut things, and what am I cutting? The heart of the Crimson Lord. Why am I cutting his heart? For the simple reason that I can do it Mike, you see he runs around here saying that no one can beat him when in fact he knows that is a total lie. Everybody can be beat at any given night, you just don't know when is going to happen all you can do is be ready for the outcome and prepare yourself for when it happens mentally and physically. So you can say all you want about me looking out for my self because you are right no one help me win the world title and no one help me bring my self to the top but me so therefore I will be looking out for myself but you do not need to worry none, and worry about your self and how this knife if stabbed right will penetrate your heart and soul and make you an easy prey for my wrath. Spawn walks over to the next item he takes a breath in and begins to speak. Spawn: This little piece of a tool is call the hammer and I got plans for this baby, what better way to make someone understand the real way of hell than by smashing his head right in and at the same time waking him up from the stupidity of life he has lead through out the years. Crimson has lead that life stupid and obnoxious and it is time I smash my friend over the head and wake him and his spirit up. And I will make him do my bidding. Mike: Man I don’t know about you but that sounds to violent to me. Spawn: Good that’s the way Crimson wants it that is the way he will get it. Now step over here do you see this? Mike: Yes I see it it’s a club and I am afraid to ask what are your intentions with this club? Spawn: The club is just for show I will not be using it only if I have to now the chains and the table with the ankle locks that is another story and I will tell you what I am going to do with them. First the chains Crimson says he is from hell. My question is what hell is he from certainly not from the hell that I am from, only the real devils dwell were I am from so what I am going to do is grab the chains and after I have beaten him unconscious I will wrap the chains around him and drag him to the torture table locking his ankle real tight, when all of that is done. That’s when the fun begins. Mike: What do you intend to do with him? Spawn: I intend to teach him about hell and what is really all about, I will show him who is his true master, who is the rightful ruler of the omens and who will come out on top this Thursday at panic. The name Spawn says it all it means the winner as Crimson means the loser. He will not be able to break my chains of hell, he will not be able to resist my tortures of hell, and he will submit to me. Mike: Spawn calm down you are starting to sound to creepy here just calm down? Spawn: Calm down you tell him to calm down, or better yet why don’t you tell him to get real and stop with this dictionary bullshit. Mike: what do you mean dictionary? What are you talking about dude? Spawn: Haven’t you seen his promos the way he speaks the words he uses is all about meaning, I tell you man he could be a great school teacher, but he has to be more realistic this is wrestling, not English school. If I wanted to learn some words I would get my own dictionary how did he get the name Crimson Lord remains to be seen what he should have called himself is the professor or the Genius. Here is a little joke for you Crimson I need a resume can you help me out or can you spell idiot and give me the meaning to it because that is what you are. With this spelling contest you have going on in your promos. Now I have something on the top shelf in my closet I have an encyclopedia you probably thought that word would be difficult for me to say huh but you are wrong. Any way back to the encyclopedia that is in my closet and what I am going to do is I am going to get this book and take it to the ring this Thursday and when you begin to speak to me in the ring and you start spelling words to me, I’m going to take the book and whack you over the head with. The effect will be that you will gain a fuckin headache and you will start spelling the words wrong and saying the wrong meanings for them. And all I am going to do is laugh big time about the whole deal. And then we shall see who will look like a fool in the ring. Mike: Now that’s funny I hear you Spawn you’re a comedy routine, and what about this shit with everybody attacking you here in FAW. Spawn: Now that is another story you have JLC talking shit we have the inforcer talking shit and also spelling his name wrong, then you have Spider who wants a shot ok, give him a lollipop, and the list goes on. But these three are important to me first Spider a good wrestler left for a while and is now back with us I fought him when I first won the gold he claims he wasn’t a hundred percent if not the gold would have been his. Wrong Spider it was my night to shine and your night to be defeated no more to be said and if we meet again the outcome will be the same. Mike: What about the inforcer? Spawn: Now that is one sick puppy and he needs to see a doctor before he can come into FAW and mess with the big boys, he knows he will get stomp on and then thrown to the garbage with the rest of the bums. One piece advice to you rookie get experience and prove yourself before you issue challenges it will save you a lot of pain in the end. Mike: Last but least the heel from hell JLC, what is up with him? Spawn: Here is a funny guy he calls himself the heel from hell whoooooo I am scared. What an idiot does he knows I own hell and his name is not on the list. Therefore he does not exist, not in the real hell world. JLC I will tell it to you straight you want me then come and get me, I am always ready for a challenge especially from the likes of you but I will tell you this you say you will defeat me wrong boy I will defeat you just like I did the last time and this time I will cause you a lot of pain from the real hell master. Mike: Man you are red hot Crimson will not have a chance in the world against you. Spawn: That is right Mike and when I am done he will think twice before he ever challenges me again. And if you do challenge me again do not make it a casket match you idiot don’t you know that is my specialty. I guess he hasn’t visit the Devil’s pit yet huh Mike, maybe is time we send him an invitation what do you think? Mike: We can give him one for tomorrows show. Spawn: Naaa he will see the show as bad he wants me trust me he will see the show. Mike: I hope so cause he going to have a big surprise coming his way. Spawn: You got that right and I am going to enjoy every minute of it. Mike: Alright are you ready to leave? Spawn: Yeah I believe I am done here so let’s go? Mike and Spawn leave the warehouse they get into their car and drive off. The scene shows Mike and Spawn getting of their car and going into a store inside the store they see a poster of the Crimson Lord. Spawn walks over to it and begins to speak to Mike about it. Spawn: Look at this Mike my partner the crimson one and the hardcore belt strapped around it.( start to think) Mike: What are you thinking off Spawn? Spawn: I am just thinking is this guy really my friend or my worst enemy can he be the one to stop the hell master, can I really trust this guy? All of these questions have to answered and it is my job to find the answers for them, because if I don’t it could the beginning of the end for the legacy of Spawn what do you think Mike? Mike: Man he’s nothing next to you that is and that poster sucks the big one, the question really is how do you feel about the belt on his waist do you want it or not? Spawn: Nope I don’t think so but if I win it then I will take it just like that you can’t stop me Crimson no one can. Come on Mike all of sudden I do not feel like buying stuff from this place is smells like rotten hell the wrong type of hell in here. Mike and Spawn leave the store but before Mike leave he grabs a pen from his pocket and write school teacher on the Crimson Lord poster. The scene fades to black.
