Amish Paradise: Part 3 (Panic RP #1) Gabriel Hunter Furiously Addictive Wrestling Roleplay #248 Date: 31st January Location: Parts Unknown Writer’s Note: I’d highly recommend reading the two roleplays I posted on the WWC Roleplay Board before reading this, to give you a better understanding of the plot.] -- The scene resumes where we last found Gabriel Hunter – Downtown Middle Of Nowhere, Amishcity. We are outside of the saloon where Markus, Greg and Gabriel entered, seeking assistance after the fuel in their car ran dry. The Western style doors suddenly swing open, as the Amish gentlemen met by Gabriel & co. steps out, squinting at the bright sunlight outside. Following him is an uneasy Gabriel Hunter, Markus Green and Greg Carhmichael, who admittedly, seems more concerned with watching the antics of the Amish citizens more than anything else. Stepping off of the decking outside of the inn, the Amish gent looks around, before turning to face Gabriel. -- Amish Gent: The name’s Jacob by the way … Good t’meet ye sonny … -- Jacob extends his hand toward Gabriel, expecting some sort of shake. Gabriel looks down at it, and reluctant to shake hands, smirks uneasily, and waves at Jacob. -- Gabriel Hunter: Gabriel Hunter … And this is uhhh … Markus Green, my agent, and Greg Carmichael, my trainer … -- With squinted vision, Jacob eyes Greg and Markus with suspicion, obviously, he hasn’t got the faintest clue what an agent or a trainer is. -- Jacob: Agent y’say? What would one of they be when they’re at home? Gabriel Hunter: Uhhh, what!? -- Markus taps Gabriel on the shoulder, and cups his hands, as if to whisper. -- Markus Green: **Whispering** He wants to know what an agent is … -- Markus steps back again, as Gabriel nods in understanding. He then turns towards Jacob, and attempts to answer his question. -- Gabriel Hunter: Right, gotcha … Well, I’m a professional wrestler, and all that, so I have an agent to organise training regimes, schedules, visitations, stuff like that … Understand? -- Jacob scratches his head. -- Jacob: Say what? -- Gabriel sighs. -- Gabriel Hunter: Ugh, never mind. Right, I believe you were taking us to someone who can fix my car? Jacob: Oh yes, right this way sonny … -- And with that, Jacob leads Gabriel, Markus and Greg across the dusty road towards the other side of town. As they walk, Gabriel, Markus and Greg are met with suspicious eyeing from the natives, who clearly are not happy with their presence. Bare in mind, these people have shunned all sources of technology out of their lives for some time now, so this is probably some sort of invasion to them. As they walk along the wooden buildings at the other side of the road, Greg and Markus exchange small talk. -- Greg Carmichael: Psst, Markus, where the f*ck are we going? Markus Green: I wish I knew, Greg, I wish I knew … Greg Carmichael: What if he takes us to some … Slaughter factory or something!? ARE THEY GONNA KILL US!?!?! Markus Green: No Greg, they’re not going to kill us. Greg Carmichael: BUT HOW DO YOU KNOW!? IT’S A CONSPIRACY DAMMIT! -- **Slap** -- Greg Carmichael: Thanks, I needed that. Markus Green: Heh. -- Finally, the quartet reach a run down garage-style building, that to be honest, looks as if it was built to service modern-day vehicles, despite the fact that it is made of wood. The words “Woody’s Services,” are painted crudely in white above the giant set of doors, which at the moment, are closed. There is a smaller door next to this large door, and this is the one at Jacob knocks on. -- Jacob: Here we are now … -- For a few moments, nothing happens, there is silence. As Jacob goes to knock again, however, the door creaks open. Not much, but just enough to see the head of the man on the other side. He to, is a bear-sporting Amish, although there is something strange in his eyes … Something, sinister. -- Shady Amish: Whaddaya want? -- Jacob leans in closer, making sure that what he says wont be heard by eavesdroppers. -- Jacob: Mah buddy’s here be needin’ yer help there, Woody … Woody: What is it? They needin’ fuel? Jacob: Aye. -- Woody shifts his glance to Gabriel, Markus and Greg, with a snarl. -- Woody: A’right, send ‘em round the back … Jacob: Yessir. -- The door slams back shut, causing some of the dust on the ground to scatter. Jacob jumps at the door’s sudden snap, before turning to face Gabriel’s motley crew. -- Jacob: Go ‘round the back, ma buddy’ll help ya out … Gabriel Hunter: Uhhh, ok … -- Gabriel shrugs, before leading his agent and his trainer between a small gap between the buildings, to the area behind the garage. Given the size of the garage, it is quite a walk, but nothing that you couldn’t complete in less than 30 seconds. Gabriel is first to step out of the gap, and to set his eyes on the junkyard before him. It’s not a junkyard as you or me would know it, but more of a stagecoach graveyard. Wheels, horse saddles, chairs, hulls, panels, almost anything that you’d expect to find on an old-style stagecoach can be found here. The trio look around in amazement, as Woody, now revealed to be a small, stout man, enters a small wooden shed in the far corner. -- Gabriel Hunter: Okay, this WASN’T what I had in mind when I planned this trip … Greg Carmichael: Good! Because if it was, I’d be seriously worried! Gabriel Hunter: Meh, I tell you, Spider and the 2 other idiots I’m facing on Panic better be hoping we don’t get out of here anytime soon … Markus Green: I swear, if we’re stuck here overnight … I’ll … I’ll … -- Gabriel chuckles to himself. -- Gabriel Hunter: You’ll what? Markus Green: I’LL SLAP YOU IN THE EAR! -- Gabriel and Greg both burst out in fits of laughter, as a cooling-down Markus looks on, bewildered. -- Markus Green: Hey, what are you laughing at? Greg Carmichael: Heh, you. Gabriel Hunter: Yeah … Promise me that you’ll never threaten me again, alright? -- Markus sighs in submission. -- Markus Green: Sorry. -- Suddenly, the voice of Woody calls out from the shed. -- Woody: Hey! Varmits! O’er here! -- His words prompt Gabriel, Greg and Markus, who walk along the purpose-made path, through the various pieces of stagecoach, towards the small shed. The shed’s interior is cluttered with all sorts of modern-day pieces of motoring equipment, including gaskets, oils, and batteries. Gabriel pops his head through the door, and immediately notices the modernised equipment. -- Gabriel Hunter: Hey, I thought you were like … Amishes … And all that? -- Woody turns around, glancing Gabriel with a look of distrust. -- Woody: My business is my own … -- Woody turns back around to a workbench, before handing Gabriel a long, black tube, and brown coloured liquid, bottled up in a grimy, well-used bottle. -- Woody: There, fill yer tank up with that … -- Gabriel once again glances the tube and bottle in confusion. -- Woody: NOW BE GONE WITH YER! -- With Woody’s sudden outburst, Gabriel rushes back through the door of the shed, and along with Markus and Greg, exits the junkyard. -- Markus Green: Hey, what’s with that stuff in your arms!? Gabriel Hunter: Ask no questions and you’ll be told no lies! -- Markus pauses for though, as his cohorts reach Gabriel’s grounded cruiser. Greg immediately hops over into the back seat, as Markus approaches the car also, following Gabriel around to the other side. Gabriel removes the dust cover, before unscrewing the fuel-cap, and sticking one end of the tube into it. He hands the other end to Markus. -- Gabriel Hunter: Here, hold this … Markus Green: Uhh, ok. -- Gabriel unscrews the lid of the bottle, letting loose a ghastly stench of unused motor oil. Then, with care, he pours the majority of it down the tube, and into the motor. However, due to the giddiness of Markus, quite a deal of it ends up on the dusty ground below – a drop of it even landing on Markus’ boot. Once the bottle is completely finished, Gabriel tosses it over his shoulder, and hops into the car. -- Markus Green: Hey! You got some on my shoes! Greg Carmichael: Never mind that! Hurry up and get in! -- Noticing that Gabriel is soon to leave, Markus runs around the back of the car, and hops back into the passenger seat. Gabriel places the key in the ignition, turning it. The engine flickers to life momentarily, before cutting out again. He turns the key again in hope, nothing. Again … Still nothing. -- Markus Green: Uhhh, Gabe … Are we stuck … Gabriel Hunter: WORK YOU F*CKING BITCH! Greg Carmichael: Jeesh … -- Gabriel punches the horn in frustration, sending out a highly startling sound to the Amishes. Gabriel tries the ignition again, this time it starts. -- Greg Carmichael: WOOO! Gabriel Hunter: … Must … Get … Out … Of … HERE! -- And with that, Gabriel thrusts his foot down on the gas pedal, as the car speeds off into the distance. -- -- Fade. --
