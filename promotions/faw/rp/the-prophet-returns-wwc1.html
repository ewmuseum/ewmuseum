The Prophet Returns(WWC1)

23rd January
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The Prophet Returns(WWC1) Crimson Lord Furiously Addictive Wrestling Roleplay #167 Date: 23rd January Location: A State of Mind 'Whether He the quaint savant's power doth hold I know not, Albeit at a thousand stars' birth He is - Quoth I that for reasons to me oblivious August of a granditude of servants is He held, And by plastic consonantry e'en more servants to the host addéd are - Pelf they are, dare I say! Maugre His diurnal seraphic deviltry I say that deviltry - 'tis forsooth deviltry! - Mind not this in scintillating shades clad is; To claim the glore is He suffer'd. «Grant me the fatlings», qouth He, «the fatter the better!», And died they of starvation; They are not slaughtering their fatlings - They are slaughtering 'hemselves. Sith I at time of yester the questions durst ask, And dare I say this burthen weightful was, Wrack of His machine-like motion was I naméd, Tho' blind and fond the jesters rebuilt The machine alike - yet whettéd and dight are its edges...' (We fade in on a blackened room, only a simple floor lamp lights the way to salvation. Next to it, an occupied seat. In that very seat, is a man, relaxed in his cushioned throne as it were. Tis Crimson Lord. He adjusts himself, and reaches over to a table next to him, pulling a cigarette out of a half smoken pack. He lights it up, and sighs. Boredom has begun to set into his large frame. He begins to pull his long, blackened hair behind his ears, and begins to mutter to himself.) Crimson: How can this be? Why him? Why me? Why do I have to find this out...at such a crucial moment? Why... (He pauses and opens his eyes after having taken another drag. He grits his teeth and exhales the smoke from his lungs.) Crimson: To those who are set to oppose me, Sunday night, I return to the TV set, the birthplace of this…this…revolution, known as Professional Wrestling. I return to the hallowed halls of hardcore, but, the only thing is…I can safely say that the two of you won’t be returning to continue your little shadow wars, like fro and foamy have ongoing as we speak. I know this for a fact. I’m here; this is the moment…to take every one of you the [bleep] out, once and for all. I’ll make you wish that you were never born. (He continues to stare straight into the camera, then looking down at his balled up fist, then, back up to the camera. His eyes have changed, and his facial expression is less bleak. A little livelier, but still ever so dark.) Crimson: I love this business...hard to believe right? I LOVE this business (Crimson grins, and continues to stare into the camera.) Crimson: I think I've been hiding the truth from myself all these years, always trying to act as if I'm the poor, oppressed wrestler rebelling against the establishment...trying to make people feel pity for me, hoping they would accept me...I do so badly want to be accepted in this world, want a shoulder to cry on, a person to hug, a person to love...People are so very right about me, I am sad, I am weak, I am pathetic, I am worthy of their pity...My pride has been holding me back all this time, keeping me pinned down like stakes through my skin...But I am free now... Thanks to the FAW...thanks to you Lisa, you piece of shit... You have shown me the way... I have always loathed people like those who serve their chemical pariahs. Lisa. They hated me because I was different, they mocked me because of my appearance, they pointed fingers at me because I didn't dress as they had wanted me to...But all that is over, for you, the underachievers, have enlightened me, shown me the path...I cannot believe how blind I was, why could I not see it myself? I...I... I can't believe how easy you are making this. Is this what you wanted? To break me down? Sorry Lisa, it didn't work....try again. (Crimson turns his back to the camera, and goes back to sitting in his chair.) Crimson: It was amusing though, I did really enjoy seeing you ball up your little fists in anger at my words. A little boy, a frightened little boy? For the love of god Fro, for the love of god Foamy, for the love of god, you really are reaching...Do I honestly look like a man who cares what people think of him? No, and I think the two of you know that. You know that is not true, but you hope to elicit some emotion from me, anger, hate, pain. You want to find a weakness in me. Admirable, try I must say. But you did speak some truth; I am not the type of person who waits for my chance... I do not sit back and play company man. I take what I want... (Crimson looks from behind his chair, eyeing the camera slightly.) Crimson: When I want... Do I fail? Of course I do. I admitted that, thank you for repeating my words... Understandable of course...Since you have no [bleep]ing thing to say, you simply repeat my words, good job, let me get you a medal. I admitted my failures to everyone, from Foamy, and Fro, all the way to every last member of FAW's roster…I said it was my fault, my pride, my greed, both led to my downfall. Once again you repeat the same [bleep]ing thing I said. Tell me, at what point during any of your rants was I exactly supposed to see this supposed bright light? At what point was I supposed to just lie down and give up because your words struck a wound so deep that I could never recover? You are nothing more than a flock parrots, repeating what I, and everyone else has said in some hopes of making yourselves look good...I see through you all. I have never seen anything like any you? I fear I must agree with you. Never before have I seen someone cling so tightly to their pillars of idiocy. Here is an idea, when responding to something I say, try to not use things I have just admitted as reasons why I am weak. It's not helping. But it did make me think. Your actions, why would you spend so much time rehashing what I just said? Could it really be, could you Skippy, really have nothing better to say than to repeat everyone's very own words? You are a walking contradiction, one minute you are repeating people’s words, trying to use their admissions as a target, the next you say that everyone else makes no sense. Which is it Skip…old friend? Tell me, which is it? You can't have both... It just doesn’t work that way... (Crimson slowly turns back, the back of his seat blocking our view of him.) Crimson: But being a contradiction seems to be exactly what you want to be. After all, my words meant nothing, right? Every single [bleep]ing thing I said was just me talking out of my ass, right? I am no puppet master, and you do not dance...and yet. I see you dancing quite lively right now Skip. One thing said by me, and once have you appeared before a camera to dispute it, why is that? Why do you protest so short and so silently? Why get so hot and bothered about a few words from a darkly clad real true to life monster? Your words, not mine. Why not shrug it off and walk away? It really would be a simple thing to do, to rise above, and yet. You can't do it, can you Skip? You need this, You need me, just like you need the rest of the mindless souls here in FAW...if you only knew why you need me old friend... Yes Skip, you need us. You said so yourself, you have no success story, but you want to have one, you need to defeat us, you need to be able to tell the story of how you slayed the mighty Crimson, and all those below him. Yes, your sob story was quite touching, but please Skip, stop talking out of your ass. What do you know of my childhood? Nothing has been said by me, until earlier, and yet you act like you know everything about it, how can that be? Ahh, what is that, another chink in the armor. You are starting to resemble Swiss cheese. My doing. I have chipped away what little armor you had left. I exposed the weak little old man beneath... Smoke and Mirrors Skip, that is all your act really is. Smoke and Mirrors. Your 'facts' and 'arguments' don't have a leg to stand on, your verbal bullets have once again bounced in the dirt before my feet. You said it yourself, we are a lot alike. You feel pain. I relish it. I welcome it, like an old friend returning from a long voyage... (He gets up out of his chair, and pulls his hair out of his face, staring a hole into the camera.) Crimson: I'm holding nothing back. I expect no less than that. I've got nothing to lose. But, I do have everything to gain. Everyone, don't lie to yourselves...Men against men, beasts against beasts...[bleep]…FAW might have finally gotten something right... (Crimson sighs, but more or less sarcastically.) Crimson: If only I could get back to the days when I still felt alive inside...the days when I cared about this business, had a real passion for it, rather than an uncontrollable bloodlust that keeps me chained to this burden...the days when I cared enough about the other people to not leave them shadows of their former selves, days when spilling blood was not a necessity. I was a battle-hardened soldier that the people within this business feared...feared, but never respected. I could do what I want, when I wanted. The person you see before you today was not created by me...EVERYONE, AND EVERYONE LIKE YOU PEOPLE created me. You cried out for more blood, more carnage, demanded I land just one more vicious move, launch into one more assault upon a victim. And, now I am shunned, for simply becoming what you wanted me to become. Any love I had for the sport has completely vanished without a trace and instead, the vacant hole that was left where it used to be is became filled with a lust for blood, violence, and destruction. I will not go off on a rant about how I am the only one who truly perceives the world as it is, instead of how I want it to be. I have a grasp on our reality like no other because I have been handed nothing. In this respect Lisa...you, and I are alike. If only you knew how much alike. We were handed nothing, but unlike you, I did not sit back and cry, I did not look into a mirror and hate the being that looked back at me. I stepped in, I took what I wanted. You blame me for being something you can never hope to be... I reflect upon life, and I laugh. Laugh at how stupid and naive I was. At one point...I swore to myself that the day this sport began to change me...the day that this world of wrestling made me into someone or something that I had feared...that that very same day would be when I walked out on it for good, no matter what the situation, I would walk out that door...FOREVER. That day has long since come and gone, but...here I still stand. Why you ask? Quite simple people, because I cannot control it anymore. That need for blood that was instilled within me by the people in this business, the wrestlers, the promoters, the fans…they are the very thing that now drives me week in and week out. I feel pain, I feel sorrow, but they simply do not matter. My cravings spilled over, and I was hated for it...now that they are controlled. I am still hated for it. So you see, I am used to being hated, disliked... Which is why I keep saying, I don't want or need your acceptance, I have my acceptance...that is all I need. It is time you two were made accountable for your actions. You created me, if I left, you would win. You would continue to think that you can create monsters, and then shut them out. It is an age old saying 'He who fights with Monsters take care, lest they thereby become monsters themselves'. You fight in this business long enough, you spill enough blood, and you begin to change. You grow cold, indifferent, you no longer care. Perhaps you seek love from people, perhaps you want these fans to chant your name in unison like some holy mantra... I will not stop you two from being youselves...in fact... (Crimson pops his neck, and scratches his shoulder.) Crimson: I will help you... Every hero, no matter how tragic, needs a villain. The lot of you have laid a story or two before us all, told us about your trials and tribulations, I could almost hear violins in the background playing a sad melody to accompany your tales. But Lisa...you are not a bard, you can tell your stories, expect to gain sympathy, but let me warn you in advance, it will make no difference. Set aside your respective egos for a moment and think, will you telling me and the rest of this roster that mommy and daddy never loved you really help you in the long run? Will it really make a difference how many times you say that Rza or anyone else never gave you a chance? At least make it convincing for us, break down and cry a little, because watching you tell the story and not let a single emotion surface makes it quite hard to swallow this story of yours... But... I will still help you... You need someone to play your villain, I will take over the role that people such as I, have assumed time and again. You want me to walk out and hear the boos of the crowds, fine by me, as I have said, it really makes no difference. No amount of cheering will help you when the blood is slowly seeping from your bodies, staining the mat a deep crimson. That is the moment I look forward to, to watch the viscous liquid flow freely, to watch your eyes roll into the back of your heads as your lungs struggle to hold on to that final gasp of air... (He smiles a wicked smile.) Crimson: Breathe...breathe.... I SAID BREATHE, we are not done yet. Not by a long shot. Breathe...no need to conserve your respective strengths… (Crimson leans against the backing of his chair, looking down at the ground, yet still focused on the camera.) Crimson: Have you ever experienced a moment like that…EITHER OF YOU? No, not a moment of pain, not a simple prick that hurts but fades, but a pain so bad it causes you to curse the very day you were born? When your cross that line, and want nothing more than to feel the cold kiss of death, and sink into the eternal slumber. It takes a lot to get there, it takes time, patience but most of all resolve. I have that within me, I will unleash that upon every the both of you... All your falsehoods... ...all your lies... They cannot save you at that moment. ...BOOM... With every word that you two scumbags say to me, with every breath taken...you breathe life into your end. Every single action taken against me only makes me stronger...if for no other reason, simply because it doesn't kill me...and that's what you will have to do to get me away from this sport. This sport that I truly do hate so much. I may be in danger of falling, my fingers clutching the edge of the cliff. But, until that day comes, you will still be able to find me somewhere around this godforsaken business. I have not reached the top yet... I would love for nothing more than for you, Skippy, to actually be able to put up a half-way decent battle against me, however I somehow find myself absolutely certain that such will not be the case. Either I will dominate you in the ring, beating you down until you can no longer remain on your own two feet, or I will tear down the walls of your mind, slashing at you with verbal attacks that will decimate you. Call it what you want, the fact of the matter remains the same. I long for retribution and this match ahead of us…and extra baggage in it are my vehicle of opportunity to acquire such. You want to break free? So do I, let go of it all and just forget my entire existence... (He rears his head up, looking at ceiling.) Crimson: I want to break free...I'm almost there. Just one more inch... Make no mistakes... I am your end... ...I promise you one thing though... You will have your moments of glory... You will go out with a... (BANG! As Crimson was about to finish his last sentence, the light in the room turn out, and a singular light shins down from a ceiling height window. It connects with Crimson’s eyes…his pale blue eyes staring into the camera. Remorseless.) Crimson: All passions have a phase when they are merely disastrous, when they drag down their victim with the weight of stupidity—and a later, very much later phase when they wed the spirit, when they 'spiritualize' themselves. Formerly, in view of the element of stupidity in passion, war was declared on passion itself, its destruction was plotted; all the old moral monsters are agreed on this: il faut tuer les passions. The most famous formula for this is to be found in the New Testament, in that Sermon on the Mount, where, incidentally, things are by no means looked at from a height. There it is said, for example, with particular reference to sexuality: 'If thy eye offend thee, pluck it out.' Fortunately, no Christian acts in accordance with this precept. Destroying the passions and cravings, merely as a preventive measure against their stupidity and the unpleasant consequences of this stupidity—today this itself strikes us as merely another acute form of stupidity. We no longer admire dentists who 'pluck out' teeth so that they will not hurt any more. To be fair, it should be admitted, however, that on the ground out of which Christianity grew, the concept of the 'spiritualization of passion' could never have been formed. After all the first church, as is well known, fought against the 'intelligent' in favor of the 'poor in spirit.' How could one expect from it an intelligent war against passion? The church fights passion with excision in every sense: its practice, its 'cure,' is castration. It never asks: 'How can one spiritualize, beautify, deify a craving?' It has at all times laid the stress of discipline on extirpation. But an attack on the roots of passion means an attack on the roots of life: the practice of the church is hostile to life. I have removed myself from this. Chosen the side not mentioned, taken the path not walked upon. I have rid myself of all emotion, torn away the last remaining fibers that bound me to humanity. There are no more barriers for me to cross, all I have in common with the uncontrollable and insane, the vicious and the evil, all the mayhem I have caused and my utter indifference to it I have not surpassed. My pain is constant and sharp and I do not hope for a better world for anyone. In fact, I want my pain to be inflicted upon others, I want no one to escape, but even after admitting this, there is no catharsis, my punishment continues to elude me, and I gain no deeper knowledge of myself, no further information can be extracted by my telling. (Crimson’s eyes lower, in a serious look.) Crimson: Who is to blame for my pain? Who is to blame for my suffering? No one. Well, that is not quite true, no one is to blame, but myself. I have inflicted this pain upon myself; I have run the knife across my wrist and watched the blood flow freely... (Another light shines down upon Crimson’s heavily tattooed arm. But we can see markings. He looks at his scarred up wrist, then back to the camera with a perplexed look. The second light disappears.) Crimson: Pity? Skip..you think I want your pity? Compassion; mercy; commiseration; condolence; sympathy, fellow-suffering; fellow-feeling. Pity, Sympathy, Compassion. I don't want your pity…any of you. Pity is for the weak, pity is for those people, who want, nay, need to be accepted. Skip...your feelings towards me simply do not matter. Yes, yes, I know, hard to believe, but what you say to me, what you do to me, none of it matters, for I simply do not care... There is an idea of a realistic demon. Some shadowy phantom haunting through this life, sifting through the static day in and day out. You can shake my hand, you can feel flesh, see sweat upon my brow, read emotion on my face. Perhaps, Skip you can even compare yourself to me, our lives may very well be comparable. Or so you think. So I let you think. I have a name, no, not Crimson. My name is Benny...Benny Juventud. I have a name, I have a life. But despite it all, despite the fact that you can see me, feel me, even hear me, I simply am not there. I was born Twenty-seven years ago, no, no mystical birth, at least at the time. No, I did not arise from the ashes, no, I am somewhat normal. Well, as normal as I can be under the circumstances. My childhood was special…per say, certain life changing events, some thought of visits from supernatural creatures, I grew up in the inner city, and from what I remember...I did though have a loving father and mother. Four brothers, two sisters. I had no dog, I had few friends, I had what I would say, some semblance of normalcy. I was Benny Juventud. I was human, I was flesh and bone and soul. I was alive... What about now you ask? Am I dead? No, I am not dead. Benny Juventud is dead. Benny died when he was ten years old. Benny Juventud lies dead buried beneath the cold confines of the earth. Benny was human. I am not Benny Juventud, I am “The True Prophet”…I am Crimson. Principle of Evil made flesh. But to you, Skip, I am nothing, I am a mere obstacle, something you can overcome, some tattooed freak. This is not a game. The sooner you realize this, the sooner you can begin to comprehend who I am. What I am. Benny Juventud was a bright eyed youth who knew nothing of the outside world, I am a battle hardened warrior, a soldier. I have seen more broken bodies in a year than most people see in a lifetime...I have felt more anguish and suffering in six months, than you you will know in all of your pathetic existence... (The light extends to show only Crimson’s face…he smiles, confident. But his face, it seems as is always has been...dark, almost like man who has seen, felt, and heard too much.) Crimson: I know this. Because Benny Juventud knew this. Benny was weak, a flash of sorrow swept over him like a tidal wave, he did what any normal human would do, he reached out and grabbed the life preserver I threw him. I saved him from certain destruction; I alone am responsible for the man you see before you this day. I am Crimson...yet, sadly I am Benny Juventud... He thought it would be so simple. He would be saved and could go back to his 'normal' life. He thought I would let him escape me, he struggled. He fought me; he even tried to destroy me. But I prevailed, I won. In the end, I ALWAYS win. Even now, as I speak these words, I can imagine all my rumble my opponents, listening, searching for faults within the web I spin for you. Search for them if you like, you will find none. I speak only the truth. Try it sometime, it really is quite refreshing. I am proud of my creation, he has performed so very well since the day my icy clutches ensnared him for all eternity. This will be the biggest challenge. This will be the final challenge... Cocky? Is that what you think I am? Nothing but a cocky punk who dresses up to intimidate. Am I getting it about right? Is that the picture you have at me Skip? Now before you go off and spout off words you will only regret later on, let me pose you this question. Why do I dress like this if I know ridicule is all that I will receive? Am I a demonic lord? No, that's not it. I do not have any plans for world domination; I do not plan to relocate hell to earth. My goals are simple, I want to do the same thing, Skip, and each and every one of our peers want to do on a daily basis. I want to survive, pure and simple. If any of you dare to have the need to find out…ask yourself Skip, ask yourself Rza…all I’ve ever had the need to do is survive… Is that really so wrong? Is the will for survival reason enough to call me evil? Of course not, not that I really care what labels you choose to bestow upon me, but I do so enjoy putting holes in your already flimsy argument, making your so-called solid wall of insults resemble Swiss cheese. Was I expected to just die? Was I expected to watch another become no better than the rest of you? Was I to let a poor, innocent young boy wither away and cease to exist? No, I saved two lives in one fell swoop, and now you all have the unmitigated gall to judge me? Who gave you any of you that right? Who gave you worthless pukes the right to point a finger at me and judge me? (Crimson still stares into the camera, a demonic smile crossing his face.) Crimson: One finger points at me, but three point back at the two of you... Examine your own lives, your own existences. Are they clean? Do you not have any skeletons in the closet? Oh, that's right, you are somehow perfect, and how easily I forget these things. You both act as if your perfection personified, with the bodies of an Adonis, with the minds of a genius. You look to preside over this world like a titan, crushing all who dare cross your path. But the cold claw of reality will slash at your neck, exposing you to reality... Darkness is rising. Like some cloud on the horizon it is slowly forming, slowly building. I am no rider of the apocalypse, but I will bring about your ultimate destruction. Deny it, try to escape it, but reality will slowly set in. Doubt will begin to form in the back of your minds, placed by me, it will grow, doubt will turn to fear, fear will lead to all of your respective demise. When you two sheep walk down that ring, your minds will have been broken down by the verbal attacks that I launch. You cannot hope to escape, you cannot hope to contain. Round two awaits us Skip...enjoy the time you have left... (The lights slowly come back on, with Crimson standing, arms folded…staring into the camera. A door opens, and into the light...a beautiful woman appears, as she comes into full view...tis Phoenix. She lies her hand on Crimson's muscled arm, and kisses his cheek, a look of worry etched across her face.) Phoenix: Are you still worried about what might happen if the stories are true? Are you still worried about...him? (Crimson looks over to his wife, and frowns.) Crimson: Why should I be worried about him? He is meaningless to me now. He can do nothing to stop me anymore. Our blood tie...cannot make me falter. (Phoenix smiles a bit.) Phoenix: Then let it be a faded memory. If he is so meaningless, let him fade, and never know the truth...about your pasts...your connections...your oneness... Crimson: Worry not... Phoenix: I would...but with you...it's hard not to... (She puts her arms around his waist, and holds him from the side, he puts an arm around her, and looks back at the camera, and she nuzzles herself in closer to her giant of a husband.) Crimson: Opposition to my reign. From Skippy to you Rza. From a hated enemy...to a young upstart. And everyone I shalt face in-between, and after. Remember these words when I am standing above you broken bodies. (He pauses.) Crimson: Veni, Vidi, Vici...I came, I saw, I conquered...Blessed Be... (The screen being to grow blackened, as Crimson stares straight into the camera, laughing. Phoenix looks up at him, and manages a smile. We slowly fade, the eyes of the soldier of the damned, “The True Prophet”, “The Psychotic Enigma”…Crimson, burn into the screen)
