In the church Dome #2

Dec 18
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
In the church Dome #2 Rzarector Furiously Addictive Wrestling Roleplay #104 Date: Dec 18 Location: In the church (The screen opens to a pure black. A narrators voice is heard in the background, a dark cold voice.) Narrator: Time has come once again, for the forces of good and evil to fight to see who is the better. Evil always wins without the will of God. Yet the good knows throughout there defeats, God will return one day to make sure that they win. God is the sword in the fist fight. He is the spirit that has no shape. He is the wind, air, and the trees. He is everything, for he has created everything. (As the narrator is speaking pictures of trees and several other things flash across the screen.) Narrator: At one point in time, a long time ago, there were no sins. No sins at all. Then one day Satan made them sin. That was it, that was what set them off. A perfect world ruined over the Devil. Now we must repent, that is all he asks of every single one of you. That is it, that is all. (The screen then shows several people praying in churches. Preachers are preaching as a dark howl is heard in the background.) Narrator: God told us never to worship icons, like the cross. For we should worship him. We should worship him. The following message from me, comes straight from the church. Here a young wicked man is seen talking to a priest. The priest see's through the evilness and looks at the good side of the boy. That is what God does. God has patience, yet God is very jealous. The date is December the 18. The time is 4:40 am. This is an interview with Rzarector. The blessed one who can communicate with God. Rzarector is unbeleivable. (The screen goes blank as nothing is heard for a second. The screen then opens up to show the red front doors of a huge church. The camera swings back around to show Rzarector, dressed in his black hooded robe. His green eyeballs are glowing out along with his white fangs. He hisses at the door as it creaks open to show a priest on the oppossite side. Rzarector hisses once more as the camera crew follows him into the church. There you see rows of benches, all red like the color of blood. Bibles are scattered throughout the floor. Rzarector hisses as he talks in his deep hideous voice.) Rzarector: Priest, since I've been away, what has happened here? Everything is disorganized, and the benches look like there covered in blood. Why is the bibles all thrown around and not stacked in the shelf over there? (The priest sighs as he looks down at the ground.) Priest: All is forgotten. The word of God just seems so senseless now and days. I can sit there and preach what I know exists. Yet people still won't listen. And as for this mess, it was after a sermon one day. People showed up, and they all left before I even finished. Like they were just fed up with what had happened. So I stopped. I went and started praying but then through myself into a violent rage. I cut my arms open, and through the bibles and my blood everywhere. It was a horrible sight. So now I closed the church. Rzarector: You did what? Priest: I closed the church. Noone cares anymore, its just an old run down church. No one will show up anymore, noone donates to help us pay the bills. They just keep saying, you just want money. That is not the case. I sit around 24 hours a day writing down God's word to teach it to everyone. Now they just don't care. (Rzarector hisses very loud.) Rzarector: After all our blood, sweat, and tears into this church. Now it is all gone? You are just closing it? Why do you think I was sent here to Earth? To make people turn from sinners to pure soul. Now that is lost by a long shot. We have to reopen, that is what we are obliged to do. We can't let down God, I've let him down so many times before, but not again. (The Priest now has tears in his eyes.) Rzarector: Why are you crying? Priest: I have sinned. I gave up on God. I know he will lead them back here, it is just a matter of time. Rzarector: Don't every cry. You will never see me cry. That is not what I do. I keep my feelings on the inside because God watches out and talks to me through the bad times. I used to get picked on to the point where I was the outcast, and that led me to wicked ways. Now I am alone, in my own world. I am no longer the outcast, I am the incast with myself. I will never let wicked get to me again. Priest: That is good to talk like that. We must get this cleaned up. (Rzarector once again looks around the church as he see's the huge cross with jesus on it tilted. He hisses loudly as he runs and fixes it. Suddenly a God sends him a message.) *Through the rough and the good times, the band of brothers will stick together. I've seen people burn crosses, burn churches, and burn pictures of me. Yet you my son, will lead them into a better time. Clean the church, get it ready for a whole new opening. Tell them of my coming, for it will not be long. * (Rzarector gives a smile-hiss as he keeps looking around. He immediately turns the cross figure back straight, and heads straight for the bibles. The priest grabs a rag and some dust spray and starts waxing the benches. For several hours they sat there and cleaned and cleaned. Finally after 2 hours the cleaning was done. The church was beautiful, and had a very nice smell to it. Rzarector and the Priest sat there in a daze at how it looked. Rzarector knew God had blessed it. Now it was time to reopen it. The Priest walked outside and put the sign back around that said, Open, now this Sunday people would come. The priest curious to why Rzarector was here, sat down on the bench with him.) Priest: So what made you come all the way out here Rza? Rzarector: I have a match this Sunday. A very strange match, one that is not at all normal. Yet I am competing in it. It's like there giving me a main event push. Yet that is one reason I came here, for you to give me a prayer. Another reason is, God sent me a strange dream about Armageddon last night. Priest: Armageddon? Are you sure? Rzarector: Yes, it was just how he said it would happen. Everyone disappeared. It was very mad, dead bodies everywhere. The beast stalked the Earth. God was taking his angels in for battle. The wickedness of men ran throughout the Earth. Yet something was very strange. Priest: What was that? Rzarector: It was in the year 2097. And the strangest thing about it was the fact that he did not take me. Apparently after I left FAW, sex, drugs, and alcohol got to me. I was a wreck. Priest; You've been that way before, God saved you. He would have saved you again. Rzarector: I know that. The point of the dream was for me to warn everyone. I keep having these dreams, and everytime I'm the worst evilness in the dream. Yet God always helps me. Priest: He is trying to show you the light. He is making you better through the dreams. At first they were scary right? Rzarector: Yes. Priest: Now they're not? Rzarector: No there not. Priest: Well see, he has made you stronger. Now when that day comes and your on Earth. You can show everyone of his arrival. That will help his army. You understand? Rzarector: Yes it all makes sense, yet I don't see why I would remain on the Earth. Priest: That is what he sent you here for. To tell everyone, and the day the evil beast comes to Earth. You will be the one to lead the Earth humans against him. You will win to. Trust me, it is all in the process of the bible. (Rzarector gets up out of the bench and begins walking around the church. He looks at how clean it is and continues his journey throughout it. He walks to the front, where the red lit candles are at, and bows down and prays. He then hisses very loudly while he is praying. The priest knows he is talking to God.) *Talking to God.* Rzarector: Why do I have to be the one? What have I done. I am getting better. *Yet you still have a long way to go. I saved you once, and I will not let anything happen to you. Beleive in my words.* Rzarector: I beleive in them like the snow in the winter, yet I'm scared. I have never been so scared in my life. *The time will come, but not right now. The years will roll by quick, now you have to get to do what you want to do. Wrestling is your hobby, you must do that. Have fun while you can, but don't forget me. Because I will be back with a fury, and all that is lost, will perish in hell.* Rzarector: Yes. (Rzarector says Amen aloud as the Priest knows it is over. Rzarector stands up and walks back over to the bench.) Rzarector: I have a match this Sunday at Evil Intentions. It is a dome match. A very strange match. Four lumberjacks on the outside, four people on the inside. Yet it is inside a dome cage. Very odd wouldn't you say? Yet I think it will be very fun. I love competition. Priest: I know, what did God tell you? Rzarector: Nothing, he just told me to be strong. Priest: Did you listen to him? Rzarector: Yes, anyways, about this dome match..... (The priest interrupts.) Priest; Rza, you can't keep running from your fears. You have never feared anything except Armageddon. Now why do you fear that? Rzarector: I don't fear anything but that because I know it is going to happen. I don't fear snakes, because I may never even get bit by a snake. You get what I'm saying? Priest: So do you fear the dome? Rzarector: No, I know it is going to happen. It won't effect my health that much. Like I said, I like competition. Yet this competition is different in my eyes. I'm fighting evil, and I'm the good. One day I will ressurrect them all to the eternal tortures of hell. Priest: You shouldn't talk like that. That will get you arressted or in a looney bin. Rzarector: I'm half way there, no stopping now. Ya know what I'm sayin? Priest: Yes, I saw you on TV the other night. Who was that that you were talking to? Rzarector: Oh, noone. Just someone from a long time ago. Priest: I see, now what were you saying about the Dome match? Rzarector: Well I'm in a main event match now. Which basically I think FAW are looking to see how I can compete with them. I think I can do farely well. I mean you know? I'm not the worst athlete, and I can take a bump or two. I've been to hell before. Now I have God as my backing. Priest: Yes, I see what you mean. What do you think about your opponents, are they actually worth it? Rzarector: Very worth it, I've already beaten half of them. Now I want to see if I can beat the best in the FAW. It will be truly remarkable if I could do anything of that kind of nature here. I've never been a main eventer til I got here. Then I worked out as hard as I could. Put my mind to the test, and here I am. Former Tag Team Champion with Renegade, who I also have to fight. Priest: You have to fight Renegade? He seemed like a nice guy. Rzarector: Yeah, well not anymore. He dissed me,and dissed me bad. Now one day I will see him burning in hell. Isn't that right? Anyways, Renegade, after those words, I'm going to rip your head off. (Rzarector eyes start turning red as he keeps talking.) Rzarector: Then I'm going to sit there and stab a leg of a table down your neck. You understand me? We used to be about brother love, now it is brother hate. For you, have messed with the wrong person. Very wrong person. Priest: Hmmm, losing your temper again. Remember God's message to keep you at tone. Remember. Rzarector: I will, but right now I have to go, urgent business. Ya know? I can't sit around here all day. Priest: I understand. Rzarector: Good, anything else? Priest: Yes one more thing. (Rzarector hisses.) Priest: If you ever feel like talking to anyone, you know where to reach me at, right? Rzarector: Yes, I live here to. Priest: Well I wish you the best of luck on your dome match. Rzarector: Yes. Priest; And may God always be with you. Rzarector: I am the Dark Fallen Angel. He is with me, for I work his devious deeds. Can't no one complain about that. Priest; Well, your words are true, yet you have forgotten one thing. God is good, not evil. Rzarector: I never forgot that, but every good thing must do evil things to cleanse the world. Priest; That is right, maybe you'll learn that someday. Rzarector: Maybe. Priest: Anyways, bye. (The camera man motions 5 minutes to Rzarector.) Rzarector: This Sunday, 9th, Bomber, Lee Smith, Great One, Renegade, you all are going to feel the pain that i Have felt. For the world has dealt me a cruel hand, and now I'm going to use that same hand, to unleash something cruel on you. (Rzarector gives a loud hiss as he says in his most wicked voice.) Rzarector: For you'll see me before you die, and you'll see me after..................... (Rzarector pauses.) Rzarector: When I ressurrect you to the eternal lake of FIRE!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! (The screen cuts off as Rzarector is hissing very loudly and his green eyes are glowing out. He then flashes his white fangs at the camera as it goes black.)
