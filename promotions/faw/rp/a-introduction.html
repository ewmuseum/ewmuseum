A Introduction Joey Johnson Furiously Addictive Wrestling Roleplay #1 Date: Tuesday 15th October 2002 Location: Somewhere =[What is this life all about? How is it possible for a man who has been beaten and bruised for the past 6 years in this business able to continue on in his everyday life? President Buddy obviously believes that it is possible... Joey Johnson has signed a contract with the Furiously Addictive Wrestling promotion. What a great day for sports entertainment. What a great day for the fans of FAW... what a great day. Period!]= =[When you see Joey Johnson walking around the streets... run up to him. Ask him for an autograph. Tell him your thoughts about his career. I mean, he is a former DRWF Turmoil champion, a former IWF International champion, a former IWF Gimmick champion, a former SWF Television champion, a former X-W Tag Team champion. He is a champion's champion. He is the man. It's that simple.]= =[When you see Joey Johnson in a FAW ring, kicking ass like he has always done, smile and appreciate a real wrestler. Appreciate the fact that you have been graced with 'wrestling' and not some bullshit that can be mistaken for 'wrestling'. There is NO SUCH THING as fighting. Not in this day and time. Everyone wants to be a Sports Entertainer. You want to know what I say to that? 'Fuck that!'! That's exactly what I think. Why be a Sports Entertainer when you can be an International Icon?!]= =[This isn't a joke... Joey Johnson is coming. I hope that you're all ready. It's about time to kick this shit off right. FAW... get ready for the Takeover. This isn't your average superstar... this is me. I am... Joey Johnson!]=
