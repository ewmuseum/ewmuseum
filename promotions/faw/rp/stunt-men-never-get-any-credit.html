Stunt men never get any credit...

Stunt men never get any credit... Flash Furiously Addictive Wrestling Roleplay #162 Date: 21/1/03 Location: Some Hollywood Studio Witherton Manor, unknown exact location 21:00 The main hall is bustling with life, filled with the sound of people chatting amongst themselves. The odour of alcohol hangs heavy in the air. The hall is huge; hundreds of guests have fitted in quite easily. The floor is made from shiny white marble. It is possible to just about hear some piano music playing in the background, music of a very ‘posh’ type. Almost without exception, the men are wearing black tuxedos and it seems as though all the women are trying to upstage one another with their extravagant dresses. Some people are dancing, many are just talking, but everybody is drinking. While most people are scattered into small groups of social activity, there is one group much larger than the rest. At the centre of this group stands the host, and therefor the most popular man around; Norman Witherton. His white hair is greased back, and the 35 year old host is wearing; you guessed it, a black tuxedo. A snobby smile seems to be stapled to his face and the look is completed by an overly posh monacle resting over his right eye. His beautiful token wife clings to his arm dependantly, almost like she was nothing more than a decoration to him. People are tripping over themselves to get a word in with this guy. Man #1: I say, Norman, where did you get that tuxedo? It really is quite exquisite. Man #2: Yes, rather smashing I’d say. Witherton: It was made to order by my tailor, of course. Poor Jeeves stayed up all night making it, and after all that effort, our slightly dim friend forgot to use my imported silk for the stitching! Imagine that! Everybody laughs, although it seems apparent that they are put-on and foney. Man #1: May I say how full of beauty your wife is tonight? Man #2: I was just about to say so myself. Mrs. Witherton: Well, tha... Witherton: Now, now, dear, they didn’t address you. They addressed me. I hope we don’t have to discuss your role at our soirées again? She sighs reluctantly, obviously unhappy. Mrs. Witherton: No, dear. Witherton:Splendid! Now where... Man #3: Hey, asshole! Everyone around gasps and turns to look at this third man. He is a short-ish man with long black hair, wearing a black tuxedo. He is wearing shades and has a slight sneer across his mouth. Norman Witherton looks at him like he’s insane. Witherton: How dare you! Who are you, and who invited you to my party? Man #3: The name’s Max Danger, and I invited myself yto your crappy party to mingle with your dorky followers. Witherton: Is that so... well, what gives you the right to barge in on our little get-together and insult me? Danger: I don’t like to see people pushing around women... now apologise to your wife. There is a pause, and silence. Witherton: Hahahahaha! Listen, mate, I’ll do whatever the hell I like with my wife, and it’s none of your business. (To wife) Go get me another drink... Now. Danger: I said, leave her alone. Witherton: Oh really? And if I don’t...? Max Danger slowly removes his shades. Danger: I’ll kick your ass. Witherton starts to turn and walk away, but he suddenly swivels around and tries to punch Max in the head. Max ducks it and retaliates with a punch of his own, before grabbing Witherton and throwing him over a table. Max hops over the table and pummels Witherton, however the esteemed host pokes Max in the eye and picks him up before tossing him into a wall. Witherton grabs a sword from the wall, but Max gets up and runs up to him before jumping onto a chair, flipping over Witherton and grabbing the other sword from the wall decoration. The two engage in a sword fight. There are gasps from the crowd as Max draws first blood, but it is only a small gash across the chest and he continues. In the midst of an eerily good looking battle, Witherton kicks Max’s sword out of his hand, and looks to go for the kill. But Max boots him in the gut, puts him in a standing headscissors and grabs the back of Witherton’s underpanys before delivering a devestating Wedgie Piledriver! Director: CUT! CUT! CUT! Flash, what the hell are you doing? ’Max’ ignores the directors pleas. When the dazed actor playing Witherton gets to his knees, ‘Max’ runs right at him and hits him in the temple with a diving knee strike, knocking him out cold. Director: CUT! Flash, you moron, stop it right now! Damn! ’Max’, as played by Flash, looks to the director, shrugging. Flash: What’s wrong? Director: What’s wrong? WHAT’S WRONG?! You just knocked out my star! That was NOT the planned ending to the scene! Did you even listen to me when I was running through the fight sequence? Flash: The SOB was taking something on me. Director: He’s supposed to be doing that! It’s in the script! You were supposed to put your hands up in shock at that point, at which time Witherton has mercy on you. Flash: That wimp? No way! This scene sucks anyway, I was just trying to make it more exciting. Director: This is not a wrestling ring! Take your piledrivers elsewhere, and I suggest you forget about acting, Flash... YOU’RE FIRED! Flash: Thank God, I was getting sick and tired of wearing this wig anyway. It makes my scalp itch. Flash walks off the set, pulling the black wig off his head to reveal some short blond hair. He drops it on the floor. Flash: Who needs acting anyway, huh? Director: That’s right, go back to Flash Studios to make more crappy movies and terrible TV Programs... Just do the movie-going public a favour and stay out of acting, okay? Flash: Forget you, man. I’m a wrestler now; in the arena, people appreciate the fact that I’m superior to them instead of hating me for it like you do. The sound of laughter rings in Flash’s ears as he marches out of the studio, with pride bruised but not broken; Soon enough he’ll get his name up in bright lights... Flash: Stuntmen never get any credit... -End-
