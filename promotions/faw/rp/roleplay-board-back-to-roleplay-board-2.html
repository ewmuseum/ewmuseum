9th Wonder Of Foam — FAW Roleplay, August 2002

August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Furiously Addictive Wrestling Roleplay Board Roleplay Board | Back to Roleplay Board 9th Wonder Of Foam Entitled: X-Rodd and Xander, both missing that X-Factor Written: 1st August 2002 Location: …. This is a paid advertisement by the FWO …. (A black and white film begins, sticks of all kinds are lined up on a table, at the end is a cardboard cut-out of X-Rodd) VO: For years the evil red scourge of the Rod has been an easy one to tackle. (An old man with a moustache, glasses and a suit stands by the table, he picks up the wooden stick and breaks it with his hand.) VO: See, wooden rods have been breakable for years with the mighty hand, metal rods have been also as easy to dispose of. (The man gets a blowtorch and puts it to the metal rod it breaks easily.) VO: Other materials have also been easy to destroy, but now the EVIL! red threat has come up with a new kind of rod. So special is this rod that it is spelled with two D’s. The compound this new kind of “Rodd” is made from is simply called X. No mortal man can break it. (The spectacled man attempts to break the X-Rodd, he struggles but he is unable to do so) VO: So the government worked on many agents found both in nature and in the laboratory and found one thing that could break the X-Rodd, it’s name? Agent Foam. (The 9th Wonder Of Foam appears in fifties style superhero attire he picks up the X-Rodd cut-out and destroys it in one attempt) VO: So now even in the face of new threats you can be sure that there will always be that F-factor to prevent the X-Rodd from making and impact in your life! …. Foamy Hills Mansion …. (The 9th Wonder Of Foam is with Floyd Rotterdam. The 9th Wonder Of Foam is holding the Foamy Finger Of EVIL! and is drinking beer from his “thirst aid” hat with the straws and the holders) Floyd: So Panic last night quite and eventful night for the Foamy One. Xander helps you win in your cage mat… Foamy: Che? Floyd: Excuse me? Foamy: Che? What did you just say Chico? Did you just say that Xander helped me win? Did you imply that Xander and not the Foamy One defeat Apocalypse in that steel cage last night? Floyd: Yes. (The Foamy One slaps Floyd with the Foamy Finger Of EVIL! A small electrical current knocks Floyd to the ground, Foamy grabs the microphone on the way down and continues to talk at Floyd) Foamy: What you failed to see my trembling friend is that I already had that match won. Oh Floyd not on the carpet, we are going to have to get you a nappy aren’t we? See Xander thought that he could come down and help me win, thinking that I would either owe him or that in the eyes of the fans I couldn’t defeat Xander on my own. (Floyd gets back onto his feet, he checks his ass-cheeks and continues) Floyd: Well we have footage that proves that Xander aided your victory and is in fact responsible for that win. …. Panic …. Apoc is going for the door as then Foamy pulls out a portable foam can from his tights and shoots it in the ref’s eyes. As he pulls Apoc from outside the door and back in as he then hits a power bomb to Apocalypse. Shane Riot: He could wins this Apoc is climbing the cage now and is the 9th wonder. On the top both men get to as Xander comes running down the ramp and he goes right for Apocalypse with the ratings sign and hits him knocking him into the cage. Meanwhile while this is going on The 9th Wonder of the foam is out of the cage. The ref then wipes the foam off his eyes seeing him out of the cage. …. End …. (Foamy looks around him) Foamy: What was that? Where did that come from? Yes Floyd you are correct he may have attempted to help me win but there was always the chance that I could still have won on my own two feet. And beside I returned the favour later that evening. …. Panic …. {Wonder swung the chair and hit EDH right in the head knocking him down on the mat. As Xander laid there Wonder pulled him over EDH as Wonder slid out of the ring and tossed the ref into the ring. The ref looked up as he started to slap the ring with the pin attempt.} One.............. Riot: Yes he is going to get it. Two....................... Floyd: Not this way. {Then the hand came down for the three count as Wonder slid back in the ring and grabbed up Xander as he started hitting him with rights and lefts. Xander started to fight back but as he did Wonder grabbed the right arm of Xander and tossed him across the ring. As he returned Wonder hit a foot to the gut of Xander. Wonder then grabbed up the chair and then hit it right across the back of him as Xander then rolled out of the ring as he fell to the floor. Wonder then tossed down the chair as he raised his hands as Xander crawled up the ramp looking at the ring.} Riot: This isn’t right. {Wonder then looked right at Xander as he signalled for the belt as the scene then started to faded. …. End …. (Foamy looks around again) Foamy: Huh? I wish you’d stop doing that Floyd! Anyway he helped me win so I helped him win. Floyd: But why let him win the match and retain the International Title? Foamy: Because not only is Xander’s ass mine but his title is mine too. I am destined to be the next International Champion not Syd or some overweight guy me, the Foamy One, the chosen one! Xander tried to beat me up before the match, during the match and yet still Panic ends as it should every week, with the 9th Wonder Of Foam standing in the middle of that ring, hands in the air, other extremities in the air and with the fans chanting his name. Floyd: So what’s next for the Foamy One? Foamy: You saw Panic didn’t you? I stand tall the victor while Xander crawls up the ramp hurt and bleeding, yet still he remains International champion. So it’s back to the drawing board to perfect my painting, one that no one will ever forget. Floyd: Also this Sunday you face X-Rodd, and old foe, member of the nXn in a straight up singles match what are your thoughts heading into this match? Foamy: My thought turn to the years past and a small company called SHWC, it lasted but only a week. But in that week I won my first World Championship, my opponent? X-Rodd. And that gives me the edge, something that makes me sure I can do the job again. Floyd: Aren’t you concerned that without the incentive of a Championship Title you may not care enough to pursue a victory? Foamy: After World Foam Wrestling the only incentive I need to kick a member of the nXn twelve shades of dead is for them to be near a wrestling ring and it just so happens that this will be the case Sunday night. Have a Foam Filled Day!
