(Panic #1) Profusion Furiously Addictive Wrestling Roleplay #223 Date: 20/01/03 Location: House, in Houston Texas <\/^'War is never good… but in some circumstances it is imperative.'^\/> --------- 'The Gift and The Curse' The Children Of The Reign Intifada (Shaking off) - the Palestinian uprising during the late 1980s and early 90s in the West Bank and the Gaza Strip, areas occupied by Israel since 1967. A vehicular accident that killed four Palestinians in the Gaza Strip in Dec., 1987, sparked immediate local protests that rapidly spread to the West Bank. The violence was marked by stone-throwing and the use of homemade explosive devices on behalf of the Arabs, and the use of tear gas, rubber bullets, and home demolition by Israeli troops attempting to quell the popular resistance. The conflict led to an Israeli military crackdown and the stagnation of the Arab economies in the occupied territories, but with the gradual establishment of Palestinian self-rule, beginning with the accord between Israel and the Palestine Liberation Organization in 1993, the violence eased significantly but has increased greatly in more recent times. Like a lost child we wonder endlessly Victims of the inadequate campaign Where truth is manipulated and fortunes run scarce Avoirdupois Arafat consumes the future promised with a glass of champagne While those he betrayed lie in the streets unaided and maimed The people he’s sworn to protect and nurture disappear in vain Yes these streets search for the peaceful resolution But give birth to the children of the reign They seek for something to believe in Someone or something to rescue them from the bane While Arafat delivers more for the fire with his shovel of treason We fight his war his vendetta of disdain Our anguish unheard the echo of potential unfounded He ships us like exports to satisfy his pain Our trust cheated we seek the son of Gandhi Passive resistance shall deliver us from the storm, we are children of the reign The enemy we’re led to believe is but a shadow of Arafat’s perfidy Where his henchmen rape us of our young and send them to Gaza on the train Another future so bright eclipsed by hatred and detest Another son, daughter, and cousin but a tangible sold like cocaine Our beliefs obscured by the mist of emotion Our self consciousness stolen along with our domain Set fire to the fields they gorge themselves upon For they will hear our voice the violated children of the reign. [In India it was Mahatma Gandhi the political and spiritual leader during India's struggle with Great Britain for home rule. Gandhi was an advocate of passive resistance during the time Great Britain occupied India. This man was imprisoned many times for the sake of his people. No he didn’t strap bombs to kids. No he didn’t take rifles and slaughter British children. This was a man who influenced the world through his peaceful demonstrations. No bombs, no guns, just heart. I don’t know how anyone couldn’t admire a man like this. You can tell this man loved his country. This man was very influential in bringing his country independence in 1947 not with arms but with heart. In the United States it was Dr. Martin Luther King Jr. during the civil rights era. A man who not only advocated a better tomorrow for all African Americans for America itself. After organizing many peaceful marches and demonstrations he finally received the respect and dignity his people deserved. No he didn’t blow up a school or an embassy. Again he fought with heart. That was his weapon. Now we fast forward from the civil rights era to the era of now. Right now we have mid east violence running rampant. And while I continue to watch the future of the Palestinians and Israelis go up in smoke I ask myself where is the Palestinian leadership? Where is the leadership that will guide its people and save them from the conflict that will consume its promise? Where is the leadership that loves its people and will risk life or limb for them. Where is the leadership that will rescue them from extreme poverty and disrepute to prosperity and respect. Because right now I see no leadership willing to help its people. All I see if fat man sitting on millions doing NOTHING for Palestine’s children. The President of the United States already called for a Palestinian state for its people but the violence must cease before the world will respect the cause. The intifada that has been waged on the Israeli government will continue to be ignored until peaceful opposition is in place. What I see on television isn’t freedom fighting. What I see is the rape of the dignity of the people who want peace and their state. I do sympathize with the Palestinian people who DO want peace and a resolution to the situation. But this will only be achieved once everyone lays down their arms and begin to talk peace.] Now staying on the issue of politics I myself waged an Intifada a long time ago. Nothing like the aforementioned by similar. The purpose was reform. Reform in the FAW. The way I came up in this business was in my opinion a travesty and will always be a black eye to this federation. While many people argued that it came with the territory I disagreed. I had seen many people in the past lose their rightful position because they weren’t participants in the kiss @$$ policy that many used that most likely ran this federation into the ground. But what happened was me. It wasn’t until the time I became a superstar in this federation did it have to start answering questionable moves and choices. I saw men who didn’t deserve the world title receive many opportunities to take it. While the more deserving minority were the pedestrians of the World Title race. But as I became an influential individual here in the FAW politics were soon outlawed. Something that was once an everyday routine became something to be ashamed of and regret. I built the bridge to the promised land for the FAW. I built the bridge that many young stars now cross. JLC for those of you who remember was an inspirational man to the FAW and now that he is back, will all be right once again? At a certain extent I will be right. In this business there is no thank you. And I’m not going to cut another promo on how under appreciated I might feel I am because I’m not. Anyone who reaches the heights that I have are cursed with the responsibility to get better and better and to do what they can to make the federation the best it can be. I now understand that its my responsibility as the Continental Champion to be the best I can be. There will be people who want your title and everything you have become. If that person is better than you and they defeat you think of it this way… its for the betterment of the federation. But seeing as this is rare for me these days I know now that I am the BEST. No one can claim they are better then me until they do defeat me in the middle of the ring 1, 2, 3. Otherwise its trivial and a waste of breathe to claim you are the better man if you have yet to overcome me. The way I have been dominating people in my career there’s no other word that better compliments my reputation than flawless. I have seen the best and I have beaten the best. Any questions? Showtime, a friend of mine once said “before me there were many, after there will be none.” Now lets do a head count of who is still here at least in my division… Me. One thing Mr. Showtime just as well as the rest of the world didn’t realize was that I was more than a flash in a pan. I had greater heart than what many took me for. The competition miscalculated their take on me. And for that I drenched the path I took with gasoline and lit it afire so that no one could follow. And no one has. Everyone who once made the FAW are now gone. Ghosts of the past. They are memories.. Nothing more… nothing less. But they are irrelevant to the present. The FAW has stayed true to this ideology and so will I. As the FAW grows older I grow better. How and why? Okay lets begin. How? How many times did I take a vacation from the FAW for more than three days of being an active member of the show? Zero. Why? Because I have always been dedicated and will continue to be. When I’m not booked I don’t show up. I do have a life outside wrestling. But I won’t be gone until I finally retire from this and when I do I’m not returning. But I will tell you all how I will retire. It won’t be on anyone else’s terms. I’m not going to back into the shadows while the world could sit there and careless. While so many have gone out the less than perfect route I maintain that I will go out on top of my game. On top of this industry. When Michael Jordan went out a champion in 98’ with the shot heard around the world; that is what I am talking about. Although I wouldn’t return unless I felt that I could reach those heights again lest I make the same mistake Mike when he thought he could and right now he’s on one of the worst eastern conference teams in the NBA. Yes time escaped Mike. Hopefully his legend doesn’t lose some of its honour because of the return. But once I achieve that goal of going out the FAW World Champion my career will be complete and I will have nothing to be ashamed of because only then will I know I lived out my dream. This is the REAL testament to any mans career that it was productive and he accomplished the greatest of dreams. No man recorded here in the FAW has gone out the World Champion. Retire with the utmost respect and grandeur. This is the goal I have set. Now right now I guess you could say that if I retired I would not have completed the dream. But this goes far beyond just retiring the champ. But the best of what the competition has to throw at you I want to defeat. With a big match drawing near I cant help but get caught up in the anticipation. I WANT to win it. I NEED to win it. Only then at least temporarily I’ll be satisfied until the next situation comes to pass that I have to see out triumphant. Let me be the spark that that set the prairies on fire. So go on ahead and take me in as your hero. I will go on ahead and accept this. In a world where hero’s are few to none I will lead the population to their own dreams, hopes and desires. Let me be the influence that leads you to your own utopia. All I had starting out was a jobber of the year award. That’s it. No one gave me anything except one of the more demeaning titles in this profession. And people ate it up. They loved it way back when. And because of it people pushed me aside like an annoying fly. Well this fly had bite and I turned the roster upside. I splintered the opposition with one fell swoop of my tongue. Those that disrespected the man only added fuel to “The Intifada” which ultimately proved to be a big success. “The Intifada” was born the day I was awarded the more humiliating prizes of the business and for that no one has anyone to blame but themselves. This conflict between respect and grandeur shot me up to the top. No one predicted this. And so how could anyone really predict anything I ever do if they missed this? With this mic device I spit nonfiction Who got the power? This be my question Tha mass of tha few In this torn nation? Tha priest tha book or tha congregation? Tha politrics who rob and hold down your zone? Or those who give tha thieves tha key to their homes? The pig who’s free to murder one Shucklak Or survivors who make a move and murder one back? Blessed with the gift of the microphone I resume punishment of you who challenges me. I drop not only promos but insight to the past, present and future. Of course I leave you with my interpretation waiting for you to reply with your own irrelevant spin. Who else drops promos that not only abuse but educate the illiterate cupcakes who call themselves FAW superstars? Who else humiliates the “legends” who erratically return once a month and sends them packing in shame. That is basically what I have done. I have placed you all to shame but none are man enough to acknowledge it. How many people’s destiny have I devoured in my relatively small time here? I am living more than one man’s dream right now and they know it. While the former FAW superstars lay in their own piss - the direct result of me denying them the remainder of their destiny they watch… that’s all they can do… Is watch and wait for me to hang it up. You see when I drop promos I’m not just education the idiots on how to bring a relevant game here but I’m educating you on the world around you. That fantasy world many have lived or at least pretended to is but a figment of your imagination. There is a lot of injustice and graphic circumstances that take place around you every day. But with every gift there is a curse. There comes a burden with everything we find joy. And so right now my burden is fighting for not only myself but for the FAW. I’m doing something that no other FAW Champion would ever do.. Swallowing their pride in this time of need. I’m basically donating classics on behalf of this federation. Because I do want to see it survive and I do want it to attract talent even far beyond my comprehending. I was bread a FAW superstar and will die one. I won’t bow out into the obscurity of oblivion like so many have. While many have maintained in the past that they are helping this federation there is no time of greater need then now and so I ask.. Where are they? It was all bullsh*t. So as I have called for some to return I now retract my request. I will take on the burden myself. “The Curse” is unpleasant but not you can’t depend on anyone anymore. It’s a personal goal now to see this place again reach the summit of what it once was. Okay who is the FAW going to sacrifice next into my path? “The Golden Nice” Jeff Harris. I have seen Jeff wrestle and he was impressive at When World Collide but there is nothing that he nor I can do to stop his destiny which is fast approaching. I am the FAW Continental Champion, the first EVER Continental Champion. Who else can say they have had that honour attached to their shoulders? No One, because there is only one, me, Profusion. I have walked the walk and talked the talk many times before but now as I am at the pinnacle of my career I believe that Jeff Harris just may be the man who can beat me. Why you ask? He has the courage and heart of a champion, but so do I? He is a true human being and will always give 100%, but so do I? Let me re-phrase myself, this will be my biggest challenge but also Jeff’s because we will put on a show for the ages. Jeff this is a Champion versus Champion match, a match for the ages, we could steal the show if we play our cards right but the thing is that I do not want to just put on a great show, I want to beat you and take some of your pride and add it to my stack. You will kneel down and show your gratitude for me after this match, after I take you to that new level, you will remember this match Jeff, for a long time to come.
