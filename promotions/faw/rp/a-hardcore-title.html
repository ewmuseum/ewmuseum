HALLOWEEN! WOOT!

A hardcore title? Rzarector Furiously Addictive Wrestling Roleplay #62 Date: HALLOWEEN! WOOT! Location: In the Church (The screen opens to show Rzarector dressed in his full black robe walking up to the front doors of a church. There on the door hangs a giant 6 foot golden cross with the words Jesus written on them. Rzarector approaches the door and begins to turn the knob when chime music starts playing. Rzarector hisses at the sound of the stupid gospel music and quickly opens the door jumps in and slams it. From the back of the church someone goes 'Who is that?' Rzarector stands there facing the back with his green eyes glowing out as a priest walks out from the back. The priest smiles as he see's it is Rzarector. The priest walks up and pats Rzarector on the back as he guides him to the front of the church. Rzarector is walking now as he passes the benches with bibles in them, and the red carpet. As the Priest and Rza reach the front a huge picture of Jesus is seen, along with several angels flying towards him. In the Priest's eyes the angels are very white and are very beautiful, but because of Rzarectors past, his eyes tell him they are black with horns and red eyes. Rzarector looks on in shock.) Rzarector: I am still black, what does that mean father? (The Priest turns around and pats Rzarector on the back.) Priest: Son, one day you will eventually see the angels to be beautiful, not black and evil like you see them now. God knows of your past, and he has cursed you until you start repenting daily and praying. Rzarector: I do repent and pray daily. Priest: Well maybe its a time test. He is just seeing if you can hold out. Rzarector: (Hisses) I don't care anyways, the angels look fine. They look like me. Priest: That my son, is why you see them. (Rzarector turns around to the preist and gives him a blood curdling look, flashing his fangs. The priest gives a slight step back as he motions for Rzarector to follow him. They go further back into the church and through a door. This door leads down into a spiral of stairs. The priest begins walking down them as Rzarector smiles because of the pure darkness that lurks beneath it.) Rzarector: Is this the basement? Priest: Yes it is. Rzarector: Why haven't you ever shown it to me before? Priest: You never asked. (Rzarector starts to hiss as the priest gives him a stern look. Inside Rzarectors head he starts thinking.) *Why does the Priest treat me like a little kid? I am not a little kid, my parents were the same way. But that was ages ago, I never see them anymore. God won't let me, they're dead. I wonder what is down here.* (Rzarector and The Priest keep walking around spirals until they reach a cold wet damp floor. Rzarector places a boot down as it splashes through water. Rats are heard running around as the Priest walks into the darkness. There a light comes on, where? IN the middle of the room, Rzarector covers his eyes, but then remembers that they keep his vision dark anyways and uncovers them. He walks over to the Priest who is standing over a table reading papers. Rzarector looks menacingly on.) Priest: You know Rzarector, this is some of God's testament that I have preserved directly for you. I'm sure you read the bible, just scanning it though. Here are some passages that might come to your interest. (The Priest clears his throat and begins to read them to Rzarector.) Priest: Number 1, if you ever diss the Holy Ghost you are eternally damned. Well Rzarector God gave you your break on this. You used to fight the holy, now you are one of them. God saved you. Number 2, If I bear witness of myself, my witness is not true, There is another that beareth witness of me. And I know that the witness which he witnesseth of me is true. That means Rzarector, all those sins that you committed, and thought noone saw. Well he did see, and you told God nothing was wrong with them. Yet you were your ownw itness, and it is always wrong, his look on you was right. Number 3 and last one, And behold, I come quickly, and my reward is with me, to give every man according as his work shall be. This means that whatever you do on earth, will be your award. Heaven or Hell, its your choice Rzarector. (Rzarector looked downa nd then back up as his eyes showed green. His fangs flashed out of his mouth, as he had a sad look on his face.) Rzarector: Father, I choose Heaven, I choose God. Priest:Well then start acting like it. Rzarector: I do act like it, more than anything you could ever dream of. I am the holiest one for God. I promise. Priest: Well leave and go show the world that. (Rzarector shakes his head yes, and begins leaving the room back up through the stairs. He exits the door and looks at the picture of Jesus and smiles. He then goes past it between the aisles, and to the front door. He kisses it as he opens it. There no chime is heard and Rzarector gives a happy look, he then exits the church and begins walking down the streets. People are crowded today and they keep giving him evil looks. Rzarector just hisses at all of them and keeps walking. There he see's a cafe, the Route 66 cafe, he walks inside of it and sits down at a table. The waitress comes up.) Waitress: What will it be sir? Rzarector: Just some raw steak, 2 pounds please. Waitress: Be right back with your food and your bill. (There Rzarector closes his eyes and tries to imaging the golden walls, and the angels of heaven. Yet he starts hearing yelling. He opens them again and sees a man arguing with his wife. They start getting into it more when the guy picks up his glass of water and throws it across the cafe breaking it onto a wall. The woman is screaming as her children are yelling momma. The man looks and starts cussing hte children as he turns back to the wife, who is now in tears. The man goes 'What are you crying for?' The woman just keeps trying as the guy slaps her in the face. Rzarector hisses very loud as the guy turns over to him.) Guy: What is your problem sir? You got one? Rzarector: Those who have sinned, will perish. Yet is it a crime to defeat the wicked? Guy: What does that mean? I go to church! Rzarector: What appears to be saying going to church, is a false prophet. Yet I do not treat false prophets like they should be treated. Why you may be wondering? Because I will not take you to hell sir, I will just show you the path. Guy: Shutup, you black hooded robe, vampire teeth, green eyed freak. I've had enough of your talk. (The guy begins walking over to Rzarector who quickly gets up out of his seat, he hisses at the guy, who now is stumbling because he is drunk. Rzarector grabs him by the shirt, kicks him in the stomach, pushes him back, and with his long thick black claws he reaches forward and slices his face open. Blood immediately starts flowing out as Rzarector hisses again as his fangs are showing. The wife of the man is screaming again as the guy looks even more pisses. He charges Rzarector who then just sticks out his hand and the guys stomach runs into the claws. The guy pukes up blood as Rzarector hisses and turns his hand, tearing apart the mans stomach. The mans eyes start to look glazed as Rzarector releases his hand, jabs his claws into the guys heart, the guy falls over with blood dripping onto the floor. The woman screams and then begins to yell MURDERER! Rzarector hisses and then leaves the cafe. Rzarector starts thinking again.) *Why are people like that? So immature and play God with his bible. The words are written, I know what is to come of this man, so why not treat him like he is? Treat him like a barbaric demon who is sent to this Earth by Satan to destroy love in people. Pure hatred, I hate it.* (Rzarector stops thinking as another voice interrupts his head, this voice is very nice. Not like the voice of Rzarector, whose voice is a soft demonic voice. The voice then begins talking into the head of Rzarector.) *My son, what you just witnessed was only a dream. Soon you willw ake back up in the cafe, only to see two raw things of steak in front of you. The guy and his wife, will be happy talking. Now you must remember, it is my duty to take care of the wicked, yet you still don't understand that. Well Rzarector, this is why you will remain cursed. This is why.* (Rzarector then wakes up on the countertop of the table in the cafe. Two raw slabs of steak are in front of him and the Waitress is looking at him smiling. As soon as his green eyes and fangs appear to her she stops. Rzarector hisses as the waitress leaves.)
