Spawn — FAW Roleplay #12

captured 13 September 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
SpAwN--The Hell Master \~~~~~~~(opening scene)The scene Mike and Spawn are shown arriving at the grave yard, Mike stays in the car as Spawn gets out to go take a look at the grave they just stop in front of. When he gets there he gets a shock look , a look of disgust, like he can’t believe what he is looking at, for a moment time dissolves on him , then his vision blurs, from anger or from something else. How long does he stands there wanting to move? Finally he goes back to the car and begin to speak with Mike. Spawn: The grave is empty do you know what happened? mike: Spawn! Something came down from the sky. Spawn: What! mike: something Spinning a helicopter, yeah that’s it a helicopter landed here, a helicopter, how the hell should I know what happened here and who stole the body. Spawn: You are the only one that knew about this? mike: Yeah! But I am not stupid to come out here in the middle of the night to dig up for a body and take it home, that’s your part you are the one that’s crazy not me. Spawn: Then who the fuck would do something like this? mike: Hey listen I’m a reporter, but even I do not have all the answer. Spawn and Mike turn to their side and see the ground keeper coming at them, the man looks shaken up and very dirty, Spawn looks at the man pockets and notice it is full of money, the ground keeper stop looks at Spawn and Mike and begins to speak. Ground keeper: A truck came here last night and took your body. Spawn: They came here dug up the grave and took the body? Ground keeper: Yes that’s exactly what happened here last night. Spawn: How many guys were there doing this job? Ground keeper: Exactly four of them, and there was a big muscled up man all suit up standing by a limo, he must have been the boss, because they were listening to everything he was asking them to do. Spawn: Did you tried to stop them? Ground keeper: Yes I tried but they hit me see ( the man leans forward to show Spawn the bump on his head, when he leans forward the money falls of his pocket) ooops sorry, let me pick up my money. Spawn: Here let me help you (bending down and helping the man to pick up his money) Ground keeper: Thank you sir. Spawn: Here you go (handing him over the money) that’s a lot of money to be carrying at this hour in a place like this? Ground keeper: I do not trust anyone in my building so I always carry my money with me. Spawn: Oh ok, now let me get this straight, they came here dug up the grave and took the body, when you tried to stop them they hit you is this correct? Ground keeper: Yes it is. mike: Sounds like a jack ass story to me if I ever heard one, and trust me I heard many. Spawn: Mike please? Let me see(looking at the man’s bump) looks nasty we better get you to a doctor. Is that ok with you? Ground keeper: Yes that’s ok. mike: Finally we can get out of here, yes. Mike < Spawn and the ground keeper all get into the car a drive off the reach the hospital and go inside, there they wait for about an hour, the ground keeper gets called in, he stays in the room with the doctor for about a half hour, and the doctor comes out to talk to Spawn and Mike. Doctor: Rest easy boys he doesn’t have a concussion. mike: That’s good news doc, now we can go home. Spawn: Just a minute Mike, Doc do you think that bump is today’s or yesterdays? I have had a lot of bumps in my time, and I know the difference between and old bump and a new one, so what do you think doc? Doctor: You are pretty good, and to answer your question is an old bump. Spawn: How old doc? Doctor: Three days the most. Must have been acquire from drinking or something. Spawn: Thank you doc. ( the doctor leaves and Spawn turns his attention toward Mike and begin to whisper to him) I’m going to find out what this guy is up to and what he really knows. mike: How are you going to do that? Spawn: Watch me, ok keep low here he comes. Ground keeper: Well the doctor said not to worry and that I’ll be fine, so we can leave now? All three men leave the hospital get into the car and drive off. The scene show the car stopping in front of the police department, Spawn and Mike get off the car but the ground keeper doesn’t, Spawn looks at him and ask him to get off the car but he still doesn’t get off the car, Spawn then begins to speak to him. Spawn: Why you do not want to go inside and report this incident, it must be reported? Ground keeper: There is no need for all of this I’m alright we can just forget about it. Spawn: Yeah like the money we can forget about that too? Ground keeper: What are you talking about? mike: You know what we are talking about, we had your number picked from the beginning you lying fool. Spawn: I think you better come clean before we take you in. I’m pretty sure the cops would be interested to see why a ground keeper has a lot of money in his pocket with the type of job he does, at this time of night. It’s not illegal but it’s suspicious and I’m pretty sure once they found out that there was a body stolen from the grave site that you maintain it will get mighty interesting to them, what do you think? Ground keeper: Alright! Already, I’ll tell you everything I know. Spawn: Good start from the beginning. Ground keeper: You see there was this wrestler and he came to me and ask me if I can do him a big favor, and that I would get pay handsomely for it. He said that some guy name Spawn buried some picture and other things inside the grave, because he wanted to do voodoo on him, I felt sorry for the guy you know so I took the offer, then after he paid me that’s when he came with the truck and the other guys and took the body. Spawn: Where did he say he was going to take the body. Ground keeper: He said to a building, a wrestling building, the guy said at the time he was taking it to the building nobody is around, so it will be easy for him to set a trap for Spawn in the building. Spawn: What is the name of the building? Ground keeper: The FAW building a big dome son of a bitch is very big you can practically get lost in there. Spawn: what is the guys name? Ground keeper: The guys name was Skippy or Cappy no it was nappy yeah that’s what it was he had that gay look on him. Spawn: That’s not his name think again? And tell me his real name? Ground keeper: Ok , OK his name is Skipster he came with some guys and took the body. Spawn: You are not lying to me right, because if you are I'll take you to the cops right now. Ground keeper: I'm tell you the truth this is no lie, can I leave now? Spawn: Yeah but make it quick before I change my mind. Mike let's go to the FAW building. Mike and Spawn get in to the car and drive off to the FAW building, the scene shows them arriving at the building they get off the car and go in inside, inside they begin to search for the body, they look everywhere finally they come to a room with a man standing in front of the door, Spawn and Mike walk up to the man and begin to speak to him. Spawn: Hey how are you doing? man: Not bad can I help you? Spawn: Yes you can I believe you have something that belong to me inside that room, and I'm here to get it back. man: I don't know what you are talking about. Spawn: (Spawn reaching into his pocket and taking some money out) Once you see the color of green you'll know what I'm talking about, answer me this how much did they pay you to do the job? man: Three hundred, buddy? Spawn: I'll give you five hundred, and all you have to do is walk away with a message, how does that sounds to you? man: Sounds good to me, give me the money and who is the message for. Spawn: For Skipster. (Spawn hands him the money) man: What is it that you want me too tell him? Spawn: You tell him that I am sick and tired of his games, this will not go on much longer, I will find him and beat the crap out of his gay ass, you tell him that what he did here was all for nothing, because this body you have here is fake, that's right it is being use for my next show, tell him how stupid he was that his plan failed again and that he has gotten nowhere in defeating me, but I will defeat him when I give him the last kill over and over again, he will pay for his sins, he will pay for his stupidity and he will pay for him just being Skipster and to finish off the end is coming for him at Summer Slaughter, now get the fuck out of here. The man leaves. While Mike and Spawn pick up the body and head for the car as the scene fades to black.
