The Falling Star

October 16, 2002

The Falling Star Chris Storm Furiously Addictive Wrestling Roleplay #10 Date: October 16, 2002 Location: Appalachian Mountains, Shinto Shrine [Each day in your life is another step. Many people take the same steps each day. They repeat where they walk day in and day out. Never trailing off the path. They just walk the same straight line everyday. This tale is about one man that is thrusted from his straight line and down a path he's never dreamed up. What starts off in a normal life quickly becomes a wonder from day to day. And it all started as innocent as the morning dew on the ground.] (The Falling Star Lands) . . . . The first of a three part story. . . . [If you had met Chris Storm before his life took a radical change, he would have seemed like your normal guy. He enjoyed what every guy enjoyed. Having fun, women, and sports. Chris was always a happy guy too. A real happy-go-lucky type of guy. Nothing much bothered him. And why should it? After all, he had a career he loved, a girlfriend he cared about, and a great home in the mountains. The beauty of nature at his doorstep. A talently and lovely girlfriend that lived out of state, but he worked with her so they had plenty of time together. His job? Well Chris Storm was just your average professional wrestler. Just with a little more success. World's heavyweight champion of WIW:NG. His girlfriend, Tessa Windsor was the reigning Intercontintal champion of WIW:NG. Chris was enjoying living on top of the company. But as much as he enjoyed and loved his work, he was glad to be giving the week off. He had the match with Apocalypse from FAW on the 20th and he wanted to focus on that. It was the most important thing to him at the moment. He had every day planned out. Down to the smallest detail. Wake up at sunrise. Eat breakfast and get ready for work shortly there after. Tend to his Shinto shrine he kept near his mountain range home. After tending to the shrine he would practice his Teal Chi martial arts. After practice he would enjoy the sights of nature as he went for a walk though the mountains, then it was to the gym and train and keep his moves sharp as a fine knife. Once he finishes in the gym it was back home for a bath and dinner. Sleep followed dinner and thus the day ended and the cycle would repeat for the next day. Do this for six days then on the 19th he would go to the arena and stay down there and meditate until his match. Then he would make the WIW:NG proud. Alas things never go as they are planned and one single action, completely unexpected, threw all of that out of order and tosses everything into choas. October 13th is a day Chris Storm will never forget. The day his life changed forever. This is his story. I ask you to listen as in times it may seem unbelievable but know that everything did happen. At times you may laugh and you may cry. So read forth and no not judge. Simply read and enjoy. For this is my tale, and it starts now.] [The air is getting crisp. Signs that fall is here. The leaves are falling from the trees and covering the ground in a carpet of colors. The sun has just started to rise in the northern skies. In the home of Chris Storm, he has just awoken to another day. He sits up in his bed and let's out a mighty yawn. He stretches out and beats on his chest. He hops out of bed and walks over to his closet. He would be nude but medical tape covers his body. Varies bandages cover patches of him. You can see where deep cuts are. Skin has been burnt. All resulting as to his hellious PPV match against Erik Erratic in WIW:NG. Chris opens the closet and looks at the mrror. A frown covers his face. He looks up to the top of the closet shelf and pulls down a box. He opens it up to show the WIW:NG World title. He puts it around his waist and looks at himself in the mirror. He smiles brightly.] Chris Storm: Much better! Damn I look good in this thing. [He takes off the title and kisses the plate. He puts it back in it's box and places it on the top shelf. Chris goes through his clothes and just grabs a normal white shirt with brown slacks. Not the most flattering clothes but Chris doesn't have to worry about impressing anyone way out in the mountains. He walks into the kitchen and looks at a calender. October 20th has been circled in a red marker. He grabs the marker and places a red 'X' over Sunday, October 13th.] Chris Storm: Merely one week until my FAW match. Apocalypse better know what he got himself into. Because there is more then just self pride on the line for this match. There is the pride of my company and every wrestler on the roster. I refuse to let anyone of them down. I won't let it happen again! This is my promise to the world. Chris Storm will not fail. [He laughs out a cocky laugh, as if he had already won. Chris walks over and fixes himself a quick breakfast. It's actually a nice meal as living on the road as a singles male has taught him the importance of knowing how to cook a decent meal quickly. As wrestler, you didn't always have a chance to eat right before your next appointment in the gym or on a trip to the doctor's office because of said injuries. Chris sits down at the table and looks out the window. There is a gentle wind blowing through the trees. The wind swoops up the leaves and blows them across the ground. The leaves appear to dance on the ground. Chris has a content smile on his face.] Chris Storm: I love this time of the season. Fall. A time for changes. The leaves change color, the weather changes, the view changes. Everything is getting ready for it's rebirth of life. Preparing itself for the harsh winter to come. I guess I could say the same for my career huh? Changes. Man it seems like life has thrown me some serious curve balls as of late. Tessa Windsor and I start a serious relationship. He retain my World's title at Retribution. I make my return to PTC after being banned and I find Apocalypse ready to pick up where we left off. Now I get to prepare for the match at the end of the week. The harsh battle ahead of me lingers. I can see it yet I know it is not time for it to happen. I must wait for it to come. Much like nature must wait for winter to come. But I will use this time to better prepare myself for the winter to come and I will be ready. Then like nature, I will come out and be just fine. Still intact, like I was before. But where nature is reborn into summer, I will return to the WIW:NG. [Chris finishes off his breakfast and grabs his lunch. He puts on his shoes and steps outside. Chris takes in a deep breath of mountain air. The Appalachian Mountains are electric with life almost. Although the sun has risen into the sky, it's still early in the morning and Chris can still see some of the stars in the sky. The brightest of the stars stand out, not wanting to disappear in the light of the sun. He cast his eyes upward towards them and admires the stars before he must start on work.] Chris Storm: Strange. That one star appears to be brighter then all the others. It's slightly larger too. Must be the North Star. [Chris let's his observation slip his mind and grabs the broom. He starts his dutiful task of sweeping the fallen leaves off the shrine grounds. If only Chris would have kept his eyes on the light far overhead he would have seen it start to glow and move. What at first thought to be a star slowly starts to fall out of the sky. It picks up speed as it gets closer and closer to the Earth. Chris pays the whole ordeal no mind and continues sweeping the shrine clean. Although boring and tiresome, Chris enjoys his chore as he feels connected to nature and all of it's wonder. The winds swiftly move around him. His loose clothing dances in the breeze. His short blond hair is blown by the wind. He has to let out a sigh as he just smiles broadly. There is just something about it all that makes him feel more alive then ever. The wind starts to pick up. It builds and becomes stronger. The leaves start to blow and causes Chris' job to become more troublesome. Suddenly a giant gush of wind slams against Chris. It nearly causes him to lose his footing. He looks out over the landscape as a white flash covers the mountains and trees. Chris' eyes grow wide with shock. He can feel the rumble. The mighty tidal wave of the blast hits Chris and sends him off his feet and to the ground. His torn body hits the stone steps of the shrine grounds. He tries to let out a scream in pain but the vacuum rips the sound from this throat. Only a silent echo can escape his lips. Chris closes his eyes as the white void consumes him.] Chris Storm: [though] What's going on? What's happening to me? I can't feel anything. Am I dead or alive? Where am I? Can anyone hear me? WHERE AM I?!? [His eyes shoot open. Chris slowly stands up. He looks around to see the shrine and his home. Leaves cover the ground. His broom lays on the ground next to his feet. Chris looks around confused. Had it all been a dream? Was there really a giant white light? Or even a gust of wind? Chris looks over the landscape. He can see a small crater among the trees. It's still smoking. Chris drops his broom and starts a full pace sprint into the woods. The pain shooting in his legs might as well not even exist as Chris doesn't feel a single thing. Something in the back of his mind told him to run as fast as he could and by Kami he wasn't going to stop until he got to the impact site. He darts through the trees and hurdles the bushes and fallen limbs. Nothing was going to stop him. Nothing could. Chris jetted out of the woods into the clearing where the crash site was. It was near the lake. Already water has starts to flush into the crater, filling it up. The area is completely silent, save the water rushing into the crater and Chris' heavy breathing. The animals, birds, and nature has gone silent. Chris looked around the site. It appears to be empty. A confused look rolls onto his brow. Why did he have the urge to have to come here if it was empty? There was nothing here but dirt and water. Even the timber of flames have died down.] Chris Storm: Nothing more then a wild goose chase. Man. . .and I have other things to do too. I can't stand around this hole all day. I need to prepare myself for Apocalypse. Him and DCV is not going to be a walk in the park dispite how much I train and work. Man, what a waste of my time. [Chris goes and walks off but then he hears it. A soft moan. Almost unaudible, yet still lingering in the air. Chris' head does an instant 180 as he spots the source of the sound. His jaw drops down to his feet. There laying next to the crater, almost in the water, was a young women, had to be no more then in her mid twentys. How could he have missed her? Was he that blind that he hadn't noticed her? He instantly rushes to her side. Chris looks around for anything else he might have missed. Kneeling down next to her, Chris gently cradles her into his airs. He tilts her head as to look into her eyes. He looks into her eyes yet he can not find any signs of life. Her eyes are strange however. Chris was use to green, blue, brown, and many different eye colors but gold? Surely it had to be fake. Chris brushes it aside though. Chris looks at her and puts his ear to her throat. She's breathing. He lets out a silent prayer and dips his hand in the nearby water. He opens her mouth and pours a little water in. He gives her some room.] Women: [cough]. . [cough] [Chris' eyes light up. The women starts to cough and soon gasp for air. Chris stares at her in disbelief. The women regains her senses and looks at Chris. She mouth slowly opens. Chris has a warm smile on his face. Relieved to see she is ok. He grabs the back of her head to tilt it up in a more comfortable position for her. He hadn't noticed before but her hair is wild and untamed. Like a stallion that will serve no matter. And it's a deep blue. A dark blue. More so then even Chris' baby blues. He runs his hand through her hair, gently lifing her head up. It's so uncontrolable and yet it's as soft as silk. Chris looks into her eyes as she simply lets him. The women leans forward and soon their noses are touching. Chris is a little uncomfotable being this close to her. But something in her face tells him not to worry. She has this confused, lost look in her eyes. For some odd reason, Chris just wants to hold her and tell her everything will be alright. The women closers and hers and Chris blinks.] Chris Storm: What the. . . [Suddenly the women opens her eyes and leaps onto Chris! She pins him down against the ground and looks over him like a lion on it's pray. She eyes feed onto Chris. He can feel her breath, hot and heavy, on his chest. Fear runs through his mind. This women, whom he thought he saved has just pinned him down the to ground. Chris closes his eyes, expecting the worse. Images flash through his mind. Not about October 20th or Apocalypse or FAW. No those images are one one person. One place in time. Chris and Tessa, together. Sitting next to each other and she leans her head onto his shoulder. She looks up at him with those soft blue eyes and Chris made a promise to himself that he would never allow anything to hurt her ever again. Then it happens. He can feel her lips against his. The soft and wet lips embrass against his. The women starts to rub her hands over his chest. Chris pushes her off and gets to his feet. Shocked and confused. He wipes his lips.] Chris Storm: What the hell are you doing? Who are you anyways? [Chris puts his foot down to farther the point. The women looks at him standing over her. Her bright golden eyes start to swell up with tears. She slowly rises to her feet and walks over to Chris. He backs up a little but she pushes herself against his chest and cries into it. Chris is taken back by this once more sudden change in emotion.] Women: Just hold me. I'm scared. [She cries harder into his chest. Chris doesn't hold her at first, thinking of Tessa. He says a pray to her and puts his arms around this women. He rubs her back, and tells her to be quiet. He softly ask her to stop crying. Chris looks over at the crater and back up at the sky.] Chris Storm: [thinking] Apocalypse, on October 20th we meet in mortal combat. But now. . it seems a new card has been thrown into the deck has it not? Just when I believe I have life figured out I get another curve ball. But Apocalypse, I will be ready. This I can promise you. And I will bring you the fight of your life. Even if I must tread through hell first. I will. [Chris just stands there holding this women he found near the crater in his arms. He doesn't have a clue who she is, where she came from, or why she is here. All he knows is she needs him and just as he would never let Tessa down, he won't let her down.] [When man tries to control his own fate, fate adds a new player to the game. Something is added they throws the balance off. When one believes he has it all figured out, he's hit with something completely unexpected. Now as Chris has prepared himself to face Apocalypse in a match that determines company pride and self honor, he is dealt a strange women. With only the idea of helping her, he must somehow figure out a way to discover who she is and defeat Apocalypse. Like we said, Chris Storm is just your average professional wrestler leading an average life.]
