Chris Storm? A man?

10/15/02
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Chris Storm? A man? Apocalypse Furiously Addictive Wrestling Roleplay #9 Date: 10/15/02 Location: A Lawyer suit ….Power? What does power really mean? Some people state that it is something that give you the right to walk all over people in life. Some say that it means that you have the better life then everyone else. Well to most it means you are above all the rest. Able to tell people what to do. But does that mean pyishicaly or mentally? Yes people can tell you what to do with their mouth but do they do it really from there. Power? Do you really know what it means?…. (The scene opens up as you see the man Apocalypse walking down a long hallway of some high priced building. Pictures gallor on all the walls. Lamps that seem like they cost millions of dollars to any homeless person on the street. But what you don’t see is the pocket books that some of these rich people have in there pockets. The money they have hidden away in there bank accounts. The camera goes in closer as Apocalypse turns the corner and walks right into the opening of a room. On the right of it there is a table with a set of people’s magazines with a picture of Tom Cruise on the front of them. Nothing out of the ordinary on the table as the camera pans over to the left of the room. A set of chairs with the sharp blue covering on them and a little white trim on the side where the sewing would be. Right beside the chairs was a little stain that seemed like it was made from a big glass of kool-aid or a red drink. The camera pans upward as you see a young lady sitting at a desk typing away at her computer. Apocalypse stands there in front of her for a few minutes before speaking up in a firm but yet soft voice.) Apocalypse: Hello miss. I was wondering….. Ms. Julie: Do you have an appointment? Apocalypse: No I don’t but I need to speak w…. Ms. Julie: Well I am sorry but you need an appointment. Apocalypse: lady, do you know who you are talking to? I make more then you could ever dream of in one night. (Ms. Julie looked up at Apocalypse with her big bright blue eyes and got a big shock. Her eyes and mouth went wide open like a shark ready to eat its prey. She grabbed her phone up real quick and started to dial an exstention number.) Ms. Julie: Mr. David, this is a man here to see you….yes….yes sir I will send him in. (Apocalypse looked down at the young lady in white and gave a smile as he walked right by her and to the door of Mr. David’s office. Opening the door you knew what was going to be in there. A nice oak wood desk with a sharp dressed man sitting behind that desk in a big leather chair. The kind of rich man that only you would see in a movie or law firm. Law Firm? That is right and the man that sat there was from David’s criminal law firm. The man sat there with a big of a grin on his face as Apocalypse shut the door behind him.) Apocalypse: You Mr. David? Mr. David: Yes I am. What can I do for you sir? Apocalypse: I don’t know if you have heard but there is this big match coming up on October the 20th between myself and WIW’s Chris Storm. I need to know if you can help me out with something? Mr. David: Well what is it that I can help you out with? Apocalypse: Well as everyone can see it looks like I am going to win this match up with no problem but the problem is the match itself. You see, I get him in a ring with nothing on the line. No title, no cage, no nothing. What I need is you to help me out with a little pay back. Mr. David: Are you trying to suggest money? (Apocalypse’s face came to a surprise as that wasn’t really the answer he though was to come out of his mouth.) Apocalypse: Yes, I want you to write up a small contract for this Sunday stating that if either won wins they win a small amount of $500,000 dollars that we will both put up. I want this contract to be bullet proof and I need it now. Mr. David: Well this is going to take sometime so can you have a seat and I will write it up. (Apocalypse looked over his right shoulder at the long leather black couch with gold buttons on the sides. He slowly leaned down and took a sit with his back slowly bend downwards. Mr. David took out a small little contract and a pin and started to write a few things on to the paper. Apocalypse didn’t look to conforable as he took a pick around the room. On the right wall there was a large tiger head that had a few black spots covering the black over the head. It was hanging right beside what looked to be a giant fish. The fish was almost say ten feet long. A bit of flesh missing from the stomach part.The camera paned over to the left side as you saw a set of books on a large shelf. The shelf stood all the way up to the roof. Every type of law book was on there. Criminal law, Divorce Law, and even Prison Law were some of them. Apocalypse turned to Mr. David as he looked up with the paper in hand and gave the eye to Apocalypse.) Mr. David: Well here is what you asked for. A legal contract for you to have at the match. What type of match are you in? Apocalypse: A wrestling match. Now thank you for your service. Mr. David: Are you forgetting something? Apocalypse: Oh yeah, here’s $3,000 for your service. (The lawyer got a big rich smile on his face as Apocalypse walked out of the room and to the hallway. The camera followed him all the way to the elevator. Apocalypse pushed the button to go to the third floor as the door’s open wide for the DCV member. Walking in, he turned around and wiped his face as the scene then slowly faded away.) -------- About fifteen minutes later -------- (The scene then opened up to Apocalypse standing right in the middle of the screen with a big grin on his face. Nothing but a white wall to the back side and a little noise form the cars that pass by. Apocalypse pushed the hair out of his way as he began to speak.) Apocalypse: Six months, two week, and so many days ago I first showed up in your face talking crap to you. About twenty days later we set up a match but nothing happen. I guess we wasn’t ready for prime time or we where just plain scared of each other. Wait, maybe it was just me feeling sorry for a loser like yourself. You see Chris, I have no loss of feeling for you in this time around. It will me to the end between you and me. Trust me son, I am going to put everything on the line against you in this match up and I will promise you a win. Nothing will hold me back from the one thing everyone know I am destine to do. My destiny is to beat you down and give you that one loss that defines you form the great one. Now I know that in your mind you think you are the man on top of everything. I don’t blame you for thinking that. No one in the WIW has what I have. Pure power that only comes from the mind and spirit. Nothing you can do to me will stop that fact that I will walk in to that ring and beat you for the one, two, three. You see, I am so sure of myself that I went out and made this contract that I will have you sign before the match even starts. (Apocalypse pulled out the contract from his pocket and flipped it open to show the lines of agreement to the camera.) Apocalypse: These line show that they must be carried out. Even if you are dead broke you will pay me the contract of $500,000 dollars. But I know what you thinking. I don’t want to lose to you Mr. Apocalypse. I don’t have $500,000 in my pocket. I suck so bad that I am broke. Well you will sign the damn thing whether you are a man or not. Whether you have money or not because I promise to man your life a living hell here and after this match. Whether I win or lose I will beat you someway or the other. Chris, it is time to come fill my storm at Distortion 19. (With that the scene slowly starts to faded out with Apocalypse leaning down with his eyes closed as the area turns black.)
