The Truck Stop

1/23/3
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The Truck Stop Skipster Furiously Addictive Wrestling Roleplay #169 Date: 1/23/3 Location: Texas (Opening Scene) The scene opens up with Skipster driving down the highway in Texas, not thinking about anything. Skipster just keeps driving and picking up speed as he goes. Finally Skipster sees a truck stop sign ahead so he changes lanes and goes to the truck stop. He pulls up and stops his hummer and then steps out and heads inside. Inside, he grabs a cup of coffee and sits down at a table, occasionally signing autographs for people that recognize him. Finally a man sits down in the booth with Skipster and waits for Skipster to look up from reading the paper. Finally he does, and when Skipster looks up he is surprised so see someone sitting there with him. Skipster-- Who are you? Man-- My name is Shaun. I’m a truck driver. I hope you don’t mind that I sat down with you. It looked like you needed someone to talk with. Skipster-- Nah, its fine that ya sat down, next time ask though. Hey, what's your last name bud? Shaun-- The name is Shaun Carlson. Skipster-- HA! I knew it… I knew you looked familiar. Shaun-- Excuse me? Skipster-- I can’t believe you don’t recognize me Shaun. Hell I went to high school with you for two years before you left for college. Shaun-- Still doesn’t ring a bell. Skipster-- Does me liking Jonilyn ring a bell? Shaun-- Andrew… Andrew Fountain?? Skipster-- Yep… that’s me. Shaun-- Wow… you’ve changed. What ever happened to you going to WestPoint and going to the Marines? Skipster-- Didn’t get accepted. Shaun-- So what did you do? Skipster-- I ended up going to The University of Missouri, getting an accounting degree and going into the FBI, eventually getting on the S.W.A.T. team. Shaun-- Just like you wanted. Skipster-- Yep. That’s right. So have you heard where Joni is now? I’d like to see her again. Shaun-- I don’t know man, I don’t know where she went. You will have to figure that one out. Skipster-- Will do. Shaun-- So what's bothering you pal? Skipster-- Nothing is bothering me, I am just trying to get psyched for my match Sunday. Shaun-- Ah yes, the 20-man Rumble. Who all is in that again? Skipster-- Well, there is Xing, Jeff Harris, Randy Rocker, Jon the Bomb, Violator, Rzarector, CJ Maxwell, Hardcore Hoefel, Atticus Pulsar, Slyphon, Gabriel Hunter, Profusion, Cyrus Skar, Flash, Dan Logan, Crimson Lord, Spider, Fatt Lardy, Lee Smith, and of course me. Shaun-- There any talent in there? I don’t watch wrestling much. I’ve seen in a couple times, hence the reason I knew you were Skipster. Skipster-- The first time they announce who was in the rumble… there were a lot of “mystery men”, and at that time there was no talent except for Rzarector and maybe Profusion. Shaun-- So what about now? Skipster-- Now is a different story. Now there are many people showing potential. Profusion, Rzarector, Xing, and Flash are showing some true potential. CJ Maxwell is getting up there. I expect to see a ton from Spider and Crimson Lord as well. I think from a newcomer standpoint, I think Flash will be a decent opponent. Shaun-- So what about Spider? Skipster-- I haven’t heard, nor seen much of Spider. I don’t exactly remember how well he is. Oh well… it ain’t that important to me. All that is important to me is that I make a great impression now that I am back in the FAW, and I am going to start by beating Spider, Crimson and everyone else in the rumble. Spider is just like me right now; he is just coming back and probably a bit rusty, as am I. But that’s all right; we have 18 other men to fight besides each other to get warmed up. We will most likely end up fighting each other; we are a few of the ONLY veterans of FAW here anyway. Shaun-- Hey, I got to go. I have to get my big rig to Arkansas as by tomorrow. Skipster-- Later man. Shaun-- Adios Andrew. Keep it real. Skipster-- Always have man. Shaun gets into his Truck and start to head down the road. About 10 minutes later, Skipster gets up from his booth and he gets back onto the highway… soon catching up to a big rig that had got his trailer wedged underneath an overpass. Skipster slows down and gets out his cell phone and calls for help, then gets out of his hummer and goes up to the guy in the Big Rig. Skipster knocks on the door, and when the door opens up, Shaun is sitting inside. Skipster starts to laugh. Skipster-- Nice man, real nice. Shaun-- Yea Shut up. Highway Patrol Man-- Get you truck stuck? Skipster whips around surprised by the new voice in the conversation. Skipster can’t think of anything to say but without hesitation, Shaun speaks up. Shaun-- Nope. I was delivering that overpass and ran out of gas. Skipster is now laughing so hard that he is on the ground. The Officer just looks at the two and then leaves. Skipster-- You always did have a way with words man. Shaun-- I guess so. Skipster-- Sorry man, I need to get going… I have a house show I gotta go speak at. Shaun-- Its cool. Later man. Skipster-- Adios. Skipster jumps back into his hummer and speeds away down the highway till get gets to the arena. Skipster finds a parking spot and walks into the arena. Skipster goes in, changes and then gets ready to go out. The room goes black, cutting off all the talk in the arena and lightning bolts start to soar across the arena. “Ride the Lightning” by Metallica picks up and the crowd gather to their feet and start cheering right away. Skipster comes sprinting down into the ring and slides in, with his nightstick in hand. Skipster looks around, and finally gets a mic and begins to speak. Skipster-- Well this is the second time out here in a week. I don’t have much to say but I do want to comment of this damn Crimson Lord who likes to talk shit about me. Now I could go and talk about him right now… that would be the easy thing to do. I don’t like easy. I make things difficult, even if they were made to be easy. Crimson thinks I repeat myself and that I repeat what he says. I may do that… I don’t realize it but I may. WHO CARES! All I know is that he is a dead man now. We were rivals back when we were both in FAW together. Now, for some reason, he thinks he is better than I am… even though I have beaten him every time that I faced him. He was sitting out here talking about his damn childhood… once again WHO CARES!? I don’t care if you had the worst childhood or you were a spoiled brat who didn’t know right from wrong. What matters is now… your emotions, your actions, your thoughts. That is what matters now. Not what happened to you in the past, but what will happen to you in the present and in the future. I could sit here and talk more and more about you, but that just shows that I can talk trash, and I ain’t about that. We will see who the better wrestler is. We all know you can talk trash, cause that is all you did. Soon… soon we will see who the better wrestler is. Soon the Rumble will start and soon we MAY get to face one another. Perhaps I won’t even have to face you. Perhaps… you will get thrown out of that ring quicker than you got in. All will be told in time… but not now… now is not the time. Soon though, very soon shall we know who the better man is. With that, Skipster tosses the mic down and slides out of the ring, leaving the arena as the cameras fade to black.
