Leopards are roughly 7 times stronger than human beings.

3rd January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Leopards are roughly 7 times stronger than human beings. 9th Wonder Of Foam Furiously Addictive Wrestling Roleplay #136 Date: 3rd January 2003 Location: Evil Scheme Room …. The Foamy One’s Bedroom – Last Night …. (The 9th Wonder Of Foam sleeps, with every passing second he looks more and more concerned, despite being asleep. He looks hot and bothered, he clutches to something that isn’t even beside him. A door in the distance is open, the light is turned on. Suddenly Foamy wakes.) Foamy: Where is she? (Foamy feels his be sheets, he looks under his bed, in drawers, he then checks his waist. She is gone. Foamy picks up the phone) Foamy: Security, get in here, she’s gone. (Foamy puts the phone down) Foamy: Where are you? You couldn’t have left me, you wouldn’t do that. Someone took you I know they did, I could feel it, even while sleeping. I can feel your distress at not being by my side. (Foamy looks to the faint light coming from his on suite toilet. He walks up to it) Foamy: She is closer than I thought. (Foamy pushes the door opens, Lars sits on the toilet seat polishing the FAW Championship) Foamy: What the hell do you think you are doing? Lars: Just polishing your belt. Foamy: Did she ask you to polish her? No. She speaks to me, she answers only to me. (Foamy slaps Lars off his chair and grabs the belt, he puts it around his waist) Foamy: No don’t apologise, It was his fault we were separated. If she wants polishing Lars I will polish her. Understand? (Lars holding his bleeding face replies) Lars: Ok. (Foamy walks back to his bed, he takes his belt off and wraps his arm around it and falls asleep) …. Evil Scheme Room – Friday Morning …. (Latin Singing sensation Enrique Iglesias, Schmidt, and Lars sits around the table. Lars has a huge white cross representing a plaster on his cheek. The 9th Wonder Of Foam enters with the FAW Championship Belt) Foamy: Gentlemen, the time draws near when the first of many pretenders will try and take her from me. The first is the man who almost desires her as much as I do, but not quite. Besides his love for her is unrequited. Enrique: (Schmidt whispers in his ear) Fear the Mole! Schmidt as an idea for global domination, one that may mean you retain your title. Foamy: I do not need help when it comes to keeping her. Because she does not want to leave my side, and I would fight down to hell and back to make sure she stays mine. Enrique: Ok it’s still an Evil scheme. Foamy: Ok go! Enrique: Well you know when you buy a Twix, there is like a one in a million chance that you will get a finger without the crunch. Foamy: What? Enrique: Fear the mole! Sometimes you will bite into a Twix and it will be all chocolate, there will be no biscuit due to a processing error. Foamy: Ok I see where this is going my blue eyed soul brother. Lars: What? Foamy: This guy is such a hummer. See I blew my top when I heard his plan. Lars: What? Foamy: Nothing. I thought that seeing as Klown is considered such a legend I would try and talk like him, yo brother, mother f**ker bling bling! Lars: Why? Foamy: Pffff don’t know just trying it on. Anyway this afternoon we will go to the Twix factory and take out all the biscuits from their chocolate bars!!!!!!!! (All of them begin an evil laugh) Lars: Oh hang on James Harris is coming round to do an interview. Foamy: Well bring him, it would be good to document our historic plan! …. The Twix Plant – Afternoon …. (Lars, Foamy, Enrique, Schmidt and James Harris armed with a video camera hidden inside a cardboard cut-out of Charlton Heston join a tour of the Twix factory. They all get on an electronic cart for the tour with around seven Japanese tourists. Lars is dressed up as Shirley Bassey, Schmidt is dressed like American President Harry Truman and Enrique is dressed as Britney Spears) Guide: Hello and welcome to the Twix tour. Today we will be showing you how we at the Mars Corporation make Twix bars. Any questions? (James Harris puts his hand to Charlton’s head and begins to fake a puppet voice) Charlton: I have a question, those damn dirty apes! (The guide unphased by the fact that a cardboard cut out of Charlton Heston with a giant glass lens stuck in his mouth is speaking to him replies) Guide: Uh go ahead Mr Heston. Charlton: Well the question isn’t for you; it’s for our other celebrity guest. Guide: Oh you mean Miss Shirley Bassey, (The guide winks at the hairy Danish man in drag) Charlton: No not that one. Guide: You must mean Miss Britney Spears! Enrique/Britney: I suck dick, that’s why I’m so big in the world of music! Charlton: No. Guide: The ghost of Harry Truman? Hang on there are a lot of celebrities on this tour. Charlton: No dam you dirty ape! I mean the FAW champion the 9th Wonder Of Foam! Guide: Why do you want to ask him a question? Charlton: More a series of questions. Guide: Is now the right time for that? We are about to start a tour of a Twix plant. Charlton: Well Charlton Heston was promised and interview with him but he decided to drag Charlton on a crazy trip to a Twix factory in order too… Foamy: Shut it James, uh I mean Charlton. Charlton: But I was only asked here to do an interview I didn’t come here to take part in an elaborate plan to take all the biscuity crunch out of Twix. (The Guide and the seven Japanese tourists look in disgust) Guide: Ok hands up who is part of an international Terrorist group plotting to take the biscuits out of Twix? (Foamy, James, Charlton cut-out, Shirley, Britney & Truman put their hands up) Guide: Shirley! Shame on you! Lars: I’m sorry Mr Guide man. Can we ever make it work between us? Guide: Not now, not now I know the truth. (Foamy whispers to Britney) Foamy: Plan B! (Britney/Enrique gets up. He brings out a microphone and begins to sing “Hit me Baby One More Time”) Enrique: Oh this song sucks, who wants to hear Enrique Iglesias worldwide smash Hero? (They all groan) Foamy: Stick to the plan! (Enrique begins to sing Britney’s song; Lars/Shirley pulls some diamonds out of her hand bag and gives them to the tourists, she also gives one big one to the guide) Lars: Remember kids, diamonds are forever! (The EFWO leg it, James carries the cut-out with him) …. Evil Scheme Room – sometime later …. (They all sit around the table) Foamy: Had it not been for James Harris we could’ve completed our evil plan. Schmidt go away and come up with a new plan. Meanwhile James lets do that damn interview. (They all leave, James begins) James: HaHa Klown finally speaks. His makes his desire for the FAW title clear; he wants it and will stop at nothing to get it back. Foamy: How can we trust the word of a man who comes and goes like the sunrise? Every two months he turns up proclaiming that he is a legend and that he will once again become champion. Then he clams up realises the truth and walks away. James: This is a man who can’t sleep because he wants to become FAW Champion. Foamy: What because he loses sleep he thinks he should have the belt? Well Klown meet me downtown in an hour and I’ll give it back to you. Listen up you jumped up child entertainer. You were a legend, WERE. Your random appearances are become as regular as a sheep with the runs bowel movements. You come, talk a bit and leave again. Your dreams are just that, dreams. James: Ha Ha Klown knows how much that title means to him. Foamy: It means a lot to him? I have thought of nothing since February 19th 2002. When she alerted me to her presence, to her pain. I could’ve simply stolen her from someone’s locker room but no. I knew to truly have her, for her to be mine she needed to feel wanted. So I pursued her for months. Unlike others such as the Vigilante I took no time off when I faltered I kept at it and I am here now. Less than a month from coming back from injury I stepped up and finally took my precious title. She means a lot to you? But you don’t mean squat to her. She loves me, her and I are meant to be together, there is no mountain I will not climb, no cliff I will not jump off, no man that can take her and no so-called-legend who can scare me. James: But Ha Ha Klown has defeated some of the best. Foamy: He defeated JLC so he can defeat me? I am not JLC I am no-one but myself. He needs to learn that I cannot be classified, I cannot be analysed because I am an un-definable entity. As long as she is by my side I cannot be defeated, and in order for her to leave my side I must be defeated, so as you can see the Klown will be going without sleep for a long time. You said yourself Klown that you can’t sleep, how does that help you? Your body will be tired, I am rejuvenated. You are tormented; I am in a state of bliss. You were a legend and come Monday morning after you have packed up your bags for the one hundredth time I will be everything you desire. I will be the legend who ended another. James: Any last thoughts? Foamy: You saw me holding that title Klown and it frightened you to your very core. Just the prospect of me becoming champion has frightened every so called legend before me. Because you along with them held me in such low regard that for me to win the title means that they, and you are not as good as you thought. But its there Klown, you can see who good I am. Watch the Dome match again, see how I never once even thought about defeat. That match Klown where I defeated the past present and future of FAW to win her. That same match where you faulted from the very beginning. What makes you think that you can defeat me? Swearing? Losing sleep? Nothing can Klown. So turn that smile upside down and get used to it. Because you are a ghost, a relic from the past trying to ride the coat tails of the latest king in FAW. And this Sunday I will kick you back down into hell. Where from you will see me and her together for eternity. Have a Foam Filled Day!
