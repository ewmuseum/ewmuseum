Rock 'N' Roll XiN Furiously Addictive Wrestling Roleplay #75 Date: 21-11-02 Location: Junk Yard NB: This is not an actual roleplay, just a very brief promo that will not be on the show due to angle matters, just to clear that up. The area, a desolate wasteland, a broken car frame here, a tyre there, basically it is a car junkyard, it is night time, dust is not settled, the workers evidently only left recently, one seems not to have left at all, he stands there, back to the camera staring up at the moon, he is not tall. He wears a blue and white hoody, baggy blue jeans and white trainers, his hands are in his hoody pocket and he has a pull on hat on. “As a famous man once said,” The man states, “I’ve been expecting you…” The man turns round; it is FAW’s latest signing, Xinao Matthias, or Xin as he is more often known. “I’ve been from Mexico, to Japan to Merry Old England,” Xin continues, “But never have I been more at home than in the US of A. Fighting for FAW, for myself, but more importantly, for all the gold I can dream of, can you imagine holding that sweet World title of FAW’s up in the air and saying ‘I am the best there is and this proves it’? I can, and when that title is mine I will be saying that. But for now I must take care of a little business.” Xin laughs mockingly, he turns and looks at the moon again, continues talking. “There are a few people in FAW,” he moans, “who really shouldn’t be there. One or two in particular, and when I come to FAW, the first show, that person best be ready for a fight, because I’m gonna’ show them up and prove to them, the world, and the bosses, that Xin is the one and only man you need to make a show Rock ‘N’ Roll!” He turns back around with an intent glare in his eyes, the spotlight goes on, he walks towards the camera and punches it and the camera goes fuzzy. “Don’t worry,” Xin’s voice exclaims, “but do panic…” The scene fades showing the FAW Logo and the Panic logo also. The words read “December 1st it begins, December 4th it evolves…”
