ODDS ARE AGAINST ME BUT I SHALL WIN

11/9/02
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
ODDS ARE AGAINST ME BUT I SHALL WIN Spawn Furiously Addictive Wrestling Roleplay #73 Date: 11/9/02 Location: BOSTON (DISTORTIONRP 1) Its is a truly peaceful day in this place Spawn now liked to call home, a place others called the New Breed Gym. Yes, once more Spawn was training his brains out for the upcoming match against Foamy and Neo a tag team match with the Great 1 as his tag team partner, a match Spawn finds to be a one sided against him. In the enormous gym, which seemed dimly lit from the beautiful sunlight outside, the long gray haired, green-eyed man was working away at a blue everlast punching bag. Both furious kicks and punches were being pounded into the big blue bag of sand as it swung viciously from left to right and back then forth. The whole area Spawn was in seemed to look very dark and ominous compared to the wonderful weather outside, most thought it was just to get Spawn into a deep, deep focus. But Spawn knew that there was no real reason for it, that’s just the way the gym was built. Even so he couldn’t help but want to go outside and just bath in the brilliant rays of sun that would feel ever so good…compared to this cold chamber called a training room he was in. But he knew that if he wanted to beat the big ugly Foamy and the idiot Neo he would just have to deal with these crappy conditions for now…after all it would pay off in the long run, right? Spawn decided to stop thinking about it so much and concentrate on the punching bag and fine tuning his skills so he would be all set for Sunday. With every swift punch and lightning fast kick Spawn saw the feeling of a great victory flash before his eyes sending a huge rush of adrenaline throughout his body causing him to go even harder with each passing second he trained. Spawn couldn’t even tell that his knuckles had started to bleed because of the intensity at which he was smacking the punching bag around, he figured it was time for a short break anyway. Spawn: 'Oww…damn, I must be going at this harder than I realize. I mean my hand is almost covered in blood and my feet are so numb I can barley feel them! Heh…well this is what the FAW is to me, getting sore and bleeding all for that one goal of being called the greatest FAW champion of all time, being the very best. Blood, sweat, and tears that’s what it is all about and I have given all three of those to this business and I think my payback is just around the corner in the form of the Great legendary status. True this tag match isn’t going to be easy but…That’s what this Hell Master Bastard is all about! Overcoming these challenges and showing the world that I can compete with the big names!' After taking a short break to bandage up his knuckles with some tape and get the feeling back in his feet Spawn got right back up and started obliterating the poor punching bag. He started to visualize the punching bag as Foamy Neo and the Great 1, which helped a lot, because once he saw those images he decked that punching bag like nobodies business! By this time now Spawn forgot about the scenery outside and was totally focused on the punching bag. The cold chill around him and his breath visible in front of his face, Spawn managed to punch a small hole into the punching bag as small bits of sand began to fall to the ground from the small hole. Of course Spawn being as focused as he was saw this as the Professor’s blood and rule number one was if they bleed show no mercy. So it took about two hours before the punching bag was totally out of sand and Spawn realized that the small hole had drained the sand. He quickly got red with embarrassment and headed out to the New Breed storage room where he knew they had at least one extra punching bag. He opened the huge solid steel door to his training room and was now in the much warmer New Breed lobby with newly cleaned red carpet and red walls…that always freaked him out, to be surrounded by red. But ignoring this simple fact he headed down a small empty hallway and reached a single door all by itself labeled ‘Maintenance’. He opened the single gray door and headed in the room and saw the extra punching bag. Spawn: 'Finally, now I can get back to training…but I wonder if this is enough? I wonder if they have a stronger material, I mean if the last one broke than what will happen to this one? Hmmm…well I guess if this one fails I can always ask Crimson for one, or practice on a trainee!' Spawn started to laugh as he headed out of the maintenance room with the punching bag over his shoulder as he was greeted by…a cameraman. Spawn rolled his eyes as he tried to ignore the camera but figured it was a futile struggle so stopped off at a couch in the New Breed lobby and put the punching bag down beside his chair. The cameraman stood in front of him signaling when he was going to start rolling…like Spawn’s eyes. Spawn: 'So let me guess, everyone wants to hear what I have to say about my match this Sunday…again? Well of course you do and I suppose I can’t blame you because you all want to see the next Great thing in wrestling, am I right again? Damn straight I am! Since you all know that I want to talk just a little bit about my opponents, first lets talk about Foamy? Well he is a freak…so we will just call him foamystein from now on, alright? So back to where I was, this is certainly going to be one tough cookie to crack! I mean he has already sent shivers down my spine in his previous match beating the ever so resilient Xander! Wow, how will I ever top someone who could do that? Its actually very simple, I just show up and I top that, because Foamy…do you realize what kind of trouble you are in now? And you know I always ask my opponents that because I want to see if their heads are on straight. But usually I have to knock it straight for them on Sunday then they realize just how foolish they were. Now Foamystein, you seem like a smart lad, just think about what is going to happen this Sunday for one second. Well, its going to be called the Spawn Advancing to the Next Round match because that is what will happen. Still don’t get it? YOU ARE GOING TO GET AN OLD SCHOOL WHOOPIN. If that doesn’t sink into that brick in your head than nothing will, pal.' After talking for a little while Spawn stretched his arms out to the ceiling then twisted his torso letting out a loud crack. Then Spawn laid back in the small couch and looked around for a short while and then picked up the blue punching bag he had just gotten. Spawn: 'Now look here, you Neo, lets say, for arguments sake, that this punching bag is you. Yes I know you aren’t this handsome but for argument’s sake lets pretend this is you. Look at you, on the outside you seem strong enough and you certainly seem to have a good build here…but something is wrong. Yes, there it is you are full of sand! Neo, instead of being filled with the passion for this business and the want for championship gold you are filled with sand that will eventually be emptied when someone like me comes along and beats the stuffing out of you. Then you will be on the floor and sand will be everywhere and medics will have to come to the ring and scoop you up off the ground and sew you all back together. Now, go back through what I said and replace sand with blood….' Spawn then got up and headed to a water vending machine, which to the New Breed members was free, so he got two bottles of water. He held one in his left hand and threw one to the cameraman, which everyone heard a small thank you from. After twisting the cap open Spawn chugged the water down and let out a refreshed AHHHH then let out a huge burp to signify that his thirst was getting quenched and not by some sugary piss water drink either, just the good stuff. Soon after Spawn sat back down on the couch and put the punching bag back on the floor and took a smaller sip of water then placed the bottle of clear fluid on a table in front of him. Spawn: 'Now Neo, you can snap back at me with the usual, no you are the punching bag Spawn, routine or you can simply die easy. Instead of wasting precious breath and energy just come down to the ring and lay down for the 1…2…3. I mean that way you don’t get your ‘sand’ splashed across the ring and I don’t have to waste the dear fans time with kicking the shit out of you. Or there is also the option of calling in sick and dropping from the tag match altogether and not have to deal with any embarrassment because you ‘suddenly’ caught the flu. Apparently the type of flue you would get would also leave a big yellow streak down your belly. So now I ask you Neo, what would you enjoy more? Getting beaten to high hell by me this Sunday or not showing up and saving you oh so much pain? But yes I know which you will pick…so I guess I will see you Sunday Sandbags. Just remember what I have said…because you know I don’t lie, this Sunday I will pick you apart and you partner Foamy if I have to, if it means getting to risky I will shoot you between the eyes with a shotgun. I am determined to win and not you or anyone can stop me…you can try, but your efforts would be in vain because this Hell Master Bastard isn’t stopping till he wins the match tomorrow.' With that said Spawn got up and then picked up his blue punching bag under his arm and shoved the cameraman out of his way as he headed back to his solitary chamber to train even harder. As always Spawn was very confident that he could take home a victory this Sunday and was almost proof positive that Foamy and Neo wouldn’t be a problem. But as Spawn continued to train his knuckles began to bleed again and his feet soon got sore again as the camera got close to the door to listen to what Spawn was saying inside. Spawn: 'Damn…knuckles are bleeding again, can’t feel my feet. Probably just numb from the temperature, stupid punching bag got another hole in it. I am going to have to go get another one.' As for you Great1 partner or not, you are still my biggest enemy…and if you even think about betraying me tomorrow, the next time the fans see you in a wrestling match. You will be in a wheel chair. Would it be that Spawn may win this Sunday but a bigger force is bringing him down? Is it that the Foamy or Neo, whom are represented by the punching bag, is easily busted through and the bleeding and numbness could be Great 1 attacking again? In the FAW anything is possible and this could or could not be true, but Spawn knew he was going to go to any extent he had to just to win the match. No one was going to get in his way, no one would mess with him…with so much at stake Spawn was totally focused…Spawn was ready and waiting for Sunday Distortion!
