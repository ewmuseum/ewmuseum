The beginning of an end (Panic #1) Xing Furiously Addictive Wrestling Roleplay #274 Date: 22nd February Location: 'Your Life Is Yours' studios “Good Evening ladies and gentlemen, I am your host Troy McClure you may know me from such films as ‘Systems a falling’ or ‘Lover I’m your brother’ you know I was the lover, or you may know me from such advertise like ‘Duff Beer convention’ yes that was me because I am groovy baby yeah.” CUT! The scream of the annoyed director of the ‘Your Life Is Yours’ stopped Troy from making a mockery of his show so the director got in his face and said those three words “Not Groovy baby!’ Troy got the message and packed his bags and left the building crying but the director did not fear he had a ready-made replacement his 12year old son ACTION! Backstage A man believed to be The Meditator dressed in all black clothes. Black trousers, black fleece jumper, black hood covering his face with darkness approaches the director while his son is presenting the show in a very stern foreign voice, “Can you tell the boy to hurry up because I am on schedule at the moment I need to get to my training qwin15 minutes any way you can only stay around losers for so long before you wont to turn on them.” Meditator began to walk back into his sly and cruel position. Back in the arena “Now ladies and gentlemen I will as, usual on this show be reading out this man or woman’s history then you guess from the clues by ringing in at ‘0 I know who the person is 201’ Now this person is a bad man recently joining the FAW federation he is currently the Levitation champion, and recently won the World Asian wrestling award last month, he was involved in a car crash at the age of two his hands were amputated, he was brought up into a rich family who paid off the schools debt that their son went to, If you went to Hai’ Beam high school in Pakistan you would fear him he was the school bully, his parents had know idea of this but pulled him out of the school and then later sued them because he was mugged by 10 members of the school, he was kicked out of his house when he accidentally harmed his mother with his beast hands which he had no idea of, he was then shot but the culprit was quickly caught by himself he hunted down the shooter and took the mans mask off it was his dad he tried to kill him because he was too dangerous to be walking on the streets, he is none other than……. We will tell you after the break! “Hello we are back and here he is……….. I will tell you after the next break only joking. He is Meditator!” Thunder Strikes! Darkness strikes the studio but you can just about track down Meditator in the darkness a Pakistani flag is waved across the screen covering the face of Meditator light shows still his face is unveiled by the Pakistani flag waved in front of him Meditator begins to talk in a stern, foreign and croaky “You see I don’t talk much so I wont change myself now just for you losers viewing this but I will say a little something I am a wrestler not an actor I have come in this fed to fight now, FAW superstars I don’t care who you are you cross my path I will get you how ever I like because I am on a collision course with FAW. It will be a nightmare that you wont awake so be warned FAW and your superstars ‘I am sick and on the loose!’ I have another motto ‘It is time to shut up! And Put up!’ The flag stops it is being lowered we are going to see his face only on ‘Your life is yours’ but no it was getting lowered but the lights turned back off and Meditator makes his quick exit as the boy presenter tries to get a view of the face as he gets ready to sit down. “There we have it Meditator, just nearly saw his face but he was to fast FAW I would be afraid be very afraid because he is sick and on the loose!” The boy ends a successful night on ‘your life is yours’
