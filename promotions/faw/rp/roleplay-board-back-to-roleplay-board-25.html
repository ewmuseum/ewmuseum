Furiously Addictive Wrestling Roleplay Board Roleplay Board | Back to Roleplay Board Xander Entitled: Shadows from an Umpire Written: 7-31-02 Location: Dingy motel room [The scene opens in a local hotel room] 'So many times' Xander decided to start off on this week 'So many times I've come out here... spread the word about why people care, and watch my matches with such interest. Now, not only do I have to cary one other guy through a match, but I have to carry two of them! There's Syd.. who I've already beat... and EDH, who I don't quite understand why he's getting a shot at the most prestigious title in FAW.. but hell, I'll beat him too for the hell of it. EDH, who's been here for a while... former IN champion himself. Mainly because I wasn't even aiming at this title just then.. so he can think he's the top sh*t just because I was new to FAW. But within the first six months, I've had ten title reigns. Five of them Hardcore, and the other five I still have.' 'As Exceptional as you may have been' he continues 'That is neither here, nor there now. Now you step into the ring with the Master of Ratings... The Emperor of Neilsons... you're next loss. I will have both you and Syd so confused, you won't know your ass, from the hand you'll have up each other. I am truely one of the few wrestlers in FAW with the kind of win-loss record I have. I have beaten Spawn, Etan Cross, Syd, Foamy multiple times, and I can within a hair of making Bomber forever remember my name.... so why should I fear EDH? Why should I shiver at the mention of Syd? I shouldn't... and I won't... [The scene fades out] OOC: Sorry it's so short, I wanted to make sure I got one out before the card, and I've been up almost two days with very, very little sleep.... so this isn't up to my standards either.
