Foam Rhymes With Dome PPVRP01

18/12/02
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Foam Rhymes With Dome PPVRP01 9th Wonder Of Foam Furiously Addictive Wrestling Roleplay #106 Date: 18/12/02 Location: The Land Of Gondor …. Foamy’s Secret Underground Lair – Wednesday Morning …. (The 9th Wonder Of Foam sits on his evil throne; Lars opens his mail for him) Foamy: So what have we got Lars me old mucka? Lars: Umm, this letter, it’s dated three weeks ago. Foamy: Whose it from? Lars: The Evil Council of America. Foamy: Ah finally my membership has been accepted. Lars: Yeah but it took three weeks to get here. Foamy: Well the council do run the post office. Lars: Why? Foamy: They run several institutions worldwide to spread unhappiness and evil wherever they can. The British chapter owns the railway system, the German chapter limits the amount of humour released to the general population and the Welsh chapter spreads the Welsh language wherever it can. Hell, it even owns Aol-TimeWarner. Lars: They want you to attend their conference and make a speech. Foamy: When is the conference? Lars: Today, this afternoon in fact in Boston. Foamy: Lars, order me some Concorde tickets, we’re going to Boston! …. Boston – Wednesday Afternoon …. (Foamy enters the conference centre; there are hundreds of EVIL people walking around mingling, Lars stands there in amazement) Lars: My god! Megatron! Skeletor! Alan Rickman! Foamy: I know this is where the cream of the world’s evil meet for liver beans and Chianti. (Jack Nicholson in full “the Joker” make-up takes to the podium) Jack: Hey everybody, I’m glad you could make it. It’s time now for our first guest speaker, please welcome from our English chapter, Alan Rickman! (Rickman takes to the stage, he makes several evil faces at the crowd and begins to speak) Alan: Welcome one, welcome all. It has been a good year for the evil of this world, coffee shops are up thirty-fold, and we have successfully given salted popcorn a 40% market share. I myself have recovered from several performances for the goodies by signing up for Die Hardest, as that German terrorist’s half brother. (The congregation clap) Alan: The real estate value of our underground lairs has risen by 37%. Not since the sale of the Pac-man blueprints to the Soviet Union in the eighties has the council of evil had such a good year. But gentlemen times are changing. (The Red Skull shouts “here, here” from the crowd) Alan: Yes times are changing but we must, we will adapt with them. In 1963 we bought the world’s only pointed rubber ear company, and one year later we brought noted actor Leonard Nimoy into our stable to develop a science fiction programme that would keep production of these ears steady. When that programme was cancelled did we close-up our rubber ear company? No we made sure that over the course of the next twenty years Nimoy produced no less than ten movies all needing a huge order of rubber, pointed ears. And during the slump in the mid-nineties did we close-up shop then? No, we forced antipodean horror director Pete Jackson to make the Lord Of The Rings, increasing profits by 1000%! (The crowd cheer, most notably Darth Vader, a congregation from the Aliens movies and a bunch of Klingons.) Alan: If the hero’s of this world think they can fight us then we will remove the fibre from their food and give them inoperable haemorrhoids leaving them incapable of fighting crime. With our mind controlling video games such as Grand Theft Auto 3 we will pre-programme hundreds of children to be corrupt thieves, bypassing the normal hypnotizing umbrella trick. (The Penguin looks at this umbrella and sighs) Alan: We will make sure that the long black hoods, capes and leather jackets once again become the fashion of evil, and not made fashionable by the new Matrix movies. When we introduced salted popcorn no one wanted to eat it, but what did we do? We simply made sure that the right flavour popcorn was never in the correct section leaving millions of consumers eating the wrong popcorn. And now I’d like to introduce one of the leading lights of Evil in the modern world, he is our new hope for domination of the wrestling sector, forget Sergeant Slaughter, Ric Flair or Triple H, your new wrestling Anti-Hero is the man once voted the greatest Wrestling hero of all time, the 9th Wonder Of Foam! (The crowd give a confused clap; the Foamy One takes to the podium) Foamy: Gentlemen, the world of professional wrestling is ripe for domination by evil. For years it has been ruled by those who consider themselves evil, yet have no hidden fortress, no pool filled with flesh eating sharks, men who don’t even own a Nehru jacket. These men simply add the work “Dark” to a name and think that they are the new Dr No. People such as the Klown think that having Dark in their title makes them the new criminal presence in the world. (Foamy coughs) Foamy: But no more. I will lead the way, no longer must the evil geniuses of this world sit back from a far and let other men do our bidding, we must strike first, and with our own fists. This Sunday on pay-per-view I face seven men in a dome for the FAW World Championship. Am I going to complain? No. Also on the same card my evil companions Neo & Sabatash take on the Klown and The Crimson one for the tag team titles. Most evil geniuses would want to take both belts on the same night. But I know that this won’t happen. Which is why it is regrettable that my friends won’t come out of the night with the titles, but at least I will walk out the FAW World Champion. Any questions? (A rather short looking Darth Vader raises his arm) Darth: Um Foamy, what do you make of your opponents in the title match? Foamy: Some of them I would not even recognise If I was walking down the street. But some of them I have been waiting for. I have been waiting for a time where I did not care who I defeated or how I defeated them. In the old days respect would be given but respect leads to complacency, you can’t be all friendly to a man whose career you want to end. Having been the hero I know a hero’s weakness, before I would have to play to the crowd, but now I’m out there for one purpose, to make me happy. Darth: The match pits you against men you have never beaten and men you have barely beaten what makes you think you can do it this time around, and against all of them in one go? Foamy: In order to make a statement, in order to make men fear you it is required that you make a great sacrifice, that you do something so against the odds that you are laughed at. Not only do I have the odds against me, I have the fans against everyone and me in the lookeroom against me, what better stage to make the statement of my career than this one? There are good men in that ring who would trust me if I extended the hand of friendship, men who would build and alliance with me for that one match, but the fact is where they would honour that alliance I would spit on it, stomp on it and un-button my fly and urinate on it. If The Great One saw two men teaming up Renegade he would come to his aide, hell there was a time when I would, but now I will laugh. Darth: What about the British Bomber, only two months ago you were aligned in the Dark Carnival. Foamy: Those ties are broken because I was foolish to think they meant anything in the first place. At the start I thought to be evil was to bully people, to out number them and crush them, this is true, but it is not what drives me. Now everyone is the same, everyone is the same ego, everyman is the same size, everyman is the same person who is scared at the prospect of the end. Take Spawn, a man who came very close to being my nemesis. A man who once claimed that my need to attention from the fans meant that I would never claim the prize as my own, well now he faces not the Foamy One driven by the need to hear his name chanted a man who does not need to see his face in magazines and his name mentioned on the internet. Darth: What drives you then? Foamy: Greed, pure greed, and the one thing that means I will rule them all. For two months I was pretty much out of it, I wrestled for a while and then an injury forced me out. And for that month the one thing that drove me was the need, the craving for that Championship. I spent every night dreaming of it. I saw FAW nearly die, knowing that I was the one man capable of restoring it to glory. And I became angry that I was not the man who could go out there and claim it. And overtime it was not the need to see FAW survive for the fans; it was the need to see FAW survive for my own glory, FAW will live on because I let it. The Great One fights for the fans, Lee Smith for recognition, Spawn the need to give pain, Rzarector for god, Scott Costella because he has a wish to fulfil and Bomber because it means is isn’t quite past it yet. Darth: So why the… Foamy: Shut up “Darth”, or should I say…. James Harris? (Lars comes up behind him and takes his helmet) Foamy: Don’t fool me James, Darth Vader was never five-foot nine. James: Sorry I just couldn’t get you interviewed any other way. Foamy: Let me continue and you shall get it. The Great One’s weakness is his fans, he must do what makes them happy, he cannot go out there and do anything, and if he wins the fans will still demand more of him, they will still want him to go out there and win every night, something that cannot be done. Lee Smith’s desire to stand out from the crowd means he will do almost anything to do what he wants, but the fact is his desire to stand-out from the crowd and impress will lead him to make foolish errors because he is doing it simply to make his name. Spawn wishes to give pain, no matter the cost, and admiral cause I know, but pain comes at a price, you cannot keep focused on that title when what drives you is the need to hurt others. Rzarector has to answer to his God, he who has rules, and commandments, you cannot murder I will. Scott Costella is billed as the real Prodigy, a heavy burden to bear one that will make him prone to mistakes. And Bomber is desperate to prove that he still has what it takes, when all that it would take to make him leave this land is a bigger paycheck. James: Then why are you so different? Foamy: Rzarector asks a similar question. Why should he fear me? Wee Rzarector it is because I am doing this for myself, I answer to no God, I do not have to dish out so much pain to feel satisfied, I will not feel the burden of making people happy. I am in this match for one reason, to become FAW World Champion. Ever since I entered this company ten months ago I’ve been touted as a potential World Champion. Something that I believed could happen, but defeat after defeat after defeat and I am still no closer to my precious prize. Being the good guy never paid off because I had rules to follow, fans to please and a cause to champion but now I live by no rules, I make no-one happy but myself and my soul cause in life is to become FAW Champion, I will betray any friend, smite any enemy and spill the blood of my brothers if it means being FAW Champion. And now I would like to lead the congregation in a verse of the Council Of Evil’s anthem “Hit Me Baby One More Time”. (The congregation stands, as Emperor Palpatine, Christopher Lee, Alan Rickman, The Joker, Lex Luther etc sing along) Have a Foam Filled Day!
