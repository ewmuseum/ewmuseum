One short, but one more Distortion #1 Rzarector Furiously Addictive Wrestling Roleplay #3 Date: October 15, 2002 Location: Middle of Nowhere ~A preacher is someone who is supposed to preach the word of God, but not every preacher worships God.~ *The screen opens to a local night scene. People are all around having fun, prositutes lining up the streets, drug trades going on. The camera crew is walking down the streets doing a documentary as they start to feel a strange prescense. As if something was following them, something was bleeding. The camera crew keeps wlaking, now shaking, looking for a good interviewer. When it hit them. They passed a dark alley and on the inside they heard howling and hissing. As if someone was in pain. They started to walk in and saw two green lights glowing at them and a set of white lights. They started walking more in. Then to there astonishment there he was, a figure in a black hoooded robe with green eyes and white fangs. The camera crew went silent, as Rzarector hissed one more time.* Rzarector: I sensed you two coming. It was so obvious that you are out here to cause trouble. Camera Man 1: No sir, no we did not intend to cause trouble. Rzarector: Well what should I think then? *The men start shaking as one of them says out the one words.* Camera Man 2: Oh god, who art in heaven, hear me, forgive me please, I do not want this mercy in my life. Rzarector: What are you saying? CM2: I was praying to God. *Rzarector smirks.* Rzarector: You'll have to pray better than that. Look at me, tell me what you see? *The two camera men peer into his eyes, as if peering into the heavens, there they begin to cry.* CM1: I see torture, pain, broken hearts, its so sad.What has happened to you that has been so bad? Rzarector: I was once a fallen angel, hypnotized by Satan himself. Now I am God's angel. Yet you sit there and pray to him, even though your a sinner. You sit and pray because you think your about to die. Well you are not going to die, but you will suffer someday. Understand? CM2: Yes sir, we're very sorry, I don't want to suffer. Rzarector: Even the most blessed will suffer. Heartbreak, I've had it always. And I will never get rid of it. CM1: What is your name sir? *Suddenly the wind stops blowing, the trees stop shaking, all noises stop. There Rzarector lets out a slow whisper....* R.....z......A.....r.....E.....c....T......o....R *The men go silent as they run back up the alley, as they do Rzarector's green eyes light up and he gives a loud hissing sound towards them. Then they are gone, Rzarector looks back into the camera they dropped, still airing and begins to talk.* Rzarector: Last night, last night was a fluke. Crimson Lord beating me, giving me my first loss in the FAW. I deserved that win, for Lord is the lord of the satanists, I am the lord of the Godly, and the lord of the dead. All who hear my word fall to there knees weeping. Yet my voice did not have a paralyzing effect on Crimson. Well that doesnt matter, becasue again this Sunday me and Renegade have a match against Crimson Lord and The Duke. Then we will see who is the better man, Crimson or me. And yet, if somehow this match is interrupted, then I want Crimson 1v1 at a upcomign PPV, to settle it all. Crimson beward, for your day is coming, you rend is coming soon. *Rzarector hisses as the camera shuts off.*
