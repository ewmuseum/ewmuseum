Darkened Days (Panic 2) Furiously Addictive Wrestling Roleplay #98 Date: 12.12.02 Location: Houston's National Herald Cemetery If the day gets any darker, the moon would dim out. The sun wouldn’t even be able to find it’s way back up the east around all the planets in this pitch black universe... That’s only if the day gets any darker... ::: Scene: Houston’s National Herald Cemetery. 2:42 am. HaHaKlown is wearing a Gucci midnight blue tuxedo (with a midnight blue tuxedo shirt and matching Gucci shoes, with a black tie...oh and of course...his midnight blue and white clown paint) and is seated at the base of a grave’s monument that reads ‘ARIES’ embossed into the stone. “1942 - 2002”. “A son. A father. A priest.”. He is seated with his back resting on the hind side of the grave and it’s front. Thick clouds of smoke can be seen blowing from the direction he is seated. HaHaKlown is smoking on a Newport cigarette, and sipping on a half empty pint of Hennessy. He resembles a person without anything left in the world, looking past the paint. All the while he has been mumbling to his self, while mourning in the cemetery... ::: HaHaKlown: You always said I’d amount to fucking shit...damnit. YOU NEVER FUCKING BELIEVED IN ME...hehe...well I sure showed you, didn’t I? DIDN’T I YOU DEAD MOTHER FUCKER...hehe....hehe.... ::: This is the grave site of HHK’s father, Reverend Trais Joseph Aries Sr. The only loss in all reality that has occured since then are the loss of bruises and welts HHK’s mother would recieve...yet mainly because she passed away as well. The Reverend never really gave a shit about either HaHaKlown nor his mother. He would always make it crystal clear that HaHaKlown could be any number of guys due to his mother being such an incredible whore. He would also make sure that his mother had nothing to say about this either, because she must KNOW and UNDERSTAND that no matter how much she wasn’t or isn’t a whore...WHAT HE SAYS GOES. Trais Sr. always made sure of such things of the sort. His mother was no better than what he claimed though. Due to the Aries’ family being so desperately poor, his mother would often prostitute to scrounge up enough money for at least lil’ Klown to get a bite to eat. Although HaHaKlown loved his mother with all his klowny heart, he had learned to despise his father. Because of his father’s drunken tantrum fits in which he would physical kick the living shit out of his mother’s ass. Because of how his father would bring home different women (and sometimes young girls) and force my mother to watch...thinking it was some sort of retaliation for her prostitution. He hated his father for what he had done to his mother for the numerous years of their existence. Even so...he had always felt strongly compelled to attempt and make his father proud. He strived to somehow achieve even the smallest hint of appreciation. He had, anyway...until the day his father went too far... ::: HaHaKlown: Every fucking day it was the same fucking bullshit from you....sniff...sniff...” IF YOU DON’T LIKE IT BOY...THEN WHY DON’T YOU JUST FUCKING LEAVE? “....” I SHOULD BEAT YOUR SORRY LITTLE ASS LIKE I BEAT YOUR WORTHLESS CRAP MOTHER. “....Jesus...I fucking hate you. But I’m still here... ::: takes a huge gulp ::: HaHaKlown: Because I, for some fucked up reason, feel sorry for what I did to you...I shouldn’t of taken the law into my own hands, dad....I shouldn’t of... ::: he then bursts into a massive crying spell. Simultaneously, one of the cemetery’s security guards is making way towards the scene. ::: Guard: Say....say.....HEY YOU OVER THERE?!? ::: turning up from his kneeling position, HaHaKlown looks into the direction of the voice ::: Guard: What are you some kind of freak? Get the hell out of here, we’ve been closed for 6 hours now. Come on and git’, before I call the police on you, weirdo. HaHaKlown: Uh...sniff...sniff...sir, I’m here visiting my dad. Guard: That’s nice, kid, but like I said...visiting hours are over. So get outta’ here before I call the cops, damnit. They’d love a nutcase dressed in make-up like you. They’d tear you to pieces, freak....hehe.... ::: the guard turns to walk away, and knocks into HaHaKlown, who just a second ago was several yards away from him ::: Guard: What the fu... HaHaKlown: You mother fucker...have you no respect for the fucking dead? Let's make your visiting hours for the dead, permanent. ::: HaHaKlown snaps his neck and lets his lifeless corpse flop to the ground, making the sound somewhat like a load of wet laundry being thrown unto the floor. He readjusts his tie, and makes way back to the grave ::: HaHaKlown: Dad...I shouldn’t of done what I did...but you know I only did it because I loved mom. And I wasn’t going to watch you hit her again....not even once more, dad. I couldn’t take it anymore. I knew that mom was helpless and defenseless against you. Without you she figured that our family would starve, even if she was making her dirty money on the side...she fucking whored herself just so you could have a few beers when you got home from your shit paying job preaching the word of a God that you weren‘t the slightest bit obedient to. She did everything for you...and all you could do was torment her with both physical and with utmost inner pain. I’m sorry for what I did...but you left me with no god damn choice. ::: HaHaKlown takes the last few chugs of his Hennessy and throws it at his father’s headstone, shattering shards of broken glass through the cool night sky. ::: HaHaKlown: After I came home and found that you had killed my very own fucking mother...after I saw the bloody pulp of what was left of my mother after you went to fucking far and beat the life out of her...after I witnessed that scene...that scene that I had the power to stop only if I hadn’t of waited til’ the last mother fucking second....I was left with no option but to slit your god forsaken throat. You were the most evilest wicked man I ever have known to this very fucking day. You made me the heartless killer that I am today, dad. You made me commit patricide at the age of 16. How could you call yourself a father, much less a priest? ::: HaHaKlown then pulls out his hard-pack of Newport cigarettes from the inside pocket of his tuxedo. He pulls out the last cigarette in the box, ‘The Lucky One’. After a quick tap of the bottom of the cigarette to the headstone (to pack it more), he places the cigarette between his paint covered lips. He then unveils a quart-size container from within his pocket. It is a clear bottle containing some sort of liquid. He pours the liquid up and down the grave and all over the headstone. He lights his cigarette using his ‘HHK’ Zippo lighter. ::: HaHaKlown: You know, dad. :::inhale::: I guess, in a way...everything worked out for the best. I mean...had I of actually saved mom from you...she could never honestly be content ever again. :::exhale::: Knowing that most of her life was spent with a hypocritical :::inhale::: abusive priest bitch...wasting her whole life on you. And after I had spilled your blood... :::exhale::: ...and for the first time felt the warmth thickness of your life juice seep between my youthful fingers, :::inhale::: and splatter my face covered and drenched...I learned the way of the wicked. :::exhale::: When I finally had killed off my worst nightmare...instead of feeling a sense of release or relief... :::inhale::: ...I felt altered by the entire situation. Almost like...for the first time in my entire life...I could see. :::deep exhale::: ::: He just stands there puffing on his cigarette lost in recollection. ::: HaHaKlown: I’d love to stay and chat, dad...but I must be running now. I have an important match in a couple of days, and I need to start training. You see...I made myself something. It doesn’t matter how many times you beat and abused me night after night...because you know what? In that fucking ring...NO ONE CAN BEAT ME. NO ONE ABUSES ME. IN THAT FUCKING RING, DAD, I DO THE GOD DAMN BEATING... ::: Upon this, HHK flicks his still lit cigarette butt towards the headstone. The cigarette hits the word “father” and explodes into a immense burst of flames with such intensity that it knocks HaHaKlown off his feet in the other direction. K regains balance and stands on his feet, posing in a manner that can only be described as overwhelming pride. He watches the roaring fire of the grave, and smiles. As he turns away, he stops in his tracks and looks back at the tremendous inferno ::: HaHaKlown: hehe...I’m not sorry for this though, dad...because if God was too foolish to eternally damn you into the flames of Hell...I just did it for him. ::: The Klown turns away and walks towards the gaping rupture in the side of the cemetery gate where he had earlier driven his Suburban into. ::: Alas...the day just got very much more dark...
