Spawn — FAW Roleplay #19

captured 12 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
SpAwN--The Hell Master \~~~~~~~(opening scene)The scene Faw house show, it is the beginning of the show as we still see a lot seating empty. The camera goes to the back were we see a lot of the wrestlers getting ready for the event, then we see Spawn coming in through the back with his back pack and going straight to the lockers. Inside the locker Spawn sees a picture hanging on his door. Spawn gets outraged and tears the paper from the door, he then throws his bag on the floor and leaves his locker room towards the other lockers rooms, opening all the locker doors as he goes along but still in a fury. Spawn: Hey did you see the person that put this picture on my locker? Wrestler 1: No I did not, I just got here my self. Spawn: (opening another locker room) Yo buddy did you notice any one coming into my locker room and putting this picture on my locker door. Wrestler 2: (looks at the picture and starts to laugh) he, he, he, now that's one funny picture who took that you ha, ha, ha, Spawn: Oh you find this amusing do you? ok (Spawn grabs a chair and hits him over the head with it)blahm! How's for amusing you son of a bitch that will teach you not to laugh at people you fuckin idiot (Spawn leaves the locker room and goes to the next one) Wrestler 3: Hey! Don't you see I'm changing here what are a pervert get the fuck out of here! Spawn: Shut the fuck up I'm going to ask you only one time did you anyone put this picture on my locker door? Wrestler 3: No man I did not! now do you mind! Spawn leaves the locker area and heads to the prez office along the way he is stopped by a reporter. Reporter: Hey Spawn wait minute ! I think I can help you out. Spawn: How can you help me out so far no one can so what is so special about you? Reporter: I'm pretty darn sure I know who put that picture on your locker door. Spawn: Yeah then tell me? Reporter: I will tell you but you have to give me something in return. Spawn: What is it you want speak! Reporter: An exclusive interview with you about the ins and out of wrestling or should we say wrestling's darkest secrets. Spawn: What are you retarded? no way are you going to get that type of information from me so you better just move a long. Reporter: Come on let's reasonable if I don't get it from you somebody is bound to sell himself and give me the information, now I figure you being the world champion and are pretty big in here you would hand over the info to me. Spawn: Is that what you think? I don't need your money. But let me ask you a question? Reporter: Sure go right ahead. Spawn: Are you a FAW reporter? Reporter: Yes I am what kind of question is that? Spawn: So you huh well you see it's kind of funny why would a FAW reporter would try to hurt the federation that feeds his ass. Just doesn't make any sense. Reporter: Now that is some strong accusations, why would I do something stupid like that. Spawn: I don’t know and I do not really care all I know if you are not out of my face within the count of three I am going to get medieval on your ass. Reporter: Let me Just…..(Spawn starts to count) Spawn: 1(pauses), 2(pause), and thre…….( the reporter runs out of sight) Spawn still angry about the picture decides to go see the Prez. He knocks on the door and goes inside, he takes a seat and begins to speak with him. Prez Clinton: Hey world champ what can I do for you? Spawn: (Spawn put the picture on top of the desk) Did you see this? Prez Clinton: Whoa! What the hell! Who did this? Spawn: That’s what I came to ask you if you know the person that did this? Look at this picture it has my family with bad words on it and what looks like bullets holes on their foreheads, I swear to you if I find out who did this I will simply kill his ass. Prez Clinton: Man I tell you I am as stumped as you are I just don’t know what to say. Spawn: Let me tell you this has better not be the work of the crimson lord, because if it is he will feel my fist all over his forehead. Prez Clinton: Why would the Crimson Lord do something like this it just doesn’t seem right. Spawn: I don’t know right now. Maybe out of jealousy or to try to piss me off and get my mind out of our cage match. But that would be an impossible task, and he knows it. Prez Clinton: So far the guy has been on the up an up with me so I would doubt he would put up a stunt like that, it could be possible but I doubt it. Spawn: Nothing is possible. Look at this? you see this belt, this belt will make anybody do the impossible, that’s how big and important this belt is. It can make sell out your friends, it can make betray your friends, and it can make you hate the person that you want to take it away from. Because is all about the competition who wants it the most. Prez Clinton: Well if he did do it then you do not have to worry about chasing him all over the place, because you will have him this Thursday. Spawn: He is lucky I do not go looking for his ass right now as pissed off as I am, no body and I mean nobody threatened my family and gets away with it. It is something I won’t let anyone slide at all. Prez Clinton: I can understand how you feel and I will do my best to get an investigation going into this matter, so don’t you worry none, I will take care of it. Spawn: Please do. Oh and one more thing did you know that there is this reporter going around saying he will pay any wrestler in here if they could give him some dark secrets about the wrestling world. Prez Clinton: No I didn’t know about that. Spawn: Well you better check on that too. I got to go so I will see you later. Spawn leaves Prez Clintons office and goes back to the locker room he starts to change but is still so upset about the picture that he decides to leave for the day. He puts everything back inside his bag, and leaves the locker room heading straight for the parking lot to get his car. As he is getting inside his car he spots the fake reporter talking to a wrestler, Spawn decides to go over there and see what is going on. When he gets there the wrestler looks at Spawn and leaves. Spawn then starts to speak to the reporter. Spawn: What are you stupid? Didn’t I tell you to get the fuck out of here! Reporter: Man you got it all wrong I’m telling you I work for this place. This is just a special assignment is all. Spawn: A special assignment! I got a special assignment for you!(grabbing the reporter by the shirt and lifting him from the ground) Reporter: Hold it, hold it please!! You don’t know what you are getting yourself into. Spawn: That’s ok I’m aiming to find out ( he smacks the reporter) Reporter: You can’t touch me! I’ll sue your ass! this is freedom of the press! You just can’t do this! Spawn: Like I said it’s ok I got a lot of money so I’m not worried about being sued, but I feel sorry for you. (smacks the reporter again, the reporter then starts to scream) Reporter: Somebody ! anybody! Help me please!! Don’t you all just stand there help me! Pleeeease!!! Every body just stand there watching as spawn smacks the reporter around and throws him to the ground. Spawn: You know what I change my mind I’m going to let you survive, but I want you to get the fuck out of here and never come back and if I see you around this area I will no doubt kick your ass so bad that your own mama won’t even recognize you. You got that? Reporter: Yeah yeah! man I am out of here please don’t hurt me! The reporter leaves in a hurry as Spawn is left standing by the parking lot still pissed about the picture, and talking to himself saying that this is far from over. The scene fades to black.
