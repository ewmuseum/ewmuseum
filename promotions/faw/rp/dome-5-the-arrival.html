Dome #5, The Arrival

Dec 22, 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Dome #5, The Arrival Rzarector Furiously Addictive Wrestling Roleplay #120 Date: Dec 22, 2002 Location: Middle of Nowhere (The screen opens to show Rzarector inside of his famous church. The walls are still cleaned from the last time we saw it. Everything looks in beautiful shape. So does Rzarector. He looks happy. He has several bags in his hands as the Priest walks up to Rza. They start a small conversation.) Rzarector: Well, Priest, it looks like its almost time for me to go. Priest: I know, I would have never of thought that tonight, you would be fighting for that world title. Rzarector: I know, it seems crazy but it is true. That is exactely what is going to happen. Yet I'm facing alot of good people in this match. I hope I come out the victor. The only real competition I'm facing is Foamy, Spawn made a stand there for a second but dropped out of the race. Now it is just British Bomber and Lee Smith. Both these people have great talent. Priest: Yes they do. You have every thing you need? Rzarector: Yes Father. Priest: Well you got your plane tickets in your bag? Rzarector: Yes Father. Priest: Very well, your plane takes off very shortly. I think you should get going. Rzarector: Yes that would be a good idea. (Rzarector hugs the old Priest and hisses as he starts to leave the huge church. He walks outside and the New York air is very musty. The media says breathing one day of air here is equal to smoking 2 packs of cigarettes. I never really beleived them. Several people are walking down the streets: punks, goths, preps, businessmen, business women, and your typical bum. Today was a new day for Rzarector, for he felt good and prepared for his match coming up tonight in the Dome. So many thoughts ran through his head. Was he ever conceited? No. Did he ever feel like he should win this match? No. Rzarector is just a very weird person. Sometimes he is good, sometimes he is bad, other times he is just neutral. Yet Rzarector does have a very big heart. Well people are walkin by in the streets and Rzarector flags a taxi. The taxi, a yellow old looking junk pile, immediately swerves around to pick up Rzarector. Rza jumps in.) Cab Driver: Where to buddy? Rzarector: To the airport. Cab Driver: Whats the occassion? Its almost Christmas. Rzarector: I'm a professional wrestler, we have a PPV tonight. I fight for the world title. Cab Driver: Isn't that something, I stopped watching professional wrestling when people like Omega Fro, HaHaKlown, and Pyro all went missing. They said they never found them. Rzarector: I'll tell you a secret. HaHaKlown is back. Omega Fro is back. And Pyro is rumoured to be coming back. Now are you going to buy the PPV tonight? Cab Driver: I'll try to. With a generous tip. Could you do something for me on TV? Rzarector: No. Cab Driver: Why not? Rzarector: You are wanting fame. You figure if I do something for you on TV, that everyone will know it was you. That is greedy and self centered. I shall never help someone's name to get publicized on TV. Cab Driver: As you say mister. You sure your a wrestler? You seem pretty weird. Rzarector: I am just not a wrestler. I am the Black Priest, the Black Angel of Death, and I'm also God's apprentince. I am Rzarector. Cab Driver: Wait a minute, I heard them talking about you on 20/20. They said you were schizophrenic and the FAW allowed you to fight for them. Is that true? Rzarector: No, why would anyone beleive I am schizophrenic? For the fact that I worship God. Which alot of these people in this lifetime no longer do. I've been sent to Earth to spread a message. That is what I tend on doing. Now either your with me, or your not. God comes in many forms, yet there is only one way to get to him. Cab Driver: I understand.......but well here we are. At the airport, that will be 11.93. (Rzarector reaches into his dark black hooded robe and pulls at a ten and a five. He hands it to the taxi driver and tells him to keep the change. As the Taxi Driver looks happy he speeds back off into the citiy. Rzarector heads for the airports. This is a huge airport. Several planes flying everywhere. Rzarector looks at the stone walls, and see how they are painted white, and still look brand new. He walks in and hands the lady his ticket.) Lady at Desk: OK Mr. Rzarector, the plane boards in about 15 minutes. If you would like to have a seat, we will make an announcement whenever it is time. Rzarector: Thankyou. (Rzarector goes back to his seat and sits down awaiting. As the announcement that the plane is loading blares over the speaker. Rzarector follows a short blonde headed girl onto the plane. He enters, and finds his row and seat number. Rzarector sits down awaiting his partner in this 2 hours flight. Rzarector orders some water as none other than James Harris walks up and sits beside Rzarector.) James Harris: Hello Rzarector. How are you doing today? Rzarector: Sinning. James Harris: Awww I see, so whats up? I can't wait for the big PPV tonight. I take it that is where your headed? Rzarector: Yes Mr. Harris. James Harris: Well listen, I don't have a camera on me. Yet I got a pen and paper. Now I'm going to write down a few things for you since we are running out of time. Just basic questions ya know? What do you think about your opponents? Are you going to win? Just simple questions like that. (Rzarector hisses causing everyone on the plane to look at him.) Rzarector: James I didn't come here for this. James Harris: I know but it is my job, and we haven't heard from you in a couple of days. So please just answer these one questions for me. OK? Rzarector: Yes. (James Harris pulls out a pen and paper as he begins to write down all his questions. Then he begins asking Rzarector.) James Harris: Starting off, what do you think of Bomber? The fact he said that he would be suprised if you walked out the champion? Rzarector: You know Bomber, we were both in DCV together. To tell you the truth I didn't like you back then. I don't like arrogant attitudes, greedy people, and rich people. These types of people burn in hell. It is true, very true. Now Bomber you don't think I will walk out champion? Well I may not walk out champion, but I will walk out beating you. I guarentee this, I shall pin you tonight, in our match. Understand? James Harris: Is that all? Rzarector: Yes Bomber, I will stay out of you and Lee Smiths way. James Harris: OK, now onto the next opponent. Renegade, we haven't heard from him yet. Rzarector: Me and Renegade were once tag team partners. A very unlikely tag team, but a good tag team. Why? We relied on each other. I didn't have to rely just on myself. Yet now, Renengade, well I hate to say it, but Renegade is a has been. It is true. I expected more out of you for some reason. After they took away our titles, I staid in condition. I kept training and working hard. You on the other hand, well you are way out of condition. Yet tonight, you may have a chance to walk out world champion. I hope you do, that might get you back on the right course. James Harris: Sad words for Renegade. Moving on...........how about The Great One? Rzarector: The Great One, my personal favorite wrestler of all time. Well not my favorite, but I do like his wrestling style. The Great One truly is Great. Yet I see a new power forming tonight. I see something major going to happen, only time will tell Great One. James Harris: Well that was short. Very short. Ummmmm well I guess move on to Spawn. Rzarector: Rumour has it Spawn that you quit the FAW over the fact that they censored you on TV. I heard, well rumour has it that you left for the NGW, now I don't know if that is true. Yet I would definently take you out of this world title match. Since you are not worthy for this match. Spawn you always were a great competitor and I was looking forward to fighting you in the future. Yet now this is your last match. So Spawn, I bid you a farewell. I will see you in the dome tonight. James Harris: Thats funny. Everyone is dissing on Spawn. Rzarector: I'm not dissing on him. I'm just telling the facts. James Harris: I see. Rzarector: There is a difference. (Rzarector hisses as his green eyes glow out.) James Harris: OK next question, ummmm what do you think about Lee Smith? Rzarector: I think this is an exceptional wrestler. Yet greed has caught him to. Ever since he added Legendary, he has sold his soul to the Devil. That is what I beleive, Lee may someday change, James. Yet for right now, he remains on the evil side. Influenced by Satans way. If you ask me I'm really in a handicap match. God's angel vs. 8 Satan's Angels. It just doesn't seem fair. Yet I am very good at collecting skulls of devils, so therefore I will be prepared for tonight. (James Harris laughs.) Rzarector: Whats so funny? James Harris: Seek devil skulls? That is very crazy. Rzarector: Yes, have you repented lately? (James Harris starts shaking as Rzarector flashes his fangs at James.) James Harris: Well OK, onto the almost last question. What about Foamy, what do you think of Foamy? Rzarector: I made my whole last TV appearence go towards Foamy. So you should know what I think. But for those of you who don't...............Foamy is evil. I am giving him til this Sunday to correct from his evil ways. If he does not, then I will be forced to take actions. These actions will not be pretty, because I have shown to be a very violent person when slaying demons. Yet I think Foamy will figure that one out on his own. Do you get what I am saying? James Harris: Yes I do. Well last question, then we will go to sleep for an hour. Or get really drunk. Rzarector: I don't drink. James Harris: Good, neither do I. Rzarector: (Hisses) James Harris; Do you think you are going to win this match tonight? Rzarector: I don't know. It just all depends in wrestling. People have good days, and people have bad days, it just all depends on where your at and where your going. I'm going there feeling good ready to give it my all. Spawn is going in there knowing he is leaving. Then we got Hoefel maybe being in the match. Yet I doubt it. Because i think they said the winner. James Harris: Well thankyou for your time Rzarector. (An announcement comes over the intercom.) Intercom: We will be landing in 15 minutes. Please fasten your seatbelts. (Rzarector and James Harris fasten there seatbelts as Rzarector lays back and thinks about what is coming up for him tonight. He can't wait because he is so excited. The plane then goes in a downward slope and lands. Rzarector grabs his bags and walks off of the plane. The scene cuts with him walking to the Rent-a-Car center.)
