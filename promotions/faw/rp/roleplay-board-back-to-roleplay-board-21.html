Joe La Fontaine — FAW Roleplay, August 2002

August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Furiously Addictive Wrestling Roleplay Board Roleplay Board | Back to Roleplay Board Joe La Fontaine Entitled: White Boy Junkie Written: 7th aug 2002 Location: Tragedy Ann's Sqaut, outskirts of town La Fontaine: I know what you are doin’… ***Tragedy Ann spins around and is confronted by her stable mate “The Bad Man” Joe La Fontaine. La Fontaine has followed Ann into the outskirts of town, an old slum area with empty, damp boarded up tenements. She had been acting strangely for a very long time…then again Tragedy Ann had never exactly acted “normal”…but recently she had been acting weirder than usual. La Fontaine found out why she was acting so strangely. He has seen what nobody else saw. The shining metal. The corruptness of the black numbered scale. La Fontaine has confronted Tragedy Ann in her squat, an abandoned flat on the Westside, the apartment is obviously used by more than Tragedy Ann. It’s a junkie’s Mecca. Syringes and drug paraphernalia litter the apartment. Tragedy Ann makes her way across the room and slumps down against the wall, her head is dipped between her knees. La Fontaine crosses the room, dodging the syringes. He slumps down the wall next to Tragedy Ann.*** La Fontaine: Etan does not know. I haven’t told him…I think X-Rodd has noticed it too…but I don’t think he yet knows what it is that actually plagues you… ***Tragedy Ann raises her head and looks beside her…into La Fontaine’s icy-blue eyes.*** Tragedy Ann: Why haven’t you told them? ***La Fontaine smiles.*** La Fontaine: What reasons would I have to tell them? What would we accomplish by telling them? ***Tragedy Ann looks puzzled.*** Tragedy Ann: We? ***La Fontaine smiles and places his hands on his knees.*** La Fontaine: Yeah…we…I mean I know what you are going through, I’ve been there myself. I know how tempting it can be, how you can just slip away and leave your problems behind… ***Tragedy Ann lowers her head again.*** Tragedy Ann: It is not like that for me…I do not do because I want to experience that feeling…it…it hurts. Inside I hurt. The pain. The suffering in my head. My heart…my soul…it comes for me… ***La Fontaine is confused, he looks at Tragedy Ann who still has her head bowed.*** La Fontaine: what…what comes for you? ***Tragedy Ann raises her head revealing a disturbing grin, part happiness but mostly a smile, a face of pain.*** Tragedy Ann: The beast of course…the darkness…it consumes me…it eats at my soul! It fills me with darkness…with hate… ***La Fontaine puts an arm around her.*** La Fontaine: I know…you can get some really bad trips when you are on that shit…that is another reason to give it up… ***Tragedy Ann sighs.*** Tragedy Ann: Its so hard…the beast…it calls to me…the beast is sly…it tempts me…taunts me… ***La Fontaine breathes heavily.*** La Fontaine: It is a hard, rocky road! It took me ages to get clean and even then it was hard…you know that I fell off the wagon and got myself fired from the EWA? Yeah, I relapsed and I lost my job…and when I lost my job at the EWA I got depressed…so I started needing more heroin to feel happier. When I lost my job there was no money coming in…eventually I was doing so much that the money just dried up…I could not get back on a skateboard again because I could not skate because I was so messed up. I ended up on the streets…stealing from shops…stealing from friends…stealing from family. ***Tragedy Ann looks La Fontaine in the eyes.*** Tragedy Ann: Wha…what did you do…? ***La Fontaine holds her hand between the both of his.*** La Fontaine: One day I just got up, took a good look at myself and decided I could not go on the way I was living. I either had to sort myself out or I was gonna kill myself…I was on the road to self-destruction. That was the worst time of my life…I decided that it was too hard, I looked at myself and new that I had to make a choice…I made the wrong choice. I tried to hang myself but luckily God spared me…a passer by found me…my neck didn’t break so he found me choking, he helped me out and I moved in with him…he helped me through my darkest days and I got myself clean again…I have not fallen off the wagon again…I owe my life to that man…ever since myself and Johnny Doe have had an unbreakable bond…I do not consider Johnny a friend… ***La Fontaine breathes heavily.*** La Fontaine: …Johnny is more like a brother…come on… ***La Fontaine pushes himself up off of the floor. He grabs Tragedy Ann by the hand and hoists her up onto her feet. La Fontaine, still holding her hand, leads her out of the squat being careful to dodge the needles on the floor. They step out into the gloom of a less than nice day.*** La Fontaine: You hungry? ***Tragedy Ann takes a couple of minutes to process La Fontaine’s question.*** Tragedy Ann: Yeah… ***La Fontaine smiles.*** La Fontaine: Jack-In-The-Box? ***Tragedy Ann smiles for the first time today.*** Tragedy Ann: Yeah…I’d like that… ***La Fontaine smiles and leads Tragedy Ann down to the black Lexus that he parked at the bottom of the fire exit of the squat. He opens the car door for her and she gets in, he closes the door. He slides over the hood of the car and gets in the drivers side. La Fontaine turns the key in the ignition and the powerful engine roars into life. La Fontaine reverses the car out of the ally and onto the road. La Fontaine starts heading towards the Jack-In-The-Box in the Westend. He switches on the car’s CD player.*** Standing In Line To See The Show Tonight And There’s A Light On Heavy Glow By The Way I Tried To Say I’d Be There…Waiting For Dani The Girl Is Singing Songs To Me Beneath The Marquee…Overload ***La Fontaine smiles and taps on the steering wheel.*** La Fontaine: You know I really am looking forward to getting in the ring with J.J Dynasty…I think I am gonna enjoy kicking his ass! Dynasty got a beat down off of my man X-Rodd last week on the first ever Breakdown and now he has the displeasure of receiving another beat down on Breakdown by another member of the nXn! Y’all know that I’m gonna kick his ass…hell I do not even know if the little maggot has the guts to actually show up at the arena! Hah! He must know what is in front of him! But hey I am not worried about the little ass-hobbit©! However I face a man on Distortion who has indeed started off impressively…he is a potential threat. Although I am very confident that I will kick the absolute crap outta Kryptonite he has certainly got more skills the J.J Dynasty and should not be taken too lightly. I however am Joe La Fontaine! “The Bad Man”! Furiously Addictive Wrestling’s “Bad Man”! When I get in that ring against J.J Dynasty on Breakdown and Kryptonite on Distortion I am gonna do bad things. I am gonna go “Old School” on their asses…no in fact screw “Old School” I am gonna go “New School” on their asses! Joe La Fontaine is gonna carve them up and hand them their asses on a plate! On a plate! Served with steamed carrots, roast potatoes, roast parsnips and a good splattering of gravy! That is what’s gonna go down boys because I’m smarter than you! I’m faster than you! I’m sexier than you! I’m funnier than you! And I boys I am most definitely…COOLER THAN YOU! ***Tragedy Ann smiles a little.*** Tragedy Ann: Yeah you most definitely are! ***La Fontaine smiles as the car comes to a halt in the Jack-In-The-Box car park. La Fontaine looks Tragedy Ann dead in the eyes.*** La Fontaine: …So…uhh… ***La Fontaine leans over slowly. She does not move. His lips are almost against hers. He stops. He has visions of X-Rodd, his boy. He knows that X-Rodd wants Tragedy Ann. La Fontaine makes another choice right then. He pulls away sharply.*** La Fontaine: Sorry, I can’t do this. There is a better guy out there for you than a reformed junkie…a good guy that will never let his friends down…maybe I could take a few lessons from him…Uhh…I’ll…I’ll be back in 10 minutes…I got some shit I gotta do… ***Tragedy Ann does not say a word. La Fontaine exits the car quickly. He shuts the door. He walks away from the car, kicking a can as hard as he can as he walks away from the car. He does not know where he is going he just has to go somewhere where he can hide his shame. He walks until the car is out of sight but not out of mind. He makes his way over to a wall where he sits down.*** La Fontaine: …What the hell was that La Fontaine?! You almost just slapped one of your best friends in the face… ***La Fontaine sighs heavily.*** La Fontaine: Well there is some more screwed up crap in my life right there, hah, I almost punked out one of the only men I would trust with my life. I just hope X-Rodd does not find out about this…he’ll be fucking crushed. ***La Fontaine suddenly realises that he is talking to himself.*** La Fontaine: Hah. Dynasty, Kryptonite that there is just some more pent up aggression that I might decided to vent on you guys when we meet and Breakdown and then Distortion! I am gonna hit you so hard that you are gonna feel like the twin towers on September the 12th! I am gonna kick you guys from pillar to post, six ways from Sunday until the energy is drained out of me and the blood is drained out of you! And you better know that if for some unforeseen reason one or both of you ass-hobbits© do get the better of me, which you two could only achieve through cheating, the nXn will hunt you down and cull you as if you were one of those overpopulating Chinamen! “The Bad Man” will prevail because quite simple put I am better than you can ever hope, ever dream of being! ***La Fontaine gets up and starts back to the car. By the time he arrives at the car Tragedy Ann is just hanging up her cell phone. La Fontaine gets back in the car.*** La Fontaine: …So…err…what do you want…? ***Tragedy Ann sighs.*** Tragedy Ann: Can we go to the Jack-In-The-Box on the northside of town…the burgers here are too greasy. La Fontaine nods.*** La Fontaine: Sure. ***La Fontaine restarts the engine and off they head to the Jack-In-The-Box on the northside. The journey takes about fifteen minutes and throughout the fifteen minutes the two do not exchange a single word. After five minutes of the journey La Fontaine puts the CD back on as to try and take some of the tenseness away from the car. It works. Marginally. They arrive after what seems an eternity. La Fontaine gets out of the car and stoops to speak to Tragedy Ann who is still inside the sporty black Lexus.*** La Fontaine: So are you coming in…you don’t have to I can get takeaway… ***Tragedy Ann smiles.*** Tragedy Ann: I’ll come. ***Tragedy Ann exits the vehicle and the doors are closed. La Fontaine locks the car and they set off towards the restaurant.*** Tragedy Ann: I need a little fresh air, is it okay if I wait out here? ***Tragedy Ann asks as they get to the entrance.*** La Fontaine: Sure… ***La Fontaine walks into the restaurant and enters a queue while Tragedy Ann gets some fresh air. After six minutes and almost constant mumbling at the length of the queue La Fontaine gets to the front. He orders two burgers, fries and two chocolate shakes to go. Just as La Fontaine gets hands over the money and waits for his food he looks out to see Tragedy Ann talking to somebody in a black leather jacket. La Fontaine makes nothing of it. The waitress finally hands La Fontaine his food just in time to see Tragedy Ann getting in a car with the man and pulling away. It was her dealer. La Fontaine curses and makes his way back over to the Lexus alone. Just as he gets inside his cell phone rings.*** Voice: Hey man…you got time to come over I need to speak to you about something… ***La Fontaine smiles.*** La Fontaine: Yeah man I always got time for you…you are like my brother man…hey you hungry, I got Jack-In-The-Box…? ***The man laughs.*** Voice: Yeah…see ya in ten? ***La Fontaine smiles and starts up the car.*** La Fontaine: For you I will be there in five… …:::FADE TO BLACK:::…
