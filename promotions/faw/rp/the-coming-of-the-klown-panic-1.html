The Coming Of The Klown (Panic 1)

12.12.02
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The Coming Of The Klown (Panic 1) Furiously Addictive Wrestling Roleplay #97 Date: 12.12.02 Location: HaHaKlown's circus tent hehe You've been here before. As a kid, you remember coming here quite often. You would love riding all the rides, and eating all the various foods that were being sold...playing the multitude of games, never once winning...but everytime convinced that 'this' time you would. You remember the cotton candy, the turkey legs, the balloon darts...and the clowns. Oh god, how you remember the clowns. Never in your life have you been afraid of clowns, until that night. That paticular clown. The one with the white eyes. You had never seen such an evil being before. It was so...UNhappy...UNfriendly...so UNnatural. It had a midnight blue and cerulean face, with pure shades of blue in it's apparel. Come to think of it, you've never really liked blue as much as you did before that day. You remember how 'it' smiled at you, revealing unnatural teeth. Shards of broken glass, and nails are it's best description. 'It' then began to wave at you, and made it's way full force running towards you. You screamed. Your parents ensured you that 'it' was just a clown. Just a harmless silly clown. You remember how your parents couldn't be any more wrong. The clown was gaining on you and finally 'it' reached you and laughed an awful horrid laugh. A laugh you will never forget no matter how hard you try. hehe 'It' then extended it's white puffy gloved hand out to you for a handshake. Of course you refused, I mean, who the fuck wouldn't? Your parents scold you, ' Just give the nice clown a handshake, quit being a baby... '. So you did. You met his hand with yours and he then smiled...but his teeth weren't jagged looking any longer. In fact, they were perfectly straight and gleamingly white. Another laugh. hehe That was all you remember before it happened. The clown snapped your fathers neck and his lifeless corpse hit the ground like all the sudden his backbone was no more. Before you could react the clown has your mothers neck in both of his powerful hands squeezing with all his might. You watch, helplessly, as your mother turns as blue as the clown's face. Her eyes start to bulge. You're screaming inside. You're dying inside. But you were just a pathetic kid. How could you possibly help your parents now? Your father was dead, and your mother was just as good as. Alongside your daddy, your mommy falls as well. You remember the excrutiating fear you were experiencing. It seemed as if no one around this horrible scene took notice at all. Everyone just continued to have fun riding the rides, eating their cotton candy, begging parents for more and more tickets. NO ONE WAS FUCKING NOTICING AT ALL. DOES ANYONE IN THE GOD FORSAKEN CARNIVAL CARE? hehe You run with all your strength dodging the crowd from side to side in search of an authority type figure. A cop! You run up to the cop and gasp for breath. ' What is it, son? '. ' The clown...gasp...the clown...he...killed...my...par...' :::snap::: There goes the police officer, broken neck and all, flop on the ground in front of the evil clown you had just ran from. How did he get here? How was it physically fucking possible? hehe You wake up. Mom calls you down for breakfast. Jesus...it was all just a horrible dream. You get up and get dressed, skipping your daily shower...you just are too shaken to be alone right now, and head downstairs. Your mom is making your favorite, french toast with strawberries. You sit down and dive in as if your dream had suddenly evaporated from existense. 'Guess what honey? Do you know what today is?', your fork clanks on the plate when you drop it. ' That's right, son, carnival time! ' It should be ok though, right kid? hehe After all...you HAVE been here before... ::: the scene: The annual carnival of Amarillo, Texas 3:42 am. Underneath the crescent moon, the rides are being taken down and loaded onto their traveling trucks. Everything is basically loaded of in the process of being loaded, all except the infamous circus tent of HaHaKlown, a massive cerulean blue and white striped circus tent that displays a huge white ball at the peak of it's top. On the white ball it reads ' H H K ', as it always has. Voices can be heard in the tent as the scene follow close by...::: HaHaKlown: Ok ok...so you're from FAW...you've said that already...what in the fuck do you want is what I'm asking. Riot: I'm offering you a chance to star in the highest rated federation in all of Primetime Central, Furiously Addictive Wrestling federation. Of course, that is only if you think you have what it takes... ::: obviously enraged by this HaHaKlown throws his now-empty glass of what was Hennessy at the floor of where his visitor sits, and it breaks shattering shards of glass everywhere, and gets up and grabs Peter by the collar of his FAW shirt and picks him up into the air ::: HaHaKlown: Don't let me ever hear you say something like that again, am I understood? Because if you ever grow some balls again with me, and get brave...you're going to have to find out if YOU have what it takes to face me, kid. I'm HaHa the happy fucking Klown. I'm known as a fucking legend worldwide. You dig? Riot: Whoah Mr. Klown, I meant NO disrespect whatsoever. Honest. I'm just trying to mentally prepare you for the overwhelming competition that you will encounter at our federation. HaHaKlown: Competition? Kid, you tell you're friends at FAW that the Klown will be back. And tell them to have the 'competition' ready to fucking rumble. Riot: Wow! So you're in? You're going to join with us? HaHaKlown: What the fuck else would I have meant by that? Of course I'm in. I laugh at so called 'competition'. You know what my record is? Riot: Yeah...I think you're undefeated, right? With like over 50 wins...right? Some people even say that you aren't even real. Like you're some ghost story wrestlers tell their kids before going to sleep... HaHaKlown: Shit...might as well be. My only defeat in my entire career has been one that I didn't even show up for. I was disqualified. Isn't that some bullshit? I'm ready for new faces though, man. I can't wait to get back in the ring and impress the FAW audience with what us Texas boys do when provoked. Riot: Indeed. What is that you're drinking? HaHaKlown: Nothing but the best, you silly bitch. Hennessy. You want some? Riot: Sure...um...you have some Coke I can mix it with? HaHaKlown: ... Riot: Or straight. Whatever. HaHaKlown: Of course, straight, you ugly fuck. You don't drink Henny any other way...and if you do you're drinking it wrong... ::: pours two new glasses of the cognac, after finishing his fourth. HaHaKlown grabs a Black 'N Mild from the shirt pocket of his klown-suit and lights in on his first attempt. He inhales deeply and exhales almost immediately. He sets it aside as he lights up a overfilled marijuana blunt ::: HaHaKlown: You smoke, right? Riot: No. Cigarettes, nor weed. Sorry. HaHaKlown (while passing the blunt): Today you do. Go ahead. Puff away. Don't be a scary bitch. Riot (hesitant at first, puffs innocently): cough cough cough...Jesus! My god that is some potent stuff! HaHaKlown: No doubt. Nothing but the best. So tell me, Mr. Riot. How's the damn federation, anyway? Riot (still holding the blunt, puffs way more intensely now): Fuck. Um..Furiously Addictive Wrestling? Oh yeah...I'm supposed to ask you if you want in. HaHaKlown: hehe...you did you silly bitch and I had already accepted. I can't wait to get back into the ring, powerpuff. I've been cold for so fucking long. I've been getting my kicks out of killing kid's parents, fucking their moms, and stalking their dads...there's got to be more to life than that man. So..I decided to get my klowny ass back in the ring. You know? :::takes the blunt from Riot who is apparently overwhelmingly stoned::: I mean, I love being an evil klown, and terrorizing little kids and what not...but I need to kick some competitive ass again, you know?:::inhale::: I need to feel the rush of having my hand lifted high in the air after defeating some pathetic rookie to a bloody pulp. And to hear the crowd chant my name, H H K, H H K...geez-us, man. I miss that shit so mother fucking much. :::exhale::: You dig? Riot: Oh yeah...speaking of that...I was supposed to ask you if you'll join FAW, again. HaHaKlown (while inhaling): I mean, damnit man. Do you even know who I am? I was a fucking legend man. No one...I MEAN NO ONE could even try to attempt to try and attempt to beat the Klown. I was a fucking wrestling god man. :::exhale::: Riot: Wrestling? Oh! I know a great federation you could join! FURIOUSLY ADDICTIVE WRESTLING! HAhahahahaha HaHaKlown: And ain't a damn thing changed. I'm still on top of my game, hell. I've just been out of the ring for 2 months, and already I'm missing the adrenaline of the fight. That's it man :::drink, and inhale::: I'm getting back to wrestling right fucking now. Riot: Where? HaHaKlown: You are one silly fuck. You can't smoke with me anymore. ::: slap ::: HaHaKlown: Now get the fuck out...and HEY...you better take a taxi, you are in no condition to drive homeboy. hehe. Riot: Ouch. Ok. Oh....and Klown? HaHaKlown (while getting into his Suburban) : What? Riot: I just wanted to say...before I left... HaHaKlown (starting the ignition) : Yeah? Riot: It would be really cool if you could join Furiously Addic... ::: HaHaKlown speeds away in his 2002 midnight blue Suburban bumping ' The Greatest Show! ' by Insane Clown Posse in search of the Furiously Addictive Wrestling arena. Riot just falls where he stands and drools a bit :::
