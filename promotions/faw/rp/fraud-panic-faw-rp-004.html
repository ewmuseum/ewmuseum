Fraud [Panic - FAW RP #004] 'Legendary' Lee Smith Furiously Addictive Wrestling Roleplay #91 Date: December 6th 2002 Location: The Sunset Hotel, Miami, California “We are outside a hotel in Miami. The media have been called to the hotel at this time, 20:06 on 6th December 2002 as Lee Smith has released a statement. Earlier today Smith was alleged to have been in Malibu, filing a complaint against Xing for assault. We will hear what Smith has to say for himself in a few minutes” the presenter says, with the TV camera filming her. Suddenly the entrance to the hotel opens and out comes Legendary Lee Smith. He is wearing his usual fine attire. Smith stands on the podium and places the paper in front of him. “Ladies and gentleman. As you understand, earlier today Xing claimed that I was in fact in Malibu police station, in California. I would like to categorically deny those allegations and deny that I have made any complaints to the police service. I would also like to say that I have never been assaulted by Xing, or in fact any member of the public. My agent spoke to Malibu Police earlier today and faxed them a picture of myself. Witnesses who were in the station at the time were called and a parade was held. Of the 12 witnesses, every single one of them said that the man they had seen earlier that afternoon was not me. And so, with the evidence that to have been there I would have had to have travelled half way across the country in what…a few minutes - and that the witnesses say it wasn’t me….” Lee coughs, as the tension builds “I will be meeting my advisors tomorrow, and considering legal action against Xing. “Well there you have it, Smith denies all claims. He’s either telling the truth, or a very good liar” the presenter says as we turn back to Lee. “I would like to take this chance to remind Xing of the brutal beating he was at the end of on Sunday. I would also like to say that I am capable of FAR more deadly deeds than the one’s I performed on you on Sunday. And so, back to topic. The only guess I have at the moment is that Xing contacted a group of impersonators, and paid them with the little money he possesses to Impersonate myself and damage my award winning reputation.” Lee takes a deep breath as cameras flash everywhere. “Not only has Xing wound me up, he has also become an assailant to a crime, grievous bodily harm. When I get mad, I get even. And the only person I can get even with at the moment Is Renegade, who I face this Sunday. I’ve already told you the crime - but I’ll tell you once more. G…B..H…My rage shall be emptied from my lionheart. Good Night.” Lee finishes, and walks back into the hotel as journalists take pictures.
