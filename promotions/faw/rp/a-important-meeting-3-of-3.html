A Important Meeting (3 of 3) Cyrus Raynes Furiously Addictive Wrestling Roleplay #31 Date: 10/19/02 Location: Indianapolis Jail - Revenge is a funny business some times, but none the less a very dangerous one. You can never tell just how much is enough for a person who is seeking revenge for some wrong doing toward them, whether it be real or imagined. Anger, fire, and passion seem to be the only emotions that ever fuel revenge, as those who seek it rarely are capable of rational thoughts any longer. It is this lost sense of all reality that can make revenge that dangerous and even deadly game that all people seem to get the feeling for at one time of another. The difference is, whether or not you are able to cool yourself off enough to forget about the idea you form out of hatred and anger. Some people can...Some people can not. That is the decision that Cyrus Raynes in faced with now, whether to let his hatred and anger fuel him into doing something that he may one day regret, or to simply take some deep breathes...step back and revaluate his situation and what his mind set it. But anyone who knows Cyrus personally, professionally, or in passing knows that rational thoughts in times of fury and anger are not one of Raynes strong points. Un-fortunately for Raynes, and anyone who he views as standing in his way, this combination of hatred, loss, and emotionless void inside of him can and will lead to some serious problems. Especially problems for those that he views as in his way...Are you going to be one of those people? And if so do you have any idea what is going to happen to you if you happen to be one of those people? NO you don’t know....Mainly because.... Even Cyrus Raynes doesn’t know. - [The out shot opens to Raynes sitting behind the wheel of his 2003 Ford Expedition, with cold, dark blue, hatred filled eyes burning right on the old style gray brick building sitting in front of him. He sits there with his hands tightly wrapped around the wheel of the car desperately fighting a losing battle with in himself. The battle to some how find that one shred of human caring that once lived in side of him. That one shred that Reanna had always been able to bring out in him, and had always been able to keep on his mind. That single, tiny, shred that died a couple of days ago...Died with Reanna on that hospital bed in that huge bright hospital room. Now all that dwells deep inside of him are all the dark and sinister characteristics that he has only mildly displayed to this point. Will they now rear there truly dark side?] {Raynes}: ‘The time has come for you to pay for what you and the world has done. I am going to make you all suffer what you did to my Reanna!’ [With that Raynes gets out of his vehicle and stands next to his car. He looks around at the nearly empty parking lot of the police station. As he stands there he begins to get that usual look of fire in his eyes as he begins to climb the steps up to the front entrance. He walks along not noticing anyone or anything that is going on around him as he walks up the concrete steps and through the double oak doors to the front entry way of one of the many Indianapolis Police Stations. He takes a couple of looks around and then walks up to the main desk to talk to the lady officer sitting behind the desk.] {Officer}: ‘How can I help you today sir?’ {Raynes}: ‘Yes you sure can miss. You see I am here to see a dear friend of mine, who happened to be involved in a terrible accident a few nights ago. He was drunk from what I understand and hit some innocent young woman’s car and killed her. I just wanted to make sure he was ok.’ {Officer}: ‘Oh you mean Teddy Thomas. Yeah he is ok, but understand ably shook up over the events. Guess he knows now why you shouldn’t ever drink then drive. A shame really, I heard the DA is going for the max on the poor guy, even though he has something on his mind that he will have to live with for the rest of his life.’ [Raynes almost turns red with anger over her feeling sorry for the man who murdered his wife, but quickly calms himself down and smile sweetly at her.] {Raynes}: ‘Yes I am sure he has more than enough torment coming to him in his life than having to spend life behind those prison bars. May I go and see him miss?’ {Officer}: ‘Sure why not. He hasn’t had much company the last few days and he could use a good friend to cheer him up a bit. I will get Officer Pankerton to take you down to lock and let you two talk. You have a nice day sir.’ {Raynes}: ‘Thanks so much miss. You have no idea how important this is to me. You are a great humanitarian.’ [With that Raynes follows the big black officer named Pankerton down the hall way to the right toward the down stairs lock up area. The two walk in silence past some cells that house your every day run of the mill offenders. Down all the way at the end of the hall is the cell where Teddy Thomas lays on his bed looking up at the ceiling. He has been in this hole for a few days and has seen no one other than his lawyer. Pankerton walks over to the door and un-locks it as Raynes steps inside the door. Teddy sits up on his elbows and just stares at Raynes, a man he has never seen before in his life. But he is none the less glad to see some one other than a lawyer so he smiles at him to show off a good front.] {Pankerton}: ‘Well I will leave you two gentlemen alone to talk. Cyrus you have about one hour and then you will have to go.’ {Raynes}: ‘Thanks my good sir. I will be out of here before that with out any assistance what so ever.’ [Pankerton walks off back down the hall and Raynes turns back to Teddy. Teddy just shakes his head and looks right at Raynes.] {Teddy}: ‘Alright, I have no idea who you are, but I am just glad to see some one other than a lawyer so I went along with it. May I ask you why you are here?’ {Raynes}: ‘Well we have a mutual friend and they told me you had been in some trouble and were worried about you. So I thought I would come down and talk to you myself and put their mind at ease. How are you doing Teddy?’ {Teddy}: ‘Ok I guess all things considered. I haven’t able to sleep well since this all happened. Then yesterday my attorney told me the poor lady died. I have been so tore up by this, how could I have been so stupid!’ {Raynes}: ‘Now, now Teddy. You shouldn’t beat yourself up over this whole ordeal. After all you are just human and you made a mistake. Grant it that it was a horrible one and you cost some woman here life and some husband his wife, but you are human.’ {Teddy (Fear in his eyes)}: ‘See was married? I hadn’t been told that! Oh man, I bet her husband can’t wait to see me spend life behind bars. I am so screwed!’ {Raynes (Smirk on his face)}: ‘Actually I heard that he is taking it kind of hard but never said anything about seeing you spend life in prison. You don’t think he would want to hurt you do you Teddy?’ {Teddy}: ‘I hope not, but could you blame him really? I mean my stupidity took his wife’s life. I wouldn’t blame him at all if he wanted to kill me.’ {Raynes}: ‘I am glad you feel that way Teddy, shows you have some human emotion in you. But I can assure you that her husband in no way wants to see you dead.’ {Teddy (Gives Raynes a confused look)}: ‘I...hope not. Hey by the way who did you say you were again?’ [Raynes looks down at the floor for a moment. When he looks back up his face is blank and the only emotion in him is that familiar fire deep in his gaze. He looks right into Teddy’s face and then smiles slightly in a very sadistic way.] {Raynes}: ‘Cyrus Raynes...That woman’s husband!’ [Shock and fear cover Teddy’s face as it drains of all its color. Just as he is about to scream Raynes gets up and grabs him, keeping him quiet. With his other hand he reaches back and is about to do something to Teddy when the scene fades out to black. There is only one thing heard after that.} ‘I am so glad that you felt that way Teddy...so glad!
