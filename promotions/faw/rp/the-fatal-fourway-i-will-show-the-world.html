The Fatal FourWay I Will Show The World

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The Fatal FourWay I Will Show The World Big Baby Furiously Addictive Wrestling Roleplay #253 Date: 1/31/03 Location: FAW HOUSE SHOW (The scene opens at the usuall lame FAW House Show.The crowd...quit and bored...In the back Big Baby Jesus Mike E.Clark the newest and bightest star in the FAW is about to cut a promo on his Fatal Four Way Match Sunday Night.This match pits Big Baby against Colby Newsom,Spider and Gabriel Hunter.AS we come back from commercial Big Baby heads to the ring.) (The arena goes black and 'It Rains Diamonds' by I.C.P.rocks the P.A.system in the arena.The lights slowly come back on and smoke fills the ramp to the ring....The crowd stands in anticapation.The smoke clears and there in all his glory isBIG BABY JESUS MIKE E.CLARKClark slowly makes his way to the ring and for the first time in his gloryous career is met with cheers.Clark looks to the crowd and flips them all off.For he doesnt perform for them..He performs for himself.He scales the ropes and the begins to boo and throw trash at him.He looks around and smiles.He jumps down off the top rope and demands the mic.With mic in hand is looks to the crowd and begins to speak. BIG BABY JESUS: So Sunday is almost here...Almost time for Big Baby Jesus to make his debut.And i see right off the bat the FAW is trying to put it to me.A Fatal Way My First Match!!! That is crazy....and against three guys i know nothing about....For all i know they could all be buddies and i am going to get screwed..This is not my kind of match but screw it...I guess that is what cards were drawn for me...So Be It!!! (Clark looks around once again and the he pulls a piece of paper out of his pocket.He onfolds the piece of paper to read just exactly who it is he is wrestling in this ridiculous Fatal Four Way on Sunday.) BIG BABY JESUS: Let's see....first on the list we have this lame excuse of a guy called Spider...Good imagination...Spider you come sunday Mike Clark will make it a point to show the world that you like your name will be climbing anywhere you can to get away from the beating that i will lay on you....And then there is Colby Cheese...oops i mean Newsome....Boy let me tell you....where i come from we dont cut two or three promos in a row to help us win....It is what we do in this ring....Not howmany times we can bore the crowd.....I may hate everyone in this arena but they didnt come here to see you guys keep running your mouths to get over.....In my opinion the only guy in this match i have a bit ....no no no...on ounce of respect for...But if it promos that win you a match around here then i don't have a chance in hell because i was just givin my contract today and everyone seems tohave a jump start on me...But i will tell you this If i would have been around all week before this big match i would have a chance....But you see win lose or draw i will be back and no one will be safe from me....But come Sunday i will give the three of you the fight of your lives and you will all have respect for my talent....Thats it for me...Now hit my music. ('It Rains Diamonds' By ICP rocks the deafenss the crowd and Big Baby Jesus leaves the ring. *Fade To Black*
