Spawn — FAW Roleplay #34

captured 8 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
SpAwN--The Hell Master \~~~~~~~(opening scene)The scene opens up to Spawn's locker room backstage at a FAW house show. His bag sits by the wall on the far side. He had put a picture of Bret Hart on the wall to give him some inspiration. It shows Bret Hart holding up the WWF Heavyweight title in the air. He has sweat dripping down his face and looks like he has been through a war. He is smiling and looks out at the crowd. Spawn is still waiting for the same. Spawn: AAAAAAAAAAAHHHHHHHHHHH!!!!! Spawn charges through the door. He actually breaks a hinge on it. He also smashed the door against the wall making a giant dent in it. Spawn: I FUCKING HATE THIS SHIT!! GOD DAMN THAT MOTHER FUCKING GREAT!!!! HE THINKS HE CAN DEFEAT ME TO GET THE FUCKING WORLD TITLE BY BEING A CHEATER!!! HE THINKS THAT I WON'T FIND HIM AND BEAT THE SHIT OUT OF HIM!!! HE'S GOING TO DIE!!!!! Spawn punches the wall and puts his fist right through the plaster. He picks up a chair and throws it over to the other side smashing a mirror. Spawn: GREAT 1, JUST WALKS INTO MY MATCH. HE HAD NO BUSINESS BEING IN THAT MATCH GOD DAMMIT!!!! HE'S NEXT ON THE GOD DAMN LIST!!! HELL, ALL OF FAW IS ON MY FUCKING LIST!!! ALL YOU MOTHER FUCKERS ARE GOING TO DIE BY MY HANDS!!! THE HELL MASTER MOTHER FUCKING SPAWN!!! Spawn starts to punch the wall again and leaves a few more holes. He then sits down on the bench and sighs. The anger still runs across his face but for the moment he seems to be under control. He looks around at the damage and sighs again. Spawn: Well Great 1 I guess your real fucking proud of yourself right now. Because of you I have lost my world title, the staff here in FAW has made sure of that, because of you, you fucking fool. You know I won that match fair and square…yet you went on to cheat, and pay the ref, And you humiliated me in front of millions by having the Staff making share my world title with a bitch like you. But there is one thing that you cannot say yet my friend. You have never beat me inside a wrestling ring, and you have never kicked my ass. You have back stabbed me twice. But you have never beat me. I bet your smiling right now as you watch me suffer, but soon the favor will be returned. A few shots to the head never phased me before Great 1, you should know. Remember that bat you took to my head. Remember when the blood started gushing out. That is what has happened again. You have started the bleeding. But remember what happened after that Great 1, remember when I kicked out and proceeded to show everyone in the world who the real True Ruler of FAW is. Remember when I stood in front of your collapsed body and held my hands up in victory. Oh that's right you couldn't have remembered that. You were to unconscious to see anything. You woke up later to find out that your enemy had won the war. I know that match is in the back of your mind Great 1. I know that you still feel jealous that I beat you man to man, and all you can do is attack me from the back. I must say Great 1, that little sneak attack was clever, but don't get too cocky. It was a cowardly sneak attack. An attack that any moron could have done. But Great 1 what will happen when I turn around? What will happen when you don't have a chair in your hand waiting to hit the back of me? What will happen when you are looking into my eyes instead of the back of my head? What will happen will be a repeat of the past. I will show the world how much of a worthless faggot you are. So keep your head titled up Great 1. Look up at the sky. Look at the clouds. Keep looking at the blue and white, because while your head is titled towards the atmosphere my fist will be driving itself into your throat. I am going to end your little fairy tail Kid. I am going to crush the spirit inside of you. And I am going to turn those colors of blue and white you've been looking at into red. A knock is heard at the half attached door. A stage hand walks into the room with a paper. This room looks like crap. Spawn: Umm...yeah your right. Why the fuck does a superstar wrestler like me get such a shitty locker room? This room has fucking holes in the wall and the mirror is smashed. This is a fucking outrage. uh...uh yeah your right Mr Spawn I'll get right on that. Here's a press release that was just given out by Neil. The kid runs out in a hurry and Spawn breathes a sigh of relief. He didn't want to use the money he made for his matches for fixing a locker room. He sits down and reads the paper. Spawn: A rumble world title match for the Evil Intentions PPV. Eight participants for this event. Well, well, look whose the first on the god damn list. Great 1. Wow buddy I thought I at least had to wait a few weeks before I get my revenge, but the FAW gods have shined on me. I think we just kicked Tony V out of his locker room, so that should be good enough for you. Spawn: Kid, get my stuff ready for me to go, I have to begin my training for this match. I don't think I have time t.... Spawn: NOW!! Yes sir. Spawn stands up and looks at the picture of Bret Hart. Spawn stares at it intently, looking at every drop of sweat on his face and every wrinkle in his skin. He stares at his eyes. They look tired and worn, but they also look happy. He looks at Hart's hands holding the championship gold in the arena air. Then he thinks of how he was screwed by Vince McMahon on that horrible Survivor Series night. He remembers Hart's face after that match. A face of disgust and disbelief. Bret never got a chance to regain what was his. He never fought Shawn Micheals for what he worked so hard for. Spawn: That's not happening to me. I will regain my honor from the clutches of that prick Great 1. He will see that back stabbing and sneak attacks have their price. A price that isn't even worth the FAW World Title. He will realize how dear he will pay when he looks into my eyes. He will see the fire that burns, and he will feel the fear gather inside. Great 1, I know this will happen because it has happened before. You looked right into these eyes when we faced each other. You saw what happens when I am provoked, and you felt it as well. Don't think the outcome will differ when you face me again. Go ahead and deny it, but soon I will get my revenge. Okay Mr. Spawn, I packed you up. But you know what Spawn is just not only the Great 1 in this match, 9 Wonder of Foam, Bomber, Lee Smith, Rzarector, Renegade, and Costella are also all in this thing. Spawn: Just as long as they don't get between me and Great 1, I don't care what the fuck they do. Now leave. You mean to tell me, you are not even worry about the other 6 in this match? Spawn: Man I tell you kid FAW is really starting to bug the fuck out of me. How come? What are they doing wrong? Spawn: Ok let's go down the list of these other six bums, and I will tell you what I think of these six fools. Alright, tell me what you think of the 9 Wonder of Foam? Spawn: That prick is a fucking no good comedian, he should not be going for the world title, I have kicked that bitch ass so many times, that it is getting tiresome just watching that bitch cry everytime I beat his ass. Foamy is not a true wrestler, he does not know the game. This game to him is a joke, his fucking name is a joke. There is nothing better in life that I rather do than to show the whole world what a true clown he really is. So even though I am tired of fighting him, I am kind of glad he singed up for this match because now I can show the world what a fucking clown he really is. Ok….what about the kid Renegade what you have to say about him? Spawn: Man I tell ya, what is up with FAW, these days they just allow all kinds of nobodies to go for the most prestige title in the world of entertainment. Doesn’t anybody do their homework, how the fuck can they make a mistake as allowing Renegade to fight for the world title, Man FAW is on a low and is sinking fast. I can't read the future so i don't KNOW were FAW will head to for allowing this class act punk in this match, what I do know Renegade is that I can beat you, but then again regardless of what you say you don't KNOW you can beat me.....I'm not a puppet to be played with......I may be playful and as you say act like a jerk but when I'm in the ring then I can be a force you don't want to play with......may your tears bring me pleasure and my pain do the same.......win or lose we are all in the freak show.....the only question is whether we make it to the big top......I'm on that path.........I won't let anyone stop me.....delays will be had but a little pain never hurt anyone.......get your head out the toilet and be at your best on Sunday......I don't want you puking on me or in the ring cause that could be nasty rookie punk....... What about Legendary Lee Smith? Spawn: What about that chump Lee Smith, is he on crutches, how old is that fool, "Lee, there isn’t a person in the entire FAW who could put an end to my tyranny and you are the last person anyone would expect to even beat the towel boy in the locker room. Speaking of which, the New Breed is looking for a new spit bucket and your head looks like it would make a wonderful target to spit at, I mean hey it couldn’t hurt your ugly mug that much. So Lee, what is it going to be? Are you actually going to show up to the match and get beaten silly or are you just going to calmly surrender before you get hurt REAL bad. Its your pick, but should you show up I won’t be held liable for any of your medical bills. So Lee, I’ll pray for your well being and try to make the match as short for you as I can, oh and I’ll try not to break too many of your scrawny little bones." Hey you know Rzarector said that he has beaten you before, and he knows he can do it again? Spawn: I think Rzarector has been on drugs for too long, the damn rookie has yet to beat me, and as I recall he was one of those rookies that FAW wanted to sacrifice so they gave him to me, because FAW knows I do not have pity for fools, Rzarector dream all you want it will not help you any come Sunday. Costella might have a chance though? Spawn: A chance on a date with you perhaps…."Now if I am not mistaken, Costella, you asked if you had a chance to beat me? And you said that you THINK you do? What kind of an attitude is that to have? You have to go into battle KNOWING that you CAN win there isn’t anytime for hesitation and that is why I will continue to look at you as a rookie. You’re damn right I underestimate you because you haven’t give me any reason at all to fear you, I have no reason at all to be afraid of you! The biggest gun you pulled out to scare me was that you beat Fro…wow, that makes me so afraid of what might happen to me oh how will I ever get to sleep tonight? Come on! Look here, I a former 3 time world Champion and I worked my ass off to beat everyone they put in front of me here in FAW until I got the title. See there is another big difference between the two of us, I work hard and you…well you make yourself look like an idiot in front of the world. Hell, it really wouldn’t surprise me if you were even an embarrassment to this kingdom of yours. Because even there you realize that your dreams will never come true because your worst nightmare is looking you right in the eyes...and that would be me. But I' will grant you that you have made it to the dreamland, I mean you think you have a chance against me so you must be in some dreamland where nothing is real! You think I am the barbarian attacking your little glass fort? Hah, a barbarian has nothing on me and you would be damn lucky if I were just some barbarian. But you know what? In your little dreamland I must look like an army of marauders thirsty for blood and ready to crack your little dream kingdom. But you know what, it all works out because I make it my business to shatter people’s dreams and guess what…your pathetic dream kingdom is next in line." Last but not least the British Bomber an all time hall of famer? Spawn: Dammit Bomber when are you really going to earned a title shot, you old fart x mas always comes early for you, I earned my title; I didn’t have to use deception to get what I had been dreaming about since I first came to the FAW I earned it with blood, sweat, pain and anger. Not because I knew I was connected to the staff, but because I was determined to get a title and I did. I do think I am a badass, I am so glad you put that much together Bomber but you know why I think I am a badass? Because of the gold belt I had 3 time around my waist, sure it may be cutting off circulation to my head and making me blind but I don’t care I feel like I am on top of the world and no one can stop me! I understand that fact that underdogs do sometimes win, infact I was one of the reasons they made that statement because once I was the underdog sitting in your shoes except I knew I could win and I had the ability, which is something you lack." Bomber you are going down as always. You know all this talking has taken me away from doing what I said I was going to do, and that is your fault kid so dammit were are you. The kid runs away and Spawn stands up. Spawn: This is my time again, by the time the night ends and once I'm done exacting my revenge I will become four time FAW world champion. The scene fades to black.
