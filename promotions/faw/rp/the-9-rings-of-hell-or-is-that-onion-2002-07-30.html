The 9 rings of hell or is that Onion?

30 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
9th Wonder Of Foam Entitled: The 9 rings of hell or is that Onion? Written: 30th July 2002 Location: …. The Atlantic Ocean – Tuesday …. ( A huge White boat cruises across the ocean it is on a cruise there are many passengers on board, and in room 101 there is one particular passenger of note. A gun is placed onto the dresser, on the bed is a tuxedo, it’s owner in the shower. Strewn across the floor is a gold gun, the keys to an Aston Martin, an empty martini glass and a wallet that contains a video rental card and a license to kill) (The door opens, it is a maid, and she looks foreign, sexy and most certainly deadly) Maid: Hmm he must be in the shower. (She grabs his wallet and looks at his license to kill, she photographs it, she then opens the door to the shower) Maid: Oh my god!!! Oh the humanity! (A little vomit comes to the back of her throat, she then runs out of the room is disgust) (The shower stops, the international man of mystery steps out of the shower, he walks into the bedroom. The secret agent is Danish, smelly and holding a small tub of Mexican prawns) Lars: What was her problem? (Lars packs up his Secret Agent Party kit from the night before and begins to get ready to leave The Love Boat. His 24-hour stay has done him no end of good, with Miss Grieves still missing Foamy is desperately worried, and Lars himself is still struggling to come to terms with his love for her. He must no fly to Panic to support Foamy in his cage match against Apocalypse) …. Foamy Hills Mansion …. (The 9th Wonder Of Foam is with James Harris, his mobile rings incessantly) James: 9th Wonder Of Foam, your childhood sweetheart appears to be missing, you seem to be off track in regards to your wrestling career and you have to go one on one with Apocalypse who seems to be on fire right now. (The Foamy Ones takes a bucket of water and pours it over James Harris) Foamy: That is what I do when people are on fire, and that’s exactly what I’m going to do to Apocalypse this Wednesday night. And as for Miss Grieves that is none of your business and is not of concern when I step into the ring on Panic. James: What about Apocalypse’s prediction of the end of the FWO? Foamy: Well in case he hadn’t been watching lately the FWO is as good as dead. The fans no longer chant for the collective they chant for the unique one, the Foamy One. I did wonder how long it would take for Apocalypse to bring out his end of the line, end of his opponent thing. There won’t be anything left of me? Are you going to incinerate me? Are you going to get out your ACME ray gun and laser me into nothing? Are you going to eat me? I think not Apocalypse. James: Have you been in many cage matches before? Foamy: Not really my friend. Apocalypse of course is a master of the hardcore match. But I am not so used to being extreme, but I have ten years of aggression built up, I haven’t had the outlet such as the hardcore division to truly let off steam, and yoga don’t work either. To Apocalypse it is nothing new, he is used to it and this is where he shall falter. He makes too many promises that he cannot fulfil. His very name and personality brings to mind things on a biblical scale, sadly it is more on a par with Teletubby sized destruction. It says In the bible that once the Apocalypse has come and EVIL! has been defeated that we shall all live in a world free from oppression and ruled by Foam. James: Are you sure? Foamy: Quite probably. James: Now onto Xander, he puts his title on the line tomorrow night in a triple threat match against Syd & EDH he could potentially lose his title. The question is will you refocus your attention to Xander or the new IN champion? Foamy: Well normally I’d say that nothing can stand in the way of me becoming the biggest thing in FAW, including my crotch but sometimes you just have to get out of the car and kick ten shades of sh*t into the idiot driving in front of you. And as for Apocalypse Neo may have stepped into the ring against your kind of greatness but have you stepped into the ring with The 9th Kind of Foamy Greatness? I think not my foe. I will climb from the fiery pits of hell and over the top into the spotlight of the adoring fans and into victory, consigning the very notion of an Apocalypse to the thing of myth and folklore. Have a Foam Filled Day!
