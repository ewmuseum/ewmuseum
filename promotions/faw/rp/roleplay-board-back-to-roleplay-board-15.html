Furiously Addictive Wrestling Roleplay Board Roleplay Board | Back to Roleplay Board Neo Entitled: destiny Written: Location: desolation The scene opens on a grit path the moon is full in the sky; the faint blue light illuminates the surroundings the camera man walks the path panning to his left revealing the elevation he currently stands at over the side of the path lies a canyon rugged and bare the jagged rocks jutting skywards. as the camera is panned and the camera man walks he kicks a small stone it can be heard spinning across the lose gravel and falling cracking against the canyon walls the echo filling the night the cameraman continues his accent to the summit of the mountain he walks upon. He continues without incident t o the peak upon his accent to the peak a huge silhouette is seen against the light of the moon the camera moves to the where the person stand moving round him until the figure is revealed to be neo in his usual attire, wraped in his arms is Rayne who tilts her head at the camera studying the face of the man who points it at her Rayne starts to speak slowly “ again the flash of gold is put into the face of the somnambulist, the tear of blood “ Neo raises his eyes to the camera lens “all men must stand upon their peak of destiny, the peak where their paths are mapped out all are given two roads to tread, the road of destiny and the road of fate. Many see these as the same but they are entirely different there are no similarities the path of fate is the cowards’ way out not facing the adversity to become what you are intended to be. Where as the path of destiny your demons are released to exact their revenge on your flesh you psyche is scared and if you reach the end of the path your weary broken flesh shall rest and your rewards heaped upon your crown, the blood you have spilt is wiped clean. The epiphany of your destiny is like being enveloped in rapturous love. Keep where no pain shall halt you, the slings and arrows shall bounce from you presence. As I set foot upon my path, I wonder whether I shall know the rapture, or shall I fall in my striving. Now my path leads me in the sadistic forum of the hardcore underbelly of this federation where me step into but don’t return as the went in. the hardcore title encrusted with the vile screams of broken men, enshrouded with the stale blood of those who strive to get it in their grasp, Into it murky waters I wade to face the man atop the river of blood the hell master spawn, the now carnie kid. Spawn your endless diatribes mealy showed your vulnerability, your uncertainty of your own identity, you attempt to jest like my brethren of the fWo but simply you come of as cheesy, the once leader has become the follower has the dark carnival robed you of your identity has the hell master become a side show for jlc and the ha ha klown, two men grasping to former glory trying to climb the peak they have fallen from without grace those in their twilight years……………. former legends Spawn attempts to lecture to me as if he was a priest, and as if I cared about his opinion. Our society hangs from the neck from the noose of vanity and ignorance. Both of which spawn has displayed on his attempts to provoke me, try to extract some feeling from me. Your vanity is you try to profile me tell me what I should do ? not while the hatred burns in my eyes. You spout forth as if I share you weakness . Your ignorance is obvious as you seem to think I would shy away from your challenge as if you were a titan beating on the very gates of mount Olympus. A short lesson for those heathens who watch this broadcast from their trailer park homes, those who’s family trees require two way arrows. The titans were defeated by the gods of Olympus . let spawns pondering on whet I shall show my face comfort him let his anticipation burn within him, let all his delusions enwrap him in a great vision of his valour, his ensured victory. Let him forget that it took two men to take the title he wairs so proudly away from me I had apocalypse beaten until the fresh Etan cross placed his influence placing apoc atop me. But that is the past and this reminiscence into my past glories bores me so many in our business seem to live perpetually in the past. I say let them enjoy there past as the future passes them by. And how can I forget you pass a foolhardy judgment on my apparent cheating. I seem to me spawn you know not who I am you seem to have me confuse with another does the grease pain of the clown cause you to hallucinate. You seem to thing I bring people with me to the ring, do you think I would soil the gladiatorial premise of you business I a man steep in the beliefs of the ancients. You will know all your mistakes when you stand across from me and you world is reflected to you in the ice eyes of mine you delusions will crumple like Babylon. The deluge of revelation will sweep you mind clear from its anchors For like Sodom and Gomorrah your mind shall be left in naught but ashes”. Neo removes his arms from around Rayne’s waist walks to the edge of the peak. “ to be away from the festering the our sprawling urban landscape present us with to be in utter solitude away from the moronic masses, the waking mindless. Am I the only one who regards these fool with shear contempt am I alone in this. But I grow tired of you presence upon this peak of solitude Now you leave Neo takes his gaze from the desolation below the peak and jars his neck round to face the camera The scene fades to Neo’s piercing gase
