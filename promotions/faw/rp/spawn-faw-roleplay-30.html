Spawn — FAW Roleplay #30

captured 31 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
SpAwN--The Hell Master \~~~~~~~(opening scene)The scene FAW house show, it is a very special night for tonight the fans eagerly await the Devil's pit a show that has skyrocket to fame in FAW. The place is full with no seats available. The scene then show the arena going into darkness, once that happens the fans start to scream the name of Spawn, The FAW Tron lights up, and now we see the mist covering the screen, the cave is being shown and the dogs of hell stand on watch duty as the camera goes through its motion, then it stops and Spawn walks up to the camera, and begins to speak to the fans from FAW tron. Spawn: Welcome to another edition of the Devil's pit. Today we have a hot story for you all, but before we begin there are a few things that I must make clear, and is about you Crimson Lord the time is getting near, and your clock is ticking faster… and what little time you have left I want you to take and enjoy it because it will be the last minutes of your life, so pay attention to this story and study it with your dictionary, find the meaning of it and be prepare for the last days of your life. Now is everyone here ready for a shock (fans scream yeah) if you are then let us begin this little story. The FAW tron begins to show the story The scene takes place in Boston, in the town call Dorchester and in this town we go to a gym and in this we see two boys in their teens having an amateur wrestling match, as their coach and the rest of their teammates watch, everybody is eager to see who is the victor of these two, and who are these two teens… there names are Kenny and Ryan and they are the best of friends they do everything together unseparable no one has ever come between them not even their girlfriends, some have tried but they have all failed. But the end is coming and we shall soon find out. The match ends and the coach calls it a draw same result as always, then everyone goes to the locker and Kenny and Ryan stay behind to speak to one another. Ryan: Great match Kenny? Let me ask you something? Kenny: Thanks man, go ahead and ask. Ryan: Will you ever want to be a professional wrestler? You know make it big maybe even fight against the likes of Pyro or Spawn how do you feel about that? Kenny: Yeah I thought about it and I won't mind. But you know what would be real nice if me and you become professional wrestlers as a tag team. Imagine that we can terrorize the tag team scene and destroy anyone that gets in our way. Ryan: Yeah just imagine we could be like Spawn and Crimson the omens of death and terrorize the wrestling scene we would be unstoppable. Kenny: Of all the team to mention it just had to be them two. Ryan: What do you mean they are the best in FAW right now. Kenny: Yeah they probably are but they aren’t trusting each other right now, and we do… that is the big difference, and I do not want to be associated with that type of friendship. Ryan: Ok man that sounds cool to me but I still like them. Kenny: Yeah, yeah let’s go to the lockers change and head on home it’s getting late? The scene changes to six years later and we see Ryan and Kenny now wrestling as professional wrestlers, they have become one of the hottest new tag team in the scene, but they are new to the game and are still considered rookies. The scene shows them in the president of the company office doing business. president: You two boys have been with this company for three years now as tag team, and I feel it is time for a change . Therefore it is my decision to break your team up. Ryan: What! You can’t do this we are getting better every time give us a little more time and you will see what we can do, tell him Kenny? As Ryan looks at Kenny waiting for him to speak Kenny just stare back at him but do not say a word, and Ryan start to speak again. Ryan: Kenny speak to me please do not tell me you knew about all of this. This is all bullshit tell him why won’t you say anything? president: There is nothing to say the decision has been made it is time to move on. Ryan: Let me ask you since all of a sudden Kenny lost the will to speak what are the angles for our single career? president: Ok it’s like this Kenny will be brought slowly to mid card status, and you will get a new tag team partner. Ryan: What! What the fuck, are you out of your mind! I am just as good as he how come I do not get a big push also? president: Because we have decided to keep you as a tag team technician you are real good at it but your partner here has potential to become world champion material and we are going to give him that chance. Ryan: That’s bullshit man you are breaking up a good team, the fans love us how are you going to explain that to them? president: Simple an angle will be worked were Kenny will betray you and put you for some time. Think of it as a vacation with more money and when you come back you will be brought back with a new partner. This angle is suppose to start next week. Ryan: And If I refuse to go with the plan then what? president: Your career here is good but if I have to let you go so be it those are your choices take it or leave it. Ryan: Not much of a choice is it. I am going to go now later. Ryan and Kenny meet out side of the office and begin to speak with each other. Ryan: What is wrong with you man? Why didn’t you back me up in there care to explain to me what is up? Kenny: It is time for a change our time is over and we need to with it. Ryan: How long have you been feeling this way? Kenny: For a long time I just didn’t know how to tell you. Ryan: I see yet it is alright for you to let me find out this way , how can you do this? I thought we were a team for ever, buddies to the end remember? Kenny: When are you going to get it man that nothing is forever, nothing stays the same. Just go with the flow and be happy you are getting more money. Ryan: So that’s it you are set in betraying me, that’s funny I remember six years ago when I made a remark about two wrestler just like us good friends and how they didn’t trust each other but I still wanted to be like them because they were the best and you told me do not compare us to them they do not know what friendship is. Well look at you who is selling out now, my best friend please I might as well start calling you my worst enemy. See you around I will be waiting for your betrayal. the next few days things get bad and then worse for Ryan and Kenny they stop talking to each other and they don’t even hang out. Then the day of the event comes and the match gets on its way it is a tag team match and Ryan and Kenny are still a team but toward the end of the match Kenny betrays Ryan in bad way sending him to the hospital. As Ryan begins his time off Kenny’s career takes off he wins all kinds of gold and eventually the world title. Then Ryan heals and comes back he get a new partner and does real well to good in fact and that pisses of Kenny. Kenny calls him on the phone and they finally meet and begin to talk to each other. Kenny: Why did you come back? You should have stayed gone? Ryan: That was your plan, all of this right from the start, to get me out of here what type of human being are you. A sorry one that’s for sure, we could have been big you and I and you screwed me for the fame. You should have listened to your heart and not your head you are an idiot you are a man with no soul and a man with no friends. Kenny: At least I am the world champion. What about you what do you have nothing a second rate tag teamer ha what a joke. Ryan: No you are the joke you see I still have pride and respect the fans still love and adore me, but they hate you and they can’t stand the site of you. Kenny: So you say but who is making more money me or you answer that? Ryan: No question you are, but money isn’t everything friendship is and you don’t have it at this time. As far as I am concerned it will never be the same you broke my heart and once I win the tag belt I will call it quits and don’t I will never go for revenge I am to much of a man for that see you around chump. Ryan walks away and all Kenny does is stares as the friend that he shared his thoughts his dreams, and his life is gone from his life for ever. The scene changes to ten year later and we see Kenny in his apartment all alone he is no longer a wrestler and is watching tv. What he is watching brings tears to his eyes for he is watching his former friend Ryan still wrestling and is the world champion just like he was before the difference is Ryan wasn’t a sell out and he was and now he is paying for it by being alone in life while Ryan parties with all of his loved ones. And that has brought on this night tears of sorrow to Kenny. The FAW Tron becomes black and Spawn is show again as he starts to speak to the fans. Spawn: Did all of you like it (the fans scream yes) that is good I am glad to entertain you but now let us get to the point at hand. Did you understand it Crimson, are you Kenny will you betray me, pray you don’t because I am not Ryan and I will exact my revenge upon you. There is no dictionary here no words with meaning it all about trust my friend. The trust I will give you this Thursday to fight a clean fight and to trust you in still being my friend no matter the outcome can you do this? Can I trust you I think I can and I know you can trust me. But just to let you know I will defeat you this Thursday, and I will demoralize you beating on you from post to post you will see what pain is all about you will witness your demise and you will beg me for mercy and as always I will show none. This is the way of the Hell Master and is the way it must be done, nothing personal just friends going at it. One more thing before I let you all go Crimson when this is all over and the best man win remember this I will always be your friend. So let it be written….. So let it be done. Hell has spoken. The FAW Tron turns black as the scene fades to black.
