Panic Roleplay 1 - Change: Episode 1

31-01-03
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Panic Roleplay 1 - Change: Episode 1 Spider Furiously Addictive Wrestling Roleplay #243 Date: 31-01-03 Location: FAW Arena/Lazlo's Home [The scene is a car, the inside; it is a very luxurious interior, leather seats, all very clean. Spider is driving, Lazlo is sitting behind him and Nina is sitting in the passenger seat, they are stopped at the traffic lights, a long queue of cars stretches down the road, trying to leave the When World’s Collide arena, they only move every few minutes. Spider is (For some strange reason) wearing nothing except his shoes and some ripped and baggy blue jeans and his dog tags. Nina is wearing some pink hot pants and a pink top aswell as some knee high pink boots, seemingly pink is her colour for the night. Lazlo is the odd one out, in a black suit with a tie and shirt underneath] Spider: “You know I’ve been thinking…” Lazlo: “Don’t strain yourself…” [Spider ignores Lazlo’s comment] Spider: “Maybe it’s time I changed…” Lazlo: “My god, the boy thinks ‘changing is the answer’, let’s lock him in a cupboard and never let him out!” [Spider ignores Lazlo again, but is evidently annoyed] Spider: “I mean, for almost two years I’ve worn the same types of clothes, had the same hair, I’ve never changed my ring style, I’m still just a cruiserweight that only cut it once because the talent in his time was crap…” Lazlo (Singing): “Oh won’t you give my dog… a bone…?” [Lazlo hums to himself, Nina shakes her head, Spider keeps talking] Spider: “I went out first in that rumble and it’s made me realise, I’m out-dated, I’m not fresh to FAW anymore, what do you think?” [Nina looks nervous, Lazlo has finally shit up] Lazlo: “Dude… you’re not getting rid of the mojo are you…? Please keep the mojo!” Spider: “I can get a new mojo!” Nina: “Babe, I love you for what you are now, not for being something else, it’s not worth changing.” Lazlo: “Listen to the woman! She makes sense!” Spider: “Lazlo, you’re my manager, Nina, you’re my girlfriend, but both of you are wrong, and you know it, it’s time I changed!” Lazlo (Jokingly almost crying): “The mojo! The mojo! Why take the mojo?” [Spider shakes his head; Nina just sits back and seemingly goes into a sulk] Spider: “Tomorrow I’m leaving early, don’t look for me, I’ll be back for tea, then you’ll be glad I rarely listen to you!” [Lazlo rolls his eyes] ~ Scene Fade ~ [The new scene is back at the house, Lazlo is sitting at the dinner table, the walls are a sky blue, the carpet is a medium grey, the table is wooden, 6 chairs surround it and a bit of plant life around the room, the table is set. Lazlo is wearing a different suit that looks very similar to the one he wore the night before. Lazlo just sits there as Nina brings his and her tea in. Nina is wearing some tight jeans and a white belly top] Nina: “Don’t help or anything.” [Lazlo grins a cheeky grin as she puts his food in front of him, he grabs his knife and fork and digs in as Nina sits down] Nina: “Where IS Vincent?” Lazlo (While scoffing food): “E dfn’t new (I don’t know).” [As Lazlo finishes his muffled sentence you hear the front door open, after a few seconds of Nina looking Spider walks into the dining room, but it’s not Spider. Spider is wearing a black hoody with his name in white bold lettering across the front (Vincent Beck, not Spider), the sleeves are rolled up, he is wearing baggy blue jeans that are not torn like his usual jeans, they also have his name in the same lettering on the back pockets and some black and white trainers with vanity plates, also with his name on, a chain hangs halfway down to his knee from the left hand side of his leg, he has his ear pierced with an upside down cross and finally a tattoo on his forearm which has a picture of a Spider and the words “RIP” in large red lettering. The most noticeable thing though is his hair, no longer are the trademark red locks flowing, instead his natural black hair is short and spiked up with blonde tints on the end] Lazlo: “Get the hell out of my house!” Vincent (Spider): “Huh?” Nina: “You… you look so different.” [Vincent shrugs] Vincent: “I told you, you like?” Nina: “I-I-I…” [Nina almost passes out, she just sits down, shocked, looking at him with awe, Lazlo is still eating, when he finally finishes he comments] Lazlo: “I like it! It’s fresh, and you got rid of the crimson hair, that’s like the trademark dude, it’s almost your whole mojo!” Vincent: “That it may be, but on Panic I’m going to’ show those sum bitches my brand new mojo!” Lazlo: “What sum bitches?” Vincent: “Never mind…” [Nina keeps just staring at Vincent, he smiles cheerily] ~ Scene Fade ~ [The new scene is Vincent looking in the mirror at his reflection. Nina is in bed, fast asleep. Vincent is just wearing baggy jeans and a chain around his neck, the same as he did in the car the night before aside from different jeans. He just stares; the look in his eyes is one of solemn expression] Vincent’s mind: “What is it?! What the hell is missing? Is Lazlo right…?” [Vincent suddenly gets a ‘That would never happen’ expression on his face] Vincent’s mind: “… have I lost my mojo…? Hey wait, what the hell? There’s no such thing as a mojo!” [Vincent shakes his head and jumps in bed] Vincent’s mind: “Is there…?” [Vincent shakes his head forcefully and lays it on the pillow] ~ Scene Fade ~
