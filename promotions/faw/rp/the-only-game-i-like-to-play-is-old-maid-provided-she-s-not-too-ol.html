The only game I like to play is Old Maid - provided she's not too old (Panic rp1) 9th Wonder Of Foam Furiously Addictive Wrestling Roleplay #273 Date: 12th February 2003 Location: Foamy Hills …. Foamy Hills Mansion – Tuesday Morning …. (The youthful sound of glee fills Foamy Hills mansion. It is a sunny day, the birds are singing, and two entities are madly in love. We see the FAW Championship belt, right close, in fact that’s all we can see, then we pull out and notice unsheathed human flesh. On closer inspection we see that standing in the doorway of his kitchen is the 9th Wonder Of Foam completely naked except for the FAW Championship Belt) Foamy: Oh what a life a belty? (Foamy sits down at the kitchen table and puts the FAW belt on the table) Foamy: It’s just you and me now. (Foamy pauses and acknowledges something) Foamy: Thank you, I think you have beautiful eyes too. You know what would make my life complete? (Foamy pauses) Foamy: No although that would be a good idea provided we get Ayatollah Kohmeni to agree to that. No imagine if you will you, me and Natalie Portman. (The FAW Championship belt jumps off the table seemingly of its own accord) Foamy: What you don’t like my idea? You never like my ideas, you thought sacking Lars, Enrique, Schmidt and those ungrateful bastard fans was a bad idea, but you know what? Ratings soared and I am now more popular than ever because I am my only fan left, 100% approval, can you believe that? Look just think about it, you’d have my heart and Natalie Portman could have my ball sack. (The Belt leaps off the table again) Foamy: A good point, your leatherback does feel nice against my throbbing crotch. You know there is a reason why they call it a Golden Nine, and it ain’t because it’s 9 inches long either. (Foamy pauses) Foamy: No, you wouldn’t fit around it. Lars! Lars! (Foamy waits) Foamy: Oh yeah I fired his ass. I’ll have to collect my own mail. (Foamy gets up but turns around at the same time and we only see his firm, toned buttocks. He grabs the belt and drapes it over his shoulder.) Foamy: I wonder what that no good useless bastard is doing now? …. A back alley, Manhattan …. (Lars is rhythmically moving his hand up and down, what is in it is uncertain, but the look on the man standing right in front of him leads this writer to fear the worst) Man: That’s it harder, harder, faster, squeeze the tip it will stop it from coming out. Lars: Sorry dude, I just can’t open this jam jar, despite it leaking from the lid there is no budging it, looks like you’ll have to ask someone else. (The man leaves, Lars curls up into a ball and tries to keep warm) …. Back At The Foam Factory …. (We see Foamy’s buttocks walking down the hallway, he opens the front door and picks up the mail, standing there is Floyd Rotterdam, and his film crew including Dave the camera dude.) Foamy: What? (Floyd begins to laugh) Foamy: What? (Foamy looks down to his crotch) Floyd: Ha! It’s tiny! Foamy: Yeah I just love these new miniature milk bottles. (Foamy holds one up in the air) Foamy: Come on in. (Foamy turns to us, he is wearing a flesh coloured jock) …. Foamy’s Study …. (Foamy has now slipped into something a little more comfortable, namely a monogrammed dressing gown, perched on his desk is the FAW Championship belt, also decked out in a monogrammed dressing gown. Floyd and his crew look on) Foamy: So what you guys want? Floyd: Well you have to face seven other men in a First Blood Elimination Match. Foamy: Ah yes there will be eight men in that ring and seven of them are homosexuals. Floyd: Really? Foamy: I don’t know, I can’t back that up with actual facts but my word is good enough, I am after all the 9th Wonder Of Foam. Floyd: In amongst these men is The Omega Fro the man you recently defeated to retain the FAW Championship and Rzarector the man you will face at Extreme Beating 3. Foamy: Yeah, yeah but what about the other guys. I mean men of the calibre of Deadman, Flash, hang on what the hell is this a comic book? If I wanted to see a comic strip I’d watch Jerry Seinfeld prepare for a shower. (Foamy pauses for laughter, there is none. Suddenly the FAW championship begins to bounce, and some canned laughter is piped in through some speaker on his desk) Foamy: Do you know what that was? Floyd: No. Foamy: That was the FAW Championship laughing at my joke. She has a sense of humour, you… do not. I mean did Lisa whatever the hell her name is actually dig up a real dead corpse and expects him to compete? Did she drag out that guy who always streaks at Wimbledon? Sadly not, I think she is trying to give FAW’s newest stars a tryout. She needn’t worry; I’ll show them the ropes, the mat, the ring post, the FoamyBomb and then the exit. Floyd: What about Lee Smith and Sean Mason? Foamy: What about them? Sean Mason is in the match not for his athletic ability, not because he deserves to be in my slot on Panic but because Lisa got drunk last week and woke up with him the next morning and to cover up the fact that she is so bad in bed she gave him this amazing opportunity to grace the very ring that the 9th Wonder Of Foam will tread. And Lee Smith? He may be a high flyer but I will ground all flights out of the city on Sunday night, including making Lee Smith come apart in mid air. Floyd: Do you think that the Omega Fro will be out for revenge following When World’s Collide 2003? Foamy: Revenge for what? I beat his ass all over that ring, fair and square. He should stop whining about it and move on, I know I have. I know that right now I couldn’t give a flying Lee Smith what he is out for because I have proved myself against him. Besides after what I did to him on Sunday shortly before I fired every single one of FAW’s fans, and my Evil squad I don’t think he will even have the balls to look me in the face. Floyd: That may be so but surely Rzarector will be out to try and get one over on you before EB3? Foamy: Get one over on me? I got one over on him Sunday night and I didn’t even do anything. Perhaps I was wrong when I said that he deserved a shot at being pinned by me. He couldn’t even defeat the FAW Hardcore champion, what chance does he have of reaching that plateau from where my plateau can be seen? He can pray to all the gods he wants but I think God grew tired of him and converted to Islam just to get away from the whiney bitch. Floyd: Finally can I just remind you that the match is a first blood match? Foamy: And? Blood does not run through my veins, the fans energy used to run through my veins before I sacked them for being fat and ignorant. No pain, anger, vengeance and Foam run through my veins, so how in the bloody hell Mary can I bleed? The answer is I can’t and from my victory this Sunday I will go on and have the great pleasure of being the most crooked referee this side of a Don King Boxing match! Have a Foam Filled Day!
