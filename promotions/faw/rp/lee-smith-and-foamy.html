Lee Smith and Foamy

12-29-02
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Lee Smith and Foamy Rzarector Furiously Addictive Wrestling Roleplay #127 Date: 12-29-02 Location: On your tv They say that I was just a evil person. Little did they know that inside this freakish body was a soul so pure that the cleanest water couldn't baptize him. I was the chosen one. For what some people don't understand is that if a person looks evil, doesn't necessarily mean that he is that evil. --The screen comes on to show highlights of Evil Intentions. The fans are screaming as Lee Smith and Foamy are brutally beating Rzarector. They run down and take Rzarector off in a stretcher as Lee Smith and Foamy continue battle. Halfway down the ramp the ref counts to 10 for Rzarector to get up. He is at 9 count when the Black Priest jumps up. The crowd screams in amazement as he runs drunk down to the ring where Smith and Foamy are still battling. Rzarector runs in and starts getting beat down. The video then goes forward in the match where Rzarector pins Lee Smith. Foamy and Rzarector are now battling where Rzarector goes for the pin and almost wins, but Foamy kicks out. Rzarector shocked as Foamy and Rza keep battling. The video cuts forward some more to show Foamy pinning Rzarector and becoming the new world champ. The first words of a champion:-- *From the PPV.* The 9th Wonder of The Foam: Ah, It’s good to be evil. --The video fades out as the camera goes black. A narrators voice, which is Rzarectors, is heard through your television. The voice is so dark, empty, and so painful to hear.-- Narrator: What has this world come to? What has the FAW come to? The dark ages are upon us all. Now I beg that they be one saviour. Yet there is still one man left with a clean heart, one man with a clean soul. The man is me the 'Black Priest' Rzarector. --The camera fades on to show Rzarector walking through Washington D.C. He is looking at the Vietnam Memorial Wall. He is hissing at it as he passes by it and stops. The camera does a full body shot of Rzarector showing him wearing his full black hooded robe over his body. His eyes are seen shining out of the robe, which are green, meaning he is about half way between evil and good with his soul. His white fangs flash like diamonds as he hisses at the surrounding atmosphere. Rzarector puts out his hand as he rubs it over the wall. You see on his hands he has golden claws, and on each claw there is a cross. Rzarector hisses again as he begins to talk.-- Rzarector: These people on this wall were fighters for our country. Some say they are legends. I say that they are no better than us. We live our lifes in complete content with ourselves. Yet we are not even worrying about death, or meeting our maker. I saw the future before Evil Intentions 2. I saw the forecoming of a dark and evil champion. Just like these people on this wall. They gave our lifes for us, but what about the Viatnamese? Were they any better than us? No they were not. They killed our people, and we killed there people. How much blood has to be shed before the war and pain will stop? --Rzarector takes a brief pause as his eyes start turning red. The Black Priest turns around to show that the front of his black hooded face hangs a cross out from his neck. Rzarector hisses causing his white fangs to flash.-- Rzarector: I never thought that this would happen! I had respect for this wrestling orginization. Well not anymore. Letting something evil be world champion. It was bad enough with the retired jobber Spawn. Even when Bomber was champion, he was nothing but a has been. They were all mislead, yet Foamy is a true evil. Foamy will bring the end to the FAW. I promise you this. --Rzarector stops for a second seeing that he is getting strong messages in his head. He looks dazed out as the voices from God start talking to him.-- *God: Rzarector, my son, you shall not diss something you do not know. Foamy is merely evil at the heart, yet it has not reached his soul yet. There is still time to get him back to where he should be. Back to the Kingdom of God with the rest of my children. One day Foamy may understand. Yet Satan has let him have this title. For I can't control what you do on Earth. Yet I promise eternal happiness after death. Rzarector, do you understand?* --Rzarector hisses.-- Rzarector: Yes I understand, yet I am still very confused. It just doesn't make sense. How can someone so evil and so wrong become something so great? I never cared about the title God, I never did. It was just a piece of metal with markings on it. All I was going to use it for was to help get popularity so I can spread your word. Now Foamy has it, and everyone that follows him, will follow the Devil himself. Maybe I don't even need to be in the FAW anymore. *God: Yes you should be. I sent you from the deep pits of hell to Earth for one purpose. To seek and destroy the skulls of devils for my cause. Foamy, he is not fully evil. Yet in the future he will become evil. Show him no mercy, yet keep his soul alive. For Foamy has the gold now, he is the leader of the FAW. A very dark day comes to the FAW. I promise you this Rzarector. I keep my word, have I ever lied to you?* Rzarector: No God. *God: Well then beleive me. Foamy will change some day. For right now go make sure noone hears him speak. For the time is upon us. May all who read this, and all who read this in the future know that out there somewhere was a child hearing Foamy's words. Acting on his every word. Foamy preaches the Devils tongue, you Rza, you preach God's word. My word. So for now, I'm going to leave you with this slight message. Find Foamy, interrupt him. Make sure that noone ever hears from him again. Go now.* Rzarector: Yes God. --Rzarector's head all of a sudden feels very empty. The messages had left, and the sign of God was no longer there. No one in the world knew that Rzarector was actually speaking to God. They all called him crazy, they knew nothing.-- Rzarector: I feel so drained, yet so alive. The world as we know it has nothing to fear. For I am here. This panic I face Lee Smith. What some will say a match. I haven't heard from Lee Smith since I defeated him at The Dome. Another devil I have put away. I doubt if we will even see him again. --Rzarector hisses out loud as the camera fades.--
