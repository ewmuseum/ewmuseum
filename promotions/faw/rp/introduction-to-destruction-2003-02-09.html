Introduction to Destruction The Great One Furiously Addictive Wrestling Roleplay #266 Date: 09/02/03 Location: Group Hideout 'You tell that mother f****r that if he doesn't get the money to me buy Tuesday, not Wednesday, by Tuesday at 5pm, not 6pm, 5 pm then I am going to hunt his ass down, tie him up and get a power drill and drill four holes in both of his f*****g knees!' Great One hangs up the phone. 'Bastards!' Great One puts his phone away as one of his associates looks at him from across the table in disbelief. 'What?' Great One questions still pissed off. 'Nothing I just don't think it's that a big a deal for you to be getting all angry and sweating like a pig as you shout down the phone at him. It's not even that much money,' says his associate trying to reason with him... unsuccessfully. 'First of all that sweating like a pig thing I find offensive and it's a stupid thing to say, I have never seen a pig with a pool of sweat underneath it, so shut the f**k up alright! Second of all it's not the fact that the cash isn't much but if we let any of these bastards welch on their overdrafts and all that s**t then they'll start taking us for granted and they'll be thinking that they can get whatever the hell they want, when they want. I'm a hard ass bastard alright, and I don't take no shit from nobody!' Great One dabs away at the sweat on his forehead with his handkerchief. 'And that son of a bitch knows that if he can get an extension on his deadline this time then he can do it time and time again.' 'But boss...' 'Don't but boss me because there ain't nothing to but boss me about. I've been taking shit from him for weeks and if he thinks he can carry it on then I'm going to have to teach him otherwise. Now round up the boys, we got a meeting to attend.' 'Okay boss,' says the associate as he slowly gets out his chair and gets the others from the recreation room. The room is full of smoke from cigars and there are beer bottles all over the place as well as numerous stacks of cards and some pages from pornography magazines. 'Yo, come on quit your f****n' around we got a meeting to go to.' One of the guys (Cornelius) in the room responds with, 'We'll be right there Al, but I don't think Sammy is going to be able to make it.' The Great One: Why the f**k not? As Great One shouts from the back, Cornelius gets a little worried. Cornelius: Well, he's a bit pissed. The Great One: Bring his ass out here. The guys in the room all come out, carrying Sammy. The Great One: Sammy wake up. Sammy I said wake up. Great One slaps him but he still doesn't come round. The Great One: How about I put his balls in a grinder, that ought to wake him up. Or maybe I'll put them in a vice. What a decision, grind them or squash them, what should I do? The others all can't believe that Great One is actually contemplating this act and then bravely one of them speaks up. Dorian: Boss, don't hurt him, he's a great asset to this team. The Great One: No, my fists are a great asset to this team, if he can get pissed before 2pm then he is a weak, dumb son of a bitch and he doesn't deserve to be in my group. You take him outside and you throw him in the gutter and leave him there. Be back in two minutes! Al: But boss... The Great One: Just do it, dammit! They take him outside and throw him into the gutter as Great One puts a blazer on to finish his outfit which is one expensive looking suit. Dorian opens the back door of the limo for Great One and Al to get in as Cornelius gets in the passenger side of the car in the front. Dorian then gets in the drivers seat and takes them to the meeting, which is only 2 minutes away. The Great One: This better go to plan man because if it doesn't then it's all of our asses that are on the line, especially that f**k Sammy's. Al: Why Sammy's? You kicked him out. The Great One: Well it'll be his fault if it goes wrong, for getting pissed. Al: What's that got to do with it? The Great One: It means that if someone is going to try me then there's only four of us to get the sons of bitches, meaning I'll go to that alleyway and cut off his balls and shove them so far up his f*****g ass that they will replace his eyes in the top of his head. Al: You get p****d off way to easily man. The Great One: Shut up! The car comes to a stop and Dorian opens the door for Al and Great One to get out. The Great One: Now, we go through here. Has everyone got a piece? They all nod. The Great One: Good. Now remember. if this doesn't go to plan then we use our pieces but unless something happens the weapons stay in the blazers got that. Again they all nod. The Great One: All right then, lets go! They all walk into the hall as a crowd of about fifty people sits around all chatting amongst themselves until Great One enters and they all shut up and look at him. Great One stops. The Great One: What? Have I split Beer down my shirt or something? He gets no response. The Great One: Well then what the hell are you all staring at? Great One walks through them all and up onto the stage. His henchmen follow him up and stand behind him on the stage. The Great One: Now, some of you may know no me and for all the idiots that don't, I am The Great One and I am now your new boss. This causes an outrage amongst all the people in the hall and they all stand up and start yelling. The Great One: Shut the F**k up before all of you have to start going to no testicles anonymous! This gets their attention and they all shut up and listen to what he has to say. The Great One: Thank you. I am your new boss. You do not get a say in it, you do not get to run alongside me in the votes for who is new boss, you do not get a say in the matter. Your old boss who was actually named Billy Joe thought he could get away with f*****g my bosses wife, so he ended up back at the place where he came from. Well let me rephrase that, parts of him ended up back where he came from. My boss talked with his boss and it was arranged that I will be your boss. In the end it came down to me and this other guy, I like to call him the nice side of me. But the nice side of me has gone home for the winter because I told that mother f****r that I was going to kick his ass. Now you all have me to answer to. Any questions? One man raises his hand. The Great One: Yes? Man: I'm sorry but that's not right. You are a dictator and this is tyranny! The Great One: Damn you are one smart little bitch aren't you, would you like a sticker for you excellent work? Man: No I just... The Great One: You just what? You just thought you would try and get people to respect you by standing up to me? Is that it? Man: No, I just... The Great One: Come up here. The man walks up with a frightened look on his face. Great One punches him in the stomach and then knees him in the face as he goes down to the floor with a busted nose. The Great One: Does anyone else have any questions? No one moves a muscle in fear of Great One thinking that they wanted to ask a question. The Great One: Good. Despite what you have seen here I can be a decent, understanding person and you will all soon get to like me. I am not just on this job but I also work in Furiosly Addictive Wrestling where I beat the hell out of people for a living. So I guess my two jobs both come together pretty well. Tonight, to celebrate my leadership, you are all invited to my Casino and you all start with ten thousand dollars and you see how fast it takes for all of you to lose it. And that's my first nice act that I've done for you, and I'll see you all later. Great One signals for his associates to leave and they walk out quickly and get back in the limo. The Great One: That went well don't you think? Al: I'm not sure, you gave them the wrong impression about you at first I think. They need to like and respect you, not hate your guts. The Great One: I just gave every one of them ten thousand dollars. If they don't like me after that then there's something wrong. Al: But you did beat up that guy. The Great One: Oh he deserved it, and anyway I got to get a bit of practice in for my match on Wednesday. Al: You need practice? Since when? The Great One: I might be God’s gift to wrestling and the world for that matter but I still got to practice. Al: Ok, but why did you challenge that kid anyway? I thought you wanted to get your hands on JLC. The Great One: I do. The car stops and they get out as they find themselves back at the base. The Great One: Who the f**k made this mess? Al: What mess? The Great One: This mess right here. Al: Oh, that one. I don’t know, it wasn’t like that when left. The Great One: SAMMY! Sammy: WHAT!? The Great One: Get your ass over here boy! Sammy: What if I don’t? The Great One: Then you better send me a letter telling me what the conditions are like at the hospital incase I get hit. Sammy: Are you threatening me? The Great One: No I’m about to kick the s**t out of you! Great One kicks a door in and there is Sammy with a baseball bat. The Great One: Put the baseball bat down. Sammy: Put my balls in a grinder were you going to do, huh! I’ll put your head in a coma when I hit it with this bat! The Great One: If you touch me with that bat you know what will happen, you know. Sammy: I don’t care. The Great One: Sammy, it’s all right. You got pissed and I got angry but I was tense. I can forgive you. Sammy: You can? The Great One: Yeah, but you gotta put the bat down first, I ain’t getting hit with no baseball bat and the person who hit me live, you know that. Sammy drops the baseball bat on the floor. The Great One: There you go, that’s better, no come here man. Great One and Sammy hug. The Great One: Now come on man don’t ever do that to me again all right, don’t get pissed and don’t go mad, swinging a bat around the place. Oh and clean this mess up will you. Sammy: I will, I’ll repay you for my actions. I’ll clean everything up and then I’ll work harder than ever, I’ll make sure that you get the very best, whatever you want. The Great One: All right then, after this is cleaned up, you go out and you get me a laptop alright, I need to write a few things up on the computer. Sammy: I’ll get you the best damn laptop there is! The Great One: Good, good, I like your optimism. No get this shit cleaned up. Great One walks out to the other three. Al: Is everything all right? The Great One: Yeah, yeah, everything’s fine. But if he gets pissed again you take him to see one of those alcohol people all right, I don’t want him having a drinking problem. Now I’m going to get some practice in for my match on Wednesday. Al: But boss, I thought we were going to go see the acrobatic lesbians! The Great One: They’re here all week, we’ll go tomorrow, I got to practice for this match. I also got to think up by diss speech, I got to make this once nice and sweet because this bitch thinks he actually has something on me, he thinks he can get away with playing The Great One game. He has been calling himself The Great One and himself the greatest wrestler and all this shit. He’s one dumb son of a bitch and hasn’t done his homework, I’m a two time all American for Christ’s sake, and he thinks he’s a better technical wrestler than me. He doesn’t have a clue about me. You know, he even tried an old Spawn tactic and made shit up, said that I had said and done things when I hadn’t. The check of this guy is unbelievable, I am going to shove my hand down his throat and rip out his f****n’ lungs for this the piece of s**t! You guys can come to resistance on Wednesday and kick his ass after the match because there is no way that he is getting away with this. I’ll beat him and take his title while you guys can watch, and then when the match is finished you guys can come in and finish the job while I talk to him on the mic and take the p**s. Al: Sounds great boss. The Great One: You’re damn right it sounds great, that’s because it came out of my mouth and anything I say or do is great, unlike this stupid dumb Profusion f**k! He says I can call him Terasa-Profusion or some s**t, I’ll call him whatever the hell I want and if that means calling him “D**khead who just got his a*****e penetrated by a baseball bat” then I’ll do it. Al: This Profusion sounds like a real wise guy. The Great One: Oh he is, oh he is! And I’m going to teach him a wise lesson that he’ll never forget!
