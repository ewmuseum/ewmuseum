Hunger.... (Panic) 'Legendary' Lee Smith Furiously Addictive Wrestling Roleplay #259 Date: 8th February 2003 Location: FAW HQ The limousine burst through the guardrail, snapping it into two. The driver drove around the carpark like a lunatic, narrowly dodging several bollards on his trip to the entrance. He finally weaved his way out of the last pillar and applied the brakes - which caused much noise. Without pausing to think the driver….a Mr Lee Smith - stepped out of the limousine instantly. In front of him was the FAW Entrance…..a small banner held high above the doors. Suddenly a figure came bursting out from inside the building. His name? Jon The Bomb. “Hey Lee - what’s with the driving?” Jon said, carrying a briefcase and a bundle of papers. “Chauffeur has fallen ill……food poisoning I think” Lee replied, juggling the keys in his two hands. The two remained silent before Lee spoke again “So….anything you want from me?” he asked as Jons face lit up. “I have some video footage I think you should see” he said, as Lee chuckled at Jons sudden interest in his career. Perhaps it was the fact that this Sunday Lee was teaming up with one of Jons arch enemies from the treasured OSW days of 2001. Jon pulled Smith by the hand and dragged him through the corridors….deep into the archives. He eventually found what he wanted, it was labelled ‘Mason Footage’. They finally arrived at Lees dressing room several minutes later, and Jon burst in (almost breaking the door down) and shoved the tape into the VCR. It had already begun by the time Lee entered with hands up in the air, yawning a loud. 10 Minutes Later Jon took the tape out of the VCR as Lee got his thinking cap on. “That Mason….one hell of a wrestler” Lee said as Jon turned to him. “I learnt that the hard way. I don’t want you to make the same mistakes.” Jon said with obvious pain In his voice. Lee didn’t dare ask how he had ‘learnt the hard way’ and instead changed the subject. “I have met him before you know…..back when I was at the helm in FAW. I didn’t like him then and I sure don’t like him now. Bbbut…..we have to co-exist. If there is one thing I’ve learnt since my return is that alliances in the ring are bad ideas……Mason will do his job and I will do mine. His job? To destroy his nemesis…Deadman. My job? My job is to show that hyprocrite Flash that he should never have quit Hollywood. And after I triumphantly beat that Hollywood-reject last week……things can’t get any closer. I felt satisfaction after that victory. Like someone was feeding me with morale. But….be warned.” Lee stops and looks deep into Jons eyes. “I am still hungry”
