Will I be at DDU?

25 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Will I be at DDU? 'The Real Prodigy' Scott Costella Furiously Addictive Wrestling Roleplay #43 Date: 25 October 2002 Location: Parents home, Detoit, Michigan The scene opens on a long, peaceful road. It is the kinda road where you would expect to find celebrities living there, out of the way of the busy suburbs. The sky is a tranquil, light blue and clouds are sparse. A passenger Jet can be seen flying overhead, leaving its white fluffy trail behind it. The camera moves in to focus on one house in particular. A rather large house, two storeys, a garage and a nice, little garden. A woman, of no more than 50 walks out of the house and into the garden. She kneels down and begins to do some garden work. The house itself, is very elegant and clean looking. It looks like it belongs to a couple who are enjoying retirement after years of hardwork and saving up. The scene switches to the inside of the upstairs of the house. The carpet is a soft, white that feels heavenly to walk on. The walls are a pales blue and have pictures all along each side. The pictures are mainly of a family of four, perahps taken 10-15 years ago. There is a man and a woman with two teenage boys - Scott and Steve. They both look like they are having fun and are getting along very well... Not at all like it is these days. The camera pushes forward into a room at the end or the corridor. There is a knock on the door and a person in the room asks them to come in. Adam Hutton walks into the room. It is a guest room, decorated like a palace. Obviously these people like to live extravegantly and make people welcome... They obviously have the money to do that! The TV, in the corner is switched on and tuned into Jerry Springer, 'On the second half of todays show we are going to be looking at women who are paid to have sex. Is this sort of thing ok to do? These woman argue that it is work, just like any other job. Lets welcome our guests...' Next comes a chorus of boos from the woman in audience and wolf whistles from the men in the audience. The volume is suddenly turned down and the camera turns to show Scott Costella laying in bed. He has his new eyebrow piercing and several, recovering cuts over his face. He has a large, white bandage around his chest and is resting on two large, plump pillows. Next to him, is a large bag of chips and a 6 pack of Faygo - 2 cans already drunk. Adam Hutton: So, Scott... A lot of us back at FAW, were worried about you, having gone missing. Rumours were circulating that you had been re-captured and taken back to the... 'The Island' again. Scott Costella: Oh... Well.. Thank you, I guess... Adam Hutton: Well... The main question, everyone wants to know, is will you be taking part in your match, against Joey Johnson, at Disaster Down Under? Scott: I am still un-decided as of yet. Although my ribs are still bruised, they are feeling a lot better and I feel that I could manage to do the match... But I also don't want to injure myself, further and risk been out of action for several months. Adam Hutton: Yes... It would be wise to perhaps, miss this match out and see how you feel in another week. Scott: Perhaps... But I feel I could beat this.. Joey Johnson... Because I am 'The Real Prodigy'... 'The Man With All The Talent'... So I'm hoping to be able to perform and show everyone that 'The Real Prodigy' Scott Costella is back... Adam Hutton: I see your near death experience hasn't made you a little more humble... Scott: And what's that suppose to mean? Adam Hutton: Nothing... However, There is only aproximately 50 hours left before Disaster Down Under will commence... If you don't think you can manage it but then you decide you can on Sunday morning... You probably wouldn't be able to get there on time... Scott: Well... Maybe I won't be there then... We shall have to see... Scott: But since I, 'The Real Prodigy' came back from that 'Island'... I have been trying to make a good name for myself... seen as though, 'The Fake' Scott Costella, nearly ruined my career... I have been working hard in the gym again, Made some friends backstage... Hell, I even read to some little 8 year olds at my old school... little pricks!... Adam Hutton: How charitable of you... Scott: But now this accident and I might have to miss Disaster Down Under... Typical... Adam Hutton: Well, we understand that you were on your way to visit your parents... And a nice house they have by the way... Scott: Thank-you... I helped pay for it... Adam Hutton: Oh... Well that was nice of you... Anyway, How did the accident happen? Scott: There I was, driving along, listening to my 'Offspring' CD, when suddenly some Cock Jockey comes up at the side of me and cuts across me to take the next lane but i had to slow down and because he was going so fast and none of the other drives knew what he was doing, he ended up smashing into another car and the back of his car crashed wih the front of mine. Luckily, I had my seat belt on and I was forced into the steering wheel. The front window, broke and ended up been thrown at me. Adam Hutton: You were lucky you came out as you did then... Scott: Yes... He wasn't as lucky though... As i understand, he is still in hospital... Adam Hutton: Oh... how unlucky... Scott: Serves him right, stupid ass! Adam Hutton: Well, it has been nice talking to you and I wish you the speediest of recoveries... Scott: Thank you... Adam Hutton leaves the guest room and is shown to the front door and asked questions by Scotts dad. Interested and Intrusive parents are such a pain! As Adam is leaving, the TV is turned back up and you can hear the audience on Jerry Springer chanting, wildly 'JERRY...JERRY...JERRY!'. Scott opens up another 'Faygo' and the scene fades out. 'The Real Prodigy' was not abducted again but instead he was lying in a hospital bed, all this time. Will he feel 100% and good enough to perform against Joey Johnson at Disaster Down Under? With just a little over 48 hours left, before it starts, it doesn't give him a lot of time to decide of recover properly. Tune into Disaster Down Under to find out if he makes it and all the other fantastic matches and shocking events that are bount to happen...
