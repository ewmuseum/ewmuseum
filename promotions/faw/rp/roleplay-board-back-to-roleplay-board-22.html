J.J Dynasty — FAW Roleplay, August 2002

August 2002

Furiously Addictive Wrestling Roleplay Board Roleplay Board | Back to Roleplay Board J.J Dynasty Entitled: For control of Joe La Fontaine's destiny (Breakdown RP part 1 of 2) Written: 8/8/02 Location: Boston Rooftop The clouds atop Boston Harbor are slowly parting way as the FAW cameras slowly pan across the skyline, showing the tops of some of Boston’s premier establishments. After turning almost 360 degrees the cameras fall upon a man whose back is towards the camera, his face shrouded by a black hooded jacket and a pair of dark wrap-around sunglasses. He turns slowly to face the camera and only then is it revealed that the man is none other than the “Master of Destiny” J.J. Dynasty. He stares at the camera for what seems like hours before finally pulling his sunglasses of and locking eyes with the camera lens, as if trying to peer into the brains of each and every fan watching live on EEL television. Dynasty: The day has finally drawn to a close and I am but two days, a mere forty-eight hours, away from standing toe to toe, face to face and rookie to legend from none other than nXn member, Joe La Fontaine. Now I will give Joe his due, he is a capable, skilled athlete who has already made a name for himself in this federation, even going so far as to help in a kidnapping scheme against our very own World Champion, the British Bomber. He is the type of athlete that I have wanted to compete against ever since signing on with FAW, not worrying about withering, has-beens like Cyrus Skar. Now it is true that last week I was defeated by Joe La Fontaine’s stooge and fellow nXn member, X-Rodd. It is also true that he beat me fair and square in the center of the ring with the X-Bomb, however I have gotten past that loss and I am man enough to be able to call a spade a spade and accept my defeat. However, X-Rodd, if you are watching out there, do not think for one single, solitary second that you and I have crossed paths for the last time. We shall meet inside that squared circle again and when that time comes, the outcome will be altogether different. Dynasty slowly begins to walk across the rooftop, stopping at an air duct, which he chooses to sit next to in an awkward silence for a few moments, perhaps off in his own little world or perhaps pondering his next though. At any rate it is a good five minutes before he finally looks up and continues to speak to the cameraman, the fans watching at home and more importantly than anybody else, the target of his rage, Joe La Fontaine. Dynasty: Joe La Fontaine, my attention must now return to the match at hand. You must realize that this match is not merely about which one of us gets to have another W put in his Win column. No this match is about pride, the pride to men have when both believe that they are the better technician, the pride two men have when they believe that the other has no chance. That pride that you have towards your technical ability is about to be snuffed out as you must step inside the squared circle against me, the Master of Destiny. I am the man who come this Saturday shall be the one with his arm raised in victory as you slowly crawl across the canvas after suffering the one, two, three at my hands. This match is going to be a fight for your heart, mind and soul Joe La Fontaine. This match will be a deciding factor in your very destiny and mine as well, are you prepared for what could happen to your legacy when I defeat you and your destiny is changed from that of a champion to that of a curtain-jerking punk? I shall take great pride in watching as twist and writhe in pain as I pummel you from one turnbuckle to the other. I shall watch the agonizing torture that you shall go through as I beat you to a bloody, broken, humiliated pulp. I shall enjoy watching, as you are embarrassed in front of all your peers, your teammates from nXn and the millions of people worldwide who shall tune in to see two of the very best go one-on-one inside the squared circle. You do not yet know what you are up against Joe La Fontaine, but in forty-eight hours you shall realize the truth behind the phrase that all of the FAW superstars shall soon learn. You cannot change your destiny and you sure as hell cannot beat Dynasty. The camera slowly fades to black as Dynasty puts his sunglasses back on and watches as the sun slowly sets across the Boston horizon and the FAW house show cuts to a commercial break.
