Meditation Xing Furiously Addictive Wrestling Roleplay #239 Date: 30th January Location: Pakistan Pakistan is a wonderful place to be at the moment the scenery is wonderful the people are amazing and look this man in a full shed of clothes has a massive huddle around him sitting on the floor. Xing is on holiday in holiday recovering from his wounds down low but we go straight to the center point of this town where there is a man and a large crowd much more interesting. 'Hmm' The crowds of people watch this man an ab-normal man at that, levitate into the air he rises the Record timers get the rulers out and the timer to judge how long and how he got. 'The timing is 10Secs and the height is...' Pause goes the viewers, as the man smiles when he sees the height he celebrates the crowds don't know why. Ouch! That has got to hurt the man has been viciously sent to the ground violent man asks the height and fast, the judges respond quickly, 'The man got up to 5cm just beating the record by 0.3Cms.' The man in the center on the floor has just been challenged by the man that knocked to the ground the violent man said with a strong accent turning on a recently acquired single 'Punjab' and cranking the volume up to extreme, 'This for all the Punjabis out there I will defeat this man for the record because that time and that height was just for pussys.' 'Hmm' the crowds draw in even closer the man on his first attempt fails the crowd begin to point and laugh at him he gets up and then knocks a member of the crowd to the floor. He begins to meditate again 'Hmm' he rises the man rises the crowd chant 1 2 3 4 5 6 The crowd stop counting when Xing enters the pack of crowded people Xing makes his way into the front of the crowd the man meditating lowers himself failing to brake the record and follows Xing out of there the crowd go silent and they quickly move out of the way. The meditating man is taken down an alley a dirty one no sun gets down darkness everywhere the meditating man begins to worry he is then pushed into a ring hidden away by Xing for when he goes on holidays to Pakistan. Xing says to him chucking a coach into the ring as well, 'Now kid I know you are pretty young but you have talent know I want to take you out of this dump ok because you are needed in FAW as my tag partner. I know what you are capable of I saw what you did to those two men you have great potential with a bit of training you can be the greatest wrestler here today well second to me of course but come with me to Canada and we will file in an application we just need a name that is all ah just the thing Meditator!' Xing has his bags packed and is waiting for the 'Meditator' to arrive he does so but is seriously late Xing gives the boy an ear bashing the Meditator gets furious and knocks down a set of bags and the trolley man sending them flying across the floor. Xing is extremely happy with his finding and walks with him giving him tips that we are not allowed to say. The plane flight ends Xing and the monster wrestler arrive outside the FAW arena when the man is given a lighter and is told by Xing to take good care of it. Xing leaves the man to fill in his own application he puts a disgusting smirk on his face and just feels in the mood to have some fun so he rings a woman’s number they get acquainted because they don’t know each other but was given it during his holiday in Pakistan. He wakes up and realises that it is his wife’s death anniversary and blows off the woman four o’clock in the morning. If god is up they’re watching he might be very upset with his actions.
