Tick Tock...Tick Tock...The Time is Now

9-19-02
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Tick Tock...Tick Tock...The Time is Now Apocalypse Furiously Addictive Wrestling Roleplay #29 Date: 9-19-02 Location: Apocalypse house ….Once upon a time there was this really big giant of a man named Apocalypse. He was what you called a dark souls seeker but of a gentle touch. Everyday he would do the same think when he woke up. Go get breakfest in the kitchen, then go get a nice hot shower to wake him up, go get dress in his work out clothing, and then go work out at the local gym that was down the street. Everyday the same results would happen. He would train his hardest and focus on the task at hand. Just like this Sunday on October the 20th. The day that He faces his biggest challenge in Chris Storm…. ….Apocalypse does really need fear to help him out right now. No, he wants fear to help him out right now. You see, if you take fear into account while the time goes by then you will fear nothing when time stops. Time is lie what you call a bomb. When it is ticking away all you see is a bit of fire and then a bit of smoke after it. Everything working against you to make you fail and to make you a worthless piece of trash. Why is this? Is it because you are you and it is it? I don’t think so. I think it is because you hate pain so much that time covers pain in a way that you don’t see. Listen, of you take time and toss it out all you have is a bit of space in front of you. A place that only few have traveled with out time. Apocalypse is doing this as the days go by to count down on Chris Storm. Can you do it while you read this? Can you toss time out the door while the words go by? Lets try and see what happens…. Apocalypse: Well I only have two more days till I go one on one with him. (Apocalypse stood over a picture of what looked to be his opponent going into Distortion 19. The man named Chris Storm wearing his gold, his WIW:NG World Heavyweight title over his right shoulder. It looks like he is a bit sweaty form some match or even a work out of some sort. Apocalypse looks down at the picture for a bit and pulls it up to eye level.) Apocalypse: Why now? Why have you and me come face to face inside the square circle? Was this meant to be so that some force could figure out who was best? So many answers to figure out with the questions at hand. (Apocalypse dropped the picture to the ground. He walked away going to the far room in the camera screen. Why was he here? Why wasn’t he working out and getting ready for the fight of his life? The door slowly came open as you could see a large big screen TV with a Sony DVD player on the top of it. There was a nice VCR with a few tapes on it. Apocalypse took a few steps forward and grabbed the remote off of the near by table.) Apocalypse: Ok, it is time to go over what I have done wrong over the last few months. See where I make my mistakes in matches. I got that $500,000 dollar contract on the line in this so no playing around. I know right now Chris is doing what ever he can to get me under his mind control. Both the DCV and the FAW are mine to make good. I am going to go out there and take Chris Storm to the edge and back. Take him to the fullest of what wrestling is all about. Anything and everything will be tossed at him. I can promise him and the fans that. (Apocalypse walked over to the coach next to the table where he sat down right in front of the TV. Pushing the power button on the remote, the DVD player came on and you saw a picture of Apocalypse in a wrestling ring. The ring was nothing like the WIW or FAW has seen. It was larger and the ropes where made of a redish color. The picture clicked over and you saw Apocalypse slamming men all over the place. You saw the eight man battle royal at the D-Day PPV and the three way match for the International Title between Xander, Apocalypse, and the 9th Wonder of Foam. The scene then switched screens as you see a man by the name of Tommy Danger and another man by the name of Todd Bishop standing in the ring while Apocalypse took a chair and slammed it over there heads. The blood came rolling down their heads as Apocalypse slammed both down with a fearsome chokeslam.) Apocalypse: Boy, I remember those days. In the IGA fighting the likes of Tommy Danger. The real American hero and the Bishop of wrestling fighting me in that triple threat for the MWA FTW Title. Boy the blood that came down there head after I hit them with everything I had. Nothing stopped me that day just like nothing will stop me from taking Storm to the limit. But then that was the passed. The passed means nothing to me and nothing to the people of the WIW or FAW. They want a show right now and they want it with style. They want to see every inch of life out of us two. They want to see up go the limit and take every inch of blood, sweat, and tears form that ring. Storm, do you have that storm ready for me? Is it burning with desire to take me out of the game that you call yours? I don’t think you are prepared to show the whole world your true game in this business. Ready to let the WIW down once again. Let them down not only in the ring but in true life. I think you are scared but afraid to say it. (Apocalypse leans down and turns off the DVD player as he setback against the couch again.) Apocalypse: The time is now for me to take what is mine in this business and you Chris Storm are holding me down from what is there. You are like a disease in my side since day one and after the 20th you will be no more. Apocalypse will be a name that everyone knows in this world. The man that took out Mort in that big Interfed match up, the match that stopped Bomber from going up further then he has, and the man that took out the WIW:NG World Champion. This Sunday, Chris Storm youw ill feel pain in places that you didn’t even know you have. The torture of every move that I make will break you. Come Storm, bring everything that you can because it will not be enough. (With that, the scene slowly fades out with Apocalypse giving a smile as it does.)
