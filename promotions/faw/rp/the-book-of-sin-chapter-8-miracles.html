The Book Of Sin: Chapter 8: Miracles

10/19/2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The Book Of Sin: Chapter 8: Miracles Sin Furiously Addictive Wrestling Roleplay #25 Date: 10/19/2002 Location: Hospitol { Launching an assault, commanding a war, attacking the enemy. These are the causes of war, the pain that one suffers from this can be extreme, losing loved ones, losing lives and losing the war. How many must die in this war? How many more people must suffer in this war known as Alcohol? } [] Once again the scene opens in a bright light area, white lights beam down showing every corner of the place as men in light blue coats run around. A body on a stretcher is rolled in fast and taken into a room labeled 'ER', they are all talking loudly as they go inside the dark room with a table in the center, lights shine brightly beaming down onto the table as the doctors wheel the body next to the paper towel covered bed and lift the body from one onto the other as the doctors pick up sharp objects and slice open the stomach of the body, blood streams from the sides down the persons stomach, staining everything it comes in contact with. The doctor reachs closer and pushes his hands deep inside the body, feeling around looking for something. After a second he pulls it out and is holding a bloody organ that looks pinkish he cuts the ties to it and places it in a tray when one of the nurses see that the door is open and close it blocking all view from the operation inside. A man in a black trenchcoat turns around from the door and begins a long trip down the bright corridors that look as if they were a gateway to heaven, he gets to a door and looks to the right of him as the cameraman follows behind him. The man picks up the trenchcoat thats in front of him and looks at the label, it reads Mrs. Raynes, a smirk comes to the face of the man as he reachs up and pulls off his sunglasses and puts them into his pocket in a quick motion. He opens the door and walks in closer to a lone body in the room, the room has no windows, no doors besides the one that the man walked in and the one that is now closed. He moves closer and stands next to the hospital bed as the oxygen machine pushes up and down with a swoosh noise and the constant comferting beep of the heart machine. He reachs up to his neck and grabs a gold necklace that is wrapped around him, its a golden upside down cross, he pulls it to his lips and kisses it. Before reaching down and pulling up the womans hands and putting them together holding them tight, he pulls them up to his face and kisses her knuckles before beggining to speak. [] {Sin} Who has done this to you my daughter? A mad man who enjoys drinking like a bastard before getting out on the road and beginning the long trip home. Little did he know that that trip would be his last and that his life would be taken that very night. But without knowing that how could he know what he did to you? He cause you so much pain and anguish that he deserved to die. A collision that will never be forgotten by you, your husband, or myself. Do not ask how I saw this crash but I did, thats all that matters. My daughter this is not your time to die, it is not the time for the almight Wraith to come and take you to your resting place. Those doctors know nothing, they beleive that you will die if taken off these machines but we both know that is not true. We both know that this is not your time to be taken from us, your in your prime and its time for you to awaken, daughter of mine. It is time that you brought back from this state of the dead. You do not deserve it and I will help you through it. [] He kisses her hand again before looking up at the camera. It is the FAW wrestler Sin, he wears his normal trenchcoat with the bible sticking out of his breast pocket. Accompanied by a golden upside down cross that hangs from his neck and lowers down to a few inchs before his shirt colar. The leather cracks as he moves slightly. [] {Sin} How could you let this happen to her? How could you do something this wrong to someone so nice? Thats right, I'm talking to you Cyrus Raynes. Your wife does not deserve such punishment for this and you let it happen by letting her out that night. She didn't have to go out, you had asked her to go to the store instead of getting off your ass and getting it yourself. Maybe if you weren't such an ass you would be the one lieing in this death bed awaiting your time to die instead of your lovely wife. Your a parasite Cyrus, a damn parasite that feeds off the succession of others. Did you win that belt that you hold so dear to you by cheating or by winning it fairly? I would bet you cheated, thats all you ever do. You cheat in matchs, you cheat in gambling, you cheat while your driving and don't obey the laws, hell you even cheat on your wife. No doubt right now your eyeing some crazy stripper nurse in low cut skirts and high stockings, drooling like your wife wasn't even here. No doubt you don't care, yes you put out the effect that you care and that you cry when the doctors told you she was going to die. But you lost your temper and had to move to the offensive and scare those doctors who were only doing their job, a bad job but it is their job. Now I bet you think that you can walk in this Sunday and walk out the champion once again, oh how your wrong was again in your pathetic life Cyrus. Not only will I defeat you but I will make sure you are punished for this, for what you did to your wife, for what you put her through and for everything she doesn't even know about. But alas I am getting off the subject, the subject is that this poor woman has been punished for something she did not do and I am here to give her her dues and bring her back to the land of the living. [] He reachs up to his neck and pulls off the backwards cross and slowly lowers it to the womans hands, he places it in the middle of the two and pushs them together, pulling the hands up so he can kiss them once again. [] {Sin} My Daughter, now is the time for you to come back to us. It is time for you to return to me. I grant you the right to live, now rise my young, rise! Mua Cuate Deoa Sinatro Danoil, Rise, Rise, Rise My Daughter! [] Sin reachs down and pulls the plug to the machines, they power down and the beeping stops. He kisses her hands and the woman lays lifeless [] {Sin} Rise My Young! [] Suddenly she coughs and begins to breath with a deep breath. Her eyes open for a split second before closing and her breathing slowly returning to normal. [] {Sin} Who needs machines when the Lord is on your side? Doctors know nothing, they like to play God but they are only playing with your feelings. They toy with your feelings and they play with your mind, kind of like what I'm going to do to Cyrus this Sunday on Distortion. [] The doctors voices, as well as Cyrus' can be heard outside the door. Sin looks up and the camera turns to the door. Sin whispers to the woman. [] {Sin} (Whispering)Enjoy life my daughter, I will be back for my necklace. [] The door swings open and the camera turns back to see no one in the room except the woman, still cluching the Golden necklace as Cyrus walks in Amazed that the machines are unplugged and she is still alive. The doctors rush to her as she sleeps silently. They look at Cyrus [] {Doctor James} Its a Miracle, she's going to be alright! [] The camera looks up at Cyrus as his face turns to total shock as he see's the Golden necklace in her hands. The scene fades out. []
