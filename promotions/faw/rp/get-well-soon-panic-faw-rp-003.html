Get Well Soon! [Panic - FAW RP #003] 'Legendary' Lee Smith Furiously Addictive Wrestling Roleplay #90 Date: 6th December 2002 Location: Conference Room, Miami, Florida He sits sipping his water. It is a few days after FAWs return to TV, the revolution fully underway. On Sunday FAWs new co- owner William Myers, the latest in a long line of owners, and his partner Jason Roberts, announced the formation of the Fatal Alliance. The announcement had catastrophic results as riots broke out. Riot police had to be called to the scene, and arrests were made. The announcement shook the wrestling industry like never before, with rival feds cancelling shows to form a strategy to end FAWs dominance. However under the leadership of Myers and Roberts it seems a strong force will be needed. Earlier on Panic Lee Smith, making his return, hospitalised opponent Xing who was rushed to hospital with several cuts and bruises. The scene is set in a press conference room in Miami, Florida. Lee Smith sits, enjoying a conversation with his escort who sits to his right. To his left is his personal bodyguard, who sits like a stone statue, not daring to move a muscle. Suddenly a door creaks in the corner. A rush of journalists, both foreign and local, enter the room. They shout their questions at Smith. “QUIET!” his escort shouts and rises from his seats with curse words flying like a deadly hawk searching its prey. Silence forms and the escort sits down. He shuffles his papers and speaks. “Many of you have requested interviews with my client, Mr Lee Smith. Myself and my client have come to the conclusion that the best result for all is a press conference. In a moment Mr Smith will give the go ahead for you to ask your questions. Raise your hand and wait until he points at you. Step forward and say your name the company you work for before answering your question. That is all” Smith stands up as every person in the room (except the bodyguard, who remains silent and still) shouts a question at either the escort or Lee. “SHUT UP!” Lee shouts and silence falls once again. “You in the centre there, start us off” A ginger haired man steps forward. “Bruce Spruce, CNN” he pauses and flashes his ID card at Smith. “On Sunday you defeated Xing. Would you say the match was a difficult one or would you prefer to refer to it as a stroll in the park?” Bruce finishes and sits down. “Well to answer your question Mr Spruce, I think we all know the what I’m going to say. The match was a simple stroll in the park. It was a warm up of things to come. Xing possesses absolutely no skill but at his age I have to admit he has some potential. Being trained by the current loser who helps him is not going to help though. So, Xing - if your watching from your hospital bed. Get well soon…Buddy” Lee replies as the majority of the journalists broaden their smiles. Lee sniggers to himself. “Next please” “One more please Mr Smith. Do you feel any guilt about defeating Xing in the way you eventually did?” “Yes, yes I do. I feel very guilty, and if my trainer is watching I’d like to apologise. You taught me to always give 100%, but against Xing, I was pushing 1%. I’m deeply apologetic” Lee responds. “Next please”. A dominant female steps forward. “Lee, do you have any fears going into the F. Alliance tournament and do you think you can win the whole thing?” she asks as several people admire her. “Name please” Lee responds, eyeing up the blonde beauty. “Emma Thompson, Washington Post” she replies, avoiding eye contact with the Living Legend. “Well, Emma, If I may call you that…No I do not have any fears going into the tournament, and why should I? I have basically no competition. Okay so we do have a few talented people in the FAW but I highly doubt any of them would be able to come near to matching my ability in a wrestling match. So, I do think I can come out the winner and win the FAW Heavyweight title. As far as I’m concerned the strap is already around my waist, it just needs polishing a little bit firmer” Lee says, intriguing many. “Next” “Chris Fitzgerald, Miami Monthly” the man says, stepping into then limelight. “Lee, I assume you have seen the card for FAW Panic - 8th December?” “Yes I can confirm that” Lee replies, butting in on Chris who continues. “You are facing Renegade. Have any analysed your opponent and if so what are you results?” Chris says - reading off his notepad. “I have studied him very little. I will wait until I am in the ring to physically assess him. But, as I say, from what I have seen he is of little threat - perhaps a smaller threat than Xing…and we all know what happened to him. Renegade is one of those midcard people that are regularly threatened with the position of resident jobber. Actually, I think I got that the wrong way round. He is a jobber - and as long as Legendary Lee Smith is around…he always will be.” Lee picks up his papers and stands up as the journalists beg for more questions. “We will continue this conference at a later date. Thanks for nothing” Lee finishes and gives his luggage to his escort. He throws the water over his bodyguard who licks it off of his vest. All three of them then exit and the live cameras zoom in on Lee. He whispers very quietly. “Victory is nigh” is all we can make out. FADE
