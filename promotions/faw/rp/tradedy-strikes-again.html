Tradedy strikes again

10/16/02
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Tradedy strikes again Cyrus Raynes Furiously Addictive Wrestling Roleplay #19 Date: 10/16/02 Location: Hospital in Downtown Indianapolis - Tragedy always seems to strike those who least expect it to be coming their way. The blindside shot that a person takes from an un-expected tragedy is greater than anyone can possibly know, even if you have been through one, you can never fully understand one's frame of mind after wards. The pain a person feels can be so great that they literly feel as if they would rather just die then go on living with this in their life. What is it that makes every case different for each person? Why is it that the same exact type of tragedy can happen to two different people, but they will react to totally different ways? Some may say it is because they are just different people but the reality is that's not the real reason for the difference. The biggest reason for the difference comes from some where deep inside a person, burried deep with in their very souls. Some where down there in that eternal spirit of a person breathes the knowledge of how a person will react to a praticular tragedy. The differences can be quite amazing. Let us take a car accident for example. Now for some when a loved one is injuried in an accident, they some how find the way to forgive the person who caused it no matter what. You can add any number of variable into the equation and every single time they will find it in their hearts to forgive and forget, let the past be the past, and what ever old saying you may have heard. One type of person can have their loved one seriously hurt or even killed by a drunk driver and yet they will be the first ones in the court room to ask the judge to show mercy and leniency on the violator. What is it that makes these people so forgiving? Is it their inherited human nature? Is it their up bringing? Is it their Soul or the almighty God in Heaven? Truthfully the answer can be found in a combination of all of them or in any one of them alone. You can never truly pin point why you may or may not act a certain way when tragedy strikes but you can hope and pray that you will be the bigger person in the act. But then there is always the flip side to every coin. The other, darker, less forgiving, deadly side of the coin. That is the side where the other half of the world resides. The side that says they are never able to forgive or forget. The people who can only think about who to blame, how to get revenge, and what they are going to do to make others feel the pain and suffering that they do. When it comes to the ones who are already cold hearted and ruthless in the first place a tragedy of mass proportions can be the thing that unleashes all the un-holy demons that maybe torturing them, so that they may see the light and find the way to forgiveness. Then again... It can also unleash that Un-Holy, evil, completely dark side of a person. The side that is completely void of anything that resembles human concience, compassion, heart or soul. It is this effect that makes a dangerous person...LETHAL! The question that the FAW is about to have to face is.... Which type of person is Cyrus Raynes going to be?- [The out shot opens to the halls of a hospital that is very bright as most hospitals usually are. There are people running around every where as the camera begins to move down the hall toward the entrance to a small room halfway down the right side of the hall way. There are a couple of doctors still in their scrubs standing to the outside of the room talking quietly to eachother. The camera zooms in to get a listen to their conversation.] {Doctor James}: 'I am afraid this is not going to be an easy thing Doctor. This man is really kind of crazy and his wife is not going to pull through. What should I say to him?' {Doctor Davis}: 'There will be no easy way to tell it to him Doctor James. Everyone is understandably upset with news like this. It is only the natural reaction.' {Doctor James}: 'Doctor Davis you don't understand what I mean. This man is already a seriously desturbed man. He is a professional wrestler for the FAW and a darn good one. But he has a very vile meanstreak when he snaps. I am afraid that he will snap on me like he does some of his opponents.' {Doctor Davis}: 'I tell you what, I will go in there with you and help you get through this one. We just have to let him down gently on the news, as gently as one can with news like this.' :::MEAN WHILE::: [In the room sits Cyrus Raynes, on a little stool next to the bed where his valet/wife Reanna lies there with tubes coming from everywhere. The quiet constant beep of the monitors, and the sound of the breathing machine can be heard in the background as Raynes holds her hand tightly and fights to hold back his tears that he doesn't want to have come. Raynes is a proud man and hates to let anyone see any type of emotion that he would consider weakness from him. In this room any number of people could walk in and see it and so he tries to put up that tougher than life exterior he is so well known for. He softly speaks to her as he waits for news from the doctors on her tests.] {Raynes}: 'Honey you need to open your eyes for me. You have to fight like you always do, show the world how tough you are. You can't let those little drunk punks win, your to strong to die from something like this. I know you will pull through, and you know how I know that? You are tough like I am...That is one of the reasons I fell in love with you in the first place, we are so much a like.' [Raynes has to turn his head away for a moment as he begins to get choked up from this. He fights very hard and some how manages to compose himself enough to not let the tears flow. He begins to shake as he fights even harder than before. Finally he looks back over at her with all the tubes sticking out of her body and smiles at her.] {Raynes}: 'You know when you pull out of this, we are going to go and shop for that bigger house that you have been wanting. We are going to go out and find that perfect home with all the closet space you can think of, even a couple of them just for all those shoes you love so much. But you have got to be strong and pull through this for me first, and I know you will. I love you so much I can't bare to think of a day with out you. You are my one thing that keeps something human inside of me.' [Raynes is startled as the two doctors walk back into the room. Raynes stands up and turns to face them. A look of great concern comes over his face as he seems to read the bad news etched in the two doctors faces. He breathes out deeply as they begin to speak to him.] {Doctor James}: 'Mr. Raynes, your wife has suffered some very serious brain trauma from this accident. We have worked on her for several hours and ran all the tests that can possibly be run.' {Raynes}: 'Stop beating around the bush about it and spit it out! Is my wife going to live or not?' {Doctor Davis}: 'Mr. Raynes we did all we could but the damage was so severe. The only thing keeping her alive right now is those machines, and that is the way it always will be. There is no possible chance of her surving or ever coming out of that coma. I am truly sorry.' [Raynes begins to shake uncontrollably instantly from the news he dreaded most. Tears begin to burn his eyes as they fight to the surface to get out of his body. He no longer tries to fight them back now and they begin to stream down his face as his face turns bright red and turns to one of anger. His whole body shakes as he begins to hollar at the two doctors.] {Raynes}: 'YOU TWO ARE SORRY? YOUR SORRY? YOU HAVE NO IDEA WHAT SORRY IS!' {Doctor James}: 'Please Mr. Raynes try and stay calm. It is understandable to be in some pain from this shock, but you must remain calm.' {Raynes}: 'PAIN? PAIN? What the hell would you know about pain? You have no idea what this is like for some one. You are just like everyone else! You walk around with out a care in the world and pretend you know what it is like to be some one else when something happens. You have no idea what this is like you jackass!' [The two doctors stand there and are reasonably fearfull for their safety. As they stand there trying to think of what to say or do, Raynes stops shaking. He looks over at Reanna, for a few moments. When he looks back there are no more tears. His eyes just seem to be totally void of emotion, completely ice cold. He just looks at the doctors and very calmly begins to speak.] {Raynes}: 'You two don't have any idea how this feels...But there are some people who soon will. (He pauses for a few moments) Now please get the hell out of the room. I want time with my wife before I let you shut off these machines.' [The two doctors politely let themselves out of the room as Raynes walks over and slowly sits back down. He looks at Reanna for a few more moments then gently grabs her hand and begins to stroke it. He speaks as the camera begins to fade out of the scene to black.] {Raynes}: 'Don't worry my love...They will know....They will all know!'
