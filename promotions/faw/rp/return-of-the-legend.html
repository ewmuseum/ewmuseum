Return of the Legend Xander Furiously Addictive Wrestling Roleplay #123 Date: 12/26/02 Location: Hotel, lobby, somewhere {The scene opens up on Xander walking around the lobby of a hotel, in which he is staying for the weekend.} 'Damn how some things just seem to stay the same.' Xander starts, and his silence is finally broken after all this time. 'It's been a while, hasen't it? False accusations, and a long drawn out battle later, and look who's back in the mix.' 'Now, I'll go straight to the damned point. A week from this Sunday, it looks like I'm tagged up with The British Bomber, and we're facing The Second Rate One, and Rent-a-gade. Wait... I could swear one of them was dead.' Xander thinks for a second. 'Oh yeah, I remember now, nevermind.' 'Not only is this tag match all set up purdy and clean, but the team that wins, faces each other for a shot at the World title at the next PPV. And, oddly enough, the man with that belt right now, is none other then that bum who could barely even cheat his way to a victory against me, Foamy.' 'Funny how some things are so unbelievable, they're almost comical. Nevermind that though, it'll be a short reign, because after me and Bomber take out Renegade and The Great One, I'll be more then happy to finally have my rematch against Bomber, and show him why I should have been champion all those months ago.' 'This time.. it will be mine... the gold, the glory.. the recognition.. it's mine. It's my turn to shine.' {The scene fades out}
