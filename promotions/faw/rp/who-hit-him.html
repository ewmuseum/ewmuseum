Who hit him? Xing Furiously Addictive Wrestling Roleplay #112 Date: 18th december 2hours after Lee Smith made his! Location: Kentucky shopping streets It is a bright sunny Monday morning in the mid-streets of Kentucky, peace all around us uh oh! Xing’s about and he is I don’t want to say this but he is actually sniffing people obviously for the people very displeasing to be around such a weird and psychotic man. “Shut up!” Xing looks at me with a mean look on his face after he makes that remark to me. But I cannot help I’m the narrator oh well we carry on with Xing in the streets sniffing peoples shoulders and what not. “Where is Lee Smith when I get my hands on him he will perish at my feet. He better watch his back because I’ll get him one way or another!” A big threat made from Xing looking for Lee Smith his biggest rival sniffing every single moving thing in site of him. Looking around he sees a man looking very worried Xing approaches him and sniffs him but a movement of arms behind the young mans head made Xing turn the man around and there was the shock of Xings life. There in that mans hands was a Lee Smith FAW poster. The fury that entered Xings face was terrifying he put on his big up pump with his broad shoulders flexing and the onlookers screaming “Crown him!” and Xing picking up the man about the age 24 looking like a kid next to Xing hurled him through the nearby restaurant. Breaking the glass in an instant with the onlookers happy yet fearful for the next victim. “Now all of you people here that witnessed that you might as well now tell me if you are like him and support that S.O.B. or I’ll find out some how and it will not be pretty.” Silence al around the streets until a bunch of laughs came from a pool bar two places from the crashed restaurant. Xing turns hearing the laughter and enters the pub sniffing the place about then Xing approaches a man with his biker mates playing pool all amazingly taller than he. The men turn at Xing with a confused look on their faces then one biker says, “What’s happening?” To Xing but Xing ignores the man and sniffs his shoulders the man looks not too happy at all and picked Xing up above his head and rammed him against the wall Xing just smiled and took the mans fist and crunched it together bringing the taller man to his knees. The mans mates run off with fear in each one of their eyes. The man on his knees begs for forgiveness and resorts to crying but Xing doesn’t let go until the bar man offers a free drink to Xing for scaring off the gang that causes every one that enters to leave with nothing spent in the bar having just fear in their faces. Xing takes the beer in one hand and the mans head in the other and says while pouring the beer over the mans head, “I hope you enjoy this because this is the last drink you take in here forever!” The bar man shakes Xings hands as the biker runs out wiping his face. The bar man then says “You’re Xing the maestro. Yeah I saw you on Kai’ en Tai get Hai.” Xing replies with a smirk on his face “People actually watch that?” “Oh yeah all the time, Hey isn’t that Lee Smith your ultimate rival walking by?” Xing turns his head to just miss the man that the bar man said was lee Smith, Xing runs out saying, “thank you.” “It can’t be him he is in L.A.X. Now!” The bar man shouts out Xing could hear him and Xing stops his pursuit for a man like Smith and walks back to the bar man and says, “ You serious he is in L.A.X. right now?” “Yeah it was on a tourist channel filming him while he was sleeping this after-noon he was at an airport an hour long filming of him sheesh was that boring. He is heading for Houston in about an hour from now at terminal 13. Quickly I’ll drive you there in no time at all!” “But that was this after-noon hadn’t they of gone by now?” Xing replies getting in to the vehicle with the bar man responding, “There is a 3 hour delay.” The car drives of with photographers following the slowly paced car flashing pictures at Xings smiling face. The car drives off into a petrol station.. They come out from there an drive on to the L.A.X. Airport and 1 hour and a half later they arrive outside the airport and Xing thanks the bar man for his help and the bar man replies, “If you’re in need for a drink you know where to come!” Xing smiles and waves as the car exits the premises. Xing enters the airport heading straight for terminal 13 where he sees the passengers and Lee Smith boarding the plane Xing runs on until he is stopped by a lady selling cookies Xing takes the tray and chucks the cookies. He sees Lee Smith being rude and arrogant to a stewardess telling her to clean his shoes she is given a tray for money roughly the same size as Xings tray by the manager who also is in the midst of Lee Smith who asks for a new drink the manager comes back with a new one also holding a tray. Lee doesn’t like the drink and spits out at his chauffeur who doesn’t look too happy while nicking a tray off an old lady serving her unlicensed drinks the chauffeur tray in hand slings the drinks and still holding the tray escorts Lee Smith to his seat with Xing the Stewardess and the manager not too far behind all with contempt in there eyes. Lee sits and the Chauffeur gets up with the tray held up high including Xing the Stewardess and the Manager all with their trays in hand up high behind Smith’s head…… BBBBBBBAAAAAAANNNNNNNGGGGGGG!!!!!!! Lee heads to the floor with a tray shaped around his head but “WHO HIT LEE SMITH?” Doom doom der der dedle doom! I just had to do that!
