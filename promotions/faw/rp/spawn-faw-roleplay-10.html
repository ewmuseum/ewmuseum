Spawn — FAW Roleplay #10

captured 29 October 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
SpAwN--The Hell Master \~~~~~~~(opening scene) Spawn: Do it again...HARDER! *CRACK* C'MON! Cue the opening shot. DeMitri Gym. Loud, thwapping cracks of a kendo stick could be heard...you just assume Spawn is just wailing away on a punching bag or something similar. What you see though...is quite different. Disturbing to say the least, Spawn was standing tall in the middle of the ring...and a gym patron is repeatedly smashing kendo stick shots onto Spawn's back. You could see Spawn's back was red and raw...several lines starting to bleed down his back, forming a small puddle at Spawn's feet. The sick fuck was roaring out at every crack...but for some reason he wasn't going down. Spawn: Is this the best you fucking got? HARDER! HARDER or I'll start doing this to YOU! The man swinging the stick gulped...and reared back...swinging one more time...a sadistic shot square to the spine, the kendo stick snapped in half..the upper half flying out of the ring with the velocity of the blow. Spawn screamed out and fell to a knee...the small puddle of blood on the canvas of the mat splattering outwards as his knee lands in it. There was silence for a long moment...then a quiet chuckle. A chuckle that grew to an all out laugh. Uncontrollable, disturbing laughter. Spawn's eyes were crazed to say the least as he stood tall...glaring down at the much smaller man with that sick grin of his.. Spawn: Thank you. You did good...you get out of here now... The man wasted no time in dropping the rest of the kendo stick and bolting out of there....you could see snow falling at a nasty looking angle outside...the wind was blowing incredibly hard. Spawn glanced out the window and raised a brow...an idea coming to mind. Spawn: Follow me. Spawn hopped out of the ring...just wearing a pair of shiny black gym pants, no socks, no shirt, no shoes...nothing else. He stepped to the door of the gym and opened it. He stared outside for a moment before snickering...stepping out and beginning to walk down the street. The cameraman was stunned at the action...and actually just stood there a second in shock before running after Spawn...pointing the camera at him from his side as he continues to stalk down the cold, snow-covered street...the blood from Spawn's back leaving a trail of thick, sinful red drips in the pure, virgin white. Spawn: Do you listen to what comes out of your mouth or is it like a fucking traffic light where green means go, red means yes, but somewhere in between there is a constant yellow light asking the question…..do I go now? Or do I stop? You have no idea what you are getting in to; you think the fed you was in was tough? You think that you had a tough time with your income taxes, you have no idea how tough, that tough can actually be. Only a few more days until I go in to that ring and pain the canvas with your innards, only a few days until I finally prove that I can defeat anyone the NGW puts in front of me. You should stop swinging around on poles and start training, you should stop trying to stop your fans from bonding you with furry pink handcuffs and realize what is directly in front of you. You may not see me at first, since shadows often change shapes, you may not be able to tell who or what is pressing that cold steel blade in to your neck, you may not be able to tell who or what is choking the air right out of your lungs, but on Tuesday you will know who I am, you will know the difference between Light and Shadow, you will know the difference between insanity and dementia, you will know who the fuck I am, not who the fuck you THINK I am. Spawn looked down to his left arm...the arm that the needle was removed from...the arm that smashed through a windshield of a car...the arm he sliced open in front of a FAW suit..it was covered in stitches. He raised it up...and then flexed it with a growl...some of the stitches 'oozing' a bit...small lines of blood running down his muscular arm down to his constantly bandages fingers...which is good this time...the cloth absorbs the blood...and only a little bit drops down into the snow. He was a fucking mess....but he loved it. Spawn didn't even seem to notice the cold...or the snow that was getting blown constantly onto his back due to the heavy winds. He just grinned to the camera...his eyes glaring into it as if he could see Vlad on the other end.. Spawn: Why exactly are you so quiet, Vlad. Trip on a phone cord and smash your jaw into a table? Did your girlfriend slapped you around a little too hard? Hmm...perhaps you have some little secret you fear you'll tell if you talk too long. Then...maybe just then I'll understand this stupidity of yours, Vlad. Perhaps you, have something dreadfully twisted planned for the Battle this week after I destroy you. Perhaps you'll try and cripple me with chairs like I did the camera man and your fan...maybe you'll taunt me and try to make afraid of you. Whatever it is...Vlad most likely knows...and since he's such a fucking sped he's afraid of even opening his mouth in fear of spilling the beans. I mean...it's a GOOD plan, ain't it Vlad? *A snicker as his eyes narrowed*...These last few weeks I've suffered the worst pain I've ever experienced...and finally I've realized it. No type of physical pain can surpass the heart-wrenching agony I felt in training for my matches. Nothing can replace that constant pain I feel. That's why this is so easy for me. My back must be so fucking raw it's bleeding...but honestly I don't notice it that much. My arm must be fucking ripped up big time...but...it's not that bad. No painkillers...nah..I don't take the punk way out. The pain I do feel is brief stabs...but nothing lingers...nothing remains. Just you, Vlad. Vlad...Vlad VLAD VLAD! The little fucking splinter that's lodged in my goddamn trachea. the needle broken off in my arm..the rib fragment pressing against my heart. You're the thing that's pushing me further and further to Oblivion. You have nobody else to thank but yourself for this one, All of this painful training all of this bleeding I am doing will be the death of you Vlad. Spawn glared downwards...then back...at all the blood he's trailed...all the blood he's lost. A smirk and a shrug before he looks to the camera. Spawn: You see Vlad, I think of myself as an artist, and the ring is my canvas. Your blood and internal organs are my paint. When I get on to the canvas, when the two of us are together, the artists and his tools….what will be created in that time slot will be beautiful, there will be splashes of crimson joy all over the ring, there will be remnants of chipped white teeth littered across the ring, there will be snot, there will be vomit, this Tuesday is no longer just a match Vlad, it is no longer fun and games Vlad, no, it is the chance I have to show my art work to the world, it is the chance I have to prove to NGW and the world that evil will take over, you Vlad are my slave, my bitch, my personal punching bag. You may think you are better than me, you may think that you are smarter than me, but….when you step in to that ring, when you stand toe to toe with me, you will realize what you have gotten in to, but by then, it will be far too late to do anything about it. That smug, smirking grin had turned into something of a scowl...those intense blue eyes glaring into the camera. Spawn: Vlad...I really do hope you say something else soon...I don't want this to end quite yet. There's only a couple more days left. I want to hear your most brutal, scathing comments. If you can muster up the courage to do so, I'd appreciate a fucking reply. All you're doing by not talking about me is pissing me off more than I already am. Not something I think you want to do, right? I'm sure. Really Vlad...I'm being dead serious when I say this. Do not play this game with me. I REALLY don't like it when my opponent sits in a fucking corner doing nothing. Get your ass a cameraman that isn't busy laughing at you...sit your ass down somewhere, and cut a fucking real man promo. I don't care if it's two words, I don't care if you pull a Chris Cunningham and walk your ass down to the ring and scratch your balls..I DON'T CARE. Not only are you making yourself look bad...you're making me look bad. *Spawn pause* I got it. If I wake up tomorrow morning...and I find out you haven't at least done *something* productive towards our match. I'm going to find you where you live and beat you like a little girl that you are. *A grin*. Call it incentive. You aren't...serious are you? Spawn: Well, I'm going to beat him like a bitch on Tuesday...I might as well bump the date up a little...the outcome won't be any different. Heh. For now though...I'm gonna head back to the gym. Spawn had to have been outside for at least five minutes now without a shirt or shoes on...the blood had stopped for the most part. He turned around...and casually strolled back to the gym...stepping inside and groaning slightly...stretching his body out.. Spawn: Extreme cold to extreme heat...a bitch on the body. Vlad...I'm gonna end it on this. I've stated on numerous occasions now that you're nothing but a new rookie punk. A lackey. A sheep... You go on and talk..well..you don't. You sat down ONCE and talked shit to me...ONCE. Here I am just grinning Vlad...because I know you better than you do..or at least...better than you'd like to admit...What makes you think YOU can do it with a single fucking steel chair in the ring or with the little bit of mind you have... I am not going to waste my breath trying to explain to you how fucked you are this Tuesday. Honestly, I could care less Remember that...and remember that I *have* fallen. I have fallen and my name brings about YOUR DESTRUCTION. Spawntook a couple steps towards the camera...glaring eyes locked onto the lens...the scene shook for just a moment before it cut to black...once again the NGW logo shattered....replaced by the rotating skull symbol with the S through it....
