Full Speed Ahead (Panic #002) 'Legendary' Lee Smith Furiously Addictive Wrestling Roleplay #82 Date: 29th December 2002 Location: Heliport, Switzerland “Mr Smith, you’re going to be late!” the polished pilot said as he chewed through his lunch with his hand on the joystick. “Calm down, I’m here” Lee shouted as a huge eruption began when the pilot hit his joystick. “Time for take off - get in your seat” the pilot said as the chopper flew into the air. It was Legendary Lee Smiths private helicopter - the pilot was indeed exclusive to him as well. Lee rushed to his seat and put some headphones on. He buttoned up his jacket and then reached into his pocket and took out a wad of cash. He separated some notes from the rest of the park and scrunched them up in a ball. He squeezed the paper balls tight and tucked them in his ear to avoid being driven to insanity by the thrusting noise of the blades on the helicopter. Suddenly they were up in the air. Lee looked down at Lake Geneva below- and at the heliport they had just taken off from which was on top of a skyscraper. The Legend took out his binoculars and zoomed in on the land below. He looked at the docks below and watched as a mysterious female walked along the path with her shopping bags. “Descend, bitch!” he shouted at his pilot who failed to hear him. “What Sir?” he replied, shouting on the top of his voice. “They say there are plenty of fish in the sea - I’ve just found one of them” Lee screams. The pilot still fails to hear him and presses a button which injects a new layer of window padding which makes sure that they cannot hear any sound from outside. “Why didn’t you do that earlier!?” Lee shouts and begins laughing. The pilot grins and turns back to controlling the craft which is now floating miles above sea level. Lee switches on a camera which is next to him and speaks into it. “Xing, I hope you’re watching this. Like my helicopter? You struggled to get a toy one when you were younger. Every Christmas a new craze sweeps the world. Whether it be a childrens craze, an escaped insane man or even a new currency. Well, this year something completely different is happening. Let me take you back a few years. The helicopter was perhaps the latest gadget. Anyway, as I was saying this year we have a new craze. His name? Well its yours truly, Lee Smith!” Lee stops momentarily to see the sun setting. “Let me make this short, quick and so simple. When we meet Xing, legends will be enhanced, dreams shattered. And most of all - the latest worldwide craze on the block will show you just why he graduated from the Keening School of Grappling Arts - just why he is a GSW Grandslam Champion. So, if you still don’t understand I’ll sum it up. I’m going to go CRRRAZZYYYY ON….YOU!” Lee turns to his pilot and shouts. “FULL SPEEED AHEAAAADDDD”. The helicopter blades rumble as it goes flying through the sky like a bullet from a gun. Bring it On.
