The Dome #1 Rzarector Furiously Addictive Wrestling Roleplay #103 Date: Dec 17 Location: In the house of Rzarector, haunted The time was dark and very ancient. Several memories were remembered about her, the war, and his family. The fact that he lived in this gruesome times was almost to the point of insanity. Yet times have changed, as he expected they would. His love, left him, the war ended, and his family was dead. He had survived though. He the one who was the strongest souled out of all of them. The time was then, but the future is now. The time was Armageddon on Earth. Now he floats with the angels. (The year was 2097, the time about 6:07. Rzarector had awoke from bed with the outside of his windows lit red. This was perfectly normal until he looked at his alarm clock. It wasn't morning time, it was still 2:30 in the morning. The sky was completely red. Being shocked he jumped out of his bed and opened the window to see terrible visions. He saw hundreds of dead bodies laying all over the roads, cars crashed into each other, and his love who was next to him in bed was gone. Rzarector searched throughout the whole house looking for her. She was nowhere. Nor would she ever be again. She was gone with God. Rzarector was speechless, for he knew it. He fell to his knees crying and praying to God to forgive him for what he had done. Since the day Rzarector left the wrestling business, he has been a sinner. Several prositutes, drugs, and alcohol ran through his mind constantly. He would never be the same again. The tortorous times that are now among him, he decided to walk outside. As he opened the front door a cat meowed and ran into the house .There he walked out to his front step almost sliding on the blood splattered on the steps. He stepped over bodies, and ran through the streets knowing noone else was around. Then it hit him, this is Armageddon. I have never beleived this would happen in my time, yet God has intended it to, and now it has come. I don't know what to think. As Rzarector sat there motionless in the middle of the street he heard a honk. At the first sign of life on this deserted planet, he quickly ran to the sound. There he saw a totalled red ford truck, and honking the horn from the inside a young, yet beautiful lady. He ran up and started talking.) Rzarector: Are you ok in there? Woman: Well could you get me out please? I don't know what happened, I was driving down the road when I slammed into that blue taurus over there. (As Rzarector started unhinging the door he looked over at the blue taurus. There he saw noone driving it, he had wondered what had happened to it. There was no trace, Rzarector was confused. The door eventually snapped off and the short red head woman leaped out.) Rzarector: Was anyone driving that car? Woman: What do you mean? Rzarector: Did you see anyone jump out of it? Woman: No, why, isn't there anyone in it? We just crashed a secodn ago. The sky turned red, and everything just went to hell. Rzarector: You got that right, things are going to turn to hell. And no there isn't anyone inside the car. Do you know where they might be at? Woman: No, where? Do you? Rzarector: They are in heaven with God, and we are left on Earth. Woman: What, your crazy. God doesn't exist. This is 2097, Science has already confirmed he doesn't exist. Rzarector: To the common eye, no he doesn't. To the wonders of science, no he doesn't. But deep down in our hearts God is everywhere. You must remember that forever. God took all these people to a better place. I wasn't good enough, nor shall I ever be. I'm destined to be back in hell where I truly belong. Woman: Oh my god, so what are you saying? The end of the Earth is now? Rzarector: Yes. (The woman started screaming as she ran off down the street yelling over the dead bodies. She eventually got out of sight as Rzarector gave a loud hiss. There he just kept walking on until he hit a payphone. He had just the right amount of change as he made a phone call to his relative. They were shocked to hear from him.) *Over the phone.* Relative: Hello. Rzarector: Hello Aunt Jesabelle. Jessabelle: Oh, thank God your alive. Where have the others gone? Has God told you anything. Rzarector: Aunt, you know God hasn't talked to me in years. I have betrayed him, he shouldn't have any love left for me. Jessabelle: Oh Rzarector thats where your wrong. He loves you, but you have stopped loving yourself. Cheer up, now where is everyone? (Rzarector hisses as Jessabelle clears her throat.) Rzarector: You never were afraid of me. All of these people are in heaven, all of these dead bodies are from the damage caused by them leaving. God is leading an army now to Earth to kill the monster. All we can do now is try to find the monster. Maybe we can stop him somehow. Jessabelle: I'm so scared, all of our family is gone, some people are roaming the streets, yet they are very violent. I think I just heard something in the kitchen. I'll be right back. Rzarector: Ok. (As Jessabelle laid down the phone a gunshot was heard. Right before the sound of the gun was made Jessabelle screamed. Rzarector instantly hisses as he heard footsteps rummaging through her house. Rzarector ran out of his house and towards Jessabelle. Only one problem, Jessabelle lived 10 miles away. Rzarector kept running....he heard more gunshots in the streets. More car and home alarms go off. He saw police cars flipped over, everything was a wreck with the demons of Earth running it. And for once in his life, he wished for order back. He wished he had his old life back. After 30 minutes, he finally reached his aunts house. He ran inside to find her dead on the floor, and a gunshot through the front of her face. Rzarector fell down to his knees and cried over his lost aunt. The tears evaporated in thin air. More lightning was heard outside, as the red picked up. A huge light then surfaced over the lands of the world, and God had finally arrived. He came to Rzarector.) God: Rzarector, you have sinned, and haven't repented in so long. You are not the holiest, but you show great love for everyone around you. Please take my hand and come with me. Rzarector: Yes master. (Rzarector grabbed his hand as God turned towards the sky and flew away with him. Suddenly Rzarector awoke to sweat, tears, and his heart racing beats. He jumped out of bed and ran to the window to see everything was normal in 2002. The sky blue, no lightning, no dead bodies, and everyone was driving down the road. Rzarector hissed and smiled as he begin praying.) *My life is very short. All that I've done in the past, is past. I need to change, I need to capitilize every situation I'm ever in. Whether it is with God's word, or if it is simple as my love. I have no love as of yet, or no real family besides Jessabelle. Yet there is something more in life, there always is. God, your still watching down on me, and I am very thankful of that. Please be forever with me. For you are my saviour, you are my light, I love you with all my life. Please one day forgive me like you have always done, and let me take your hand from the beginning. Amen.* (After Rzarector prayed he hissed some more as he through on his black robe. There he went downstairs to the fridge and grabbed some poptarts. He ate them very quickly and went into his living room to his camera set. There he would record a segment for the FAW. He was still in wrestling, very glad for it. He walked over to the camera, and flipped record. Then he went back and sat down on his couch shaped like a cross, and began to talk.) Rzarector: The dome, 8 men, trapped inside a little cage. What kind of mayhem is this? Your going to throw me in a cage with Spawn, The Great One, British Bomber, and the 9th Wonder of Foam? This is craziness. These are all legends in the FAW. How could this have ever of happened. I mean Renegade and Lee Smith? Don't joke me to much, those can't be to much to stop me in my path. I mean, I'm not capitilizing on everything that I have ever done. And now I am going to rise to the top of the FAW by no means necessary. You look at this hideous christianic face, and you think of two things, God and his Helper. See I have came to Earth to stop sin. And since every one of my competitors sins, then I guess that means you all are going to be demolished. Like for example..... (Rzarector hisses as his blue eyes shine out of his black robe.) Rzarector: 9th Wonder of Foam, what kind of joke is this? You are a joke. You sit there and make the stupidest stuff I have ever seen in my life. Planet of the Apes? Star Wars? Evil Foam World Order? How original is that? Anyways, when I get you in that ring, I have beaten you before, I have beaten Spawn and the Great one before. So why should I fear either one of you three? And as for Renegade, he is a very talented competitor. Yet for his sake, I Say he keep his talent outside of the ring. Maybe a commentator or something. (Rzarector looks more into the camera as his eyes turn green. Suddenly he gets a vision from God in his head. God begins to speak.) *Rzarector, now the dream I sent you was a message. Not a message to be cocky about your opponents, a message to see your true self. I never hold you back, you should not worry about being greedy over the title. I trust that you won't be. Win the titles, make your life happy, or you will fall again. For if you desire something get it. Or else you will fall. Trust me, I make the rules. I Will trust you to follow them.* (Rzarector looks shocked as he looks back at the camera.) Rzarector: I'm sorry 9th. I never meant to be that cocky. You are a very good competitor. You really are, so is the rest of the people in the Dome match. Everyone, including Renegade. He should always be a wrestler. I'm a good competitor in my own opinion. And all this match is going to be is competition. It is going to be very funny to see how the outcome finishes. Hopefully, I will be the last one standing. Yet all would work well if Spawn, The Great One, Bomber, and 9th would get eliminated first. Then I can match up to people of my caliber. I'm not main event status, nor will I ever be main event. Yet it is getting obvious to me, that more and more of my hardwork eventually pays off. Putting me in matches like this one. That are truly unbeleivable. The president of the FAW couldn't of done any better in boosting the ratings than putting people like Foam and Bomber in these matches. (Rzarector starts to talk again but the phone begins to ring. Rzarector hisses as he picks it up.) *On the phone.* Rzarector: Hello. Mystery Guy: Hey man, whats up? Rzarector: *hissing.* Why have you called during this hour of the day? Mystery Guy: It is like 12 noon. Rzarector: Yet that is to early. Still your lucky it is Tuesday. I am usually in church. Mystery Guy: I know that man, I know. Anyways, I was wondering, whats up with you and the FAW? Rzarector: I am very happy with the FAW. Mystery Guy: Well the offer still stands, if you want to come to &% Wrestling, then go ahead and message me. K? Rzarector: The offer still stands, yet I am not interested. Mystery Guy: K, later. Rzarector: Bye. (Rzarector hangs up the phone as he looks back at the camera.) Rzarector: After the FAW pushing this Dome match, I have been getting alot of offers from other federations. But I stay true to my home. The FAW is the best wrestling out there, now and forever. (Rzarector flashes his white fangs as he speaks his final words.) Rzarector: This Sunday is Evil Intentions. The dome match is upon us. I sit here to tell all of you the best of luck. For we all will need it. Most of us won't even wrestle the next Sunday due to injuries. Yet we will hopefully all make it out alive. Then we can rejoice in celebration afterwards out our exciting match we just had. The best of luck to everyone, including you Renegade. (Rzarector hisses as his blue eyes flash. He then walks over and cuts off the camera.)
