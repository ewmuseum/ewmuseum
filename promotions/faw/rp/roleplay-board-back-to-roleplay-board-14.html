Furiously Addictive Wrestling Roleplay Board Roleplay Board | Back to Roleplay Board Nightmare King Entitled: Mass Murderers Breakdown 1 Written: August 6, 2002 Location: In the MM living room [Recorded live at the Mass Murderers Trailor on Red Armor Road in DC. Captioning is possible. English only, they don't speak Spanish bitch. Censoring was taking place, but we could only get all of it we knew of.] [The screen opens to a dreary picture of red and black. The camera then starts zooming in to show a match lighting. It zooms out to show Nightmare King lighting up a cigarette laying on a green couch. On the other side of the room is Black Ghost laying on a red and black couch. Black Ghost is staring a the pictures of demons painted on the ceilings and Nightmare King the same. Nightmare King takes one puff off of his cigarette then begins to talk.] Nightmare King: You know I was thinking. Black Ghost: [Waking up from his relaxation] Yeah? Nightmare King: I was thinking that what if the road doesn't lead the way we want it to. What is going to happen in the future. It is all unknown, isn't that strange? Black Ghost: Everyone has a fate. You know it has to be true. If God can tell us in the bible what will happen at the end of the Earth, I'm sure he knows what is going to happen for everyone on this planet. The same for us. Nightmare King: [taking another hit]: Yeah, but why don't we just all have a list that tells us what we're supposed to do? Our mistakes, our problems. We could solve all of that. Black Ghost: See listen, there is things that must happen. There is Satans side and God's side. We have basically chosen Satan's side over everything. Like we want to seriously dismantle peoples bodies with butcher knifes in that wrestling ring. Nightmare King: Yeah Black Ghost: So we could just go in there and be the next Bret Hart, when he was good, but we chose to be the crazy ass serial killers. Nightmare King: Yeah I guess we did. [Chuckles] Nightmare King: You know what? I was watching the rerun of Breakdown, adn the announcers said an impressive win for us. It's like everything is going perfect. Black Ghost: You know the sayin though, Life moves quick, if you don't stop and look around every now and then. You could miss it. Nightmare King: Well, I'll be fucked if I miss it. I love life, I love living. After that damn medication they had me on, I think I'll start mating with damn butterflies or some shit. Black Ghost: Calm down bitch. [Nightmare King takes the last puff of his cigarette and puts it out. He is smoking a black and red pack.] Black Ghost: Anyways, we got a match against that Cape Crusader himself....... SHENSUE YOGASHI!!!! Thats right, I guess FAW was going to give him a second chance. I mean we did destroy him and his partner on Saturday but I guess we'll have to do it again. Nightmare King: Then you got a match for that Damn Hardcore title. Black Ghost: Yeah I thought I would go ahead and claim singles gold real quick. Know what I'm sayin? It sounds easy enough. Anyways, look at my competition, SHIT! Nightmare King: Thats right, there like wart with no puss. Now who would have sex with a woman with warts, but no puss? Black Ghost: I mean the fuck...come on right? [They laugh as they turn around to the cameras and smile knowing that Neil is oging to read this and shit his pants. :)] Nightmare King: You know president or whatever he is, Neil Peters? I bet he looks at us and see's only one thing, rebels. People he despises and wants to die. People that he could care less about and won't quit til he gets his greedy little hands all over our money. Well fuck that, we're not for the damn money, and on another hand, we may not be one of those people acting like they're ratings BULLSHIT! The only way to get ratings is fight Xander? Well shiitttt! We'll show them ratings. Black Ghost: Yeah, I'm from the House of Mirrors. Where there is no way out bitch. Shensui you will feel our pain this Saturday. Again and again and again. Nightmare King: Time will come, when that clock finally stops its tock, and coldness feels your arms as your laying there breathless waiting for that 3 minutes to pass, so you can move on to HELL! CUZ YOU WERE MURDERED!!!! [Nightmare King laugs as he begins to light up another cigarette. Black Ghost just sits there like SHIT WHAT???? The camera fades to a little mouse eating a thing of cheesse in the damn corner of that piece of shit trailor.]
