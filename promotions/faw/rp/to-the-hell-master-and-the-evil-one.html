To the Hell Master and the Evil One

12/20/2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
To the Hell Master and the Evil One Rzarector Furiously Addictive Wrestling Roleplay #110 Date: 12/20/2002 Location: In a park (The scene opens to show a national park somewhere in New York. The scene is very pretty. The sun is shining bright, the clouds are pure white, and there is a slight warm breeze. Birds are heard chirping throughout the park, as several tourists are walking around in there shorts, and there white colored shirts. Some joggers are seen coming through the park clocking there speed, while others are there with their family playing football, basketball, and frisbee. Everything seems good in the park. More families are heard laughing as the camera zooms over some benches. Then they get to the last bench, this place is very dark. A very dark evil prescense is felt. The kids are running from him and screaming at him. For the man in the bench is wearing a black hooded robe. His green eyes, and white fangs are shining out of the black hood like a candle in the dark. The camera zooms back to the other side of the benches and laughter is yet heard again. One kid tosses his frisbee to his dad, who misses it, the kid says he'll find it. Little does he know, the frisbee landed at Rzarector's feet. The kid runs up and screams as he looks shocked.) Rzarector: (Hisses very loudly.) Why are you so afraid of me? What have I ever done to you? Kid: I don't know, you just look so scary. You look like the devil. Rzarector: Well kid, I'm far from it. I'm a prophet of God. He sent me to Earth to punish the wicked. Have you ever been saved? Kid: No, I haven't. Rzarector: Well see, that is my purpose, to see and destroy the skulls of devils. Now kid, your lucky right now, because if you were older........ (Rzarector pauses, and hisses once again as he opens his mouth to show his fangs.) Rzarector: Because if you were older, I would rip your body to shreds of flesh. (The kid screams as Rzarector starts hissing. The kid rapidly grabs his frisbee as he runs back off to his parents crying. Rzarector's green eyes shine brightly now as he awaits the arrival of James Harris. Rzarector thinks back of James Harris, when all of a sudden he hears a message from God.) *Rzarector, you should not treat kids like that. They are to young to know better.* Rzarector: Well that kid did know better. He was ten. I was out slaying lambs when I was 10. *I know Rza, but sometimes you got to think. Time will get to him. If you scare him, and then he thinks your connected to me, then he will be scared of me.* Rzarector: I thought that is what you wanted, for people to fear you. *That is what I wanted, but not that way. I want people to fear me for my powers. Not because I can make little children cry.* Rzarector: Yes God. I have learned my lesson now. Do you remember back in the old days when there was nothing better to do, but sit around and raid villages? Remember the first time you talked to me? *Yes I do, you were sitting in hell. Screaming, rotting, and crying because of all the pain you were going through at the moment. It is one of those things I never want to see of my children, yet alot just don't beleif anymore.* Rzarector: Yes I know, see for once in my life. When I was in hell, I Figured I had learned my lesson. And you knew it didn't you? You knew that I had learned my lesson. Because the burning everyday, it just was to bad of a punishment. You really are forgiving. *Yes I am.* Rzarector: Well, you know what I hate? *What?* Rzarector: I hate those Jehovah Witnesses that come to your door and annoy you. You know what I mean? *Yes I do know what you mean. Everyone has there own religion, what is right, and what is wrong I cannot tell you. You must study religion, and then you will be able to tell which one is right, and which one is wrong. That way, you can make it into the golden walls of Heaven.* Rzarector: That sounds perfect, thankyou God. Anyways, I see James Harris coming up the road, so I'm going to have to respond to the comments made by Foamy and Spawn. If you will excuse me? *Yes, have a great day. I might talk to you again, just to see how people are acting. Keep spreading my message, I love you son, and I will always be with you. Bye now.* Rzarector: Bye God. (Rzarector looks around and hisses in excitement as he just talked to God. Out of the corner of his eye he see's a huge gang coming towards him. He slowly turns around as he see's the camera crew and James Harris stumbling towards him. James Harris catches a glimpse of the green eyes and gets frozen in his tracks. Rzarector hisses as James Harris, wearing a blue tuxedo with yellow undershirt, walks up to him and attempts to shake his hand.) James Harris: Hi Rzarector, I have so much to talk to you about. These past couple of days have been hektik. (Rzarector does not shake his hand. Rza just puts his head down and opens his mouth to reveal his fangs.) Rzarector: Yes it has. James Harris: Well first off, what is your comments on the departure of Xander and Scott Costella from the FAW? Rzarector: I never really knew either one of them. I remember Xander being very full of himself. I remember him saying things like 'You want to be me.' Just several things like that. He didn't seem like a person you would really want to talk to. He could wrestle though, that is a fact. James Harris: What about Scott Costella? Rzarector: Well look at the facts. When he was Prodigy, he won the tag titles. Then it turned out that Scott Costella is the real prodigy. Now that can get very confusing. What I don't really understand is the fact that he is leaving. Yet he never accomplished anything in the FAW. The one thing that stands out from Scott Costella that I remember is when him and Renegade turned on me. That is about it. So towards Xander, I Think I would give him a 3 out of 10 rating. I would give Costella a 0 out of 10 ranking. If either one of these two want to come back and answer my words, so be it. I'll be waiting here, yet I don't think they will ever come back. James Harris: Why do you say that? Rzarector: Rumour has it that they have both signed with another wrestling federation. They are just making up excuses to get there release. (James Harris looks shocked as Rzarector hisses out loud.) James Harris: How would you know something like that? Rzarector: Maybe I was asked to join the federation to? Maybe they told me that if I signed, I would be a big star. James Harris: Well what did you say? Rzarector: I said no, I'm happy with the FAW right now. I work very hard, and then they reward me with main event title shots. See that is what I like in a federation, ya know? I train, I'm on TV as much as possible, and I also keep the backstage area nice. Except for all the evil ones backstage who hate me. They even burn crosses on my door. Those are the people I will see in burning in hell after they die. Because I will make sure I ressurect them to the lake of fire. That you can promise. (James Harris smiles as he see's that Rzarector is still going to stay with the FAW. Mr. Harris then starts to notice something. He looks around and see's nobody around. It is still daylight, yet there is not one person within 1 mile of Rzarector. James Harris is amazed by this.) James Harris: Do you notice that there is not one person around you within a mile? That is amazing, how is that possible? Did you do that? Rzarector: Yes I did. (James Harris looks really excited.) James Harris: Well how? Rzarector: I told a kid if he was older I would rip his body into shreds of flesh. See how nice i am? James Harris: Yes I do, Rzarector, do you have any love at all left in your heart? If you don't want to answer that, you don't have to. It will not affect me, we have to be getting back to the point of this interview shortly anyways. (Rzarector hisses loudly as his eyes start turning a reddish-green color.) Rzarector: I'll tell you a secret James. Once I had all the love in the world. It all vanished though. See I loved this girl with all my heart. She told me that she loved me with all our heart and that one day we would be married. It turns out that I lost my virginity to her. So I sat around her house all day and we talked for hours upon hours. It never got boring James. Never. But this was in my wicked stage. And see, this girl was wicked. For she had been in so much trouble she was going to get sent off. James Harris: That is bad, tell me more. (A black tear runs down Rzarector eyes.) Rzarector: James, this is a very heart stabbing moment. You never knew of anything that meant more to me than her. She will always mean more to me. Yet anyways, she got sent off, and about 2 weeks after getting sent off I got some very bad news. Her friends told me she had cheated on me. So I went crazy, I wrote her a note through the mail cussing her out, and calling her slut and whore and all these vulgar names. Yet I never got a letter back. It drove me crazy. In my notebook's I had for school I had several papers with notes for her, yet I never could write them. I felt so bad, my one true love has deserted me. How is this possible? It was truly a change in my life. (James Harris is crying.) James Harris: How was it a change in your life? What happened when she came back? Rzarector: Well I'll first start whenever she came back. This was very tragic thing. She came back and called me. She was crying wanting to talk to me. I felt so bad, this girl I put all these tears into. This girl meant everything to me, she still does mean everything to me. God knows how I want her. But I can't have her. She is not mine for the taking. She is my friends girlfriend, Erics girlfriend, Brandons girlfriend, and whoever else. I would be just another guy in the mix. I Got to learn that she has to be mine, not everyone elses. That is the crazy thing about it. So she called me, and I talked to her. And eventually met up with her. She had not changed, yet I have. People just don't understand once you feel so much pain from one person, you withdraw yourself from those people. So we still kicked it off and everything, everything was going perfect. Then I ended it. Right there. James Harris: Why? Rzarector: I wasn't going to feel the same pain a second time. I saw through it this time. She was still having sex with my friend. And I knew it wouldn't work. If she really loved me this much, why would she be having sex with him? It doesn't make sense. So now I know I will never have her again. It drives a stake through my heart, yet it is a breath of releif. No more pain. That is how I like it, the less the pain the better I feel. James Harris: So where is she at now? Rzarector: Well she is still with my friend. I haven't seen her in a long time, but how I want to see her. I'll die in love, and I'll die in pain and heartbreak. For as a once great man said, 'The world dealt me a healthy hand of pain and lies, and you can see the hate in my eyes, its no suprise.' That exactely describes how I changed. (James Harris wipes away the tears and blows his nose as Rzarector is breathing heavy. He is not crying except for that one black tear. He then hisses the loudest you have ever seen him hiss, in Rzarector(God's Eyes), he see's the sun turn red. James Harris still see's a yellow sun. Rzarector see's the clouds turn black, and the sky turn the color of the brighest star. Rzarector keep's hissing as the park's tree's look like they've been dead for years. The park's green grass is now brown dead grass. No more people playing, no more happy families, all that is left is the sorrow of death. That is where Rzarector keeps it over this girl.) James Harris: Rzarector are you ok? Rzarector: I'm fine. James Harris: Since we are about out of time, you mind if I get back to the point, if I even can remember where that was? Rzarector: Go ahead. James Harris: OK, you were the first one to speak. But over the past couple of days, we have had 3 people speak aloud. These people were Foamy, Spawn, and Lee Smith. Now I'm just going to ask you if you have seen there promos. Rzarector: Yes, all of them. James Harris: Well lets first start with Lee Smith, what do you think about his little statements? Rzarector: What statements? He didn't do anything but show video clippage from the past. Then talk about his violent mood swings and how people are going to see his anger. I say that this Legendary status isn't so legendary. I mean, I don't claim I'm higher than Officer McGruff. Because I'm not. We are all equal, yet you have to have heart. In my own opinion I do not think that Lee Smith has the heart that he once had. Because some lose it without ever knowing it. Lee Smith is one of those. James Harris; You have a good way with words Rzarector, you are very happy today? Rzarector: yes I am. I have talked to God, and he told me good news. He told me he is happy with me and for me to keep spreading the word. (James Harris smiles.) James harris: Now after watching Foamy's promos, he had some stuff to say. You have seen those like you said, so now, I think that you are going to say what is on your mind. Rzarector: You know he was absolutely right, I do fight for God. That is the main point. And Foamy says my God has commandments and rules, like I shall not murder. Yet Foamy will Murder. Maybe Foamy never really understood me. I've been sent to Earth to seek the skulls of devils. And Foamy, since you have no God, in my book you are a devil. So I'm coming for your skull this Sunday at Evil Intentions. So yet I'm just a piece of crap that you are going to step over. You are a great athlete Foamy. You said you turned evil because you never got anywhere being good. Well look at Bret Hart, Hulk Hogan, and Shawn Michaels. They get everywhere from being good. Unlike the list of evil people you presented. Then again, I guess those people I mentioned actually had what it took to be a champion. Foamy, I'm coming for your skull. (James Harris mouth drops as he thinks that a murder is actually going to take place at Evil Intentions.) James Harris: Are you really going to murder Foamy? Rzarector: Yes. (James Harris looks frightened as Rzarector thinks in his mind, 'Mr. Harris is very stupid.) James Harris: Well what you think about Spawn's latest statements about everything. That should do us up for today because noone else has answered. So anyways, Spawn, talk on him. The 'Hell Master.' I know you don' t like that to much. Rzarector: No I don't like that very much. You know Spawn, you may be the hell master, but I've been in hell. I've seen the souls burning and the maggots eating there flesh. If you have really been there then you would never want to go back. You speak of what you have no knowledge of. I have beaten you, and no I have not been on alot of drugs. I may be a rookie, but I'm a competitor that you are going to have to deal with. Because you look over all of these people, and just go for the great one. Then You are going to find yourself in alot of trouble. Because some of these people you look over, like Foamy, and the British Bomber, they will defeat you. Because they have made there name here. I've just been a tag team champion, yet hopefully someday I will be more. I hope your convinced Spawn. I never said I would ever be the world champion leaving the arena on Sunday night. Sometimes I talk without thinking, God always calms me down though. So I know your a great competitor Spawn, and the best will come out. I could sit here and hope it was me, yet then again, I could hope it was the person with the most heart. Spawn, you don't have any heart anymore, your as bad as Lee Smith. With your anger mood swings, all you do is cuss and say God's name in vein. That makes a big man out of you doesn't it. Coming this Sunday, I'm going to smack you in front of the world for dissing my God. James Harris: Well that looks like its a wrap. Rzarector: Thankyou James. James Harris: Join us live this Sunday, to see all 8 of these men back up there mouths. Only 89.99. (Rzarector hisses out loud as James gets up and begins getting his equipment ready. Rzarector keeps hissing as the scene starts changing on him again. The Red sun, the black clouds with the sky the color of the brighest star. Everything is dead. Everything was dead. Rzarector is losing his sense of reality as God gives him one more message.) *Rzarector, you must not let your pain get to you. The love of many, doesn't make up the love in your heart. The world has backstabbed you, don't hate, just move on.* Rzarector: But it is so hard. *I know.* (Rzarector closes his eyes and opens them to see everything normal. James Harris is seen walking off with the camera crew.)
