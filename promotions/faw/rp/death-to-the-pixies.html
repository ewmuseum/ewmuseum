Death To The Pixies

19/10/02
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Death To The Pixies 9th Wonder Of Foam Furiously Addictive Wrestling Roleplay #26 Date: 19/10/02 Location: Miami, Florida .... Miami University .... (The 9th Wonder Of Foam stands outside a dormroom, Melissa Farrow & Lars are with him) Foamy: Ok people here we are, Miami Florida, just a stones throw away from The American Airlines Arena where from Distortion will come from tomorrow evening. Melissa: So why are you here at the University of Miami? Is to to use their training facilities? Foamy: No I have that at the hotel, I am here to dish out some justice to the little shit that has been trying to hack my computer from this very Insititution. Melissa: What? You've come here to get revenge on a small child for hacking your PC? I thought we were coming here to film you train. Foamy: Well I tricked you Melissa. Someone tried to break through my firewall, probably to see what I was up to before I had done it, but they left their ISP address, and using Norton Internet Security I managed to trace that little bitch to the Miami University, room 356. Melissa: So what now? Foamy: Now I'm going to show him what happens when he f**ks a stranger in the ass! John Goodman style, if you care to follow me. (Foamy bursts through the main door, he encounters another film crew, Jason Biggs is standing there) Foamy: Out of my way! Jason: Hey I'm Jason Biggs and I'm filming next summers smash teen movie! Foamy: Out of my way, what are you like thirty-eight or something? (Foamy bursts past him, Jason then takes off his wig revealing a head bereft of hair) Jason: thirty- six actually. .... Room 356 .... (A 19 year old boy name Orwell sits at his computer looking at the FAW website) .... Outside .... (Foamy comes up to room number 356, there is a Canadian flag on the door) Foamy: Ah easier than I ever expected. (Foamy breaks the door down with his foot) Foamy: Hello moose-f**ker! Orwell: What the f**k? (Foamy spots the Damien Ryan t-shirt that Orwell has on his body, he also spots a replica FAW Championship Belt on the wall) Orwell: Keep looking boy, that's something you'll never have. (Foamy lunges at Orwell and slaps him across the head knocking him to the ground) Foamy: I see, now I see the game your playing. Thinking that if you hack my computer that I'll be unable to kick Damien Ryan's moose loving ass back to the homeland? Do I look like a computer controlled automaton? Orwell: No I was just trying to put you off! Foamy: Huh? Just because you go to Extreme measures to defeat my computer that means your hero can break me? (Foamy grabs Orwell by the hair and drags him to the ground, Foamy smashes the studnet's computer into many bits, he then slaps Orwell about some more before getting him in a headlock and sitting on the bed) Foamy: Ok Melissa, now is a good a time as any to interview me. Melissa: Sure? Foamy: Oh yes. Melissa: Ok Foamy Damien Ryan is the hardcore champion he has survived many beatings and given out a number of them himself too. Foamy: Yes. I agree. Orwell: See Damien Ryan rules! (Foamy tightens his grip) Foamy: Sure he has dished out hardcore violence and sure he is undefeated but one day he is going to be crying alone in his hotel room because his unbeaten run came to and end. Orwell: Never! (foamy tightens yet again) Foamy: See Orwell you don't know when to shut up. You don't know when to stop struggling and stop, then think about your next move. You keep struggling. Orwell: I am the best damn hacker in the country! No-one has broken my code yet! Foamy: Well Orwell let us re-cap the situation, who has whose head in their hands ready to cut the blood supply off? Orwell: Uh you. Foamy: You what? Orwell: You sir. Foamy: That's better. No Damien Ryan back to you, this fan of yours seems so much like you, he keeps harping on about how good he is, he keeps going on about being good at what he does yet he is the one caught between the hard place and my fist. Orwell here just like you doesn't realise what position he is in. Like you Damien he doesn't realise that he is no longer struggling against another weedy little hacker, he is trying to outwit the Foamy One and that can't be done so easily, just ask Xander and Spawn about Entrapment. Melissa: That was over a month ago. Foamy: True and the Disaster Down Under is in Nashville in little over a week. And I aim to be on that card doing something, making my mark but first I have to teach Damien a lesson. (Orwell slumps over he has passed out) Melissa: But Damien Ryan is a better fighter than Orwell. Foamy: In his mind yes, but when I look at him from across the otherside of the ring Sunday night I will see Orwell here, he is no different to him. Both sit behind their achievements and yet still cannot use that to their advantage. Orwell was a great computer wizkid but he couldn't fight. And Damien Ryan may be Hardcore Champion but he has no passion for it, he likens defeat to a quick shower. And that will be his downfall, hitting people with chairs maybe enought to win the Hardcore Title but you have to do a little more than parade around being 'Hardcore' to defeat the Foamy One. Have a Foam Filled Day!
