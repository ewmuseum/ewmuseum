You disgust me... (Distortion RP #3) Damien Ryan Furiously Addictive Wrestling Roleplay #30 Date: 10.19.02 Location: Hotel outside Miami (the screen opens outside a hotel outside Miami. In the parking lot we see Damien Ryan's navy blue pick-up. He has the tailgate down and seems to be loading some bags into the bed of the truck. He seems pretty pissed off. He's wearing a t-shirt and jeans, his hair pulled back under his Toronto Maple Leaf's hat. He pulls up the tailgate and pushes it close. He walks over to the drivers side door and opens it then turns around to the camera) Ryan: Well, a regular one on one match up with normal rules looks like it's going to be the game tomorrow night. Opening match-up in Miami, week for before Disaster Down Under. Been quite some time since I've been in a regular one fall match, the primary match I was trained to fight it. So grand! I can finally put some real talent to use when I bend the 9th Wonder of Foam in ways he's never been before. Grind his body into a pretzel, or maybe turn his groin musle so far around, the guy in the second row of section C seat 29 at the arena will feel it. Personally, I'm sick and tired of all Foamy's queer antics and comedic promos. Trying to win the fan's approval by making them giggle a little bit, using his 'jackass' stunts to try to get a point a through...not very intelligent. You see a lot of symbolic lines from wrestlers in this industry, but the 9th Wonder of Foam seems to express it in an idiot way, and truely I don't find it relevant because he's going to lose anyways and will never come close to actually have some sort of victory over me. (he gets in the truck and closes the door. He turns on the engine, loud music begins to play but he turns the radio off to finish the promo. He checks the dashboard and the oil light shows up. He pops the hood of the truck, and gets out. He reaches behind the drivers seat for a bottle of oil. He walks over the truck and lifts the hood, he pours some oil into the tank) Ryan: Foamy, please don't test or analyze my thoughts about what I think about the title I hold. You have to realize that I came to this company to be number one and to be at the top of the game. Now personally the Hardcore title is probably the lowest ranking title in the FAW, can you agree? The most coveted in the FAW World Title. Now I said at my first appearance that I wasn't going to be one of those guys who was going to walk in and start ranting about winning the World Title, and I haven't done that. The only thing I said was that people should keep my name in the back of their heads and keep their eyes peeled so they can remember to watch me when I clime the ladder to sucess. I want to the best, I was trained to be the best, I am from the best country there is, I have one of the best records, and I will be the best opponent you've ever been matched up against Foamy! I want that title, that's why I could care less about the Hardcore belt. But I plan on continuing to beat people, and continuing to be undefeated so I can finally make my way to that match against the champion and that's when I will be the best, and I will be the best EVER. (He finishes pouring the oil. He screws the cover on then drops the hood. He tosses the oil bottle into the barrel a few feet away and goes back to the drivers side door) Ryan: Foamy, I bet you have been underestimated, I bet you've gone through extreme matches, I bet you had to fight your way to the top. A good wrestler does that. But I'm going to ask you something...Have you ever been defeated by a great Canadian? I obviously have to do somemore of my homework for that question. You're right, all things must come to an end, but who ever said it has to happen right away... as you said, people die...you'll die, I'll die, even Christ died. But most people don't die till their in that 70-90 years old range... Now how long could I actually go without losing? A day? A month? A year? A whole career? It could be done. And I actually don't think you are going to end my career, and I really don't think I'll be sitting in a hostpital bed any time soon Foamy. Your not the brightest bulb on the tree Foamy, and I can see that maybe you have a small disabilty is realizing the fact that you will not beat me. And I'll take that into consideration, but if you continue to keep telling yourself that you will beat me, that's just unhealthy. Be ready for tomorrow, because it's going to be one of those days when the Foam doesn't seem to come out. (he gets in his truck and starts the engine) Ryan: Oh yeah, one more thing. I have a few things in the back here, and I've already delivered a few things to the arena, Why? Because I'm not going to sit back and let what happened after my match last week happen. Anybody who's not in their right fucking mind would think I was going to forget about the whole god damn thing. I have a few ideas on who it might have been, but all I got to say is that tomorrow night, after my match I'm on a baseball bat rampage! That's right, I will find whoever attacked me last week and I will put them out of their misery, and they will pay the Canadian way! (He shifts his truck into reverse and backs out of the parking lot, shifts again then drives off down the road heading towards Miami. The screen fades out)
