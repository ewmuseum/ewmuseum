Can A Clown Make People Cry?

19/12/02
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Can A Clown Make People Cry? 9th Wonder Of Foam Furiously Addictive Wrestling Roleplay #108 Date: 19/12/02 Location: Evil Scheme Room …. Foamy’s EVIL Underground lair – Thursday …. (The 9th Wonder Of Foam is curled up in the corner of his evil bedroom, the sheets are silk, adorning the walls are signed photographs of some evil guys, but the Foamy One is not enjoying his surroundings, he sits in the corner rocking back and forth) Foamy: My title, my precious title. (Lars enters) Lars: Foamy, Foamy, James Harris will be here shortly. Foamy: We will be united soon my title. Lars: Foamy! (Foamy finally notices Lars and turns around) Foamy: Oh it’s you. Well then meet me in the evil scheme room in five minutes. …. 5 Minutes later – The Evil Scheme room …. (The 9th Wonder Of Foamy lies across a leather chair, around the “table of doom” sit Lars, Enrique Iglesias, and a German Scientist named Schmidt with a lisp) Foamy: Gentlemen welcome to the first meeting of the world newest world-wide criminal organisation, the Foam World Order. Neo and Sabatash are in training for their tag title match as will I be soon. Basically I’d just like to address any of your concerns about your roles in the organisation, but first I’d like everyone to introduce themselves. Schmidt: Willkommene Herren, bin ich froh, ein Teil der Mannthchaft thu thein! Foamy: Anyone have any ideas on what he said? Lars: Nope. Foamy: Do you understand English? Schmidt: Nein (Foamy sighs then look back again) Foamy: But if he could understand the… now my brain itches. Ok then gentlemen from now on we will simply nod and smile when Schmidt talks, next up Hispanic man. Enrique: My name is Enrique, fear me, fear my facial mole, the source of all my evil powers! Foamy: what exactly are your evil powers? Enrique: I am Enrique, fear the mole! Well I can belch the Danish alphabet. Foamy: Lars, did you recruit Latin singing sensation Enrique Iglesias because he could belch your native alphabet? Lars: Possibly. Look what we all want to know is as the newest evil super genius on the planet how do we know that you will not fall prey to the same mistakes as other evil men before you? Foamy: Look I have all I need to know in this handy book entitled “What not to do when you’re an evil genius”, go on test me. (Foamy throws the book to Lars) Lars: Ok planning regulations, what size should all air ducts be? Foamy: It doesn’t matter as long as they are too small to crawl through. Lars: Correct. Ok where should you safe guard the source of all your power? On the mountain of despair beyond the river of fire, guarded by flying dragons or in a safety deposit box? Foamy: Safety deposit box. Lars: Well done. What does the rule about self-destructions clearly state? Foamy: Do not include a self-destruct mechanism unless absolutely necessary. If it is necessary, it will not be a large red button labelled 'Danger: Do Not Push'. Lars: You are learning well. Ok henchmen dress code what is the rule? Foamy: Henchmen will not have tattoos identifying them as members of my organization, nor will they be required to wear military boots or adhere to any other dress codes. Lars: Ok finally, what should the standard time remaining counter be on a digital interfaced bomb? Foamy: Ah a trick question. Never employ any device with a digital countdown. If I find that such a device is absolutely unavoidable, I will set it to activate when the counter reaches 117 and the hero is just putting his plan into operation, thus setting the bomb off early. Lars: Well done. (One of Schmidt’s desk lights flashes) Schmidt: Ith denke dath Telefon thchelle! (Foamy, Lars & Enrique all nod their heads) Foamy: anyone have any ideas? Enrique: I am Enrique, fear the mole! I think there is someone at the door. Foamy: Lars go and fetch Mr Harris, the rest of you can take the afternoon off. (Enrique and Schmidt walk off to the nearest bar; Enrique puts his arm around Schmidt) Enrique: Did I ever tell you about my mole? (As they both disappear Lars enters with James Harris and his film crew) Foamy: Lars, see to it that Enrique is sent to Iraq bearing a t-shirt “I love America” while crooning the National Anthem on his next tour. Well hello James. James: Hello Foamy, a lot has happened since we last met. Foamy: Yeah Spawn apparently spoke, and in English, and coherently. James: You kid? Foamy: Ok it wasn’t coherent. James: So 9th Wonder Of Foam Spawn accuses you once again of being the clown, the fool the idiot. Foamy: Has he the power of sight, the power of hearing? Does he not know that the Foamy One is reborn? I am called a Clown by a man who makes a living wearing red and white spandex adorned with little whit skulls, a stupid mask and a comic book character for a name. James: He accuses you of not knowing the game. Foamy: The game? He thinks that FAW is a game? Games have rules Spawn, a set of clearly defined rules, I follow no rules. When children such as yourself play games one whiff of defeat and you pack up and take the ball home. Something which you have done in the past. If the thinks that this is ONLY a game, then he should just retire now. This match is my life, when I close my eyes and I see nothing more than the FAW World Championship, I see not the men I need to defeat just the prize at the end of my journey. James: He uses the word Clown a lot? Foamy: Do I sound like a clown? Do I have a red nose? Do I have a mini packed with seventeen of my closest clown pals? No. I may have used to be much like a clown but the Foamy of new knows how to achieve his aims. Last Sunday I aimed to piss off the Klown, did I go to the ring and ambush him? No. I put a simple music tape in his car and made him more riled than any three on one attack would do. Why do I want to rile him up? Because I can, and I now know how to handle it. James: Spawn also comments on your “silly” name. Foamy: Does Spawn think that he is facing a three foot high fifteen-letter foam representation of my name? No. Is he facing Jerry Seinfeld? No? He is facing the man beneath the name, the man beneath the suit, beneath all the healthy banter, and clearly you have no idea who I really am. James: Any closing remarks? Foamy: If Spawn thinks that being a bad ass, being unstoppable means wearing stupid mask and dying his hair white then he is mistaken. If he thinks that littering his syntax with swearwords somehow makes me scared of him he is mistaken. His over use of oft-used expletives just shows me how little he has to say, his repeated use of the word Clown is the best he has to offer me? I hope his in ring vocabulary has a wider definition of terms because the new improved Foamy One is not intimidated by anyone. At the end of the day Spawn, Bomber, Costella, Smith Renegade, Rzarector and the Great One are no more than pieces of crap I have to step over to get the only thing I care about, the only thing I love, the FAW World Championship. James: I’ve just had word that the man who gets the pinfall in the tag title match will enter the main event along with the winner of Ninja V Hoefel what is your response to this? You could end up facing one of your own, or even HaHaKlown. Foamy: If I end up facing one of my brothers then so be it, I will still show them no mercy, I will still end them if victory requires it. And should the Klown emerge victorious he will hear my laugh ringing in his head for the rest of eternity, not just on his car stereo. Have a Foam Filled Day!
