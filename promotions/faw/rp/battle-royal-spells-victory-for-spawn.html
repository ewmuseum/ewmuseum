BATTLE ROYAL SPELLS VICTORY FOR SPAWN Spawn Furiously Addictive Wrestling Roleplay #101 Date: 12/14/02 Location: PANIC RP 1 Glory, a feeling that couldn’t compare to anything in the known world. And now Glory was around the waist of Spawn in the form of the world Championship Belt. True, Spawn would have liked both belts but he was just as happy with one and now that he had this glorious belt he never went anywhere without it. This belt, to him, symbolized everything he had worked hard to accomplish since he began in the FAW. It stood for all the blood he left in the ring, all the sweat and all his emotions…and it all paid off with this single title of world Champion. Spawn now more commonly wore a smile across his face, unless of course he was in the ring in which case he was always serious. But for the most part everyone he ran into now noticed him because of his championship status and asked for autographs and such. Like he cared, it was almost as if the highlights in his hair were a gleaming fire that came from the glory of his very soul and all around him could feel that intense heat radiating from Spawn. But all that aside this was a few months ago now he is going to climb the ladder again and pursue what the company took from him his FAW world title the day of daydreaming is over, right about now Spawn felt like doing one thing, not going to Disneyland, but going and getting wasted at the biggest bar he could find. So he went to a somewhat big bar with all the neon lights outside and all the top selling beer company’s logo’s outside and headed right on in and was treated like a VIP and even, for some weird reason, got his own personal bartender! He was a pretty buff guy, didn’t look too stupid so he figured he could avoid any conflict with him. Spawn: 'Barkeep, give me the most expensive beer you people have in stock. Cause tonight we celebrate, Spawn-Style!' The bartender smiled as he just now noticed the golden leather belt across Spawn’s shoulder as he headed into the back to get the expensive beer. While he was away Spawn looked up at a TV monitor from his stool and noticed a HHK and a Foamy promo being run. After watching it all the way through he held his nose with his right hand and began waving the other one around like someone just dropped a 10 pound stink bomb in the room. Spawn: 'Oh god, please can we watch something that doesn’t look and smell like crap? I am trying to drink over here…think I am going to be sick after watching that pathetic excuse for what HHK and Foamy calls promos!' With a few slight snickers, no one wanting to be offensive either, the channel was changed to the daily news. But the foul stench left by HHK’s and Foamy’s’ promo just wouldn’t leave and when Spawn’s golden ambrosia arrived he figured that should get the smell away…but alas, such a foul stench is not so easily washed away. Then Spawn looked up as if remembering something and started to laugh. But like any good thing it has to come to an end, a FAW camera crew found Spawn’s whereabouts and started to interrogate him as to why he was laughing. 'So you were the idiot I polished the floor with that night! You are the one that actually lost to Xander, the man I made Forgotten? Well I am glad you haven’t forgotten me because I had long since forgotten your ugly mug, Foamy. All the scars I left you, oh please if I remember right I did one move to you and now you’re scared for life. I would think losing to Xander on that night would have hurt more than anything I could have done to you. Now be honest with yourself here, Foamy, do you really think you have a chance this Sunday against me? I mean your chances would be slim enough in a regular match but this match will be a Season To Be Jolly Santa Claus Battle Royal match! I could damn near end your career if you aren’t on top of your game little man! Of course, you would probably be comfortable when I slam you into a table because it will be just like you were back at home where Mommy and Daddy left you. As for the Tables and Ladders…well when I am done you won’t be able to get the taste of steel and wood out of your mouth.' Spawn took a quick swig of his beer and looked around the bar with those cat-like eyes that were burning with a certain passion as he spoke. A burning that matched with the highlights in his hair very well as he took another sip of the golden liquid from his glass and glanced back at the camera before starting up again. 'Former FAW world champion, yes Foamy that’s me, something you could only dream of accomplishing for yourself. But this match on Sunday would bring your dreams close because at least you would be in the same ring as me, but for you that might be too much punishment. Cause you see, I don’t regret taking you down that one night long ago because frankly I wasn’t even aiming to do it in the first place. It’s just your ugly carcass got in the way and I felt it only proper to the fans that I remove such garbage from the ring. Because when it comes down to it Foamy you just don’t belong in the same ring as me, hell you shouldn’t even be allowed within ten feet of me, that’s what I think of your so called skill and this blind revenge of yours. Maybe you ought to think before signing matches Foamy, because you are going to get beat and beat badly unless some miracle knocks you in the forehead…but wait that wouldn’t help because then you’d be knocked out for the rest of the day and want revenge on all miracles. You want revenge, than go hire a hitman to kill your dog and seek revenge for something worth it. Revenge is a strong thing to just throw around Foamy; it’s not one of those petty little things you are used to dealing with. Revenge should be used for something more than getting hit by me because you got in the way, revenge should be used so much wiser. But then again, what would you know about being wise…foam head. So for now save that false revenge for when I bloody and beat you within an inch of your life this Sunday, then you will have something that revenge is properly used for. Until then, enjoy your tacos while you can still eat solid foods.' Spawn takes another sip of his beer looks at the camera and begins to speak again. This company is going through some changes and their is rumor of some of the greatest wrestlers ever to step foot in the FAW ring are to be making a return soon. Well it's time to step up to the plate and show these vets what the Hell Master is all about, I am not just a curtain jerker that was made into top level talent because of lack of employees I am the rising star, and I will have my day. Starting with this Jolly Santa Claus Battle Royal match! thing I will destroy anyone who stands in the path of The Hell Master, and prove that I still gots what it take to be the top dog in this place. Omega Fro, HHK you two bozos are going to have to do more than just step back into a FAW ring and try to mess with the true ruler of FAW, train and prepare yourselves the proper way, if not you are just setting yourselves up for a nasty fall, and your short comings will be just that short. Spawn takes another sip of the golden liquid from his glass and glanced back at the camera before starting up again. I've said before it's not about the gold. I have a certain person I don't wanna disappoint, as well I know the fans are looking forward to this. If FAW has faith enough to put me on a wrestling card while I'm in a comma well damn it I'm gonna wrestle. Now Great 1 this is strickly for you, you act as if you are one of the top guys in this fed. You sit there with that stupid little smirk on your face thinking that your tough shit. You run your mouth, but you never back it up. Look at what you have done in the last couple of weeks. Nothing , nothing at all just run your shitty mouth. I completely kicked the shit out of you yet you got the cheap victory. I mean you would have been better off not even walking down to the ring. That’s it. So how the fuck do you think that your such a mega superstar? Because you screwed the guy over who helped you win the match? The answer is YOUR NOT. Your a cheap piece of shit who held on for the ride and got half of the world title because you gave head to half of the staff in this place. Great 1 you are retarded for not realizing this, and Bomber I guess the anal sex is good because you still hang out with this faggot. Your a leech Great 1, nothing more. Spawn takes a breather, looks at the camera and begins talking again. Costella You committed a deadly sin in your last promo. A sin that has now condemned you to hell for all of eternity. You bashed one of the greatest wrestlers ever to perform inside a ring. The Hell Master. Since when do you think you have the balls to badmouth the last greatest heel of FAW? That's right the LAST GREATEST HEEL. Not JLC, the biggest heel ever but for some reason people liked, or Skipster, who is a movie star now and only comes back every six months to push a heel, THE HELL MASTER. could kick the shit out of any wrestler in this fed. A survivor of THE DEVIL's dungeon, and in my eyes still the true FAW champ, I should be respected and treated with dignity. But Costella, you decided to bad mouth me!? God you should have both of your legs ripped off with a Sharpshooter. Before you can ever call yourself a wrestling superstar you have to know what one looks like you faggot. And as far as me whining, what the fuck would you do in my situation? What would you do if you lost the biggest title in the industry because staff decides let begin from the beginning all titles are now vacant? You would bitch your little ass off. You would probably break down and cry right in the middle of the ring. So in the name of Spawn, FUCK YOU, you worthless sack of shit. Spawn takes another sip of the golden liquid from his glass and glanced back at the camera before starting up again. The bottom line is this the whole FAW is going to be in the ring this Sunday guys like Lazlo Hawk, Neo, Sabatash, Rzarector, Lee Smith, Loco Creed and so on, and on, and so fucking on. But you see their can be 20 men or 30 men or 40 men in a FAW ring but their can only be 1 winner, and I will be that winner, I have decided that this match is very important to me, I will reach the world title again with the victory of this match. I Spawn The Hell Master after this match is over and my arm is raised in victory, I will be establishing my final goal in FAW, and that is to be the first four time FAW world Champion. And this Sunday the first step toward that goal begins. I am pretty sure a lot of people remember when I first came to FAW I set out a goal….That goal was to rule FAW, to put FAW in a COMA…I have accomplished both goals. But now I will do it all over again, and this Sunday at Panic on that night in the Season To Be Jolly Santa Claus Battle Royal match! I will once again accomplished both goals, RULE FAW, and put FAW in a COMA, and no one will dare stop me. That fire still burned in Spawn’s eyes as he finished off his beer and paid his bartender in full and even gave a nice tip! As he stood up he looked around quickly then hoisted his gold buckle belt upon his shoulder and headed out the door. The soft glow of the moon on his new buckle made him feel a bit more calm but as Spawn knows he can never stay very calm when any sort of match is coming up. The cameramen soon headed back home for a full nights rest when Spawn left…wich made Spawn feel somewhat liberated. But would this next match be the end for Spawn or would it be another reason to buy another beer? Did Spawn even seem to think FAW could compete with him, and would that small factor be his downfall? Well now if we knew that we wouldn’t be reading this now would we?!
