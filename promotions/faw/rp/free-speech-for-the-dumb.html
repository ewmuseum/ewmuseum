Free Speech For The Dumb

Free Speech For The Dumb 9th Wonder Of Foam Furiously Addictive Wrestling Roleplay #129 Date: 31st December 2003 Location: Evil Control Centre …. Evil Control Centre – Beneath The foam Factory …. (The 9th Wonder Of Foam sits in his raised chair, Latin signing sensation Enrique Iglesias sits there as well as Schmidt the German scientist with a lisp and Lars. Foamy strokes his World Heavyweight Championship Belt) Foamy: Gentlemen, this is indeed a great new era in FAW history. And now we must work hard and fight to keep me atop the FAW title tree. And now from here we can work for global domination, of not only the Wrestling industry but also that of the entire World! Any ideas gentlemen? Schmidt: We… Foamy: What’s that? Oh that’s an excellent idea. Lars: Who are you talking to? Foamy: the FAW championship. She talks to me, only I can hear her. Oh I do love that idea, it’s awesome. Enrique: Fear the mole! I’m sorry but how can a hunk of metal speak to you? Foamy: A hunk of metal? Is that what you think this is? This is no hunk of metal. A little under a year ago she spoke to me, in my dreams and in my nightmares. She needed salvation, she needed to be rescued she needed me. And now I have her and I will never let go of her. And now her power is mine, she gives me guidance on how I may hold onto her and she has given me another great idea. Enrique: Fear the mole! Me and Schmidt. Foamy: Schmidt and I! Enrique: Schmidt and I. Fear the mole! Have come up with an evil scheme to rid the world of your greatest foe! Foamy: Who is this foe? Enrique: Her name is Britney Spears. Foamy: Britney Spears? How is she a threat to me? Enrique: She sells millions of records every year denying you of greatness. Foamy: You mean she denies you, Latin singing sensation Enrique Iglesias of triple platinum albums? We are not here to preserve your worthless hide Enrique; we are here to wreck havoc, mayhem and to ensure that she never leaves my side. Schmidt, go and being research into a time machine, find me a Delorean and a flux capacitor, and build me a time machine! Enrique, go and eat some jello. Lars: Uh Foamy I have an evil scheme! Foamy: Go ahead. Lars: Coffee. Foamy: What about coffee? Lars: Imagine if we took all the caffeine out of coffee and sold it to people, society would collapse. Foamy: Actually they do that now its called decaff. And in fact it has benefited society; we want to bring it to its knees not make it less alert, now go and call James Harris it’s time that we talked. …. Hours later …. (The 9th Wonder Of Foam sits in the evil scheme room, he is talking with the FAW Championship Belt) Foamy: Remember it? Of course I do. I was there in that dome; I had to pinch my leg to stop myself from looking at you too long. The people I passed through to get to you, and now we get to make sweet love all the time. (James Harris enters with his camera crew) James: Hello Foamy. Foamy: Hello James, have you met my one true love? The FAW Championship? James: Uh, yes. (Foamy stands up, his trousers are around his ankles, James Harris stares at him) Foamy: What? A man can’t be intimate with his championship belt? (Foamy pulls his trousers up) James: This Sunday Foamy you face Ha Ha Klown one of the greatest FAW Champions of all time in your first title defence your thoughts on this match? Foamy: Is this Lisa woman jealous of my relationship with the title? Does she want to see me and my precious title separated so that she can have her wicked ways with the Foamy One? Her plan has two major flaws, Ha Ha Klown cannot defeat me, and number two the FAW Championship does not want to leave my side and I will no let her leave. Schmidt! (Schmidt opens two previously unseen doors, and rolls in a Delorean with various household objects attached to it using sticky tape.) James: What’s this? Foamy: Its and idea the Championship came up with. She knows how Ha Ha Klown can defeat me and this is the answer. James: Care to elaborate? Foamy: Well James. The only way the Klown or anyone else for that matter will ever get to hold the FAW Championship ever again is to travel back in time to before I held it. To defeat me now is impossible as neither the belt or I will allow it. James: Do you think it was fair that Ha Ha Klown of all people was chosen as your first defence? Foamy: The fact remains that Ha Ha Klown doesn’t deserve this title shot. He couldn’t make it into the last three of the Dome, why should he have a title shot? But Klown had it been anyone else as undeserving as you I would not allow the match to go ahead. But it’s you Klown. The Legend, the one person who they all see as the man. You Klown are seen as the FAW champion for all time, and you are the man I must defeat in order to cement my place in history as the new FAW legend. When I defeat you they will finally see that you were merely a prelude to my reign. What you did was impressive but I can take that further. James: But what about Ha Ha Klown’s colourful and impressive past history? Foamy: As Spawn said he was a three time World Champion, so he would defeat me but he didn’t. The odds were stacked against me saving her, the names involved the past that they all dragged behind them. But their past achievements did not aid them, they weighed them down. I was previously the annoying fool who didn’t have what it took to become FAW champion but I did it. And within ten months of my arrival too. So whatever Ha Ha Klown did in the past I will ignore because it won’t matter to me and it won’t matter to her because she is going nowhere. James: Do you think that Lisa and Ha Ha Klown might have something going on here? Foamy: It wouldn’t surprise me that the Klown might try and sleep his way to the title. The question she asked me to answer is do I deserve to be FAW Champion? I fought through one of the greatest, most brutal matches in FAW history and won it. Ha Ha Klown fell at the first hurdle and now he is rewarded. I don’t need to prove anything to anyone but myself. Which is why I look forward to ending the legend of the Klown, and then destroying the winner of that tag match at WWC. And then on to Extreme Beating 3, where I will defeat the winner of the WWC match and then I will rule all, and she will never know the touch of any other because she will be me and I will be her. Have a Foam Filled Day!
