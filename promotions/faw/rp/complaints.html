Xing Furiously Addictive Wrestling Roleplay #89 Date: 06 December Location: Malibu, California Police Station “It was after-noon after Xing was let out of jail and it was certainly a terrible night for Xing locked up in that cell served with slopped or what people say is slope no offence Ihope was taken. Now Xing when he got out wasn’t a happy man at all so went out of jail and drove off. His car was seen outside a burger bar at 12 o’clock and it was then 1 o’clock and the place was burnt with no sign of Xing. When we see a man whose appearance is covered by darkness and the person is anonymous being curios as I am we followed it. When the worst thing happened! My butler ran off crying so I had to get my hands on the wheel now it was my first time in the drivers seat except for in the ring. When I went round the corner and saw Xing with a woman who was getting certain abuse off him but I drove up to the couple and addressed them but he Xing approached me and just knocked me down. As you can see my heavily bandaged up arm now I want Xing to rot in jail so I have no threat for that title not that I’m scared we exceptionists don’t get scared.” “Well thank you for that Lee Smith we will be considering the file of complaint and you just wait to see the outcome.” “But that is it, I want Xing to rot away his life here your useless.” Lee Smith replies with anger and fury to a police officer receiving his claim against Xing in a California Police Station. When Lee Smith goes other the top and starts poking the officer and slagging off the whole police station and the state. We are outside of the police station were we are caught up with a woman looking not too happy pulling up opposite the station getting out the car with contempt on her face files in her hands and a very covered up man lerking behind her also getting out of the car. The woman approaches us and says, “Which way in I am making a complaint about Mr. ‘Charm’ Lee Smith I not telling you why but if you follow and direct us the way you will soon find out.” The man leans over me being so tall and breaths very heavy on face. He lean back and quickly dodge him and hop to get cover near the woman I direct her into the station where the two people catch Lee Smith complaining “This man Xing better be locked up soon or more woman will get…har..med.” Lee slows down when he sees the two people at the door obviously knowing them from some place the woman then confesses the real truth over that after-noon she claimed “The sling is as for no use he has no broken arm because the truth is Xing was helping from the flame and I was screaming at him because I was scared for my parents who were trapped inside the burger bar, he was yes helping me and Lee you low life liar he did not hit you he asked you to move away from me for my safety and he grabbed your arm and moved you away and yes you fell we laughed at you so that’s why you complained.” As the woman was talking the covered up man took the files off of the woman and took out a tape when Lee saw him and looked scared. The woman had finished her lecture to Lee but Lee was not interested in what she had to say; but the police were and took her into a room; he was more interested in what the man was holding which was a tape with a tag on it saying ‘Video Evidence’. So Lee attempted to take the tape off of him but he did not succeed instead the hood fell off of the man and he was revealed as Xing. Lee and everyone else was stunned and Lee pushed Xing shouting out “You dirty scum you had your laugh now give me the tape!” The man, Xing shook his head and pushed Lee back within seconds police were on to the pair and broke up the fight. The men were out of breath and the tape was nowhere to be seen until Lee shouted, “got it!” Xing went to go forward and hit him but was restrained back and had to view the tape burn by Lee his archenemy. Xing went for a punch which Lee Smith dodged so the police had to take Xing and lock him up in a small cage. We were following Xing the whole time and it comes to now where Xing looks out the window and sees Lee touching up the woman Xing had walked in with earlier to file the complaint. Xing rattled the cage in rage the officers tried to calm him down and then offered Xing a one-phone call, which was to a person unknown yet. “Hello, who is this?” Asked a man answering the call made from Xing sounding very suspicious. “Well Ken that would be telling wouldn’t it? But what I can say is that come Sunday on Panic watch your back because I’m going to come at you so hard you won’t be walking for a week. Yes Kenny boy it is me Xing the one you face at Panic now my collision has started up against the FAW fed and you are my 2nd victim but don’t be fooled by the knock out he got lucky but watch out for my strike against you.” BEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEP! The phone hangs up as Xing sends a warning to be aware of to Ken Ruslan but is going to let out his Lee Smith anger on Ruslan
