The things people say....(Panic #1)

Jan 29 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The things people say....(Panic #1) Rzarector Furiously Addictive Wrestling Roleplay #227 Date: Jan 29 2003 Location: In the church Sometimes men are tested by the words of others. Those words may have no influence on some human beings, yet on other human beings they may have every thing to do with there lifes. *The screen opens where Rzarector is seen sitting in the church. He is wearing his normal black hooded robe, with his green eyes and white fangs glowing out of the facial part. The camera zooms in to Rzarectors neck where he has a new tattoo of a black priest. This is to represent that Rzarector is the Black Priest. There the camera zooms out to get a closer look at the Church. You see several rows of benches, numerous bookcases, and alot of crosses all around the church. The carpet is red with the front of the church hanging a huge statue of Jesus with red candles glowing all around. Rzarector sits in the very front. There the Priest walks out from a black door on the side of the church. He walks up to Rzarector.* Priest: I thought I would have seen you here. Congratulations on the Rumble last Sunday. Rzarector: Thankyou. It was a hard victory but somehow I managed to pull it out. I was hit with a chair, beat down by two to three people at once, and still came out on top. I owe all my wins to God. Then again I did get lucky. Priest: Lucky? You looked pretty dominant in the match. You through out so many people I couldn't even keep count. The only way you could have got lucky is the fact that Skipster through out Crimson Lord for you. You and him always had up and downs in your matches. Rzarector: Yeah I remember that. I was kind of worried about Skipster as well. And oh yeah, those people I throughout in the rumble, well to be exact, I through out five people in that when worlds collide rumble. You know that is basically 25 percent of everyone I eliminated? And yet I was number six in the rumble. So I had a long fight with everyone. *As Rzarector is looking like he is bragging the Priest quickly stares him down.* Priest: You know God helped you train for that match, now your going to sit here and brag about it like that? I don't think so. God gave you everything, you might have the talent, yet you do not have the experience or training yet. And you are not wrestling for yourself, you are wrestling to spread the word of God to the unholy. *Rzarector hisses at the Priest.* Rzarector: You are not God, I will have God tell me what to do, not you. You are no better than me. I am God's warrior. I am his angel of death, the black priest, I am Rzarector. I won fair and square no cheating. Now if I can't be happy about my win, then I don't even want it. Priest: You were bragging. Rzarector: I was simply stating the facts. (At this time Rzarector heres some words in his head. It is the sound of God.) (*God: Rzarector, you need to stop fighting with the Priest. For one fact, you two are on the same team. You were bragging Rzarector, there is a difference in stating facts and acting egotistical. I have seen to many of my angels fall for sinful greed like you were just showing. Now please Rzarector, just take the time to sit back and think for a second of what it is worth to you. You are going to get your world title shot, that should be something shouldn't it?*) Rzarector: I guess. *The priest looks confused as he doesn't know who Rzarector is talking to.* (*God: Now it is my time to go, you will have a guest soon. Be prepared to talk to him in a better manner than you talked to me, understand?*) Rzarector: Yes God. Priest: That was God? Rzarector: Yes. Priest: Did he say anything about me that I should know about? Rzarector: No, but we will have a guest at the church in a while. I'm not sure how long, but I am sorry for bragging and us arguing. Priest: It is ok Rzarector. *****15 Minutes Later.****** *The doorbell of the church is set off as the chimes of God's music is heard throughout. Rzarector and the Priest are still talking on the bench as the Priest gets up. He is wearing a black tux with a white collar to represent he is the Priest. He walks over to the door and answers it. There before us is James Harris wearing his FAW tuxedo, pink with purple stripes. The Priest looks strangly but lets him in. James Harris walks over and looks at Rzarector.* Priest: I take it you are here for Rzarector? James Harris: Yes just tell him this is James Harris and I'm here to do a post match interview. *THe Priest walks into the main room of the church once more again, and starts to tell Rzarector something. Rzarector nods as the Priest comes back and motions ot James Harris. James begins walking into the main part of the church and sits down next to Rzarector. The Priest then exits to the black door again.* James Harris: Hello Rzarector and congratulations on your rumble win. Rzarector: Thankyou James Harris. Why are you here, what does this interview have to do with anything? James Harris: Well Rzarector, bad news are floating around backstage. Alot of the veteran wrestlers are saying that you don't deserve to be a contender for the World Champion. They say that you are not physically ready, or mentally ready to be able to hold the title. What are your thoughts on that? Rzarector: You know James, stuff like that doesn't even matter to me. It is the fact that I did win the rumble. I beat alot of those old veterans. And they may say that I didn't throw them out but it doesn't matter to me. For one fact, that is the fact that the rumble match has different rules. Whoever lasts the longest wins, and I lasted from #6 in the rumble all the way til #20. That is an accomplishment enough for me. I trained very hard for that match James. James Harris: I can tell. You know alot of those veterans are just to upset over the fact someone will be taking there air time. That is probably the reason they said that. Rzarector: Well Mr. Harris, it was God who gave me the win. I can't deny that. But something else that upsets me about those veteran wrestlers. Sometimes I feel as if the titles went to there heads. There success in the FAW was just something that set them off to thereselves as being the best. Well that is not the case. I've never had any self confindence. I am not the best, I may never be the best. The only thing this rumble has proven is that I am not the worst. Which makes me very happy. You all can just not beleive it. I am so happy, and nothing could make me sad right now. *James Harris pauses for a second as he smiles at Rzarector. James Harris is thinking that he did deserve this and he worked very hard to get to this point.* James Harris: Well Rzarector, you did your first step. You got past the Rumble. Now you got to go to step two, the last step. And that step is to win the world title. But first, before I ask you anything about that, you have to face Skipster this upcoming Sunday on Panic. You and him were the last two in the rumble, and some are saying that he deserved it just as much as you. What do you have to say about that? Rzarector: It is true. Skipster did deserve that rumble match just as much as me. Yet I was the better man just for that split second. Which made the whole difference in that match. Skipster came in late, had all that energy. Yet one mistake cost him the whole match. It is weird how wrestling works out, but none the less I hope Skipster is happy for me. Because I will say that I will see him again next year in the rumble and this week at Panic. James Harris: That is a nice thing to say Rzarector. Okay, now since we moved on from Panic. What are your thoughts on the World Championship. Do you think Foamy will still be the world champion at Extreme Beatings 3? Rzarector: Absolutely, Foamy does not make a better champion. Just for one fact, he is awesome. The only problem with him is he is very evil, so when we fight at Extreme Beatings 3, I will be out to seek his skull. That is my duty you know James? To seek and destroy the skulls of devil for my cause. And my cause is God. So I will not hold back at all for Foamy, I have never. Me and Foamy fought in the dome, and he was the better man that night. But not this night, I want it to much. James Harris: Well Rzarector, if anyone else could win the world title before Extreme Beatings 3....who would you like to win it? *Rzarector pauses for a second as he sits back and thinks. His eyes glow up as they finally stop glowing.* Rzarector: I think that I would like to see Cyrus Skar win it. I think he has alot of upcoming talent. Yet then again you got Gabriel Hunter. Maybe him. I think he would be a good opponent, but he might beat me. James Harris: He is very talented, but whether or not you ever fight, you still get your world title shot. Remember that Rzarector, this Extrreme Beatings 3 will be your time to shine. You got the fans behind you, now just go out there and prove to them what you got. Rzarector: If there is anything that means more to me than the World Title, it is God. He means more to me than anyone. He is my father in Heaven. I serve him til the day I die, then I continue serving him in the second life. God I love you and thankyou for the win. Thank the fans for there support, and thank the FAW for there time in dealing with me. James Harris: Your welcome. (*God: Your welcome, continue your fight my son.*) Rzarector: That is all for me. I look forward to fighting you Foamy, remember, repent or your time is coming. You never know when you will die, and when you die. It will be eternal torture. For I am the true warrior of God, and I will ressurect you to the afterlife of eternal burning!!! I am the RZARECTOR!!!! *Rzarector stands up and forms a cross position as he hisses out loud. His eyes glow up to a bright green and his white fangs flash out. The camera starts trembling at the weird sound of his hissing voice. James Harris jumps in front of the camera real quick.* James Harris: THIS PANIC RZARECTOR vs. SKIPSTER! THEN WE WILL SEE RZARECTOR GO ON TO EXTREME BEATINGS 3 TO FIGHT THE WORLD CHAMPION! WHOEVER THAT WILL BE!!!! *The camera fades as Rzarector is still hissing and James Harris is finishing shouting out BE!!!!*
