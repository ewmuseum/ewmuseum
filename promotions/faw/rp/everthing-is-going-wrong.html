Everthing Is Going Wrong! Renegade Furiously Addictive Wrestling Roleplay #60 Date: 30/10/02 Location: Renegade's House (It’s early mourning in the sun shine state. We are in Renegade’s kitchen. The early mourning sun light pierces through the blinds on the window, casting long shadows across the room. A half eaten blow of cereal is left un-finished in the middle of the table, a empty bottle of milk and a box of corn flakes also inhabit the cluttered table top. The camera slowly pans around to the right. We see more signs of an un-eaten breakfast, a lone slice of toast and a glass of freshly squeezed orange juice are left untouched. As the camera continues to the right, the FAW tag team title belt comes into few, spread across the table. A hand then comes into few and rest on the belt) Renegade: What am I going to do? (The camera moves around more and we see Renegade sitting at the kitchen table looking rather distressed. He hasn’t even bothered to get dressed and is still in his PJ’s!) Renegade: Everything is going wrong. Why is everything going wrong? All these plans I had when I first joined FAW have all been pissed away by someone else. I suppose that’s what I get for placing my fate in someone else’s hands. Well all that’s gonna change from now on. I am the only person from now on that will dictate my path in FAW, or life! (Renegade’s voice starts to get more and more aggressive as he goes on) Renegade: Not some so called friend of mine. It’s over. It’s all over. That arsehole can burn in hell for all I care. He’ll pay for fucking with me. He of all people should know that it’s not good for your health to fuck Renegade about. He’s been on the receiving end of many a Renegade beating in the past. The fucker will pay for this! He’s ruined everything. We ended something great before it could even get started. We could have shown this fed things that they’d never seen before, things that they’d only dreamt of. (Renegade picks up the tag title ad rests it on his lap, and looks down at it. He talks in a somewhat calmer tone now.) Renegade: You the only thing that makes sense to me anymore. You’re the only thing that keeps me going. Without you I’d be lost. (Renegade leans forward and grabs the glass of orange juice. He takes a small sip before hurling it towards the wall at the far end of the kitchen. The camera follows the glass thought the air as hit explodes upon impact against the wall in a shower of liquid and glass. The camera zooms in on the wall, the orange juice slowly runs down the wall, carrying small fragments of glass along with it. We then hear Renegade getting up from the table and slamming the door to the kitchen on his way out. The kitchen is now silent apart from the drip, drip, drip of the orange juice. A far cry from what it was like just a few minutes ago. The scene then fades to black. Leaving you wondering just who Renegade was talking about. Who has ruined everything for Renegade?)
