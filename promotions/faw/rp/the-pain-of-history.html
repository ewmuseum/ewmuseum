The Pain of History Profusion Furiously Addictive Wrestling Roleplay #125 Date: 28-12-02 Location: Houston, Texas “Get him, get that little son of a bitch” A loud voice called out from a tall muscular teen. A gang of 16-year-old boy’s gathered around a small 13-year-old boy who was shaking repeatedly and was obviously scared to death. The five older boy’s gathered around forming a complete circle around the young boy and began the throw abuse towards him. “Little asshole” one shouted in a raging voice. “This will teach you not to hit on my sister” The seeming leader of the wild pack of boys. He grabbed the small boy by the scruff of his neck and held a firm grip. The small boy’s face was like death warmed up. The little boy was shaking even more and looked like he was going to pass out. “pppppplease…..dooontt….hhhhuurrttt…mmee” the little boy stuttered out. “Hurt you, Hurt you, were going to bloody kill you” The older boy shouted in the younger boy’s face. The older boy held the younger boy up closer to his face and released a huge spit into the smaller boy’s face. The crowd of boy’s all laughed together like a pack of wild dog’s howling into the night. The older boy through the younger boy to the ground and laid in a swift kick to the younger boy’s stomach causing him the quench up with the pain. “How do you like that, you little toe rag?” The older boy exclaimed with a vengeance. “Hey, leave some for me!” One of the other boys shouted out as he leathered a right foot into the lower spine of the younger boy as if he was a football. The younger boy was now bent in an arch shape from the excruciating pain from his lower back. The leader of the pack of lad’s disappeared behind the wall, while the other boy’s continued to stick their boots into the younger boy’s abdominal region. “ Please STOP!” The younger boy screamed out in a gasp while tears ran like a stream down his cheeks and onto the cold cobble stone road. The older boy’s laughed as the younger boy continued to scream in pain. “We should have got this on film lad’s” One of the boy’s said sarcastically while kicking the younger boy in his lower region. The younger boy let out a scream of pure agony as he lay on the cobble-stoned road, covering his lower region with both of his hands, leaving his face exposed. “Quickly someone rearrange his face so his mum will think that the dog has lost its hair and has got taller!” One of the boys’s said, but not very amusingly. One of the boys then raised his sized eleven, Doctor Martin boot’s into the air and kicked the small boy several times on the face leaving several cuts on the bridge of his nose and forehead. The younger boy screamed but seemingly gave up trying to fight back. The boys began to walk away from the beaten and bloodied body of a younger human lying on the road. The younger boy trying to regain his senses spotted this and tried to put the pain out of his mind and get back to his feet. “Look, the little bastard is getting up!” One of the boy’s exclaimed while pointing in the younger boy’s direction. The younger and beaten boy saw this and ran for his life crying as he ran down the road. He ran with four older boys chasing him. The younger boy found a small cove and ran in there. He hid behind a wheelie bin as he watched the boys run right past him. The younger boy found it safe to get up and walked out looking behind him he saw a figure with something in his hand. The younger boy’s heart began to race like a humming birds wings. The figure got closer and then all of a sudden the younger boy was knocked back down to the ground by a metal pipe which was swung by the leader of the group of boys, who had disappeared minutes before. The younger boy let out a huge scream of excruciating pain. The other four boys heard this and hurried back down to the cove where they found their friend standing with a steel pipe in his hand and standing beside a fallen and bloodied thirteen-year-old boy. “Well lad’s I think that’ll teach the little bastard some respect” The older boy said with a smug look on his face. “Maybe another one wont hurt, lift him up Gary!” The older boy ordered. The older boy named Gary picked up the helpless young teenager as the boy drove the steel pipe into the younger boys stomach not once but twice. The younger boy screamed out in pain and once again the tears where flowing down his cheeks like a river. The older boy went to drive the pipe into the younger boys stomach again but sirens where heard. “Shit lad’s lets get out of here!” The boy exclaimed as they ran for the fields which where opposite where they were previously standing. “Help! Help! Please help me!” The younger boy screamed for the help of the personnel driving the vehicles with the sirens. Then a police car pulled up beside the young boy as a look of relief came upon his face. A police officer stepped out of the vehicle and rushed over to the young boy. “Marge, call and ambulance for me, I’m at Clifton Road, hurry!” The policeman said wearily down his walkie-talkie. The policeman helped the younger boy back to his feet and saw that he was bleeding and loaned the young boy his sleeve to wipe his face on. “Thank you, thank you very much!” The young boy said still panting with pain. -------------------- A sweaty, warm and worried Profusion woke up and was sitting in his chair. “Jesus Christ, no more bloody Doritos for my before I go to bed, that’s the third time this week!” Profusion said still panting from the nightmare. “I hate having these flashbacks of my childhood, they are very disturbing for me” Profusion said while checking the time. Profusion got out of his chair and headed for the sink in the kitchen. He turned on the water tap and cold water poured out. Profusion dipped his hands under the running water and threw it all over his face, trying to revive himself from the shock. “Stupid bloody Doritos, I have the right mind to call and sue those assholes!” Profusion said sternly. Profusion put on the kettle and took out a clean cup and his coffee granules. He scooped out two spoonfuls of the granules and put them in the cup with a small amount of milk in it, waiting for the kettle to boil. “I can’t help it, those Doritos are so furiously addictive. But so is sex and you don’t see me getting much action in that department.” Profusion said with a glum look on his face. Profusion then had a hard and thinking look on his face. He was thinking hard and then suddenly his face light up. “Furiously Addictive? Holy Shit that’s right, my contract signing is today. If I’m late I’ll blame it on those damn Doritos.” Profusion said hurriedly as he took the boiled kettle and poured the boiling water into his cup mixing the contents around with a teaspoon. “I wonder if this coffee is furiously addictive?”
