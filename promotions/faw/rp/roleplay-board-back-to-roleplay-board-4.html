J.J Dynasty — FAW Roleplay, August 2002

August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Furiously Addictive Wrestling Roleplay Board Roleplay Board | Back to Roleplay Board J.J Dynasty Entitled: Spare the X-Rodd and Injure the European Champion Written: 8/1/02 Location: Boston Cemetery The FAW cameras cut to a shot of a graveyard, located in the middle of Boston, MA. Near the far end of the graveyard are two small gravestones, simple in their appearance yet also quite lovely. Kneeling between the two graves is a man, whose back is to the camera, wearing a dark, full-length trenchcoat and a black Nike cap. Man: Mom, Dad, it’s been a long time since I have been up here to see you. I have not come up here since I broke my neck 20 months ago and I figured I might as well bring you up to date. The camera moves around so as to showcase both the graves and the face of the man. It is none other than J.J. Dynasty, he looks fine except for a bandage covering the left side of his forehead due to the attack he suffered this past Wednesday on Panic. On one of the gravestones reads “Kyle Jacobson, 1942-1997” and on the other reads “Michelle Jacobson, 1948-1997”. Dynasty: I have gotten another contract, this time it is for the FAW, and it is a really good fed with a lot of talent. My first match is this Saturday against a guy named X-Rodd; I do not know a ton about him other than he has a very successful history. I am not gonna just lie down for him, when he steps into the ring with me he will be stepping into the ring with the old J.J. Dynasty. He will not be facing the psychotic J.J. Dynasty who was in CCW; no he will be facing the J.J. Dynasty who won 4 United States Titles, 2 Cruiserweight Titles and 1 Intercontinental Title. He will get to step into the ring with the man who destroyed careers with the Road to Hell, he will be facing the man who developed the most dominant winning record in UWA history at 67-3. He will get to face the man who broke his neck and came back into the ring to compete again. Dynasty reaches into his coat and pulls out a single red rose and a letter. Dynasty (setting the rose on his mother’s grave): Here you go mom, I know you are still watching me and I hope you are proud. Dad, here is a copy of my acceptance into FAW, I thought you may like to see it. I am gonna prove to both of you that I have what it takes to make it to the top in this federation, and X-Rodd is merely a small obstacle, the first step on my climb to the top. Speaking of obstacles, there is one man in the FAW who has already gotten my attention. He and I used to work in CCW and now he is the current FAW European Champion, Cyrus Skar. He attacked me backstage yesterday and I am gonna show him that when you mess with a Jacobson there is gonna be hell to pay. I will admit that I was hoping that he and I would cross paths, maybe in terms of a European Title Match down the road so that I can prove to him and the rest of the world that he cannot change his destiny and he sure as hell cannot beat Dynasty. Dynasty bends down and kisses his mother’s grave before standing up and putting on his black sunglasses and staring at the stars that are beginning to twinkle in the heavens. Dynasty: However, as far as X-Rodd and tomorrow go…well, your son is about to give X-Rodd a beating the likes of which nobody could ever comprehend. I shall do it for my career, I shall do it for my reputation, I will do it for my mother, I shall do it for my father, but more importantly, I will do it because I can. Dynasty bows his head, crosses himself and slowly makes his way out of the cemetery towards his car and towards the first ever FAW Breakdown.
