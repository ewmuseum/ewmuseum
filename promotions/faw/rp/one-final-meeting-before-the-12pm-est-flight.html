One final meeting before the 12pm EST flight

22nd December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
One final meeting before the 12pm EST flight 9th Wonder Of Foam Furiously Addictive Wrestling Roleplay #122 Date: 22nd December 2002 Location: Evil Scheme Room …. Evil Scheme Room – Sunday …. (Enrique Iglesias hurriedly sits down, he is barely dressed, Foamy enters the evil scheme room with a stack of papers) Enrique: I am Enrique! Fear the Mole! What’s this about? Foamy: Where is Schmidt? Enrique: He is sleeping off a double ender Fear the mole! Foamy: What? Enrique: We just watched two episodes of Angel back to back. Fear the mole! What did you think I meant? Foamy: Oh nothing. Enrique: Why have you called us to this last minute meeting? Foamy: Well I thought the deadline for my plane to the PPV was at 12pm GST but the ticket says 12pm EST so I thought I’d get one more last minute meeting in seeing as I had some spare time. Enrique: I see, fear the mole! Foamy: My opponents for tomorrow gentlemen. (Foamy pulls down a projector screen and Rzarector’s face appears) Foamy: This gentleman is opponent number one. Rzarector, he believes himself to be the man who will make me change my ways. I like being evil, he doesn’t understand this so I will show him why Satan lives in me, why I am the unstoppable force in tonight’s match. Enrique: Why don’t you listen to his advice and become a hero again? Fear the Mole! Foamy: Why? Because being a hero got me nowhere in FAW. This is a grown man who wears plastic fangs in his mouth to make him appear “evil”. I am supposed to listen to this imbecile? Enrique: Fangs? He doesn’t sound like a wrestler? Foamy: I don’t think he even knows what he is. This is a man who wrestles semi-nude men in a ring while wearing spandex. Yet he thinks he is something more than an entertainer. This man thinks he will make me change my ways. My ways can’t be changed and some Dickens-esque morality tale isn’t going to make me change my mind because what he doesn’t realise is that I will become FAW champion whether he likes it or not. My evil ways will get the better of everyone in the dome. He can’t change me; he’s not some servant of god no more than I am a ballerina. Enrique: But what about that tutu? Foamy: Shut it! See Enrique Rzarector thinks that my personal demons are a bad thing, when in fact they are what drives me, she will be mine and nothing can stop that fact, not even a grown man In a Halloween costume. (Foamy flashes the next opponent on the screen, Spawn.) Foamy: Ah I’ve said enough about this guy. Finally I will wipe that smile from his face. Next! (The next face appears, it is the Great One) Foamy: This Enrique is the man who took my fans from me, and to honour him I will gladly shove them up his ass for him, along with any hope he had of becoming the king of FAW. Next! (The next face appears, it is Renegade) Foamy: I deal not with children. Give me a real opponent. (The next face appears it is Lee Smith) Foamy: Ah yes the “Living Legend”. Soon to be a deceased one, hell not even a deceased legend, a deceased chump. I will end The Bomber’s FAW career when he is so broken that a man such as me could dominate his ass that he has to quit. This man Enrique lives in his own dream world where he truly believes he is a “Living Legend” but some sweat pants don’t equate to greatness, what I do on pay-per-view will be perceived as Legendary. (Foamy flashes another face, it is The British Bombers) Foamy: And finally, the man who finally deciders it was time to open his mouth… and cover us in crap. Oh his wife left him, his child is gone, and I would pity him if I felt him worthy of it. So I will simply crush him, and let him go into the darkness so that he can truly know what sadness is, sitting afar watching her taunt me while she drapes herself over the bodies of other men. He was right, he did say he would destroy the DCV, then why did he join us? Is his word truly that worthless? Can anyone truly trust his assertion that he will walk out of Evil Intentions as World Champion? The fans don’t care anymore about him. Enrique the 24th of December will no longer be known as the day before Xmas it will be the day the old legends finally die, the day that the pretenders to my throne finally give up, the day Enrique that she finally takes my hand and becomes mine, forever. Enrique: Fear the mole! That’s all well and good Foamy, but why are you telling me all this? Foamy: I don’t know. Have a Foam Filled Day!
