Prodigy — FAW Roleplay, August 2002

August 2002

Furiously Addictive Wrestling Roleplay Board Roleplay Board | Back to Roleplay Board Prodigy Entitled: Fallout: The End? (part 2) Written: 30 July 2002 Location: On my Computer Blue Text = Prodigy Green Text = Marty Red text = Number 2 Purple Text = Old Number 2 Maroon Text = Number 1 -And Now The Conclusion- Prodigy comes face to face with 'Number 1' - a large, electrical tower with a mechanical eye. There is a small key pad and a slot, perhaps for something to be put in or come out. Prodigy, stares at this, unable to to beleive that this machine is Number One after all. 'Meet Number One' 'This is Number One' 'Yes... The all powerful and all seeing, Number One...' Prodigy, deciding he had had enough of this crap and that it was time to take a chance, punched Number 2 in the face, knocking him out. Prodigy, now all alone, looks for the exit but it is hidden. He runs his hands along the smooth, metalic surface of the wall and finds a small groove. He pushes on the groove and a door opens. He, cautoiusly steps out and checks to see that no one else is around. He is in a long, narrow corridor with hanging lights above. He walks forward and finds a elevator. It opens as he nears it and he finds it empty. He presses the button to go to the bottom floor. It takes a while as the elevator slowly moves down. He finds himself, in what seems to be some sort of holding room. Down each side are cells, all empty, except one. This contains 2 people - a man who looks like he is a 50's reject and a Number 2 he rocognises from his second encounter (Chimes of Big Ben). 'Ah... Numb... Scott.' 'You said my name' 'Let us out' 'Why are you in a cell' 'Well.. After failing to brake you... They normally kills us off... However they placed me in here, thinking they would bring me back as your accomplish and i would then brake you...' 'Who's that in there with you?' 'Oh... This is Marty...' 'Marty Macfly' '... he's been here since 1956. After failing to brake him, they placed him in a cell so that he couln't escape. Are you gonna let us out?' 'Well you just said that you were gonna betray me, given the chance... .How do i know this isn't how it starts?' 'I'll tell you whats going on... Right now your in a rocket, They plan on shooting it at the moon. I can help you... all of us escape... You have to trust me' 'Ok... But how do i let you out?' 'There should be a hidden button...' Prodigy feels around for a hidden button and manages to find it. The cell door swings open and the old Number 2 and Marty walk out. 'Hey... Right on Daddy-O' 'Come on... Lets get out of here' 'We have to find Number 1...' 'But i destoyed it... A mechanical eyed tower...' 'No... That's 'The General'... another ploy to brake people... The real Number One would, most likely be near the top of the rocket. He is quite mad...' 'Mad...!?!... Come one... Lets make like a tree and get out of here....' 'Thats er... Make like a tree and Leave' The trio go back up the elevator to the top floor. On the way, the old Number Two shows prodigy a room, with all his old gear and Prodigy changes back into his own clothes. He keeps the 'Number 6 and Half' badge, as evidence. Number Two leads them down several corridors, where they come to a final doorway. They open the door to find an empty room, all excpet one control pannel and a a chair with its back to them. Someone is sat in it. Prodigy walks over and the chair swings round to reveal a man in a hooded cloak. Prodigy pulls back the hood to reveal a chimpanzee. 'Good gravy... .What an unexpected twist to this crazy story' However it turns out to be a mask, which Prodigy pulls off to reveal.... The old Number Two stood next to him. Their faces drop with shock as Number Ones face is lit up by a creepy smile. 'Whats going on?' 'This would be my twin brother...' 'This is the furthest anyone has ever got... I congratulate you. However... this is where you stop.' The Number 2, from earlier, enters the room with two other men. They guard the exit. 'Did you know your brother was Number 1?' 'No...' 'Don't act so surprised brother... I was always better and deserved to be higher than you. Unfortunatley... I cannot let you go.. .so looks like you'll be going on a trip to the moon.' 'Your crazy man... Crazier than the twisting blue suede shoes, Daddy-O' 'Why yes.... I supose i am.. Now if you'll escuse me... i am gonna get of this thing' He stands up and walks out of the room. Number Two and the two henchmen still guarding the door. Prodigy looks at the other two and they burst out into a brawl. Prodigy takes out the Number Two while, the old Number Two and Marty take out the henchmen. When the fight is over, they leave the room and go back down the halls to the elevator and the old Number Two leads them to the exit. They run down into the Village and the place is deserted. 'I am guessing the place was evacuated into the underground shelters...' 'Look theres the crazy daddy-O' 'Could you stop saying that' They chase after Number One who leads them to the rocket control room. He starts the countdown and begins to luagh, demonically as they enter the room. 'You can't stop it now...' He runs off, through another door. 'Listen... I'll go after him.. .You try stop the rocket. If you can't or do manage to... So i've deavtivated the helicopter controls so, get off this Island, while you can' 'Think Marty.. What could the deavtivation code be?' 'No idea Dadd... man' Prodigy thinks for a while before coming up with an idea. He finds the control pannel for the rocket and enters in the code '0001'. A flashing light comes on the countdopn stops at '04'. 'That was cool... cool as a pair of blue suede shoes' 'Come on... Lets get out of here... Wait...' He walks over to the mechanical tower with the eye and punches four letters into the control pad. It whirrs for a second before overheating and exploding. The room then starts to shake and the other control pannels begin to explode. They run back out of the control room and into the deserted Village. They make their way down to the helicopter and get in. Shortly after, they are in the air, making their escape. As they look down on the Island, the rocket and control tower blows up. 'What did you ask that... thing?' 'The insoluble question.... W-H-Y Question Mark' 'Oh...' They look back and can see The Island in the distance. Prodigy gathers the information from the maps and dials in the helicopter so he can get The Islands position. They make their way back to America. Several hours later, they are back in Dakota. 'Where are you going?' 'I guess... Ill take this thing and just... fly round...' 'Cool daddy-O' They go there seperate ways and Prodigy watches him fly off. He feels his jeans pockets to find roguhly $50 and gets a Taxi Ride, towards The Fargo Dome, where he knows FAW will be next. He wonders whatever happened to the old Number Two and Number One... As soon as he got back to FAW and he could tell everyone he is ok and that he is back he would then go to the top with this one... The FBI maybe... Prodigy slowly drifts into a much needed sleep.
