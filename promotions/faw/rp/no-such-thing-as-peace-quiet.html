No Such Thing As Peace & Quiet...

Jan 22, 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
No Such Thing As Peace & Quiet... CJ Maxwell Furiously Addictive Wrestling Roleplay #164 Date: Jan 22, 2003 Location: Fancy Hotel Dining Hall [~* Here's what we do know. CJ Maxwell is in his wrestling prime. He has competed now for three years on the pro-circuit and is finally coming into his own frame in the sports entertainment industry. He brought the 'Age of Innovation' to the federations he participated in, where he preformed skilled and innovative manouvers never before seen or imagined. He then realised a short-term fortune when his t-shirt sales did unexpectedly well and made him a wealthy man. He was later auditted by the IRS and his mother was put in jail for attempted robbery and extorsion, trying to frame CJ and use his money to take away his success that she didnt feel he deserved.*~] [~* Putting that rough family trouble behind him, he refused to return to the ring until he felt he was ready to handle the workload again. Until he was in the right frame of mind physically, emotionally, and mentally, to make the kind of comeback that only a man like CJ could. And what better place to do it then in a Battle Royal at the FAW 'When Worlds Collide' Pay Per View. *~] [~* Maxwell has now ended up, inexplicably, with an 18 year old manager by the name of Valerie, whom he gained gardianship over through a public program where he was supposed to 'Give Back' to his community. CJ, himself, still does not know who the anonymous call came from to set the whole thing up, but he took the young girl under his wing. He was expecting a manager, and what he got was first ever 'child' to take care of; something he was not planning on having to manage until maybe later, if at all... *~] PRESENT DAY, 8:30 AM [~* Sitting in a fancy hotel dining hall, CJ Maxwell is hunched over a bowl of cereal and reading the sports section of the local news paper. While others in the room are dressed in their best clothes, preparing for a hard business day's work ahead, Maxwell is still dressed in his flannel pajamas, labeled with blue moons and a darker blue base colour. His hair all over the place, he continues on during his peace and quiet, while others stare and the muscular FAW superstar in awe. To them, he seemed nothing more than a large 4 year old. His seat facing the door, he sees the young Valerie waltz into the room, chipper and fresh out of the shower, her hair still wet. She smiles and waves at CJ. His eyes bulge and he dips his head back down towards his newpaper and bowl of cereal. 'Oh god! Here she comes! Not again!', he thought. She finished pacing and stands at the opposite side of the table from CJ. Her innocent smile could have lit up the room.. her voice is perky, and irritating... *~] Valerie - Hi there, CJ, hunney... hows it going? [~* He sits there and continues to eat, pretending not to notice that she's trying to talk to him... *~] Valerie - You know what? That looks really delicious! What is it exactly? Maxwell - ... Valerie - CJ, I've been noticing that you don't talk to a whole lot of people when you get up in the morning, or at least you haven't so far. I just want to share some nice conversation, is that so wrong? A little hospitality, just trying to get you in the mood to wrestle or get competitive. And, you haven't said anything to me since the last night; or at least I did. You went straight down here after your alarm clock went off this morning... now, whats going on? [~* She breathes heavily on the back of her hand, then brings it to her nose to smell it. She just winces a bit and doesn't seem upset at all. She seems satisfied with her breath. But CJ remains focused on his bowl of cereal. Absolutely nothing coming out of his mouth except the occaisional overflow of milk. He stares down at the morning paper placed in the middle of the table, glancing through the politics and world issues section. Taking a sip of his orange juice, Valerie speaks up again when she doesn't receive an answer.. *~] Valerie - It's definetly not me, it must be something bothering you! I know, its that FAW PayPerView thing isnt' it? Is it giving you nightmares?! I can take your place in the rumble for you..?! Maxwell (looks up slowly) - ...? Valerie - Say no more! Well, not like you would have said anything but, consider it done! If thats what you want? Cuz I think you really want a piece of me CJ... oh yes, I think you want me all to yourself... [~* Valerie begins to undo the top of her blouse and expose her clevage, off camera of course because this is a family show. For some reason, this acctually does get a reaction out of Maxwell, quite a large one, but not a typical horny man approach.. no, something much different.. as his head peaks up and he sees this site across the table from him, he slams his spoon down in his bowl and throws his arms up in the air helplessly. He leans back in his chair as if he's given up all hope in trying to finish his breakfast... *~] Maxwell - What is the matter with you? Are you some sort of horny temptress or something?! You come here, you interupt my peaceful breakfast, you start to talk my ear off, whine and complain, and then you start to strip yourself bare naked to intise me?! HOW AM I SUPPOSED TO EAT MY FRUIT LOOPS WHEN YOU'RE TRYING SHOW ME SUGAR COOKIES?! Valerie - I'm just... Maxwell - Yeah, yeah, sure just 'doing your job', right? Where in a manager's job description does it say that you can call me at 4am to try and get me to say a few words? Where does it say you can leave mushy love letters, sliding them under my door in an attempt to flirt with me? And where does it say, that you can show me your cleveage to interupt my good, wholesome, fruity bowl of Fruit Loops?! Do I pull my pants down in front of you when you're eating Blueberry Eggo Waffles? Valerie - Well, no.. Maxwell - Exactly.. are you happy now? Are you happy you got some words out of me? You should be ashamed of yourself young lady... prostituting yourself off like some Lunch Lady Doris, serving sloppy Joe's. You're not like that at all.. I know that, you know that. The only thing I ask, if you want to sit and chat with me, is to stop the whining, the theatrics, and the expositions... and let me finish my cereal, ok? Valerie - Yeah, I got it... I'm good.. Maxwell - Now what is it that you want to ask me about.. wait, my sarcasmic physcic readings are getting something.. something about When Worlds Collide.. and, wait theres more, something about a Battle Royal... [~* When he finishes his sentence, Valerie's eyes widen in sheer amazement, as if she'd never have imagined that he would've guessed it; despite the PayPerView in a matter of days... duh?? *~] Valerie - Wow, you're good! How'd you do that??? Maxwell - ...? Uh, let's just call it a gift...? Valerie - Sounds good. What are you going to do for the Rumble, I mean its going to be a real battle isnt' it? Especially since you're considered the underdog and everything cuz nobody knows a single thing about you. Maxwell - Your insight is charming, but what do you want me to say? That I'm going to lose? I'm not going to do that. I'm just as capable as the next guy of picking up a win in that Rumble. Even if I don't win, I'm going to brawl it out with the bunch of them and show them what I can do. You know that, its so cliche! If anything, my being new is only going to work FOR me, not against me. Valerie - I guess you've got a point there. But what if they all team up on you at once just to teach you a lesson? Or you slide into the ring when your time comes and you get flung right back over the top rope and never get a chance to show your stuff? OR.. what if- [~* CJ holds up his hand in disbelief and shakes them to get her attention. He leans forwards and moves his hands as he speaks, trying to reach her young, immature, mind in some way.. *~] Maxwell - Whoa whoa whoa! Time-out short stuff! Now your job as my manager is to 'encourage' not 'discourage'. There's going to be alot of 'What-If's' along the way, and you're going to have to watch an experienced wrestler, me, this Sunday, and see how its done. Now let's be honest for a second.. do you have cable? Valerie - My mother couldn't afford it, not even a TV. We had a busted-up car radio that my brother found, and he got it working.. Maxwell - You poor, poor thing! Forget about wrestling, You mean you've never been exposed to Gilligan's Island? The Simpsons? Three's Company? The Dick Van Dyke Show?! [~* Sadly and discouragingly, Valerie shakes her head 'No'... Maxwell almost falls out of his chair as he stands up and grabs the young lady's hand, pulling her to her feet. Maxwell starts dragging her across the crowded dining hall towards the exit... *~] Valerie - Oww! Wait! Where are we going?! Maxwell - To Blockbuster Video! We are going to watch re-runs until you're drunk, high, or passed out on the couch with sacks under your blood-shot eyes! You've been seriously deprived! With that, Maxwell finishes dragging his young manager out of the hall and out onto the street, where she walks at her own free will. The scene fades out.. TO BE CONTINUED..
