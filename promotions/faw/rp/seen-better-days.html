Seen Better Days Renegade Furiously Addictive Wrestling Roleplay #13 Date: 16/10/02 Location: Renegade's Home (The scene opens with an external shot of Renegades small house in Orlando, Florida. The camera then zooms in slowly, focusing on the front door. The scene then moves inside the house and we see the camera slowly making its way through the hall and up the stairs. The stairs are wooden and the walls a covered in a pail blue wallpaper. Framed pictures of Renegade and other wrestlers line the walls. One is of Renegade celebrating a win back in his UCW days with someone very familiar. The camera turns left at the top of the stairs and heads towards what looks like a bed room. The camera moves into the room and we see Renegade sitting on the edge of the bed with his back to the camera. The bedroom is decorated in much the same style as the rest of the house. Renegade’s right arm is laid across his body clutching his ribs. He also has a neck braise on due the fall he took last Sunday against Apocalypse. Renegade turns slightly towards the camera and begins to speak) Renegade: You pleased with yaself Apocalypse? ARE YOU? You damn near killed me last Sunday you SON OF A BITCH! (Renegade turns round all the way now to face the camera. You can see in his eyes, in his face and hear it in his voice that he is in a very pissed off mood) Renegade: First you tossed my body out of the ring onto the god damn security railings, and then be dropping my square on my frigging head. But that wasn’t enough for you was it Apoc. Nooooo. You picked up me up and gave me the Rise Above. I was unconscious you sick bastard. You know how long it took for the feeling in my legs to come back? 10 hours. 10 fucking hours. You did that to me. You made he unable to walk out of the arena last Sunday. I’m one half of the god damn Tag Team Champions, show me the respect I deserve you arsehole! I don’t deserve to by treated like that by anyone, let alone you! This isn’t over between us Apocalypse. Not over by along shot. If you hadn’t have dropped me on my neck I would have pinned your sorry ass 1..2..3. And you know it. I know it. Everybody in that arena knew it. They could feel the buzz that was in the air from me. They new that summut special was about to take place. But you put a stop to that BY DROPPING ME ON MY GOD DAMN HEAD! Not only did you drop me on my head, you through me out of the ring like a rag doll onto the steel security railings. Severely fucking up my ribs. (Renegade lifts up his shirt to reveal a heavily taped up chest) Renegade: I’m 23 years old damn it. I’m not ready for my career to be ended. Especially not in a nothing match, against a nobody like you. (Renegade tries to regain his composure and calm himself down a bit. All this shouting isn’t doing anything for his injuries!) Renegade: But it’s not me I feel sorry for. It’s not even Apoc I feel sorry for, ooooooh no. I feel sorry for my opponents for next week at Distortion. Crimson Lord and The Duke. ‘Cause all of this pent up anger, frustration and hate I have built up inside of me the last few days aimed towards Apocalypse, I will take out on those unfortunate soles. There gonna wish they’d never been signed up for that match against me and Rzarector. I am gonna take it out on you and anyone else that wants a piece of me. Bring it on bitches. I don’t give a shit anymore. I’ve gone past caring now. It’s time to not give a shit about anything and do what ever the fuck I want. It’s time for me to live up to my name. Renegade! The era of the extreme is upon you my friends. Soon it shall be known. You have been warned! (With that the camera slowly fades out to black. The camera is focused up close on Renegades face. He continues to stair straight though the camera as it fades out)
