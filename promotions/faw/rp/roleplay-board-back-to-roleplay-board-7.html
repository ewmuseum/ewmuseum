Xander — FAW Roleplay, August 2002

August 2002

Furiously Addictive Wrestling Roleplay Board Roleplay Board | Back to Roleplay Board 'Exceptional' Dave Hurst Entitled: Title Dependancy (rp#3) Written: 30-7-02 Location: University of Boston I sit in the middle of the common room at Boston University sipping a steaming hot cup of coffee and watching the younger students playing pool, or sitting listening to the music playing through the speakers. They seem to be having a good time chatting to each other, not really the kind of conversations that I want to take part in, talking about pop music, or the last episode of Friends on the television. There is only one topic of conversation that I can have with these people that I’d feel comfortable in. That’s wrestling. If they wanted to talk about Baseball, or Football (that’s the American Football, not Soccer) I don’t think I’d be able to talk to them. I mean, I thought Yankees was a kind of bet, I don’t think I can speak to these people. Then as I think this, I notice that one of the students is staring at me from the other end of the room. This guy is spooky; he’s staring right at me. Then he gets up and wanders towards me, he gets closer then a big smile is on his face. “Dave!” he says as if he’s known me all his life. “Hello, do I know you?” I reply. Then I realise. It must be an FAW fan. No wonder he looks shocked at seeing me. “Oh, I’m just your biggest fan! So what’s up? Have you given up wrestling now?” “No. I’ve had to go for a new job now I’m not making many appearances on the FAW anymore. I’ve been getting paid on a match-by-match basis and seeing as though I haven’t had a match for months and won’t be having too many in future, I’ve gone to University to get a teaching degree and hopefully become a teacher.” “Oh that’s great! I’m sure you’d be a great teacher, Dave. I’m Richard by the way.” “Well it’s nice meeting you Richard,” I say expecting an end to the conversation. “Yeah, it’s nice meeting you Dave. I hope you get some matches soon. You kicked ass the last time you were in the federation. Do you remember that, Dave.” “Yup, I remember. Won the International Title, defended it for about 5 matches then got injured and was out for months. Well, hopefully I get to win it back soon. I’ve got an International Title match at Panic this week.” “You have? Oh that’s great! I’m sure you’ll kick Xander’s ass! I mean, he’s great and everything, but the whole Xander = Ratings thing is getting very old, very fast.” “Yup. Well, Xander may have some kind of mental condition. It’s called Self-Obsession. I don’t think I’ve seen any other superstar as obsessed with himself as this guy. The way he looks, the way he acts, and the way he wrestles. He loves all of it. He can’t get enough of it. He’s spends so much time telling himself how good he is that he hasn’t got time to realise that I’m going to beat him and take his International Title from him. You would have thought the guy would be slightly worried that he’s facing me. I am the guy who held the title and defended it every week while he was down at the bottom of the card trying to impress people with his stupid sign that read ‘Free Range Eggs’ because the idiot had picked up the wrong f*cking sign. While he was looking a complete idiot in front of the FAW fans trying to impress them, obviously thinking he was the dog’s b*llocks because people were laughing AT him. While he was doing all this, I was the International Champ. I was beating the living hell out of any asshole that dare make the challenge. Nobody could beat me. So now it’s time I take the title back. Now it’s time that EDH stepped up to take back what is rightfully mine. I want that International Title and I’m going to get that title and there is anything that Xander can do to get in my way. I’m sure Xander doesn’t even know what day it is, let alone what kind of a beating he’s going to get at Panic. I’m sure he’s busy doing his nails, or powdering his nose, or looking at himself in the mirror to realise that I am not only better than him, but the best International Title holder there ever was. This title is mine; Xander can’t do anything about it. That’s just the way it is.” The kid looks shocked. That was some outburst that I just made. I’m obviously very angry that I haven’t got the title. I never realised this before. I never realised I needed an FAW title as much as this. When Panic comes, I might do something dangerous. I might really hurt this guy. I have no big quips with Xander, but the fact that he has the International Title may push me over the edge. I mean, it should be mine, I should have the title, I should have the gold around my waist because I never gave up the title. The FAW owners took the title away from me. They felt because I couldn’t defend it through my injury that I shouldn’t have it anymore. So they made the title vacant. So why don’t they just hand it back to me when I return? Because they’ve already handed it to some idiot who hasn’t got the talent, the skill, the ability that I have. Some kid who has no sense of respect for the title, or what goes with it is given the belt and only defends it because they don’t let him defend it against anybody who deserves the title. Like me of course, I should hold the title; I will hold the title, that title is mine. When Panic comes I’m going to take the title and give it the respect it deserves. I’m going to hold the International Title for as long as it takes. I’m going to destroy Xander physically and mentally, when the match is over he will be so broken he will no longer love himself, he will hate himself. He will realise the loser that he is, he will realise that he is nothing but a buttered-up, no good champion, who only keeps it because the owners of the federation only pit him against idiots that couldn’t beat him anyway. The little kids of the federation who won’t be good enough to take the title, or any title for at least 3 years. The kid is still there. He hasn’t said anything as he sees how annoyed I look. I’m pumped for this match-up. I’m ready. I get up from the table and leave. It’s nearly the time for my next lesson anyway. I walk down the corridor with the adrenaline pumping through my veins, I’m ready to take Xander apart and take his title now. I have been so long out of wrestling that I’m ready to take out all the frustration in not wrestling out on this kid, this poor defenceless kid. Xander can’t help it. He wants to make his big break in wrestling just like everybody else. He’s just some kid getting to his top form that has taken the opportunities that have come his way and now has reaped the benefits for his hard work. He has the International Title. But I want it! I don’t just want it; I need it! I don’t just need it; I can’t do without it! I’m panting with anger, I’m hyperventilating with fury, My heart is pounding I’m so frustrated. This match is going to be bloody; it’s not going to be pretty. Xander will walk in with a smile on his face and leave without it being physically possible to do so. I will be relentless, merciless and deadly. I will break apart Xander. He won’t be able to walk, he won’t be able to speak, he won’t be able to breathe and he won’t be able to think. This match is going to be the last for Xander. I feel this may be the end of his career here. As sad as it seems for a superstar with such a talent, I feel this match may be too much for him. I also feel that this match will be the advent of a whole new Exceptional Dave Hurst. I feel this is the start of an Exceptional Dave Hurst that is so hungry for success, so hungry to take and then keep the International Title that all-comers will be mortified, they will be mutilated, and they will be destroyed by me. I will take each wrestler that stands in my way and will beat on them and keep beating on them until they stop moving.
