Spawn — FAW Roleplay, August 2002

August 2002

Furiously Addictive Wrestling Roleplay Board Roleplay Board | Back to Roleplay Board Spawn Entitled: HARDCORE ALL THE WAY Written: 8/6/02 Location: BOSTON Its was the Monday after Sunday Night Distortion, Spawn came back to the empty arena and sat right in the middle of where the squared circle would be and just sat there and looked around. He was remembering the adrenaline rush he got when his music blasted over the PA system and he heard the fans boo...it was like nothing he had ever felt before, and he would feel it again this Wednesday. But this time he knew it would be a much harder battle against a tougher Neo. Sure he was nervous but he was also nervous against his last opponent and he did fine. He took one more look around the empty stadium and got up from the hard grey cement floor and started walking around. SpAwN: 'It looks like Im gonna be having another match around here good friend but this time against Neo, its funny, you would think with his name I would want to show him some mercy but I can't...this is too important to me. Mercy, something I can't give to any of my opponents, I have to be rutheless against whoever is in the opposite corner. These fans don't pack this stadium to watch someone be merciful...they want to see someone get the crap kicked out of them, and I'll deliver by doing just that to Neo this Wednesday Now he started to remember how people started comming up to him asking for his autograph and giving him a small piece of paper to sign because frankly he didn't have any merchandise for them to give ...like he cared, he had fans, thats right fans! Something he always dreamed of...now he just had to keep them happy, easier said then done, sure he would do his absolute best but would that be good enough, would the fans still his entertaiment even if he lost? This was a question that plagued Spawn ever since he got back to the locker room when he won last night...could he keep the fans entertained...maybe he should just worry about keeping in shape for now. SpAwN: 'So, will this Neo guy be any better than my last opponnet or will I not hear from him until Wednesday? The ring has become almost like a second home for me...now Neo is going to come into my house, he is gonna come into my dream and try to crush me, my spirit, and everything I have worked for, not likley. For all I care he can bring everything including the kitchen sink but there is no way in hell I am gonna let him beat me. I have sacraficed too much to be here to lose to him...I have to win, I have to prove my point!' An andrenaline rush was pumping Spawn way up as he said this to himself. It just made him feel like he could run straight through a brick wall and not be hurt at all! He felt invinsible and totally in control, maybe it was the solitude of the empty arena but he knew he could beat Neo and he even began not to worry what Neo had to say because to him it would be babble, nothing more than the dead talking to him...yes, now he knew he was in the right state of mind. But the real question was, what if Neo gets to him and what he says doesn't just seem like babble? SpAwN: 'Its amazing how someone could be so nervous...yet so pumped at the same time. Now Neo, what will you be like? If you think your gonna play mind games with me and that your gonna take a stroll into Panic your dead wrong because for you...Survival is exactly what you need to think about because this asshole isn't going easy on you.' After saying that Spawn figured he'd better get home, after all it was getting pretty dark and some maiteanece people were getting a bit ticked off at him for blocking their area. So with that Spawn stepped back outside into the cool crisp night air and took a nice deep breath and slowly started to walk home. He walked halfway home and then started to talk to himself again. SpAwN: 'Survival huh...its time for the real show to start...' Pacing back and forth, this is what Spawn did while he waited to hear from the ever so mysterious Neo He wondered if Neo was even going to show up on Wednesday, and if he didn't Spawn was going to be pissed off because he is going to Wednesday Night Panic to fight in a match and give his blood and sweat for the fans...not some lame half ass win he got because Neo didn't show that just wasn't his style at all. Spawn paced even more on his way home,when he got home he looked at his place,he knew this was a temporary place a place to hang out when he wants to get away from the world, he had thought about jucing the place up when he got his next pay check, but for now his cozy little apartment would look like it would to any man with a regular salary. But Spawn couldn't help bounce off the walls wondering what Neo was going to say or if he was going to show up, this type of stress sure isn't what Spawn had in mind when he signed up! Spawn finally decided that he would review some of his old tapes from when he was wrestling for petty cash and simply the love of wrestling. Slowly he walked over to his VCR, the time was blinking 12:00...damn VCR clocks, he didn't know anyone that could set the bloody thing, and popped in a tape he labeld 'Extreme'. Looking at the title brought a smirk back to his face, he figured this must have been the tape he won this federations Extreme, or Hardcore, title. SpAwN: 'This brings back some great memories, being beaten to a bloody pulp by six guys and still retaining the Extreme Title...maybe thats why I don't like those damn no holds barred matches, but no matter how much blood I lost it was always ...well, interesting.' Spawn sat down on his sofa, it was one of those sofas that looked like it should be in your grandma's house. The pattern looked like flanel and there were a lot of holes in it that were mended together with the same tape Spawn used to tape his fingers and wrist before a match...talk about your dedication. Well, after siiting in the rustic chair Spawn grabbed the remote and clicked play on the small black remote. It was his the last match he had before comming over to the FAW, it was about over five years ago. The screen flared on and was black for a good three or four seconds before showing the packed arena that he was oh so used to wrestling in. Thats when 'My Plague' by Slipkot came over the arena's loudspeaker, but no fireworks. He rememberd that night well, he didn't have any fireworks because the company made you pay if you wanted pyros and at the time he wasn't in any position to be 'wasting' money on pyros, he was wearing some baggy jeans that he wore even when he didn't wrestle and had some tape he borrowed from another guy on his hands. Spawn watched as his opponent's music hit, no pyros for him either...guess he was in a finacial hole too. Spawn watched as he didn't waste anytime getting the match started on the Extreme Champion with an early shot to the headwith a trash can. The shot sounded a lot more vicious on the tape then he rememberd it...but then he figured it was just his hearing. As the match went on he noticed himself doing a move he didn't remember for some reason, he pulled it right out of the book of RVD, he knocked the champ flat on his back and placed a trash can ontop of him then performed a very pretty Splitleg Moonsault...now he rememberd the pain from that...just not doing it. SpAwN: 'Shit, I didn't think I was that stupid back then...although it was a damn nice looking move. I think my ribs were broken for about 4 months after!' He continued to watch in awe as memories from his old days flooded back into his head. His eyes were almost glued to the screen as he was trying to remember all those high flying moves he had long since forgot that he could use on Wednesday. He was so into it he was cheering and booing along with the crowd in the tape! It was like he was in a totally different world, like he was reliving his past and he was loving it! Finally the end of the match came and he watched as he did his finisher different from what he uses now, he called it the Top Rope Hurricanrana but most referd to it as a Springboard Hurricanrana, and watched as he got on the floor himself and counted like the ref 1...2...3! SpAwN: 'Here is your winner and new Extreme Champion, Venom my name before Spawn!!! Oh yeah!' It was times like this that made Spawn the happiest man on earth...however, he still worried about whether or not this Neo fellow would show up or he would put his tail between his legs and run away like a scared little puppy dog. But he figured now wasn't the time to worry so he pressed stop on the tape and returned it back to its place under the VCR. Right now Spawn decided to go get some sleep and relax while he still could, because come Wednesday, he kinda knew he wasn't gonna get much sleep in. SpAwN: 'Neo, come Wednesday we will see what your true colors really are, are you a yellow bellied coward or just not like talking to the opposition? Like it matters...At Panic I'll show everyone that Im the one who rises to the occasion instead of running away from it!' with that Spawn walks away from the camera as the scene fades to black.
