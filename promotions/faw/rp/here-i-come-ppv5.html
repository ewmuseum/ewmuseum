Here I Come!(PPV5) Crimson Lord Furiously Addictive Wrestling Roleplay #53 Date: 26/10/02 Location: Unknown Scene: All around him elderly people, shopping and acting innocent, stealing as much as they can. There retirement funds not good enough to pay for a loaf of bread. He watches, not in amazement for there is more amazing things than some crippled man, stealing lip stick. Better things to witness than, these people bumping into doors saying excuse me and doing it again and again. It's almost sad, when you try and analyze it but they look like their having fun. Forgetting what they were shopping for, so they pick something up and stuff it down their coats, instead. Maybe when they walk out, if they ever do, under their own free will, they'll wonder why the hell they took this stuff, but most likely they'll forget and do it again next week. The employees, trying to follow him, despite the facts. He's the only one there to shop, he's the only one not making a mess Crimson Lord not phased, by the constant stalking, walks casually with the magazine he came for in his right hand. Making his way around the maze of people, till finally reaching the front to pay for his item. Not even surprised there is well over a hundred and twenty people plus and not one is paying for a damn thing on the lines. The women grabs the magazine from Crimson Lord and mumbles under her breath, 'nice costume.' Crimson Lord looks at her, in her eyes when she turns around to get the money, not saying a word, Crimson Lord slams a ten dollar bill down from his pocket and smiles. The women disgusted, takes the dollar and hands him his change. Once outside, the cold air welcomes him, as he walks in the stores parking lot. The moon provides more light, then there jaded street lights as he flips thru the magazine. Reading only a few paragraphs before he begins to speak. Crimson Lord: You know what's amazing? I think it's amazing how our government pays these guys in white coats, to clone little kittens and then try and have a sense of humour by giving the little Experiment a cute little name, like Copy Cat. I give them credit though, there proving themselves to be the morons I knew they would be. Consider this, in 1998, they cloned a sheep, now in 2002 their cloning cats. I think the cat should come before the damn sheep and a sheep could be shaved and used more than some ordinary house pet. But who cares right? Lets now worry till, some distant country clones murders and use's them in the army, making their numbers much bigger. Don't worry we'll counter it with our Robot's trained by the brutal State Troopers, that threaten us each and every day, all for a quota and don't forget they hate your black ass too. The world's an interesting place; I love politics and all that useless garbage of what's going on in our country, the great U.S.A alone. I'm not gonna speak of all this sh!t though, you, yourself should know your world and what's going on in it, if you don't, your missing out on some very hilarious stuff. I promise, if you have some kinda sense of humor, you'd laugh if you took some time out of that busy little schedule and had enough common sense, to see, what's really going on and why. But enough of it all, cause I think my matches and my life hold more meaning then George W. Bush choking on a Twix. It's a damn conspiracy, but seriously enough now. Sensei is up at the pitchers mound now. I know nothing about the man, so Sensei, guess you should just know I am ready for a war, this Sunday, not a match. I'm ready to push myself that extra step, but wait there's more. I'm also ready to Hurt...Mock and Destroy you anyway possible as well. But I always do that, so that's nothing new. Sensei, I don't know you, I don't wanna know you. You ever see a war movie? think of that, but just us, just me and you. Thousands of people watching, being witnesses and millions at home watching. Can you see the smiles on their faces, as we beat each other to no end? Can you see the glorious laughter that will echo in their homes when we spill each other's blood? I can, I've seen plenty of times in my seven year career doing this and still to this day, a walking scar and still not a man, no more, no less then seven years ago, just the same. Still loves doing this, but not for the fans, not for the money, cause after the all the hospital visits and nights in jail, I can honestly say, I have no money. I don't care though, just like I don't give a Sh!t about you. How do you beat a man that doesn't care? What could you possibly do to me, that hasn't been done before? Don't even try to tell me what you can do, cause I as well as anyone else, know that the human mind is endless in limits. And I know you can come up with some stupid ass plot, but just as it is, stupid you'd only be giving me the right to say I did it. Now why would you wanna go and do something like that for? Now excuse me, I have an article to read as it is quite interesting. Scene: Lowering his head, as he removes the magazine from his pocket, Crimson goes back into flipping thru the magazine till he comes upon the front cover article. Minutes later, looking up at the camera and then back down at the magazine for the first time of many quick glances to come. Finally, Crimson puts the magazine back in his pocket, as he keeps his focus on the camera. Crimson: Shensui, I just hope for your sake, your ready for the Sickness, I hope you are, cause I know that this time I honestly am. Don't let the past that you know of me fool you, we all make mistakes and well as humans we tend to do that...Sorry GOD, please don't punish me cause I disregard your perfect creation, in your form as a damn joke. Yeah, GOD truly is a great guy, he takes my verbal abuse and he blesses me with the chance to nearly kill you and might I add, get away with it. That is till I can't identify the difference between the ring and reality and hurt innocent people and spend the night in my second, or third homes Jail and Hospital. The ring being my first and most spend home, other than that, I'm a nomad, just wondering around and loving it. But not nearly as much as I love that ring. Oh yeah, a place where I am gonna hurt you, a place I wish I could be right now, instead of waiting till Sunday night, for you Shensui. The silence is deadly, as I wait for you, the less you hear of me, the more your gonna wonder exactly where am I. I tell you now, my strength is my calm attitude, the one I am gonna use when I naturally rip you apart with. Yet exploding in a rage, despite the calmness, like a bomb I am ticking and ticking and I am set for Sunday night on self destruct. Shensui, if I can't take you down, which is a pretty big f*cking IF, considering it's rare for me to lose twice in a row, but has been done before, you'll see first hand, that if I can't have you, I'll take someone else instead to satisfy my hunger for pain, upon my brother's and sister's of FAW. I'll try my best not to let it get that far, cause all I want is you. I'll be contempt for the time being with just your mind, body and soul being tested, right before your eyes on Sunday night. I don't know why I am talking about the possibility that you should escape, for there is no escape. I'll let Renegade think he has for the time being, let him think he's safe at night, cause that's when your the weakest, when you don't expect it! There is no hope for you though, Shensui, your my example, of why you don't get away in the first place. The pain that Renegade should have endured by my hands, will rest upon your shoulders soon. I'll see you soon, but not soon enough for the longer I wait for you, the more I think about your demise and the more that happens Shensui, the closer I am to having it realized. Soon your gonna be in that ring with me, ready or not here I come! Blessed Be! (scene fades)
