Flusing a dog down the toilet

17th January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Flusing a dog down the toilet 9th Wonder Of Foam Furiously Addictive Wrestling Roleplay #147 Date: 17th January 2003 Location: Foamy Hills New Jersey … The Foam Factory – The Lounge – Friday …. (Lars sits in the sofa reading a book) Lars: Holy living f**k, they put a man on the moon? Well what do ya know; next they’ll have pre-sliced bread, and a pile cream that works. (The phone rings) Lars: Hello? … Lars: Hello? Who is this? … Lars: Well tough luck bye! (Lars put the phone down. He continues reading. The phone rings again) Lars: Hello? … Lars: Hello? Look answer me! Foamy: Lars dammit! We are members of a secret evil organisation hell bent on EVIL! and destruction. You are supposed to say the code word when you answer the phone. Lars: What is the code word? Foamy: Cunninglingus. Lars: What? Did you just say cunninglingus? Foamy: What? I said cunning linguist you tit. Get down to the evil war room, we have some things to discuss. …. Evil war room – minutes later …. (The world renowned German scientist Schmidt and Latin singing sensation Enrique Iglesias sit around a round table, Foamy sit stirring some coffee. Lars enters) Foamy: How nice of you to join us! Lars: Sorry I was reading up on the moon. Foamy: Marvellous, did you find anything of use to our cause? Lars: No. Foamy: I see. Lars: Well actually there was one thing. We could tell everyone it is made out of cheese. Then millions would flock to the moon in search of cut price cheese, but in fact be standing there with nothing but moon rock, oh how we would laugh. Foamy: Not really our kind of thing Lars. There is a more pressing issue to discuss, that of FAW and its flagrant mismanagement of my title defences. Enrique: Fear the mole! But you are the champion, you should be able to defend and defeat anyone they put in your way. Foamy: And I will do that boy. But the fact is that this Lisa chick doesn’t know her headlock from her arsehole. She is giving long gone relics from the past title shots that they don’t deserve. Does anyone have any plans? ( A long frosty silence sweeps over the room) Foamy: I see. Well look this Lisa bird thought it would be fun to make me defend my title against The Omega Fro, or as some like to call him: Bob. But to further rub salt into this little thing she had in all her infinite wisdom decided to make me and Bob team up against Ha Ha Klown and Hardcore Hoefel. Enrique: What’s the problem with that? Foamy: She’s taking the piss, that’s what’s wrong. She knows damn well there is no-one here worthy of having a title shot so she calls up people from FAW’s past who think because I have a peculiar name I can be beaten. When I won this title no-one aside from the Klown deserved a title shot. So knowing that I could well have When Worlds Collide off she decides that it would be bad for business. So she kicks the Klown out of things and puts another has-been in his place. Lars: Why don’t you call her? Foamy: Good idea. View screen!!!!! (Enrique’s chair lifts off the ground into the air as beneath him a giant teleconferencing screen appears, Enrique now has to balance his chair on top of the screen for fear of falling off.) Enrique: Fear the mole? I fear the floor! Foamy: Shut up. (Melissa Farrow appears on the screen) Foamy: Ah Melissa my dear girl. Could you put that chick Lisa on screen for me? Melissa: She’s not in. Can I take a message? Foamy: Tell her I can see what she is doing. She thinks she can force the FAW championship off me? She thinks that by trawling through wrestling’s nursing homes she can wrench the championship from my waist? Tell that bitch that I know that she doubts Omega Fro’s ability to defeat me, which is why she has called in Tom the Bomb, Jon The Bomb, Jeff Harris, Randy Rocker, Skipster and several mystery men. I’m beginning the think she doesn’t like me. Well I say bring them on Melissa. Tell Lisa that she can bring Satan himself into the arena, she can fill that rumble with 8-foot high orcs if she wants but the fact is that at the end of that PPV I will be world champion. After Extreme Beating 3 I will be champion and for a very long time to come I will be FAW champion. Trawling out these FAW legends to try and scare me doesn’t work, when I defeat their sorry opportunist asses it will only add to my legend. And if she thinks that me and the Fro can work together Sunday night she’s got another thing coming. I learned a long time ago never to trust anyone. I’ll prove to the Klown, to Hoefel and to the Fro why they should never have re-signed to FAW because the FAW they knew died a long time ago, this is my FAW now. Have a good afternoon Melissa. Have a Foam Filled Day!
