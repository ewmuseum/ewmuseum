Has Crimson gone mad?(rp2)

16/10/02
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Has Crimson gone mad?(rp2) Crimson Lord Furiously Addictive Wrestling Roleplay #12 Date: 16/10/02 Location: Unknown Discription Crimson Talking Crimson's Mind Talking [Fade In] [The same living room where Crimson at least knows he's worth something to something comes into view. The room basically looks identical to the way it did two nights earlier. The couch, the chair, the coffee table, the TV, the phone on that little tiny wood table, the prone body, the pillow, nothing has moved.] [Ring.] [Meh.] [Ring.] [Go away.] [Ring.] [Sleep...Duke...] [Ring.] [Crimson jolts up as the ringing of the phone finally gets to him. Crimson starts to stand up out of his chair when the machine kicks on.] Crimson On The Answering Machine: Either I'm not here or I don't want to talk. Leave a message and I'll call you back later. Duke On The Answering Machine: Hi Cy, it's Duke. I haven't heard from you in awhile so I figured I'd check up on you. I heard you had a rough time during your matches the other day and I'm afraid something is wrong. I really want to talk to you so I'll try back later. gotta go train cya [The help is right there.] [Just reach out and touch someone.] [Crimson stretches a bit before hoisting himself out of his chair to walk toward the phone. He clumsily grabs hold of it begins to dial.] [Beep...Beep, Beep, Beep...Beep, Beep...Beep.........Beep...............] [Crimson looks at the phone again and then quickly hangs it back up. He looks around the room once before taking the few steps needed to make it to the front door. He unlocks the deadbolt and then turns the knob letting the cool summer evening air in. After a few seconds, Crimson slips out the front door of his trailer and walks out into the front lawn. He looks around for a few seconds, but the street is deathly still. After glancing up and down the road, he gazed toward the zenith, but the stars seem to offer him little comfort. Several moments pass before Crimson swings ninety to his right to head toward the nearby park. He walks through the small, unkempt hedgerow that separates him from everything else before crossing the small park road. He walks through a small field, staggering just a bit as he does before finally stopping in front of ramshackle park bench. He sits down on the horizontal wooden slats and begins to think to himself for a bit.] [He got what he wanted, he finally started his payback on Him and the rest of FAW.] [He was so close to leaving the company after being humiliated by being jobbed.] [He was so close to call it quits.] [He was so close to finally breaking through after dominated the first few rounds of the tournament.] [He was so close to having real friends before Raza and Renegade got injured.] [He was so close to ending the feud with Darkness on top.] [He was so close to not being signed to the FAW at all.] [He was so close to getting himself a Heavyweight Title shot in the FAW.] [He was just so damn close.] [And so damn far.] [Anarchy!.] [Crimson leans back and looks around some more. His eyes seem to glaze over, seem to leave us before they snap back, staring right into the camera. The camera gives us a good shot of his face and it's not the same we have previously seen. The swelling around his eye has diminished some. He whole face is starting to return to its normal flesh color. The stitches in his forehead have been removed, but the gash is still noticeable as its not fully healed. His eyes just aren't the same. Whether they're somewhat warmer, or just not as cold, it's hard to tell, but they're not the same.] [Must be another face injury.] [Crimson allows once really hard before looking into the camera and attempting to speak.] Crimson: Why? What is it with me? Why the hell can I not achieve a damn thing? It's as if everything I've done here in the FAW since I've been here and everything I've done even before that was just the smell of the carrot in front of my face. I know it's there and I can smell it. I know that I want it. I salivate at the thought of actually achieving something, but yet, I can't ever get anything. [Voice hasn't changed.] [A victory or a tragedy?] Crimson: I guess the Match this Friday is just a culmination of everything I've tried to do here. I want more. It's just like a Drug addiction. When you start to do well, all you can do is want more. Everyone knows this fact, but they just fill themselves up with feelings of pride and cockiness to protect themselves from the fact that they are vulnerable. Nobody can succeed all the time. We all know this and we are quick to tell each other that. But when it comes to ourselves, we don't want to admit we can't go out there and win every single match. We are not perfect, yet we keep telling ourselves that we are. And just on cue, I started to do what I warned everyone else about. I told them when you start to do good, you suffer the biggest setbacks that you will ever face. When you climb all the way to top of the mountain, it's one hell of a fall to the bottom. But when you just start that climb for the first, second, third, whatever number time, it's not that bad of a fall when you mess up. You're so close to the bottom that when the bottom falls out of your ego trip, you really haven't moved very far at all. That's what I tried to tell everyone and then look at what I did. I got to the final eight and when I actually began to feel like I had a chance to come out on top, I had to lose it all. Crimson: But why all this happened? Was it better this way? Did that lead to my joining to the Dark Nation? Did my hate drive me nuts, that I just wanted to get to the top and bring chaos which has set me back all my career? But can’t I just put my past behind, and just say, Don’t worry, be happy , can’t I forget about Raza. Can’t I move on, and enjoy my Future? Haven’t I become the mock of everybody in OSW, oh here it comes the FAW reject, CRIMSON!!. Wait what am I saying, I aint no reject, I’m just a @#%$ pathetic. Oh yeah, Raza I enjoyed the beating I gave you. Crimson: But I can tell you this Raza. I'm just as lost as I've ever been. I don't know why I can't seem to get my career back on track and I just don't know why I can't seem to get something that means anything to me. I don't know what to make of it. One thing I do know is that it's not the work of what people call destiny. That much, I'm positive of. Destiny is just a huge load of @#%$ that means nothing in real life. When something good happens to someone, they say it was their destiny. I guess we were just destined to meet that night. I guess I was destined to rot away . I suppose it was destiny that has taken so much away from me. Destiny is nothing. We create our own destiny. So a man and a woman that got married just happened to meet on the pier one summer night. It was destiny that brought them together, it was their own choices. Maybe they wanted to walk the pier for exercise. Maybe they wanted to see the ocean at night. Maybe they just wanted to get as far from civilization as they could without getting wet. Whatever the case was, it happened because they made it happen, not because it was just supposed to. And if something bad happens, it's not destiny's fault either. Maybe if I would have been able to capitalize on one of the few breaks I got earlier in my career, I wouldn't be here. Maybe if I had lucked, But it was all my fault. There were no reasons that I couldn't control that made me turn out this way. I could've found a way to get out of that house sooner. I could've ran away sooner. I could've killed myself to take me out of this nightmare for good. I could've turned off the TV so that I wouldn't have to listen to Raza's underhanded comments. But with all of these solutions, I didn't. I chose to have things how they were. I was probably wrong in my choosing, but I still did choose these things. Nothing called destiny chose them for me. Crimson: But just like there was no destiny to determine my past, there will be no destiny to determine my future. If I come on Friday and decide to take Raza down, destiny wouldn't have made me do it, it would've been my own anger and rage that helped me chose to do it. . Maybe the rest of your friends will turn on you. Maybe your career might fall farther down than you could ever imagine. Face it , there are all things we need to be scared of and it doesn't make you any less of a man to be worried a bout it. It happens to the best of us. It's just a natural reaction to everything. Sure, there is an outside chance that we could be seriously hurt or even killed doing something as trivial as crossing the street or taking a shower, but those fears are understandably pushed aside, but this match what I can do to you goes beyond that. I'm not saying anything bad will happen, but I just want you to know it could and putting up defence shields to numb yourself to these fears won't change any of that. I convinced myself when I first began to wrestle and now, only a couple of years later, I've lost friends and have almost lost my sight, the use of my limbs and my life. [Crimson puts his head in his hands and just breathes for a few moments before grabbing his head once again. Eventually, he stands up and begins to the slow, labored walk back to his house. As he crosses the divider hedgerow, the camera watches him for a minute before panning toward the horizon.] [I guess it's time to start climbing back out of the hole.] [But this time Crimson isn’t alone, he has the Nation to back him up.] [Hopefully, Crimson can at least get himself to a respectable level just like he has done so many times in the past.] [He's just too damn hard to keep down I suppose.] [You know what I mean right?] [Because after all...] [Weebles Wobble But They Don't Fall Down.] [Let's see if that same philosophy applies to anyone this week, shall we…Blessed Be…] [Fade Out]
