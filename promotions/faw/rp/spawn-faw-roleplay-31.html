Spawn — FAW Roleplay #31

captured 31 July 2003

SpAwN--The Hell Master \~~~~~~~(opening scene)The scene opens in a dimly lit small auditorium that looks like it could hold a few hundred people at most. Glancing around the room we see rows of steel folding chairs set out around an old ring, which has been set up in the middle of this small building. We move closer to the ring and see a man sitting on the side of it, on the apron. As we get closer we see that it is Spawn. He is wearing a pair of blue jeans and an old NGW shirt with the sides cut out. He is looking down in his lap at his newest piece of hardware, the World Heavyweight Title a trophy just given to him by FAW for winning the world title 3 times. He stares at it for a few more seconds as the cameraman gets into position in front of him. Then with his gaze still fixed on the gold strap in his lap, he begins to speak. Spawn: You know I grew up wrestling in buildings like this. I wrestled in front of crowds even smaller than could fit in this building. I used to bust my ass in front of an audience of only a hundred or two. Back then I thought that if I wrestled hard enough, that people would notice me and that I would move on to bigger and better things. So, I would come in and work really hard each day, and I would put on some good matches. I paid my dues, hanging around the lower end of the card the whole while, and thought that if I just minded my self than glory would inevitably find me. Back then I was oblivious to the backstage egos and to the mind games. I was blind to the politics being played out in front of my very eyes. I was just a young man back then and I was naïve about the whole business. I was happy to just come to work and wow the fans and then collect my meager paycheck at the end of the night. Better things were coming, I kept telling myself, and I waited. There is a slight pause from Spawn, who remains motionless looking down at the belt this whole time. Without looking back up, he begins to speak again. Spawn: I waited for ten years for something better to come along and nothing ever did. I wanted to tell myself that I had been unlucky and that things were bound to look up soon, but even I knew the truth of what was going on. I knew that there were people on the top that wanted to stay there. They didn’t want some new guy like me to come along and wipe them from their precious main event spot that they had been at for a decade. I continued to impress around the country at the bottom of the card. I was a curtain jerker. No one gave a damn about what I thought. They all just wanted me to stay where I was at and smile about it. And for ten years I was dumb enough to accept that. I didn’t know the full truth about what was going on behind the scenes and so I remained at the lower end of the pay bracket for a decade. Then I thought things were picking up, but I was wrong. Again there is a slight pause from Spawn, with his eyes still glued to the World Heavyweight Title. He doesn’t so much as blink as he begins to speak once again. Spawn: I got a job at this fine company about a year and a half ago and I thought that maybe, just maybe, things were beginning to look up. Oh how wrong was I. I was played for a fool yet again. I was left to rot in midcard hell for the better part of a year. Sure, I won gold and held it longer than anyone ever did. But what did it mean? Nothing. It meant jack shit because I had nobody decent to challenge for the belt. A little while after I lost it though, I was in the spotlight, or so it seemed at the time. I even got a World Title shot against Bryant Shaw. I carried his talent less ass to a watch able match, but it didn’t matter because I was the victim of a conspiracy. I wasn’t in a World Title match; I was set up for an elaborate beat down. I was merely being toyed with. They were dangling the carrot in front of my nose to keep me here, but they never had any intention of making it possible to get. Once again I was screwed. Again Spawn pauses, with his eyes still on the gold belt in his lap and not straying elsewhere, even for a second. A short time later he begins to speak. Spawn: That has been my story. I have been screwed with and neglected time and time again. I have been toyed with over and over again despite my obvious talent. I have remained in the lower part of the card for eleven years, only flirting with the main event spot lately. None of my main events have been fair matches for the World Title. They handed me the Tag Titles for a week in some scheme to keep me happy. They always gave me shots at worthless titles but they never gave me the opportunity that I had earned through eleven years of blood, sweat, and tears. Sure, I was given matches against legends, and they no-showed. Every marquee match I have been in has meant absolutely nothing because no one had the balls to face me like a man. Everyone was and still is afraid of me because I make people look like morons in that ring. I hurt people and I like doing it. That kind of thing intimidates people. Maybe that’s part of the reason, no excuse, for not giving me a true title shot. What a bunch of crap. I don’t get a shot at the most prized belt in wrestling because I’m too good. Spawn chuckles a little bit, still looking down at the belt in his lap. A few seconds pass uneventfully and then he begins to speak once again. But this is no laughing matter and it was only becoming an even worse trend over time. Because of this, I was forced to take matters into my own hands at the first episode of Battle Of The Titans. No one was giving me a shot at the belt, despite me being the most talented performer on the roster. No one cared about my talent, they only cared about the so-called “proven” talent. Well if eleven years of busting my ass in some of the greatest matches ain’t proof enough for them, then I don’t know what the hell is. The only way I was getting the World Title is if I took it and that is exactly what I did. I took matters into my own hands and no one is taking this thing away from me. The people that were put past me time and time again will now cower in their pathetic fear of facing me. No one wants to face me. I don’t blame them frankly, but it is a little pathetic. from there I went to FAW and finally conquer my dream not once, not twice but three times you hear me every one in NGW 3 time FAW world Champion A few seconds of silence follow those words and then Spawn, eyes still on the gold, begins to speak again. A big part of holding me back over my time here has come from our wonderful President, in HCW. She has been a major force holding me back for all of these months and that was partly why I sent her to the hospital at Battle Of The Titans. Deviant just happened to be there with her, participating in some worthless ceremony, so he got the same thing as she did. Anyone who gets in my way will get what these two got at Battle Of The Titans . They will get beaten into a bloody pulp and then hospitalized. If anyone wants to test that out, then they are more than welcome to challenge me. Although with the current roster of cowards and has-beens here in NGW, I am not too optimistic about a good challenge coming from this. There is one poor unfortunate dick that must face me at the next show, however. A pause follows from Spawn as his eyes look up from the belt and into the camera a few feet in front of him. A second or two passes and then he begins to speak again. This man’s name is Death. He got lucky coming into NGW for the first time and getting a fight with me it means some one up there must like him, so now everyone is jumping up and down saying that he needs a title shot. Funny how they always overlook what is right in their face as far as talent is concerned. They give him this match for the belt and I am sure he is going to give one hundred ten percent in this one, but that won’t make a bit of difference in the end. Because when it all comes down to it, I am better than this Death guy. I am a better wrestler and a better fighter. It all comes down to skill and well; he can’t hold a candle to me. Because of that and that alone, I will walk out of this coming Battle on this Tuesday show unscathed after an uneventful win. It is all that simple really. Maybe Death should be trying to shut up the latest man that thinks he is Satan rather than trying to take the legendary name from the best wrestler to have ever lived. He should set his sights a little lower is all. Now I know that management was just dying to give the guy a chance, but they screwed up, as seems to be customary of them. Death is not ready and after the massacre ends, everyone will know that. Spawn glances down at the title lying in his lap and then back up to the camera a second later. A smile comes across his face for a moment as he speaks to the camera once again. You know, I have dreamed about holding the title for years and years in NGW. Ever since I broke into this business, I have thought of nothing more than being a World Champion. And now, it is my time to be champion again, to conquer the world of wrestling like I did before when all I was, was world champion material. There is some technicalities to go through, but I am the number 1 man in this fed, make no mistake about it. I have waited eleven years to get here, and if it were up to management, I would have to wait another eleven just to get another World Title shot. They have this weird thing about showcasing talent. It seems that they are bound and determined not to give anyone with wrestling skill a chance. They never gave me a true title shot in the year and a half before this moment and they never would have. They would have always stacked the deck against me and claimed that I was getting the push I was deserving. That would be bullshit, because I ain’t got crap in the time I have been here. I have been lied to and spat on by higher ups and I am sick of it. Enough is enough, and it is time for a change. This change may scare some people though. Some may not like me representing this company, but they are going to have to live with it because I am going to win the NGW world champion and keep this belt for a very long time. There isn’t a thing Death, or anybody else can do about it either. Another pause follows as Spawn moves the belt to his right shoulder. He holds it there with his right arm as he looks back to the camera. A few seconds later he begins to speak again. It looks good on me don’t it? People had better get used to it here because when I finally win the World title here in NGW that is where it is going to be staying for a very long time. I have waited my whole wrestling career up to this point to strap this thing on, and I am not letting go of it to anybody in that locker room. Nobody is good enough to take this thing from me. So until someone with talent and balls comes along, the NGW title is going to to have a nice home around my waist once I win it. Devastation hops down from the apron, still holding the Worlds Heavyweight Title on his shoulder. He walks back to the curtain and disappears behind it a moment later. The camera turns back to the ring and in a few short seconds the scene fades away to black.
