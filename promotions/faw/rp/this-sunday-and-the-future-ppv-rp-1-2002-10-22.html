This Sunday, and the future.... (PPV RP#1) Damien Ryan Furiously Addictive Wrestling Roleplay #34 Date: 10.22.02 Location: Gas station, central artery, Toronto (the screen opens up in the urban part of Toronto, the sun's gone down but its still kind of busy, with the steets having traffic, its rush hour. The camera is set at a Exxon gas station off the busy artery then we see a navy blye pickup truck pull off and drive into the gas station lot. It pulls up to the gas tank, and then the door opens. FAW Hardcore Champion, Damien Ryan steps out of the vehicle and sets the tank to what he wants it, puts the nozzle in the car and begins pumping. He doens't seem to be in a good mood, then he looks up to the camera) Ryan: I feel so defeated right now. Started off with a clean five and zero record and it came to an end on Distortion 19. Foamy, I got to hand it to you, there was one hell of a fight, and you have to admit, but the better man won out there, and you know what... I respect that. We shared some awful but determined words all last week. We gave comments, critisism, insults, and more, but we also were on the same page. And I bet everyone out there knew it, and they were excited while watching that match. I will get my shot to fight you again in the near future Foamy, and next time you will not be so lucky. You've been around much longer than I have, hell I've only been in wrestling alone for about a year now. Ten of those months were training, the rest is my FAW career and my small traveling shows before that until I got my contract here. I'm young, I'm new, and I'm inexperienced, but look where I'm at Foamy. Only five and one, Hardcore champion....imagine what I'll be like in the future Foamy, it's scary isn't it. We will knock heads again. (he checks his price range but its only around ten, for he's intending to fill his tank) Ryan: Disaster Down Under, my second pay-per-view event. Last big event I defeated Neo for the Hardcore Title, now I've held it for a month! Now, on Distortion I came out during that number one contender match for a reason. To show each and every one of those clows what they are ready for, and it just has happened that The Duke was in the right spot and the right time, and picked up the win by pinfall. Duke, like me you are a rookie, you might have been in other organizations before though. I ain't. Yet the fact of the matter is, is that I've been underestimated since I've walked in here, you weren't here just yet, but I was raked second to last and was predicted to be driven right out of FAW. I was put in the FAW minor leage called Revolution Wrestling, I kicked serious ass there, and now I'm here, still kicking ass, and Hardcore champion. The point I'm trying to make is don't underestimate me Duke, that chair shot the other night was 0.5 percent of what your going to encounter this coming Sunday. (the gas stops pumping and he takes the nozzle out and puts it back. He closes the tank cover and walks into the store to pay for his gas. He walks to the counter and puts a fifty in front of the cashier. The clerk gives him his change back and Damien walks out back to his truck. Before he gets in, he speaks again) Ryan: Who knows what kind of stipulation they're going to put into this Hardcore Title match, Duke. Maybe you have experience in some extreme matches, I don't know. All I do know is that I plan on winning this match, because as I told the 9th Wonder of Foam last week, I'm worried about my record, and winning. Making my way up the ladder to the top of the game, where the big boys are and the matches that fans really pay their money for. Where I am right now is like the band opening up at a music concert. I want to be the show, the main event. I want my name on the tickets that the people buy! I am an ambition wrestler straight out from Canada, and you defeating me is not something not on my agenda, Duke. You will get a nice pure Canadian ass beating this Sunday, get ready. Lace up your boots, put on your silly ass contume, and your mask to hide that ugly ass face and get ready to be thrown around like a rag doll. You better learn some counter moves also, Duke, because there may be a couple holds you might be able to get out of, but don't count on it, especially when you feel your body burning when I lock my Burning Wings on you, and you will feel pain you have never experienced before! (He gets in his car and gets back onto the artery heading to his home. The camera then cuts to another inside Damien Ryan's pickup, on the passenger side seat and tapes him driving) Ryan: It seems like our FAW is hurting a little bit. We have a couple key wrestlers being released at the moment. I'm pretty upset that I didn't get a chance to face of a couple of them, one I already defeated twice, but it's a shame that these guys had to leave. And it's also a shame that I couldn't get a chance to fight the other two and show them what I could do. You see I'm on a mission folks, I was put through boot camp training, the hardest kind of training a human being can go through. I went through the coldest days of Canadian winters, and then flies down to Brazil and trained in the hottest summer strifes. That's only a little bit of what I did, but it's paying dues right now. I mentioned this last week, that the first day I came here I told people to keep in the back of their minds that I was one day going to be the top man in this federation, but not from the beginning. One of my head trainers was a man who was a masked character in a federation that went on from 1997 to 2000. He was at the bottom of the barrel his first couple of months, but soon worked his way to the top. He soon won almost every single championship in that league, and was ranked number one, from like twenty-seven to number one. He really enlightened me, and I know now that I will start at the bottom of the cards and then after time be at main event, like he was. I'm doing that as I speak, and I tell you now. Look where I am, I'm a lower/mid carder right now, but I will soon be up there. There are some guys who can step into the ring their first match and impress people so much that in weeks, they are main event. Now I, obviously, don't have that luxery. I was known as a Canadian crapper when I walked in, some scrub out of Rev Wrestling. The reports, and critics, slammed me. Then I was peaked at number four on the rankings, and in time...I will be number one. (He takes an exit off the artery onto smaller roads, he takes a few turns and then reaches a small quieter street with really nice house and he decreases his speed on that street) Ryan: This is my street so we have to cut this promo pretty soon. But all I'm trying to get across is that this Sunday will be the last match in which I am one of those guys that people watch, cheer, then forget about twenty minutes later. No, I'm going to be the guy with the giant posters planted on the walls of arenas, I'm going to be the guy headlined on PrimeTime Central. I'm going to be the number one man in this industry in the near future, and it starts this Sunday when I destroy Duke, and put Canada on the map for the FAW tour. I was checking the road list for the FAW and FAW is not planning on traveling out of the states and into Canada, you know why? Because the Canadian superstar hasn't been noticed yet, so when you see that FAW is traveling for a show somewhere in Canada, you'll know Damien Ryan is at the top of the game. (he reaches his house and pulls into the driveway. He opens the door and gets out but glances back at the camera) Ryan: Duke, be ready for Sunday, because you are going to experience something, you've never experienced before... you will step your Irish ass off that island and smell a little bit of fresh Canadian air when The Maple Leaf is beating your ass. (he closes the door and heads into his house as the camera fades out)
