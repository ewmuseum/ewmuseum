Realisation 1 'Golden Nine' Jeff Harris Furiously Addictive Wrestling Roleplay #261 Date: 8th February Location: Secret Volcano Layer, Hawaii :.:There was once a man, a man who was feared by many and respected by many more and this man had no fear at all when it came to pain. This man was known by most as a bad ass, a man with forceful power and someone who could get the job done. His name was “All Fkn That” Jeff Harris, or in other words… Me! But this man who lived his life to the fullest has been converted to a joke, someone people like to laugh at. I am no longer feared and very few respect me for who I am and what I have done. I know what is going on, I had been brainwashed by Jean Deas and Pete Harris to become one of their minions but that is not the case now. I’m not going to portray the “funny guy” much longer, when the time comes people will realise that All Fucking That never did die it was in fact hidden deep down inside. The time is near for me to show people my full potential and show that some shoulder injury made by that whore British Bomber will not slow me down. I will be one of those people who other people will look up to once more, but for now I will concentrate on getting away from these freaks and geeks then try to re-establish my life:.: .::The scene comes into focus in a golden room in the Golden Volcano of Hawaii. The room is deathly quiet, and the gold seems as if it would swallow everything whole if it weren't for the slim rays of the moon castings its radiant glow. The silence interrupted by a sudden crash and series of screeches and hisses. The camera jumps up in fright and quickly searches for the source of the sounds in a frenzied panic. Shortly after the camera comes to rest on a pair of beacons, shining light green in the golden corridor. However, the beacons turn out to be the eyes of an alley cat, and not only that, but they aren't even locked towards the camera. Instead, they focus on another cat, a scrawnier version of the former, with more traces of grey in its fur and a look of hunger in its pointy ribs. The camera lets out a loud sigh of relief as a nasally voice can be heard from behind muttering at the cats and cursing for them to leave. The camera turns towards the source of the voice, aka Pete Harris who is wearing his standard tracksuit bottoms and dirty vest. This one is brown with chocolate and beer stains all down the front. Pete looks up at the camera and mutters again before making a custom seat out of an overturned drum. He doesn't bother moving over to allow the cameraman to take a seat; instead he reaches into his pocket and removes a pack of cigarettes. He taps the carton with his hand and pulls a cigarette out in his lips. With a flourish of his hands he has a Zippo alight. He holds it to the cigarette and takes a deep puff before exhaling. He then offers the cigarette to the cameraman who gives a quick negative nod::. Pete Harris: Holy shit, did that ever scare the crap out of me! Stupid, fricken alley cats. What the hell are alley cats doing in a huge bitch of a volcano in the first place? They deserve to be shot. Someone must have left the Volcano door open when… Come to think of it, Volcano’s don’t have doors and Hawaii don’t have alley cats… Spooky… HAHA! I LOVE IT! However I am wondering why Mr. Golden Nine wanted me to meet him in this empty golden room, do you know?! .::The cameraman gives another negative nod::. Pete Harris: HAHA! I LOVE IT! .::Footsteps are now heard, they’re heard coming down from the corridor, ever so closer, Pete Harris looks out of the doorway, the cameraman follows showing Jeff Harris wearing a black tuxedo. He glares at Pete Harris who smiles back nervously. As Jeff walks closer to Pete, Pete backs off::. Pete Harris: W-what up Jeff? You seem angry. Jeff Harris: Angry? That doesn’t even describe what I’m feeling at this moment in time, do you know why I wanted to meet you here in this abandoned room in this particular part of the volcano? Pete Harris: Uh.. No I don’t. Jeff Harris: Because no one can hear you scream for help when I drain the life of your fat disgusting body. Pete Harris: …J-Jeff? Jeff Harris: 6 Months! That’s how long you’ve taken out of my life, and although I can’t get those 6 months of my life back, I will compensate it by taking yours. Pete Harris: HAHA! I LOVE… Wait… what?! Jeff Harris: I know what you did to me back in October, you and Jean Deas brainwashed me and made me look like a joke to everyone in the world. Pete Harris: 009!? Jeff Harris: Guess again bitch. .::Before Pete Harris can respond Jeff Harris leaps at Pete and begins laying punches into his face, Pete Harris cries out in pain but no one responds, the cameraman stands back in fear as Jeff Harris opens a bloody hole in Pete’s head, blood begins to drip everywhere, Jeff Harris then picks him up and throws him against the wall, knee’s the back of his leg making Pete drop to his knee’s then Jeff proceeds to break his neck as the crack of the neck can be heard echoing down the corridor. Jeff gets up to his feet now a bloody mess; he stares at his hands and begins to smile, he flicks the blood from his hands at the cameraman then exits the room. The scene re-opens in Jeff Harris’ room, now clean and wearing clean clothes, he exits the room and begins walking to the control centre, as he reaches the door he can here Jean Deas shouting, Jeff opens the door finding Jean shouting at Jean-Bots telling them to get Pete Harris in here now. Jeff smiles and walks up to Jean::. “Golden Nine” Jeff Harris: What’s all the shouting for? Jean Deas: I need Pete! “Golden Nine” Jeff Harris: Why? Jean Deas: Because he hasn’t checked in since 3 hours ago, have y’all seen him? “Golden Nine” Jeff Harris: …No Jean Deas: Why did yaw hesytet? “Golden Nine” Jeff Harris: I think you mean hesitate… And I didn’t hesitate I just haven’t seen him. Jean Deas: You seem to have changed, do yaw need to sleep? Are you stressed? Maybe you should sleep with me tonight. .::Jeff raises an eyebrow::. “Golden Nine” Jeff Harris: Lets not and say that we did, the thing is I am changing and in a way you are not going to like. The thing is we will not be sleeping together ever again, the reason being… Well you will find out soon enough. .::Jeff begins to smile as he leaves the command centre leaving Jean Deas puzzled and tears in her eyes, the cameraman gives him an evil glare behind his back as the scene fades::. :.:Anger arisen and funniness was denied in my mind, things are going to change soon… Very soon a new era will begin:.:
