Getting what we want... (Distortion RP#1) Damien Ryan Furiously Addictive Wrestling Roleplay #67 Date: 11.6.02 Location: Pittsburgh, Penn. (the screen opens up in an automobile repari garage. The camera is moving and we see some workers working underneath some vehicles and some workers are underneath the hoods of some cars. We see some cars that arre fine and need probably a tune up or an oil change. There are ther cars with rather large dents in them and have pretty nasty accident marks in the front on the rear. The cameras move towards the back of the garage where the offices are and we see a couple of men checking out a pretty familiar vehicle, its a navy blue pick up truck ands its up on the rack. Its all smashes up in the front and on the driver's side. We see a man in a suit and FAW superstar Damien Ryan standing there looking at it. Ryan is wearing a tight black t-shirt, and some raggy jeans, and his hair is the usual way, dark, wet and curly. The other man is wearing a suit and tie, he has light hair and its slicked back. It looks as if he was the service mangager of the garage) SM: Well, the truck definetly took a beating. Ryan: Well, it's been one hectic week, I'll tell you that. I mean, I've been trying to get to FAW studios but I keep getting side tracked by something or the other. SM: I guess I can understand where you're coming from. Ryan: You're a fan? SM: Oh hell yeah, ever since the beginning. Well, lets go take a walk to the est wing of the building, I think there is some there you might just like. (they leave the repair garage in through a set of double doors into a corridor. They get to an elevator and the service manager pushes the button) SM: So what the hell has been up with you? Whats this hectic week all about? Ryan: Well... (They elevator doors open and they both step in and the service manager pushes the floor button, the doors close) Ryan: To tell you the truth, I fucked up my hamstring last Sunday in my match, I did a pretty good job of keeping it a secret, by you know, not limping around and such. But people figures I wasn't up to par with my usual doings and the press and media caught on. I got a huge budget now, and a shitload of my salary is going to boost up my insurance plan to cover this. Now I've been in bthe hospital each day this week for over like fourteen hours a day gettings primary treatment with rapid recovery so this injury doesn't slow down my wrestling career, and to get it healed for Distortion this week, especially being the Hardcore champ of FAW, being in an inter-fed pickle with our old Sin, and and being in a PTC Tag Tounramnet with my younger brother. So I needed that speedy recovery. SM: Jesus.... Ryan: Yeah. (the elevator doors open and they walk into a showroom of nice fantastic expensive looking cards. They walk around and pass shiny metallic vehicles and walk all the way the end.) SM: Now I know your having these problems, but I wanted to show you something, probably not your taste, but something a little different... (The service manger shows him a Kawasaki Ninja motorcycle and it is a awesome, beatiful bike, shining white, silver and blue.) Ryan: Whoa, I can't afford this man, especially right now, with all this medical shit and- SM: You want to know where I'm from? Ryan: Where your from? SM: (chuckles) Good ole, beautiful Toronto, Ontario, Canada. Ryan: Get the fuck out here!? Man: Let's just call it a Canadian deal, alright? Cause remember, we always get what we want and need right? ~~~~~~~~~~~~~ (The screen then cuts to a freeway, about midday out and its totally empty, then you see a that motorcyckle come racing by the screen on the freeway. On the bottom of the screen it reads '45 MINUTES LATER', and it shows a few different clips of that bike zooming around the freeway. Then the driver rides off the road onto a wooden rocky path leaving smoke everywhere and it begins skidding and and slides sideways right before a rocky cliff. The camera shows a view of that cliff it looks like a good fourty foot drops into a flam resevoir. The shot zooms into the rider and we see him pull off his helmet and its Damien Ryan, and he sits on his bike glaring at the resevoir. He shuts the engine off and kicks back the kickstand and looks at the water somemore. The hot sun hits the water and sends a blinding glare to the camera.) Ryan: Well, it definetly has been one crazy, hectic week for me. but I've managed and I can say I am ready to get into this week's Distortion as a strong competitor. There are a few things I'd like to address first though... First of all theres Crimson Lord. Lord has that fight won from the beginning only because I failed to respond to him, secondly I was not one hundred percent. Now, i'm done with escuses, but Crimson, I do not have to prove to you that i can beat you because I have already in the past. My first career match. And trust me, I wasn't trained to do wrestle the way I did this week, even though it was a pretty good match in some people's eyes. Now, you can parade around with your silly little chumps, about how beat me, with those two clowns and that hideous lady of yours. Bout when you check back into reality Crimson, I'll be there waiting for you at the gates, and I'll will be there to give your punishment, along with those other nitwits of yours. Now on the outside of our little game, seems like our former Internation champion and superstar Sin, who deserted us not to long ago, traded sides and went to WIW seems to have a little problem with me. Last week I ran into Sin and ofcourse it didn't go off too well. And already there are press conferences being scheduled for questions to set a date for when, wheref, and how me and Sin will battle, and let this be known...There is not one FAW superstar, staff member, on one fan on the face of this earth who wouldn't want me to tear Sin a new asshole! So, inter-fed match-up coming? Hell yes. Another important matter at hand is the Duke. I lobe how the FAW staff sets up all these huge rumble matches and elimation matches for some scrum to sneak in a win and get a shot at my title. Then the next week they step into the ring and I kick their ass, they piss their pants and get them selves into unfortunate trouble. And Unfortunatly for Duke, a fifteen foot drop from a ladder into a set of tables was his fortune. And he payed dearly, and fiund out what it is like to be beaten, the Canadian way. Last but not, The last topic on my agenda for today is the tag team champions themselves, Rzarector and Renegade. (Ryan starts clapping) Ryan: Congradulations on your performance boys, now you get to step into the ring with a real wrestler. Oh, now don't get me wrong, both Renegade and Rzarector are both incredible athletes, they managed to be put together at random and become champions, but what a predicament! Both of have two be against eachother once again, battling for a title against the champion himself. Man, things couldn't get more heated up, unless your Syphon X having a lot of men naked around you...but finally the tag champs go at it. Now lets bring this to topic, you two weren't even allies at the beggining, you were two rookies trying to make a name for yourselves when you both ended up winning an elimination match to be number one contenders. You managed to work together, but can you manage to be against eachother? Hmmmm, huge question. Now I on the other hnad definetly have to think, am I going to have the shit kicked out of me by a tag team here in a handi-capped match, by two guy capable of working together? Or will I manage to get the upphand, take advantage, and make you two work against eachother. Question for the week boys, and this week we'll talk, and when Distortion comes, Canadiam air will be lingering...and there will be trouble for you two. And just like when I got this nice vehicle earlier, Canadian's always get what they want...and what I want is destruction of you two, and victory for me. (He puts his helmet on and revs up the ngine to the motorcycle. He gives a wave to the camera, spins up the tire sending smoke, dust, dirt, and rock everywhere and drives off back towards the free way as the cameta fades out)
