Spawn — FAW Roleplay #24

captured 8 August 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
SpAwN--The Hell Master \~~~~~~~(opening scene)The scene Foodie's Supermarket store in Boston, the place is well packed there are a lot of customers doing groceries, all of a sudden three weird looking guys come in the store with long trench coats one of them stand by the door , the other one stands behind one of the cashiers and the third goes to talk to the manager. crook 1: Hey how are you? You mind if I speak to you for a minute. manager: Sure what can I do for you? crook 1: It's like this you see this inside my jacket(showing the gun in the jacket) manager: Yes I see it and I don't want any trouble please. crook 1: Good man, if that's the case then this is what we are going to do. This store is being held up, look over there you see that guy by the door he is going to make sure nobody tries any funny stuff. Now if you look to your left you see the guy standing by that pretty cashier girl there well he is there just in case any body gets out of line she will be the first on to go. Are we clear on all of this say yes. manager: Yes It's clear. crook 1: Good, good man now let us both go to the back and get the money from your office, move. manager: Wait a minute how did you know I had money stashed away in my office? crook 1: Boy you are stupid like I am going to tell you that information, just do as you are told and no one gets hurt now move it for the last time. The manager goes inside the office with the crook opens his safe and takes the money out he then puts the money in a bag. The crook tells the manager to turn around, take a long rope that he had in his jacket out and proceeds to tie him up, talking to him at the same time. crook 1: Listen up you are to stay in this room do not and I repeat myself do not attempt to escape and call the cops. Because if you do I will shoot every one in this place and then come back for you and before killing you I will torture you. The crook tell the store manager to take a seat and leaves the office with a bag full of money he then goes to the front of the store and begins to talk to the people. crook 1: Listen up! No one make a move this is a stick up and we are robbing this place if any one tries any funny business we will not hesitate to shoot! This is what I want everyone to do empty your pockets, purse and get on the floor with your hands on the back of your head do this and no one will get hurt. Everybody listens to the crook and begin to empty their pockets and the women place the purse on the counters and empty them, once they do that everyone begins to lie down on the floor, the crooks take everything plus the cash register money and leave. Just like that it happens and it ends. The scene shows the crooks in their getaway car talking about their heist. crook 1: I told you it was going to work perfectly. crook 2: Yeah you was right on the money, but we better stop talking shit and get our asses home before the cops start coming after us. The scene shows the crooks in the apartment as they begin to count their money eat their food and start to joke about their heist. crook 2: Wait to go man we are loaded, how much money we have dogg? crook 1: Let's see 9, 10, 11, alright 11,000 dollars we are looking good. crook 2: It is good loot but we need more a lot more. And it's time for the big times. crook 3: What are we going to rob next? And are they going to be any killing, because I'm dying to kill some one. crook 1: I’ll tell you what we are going to rob next we are going for the banks man can you dig that. crook 1: Are you for real? crook 1: That’s right I’m for real and you know what we will start tomorrow, I can see us right now on top of the world when we become rich. So are you guys in or out? Both guys nod their heads in agreement. It is the next day and the scene shows Spawn coming in to the bank with Prayer they are about to make a withdrawal when all of a sudden the three guys that robbed the store the other day go in to rob the bank. Spawn: You hear about those three guys that robbed the food store around the area? Prayer: Yeah I heard about it, those are some sick guys. Spawn: Guess what don’t look too obvious about it but if you look to your left I think this place is about to get robbed by the same guys. Prayer: I see them. What should we do? You want to get the fuck out of here and get help? Spawn: That is not such a good idea I’ll tell you what we are going to do, we are going to go to the front and tell the teller to slowly press the button for help and then hope that the cops get here before anyone can get seriously hurt. As Spawn and Prayer prepare to go tell the teller the three crooks start to move in on the action, Spawn spots the movement and takes out a little knife from his pocket, while Prayer tells the teller to buzz for the cops, Prayer then talks to Spawn. Prayer: The chances of this happening with us in this bank is unbelievable I tell you it’s amazing. Spawn: Listen the cops should be here any minute. So let’s start planning on how we are going to survive this night. Prayer: What’s the plan? Spawn: Let’s attack them before they can begin the attack on us plain and simple. Are you ready? Prayer: I’m all for it let’s do it. font color="silver"> As the robbers are about to start with their plan Spawn and Prayer move in on the action at the same time the cops siren can be heard getting closer to the bank the crooks hear the sirens and begin to panic not knowing what to do. Out of desperation one of the crook reach in his pocket for his gun but Prayer throws his bag at his face knocking him down. The other two robbers see this and goes after Prayer, Spawn jumps on one of the crooks from behind and body slams him, while this is happening the cops come in to the bank as everybody freezes.The scene then shows the cops taking the crooks to jail as the camera crew and the reporters interview Spawn and Prayer. reporter: You guys must feel good about saving the day? Prayer: It did feel good and we would do it again if we had to. reporter: And Spawn how do you feel about all of this? Spawn: I feel justice was done here today and if everybody can get together like what happen here today their would be less crime in America today . That’s my opinion and that’s the way I feel. reporter: Has all of this taken your mind of your match for tomorrow against Omega Fro in his return? Spawn: I’ll be honest with you yes it did but despite all of this I am still ready for the fro man, I will win tomorrow because I know I can not taking anything away from Omega Fro who is considered one of the best in FAW but he has been doing nothing as of late and me well I have been preparing my self for everything that comes my way. And right now I do not see the Omega Fro as threat. And if he is it’s not a big threat. He just better be ready for the worst that’s coming down his way because hell is going to rain on his welcome back parade tomorrow. reporter: Before I let you go what do you have to say about the new kid on the block Juvenile calling all of the wrestlers in FAW chicken and bad names he even when as far as to insult you what do you have to say about that? Spawn: ha, ha, ha, ha, You know why I am laughing because that kid is funny he sure is and I do feel sorry for him because guys like JLC, The GREAT 1, Mortician, and Skipster are going to beat the crap out of him. But you know what I hope they do not beat him entirely so they can leave a piece of his hoody ass for me. Somebody needs to tell his ass that this is wrestling and not the guetto. So Juve you and your rap group better just let it be and do the next best thing and that is leave town before you really end up getting hurt. Be realistic and take a hike, you know you are not going nowhere in the tournament dawg. reporter: Well thank you for your good deed today and good luck in your match tomorrow against the Omega Fro at panic. Spawn: I don’t need luck wish the luck to the Omega Fro he is the one that’s going to need it when I give him the beating of his career and his life. The reporters leave, and Spawn and Prayer get their money and leave the bank as the scene fades to black.
