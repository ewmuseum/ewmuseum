Evesdropping On Crimson(ppv1)

22/10/02
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Evesdropping On Crimson(ppv1) Crimson Lord Furiously Addictive Wrestling Roleplay #33 Date: 22/10/02 Location: Minnesota (The soft drumming from the opening of Billy Joel's 'River of Dreams' begins to emanate over your television screen. We slowly fade up as the soft symbol tapping and the light bass drum is heard tapping. We see a familiar figure from behind, walking through a grove of trees. The red hooded cloak is a dead giveaway that we are following “The Wild Wiccan” Crimson Lord in a walk through this forest of sorts. He carries with him, as always, his crystal-emblazoned staff and his red bag, which was seen before as containing items for his spells and rituals. He rounds a bend of trees and we see a small river stretching out in the clearing ahead. The moon in the clear Minnesota sky is the only light shining upon the scene. It is dark; some might even assume it's the middle of the night. Billy Joel sings.} In the middle of the night I go walking in my sleep From the mountains of faith To a river so deep I must be looking for something Something sacred I lost But the river is wide And it's too hard to cross {Anyone who knows Crimson Lord well enough can predict his succession of promotional videos, this being the third always being a break from his long-winded talking towards the camera. We assume he doesn't even know we are watching, hence the title of 'Eavesdropping'. He walks towards the river, where it looks like he has already set up a type of camp. There is a large cauldron resting inside a circle inscribed with a heartagram. The cauldron is black cast-iron with three claws for feet, like something straight out of Shakespeare. Around the cauldron, with its fire lit and water beginning to boil within it, are candles of various colors that seem to be half-way melted, leading us to assume he's been working already for a while. Crimson walks towards the cauldron and looks inside, apparently satisfied with the boiling water. Suddenly, he drops his bag and digs his staff into the earth, leaving it standing upright. He removes his cloak to reveal nothing but a pair of red cotton sweatpants underneath. He rolls up the pant legs and walks towards the river, after taking a chalice out of his bag.} And even thought i know the river is wide I walk down every evening and I stand on the shore And try to cross to the opposite side So I can finally find out what I've been looking for {Crimson Lord walks straight into the river, pretty strange considering he's naked from the waist up and it is October in Minnesota. Obviously, the symbolism of the purification of the river is much stronger than the icy chill that he must experience by wading in such cold waters. He leans over and dips his face into the water, then wipes it clear with his large right hand. In his left hand is the chalice, which he dips into the water as well, filling to the brim. He then turns and walks out of the water with the bottoms of his pants still being soaked with water even after rolling them up some. He walks towards the cauldron and kneels down next to it, carefully placing his chalice onto the ground beside the fire.} In the middle of the night I go walking in my sleep Through the valley of fear To a river so deep And I've been searching for something Taken out of my soul Something I would never lose Something somebody stole {He reaches inside his bag of goodies and pulls out a pouch. He gently empties the contents of the pouch onto the earth in front of him. A various assortment of colored crystals and gemstones now adorn the green grass and brown dirt between him and the fire of the cauldron. He selects a brilliant looking orange crystal from the pile and holds it up to the fire, the only abundant source of light in the darkness. It glimmers in the flickering firelight as Crimson Lord places it inside the elaborately decorated chalice. He places the new concoction of crystal and water to the side as he stares silently into the fire.} I don't know why I go walking at night But now I'm tired and I don't want to walk anymore I hope it doesn't take the rest of my life Until I find what it is that I've been looking for {Crimson Lord stares into the fire and then blesses himself. He touches his fingertips to his forehead, his left breast, his right shoulder, his left shoulder, and then his right breast. With his blessing motion, he has formed a five-pointed star, or pentacle. He closes his eyes and takes several deep breaths.} In the middle of the night I go walking in my sleep Through the jungle of doubt To a river so deep I know I'm searching for something Something so undefined That it can only be seen By the eyes of a blind In the middle of the night {He opens his eyes and reaches to grab his staff, pulling it from the earth and burying it under his bag, hiding it from view. He reaches into his bag and pulls out another cup, this one similar to the chalice he placed water and the orange crystal into. He reaches into the boiling water with the cup and pulls it out, full of whatever he is cooking inside the cauldron. He takes it to his lips and lightly sips. He pours the rest back into the cooking pot.} I'm not sure about a life after this God knows I've never been a spiritual man Baptized by the fire, I wade into the river That runs to the Promised Land {He reaches over to the four candles that are lit and burning around the cauldron. He snuffs out the fires of each with his fingertips, one at a time. First he douses the flame on the purple candle, followed by the white candle, next goes out the green candle, and lastly the fire on the red candle is extinguished. He looks at the four candles and reaches into his bag to pull out a pack of matches. He places them in front of himself, on the pile of the remaining crystals, which he has yet to put back into their pouch.} In the middle of the night I go walking in my sleep Through the desert of truth To a river so deep We all end in the ocean We all start in the streams We're carried along By the river of dreams In the middle of the night {Suddenly, the music is removed and the sounds the can be heard are natural. There are the crackling sounds of forest animals walking over the fallen autumn leaves. The popping of a campfire fills your ears, the wood burning underneath the cauldron of boiling water and presumably herbs and the like. A heavy breath is lifted onto the air by our Wiccan friend. Crimson Lord takes a deep breath and surveys his ritual area.} 'Goddess, the holy Samhain has come and gone without my properly acknowledging the revival of your spirit. I humbly apologize not recognizing you in the correct manner and hope that this shall not dampen our relationship. I have been faced with a great challenge in my wrestling world and I must practice long and hard to defeat the skilled opponents that I face on Sunday. It has been but one day since you took the place on the throne of Nature from the Sun.' {Obviously, he's referring to Halloween as Samhain, one of the four greater Sabbaths of the Wiccan religion.} 'The God has died and gone to Summerland, eagerly awaiting his return and rebirth come Imbolc. Please be aware that I give you due recognition, Goddess, and have placed my staff out of your watchful eye and dedicate this ritual to you and you alone. Please forgive my tardiness and grant me the energy to complete what I find necessary for my success on Saturday.' {A light wind blows, pushing the fire up around the cauldron a little more than it had been before. Crimson Lord notices this and quickly sees it as a sign that the Goddess has heard him and responded.} 'Thank you.' {He picks up his matches and begins to re-light the candles. He lights them in the exact order that he had previously extinguished them; purple, white, green, and then red.} 'Goddess, I light these candles in symbolic form of the four men that will step into the wrestling ring on Saturday and vie for the right to be called the FAW’s International Champion. I grant the color purple in respect for Shensui Yusuki, honouring the knowledge that he is a very powerful man - even more so than myself. He has great strength and that may be his main asset to this battle. I note white to represent the ultimate truth and newfound purity that this man seems to possess. I give green to symbolizing his wealth and even his well-noted fertility. He is a great man in the material world, but the material world is not always so great. I, of course, hand red to myself. In this case I do not give it to myself to mark a part of my personality. Instead, I give myself the red candle in the hopes that you shall grant me the courage and energy I need to achieve a victory. I ask you to note all four of these and keep them in your spirit.' {He reaches into his bag and pulls out a small loaf of bread. He breaks it in half, not using his athame like he normally would as it is a phallic symbol of the God, which he has already said has 'died'.} 'I share this modest feast with you, my Goddess, and hope that it is enough to convince you that I need your energy to assist my spells and grant me the powers I'll need to win as well as give me the answers to the questions that I always ask of you.' {He places one half of the bread into the cauldron and allows it to disintegrate within the scalding hot water. He takes a bite of the other and then places it on top of his cloak, which is on the ground next to him.} 'After the seemingly unavoidable discourse and dialogues of words between myself and my opponent, I always feel perplexed at their inability to understand what I have said. Goddess, I tried to explain to Shensui Yusuki that one person of a particular group is representative of the entire group. He didn't grasp that if one member of the Model Society assists someone else, that the entire group is responsible for the actions. He fails to comprehend that mentioning other people that aren't in the fight are used as comparative pieces. I Believe that Saotome has no bearing on the fight, but makes it a point to say he's held titles- but I just don't see what exactly that has to do with the match either. People just really confuse me sometimes.' {He sighs.} 'He thinks I'm jealous of those of wealth, when I feel I've made it clear before that I'm not interested in those things. I wish for only the money I need to get by in life and Nature provides me with anything else I need. Even after I explicitly told him that I was quoting a critic, he still tries to call me unoriginal for saying those things. I told him twice that I was citing my sources and not claiming it was my own words. Twice he didn't understand and thought I was trying to insult him. I try not to be so blunt but that's just intentional 'selective memory' or it's just plain stupidity.' {He can't help but let a laugh slip out.} ' Sure, one can gain a lot of experience by winning matches and fighting a lot of opponents… but I think Shensui, through his unfortunate lack of success, has learned more than all of us. He speaks as though the only matches I've ever had were in the FAW and probably doesn't even know of my success in Japan.' {He shrugs.} 'But I apologize for placing my woes and confusion onto your shoulders, Goddess. Samhain is a time for reflecting on this past year and to discover what was good and what needs work. In my wrestling career, I did not have a year that would spark interest within anyone of notoriety. I have meager victories over Skipster, The Omega Fro, and Renegade. I have losses against Spawn, The British Bomber, and Tony V. I spent several months of this past year running from the assassin psychotic called Tom The Bomb. I didn't allow my talents to come to full fruition in the FAW and it gives people the impression that I am still a rookie. In my life, however, I have had nothing but success. After barely escaping from Minnesota in my first stay with a Glass wound in my shoulder, I found and embraced you Goddess. You helped me survive and gave me the strength to return to the wrestling ring. You took away the anger and rebellion for the world that consumed every fibre of my being. You made me whole. You alerted me to the danger of facing The Great One at Deceit 2001 and kept me from going. You gave me the strength to return to Minnesota once again. You have given me everything I have and make me who I am, and I am undyingly grateful for that. I ask of you but another favour and give me a sign please as to how I shall fare on this Sunday fast approaching… please.' {Suddenly, the wind kicks up a notch. The camera focuses on the four candles that Crimson lit and explained represented the two men in the title match on Old School Sunday. The wind dances with the flames and causes them to stir. The purple candle flickers out and no longer burns. A few seconds later, the white candle falters as well. Then suddenly, both the green and red candles go out at the same time.} 'I don't understand… but I suppose this could mean it comes down to Shensui and myself. I can deal with that and shall try my hardest to be the last flame standing. I have no fear.' {He reaches over to the chalice in which he placed the orange crystal several minutes ago. He plucks the crystal from the river's water and places it on the ground. He pulls the chalice to his lips and speaks.} 'Skill can only get one so far. I drink of this water and ask that you, Goddess, have released the luck from within the crystal into it and allow it to flow through my lips and into my own body and soul.' {He drinks. He places the cup back on the ground and smiles at the fire.} 'Thank you Goddess.' {He takes a breath and stands, eyeing the completely disintegrated bread within the boiling water of the cauldron.} 'Blessed be.' {Camera off. No more vision.}
