SpAwN--The Hell Master \~~~~~~~(opening scene) The scene Boston, Mass, a small town in the suburban area, it is a dark and windy night the trees leaves are blowing in the air, the streets are clear there is no one out side but only a girl and she is all alone a very bad time to be alone in the night with the creatures of darkness among us. As she walks the streets she hears sounds behind her and she begins to speak to these sounds. girl: Hello? Where is everybody? is there anyone in this town? Hello? Come on you guys where is everyone? Come out all of you damn it, I know this town can’t be empty is impossible, so stop playing games and some one answer me? All of a sudden something from the sky moves It looks like the shadows are coming together and forming what seems to be a human form. girl: My god, what’s going on? Those clouds are taking a funny shape huh what the! They seem to be coming at me, some one help me oh shit NO! please some one help me, (the girl starts to run and scream) aaaaaaahhhhh! Oh god some one help me. The girl is running hard for her life then all of a sudden she stops dead in her tracks. Spawn: Don’t run little girl, You do not need to be afraid. girl: Who are you? What do you want from me? I haven’t done nothing to you? Spawn: I know that but you are a lost soul, and I Spawn the redeemer need lost souls, now welcome to my world of darkness and be ready for a wild ride. girl: No way you are not getting me or my soul I’m out of here, so fuck off. Spawn: You can’t get rid off what you can’t destroy you can only join it, and enjoy it. As the girls tries to run off Spawn reaches out and grabs her by her hair in that instant the scene switches, to the same place that the girl was just in but this time there is no girl or Spawn just four boys talking amongst each other. And they are picking on a little boy. boy 1: Hey bubble lips what’s you hurry? little boy: It’s Derek, Derek Loomis and you know it, and I don’t know what I’m doing here some fool came to my house and told to come by, I just want to go home because this place is to dark and creepy for me. boy 1: (reaching out for his glasses) Not with out your specs your not runt. little boy: Geez… you watch to much TV- Land or maybe to much wrestling on TV you are starting to think you can woop every bodies ass. boy 1: How come we never know what your talking about chump, and how did you get assign to our pack in the first place? little boy: ’Cause I’m talking English instead of jock? Listen guys I thought you’d gotten this crap out of your system by now after a month I figure you stop picking at me already. boy 1: You’ll always be our punk to pick at bubble lips, our fall guy…haw haw! (they start to push the kid around) little boy: Hey whoa! ( the kid fall to the floor)this is too much even for you apes let me up! boy 1: Nah but I will let you have your stuff back after I cream you with it. As the bully was about to hit the little boy with the bag a hand reaches a grab his arm, the rest of the boys stare in disbelief. Spawn: That’s enough! boy 1: Who the hell are you? Spawn: The one that will proclaim your souls, the way you have treated this little boy shows me that you guys won’t be needing your souls right now and I could use them down there with me. boy 1: What do you mean down there what down there are you talking about? Spawn: Hell is what I’m talking about and we are all going to take a little journey to my world. boy 1: No we are not we were just goofing , we weren’t going to hurt him honest we weren’t. Spawn: Right and if you four goofs were smart you would have begun this in the first place, so now is time to pay the piper, hell has come to pick you up boys. boy 1: No way let run up out of here guys? The boys begin to run as Spawn is left standing with the little kid who he is helping get up from the floor, once the little is up he speaks to Spawn. little boy: Thank you mister but I’m pretty sure I could of handle the situation, it was pretty much under control. Spawn: I’m sure you had it under control but I figure I give you the extra hand anyways. little boy: what are you going to do to them sir? Are you going to kill them? They are not bad guys honest, don’t hurt him alright? Spawn: Don’t worry I wont you just go back home now and go straight to sleep, I’m just going to teach them a little lesson in respect ok. little boy: ok bye, bye. Spawn then begins his pursue of the four boys , he catches up with them and begins to punish them , one by one they fall till the last one the bully is left, Spawn still in hot pursuit begins to speak to him. Spawn: Come to me you can’t hide and you can’t get away, your friends are gone, you are the only one left, do not resist and face hell. boy 1: Leave me alone! Aaaaaahhhhh! There is no way you are going to get me! Spawn: Yes I am you are just within my grasp, it will only be a matter of second now. boy 1: No way! Get away from me! The boy runs as hard as he could, he reaches a black house, when he goes to open the door three dogs come out barking at him, then from behind Spawn grabs him and throws him inside the house, with the dogs, he then locks the door and leaves. The scene fades and switches we then see a FAW house show and the fans watching in disbelief at the FAW tron, they are all in shock of the events that are happening, for the reason that they can’t believe what has just happen in the Devils Pit, what they are watching is Spawn with his three dogs getting closer to the FAW tron about to talk to them and behind him the fans sees the kids all inside the coffins, thenas spawn reaches the FAW tron he begins to speak. Spawn: Did you like this edition of the Devils pit, did is scare you out of your pant, I'm sure it did as I hope it scared my three opponents at Summer slaughter. Skipster, Shawn and Spider, I hoped you paid good attention to this show I did this for you guys, you see there are somethings in life you do not take for granted, you do not play around with, and that is the rules of Spawn because if you do it can come back and bite you in the ass, the crying stops here boys, pull up your pants and tighten up your belts no more whining it is time to put up or shut up, excuses will not get you no where and the beating of your lifetime will be administer to you guys by me the hell master, so start your prayers early, pack your bags, kiss your girl friends for the last time and say good bye to your family, for your visit to the devils pit will be the last visit you will ever have. And when is all said and done the memory you will have of me is as your conqueror and how I stole your souls from your body and torture you guys till eternity. Now look behind me everyone as I torture this kids to shame. So let it be written…… So let it be done. Hell has spoken. Spawn goes back to the kids and starts to release his dogs as the FAW tron fades to black. The scene switches to Mike shouting out some words. Mike: OK! Cut that's a wrap good job people, get those kids out of here pay them there money and give them a ride home, cameras of camera one stays on and Spawn come over here. Spawn: What's up Mike? Mike: What did you think about the show? Spawn: Beautifully done you are one hell of a promoter, I should have came to you earlier instead of that dummass I had before, this is a great production I'm glad that you are directing it even though Pyro is gone it will be great working with you, but I hope you understand I'm not paying you as much as he did you are good but not that good. Mike: (holding fist in the air) I am the best! I fly like a butterfly and sting like a bee! Spawn: Man put your hand down, you fly like a injured bird and stink like an ass. Mike: Man I can already see this coming. Pyro is gone and I still going to get treated like a bitch. Spawn: That's right, now go get me some water. Mike walks away mumbling (that son of a bitch) and Spawn replies. Spawn: What did you say! Mike: Nothing, nothing at all. Spawn: Be careful I make you pay checks, their small but I'm sure you still want them, so don't get on my bad side. Mike: Well I didn't say nothing but I'll keep it mind. The scene fades to black.
