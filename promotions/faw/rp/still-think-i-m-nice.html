Still Think I'm Nice?

29/10/02
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Still Think I'm Nice? Renegade Furiously Addictive Wrestling Roleplay #59 Date: 29/10/02 Location: Orlando, Florida (The scene opens with a close up shot of Renegade’s face. He has a big grin on his face, obviously happy about something) Renegade: How’s your arm Foamy? (laughs) I told you I wasn’t nice, I told you I could be a nasty little shit when I wanted to be. And last Sunday I wanted to be very nasty (smiles). Did you hear that beautiful sound when I landed the DFA on you arm, which in turn was trapped between a steel chair. I sure did. And I loved it. I told you that I was born to hurt people. I told you all this before hand Foamy, but did you listen? Did you hell as like. You thought to yaself, ‘there only rookies, I can walk all over these boys without even trying’. But I wasn’t like that was it Foamy? Oh no, far from it. With out a proper tag partner you were up shit creek with out a paddle. You blabbed on and on last week about me making rookie mistakes. Telling me I shouldn’t have said this and I shouldn’t have said that. But it was you that made the biggest mistake of the week at DDU. You underestimated me and you underestimated Rzarector. You thought that your name alone, 9th Wonder Of Foam, and all your past attachments, would be enough to get you though the match against a couple of so called ‘rookies’. I may be relatively new to FAW, but I’m certainly not new to wrestling, or to tag team competition. So you cocked up big style when you decided to go one-on-two with me and Rza. I mean come on man. What the fuck was all that invisible Jim bullshit? You looked like a fool out there man. But not only that, you made a fool out of the FAW tag team belts by not taking the match seriously. You pissed on the belts, and you pissed on me and Rza with that whole Jim charade. And that’s why I tried to brake your arm last night. I try to make you take me and the damn match seriously. I also tried to changed your perspective on me. You don’t rate me much do Foamy? Is it because I’m a bit shorter the most people? Or is it because I’m not from this country? Whatever the reason is, I don’t think you make the same mistakes again. I may be only 5.10ft and 195lbs, but I know how to wrestle. I’ve grown up with people being taller then me, so I’ve learnt over the years how to wrestle against bigger guys then me. Remember that. (The scene then fades out slowly to black)
