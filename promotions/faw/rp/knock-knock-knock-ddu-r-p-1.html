KNOCK…KNOCK…KNOCK (DDU r/p 1)

24/10/02
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
KNOCK…KNOCK…KNOCK (DDU r/p 1) Renegade Furiously Addictive Wrestling Roleplay #37 Date: 24/10/02 Location: Renegade's House (Renegade is sitting down in his living room, watching a tape of his match Distortion 19. He is dressed in what he usually dresses in to hang around the house. Baggie pair of shorts, a crappy old t-shirt and a backwards NY baseball cap. But today he is also wearing a neck brace. The same one we saw I’m wearing last week after his match with Apocalypse. It’s just got to the part in the match where Crimson speared him from behind. Renegade cringes as he watches his own head bounce back off the bottom turnbuckle. He picks up the video remote and rewinds the tape. Pausing it at the point of impact. He puts the remote down again and stairs and stares at the Television screen, motionless. Just staring at the image of his very own head being violently snapped back. His eyes a permanently fixed on the screen. You can visibly see the anger building up in his face. His mouth tightens up, his eyes get wider and his hands clench up into fists. When suddenly there’s a knock at the door) KNOCK…KNOCK…KNOCK (The interruption breaks Renegade out of his trance like state. He turns the TV off and tosses the remote onto a near by chair before making his way to answer the door. He is trying to calm himself down as he gets closer to his front door. He opens the door and smiles when he sees who it is) Renegade: Hey Melissa. How’s things? **FAW’s front office called Renegade earlier to say that someone would be calling by later to do an interview with him. Once he got watching the tape he totally forgot about it** Melissa: Hey Ren. I’m good thanks. Is it ok if I come in? Renegade: Oh yeah, sure. Sorry. Come on in. (Renegade moves aside and lets Melissa in) Renegade: Can I take your jacket? Melissa: Thanks. Sure, here you go. (Melissa hands Renegade her jacket and he hangs it up out the way) Renegade: So you want to do an interview yeah? Melissa: Yes please. If that’s ok with you. You know, if your up to it. Renegade: Yeah I don’t mind. Lets go though to the living room so we can get started. (Renegade shows Melissa the way to the living room. As they walk though the hallway, Melissa admires some of Renegades wrestling achievements which are hung up on the walls. When they get into the living room, Renegade sits down in his usual chair and Melissa sits down opposite him on the couch. Melissa takes a tape recorder out of her bag and places it on the table in front of them) Melissa: First off I’d like to say thank you for inviting me into your home to do this interview. Renegade: That’s no problem Melissa. I’m just glad they didn’t send Hutton round to do this. (The both laugh) Melissa: Anyway. Shall we begin? Renegade: Yeah, fire away. (Melissa leans forward to turn the recorder on and gives Renegade a nice shot of down her top. Which Renegade can’t help but notice and smiles. Melissa, oblivious that she just gave Renegade a sneak peak, thinks that Renegade’s smile is down to the fact that he’s so please that she’s doing the interview and not Adam Hutton) Melissa: First question. How are you holding up? The last couple of weeks your neck area has taken quiet a pounding and I see that you’ve got a neck brace on. Renegade: All things considering I’m holding up quite well. Yeah my neck has taken a far old beating the last few weeks, but I’ve had worse injuries in the past. Broken bones, bumps and bruises. You name it I’ve had it. It’s all part of the job. It’s one of the many hazards of being a pro wrestler. Injuries happen. Melissa: Are you disappointed about the dispatcher of Apocalypse? After all, it was him that first injured your neck and there where rumours that you where out for revenge. Renegade: Yeah I am a bit disappointed about that yeah. But I can’t do nawt about it so I’m not gonna bitch and moan about something that I can’t change. Even though I do still have some unfinished business with that arse hole Apoc. People come and go in wrestling all the time. Shit happens! Melissa: Ok, good. Now onto last week at Distortion 19. You and Rzarector have become quite a good tag team over the last few weeks. Renegade: Well we are tag champs so you could say that yeah. Melissa: (laughs) Yes that’s true. You are indeed an odd pairing, but I seems to be working well. What are your feelings about this partnership? Renegade: When I first found out that I would be teaming up with Rzarector, I was dead against it. Personality wise were the exact opposite, but in the ring there are a lot of similarities. Were both very completive and don’t like to lose to anyone. We both want championship gold and we both give 110% out there every night. So in that respect we make a very good pairing, and it shows out there in the ring. Melissa: Back to last Distortion, you and Rzarector were involved in a none title match up against Crimson Lord and FAW new boy The Duke. Even though you and Rzarector picked up the win, it looks like the match took it’s toll on you? You’ve got your neck in a brace again I see. Renegade: Yeah I have. It’s just for precautionary reasons. My neck is know were near as bad as it was two weeks ago. I went of an x-ray on the Monday mourning just to make sure that there was no serious damage done to my neck. Which there wasn’t. I’ve always been a quick healer you see Melissa. You either heal quick or you spent lots of time out on the sidelines injured. I learnt that at a very young age. Crimson and Duke did indeed aggravate my neck after last Sunday, especially when Crimson got the cheap shot in on me an speared me into the turnbuckle. Which seriously fucked me up for a while in that match I can tell you. But I was able to exact some revenge when I nailed Dukey boy with the ‘Blackout’, and covered his sorry ass for the one…two…three. Welcome to FAW kid! (smiles) Melissa: Yes your ‘Blackout’ as you call it, is indeed a very effective finishing move. Renegade: Well I’ve been using it for over 3 years now so I’ve perfected it. It’s gone though a few name changes, but the resulting effect is always the same. They blackout. (laughs) Melissa: So on to Disaster Down Under. This will be your second Pay Per View here in FAW. Last month we saw you and Rzarector win your match to earn yourselves a shot at the tag titles. And now a month on your going to be defending the said titles, for the first time I might add, against 9th Wonder Of Foam and….well…invisible Jim! Renegade: I’ve paid close attention to Foamy since I joined FAW. He is indeed a good worker. But to be honest with you I feel sorry for the poor lad at the moment. He doesn’t seem all there does he? He’s lost something and he’s damned if he can find it again! (laughs) And if he wants to have Invisible Jim as his tag team partner then I say good luck to the lad. I hope it goes well for them both. (laughs) But having said that I’m not going to take Foamy for granted. I’ve wrestled against loony people before and they might not be all there but they can still wrestle. So I’m gonna treat Foamy and Invisible Jim exactly the same way as I do all my other opponents. But having said that I’m still very confident that me and Rza will pick up the win and retain out tag titles. Like I’ve said before, I plan on holding onto this belt of a long, long time. Melissa: Ok. That’s great thanks. I think I’ve got all I need. (smiles) Renegade: No problem. (smiles back) (Melissa leans forward, picks up the recorder, turns it off and puts it away in her bag again) Renegade: Would you like a drink before you leave Melissa? Melissa: Um no I’d best get back to the office with this and get it written up. I’ve got other appointments later on you see. Renegade: Oh ok then. Some other time then maybe. (smiles) Melissa: Yeah maybe. (Renegade shows Melissa to the door and hands her back her jacket) Melissa: Thank You. Right I’ll see you again. Thanks once again for doing this. Renegade: It’s no trouble. Anytime. Ok, I’ll see you this Sunday at Disaster Down Under no doubt. Melissa: Yeah no doubt. Bye Ren. Renegade: Yeah see ya again Melissa. (Renegade waves to Melissa as she drive off in her car then shuts the door to the bright Florida sun and goes back though to his living room. He sits down in his usual seat again, picks up the remote again and switches the TV back on. The video was still left on pause so we yet again see the image of Renegade’s head bouncing off the bottom turnbuckle. He continues to stare at the image for a few minutes. Once again falling into that trance like state that Melissa had broke hi out of only minutes earlier) Renegade: Revenge will be sweet. Revenge will be Renegade! (The scene fades out slowly to black as Renegade continues to stare at the TV screen)
