Spawn — FAW Roleplay #25

captured 13 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
SpAwN--The Hell Master \~~~~~~~(opening scene)The scene Cathedral projects in Boston it is the place were gangs members hang out and do their dirty deeds. The scene shows three guys just drinking beer and smoking weed, having a good time talking about the hood amongst other things. A black car comes in to the scene, when the driver gets out it reveals to be Spawn. He walks over to the guys and start to talk to them. Spawn: Hey guys how's things? Thug 1: Yo, yo yo Spawn The FAW world champion. What brings you to this part of town dog? and how is things man? Spawn: I have been good still have my belt and fighting like a champion. How are the guys you know the boys from the old days? Thug 1: Everybody is doing good dog, just trying to stay out of trouble. You know how it is. Spawn: Yeah I know just because of your way of life people judge you wrong. I feel you man. Thug 1: I know we can just be chilling here passing the time and out of nowhere some fool will get us into trouble. Claiming we were in a drive by or a shoot out. And you know the news they will corrupt the story as much as they can not giving us a chance to defend ourselves. Spawn: I hear you man, the way of life sometimes sucks. Thug 1: So Spawn what brings you the old school block dog? Spawn: I want to talk to you guys about someone? Thug 1: Sure who is this guy? Is he bothering you? Cause you know us we will pop a cap in his ass for you dog. Spawn: Actually you guys might have heard of him. He goes by the name of Juvenile the #1 stunna, he is a prick and runs his mouth a lot never shuts up always cussing and swearing. You guys ever heard of him? Thug 2: Yeah we heard of him. Spawn: Are you guys down with his gang. I believe is from the west side. Thug 2: No way man we do not get along with those west side motherfuckers, they are a bunch of fake thugs, we call them the wanna be's, especially that juvenile cat. Why do you ask anyway? Spawn: I have a match with that idiot this Thursday at panic, and I want to prepare myself for his ass. So I am doing a little studying on thug life, and I figure the best place to get some help on this subject is from you guys. I'm pretty sure you guys know everything there is to know about the hood and you all can give me a run down on it. Thug 1: Sure we can how do you want to begin? Or should I say what do you need to know? Spawn: First of all is this guy as tough as he say he is, because I feel he isn't so tough at all. I mean just looking at his wrestling record makes me laugh to think a guy like him would lose a match against a woman. Sheeeesh what an embarrassment not that I'm taking anything away from Her any female that gets into a wrestling ring against a man deserves all the credit in the world. But this guy comes in to FAW demands a world title shot and then loses to a woman what a bum. I don’t even know if I should fight this guy or just slap him around and treat him like a little prick that he is. Because he needs to show some respect when he steps into the ring with any wrestler of FAW in order for him to get respect and I don’t think that is the case with him. Thug 1: Man that guy has always been a punk he thinks he is the number one thug but that is just in his dreams, let me tell you the west side group are a bunch of sissies yo they can come over to our turf, try to do a drive by while we are at sleep and still couldn’t get the job done. That’s how whacked they are. Spawn: Damn he is that stupid, now that is a shame, no wonder he lost to a female for crying out loud all that shit he talked about how he was going to kick the great 1, ass, the omega fro, jlc, skipster, and mortician, I guess that was all bullshit just like his name all bullshit. I mean think about it how can anyone call himself the number 1 stunna and the fool can’t even stun a woman. Figure that one out will you. Thug 1: Man that is something you can’t figure out, I’ll tell you this playa that motherfucker better not come down here with his so called posse and try to shoot the house down cause it ain’t going to happen if he does me and my boys will be ready for his ass. And we’ll pop some caps on his ass so fast he won’t even have time to blink. Thug 2: You know Juvenile never comes around here, he knows the scoop we do not like fakes in this hood. Spawn: And what is this thing he got about him having all of the girls in the palm of his hand is this for real or is he just talking out of his ass again. Thug 2: Haaaa, man that dog is funny, he doesn’t have any girl, I know that nigga is still a virgin. Thug 1: That’s right pardner and if he is getting any is because the fool is paying for it. He is probably running out of money by now, and still haven’t gotten any (laughin) As Spawn and the guys are chatting away, three guys get out of a car and start to walk towards them, they stare at each other for a good second and begin to speak. Thug 1: Yo excuse me Spawn gots to take care a little business here (looking at the other guys) So what up pardner what you all need? Other gang: Yeah we need some smack you know what I mean, what you all got? Thug 1: We got a lot of shit all you need is to have the green playa. Other gang: Green is no problem let’s do some deals up in here.(the two thugs go to a corner to there deals while Spawn waits with the other guys) Thug 1: Alright pardner good doing business with you easy money is what I call this, now I see you all lataz peace.( the guys get back in their car and drive off as Spawn begins to speak to his boys) Spawn: You boys sure do make a lot of money unlike that juvenile delinquent number 1 bonehead? Thug 1: So what’s up Spawn you want some of the action dog with a guy like you around here nobody will try to muscle into our goods. Spawn: No way man I alright with the action I’m doing right now and that is defending this world belt in the world of wrestling. Thug 1: I can dig that, it’s cool so you want me and my homeboys to take care of this Juvenile for you? Spawn: No it’s ok this little punk is nothing more than a flea in my way. And it should be real easy to defeat him this Thursday. Thug 2: How did he get a fight with you if he has been losing all of his matches. What happen did the Prez get scared out of his pants when he did the shooting in his office. I am telling you let us take care of his punk ass. Will get our burners dog and pop some caps on him and his family what do you say playa? Spawn: That is too extreme I don’t want to kill him I just want to beat him. It is probably the only reason that the Prez gave him a non title match against me. So that I can teach his ass a lesson beat him senseless and shut his mouth once and for all. I don’t think that the Prez being scared had nothing to do with it. Thug 2: So you are going to take care this punk all by yourself. I don’t know Spawn he is going to have a lot of back up you should take us with you dog. Spawn: In the ring is just me and him, and he knows it no one will help him and if they do he is going to need more help than those little punks he have with pistol. Shit if they couldn’t even back him up against a woman the chances of them backing him up against me are slim and none. Thug 1: We should still go just in case, there are some shit about him you should know, yo dog (talking to one of his boys) go inside my crib and bring me those pictures of juvenile I have Spawn needs to see this. Thug 2: Alright playa be right back( thug #2 goes inside the house and brings back the pictures handing them over to his pardner) here you go playa all of them are there. Thug 1: Thanks dog alright Spawn check these pictures out man you are going to enjoy this. They begin to look at the pictures the first picture has Juvenile fighting with his girlfriend tyka and he is getting a beat down. There are about four pictures of him and his girlfriend beating up on him a complete set. Spawn and the guys begin to laugh hard. But then the laugh stops with the second set of pictures as it shows Juvenile popping a cap on his girl leg Spawn eyes bloodshot as he can’t believe what he is seeing. The next sets of picture have Juvenile and his ganst boys doing a drive it look like juvenile had a good plan but his boys can’t shoot worth a shit and they miss their target by a mile. They continue to look at the pictures and come across a set of pictures of juvenile getting slapped by his mother sort of like being punish in front of his boys. Spawn and the guys started to laugh hard as they were having a hell of time in Juveniles behalf. After finishing looking at all of the pictures they then begin to analyze them or like they say talk bullshit about them. Thug 1: What do you think? You still think this nigga is threatening? Spawn: Hah! Looking at these pictures I can now accessed the real deal. And that is Juvenile is a punk and a piece of shit that need to be taken out of the face of this planet, he gives thug life a bad name. Thug 2: So you are all set for his ass this Thursday when you fight him? Spawn: Yes I am and he will not have a chance in hell, I will beat him senseless washing that rotten mouth of his, I will get into his mind and confuse it all make him look stupid , make him feel like a girl. The beating he receive at the hand of a lady was just a small step in pain. Now comes the real pain and is going to be with a force so strong, he won’t know what hit him. Thug 2: You know he is going to try to cheat his way to victory dog I'm telling you let's pop some caps on him. That will take care of him forever. Just look at it this way one less piece of shit in this world, I mean what does it matter any. It's not like his mom gives a two shit cent about him, or any of his family. I'm telling you dog his is alone in this world no one will come out looking for him, this is our chance we will not get another let's do it. Spawn: No way for the last time man it is not worth it he is not worth it. I am not going through a lot of hassle for some one that all he does is talk shit out of his mouth gets beat by women including his mother, and does not know the way of thug life at all. Thug 1: I hear you dog and I guess you are right we will…..(gets cut off by a fast car coming in, the car stops in front of them and the drivers get off and go directly to them, they then begin to speak) Other gang: Yo listen man this shit is no good! Who are you trying to rip off selling me shit that sucks! You better come up with my money that I just gave you, or else we are going to have ourselves a problem! Thug 1: Man calm your bitch ass down dog and speak to me in english, there is no need for you to come into my turf and disrespect me like this. Now let's start from the beginning. Other gang: What do you mean calm down you sold me cheap shit and I want my money back! Thug 2: And if we don’t you are going to start a war is that it? Other gang: You got it man, are you going to give me my money or are we going to war? Thug 1: Hold on one minute dog (turning to Spawn and asking him something) Yo Spawn I think you better flex man this looks like is going to get ugly all up in here. Spawn: Why don’t you just solve it the right way? Thug 1: How man the motherfucker came into my turf and disrespected us it can’t stay like this you know what I mean. So how can we let this go? Spawn: Let me think, ok I got it put your guns away and let me talk to them. I will fix things. Spawn goes over to the other gang and begins to talk to them the talking last for about a good twenty minutes, they then shake hands and the gang leave. Spawn then comes back to where his friends are and they begin to speak. Thug 1: Yo dog what did you say to them they just got up and left no hassle at all, what gives playa. Spawn: I told them that the stuff you guys sold them it didn’t come from you guys. Thug 1: Get out of here so who did you told them it came from? Spawn: I told them Juvenile gave you all the stuff to sold. So they ask me where they can find him and I told them that he has a match with me this Thursday at the Docklands Arena in London. Thug 1: That’s all and they just left it at that? They did not say if they were going to do something about it? Spawn: Actually they said for me not to kick his ass real bad and to save a piece for them, they know who he is and that they were going to take care of it as soon as he comes back to the state. So I told them not to worry about it I would hand him over to them in a silver platter. Thug 1: Man I have to you bra that was some smart move you put there on them boys not only that but you took care your juve problem very smart indeed. Spawn: Well I have to be getting along so I’ll see you guys later peace. Spawn leaves the hood as the guys stay in the area hanging around and drinking their beer as the scene fades to black.
