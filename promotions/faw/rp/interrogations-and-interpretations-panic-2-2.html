Interrogations and Interpretations (Panic #2)

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Interrogations and Interpretations (Panic #2) Profusion Furiously Addictive Wrestling Roleplay #236 Date: 29/01/03 Location: House, in Houston Texas Interrogations and Interpretations [We find ourselves in the gloom of my self unconsciousness. Yes its dark, its humid and its foreboding. Its like walking in the dark unknowing of what you are going to bump into. The echo of the promo is still heavy here as we take a ride into the oblivion of Profusion’s cerebral impulse. But finally in that one moment you begin feeling concerned of what may be on the horizon or basically just don’t want to know what sick sh*t I’m going to drop on you we find a light. A dim light but yes a light. We slowly pace ourselves to the vicinity of possible sanctuary from the malevolence that might possibly be near. After finally reaching the light we find this seems to be a light hanging from a ceiling. We see the string that could turn it on and off. And so we pull it. Darkness. Lets turn it back on. So now that its on we turn to our right and find a scene developing here. There is a man sitting behind a wooden table. His eyes barely apparent since shadow seems to dominate here. We can a bit of half of the mans face but the other half is construed in shade. The man sits behind a wooden table and sits on an unforgiving steel chair at a table in the middle of the room. Nothing special about these objects expect that they give off an older vibe. If they were of the realm we know as of reality they most probably would be older pieces of furniture. But as the light sways back and forth the light reveals and obscures the man. But as we watch it is clear this is Profusion. He continues to sit motionless. As far as his attire goes the only visible article of clothing is a dark red New Jersey Hockey t-shirt. Then a voice.] Voice - Are you overpaid? [After the question Profusion’s eyes are more visible and striking in their appearance.] Profusion - Hell no. If anything I'm underpaid, with everything I do. That's a ridiculous question. I have to do everything for this federation. Voice - Are you tough enough to continue on? Maybe you should just quit like everyone else and accept defeat. Profusion - That ain’t my game nor style. I never give up. If this ship sinks I sink with and I drown. I don’t quit I back my words with actions. I don’t ever say I’m the best without proving it. Who still here? Me. Voice - What do you have to show? Profusion – In my short time here I am defeated in one, yes one match and I am the current holder of the Continental Title. I am the first ever Continental Champion and I will go down in History. Who else did that? Voice - What does that get you? Profusion - It gets you where it gets you. Voice - Does it make you the greatest of all time? Profusion - Give me the ring and the gloves and call me Muhammad Ali. Voice - Word on the street is you were soft during your early day’s? Profusion - Soft? Voice - Where was your Continental Title when you were at the bottom, the pit of it all?? Profusion - You saying I’m inferior to past competition? Voice - Maybe. Profusion - If they had the edge on me why have I outlasted “The Best”. You can go on ahead and take me take as not only the greatest of all time but you can mark me down as the mutherf*cking sh*t. Voice - What have you learned from that? Profusion - To not tolerate the bullsh*t rhetoric that comes out of the opposition’s mouth. Voice – Has Lee Smith, Cyrus Skar or even the Ninja even defeated you while you have been the Continental Champion.? Profusion - Hell no. Voice – Do you think Jeff Harris will? Profusion - No, like I said give me the mother fuc*ing gloves and put me in the ring and call me Muhammad Ali. Jeff Harris may be one of the stars here in the FAW but it doesn’t matter what your past is because this is the future, not the past. Voice –What will it be like for you when you loose that FAW Continental Title? Profusion – Loose the Continental Title? You truly are a crazy mother fuc*er! Voice –I have heard inside sources that you, Profusion will end up sometime in spring this year, jumping to another federation, is this true? Profusion – What are you talking about? Haven’t you been listening to what I have just been talking about for the past five minutes. I will always be loyal to the FAW and when the time is right I may move on but FAW is my career now, they have offered me a lot and I tend to go out on the top! Voice –You still think that you are the best in the Continental division? Profusion - Hell yeah. Where the rest at? Because I don’t see em. Voice –Will you be willing to give anyone who tries to step up to your plate? Profusion - Always. Voice - Like who? Profusion - Anyone. Voice - When? Profusion - Anywhere I’ll bring the gold and you bring your @$$. [Suddenly as Profusion looks on, a man in an Armani ensemble slams his arms on the table and stares Profusion in the eyes. But the thing is the man in the suit is Profusion too. A conflict of the ego seems to have been taking place and the questioning of Profusion’s heart seems to have been then issue that has unfolded before us. Profusion who bears the suit reveals the Continental Title and hands it over to Profusion who sits in the chair in the t-shirt.] Profusion - The Continental title is my key to immortality… lest I forget. Voice/Profusion - Who’s winning on Sunday Jeff Harris? Profusion - Me. It begins with Jeff Harris and will begin the reign of terror, the reign of the Profusion [Fadeout] And so I continue my undertaking, to prove you fools that I am a wrestling god. To prove to you idiots that I am anything but a wrestling façade. But come to think about it what have I not yet proved? I have not yet proved that I can be the World Champion, but some day, some day. Cannon to the left of me, Cannon to the right of me, Cannon in front of me, As the shot’s “Volley’d and thunder’d” While yet another blunder’d.
