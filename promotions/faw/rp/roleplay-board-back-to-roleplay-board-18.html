Spawn — FAW Roleplay, August 2002

August 2002

Furiously Addictive Wrestling Roleplay Board Roleplay Board | Back to Roleplay Board Spawn Entitled: NEO YOU ARE DOOM (PANIC RP 3) Written: 8/6/02 Location: BOSTON Scene opens to the same open area the the last promo ended. By now it's nighttime. Camera pans around, although it's very hard to see, being that the only light around is a small fire by Spawn's Hummer. Camera begins to zoom in on Spawn, who's sleepin in the back. As we get closer it seems as if he's not having to good a dream. We listen in as he silently mummbles to himself. SpAwN: Motherfucka always hating. I'll get you, just you wait. Try it again bitch I dare ya. Spawn suddenly awakes and looks around cautiously. After realizin were he's at, he notices the camera. He rubs his eyes and we zoom in a little more. SpAwN:Oh it's just you. Here to find out more of my thoughts on my upcoming match? Well let's see what Neo has to say. Spawn grabs a tape and pops it into a VCR. The camera pans over to a TV mounted on the ceiling. We wait awile but nothing plays. Spawn stops the tape and the camera turns back to him. SpAwN:Just as I expected. Nothing at all. Very smart, yet dumb as shit at the same time, and you wonder why you've been a jobber so long. You wonder why you're not gettin title shots, or even worth while matchs. Cause you produce bullshit like this. But as I said I didn't expect anything less from you. Spawn reaches in his pocket and pulls out a Newp. He light it up and continues. SpAwN:I've been sitting out here for hours waiting and hoping that you'd open your mouth, and show us all that you've got a pair. And you know what you've shown us. That there's a big smelly pair of lips down there. Sad Neo, very sad. The big bad bullshitter that you are. Come on Neo I know you better than that. Lets make this shit just a little interesting, why don't we. Tell us about how you'll use your awesome skills to take me out? Talk shit about how I used to be nothing but a big stoner, who care more about weed than winning a match. Do I really have to do this for you? Do you need some more ideas? Would you like for me to do a promo for you? I'd probably do a much better job anyway. Come on dog, do something. Spawn jumps in the front seat and starts the Hummer. SpAwN:Maybe your to busy shining your trophies, or beating off to pictures of your wanna be girlfriend, Or maybe you just realize that no matter what you do its not gonna do you any good. Cause in the end, it will be just that.....THE END.The end to your dreams, the end to your career, the end to your life, that's right Neo you heard what I said the end to everything that you owe and hold dearly. You see that is the price you pay for trying to take what is rightfully mine the hardcore title. I know a lot of wrestlers in the back are probably saying this belt means nothing, is a second rate title, but you know what Neo it is my second rate title, and I will do what ever is necesary to keep it on my waist. You say you will take me out well guess what, I can't wait for the moment and you know what else Neo I'll be waiting. All you have to do is bring your sorry scum bucket ass to the ring and we will get down to business Spawn style SO LET IT BE WRITTEN.....SO LET IT BE DONE. HELL HASS SPOKEN. SO SAYS THE HELL MASTER sPAWN puts the Hummer in gear, and pulls off into the darkness. We watch as the fire next to us slowly dies out. You know what this means......FADE TO BLACK
