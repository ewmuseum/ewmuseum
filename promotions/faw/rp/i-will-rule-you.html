I will rule you! Xing Furiously Addictive Wrestling Roleplay #140 Date: 4.1.02 Location: Canada In a dark room on his own Xing playing back a video of his past match of him and the old foe Lee Smith his face reaction to the tape is useable because of the darkness but we see movement from Xing as Lee Smith sets up for the move that had Xing in a puddle of Blood and a puddle of defeat from a knockout blow. Xing walks up to the VCR and turns it off bringing out another tape but this time of his next opponent the man who Xing believes to be nothing but a jackass that talks instead of acts in the ring none other than Big V man the Violator. What the video is showing is Violator in his previous matches being an ex-wrestler and now returning but during that match we turn straight before Violator does his special for the victory it goes straight to his household in Chicago where we see Violator drinking a beer. The video stops when there is another man revealed to be in the room as Xing sling s the TV and VCR across the room the other bloke switches on the light we see Xing in complete an utter fury turning a rage on then Xing screams “I told you to get dirt from that bastard not him drinking beer every hill Billy in America does that 24/7 that’s why they are the fattest population in the world just look at the so called ‘great’ Bam Bam Bigelow that just says it all I told you to get dirt but what did you bring me some jackass American having a beer, just get the fuck out and hope that I never lay eyes on you again or rest assure that I will kick your ass! Wait a minute do you even know why I want that dirt no I didn’t think so hell it’s because… Xing pauses in the middle of his speech holding his long black hair to get his breathe back and continues while grabbing the poor sole “It is because I am all about impact yeah and I wanted to bring in dirt to get the upper hand on our match not that I need it because I rule ass and I am the self proclaimed King not that dirty runt Lee Smith of the world and hey a king needs respect yeah now that the Xing things have turned I need them back but I wouldn’t count on the thick shits of America to understand about me and my needs. You get it now go get me a drink now you silly little American punks you’re all the same go on git now!” Xing looks bemused when the man takes off his top and puts up his fists and says “Don’t talk about Americans like that or else!” Xing laughs and stands up and all of a sudden turns in pure anger and then rams the man straight through wall and crouches down by his head and says “Americans suck sort of like you, now I know I called you fat that may not be true, but let me tell you this, don’t mess because I’ll always take the piss, I’ll make you famous, and hell I’ll make you bless, bless to my ass sucker!” Xing pulls down his trousers and stinky faces the American that stuck up for his rights. Xing stops beating on the poor soul and stands shouting “Violator you think you strong, but you won’t last long, when involved with Xing, I’ll make you sing, hell I’ll make you cry, how about let you fry, hey I’m not a rapper just a King, you disagree when you like at my diamond plated ring, hell I ain’t got much respect for you, but rest assure that you will beg for a break in the loo. Now I’m going to end this rap, with this splattered like crap.” Xing looks down at the kid on the floor as he finishes his rap and he picks him up a like a little toy and power bombs him making a dent on the floor Xing walks out happy and goes outside of his Canadian hook up and strangely enough there is an Ice Cream guy opposite the road Xing crosses with out looking and causes massive skids from cars as they come to a halt immediately causing a car to get rammed up the back but Xing still managed to make it across the road unharmed not even getting hit by the cars which were going at 45 miles per hour any more and they would have put Xing away for good but we carry on with Xing across the road almost causing a devastating car accident pushes in front of a queue of kids begging for Ice Cream but Xing don’t care and shoves past them all feeling very upset Xing had a few quite words with the guy in the van the man gave Xing a box of Ice Cream and a speaking horn and shouts out “Come on Canada let me hear it Americans suck Canadians rule! Come on another time keep it going people yeah you got it Americans Suck Canadians rule!” Xing grabs everyone’s attentions making all the Canadians chant at the tops of their voices Americans suck Canadians rule! Xing then shouts out to his countrymen and women “you can get free Ice Cream if you cheer for me against the Violator at panic and believe that the American punk is a drugee I know a big shocker I know but that’s Americans for you as thick as shit because come Sunday I won’t receive the Judement day pain it’s you and all of your disgusting American fans that will feel the pain as Xing will Rule your ass!”
