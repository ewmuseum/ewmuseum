Twilight Of The Dead & Of Damien Ryan

16/10/02

Twilight Of The Dead & Of Damien Ryan 9th Wonder Of Foam Furiously Addictive Wrestling Roleplay #14 Date: 16/10/02 Location: Foamy Hills - New Jersey …. An undisclosed Military Installation – The Near Future …. (Lars, a military commander named Steve “Forlorn” Cooper, and his two friends Andrew “ Ass lips” Stevens and Carlton “Storm” Bruce all sit around a table in a bunker deep below one of America’s biggest cities) Steve: They’re everywhere, the Zombies outnumber us by about 400,000 to 1. Lars: But there are four of us in here. Steve: You know what I mean you stupid asshole. Lars: Don’t call me an asshole milk stealer! Andrew: What? Lars: Look three of us got here on Wednesday and worked our asses off to make this compound secure from Zombie attack, then suddenly at Six O’clock Saturday Steve here turns up with his hunk of gold medal pinned to his chest claiming to be the new commander of the out post and blatantly breaks the milk ration rule! Steve: Look I’m the commander of this outpost; I’m the chief the top of the tree I can drink as much damn milk, as I like. Lars: Who sat in this very room all of the week milking cows? We set a two pints of milk a day rule in order to keep the rations for as long as possible because that cow isn’t going to live forever! The fact is Steve you waltzed in here four days after the rest of us, sat down, took command and then drank three pints of milk in the space of what must’ve been four hours!!!!! Carlton: Look, it’s only milk! Lars: That milk is what’s keeping us going without it this place would be empty! We’ve already had to kill off Trevor Vilanni because he didn’t like the system or any of its rules. Now I say we do the same to Steve! Steve: That ain’t happening no more chump, I run this outpost. Andrew: Yeah he is in charge you are just a guy in a green shirt with a bb gun, you have no military training. Steve: I say we get rid of this asshole Lars! Whose with me? Andrew: Yay. Carlton: Nay. Steve: Well Carlton bad move Lars this is a democracy, what do you say? Lars: Yay, I mean nay. Steve: Wrong move dumbf**k! …. Outside the outpost …. (Lars is gagged and brought out into the courtyard; outside the fence there are hundreds of Zombies. Steve, Andrew, Carlton and the cow named Cyril look on laughing from a crow’s nest as they open the gates and the Zombies stumble in. They tear the flesh off Lars’s skeleton.) Lars: Arghhh!!! …. Foamy Hills Mansion – Wednesday Morning …. (Lars wakes from his dream) Lars: Finally it’s over my Zombie hell is over I’m free! (Lars gets from his bed he walks down the hall into one of the offices, there is a fax from FAW HQ, he takes it straight to the Foamy One’s bedroom) …. Foamy’s bedroom …. (Lars opens the door, the Foamy One lies on the floor asleep Lars notices a huge stepladder set up in the middle of the room, it looks like Foamy had fallen from it, he decides not to wake him and places the fax on the floor next to Foamy, then the doorbell rings, Lars runs out to answer it) (Foamy suddenly opens his eyes; he looks at the fax on the floor) Foamy: Damn ladder! Oooo Damien Ryan… (An Evil grin comes to Foamy’s face. His bedroom door opens, it is Lars) Lars: Melissa Farrow has arrived with her crew, she is in the Study. What hell happened to you? Foamy: Oh I was trying to grab one of those damn Helium balloons that had flown in through my window. But the ladder was too tall, every time I’d get near the top I’d lose balance and fall off, but never mind the balloon has now disappeared. (Foamy begins to climb the ladder once again) Lars: What the hell are you doing? Foamy: Just because the balloon has gone it doesn’t mean I’ve defeated the ladder itself yet. Lars: Yes but you have an interview to do. Foamy: Ok dammit! But ladder I will return soon and I am going to climb you and laugh my ass off at your attempts to knock me off it you hear ladder? (Foamy & Lars leave) …. Foamy’s Study …. (Bob the Mocking Bird is perched on Foamy’s desk; he is trying to woo Melissa) Bob: Oh come on give us a f**k. (Foamy enters with Lars) Foamy: Hey bird off the desk! Bob: Oh shut it loser. Foamy: What did you call me? Bob: Do you want me to cry at your loss? Do you want me to get some tissue and cry into your arms because you lost to a comic book hero? Well I’m afraid Mr.Foam the ability to cry to your misfortune passed me long ago. Foamy: Bob, you’re getting on you know I’ve been looking at your retirement options, I’ve read about several good taxidermists. (Bob looks as shocked as a talking bird could be) Bob: Oh you wouldn’t dare, I’m off to browse the Internet for a replica FAW Belt for your birthday, you’re not getting the real thing after all. Foamy: Lars escort Bob out of the room now! Melissa: You know the wing’ed one has a point. Foamy: Yes, thanks for pointing that out but Melissa you forget Sunday night so easily it amazes me. There was no 1-2-3 count, in fact the match didn’t even finish. Just as I predicted Spawn took the coward’s way out, in fact he did both things I said he may try. First off he had his ass boy Apocalypse come down with him to wrestle what looked like a handicapped match. Spawn abused the referee and then just as I had predicted that happened. Melissa: What happened? Foamy: Another week, another title defence, and another new member of the DCV. This time it is Cyrus Raynes and his International Championship, which I must admit, looks all the more appealing as time goes by. Spawn proved nothing except that he has a need to surround himself by mindless Zombies to make him feel secure, again he ducked out of actually fighting me and opted to make yet another mockery of the FAW World Championship. But as Spawn also found out there are certain individuals within FAW who are ready to stand up and fight back against his mediocrity. The Great One, Xander, The British Bomber and myself all ended Sunday night of a high note, sending a message to the Carnival that their lease on this part of the land is running out and soon they’ll have to haul their carnie asses off our land and move on. Melissa: Ok lets; move on to your next opponent Damien Ryan. Foamy: Oh yes Damien Ryan the FAW Hardcore championship. I know many that would pass up this opportunity because they don’t feel that Damien is worthy enough but I know different, Damien is the FAW Hardcore champion. And it really is a pity that the powers that be didn’t make Damien put his title on the line, or perhaps Damien declined because he knew that I could take it away from him. Melissa: You want a Hardcore Title shot? Foamy: Hardcore wrestlers are some of the craziest mo-fo’s in this industry, and when I brutalised Xander two weeks ago I proved that I can be extreme. But sadly it seems either Damien Ryan is too scared to take that risk or those who control my destiny have other plans, namely a shot at Apocalypse, or Cyrus Raynes. Melissa: We’ll come back to the DCV in a moment. But you really think you could take that Hardcore Title away from him? Foamy: One can never be totally sure of anything anymore, but I know from past experience from being a former PWA Hardcore champion that I am up to the job, but so be it, I will face Damien Ryan in a regular match I will give him the respect he deserves but no more. Were I to face him for that title? They would probably have to re-name the championship the Extreme Championship because that is how far I would take it given the chance. Melissa: Earlier you said you would invite a match with the two of the FAW members that aren’t currently tied up in matches? Foamy: Yes, the title shot at Spawn now moves onto someone who is more deserving, who has more of a reason to want to slice Spawn’s throat and let him Championship rain pour out he being the Great One. Chris Thunder will be occupied with the beating that the Bomber will hand out. But Apocalypse, and Cyrus the International Champion are free that night; they are without a date for the big event. And I’d like to ask one of them out, either of them I’d like to face Apocalypse again and show him that our cage match a while ago wasn’t just me being lucky, or Cyrus who holds a Title I feel I have unfinished business with. So to either man I say bring it on, and I will re-iterate this request at The Foam Show taping tonight and at Distortion on Sunday. I want a piece of the DCV to call my own, one of their heads on my hunting wall that I can laugh and point at. Melissa: Final thoughts? Foamy: I have fallen from the ladder again, I have lost my balance and now someone else takes my place but I will start at my beginnings a new, I will take back victories and build myself up again to take another shot at the ladder sometime down the road. And soon perhaps people will take me a little more seriously, The Mass Murderers, Trent Hunter, Apocalypse, Xander & Tony Vigilante in one night all these names, some of them illustrious some of them not so, but they are all names but a few on my list, all victims some I have yet to finish off, but all of them victories. I am going to make this list grow and grow Damien Ryan, Apocalypse and one day perhaps Spawn. But now I must concentrate on my next name; Damien Ryan, soon to be the only man ever to defend his Hardcore title from his own hospital bed. Have a Foam Filled Day!
