Spawn — FAW Roleplay #42

captured 22 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
SpAwN--The Hell Master \~~~~~~~(opening scene)The scene FAW house show were we see all of the fans hot on their seat as they await the beginning of Spawn’s Devil’s pit Finally the wait is over and the mist appear Spawn is shown on the FAW tron and he begins to speak. Spawn: Welcome to another edition of the Devil’s pit this is the place were all your worst dreams are revealed who did you betray and why did you betrayed the person you knew you couldn’t yet you did. This is the place that will make your home trip be a very scary long one. Our subject here to day is about Skipster the man that is next on Spawn’s hit list, skippy old boy listen look and learn this story is for you and only for your eyes to see, the words or the action can not describe what will happen here today but you will see and you will fear it for it will harm thee to eternity. Now let us begin our little story. Are you ready can your heart take it then we begin if you can another edition of the Devil’s pit. The scene begins in Boston Mass were we see the team of the Cathedral high school football team, in this team there is a boy name Jamal who has been trying out for the team but has always been turned down. He has been trying for the last few years and is getting very pissed of because he does not make the team ever. So this year he promised himself he would make the team even if he has to break the rules some. The scene begins in the football field were we see Jamal throwing the football with two other boys, and as they practice they are having a rough conversation. Jamal: Hey man why you guys are always throwing the ball all wrong to me that’s not right man. Ben: Oh shit Jack? Jamal is at it again mr goodie two shoes is complaining and whining again. Jack: That’s why he never makes it in the team because of your goodie two shoe role be a man for a shange and do something about it? Jamal: I am a man and this year I will make the team you will see. Jack: What we will see is you crying like a mama’s boy as always. Jamal: You are wrong I never cry I am a man and if you keep saying that I cry like a girl you are going to find out what I can do. Jack: Are you threatening me Jamal? I hope not because you know what happened to you the last time you threatened me I kicked your ass remember. Jamal: That was then this is now it is totally different now. Ben: What is so different about now? Jamal: Now I will do even better I will put my entire force to making the team, there will be no stopping me because I know that I can do it, I have faith in myself I can do it because this time is totally different, you will see. Ben: Blassy, blassy, blas, there you go with your sorry stories boo hoo hoo go cry somewhere else. Jamal: I am not crying! Jack: Then prove it you are not crying, make the team? Jamal: How can I do this how can I make the team? Jack: You really want to know how you can make the team? Jamal: Yes I do I want to know because this year is my year so I do want to know? Jack: OK man I will show you let us stop playing this tossing game and I want you to come with me to this place, my friend will show you how to get in the team. Jack, Jamal, and Ben all meet after school and head over to the house of Jack’s friend in the house we see a lot of chemicals being mixed this man’s room is like a chemistry lab, he then approaches the guys and begin to speak. Man: So this is the boy you was telling me about, the one that has tried so hard to make the team but never succeeds, now you come to me for help… very good this can be done but at a high price so very high that there might not be a return back to how you once was. Jamal: What do you mean by that sir? Man: What I mean is boy what you lack is an evil heart, that is why you do not make it in life your good heart do not allow you to do the things possible for you to get up high in life. Jamal: And you can help me out with this problem? Man: Yes I could boy but it will cost you big? Jamal: How much money will it cost me because as of right now it does not matter to me any? Man: It will not cost you any money but there will be a significance change in your life that’s what it will cost you. Jamal: Ok and what is it that you want me to do? Man: Plain and simple I will give you a potion and all you have to do is trust me and drink it. Jamal: Ok I do that then what after I drink it? Man: Then you let the potion do his work, so now kid are you ready (Jamal nods his head in agreeing motion) then here drink up and let nature take his course. Jamal drinks all of the potion as the scene fades away. The scene four weeks later, shows Jamal at the football field trying out for the team, everybody are impressed at the way Jamal is playing, there is no stopping him he out does everyone it looks real good for him as he is a synch, to make the team this time around. But then the unexpected happens Jamal goes after the kid that always beats him for the spot nocks him down and tackles him to the ground when he got him on the floor he begins to pound on him none stop everybody rushes over to the scene and breaks it up, they are all in shock as what has just happened, they break up practice and sent everyone home, this happens for a few more weeks with Jamal hurting at least four more guys, finally he makes the team but in the process loses all of his friends, but he does not care all he care about is making the team. The scene changes and we see Jamal in the man that gave him the potion house talking to the man. Man: So do you like what the potion did for you? you are in the team now right? Jamal: Yes I am but what I do not understand is why did I hurt so many people? In the process of going to the top and acheveiving what I wanted to achieve? Man: It is the price I told you it was going to cost you. Jamal: What do you mean price? what are you talking about? Man: That potion you dranked was a devil’s potion now I can say to you welcome to my world you are now one of us, so prepare for a new life in the dark. Jamal: What are you crazy! I can’t live like this it is not me you are insane no way man I am out of here! Man: You can run but you can’t hide and you know belong to me forever. Jamal: No way man this is not what I wanted. Man: It is what you wanted if you didn’t you wouldn’t have drinked it. Jamal: I am leaving and this deal is over you will never see me again. Man: You do not have to come back to me, you already belong to me. Jamal leaves and the scene changes to showing Jamal living the rest of his life as a hard ass hurting people when ever he gets the chance a life he never wanted to begin with. The scene fades and it shows Spawn talking on the FAW tron. Spawn: Did you see skippy did you listened and pay good attention, I just gave you a tip, with this beautiful sadistic story do you want to know what was the tip, Let me tell you what it was, the tip is if you want to beat me then you must have a black heart your goodie two shoe image will never cut it in my back yard you must be as evil as I am or even more evil you must be fearless as I am or even more fearless if you can be this then you will have a chance in defeating me but if you can’t then you can just kiss my ass. Personally I know you can’t so this story was for nothing but I tried and that is better than nothing. One last thing before I let you go Skippy pay good attention in our match tomorrow and be very prepared or else you will go out in a coffin. So let it be written…. So let it be done. Hell Has Spoken. The FAW Tron fades to black as so does the scene.
