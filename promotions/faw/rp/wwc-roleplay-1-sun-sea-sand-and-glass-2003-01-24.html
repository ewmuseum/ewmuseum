WWC Roleplay 1 - Sun, Sea, Sand and Glass

24 January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
WWC Roleplay 1 - Sun, Sea, Sand and Glass Spider Furiously Addictive Wrestling Roleplay #240 Date: 24-01-03 Location: Bondai Beach, Australia DATE SET: 18-01-03 TIME: 13:54 LOCATION: Bandai Beach, Australia [The scene is a gym, tiles on the wall a perfect pristine white, the floor in a laminate wood flooring that is well glazed, the equipment all around, all very fresh, clean and new. The sunlight of the hot suburban Bandai beach sunlight beaming through the windows, one lone man working is the weights, on medium to high level, lifting up and down. The man, with his shoulder length crimson hair and grimace, wearing nothing except a pair of light blue training/cycling shorts, is Spider] Spider: “I’m going to win… this rumble is mine… I’m going to win… this rumble is mine… I’m going to win… this rumble is mine…” [He stops, puts the weights back and stands up, he grabs his water bottle and sneers and throws it when he sees there is none left. Spider walks out of the room, suddenly a smack is heard] Spider: “Stupid machine! Don’t eat my money!” [Another large smack followed by a smash and much moaning follows, Spider hobbles through the door with his drink… and a lot of blood and glass on his foot] Spider: “Argh! Where’s that damn mobile?!” [Spider searches and searches but to no avail, he takes his drink and towel and hobbles to his locker room, he opens the locker and grabs his mobile, a red Nokia with his logo on it, he presses the buttons and calls] Spider: “Yeah hello? Lazlo?” [A voice is heard but it cannot be interpreted] Spider: “It’s Vinny, I kind of’ busted my foot badly, I need a ride… five minutes? Okay cool thanks bud.” [Spider gets some clothes on and wraps his towel around his foot as a bandage, he grabs some keys and limps outside, he opens, closes and then locks the door firmly and sits on the steps outside looking out to the beautiful Bandai Beach] Spider: “I can’t believe this, five days until my return and I fucking go and break the water machine!” [Spider hums “Gainesville Rock City” by Less than Jake as he waits, after a few minutes a sleek black Lotus-esque car pulls up, the blacked out windows roll down and brief FAW wrestler Lazlo Hawk appears at the window with a wide grin on his face, he has bleached blonde hair and a little goatee, he tips his black sunglasses down] Lazlo: “Get a little foot-to-glass action?” [Lazlo laughs, Spider snarls] Spider: “Shut up and get me to the hospital.” [Lazlo shrugs and rolls down the window, he opens the door for his friend and goes back to his seat. Spider struggles to stand and sit but he manages it, he closes the door and they are off, Spider looks uncomfortable] Lazlo: “You okay dude?” Spider: “Yeah just got to sort this out, don’t want to screw up on my return do I now?” Lazlo: “Maybe you do, maybe you want an excuse as to why you will lose?!” [Spider turns to him with an evil look on his face; he grabs his friends’ collar as they reach some traffic lights] Spider: “I would NEVER do that, never fucking say that again or I’ll take away your neck like that magic trick where they take away the table clothe!” [Spider lets go and shoves his friend back in his seat, Lazlo shakes his head, they drive on after the lights change for 10 minutes, stopping and starting at the correct places, eventually Lazlo pulls the car to a full stop] Lazlo: “We are here…” [Lazlo climbs out of the car and goes around the other side, he see’s Spider struggling, he helps him out of the car, Spider starts limping to A and E while Lazlo checks his car, he walks off and presses the lock button before catching up with his argumentative buddy] Lazlo: “Dude don’t just walk off, you’ll need to have help resting that.” [They walk into A and E, it is very drab, grey walls, metal-esque flooring covered by a very thin layer of carpet. There are a few people there, around eight, two parents with two kids, a teenager and his mother and a man on his own, they walk up to the reception desk. The woman at the desk is overweight and has a very 80’s hairstyle] Spider: “Hi my name is Vincent Beck, I’m here on vacation and I um…” Nurse: “You do not need to tell me your condition, the doctor will see about that.” [Spider nods, he and Lazlo take the nearest seats] Lazlo: “I told you that you should be resting while we are here, I’m not leaving you alone until the Super Card now.” Spider: “That’s if I can even be on it!” Lazlo: “You will be dude, don’t worry.” Spider: “Hehe…” [Lazlo looks confused] Lazlo: “What’s so funny?” Spider: “Nothing, nothing.” Lazlo: “What?!” Spider: “The look in your eye when I grabbed your neck, you looked ready to piss your pants, you never would have made it as a FAW superstar.” [Lazlo mutters something that is un-hearable] Spider: “What?” Lazlo: “Didn’t say a damn word.” [They sit in silence for a few minutes, the two kids and the man go in first, followed by the teenager, as the teen enters Lazlo ponders aloud] Lazlo: “Do you really think you can win the rumble?” Spider: “What’s that supposed to mean?” Lazlo: “Just answer.” Spider: “Of course I can, whether I will is another matter, I mean there is some top quality in there, I can’t underestimate anyone, but I’m confident I can do it, and with a bit of luck I will, depends where I am drawn and if I still have it doesn’t it now?” Lazlo: “Do you think any luck will come of that?” [Lazlo points to Spider’s foot] Spider: “Dude I just hope it won’t stop me getting in the ring, I don’t need luck for that, I need a good doctor.” [They sit for another few minutes until the teenager comes out, his mother fusses him, you now see more people have come, but there is no time to see] Nurse: “Doctor Bellestine will see you now Mr. Beck.” [Spider stands up; Lazlo helps him to the room] Lazlo: “You want me with you?” Spider: “May aswell.” [They enter together, the doctor points to the bed, Spider lays down, Lazlo sits down in a nearby seat, the doctor has barely any hair but the little he has is grey, his small beard covers his mouth and he is wearing a white coat, the nurse is a pretty young woman, blonde hair tied back and the nurses uniform looks extremely sexy on her] Lazlo: “I know who I’d rather be treated by.” [Spider snickers] Doctor: “Well Mr. Beck I see you met your foot with some glass, how did it happen?” [The doctor prepares a needle] Spider: “I kicked the vending machine okay? You happy?” [This time Lazlo laughs] Doctor: “Don’t act like a Sheila. Now this is just to make your foot numb.” [The injection occurs and Spider hums, the nurse starts picking out the glass with tongs] Nurse: “If it hurts just tell me.” Spider: “I wouldn’t mind you hurting me!” [Lazlo laughs again] Nurse: “Sir, please try to restrain yourself.” [Spider lets her take the glass out, the doctor then looks at the foot] Doctor: “Can you walk on it?” Spider: “Not well, but nothings broken, I’ve broken bones before and it’s not… besides I’d rather have it checked out in my home country.” Doctor: “Very well.” [The nurse cleans the blood with a towel and then wraps his foot in a bandage] Spider: “Doc will it be okay by Sunday?” Doctor: “There only seems to be flesh wounds, you should be okay.” [Spider nods] Lazlo: “We’re off home tonight anyway, thank you doctor.” Doctor: “Don’t forget to rest it; try to walk as little as possible over the next week or so.” [They leave the room] Doctor: “That boy should be more careful about what he does…” ~SCENE FADE~
