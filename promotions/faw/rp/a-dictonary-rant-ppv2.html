A Dictonary Rant(ppv2)

23/10/02
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
A Dictonary Rant(ppv2) Crimson Lord Furiously Addictive Wrestling Roleplay #36 Date: 23/10/02 Location: Minnesota {It's party time children. If you thought Crimson Lord had a lot to say in his most recent promotional video, you've yet to see how long he's capable of going without breath. Any of those that followed him around the wrestling world will know what comes second in his predictable pattern of promos. We fade up to his face. The beaten, scarred face of the large Wiccan stares out into the television audience. Supporting him in the background is nothing but black darkness, as if there's any other colour darkness. His long straggly red hair falls around his seemingly bare shoulders. His face is stern.} “Accomplishment. Something completed successfully. An achievement. The act of succeeding or doing. Yes, Sensei, I would consider defeating Rezerector in a match as an accomplishment, as would anyone who knows anything about wrestling. You can flower up the story to make yourself look the victim however you want, but that doesn’t relieve the fact that you were a good Fighter. The very note that you were even given the title shot meant that the management team in FAW felt you deserved the chance at the accomplishment. You earned a shot at an achievement. You were put in the position by the management in Australia to succeed at that level. You don’t get a shot at a title like that and not hold some place of valour in the eyes of the management team. You, my friend, are poorly attempting to create a sense of pity surrounding your self-imposed failures. That, in and of itself, is pitiful.” {He doesn’t blink. He merely breathes.} “Champion. One that is clearly superior or has the attributes of a winner. Well, Sensei, you may have possessed the necessary character points to have once been a champion, but you’ve lost them by now. Winners don’t whine. Winners don’t complain. Winners go out day after day and fight whatever battles need to be fought, be it in the ring against an opponent or backstage in the politics against an employer. Winners don’t give up and run away when things don’t go their way; they stick it out and earn their positions by defying against those that do them wrong. You were once a winner, Sensei, I will give you the benefit of the doubt on that issue. The sad point that remains is that you no longer are a winner. You’ve allowed yourself to succumb to the stereotype of the unappreciated worker. You’ve fallen into place as someone that wishes to be viewed as the good guy in the fight against a political evil. You attempt to be a victim… but you fail again and merely look like a commonplace crybaby. I hate to be blunt, Sensei, but that’s as simply as it can be stated.” {He cracks a slight smile. His left eye blinks.} “Overlook. To rise above. To look over from a higher place. This is one of the definitions of the term that you so love to triumph as your battle cry. While in a literal sense it means that you are physically above something and looking down upon it, the word can easily be misconstrued to take a metaphorical sense of looking down upon an inferior. Well, I know this isn’t the meaning that you intend for the expression… because in your eyes you were most definitely not overlooking anyone or anything. In my opinion, however, you were most certainly above many of the wrestlers in your previous federations. You had a great view of the heads of many a strong worker in FAW amidst your complaining and whining, yet you still quit and come here to Minnesota. Now you have a great view of the feet of many a strong worker. You were near the top. You quit. You’re near the bottom now. To look over or examine. Yes, perhaps this is the true definition of what the management of FAW and your other previous federations were trying to do. Again, this is easily not the definition you imply of the word overlook, but think about this: perhaps you were just being tested by your employer. Many owners of these federations feel it is a good idea to test their employees before bringing them to the next level. You could continue your advancement and eventually stand against the likes of Crimson Lord. And just maybe… you failed that test. To fail to notice or consider. To miss. To ignore deliberately or indulgently. To disregard. Ah, now here is what you intend the word to be defined as, Sensei. Maybe you should be a little clearer next time to provide less confusion for those of us around you. So you feel that the officials of your previous workplaces have decided to ignore you and completely disregard you? Had you taken the same spot as Duke in the Match on Distortion, you would have been fighting for a shot at the FAW’s Hardcore title. Duke earned that shot by being a great worker. He’s not one of the top guys in the league, but he will be… because he won’t quit like you. If anything, you were passed up. Then again, he defeated you for that very same title you deemed beneath you. The object of the fight is to be the first to run away. You’d have been a clear-cut favorite.” {Blink. Smile. Breath.} “History. A narrative of events. A story. Something that belongs to the past. An interesting past. Sensei, you have a history of two things in your career; bad luck with federations closing while you are making progress and a bad reputation for running away. I know your history, Sensei. It doesn’t take much to look up facts in the historical archives of the FAW. Wrestlers have portfolios, taped matches, video promotional videos, and a slew of other information ready at your disposal if only you look. Had you taken the time to find mine you would have known that I have wrestled outside if Minnesota. It may not have been the greatest career in Hawaii.” {Pause.} “Quit. To depart from. To leave. To leave the company of. To give up. To relinquish. To abandon or put aside. We’ve both quit, Sensei. We both have pasts of leaving federations for whatever reason. The only difference, my friend, is that I have a legitimate reason aside from simple petty whining about my station in the league. You see: you left because you were unhappy. I left because I feared for my life. In March of this year, I was Stabbed by the lunatic Shifty during a match against Shifty right in the square in the shoulder.” {He points to his right shoulder to reveal the Scar wound.} “I left FAW for a very viable reason. I felt the need to tell you that just to prevent the possibility of you making an ass of yourself and assuming I was a quitter like you. I didn’t want to remain in a league where a man would be legally protected by the owners even after a shooting. In case you’re wondering why I left OSW, it was because the OSW found it funny to place me in a match against Hype Johnson on Vortex. Suffice to say, I didn’t show.” {He cracks a smile.} “Perhaps it was a bad time in your career to lose the nickname of ‘Jobber’. It’s obvious that I’m not really one for being shot. Then again, who is?” {Pause.} “Rumour. Unverified information of uncertain origin usually spread by word of mouth. Hearsay. Well, Smith, it appears you allow untruths to easily be believed in your mind after your response to what that moron on the radio said about me. If you view my first promotional video, you will see that, in no way, shape, or form, did I say that I would easily defeat you. In fact, I went back and watched it myself to see if I did – and it came off fairly complimentary, I think. Be careful about what you allow rumours to do to your ideas upon things. Pretty soon you may hear some rumours about witches and then you may get the impression that you should burn me at the stake.” {He smiles a simple smile. Not cocky, but knowing.} “Disappointment. The act of failing to satisfy the hope, desire, or expectation of. The act of frustrating or thwarting. Sensei, if you feel you can just walk into FAW and kick Crimson’s ass, well, you’re going to be very sorely disappointed. I am not here to be a stepping-stone. I am not here to be a paper blockade on your assumed path to stardom. I’m not here to just sit around and wait for other people to pass me by on the golden road to fame in FAW. I’m here to win. I’m here to fight. Unlike that DJ on the radio said, I’m not here to tell others that I will easily defeat them or make idle threats. No one would be in FAW if they didn’t have skill. It’s a top-notch organization, perhaps the most competitive in the entire Roster. I don’t think I’ll easily defeat you. However, I do think that I will come out victorious. If I didn’t believe I’d win, it’d be senseless to try to even fight. Keep the word ‘disappointment’ in mind if you have high-grade ideals for what your stay in FAW will be like. You’ll be faced with it sooner than you think.” {Pause. Blink.} 'Seed. A ripened plant ovule containing an embryo. A spore. A source or beginning. Offspring. In sports, the rankings given to people or teams in a tournament. Yes, there are many ways the word 'seed' can be changed and thought of. Those of you that watched what I did two days ago saw the most common and literal sense of the word seed as I planted one for myself in that field. Those of you that had the unfortunate task of listening to Sensei. You see, every person has a seed of some sort stuck inside of them. For people of lesser intelligence, such as Sensei, they construe the term to mean your most base and simple desires of mankind, At least Sensei was intelligent enough to understand what I meant and even bring up intelligible debates to counter-point my words. For that, I give him notice…' {Pause.} 'Expectations. To expect is to look forward to the probable occurrence or appearance of. It is to consider likely, reasonable, or due. It can even be viewed as meaning to presume or suppose. In this world that we all make our living, everyone has expectations. In fact, in the world at large everyone has their own expectations. It is human nature to look forward to things approaching in their lives. It is only human to work day in and day out in an effort to have things come to you that you foresee. It is inherent within the body of every person that we see things to come to us that are due to us: a paycheck, opportunity, life itself. The Two of us that will walk into the arena on Sunday go in with our own expectations. I expect my seeds to come to fruition and I expect that I will come out victorious over my three opponents. If I didn't believe that I would win, there would be no sense in showing up. It would just be moronic to fight a fight I felt I couldn't survive. I also expected that my opponents would be bright enough to understand and contend with the intelligence level that I possess. Sometimes our expectations are met and sometimes they aren't. Sensei expects that we understand his references to literature. Sensei shows a grasp of things that one can hardly expect from someone like him. Sometimes your ideas of people are proven wrong. Sensei expects us all to be worried about him because his name happens to be mentioned from time to time in the circles of the FAW. He expects us to cower because he won the some match. I also expected that Sensei would be quick to mention his achievements and I was hardly let down. Sometimes your expectations are correct and sometimes they are off by a mile. One problem with expectations is that they lead to something that can be dangerous. They lead to a word that Sensei even mentioned he did from time to time.' {Crimson pauses and takes a deep breath. The hair on his head moves and his left eye blinks. The right eye always has trouble blinking due to the scar.} 'Assume. To take something for granted. To presume or suppose to be true without direct knowledge. To affect and pretend to have. Make sure to note when I insult people I don't do it through ambiguous and hard to understand brief mentions. I make it very clear to whom I am speaking and to whom my words are directed. The Model Society, the brainchild of The Duke. Do you notice the trend here, Sensei? You're right Sensei; I can understand though why you'd feel insulted You did it all on your own. I'd you for that but I'm not in the mood. I suppose the rest of the FAW just didn't like you Sensei, or they would have helped you.' {Grin. Breath. Continue.} 'Respect. To have deferential regard for. Esteem. To treat with consideration. To have reference to. Concern. You know… this is a word that I often try to avoid giving out and using because it always comes back to haunt you in this wrestling business. I can't remember ever telling a person that I respect them… but I will admit that I do respect certain aspects of a person's character or being. Sensei, you don't know me well enough to respect me. I'll give you the benefit of the doubt and say it was a Freudian slip but just warn you to be wary of releasing the word so easily. You may respect things about me, just as I do you. Perhaps we both respect the fact that we've grown up well enough to be changed men. I definitely respect that in you and in myself and I do highly appreciate it if that is what you respect in me. But as for The Great One, using the word respect is hard for me. Without a doubt, you can't disrespect the man for his in-ring ability. His track record speaks for himself: he's one of the top ranked men in FAW, he's a former Hardcore Title holder, he's a future World contender, he's held various titles in various leagues, and he's got lots of money. Well, ok… perhaps the last part was just a sarcastic shot at his flamboyancy but I couldn't help it. Well, I can respect his wrestling ability - but that doesn't say much. I respect the ability of any man like myself that takes the chance of stepping into a wrestling ring along with me. What I refuse to respect is the arrogant, show-offish, better than you attitude that the man exudes through every pore of his being. It's impossible to respect something like that. To me, it's like trying to respect Charles Manson for killing people. It's just unnatural.' {He seems to shiver a bit.} 'Original. Immediately produced by one's on mind. Not copied. Of or belonging to the beginning. Fresh. New. Allow me to alert everyone to a shocking discovery… there have been people exactly like Sensei in wrestling before. There have been people in the FAW before. You, Japanese Boy, are nothing but a carbon copy of the man that defeated you before, Foamy. Arrogant rich boys with a bit of skill that work your way through the top by cheating with the hands of friends that end up turning their backs on you. You don't even seem to realize that you are nothing special. Now, if any of you out there happened to see the movie Multiplicity you will know what exactly happens when you make a clone of a clone such as we have here in FAW in the form of Foamy. For those of you who don't know the movie, just take a look over in FWO at a guy named Neo and you'll understand.' {Another smile. Another breath. Another continue.} 'Critic. One who judges anything by some standard or criterion. One whose profession is to judge or evaluate. A faultfinder. Sensei, you can try to mock my own originality by saying that I merely rehash things that the critics have said about you and that would be a very sound argument… …had I not made it abundantly clear that I WAS rehashing what the critics have said. You see: there's a difference between plagiarism and citing your sources. I'm sure you wouldn't know that since it's inherently obvious that you don't exactly have a college-level education. Well, even if you do… community college doesn't count. I'm not a critic Sensei, unless you'd like to call me a faultfinder. In that case, you'd be admitting that you had faults. We definitely don't want that, because it would taint our huge quantity of prestige that you hold in our eyes.' {Yet another smile. Yet another breath. Yet another continue.} 'Recognition. Attention or favourable notice. Sensei, it may be true that you are very well recognized in comparison with myself and the other two competitors in this match, but recognition does not win your matches. The notice that you gain may earn you matches and title shots, but it does not guarantee you a win. Simply because you are the upcoming challenger to the FAW International Title does not make you the prime candidate for the FAW World title. Your newfound fame does not automatically make you the victor in this match; it merely makes you a favourite. That means nothing to myself, coz it’s us whom are generally pegged as underdogs anyway. On the topic of recognition, it may also be true that I haven't won any huge matches to gain a level of notoriety to compare with you. I suppose my biggest victory comes in the form of former FAW World Champion, Skipster. Besides, the majority of the wrestlers in this FAW can't even comprehend simple sentence structure, let alone median mathematics. Allow me to speak yet another cliché often spoken by our contemporaries… the past means nothing. The future means nothing, aside from the fact that the four of us will stand face to face and wrestle our hearts out to try to earn the bauble that proclaims us champions.' {He sighs.} 'Elench. That part of an argument on which its conclusiveness depends. That which convinces of refutes an antagonist. A refutation. A specious but fallacious argument. A sophism. Coming from the Greek term elenchos, which means an examination or more specifically a cross-examination. Now, none of you used this term but I really felt I should throw it in here to explain exactly what I'm doing if you don't understand. Sensei, you may have fairly good rhetorical skills but the fact remains that you have no basis of an argument. Like a politician, you blow a whole lot of wind into the air yet when the wind clears - not a sound is heard. Socrates said that no man should be judged on what he appears to say but what he truly says. According to that philosophical tradition, you would come up as a pure lot of nothingness. You can try to sound like you know what you are talking about and go on and on about your accomplishments and try to insult me, but the fact remains that you have no central focus or point from which you draw your arguments. On the topic of speaking praise, Socrates said the following, according to Plato's Symposium: '…it now seems that praising things well isn't like that; it seems to be a question of hyperbole and rhetoric, regardless of truth or falsehood.' If you take that fragment and replace 'praising' with 'speaking', we find out exactly what is wrong with the world that we work and live in. Nobody focuses on the truth of their speech anymore, only on the speech itself. If all of us were as wise as Socrates, we may not have the problems of people like Sensei running around - but unfortunately, we do and they have to be dealt with.' {Pause. A few breaths. A shake of the head.} 'Misconstrue. To interpret with error or mistake. To misunderstand. Now, this is something that is generally done for one of two reasons: intentionally to upset the speaker of the original words or accidentally through sheer ignorance. Let Sensei try to tell you for all I care, but everyone makes their own selection when they give an opinion of their opponents. I feel that Sensei just doesn't understand what I'm trying to tell him due to his own ignorance. Perhaps he read a transcript of what I said or a review of something while he was busy playing on the Internet that changed my words to something different. That is what got Sensei so upset at me. Regardless of the situation surrounding his ignorance, it exists nonetheless He didn't understand that I was merely quoting the critics and not directly criticizing him myself. He didn't understand that I predicted his mentioning of his recent achievements and mentioned them anyway. He attempted to misconstrue my words to be direct insults for the sole purpose of trying to contest those insults. In the end, he merely ended up looking like an ass. A big golden ass.' {He can't help but stifle a laugh.} 'Trust. Firm reliance on the integrity, ability, or character of a person. Care. Custody. I don't trust many people in this world. I can't even fully say that I trust Xander and Spawn yet, but they're higher on the list than anyone else in this league. On the scale of trust in this upcoming match, however, Sensei, I'm not saying that I trust you - but I don't mistrust you either.' 'Fried Chicken. 12 World Chicken Festivals have been hatched. The 13th chicken fest will cluck Sept. 26-29 in London, Ky. The egg-citing event takes place in Laurel County, home of the Colonel's first restaurant. The festival draws more than 250,000 cackling humans…You will turn into a chicken!' {His face suddenly grows stern.} 'Gold. A malleable and ductile metal. Money and riches. Something regarded as having great value or goodness. This is what it all boils down to, doesn't it? I mean - after all of the words are done spoken, after all of the punches are done thrown, and after all of the blood is spilled - one of us will stand triumphant as the North Star Champion. One of us will hold the gold, the standard of greatness in this world of ours. One of the two gladiators heading into the arena will be the true 'Golden' one. Sensei may have a nickname but only Sundays results will determine which of us will come out on top and possess the item of great value. I hope it will be me. We all have the hope, so that isn't what will win us the match. We all have skill or we wouldn't be here, so that isn't exactly what will win us the gold. We all have the drive… but the level of it will be what determines who becomes the champion. I have a high level of desire. Whether or not any of you three have a higher level remains to be seen. I shall see you all on Sunday for our desire test. I'm studying, I pray you are as well.' {Blink. Stare.} 'Blessed be.' {Colour gone. Blackness envelops.}
