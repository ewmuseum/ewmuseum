Can you focus or are you scared? Apocalypse Furiously Addictive Wrestling Roleplay #20 Date: 9-17-02 Location: In a workout gym and at Apoc's house ...Have you ever just woke up and set your mind to something? Something that was to make you or break you in your life. Something that you never wanted to happen when you are right in the moment. Well sometimes it happens. Things happen in your life that make you either weak or strong. Can you come back from them or will you fall to a nothing. In this piece you will see if the one they call Apocalypse can or if he falls from the Storm... (The scene opens up in a sharp but dusty work out room. Smoke and sweat form every work that has either stepped foot in or stepped foot over the area. Nothing seemed too special at the time but would it get real fun real fast? As the camera paned out and around the room you could see all kinds of stuff. Pictures of old things, work out gear that people haven't used for awhile, and even a few things that don't belong there. Nothing you couldn't tell that should be there indicated what would happen next. A loud sound came form the back of the room as movement from the camera twisted around. From the shock of the eye you can see one man lifting what looked to be five hundred pounds on bench press. Nothing that a normal man could do. Who was this man? What did he do to be lifting five hundred pounds?) (The man pushed up the weights from his large chest with a breath of air each time. Up and down, up and down, up and down was all to it. Not letting yourself get beat by a little tiny thing of weights. The camera came in closer to maybe get a pick of him but you could already tell. It was Apocalypse himself that was working out there. The effort he was pushing with was more fearsome then a block coming at your head. After about ten so minutes there was a knock at the door. Apocalypse looked over at the door and set the bar back on the rack and pulled himself up. Swinging over to the side of the bench, Apocalypse looked up at the open door and saw his partner in crime Chris Thunder standing there.) Chris Thunder: You working out already? Apocalypse: Got to man, I don't want that two bit World Champion beating me at Distortion. If that happened then I would be a loser forever. Chris Thunder: How you figure that? Just forget about this man, you got all the talent to beat him with out working out. Remember you are a DCV member. We kick ass without trying. (After that was said Thunder walked out and through the door as Apocalypse just got up and walked over to the wall where there was a set of bar bells there. Weighting about a hundred pounds each and a good piece of metal that they where. The right side of the weights went up in a fast motion as Apocalypse made a face for the mirror that was in front of him. Looking right at himself with every intend of think about this Sunday with Chris Storm. The place where a man will be crowd after five months of waiting. The blood that went into his veins drove Apocalypse into every pull up on the bar. It seemed like nothing was going to stop him. As the big man was about to do his tenth one on his left the door had a knock on it again. Apocalypse turned around and saw that it was his other partner in crime. The Faw World Heavyweight Champion.) Apocalypse: What's up Spawn? Spawn: You, why you training for a weak champion like Chris Storm? Apocalypse: Do I really have to explain to you man? You of all people should understand why I am training. If I don't then I will be beat for the one two three. Plain and simple so can I have some privacy? Spawn: Ok man but I was going to go down to that local bar and shoot some pool. (Apocalypse dropped the bar bells and walked over to the other side of the room. Passing the bikes, incline, and the military press. Right to the super kat was where Apocalypse wanted to be. He took his body and lowered his butt down to the ground. Once in that position he took his legs and set them upward to where they could push up the metal plate that held the weights. Once he was in that position Apocalypse took off. One thousand and two hundred pounds was what he lifted. Every inch man the man sweat like a pig. The fear that he said he didn’t have was there. The fear of losing for both him and the federation. He had been waiting for this moment for all his life. The moment to take out a champion in side a square circle.) (Apocalypse kept lifting away as there was once again no one in the room. After about twenty or so lifts he stopped and put the safety bar in place. Rolling away from the one thing that brought out what he didn’t want out. The room was even hotter in it then it once was. The humidity was getting to Apocalypse but be wasn’t going to give up. Just like the Green Bay Packers didn’t give up on super bowl 2. They didn’t give up on there dream and either was Apocalypse. He walked over from getting up from the floor and grabbed the bottle of water that he had on the table. Pouring it over the top of his sweaty head and body, he didn’t even notice the women standing at the door way.) Women: I love a man that is all sweaty. It so turns me on. Apocalypse: Can I help you miss? Women: Oh yes, come help me out on this nice bench press or maybe the curls. Apocalypse: Well I will help you out anyway I can but I am pretty busy. (The women took off her long red shirt that she had on and there was the damnedest thing. The women had nothing on covering her tits. Her bare naked body was showing all the way over Apocalypse eyes. He didn’t even know what to do but just walk over. As the seven footer got over there he grabbed her by the hand and leaned down to her ever so gentle lips. The air was really making it worse as the two felt hotter then ever. There two lips ever so most touched for the first time as a long engaged kiss to place. The woman was better looking then anything he had ever seen. This was the one thing that he didn’t want to happen. Apocalypse, after about thirty seconds pulled away from the women and looked at her with a worried facial.) Apocalypse: Woman, you better hope you weren’t sent her by anyone that I know. Women: I wasn’t Mr., I promise sir. I just needed your hot body over mine. Please take me. Apocalypse: Listen lady, if you don’t get out of here right now and let me train for my match this Sunday on Distortion then there will be hell to pay. (The women took off right out the door as Apocalypse just stood there shaking his head back and forth. Nothing but pain over his face but he didn’t want that to show. If he knew it was going to then it would really go against him. Who ever said that it was going to be easy then they where dead wrong. Apocalypse knew everything was up for grabs in the match. $500,000 dollars was one that was up for grabs. That was a lot of money he had on the match. Was he stupid or was he smart in doing so. Sure that he was going to win the match in true fine Dark Carnival evolution style. The only way he should win. Apocalypse looked over at the clock and saw that it was time for step two in his quest to win his biggest step in life.) (The scene switched out to the parking lot as Apocalypse pushed his alarm key to deactivate his car alarm. The sound form it went of and the 2003 dodge ram pickup was right there. The blue outside with black trim. The man’s ride in his dream of life. He started the truck up and pulled off right into reverse as the smoke came from the tires. Everything was going well for him except for the three interruptions from Spawn, Thunder, and the weird naked women. Nothing was really going right but it could all be worse. Could it? Well we will find out as Apocalypse pulled up to his big house in the middle of nowhere called Quinlan. He pulled in to his fifty-foot drive way and up to his three-story house.) Apocalypse: Home at last. No one will know I am here. I can get some real action in here so that Chris Storm will not be ready for action or to beat me. (The driver side door came open and out stepped Apocalypse. Ready as ever and with a dry body. The air must have cooled him down inside the truck. As you walked up the sidewalk of the three-story house, you could see that there was a swing set on the right side of the path and a tire swing on the left side by the oak tree. The big man got to the front door and opened it right up for the fresh outside air to come in. Apocalypse tossed down the keys on his nice oak table next to the door where his phone was. To bad there wasn’t even a sound in the house as Apocalypse took of for the kitchen. Opening the refrigerator door and pulling out a coke, he pulled the tab back and pushed it open. From the shaken of it, coke went over flowing from the top as Apocalypse screamed out loud.) Apocalypse: Man, am I ever going to get some training in for this match? Why does it always have to happen to me? If it were a match say against Bryan Deas then everything would be fine. I have to go into this match with every button in my mind locked on to him. So please, I got to think about everything I do today. I cannot let my ability’s get in the way. (Apocalypse started to walk for the door but then there was a knock at the door. Anger took over the fear in his eyes. Pain for what he was going to do. Maybe kill someone? Maybe hurt they’re very bad? Apocalypse walked over to the front door and opened it right up and saw that it was the king of interview, James Harris.) James Harris: Did I come at a bad time man? Apocalypse: Well if I say yes then you will be wasting my time for me even standing here. If I say no then I will be lying and still be wasting my own time to get ready. James Harris: So do you need me to come back for that interview that you called for? Apocalypse: I never asked for an interview today. I never asked for one period. How did you come up with this? James Harris: Well it was on the Faw business message machine. You wanted me to come personally. So you want me to come back? Apocalypse: No, just get in here now. (James walked right into the house with cameraman and all. The sharp breath and anger form Apocalypse was really showing. But now there was confusion on his face. He didn’t know if he really did this or not. What did he really care about or not? All he wanted to do was train in which he hadn’t done before. Make blood come from his body and then some. Apocalypse walked into the living room of the house as James Harris took the cameraman and told him to set up right beside the black piano that Apocalypse had. After about ten or so minutes, Harris was ready and set up for the big interview.) James Harris: Well Apocalypse tell me what is going through your head as October 20th comes along? Apocalypse: Can you handle what pain I will bring to the game? The pain that I bring is far more then anything I will ever bring. I say everyday that I am afraid of nothing. Well today I say I am afraid of one thing. That one thing is Chris Storm. I am not afraid of his body or of his talents. No, I am afraid of what will happen to him when we go into that ring. What blood that will be tossed. What sweat that will be poured out of our souls. You see, once in your life you come to something that you must break. Something that holds you back from the possible things that you can have. For five months Chris Storm has been on my mind. Five months he has been there tell me when we will fight. October 20th, that is the day that everything I wish for will be brought out. James Harris: But remember Apocalypse, you have your whole federation behind you. Why would you be afraid of him? Apocalypse: You are a fool if you go into a battle like this and expect home field advantage is better for you. Hell I will be the first to tell you that it doesn’t. You have your fellow wrestlers looking on and then you have thousands of fans watching you. Nothing that you think will settle you down from what is happening. Nothing will settle you I promise. But also, if you look at it in another point of view, it does help you to have home field. You can always cheap your way to a win. But you see, I Apocalypse promise that if any DCV members come down then I will personally break them into. I want to either lose this or win it on my own. The saga of training begins now Chris and on the 20th you will be history in the Faw. World Champion, you may be one over there but here you are just Chris Storm. A great in ring wrestler with lots of skills outside the ring. But Chris, you know and I know that this is more then just about federations and respect. No this is about pride and dignity. (Apocalypse looked over at the camera before rubbing his hair back a little to the side. His eyes focused on everything that he was saying. All the fear and pain right there for him to see. Nothing being held back and everyone watching.) Apocalypse: James, I want to ask you something. Can you tell me what you see in these eyes? James Harris: I see determination to win. Apocalypse: No you don’t, you see a man with a set of eyes ready for the 20th to come around. Everything on the line against you Chris, everything that I have will beat you. In that ring, in your mind I will punish you. Can you feel the heat from the though of that day? You and me in that ring facing each other when that bell rings. I know you can because I can also see it clear. Who wins Chris is unknown, but rest assure I will stop at nothing to get at you. Chris, prepare yourself for the battle that you can’t win. Chris, see you in that ring on the 20th. See you on the mat where the battle begins. (With that the scene slowly fades out to show nothing but darkness on your screen.)
