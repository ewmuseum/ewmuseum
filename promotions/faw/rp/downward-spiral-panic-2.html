Downward Spiral (Panic #2)

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Downward Spiral (Panic #2) Rzarector Furiously Addictive Wrestling Roleplay #139 Date: Jan. 04 Location: Nowhere *The screen opens to pitch black. A small breathing is heard as it is Rzarectors deep breathes. The cold tone is heard as Rzarector begins to speak.* Rzarector: So many times in ones life they are on the wing of destruction. It has happened to several before, and it will happen again. Never once would I ever envisioned it happening to my priest, which it already has. Alot of people are ready to die, yet I ask why? If you are good you will go to heaven. If you are evil you will go to hell. Why would you die not knowing where you are going? That is a fatal mistake of pain and torture. I have seen it happen to several people. The screams of hell still run very vivid in my mind. The story of life always remains the same, no matter where you're at, up or down, there will always be a role for you. Today, I am going to tell you a story of sin, lies, and blood about life. About a guy named Tom Lance. *The voice of Rzarector disappears as Tom lance is heard echoing in the background. As this little hideous name stops echoing the screen opens up. The location is a pleasent neighborhood. Several large white houses, picket fences, and green shrubs surrounding each house. Seventy-five percent of these houses have pools in there backyard. The others have nice looking statues and flower gardens. The roads are trash free as several people are walking the streets in there khackis and dress shirts. The camera zooms around as it shows the very elegant style of this neighborhood. Then the camera hits it. The camera hits a very nice large mansion, yet in the front yard is a very odd man. Walking down the driveway is a figure in all black. He has cuts on his wrists and cuts all over his chest. The man is obviously depressed. As the camera zooms in a little closer to him you notice the black under his eyes. He who has not slept in days, is the same man that will be telling you this story today. The man, Tom Lance, keeps walking up his driveway to check the mail. At the same time the mailman drives up.* Mailman: Good morning Tom, how are you doing today? Feeling any better? *In a depressed tone.* Tom Lance: Well, I guess I'm doing ok. The fact that the world is falling on me and everyone else is smiling does not effect me. I will still be depressed, my life is going to hell. Everyone knows it, even you. Mailman: Tom, listen, whatever is wrong man, we can talk about it. It doesn't matter if it is something that is driving at your mind. Or if it is just simply the loss of something. Whatever it is I'm sure we can work things out. Because just remember, no matter how tough life gets, it all doesnt matter in the end. Remember that. Okay? *Tom puts his head down.* Tom Lance: Well I got an appointment with my therapist today. Hopefully she will be able to work things out for me. You know what? My world is falling around me, but I see signs of getting better in the future. I see several signs of a prosperous thing that is going to happen to me. I don't need anyone to try to console me. I will be a better man. I promise you that. So do I have any mail? Mailman: Well lets have a look here. I see, no mail, just bills. I'm sorry Tom. Tom Lance: Doesn't matter anyways. I haven't heard from my family in over 5 years. Ever since that horrible night. They never send me mail, gifts, or anything anymore. I'm just forgotten in the ancestor tree. They even probably burned every single one of my pictures. I wouldn't doubt it. What do you think about it? Mailman: Well Tom, you know you have to pay your bills. And after what you did to your family, I don't know what to think. First you kicked them out of your house on Christmas Eve. Left them on the streets or in some rinky dink hotel over night. That ruined there whole Christmas. And it was the first time your brother got back from the army in 2 years. Now 7 years have passed, and still no sign of them. Some people it takes longer for them to forgive someone. Or maybe if you called them they could forgive you. *Tom Lance sighs and talks sarcastically.* Tom Lance: I WILL NEVER CALL THEM! Why should I call them? I mean why don't they call me? All I do is sit here by myself, not caring anymore, and paying my damn bills. This world will never end. Why in the hell would I expect it to anyways? God has sent me here to torture on Earth. Maybe I will have something better in the afterlife. *Pauses.* Tom Lance: And I don't need people messing up my life. My brother comes home, so what? I mean come on, look at him, a reject. Then my family just talks to him and ignores him. It's not as if I feel bad enough, that just cuts the line, I don't even care what they think. They want to ignore me, then I'll just kick them out. That way I won't even have to see them anymore. Now they haven't called in five years. And to be truthfully honest, I don't even care if they do or not. Mailman: You talk about the afterlife and God. God tells you to respect your parents. You show no respect. Tom Lance: Good day. *Tom ignores the mailman as he walks back down his driveway to his house. He looks through the bills and laughs as all of them says 'Have a good month.' Tom was a very tall person. Black hair, artificially dyed, short black beard, and dark brown eyes. He always wore the same attire every day, black t shirt, and black pants. He had big bushy eyebrows, soft big lips, and teeth the color of coffee itself. This wasn't always how Tom looked however. He once looked different. As the camera freezes on a picture of Tom walking into his house, Rzarectors voice is once again heard in the background.* Rzarector: Mr. Lance, as everyone called him, was a very strange person. From about six years ago he changed. Once the richest man in this small community, now the outcast of it all. He worked for a very large business by the name of Intocol. It was a graphic design company that did all the main graphics for all television shows. He was very popular on tv. Mr. Lance still owns the largest mansion in this town. Wearing only tuxedos and gel in his hair, Tom gained everyone's respect for being nice and being very courteous to everyone else. Then the day of May 27, 1996 hit. Tom never even say it coming. *Flashback to May 27, 1996.* *Tom Lance is seen waking up in his rich silk sheet bed. He hops up and gels up his hair and throws on a very nice elegant black tuxedo. Mr. Lance walks over to the mirror and smiles at himself as he sprays the worlds finest cologne on his shirt. Two squirts, just like he always liked it. He quickly ran downstairs and grabbed a raisin nut bran oatmeal muffin. His usual mornings but this one was very different. He went outside to greet his mailman before he left off to work.* Tom Lance: Why hello there! Mailman: Hello Tom, whats happening this beautiful morning? Tom Lance: Nothing much, but you know what? I feel so alive today, yet so free. Mailman: That is wonderful. Tom Lance: Well I got to get off to work. When business calls, it calls. Mailman: Have a good day then. *Tom went back to his driveway and opened up the garage. There you see a yellow BMW, year 1996. A beautiful car that Tom then jumps in and starts up. It roars as he takes off down the road. This little community is still very beautiful. Birds chirping, green trees, several flowers, and the nicest yards you have ever seen. Mr. Lance drives by all the neighbors waving as he gets to the road of his business. He makes a right and then another as he turns in to the employee parking. Mr. Lance jumps out looking very elegant as he see's several people outside crying. Tom wonders why so he goes up and asks one.* Tom: What is wrong with everyone? *Woman sobbing.* Woman: Well we came in to work today to find out that, we are shutting down! *Tom is very shocked.* Tom Lance: WHAT???? This can't be happening. Please tell me this isn't. Woman: I'm sorry but it is. We lost our advertisements today. And now nobody is willing to lets us make photos for them. My career in this job is over, I'm going to go bankrupt. Tom Lance: Well your not the only one. *Tom frowns as he walks back to his car and drives away. Lance goes straight back to his house and walks through the front door to hear the phone ringing.* Tom Lance: Hello? Police Officer: Yes this is Deputy John Mcgruff. I have very bad news for you. Is this Tom Lance? Tom Lance: Yes it is. What is wrong? Police Officer: Well I'm sorry to say but your friend, John Litterman, died in a car crash this morning. Tom Lance: What? Police Officer: Yes I'm sorry, but he died on impac....... *Tom immediately hung up the phone as he went over to his bed and laid down. John meant everything to him, yet Tom didn't know how to react. His whole world crashed down on him right before his eyes. Yet Mr. Lance never cried. Didn't shed one tear. For the time of 1996 to present, Tom has never looked back. He kept all his feelings inside. More disasters led up after this, yet Tom just kept ignoring them. The problems kept building up inside his mind til he became more and more depressed. Then one day he went crazy and tried to kill 8 people. He was arressted but released for mental issues. Now he must see a therapist once a week. Rzarectors voice is heard once again.* Rzarector: Now we take you back to present. Tom has already gotten ready and is now talking with the therapist. *The room of the therapist is very nice. Wooden walls with a wooden floor. A very nice country smell to it as candles are burning all across the room. Tom looks relaxed as he is laying on a nice leather couch as the therapist is sitting in a wooden chair. She begins talking with him.* Therapist: So how has your life been treating you this past week? Tom Lance: Same ole, same ole. You know everytime I come here you ask me that same exact question. I can't help but wonder is it the starting point to our little conversation. I'm not sure right now, yet I can rest assure. My life will be changing, it has already changed. Therapist: But Mr. Lance, you still wear black. Still very depressed. And Tom, you still have fresh cuts on you from your encounters with the blade. ToM Lance: The blade doesnt matter anymore, I cut myself to release my pain. It works, but you'll never understand. Yet one day you will meet someone who you will connect with. They will then explain it to you. You understand? Therapist: Yes I do, Tom have you been taking your medication? Tom Lance: Yes I have. All of it, and not missed one dosage. That is the funny part, it has still not affected me the way it is supposed to. I have more of a free mind than you think. Therapist: So your saying mind over matter? Tom Lance: That is right, mind over matter. That is why the pills are not working. Thats right, I will show you tonight just how I can be. Trust me, tonight my life will change. Therapist: Your not going to do anything stupid are you? Tom Lance: What do you mean by stupid? Therapist: You know, like hurt yourself, or cause harm to other people? Nothing bad is it? Remember Tom, positive. Tom Lance: Positive, positive, positive. I know that. That is why this is going to be positive. I'm going to trip. Trust me, trippin like that. Therapist: Drugs? Tom Lance: Nope, an even better trip. Therapist: What? Tom Lance: Wait and see. *Tom Lance left that therapist office that afternoon without the therapist ever knowing what was happening. Mr. Lance walked home that day a better man, a man that was to defy all odds against him. Still a millionare with a chance at everything he ever wanted. But depression had got him,and now he was on a downward spiral. Rzarectors voice is heard once again.* Rzarector: Tom Lance, the strange person who once was normal, went home that night and shot himself in the head with a pistol. The police said he had a smile on his face as half his head was blown off. Mr. Lance was never forgotten. Everyone thought he was doing better. Even he did, for he thought he was going to do better in the afterlife. Little did he know that his name was not in the book of life. So now he serves an eternal torturing life in hell. See Lee Smith, Tom Lance life was kind of like yours. You want your old life back. The legend status of being a main eventer, being the heavyweight champion. And once all of that was gone you went downhill. What you want can not always come true, no matter the money or the power. For everyone goes on a downward spiral every now and then. That is the truth, you shall beleive me someday. If you ever win the title Lee Smith, it will corrupt you. Once you lose it, you will go downhill just like Tom Lance. Leading to mass depression and suicide. Remember that, is the title worth that much to you? *The screen closes.*
