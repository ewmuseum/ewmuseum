Priests, Sin, and Bad news

10/15/02
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Priests, Sin, and Bad news Cyrus Raynes Furiously Addictive Wrestling Roleplay #4 Date: 10/15/02 Location: Catholic Church - Sin the universal bad word for evil acts. Yet every single man, woman, or child committs a Sin at one time or another during their lives. Some committ greater amounts of or just plain worse types of Sin then others and that is all that really seperates us from one another. There are liars, cheaters, rapists, murders, theives, and many other types of Sinners in the world. They are possibly the most diverse group of people in the world... Simply because every one in the world falls under this heading....Including Cyrus Raynes. As of now Raynes has lied, cheated, backstabbed, decieved, and basically destroyed anything and anyone he has ever wanted to in his life time. He has decieved the fans, most recently on Sunday when he and Apocalypse deceived the entire world with a make believe attack. He has cheated both in and out of the ring in his life and gotten away with it every single time. He has backstabbed people in the past who truly thought that Raynes was their best friend. And what has all this Sin in his black heart got him? The fame and fortune of a life time in the wrestling world, and most recently HIS International Title. But that is the funny thing about Sin and how it can come back to bite you in the funniest of ways and some times....In the not so funny ways. [The out shot opens inside a large beautiful church some where in Indianapolis. The place is lit by candle light at the moment and it glows just enough to see the beauty of the oak pews and the well painted ceiling. The church is only about a quarter full at this time of the day, as it is early in the morning around seven A.M. The camera panes around the church and gets a look at about the twenty or so people who are strung out about the church praying, talking with priests, or other religous activities that go on during a normal day in the Catholic Church. A few moments later FAW Superstar and current International Champion Cyrus Raynes walks into the building wearing some very nice black slacks, a wonderfully done dress shirt, and a long black over coat. As he takes a few steps into the church he removes the shades from his face that he had been wearing, revealing those familiar cold dark blue eyes that he possess.] 'What is your business here today my son?' asks the elderly Priest, who is dressed in the traditional black robe, with the type of soft and gental voice that you might expect to hear come from a grandfather to his grandson. 'I must speak with you Father, for I have some questions about Sin.' Raynes looks directly into the elderly priests eyes with a certain amount of importance in his voice. 'Of course my son. We are all here to help the weak with their problems with Sin.' speaks the elderly man as he motions for Raynes to follow him to a pew and have a seat. [The two walk a few rows down until they are not in ear shot of any of the other people who are there at this time. They sit down on the soft red padding that covers the hard oak bench. The elderly Priest looks with caring eyes at Raynes and waits for Raynes to tell him what his troubles are.] {Raynes}: 'Well then, I want to know what exactually qualifies as Sin, what I have to do to repent of it, and does it count if it hasn't happened yet Father?' {Priest}: 'Well my son a Sin is a Sin no matter what, if it is something that you know would not be looked apon favorably in God's eyes. You must not only repent but you must be truthful and sincere about it at the time. Now tell me, is it that you have cheated?' {Raynes}: 'Yes I have done that, but that is not it.' {Priest}: 'You have lied and deceived some one?' {Raynes}: 'All the time, but that has nothing to do with it either.' Raynes speaks with a small smirk on his face. {Priest}: 'Well, then have you laid down with some one else's wife recently?' the Priest asks with some discomfort at the easy ness with witch Raynes has answered these questions. {Raynes}: 'Not recently, I am married and very faithful, but I am quilty of that one as well. But we are wasting time. I am much more concerned with what happens under the destruction and disfiguring catagory.' [The Priest shifts in his seat uncomfortably as Raynes sits back and crosses his arms over his massive chest. Some of the Elderly Priests kindness has begun to wear off of his face as he takes a deep breath and attempts to answer Raynes question.] {Priest}: 'Well my son, destroying things and disfiguring things is a Sin because it is against the law. And the Bible says that we must not only must we obey God's Law but we must also obey Man's law. Now what is it that you destroyed or disfigured.' The Priest asks with some unease in his voice. {Raynes}: 'It is not actually a thing or a what, but a who. And though I have laid down some terrible beatings on people in the past Father, I am afraid that they in no way compare to the ones I intend to administer over the next few weeks.' Raynes simply smiles arrogantly as he looks on at the completely shocked priest. {Priest}: 'Striking a man unprovoked is not right sir. You must find a way to unleash this anger you seem to know you are holding inside in another way or you will destroy your self.' {Raynes}: 'Then I should be alright after all since these will not be un-provoked. So what do I have to do simply for the act then?' {Priest}: 'You can not simply do anything to take care of an evil deed that you know you are going to committ before hand. But out of curiosity I would like to know what it is that provoked these thoughts from you?' [Raynes uncrosses his arms and leans forward to look directly into the eyes of the Priest with complete seriousness on his face. The Priest seems to be a bit terrified by what the answer might be and the fear shows. This only makes Raynes even more comfortable with the fact that he came here.] {Raynes}: 'Ignorance, weakness, smart mouthness in general. The wrestlers of FAW have walked around as of late as if they are something when they are simply not at all worth the air that they breath. I am going to start destroying every single one of them that the fans seem to think are worthy of praise.' [Complete shock covers the Priests face as he stumbles to stay calm. He has to take a few deep breathes before he can compose himself fully. Finally he looks back toward Raynes and sighs a couple of times before he begins to speak once again in the soft and caring tone that he began with.] {Priest}: 'My Son, you can not simply attack people for those reasons. Ignorance, praise whether it is deserved or not, or their arrogance. If people could get attacked for those reasons then practically every person in the world would be under those attacks. Everyone is ignorant, praised wrongfully, or arrogant at one time or another in their lives. Even you my Son.' [Raynes begins to laugh loudly catching the attention of the other people in the church. They all stop and stare at Raynes who is now standing over the frightened priest. The laughter is now gone and it is replaced by complete anger and fire. He points his finger at the Priest as he begins to shout.] {Raynes}: 'YOU DARE TO LECTURE ME OLD MAN! NEVER MAKE THE MISTAKE OF PUTTING ME IN THE SAME CATAGORY AS MEN LIKE SIN AND YOURSELF. I AM ABOVE ALL OF THAT. YOU HAVE TO HAVE A SOUL TO SIN YOU DAMN FOOL AND I LOST MINE LONG AGO. THERE IS BARELY ANYTHING LEFT IN ME THAT IS REMOTELY CARING ANYMORE AND IT SURE ISN'T FOR YOUR RULES OR OF SIN AND PEOPLE! (Raynes quits shouting but speaks loud enough for people to still hear.) To think that you would ever put some one in the same catagory like Priest who rape alter boys and Sin who claims to be a savior. You will pay very soon for this and you will not like it.' [With that Raynes walks out of the church and into the sun light of the outside world. The people look around shocked at the scene that they just saw. As Raynes walks a few steps out of the church a limo pulls up at the curb and stops. The back window comes down and we see Peyton Manning. His face if white like he saw a ghost and he is frantic.] {Peyton}: 'There you are Raynes. Come on lets go man Reanna was in a bad accident and she is in the hospital!' [Raynes quickly gets into the limo with fear in his eyes and very quickly. The limo speeds off down the road as the shot fades out on the tail lights going off in the distance.]
