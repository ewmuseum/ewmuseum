Time has ended, the true meaning is out.

November 8
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Time has ended, the true meaning is out. Rzarector Furiously Addictive Wrestling Roleplay #69 Date: November 8 Location: Middle of nowhere Narrator: What is it that makes a man? A glorious prize? The fact that this one prize could make him stand out? Not really that others want to be like this one being with a prize, but more like they want the prize itself. That is what a wrestling championship is all about. The greed and hate that goes into the title, none other than that. Every man wants it to show that he is the best, it is a motivating force in wrestling. Nothing will unsurpass a title belt. Yet so many have them now and days, so why do they still sulk in this hate and greed? *The screen opens to show Rzarector sitting on a coffin in the middle of nowhere. Dark shadows cover the land as the howling winds spread throughout the trees. Rzarector sits there in his black robe and green eyes shining out of it. The black hooded figures lays down on the coffin looking up into the sky of red stars and the black sun. Rzarector hisses as a red shooting star goes across the sky.* Rzarector: What is it with people this day? Running around acting like they are better just because they have a title. Well that is in the bible, under greed and hate. Share the wealth, never sulk over your own riches. Yet several still do it that way, why? Why is it there every where I turn in the FAW, there is someone saying they are better than other people. You got Xander for example, he is the Ratings of the FAW. You think that if we cut all the wrestling and just let him talk for 2 hours, you think people would still watch? Probably not, or maybe the fact that if Damien Ryan took his Hardcore Title, does that make him better than us? No it doesn't. Maybe some have forgotten, maybe they haven't. I am the tag team champions. Now have you ever seen me gloat over this title? No you haven't, I just act like it doesnt even exist. The fact that I beat the two best people in the FAW, that doesnt mean anything to me. I am not better than them for one reason, the reason that Spawn and Great One fought each other in that match. So Spawn was going in 3 on 1, now who could have survived that match? (Rzarector sighs as his forehead shines in the black suns light. There you see a imprint of a cross shining out over his face. God was truly looking down on Rzarector.) Rzarector: Some say that my words are not true. I am not really a prophet, yet I have always been a prophet. I will never be more than that. I'm a wrestler with a title belt that means nothing to me. Does wrestling mean nothing to me? No, wrestling means alot to me, but not as much as God. I am God's angel, I am the black priest that most hate, yet some love. Some see me as the one who will bring God to Earth. That isn't me, I will not bring God here. I Just talk to him, does he ever talk to me? No probably not, yet sometimes I feel as if he does. Why? I don't know, I just know he is coming soon: now, then, or there. He will appear in the darkest of times. When the Christians are on there knees begging for food. The world will be rotting when he comes. (Rzarector hisses as he turns around on the coffin he starts clawing at it with his long black fingernails. Rzarector lays there with his eyes watering as it appears he has learned something from his lifes. He's letting out his deepest confessions. Rzarector rolls back over as he smiles once more.) Rzarector: Damien Ryan, you think I have forgotten? You think I have forgotten about our match this Sunday? No, soon I will have that Hardcore and Tag Titles around my waist. Yet will it mean anything to me? No it won't mean a thing. Just a award that some envy around me. Why don't I envy these awards? Because I have trained myself to never feel any jealousy, hate, or greed in me. Some may feel the greed, they just don't understand. I have felt love, hate, and pain in my life. Pain so harsh that one living man could never heal from it. I have never healed from it, all I do is walk around with no purpose, looking for that light again that made me feel so good. I will never find it I feel, yet I know it is out there. I cried for 3 weeks in a row, God was never there. I prayed and prayed for mercy on my rotting heart. Yet no calls back, then he did give me a call. Now I look at God and say this. (Rzarector pauses.) Rzarector: Just leave me in pain.....and no lies...... (Rzarector yells as loud as he can.) Rzarector: JUST KEEP ME WITH THE THOUGHT OF THE HAPPINESS!!!!! (The echoes of this voice spread throughout this small clearing in the middle of nowhere. Rzarector looks down as the hood of his robe fell off of his face. There his green hair, white fangs, and green eyes shine out from his face. The cross in the middle of his face is now red as his face is turned into his arms. The black sun starts turning yellow as he raises his face. There you see it. There you see streams of tears coming down from his eyes. Rzarector is crying.) Rzarector: What is this? Tears coming from my eyes? God help me, I feel so lonely. I feel unhappy to the point where I can't bear it anymore. Release me from this life, just let me go with you. I will be at your command everyday. Just clear my mind of my unbearable pain that has resurfaced again. I cry every night as I think about it. The pain was love taken away from me, then it turned to hate that I had for everyone. Now the pain is just pain, wrenching my heart into a twist. My heart is choking on the fact that I can't get what I want. Every night I think about it, would I be happy with this pain? Or would I just be more heartbroken over it? I don't know yet, yet I look for the signs from you God. Just please help me out some. I'm not begging for more, let me come face to face with the pain. I've done it before, just let me do it again. Give me courage, I don't know which type of courage, but one or the other. Just give me one more chance to do what my mind and soul tells me to do. Then I will never ask you of anything else, never help me again. I will still be there praying and being at your every command if you do call on me. Just God, please let something happen. I need to let the pain break the surface, I just need to let it leave my body once and for all. Even if I go for the pain, or I choose to let it go, one or the other, I want to be happy. Because if I'm not happy, then whats the point of life? (Rzarector's tears keep pouring down as his face looks very sad. His lips are quivering causing his fangs to slice his lips open. His nose is running onto his lip as he is wiping it off with his hand. The eyes of the once great prophet who was invulnerable, is now bloodshot from the salt in the eye. Rzarector gets off of the coffin and starts walking away as the black coffin disappears. Rzaretor pauses as he looks back, there a dark shadow appears from it. Is it? Could it be? It's the once gone but now back Mass Murderers from the FAW. They are there in there painted faces looking at Rzarector. ) Black Ghost: You know man, we were once in the FAW. We had all our dreams, we heard you talking to God and your right. Nothing will ever make you feel better unless you follow your heart. Rzarector: I want to, it just seems impossible right now. How? Black Ghost: How? You got to just take a leap, life is short and if you don't try while your young, you got nothing when your old. Know what I'm saying? Nightmare King: Yeah because you make what you want when your young, then when you get older you fall back on it. I don't care what it is, money, power, or pain. When you get that going young, when your older you'll have it. It's your choice, if not now soon. You need it, now you go get it man. It's your destiny. Rzarector: I always looked up to you guys, even if you werent the cleanest souls. Yet I Knew you had a message. So follow the pain? Black Ghost: The only way to get rid of a pain is to face it. That is my beleive, if you love so much, and you never face it. Then how do you know if you love or not? That is why you have to face the pain. Rzarector: To finish off the contract, figure if it will work or not? Nightmare King: Right, now there are ways to do this. Yet you have to find your own. Man we gots to go. You've been called. (Nightmare King and Black Ghost of the Mass Murderers start to disappear as Rzarector goes into shock. No more tears are coming down as Rzarector pulls up his black hood of his robe back over his head. Now his green eyes shine brighter and his fangs are whiter. The cross is back to black as Rzarector starts feeling weird. There he wakes up from his dream.) Rzarector: That was weird, very weird dreams. (Rzarector gets up out of his bed and walks in the living room to get something to eat.)
