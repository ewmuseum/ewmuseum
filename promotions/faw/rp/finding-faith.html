Finding Faith

10th of February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Finding Faith Spider Furiously Addictive Wrestling Roleplay #268 Date: 10th of February 2003 Location: The Unknown Church [The scene is a church, the pews are empty, the candles are lit and they have a spectacular effect on the stain glass windows. Alone at the front praying to a statue of the Virgin Mary is a lone man. The person is wearing some baggy jeans, a chain dangling from the jeans, some black and silver trainers and finally a black hooded sweatshirt, with the hood over his head. He is kneeling and looking at the statue while praying. The image pans round to reveal Vincent Beck, a lone figure surrounded by solitude. After moments he rises and takes down his hood, clasping his hands at his front he slowly walks out of the church, each step showing respect, as he reaches the large wooden doors he turns and admires all the this place of bliss before turning to the open half of the door and walking through. Outside is a place of surreal beauty, trees, flowers, birds singing, unusual for the time of year. Leaning against a Land Rover is Lazlo, Vincent’s buddy; he is wearing some blue jeans and a sky blue shirt] Lazlo: “Did you find it…?” [Vincent merely nods and gets into the passenger seat of the car, Lazlo looks around and sighs, looking slightly bored he opens his door and climbs in, he starts the car but does not drive, Vincent takes in the tremendous innocence of the area as Lazlo talks] Lazlo: “Was he right?” Vincent: “… he?” Lazlo: “The priest.” [Spider shakes his head and sighs heavily; he pauses for a moment of thought before looking at Lazlo again] Vincent: “No, no he was not right.” Lazlo: “So you’re going to be like this for longer?” Vincent: “Like what?” Lazlo: “Stuck in thought, unhappy.” Vincent: “Oh no my dear Lazlo, the priest was not right, it was his boss that was correct.” [Vincent smiles a very ‘Cheeky’ smile and looks out the window again, still taking in the scenery] Vincent: “The essence of life… the wonders… all created…” Lazlo: “Umm… are you okay?” Vincent: “Better than ever my good friend.” [Lazlo looks kind of scared and worried, he starts driving, the scenic route is still being admired] Vincent: “I didn’t find him…” Lazlo: “Who…?” Vincent {Ignoring Lazlo}: “He… he found… me?” Lazlo: “Dude you aren’t making any sense. Did he find you? Are you asking me? Who is ‘he’?” Vincent: “Lazlo James Hawk. Do you really need me to spell this out for you child?” Lazlo: “Spell WHAT out?” Vincent: “I’m not spelling ‘what’, I am spelling his name.” [Lazlo just rolls his eyes and keeps driving, they eventually reach a gate, it is open, they drive straight through and get to a main road, the car sits for a moment before Lazlo spots a space to get in, he quickly darts in as another car comes towards him, he barely escapes by the skin off his back as the other car goes blaring by, the already down window gives him an opportunity] Lazlo: “FUCK ME ASS-HOLE!” [Vincent tuts, Lazlo brings his head back in as he drives, he quickly glances at Vincent and see’s him tutting before turning back to the road] Lazlo: “What’s wrong NOW?” Vincent: “I should scrub out your mouth with soap and give you a new tongue for such language is uncalled for!” Lazlo: “What the hell?” Vincent: “See what I mean?” Lazlo: “See what? That I swore? {Sarcastic} Sorry Mr Fresh Lips!” Vincent: “So I have said a few curses in my time, I’m going to make up for it. But you just do not understand do you my dear Lazlo?” Lazlo: “Well look at it like this, if I did I wouldn’t be guessing now would I?” Vincent: “Ah but you would, for you would be guessing what changed my mind. He came to me, my mind was cleared and my sins were forgiven.” Lazlo {Shocked}: “YOU FOUND GOD?!” [Lazlo laughs] Vincent: “No, were you not listening? As I said previously, HE found ME.” Lazlo {Laughing}: “Whatever you think man… {under his breath}… screwball…” Vincent: “You think I am crazy? My dear Lazlo, I will show you on Panic. With the help of Jeffrey Harris and the ‘So Called’ Great One and the real Great One my losing streak will not continue!” Lazlo: “Losing streak? You lost two god damned matches!” Vincent: “I’d ask you not to say such words in front of me, thou shalt not use they Lord’s name in vain. Besides it’s still a streak, I have never been happy with cheating, but this is the type of interference I need, interference from the sublime power…” Lazlo: “Don’t you mean a sublime power? You want that you go to Lisa Foster, whoa she is fit!” Vincent: “One of the deadly sins is lust young Hawk.” Lazlo: “Don’t call me that.” Vincent: “Call you what Hawk?” Lazlo: “Hawk.” Vincent: “Honour thy father and thy mother my dear Hawk, honour them by not being ashamed of your names that is why I am known as my given name, Vincent Kristopher Beck.” Lazlo: “You really are a dick you know?” Vincent: “I pity you, being devoid of belief.” [They stay silent as Lazlo takes up a junction, they come to a series of large houses, not quite big enough to be mansions but not actually small enough to be houses, Lazlo slowly drives down the road and stops outside one that has large gates outside] Lazlo: “Please dude, tell me, are you serious?” Vincent: “Deadly.” [Lazlo sighs] Lazlo: “I would never leave you man, you’re like a brother or father or something to me…” Vincent: “You already have two fathers, no need for a father…” Lazlo: “First of all, don’t forget you adopted me, so you are my father, but you treat me like your brother, and until now God was not my father, but you are the one person in this world I would trust with my life. So I guess this is goodbye alcohol and sex huh?” Vincent: “No Lazlo. You’re incorrect, God does not forbid such items, after all at the last supper they drank wine. Perhaps you just need to read up on your Bible, learn why we are here, I need to also.” Lazlo: “How often?” Vincent: “Often.” Lazlo: “You know, I’m not sure Nina or anyone else will be happy with this change. Nina always liked the renegade that just didn’t give a da… {Stopping in the middle of the word he takes a breath}… that just didn’t seem to care.” Vincent: “You are learning. But now I have something to care about, slobs, lazy children, lack of faith, how many people do you think of the millions that die are sent down to that son of a witch Satan?!” Lazlo: “I’d say about…” Vincent: “Rhetorical question Lazlo.” Lazlo: “No really, I’d say about one in every million.” [Vincent sticks out his lower lip and nods knowingly] Vincent: “I would bet you’re right but gambling is against God’s word also.” Lazlo: “Aww…” Vincent: “Do not worry, we can find other legitimate vices, like the thought of entering heaven and helping all others to heaven!” Lazlo: “Very well…” [They get out of the car and lean on either side] Vincent: “Together…?” [Lazlo nods] Vincent: “We will bring back the faith to the World.” [They look up at the sky on either side of the car; they are in complete awe of the sun that shines down on them. Happy with themselves they keep staring as the scene ends]
