Frontlines(2/3) WWC #2 RP Rzarector Furiously Addictive Wrestling Roleplay #168 Date: Jan 23, 2003 Location: Same in Heaven ~~~Last time we saw the five demons come to life in front of Rzarector. The gates of Heaven were being torn apart by these evil monsters, therefore God sent Rza to Heaven to try to get back at them. Rzarector, not dead or alive, is letting God set his soul apart from his body. Now the demons are in for a fight as Rza is in the fighting mood.~~~ *We are back as Rzarector is still in the air about to deliver the kick to Demon Profusion. Rza's blue eyes shine out as God's words still run wild in his mind. Profusion looks on as Rzarector is coming down and is about to land a wild kick to the side of his demonic face when Demon Violator jumps in and rams his fist into the back of Rzarector. Rza falls to the ground as The five demons circle him once again. Rzarector stands up as he looks like he is in pain from that harsh kick by Violator. Rza then hisses.* Rzarector: You guys think you can beat me? You think that when I am about to lay the demon Profusion out on the clouded ground you can jump in? Well I'm tired of this. Even though this fight has just begun, beleive me that it will be over soon. Because I cannot play around like this. *Demon Profusion jumps in.* Demon Profusion: You think that I wouldn't have saw you in the first place? You are a very funny character Rzarector. See I saw Violator coming, or else I would of just grabbed your leg and through you to hell where you belong. But I held back. Yes thats right, we would rather beat your soul til it's lifeless, then throw you into hell. There you would be eaten by the demons. Demon Atticus Pulsar: Okay then, lets send him to hell. *They all nod there heads as they begin walking over to Rzarector. Rzarector hisses out loud as God's word then comes into his mind.* (God: Look deep into your soul. Then maybe you can find a way to beat these demons. Because there is going to be alot of stronger demons besides these. Look Rza, look.) *Rzarector looks deep into his soul where he then gets an idea. He starts hissing at all of the demons and eventually they all start hissing back. There red eyes glow out as there fangs flash very vividly. They all jump at Rzarector wiht there claws in both hands. Rzarector ducks as they each go jabbing into each other. Rzarector then stands back up where the demons are laying on the ground bleeding green blood. Rzarector hisses at them as God messages him.* (God: Good job Rzarector. You defeated those 5 demons with ease. Maybe that will be all of them. Right now Heaven is in a great shape because of you. Your name is now in the book of life. Now I will send you to your body so I can get you back to Earth. Yet remember they may be back. Because like I said before, 'Where there is one demon there is always another.' Now go back to Earth and be happy, and I'll hollar at you later. Thankyou son.) *Rzarector interrupts.* Rzarector: Hold on one second please, there is something happening. I don't know what it is, there bodies are.....what? *Rzarector looks down at the bodies of the demons as they start glowing bright blue. They all form into one bright blue ball as the ball goes up above Rzarectors head. Rzarector looks above as he wonders what is going on. Then the ball starts rotating above his head. Rza hisses as he reaches for it. Yet, right as his hand gets to the ball it breaks off into four smaller blue balls. Rza then trys to grab one but as soon as his lifeless finger touches it, it catches on fire. Rza touches all the balls. They all turn to fire. The Black Priest then sits there and watches as he hears something. Screams are coming from the ball. All of a sudden the balls burst into huge flames and go back down through the hole, through purgatory, and back into hell. Rzarector hisses as he hears something else.* ~~~rZarEcToRyOuThInKiTiSoVeRbUtItIsNot~~~ Rzarector: What is it you guys are saying? Why are you talkign to me? Who is talking to me? Answer now! *Rzarector gives out a loud hiss as God comes back in touch with him.* (God: I heard it to Rzarector. That only means that there are more demons coming as we speak. I would send you back to Earth, but I can't now. Now please listen to me, and just stay here.) Rzarector: Anything for you God. Just protect me the best you can. I'll protect you the best I can, or else, I will die trying. Now how many demons are they? Can you see them anywhere? (Yes there are four more demons. That is all I see, yet I know that voice you are hearing is there commandor. Now you must wait on them to show up, then if you defeat those four. I guarentee you will have to fight the powerful commandor. He will hold the secrets to everything. Beleive me when I say that. Because he will be so strong that he is almost unbearable. Now did you get hurt from the previous fight?) Rzarector: Just a little cuts and scratches. Nothing major God. Just that those demons would have been pretty tough if I didn't think. These demons will be even tougher. How am I going to withstand them? I don't think that there is a way that I can. So what is your idea for my fight against them? Heaven relies on me God, and you control Heaven. So I need you to give me your opinion. *There is no answer as Rzarector stands there awaiting his word. Five minutes pass, still no word. Rzarector starts looking nervous as the whispers keep getting closer.* ~~~WeArEaLmOsTtHeRe~~~ Rzarector: And as soon as you get here. I will defeat you and send you both back to hell. You better beleive it to, I am Rzarector. Son of God, there is no way you can beat the holiest, darkest, and strongest angel alive. Because as soon as I die, I'm not going out in a closed casket. It will still be open, and eventually close to all of the demons inside of it. ~~~tHaTiSwHaTyOuThInK~~~ *Rzarector hisses in anger.* (God: You know Rzarector, now is not the time to get cocky.) Rzarector: Yet they are teasing me. I can't take it. I wish they would just show up so I can destroy them. (God: Yet you might destroy them, but telling them you will kill them all will only attract more of them to heaven. Remember they are like a virus. You can't attract one, without the rest.) Rzarector: Well I guess I will take them all out then. How does that sound? (God: Not good, for now they are alright. You will just have to wait til the rest get here. Understand?) Rzarector: Yes and what do you want me to do? Sit there and play cards with them? I mean come on God, you know as well as I do there here to kill everyone in my home. Now if you would just take the time and sit back for a second, then you would realize the fact that they are here for no good. Now Go.... *God interrupts.* (God: LISTEN TO ME! I understand they are here for no good. Now if you would listen to me for one second then you might realize that I know this. See what I was saying since you were arrogantly talking without listening to me is that.......they are a VIRUS! They are here to be no good. I know. Yet since they are all connected, you talk to one like you were talking. That is like talking to everyone in hell. Now keep it up and you will die up here. Yes you will go to heaven, but basically it will be hell. Because if you fail, then Heaven will be taken over. And you will be dead on Earth.) Rzarector: Yes God. Sorry for losing my temper. Sometimes I just get so irritated though. Do you know what I mean? Sometimes I wish I could just grab them and rip there heads off. But then again I don't ever want to hurt any normal human beings. Just the devils like I am seeing. I don't mind them dieing. (God: Yet I gave you the power to decide. And if you would use your brain, then you could decide very good. Now make me happy, I sense they are almost here.) *The whispers come back to Rzarectors head. As soon as he hears the first word he whispers and focuses on the hole in heaven leading to purgatory. There he waits for the arrival of the five demons.* ~~~~~WeArEhEaR~~~~~ (God: Good luck my son.) *Rzarector stood awaiting for the demons coming through the black hole in the clouds of Heaven. He heard whisering and hissing coming from the hole as mysteriously one black hooded head popped up from the hole. That creature jumped out onto the clouds, then a second head popped out. Then a third, fourth, and a fifth. All of a sudden Rzarector was surrounded by five creatures in black hooded robes. Just like the rest these people had red eyes, white fangs, and each had a resemblance of hell on there body. The first stood looking at Rzarector.* Rzarector: Now since you all finally made it from the deep pits of hell, are you going to say anything? Because I am anxious to see who you are, and to see if you are any smarter and stronger than the original demons that were just up here. Because that fight was nothing. This fight I am hoping is somewhat of a challenge. *The first demon walked up and stood in front of Rzarector. He hissed as his black tongue came out in the face of the Black Priest.* Rzarector: Yes? First Demon: I am here to prove to you and Satan of our powers. Now we will act upon Heaven and you to destroy all of it. Now may I introduce myself? *The demon slowely stood back as he ducked his whole body down in a doggy-style position. His two black burnt hands quickly covered the front of the hood and flicked it back. His head was shaven red and there was a burning cross in the top of his skull. The demon slowly raised up to reveal....Jeff Harris. He hissed at Rzarector as you saw the wide red eyes, white fangs, and the black tongue. The tongue was in the shape of a snakes tongue: long, wet, and very black.* Demon disguised as Jeff: Would you expect anything less? I am the next opponent, and yet a very strong demon. Now Rzarector, if you think you can get past me, then why don't I show the rest of us to you? Rzarector: The quicker the better. Demon Jeff: Very well. *The second demon, wearing a long black hooded robe with Lucifer on the front, steps up hissing at Rzarector. His red eyes shine out of the robe like sun. His white fangs are very long, and instead of two long fangs, he has 4 very slender and sharp fangs. He steps up as he grabs his hood and pushes it down. Rzarector stands shocked as the person behind the robe is The Legendary Lee Smith. Rzarector hisses back at Lee Smith as the Demon disguised as Lee Smith starts to talk.* Demon disguised as Lee Smith: You know Rzarector. You have no chance of beating us. You have heard this several times. Yet more and more times you keep saying you can. So just more and more of us keep coming. You may be able to hold us off for a while, but not with these strong demons. And yet there is only demons 10 times stronger than us. And everyday more people die on Earth. The more they die, the more that come to hell. Rzarector: I know I used to send people like you to hell. I aint got no love for demons. That is my purpose on Earth. That is why I am there. Did you know I used to be in hell? That was when God came and got me and told me to go to Earth to spread the message of God. So I did. Now he wants me to seek the skulls of devils, yet I don't have one from this fight. So are you going to be the first one to get his skull decapitated? Demon Lee Smith: *laughing* You know Rzarector. That is only the beginning of your problems. Still running your mouth, aren't you scared that we will just keep coming. Do you have any fears? Rzarector: If you play the game of death, then you play the game to die. Demon Jeff: Very well then. I guess you will die, and be back in hell. Oh wait, thats not right. We heard God telling you that you were now in the book of life. Does it feel good to ride his nuts? I mean tell us the truth. You are the only one that talks to him out of everyone on Earth. Well you are the only person that talks to him and talks back. Rzarector: I do not ride God's nuts, I'm here simply to help him out. I've admitted to my sins and he gave me a second chance, maybe if you admit to your sins he will give you a second chance. You can help me defeating people like you. Demon Lee Smith: Are you crazy? We despise people like you. You must have not been in hell that long. If you were, then you would have known something. Rzarector: And what is that? Demon Lee Smith: That is that the more powerful the demons, like us. The more that we hate God. We hate there followers, and we hate there servants, and we hate his Hitmen. Now listen to us, since we are stronger than the original five. That means that when we were on Earth, we had no love for God. Since we are in hell, we still don't. We don't mind burning, you get used to it eventually. The screams of others only brings delight to us. Now if you will please allow the third to introduce himself. *The third demon in the black hooded robe steps up. His eyes are more reddish with a slight hint of green in them. Rzarector looks confused as he knows the green means that there is still some good left in this demon. The demon walks up and Rza immediately notices no fangs. The demon hisses in a low growling snarl as he flips off his hood. This time revealing Gabriel Hunter.* Demon disguised as Gabriel Hunter: Rza Rza Rza. How could I know this time had been coming? Do you not remember me? I remember when you were in hell. You were just like me, a little bit of green in there eyes. That means that you had some good left. Well I used to be your murdering partner. Remember we used to kill the innocent just for the hell of it? Now you fight for the innocent. Isn't that kind of weird? Think of all the people in heaven that we killed that you are defending? Why? Because of your God, that is why. Rzarector: Well why does your eyes have green in them? Demon Gabriel: Well if you have noticed, I still have some good in me. That good is when I see you. Maybe I can bring you back to hell. I know it doesn't sound very good, but with your help we can bring Hell to the top of the list when we take over Heaven and Earth. What do you say???? *pause* brother???? Rzarector: I say that you can die just like the rest. That is the reason I left hell, to get away from people like you. Didn't you ever think that there is more to life than burning? Look at me, I got away from my bad influences and now I am going to Heaven. I am making an impact on Earth doing whatever I didn't do while I was a devil. I should have killed my own self when I was still alive. Demon Gabriel? Then why didn't you? *The fourth demon interrupts as he walks up right into the face of Rzarector. The other three demons are still standing in front, but behind the fourth demon. The fifth demon is next to the hole in Heaven. The fourth walks up as there are scars all over his body. He is burnt very bad as his eyes are very red. He is wearing a black robe with big fangs. Rzarector looks on as the demon pulls back his robe. His eyes are now rolled back in his head revealing......Spider.* Demon disguised as Spider: Welcome Rzarector, welcome to my hell. I am the demon of torture, and I am here to turn your body into a different shape. I will cut you up, and chop you up, and then I will serve you in hell over the eternal lake of fire. How does that sound? *Rzarector hisses.* Rzarector: I have been in hell once, and I refuse to go back. Now if you would like to step and make a challenge like that and really think you can get past me. Just remember I got seven more angels behind me backing me up. Now Gabriel back to you. Demon Gabriel: Yes? Rzarector: Why don't you have any fangs? Demon Gabriel: I cut them out. Why you are going to ask? Well the simple reason, I am the demon of disguise. I like to disguise myself. That way if God ever see's me wondering in Earth or up in Heaven, well he won't think anything. I just cover my eyes, throw on a white robe, and since I have no fangs, he will think I am an angel. Isn't that funny? Demon Lee Smith: No, what is funny is when we reveal this fifth demon. Then we will all slaughter Rzarector and see his blood red soul laying on the clouds and evaporate. To where? To the inside gates of heaven, but that doesn't matter. Because we will soon take that over and drive you all back to hell. Isn't that funny? Isn't that stuff very funny? I think it is. Now maybe we should go ahead and reveal the last demon. *All four of the demons hiss at the same time and say, 'NAH ITS NOT SATAN! BUT ITS JUST AS EVIL!' The fifth demon walks up and stands behind all the four. You see fire glowing in his red eyes as he has long black fingernails. His tongue is splitted into four different parts, and they are all black. His fangs are very long and they curl back up into his lip. He walks forward as his black robe, with a red hood is being took down. Rzarector stares on at his black fingernails as he removes his hood. Rzarector is then looking in the face of Skipster. Skipster smirks.* Demon disguised as Skipster: Well Rzarector, now since all five of us are finally shown and you know we are demons. We will give you one more chance to come back and help us fight before we destroy you. Rzarector: No, I stand by God, God alone. Demon disguised as Skipster: Well if that is how it will be, then thou punishment shall be received. Just remember there is a God in Hell, his name is Satan. Rzarector: And some God that is. A god who lets his people eternally burn. How about that? Demon Skipster: It doesn't matter what you say, I am the strongest of these demons. Because like you are God's son, I am Satan's son. Yet I am not the strongest. Our commandor is still on the way. He had some business to take care of in Hell. Yet by the time he makes it, it will just be to finish tearing down the gate. Because we will already be done with you. *The demons circle Rzarector as he hisses. They all hiss back as there eyes glow up. Lee Smith and Jeff Harris step up first, they both flash there fangs as they charge Rzarector. He ducks from both of them and turns around and dropkicks them in the head. Skipster hisses loudly as Jeff and Lee jump back up. Rzarector is now being circled by five demons.* WHAT WILL BE HIS FATE???? ****To be continued.*****
