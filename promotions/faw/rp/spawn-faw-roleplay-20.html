Spawn — FAW Roleplay #20

SpAwN--The Hell Master \~~~~~~~(opening scene)The scene FAW house show in Boston. It is the last half hour of the show, and the place which is packed is very quiet, in anticipation of what is about to come. The Devil's Pit, once every week the Devil's Pit visits a FAW house show, it is a scary segment, but the fans love it and find it very entertaining. And now that Mike has taken over as the new producer and director to the show, it has gotten higher ratings, fan tune more into it than before, the reason for this is because Mike has made more into an adventure type of show with real people in it, almost making it look to realistic. The scene goes back to the FAW tron, as the lights go off everywhere, then the mist appears on the screen, the cave is being shown with the three dogs of hell and all of the coffins stacked against each other on the wall. As always the camera follows inside the cave, giving us a tour and showing the fans the horror that will fill their hearts. The camera now goes thru the black door and straight to were Spawn is shown sitting on his chair, sitting there as the sole ruler of hell. He then stands up , walks towards the camera and begins to speak. Spawn: Welcome to the Devil's Pit, this night we have a real juicy story for you. It deals with what happens when a person try's to pass himself as the ruler of a place he does not know anything about. This story will show you what happens to those that think everything in life is easy. I will not give the answer to this story away. Now all of you fans and Crimson Lord watch closely as some one gets ready to pay the Devil his price a new soul. Spawn fades from the screen as the story begin. The scene shows a man in a bar drinking with his buddies and they look like they are having a good time, as they are talking loudly amongs each other. simon: Hey guys this is the good shit man I'm glad were are hanging out this night, cause we are going to have ourselves some fun. buddy: Yeah after this we could go to a club or something, what do you all think? Stanley: I don't know about that, I need to get my ass home my wife could be worried you know. simon: Man you are always with some bullshit, what is your problem dogg. Always talking about wife this wife that give me a fucking break. buddy: He is right Stanley you need to put in check, you are the man of the house not her. Stanley: You guys do not understand, she is a good lady and respect me, that's why I treat her with the utmost respect and love her to death. simon: What yoou are is a bitch a faggit more like it, what you need to do is smack her around and show her who is the boss of the house. And cut this sissy routine off. Take me for example I'm the master of my domain, it is hell in my crib, but you know how come, I made it that way I got my fuckin bitch ass wife in check. I take her fuckin money, I make clean the house all day, she can't speak till I tell her to, she can't go outside without permission, and I also get my shit at night when ever I want it. That my friend, is what you would call the ruler, the master of his castle. And that's what your stupid ass should be doing. Stanley: That my friend is what you call an abuser not a master. simon: What! I'll kick your ass for saying stupid shit like that. Stanley: Come on I'm not afraid of a wife beater come and fight a real man you bitch! buddy: Stop it guys let's be civilized here and stop acting like kids. In that moment a man walks in to the bar and goes straigh to were the guys are sitting and ask them a question. man: Hi guys I was wondering if you can help me out here? Stanley: Sure we can, what is it you need. man: I'm kind of lost, well I have been lost all day and I really need to get home. I would be very grateful if you guys could show me how to get there. simon: How are we going to do that? You got an adress or something? Let me see it?(man gives Simon the address paper) are you out of your mind ! that's the other town over man it will take you an hour to get there by bus. man: I am willing to pay a good amount of money if someone would give me a ride there. simon: Oh yeah. Stanley: Don't worry about it we will do it for free. simon: We will do it for free are you cra……. You know what I apologizes were are my manners when a person needs help. Sure we will help you. Hey Stanley here are the keys why don't you all wait for us in the car. Me and Buddy are going to finish our drinks real quick and then we are out of here alright. (Stanley and the man leave the bar and go to the car to wait as Simon, and Buddy stay talking to each other) buddy: What's up Simon why did we stay back here. simon: Simple we are going to give this fool a ride but not to his house. buddy: Were are we taking him then? simon: Were no one in this world will ever find him, we are going to take all of his money, kill his ass and dump the body. buddy: Yo man that is deep, I'm game but are you sure about this? simon: Never been to sure in my life. Are you ready? buddy: Yeah let's go. The guys walk out of the bar and they all get into the car and drive off. In the car Simon does a wrong turn to a dark alley. He gets off the car but before he gets off the car he opens the glove box of the car and takes out a knife and puts it in his pocket without letting the other guys see him. Once off the car he ask Buddy to help him. buddy: Yo anything wrong? simon: Yeah man the engine is thirsty and we are going to need some water for it (Stanley and the man also get out of the car) Hey Stanley be a sport and see if you can go to the store and get us some water. ( Stanley nods his head in agreement and leaves to get the water) hey man (pointing to the man) could you come here for a second and help me out with this. As soon as the man comes close to Simon, Buddy grabs him from the back, and Simon starts to punch him in the stomach, as the man start to scream. man: What are you doing huh! Help me some one!! Huh, huh simon: You know you are screaming to much, maybe is time I shut you up for good. Buddy holds the man tight and covers his mouth as Simon takes the knife out of his pocket and begins to stab him. He stabs him a lot of time. Then Buddy lets the man go as he slumps down to the floor in a pool of blood dying slowly. Simon and Buddy takes the man's money they begin to laugh get into the car and sped off. As they were driving fast they see Stanley and run him over. They stop the car and look at him to see if he is dead they then drive away. The scene shows Stanley getting up slowly and heading towards the man who he picks up from the ground and finds that he is dead. The scene switches to Buddy and Simon talking in the car. simon: How much money did we get? buddy: That stupid son of a bitch was carrying 800 dollars you believe that? simon: Yeah it's a good night for us bad for them. buddy: You think we should have done that to Stanley he was our friend after all? simon: Fuck Stanley he got in our way the bitch got his too. May their stupid ass rest in peace. Ha, ha, ha. The scene then switches over to the ambulance picking Stanley and the man up, they get taken to the hospital and Stanley is questioned by the police on what happened. Once they finish Stanley goes home tells his wife what happened, they hug and kiss and they go to sleep. But Stanley waits till his wife goes to sleep gets up from the bed… goes to the dresser and gets his gun, he then tipi toes out of the room and leaves the house. He gets into his car and drives off with out his wife suspecting anything. The scene then shows Stanley driving around looking for Buddy and Simon. He drives for a long time till he spots their car, Stanley parks his car besides theirs and proceeds to look for them . He spots them in side a fast food joint and decides to go inside after them. When he gets inside he pulls the gun from his pocket goes to their table sits next to them and starts to talk to them. Putting the gun on the table. buddy: Hey Stanley what happened to you ? you just left us back there, and what's with the gun? simon: Buddy shut up we know why Stanley is here. Are you going to kill us Stanley? You do not have the balls, to do it, you never were strong. Now me that's another story I will sell out my mother for a penny if I have to. buddy: Shut your trap Simon! Stanley listen to me it wasn't my idea Simon made me do it believe me. simon: Don't beg this bitch he isn't going to do it you can't shoot no one stanley you are a punk you woman is the boss. Maybe after I take that gun and shove it up your ass I'll go to your house and fuck the living daylight out of her show her what a real man is all about. Stanley gets up from the table walks towards the counter and tell everyone in the place to get out except for his two friends if they leave he starts to shoot everyone. Everybody leaves except for Buddy and Simon, Stanley then goes to the door and locks it. He then goes to the table and begins to speak. Stanley: Now it's my turn to speak and you guys are going to shup up the entire time and listen. We have been friend for 10 to 12 years, and all you have done is put me down make fun of me, talk about my wife and family and treating me like if I was a nobody. Well you know what no more! This is ends now. You guys are evil and in this world there is no room for guys like you. Before I do what I have to do I have a confession to make that guy you guys shot was not for real I set it up because I was fed up with you guys pushing around, so I made a deal with this friend and you guys were the bait. simon: Fuck you! you are lying! You aren't smart enough to pull a stunt like that. Stanley: That's what you think, they always say the good ones are really the bad ones in life. simon: Man get the fuck out of here! your stupid and I'm going to kill you! Stanley: Like you try to do earlier and fail I doubt that very much (Stanley points the gun to Buddy and shoots twice killing him instantly) simon: Son of a bitch!! Your mine I going to kill you ( gets up from the table and leaps at Stanley who in returns shoots three time dropping fast) Stanley: Poor guys and the sad part is. The cops aren't coming because this whole place was just a set up. Like I said before you just never know who the bad guy really is. The scene ends as the FAW tron is shown again and the fans are still looking at it. Then Spawn shows himself and begins to speak. Spawn: Did you enjoy the show, did it scared you off your seats, if it did then it was a good show. Now Crimson Lord heed my words and see the example that was laid out to you this night. A piece of advice he who goes around saying that he is the real lord of darkness should think before he does such a thing. Because you never know who lurks in the darkness and is awaiting to come out and show his true form and kick you up the ass. So beware and prepare your self for total war. So let it be written…..So let it be done. Hell has spoken. The FAW tron goes black as the scene fades to black.
