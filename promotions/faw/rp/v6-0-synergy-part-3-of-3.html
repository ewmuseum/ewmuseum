V6.0 (Synergy) (part 3 of 3) Violator Furiously Addictive Wrestling Roleplay #132 Date: 1/1/03 Location: Sears Tower CONTINUED: (Greatest is not defined by how many fans you accumulate, or even the extent of ass kissing that you engage in during your prime lifetime. What defines greatest is that single moment, when all else seems to be lost, when the darkness has completely set in and the end seems near, you arise from the grave. You step from the darkness into the light and become immortal, even for that single instance, you crush your opponent underfoot. This is what defines a superstar, and many in the business have lost track of these guidelines.) (Xing believes that he is on of the great warriors of this sport, that because of his so-called “position” that he can claim to be one of the elite, pity he cannot further understand what is lacking in his situation. Blinded by deceit and greed he cannot see his ending looming in the shadows, only mere moments for this very point. Life is an ever changing game, and without the power to adapt no one can survive, Xing has not been able to adapt, and so…his end comes near.) (Violator’s journey has come to a halt now, and fate has brought him to this place, the pinnacle of what the windy city signifies The Sears Tower. Its sleek black body lumber above the masses, almost touching the grounds of heaven, and is needed can provide piece, a place away from reality, even if for a single undefined period of time. Violator sits here, a small makeshift couch set prone in a position overlooking the industrial section of the city. His dark figure overlooks these proceedings, the cars below changing almost, in their diminutive state, into meaningless ants, unaware that a leader looks before them. With an expressionless face, on the wake of the new years, deep in contemplation over his apparent wars, January 5th its call date.) Violator: Fate has seemingly cast me into a position of prosperity. A foe stands before me with know prior knowledge of what madness he shall engage into four days removed from this. So much to learn, in so few a time span. Maybe I should send him a lesson via mass media at this moment, and give me some type of sporting chance, hopefully to change his ways, and understand that an enigma stands as his foe. (Violator henceforth pulls his cell phone from deep inside his trench coat, and with a single voice dial command the FAW media offices are contacted, with him sitting patiently awaiting response from the other end.) Receiver: Fully Addictive Wrestling media and television, I am Carol, how may I be of service. Violator: Hello Carol this is Violator, FAW alumni, I would like to request a camera crew. Chicago stationed, upon the ninety ninth floor of the Sears Tower building. Carol: No problem Mr. Violator, may I have your conformation number. Violator: Conformation number 87-862-24 Carol: One second please, (Indistinct typing sounds heard) Conformation number received, camera crew en route to location. Shall be within range in twenty minutes time. Is there any more business I can assist you with? Violator: No that shall suffice, thank you for your assistance. Carol: Okay then Mr. Violator, enjoy your promo, and good day. Violator: Good day. (Violator hits the end button and places the cell phone back into the inner pocket of his coat, and sits back slightly, relaxing in anticipation of his camera crew. Taking into account the time left before the arrival of his company he stands, and begins to pace the floor, taking in the scenery of Chicago at a distance, from every angle, atop the highest plane in this fair city. A sense of peace seems to overwhelm his body, being that the sky deck has been closed for the day, and he is alone, for the moment, to converse with his thoughts. Time has such only comes around once a blue moon, and his enjoyment signifies that he is taking full advantage of this situation, speaking to himself aloud in a continuous effort to complete the questions.) Violator: Strange that with each passing wrestling organization, those same occurrences appear and begin to haunt me. Someone always stands at the entrance, denying my acceptance into infinity, pacing me to respect their domain. They test me because in their ignorance they can still feel the power, and the electricity of what I am, of who I am. They understand that I am something more than just a normal wrestler, than just a normal foe to which a normal match shall commence. I more than that, I presence in their dreams, a nightmare that wakes their soul in the coldest of hours, they understand that I am Violator, not just a name, but an existence. Foamy doesn’t see it; he can’t see it sitting in his high chair, just because he believes he owns the world. He can’t see it but he knows that I am here, and that cold wind, which blows across his spine, is my presence, slowly pressing towards the World Heavyweight Championship. I told the world before that I would be champion, and there would be no one to stands in my way of this, and as of now that drive has not changed, only magnified and accelerated into a monster, a wrestling giant not to halted until that final moment, when all comes into the light. Xing wants a wrestling match, I shall give him something more, I shall give him a battle, a war for his life, leaving only that small section of breathing room for him to sit and watch my dominance. And when that dominance has concluded, when all is said and done, the last thing he shall witness shall be…judgment. (His words comes just as the elevator’s light illuminates the hallway, and the doors release to supply him with his aforementioned company, the figures of two cameramen, each which their own respectable equipment in hand. They stop Violator quickly, him returning to his former seat, and turning back towards the afternoon Chicago sky, truly not giving the cameramen that much attention. They stroll over posthaste, and with permission from Violator’s silence set the camera’s up in record time, a bit cautious with the presence of the enigma looming over them. Timidly the cameramen give Violator the signal that all is set and running, and after a minutely moment of silence Violator word flow like a raging river, deep and full of a dark content and knowledge.) Violator: Xing, my poor misguided soul, haven’t you come to understand this dire situation yet? You still seem to take this as some type of game, as if I am in the mood for your branded sense of humor. Do you see a smile spread across my face, is there any humor whatsoever in the sound of my voice? I thought now. I stand trying to teach you the errors of your ways and yet you continue to lead yourself down that path of destruction, blinded by the light of fame. Even from my words you can taste that hint of championship immortality, that burning determination to become the best, what is absent and void inside of you. You are nothing more than what you claim yourself to be, a bodyguard, and wet rag for the wastes of your president to cleanse themselves with, a court jester in a city of fools. Come from your world and enter this reality, witness that you are nothing more than mortal and your judgment is at hand. When you understand this maybe your chances will improve, but even with my rudimentary knowledge of what you are I see that your stubbornness shall not let this happen, so I say pray for him, please...pray for his salvation. I am not a religious figure of any sort, and this comes not to be a plead for your soul, seeing as that is the last thing that I would care to do in your case, I just feel that salvation in your only choice, because your ass will soon belong to the piper. Are you afraid of what the world may uncover when that bell rings. The Truth that shall comes to light, letting the people that you are of coward’s blood, and nothing you can say or do can change that, because I am the standard, I am the enigma, I am Violator. You leave me no choice to but to show you the true meaning of Judgment Day, and to give you hell beyond anything so have encountered before, heaven is only a three count away, and death an afterthought. I promise you that it shall be quick and straightforward, and in the end, truth shall be the victor. Welcome to the beginning Xing, welcome…TO JUDGMENT DAY! YOU HAVE BEEN WARNED! (Violator breathes in as he can feel the power swarming around his body, and even the cameramen are in awe of what they behold. All of the words have ceased, and now all that is left is actions, and destiny. Xing has no idea what is about to happen to, he has no idea that he has come in contact with the one, true champion, the iconic enigma, THE ONE…Violator. It has begun!) “Destiny has but one soul, and one champion, pray for the chosen, pray for me!
