Spawn — FAW Roleplay #23

captured 8 August 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
SpAwN--The Hell Master \~~~~~~~(opening scene)The scene Spawn's locker room we see Spawn watching the Great 1's latest promo, the scene shows Spawn laughing very hard at the comedy routine of the great 1, Spawn laughs so hard that he falls of his chair. Getting up from the floor he goes over to the TV and shuts it off. There is a knock on the door he opens it and it happens to be Mike…. Mike comes in and they begin to speak. Mike: Hey Spawn how are you? Spawn: Well I was kinda ticked off but now after watching the Great 1's latest promo, man I tell ya, that boy has made my day. Mike: What do you mean? Spawn: You haven't seen his latest promo? Mike: I'm afraid I haven't…what is so funny about it? Spawn: Well his promo was about the movie called the Titanic. Is a love story about two guys, the great ho and his agent who falls in love inside this big ship, but they come to find out that their love will not last for a long time because the ship hit an iceberg and is sinking fast, So the great ho decides to suck his agent's cock just to get a quick taste before they both go down. (Spawn Laughing) Mike: that's not the way it goes stop messing around, Spawn? Spawn: You are right the story is not about two guys, I just wanted to show you how stupid his promo is. This is wrestling not theater the Titanic does not belong in FAW, and nobody cares about a trip the great 1 took in a ship that sanked 40 to 50 years ago, we call it an impossible story or better yet a bullshit of a promo. The Great Ho can talk all the shit he wants because that's all it is shit, shit comes out of that boy's mouth non stop, what he needs is an enema for that problem. Great 1 better get his facts straight or I will still kick his ass and shove the Titanic down his throat. I don't see what the titanic has to do with wrestling Mike: Some one will like it. You know the Great 1 is very popular, I know his fans will like it. Spawn: Bullshit! His fans can all kiss my ass, Shit the Great1's fans would cheer loudly at anything that cocksucker would do, I can see him right now french kissing the referee and the fans loving every minute of it. The Great 1 is a fool and you and everybody in the world know it…his promo sucks just like him. That Titanic promo is a promo that don't make sense since the titanic went under 40 to 50 years ago. This fucking asshole has the Titanic dates all screwed up. Mike: Spawn it is just a promo…it does not have to be accurate. Spawn: What! Fuck you Mike it has to be accurate how in the fucking hell are his fans going to understand it? Mike: They will understand it, As you said earlier every one loves the Great 1. Spawn: Not every one, that is not true I never said that!…Unless you are one of those lucky fans that are in love with the Great Ho? Mike: Come on now Spawn you are acting like a kid with that stupid remark. Spawn: Now I am stupid is that what you are saying? Mike: No! you know what I mean? Spawn: I don’t know….sometime you like to take the wrong side of the road? Mike: No way Spawn you are my best friend and I ‘m on your side all of the way. Mike: So what now? Spawn: Now, now I am going to show the Great 1 how to do a real promo give that fool some brains something he lacks in. Mike what I want you to do is go and bring me a camera crew…I will be in the gym waiting for them ok. Mike; Ok I shall be back in a few minutes. As Spawn gets the gym ready for the promo, Mike comes in with the camera crew. Spawn then starts to put every one in their places as he begins the show. Mike: Ok, you got us all in our destined area so what now? Spawn: Now Mike….now I will show the Great 1 what a real promo should look like, I will show that bitch why I am still the number man in FAW….I will show that bitch why I am the TRUE RULER OF FAW. Mike: AAAh….Spawn? Spawn: Yeah what Mike! Mike: I don't mean to interrupt you, but could we start this thing? Spawn: Fuck you, but you're right. Ok here is the deal. I am doing some heavy training in this promo, and from time to time I will be talking about that clown Great 1 and about our match. What I want you guys to do is make sure you focus the camera on my face and on my sweaty muscles and zoom in a lot also. Are we clear on this (everyone nods yes) good then let us begin. Mike: Ok everyone to their places, we are going live in fives seconds. 5….4….3….2….1….action The scene opens to the same locker room where we were at right now. As we look around we see Spawn on the floor doing pushups. As he pushes himself up, he claps his hands together under his chest and then lands back on his hands and does the same thing over again. We see him do about five of these and then look up to the camera. He then stands up and looks into the camera. A second later he begins to speak. Spawn: We are in the stretch run now Great 1. The day will soon be upon us as Panic comes to us on Sunday. The Cage in a Rage awaits both of us in our future. Both of us will walk in, if you are lucky earlier in the night that is, but only one will walk out. That man is I. I have been through the most preparation and training while you are partying making movies and taking Titanic trips. You are in no condition to fight a man my caliber in a Cage in a Rage match, especially after you have yet to trained for this Match. Everyone knows this and everyone will be watching Sunday night. Thousands will watch as I destroy you in a savage match. It is time to show actions kid. The time for talking is just about over. When the bell rings I will be ready. I will be ready for a challenge but prepared for a massacre. Spawn pauses for a minute as he looks around the room. He looks back to the camera a moment later and then begins to speak again. Spawn: We hear it once again from Great 1 as he opens his mouth in some pathetic attempt to get the upper hand in the endless mind games. He claims that I can break his bones but not his spirit. Kind of like that crap that they always used to tell you about how sticks and stones can break my bones but words will never hurt me. You can come at me with everything you have. You can come after me with high spirits; it does not make a difference. At the end of the night it will be me that walks out on top. You have your dreams of pleasing the fans and earning the full share of the world title somewhere down the road, but unfortunately for you, that will never happen. You may please the fans, but it will only be in defeat as they cheer in the bumps that you take in the Rage in a Cage. I have not underestimated you like you have me. I have told you the truth about what is going to happen this Sunday. I have told you what you are up against and the odds against you. You are heading into an impossible situation this Sunday when you step into the cell with me. After the last time we fought and I destroyed you…. you will be lucky to walk into the Cage on your own two feet and after the cage you will be lucky if you have a career left. Spawn pauses once again as he looks into the camera. He waits a few seconds and then begins to speak to us once again. Spawn: In two days Great 1 will meet his fate. The Rage in a cage awaits the both of us and an epic battle is soon to commence. It will be a bloody battle for sure, as you have said. It will not be pretty and in the end one man will walk out of the cell with the right to call himself the better man. That man is going to be me. I have told you the reasons for this and on Sunday it will come to pass. Sunday is when all of this comes together as we clash inside the steel. The end will see me put you away and walk from the cell victorious. You, on the other hand, will be carted away in shame and disgrace for failing to live up to your own pathetic hype that you have created this week. You have said a lot of things that are going to be hard to live up to. You have mouthed off things that will be impossible to do. The main one being defeating a legend like me. Soon you cannot hide behind your hype machine anymore. Soon you will have to live up to the standard you have prepped everyone for and when that time comes you will fail and fail miserably. Sunday marks that time. Spawn walks a few feet away from the camera and over to a mini fridge. He opens it up and pulls out a bottle of Gatorade and then shuts the door. Then he walks back over to the camera and begins to speak again. Spawn: It does not matter whom you talk to on the road to this match Great 1. I do not care if you talk to Fro, Tony V, Xander, or whoever. Yes, Tony V, was one of the lucky ones. He defeated me at a PPV. He got lucky. If him and me ever met one on one, we all know who would come out on top. You might think it wise to get tips from the last man to beat me, but you are only hurting yourself. What worked for him will not work for you. He beat me in a handi match he had his goons do the work for him, while you are stuck alone with me. He was never inside a Cell with me like you will be this Sunday. Go ahead and take advice from him and dig your grave deeper kid. That is exactly what you will be doing because what worked for him will never work for you. I can focus everything on you and not have to worry about anyone else trying to win the match. I also have you locked in a cage Great 1. There will be nowhere to run to when the bell sounds on Sunday, spelling your impending doom. Spawn unscrews the lid to the bottle of Gatorade and takes a drink of it. A moment later he looks back to the camera and begins to speak once again. Spawn: The time is almost upon us Great 1, and there is nothing you can do to slow it down now. Soon your name will fit, as the crimson will flow freely from your head. At Panic it will be you that will wear the crimson mask. All from your obsession over making a big deal about something that was not. You pushed the issue and now you will pay for it as I end the issue. You are but another rung on the ladder that I must climb to prove to the world that there can only be one world champion here in FAW, a belt that will be around my waist and only around my waist sooner than later. You are nothing more than cannon fodder thrown at me to keep me busy while others gear up to fight over my title. Your dreams of beating me and earning my respect will never come to fruition. Your hopes of walking out of the cage victorious will never materialize, as you must face the greatest technical wrestler in the world, who just happens to be a pretty good brawler too. Whatever the fighting style kid, you can never hope to defeat me. Sure you may have illusions of grandeur but the reality is that you can never achieve that, at least not against me. Your time is just about up and there is nothing you can do about it now. Spawn takes another drink of the Gatorade in his hand and then puts the lid back on it. He looks back up to the camera and begins to speak for one final time. Spawn: Talking time is now over. It is time to put up or shut up Great 1. In two days we will walk into the cage to be forever changed. You will fall to obscurity and I will rise to immortality. The fans will get a good show as they watch me physically dissect you as only I can do. You will soon meet me in the ring, inside the cell, and I promise that it will be a day that you will never forget. The match will live on in history, and your career will be all but history at the end of the match. Rest up and prepare for your destruction, because it will be the biggest spotlight you will ever be in. The biggest match of your career awaits you on Sunday in what will go down as your toughest challenge. Your darkest hour is upon you as Panic draws nearer. Soon the bell will toll and your nightmare will begin. Except, this will be all too real and there will be no escape. The day comes upon us Great 1. I will see you inside the cell. Spawn turns away from the camera and walks over to the fridge where he got the Gatorade. He sits a half empty bottle on the top of it and then walks over to where he was doing pushups. The camera begins to fade away as he lies down on the floor and starts to do some crunches. As he does about five of them Mike: Very good promo Spawn. Spawn: What do you mean very good promo? Mike: Just like I said very good promo. Spawn: Mike I’m afraid to ask you this but did you just turned of the camera on me? Mike: Yeah why isn’t over? Spawn: (grinning) Mike you stupid son of a bitch! How the hell can you cut me off the fucking promo wasn’t over yet! You idiot! Mike: Hey calm down man…we can fix it ok. Spawn: You better because your life depends on it. Mike: Yeah we can fix it is simple all we have to do is start off were we left of at and take it from there now…..gets cut off from two wrestlers that have just walked in to the locker room, They then begin to talk to Spawn. Wrestler1: You know what Spawn we are getting sick and tired of the way you have been bashing the workers, and the Great 1’s promo it is getting sickening and we are demanding you put a stop to it as of this minute? Spawn: (laughing) Oh yeah is that right, and if I don’t stop what are you all going to do about it? Wrestler2: We are going to make sure it stops right here right now. Spawn: Are you two bozos are saying you’re going to kick my ass to stop me? Wrestler2: That’s correct. Spawn: You two guys are nothing but clowns. And I do not waste my time with clowns, so get the fuck out of here, and I will forget this ever happened. Wrestler1: No way fuck this shit you have insulted the Great 1 and for that you must pay big time. Spawn; Oooh that’s all I have to do, why didn’t you say so in the first place, here allow me. Spawn goes to his locker gets his pants goes to the back pocket gets his wallet out takes out a twenty crunches it walks back to the two guys and throws it hitting one of the wrestlers on the face. Both wrestlers look down at the twenty dollar bill, they then look at Spawn who begins to speak to them. Spawn: Allow me to tell you both something, do you really know what you are getting yourself into? I mean, I have slaughtered the competition when ever I feel the need for it because I do what I want when I want and no one can stop me, I have held The FAW world title for nearly four months. I am the longest reigning champion in FAW history. Do you know what that means? That means I beat people like you two idiots week in and week out for months. I have seen your kind come here before, and the result has always been the same. Their dreams have always been crushed and their hopes have always been dashed. You will be no different. The reason is that you are messing with the best wrestler you have ever faced. I am the best technical wrestler in the world. That is a deserved moniker too. I did not come up with it, it was given to me through my number of great matches. And you two have the ordasity to fuck with me, I guess you are both tired of living oh well like the saying goes que sera, sera, whatever will be will be. But you know what the sad part to all of this is? Your putting your life on the line for a has been like the Great 1. Like Mr.T always say “ I pity the fool” Wrestler1: Shut the fuck up already and take this up mouth asshole. Wrestler 1 swings a punch at Spawn but Spawn moves making the wrestler miss but his partner sees this and grabs Spawn from behind, as the first wrestler begins to hit Spawn on the stomach, Spawn gets hit with five or six punches, as blood spurts out from his mouth, The wrestler that was holding him lets him go as Spawn slumps down to the floor in pain and hurt. The two wrestlers begin to mock him. Wrestler1: Ha, ha, ha, what happen the mighty Spawn is hurt. Wrestler2: Yeah I thought he couldn’t be beat, all that time making a speech for nothing. Wrestler1: We kicked his ass in his own locker, and it has all been film, we are moving up in the world because of this. Wrestler2: Any last dying words Mr. Spawn? Spawn: Yeah you two guys are idiots, and now it is my turn to kick ass. Spawn gets up from the floor, wipes his blood from his mouth grabs the first wrestler and kicks him in the groin, he sees the second wrestler coming at him from behind but quickly, Spawn gives him a back kick to the gut then grabs him and hits a side slam! As the wrestler is lying still on the floor Spawn gets on the bench and comes down at him with a leg drop, Spawn goes to the other wrestler who is still holding his crotch from the pain, Spawn grabs the O2 tank from the wall and begins to pound the wrestler furiously, you can see the blood just splurt from the wrestler’s head as he goes down to the floor hard. From there Spawn goes to the other wrestler kicks him in the gut as he is getting up slowly and place s him in his finisher the Last kill delivering it with full force. Ending the fight, Spawn walks up to the two wrestlers on the floor and begins to speak to them. Spawn: LET THE Hell Master give you both fools a piece of advice, Anarchy is described as the complete absence of government, or the political disorder and violence or lawlessness. And while the politics go on and the government of this promotion changes what seems like every week, one thing remains constant. Among the chaos and disorder there is a constant variable, one thing that will never change with the time, a thing that will exist forever. That is the fact that I will continue to steamroll through the competition and take out every poor fool that dares to step in my way. We have seen it before in the past and we will see it again at Panic. So bear that in mind whenever you step into my turf you do the next best thing and bow before your master. Spawn turns to Mike looks at the cameras and ask Mike a question. Spawn: Mike now that this is over and done with let us finish this fucking promo shall we. Mike: Nice fight Spawn you sure kicked their ass (with a smile) Spawn: Mike what are you smiling about? Mike: Oh nothing , nothing at all. Spawn: Don’t bullshit me Mike (looking at the cameras) you fucking filmed the fight didn’t you? Mike: Now would I do something like that? Spawn: Yes you would. Are you out of your mind? Mike: Just think for a moment what a nasty promo this would make, their is no way the Great 1 can compete with this promo no way in hell man? Spawn: I don’t know Mike this was a cheap shot on your part. Mike: Just think of how the Great 1 is going to feel when he sees this promo. Spawn: All alright man but let us finish this thing off, I want to get home and watch some TV. Now we are going to start with me just finish these two puke heads and going back into training you got me Mike: Ok man got you, people to your places! we are going live in fives seconds. 5….4….3….2….1….action The scene continues inside of Spawn’s locker room were he has just finish knocking out two wrestlers, even though he is winded he begins to train again for his upcoming match against the Great 1. We are watching a barbell move up and down in front of our screen. This lasts for several seconds and then we fade out to reveal Spawn lying on a bench lifting the weight. He does it several more times as we look on and then with a grunt he sits it back on its bar and sits up on the bench. He grabs a towel and wipes some of the sweat from his body and then tosses it aside. A second later he looks forward to the camera and at the two guys on the floor and then begins to speak to us. Spawn: Preparation is key for any marquee bout such as the one Great 1 and I are about to engage in. It can win or lose the match for someone. I pride myself on my training regimen for matches. I am sure Great 1 prepares for this hard too, seeing as it is the biggest match of his pathetic career. There is a difference for him though. No matter how much he prepares he cannot change the outcome of this match. No matter how hard he has trained he cannot come close to defeating me. The reason being that as important as training and preparation are; there is one thing that is much more important than them both, something that he lacks. That is skill and talent. No matter how much someone trains for a match, the more skilled wrestler will always win. That is the case here. I am the most talented and skilled wrestler between the two of us and that is only one of the numerous reasons why I will walk out with the victory this Sunday. I will be the fresh man, as he will be coming off of a hellacious acting job and will be far from one hundred percent. The odds are stacked against him and he will not overcome them. He will walk blindly into his own devastation in the most brutal war of them all, the Rage in a cage. Spawn reaches off to his side and pulls back a half drank bottle of Gatorade. He unscrews the cap and takes a swig of it he takes another swig and does not swallow he then spits it atone of the wrestlers on the floor he then looks back to the camera. A second later he begins to speak again. Spawn: That is also something that the Great1 seems to take for granted. He is walking into the hallowed grounds of the Rage Cell. A match that has changed lives forever. It has forged careers and broken them. He walks into it any other gimmick match. This is not any other match. It is a very dangerous match and that is just another obstacle thrown in his path. It is just one more thing for him to overcome on his impossible journey past me. It is a journey that will hit a roadblock this Sunday as he squares off inside that cell against the greatest of all time. I have said this week how his career will come to a miserable weak end in this match. I have showed him his fate and it will come to pass at Panic. Idle threats, meaningless insults, and tired clichés will not stop or even slow down the inevitable. He must face this and meet his destiny. He had to push the issue. He had to make it an issue in the first place. He chose his path of destruction. He chose his grave and now he will lie in it. He chose to mess with me and now he will have to deal with the everlasting consequences of that poor decision, a decision that will have cost him his very livelihood. Spawn takes another drink of the bottle of Gatorade, downing the rest of it in a few gulps and then sits the empty bottle down on the floor beside him. He looks back to the camera and begins to speak. Spawn: Regardless of the enormous odds stacked against him, it seems The Great 1 always comes out here with his head held high rambling about the same tired crap. He always has something positive to say about his impending destruction in the cage. If he is able to put a positive spin on it, then good for him. Soon things will not look so rosy though. Soon things will look very bleak and anything but positive for Great 1. He comes out here and says things like how injuries do not affect him the way they do other people. He claims that the show must go on and he will crawl to the cell if he has to. This is the ignorance of The Great 1. If he has a broken neck he will be out for a long time, he will not be able to compete in a Rage Cell match. If both of his legs are broke, then he is out and there will be no match. He does not see the seriousness of the position he is in. He does not realize that he is one wrong move away from ending his career. He turns a blind eye to reality and instead focuses on some fairy tale he has pictured for Sunday night where all the good guys win and the fans cheer them for their efforts. This Sunday will be anything but a fairy tale. Spawn pauses for a few seconds as he looks around the locker room and throws a smile at the two guys on the floor. He then looks back to the camera in front of him and begins to speak again. Spawn: He blissfully walks into this match thinking that if he just gives it his all everything will be all right. If he can just put on a good show for the fans then it will be a good night. In two days he will have to wake up to reality and realize that no one really gives a crap about him. The fans are not coming to Panic to see The Great 1. The only reason they would even cheer him in this match is if he did a sick bump. That is the kind of mindless vampires that some of these fans are. They do not care about you. They only come to watch you get hurt and to chant holly shit at your expense. That is the cold harsh reality Great 1. Soon you will face this as I defeat you at Panic. What you have chosen to ignore up until now will be thrown in your face in the Rage in a cage. There will be no glory for you. There will be no ovation or respect given. There will be no victory for you either. There will be nothing more than a crushing defeat in front of a crowd that did not really care if you won or not. You have to come to grips with reality this Sunday. This Sunday reality walks up and slaps you in the face as I walk out as the victory. Spawn pauses once more. He looks down to the ground for a second and then back up to the camera. A moment later he begins to speak once again. Spawn: I do not care who you are fighting for and I do not care what you are fighting for. I do not know why you signed for a match in which you have no chance of winning, and I could care less. No matter how much you prepare or dedicate this match; that will not change the inevitable outcome. You simply cannot beat me, especially after coming out of a brutal titanic trip (smiling). You will not even be fit to wrestle but still you strive to give the fans what they paid for and to compete in this match. Even though you have no chance of winning or even putting up a fight in the Rage Cell you still march towards it in anticipation. You talk of putting up a fight and pushing yourself to a new level when you will be lucky to walk into the Rage Cell on your own two feet. Even if you are able to do that you still have to fight the best that this company has to offer in the most dangerous match we have to offer. Simply put, you do not have a chance in hell of winning this match. I am not underestimating you; I am simply telling you the facts. You will see when the smoke clears at Panic. Spawn pauses once more, not taking his eyes from the camera this time. He waits for a few seconds to go by and then he begins to speak again. Spawn: Great 1, this Sunday is your own inevitable devastation. It comes at the hands of the greatest technical wrestler in the world inside the Rage Cell. There is nothing you can do to stop it, or to avoid it. You will have to face it this Sunday at Panic. The clock has just about run out kid, and the curtain is coming down on your short future. There is only a little time left for you before you run into a brick wall at Panic. You made this an issue Great 1, and now you must pay for that. You must face your decisions and live with the consequences for them. They will last a lifetime. These consequences will prove to be fatal for your career and all because you had to have your stupid revenge. The hour is almost upon us, and all you have to do now is wait. Spawn stands up and walks over to the dumbbells and picks up one from the bottom of the stand. He begins to do some curls with it as the scene gradually fades out. We see him do about ten of them and then we fade to black. Mike: Cut great job Spawn, a very good promo man. Spawn: Thanks Mike, you also did a hell of a job as well. I would like to stay and chat but I have things to do so I‘ll be seeing you, oh and Mike. Mike: Yes Spawn? Spawn: Could you have some one peak those two bums from the floor, they are wasting my space in the locker room. Mike: Will do Spawn see you later. Spawn leaves the locker as the scene fades to black.
