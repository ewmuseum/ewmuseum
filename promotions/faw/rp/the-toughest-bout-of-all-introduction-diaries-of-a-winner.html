'The Toughest Bout of All' Introduction: 'Diaries of a Winner'

'The Toughest Bout of All' Introduction: 'Diaries of a Winner' JLC Furiously Addictive Wrestling Roleplay #200 Date: Jan 03 Location: In the mind. Eighteen Months, One and a half years, In the past one and a half years what has changed? Terrorism becomes the mutual enemy all across the world. Some of the greatest thinkers and leaders have died. When I look through a modern history book now, there are not many you can point to and say 'he's alive.' Muhammad Ali, … Any others, not to my knowledge? But what has effected you personally? How have you changed? You've maybe matured more, maybe, in some cases. You've gained more responsibilities. Some of us may have gained an education, got a nice new job. Not only that, but probably someone close to you has passed on. Unfortunately diseases are still in the world; crime is still in the world, as is bitterness. But fuck you for a minute. I've spent most of my life thinking of other people. I want to talk about me. Eighteen months, the total amount of time I've spent at FAW, but last year has to have been the most life changing. How long did I spend in FAW? January to July, most of it, working behind the scenes. Then I started up again from August to September. Winning the FAW title and not getting beat for it again. New record. Big whoop! It wasn't a great achievement winning a scrappy strap that has been passed around the roster more than the British Bomber's mother was. It’s a nice little prize, don't get me wrong, but lost its mystique when… well, I won it. Last of the true champions. That's how I see myself, which would probably explain a lot. But I'm no shrink; I'm not going to self-analyse myself; it's not my scene. But fuck FAW for a second. FAW. It's all my life has been. I'm sick of it. On January 12th of this year, my then fiancee Michelle announced to me that she was pregnant, with my baby. I was flabbergasted. I didn't mean for it to happen. I couldn't be bothered. I went and buried myself in work. FAW. It wasn't until March I finally accepted it. I went from shocked, to frightened. For the baby. Was I ready for being a father? Would it come into a loveless world? I love Michelle; she's a perfect creature. Beautiful, long black curly hair draped on her perfectly formed shoulder. Her light hazelnut brown skin is perfect. Her cute face is perfect. Her figure is perfect. I don’t know why she's still with me. You'd think someone with this beauty would leave me. I deprived her of a loving husband. Something she deserved. Extreme Beating 2 came. A two-bit jobber who just fell of a van and took me out with a bat humiliated me. Big woop. I'll never forgive Skipster. I hope he fucking rots in hell. As soon as I fell out of the pit, I knew I had lost it. The has-been trying to reclaim his fame. That night, I went back to my hotel room. I looked at the mirror. 'I fucking hate you.' I had a perfect wife why the fuck was I wasting my time putting jobbers over? It's like the promo for Breaking Point said, I was rusted and weathered. But yet I continued. I look back and to this day I don't know why the fuck I carried on? I say for the fans. They may have cheered me on. But did they respect me? Did they fuck? They were there for the latest craze. They just cheered me on because I was the victim. I look up at them and honestly want to know, if they have a clue what the fuck is going on. 'We love you James.' 'FAW wouldn't be here without you, he love you.' 'Unlucky Jim, you deserved the win then.' Fuck off you yes men. If I wasn't so gullible I might have enjoyed my life. FAW was as enjoyable as a Chechnyan burial ground. So I went on, beat Tom 'The Bomb' Johansen. Big Woop. Then my first true defeat. To a gentlemen, HaHaKlown. The only guy who has actually stuck by me, behind the scenes. It's no myth. I often dabble in backstage politics. Often I'm wrong. But that guy has been a tower of strength, the only one who is. He deserves a fucking medal. I wouldn't have stuck by me for most of it. He beat me. Not only that. But he beat me well. When he beat me I knew I had been beat. I had gained the honour that was stripped of me when I lost the title to… nobody, then it went to a nobody. I had deprived the 'fans' of an honourable title. How did I regain my honour? I went out to a true champion. I returned. I won the title. Then I stuck my nose where it supposedly didn't belong. I was right. It was proved later. Hardly anyone believed me. But, I terminated my contract. Fucking suits. They say they care about FAW, bollocks, they care about themselves. Well if they care about FAW so much, let's see how they re-act when their champion leaves them high and dry. I went home and became a man. I became a proper husband. I did the chores, I cut out the drink. At on 12:39am in St Mary's Hospital, San Antonio, Texas. My daughter Jessica Maria Grimston was born. This was the first round of what would be my toughest challenge… To be Continued What happens to Jessica? Why did JLC Return? It's all in the next chapter.
