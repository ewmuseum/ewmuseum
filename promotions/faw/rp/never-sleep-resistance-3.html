Never Sleep (Resistance #3) Profusion Furiously Addictive Wrestling Roleplay #267 Date: 10-02-03 Location: Houston, Texas ((.. fade in..)) (Profusion standing on a giant glimmering black stage in front of pixelated studio lights which spell out 'PROFUSION' similar to what P.Diddy uses. He stands before the huge sign with a shiny black Versace pants, black patton leather Stacey Adams shoes, a blue dragon hawaiin shirt with japanese designs, platinum and gold rolex with diamonds around the face and on the band, a platinum chain shaped like a diamond with the words 'Understanding' going through it in cursive. He slowly holds a mic to his mouth at this Fall Jam event, the mic rests in front of his mouth as his bloodshot eyes focus on the see of fans.. he mutters something out grimly.. ) 'I never sleep... cuz sleep is the cousin of death' ~Nasty Nas, Illmatic, 1994. 'I hate sleep.. and I'ma tell you all why... because as you sleep, the unknown begins to creep into this living realm and causes change. I hate change. Change is something so unpredictable that rather then finding out whether the results are good or bad, it's better just to avoid it at all costs, because as you change for the better.. you simultaneously change for the worst... I never sleep.. cuz sleep is the cousin of death. As many scientists would dispute, when we sleep our brain begins to help us to cope with the things we have been through and problems/issues we at the time didn't know how to resolve. The Great One better not ever fall asleep again, because one day he might awaken to a completely parallel world, one that will soon be realized at the next event... You see... Profusion is about to 'change' things around, he's about to make everyone realize that all the great things The Great One has accomplished in the past... ain't sh*t... I got young bucks spittin at me AND 'wannabe' champs gettin at me, I think back and Biggie predicted the @#%$ exactly. 'Mo' Money, Mo' Problems' and now I gotta move carefully, cause faggots hate when you gettin money like athletes. And Great One. Lyrically, you not feelin me? Fine; it costs you nothin -- pay me no mind! Look, I'm on my grind cousin and I ain't got time for frontin. Fatal Impact? Sensitive thugs, y'all all need hugs if you hatin on The Understanding. Damn lil' man you ain't gotta hate me, I'm just tryin do Profusion., who else can I be? Damn, sure don't wanna be like Great One. Ya see. You knew me before F.I., you never disrespected me, but now that I'm successful you'll pull this? Bullsh*t! Let's end the speculation, I'm talkin to all of y'all. So if Great One thinks he can make me quit, just set the date. But first lemme tell him, males shouldn't be jealous that's a female trait, what you mad cuz you done more as champ, but I still get respect? That's because my ability make this whole feud look like an exercise in your futility. You see it ain't about the music, the flash, the style, the promos, it's about Profusion’s destiny. Ever since that fateful day in January which you constantly refer to, it made me that much closer to become your greatest opponent. Not because you WOULD NOT have become champion without your mates, but because it foreshadowed this very moment, where The Great One would rise up to challenge Profusion and finally 'The Understanding' would be realized. The ills of the world are irrelevant to what is going to soon happen, you can throw all those useless examples, and meaningless side talk into all of your promos, but it justs proves that you are scrambling for words to throw at Profusion. None of it means anything, what does Cancer, Aids, the war against terrorism, dancing, singing, the state of the world, what you ate for breakfast, looks, karaoke battles, who the fans cheer for, what others say, or what you think matter? None of it means sh*t. Your whole promo means sh*t. You mean sh*t. The cliche that is The Great One is soon going to pass like every other fad, it was cool for awhile, but the time has come and you've wore out your welcome, the people are ready for a real champion. Not a guy that just blabs on and on about world hunger in the middle of the ring. I mean let's face it, if you want to talk about all that, then tell the people the facts and not your shady bullsh*t. AIDS was introduced to the human race from the Green Monkey, and the sumb*tch who raped that monkey was young Great One. There, why beat around the bush, your secret is out and now maybe you can say something with meaning. Now maybe you can piece together a thought that deserves to be put out there for people to here, you ain't even the best wrestler in FAW, so how the hell are you gonna claim to be the best in the world, let alone better then Profusion. My Time ain't over, I haven't had it yet, get it through your skull, YOU CAN'T BEAT ME. Why won't you submit to the truth? I mean let's face it, you have to reference your history, yet I AM the one who is still going to have to reflect on the past? You talk about me getting ambushed by the under lords, yet I AM the one who is in need of the realization of who is the better between us? Quite simply, it's no contest, you don't come close, you aren't up to par, you can't cut the mustard, you get the snub, you're cut, you can't even lace my boots, your name doesn't deserve to be in the same sentence, you suck, you suck, you suck. How many different ways and in how many different languages do I have to express this? ...I never sleep.. cuz sleep is the cousin of death... The Great One. When he just started out and he already has one foot in the grave and another on a banana peel, you think it's just the greatest thing to be on top of the world don't you Great One? But, what you don't realize is that it only puts you that much more at risk. You don't have any room for error, no room to make mistakes, you have to play your hand perfectly from here... and that bullsh*t-ass promo you just did was your first slip-up so I guess it's already too late for you to claim greatness. I'm ending your greatness before it even starts, I never f**kin sleep because I have to keep my guard up at all times, I grew up that way, I grew where you held your blacks up, and the feds tried to trap us. That's how I know you can never win Great One, because the stipulation of the match revolves around 2 words that are not even in my vocabulary. That's how I know you can never win Drake, because you have underestimated, miscalculated, under compensated, and took every other wrong turn on the path through Profusion. Your Destiny is Mine. And through all the time you've spent having status handed to you without any real effort on your part, Profusion was stacking up a list of accolades that scrapes the heavens. Sure you mastered in most matches, you beat alot of good fighters, and you're about the best cheap shot artist their is. And when it comes to sticking ones head up their own ass, you're second to none. But, what have you done to put you in the same league of Profusion? I've never made the big time? All I do is sing and dance. L is for Loser. Again. Tell me Great One. How does it feel to lose to a nobody? How does it feel to be outclassed by the legend that never was? How does it feel to have me punching you in the chest into it feels like your heart is going to explode? Yeah we know Great One... you're a sorry sumbitch ain't you? Well you get no mercy! And if you want to escape with your life, stick 2 the script.' [In order to truly master war, one must learn the art of deception] ~'The Art of War,' Loose Translation. 'Yes, I see Great One likes to write journals now. Yet another style Profusion brought to the FAW first and no, it wasn't perfected by Great One, how can you perfect what is already too potent to contend with? But, anyway.. as you could already tell Profusion was ready for this to happen at any time, but some still just don't understand. Great One claims to have been Profusion’s best friend? I think I recall a famous artist once saying 'f*ck friends, I only have family' well Great One.. that applies to you. You were not Profusion’s friend, you were his guinea pig, you were nothing more then a worthless lab rat, a pawn in a game of chess that is on a scale much larger then you could ever hope to comprehend. 'Personal sh*t' The Great One you don't know 'personal sh*t', this ain't personal, this is strictly business. There is an unwritten bounty on your head, and Profusion will collect the pay. No, there is not anyone who has moved to have this price tag set upon you, but the fact that Fatal Impact is now the mortal enemy of all who represent the FAW.. it is understood to be there. What is my reward you say? My reward is to be known as the man who put The Great One in his place and retaining his Continental Title against the sumbitch.. Or as some would call it, who recognized it's significance before these days arrived, the 'symbol of greatness.' At one time that Continental Championship was reserved for men of a higher calibre, with skill, with strength, with mental and physical abilities that exceeded those of the ordinary con artist.. a.k.a. The Great 'Wet Dream' One. That is why, for you to 'emerge triumphant' would be a crime against everything immortal about that title belt, it would be an injustice to the true 'legends.' Not those who sit around and try to get by on their name alone, not those who pick fights with those they believe they can surely defeat, not those who got lazy and flopped back on their old asses. But, to men who set examples for all the new generation stars like myself. JLC survived bomb fires, hell in a cell matches, and so many other brutal matches, but Profusion has long since passed that. Chart Topper, Bowery Death, Triple Threat Hell In A Cell, Inaugural Brawls, Assault and Battery, and so many more I've survived. The Ninth Wonder of Foam though he was a World Champion, is surely remembered for his great title reign, Profusion has long since proved that he could exceed that. I have proven in the past that I can brawl with the best of them and if I have proven to be able to do that then I can guarantee that I will walk away victorious at Resistance. Great One, you have had your career handed to you on a silver platter and you know you have only gotten as far as you have today but ass kissing and in your defence your year or so here in the FAW. There have been men who have come and gone who where far superior to you and men like HaHaKlown comes to mind as being one of those men. Another is a superstar who is in the FAW today, All Fk’N that Jeff Harris. I meet the man at Vendetta with my Continental Championship on the line, and we both know that we are going to put on a show for the ages. We may not see eye to eye but I can tell you this, we can, when combine forces destroy any mere mortal here in the FAW. When my legacy has surpassed the FAW and onto the OSW I will leave my mark, and my mark shall continue my legacy for beware Profusion may be leaving but his next of kin shall come in and reign supreme. In order to truly master war, one must learn the art of deception. Great One you must be kidding yourself, have you not fully understood the stipulation yet? Let's take a look, HIT THE VID-SCREEN.' 'First of all Great One, this is an I.Q. match really, to see who is the more intelligent man inside the ring. Why am I not saying it out loud? You might use some kinda of tape recorder or something to make it sound like I am saying those fateful words... 'I.. well you know.' But, as far as the rest of that goes, you are half wrong and half right, just like you're half good and half crap. Ya see it is quite simply impossible for you to set up the 'Wet Dream,' on me, since it is OH SO similar to one of the many variations of 'The Understanding,' and since I have thoroughly studied the way you fight, I can not only sense it coming.. but reverse it. You see you can make all the jokes, all the lies, all the nice little parodies, but again.. it's just irrelevant bullsh*t. You are irrelevant bullsh*t. Fatal Impact is irrelevant bullsh*t. I'm going to end it once and for all, by cutting the head of the snake off, you are the best F.I. has The Great One.. and I'm the best FAW has, but the difference is.. I still have my piece of mind in this place. You have to live with the fact that you sold your soul to the Devil, you have to live with that in the back of your mind, eating away at you, destroying you, I can't even put into words how sweet the taste of victory will be. I'm going to shut your mouth from the cliched promos to that wack ass rap you wrote, I mean look at it, the f**king answer back's a joke! 'It's Dark, and Hell is Hot,' but you have to realize that even if I were to 'die' from your so-called 'suicide' I would be going down fighting for the FAW which I represent, 'Flesh of My Flesh, Blood of My Blood,' damn it looks like Profusion got some classic sh*t too. Everybody loves Profusion, and it ain't no mistake!' (He pulls a bunch of fans up on stage of all backgrounds and races) Kid 1: ce qui est Profusion est le plus grand de tout le temps!!!! Great One, you have chose your path to your defeat, I never wanted this match but you were granted it because you were trying to act like the big man and stop me walking away to OSW with the FAW Continental Championship, well since you like being the big man, you are going to witness what happens to people who think they are big and I am going to take you to hell’s gates and back. Atticus Pulsar found it out yesterday at Panic and you are going to find it out, two day’s from now at Resistance. I enjoy being the Continental Champion and if you think I am going to throw it away by being beaten by a little punk like you then you are severely mistaken. You should take adherence to this little promo of mine because it will teach you a valuable lesson to not mess with men who you know nothing of. You knew nothing about me and you have got yourself into this shit, but I can guarantee that after Resistance, you will know more about me than almost everyone. Resistance will be a long, gruelling match with me picking you apart and making you understand just who the big gun is around here. As you have guessed, I am very underrated around here in the FAW but it shall change not for me but for my kin. Take head Great One because the time is ticking away for you to make a response to plead for mercy from the one and only true contemptuous killer, you fear me, and I fear loosing my Continental Title someday, but not Wednesday, not at Resistance and not to you.
