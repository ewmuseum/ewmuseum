The Best Ideas are Often the Simplest

16/10/02

The Best Ideas are Often the Simplest The Duke Furiously Addictive Wrestling Roleplay #15 Date: 16/10/02 Location: Various ‘Ow…’ I rubbed the back of my neck, the pain inflicted from that excruciating powerbomb still in full effect. The idiot didn’t know what the hell he was doing in that ring. I cursed to myself, hoping that I would get better opponents in the future than lumbering imbeciles. It simply wasn’t something I was willing to endure, having to the humiliation of facing such pathetic excuses for wrestlers. I had a reputation to uphold. I pulled off my mask and sat down on the locker room bench, the sweat pouring off my head like it was the Sahara desert I was in, not Winter in America. Man I was out of shape. Even with all the time and training I had had I still wasn’t close to what I should be. Nothing to excuse it, but damn I could still do it! Doesn’t say much for the crap in FAW that myself, not even close to 100 percent, probably more like 70, was still supremely more rounded to beat a man who has been in FAW for some time now and has a regular schedule. Now that is depressing. Why even bother to try? No, I’ll try, and I know why. I’ll try because to prove it to the fans, show them how wrong they are about things. No matter what they say about me and how much they boo, they won’t be able to deny that I am the greatest, the best damn guy they’ve got anywhere around. That’s not just a truth, it’s going to become fucking true. But where can I start? How will I begin to bring in back what I need to become the wrestler that I used to be, the wrestler that used to be peerless under the pressure, flawless when it comes to the technique, unparalleled when it came to the desire? Where had it gone? Where could I find it? There was a knock on the locker room door and a man walked in. Just an average Joe, nothing special about him, a face in the crowd. He could be a hobo, he could be a complete drug abuser, a sex addict, all kinds of crazy shit but I didn’t care, he was nothing in comparison to what I was. ‘A note for you sir, next week’s card.’ I jerked my head, sending jets of hot pain up my back, the neck still sore. Oh this guy was going to pay now. ‘Yeah whatever, give it to me.’ I snapped the paper from his hand before kicking him right in the groin, sufficient payback for his infliction of pain on my neck, no matter how indirect. He scuttled off, sideways with the searing pain raging through his nuts. I laughed to myself, looking at the piece of paper. ‘WHAT!’ My first reaction wasn’t the greatest. I was on it twice. First in a Hardcore Triple Threat to decide the No.1 Contender and then again in a tag match with Crimson Lord against Renegade and Rzerector. This was bullshit, this was unbelievable this was… Perfect. What better way to prove myself? What better way to show them that I was definitely the best and what better way to improve my readiness for what I really wanted to sink my teeth into, a serious Singles Title. Oh yes this was good, very good. Now the only problem was, how am I going to train? Working against myself only does it for so long, I need a sparring partner. Hmm I don’t have anyone here right now so who else could there… Sean. Wait, how am I going to ask him? I mean he was my original trainer for nigh on 6 years but how the hell can I just come back to Kildare, walk in on him and ask him to train me again? That was a bit much, even for me. However he owes me, he owes so much after I brought him fame, recognition, credibility. I mean he must be doing great with the publicity from my glorious conquests. I mean without me he’s probably still stuck in that small gym, slaving away with some kids, hoping one of them could be like me, hoping one of them could make their break and he could hopefully garner some good from it. Yeah, Sean always was the coattails man. Well now was his chance to earn his way on my coattails. I grabbed my bag, shoved everything in as quickly as possible and moved off, out of the locker room. I said I’d make something of myself Shona before I come back… **Next Day** The planes seemed to drift in the sky as I exited Dublin Airport, back on Irish soil for the first time in over six months. The memories flooded back but I shut them away, not willing to relive them right now. I had a purpose for being here and I didn’t want to cloud my already murky head. I hailed a taxi and dove in, telling him to bring me to the Bus Terminal right in the heart of Dublin, I didn’t want to waste half my money on a bloody rip off taxi to Kildare. Traffic was light which was surprising for a city like Dublin, at least how I remembered it. It resembled a sprawling expanse of haphazard buildings and worn monuments. The place held over one quarter of the population of Ireland in the city alone. It was a bitch to live in, the usual evening entertainment now becoming a regular round of knife fights to the death… But I loved it. I love Dublin, it was home, it was the place I loved to be in, not in sterile American cities. Here there was buzz, there was excitement, there was a…for lack of a better word, a scene. There was a general consensus of knowing people here in Dublin, even with the size of it. Some people just were known by a lot. The famed Four Courts building, home to where we fought each other over our country was still standing tall and proud, its bullet riddled exterior showing the signs of the war from nearly a century ago now. It was still an impressive building, full of rugged beauty. ‘Alright that’ll be 24 Euro.’ Damn stupid over bloated economy, seeping the money right out from my pocket… ‘Yeah, here you go.’ I hopped out of the car, wanting to be rid of that degrading experience of being forced to pay whatever he said. I pulled the bag right out of the trunk and left without a word, darting into the terminal. There wasn’t much of a queue there and I strode up to the kiosk. There behind the glass stood the biggest sorry excuse for a woman there ever could be. I mean when you think of ugly, you think like Rosie O’Donnell ugly but this woman made Rosie a fucking goddess in comparison. For a start she had this buzzcut army style, but yet it wasn’t at the same time. It was like someone had thought halfway through that it looked crap and tried to change it. Unfortunately they weren’t good and instead created this demonic mutation of buzzcut and long hair mess. I mean if you had said I had to differentiate her and a mule’s ass, I couldn’t tell you. Next there was the face, or what one could try and fathom as a face if you could stretch it. It had more bumps than a piece of botched graft of several bars of soap. It looked gritty like sandpaper and if you looked close enough you could find the ridge of a nose. Finally was the stench. It was revolting, like rotten apples, vomit and toilet cleaner combined in some unholy matrimony. Truly she was a demon, nobody could smell that bad and still live. So despite all this I had to endure it to get a ticket for Kildare. So stepping up with handkerchief placed firmly in front of the face, sunglasses on to stem the horrifying sight of the apocalypse, I bravely moved forward. ‘Yes…sir?’ ‘I’d like a ticket to Kildare as quickly as possibly please!’ ‘Okay…that’ll be 8.50 Euro sir.’ I rifled the money through to her, this ordeal a lot to take. She agonisingly and slowly processed the ticket, each moment a hoard of disgusting putrid smells and sights. Finally she placed the ticket through and pulled a contorted facial expression. If it was a smile, god help anybody who annoyed her. ‘Thank you sir, enjoy your trip.’ I bolted like a lobster out of hot water and raced to the terminal required. Luckily the bus was to come quickly and I didn’t have long to wait. In short order it was ready and I stepped on, among the crowd of peasants. I was surprised no one knew me. I was, after all the greatest export Ireland had had in some time. Why shouldn’t they be falling over themselves for me? Bah I didn’t need to worry myself with these insignificant peons lives. I had a more important schedule. I took a seat at the back, eased in and relaxed. **Three Hours Later** ‘OW!’ The bus screeching to a halt shoved me forward, my head meeting the back of the chair in front. I was sent into stars from the impact but it did give me the jolt awake, however shocking and painful it was. I looked up, rubbing my forehead to see I was here. I was truly home. I grabbed my bag and walked steadily down the bus corridor and out into the fresh air. Kildare Town, my home. For nigh on sixteen years it was my home, the place I rested, the place I had fun, the place I just enjoyed life in general. It was still home, in a memorable sense but it was alien, everything looked changed and different. Nothing could bring back that time now. Again I digress. I sprinted across the road and headed off down the beaten path, up to the gym of old. Many times in the past I had walked this path, excited to be back again, excited to learn more about what I could become, excited and dreaming. Dreaming of fame and fortune. Dreaming of the sex, drugs and rock and roll lifestyle that was to come. I suppose I had had two out of those three in some way but I had lost it all again. Now it was just me. And here I stood, in front of where I began originally, where I would begin again. The gym was still the same, brown and mouldy looking. The dull day didn’t exactly add to the glamour of it at all, the paint chipping away from the cracks in the walls. It was a solid block of concrete but it hads housed a great. Me. I shoved open the door and stepped inside. I walked down the single corridor leading to the main gym area. Inside would be the old gear I could guarantee. A ring, old and blue, the faded mats around it giving some protection. Several punching bags to one side and a couple of weights in another corner. Students’ bags littering the area and generally making it a mess. And by god was I right. Of course Sean was standing in the ring, two students sparring in front of him, his still impressive build barking at them to get stuck in, telling them to do anything it took. His mane of red hair was now cropped back, a receding hairline probably a major reason for doing so. He was six foot four from what I remembered but I wouldn’t like to guess his weight, times do change after all. When I left at sixteen he was already thirty eight. Now he was pushing forty six into forty seven. One of the students finally managed to get a grasp of things and managed a body slam. Sean clapped and shouted in a hurrah, more than likely because the kids had been tensed in the position for a long time. He went to turn to his students and he caught my stare. I smiled and dropped the bag, startling the other students who were watching. Sean looked at me very caught off guard and didn’t know what to say. I took it for him. ‘God you guys look like you couldn’t even get an arm drag down properly! Jesus Sean what’s happened to the crop of Kildare these days?’ He smiled and took it from there. Near the end of my stay in Ireland I used to play a trick on the younger kids with Sean. He would be slaving over them for ages and they’d be doing nothing useful. I would act as if totally unimpressed and challenge them, their little heads unknowing of my frequent flyer miles as Sean liked to call it in the ring. Of course it only worked on the first couple of days or so, otherwise they copped who I was. ‘Ah too true, too true. But I bet you couldn’t do any better you lump of dung! I bet Tommy here could kick you around silly!’ The kid in the ring was furious and chimed in with Sean. ‘Of course! You think you can walk in here like some superstar?’ I pulled off my coat, leaving my shirt. I had my baggies on and wrestling boots underneath so I was pretty much ready for the slaughter. ‘Does that answer your question?’ The kid smiled and Sean winked before taking control. ‘Alright, everybody out! We’ll see how this newcomer can take hold of things!’ Everyone moved back and I slid into the ring where to my surprise the kid dived on me. I rolled to the side and got to my feet, the kid a bit annoyed he missed. He got to his feet and let him come, sure he wouldn’t know what was coming. He charged forward like a bull and as I winded up with the thrust kick his face took on a look of terror, the realisation of what about to happen slowly hit him. Then I hit him. Hard. His head snapped back viciously and he fell to the canvas without a word. I went to pick him up, however his sparring partner slid in and dived at me low, not wanting to be a victim of my deadly legs. So instead I nailed him with some punches. He staggered back, falling into the ropes. I moved forward and whipped him at the others and as he came back I nailed him with a reverse elbow, sending him spinning to the mat. Hopping to the ring apron I vaulted using the ropes with a somersault body attack, knocking the wind out. I deftly landed and got to my feet before turning to the crowd. ‘Well? Anyone else?’ There was complete silence in the room. Slowly Sean began clapping before sliding in. He hugged me and turned to them. ‘Boys, I want you to meet my most successful wrestler I have ever taught, Z…’ ‘No, not my real name.’ ‘Sorry, Duke.’ They all looked gobsmacked, probably they’d been told about me and were unsure to take it as if it was true. However Sean couldn’t care less and neither did I. ‘Anyway, class is over guys, see you next week and could somebody wake those two lumps behind me? Tell them it was a lesson learned…in humility.’ They all walked off in small clusters, excited about what had just come to pass. They whispered among themselves as they walked off, a couple pulling the two boys out of the ring. Soon we were alone. Sean was glad to see me and we began talking like old friends, swapping stories and the like between ourselves. Inevitably the mood changed to wrestling and soon to myself. Sean was insistent with questions and eventually I got down to it. ‘…So now I’ve nobody and I need to start again Sean, I need to return to my roots and that’s why I’ve come. Sean I want you to train me.’ He looked at me for a while, studying me before answering. ‘I…I don’t know what to say but Duke, I can’t train you all the time. I don’t want to move from here and I know you won’t want to stay. Sure I’ll give you some training every now and again but I can’t be your sole help. You’re going to have to find somebody more important. What have you got for this week.’ ‘Hmm I’ve a Hardcore match against a good few guys and I’ve a tag match as well…’ ‘There you go. Ask your partner, have you spoke to him before?’ ‘Yeah…once in the back.’ ‘Well then, give him a call and see if he can meet up with you.’ He was right, Crimson Lord could be perfect. I told him I’d need a minute and moved over to my bag, picking my phone out of it. I dialled the number I had got from him and waited. It rang. Rang. Rang. Rang. Nothing. Either he didn’t want to talk to me or… ‘Either I'm not here or I don't want to talk. Leave a message and I'll call you back later…’ Answering machine. Hearing the beep I got my message in. ‘Hi Cy, it's Duke. I haven't heard from you in awhile so I figured I'd check up on you. I heard you had a rough time during your matches the other day and I'm afraid something is wrong. I really want to talk to you so I'll try back later.’ With that it was over. I was about to try again maybe when a towel hit the back of my head. It was Sean waiting in the ring. ‘Well? You want to train or not?’ I screwed my fists up and dropped the phone. Now this was more like it.
