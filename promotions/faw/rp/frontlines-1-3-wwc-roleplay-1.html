Frontlines (1/3) WWC Roleplay #1 Rzarector Furiously Addictive Wrestling Roleplay #161 Date: January 21, 2003 Location: Heaven ~~~~~A strong urge is pulling at my soul. An urge like no other. An addiction that is worse than any drug is tearing me apart.~~~~~~ This Sunday I will be entered in a 20 man royal rumble for a shot at the World Title. Is this the urge that is tearing me apart? Knowing that I almost had Foamy beat? Knowing that this time I will? *The screen opens to pitch black. There a very dark, low, and evil growl is heard. A voice that could collapse towers is then heard speaking.* Narrator: The time has come once again. For the forces of evil and good to battle for the leadership of the souls on Earth. Rzarector always said he could feel the urge of the World Title pulling at him throughout the night. Yet his word still remains true. He doesn't want the world title for the greed, he wants it because he needs to spread the message of God. Today we will show you how Rzarector is doing and getting ready for his match. Next time the prophet will be back to spread the message of God on all of his opponents. *The camera remains black as a small echoe of the voice is heard in the background. The screen eventually starts to light up. There Rzarector stands, dressed in a black hooded robe like always, with his green eyes and white fangs shining out from under the hood. Rzarector is standing next to a park bench as he looks at everyone walking past him. He hisses at some of the gothic people for being satanists. Rzarector walks over a little ways and sits down on a green park bench directly opposite from the one he was just standing by. He puts his head into his lap and begins praying to God. At that same time, God answers him back.* Rzarector: God, you know, this Sunday I have another shot to get my goal accomplished. It is going to be truly outstanding for one reason. That reason is that if I win I know I will go to Extreme Beatings 3 to face Foamy. Yet I think I can beat Foamy if I truly try. I hope I can make it through this battle royal. The faces I will see all hate me. They say that I am evil. Yet the truth is they are the sinners, they are evil, and I am left here by myself to fight off all of them. I promise I will seek all there skulls, and destroy them. Then I will put the last man out of the match, which I hope is Crimson, and I will win the entire match. No problem whatsoever. Now God I have to ask you of one thing.......... *Rzarector hesitates.* Rzarector: Well I am not in the best of shape for this match and I was wondering if there is anyway you could possibly help me in training. I only have a week, but then again that is all I should need. God are you there? *Rzarector stands there as noone is responding inside his head. Rzarector hisses a small hiss as his tongue slides out onto his lips. His fangs are hanging over the bottom of his lip as he asks God inside his head one more time. 'God are you there?' That was when the clouds started turning black, the wind picked up blowing trees over, and the snow and rain began to pour down onto Purgatory. Rzarector looking stressed ran over to a concrete bathroom and went inside. To his suprise everyone else was in there to. Rzarector asked once again, 'God are you there?' That was when God answered.* (God: Yes Rzarector I am here. There is something happening up here in heaven. I don't know what it is, but you need to come as quick as possible. It is very important.) Rzarector: But God I am still mortal. I can't die right now. Please don't let me die, just take my soul. (God: Times have changed. The skys are black, the moon red, rain and snow falling, and hurricane winds are blowing through Earth. You don't have the time, find a place to lay your body and close your eyes, then I will send you up here so we can have a conversation. That way I can tell you everything that is happening. Rza, hurry.) Rzarector: Yes God. *Rzarector immediately rushes out of the bathroom. He jumps over piles of snow as the hail from the sky is now cracking his skull open. The wind blows him over once into a fallen tree as he hurries and gets back up. Rzarector runs for shelter in this back ally. He finds one little spot that is covered by concrete steps. He quickly runs under them and lays down in the back covering himself up with boxes so noone can see him. Rzarector then once again, 'God are you there?'* (God: Have you found a safe place for the time being?) Rzarector: Yes God. I'm under these concrete steps. The snow and hail will not hit me, and I am out of sight from everyone. That way noone can rob me while I'm unconscious. God, what is happening up in heaven right now? (God: Heaven's gates have now been broken. War is among us, armageddon is now breaking out. Rzarector, as you are my lead angel I would just like to say that I need you up here right now. I can't really tell you what is happening but once you get here you will understand. So are you on your way now or are you going to take more time?) Rzarector: I'm on my way God. Just tell me where to go and I will be there. Just put my body under your control and make me come up there. I'm ready. (God: Okay, here we go.) *Rzarector sat down with his back leaning on an old building under those old steps. He felt his head become fairly dizzy as he closed his eyes. The Black Priest then felt half his body go numb as his mind just overtook him. There he opened his eyes to see several bright lights. Rzarector looks down at his feet which are not on any ground. Everything around him is pure white, and he feels as if he is floating upwards. As he looks up he see's sparkling diamonds and a calming voice telling him this way. Rza smiles as he keeps floating upwards through this mass dimension in space of nothing. The voice keeps calling his name in a faint whisper which is at the same time very loud. 'R z A r E c T o R' Rza looks up and keeps smiling as he is still in his black robe. Which his eyes are now blue, and his fangs are now longer than they were. 'R z A r E c T o R' Rzarector looks up as he hisses and yells out, 'I'M COMING!!!!' Rzarector's body then starts to shake violently as he gets closer to the diamonds. Rza blinks as when he opens he see's the diamonds right in front of him. He looks up once more to see a gate made out of gold with diamonds embedded in it. Rzarector feels his body with no motion in it at all. So he decides to walk towards the gate. He begins walking once he notices he can, the voices are still calling him. 'R z A r E c T o R t H i S w A y' Rzarector looks determined as he hisses and his green eyes light up. He is about 2 feet from the gate when he finally stops dead in his tracks. A huge ball of blue light starts to develop in front of him as the reflection is seen through the greens eyes of Rza. For a slight moment it is as if his eyes are now turning yellow. Rza watches on as the ball of light starts to form, which looks like a resemblance of a tree. The particles of light hurry around through the white space of nothing as they get more and more together. There you see it, a podium, and a man standing behind it. On the podium however is a book. Rza looks on as a caucasian man, with a white robe on and brown hair appears and starts to talk.* Man: Welcome to Heaven sir. Since you are here, somehow you have died. Maybe you can tell me your name so I can check to see if it is in the Book of Life. *Rzarector looks confused as questions start running through his head.* *Did God trick me? How could he do this? He told me there was an emergency in heaven, yet all he did was make me die. Now I will spend an eternity in hell. He gave me forever to correct my sins, yet I never did. But I tried, maybe my name will be in the Book of Life.* Rzarector: My name is Rzarector, or Prince Paul. *The man nods his head as he opens up the book of life and starts to go through it. Then the guy looks up and slams the book.* Rzarector: Well am I in it? Man: I was looking for Prince Paul right? Rzarector: Yes you were. Did you find it anywhere? Man: No. I'm sorry, your name is not in the book of life. It has already reported that you have already died and went to hell. How you escaped i don't know. But I'm sorry, I must send you back. *All of a sudden the clouds under Rza's feet disappear and he starts falling. On his way down he looks and see's a huge lake of fire underneath him. You will never want to go back to life.) Rzarector: That is all I hoped for. *Rzarector continues flying on the cloud as it reaches the palace and goes inside. There it stops and Rzarector gets off. He walks over to this chair and sits down in it. God's voice is heard once more again.* (God: Okay Rzarector. Here is the basic situation. See they might have just been out in the white space, never to be found. Yet they have found enough people to actually strike an attack. The evil spirits you sensed on your way through the gate were them. Very strong people, yet my power can block them off for a while so you could get in. Now as I speak they are at the gate pounding on it, trying to open it. Eventually they will open it. There is nothing we can do about it.) *Rzarector stands there shocked. It will be a hard battle Rzarector. I just hope that I can wish you the best of luck.) Rzarector: God I doubted you for one second. And I truly am sorry. Then everything will be lost.) Rzarector: God, time has come. I must defend what I am spending my whole life to get to. Now is not the time for apologies. Now is the time for me to do my job. To correct people of there sins. Demons never can be corrected. Yet the only way to kill a demon is to severe there head and bring back there skulls. So how ever many they are, just be prepared to collect all of there skulls. Because I am not in the mood to play around. *All of a sudden the whispers were heard once again.* ~~~rZaReCtOrWeNeEdYoUnOw~~~ Rzarector: They are talking to me once again. Not the Black Priest Rzarector. So whatever they are saying just remember that they don't know that your good yet. Understand? So no tricks. Walk up to them, get rid of them. No time to waste. Just then Rzarector jumps on the cloud and God's voice is heard. The cloud immediately shoots off through the front door of the palace. Through the happy people talking on the streets. Through the golden buildings. And eventually to the front of the gate. There Rza stops as he looks at the golden gate. You see it has claw marks on it and is shaking violently. They are more human looking than demonic. Rza hops off the cloud and walks forward as the gate busts open. 5 pairs of red eyes are seen.* Rzarector: What do you guys want with heaven?
