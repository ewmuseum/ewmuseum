BOMBER I WILL BLOW YOU UP

10/15/02
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
BOMBER I WILL BLOW YOU UP Spawn Furiously Addictive Wrestling Roleplay #7 Date: 10/15/02 Location: DISTORTION RP 2 The screen opens up in a cave filled with voodoo dolls all over the place and candles lit. Its is dark out side and the only light in the cave in from the candles. The Flames are moving in the wind. The light is swaying back and forth. The light keeps catching a glimpse of Spawn sitting in the corner in a black robe with the hood over his head. You can see the Evil look in his eyes. They seem to be glowing when the light shines away from them. Spawn waves his hand threw the air and all the candles suddenly go out. A lighter is struck in front of the camera and Spawn is standing in front of the camera with his face almost touching the lens. Spawn''' Welcome. Welcome to my world. Welcome to hell. A place of trapped souls. Spawn starts to laugh and then he backs up away from the camera as he lights a candle. He then moves a little closer to the camera. ( Not as close as before) Spawn''' Bomber, Bomber, Bomber. You do know me very well do you? Your calling me a Goth veteran. Maybe here, But that dint mean shit. You want this other guy to team with your bitch ass? Hell, go right on ahead. I've got to take the two of you any damn way. So do as you wish. But Hell will rain once again and defeat your asses. You talk about good always winning. Son, I think you have watched a little to much Cartoons. But that's not the point. The point is me kicking your ass at Distortion and be the survivor. That's a good name for the show. Who will Survive? It sure as hell isn't going to be you. Hell will win. Hell will prove to the two of you who the best is. Hell will not be defeated. Spawn Roles his eyes to the back of his head. Spawn''' You said something about people getting pissed at me about the Goth gimmick! Well, let them be pissed. If they have got a problem with it, Ill kick there ass too just Like I am going to do to you come Sunday. Ill take there souls as well as I am going to do to you. I will prove to all of you, As well as the fans. That hell will rain once again. Show all of you who the best is. Spawn scratches his chin for a moment while he is thinking. Spawn''' Hells Coming. And you will be sorry you ever fucked with it. You will be sorry you ever fucked with me. And you will be sorry you was ever put in this match. Spawn pushes his hair out of his face and keeps on talking. Spawn''' I see that you are trying to be smart. Your not wanting to take me to lightly. And that's a good thing. See I think you might be a little scared of me. That's probably the reason you want Great 1 to team with you. But like I said before none of that will make a difference. The Only thing that will be happening is me kicking the two of your asses and making a point to every one in the FAW. Proving to every one I'm not some little Bitch. And to you that I'm not a fucking Jobber. But I will Job your ass after this match. Your carrier will be put on hold. Because this will be a gruesome match. And someone will be injured. Yes, it might be me. Yes, it might be you. But the only way I will get hurt is by hurting you son. Making you pay the price. Making hell rain one more time. And moving on to bigger and better things besides you. After Distortion, Every one will bow to the name of Spawn. Every one will know The power of Spawn, and every one will finally know whose Hells taker and Keeper is. And you Bomber will be a forgotten bitch. Spawn walks out side of the cave where there is a full moon and a couple of trees near by. There is a rocky cleft beside him. He turns back around and faces the camera. Spawn''' A night just like tonight. You will find out the reason I am in FAW. That reason is to steal the souls of innocent people and bring them to hell with me. You and will be my Victim this Sunday Bomber. You are going to pay. But before I make you pay. Ask your selves one question. Spawn''' ARE YOU READY TO BURN IN HELL?¿?¿? The next night Screen opens up at the gates of a cemetery. The wind is swaying the trees and making a whistling sound. The Camera moves on in to the creeping sounding and creepy cemetery. The Camera zooms around for a moment and stops on a man sitting on a tomb stone . The Cameras get closer and closer until there a few feet away and stop. The man gets off the tomb stone and walks even closer to the camera. He roles his eyes to the back of his head and begins to speak. Spawn''' Welcome, Welcome to hell, Welcome to my world, Welcome to the Place of lost souls. Spawn starts to laugh. Finally he stops and gets a serious look on his face. Spawn''' Another Place. Another Place to steal some souls. Another Place for hell raising. [/color] Spawn licks his lips as if he tasted the souls. Spawn''' This Sunday’s soul will be from some guy named Bomber. He will fill the pain of hell. Feel the Pain from Spawn. Spawn scratches his chin as he thinks for a moment. Spawn''' Have you ever in your life came in counter with the real hell? I doubt it! So you have never felt the pain of hell Bomber. But that will soon change. Come Distortion. YOu will feel the pain. You will be getting a different view of Hell. You will finally learn the True meaning of hell. You will burn in hell. For a long time you have been a thorn in my side Bomber well not no more, This Sunday I will put you away once and for all, I have waited a long year plus counting, to do this with you, but I was patient and now you are mine to torture, man oh man I can’t wait for Thursday…..Bomber your end from FAW is finally coming. I just can’t wait because FAW do not need any more waste of space. Spawn: Spawn pauses for moment.] Spawn: Now I cant really say much now can I? I really don’t know you ha! Just kidding Bomber…so stay on alert, because it is the way I want you for my act. But to hell that doesn’t mean shit. See I hae nothing to worry about. Im not fighting alone. I have got hell on my side. And you two will fill the pain. Now before I go, Let me ask you one question. ARE YOU READY TO BURN IN HELL?¿? The scene fades to black.
