You're a What? Chris Storm Furiously Addictive Wrestling Roleplay #22 Date: 10/18/02 Location: Shinto Shrine [After something unexpected happens to us we tend to let our guard down. We think since we are still dealing with the first surprise, another one can not hit us. That is a dangerous flaw in humans. One that has shown to be very costly. Not long after the first bombshell hits, the other is already in the air getting ready to hit us. Granted it always doesn't happen that way. But then again, it's this uncertainity that makes like so interesting.] (You're a What?) . . . . The second of a three part story. . . . [Chris Storm may wish he had never woke up on October 13th. With the events that happens to him, most would agree with him. He had enjoyed his life. Dare I even say, loved his life. But now there was a new factor into his life. One he can not understand for the life of him. By some force he doesn't understand, a women has been thrusted into his land and he can not make heads or tails of her. Yet he knows that he can not just leave her to the animals in the woods. So he has taken her into his home and watches her. This trying to make up his mind about her.] [Chris Storm is sitting on a chair looking at the door. It's his spare bedroom door. He's rubbing his chin trying to figure things into his head. Thoughts of the strange events that have happened recently to him run through his mind. How could he have missed her before? Where did she some from? Why was she in the woods near the large crater? What even caused the crater? After Chris had brought the women into his house to let her rest, he had gone back to the crater to see what had caused it yet he found nothing. Not that he could search too well as it is. The crater had filled up with water from the lake. He searched around the site and found nothing that could have caused the impact. Puzzeled, he had returned to the house. He had grabbed a chair and placed it in front of the guest bedroom door and he watched it. Knowing full well that she was laying inside the room sleeping. His train of thought returned to the moment when she had tackled him and pinned him to the ground. Chris thought for sure the women had gone mad and was going to attack him. But what happened after it took him completely by surprise. He touched his lips. Why had she kissed him? Was she thanking him for saving her or was she simply confused? The only way he would find out was to act her himself. And he had full plans to grill her with questions as soon as she had the strenght to answer them. Chris has been thinking about the events so much that he didn't notice the doorknob was turning. The door swings open and nearly hits Chris in the head. He jumps back surprised. There the women stands, in Chris' robe. Chris could already feel the blood running from his nose. There in front of him, the women stood in his robe, but she hadn't closed it. Thus all of her 'assets' where exposed. Chris closes his eyes and backed against the wall. Not one of his best ideas. The women slowly made her way towards him. She stood directly in front of him, merely inches away. She had a devilish smile on her face to where Chris' was masked with worry.] Women: Open your eyes. I won't bite. [her words hung in the air.] Unless you want me to. [Chris' voice got caught in his throat. He knew he had to get himself out of his current state. He moved off the wall and got away from her. She slowly turned toward him, almost mockingly.] Chris Storm: Look I have a girlfriend. I really care about her too. [The women strolled up next to Chris. Standing at his side, facing opposite directions.] Women: And where is she? [Chris stepped back shocked.] Chris: Well she lives out of state. But we work together and see each other all the time. I'm just on vacation for the week while she has to tour with the company. Women: So you are all alone for this week? [The question was filled with lust. You could almost smell the scented candles and feel the heat. Chris starts to sweat. He looks around.] Chris: Look I need to be ready for my match against Apocalypse. He's a very serious wrestler for FAW. I wrestle for WIW:NG. That means this is a once in a life time deal! I need to beat him and I need to train to beat him. I don't have time to be dealing with you. Do you understand what I mean? [The women lets out a soft giggle. Chris looks at her confused.] Women: You know you saved my life right? I think I should thank you for it. [Now Chris isn't the smartest of guys at time but even he instantly knew what she meant. As soon as she took one step towards him, Chris took off full speed out the door and into the woods. He didn't even bother too look behind him. He just ran as fast as his feet could carry him.] Chris Storm: Ah damnit! What have I gotten myself into now? I'm suppose to be training on how to beat Apocalypse. Well I should look on the bright side. If I get in over my head I can run my ass away from him really fast. I should be able to. . . [Chris turns to look behind him to see if she was even following him but before he could finish the sentence he ran into something. A tree? No it was too soft to be a tree. And firm?!? Chris turns around to see what he ran into.] Chris Storm: What the. . .?!? [Chris has just ran full head into the women's breast. His nose was sticking between the two mighty mountains. Chris had already developed another nose bleed.] Chris Storm: How in the world did you beat me? I was running as fast as I could! You don't even look like you broke a sweat! Women: Oh, if I had known you was into my breast I would have let you stick your face in them long ago. Chris Storm: I'm so sorry! I didn't mean to. It was an accident. I swear! [Chris bows to her to say he's sorry but when he bends over she shoves her breast in his face again. Chris' eyes shoot open. He jumps back surprised. The women laughs at him.] Chris Storm: Would you stop that! I don't even know who you are, where you come from, or anything else for that much! [The women stands upright and proud. She flicks a lock of her hair out of her face and looks at Chris with a smug look.] Women: I'm Minagi! Universally known Space Pirate! Chris Storm: Space pirate? [For the first time in a long time, Chris burst out into laughter. The women looks at him annoyed. The more Chris laughs the madder she gets. Her face turns a deep red as the vains on her forehead start to stick out.] Minagi: What the hell is so funny? Chris Storm: I'm sorry but you are a space pirate? Minagi: Yes! What you don't think a women can be a space pirate? Chris Storm: I don't even know what a space pirate is. I mean do you have a little boat and sail around the seas or something? Minagi: I'll have you know I have a spaceship! Well, had a spaceship. It exploded when I sort of crashd here. Chris Storm: Spaceship! But I thought all you martians were green and had oversized heads. Minagi: It's true! [Minagi can feel her rage building up. Her fist clunch up. She turns to her side and punches a tree. Chris' stomach sinks into his stomach. His heart rate just dropped quickly. This women claiming to be a space pirate just punches straight through a tree! Chris can not believe his eyes. He rubs his eyes and blinks to be sure it really happened.] Minagi: So now do you believe me? [Chris doesn't know what to say. He just shakes his head up and down. Minagi smiles. She blows her hair out of her face and walks over to Chris. She puts her arm around him and runs her finger on his chest. Chris backs away from her. He looks around nervously.] Chris Storm: I don't understand. Space pirate? You mean you are. .are. .an alien? [Minagi stands proud.] Minagi: What? You've never seen an alien before? I guess that makes me the cutest one you've ever seen! [thinks about what she just said.] Oh you think I'm cute! [She runs to Chris and gives him a crushing bearhug. Not even Apocalypse could have a grip like this. Hell Ivan Stanislav couldn't have a tighter grip. Chris can nearly feel his ribs start to break. Yet she has no clue.] Chris Storm: I uh. .never said that. .uh I can't breath! [She notices she's crushing him and loosens her grip. She doesn't let go though. She leans her head against his chest.] Minagi: Can I ask you something? Chris Storm: Uh. .what? Minagi: Ever wanted to be a prisoner of a space pirate and become her boy toy? [Minagi raises her head and looks into his eyes. She has a smile on her face that shows she's actually serious. Chris looks around nervously. He gets away from Minagi. Chris' watch goes off. He looks at it and suddenly becomes very alarmed.] Chris Storm: Oh my goodness! I need to go to town and get to the gym soon. I completely forgot all about it! Minagi: Oh can I come. I love going into town. [A picture of the local town in flames flashing in Chris' mind.] Chris Storm: NO! I uh..need to train for my match against Apocalypse so I can't be distracted by anything. This match is extremely important to me. So important that I pulled other dates I was going to work. In fact I'm putting up $500,000 with Apocalypse to make this match happen. If I lose I will be hurt in the pocket book badly. So why don't you go take yourself a dip in the bath house or something? Or go for a walk. [Minagi thinks about it for a second. A plan develops in her mind. She slowly walks over to Chris and stands next to him.] Minagi: I'll make you a deal. You take a bath with me and I won't bother you when you go into town. I promise. Chris Storm: I really don't think. . . Minagi: You're right. Let's not think! [With that said, Minagi grabs Chris tightly around his waist. Soon the air around him becomes thin. His body feels like it is being streched everywheres at once. He tries to say something to her but before he knows it he's standing in the bath house. Chris looks around confused. Minagi starts to take off the robe and Chris turns his head and looks the other way. Minagi laughs as she walks behind Chris. She press her chest against his back, causing Chris to jump into the air. She laughs as she slowly steps into the water. She splashes some water at Chris.] Minagi: Come on in. The water is great. And plus it's so unfair that you've seen me naked and I've never got to see you naked. I bet you are cute! [Minagi flashes Chris a giant smile. Chris hides behind a changing wall. He slips into his towel and walks to the other bath. He slips into the water. But as of right now no one seems me naked. [Chris has to laugh as he thinks back to his collection of porno movies he stared in. Or the adult magazines he's covered. Chris just hops that space pirates don't know about the internet.] Minagi: Why are you over there Chris? It's so lonely over here. Chris Storm: Because I need to worry about Apocalypse and not some women trying to make me cheat on my girlfriend. I mean you maybe a space pirate but I'm a wrestler. I need to wrestle and win or I don't have a job. Do you understand this? Me and Apocalypse is a serious fight that is being hyped all over. Minagi: Wrestling is important to you isn't it? [Minagi leans against the wall and looks over into the guy's bath. Chris is leaning against the wall himself, but he's looking out away from her. Chris has a far off look in his eyes as he starting thinking about his career and what it's meant to him.] Chris Storm: I've been wrestling for a little over two years now. My mentor got me into the wrestling business. Man, I've always wanted to be a wrestler. Not just any wrestler though. I wanted to be famous. One of the best, Now inside my little fish bowl, the WIW:NG I'm the biggest fish. I hold the World title and I'm acclaimed as their best star. But outside my fish bowl in the larger pond , I'm just a small fry trying to be huge. But. .but if I can beat Apocalypse, then for a moment I can feel like a big star. And I can walk with pride after that. I can go back to my league knowing I defended it's honor and my own. I mean this match with Apocalypse is bigger then the money. Bigger then FAW or WIW:NG. It's bigger then either of our egos. This match is for all the bragging rights. The lowly cable company vs the big time network league. Image how FAW would feel knowing I beat their guy. How the DCV would feel to know I beat one of their guys. It would just be awesome. Something I can really be proud of. Minagi: Phst, you guys do the stupidest stuff because of pride. Chris Storm: Oh and what about you? You're the one chasing me around, making passes at me. I'm happy with my girlfriend and I'm happy with the way my life was going. Minagi: I know you like it. It helps your ego. Besides, if I was serious I would have already had you. [Minagi disappears and soon reappears in front of Chris, naked still. He turns around to grab his boxers but spots Minagi standing in front of them waving at them. She looks down.] Minagi: Owww! I beat your girlfriend is happy! Looks like he is too. [Minagi gives Chris a big smile as he just hangs his head in embrassment. He turns around and puts his towel back on and walks off. Minagi starts laughing. Chris just walks out of the bath house, hoping to forget this day even happened.] [As his match with Apocalypse comes closer, everyone is on the edge of their seats waiting. Will the match live up to the hype? Can to the preform at the level they want? Who will win? Only time can answer these questions and it isn't giving any hints. Both men try hard to make it to the gym and train but life seems to be wanting them to hold back and prevent them. Is this a sign of things to come? October holds all the answers. Get ready for Distortion. Live on FAW!]
