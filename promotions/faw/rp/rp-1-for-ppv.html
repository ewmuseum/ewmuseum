RP 1 for PPV Cyrus Skar Furiously Addictive Wrestling Roleplay #114 Date: 20.12.2002 Location: Salford, Manchester, UK OOC: This roleplay may have something you have not encountered before in it, where there are *’s in-between is what Cyrus Skar is imagining is happening. It is not real only in Cyrus Skar’s head. That is to aid any confusion, thank you. ::Winter time in England is looked upon with different attitudes. Some love winter, no flies or insects bothering you, Christmas to look forward to, the promise of a New Year. Others however, despise the winter; they despise Christmas and live a life of solitude never talking to anybody they don’t have to. They moan about the weather when they do speak, these people are the rejects of society. The outcasts from the society that is thought to be ‘acceptable’ these people have to make their lives exciting. They don’t have friends, they don’t have relationships. They just have themselves.:: ::Cyrus Skar slowly walks up his frozen drive way to his front door. The rotting wooden hinges give way as Cyrus pushes the door open. The door falls to the floor causing rats to scatter from the crash zone. Cyrus lifts the rotten door up with ease and places it in the newly created gap. Now it is no means of protection from anybody, but then again does Cyrus Skar need protection from anybody?:: ::The camera is now in the stinking hell hole that is known as Cyrus Skar’s home. Cyrus slowly trudges through the sewage ridden floor to get to a set of rotten drawers. He rams open the top one to reveal a piece of paper wrapped in a plastic bag. He slowly unwraps the bag from the piece of paper; surprisingly the paper isn’t covered in mould or anything else. Cyrus reads the writing on the paper and smiles. He drops the drawer and bag onto the floor creating more rubbish. He walks out of his home and down his frozen drive. He looks back one last time as if to say goodbye and calls a taxi. He gets in and the cameraman follows him.:: Cyrus: It’s been a long time in coming but I finally came back. I said I would when I left the first time, when I left my precious US Title here. FAW changed ownership and my title went, now the title I wish to capture first is the Continental Title. But that is not what I wish to talk about right now, there is one man on my mind at this moment in time and he is The Omega Fro. Now some may say I had no agenda for beating this man up as he conducted his interview. I had my own agenda. I needed to come back into this federation with a bang and that was exactly what I did. Who, when and where did matter. It had to be somebody reasonably known, no puny little punk at the bottom of the roster, somebody with at least an ounce of credibility to their name otherwise I would have started further down the ladder than where I was when I was last here. I had to do it before the last PPV of the year. To get myself a nice match at the PPV and to show the doubters who haven’t seen my previous work just how good I really am. Now we’ve heard a lot about who I’m going to face and why I’ve put myself in this situation but I haven’t talked much about me. Why? Maybe I find it difficult; maybe it’s the fact of what I have to explain. ::The taxi stops outside a rather splendid looking hotel. Cyrus thanks the taxi driver and pays him with a large wad of cash. The taxi driver says thanks as Cyrus tells him he can keep the change and leaves the chariot he arrived in. The taxi drives off as Cyrus steps up to his home for the next few hours. He slowly walks up the steps and into the reception area of the hotel. Cyrus spots the rather beautiful young receptionist sat behind the desk. He walks over to the desk and leans one of his rather abnormal sized arms on the desk. He slowly brushes his long hair out of his face to reveal his piercing blue eyes. Cyrus slowly begins to speak to the gorgeous receptionist.:: Cyrus: Hello, I’m Cyrus Skar, I’ve got a room booked for this afternoon. Receptionist: I’ll just check for you which room you’re in. ::The receptionist disappears into the ‘Staff Only’ area of the reception as Cyrus takes a seat. He picks up a leaflet on local training facilities. He quickly browses through it, seeing nothing of great interest Cyrus puts the leaflet back on the table in front of him. The receptionist appears again and walks from behind the reception over to Cyrus. She hands him his key and tells him which way his room is. Cyrus slowly rises out of his leather seat and walks over to the lift. He presses the button and waits for the lift to arrive. As he gets in a young, rather smartly dressed businessman rushes in as well.:: Young man: Thank you for holding the doors open for me, my boss is having a video conferencing meeting in a few minutes with me on my laptop up in my room. I don’t know what I’d do if I was late! Thanks again Mr.? Cyrus: Skar, Cyrus Skar. John: John Deed. Nice to meet you. ::As those few words leave John’s mouth the lift breaks down. With a jerk and a screech both men are trapped inside the lift. John panics and tries to pull the doors apart. He then presses the alarm button setting off the alarm. He then looks at Cyrus.:: John: You can help. Yes you can help me get out of here. Can’t be late John, can’t be late. Don’t want to lose your job do you John, no didn’t think so. Mr. Skar please help me open these doors. Cyrus: Both our combined efforts would be worth nothing, those doors are magnetic and can’t be opened with brute strength. John: No you can’t let me lose my job you’ve got to help me! ::Cyrus sighs and goes to pull at the doors. They don’t budge.:: Cyrus: I told you… John: No this can’t be happening to me, no, no, no! *Cyrus walks over to John and grabs him by his hair. He can hear his screams but he doesn’t flinch. He rams his head into the side of the lift and it shakes with a sickening thud. John’s forehead is busted open and he’s begging for Cyrus to stop, but Cyrus isn’t himself, he’s his other persona. He rams John’s head against the steel doors this time. They dint as John’s head bounces off again and again. John’s practically dead, he’s brain damaged, there’s no way he’ll ever be the same again…* John: Excuse me Mr. Skar this is no time to be day dreaming. ::Cyrus ignores John and opens the emergency exit in the roof of the lift. He climbs up lifting himself up buy his arms with ease. John begs for him to be taken to but Cyrus carries on ignoring him as he shuts the exit and the camera fades out.::
