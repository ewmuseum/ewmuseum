9th Wonder Of Foam — FAW Roleplay, August 2002

Furiously Addictive Wrestling Roleplay Board Roleplay Board | Back to Roleplay Board 9th Wonder Of Foam Entitled: I went nuts on a pencil dammit! Written: 6th August 2002 Location: Foamy Hills Mansion …. Foamy Hills Mansion – Tuesday – Evening …. (The 9th Wonder Of Foam sits at his desk, he is once again scribbling in crayon, in the lower levels, beneath sea-level is the training complex, Neo is power bombing a wide variety of woodland creatures. The Foamy One is ready for a big announcement concerning Miss Grieves and his career, she is still missing. But in the meantime he has other matters to tend with) “Hey Foamy what you up to?” (The Foamy One turns around standing there in a red boob-tube, pink thong, a long wig and morphine tubes sticking from his arms is Floyd Rotterdam) (Foamy flinches, what in Hades name is this man doing in his house dressed up like Tragedy Ann?) Foamy: Holy Jesus, mother of Carl! Did you have to jump up like that? Floyd: What you up to? Poetry? Foamy: Oh this nothing. (Foamy quickly covers up the notebook with a copy of Ralph Bashki’s “Lord of the Rings” He is unaware where Floyd caught a glimpse of his crayon edition of the Karma Sutra or not, but he decides there and then not to bother asking him) Floyd: Cigarette? Foamy: Ok. (Foamy lights up, he inhales the deadly smoke into his lungs, luckily he only has the odd cigar so his chances of catching Cancer are far lower than that sad portion of society who do smoke. Anyway he begins to cough violently) Foamy: Damn! (Foamy then attempts a smoke ring, but he coughs violently once more) Foamy: Ah f**k it! Floyd: So where is Lars? Foamy: He’s resting his tortured mind, he’s watching a “Naked Girls on Crutches” special on C-Spandex Adult this evening. Floyd: So how about that…interview? (Floyd picks up his fishnet stockings and re-adjusts his fag and morphine tubes) Foamy: Ok but only if you get out of that freaky get-up dude. Floyd: Hey, only trying something different. …. Several minutes later …. (Floyd has borrowed some of Dave the cameraman’s clothes, subsequently although he is off camera I think it is fair to warn you that now Dave is naked) Floyd: So 9th Wonder Of Foam tomorrow night you have a re-match against X-Rodd on Panic. Foamy: Indeed I do, a re-match for no apparent reason, I beat the bitch Sunday night. Trust the powers that be to get it wrong. If there is one person one this planet who deserves a rematch it is I. But not against X-Rodd I’ve already proven that he cannot keep up with me. I deserve a rematch against Xander, Skipster hops into the light like a frigging Kangaroo and gets a shot at the International Title? Hell no Xander. I’m going to make sure, by force if necessary that you remain International Champion so that soon, maybe not Thursday, maybe not Friday but soon I will get you prise that Championship from you. Floyd: And what about X-Rodd? (Foamy spots a pencil on his desk, he attacks it violently, he performs several moves including the FoamyBomb a DDT and a moonsault on the pencil) Foamy: Die Rodd die! (Foamy gets up and resumes) Foamy: See I can see people in other things too. I hear that Rodd beat up a trainee, and not even convincingly thinking it was the Foamy One. And if he can’t even get the job done properly against a trainee kid then why comeback and try again? And Etan, you don’t show that much belief in X-Rodd. “We’re not gonna stop doin’ this till we know you can take his ass out” You sound like a father whose son at the age of twelve still cannot handle his bike without stabilisers. If you want to keep having rematch after rematch I’d better pack some fire wood because the day he beats me they’ll be playing ice-hockey in hell. Floyd: What about Miss Grieves. (Foamy looking clearly distressed replies.) Foamy: She has nothing to do with this, move on. Floyd: But do the police have any leads? Do they have any suspects? (Foamy punches Floyd) Foamy: I told you to move on! X-Rodd I’ve proven it so many times it hurts, but if you wish I’ll show you once again why I am the teacher, and you are but the learner, you are but the learner Hogan! (Psycho Syd walks past tutting at Foamy use of his Wrestlemania 8 promo-line) Foamy: Xander, keep watching your back because while your watching your back I’ll be round the front stealing your title! Have a Foam Filled Day!
