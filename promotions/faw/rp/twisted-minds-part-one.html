Twisted Minds Part: One

Twisted Minds Part: One 'Golden Nine' Jeff Harris Furiously Addictive Wrestling Roleplay #244 Date: 31st January 2003 Location: Secret Volcano Layer, Hawaii :.:The cheers, the noise, the adrenaline, the pain, the victory… Everything a man could ever ask for in a wrestling career. Many people take it for granted because their top-notch new young stars with great potential, I however love every minute of it yet for some reason I do also despise it. After the match at Panic defeating 5 people, 5 new stars with the potential to make it big in FAW and being given a standing ovation after the match ended I have come to realise why the hell I am paired up with Jean Deas and Pete Harris, two of the most evil sons of bitches the world has ever known! Gah! What am I saying? It’s because I am an evil beast! My nine has the capacity of, of… What the hell is wrong with me? First I’m in love with a fat bitch named Jean Deas the next I have extreme hate for the beautiful woman. The confusion I feel has only just begun:.: .::The scene opens in Hawaii in the secret volcano layer. The Golden Nine is sat in the command centre on his own with his head in his hands obviously thinking about something important, as the scene fades round the room it’s quite obvious that everything is made out of 24 Karat gold. The only thing that isn’t gold is the view screen because let’s face it; having a golden computer screen would be pretty stupid indeed. On the main view screen located at the front of the command centre is playing the end of the hardcore gauntlet match for the FAW Hardcore title in a continuous loop. Pete Harris (not related mind…) walks into the room, the bulging fat is almost visible to the naked eye and is extremely disgusting. Pete Harris looks at the troubled Jeff Harris with one eye bigger than the other::. “Golden Nine” Jeffrey Harrison: What the hell do you want? Pete Harris: You seem to be troubled. “Golden Nine” Jeffrey Harrison: Observant fucker aren’t you? Pete Harris: HAHA! I LOVE IT! “Golden Nine” Jeffrey Harrison: Well if you don’t mind I was thinking about my hardcore match where I beat five people then losing the Rumble last Sunday. Pete Harris: Your shoulder isn’t hurting is it? “Golden Nine” Jeffrey Harrison: Quite the opposite, it feels great and it just makes me wonder what I am going to do to this newbie Profusion on Sunday. He thinks he is evil enough to beat The Golden Nine! He isn’t evil enough; he is the diet Pepsi of evil, semi-evil, the little fish in the ocean of all-evil. I however am the hammerhead shark of all evil and I’m going to eat him alive. He can’t even speak with proper grammar because he called me “The Golden Nice” how is that an insult? Pete Harris: HAHA! I LOVE IT! “Golden Nine” Jeffrey Harrison: Oh piss off and please try to lose some weight. Pete Harris: HAHA! “Golden Nine” Jeffrey Harrison: Go… Away… Now! .::Pete Harris quickly runs out of the room at about 3 mph with the fat wobbling making an almost hypnotic motion, Jeff Harris turns away from him and slumps back in his chair seemingly mumbling to himself::. “Golden Nine” Jeffrey Harrison: First ever Continental champion? I don’t know who he beat for that but it’s not much of an accomplishment. It just means you are going to be the first person to lose the title, probably to me once I show in this non-title match how better the hardcore champion is than the continental champion will ever be. You probably haven’t been in the business for long and that is ok but to be cocky and say you will defeat someone who has been in this business since the age of 19 is quite retarded. I wasn’t impressive at all at When Worlds Collide, if you want to talk impressive try beating 7 extremely excellent wrestlers with an injured shoulder in The Warehouse and winning the world title. Try beating 5 people in FAW for the Hardcore champion, I don’t know how things are run in FAW now I have been out of the loop for quite a while but it better be better than when Neil Peters was running things. Hopefully now the titles actually mean something, something good. You are not a challenge for me, you have walked the walk and talked the talk many a time but so have I except I have done it more times than you have. Oh yeah… Have you been awarded the world title and had a show dedicated to you in FAW? No? I didn’t think so, but I have after I got injured, after I got screwed out of the world title. But you’re right it is Champion vs. Champion could possibly be a match for the ages but if you beat me you will not take any of my pride. Because quite frankly I didn’t have any to begin with, I’m not one of those people who think they should just win a match because they are evil, that is not what makes a champion, a champion is like me… Fearless, Tough to beat but has a sense of reality which I have. I admit you could very well beat me because you are young and fast at only 29 years of age but at the age of 35 injuries have slowed be down. That is my sense of reality, but someone that you don’t know is I am willing to literally kill my self just to win a match no matter who it is against. You believe if you beat me I will be just another stepping stone on your way to the top but the reality is if you beat me you will already be at the top, the top is where I myself belong not these jokes Rzarector, The Great One, The Omega Fro or even 9th Wonder of Foam himself. The top is where I belong and it is where I will go eventually and I just remembered whom you beat for the Continental title, it was The Ninja… I’ve seen him wrestle and believe me, he isn’t a challenge. After I kicked your scrawny little ass and prove who is the greater champion perhaps you will want another go and this time but that Continental title on the line. Otherwise you shan’t be bothering me ever again. .::Jeff Harris stops talking to himself and tries to concentrate his vision on the screen showing the crowd exploding into cheers after he wins the hardcore title. Jeff Harris adjusts the black tie and straightens his black tuxedo. He gets to his feet and begins to walk to the door leading to the corridor outside of the command centre::. :.:I am beginning to get these thoughts, these memories of me wearing some sort of white tuxedo and wearing sun glasses and being extremely popular with the women, nice women not fat whores like Jean Deas. Something must have happened to me 5 months ago and I need to remember what it is before I can truly concentrate on the match at hand. Profusion is my secondary target remembering my past is my first. I need to remember what happened five months ago… it’s a must:.:
