Breaking of the silence, an interview with the black priest

10/24/02
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Breaking of the silence, an interview with the black priest Rzarector Furiously Addictive Wrestling Roleplay #42 Date: 10/24/02 Location: In a mortuary (The screen opens to show pictures of dead bodies. Several frozen but thawing out for there autopsy's. The camera is zooming around the room as you see several utensils, bodies, and plenty of freezer storage bags. The camera then cuts to a commercial.) *We are proud to announce a new Glad ziplock bag. This bag is specifically designed for all your measuring needs, measuring 6 foot by 4 foot, it is perfect to fit all your bodies in, and then store them in the freezer. Thats right, all your dead bodies, and after 1 yr in the freezer, they are still fresh like the first day they were killed. And thats not all, included now is a special cutting knife for all those bodies. So after you kill them, you can cut them up, and then store them. That way they are ready for a oven cooking delight. Found in all your retail stores.* (The screen comes back to the camera still zooming around as it stops by a doorway. There is a man in a black hooded robe, Rzarector, standing wiht his back facing the camera in a cross like position. There he sits there praying. The camera zooms in to Rzarector as the other doors busts open. There is James Harris and staff, walking in and setting up there equipment. The camera comes back to Rzarector and zooms in close to hear him praying, he stops and starts breathing hard, a loud hiss breaks out of his mouth. There he turns around, you see his dark face under the robe, with his green eyes and white fangs glowing out. He hisses at the camera as he walks over and sits down in the chair. James Harris motions its go time,and the red light on the camera comes on.) James Harris: Ok faw fans, we're here today with the black priest himself, Rzarector. Apparently we are in a mortuary, just where we were requested to be at. Now I guess we ask some questions, but like always, this holy man tells us about mortal sins, and the consequences. Now Rzarector. (Rzarector flashes his white fangs as the camera zooms to him.) Rzarector: Well Mr. Harris, once again we meet. Yet I have a feeling we will be meeting again. You broke your confessions to me last week. I enjoyed that, me and God releived you of your sins. You told me, the priest, and yet God heard you. God healed you, and I sent the message to him. James, do you feel better? James Harris: I sure do. It's finally a releif to be able to tell someone all of my wrong doings, and finally, I feel at a good state with God. Rzarector: Thats good, people just don't understand a rebirth. I once thought it was non-sense, now it is complete sense to me. Through God's eyes the world is mine. Yet there are still some opposers. James Harris: Who? Rzarector: My upcoming partner, 9th Wonder of Foam. I've seen him for along time now, I've seen him fight, I've seen how he acts. He is a complete goofball, that is what he appears to everyone, but evil is very deceiving, and that is what 9th Wonder of Foam is, Deceiving. Yet he goes by Foamy right? He is Evil, the world will see this Distortion.....the world will see. James Harris: What does that mean? Rzarector: James, sins are deadly, and Foamy has broken a deadly sin. James Harris: (sighs) OK, you are one nut short of a screw, anyways, what do you think about the FAW maybe closing doors. Rzarector: Well I don't care about money, and FAW doesn't pay me alot of it, so I couldnt buy it. And if I did, I would probably make there teleivision slot a church channel. Yet, I feel it will never shutdown, like me, I will never DIE! James Harris: You know Rzarector, I sense something different in you, does Renegade notice it to? Rzarector: You know Harris, everybody has there own perspective on me: satan, wannabe god, demon, good church going person, and a complete insane serial kilelr. You konw, I get put down and everything every single day, by people that don't know me. WEll I don't care anymore. I live by GOd, people odn't know me, so should they approach me? They sit there all happy with me, like you right now, then once I leave you'll sit around and talk shit about me. Know what I'm sayin? It's completally hipocritical, and liars go to hell. 3rd gate. James Harris: 3rd What? Anyways what about Renegade, is he in shape? Rzarector: Renegade has a strong soul, he will be ready for it, when is he not? Renegade and me are Tag Team Champions, undefeated, and I don't ever consider of being defeated by INvisible Jim or The Evil One Foamy. The black priest will use God's will. James Harris: Yes you will, Rzarector I see a good clean soul inside of all this exterior, I hope you do win. You see any odd things about this match? Rzarector: Odd like a graveyard, thinking of people that once knew people, now they all are in coma for the rest of eternity. 9th is a main eventer, me and Renegade are not....we earned these tag titles, by Great One hitting Spawn. But we still won them, so that means we are the champions. 9th has held the tag titles before, why should he want them again? Let us reign in the glory. 9th if you are listening, step down. Step down before God has his mercy on you. Beleive me, I will ressurrect you to the afterlife. James harris: Why did you bring me to this God awful place? Rzarector: God has nothing for you, only for me. And yes, I was going to get to this, you see these bodies? (James Harris and Rzarector looks around at the bodies.) Rzarector: They each signify dead souls, and since they just died they are all waiting in line for the book of life. But what will it spin them? Down or through the golden gates? Yet mortal sin will never repent, such crimes as murder, they will never be forgived. You see serial killers on death row, talking about how they love to kill, then a day before there execution they repent and get saved, thats stupid. Thats like me going and killing someone, then being like, God I love you. Even though I know I am going to die. Well each of these bodies are people like that, did they repent in time? Did they let God into them? I guarentee only 15 percent of these people will make it into those golden walls, and thats my crystal ball. Sins are deadly, Foamy will learn. (James harris looks on in disgust, as Rzarector gives a loud hiss and gets up and leaves the room. Right as he is exiting the door, he stops, and makes a cross figure.)
