Burning Rage (ppv3) Crimson Lord Furiously Addictive Wrestling Roleplay #46 Date: 25/10/02 Location: Unknown Scene: An electrical hum sounds, as a figure in the darkness, pushes the play button to a stereo. The camera, looking down at his hands, noticing the black boxing gloves, and then looking back up, almost seeing the sick smile on his lips. Everybody's looking for something. Crimson quickly turns around and looks at the camera, standing still the rest of the time. Some of them want to get used by you. Some of them want to abuse you. Crimson rolls his eyes, and looks straight ahead. Some of them want to be abused. Crimson, turns back around, as the song begins to skip, repeating the words abused, over and over again. Crimson head singing those words too, as he explodes again with rage, his words tossed in the air aimed at nothing. Crimson: The endless march of the machine... you don't care about how I feel, I don't feel it anymore. But I feel numb, as I sit back and embrace the every day suffering, the accounts of the harshness of reality. And I realize, I can't f*cking take it anymore, I want to destroy... They dare defy me, as they know nothing, not a damn word of truth, as they speak out of their asses. My fault... hardly, their own mistake, but mine for the taking. The scars, all of them covering his body, like a roadmap, and from his waist to his neck, now lays exposed for all of FAW to see, for the first time for anyone to see, as Crimson's never done this before. The camera, zooming in on his eyes, as they still remain ablaze, looking down at his chiseled physic, almost scary, as nobody's ever known what they were truly looking at, until now.
