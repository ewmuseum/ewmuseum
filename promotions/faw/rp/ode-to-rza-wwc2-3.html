Ode to Rza(WWC2)

24th January
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Ode to Rza(WWC2) Crimson Lord Furiously Addictive Wrestling Roleplay #182 Date: 24th January Location: A State of Mind 'Dark for fear of failure an inner gloom as wide as an eye and fermenting roiling hate death grip in my veins unveiling rancid petals flowering forth foul nectar the space between a blink and a tear ...death blooms.' (The camera fades in on an FAW camera crew walking down a long forbidding hallway. It's stone and asbestos lining, crumbling away like the sands of time. There are doors, steel doors...like locked cages lining the wall in certain place. What is this? A prison? Perhaps, a mental prison, but who knows. That will just be our guess. Laughing. A person in this place? What the hell? The camera man darts off to a row of doors, until he comes across one. The laughter grows louder, deeper, like a bellowing. That one particular door catches his eye however. It looks like it's been kicked in, or punched...blood. What in the hell is this? Why is there blood? The camera man looks through the bars on the top part of the door, darkness. But, slightly visible is a shadow, possibly a man. Who's to know, who's to know? Unless. The camera man opens up the door, and that ever so hideous laughter streaming out of the room. Still, darkness. The camera man slowly creeps into the room, towards what looks like a man staring out a barred window into the night, rocking back and forth. The laughing, growing ever so loud. Then the shadow stands, a huge frame, towering over the crew.) ...: You've come. Good, I've been waiting ever so long. Clearing my thoughts. Readying myself for a battle, a long looked forward to struggle. A step forward in the right direction. Preparation, this I doubted I needed, but it has become apparent that I must prepare to come out with a victory. Victory, is what I'm swearing upon, nothing less than just that. I have promised myself this victory. I have committed myself to this battle...and there is no turning back. There again, why would I want to turn back and run? I prefer to fight. And fight I will. No questions asked. Why run when I need this strong leap forward? I will prevail nonetheless. Just like I would any other time. I swear on it, just like I've sworn on many other things. But, tell me...is it difficult to dethrone an immortal? (This shadow walks into the moonlight that is shining into the room, and all we can still see is his outline. Long hair flowing behind him. The shadow turns and leans against the window.) ...: Long have I waited for this moment. Long have I dreamed to actually get the step up. I'm hearing the calling, the demand for another victory, another conquest. Conquest indeed. Conquest is something that I can truly call this. But, allow me to ask again. Is it difficult to dethrone an immortal? (Then the shadow turns to the camera and walks into the light on the camera. Those eyes, nobody can mistake those eyes. Cold, callous, piercing blue eyes. And, and that smile, wicked, devious. We know who this man is. It is now totally apparent. Crimson Lord. Clad in his usual drab, black as nightshade. Baggy black jncos, boots, and a black Nunez 'Darkest Days' t-shirt. The camera man pans up to his face.) Crimson: I think that this is a challenge though. The bringer of death and agony...vying against a supposed immortal. But, Rezerector...don't you think that you should get off your high horse for just a moment and take the time to realize that your a damned human. As am I. No immortality stands before us Rezerector, you should see that. You seem to have some semblance of intellect. So why can't you realize that immortality is not something that you cannot hope to achieve. Those who are immortal are those who do something for the good or bad of human-kind. Like write a book, be like Hitler...or drop an atom bomb on another country. Simple as that Rezerector. Your far from being an 'immortal', your just a measly human being, therefore you can be toppled. You can be defeated, therefore...your going to be defeated...and that's that, nothing you can do about it except fight it as hard as you can. But, you see, no matter how you fight...no matter how hard and fast you run...you'll only die tired. (Crimson walks back over to the window and looks out at the night sky.) Crimson: I wish, for your sake, that you'd get off that high horse for yours Raza. It may help you in your up hill struggle against this monster. It just may help you stand a chance. Your going to have a shock dealt out to you Sunday. Shock, me winning, your first loss in quite awhile, your Chance at the title...going to the far better man. The worthy opponent. But, you can't seem to come to terms with it. You just can't seem to believe that I'm worthy. How can't you. Look at the track record. I hold victories over three of FAW's World Champions of past and present. Bomber, I beat him from pillar to post back in the xcw when he tried to invade it. Tony Vigilante, this company's last year WWC champion. And, last but not least...JLC, to bad it wasn't for his World Title back then... I hope that you can go toe to toe with the One Man Army... (Crimson lowers his head, and stares at the dusty, dirt encrusted floor.) Crimson: I...I truly am the way Rezerector, I hope your prepared with your round of salvation. But, if you haven't noticed Rezerector. I'm not a god fearing man, far from it. Nor am I an immortal fearing man. But as I stated...your not a fucking immortal. On with it though. I'm not a god fearing man. I like to think of god, as well...a teacher. One I can learn from...not some savior here for my better benefit. I learn, I'm not some old dog you can't teach your tricks to. I can learn. And, believe me...I've learned something about you, just by watching you. Inside that overly sexual shell...your a scared, crying, little boy. You put yourself out to be a man of such intense prowess, but your far from that. You just that scared little child...crying in the corner, waiting for his mother to come and pick him up to comfort him. I can see through you Rezerector. I can way through that shell you put out in front of people. I know the truth Rezerector, I know...your secret. (He pauses again, and pulls his hair out of his face.) Crimson:...I swear to you Rezerector...I swear...that Sunday, I'm going to walk out with the guaranteed title shot, no if's, and's or but's. It's as simple as that. So comprehend it...it's the reality that I'm handing out to you. A little vision perhaps. Yes, vision. You see Rezerector...I ended Harris's long, long shot chance. If I could do that, something nobody could do...what in the hell makes you think that you could oust me? What in the hell makes you think that you or anyone else could even come close to defeating me? The nerve. Apparently you've got enough of it. You, the guy who bitched all night long about demons taking things away from you...and you got your way. Well, you've got it...come the fuck on. Step up to the plate...let's see if you got what it takes to even make me break a sweat. But, I'll let you know...for every bead of sweat that drops from my brow...that's a time I slice you. Make you bleed. Yea...bleed. Crimson: Rezerector, I do hope that you'll enjoy our little encounter on Sunday. I know I'll revel in the fact that I've been given the right to chop you limb from limb...make you suffer, and writhe in utter agony. I'll enjoy that much of it. Oh...you can bet I will. I'm coming for you...the reaper is on his way...it's only just a mear matter of time before I take you into my realm. Into my personal hell, and make you feel my anguish...suffer my suffering. As I stated, I'm going to enjoy every waking moment of this. I'm going to enjoy the suffering...the snapping of the sinew...bones, cracking...blood, bursting forth from your veins. Oh, yes...I will enjoy ripping you apart. Like a PitBull ripping apart a chew toy. Lisa threw you to me, hoping that he could use you against me...the One Man Army. Well, Lisa was wrong. Dead wrong. she has sent a boy to do a man's job. Rezerector...trust me...your no man. Just a little child, needing to go back to the sandbox. But, it's time that someone show you what being a man is all about. Maybe that's why Lisa sent your protesting ass to me. To, straighten you out. Hoping that you'd grow a set. It's time Rezerector. Time to grow up. Time to face the music. What music you say? That organ...chugging out it's beloved funeral dirge...it's calling your name. Rezerector. Rezerector. Rezerector. I'm just the messenger. Maybe not. Correcting myself. I'm the judge, jury, and executioner. And, Sunday. I'll be ready to raise that big axe, and slice your head off. Much like a little boy does to a garden snake. Or, like I do to rats and rabbits to feed our boa. Either way. Rezerector. It's time to go face to face with the One Man Army. Whether or not you can survive...is all up to me. Up to me... (Crimson frowns slightly, turns back to the window, and looks outside. A grin on his face. Crimson slowly turns his head to the camera.) Crimson:...Your fate...is up to me Rezerector. Your future...is in my hands Rezerector. Do you think that these hands of fate will move in your favor? Simply put Rza...fate a bitch...and I have her by the throat. Rezerector, Sunday, your oh so immanent doom will be giving to you by these cold blooded hands. Snap, crackle, pop...your bones snapping like twigs, your sinew being ripped from your bones. Rza. You aren't wanted, or needed here. Therefore, I'm going to send you packing...running off like a scalded dog. I'm going to do to you...exactly what I did to Xander, JLC...and all the others...destroy you. And, while you may not have a title...I will still take your dignity. For society took my humanity...this is a merely a quest to gain it all back. You must be the lamb sacrifice...you must be slaughtered for all the crimes committed against me. Pain...agony...suffering. Rezerector, you'll know them all to well. Yes. Yes you will. Crimson: Before you have time to react with the 'your a fake too' bullshit. I have to inform you that this...Crimson Lord...the person I portray is myself. Me, myself, and I. No cheesy gimmick, just pure, unadulterated attitude. Just thought I'd clear that up with you. (Crimson turns his head slightly back to the camera.) Crimson: Evil Demons huh? Well, let me enlighten you a tad. That won't help you one bit. No, not in the least. I'm not worried about coming away with that title of yours. Yeah, of yours, but...like life...that's going to be a temporary thing...your just going to have to live with the fact that I'm going to be the better man. And everyone of those words that you've spouted in days previous...I will make you choke on them...plain and simple Rezerector. I just hope that you can comprehend that, and not go insane with grief once the referee hands me my title, and your taken away on either a stretcher or in a straight jacket. Looking back at the ring....watching me hoist your beloved title above my head. Then you'll realize, you'll realize just why I've been boasting. Oh, you'll see soon enough my boy...and, I can guarantee that you won't be pleased. Crimson: Am I really a dumbass? I believe it is the other way around my friend. I can see through you...realize that. I can see why it is so important to you, it apparently is the whole entire world to you. Like you could give a fuck about nothing more than yourself and your own personal glory. That title is your lifeblood...it's what keeps you going. Hell, I could go out a limb and say that it's your own personal viagra of sorts. It keeps you from going off the deep end and going and slitting your wrists. I what helps you get hard so that you can fuck all those 'women' that have around you. Without it...I bet you couldn't get it up. Crimson: Now we come to my reasons for wanting that title. Why you ask? Oh, so now your the dumbass Rezerector. I want that title for my own personal glory, just to shove it down all you motherfuckers throats that I can do it. I'm title material, I deserve it. Face it Rezerector. I deserve it. Because, it's assholes like yourself that have attempted to hold me back my entire career of over twenty years. You've tried to hold this vicious monster back, and where did it get the lot of you? It got everyone like yourself fucked the hell up, and trampled all over. It got people like you destroyed, it got people like you thrown back into the mix with the rest of us...the citizens. The ones that go overlooked, because for some reason the classy assholes such as yourself, label us as subpart. I hated your type, and still do to this very day. Because of you, I got held back...even though I always proved that I was the better man...by fucking far. But, Sunday... Rezerector, I will show you once and for all why I'm the better man, why I deserve a lot more than you think. Why I am truly a one man army. (Crimson turns back around and walks towards the camera a little, and looks into it.) Crimson: Friday, Rezerector...you and I, were going to separate the boys from the men. Or, as Mushroom head would say...separate the users from the whores. In other words, were going to see who's the best of the best, and who deserves to die like the rest. Like I tried to make the point by quoting Darwin...survival of the fittest. Only the strong can really survive Rezerector, I know for a simplistic fact that I'm not weak, nor have I ever been, nor do I fucking plan to be weak. All I plan to do, is go out there Sunday...embarrass the hell out of you, toss you around a little, make the me the Number1 contender and get my shot at the World Champion. You probably wouldn't ever see this as truth. But rather falsities. So, Sunday, I will enlighten that feeble mind of yours. Rezerector, here's a little quote for you. And I think that this holds true to its own. You wont know peace 'till you've had suffering. Well, Rezerector...I'VE FUCKING SUFFERED. You haven't yet once tasted pain like I have. But, Sunday...for all your falsities that you tout...you will know the exact meaning of the word pain...I swear this to you. I will not fail...no I wont. (Crimson turns his back to the camera. And begins a slow guttural laugh, he balls his fists.) Crimson: Rezerector, prepare for a shock, prepare to get yours on Sunday. I know, you'll be pleasantly surprised...then again, who's to say. Maybe I'll let you survive...but not without prying that title out of your hands. I'll see you Sunday...and everything you live for...will be mine all mine...for I am not only you master, I'm not only your ruler...hell...I'm not only your god... (Crimson keeps his back turned, but outstretches his arms like the crucifix position.) Crimson:...I am your fucking messiah...the one man army...The True Prophet…Blessed Be… (Crimson laughs, and returns to his roost by the barred window, as the camera slowly fades out on him rocking back and forth on the floor.)
