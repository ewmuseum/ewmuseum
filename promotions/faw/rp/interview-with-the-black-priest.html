Interview with the Black Priest

Dec 06
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Interview with the Black Priest Rzarector Furiously Addictive Wrestling Roleplay #88 Date: Dec 06 Location: In Rzarector headquarters, church *The screen opens to the front of a church. James Harris is seen walking up to the door when the clock strikes 12. Twelve chimes are heard very loud and the wicked sound to them makes James curl in shock. James Harris then notions for the camera crew to follow him to the door. They proceed onto the steps as they are almost to the top when the door makes a squeeking sound. There it swings open to show the actual priest of the church. He greets them.* Priest: So, you must be the FAW camera crew, and ofcourse James Harris. You know I've been a huge fan of the FAW for a long time. I'm glad Rzarector signed with you. Now he can spread the blessings of God to everyone. That is so wonderful. James Harris: Well thankyou sir. We are very glad he signed with us to. Even though I haven't talked to him in a while, I still miss his red eyes, white fangs, and his black robe. You know they really bring out the best in people. Priest: Rzarector once wicked, God cursed him with those clothes til his words make several people good, he will remain like that. But one day you shall see the true Rzarector, not the wicked black angel of death he is now. James Harris: I thought he was the black priest? Priest: Well that he is, he just still resembles the Rzarector who spent decades under the devils wing. Now God has given him one more chance, and he is tired of burning. So Rzarector accepted this mission, to make the world pure and good again. This act by God is only prolonging armageddon. James Harris: I understand, so can you please tell me where I can find Rzarector at please? Priest: Yes right this way. *The priest leads the FAW camera crew into the Church. They walk through a black curtain into the main praying room. There you see several lit candles, covered by red cloth, illuminating the church to be red. The priest smiles as he is walking down the aisle where on the right and left is rows of booths for people to sit in. On each side of the booths is bookshelves full of every sort of bibles. James Harris stops to speak, the camera is now rolling.* James Harris: So, why is there several different versions of the bible in these bookshelves? Priest: Well we like to study different bibles to find out which one actually speaks God's word. We have changed several lifes with these. They are very fascinating, we do not have a set following in Christianity. We are not baptist, jehovahs, or Catholics. We just speak all of there words and let people decide which one they want to beleive. It usually works out the best. Have a look.... *James Harris starts going through a bookshelve with amazement as he see's all the different versions of them. Then he reaches one of the bibles that looks almost original.* James Harris: Why is this book so old? Priest: Well, it was handwritten by a priest ages ago. Before printers, so he had to write it by hand. Not the original bible, but just recopied. James Harris:Thats amazing, is Rzarector here yet? Priest: Yes right this way. *The priest leads them past the booths to the front of a church, where the red candles are, and there is a huge shrine of jesus on the cross. The Priest bows his head and prays as James Harris looks on.The priest then raises his head and continues to walk to a door to the left of the shrine. The door is black and has a red cross on it, when the Priest opened it it had a spiral going downstairs. There James Harris look scared as he began walking down it. It kept spiraling as it appeared they walked atleast a smile. They finally got to the bottom where it was pitch black, except for a glowing cross on the wall.* James Harris: Is this where Rzarector is at? Priest: Yes........yes it is. James Harris: I dont see him. Priest: I do............look to your left. *James harris looks to his left as he see's blue eyes glowing in the dark, and below them white fangs are also sparkling from the glowing cross on the wall.* James Harris: I don't get it priest, when did his eyes turn blue? They were green, then red, and now they'r eblue. I don't understand. Priest: Well they were green when he had neutral control over his soul, he was as much wicked as he was good. Then they turned red when he was completely wicked in the Dark Carnival Evolution. Now they're blue since he is sincere with his heart right now. He has done no wrong, but once he does a wrong, his eyes turn back to red. Then they will go green when we becomes neutral again. So if the blue leaves and green appears, he is getting angry. If they turn red, he has committed a sin, understand? James Harris: Yes I do. Now can I interview him? *The priest smiles as he begins his walk back upstairs. Rzarector is heard hissing as James Harris jumps in shock. The two glowing blue spots start coming closer to Harris as he begins sweating. James Harris see's a chair under the cross and sits down. The blue lights stay where they are.* James Harris: Are you there Rzarector? *A hissing is heard.* James Harris: Do you mind if I interview you? Rzarector: No, not at all Mr. Harris. *James Harris gives a huge swallow as he pulls out a paper. He places it in front of the lighted cross and begins to read from it.* James Harris: So Rzarector, why did you join the FAW? Rzarector: Why does anyone join wrestling? James Harris: I guess to become famous? Rzarector: No not at all, well most do, but not this Black Priest. I joined FAW just for the fact that I needed to spread a message to the world, and the only way to do it is to be on TV. Since I am talented I guess I knew I could wrestle.......for God gave me the ability. So now I am here, spreading the message. James Harris: Yeah, the priest told me about the message, and your eyes changing colors. Rzarector: Did he? James Harris: Yes he did. It was very interesting, hard to beleive, but I guess it is true. Rzarector: Very true. *Looking at the paper again.* James Harris: So where did you grow up at? Rzarector: I grew up in hell, now I stay in the church, yet I only go out at night to the airplane, if I have to wrestle the next day. James Harris: What do you get from being in the church? Rzarector: Satisfaction, I get the good feeling from God watching my every movement. And I always try to do good. James Harris: Interesting, now Rzarector, you have a huge fan club. Is there any name that you would like to call your fans? Rzarector: Yes there is, the Black Angels........for thats what they are Black Angels of Death, and I am leading them to God, and he will take them from there. James Harris: That is what you were before you became the Black Priest. Rzarector: How did you know? James Harris: The priest told me. He also told me that you were under the devils wing for a while. Rzarector: For a while? Yes that is very true, I was for a very long time. But I got everything straight now> I don't care for the devil, i'm glad he is burning. James Harris: Are you allowed to say that? Rzarector: Yes the man upstairs cares, but only somewhat. James Harris: Well, how did you feel about your FAW Tag Team Titles being stripped from you? Rzarector: Like I told you before, I don't care about titles. It just gets me more popular so I can spread the word of God better. That is all. James Harris: I see. *James Harris is about to read out another question when the blue lights start moving. James Harris jumps out of his chair as Rzarector hisses. The lights start blinking as Rzarector is closing his eyelids. The fangs then flash as James Harris yells out 'PLEASE STOP!' Rzarector hisses and stops.* Rzarector: Why do I scare you? Is it how I look? Because you know I can't help that, I never will be able to. Until I can spread my word to enough to stop the world from armegeddon, then I will die and be in heaven, and the world will be safe, until I am needed again. James Harris: Rzarector, I have to say this, for your scary look, you are a very nice guy. Well most of the time, unless your eyes are green or red. Then you are very evil. How did you get them blue? Rzarector: I repented the day FAW shut down. Then I stayed in this church praying every single minute of every day, all I ate was bread. Then after about a weeks time with talking to God, my eyes turned blue. It is a sign that God loves me. James harris: If you ever went to a psychologist they would say that you were crazy. Did you know that? Rzarector: Probably, and they would banish me into a white padded cell. Then after they died, God would banish them to a lake of fire. James Harris; Do you think I will burn in a lake of fire? Rzarector: Have you ever been saved? James Harris: Yes. Rzarector: Have you ever repented? James Harris: Yes. Rzarector: When was the last time? James Harris: When I was 18. Rzarector: So your soul is dirty? God will allow you into the golden gates, but you should repent and pray everyday. God loves talking to everyone, he loves everyone. It is a fact. James harris: Thanks. Rzarector: What other questions do you have? James Harris: Whats the hurry? Rzarector: Almost time to pray for my match this Sunday. James Harris: OK, I'll hurry up then, Your upcoming opponents. What do you think of Lazlo Hawk? Rzarector: Loads and loads of talent. I especially like how he can make a show, Rock n' Roll. Yet there is something I see that God would not like. Greed, he wants to become the world heavyweight champion. He is very unsettle in his temper. Punching out a camera? That is just crazy, please tell me why anyone would do something so hideous? James Harris: I don't know, so you think he will be a challenge? Rzarector: In the ring, yes. In me convincing him to move to the light, yes, he will be a very strong challenge. Now please, onto the next guy, time is running out. James Harris: Sabatash, remember 9th Wonder of Foam's allie? Rzarector: I remember..........now I have never seen him wrestle, or talk about anything. So my thoughts on him are purly none. For I cannot speak on something I don't know of. So what would it do to me if I lied about him? Everything, eyes back to red. Now please onto the next. James Harris: Officer Lawrence Mcgruff? He is a new guy to the FAW. So you really are up for new talents with everyone in this match. Considering the fact that you don't really know how any of them wrestle. Lazo Hawk, a hardcore luchadore, Officer Lawrence McGruff, a powerhouse brawler, and Sabatash, another powerhouse brawler. Then you got you a technician brawler. So how are you going to win this match? Rzarector: First off, lets start from the first question. Same as Sabatash, I don't know him.Never heard of him talk or wrestle. He is just a no show, he was at the last Panic. But he didn't arrive til it was to late. Then he just came out and started brawling. James harris: Is that all? Rzarector: Yes and now your last question. I am goign to technically take out the luchador, which would be his legs. Then after he is out of the ring, I am going to brawl with every last brawler til they are on the ground tired and out of energy. Then I will work on there legs, and there upper body........that way they won't be able to swing as hard if they are so sore. You think that is a good strategy? James Harris: Yes it is, a very good strategy. So what happened when everyone started brawling, what did you do? Rzarector: Well it was like, I came out to the ring when they called my group out. Then everyone started feuding, and someone punched me in the back of the head. I turned around flashing my fangs, and my eyes were pure green, then I hissed very loud. And they guy started laughing, I can't remember who it was. Maybe it was Shenshui, I don't know can't remember. Anyways, I kicked him and then uppercutted him. After that I got jumped and knocked out of the ring, from there the show ended, and I walked to the back. Then I left and came right back here to the church to pray, and turn my eyes back blue. James Harris: Amazing, anything else you have to say? Rzarector: Yes. *Rzarector hisses as he walks into the light. There you see a dark figure with blue eyes, white fangs, and covered in a black hooded robe. The light from the cross is illuminating on his body making him have a huge cross symbol on him. There he opens his mouth and hisses again as he starts to talk.* Rzarector: The world is evil, and a very unknown evil. I come here spreading the message. It is a good message, I shall not let any of you down. Please listen to me, for it has come to pass a dark hour for the world. The last hour, the last decade. Before my time is up, even if it is an eternity, I shall spread this message. I have to save the world. *Rzarector runs away to the back corner. James Harris looks into the camera.* James Harris: Well there you have it, an interview with the great one himself, Rzarector. *Rzarector is heard hissing as the camera zooms in on his blue shining eyes..........there the camera cuts.*
