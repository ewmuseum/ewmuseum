20th December 2002

Someplace, Sometime, Somewhere 9th Wonder Of Foam Furiously Addictive Wrestling Roleplay #111 Date: 20th December 2002 Location: Evill Scheme Room …. Someplace, Sometime, Somewhere …. She runs in the shadows, she laughs, she points, but most of all she smiles because she knows that he is nowhere near her. For now she is safe. Many have held her with pride and love but one man is yet to feel her touch. He has been given chance after chance but every time he gets near her he messes it up, he says the wrong things, and he touches her in the wrong way. And now his frustration has become his life, she is all he can think about, if he can’t have her no-one will. It has come to this, on Sunday Night she will offer herself to eight suitors and only one can have her hand in marriage. One man, the comedian among them has lost his smile; she torments his thoughts and tramples on his dreams. So now he comedian must eliminate the other men, he must show them no mercy, he must make their suffering swift yet scornful. And if she does not relinquish herself unto him he will do what they never thought possible, the comedian will become the devil. If she will not give herself to him he will destroy her and those who seek to gain her trust, he will stop at nothing to win her back, or end her life so that he may suffer no more, and that no other man can have her touch. She is no human; she is a prize, a symbol of all that is powerful in combat, she is a metallic compound attached to the hide of an animal yet she garners so much power, that to have her in your arms is to rule them all. …. Foamy’s Evil Underground Lair – Friday morning …. (The 9th Wonder Of Foam walks into the evil scheme room, Schmidt and Enrique are sleeping on top of the table sleeping off last night. They both appear to be naked and are sleeping under Enrique’s duffle jacket. Lars walks in with a pot of coffee) Lars: You look like hell. Foamy: Just bad dreams that are all, but they will go soon. (Foamy walks over to Schmidt and shouts in his ear) Foamy: EICH EIN BERLINER! (Schmidt wakes, he gets up wearing only a pair of red leather y-fronts, he sits on the table, and then Enrique wakes) Enrique: I am Enrique, fear the mole! Where am I? (Enrique gets off the table; he puts on his clothes and limps over to his chair. He wipes the crust from his eyes) Enrique: Ahhhh! Schmidt: Dath war irgendein guteth Gethchlecht! Foamy: What did he say? Enrique: He was talking about the furry mole on my head. Foamy: But the mole on your cheek ain’t furry. Enrique: Well he wasn’t talking about that head. Foamy: Oh, ahhhhh!!!! Look anyway Schmidt, my nemesis… (Foamy thinks) Foamy: Nemesi, Nemeses, what’s the plural of nemesis? Lars: Don’t know. Foamy: Well looks both Rzarector and Spawn talk of hell, I wish for Schmidt to come up with some kind of evil device that can let me into hell. Schmidt: Aber Hölle bethteht nicht! (They all nod) Foamy: Oh look there’s no point, because the fact is hell doesn’t exist, there is no god and no devil, otherwise he wouldn’t have lumped me with you lot. Ok any news to report before I go training? Lars: Well James Harris is due any moment. Enrique: I have a few ideas for global domination. Foamy: Shoot! Enrique: I have bundled them together in a neat presentation if you will. Foamy: Ok. …. 45 Minutes Later …. (James Harris, having arrive 43 minutes earlier sleeps with the rest of his crew in the corner of the evil scheme room) Foamy: Call me crazy Enrique but these plans all include you getting the biggest Album sales of the year and include you having copious amounts of sex with Anna Kournikova. Enrique: You asked for plans of world domination. Foamy: Yes plans that involve evil Foam World Order style domination of the wrestling industry not your intentions to dominate the music industry. Look get out of here both of you and comeback tomorrow with some actual plans. Ok Lars wakes James Harris. (Lars pokes James Harris with an electric cattle prod waking him up.) James: Ah, what? Lars: You can interview Foamy now. James: Ok. (James and his crew scuffle over to Foamy; James sits down and gets his pad out. Dave the cameraman turns on the camera and they are ready to roll) James: 9th Wonder Of Foam, it’s been a busy 24 hours Scott Costella has signed with XCW it seems, how does this affect you? Foamy: All this means is that there is now one less crack in the pavement for me to step over. James: Have XCW or any other federation contacted you? Foamy: No, and even if they did it would be pointless them trying. I lie awake at night because of one thing; I get nightmares that you can’t begin to imagine because of one thing, the one thing I want more than any other, including the ability to breathe is the FAW World Heavyweight Championship, something they could never offer. James: Some of your other opponents are yet to speak. But before we contemplate them I’d just like to get your thoughts on the new additions to the match now that you have had time to think. Foamy: Well aside from the other people in the dome there are six other men who could end up facing me but only two of them have that privilege. It could be Crimson Lord, HaHaKlown that Ninja dude, Hardcore Hoefel, or even my brother’s Neo or Sabatash. I think that this mysterious Ninja guy has picked the wrong night to unmask himself, whether he is a so-called legend or not matters little to me. Because he is just another man, another soul that wants her, but I will not let him touch her. I have defeated Hoefel before but that was the easy going Foamy, his defeat at my hands last time will be nothing compared to Sunday night. And both Lord and the Klown now know that I am more than a diet Jack Black, or Duce Bigalow. I can get under their skin with the smallest of touches, and then I will destroy them like a virus. And to my brothers, who I wish the best of luck, but I pity them if they enter the main event having become Tag Team Champions and expect me to help them, they will get one title and one title only. James: So now onto Lee Smith, what do you make of him? Foamy: Well he is a very courteous young man, which sadly will not help him Sunday night. When he gets angry at Xing does he show his anger by beating him down? No, he writes a stern letter and demands an apology. Well Lee Smith I’m afraid its time I showed you why you have to ask Santa Claus for an FAW Championship Belt instead of taking one yourself. Last week you talked of ratings sky-rocketing because of your arrival but the fact remains ratings do not equal you being the best, just ask Xander. You have to fight for your right to be the best, you remind me of the old Foamy, all smart ass and no thought. And this hotline 1-4000-KISS-MY-ASS you need it no longer my friend because come Sunday I’m going to be you so hard your head will be right next to your ass meaning that you can kiss it yourself. James: What about Renegade, another of the silent ones. Foamy: It’s good that they remain silence; it shows their yellow streak even more than their words could. Renegade is a mere 5”10 what can he do, bite my nipples off? Renegade is a great athlete he can fly like no other the problem is in that dome all of his instruments will be malfunctioning, and as he glides through the air he will noticed a 6”7 300lb man mountain directly in front of him, he will crash into it and burst into flames, that mountain? Me. James: The Great One is a man with whom you have a small background with, yet this Sunday you face him for the Championship. Foamy: I have defeated him once I can do it again. What he fails to see is that true Greatness come not from having the word “Great” in your name. It comes from the willingness to make great sacrifices, to beat the odds and stop at nothing to achieve your aims. My aim is to become FAW Champion. The Great One is happy as long as he still has fans to grab and shove up his ass. I will prove to him that I will stop at nothing and at no-one to be Champion, and at the same time I will prove that he wasn’t THAT Great after all. James: What about this name, the British Bomber, surely on of the franchise names of FAW. Foamy: How can he be described as a franchised when he is never sure which company he works for? He’s changes companies like Prince changes names. How can one man truly fight for something that he doesn’t even love? He cares about one thing: money. He is happy to fight where ever and whenever as long as the money is right. Bomber cares not about the FAW Championship, he cares about showing his face next to others such as myself, and that will show when he is kicked out liked the overweight, past the sell by date, loaf of bread he is. James: Ok now onto one of the two men who have spoken, first of all Rzarector. Foamy: The man who is out to take the skulls of Devils. Well Rzarector you gave me a right old chuckle. I seem to remember you saying something along those lines when Jim and I faced you and Renegade for the Tag Titles, I was “going to be sent to hell” etc. But the fact remains Rzarector that it’s the same guff that every other devil man peddles all around this country. You didn’t send me to hell that time you simply grazed my knees and here I stand once again, ready to fight. Why not start at the top? Why not take the skull of Lucifer himself? Well I tell you why not because you’re talking a load of hot hair. You talk to a God who lest we forget is a merciful God, I put it to you Rzarector that you are nothing more than a confused loony with big arms who escaped from the mental wing. And I took a look at those heroes who you named. Bret Hart – well all know what happened there. Hulk Hogan – a washed up chump still desperate for the fans attention. And Shawn Michaels, decreasing his legend with every step he takes and with every hair that falls from his already thinning thatch. Being able to make James Harris cry doesn’t not bother me, you believe you have another mission, skulls collecting, it is not to be FAW Champion, that is my mission and no-one shall get in my way. James: And finally Spawn. Foamy: This guy is amazing. He ignores everything I say and everything that I have done. You may change the lyrics once and a while Span but the song remains the same. You’re f**king this, you’re cock sucking that, it’s not scary to simply swear your way out a corner. Scared little children swear their mouth off to try and appear big; they ignore the sage words of others to appear all high and mighty. While I train everyday, every night, while I spend all of my thoughts on her: the Title. You spend your days watching basketball games to give the appearance that you do not need any more preparation. Or is that that you have already given up hope? And you are merely enjoying your last days as a professional wrestler? (Foamy coughs) Foamy: See Spawn every time we meet you says that you will finally end my career. And yet I stand up, wipe the sweat from my brow and stand taller and firmer than the last time. And every time I do so your threats get even more meaningless. But now there is an even greater difference between the Foamy of old and the Foamy anew. I now have a rage you cannot subdue, a fire burning that cannot be extinguished, a determination that your idle threats cannot put down. I will walk across fire, I will fly head on into the wind, there is not an ocean I cannot cross, and there is not a man alive that can knock me down for eternity. You’re right Spawn I’m not the Hell Master, but then I wouldn’t want to be. Just think, after this Sunday you can spend all your time getting kicked out of basketball matches, and not just when you should be training for the fight of your life. And now James you will go. Have a Foam Filled Day!
