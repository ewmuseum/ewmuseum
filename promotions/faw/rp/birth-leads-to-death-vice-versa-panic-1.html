Birth Leads to Death & Vice Versa (Panic 1)

Birth Leads to Death & Vice Versa (Panic 1) Colby Newsome Furiously Addictive Wrestling Roleplay #229 Date: 1-30-03 Location: Penthouse/Taxi/Hotel It were as if you were watching a home video and someone pressed the pause button without you knowing. Everything was still, frozen, time had seemingly stopped. The penthouse was spacious and well decorated. Cleanly and well managed the house was an expensive bachelor pad. A 65' big screen tv was pressed against the far wall. Two black wooden towers were to either side. The tower on the left held a large stero system and some pictures from early in Colby Newsome's career. The tower to the right held a wide assortment of DVD's and a DVD player. Centered in front of the big screen was a glass coffee table with several wrestling publications resting on it. On the near wall, in front of the glass table, was a large, black leather couch. Off to the right was a black loveseat and to the left was a black leather Laz-E-Boy. In the distance you can see where the kitchen is located, a hall way and several closed doors. On either side of the big screen t.v. are two sliding glass doors. The vertical blinds are pulled back exposing a balcony and a view of the Gulf. Sun beams bathe the floor with light and warms the room. Suddenly someone presses the play button and motion brings the room back to life. From the bottom of the camera's view a tall lanky man enters the room hurriedly. He's wearing navy blue dress slacks with a matching sport coat. A light blue dress shirt goes tieless with the top two buttons are unbuttoned. His hair was parted from the left side of his head towards the right, all except the steadily growing bald spot in the back. Quickly the man scans the room despartely searching for Colby Newsome. Man: Colby? Aiyo, Colby are you here? From the back bedroom Newsome heard the voice of his road agent, Daniel E. Corso. Usually individual promotions had road agents that would arrange all the traveling details, however, Newsome liked to have a personal agent thus nothing would go wrong. Corso wasn't exactly a looker, hell, he wasn't even a great dresser. However, Corso not only knew his job, but, he was extremely effeicient at it as well. No detail went unnoticed. Most of the time Newsome wasn't alerted of concerns or mishaps, instead Corso would take care of them. Newsome and Corso went way back, even before Newsome's days in XCWF. Newsome trusted and believe in Corso's abilities to complete the necessary tasks. He also felt a sense of loyalty from him. Again Newsome could heard Corso's scratchy voice.... Corso: Colby? God damn it! If you aren't hear I swear to god I'll kill you! Finally Newsome replies... Newsome: Take it easy Danny boy! I'm in the back bedroom.[Colby speaks loud enough for Corso to hear him.] As Corso heads towards the back bedroom he mumbles something under his breathe. Upon opening the door he spots Colby shoving several articles of clothing into luggage. Several bags are lying on the spacious king size bed, packed and ready to go. As Colby forcecs the lid shut... Corso: You ready to go yet? Newsome: Does it look like I'm ready to go? Corso: I never know. Newsome: True. I'm pretty sure. I've already double checked to make sure that I haven't forgotten anything of importance. How much longer until our flight? Corso: Not very long at all. If you don't hurry you'll miss it. I reserved two first class seats, one for the both of us, we'll pick them up at the airport. Newsome: You know what's great about your job? Corso: No, but why don't ya tell me? Newsome: You get to travel all over the country for free! Hell, you get paid to do it. Corso: Ah, small price to pay for a professional baby-sitter. Newsome: Ouch, low blow Danny-boy! Corso lets a small grin appear on his face, satisfied he got one over on Colby. The two would throw friendly insults at each other constantly. It was such a relationship that Corso enjoyed. The exited Colby Newsome's playboy bachelor pad. Corso could remember sevral crazy, out of hand parties held there. As Corso shot a glance over his shoulder he spotted Newsome locking the double doors and placing the keys in his pocket. Newsome: I really hope I didn't forget anything. Corso: Just make sure you've got you're big brass balls with you. I'll take care of anything else. Newsome: You been looking Corso? Corso: No, Colby it's all in the rumors. Personally, I don't care to verify them. Newsome: That's good because I don't like to cough. The two enjoy a brief laugh together. As the leave the large complex in which Newsome resides silence falls upon them. It always did. Individually they drifted off to their respective upcoming tasks. Corso took care of everything that needed to be taken care of while Newsome took care of anyone who stepped in the ring with him. [Hours have now passed. Newsome and Corso have gone to Tampa International caught a departing flight and left Tampa, Florida behind. The flight has been completed and Newsome and Corso have just retrieved their luggage and are busy making a path through the crowd of people exiting the airport.] Ahead of the 'co-workers', if you will, a large crowd of people pushed and shoved while individuals attempted to exit the airport. Upon standing back and observing keenly, you noticed that the group and a sway to it. As if it were doing the wave only using their torso instead standing while raising arms over head. The pushing and shoving never bothered Colby Newsome, or Daniel Corso for that matter. Newsome was larger then most, if not all, the people who do the pushing or recieve the shoving. No one dare attempt to budge Colby. Instead the crowd parted like the Red Sea and Colby venture through as if he were Moses, Corso following behind as the liberated Jews did. Newsome, luggage in hand, shoulders through a revolving door and out of the airport, stopping to inhale a deep breath of fresh air. Not noticeing that Newsome had halted Corso walks into the back of him, prompting Newsome to turn around slowly. Corso: Damn you Colby! What'd you stop for? Newsome: Damn you Danny-boy! Why don'tcha watch where the hell you're going! Corso: Walking behind you is like following an eclipse. When you stop, the shadow is still there. Newsome: You weren't watching me? Corso: Well no. Newsome: Why? Corso: Well, I was checking out the girl who was checking you out. Newsome: What girl would this be? Corso: Actually, she's a woman and she's over there. Corso nods his head in a general direction as Newsome searches for this unidentified female. He scans the crowd unsuccessfully. Newsome: Where's she at? Corso: Just disregard anything I've said. It doesn't matter because we don't have time to chat. We have to check-in the hotel a-s-a-p! Newsome: All work and no play! Corso: All work and more PAY! Corso emphasizes the last word of his sentence. Newsome just shakes his head. Suddenly Corso steps out in front of an on coming taxi. The yellow cab comes to a screeching halt inches from the skinny, pale knee caps of Corso. The driver is visibly and verbally upset as she spits several four letter words and waves her hands at Corso. Corso: Throw your luggage in, lets go! Newsome quickly loads his luggage in the trunk of the taxi. Then he climbs into the backseat. The taxi reeks of sweat and human odor. Newsome swiftly decides to assume all breathing through his mouth. Corso: Here's the schedule. Right now we check into the hotel. Tonight is an easy night. You have nothing scheduled, however, I figured we'd go out to eat. Newsome: Sounds good. Corso: Tomorrow you have several promotional efforts through out the day. One of which is a radio slot. Then Sunday night is FAW-PANIC! Newsome: Are you aware of what match I'm involved in? Corso: You better believe it. A fatal four way with Big Baby Jesus, Spider and Gabriel Hunter. Newsome: The last time I was in a fatal four way these girls they... Corso: Whoa, whoa. Strictly business. I don't know much about these three, however, I have research planned for this evening. Newsome: Sounds good to me. [A short time passes and the taxi pulls to a halt in front of a Radisson Hotel Resort. Corso hands the driver several bills, grabs some luggage and head towards the entrnace with Colby.] The two enter the building and are now in a large lobby. The finest tile along with marble are on the flooring as well as the walls. Corso and Newsome approach the counter and retrieve the keys to their rooms. Newsomes takes in his enviroment. Corso motions for him to follow. The two get on the elevator and make their way to the seventh floor. As the doors slide open the two step out into a long hall way. Reading the numbers on other doors they determine in which direction to head. The take a left and reach their suites three or four doors down. Newsome and Corso have adjourning rooms. The seperate ways and enter their respective suite. Upon entering Newsome checks his suite out. Placing his luggage on the bed he makes his way towards the mini-refrigerator. Inside he finds complementary choclates. With one hand he grabs a choclate, with the other he removes his shirt. Once in the oversized bathroom he fins a jacuzzi tub. As he turns he handles the faucet comes to life and begins to fill the tub. Once the tub is full Newsome turns on the jets and removes the rest of his clothing. The scene fades as Newsome makes himself comfortable to enjoy the warm jacuzzi.
