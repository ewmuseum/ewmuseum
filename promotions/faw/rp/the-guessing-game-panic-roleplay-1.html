The Guessing Game [Panic Roleplay 1] 'Legendary' Lee Smith Furiously Addictive Wrestling Roleplay #133 Date: 2nd January 2003 Location: Chinese Whispers Restaurant, Tampa, Florida Tick. Tock. Tick. Tock. Lee Smith was sitting outside a restaurant in Tampa, Florida staring at his watch. He wore a cheque buttoned shirt and black trousers. He sighed and became increasingly agitated as the seconds turned to minutes. Minutes then slowly turned to hours. Three Hours Later Nightfall had fallen….the blackness resembled the anger in Lee Smiths body. Lee looked around and peeked inside the restaurant, only stopping for a split second before turning to his watch once again. A waitress approached his table, and he slowly got up and left. His head was down, his body fatigued…he had had enough. Lee put on his trench coat and exited the seating area. He took to striding down the street in a peculiar fashion, with several heads turning along the way. His mobile vibrated in his pocket. He took it out and flicked off the lid. He then put his finger in his ear and spoke. “Where the hell are you?” Lee asked, with his eyes becoming bloodshot. “I should think so too, you’ve got a nerve you know” Smith said, as his face boiled over. The Living Legends face calmed down as a threat was issued over the phone. “Oh no, I’m sorry, you can’t back out now!” he shouted with urgency over the phone. He then hung up - whispering a ‘phew’ as he tried to flick the lid on his mobile. It became jammed as we saw the name of the person who he had just been speaking to…… B That was the last letter out of the persons initials. The lid closed as the words echo around the viewers minds….. _____________________B…. And then, like a game of hit TV show Blankety Blank Lee muttered the first name of the person he was speaking to. However, the sound became muffled and only the most attentive of people would have heard. Lee arrived back at his hotel shortly - and made his way up to the Presidential suite. He jumped on the bed and flicked his shoes off. He grabbed the remote control for the widescreen television and switched it on. The FAW Channel automatically came on and a preview for the next card. The presenters talked about the importance of Lees match to his opponent Rzarector and how it could make or break their careers. They dodged a touchy subject that only Lee knew was coming. A storm was brewing - like Thunder and Lightning from the sky. Except bigger and more lethal……this superhuman force was opponent to hit. Bullseye.
