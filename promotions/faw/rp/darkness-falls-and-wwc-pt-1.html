Darkness Falls and WWC PT 1 Colby Newsome Furiously Addictive Wrestling Roleplay #220 Date: 1-25-03 Location: Tampa, Florida His brown hair was short and well maintained, even though it was rather windy outside, not a single hair was out of place. His jaw was sterdy, cheekbones high and those deep brown eyes seemed capable of drilling a hole through someone, possibly an innocent victim. Not the victim of an unimaginable or violent crime, but, a victim of intrigue. He was taller than the average man. His shoulders were broad and his muscular frame bulged from underneath his navy blue sweater. Those characteristics along with his tan complexion caused him to stand out in a crowd. That's why Cindy noticed him from behind the plexiglass, him several customers back and her selling movie tickets as a part time job. Although she knew nothing of his morales or values she was convinced that the man she was to marry had to look similar to him. He was very attractive and noticably confident in himself. For a moment she thought about writing her number on his ticket but quickly dismissed the silly idea. After all she was only seventeen, his type probably didn't go for her. Colby Newsome stepped forward towards the plexiglass and spoke into the metal microphone... Newsome: Two for Darkness Falls please. Cindy: Yes sir. His voice was deep and caring. She fretted over how she replied. Did she give herself away? Could those eyes peer right through her? Two tickets, did he have a date? If so, he was standing alone in line. She fought the urge to tremble as she retreived the twenty dollar bill from the slot. While she was making change for the bill the two tickets printed up. She pulled them out and handed the gentleman his tickets, careful to touch his hand. They were big and smooth. As she watched him walk away her friend muttered something from behind her. Whatever it was, Cindy missed it. As Newsome walked towards the concession stand he thought about the young girl. She was pretty. Shoulder length blonde hair and hazel green eyes. Somehow, she seemed uneasy. Maybe she recognized Colby as a wrestling superstar from his days in the XCWF. Maybe she was new to the job. Whichever it was, her usual milky cheeks turned a pinkish rose color when Colby stepped up to her window. Colby stopped at the end of the shortist line. There was a group of teenage boys standing ahead of him, all wearing letterman jackets. Newsome silently hoped that no one would recognize him. It'd been a while since XCWF had been on the air and for some reason casual wrestling fans had short term memory. For that, Colby was thankful. The line moved slowly, but, Colby didn't mind this at all. He enjoyed the relaxing lifestyle that southern Florida offered. The sun, sand and you better believe it..the ladies. Wrestling wasn't just a job. To be good at it, you lead a fast paced, high intensity life. The road was cruel and has broken it's share of relationships, families and men down. The career was demanding but Colby reveled in it. That's where he was his best. On the road in front of 19,000 screaming fans. He truly loved it. As the line shortened Colby read the menu and decided on his order. Stepping to yet another counter Colby placed his order.... Newsome: A large popcorn and a regular pepsi please. Clerk: Right away sir. The clerk turned and repeated the order to a crew of five or six people. Turning back around he read the total to Newsome. Fishing the change from the movie tickets from his right pocket Colby paided the clerk. In just a few seconds Colby's order was handed to him and he was on his way to see Darkness Falls. With the bag of popcorn in his left hand, he held his drink in his right, the movie tickets shoved in between his index and middle fingers. Arriving at the end of the hallway a pimply faced kid with red hair asked for his ticket. Newsome handed him one of the tickets, he always bought two and kept one in perfect condition for his collection. The kid ripped it in half and handed the stub back to Newsome. In a squeeky voice said... Red Headed Kid: Third theatre on your left sir. Newsome merely nodded and went on his way. Entering the theatre he was greeted by a black lady, probably in her mid-thirties, greeting him and showing him where he could find a seat. Apparently the movie had sold out. Just after a preview ended the same lady addressed the enter audience and instructed them to fill all empty seats in order to make room for more patrons. Colby guessed that she was a manager, however, he demeanor was more of a business executive. In a different enviroment she would blend in easily. The movie started and the conversations died. In the darkness of the theatre Colby's mind drifted away from the movie and toward other subjects. One such subject was the dilema of turning down the Super Bowl for When Worlds Collide. This was a hard decision but a pay check must come before play time. Growing up in Tampa Newsome turned away from the Buccanneers sometime in the mid-80's. Colby hated losing and The Bucs were the epitome of just that. They were losers for life. In all actuality, Colby was a Raider fan. He loved the fans, too. They were wild and crazy. The kind of fan that would slit your throat if you dare talk bad about the Raiders. As for the Raiders, at the time, they were dirty but they won. A philosophy that Newsome would later incorporate with his professional wrestling career. TO BE CONTINUED(The Super Bowl my friends)
