Lethal Injection - The Dome Roleplay 5 - Special Guest Appearance from former FAW Star 'Legendary' Lee Smith Furiously Addictive Wrestling Roleplay #121 Date: 22nd December 2002 Location: FAW 'Evil Intentions' Arena The day had come….22nd December 2002.…and more to the point, Evil Intentions 2. The eight superstars were all completely focused….none more so than Lee Smith, who was intent on destructing the bad memories he had of this pay per view. He awoke with a startle earlier - and watched the sunrise from his hotel room. It was like a dream - however he never was good in separating dreams from nightmares. Yesterday he popped down to the arena and saw the wonderful structure he would compete in tonight. On his way back to the hotel it hit him, the key to success. Preparation. And so, on an unusually warm winters day…22nd December 2002, he once again drove down to the arena. The gym to be more precise. ---- “Calm down” the trainer said, with a raised eyebrow. Lee hadn’t been himself lately. At times he had been anxious, full of fear. And at others he had been his useful cocky, arrogant self. Lee took off his scarlet boxing gloves and past them to his trainer. Lee was wearing Adidas tracksuit bottoms and a black shirt which had “The Living Legend” scribbled on it, and the Furiously Addictive Wrestling emblem underneath. Lee glanced at the trainer, who was - among other things - wearing a suspicious face. “Go and have a sit down” the trainer exclaimed - and soon Lee was on his way to the bench. So far he had lifted some weights, done a bit of running…and most recently practised his punches on a punch bag. He stretched his muscles and looked around the gym at the other athletes. Many of them were FAW Development talent, looking for their big breakthrough soon. Lee had been fortunate, he hadn’t gone through that procedure. Once the staff had heard him knocking on the door that was it. Done and dusted. He felt a nudge on his shoulder and slowly turned. “Lee Smith in a gym. Now that’s a first” the man said, with a smirk on his face. He sat down and Lee took a few seconds to recognise him, for his physical appearance had altered. “Jeff!” Lee shouted, and embraced his old pal. This ‘Jeff’ was of course Jeff Harris - ex-FAW and theEdge sensation who had left FAW months ago. His long list of accolades amounts to months of memories. Sadly ‘AFT’ - as he liked to be called - had had his career cut short by none other than The British Bomber, one of Lees opponents later tonight. “So, how have you been?” Jeff asked as Lee smiled for the first time in what seemed like weeks but was probably more accurate to say hours. “Alright I suppose, preparing for the main event tonight” Lee answered as a curious look became engraved on his face. “Hang on…you’re retired….what are you doing here?” Lee asked. “I’m part of the FAW team nowadays, training up some young’uns so that they can be All Fk’n that. I’d like you to meet one of my best students - Jonathan Palmer” AFT replied. A young man of a tender age sat down next to AFT and reached out his hand to Lee. Lee acknowledged and they shook hands. “Nice to meet you” Palmer said with a disgruntled lack of interest. “Look forward to seeing you in FAW” Lee replied, as JP went to practice on the punch bag. “Seems a nice lad” Lee muttered to Jeff who nodded. “Yeah…needs to control his mood swings though. Anyway…you were saying, the main event tonight - whose in it?” Jeff enquired. “It’s for the FAW World title. The Great One, 9th Wonder of Foam….Rzarector…….um…..Renegade, Xander plus one other who we don’t find out until later tonight…and…..The British Bomber” Lee sighs and Jeff frowns. “Make sure you end his career - preferably by escorting him to intensive care” Jeff shouts - and for once - he isn’t joking. “Dead on Arrival I say…” Lee replies and both laugh ecstatically. “So, are you confident?” Jeff asks casually. “Quietly confident. Quietly confident.” Lee replies with a hint of uncertainty in his voice. “How unusual for you.” Jeff replies and pats Lee on the back. A man in a white cloak comes through the gym door and several wrestlers turn. He makes his way over to the duo and Jeff stands up. “Listen, we’ll catch up later” Jeff says - he seems to be aware of who the man is. “Lee Smith?” the man says in an English accent. “That’s me” Lee responds, as the man takes out an ID. “I’m part of the FAW Medical Team - we have been asked to take saliva and urine samples of all FAW Wrestlers taking part in the main event tonight to make sure that none of you are on anabolic steroids or other types of drugs” he says. Lee stands up and the man takes his hand. They exit the gym area and walk along the FAW corridors in silence. This was a warning. He walked into an open door and shivered as he saw needles all around him. In just a few hours time he would be injecting his name into the history books.
