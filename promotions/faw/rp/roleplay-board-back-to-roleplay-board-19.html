Cyrus Skar — FAW Roleplay, August 2002

August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Furiously Addictive Wrestling Roleplay Board Roleplay Board | Back to Roleplay Board Cyrus Skar Entitled: US Title (Distortion{17th} rp 1 ) Written: 7.8.2002 Location: A house....... *Cyrus Skar is seen pictured in a house. By any means it is not a spectacular house, but it is not a dump. Cyrus is sat on a brown leather chair. He is watching videos of the Highwayman wrestling. Skar switches them off to look at the camera. He grabs his US Title and places it over his shoulder.* Cyrus: So then, I'm the first ever US Champion. An acheivement? Yes, indeed it is. I am quite proud of holding this title, mainly because it's gold. The thing I am not to happy about is having it represent the US. I, as you all should know, am from good old England. I do not have a problem with representing the US, I'll do a good job, I'll see if I end up holding this belt till Bomber decides to bring a new one into the federation and retire this one. Well, I won yet another match, allbeit there were two winners I was still announced as winning the match. Apoc has some good ability, he will go far, maybe he deserves a shot at Bomber, maybe he doesn't deserve it. I feel as though I have acheieved a lot here in FAW, I can see myself going very far, I shall not be stopped by anybody. Now I have to defend my new title against a 300lb man, this shall be a reasonably easy defence. The Highwayman hasn't wrestled for a while in FAW, he will be rusty. I however have been wrestling non-stop in FAW, doing exceptionally in every match I have been booked for. I am just wondering my shot at Bomber will come, will it be before his the World Title defence against Vigilante? Or it will it be afterwards against Vigilante or Bomber, whoever wins? I am quite intrigued to find that I am still being booked against people I am better than. I have proved I can fight with the best of them, I should be being booked in the main events now. I have showed I am worthy of being in the main events. I could give Bomber a damn good run for his money if I got put into a match with him, I could show people what they didn't want to see, somebody actually worthy of fighting Bomber. I know the fans don't want to see their beloved President lose his title, but the fact is he's been protecting himself. He's only just let a real opponent face him, Tony Vigilante. When I do eventually get my shot at Bomber, just hope he's at his best, or a nasty upset may take place. *Cyrus decides he's going to go for a walk, he starts to walk out of his home and towards a near by street. He walks over towards crowds of people rushing about from A to B. Cyrus stops a little kid wearing a FAW t-shirt.* Cyrus: Hey kid, what do you think of your new US Champion? Kid: I think you shouldn't be the US Champion. Cyrus (Shocked): And why's that sunshine? Kid: Because you're English! Cyrus: Well I don't think that's very fair is it, I mean I hold this belt for your countries benefit, I give the US a good image and then I get told I shouldn't be holding the belt. I am shocked and appalled at the lack of manners you have young man. I think you should go home and get your parents to punish you right now! *Cyrus ushers the kid away from him as he walks over to a young man wearing a British Bomber t-shirt.* Cyrus: Hey you probably recoginse me off FAW, I'm Cyrus Skar. Would you like to be in my latest promo? Guy: Yeah sure dude. Cyrus: Ok, what's your name then? Guy: My name's Alex dude. Cyrus: So why do you support the British Bomber? Is it because he's the World Champ? Is it because he has power and you like men who are in control? Alex: Eh? Cyrus (Sighs): Why do you like Bomber? Alex: Oh right dude, you should have said that in the first place. *Cyrus shakes his head in dis-belief of how stupid this young man is.* Alex: Well, I like Bomber because...er...because...er...he's cool! Cyrus: So you don't like him for his wrestling ability? Alex: Wrestling ability? What you on about dude? Cyrus: I'm on about his talent, skill, ability. You know, how good he is in the ring? Alex: Well he isn't that good dude, he's just cool. Cyrus: Why is he cool? Alex: I dunno dude, he just is ok, can I go yet? Cyrus: Yes fine get out of here. *Alex carries on where he was going, Cyrus looks at the camera and sighs.* Cyrus: I'm very sorry about that obnoxious young man. A typical wrestling fan, knows nothing about who or what he follows. These stupid slobs are the people who make me dispise all fans. There may be the odd intelectual fan, but they're about 1 in every 100000 fans. It makes me realise why I don't respect the fans, I don't need to they all suck! Now Bomber, and other idiots in this company, it is up to you whether or not you incourage these fans. But I do not want to, I shall not participate in this mindless cult that is that of a wrestling fan. I know some high flyers think wrestling is all about the fans, but it's not. It's about me, all about me. The reason these people don't like me is because I'm good, they don't like it because I don't do it for them, I do it for myself. I do things for me, it's helped me be so successfull not worrying what others think. It is one of my many virtues. Now I fear I have taken a long enough break, I shall go now. Remember, beware of the Night Fall... *The cameraman trips and shows the ground for a spilt second, the camerman then tries to look for Cyrus Skar, but he has gone, the camera fades to black.*
