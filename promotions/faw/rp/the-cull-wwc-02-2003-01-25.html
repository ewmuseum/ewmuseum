The Cull (WWC 02) 'Legendary' Lee Smith Furiously Addictive Wrestling Roleplay #216 Date: 25th Jan 03 Location: 3600 feet above San Francisco.... We left you last time with Lee Smith making a quick exit after appearing on British Television show ‘Parkinson’. Lee made a number of comments on the show and promoted When Worlds Collide. Now, just hours later, he is on his way to the arena via private jet….. There were only 20 seats. Room for Lee and his associates. Lee Smith sat on the chair, sitting back and stretching his legs in preparation for the cull that would be When Worlds Collide. He was wearing more casual clothing now, some plain trousers and a green fleece which wrapped around his pale body. He wriggled his way to the window and looked down at the city of San Francisco below. Sky Scrapers galore he thought as the plain tilted to its side. It was a cold day down below, but yet the Golden Gate Bridge still shone from up above. The pride of the Golden state, California. The pride of America. Lee shivered as a wave of bitterness swept him back to the middle of the seating area. The resemblance clicked in an instant. That was Lee’s aim in life. Well, at least in his wrestling career. To be the pride of America – to be a compliment to the Smith name. To be known worldwide. Instead of being the pride of the Golden state, he would be the bearer of Gold. 2000 years ago, not to be precise of course, a baby had been born in the little town of Bethlehem. He would later be known as Jesus, the Messiah to all Christians. When he was born…in that stable in Bethlehem, three shepherds brought gifts. One but Frankincense….one brought myrrh and the other…Gold. If another Messiah was to be born to Lee’s religion, Christianity…..he would need a gift. And so, Lee’s determination increased. He needed Gold. Not only to be the pride of his country. Not only because of his religion. But for himself. Lee leaned back in his seat as one of his trusty employees swept down the aisle with food and drink. He folded down his table and took some pretzels and some Cola. The pretzels were thrown into his mouth and upon arrival melted almost romantically. The cola bottle was open in a second, that too evaporating down into his stomach. Once upon a time, a long time ago, Coca Cola was banned from being sold because it contained some drugs. The drugs were removed immediately. As Lee drank with the cold San Francisco Bay and Pier 39 below he hoped to god that perhaps those drugs had been restored. He could not hide from the fact that his chances were slim. However…..it would surely take a lot more than drugs to overcome this vital hurdle.
