Spawn — FAW Roleplay #37

captured 18 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
SpAwN--The Hell Master \~~~~~~~(opening scene)The scene opens up to a prison in Albany, New York. We see Spawn's Cobra parked out front. He gets out and walks towards the door. He has a serious expression on his face. The paper from his father is in his hand. He gets to the front desk where a fat guard is sitting. Spawn: I'm here to visit. Guard: Okay what inmate. Spawn: Angel A. Rivera Okay sign your name here and you may go inside. Spawn signs his name and walks inside the room. There is a steel gate in his way. A guard lifts the gate and Spawn walks through. He walks down a small hallway and enters a door on the right. He walks in to a room with no windows. There is a giant table with a slate of glass running over the middle of it. Just like on TV. The phones hang on the walls and Spawn walks over to it. He sits down in one of the chairs. He sighs and thinks to himself. Spawn: What the fuck does this asshole want. I don't even know why I came. Did I think that he changed? No. That bastard can't change. He will always be the sick abusive fuck that I hate so much. And nothing can change that. Not even 20 years in prison. This better be good. .A guard then walks in the door on the other side of the glass. He has Angel with him. Angel is dressed in a blaze orange jumpsuit. He now has a gray beard and a gray mullet. He looks like shit. He has cars all over his face and he now walks with a limp. He sits down across from Spawn and grabs the phone. I'm glad you came. Spawn: Don't be. I don't want you to feel any enjoyment ever again. I see your still mad. Spawn: You could say that. Your still the same mouthy little bitch you used to be. Just like your mother. Stupid whore never shut up. Spawn: So you shut her up for her. Yep, I guess I did. Spawn: So why am I here? I have something to tell you son. I have been watching you over the last couple of months. You have been doing well for yourself. I have to congratulate you for finding a job that pays you for beating the shit out of people. I wish I could get paid for that. Anyway, I noticed that you get your "strength" from me. That anger that burns inside. That rage that powers you. You get that from me. I find that interesting. The guy that you hate the most is powering your career. Ironic isn't it. Well I'm here to tell you something that will help you never be the same again. Something that I have with held for over 20 years. And today I am going to tell you. Spawn: So I'm here for fucking story time huh. Well hurry the fuck up. We'll start with the day before. I woke up that day and she was already bitching. She told me I'm a worthless sack of shit. And that I smell and what not. I told her to shut the fuck up. But she wouldn't. So I left for work. I worked form 6 to 9 that day. Fucking 15 hour day. Nobody works a fucking 15 hour day but me. I provided for you worthless asses. And I got shit in return. Nothing but bitching and crying. I went to the bar that night and drank with my buddies. I had about 7 or 8 and I drove home. I went to the fridge and grabbed a beer. I turned on the baseball game and sat down. The bitch came a hollering. She was saying all this shit. I wasn't listening. All I wanted was to watch that game. But no that stupid bitch won't shut up. I stood up and told her to fucking leave me alone, but no. I slapped her and she ran away crying. She was always a pussy bitch. A whiner just like you. Spawn: Fuck you asshole. I didn't come here to listen to you bitch about life you faggot. Now are you gonna tell me something or not. Cause if you don't I'll leave now and never come back. Always bitching you two. Always. Don't you ever stop. Now sit the fuck down and listen to me for ten minutes. Spawn sits down and his dad continues. All she did was whine. The next day was the same. I worked for fucking 15 hours on cars, producing money for my family. Just trying to make ends meet. But I still get bitched at. I went to the bar and drank. I drank and drank and drank. I didn't know where I was and I had someone drive me home. The bitch came at me. And she wouldn't stop. She complained and bitched. I told her again to shut the fuck up. I slapped her in the face. She fell on the ground. She started to cry but she wouldn't leave me alone. The stupid bitch complained again. She stood up and punched me. I grabbed her by the hair and slapped her again. She fell down but got up again. She wouldn't stop. She kept arguing and complaining. My head was screaming in pain with her bitching and she wouldn't stop. Then I lost it and started beating her senseless. I punches her in the face and she fell into the lamp. I ran over there and grabbed her by the shirt. I raised her up and punched her again. I did that for a while. Hitting her with my fist. Again and again. She started to bleed and I stopped. I threw her into the wall and threw a book at her. It hit her in the face and she screamed. I ran over to her and kicked her in the head. She stopped screaming. I picked her limp body up and punched her again. This time the blood was flowing heavier. It kept purring out. I didn't care. I thought I would teach her a lesson that she would never forget. I smacked her again and again. The blood splattered all over the rug and the wall. When I looked up and saw the mess and stopped. I stood over top of her and screamed at her. I told her that the next time she wouldn't be so lucky and that she was worthless or something like that. I was too drunk, I can't remember. Spawn: That's it thats what you have to tell me. Stuff I already saw with my own eyes. Your an asshole. And you will never change. I should've known. Good I hope you burn in hell you piece of shit. Stop. I'm not done you idiot. I still have to tell you what you don't know. After I was done I walked up stairs. I walked into the bathroom to take a shit. That's when I saw a box. It was red and yellow and had a picture of a baby on it. I walked over to it and it said Pregnancy Test. She took a pregnancy test. I walked over to the trash can and looked into it. Spawn: No, you ass no. I picked it up and looked at it. Spawn: Don't say it you asshole, don't say it. It was positive Landy. Spawn: No, it couldn't have. You aren't that evil. Ha, ha, ha, maybe I am son. It was positive. Spawn: You asshole. Two birds with one stone huh Landy. HA, HA, HA Spawn: YOU DICK, YOU KILLED MY FUCKING MOTHER AND MY SIBLING. HA, HA, HA, HA, yeah I did. I killed the worthless bitch and the one on the way. Funny ain't it. Spawn: HOW COULD YOU? HOW COULD YOU THINK THAT'S FUNNY? When you look back on it you'll think its funny. HA, HA, HA, HA. Spawn: YOUR FUCKING LUCKY THAT THIS GLASS IS HERE. OR I SWEAR TO GOD I WOULD KICK THE SHIT OUT OF YOU. Oh, come on tough guy let's go. Charge threw the glass. Spawn throws one of his fists into the glass. It shatters into pieces. Spawn runs threw and grabs his father. He punches him in the face and throws him to the ground. He grabs his head and pounds it on the ground. The hard cement cracks open his head. Spawn then begins to punch him in the face over and over again. He doesn't stop. He keeps pummeling the old man. The blood keeps spilling out of his head like a faucet. The guards run in and grab Spawn. Spawn: HOW DOES IT FEEL? HUH? HOW DOES IT FEEL ASSHOLE? HOW DOES IT FEEL TO BE BEATEN LIKE A DOG? HOW DOES IT FEEL TO SEE YOUR OWN BLOOD SPILLING AROUND YOU? HOW DOES IT FEEL TO GET PUNCHED WHILE YOUR HEAD SPILLS BLOOD? BURN IN HELL! BURN IN HELL FOR THE REST OF ETERNITY! FUCK YOU! FUCK YOU! FUCK YOU! Okay buddy, let's go cmon. The guard carries him out to the main area. He sits him down in a chair and begins to talk to him. Okay, we got what he said on tape. We're gonna say that he was in a fight, as long as you just leave. Spawn: Go kick him in the face for me. Spawn then walked back out to the parking lot and got the camera man out of the car. Spawn: Start shooting. Okay go. Spawn: “Talk, Talk, Talk. This is all that I have done since the FAW has come back to life. Talk is good, but empty. Words, ideas, so shallow. The greatest words in the history of the known world are useless with out the ability to carry them out. Do you all realize how often I have heard words, boasts, idle male hormone induced rambling? One almost becomes immune to it, as if getting the common cold one to many times. Think about it, would the Declaration of Indolence mean shit with out the efforts of the repopulated Euro’s overthrowing their king? Would the War Crimes Tribunal of the united nations be worth anything if it just gave the Arab world carte Blanche to just run rough shod over women’s rights and freedom of expression in general and hide it under the guess of organized faith?…. Oh damn that’s right, bad example, considering how corrupt the United Nations is currently. But I am not here to talk politics.” Nothing to calm the soul like this. But I think my foe Great 1 has gotten the wrong idea. Now I respect the kid, and he respects me. That’s nice isn’t it? Makes you feel all warm fuzzy inside? Makes you want to sit around a fire and sing kumbia my lord? Maybe have a huge group hug? Poor Great 1, like I said I do respect you, but this is where respect ends. This is where the verbal blowjobs we have been giving one another ends. You need to stop being so damn idealistic and ask yourself a simple question? Who am I facing this Sunday? No I don’t mean my history in this federation, titles, wins, etc. Do you even know me? Do you even know why I am in the sport?” What are his motivations? Did you ever think to ask that? Why did he get into wrestling? Why is he so fucking angry? Why? Why? I didn’t think so. Allow me to tell you why? You see when I was a much younger Spawn. Roll the tape Camera Spanky…” The camera fades in again. The video is of old quality, transferred from 8 mil. Tape from some time ago. A proud mother and father, the mother standing 6’0 even, very pretty, dressed in a black sundress, her brunette hair up over her head. The father a large man, about 280 and 6’6, dressed in a pair of casual slacks had his hands around the tall woman. Standing beside them, locked in some kind of wrestling hold, two teenagers stood there, both semi tall boys, dressed in the same WWF hulk mania outfits, and both about 5’8 tall. Mother: “ Honey, look he did it again. That boy is always inventing new holds and locks. Its great he has a interest, but why can’t he be more like his cousin.” The father looks on at the son and his cousin. Father: “ Oh dear, I understand what you mean, but you have to let him go his own way. Both of them love the wrestling show on Saturday; you can’t pull them away from the screen. That superstars program…” One of the boys calls to the parents. Son: “ I finally got it, I finally created the most insane move in all of wrestling (Laughing methodically). cousin come here let me do it on you!. He grabs the cousin, against his will with youthful zeal, and forces his head down, bending him over and undercooks the arms. Shifting his weight into his back, he flips his cousin over and grabs his neck to fall with it in his arms. (Tiger Driver 91 cutter) Time seems to stand still as he falls to the ground with his beloved cousin’s neck. The loud cracking sound of his neck resonates over and over again in the vortex. The kid cringes as he rolls over and stands up and checks on his cousin. Mother: “ Oh my god, Landy Rivera, what have you done to your cousin, he isn’t moving, oh my. That blood is everywhere, everywhere A young Spawn simply stands there, in shock, looking at his cousin … the camera fades out…. The scene cuts back to the nightclub where Spawn is standing with a sick sadistic grin on his face. Spawn: “You see, Great 1, when I say I live for the sport, I breath it, I shit it, I would die for it, I truly mean it. Due to my love of creating new wrestling moves, I put my own cousin in a wheelchair for 5 years, all for the love of creating a cutter version of the Tiger Driver 91. I didn’t know it then how flawed my morals were, that I would put my own flesh and blood at risk just for that. Then it came to me, flying in my face. The truth, that I was and will always be a wrestler. The sport is my family, my friend, my existence. Some people go home at night, to be with their families. Wives, kids, etc etc. All that sick pathetic sheep like existence. We are different then them, us both Great1 . We are both artists of the greatest art form in existence. Where as some use paper as canvas or AutoCad, or Poser, or what have you, we make out masterpieces in the ring. We know we can lose our life’s, cause great amounts of pain, put a man in a wheel chair to go home to his wife and children to see him a broken man. The ultimate price that is what I am willing to give to the sport. I could give a damn about my own cousin, stupid bitch, got what he deserved, marking out for that damn Hogan and his legdrop.(he laughs).” he looks to the camera. Spawn: “ Tell me, Great 1, , are you willing to sacrifice yourselve or someone else, for that great move, for that great moment, for the honor of taking someone out? The heel is back! And love or hate me, you will feel me, but not in a way that will leave you satisfied.” Great 1, be ready for a fight on Sunday. Be ready to feel pain, and get ready to feel suffering. I need to get a little aggression out and I plan to let it go on Sunday in that ring. I plan to let it attack you and to beat you to the ground. I plan to use it to make me the real legend in the FAW. And once all is said and done I will be the one standing in victory, and you will be on the ground begging for relief from the pain. The pain that I will give to you, and it is the same pain I hold now. The pain from what I just learned inside those prison walls. I won't tell you what it is, but after Sunday you will understand how much it hurts. See you Sunday. Cut. Get in the car. What's the matter? Spawn: GET IN THE CAR! the scene fades to black.
