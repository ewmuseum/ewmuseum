Disaster Down Under? - Rp1 9th Wonder Of Foam Furiously Addictive Wrestling Roleplay #32 Date: 22/10/02 Location: Austrailia - The Desert .... Australia - The Desert - Monday Morning .... (The 9th Wonder Of Foam crawls on his hands and knees, Lars follows behind standing tall) Foamy: How the hell can you last in these conditions? Lars: I spent twenty years in Denmark being rained on; I have lots of water storage. How long until we reach the arena? Foamy: Not sure, it can't be far. (Foamy's cell phone rings) Foamy: Hello? What? .... Foamy Hills - Tuesday Morning .... (Foamy pulls up in the F-Team van, Lars follows carrying his bag) Foamy: Why the hell call it Disaster Down Under if you're holding it in Nashville? (Foamy opens the door and walks in) .... Foamy's Study - Noon .... (Foamy sits at his desk, he is sitting there thinking. On the wall are copies of all eight of his tag team championships, and his singles titles. He continues to think, while staring at a fax from his machine) (Lars enters) Lars: Hey Foamy, I found this Klown's nose on the kitchen floor, do you want me to throw it in the trash? Foamy: No give it here. (Foamy takes the Klown's nose; he opens a case on the wall of memorabilia from his career. Etan Cross's wrist tape, Jon Farrer's tights, a turnbuckle cover from Extreme Beating II and Kris Midnight's lunchbox. Foamy places the Klown's nose inside and locks it again, he sits back at his desk) Foamy: So, Lars I'm in a bit of a quandary. Lars: Huh? Foamy: A quandary, I'm in a bit of a quandary. (Lars scratches his head) Lars: This really doesn't help me am I having another acid trip? See I thought we were in your study now you tell me we're mining for rocks? Foamy: A quandary not a quarry you fool! I'm in a pickle, I'm stuck I need your help. Lars: Shoot! Foamy: This Sunday as Disaster Down Under I have been placed in a match against Renegade and Rzarector for the FAW Tag Team Championship. But I need a tag partner, it's down here as '???'. Lars: I'm a good tag team partner? I fought well on the streets of Copenhagen when I was homeless. .... 1981 - Copenhagen (bad gaga alert) .... (Lars, Hoel & Lenny all huddle up together, on a cold, brisk winter’s morning) (Hoel wakes up; she pushes Lars & Lenny in order to wake them up) Hoel: Hey I just had the greatest dream, I dreamt I was being given a hand job, man it was one of the most realistic dreams of my life! (Lenny turns to him excitedly) Lenny: So did I. I had a brilliant hand job, I even came in reality! (Both Lenny and Hoel turn to Lars who is huddled between them) Lars: Funny you should say that, I had a dream I was skiing in the Alps. .... Present Day .... Foamy: What the hell has that got to do with being my tag team partner? Lars: Uh, nothing. Foamy: Look, I got Melissa Farrow coming over for an interview, when she gets here bring her straight to me I am going to announce my tag partner for Sundays title match! ... 37 Minutes later .... (Foamy paces up and down his office, Melissa Farrow enters, and she looks around for Bob the Mocking Bird but sees nothing) Melissa: Where's the bird? Foamy: Oh he's been sent to a correctional facility for a while to have his nads chopped off so he won't be horny. Melissa: So Sunday night, you get a tag team championship shot. Foamy: Indeed I do, a shot that came quite frankly out of the blue. But thins like this normally do, one day you're walking down the road and then suddenly destiny crashes a bus into your face. I don't know how I earned this shot but the fact is I have this opportunity so I'm going to grab it by the testicles and make it cough. Melissa: The identity of your opponent is currently a mystery. (Lars enters wearing amateur wrestling gear) Foamy: Not for much longer my dear friend, I can now officially reveal that my Tag team partner will be.... (Foamy pulls out a row of three questions marks from his desk. The 'model' is around 6 inches high) Foamy:... Yes that's right my tag team partner will be this row of question marks! (Lars looks dejected) Melissa: What? Foamy: Ok I kid you. (Lars smiles) Foamy: My actual partner for Disaster Down Under will be a man who has stepped into the ring with me before... (Lars looks even more excited) Foamy: A man who I have known for a very long time, and old friend of mine, an old tag team partner of mine... (Lars suddenly looks depressed) Melissa: Neo? Neo is returning? Foamy: No Melissa he is doing some soul searching, my tag team partner is going to be he is lurks in the shadows, he who lurks in the light, his name is Invisible Jim. (The sound of Lars slapping the front of his face can be heard) Lars: Oh no. Melissa: Invisible Jim will be your tag team partner? Foamy: Yes I won the OAWF Tag Team Championship with him before and Sunday night I will become a Two Time FAW Tag Team Champion. And a nine-time, nine-time, nine-time, nine-time, nine-time, nine-time, nine-time, nine-time, nine-time, Tag team Champion, rather apt considering my name don't you think? Melissa: And Invisible Man will be your partner? Foamy: Yes, what is so difficult to understand? Melissa: Well, Can I get an interview with him? Foamy: Uhhhhh, tomorrow, yes tomorrow I will grant you access to FAW's first ever transparent superstar! Melissa: Well....ok. So your opponents Renegade and Rzarector formidable opponents? Foamy: Indeed. But they are still newbie’s, they still need to learn something’s about FAW. And besides the manner in which they won the titles didn't exactly strike me with fear, The Great One came down to the ring and smashed Spawn over the head with a chair and they just pinned him, it wasn't exactly something to be proud of. Melissa: So you can win? Foamy: Well, I’m not overstating anything, they are more than capable of winning, but let us remember that they are yet to defend the titles, the fact remains that they are yet to pick up an untainted win in defence of those titles, while I have. If you ask the people on the street who has been an FAW Tag team champion so far this year the only letters that they will remember will be the F.W.O. Every other tag team has been afraid to mention that fact, every other team has been ashamed to hold those titles. Melissa: But you? Foamy: I wear those titles with pride when I hold them. Now Spawn make rightfully make claims to me being not quite the singles specialist, but if there is one thing I can lay claim to in FAW it is the fact that I am the Tag Team Specialist in this company. I will do anything to anyone with anyone, just like a cheap slut. The only difference being I'm proud of that fact. Melissa: Any last thoughts? Foamy: I here that Rzarector is a religious man, well buddy start praying, pray for salvation because it is unfortunate that in your first title defence both you and Renegade have to face the 9th Wonder Of Foam and The Invisible Man, how do you fight an opponent you cannot see? Tell me this gentlemen, I await your response. Have a Foam Filled Day!
