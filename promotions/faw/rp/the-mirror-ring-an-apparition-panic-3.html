The Mirror Ring; An Apparition (Panic 3) Furiously Addictive Wrestling Roleplay #99 Date: 12.12.02 Location: The Mirror Ring in HaHaKlown's mansion, The Big Top ::: inhale ::: ...through the darkness of the shadows; within the echoes of screams; into the aura of pain and suffering the light flickers on. Rustling sounds are heard at the very edge of the darkened hall. The camera slowly proceeds, ever so carefully, as if not to disturb the presence of the wraith of Death that lingers throughout the walls of this place. Grunting noises break the silence as if there was a conflict on the other side of this gateway. The camera hesitates for a second. Then continues... ...along the way the sounds increase to an incredible rate. The sounds are more clear now and resemble that of a brutal brawl. One of which, a sole person is the dominating force of it. Echoes of fist hitting flesh and bone become much less discreet, in a manner of speaking. The upcoming scene is that of a person getting mauled to death by an uncaring heartless bastard. Who in their ‘right’ mind would do such a thing to another human being? Who in their ‘right’ mind would fatally pummel another life into a massacre of bloody nothingness? Who in their ‘right’ mind could possess such a relentless etiquette? ...he who hasn’t a ‘right’ mind...of course... ::: Scene: “The Playground” (HaHaKlown’s personal training center, located in the underscore of his mansion, “The Big Top“) 4:42 am. The second room to your left as you walk into the Playground, dubbed “Mirror Ring” in which a sparring ring is in place surrounded by wall, ceiling, and floor mirrors. Inside, HaHaKlown is seated on the top rope (dressed in his usual apparel, yet hasn’t any face-paint so his head is covered with a Mexican luchadore wrestling mask that is designed in HHK’s face paint) smoking a overfilled marijuana blunt, staring at the man on the canvas of the ring. His sparring opponent is...well...maybe it should be said...his sparring opponent WAS the old school wrestler from FAW, Tom The Bomb, is laying in what seems like an impossible position halfway off the ring. He just received one of the most brutal beating in his lifetime career. The man appears to be deceased ::: HaHaKlown: Tom...TOM! ::: little movement occurs from the post-corpse ::: Tom: uh... HaHaKlown: Good...you’re alive. Get the fuck off the ring, you little shit. And be glad I didn’t fucking Whoopie Cushion you... ::: no response to this. In accordance, HaHaKlown walks over to him and picks him up by the back of the neck...positions him...and lets out a double Whoopie Cushion (consisting of six massive power bombs in all). The intensity of the move evens winds HaHaKlown a bit as he stumbles back to the corner. The OSW legend, Propaganda, is now dead. Once K’s breath was caught, he picks up the carcass using both hands and accomplishes an impressive throw into the corner of the room, which reveals four other bodies that obviously have been ‘sparring partners’ with the Klown this night. ::: HaHaKlown: How fucking hard is it to get good help around this bitch? I bet the Lion Den, down in Dallas, is getting hella’ mother fuckers in their training program...where the fuck is the competition? ::: at this, a voice from the darkened hallway is heard ::: Voice: You want competition, eh? HaHaKlown (more angry than startled): Who the fuck are you? Bitch, if you want some all you gosta’ do is ask...show your face. Voice: You can’t beat me. You’re too weak against what I have. HaHaKlown (enraged): He that seeks trouble, seeks death. Voice: Peter, chapter seventeen....verse eleven. HaHaKlown: Who...are you? ::: what little light that illuminated the room bursts, leaving the room in utter darkness, with the exception of a single religious candle (with picture the crucifixion of Jesus Christ, which has been modified to be upside down) that burns in the corner of the room. The person behind the voice steps into the room...he is holding a gleaming chrome hatchet that reads “In saecula saecularom” on the handle ::: HaHaKlown (now thoroughly pissed off): Where in the fuck did you get that? Wait a minute...wait...you can’t be...there’s no fucking way... Voice: Ah...but there is you see... ::: as the ‘voice’ steps closer to the ring, a faint hint of his apparel can be seen, he is covered in all black leather (trench coat, and top-hat). The only visible facial feature is his uncannily pearly sharpened teeth of his smile. ::: Voice: What did you think...I was going to leave you alone forever? How could I ever leave you alone? HaHaKlown: Wha--wha---why are you here? What do you want from me? Voice: You see, kid...although you were the smartest person I had ever met in my life...I despised you for it. I was constantly under looked by everyone that came our way, every one at the church, at the school...every one wondered how an imbecile like me ever be the....sigh....I was always looked as a fucking no body. No body ever could see any potential in me even as a fucking child, much less an insignificant adult of the meager side of town. HaHaKlown (lightly sobbing): But...but... Voice: Did you know that before your time, I was the greatest fucking wrestler that had ever stepped foot in the squared cell? Did you fucking know that? Of course you didn’t. No one remembers that. No one remembers the careers that were stopped by unstoppable forces. Ones that couldn’t provide for the mouth of their family and had to quit their passion and their pursuit of dreams and lifelong goals. I HAD TO FUCKING PROVIDE FOR A FUCKING FAMILY THAT COULD GIVE A SHIT LESS ABOUT ME OR WHAT I WAS ABOUT. HaHaKlown: That’s not fucking true, and you know that. Everything your family did for you was specifically for YOU. Everything revolved around pleasing YOU. That’s fucking bullshit, and you fucking know it. You were the center of the family’s fucking universe. Voice: Oh yeah, is that a fact? Well then, how come I never got any respect? Huh? Did I not earn myself some fucking respect in my household? Was I not good enough for them, or what? HaHaKlown: You were fucking overfilled with respect from your family. Even though you were an egotistical power-maniac, your family still loved you and wanted...to...make you proud. ::: suddenly the candle flickers out, as if two fingers extinguished it by pressing the wick together, leaving absolutley no light whatsoever. Within a matter of seconds, the ‘voice’s cheek is pressed against the back of HaHaKlown’s head and the hatchet to his throat. HaHaKlown doesn’t even wince at the extraordinary bitter chill of the man’s icy cold hands upon his flesh. ::: Voice: Make me proud? MAKE ME PROUD? YOU WANT TO FUCKING MAKE ME PROUD, GOD DAMNIT? ::: HaHaKlown begins to cry and rips off the mask he is wearing. He turns around to hug the man, but he is not there any longer. He old grasps the winter chill. The hatchet reappears at his throat much more aggressive like this time, as the man is now standing behind him again ::: Voice: There’s no use in crying, kid. What’s done has been done, and there is nothing you nor I could change about it. I came only for one reason, K. I wanted to tell you something, and give you something as well. First...I want you to have this... ::: The bitter cold hands of the man hands the Klown the hatchet with some sort of chain wrapped around it ::: Voice: Keep this forever, and remember the times that we should have had. HaHaKlown (hindering no emotions, crying aloud): I’m sorry...I...am so so sorry..... Voice: And I wanted to tell you that...you’ve made me proud, kid. You made everyone proud. The intensity of my pride for you overwhelmed me with jealousy and envy of wanting to live your life. The life I was stripped away from. I love you son, and I’m sorry for not being the father you deserved... ::: The apparition of the Klown’s father, Reverend Trais Aries Sr., then steps through and into the body of HaHaKlown. A feeling like no other overpowers him, and sends waves of electric numbing throughout his entire being. Through the mirrors, the Klown’s eye become extremely glowing giving off a radiantly bright illumination of a pure white. His eyes glow white as he stares into the mirror. The light flickers on, and the face of the Klown is now covered in his usual face paint, and his eyes still glow brightly surpassing the brightness of even the sun. Upon looking in his hands he sees wrapped around the hatchet in which he had buried along side his father at his funeral, a metal rosary is wrapped around with the words “ Aries Jr. “ engraved in the flipside. With it, a small form of paper is wrapped alongside the hatchet ::: HaHaKlown (reading the note): He that follows the footsteps of his father, follows the footsteps of God. He that kills demons...kills evil... ::: HaHaKlown rereads the last line ::: HaHaKlown: Kill...evil. :::: finally comprehending the subliminal message, HaHaKlown grins and looks at his self in the mirror. His gleaming eyes crease with laughter of irony, as he now has the power of the ‘other side’ with him. He crumples up the note, places the rosary over his neck, and grips the hatchet firmly ::: HaHaKlown: I must kill the demons...if not for the God I should have had...the father that now burns inside of me... ::: exhale :::
