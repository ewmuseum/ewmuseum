Evil by any other name is Foam

13/12/02
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Evil by any other name is Foam 9th Wonder Of Foam Furiously Addictive Wrestling Roleplay #100 Date: 13/12/02 Location: Underground Lair …. A Secret Underground Lair – New York – Under A Factory …. (Deep in the heart of the secret underground lair of the EVIL! Genius 9th Wonder Of Foam the Foamy One is hard at work on his latest scheme) Foamy: Ah yes, this will be so EVIL it will turn the world upside down, it will make fruit turn sour and it will make the French smell nice. (Lars walks up to Foamy still in his white furry cat suit) Lars: Meow. Foamy: Does my evil little kitty want a petting? (Lars leaps onto Foamy’s lap and Foamy begins to stroke him) Foamy: Hang on a minute! (Foamy drops Lars onto the floor) Foamy: You’re only supposed to be dressed like that for when I do that evil twirl in my chair, why are you dressed like that now? Lars: I don’t know I’ve just been doing some cat things. (Out in an alleyway a girl cat looking flustered takes out a cigarette and begins to smoke it) Lars: What are you working on? Foamy: A scheme so EVIL! It would make Darth Vader think about changing sides… well before Luke made him change anyway. Lars: What is it? Foamy: I’m going to change the side of the road cars are meant to drive on. In America they will drive on the left, in Britain on the right, it will be total chaos! Lars: Are you going to use some kind of lane change laser? Foamy: No, I’m writing a stern letter to my congressman so that he can propose it and make it law, thus creating a chaos effect that would bring down society, leaving me as its rightful ruler. Lars: That’s not very evil is it! (Foamy slaps Lars EVIL style with the back of his hand) Foamy: Enough of this insolence. Lars: But I’m not diabetic! Foamy: Insolence you fool. For your idiocy I want you to jump into the pool filled with hungry flesh eating haddock. Lars: They don’t eat flesh; they only eat m&m’s. Foamy: The when James Harris arrives, jump into the paddling pool and make it look like your writhing in agony! Capice? Lars: Capice boss. (James Harris walks out of the darkness with a frightened film crew) Foamy: What’s wrong with you? James: We were just scared that’s all. Foamy: Scared of my EVILNESS? James: Uh no, we spotted a Barbara Streisand CD playing on the way down here. Foamy: Lars! (Lars wriggles in agony in the child’s paddling pool, throwing the odd haddock into the air as if they are attacking him) Foamy: So you wish to speak with me, the evil genius that I am? James: Yes, out of all the opponents for the Santa Clause Battle Royal only you and Ha Ha Klown have spoken thus far. Foamy: Ah yes, the clown. The man who thinks sprinkling his syntax with the odd swear word equates evil. Being evil does not come from being able to use a lapel flower filled with water to comedic effect no. Evil is not humorously riding a tricycle; evil is not riding around in a mini cooper filled with twelve other clowns. In order to be truly evil you have to have an evil heart, a black one. You do not accomplish this by going to a cemetery picking a grave at random and creating a credible story to back up your craziness. No evil is a certain style, the difference between dangling someone above a circus ring and hanging them above a shark filled with flesh eating piranhas. Klown you have no style, rage is not evil, evil is not something you can switch on and off by deleting the work fuck. To be evil you have to encompass it, you have to have the evil underground lair, the Doctorate, the elaborate scheme in which to do your end, etc etc. James: Perhaps Klown isn’t evil he is just hateful. Foamy: That maybe so but hate breeds anger and anger leads to the dark side. Darth Vader wasn’t the evil genius he was because he was “a bit miffed” he was black down to his very soul much like me, I will do anything to win, I will eradicate whoever is necessary to become the Santa battle royal champion! James: But you are facing everyone in FAW, and awesome task if you don’t mind me saying. Foamy: And awesome task? That is nothing compared to what I am capable off I just need a foe that is capable of bringing it out of me. James: But the names involved in this battle royal. Foamy: Ok then Harris name them and watch me mock them like only an evil genius can! James: Ha-Ha Klown Foamy: Child’s entertainer. James: Ken Ruslan Foamy: Barbie’s Ukrainian boyfriend. James: “Legendary” Lee Smith Foamy: Sorry legendary? What’s he done that makes him stand out from the crowd his surname is Smith for Satan’s sake. James: Loco Creed. Foamy: Rocky Balboa’s opponent in Rocky VII: Aspiro El Asno. James: Neo. Foamy: No comment, one day he will make a great Evil lord! James: Officer Lawrence McGruff. Foamy: What are they letting the local yokel police enforcement wrestler these days? James: Renegade. Foamy: A high flyer, who shall be shot down with my DEATH RAY! James: Rzarector. Foamy: Rzarected for the last time. James: Sabatash. Foamy: A good evil name. James: Shensui Yusuki Foamy: Sounds like a man I could have on my staff, perhaps with a bowler hat made of razor sharp steel. Also I thin I may own one if his bikes. James: Shifty. Foamy: Thinks he has an evil name, in fact has the least evil name since Jesus Christ himself. James: Spawn. Foamy: Thinks being named after a bad movie adaptation means he is all evil, finally I shall defeat him. James: The Great One. Foamy: A name is just a name, Great is below Foamy. James: The Omega Fro. Foamy: His epitaph shall read: “What did he invent a haircut or something?” James: “The Real Prodigy” Scott Costella. Foamy: I’ll show him why Prodigy’s never live up to their hype. James: Tony Vigilante. Foamy: The Italian American’s may have ruled the evil underworld during the gangster era but now it’s time for me to whack that guy and become the Godfather. James: Xin. Foamy: Get a dictionary you freak. James: Xing. Foamy: Xang. That guy could have copyright trouble from the Xin guy. James: And that’s your opponents done. Foamy: How do you think I did? They weren’t all terribly witty were they? But hey I’m just starting out on this EVIL thing; it takes a while to get your bearings. Anyway I’m attending a seminar tomorrow by Alan Rickman on how to be evil, English and have a bag full of witty, yet evil remarks on standby. James: Uh ok. So Foamy do you want to wrap it up? Foamy: Listen up FAW. The Foamy One is back, but like you have never seen him before, and I’m not just saying that like so many movie sequels do. The old Foamy would give respect where respect is due, the new Foamy One takes it, demands it. I am no longer going to be the soft touch you knew before I am evil. If you count yourself as a good guy just remember this, I was once the quimntessential good guy in FAW, but now I’ve seen the dark, and I’ve taken with me the knowledge. I know how to defeat good men; I know a hero’s weakness because I used to be one. But now not even those who think themselves evil are evil anymore. I have just raised the bar for evil to a whole new level. (Foamy scratches his chin) Foamy: And FAW, the pitiful excuse for fans that there are watch me, because come Sunday night there will be one Santa Clause standing in that ring but he won’t be red and jolly, he’ll be ready to steal Christmas, just like the Grinch. But I haven’t lost my heart, its dead, and you can’t find what you don’t have! Have a Foam Filled Day!
