The Foamy Church Opens! (DDURP003)

The Foamy Church Opens! (DDURP003) 9th Wonder Of Foam Furiously Addictive Wrestling Roleplay #52 Date: 26/10/02 Location: New Jersey …. New Foam Church – Saturday Morning …. (Reverend Foamy stands in front of a congregation of around 20 people, he is about to begin his sermon) Foamy: And so I stand before you all today, the day history shall be made, as the New Foam Church begins its reign, gone are the days when one would worship on a Sunday for as the lord once said, save Saturday for the Sabbath, and Sunday for whence you shallt woopeth some ass! (The congregation claps) Foamy: And now I read from Foam 3:16.”And in case a man should sell his soul as a slave, his soul will not go out in the way that it would had he remained pure. If he is displeasing in the eyes of his master so that he does not designate himself as a champion and so much that he is not redeemed, he will be entitled to sell his sole to a foreign people for his treachery to the lord will not go unpunished. And if it should be to his mortal enemy that he designates his soul, his enemy should control all that the man’s soul does. If he will not render his soul to another the broken man will forever remain a pittance unto himself.” (The crowd look at each other confused) Foamy: And now Lars shall sing. (Lars takes to the pedestal in choir boy robes, the organist begins her playing) Lars: People, Keep on learnin'. Soldiers, Keep on warrin'. World, Keep on turnin'. Cause it won't be too long. Powers, Keep on lyin', While your people Keep on dyin'. World, Keep on turnin', Cause it won't be too long. I'm so darn glad He let me try it again, 'Cause my last time on earth I lived a whole world of sin. I'm so glad that I know more than I knew then. Gonna keep on tryin' till I reach the highest ground. (Lars leaves the church front as Foamy returns to the stand) Foamy: And there was a hymn from the reddest, hottest Chili Peppers there ever was. And now onto the subject of today’s sermon, Rzarector and Renegade. Two sinful, idiotic men who want to attempt to poison and destroy my garden, those who pretend to be FAW Tag Team Champions. Those who won their trophies with the help of others, who are happy to sit back and let others do their work. Undefeated in non-title matches their first defence comes tomorrow and they have no idea what is coming. (Lars claps) Foamy: And so Rzarector shows his own yellow streak by pleading with the Foamy One. He calls the Foamy one by a most heinous name; he calls the Foamy One EVIL! Am I the coward who pleading with Rzarector to give me mercy? No. Let me quote from Rzarector’s bible. “9th has held the tag titles before, why should he want them again? Let us reign in the glory. 9th if you are listening, step down. Step down before God has his mercy on you. Believe me; I will resurrect you to the afterlife.” Foamy: Does this not sound like the cries, the pleading of a man scared to face the Foamy One and Lars? Let him reign in glory? It sounds as if Rzarector believes he is going to lose, he is asking the Foamy One to allow him to retain the Tag Team Championships, he is asking me for permission to win. Rzarector does not need to resurrect me in the afterlife because I am soon to be resurrected myself a new and that begins by winning the Tag Team Championship. God will have mercy on the Foamy One? Does Rzarector have so little faith in his own abilities that he prays to his god? Will his God strike me down with a bolt of lightening? Will a giant foot raineth down from the heavens and squish me? No it will not. Because at the New Foam church we believe that you have to do things on your own, no matter the cost. When the Foamy One gets to the pearly gates he shall not be judged and then be cast into the pits of hell because he expected his God to do all the work, the Foamy One understands that rather than praying to God to help me through my trials that I have to solve them myself, not matter how foolish the solution. Lars: Praise the lord! Foamy: Hell no! Praise the Foamy One! Crowd: Praise Foamy! Foamy: In the same way that Rzarector’s bodies represent lost souls each of you people, all the members of my congregation you all represent the different ways I could whoop both he and Renegades ass. Twenty different ways I could come out of Disaster Down Under the Champion, but only one way need suffice, but the method of his destruction shall be kept secret until the judgment day falls upon him. And now a reading from my Tag Team partner… Invisible Jim! (Foamy leaves the front) …. Seven minutes of silence and bewilderment later …. (Foamy walks back up to the front and claps) Foamy: Praise be what a rapturous speech from Invisible Jim. (The crowd again look at each other wondering whether they had just experienced group deafness) Foamy: And now onto Renegade. The man who is not very nice apparently. That’s what I like to see, yet again Renegade makes another rookie mistake. Instead of letting me think that he is a mild mannered reporter or whatever he is he reveals too much once again. I now know that Renegade isn’t all sweetness and light. Would it not have been better if Renegade had allowed himself to be cast as a pushover so that in the ring I wouldn’t have prepared for the eventuality that perhaps Renegade is a psycho in sheep’s clothing? (Foamy pauses and strokes his beard) Foamy: Renegade is correct a nice person wouldn’t beat someone over the head with a chair, but we ain’t nice people, we are wrestlers. But there is another kind of person who would resort to using a chair, a desperate person. Was Renegade losing so badly that he had to use a chair? His own powers so small that he couldn’t get the job done? And yet Renegade manages to strike fear into the Foamy One with the statement that he is a very nasty person indeed. Forgive me people but I will reserve my pissing in my boots until I see something nasty from Renegade, his foray into corporate slut-hood has taken another step further now that he has been taken over by the Disney Corporation. (Foamy pauses and opens his bible) Foamy: And now we end today’s sermon with a little speech I got memorised for times like these. “And I will strike down upon thee with great vengeance and furious anger those who would attempt to poison and destroy my garden, and my dreams. And you will know my name is the Lord when I lay my vengeance upon thee. And as I walk down the valley in the shadow of death I fear no EVIL! Because I truly am the baddest mother fucker in the valley!
