Here Comes Mamma! Chris Storm Furiously Addictive Wrestling Roleplay #28 Date: 10/19/02 Location: The Shrine [Waking up late. Having to hurry up to get ready in the morning. Accidently burning your hand on the coffee. Missing your ride. Being late for work. Having to do extra work because you was late. Not being able to catch a taxi home. Finding nothing but bills in your mail. Having a load of chores to do. Burning supper. Not being able to sleep well. Knowing you have to repeat the same thing the next day. Sounds pretty bad doesn't it? But there is something much worst then this. A women's mother!] [Here Comes Mamma!] . . . . the final. . . . [Chris is walking around town thinking. He had just ducked away from Minagi in the bath room. Although he had left her home alone he wasn't worried. I mean how much trouble could she get into? He's only going to be gone for a short period of time. Mere hours. Sadly enough, for Minagi hours is plenty of time to get into trouble. But Chris doesn't give it another thought. Instead him mind wanders off onto other subjects. He arrives at the gym and looks around. Multiple people are working out, trying to look their best. Chris sneaks away to the back private rooms. The room is rather small compared to the others. But it still has everything Chris needs. He had already called ahead at the start of the week and reserved a private room to train in and listed everything he would need. Chris walks into the center of the room. His mind is focused on Apocalypse.] Chris Storm: Finally a momen to myself to train. Man this has been living hell. I don't know how it could be much worse. But at least I know now I can think about just one thing. Apocalypse. And beating him. I mean with $500,000 on the line I don't have time to be playing round. I need to win this match or I could be in some serious trouble. [Chris looks over at the mirror on the wall. He admires his body. Then he takes off his shirt. You can see the scars on his once perfect body. In the middle of it all is the ill fated 'CB' on his chest. Chris rubs it with his hand. A depressed tone wraps around Chris.] Chris Storm: My mission into DWO proved to be a failure. Not only did I make the WIW:NG look bad, I made myself look horrible. And I allowed that devil women to scar my chest. But I will get Callisto in the end. Which is why I can't lose to Apocalypse! I won't make the same mistake that I made with Callisto. I can not let my guard down for a second. I know Apocalypse is in Dark Carnival eVolution. Which means he will have back up. Even if he wants it or not. When you are in a stable, those guys have your back to protect the image of the stable. This is a double bladed sword however as they are also protecting their companies image. I mean I'm not an idiot. I had stables to help me win matches before. Even when I told them I could handle it, they still ran out the first sign of trouble. Always with the same results. [Chris stares at the 'CB' on his chest. He can remember seeing Chryste and Draven catch him off guard and planting him with the Last Rites. Then the moment he'll never be able to forget. Callisto coming out with the knife in hand. He can remember telling his body to fight but he couldn't move. Not after the beating the DWO members put on him. Callisto raised the knife and cut Chris' chest. The knife was flaming hot. Chris could smell the burning flesh. Thinking back on it now, he can still smell it. Chris can not remember much after that has he passed out from the pain. He comes out of his flash back. Shocked as how he could almost feel the moment.] Chris Storm: This is all about league pride. I know the guys in FAW have no problem with seriously injurying me. It's not like I need to get up the next day and work for them. I let myself think that this company will protect me when the only think it cares about protecting is it's own interest. Mainly Apocalypse. But I'll beat the entire league if I have to. I'll give it everything I have and expect everything. I won't allow myself to shame my league again. Me and Apocalypse have been planning this too long now. I refuse to let my own stupidity cause me another downfall. [Chris turns from the mirror and looks around. He spots the perfect thing to release his anger. A punching bag. Chris walks towards it fast and sure. Chris is determined. He stops right in front of the punching bag, reaches back, and hits it as hard as he can. The bag swings back at him and he drives his fist into the bag. It would have been a crushing blow to any man's ribs. Chris knees the bag and comes up with another hard straight punch to it.] Chris Storm: I will be harder then any opponent Apocalyse has ever known. I will introduce him to new levels of pain. Doctors won't be able to find all of his broke bones. I'll be able to walk back into the WIW locker room full of pride. If I lose this match, I doubt I will be able to look myself in the mirror. If I lose then I'll know I didn't give this match 100%. Because I know I can beat you. I've waiting too long and trained to hard to lose. [Chris continues to punch away on the bag. Sweat starts to form on his body. Meanwhile at his home, Minagi is laying in Chris' bed naked. Her body is still wet. The sheets cling to her body, covering her private areas up. She rolls around the bed, too bored to sleep. Minagi gets out of bed and materializes some clothes on. She has a soft silk kimono. It's a beautiful blue with yellow trimming kimono. It's open a little to show off Minagi's cleveage. On the back hangs a tail. She rises off the ground and starts to float. It is a good thing Chris didn't see her or he would have flipped out. Minagi floats around the house, looking for something to do. She manages to find her way into the kitchen. Her tummy starts to grumble at the sight of the food laying around.] Minagi: Gotcha. Feed me now or you'll be sorry. I understand. [Minagi sticks her head into the refridgerator and looks around. She finds a bowl of noodles Chris hadn't finished. She searches around his kitchen until she finds the jackpot.] Minagi: Ah ha! I never a guy like him had to have some around here someheres. [Minagi pulls out something and holds it up. Its Chris' hidden bottle of sake. She smiles broadly and pours half of it into the noodles. She grabs a tea cup and walks into the living room. Minagi flops down onto the couch with her noodles and sake and places them on the table. She looks around and produces the remote for the TV. She flips through the channels. However she is disappointed to find that Chris only has the basic air channels.] Minagi: What is this? There is nothing on television! Maybe he has a movie or something I can watch. [Minagi looks around and walks over to the TV stand. There is a bunch of draws around the TV. She opens them up to find different things. Some parts of the TV, useless items, and some of his movies. She comes to a drawer she hasn't opened yet. It's the only one with a lock on it. She tries to open it only to find it locked.] Minagi: He thinks he can keep a secret from me? I'm the greatest space pirate in the Universe! There is no lock I can not pick. [Back at the gym Chris suddenly got a very ill feeling in the pit of his stomach. He has no idea what it is. There is just this sinking feeling that something isn't right. Chris pushes it aside as he focuses on his training more. A costly mistake as Minagi easly picks the little lock and opens the drawer. Inside is different DVD movies and VHS movies. There is also magazines and such. All a more adult then the rest. Minagi's eyes grow wide as she sees Chris is on the cover of all of it. She just found his hidden porno files. All the movies and magazines he did while he earned move doing porn and dirty magazines while in the WIW:NG. However it doesn't sink into her head yet that this is porno. She holds up one of the movies, 'Kim gets the the Storm: Pt. 2'. There is a picture of a women standing in front of a storm.] Minagi: Uh, I've never heard of this movie before. It must be a sappy love story that Chris doesn't want anyone to know he watches! Oh well, I don't have anything else to watch. [Minagi looks around and spots the VCR. She pops the tape in and sits down on the couch. She starts to eat her meal as the movie starts.] [The scene opens to Chris' hotel room. Kim Simpson is sitting on the end of the bed. Suddenly Chris enters the room with a six pack of beers. He grabs two and hands one to Kim. The two start to drink the six pack until all the cans are empty. The two sit on the bed and start touching each other all over their bodies. Chris starts kissing Kim's neck and moves down slowly. He starts to remove her tanktop. The two appear to have been drinking for some time now. Kim looks to be almost drunk. The two lay back on the bed and Chris turns out the light. The camera can see clothes being thrown around the room. The same moans we heard at Chaos echo out again. Kim screams out Chris' name in the dark. It's followed by heavy breathing and loud moans of pleasure.] [Minagi freezes the movie. She is competely and utterly shocked by what she has just found. She looks around to see if anyone is around, dispite the fact she knows she's all alone. Minagi grabs the other DVDs and VHS movies and looks at the back of them to find that all of them are pornos staring Chris with different women. A giant smile broadens her face. She looks like the cat that just ate the canary. She quickly grabs the magazines and starts flipping through them. They show Chris in rather questionable positions with different women. Minagi starts to laugh as she unpauses the movie and watches.] [Back at the gym Chris has finally finished his training. He's taken his shower and is already out of the building when he accidently bumps into a child. She could be no older then 12 or so. She has the oddest red hair. She's dressed in strange clothes also.] Chris Storm: Oh, I'm so sorry for bumping into you little girl. I must have not seen you. Little Girl: Have you seen my mommy? Chris Storm: Oh, well what does she look like? If she was in the gym I might have. Little Girl: She has blue hair and gold eyes. Chris Storm: [shocked] She's your mom? Well she is over at my house. Why don't you follow me and I can bring you there. [The little girl flashes Chris a giant smile. Chris looks a little spooked but smiles back.] Little Girl: Ok but don't try anything. I maybe cute but I can defend myself. [Chris looks confused at the little girl. What did she mean by that? He blows it off and leads the little girl home.] Chris Storm: [thinking] Thank you! Maybe this little girl will actually be Minagi's daughter and she will finally leave. Then I can focus none stop of Apocalypse and my match. But how can I be sure this is the right women? Oh come on now, blue hair and golden eyes. Not many women have both. Man, and it's already getting late too. By the time I get a serious day of training in I'll be needing to get to the airport to get to the show. Oh well, life is just tough I guess. [Chris leads the little girl to his home. Minagi, unknowning Chris will have a guest, has gotten the house all ready for Chris. A copy of one of his pornos is playing on the living room TV. Multiple magazines with Chris in it lay around the house. All of it leading to Chris' room where Minagi lays once again naked in his bed. Chris opens the door and instantly can hear the movie. His eyes start to water as he realises Minagi must have found his collection. He quickly slams the door and turns to the little girl.] Chris Storm: You ah, wait right here and I'll go and ger her. My house is sort of messy and I ah, don't want you to see it. So just stay tight. [He gets into the house and looks around. He sees all of his adult material around the house. Chris quickly gathers it all up. He runs into the living room and stops the movie. He ejects it and shoves all the things he has in his hands at the moment into the drawer. He looks over and sees a trail of magazines leading to his room. He scoops them up as he makes his way towards his room. Chris opens the door and drops the magazines. Minagi crawls toward him and gets to the edge of the bed. Chris looks around worried.] Chris Storm: Get dressed! You have someone outside looking for you! [Minagi jumps up worried. The bed sheets fall off her body. Chris closes his eyes.] Minagi: Who is it? What does the person look like? Chris Storm: Well uh, she is a little girl. She's looking for her mom. Minagi: What? Where is she. Chris Storm: She's right outside the door. I would have let her in but someone had pornographic material laying all around the house! Minagi: Oh me and you are going to have a nice little talk about that too. [Minagi gives Chris a devilish smile, Chris starts to sweat. Minagi walks past Chris. He starts to leave also but notice the magazines on the ground that he dropped. He kicks them under his bed and follows her. Chris hurries in front of her and opens the front door. Chris looks out but the little girl is gone.] Minagi: Is this some kind of joke? [Chris looks around outside. He scratches his head confused. They turn around and nearly bump into the little girl. Minagi's completely shocked. She looks around bewildered.] Minagi: Mom?!? Chris Storm: What? I tought you where her mom. Little Girl: How is my little darling doing? Chris Storm: Wait you mean to tell me you [points to the little girl] are the mom and you [points to Minagi] are the daughter? Minagi: Yes, this is my mom Washu. Washu: The greatest scientific genius in the universe! [Chris rubs his temples and sits down on the couch. Minagi sits down on one side of him and Washu on the other. Chris looks at both of them.] Chris Storm: Why are you both her? What did I ever do to deserve this? I mean I have my match with Apocalypse very soon! I don't have time to be dealing with both of you. Minagi: Oh you know you would be bored if I wasn't here. [She leans her head onto Chris' shoulder. He lets out a sigh, knowing words do not reason with this women.] Chris Storm: [Looking over at Washu.] And why are you here? Washu: I'm here to make sure you treat my daughter right. I won't let my future son-in-law be a bad husband. [Chris jumps off the couch.] Chris Storm: What?!? I have a girlfriend already! I'm not interesting in Minagi. Washu: So what is wrong with my daughter? She's perfect in every way. You claim to be the perfect male in every way. You two could have perfect kids. Chris Storm: I'm happy with Tessa Windsor. Washu: [to Minagi] I think we should go find this Tessa Windsor and deal with her. [Minagi nods back. Chris' blood starts to boil.] Chris Storm: Don't touch her! Look I'm going for a walk. Don't follow me and try to stay out of trouble! [Chris walks to the front door and grabs his coat. He throws it on and walks out the door. Chris slams it behind him causing Minagi and Washu to jump. The wind is blowing hard on this evening. His hair blows in the wind. Chris looks around and decides to walk toward the shrine. Maybe he can get some peace and quiet there. Chris starts down the dirt trail through the woods.] Chris Storm: The clock ticks down. Moments are turned into hours. There isn't much time before I must wrestle Apocalypse. This could be one of my most important matches in my career. I need to beat Apocalypse for so many reasons. I mean there is my league, my pride, my love, this sport, my record, and many others. The match is so soon and I don't think I've had the proper training. I'm sure Apocalyse can say the same thing. I saw his promos in town. He seems to be having his own problems. Why is it always harder to do things that seem so important to you? [Chris passes through the sights of nature. The wonders of the colors. He lets out a sigh as he forgets his troubles with every foot step. Among the leaves and colors of fall, Chris completely forgets about Minagi, Washu, WIW:NG, Apocalypse, and everything else. He leans up against a tree and he can look out and see the mountain range. For once in a long time, everything is peaceful in Chris' life. That is until he hears the explosion.] Minagi: Sorry! I discovered a gas oven! [Chris just sighs and walks farther away from the house. He just shakes his head.] [You see a video package play airing the fued going on between Apocalypse and Chris. It's simple start as mere trash talking. The young go getter Chris Storm trying to make a name for himself. The known Apocalypse shutting him up. Now Chris Storm is a little more seasoned and Apocalypse is back. They will meet in the ring and settle it all. Only on FAW television.]
