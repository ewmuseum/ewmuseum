Profusion

Profusion Furiously Addictive Wrestling Roleplay #143 Date: Location: The Million dollar colonial mansion sits tucked under the backdrop of Ajax Mountain in Missoula, Montana. The property belongs to a one Profusion. The wrestler who lives his life somewhere between John Wayne and Jesus. Part Ice Hockey player and part rough neck the wrestling legend emerges from his house dressed like Johnny Cash in 1963 where nothing but black. In his mouth between his teeth he grips a 30 dollar cigar. “Fuck” yells Profusion in frustration. It dawns on Profusion as he watches the door slam shut that he left his keys in his other coat. It’s been a hellish few weeks. The divorce with his second wife was finalized on Wednesday and he is again left Lonesome, On’ry, and Mean. Wrestling is the only thing that is being processed in his mind. But tonight his sole focus is again the sport that he loves to hate. “Jackass, bitch, asshole piece of steaming shit!” yells the angry Profusion into the cold Montana air. Profusion sighs with frustration as he kicks his two hundred dollar ostrich skin boots against the cold steel door of his cell. Profusion then remembers his hidden key laid in the fake rock on his lawn. His cold breath tails him like a jet liner flying across the skies as he walks over to the phony rock. He opens it up and pulls out the emergency key for the emergency situation. “Always have a back up plan” Profusion says. A lesson he was taught as a young man before wrestling. Back when he was in the College Ice Hockey team. Profusion reminisces for a second about the time he was chased by the DEA with helicopters in the deserts of Arizona. Thank God he had an out switching cars in Yuma or his ass would still be rotting in cellblock ten. But that life is a long time gone. Profusion uses the key to enter his spacious property filled with gold records and pictures of his wrestling career. Before getting his keys from his FAW Wrestling Bag he walks over to the bar which sits in the back of the front room and pours him self a glass of whiskey. Laying on the counter going stale is a bag of Indo. “Why let that go to waste?” philosophies Profusion as he rolls a fat J. Profusion rolls the joint with as much speed and accuracy as a Chef would chop a piece of celery. He puts down his cigar and lights up his mind altering smoke. The phone rings but Profusion just sits down and tokes his life away. The answering machine picks up the call and starts to play the message out loud. “Profusion?? This is Lisa, the president of the FAW. I want to talk to you about you being charged for theft. Please get back to me as soon as possible..” The machine goes click as the call hangs up. “Why don’t you cut this honey” yells Profusion as he grabs his crotch and gives the machine a dirty look. “I'm the God damn Continental Champion! She cant threaten me!.' Profusion takes a few tokes then puts out the remaining hash and heads to the closet to grab his keys. His blood boils with anger from the threat laid on him by that fat, fucking, office clerk. Profusion grabs his keys from the pocket of one of the numerous hanging coats. He slams his keys down on the table as he storms to his production room. The room has a camera pointing to a leather chair with a small coffee table next to it. The background is a photo gallery of Profusion's greatest wrestling moments. Profusion walks over to the camera and turns it on to record a promo then sits down in his black leather chair. His chrome shades reflects the professional studio light shinning from the setup behind the camera. “Ahhhh, welcome all my friends. I bet you are all wondering how I am feeling after my match with Lee Smith. It was a great match and obviously I had the upper hand coming to the end of the match but as the kind hearted person I am, I let Rzarector come in and beat the hell out of the not so legendary Lee Smith.' Profusion takes off his shades and sets them down on the coffee table beside him. He scratches his forehead and then continues to speak. 'Lee Smith was a good match and my first title defence were I defended my title in true fashion, just like a true champion would. But now there is another person on my mind, Cyrus Skar. I took this mans place two Panic's ago and won my Continental Title. Cyrus was supposedly injured and I bet he feels terrible now that he did not wrestle in that match because he has not been granted a title match at Panic. Sure I would have been up for giving Cyrus a title shot, but i'm not the boss around here so I can not go around handing out title shots. I may be the holder of the second most prestigious title here in the FAW but I sure as hell aint a booker.' Profusion takes a sip of whiskey that is sitting on the table beside him and then begins to speak again. 'Cyrus, I am really looking forward to our encounter in the ring because I have never faced anyone like you, so until next time, terra.'
