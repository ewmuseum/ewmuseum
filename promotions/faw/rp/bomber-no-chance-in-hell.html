BOMBER NO CHANCE IN HELL

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
BOMBER NO CHANCE IN HELL Spawn Furiously Addictive Wrestling Roleplay #6 Date: 10/15/02 Location: DISTORTION RP 1 Scene opens to the wee-lit streets of downtown Queencity. We focus in on a club in the middle of the strip. We watch as individuals walk in and out of this club. Suddenly to our left a red and black Neon pulls up. The door open, and out steps a pair of black and red Nikes. The camera pans up showing a pair of black break-away joggins pants and a black fubu shirt with red flames around it. Spawn looks at his surroundings through his black shades and closes the door. He then walks up to the front door of the club and stops at the bouncer. Whats up Spawn, I aint seen you in a minute. How you been, playa. Spawn: Not bad, just came to get my drink on. I feel you. Go on in. The clubs poppin tonight. Spawn shakes hands with the bouncer and walks inside the club. We follow as he walks up to the bar. A few minutes after he sits down the bartender walks up. What can I get you tonight Spawn? Spawn: Let me get a Corona and two shots of Tequila. The bartender walks away and come back with his drinks. Spawn hands him a credit card and tells him to run a tab. He then takes the shots and throws them back. He then spin around on the stool and watches people on the dance floor as he sips his beer. A few minutes later and guy walks up to the bar. A few seconds after ordering his drink he realizes that Spawn is there. Hey, your Spawn, aren't you? Spawn: After taking another sip of his beer.You are correct. Damn dog, i been watching you since you was in HCW back when you was beefin with Davyd Wolf. You had some great matches with him. Spawn: Shit man, that was two years ago. Yo man, let me buy you a drink. What you having? Spawn: Tequila. The guy orders two shots for Spawn. After a few seconds the bartender brings the drinks. Spawn grabs one and drinks it. Spawn: Thanks man, good lookin out. No problem dog. I'll be rooting for you this Sunday when you beat the shit out of British Bomber. The guy walks away as Spawn get a hell of a surprised look on his face. Spawn: WHAT?!?!? Spawn pulls out his cell phone and gets to dialing. After a few second he starts talking. Spawn: Yo Mike, what's up man. What's this I'm hearing about me fightin Bomber on Sunday? You can't be serious. You are?!?!? Alright, man. Oh by the way, sorry about how I acted on Sunday, but I was pissed about not having a match at the last house show. I didn't mean any disrespect. Alright chief, I'll talk to you later. See ya. Spawn hangs up and drinks his fourth shot. He lights up a Newport and drinks the rest of his beer. Afterwards, he gets the bartenders attention and points to the empty beer bottle. The bartender nods and brings him a Corona and two shots of tequila. Spawn: I didn't order the shot, dude. Don't worry about it. It's on the house. Spawn shrugs his shoulders and drinks his fifth shot. After taking a few drags from his cig, he drinks the last shot and finishes the beer. He then finishes up with the bartender and walks out the club. He shakes hand with the bouncer and steps into his car. As the camera zooms in we can tell that Spawn is starting to feel the effects to the shots. he's not drunk, but he has a damn good buzz. As he starts the car the scene fades out. After about 30 seconds it fades back into Spawn's living room. He's sitting on the couch and in front of him is a half empty bottle of tequila. Bomber’s first promo is playing on the TV. By now Spawn is shit faced. Once the promo goes off, the camera focuses on a drunken Spawn. Spawn: Bomber, I don't know how the fuck our paths crossed again, but far be it for me to question an awesome man like Mike my agent. He's been in the business for a long time and he knows what he's doing. But yes, you should be impressed that your stepping in the ring with the FAW world champion. And I busted my ass to get the world title, unlike you. Don't think I'm saying that you don't have talent. I always give credit where it's due. But, I won't kiss anyone ass to get ahead in this business. And what makes you think I don't realize who made me what I am today. But you realize this. I helped make this fed what it is, along with everyone else who put their life on the line week after week from the day this fed opened. You come here and get on the staff's good side and think that you have the right to talk shit to a FAW veteran. Who the hell do you think you are? Who the fuck do you think your talkin to? I have respect for those who earn it guys with the charisma, i have a lot of respect for Fro and Thunder. Great 1 is a totally different story, and very long one at that. All three have known me for a very long time. And now it is me and you again, I mean come on Bomber don’t you get tired of me wooping your ass all of the time, I don’t think so if not you would not have asked for this match well Bomber what can I say but to tell you that, The End is not to long of a wait for you, what I can tell you is that it will be short and quick but very painful. But don't worry, this Sunday, you will get caught up. And you will realize just what it's like to come face to face with.... THE END With that Spawn lays his head back, and after a few seconds he's passed the fuck out and the scene fades to black. The next day the Scene opens to a dark street in the boondocks of Queencity. Camera pans around and stops on a young lady walking down the street. She appears a little nervous about her surroundings, but continues to walk down the street. Suddenly out of nowhere and man wearing a ski mask jumps out and grabs her. You shouldn't be out here this late, bitch. Now your coming with me. The lady screams and tries to fight but he is to strong for her. He picks her up and turns around, but bumps into somebody. He looks to see who it is, as the camera does the same. And standing there is what appears to be Spawn wearing a bootleg Superman outfit with a big 'C' instead of the S. Little early for Halloween, isn't it? Spawn: Little late to be causing trouble? The guy drops the girl and throws a punch at Spawn...uh whoever this superhero is, but it doesn't even faze him. The superhero picks the guy up and tosses him into the side of a dumpster. He then goes and helps the lady up. Oh thank you so much. Spawn: All in a days work, ma'am. Now hurry up and get home. This isn't a place for young ladies like you to be around. By the way who are you and how can I repay you? Spawn: Think nothing of it ma'am. For where ever there's a damsel in distress you will find......'CAPTAIN SAVE A HO!!!!!!' After saying this Captain Save a ho runs off into the night. The lady walks up to the guy, stomps him in the nuts and runs off. Then suddenly.......BOOOM. Spawn is awaken to the sound of Bomber being powerbombed to the mat. Spawn grabs the remote and quickly turns the volume down, grabbing his head afterwards. Spawn: OOOOO to much tequila. The look of pain on Bomber's face grabs his attention. He watches for a second and walks into the kitchen. He grabs a glass of water and two alka-seltzer and returns to the living room. After dropping the tablets into the water and drinking it, making a disgusted face after finishing it. Spawn: What the fuck is this shit? Bomber getting his ass whooped. I didn't know you could practice losing. Spawn goes to grab a cigarette, but changes his mind cause it will most likely just make him sicker. After a long match Bomber hit's the British lock whatever the fuck you wanna call it, and gets the submision. Spawn: Holy shit he actually won. This bitch's got ball. I wonder if he really a man or a female? But just when you though he'd take his win and dip off,Bomber grabs the mic from the announcer.....FUCK. So now we have to sit thought more of his jibba jabba. Thought we can stomach it, Spawn can't. He runs into the bathroom and pukes. Luckily he comes back just in time to hear what he has to say about him. After he finally shuts his ball warmer, Spawn turn the tv off and looks into the camera shaking his head. Spawn: Bomber when will you learn? When will you realize to quit while you ahead? No not giving head, Bomber, I said ahead. Do you honestly think that I can walk around this fed and demand what ever I want, cause I asked for a match. Hell If that was the case I'd be facing Great 1 for the world title. Stupid bitch. I know that you brain was rattled from getting it knowcked around the ring for the majority of that match, but damn. Are yu that fuckin dumb? And by the way, what fuckin gimmick are you seein over here? I don't use a fuckin gimmick. This is me, like it or not, love it or leave it. I don't walk around stealing people's underwear and and stop yelling,'YESSSSS'. I don't act all extra spooky, pull disappearing acts and tell people about how they need to 'Give In'. And I damn sure don't walk around calling myself the anti wrestlecrap, and by the way, what the fuck does that bullshit mean? I am know as the firestarter for one reason and one reason alone. Cause I do just that. I start fires. Fuck around and something you hold dear to you could be charcoal. It doesn't matter who you've beaten, how many belts you've held, or how many dicks you've sucked to get where your at right now. Cause you will always be some stupid bitch that's trying to prove that he can survive in a man's world. So far you have. But that won't last for long. Cause this Sunday you step into the ring with the Bobonic Plague. And I guarantee you, your life will never be the same. For you will have beared witness to.... THE END SO SAYS THE...... HELL MASTER Spawn gets a sickening looks on his face and runs out the room. The scene fade to black as we here sound of cookie tossing
