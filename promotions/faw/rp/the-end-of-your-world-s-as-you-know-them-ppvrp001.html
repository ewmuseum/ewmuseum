The End Of Your World's As You Know Them PPVRP001 9th Wonder Of Foam Furiously Addictive Wrestling Roleplay #117 Date: 21st December 2002 Location: Evil Scheme Room …. Evil Scheme Room – Saturday …. (Foamy sits on his evil leather chair. Hispanic singing sensation Enrique Iglesias tiredly sits down, Schmidt then appears in a puff of smoke from nowhere) Foamy: What – The? Look gentlemen the time is drawing near, soon I will have her, she will be in my grasp and using her unlimitless power I will rule this world. So gentlemen yesterday when I sent you away to create an evil scheme I expected you to come here with something in mind. So show me, what have you spent the last twenty-four hours creating? Enrique: I am Enrique! Fear the mole! I have come up with a super army of monkeys capable of coming together to form the most powerful primate known to man Godzilla! Foamy: Godzilla? Don’t you mean King Kong? Enrique: I am Enrique! Fear the mole! Yes. Foamy: How would these monkey’s come together to form King Kong? Enrique: I am Enrique! Fear the mole! Well there would be 14 monkeys each armed with a plank of wood with a nail in the end. And then much like in the Power Rangers in tie of great need they would come together and form King Kong. Foamy: Yes but would they be mechanical monkeys, or what? How would they physically come together? Enrique: I am Enrique! Fear the mole! Well we could use some kind of industrial adhesive or even sticky back plastic. Foamy: This isn’t Blue friggin Peter! Enrique: Schmidt has also looked into the possibility of using human semen to bind them together. Foamy: How did he investigate this? Enrique: How do you think? Fear the mole! Foamy: So what you in fact did is went away and masturbated all night? Enrique: Among other things! Fear the mole! But we also came up with the monkey plan. Foamy: Oh yes my legion of evil hoards all armed with planks with nails in the end. Enrique: They’d be rusty mind you. Foamy: Oh then lets hope they haven’t yet had a tetanus shot. And then how can we forget your ingenious plan of duck taping fourteen monkeys together somehow thinking that they would create King Kong. Come on people in 48 hours I am going to be FAW World Champion; we need some better plans than this. (Lars enters the evils scheme room) Lars: James Harris is here. Foamy: Lars, how come every time I have an evil scheme meeting James Harris turns up? Lars: Well partly so that you don’t have a huge gap between meetings and interviews. Foamy: Fair enough. Ok let him in Lars. Now you two, go away and think of something better than 14 monkeys covered in spunk. Enrique: Fear the mole! Can we still masturbate? Foamy: You’d better leave it for today, I don’t want my council of evil to go blind before kick off day. (Enrique and Schmidt leave, James Harris and his crew enter) Foamy: Hey Jimmy boy. James: Please it’s James. Foamy: You dare to answer me back? Do you know who I am? James: Yes, look could we get this started? Foamy: Yikes, what’s up with you? James: I got a date tonight. Foamy: Ok how much did she charge? James: Hey up yours! (Foamy throws his chair back and grabs James by the head, he makes James look him in the eyes) Foamy: Do not mistake my joker demeanour for some kind of niceness. One wrong move son and I will break you. James: Ok, sorry Foamy. Foamy: That’s better now lets get this started I’ve got stuff to do. James: 9th Wonder Of Foam, since yesterday only one more contender has come forward and spoken about The Dome. His name is Lee Smith. Foamy: Yes the Legendary Lee Smith apparently who apparently doesn’t understand me. Is that all he has to say? He is one of nine lucky men who will grace that Dome on the seventh day of the 51st week of the year. And he is doing little more than getting back rubs, watching television and paying his opponents little more than lip service. If you are this laid back in the ring then the Legend of Lee Smith will end right there. James: What do you think of the air of supremacy that Lee Smith carries with him? Foamy: He is simply a misguided young fool. James: Well just like you he simply seems confident of his abilities. Foamy: What abilities? I have confidence in my abilities because I have some. Because I know that nothing will stop my ascension to the top of the mountain, I have said it before but this time, nothing and no-one will get in my way not least some punk who got lucky. He is right to keep the champagne for Monday because if I am in a good mood he will have something to celebrate, the fact that he still breathes without the aid of a machine and that is all he will celebrate. James: Can we now for a second return to Renegade and Rzarector, two men who defeated you for the FAW Tag Team Championship two months ago. Foamy: Excuse me? Can I just remind you James that I fought on my own, my tag team partner was a figment of my imagination, and I fought that match with a handicap. James: But still they have the victory against you. Foamy: It took two of them to overcome me and they barely did that. Beside I am not facing both men as a team; if you remember that very night Rzarector left his side and joined the DCV with myself and the Bomber. Rzarector’s mental strength is weak; he hears voices from a deity who does not exist, he walks this planet believing himself to be some kind of mythical skull collector and he switches allegiances with the bat of an eyelid. This shows that Rzarector is unsure of his own abilities and seeks out new alliances to give him some leverage. He seeks out the impossible; skull collecting. Whereas my dream, my desire is a very real possibility, if not in fact what is going to happen. Rzarector dreams of things he cannot possibly do, but I have seen the future, and the future is Foam! James: And Renegade? Foamy: I will toss his sorry behind around that ring for sport, for fun. He will try and use his high flying abilities to fool me, to trick me. On any other night his amazing ability would be enough to make me look in his direction but not on this night. On this night I will be impervious to pain, if I am knocked off my feet I will get right back up, if I bleed I will not even give it a pause for thought. I suggest that Renegade brings some extra padding, he will need it. James: Two of the three men who are considered the favourites in this match are yet to pass comment on the task ahead of them, why do you think this is? Foamy: Ignorance, blind ignorance. Ignorance that I will punish, The British Bomber thinks that he does not need to comment on any of his opponents? He might think that it is prudent not to share his thoughts on what will be the biggest match in FAW’s history but it is easy to mistake his ignorance for fear. When we are afraid we hide, we lurk in the shadows and stay quiet. But I have no fear, I lurk no longer, when I speak I do not suggest, I’m telling you that I will walk out of EVIL Intentions the FAW World Champion. In fact I will make this bold statement now. (Foamy strokes his beard) Foamy: Of all the people who can’t stand to see me as their champion the two who will hate it the worst will be Spawn and The British Bomber. I’m telling the whole world now, that when I walk out of Evil Intentions the World Champion The British Bomber and Spawn will be so broken, be so dejected that they will walk from FAW because they know that all along I have had it inside me to become FAW Champion. And yes Spawn is a three time champion but men have been known to be lucky three times. James: Any final words? Foamy: Only this, that tomorrow night in that dome nine men representing FAW’s past, future and now will showcase their talents. And I am the NOW, I am the next FAW World Champion, I will be FAW from this Sunday onwards. Remember this face ladies and gentlemen because soon it will adorn the faces of every magazine, every website and every FAW programme from now on. Just remember where you heard it first folks, the 9th Wonder Of Foam soon becomes your new ruler. Have a Foam Filled Day!
