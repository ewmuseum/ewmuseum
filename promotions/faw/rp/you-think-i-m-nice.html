You Think I'm Nice?

25/10/02
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
You Think I'm Nice? Renegade Furiously Addictive Wrestling Roleplay #48 Date: 25/10/02 Location: Orlando, Florida (The scene opens with a close up shot of Renegade’s smiling face. He has his red NY hat on backwards as usual and is wearing his rather sexy looking silver Oakley shades. It is a typical sunny Florida day and Renegade is outside enjoying it. We see lots of happy looking people passing by in the back ground, mostly families with children. Just where is Renegade?) Renegade: So you think I’m nice do you Foamy? (laughs)Well thank you very much for the kind compliment, urrrr, I think! (looks puzzled) Anyway. I’m concerned Foamy. You want to know why I’m concerned? Well ok I’ll tell you. I’m concerned because you think that I’m a nice person and that I wont be able to hold my own in FAW unless I change. Well I’m gonna let you in on a little secret of mine Foamy. (Renegade users the camera to move in closer and whispers) Renegade: I’m not as nice as you think. (The camera moves back out an Renegade speaks in a normal tone again) Renegade: Could a nice person beat someone repeatedly with a steel chair until he was unconscious? Could a nice person ambush someone from behind with a steel chair, caving the back of there skull in? Could a nice person do those things? I don’t think so. I’ve done all these tings and more in the past. And I plan on doing a lot more of the same in the future. I like hurting people. I was born to hurt people. You see, you got the impression that I was a nice person from the interview you read that I did with the very fine Melissa Farrow earlier this week. (smiles) I’m really a not very nice person at all. I can be a right nasty person when I want too! Honestly I can! And I’m gonna show you just how nasty I can be this Sunday when me and Rzarector retain out titles and beat you and….Invisible Jim (laughs under his breath) Sorry, sorry I couldn’t help myself. I tried to keep a straight face honestly I did. And yes I do think ya mad if you think you can take on me and Rza on your own Foamy. We’ve prevailed over two opponents so what makes you think you can beat us, the FAW tag team champions, single headedly? I really do feel sorry for you Foamy. You poor, delusional, loony fool. You have my sympathy. But what can I do? If you think I’m a nice person then I must be a nice person. (Renegade shakes his head and looks at the floor. His head then suddenly pops back up and he’s back in a very cheery mood once again) Renegade: Now, if you don’t mind, I’m off to spend some quality time with some of my nice friends. (The camera pans out and we see that Renegade is standing outside the entrance way to Disney’s Magic Kingdom in Orlando Florida. He stands there with his arms out by his sides until his two very nice friends come to join him by his side. They are of course Donald Duck and good old Mickey Mouse. Renegade puts his arms around them both as the trio make there way though the entrance gates and off into the Magic Kingdom. Home of niceness!)
