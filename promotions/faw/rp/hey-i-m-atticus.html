Hey, I'm Atticus

Hey, I'm Atticus Atticus Pulsar Furiously Addictive Wrestling Roleplay #154 Date: 01-17-03 Location: Roof top ::The scene opens to the sky line of a city, the sun is setting beyond the tall, menacing buildings in the foreground. The sky is a range of beautiful colours, from bright orange to a golden yellow. The scene, however. seems dead. No movement, no life, only with the faint sound of cars below add the sound. It’s like a fantasy painting, too good to be true.:: ::... A few seconds in, and a man steps into view. His name? It’s Atticus Pulsar. He’s a well built man, with a reasonable amount of power and strength in him, but of course, we’ll have to leave it for the ring, right? Pulsar has his arms folded and is resting against the wall, he has a somewhat expressionless look on his face. :: Pulsar: Everything changes, but nothing is truly lost. Do you agree? I do. I’m 30 years old, and I’ve been almost everywhere. I’m not the man I was once, nor am I the man I will be in 10 years time. Presently, I’m a new addition to FAW, and being the ‘new dog’ in this place, naturally, I am wanting to make an impression. That said, I’m in no rush, I know fine well what I’m capable of. No doubt, though, the old timer’s from FAW will be soon to over look what I am able to do, and achieve, and in time, I will. You’ll see. Pulsar: Obviously, that will certainly not happen over night, and I accept that, I have come to learn that one can’t walk into a federation and expect that red carpet to be laid out infront of them, I respect that fact that one has to pay their dues, so to speak, and that is just what I am planning to do. You may have noticed, I seem to be telling everyone watching this what I will be doing over my career in FAW, and to be perfectly honest, I have a very good idea of what I want to happen, and seeing as I do have a picture of how I want things for me to go here in FAW, I’ll try my damnedest to make sure it happens. It’ll be a cold day in hell when I dont get what I want, trust me. ::Pulsar unfolds his arm and looks directly into the camera:: Pulsar: However, I dont want to give out the impression that I am somewhat cocky. That is wrong, I’m merely confidant of my own abilities. I’ve been watching FAW well before I signed my contract here, I know damn well who I have to beat around here to be recognised and, just maybe, be respected by others. My goal? For everyone to know who I am, and to know that I can beat anyone here, time, it’s all I need... and I have planty of it! Pulsar: Hardcore Championship Gauntlet Match. Sounds inviting. I guess you can call it my welcoming party, eh? I’m predicting all the other participants in the match will not be so familiar with me, correct? Correct. Why should they be? I’m new here, so I could be classed as the underdog, maybe? Possibly, on paper I am. That said, I’m making no predictions. I know each wrestler which is taking part in this match up, I have watched them in the past few weeks, and thats where I hold a slight advantage over those in this match who have been in FAW prior to this fight. Honestly? I’m looking forward to showing FAW what I can do. Pulsar: ‘Cause I’m Atticus Pulsar. You’ll be seeing a lot more of me in time to come... ::Pulsar smirks for the camera, it’s very false, but none the less, he carries on:: Pulsar: Xing, Violator, Slyphon, Jeff Harris and Gabriel Hunter and I all up for the same prize. Six to one chance in coming out victorious. I’m counting no one, and no one should count me out. ::Pulsar looks out across the city skyline behind him, and turns back around, his head lowered:: Pulsar: There are so many opportunities out there for everyone, but you have to take them when you get the chance to. This coming week at Panic, is my first opportunity to prove to the whole of the FAW world that I’m worth being on the roster. But not only that, but I’m promising you that I will show you why FAW signed me. Taking on these five men will push me, just wait. Pulsar: I’m not a one hit wonder FAW, I’m pretty much here to stay. I dont want trouble, I want success, and I’m going to make sure that I get it. The road, it starts right here at Panic. ::Scene cut::
