Twisted Minds Part: Two

31st January 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Twisted Minds Part: Two 'Golden Nine' Jeff Harris Furiously Addictive Wrestling Roleplay #245 Date: 31st January 2003 Location: Some Café, Some Bank and Secret Volcano Layer, Hawaii .::The scene opens with a huge white BMW outside a café, the streets are clean so clean in fact that it keeps the whole car clean. No one can be seen on the street no cars, no people nothing. Only what seems to be an empty café and Jeff Harris sporting a white tuxedo, black shoes and tie drinking coffee on his lonesome. Jeff Harris pulls out a card that seemingly is his business card it reads: “Super Secret Service, Agent 009 Jeff Harris” it then says his cell phone number but we won’t go into that, Jeff Harris stares at the card for awhile as he drinks his coffee, his face remains still for awhile until it shows some anger, he rips the card in half and throws the two halves on the floor. He throws the empty cup at the wall and gets up; he opens the door and gets into the car. He begins driving aimlessly until someone jumps in front of his car hitting the floor with incredible force. Jeff gets out of the car and sees if he is all right, while he is doing that a gunshot is heard. Jeff looks around and notices that the gunshot came from a bank, Jeff walks up to a pretty fit woman and taps her on her shoulder::. Woman: Oh thank god you are here! Where have you been all these months? The city is in caos! “009” Jeff Harris: I’ve been busy… (Ruling the world) Woman: What was that? “009” Jeff Harris: Nothing, so what’s going on here? Woman: Two big dudes went into that bank with shotguns and started firing, two people are shot I think, I just narrowly escaped with my husband who you just killed and made road kill. “009” Jeff Harris: I see… Well leave it up to me ma’am. I’m gonna kick their asses. .::Jeff Harris reaches for his inside pocket and pulls out three things, a pair of glasses, a Cuban cigar and a small gun. He places the sunglasses on his face, puts the cigar in his mouth, lights it then grips the gun firmly getting ready for action. He walks over to the bank door and kicks open the door and finds not two but three people pointing shotguns at him, Jeff looks shocked as do the three men as they seemingly recognize to each other::. “009” Jeff Harris: Don’t I know you three from somewhere? Robber One: Yes, we work for you sir. I apologize for point… Why are you wearing white? “009” Jeff Harris: Because I am a secret agent. Robber Two: I thought you were a super villain… .::Suddenly in a fit of rage Jeff shoots all three men in the knee caps blows the smoke away from his gun and walks back outside, the woman comes running up to him with arms wide open::. :.:What the hell am I doing? I am Golden Nine! Why did I just stop a bank robbery and shoot my own men? I’m fucking screwed up, but I have the sudden urge to drink martinis. What am I doing with this cigar in my mouth?!:.: .::Jeff takes out the cigar from his mouth breaks it in half then shoots the woman square between the eyes as she runs at him. Jeff looks shocked as to what he’s just done, he drops the gun and gets in his BMW then races off as police sirens can be heard, and the scene re-opens at the secret Volcano layer in Hawaii how he got there so fast we’ll never know, he enters the Volcano through… The side door with blood all down his shirt and jacket, he takes off his tie and makes it to his sleeping quarters. He gets changed and washed then begins to polish his nine. After about an hour of polishing it he attaches it back to his golden genitals then begins to mumble again::. “Golden Nine” Jeffrey Harrison: What the hell did I just do? I just killed someone, the thing that bothers me is that I don’t think I shot her enough.. GAH! My mind is screwed beyond belief, something must be done, this can’t go on but I believe more will tells as the days go by. What ever these other emotions are I must learn to control them and concentrate on my match with that cocky fuck Profusion… He is no match for my greatness and me oh it doesn’t matter what my past is? Well after the things he said, that is where he will remain. He shall remain in the past; he is champion of a title no one cares about going for Mr. “Oh well look at me, I’m invincible because I beat a jobber to win this very prestigious title!” Ah shut the up bitch. After I am through with you, you will remain in the past jobbing to people like Lee Smith and The Ninja for the rest of your life, I am Jeff Harris and I bow down to no one especially some egotistical midget named Profusion, if you think that this Sunday you are even a match for me try growing a few inches before you return and no I’m not talking about height. .::A voice is heard…::. Jean Deas: JEFFREH! JEFFREH! “Golden Nine” Jeffrey Harrison: What is it my love? .::Suddenly a huge, fat and disgusting woman enters the room she is just as disgusting as Pete Harris::. Jean Deas: It’s time for you to gimme some lovin’ babeh “Golden Nine” Jeffrey Harrison: …With you? Jean Deas: Yaw! “Golden Nine” Jeffrey Harrison: Fuck that. Jean Deas: W-what?! “Golden Nine” Jeffrey Harrison: You may have been able to take advantage of me in the past but now I am going to show you who is truly evil, no more sex for you, you fat bitch. Jean Deas: I fort you loved meh! “Golden Nine” Jeffrey Harrison: I was never in love with you, you big huge trailer park poor whore. Piss off before I bitch smack you. .::Jean Deas runs off crying::. “Golden Nine” Jeffrey Harrison: *sigh* I love my life… Sunday Profusion, Sunday. .::The scene fades with Jeffrey Harrison sat on his bed smiling away now sitting in a black tuxedo, the sounds of the ever so fat Jean Deas crying can be heard faintly as Pete Harris shouts, “HAHA! I LOVE IT!”::.
