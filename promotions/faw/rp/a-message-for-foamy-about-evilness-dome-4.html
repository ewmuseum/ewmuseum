A message for Foamy about evilness (Dome #4)

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
A message for Foamy about evilness (Dome #4) Rzarector Furiously Addictive Wrestling Roleplay #118 Date: Dec 21, 2002 Location: In middle of nowhere Why the children? (The screen comes on to show a black screen once again. Pictures of serial killin molestars flashes on the screen as a narrators voice is heard.) Narrator: Why the children? Why are these sick people always out looking to get children? Children are God's purest form. They are sinless in there earlier days. Yet these huge men take control of them in ways God is not going to support. The thing about it is, once these men get caught and arressted. Well after that they get saved and convince everyone they changed before they get executed. See this is another form of Christianity that is not at all Christianity. What do you think God tells them before they are going to die? He tells them they are already eternally damned. So the people try to escape the death penalty by manipulating everyone. Yet it never works. People are to stupid to fall for that. The courtroom people are to stupid to fall for that. So yet as they get executed, only one person knows what has happened to them. Well two people, God and Rzarector. For Rzarector saw what was coming, and God told him it could happen. So now these child molestars burn in hell screaming in pain. For what reason? The same reason several burn, mortal sin. (The screen stops showing pictures of serial killers as the screen goes black once again. Then it opens up to a bright red light. Rzarector's red eyes are shown shining into the cameras.) Rzarector: Have you ever seen something so horrible it just makes you tremble on the inside? Have you ever heard of someone talk so bad about the being who made them? (Rzarector pauses and hisses to the camera as it backs up some to show his full face. His white fangs are flashing out.) Rzarector: I have, I have seen both. 9th Wonder of Foam, The Evil Foam World Order, this is who I am talking about. They are saying what I worship to does not exist. Well I am here first hand to show you that it does exist, that God really does. For I have talked with him. No I am not schizophrenic. I think that Foamy is mislead, he is a soul that is going in no direction but down. (Rzarector opens as his fangs are now drooling.) Rzarector: For I have seen what is to come Foamy. I have seen what you drive on. You drive on Evil. Why I capitalize Evil? For Evil is a very harsh word. Foamy, do you ever feel like your soul is being sucked under the ground? Well there is a reason for this. You are being driven by the desire of your evil ways. Those evil ways are nothing more than the manipulations of Satan himself. Yet God has mercy on everyone. Foamy, you are not dead yet, that means you still have all the time left to change from your bad ways. I remember the Foamy that everyone loved, the Foamy that was very hilarious and always was in the main event status. Yet after you dropped out of the main event status you became evil. Your still hilarious to the fans, but to me no. You do not worship God, so your hilarious evil ways are being driven by Satan himself. All those fans that are chanting you, well they are mislead. They are worshipping an evil icon. Which will be the damnation of Foamy and his followers. (Rzarector hisses once more as the screen starts flashing people who are holding up Foamy signs. Then shows a picture of Foamy when he was good.) Rzarector: You see how much happier you were? Do you see it? There is nothing in this world that couldn't change you back to that. Even though God has pity on people, I do not. Foamy, you once were good, and you are now evil. I am going to give you til this Sunday to change from your evil ways and follow the light. Do you understand? So come this Sunday in the dome, if you are still evil. I shall seek your skull. I will destroy it and as soon as your skull is in my hands, your soul will NOT float upwards. Instead it will be sucked down into the devils lair, once and for good. For the burning doesn't last a couple of years, it lasts for eternity. Now Foamy, I am here to tell you a story. Better yet let you watch a video of a story, that once was remembered. Now is forgotten, now Foamy is the time to follow God. I will show you the reasons. (Rzarector hisses as the camera goes black. As the hisses of Rzarector start to fade the screen opens up to a 1990's scene in New York. The streets are crowded as people are in a hurry to get to work. The time is definently Monday morning as you see the usual coffee drinkers lining up at the stores. The camera focuses on one man, one man alone. He has a short figure, long arms and legs, a short mustache, black hair, and his eyes are brown. The man looks very agitated and violent. Probably for the fact he is running late as he keeps looking at his watch. He is pushing people out of his way, and spitting on bums as he eventually gets to work. Where he works? McDonalds. He walks in as his boss greets him at the window.) Boss: Your late Daniel. Daniel: I know I am late. I'm very sorry, it is just Monday morning and the traffic is so bad. I was literally running here. Boss: Well you are 28, you should be more responsible. You know it is Monday morning, you are late every Monday morning. So you should leave earlier. That way you won't be late. Daniel Yes I know, that will be the last time. I promise. Boss: Well you know, that is your last time. Daniel you know how many people are out there looking for jobs like you got? Daniel: Yes. Boss: Well Daniel, I'm sorry but I'm going to have to let you go. I'm sorry. (Daniel got very mad as he pushed the Boss and ran out of the store. He started thinking in his head about how bad he had it. Then he eventually started cursing God's name. Daniel finally got to the front of his run down apartment, and he sat there looking at it. As he put the key into the door he began to whisper.) Daniel: You know, I have never gotten anywhere following God. In the bible it promises if I follow him I shall be rewarded. Well now I am about to be kicked out on the streets, no job, no friends, and no family anywhere. You know its funny, this God guy is. He always told in the bible how much he loved us. Well is this love? No I don't think it is. Well no more, no more of this shit. I think it sucks that I haven't gotten anywhere. Well from now on, there is no God. I live life for me, I am now an atheist. Screw God, screw everything. (Daniel opened the door as he walked into his little apartment. There was one bed laying in this small one room apartment. On the left was a refridgerator with some sandwich meat in it. Daniel quickly made him a sandwich and ate it. He went over to his bed and laid down on it. As soon as his eyes shut the phone rang. He answered it.) Daniel: Hello? Man: Yes hello Daniel. Daniel: Who is this? Man: Daniel, you don't know me but I know you. I know that you were just fired from your job and you have nowhere to go. Well I got something that can make you lots of money. Daniel: Yes, I'll do anything. Please just tell me what it is. Man: Well Daniel, I run a small business in New York that noone knows about. We must keep it completely secret. Some say it is illegal, but then again, we have no other way of making money. So do you want to know more? Daniel: Yes I do. Man: Well if I tell you more, then you must do it. I run a very risky business, one that lets noone escape. So if you decide you don't like it, then it will already be to late. (Daniel seem's nervous.) Daniel: OK, just go ahead and tell it to me. (The man takes a long pause as his breath is heard through the phone. His dark voice begins to speak.) Man: My business is a very strange kind of business. Let me go into more details. Well first, we get money by any means necessary. There is a thing called a black market. That can sell babies for alot of money. Then we can just rob people and steal there wallets. This is the least amount of money we can get, money goes along with jewelry. Then we can rob gas stations for food. See it all works out. Yet there is one kick to all of this. Daniel: Yes. Man: We don't leave any survivors. You take a child from a mom, you make sure she isn't breathing anymore. Same with the store owner, same with the people you took the jewelry and wallets from. Understand? Daniel: Yes sir. Man: OK, now look under your bed. And I will contact you in due time. (Daniel looks under his bed to find a package. He opens it to see a gun, some bullets, and a long paper with directions on it. Daniel reads it in his mind.) *Daniel, I knew you wouldn't refuse. So I have decided to go ahead and get one of my people to put this in your room for you. The point of this letter is to be your first job in our small 'business'. Now what we want you to do is get us some money. Now there is a charitie tonight in the elegant hall on west avenue. There is going to be several old people there who are very rich. So we want you to follow one home and kill them. Then you search there house taking all money, valuables, and all the valuables off of him. On the money you shall pick up more ammo for your gun. Do we have a understandment?* (The letter ended there as Daniel looked at the clock. It was no 10am. Daniel heard about this fancy dining at the Elegant Hall on west. He remembered it started at 7 pm. So he still had 9 hours to get everything together. Daniel has a conscience though. He thought about killing people and the consequences. Yet one thing was wrong. He beleived there was no God, well that is what the Devil convinced him of. So Daniel through away most of his conscious for this one night. He then got out all of his black clothes, laid down on the bed, and fell asleep. Next thing he knew he was awoke by the phone ringing. He looked at the clock, it read 5:00 pm.) Daniel: Hello? Man: Are you going to go? Daniel: Yes, I don't have a car, but I am going to walk. Man: No need. I can have one of my cars come and pick you up on the way. Does that sound ok? Daniel: Why yes, yes it does. Man: It will be a limo, since this ball is professional. I have already got you in, all you have to do is go in, eat some. Then you have to leave with the richest person you see. That is it, remember, I will contact you about the money. The car will arrive shortly. (The phone disconnects as Daniel looks over at the clock. It is now 5:30 pm. Daniel walks downstairs and waits in the waiting room at his apartments. He see's a huge white limo drive up. Daniel smiles in elegance as he walks out to the limo. He hops in the back as his gun is packed in his coat jacket. The limo driver is very mean looking as they drive off. Moments later they arrive at the Elegant Hall. Daniel is very worried as his nerves are getting to him. The limo driver asks him if everything is ok and Daniel nods. As Daniel gets out of the limo he see's several rich people walking into the hall. Daniel walks up to the booker.) Daniel: My name is Daniel Evan. Booker: Yes I have you right here. Table #5, your food is awaiting. (Daniel walks into the Hall and finds Table #5. Everything looks so professional. Tables with white clothes, fancy silverware and glasses. And there is steaks at everyone tables. Atleast 2 pound steaks. Daniel sits down and immediately begins to eat. Then there is a toast. Everyone is looking at Daniel as if in disbeleif. Daniel smiles as he stops eating and listens to the toast.It is some old rich guy talking about how good it is for everyone to be here. Daniel just sits there and thinks about how this guy probably bought all of this. So Daniel claps the loudest once the old man finishes. Daniel immediately begins eating. Everyone is talking as hours past when suddenly..............everyone gets ready to leave. Daniel begins sweating and shaking. Thoughts race through his mind.) *How can I do this? What is the cost going to be? How much will I get paid? Will I get caught? What if someone see's me leave with him? What will happen to me? Will I actually be able to do this? Am I that scared? There is no God, there is no afterlife. So nothing will happen except in real life. Time to do this.* (A man and his child are leaving as Daniel remembers the Man on the phone saying that children are very expensive. Daniel immediately gets up and follows them outside. The man and his child hop in a limo, as Daniel hops in his. Daniel is following them now down a road. The man makes a turn and another. Daniel's limo drivers stays at least 1/2 a mile behind them. Then out of nowhere the man pulls into his driveway. The limo driver pulls off into a gas station and tells Daniel he will have to walk and does he have the gun. Daniel nods as the limo driver turns off the car. Daniel hops out, he is soaking in sweat. He begins the 1/2 mile walk to the man's house. Along the way he looks at the sky to see a full moon. A cold wind is blowing across the land, as the tree's are shaking like they are saying something. Daniel is feeling very weird and is conscious is gone now. He just keeps walking until he reaches the driveway. Daniel goes up to the door and rings the doorbell, they're the man answers it.) Man: Yes can I help you. Daniel: Yes you can. You were at that party weren't you? Man: Yes, but why are you sweating and shaking? Daniel: Oh, I'm just cold. Man: Well come inside. (Daniel walks inside and sits down on a huge red sofa. The man makes him some coffee.) Daniel: Well I was there to. Man: I know I saw you. Daniel: And I was just wondering something...... Man: Yes... Daniel: Where is your child at? Man: Well that is really none of your business, but he is in his room. Daniel: Oh well.....thats good. (All of a sudden Daniel whips out his heater, the 9 millimeter, and put the Man in the shack. Daniel starts sweating after seeing the blood and the deady body there. He immediately gathers the money off the man and runs around the house looking for other valuable stuff. He grabs everything in sight. As soon as Daniel gets done he remmebers the child. He runs upstairs and grabs him. Daniel runs outside to see the limo driver pull up and Daniel hops in. They drive off to the Business owners crib. A huge house. Daniel gets out of the limo, in pitch black night, and carries the good and the child inside. The business man on the phone is suprised.) Man: You actually did it. I am very proud of you. (The man has black hair, and is wearing all black. His eyes are almost black aswell. Daniel looks frightened.) Man: Yet, the child will be sold for porno reasons in other countries. I will make a billion off of him. Yet I have one more thing for you Daniel. Daniel: Yes. (The man reaches into his pocket as Daniel smiles. Then all of a sudden the Man pulls out a gun and shoots Daniel in the heart. Daniel falls to the ground dead. The man smiles and laughs as he gets one of his goons to drag Daniels body off. Now since Daniel is dead, his afterlife comes to happen. Daniel is in a line, full of people in front of the book of life. Daniel screams as he see's God does exist. Now it is Daniels turn at the book. Daniel was sent to hell to serve an eternal torture. And the last thing the Book of Life guy told him was, 'The man you were doing business for, he was Lucifer, the king of hell.) (The screen goes black as Rzarectors red eyes appear.) Rzarector: You see Foamy. Just because you get nothing being good, doesn't mean that being evil will get you somewhere. It can be very deceiving for Satan is very deceiving. The moral of that story is that Daniel was gettin everything since he turned against God. Yet in return the devil took his soul. Now Daniel did business with the Devil and is serving an eternal burning in the lake of fire. Daniel made the mistake, will you Foamy? (The screen goes black.)
