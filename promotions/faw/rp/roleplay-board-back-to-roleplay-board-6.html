Lone$tar — FAW Roleplay, August 2002

August 2002

Furiously Addictive Wrestling Roleplay Board Roleplay Board | Back to Roleplay Board 'Exceptional' Dave Hurst Entitled: Why the dollar? (Rp#2 for Panic) Written: 2-8-02 Location: Boston, MA I move on from my International title match upset, to the next chapter in the legacy that is Exceptional Dave Hurst. I move on to the next event, Distortion and a match with a man called Lone$tar. As if the guy is better than everybody else is because he has a dollar sign in his name. Do you think that makes you better than me, Lone$tar? Do you think that your dollar sign puts everyone in his place? What makes you so much better than me, Lone$tar? What do you actually possess that makes you worthy of even facing me? Because you have money, Lone$tar? Because you can buy your way into big matches with superstars as big as Exceptional Dave Hurst is? I appear in front of you. It’s okay though; I am on the television screen. You’re safe for now. You sit there comfortable in your chair with the paradoxical feeling of discomfort. It seems very weird that sitting in a chair with a cushion as thick as this one, almost snug, but looking at the screen and seeing me there fills you with so much discomfort. You wriggle in your chair to get comfortable, but you know why you feel discomforting. You see me on-screen. There is nothing behind me but blackness. My look pierces you like a bullet. I know you are watching this and I want you now. I want to get you now. I don’t want to wait for the match. I am ready for you now. I’m still not saying anything. I know what you are feeling and want your anguish to continue for a bit longer. “I feel your pain. I feel the suffering that you hold at this very moment. I feel the bitterness that your soul possesses. You don’t quite realise this yet, but you can hurt me. I’m not a machine, I’m not a killer, and I’m not the psychopath that everybody tells you that I am. I’m just like you. I’m an ordinary wrestler that just wants a lucky break. I won’t hurt you. I won’t rip you limb-from-limb, because that isn’t what I do. I’m just here to have a competitive wrestling match.” You look at the screen. I still have my teeth gritted and a couple of drops of sweat drip down the face of me; you follow the drop as it drips down my cheek. You don’t believe me. Why would I mention all this if I wasn’t what everybody claims I am? You look back at the screen; I am still looking straight through you as if you were transparent. I’m not in the room with you, but you feel a sense of danger and that a great threat is with you anyway. “Do you want to believe everything that I just told you? Are you confident from the few comments that I just made that you can step into the ring for our match? If you do, you are a very ignorant person. Why would you believe someone that tells you that he isn’t good enough to face you? Everything that someone says to you has a particular meaning, whether they know it, or not. You want to progress in your career by beating me. Whether that is by pinning me or by making me submit, you want to beat me. So of course everything you say in your promotions is to make me feel somewhat scared of you, to make me feel like I have something to fear before my match. That’s understandable. I know you want to make me feel fear so that I don’t perform to my maximum and I then step into the ring with you and you have an advantage, great. So, if you look at the other side of the equation at the moment, you obviously think that the same thing will come from me. But that isn’t quite it. You may have figured out already that I am put into that category that all people that act like me seems to be put in. I am the stereotypical psychopath. What you fail to realise is that while all the others in my category just act crazy and weird and try to get you wondering what the hell the guy is on. I am the real deal, not just some stereotype. I am Exceptional Dave Hurst. Do you know why they call me exceptional? Not what you probably think, which is exactly the same as every other wrestler without a proper vocabulary at their disposal. They think that exceptional in this case means I am ‘great’, or as the dictionary definition states ‘above-average’. I’ll tell you now, I don’t consider myself ‘above-average’ I don’t see myself as a ‘great superstar’ that’s just not what I’m all about. I’m about the other meaning to the word ‘exceptional’. I am out-of-the-ordinary, to say the least. I’m named Exceptional Dave Hurst because I am the exception to all the other stereotypical psychopath-types that you see flaunting themselves all over the wrestling world like they have an addiction for hallucinogenic drugs. So what I’m telling you is the truth. I will step into the ring with you and I will try to hurt you as much as I’ve ever hurt anyone before in my life. I’ve ended careers and I’ve tried to kill people. I’m not proud of any of it; it’s not something I do on purpose. I don’t plan out the whole thing; I just go ahead and do it. I don’t want to hang about targeting areas of your body for weaknesses. What is the point in that? I want to attack you. I want to hit you over and over and over again. I want to destroy you. I want to leave you a shell of your former self. I want to beat you to within an inch of your life and if necessary inches further. I won’t mess about in the ring. I want to end it as quickly as possible, but that doesn’t mean I’ll wear out if you manage to survive for long enough, oh no. I will continue to beat on you until you stop moving, or stop breathing, whatever comes first. Or whatever I feel like at the time. There isn’t any question over which one of us will win in our match. It will be me. That is obvious, you do not possess the technical ability, the intensity and the will to win that I have. You don’t have the determination, the mercilessness that I possess; you won’t go to the lengths that I will to ensure victory. That isn’t the correct question to ask. The question is ‘Will you come out of the match in a condition to continue your career’. If you want to call anyone a ‘career-killer’ there isn’t anybody that comes close to me. So if you decide to show up to our match, here’s a word of advice. Watch your limbs.” You still look at the screen. Adrenaline is flowing through your body. You want to face me, but you are also feeling unsure about the match and whether I can back up my claims, because if I can back up my claims, you are in for a very rough ride, Lone$tar. You’re in a whole world of trouble now and you know why. You are about to face the single most dangerous person in the FAW. You are about to face someone who took American Psycho and put him out of commission. The guy is probably still wallowing in his self-pity. He’s still trembling in fear after I beat him so badly the guy hasn’t made an appearance in the FAW since. This is the guy that is in the FAW hall of fame. This is the guy that, before he faced me, was proclaimed the most psychotic superstar the wrestling world has ever seen. If this guy is, or was, the most psychotic guy that the wrestling world has ever seen, what about the psychopath who beat him so badly he hasn’t been able to wrestle since. I’m not just flapping my gums. I’m not just giving you a bunch of lies and empty threats. If you don’t believe me, go to the archives and see the results from my match with American Psycho and check all the results since. See how his name isn’t there, now I wonder why that is, huh? Then you might just think that my psychotics, is fake and that I’m just trying to scare you. If you don’t believe me, then check it out. You can check my Certificate of Insanity and you can check out the Boston Mental Institution files and see for yourself that I was a patient there. I am a psycho, I am the real deal; I am Exceptional Dave Hurst. It’s about time you and everybody else in the FAW realised just who I am. I am the greatest, most sadistic, most psychotic superstar ever to step foot in the federation. I shouldn’t only be having my World title shot now. I should be put down in FAW history, next to American Psycho with the excerpt: Exceptional Dave Hurst – American Psycho was the most psychotic guy in the wrestling world before this guy stepped in and put him out of a career. So as you see the screen fade to black and you get up from your chair, you have a good, long, hard think about what you’re doing. You’re about to face the most dangerous man in the wrestling world. Having a dollar in your name will not be enough in the match. You can’t buy me out. You can’t get me to go easy on you, to throw the match. I want to hurt you and I want to hurt you bad. I want to end your career. I want to rip you apart. When we finish this match, they won’t be able to recognise who the corpse is that lies in the middle of the ring. It will be a bloody mess that used to be Lone$tar, but nobody will be sure. You will be so much of a mess you could be anyone, because I do that to people. I don’t hurt people; I destroy people; I kill people; I mutilate all-comers and you will be no exception this Sunday, when you lie on the ground a bloody shell of your former self.
