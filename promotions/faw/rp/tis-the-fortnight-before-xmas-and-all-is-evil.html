Tis the fortnight before xmas, and all is EVIL!

10/12/02
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Tis the fortnight before xmas, and all is EVIL! 9th Wonder Of Foam Furiously Addictive Wrestling Roleplay #96 Date: 10/12/02 Location: Evil Underground Lair …. The Foam Factory Basement …. (Deep in the heart of the Foam Factory lies an underground lair. Adorned on these walls are macaroni & cheese representations of the most EVIL people on the planet, Dr No, Scaramanga, Larry King, Saddam Hussein, Jaws, Jeremy Beadle, Don King, Godzilla, King Kong, and of course Big Bird from Sesame Street) (Sitting on an evil throne is the 9th Wonder Of Foam, he is arguing with Lars) Foamy: Look Lars, you can’t be my evil sidekick, evil genius’s do not have sidekicks, superhero’s do. If I do my evil strut into that arena Sunday with a sidekick I’ll be laughed out of the place. This Sunday night I resurrect my career as the most evil person in FAW. I will walk down that ramp and whoop some ass in that battle royal. I cannot do that with a sidekick. Lars: But I’d be the most cunning sidekick in history. Foamy: Look the only kid of sidekick evil geniuses have are the big fat dumb ones, but for that to happen I’d have to be a small genius and I am not small. Name me one successful evil genius to have a sidekick. Lars: The Penguin and Catwoman in Batman Returns Foamy: They were both equal. Lex Luther, Doc Croc, Dr No, The Riddler, Commodus from Gladiator, the shark from Jaws, Magneto all of them have evil henchmen, not sidekicks. Lars: Then what am I going to do? Foamy: Well my friend before James Harris get’s here I want you to change into something that will reveal your new role to the world. Meanwhile I am going to practice my EVIL laughs. (Lars trundles off, as Foamy attempts a variety of evil laughs) …. Sometime later …. (James Harris is brought into the evil lair Foamy sits on his throne thinking evil thoughts) James: Uh Foamy? It’s me Harris, James Harris Foamy: That’s Dr Foam, I’ve been expecting you James. (Foamy swivels around but falls off, he gets back up and waves his hand across James’s face) James: What was that about? Foamy: I just used the dark side of the force to blank that last moment from your mind. James: I see. Foamy: Would you like the tour of my evil underground lair? James: Ok then, but first of all why have you put this stamp on my hand when I entered? Foamy: Well when you go to clubs or gigs they stamp your hand with that bloody ink and it takes days to come off, I simply thought it was a piece of sheer evil genius so I thought I’d use it. James: So the tour? Foamy: Uh no, us evil Dr’s don’t walk, I’ll simply illuminate the lair from my seat using a system of lights and pullies. (Foamy presses a button) Foamy: Recoil in horror at my flesh eating shark pool! (A paddling pool is lit up, there are fish swimming around inside it) James: Ha! Foamy: Indeed, the nervous laugh of a scared man. James: No I really am laughing. Foamy: What? (Foamy turns around and spots the inflated Childs pool) Foamy: My first day and already my first clusterfuck. Lars!!!! (Lars walks from the shadows in a white furry suit with a tail and cat ears) Foamy: I thought I told you to walk like a cat! On all fours boy! (Lars gets down on all fours and meows) Foamy: That’s better. James: Why is he dressed as a cat? Foamy: Sadly I had no need for an evil sidekick so I mad him my evil genius pet, much like Dr No’s cat, Skeletor’s panther or Mumra’s pet hamster. James: I see Foamy: Lars, why on hell is my evil shark infested pool a Childs inflatable paddling pool filled with fish. Lars: Haddock Foamy. Foamy: Haddock? Lars: Yeah have you seen the price of Shark? Foamy: So Lars, I do in fact have a paddling pool of Evil filed with haddock? Lars: Yes. Foamy: Ok, then James Harris, be prepared to be amazed at my flesh burning laser beam! (Foamy flicks another light, there stands a man with a Wrestling t-shirt on holding a red light pen, and he flashes it in James eye) James: Ahh! Foamy: See it burns. James: No those things are dangerous! Foamy: So not even a small sensation of heat? James: Not really, but I’m very annoyed. Foamy: Hey that’ll do it’s my first day on the job. So you want to press ahead with the interview? James: Ok. So Foamy this Sunday you do battle in a Santa Claus battle royal, with the winner getting all the points from their group. Foamy: Hang on Santa Claus, what’s he got to do with anything? James: Everyone has to dress up as Santa. Foamy: Oh you’re yanking my chain! I am evil genius! I can’t be seen doing my evil bidding in a red fricking Santa costume! They’ll never take me seriously. James: So now after a small break your back in FAW, what’s the plan? Foamy: Put quite simply so that the FAW fans and wrestlers can understand I’m going to win every title there is and encase them in Foam, ohhhh ha ha ha! James: Is that it? Foamy: Also in doing so I will take over the world with a series of cunning yet evilly brilliant schemes, such as making sure that every single chocolate in boxes of chocolates are those coffee ones that every one leaves until last, to make sure that every single packet of crisps in the world has already been popped open, and most horrifyingly of all I’m going to eradicate the world of parking lines leaving all car parks in the world in a state of total anarchy! James: Uh I see. Any words for the fans of FAW and for the wrestler in the back? Foamy: A new evil force is coming boys, The Klown’s warnings were true but the evil comes not from the make-up box of a child’s entertainer, but from my mind the newest and only evil genius left on earth. Fans will wish to join my quest; the boys in the back will want to become my evil henchmen, and those who are left will be victim of the greatest work of evil since they decided to re-name Marathon bars to Snickers! Have a Foam Filled Day!
