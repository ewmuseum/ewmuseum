Costello chew on this

12/07/02
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Costello chew on this Spawn Furiously Addictive Wrestling Roleplay #92 Date: 12/07/02 Location: panic rp 1 (Run for your lives.) Spawn: 'This is not good news. Not for anyone. Especially not for you.' Spawn: 'But let’s face it Costello when something is on the line, I always come through. Just when I beat The Great 1, just when I beat one of the so called greats in Omega Fro, and just like I’ll do at Panic to you. See there’s a difference between you and I. I’ve already sealed my place as one of the best the FAW has to offer. I’ve overcame the obstacles that were thrown my way by Tom, and the corrupted staff. I’ve amounted to the best when I was giving nothing. I‘m not going to come out here and say I‘m going to kick your ass and take your pride. No, I‘m going to go in there, beat your ass win the match and exit the ring taking your pride with me to the locker room and dumping it in the trash. You will be left to think what is next for your career!” Spawn: 'I was just put into this match. What does that show you Costello? That just proves my point. I‘ve worked my way up Costello why you still have to earn your shot. I don‘t need to be in some pointless tournament. I‘m the best, see without me in this match it wouldn‘t be so high up on the card. Without me this match is boring and probably opening up the show. The man who makes the big show look good, I am the master of FAW. So I‘ll settle for the number one man, the best there is, so I can teach you Costello how to wrestle for about 15 minutes.' (There's that cocky smirk again. Truth is, if Spawn didn't spend most of his time smirking, his lips would fall off. No, really.) 'That bring‘s us to you being the idiot in this match, Costello. Not that anyone cares, really. But I digress.' 'No I could never have thought you would never fight me because I was better than you right Costello? That is pure and simple pure luck. But I guess it will just be luck this weekend when I hand you your own ass. Like I said before no one care’s that you’ve beaten the has beens of the FAW. When you step into the ring with me your stepping into a whole new kind of world. I would have thought it would have sunken in by now. But I guess all those chair shot’s you have taken in all of your matches gave a slight bout of amnesia.” (He rolls his eyes, and for a moment, considers putting them out, just to get rid of the mental image. This might be going a bit too far, however, so Spawn simply continues on with his little points.) 'Moving along. Costello you can rant and rave about how you’re better than me. But you have never beaten me. As a matter of fact if you would to fight me in a hardcore match, your style of match…..I am sure I pinned your ass in a hard-core match rather easily. I’ll beat you anywhere especially now that you are at your best. When you were riding high after just arriving to FAW, thinking you were invincible. Hadn’t even been beaten yet, until I came around you shall see. I know you will remember your first fall for a long time to come, you will mention it out of fear every time you open your mouth. You talk big but you fear me. You know even if you give it all you got it will not be enough. You will give it all your pathetic body had to give. But it won’t be good enough. It never will be Costello. I am superior to you and on Sunday I’ll prove it!” 'Costello keep hoping and thinking that you are going to walk to walk away with a victory over me. I’ve won the world title before and it’s time for Spawn to win it again. It doesn’t matter what kind of match were in my skill will always surpass yours.” (With this assertion of purpose, he takes another sip of piping hot java. Nothing goes together like editing technology and coffee.) (You ever notice how Spawn doesn't seem to do much in his promos anymore? He used to walk around, talk to people, threaten to jump off of bridges--all kinds of exciting stuff. But lately, all he does is stand in the same place and make facial expressions, mostly smirks. Maybe he's getting lazy these days. Maybe he just doesn't have to try.) (Yeah, that gets a great big grin.) 'Here's a hint, Costello; one that so many in the FAW have failed to learn. If you don't want to look completely stupid, then don't say completely stupid things. Come to think of it, you're probably best off not saying anything.' 'This time--this one time--I'm going to give you the benefit of the doubt, so to speak. I'm going to give you a chance, while I wouldn't give most people in your position the time of day. Here's a little bit of advice for you.' (Just when you thought Spawn didn't have an ounce of humanitarian in him.) 'Don't waste your time this week. Try to do something different; something interesting. Failing that, try not to do something stupid. I've had enough of that lately.' 'If you've got no staying power in people's memories, you're nothing in this business. Give me a reason to remember who you are.' 'Make me laugh. Make me hurt. Make me think of something when I hear your name, apart from misspelled tattoos and halitosis that can kill a yak and five hundred paces. Most importantly, Costello...' 'Make me care.' (At this point, a sardonic smile creeps across his face, after being repressed for far too long--two, maybe three minutes.) 'I'm not holding my breath, though.' (Coffee cup still in hand, he heads back toward the door which leads to the editing bay.) 'Now, if you'll excuse me, I've got more important things to take care of.' (And there he goes, to take care of them. Leaving the rest of us to wonder what Spawn has in store for Costello at Panic this Sunday...or even, if Spawn thinks he needs anything.) (Guess we'll find out, won't we?) [Fade to black.]
