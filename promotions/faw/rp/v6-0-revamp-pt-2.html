V6.0 (Revamp) (Pt.2) Violator Furiously Addictive Wrestling Roleplay #130 Date: 1/1/03 Location: Chicago, Illinois CONTINUED: (His eyes open, the blurred reality coming quickly into focus. He squints slightly, trying to gain some sense of balance, and through this he looks down towards a half empty glass. Shaking his head the bartender begins to come into focus along with the entire bar structure, and then the memories return, cold and unwavering. The bartender slides over to him, reaching towards his glass in an attempt to relinquish the remainder of the clear liquid. But his hand drops down swiftly, cutting the bartender off at the head, as a shocked expression slides across his face and the words come post haste.) Bartender: Calm down, clam down, I just figured that you had enough, being that you dosed off and all. Figure: I am fine. Bartender: Bull, you have been in here a lot lately, and I know from experience that anyone who comes in a place such as mine with that denomination of frequency has something more on his mind than just a good time. Now seeing as you are not drinking any hard liquor, why don’t you talk to me? Figure: How could you understand, how could you begin to know? As I not as I seem, whatever source you have encountered in the past pale in comparison to what I am, and what I do. I truly suggest you dare not enter into this world, because you may get yourself into a place that you are far from ready to encounter. (Stands from bar stool pulling a few bills from his pocket) Fear…reality. (The figure drops the bills on the counter and turns slowly heading through the sun engulfed doorway, with the bartender standing in completely awe, watching the dark figure step into the street, and disappear into the day. Outside he strides down the avenue, the sounds of drifting cars a small bug in his ear, his thoughts being the majority leader in this situation. Crossing the avenue and taking a sharp left he continues down the December Chicago streets, in a desperate struggle to bring his mind into focus.) Figure: January 5th, I can feel its presence surging through my like a volt of lightening. It is almost like pulse, the first breath of a newborn, giving life to a shell, foreshadowing the growth of a human being. Beautiful and terrifying all in one instance, it is like a sword piercing the soul, bringing reality a step closer to death. Only a select few can understand what I am and why I am, so for all others they can only contemplate, unaware of the power that beheld. Who am I, Shawn or Violator, I don’t know anymore. That I do know is that I am a warrior, fighting for the spirit of every solider, engaged in a battle with myself, and the world. (Violator looks towards the sky, the white clouds smothered in anticipation of a snowstorm. Suddenly they begin to move, forming in a cohesive unit. Violator stands awe struck for a moment, watching as they form a makeshift sword, a dragon encircling the blade. He blinks quickly and it has disappeared, leaving him standing with only the memory, and a doubt about his sanity. Looking back towards the city he continues on, a large car park looming only a single block away.) Violator: Reality playing tricks, testing and trudging at immortality far fetched at this very moment. Xing is the beginning, the first step in challenging that eternity, only he and I can understand that. I wait for him at the edge of uncertainty, calmly anticipating his arrival, calmly awaiting his demise. Does he take this match seriously; I don’t believe so, because he has no idea the extent of opposition standing opposite of him. A first match lost, not an option, not a possibility, only a dream for those bleak minded individuals unable to see the truth, unable to see destiny. (Violator enters the car park and promptly enters the elevator, watching as it ascends to the realm of the 7th floor. The door opens and as he comes to step from inside there standing in his wake is several samurai, all holding drawn blades only inches from his face. Once again the blinks come quickly, and again they disappear, leaving only Violator, and the questions. He cannot stress this factor long and continues journeying across the indoor parking lot, in search of his suitable means of transportation. (Several feet down there stands such a vehicle, it black and chrome interior shining in the artificial light. The sleek body of the infamous Lamborghini Diablo stands awaiting its master command, but also with an added incentive. A woman is present as well, leaning against the chrome driver side door watching as Violator continues towards his prize, the Rayban sunglasses shrouding his eyes in a cold darkness.) (Stepping to his vehicle immediately her presence is known, and he slides his long black trench coat back slightly to reveal a 24-inch blade, touching it softly along the lining with his palm.) Violator: I would suggest that you speak quickly and with added caution with your phrasing. Seeing that I have no idea who you are, and with short patience this could become a truly ugly situation. Woman: Maybe it always has. (She pushes herself away from the door and walks over to confront Violator, standing inches from his face with a confident smirk. He eyes seem locked on her position as she leans over and rubs herself against the blade, smiling and licking her lips viciously.) Woman: Beautiful blade, lucky someone who has gained the privilege of having it thrust into their body, feeling the cold steel melt their inside, knowing soon it would be the reaper of their wayward soul. Tell me, how many people have become pleasured by this weapon? Violator: Not enough considering the current situation. Other than the fact that you admire my Kika, what is your business here, and more so with me? Woman: Poignant, I like that in my man. My business is simply to warned you, and to let you understand that no matter how large you may believe you have become, you still must pay your debt, we all, must pay our debts. (A flash of light takes Violator by surprise and he grimaces backward for a moment regaining his composure quickly and coming to a point to confront his womanly opposition. But instead he finds nothingness, only his car standing dormant awaiting his arrival, and a mind filled with unanswered questions, becoming more and more tedious as the inquires continue in record numbers. “Click, Click” he car comes to life and the door swing open as Violator depresses and automatic lock and alarm remote control. He enters and once more depresses the remote control, bringing the car to life and closing the doors with one fell swoop. 3…2…1 the Diablo pulls from its vehicular slumber, slides from its parking spot, and barrels down the parking garage ramps, coming to a halt at the exit in only a matter of minutes. He swipes a gold 2 X 3 inch card across the reader and waits patiently for the security to rise, before bursting from the parking garage, quickly onto the streets of Chicago. Shooting down the avenue in speeds of 50 and above his body keeps the car in control, but his mind continues to flow a hundred miles a minutes, thinking about the future, thinking about the present, thinking about the man known as Xing.) Violator: Xing, he doesn’t want to make that leap of faith, not here, not now. If I have to I will bury him to get my point across, and in my eyes he shall be nothing more than a casualty of war, nothing more. I can fell it surging through my body, that championship electricity, that competitive drive that shall separate me from that rest, and define me as the standard by which all other are compared to. He calls himself “The Bodyguard” maybe aptly named yet who exactly is he protecting, and if so do they truly believe they are safe, especially if he is far from safe from someone the likes of myself. Xing shall fall, Foamy shall fall, and all others, who stand in my way, no one shall be safe, except for the people. The fans shall guide me to the promise land, and when I arrive I will look back, raise their position to a higher echelon by raising that standard of this company, and giving them life, and hope in the form of the World Heavyweight Champion…Violator. Violator: God only knows how long this day has been in the making, how long this match has stood in the stars. They find themselves humorous by placing this match as a “Welcome to FAW” battle, well I shall give them a true welcome, plain and simple in the form of F.U… forever unchained. Success is my only option, failure only an afterthought, and glory a three count away. Everyone has been sent here to do something to benefit the world and themselves, this…is the reason that I breathe, that I live. Xing wants to play this game, then I say…let’s play, let’s play indeed. (Violator cranks the stereo to 11 as “Name of the Game” by Crystal Method shakes the subwoofers, and Violator puts the petal to the metal, exploding from the main streets onto the Lake Shore Drive expressway. In the distance a figure watches, the ominous womanly being from his earlier encounter. She watches his vehicle disappears and then produces a cell phone, by which she begins a conversation with an unknown source, Violator car gone in the distance.) Woman: It has been set in motion, he is preparing himself. We shall see just how good he is in fact, and if he is what you say he is, I believe that we may have found your unicorn, our diamond in the rough. (Pauses) No sir, no he did not confront me to that magnitude, everything is in perfect harmony, yes, trust me. That match with Xing is scheduled as per the contract, I guess now there is nothing more to do but sit and wait. (Pauses) I understand, we shall be there prompt and set. Good day sir, good day. (She hands the phone up and smiles to herself as she looks back towards Violator past trails, he turns away from the expressway and begins to walk and just before entering the black Lexus Coupe turns her head and speaks sincerely with only the slightest hint of malice contained in her voice.) Female: We shall come to see exactly how good this Violator character truly is, and if he is all what he is cracked up to be, you may just have what we are looking for, Infinity’s warrior. (Turns to driver) Let’s go, we have a lot to prepare! TBC...
