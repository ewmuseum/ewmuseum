Crimson's Horizon

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Crimson's Horizon Crimson Lord Furiously Addictive Wrestling Roleplay #11 Date: 16/10/02 Location: Unknown (The scene fades into a FAW camera crew moving through a dimly lit hall-way that is covered with framed old magazines, title belts, trophies, and other various wrestling memorabilia. The crew keeps moving through until they find an exact door they were apparently looking for, a old, rusted steel mental ward door, with the word 'SOUL' scrolled across it. They knock on the door.) Voice from beyond door: Come in... (The cameraman reaches for a rusted door knob, and twists it. The door slowly creeks open, revealing a hazy, incense filled room. The crew makes their way inside and pans across a candle lit room, the smell of incense is near intoxicating. The camera catches a dark clothed man sitting in the middle of the room, in the centre of a huge candle lit Bathometry, meditating. The camera closes in on this mans face, they see the face of FAW's demented madman, Crimson Lord. He sits there, meditating, his eyes closed, in a deep concentration. The camera is still closed in, suddenly he smiles, and tilts his head towards the floor.) Crimson: Victory number one, the first leg of the gauntlet has been won, the first leg of the marathon has been run. Raza, I hope you enjoyed the little message I sent to you, at the expense of your little buddy Renegade last Friday. I hope you enjoyed his beating at the hands of Apoc, because Raza it was just the first of many. Viewers beware, the reign of blood and agony has only just begun. (Crimson opens his eyes, and stares at the candle lit floor in front of him, he merely grins.) Crimson: Now to the matters at hand, The Duke and I's tag team match Friday night. Could It be the rebirth of the Nation?...First lets touch on Renegade. (Pause) Crimson: Renegade, was that your name? Yeah, that's it. Renegade, Friday night your in for a treat, your in for a fight, your in for a war, I just hope your ready for a grade A onslaught. Why? Because, frankly I...along with The Duke, am going to pick you apart...piece by damn piece, until there's nothing left, but your bloody remains. To tell you the truth they might need to call in your family to make a positive I.D on your dead ass once I'm through with you. As I've stated time and time again, I show no mercy, I take no goddamned prisoners, so please don't beg for mercy...because your prayers will go unanswered. So please, do me that favor, I'd rather hear you scream, rather than beg, I would hope you'd like to show some dignity. Crimson: Renegade, your now in my sights, your just another asshole in the way of my main target, and I'll take you out just to get to your championship belts. Your in my way, and I won't stop at anything to get them, not even you will prevent me from my total glory. Renegade as far as I'm concerned, your dead...no questions asked... (Crimson looks to the camera as it closes in on his eyes, then just on his face.) Crimson: Now, Raza, a man who yes, sadly I never got to finish what we started. Yes, 'tis very sad indeed. And yes to answer your question, it is a little odd to meet someone you met awhile back, and to be face to face again later on in your career...indeed that is funny. But Raza, now that we finally meet head on again, you'll finally get to experience what everyone had said about me. All the myth, and legends that people spread about my insane, and no fear, take no prisoners attitude. Raza you finally get to see what everyone was talking about, why the veterans were afraid to step up to me, and why the rookies were all too bold, and foolish to attempt to dethrone me. Raza, your one of those bold ones, still young as far as your career goes, wanting to dethrone this king, to attempt to topple my kingdom. Well, frankly...it isn't going to happen my friend, not for a long time to come...until I decide it's over with. Crimson: Raza, like I said to your partner, I take no prisoners, I give no fucking mercy, so don’t expect me to lighten up for you, just because you knew me some time ago. Don't expect anything more than a simple execution. But wait...while were at something...how about this. If Duke and I win this match, let's say you give us a title shot at Disaster Down Under, for your Tag Team Championship. How about it Raza? Are you man enough to accept a challenge that Bayne pussied out on? Are you worthy of that goddamned title you have around your waist? Wait, lets not get to overzealous, lets just wait and see what happens on Friday, and from there...maybe I'll kick your head in again for your belt, that is...if your man enough to face me one on one. (Crimson laughs, with a hint of bitterness in it.) Crimson: The Nation is on the Horizon... Renegade and Raza, The Duke and I will see what the both of you are made up of on Friday. Whether it be steel or tin...we'll see, we shall truely see. Crimson: Renegade, Raza...that is why I'm not only your master, not only your ruler, hell...why I'm not only your god... (Crimson pauses, smiling slyly.) Crimson:...thats why I'm your fucking Savior! (Another short pause ensues.) Crimson: Now as for you Renegade, the clock has begun to tick away for you, I hope you enjoy the suspense, I know I will...Blessed Be… (Crimson laughs to himself as the camera slowly fades away, closed in on his piercing, Pure Blood Red eyes.)
