Canada: The 51st State

18/10/02
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Canada: The 51st State 9th Wonder Of Foam Furiously Addictive Wrestling Roleplay #24 Date: 18/10/02 Location: Foamy Hills - New Jersey …. Foamy Hills – Friday Afternoon …. (The Foamy One sits at his desk in his study reading a fax and stuff; Bob the mocking Bird is perched on his desk) Foamy: Look Bob, I’m not testing you again. Bob: I’ll not get into MENSA otherwise. Foamy: You really think you’re intelligent enough? Bob: Of course, intelligence in animals is underrated. Foamy: No Bob, it’s a myth. Name one animal that has ever made a scientific breakthrough by itself, as opposed to having 40,000 volts sent through its brain to test the latest Vegetarian Quorn burgers. Bob: Mice my dear boy, mice are the lowest of the low. Now sloth’s they have some brain power. Foamy: Name me one three-toed sloth who is a card-carrying member of MENSA? Bob: Uh… well, uhh shut up! Foamy: And cows, what dumbf**ks they are! If I was a cow the minute I saw that slaughterhouse sign I’d be making a run for it the minute they open the back of the van. (Bob grimaces, he then jumps off the table and flies back to his perch, Foamy looks down at the desk and finds a large bird turd steaming on the desk) Foamy: Charming. (Lars enters the office; he looks like he has had a severe lack of sleep) Foamy: Hey. Bob: Introducing Michael Jackson! Lars: What? Bob: Oh sorry, its just you look whiter than the average Caucasian male. Foamy: Didn’t get much sleep last night? Lars: Well you know I’ve been having those Zombie nightmares? Foamy: Indeed. Lars: And you suggested that I rent Invasion Of the Body Snatchers to take my mind of things? Foamy: Indeed. Lars: Well why did I awake in the middle of the night to find a giant seedpod next to my bed? Was it you? Foamy: (He laughs) quite probably. (Foamy’s intercom turns on, Floyd Rotterdam speaks) Floyd: It’s me let me up. Foamy: Ah Melissa Farrow. Lars: I think its Floyd. Foamy: Ah Melissa Farrow. …. 10 Minutes later …. (Floyd opens the door to Foamy’s study) Foamy: Ah hello Melissa please enter. Floyd: No it’s me Floyd Rotterdam, Melissa had an appointment at her gynaecologists. Foamy: I think not Melissa, I think you had a bad night and can let yourself go because you’re only interviewing your old friend Foamy. But no Melissa in the back I have a spare one of your rubber dresses from that time you stayed the night, go through to my bedroom and pick it up. …. 4 Minutes & a lot of powder later …. (Floyd stands awkwardly in high heels and a red rubber suit; he then begins to interview Foamy) Floyd: So um Foamy Damien Ryan accuses you of thinking Damien to be an idiot and an easily defeated opponent. Foamy: Ah Melissa there is something not quite right about you, sleep it off and comeback tomorrow. (Floyd gets up to leave) Foamy: Hang on woman, you may not look 100% but I have some words for Damien Ryan. Now I simply said that I thought that given the chance I could give Damien a good run for his money and yes, probably come out of any match Hardcore Champion, never did I say that he sucked, but what he gets up to in the privacy of his own home is his business, whether he sucks, swallows, spits, blows, bites, nibbles, tickles or inserts that’s nothing to do with me. Floyd: hmm, Ok moving on. He also states that you said he was not main event material and that he shouldn’t be Hardcore champion. Foamy: When? Can Damien Ryan stand up and show me some video evidence of me clearly stating that? Damien Ryan may indeed be main event material that is not my place to decide, he should be Hardcore Champion because he rightfully defeated the Hardcore Champion at that time, I merely commented that given the chance I could take that title off him. Floyd: Damien Ryan is undefeated, does that perhaps give you reason to doubt victory? Foamy: Sure enough he is yet to be defeated, but everything comes to and end, be it a title reign, a life, or even a winning streak and there is always a time and a place for that kind of thing and FAW’s Distortion is a good a place as any to end that kind of thing. Floyd: He also says that he would be willing to put the title on the line but that’s just not possible, he also says that he would put the title around Britney Spears and make love to her to defend the title. Foamy: That’s it? The furthest he would be willing to go to defend his title would be to have sex with a singer, and a bad one at that? If you had done your homework Floyd you would also note that Damien Ryan said that the FAW Hardcore Title means shit to him, and that he doesn’t give a shit if he loses it. I’d take comfort in that If I was facing him at the next Pay Per View Extravaganza, Damien clearly has no passion and no love for the title, and any good champion should want to cling to his Championship not matter its name or status as hard as possible. Floyd: So wh… Foamy: Oh wait a minute Floyd. He also tells me ahead of time that if he loses the belt he’ll move on. If that is his ethos for every match then good, he should be able to brush off defeat as easily as he brushes off his Canadian dandruff in the morning but only after a defeat. It is clear that Damien Ryan is already prepared for defeat, and that he doesn’t really seem to care about our little match. A good wrestler will brush off defeat only after it has happened but one who readily accepts defeat before it occurs has exploitable doubts in their head, and Damien doubts his own ability to keep hold of the Hardcore Title and he also doubts his ability to win, which is why he likes to cast himself as the underdog. Floyd: Rightly so don’t you think? He accuses you of underestimating him. Foamy: You don’t think Damien that I’ve never been under-estimated in my career? You don’t think the minute I signed up for the Golden Turnbuckle that I wasn’t laughed out of the building that first night? But who came through to the last sixteen in that tournament? I had people calling for me to quit because I was bringing the Tournament down into the gutter with my antics but I fought adversity. I’ve fought so hard that last Sunday night Spawn felt too afraid to fight me man on man. Since I made a fool of Spawn at Entrapment he has been too afraid to face me on a fair match, he brought Apocalypse down and made him to the job for him, he didn’t want to risk the title, that is how afraid our FAW champion is of losing to me. So Damien perhaps you should overestimate my threat because I am no longer the underdog, I am a rabid, blood thirsty dog ready to tear the flesh from any fresh piece of meat I am handed. Floyd: Damien also says that he will deal with you the Canadian way? Foamy: The Canadian way? Making me listen to the cultural equivalent of the bubonic plague by making me listen to Celine Dion & Nickleback? Is the Canadian way making me overrun by the French? Is the Canadian way making me bow down to another countries Queen as my new ruler? Or is it Damien beating me so bad that people refer to me as that Country next to America? Floyd: Last words? Foamy: Damien you obviously don’t get iconography, my ladder fall was a sign, a metaphor for something else which you shouldn’t concern yourself with, I wasn’t actually trying to climb a ladder, the ladder stood for something which most have missed. But Damien be it Ladder, cage, fish tank barbwire, or Hardcore match I can beat you. But let me leave you with this thought, our own World Champion won’t even face me in a one on one title match, so perhaps you should count yourself lucky that only your body is on the line and not nothing else because I fear just like Spawn you will do everything you can to avoid fighting me. Have a Foam Filled Day!
