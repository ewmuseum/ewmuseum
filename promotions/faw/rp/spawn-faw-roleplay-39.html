Spawn — FAW Roleplay #39

captured 19 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
SpAwN--The Hell Master \~~~~~~~(opening scene)The scene opens in Boston it is midnight and it is cold and very dark there is a black car that pulls over the door opens and out of the car a midgets is thrown he is hurt bad bleeding a lot the midget gets up and and starts to scream at the people in the car they then get out of the car and shoot him and drive off in a hurry. The scene then shows the next morning and we see Spawn just getting out of bed he goes to the bathroom and begins to wash and clean up. When he finishes he goes to the living room and turns the TV on he changes the channel until he reaches the news. He sit s down and start to listen to the news. TV: Hello every one and welcome to CNN news channel our top story today is a man was found in the park today shot in cold blood, police think it was a mob hit from a long time deal gone bad. Cops are investigating the crime as we speak. The man height and weight are unknown but we have a picture of him ( the tv shows the picture) Spawn: Holly shit! I better call Mike. Spawn gets on the phone and start to call Mike , Mike answers and thy begin to speak. Spawn: Hey Mike check this out man I want you to turn the tv to channel 6 and check out the news you are going to find this very interesting. Mike: What is it? What can be so important? Spawn: Just wait till it comes back again you will see it will shock you. The scene shows Mike still waiting for the big news Spawn is talking about to hit. After a few minutes it shows it again and Mike goes into a shock. Mike then begins to talk to Spawn. Mike: You have got to be kidding me is this for real? Spawn: Yes it is and it couldn't have happened at the wrong time with me coming to fight JLC and all this does not look good. What should we do? Mike: I don't know maybe we should go to JLC's house and see if one of his midgets is missing. Spawn: Come on Mike that is the dumbest thing I have ever heard. What I hope they do not think I have something to do with it since I am fighting JLC this Sunday. Mike: That's true and you know what the guys that did it had a black car just like ours. Spawn: Yeap that is another bad sign let's hope everything turns out good for us. Ok Mike I will talk to you later. Bye, bye. Spawn hangs up the phone and begins to get dress as he finishes getting dress he hears his doorbell he goes to open the door and see two cops standing there they start to speak. cop 1: Are you Spawn the wrestler? Spawn: Yes I am and what can I do for you officer? cop 1: We need you to come to the station with us for some questioning this will not take long, Spawn agrees to go with the cops . The scene shows Spawn arriving at the station, he is taken to the morgue were he is shown the body of the man that got killed, he stares at it as they are asking him questions. cop 1: (pulling the sheets to reveal the man) Do you recognize this man? Spawn: Never seen him in my life. The cops cover the body with the sheets and take Spawn upstairs to an empty room they tell him to seat down and begin to ask him question. cop 2: Ok lets start from the beginning shall we. Were, were at midnight yesterday? Spawn: I was in my house sleeping I had just come home from a workout at the gym and I was tired so I went straight to bed. cop 2: At around what time did you arrive to your house. Spawn: It must have been at least ten or eleven at night. cop 1: Do you always work out late at night? Spawn: Hey you know what I resent these line of questioning what are you trying to get at? cop 2: Don't worry this is just routine stuff, we will be done in no time so just sit still and you can be on your way. cop 1: Were you alone when you went for the gym or did you go with a friend? Spawn: You guys are fucking insane! I am out of here. cop 1: It's true you can leave but you will be let known not to leave the country. Spawn: Why am I a suspect because if you do not have nothing to hold then I am out of here. cop 1: You can leave sir but we will tell you that we will be in touch. Spawn gets up from the table and leave the station. The scene shows Spawn arriving at his house he quickly goes in and picks up the phone and calls Mike. Spawn: Hey Mike did the cops come to see you too? Mike: Yeah they did, they ask me all kinds of question but they didn't have anything on me, so they let me go and they told me not to leave the country. Is that what they did to you also? Spawn: Yeah man they did the nerve of them, I told them I didn't have nothing to do with it, I don't even know the guy. So they had to let me go but they reallly pissed the fuck out of me. Mike: Don't worry about it you know how cops are always trying to change the system, you have to outsmart them all of the time. Spawn: You think they will come again? Mike: I don't know maybe they might who's to say the way the cops work now and days we will just be prepared is all. Spawn: All of this because of the bar fight with the midgets. I tell you this fight with JLC have brought me nothing but headaches man I feel the faster I defeat him the better it is for me. Mike: I don't know about that Spawn the way he has been cutting promos I believe this time you have your work will be much harder this time. Spawn: Yeah it will be but I know I can take him I have beaten the best all of these sons of bitches can't touch me I am just to fuckin good, and JLC is never gonna know what hit him this Sunday, so he is the one that better be prepared or else his ass is going to get smoked. Mike: Oh you don't have to worry about smoking his ass the great1 is always smoking it, you know how it is with these secret lovers. Spawn: Yeah that's true it's a shame how those cops could think that we would kill a midget like if we were crazy or something you know what I mean. As Spawn and Mike continue talking on the phone the doorbell rings and Spawn tells Mike to hold on as he gets the door,when he opens the door the same two cops from earlier are standing there and they begin to talk to Spawn. cop 1: Spawn you are under arrest for murder. Spawn: What the fuck you have gots to be kidding me right? cop 2: This is not a joke you can come peacefully or we will take you by force. Spawn: Those are my two choices well I got news for you two guys I will not go peacefully for a crime I did not commit. As the two cops go to grab Spawn, Spawn takes a swing at one of them hitting him in the jaw, he then kicks the other one in the groin and leaves his house he start to run as fast as he can , knowing in his mind that if he gets caught it will be all over for him, he runs fast as fast as he can, it is the first time that he has ever run from anything in his life. But then just as Spawn was about to make out it the two cops that are chasing him had called for backup, as Spawn turns the corner of the park the midget was shot he slips and a bunch of cops jump on him and grab him he struggles to free himself but there is too many of them and he is subdue. They stand him up and take him to the paddy wagon as he is pushed inside he hits his head and gets knocked unconscious. The scene shows Spawn waking up but on his bed he looks up at the ceiling gets up from the bed and realizes it was just a dream. He cleans up and gets on the phone, to call Mike. Spawn: Hey Mike let me ask you something? Anything strange or unusual happen in the news today? Mike: Not as far as I know Spawn everything has been well rather the same here in the news nothing same old bullshit. Why you ask? Spawn: Never mind Mike but you know what in my mind JLC is really in trouble now because I am more pissed of than I ever was. All I have to say is he better prepare for war. And let the better man win which will be me. The outcome will be the same as always with my hands being raised in the air in total victory. Well Mike I have to go so I will see you soon bye, bye. Spawn hangs up the phone as the scene fades to black.
