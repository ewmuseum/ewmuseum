The Great One Prepares The Great One Furiously Addictive Wrestling Roleplay #86 Date: December 4th 2002 Location: Gym As winter approaches and the weather gets wetter and colder and the nights get shorter and darker, your morale starts to drop and your overall happiness comes down. Christmas for some people puts the smile back on your face but for others it makes you even more miserable. Myers has changed the face of FAW after his first event in charge and has created a new set of matches that should make FAW better than ever and more competitive than ever. With four groups of five and each man in that group working together to get points but against each other so that they can become the number one contender for the undisputed title, things are looking up. Some wrestlers are still taking time off but The Great One has been working since the second he found out about his match with Spawn and did not stop training after he defeated him. He took a week long break which involved downing alcohol and junk food, (lots of it), which is something he hasn’t done in a long time. Now he plans on shredding off those two pounds that he acquired over that week off by working harder than ever because this match with The Omega Fro could either back up his claims of him being the greatest FAW superstar to ever enter the federation, or damage his reputation, shut him up and crush his ego. If he loses to The Omega Fro then he will have a lot of explaining to do and have to come up with a damn good excuse for the loss. As buckets of sweat pour down like rain from The Great One’s head and arm pits, and he gasps at the rate of a gale force wind from the hard schedule that his trainer is putting him through, he takes a swig from his water bottle and swallows a mouth full of the one litre bottle and pours the rest over his head and body, which is looking better, stronger and more muscular than ever. Great One sits down on a hard wooden bench at the side of the gym as his trainer stands next to him hands on hips looking disappointed. His stopwatch that is draped around his neck is taken in his right hand and it reads “31:27:56” and brings a frown to the trainers face. Trainer: Is that all you’ve got pussy? Thirty-one minutes and twenty-seven seconds is a dismal amount of time for a workout. I have watched the television and seen people do what you just did for seven hours without hardly breaking into a sweat. You know what I am going to do about that? You’re too knackered to answer me well then fine I’m going to do worse. I’m going to go over to that stereo and I’m going to play Justin Timberlake’s new song. The Great One: No, no please, anything but that, I’ll do anything. I’ll work harder than you’ve ever seen me work before, I promise. Trainer: Good. The Great One: Damn it the music industry has gone downhill over the last few years. I wasn’t really bothered about it but the last three weeks since FAW announced its closure it really made me look at all the crap, disgusting and appalling things that go on in the world, music being one of them. You got all that shit music that is promoted and talentless little bastards get loads of fame and money and recognition for it and they don’t do shit. All that teeny bopping disposable crap that is in the charts and ruining the world. And pornography is all over the place, that’s disgusting. And… Trainer: Hey man, take a look at this. The trainer holds up a copy of playboy with an absolutely gorgeous super-model posing for pictures. Great One moves over and grabs it off him with both hands and opens his eyes wider than he has ever opened them before. The Great One: Damn, she is fine and those buns are huge! What I would do to get to play with those puppies, damn. Trainer: You do know that you just totally contradicted yourself don’t you? The Great One: How do you mean? Great One talks to his trainer while still having his eyes attached to the pages of the magazine. Trainer: Well you just said that pornography is filling the world and it’s disgusting, yadda, yadda, yadda, and now you are ogling that woman’s breasts. The Great One: Damn it man you tricked me. Oh who cares what I say, all I do is talk trash anyway, half the time I don’t know what I’m saying, it’s just comes out. I am a role model and I know it’s not right for kids to be looking at porn and shit and everything else, which is why I try and behave myself all the time, especially when I am going be on TV. Right now I’m not on TV or anything so I can look at this amazing magazine. Trainer: You might not be on TV but you’re going to be in the paper probably. The Great One: Oh yeah, why’s that? Trainer: Because of that photographer down there who just took a photo of you looking at that porn mag. The Great One: Oh so what, everyone looks at it. Trainer: But you just said that… The Great One: Most young kids don’t pay attention to what they see in the papers anyway, most don’t even read them. Trainer: Maybe so but these days all kids go on the Internet. There is a slight pause and then Great One’s head moves upwards and out from the magazine. The Great One: Shit. Trainer starts to laugh. Trainer: Yep, it’s going to be all over the Internet in fifteen minutes. The Great One: Oh well, I’ll just provide a good piece of role model material by showing the world how to kick the crap out of a yoghurt for the third singles match in your career, and the fourth match that we have actually been in together by sending The Omega Fro straight to Greatness. What the hell is he doing back anyway? That guy has returned to FAW more times than Pamela Anderson went back to Tommy Lee! Trainer: I guess he wanted to come back, and this time it could be very successful. He might have taken some time out but I bet he’s fit and I bet he’s been training hard in anticipation for this moment. The Omega Fro is a two time former FAW World champion and he knows what he’s doing. The Great One: So what if he’s a two time former FAW World Champion, I’ve beaten him twice in singles competition and beaten him and Skipster together in a handicap match. I am just simply better than The Omega Fro. I might not be a two time world champion, but I am the king of the world, the greatest man to ever step into a wrestling ring and simply the best. Trainer: That attitude will get you nowhere. Now put the porn down and get on the treadmill. I’m going to put it up to the fastest option so you better be ready. The Great One: Oh I’m ready. Trainer: Well then put the porn down and get on it. The Great One: Can’t I run and look at the magazine at the same time? Trainer: Put the porn down. The Great One: I am a highly skilled individual and can do two things at once with ease. In this case I’ll be running and looking at porn. Oh wait, I’ll be doing three things because I’ll be jack… Trainer grabs the porn but Great One has a tight grip on it. Trainer: That’s enough, now put the porn down. The Great One: I guess you could staple it to the wall and I could look at it though. Trainer now gets very angry and raises his voice, yelling at Great One. Trainer: GIVE ME THE FUCKING PORN! A woman who was walking through the gym at the time suddenly stops and looks towards them looking very scared and disgusted but quickly walks away from these two weirdoes that she has just encountered. Great One looks very alarmed and frightened, frightened maybe sarcastically, and releases his hold on the magazine. Trainer: You know what, I will staple it to the wall in front of the treadmill, and then when you are on it you will run harder and faster towards it because you will want to get to it won’t you. The Great One: That’s a good idea. Glad I thought of it. Trainer: I can’t be arsed to argue with you. Just get on the damn treadmill while I get some blue tack; that should be easier than having to find a stapler. Great One gets on the treadmill and starts at a slow pace, gradually increasing the speed. Trainer walks back in after about a minute with some blue tack. Trainer: I got some, now you can really work hard. GO! The Great One: You know what I was saying earlier, about the music industry and everything? Trainer: Yeah. The Great One: Well I really couldn’t give two shits about it. But FAW, like the music industry, has gone downhill and it’s going to be an uphill battle. Trainer: Yes that’s correct. The Great One: It’s going to be a tough uphill battle that is going to be hard to win. Trainer: Correct again. The Great One: But it’s a battle that we will win because we always defy the odds and we always come up trumps, we always win, we have to. Trainer: Possibly. The Great One: No, not possibly, definitely. Definitely just like it’s definite that I am going to beat The Omega Fro at Panic on Sunday. Trainer: Usually I would say to people that it’s good to convince yourself that you are going to win and have that kind of confidence, but you are way over confident, and way too cocky. The Great One: But when you are as good as me you can be cocky. And it’s not cocky if you can back it up and back it up I can. Did I not say that I was going to finish Spawn off and finally get my revenge for what he tried to do in February? Trainer: Yes but… The Great One: But nothing. I said it and I did it. I act cocky and like I have an ego that could fill the Whitehouse and it’s like I always hit my head on the door because it is so damn inflated, but if I didn’t think I could back it up then I wouldn’t do it. I say what I say because I dare to say it because I know that I won’t regret it afterwards because I know that I can do exactly what I say, when exactly when I say it. Trainer: Have you been watching Matilda? The Great One: What gives you that impression? Trainer: Then when you said when exactly when I say it, it’s taken from Matilda. The Great One: In don’t remember hearing it. Trainer: Well, Danny De Vito says it to Matilda. The Great One: You are one sad, sad little man. What the hell are you doing watching it? Trainer: I have a seven year old daughter and she has the video alright. The Great One: Sure, sure, you just say that. Your seven your old daughter wouldn’t happen to be stuffed would it? Like you seven year old daughter doesn’t happen to be a dolly does she? Because I you are watching Matilda then you probably buy dolls as well, and not just ordinary dolls, you have blow up dolls as well don’t you that have holes in them in certain places that you can put things in. I don’t mean a blow up doll pencil holder either. Trainer: Shut up now before I pull the magazine down! The Great One: Sorry, I was only having a bit of fun. Trainer: Hahaha, so was I, got you. The Great One: Man, you tricked me again. Trainer: Yeah, that’s twice, and you cal yourself a practical joker who always plays tricks on people. Well that’s two I’ve gotten you with, so I guess that makes me the king of the tricks, eh? The Great One: You’re the king of something that rhymes with tricks! Trainer: Why thank you, it is quite big to be honest. The Great One: I just can’t win with you today can I? Trainer: Nope. But anyway, what do you think about this new system in FAW? The groups thing? The Great One: I’m not sure to be honest. I’d like to see where it goes and even though I think Myers is the biggest jimima’s crap to ever be shitted out of a jimima, he could make it work. From what I hear it’s had a lot of good feedback from fans and other members of the fed, so even though I doubt anybody likes him from what he has said to us all so far, I think they respect what he is trying to do for this company and like his ideas. I’ll give it a shot and see what happens. I’m wondering what the outcome of it all will be though, like if it’s done like the champions league or something. Like you have the four groups, and the two people who finish first and second in each group goes through to the next round of the title tournament and it then goes into knockout stages where the winners of the group are in one hat, the runners up in another, and they draw one guy from one hat and one guy from the other so all of the matches are winner vs. runner up. You go on and the winner of the whole thing is crowned the World Champion. The people who finish third all go into a match as well for the international title, and the people who finish fourth and last all get put into another knockout tournament for the hardcore belt. Trainer: I don’t know what will happen with it to be honest but I think it’s a really good idea. Myers seems like a really intelligent person and someone who could take FAW to the top. The Great One: Yeah, even though I came this close (puts index finger and thumb an inch apart) to knocking his lights out on Sunday, I have to agree with you. And with four owners, well, that’s a lot of heads and lot of ideas, it’s just a case if they can all agree to things and if they all get along with Myers which seems like a very hard task. Trainer: You really have it in for him don’t you? The Great One: Yeah, but not as much as I have it in for Omega Fro, he needs a slapping. He keeps coming back for more and just won’t go away. It’s like a dog. You give a dog you see in the streets a biscuit and then he follows you and keeps coming back for more. I gave Omega Fro a beating and he keeps coming back for more. He just won’t leave me alone. Why he loves to get in the ring with me so much I don’t know, but I know that when we do get in the ring together we put on a show. Well we should put on a show really because we’re both from Los Angeles and so we both know about Hollywood and how to put on a show, especially me. Trainer: If you were gay who would your perfect partner be? The Great One: Well that’s easy, myself. Trainer: I thought you might say that. The Great One: And I thought you might think I would say that, and that’s why I said it. Trainer: Yeah whatever. The Great One: I told you I could do it at this top speed. Trainer: Oh you’re not at top speed, not by a long shot. The Great One: Yes I am, look, it says there top speed (he points to the part that says top speed) Trainer: When did you put it on top speed? I know I didn’t. The Great One: Just after you came back in with the blue tack. Trainer: So you’ve been running it at top speed for the last ten minutes? The Great One: Yeah, and it’s no problem like I said. Trainer: Oh, you’re fitter than I thought. Maybe I should make you hold a hundred pound log over your shoulders as well? The Great One: That’s ok man take it one step at a time. Why don’t you go bug somebody else for a second huh? Trainer: I don’t consider myself to be bugging you and in any case, there isn’t anyone else to bug. The Great One: There must be like a fly or something somewhere in here, go bug it. Trainer: Oh, I see what you’re saying, ha ha, very funny. The Great One: Wow, I’m not even trying to be funny and I am, damn I’m good. Trainer: So you, Omega Fro, Sunday, what are your tactics? The Great One: Well first I’m going to go the ring and wait for him. Then when he gets in the ring I’m going to tie my shoe. Then I’ll ask a member of the audience for a comb and brush my hair. Then when Fro starts the match and tries to hurt me I’ll rearrange my pants and take a ten minute nap. I think this match should give me enough time to kill ten minutes by sleeping, I mean it’s not as if Fro is going to actually do anything to me so I should be able to take a quick nap in the middle of the match. That reminds me, I’ll have to take in an alarm clock because Fro’s punches and kicks and moves won’t be able to wake me up because I won’t feel a thing. Then when I awake from my nap, I’ll ask for a bar of soap and a glass of water and have a quick wash. Then I’ll look Fro in the eyes, then look at my right hand, then slap the taste of his mouth and knock him the hell down, to the ground. Then I’ll pick that son’bitch up, slap him around for a bit, give him a couple of bitch slaps you know, just to soften him up a little, then I’ll take a quick bathroom break and use Fro as the bathroom, that’s right I’ll piss all over him, and then making sure I don’t come in contact with any of the piss on his body, I’ll do a very nice thing and help that son of a bitch to greatness with one swift movement. Then I’ll cover him for three seconds and pick up the win. It’s that simple. Trainer: I am ashamed to be your trainer. The Great One: Not when I beat the hell out of Fro and then go on to win my group and then go on to lift the FAW World Title, which I will sure as hell do because I have me and all of my billions of fans around the world behind me. Every single person on the planet will be chanting my name and giving me energy, energy which I will use to clench my fist and punch Fro in the mouth, energy which I will use and suplex Fro to the mat, energy which I will use to go home later and pick up some fine ass bitch and give her one hell of a time. Trainer: Oh you’re going to Disney land? The Great One: The Great One tells you to put a chicken it you fat son of a bitch. You know what, screw this, you’re the one that needs the damn exorcise, you get on the freaking treadmill bitch. Trainer: What!? I don’t want to… The Great One jumps off the treadmill and pushes him on it and the trainer has to run as fast as he can to keep up with the treadmill. Trainer: I don’t need to be doing this, you do! The Great One: I have seen the size of your trousers increase by inches over the last few months and you need to lose weight, now come on run, run Forest, run! Trainer: Get me off of this thing. The Great One: You told me to get on it and run for my life, now it’s your time. You’re the student and I’m the trainer, now come on get those knees up. Trainer: I don’t want to get my knees up, I’m not supposed to do this, I’m the trainer, I tell you what to do and watch while you tire yourself out. The Great One: Oh come on bitch put some damn effort into it! I’ve seen three your olds who can run better and faster than you. You disgust me, you are a disgrace, I am appalled to have you as a trainer. Trainer: Wahhhhhhhh! Trainer can’t keep with the speed and goes flying backwards off the treadmill, banging his head. Great One gulps. The Great One: Paul? Paul, you are ok? Paul wake up. Trainer stays down, out cold, on the floor. Great One picks him up and slings trainers 200 pound body over his shoulder and takes him to the medical room. Nurse: What happened here? The Great One: Well, he fell off the treadmill because it was going too fast for him; it was his own fault. Nurse: Give me that glass of water there please. The Great One: Sure. Great One hands her the water and the nurse throws it over the face of the trainer and he wakes up. Trainer: What, what, what, where, where am I? Nurse: You’re in the medical room Paul; you bumped your head that’s all. Trainer blinks for a moment trying to remember what happens. The Great One: Yeah man, you really didn’t need to show me how to do the treadmill, I could have managed. Trainer: YOU! Trainer leaps off the bed and runs after Great One who just ran out of the room. The Great One: You should thank me. For one I’ve given you a bit of exorcise for once and I could have done a lot worse. I could have done a demonstration on you of what I’m going to do to Fro. Trainer: You’re not going to be able to touch Fro after what I do to you. The Great One: What the hell am I running for? Great One stops but Trainer can’t stop and runs straight into Great One and falls over. The Great One: What the hell is the matter with you man? You not going to be chasing me. I’m the star all right, I’m the God! Now if you want to chase me and beat up on me, then you’re going to have to go through me to do it and you can’t go through me because I’m the next FAW World Champion, I’m the current king of the universe. Now I’ve had enough for one day and I cannot be arsed to train anymore. I am going home because my daughter is coming round later and want to spend some quality time with her. Now I’ll see you later. Oh, and you might want to get an ice pack for your head, that’s going to come up in a lump! Bye Paul. Great One walks towards his locker room to take a shower and go home while trainer Paul goes to get an ice pack for his head.
