BOMBER AN EASY PREY

10/15/02
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
BOMBER AN EASY PREY Spawn Furiously Addictive Wrestling Roleplay #8 Date: 10/15/02 Location: DISTORTION RP 3 The scene opens to a man lifting a barbell inside of a gym. As we approach him we come to realize that it is Spawn. He is wearing a pair of blue shorts with a white tank top as well as a pair of Reeboks. He lifts the weight about ten more times while the camera remains on him and then sits it back in its place. Spawn leans up on the bench and wipes some sweat from his face. The camera is still on him and now Spawn sees it. He waits a moment and then begins to speak. Spawn: So now the spotlight has been thrust back upon me as I make my name rise higher for this company. I have conquer and the center stage is mine immediately. Yes, I have conquer from a brief slow pace that I was in and I have returned with visions of grandeur. There is a lust inside me for a prize that I have never had in my possession. I speak of the legends Title. A title that has never been around the waist of the one man that deserves it more than anyone. It has instead been fed to washed up old men that have to cheat to win as well as fan favorites with not even half of my wrestling skill. I should be used to this by now but I am still not. It was the same way when I held the world Title. My battles over that belt were more hard fought and more meaningful than the men that competed for the richest prize in the game. Nothing has changed now. Sure there are great matches fought occasionally for that belt, but I was consistent. I gave a great match every night out. I made the Title worth fighting for. I gave everything to keep it and when someone won the belt from me, they won it fair and square and with honor. Such is not the case with the legend Title. Well, that has to change and change quickly. My sights are set on the legendary status and FAW. That will soon be mine. Spawn pauses for a moment and then continues to speak a few seconds later. Spawn: Until then there is other business to take care of. There are other obstacles, which have been laid in my path that must be overcome. The first of these is Bomber, who I will fight in the main event of Distortion. I will be stepping into the ring with him in a one on one match for the first time in months. It will be just him and me in a great wrestling match. It seems we do not see many of them these days. This Sunday we will see an epic, but not an Evans Epic, one of my own. Sunday night will be a scene that will become very familiar as the weeks roll by. It will be one of my standing in victory over a defeated opponent. This will ultimately lead to a legendary match and a new era in professional wrestling. The wheels will be laid in motion this Sunday at Distortion. For a second time Spawn pauses for a bit and looks around the gym. His attention turns back to the camera seconds later as he begins to speak again. Spawn: I am very familiar with my opponent for Distortion. Bomber and I have been in the ring together before, in war against each other and as teamates. I too remember the TLC match like it was yesterday. That was a long time ago and we have both gotten better since then. We are undoubtedly the two best technicians in this company. We are set to square off in a match this Sunday that will determine just who is the better. The outcome has yet to be decided, but should be quite obvious. As good of a wrestler that he is, there is one better. That man is I. I am the one person that could beat him in a straight up fair wrestling match. That is what will go down this Sunday and the result will be myself walking out the victor of a well-fought contest. It will be me walking away with all of the glory as I usually do. A hard fought match will end with the better wrestler walking away with the win. It is really that simple. he is a great wrestler, but I am perhaps the one person in this company that can outwrestle him. I can outwrestle anyone in the world. he will soon figure this out. he'll soon learn that I can make even the most accomplished ring technician look like an idiot. Again there is a slight pause from Spawn and then he begins to speak again. Spawn: Well now that all that pleasantries crap is out of the way I can get down to business. It seems no matter who the hell I fight someone always brings up the fact that I was much better before I snapped. They lecture me on how I had it all before I turned and now I am a shell of my former greatness. They seem to forget that turning was what got me a match with Foamy and Xander. It got me noticed. Sure, I was great before, but nobody cared. I got title shots and I got screwed out of them left and right. I was nice to everyone and it got me jack shit. Now I have taken matters into my own hands and I am determined to get to the top on my own. I do not need anybody, including the fans. They stabbed the knife in my back once and it won't happen again. Once more Spawn pauses only this time it is longer as he walks a few feet over and grabs a can of Mountain Dew from his cooler. He walks back to the camera and begins to speak only seconds later. Spawn: Bomber also brings up the same sob story that both Xander and Foamy threw at me. They say that I inspired them with my reign as the World Champion. I inspired Bomber to come back. I taught Prayer that the person makes the belt. I gained the respect of Fro. That was all well and good, but being everyone's buddy was getting pretty old and it was not doing me any favors. I took matters into my own hands and now I am looking out for me and me alone. There is another pause from Spawn as he pops the top of his can of pop and takes a drink of it. He then looks back at the camera and begins to speak again. Spawn: Bomber has part of the story wrong. he says that I snapped after I lost my shot at the World Heavyweight Title. That was indeed a miscarriage of justice but it was not the thing that pushed me. As much as I wanted revenge for that it was the return of Tony V to this company and the immediate fan idolization of him despite him not doing anything of worth to deserve it that pissed me off. They left me and went to him after all I had been through with them. They left in a split second when Tony V came back. So I taught Tony V a lesson and actually proved all the fans wrong. I beat Tony V fair and square in the middle of the ring. I proved that he did not deserve the respect that they gave him and that he was washed up and they still did not give a damn. They still ignored me. I will not be overlooked any longer. This Sunday starts the road towards a new era in wrestling history. It all begins with you Bomber. There is a pause from Spawn and then he begins to speak to us once again. Spawn: You seem to forget that the man makes the belt; the belt does not make the man. The World Heavyweight Title has lost some of its value with the screwy title changes and the bogus title wins of past champions. Now that it is in my possession for a third time I will return it to its past glory and beyond. I will do for it much more than I did the tag Title. The belt that has eluded me in the past has once again hang in the balance of one of my matches, and this time it will leave the squared circle around my wais tfor a long time to come. It will leave the arena in the possession of the one man that deserves it more than anybody in the world. Perhaps for one time in this company justice will be served and the right man will win all of the time. Of course the fans will not view it that way but they are too biased. They love only the people that love them back. They don't care about technical skill. To everyone out there I am an evil person but I am actually the most deserving champion that we have to offer. Spawn takes another sip of his Mountain Dew and then eyes the camera once again. A second or two ticks away and then he begins to speak again. Spawn: It seems that even you have fallen into thinking that Bomber. You have said that I am bitter and evil when in fact I am merely misunderstood. The mark in you is speaking out Bomber. You are seeing me as the fans see me, and that is a big mistake. Sure at times I can appear to be an asshole, but I am only doing what anyone out there would do if put in my shoes. I am doing whatever it takes to make things better for me. I am doing what everyone one out there would do to and if that makes me an asshole then I guess every single person in the stands and the locker room is. He takes another sip of the Mountain Dew and looks over the quiet gym. He looks back to the camera a moment later and begins to speak one last time. Spawn: I look forward to our meeting this Sunday Bomber. I look forward to showing everyone in the stands and everyone in the locker room what wrestling is all about. That night the two best wrestlers in the company will lock up in pure competition, like it should be. No angles, no gimmicks, just two great wrestlers wrestling. In the end I will emerge victorious as I almost always do. It will be no surprise, but I look forward to it nonetheless. I cannot wait to put you in your place this Sunday at Survival. Spawn sits the pop can on the cooler and walks back to the bench. He lies down on it and grabs a hold of the bar from underneath as the camera begins to fade away. He lifts the barbell off its place and lifts it up and then back down again a few times. The scene then fades to black.
