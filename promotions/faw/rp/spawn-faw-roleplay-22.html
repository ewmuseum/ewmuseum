SpAwN--The Hell Master \~~~~~~~(opening scene)The scene FAW house show were we see the ending of a match between two unknowns, at the end of the match the lights go out and already the fans know what the deal is as the mist start to appear everywhere. The FAW tron, shows a cave full of mist, as the cameras, once again begins a trip down the cave. The trip is a long one. As always with the camera showing all kinds of scary and weird thing inside the cave. The cameras continues their trip until they reach the back door with the dogs of hell guarding it. It goes past the dogs and goes directly to where we see a tall dark figure sitting on a chair, Everybody in the audience watching the show knows that the man sitting there is Spawn. Spawn gets up from the chair gets closer to the screen and begins to speak. Spawn: Welcome to another edition of the Devil's Pit. Are you ready for the thrill of your life and the fright you are about to receive, can your heart take this scare or will you give in to the bidding of the Hell master. If you can take it then lets begin shall we, Our story for tonight deals with what happens, when a person in life feels and thinks he is better than everybody, a person that does not care about no one but himself. This person feels he is the greatest of them all, by considering himself to be the greatest. Prepare yourselves as we take a journey into this person life and time of greatness and pure terror. And find out what happens to those who think they are bigger than most. Now let us begin. The scene Boston, Mass the place Madison Park high school and inside were the gym is located we see a bunch of kids playing a basketball game. It is a three on three type of game and everything goes, its is the type of game people call street ball, on the side we see three pretty girls watching the game, the gym is full of kids on this school day. The game is well underway and the score is tied at twenty eight a piece, Danny who is the leader of team one is waiting for Frank to pass him the ball, and he begins to shout at Frank. Danny: (talking to his teammate) Pass the ball you know I will score all of the points, pass it over here before they strip you of it? Frank: Don't worry I got this! I'm going to jay it on his ass in about a minute here. Danny: Man screw that shit what's going to happen is they are going to stuff you ass and we are going to lose. Now pass me the goddamn ball! Frank: I told you I got this here it comes(Frank goes up for the lay up but gets rejected the other team gets the rebound and score) Pierce: You two need to cut this shit out, you both are going to cost the game let's play as a team? Shall we. Danny: Man you tell the bitch here he's the one that should have pass the ball, you know if it wasn't for me this game won't be close right now. Pierce: Alright, let's beat these guys. Pierce passes the ball to Danny who dribbles it down the middle it looks as if Danny is about to tie the game, but Frank starts to call him to pass the ball to him because he is open on the left side, Danny just ignores him and continues for the hoop. Frank then gets upset and goes toward the hoop also hopping Danny will give him the pass for the lay up, but instead they cross each other and Frank accidentally, knocks the ball of Danny's hands, allowing the other team to get the ball shooting it and scoring the winning point. After the game the three girls come over to talk with the guys who are still very angry. vicky: Come on guys it was just a game? Danny: Just a game ! no! it was my game that should have been me winning it all but this fuckin blew it for me. Frank: Blew it for you! kiss my ass!! If you wouldn't have been so greedy and pass the ball once in a blue moon none of this would've happen. What you need to do is stop trying to think you are the best of them all because you really suck got me. Danny: What! I am the best in this entire school nobody can beat me in any sport and you know this, not even in a fight I just can't lose. That's why I am known as the greatest. Frank: Yeah you are the greatest alright. The greatest pussy in the world you bitch. Only your ass calls yourself the greatest not no one else. And your wrong I can take you in a fight. Danny: Come on try it then, we'll see who will be kissing the floor first. Pierce: Will you two cool it already, we are friends not enemy. How come you guys always have to be going at each other it just doesn't make any type of sense. Danny: Don't say it to me, tell the punk he's the one that is always jealous of me being known as the greatest. Frank: Fuck this bullshit! You are so fucking great then come on one on one in a pool game right now let's go! Danny: Ok but I do not play for free the. You must put up something on the line. Frank: Like what you asso? Danny: Like something big, like money and I mean a lot of money. Frank: Where am I going to get a lot of money are you stupid? Danny: You have a lot money your college fund remember put that up for grab and I will put mine. Frank: Are you insane we are friends, how can I beat you and take your life I am not a cold person like that are you? Danny: My answer to that is put up or shut up, are you afraid of the greatest, then come on let's play. Frank: You're on and if I win I'm going to make you suffer a bit before I give you your money back, and if you win I hope you do the same. The next day Danny and Frank meet for their pool game. It is late in the day and the game is taking is toll on both of them after an hour Danny comes out the winner. He then goes over to Frank and ask for the money. Danny: Alright what did I tell you now hand over all of the cash. (Frank give all of the money) that will teach you to pay attention when I say I am the greatest. Now I gots to go party with all of this cash. Pierce: Hey Danny wait a minute you are going to give Frank his money back right? You know how his mom is man, she will kill him that's all the money his has man don't bleed him dry you are his friend. Danny: And what do I care he lost and that's the way it is better luck next time now I'll see you all. Frank: Then give me a chance to win it back, one last chance dude. Danny: Well let me see how about we play… no that's not a good idea. I know what, let's have a fist fight but the stakes have to be higher. And you know me I do not lose in a fight. What do you say or are you afraid. Frank: Me afraid of you never! Go ahead tell me what's on the line? Danny: Ok if I win I want your car. And if I lose you will get your money back plus my car. What's it going to be. Frank: My car is better than your car, are you out of your stupid mind. No way man I’m not accepting this one no way man. Danny: If you do not accept this bet how are you going to get your money back. Because there is no way I’m giving it back to you with out a fight. Now you are always saying I’m not the greatest prove it then beat me in a fight and shut me up once and for all. Come and get your money. Frank: Alright! I’ll do it, but if I win and I know I am You will apologizes to me and give me my money that is all I want deal. Danny: You got it it’s a deal. So we meet here tomorrow at eight in the morning that’s a good time for you. The guys meet at eight the next day it is a cold day in the morning and there is a big surrounding of boys in the area already. The fight will take place behind a deserted parking lot Frank and Danny are ready. They look at each other and begin the battle Frank gets the upper hand first with a fist to the face and a kick to the stomach, but Danny rallies and sweeps Franks leg from under him as Frank falls on his head hard and is now feeling somewhat dizzy. He gets up slowly but Danny hits a flying drop kick sending him back to the floor, and as Frank lies on the floor Danny starts to kick him on the chest and back by now Frank is really wobbly and he can’t hardly get up, Pierce starts to notice that Frank is at a loss and tells Danny that is enough and that he has won the battle. Danny then starts to speak. Danny: I told you all I can’t be beat I am the greatest. Now for my new car yes, yes, yes, yes.(walking over to Frank and snatching the keys from his hand) you are a fool I told you, you could never be like me no one can I am the best and that is final. Frank: Danny do not take my car we are friends do not destroy my life like this. Danny: Sorry Frank all fair in loving war, ha, ha, ha, The next few days we see Danny spending his money and driving his new car, as Frank is nowhere to be found then one day in the youth center Pierce and Danny with two girls are hanging playing pool. When all of a sudden a stranger walks in to speak to them. Stranger: Are you friends of Frank? Danny: Yeah who wants to know? Stranger: No one just came to let you all know that he is dead? Pierce: Yeah right cut the crap this ain’t funny. Stranger: It is not a joke he is dead. Danny: Get the fuck out of here! Get out of here we do not want to listen to your ass no more. Stranger: I will leave but I must leave you with this message Frank shall return for his stuff and for your soul. Danny: Fuck you! What happened to Frank is not my fault so f off. The stranger throws a smile and leave. For the next few weeks Danny’s life changes his car breaks down on him he starts to lose a lot of sport games, his girl friend leaves him, all of his money is gone and he even loses a fight. Danny doesn’t know what to do he stop going to school and even stops talking to his friends. He now finds himself all alone in the world, the word greatest doesn’t even come out of his mouth anymore, he finds himself lost and does not know what to do. Then one day Danny decides that is time to take his life because there is no place for him in this world, he grabs a kitchen knife and is about to stab himself when all of a sudden he hears the voice of Frank. Frank: Danny what are you doing? Why are you trying to kill yourself? Can’t you cope with life ? aren’t you the greatest? Danny: Leave me alone ! you are gone! It is only my mind playing tricks on me! Frank: But it isn’t your mind Danny it’s me coming back for my stuff and your soul and this time this is one fight you can’t win. Danny: Get out of my mind! leave me alone I swear I will change! I’ll never do all those bad things I did. Frank: Ok Danny I’ll make a deal with you I will leave you alone if you do something for me? Danny: Anything I will do anything. Frank: Jump out the window. Danny: I thought you said you would leave me alone if I did what you said. Frank: I did say I leave you alone I didn’t tell you how, but now you know, so if you do not jump you will never be left alone. Go ahead Danny jump aren’t you the greatest. Now JUMP! Danny is pushed to the window by a cold breeze and falls of the window. The scene ends and Spawn is show back at the FAW tron and he begins to speak. Spawn: Did you enjoy the show did it scared your souls right from under you. What about you Great1 did it scared you I know it did. Feel this story rising thru your veins as it shows you that even the greatest of them all get beaten. That’s what going to happen tomorrow at FIW when I defeat you in the middle of the ring and take you out for good. I will do this easily, I will do this the right and that is greatly and you will go down for the 1, 2, 3, greatly. Spawn fades from the FAW tron as the scene fades to black.
