WWC Roleplay 3 - Urine and Arguments Spider Furiously Addictive Wrestling Roleplay #242 Date: 25-01-03 Location: TV Studios/FAW Arena DATE BASED: 26-01-02 [Spider and some kid are playing a computer game, looking intensely at the screen in a very messy bedroom; Spider jumps up and celebrates like a child] Spider: “I win!” Director: “And cut!” [Spider goes back to normal, he is wearing vest-top and ripped blue jeans, his sunglasses tilted a little. He strolls up to the director] Director: “It took you ten bloody takes but you did it!” [The director walks off, Spider follows, and he stops when he hears the director and another man talking.] Director: “That man is the worst actor I have ever met, all he had to do was jump around!” [Spider looks angry, he storms around the studio and see’s the directors bag, he searches through it and finds a drink of apple juice that is half full and one that is full, he takes the half full one and unzips his trousers, he turns away from everyone’s view, you hear the sound of liquid dropping to liquid, it stops, he shakes and zips up his trousers again, he puts the drink back and see’s Lazlo has been watching him the entire time. Lazlo is wearing a suit] Spider: “Umm… I…” Lazlo: “Don’t want to know dude.” Spider: “I’m just going to…” Lazlo: “Get your bag and leave?” [Spider nods and laughs, he walks off, Lazlo stands around, the director comes and picks up his bag, he sighs and talks to himself] Director: “Where can you get good help?” [Lazlo looks like he figured out why Spider did what he did as the man himself walks out with his sports bag] Spider: “Ready?” Lazlo: “Hold…” [The director pulls out his apple juice, it’s the same bottle, he unscrews the lid, and takes a swig, the look on Spider’s and Lazlo’s faces are ones of hilarity and disgust respectively, they laugh and walk off, the director looking quite disturbed] Lazlo: “What a retard!” Spider: “Class a, dude.” [They walk to the exit, they stand around when suddenly a young woman jumps on Spider’s back] Nina: “Guess who!” Spider: “Hmm… who could it be? Nina perhaps?” [Lazlo fakes looking shocked as she jumps down and kisses him. She is a combination of cute and sexy, tanned skin, brunette with blonde highlights that goes down to the middle of her back and her clothes are all very well hugged to her body] Spider: “Hey baby.” Nina: “We all ready to go?” Lazlo: “Yeah, the taxi to take us to the arena will be here in about a minute.” [They walks outside when, just as Lazlo says, a taxi pulls up, they all get in and chatter] Spider: “To the FAW arena please?” Taxi Driver: “Yes sir.” [Spider sits on the opposite seat to Nina, Lazlo next to him] Lazlo: “So how far is the arena?” Spider: “I don’t know. Well actually I do, 15 minutes by car.” Lazlo: “Ah cool.” Nina: “I don’t know why you’re going there, you won’t win. You’re rusted in the ring.” [Lazlo and Spider look at Nina with disgust] Spider: “This is great, my best friend and my girl friend don’t think I can win!” Nina: “I don’t think you can win because lets face facts, you screwed your foot, your rusted, you’ve been too tied up even to see who you’re facing!” Spider: “Now lets see the good points, Hall of Famer, this time last year I made my name, the World title is on the line, I do know who I’m facing and a lot of them have talent, and also, I have my girl and my best friend SUPPORTING me.” Nina: “Babe I support you…” [Lazlo praises thanks] Nina: “… I just don’t think you’ll win.” [Lazlo praises swears] Spider: “Whatever.” [Spider becomes cold and silent, so does Nina] Lazlo: “You could cut the atmosphere in here wi…” Spider and Nina: “SHUT UP LAZLO!” [They all stay silent for the next ten minutes, they finally arrive and Spider storms off, Nina follows, Lazlo pays the taxi driver and walks off on his own] Spider: “Leave me.” Nina: “No, Lazlo told me he felt the same about the match.” Spider: “That’s Lazlo, he’s my friend, you’re my girl, you’re meant to believe in me and everything I do, how can I believe in myself when you don’t even believe in me?!” Nina: “Baby I believe in you, I swear, but there’s got to be 19 other people saying that to 19 other wrestlers, calm down, work out and I’m sure you will win…” Spider: “You tell the truth?” Nina: “Of course, I always do.” Spider: “You did in the taxi…” [Spider smiles and they embrace] ~SCENE FADES~
