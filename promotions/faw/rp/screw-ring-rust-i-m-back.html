Screw Ring Rust, I'm Back! The British Bomber Furiously Addictive Wrestling Roleplay #119 Date: Saturday 21st December 2002 Location: Birmingham, England Life seem's kinda funny when your a pro wrestler, you have your up's and down's. And 12 month's ago it was certainly a up, Sunday December 23rd 2002, Evil Intentions, 8 man Hell In A Cell TLC match, out of the 8 superstars there would be a new champion, a new kid on top of the world and who other than the man that had been in FAW since April 2002 than The British Bomber. British Bomber: So here I am, sat in my Jaguar in my hometown of Birmingham, England in the UK. It is a cold, night where snow falls around the scenery but what the hell, its less than a week till Christmas where I get to spend time with family. See I had family but I lost my dad when he was shot in a bar earlier this year, my mom died years ago due to cancer and my ex Kathryn Velmont-Thomas has gone with my kid Taytum-Cordelia. Life's a bitch yeah but tell me this, why the hell me?! I loved that woman, hell I still do but for all I know she could have died and my baby girl could have all her money and be livin' it up. But 12 months ago I was the happiest guy in the World, got a gorgeous fianceé and I was World Champion, now I have chance to repeat history, this Sunday I face Spawn, The Great One, 'The Real Prodigy' Scott Costella, Rzarector, Renegade, Lee Smith and Foamy. Bomber pauses then looks out the window, he looks into the sky then back towards the camera. British Bomber: I sit here today wearing a leather and jean jacket, jeans and £100 trainers, I obviously have the money to look after a kid, I am mentally stable! But hell, back to the wrestling, 12 months ago I battled through that knee injury that has bugged me for over 4 years now and I put myself through the pain barrier to win that belt, and I'll be damned that this Sunday if I dont win that title in this Dome match then it looks like I am done. But the bottom line, I go in to win the title for the fans, to win it without help, just me and the fans. He scratches his chin then flexes his shoulders. British Bomber: Spawn, so many battles, so many outcomes and sure, we had our fair share of screwjobs but this Sunday, its all in a Dome, just me, you and 6 other guys that will battle for the championship gold. Last year I had Evil Intentions running through my head as you saw, and witnessed me become champion, all for the wrong reasons but this year will be different. This year I do it for the right reasons as you shall know by the end of it all. He then smiles and looks onto the dash. British Bomber: Then Lee Smith, deja vu is damn right. You saw what happened to your beloved federation 12 months ago how me and Tom masterminded the biggest scam in history of FAW but this year you are in the match, unlike last year, you interfered and tried to cost me the title because you was selfish, I had it won but NO! YOU TRIED TO COST ME MY DREAM! You talk about releasing your anger, Sunday I finally get the chance to release my anger after 12 months of waiting, you bitch Lee. Payback will damn sure be a bitch. He pauses and takes a drink of water from the dash, what he was looking for before. British Bomber: Foamy, you talk about DCV..... in my books DCV was a mistake, a huge one but thinking of it, maybe not because at the beginning I always said I would destroy DCV no matter what.... look what happened! DCV are gone! But enough, you say I am in the match to prove I am not past it, to prove to myself I still have it. Well Foamy maybe your right, but one thing you missed off, I am in the match to make careers and break them, just like last year in the HIAC TLC match, I made Chris Thunder, I made Jeff Harris, I made Stunning Stan then I broke them all because I could. I am in the match to prove to myself I aint past it, whether the fans feel I am or not I dont know, but this one last match this Sunday is FOR the fans, and partly for me, not for you, but for the fans as a send off. But Foamy, I'll tell you this, no matter what reason it is, no matter how past it people think I am I will go oyut there and give 110% because I can and I will be World Champion come the 23rd of December! He pauses again taking a sip of the bottled water, he puts the water down then looks into the back of the car. British Bomber: Renegade, Rzarector former tag team champions. You two are undouftfuly the long shots in this match, if either of you 2 walked out World Champion I would be surprised but anything can happen in wrestling as we all know. I'll give you guys credit where it is due, you have talent, but the question is can you hang with the big boys, can you hang with the likes of Costella, The Great One, Spawn, Foamy and me? This Sunday you get the chance to prove you can and I hope you dont come up against me or I will prove that you CANT hang with us because you take a look at that sledgehammer I have there, all I gotta do it put that under the ring and take a few swings and BANG! Here is your NEW World Champion! But I hope it doesnt come down to that because I want to do this fairly, and I will. You play by the book and everythings fine, you play with fire and you get burnt, remember that. Bomber scratches his forehead then turns back to the front. British Bomber: 'The Real Prodigy' Scott Costella, one of my favourites to win the thing, Scott me and you have wrestled before and damn you got talent boy, now the question is can you do it? Can you go on and be the man around here? Scott you have nothing to lose in this match, its all or nothing and you gotta do it. Scott like I said to Renegade and Rzarector dont get in my way, I got a personal battle going on in that match with Smith. Good luck kid. He takes one last sip of the water. British Bomber: Then fianlly.... The Great One. You know out of our long careers this is the first time we have faced each other one on one? This Sunday there will be 6 other guys apart from us, your my absolute favourite to win the match and title. Great One you need that title, I know you do and so do you. You go out there and win that sumtha bitch and cause if I dont you better do it. Bomber takes a drink of water then throws the bottle. He starts up the car and lets the cameraman out then drives off......
