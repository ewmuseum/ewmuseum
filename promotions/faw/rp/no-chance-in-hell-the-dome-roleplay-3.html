No Chance In Hell - The Dome Roleplay 3

21st December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
No Chance In Hell - The Dome Roleplay 3 'Legendary' Lee Smith Furiously Addictive Wrestling Roleplay #115 Date: 21st December 2002 Location: Presidential Suite of a Washington Hotel “Oh…Yes….That feels good…that’s right….OOOOOHHHH” Smith groans as the camera pans out and we see him lying on a bed. His masseur rubs some more lotion onto her hands and drives her hands into his shoulder blades. It was 21st December, just a day away from Evil Intentions 2. Lee had arrived in this elegant hotel a few hours ago and had dumped his bags on a teenage boy who offered to carry his luggage for a small fee. Since then he had arrived in his Presidential suite to find a masseur willing to ease his nerves. The room is painted a creamy colour which blends in with the rest of the room. Lee sits on the end of the bed with a towel covering his lower body. The widescreen TV in front of his face is on, and currently on CNN. “Pass the remote” Lee whispers to the masseur who passes it to Lee. He turns to NBC and sees the news. He stares intently at the screen as the host recaps the main headlines. “And so the main headlines for this afternoon, Bush promises all out war against Saddam, Pamela Anderson admits to passionate affair with Cherie Blair and fans gather outside an arena in America in anticipation of Furiously Addictive Wrestlings Evil Intentions. Lets go live with Suzanne Shaw who is live outside the arena.” the host says as the camera cuts and Suzanne appears on screen. “Yes hello Leo, there has been some massive build up and hype surrounding this event. Among the matches is a main event taking place in a Dome - the first time in wrestling history an event like this has taken place. FAW fans have been camping outside the arena in hope of citizens with tickets arriving at the arena and selling tickets to them. A blackmarket for tickets has already assembled and thus there is some police activity. More news on this story as we get it” “Okay that was Suzanne Shaw and now Fred Hurst who is outside the US Embassy…” Lee switches off the television as his masseur rubs her hands with oil. There is an abrupt knock on the door. “Get that please” Lee insists and the masseur opens the door. Lee listens closely and hears a man talking to her. “Mr Smith, it’s a….” the masseur says waiting for the man to tell her his name. “Brian Fradley, Washington Gazette. I am here to conduct an interview with Mr Smith.” “Let him in” Lee replies and the man steps forward. He seems shocked to see Lee half naked and covers his eyes. Lee turns his head and sees the man dressed in a brown suit with a tail. He is holding a notepad which breaks with his nerves. “I’ll just go and get some clothes on” Lee says and walks towards his wardrobe. He struggles to find any clothes and in the end puts on his white dressing gown. “That’ll have to do….Now…what was it you wanted Mr….” “Fradley” the man replies. “Oh yes…Mr Fradley. An interview wasn’t it?” Smith replies tightening his robes. “Yes. May I begin?” Fradley asks, wooing Lee into interrogation. Lee replies within a split second. “You may” he responds and sits down at the edge of the bed. He dismisses his masseur into the next room, throwing his wallet at her. “Lee..I’m sure you’re aware of the big match this Sunday. Who do you think is the biggest threat?” Fradley asks, preparing to let rip on his notepad when Lee answers. “That’s not easy. I’m up against 7 other men. The good Samaritan thing to say would be that all of them are equal, but its simply not quite true. All of them bring a unique essence into FAW. There’s The British Bomber…who has struggled to live up to his status in recent months. And then we’ve got 9th Wonder Of Foam…I never quite understood him. The Great One is another contender, he has struggled to live up to his name recently. One guy who stands head and shoulders above the rest? ME!” Lee laughs to himself as Fradley finishes writing down the answer. “You’ve already mentioned TBB, 9WF and TG1. What about the others, do any of them stand a chance of toppling you?” the journalist asks. Lee quickly replies. “No, no one stands a chance against someone so dominant as myself. But there are some talented competitors - Rzarector for example is one to watch, he may just inflict some damage on me - a very rare occurrence. Meanwhile Renegade…well…..how can I say this without bringing offence…..I CAN’T! HE’S A JOBBER!” Lee answers, shuffling further away from Fradley. Brian seems in deep though and several minutes later comes up with his next question. “Do you think the stipulations of The Dome could undermine your supremacy?” B.F. questions. Lee strokes his miniscule goatee before answering. “No.” he replies as he becomes frustrated. “With the anniversary of the betrayal of Tom The Bomb, do you still think of that incident?” Brian asks…striking a rather touchy subject. Lee walks towards the door and swings it open. “Out” he shouts at Fradley who runs towards the door in fear. He then turns and grabs his notebook and makes it out before the door slams. Lee frowns and curses under his breath. He walks into the next room and sees his masseur lying on the bed. Lee takes off his dressing gown. “Now…where were we” he whispers as the camera pans out and fades to black, we hear the masseur giggling.
