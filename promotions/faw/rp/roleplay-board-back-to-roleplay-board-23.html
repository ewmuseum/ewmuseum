Furiously Addictive Wrestling Roleplay Board Roleplay Board | Back to Roleplay Board Shensui Yusuki Entitled: Blood From The Tap Written: 09/08/02 Location: The Room [Shensui enters a very dark room as the door oils as if it was as dry as a old bone. The rust clogging the door fell to the floor as it had down before and there was a fair mount of it piling up. The door was a large wooden oak door, which had a small keyhole on the left of the two double doors. Shensui looked around the room checking everything was in the right place (a routinely check). The sofa sat in a streak of moonlight peeking through the crack of the curtain revealing, the dull worn-away blue patterning on them. The mahogany coffee table sat on its hand carved lions ‘feet’ as it would usually with a vase full of dead, rotten old flowers. The smell of the flowers was like a mouldy orange in a dustbin. Yesterday’s newspaper lye beside it with a coffee stain over the half covered headline ‘BUSH TO…’The old portrait painting hang weighed down by dust and the 24 carat gold frame in was held by of course, which was moulded into beautiful shapes, the picture was of an old lady in a rocking chair in a little woodlands cottage alone. The television set sat on a noticeably fake painted chest of drawers that where supposedly ‘oak’. There were old movies that he never watched like ‘The Elbert Riley’ and ‘ Doctor Patterson’. He could see the old CD player that had a turntable on it. It was sitting on the bear floor next to the fireplace and it looked as if it would collapse at the slightest sound. The fireplace was old and dark. It was plated with blue marble that was coming away. His tall and willow figure moves slowly toward the centre of the room touching nothing and approaches a coffin which has a white gothic gross nailed to it with several large rusted nails. Shensui looks towards the coffin in the very corner of the room and there is a collection of dead animals hanging and the stench would make any normal person gag but Shensui smiles at it. Then he turns to the coffin that is standing up right and turns back to the coffin in front of him. Shensui begin some kind of weird ritual walking around the coffin and humming chanting and finally he stops. He opens the lid like a hawk peering under the red silky lining of it is revealed there are nails pointing up from everywhere Shensui spins onto the rusty nails in one quick sharp movement and he is now laying down in Midnight Darkness in a coffin full of nails. Shensui remains perfectly still and everything is silent. Shensui pulls the lid of the coffin down like a balloon in a hand. There was no glimpse of light inside the bars of the coffin.] *Shensui lies seemingly comfortably on the bed of nail trapped with hardly the space but to move inside a gothic coffin. * Shensui: Cold… Cold like the dripping blood of Human corpses the corpses of Nightmare King and Black ghost. *Shivers* Shensui: My blood runs cold and yet I fear nobody but myself. [Shensui gets up from the coffin pushing the lid open with great force as it bounces back from the bare grey concrete floor leaving scratches on the cross. Shensui goes to a coffin which is standing upright against the bare walls of his tiny cellar he opens it. Inside there are lots of tall white wax candle Shensui removes them from the cupboard and sets one on each corner of the coffin. He proceeds to take some woodchips from the cupboard and throws them into the large fireplace and he takes one match and lights all four candle the throws it into the woodchips and the fire is lit. The back of the coffin is covered in blood stains… Shensui: Now the darkness is all there is to fear, these candles are of hope. The darkness to fear is that of my heart for the blood runs cold through my artic body. [Shensui uses the same spinning motion to land on top of the large rusty nails once again. The bed punctures the bare back of Shensui and his blood can be seen dripping down them as it stains the satin linen.] *Shivers Again* [Shensui peers towards the blood and smiles sickly like a freak and lets out a blood curdling howl.] Shensui: No… It is still cold it always will be. I am cold so cold. I am cold and unforgiving. Cold and Entrapped in a world of haunting. I feel that shiver down my spine for I am not but an iron man. The tingle down all of your spines that howling around the windows the curtains… the shadows moving. That is my presence I thrive on the fear of you men you women and the children… the children… tonight I shall be thriving from one source only that source is Nightmare King that source is Black Ghost there fear is so great I shall not have to feed until the time is past... The time of the moon. [Shensui closes the coffin lid and it lets out a final creak as silence falls once again of the cellar of Shensui Yusuki and in the far away distance the striking of 6 am can be heard as the light of day pours in Shensui is not rising no but falling to rise once again the following night.] *Dong …………… Dong …………… Dong …………… Dong …………… Dong …………… Dong ……………*
