Drop The Dead Donkey DDURP002 9th Wonder Of Foam Furiously Addictive Wrestling Roleplay #39 Date: 24/10/02 Location: Foamy Hills - New Jersey …. Foamy Hills – Thursday Morning …. (The 9th Wonder Of Foam is on the phone in his study) Foamy: I’m sorry Melissa, you couldn’t have done the interview yesterday because we couldn’t locate Invisible Jim, but we’ve found him now, in fact he’s sitting in front of me right now. (Foamy nods to the empty space in front of him representing Invisible Jim) Foamy: Look come over now we’ll let the world know the power of Invisible Jim and the 9th Wonder Of Foam! (Foamy puts the phone down, Lars walks in and sits down on the chair in front of Foamy’s desk) Foamy: What the hell do you think you are doing? Lars: Uh, sitting down. Foamy: You’re sitting on Invisible Jim, my good friend and tag team partner! Lars: No Foamy, this is a chair. Foamy: Look sonny, you are sitting on Jim, Jim is quite furious with you, Jim wants me to hit you. (Foamy slaps Lars, he gets off the chair and stands up) Foamy: That’s better. Lars: I don’t see what the problem was anyway, Foamy: Well imagine Lars, if Jim’s penis had incidentally entered your colon, imagine the diseases you could transmit to him putting our Tag Team Championship shot into jeopardy! Lars: Ok, Ok, can we just move on and forget it ever happened? Foamy: Jim wants an apology! Lars: I’m sorry Jim. Foamy: Well look at him when you say it you ignorant fool! (Lars turns to “Jim) Lars: I’m ever so sorry Jim, I beg your forgiveness. Foamy: Jim accepts your apology. ….. 32 Minutes later …. (Melissa Farrow and her filming crew enter Foamy’s study, Lars follows them, Melissa sits down in Foamy’s chair as Dave & Carl set the equipment up) Foamy: Jim would like to tell you that he is very aroused right now. Melissa: Jim is here? Foamy: Of course. Melissa: Where is he? Foamy: Well surely you can feel him? Melissa: What do you mean? Foamy: Well you are sitting on his penis, can’t you feel his extraordinary girth? (Melissa looks disgusted and leaps out of the chair, she grabs another and sits next to “Jim”) Foamy: Oh Jim, it appears that she didn’t like your girth, in fact I think having now had both of us she preferred by girth. Melissa: Look can we get on with this? Foamy: Ok. Melissa: So Foamy, this Sunday you face Rzarector & Renegade for the FAW Tag Team Championship. Rzarector thus far remains silent, but Renegade has spoken, he calls you a Looney, one that can wrestle but essentially and easily beatable opponent. Foamy: I know of this interview Melissa, and quite frankly I’m astounded at his rookie errors. I could harp on about the ten-inch height advantage that I have, or the fact that I am one hundred and five pounds heavier, but Renegade has already pointed his weaknesses out to me. If you have a bad neck boy you don’t tell your opponent your weak spot, you don’t point out in three easy to follow steps how we can defeat your ass. (Foamy leans forward and leans back) Foamy: Jim agrees concurrently. Melissa: So you are confident of victory? Foamy: Of course not, Renegade is lighting fast, and Rzarector is not to be messed with in the ring, both are accomplished athletes but they lack the cognitive ability to really get a grasp of their situation. They are facing the most charismatic crotch this side of the panty line, and a man they cannot see, a man I doubt they believe even exists. Melissa: Yes Renegade does make light of the fact that your tag partner would seem not to exist. Foamy: If that is the case they are facing a man all to willing to challenge for the FAW Tag Team Championship on his own, without a partner, does that make me more dangerous or less dangerous, does that make me a greater “Looney” or not? Melissa: The team of Renegade and Rzarector is still in its infancy will this affect the match? Foamy: It could, I mean myself and Jim have been champions before, haven’t we Jim?… (Silence) Foamy:… See, but Renegade and Rzarector have made the rookie mistake of forming a team in their first month with the first ugly girl that comes along because they need desperately to loose their tag team virginities. But this girl may just end up pregnant, and instead of a lovely childhood romance they could end up in their own personal hell, the girl asking for money the boy running away from his responsibility, and I see this in our tag team champions. Renegade is correct they have little to see eye to eye with not least because Renegade is comparable in height to that of a small boy. They may duel well in the ring but sooner or later their ideological differences will show their true colours. The only question is who is the naive boy and who is the girl who liked to pretend she was younger than she looked? My money is on Rzarector being the boy, after all he seems to be a catholic, and we all know how successful the rhythm method is! Melissa: Slightly off the point but never mind. Any last words? Foamy: Renegade you have a good head on you for a rookie, you’re calm and composed but you seem reserved, too reserved you seem to lack that mean streak that all us unstable people have. You don’t seem to share that small shred of cockiness that all champions possess, it seems you’re too nice. Don’t let that haunt you boy, because us unstable lunatics have a tendency to be very nice one minute and very very nasty the next. And with FAW in the shape it is I may just be out to loot what I can before the ship goes under, and nothing can stop Invisible Jim and I from becoming FAW Tag Team Champions. Because if there is one thing that can save FAW, it’s the 9th Wonder Of Foam holding the gold, that gold, your gold, my gold. Have a Foam Filled Day!
