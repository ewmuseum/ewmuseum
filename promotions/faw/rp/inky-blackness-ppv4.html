Inky Blackness(PPV4) Crimson Lord Furiously Addictive Wrestling Roleplay #47 Date: 25/10/02 Location: A Darken Room (The scene fades in on a darkened room, the last Crimson promo has just been aired on FAW TV, and he's back for more. We see Crimson hunkered down in his steel king's throne, smoking a cigar. The camera focuses in on his face, as he closes his eyes, and smiles, setting his arms down on the arms of the throne.) Crimson: Hey, Sensei! Back for more, I suppose. I've already dug your damn grave once, Sensei. I guess you turned over in it so damn much that you popped out of it. I guess it's time to get the shovel again, huh? Crimson: But before I put you back in the ground, I should let you know a few things. your ass will be on the end of my foot. That was the biggest f*cking insult anyone's ever aimed in my direction! To think, someone stooped that low! But you know something, Sensei, you are by far the weakest of everyone I have ever faced. You have a brain the size of a pea, and the balls to match. You were ALWAYS weak-willed. Why the hell do you think I could knock you on your ass so effortlessly? If you said luck, then you're as dumb as you look, sound, and act. Now, I know you think that your intelligence impresses your below average toddler, but it doesn't mean SHIT to me. What matters to me is that retardation isn't transmitted through a foot up your ass. (Crimson smiles slighty.) Crimson: While I applaud the long overdue attitude adjustment, I'm sorry to say it's like jumping from the frying pan into the fire. This 'holier than thou' kick you're on....it's gotten tired. If you like yourself so much, why don't you take a mirror and move to the mountains so you don't bother anyone? You think you're so good, don't you? How well did you do in trying to get the respect you disserve? Not very successful, huh? Where the fuck does that leave you against me, arkside? I'll tell you where. Clueless, defenseless, and most of all, hopeless. Hope I'm not dissappointing you here, Sensei. I know you'd love to truly believe that you believe are the 'top ratings draw' around here. You aren't. I know you think that your shit doesn't stink, but do you know what that makes you? A GUY WHO LIKES TO SMELL HIS OWN SHIT!! (He pauses, and looks down at the floor.) Crimson: You were better off staying away, Sensei. Look what you've gotten yourself into now. You're sided with the very people you KNOW you can't trust, you're about to go head to head with a bonafide Madman, and worst of all, nobody feels sorry for you. I mean, you've got to have a title belt to feel special, even if it was one found in the garbage. You aren't even considered a paper champion! You're a piece of shit, Sensei, and you damn well know it! For crap's sake, Sensei! I never thought a I'd see the day that a goody two-shoes little whore like you had to resort to something like this. Xander, maybe, but not you! You always had so much pride. She could be walking down Rodeo Drive right now, her ass hanging out, wearing a bra thirteen sizes too small for her, trying to peddle her pussy so she can get the diamond earrings you refused to buy her. The point is YOU DON'T KNOW. It kinda reminds me of an old joke. What's the difference between your supposed girlfriend and a drug dealer? She can wash HER crack and re-sell it! Crimson: And what's with this 'Hard Image' bullshit?, You look at yourself and you see perfection. I see narcissism wasted on a peon like yourself. You shouldn't toot your own horn, Sensei. You can't even play a damn kazoo right! Yet, you continue right down your path to emptiness. You're going hit a real brick wall, Sensei. Know what it is? It's reality. It's going catch up with you, and you're going splatter your brains everywhere. Sure, you may think you're good looking, talented, and smart. But you know what, Sensei? You've NEVER been talented. You can't even walk and chew gum at the same time. You definately aren't smart. You filled out a job application and when it said 'Sign Here' you put down 'pisces.' And good looking? Well, you may think so, but looks only last so long, Sensei. What are you going to do in thirty years when you can't get by on your looks? Write a book? You can't even read, so that's out of the question. Play golf? Can you even put a ball on a tee? I'll tell you what you're going to do. You're gonna go on a three month bender. You're gonna be drinking and smoking pot and smoking crack and shooting up with heroin, morphine, and whatever illegal narcotic you can get your hands on. And you're going to lace everything with angel dust so it'll get you nice and high. Well, nice and dead is more like it. The stress is going to get to you! You heard about all the bombs in Ireland, right? Those weren't bombs. Those were former models blowing up because their looks weren't cutting it. I predict that in ten years, you'll more than slightly resemble Twiggy...just in male form, and you can quote me on that one, bitchwipe. Crimson: It's coming nearer, Sensei. Tick tock. Tick tock. Tick tock. Time's running out. You're on your last few grains of sand. And still you won't shut up. You tell people that 'pretty soon, they'll be the one who burns'. Well, Sensei, that may be true. I may burn in the future. But you're already burning. YOU YOU YOU! I can't spell it out clear enough for you, Sensei! So let me say it in a way I THINK you might be able to understand. (He pauses and looks up at the camera.) Crimson: Too bad, so sad, you're fucked... Crimson: Just show up, Sensei, and I'll show you how damn right I am. I'm Crimson Lord. I'm 'The True Lord' and 'Your fucking Savoir'. I'm a former Hardcore Champion, former Tag Team Champion. (Crimson grins, looking up at the darkened ceiling.)
