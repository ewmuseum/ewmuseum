Blast From The Past: UGH! TRAINING! (Panic #2)

Friday 17th January
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Blast From The Past: UGH! TRAINING! (Panic #2) Gabriel Hunter Furiously Addictive Wrestling Roleplay #152 Date: Friday 17th January Location: Some Generic Gym We open up in your typical gymnasium. Inside the lavish light-blue walls, muscle-bound guys, overweight lard-burners, skinny no-hopers and just about anyone else you’d expect to find at such a gym, are pumping iron via the expansive and incredibly expensive-looking equipment. We pan around the pieces of equipment, and their various users – to some estimation, the place looks about 50% full. Among the various people and equipment, we find Gabriel Hunter in a lonely corner of the gym. This corner is home to several assorted weights, benches, and some of the smaller equipment on offer. Hunter, himself, is performing press-ups somewhat sluggishly on a large, blue mat, wearing a sweat-drenched white vest, a pair of navy blue sport pants, and some cheap-looking white trainers. As we home in on him, it becomes apparent that he has been working out for a good deal of time, and is in a highly groggy condition. He has a look of incredible strain across his face; beads of sweat run down an already soaked forehead, as Gabriel puffs and pants, trying to keep the routine up. “195 … 196 … 197,” Gabriel counts lightly through heavy breaths and press-ups. “198 … 199 …” he continues. He pauses for a few seconds, before going onto his two hundredth press-up; “200 … Phew.” Gabriel collapsed, a tired heap lying in the middle of one of the exercise mats. He closed his eyes – his body ached all-round. Gabriel continues breathing heavily, as his body heat intensifies through idleness, “Goddamn,” he mutters to himself. It was now late afternoon, and he had been training since the early hours of the morning. Of course, it was always going to be hard training up for a return match, knowing just 6 days prior to the actual event, but even Gabriel thought it would be a tad easier than this. He just lay there, a motionless wreck, just trying to regain a little stamina. He went unnoticed in the gym, lying there – everybody was more concerned with his or her own business. But then again, Gabriel was never one to be noticed, and even at the peak of his short-lived wrestling career, those around him had only considered him an also-ran. The name Gabriel Hunter has rarely meant anything to those around Gabriel, but he was out to change that. “Must … Have … Water!” Gabriel proclaimed through tired-breaths. He turned his head toward his right, noticing a typical water dispenser just a few metres away. Slowly, he turned his body, pivoting on his left arm. He lunged one elbow forward, pulling all his weight forward with a snail-paced military crawl. After what must have seemed like an eternity to Gabriel, but was more like 40 seconds in real-time, Gabriel had reached the water cooler. He tried to look up toward the aqua-turquoise of the water-tank, although the fact that he was little more than an inch from the cooler prevented him from doing so. Slowly, but surely, Gabriel rose, sitting down on the floor. Raising the back of his right hand toward his brow, Hunter wiped the dripping sweat off, and paused for a few breaths. From there, he leaned forward, clasping hold of the cooler with both hands. Using the cooler, which was thankfully attached to the wall, Gabriel managed to hoist himself to his feet with an almighty “EUUUURGH!” call, which could probably have awoken the dead. Grabbing hold of one of the annoyingly minuscule paper-cups, Gabriel blindly filled it up with water. Due to a lack of concentration, the water ran over the edges of the cup, and down Hunter’s hand. Gabe didn’t care to notice though – he was totally out of it. Raising the small cup up to his lips, he quickly downed it’s contents in an almighty gulp, discarding it into the nearby bin. “More! Must have more,” Gabriel said to himself, as he yanked another cup from it’s placement, filling it up, and quickly downing it in similar fashion. As Gabriel went to take another cup, however, he was interrupted, as a large hand slapped down on his shoulder. The FAW Superstar turned around, to see a large and incredibly muscular man standing before him. He must’ve been a few inches taller than Gabriel, and Joe Average would cringe at his freakishly huge muscles, as Gabriel would have done, if he wasn’t so damn drowsy. This gent had short, black hair, no sign of gel at-all, and in all honesty, had quite a handsome face. He wore a white t-shirt, advertising the name of the gym in blue, with a pair of sky blue shorts. “Wha … Whaddaya want?” Gabriel enquired, questioning the man that stood before him. The man looked like he had also been working out, although for not nearly as long as Gabriel had been. “You Gabriel Hunter?” he replied with a light voice, “Course you are, thought I might find you here. The name’s Greg, Greg Carmichael, I’m yer new personal trainer!” Gabriel eyed Mr. Carmichael with a look of suspicion, he knew nothing of plans to employ a personal trainer. “Personal trainer?” “Yeah, s’right. I’m pals with your agent, Markus Green, he told me you needed trainin’ up for a return to pro-wrestling!” was the response that flew in Gabriel’ direction. “Uhh, look, Mr. Whoeveryouare … I’m sure you’re a great guy and all, but really, I don’t want no damn personal trainer, I’m fine on my own thanks,” Gabriel said, seemingly dismissing Carmichael. Greg only flashed a sly grin, and chuckled to himself, not taking Gabriel in the slightest bit seriously. “Well, Gabriel, I’ve been watching you all day, and let me tell you …” Greg paused, as Gabriel’s facial expression turned to that of complete and utter confusion, “You’ve got a lot of work to do, brushing up your training regime.” “HEY! I’ve been hear since SEVEN ‘O CLOCK IN THE F*CKING MORNING!” Gabriel snapped, taking offence to Carmichael’s words, he glanced at a nearby clock. “It’s now FOUR in the AFTERNOON, which means I’ve been here busting ass for NINE HOURS! So don’t tell me that my “regime” needs brushing up on!” “I know, I’ve been watching you ever since you came in.” “Jeesh! So what are you now, a stalker!?” Gabriel’s response prompted a faint chuckle from Greg, who spoke; “Not quite Gabriel, not quite … But tell me, out of those nine hours, how many have you actually spent TRAINING.” “All nine of course!” Gabe promptly replied, “Well, I took a quick break, okay, maybe a couple of quick breaks … Then I went to the cafeteria for awhile, and got a back massage …” Again, Carmichael chuckled. “Thought so.” For a moment, there was silence between Gabriel Hunter and Greg Carmichael. Neither of them had even the faintest idea how to continue the conversation – although Greg had a few important matters that he had to get across. “Okay,” Gabriel said, breaking the silence, “so now I’ve got an agent, and a personal assistant? What am I? A walking Wrestling stereotype?” “Well, if you hope to make a successful return to wrestling, Gabriel, you’ve got to do a bit more than a couple of press-ups,” replied Greg, ignoring Gabriel’s rhetorical question. “We’re gonna have to get you shaped up, and damn quick, so I want to see you in here whenever you get the chance, alright?” “Alright,” Gabriel agreed reluctantly, as a grimace came across his face. “Say, who is it you’re facing again? The names escape me …” “There’s The Violator, Xing, Jeff Harris, Atticus Pulsar, and Slyphon, I think,” Gabriel replied, answering the question posed to him. “Pretty tough line-up there, Gabe,” considered Greg. “So Markus told me, but to be blatantly honest, a lot of these guys have disappointed me thus far,” revealed Gabriel. “I mean, the only two, apart from myself, to have actually had the decency to show up and cut a promo for this so far, have been Xing and Jeff Harris. Xing’s supposed to be a pretty established guy here, but from what I’ve seen thus far of him, he hasn’t really impressed me. I’ve gone against bigger guys, and one. Jeff Harris, on the other hand, looks pretty good, he’ll be a handful, although I am still quite curious as to what exactly a “Golden Nine” really is. I’ve been told that The Violator is a force to be reckoned with … But if he doesn’t pull something out of his ass real quick, then he’s gonna got knocked out like Atticus Pulsar and Slyphon, who have possibly the worst two names EVER known to man. I mean, Atticus Pulsar!? Just when you thought Babylon 5 was dead and buried, this guy pops up and reminds you that stupid names are not a thing of the past. And Slyphon? Please, don’t make me laugh, this guy wouldn’t look out of place in that Metal Gear Solid game, or something.” Greg grinned. “Ahhh, a bit of confidence, always good to see!” “Meh, what do you expect me to do?” asked Gabriel rhetorically again. “Mope about how much better, or higher-up than me, that these guys are? That’s just not me. I finished moping around when I left Carnage, and I don’t intend to resume any time soon.” Again, there was a pause between the two. Gabriel took this time to get another tiny cup of water from the dispenser, taking two gulps to down it this time. “So I bet yer looking forward to this match on Panic, right?” Greg Carmichael asked, breaking the silence. “Well, to be honest,” Gabe replied, “I really wasn’t keen on it at first. I mean, I left wrestling for a reason – it was just too damn much for me to cope with at the time. But now that I think of it, a FAW career mightn’t be that bad after all. When I look at the environment and the level of competition, it looks like a decent place to kick start this career of mine.” “Excellent,” Carmichael grinned. “Come on, we’ve got training to do!” He turned toward the various pieces of equipment, leaving Gabriel standing somewhat bemused. And then, it came to him – after nine hours of “training,” he was going to have to do it all again. “B*stard.”
