Joe La Fontaine — FAW Roleplay, August 2002

August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Furiously Addictive Wrestling Roleplay Board Roleplay Board | Back to Roleplay Board Joe La Fontaine Entitled: Under The Bridge Written: 10 Aug 2002 Location: Detroit, MI ***The scene opens on a dull and cloudy day. We are back in the heart of the bustling city. Hundreds of people walk the street today compared to the thousands, maybe even millions that would drag their Prada shoes, Adidas trainers and Rockport boots. People are dressed warmly today as there is a sharp chill in the air that would go right to your bones if you let it. Close-ups of the on coming people in the crowd reveal many faces without a hint of happiness. We are lost. Swept along by the current. Struggling we break free onto the road. The camera pans around searching, looking. It surveys the city skyline until it notices some figures breaking the horizon. There are people on the roof. Slowly the figures become more focused and recognisable as we draw closer to them.*** La Fontaine: Hey! Hey! Kroeger, Scott get off of this roof! I had this roof booked today! Just because you made a little money from playing on top of a roof…that does not mean you can just walk on and steal somebody’s pre booked roof, does it? Well…does it? ***The camera locks onto Joe La Fontaine and Johnny Doe. La Fontaine is wearing torn jeans, a Red Hot Chili Peppers “By The Way Tour” T-Shirt and a black Adidas beanie. Johnny is clad in his usual pinstripe suit and orange tinted sunglasses, his slicked back hair reflects what little sunshine there is.*** Kroeger: …No Sir… ***Kroeger and Scott start to pack up their gear and head to the fire exit that will lead them back down to the street below.*** La Fontaine: Yeah…that is damn right Mr Ugliest Man In Rock! Yeah…there you go…off the roof go on… ***Kroeger’s eyes fill with tears as he walks away. Scott tries to comfort him by putting an arm around him. Scott looks back at La Fontaine and shakes his head…La Fontaine grins.*** La Fontaine: Thank God they left…he really is ugly isn’t he…? ***Doe nods as Scott and Kroeger disappear.*** Doe: Oh yeah…hey…what’s… ***Before Johnny can finish his sentence U2 led by Bono make their way onto La Fontaine and Doe’s pre booked roof top.*** La Fontaine: Crap…hey Bono…we booked this roof…take a time check buddy this is 2002 not the fricken' 1980’s…get lost… ***Bono walks off, he takes off his giant novelty sunglasses and leads The Edge and the other band members from La Fontaine and Doe’s pre booked rooftop.*** La Fontaine: Jesus H. Christ…who next? The bloody Beatles? ***La Fontaine is taken aback.*** Voice: Actually… ***La Fontaine closes his eyes. He hears a voice he has heard many times before…La Fontaine spins around…*** La Fontaine: Hey look! Johnny, it is that guy that done the voice for popular children’s programme, Thomas The Tank Engine! And he is standing next to Sir Paul McCartney! ***Sir Paul smiles.*** Voice: …Ringo Starr! ***La Fontaine shrugs.*** La Fontaine: Yeah whatever…as much as I like The Beatles and as much as I love Thomas The Tank Engine…I’m afraid this roof has been pre-booked! This is not Liverpool ya’ know Paul…you can’t just get up on a roof and sing “Get Back” anytime you want man…we have pre-booked this particular roof…this particular day…leave me a signed copy of Sergeant Pepper…and on you go… ***Sir Paul McCartney pulls out a copy of Sergeant Pepper and he signs is before passing it on to Ringo Starr who signs it. La Fontaine waves them off of the rooftop. He turns to Johnny Doe.*** La Fontaine: What an ass…some people just cannot keep their egos under control man… ***Johnny Doe nods in agreement.*** Doe: Damn right man! ***La Fontaine smiles.*** La Fontaine: Bonjour, hola, hallo, ciao and hello. As you all know I am “The Bad Man” Joe La Fontaine…what you may not know is that all we professional wrestlers like to walk aimlessly about on rooftops looking like bad “mofos”…what you also may not know is that it is bloody expensive to rent one of these roofs out…no wonder J.J Dynasty was walking about with his face tripping him! Actually I know what you are thinking…and you are right…we do not all walk about on rooftops…yes I was talking crap! I don’t know, I can see why Dynasty likes hanging about up here…there are some spectacular views and you never know who is gonna drop in, Chad Kroeger, Josey Scott, U2, Sir Paul McCartney and even that goofy guy with the voice that narrates Thomas The Tank Engine! I fail to even imagine why he would also like to eat the crud that they serve up in the Furiously Addictive Wrestling canteens! You will not see any of the main players in there…not only does the food suck…but why would we want to go in there and rub shoulders with the common freaks like J.J Dynasty. Dynasty something confuses me…you like to talk about an “unparalleled rise”. Yes you are unparalleled…you suck more wiener than any other man I have ever seen. You may think, “oh La Fontaine is stupid boy, all he can do is question my sexuality…” That however would indeed be wrong because there is no question only an answer…sailor! ***La Fontaine walks over to Johnny Doe.*** La Fontaine: Hey John, do you think we should use “blood red” or “cowardly yellow”? ***Doe laughs.*** Doe: I have a feeling that “cowardly yellow” might be more appropriate for this particular job Joe… ***La Fontaine smiles, reaches out and slaps Johnny Doe’s hand. He reaches into a box at the edge of the roof. La Fontaine turns around and surveys the city skyline. The tops of buildings, amazing testaments to human ability and endurance, aeroplanes and helicopters, evidence that mankind have succeeded in there battle to claim the sky as their own, yet La Fontaine looks down on the masses that walk the streets. Beings not people. They simply be, they do not live. They walk faceless, emotionless, helpless. There is nothing they fear nor nothing that are not afraid of. Mankind’s existence is an enigma. Why have we evolved, adapted and survived for what we have now? It is indeed a mystery. Why with all of our know-how, technology, intelligence and superiority…are we so helpless. We do it to ourselves. We bring it on ourselves. We enjoy it. We war so our community are united against one common enemy. Why war? Can we not learn to get along? Are we as a race destined to disappear leaving only faint reminders of our past greatness? Are we to pass away like the creatures before us? If we are so advanced then why can we not live together in the old corny cliché of peace and harmony? I fear without living up to the cliché we are destined to fade to black. I fear without our inadequacies, war, destruction and self-destruction we would fade to black. It is a Catch-22 situation we wish to elevate our inadequacies but without them we could not survive. The wheel turns. La Fontaine turns. He clears his throat.*** La Fontaine: If we are going to do this…lets do it now! ***Doe smiles. He walks over to La Fontaine, he limps slightly, favouring his right leg. He pats Joe on the back. La Fontaine smiles as he shakes the spray can of “cowardly yellow” spray paint. Hypnotised by the familiar rattle. He stops. The plastic non-biodegradable cap is removed. He tests the can by spraying it into the air. “Cowardly yellow” spray paint hangs on the air before its density carries it down slowly to the ground. La Fontaine smiles. La Fontaine walks out into the open of the roof. He holds the can in one hand and spins madly, his arms outstretched as if he was being crucified. La Fontaine stops. He starts to go to work. Flashes of white and yellow paint drift on the air carrying with them their sweet scent. La Fontaine sprays madly, even having to renew his cans several times. After what seems like an eternity…an eternity that humanity has managed to somehow keep a grasp of…La Fontaine stops. His creation is done. It lives. A blur of yellow and white that can be seen from the helicopters and aeroplanes that fly above him. In large yellow and white lettering La Fontaine has sprayed “Dynasty Sucks!”. La Fontaine smiles. He shakes his head and heads over to Johnny Doe. La Fontaine throws the last empty can of paint over his shoulder. He puts an arm around Johnny Doe’s shoulder and together they head from the roof, leaving their mess behind them. J.J Dynasty has just been punked out. “Bad Man Styley”. La Fontaine and Doe make their way down to the black Lexus that La Fontaine has parked down at the roadside in front of the tall building. La Fontaine stops at the bottom of the fire exit and surveys the faceless ants that inhabit the city. Crawling through her hills. Infesting her streets. They are a pest. A pest that can be easily crushed. The great pest control man that started it all could easily take away the life of the ungrateful slime that inhabit his perfect world. His real life Garden of Eden. The World. Our Planet. Earth. La Fontaine stops on the pavement he is now an obstacle the crowd must negotiate. La Fontaine stops a man with silver hair and thick glasses, he carries a walking stick.*** La Fontaine: Excuse me but have you ever heard of Dynasty? ***The old man smiles revealing a yellow set of false teeth. The old man smells like shit.*** Old Timer: Yeah! Of course I’ve heard of Dynasty sonny… ***The old man’s false teeth force him to make a slight whistle when pronouncing his “S’s”. It is a great opportunity to poke fun and make a jibe at the old man…but he is just an old man that cannot help but whistle when he says his “S’s”. The old man smiles again before going on to elaborate.*** Old Timer: My wife loves Dynasty…she is a great fan of trashy soap operas…although Dallas was better wasn’t it? ***La Fontaine smiles and shakes the old man’s hand as he makes his way over to the parked black metallic Lexus.*** La Fontaine: Sure it was! Dynasty sucks and always will do, thank you for answering my question…although I could not understand everything you said…you whistle to much…and I also kind of switch myself off when I am talking to old people because all they talk about was the war and how hard it is to defecate and to piss! ***La Fontaine and Johnny Doe laugh at the shocked old man.*** La Fontaine: So there you have it J.J Dynasty “The Master Of Destiny”! You got your ass kicked last week on Breakdown and you will again on this week’s Breakdown…hey this is becoming a bit of a habit is it now? You know maybe you are in the wrong business Dynasty…I mean you obviously have not got the skills to get it done in the ring. Maybe you could go into television maybe do some commercials or maybe even star in a soap, Days Of Our Lives, The Bold And The Beautiful…you know something like that…that way you still get decent money…only this time you will not be getting your ass kicked will you? Or of course you could always do what George Foreman did and lend your name to some shady, dangerous kitchen appliance…yeah I really think that would suit you Dynasty! It is obvious to me your wrestling career is going absolutely no where… La Fontaine fires up the car’s powerful engine. La Fontaine has succumbed to temptation and mocked the old idiot. Ahh well. La Fontaine pulls away from the building with the pre-booked roof and spectacular views. No he is not headed to the Furiously Addictive Wrestling cafeteria…he would not be caught dead eating there. He does not know where he is headed just as many other people on this plain of existence do not know where their destination is. The wheel turns. The car turns. The car turns down under an old bridge. La Fontaine smiles as he reminisces.*** La Fontaine: You know Kryptonite I am genuinely quite happy that I am facing you on Distortion. And yes you are right I am very confident…I have one hundred and ten percent faith in my ability to wrestle in that ring…I believe I am the greatest mat technician in the world today…I believe that only Etan Cross is on a par with me. The reasons I am so glad to be facing Kryptonite at Distortion this week is because not only will he be a challenge in the ring…but he is also an interesting guy…much more intellectual that half of the scum that hide out in the Furiously Addictive Wrestling locker room…like J.J Dynasty for example. I could come out here and just try and punk Kryptonite out but I believe that this would not bother Kryptonite at all…he seems to take everything with a pinch of salt. In fact if I did not despise Kryptonite so much I actually might like the guy. I mean I would buy him a beer before I bought Dynasty, Prodigy, Bomber, Thunder, Anstex and a lot of the others. However be under no impression that after the upcoming match I will pick Kryptonite up off of his back, after he has just faded to “Bad Dreams”, embrace him and shake his hand. No. No. No. That is just something that will not happen at Distortion this week. Anything else could happen, it could indeed get very messy in that ring but when all is said and done I will not be commiserating his hand. Just as I do not expect him to get up off of his back and raise my arm. You know…I am really finding it kind of hard to resist, I might just have to punk him out…no…no…must resist. That would just be oh so immature. You know I just hope that J.J Dynasty “The Master Of Destiny”…does not get involved in our match because he is sour at the fact that I kicked him about, from pillar to post, from here to there, hither to…uhh…yonder, six ways from Sunday, eighteen ways from Thursday, forty-three ways from Tuesday…I do not care if I have to knock him into next December or kick his ass all the way back to last Julember! If, after I have kicked his ass, he makes an appearance at Distortion I swear to God I am going to do something so terrible that J.J Dynasty will regret it and he will come to rue the day that he ever got in “The Bad Man” Joe La Fontaine’s face, tried to punk him out and tried to cost him a match. This here is your official warning Dynasty after you have got your ass whipped in that ring do not cause yourself further embarrassment or any further harm by getting in my way again! ***La Fontaine walks in right under the bridge. He stands in the middle remembering past times. He walks over to a large concrete ledge, it is filthy, he sits on it like he used to. He reaches into his jacket pocket and pulls out a packet of cigarettes…X-Rodd’s favourites, Marlboro Reds, La Fontaine pulls one of the cigarettes out of the pack and rests it between his lips, he lights a match and shields it as he slowly brings it up to light the cigarette. He draws in deeply before releasing a cloud of smoke from his nose.*** La Fontaine: You know the only thing missing from here is a tarpaulin. You see I brought y’all here today because this is where I used to sleep when I was rough, this is where I used to do my drugs. Nice isn’t it? Well it is not much at all but it kept me safe when I needed it to, it also made me happy when I needed it to…I have been through a lot of crap under this bridge right here. However I have been through more crap in the ring. When I am here under this bridge I get an uneasy feeling but when I am in the ring I feel very, very much at home. I know every inch, every millimetre of that Furiously Addictive Wrestling ring. It has become my new home. No matter what city we are in, no matter what country we are in, no matter what continent we are in I always feel at home in the middle of that ring. I also got a lot of family that share that ring with me…Tony Vigilante…although we now have tremendous differences in that ring and there is no way that I will EVER team up with him, he cannot be trusted, but I would like to think that he would watch my back and I would do the same for him. You all know though that I roll with the nXn and I always will roll with the nXn hence I will always have the support of the nXn! Now I am not saying that the nXn will get involved in our match Kryptonite…hell I think it is going to be a heck of a match and that would just taint it. I know however that the nXn will be backstage in case somebody tries to pull something. Before you get up on your high horse and say you will not have anything done…I already know this. I know this for a fact because nobody likes you enough to actually help you out anyway man! Hah Hah! ***La Fontaine gets up off of the slimy concrete ledge. He tries to wipe some of the dirt from himself. He walks over to the entrance to the bridge, he stands perfectly erect and perfectly still. He looks out at the city he loves. The city he grew up in. The city he was born in. The city he got his first tooth in. The city in which he first walked. The city in which he spoke his first word. The city, which brought him his first girlfriend. His first car. His first beer. La Fontaine turns to Johnny Doe.*** La Fontaine: I have seen all that I wanted to see here…let’s go man… ***La Fontaine walks over and slowly opens the door of the flash black Lexus. He takes a final look back at the bridge where he used to sit and dream the world away. He stamps the cigarette out and gets in the car.*** Doe: So Joey where do you want to head to now? ***La Fontaine scratches his chin with one hand while steering with the other hand.*** La Fontaine: Let us go and see X-Rodd. The nXn will be represented by us both on Panic when Kryptonite will have the perfect opportunity to revenge his inevitable defeat at Distortion. He has Syd as his partner. Hah. I bet neither one of those guys were too happy. I mean Kryptonite is depressed enough as it is without the wonderful added bonus of the oh so happy Syd bringing him down! It will be good to go over what we are gonna do when Panic rolls around. I think you can already mark that one off as another victory for the nXn! I mean I know X-Rodd is not going to let the team down and hey…it is impossible, one hundred percent impossible, that I will screw anything up. Plus we will already have the advantage over Kryptonite thanks to my impending victory over Kryptonite at Distortion. I can see us easily rolling out of the arena in this car as the victors in that little tag team confrontation! ***Doe smiles.*** Doe: Yeah and we have also got Etan returning later next week! ***La Fontaine smiles it has been quite a while since he actually spoke to his old friend.*** La Fontaine: Yeah! I just cannot wait for Etan Cross to get back! Then the nXn will be tearing some serious shit up! Who is going to be able to stop the might of “The Bad Man” Joe La Fontaine, X-Rodd and the “Anti-Hero” Etan Cross, huh? Nobody, baby! That is exactly who, nobody! Whooo! ***La Fontaine twists and turns through the streets as he nears Rodd. The high power Lexus engine roars ferociously like a lion as they go forward. X-Rodd is no longer at Black Mountain, in the Crossroads Training Centre, he is in his flat on the Southside. La Fontaine takes another corner comfortably and they are at X-Rodd’s humble abode. La Fontaine and Doe step out of the car and enter the foyer of the luxury flats. La Fontaine tips the doorman on his way by. La Fontaine calls the lift. He watches the numbers above the lift flash in descending order until the lift doors slide open. The lift is empty. “The Bad Man” Joe La Fontaine and Johnny Doe get into the lift and press the appropriate level number. Cheesy lift music plays in the background as the lift hums its way up the shaft. Softly it comes to a halt on the top floor. X-Rodd owns one of the expensive penthouse suites. La Fontaine walks to the door and knocks hard on it three times. You can hear X-Rodd call from the inside.*** X-Rodd: Who is it? ***La Fontaine smiles.*** La Fontaine: It is the police! ***There is no sound from the inside for a couple of minutes.*** X-Rodd: There is nobody home… ***La Fontaine laughs.*** La Fontaine: Open the door you clown its La Fontaine! ***X-Rodd laughs.*** X-Rodd: I know…its open… ***La Fontaine opens the door and steps into the lavishly decorated flat. Johnny Doe follows La Fontaine in then closes the door behind him. La Fontaine embraces X-Rodd as he gets out of a chair, Johnny Doe steps forward and embraces him too. X-Rodd heads into the kitchen.*** X-Rodd: Hey guys have a seat…you want a beer? ***X-Rodd calls through to the two men who are sitting on plush leather arm chairs.*** La Fontaine: Yeah man. Beer would be good… ***X-Rodd comes through with three bottles of Budweiser. He passes a brown bottle to La Fontaine and then the other to Doe. X-Rodd searches frantically in his jean pocket.*** X-Rodd: Crap I’m outta cigs…can I bum a smoke? ***La Fontaine pulls the packet of Marlboro Reds back out of his pocket along with the matches. He tosses them to X-Rodd*** La Fontaine: Keep them. You know how Brits call smokes fags… ***X-Rodd smiles though he is a little confused. La Fontaine starts to laugh.*** La Fontaine: Do you think they ask if they can “bum a fag”? ***The three men in the room burst out laughing. So much so that La Fontaine almost knocks his beer over. X-Rodd lights the match and then the cigarette.*** La Fontaine: Well man you might have guessed…this is half social call…half business. You and me will be representing the nXn on Panic as we take on Syd and Kryptonite gets another bite of the cherry to try and put away “The Bad Man” Joe La Fontaine. Don’t worry man I am not here to say that you better study because you are not good enough, hell no, I am just here to make sure that we have covered all of the stuff…hell I have never actually faced Syd before so I will have to do my homework on him… ***X-Rodd exhales smoke and then smiles at La Fontaine.*** X-Rodd: Hey we got lots of time before Panic! You should be focusing on your match with Kryptonite on Distortion first… ***La Fontaine laughs.*** La Fontaine: I am fully prepared, Kryptonite does not stand a chance…”why?” I here you ask, well its simple. I’m smarter than him! I’m faster than him! I’m sexier than him! I’m funnier than him! And I Kryptonite I am most definitely…COOLER THAN YOU! …::::FADE TO BLACK::::…
