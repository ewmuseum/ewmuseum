Furiously Addictive Wrestling Roleplay Board Roleplay Board | Back to Roleplay Board Neo Entitled: perplexion Written: Location: The scene opens in a long empty warehouse the centre of the warehouse is illuminated by a high narrow window on the right wall of it. Loud echoing footsteps can be heard and Neo and Rayne burst into the lightened part of the warehouse and walk towards the camera Neo has his hair in red and black dreadlocks and has on a pair of plain black jeans and a centre clipped shirt similar to blades chest plate and his long black trench coat and a pair of gunmetal sunglasses with red lenses. Rayne wears a red lace corset and a long black rubber skirt covering her feet Neo steps to the camera and says: “Again the delusions of spawn flow as freely as the mountain stream, again he speaks of me with no relevance, he seems to have created a effigy of neo forged from many personalities and form into my likeness. Spawn seems to think I plagiarise a moron like the rock, do I walk you to the ring wearing enough oil to fry an entire centaur. Do I claim to be the peoples anything; do I spill forth catch phrases like rodepoo. Do I ask people to smell what I’m cooking? No I do not spawn again the grease paint the haha klown wears must be causing you to hallucinate. What images can be brought from the mind of a man already so deluded, that is a question worth thought. As Osiris and Azriel before me I have wandered broken into the darkness and been reborn, forged from the ghosts of my past I have made shadow my domain and with me I take with me my Isis Rayne the one who keeps my anger from surging forth toi the moronic masses of the general public. In your Boeotian ramblings spawn you seem to think I invent words to make me sound cool, do you wan me to give penance for the fact I have a firm grasp on language and its permutations or were you too stoned in you’re your to grasp the simplest fineries of the English language. This I ask you spawn when do I mention conflicts of the past when they bare no relevance on the events of now. Next time before you deem it necessary to spew forth you vocal drivel actually look at the person you are committing a character assignation against you have no clue who I am, yet you light the touch paper of my hatred try to find words of actual relevance as you simply show you for the fool you are Our match on panic was a scouting exploit for me nothing more you see spawn we have a nice little run together so I shall bide my time learning your idiosyncrasies in the ring then I shall exploit them. It is true that there is always someone better but on panic you did not face me as I am but how I chose to let you see me, To gauge your talents your weaknesses you cannot know a mans skills until you are face to face with him. So you are known to me spawn but my resolve still hides. The wounds you gave me simply act as physical outlet to my mental torment. So bring with you your urge to destroy me, pain is pure in it I shall revel as the blood falls from my brow you shall gain the revelation that I chose to stand there and bleed I choose to allow this injury on this shell. For us there is no botticellican vision there is only pain and blood, so let It be as I stand in that ring with the high priest of hypocrisy and there seemingly mute children added to the match. Yes the high priest of hypocrisy just look at your own promo your entire diatribe about you not needing catch phrases then to end with two entire catch phrases and one so clichéd that it has been said since the sacking of Constantinople I grow weary The somnambulist has spoken
