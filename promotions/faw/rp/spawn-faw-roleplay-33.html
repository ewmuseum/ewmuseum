Spawn — FAW Roleplay #33

captured 29 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
SpAwN--The Hell Master \~~~~~~~(opening scene)The scene opens in the backstage area of the NGW Sports Complex in one of the hallways. We see a few road agents pass by and a few tables with coffee and other things on them. Our scene remains like this for a few more seconds and then we see Spawn and Mike Stanton round the corner talking to each other. Our camera moves a little closer and then follows them down the hallway picking up the conversation. Mike: Do you believe the crowd out there tonight? That had to be one of the most retarded groups of assholes that I have ever been around. Spawn: Wait until Tuesday night; then I get to be in the presence of Vlad. He makes those morons out there look like Einstein. They both laugh and we discover that this is a few hours after the house show that featured the Spawn appreciation night. Our cameraman continues to follow them as Mike begins to speak again. Mike: You can beat his ass, a baby can beat his ass, hell, I think you could just about pick anybody at random and they would, well, devastate him. Spawn: Of course they would, hell, I could go into Tuesday Night Storm with one hand tied behind my back and blindfolded and I would still beat the shit out of this guy. On any day of the week I could…what the hell are you doing here? Spawn mouths those words as he spots the cameraman for the first time. He approaches him and grabs him by the collar as he speaks to him again. Spawn: I said, just what the hell do you think you’re doing here? Do you think you can just follow us around anywhere we go and tape everything we say? Who the hell do you think you are? You’re nothing more than a jackass cameraman that’s too stupid to get any other kind of job so they stick your face behind a Goddamn camera with a license to spy on anyone any time. Well I don’t think so! With that Spawn lands a right hand to the head of the cameraman who crumbles to the ground in a heap. Spawn continues to kick him as Mike Stanton finds a steel chair. Spawn picks up the cameraman and throws him into a vicious chair shot by Mike that levels the cameraman. He’s busted wide open as Spawn picks him up again and delivers a Last Kill on the concrete floor of the hallway. Mike Stanton gets down in his face and yells at him. Mike: That is what you get when you mess with the best in the business! You can tell all of your little buddies that that is what they have in store for them if they choose to follow us around any more. I told everyone before, when you mess with us; you’re going to pay the consequences! Spawn and Mike Stanton turn and walk down the hallway as the image on the fallen camera scrambles out into static. There are a few moments of static and then our scene fades back in to another camera behind a door. The door is cracked ever so slightly and we can hear voices coming from the other side. Our cameraman cautiously approaches the door and looks through the crack in it as we see Spawn and Mike approach us. Mike: Can you believe that jackass? Some people just won’t listen to the message and insist on being morons. Well, he just paid the price. Spawn: You’re damn right. And so will any other morons that get in our way. And…hey, look at this idiot. Spawn and Mike pass by the doorway as the cameraman edges the door open slightly. We see Spawn and Mike approach a man wearing a Vlad shirt and, as we can see as we come in closer, a backstage pass. Spawn shoves him and then speaks to him. Spawn: Just who the hell do you think you are? And how the hell did you get back here huh? Well sir, I have a uh, I have a back…a backstage pass uh, sir. Spawn: Oh, well how fucking convenient of you. So you think that gives you the right to hang around anywhere you want back here and eavesdrop on our conversations? Do you think you’re just some untouchable punk now that you got your Goddamn pass? Well, I just… Mike begins to speak now to the obviously terrified fan. Mike: Shut the hell up! You don’t know who your dealing with guy. This is the best wrestler in this company! We don’t take shit from anyone, including some punk with a backstage pass! But I won this in a… Mike: I don’t give a shit how you got it! You were eavesdropping and I don’t appreciate that! Spawn doesn’t appreciate that! You’re lucky we don’t kick your ass right now! Mike throws the guy up against the wall and Spawn and him start to leave. As they pass the guy they turn around and Mike chuckles a little. Then he speaks to the still terrified fan. Mike: You didn’t think we were going to let you off the hook that easy did you? We’re not idiots, although judging by your t-shirt you seem to be one of the biggest idiots of all of those people out there. Tell me, do you really think that this newcomer Vlad has a chance to beat Spawn this Tuesday night? Well uh… I think that um, he has a good shot at, at beating Spawn. Spawn rolls his eyes and then gets in the guy’s face and speaks to him. Spawn: Boy, that was a really dumb answer you know that. I mean, out of all the possible responses to that you say that I am going to be handed my ass by that idiot? Mike grabs the guy by the shirt and pushes him against the wall before he has a chance to answer. Mike tears the t-shirt off of the guy and then tears it into pieces and looks back at the guy. He screams his next lines to him. Mike: This piece of shit doesn’t deserve to have anybody come out and cheer him. He doesn’t deserve one hell of a thing. The man that deserves all of the attention is standing next to me. Spawn deserves your money. He deserves your praise. Not some lunatic that is learning how to read invitation notes to entertain you sick morons. The obviously horrified man shakes against the wall and begins to stutter out the following words. Well, I uh… I didn’t m…mean any disrespect g…guys. I…I’ll b…buy a Spawn s…shirt. I…I’ll d…do…anything, j…just d…don’t hurt m…me! Mike pushes the man aside and then scoffs at him. He begins to speak to the very scared man. Mike: All right big shot, you want to get out of this alive huh? You want to prove something to us? Fifty pushups, right now! Go ahead, get started you idiot! A...are you s…serious? Spawn pushes the man up against the wall and talks in his face. Spawn: Maybe you didn’t hear the man. He said fifty pushups and I want to see you do each and every last one of them. We don’t have all day! The man drops to the ground and begins to do the pushups as Spawn and Mike stand over him watching. He gets to about thirty and Mike disappears off the screen. Spawn continues to watch as he gets up to forty and then Mike returns to the shot with a steel chair. He hands it to Spawn, who holds it until the man gets to fifty and the pastes him with a brutal shot to the back. Spawn lands about ten stiff chair shots to the man’s back before he tosses the chair down the hallway. Mike picks the man up and hands him to Spawn. Spawn throws him to Mike, who meets him with a vicious karate Kick right to the jaw! After that Spawn picks up the unconscious man and sets him up for a Last Kill. Mike Stanton goes down the hall a few feet and grabs the chair that Spawn threw and places it underneath the skull of the fan. A few seconds later Spawn drops him with a Decapitator on the steel chair. As Spawn gets back up both he and Mike start to laugh as Mike yells out the following at the unconscious fan. Mike: That's what happens when you mess with the best in this business! It doesn’t matter if it’s a lowly cameraman, a pathetic fan, or that jackass hero of yours Vlad, when you mess with Spawn you had better be prepared to handle the consequences! Spawn and Mike Stanton turn around and begin to walk towards the exit of the arena as the cameraman focuses on the bloodied unconscious body of the Vlad fan. We remain on his figure for a few seconds until the scene leaves us and fades to black.
