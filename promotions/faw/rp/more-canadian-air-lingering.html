More Canadian air lingering... Damien Ryan Furiously Addictive Wrestling Roleplay #5 Date: 10.15.02 Location: Doctor's office, Toronto, Canada (the screen opens on Damien Ryan and a doctor behind him. They are in a room in a hostpital. The room is all white with cabinets, tables, and counters with all kinds of materials on them. Ryan is sitting on bed and the doctor seems to be stitcking up a wound on Ryan's back. We can see by Ryan's facial expressions that he is in some pain, yet he's taking it. He's wearing jeans yet obviously no shirt. His hair is dryed out a frizzy as if he had probably stayed there over night. There is a small cart with some food on it, doesn't look like Ryan ate much.) Doctor: Well, you really took a good one Damien. Ryan: Yeah, and when I find out the fucker who slammed my back with a chair I'll tear him a new asshole! Doctor: Alright, you're all set buddy, just try to watch yourself over the week, come back on Saturday, we'll keep the stitches in but put a special bandage and wrap to keep it from being exposed and you'll be all set for your match on Sunday. Ryan: Thanks doc. Doctor: Have a good one, see you Saturday. (Ryan walks out of the room and finds his shirt that was on some chair and slowly puts it on trying not to touch or affect his stiched back. He walks out the automatic doors into the parking lot of the hospital, he reaches his pick-up truck when a FAW camera crews stops him, Jeff Harris has a mic) Jeff: Damien! A word, please. Ryan: Alright, I mean I haven't said a word in like weeks, what's up guys. Jeff: You picked up the win last night, but what the hell happened afterwards? Ryan: Let me just start off by saying that Sin put up one hell of a god damn match. Yet after I walked away with my title and randomly some cowards shuts off the lights in the place and feels he has to beat the shit out of me with a god damn chair. What the hell, or who the hell did this? I obviously don't know, do you know? (Jeff shakes his head) The only person who knows, is the person himself who did it. And when he stops being a fucking coward, he'll come out and say he did it. Then I'll hunt him down and drag his ass out down to the ring on Sunday and I will beat his ass in front of the thousands watching that night and the millions at home watching on TV. AND THAT will give you your ratings FAW. What trouble that caused me last night...I'm pissed now. I had to somehow stop the bleeding and they made me stay here overnight, and now I got stiches and have to wrestle less than one hundred percent, especially again a main eventer, a top contender for the world title. I will find out before this Sunday and I will walk into Distortion and hunter looking for his prey. Jeff: Foamy? Ryan: The 9th Wonder of Foam...definetly a superstar here in FAW. He's one of the greats and probably go down in the books as one of the all time best performers here in FAW. I have a lot of respect for the man, especially being somebody who is anti-DCV. I personally, after only being new here, have had an experience with those nitwits already. I had a match a few weeks back where they randomly attacked me because I beat one of their scrub wrestlers! Now, I'm not going to go soft on Foamy just because I dislike DCV, because I'm not like that and I expect to be at his level in the game pretty soon. Foamy, I'm glad I've been booked in this match, it's definetly a challenge, it's definetly going to be an anticipated match, and it's definetly going to be an honor....an honor to kick your foamy ass! Welcome to Canada. Jeff: Ryan, anything else? Ryan: Yes actually, I'd like to bring back an old problem of mine. How are all you ratings guys doing? All you reporters and critics? All you reviewers? Look at my record boys...UNDEFEATED! I think it's about time that you'd figure out where this Canadian youth is coming from, and what his mission is. I think it's time for you to notice that the Maple Leaf has been ranked top ten since he's been, peaking fourth place. I don't know who's paying you around here, but you better smarten up and know your shit, because you guys are nothing but crap and wouldn't know the color of the own shit that comes out of your ass! MONEY's WORTH! THATS WHAT PEOPLE GET SUNDAY'S WHEN SEEING ME! (he gets in his truck, closes the door, starts the engine and drives off leaving the camera crew standing there, Jeff, grins, shrugs his shoulders and turns to the camera) Jeff: Alright folks, words from your Hardcore Champion, we'll see you later. (camera fades to black)
