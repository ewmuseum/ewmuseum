A Call From A Friend Renegade Furiously Addictive Wrestling Roleplay #23 Date: 18/10/02 Location: On The Road (Renegade has just left his house in Orlando Florida and is making his way down to Miami for this weeks Distortion. He’s cursing along the highway, the beefed-up stereo of his Dodge Intrepid blasting out “Poem” by Taproot, when his cell phone rings) RING RING RING (Renegade quickly turns the stereo down and picks up the phone off the passengers seat, looks at the screen and smiles when he sees who the calls from) Renegade: Hey dude. What’s up? Caller: ……………………………………………………… (The combination of the noise from the traffic and the music, makes it impossible to know what the other person is saying) Renegade: Yeah I’m not to bad thanks mate. My neck is still acting up like, but I should be ok to compete this week. Just gotta take it easy until then. Nothing to strenuous. Caller: ……………………………………………………… Renegade: Yeah true. It wont be easy against The Duke and Crimson but I think we can pull off the win. Caller: ………………………………………………………… Renegade: Yeah don’t worry, I aint forgot about Apoc. I’ve summut planned for him down the line. Summut BIG. Revenge will be a bitch! And outta everyone I know, you know what revenge at the hands of Renegade is all about. Caller: ………………………………………………………… Renegade: Yeah I’ve heard the rumours. I rung the front office as soon as I heard like to tell them I knew shit. Just in case they were gonna dock my wages or summut. Hell, this lifestyle don’t come cheap ya know. What kind of an idiot misses his match anyway?! (laughs) Caller: ………………………………………………………… Renegade: Hey, you said it not me. (laughs) Anyway I’ll let you go. I know ya got some business to take care of. I’ll give you a ring once I get to Miami so we can meet up somewhere to discuss…Well, you know what. Caller: ………………………………………………………… Renegade: Yeah see ya later man. (Renegade hangs up the phone, tosses it back onto the passengers seat and cranks the tunes back up. He sits back in his seat, the music filling his ears and vivid images of what lies ahead filling his head. A small sly smile creeps across his face as the scene fades out to black)
