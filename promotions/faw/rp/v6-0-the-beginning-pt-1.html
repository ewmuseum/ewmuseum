V6.0 (The Beginning) (pt.1) Violator Furiously Addictive Wrestling Roleplay #126 Date: 12/29/02 Location: Unknown (You could think of it as a job, an occupation by which you make ends meet, and something to help supply you with worldly things. If you are good enough at what you do you move up in its ranks, prestige and glamour an added incentive of your acceleration, culminating to that crescendo, when you stand as the best of the best. Many see it this way, and that is the one reason they sit dormant stand at the bottom rung, struggling mightily to step up to that next level and to reap the benefits, they don’t understand, they can’t understand…until now.) (The FAW is a place of infinite opportunities and anyone who possesses the right amount of ability can unlock these spoils of war. But only those which contain every trait can step into this limelight, and though you may not hold every aspect needed with time comes understanding, and lessons to which you may grow, and obtain the aforementioned traits, pushing you along in your quest to reach that nirvana, the instance every individual in this particular world strives for. But you dare step into this world believing that what you do is considered a job, you shall perish where you stand because you have no deserving ability to be here, known true by your uttering of those sacrilegious phrases. What you do is far from a job, it is a lifestyle, a submersed world where you live each day of your lives, you become it, you create it, you are it. Nothing else matters but dominance in its ranks, failure only an afterthought, and perfection only a three count away. This is what the best of the best understand; this…is the true meaning of what is universally known as the battle of titans, of the elite, of FURIOUSLY ADDICTIVE WRESTLING!) (The avenging angel, returning from the depths of eternity to change his destiny, and to finish a chapter in his life, that is why he is here. They welcome him back with doubt in their heart, and malice which morphs into the form of opposition. They proceed in this tedious manner because they do not understand what he is, and why he is. They see this as a match; he sees it as a challenge to his destiny, fate dancing with the opposition, only for his response. There is no afterlife for him, only the challenge, only the lure of being the best, and that is why he drives, that is the reason that he continues to fight and that is the reason that he is there, awaiting his first challenge.) “Welcome Back” “Xing” (FADE IN) (The darkness swirls around him as he stands basking in the glory of the night. The full moon plays tricks with the universe as a red glow covers the earth, and the choppy waters surround him, threatening to swallow his soul whole, with one misplaced step. But he is still, staring towards a distant landmass, shrouded by the darkness but still silhouetted by the reddish moon, whispering for his attention. His breathing is methodical and deeply covered, complementing the vicious air around him in an attempt to bring his long black leather coat to life, it lifting to the air neck high, and flying gallantly in his wake, foreshadowing his hastened first movement, a sign of life from his existence.) “Silence is Death, let life breathe from his veins, and pour from his lips” (The wind subsides and the lightening ceases, the water calm and the moon’s red glow seem to flow towards this figure, as the world almost stops for this one instance, for this one occurrence. His breathing begins to hasten, one deep breath after another, his chest pulsating with the vibrant primitive dance from his heart. All at once from his loins a fire swells, and is released into the air as a magnificent yell, shaking the earth as this figure tilts his head to the sky, and continues to call towards the universe for attention, for respect, of his existence. His head slowly begins to return to normal positioning as his mouth closes and the echo crosses the land and seascape. One more deep breath comes to be in order, and from that, finally…his lips spread…and the experience begins.) Figure: I…am…VIOLATORRRRR! Understand that and the rest shall comes as simple mathematics to you, if you dare want to know of my reason being here. I am the avenging icon, the truth revealed once again for the world to take heed, and infinity’s chosen warrior. When I left something was undone, many tasks unfinished, a piece of this puzzle left unscathed. I apologize for that, seeing as I left this entire place without a witness to greatness, without a true champion. The water from a teardrop is enough to move an entire nation, and from these words, I shall move you all as well. Xing is what stands in my way now, he is the task at hand for my so-called “Welcome Back” celebration. Many things have transpired and changed since my first tenure in these hollowed halls, but one thing that stays true and constant is the drive for perfection by each and every individual here, all fighting for the right to be known as the one true champion. At the moment only one can call himself the champion, being the 9th Wonder of Foam. But as much as I would enjoy stepping into that ring against the likes of his stature for that title I am not worthy at this moment, I am only worthy of one individual, and that is Xing. I know that you do not fear me Xing, why should you? You have no reason to do no such thing seeing as you know nothing of me, and of what I can accomplish in that ring. The same could be said on my behalf, meaning that you and I stand on a level playing field, adjacent from one another of the path of destruction. Taste the knowledge I am giving to you my friend, because it shall come quick and sharp as a knife, and shall cut through you without unmitigated malice and hatred, for you are the opposition hindering my progress, you are that which I have to despite, only for a moment, in my quest for destiny. (Violator steps forward, the waters parting to reveal a silver lined walkway, the word “Violator” spelled down its center leading as far as the eye can see. Violator begins to tread forwards, continuing his conversation, with the moon glow illuminating his determination henceforth.) Violator: Am I a sadist…hardly. A misguided evangelist working towards his own view of heaven on earth, don’t humor me. All that I am is he, is this, Violator. FAW is my life now, and there is nothing more to be said and understood about that, only that I plan to become the best, I feel that I will become the best, and I know that I shall become the best, no matter what steps into my world. I walk the path of fire with bare feet because I do not fear reality, only the contents of a reality without this, an existence void of this drug. You do not dare stand in the way of an individual who breathes this life, and sees this not as a wrestling match, but a trail by fire. January 5th the reckoning will be at hand, and reborn will be the icon, reborn shall be infinity, REBORN…shall be Violator. Violator: A torrid novelist once wrote that death is only the protocol of failure, and in death a lesson is learned. That lesson being that life is a struggle, and nothing more. The objective of this struggle is to hold on as long and hard as you can, because in the end everything will perish, and in that will you perish as a mortal, or as a legacy. Make your decision if you will Xing, but remember, at the end of that road I shall be waiting, and with me, Judgment Day shall not be far behind. Heed my world well my friend, heed them well. (Violator continues down that elongated path, his body becomes on with the sky, and the blurred vision of his existence slinking softly in the distance. We slowly fade out, and the completely darkness is with us once again. We have seen the future, and now, it is time to witness the present and the past. Welcome to the rebirth, welcome…to Judgement day!) TBC…
