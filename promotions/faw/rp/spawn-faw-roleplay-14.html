Spawn and Demon begins the fight, the two man lock up back and forth they go till Spawn gets the upper hand, no clean break is giving once on the rope and Spawn gives Demon a kick to the midsection, he then grabs him by the hair, picks him up and body slams him, he goes to the ropes climbs up to the second rope and comes down with a knee to the stomach, Demon is in a lot of pain right about now, Spawn grabs him by the arm and drags to his and Mike’s corner, he then tags Mike who climbs the top rope and does a frog splash on Demon. Mike stays on top for the cover but Demon kicks out at the count of two. Mike then picks up Demon and swing him to the rope he tries to clothe line him but Demon ducks and comes back with a clothesline of his own he then Picks Mike up from the floor and throws him out side of the ring, he follows Mike out side of the ring grabs a chair and whacks Mike on the back with it, he then throws Mike back inside the ring and tags Skinner. Skinner begins to talk to Mike. man(skinner): Get up bitch your days are now numbered this is all for my boy Skipster. Skinner grabs mike by the hair and starts to slap him around while talking at the same time. man(skinner): What you gonna do (still slapping him,) you fucking hoe do something. Mike is down on the floor Demon calls over Skinner and tells him to tag him, but Skinner refuses instead he goes outside and grabs a chair and brings it inside to whack Mike with it. But Mike who by this time had gotten up gives Skinner a low blow, Skinner drops the chair and Mike grabs it hitting him over the head with it, not once but three times. Now is Mike turn to talk to skinner who is practically out of it. mike: Who looks fucked up now bitch not me that’s for sure I think is time to finish this shit and shut your mouth once and for all. Mike grabs the chair and whacks Skinner who is motionless on the floor he then goes for the cover but Demon gets in a breaks it up. Spawn sees this and gets in also whacking Demon on the face then kicking him on the belly setting him up for the last kill. After spawn gives Demon the last kill he throws him outside of the ring, he then goes over to Mike grabs him and presses him high over his shoulder slamming him on top of Skinner for the pin the referee counts 1,2,3, and announces Mike and Spawn as the winners, Pyro then goes up to the ring and celebrate with his friend as the fans goes wild,. The scene then switches over to Spawn, Mike and Pyro in the locker talking. mike: I showed that fool I am not to be taken lightly, they won’t call me a little runt anymore that’s for sure. Pyro: You did real good Mike, Skipster ,Spider and Shawn must be shitting in their pants right about now, what do you think Spawn? Spawn: I feel you and I’m pretty sure they are but I bet you any amount of money Skipster is probably hiding under his sheet right about now, hell he might not even show up for Summer Slaughter, but he better or I will go to his house and drag his ass to the main event, You know what is amazing the punishment that I will give these three bozos will shock and amaze everyone and it will all be worth it for the FAW world tittle. Pyro: Alright it’s celebration time so let’s get out of here. Spawn, Mike, and Pyro, leave the arena as the scene fades to black
