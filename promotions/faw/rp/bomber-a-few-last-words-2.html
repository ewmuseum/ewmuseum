BOMBER A FEW LAST WORDS

10/16/02
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
BOMBER A FEW LAST WORDS Spawn Furiously Addictive Wrestling Roleplay #18 Date: 10/16/02 Location: DISTORTION RP 4 The scene opens inside the home of Spawn. He stands next to a window looking out at his lawn and the woods past it. The camera moves around an oak dining table and to the left hand side of Spawn. He remains seemingly unaware of the camera and stares out the window still. He wears a pair of baggy blue jeans and a red plaid shirt unbuttoned over top of a plain white t-shirt. On his feet is a pair of black and white Reeboks. He continues to look out the window with his back to the camera. Seconds pass by and then he begins to speak as he looks out the window. Spawn: In life all good things come to an end and good things come to those who wait. This holds true this week at Distortion as well. For Bomber it is the end of a forgettable run in this company trying to be something that he is not. He is not the best wrestler that we have. That distinction belongs to myself. For me I have waited over a year. It has been a struggle spanning over a decade and in a few months if not weeks it will reach its conclusion. That struggle is of course my fight for legendary status. I have waited and soon the status will be mines to carry. I have also waited a long time to face Bomber once again in the ring in a one on one wrestling match. Since he first came to this company I have watched his matches and I was impressed with his ability. I wanted to fight him but I had bigger things on my mind then. Now the time has come for us to wrestle each other. The world will soon witness a magnificent wrestling match that will go down as a classic. They will also be privileged enough to see the greatest wrestler to ever lace up a pair of boots. They will see me walk away with the win over a great, but lesser opponent. The stage has been set for an epic this Sunday. It will prove to be the match that begins my ascension towards legendary status in wrestling history. Spawn raises his right arm and rests it against the wall of his house. He continues to look out the window without turning back to the camera. A few seconds pass by and then he begins to speak again. Spawn: Bomber and others may claim that I am not the man that I was. They may claim that I used to be great and then I threw it all away. That is not the case. I am more dangerous now then I ever was. Some people ignore that and then they end it up paying for it dearly later on. Some people choose to gamble that way and it always comes back to haunt them later. Many people have underestimated me Bomber. I just hope that you are not one of them. I do not think that you are, but either way, you are in for the toughest battle of your pro career this Sunday. You are going into a match that nothing can prepare you for. Nothing can prepare you for the fight you are about to start, a fight that will be the highlight of your career unfortunately. It will be as far as you ever go in this company. Spawn turns from the window and looks into the camera. For a moment he does not say a word as he stands there in silence. Then a few more seconds go by and he begins to speak once again. Spawn: There is a simple and terrible reason why you will never make it farther than a one on one main event match with me in this company. Despite your obvious talents and abilities, there are men at the top in this company who are prejudice. Whether they want to admit it or not, they cannot stand the fact of a bozo like you challenging them for the top prize. I am okay with it because I know that I am better than you. I know that you might be a great wrestler, but I am and will always be better than you are. I know I can defeat you so I do not care about facing you. But there are other people that know you are better than them and so they are scared to face you. They are scared to be beat by a hall of famer. It is unfortunate, but it is the truth. You will never make it to the very top in this company. I hope you prove me wrong because one day you and me should be headlining a pay-per-view fighting for the World Heavyweight Title. That day is a long way off, however. Spawn looks down at the floor for a moment thinking about his next words. He raises his head back up and eyes the camera. A second passes by and then he begins to speak again. Spawn: I can relate to you in some ways. Back when I was struggling on the independent scene, I was never accepted to the main event. No one at the top wanted to fight me and the reason for that was simple. I was too good. They were afraid that they would look bad if they were put in the ring with me because they knew I would outperform them. They were right too. I never really got a chance to shine at the top until I got an attitude in this promotion. The very thing that you say brought me down was the one thing that got me over and into the main event consistently. Maybe that is what you need to do. Maybe you need to get an attitude and just go around giving everyone a Tiger Driver or something. You need something to make it to the top because right now everyone up there is too afraid to fight you because of their precious image. It is a sad thing, but unfortunately it is true. Spawn looks around the room for a minute, his eyes wandering from the camera for a few seconds and then back to it. As he eyes the camera once more, he begins to speak once again. Spawn: Bomber thinks that I forsake my honor in going after Xander. You thinks that I let my emotions get in my way and tried to play the sympathy card.You could not be more wrong. It was because I was a wrestler with honor that I had to go after Xander. It was that exact reason why I had to target him in the first place. It was not come pathetic cry story to try and get the fans on my side. I seen a man come in here and get something that he had not earned back. I believe that you have to earn your rewards and this was something that he did not do. It was because of that, that I went after him in the first place. It was not to make up for some mistakes that I have made. It was not a desperate act to get sympathy, as you would have everyone to believe. It was because he came in here and immediately got cheered for doing absolutely nothing while I got ignored when I was putting on wrestling clinics every week. This time around things will be different. This time around people will notice me and I will have that legendary status when the time comes. Spawn looks back out the window and then down to the floor as he shakes his head. A chuckle comes from him and then he turns back to the camera. A moment later he begins to speak again. Spawn: Bomber claims that I am letting my greed and my lust after winning the World Heavyweight Title cloud my judgment. He thinks it is all about the company, and I agree to some degree, but where would the company be without the wrestlers? Where would the sport be without the wrestlers? There would not be a company, any fans, or a sport at all if it were not for the wrestlers. I happen to be a great wrestler. I am not going to be modest about it; I am going to show off my abilities. I respect the business and I also know what I can do in the ring. I know that I can beat you in a wrestling match. I know that I am the better of the two of us and I will prove it at Survival. Spawn pauses for a moment as he looks around the room. A few seconds pass by without a word. Then he breaks the silence as he begins to speak again. Spawn: Bomber seems to be making the same mistakes that many other people have before him. He is falling into the trap of name-calling already. He of all people is wasting his time calling me names. I would not have expected it from him, but I guess that everyone can fall into it some times. No matter what the case may be, he is in for a tough battle at Distortion, one that he cannot win. There is a slight pause from Spawn and then he begins to speak again. Spawn: Bomber also claims that I am greedy and that I think everything is owed to me. He thinks that I think I should have had have the World Title for a long time now and he is right. I do deserve it, as I am the best wrestler here. Is the World Heavyweight Title not for the best wrestler in the company? I am that man and so should I not have the belt around my waist? It is simple really. I deserve the best strap because I am the best wrestler. He does not seem to realize that me earning and winning the World Heavyweight Title would not be the fulfillment of my own greed, but rather the good thing for the company. She said that I do not care about the company and that I only look out for myself. But with me as the champion would the company not flourish? It would seem that a wrestling company would excel with a fantastic wrestler at its helm. I guess Bomber overlooked that little point though. Spawn looks down at the ground for a moment as a smile forms over his mouth. He begins to speak shortly after that, raising his head up slowly to meet the camera as he speaks. Spawn: Now Bomber comes out and attacks my moveset, saying that for a great technical wrestler I have not expanded it. He says that I have used pretty much the same moves during my stay here. For the most part he is right. I did add a few new moves, namely the Decapitator, but for the most part it has remained the same. Do you want to know why? Because most of the time my current moves are more than enough to get the job done. Most of the time I can get out with a win with a simple Wrecking Ball. There are times when I bust out some new moves, but usually I do not find myself in that predicament. I have the knowledge of them. I know them. I could break out some Japanese move if I wanted to, but I do not need to. But, since you insist on nitpicking about my moveset, I think I might just have a little something special for you at Distortion. It will be adding insult to injury when I inevitably defeat you this Sunday at Distortionl. It will be like something out of a bad dream. But it will be all too real for you. Spawn turns back around to face the window as the camera moves out a bit. He stares out the window wit his hand above his head, resting on the log walls. The camera fades back just a little bit more as we continue to look in on Spawn. Then seconds later, the scene fades to black.
