We did it again 9th Wonder Of Foam Furiously Addictive Wrestling Roleplay #68 Date: 08/11/02 Location: Foamy Hills …. Foamy Hills – Friday …. (Melissa Farrow walks slowly through the corridors of the mansion, the lights are now a red hue, she and her crew open Foamy’s study door, they walk inside, Foamy sits by his chair draped in darkness, on the walls are pictures of the great men of the 20th Century, Mussolini, Jack The Ripper, O.J. Simpson and David Crosby) Melissa: Foamy? (Lars appears from nowhere) Lars: Yes, what do you want, who wishes to speak with Mr.Foam. Melissa: Melissa Farrow of FAW. Lars: Is she worthy Mr.Foam? Foamy: Just about. (Melissa sits down on the chair opposite Foamy) Foamy: What do you want? Melissa: An interview, we need to know why you turned your back on the fans, why you joined the Dark Carnival and your thoughts on Spawn and The Great One, plus where Neo stands in all of this. Foamy: Who are you to ask such questions? Why should I have to give answers to the likes of you? Melissa: Because the fans need to know. Foamy: I don’t give a shit about the fans. But seeing as you ask, for the entire stretch of my career thus far the EVIL! Ones have always got the gold, the place in history, until Disaster Down Under I was just a joke, a fool, someone too eager to play to the fans attention than for what I really wanted, and I almost did it like that. But it wasn’t good enough. Melissa: But why the DCV? Foamy: Because Melissa, the DCV does not stand for “Daring Christian Virtuosity” it stands for “Dark Carnival eVolution” Under the guidance of Ha Ha Klown I shocked the world when I joined their ranks. We are now the stuff of nightmares, what was once thought impossible has happened; Foamy has joined them, and now works for himself, not the fans. Melissa: So what is the plan of the invigorated DCV? Foamy: Simply? To hurt, destroy, maim and to win. We want to make those who would smite us cry, we want them to realise the power that we yield and to realise that no longer will we be made corporate pushovers. We had to get rid of the weak, in Spawn we then re-grouped and are now ready to dish out some Carnival Style punishment, with the Klown guiding us FAW will be unprepared for the terror that we shall unleash. Melissa: So this Sunday you face the Great One and Spawn in a tag team match, your partner? Neo. Foamy: Ah yes Neo, I did not even tell him of my plans. I hope Neo can impress us, perhaps there is a space in the DCV for him one day. But should Neo take on step back instead of forward then myself, Rzarector, Ha Ha Klown and The Bomber will take our vengeance out upon him. Neo will follow my rules, just as he always has, and if he doesn’t like being my bitch, like he always has then we are going to have to teach him a harsh lesson. Melissa: So what about Spawn and The Great One, your opponents? Foamy: Well The Great One teams up with the man he was ready to kill not long ago, it just goes to show how desperate they both are to defeat me. Both men are champion that also shows the world that neither was good enough to defeat the other. This Sunday I will one up them both by killing their careers at the same time. Spawn a word of warning, I am not the all laughing, fun making Foamy One I was when we last met, I have changed, I have seen the error of my ways, and now I have insurmountable rage, but it is not uncontrollable as you seem to think. Melissa: Why? Foamy: Because now I have a purpose, I will become World Champion at any cost, whether it be the fans adulation, the health of my co-workers or even my own life. Plus should anything go wrong I have the Dark Carnival to back me up, what does Spawn have? A bunch of enemies and a poor mans DCV in the Nation. We kick you out of the DCV because you aren’t good enough then you form the Diet Coke of Evil in the Dark Nation? Your poor mans brand of evil will not win this time. Because now Mr.Foam is ruthless, he will stop at nothing, I am no longer that idiot I was, I shocked you at Down Under Spawn and on Sunday night I will give you the biggest shock of your life, which co-incidentally shall be your last.
