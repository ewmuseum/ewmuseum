Spawn — FAW Roleplay #15

captured 8 August 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
SpAwN--The Hell Master \~~~~~~~(opening scene)California knows how to party. 2pac and Dr. Dre’s song “California Love” plays in the background, as we’re greeted with a black screen. The scene begins to fade forward; and the song begins to become louder. The camera is an overhead cinematic camera view of the Los Angeles streets. The day is bright; the sun shines down and reflects off of automobiles and buildings. The camera zooms in, coming closer to the ground, as we fade to another camera, on ground level. A car rests in front of the camera, an H2, actually. Spawn’s blue H2. The window is fixed, and what have you. The song still plays loudly, until a door is opened, and slammed. Suddenly, “I Stand Alone” by Godsmack plays rather loudly, the screen beating with the bass, until finally we see Spawn walk out from behind his beast of a vehicle. Spawn wears an Boston Redsox’ baseball cap, and a Red sox t-shirt, black as is the hat, and black Route 66 jeans. His shoes are white Lugz shoes, the shoe strings tied neatly. Spawn walks towards the camera, then passes it, then the camera turns to follow him. Instead of being greeted with the backside of Spawn, we’re caught with the man’s face. You’re forced to jump, stunned by Spawn’ actions. Spawn speaks, the song dying out. “Who will call me a moron next? Vlad did once, I beat his ass. Mike, tried to tell me what to do, and called me a moron… I sent him flying out of an Ireland Hotel. Omega Fro from FAW called me a blabbing drunkard, and I give him a fucking Killer Crossover to win the match. That’s not even my fucking finisher, and it finished him. Everyone in the NGW seems to be underestimating me. Now comes Death , another fool fighting me the Hell Master on his first match, fucking shit, you challenged me, you fucking challenged me to this match. I believe your words were something like, ‘the winner of this match gets to decide our next match’ or something retarded like that, I have endured pain, suffering, torment, and betrayal since I've made the NGW my home. At first my loyalty was in the New Breed. My loyalty is now and forever will be strong, but no longer will it rest with the squad called the New Breed, no.....for I have a love that goes deeper than anything the New Breed could ever stir, and that is to my finisher. . .The Last Kill. She has always stood by my side. When the NGW forgot the pain and suffering I went through in my matches, when the fleeting affection of the fed that took friends and family from me, quickly flew to the newest flash in the pan. But then it occured to me, who has always stood by my side? New Breed? Crimson? Mike? NO!!!! I have only always had The Last Kill. And now, more than ever she will have me to protect her and guide her. So, Death I guess you have forgotten the fear of Evil, I am here to put it back in your heart. For any who forgot what it felt like to be scared going into a match, I will remind you in the most savage and brutal fashion imaginable. I am Spawn and I WILL NOT be beat. For those of you who have heard the negative press from the likes of Death, never forget, that I have set the standard for the lowlifes and humps that one day dream of being something. I have ruled longer and harder in this sport than any other. My name has been a whispered legend before they had second rate second stringers like Vlad and now you Death. I was setting records and breaking the rules when the rest of these pantywaists were still learning how to do a clothesline. I've beat every real talent that has ever graced the once mighty sport, and I will beat and lesser posers that think they have what it takes. Make no mistake, I am the monster that the shadows warn you about. So kiss your mothers for the last time, make your will out and step into the ring, because Spawn the Hell Master is about to rule the world. So come one and come all, for the Monster has spoken. So how does it feel Kidd.....how does it feel knowing that a monster is coming for you to destroy you on your first match. There is nothing you can do about it. No one will stand in my way...not Crimson...not Vlad...and not the New Breed. I will not be stopped. Not even by a punk like you. And you will soon learn, Kidd, that there is a time when the king comes but home and the jester must get out of his chair or pay the price. So guess what Kidd Jester, I'm back and it is time for you to remove your ass from my throne before NGW fans all over the world see you get buried in the middle of the ring. Because if you don't use your better judgement and just stay home and not suffer any shame. Then the ring will become a very violent place for you. It will be a very brutal resting place for whatever is left of your body. My unforgiving fists and unforgiving feet will brand your body with bruises that will tatt my hatred for you letting your friends piss on my pride. I will bury you. Death isn't the only beast in this fed, hell he is not even an insect, like I said before you are just a punk is all. It used to be about respect, Kidd...now it is about blood...your blood!!! Spawn turns from the camera, walking down the sidewalk, leaving his H2 parked on the side of the street. The sun shines down on Spawn, creating a spotlight, par say, on the man. People walking down the street were beginning to stare at the man, realizing who he is. Spawn walks down the street, his face emotionless, which gives it power. The heat radiating off of his body seems to be hotter than the LA sun. He turns down a corner, and bumps into a man. The man, who was talking on his cell phone at the time, begins to curse at Spawn in Spanish. He fumbles around the ground, picking up his cell phone. The man is dressed in a nice Armani suit, that looks like he paid a fortune for it. Spawn stands with his shoulders crossed, not making a sound, not caring about the man. The man begins to eye up Spawn’ from the feet up, the camera catches his face as the man meets Spawn eye-to-eye. “’Ey, essay! What are you doin’ here, man! I was talkin’ on the phone, an’ you bump into me, man.” The man has a thick Spanish accent, Spawn stands with his arms crossed. “Oh, what, essay? You’re too… good for me, man? You can’t talk to the little spic, can ya’, man?” Spawn shrugs off the man, walking past him, wishing to cause the man no harm, as the incident was just as much his fault as it was the other man’s. He walks down the street, about to begin talking to the camera again, when the man comes up behind him, spinning him around. Spawn grabs the man’s hand before he can let it off of his shoulder, and arm wrenches it, turning the man around. He leans down and whispers to the man’s ear. Spawn: “Listen here, you little mother fucking drunk bastard. Go back to Mexico, eat your worms, and chill the fuck out, before I really give you something to be pissed about, asshole.” Spawn shoves the man forward, then watches him walk away with his cell phone, grabbing his wrist. Spawn shakes his head, then looks over to the camera again. Spawn: “Well, before I was rudely interrupted, I was going to speak, but I’ve lost my trail of thoughts. Ah, never mind. Every time another man bumps into me from behind, it reminds so much of you. When I brush up against a woman, I think about you, Death. Why was I thinking of you? Because I knew your mother-fucking ass will try to. Come up from behind and take advantage of me. At least when I attack you, you see me coming, but you, Death, you have to show up when I’m not looking, when I’m not expecting it, when I’m talking to the fans about my feelings of our match, and of you. You, Death, are a selfish bastard, whom I plan on setting back into place. What exactly is your place, Death? You are the scum of the NGW. I shouldn’t be wrestling you, Death I should be on to bigger… better… and taller things. This Tuesday match between you and I there will be blood in the ring. Not so much of mine....but a helluva lot of yours. And why...why!?! Because you think that you're is better then me. Kidd thinks that he can go with the best to step into this ring. And that’s very funny, Death. I am the one true devil in this fed...and I shall rule, but I guess I can wait for that, Death. Because regardless of that, I still get to beat the Hell out of your punk ass.. This Tuesday Night Storm you will attempt to step into my ring and you will attempt to try to beat me at a sport that I trained all my life to be good in. You will soon learn what everyone else has learned....The Last Kill is a dangerous move that can hurt anyone. And anyone can go up for it. It is a vicious move that can break the toughest of men and the weakest of souls. You will bare witness to "The Monster", Death.....that you will. Spawn walks away from the camera again, the camera once again following him as he walks down the street. The sides of the buildings have graffiti written all over them, from the different gangs in the area. Spawn turns into a store, a convenience store, with the windows barred over. He and the camera step into the cooled air, and immediately sigh relief. Spawn walks to the back cooler, grabbing the last Code Red, and then quickly walks back to the counter. The man looks down at Spawn, through the bulletproof glass. “Hey, man. Aren’t you Spawn? ‘The Creepy guy?” Spawn looks to the man, stunned. The Creepy? He’s never even heard of the The Creepy, let alone is the man that is called Creepy. Spawn: “You got a computer, ‘home slice’?” He says, trying to mock gang terms. The man nods, surprising Spawn, and pulls out his laptop computer. NBC it is, right away, and Spawn is shown the picture of the man. Spawn: “That’s who the bastard is. Death. Not me, Spawn. You know the guy, right?” “Nope, can’t say that I do. I don’t watch much of that wrestling stuff. If I do, it’s not NGW, I’ll tell ya’. I barely know who you are, Spawn.” Spawn looks to him, quickly, shooting him the look from under his glasses, the look so powerful as to cripple a man. “I mean, Spawn. I saw you, man. You tossed that Wiener guy out a window.” Spawn: “It’s Mike, but go on.” “Oh, my bad. And then you destroyed that Ya… your… your-mother person?” Spawn: “Vlad, Vlad man.” “Oh, yeah, him. You’re pretty good, almost unstoppable.” Spawn: “Almost… almost unstoppable? Please tell me you didn’t say that. I’ve been wrestling for 10 years, and I’m ALMOST unstoppable? C’mon, Tommy Chong, I’m the most powerful man in the NGW. I’ve been in the Tri-Fed alliance for my entire career. I know the ropes. I’ve been surrounded by guys like Omega Fro, HHK, and my good friend Pyro ‘the legend, who wishes to throw away the past, rather than accept it. I know how to win the NGW, and Death… doesn’t.” “Yeah, essay, but that’s just Sydal.” Spawn: “What’s with everyone saying essay?” “That Gonzales guy got us caught on it here, es…” Spawn interrupts him, quickly. “Say what?” “Eh, nothing. Hey, man, you going to pay for that?” Spawn: “Yeah, how much?” The man rings up the bottle, Spawn immediately grabs the bottle, reaching into his pocket for some loose change. “It’s a buck twenty four.” Spawn throws the money at the man, and walks out. The camera follows him as he walks back towards his hummer. He walks with perfect posture, his eyes always facing forward as the camera trails him. Spawn never makes a peep, keeping silent with himself. He walks toward his vehicle directly, as it comes into sight. He leans on the car as he opens the bottle. He takes a deep drink, as the sun beats down on his face, little beads of sweat forming on his face. As he is about to speak, the kid who he had a scuffle with comes back with a few of his friends. He walks up to Spawn looks at him straight in the eyes and begins to speak to him. “Hey essay you almost broke my arm and my back, back there when you tossed me around. This is my turf bitch and what you did to me essay is a fucking no, no. So I don’t give a fuck who you are, or how much money you got. You better get on your hands and knees and apologizes before we shank your shit up hoe.” Spawn looks at the four kids and throws a smile. Spawn: You little toddlers, you come here and disrespect me….who the hell you think you are talking to. I should just slap all four of you and send you all crying to your mamas. “Come on essay” what you waiting for do it bra. As Spawn takes his shades off and put his drink down to begin the battle. The man from the store who has been watching the whole thing scream out to the kids. !!!You punks better leave him alone and get the hell out of here, I just happen to call the cops and they will be here any minute!! The kids begin to run out of the area while talking to Spawn and the man. Fuck you old man!, and you, we see yo ass around here again we are going to smoke it bitch. Spawn: (grabbing his crotch) !!!smoke this motherfuckers>!! Spawn picks up his drink, puts his shades back on….looks at the camera gives it a quick smile. And begins to speak again. Spawn: “I know that took a while, when all I wanted to get was a fucking soda. We got idiots on cell phones bumping into me, thinking I’m a rich ass bastard that wont do jack shit to anyone if they try to pick a fight coming over here with his fucking gang thinking I am going to run away. Then we got a store clerk who can’t stop saying ‘Essay’ and shit. What the fuck is a fucking essay? A thesis? A dissertation? What the fuck does an essay have to do with human beings? Ah, who gives a flying rat’s ass? The point is, this world is full of idiots, just like Vlad, just like… Death. Death, I’ll talk to you man-to-man sometime, but right now I must resort to speaking at you. I know you’re a busy man with all your parties, and all the women that you can entertain with your stomach and what have you, and you’re ‘sexiness’ but you can’t entertain me, or my fans. One day you’ll see things from my perspective. If you were a veteran, and you get thrown into a match with Crimson and Bomber, then get into a match with you, a rookie, and then Death. You’d get pretty pissed off. You’re not fighting the big guns; you’re taking on the midgets, and the luchadors when you outweigh them by a hundred fucking pounds, and are taller than them by a fucking foot. I’ve been put into matches with retards, idiots, and guys that fucking think they’re Lucifer. What in the Holly fucking hell did I do to be put with these people? I broke my fucking leg, big deal. I’ve broken my back before. I’ve spent a good time out of action, big deal. I was electrocuted and made a full recovery. I’ve proven my place. Death I’m not an opener, I’m fucking main event talent. When I beat you this Tuesday, I will not justt just pin you, I will beat your fucking ass. I will out move you, I will out graced you, I will fucking out smart your ass. You will Remember for ever the last thirty seconds of our battle?. The pain and blood that you will lose will be a gift of my pride, Death. The last thing that will cross your mind is why!?! Why did I take this fight why was I so stupid. Everything that will happen will be all because you thought that it wasn't important...YOU THAT IT DIDN'T MATTER!!!! Well, guess what...it did. It was important enough to upset my fragile mental. Now...I have to listen to the voices and show you the fire, Death. The fire from a flaming table...a flaming table that will serve as you tombstone in the middle of the ring. Fire....the heat....hahahahahaha....the flames will love feasting on your flesh as you scream in agony!!!! And as you feel you flesh baking and you body screaming for comfort...I shall be there....and I shall whisper these words to you..........The days of people forgetting the horrors and power of Spawn, WILL END. The days of lackluster performers believing they stand a chance against the man that invented cruelty and sadism, WILL END. And the days of The Hell Master, ARE NOW!!!!!!! Now we get to see the battle of the strengths, the rage, and the brawling ability, where I believe I have the greatest advantage. This match isn’t going to be quick, because I wont let it be quick. I’ll beat you when you’re down, and leave you for dead when I pin you, just like I did on the 30th. Thanks for playing though, Death. Welcome to the ER. Welcome to the worst night of your life. You’re ready for me, you’re ready for the Tuesday battle… but are you ready, Death? Are you ready? Ready for… Hell?” “No you’re fucking not, you little fucking ankle biter. You’re not ready for me., I’m ready to take your ass to fucking kindergarten, all over again, and show you the fucking kids that took your fucking lunch money. I’m ready to send you back to third grade, when you fell off the monkey bars and lost your two front teeth, I’m ready to send you thinking about your high school sweet heart, and I’m fucking ready to make your fucking life MISERABLE, Death.. I’m ready to show you that Spawn isn’t going to die, just yet. He’s going to put up a fight, no, he’s going to be the fight. This dog has rabies, Death. Are you ready for that Death? Look what I fucking did to Vlad scared him so bad he never came to the match. Before me, he was a fucking prissy bitch, now he’s taking out mirrors, trying to be like me, Death. I’ve done it to him, I’m going to do it to you. You’re going to catch it, Death. You’re going to catch… THE MOTHER FUCKING HELL MASTER.” With that Spawn storms away from the camera, slightly stomping towards the drivers side of the car, “California Love” begins to play again in the background as the H2 starts, and Spawn drives away quickly, cutting off a car. The scene fades, leaving you with the rest of the song before the scene ends.
