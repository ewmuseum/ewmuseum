Suspicious allegations...Part 1/2 (Distortion RP# 2) Damien Ryan Furiously Addictive Wrestling Roleplay #71 Date: 11.8.02 Location: Warehouse, someplace in Virginia (screen opens up in some empty warehouse. It's really dark, the only light is coming throuh the open garage door and the holes from some of the broken tinted windows of the warehouse. The warehouse is rather large, and most of it is empty. The ground is dusty with dirt on it, yet in the corner we can see a set of steel folding chairs, and some folding tables. We then hear the sound of a truck and then the beeping from it being shifted into reverse and backing up. The light in the warehouse begins to fade a little because the truck blocks some of the sunlight coming in through the door. The driver's side door of the truck opens and then the passengers as two men get out and walk into the warehouse. The are both wearing blue jumpsuits with nametags. The two walk into the warehouse and stand there taking a glimpse.) Man1: Where the hell is he? He said three PM. Man2: I don't know. Man1: Well let's just load this shit in the truck and hopefully he'll show up by then. (One of them opens the sliding door of the back of the truck and they lower the ramp. They start hauling the tables and chairs into the back of the truck. We hear the sound of another vehicle coming towards the warehouse. The camera gets a few of it and sees it is a police cruiser, it pulls up right next to the truck and the officer gets out) Officer: What you boys up to? Man1: We got an order to bring these materials someplace. Officer: Well that's rather odd. I thought only the city had a right to this place. Man1: (seeming to be nervous) Yeah well... (The man stumbles over some words and begins shaking and the officer looks at at him stragely) Man1: Well... Officer: You guys didn't happen to catch the Lakers/Celtics game last night, did you? (the man calms down in releif that the officer changed the subject and stops sweating, the officer doesn't seem to suspect anything anymore) Man1: Yeah, the Celtics won in overtime. Officer: Eh, well the Lakers didn't have Shaq. (As they continue to put the tables and chairs into the truck, a motorcycle is heard and it pulls into the warehouse. We see the driver pull off his helmet and its FAW's Hardcore champion, Damien Ryan. He walks in, kind of hesitate when he sees the police officer) Officer: Can we help you? Man2: He's with us, he's got the order. Ryan: Uhh, yeah... Uh, lets go guys, we are on a hectic time schedule. Officer: If you don't mind, whats all this stuff for anyways? Ryan: Dinner party. Officer: (Getting suspicious) Oh...I see. (the two loading men finally finish loading the truck, and close the back sliding door shut and the noise echoes the warehouse) Officer: Yeah, well I noticed the truck so I came over. We are pulling over all moving vans and trucks. Seems like a lot of materials like these tables and chairs, they took street signs, bathroom materials, all kind of stuff has been shipped and truck companies have no idea about the problems, they can't figure it out. They have been having delays and shipments not being delivered, not meeting their destinations, so they called the authorities. Man2: That's messed up. Officer: Yeah so, what's this dinner party? Ryan: It's a varmitzva at Leonardo's Italian resteraunt on the corner of 23rd and Washington. Officer: Nice place, great garlic bread. Man2: Hell yeah. Ryan: Alright, we better head out, we can't be late, our customer will have our neck if we're late. Officer: Have a good one. (The two shippers get into their truck and Ryan hops back on his pick and they both drive off. The officer ways behind, opens the door to his cruiser and picks up his radio) Officer: 2372 to base. Base: Go ahead 2372. Officer: Yeah, can you give me a direct connection to Leonardo's Italian Resteraunt. Base: One second. (the line changes to the resteraunt) Phone: Leonardos Officer: Yeah, do you have a varmitva planned this weekend? Phone: Varmizva? No, we don't do any sort of functions or parties of any kind like that, we're stricly a nightly resteraunt. Would you like to make a reservation for tonight? Shrimp Scampi is one of our specials tonight. Officer: No, thats alright, thanks. (he puts down his radio, and just sits there) Officer: Mmmmm, hell..... (he picks his radio back up) Officer: 2372 back to base, I got a visual of the truck. (camera fades out) PART 2 LATER
