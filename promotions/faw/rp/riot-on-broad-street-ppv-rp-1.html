Riot On Broad Street (PPV RP 1)

Riot On Broad Street (PPV RP 1) 9th Wonder Of Foam Furiously Addictive Wrestling Roleplay #163 Date: 21st January 2003 Location: The Foam Factory A 1950’s style black and white Film studio title screen appears. A Tabby cat is perched on a rotating silver platter, in the background there is a mountain not unlike the Paramount one, in the cats mouth it a tongue, it has The Omega Fro written on it. Foam Factory Productions Presents: Robert Jaxom off. …. Underground: The Foam Factory – Tuesday …. (9th Wonder Of Foam is in the EVIL Scheme room alone, the FAW Championship Belt is resting in the table. Lars his butler walks in) Foamy: Lars, as part of our employee review I have called you in here to discuss how you think you are performing in your job. Lars: Oh I see, take me to a crowded area, like a restaurant where I won’t make a scene, then fire me. Is that why you’re brought me here? Foamy: Lars we are in the Foam Factory, alone! Lars: Oh yeah. Alone you say? What’s this are you trying to seduce me? Foamy: Afraid not Lars. I have called you here to give you a pay cut. Lars: Give me a pay cut? Foamy: Ok I’m not technically giving you anything but your general Evil levels have been disappointing. What have you done that has been remotely evil this past week? Lars: I pissed on the toilet seat. And I left it up after I used it. Foamy: Is that it? On Sunday night I destroyed two men using a silver platter while their backs were turned, what is more EVIL than that? Lars: Perhaps If I was a women who was screwing you over? Foamy: The only woman in my life is the FAW Championship, and she doesn’t screw people, those who wish to steal her from me will screw whoever it takes to get their hands on that piece of gold. Now be on your way. Lars: Melissa Farrow is on her way. Foamy: Yay! More questions! (Melissa Farrow enters; Foamy hides the FAW Championship under his desk) Foamy: Hello Melissa. (Foamy feels something kicking his leg) Foamy: (whispering) Stop it! Melissa: Ready for the PPV? Foamy: I’m… ready for anything! (Foamy feels it kick again; he ducks under the table, we can just about hear him whispering) Foamy: What? It’s only Melissa Farrow. You don’t trust me? Oh come on it was only a little bit of harmless banter, I’ve seen the way you were looking at Robert Jaxom Sunday night, if you could gush you would’ve. (Foamy pops his head up above the table) Foamy: Sorry about that, trouble with the mrs. (Foamy gets kicked again) Melissa: What the hell is under the table? Foamy: The love of my life. Melissa: And you keep her hidden under the table? Foamy: Well yeah, she gets jealous sometimes. (Foamy pulls out the FAW Championship belt and talks to it) Foamy: See, she isn’t naked, she just wants to talk. Melissa: Ok, give me five minutes for the crew to get everything set up. …. Five minutes later …. (Melissa Farrow sits with the 9th Wonder Of Foam, he has over his should the FAW Championship belt.) Melissa: On Panic Sunday night you faced and defeated Hardcore Hoefel and The Omega Fro, with the assistance of a silver platter. Foamy: Yes, yes I did. You made that sound as if it was a bad thing. Melissa: Why didn’t you try and win fairly? Foamy: You called that match fair? Both men were vying for one thing, my championship, that wasn’t fair. Fair would be a non-title match because we would all be looking to kick each other’s ass but as it stood they were both after me. And history will forever record that I defeated both The Fro and Hoefel in that match and retained the FAW Championship. Add to that that fact that in a moments notice I decided to end the match there and then, they did not figure in that decision. I was tired, so I ended the match and got the pin. Besides why humiliate The Omega Fro on live TV when I can make those ungrateful fans pay for the experience? Melissa: The Omega Fro did a good job of humiliating you by playing JLC’s entrance music; the look on your face was not one of joy. Foamy: You can’t draw a line between fear and hatred. What you saw was a physical manifestation of my dislike for Billy Corgan. Melissa: And not fear that JLC was coming down to the ramp? Foamy: Of course not. Have you ever seen Billy Corgan? He is completely bald! And he chooses to do this by choice! I was merely peeved that JLC would choose music by such an awful band. True that bass player is one foxy chick… (Foamy’s line of sight is drawn to the title belt) Foamy: What? She’s a fox! Melissa: So if faced in the future with the prospect of defending the title against JLC you would not be afraid? Foamy: Me? Afraid? The fact is JLC has had plenty of time to come forward but he chooses not to, not because he is biding his time but because he knows he couldn’t beat me. And why did the Omega Fro choose to play JLC’s music? Why did The Fro not play his own music? Would it be that I would’ve laughed so hard my sphincter would’ve dropped onto the mat there an then? He is trying to scare me by using cheap musical tricks, not buy showing me whether he is up to the job. Had he gone out there Sunday and not been pinned like the pussy he is then perhaps I would have reason to fear him but no, he came out of that broadcast looking more like a fool than he did going into it. Melissa: This Sunday’s title match will be fought under Lion’s Den rules; will this have an effect on the outcome of the match? Foamy: First of all the chick that runs things around here has no idea about wrestling does she? We are not Ultimate Fighting Warriors, we are professional wrestlers, and we don’t fight in Lion’s Den’s we fight in a ring. But nonetheless I can fight any match, and I can win any match. She has missed judged me if she thinks she can get that title off me by fiddling with the matches. Melissa: So you believe that you can win that match? Foamy: I know I will win that match and go onto Extreme Beating 3 and destroy the poor deluded fool who wins the rumble. I will train harder that Fro, I will focus harder than Fro, I will hit harder than Fro and I will celebrate harder than Fro. I beat him and one other man in two minutes on Sunday night, what makes him think the following Sunday will be any different. Melissa: But you did use a foreign object? Foamy: And don’t think I won’t do it again! I will hold onto this title for dear life, by any means necessary and if that means fighting to defend her another day then I will. I will hold her tighter than any other and I will hold her longer than any other, mark my words those entering the Rumble would have a better chance of become Champion if they moved to another company. Melissa: And if you lose? Foamy: If I lose? I will reclaim her, simple as 1-2-3. It won’t be like before where I didn’t know what she tasted like. I have now acquired a taste for her, and every week at the end of every show I look forward all week to the next Sunday night where I can show her off again. Melissa: Any tips for the Rumble match? Foamy: I’ve told you before I don’t care. I’d put my money on some young up and coming buck to win it, but then as we have seen people can just stroll in off the street and demand a title shot as Fro did, so anyone could easily enter and win it. I just wished I had entered it. Melissa: Why? Foamy: If I won that rumble match it would mean that not only I would be the best damn FAW champion in history it would also mean I would never lose her, it would also guarantee me a title shot against myself, headlining the biggest night in FAW history. Melissa: Speaking of Extreme Beating 3 are you looking forward to attending that event with or without the title? Foamy: It has been my goal to headline EB3 ever since I first won the FAW Tag titles at EB2. And one year later I will not only headline EB3 I will also successfully defend my title at EB3 and I will also destroy the hopes and dreams of the winner of the rumble all in one night. Melissa: You spoke earlier of the head of FAW attempting to get the title off you? Do you think there is a conspiracy against you? Foamy: Of course there is! This hooker walks in off the street and thinks she can run FAW? Does she have business training? But I think she sickened by the sit of someone like me holding the FAW Championship. Just as The Fro is, just as Crimson Lord is, just as Hoefel is, Jon The Bomb, Rzarector, Randy Rocker and that is what drives me to keep hold of the title, as long as there are idiots out there who actually think they can beat me I will continue fighting until I get this title retired in my name because I was just that damn awesome! Have a Foam Filled Day!
