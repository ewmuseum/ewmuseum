Twisted Minds

30th November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Twisted Minds Jeff Harris Furiously Addictive Wrestling Roleplay #81 Date: 30th November 2002 Location: A Park bench, Trenton, New Jersey -The late-afternoon sun hid behind grey banks of snow clouds and a cold wind whipped loose leaves across the dull field in front of the block of apartments in Trenton, New Jersey. There was the feel of snow in the air but the thermometer hovered just at the freezing mark and the clouds could turn either into icy rain or snow. Jeffrey Harrison stepped out of the entrance of the apartments and shivered as a blast of wind hit him. He pulled up the zipper on his loose black leather coat and paused to gauge the storm clouds building up to the west.- -The broad planes of his cold face turned into the driving wind for a moment and then he looked back down at the weather report in his newspaper. Behind him, the door of the apartment blocks shouldered open by other tenants, Jeffrey Harrison ignored them as they nodded at him. Jeffrey Harrison turned back in the cold wind for a second before taking a deep breath he then walked away from the building heading around it and finding a park bench and then sitting down. Jeffrey Harrison then folded up the newspaper and set it beside him as he looked up at the dull sky.- : Simpletons, simple fools. Simpler than any petty tools. Walk together, walk in unison. Conform to what is considered right; conform to what you think well. Realize not what you do, do only as you are told. Take your orders and blink not twice. Think for no one, be thought for. So much like all the rest, like the rest in the world. So much like the fools in FAW. So much like Sabatash. Puppet on a string, dancing his jig. His time is up, no longer does he entertain. The children grow tired of his antics; grow bored of his 'talents'. Puppet on a string no longer, puppet cut loose. Flail on the ground, convulse little puppet. Dance a new jig, dance the dance of pain. Think what you think, believe what you believe. Conform Sabatash; conform to the rules, to the ways written by those drunk off power. You yourself one of those people now, power that you don't even have. Handed to you, everything. Ignore the facts if you wish, the truth can't be changed save in your mind. A mind, which doesn't matter, not in the sea of conformity. Not amongst the flock of sheep. To the slaughter you will go, to the light you will come. Understanding will dawn upon you once more. The scum will welcome you again, arms wide open. They grow tired of you puppet. Your failing on the ground will stop. You will go unnoticed once more, no one will care you existed, no one cares you exist. : -Jeffrey Harrison, grinning now, slowly shakes his head in disgust and then closes his eyes. Staring into himself it seems, as he remains motionless. Arms resting on his knees. He now sits back, the bench digging into his back. A grimace of unease appears. He opens his eyes once more and all is forgotten. Staring directly at the hands of a man. Hands that enter a pocket, removing a wallet. A wallet not belonging to the hands. Sly, moves. The thief disappears into the sea. Standing out only for a moment. Jeffrey Harrison grins once more, the unease forgotten. His mind restarts.- : What the hell is wrong with me? Being injured for life could have something to do with it… Shoulder damaged beyond repair, damaged by The British Bomber, it hurts, the pain is exceptional to anything he or anyone else have felt, on the shelf for four months, damaged it more in the Warehouse, it is because of FAW why I have lost all of my money, my respect, my pride, my strength! Now is the time to get Sabatashenge, Sabatashenge on FAW, starting with Sabatash. Logic states that you do what makes most sense. Logic is false. Logic is a mere story told to little children wishing to believe in something more. Logic has been dispossessed from these vermin, these scums of the Earth. Nothing is what they are worth. Hate me; they do, for good reason perhaps. The saviour I am not, but enlightened yes. To be understood by them, never. To be respected by them, never again. What does it matter, a flock of sheep they are. Puppets on strings, dancing to the same tune at a different pace. To some I am evil, to some I am cruel. To some I am even a fool. The truth they can't see, as usual. Believing what they believe as they see fit. Willingly being taken advantage of, their motto. So be it, I can't resist. I wont resist. On my lap sits the 'best of the best'. Safety and allusion for him. Pick his pocket I just might, in an entirely different way of course. Off centre. Let the truth betray him in the end. For never was it on his side, never was it in his grasp. Never was it in his vocabulary, in his mind, in his being. Mislead entirely, life long dreams. Crushed under my foot, they will be. Crumbling comes the empire, decaying are the walls. Roots through the stones, a task as easy as breaking his bones. Child's play it seems, soon it will be my turn to build a castle. To rule supreme. Just sit there another moment, let me pick your pocket. Let me herd the sheep, control the puppets. : -Jeff let’s his head fall downward once more, hanging there limp. His arms turned inwards, hands limp as well. His body suddenly begins to shake now. His head whips upwards, eyes half closed as tears of laughter begin to roll down his cheek. The sound echoing throughout the dull buildings along the street. People pause briefly, casting Jeff queer looks, as if he has lost his mind. No doubt, he already has. Jeff's laughter roars on for moments. More and more people pause briefly, continually staring at him before making a decision upon his sanity. Finally, Jeff just abruptly stops. His face transforms into one of anger. Staring silently ahead at the crowd. He casts a sneer at some of his watchers.- : That's right fools, look at me. Take in this 'sorry' site. Make up your minds that I am nothing more than another insane fool on the streets. Nothing more than a drugged up moron, nothing more than a waste of space. Form your opinions, work on your insults. Sigh in disgust, roll your eyes. Do whatever it is that you think is appropriate. Do whatever it is that you think is 'cool'. Do it under the impression that your worthless lives have some meaning. Do it thinking that whatever you do will have an effect on someone else, that what you do is important. That you are important. Do it under your false impressions, your misguided ways. Your stupid little laws of popularity, of humanity. Or what you call humanity at least. A disgrace, you all are to this species. Who is the waste in this scenario? Who is the fool, the moron, the used up, washed-up fool? Ask yourself little boys and girls. The answer, the truthful answer, will surprise you. Will fracture your frail little worlds. Will make the ceilings crumble, the walls shatter. Similar to what Sabatash will experience. No, no, exactly like what Sabatash will experience, since he is exactly like the rest of you. He is one of you. He can think he is better, he can think he is the best, he can think he is important, he can think anything he wants really, but he has no proof. He has no actual fact to base it upon other than his confused little opinion. He has nothing whatsoever other than the fairytales he fabricates in his own little world. How sad, this fairytale wont have a happy ending. This fairytale wont entail you living happily ever after, Sabatash. This fairytale wont have you living at all. Should I get my way, should justice get its way, you will be sent back in your place. Sent back to your grave, one dug many months ago, many years ago for that matter. : -Jeff now brings his attention to the eyes of one of his watchers. The two men stare face to face for a moment, before the 'watcher' finally breaks the stare and walks off at a rapid pace. Looking only straight ahead, attempting to go unnoticed. Attempting to forget what he just saw in the eyes of Jeff. The scene refocuses on these said eyes, on their pupils and the colour surrounding them. In them can be seen a reflection of the world. In them can be seen an intelligence, a way of life. A Sabatash.- : Why doesn’t this man speak? He has yet to comment on me, on the match, on how scared he should be. I’ll make sure he feels the pain I once did, the pain I feel now and the pain I will always feel. The pounding in my head, grows louder and louder. The hunger stronger and stronger. The time I have left grows shorter and shorter, the home stretch is in site. Sabatash is the line I must cross. A line that I will cross no matter the circumstance. Sabatash is nothing more than a minor obstacle in my way. Blocking my view for only a moment. A wall I will scale, a catwalk I will run across, a bridge I am fully willing to take. Standing in my way, he wont be for much longer. Knocked down like the rest, set ablaze as he comes crashing down. Roasting in the flames, melting away in the minds. Forgotten soon after, remembered only as a fool. Dreams will be ripped from him, like my dreams were ripped from me. The place I have seen have at least taught me. Experiencing the worst, maybe not. But pain, that will be his designated misery for certain. Clutch that title little boy, grasp it with all your might. Fight off the darkness for as long as you can, but sooner or later the lights must go out. So comes your fate, so comes your time. An enemy you can't defeat, an enemy called time. The bearer of bad news I will be. To your knees you will drop, begging every which way. Ha, a joke you have been. A joke you still are. Soon, this joke will finally receive its much deserved punch-line, will receive its climax and ultimately its downfall. Your walls crumble, your empire is fading Sabatash. My hunger grows, and my power rising. Invulnerable always, yet again. Invincible, you'll find out soon enough. Break me you can't, you never will. Your words are meaningless, and you voice shrill. I laugh at you, despise you. I know your time is up. Eccentricity taking over in your final hours, your final days. Shore up your defenses, as I come unnoticed through the back door. The sky is falling my friend, the sky is falling and it is your end. : -The scene zooms in closer, on the eyes of Jeffrey James Harrison. The blackness engulfs the screen, engulfs the picture. His eyelids blink slowly, and the cold unforgiving scene fades away. The shadows crawl across, slower than before. The sounds of the passer-by’s, are subsiding down to a minimal roar. The eyelids blink again, even slower now. The head tilts, and the darkness becomes complete. So the scene fades, Jeffrey left sitting in his seat-
