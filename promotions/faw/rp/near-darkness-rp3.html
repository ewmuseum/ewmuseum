Near Darkness(rp3)

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Near Darkness(rp3) Crimson Lord Furiously Addictive Wrestling Roleplay #27 Date: 19/10/02 Location: FAW Dressing Room (We open up our scene with...nothing really out of the ordinary. We've got a locker room, almost empty, save one bag. The gear, a pair of black baggy juncos pants, with crimson stripes up either leg, and two title belts hanging out of the bag, gives off the obvious owner of one, none other then the man walking onto the picture, 'The True One', Crimson. He's dressed in a pair of Camo cut-off shorts, reaching his knee in length. A towel is over his shoulders, and his hair is dripping wet, as he looks focused as can be.) Crimson: Here we are again, Crimson versus Raza...however, things look different this time around. This time, we've got Renegade, going with you, and none other then the world's luckiest bastard, The Duke, my Newest friend, as my partner. What Tom The Bomb was thinking is beyond me, but hey, I can deal with it. After all, I'm 'The Living Savoir'. I'm going to be whipping ass from pillar to post, and that's anyone who gets in my way. If it's Raza, he knows what it's like to get in the ring with me. Renegade? I'll knock some sense into him, and make him realize that if he steps wrong, I'll be right there to force him, rather unpleasantly, back on track. Crimson: So who should we kick this shit off with? Raza? Nah, let's start with the one everyone's expecting us to start with...Renegade. Renegade, they say two time's the charm, but really, it won't work for you. You tried the first time to take me down. What was the result of that one? I fucked you up, and you know it. Now, a few days later, you're all bitchy and everything, What makes you think that this match is going to be any different? You have Raza, watch me quiver in fear. Though you and Raza, you do seem to love a good story...so I'm going to spin one up for you right now. (Crimson sits down, and smiles. He reaches over to his bag, pulling a water bottle out of it and taking a sip. As a unknown Woman walks out from behind him, wrapped in a towel, still wet from taking a shower. She kisses his neck, and Crimson just closes his eyes.) Woman: I'll be waiting for you dearest... (Crimson looks at the camera and smiles, as the woman trots off into the back. He shakes his head and goes back to his focused look.) Crimson: You wanted to make a big story to please all of the fans and to push your career, well Renegade, Raza, I've given you exactly what you like. A nice little story in which there is a hero...and a villain. Crimson: You... Renegade, Raza, are the slimeball heros. The Cops, EMTs, shit like that if you will. While men like Duke, “Him”, and even myself, are reality. And, we all know what happens in everyday life, people are out for their own personal gain, and usually good doesnt prevail, in this case, FAW. But unlike in the movies, you two will not come back for the sequel. It will be the final curtain call for you. I look at The Duke and see a guy who fought against the odds, A man who against all the logics of the world, against all everyone THOUGHT they knew, Forget the fact that he did it after beating injuries, and forget the fact that physically, mentally, and verbally, you guys may be better but look at the fact that he got the job done. He deserves to be the man who represents the FAW as the Devil, as much as it pains me to say. And Renegade I'll make sure that The Duke and I will walk out the winners, Whether it be Disqualification, interference, a fast count, it's no matter kids. I just want my win. I want to feel your blood on my hands and feel all the years of hatred flow through my fists as I land blows to your stupid smirking faces. I hate people sometimes, yes, but I hate you two more than words can explain. I know everyone else does as well. Crimson: One thing is for sure, I have one and ONLY one thing on my agenda for the PPV and that is simple...winning. My win comes first and your little bitch-asses come second. But I will promise you that in my glory, you will also shine just as brightly. As two stars held down in the shadow of those more powerful and pretty. We are the ones who make so-called heros like Xander look like fools and expose them for what they really are. Because you see Renegade, Raza, I've waited for this opportunity while I watched things from the outside. And when contacted by The Duke, who I might add has seen what a dick you two can be, he brought me in to the FAW picture. And what a pretty picture it will be, now that I'm here to fucking stay. (Crimson smiles, dropping the water bottle, letting it hit the floor, watching the precious liquid flow freely from the plastic container, soaking the floor.) Crimson: One of the most important tasks you'll both face in your life is the struggle to avoid being stupid. Being stupid is easier than it sounds, and is a danger we all face daily. Some people, like you two, have an inherent talent for stupidity. These people, my friends, are what we refer to as the lost causes. The majority of us, at the very least, can understand the most basic rules of life. These rules include: Don't kick porcupines with bare feet, don't pour salt in your eyes, don't microwave yourself, and don't listen to music from the Spice Girls. However, there are actually people in existence who cannot understand even the simplest of these rules. You know, I try to lighten the mood a little with a bit of sarcasm, and what happens? It flies right over your collective heads. Seven foot jokes, we call them. I try to play it so what I have to say doesn't come off as offensive as I'd like, and you two will act like the world is about to end. And god forbid I ever show more than one emotion, or decide that maybe I shouldn't act like a fool in certain situations. Forgive me if my personality ain't as one dimensional as Xander. I'll try harder to dull myself down next time. When I say the things I do don't matter to anyone but me, don't try to take that as anything other than what it sounds like. It makes you look stupid. And I wouldn't know these things, but judging on your combined losing skids, it would be you that look like the experts on stupid. It's a rare occasion when I get a shred of respect from anyone, but really, the only opinion that matters to me, is my own. I don't give a fuck what you and Renegade think . I don't give a fuck what anybody thinks, and I sure as hell don't give a fuck what the fans think. All I give a fuck about right now, is whipping your two collective asses from pillar to post, taking the win, and walking out tall, as the motherfucking Savoir. Crimson: I am not sitting over here thinking that cheaters never prosper. I am not over here with the delusion that if you are a good person it will all work out in the end. I have always strove to be the best man I could be, not just in terms of wins in losses but how I won and how I lost. I know that the easiest way to get what you want is to take it by any means necessary. I ever decided I'd rather just take something or rather just do something I know I could have it. If a day came where I decided to sell my soul like the rest of you, the rest of you wouldn't have a fucking prayer. You would have no way to stop me and no matter how much you take my moral code for granted, that is something that should give you pause. You are free, you say, and I am not. And maybe in the terms of you aren't too proud to take a win anyway you can get it, then yes. Against all kinds of people with the same freedom you say you have. Just because it is new for you does not mean it is new for me, you stupid little asshole. You think I haven't faced someone willing to do whatever it takes before? You KNOW that's not the case. what the fuck makes you two any different? You going to be big men? Hit me with a chair? Get your friends to run in? You going to do illegal moves? You going to cheat? You going to hit me with low blows and try to cripple me? (Crimson slams his fist into the tile wall, cracking it, and leaving a rather large indention. He pulls his hand back, and looks at the blood trickling from his knuckles.) Crimson: Go ahead boys. Go ahead and try what every cocksucker in the world has tried and failed to do. Just because you can draw neat little post modern literary parallels doesn't make your point valid. I have seen all this before, I have heard all this before, and you know what? It doesn't work. Like it didn't work last week, So, you soulless little twats, you'd better have something more than being more fucking free than me or you are going to taste your own blood. And you are going to lay on the mat, out of breath, will broken, and you're going to realize that I am who I am for a reason. And I beat who I beat for a reason. And I rise from what I fucking rise from for a reason. I let the world kick me around? Is that fucking so? I am willing to risk my job, my life, for my dream. Did it succeed?. Yeah, I've been beat up, No, I won't go kill your loved ones like Others would. No I will not even hurt them. Because not everything is measured in wins and losses cumbubble. I will not compromise who I am and I don't need to. Bad things happen and you know what, have recovered from all of them and I am still a champion to be proud of and I am still a man that look himself in the mirror and know that I do my best. (He pauses, and continues to stare at his knuckles, looking up at the camera a little.) Crimson: If that's allowing myself to get kicked around, I guess more people should. Crimson: I guess I was letting myself get kicked around when I debuted. I guess that was me not taking control of my destiny when I beat good 'ole Renegade in the middle of the ring, when I embarrassed him. I felt pity for him for a brief moment. That isn't being pushed around, that is having principles. And while principles don't win matches like being a cheater does, I won't be trading them in any time soon. Who I am is not dictated by what I want. There is more that I care about than winning titles and being famous. There is more that I care about than power. And those things reward me for the kind of person I try to be. I make people happy, they hate you, they hate Renegade and Raza, and while you don't care about anything but winning I do care. It feeds the soul. It's a rush. It's pressure, you know it is, you know how it is, and I think it's worth it. You fucking dolts. You don't even have any idea what you two have done yet. But you will. Someday you will rue the day that you stopped. Just another couple thugs. Being a good man doesn't mean that you will get all that you deserve. It means though, that years from now, you can walk with your head held high and know that you did your best. That is a hell of a thing. That is something to be proud of. To live and know that no matter what you didn't allow the bad things in the world to twist you into something else. Regardless, when you're that age, you won't be looking to be those righteous people. Instead, you'll be locked in your own private Sanctuaries, and you'll have been... (Crimson kisses his bloody knuckles.) Crimson:...bitten by a Glory Hound! Crimson: Really? Savoir your lives as they stand right now, because your redemption is coming Tonight. Whether I have to do it myself, or with Duke helping out, we will emerge victorious. Bye bye little bitch-cakes…Blessed Be… (Crimson smirks, and gets up, slinging the towel off of his shoulders. He looks back at the camera, as he goes to join his wife in the showers. Fade to black.)
