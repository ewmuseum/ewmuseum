How Low Can The Page Go? (DDURP004) 9th Wonder Of Foam Furiously Addictive Wrestling Roleplay #56 Date: 26/10/02 Location: Foamy Hills - New Jersey .... Foamy Hills - New Jersey - Saturday Afternoon .... (The 9th Wonder Of Foam walks into his study, he strips the dog collar off his neck and takes off his Priest's outfit to reveal his ring attire) (Lars walks in) Lars: Where are you headed? Foamy: I'm off to the training room with Jim now. Then later we've got to catch our flight to Disaster Down Under.... In Nashville. Lars: Melissa Farrow is on her way up. Foamy: Oh ok, I suppose. Let me ask you something Lars, what is your favourite TV Show? Lars: The Simpson’s. Though recently it has lost its way. Foamy: Well I prefer the later episodes myself but wrong answer. Your favourite TV show is the Foam Show which debuts tomorrow night! (Foamy winks) Lars: You may enjoy the later Simpsons episodes, but so fucking what? I just expect more from such obviously talented minds. The whole purpose of art (nb. I consider, for the purposes of this argument, that art is any mode of expression, including television) is to express a personal, intrinsic talent. If you have no talent (Tracey Emin, et al, I'm looking at you), or have simply exhausted it (the Simpsons, the writers of Frasier, The Manic Street Preachers), then don't try to express it anymore. Foamy: Lars leave this room, no-one smites the Simpsons, Frasier and the Manics all in one sentence! Lars: How about I insult them e separately? Foamy: Out! (Lars leaves, Foamy sits behind his desk. Melissa Farrow then enters the room) Foamy: Hello. Melissa: Mind If I get a few words. Foamy: Sure, where is the crew? Melissa: Oh this is for the website; I’m going to type it out later before I head down the pub. Foamy: Ok sure, fire! Melissa: Rzarector is it seems a Black Priest, why did you make fun of his devotion to Religion? Foamy: Because their inquisitions persecuted us for centuries and still do today - its time the tables were turned. Plus religious people are easy to wind up. Of course I could wait for Rzarector to split up into factions and kill each other but I'd prefer to do the job myself tomorrow night. Melissa: Rzarector has even compared you in some small way to the antichrist. Foamy: No Melissa I'm not the antichrist. What gets me about this guy is that he truly believes he is talking to God, in religious circles he is worshipped, but out here were intolerance is not tolerated we call that schizophrenia. Yet again Rzarector falls into the same trap, a trap that has caught Xander, Apocalypse and the Mass Murderers; he is underestimating my worth, while over estimating their own standing in the world. Melissa: How do you mean? Foamy: I act like a fool, I make jokes, they get in the ring expecting to be facing noted Jew Jerry Seinfeld, but then I walk to the ring and suddenly it hits them. 'Could this guy be f**king with me? Could it be that he acts this way to fool me?' But by then it is too late to change their mindset. Melissa: You talk of people overestimating their worth? Foamy: Indeed. Rzarector can harp on for months about being the black demon master but the fact remains he is only a professional wrestler who perhaps needs to spend more time in the gym than in church. Rzarector like so many others believes that he is some higher being when all he does for a living is wrestler semi-nude men in spandex. I know that, you're not going to kill people, burn people’s souls or any of that crap. You're going to walk down to the ring and wrestle me. Your power extends only to the fans watching at home and the thousands in attendance that is it. Melissa: Any words for Renegade? Foamy: Yes, listen up boy. I don't know who created you but you're obviously some freak of nature, 5'10? Did the U.S. Government try and create and army of midgets in order to make the fight with Communist China fair for both sides? But I digress. You're going to be quick, your size affords you that but your mind still thinks your ass is bigger than it actually is. Your mind thinks that you can pass the largest of turds when in fact you can only manage at this time small rabbit sized droppings. And please no crap jokes. But no you're going to be a quick little f**ker, but tomorrow that will not matter, on any other day it might have, but not tomorrow. Tomorrow is too important so give it your best try. Try and fight an opponent you can't see or feel, try and defeat the Foamy One. Try... Try ... Try. Melissa: Any last words? Foamy: Resurrection. Learnt he words boys because this is what your career will need after tomorrow. Have a Foam Filled Day!
