Realisation 2 'Golden Nine' Jeff Harris Furiously Addictive Wrestling Roleplay #263 Date: 8th February Location: Secret Volcano Layer, Hawaii :.:It’s been almost three hours since I brutally murdered Pete Harris, I’m still feeling the adrenaline rush, it’s been a while since I actually attacked someone in that way, at this moment in time though I know everything that happened and everything that I have been doing in WFW and FAW as this 009 character, why I portrayed him I do not know, but one thing is for sure though he is no more, Golden Nine is no more. The only thing that survives is my true feelings, my true thoughts, and my true intentions. This woman Jean Deas, mother of Bryan Deas who is a man I have had a past with must die, Bryan Deas must die… Everyone must die around this nut hole. I’m going to get my vengeance on what they did to me; these past few months have probably been the worst of my life, becoming best friends with Bryan Deas and Pete Harris… Being Jean Deas’ sex slave it’s fucking sickening! But no more, this is my time, my time to show what I am capable of. I shan’t rush what I am going to do though because after all that would spoil the surprise… Random thoughts have filled my head, last night I dreamed about my wedding day with Kathryn Velmont Thomas, what a whore she was, not only was The British Bomber trying to slip her one while she was pregnant, she was carrying Tom The Bomb’s child and was during being married to me, I’m glad I divorced her, gah I’m babbling to myself again, stupid thoughts:.: .::The scene opens with Jeff Harris sat on a bed with his head in his hands thinking random thoughts, the walls are exceptionally gold, walls that Jeff Harris should be sick of seeing, these walls are also exceptionally bright also with the gold being reflected off Jeff’s skin. Jeff still wearing a black tuxedo with black shirt and tie stands up and walks out of the bedroom, he walks down a hall and bumps into a Jean-Bot::. “Golden Nine” Jeff Harris: Oi, watch it. Jean-Bot: Jean wants to see y’all in the command centre. “Golden Nine” Jeff Harris: What the fuck for, I’m busy. Jean-Bot: She said it’s urgent. “Golden Nine” Jeff Harris: Oh for god sake. .::Jeff Harris and the Jean-Bot following behind makes their way to the command centre. Once he walks in he sees a lot of Jean-Bots and Mini-Pete’s crying, they are all crowded in a circle as Jeff walks closer he can see a bloody Pete Harris on the floor, Jeff looks shocked as he sees on one of the television screens the death of Pete Harris being played over and over again::. On The Screen Pete Harris: W-what up Jeff? You seem angry. Jeff Harris: Angry? That doesn’t even describe what I’m feeling at this moment in time, do you know why I wanted to meet you here in this abandoned room in this particular part of the volcano? Pete Harris: Uh.. No I don’t. Jeff Harris: Because no one can hear you scream for help when I drain the life of your fat disgusting body. Pete Harris: …J-Jeff? Jeff Harris: 6 Months! That’s how long you’ve taken out of my life, and although I can’t get those 6 months of my life back, I will compensate it by taking yours. Pete Harris: HAHA! I LOVE… Wait… what?! Jeff Harris: I know what you did to me back in October, you and Jean Deas brainwashed me and made me look like a joke to everyone in the world. Pete Harris: 009!? Jeff Harris: Guess again bitch. .::Before Pete Harris can respond Jeff Harris leaps at Pete and begins laying punches into his face, Pete Harris cries out in pain but no one responds, the cameraman stands back in fear as Jeff Harris opens a bloody hole in Pete’s head, blood begins to drip everywhere, Jeff Harris then picks him up and throws him against the wall, knee’s the back of his leg making Pete drop to his knee’s then Jeff proceeds to break his neck as the crack of the neck can be heard echoing down the corridor. Jeff gets up to his feet now a bloody mess; he stares at his hands and begins to smile::. .::Jeff punches the glass on the television stopping the continues loop, Jean Deas enters the room with an angered filled face::. Jean Deas: Y’all killed him! Yaw a bastard! “Golden Nine” Jeff Harris: Oh shut up, in case you haven’t noticed no one is here to protect you and your little command centre anymore. I know you are probably thinking of the Mini-Pete’s and the Jean-Bots but that won’t work because I will simply kill them all at once, I will not be known as the Golden Nine anymore. Jean Deas: I knew that there was something different about y’all! Well Mr. 009 I will simply brainwash you again. “Golden Nine” Jeff Harris: Mr. 009? I’m not 009, far from it in fact, and you will be doing nothing because… I am going to kill you, then I’m going to get rid of this shit pit called the Golden Volcano, face it Jean, the era of Deas is over. .::With that said Bryan Deas enters the rooms and runs at Jeff, Jeff however sidesteps this attack making Bryan trip over, Jeff pulls out his golden nine and hits Bryan hard over the head with it, Jean Deas comes charging at Jeff only to get the Golden Nine stuffed in her mouth, Jeff pushes so hard that it begins to choke her, Jean falls over choking then soon enough stops breathing. The Jean-Bots and Mini-Pete’s shut down as Bryan Deas awakens::. Bryan Deas: Yaw killed ma maw! MAW! WAKE UP MAW! .::There is no response as she lays dead on the floor, Bryan Deas turns around only to find that Jeff Harris has disappeared, Where Jeff actually is located is outside the Volcano walking away, the scene cuts back to Bryan Deas, Deas looks at a computer screen showing numbers… Numbers counting down, 10,9,8,7,6,5,4 as the numbers start to tick Bryan begins to squeal as the Volcano explodes, the gold from the volcano starts showering nearby towns making everyone trying to catch pieces of it, the hot liquid golden lava begins running into the sea. Jeff is now in a car, a black BMW to be exact driving away from the scene::. :.:And so it begins, this is the first day of the rest of my life. I am now ready to concentrate on the real things in life and not that comic book joke for a life I had before, no more business cards, no more Cuban cigars, no more golden nines, Jean Deas, Bryan Deas, Pete Harris, Mechanical versions on them self it’s just me, me and my old life. Furiously Addictive Wrestling will be my focus now. I shall reclaim glamour and rise up to Foamy’s level… Oh wait he’s beneath me HA HA HA HA HA!:.:
