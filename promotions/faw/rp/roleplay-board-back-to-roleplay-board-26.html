Cyrus Skar — FAW Roleplay, August 2002

August 2002

Furiously Addictive Wrestling Roleplay Board Roleplay Board | Back to Roleplay Board Cyrus Skar Entitled: Doctor (Rp for Distortion) Written: 10/8/02 Location: Doctors Surgery So I sit here waiting, not much time has passed. It seems like an eternity. I watch the second hand tick round the clock in the room. The brown chair I am sat on was obviously not made for somebody as tall as me. I sit here feeling very uncomfortable, I can tell people don't understand me just off the looks they give me. I am still sat here, it's only been ten minutes but it seems like ten hours. I anxiously wait, then it happens. Nurse: Cyrus Skar? Cyrus: Yes? Nurse: The Doctor will you see you now, go straight on through. I get up out of my seat, I start to walk not knowing what to expect. Should I be scared? Should I be nervous? I am not nervous or scared, I am just curious to what awaits me on the other side of the door. I walk slowly but surely towards the white door. A nameplate stuck to it reads 'Dr M. Halliwell' My hand grasps the handle of the door. It slowly turns under the force of my hand. Dr Halliwell: Ah, you must be Cyrus Skar please take a seat. The average man I see before me is about 5ft8, not fat but not skinny. He has short brown hair and piercing blue eyes. It feels as though he can look into my soul, maybe he can. He looks at me through his spectacles. He remains in his seat whilst I sit down opposite him. The chair in here is much bigger, for the first time since I've been here I feel comfortable physically and mentally. Dr Halliwell: What seems to be the problem then Cyrus? Cyrus: I have a split personality. Surely you have notes on me already? Dr Halliwell: We seem to have lost them, we'll have to start from scratch. We'll start this off with a word association. I'll say a word you tell me the first thing that comes into your head. Ok? Cyrus: Ok. Is he playing with me? Why does he insist on me taking part in a meaningless game? I do not need to play these childish games. I came here for help, not to be humoured. Dr Halliwell: FAW… Cyrus: My workplace. Dr Halliwell: Good, ok what about US Championship… Cyrus: Mine. If you have no notes on me how come you know what I do? Dr Halliwell: I watch wrestling. Next one, JJ Dynasty… Cyrus: Loser. Dr Halliwell: Fool? Define what you mean. Cyrus: He's lost his first match here in FAW that is only the beginning. He'll lose again on Breakdown. He thinks that I am a 'has-been' to quote him. How old am I? Dr Halliwell: 25? Cyrus: Yes I am. Have you ever heard of somebody being a has-been at 25? No, I didn't think so. Has-beens are people like the clown. I am at my best; my worst is better than JJ's best! I am the US Champion for a reason, because I won it. I won it fair and square as well. One, two, three. JJ annoys me thinking I am a nobody. He says that great people have held the belt that I hold. Does he honestly think they were great when they held this belt? Of course they were! Unbeaten runs like me, plenty of victories to their names. But the fact was they were unknown just like me when that happened. They weren't big names when they won my title, they were nobodies like me. Winning this title was the first step on the journey to becoming big names. I shall take the same journey and become the World Champion, a bold claim, but when you're as good as me you can make claims like that. Doctor Halliwell: Interesting, you have a lot to say about this JJ Dynasty character, you seem to dislike him a lot. Cyrus: Would you hate a kid like him thinking he's great when's he accomplished nothing in FAW? Dr Halliwell: That isn't why we're here Cyrus, come on stay focused here. Cyrus: Would you honestly like the guy? Dr Halliwell: No, next word, parents… Cyrus: Dead, Dr Halliwell: I'm sorry I didn't know. Cyrus: Don't be sorry, I never knew either of them, my mum died through unfortunate circumstances. My father died because he was a d!ck. He committed suicide because he didn't think he could bring me up on his own. Dr Halliwell: I see, next word, anger… Cyrus: Him. Dr Halliwell: Him? Who's him Cyrus? Cyrus: My other persona, he is all my hate and rage built up inside of me, I held my anger back when I was kid. It gathered up inside of me and made me what I am today. Dr Halliwell: Ok, now we know the problem I can start analysing you and thinking of a solution to this nasty mess. Cyrus: May I leave now. Dr Halliwell: Yes you may leave, may I add that this little interview will cost $5000. *** I walk over to his desk, I pick his fragile frame up and slam him into the desk. I take the money out of my pocket and ram it down his throat. He starts to choke but I carry on stuffing the bills down his oesophagus. He starts clawing away at my chest, but it is no use. He tries to breathe one last time before falling down, his hands slip down off my chest and onto the table. I pick up his limp body and throw it out of the window. *** Dr Halliwell: Cyrus? Hello? Cyrus: Sorry I was just thinking about something. Can I ask you one thing before I go, will it help myself if I think about getting revenge on people rather than doing it? Dr Halliwell: No, that will make it worse for whenever you snap, it will make more hatred and anger for your other persona to feed off. Cyrus: Really? Thank you for that. I'll remember that. I walk out of the room with a smug smile, I know how to feed my persona. I did not want him to go, he is what I am all about, he helps me hurt people more than I could normally. He takes away my remorse, only people who are not I see that as a bad thing. I can now cause my hurt for my opponent in that ring, the no-show Highwayman will be in for a shock if he does actually turn up at Distortion, I shall show him the reason why I haven't been pinned, the task is near impossible. I shall show him I can take any pain he can throw at me. That is why I have never tapped out, I can take any amount of pain that could possibly be applied in a wrestling match. I think to myself all the ways that I can hurt the Highwayman. Then I realise I had better pay before I leave. I stop at the desk and take the money out of my coat pocket. Cyrus: There should be enough there. Receptionist: Yep, that's fine.. See you again Mr Skar. I whisper under my breath 'Not likely,' as I walk away from the desk and towards the door, I leave and begin contemplating where to go for my lunch. I decide to go home and cook something. I start walking towards my house. *** I see the Highwayman walking down the other side of the street, I walk over to greet him. He doesn't recognise me. I go to shake his hand and tell him who I am. I leave my hand out but he doesn't accept it. I take offence to this and throw him through a shop window. People around my stare whilst one tries to arrest me. I throw him into the road and a car hits him. His body brushes over the bonnet and up onto the windscreen. I carry on walking without looking back. *** Woman: Are you ok sir? Cyrus: What? Oh yeah, I'm fine. Just leave me alone. Women: Ok. I carry on walking down to the street and walk up my drive to my house. *** He's inside my house; I can see him looking through my possessions. I rush up to the door, I quickly push past the open door and into the house. I see him going through my drawers. I slam his head into them and he starts to bleed from a cut on his forehead. I throw him into the door, smashing it off it's hinges. I pick him up and throw him out of my house. I pick the door up and put it back up. *** Paperboy: Are you ok dude? Cyrus: I'm fine! Now piss off! I walk to the door and go inside…
