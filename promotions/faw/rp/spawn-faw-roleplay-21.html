Spawn — FAW Roleplay #21

captured 8 August 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
SpAwN--The Hell Master \~~~~~~~(opening scene)The scene opens up to a family fun center. It has a mini golf course, a driving range, an arcade, a rock wall, and a batting cage. We see Spawn and his camera man walking into the front of the building. Spawn walks over to the change machine and puts in a ten dollar bill. The change falls out and Spawn puts it in his pocket. He then walks towards the batting cages. Spawn walks in and puts in the coins. Spawn: This is a thing I picked up from Happy Gilmore, it was a joke at the time, but the excersize actually works. He steps in front of the base and gets ready for the first ball. It hits him in his thigh. Spawn flinches but does not move much. The next ball hits him in the pectoral. Spawn flinches again but does not move. By this time the two spots have started to become numb. The next ball flies and smacks Spawn in the face. Blood splatters from his lip. Spawn is forced to turn his head and spits out a pool of blood. He pops his head back to face the machine again. Another ball flies out and hits Spawn above his right eye. The eye begins to swell over and his sight begins to fade. The next ball hits him in the stomach. This shot does not affect him because of his abs. The next shot hits him in the left bicep. His arm falls completely limp. Another ball comes flying and hits him in the chin. His head flies back but he remains stationary. Blood again flies from his mouth. Another ball hits him in the other thigh. Spawn now has lost most of the feeling in his legs. The next ball hits him in the other bicep and his arms have gone numb. The last ball delivers another shot to his mouth. A tooth flies out of his mouth along with a pool of blood. Spawn then hobbles out of the cage to the amazement of the crowd who had gathered there. Spawn: These baseballs act like punches and kicks thrown by my opponents. One they have a great amount of strength behind them, two after a while they numb your muscles. At that point it is almost impossible for them to function. I put myself in front of the machine to simulate a match. Now I try and train with these disabilities. Okay stop the camera. Man your crazy. Why can't you train like a normal person? Spawn: Because I'm not normal. I'm leaps and bounds above it. Therefore my training is leaps and bounds above normal standards. It is the only way I can make myself better. Of course, a hidden meaning what else is new. The two walk over to the rock wall. Spawn hands the man a 5 dollar bill and walks towards the wall. Man: Wow buddy what the hell happened to you? He had a car accident. Why is he here? He should be in a fucking hospital. Spawn: Are you a doctor? No Spawn: Then shut the fuck up. Spawn then begins to climb the wall. His arms and legs are wobbling from the bruises on his body. He struggles with every move. He slowly grabs each rock and strains to pull himself up. He slips a few times but never falls off. He then reaches a point when the rock wall slants outward. This part requires just the use of his arms. He takes a sigh and reaches for the first rock hold. He lets his legs dangle below him. Spawn then grabs the rock hold to the left of him. He pulls himself up and reaches for the next hold. He rests for a minute and continues on. His hand shoots out for the next hold. Spawn then pulls himself up again. His arms are shaking very much. They strain to move much less lift his body weight. But the tired muscles proceed. The last hold is the hardest to get to. It is about five feet away. Spawn takes a deep breathe and reaches out for it. He tries to pull himself up but can't get it. The camera man watches in dismay as Spawn tries to reach the last rock. Then he pauses again. He looks down and looks back up again. Then he tries to reach again. This time he screams very loud. His arms shake and the veins pop out of them but they continue to push themselves. He is about an inch away. He can't quite make it. He screams even louder and forces his arms to use more energy. It is just enough. He grabs onto the hold and drags his body up to the top. He stands on top of the wall and looks down at the camera. Spawn: Did you see that my friends? Did you see what anger can make a man do? Everyone counted me out and thought I would fail. But then that anger kicks in. That thought of my father pops into my head and my energy forms. The energy that can bring me above what any normal man can do. And it is the same energy that will drive me past Great 1 on Sunday. It will put me past him and into the FAW biggest spotlight where I belong. It will make me the biggest wrestling superstar in the wolrd. A thing that I have had my eyes on for a very long time. And a respect I will get on Sunday. You see Great 1, I want to take my career to the next level. I want to separate myself to the rest of the pack. The only way I can do that is by going through you. Something that is a definite possibility. All I have to do is use that anger built up inside of me to beat the living hell out of you and then I can truly be the Real Ruler of FAW which by the way I am anyways. No longer will I be a worthless mid carder, and no longer will I have to face stupid newbies like Costella. Because once I am the FAW world Champ good things will happen to me and my career. Better matches, more glory. Two things that you people won't be seeing for a very long time. Be prepared to have your hopes and dreams crushed forever, because on Sunday mine will be fulfilled. Spawn then repels back down the wall to the bottom. He unstraps himself and heads for the arcade. The camera man is very puzzled by this. What are you doing? Spawn: Where going to play some games. What? Spawn: How many times do I have to tell you to shut up and just follow me? Don't ask questions. Just do as I tell you. Spawn: walks to the back of the room towards a booth. It has the words Kickbox written across it. A man waits at the door. Spawn hands him 3 dollars and walks in. It is a kickboxer simulator. Spawn grabs the gloves and shoes on the side wall and puts them on. He walks to the wall and presses the button. Spawn then walks over and puts the helmet on. The camera man walks back outside the booth and watches the TV. We see a red man and a blue man on the screen. The red man is the opponent and the blue man is Spawn. The match starts and the two square off in the center. Spawn delivers the first kick to the man's midsection. The red man bends over and Spawn delivers an uppercut to his face. The red man staggers back. Spawn grabs his head and jumps and delivers his knee into him. The man falls to the ground and the count begins. Spawn: I'm pretty good at this game. Considering I have been kickboxing since I was twelve. Technology is crazy. The ref only gets to a six count and the red man stands up. He comes back at Spawn but misses with a wild punch. Spawn hits a right into his face and hits a left to finish the combination. The red man retreats back into the corner and tries to regroup. He walks back towards Spawn. He stands pretty far away and has his guard down. He is not far enough. Spawn jumps and gives him a roundhouse kick. This flips the man and he falls again. Spawn: Did you see that Great 1? Did you see what I just did to that guy? Be prepared for the same on Sunday when I beat the living hell out of both of you. The red man staggers back up once the ref gets to 8. The man puts his guard up and advances towards Spawn. He shoots a right fist. Spawn blocks it and tries to counter with his left. The red man blocks this and squares up again. The man tries a right low kick. Spawn blocks it and jumps back. He comes back with a kick of his own. The red man blocks and tries a right. Spawn blocks. The red man tries a left in hope of catching Spawn off guard. Spawn blocks and counters with a right. It lands in his face. Frost then throws a left and a right combo. They both hit. Spawn then follows with a side kick which makes the man stagger back. Frost then finishes him with a superkick. The fight is over and Frost walks back out. Spawn: My fighting skill cannot be matched by anybody in the FAW. That you can see. However I know that I need more than fighting skill to win this match. Hopefully today you have seen what other skills I posses. Skills that will be used against you on Sunday. I hoped you took notes my friends because the next time you will see this it will be on the test. The test to see if you will survive our match, or perish during it. Spawn then turns to the screen. He watches as the blue man is waving his arms in victory while the red man is still flat on the ground. Spawn: Be ready to feel my fists and feet on Sunday. And get ready for the pain that will surely follow. Because when it hits you will fall. You will fall to the ground in defeat leaving me to take my prize. One that I richly deserve and one that I will never let go of for a very long time. The FAW Championship belt. See you Sunday boys. Good, cut. Okay let's go home buddy. Spawn: Good idea. The two walk outside to Spawn's Cobra. They both hop in and drive away. Spawn is sitting in the car while the camera man watches him. He wants to say something but he looks somewhat scared. Is that stuff about your father true? Spawn: Yes. How did you deal with it? Spawn: I didn't. What do you mean? Spawn: I never dealt with it, that's what I mean. If I forgot about it I wouldn't have this anger anymore would I. I guess your right. But is that really that healthy? Spawn: First, I don't care. Second what did I tell you about asking questions. Sorry. They pull into the driveway and Spawn gets out of the car. He walks down to the mail box and grabs his mail. He looks at the top letter. It came from the Albany State Penitentiary. He opens the letter and reads it. Spawn: Landy, This is your father. I need to speak to you, about your mother. Spawn looks at the letter and runs inside the house. He grabs the camera man and drags him outside. He throws him into the car and hops in himself. He starts the car up and drives away.
