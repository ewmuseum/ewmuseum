The Home Shensui Yusuki Furiously Addictive Wrestling Roleplay #45 Date: 25/10/02 Location: The Home At The Home We see Shensui sitting in a corner of a room, which has nothing in apart from a small bed with no covers or pillow's, in the other corner there is a small bucket just on the floor. Saying the room was dark, dingy and basic would be an under -statement as he rocks back and forth on his chair, which is small and made of nothing but solid hard wood straight from a tree. The camera turns around the room to show a window in a corner which can not shut it only had metal bars protecting this room from the outside world. Sound then starts to come into play with the chimes of some church in the background yet Shensui does not move from the set. You hear the sound of children playing in the background. In his chair you see Shensui start to move. He stands up and moves towards the far end of the room removes the bin; he takes a small key out of his pocket and places it in the corner of the room. He jacks up the sideboard as a floorboard gets removed; he takes a bandana from there along with a photo. He quickly sits back down and blows some dust of the picture and starts to look at it he laughs then sighs. He takes a knife out of his pocket, he rips open the back of the picture and removes a small peace of paper he holds it up and starts to read it. (The Paper= Translated) To Be Friends for a long time is hard To Be Friends forever is even harder To Be Friends for a week maybe fun To Be Friends for be a friend until one departs is are justice. Standing by one another When the other falls The other will be near To help the other back up If anything should happen To one of us The other should have this… (The Thing) He unfolds the photo to reveal a signed photo by Amagosi Thisanj, Shensui lets out a slight laugh before putting it down and opening up the bandanna. He takes a key from it and then opens another floor board taking out a second bandanna he shakes it as two tickets fall out they are both winning tickets to any Action of Japan Wrestling event. Taking the tickets he gets up and exists the room he makes his way down an under lit corridor. At the end of the corridor he takes a left and opens a door, which goes into some kind of church. Entering the church a few of former rich children notice him and the ones that watch wrestling. He walks to the front of the room and peers into the coffin to see his former friend lying stone cold dead in a coffin. Turning around he pays most attention to a middle age lady at the back he gulps before walking off of the stage area and down to the back of the room. (An old Teaching) He slowly walks up to the lady she does not look all that happy he goes to say something but stops himself he then trys again but again can't bring himself to say it. She looks at him in manor that is very unwelcoming a child runs past them and she slaps him on the back of the head. 'Hello…Mrs.' (Sweet Dreams) Then it cuts to a bedroom where a distraught Shensui lay in the comfort of his own bed. He sweets a lot as the tears run down his face along with the hot atmosphere which runs up and down his body in the hot Japan air. Getting out of bed he opens a cubed and pulls out a glass he reaches into his fridge and from there brings out a curtain of milk. He drinks it down fairly quickly as he sits down on a chair and places his head in his arms in thought. He soon brings it back up in stress he grabs the towel and takes the sweet off of his head again. He moves across the room to the television set he picks up a remote control and tunes the television into the Japan news! (Can't get your head around it) The news comes on as a bold middle age oriental man sits on a nice chair he then stands up to speak. -Japanese to English- Mr. Minoshi: This weekend FAW has it's biggest pay per view to date and the local hero Shensui Yusuki will get his chance to win the FAW International Champion. He will be up against the greatest ever hardcore champion to live he says, Crimson lord. So can are local hero win after just two and half months since the death of his dear friend Amagosi Thisanj. Shensui throws the remote control at the television set ands it goes right into the middle of the screen. “I am so pissed off, why the hell should I have to fight this idiot, FAW making me come back to work early, the stupid crying baby’s, I am going to make Crimson Lord pay.” It goes quiet as Shensui begins again to speak to himself. “He thinks because his mummy and daddy brought him up in a nice way and I was in a orphans home that he is better then me. Did he have to fight for his food, eat from a pig’s troth? Did he have to sometimes not eat for a whole night because he forgot his prayer? No he never (cry’s) but I did.” He sits there not speaking. “Did he get beat until he told on all the bigger kids only to get beat by the bigger kids instead. The people of this world think all of the Japanese are rich well I was not I had nothing no one to go to no one to cry with no one to love me and no one to care for me.” The depressed Japanese gets onto his feet and walks over to his cupboard and pulls out a whisky bottle he starts to drink it from the Bottle and then collapses onto the floor as the camera turns off.
