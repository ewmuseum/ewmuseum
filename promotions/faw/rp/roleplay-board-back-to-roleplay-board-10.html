Spawn — FAW Roleplay, August 2002

August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Furiously Addictive Wrestling Roleplay Board Roleplay Board | Back to Roleplay Board Spawn Entitled: THE BESTD CV Written: 8/3/03 Location: BOSTON The scene FAW house show in Boston, Ma, The place is packed and you can tell the crowd is very live. The camera takes us to the parking lot were we see a black limo pull up. The fans gather around the limo surrounding it completely, as everybody stares at the limo waiting anxiously to see who is the person that will step out of the car, out of nowhere a man comes rushing through the crowd, opens the limo door and finally the person in the limo steps out. The camera man gets closer to the man that stepped out of the limo to reveal him to be the former 2 time world heavy weight champion Spawn the Hell Master. In a matter of seconds another black limo pulls up and once again the man rushes over and opens the limo door, out of the limo the camera reveals HHK, and JLC, The camera shows them going inside the FAW house show together the scene ends. The scene shows DCV going down the rampway and straight to the ring. The fans are booing very loud and as DCV goes down the rampway to the square circle you can see HHK sticking his middle finger to the FANS. Nothing faces this team they know they are unstoppable. Inside the ring there is a match going on between two jobbers, Spawn and JLC stand at each of the ring as HHK goes to the pronouncer table and grabs a microphone, and begins to speak. HHK: Well, well, well, since when did FAW begin women wrestling. The two wrestlers stop fighting each other and stare at HHK, as HHK begins to speak again. HHK: That’s right you heard me women’s wrestling because that’s the way you all are fighting like two females in heat heh. And what the fuck are you staring at you want to say something back to me? (looking at Spawn) Hey yo Spawn toss that bitch a mic. Spawn pics up another mic and tosses it to the wrestlers. HHK: Well you got your mic now speak bitch. Wrestler: Huh, who the hell you guys think you are coming in here and breaking up our match? HHK: Ha! You hear this fuck JLC? he is asking who are we… hey Spawn why don’t you get in the ring and show these two fucks who we are. Spawn climbs the ring and goes after the two wrestlers, then JLC climbs the other side and climbs the top rope. As Spawn takes on both wrestlers, HHK tosses JLC the chair who catches it and jumps down hitting one of the wrestlers, JLC then tosses the chair to Spawn who catches it and hits the other wrestler over the head with it. DCV then begins to punish the two jobbers slapping, beating them and humiliating them in front of thousands of people, when the beating ends HHK grabs the mic and begins to speak to the fans, who are booing very loud. HHK: Shut the fuck UP! And let me speak, you want to boo someone boo your moma biatches. Now Bomber, Thunder and Prodigy listen up to what DCV has to say, what you saw just now in the ring was a pure taste of DCVmania and FAW is full of it. We are taking control of this fed and no one will be able to stop us, because no one can stop us. DCV is just to damn good, Tell em JLC? JLC: Three men stand in our way of reaching our next goal. They all stand in the way of DCV of becoming the best stable ever in FAW. This is another opportunity in a series of them and one that we can’t afford to lose. We have been taking it easy with you people too many times and it is time for us to really get down dirty , they might not come as frequent as I would like them to. So you could say that my back is up against the wall and that I have a lot riding on this match. There is a lot on the line for me in this match, much more than just a chance to just compete again. I have my pride on the line. I have the respect of my peers on the line. It’s not a new situation for me, but it is one that I never get used to. I am a desperate man, one that will do anything to get back what he once had. This makes me a very dangerous man. This Sunday when the bell rings it is all about survival for me. What an appropriate name for the event, because that is exactly what I find myself having to do. I have to survive this match and survive the valley that I find myself in right now. I have to rise above where I am now and make it back to where I once was. I have to find some gold, and what better way to do it than by tagging with two of the most decorated wrestlers in this company’s history. You tell em Spawn. Spawn: This Sunday is going to be the start of a new era for DCV. It will be the night when I rise out of this slump that I am in and reach new heights here in this company. Bomber your team will fall in our path as we move on to bigger and better things. It is past due time for something good to happen to DCV and this Sunday that something is going to take place. This Sunday at Distortion it will be DCV taking home the victory. It will be a new era in wrestling. There are a lot of changes going around here in the past week. We have seen old shows scrapped, new shows started, and titles have changed hands. It is a much different company than it used to be a few months ago. Some of that is for the good, and some is for the bad. But I am one to always welcome change and in this case, maybe I can change with it. DCV is the team to start a change with it will be a good change good for me bad for everybody else. Team Bomber get ready you are the beginning of this change with your team we shall spill first blood and then we shall continue OUR TORTUROUS WAYS IN MONTHS TO COME. Tell them HHK. HHK: Thunder... I don't even fucking know what to say to you. You're pitiful, man. You're listening to some jamoke named Bomber? You're listening to a man who's career is washed up and broken? To a man that loses to jabronis? C'mon, Thunder... I thought you were smarter than that, rookie. You think you're going to set up a group that will scare us, hit us when we don't know it? Just saying things on camera makes us know that it's coming, dick head. You think I'm stupid? WHAT IN THE HELL? You're a feminist group or something? or some shit like that? Think about it, cunt bag. Get your queer party buddy Bomber and Prodigy to rethink this whole idea, especially if you don't want to be called a feminist group, queef. I'm tired of your stupid ass shit, man. No, I wasn't trying to scare you. No, I wasn't trying to show you that I can be extreme, too. I'm pissed off. Everything thinks less of me than they should. This Sunday, the three of you will know that you don't underestimate your opponent. Don't underestimate DCV, kids. I'm your worst fucking nightmare. Lets get psycho. DCV continues their carnage in the ring as the scene fades to black.
