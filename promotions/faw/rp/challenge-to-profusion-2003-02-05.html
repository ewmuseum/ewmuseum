Challenge to Profusion

5 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Challenge to Profusion The Great One Furiously Addictive Wrestling Roleplay #256 Date: 05/02/03 Location: Locker Room Sitting back in his chair with his red waistcoat on is The Great One. He moves his shades down to the end of his nose so that he can see his door open and James Harris walk through it. He doesn’t look that amazingly pleased to see him. Harris: Great One, what the hell is going on in your head? Great One sits back in his chair, resting one leg on the other. He takes off his shades; then jumps up and grabs Harris by the shirt. Great One: Listen tosser, no one walks through my door without telling me and without knocking, and as if that wasn’t bad enough, you come in and start yelling at me and asking me questions!? Harris: Sorry. Great One: It’s a good job your apologized because I really didn’t want to make any head sized holes in my wall. Harris: Ok, well, can I just get your thoughts on a few things? Great One: Hey bitch fist, why do you always ask for peoples “thoughts?” Harris: I don’t know, so that I can find out what people think about certain things. Great One: But you always say “can I get your thoughts on this” or whatever. Why not think up something new like, “Slap my ass and call me Patricia, give me some info before I hitcha!” That’s how you do it man, you have to make the interviews more fun more hip. Chop off a couple of rhymes in the middle of interviews and shit. Harris: I don’t know, that sounds tough, but I better try it before you call me a puff. Great One: You damn right I will! Harris: No I actually did one right there. Great One: That doesn’t count. Harris: Why not? Great One: Don’t question me you little fuck, because I’ll throw a punch and you better make sure you duck! Harris: Sorry. Great One: I’m just joking, I was rhyming to. Harris: These words go together like they were stuck with glue. Great One: What in silicon heaven? Harris: No not heaven, I’m going to Devon! Yeah I’m getting good at this. Great One: Devon? Where the hell is Devon? Harris: It’s some place in the south of England. Great One: Only you would be sad enough to go on holidays to England. Harris: Well, you know. Great One: Hey didn’t you say you wanted some info? Harris: Yes please. Great One: On what? Harris: Well we could start with JLC. Great One: Jim the loser, sure if that’s what you want. The bottom line is I am sick and tired of everyone preaching that son of a bitch and saying that he is the best in FAW’s history, he is the ultimate FAW wrestler, there will never be anyone better than JLC. How any of these sons of bitches and can say that I don’t know because that little bitch has never fought the best, and the best is The Great One. Ooohh, I have won every title there is in FAW. Do you really think I give a damn? You know what I am going to do, I am going to win every title in FAW as well, all I need to win is the continental title and then I’ve done it all. I’ve been the hardcore champ when I beat the Crimson bitch, I’ve been a tag champ with Spawn when fooled everyone into thinking I was Bayne because everyone in this world besides me is just totally stupid! And I beat Spawn, not drew with him, I beat him and won the World Title. All I need to do now is to win that Continental Title and I’ve won everything that is currently on offer. Harris: So you want to face Profusion? Great One: Yeah, I was going to issue a challenge to that little fuck anyway because there is not a chance in hell that he is leaving this federation with a FAW title and then going to OSW. Profusion got nowhere in FAW so he will get no where in OSW, he’s a complete failure and I will prove that by beating the shit out of him on the very first screening of Resistance. So Profusion, will you grow some testicles before you leave FAW and put the belt up on the line against The Great One next week at Resistance? I’ll answer for you, oh hell yeah you will! And as far as Jim the Loser goes, I kicked your ass two Sundays in a row and I am planning on making it a hat trick so get ready for a big fat whoppin’ of your ass when The Great One walks in next Sunday. And James Harris, get ready to feel my foot smack against your ass as your go out the door, go on get out! Great One kicks him in the ass and pushes him out of the door. Harris: Thank you Great One for your comments, glad with my head you didn’t have to make any dents.
