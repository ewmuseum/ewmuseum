Drive-Thru From Hell (Panic RP #2) Gabriel Hunter Furiously Addictive Wrestling Roleplay #249 Date: 31st January Location: Some place -- We join our hero, somewhere warm, cruising along at a steady pace. In a week of failed attempts at rest and relaxation after getting stuck in an Amish village, Gabriel has chosen to drive around the city, looking for something to do. Playing it cool, and not taking any chances. With another important match-up this Sunday at Panic, Gabriel faces a stern, stern challenge, and should really be training at this point in time. But damn, this is a Gabriel Hunter promo, not a Mr. Motivator video! -- -- The scene opens up a-top the hood of a sparkling, new-looking red convertible, as it cruises along at a steady speed. Above the soft purring engine, the sounds of your average inn-city highway can be heard. The loud buzzing of older cars’ engines, the tooting of horns, and that damn annoying noise scooters-make. Still focused on the hood, in the reflection we can see the reflection of little fluffy clouds upon a picture-perfect blue sky. Aww, how cute. As the hood slopes down to the sides, the reflections of cars and various low-roofed commercial buildings can be seen – Florida, judging by pure guesswork. The camera pans out and down, focusing on the font of the car. The sun glimmers off of the Mercedes Benz badge, the surprisingly, the oval-shaped front grate isn’t covered with a mass of squished flies and insects. All around us, cars move around the busy highways, some turning off to visit the many attractive-looking stores and attractions, others just going their way. Once again, we pan across, this time toward the interior of the car. Gabriel Hunter sits at the driver’s wheel, sitting on top of the marvellous looking cream seats, one arm on the wheel, the other leaning against the side of the rolled down window. He wears a pair of black sunshades for obvious reasons, as well as a pair of slightly baggy blue jeans, and noticeably, a Furiously Addictive Wrestling t-shirt. -- Gabriel Hunter: Hmmm … Where to go, where to go, where to go … -- Gabriel looks around at the surrounding buildings, obviously looking for something to do. Eventually, after getting tired of looking at signs for “Ponderosa Steakhouse” and “Beanie Baby World,” Gabriel shakes his head, bored. -- Gabriel Hunter: God, this sucks … Nothing to do, nowhere to go … And did I ever mention that I HATE the sound of cars!? -- Gabriel sighs, shaking his head once more, before looking down towards the shiny new CD player built into the car’s interior. Somewhat dangerously, with one arm he leans over toward the glove compartment at the passenger’s side of the red Mercedes Benz SLK 230, before pulling it open. Like an avalanche, a barrage of CDs flow it, some opening up as they hit the bottom of the car. Gabriel rummages around blindly, still keeping his eye on the road, as he pulls a fallen CD up, regaining his posture in his seat. He holds it up to read what CD it is … -- Gabriel Hunter: Hmmm … Justin Timberlake … Wait a minute … JUSTIN TIMBERLAKE!? How the HELL did that get in there! -- Without hesitation, Gabriel tosses the CD over his shoulder, it flies through the air, coming to a rest on the road below, only to be crushed by the wheels of the next oncoming car. -- Gabriel Hunter: That’s the last time I let my damn agent borrow the car! UGH! -- Again, Gabriel reaches down, hoping for a better choice of music, this time pulling up something a little more preferable than the last choice. -- Gabriel Hunter: Ahhh, yes! That’s better … -- Gabriel pushes the CD into the player, is the mechanism pulls it in. A few seconds later, the opening guitar riffs to a song that would be recognised by anyone who knows anything about punk rock, as “Can I Borrow Some Ambition?” by Guttermouth begins to play, providing the perfect backdrop for the sunny afternoon, whilst drowning out the sounds of nearby cars, and inadvertently causing illegal noise pollution. Like a fool, Gabriel jumps and jives around in his seat, along with the tempo. -- Gabriel Hunter: WOOOO! -- Before him, there is a set of traffic lights. Of course, the car in front of him manages to scrape through, just before the lights turn red. Gabriel fails to notice this, and just at the nick of time, screeches to a halt right on the line. He breathes a sigh of relief. Beside him, a business gentleman pulls up in a similar looking black convertible, talking on a cell phone. Upon hearing Hunter’s “noise,” he immediately cringes, plugging his ear, trying to speak over the top of it. -- Gabriel Hunter: Hurry up, hurry up, HURRY UP! Damn I hate traffic lights! -- Failing to speak over the music, the man shakes his head, and moves the phone away from his ear. A fuming look across his young face, he wails at the top of his voice towards Gabriel. -- Businessman: EXCUSE ME! COULD YOU PLEASE TURN IT DOWN A BIT! I’M TRYING TO HAVE A PHONE CALL HERE! -- Gabriel barely notices the man’s warbling, and turns to face him, yelling back. -- Gabriel Hunter: NO! I DON’T LIKE COTTON CANDY! BUT THANK’S FOR ASKING! Businessman: I SAID; CAN YOU PLEASE TURN IT DOWN?! Gabriel Hunter: WHAT!? NO! I DON’T WEAR A CROWN! Businessman: OH FOR GOD’S SAKES! JUST TURN THE DAMN MUSIC OFF! Gabriel Hunter: WHAT’S THAT!? YOU NEED TO COUGH?! -- The businessman shakes his head, as the light above him flashes green, and he pulls away. As he speeds off from Hunter, he mutters under his breath, before resuming his call. -- Businessman: Idiot … -- Gabriel meanwhile, shrugs, and pulls away to the right, heading down a road that is almost to the one he just pulled away from. Choosing the lane nearest to the curb, Gabriel continues to drive on. -- Gabriel Hunter: Hmmm, maybe I should turn this down a little … People might complain … -- Hunter reaches for the volume control, greatly reducing the sound’s intensity, even though he failed to comprehend a word that the businessman said. He drives on for a few seconds, nodding his head to the temp, before beginning to speak. -- Gabriel Hunter: Hmmm … Schedule … Today, drive around like a hobo looking for something to do … Tomorrow, meeting with FAW representatives … Sunday, match. Fun-a-roonie. Guess I should speak a bit more about my opponents – well, they mouth off enough. I mean, have you ever WATCHED a Spider promo!? Talk about boring ... -- Gabriel pauses for a second, before continuing. -- Gabriel Hunter: Ah well, hopefully I’ll have more luck than I’ve had in past weeks, heh. Fatal Four Way? Why cant I just have a simple, one on one match, dammit!? -- Getting bored of the same old music, Gabriel presses “eject” on the CD player, as the CD elegantly slides out, he tosses it carelessly down onto the floor below, where the other CDs lie, before leaning down, and picking up another random CD. Sitting back up and sliding the CD in, “Oi To The World” by the Vandals begins playing gently, as Gabriel continues. -- Gabriel Hunter: I know, Gabriel Hunter hasn’t exactly set the FAW world alight thus far, but come Sunday night, Im out to change that. I’m not going to bore you all with talk of how badly I’m going to beat my opponents, because frankly, I’ll let my words do the talking … And pretty soon, I’ll be standing on top of a mountain so big, that NOBODY else will be able to scale it! … WHOA! Hardcore metaphor! -- Gabriel salutes himself, with a quick grin, and sits back in his chair, as a satisfied look draws across Hunter’s youthful features, as he looks on ahead. About 70 yards away, he notices a large, trademark Burger King Drive-Thru sign. Below it, hangs one of those kick-ass digital signs, reading “Get TWO Whoppers For The Price Of One! Only This Weekend, At Burger King!”. -- Gabriel Hunter: Hmmm, two whoppers for the price of one? That, my friends, is an offer nobody can refuse! Lets go! -- Gabriel speeds up, hitting the right indicator, before turning off slowly into the drive through. Amazingly for this time of day, there is no queue whatsoever tailing around the outside of the single-floor, red-brick building. Gabriel’s car slowly crawls along the single-lane road, past the various shrubberies at his left hand side and the red-brick walls at his right, before reaching the microphone/speaker combo, and the giant illuminated menu before him. Gabriel leans out of the car, browsing through the various items on the list, as a technically altered voice begins to speak through the speaker. -- Assistant: Mmmfmmm fmmumgarmph Burger King! Pmmmfopor yoof mumf! Gabriel Hunter: Uh, yea … I’d like to order the double-whopper deal thing, with a Pepsi and fries, please? Assistant: Goomph mmmmfmmook finmph tramphplaaaaamph? -- Gabriel scratches his head in confusion. -- Gabriel Hunter: No! I said two whoppers, fries and Pepsi! Assistant: Thaaamph wofffff imph saaaaaaaaaaaaaampheeed! Maraamph! Gabriel Hunter: Dude, you really need to get that sore throat seen to, I’ve got some soothers in my pocket if you want them … Assistant: Brumph mumph? Gabriel Hunter: Who’s your doctor? Assistant: Imph dumph mmmmmfffmmmmf amph dooomptomph. Gabriel Hunter: … Fred Durst isn’t a doctor you idiot, but anyway, I’m coming round to pick up my order … Good luck with the throat! Assistant: Muuufck. Gabriel Hunter: I agree! -- Gabriel slots the car into 1st gear, and slides around the corner, towards the pick up point. Through the hole in the wall, sits a teenage male, dressed in a Burger King uniform. Frankly, the guy looks like a geek, with metre-thick glasses and a bowl haircut. Gabriel looks up at him, as the employee turns around, typing a few buttons into the computer before him. -- Gabriel Hunter: Got my order ready yet? Employee: Yeah, one flame-grilled chicken burger, garden salad, and a diet sparkling mineral water? That’s $2.92 please sir. Gabriel Hunter: … That isn’t what I ordered. Employee: Well, That’s what the machine says, and machines don’t lie, sir. Gabriel Hunter: I specifically ordered the double whopper, fries and f*cking Pepsi meal! And if your machine can’t interoperate that, then that’s just damn tough! Because I want my food now, dammit! -- An uneasy look comes across the face of the young employee. -- Employee: Err, right … I’ll try and get your corrected order to you right away sir. Gabriel Hunter: Good, now hurry up ya little douche. -- The guy stands up, walking out of Gabriel’s view. Gabriel sits back in his seat, sighing at the terrible service, as through the gap in the wall, the employee can be heard communicating with some other employee. After about 40 seconds, the employee returns with a full, brown paper bag of Burger King “food,” handing it to Gabriel. -- Employee: Sorry about the delay sir, the speaker back there isn’t as clear as it could be … -- He hands the bag to Gabriel, who places it on the passengers seat. Gabriel reaches into his pocket, pulling out a half-full roll of throat candies. He throws it to the employee, who fumbles it around, before catching it. -- Gabriel Hunter: Give that toe the guy who can’t speak properly … Poor guy … Employee: Uhh, that was a fault with the machine, sir, it had nothing to do with the e- Gabriel Hunter: JUST DO IT DAMMIT! … Now how much do I owe you? Employee: Ummm, that would be … $3 exactly sir. -- Gabriel reaches down into his pocket again, this time pulling out a handful of coins and notes. He throws them down on the dashboard, before scattering around, looking the for required amount. Picking out 3 dollar bills, he hands them to the employee, who tucks them away into the till. -- Employee: Thank you sir, enjoy your meal. -- Without response, Gabriel pulls away – not going very far admittedly. He travels around the corner of the drive through, and straight out the front of the restaurant, into the near-full car park. He finds a space situated facing right into the oversized restaurant window, drawing to a halt, and un-buckling his seat belt. Gabriel reaches across toward the passengers seat, pulling bag across, before gently placing it on his lap. He un-rolls the top of it, looking down onto his order. Lo-and-behold, it’s the correct order! Yay! PARTY! … He grins, before reaching down and grabbing one of the burgers, undoing the paper around it. Hunter then sinks his teeth into the oversized burger, taking a huge bite out of it. Suddenly, the look on his face turns sour. -- Gabriel Hunter: I … HATE … RELISH! SOMEONE’S GONNA DIE FOR THIS! -- And … Fade. --
