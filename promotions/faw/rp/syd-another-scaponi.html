Syd... Another Scaponi?

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Tony Vigilante Entitled: Syd... Another Scaponi? Written: Fuck if I know Location: Backstage, in the Corridor He opened his eyes. The hallway was narrow and claustrophic, painted an antiseptic green that offended the senses, and its floor bare concrete. He knew without turning that the journalists were following a pace behind him. He glanced down at himself and saw the media greys with something like surprise. A frown crossed his face. 'What the fuck do you want?' he asked, in a rude manner, not slowing his pace. He had a vague feeling of deja vu, and a worm of fear burrowing in his gut. 'You scaponis looking for trouble? 'We got a real genius here,' smirked the journalist, giving him an untidy smirk 'But I don't--' he started, then paused. 'Listen, dude, I'm not putting up with your topping no more.?' he asked, his voice cracking, a drop of sweat beading on his forehead. 'We just want a few words from you, after all, you are scheduled to return to a FAW ring on Sunday' The journalist said it in an icy monotone. He shivered. He didn't know what to respond with, but he thought. God help him, he thought. Tony looked directly into the eyes of the journalists scurrying around him. And like the pack of wolves they were, they were ready to eat on Tony's words. 'Syd, the scaponi who's trying to make a name for himself. Tha easy way to make a name for yoself is to get it changed with de depoll, you know what am saying? But hey, Syd, if you're planning to make a name for yoself and using me to elevate yoself, you better think twice, scaponi. Like I always say, you can fah get about anything of that sort.' 'If you want to talk bitches, scaponi, we'll talk bitches in the middle of the ring. Y'see, the philosophy has always been my opponent be the bitch and I be the dog. Usually the dog fucks the bitch senseless, and I'm sure the story won't be different come Distortion. As for stealth and power. Scaponi, the only things you're going to attack wid are your longer finger nails. Your long finger nails that are overdue a good trimming, well I'm the man to cut you down a size. Know what I'm sayin, scaponi?' 'My fate, and my life might be in your hands, but then again, I can feel it in my hands. It's stuck to my palm and it'll remain there as long as I systematically shut you down. Syd, you will fall to the Masterpiece Conspiracy.' 'That's all-- ... Scaponis!' Tony Vigilante walks off in a pace as the journalists are left taking notes for there articles.
