Littering is a big problem (Panic #1) Xing Furiously Addictive Wrestling Roleplay #275 Date: 26.02.03 Location: House of the Gods “Lovely California day in the bright and clear estate where Meditator is staying at the moment would think he would getting a rest after taking such a beating from CJ last Sunday that he would be just about to make it to the Ppv which he come out to win to make an illustrious debut in FAW. But know I am mistaken he what seems to meditating lets go in and lets hear what he is doing. “Aum………….. I am free with my inner monster I am being enlightened I can feel it burning down my back hot yet dry I am getting an answer from God he is saying, Huh? He is saying tea running down my back? What does that mean?” The servant of Meditator had accidentally spilt tea down Meditators back and replies shaking “Err…. Well about that remark from the God well he is saying that because I spilt your non-milk tea.” Meditator stood and was about to throw a fist but held back when he heard voices telling him to wait till Sunday when he can kick too assholes. “Get me another one you punk and this time make it less hot no sugar or no Milk!” Meditator sat back down trying to reach the enlightenment. The servant went out mad and for revenge put milk in the tea he was about to give until God held the man back he could not give the drink to Meditator because time was stopped he then some a shadowy figure that said to him “Don’t do this in the house of God the almighty yours truly, just think about it.” Time was restored to when he was about to pour the milk into the cup but without knowing he gave Meditator a non-milk or sugar but boiling hot tea. Meditator took it and with in an instant spat it out and pushed the man down and told him to leave keeping his aggression in God was pleased and we have a just confirmed interview in with God himself and Meditator. God: “Now Meditator I am impressed with your power to overcome your anger by not hitting that ingrate trying to burn you in the house of almighty that is me how dare he but I think you shall bring him back because he might turn into a traitor to me God and join CJ and V man those punks.” Meditator: “Understand this God those will not get away from Vendetta walking they will be out of there in an ambulance because that punk CJ needs teaching that you don’t mess with me Meditator because he will always come out the victim of a beastly attack from me. That punk Violator put away my good friend Xing so he is going down because you know why ‘I am sick and on the loose’” After hearing we got a close up with Meditator and we asked his thoughts on the upcoming match and last Sundays match and this is what he had to say “CJ Maxwell is not even close to knowing what it means to be against god and me now I may have beat last Sunday on my debut who would have bet against me but he will soon begin to suffer through his whole body when I am through with him on Sunday and as for Violator well what can I say he is one of those men that were booked to go to hell when he was born with that name Violator he does not listen to the rules he goes on porn sites he is a drug addict and oh no this is a big boner maker for the devil down there he Litters! Yes you heard it he litters so I am sorry to say I will be littering this Sunday yes I am so sorry but I will be littering by chopping up that crude Violator so when that night ends I will be victorious once again because those punks are just dirty old Judas’ that will not walk again come next Monday.” Now there we have what a threat just made there on our show of undercover Meditators and he is the best one and we got the dirt out of goodnight and goodpeace.”
