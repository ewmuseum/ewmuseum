Why oh Why! Xing Furiously Addictive Wrestling Roleplay #84 Date: 02 December Location: Brooklyn Xing is entering the bad back streets of Brooklyn obviously thinking of the good times when he held an unbeaten record down in that very dumping ground, trash everywhere now and no dump to be seen but this a definite certainty that you won’t find Lee Smith around in this sort of area that’s for sure. It is getting dark and darker by the minute with a very hard breeze sizzling down Xing’s back. What a very scarce scene of night, but is Xing alone? He is definitely feeling depressed following his Panic defeat to Lee Smith, so with all his rage Xing screams out “How?” and ferociously strikes a can which hits a trash compactor that rattles and then stops immediately. Silence spreads around the back streets which shocks Xing no sound not even a gush of wind to blow in his ear. A few seconds had passed and the silence was restored with an uproar of men shouting. Xing got anxious and started to go out of the dead end back streets but before he could at the least move to the exit of about ten men surrounded him blocking off the exit. The men approach Xing with a creepy yet angry smirk, which sends Xing to put up his fists and got him ready to fight. Each man had a weapon but Xing was not afraid. He put on his great Xing No Go stare and made his big pump action with his shoulders the men obviously thinking of his wife or the big defeat was sent into laughter at Xing this sent Xing extremely mad he realises that they could be laughing at his wife’s death he pushes each and every one down like a pack of dominoes. Xing escapes into his car, he was furious at the blokes, after a long hour of a drive he enters an English cemetery in Canada! He pulls up outside and asks to get in. The bloke calls out to his friends “Look this is that bloke that lost his wife and the match.” Xing pumps up himself and the car and zooms straight into the big metal gates and knocks it down with sheer power the car was damaged but it didn’t look like Xing could care less about the damage at all. He then pulls up next to a field with a grave stone right in the middle of it with no other gravestone to be seen nearby. Xing approaches the stone we see that it is his wife’s deathbed. He crouches down by it and hears people laughing at him and heard one couple say “Look over there at that grave stone on its own obviously no one liked the person I know my dad wouldn’t like to be hated like that with no one to care for him that’s why he is here together with his family hey isn’t that the same surname as that grave stone all alone.” they point out at another grave which is his mothers. Xing begins to weep and covers his head and recites a prayer “Your life has come to an upsetting and heroic end you are an Angel in the sky why oh why did you the kind woman that you are have to die.” The place goes silent and the night turns even darker so dark you can see no stars up in the sky. A few years back at a field near Xing’s aunties and uncles house……… Xing looks up into the sky and questions a floating object above his head “Who are you?” he receives no reply so he asks the object another question “What are you?” a few seconds it took before words came out of the thing above his head and he or she said to a bemused and puzzled Xing “I am what you obviously first thought I was, I am yes! A ghost” Xing after finding out wasn’t too surprised but freaked out so the ghost carries on. “But why? You may ask well because I am here to guide you and show the ins and outs of your life that you didn’t see, to put it in simpler form I am her to show you how much people disrespected you and your wife’s feelings. Xing do you remember this day?” The ghost points straight ahead at a house which gets Xing’s attention when he sees a very smartly dressed man with a tear in his eye and a smile on his face ringing a door bell on the house and Xing replies with “Why yes I do remember that day and that is me and my Aunt Lancelet answering the door now its when my wife had just died and I was giving out the funeral invitations for the date of December 1st, but why do you show this to me this shows unity in this family oh I see you think that my Aunts and Uncles didn’t go because they don’t love me oh no! They were sick on the day of my wife’s funeral and they were really desperate to go but they were too sick.” The ghost looks down at his black shiny boots and says, “Carry on watching this is what you didn’t see.” Xing is gob smacked to see his family chuck away the invitation to the party and then complains by saying “That can’ be true they called and they was sick I could hear the sickness in there voices any way how did you see that?” “Xing you didn’t see them chuck it away but it’s true and so is this up here my boy you see all that happens and I because I maybe related to you had to guide you in your life” the ghost replies and pushes him forward onto a voicemail machine and what he could hear was the call he had picked up the night before the funeral which was from his so called ‘sick’ Aunt Forestical and it said “I’m sorry ‘Cough!’ ‘Cough!’ I can’t come tomorrow me and the others aren’t felling to well – “He! He!” shush.” “Who was that?” Asked a curious Xing “Oh it was the TV.” Replied his Aunt “It’s a shame you can’t come It doesn’t matter though if your ill your ill it’s a real shame I’ll send her your love ok bye get better.” The call ends. “What was wrong with that?” Asks Xing “Well what you didn’t see on the other line was a tissue other the other the phone to make the her voice sound ill and the people laughing were your Aunt and Uncles not the TV.” The ghost replied much to the hatred of Xing. “I don’t want to show you what I saw for the other people that couldn’t make it is too sickening to say what they did to get out of the funeral. Lets get you out of here and lets take you to the church on the day of the funeral for your lovely wife do you know what happens here then?” asks the curious ghost and receives and bland “yes.” From the upset Xing “Why so glum?” asks the very questionative ghost. Xing replies still upset, as he points toward the church with “Well look it’s just me on my own with absolutely no one there but the vicar to lean on and cry by.” Xing weeps while listening to the ghost saying, “I hope I have showed you how many people love you and when she was around your wife and how many people loved her. I don’t want to say this but I will it is not many at all that actually care for you and that is the truth. Look at the time oh no! Adios I got to go! I hope I have been help and don’t do anything drastic like murdering someone.” The ghost waves and misses the call from the desperate Xing “Who are you???!!!???” Back to the Grave Stone of Xing’s wife where Xing wakes up after being asleep… “Who! I’m back here again thank god it was just a dream.” Sighed a very relieved man in the form of Xing “No it wasn’t a dream!” came a voice from above. Xing kept thinking about his dream or whatever it was as he drove off to still find the people laughing at him by the front gate he gets out and takes a small fag that he has been smoking for nearly forever and burns the man’s work coat with it the bloke and his mates were stunned but didn’t attempt to stop the very angry Xing. Xing stormed back into his car and shouted out that he will get a backlash against Lee Smith and he won’t just stop there he said he will get his hands on those belts and reign as king. He drove for another hour and arrived back in America, in New York Times Square for FAW. He got out of his car and entered the building at the back when he heard the announcer, Chris call out for ‘The Stone.’ Xing jumped up onto the stage which sent The Stone running for obvious reasons the crowd were shocked to see Xing but got into his groove when he made his presence known by shouting out this speech which sent echoes around the stadium “I have been made to believe that me and my wife weren’t loved but that is not true because I have just realised that I have my fans the Xingthings. I know I got off to a bad start at Panic but you shall rest assure that I will defeat him I know because when you have fans like I do the Xingthings Jackasses just come as a treat just like a punching bag and I shall reign victorious.” A roar erupted in the arena of FAW’s very own New York Times Square. He carried on with his speech and said “As for Lee Smith he is a betting man right as you all know so I say why don’t you bet that you this boot get the camera there so he knows what he is going to be get a bite out of you see that, bet this Lee that this boot will get stuck up your royal ass oh yeah you will get a lot of money for I’ll guarantee you that.” Another roar but this time it is with laughter. “Now I am a No Go area and Lee Smith has taken a gamble in wealth and for definite his health by entering the No Go area of Xing and that was certainly the wrong gamble to take. Yes he got extremely lucky at Panic but I had no pump behind me but now that has changed with the Xingthings behind me you don’t stand a chance.” The night came to a very good end for Xing and the Xingthings when two very fit stewardesses were pulling Xing off the stage; he smiled out to the crowd and made and cheeky gesture to them and ‘accidentally’ knocked off the key attached to the trousers of the stewardess. They fall onto the floor the two stewardesses bend down to get the key. The crowd cheer the camera zooms in on the buttocks and Xing staring with a gawky mouth which drool is coming out from it just had to pinch the two buttocks of the ladies he put up his thumb to the crowd and went and pinched it. The crowd roared into laughter which soon turned to hysterics when the two ladies smiled and slapped there Buttocks at him the roar was loud but Xing managed to get over the noise and say “Watch out Lee as I said you got lucky but no one and I mean no one gets out of Xings no go area that easy especially without felling my heat.” Xing got pumped up with the stare facing down the camera and he then raised his arms to make an X.
