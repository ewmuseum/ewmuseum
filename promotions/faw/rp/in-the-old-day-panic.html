In the Old Day (Panic) Profusion Furiously Addictive Wrestling Roleplay #258 Date: 07/02/03 Location: A Book My wrestling life. It wasn’t Joe’s fault – he was more than willing to throw me around and twist my body in ways that nature never intended. I just wasn’t physically able to set up the ring in time to fit in our training. In those four sessions, however, I had begun to learn one thing – how to bump. Or as armchair-wrestling experts around the country would say – “I knew how to land.” Bumping is, without a doubt, the most valuable thing a wrestler can learn. Without it, a career would be awfully short, so the injuries would pile up in a hurry. I guess it’s no secret that the trick in landing is to try to disperse the fall over as great and area of the body as possible. I’ve heard for years that I don’t feel pain, which is ridiculous, but I do believe that I was blessed with a perfect body for bumping – wide back, wide hips, and a wide flat ass. As a result, I don’t look al that good in a tight pair of jeans, but I can absorb unbelievable punishment. I also believe that the human body can adapt to the physical grind we go through. In the same way that a body isn’t made run a marathon, but instead is trained to do it, I believe that my body has been trained to take punishment. Michael Petit, a master in shiatsu massages, swears that my body is the strangest that he’s ever worked. “meek meek” he always says in his French pronunciation of “holy shit, your spine is like that of a crock-o-dile it’s unbelievable. I learnt many a lesson from Michael about how to take bump’s etc, but still to this day all he told me was a big pile of crap. I like to think myself as an intellect and in school that’s exactly what I wasn’t. I flunked almost all the subjects there was to flunk apart from the obvious physical education. Today I remind people of how smart I was in College and the dumb fucks actually believe it. All they have to do is to go back and check my records for the real deal. I ain’t no Albert Einstein but I can cook one hell of a tortilla. My favourite subject in College had to be Physics, for the sheer reason that, Mrs. Moorehead, our Physics teacher was one dish. Each time we walked into her class my heart raced and I couldn’t help myself but drop my pencil on the floor to get a good look at her shaven leg’s. This woman is one of the reasons that I made it where I am in the wrestling industry today because if it wasn’t for her sending me to detention for shouting in class then I would never have discovered the wrestling academy in our school. Joe, who was my mentor in the skills of professional wrestling took me down to Detroit one weekend to his new gym he had just purchased. Me and a few of the other trainees went down there and we worked out butts of with me coming out of that with a bruised thigh and one hell of a head-ache. It was definitely all worth it because Joe came up to me that night and said, “ Hey Kid, tomorrow night is your big break. I am having a wrestling show here in Detroit, nothing big but I want you to top the bill with one of the other trainees, what ya say kid?” I stood there as my knees nearly gave way to the opportunity to step into the ring, in a main event. Without hesitation I replied, “ Damn right, I will.” I said with a gleaming smile as the head-ache went away. “great then kid, it will be a barbed wire match.” Joe said smiling. My heart sunk, a fucken barbed wire match could tear me to shreads, then suddenly the headache came back on again as I also felt a slight sensation go down my spine, it was either my need for a fart or my spine was about to give way. I didn’t wrestle the next night, and I’m damn glad I didn’t because the two kids went out and tore themselves to shreads. I never heard of the two kids again but if I had went into that match, I might not be where I am today in the wrestling industry. My dad always told me not to go into the wrestling business because it was fake but when I showed him my scares and bruises he quickly changed his mind to, don’t go into the wrestling business because it’s too dangerous. He still doesn’t accept that I am a professional wrestler today but whenever he can he comes to watch me and did so when I picked up the FAW Continental Title. I had an older brother, Bill, who was also interested in wrestling until the day when I put a hammerlock hold on his and broke his damn arm, the guy still hasn’t forgiven me for that to this day, and with good reason. My mother was different from the two because she was actually for the wrestling business, she said it was a great opportunity to meet new people and to travel the world, damn was she right. I have now visited places I thought I would never visit such as Japan, Thailand, Australia and Beijing to name a few. She enjoys the odd brawl on television but is not a big fan of the “busting wide open” these day’s. I tried to tell her that the blood was fake but she knew fine rightly that it wasn’t, luckily she has never seen me with a cut in a wrestling match, and otherwise I would have a big mark from a frying pan on my forehead. (I am retelling my life history from college to the present day in my next few promo’s and this is the beginning.)
