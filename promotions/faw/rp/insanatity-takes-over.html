Insanatity takes over Xing Furiously Addictive Wrestling Roleplay #78 Date: 24 november Location: Streets of Montreal “What a wonderful place to film with this beautiful sunlight and the wonderful scenery to look out on to. Perfectly clean side roads with a extremely quiet area to live so let’s film here. Hello mum and happy birthday we are sending you a home made video because we didn’t know what to get you.” “Hi mum was it you that killed my wife or was it you camera boy or you little boy or maybe you who was it?” “Who did what to your wife?” “Someone killed her and it was you how could you kill my wife no know I remember it was you wasn’t it?” “Well first of all can you just calm down and put on some proper clothes now.” “What is wrong with what I am wearing this is my new style Xing‘s way of life and I’m out to find out who killed my wife and I know who it was it was everyone they had a grudge against her so you and you and definitely you hated her.” “Do I know who you are?” “You killed my wife didn’t you why are you sick or something she would never harm you or you but why what a cruel world!” “Chill you are looking awful take a break and take your insanity out on Legendary Lee Smith on December 1st.” “Wait wasn’t it him who did it I don’t know” A pause as Xing knocks out on the streets of Montreal…. Ten years back… “Honey are you going to work soon? Or not your going to be late.” “Calm down it’s like you want me out of the house do you have another woman or something wrapped upstairs or something… Ow that hurts.” “Go to work you are drunk and don’t cause a scene.” “Bye honey I love you miss you lots.” “Yes I love you too honey now be off with your self.” CRASH!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! “No Sara! Watch out for that car.” “Xing or whatever your name is are you ok you passed out on the floor.”
