Born Again (Resistance #2) Profusion Furiously Addictive Wrestling Roleplay #265 Date: 09/02/03 Location: Virginia 'Before me there was many...after...there will be none' [In some countries of the world there is an entity by the name of 'Probable Cause'. It's a very useful creation that is used as a tool to protect freedom from itself. It's a safe guard, a civil blockade if you will, because without it the civil and natural rights of man to have privacy would be violated. For those uneducated, 'Probable Cause' keeps the government or the police force from entering your personal property without 'Reasonable Doubt' that you have without a shadow of doubt been involved in some wrong doing. 'Probable Cause' however is hypocritical. Much like many superstars in the FAW, it tries to do one thing, but ends up serving a different purpose. You see, sure 'Probable Cause' protects you from certain things, but it also can be used as a way to undermind the process of getting a Warrant. With that said, another question arises, who are we trusting to make the decisions on whether something is solid evidence or not? Knowing this, we may apply this to ourselves and insert it into our own personal reservoir of knowledge for later use. How you ask? Simple. When going through the daily grind of life, at each turn in the road, ask yourself... who made the decision for this to happen or that to happen. Who decided to put a stop sign here, a traffic light there, a trash can over there, a State park over here, who decided to give you a raise in salary, who decided to build the house you live in, what group of people is passing the legislations you act under. Or in other words, who is the 'Head Man In Charge'?? Do you know the people who effect your life everyday? No. Why not? It's because of the tangled webs we weave, they leave is trapped in our own gauntlet of responsibilities and worry. How can we break those bondages around our mind, body, and soul? By realizing that 'Probable Cause' is in effect every day.] [Now in FAW, eras come and go. Authority is always challenged. Balance is a rarity. And mayhem is guaranteed. But, some might complain that things are just not as good as they used to be in the days of the Profusion era when we were guaranteed to see someone almost get axed, suplexed through the steel ramp, slammed through flaming tables, etc., Under The Great One, there is 'Probable Cause' that the authority behind his actions isn't his own, but instead the FAW’s. Those of us who're ignorant believe this. Some may say that The Great One now controls his own destiny, those people are fools. But, some recognize the deeper force behind recent events. They've noticed the gravitate pull that has linked Profusion and The Great One together into a match. What does this mean? That's for you to formulate, there is no correct answer to that question, however there is a basis from which all responses must be taken. And that is....., Who can you trust? As a man named Mick Foley once said.. Power is a thing best kept from those who crave it, because the abusers of power are decendants of 'The Prince' who chooses to extort the subjects in this kingdom or in this case FAW. While those who claim power, but do not possess it are even worse, they are desperate men who do desperate things, in desperate times. Take The British Bomber, A man who has returned more times than enough. We cannot trust this man, too indecisive, too deceitful, too unsure of himself, and unable to bring prestige to this company. But, we mustn’t forget The Great One himself, no, we can't do that can we? The man, who believes himself to be on top of the game, filled with plots/plans, un-de-feat-ed, immortal. So much so that he would even go far enough to insult the victims of September 11th, he's no American citizen, that's why. We have 'Probable Cause' to belive a man who could say such things about people who died senselessly, deserves to have his heart torn out and handed back to him on a dinner trey. We have 'Probable Cause' to believe he can never be great enough to lead the people of the FAW to prosperity. WE HAVE 'PROBABLE CAUSE'.. to believe... that Profusion is going to liberate us all from this tyranny some day, that this man will rise up and rectify the mistakes of the former Champion. But, still... Who can you trust? Jeff? No.. He was plotting on Profusion the entire time he was fighting against me.. Imagine what he would do to anyone else that he saw fit to inflict his power upon. The Great One? No. Never. Reasons previously specified prove him to be a man that changes his disposition more then British Bomber changes sides of the plate. Why? Almost every word that he speaks is just a small piece of a life-size puzzle. So many promos that I could name have all been leading upto this one moment, even going 9 almost 10 months back. He's never been completely outclassed by anyway, rarely been beaten cleanly, always had the interest of the survival of the federation on his mind, never lost the desire which festers within his heart and mind. How can you doubt a man that puts in nearly 3/4 months of being screwed and fighting in circles and still puts the survival of his people and the entertainment of his fans first. So many say that the fans don't matter, they don't care, they will turn on you, but this is the ultimate exception. Profusion possess charisma upon charisma, there really is no way to explain it accept through the word 'Immaculate'. The stories, the legends, the information, the prophecies, the testimonies, the blood, the sweat, the desire, the will, the effort, the attitude, 'The Movement.' But, what exactly is 'The Movement' that Profusion has claimed? Is it a group of people? Or is it a cause? Is it a sequence of events? Is it a move? Is it a single person? Is it even in existence? The thing about it is.. There is no way to know until it comes.. or doesn't come.] [So now that our subject 'Probable Cause' has been identified. Our case has been laid down. There still needs to be one more component added into the mix. The Thought Process.... ...all you people.. say that I'm foolish I only do lyrical assaults, do you fools listen to promos or do you just skim through them? See I'm Influenced. The champion you tried to ruin. The same dude the federation gave nothin. I don't just talk into the mic like the others; I give you life lessons and news with a twist of just my point of view. I still continue to be the greatest no matter what the others say, matter of fact, I just heard a promo that tried to f**k up my day. How does The Great One rate wrestlers that the industry relates to, I brought these people prosperity and prestige NOT YOU. Bottom-line is you can't step in my pants, can't walk a mile in my shoes, I help the newbies see there way through this NOT YOU. But, this is not what is on my mind now, so often in recent periods I have seen you mention the stopage of the Thought Process, but this can never happen without paying the ultimate price. You want to end the thought process? Kill me, then I will go away, but this is not what's on my mind. The Thought Process Continues. You see, once again there comes a time when a man has to step back from his life, his ways, his customs, his career, his aura, his accomplishments, his goals, his needs, his wants. He must step far back and just take a look from the outside in, at what he's become. I know for a fact I'm better then all these others around here that are doubting Profusion, they want to know who I am, then just step through the ropes and find out. Man, don't think of me as arrogant however. Look at The Great One for that, he believes he has everything in the palm of his hand and has been controlling what's going on. How obnoxious can you get? People have been beating the h*ll out of him for the past few shows and all he can say is 'You were no longer needed', no it's just a cover-up. A pathetic cover-up. But, that's not what's on my mind The Thought Process continues on still. I don't have time for that. Childish ways have no place here now, I'm destined to move up and I can't stop now.. They can't even comprehend this, how are they going to stop me? It's like I'm moving beyond light speed and the rest are still stuck in neutral. It's a wild concept I'm trying to ponder. It's almost like wearing Michael Jordan shoes, before Michael Jordan wears them. No one would have ever thought it would happen, until it did. No one would have ever thought Profusion would be the first Continental Champion, but he was. Anyone hating me because of what I've done, just know males shouldn't be jealous that's a female trait. What? You mad cuz you's a nobody and I'm a Super-Heavyweight? They ask me, why haven't I done another Reasonable Doubt? I've already proven myself to be the best wrestler out there, I can never again return to those days of proving myself. Reasonable Doubt 2 isn't out of the picture, why, because you're looking at it now. What is the synonym for Reasonable Doubt? Well of course it is Probable Cause. I have Reasonable Doubt that The Great One has a plot for the match, but I also simultaneously have Probable Cause that it will not work.] (((... fade in... ))) 2nd Childhood, 2nd Coming (The Scene begins with a very familiar backdrop seemlessly interpolated into this portion of what is birthing into a classic promo. He sits there. Back Home Again. Profusion leaning on the corner of Camelot Blvd. and Aaron Drive with a cigar in hand. He is leaned over forward just staring at the cracks in the concrete and then looks over at a single rose growing threw the pavement and sprouting in an otherwise asphalt jungle.) 'Yeah, this is Camelot. The place where friends rob friends, strangers kill strangers, trust doesn't easy, fights are common place, and h*ll.. Existence is a constant struggle. No one knows what happens when we die, is it like some say with pearly gates and shining spiral staircases made of crystal? Or is it like others suggest, just blackness, nothingness, emptiness, open void, the absence of matter? I have Reasonable Doubt to believe that it is neither hither nor yonder. There is no way to know until the end finally commons whether suddenly or expectedly. Those who have been revived from NEAR death may have given accounts, but these are people talking off of brain cells which were possibly inactive and rotting for a matter of seconds or minutes. But, seriously, what is the point of this all? Well. If we were each given the ultimate gift, it would be the ability to turn back the hands of time just once. A 2nd Childhood of sorts. The chance to re-live the tender years of exploration and ninja turtles. The chance to re-kindle the days of having no worries in the world except eating, sleeping, and watching TV. The chance to re-invent oneself in the past with knowledge from the future, the possibilities would be endless! However there is one man who will be fortunate enough to have this opportunity. His name is Profusion. In some religions, they believe that you are born into a world of sin and upon confessing these sins and committing ones soul to the belief you are 'born again'. Applying this to FAW it is as newcomers we are born into a world of opportunities that are not meant for you to take. A world where the weak are crushed. A world where titles don't come easy. A world where much like my 'hood.. Survival was a daily struggle. However, upon reaching the peek of achievement and attaining the FAW Heavyweight Championship, you are born again as a person and a wrestler.. never to be the same again. It changes the way you think, changes the way you see things, changes the way you DO things, changes your goals and priorities, it eats at you. As my cliché' goes, it's like a drug that gets into your bloodstream. You become dependant. And will stop at nothing to get it again and hold it for as long as possible.'
