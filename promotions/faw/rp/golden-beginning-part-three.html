Golden Beginning Part Three 'Golden Nine' Jeff Harris Furiously Addictive Wrestling Roleplay #151 Date: 17/01/2003 Location: Secret Volcano Layer, Hawaii January 2003 (Current) :.:Over a month now, over a month and I have seen no action… The only action I saw was last month when I attacked Adam Masterson, when I beat him for the first time ever in my career, a former friend and maybe now a new enemy. The name Jeff Harris does not mean coward, to me it means a fearless warrior, someone who can overcome incredible odds. FAW will be a new challenge even though I have been in this place, this insane asylum before but now the talent has changed. I have a match at Panic on Sunday a match that will remind me of a match at the Warehouse in the first of two matches as I go against 4 other superstars, 4 people who I have never seen in battle before. I don’t know how it will turn out, in fact… I do, it will be me showing them how big of a power shift can happen over night. My Golden Nine will be at it’s fullest, I know they want to look at it, they too will be obsessed with it’s power! MWAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHA! The Golden Era hath begun… :.: .::Jeff Harris is sat behind his desk, looking calmly at the television screen. His phone rings, Jeff answers it::. “Golden Nine” Jeffrey James Harrison: Hello? Jean Deas: Hey yall! I have a surprise for you. “Golden Nine” Jeffrey James Harrison: Oh nothing can be better than the “twister” you gave me last night. Jean Deas: Well it’s even better than that! .::Jeff falls off of his chair in excitement::. “Golden Nine” Jeffrey James Harrison: BETTER?! Jean Deas: You know that Golden Volcano you wanted? “Golden Nine” Jeffrey James Harrison: Holy shit! You finished it!? Jean Deas: Yaw! Piggly Wiggly do pay well you know. Well you should come and see it! .::The scene automatically switches to the Golden Volcano Layer in Hawaii. With palm trees surrounding the Volcano and the Sun shining brightly in the sky reflecting against the light blue sky, the volcano is solid gold and inside the core is molten golden lava. The scene switches inside where Jeff Harris is seen walking through the door leading into the main command area. A big screen is on the far golden wall. Jeff Harris clicks his fingers and Big Balls by AC DC starts up, Jeff Harris smiles as the Jean-Bots and the Mini-Pete’s at the controls. Jeff Harris sits down in a bright golden chair marked “Golden Nine”, he looks around and smiles. Bryan Deas, Jean Deas and Pete Harris enter. Soon after Jeff-Droid enters::. “Golden Nine” Jeffrey James Harrison: Now everything is complete and ready to go, excellent. First order of business… Write a message to Xing, Violator, Gabriel Hunter, Atticus Pulsar and Slyphon. Pete Harris: HA HA! “Golden Nine” Jeffrey James Harrison: I LOVE IT! Jean Deas: The message is? “Golden Nine” Jeffrey James Harrison: Tell then I’m ready to attack and their health benefits are about to expire, but three X’s and sign it by Golden Nine. Jean Deas: Okay done. “Golden Nine” Jeffrey James Harrison: Now, any word of the happenings of the Secret Service? Pete Harris: Well they have found out that you aren’t missing and in fact you are planning to take over the world. “Golden Nine” Jeffrey James Harrison: Good that means they’ll send someone after me then I shall hurt them beyond belief. Then I shall show him my nine making him die instantly. Pete Harris: HA HA! I LOVE IT! “Golden Nine” Jeffrey James Harrison: Me too old friend, me too. Anyway, this coming Sunday will mark the era of Gold. The era of Gold will be a horrifying experience for the people of FAW and the fans, as they will see more gold than ever before! Can you dig? Pete Harris: Yes I certainly can. “Golden Nine” Jeffrey James Harrison: Good, now all we need to do is wait until Sunday, then I kick ass. MWAHAHAHAHAA… MWAHAHAHAHAHAHA… Pete Harris: MWAHAHAHAHAHA Everyone In The Room: MWAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHA .::The scene fades with the whole room laughing evilly except Bryan Deas who is standing there wondering what everyone is laughing about::.
