Déjà vu

Déjà vu 'Legendary' Lee Smith Furiously Addictive Wrestling Roleplay #105 Date: 18/12/02 Location: L.A.X. He had been here before. Overcome with obsession once again. It was approximately one year ago to the day. Evil Intentions 2001...December 2001...Winter 2001. That winter had been one to remember. Lee had interfered in the main event of Evil Intentions, and created many long standing rivalries in the meantime…he never got over what happened in those 24 hours…. They used to adore him. Legendary Lee Smith..co-owner of Furiously Addictive Wrestling. And then there was his equally adored partner - Tom The Bomb. That night had changed everything. There was no way back… 12 Months Ago…. Lee sprinted out and climbed the cell. His heart thumped as the crowd cheered his name. What was he doing here? He must have forgotten to mention it, nothing to worry about. Tom The Bomb stood tall with a menacing glint in his eye as he stood next to The British Bomber. Tom then joined Lee as Bomber looked incredibly frightened, unusual for a man of his mental strength. Lee wore a black sleeveless shirt, embroidered with the FAW name. Tom wore a tuxedo and a shirt underneath. He quickly got rid of his tuxedo and threw it into the crowd - who were by then shouting the trademark “Let The FAWce Be with You!”. Tom was wearing a similar shirt to Lee as they stood face to face with their nemesis, XCW Owner and long term FAW member The British Bomber. Tom had picked up a chair…..the chair that changed everything. The crowd had switched chants, chanting his name. He turned to The British Bomber as almost in unison with the crowds chants he ran towards him with the chair in the air. He prepared for impact….. “What The Hell…?” He remembered muttering those three words as Tom held the chair high. Bomber was wearing an xCw shirt, the only one in the 60,000 sellout arena. Tom turned towards Lee. Had he noticed something about TBB? Perhaps there was someone behindLee, and Tom was protecting him by attacking the person behind him. Suddenly the slow motion effect seemed to stopped. BBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAANNNNNNNNNG! The chair struck Lee as the crowd gasped and shrieked. Lee fell to the floor in an instant, his livelihood waning with it. Lees eyes watered as he stared at Tom, who grinned. It all made sense now. Well, on second thoughts….it made no sense at all…. Tom was getting him up…..maybe it was an accident? No, he meant it all right. TTB dropped the chair and spread his arms around his chest. He pulled them away from each other as his FAW shirt ripped into two. Below Toms FAW shirt….just above Lees face…was an…. XCW Shirt. The fans didn’t boo any longer…they were silent, struggling to take in the extent of Toms deceit. Tom turned to The British Bomber and shook his hand as the cameras flashed around the arena. They hugged like old buddies. Lee was enraged. He had never felt like this before. He climbed to his feet and stretched for the chair. He took it by both legs and ran towards Tom. Tom and Bomber separated at the last possibly mini-second and Tom nailed the Tom-Bomb on Lee. He then left the rest to The British Bomber, who continued this heartless assault. The two joined hands as Lee was on the edge of the cell. They nailed a double clothesline which knocked Lee off his feet. He flew 20feet from the cell and through the announcers table below. He then remembered hearing the bell ringing and British Bomber being announced as the New FAW World Champion. No more Mr Nice Guy. “Master Smith, Sir..sir, wake up, we have arrived at the airport….” the chauffeur said, as Lees eyes separated. He yawned and stretched his arms. The chauffeur opened the doors and he got out. They entered the airport together as autograph hunters distanced themselves, aware of Lees violent mood swings. They were thousands of people running across Lees path. His head still throbbed. He shrieked and they parted like Moses parting the red sea. Lee wasn’t used to all this, he usually used his private jet for flight, but that was having technical difficulties. One Hour Later “Could the passengers departing for Houston please come to Terminal 13.” Lee got up with his chauffeur carrying his hand luggage. Number 13. Lucky for some. As far as Lee was concerned luck wasn’t a factor. He was ready to unleash his anger.
