Smells Like Team Spirit

07-12-2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Smells Like Team Spirit XiN Furiously Addictive Wrestling Roleplay #94 Date: 07-12-2002 Location: FAW Arena Prologue The draw has been made, the matches announced, FAW’s New Era is truly underway. But the wild card is in place, Lazlo Hawk, trainee of Spider is placed within Group 1, the likes of Loco Creed and Spawn are to be with Hawk. Lazlo though isn’t interested in his match, he’s just after a little bit of team spirit… The Story The scene is backstage, a stage is setup in a large open area, all of Group A from the draw is seated including the Group Leader but minus Lazlo Hawk, Loco Creed and Ken Ruslan, they are all facing the stage looking confused. “Where is that punk wannabe’?” Roberts exclaims. The second the words leave his mouth “Smells like Teen Spirit” by Nirvana hits and Lazlo jumps out playing Kurt’s part on the guitar, he begins singing his own words to the song: “Load up on fun and sing to friends, Its fun to laugh (at enemies) and to hurt them, We’ll hurt other groups, we’re self assured, Oh yeah, Great One’s a dirty turd! Loco, Loco, Loco, Spawn too! Roberts, Roberts, Roberts, Foamy! Ruslan, Ruslan, Ruslan, One More! Lazlo, Lazlo, Lazlo… With a big Trout, we will slap them, They are arse now, we will kick them, We are brilliance, and so famous! Here we are now, we wear trainers, A fat cow, that’s Bomber, a lazy cunt, that’s Vigilante! Yeah! We are much better, than the rest, Perhaps that’s because we’re the best, Xing bites ass, Shifty is a lass, They are retards until the end, Loco, Loco, Loco, Spawn too! Roberts, Roberts, Roberts, Foamy! Ruslan, Ruslan, Ruslan, One More! Lazlo, Lazlo, Lazlo… With a big Trout, we will slap them, They are arse now, we will kick them, We are brilliance, and so famous! Here we are now, we wear trainers, A whore lady, that’s Xander, a bondage bitch, that’s Saba… Tash! (In place of yeah) Yusuki is such a waste, And ‘The Law’ smells so vile, But worse still, Lee Smith sucks ass, And all the rest, are filthy swine! Loco, Loco, Loco, Spawn too! Roberts, Roberts, Roberts, Foamy! Ruslan, Ruslan, Ruslan, One More! Lazlo, Lazlo, Lazlo… With a big Trout, we will slap them, They are arse now, we will kick them, We are brilliance, and so famous! Here we are now, we wear trainers, A loser, that’s Neo, a fuckwit, that’s Jeff Harris… They’re in denial (x9)” Lazlo finishes with the infamous fade off, the camera turns to his fellow group members who all look stunned. “What the FUCK was that?!” Spawn shouts, “We’re meant to be a team but how can I team with THAT?!” “Calm down big guy,” Roberts says. “Hey yeah big guy, just because you’re bigger than me,” Lazlo complains, “I was trying to make you laugh and unite!” A small laugh comes from the other group members; the others turn around to Foamy. “Well it was pretty good,” Foamy explains. “Pretty good?!” Lazlo exclaims while jumping off the stage, “It damn rocked! Especially seeing as it was freestyle!” Lazlo jumps around strumming the guitar and moshing. They all look at him like he is a maniac; Spawn walks over and grabs Lazlo by the throat. “You take this seriously or I’ll rip you apart little man,” Spawn smirks. Lazlo struggles to get down, Spawn lets go, Lazlo holds his throat. “Ach, whatever, you can’t pick on me just because of my size,” Lazlo complains, “You always did that to Vinny!” “Who is Vinny?” Spawn ponders. “Spider!” Lazlo shouts. “Ah yes that little runt,” Spawn chuckles. “Runt? I can still kick your ass Spawn, never mind if you beat me for that World title, you would’ve lost to me if you had had the fortnight I’d had,” a voice proclaims. The camera spins round to reveal the one and only Spider, Lazlo is smiling like a Cheshire cat as Spawn looks at Spider with hatred. “Here to collect your over excited puppy?” Spawn mocks. “HEY!” Lazlo shouts. “Forget him,” Spider explains, “Spawn I’m here because I want to see my boy here win his first match and win YOUR team a point, here is a thought, how about instead of complaining at him you actually talk to him?” “Oh so you’re the calm one now?” Spawn laughs. “Spawn,” Spider laughs, “you never learn do you? I retired because I was too calm, I was tame and yet I could still kick your ass.” “Strange I don’t remember you ever beating me!” Spawn smiles. “Yeah,” Spider confirms, “I never beat you, but every time I faced you I had had more important things to deal with, an 8 Man Hell In the Cell TLC for example, or a 24 hour defence, a 4 on 1 gauntlet aswell, every time I was tired out, but Lazlo here will finish the job unless you start treating him with some respect!” “Hey wait!” Lazlo intervenes, “You expect me to beat HIM?!” “Of course,” Spider tutors, “If you do not believe you can do it you never will.” “Spider, Spider, Spider…” Spawn triumphs, “Are you a philosopher now? Don’t answer, please, I wouldn’t want my time wasted, but you know… I’ve got a piece of philosophy for you and a little tune for Lazlo…” Spawn walks to the door, as he walks out he shouts. “Life is a bitch, and then you retired, so I’m gonna’ hurt him, and make you a liar…” Spawn laughs as he leaves. “Urgh, that idiot,” Spider says with disgust, “I don’t know who he thinks he is but he’d better learn he’s gotta’ co-operate.” Spider shakes his head, Jason Roberts walks over to him and smiles, he extends his hand. “Nice to see you around again,” Roberts beams. “Whatever,” Spider replies half-heartedly. Spider denies a handshake and walks to the stage, he sits down. Foamy slowly stands and whispers to Roberts, they both nod and walk out leaving just Spider and Lazlo. “What IS Spawn’s problem?” Lazlo inquires. “Never you mind that, you’ve got a match on Sunday,” Spider says trying to change the subject. “Yeah, yeah I know,” Lazlo bleeds, “Concentrate on the future not the past, that’s where I’m gonna’ be the rest of my life…” “You’re learning the way,” Spider smiles, “I learnt it a little too late, I can never wrestle again because I always went in head first, small stature guys like us can’t do that, you need to think. Now who are you against?” “Umm…” Lazlo ponders, “Sabatash…” “What you think to him?” Spider questions his pupil. “He’s a well refined wrestler who has experience on his side,” Lazlo notes, “however his weakness comes from the fact he has not wrestled for a while whereas I have been finishing Indy cards recently” “Good, good,” Spider replies, “So who are the other two?” “Rzarector and some whore named Officer Lawrence McGruff,” Lazlo explains, “I don’t think I need to worry much about McGruff, but Rzarector seems to somehow know my ability, it’s like he’s picked me out from this match against the other two.” “That’s because he knows talent,” Spider replies, “Don’t worry you can beat him hands down, all you need to think about is the end of this half of your journey, holding that World title, being kind of the World like I was for a fortnight… just make sure no-one screws you out of it though!” “I know, I know,” Lazlo moans, “Don’t live on faith alone…” “You’re learning, evidently bored though so I’ll go and finish what I was up to,” Spider sighs. “What are you doing?” Lazlo questions. “Signing papers so I can be your new manager buoy! See you at ringside,” Spider laughs as he walks out. Lazlo is left looking a little lost for words as his mentor walks out. He grabs his guitar and puts the strap over his head, he sits on the stage playing a few notes and talking to himself. 'Why does nobody seem to trust I can do it myself,' Lazlo ponders, 'Everybody thinks I need help, I got here without help, I still don't need it, damn I need a spliff...' Lazlo grabs his bag and slings it over his shoulder, his guitar over one shoulder and bag over the other he walks out. 'I'll show Spawn, Spider... I'll show them all...' The scene fades with Hawk muttering to himself still. Epilogue So Spider is back as Lazlo’s manager, but what differences will this make to “Rock Superstar”? We’ve heard his analysis of his opponents but it seems Spider wants to hype his ‘buoy’, what has he got planned? Wait until Promo 2 for Panic 8th December from Lazlo Hawk and perhaps a little bit of Spider…
