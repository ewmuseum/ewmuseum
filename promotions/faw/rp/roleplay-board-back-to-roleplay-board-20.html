J.J Dynasty — FAW Roleplay, August 2002

August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Furiously Addictive Wrestling Roleplay Board Roleplay Board | Back to Roleplay Board J.J Dynasty Entitled: A new outlook on life (Cyrus Skar read) Written: 8/7/02 Location: Hotel Game Room It’s the middle of the afternoon on August 7, 2002 and many of the FAW’s finest have chosen to relax in the plush comforts of the hotel provided to them by FAW’s front office. Some wrestlers, such as Etan Cross, Baz and Kryptonite are making their way out of the hotel to check out the nightlife of the town that they are in. Others, such as JJ Dynasty, have chosen to stay in tonight and relax at the pool table. The FAW camera zooms in towards the poolroom, where JJ Dynasty and James Harris are in the midst of what looks to be a very one-sided game of 8-ball. Harris: I hate pool, I hate 8-ball, but more importantly than that, I hate you. Dynasty: Aww, come on Jimmy, just because I have beaten you 8 out of 9 times today does not mean that you are not a good pool player. You just are not as good as me. Harris: I was the fourth ranked in all of New York City back in high school when it came to pool. Dynasty: I was eleventh… Harris: See I am better than you… Dynasty: …in the country… Harris: Oh… Dynasty (laughing): Don’t take it so personally man, it’s just a game. You have to learn to take life less serious all the time. Harris: You should talk. Dynasty: What’s that supposed to mean? Harris: Look, everybody knows how pissed you are at Cyrus Skar for attacking you last week. Everybody also knows how pissed you are that your flight was cancelled and you had to miss Distortion last week. It seems to me that I am not the only one who takes life a little to serious a little to often. Dynasty: Touché…Well, wouldn’t you be pissed if after two weeks of waiting, you finally got to debut on screen in the greatest federation ever set up? Then, in the midst of your on-camera debut you get attacked by a no good, lousy, jackass and he makes you look like a fool by leaving you bleeding on the floor in front of millions of people? Harris: Well yeah, if that had happened to me, I would want to kick that guy’s ass too. But hey, don’t worry, you will get your chance soon enough and when that chance comes, I am sure that Cyrus Skar will wish he had never messed with the Master of Destiny, J.J. Dynasty. Dynasty: You are damn right he will be sorry. All I wanted to do when I came to FAW was to work hard, wrestle well and one day make my way to the top of this federation and reign as its World Champion. I knew that I would have to pay my dues and all, but come on, since when did those “dues” include getting your ass kicked in a locker room backstage? Cyrus Skar stepped over the line last week and somebody has to make him pay. Now I want that person to be me, more than anything else in the world, I want that person to be me. However, I do not mind waiting, after all I am a very patient man…to a point. I heard what Cyrus Skar had to say regarding me a few days ago and quite frankly he is partially right. I did not come into this federation to go after your title because I knew I was not mentally ready to be a champion. I knew that it could take weeks or even months before I was in the right frame of mind and with the right amount of experience behind me to go after a title, much less one so highly regarded as your European…or should we say United States Championship. Look at some of the names that have held the European/United States Championship. JLC, the current World Champion the British Bomber, Skipster and Cyrus Skar? Hmm, which of these names does not belong, which of those four names have done nothing to deserve a reign as champion, which of those names are nowhere near the level that is needed of a successful wrestler. Which of those names does not belong in the FAW Federation? Harris: Umm, Cyrus Skar? Dynasty: Correct my friend, Cyrus Skar is the boy among men, he is the name in that list that is not worthy of being on that list. He is the one who I have decided must be taken out of FAW as soon as possible or risk the chance of him causing this federation to shut down just like he was the reason why the CCW shut down. You did not know that did you? You did not know that it was because of him, because of his ego, his attitude and his vacuum of charisma that the CCW had to close its doors. Well, despite what the reports say of how the CCW was in financial peril, those of us who were in the federation knew that it was Cyrus who was to blame for our misfortune. He has been the reason behind why I was unemployed and just when I found a federation with enough of a reputation and enough of a star-studded roster to complement me, here is Cyrus Skar, once again trying to ruin my chances of success. Well, he can try to stop me, he can try to slow my success, and in fact I hope he does try. I want to finally have a reason for kicking his ass, for breaking his spirits and for leaving him a bloody, broken, shell of a man in some dark alleyway next to an arena. A symbol of all that is wrong with the world today, which is what Cyrus Skar is. I have a new outlook in life, and I have Cyrus to thank for it. I realize now that our world must be rid of the those who try to demean the rest of us, those people who try to hurt the rest of us and that minority of people who try to take from the majority what is rightfully someone else’s. Cyrus Skar is in that minority and I am in that majority and it is my duty to do what I can to show him and the rest of the world that what he is trying to do to me does not matter. In the end all will learn that you cannot change your destiny and you sure as hell cannot beat Dynasty. Harris is awestruck at the words of Dynasty who slowly racks the pool balls up again and slowly breaks them to begin the next match as the FAW slowly fade to black and go to a commercial break.
