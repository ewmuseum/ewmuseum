Knock out Xing Furiously Addictive Wrestling Roleplay #218 Date: 23rd January Location: School It is a reasonably warm day after school that is why the children walk home without there coats Xing stands outside waiting while watching for all the kids to leave the building Xing still stares into the classroom owned by the old school bully teacher who doesn’t notice him at the window still doing his computer work and marking recently done tests after having a free teaching period Xing rubs his chest feeling for something he feels it and smiles very sickly when he realises that students may have detentions being an extremely strict school and all so Xing asks a worker at the school to give the old school bully teacher a Cd player with headphones including a note reading “Turn it up real loud so you wont know what hit you.” The worker walks into the classroom and gives the gift to the teacher without saying it was from Xing luckily for him his was beginning to start when the teacher cranked up the volume of the tape and started bobbing to the music Xing smiles even more Xing then makes a move into the school turning on the fire alarm now this is where it gets interesting everyone in the school hears the alarm and walks out of the building Xing hides at the back of the school the worker made a register for everyone that was in the school at the time in case of a situation like this one. The old school bully teacher un-aware of the alarm still bobs away to the music in his class. Xing makes his move into the school still the teacher does not know what is happening Xing makes his way outside the door feeling very sick when the worker outside realises someone is missing from the list the teacher Xing walks into the classroom taking out the thing from his chest which is a crowbar still the teacher does not know what is happening Xing strikes the man over the head viciously over the head three times leaving it like a pig after being eaten. Xing heres the worker come in the school calling out “Teacher!” she makes her way into the classroom and screams Xing is nowhere to be seen for the worker but for us he is behind the door she walks in and forgets about the incident and answers the ringing phone Xing then tries to escape but he makes a noise that catches the workers attention she screams only to be hit across the head also three times. The people outside do not know what is going on so after waiting for twenty minutes and then leave the premises. Meanwhile back in the classroom Xing takes hold of the old school bully teacher’s computer camera and starts rolling the film he says; “Now we all know that I am going to win the rumble and there is nothing you ass clowns can do about it but some how you will ruin my child hood dream now that I am rich I will become the rumble winner and lift my honours like I did with my other Winnings which are in the bin. Now you could say that is ungrateful but wining comes easy for me a king, Xing I bet you all the money in this world that I will come out victorious this rumble whether it be wining the rumble or taking the piss out your favourites Harris and Violator thank you and goodbye.” Xing hacks onto every site on the net that is popular including most porn sites and puts on his face and what he said on each and every popular site he had hacked into.
