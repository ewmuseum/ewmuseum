Saviour Xing Furiously Addictive Wrestling Roleplay #144 Date: 14.01.03 Location: His House Xing wakes up late with a hangover holding his head in pain after having an extremely late night out in the pubs and streets. Xing leans over to turn the T.V. on he sees his old school’s wrestling team, Xing grunts out loud which only gets louder when he sees his old school teacher who is in charge of the team that have just made the finals in Canada. The teacher begins to talk and says, “We are doing so well in our Country and we need all the help we can get.”- Before the man could finish what he was going to say Xing in disgust turns off the T.V. and quietly to himself with a stern voice said, “Yeah well you wont get any help from me that’s for sure!” Xing then starts to get up and worryingly starts looking for his last night unfinished beer Xing holds his head feeling very sick. He could not find it any where in the house he scratches his head as he thinks to himself he then goes down in to his pocket to pull out his mobile and then a mobile number behind it he thought to call the place because he was offered a free drink from there after saving the mans pub/pool bar. Xing walks out his pre-rented flat because his house is having work done on it, and starts dialling the numbers from the piece of paper the phones rings about 7 times before Xing decides to give and is about to end the call when he gets a response from the man Xing then says in happiness, “Yes you picked up look it’s Xing I needed a drink and I remembered your place err I was wondering is that free drink still available” There is a pause for the phone call then Xing then asks “Are you there?” He then gets an almost silent response from the man who says, “Sorry to keep you waiting those guys are back in the pub quickly can you help me out and you can get two free drinks but you better hurry please!” Xing ends the calls and puts the phone into his pocket and takes out his car keys and opens his car door and then drives off and he gets to the pub/pool bar as soon as he could he makes it to find the four men looking a bit more confident than last time they met. The men didn’t leave this time and stood to fight against Xing so Xing showing off his Hardcore skills takes a cue stick and whacks it over the head of the first man that approaches the cue snaps in two Xing takes one half of it and rams it into the down low area of the next man that tried to attack he took the same man and chucked him over the top of the pool table a bit of Rumble skills shown there. The third man kicked down low and was put into a power bomb through the table and without Xing knowing the lead man took a glass and whacked it over Xings head but that blow did not affect him Xing turns round and smiles at him and took hold of the man and put him into a ferocious bear hug the man screamed in pain and agony Xing dropped the man and untied the bar man walking over the path of destruction that he had left Xing then calls the police man and they arrive shortly after the men then got put away for the night. A cop then holds back Xing and asks Xing “Xing would you mine being in our protection agency of undercover body guard cops? I know you have experience in the bodyguard depot. So what do you say?” Xing walks past the man like he didn’t say anything then the cop says, “We have your no. so I’ll call you!” Xing phone then rings much to the joy to Xing to get the cop off of his back he answers it as the cop walks off “Hi! Xing I don’t if you saw me on T.V. this morning but I am your old high school teacher if you didn’t I was wondering you can be that person that can help our team out seen as we are in the finals” Xing puts his hands on the phone and looks up- Flashback Xing is a child in his home looking scared when the phone rings he knows who it was it was his teacher his mum had answered the phone the teacher said to Xings mother, “ I am your sons high school teacher can you help me out I need your son here at school he has been very naughty and I think”- The phone was quickly put down and Xing was pulled into the car by his ear this was a regular routine for Xing and the same thing happened he was in his classroom getting whipped several times to his teacher and mothers enjoyment.- Nowadays The teacher is still on the phone Xing puts his ear to the and hears “Can you please come to the school soon if you can hear this.” Xing puts the phone down.
