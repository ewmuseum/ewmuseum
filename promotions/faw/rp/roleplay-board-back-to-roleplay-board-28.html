Kryptonite — FAW Roleplay, August 2002

August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Furiously Addictive Wrestling Roleplay Board Roleplay Board | Back to Roleplay Board Kryptonite Entitled: Choices....Desicions Written: 8-10-02 Location: Unknown :::Visions of madness strike a large screen. The projector emits a steady hum and images are cast onto the once blank screen. Jews from concentration camps march single file at the whim of German soldiers. Pushing little children with their fully automatics, they like to push the weak around. The pain scratched on their faces are from the talons of memories that still burn their way in every skull, every hollow bone. They are thrown in ovens by the dozens, slowly burning to cinder. The scene moves to a man blindfolded in front of a firing squad. He is gunned down after being denied his last wish, dying before even being given the justice of hitting the ground. Blood flies and bullets pierce his body, his heart no longer pumping blood to his body, as it has exploded. In the next, a fourteen-year-old girl is raped by a gun-wielding man. Her hair lies in different blonde patches. Where once was virginal beauty, there is now pain and suffering as the man squeezes her breasts, thrusting into her bloody body and forever impurifying her. He is so rough that his thrusting is causing her to bleed badly. His dark hair falls upon her, drenched with sweat, until finally he finishes, abusing the girl like a piece of meat before giving her the dignity of killing her and instead leaves her in that dark, wet alley nude and dying. A needle is then shown going into a kid's arm. His buddy told him shooting heroin was the cool thing to do. But neither one of them have the experience to know the bump he took was far too large. The boy, no older than thirteen, turns sheet white and convulses on the floor. His friend does not know what to do. He picks up the phone to call 911 but only drops it when his friend completely stops moving as he dies. Another life taken by the expense of trying something he had never tried, thinking things he had not previously thought. Only another young boy in a coffin. He can see the tears fall from his mother's eyes as the herd are dressed in black, weeping for him. Then the ultimate. Planes crash into the Twin Towes in New York City. It is repeated many times, even sometimes in slow motion, with fake piped in cries of burning people and dying sheep. Over and over. Slow motion. The dust evry slowly bursts from the buildings as they are hit and implode within themselves. Debris falls everywhere. Individual lives are shown passing in front of his very eyes, those emotionally attached to husbands, wives, sons, daughters, mothers and fathers, nieces and nephews. Each had something to live for, each dying on impact. This is what the world is coming to. Kryptonite himself is then shown in various forms of pain throughout his Wrestling years and even as a youth. He takes a fall from the balcony. Then from his childhood, watching his father's fists go rapid fire on his mother's face. She calls for him, tells him to go back to his room. But the damage is done. The scarring will happen long after he is cut. He feels himself press the knife against his wrist, only a movement away from escaping this loathing. A final bullet is put into a man's head and Kryptonite almost dies from the very heartache of it. A chapter closes. He must escape this loathing. This pain. Kryptonite looks up to the camera with a look of disspear on his face, like nothing matters::: Distortion is just a heart beat away, and Joe La Fontaine has already lost all hope in the match. In fact, he lost hope the minute he signed for this match-up. Too put it in simple retarded form for those fans of the FAW...Joe has already lost the match, even before the match begins to start. All his faith, all his power, all his mental practice will go down in shambles. I love it how people come out here and ramble on about me..as if I ramble on about them. Fontaine the misunderstood prophet, the man that displays a double standard. A man who this week has made light of one Kryptonite. He decides to prove his intelligential advantage as if knowing about Roman history, Biblical terms, and being cool will actually benefit him someway in the this match. He believes that just because he is able to portray me as some uncivilized baboon when it comes to biblical history, Roman history, or even building a house of cards that he has this match won. Fontaine why do you seem so cocky? Why do you seem so confident? Why do you believe that all you have to do is show up Sunday Night and you will have the shattered one lay down for you? You must be dreaming. You must be wanting to think the shattered one is an unworthy opponent. You must be wishing that he indeed is. The only problem with wishing Fontaine is most of the times they never come true. Fontaine, you are like the rest of the nXn, you too have a house of cards glued out of lies. Lies that started when you joined with the moniker “The Anit-Hero“. Why do you want to call yourself the best? Is it you are overconfident? Is it you are ego-driven? Is it you are really have a message from God or one of the twelve Apostles? I think not. Joe, why do you remain quiet this week? I thought you being a man of your word you would want to express your message to the world. What better way through the media venue known as television? Your silence proves my point exactly. Your silence shows you are no contender. You are just another person personifying a front to get inside your opponents mind.. You think you have the match won. You think you have it all wrapped up. You started thinking about what you are going to do after the match then BAM!! I snatch your DREAM of winning this match.. So you look to rebound after that match by beating Kryptonite do you? Trust me you can look and look but you will never find that ever happening. I am on a war path straight to Bomber. You just happen to be standing in front of him. Thus you will be either run over, around, or straight through. Either way you will not stop Kryptonite's mission! Everything that would be, could be a lie. This false barrier that protects usfrom what is supposedly true hate. Love. Like manipulating hate before it, we exploit love and what it means to us. The projector boots up again with greater difficulty and Fontaine sees himself standing behind the reel, watching that familiar ghostlike glow on the wall before him. Hate is obvious but love is not so easily seen. It is as if the shroud over Planet Earth only exists as love masking hate, not hate masking love. Because we are all pessimistic by choice, not by force. Joe awaits this chance. He knows the answers but he wants to feel what love is again. What it once was. He did not want to see hearts or purple kittens. He would close eyes not his to the images of valentine's day and wedding rings. Wedding ring. He sees the wedding ring on a woman's finger as well as the one of his own. This is what is deemed love. He feels like a robot for 'deeming' that so. In truth, it was love. She rescued him from the world of robots, gave him something he could never have without her. He had the intelligence, yeah. He had the technical prowess and athletic ability. He had good looks and a career. But he never cared for anyone else right Fontaine? Not until he had his worst nightmare handed to him. The light of his life, his reason for living, his legacy. :::In a flashback, Kryptonite is shown as about an eight year old boy, sitting on his mother's lap while she read him a story. The story isn't purtinent but the love and adoration in her blue eyes is. They would bring tears to his own, surely, if he knew where they were. He would one day have a child and take for granted what he had. And he could not do that any longer. He knew these could be the only two scenes he saw as love, for he never thought he recognized it from anything else. But that did not sum up everything. The scene cuts to Kryptonite drinking beers in happier times. In a perhaps happier environment of the lifetime. Joking about the ungodly gut of their boss. Joking about Jobber's adventures and the downfall. And then he and Joe La Fontaine warming up for a match, mutual respect polluting the atmosphere, right after the birth of the original NXN. How they did not know of each others' past but thought so much of each other. There is no balcony here in love. There are no superkicks from friends, no knives in the backs from best pals. There is only companionship, respect and admiration. There is only the feeling of security--something Kryptonite has learned for a LONG time--not the feeling of being closed in and claustrophobic. Security and comfort that he has a career that will support his family, and people who do love him even if he does vehemently deny them doing so. He will deny it all, turn everything down to fashion the character he has made for himself. Until he realizes the shroud of love encompassing hate resembles the shroud covering all things human on his body, he will not know the true toll he himself has taken over the years. And he will not know love will be there to grace him with her hand once again in his darkest moments::: Come Sunday Night Fontaine, this will be no easy match for you. This will be no chess match in the center of the ring. Kryptonite is a man that is now only filled with hate and loathing that were created when dreams, glory, and pride left. Kryptonite though is intelligent common sense wise, street wise, and now emotion wise through the state of being he is right now. Fontaine you claim that I am a jobber, go ahead claim that, but after the end of the night that is all they will be…..claims. You can claim all you want. You can impress us with your vast knowledge of history. Yet you can’t impress us with empty promises. Yes Joe, you promise that I will not pose any type of threat to you is just that an empty promise. You are overconfident Fontaine, something that leads to the downfall of many men. You will be no exception. You will fall. The shattered one will get passed you. You may think you are a fault in the path of Kryptonite. Joe you are just a mere speed bump. You may think you have lost just a little confidence in your lin your last match that you had. Yet Sunday Night you will know that you have a shattered heart after I have disposed of you accordingly… ' :::Kryptonite puts his hands in his pockets walking onward, he look sat what could have been. Kryptonite puts his head down in disgrace.. He stops and sits on a bench. You can see that his hands are over his face tho you can't tell his state of mind::: So we look at the week ahead for Kryptonite. The week in which I was suppose to face two roadblocks now I must only face one. Does this mean the journey will be easier. No, the journey will never be easy. The journey must be difficult. If it is not then it isn’t really a journey is it? Fontaine comes in with a ego inflated head, though he will depart with a deflated head and shattered pieces of his life. You scoff Joe? You may not scoff come this Sunday. Because I am focused, you have wonder into his focus thus you must suffer the consequences of it. Sunday will be the night in which the whole world and FAW bare witness to the violator. They will see he is a man on a mission. He knows there will be bumps along the way. Though the bumps will not take him off course. He will strive, he will drive, and he will survive. Fontaine if you think you will be the one that knocks him down forever than you are giving yourself way too much credit. Head of NXN, Joe you must face fate which was determined by the previous choices we made ,and your choices have led you down the wrong path. You will see what I mean. You will see that your may be misunderstood ,but you will understand the pain and suffering I have in his soul…. It depends how stubborn you are in what you will be in the match. Fontaine you though kind of know the pain that I am experiencing as you had a glimpse of it at Distortion. You felt that pain when the official’s palm hit the mat the third time. Your body went numb didn’t it, Fontaine? You thought this must be a bad dream. You pinched yourself to make sure. The bad thing was you didn’t wake up. You were indeed in reality. You had the match won, yet it was taken away from you. It was ripped from your grasp by non other then yours truly. You see Joe that is what my life feels like all the time. I feel this is a bad dream though I don’t have any dreams anymore. I am forced to live out my life with an empty void feeling I have. I must live with a heart that is now filled with rage and loathing. Fontaine you will bare witness to the Kryptonite’s rage. You will feel the loathing that I have. Your prophecies will not seem so important when I unleash my violent outrage upon you. I will continue the onslaught till finally you cannot defend from it. You then will experience that same feeling from the last time we meet. Except this time you never had control of the match..Faith is a double edged sword that has more often than not cuts Kryptonite. Not known for my religious beliefs, I feel I am still scorned by God for being something I shouldn't be. But faith is not necessarily religious. Faith is in the eye of the beholder. Without faith one would soon fall. :::The projector kicks to life again and shows Kryptonite beaten by DCV. At emotional peak, he made rash decisions that he thought would fill him out as a person, but all they really did was burden him even more. All the things he set up to prove at one time or another slapped him in the face. He could find no solace in his own actions because he had faith in so many things that just didn't fucking matter. He could not 'transcend' to something greater than he was. He put too much faith in his use. The words 'shattered faith' show up in glossy blue on the top of the screen, flashing a cross and then showing Kryptonite on his hotel room floor, gasping to breathe, hoping each one will not be his last. For he was the victim of an attack that night, asking too much of it and not enough of himself and what he was given. So determined to prove himself for what? How does that make him better than the rest of these mindless morons running around, proclaiming themselves the greatest to ever live. Kryptonite proved that every night and yet there was always something msising. Always a void. Like a word that sticks on the tip of your tongue but you never fucking realize what it is. There was an unfillable gap that he had to close, and the fact is he used everything possible in his mind to fill it, only to realize it would not happen. Perhaps it was faith in a life better than this one. An almost normal life with two parents and a desk job. But it's not that that was not what he was meant to be. It's that that's what he could not, would not be. He couldn't create a false version of himself, he deserved more than tube feeding lies to his body for the rest of his life. In fact, the gap became such an integral part of his being throughout his life that it has become a seperate appaendage just like everything else. Just like all the hate, just like all the love. But he lost faith in the industry. He lost faith when his own boss turned against him and started fucking up the situation. He lost faith when he felt he had no further direction, and that he may as well die where he stood. But the promises living would bring snuffed the temptation altogether, ultimately, and he would contiunue on with his life. But faith in a religious fashion? No, that would never happen. It could not happen for he would have to sacrifice everything he worked for and if he ever prided himself in one thing, it would be individuality, being his own man::: Fontaine, welcome to the table -- I hope for your sake you've been dealt a good hand. Trying to make it in this company is never easy, which is why I want you to understand that you'll be cut no slack on Sunday night. Yeah, I'm what you'd call a whole different equation than your last opponent. And while I'd love to sit here and tell you that I've been where you are right now, I can't. The fact is, I entered this company as a household name. I built my reputation elsewhere, whereas you're just one of the many right now who are looking to take advantage of the mass FAW exodus to the title community. Sunday night will not be an upset victory, it will not be a defining moment in your career. Why? Because I won't allow it, not when it's at my expense. You've got to learn to crawl before you can walk Fontaine, and right now you're up against a big seed in this company. Need I say more? No. My track record speaks volumes, and like a handful of others right now -- my focus is on qualifying for a shot at the FAW Heavyweight Championship at any chance I can. That will come about by making no mistakes, by not slipping up against a rookie in what should be a routine victory. You spoke last week of fear and how you're not afraid to admit you have it, and I hope for your sake that you're able to channel that fear and use it as a means of motivation -- as a form of extra strength. Because Joe, if this one's to even break the one minute barrier, you're going to need it. Because anyway you cut it, whether it's in the back or in the ring, you will come face to face with your Jagged Reality. A puddle of muddy water grows bigger; bigger with each droplet of water from the damp wooden edge of the roof. The wind blows silently, ghostly, crackling the loose wooden boards along the slanted wooden fence that travels for miles into the mountains. The mountains rest, high above all else, in a calm confidence. A regular man is the plane; A champion is the mountain.Dead, stalky grass blows sternly in the wind, knee high, blending in with the surrounding Oaks that seemingly lean forward, watching with weary eyes. Weary eyes that never sleep. Weary eyes that must watch all, and protect all. Weary eyes that give comfort with shade, and blow peacefully in the night. I have those weary eyes. :::A dirt path leads through the forest to the broken steps. The collapsed steps lead up to the baren patio, where a rocking chair creaks back and forth, back and forth...back and forth. Endlessly rocking in time. It's occupants is a man, a man dressed in beaten attire, a man shaded with dirt that fits in with the surroundings. A man calm as the Oaks in demeanor, yet large as the mountains in personna.The man you may ask? Kryptonite. The scene zooms into the man, showing the slight glimmer on his face, the crooked smile, and the unshaven, spurratic facial hair. I've been in this game a long time, but a man must never leave his true roots, and these are mine. Just as these great Oaks have grown, I have grown, and I have matured into my being. I have learned by trial and error, I have fallen to the depths of hell and into the fiery pits of misery yet only to rise to the glory of the gold, the gold I held swiftly and rightfully. Only to return it for a false imprisonment, and only to returnwhen the day was right. When is that day you may ask? :::The man continues to rock as he looks out into the forest, that stretches endlessly on to the East, with the sun shining down beautiful shades of color. He chews on a peace of wheat and removes it with his left hand, tossing it aside onto the wood patio, only to be blown away by the wind::: The day is soon. Fontaine is my victim. I can't wait forever to claim what is mine. Life is only so long, and lessons learned must be lessons taught. I have much to teach, I have much to reveal, and I have much to replay. The way I see it, life is a journey, a journey on a ship that rides the waves of the oceans, and only you are the sailor directing the steamy boat in whatever direction you want to go. The only downside is that the directions are endless, never ending, and painful. You have to make the best of what you find and capitalize every last moment you have, whether it be positive or negative, and take life for what it's worth. :::The sun slowly fades off as shadows emerge from the Oaks, covering the man in a cool, wind chilling freeze. The winds begin to pick up, slightly carrying the howls of the wolves. The cringing sounds rise the hair on the neck ever so slightly, only to be blown in the breeze::: My past consumes all that I am, and my spirit reveals the journey I have portaken, only to throb, and cause this feeling of disention within me; a feeling of guilt, a feeling of utter disbelief: I am not fulfilled. I have not done all I can do. What I once thought was lost, gone forever, taken in the summers breeze, I realized can be recaptured. With every last breath I will fight the sullen fight to regain my glory. Joe, you stand in my way,to gain championship gold. :::The man covers his eyes with his hands before removing his hand and opening his eyes again, presenting a new look; a passionate evil type stare, one concentrated on the cause::: This game you play, that you call wrestling Joe, is much like the puddle that grows bigger. With each drop a new piece ot the puzzle is added, but sometimes, the drops blow in the wind, causing another puddle. This time, the piece to the puzzle is as big as the other puddle itself, and the new game will overtake you over. What you knew.. what you thought.. will all be torn to pieces.. ripped apart.. shredded and burnt in the pit. Whatever you think you know, or think you are, will be tested. You never know a man until a man knows himself. This game you play, you play it all wrong, because you thrive for another mans destiny, another mans glory, instead of realizing your own. My glory has yet to come. Distortion is my first step. You listen to each note and follow along, captured by the noise, sucked in by the desire to be who he is. You are not who you apear. None you are no Champion. To beat me Fontaine, you must realize who you are first, and realize where you want to go, and partake in your own journey. Following down my road will only lead you to a stumbling fall, a fall you will NEVER recover from. Maybe this is why I am the destined man to take what I want. In the past, I followed down the wrong path, took what wasnt mine while I sat in a cell watching myself reap the benefits. In the past, I was weaker, younger, but now have grown on his own identity, my own call. All on my calling card. It is time I returned to my roots, and ripped out the vein he has planted, ripped him free from me, and strip him of everything he holds that is mine. It is my heart. My soul. My mind. Everything that was once mine. Everything that was perfected to the finest beat, every movement to the swiftest song, back. I am living MY life, and my life Fontaine, begins with the FAW. One mans destiny at stake, anothers life on the line. Who succeeds when there can be no winner? :::The man recoils his collections and thoughts as his eyes open wide to the dawning sky. His starlit eyes watch as his brain succeeds in shutting down for the time being::: That is, for now. But chaos will emerge at Distortion. Society everywhere will know and feel the wrath. The past is always present as it is etched in the hearts, minds and souls of all. It is time once again for the rekindling. Feel the Fear! :::It begins to rain, Kryptonite looks up as the rain pours down. He groggily opens his eyes to the cheering fans. He has found his body once again. It all makes sense. Kryptonite knows it was all the truth. He looks to his arm where it bears a jagged cross with 'faith,' 'love,' 'hate,' and 'nothingness' at the arms.. The scene fades away:::
