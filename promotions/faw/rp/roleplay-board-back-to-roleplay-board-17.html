Kryptonite — FAW Roleplay, August 2002

August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Furiously Addictive Wrestling Roleplay Board Roleplay Board | Back to Roleplay Board Kryptonite Entitled: A Life of pain Written: 8-6-02 Location: An Empty Arena {The scene opens up in an abandoned arena in the middle of nowhere. Many chairs are around as the center of the arena sits Kryptonite. The camera comes from the left side and works his way towards Kryptonite. A dim lighting is placed across the arena as Kryptonite sits ina steel chair with a sledgehammer in hand. He takes a cold stare into the camera and begins to speak} The Night is my haven. It's the permeating darkness that makes me feel at home, almost as if my spirit has leaked free of my body and is floating all around me, protecting me. I feel relaxed and completely at ease, moving through the shadows, melting into their depths so that the naked eye doesn't see me until it's far too late. I would change this sentence to: 'I feel at ease in the shadows, where the naked eye can't find me until it's too late.' As you see, must simpler, more to the point, and it contains the same idea. Try to keep sentences concise; otherwise, the audience might become confused with your thoughts Apoc. Steer clear of repetition; you've told us that you want to hold the World title--you don't have to belabor us with it. I was lucky, and was gifted with a catlike grace that served me well. Without it such an occupation would be neigh impossible. {Kryptonite pauses as he stares across the arena} About 'my profession??' I was relying on all that given skill. How about just 'my life.' Otherwise, the my expression sounds off course--what given skill am I talking about? None other than standing in your way and taking your spot as a World Title contender. As I slipped invisibly from wall to wall, shimmying up alleys and climbing over rooftops with rapid, experienced ease. It had never been difficult. On the contrary, it was like a game of cat and mouse, and in this game, the mouse never knew the cat was coming. You Apoc.......have no idea what is coming!! {Kryptonite lifts up his hoodie and reveals his cold blue eyes. The camera zooms in as he continues} How about hell Apoc. Lets meet in hell, for the very first time the real man will come out on top. Otherwise, you have a repetition of just the beginning of a losing streak. The audience is fairly familiar that in the classic game of cat and mouse, the mouse never knows what hit it. A fatal, and enjoyable, distinction. My time has come, yours has gone and past and shall never return as long as I have my foot planted on FAW ground. An amused smirk flickered across my face just moments ago Apoc. My eyes trailed after I'd simply combine these two sentences. '...across my face as my target...' my target, a Mr. Apocolypse. I wasn't particularly interested in his name, seeing that he'd wouldn't be using it after I was through, but they always insisted on giving me all the details. I suppose the boss assumed it made us more comfortable to know things like that. But Apoc I will tell it like this, the man who walks out of that ring the victor will be guarenteed a World Title contender, no matter what!! {He lowers his head yet again and covers his face with his masive hands} Apoc is and will continue to be a blissful ignorance of my existence. Apoc would be coming my way soon. I'd had it all mapped out, going over every little nuance for anything that could go wrong. So far I hadn't had to worry. He'd been playing straight into my hands. Although many people are against me. He came closer, completely unaware of any eyes on him. He certainly wouldn't have thought to look up, anyway. People rarely do. He wove through the infrequent, faceless strangers that graced the sidewalk and turned right, following the corner, passing right under me. You now are across the roof and leaping down into the alley that joined the street that is owned by Kryptonite. Whether or not anyone would find him soon, I will take you out Apoc. Panic is just one stepping stone for me, not you. Maybe you have been here longer, does it really matter? Or are you afraid of a man not known very well to the FAW taking you off of your pedestal. No Hardcore title, no European, no Tag, no International is what I want. I want to take it to the xtreme. I want to get to the limit of my ability. The World title is what I want and you stand in MY way. Maybe you dont know who I am or maybe you do, but you most definatly will after tomorrow night. This is a sure sign that playtime is over. As soon as I started looking at papers, it was on to the next mission. I fought back the urge to groan. I'd just finished an assignment and already I had another one. Pulling out a sheet of paper that looked exactly like every other piece of paper I'd ever seen the week before for Distortion. I don't know how I could tell them all apart. The beginnings of a smile played across my lips, but I neglected to comment. But your name appeared on the slot Apoc. The man that Kryptonite would face for a prize postion. It hinted at quite unpleasant things. 'Why? What's so special about him?' He's... Not your average assasin. I was amazed to see what was obviously a hint of uncertainty creep into his eyes when he say that I was his opponent. A former champion, Kryptonite himself. Nobody wanted to tangle with you Apoc, until I came along. I offered it to a few others, but they all refused rather adamantly. They all decided you'd be the best pick for me to ablidge on. I agree with them, for the most part. Though, I am at least as skilled as you. I felt you needed a break... but...a sure sign of agitation came from you. Like you didnt want to be bothered. Oh well. You leaned back in my chair, then fixed me with a look that made me want to gulp and take back all my former impertinence. The front office thought very highly of me, I knew. And I of you Apoc. We'd known each other forever; though, I admit, my beloved pride I'd known longer. Apoc was here when I was a scruffy kid with no morals, no life, no faith for anyone but myself. I dont owe him alot, just my respect. And, in those few moments while we are in that ring we will exchange that look, and I will know he was asking much more than simply what was on the surface. He was calling back the favor. My life, if necessary, to be laid on the line for that world title. I will drop your eyes to the floor, Where they will immediately rolled under the the surface of the earth to my territory... but I can feel him still, eyes burning into me. All vestiges of insolency will be gone when I make that final course towards the ring. See you in hell ?? Not if I see you first!! Feel the FEAR!!
