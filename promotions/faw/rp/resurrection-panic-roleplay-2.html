Resurrection (Panic Roleplay 2) 'Legendary' Lee Smith Furiously Addictive Wrestling Roleplay #158 Date: 18th January '03 Location: Liberty Hotel, Unknown Area It is only a few hours since Lee and Jon got back from the police station. Fans will remember earlier today they were the heroes as they put a stop to an armed siege in a bank. After hours of questioning they return and head their separate ways with Lee heading to his hotel room and Jon staying downstairs for some much needed alcohol. -Jon- He made him feel alive. It was only a few weeks ago that Jon The Bomb had been unveiled as Lee Smiths new valet but since then the troublesome two had made a huge impact. Lees next match was up against Rzarector in an Extreme Lucha Libre match. Jon had newly installed faith in his client after the mental victory at the Bank. He was confident. Confidence can be a curse. “Your Cocktail, sir” the waitress said passing over a small drink to Jon. Jon thanked her politely and gulped down the drink within seconds. He let out a fresh gasp and put his feet up on the chair next to him. The Bomb could still feel the wounds that had ended his career, they had affected his life forever. There was the scar and stitches on his back, placed there in December 2000 when he took his first ever bump, tumbling off the hell in a cell in a federation called BAWF. The stitches remained there to this day as a souvenir - threatening to rip open any moment. Oh, and then there was the hip injury which wasn’t visible to the naked eye. Surgery had been needed after a fall through a glass table in a match in FAW at around the time of April 2001. That injury had halted his career for several months, but now he was back for a short while until the next injury….an everlasting cycle. Jon clicked his fingers at the waitress who had his next round on her tray. She passed the drink to Jon who turned, keeping an eye on the opposite sex. It was the same old story as he downed this drink in a larger amount of time with a beer belly beginning to form. It was some drinks later that Jon The Bomb finally decided to head for the bed and hit the pillow. He liked being a manager - it meant you could drink to the crack of dawn and not have to worry about fitness and the like. Wrestlers these days were under so much stress…. -Lee- SHUT UP. SHUT UP. SHUT UP. Lee too had a neverending cycle, thoughts through his brain. The latest thoughts passing through this junction were (unusually) not perverted one’s but the simple two words. SHUT UP. Lee was very unhappy with this Presidential suite. It had more of a resemblance to a French Toilet - or so was his opinion. Below in the bar noise belted upstairs floor by floor, disturbing everyone. Lee turned over in bed holding his head. And it wasn’t the first time he had tossed and turned that night. “UUUUUUUUUUUUH” He groaned under his breath with a terrific sigh. He rolled over again, instead this time reaching the end of his tether, quite literally, and falling off the kingsize bed. He had been crowned a hero after the robbery, but that was not what he wanted. He wanted to feel the sweet taste of victory once again, to remain undefeated in singles matches in FAW and to maintain his reputation. He agreed with his opponents name to a certain extent, for Rzarector would need resurrecting once The Living Legends fist connects with his skull. And so, with only two hours before his alarm for morning goes off and after a whole night of tossing and turning, Lee Smith finally fell asleep. On the floor. There is a first time for everything.
