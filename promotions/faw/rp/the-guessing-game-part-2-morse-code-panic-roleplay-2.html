The Guessing Game Part 2 - Morse Code [Panic Roleplay 2] 'Legendary' Lee Smith Furiously Addictive Wrestling Roleplay #137 Date: 3rd January 2003 Location: Hotel in Tampa, Florida *KNOCK KNOCK* Lee ignored the rattle on the door and turned the sound up on the television. Unusually, his morning attire had gone out of the window…not literally of course. It was around 10am in his hotel in Tampa, 2 days before FAWs next Panic event. A few weeks ago he had wrestled in the main event at Evil Intentions 2, and had struggled to get over his loss. With the help of a new mentor - whose identity has not been revealed, he had recovered the urgency that had made his career. But for the time being he didn’t care about that. The camera zooms in on the bags under his eyes, emphasising the lack of sleep he has had. Next to the TV stands a Playstation 2, the ideal electronic gaming accessory - arguably the best console on the Planet. Many young teenage kids would be waking up around now, switching on the TV and grumbling as they too rubbed their eyes - as did Lee. Meanwhile the pre-teens will have been up since 5 in the morning, enjoying the length season mode. What am I talking about? Why the most popular game on the Planet - and no this time no one can argue. Its name? FAW: Extreme Beating The game was produced by EA Sports, a rare wrestling game for them. It had overhauled greats such as Smackdown 4: Shut Your Mouth, Grand Theft Auto: Vice City and The Getaway on its rise to the top of the charts around the globe. Within a week it had become the fastest selling game of all time, and cheats were flying in online with dedicated phone lines being opened for gamers to explain their latest dilemma in the wrestling circuit. The game itself is pretty self explanatory. You select a wrestler and choose to fight in a variety of matches. Among them the new “Dome” match which was specially added for US release. The game is based on FAWs Mega PPV, Extreme Beating, which this year will be the 3rd in the series. Never had a game been so compelling, as not only did it have a unlimited time season mode, an online game for PC Gamers AND a bonus ‘Making Of’ DVD, but also a detailed ‘Create-a-Wrestler’ mode. EA had also succumbed to the pressure of the fans by supplying punch to punch combat commentary from Shane Riot and Floyd Rotterdamn as well as the odd remark from Mario Ciaralli. Lee Smith was sitting on the edge of his bed, as he quickly made his way into exhibition mode. He set the match type as a ladder match, and then decided he would not be playing. He then went onto the competitors screen and avoided the unlock able superstars - among them greats such as Randy Rocker, Pyro and American Psycho. Instead he selected himself, and chuckled as he saw his stats raised to 100. His doing of course. He decided that his virtual self would battle against Rzarector in a simmed preview of the match on Panic. He then sat back as the game loaded, and digged deep into his Doritos packet. The family bag was empty, and he walked into his miniature kitchen and threw it in the bin. The whole bin was filled with the same family bag packet, and he simply went into the cupboard and opened another packet. The salty flavour melted in his mouth as he returned to the edge of his bed with the game loaded. With the lack of sleep came a lack of sanity and he began to commentate on the match. “Right hand delivered By Rzarector, Smith counters with a sidewalk slam. Smith taunts Rza, whipping into ropes. Smith taunts again as fans give mixed reaction. Rzarector strikes back with an axe handle smash. Rza takes ladder and sets it up. R starts to climb but The Living Legend makes the save, toppling the ladder and sending Rza plummeting down. “Legendary” Lee, equipped with a steel chair, rams the weapon into the skull of his enemy. He raises Rzarector and irish whips him against the ropes. The referee attempts to pull the chair off Lee but Smith breaks free and smashes the chair against Rzarector seconds later. The crowd chant for Rzarector as a replay is shown of that chair shot. Smith climbs the turnbuckle and dives onto Rzarector. Lee then attempts to seal the deal by climbing the ladder. Rzarector soon joins him at the top and they grapple. Lee uses his weight to disastrous consequences as they both fall and are knocked out.” Lee yawns and stretches as he watches the AI suffer with added pain, there is another frantic knock on the door and Lee walks to it. He opens the door wearing only his underwear and sees a hotel employee standing behind a tray which holds his breakfast. He snatches the food off of her and throws a dollar note at her which provides a tip. He returns to see that the match is over and “Mein Herz Brehnnt” by Rammstein hits over the sound system and he sees his virtual self celebrating. That is how it would be, Lee retaining his record as unbeaten in singles matches. Or so he hoped. Rzarector had put up a good fight in the Dome, but the fact that he had encouraged medical attention proved to be the killer. Lee was proud of himself - for he would never stoop as low. Rzarector had been given an unfair advantage, but still Lee had cast him aside. There is a first time for everything the saying goes , and there is also a second time,which is why Lee would be ensuring that more paramedics would be rushed off their feet when he met Rzarector one on one in the ring. One On One…..he wasn’t so sure about those odds any more. Surely…Rzarector had been given an upper hand previously….how about Lee entwined himself in a fellow Gods will….mix with things a little….make things even. He came out of his daze and swallowed his last croissant full before once again turning to the widescreen television. He fiddled with the controller at lightning speed and entered the create a wrestler mode. Lee selected a male model and quickly added the odd feature here and there to make this fictional character realistic and at the same time unique. At the same time a rather strange resemblance built, and the fictional character was no more. This man was real…in a way….as some would say that his versatility is unreal. So many falls, so many broken bones…and so many tales of heroic courage and bravery. He had been absent for a long time now…..B……But what did the B stand for, and what were his other initials? That didn’t matter at the moment. A sharp fall had cut his career short a long time ago in a faraway land….in time circumstances. In location, it was as far from the truth as can be. Tampa..Florida…..early 2001 is where it had all ended, a dream shattered. It was in that exact arena, that sharp fall. Or so hours of mobile phone conversations this was what Lee had learnt. In a sense B was very similar to Lee. They both shared a strong aura of determination. And they both stood for the same beliefs, pride comes before power. Lee continued with creating his motivator for a few more minutes, it began to lack detail. He added some fine touches before moving on to profile. He worked his way to the bottom. 6’0 he was- just slightly taller than Lee. His weight was unspecified. Now onto the name. Lee was aware of the publicity surrounding his mentor…and had all the tabloids guessing who the man only known as B could be. He sniggered to himself as he revealed the first name of his mentor. 'Jehovah Is Gracious' Morse Code. Perhaps it was a translation from a different language…..Hebrew? Open up your dictionaries….before its too late. Explosion upon Impact.
