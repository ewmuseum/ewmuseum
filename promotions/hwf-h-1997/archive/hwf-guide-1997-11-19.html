Hello. My name is [name withheld]! I'm in charge of the HWF! I'm here to tell you that this is without a doubt one of the greatest leagues on America On-Line! This league brings all the greatest Roleplayers together! This league is for the serious Roleplayers out there! I want the best! If you think you are the best then I WANT YOU TO TRY THE HWF! It won't be a waste of your time! PLEASE DON'T JOIN IF YOUR NOT GOING TO ROLEPLAY, YOU'LL JUST BE WASTING YOUR AND MY TIME! THANK YOU! Friday Facial-This show is not as grafic as Tuesday Timebomb, but it's still the best thing going on Friday Night! Sunday Slam-This show is like Shotgun Saturday Night! But you won't be disappionted with this show! Trust me!
