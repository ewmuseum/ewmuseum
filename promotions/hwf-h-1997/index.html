A November 1997 real-wrestlers-only Usenet fed built around a twelve-month pay-per-view calendar and a named starting roster. Written by AI, 2026

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

This Hardcore Wrestling Federation opened on rec.sport.pro-wrestling.fantasy on 1 November 1997. Its own founding post introduces its founder as the former president of the EWF, freshly out of that league after a computer virus and starting over from nothing: “I'll be honest you all know that its not easy to start a Federation with good roleplayers, that's why I'm writing!” It asked prospective members to e-mail a list of ten wrestlers they wanted to play, and asked separately for a web designer and writers, promising “YOU WILL BE GIVEN FULL CREDIT FOR YOUR WORK!”

By its fullest surviving guide, dated 19 November, the HWF had a weekly card slate — Tuesday Timebomb, Friday Facial, Sunday Slam — and a full twelve-month pay-per-view calendar mixing invented event names of its own (Death in Dallas, Carey's Birthday Bash, Hate Your Neighbor, ColdBlood 1) with real WWF pay-per-view names carried over wholesale (Royal Rumble, WrestleMania, SummerSlam, Halloween Havoc). It ran, in its own words, “an all REAL wrestler league! Sorry no FAKE guys at all!” and offered a roster of real wrestlers of the day for members to choose from, among them Raven, Taz, Sabu, Chris Jericho, Chris Benoit, Eddie Guerrero and Ric Flair, plus tag teams including the Dudley Brothers and the Legion of Doom.

Its own operator structure changed within its short attested life: its earliest guide editions named a co-president alongside its founder, and by the 19 November edition its founder alone signs as being “in charge of the HWF,” still recruiting for a vice-president, a newsletter publisher, a web page designer and a card writer. No card, result, or title survives past that guide, and the surviving record carries no closure notice — it simply stops there, three weeks after it opened.
