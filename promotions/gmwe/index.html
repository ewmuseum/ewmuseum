Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

GrafixsMan Wrestling Entertainment was put together in the last months of 2002 and opened its doors on 11 January 2003, hosted by, and named after, the design studio behind it. Its own About page said the plan was regular updates, an eye-catching site and handlers involved in running the place rather than only wrestling in it.

It ran a show called ICE, posted the card for the first one three weeks after opening, and filed every promo as a numbered GMWE Interview — thirty-seven of them by the second week of March. Six survive. They are almost all the work of the same two men, a tag team called Wild and Crazy who spend one of them noticing out loud that they are the only tag team in the federation and will have an easy shot at the titles. Its champions page, captured in February, lists a World, a US, a TV, a Hardcore and a Tag-Team belt, and the word None under every one of them.

Its own April roster names thirteen wrestlers across seven lettered sections, most with no further trace in the record. Two of them, Karl Commons and Brian Davis, are promised a beating that same week by Wild and Crazy's own promo as a newly formed pair called the Texas Twosome. Its founder wrestled too, under his own staff-page credit: a co-booker's own quote there puts his singles record at one win and fifteen losses.

Crazy left on 10 March, in a promo that says the place is going nowhere and that he has already signed elsewhere and won a title there. Wild said he was staying. In June the fed’s own front door announced that a federation called ICW had become part of it — “Same Federation: New Name” — and asked visitors to stick with it through the retooling. The last new thing it left is a set of wrestler bio cards captured at the end of July 2003.
