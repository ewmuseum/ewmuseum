Scott Evans, 23 February 2003

23 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Scott Evans GMWE Interview #34 Date: 02/23/03 Location: Chicago, Illinois ~Voice Over: This Sunday is just a beginning, I may not be the most talented thing to hit GMWE ever but you will see me rise. I'm not exactly the best in this game but I'm not the worst either, I haven't been wrestling that long, my career literally began a few months ago yet I still spread the start of something that will end the little losses that wrestling as a whole has been experiencing. I am here in GMWE to start that and Jim Steele will be my first opponent, I will show him why I am a gladiator.~ We are in a very dim-lit alley in Chicago, Illinois where GMWE wrestler(he's not a superstar yet) Scott Evans stands in a blue hoody, blue sweatpants, and he wears a pair of sneakers upon his feet. He stands for a few moments before he starts to walk out of the alley and onto the street. After a few moments of walking he stops and turns towards us. 'Well I guess I better start to talk about things. My whole life has been one crazy roller coaster type ride, if anything ever goes good it is destined to turn seriously wrong, like when I had everything in my life going right when my wife and child were still alive, when my brother was still alive. Then came that one night that brought me to insanity, that night when my brother was shot nearly to the death, I remember his last words, I remember them well, 'Go and finish your dream.' then afterwards I went home to find my wife and child dead on my front lawn, at the moment I knew who did it, and 3 days later the people who did that were all lieing in their graves, next thing I know I'm being put in jail where they discover that I'm a guy with multiple personalities, days after that I'm in an Insane ward where they want to cure my illness, by 1997 I was out of there and I spent the next couple of years on the street until one night I ventured out of my little home and went and saw a wrestling event which re-awoke that memory and all those childhood memories I had. Next thing I know I'm running down the aisle in the arena towards the ring, somehow I get past security and I hop in the ring and knock both the guys out of the ring taunt security and I find myself getting asked to sign a contract that next for my first wrestling federation, the rest is probably easy to figure out. Now my first opponent in GMWE is Jim Steele, and usually I'd make fun of his name or something like that, but I'd rather just say that I will not lose, sure I may not win, but this match is one I will not lose, I'll draw it or win it, that is all I have to say for now.' As Scott finishes the scene fades to black.
