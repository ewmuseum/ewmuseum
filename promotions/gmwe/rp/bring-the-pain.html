Bring the Pain

20 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Bring the Pain Wild GMWE Interview #33 Date: 2/20/2003 Location: Atlanta, Ga {Wild and Crazy are riding in a limo with Jack Bateman. They are discussing something. Crazy and Wild are smiling as Jack tells them something. The limo stops.} {Jack:} Finally. We're at the club. {Crazy:} Hang on. You and Wild go on in. I am going to cut a promo real fast since we have thsi nice camera. {Wild:} Ok. {Jack:} Ok. {Crazy looks dead into the camera as Wild and Jack get out of the limo to screaming fans. They shut the door. Crazy gets a wicked grin on his face.} {CRAZY:} This Saturday. Davis has said Hell is coming. He might just be right but he ain't right about him and Commons kickign our asses. We are the number one tagteam in this federation. We are the only tagteam in this federation. The Texas Twosome...sounds like a dance. What a group of...well you get the idea. Commons had his ass handed to him once and now he will get it again with one thing different. Now I have a partner with me to beat his ass. Wild ahs his own agenda for Brian Davis but I have only one thing on my mind. That is to beat Commons till he retires before he even gets his career started. He will not walk out of Ice this week. If I have to chase him down I am going to make sure he doesn't walk out. Commons with his redneck slur of howsit and shit like that will pay for his idiotic comments about he can beat me and Wild. This is the end of the line of the newly formed Texas Twosome. BEfore they can even get off the ground I am going to make sure they don't survive. {Crazy grins wickedly again and then climbs out of the limo. GMWE Fans tryign to get into the club start chanting his name. The door shuts to the limo and it drives off.}
