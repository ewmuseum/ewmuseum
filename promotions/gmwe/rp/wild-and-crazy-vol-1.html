Wild and Crazy VOL 1

Wild and Crazy VOL 1 Crazy GMWE Interview #3 Date: 1/23/2003 Location: Personal Weight Room in FL {Wild and Crazy are running moves in their personal training ring in their mansion on the beaches of Panama City. Wild kicks his training partner in the gut and then sets up as if he is going to pedigree the poor soul. Wild suddenly lifts the trainer up and powerbombs him. Crazy then jumps off the top rope into a shooting star press but starts to rotate making it a twisting shooting star press. Crazy lands on the trainer as Wild counts the three. Crazy and Wild then take a break.} {Crazy} Wild, did you see the interview with Sabbath? {Wild} Yeah, the guy is such an idiot. He thinks he is hardcore. There is no possible way he is as hardcore as me. Since you moved on from hardcore I have becoem the king of HARDCORE! {Crazy} True but you still haven't had a match in a year and a half. You sure you can take him. {Wild} Of course I can take him. With such a devasting move as the WildFire and it is so easy to set up for why wouldn't I be able to. {Crazy} Just wondering before you went and challenged him. {Wild} Crazy? Do you realize we are the only tagteam in this fed so far? {Crazy} Yeah it sucks we have no competition. The deadly Insanity Break won't be able to be used. That sucks. Well at least we will have an easy shot at the titles. {Wild} That sucks because we have been like this before and noone wanted to challenge us. {Crazy} Yes. But remember..... {Crazy smiles at Wild. Wild nods.} {Wild} Yes. They can just wait. How do you think the World Title will be decided. {Crazy} I don't know but one thing is for sure. I will be the one standing with it when it is all done. That is my only goal for now. I am hopeing that my old nemesis comes back. Remember the insane hell in the cell we had. {Wild} Yes, I remember. That almost ended your career. But enough with this. We need to get back to training. The first show will be here before you know it. {Crazy} I guess you're right. {Wild and Crazy get back in the ring and go back to workign on moves. They set the trainer up for the Wildfire and CrazySplash once again.}
