Charisma Get ready for the pain.

4 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Charisma Get ready for the pain. The Sabbath GMWE Interview #2 Date: may 4th, 03 Location: Places ***The camera comes on and shows Butch and Oz in 'The hippie pub' for the weed and the drinks. Oz is over at the arcade playing the new ICW game 'ICW & GMWE -vs- The World' and guess what type of match he is playing in? Of corse it is the great match from a few months ago. X-Sabbath (X-Treme & Sabbath) -vs- The Sweedish Pickles in that hardcore electric cage match. Butch walks up with the beers and they start to play.*** Butch: ya I think it was a good idea to come here. We need to relax before my match against Charisma, actually I will be relaxing in the ring when I kick his ass 5 times before I get the win and make you tap out. No body is going to get in my way of winning that tournament and the pride of being the best. Oz: Ya just shut the fuck up and play the damn game. The match is starting. ***The screen in the arcade game turns on and X-Sabbath and The Sweedih Pickles are in the cage and the match starts. Butch and Oz are going crazy in the game while people are walking by just staring at them. A few minutes later the match ends and They unplug the game and oz takes it to the car. Oz comes back into the bar.*** Butch: Ya that's the shit. What's on TV? Oz: America's most wanted. Butch: Turn that shit on then! ***Oz turns pn the TV and a picture of Butch and Oz come on the screen.*** John Walsh: These two people are Butch Michaels and Oswald Johnson but they go by the names Butch and Oz. A few weeks ago they went to ICW training grounds and a few minutes later it exploded. They are to be brought in alive for questioning. We do belive that they could have been the perpetrators of this crime. ***Oz turns off the TV and everyone is looking at them. Butch walks out and Oz does too shortly after. They hop into the truck and drive off.*** Butch: Damn that fucker. Who the fuck rated us out? Oz: I think I know. You knw that kid I beat the shit out of when we blew up: ------{Flashback}------ The camera comes to the ring and shows Oz picks up one of the students in a suplex position and drops him down in a neckbreaker. He slides out of the ring while EMTs take the kid out of the building. Oz: You see that. I call that shit the Jail House Rock. John Daymite: You have got a great new partener there Butch. So your the Oz he's been talkin about. You seem like you could make it in this fed. Want a match against me; 1 on 1 in the middle of that ring? ------{End}------ Oz: we went a little too far in that flash back when John started to talk. but oh well. The fact of the matter is that the fucken kid I beat the shit out of in the middle of the ring told the damn cops about us doin that shit. Butch: Ya but we gots ta go to the arena and run a promo for the fans. Oz: Well we can just call Hayes and get him to send us a cameraman. We will send the cameraman back with a tape and have it shown to the fans. Butch: Thats not a bad Idea. Okay you get out the cellphone and call him. ***Oz gets the cell phone out of the glove compartment and calles Matt Hayes. An hour later Guy Riff ICW's main cameraman comes to the outskirts of town and sets up the camera.*** Guy: Okay were on in 5..4..3..2..1..and action! ==========Promo Begins========== [The camera comes on and shows Butch and Oz standing on the top or thier truck. Butch is smokin a cig and Oz is just drinkin some Jack Daniels.] Butch: hey people we can't be there, and thank god cause you people absolutely suck!....wait for the boos.... Hey its not my fault you suck. Oz: Just get to bidness you fuck. Butch: Ha! Okay. I am here to tell all 5 of the Charisma fans out there that your hero is going to get his ass kicked at the KOTC tourny. I'm going to make that stupid fucker scream 'I QUIT!' And if he doesn't I will just have to kill him in the middle of the ring. You see I'm not talking cause I think i'm going to win. I know I'm going to win. You ICW or GMWE superstars can't deny that I am your king and you are all my bitches. So I guess I will be seeing someone in the finals, in the middle of the ring with my foot up your face. {CFTB} ==========Promo Ends============ Guy: That was great. I'm gonna go get this to the fans. Oz: Okay, thanks fo the help man. Butch: Now get your ragity ass out of here. Okay Oz lets get to the arena. Oz: Sho' {NOW THE CFTB}
