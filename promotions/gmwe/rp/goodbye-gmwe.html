Goodbye GMWE

10 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Goodbye GMWE Crazy GMWE Interview #37 Date: 3/10/2003 Location: Gold's Gym {Crazy and Wild are working out together. Crazy is thinking and Wild looks mad.} {Wild:} WHY? {Crazy:} This place is going nowhere right now and I have to further my career. I have moved on. I can't wait ehre anymore. {Wild:} So. Joey hired a fluke. This fed will rebound. Every fed Joey has owned and you worked for him you have been great. Please give the GMWE another shot. {Crazy:} I am leaving. That is all there is too it. I have already signed a contract with another fed and in my first match won the Xtreme Title. {Wild:} I know this. I'm not leaving however. We had plans and I plan on continuing to carrying them out. WE will survie without you. {Crazy:} Hey, you could come with me. It would be great. THe tag divsion doesn't even look like it could give us a minor challenge. {Wild:} I've made up my mind. I am staying here. So go ahead and say your goodbye. {Crazy:} GMWE Fans and fellow Wrestlers. I have signed a contract with another fed. I ahve already won a title and cannot perform in the GMWE anymore. I know this is a shock because I was on a roll. I will miss this atmosphere but I have to think of my career. So long. Wild, we''l get abck together sooner or later just like always. {Crazy stands up. Grabs the Xtreme title he kept talking about. HE also grabs another belt he had with him. He hands it to Wild.} {Crazy:} You won this a long time ago. The one match that never got aired. I've held it this whole time but it should've been yours. Now you can defend it like the champion you are. {Wild:} What?...wait...this is...Oh my god... {Crazy walks away as Wild stares in awe. Wild can't believe what he is looking at. He watches his friend leave.}
