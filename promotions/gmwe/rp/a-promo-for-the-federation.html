A promo for the federation The Sabbath GMWE Interview #5 Date: Superbowl Day! Location: Places [The lights go out in the arena as blue and red lights shine around the arena and the crowd yells 'FAYGO! FAYGO!' The camera spins around and stops in the middle of the crowd. The Sabbath comes out with a cannon-like object. He slides in the ring and shoots out Root Beer Faygo and green fireworks. He shoots it in every corner and the crowd cheers. he jumps on the top rope and taunts to the crowd as the yell out 'Faygo! Faygo!' The Sabbath slides out of the ring and looks under the ring. He pulls out a huge tub of Faygo bottles and a sircular blade. He slices open the bottles stands on the top rope and throws the bottle into the crowd. He jumps off the rope and taunts in the ring.] The Sabbath: You know what people. Your fave superstar is back and in full action. And this time I won't be comin out to the ring and telling you all that I will win a certain title. I will come out here and tell you all that you absolutly suck! [The crowd boos him] The Sabbath: I mean come on. You are all nothing but Hillbilly mother fuckers with no talent and absolutly no brain whatsoever. Now there are only two people that are stupider than you. One of them is george bush. The only man that can take a perfectly good Idea and fuck it upo faster than Bill Clinton with 3 high school chicks. And this 'Crazy' guy. i mean he thinks he scares some of us superstars by capturing a superstar and chaining him up. HAHAHAHA This guy is a fucker who will never get laid. I mean he doesn't know what the meaning of being a psycho is. I mean holy shit. He is just like you people. I mean he's a freak, an ass, and a homo who purposly drops the soap in prison for some butt-sex. I was shipped off to Guatemala in a crate because I fucken killd a guy. I had to live without human contact for a while and to be honest I learned how to adapt by myself. I mean this guy hasn't seen the light. I don't think I can kick his ass. I know I can kick his ass. I mean this guy is nothing more that a self-righteous bitch. So come on. i mean this fed isn't even up and running and I have already seen some of the worst superstars in wrestling. Jowk Knowles may be the owner but if he gets in my way on my shot for the world title he won't know what hit him. i see superstars like Night Hawk, Neo, and Master. More like Might Fawk, NO, and Masterbater. I mean come on these guys are stepping stones you hear me? STEEEPING STONES! You people have one thing in common with Wild. Your dirrty, assholes who will never get a job. You know what I think my IQ is dropping staying right in the same arena with you dicks. I'm out. [The Sabbath dropps the microphone and leaves the ring] {FTB}
