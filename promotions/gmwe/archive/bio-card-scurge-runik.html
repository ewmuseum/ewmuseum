Nicknames: Status: Face Entrance Music: 'We Live No More' by Black Label Society Information Wrestling Style: Speed/ Aerial Set Up Move: Spinning Heel Kick (Spinning Heel Kick ) Finisher: Runik Tribute (Corkscrew Moonsault Splash) Title History: Former US Champion Award History: History/Biography: Scurge was always bullied at school and never had much of a chance, he didnt know how to fight or how to defend himself. He wasnt Strong but he wasnt weak either, he was always outnumbered. One time at the age of 16 he was being chased by a group of about 5 bullies. He ran into the schools gym to hide from the gang, in there he met the schools mexican janitor who seemed very interested in what was going on. Scurge told the janitor about how he was always being bullied. The janitor then revealed to Scurge that he was the great luchidor Runik Storm, he then offered to show Scurge a few things to help him stick up for himself. Scurge then trained under Runik for about 16 months where he learnt the best moves wrestling had to offer.One day the gang of bullies were wondering why he was missing every lunch time so they investigated. They found out about Scurges secret training. They decided to teach him a lesson. One day at the end of the school day Scurge was walking to his locker where he was jumped by the gang of bullies. He used wat he had learnt against them, he ended up beating them all and seriously hurting their reputations. The bullies intent on revenge decided to beat the source of Scurges abilities, they decided to attack Runik. Runik was quietly mopping the floors of the corridors when the bullies jumped him from behind. He was whacked across the back of the head with a baseball bat, and then the bullies proceded to beat the 54 year old man.Runik was lying beaten and broken when Scurge stumbled upon him. Runik then told him wat happened, Runik then lay back and died. Scurge was distraught, Runik had been the only one that had helped him. The death of Runik deeply effected Scurge. Scurge Decided to never let Runik die and he decided to carry on the famous name. He took the alias of Scurge Runik and decided to carry on his legacy by becoming a great wrestler. He fought in Amatuer leagues and started making a name for himself. His Runik Tribute which he had learnt from his master was one of the most feared moves among amatuers and he never lost a match. The Runik Tribute knocked out his opponent every time. He was not pleased with the small time leagues though, so he decided to chase bigger prospects and decided to join the ICW, which is where he is today.
