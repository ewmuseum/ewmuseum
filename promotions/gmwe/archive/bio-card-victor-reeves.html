Wrestler Logo Here Wrestler Info: Victor Reeves Real Name: Victor Reeves Birthdate: 4/13/82 Hometown: New Orleans Height: 6'4' Weight: 219 lbs. Nicknames: The Bouncer Status: Face Entrance Music: 'Remember' by Disturbed Information Wrestling Style: All-Rounder Set Up Move: Bourbon Street Slam (Air Plane Spin into a F-5 ) Finisher: New Orleans Press (A Shooting Star Press into a Guillotine Leg Drop) Title History: Award History: History/Biography: Born into a rich home in the suburbs of New Orleans, Victor Reeves had the life. The problem of his big house, wealthy surroundings, and no worries at all, is being lonely and bored as hell. Then one day he got into pro-wrestling. If it wasn’t for wrestling, he would never decided to become one. Another dream that Victor wanted to do is to fight. He often gets into fight at school, parties, and other places for fun. He mostly win, and only lost due to interference. Once he turned 17, he decided to go out into the city of New Orleans. He met someone that got him into a street fighting circuit. There he went to the ranks towards the city champion. Once he had the chance to fight the city champion, he took it and grasped. In that fight, he was dominating over the city champion. But when he was going to deliver his finishing blow, one of the champ pals interfered. Soon there was a all out brawl, and when the police came to stop the fight. Two people was dead, and a lot more was injured. Victor escaped getting arrested, and decided to get a job. By this time he was 18, and he decided to become a bouncer. One of his friends got him a job at one of Bourbon Streets bar. They he got to kick ass for better money. He often stopped fights, or get involved in them. Soon the bar was known for it brawls, and more people came to see them. Victor often kicked the brawlers outside, but they would still fight on the sidewalk. Sometimes it get boring, and Victor had to pick up underage drinkers and throw them out the door. Yet his friend got fired for stealing money, and Victor was fried as well. Pissed off, he went to another bar called Rotation Symmetry and got a job there. The owner was often in illegal shit, and couldn’t call the cops that often. So Victor often beat the hell out of the people who started fights, or kicked the guys out on their Asses with at least a broken bone. Victor often enjoy getting into the bar brawls. However, he never forgot his dream. So he went into a pro-wrestling as a part time thing. But soon other people and himself found himself as a great wrestler. He appears so realistic, and his moves was done perfect. So he decided to go for the big time, ICW maybe his only gate way to fulfilling his dream.
