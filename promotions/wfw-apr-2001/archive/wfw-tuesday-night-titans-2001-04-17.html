WFW (Fanatic, 2001) — Card

Tuesday Night Titans

17 April 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[ The camera fades in to a wide pan shot of the inside of a jam packed
Pengrowth Saddledome in Calgary, Alberta. The audio fades in at the same time with the growing cheers of the fans. Blue and white lights flash from the entrance as "Get Ready For This" by 2 Unlimited hits the sound system.
Fans stand to their feet and give off a huge pop as World Fanatic Wrestling
CEO Art Rousseaux walks out from the back wearing a brand new WFW hockey jersey.
He salutes the fans with a raised, pumped fist in the air and a wide smile. He then trots his way down to the ring which featured a blue tinted mat, blue-silver-blue ropes, a black ring apron with WFW on two sides,
http://wfw.i85.net on the other two, and the WFW logo on every turnbuckle. ] EDGERIN: Ladies and gentleman.. please welcome the Chief Executive Officer of World Fanatic Wrestling... ARRRRRT RRRRRRRROUSSSSSEAUX!!
[ The crowd gives off another loud pop as Art takes the mic from Trent and stands in the middle of the ring. Some "dub-eff-dub" chants are heard in the arena as the crowd dies down a bit to hear Rousseaux ]
ROUSSEAUX: CALGARY, ALBERTA, CANADA!!! [ HUGE POP ] ROUSSEAUX: Welcome to the dawn of a new era in sport entertainment!! [ POP ] ROUSSEAUX: Welcome to TUESDAY NIGHT TITANS!! [ yet another POP ] ROUSSEAUX: And along with the rest of the world that is tuning in tonight, I welcome you all to WORLD FANATIC WRESTLING!!
[ Another huge pop as "Get Ready For This" again plays. Art hands the mic back to Trent Edgrin. He waves to the fans a final time before heading back out the ropes. The sound of the crowd and Art's music fades and so does the image of Rousseaux himself as we go into the opening TNT sequence... ]
************************************* [ A clip of "Here We Go" by Templar plays in the background. ] # Here we go... # [ Image of a ref signaling for a match to start by crossing his arms. After a few hard guitar chords and images of various fans cheering images of WFW wrestlers appear with the words. ]
# Ride... Ride with me yeah... # [ Clip of Captain Canuck hitting his "Captain's Crunch" finisher ] # And you wonder why... # [ Clip of Max Damage and Liz - both looking up to no good walking down an isle arm in arm ]
# It could be so bad.. # [ Clip of K. Oh on the top turnbuckle then leaping off with a missile dropkick ] # And you give yourself away.. # [ Clip of Shane Donnely and Clint Cote calling a match behind the broadcast booth ] # And I'm alone # [ Clip of Justun Stopable hitting his finisher "The Facelift" ] [ A few more notes play while an image of Ice and his valet Ms. Cold Shoulder beside him standing in front of exploding fireworks with arms raised. The camera starts panning up above the pair and the fireworks to reveal a WFW logo.]
# Here we go! # [ As the music suddenly stops with those final words the WFW letters "explode" into three different directions and the Tuesday Night Titans logo fades in. It appears for a few moments before zooming forward leaving the screen black for perhaps a second or two before cutting into a live shot of the arena and an instrumental version of "Here We Go" ]
************************************* [ Pyro explodes at the entrance and above the ring as a small graphic in the left hand corner says "LIVE - WFW TNT - PENGROWTH SADDLEDOME" and below that text is "CALGARY, ALBERTA, CANADA" After all the pyro is finished, the arena lights come on and a huge crowd pop is heard. The camera zooms in on some of the more creative signs... "WHO NEEDS THE FLAMES? WE HAVE WFW!,"
"COTE CAN SCORE WITH ME ANY DAY!" and "WFW = THE THRILL'S RIDE." ] DONNELY: Hello everyone and welcome to the debut of Tuesday Night Titans!! I am Shane Donnely along with Clint Cote, and 'coats,' we have a heck of a show kicking off World Fanatic Wrestling's existence!
COTE: Without a DOUBT! Shane... I'm lovin this!! Ya know, no one has really paid any attention to WFW and it's search for talent the last few weeks, but after tonight I think... after WFW puts on a show that will exemplify what you can come to expect week in and week out.. well I think a few more people will sit up and take notice!
DONNELY: Amen! You know that may be the only thing the two of us agree on ever. COTE: I wouldn't say that Clint! DONNELY: I would! COTE: Ok, see we are disagreeing already... DONNELY: Didn't take long didn't it? Folks lets go to "The Edge" and get to our first match!
------------------------------ -- RABID ROB vs BANDIT BILL -- ------------------------------ EDGERIN: Ladies and gentleman this contest scheduled for one fall is the first ever match in World Fanatic Wrestling HISTORY!
[ The crowd cheers loudly as "Cowboy" by Kid Rock starts to plays over the speakers. The crowd rises to it's feet with a medium level cheer. ]
EDGERIN: Introducing first.. weighing in at 244 pounds.. from Angel Fire,
New Mexico.. BAAAAAAAAAANDIT BILLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLL!!
[ The crowd pops as Bill hops over the top rope and into the ring. He raises his arms in the air and salutes the fans from the second turnbuckle in one of the corners.
EDGERIN: And his opponent.. [ "Highway To Hell" by AC/DC kicks in over the PA system as the crowd starts to boo ] EDGERIN: ... weighting in at 230 pounds and hailing from the local asylum... RAAAAAAAAAABIIIIIIIIIIIIIDD ROOOOOOOOB!! [ Rob spits and snorts his way down the isle as he looks with pure hate out into the crowd. He is hit with the odd paper ball and before he gets into the ring he is hit with a plastic cup full of beer covering him head to chest. Rob looks stunned for a moment. ]
COTE: DAMN! That was not a good move at all! DONNELY: Without a doubt! Fans should never be throwing things at the wrestlers, they may end up causing serious damage!
COTE: That did cause serious damage! Serious damage to the beer!! What a waste of a brew!
[ Rabid Rob's face turns redder and redder as he starts shaking, enraged by the beer hitting him. He lets out a horrifying scream into the air and jumps into the ring and spears Bandit Bill ]
DING DING DING DONNELY: Oh my! And with that we are underway! COTE: Apparently Rob thought it was a waste of good beer as well! DONNELY: I highly doubt that's what he is thinking! Rob pouncing onto of
Bill and he starts feeding him lefts and rights to the head. Bandit Bill is trying to cover up here from this attack! Rob has just snapped!
COTE: Oh gee that's too bad! He seemed like such a *calm* personable guy a moment ago as he spat on a fan!
DONNELY: Rob now trying to choke out Bill! C'mon ref get him off!! The ref does but nearly gets taken out himself as Rabid Rob violently charged off
Bill and at the ref before backing away.
COTE: Lesson number one here in WFW. Don't piss off Rabid Rob! DONNELY: And poor Bill is finding that out first hand and to think he wasn't the guy that pissed Rob off! Bill trying to get his wits about him now, but not before Rob goes down to one knee beside him and lands a stiff right hand to the side of Bill's head.. knocking him down again.
COTE: Didn't realize it until now... but we have double B versus double R! DONNELY: I see those extra 3 years of high school paid off. So tell me who are you favoring here? Double B or double R?
COTE: Does it matter? These guys would be nothing compared to a set of double D's! DONNELY: Sheesh... Rob now with a few more right hands to the head of Bill. COTE: Rob is being quite unmerciful here!! The ref can't do anything to stop his attack of Bill here!
DONNELY: Rabid Rob now pulling Bill up by the hair, and whipping him to the ropes... Bill back on the rebound and he's knocked down hard with a stiff clothesline!! Rob now dropping a series of knees now on the shoulder of the
Bandit.
COTE: Of course that is setting up for Rob's submission finisher: "The
Straight Jacket" DONNELY: And a painful maneuver that is! Rob now grinding his knee into
Bill's shoulder for a few more moments before pulling him up again by his right arm. Rob with series of shoulder thrusts into Bill's shoulder, knocking Bill back down to one knee. Rob now backing off slightly as Bill is trying to get up. Rob charging now.. Oooooo swift kick to the FACE!!
COTE: Wow what a shot! Bill isn't a bad looking guy but shots to the face like that won't help at all!
DONNELY: Rob pulling up Bill again by the hair. Bill with a few desperation shoots to the gut of Bill as he gets pulled up! That knocks Rob back a step or two and Bill backs up slightly. Rob doubled over in pain as Bill backs up and then charges at Rob... High knee lift right to the face of Rob knocking him down and getting a cheer from the crowd.
COTE: This is it.. Bill has to get on top of him. DONNELY: Bill with a leg drop onto the chest and neck of Rob... He follows up with another quick leg drop. Rob trying to get back up.. Bill pulls him up and quickly whips him to the ropes.. Samoan drop delivered by Bill. Bill looks around and pumps his fist in the air as the fans sound their approval!
COTE: No, no, no you idiot!! Don't go to the fans, get on Rabid Rob and finish him off!! DONNELY: Bill now back over to Rob. He bends down to pick him up.. Rob reaches up.. thumb to the eye of Bill! That staggers him back as Rob crawls back up to his feet. Rob charging in with a right hand but Bill ducks...
Bill tries to respond with a right hand but Rob sidesteps that.. both men throw themselves off the ropes and charge at each other.. double clothesline!!
COTE: Both men had the same idea on that play and now the ref lays down the count! 1... 2... 3... 4... COTE: Rob is starting to stir.. I still don't see Bill moving at all! DONNELY: I think he may of landed hard on his head. 5... 6... 7... DONNELY: Rob is to his knees and now on his feet as he makes his way over to
Bill... Leaning down to pick him up.. Bill pulls him down!!! Small package and the cover!!
1... 2... COTE: NO! Rob kicked out! What a desperation move by Bandit Bill!! DONNELY: Rob springs up to his feet looking stunned and angry as he kicks at
Bill's head! COTE: The ref is trying to pull Rob off... Rob slugged him!!! The ref has been knocked out!
DONNELY: That's gotta be a DQ! COTE: I'm sure it will be as soon as the ref comes to. DONNELY: Rob now looking truly pissed here as he jumps out of the ring... COTE: He's got a chair and is going back in! Bill is to his feet as Rob charges... WHAM!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! DONNELY: What a shot!! Bill is knocked clean out as well!! Rob drops the chair and covers Bill...
COTE: No ref Robby... ya dimmed his lights! DONNELY: Rob goes over to the ref and tries to revive him... Bill slowly getting up as Rob is tugging at the ref's shirt trying to wake him up.
COTE: I think the ref is coming to as well, but Bill has the chair!! DONNELY: Rob turning around to face Bill... WHAM!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! COTE: HEY REF!! That's illegal!! DONNELY: I think the ref saw it... he's looking at both Rob and Bill... he's calling for the bell!! DING DING DING COTE: That's right! A DQ win for Rob after a chair shot to the head by Bill! EDGERIN: Ladies and gentleman, the ref has ruled this bout a DOUBLE DISQUALIFICATION [ fans boo slightly as no winner was decided ] DONNELY: Not a popular call but really no other way around it. COTE: Oh shut up.. you know as well as I do that Rob didn't deserve to lose! DONNELY: Knocking out the ref with right hand isn't reason enough to be
DQ'ed? COTE: I don't recall seeing that for some reason... DONNELY: For some reason I'm not surprised you didn't... Folks let us take you now to Justun Stopable who isn't here in the house tonight but WAS in his house when we managed to get these taped comments:
[The screen opens up to a home, a rather nice looking home with clay colored siding, two stories with windows layered on top of each other and is surrounded by woods. you can hear nothing but the sounds of nature...a high piched bird chirping, the sounds of crickets and others bugs makeing there noices, and the sounds of brussling as a light wind passes. The camera then walks toward the door as it opens up. It is Justun Stopable and this is his house.]
Justun: "Why hello. And you must be from the WFW. I've been expecting you. [He welcomes the camera into his house as you see expencive works of art, candles, and the aroma of vanella in the air. Justun motions the camera threw a hall and into a large room which looks to be a room for relaxation.
He sits down in a black leather seat and begins to speak.] Justun: "So, I should first tell you my name. I am Justun Stopable. This is my house. Yes all of the DVD's and all of the CD's, yes, are mine."
Camera Man: "So what do you plan on doing as not only a wrestler but as a
WFW wrestler? Justun: "Well look here, it's this simple. I plan on coming into this fed and simply controling it. I'm going to take every single one of these
"wrestlers", I'm going to stand in the ring with them, pin them for the
1-2-3, and carrying that Championship belt around my waist."
Camera man: "You seem to be pretty confident there Ju..." Justun: "What else is there to be when you have the wrestling capabilities as me, Justun Stopable? That's it! There is nobody in this..."
[He stops, thinks and continues.] Justun: "Nobody in the wrestling business who can beat me. So there it is. So when you're in the ring with me, be afraid because I'm "Just unstoppable." [ The camera gets up and begins to leave as it fades to black and then fades back into a shot of Shane and Clint together at the booth. ]
COTE: [ in a mocking voice ] Be afraid because he's Justun Stoppable... PLEASE! First off, who is his interior decorator? Ray Charles?? And second, how dare he stake claim to the Universal title like that!
DONNELY: Why not? Hey everyone is going to be gunning for the top prize but only two will get to compete for the honor to hold it first next month on the 27th at WFW's first Pay Per View titled fittingly enough: MAYhem!
COTE: That is going to be a huge event! The Metrodome, home of the Twins, home of the Vikings.. and yeah they both suck, but not to worry Minnesota faithful! You will at least for one night see a championship winner play in your dome!
DONNELY: Ok that's enough.. COTE: What?! What did I say??
