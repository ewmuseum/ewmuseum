Yes, WFW still only has three guys and yes World Fanatic Wrestling is still looking! We've been around for three weeks now and given the current state of affairs here in WFW things have been kinda slow. Most other feds would have given up by now but the guys that ARE here are dedicated and wanting to see WFW work.
Go to http://wfw.i85.net and take a look around. Read the FAQ, check out the two cards that has already been put on by WFW. If you like what you see then by all means join!
