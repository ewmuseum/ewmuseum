Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

World Fanatic Wrestling opened on 11 April 2001 with a spare recruitment post promising aspirations of one day becoming respectable, pointing readers to its own FAQ and application form. Three wrestlers had signed on inside the first week.

Its debut broadcast, Tuesday Night Titans, aired six days later before a packed Calgary crowd. Chief executive Art Rousseaux welcomed the arena to the promotion's own first night ahead of its first-ever match, a brawl between Rabid Rob and Bandit Bill that ended when both men turned a steel chair on each other and the referee called a double disqualification. A taped segment closed the broadcast: Justun Stopable, filmed at his own home, vowed to walk out of the promotion's ring with its still-vacant top title, timed to a pay-per-view the broadcast's own announce team billed for the following month.

That pay-per-view never aired under this name. The weeks after the debut brought only a slow trickle of new talent, its own operator conceding the pace while insisting its dedicated few were determined to see it through.

By 11 May 2001, the promotion's own co-founder confirmed it had merged into another federation, continuing under that federation's own name and address.
