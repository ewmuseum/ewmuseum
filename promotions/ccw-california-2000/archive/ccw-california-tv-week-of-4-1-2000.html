CCW TV - Week of 4/1/2000

29 March 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
ccw tv program (Airing the week of April 1st, 2000) ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- ---- (LAST WEEK AT BAY-AREA BLAST OFF:) Steven Ruth: "Karma and Marshall, Irish whip on Hostile....DOUBLE CLOTHESLINE! Hostile knocked both of them out of their boots! Hostile going after Karma! Kick to the face! OUT GOES KARMA! Dan Karma is eliminated!" Quan Shabuya: "Kevin Marshall had better toss himself over the top rope!" SR: "WHAT THE HELL! KARMA JUST CHOKE SLAMMED A REFEREE ON THE OUTSIDE! What a sore loser! Get him out of here! Hostile's setting Marshall up for the Sky-High Power Bomb! Karma is attacking referees left and right! WHAT THE HELL!!" (All of a sudden, "Crippler" Sean Davis emerges from the back, slides into the ring, and waffles Hostile in the back of the head with a baseball bat.) SR: "JEEZ! Marshall! That bastard! Davis! Hostile is staggering, but he's not down! Davis split! Hostile is tied up in the ropes! Here comes the King! Marshall's going for the kill! Kevin Marshall is going to be the champ! Look at him mocking Vinnie Hostile! He's going to clothesline him out of the ring!" QS: "What a way for this show to end! That....that....I can't say it." SR: "Marshall...." (Kevin Marshall winds up for his clothesline, bounds off the ropes....and falls out of the ring when none other than "The Predator" Jeff Monroe pulls the rope down!) SR: "THAT'S JEFF MONROE!!!" QS: "HOLY CRAP!!!" Lollie: "Oh yeah....I forgot about that, too...." SR: "MARSHALL IS OUT! MONROE JUST SMASHED A CHAIR OVER HIS HEAD! THE FANS ARE GOING BALLISTIC! AND IT'S OVER! VINNIE HOSTILE IS THE CHAMP! VINNIE HOSTILE IS THE HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPION! UNBELIEVABLE!!!" Russell: "Your winner, and NEW CCW Heavyweight champion, Vinnie Hostile!" ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- ---- (Cut to announcer's booth; Steven Ruth and Quan Shabuya are ready to kick things off.) SR: "Fans, we're here this week at San Leandro High School in San Leandro, California! We have a NEW Heavyweight champion, and his name is Vinnie Hostile! And this week, we crown a Television champion!" QS: "Some twists and turns and a few double count-outs led to a three week tournament going down in just one evening. We have the final match in the tournament, and tonight, you get to see who won the CCW TV title!" SR: "In our main event this week, Jeff Monroe, who made his triumphant return to the sport last week in San Francisco, in front of a national pay-per-view audience, takes his first step towards No Limits 3 in Oakland when he steps in the ring against "Crippler" Sean Davis!" ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- ---- (commercial) ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- ---- (Back from commercial, San Quentin and Attica are in the locker room, watching their Team Lock Down stablemates, Shawshank and manager Enzo Fugazzi, cut a promo. Attica and SQ are unmasked, with their backs to the camera.) Attica: "Ah don't know 'boutchoo, but dat punkass right there (poking his finger at the picture tube of the monitor, right in the middle of 'Shank's forehead) is in fo' a world a' hurt! Da' (explitive) he pulled at dat hoopty-ass pay-pa-view was sump'in ah can't overlook, ya' know?" SQ: "Yeah, I know, ese. I mean, it wasn't like we were tryin' to win that match or anything, holmes. To be honest, I'm just here to get all chingon on these pinche gavachos and collect my paycheck, ese." Attica: "Ah' hear ya'. Ah could give a good (expletive) 'bout a win-loss record. Dat little (explitive) Enzo can take his win at all costs an' stick it right up his ass." SQ: "So, what you want to do about 'Shank, ese?" Attica: "He gots dat tourney match dis weekend, don't he? Well, Ah say da' same type o' treatment he gave us, we give it right back ta' him." SQ: "I like the sound of that, ese. Oye, Enzo's callin' for us, time to put the masks on and 'play the game'." (The masks do indeed go on and the two men step into the camera shot and 'play the game', for now. The scene then cuts to Shawshank's pre-match promo.) Mike Perez: "We're here with Enzo Fugazzi, the BFD. Enzo, your man Shawshank has got a match tonight against Matthew Bullock, but what I want to ask you is what happened at BABO?" Fugazzi: "Nevermind that, (expletive), that's past history! I'm here to tell Matthew 'Bullocks' that this man stadning next to me is the future Television champion and you my friend are standing in his way! Huh, 'Bullocks'....do you know what that means in 'jolly ol' England'? It means 'ass', that's what that means and tonight, that's what this big old redneck is gonna kick, your ass! Tell'em 'Shank!" Shawshank: "HEY ASSHEAD!!! LET ME FILL YOU IN ON A LITTLE SECRET!!! WHEN I WAS IN THE SLAMMER, THERE WAS ASS ALL UP AND DOWN THAT CELL BLOCK!!! YEAH, REAL SWEET MEAT ATTACHED TO A (explitive)!!! TENDER AND JUICY AND RIPE FOR THE PICKIN'!!! AND DO YOU KNOW WHAT I DID TO THOSE LIL' SWEET CHEEKS RUNNIN' AROUND, LOOKIN' ALL GOOD AND (explitive)?!?!?! I DOUBLED THEIR HOLES BY STOMPIN' A SIZE 12 RIGHT ON THE CRACK!!! GODDAMN LIL' QUEERS!!! THERE WAS NO SALAD BEIN' TOSSED NEAR MAY PRIVATES AND LORD HELP ME, THERE NEVER WILL!!! WHY DID I JUST POUR MY BLACK HEART OUT TO YOU RIGHT NOW?!?!?! BECAUSE I KNOW THEM LIL' (explitive) PUSHERS GOT IT FROM!!! FROM THE 'OLD WORLD'!!! YOU KNOW, THE WORLD YOU CALL HOME SWEET HOME!!! YEAH, THAT'S RIGHT UNION JACK!!! LONDON, ENGLAND!!! YOU AND YOUR KIND BROUGHT ALL THAT 'SWISHY SWISH' OVER THE OCEAN AND GODDAMN IF IT DIDN'T SPREAD LIKE THEM QUEER'S 'BULLOCKS' IN THE PEN!!! WELL, LET ME TELL YOU ONE THING, YOU (explitive) LICKIN', NO GOOD, FUDGE PACKIN' - " Fugazzi: (Snatching away the microphone from 'Shank) "Whoa, easy big guy. OK 'Bullocks', you obviously know where Shawshank stands and if you and your little group of 'Bullocks-heads' try anything funny, (motions off camera for Attica and SQ to step into view of the shot, which they do), the rest of TEAM LOCK DOWN will have something for them as well!" (The scene cuts to the Shawshank/Bullock match mid-progress.) SR: "We're joining this first round TV title tournament match in progress! Shawshank, one of the members of Team Lock Down, taking on Matthew Bullock, the self-proclaimed "Future of CCW". We've got a wild one here, Quan Shabuya!" QS: "Matthew Bullock is the most underrated young talent in all of professional wrestling, in my opinion! But there's just something about this Shawshank guy....he looks so familiar!" SR: "Shawshank and Bullock exchanging blows on the outside. This has been a non-stop brawl for the last five minutes, the match has only seen the inside of the ring once. Shawshank with repeated chair shots to Bullock! Bullock down on his knees, and Shawshank connects with a stiff kick to the chest! Enzo Fugazzi is on the outside, shouting instructions to his masked charge." QS: "I despise managers, and why this guy thinks he needs to be at ringside is beyond me. That goes for Dr. Woychuk as well, even though he has much more class than Enzo Fugazzi!" SR: "Shawshank whips Bullock, Bullock with a reversal, Shawshank into the guardrail! Bullock slides into the ring, he's been victimized by this huge masked man from the start of the match!" QS: "Bullock needs a breather if he's going to pull this one out!" (Attica and San Quentin storm out from the back and attack Matthew Bullock.) SR: "Team Lock Down just hit the ring! They're attacking Matt Bullock! San Quentin just DDT'd referee Pete Zolo! Here comes Kevin Thibault! The Canadian Kamikaze, climbing the ropes, missle dropkick on San Quentin! Shawshank cuts him off! Clothesline by Shawshank!" QS: "And look on the outside! Enzo Fugazzi with a cheap shot from behind on Dr. Dane Woychuk!" SR: "Attica and San Quentin just hit Matthew Bullock with a double-team neckbreaker bomb! Emzo Fugazzi's trying to round up his troops, referees and officials have poured out from the back, this match will be thrown out! Team Lock Down attacking the officials! Someone get these guys under control!" (Team Lock Down continues beating on anyone they can get their hands on until a gang of uniformed "corrections officers" make their way out with handcuffs and clubs.) QS: "Here come the gangbusters! The CO's are here to save the day!" SR: "I don't believe this, but Shawshank, San Quentin, and Attica are being cuffed and shackled! This is totally unprecedented! I have never seen anything like this!" QS: "Then you haven't been to Folsom on conjugal visit day, have you?" SR: "I....can't say that I have. It's official: Shawshank and Matt Bullock are both out of the tournament, which means Super Sexy has a berth in the finals!" ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- ---- (commercial) ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- ---- (Reurning from commercial, promos are shown for the No Limits 3 pay-per-view, as well as upcoming TV tapings in Pacifica, Lompoc and Healdsburg, and brand-spankin'-new CCW merchandise.) SR: "Welcome back. The CCW TV title tournament was five matchs full of chaos. Two of those matches ended in no contests. One of them, the war you just witnessed between Shawshank of Team Lock Down and Matthew Bullock. And another battle between Thunderstrike Kid and "Mr. Gasworks" Marvin Harris, which saw Heavyweight champion Vinnie Hostile make his presence felt as, in the midst of their brawl, TSK and Harris broke into the bathroom that Hostile was, well, using. TSK paid for it big time, and the match was ruled a double-countout." QS: "I think Thunderstrike Kid smelled what The Vinnie was cooking!" (Steve Ruth looks at Shabuya for several long, uneasy seconds.) QS: "WHAT?!" SR: "And rounding out the finals was Freak, who scored an upset victory over Tom "Da Bomb" Domi when an injured Matthew Bullock came back out for one last attack on his arch-rival." (Cut to the Domi/Freak tournament match, where Matthew Bullock smacked Tom Domi with a lead pipe while valet Serena Yanamoto distracted the referee. Bullock then hits his "End Game" finisher on Freak and lays Freak on top of Domi just in time for the ref to count the fall.) SR: "CCW promoters ordered that the finals take place that very night, as opposed to next week. Super Sexy and Freak, for the Television title, we will bring you that match right after this!" ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- ---- (commercial) ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- ---- (Returning from commercial, Freak is on his way to the ring. Ring announcer Shannon Russell is making the introductions.) Russell: "....and is for the CCW Television championship! Introducing first, from Southtown, California, weighing in at 265 lbs., Freak!" SR: "We are moments away from crowning a CCW TV champion! Freak is still reeling from the effects of the End Game, which he felt first-hand at the hands of Matthew Bullock!" QS: "At least he's doing better than Tom Domi, who might have to suck his meals through a straw for the next month!" ("Hero Of The Day" by Metallica starts playing as Super Sexy makes his way to the ring.) Russell: "And his opponent, from Pasadena, California, weighing in at 268 lbs., Super Sexy!" QS: "This is going to be a toss-up. Neither one of these men fits the description of the typical wrestler. Super Sexy is another in a long line of muscleheads, while Freak is....well, a freak!" SR: "Freak just jumped onto Super Sexy with a plancha! This match is underway!" (The bell rings.) SR: "Freak just took a steel chair from a fan at ringside! He waffles Super Sexy with it! Super Sexy is down on his back, just seconds into this match!" QS: "This may be Super Sexy's undoing. At the pay-per-view, he fought a smaller, less powerful technical wrestler, and won easily. Here, he's up against someone about his size, who's crazy enough to climb on the apron and JUMP ONTO SUPER SEXY WITH THE CHAIR! WOW!" SR: "Devastating high-risk move by Freak! He has to realize that this isn't a falls-count-anywhere match, he'd better get in the ring!" (Freak works some ringside fans, threatening to hit them with the steel chair. Super Sexy struggles to get to his feet.) SR: "Freak slides in the ring long enough to break the count. Goes back after Super Sexy, front facelock, shove-off by Sexy! Freak charges, back bodydrop by Sexy! No protective mats here in CCW! Sexy picks him up, handfull of hair, smashes Freak into the ringsteps! Head-first! Kick to the ribs, Freak retreating now. Super Sexy follows him in. Here comes Super Sexy! Freak just nailed him with the ring bell!" QS: "He lied in wait for Super Sexy to round the corner, then made him eat the ding!" SR: "Freak back in the ring, he has a chair. Freak sets up the chair....Freak isn't much of a high-flyer, but he'll throw his body anywhere, as long as it inflicts pain! Freak standing on the chair...." (Freak leaps off the chair and does a 180-degree turn in mid-air, landing head-first onto Super Sexy.) SR: "HOLY FREAKIN' (expletive)!!" (The crowd picks up a "Holy Shit!" chant.) QS: "If Freak didn't break his neck doing that, I'll be totally shocked!" SR: "He's moving, he's trying to get to his feet, and Super Sexy is coughing up blood! Let's see that again on replay - Freak dives down and connects skull-to-stomach on the prone Super Sexy! UNBELIEVABLE!" (Both wrestlers remain on the floor recovering from that last spot as the ref initiates a slow ten-count.) QS: "What happens if we have a double countout? Who wins the TV title?" SR: "I....I don't know! This beats all I've ever seen! Freak is finally getting back to his feet, but Super Sexy looks to be done for! Picks up Super Sexy, throws him back into the ring, here comes Freak. This match is over! Super Sexy is done! Cover, ONE! TWO! HE GOT A SHOULDER UP! I DON'T BELIEVE IT! Freak is livid! Grabs the steel chair, referee Brad Gastwirt yanks it away!" QS: "Bad move, Brad! You do not want to piss this guy off!" SR: "Freak argues with referee Gastwirt, from behind is Super Sexy! ONE! TWO! ALMOST GOT HIM WITH A SCHOOL-BOY! Sexy to his feet, right hand! Another one! Picks up Freak, and a bodyslam! Super Sexy is just totally out of steam! He clutches his stomach, the blood continues to pour! I'm sorry, but the ref needs to stop this match!" QS: "Super Sexy is literaly vomiting blood! He's suffered some serious internal injuries!" SR: "Freak with a kick to the midsection. Another kick! He keeps kicking! Vicious boots to the stomach and chest! He's trying to further the damage! Sexy grabs a foot! Dragon screw! Whips Freak's leg out from under him! Sexy rolls away, Freak tackles Super Sexy, Sexy turns it into a pin! ONE! TWO! Freak gets out! Freak clawing Sexy's face now, there isn't much defense for that! Picks him up, Irish whip, off the ropes, Sexy ducks the cross body! Freak back to his feet, Sexy nails a lariat! Down goes Freak! Pick up, Freak fires with a punch, front facelock, Sexy hits an uppercut! Front facelock by Super Sexy, SEX APPEAL! He hit the neckbreaker! ONE! TWO! THREE! HE DID IT! HE DID IT! HE DID IT!" Russell: "Here is your winner, and NEW CCW Television champion, Super Sexy!" QS: "That has to be the greatest show of resiliency I've ever seen!" SR: "Super Sexy has shocked us all!" ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- ---- (commercial) ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- ---- (Returning from commercial, another series of ads for upcoming shows, No Limits 3, and CCW merchandise.) SR: "Fans, we want to keep you updated on the condition of new TV champion Super Sexy. Super Sexy suffered a minor internal laceration. We have not yet received word on the severity of this injury, and it is still up in the air as to whether or not Super Sexy will be healed by next Sunday to defend his TV title." "In addition to Super Sexy's incredible victory, we also got our first look at Vinnie Hostile as the CCW Heavyweight champion! It took Vinnie Hostile less than two minutes to destroy Thunderstrike Kid, who made a challenge to the champion after the bathroom stall incident during the TV title match! Remember, fans, if you want all the latest CCW news and results, check out our website at crash.to/ccwrestling !" ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- ---- (commercial) ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- ---- (Back from commercial, we see "Crippler" Sean Davis in the ring.) SR: "It's time for our main event on tonight's broadcast! "Crippler" Sean Davis is in the ring, and he's set to take on his opponent!" ("Sad But True" by Metallica starts playing as Jeff Monroe makes his way to the ring. The fans begin to spaz out for The Predator.) QS: "The fans are going ballistic for Jeff Monroe! I haven't seen anyone get this kind of reception since Lucy Lawless sang the National Anthem at a Anaheim Mighty Ducks game!" SR: "O....K, Davis just attacked Monroe on the outside! Davis punching away at The Predator! Head into the guardrail! Sean Davis wants to take control early! Davis with a stiff kick, pick-up and a bodyslam on the concrete floor! Elbowdrop by Davis! Davis grabs the legs, Boston crab? No! Monkey flip, Monroe lands neck-first against the guardrail! Davis has a chair...." QS: "I think the referee might be afraid to try to bring order to this match!" SR: "Sean Davis with a stiff chair shot to Monroe's back! Jeff Monroe is getting his head handed to him by the Crippler! Davis is picking up a guardrail! He has a piece of the guardrail! Throws it on top of The Predator! Sean Davis is a madman!" QS: "Sean Davis is a mercenary for Kevin Marshall! Davis doesn't care if he wins, loses, gets counted-out, gets disqualified, he has one mission and one mission only: destroy Jeff Monroe at all costs!" SR: "Davis choking Monroe with the guardrail. Monroe has had a lot of steam taken out of him! Davis drops a knee across Monroe's ribs. Sean Davis is beating the ever-loving crap out of Jeff Monroe!" QS: "And he's letting the fans know it, too!" SR: "Davis is showboating, this could be a mistake. Jawing with the fans, that kid might want to take a few steps back, because Davis would knock his block off just as soon as he'd knock off Monroe's!" QS: "Look out!" SR: "Low blow from Monroe! Monroe with right hands! Big right hook from The Predator! Davis dumped out into the crowd! Monroe follows him, on top of the guardrail! Jumps off with the clothesline! Monroe took out Davis, and about four fans who didn't get out of the way fast enough!" QS: "Boy, those expensive seats are really worth it, huh Steve?" SR: "Monroe picks up Davis....POWER BOMB OVER THE GUARDRAIL! He picked up Sean Davis and power bombed him over the guardrail onto the concrete floor!" (Monroe flips himself over the guardrail and lands on Davis with a senton.) SR: "UNBELIEVABLE! Monroe has fired back! Monroe picks up Davis, they're headed for the ring! Monroe tosses Davis inside, follows him in, Davis stomps Monroe as he slides in the ring. Pick up, butterfly, takes Monroe over with a suplex. Monroe holds on! ONE! TWO! Davis barely escapes! Smart move by Monroe!" QS: "I have never seen that! Jeff Monroe grasped his hands together and locked Davis' arms in the suplex, and nearly pulled off the pin!" SR: "Davis viciously attacks Monroe with a series of kicks! Picks him up, Irish whip, sick clothesline! Cover, ONE! TWO! Monroe kicks out! Davis back on the attack, picks up Monroe, drops him with a stomach breaker! Davis locks on a STF! Monroe is in the middle of the ring!" QS: "I have never seen Jeff Monroe tap out before! This could be a first, because I've never seen anyone escape Davis' STF!" SR: "Monroe is pulling towards the ropes with his hands! He's pulling Davis with him! What a show of strength! Davis releases the hold! Kick to Monroe's knee! Picks up the leg, and smashes it against the mat! Davis, looking for a figure four, kick off by Monroe! Monroe to his feet, Davis charges, hiptoss by Monroe! Davis up again, spears Monroe! Cover! ONE! TWO! NO SIR!" QS: "Most men would not be breathing after getting hit in the gut with one of those!" SR: "Jeff Monroe has had all the wind knocked out of him, Davis picks up The Predator. Waistlock, belly to belly....Monroe blocks, nails him! Belly to belly suplex! Monroe to his feet! Arm drag and twist on Davis, ARMBREAKER! Monroe's working on the arm! Butterfly by Monroe, pick up, drops him on his shoulder! Monroe calls that the Shoulder Crusher! Pulls him in the middle of the ring! THERE IT IS! PREDATOR ARMBAR!" QS: "This one's over! NO ONE escapes the Predator Armbar!" SR: "DAVIS TAPS OUT! DAVIS TAPPED! MONROE WINS! THERE'S DAN KARMA! THERE'S THE KING!" ("King" Kevin Marshall, Jester, Dan Karma and "Nature Boy" John Quadro hit the ring and attack Jeff Monroe.) SR: "It's a five-on-one attack! Marshall has the Royal Pain locked on! Karma nails a legdrop! We're out of time! Someone get help for Jeff Monroe!" (Copyright 2000 - 4:20 Productions)
