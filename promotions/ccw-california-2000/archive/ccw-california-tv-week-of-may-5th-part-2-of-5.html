California Championship Wrestling CCW TV Week of May 5th, 2000 Taped in San Rafael http://ccw.50megs.com =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= SR: "Fans, another championship was determined tonight, as the vacant Television title was up for grabs in a four-way elimination match! Let's hear comments from some of the participants!" (The scene opens in the back after No Limits 3, and "The Teen Idol" Thunderstrike Kid is standing next to a soda machine, and drinking a (Censor bar: blocking the product name). He is wearing his gear, yellow pants with black and white thunderbolts down the side, and black shoulderstraps with white lining. He looks content with his win, but still bothered by something. He is staring into space, and as he notices the camera, he pushes his sunglasses down, to stare into the camera as he talks.) TSK: "Tonight, I beat one of the "greatest high flyers in the world". Well, you know, I proved that a technician can beat a flyer any day of the week. Clint, with all due respect, you are a great competitor, but you aren't even on my level. I am one of the greatest technicians in the world, and not only that, I can fly just as good as a Cruiserweight." (He takes another sip of his soda, and continues to talk.) TSK: "Some may ask where Lou Zer and Guy Masters are. Well, Lou is already gone, he went to the club, and Guy is looking for Martinez, and going to fix this mix-up, and make sure it's known it was inadvertant." (He pauses and starts to drift back into a trance, but he comes back in a few seconds.) TSK: "Now, as for this Four Way Dance for the CCW Television Title. Everyone knows, even the idiotic fans of San Rafael know, that I am supposed to be the Television Champion. I was screwed out of a title reign because I went to a double count out with Marvin Harris. That is a load of sh-t! Speaking of sh-t, let's talk about my competition. First of all, there is Brian Patterson. This man doesn't quit, does he? When I was smaller, and wieghed 224.8 lbs., I beat him TWICE! Now that I'm a little heavier, and more experienced, I can take him out before he has a chance to bitch about it!" (He sighs before going on.) TSK: "Of course, there is Marvin Harris. Everyone knows the history. I beat him FAIR AND SQUARE, then we had a rematch, and went to a double count out, and once more we fought, and someone came out of the audience, and hit me with a bowling ball. I'm still in shock from that shot. I wake up screaming at night from nightmares of being a bowling pin, and more and more bowling balls come at me! I had to go to see a shrink to help me out with my emotional trauma!" (He blatantly fakes some fear, and continues to talk.) TSK: "Finally, there is Super Sexy. This guy is truly tasteless. He thinks he's a ladies man, some kind of Romeo, a womanizer. The problem is, he may wear the pants in relationships he gets in, but only when he's told he can! This guy can barely take the initiation to CCW, AWF, NBSWF, SMeW, and other federations... so how can he take on the GREATEST wrestler in the world?" (TSK laughs arrogantly, and takes another sip of his soda.) TSK: "You see, I'm not worried! I KNOW that I have what it takes to be the CCW Television Champion within FIVE MINUTES... but I'll play with them a little, have a little fun before I get what I deserve, the first step in my climbing of the CCW ladder! This will happen, even if my opponents watch out for when THUNDER STRIKES!" (TSK picks his leather jacket up off the ground, and walks off camera, but the camera follows him. TSK stops at his locker room door, where he threatens the cameraman, most of it not being able to be shown on TV. The scene fades as TSK approaches the camera.) (cut to the ring, and an interview with Super Sexy) (The scene opens with a CCW arena buzzing with anticipation. There is a sea of signs supporting an array of wrestlers. Spotlights are swinging around throughout the arena. Then, "Hero of the Day" by Metallica plays over the loudspeaker. Some of the fans jump to their feet in admiration and others boo. Super Sexy doesn't waste any time running down the aisle and to the ring. He rolls in under the bottom rope and grabs the mic out of the corner.) Super: "Where the hell is MY TV title. Harris, you son of a bitch. You know that you did not win that title. Without the interference of the entire state of California, you can't come within ten nautical miles of beating me. AJ Martinez, who the hell do you think you are? Lollie drops into a coma, and you think you can just come out and vacate the TV title. Well, son that is MY title. So I suggest you come out and give it to me. But I know that's not going to happen. You want me to fight for it. Well, lucky for you, I like to fight. I don't care if I have to go through one, two, three or three hundred men to get that title. That is exactly what I am going to do. Martinez, just make sure your little bitch ass is there to hand my title back to me once I have eliminated those other three twits. And you know who I blame for that piece of @#%$ thing we called a match on Sunday? I blame you idiots sitting out there in the stands." (The entire crowd goes into a tremendous boo as Super climbs to the second turnbuckle to soak it all in.) Super: "That's right. I blame each and every one of you. You want to see all kinds of people running in and costing YOUR hero a match. And that's what the suits see. And they send then entire roster out to try to upend me. Somehow, that bastard Harris wound up with a pin on me when it was all said and done. But I guarantee you that will NOT be the case this Sunday. Why, you ask? Because I am just that damn good, that's why. That's all there is to it. No one will be able to beat me, not Brian Patterson, not Thunderstrike Kid, and certainly not that resident piece of @#%$ "Mr. Gasworks" Marvin Harris. Oh yea, and not you nitwit fans out there watching." (The crowd continues to boo. But Super seems to like it. He paces around soaking in his reception before continuing.) Super: "Brian Patterson, where the hell did you come from? You think you can just walk in here and try to mess up my match? Big mistake bitch. You don't come in and mess with me and expect to get away with it. And now, you are going to be in the same ring with me on Sunday. You don't have to act tough. Deep down inside, I know you are scared. I know you are shaking in your boots at the though of having to be in the same wrestling ring with someone with such greatness as myself. I am the inaugural TV Champion, and the ONLY TV Champion here in the CCW. Harris can THINK that he is or was the champion, but he's not fooling anyone. Patterson, I am warning you, do NOT get in my way Sunday. If you do, bad things will happen. Don't think you are going to be immune to the pain I will inflict. Because it will just mean a bigger ass whipping for you." (Super pulls a bottle of water out of his pocket and chugs about half of it. The other half, he tosses into the crowd. There, a couple of hypocrites who had just booed Super fight over the 'souvenir.' Super rolls his eyes and continues.) Super: "Next, I am brought to the Thunderdyke Kid. TSK, don't go claiming things that you dream boy. You are not the greatest wrestler here in the CCW. That title belongs to me. How long are you going to cry about the double countout loss to Harris? I mean, that one is your own stupidity. Its no one's fault but your own that you don't know how to climb back into the ring. So, do us all a favor and SHUT THE HELL UP! Enough bitching. If you are one thirty eighth as good as you think you are, go out to the ring and prove it instead of spewing the @#%$ out of your mouth that no one believes anyway. You think you are the deserving TV Champion? Come on out on Sunday and try to prove that. I promise you, it will not be as easy as you think it will be. Those other two twits may just roll over for you. But I for one sure as hell will not. You are going to have to earn every spot against me. But just face it, when it all comes down to it, I will remain YOUR Television Champion. Prepare to have your neck snapped Thunderdyke." (Super walks around and soaks in some more boos from all his 'adoring' fans.) Super: "Now that brings me to the man who THINKS he beat me on Sunday, Marvin Harris. Harris, you may have fluked out and gotten the pin. But that is because everyone in attendance interfered. Among all the confusion, you wound up with a pin on me. The right thing to do would be to just hand me that title. But Martinez is too much of a little bitch to do that. So I will have to beat three asses at one time. That is something I have no problem with. Harris, you will see what I am all about. There will be four men in the ring, but I am going to take it upon myself to just straight up kick your ass. Harris, you are mine. That's all there is to it. I am going to eliminate those other two jackoffs, but I am going to obliterate you. I am going to hit you with a Sex Appeal and then pick you up and do it again and again just for good measure and to make you suffer. I am going to make sure that you wind up in the hospital. On Sunday, I will get my TV title back where it belongs. And you three will feel the Sex Appeal." (With that, "Hero of the Day" hits and Super climbs out of the ring to a chorus of boos. He slowly walks back up the aisle soaking in all the admiration.) (cut to a pre-taped promo featuring "Mr. Gasworks" Marvin Harris) (The screen fades in and we're in the backstage area of an arena at the first House Show, post No Limits 3. The Gasman himself, Marvin Harris, leads a cameraman hurriedly into the deeper alcoves of the maintenance area. In the background we can hear the faint sounds of yelling, but the voices and words are unintelligible. After travelling a good minute Marvin stops the cameraman aside and begins to explain..) Marvin: "Let me make this short and sweet, Alice..*I* won that TV title fair and square as I could possibly make it..Nobody I know came down and 'helped' the Gasman win..Thunderstrike Jackass and his cronies came down and whooped on you, because *I* cost him his shot at the title..Go figure..Then these two P.N. News wannabes start jumping me from outta nowhere!? Is it just bad karma or am I getting more than my fair share of enemies? Don't think it ends with the match folks..When those security guys came and pried *MY* title out of Sickman Fraud's old wrinkly hands he was pissed to the thirtieth-degree, and I KNEW he was gonna wanna beat my (explicit) for old times sake..So, right now I'm on the run from him and Temporary Insanity 2K.." Taylor: "Not for long you useless sack of (explicit)!" (Suddenly, Marvin is hit from behind firmly with the business end of a steel chair and topples to the ground in a heap. Of course this is followed up numerous boots to the back as Sickman Fraud and both members of Temporary Insanity 2000 come into the camera's view..In the background Brad Thompson looks on, shaking his head in disagreement. They continue the infliction of punishment on the Gasman for another good moment before they kick him aside, and Prof. Fraud addresses the cameraman..) Fraud: "Alright..GOD DAMMIT!..There I've vented the remainder of my excess aggression, and I can now proceed with a recapping of my team's performance at No Limits 3..Does it not surprise and SHOCK anyone else that the only one among us who pulled off a 'clean' victory was that sad excuse for a human being behind me who thinks he's the godhead of small rodents? Harris here, I was expecting him to (explicit) up royally, but you two!?! My pet project, don't tell me your going to meet the fate of your predecessors so soon?!..Explainations..NOW!" (Steve Winters quietly backs away into the background, motioning David Taylor forward towards the professor. Taylor gulps, and then stands at attention in front of Fraud.) Taylor: "Sir, we had the situation firmly under our control. We did not expect the sudden appearance of the three unidentified saboteurs that DIRECTLY cost us the match, sir..I can assure you that come this Sunday we will SEEK AND DESTORY the jerk-off's who did it, and still have the wind left to put down our competition in this round of the tag tourney in short order, sir.." Fraud: "Pretty words, you militarized Charlton Heston clone..You better live up to them, or you'll be the next to meet Mr. Harris' fate..DO YOU UNDERSTAND!?!" Taylor: "SIR, YES SIR!" (With that Fraud nodded and turned his attentions on so far quiet Brad Thompson, he approached calmly, and then back-handed him hard across the face..Once Thompson recovered it looked like he was about to cold-@#%$ the Professor, but of course the two members of Temporary Insanity were standing by and held him back..) Taylor: "Now that, felt liberating..Don't think I didn't hear you SNICKERING as I was having an emotional breakdown backstage after No Limits 3, Braddy boy..Now then, before I get the notion to do somemore 'venting', I'd advise you tell me just what your going to do to 'Christopher Creed'.." Thompson: "Well, gee Professor..I'm going to do to that ingrate newbian EXACTLY what I'm going to do to you when I get my hands on your scrawny Caribbean schooled neck..I will effectively show him the FURY of the man-hamster..Seij Tankian felt it, smelled it, LIVED IT!..He will never be the same again..Chrishy-bo-bissy best PRAY to anyone who'll listen that I'm not on the same rabies medicine Sunday night that I was at No Limits 3..I'm not an evil man, I just get subtle pleasure from PUNISHING the grown versions who torture my CHOSEN speices..Thats all I have to say, now let me go, and get Marvin an ambulance.." Marvin: "No..I'm okay now..Just let me say a few more things about my upcoming four-way elimination match this Sunday, and then I'll check myself back into the mental trauma's ward at the local Emergency Room.." Fraud: "Thats the little trooper I almost respect in you, Marvin..Go on get me overly psyched with more pretty words before I have Mr. Taylor bash your skull in again..Trust me, you'll need more insurance than you can currently afford if that happens, Marvin.." Marvin: "Right..Let me first say, that I'm sad I didn't get to SHUT Super Sexy's big 'vaccum cleaner in reverse' mouth up when I had the chance..This Sunday he is MY top priority on the (explicit) beating list..Second being TSK, he's STILL whining..Am I the only TV title competitor that isn't belly-aching and bitching up being cheated?! My ultimatum to ALL three of my opponents is this..I AM THE GASMAN..I WILL BEAT THE HELL OUT OF EACH AND EVERY ONE OF YOU..AND I WILL KEEP IT CLEAN!!!.." (After finishing his yelling directly into the camera lens, Marvin's eyes sudden roll up into the back of his head and he collapses backwards. With a simple hand motion Steve Winters grabs ahold of Marvin's left foot and drags him away as the entire of Group Therapy march off too the nearest bus stop..With that, we fade to black..) (cut to the ring, where Mike Perez awaits The New Compton Posse) (Scene opens with CCW interview guy Mike Perez standing by just an ordinary wall. He paces back and forth awaiting his next guests...who they are is unknown at this time...) Mike Perez: "Okay...I'm giving them thirty more seconds before I just walk out. Let them cut their own promo for all I care." (Just then, we see Brian Patterson, Jules Blackwell and Gangsta Ray saunter onto the scene. They approach Perez and circle around him.) Jules: "Well, well, well...look at mister fancy ass here! He's got himself a camera and mic with not one cracka to interview!" Ray: "Hey, you guys thinkin' what I'm thinkin'?" Brian: "That we toss his ass outta here and cut our own promo?" Jules: "Yeah, let's toss this bitch's ass outta here...on one, two...THREE!" (We then see Brian and Ray rush Mike, begin pumelling away on him and give him a boot in the ass. Ray then picks up the mic and looks directly at the cameraman, who tries to run away...) Ray: "You ain't goin anywhere...cause you got a new job with...POSSE-VISION! And I am the MASTER blaster of ceremonies Gangsta Ray, and with me here is Brian Patterson and the baddest motherfu-..." Brian: "SHUT YOUR MOUTH, SUCKA! Now, it is GREAT to be back here in the CCW...and after working all of the smarts and turning them into marks, it FELT GOD-F**KING-DAMN good to beat the sh*t outta that walking spokesman for GALA, Stupid Sexy! Hey, Sexy boy, remember what Jules and I did to you LAST time we crossed paths after you humbled...nay...EMBARRESSED my man Crazy Canuck! This Sunday, I am GONNA avenge him by not only beating your Scott Steiner-like 'I AM NOT GAY! I LIKE TO F**K WOMEN!' ass, but I intend on takin' the ONE THING you seem to like that...heh...spankin' Little Sexy...and that is the TV Title!
