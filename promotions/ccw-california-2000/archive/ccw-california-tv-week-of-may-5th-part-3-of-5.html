CCW TV: Week Of May 5th (Part 3 of 5)

2 May 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
California Championship Wrestling CCW TV Week of May 5th, 2000 Taped in San Rafael http://ccw.50megs.com =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= SR: "Fans, we kicked off the CCW Tag Team title tournament this week in San Rafael with four first round match-ups!" (clips from each of the matches are shown as Ruth and Shabuya continue to speak.) SR: "In the first match, Tom Domi and Clint Halford, in their first match as a tag team, pulled off what could only be called an upset, defeating The Converted, with Tobias J. Smith!" QS: "Then it was the Midnight Revolution making a fantastic debut against Team Lock Dowm, who once again self-destructed mid-way through the match, cosing Enzo Fuggazzi another win!" SR: "Match number three saw Temporary Insanity 2000 beat The Brooklyn Boyz, Theo-X and Gamberro Despiadado, and after the match, the Boyz got punked by The New Compton Posse!" QS: "The Posse obviously felt bad about getting slighted from the tournament, but no one felt worse at the end of the night than Sean Fox and Angus McCracken, who got their asses handed to them by "Crippler" Sean Davis and Dan Karma!" SR: "The quarterfinals and semifinals matches will take place next week, Sunday, May 7th, in Merced at the Golden Valley High School! Great seats for that show are still available, and you just might wind up on TV! And of course, the winners of the semifinals match will go on to Gold Rush 2000, Sunday night, May 14th from Kesar Pavilion in San Francisco, where they will face The Suicide Kings for the CCW Tag Team titles!" QS: "The Suicide Kings, by virtue of their win in the four-way elimination match at No Limits 3, are the number one contenders to the tag team titles, and the road to the belts goes through them!" SR: "Coming up next, a four-way elimination match for the CCW Television title!" (commercial) (returning from commercial, announcer Shannon Russell is in the ring) Russell: "Ladies and gentlemen, this contest is a four-way elimination match for the CCW Television title!" ("Hero Of The Day" by Metallica plays as Super Sexy makes his way to the ring to the disapproving boos of the crowd.) Russell: "Introducing first, from Pasadena, California, weighing in at 268 pounds, Super Sexy!" SR: "This time last week, Super Sexy was the CCW TV champion! Now he's looking to regain a title he feels was stolen from him!" QS: "Hey, he got pinned! There's no arguing that fact!" (Sexy's music ceases, and "Hang Ten" by Edwin starts playing as Brian Patterson comes out.) Russell: "His opponent, from Whistler, British Columbia, Canada, weighing in at 224 pounds, "The Loose Cannon" Brian Patterson!" QS: "Patterson's hesitant to get into the ring!" (Patterson's music is cut, and "The Ghost Of Tom Joad" by Rage Against The Machine plays as Thunderstrike Kid runs out and dropkicks Patterson from behind.) SR: "TSK just attacked Brian Patterson from behind!" (Shannon Russell high-tails it out of the ring, as does Super Sexy. Sexy attacks Patterson and TSK on the outside.) QS: "They're going at it on the outside, but where's the Gasman? Where's Marvin Harris?" ("Jeremy" by Pearl Jam plays over the speakers, and the fans pop as "Mr. Gasworks" Marvin Harris runs down, steel chair in hand, and goes to work on the trio outside the ring.) SR: "Here we go! Harris is here, we've got a foursome on the outside! We've got a brawl for the TV title! Harris levels Patterson with the chair! TSK and Super Sexy, Sexy goes into the ringpost! Harris is in the ring! The fans are going nuts! This is an elimination match, the last man standing will be the champion! Patterson into the ring, and in comes TSK, now we're getting in the ring. Now we're gonna really start this thing. Lock up, into the corner, will we see a clean break from TSK? Yeah....yeah, TSK, clean break, Super Sexy and Marvin Harris now going at it on the outside." QS: "The referee is gonna have a very tough time keeping this one under control. Maybe he can keep order with the two in the ring, but there's very little he can do about the action on the outside." SR: "This is supposed to be tag-in format, two in the ring, two on the apron. Sexy and Harris brawling on the outside, Harris with a scoop slam, drops an elbow! In the ring, Patterson with a cross body, COVER, ONE! TWO! Two only. Thunderstrike kicks out. Patterson picks up TSK, going for a suplex, textbook vertical suplex by Brian Patterson. Patterson with a headlock, getting a rest while wearing down on TSK, rendering him unable to breathe. Marvin Harris just got sent into the ring steps! Back in the ring, Patterson whips TSK into the ropes, TSK ducks the clothesline, TSK with a flying chop! Patterson quick to his feet, TSK with another chop! Patterson to his feet, TSK takes him down with a clothesline! TSK with a leglock on Patterson. Back to the outside, Harris just DDT'd Super Sexy! Marvin Harris, former TV champion, stomping away on another former TV champion! The TV title is on the line! Four men for the gold! TSK is working on Patterson's leg in the ring! The referee is not counting those men out! TSK picks up Patterson, Irish whip, Patterson grabs the ropes, avoids the dropkick! TSK hit the mat HARD! Marvin Harris grabs at Patterson's ankle! Patterson off the ropes, plancha! Marvin Harris moved....Brian Patterson connects with Super Sexy...." QS: "He's hurt, Steve. That....didn't...." SR: "I think he hit knee-to-head with Sexy....and Patterson's head, let's see it on replay." (The replay from a different camera angle shows Brian Patterson's knee connecting square with Super Sexy's head, and Patterson's head hitting the steel guardrail.) SR: "That....definetly....we need help out here." QS: "Yeah. Patterson miscued that one badly. He was going for Marvin Harris, I believe, and...." SR: "Yeah. This match has just kinda stopped suddenly here, Brian Patterson is busted open, and Super Sexy is out cold. Here come the paramedics, and TSK just attacked Marvin Harris from behind! The Gasman stopped to show some sympathy for Sexy and Patterson, and TSK jumps him!" QS: "Here comes a stretcher." SR: "The stretcher, yeah....TSK and Harris are in the ring, the ref is back on the job, Harris slams TSK. Harris with a pick up, and a backbreaker, nicely done. I'm sorry I'm not more into it, but Super Sexy....is he still out?" QS: "I think so, Steve. Wait, hey, if you don't mind, I'm gonna go over there. Are you cool on your own?" SR: "Absolutley. Go ahead." QS: "Lemme see what's up." (Quan Shabuya takes off his headset and walks over to the accident scene.) SR: "We gotta take a break...." ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- ---- (commercial)
