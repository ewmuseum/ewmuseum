CCW TV - Week of May 12th, 2000 (Part 1 of 5)

12 May 2000

California Championship Wrestling CCW TV Week of May 12th, 2000 Taped in Merced http://ccw.50megs.com =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= (The show kicks off in the ring, in the middle of a match between "Wyld" Doug Wylson and "The Prophet" Daniel Jurin.) Steven Ruth: "....Jurin with a nice headscissors takedown! Very impressive move by the newcomer! Thank you, fans, for tuning into CCW TV! I'm Steven Ruth, along side my tag team partner, the Living Legend, Quan Shabuya, and we are watching the deubt of 'The Prophet' Daniel Jurin, he's taking on 'Wyld' Doug Wylson, and is well in control!" Quan Shabuya: "Jurin is really wowing the crowd here in Merced, he's coming here with a fabulous high-flying effort, and is showing that he's definite CCW material!" SR: "Daniel Jurin with a clothesline off of the Irish whip. Jurin sits Wylson up, off the ropes, dropkick to the back of the head! Jurin steps out, between the ropes, springboard, legdrop! Awesome move by Jurin! Goes for the cover, but Wylson is in the ropes. Picks up Wylson, scoop and a slam. Jurin, drags Wylson, into position, this could be it....Jurin climbs the ropes, this is his move...." (Jurin hits a moonsault on Wylson, landing flat on his body, completely parallel with Wylson.) SR: "THAT'S THE SHROUD OF JURIN! And this match is over!" Shannon Russell: "HERE IS YOUR WINNER, DANIEL JURIN!" QS: "Very impressive debut by Daniel Jurin, and that finisher is spectacular. He lands flat on Wylson after getting about ten feet in the air with that moonsault!" SR: "What a way to kick off CCW TV!" (show open) (cut to Ruth and Shabuya at ringside) SR: "Fans, welcome to CCW TV! I'm Steve Ruth, Quan Shabuya doing color, and we're here in beautiful Merced, California, and look at our main event! This has the potential to be one of the biggest matches in the brief history of this television show. "King" Kevin Marshall, the number one contender to the Heavyweight title, the man who will face Vinnie Hostile for that belt this Sunday at Gold Rush 2000, will wrestle Super Sexy, the former Television champion!" QS: "Super Sexy has nothing to lose and EVERYTHING to gain in this match! If he can beat the number one contender tonight, look at the position that vaults him into! He will have every right to ask for a Heavyweight title shot if he can beat Marshall! And if Kevin Marshall loses, then how does he go into his match at Gold Rush? He goes into that match on a losing streak, having lost to someone who he feels is beneath him, and he's easy pickings for Vinnie Hostile!" SR: "Also on this show, we will see the semifinals of the Tag Team title tournament. Here in Merced, Temporary Insanity 2000 and the unlikely duo of Clint Halford and Tom Domi moved on, past their quarterfinal matches, into the semis, and the winners go to San Francisco next week at Gold Rush 2000, and take on The Suicide Kings for the titles! But now, you just saw him win his first match here in CCW, lets go to an interview with Daniel Jurin!" (Daniel Jurin, having just won his debut match against Doug Wylson, walks through the back of the arena as the camera follows him.) Jurin: "It is written in the Book of Jurin that the first to fall will be one of strong character, but meek talent. Doug Wylson was covered with the Shroud Of Jurin, and he shall not be the last. All those who oppose the Prophet shall fall at the will of Pookong. I am the prelude, Omega comes soon. I am Alpha, the final chapter shall soon be written. Fear my word, heed my warning. The Prophet has spoken!" (Cut back to the announcer's desk; Steve Ruth and Quan Shabuya look at each other with blank, slightly frightened looks on their faces.) SR: "....Here's some news on Gold Rush 2000!" (Cut to a promo for Gold Rush 2000.) Voiceover: "A King without a crown....a Hostile monster that crushes all in his path....a Predator stalking his prey....an Ironman with no remorse....they all have one passion....GOLD! 4:20 Promotions and California Championship Wrestling present GOLD RUSH 2000! Sunday, May 14th, LIVE on pay-per-view! Order this event on In Demand pay-per-view. In Demand: You Rule!" (commercial)
