CCW TV - Week of May 12th, 2000 (Part 3 of 5)

California Championship Wrestling CCW TV Week of May 12th, 2000 Taped in Merced http://ccw.50megs.com =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= (returning from commercial, back to the match) SR: "Winters picks up Domi, going for another piledriver! Can't lift Domi! Can't lift him! Domi with a big backdrop! Here comes Taylor! Dropkick by Domi! To his feet! Domi makes the tag! Here comes Clint Halford! Here comes Halford! Halford's in the ring! Dropkick to Taylor! Right hands on Winters! Irish whip, FRANKENSTEINER! Awesome move by Halford! Halford whips Taylor to the ropes, dropkick right in the mush! Clint Halford is on fire! Halford off the ropes, Fraud tripped him! Sickman Fraud tripped Clint Halford! Tom Domi saw it, but the ref did not! Domi going after the Professor on the outside! Domi cold-clocks Fraud! Taylor with an attack from behind! Taylor goes for a suplex on the concrete! Blocked by Domi! Domi blocks! Atomic drop by Domi! Inverted Atomic drop! Back in the ring, Halford with an Asai moonsault, Winters got the knees up!" QS: "Tom Domi just smacked David Taylor with a steel chair!" SR: "Domi! Going for a suplex now on Taylor, crotches him on the steel guardrail!" QS: "OW!" SR: "Domi smacks Taylor with the chair again! Taylor spills into the front row! The fans clear out! CCW Security making sure no one gets hurt, except the wrestlers! Back in the ring, Winters hits a Northern Lights bomb! Not going for the pin! Winters climbs the ropes, Halford to his feet! OH!" (Halford runs into the ropes, causing Winters to fall and crotch himself on the turnbuckle.) QS: "Temporary Insanity 2000 will be singing soprano from now on!" (Meanwhile, on the outside, Tom Domi climbs the ropes, throws the steel chair at David Taylor, who catches it; Domi then leaps off with a flying kick into the first row - a suicidal Van Daminator!) SR: "UNBELIEVABLE!" QS: "That was suicide! What a move! What a stupid, stupid move!" (The fans pick up a "Holy Shit!" chant.) SR: "Back in the ring! Halford to his feet! To the corner, Winters is reeling from that fall! Waistlock, GERMAN SUPLEX! FROM THE TOP! ONE! TWO! IT'S OVER!" Russell: "HERE ARE YOUR WINNERS, THE TEAM OF CLINT HALFORD AND TOM DOMI!" SR: "HALFORD AND DOMI TO THE FINALS! They're gonna face The Suicide Kings at Gold Rush 2000! Halford and Domi! They get the title match! The crowd goes wild!" (commercial)
