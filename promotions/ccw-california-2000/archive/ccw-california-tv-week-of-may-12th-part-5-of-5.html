CCW TV - Week of May 12th, 2000 (Part 5 of 5)

12 May 2000

California Championship Wrestling CCW TV Week of May 12th, 2000 Taped in Merced http://ccw.50megs.com =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= (Back from commercial, we see Super Sexy already in the ring. "Villains" by The Verve Pipe is playing over the arena speakers, and Jester leads "King" Kevin Marshall to the ring.) Russell: "AND HIS OPPONENT, ACCOMPANIED TO THE RING BY JESTER, REPRESENTING THE SONIC UNDERGROUND! FROM TROY, NEW YORK, WEIGHING IN AT 217 POUNDS, "KING" KEVIN MARSHALL!" SR: "I have a feeling we are about to see one of the best matches you will see in ANY federation this year! Super Sexy looks more focused than I have ever before seen him, and Kevin Marshall will do anything to avoid a loss tonight!" QS: "Steve, we are just HOURS away from Gold Rush 2000, and this match could have a HUGE effect on the main event, especially if Super Sexy gets any kind of win here tonight!" SR: "There's the bell, we are underway! Collar-and-elbow tie-up in the middle, both men towards the ropes, rolling against the ropes, Marshall gets Sexy in the corner. There's a chop! A right hand by Marshall! Irish whip by the King, Sexy bounds into the corner. He hit hard! Marshall with an arm wringer, into a hammerlock. King turns it into a side headlock. Great combo wrestling by Marshall. Belly-to-back suplex! Sexy with a reversal out of the headlock, nicely done! Sexy stomping away on the King, trying to take the advantage. Super Sexy picks up the King, scoop slam, reversed into a small package! ONE! TWO! No, two only! Sexy to his feet, stomps the King in the head. Sexy, Irish whip, knee to the midsection by Sexy! Sexy hits a gutwrench backbreaker! Nice move! ONE! TWO! King kicks out!" QS: "Super Sexy wants to get the job done quickly!" SR: "Sexy picks up Marshall, kick to the midsection, front facelock, GOING FOR THE SEX APPEAL! HE'S GONNA FINISH IT EAR- Marshall with a back kick! Sexy was going for the early finish, Marshall with a dropkick! King chops Sexy, knifehand chop. Irish whip, King drops down, Sexy leaps over, rebounds, leapfrog by Marshall, rebound, hiptoss by Marshall! Super back on his feet, Marshall with a thumb to the eye! Sexy backs into the corner, Marshall kicks Sexy in the gut. A second time. Marshall leaps, jumping DDT! Excellent move! ONE! TWO! Sexy kicks out! King to his feet, off the ropes, drops an elbow! King with a choke hold! Referee Brad Gastwirt administers a five-count, the King will pay no attention! Marshall with a short arm scissors....great submission move by the King. Marshall looking for a submission, a tap out by Super Sexy, I don't think it's gonna happen!" QS: "No way. This is the biggest match of Super Sexy's career! I don't think he's gonna tap out!" SR: "Marshall lets go of the hold, both men up to their feet. Lockup, Sexy with a bodyslam. Super Sexy favoring that arm, the arm that Marshall was working over. King to his feet, Sexy with a clothesline! Big-time lariat! COVER! ONE! TWO! NO SIR! Two count only! Sexy grabs the legs of Kevin Marshall! Make a wish, Super Sexy! Drops a leg down on Marshall's nether-regions! The King is reeling!" QS: "The King is merely a Prince after that move!" SR: "Sexy with a handful of hair, going for a suplex on the King. Picks him up! Big-time vertical suplex! DROPS KING ON HIS FACE! Face-first vertical suplex! ONE! TWO! Kickout! Sexy stomps on Marshall. Drops a leg on the King! ONE! TWO! Shoulder up! Sexy picks Marshall back up, German suplex! ONE! TWO! NO!" QS: "What will it take? Super Sexy wants this one BAD!" SR: "Sexy picks up the King, tombstone! Going for a tombstone piledriver! He nails it! Tombstone piledriver! That's gotta be all! TWO! JESTER PUT A FOOT ON THE ROPES!" QS: "I didn't see it!" SR: "BALONEY! Jester put Kevin Marshall's foot on the rope! Super Sexy saw it! He is going after Jester! Super Sexy going after Jester! He's got Jester! He caught the little rat! Picks him up! Gorilla press slam! Drops him across the guardrail! Neck first! Sexy back into the ring, Marshall with a baseball slide! Caught Super Sexy right in the face as he was attempting to re-enter the ring! Marshall grabs Sexy by the hair, chucks him into the ringpost! Marshall has a chair! Waffles Sexy over the head! Sexy isn't fazed! Super Sexy is not fazed! Kick to the midsection! Takes the chair! NAILS THE KING!" QS: "The referee is counting! We could have a double countout!" SR: "Sexy tosses the King back into the ring! Super Sexy climbing the turnbuckle! Waiting on the King! Sexy on the top rope! King to his feet! Flying axehandle! Down goes the King! Fell like a redwood! COVER! ONE! TWO! Y...NO! Two and NINE-TENTHS! Sexy picks up the King, monkey flip! Super Sexy follows in, legdrop! COVER AGAIN! ONE! TWO! NO SIR! Super Sexy is pissed! Sexy picks up the King, Irish whip, reversal, drop down by the King, Sexy collides with the ref! Brad Gastwirt goes down in a heap! Sexy grabs the King, SPINEBUSTER! COVER! ONE! TWO! THREE! FOUR! FIVE! DAMNIT! Sexy has the win, but there's no ref!" (Suddenly, "Crippler" Sean Davis runs into the ring and hits Sexy from behind with his baseball bat.) QS: "AH! DAMNIT! Sean Davis! Sexy is out cold!" SR: "Jester's trying to wake up the referee!" QS: "This sucks!" SR: "Gastwirt is conscious! Marshall is covering Super Sexy!" (Referee Brad Gastwirt crawls over to the two men and makes the count.) SR: "ONE! TWO! THREE! Marshall STEALS the win!" Russell: "HERE IS YOUR WINNER, "KING" KEVIN MARSHALL!" SR: "Here comes the Sonic Underground! Dan Karma is in the ring! "Nature Boy" John Quadro! Super Sexy is getting punked! Davis has Sexy in the Kata Hajime! HERE COMES JEFF MONROE! THE PREDATOR IS IN THE HOUSE! HE'S GOT A CHAIR! Nails Jester! Nails Quadro! Nails Dan Karma! Nails Karma again! DROPKICKS HIM OUT OF THE RING! MIKE CRAWFORD FROM BEHIND!" QS: "The Ironman strikes!" SR: "Crawford picks up Monroe, BOSTON BOMB! Marshall and Crawford double-teaming Monroe!" ("Bow Down" by Westside Connection blares over the arena speakers, and the fans pop as C.N. Butler runs out from the back, slapjack in hand.) SR: "IT'S C.N. BUTLER! C.N. BUTLER! He's got a slapjack! Nails Crawford! Crawford goes down! Nails Davis! Davis goes down! Swings at the King! Marshall ducks! Turn around, Kevin Marshall! Turn around!" (Marshall turned around. He got nailed by Butler.) SR: "BAM!" QS: "The Underground is down and out!" SR: "WE'RE OUTTA TIME! SEE YOU AT GOLD RUSH!"
