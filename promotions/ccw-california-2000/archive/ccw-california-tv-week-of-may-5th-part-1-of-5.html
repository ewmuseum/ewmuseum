CCW TV: Week Of May 5th (Part 1 of 5)

2 May 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
California Championship Wrestling CCW TV Week of May 5th, 2000 Taped in San Rafael http://ccw.50megs.com =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= (The show kicks off with "Sad But True" by Metallica blaring over the arena speakers and "The Predator" Jeff Monroe making his way to the ring, mic in hand.) Steven Ruth: "Fans, welcome to CCW TV! Jeff Monroe has the mic, and he's hot! We are one week removed from No Limits 3, an event which saw this man get SCREWED by Mike Crawford, Kevin Marshall, and the Sonic Underground!" Quan Shabuya: "No doubt about it, Monroe was cheated out of a win at the hands of his supposed best friend Mike Crawford, but he had the oppurtunity to beat Kevin Marshall! Vinnie Hostile had the match won for him, and he refused to make the cover!" Monroe: "CUT THE MUSIC! Hostile! One thing you need to realize is this: if you had done your job and dealt with the Sonic Underground OUTSIDE THE RING, I'd have beaten Kevin Marshall on my own! And Mike Crawford, you ungrateful piece of garbage! I kept you afloat for three years! I paid your bills! I fed your kids! And you have the nerve to turn on me! Crawford, your day will come! But as for right now, Commissioner Lollie is in the hospital, so Martinez, A.J. Martinez, get out here, because you and I need to talk!" SR: "He's calling out the Interim Commissioner, Alex J. Martinez! Justin Lollie is still hospitalized, we'll talk about that later in the show, but right now, here is the Interim Commissioner!" (Martinez walks out from the back, mic in hand.) Monroe: "Now....I know Lollie's rules. I know about his Heavyweight title defense rule. But A.J., Lollie isn't here, is he? You're the Commissioner, and I say F**K LOLLIE'S RULES! I want a match against Vinnie Hostile TONIGHT for the CCW Heavyweight title! I deserve it, damnit!" Martinez: "Jeff Monroe, you're right. I saw the match, I saw you and Marshall, and Kevin Marshall is the number one contender to the Heavyweight title. There's nothing I can do about that; it was in the contract. But I CAN order a match tonight between Vinnie Hostile and you, Jeff Monroe, with the title on the line!" (The crowd pops) QS: "He's gonna do it tonight?!" SR: "I can't believe this! Alex Martinez has been Commissioner for less than a week, and he's already booking title matches?!" (Suddenly, "Open" by Chevelle plays over the arena speakers, and Vinnie Hostile, CCW Heavyweight title over his shoulder, walks out from the back. He grabs the mic away from Commissioner Martinez.) Hostile: "First of all, I don't know WHO you are, I don't know WHO you THINK you are, but if you're gonna come around here telling me who I'm gonna fight, who I'm gonna defend my title against....Jeff Monroe, you LOST last Sunday! Kevin Marshall pinned you, 1-2-3, in the middle of the ring!" SR: "Yeah, after getting hit in the head with a baseball bat by Mike Crawford!" Hostile: "But Monroe, if you're so eager to get your ass kicked again, then who am I to stop you? Every time you and I step in the same ring, you always wind up on your ass! So Jeff, put your money where your mouth is! You want a shot at me? Let's do it RIGHT F**KING NOW!" SR: "You're kidding me!!" (Hostile drops the mic and the CCW title belt, runs in the ring, and starts brawling with Monroe. A referee runs out from the back, the bell rings, and this impromptu match is underway!) SR: "Fans, this is unbelievable! We've got a Heavyweight title match right now between Jeff Monroe and Vinnie Hostile!" QS: "Right now, if I'm Kevin Marshall, I'm pissed! What if Monroe wins the belt? It's Marshall vs. Monroe at Gold Rush 2000? What gives?" SR: "Hostile hits a headbutt! Down goes Monroe! Hostile drops an elbow! COVER! ONE! TWO! NO SIR! Two only! Hostile with a pick-up, another headbutt! Hostile is dominating this impromptu match early! Hostile with a pick-up, forearm to the back! Monroe is down on his knees, and another forearm! Hostile, whips Monroe to the ropes, Hostile follows in, DEVASTATING CLOTHESLINE! Monroe spills to the outside!" QS: "Just another day at the office for the CCW Heavyweight champion! Hostile seems to have Jeff Monroe's number; I don't think the Predator can take this one!" SR: "Hostile might take the countout victory here, the referee is counting. Up to four, Monroe struggles to get to his feet. Monroe had better make use of the entire count, he needs all the time he can get to recover from that massive clothesline! Monroe back in, Hostile stomping away on the Predator. Monroe grabs a leg and trips Hostile! Hostile's down! Monroe drops an elbow on Hostile's leg! Monroe now kicking at Hostile's knee! He's gonna try to work on the knee!" QS: "VERY smart move by Jeff Monroe. Vinnie Hostile may be seven-foot-two, but when he's flat on his back, Monroe is the big man in this match!" SR: "Monroe is giving up sixteen inches and almost 250 pounds to Vinnie Hostile! This is truly David versus Goliath here tonight! Monroe with a leglock on Hostile, he's trying to wear down the knee. He's trying to keep Vinnie Hostile on the mat! Monroe drops his knee down on Hostile's knee! And again, another kneedrop! It is amazing how the fans have turned against Vinnie Hostile! They love The Predator here in San Rafael! Monroe's climbing the ropes....flying kneedrop on Hostile's leg!" QS: "I like Monroe's strategy, but how is this gonna help him put on the Predator Armbar?" SR: "Monroe gets a two count from the cover! Monroe challenges Hostile to get to his feet! Hostile can't stand on the knee! Hostile wobbles! Dropkick by Monroe! Down goes the champ! Jeff Monroe seems to have done it! Jeff Monroe seems to have beaten Vinnie Hostile! Monroe with a pick up, DDT! DDT by Monroe! COVER! ONE! TWO! Hostile kicks out with authority! Hostile to his feet, limping on the leg, kicks Monroe. Whip to the ropes....SIDEWALK SLAM! It's over! ONE! TWO! MONROE KICKS OUT!" QS: "Many more of those, and we'll be scraping Jeff Monroe off the mat with a spatula!" SR: "Hostile picks up Monroe like a rag doll! He looks like a child in Hostile's arms! Powerslam by Hostile! Here it is! Hostile's calling for the Sky High Power Bomb! Hostile's gonna finish Jeff Monroe off right now!" (Hostile picks up Monroe for the power bomb, but Monroe reverses the move into a huracanrana, sending Hostile ass-first into the corner.) SR: "HE JUST HURACANRANA'D VINNIE HOSTILE! UNBELIEVABLE! VINNIE HOSTILE TAKEN DOWN BY A HURACANRANA BY JEFF MONROE! Monroe stomping away on Hostile! Sets Hostile into position, Monroe climbs the ropes! This could be a moonsault! SPLIT-LEGGED MOONSAULT BY MONROE! ONE! TWO! NO! HE KICKED OUT! Vinnie Hostile kicked out! What does Jeff Monroe have to do to pin Vinnie Hostile?!" QS: "I don't think he can do it! Vinnie Hostile is seven-foot-two! 470 pounds! It's like pinning a car! You can't do it!" SR: "Hostile fires back, forearms on Monroe. Hostile, pick-up, small package! ONE! TWO! THREE! HE DID IT! HE DID IT! JEFF MONROE WON THE TITLE! MONROE WON THE TITLE!" Shannon Russell: "HERE IS YOUR WINNER, AND NEW CCW HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPION, JEFF MONROE!" SR: "I DON'T BELIEVE IT! JEFF MONROE HAS JUST WON THE CCW TITLE! MONROE IS THE CHAMP!" QS: "Hostile was right there by the ropes! That was too close to call, but his shoulders were on the mat for three! Jeff Monroe has done the impossible: he has pinned Vinnie Hostile!" SR: "LADIES AND GENTLEMEN, WE HAVE CROWNED A NEW CHAMPION HERE IN SAN RAFAEL! JEFF MONROE IS THE CHAMP! JEFF MONROE IS THE CCW HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMP!" ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- ---- ("CCW TV" intro plays) SR: "Fans, welcome to CCW TV! I'm Steven Ruth, alongside my broadcast colleague, the "Living Legend" Quan Shabuya, and what you just saw was the crowning of a NEW CCW Heavyweight champion, "The Predator" Jeff Monroe! But it was not to be...." (cut to footage of Vinnie Hostile barging into the office of Interim Commissioner Alex J. Martinez. He has a videotape in hand.) Hostile: "MARTINEZ! MARTINEZ! SHUT UP AND LISTEN! F**KING LISTEN TO ME, GOD DAMNIT! Where's the VCR? GET OUT OF MY WAY!" (Hostile shoves one of the suits out of the way and commandeers the TV/VCR the suits and Martinez was watching.) Martinez: "Vinnie, you'd better have a damn good reason for this!" Hostile: "I do! I do! Look, I'm sorry I was loud and all, but you HAVE TO SEE THIS! Look at this!" (The tape starts playing, and footage from the Hostile/Monroe match where Monroe won the CCW title is shown. From this different camera angle, we can see that Vinnie Hostile's foot was clearly in the ropes when Monroe made the pin.) Hostile: "LOOK AT THAT! LOOK AT THAT! IN THE ROPES! IN THE ROPES! THAT PIN SHOULD HAVE NEVER COUNTED! I SHOULD STILL BE THE CHAMPION!" Martinez: "OK! OK! Just calm down...." Hostile: "Now, Martinez, do the right thing! That was NOT a pinfall! Go out there, get MY TITLE BELT back, because as you can PLAINLY SEE, JEFF MONROE NEVER BEAT ME!" Martinez: "Alright! Alright! I'll....OK." Hostile: "YOU'LL DO IT, DAMNIT! Get my title back! He never beat me! My foot was in the ropes! Give me back my belt!" Martinez: "OK! Damnit, Vinnie, get the hell out of here! I'll get your title back! Just leave, damnit!" Hostile: "You'd better, Martinez. You'd better do the right thing...." (Hostile leaves, videotape in hand, as the screen fades to black.) ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- ---- (commercial)
