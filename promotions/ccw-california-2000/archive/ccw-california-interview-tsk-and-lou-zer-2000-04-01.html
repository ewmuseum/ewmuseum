A CCW Interview with TSK and Lou Zer

1 April 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The scene opens with a CCW interview guy standing in the back. To his sides are "The Teen Idol" Thunderstrike Kid, with his red hair w/ dyed blond streaks parted to the sides, and "The Role Model" Lou Zer, who is wearing his shoulderlength blond hair in a ponytail. Behind them is "Tough" Guy Masters, who has his usual crew cut. TSK is smiling, though obvioulsy in pain, and Lou is in his regular nervous state.) Interviewer: TSK, Lou Zer... you both felt losses today. TSK, you fought a draw with Marvin Harris, and later fought World Heavyweight Champion Vinnie Hostile, and lost in under two minutes. Lou, your loss was an upset to "Suicidal" Sean Foxx. Your thoughts? (He puts the mic up to Lou's mouth, and Lou hesitates, and then talks, with a lacking of confidence shown by his stutter.) Lou: Well, I'd... um... have to say that it... well... uh... (TSK whispers in Lou's ear.) Lou: YEAH! Sucks, it definately.. uh... sucks. (The interviewer turns to TSK.) Interviewer: TSK, after your bad day, how do you feel? (TSK pauses, and thinks the question over, and is about to talk, but stops, and puts the mic a little closer to his face, and then starts to talk.) TSK: Well, simply put... I have complete respect for Vinnie Hostile. I just wanna say that someday, that title that you wear will be around MY waist, and I'm hoping that it's you that I get to take it from. (He then stops to call a person on camera with a mirror, and TSK checks his hair, and then when it is perfectly straight, he talks again.) TSK: Now, Marvin Harris, "Mr. Gasworks", you need to consider this chance I'm gonna give you. Seeing as how you believe that my win isn't legitimate, I'll make this simple. I'm challenging you like this... [---Thunderstrike Kid vs. Marvin Harris in a Last Man Standing Match---]. All you need to do is accept, or decline. And I'll even declare that MASTERS AND ZER WILL NOT BE AT RINGSIDE! You'd be eligible to be Institutionalized if you don't accept that! All that's left to be said is this... (TSK pushes the mic to Lou.) Lou: ... um... uh... Thun... der... Str... uh... I forgot. (TSK smacks himself in the head as Masters starts laughing at Lou, and the scene fades out.)
