CCW TV: Week Of May 5th (Part 5 of 5)

2 May 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
California Championship Wrestling CCW TV Week of May 5th, 2000 Taped in San Rafael http://ccw.50megs.com =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= (back in the ring, Interim Commissioner Alex Martinez and Vinnie Hostile await) SR: "The decree has come down from the Interim Commissioner, Alex Martinez, that Vinnie Hostile gets the Heavyweight title back!" Martinez: "OK, Jeff, we've already talked about this....you're gonna do this like a man....come on out and -" (Hostile grabs the mic from Martinez.) Hostile: "MONROE, GET YOUR F**KING ASS OUT HERE AND GIVE ME BACK MY BELT!" ("Sad But True" by Metallica blares over the arena speakers, and the fans pop as Jeff Monroe comes out with the CCW Heavyweight title belt strapped around his waist. Monroe pauses at the entranceway, then walks to the ring.) SR: "Jeff Monroe WON the CCW Heavyweight title tonight from Vinnie Hostile, but Hostile showed another camera angle to the Commissioner, his feet were in the ropes, and Martinez has, in essence, reversed the decision!" QS: "I'm sorry, I've been friends with A.J. Martinez for a long time, but this is bullcrap. There is no instant replay in wrestling!" Martinez: "Now, Jeff, we're gonna do this nice and-" (Monroe snatches the mic from the Interim Commissioner.) Monroe: "F**K YOU, MARTINEZ! YOU SPINELESS PIECE OF S**T! SINCE WHEN IS THERE F**KING INSTANT REPLAY IN WRESTLING? HOSTILE, I BEAT YOUR ASS FAIR AND SQUARE, I PINNED YOUR F**KING SHOULDERS TO THE F**KING MAT FOR THREE, TAKE YOUR GODDAMN TITLE AND SHOVE IT UP YOUR ASS!" (Monroe rips the title off from around his waist and throws it at Vinnie Hostile's face. Hostile grabs it, and shoots an ice-cold glare at Jeff Monroe. The fans are on their feet cheering Monroe.) Monroe: "Hostile, enjoy that title, because as far as I'm concerned, you're nowhere near talented enough to beat Kevin Marshall! I hate Kevin Marshall, but he's got more talent in his pinky finger than you have in your entire over-sized body!" (Monroe throws the mic - forcefully - back at Martinez, then goes to exit the ring.) Martinez: "I'm not done." (Monroe stops suddenly, Hostile looks on with intent.) Martinez: "As far as I'm concerned, there was no finish in that match between the two of you. You never pinned him, he never pinned you, therefore, there was no finish, and therefore, we're gonna restart that match RIGHT NOW!" (The fans pop like mad, the bell rings, and before Hostile can react, Monroe runs back in the ring and clotheslines the hell out of him.) SR: "HERE WE GO!" QS: "I have NEVER seen Jeff Monroe this angry!" SR: "Monroe slammed Hostile! Monroe bodyslammed Vinnie Hostile! 500 pounds! He slammed him! Predator Armbar! Predator Armbar! Predator Armbar! He's got it locked! That big crane of an arm is locked in the Armbar! Hostile is locked! He's gonna tap! It's locked on!" (Suddenly, "King" Kevin Marshall, "Ironman" Mike Crawford, Dan Karma, "Nature Boy" John Quadro, and "Crippler" Sean Davis run out and attack Monroe.) QS: "Son of a bitch!" SR: "The Sonic Underground! For the love of God! We can't have a clean finish, can we? Marshall, that turncoat Crawford!" QS: "They're not even touching Vinnie Hostile! The King's gonna face Hostile for the title at Gold Rush 2000, and they're not touching him!" SR: "Something's up! This match will be thrown out! Vinnie Hostile keeps the title! The Sonic Underground is dessimating Jeff Monroe! We're out of time! What next?!" (screen fades to black....)
