CCW TV: Week Of May 5th (Part 4 of 5)

2 May 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
California Championship Wrestling CCW TV Week of May 5th, 2000 Taped in San Rafael http://ccw.50megs.com =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= SR: "My broadcast colleague Quan Shabuya has gone over there to check on Super Sexy and Brian Patterson. If you're just tuning in, that car wreck scene outside the ring is a miscued plancha by Brian Patterson, his knee hit Super Sexy's head, and Patterson's head, in turn, hit the steel guardrail. Patterson is conscious, I think, I can't see Sexy, I think he's still out. Quan Shabuya is going to check on it. We still have a match going on, TSK and Marvin Harris....I think it's safe to say that Brian Patterson and Super Sexy are not a part of this match any longer. This is now a two-man battle for the Television championship, the Gasman and Thunderstrike Kid. TSK with a very nice frog splash from the top! TWO! Harris kicked out, TSK almost won the title! Here comes Quan Shabuya, Super Sexy is on a stretcher." (Quan puts his headset back on.) QS: "It doesn't look good, Steve." SR: "How it coming? What's up?" QS: "OK....Brian Patterson has lacerated his head. He's a little woozy. Super Sexy is conscious, he is here, he was knocked cold for about a minute, probably has a concussion, we're not sure. They are going to put a neckbrace on Super Sexy and keep him level, just to be on the safe side. They did the same thing for me when I got my neck hurt. We don't know if there's a neck injury or not. It's just a precaution. We'll know more, of course, when he gets back from the hospital. They're both going to the hospital." SR: "OK....we can just....hope for the best. We still have a match here, I know the camera has been on the ring for the most part, and my focus has not been on this match, but we have a clinic going on in the ring between the Gasman and TSK. Great technical action, Harris has the advantage, hits a DDT on TSK! There's a cover! ONE! TWO! NO SIR! Two only! Super Sexy is on the stretcher, his neck is being secured, like Quan said, just as a precaution....Harris just bulldogged TSK! Classic bulldog maneuver, face into the canvas! COVER! ONE! TWO! TSK kicks out! Harris, pick up, Magistral cradle! ONE! TWO! The ref was out of position for that one." QS: "For a man of Marvin Harris' size to be able to execute a move like that is truly amazing!" SR: "Harris has the upper hand, TSK with a dropkick! Defensive move helps TSK gain the offense! TSK, Irish whip, rebound, TSK drops down, Harris leaps, rebound Harris, TSK up, leapfrog by TSK, rebound Harris, backdrop by TSK! 300 pounds up in a backdrop! That took a lot out of Thunderstrike Kid. TSK with a headlock. Thunderstrike Kid....wrenching on the neck of Marvin Harris, the Gasman being deprived of wind. TSK to his feet, bounds off the ropes, dropkick to the back of the Gasman! TSK pulls Harris to the center of the ring, here it comes! Going for the Shocking Grasp!" QS: "If he can lock this in the middle of the ring, he's the new champ!" SR: "Harris kicks off! TSK spills to the outside! Quickly back in the ring is Thunderstrike. Harris off the ropes, spinning leg lariat! He just hit a spin kick that almost took TSK's head off!" QS: "Caught him as he was coming in the ring! I'd have waited a few seconds, but then again, I'm not insane!" SR: "Auspicious by his absence is Professor Sickman Fraud, who is letting the Gasman goes at it alone. Harris drags TSK in the ring, cover! ONE! TWO! NO SIR! Almost a three count, almost got the TV title back! Harris, pick up, spinebuster! ONE! TWO! TSK gets out of the pinning predicament! Harris, whip to the turnbuckle, follows in with a clothesline! Down goes TSK! Gasman climbing the ropes! Second rope! Flying elbow! COVER! ONE! TWO! TSK kicks out again! Harris is pissed! Picks up TSK, backbreaker! Standing splash by Harris! ONE! TWO! NO! Almost! Picks up TSK, TSK with a small package! ONE! TWO! Harris escapes!" QS: "We crowned one new champion tonight with that move, how about two?" SR: "TSK trips up Harris, going for the Grasp! Shocking Grasp! THERE IT IS! Harris is nowhere near the ropes! Harris is nowhere near the ropes!" QS: "It's over. It's locked tight! TSK is the champ!" SR: "Harris is fighting! Harris fighting! He's gonna try to reach the ropes! He's gonna make it to the ropes! Harris! Powering out of the Shocking Grasp! That Texas Cloverleaf finisher of TSK's! He's gonna make it to the ropes!" QS: "I can't believe it! Marvin Harris is powering out of the Shocking Grasp!" SR: "He's there! Just another inch! So close! Grab the ropes, and he breaks the hold! TSK....TSK dragged him away! Sits WAY back on Harris!" QS: "He was at the ropes, and TSK pulled him away! He has that hold cinched!" SR: "Marvin Harris has been in the Shocking Grasp for well over a minute! About....about 90 seconds! Harris will not tap out!" (The fans start a "Gasman! Gasman!" chant.) QS: "His knees are gonna be broken! His legs are probably cramped all to hell! How long can he stand this?" SR: "This is amazing. Harris is practically in tears, and he will NOT tap out! He will not surrender! Harris! Fighting for over two minutes! This is incredible! Unbelievable! He's gonna power out! He's trying! He's trying again!" QS: "I can't believe it! How can he withstand the pain?" SR: "He just....doesn't have it left...." QS: "Amazing." SR: "The ref's calling it! There's the bell! Did he tap?" QS: "I didn't see him tap out! I did not see one sign of submission from Marvin Harris! I think the ref is just calling this one." SR: "But we have a new TV champion, and it's Thunderstrike Kid!" Shannon Russell: "HERE IS YOUR WINNER, AS THE RESULT OF A SUBMISSION, AND NEW! CCW TELEVISION CHAMPION, THUNDERSTRIKE KID!" SR: "I don't know if he tapped out or not! I didn't see it, but I didn't have the close angle the ref had. Maybe he just said "I Quit", I don't know. But TSK is the champ!" QS: "I hate him. Give it up for Marvin Harris. That is a painful, PAINFUL submission move, and he stayed in it for well over two minutes, almost three!" SR: "TSK is the champ, and when we come back, the Heavyweight title is on the line again!" ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- ---- (commercial)
