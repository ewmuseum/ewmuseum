CCW TV - Week of May 12th, 2000 (Part 4 of 5)

12 May 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
California Championship Wrestling CCW TV Week of May 12th, 2000 Taped in Merced http://ccw.50megs.com =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= (Back from commercial, we see Matthew Bullock executing his "End Game" finisher on Venom. He covers, the ref counts three, and Bullock is victorious. His music starts playing....) SR: "Another win by Matthew Bullock!" QS: "This was sheer domination! Here is a man, without a doubt, ready to make a run at any belt he wants!" (Suddenly, an incredibly loud explosion is heard over the arena speakers. Everything stops. The fans turn their attention to the entranceway, and the crowd absolutely goes nuts.) QS: "What....what the hell?!" SR: "IT'S DOOM!" (Matthew Bullock practically pisses his pants; he is, for the first time in over a year, face-to-face with a man who he set fire to. Bullock's manager, Dr. Dane Woychuk, starts going schitzo. HISTORY LESSON: at the AWF Wrestlestock pay-per-view in January of 1999, Bullock and DOOM fought in an Inferno match. Bullock won. He proceeded to douse DOOM with kerosene and set his entire body afire. DOOM has not been seen in public since then....until now.) SR: "HOLY S**T! IT'S DOOM! IT'S DOOM! DOOM'S BACK! DOOM'S BACK!" QS: "I can't believe it! I never, in a million years, thought this man would ever see the light of day again!" (DOOM approaches the ring at full speed; Bullock and Woychuk immediately exit the ring, jump the guardrail, and high-tail it through the crowd as fast as they can. DOOM steps between the ropes, but he's not fast enough to catch Bullock and Woychuk, who don't even turn around and look back. The crowd is going bonkers. DOOM is back.) (Cut to an interview with DOOM.) [FADE IN to a broad shot of an empty arena somewhere in California. In the center of the floor is a dimly lit wrestling ring and inside that ring stands a man that almost everyone knows from the past. His name is DOOM. As the camera shot fades to a closer shot of him, he walks to the ropes, rests his arms on the top rope and starts to speak.] DOOM: You know, I've gotten a lot of calls over the past couple of days asking me about the show in Merced. Everyone who knows me knows that I live on the east coast of this country and some have asked me why I would be willing to uproot my family and move to California. Others have asked me why, after nearly a year away from the business, would I even bother coming back...especially to a territorial indepentant organization. Afterall, I've been to bigger and better places and would have no trouble going back. And still others have asked why I would come back to get even with Matt Bullock after what he did to me...after he nearly ended my career. Why would I risk it? Why would I move cross-country and take a cut in pay...especially just for the sole purpose of getting revenge? I'll tell you why I did why I did. I'll tell you why I moved my family to California...why I took a cut in pay...why I'm risking my very being on this earth. One reason....Pride. Matthew Bullock, you've done a lot to me over the years. You burned me and you scarred me for life. You damn near ended my career...my livelyhood. You not only permanently scarred me, you put my family in jeopardy financially. And you know, I wasn't going to come back. I didn't think I had any reason to. I had had it with the business. I had had it with all of the games....I was tired of it all. Until last week. Last week I was walking through the mall in my hometown. Not surprisingly, I passed by a display of wrestling related merchandise and I overheard a conversation between two fans. Both of which used to watch the shows we put on back in the day. And one of the guys picked up an action figure of the Undertaker and jokingly said...."You're Doomed!". The other guys started to laugh and replied.."I remember him! What a wuss. He just let that Bullock guy run him off. What a joke.". That, my friend, changed my life forever. I realized it was no longer about getting revenge. It wasn't about being the better man...or the better wrestler. It was a matter of pride. I had no pride. Until Merced. I shocked the world that night and judging by the look on your face, I shocked you too. You thought it was over. You thought you had run DOOM out of the business for good. You thought DOOM was a coward. Well Matt, think again! I'm back. I'm in the CCW. And at Gold Rush 2000, I'm going to regain something I lost nearly a year ago.....my pride. And if you think for one second that your sorry ass manager is going to help you out, think again. If he even thinks about getting involved, I'll kick him so hard in the ass he'll be eating @#%$ for breakfast! Bullock, say your prayers. At Gold Rush 2000........ You're Dead! (Cut to a promo featuring The Midnight Revolution, who will face The New Compton Posse at Gold Rush 2000) (The scene is backstage at the arena in Merced. Mike Perez is looking for someone to interview, when he spots the Midnight Revolution stepping out of the locker room. They're in street clothes and headed for their car.) Perez: "Excuse me! Excuse me! Aaron Starr and Alex Valour, the Midnight Revolution! Can I have a word with you guys?" Starr: "Huh? Oh yeah, sure. Sure. Alex! Alex! Le type chauve veut nous parler! Venez ici!" Valour: "Je déteste parler à cet imbécile." Perez: "Aaron Starr and Alex Valour, the Midnight Revolution! Tonight, you were eliminated from the Tag Team title tournament by Clint Halford and Tom Domi. What are your thoughts on that match?" Starr: "We're never proud of losing, but that was a tough match. Clint and Tom are both great wrestlers, and we wish them luck in their match at the pay-per-view. I think they can win the titles, and I hope that we'll get a title shot." Perez: "Yes, it was a great match." Valour: "Naturellement il était." Starr: "En effet. Qui nous pense-t-il sont-il, Brooklyn Boyz?" (Starr and Valour laugh heartily.) Perez: "Speaking of great matches, you two have a match at Gold Rush 2000 against The New Compton Posse! What do you have in store for the Posse?" (Valour takes the mic out of Mike Perez's hand) Valour: "Je crache sur le Compton Posse. Je ris de leur compétence faible de lutte. La semaine prochaine, à San Francisco, nous ferons un pas partout vous. Tous les deux vous tomberont aux mains de la Révolution! Et vous, Mike Perez, vous sentez comme un poisson de décomposition. Allez-vous -en et ne nous tracassez pas plus." (Aaron Starr has to slouch away and put a fist over his mouth to contain his laughter.) Perez: "Uhhh....thanks! The Midnight Revolution, ready for their match at Gold Rush 2000!" (Now we cut to an backstage interview with the CCW Heavyweight champion, Vinnie Hostile.) (The scene opens up with Mike Perez catching up with CCW World Heavyweight Champion Vinnie Hostile in an underground parking lot, just as Vinnie is about to step into his black stretch limousine to leave. Vinnie looks disturbed just at the sight of Perez, but he's still in a rather playful mood, wearing sunglasses and a Burger King crown.) Perez: "Vinnie, I was just hoping we could get a few words with you! It was just announced that the main event at Gold Rush on May 14th will be you facing "King" Kevin Marshall for the World Heavyweight Championship! Would you care to comment?" (Vinnie finally stops staring into space after a few seconds of silence, realizing that Perez is finally done yammering.) Vinnie: "....Who the hell do you think you are? Just running up to the champ like that, thinking you should just be granted the privelege of talking to me? Who pissed in your gene pool? Michael Cole...step away from the camera because nobody wants to see your weasel face." Perez: "It's Mike Per..." (Vinnie simply pushes Perez by his face out of the camera's view, and takes the microphone.) Vinnie: "Now, you think I didn't know that that little pissant Kevin Marshall fellow would sooner or later go whining and crying to Justin Lollie, Alex Martinez, or whoever the hell is in charge nowadays...about not getting a title shot? I knew it. Because since day one little Kevy Marshall has been a bitcher, a complainer. And worst of all, little Kevin Marshall can't do a damn thing on his own. Kevin Marshall can't even wipe his own ass without help from the SU let alone beat me." (Perez somehow works his way back into the scene, to ask one last question.) Perez: "And what about the events this past weekend with Jeff Monroe and the controversial title change?" Vinnie: "What title change?! There was no title change, you moron! I walked in the champ and I walked out the champ! Do they pay you to be this stupid, Michael Cole?! You will never see Jeff Monroe's name in the history books under the title CCW World Heavyweight Champion! The only thing he'll ever go down in CCW record books for is for being the oldest, most pathetic, most dried-up piece of crap this organization has ever brought back from the retirement home! Jeff Monroe, you absolutely suck! There's just no two ways about it. Now I know the fans want to hear more from their hero, but time is of the essence...and I've got a plane to catch. Now go bother somebody else with your microphone and your tight stonewashed jeans and black greasy hair slicked back!" (Vinnie steps into the limo and it pulls away, leaving Mike Perez alone in the parking lot and speechless. The scene fades away with Perez trying to think of a closing line...) (Cut to the announcer's desk) SR: "When we come back, Super Sexy takes on "King" Kevin Marshall!" (commercial)
