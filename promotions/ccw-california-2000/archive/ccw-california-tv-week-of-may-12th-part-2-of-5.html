CCW TV - Week of May 12th, 2000 (Part 2 of 5)

12 May 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
California Championship Wrestling CCW TV Week of May 12th, 2000 Taped in Merced http://ccw.50megs.com =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= (Back from commercial, in the ring stands Shannon Russell.) Russell: "THIS CONTEST IS A SEMIFINAL MATCH IN THE CCW TAG TEAM TITLE TOURNAMENT! IT IS SET FOR ONE FALL, WITH A THIRTY MINUTE TIME LIMIT!" ("Master Of Puppets" by Metallica blares over the arena speakers, and the fans cheer the appearance of Clint Halford and Tom "Da Bomb" Domi.) Russell: "INTRODUCING TEAM NUMBER ONE! FIRST, FROM ORLANDO, FLORIDA, WEIGHING IN AT 198 POUNDS, CLINT HALFORD! HIS TAG TEAM PARTNER, FROM BRANDON, MANITOBA, CANADA, WEIGHING IN AT 210 POUNDS, TOM "DA BOMB" DOMI!" SR: "Without doubt, the surprise team of this tournament, Clint Halford and Tom Domi are just one match away from a shot at the Tag Team title belts, but to get there, they have to face a pair of tough dudes called Temporary Insanity 2000!" ("My Own Summer (Shove It)" by The Deftones plays over the arena speakers, and the cheers turn to boos as Prof. Sickman Fraud leads Temporary Insanity 2000 to the ring.) Russell: "THEIR OPPONENTS, ACCOMPANIED TO THE RING BY PROFESSOR SICKMAN FRAUD, AT A COMBINED WEIGHT OF 443 POUNDS, THE TEAM OF DAVID TAYLOR AND STEVE WINTERS, TEMPORARY INSANITY 2000!" QS: "I have not seen a team so focused on one goal, Temporary Insanity 2000 has one motivator, and that's fear! Professor Fraud is one sick Sickman." SR: "Indeed he is. He gains a sick pleasure out of causing pain and inflicting fear in his charges. We're ready to go in this match, it looks like it's gonna be Domi starting for his team, and....Taylor? No, Fraud wants Winters in there for the start. Here we go, there's the bell! The winners of this match will go on to Gold Rush 2000 and fight for the Tag Team titles, they will face The Suicide Kings, who have been hard training for that match! Winters and Domi lock up, Domi pushed into the corner, ref in with the break, Winters with a chop on Domi. Another chop! Domi with a reversal! Chops by Domi! Domi with a whip to the turnbuckle, follows in, high kick by Winters. Winters charges out with a clothesline! Winters with a pickup, scoop and a slam. Winters, grabs an arm, cross armbreaker locked on by Winters. Submission hold, wear down hold by Steve Winters. Winters drops a knee on Domi's arm. Pick up by Winters, into the TI2K corner, Winters tags to Taylor. David Taylor in the ring, arm twist by Winters, Taylor with an elbow across the arm! Taylor takes the armlock, hammerlock by Taylor. Domi, back elbows, breaks the hold. Domi, off the ropes, cross body, Taylor catches him! Powerslam by Taylor! COVER! ONE! TWO! Kickout by Domi." QS: "This is gonna be one of those see-saw, back and forth match-ups, it'll be the first team to make a mistake that'll lose this match!" SR: "Taylor with a pick-up, side slam. Another cover! TWO! No sir. Domi, Taylor chucks him to the outside! Here comes Halford, ref cuts him off! Referee Brad Gastwirt intercepts Clint Halford, and there's a double team on the outside of the ring!" QS: "Make that a triple team!" SR: "Sickman Fraud joins in on the attack! It's three-on-one on the outside, Temporary Insanity 2000 putting the boots to Tom Domi! Tom Domi rolled back inside the ring. Steve Winters comes back in, he is not the legal man! Winters, textbook snap suplex! Tag to Taylor, here comes the legal man. Taylor climbing the ropes, double axehandle on Domi! Temporary Insanity is in control! Taylor with a reverse chinlock on Domi." QS: "This has been classic, excellent double-team work, great tag team wrestling by Temporary Insanity!" SR: "They triple-teamed Domi on the outside!" QS: "Hey, it's all part of the strategy! If they can do it and get away with it, more power to them!" SR: "Taylor still has a reverse chinlock on Tom Domi. Domi is reaching for the tag, but he's still several feet away from Clint Halford. Taylor wrenching way back on Domi, bringing him to his feet. Knee to the back by Taylor! Reverse DDT! Interted DDT by David Taylor! ONE! TWO! Halford makes the save! And here we go again! Again, the referee turns his attention to Clint Halford, and again, TI2K double-teams Domi! Double DDT by TI2K! Taylor with the cover! ONE! TWO! THIS COULD BE THREE! Temporary Insanity has the win, but the ref is out of position! Here comes the referee! ONE! Domi kicks out!" QS: "For once, the ref being out of position is a good thing!" SR: "Domi really needs to make a tag here. Taylor with an Irish whip, back bodydrop! High elevation on the bodydrop! Taylor picks up Domi, into the Insanity corner, tag made to Steve Winters. Taylor lifts Domi, tombstone piledriver! They're gonna hit a spike tombstone piledriver! AWW!" QS: "Devastating move!" SR: "That's gotta do it! ONE! TWO! NO SIR! Domi kicks out! Unbelievable! We gotta take a break! We'll be back!" (commercial)
