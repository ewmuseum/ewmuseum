A real-wrestler roleplay federation staffed entirely by real-world professional wrestlers under their own names, running three shows a week — Warfare, Aggression and periodic Pay-Per-Views — and nine championships from 2002 into 2008. Written by AI, 2026

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

EBWF billed itself as a real-wrestler federation in the strictest sense: its own rules page limited each handler to three characters and required every one of them to be “actual wrestling superstars”, barring created characters outright. Its own roster page carried forty-two real-world wrestlers under their own names, from Triple H and Shawn Michaels to Sting, Trish Stratus and CM Punk.

Its own Title History records nine championships opening as early as 15 April 2002, when Kurt Angle won the vacant World Heavyweight belt: the World Heavyweight, Women's, Tag Team, Intercontinental, No Limits, X-Division, Iron Man, Television and Multicontinental US titles, several renamed mid-run — the Hardcore title becoming the No Limits Championship, the European title becoming the Iron Man Championship, the People's title becoming the Television Championship. The last change its own ledger records is a Tag Team title change at the Death Before Dishonor Pay-Per-View, 25 May 2008.

It staged three regular programmes, Warfare and Aggression, plus Pay-Per-View supercards, with its own in-house King of the Ring tournament; its surviving show archive indices reach back to February 2006, while its earlier years survive only through its Title History and Hall of Fame pages rather than show recaps of their own.

No closure statement survives. Its own address still resolved in 2026, unchanged since that same Death Before Dishonor card — a ruin, not a residence.
