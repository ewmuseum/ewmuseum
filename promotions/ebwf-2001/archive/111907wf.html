SEGMENT
 The regular Warfare intro played and the cameras panned the crowd. The East Rutherford, New Jersey crowd displayed their signs. The usual pyro went off and then the tron flickered. The crowd cheered when Wes Ikeda appeared in front of an EBWF Interview Backdrop. He spoke directly into the camera.

Wes Ikeda: In six short days the EBWF Returns to Montreal, but more importantly, though not in it's purest incarnation, EBWF is taking Survivor Series back to Montreal. I can sense the anticipation . I feel the electricity even right here in New Jersey. Over 550 miles from the most controversial wrestling cities in the world, and already the cheers can be heard throughout Montreal's Belle Center. And on Sunday night when the only sound filling my ears is "You Screwed Bret", I'll be remembering every wrestling fan in America. I'll be going along with every silent thought, of every EBWF Fan in the United States, and though I may be the only one in the whole of Canada, together with all of you I will be chanting "H-B-K, H-B-K". And because I feel so strongly about Shawn Michaels finally shedding the stigma of the Montreal Screwjob this is the last you'll see of me until the curtain closes on Survivor Series. The curtain will close with Shawn Michaels EBWF World Champion.

Match #1
 Bret Hart defeated Sting with the Sharpshooter. After Sting tapped out the arena went dark. The crowd seemed confused and the tron lit up once more. It read "Rebirth" - "Renew" - "Revolt" and after a flew flashy graphics it said

(2)+(1)+(9)+(7)= 19
Team Play Game
Punk LionHart Y2J
Tops No Limit
Slam_Heel_.x04
KOSS.fly

Warfare went to commercial.

Winner: Bret Hart
Reason: More of a One Liner.

Match #2
 Nattie was accompained to the ring by Teddy Hart and beat Katie Lea via pinfall.
Winner: Nattie
Reason: Better RP.
Match #3
 Jericho defeated Rodney Mack with the Walls of Jericho.

Winner: Jericho
Reason: Better Focus.

Match #4
 The young Black Machismo could have put on great matches in the EBWF, but unfortunantly tonight wasn't one of them. He seemed to come up short...Randy Orton's wrestling skills seemed to take on a life of their own, and he completely dominated the match. The end came as Lethal was trying to hit the Lethal Elbow Drop Orton. As Lethal leapt off the ropes, Randy rolled to the side, apparently playing possum. He got up, dragged Black Machismo to his feet and hit him with the RKO for the 3 count and the victory.

Winner: Storyline
Reason: New Character for Andrew.

Match #5
 Dupree, knowing he was in trouble tried to channel the power of the French people to help him overcome the onslaught of Edge. Edge had found a new level of motivation in his absense from the ring, and he relished the opportunity to showcase his skills against the Former World Champion. Edge and Dupree had a good showing, and the two fought hard until Edge caught Dupree off guard and hit the spear for the 1..2...3...

Rene threw a bit of a tantrum as he couldn't believe his luck. CM Punk's Music hit the PA and he made his way out to the ramp. Edge looked confused, but Punk began to speak.

CM Punk: And this is the guy that gets to face Shawn Michaels at Survivor Series? He loses two in a row and we're supposed to believe that HE is the number one contender? Well I've been told that the Survivor Series match won't be rescheduled, and while that is unfortunate I've come out here to issue a challenge. Because I defeated Rene Dupree last week I demand that I be given the first shot at the World Champion, whomever that may be...

Edge: Whoa, whoa, whoa. Hold on a second there jerk off! Maybe you weren't watching but I just defeated Rene Dupree myself, so perhaps you should get in line. You're last weeks news!

CM Punk: Well, I can compromise. How about we face each other. Winner gets a shot at the EBWF Championship.

Edge: No one deserves it more than me! Me and you - I'm surprised that after seeing me sphere ole Roi to the ground here that you would have the balls to stand up to me Punk. Not only is there no one in the back who deserves a shot, there's no one who has the guts to claim they do. In fact...

EBWF went off the air.

Winner: Edge
Reason: Better RP
