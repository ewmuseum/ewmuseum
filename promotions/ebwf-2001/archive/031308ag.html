Aggression Results

SEGMENT
 A Wrestlemania Package began to play in order to start the Aggression show. The North Carolina crowd seemed somewhat enchanted as the Aggression Intro followed, and then the screen flickered for the second show in a row. Wes Ikeda appeared and let a slow grin creep over his face.

Wes Ikeda: North Carolina... smells like whores and spot monkies.

He paused and smiled broader, letting the crowd digest the innuendo.

Wes Ikeda: It sucks to be here in North Carolina. It's second on my list of places I hate to go. Second only to Canada, what a feat. Ah, Canada... I guess that brings me to Lance Storm. You've yet to comment on my promise to remove your limbs from your body should you interfere in the world title match at Wrestlemania. That means one of two things, either you didn't get the message or it sunk in. I hope it was the latter Lance. Now, NC.... I'm not showing my face in this second rate state tonight, because seriously I checked the road schedule and it turns out that next week the EBWF goes live in Detroit, Michigan. I kind of got to save my energy for the beautiful homecoming. Here's the first class show you don't deserve Fayetteville. Thanks for the trial run.

Wes smirked and the crowd booed as the screen went black

Match #1
 MVP got the upset on Stone Cold Steve Austin. He celebrated in the ring and then made the belt motion across his waist before leaving the ring.

Winner: MVP
Reason: No Austin RP

Match #2
 Killings and Keenan tried to keep the match inside the ring, but they went to the outside with it. The referee became frustrated and gave the ten count. The match ended in a no contest.

Winner: No One
Reason: Impossible to rate one liners.

Segment
 Chris Jericho was seen seemingly making his way out to the ring. He was on a mission and it seemed he had Lance Storm on the brain when a figure came flying into the frame. Jericho fell to the ground and within moments Lance Storm was revealed as the attacker and once Chris was in a fallen heap Lance grabbed him by his hair and yelled in his face.

Lance Storm: Challenge accepted!

The crowd cheered as the scene faded.

Match #3
 Dawn Marie beat Bobbi in her debut match. She rolled her up with the small package, and celebrated in the ring to cheers from the crowd.

Winner: Dawn
Reason: No Bobbi RP.

Match #4
 The match started out with Joe using his strength advantage over Paul London to ground the highflying superstar, London kept trying to get in some of his highflying moves but Joe would have none of it finally Joe hits London with the Chimeraplex out of nowhere and locks in the coquina clutch.

Winner: Joe
Reason: No RP from Andrew.

Segment
 The camera cuts to the production truck just before the beginning of the Dusty Rhodes/JBL Match up and we see that Jeff Jarrett is standing in the truck as he looks at a camera.

Jeff Jarrett: You know what disappoints me the most? Is the simple fact that no one in the management takes me seriously at all and doesn’t think that I won’t hold up Wrestlemania or any other EBWF show that I want. Well I am about to prove Wes Ikeda and all of his other cronies wrong.

With that Jarrett manages to cut the feed to the Aggression as the screen fades to black before some commercial run before we are back in the production area where Wes Ikeda is standing.

Wes Ikeda: Where is that Son of a B*tch??? Someone tell me please where Jeff Jarrett is. I want to know NOW!!!

No one can give Ikeda the answer he is looking for so he turns around and faces the camera with a few words for Jarrett.

Wes Ikeda: Jarrett if you want someone to listen to you then I am inviting you to come down to MY ring on Warfare and we will see what you want. With that we are back to our regular scheduled programming.

Match #5
 JBL who is just returning to EBWF from a brief Hiatus and trying to rebuild himself gets up in the American Dreams face and starts yelling profanities before bitch slapping the legend. Rhodes returns with a slap of his own. Which evolves into an all out brawl. JBL some how finds a very narrow opening and hits Rhodes with the Clothesline from hell and overs for the win

Winner: JBL
Reason: Great RPs from both. The second RP too JBL over the edge.

Match #6
 Age of the Fall was out to the ring first, followed by Triple H and Chyna. Lacey and Chyna started out, but Lacey tagged in Tyler Black. Chyna went toe to toe for a short time, before tagging in Triple H. The match continued with double team moves galore, and Triple H seemed to have the upper hand. He took the opportunity to tag in Chyna, and the crowd roared. There was a flurry of activity on the floor and suddenly someone jumped the guardrail. Triple H didn't have time to react. The unknown figure distracted Chyna for just a moment and when she returned her attention to Lacey she was promptly met with a DDT. Lacey went for the cover and before Triple H could go for the save he was face to face with Jeff Hardy! The crowd roared when Jeff grabbed Hunter by the hair and pounded him head first into the turnbuckle. Hunter flew off the ring apron and to the ring floor. Chyna was down from the DDT, and Age of the Fall, though seemingly not intimidated left the ring to allow Hardy his space as he climbed the turnbuckle over the fallen two-thirds of DX, extreme gunz in the air.

Winner: AOTF
Reason: Jon wrote a better RP.
