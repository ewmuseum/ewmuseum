Warfare Results

Match #1

 The show opened with Batista walking to the ring to his I Walk Alone entrance theme. He stood in the ring and awaited the arrival of Ron Killings. Killings was accompanied by Lacey as he made his way to the ring. The two began to brawl and Batista had the obvious strength advantage. He threw Killings into the corner and speared him, forcing Killings back into the ringpost. Killings doubled over in pain, and Batista went for the move again, but this time Killings dived out of the way, sending Batista shoulder first into the ring post. Killings attempted an early small package but Batista used his animal like strength to power out. The match continued for a few more minutes with Killings using his speed and agility to avoid being punded into the ground by The Animal. Batista knocked Killings down with a shoulder block, when he was distracted. John Cena stepped out onto the ramp and began to walk to the ring. Batista and Cena started exchanging trash talk while Cena stood outside the ring. The referee directed his attention to the self proclaimed champ, telling him not to get involved. Cena held up his hands to protest his innocence and while the referee was distracted, Killings caught Batista with a low blow. He took advantage of Batista's weakened disposition and hit him with the Truth Conviction. Killings covered the big man and yelled at the referee to count the fall. The referee did so and Cena left the ring looking proud of himself for costing Batista the match.

Winner: Killings
Reason: No Batista Roleplay. Julien had computer trouble.

SEGMENT
 After a commercial break, the scene opened backstage with Batista walking down the hallway looking annoyed. He tossed over a chair as he stormed down the hallway, and as he turned a corner he almost steamrollered through Shawn Michaels and Chyna, who were standing there talking. Shawn stared up at Batista with an icy glare. He wasn't intimidated by the fact that the man was twice his size...and Batista didn't seem the least bit impressed with the world champion.

Shawn Michaels: How about you watch where you're going there, big guy? I know the knuckles dragging along the ground must impair your movement...but put a little more effort in, got it?

Shawn turned back around to continue his conversation with Chyna, shaking his head in disgust. Batista grabbed the champion by the shoulder, and spun him around so they were facing each other again.

Batista: How about you say that to me again, captain sarcasm.

Shawn Michaels: You think that was sarcasm? I was being completely serious. Sorry if it went over your big dumb head. Now, beat it...I'm with a lady, ok?

Batista looked immensely aggravated, then he broke into a small but menacing laugh.

Batista: Oh...is that what she is?

By this point, Chyna stepped up. She wasn't about to let Shawn defend her.

Chyna: Yes, it is. I'm the only woman backstage who can kick the shit out of you and give you a hard on at the same time...and you jealous bastards can't stand it. But I'd listen to Shawn if I were you.

Shawn Michaels: Joanie! Don't say that...ever! He's not me, he never will be. He doesn't have the talent, the passion, or the good looks to ever be compared to Shawn Michaels. Besides... don't spread this rumor around, but I actually have a brain.

Batista had experienced enough of DX, and he shoved Chyna aside and threw Shawn Michaels into the concrete wall roughly. Shawn flinched as his back came into contract with the solid concrete, and Batista started to pound him with right hands. Shawn flung as many right and left hand's in Batista's direction as he could. He knocked Batista backwards momentarily, and saw Chyna make a slight move, as if to suggest she might get involved. He turned his head and looked at her sternly.

Shawn Michaels: Don't.

She didn't, but the momentary distraction was enough for Batista to gain the advantage again. He grabbed Shawn around the neck and threw him into a table with production equipment on it. He punched Shawn a few more times until Shawn didn't seem like he had the fight in him to continue any more, then he backed off slightly.

Chyna: You idiot! My team needs that stuff, and If you've broken any of it your ass is so fired.

Batista just responded with a scowl, and he walked away, leaving Chyna to help Shawn as the scene faded.

Winner: No-one
Reason: Julien wanted to feud with DX.

Match #2
 Billy Gunn's music hit and he made his EBWF debut to great approval. He waited in the ring while John Cena made his entrance. Cena was looking pleased with his antics earlier that night, and he seemed somewhat cocky. Gunn took the advantage from early on and he was immensely impressive in his debut. He took the fight to Cena and made it clear that he was here to accomplish big things in the EBWF. Gunn eventually hit Cena with the fame-asser and covered him for the three count before celebrating his first EBWF Victory.

Winner: Billy Gunn
Reason: A Late RP is better than no Rp. Welcome to the fed!

Match #3
 All That Remains by Fozzy hit and Chris Jericho made his way to the ring to a nice reaction. Metalingus hit and Edge walked to the ring, with mixed boos and cheers meeting him as he stepped through the curtain. He didn't seem to care, and slid into the ring. The match was the highlight of the Warfare show, as both men gave it their all to create a great back and forth contest. Every time one man got the upper hand, the other fought back to even the playing field, and the match kept on continuing. Jericho got Edge in the Walls Of Jericho, and the fans cheered as they thought it was over. Edge managed to reach for the ropes, though, and the referee broke the hold. Edge fought back, and caught Y2J by surprise with a spear. He covered his opponent, but Jericho kicked out after two and the fans were on the edge of their seats. Edge pulled his hair in frustration and dragged Jericho back to his feet. Jericho elbowed Edge in the stomach and went for a DDT. He got it, and got a 2 count. Edge got back on his feet again, and Jericho threw him into the ropes. Edge bounced back and hit a second spear as he rebounded! Edge covered Jericho and the referee counted 1...2...3! Edge was declared the winner. Edge left the ring, and as Jericho began to climb to his feet, a figure slid into the ring having walked through the crowd. The man was dressed all in black, and his face was covered by a black ski mask so only the eyes were visible. The man attacked Jericho from behind and started beating him repeatedly. Security ran into the ring to try and force the mystery attacker off, and they attempted to remove the man's mask, but he fought them off before exiting the ring and making an escape through the crowd. Jericho fought back to his feet, looking somewhat hurt, and he watched in annoyance as the man disappeared into the sea of spectators.

Winner: Edge
Reason: This was a really close one and I had a hard time deciding. But in the end, Kirsten's Edge RP did it for me. I liked the passion in it. Both were very good though. Nice job, guys. The masked man is storyline.

SEGMENT
 I Walk Alone hit and Batista made his second scheduled appearance of the night. He walked to the ring accompanied by his Manager Search Contestants. Kane looked stiff and angered while Piper tried to avoid him, standing as far away as possible next to Stacey and The Kat. The Kat was dressed in black and Stacey was pink, both ladies were standing next to each other.

Batista: All right guys, I haven't had the best night...but I took out my frustrations on that cocky SOB Shawn Michaels, and now I feel a little better...So it's time for out second elimination.

The camera showed Kane giving a sly grin, While Piper looked a bit frustrated. The Kat was crossing her fingers and Stacey was puckering her lips.

Batista: Once again my cell phone has been flooded with millions of text messages. But the person recieving the least amount of votes is.....The Kat.

The Kat immediately looked disappointed.

Batista: Sorry babe but you are leiminated. However I'll give you my number and we can work something out...just kidding.

Batista gave The Kat a hug and The Kat tried to smile while also trying to hold back her tears as she realized that her dreams of becoming Batista's manager had been shattered. The three remaining contestands looked pleased as The Kat left via the rampway.

Batista: Check out Next Week's Warfare as the Final Three duke it out in a Diss The Manager Contest. And one week from today there will also be another elimination. Thats it for now and don't forget to text me who you want eliminated.

The titan tron lit up with the voting information.

Text Batista (PM Julien)

1 if you want Piper eliminated
2 if you want Kane eliminated
3 if you want Stacey Keibler eliminated
Batista and his contestants began to leave the ring with Batista walking in front. The crowd cheered as Batista paused at the top of the ramp to pose. He turned around to walk to the back and was met with a Sweet Chin Music by Shawn Michaels, who had ran out from behind the curtain and taken the big man by surprise. Shawn yelled some abuse down at Batista's fallen body, then held up his world title proclaiming himself as the best the EBWF had to offer. He grinned slightly as he headed back through the curtain.

Winner: Nobody.

Reason: More storyline stuff.

Match #4
 The high-flying member of the Hart family took on Sting, who hadn't made many EBWF appearances lately. The match had only been underway for a few minutes when the lights went out. There was a buzz of anticipation throughout the arena when the titan tron lit up. The video package opened with the image of the night sky, followed by a shot of a graveyard in the rain. The camera moved throughout the graveyard, as Sting and Hart both stood in the ring staring at the image. A dark figure could be seen in the rain. The figure was sitting on the ground, slouched against a tomb stone. His hair was wet, and clung to his face. The fans cheered in excitement as the camera zoomed in just as the figure looked up. It was Raven.

Raven: Sting, Sting, Sting... haven't you heard? I'm back. Don't look for explanations, don't look for answers. The search is futile. Nobody can know the mind of a Raven. My presence is the only explanation you need. So if you're confused...good. I like it that way. I'll see you... very soon.

The titan tron flickered back to a plain black screen and the lights came back on. When they did, Raven was standing in the ring holding a steel chair. He hit Sting in the head with it from behind, and Teddy Hart quickly escaped the ring. The referee rang the bell, and announced Sting as the winner by DQ, as Raven continued the attack. He pounded Sting with the chair until blood stained the red and white paint. Then Raven left the ring with a sadistic grin on his face.

Winner: Sting
Reason: Just storyline. Sorry, Ash and Brian...I didn't know exactly what you wanted to happen, so I just did my best.

Match #5
 Sexy Boy hit and Shawn Michaels made his way to the ring. He was no longer accompanied by Chyna, but he had his world title over his shoulder as always. He stood in the ring showboating as he waited for James Storm. Storm's music hit and he made his way to the ring. Storm was drinking his beer, as always, and Shawn didn't hesitate to slap the drink out of his hand. Storm looked annoyed and yelled at the world champ for relieving him of his beverage, before throwing a punch. Shawn Michaels ducked the shot and hit Storm with a punch of his own. The two fought, and the match went back and forth for a few minutes before Storm started to gain the upper hand. He forced Shawn into the corner and Shawn fought his way out by poking Storm in the eye. Storm grabbed his face and while he was vulnerable HBK fought out of the corner and hit The Tennessee Cowboy with Sweet Chin Music. Shawn made the cover and the referee counted...1...2...3. Shawn Michaels was declared the winner. His own theme music hit and he once again held his world title up high as the show went off the air.

Winner: Shawn Michaels
Reason: No James Storm Roleplay.
