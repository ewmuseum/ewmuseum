1.) Do not use characters that do not belong to you in your RPs. There are "Global Characters" on the staff page all of who are free for use. 2.) In this new age of RPing it is a lot more acceptable for your character to talk about going out to the ring and cutting a promo. However, basic rule of thumb. Do not mention, "rps" in your RP. 3.) This is The EBWF. If your character doesn't know where they work we're going to assume you did some poor copy/past plagerizing of either your own or someone elses work, and you will more than likely lose your match. 4.) Absolutely No Racist remarks. The Owner has dogs and she will sick them on you. 5.) All RP Board Memberships require authorization. Please fill out a join form before applying for board membership. If you have not submitted a join form your authorization request will be denied. 6.) Ashlee openly welcomes whining when you lose. Just no whining when you get put in your place. ;) 7.) You are not to rip-apart other people's rps! If you do not like their rps you do not have to tell them. It is not your place to attempt to put them down, because there are mistakes that you don't like. Yes, you might be the King of the Death Match, and yes you may have ended 765 careers, and been rping for 7 years, but we don't need you to nitpick other people's RPs. It's for fun, and we're all doing the best we can here. 8.) Please PM Ashlee or Shev with any Character, Stable, or Tag Team Changes. 9.) Let's try not to harass each other! 10.) Your character limit is 3. They must be actual wrestling superstars. NO CREATED CHARACTERS ALLOWED! You may have a fourth character for a manager. That person must be a real superstar or manager, and should not be a big name character that you're using to hog up for yourself. Ashlee will determine if the person is management material or if the character should be left open for other people to use as an active character. 11.) NO LATE RP'S for any reason will be counted towards a match. A late rp is better than no rp, but if deadline is 12:00 and your rp is up at 12:01 it will not count. Our suggestion is if you're worried about whether or not you can post the roleplay...post early. Be sure you stop writing your roleplay early enough to have time to post it and be sure your links work if there are any. Ashlee also doesn't have much of a life and will be happy to post your RPs for you if you catch her on messenger and ask her to. 12.) RP Layouts are not necessary but they do look nice!
