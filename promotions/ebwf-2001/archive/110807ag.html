Aggression Results

Match #1
 The opening match of the show was cancaled because neither man showed up to work.

Winner: Nobody
Reason: No RPs.

Match #2
 Tammy Sytch got a good reaction as she made her debut, and the crowd reacted posititely to Ashley as well. The two locked up, and Ashley broke their collar and elbow tie up by bringing her knee into Tammy's chest, then she took Tammy down with a DDT. Tammy Sytch kicked out after one, and took Ashley down with a clothesline. Ashley was up fairly quickly, and she went for a dropkick but Tammy Sytch swatted her aside, and Ashley met the canvas. Tammy Sytch went for a leg drop, but Ashley rolled to the side and Tammy's elbow hit the canvas. Ashley got to her feet and hit Tammy with a neckbreaker, followed by a leg drop from the second rope. She dragged Tammy up by the hair, and hit her with the Starstruck for the win. As Ashley was celebrating, Melina stepped out onto the stage. The two locked eyes, and the fans popped at the return of the Women's Champion. Melina raised her belt in the air and pointed to herself, but she didn't come to the ring. Ashley nodded at her, and Melina stepped back off the stage as suddenly as she had appeared.

Winner: Ashley Massaro
Reason: Better Roleplay.

Segment
 Before the Lacey/Black Machismo match, the titan tron lit up and a video began to play. The fans cheered loudly as it started.

As the video came to the end the message "11.12.07_Y2J" appeared. The date could mean only one thing... Chris Jericho was returning on Warfare!

Match #3
 The crowd booed as Lacey made her way to the ring. Black Machismo had his usual Macho Man get-up on as he fought for a shot ad advancing in the No Limits title tournament. Black Machismo charged towards Lacey but Lacey took him down with an X-Pac style spinning heel kick. She dragged Black Machismo back to hit feet, and hit him with a perfect German suplex. She went for a cover...1...2... kickout. Lacey semed to be doing quite well against the male wrestler, but he clearly outmatched her in terms of strength, and when he got back up, he locked up with her and forced her into the corner. Lacey used her initiaitve and hit him with a headbutt to free herself. Black Machismo stumbled backwards and Lacey hit the Lighning Spiral then covered him for the three count
Match #4
 Killings, with his new found look on life tried to to use every dirty move in the book. But Storm would have none of it, and caught Ron Killings with a drop kick out of nowhere before starting working on the legs of Killings so he could use the maple-leaf. Killings made his way back to his feet and hit Storm was a spinning elbow sending him down and while Storm was on the mat Killings taunted the crowd by telling them to Shut the Hell up. Killings then turned around and got caught with a stiff punch to the face. He didn't go down, but returned the offense a stiff bitch slap to Storm's face. This continued for a few seconds before Killings poked Lance in the eye while the ref was distracted by Rodney Mack. Lacey threw a chair in the ring Ron Killings hit Lance Storm with it. He threw the chair back outside the ring, and Lacey put it out of sight. Rodney Mack let the referee go, and he turned around to count the cover. 1...2...3! Killings advanced in the No Limits Title tournament.

Winner: Ron Killings
Reason: Felt his roleplay had more match focus.

Segment
 “La Marseillaise” hit and out walked Roi Rene Dupree holding the Iron Man Championship in one hand and the French flag in the other. He walked down the ramp and got in the ring. He grabbed a microphone and began to speak. Roi Rene Dupree: According to the Aggression preview, there is a message from us regarding this past week’s Warfare. There is? Umm…ok…we lost. Seriously, we will not make excuses, only dishonourable men do that, which we do not belong to. Come Survivor Series, we will regain the championship and not lose it on our first defence. As for our match with Shawn Michaels at Survivor Series, there is really only one match it can be. One year ago, we became the Iron Man Champion, and we continue to hold that belt. And the match we won it in is the only match we can wrestle in come November 23rd. That is why, M. Michaels, brace yourself for sixty minutes, the Iron Man returns to the match that made him famous. Think of it as iron man II.

The fans burst into a collaborate roar of approval as Shawn Michaels music hit and he stepped out onto the stage, appearing on EBWF Warfare for the first time as the EBWF Champion. He had the belt on his shoulder, and a microphone in hand.

Shawn Michaels: Iron man match, huh? I don't have a problem with that... you are the "Iron Man Champion" after all...what else could I expect you to choose? I accept your challenge, Rene. In fact, I'll accept a challenge from anyone, any time. I'll even defend the title against people who don't challenge me... which is why I'm out here to make an announcement of my own. Monday night on Warfare, in my first ever EBWF Title defense, I'll go up against...Lance Storm! Bring it, Lance. But just remember...I'm on quite the winning streak against Canadians. With that, Shawn left the stage with a smirk. Rene gave the microphone back to Lilian Garcia and left the ring.

Match #5
 The number one contender's spot for the X-Division title was on the line, and the fans seemed thrilled at the debut of Edge. Orton didn't get such a warm reception. The two had a great match, and definitely earned the main event spot. Edge went for a spear early, but Orton dived aside and Edge went into the ring post. Orton grabbed Edge by the tights, pulled him back slightly then threw him into the ring post again. He then took Edge down witn an armbar takeover, and kept his legs wrapped around the arm, putting pressure on the shoulder. The referee asked Edge if he wanted to tap, but Edge got to the ropes. The referee ordered Orton to break the hold, and Edge favored his arm sligtly as he got up, but he fought back. He used his good arm to throw some hard punches in Orton's face, then hit Orton with a DDT. He went for the cover, 1...2...kickout. Edge shook his head and dragged Orton to his feet. Orton elbowed Edge in the stomach as he got up and rolled Edge up in a small package. 1...2...kickout. Edge got up, whipped Orton into the ropes, and took him down with a drop toehold. He then hit an elbow drop to the back of Orton's head and stood back waiting for Orton to get up. When Orton did, Edge hit him with the Spear and covered him for the three count. Edge's shoulder seemed to be hurting a little as he got up, but the victory out-weighed the pain, and Edge left the ring with a grin on his face.

Winner: Edge
Reason: Better roleplay. Nice work Kirsten.
