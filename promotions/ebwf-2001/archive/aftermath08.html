PPV Results

Match #1

The show kicked off with Christy Hemme v Beth Phoenix. Christy seemed fairly confident, as she already had a win over Beth under her belt. Beth was looking to avenge her recent loss, and she fought with a passion. The fans were into the opening contest and the two ladies kicked off the show with a bang. The match could have gone either way, but Beth was in control when Jillian Hall made her way onto the stage. Beth noticed Jillian, but ignored her. Jillian stood at the top of the ramp and discussed the in-ring happenings with Rico. Beth hit Christy with a DDT and covered her. The fans cheered as Christy kicked out after a long two and Jillian stared intently as Christy got back to her feet. She went for a dropkick but Beth swatted her aside and got her in a Boston Crab. Christy reached for the ropes but couldn't reach. Beth had her back to the ring and didn't see Jillian slowly advancing. Christy strained a little harder to reach the ropes, but Beth didn't let up on the hold and Christy still couldn't reach. The referee crouched down, asking Christy if she wanted to give it up. She shook her head but gritted her teeth in agony. Jillian slid into the ring and knocked down Beth from behind. The referee rang the bell and Jillian tackled Beth through the ropes to the outside. She irish whipped Beth into the steel steps and shrugged as if it had all been an accident. Christy got up, looking a little confused. Jillian ignored her and pointed to her waist. She and Rico then strolled up the ramp.

Winner: Beth by DQ.
Reason: No RPs, but Jerome is taking some time off. I just wanted to give him a place in the Women's title hunt ready for when he returns.

SEGMENT
 The scene opened backstage with Syxx walking through the hallway. He had his bag slung over his shoulder and didn't look as if he was arriving. The camera followed Syxx as he stormed through the back door and into the parking lot. The door swung shut behind him and the shot flicked back to Joey Styles and Jerry Lawler.

Joey Styles: Where does he think he's going? He has a match with Matt Hardy tonight!

Jerry Lawler: Well Joey by the look of things...not any more he doesn't.

Reason: Storyline.

Match #2
 MVP was standing in the ring waiting for the arrival of his opponent Kevin Nash and finally Nash makes his way out onto the stage. He was counting some money before handing it off to an assistant and MVP rolled his eyes, looking aggravated. Nash slowly walked down to the ring as he continued to get under the skin of MVP, who was pleading with the referee to start counting Nash out for his blatant stalling tactics. Nash finally made his way to the ring and climbed over the ropes, not even stretching to do so. MVP didn’t waste any time is starting the match as he laid an Elbow into the chest of Nash, causing the big man to stagger. Nash fell backwards into the corner and MVP quickly charged in, delivering a shoulder into the midsection of Kevin Nash. He then climbed up onto the second rope and started laying some right hands to the top of Nash’s skull. Nash wasn't going down that quickly, and he fought MVP off. Nash used his strength to his advantage, grabbing MVP who was in a vulnerable position above him. He walked to the center of the ring and took MVP down with a Reverse Atomic Drop followed by a Elbow to the Jaw. Nash then took a small breather and leaned on the ropes, flicking his hair back as MVP got up. Nash charged towards his opponent, looking to connect with a big boot. MVP ducked under the big boot and hits Nash in the knee with a closed fist. As Nash buckled down onto one knee MVP ran towards him and smashed Nash across the Jaw with a running Knee. MVP made a cover ONE…TWO...Nash kicked out. MVP waited for Nash to get up and kicked him in the stomach, forcing Nash to bend down in pain.MVP tried for The PlayMaker but Nash’s height proved to make the move difficult. Nash pushed Porter off and MVP bounced into the ropes. He reacted quickly as he ran back towards Nash, and adjusted himself to catch Nash with a RockerDropper. The big man staggered before falling down to the mat. MVP then hit his Ballin' Elbow Drop onto the back of Nash. He rolled him over but that took some effort. MVP got a Cover but again Nash managed to kick out...albeit at 2 and a half this time. MVP dragged Nash up and Irish Whipped him into a corner. He then charged in looking for a Kawada Kick but Nash moved out of the road in a surprise act of quick movement forcing MVP's foot to catch the turnbuckle and causing his leg to lock up. Nash saw that as an advantage and used an Elbow to the side of the head to wound MVP some more before hitting a kick to the midsection. The kick led to Nash hoisting up MVP for the Jacknife Powerbomb. He hit the move, planting MVP hard into the canvas! ONE…TWO….THREE!!! NASH gained a victory after sidestepping some great MVP offense. Nash’s experience and ability to adapt came through in the end and he grinned confidently as the ref raised his hand.

Winner: Nash
Reason: Better Roleplay...great character. Thanks to Ian for this match.

SEGMENT
 The scene opened with Triple H walking down the hallway, World title on his shoulder. He had a bottle of water in hand and as the camera panned out it was clear that Shawn Michaels was walking beside him. Triple H and Shawn turned a corner and Hunter put his hand out in front of Shawn's chest to stop him. HBK stopped, looking confused, but he soon saw the reason behind Hunter's action. Jeff Jarrett and Dusty Rhodes were standing aside, having a talk about the game plan for Jarrett's match with the Miz. Hunter frowned and walked over. Shawn followed, and Dusty and Jarrett stopped their conversation to stare at the tag team champions. Hunter spoke first.

Triple H: Rumor has it you've got a problem with me. I listened to your little promo... and I just have one piece of advice. If you're gonna trash me, do it to my face you gutless son of a bitch!

Jarrett stepped off the wall, not backing down from the world champion.

Jeff Jarrett: Gutless? I'm the gutless one? You're the one walking around with the title on your shoulder that you're too scared to defend! Ain't that right, Dusty?

Dusty Rhodes: That's right, Daddy-O.

Triple H glared at Dusty.

Triple H: Nobody of importance asked for your opinion, bitch-tits!

Triple H looked back at Jarrett and Dusty stepped forward, pushing him in the chest.

Dusty Rhodes: Now, seems to me you better watch your mouth there partna!

Triple H: Watch my mouth? Yeah...you're right....Shawn, I really should be more polite. Let's go.

Hunter tapped Shawn on the shoulder and started to walk away. He only got about three strides in before he suddenly turned back around and launched himself at Dusty and Jarrett. Shawn joined in too...he wasn't about to let his partner to 2 on 1 with Jarrett and Rhodes. There was a scuffle and a few good punches thrown before backstage officials ran over to pull the four men apart. Jarrett yelled as some road agents pulled him back.

Jeff Jarrett: See what I mean, Helmsley! No good coward! You're a disgrace to that belt and to every champion before you.

Triple H just smirked and didn't resist the hands that were guiding him back.

Triple H: We'll see, Jeff... just wait.

DX walked away as the scene faded.

Reason: Ian, I read what Jarrett said in your RP and thought if would be fun to make something of it. Hope you don't mind!

Match #3
 Jillian Hall stood in the ring with Rico at ringside. She seemed pleased with herself for her actions earlier in the night, and wore a confident smile as the Women's Champion Karen Angle made her entrance. Karen held up the Women's title before handing it to the referee. The referee handed it to the timekeeper and signalled for the bell to be rung. The bell rang and Jillian and Karen walked up to each other, exchanging some trash talk. Jillian started off the action by slapping Karen in the face. Karen's head snapped back and she looked shocked for a moment before charging at Jillian with a spear. She took Jillian down and caught her with a few right hands. She then got back up and waited for Jillian to get up before hitting her with an atomic drop, followed by a clothesline. She went for an early cover...1...2...kickout by Jillian Hall. Jillian fought back to her feet and went right back after Karen. Karen attempted to take Jillian down with an armdrag but Jillian landed on her feet and turned around to catch Karen with a clothesline. She then ran into the ropes and hit a running splash before going for the cover. Karen was too close to the ropes and grabbed onto them. Jillian got back up and Karen soon got up too. They were back on equal footing. They locked up and Karen ducked behind Jillian, getting her in a hammerlock. She applied pressure to the arm and Jillian looked to be in pain. The referee asked if she wanted to give it up and she shook her head, giving a definite no. She used her free arm to attempt to elbow Karen in the head, but Karen ducked out of the way. Jillian mule kicked Karen, catching her by surprise in the leg and causing her to loosen the hold. Jillian got out of it and hit Karen with a snap suplex. Cover...1...2...Karen kicked out and the fans applauded. The girls battled it out and the fans gave them a respectful reaction as they gave it their all. Karen gained the advantage after about 7 minutes and took down Jillian with a drop toe hold before locking her in the Karen Klutch! Jillian writhed in pain but refused to tap. Karen seemed somewhat surprised and impressed by Jillian's endurance and she fought her way to the ropes. The referee broke the hold and Jillian was a little worse for wear. She still fought back to her feet and was a little slower than she was before, but still very impressive. She managed to regain control when the tables turned. Karen went to the top rope as Jillian was groggy, and Jillian pushed the referee into the ropes, causing Karen to fall back into the ring. The referee admonished Jillian but she insisted it had been an accident. Jillian waited for Karen to get up, practically stalking her. As soon as Karen was on her feet, Jillian hit the Hall of Famer! She went for the cover...1...2...3!!! Jillian was ecstatic as she was announced as the new women's champion! The referee handed the belt and she instructed Rico to get her a microphone.

Jillian Hall: I...did it! Just like you all knew I would! And to mark this momentous occasion...I'm going to grace you all with a song!

The fans booed as Jillian cleared her throat.

Jillian: I ammmm the champiiiionnnn....I am the champiiiiiiion! Nooooo time for loooooooseeeers, cause I ammmmm--

The mic went dead and Jillian frowned, looking shocked. She looked like she was about to throw a fit and Rico attempted to calm her down. He succeeded, and Jillian shrugged. She raised the belt above her head and left the ring.

Winner: Jillian Hall
Reason: Best roleplay.

Match #4
 It was time for the Syxx/Matt Hardy match. Lilian stood in the ring with a microphone, and held it to her lips.

Lilian Garcia: Ladies and gentlemen...I've been informed that neither Syxx or Matt Hardy are currently in the building. As a result of this, the following match has been cancelled.

The fans booed as Lilian remained calm and introduced the next match.

Match #5
 RVD and Chris Jericho stood at opposite sides of the ring as the referee patted each man down looking for any hidden weapons. The referee gave both the all clear and rang the bell to start the match. Both men circled around each other before making their way into the center of the ring. They locked up and Van Dam got the early advantage and moved behind Jericho. He tripped him down and went for a standing moonsault but Jericho got out of the way and charged back in. He took Van Dam down with a bulldog and went for the Lionsault. Van Dam gout out of the way as well as both men got back to their feet, at a stalemate. They both charged back toward one another but Van Dam knocked Jericho straight down with a standing kick to the head. As he dropped for a cover Jericho rolled away. Jericho seemingly having enough with playing games sneaked in a rake of the eyes and gained himself the advantage. With the advantage Jericho whipped Van Dam into the ropes and sent him down to the mat with a chop. He dropped down for a cover ONE…TWO... KICKOUT by Van Dam. Back up and Jericho took Van Dam up in a Vertical Suplex but Van Dam tried to fight it. Jericho adjusted his positioning and hit a Piledriver on Van Dam instead. He covered but only got a 2. Jericho was frustrated and argued with the referee about the count but all this accomplished was to allow Van Dam to get a roll up on Jericho. The referee dropped to make the count but Jericho kicks out after a two count. Van Dam then took control sending Jericho loopy with a few Arm Drags before catching him in a German Suplex. Cover...1...2...kickout. Jericho the squirmed underneath Van Dam and they power-up with both men looking for an Advantage. Jericho got it and slid Van Dam down for a backslide but again a count of 2 is all that he could get. Both men were up and feeling the effects of the match. Jericho charged at Van Dam who turned it into a Monkey Flip. With Jericho down on the mat Van Dam made his way up and looks for the split leg moonsault but Jericho somehow got up and caught Van Dam on his shoulders! Van Dam managed to wriggle free and he took Jericho down to the mat with an Ace Crusher. He made his way up to the top of the ropes and came flying down with a 5* Frog Splash! The fans applauded, and RVD lay on top of his fallen opponent...1...2..3!!! Van Dam got up, clutcing his ribs from the impact, and the referee raised his hand.

Winner: RVD
Reason: Better Roleplay. Thanks to Ian for writing this match.

Match #6
 The crowd cheered for nostalgia's sake as the Ultimate Warrior came out to the ring. Undertaker came out to the ring to a loud reception. They locked eyes in the ring as the casket match got under way. They exchanged hard punches and the crowd began to roar. Undertaker dropped Warrior with a DDT, and the crowd got onto it's feet. The match became surprisingly fast paced, as Warrior got the upper hand. They ended up moving to the outside and Undertaker threw Warrior on top of one of the caskets. The action was getting intense as Undertaker pinned Warrior to the casket with his leg. Warrior chopped Taker in the chest and then grabbed him by the arm throwing him onto the casket. Warrior chopped Taker in the chest again and again. Taker groaned and managed to grab Warrior by the scruff of his neck. Taker threw him into the ringpost and Warrior came off holding his head. After a hard fought battle on the outside, the men found themselves back in the ring. Undertaker managed to hit Old School on Warrior and the crowd cheered. Warrior took a leap and knocked Taker out of the ring. They exchanged blows against the security railing, and Warrior was able to clothesline Undertaker back toward the caskets. Warrior closed Taker into the casket, and the crowd cheered in anticipation of the matches end. The cheers grew louder as Warrior leaned over the casket in order to close it. Undertaker reached up and grabbed Warrior around the throat. The crowd was electric as Taker kept a firm hold of Warrior's throat and managed to climb out of the casket. He got both feet on the floor, and then executed a thunderous chokeslam. Warrior landed motionlessly in the casket. The crowd cheered as Taker slammed the lid shut, winning the match.

Winner: Undertaker
Reason: Amazing battle guys! I would suggestions longer rps and less posts just because it would be easier to judge that way. I chose Undertaker because it seemed like his character's stance developed a lot quicker! Good job from both.

Match #7
 John Cena got a thunderous reaction as he made his way to the ring. He saluted the crowd and grinned as a good portion of them booed him loudly. He marched down the ramp with his usual charisma and slid into the ring. Wazup began to play and the crowd booed as Ron Killings stepped out onto the stage. He was accompanied by Konnan and BG James and they all touched their fists together, exchanging words of encouragement before Killings slid into the ring. Killings and Cena exchanged a little trash talk while Shelton Benjamin made his entrance. Shelton got a good reaction as he slid into the ring. The referee checked the men for weapons before signalling for the timekeeper to ring the bell. Killings and Shelton teamed up to go after Cena for a few minutes. They hit him with a double suplex but Killings turned around and kicked Shelton Benjamin in the ribs, before hitting a DDT. Killings covered Shelton, 1...2...strong kickout after two. Shelton got up and pushed Killings, arguing with him. Killings swung a punch at Shelton who ducked it and followed up with a dropkick. He then hopped onto the top rope and dived off, catching the falling Killings with an elbow drop. He taunted for a split second before making the cover, but by that time Cena was back up and broke the count with a diving fist to Shelton's head. Shelton rolled off, holding the back of his head in pain and Cena kicked him in the back for good measure before picking Shelton up and throwin him out of the ring. The referee's back was turned and Konnan got a few cheap shots in on the "Gold Standard" while BG James cheered for Killings to get up. Cena hit the five knuckle shuffle and went for a cover. 1...2...kickout. Konnan was done kicking Shelton and he rolled him back into the ring. Shelton got up, slowly, and went back after Cena. The two locked up and Cena got Shelton in a side headlock, which he then turned into a bulldog. He climbed to the top rope himself, and hit a legdrop. Cover by Cena...kick out at two! Killings hit Cena in the face with a forearm as soon as he got back up and he kneed him in the stomach. Cena was vulnerable for a second and Killings went for the Truth Conviction. Cena reversed! He hoisted Shelton up onto his shoulders and there was a mixture of loud cheers and boos as he went for the FU! He got it. Cover by Cena..1...2...Shelton got up, and ran towards the cover...3! Shelton didn't make it in time! He kicked Cena in the back and the two exchanged punches before the referee broke up the fight. He raised Cena's hand and Cena saluted the audience once more before leaving the ring.

Winner: John Cena.
Reason: Best roleplay. Killings' was late. Sorry you had to wait for the match, guys.

SEGMENT
 Joey Styles: Ladies and Gentlemen thank you got tuning in to EBWF Aftermath. Our main event tonight... Wait... King? What's this?

The tron lit up and Undertaker was seen leaving the arena. A car sped around the corner, and hit Undertaker. The Phenom went down on impact.

King: Someone has just hit the Undertaker! I can't believe what we're seeing Joey!

The camera panned out to see Paul Bearer exiting the car along with a few Druids. They threw Undertaker into the back of the car.

Joey Styles: The Undertaker looks very hurt! Wait, what's Paul Bearer doing?

Paul Bearer got into the car and sped off with Undertaker in the backseat.

Reason: Storyline.

Match #8
 The crowd gave Paul London a luke warm reaction and popped loudly when "Sexy Boy" hit. Shawn came out to the ring with his usual entrance and purposely ignored the crowd in an attempt to gain some heat. The two men locked up, and London took the lead with a inverted DDT. The crowd cheered as Shawn grabbed at his back, and London picked him up by the hair. Shawn took hard jabs to London's mid-section and the crowd started to "Wooo" when he started the chest chops. The match seemed to be going in Shawn's favor, and the crowd popped mostly out of confusion. Shawn irish whipped London, who inadvertently hit the referee. Shawn soon realized the confused cheers were for The Miz who had appeared from the stage and was now entering the ring. Shawn tried to counter a tornado DDT, but fell victim. Miz exited the ring as quickly as he came. London made the pin and the ref came to, counting the 1...2...3. The crowd booed, and Shawn made it to his knees. He made eye contact with the Miz as London was being rewarded the X-Division Title. The Miz said "Just Business" as a video package for the next match began.

Winner: Andrew
Reason: Shev's didn't have much promo. :)

Match #9
 Alive and Amplified hit and The Miz made his entrance to a pretty good reaction. He played up to the crowd a little, but not too much. When Chosen One hit Miz stared up the ramp as Jarrett began to make his way to the ring. Jarrett had his guitar with him, and Dusty accompanied him. Dusty and Jarrett exchanged a few words before Dusty took a seat at the announce table and Jarrett climbed into the ring. The cage was lowered from the ceiling as The Miz and Jeff Jarrett stood in the ring, staring a hole through one another. Once the cage was properly secured, the bell rang. The two remained motionless for a moment before Jarrett charged at the Miz, still holding his guitar. He swung it. Miz ducked and Jarrett dropped the guitar and spun around quickly. Miz caught him with a kick to the midsection followed by a neckbreaker. Jarrett held the back of his head but didn't stay down for long. The two locked up, engaging in a test of strength. Jarrett tricked Miz as he kicked him in the knee as soon as they locked hands. He then threw Miz into the corner of the ring before running at him with a clothesline. Miz went down after the clothesline but Jarrett didn't cover him. He furiously picked Miz up and threw him into the cage. Miz held his head as he fell back through the ropes, and Jarrett still didn't cover. He slingshot Miz into the cage and only then did he attempt to go for the pin. 1...2...Miz kicked out. Jarrett didn't seem to mind. He wasn't done yet. Miz caught Jarrett with a stiff right hand to the jaw as Jarrett was picking him up. He then hit him with a jawbreaker and Jarrett fell back, holding the underside of his face. Miz went for a quick Reality Check but Jarrett managed to get out of the way and Miz missed the move. He ran towards Jeff again, attempting what looked like a Shining Wizard, but Jarrett charged too and the two collided in the center of the ring, with Jarrett knocking Miz down with a clothesline. Miz got back up fairly quickly, and slapped Jarrett in the face. Jarrett was even more furious than he had been previously and the two started to exchange right hands. Miz got the better of the exchange and he threw Jarrett into the ropes before hitting him with a spinebuster. He went for a cover..1....2....Jarrett kicked out. The two continued to brawl and within no time both men were bleeding. It was most definitely a grudge match, and the most violent of the night, by far. Neither man attempted to escape the cage. They wanted to win the match by pinfall or submission. Dusty cheered Jarrett on from the outside as he picked up the guitar and swung it at Miz. Miz ducked, and swept Jarrett's leg. He picked the guitar up himself, waited for Jarrett to get up then hit him with his own guitar. He went for the cover...1...2...3! The Miz was announced as the winner as the cage was lifted. He left the ring grinning as Dusty Rhodes climbed into the ring to make sure Jarrett was okay.

Winner: Miz
Reason: I felt his roleplay was more entertaining and original...but both you guys did a great job.

Match #10
 (Sic) by Slipknot hit and Samoe Joe and Nick Mondo made their way to the ring. Lilian introduced them as "Twisted" and the fans gave them a pretty good reaction. They waited in the ring and soon enough "Break It Down!" blasted over the speakers. DX stepped through the curtain, each holding a tag title. Each also had a belt around their waist. Triple H slid into the ring first, looking furious with nobody in particular. He didn't even bother with the "Are you ready?!" spiel...he just took off his belt and started to hand it to the referee before turning around and clocking Nick Mondo in the head with it. Shawn simultaneously charged at Joe and the two started to fight. The bell rang and the match was underway. The referee admonished Triple H, but Hunter didn't care...there was nothing the ref could do anyway. Hunter turned around to see Samoe Joe hit Shawn Michaels with a Samoan Drop. He then ripped Shawn's X division title from around his waist and threw it out of the ring. Hunter went for a forearm but Joe lifted him up onto his shoulders and hit a fireman's carry. Hunter held his ribs as Joe's weight fell on him and he gasped in pain. Joe then went to see if Nick Mondo was okay. Nick was a little groggy, but DX had made him mad so he got back up quickly. Triple H and Shawn Michaels fought back to their feet and Hunter kicked Joe in the ribs. The fans booed, thinking Hunter might be going for an early pedigree, but he just hit a hard DDT. He got back up in time to be met with a spinning heel kick from Nick Mondo. Mondo elbow dropped the Game and this time Shawn hit Mondo with a dropkick. The referee warned Shawn to get out of the ring. He hadn't had control of this match since it had begun. Shawn argued with the referee but went to his corner. Mondo rolled out of the ring and made his way to his corner, leaving Triple H and Joe. Joe got up first and pulled Hunter up too. He threw him into the ropes before hitting him with an even rougher Samoan Drop than the one he had hit on HBK a few moments ago. Cover. 1...2...Triple H kicked out. Triple H fought back and swept Joe's leg. Joe was on his back and Hunter kicked him in the groin area. Joe squirmed and the referee warned Triple H about the low blow. Triple H insisted he had kicked Joe on his inner leg, but the referee told him to "knock it off". Hunter got in the ref's face and Joe started to get back up. Before Joe could go anywhere HBK ran into the ring and hit him with the Sweet Chin Music. The referee turned around to admonish Shawn now. He was getting furious with DX for continuing to break the rules. While the ref's back was turned Nick Mondo ran into the ring and hit Hunde with a low blow before yelling a string of curse words in his face and hitting him with a DDT. The referee started to count to 5, and Shawn reminded him they they'd keep the belts if he DQed them. Joe got up and charged at HBK, knocking him over the top rope. He then went for a cover on DX again...1...2...kickout. Joe shook his head and he and Triple H got up again. Joe threw Hunter into the ropes and Hunter slid under them. He grabbed a chair and looked immensely frustrated as he ran back into the ring with it. HBK had pulled Mondo off the apron and the two were fighting on the outside. The referee was yelling at Triple H to put the chair down but Hunter didn't listen. He mouthed something to Joe who just glared at him. Before either Joe or Triple H could move "Almost Over" hit and Wes Ikeda stepped out onto the stage with a mic. Triple H frowned up at him and mouthed "what the hell do you want?"

Wes Ikeda: Oh...hi Hunter. You're sure doing a lot to prove your masculinity tonight, that's obvious. But you'd better think a little harder before swinging that chair. Because as of right now...in this match...the tag titles can change hands in the event of a DQ or countout.

Samoa Joe broke into a grin and held out his arms as if daring Triple H to hit him. Triple H stared only at Wes, then without hesitation he swung the chair at Joe's head, knocking him down. The referee rang the bell and Lilian declared Twisted as the new tag team champions. Wes and Hunter made eye contact for a moment before Wes shook his head and went backstage. Triple H watched him go and got hit with a kendo stick from behind. Mondo was back in the ring. HBK was knocked out on the outside after being thrown into the steps. and Nick was the only one standing. He helped Joe up and the two raised the tag belts before leaving the ring.

Winners: Twisted
Reason: I didn't RP...because I suck!
