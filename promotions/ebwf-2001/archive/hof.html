Entry #1
Name: Eddie
Character(s): Tommy Dreamer, Mike Awesome, The Sandman, Jerry Lynn, Jeff Jarrett
Accomplishments: Eddie was the first person to succesfully run a PPV caliber storyline with himself, using all three of his characters in a dynamic fued. Personally, Eddie is one of the sweetest guys out of character, and never forgets to just hang out, and have a good time. Congratulations Eddie on your well deserved HOF spot!!
Entry #2
Name: Doug
Character(s): Raven, Cactus Jack, Mick Foley, Stevie Richards, Scott Steiner, Shaggy 2 Dope, Violent J
Accomplishments: Doug has done almost everything in the EBWF. He has been here since the days of the EWF and the only person that has been here longer than The Badd Boy and Ashlee. Doug has held almost every title and has raised the bar to new standards of the World and Hardcore divisions. Congratulations Doug on your long over due spot in the EBWF Hall of Fame!
Entry #3
Name: Mike S.
Character(s): Triple H
Accomplishments: One of Mike's claims to fame here in the EBWF has been his accomplishement at the Intercontinental Championship level. He has by far been one of the best if not the best at this level and has done more work than anyone else. Only one man has came close, but has not surpassed Mike and his work. Besides that he has been a loyal member of the EBWF and has stuck with the federation in both the slow times and the good. The EBWF owes a lot to Mike, we only hope that this is just the start of his legacy.
Entry #4
Name: Christina
Character(s): Eric Bischoff; Victoria; Lita; Commissioner Chaotic Christina; Montanna Bischoff; Christina Hancock; Accomplishments: Christina's has always been a top notch competitor. It was common to find her in the contention for Royal Rumbles and King/Queen of the ring tournaments. When she put up the fight for a championship, she did just that, but what made her most well noted were her storyline ideas. Always thinking up a new plot involving her characters she helped be sure to keep things entertaining. One of the most famous was her noted storyline of The Rock and Lita relationship. We remember all of the ideas she has had, and look forward to all of her future ideas. Sometimes it isn't all of the gold that you won, even though she did win it, it can also be all about your participation and what you give to the federation. Thank you for your dedication Christina.
Entry #5
Name: Brandon
Character(s): Bret Hart; HBK Shawn Michaels; Accomplishments: Brandon has seemingly done it all here in the EBWF. Using Bret Hart to win all of the EBWF championships offered at one time was one of his greatest feats. This included him wearing four of them at the same time. He has an incredible win/loss record and as of 2004 he was both the Royal Rumble and the King of the Ring champion. Winning both in the same year was unprecidented before Brandon. The future looks strong with this tough rp'er as he is destined to do even more down the road.
Entry #6
Name: Larry
Character(s): The Undertaker; Kane; Ultimate Warrior; Christy Hemme; Accomplishments: You hear this a lot when talking about people being inducted into the Hall of Fame, but Larry has just about done it all here in the EBWF. He has been a multiple Hardcore, Peoples, Intercontinental, Tag Team, and World Heavy Weight Champion. Even holding the now long defunct Massacre Championship in the days of old. The Undertaker has been a staple of the EBWF from its beginning. Always a feared competitor, he's always been right at the top of the Royal Rumbles and King of the Ring tournaments only inches away from winning. His hard work and dedication to the EBWF is unrivaled by most who visit with the federation. He seems far from being done, and will be looking only to add to his decorated list of accomplishments in the EBWF. He's worked hard and earned his spot in the Hall of Fame. Thank you Larry for your hard work, we hope you've only begun to reap the benefits of your work and dedication.
Entry #7
Name: Joey
Character(s): Chris Jericho; Jeff Hardy; Accomplishments: We had been planning to induct Joey for sometime, and we always make an induction at Wrestlemania. He has been here for several years, starting out as Chris Jericho and then picking up Jeff Hardy as he matured as an RPer. He has been one of the greatest people to work with, never causing any problems (except that one time… what lol) and I have thoroughly enjoyed working with him. Tony and I have been honored to have him, and [name withheld] is welcome in the EBWF whenever he would like. As long as he doesn’t pull a [name withheld]… my one regret is that all Joey ever needed was the People’s title to Grand Slam. So buddy if you get bored this summer. My door is open. Joey also has the sexiest voice in efeding.
Entry #8
Name: [name withheld]
Character:
Chris Jericho Accomplishments: Ben is one of the kindest friends you could be lucky enough to have. For the majority of his career he always put his best foot forward and did everything he could to help others personally and in the game. Ben is the longes reigning champion having held the the World Title for 112 straight days. He held the Tag Team Championships with [name withheld] (Bret Hart) and was one of only two teams who was able to beat Raven and CM Punk in their heyday. Ben is also a 4x People's Champion. Among his other accomplishments he managed to successfully operate the IXWF while competively rping here in the EBWF, not to mention that he has been a dear friend of the Ikedas for over 5 years, which is a feat in itself. Congratulations Ben, and Welcome to the Hall of Fame!
Entry #9
Name: [name withheld]
Character (s):
Alex Shelley, Syxx, Scott Hall Accomplishments: [name withheld] is a down to earth and generally fun person to write and chat with. He not only has a succesful career in terms of championships and accomplishments, but he has also contributed wonderfully to EBWF Storylines, running numerous feuds pitting his own characters against each other, as well as mingling with the rest of the roster. He also shows great variety in his writing, being able to bring more than one persona to life in an entertaining way. His run with Alex Shelley as the TV (People's Champion) is the longest in history, and at the time this award was given, still had not ended. Alex Shelley is also the youngest ever World Champion, and Ben's Syxx character played a huge (if not, the biggest) part in the nWo invasion of 2007. He has more than earned his spot in the EBWF Hall of Fame. Congratulations, Ben!
Entry #10
Name: Ashlee
Character (s):
Wes Ikeda, Trish Stratus, Chyna, Lita, Teddy Hart Accomplishments: Without Ashlee, there wouldn't be an EBWF Hall of Fame, or even an EBWF, so I think it's only right that she be given her rightful place along its elite. Ashlee's accomplishments here would take forever to list, so I'm just going to list the things I think make her stand out the most. Ashlee is one of the most gifted writers I've ever known. She writes so convincingly that her characters seem to jump off the screen. Her talent for detail is simply amazing. She deeply explores not only the environments she is writing about, but also the inner depths of the characters themselves. Also, Ashlee is one of very few efed owners never to put the world title on herself. And I think that proves that the reason this place is around is for us, just as much as it is for her. Ashlee helped the EBWF develop into a place where e-fedding wasn't just about promos, but about characters, real and on-screen stories, and human beings. She's given us not only a hobby to engage in for the last 6 years, but she's also provided us with an EBWF Family. The EBWF isn't just an efed...Ashlee has created a place where we can meet new friends, or re-meet old ones, and I know I'm grateful for that. You know what? The hall of fame is not worthy. It should bow down to Ashlee! We all should...figuratively speaking, of course. Congratulations, Ash. This has been a long time in coming, and you deserve it.
Entry #11
Name: Shev
Character (s):
Triple H, Shawn Michaels, Christy Hemme, David Flair Accomplishments: Without Shev results would pretty much always be about ten days late. Her commitment to the EBWF speaks magnitudes about her character. She is an amazing writer, an amazing friend, and has a love for everyone who RPs in the EBWF. She brings characters to life with personalities that were once not thought important in EFedding. Shev is the first and only female rper to hold the EBWF World Title, and the first and only female to successfully handle two characters, form a tag team and earn the titles. This induction is a long time coming, and I am happy to welcome her to the EBWF Hall of Fame.
