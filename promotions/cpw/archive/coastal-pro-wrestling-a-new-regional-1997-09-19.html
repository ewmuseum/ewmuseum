[CPW] Coastal Pro Wrestling! A new regional!

19 September 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Dear RSPWF, My friend [name withheld] and I are starting up another new regional fed, called Coastal Pro Wrestling. It should shape up to be great and we've already got some pretty damn good roleplayers here. If you're interested, drop me a line and you'll get an app as soon as possible. We're only looking for the cream of the crop when it comes to roleplaying, so if you're not the tops, you need not apply. I'm the Vice President and have a pile of e-wrestling experience in EWA, PCW, MLWO, ect. I've also run my own fed on numerous occassions and if you want to ask some people about my credentials try [detail withheld] , [detail withheld] , [detail withheld] , ect. My partner in crime, the President of CPW, handles Sean Stevens in the MLWO. He's a great roleplayer and a great head, we're looking forward to starting this organization off. I'm the head recruiter, by the way, in case you were wondering and I'm going to do my best to recruit some top talent from RSPWF, AOL, LOOP, Summit, and more. If you think you can handle Coastal Pro Wrestling and it's extraordinary expectations, please fillout that application below or send in your own application to [detail withheld] with a cc: [detail withheld] . We'll be looking forward to hearing from the best of you! Thank you for your time. Stay Cool, Briggs P.S.
