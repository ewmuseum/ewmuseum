TRY to follow these guidelines when sending stuff in! It's NOT the end of the world if you DON'T but it IS nice if you DO! :-) WHAT TO SEND? v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^ - TO GET MATCHES: Challenge, Challenge, Challenge! - TO WIN: Roleplay, Roleplay, and (did I mention?) Roleplay! - ALSO IT HELPS TO: Do in ring interviews! (Saturday Night Drop Zone) Just leave space for the announcers to ask questions, or write them yourself. You fill in the answers or whatever and we'll do the rest! These are due the Thursday before the card if the questions are to be filled in by me, otherwise it's OK to have them in for Friday. - AND IT NEVER HURTS TO: Send in a STRAT! That's short for STRATEGY. A strategy would include how you want your guy to go about wrestling the match, or any sneak attacks/double crosses that you want to see happen. This is not required every time ... just when you want something special to happen. It REALLY helps improve the angles and plotlines. We can't always follow your suggestion to a "T" - for instance, say that another person has a strat that conflicts with your strat. Then, we have to compromise - but we will try. - THE BOOKING SYSTEM: This is fairly simple and laid out. Your flashes and in-rings count for three quarters of winning/losing. Strats and the heat you build count for 5%. And angles count for 20%. What this means is that flashes/in-ring are the most important, and most of the time if your flashes are better, well, you win. __BUT__, if there is an angle that I like, or Len likes, you MAY job to fulfill the angle, ESPECIALLY if the flashing is close. In this fed, there is an "everybody jobs at one time or another" clause. None of us are omnipotent, therefore, you can and will job at times. Now, if you don't like it, there's the door. We're not forcing you to stay. __BUT__, these are fair rules and shouldn't cause too many problems. Let's have some fun! RP for your matches! I mean, it ain't that hard to do one rp and an in-ring, after all :) If you are chronically not flashing then you are going to be expelled from the fed. I can't have inactive flashers. - RP'ING RULE #2: You are allowed the following _PER WEEK_, as in number of flashes: * _2_ roleplay * _1_ in-ring (wrestler(s) get(s) in the ring on card and speaks) * _1_ character development flash (does not count towards matches) Don't feel that you absolutely need to do the rp _and_ the in-ring to win. But you'd better do one or the other, at the least, unless you wanna job. In case you don't know, the week begins on Sunday and ends on Saturday. - RP'ING RULE #3: Chronic rulebreaking leads to jobbing. - RP'ING RULE #4: Our cards are almost always on Saturdays. That being said...challenges are due 6 days before the card at 8 p.m. EST, strats are due 4 days before the card at 5 p.m. EST, and roleplays and in-rings are due the day before the card at midnight EST. THEREFORE, for a Saturday card, challenges are due Monday at 8 p.m. EST the week of the card. Strats are due Wednesday at 5 p.m. EST, and in-rings and roleplays are due Friday at midnight EST. BTW, a day starts at 12:01 a.m., so by midnight Friday I mean Friday night at midnight, not Thursday night. v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^ R U M O R S v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^ [Check'em out at http://members.aol.com/[name withheld]/cpw.html !] v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^ R A N K I N G S v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^ # Wrestler [W-L-D; Win Percentage], Points [10/Pinfall or Submission Win] [5/Count Out or Disqualification Win] [0/Draw] [-5/Count Out or DQ Loss] [-10/Pinfall or Submission Loss] 1 "Fearless" Frank Colite [2-0-0; 1.000], 20pts 2 Kid Vicious [1-0-0; 1.000], 10pts 2 Stony Ruiz [1-0-0; 1.000], 10pts 2 "Superstar" Scott Storm [1-0-0; 1.000], 10pts 2 Brian Darling [1-0-0; 1.000], 10pts 2 Jason Priest [1-0-0; 1.000], 10pts 2 Edain Spears [1-0-0; 1.000], 10pts 3 Kaine [1-1-0; 0.500], 0pts 4 Polo Stetson [0-1-0; 0.000], -5pts 4 Xavier Othello [1-1-0; 0.000], -5pts 5 Omni [0-1-0; 0.000], -10pts 5 "Living Legend" Eric Cooper [0-1-0; 0.000], -10pts 5 "Studly" Steven Black [0-1-0; 0.000], -10pts 5 Generic Jobber [0-1-0; 0.000], -10pts v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^ INTERNET RELAY CHAT [IRC] v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^ You'll find all of us on EFNet, on the channel #e-wrestling.
