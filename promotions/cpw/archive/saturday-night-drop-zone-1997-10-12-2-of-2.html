[CPW] Saturday Night Drop Zone [10.12.97] Part 2

14 October 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[MAIN EVENT!] NO DQ MATCH FOR CPW TAG TEAM TITLES! Tubanators vs Satan's Minions vs Activists From Hell By: [name withheld] <OOC: For whatever reason you didn't catch Monday Night Raw...watch it. It was seriously one of the best shows they've put out in a _while_. Badd Blood was awesome too...Vader _LANDED ON HIS FEET_ after a missed moonsault, Michaels fell 10 feet through a table and was blading bigtime, Owen won the IC belt w/ help from Austin...for those of you watching the WCW, I suggest you flip the channel on Monday nights. OK, now that my no-reason WWF plug is in here, commence the main event. Enjoy.> MIKE: It's been a great night...and up next is the match that'll make it even greater. In our Coastal main event, we will decide the next tag team champions. The Turbanators will take on the Activists from Hell...and both of them will take on Satan's Minions. DANNY: This should be cool... TOMMY: Especially with the added stip. MIKE: Yes, sir, fans, over the break, we found that this match has been changed to a no-disqualification match, to make things more interesting! It should be great...let's go down to Zach Michaels as we determine our tag team champs! ZACH: Ladies and gentlemen, the following contest is a tag team triangle match with a 30 minute time limit! <pop> ZACH: The winners will become the first-EVER Coastal Pro Wrestling Virginian tag team champions! <bigger pop> ZACH: _And_, I have just been informed that it has been changed to a NO-DISQAULIFICATION matchup! <HUGE pop> ["Here Comes The Hotstepper" by Ini Kamoze plays over the loudspeakers.] ZACH: Introducing first ... originally from Afghanistan, now hailing from New York, NY, weighing a combined 625 pounds, here are Asad Bahadur, Saqib Sneeb ... THE TURBANATORS!!!!!!!! [The Turbanators walk down the aisle wearing their traditional red turbans, and white baggy indian silk pants which have a belt on them, and wear sunglasses. They always taunt the fans and they have an odour of indian food on them.] [They get into the ring and take their turbans off. They both have long black hair which is tied up.] ["Here Comes the Hotstopper" slowly fades, and "Down Rodeo" by Rage Against the Machine plays over the loudspeakers, taking its place. ZACH: Introducing next .... hailing from Minneapolis, MN, standing 5-10, wighing 245 pounds, here is .... THE LIBERAL ZAK RAGE!!!! And his tag team partner, he also hails from Minneapolis, MN, he stands 6-3 and weighed in tonight at 275 pounds, here is .... THE ADVOCATE JOSHUA BAKER! Together, they comprise .... ___THE ACTIVISTS FROM HELL___! ["The Liberal" Zak Rage walks down the aisle. Zak is handing out leaflets and babbling away incessantly about recent events and issues. His tag team partner is basically standing behind him, doing the same.] ["Down Rodeo" fades, and immediately the arena turns pitch black as a soft, lurking guitar riff grumbles over the PA. As "Children of the Grave" by Black Sabbath becomes more apparent, blue strobes begins flickering from the ground, as two big bruisers begins jogging down the aisle.] RING ANNOUNCER: Introducing first ... hailing from Camden, NJ, at a combined weight of 558lbs... the Innovators of Doom... seldom have two men been feared and cheered at the same time so often as these two monsters... these journeymen are the deliverers of hell, battling evil with evil. They have topped the charts in leagues like SOW, WWA, and TWA. They come to CPW to do the same. Welcome now to the ring... ARCHANGEL.... BLACKBLOOD... they are SATAN'S MINIONS!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! [Two men, standing at an average height and a thick frame enter the ring. One wears a "Bark at the Moon" t-shirt and cut-off denims. He has a slightly goofy, slightly psychotic cockeyed smile, and a shaven head. He is Blackblood. The other wears his hair in a long black ponytail that hangs down to his back. He wears a leather jacket covering a Metallica's "Jump In The Fire" t-shirt. He is Archangel.] MIKE: Well...it looks like the Activits will send Zak Rage into the ring, and the Turbanators will send Asad Bahadur. Remember, in this match you can tag out to anyone at anytime. <Zak and Asad lock up...Asad immediately hits a kick to the stomach, but Zak reverses the subsequent whip into the ropes and immediately hits a savate kick. Rage immediately gets back onto the bigger Asad with a kick to the stomach, and another. Zak then picks Asad up, and whips him into the turnbuckle. He then runs in with a Stinger splash, but Asad puts his foot up. Zak sees this, and drop toeholds Asad down to the canvas. Zak then climbs to the top as Badahur gets up, and missle dropkicks him from upstairs. "The Liberal" is all over Asad in the early going, as he picks him up and once again throws him into the turnbuckle...the problem is, this is an unfriendly corner, with the Minions waiting. Zak charges into the corner, but Blackblood pulls him out of the way. Joshua Baker, Zak's tag team partner, takes offense to this and runs over to the Minions' corner. He is back body dropped out of the ring after charging at Archangel...but he lands on his feet and takes the time to pull on Archangel's leg, which drops Arch's face right on the ring apron. Blackblood and Josh start to brawl inside of the ring...as the ref turns around, the Turbanators take advantage of this, and begin to doubleteam Zak in the ring. They hit a double elbow, and then a double dropkick. Zak is then picked up, and just _pummeled_ in the corner by the men in turbans...until the referee turns around, and immediately Saqid Sneeb stops to pelt Zak, and the ref doesn't suspect a thing. Mental mistake, as the match is no-DQ. The brawl continues on the outside between Blackblood and Joshua, as Archangel slowly gets up. Asad whips Zak into the ropes, and clotheslines him back down.> DANNY: Already...we're maybe 1 minute into this match, maybe a minute and a half or maybe 2, and we have two teams brawling on the outside. Amazing. MIKE: Well, I think all 3 of these teams won't stop until they have the gold. And remember, this match is no-DQ! TOMMY: <sarcastically> No, really? MIKE: <sigh> <Asad then drops a leg on the neck of Zak, and climbs up, and promptly does it again. Asad then elbows Rage's knee, and stomps on it for good measure, trying to ground the high-flying sensation. Badahur whips Zak into the ropes, and on the rebound, Zak is tilt-a-whirl backbreakered. Asad then climbs to the top rope...and MOONSAULTS "The Liberal", who collapses against the canvas. He covers...one, two, and that's all he gets, as Rage yanks a shoulder up before the ref can slap the mat for a third time. Zak is now picked up, and a tag is made to Saqid Sneed, Asad's tag team partner. The brawl between Blackblood and Joshua on the outside is finally broken up, and the Minions and Joshua are both standing on their respective turnbuckles. Zak is whipped into the ropes by both Turbanators, and he is double headbutted back down to the canvas. Sneed picks up Zak, and he whips Rage into the ropes. On the rebound, Zak leaps over the ropes and lands on the apron. Saqid, dumbfounded, just stands in the ring like an idiot...Zak tries a springboard hurricanrana, but Sneed regains his awareness and _PLANTS_ Zak with a HUGE powerbomb, and holds the legs for the cover. There's a one, a two, and _NEARLY_ a count of three, as Joshua makes the save for his partner. Asad runs over and knocks Joshua off the ring apron, angered, and Baker hits HARD against the guardrail. Asad climbs out of the ring, but immediately comes back in the ring, and this time he has a PRESENT...otherwise known as a table.> MIKE: I wonder what he has up his sleeve. DANNY: Something that hurts... TOMMY: _Duh_. <Asad and Saqid set up the table in mid-ring...Saqid places Zak on the table, and then lays ON TOP OF HIM...Asad then climbs to the top, and comes _CRASHING_ down with a flying splash through the table! HUGE CROWD POP! Zak is out cold, and blood starts to trickle down his face...Asad finally leaves the ring, giving his partner, the legal man, Saqid Sneeb, the privledge of pinning Rage...the elementary cover - one, two, KICKOUT! Another huge pop emits from the crowd as Zak Rage is able to power out of the cover after having over 600 pounds of human weight - both Turbanators - put onto him as he went through a table. Zak is still hurt though, and Saqid now tags out to Archangel of Satan's Minions. Archangel picks up Zak, and whips him into the ropes...and immediately kicks him in the gut and delivers a beautiful, stiff powerbomb. He holds the legs for a pin...one, two, but Zak once again slips out. Archangel then picks Zak up, and kicks him in the shin, and that sets up for a double underhook DDT, which he _nails_. Zak is down on the canvas...Archangel picks him up again, and whips him into the turnbuckle. Zak hits hard, and comes off a rebound, and is met with a mule kick by Archangel. The Minions member now picks Zak up, and chops him in the chest, and again. He then kicks Zak in the chest, and whips him into the corner...and begins pummeling. He kicks Zak 3 or 4 times in the stomach. He then places Zak on the top rope...and climbs up himself, setting up for a superplex. Archangel takes the Minneapolis resident..._from the top_...superplex...REVERSED INTO A FLYING CROSS BODY! One, two, shoulder up!> DANNY: Zak needs to tag, and BADLY. He's been in trouble nearly since the beginning of the match, and you have a relatively fresh Archangel in there ready to tear him apart. TOMMY: Zak sucks. DANNY: Funny that you're telling us about it... TOMMY: YOU ARE DISGUSTING! <Danny cackles maniacally> MIKE: Both of you, shut up! <Archangel and Rage both lay on the mat, hurt and dazed. Archangel is up first, and Zak is unable to make that vital tag. Archangel takes Rage, and whips him into the ropes, and delivers a HUGE elbow. Zack spits out due to the impact, and Archangel catches some in his face. This gets him mad, and Archangel kicks Zak in the stomach, and then whips him into the turnbuckle, and hits a HUGE clothesline with authority. He then climbs to the top while Zak takes time to recover, leaps, and hits a _beautiful_ kick, right to the back of the haed. Zak crumbles to the canvas, face-first, staining it with the blood from his cut. Archangel has no mercy and is right back on Zak, with a pickup, and an atomic drop, followed by a kick to the back of the neck. Rage, who has been thrown around like a rag doll since the beginning of the match, once again finds himself on the mat. Archangel drops, and hooks a leg...one, two, thr-NO! Zak is _barely_ able to pull his right shoulder off of the canvas. Archangel, frustrated, hits the mat with both palms, and then climbs to the outside of the ring, waiting for Zak to get up. He does, and Archangel leaps with a hurricarana...but Zak gets out of the way at the last possible second! The crowd cheers, and Zak immediately starts staggering towards his corner. Joshua Baker reaches out an arm...but at the last possible moment, Archangel pulls Zak back, once again stopping the momentum of "The Liberal". Archangel knees Rage in tn the back, and sends him cross-corner into the Minion's corner...where Rage suddenly tags an unsuspecting Blackblood! HUGE CROWD POP!> TOMMY: NO! NO NO! This isn't fair! How...how can you expect the Minions to _fight_ each other? DANNY: I'm not sure. And for once, Tommy, I agree with you. Having to fight yer own tag team partner isn't exactly one of the highlights of your day... MIKE: But such is the way the match played out, and such is the way it mut must be done, according to the rules of this matchup! DANNY: I'm surprised someone hasn't really taken advantage of this no-DQ thing yet. MIKE: As am I... <Blackblood and Archangel look at each other, and both are obviously perturbed that they must face each other. They shake hands, and immediately afterwards Blackblood lands a hard left to the hand of Archangel. Archangel responds by something that looks like biting Blackblood's ear...but then, Blackblood immediately falls to the canvas. Archangel covers him...one, two, and Saqib Sneed makes the save.> DANNY: That was _CRAFTY_! I like it! The Minions tried to win using brains instead of brawns... TOMMY: They took the wimp's way out, people will say, but a win is a win. MIKE: It _was_ a smart idea...honorable, perhaps not, but the Minions don't care about honor...they want the GOLD. As they well should. <With his plot failed, Archangel gets up, and moves over to the Turbanators' corner where he tags in Saqib Sneed, who prevented their title win. Saqib steps into the ring and stares down Blackblood. The two circle each other, attempting to gain some sort of psychological advantage...and then they lock up. Sneed, the bigger man, gains the advantage and whips Blackblood into the ropes. On the rebound, Blackblood clotheslines Sneed...who nosells. Sneed laughs at Blackblood, and picks him up by the neck, and in a show of strength, chokeslams him to the mat with one hand! Saqib now climbs to the outside, and comes back with a steel chair. Blackblood sees this, though, and as Sneed goes to hit Blackblood with the chair, he dropkicks Sneed down to the canvas with the chair on his face! The crowd roars their approval, and a crimson mask appears on the face of the New York taxi driver turned pro wrestler. Blackblood picks Sneed up, and whips the big man into the ropes. On the rebound, Saqib is clotheslined right in the chest by Blackblood, and he doubles over. Blackblood immediately punches Sneed in the...umm...let's just say it hurt. :) Saqib falls on one knee as the crowd "ooooooooooohs". The member of Satan's Minions takes the bloodied chair, and moves over to Saqib, and SMACKS him with it! Saqib falls to the canvas...hurt...unable to move.> MIKE: This could be it! THE MINIONS ARE GONNA WIN THE TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIPS! <The cover is made...one, two, and Joshua Baker attempts to make the save, but is held off by Archangel...the hand hits the mat for the third time, but the referee immediately holds up only two fingers! The crowd pops for the moxy of Saqib Sneed. Blackblood knows he's in trouble if the big guy gets up, so he promptly tags in Joshua Baker of the Activists from Hell. The 275-pounder steps into the ring...and is promptly met with a clothesline from a quite angry Saqib Sneed. Blood spurts out onto Joshua...who gets angry, and spits right in the big guy's face. Sneed is enraged at this, and punches Josh right in the face, and then punches him again. But Josh is _not_ intimidated, and gets right up in the Afghan's face. This prompts Zak Rage to come in and save his partner. Zak's interference brings in Asad Bahadur to come in the ring, and we have a 4 way brawl! The bigger Turbanators get the advantage, and bring the Activists to the corner...a double Irish whip ensues...BUT ZAK LEAPFROGS OFF OF BAKER'S SHOULDERS INTO A FRANKENSTEINER ONTO ASAD! _Huge_ crowd pop as Zak holds the legs for the cover...one, two, thr-NO! Asad powers out at two and a half, springs to his feet, and clotheslines Zak over the top rope. Now the Turbanators can doubleteam freely, and they do...Joshua Baker, the legal man along with Saqib Sneed, is being doubleteamed in the corner. The Turbanators are just pummeling him...Zak gets back into the ring, and starts to go over by the corner too, and 4 men are brawling, just in the corner. Meanwhile, Satan's Minions just stand by idly, catching their breath.> DANNY: We have a HUGE brawl here! A punch by Zak to Asad, Asad fights back, and now Saqib is mauling Joshua Baker with his rights and lefts! Zak hits Sneed, Sneed hits Zak, Asad hits Baker, Baker hits Asad, this thing is impossible to call! Zak figh - <The camera view changes to behind the turnbuckle, and we can all clearly see what made Danny quiet - a yellow sign on the turnbuckle that says "CAUTION: Maximum Capacity 1000 pounds!" As if by cue...> <<<<<SNAP>>>>> TOMMY: ...OH MY GOD! DEAR LORD, WHAT IN GOD'S NAME JUST HAPPENED! <The crowd pops HUGELY!> MIKE: THE TURNBUCKLE BROKE! IT SNAPPED FROM THE ROPES AND ALL 4 MEN COLLAPSED TO THE OUTSIDE! ZAK RAGE LANDS NECK FIRST ON THE GUARDRAIL! ASAD BADAHUR FELL FACE-FIRST ONTO THE CONCRETE AS DID HIS TAG TEAM PARTNER... DANNY: <horrified> And Joshua Baker just lost his children. MIKE: ...That's an..._unusual_ way to put it...<Tommy snickers>, as "The Advocate" Joshua Baker just got crotched on the guardrail during that fall. DANNY: Wha...LOOK OUT! <<<<<CRASH>>>>> <HUGE pop, as Archangel of Satan's Minions just hit a somersault plancha onto BOTH MEMBERS OF THE ACTIVISTS! The crowd is loving it as both Joshua Baker _and_ Zak Rage go flying into the crowd. Archangel raises his fist to the air, and the crowd replies with yet another pop. He slides back into the ring as Saqib Sneed gets up, and after some stumbling, at the referee's count of 7, throws Baker back into the ring and climbs in himself. He then picks Joshua up, and picks him up for an atomic drop, but Josh slides out, and gets in behind for an a back suplex...but Saqib pulls brass knucks out of his turban, and WHACKS Joshua with them! The ref, who can't admonish this behavoir, is forced to count as Sneed drops down to cover...one, two, and Archangel comes off the top with a flying legdrop, saving his chances of being a co-tag champion. Sneed, angry moves over to Archangel...Joshua rolls him up! One, two, thr-NO! Saqib grabbed the ropes at the last possible second, thus nullifying the cover. Joshua is up on his feet first, and moves over to Saqib, who is getting up. He kicks him in the stomach, and double underhooks the arms - PEDIGREE! Josh climbs to the top, and hits a flying elbowdrop right onto the neck of Sneed, who spasms, and Baker drops for the cover...one, two, thrNO! Barely a kickout by Saqib, who is in trouble, and knows it. He tries to make it to the corner of Satan's Minions, but Joshua wants the weakened Afghan in the ring, so he kicks him in the back...and propels him right into the corner of the Minions, where he tags Archangel into the match.> MIKE: That kick just backfired on Joshua Baker. TOMMY: No, really? God, you guys are so stupid sometimes. The fans can _see_ that the kick backfired. Have you any BRAINS!? MIKE: We're stupid and you're annoying. God help us all. <Danny rolls his eyes> <Archangel leaps over the ropes and catches the unsuspecting Joshua Baker with a slingshot dropkick...he immediately climbs to the top rope as Josh gets up...> MIKE: ___FALLEN ANGEL PRESS___! THIS MATCH COULD BE OVER! TOMMY: ONE! DANNY: TWO! TOMMY + DANNY: THREE! <ding ding ding> <The crowd EXPLODES! The pop is ENORMOUS in the CPW Arena, shaking the 5,000-seating building to its foundation! Archangel, bloodied from his plancha to the outside of the ring, sweat-filled from the action of the match, and overjoyed from the achievement he has just fulfilled, collapses to the canvas. Blackblood comes in the ring, and picks him up. Josh Baker gets up, looking upset, and the Turbanators curse themselves on the outside for not making the save soon enough. Zak Rage consoles his partner as the Minions are rewarded the belts from Zach Michaels.> ZACH: LADIES AND GENTLEMEN...HERE ARE YOUR WINNERS...AT A TIME OF TWENTY MINUTES, TWELVE SECONDS...AND _NEWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWW_ COASTAL PRO WRESTLING VIRGINIAN TAG TEAM CHAMPIONS...BLACKBLOOD AND ARCHANGEL... SSSSSSSSSSSSSSSSSSSATTTTTTTTTANNNNNNNNNNNNNNNNNNNN'SSSSSSSSSSSSSSS MMMMMMMMMMMMMMIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIINNNNNNNNNIIIIOOOOOOOOOOONSSSSSSSSSSSSSS! <Although it may be amazing that the fans are cheering for two men who claim to be the devil's advocates, they are all on their feet screaming like mad. The Minions hold up their belts, and the crowd SCREAMS with delight. The announcers remain silent, letting SM have their day in the sun. They eventually exit to the back, among the loud yells of the fans. The Activists and the Turbanators follow.> MIKE: What a night! DANNY: I would have to agree. TOMMY: It's been great, all-around, a spectacular night and a spectacular show. And remember, I don't impress easily. See ya, punks.
