Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Coastal Pro Wrestling introduced itself on 19 September 1997 as “another new regional fed,” its own Vice President pitching it to the newsgroup as an operation “only looking for the cream of the crop,” run by three co-presidents out of Virginia Beach. Its website went up six days later.

By its 14 October update, three of its four singles titles — the East Coast, Appalachian Valley, and Television Championships — stood vacant since the founding week, and its own planned first pay-per-view had already been postponed “so we can make a bit more sense out of this fed.”

Its one surviving show, Saturday Night Drop Zone, opened on 12 October 1997 with a tribute reel to Brian Pillman (1962-1997) before cutting to its own in-fiction cold open: a mock newsreel restaging the atomic bombing of Hiroshima and Nagasaki to announce that Virginia Beach's own CPW Arena had been chosen as wrestling's new “Drop Zone.”

Its main event, a no-disqualification three-way among the Turbanators, the Activists From Hell, and Satan's Minions, decided the promotion's first-ever Virginia Tag Team Championship — the only in-fiction title change its surviving record carries — with Satan's Minions coming out on top.

No closing document of its own survives; a separate account outside its own record puts Coastal Pro Wrestling's closure that November, less than two months after its website first opened.
