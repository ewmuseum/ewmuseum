GWC Voices - THE SNAKEPIT - 11.27.01

27 November 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
*************************** GranD WrestlinG CorP. PresentS... GWC VoiceS -- THE SNAKEPIT *************************** [Storm clouds gather on the screen, and soon the first lightning strike. The screen is hit, bathed into a bright light that disappears but leaves the letters GWC. A second lightning hits, and this time smaller words appear under the GWC logo. "Dedicated to Your Continued Entertainment".] ["Snakebite" from Alice Cooper starts to play as the screen fades to black and is replaced by the view onto a stage that's entirely decorated like a place somewhere in a jungle, with artificial trees, lianas and small stone statues from some minor gods. In the center of the stage is a hole of about 6 feet in diameter and 2 feet deep. In this hole crawl some snakes around. A man enters the scene, carrying a viper around the neck and holding her head in his left hand. He has short black hair and wears black jeans and a white GWC T-shirt. He's visibly well-trained.] Snakebite: "Weelllcoooomeee. Welcome to the very first edition of my new show, the Snakepit. I know, some of you out there will ask themselves: What's this supposed to be? And others perhaps even: Who's that guy? Let's start this very slowly to be sure everyone has a chance to understand it. My name's Snakebite, and this here is Hillary. [He smiles and kisses his pet viper, Hillary.] Snakebite: And what am I supposed to do here in the GWC? Well, actually the thing I do second-best; ranting about wannabe wrestlers. And best of all, I get paid for it. Snakebite: What makes the GWC believe I'm the best man for this job? I was IEWA Cruiserweight Champion and I retired among others, the ESWP Bantamweight title. Two of the most-respected federations when I was still wrestling, but you rookies probably haven't heard of them. Doesn't matter, you're the ones to have missed the real wrestling action. Snakebite: But on to modern times, and the future of wrestling ... the GWC. There are actually some talented guys around here, otherwise I wouldn't waste my time signing for this job. Snakebite: But enough from me for now, let's go to the competitors that will clash this week on Titans, BaiN against Alex Deadly. BaiN lost his first match here in the GWC last week against Ronnie "The Wolf" Black. Now he faces one of the hot new rookies signed by Chairman Foster, Alex Deadly. Let's see what the lunatic monster BaiN will say about his new challenge in Alex Deadly. ***************************************************** BaiN ***************************************************** [The scene opens to an enraged Bain walking circles around a table in his hotel room. He is trying to wonder how he let Black beat him. He luckily had the reach to hit him in the groin to break the hold. Bain stops walking suddenly and says out loud.] Bain: Oh Sh*t. [Bain then runs out of his hotel room and bolts for a near-by phone. He calls a taxi and in 5 minutes it is there.] Bain: Airport, 20 bucks says you can't get there in 5 minutes flat. Driver: You're on! [The driver then speeds off, trying to make his 20 bucks.] Driver: Whats a guy like you riding in a taxi? Bain: What do you mean? Driver: I mean you, Bain, one of the big men of GWC. That Ronnie Black cheated with the low blow. You had the match won. Bain: Very true to that statement. Driver: Don't you have a match next week? Bain: Yea, against some Alex Deadly guy. He's no trouble for me though. I'll break him in half with the Hades or I'll just send him through a few tables. Thats fine by me. Driver: Are you always this angry? Bain: Yeap. [The driver screeches to a halt at the Airport. Bain gives him the price and the 20 bucks agreed. He also gives him an extra 50 for talking to him. They say their good byes as the driver once again speeds off into the street, almost hitting a car and an old lady in the process.] ***************************************************** [Snakebite now sits on a large stone throne, still holding his pet viper, Hillary.] Snakebite: Sounds strange to me. BaiN wants to destroy Alex Deadly, who wouldn't have predicted that, but for the first time no person is hurt during a BaiN promo. Is BaiN saving his rage until next Tuesday? Snakebite: Let's see what the rookie Alex Deadly thinks about the GWC and in particular, his first match against BaiN. ***************************************************** Alex Deadly ***************************************************** [Scene opens to the entrance of a local gym in Lansing, Michigan. A local reporter, Jerry Bob is standing out waiting for the arrival of Alex Deadly, it's a pretty cold day so Jerry is shivering a bit. The camera pans around as Alex begins to walk up the sidewalk to the gym, he's wearing sunglasses, a leather jacket and has a kit bag on his shoulder. He noticed Jerry and stops before entering the gym.] Jerry: Alex, could I get a word with you? Alex: Jeez Jerry, what are you doing out here? It's not that warm out you know, why do we move inside the gym bud... Jerry: Ssssure thing.. [Camera fades and opens inside the lobby area of the gym, Jerry looks a bit more comfortable.] Jerry: Alex, some big news in that you've been accepted to GWC! What's your thoughts? Alex: Naturally. I mean, they would have be brain dead to not accept me. Listen, it's great that I'm in the GWC, and sure, I'm happy. But that's the first step. The next step is to shake up this league, and show these lads that I'm here to kick some ass. The first league I entered, I conquered. I won the world title faster than anyone else in that league. Then because of a crooked president it went broke and folded. That was a major downer for me. It's almost a year later, I've paid my dues again in the local leagues here and now I'm back in the pack, I'm back in the big leagues. I could do the song and dance that everyone is gonna pay, and I'm gonna defeat all on comers. But the fact of the matter is this; I am the New Generation superstar in this league. Jerry: You sound pretty confident of yourself, are you looking forward to meeting anyone specific in the ring?? Alex: Yes I am Jerry, who's the current champ? Jerry: Umm The Messiah I believe.. Alex: Really? Well I guess he would do. Actually it doesn't matter, who ever is holding that piece of gold is who I'm gunning for. I am about winning. So if it's The Messiah or The Xtremist or anyone other joker in this league. I will repeat: It doesn't matter who you are. If your holding gold, your in my targets. I'm not a complicated man, that's what I'm about; winning and more importantly winning titles. Jerry: Thank you for your time. Alex: No problem Jer, but don't worry, I got a lot more to say after my workout.. Later little man. [Alex continues on through to the gym as the screen fades to black.] ***************************************************** [Scene opens to a large conference room filled with various reporters and media sources. At the front of the room there is a long table, setup for an upcoming press conference. A number of mics are setup near the middle of the table. As the camera pans around the entire room, Alex Deadly enters through a door of the right of the head table and walks slowly up to the mics. He has on his black leather jacket and jeans with his hair tied back. He coughs to clear his throat and begins...] Alex: Welcome one and all, I am using the opportunity to release my statement for my upcoming match on Titans and answer any other questions you might have. [Alex takes a drink from a glass beside the mics and continues...] Alex: First and foremost. This upcoming week at Titans, I am scheduled to face a man by the name of Bain. Now apparently this man has something of mean disposition; a rather nasty individual. And Bain, after watching your little interview last week I must say; you are nasty. However, after watching your "match" on Titans last week, I came to the conclusion that; you may be a nasty person, but your in-ring skills.. well.. they suck! ...to put it bluntly. Bain, I'm sure power bombing a 5 foot nothing reporter through a table makes you a bad dude, it lets you come across as a tough guy... Sure, why not? If I went around beating on everybody smaller than me and those who have no wrestling abilities at all, I'm sure I would be considered the tough guy of the federation. Imagine how much of a bad dude I would be Bain. Imagine how much I would make my opponents tremble. They would be "No Alex, you are so tough because you beat up reporters but get your ass handed to you in the ring!!".. See; you can clearly see how frightened of you I truly am, can't you Bain. You can see that when we enter the ring on Titans, you will tower over me as I shudder under your power. Can you see that Bain? No you can't. What you see if Alex Deadly taking you apart in that ring, piece by piece. And when I think you've had enough of what I have to offer I'll give it to you two-fold. Bain, to put it in terms you can understand, I think your a bad person, you might be proud of that; I don't care. I don't really like what I've seen so far, that spans from your coward attack on the reporter to your sub-par wrestling abilities. You don't intimidate me bud, in fact, beating the snot out of you on Titans gets me excited. It gets me pumped. And when all is said and done on Titans you'll end up on the mat, in pain, Bain. [Alex takes another drink of water..] Alex: Alright, I guess I'm ready for some questions.. Reporter #1: Alex, pretty strong words for Bain, after what happened on Titans with the titles, The Messiah and Little Blue Super Jew seem to have formed an alliance of sorts. What's your thoughts on that? [Alex smirks a bit at the question...] Alex: Well, it doesn't really matter to me right now; to tell you the truth. You see, when the time comes, those two can be joined at the hip for all I care. It won't matter, I am in this league to win, and more specifically win titles; and having an "alliance" between those two jokers won't cause a problem. Be it the Messiah or the little Jew guy, when I meet the champ in the ring, the result will be the same; my opponent will be a "former" champion and I will be walking out with the gold. Reporter #2: There is a lot of new guys in the league, a lot of young blood. Is there anyone in specific that you should keep an eye on? Alex: No. Next question... Reporter #2: Alex, come on, there has to be someone that could give you a run for the most valuable rookie here? Alex: Again no. Look there might be some superstar caliber talent other than me getting started in GWC, all the power to them. However, they are just going to end up being a stepping stone in my path. Ronnie "The Wolf" Black seems to be my only worth opponent in my personal opinion, however, I am sure he'll just disappoint my expectations in the end. Reporter #3: There hasn't been much fan fare to you joining GWC, and you seem to be talking quite a bit of "trash talk". What makes you so special? Alex: True enough, there hasn't been much fan fare, or rumors about me being the next big thing. That's fine and dandy, I am not the type of person who expects that or hopes to bring that before I even have my first match. When I step in the ring, my words speak for themselves. The fans can decide either way, cheer for me or boo for me, it is their choice. But I put on a show that people get their money's worth. I put on a show that lets them walk out of the arena with the feeling that they just didn't spend an hour or two watching second rate clowns act like a bunch of idiots. The fans can like or hate me, but in the end they respect me. In the end I will walk out of the ring the victor. I make them and anyone else who watches me, that I am the New Generation Superstar. [Alex takes another drink of water and continues...] Alex: I'm sorry to cut this short but I've got a couple meetings to attend today. Before I go, I have a couple more words for Bain to chew on. Bain, how you won your first two matches is beyond comprehension for me; I guess that shows the level of competition in the league, I don't know. Never less, come Titans, Bain, you can be as bad as you want to be, you can power bomb ten reporters through a table to warm if you want tough guy. But in the end, the New Generation Superstar will whip your ass around the ring, and when "I" feel like it ... I will end the match with a victory. Simple as that. [Alex walks off to the right exiting through same door he entered. Camera fades to black.] ***************************************************** [Snakebite now stands near the pit, holding a mouse in hand.] Snakebite: The least we can say about Alex Deadly, this man is confident. He knows what he wants, and he doesn't shut his mouth. Single-pointed determination. That's one thing to make the difference between stars and superstars. But he didn't know who was the GWC champ! Alex, take this advice from a ring veteran: If you know your enemy, then you don't have to fear any battle, if you don't know yourself, you'll lose every battle. [Snakebite drops the mouse into the pit.] Snakebite: Next up on the card, a three way threat between Asylum, Scottie Rayda and Calypso. Asylum is another hot new rookie, Scottie Rayda lost his first match in the GWC against BaiN, and Calypso lost of four way match. One thing's for sure, one of these guys will celebrate his first victory here in the GWC next Tuesday. But unfortunately none of them had time for us. I think they are all preparing for the match. Should be interesting. Snakebite: Coming to Ronnie "The Wolf" Black against George De Los Santos. These are two young and hungry men, on their way to the top. "The Wolf" is undefeated, making BaiN lose his first match last week. George De Los Santos wasn't that lucky last week, when he was maltreated by Chris Hoff, after nearly defeating the ring veteran. But there's still my friend John Steele, who saved De Los Santos before Hoff could hurt him seriously. And mark my words, John wouldn't to come to the ring and help someone, if that guy was a no-talent. Let's see from Ronnie Black first. ***************************************************** Ronnie "The Wolf" Black ***************************************************** [Ah, Thanksgiving. On this fine holiday afternoon, we open inside the home of Ronnie "The Wolf" Black. The camera pans over the table that only hours before hosted a masterful collection of food. Now only bones remain from the turkey, and empty bowls with spoons licked clean remain. We enter the living room, where the majority of the family is crowded around the television. It's Detroit, and it's Thanksgiving, and the Lions never disappoint.] [Yeah. Whatever.] [From the television: The onside kick is recovered by Green Bay, and Detroit, it seems, will drop to 0-10 after all.] [Ronnie Black slumps back in his chair.] Black: Just when you thought Lady Luck was in your corner. [The phone rings, turning his attention away from the game. Thank goodness.] Black: Oh, darn. I'll miss the Cowboys game. [He snatches the phone off the hook.] Black: Yeah? You again? Sure. Anything to get me away from this. Where are you? Hey, that's not too far from here. I'll be there in 10 minutes. [He hangs up the phone. That way I don't have to describe that annoying eh-eh-eh-eh phone-off-the-hook noise. Oops, already did.] Black: Man, I've got to get in touch with Shizza Hotee's travel agent. I don't know how he does it. [We cut to the meeting at a nearby Starbucks. Shizza Hotee and Ronnie Black are seated next to the window, and are already talking as we go inside.] Hotee: .it really wasn't all that bad. Especially for today. Black: I just can't understand how you can be in all these different places at once. You sure you don't have a twin brother or something? Hotee: For the last time - no. Black (shaking his head): I don't know. It just doesn't make any sense to me. Hotee (changing the subject): So, why don't you get some coffee? Or a cappuccino or something? Black: No way. If I drink coffee I end up having these crazy dreams about blue elephants or one-legged leprechauns. Sometimes both. It messes with your head. Hotee: .right. Black: Man, I'm just messing with you. But I don't think you came all the way to Detroit to ask me about coffee. Hotee: Straight to the point, as usual. Well, with your win over BaiN you're on the rise here in GWC. This week on Titans, you must go through George De Los Santos. Any thoughts? Black: No. Turkey has a way of zoning that out. Hotee: Seriously. Black: All right, all right. I've noticed a funny little trend here in the GWC. First we had 'the Dragon'. Then came the 'Crouching Tiger'. Then, last week, we saw 'the Gorilla' make his first appearance. And of course, there's me, 'the Wolf'. Maybe it's just me, but it seems like it's either the Chinese New Year, or the GWC is turning into a damn zoo, because there's way too many animals here now. What ever happened to normal names? Hotee: Like John 'The Liberator' Steele? Black: Don't interrupt. And I'm trying to make a point, so stop unmasking all the flaws in my logic. Hotee: Are you having fun with this? Black: Absolutely. But you're right, it's time to get serious. From what I've seen, Georgie boy has a lot of good things going for him. We call him a rookie, but he's probably not much greener than I am, and I'm ripe as we speak. He's got good skills, and has the talent to go far if he sticks with his instincts. And to tell you the truth, I like the guy. It's hard not to. Black: But now it's time to cease with the butt kissing, and commence with the trash talking. As in any sport, you can have as many friends as you want on the other team, but come game time, you better be worst enemies. In a team sport, that's the only way you can play at your best. But wrestling isn't a team sport, and there ain't nobody on your side. And what separates the legends from the human mops is the ability to be go to war with anyone, anytime, anywhere. Even if it's your brother. Black: But that's not all. You have to have the desire, and the fortitude, to see the job through to the end, and be the best at it. You could say there's two kinds of people in this world, those who can tolerate being second best, and those who skip the Cowboys game because they have better things to do. So, George, as much as I respect you, don't hold nothing back. Nobody is harder on me than I am, and I don't settle for second best. I don't settle for losing. Winning may not be everything, but it sure seems like it sometimes. I come to ring every single day with that one thing on my mind, and it'll be no different this Tuesday. Black: So enjoy Thanksgiving while it lasts, because next Tuesday you won't want what I'll be giving to you. Thanks, or no thanks. [Ronnie shrugs in a that's-the-way-it-is-even-though-it-doesn't-sound-quite-right kind of way. The cameraman then bends over to tie his shoe, and carelessly allows the camera to slip off his shoulder. The ceiling spins around, and then everything turns to static.] ***************************************************** [Snakebite sits again on the stone throne.] Snakebite: George De Los Santos didn't have anything to say, but one of those two guys will continue to climb the rankings, and eventually get a title shot. Which one will be decided next Tuesday. Snakebite: The meeting of the wild animals. The Gorilla against The Dragon. Personally I think there's only one possible outcome to this confrontation, The Dragon will win. But, the question is: Does Serpe merit the nickname "The Dragon"? See, there seems to be a new breed of dragons on this planet, but they don't have much in common with the majestic animal of fantasy literature. As for "The Gorilla". If Hoff wants to conquer that GWC Heavyweight championship, then I hope he has more intelligence than the common gorilla. Cause strength alone won't earn you the title here in the GWC. Let's see what he had to say. ***************************************************** "The Gorilla" Chris Hoff ***************************************************** [The scene opens up at GWC Headquarters. Walking in the building is GWC Superstar and future GWC HC Champion, "The Gorilla" Chris Hoff. The wrestling legend is clothed in a blue flannel, AE jeans, and Adidas white running shoes. His brown hair is spiked up and he has his trademark grin on his face. All of a sudden, GWC commentator Monkey Boy runs in front of Hoff.] Hoff: What the hell are you doing? Monkey Boy: I needed to get an interview with you! I cannot believe you are actually here at GWC Headquarters! Hoff: [grins] I had to get some extra incentive from Chairman Foster for my contract. Monkey Boy: He is paying you more money? Hoff: Man, you must be really dense at this, huh? Monkey Boy: Hey, I am a monkey! You are a gorilla! HAAAAA!! [Hoff slugs Monkey Boy, who lets out a scream.] Hoff: Knock it off with the B.S. Your comments were hardly funny on Titans. You may as well walk with me to his office and ask your damn questions. [They start to walk.] Monkey Boy: Well, you defeated George De Los Santos in your debut match here in GWC. What did you think of him? Hoff: Heh, the kid is raw. However, he did not do that bad for himself. I think he has the potential to go far in the GWC, but his skills and mic work need to develop. He needs a personality to really take him somewhere. I have a vision of him ruling this place one day. [laughing] It was a damn shame he had to run into me though! He did not even have a chance in heaven, earth, or hell! Monkey Boy: What did you have to start building him up like that and than make a sarcastic remark? Hoff: Kid, you are talking to the purest wrestler, best ass kicker, and bloodiest blood maker! I have been around feds forever and know how to operate. What I say about someone, goes. I am the wrestling authority and could give a damn what anyone else thinks. Monkey Boy: What about the man who attacked you after the match? John "The Liberator" Steele came down and gave you a nice welcome to GWC. Hoff: Does this little boy know who he is dealing with? Johnny, let me make a few things clear to you. Kid, you suck. Your record is 2-3? Is the GWC that desperate for talent that you would be number four in the ranks? Dude, you really are going to get a nice foot up your ass for making yourself my business. Do you really feel that coming in after my match and attacking me was wise? Did you want to give De Los Santos a little booty call or something? Boy, if you swing the bat from the right side, that is your business. However, you are going to pay for your win against the GWC's premier star. Monkey Boy: Did you just make a reference to Steele wanting to ah ...with Santos? Hoff: Hey, if Steele mashes the dinner, that is his business. All I know is he screwed with the wrong legend. He wrote a check that his ass will be cashing very soon after I kick it. Monkey Boy: Are you saying that you want to get revenge on Mr. Steele? Hoff: I am going to do a hell of a lot more than that. He should have left Santos well enough alone so I could continue to teach the young punk a few lessons. Everything will come around in due time. Monkey Boy: What about your next match here in GWC? You face off against Tony "The Dragon" Serpe. Hoff: Yeah? Monkey Boy: What do you think of him? Hoff: Who? Monkey Boy: Tony Serpe! He is your opponent for the next Titans! Hoff: Never heard of him. Monkey Boy: He is a player here in the GWC! Hoff: His record? Monkey Boy: He was rated number six with a 1-2-1 record. Hoff: [grins] Easy win. Monkey Boy: That is it? Hoff: Why should it not be? I gave Santos a little more respect because I felt bad for the kid. Sure, he will never get a real woman in his life. However, he will be something in the GWC someday. Monkey Boy: You do not think Serpe will be something? Hoff: He sounds kind of boring to be honest with you. Serpe will just be another stepping stone in my easy walk to the top of GWC. The little boys can play at the top, but they know the purest wrestler, best ass kicker, and bloodiest blood maker is on his way up. I feel bad for this Serpe character. Looks like he will be the next sacrifice and his losing ways continue. Monkey Boy: You have somewhat of an ego. Hoff: Hey, I deserve it. I have paid my dues and can do whatever the hell I feel like. Now, if you will excuse me, Chairman Foster awaits to talk money with his prized savior of GWC. [Hoff enters Foster's door. The scene fades to black.] ***************************************************** (The scene opens up in Bear Creek Park in Brooklyn Park, Minnesota. The snow is falling for the first time in the state and people are enjoying it. Walking on a trail is GWC superstar "The Gorilla" Chris Hoff. The future World Champion is clothed in a hooded Abercrombie gray sweatshirt, AE jeans, and his usual white Adidas running shoes. A GWC cameraman is following Hoff on the walk.) Every GWC fan can thank Minnesota for producing such a fine wrestler as me to lead this fed into fame. (Hoff starts to jog a little bit down the trail.) Hoff: So, Tony boy, how was your Thanksgiving? After I make that belt famous, these Peter Pan pounders like Messiah can at least say they held it once. (Hoff grabs a water bottle out of his backpack and takes a drink. He starts to walk again.) I have made so many sacrifices for feds in the past, but now this is my show. Why should I have respect for my fellow wrestlers? I could give a damn about these people because they are not at my skill level! I could give a flying rat's ass about any past fed any of these people. List me your accomplishments because mine are better! All I can do for you fools out there is prove it. I made an example out of the rookie last week. Now, I will make another one out of the next moron on the list. What will happen after that? I will probably get another idiot to defeat. The lines will keep falling until I defeat Messiah or whatever fairy masher holds the top belt. GWC will bring more and more established stars to oppose me and all of a sudden, this fed's top prize will be a sought after item. (Hoff stops, stretches, and grins.) Hoff: That brings me to George De Los Santos. Well, kid, how does it feel now? How does it feel a week later to be brought down to earth that you are a rookie scrub for the time being? Sure, you will rise, but against me, you will always be scum. At least at your Thanksgiving table, you got to brag to the family about being in the ring with a legend. Life had been gracious to you, George. Be happy and healthy. Watch as your superior continues to move up and run this place. Maybe even if you are a good little boy, I might give you a shot at the belt. Sure, you will lose and job, but at least your name will be in lights. (Hoff starts back up on the trek again with the cameraman jogging behind him to keep pace.) Hoff: I am not a flashy guy. I do not need a lot of crap going on in my promos. See, I make a simple point, talk to the Tweety Bird rapers I need to speak with, and prove that I am the savior of this fed. I need not a girl to show her assets on camera. I have a chick at home to get my groove on. My personal life need not be shown at work because my fame speaks enough for me. After a while in this business, one will learn to simply go after the other lame asses in the fed, make a name, get gold, become a legend, and retire. Wrestling has the same career path and dreams for everyone. It is hard to actually say this time will be it. When I retired before, I tried to stay away. However, I always thought about the massive returns, the crowd reaction, and beating everyone's ass once again. Well, this time, I will be done. I need to prove a point to the world that I am that damn good. The GWC got the last of me. I have enough belts to call it a hall of fame career. The GWC gold will be my last hurrah. The master has to leave his trade and my time will soon be calling. (Hoff sits down on a nearby bench.) Hoff: This brings me to the last hotdog pony. Johnny Steele. So, boy, you happy? You seem to want to make a name for yourself. I admit you have guts, but you do not have brains. You will suffer revenge and even end up getting jobbed to me in due time. Enjoy your time at the top, because we both know it is going to come down. I will surpass your Sesame Street watching ass and leave you in the dust. Watch your back. The purest wrestler, best ass kicker, and bloodiest blood maker is coming soon . very soon.
