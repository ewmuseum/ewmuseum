Members: I'm rather excited about the PPV, it being our first and all. I would still like to see a rise in participation in the future. I should be at a level exceeding what it is now. I will be going on another recruiting campaingn in order to beef up the roster, but also find solid roleplayers with a record of regular participation. In the future, if the current members fail to rise to the level of new players, I will be doing a sort of "spring cleaning". So folks, I hope to see all attempting to better their abilities and get into a regular routine of roleplaying/strat-ing. Thanks! John Steele sits in an office, decorated with african style masks, statues and stones of all kinds. Behind a desk sits an african-american woman, with a slight muscular build, but not too much to hide her feminity. She has shoulder length black hair and hazel eyes. The former #1 RSPWF female wrestler of the world. Jessica Starbird. And founding member of the Warriors of Rock stable with Estrago, Richard Starr and John himself.] Starbird: I'm glad you've come to see me. I'm sorry, but my life has been a hectic one recently. I should have phoned you. Steele: No, I should have phoned you. A lot sooner. Starbird. That doesn't sound good. What happened? Steele: How much do you know about my last three years. I mean, what happened to me since the IEWA closed? Starbird: I know that you left for Europe. You got married in February 2000. Then nothing until about a year ago. You and Richard met in a small fed. But that's about it. Steele: The SWL closed only two months after we joined. I stopped wrestling and started building gyms all around the country. Starbird: Yes. You've sent me an invitation some months ago for an opening. But I didn't know you've stopped wrestling. Steele: In fact, Helen has become my manager. And there wasn't a single good offer since the SWL closed. So I was at home, waiting, and taking care of my gyms. I own 14 of them now. Starbird: But? Steele: Well, I restarted wrestling. I've signed a contract with the GWC, a small but promising league. And I'd like you to watch this tape, and tell me what you think about it. [John takes a video tape and hands it to Jessica. She takes it, and puts it into a VCR. The first picture we see is the GWC arena during the Messiah vs. Aj Nin Red Rum match. Messiah has locked Aj Nin in a Texas Cloverleaf. But Aj Nin tries to get to the ropes. Suddenly John appears at ringside. Messiah releases the hold and starts to exchange some not too kind words with John. John flips him the bird but officials arrive and drag John backstage before a brawl can erupt.] Starbird: You interfered in a match? I hope you had a good reason for doing so. Steele: Hmmm ... I had none. That's why I'm worried. But it continued. [Now John comes to the 'Dragon' Tony Serpe vs. Fusion match. He sits down at ringside, watches the match, and afterwards he congratulates both men.] Starbird: That's more like I know you. I suppose these both are friends of yours. Steele: Not yet. But they could become allies. At least I try to watch after them, in case someone tries to interfere in their matches. But look on. [Now John is wrestling. He has Dragula lifted in a Gorilla Press and throws him out of the ring. Then he returns to The Xtremist and lifts him up into a powerbomb. But Xtremist counters it and rolls John into a small package and gets the three-count.] Starbird: I can't remember you doing such a mistake. Someone manages to surprise you? In the old days it was the other way around. You surprised your opponents when you found an opportunity for The Liberty. Steele: But what happened after the match is more important. [On the screen, John seems frustrated, and suddenly he executes his patent finisher, The Liberty, on Xtremist. But not enough, he goes for two more of his spinning backbreakers. Then, finally, he leaves the ring.] Starbird: John?! I can't believe what I've seen. How could you ... do ... such a thing? Steele: I don't know. That's why I'm here. I hope you can give me an explication. But I have one more scene on the tape. [John comes to the ring during the Joe Six-pack vs. Dementor match. His presence alone is enough to distract Dementor, who gets surprised by Joe Six-pack and pinned.] Starbird: At least you didn't attack someone there. Steele: But I can't remember walking to the ring during this match! [Jessica looks at John. She's visibly shocked.] Starbird: You made the only right thing. You came to me. And I think you're in serious trouble. I don't know if a doctor could help you. You haven't been wrestling for 8 months, so your memory loss can't be explained by hits you've received. I'd rather say we have a split personality problem. At times you act like the John Steele we know, and sometimes the evil personality inside you gets the upper hand. And it seems to happen in times of extreme stress. Like you loosing a match like a rookie. Steele: Yeah. Starbird: Now we need to know what made this evil side appear in the first place. What happened to you in the last two years, apart from you marriage? Steele: I can't think about something in special. Starbird: Something personal. I know your parents died a long time ago. Perhaps Helen was pregnant and something happened to the child? Steele: No, .... but ... I met my brother. Starbird: You have a brother? I thought you were a single child? Steele: No, the fact is ... I have a twin brother. I didn't know about it until he appeared in the PCWF in mid-99. He started to attack me at every opportunity. Because of him I lost my PCWF World Heavyweight Title, but some months later I managed to get a rematch. A cage-casket match for that title. And the winner had to stop wrestling forever. Starbird: You're still wrestling, so I suppose you won the match. Steele: Yes, indeed. But more important is what happened during the match. When I first opened the casket to get David, that's my brother, inside, I found Helen in it. Starbird: That's where you finally found her? I remember that she had gome missing inlate 98. Steele: Yes. David slapped his Oppression chicken face hammer lock on me, and I faded out. But from the tapes I know that Sengir came to my rescue. The final point is, I won the match. Starbird: And David had to stop wrestling. That could be it! You said he attacked you. Did he attack other people too? Steele: Think about a fox inside a chicken house. Starbird: OK. I believe you think you're responsible for David's fate. But you ain't. You didn't have the choice. Or would you prefer to stop wrestling? Steele: He's my brother. Apart from Helen he's the only family I have. Starbird: Well, then perhaps the only thing you can do is to forgive him. And it could be possible that you can allow David to wrestle again. After all, it's you who put him out of business. Steele: No. He was too dangerous. I can't let him return. Starbird: Then we will have to try something else. Hypnosis. We have an expert working here. He could try to get the thoghts of culpability out of your head. Steele: Thanks Jessica. I'll try it.
