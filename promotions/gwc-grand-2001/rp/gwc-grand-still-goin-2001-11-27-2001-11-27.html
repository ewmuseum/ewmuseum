Signed, [name withheld] ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ 2. ] GWC Ear to the Floor ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ Back for round deuce. And my how things have changed. Messiah has began construction of his "Dark Project". Little Blue Super Jew was the first inductee. Asylum was its first opponent. John "The Liberator" Steele shifted his attention from the Dark Project to his newly arrived brother David Steele. "The Gorilla" Chris Hoff threw himself in the mix when he attempted to avenge Steele's intervention in his previous encounter with George De Los Santos while ending George's career and attacking Steele on the same night. Hoff met stiff opposition with Ronnie "The Wolf" Black; the two having to be separated by event security not once, but twice on Tuesday night. The pot seems to be boiling over leading up to where the lid will blow off: CHIVALRY I. Let's dissect the recent happenings, shall we? Start at the top. The Messiah is the heavyweight champion and like anyone else would like to stay that way. To cement this he seems to be assembling a force, his "Dark Project" as he calls it. The first member of this assembly was the current GWC Regional Champion, Little Blue Super Jew. This was brought to light when John "The Liberator" Steele was screwed out of the GWC title. With this Messiah seems to have added another member in the mystery man that entered the mayhem in the ring at the close of Titans this week. That's a Dark Project Trio that seems to be ruling the GWC. This trio may be needed for Messiah to keep his holy hands on the GWC championship this week in his first "QUEST FOR COMPETITION" match up against former GWC Regional title holder, The Xtremist. The Messiah was whining in his typical fashion about how there is no challenge to his precious GWC gold. Well, be careful what you wish for is how the saying goes. The Xtremist answered the foolish challenge and will vye for the top prize this Tuesday night on Titans. Many say Messiah will need all the back up he can assemble to take out the GWC's King of Extreme. Next on the agenda...Little Blue Super Jew. He is the newly crowned GWC Regional champion and the cohort of The Messiah. What's next? Little Blue Super Jew made what some say was the mistake of challenging the Xtremist's ability to perform at the level of "extreme". As seen on the Titan's schedule next week, these two men will go at it in a trio of nothing-less-than-vicious matches to find what warrior will be the "King of Extreme". John "The Liberator" Steele. His stay in the GWC up to this point has been nothing short of stressful for the Liberator. Since his arrival his hands have come dangerously close to capturing the GWC championship yet he has yet to do so. He has repeatedly been 'screwed' so to speak by the so-called monarchy at the top, Messiah and his Dark Project. Steele has been the under-dog since his arrival, fighting against the current, against a stacked deck. It seems as of late that Steele has become fed up. He snapped when fighting Little Blue Super Jew several weeks ago, stepping outside his 'good-guy' personality. John Steele then decided to construct his Warriors of Rock stable. He is eying men like Ronnie "The Wolf" Black and George De Los Santos. The latter was injured to the point that he may be out of wrestling forever. So Steele had better start making friends fast or he may stay in his "almost there" rut that he finds himself in currently. Not to mention that his brother David Steele who was kind enough to drop in and watch his brother wrestle this past week on Titans is lingering somewhere in the GWC. The Xtremist. He's one of the most intense, most incredibly skilled and toughest competitors in the GWC. He only recently was defeated for the very first time when he lost his Regional championship to Little Blue Super Jew. The current "King of Extreme" will soon challenge the man who took his gold from his waist to a series of extreme matches to cement his now argued status as the King of Extreme. Not to mention he may be wearing the GWC Heavyweight Championship belt around his waist when he does so. The chaos surrounding this week's Titans is still being sorted out. The main event is where all the controversy is centered. John Steele and The Xtremist challenged The Dark Project consisting of The Messiah and Little Blue Super Jew to a tag team match up. After what seemed to start off as a solid tag team bout, all hell broke loose. First, Asylum struck. The maniac appeared from beneath the ring and snagged Messiah from the mat. Beating him like a wild man while LBSJ was busy with Steele in the ring, Messiah had no help. That was until the third member of Messiah's Dark Project party, the mystery emerged from man the crowd. In his fan costume, he proceeded to brawl with Asylum while Messiah regained his bearings. In the ring, Steele was blind-sided when "The Gorilla" Chris Hoff struck from the rampway. He was angry at Steele's intervention in his attack on George De Los Santos last week and avenged it for a moment. Steele was indeed taking a beating until Hoff's newest adversary, Ronnie "The Wolf" Black, with whom he had two previous encounters that evening rushed to Steele's rescue. If THAT WAS NOT ENOUGH! David Steele shows his face in the GWC for the first time in history! The look on Steele's face spoke volumes. He wore a mask of mixed emotions; anger, shock, guilt, fear and determination all twisted into a frozen moment that will be remembered for a very long time to come. And where was the Xtremist during the mayhem? Beating on the only man to ever defeat him and the one man who has ever challenged his claim of being "King of Extreme", Little Blue Super Jew of course. However this whole mess of activity is left open ended. Who is the next member of the GWC's royal family, the Dark Project and what is their next move? What about the resistance to the monarchy, the Warriors of Rock and their leader, John "The Liberator" Steele? Who will come to his aid and become the first warrior? Is Ronnie "The Wolf" Black the first? What will Chris Hoff's sickening acts this week on Titans against George De Los Santos bring to him and what will come of the seemingly imminent feud between him and the Wolf? Is "The Gorilla" the next member of the Dark Project? Is David Steele? What is on David Steele's mind and what are his plans for his brother the Liberator? So many questions, none yet answered. Then there is the rest of the GWC roster. How could they play into this melting pot of anarchy or what else could possibly happen apart from this? As before, only time will tell. ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ 3. ] Official Updated Roster ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ - Lucky 13 [ 6'3", 235 lbs., Tweener. This week the Dark Project was revealed and a mystery man was inducted as it's newest member during the main event carnage on Titans. His next challenge that he ASKED FOR is against the incomparable Xtremist. Many say Messiah will be knocked off his throne... Number Two - Little Blue Super Jew, 2-1-1 (RC) - Ruffling a number of feathers since making his shocking return and costing Aj Nin Red Rum the GWC championship, LBSJ recently captured the Regional Championship. He makes the first defense of his strap against a man who 'snapped' him in two wee little pieces in their last meeting...John Steele. Number Three - The Xtremist, 3-1-2 - The Xtremist charges into this week with his sights on the top of the heap. He challenges The Messiah for the GWC Heavyweight Championship in the first "Quest for Competition" matches proposed by Messiah. Down the line, possibly as the Heavyweight Champion, he will attempt to solidify his title as "King of Extreme" when he takes on LBSJ in a trio of extreme style bouts. Number Four - John "The Liberator" Steele, 2-3-1 - The GWC's Liberator has began assembling the only opposition to our champion's Dark Project and calls it the WOR, or Warriors of Rock. The rumored first member is Ronnie Black, but that has yet to be confirmed by the Wolf himself. Steele goes into this week taking a stab at GWC gold, this time LBSJ's Regional Championship... Number Five - Dave Fresco, 3-0-2 - Fiery GWCer Dave Fresco has been nothing but impressive since his arrival, just recently posed a serious threat to the Xtremist's Regional title reign. Fresco took a time off this past week but looks to throw his name back into the GWC title picture very soon... Number Seven - "The Gorilla" Chris Hoff, 2-0-0/Ronnie "The Wolf" Black, 2-0-0 - The Gorilla and The Wolf simultaneously chase each other up the rankings in what is being billed the newest feud in the GWC happenings. This week they were at each others throats at every opportunity and it should continue; as should their rise. Number Eight - Tony "The Dragon" Serpe, 1-3-1 - The Dragon is in a rut recently and needs badly to remove himself from it. He was making a run at the Regional title and is now only making a run at not disappearing from the top ten. Number Nine - BaiN, 2-2-0 - The unpredictable monster took his second loss to newcomer Alex Deadly. BaiN seems to be in a bit of a slump but is looking to get out of it in the future. Number Ten - Asylum, 1-0-0/Alex Deadly, 1-0-0 - Two newcomers to the roster of the GWC both picked up wins this passed week and break the top ten due to their perfect records. We shall see if they stay in the top ten for long.
