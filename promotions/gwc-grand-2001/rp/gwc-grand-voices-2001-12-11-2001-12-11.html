So many things have happened tonight and left people to do some thinking. Sitting in the back and drinking a Mike's Hard Lemonade is the GWC career ender, "The Gorilla" Chris Hoff. The wrestling legend is still clothed in his in ring outfit and is sweating a bit. He is relaxing as a knock at the door is heard. Do not believe for one second you are as good as me. Sure, you probably think that you have a massive future since you are young and feuding with a veteran. You know your way around the ring pretty good and can handle the battles. However, just remember you are facing a man who will do anything possible to get ahead. They will sadly be torn apart by the vets who know the real meaning of surviving. See, I have made it through so many things. I would think the deck is stacked against you. Hoff: (again, sarcastically) Is it really? Oh, I am so scared! I am going to be beaten to a bloody pulp! The two mighty GWC men are ready to dominate me! Monkey Boy: I may not like either Fresco or Black, but they are going to go after you. Hoff: (grinning) Let them! Bring it on! I have done triple threats numerous times in my life where both opponents have wanted to literally kill me. I have a plan as always to make sure I have a chance. Like I really even need a chance! Black and Fresco will not defeat me. Together, they are not at my level. Past history aside, I could kick both of them a new one even if I was a green rookie. Monkey Boy: Strong words. Hoff: You know it is true. No matter where this match goes, I will be ready to do whatever it takes to win. If that means I break Black's legs, so be it. If that says I need to paralyze Fresco, easily done. If it takes bringing a jar with me to the ring and filling it with both of their blood, I will be set to pour it all over the GWC fans. The fact of the matter is I am going to win and I will win in a bloody way. This match is going to be something people do not want to watch again because it is going to be sick. Fans that see this will be throwing up in their seats. Why? Because when I wrestle, I make a statement. After that, I will mach right onto Little Blue Super Jew and kick his ass up, down, and sideways. That is just the way it is. He seems to have a reputation around here that states he is superior. Normally, I do not give people very much of a chance, but I waited to see with him. When I witnessed his Titans promo, I almost gagged in disgust. He is a lazy bastard that cannot get things done. It makes me think Dark Project is some joke that Foster let happen just to get some laughs in. Does Messiah and LBSJ really think they are something? I guess so, but in the end, the joke will be on them. I could defeat both of those fruits with my hands tied behind my back. They have no idea what the meaning of sick and twisted really is. I am going to continue making my example out of Black this coming Titans. Fresco gets to joy ride with him down hell hill for this certain adventure. Monkey Boy: Where do you see your short term future in the GWC? Hoff: You better believe that I will be close to becoming the man in this fed. I would love a reign as GWC Regional Champion first. You will be damn sure I am going to get it very soon because when I make a promise, it comes true. There is really no competition for me here, but I will make due with what I have and toy with it. Monkey Boy: What about John Steele? His career may have been ended by his own brother. Hoff: (laughs) Damn, I wish that I could have done it! Oh well, someone beat me to the punch. If he ever comes back, his ass will be grass. I will not lose out on taking that punk to the butcher next time. Monkey Boy: Any final thoughts? Hoff: Just know the purest wrestler, best ass kicker, and bloodiest blood maker is stalking people around all the time. You never know when I will hit and you better pray you can walk after I am done. Tell Black and Fresco that I will see them soon . really soon!
