Signed, [name withheld] ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ 2. ] GWC Ear to the Floor ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ This week's events were a bid milder than those of week's past and it was a welcome vacation from the carnage. However, the actually physical carnage in the ring topped anything ever seen in the GWC prior to Tuesday night. Little Blue Super Jew and The Xtremist blew the TOP OFF that arena and flirted with career disaster in the process. Let's review. "The Gorilla" Chris Hoff, Dave Fresco and Ronnie "The Wolf" Black. Lethal combination to BEGIN with. Throw in Monkey Boy's chair and some anger and what do we have? The GWC's "Career Ender" blowing a gasket. After Ronnie "The Wolf" Black took away the win thanks to Fresco knocking out Hoff with the previously mentioned commentator's chair, Hoff got his panties in a wad and went on another rampage. He nearly decapitated the Hawaiian Giant with his second helping of steel for the night and then made sure The Wolf didn't feel left out. To top it off, Black got a Gorilla Press Slam to the outside just for good measure. However that second steel sandwich may have awakened a sleeping giant as Fresco scooped up the chair he'd been sucking on all evening and went Hoff hunting. Too bad for all those damned officials or Hoff woulda been having left-overs. Fact is this dirty little love triangle between three of the GWC's best, brightest and sickest can only go up from here. David Steele. He's had his nose in JUST about everyone's business after ending brother John's career, or at least trying. The question is, what happened this week? Nothing. And that is PRECISELY what is irking me. WHY? Why didn't David Steele have an itch to scratch with someone on the roster. Why wasn't David Steele in the arena? Or was he? Who knows, but the resident maniac has been a little TOO quiet as of late. Watch for him. Because sources of the most reliable kind tell me he is still here. The Messiah destroyed Asylum. Whoop-dee-freaking-doo. I can't say I wasn't expecting it. Now, don't take this as the Asylum-bashing section of the column, I'm just calling it how I see it. The Messiah with out gold is like starving a wolverine. You just...don't...do it. The man is on a mission and not even six-foot-seven-inch road blocks stop him. So...if I were the Xtremist I'd be quivering in my Hanes. But, after what we saw this week on Titans, I'm not seeing any quivering. That all on top of some other unmentioned cherries on top are going to make for one INCREDIBLY interesting Chivalry card. Do make sure you stay tuned... ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ 3. ] Official Updated Roster ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ - Stan "The Spyder" Murdoc [ 6'4", 252 lbs., Heel.
