Members: I'm rather pleased with the post-PPV edition of Voices. Everyone seems to have stepped it up a notch with some quality material. We also have some very, very promising newcomers that all should keep an eye on. This week's Titans should be an exciting one to say the least. So, I hope you all enjoyed the PPV and I hope you all enjoy Titans.
