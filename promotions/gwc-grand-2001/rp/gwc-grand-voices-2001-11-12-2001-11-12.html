All I know is that you're standing between me and GWC World Title, and so I intend to go through you like Moses through the Red Sea. And yes, I will split you in half if that's what it takes." LBSJ: "My people are down there . . . my fans, my followers, the people that just like seeing me in action. And I like giving them that action. That's why I love what I do - it makes other people happy. One question plagues me though. At the beginning of Titans, when Messiah came out, he seemed to be on the receiving end of quite a few cheers. I've followed Messiah and while it's true that he struggled in an admirable way to achieve the belt that I finally let him win, he's never exactly been a guy to like. Could it be that the fans admire him, they appreciate what he does? And, if that is the case, could it be a mistake on my part to try and take his hard-earned GWC Title away from him just because I think I'm better suited for the job?" (LBSJ looks harder out the window, at the cars going by, the buildings, the setting sun and flocks of pigeons swooping down low. Half a smirk settles on his face as he closes the segment.) LBSJ: "I guess I'll just have to win the belt and find out, won't I?"
