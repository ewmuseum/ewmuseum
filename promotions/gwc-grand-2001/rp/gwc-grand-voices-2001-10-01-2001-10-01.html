GWC (Grand, 2001) — Roleplay

GWC Voices - 10.1.01

1 October 2001
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
This is the very first edition of GWC Voices. Though after my note, I did receive a few more roleplays, I'm still a bit disappointed in the turn-out. The whole idea, the core of this fed is roleplaying. You don't do that, there isn't much of a point in you playing the game. With that said, here's Voices... The GWC Heavyweight Title will indeed be mine. If you doubt this then you are a fool. GWC is a top of the notch company and I will prove to the world how I got to be a legend by defeating you and in the end being the GWF Heavyweight Champion. <<A woman walks out of Aj Nin's bedroom. The little girl pushes him out of the way.>> Jane:: That's right. You suck. Ha ha ha ha. <<They two kids run into the living room to watch cartoons.>> Aj Nin Red Rum:: Well Little Blue Super Jew it seems that my kids can beat you too. Must run in the family. <<He sits forward. >> Aj Nin Red Rum:: Little Blue Super Jew, no matter what you do you will still lose at GWC Titans. I have nothing more to say for now. <<He turns the camera off and takes the tape out to send to GWC headquarters. I am that good though, so it's not without warrant. (laughs) I do however have an issue with Shane Dumbshane. How dare you call yourself the ironman? I mean Cal Ripken Jr. is baseball's Ironman, and for you to try and steal his thunder, to ride his coattails. To try and make your rep off a great career, one that has thrilled millions is just wrong. I mean, I know you are just trying to make a name for yourself and you want to become the legend that is Cal and that will be Weasel, but oh no. Know this... you are no Cal Jr. You big dummy and if it takes the Weasel to tell you this, if it makes it my duty to let you know this, I will make you see the error of your ways. And if you want to make an issue of it, keep in mind that Weasel hits more homeruns than Bonds, and you'll strike out more than the Brewers. Why? Because you suck and that's why you have never made the majors. Dork.
