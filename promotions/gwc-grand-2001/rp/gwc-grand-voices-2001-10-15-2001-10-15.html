Some 22,000 people are in attendance, as the Detroit Pistons are facing the Washington Wizards. 22,000 people at a pre-season game? No! 22,000 people witnessing the return of Michael Jordan. In the crowd, a Chicago Bulls fan, and former wrestler, who didn't want to miss this game. His long blond hair is fixed into a ponytail and he wears blue jeans, a white T-shirt and a black leather jacket with the picture of the statue of liberty on its back. Suddenly a camera team approaches the man.] Reporter: Excuse me, sir. Are you John Steele? Steele: Yep. [He looks at the reporter, notices the camera and the large GWC sticker on it, starts to smile, gets up and shakes the reporter's hand.] Steele: So, you're from the GWC. Glad you could make it. Cause this is the perfect event for what I'd like to say to the world of wrestling. See, tonight is MJ's second comeback and I've been thinking a lot about my career in the last weeks. Unlike MJ I wasn't burnt out and had to take a break from the game, I was forced into retirement twice because I signed contracts in leagues that closed their doors a short time later. It should have been easy to find a new employer, considering my achievements in this sport, but my wife, and manager, wanted to be sure that past mishaps won't happen again. Steele: In comes the GWC. A new, small, but promising federation. When I started wrestling, only 4 years ago, it was in an even smaller federation. But after less than a year, we were one of the greatest federations on this planet, one of the big three, competing with and against G-Pro and ESWP. I got to wrestle with or against some of the greatest stars in this business, Richard Starr, Bart 'Blockbuster' Blair, The Banshee, Dan Shadowburn, Estrago, Firestarter, 'The Thunda from Down Unda' Jason Storm, Kabuki Master, Prince Khali, Robert 'The Bullet' Payne, Sledgeman, Weasel and my twin brother Dave. I was part of what made the Rage family and the Victory Sister superstars, not to forget the one name, the greatest of all female wrestlers, Jessica Starbird. We all, and many others, were the IEWA, and god help us all, I'd like the GWC to have only half as much success as the IEWA had, and we'd be the greatest thing in wrestling today. Steele: Now, perhaps these names don't tell you anything. Those days, the wrestling world was different from today's. We had a small number of feds, but those were top-notch. Today we have hundreds of wanna-be federations. And many of the names I mentioned could be retired today, names lost in a giant list of new talents. Steele: But you should know at least one of the names. Weasel. We've never faced each other but I'm glad to meet one of the craziest men in wrestling again, of course as long as we don't meet in the ring. Say hello to Louie. Steele: So, why am I telling you this? Like MJ, I've had enough. I can't sit at home any longer, watching this sport on TV. God, I'm only 26. My finisher is one of the most devastating moves in the history of professional wrestling. Only one guy ever managed to beat the following three count, my brother, and I forced him into retirement for good. I should be out there, in the spotlight, and do what I do best, entertain people. Steele: Let's make it clear, The Liberator IS back. The GWC will be his new home. And boys, you'd better get ready, cause we'll take this whole thing to the next level.
