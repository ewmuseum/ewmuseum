GWC Voices - 10.8.01

Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Members: I'm very pleased with the roleplaying this time around. Things definitely picked up and I hope it continues to do so. I think this week's Titans will be much better than the first. I hope you enjoy it. Be sure to continue to check out the website as updates are constantly being done. Also, I will be re- sending the roleplaying tutorial. I feel a lot of you could benefit from reading this, so DO SO! If you want to succeed here you should strive to be the best. So, with that said, here's this week's Voices... So Shane, just keep in mind though. you're the actor and I'm the real thing. I make my living getting base hits, outstanding catches in the outfield and always, and I mean always gives 99%. Now, I know what you're all saying. 99? Not a 100? Well, there's always room for improvement and that's just what I'm all about. Perfecting my game until my name is mentioned in the same breath as Ruth, McGwire and Griffey Jr. And then Shane the Shane can play me in the movie!
