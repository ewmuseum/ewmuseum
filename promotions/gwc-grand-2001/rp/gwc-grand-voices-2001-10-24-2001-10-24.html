GWC (Grand, 2001) — Roleplay

GWC Voices - 10.24.01

24 October 2001
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Members: Listen up kiddies, I'm a bit disappointed in the roleplaying this week. Bit skimpy, ya know. Activity should be a bit higher, so get on it! We have our first pay-per-view coming up and I would like to see everyone participating. So...on with Voices... oOoOoOoOoOoOoOoOoOoOoOoOoOoOoOoOoOoOoOoOoOoOoOoOoOoOoOoOoOoOoOoOo - John "The Liberator" Steele - The Messiah - The Xtremist (will be shown on Titans) oOoOoOoOoOoOoOoOoOoOoOoOoOoOoOoOoOoOoOoOoOoOoOoOoOoOoOoOoOoOoOoOo [Last week's Titan show. We're backstage, following John 'The Liberator' Steele as he leaves the arena. He wears black jeans, a white T-shirt and his trademark black leather jacket with the picture of Liberty on its back. His long blond hair is fixed into a ponytail, as usual. He doesn't stop to walk as a GWC reporter tries to talk to him.] Reporter: Sir, we'd like to know ... the fans would like to know why you interfered in today's title match. [Steele stops and looks for the first time into the camera.] Steele: Why? I guess everyone saw it! Messiah had Aj Nin in that submission hold, but did Aj Nin submit? No! For more then two minutes. That alone should have been enough for the ref to break the hold. But did he do it? No! And then Aj Nin even manages to get to the ropes and get a grip on them. Yeah, sure, it was only for a fraction of a second, but Aj Nin touched it, so the ref should have broken that hold again. But still nothing! That's when I have to step in. Wrestling follows a set of rules, and if these rules are broken, then I take actions. Reporter: But your interference cost Messiah his title, he was pinned just seconds ago by Aj Nin. Steele: Where's the problem? If the Ref had been man enough I wouldn't have had to go to the ring. And ... do you really think someone deserves to be the champ in this federation if he can't put away a guy whom he had locked for two minutes in a submission hold? Reporter: So you think the man who couldn't free himself from that hold deserves to be champion? Steele: In fact ... No! Reporter: But these two men are the top competitors in the GWC. Do you think someone else should be given a title shot? Yourself? Steele: No. I just joined the GWC. I didn't even wrestle a single match yet. And chairman Foster is the only person to decide who gets title matches. Not me, and no other wrestler. Reporter: You didn't wrestle yet in the GWC, but your first match is scheduled. You'll face Dragula and The Xtremist in a triple threat match. And the winner gets a regional title shot. Steele: First, I don't care if it's for a title shot or whatever. The fans will be there to see good wrestling, and my job is to give them what they want. As for my opponents. Dragula? A rookie. OK, I know, you should never underestimate someone, but he faces a man with 4 years experience in the ring. I think his biggest advantage is that I can't get any tapes about him, so I can't prepare for this match as I would normally. The other guy? Xtremist? I feel sorry for his childhood, but I'm an orphan myself. This guy can be a real threat for me. We are like fire and water. The only division I never wrestled in is the hardcore division. And there's my advantage: this match isn't a hardcore match. Xtremist will have to adapt to the rules, or he will get disqualified sooner then he likes to. And one more thing. I think extremists aren't welcome in this country any longer, and the good old red, white and blue will strike back. [Steele starts to walk again while the reporter follows him.] Reporter: Big words. What if Dragula and Xtremist team up on you? Or, if Messiah wants to interfere. [Steele walks into the parking area of the arena.] Steele: My first ever match in september 1997 was also a triple threat match. I was a rookie and noone would have believed I could ever walk out of the ring. My opponents were two of the biggest, meanest wrestlers I've ever seen. I think their combined weight is some 200 lbs more than that of Dragula/Xtremist. But I was the one who walked out of the ring, arms raised, while they were lying there, knocked out by the power of Liberty. It was but the beginning of Liberty's reign in the world of wrestling, and next monday will witness the next level of that reign. [He walks to a red, white and blue Dodge Viper and gets into it. He starts the engine, but then he opens the door once again.] Steele: I've been waiting 8 months for this. I'm ready for the match and I'm hot. For the first time in 3 and a half year I won't walk to the ring with some injury. Do you really believe someone could stop The Liberty? [Steele grins as he closes the door and pulls the car out of the parking lot. Fade to black.] oOoOoOoOoOoOoOoOoOoOoOoOoOoOoOoOoOoOoOoOoOoOoOoOoOoOoOoOoOoOoOoOo **Dave Scherer of 1wrestling.com conducts an interview with the first ever GWC Heavyweight champ who lost his title to Aj Nin Red Rum on last weeks Titans** Dave: Thanks for taking the time to have an interview with me. Now Messiah, you have been through a lot in the past few years in this business. Last week, you were proud and this week you seem at a loss, no pun intended. Messiah:(discouraged)Yeah Dave. You could say I am at a loss, pun or not! Fact of the matter is, I requested a submissions match for the title. What happened? That (censored) Aj Nin, didn't accept my challenge. And you wanna know why? Cause he knew he would pass out again to the Resurrection. As far as I am concerned, that title is still mine and come pay per view, it will be mine again. Dave: What about this JOHN 'THE LIBERATOR' STEELE? Messiah: What about him? Dave: Well for starters, do you think he is the reason you lost the title? Messiah: John Steele is a scrub that is not even close to my level. What? He flips me off and that all of the sudden makes me scared and him a tough guy? It's not like I haven't been flipped off before. Fans have done it in every fed I have ever been in, and this guy is a no-talent hack that had no reason coming to my ring and interupting me forcing Aj Nin to go to bed early for the second time in the span of a week. Truth be told, if that Austin rip off comes down and so much as lifts that middle finger in the air to me again, I am gonna break it off. Dave: So what is in store for you this week? Messiah: (laughes) Just wait and see. I haven't been here long, but you can bet that I will gain that title again and be the best Heavyweight champion in the sports entertainment world. Do you listen to the fans. Love me or hate me, they respect what I do in that ring. And come pay per view, when we are in the cage.... there will be no interuptions. There will be no Liberator Bull (censored). No! Just the two top names in the GWC giving it all and in the end, you will see Aj Nin in a pool of both of our blood mixed together.... and you will see my hand raised as the new GWC champion. Steele, you got an itch, I'll make you my (censored). Cause your pain,...what's the line Dave? Dave: Oh...Is my gain?? Messiah: No!......MINE!!! **concludes the interview** oOoOoOoOoOoOoOoOoOoOoOoOoOoOoOoOoOoOoOoOoOoOoOoOoOoOoOoOoOoOoOoOo -- [name withheld] Chairman, Grand Wrestling Corp. - http://www.geocities.com/gwcwrestling2000 <><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><> "Dangerous" William Devonshire (IWF/WOW, MLW) <><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><> [detail withheld] "Would you care for a CHOCOLATE COVERED PRETZEL?" - Brody, Mallrats (<--- tied for best movie in history w/ Ferris Bueller's Day Off)
