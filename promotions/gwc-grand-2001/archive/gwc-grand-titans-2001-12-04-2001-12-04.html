GWC Titans - Tuesday, December 4th, 2001

o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o Grand Wrestling Corporation presents... ~ GWC TITANS - Tuesday, December 4th, 2001 ~ o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o Tonight's Titans will showcase the talents of... [ QUEST FOR COMPETITION MATCH #1 ] [ GWC Heavyweight Title Match ] The Messiah (HC) vs. The Xtremist :::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::: -- MESSIAH begins his QUEST FOR COMPETITION by taking on ALL COMERS! THE XTREMIST was the first to step up! [ GWC Regional Title Table Match ] Little Blue Super Jew (RC) vs. John "The Liberator" Steele ::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::: -- The Xtremist lost the title two weeks ago to the current champion LITTLE BLUE SUPER JEW who makes his first defense against a man who defeated him three weeks ago when he went berserk in the ring...JOHN "THE LIBERATOR" STEELE! [ Regional Title Bound -- Grudge Match ] Ronnie "The Wolf" Black vs. "The Gorilla" Chris Hoff -- The two men are beginning to blaze a trail all the way to the top of the GWC. One of them will come a bit closer to a shot at the GWC Regional Championship! [ Singles Match ] Alex Deadly vs. "Lucky" Adam Wilde -- ALEX DEADLY comes off a solid win over BaiN last week and attempts to keep it going by defeating promising GWC newcomer, LUCKY 13! [ Dark Matches ] Cobra vs. "Prime Time" Andre Harris Dave Fresco vs. BaiN Asylum vs. Mr. Jersey* [...screen reveals darkness with an unusual metallic-like shine to it. Slowly, it tilts, with the lighting providing the shine pouring over certain sections revealing assorted letters and designs. Then suddenly, a flash of light reveals the GWC logo and then disappears. This screen dissolves to the interior of a studio-like setting. There is a desk with the GWC Titans logo on the front, constructing of steel mesh and textured plating. Two microphones are perched on small stands, sitting behind them are two recognizable faces. Monkey Boy scratches his temple, the stupefied look plastered seemingly permanently on his face. The other chair is occupied by Winchester Smith, who characteristically shuffles papers, then puts on his smile after noticing the camera...] Winchester Smith: WELCOME everyone to the DECEMBER 4TH, 2001 edition of GWC TITANS! Monkey Boy: My personal palace of LUUUUUUUUUUVVV. The place where your eyes are permitted to behold the sexiest man to _EVER_ don a monkey suit. WHOO! Winchester Smith: Oh brother. Where DO you get your illegal hallucinogens? Monkey Boy: Emm...4th and Main? Don't tell anyone though, keep it on the DL. Winchester Smith: The what? Monkey Boy: What? Winchester Smith: Never mind. We have a job to do. I'll probably be the one doing it while my ignorant counterpart scratches himself. Monkey Boy: [scratching himself] Winchester Smith: On with the card. We had some action before the cameras came on and here's what transpired... [ Dark Matches ] "Prime Time" Andre Harris def. Cobra -- Rating: ** out of **** Dave Fresco def. BaiN -- Rating: *** out of **** Asylum def. Mr. Jersey -- Rating: * out of **** Winchester Smith: Some hot action none the less. Andre Harris taking home his first EVER victory in the GWC after a severe slump. Dave Fresco took out the GWC's resident monster, though BaiN put up a horrific fight as usual. Monkey Boy: Yeah, yeah and Asylum massacred Joe Blow. Can we get on with it already! Winchester Smith: Oh, you DO read the papers they put in front of you. But his name is Mr. Jersey. Or was... Monkey Boy: So, gotta get my back waxed after this so LET'S GIDDY-UP! ON-DA-LEY! Winchester Smith: Our first contest pits two more promising young GWCers against one another. "Lucky" Adam Wilde challenges the blazing youngster, Alex Deadly! [ Singles Match ] Alex Deadly vs. "Lucky" Adam Wilde ^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^ ~ MATCH OVERVIEW ~ ^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^ Match was quite high tempo from bell to bell. Both men put out there all in the contest which saw some great wrestling. Deadly took the first real upper hand in the bout when he simply tossed Wilde through the middle rope and to the outside like a sack of potatoes. Wilde then stumbled to his feet and was met by a suicide dive/shoulder block which put both men down. This then lead to a pretty intense brawl on the outside. Deadly ducked a charging Wilde, using his momentum to send him head first into the ring steps. Spilling momentarily into the crowd, the battle was routed back to the ring by event security and it raged on. Wilde came back into the ring with a springboard dropkick and then quickly followed up with a swinging neckbreaker and a two-count. Wilde was on a roll, then the tide changed after a long, hard brawl leaving both men out on their feet. A reversal of a waist-lock lead to Deadly nailing a Deadly Sin driver out of nowhere and BARELY getting the cover. WINNER: Alex Deadly, via pinfall, 18:25 Monkey Boy: Wow! What a fight! Winchester Smith: Indeed! I must admit that even I did not think we'd see that much heart in the ring tonight, but both men gave it their all! What a win for Alex Deadly while taking NOTHING away from "Lucky" Adam Wilde! Monkey Boy: I like this Deadly guy, he's a hell of a fighter. But on the flip side, Wilde has a pretty bitter mean streak of his own! Winchester Smith: Both are shining talents to keep an eye on, that much is certain. Monkey Boy: Ten-four _OLD_ buddy. Winchester Smith: Not gonna waste my time responding to you. My shrink said pretend he's not even there. So I'm pretending...he's not there...he's not there...he's not there... Monkey Boy: [pokes his head around Winchester's shoulder] BOO! I'M HERE!!! Winchester Smith: [PIMP slaps Monkey Boy rightout of his chair]. *CRASH* Ahh... much better. On with our card. Next up, we have a seemingly 'gold-bound' Ronnie "The Wolf" Black taking on the GWC's sickest man, "The Gorilla" Chris Hoff. [ Regional Title Bound -- Grudge Match ] Ronnie "The Wolf" Black vs. "The Gorilla" Chris Hoff ^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^ ~ MATCH OVERVIEW ~ ^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^ Ronnie Black started out by simply charging black who was taunting crowd members and folded him up with a vicious spear. This put Hoff on his back which lead to a series of hard stomps and a few elbows to the sternum. Hoff mounted little offense in the beginning of the match though this all changed when Black made the mistake of attempting a Wolf Bite too early. Hoff reversed it and executed a Gorilla Press Slam TO THE OUTSIDE...and Black landed on the TIME KEEPER'S TABLE! Hoff simply stood in the middle of the ring laughing while The Wolf attempted to drag himself from the pile of toothpicks he had just created. Black began to crawl from the mess when Hoff made his way to the outside. He then scooped up Black and rammed him back first into the ring post. The gave way to a POWERBOMB ON THE FLOOR!!! Hoff, again, just laughed with a sadistic looked plastered on his face. Hoff rolled the limp body of Black into the ring and pulled a chair out from under Winchester Smith. Hoff then proceeded to tie Black up in the ring ropes, taking his time. The official repeatedly warned Chris Hoff, then Hoff whacked the official good with a stiff chair shot. Hoff sized up Black. A swing...*CRACK* BLACK GETS HIS FEET UP AND KICKS THE CHAIR INTO HOFF'S FACE!!! Some officials are now making their way to the ring and make the mistake of untying the enraged Black. He straddles Hoff and begin pounding away, the two men beating each other senseless on the mat. Hoff headbutts Black and goes for the chair again. Black gets to it first, and nails Hoff on the back. Security proceeds to flood the ring and separate the two men. This doesn't stop to six foot eight, nearly three hundred pound Gorilla who tosses several officials off and jumps on Black. More officials flood the ring as well as police and both men are taken from ringside. WINNER: No Contest, at 17:46 Winchester Smith: That man is sick! He wanted to take care of Ronnie Black this week, too bad his plan was foiled! Black won't take a DAMN thing lying down and now Hoff knows it. Monkey Boy: Chris Hoff...you are my hero...WHOO! Winchester Smith: And you're damaged as well. These two competitors WILL meet again and settle this thing for good! Monkey Boy: Enough with the dramatic set-up jargon, get on with it. Need I remind you? Back-waxing? Winchester Smith: Well...I WAS going to eat when I left the arena tonight. Thanks for ruining that. Monkey Boy: My pleasure. Winchester Smith: I'm sure. Well folks, next up we have a Regional title match! This will be Little Blue Super Jew's first defense since capturing the championship a few weeks ago right here on this program! Monkey Boy: Does John "The Liberator" Steele have a chance? Winchester Smith: Well, we'll see in a moment-- Monkey Boy: I wasn't asking you. [shakes his magic eight ball] Signs point to NO! Ha! Winchester Smith: Let's find out... [ GWC Regional Title Table Match ] Little Blue Super Jew (RC) vs. John "The Liberator" Steele ^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^ ~ MATCH OVERVIEW ~ ^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^ The size difference between these two guys was amazing. Steele DWARFED the Regional Champion in the ring and immediately went to work. Steele went for LBSJ who did a baseball slide between the big man's legs and hit a low blow on his way. With Steele doubled over LBSJ hit the ropes and took him down with a bulldog. Steele tried to shake it off on the canvas as LBSJ went up top. A botched "Mosesault" (split-legged moonsault) lead to Steele taking control. John picked up LBSJ by his neck with both hands and drove him down in a powerbomb position for a near fall. Steele, determined more than usual shoved the champion into the buckle with a crack and went in with some hard elbows and knees. Steele brought him across the ring to the other buckle with another sickening crack and charged. Missing the big splash when LBSJ simply slumped to the canvas, Steele went down as well. Both men on the mat, the crowd then erupted. DAVID STEELE WAS MAKING HIS WAY TO THE RING!!! David stalked to the ring and eyed the two fallen men for a moment. By this time LBSJ was on his feet. Steele had grabbed a steel chair and slid it in the ring, following it in but LBSJ picked it up!!! Steele stood up and stared face to face with LBSJ who didn't cocked the chair, ready to blast David! David stood firmly in front of his fallen brother...as if... ...PROTECTING HIM!?!?! LBSJ looked as is he would take a shot at David, but before he could do so...DAVID TURNED AROUND AND NAILED A LIBERATOR BACKBREAKER ON JOHN STEELE!!! AND...... ...ANOTHER ONE!!! TWO CONSECUTIVE LIBERATORS ON JOHN STEELE!!! David wasn't done yet however, as LBSJ TOSSED STEELE THE CHAIR! David proceeded to sit his brother up, and NAILED HIM with a VICIOUS chair shot!!! John Steele, now busted WIDE open was tossed in the corner and stood up with it's support. Then... ...THE CROWD EXPLODES!!! IT'S RONNIE "THE WOLF" BLACK!!! He comes to the ring and... ...CHRIS HOFF OUT OF THE CROWD!!! THE TWO ARE BRAWLING INT HE AISLE WAY!!! David Steele now...cocks back...WOW! The crowd hushes as David Steele rips the most incredible chair shot you could imagine. Steele is bleeding from the forehead profusely now as LBSJ laughs hysterically. John Steele slumps down, no motion, eyes closed to the canvas. Not moving an inch. David Steele still holds the dented, blood covered chair as he stalks towards the ropes. LBSJ holds out a hand...AND STEELE SHOVES HIM OUT OF THE WAY!!! Steele just leaves the ring, chair in hand, and exits through the masses!!! EMTs now rush the ring to aide John "The Liberator" Steele who lies motionless in a pool of his own blood. WINNER: No Contest, 8:03 Winchester Smith: When is this going to stop! Really! It seems as though we need some U.S. Marines at ringside to curb this unwarranted violence! Two men in a week's time have been injured at the hands of some lunatic with a vendetta! Monkey Boy: ISN'T IT GREAT! WHOO! Winchester Smith: NO! No, it most CERTAINLY IS NOT! John Steele could be seriously injured in there and you think it's funny! David Steele's psychotic actions may have ended his career in this very ring! Monkey Boy: Errr...DUH! I saw it, dummy. Winchester Smith: My gosh...well...as hard as it may be after what we just witnessed I'm being told we must move on. Monkey Boy: Yeah! The show must go on!...and stuff. Winchester Smith: Unfortunately it must. We have a Heavyweight title match to get to yet. Monkey Boy: That's right! The Xtremist! WHOO! Winchester Smith:...takes on the current champion, The Messiah. I suppose we should call it three against one, however as you KNOW the Dark Project will be there to fight his battles for him! Monkey Boy: Yeah. Whatever. On with it... [ QUEST FOR COMPETITION MATCH #1 ] [ GWC Heavyweight Title Match ] The Messiah (HC) vs. The Xtremist ^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^ ~ MATCH OVERVIEW ~ ^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^ You could reach out and touch the tension in the arena, that's how thick it was. The Messiah came to the ring...ALONE! Surprised even Xtremist. The two men went from pillar to post as soon as the bell sounded. The Xtremist gave up some size but made up for it in tenacity. He started off with a trio of dropkicks that had the champion reeling then clipped out a leg and followed with a legscrew. Then a hard wrapping of the limb around a steel pole finished the series. Xtremist attempted and failed to drop a second rope elbow on the exposed knee giving Messiah the time to struggle to his feet. The champion then went on the attack. He came around Xtermist with a kick to the head, then a legdrop on top of it. He dragged The Xtremist to his feet, then sent him to the ropes giving the momentum for what was a TREMENDOUS DDT! A near fall sent the fans into a frenzy but Xtremist recovered rather quickly. Messiah was visually intensifying and maybe a bit frustrated when a failed powerbomb attempt that Xtremist turned into an odd sort of pinning combination set him off. Messiah pulled an unidentifiable foreign object from his tights and just wailed Xtremist in the face. He then locked on the ...RESURRECTION!!! Xtremist was damned near the middle of the ring and he was locked in the Resurrection! Xtremist was wailing in pain but refused to submit. Xtremist dragged himself a bit, and Messiah rose to drag him back. When Messiah did this, Xtremist seized the moment and kicked with all his might, sending Messiah through the middle ropes and to the floor outside. After taking a moment, Xtremist miraculously rose to his feet after that hold. He hit the near ropes and DOVE TO THE OUTSIDE WITH A CORKSCREW PLANCHA!!! Both men lye on the floor, motionless. What the hell!?! IT'S THAT DAMNED MYSTERY MAN AGAIN!!! The crazed fan jumps the rail!!! He's coming around the ring and-- The crowd erupts! MY LORD!!! IT'S JOHN "THE LIBERATOR" STEELE!!! Steele is barely able to walk, limping and favoring his right arm. His head is bandaged and his face stained red. The menacing look on his face was fear inducing enough, but the Liberator totes with him a BASEBALL BAT! Steele comes up and meets the fan have way...NAILS HIM!!! The Dark Project Mystery Man slumps to the floor! EMTs are trying to get Steele to return to the backstage area! !! DAVID STEELE FROM BEHIND !!! A scoop...PILEDRIVER ON THE FLOOR! MY GOD! MY GOD! David Steele disappears into the crowd!!! HERE COMES LITTLE BLUE SUPER JEW!!! He's got his Regional strap in hand and he's coming down to ringside!!! Messiah is being rolled into the ring by The Xtremist, finally back to the match. But LBSJ is closing in!!! NO! NO! NO! ASYLUM IS DOWN FROM BEHIND! HE TACKLES LBSJ AND THEY'RE BRAWLING IN THE AISLE!!! ! NEW DISEASE ! NEW DISEASE ! MESSIAH JUST GOT PLANTED!!! But Xtremist doesn't cover...no...HE DELIVERS ANOTHER!!! TWO CRADLE POWERBOMBS! IT'S OVER!!! WINNER: The Xtremist, via pinfall, at 20:13 Winchester Smith: My gosh almighty!!! I didn't think it was possible! Someone dethroned The Messiah! WE HAVE A NEW GWC HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPION!!! Monkey Boy: And it's THE XTREMIST! WHOO! Winchester Smith: What is with all the whoo-ing. Well, even the Dark Project couldn't stop the "King of Extreme" from snatching GWC gold right here tonight!!! For my cooky counterpart, I'm Winchester Smith saying...GOODNIGHT! Monkey Boy: TOODLES!
