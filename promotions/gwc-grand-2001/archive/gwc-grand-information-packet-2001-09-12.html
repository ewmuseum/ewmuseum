Info * Contacts * Outro/Welcome to GWC <><><><><><><><><<<>>><><><><><><><><><> Introduction/Welcome to the Grand Wrestling Corp. <><><><><><><><><<<>>><><><><><><><><><> Prospective Member: Welcome to the fresh, new, trail-blazing entity in the fantasy wrestling world. Our mission statement is to provide an enjoyable, organized, fair and fresh e-wrestling environment for all who chose to join our ranks. A goal of ours is to create names, not take them. We want to find the fresh talent just busting at the seams to make a name for themselves. We know you're out there, the guys who know if the "big time" feds would let you in you would dominate. If they would accept you, but you aren't a "big name". Well, this is the place where the doors are closed to those "big names". We don't want an e-wrestling Hulk Hogan stepping in and thinking he owns the place and crying like a baby when he gets stomped my Joe Blow, fair and square. This is a place where competition will be fostered and a place where hard work, commitment and skill will be rewarded. Originality and creativity and victory are your motivation. Are you up to the task of showing the big dogs how it's done? Signed, [name withheld] GWC Chairman <><><><><<<>>><><><><> Rules <><><><><<<>>><><><><> The rules are quite simple and probably similar to the set you have seen in your past experience. I will list them regardless so that I can avoid angry emails. These are the bare bones set, and will surely be edited/added to in the future and will be sent out to the members when they are updated. - We are a booked fed, meaning roleplays are the primary mode of participation and the primary criteria judged. Strategies are welcomed and are classified as participation, as are angle suggestions and heat. The more you participate in the handling of your character, the more successful you will be. If you think you were shafted and would like to know why you lost a particular match, ask me. POLITELY. I will not tolerate complaining about a loss. It's a subjective process and I decided that you were the loser in the contest. Deal with it. - PARTICIPATION IS KEY! If you don't participate in the game, you aren't playing it and will therefore perform poorly in it. I will not hesitate to eliminate your character from the roster and your email address from the list without notice if you fail to play the game. If there is something more important in your life than a game, I understand! Most things are! Just communicate this to me, and you will be granted a leave of absence for as long as you need. - Roleplays are the heart of this game. Though I ask that you be original and creative, there are some restrictions. Off limits are: excessive cursing, slander of a race(s), slander of religion(s), battering women, (physically) attacking another, etc. I think you get the gist of it. Any violation of these restrictions will lead to a reprimand and then termination w/o notice. - More to come. <><><><><<<>>><><><><> Shows/Misc. Information <><><><><<<>>><><><><> Starting out, we will operate on one summary card per week. This will increase to a larger "live" format when writers are found and the roster has grown and is solid. Then another summary show will be added on to complete the equation. We will also have the flash show (roleplay packet) which will be sent out twice a week. Here are the details: **Weekly Shows** GWC Titans (Tuesday): This is the flagship card, and will start out as a summary card and will MOST LIKELY increase to play-by-play format with more matches, in-rings, etc. GWC Invasion (Friday): This is the sister show that will remain a summary card permanently (unless by some act of God we have an abundance of committed writers). GWC Voices (Monday and Thursday): This is our one and only flash show. Roleplays will be sent out in one packet, twice a week. If a roleplay misses posted deadlines, it will be sent out with the next packet and will not have an impact on a match taking place the following night (i.e. You miss a Monday deadline w/ a RP, it won't have any effect on your Titans match on Tuesday). Making deadlines is important. **Pay Per Views (PPV)** February: GWC Frigid April: GWC Rampant Rage June: GWC Summer Daze August: GWC Code Red October: GWC FightFest Also, another important tidbit of information. Championships. The descriptions are below. I have omitted a tag title because the divisions are usually dull or non-existent. I have instead replaced it with a yearly tag team tournament. It will occur at a pay-per-view which has yet to be decided. **Championships** GWC Heavyweight Championship (name will be altered to World Championship as time progresses) GWC Regional Championship **Booking** Booking will basically be done blindly. I will try arrange them logically, leading to people advancing in the ranks, getting deserving title shots, etc. HOWEVER, you can have a tremendous impact on booking. If you want a match, tell me. If I don't give it to you, challenge him in a roleplay. I will get you the match eventually. Create an angle. Challenge. Ask. You have immense control over your character, use it. <><><><><<<>>><><><><> Contacts <><><><><<<>>><><><><> GWC Chairman (and every other job until I get some help!): [name withheld] - [detail withheld] AOL: [detail withheld] Webpage: http://www.geocities.com/gwcwrestling2000 <><><><><<<>>><><><><> Outro <><><><><<<>>><><><><> I thank you for your interest in the Grand Wrestling Corp. I speak for everyone here, that we eagerly await your application and future participation in the GWC!
