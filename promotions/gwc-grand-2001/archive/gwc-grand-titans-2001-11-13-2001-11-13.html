GWC Titans - Tuesday, November 13th, 2001

13 November 2001
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o Grand Wrestling Corporation presents... ~ GWC TITANS - Tuesday, November 13th, 2001 ~ o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o Tonight's Titans will showcase the talents of... ooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooo $$ MAIN EVENT $$ ~ Number One Contender Match ~ John "The Liberator" Steele vs. Little Blue Super Jew ::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::: -- John "The Liberator" Steele mysteriously 'gave' the win to LBSJ last week on Titans, and the two battle it out this week for a shot at the GWC CHAMPIONSHIP, and Messiah, a man who LBSJ defeated just last week in a non-title bout! Should get interesting fast... ~ Regional Title Match ~ The Xtremist (RC) vs. Dave Fresco ::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::: -- The Xtremist comes into yet another title defense, this time against a man who won his chance at the gold last week by defeating Tony Serpe! Should be interesting as the trail-blazing young talent takes a shot at REGIONAL GOLD! ~ Singles Match ~ Scottie Rayda vs. BaiN :::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::: -- Newcomer Scottie Rayda attempts to make a name for himself at the expense of hot newcomer, BaiN! ~ Four-Corners Match ~ George De Los Santos vs. Calypso vs. "Prime Time" Andre Harris vs. Fusion :::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::: ::::: -- Four newcomers to the GWC try to leave their mark, while two make their first impressions! Before Titans got underway, comments from the GWC Heavyweight Champion were shown... ooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooo **"Imperial March" by John Williams plays over the PA and down both sides of the entrance ramp are security dressed in Stormtrooper attire. And down the center dressed in a hooded cloak is none other than the GWC champion Messiah. He walks with mic in hand and steps into the ring.** Messiah: (mixed reactions by the crowd) No I have not jumped on the Episode 2 bandwagon. No no no! As a matter of fact, I wanted to use a little symbolism tonight. Apparently, it has been decided that I am the "DARKSIDE" of the GWC. And that is fine by me. And now, it is time to change things up. Little Blue Super Jew. I have heard that you are bragging in the back that you defeated the Champion. Well let me inform you and the rest of the people out there that, that my friend is a straight up lie! Show the footage... **footage is shown on the Grandtron of Messiah passing out and the match being called last week on Titans** Messiah: I never said "I quit". And therefore, I never lost. But as far as it is concerned... I don't even mind. But that match has given me 20/20 vision. And I want to make a proposition to you LBSJ. Why don't you join me? With our combined strength, we can bring order to the GWC. Now I don't want to destroy you, even though I could any day of the week...twice on Sunday. Now it only stands to reason that a master needs an apprentice. And LBSJ you have proven yourself worthy to join me. So I await your answer. But remember the old phrase," if you aren't with me....you are against me." But if you join me, you will be next in line for the GWC title one day. As far as the Liberator goes, you are worthless. You are nothing. You are simply (censored) on the bottom of my shoe. And I am gonna scrape off that (censored) and take care of you personally. You got an itch....I'll make you my bitch cause your pain.... Crowd: IS MY GAIN!!!!!!!!!!!!! **Imperial March plays as Messiah exits** [...screen reveals darkness with an unusual metallic-like shine to it. Slowly, it tilts, with the lighting providing the shine pouring over certain sections revealing assorted letters and designs. Then suddenly, a flash of light reveals the GWC logo and then disappears. This screen dissolves to the interior of a studio-like setting. There is a desk with the GWC Titans logo on the front, constructing of steel mesh and textured plating. Two microphones are perched on small stands, sitting behind them are two recognizable faces. Monkey Boy scratches his temple, the stupefied look plastered seemingly permanently on his face. The other chair is occupied by Winchester Smith, who characteristically shuffles papers, then puts on his smile after noticing the camera...] Winchester Smith: WELCOME one and all to the NOVEMBER 13TH, 2001 edition of GWC TITANS! Monkey Boy: So...yeah. Here we are. Once again. Titans. Matches. Yup. Winchester Smith: Well aren't you a sharp knife. Sheesh. Monkey Boy: Sharp knife? What the hell is that supposed to mean you old prune? Winchester Smith: Nevermind, schmuck. Moving on to the entire reason I drag my freckled arse out here every week, the wrestling! First off, we have a four-corners match comprised of the new wave of talent in the GWC! These are the future, the next champions...so it should be interesting to see how this one unfolds. Monkey Boy: Future? This is the bottom of the barrel boys and girls. The part of the show where you take your bathroom break. (grin) Winchester Smith: Yeah...not even gonna waste my breath arguing with ignorance...ON WITH TITANS! Wait...I'm hearing we have a shot of George De Los Santos backstage? Well, lets see what this is all about... [camera momentarily switches to a shot of the backstage area. Wrestlers such as Scotty Rayda and Dave Fresco are seen walking around as well as many GWC officials and random personnel. GWC rookie George De Los Santos is seen shadow boxing in full wrestling attire in preparation for his imminent bout with three others. He nods at Rayda as he walks past, Rayda saying "Hello.". Fresco gave a wave after Santos said hello as well, and then the camera flips to the ring...] ooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooo ~ Four-Corners Match ~ George De Los Santos vs. Calypso vs. "Prime Time" Andre Harris vs. Fusion ooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooo MATCH OVERVIEW: ------------------------------ -- The match was a bit chaotic at the bell, more of a barroom brawl than a wrestling match. Finally, things shaped up when Fusion was incapacitated by a Harris chairshot on the outside. This then lead to De Los Santos and Calypso teaming up on Andre Harris. Harris struggled to maintain himself, but made a slight comeback when he ducked a double clothesline attempt by De Los Santos and Calypso and leveled them both. By this time Fusion had made his way back into the ring and was doubled over by a low blow from Harris. Fusion, on his back, had Harris's attention before a release German suplex sent Harris to the outside. Santos then turned on his 'partner in crime' from the beginning of the contest, Calypso. Santos nailed a cross-body block then followed quickly with an Asai moonsault. Santos then went to the top, waiting for Calypso to rise to his feet. Santos went for a missile dropkick, but Calypso had been allowed to gain his bearings and swatted away Santos as he ducked. Calypso then, however, failed with a waist-lock set-up that lead to a Santos victory roll and pinfall. WINNER: George De Los Santos, via pinfall, at 13:11 Winchester Smith: Solid performance and victory by the exciting newcomer, George De Los Santos! Good showing...I'd be keeping an eye on this one if I were men like The Xtremist and Messiah. Monkey Boy: Whoa there bucko! He's got some way to go before he steps in the ring with the big dogs. Winchester Smith: Finally, you speak the truth. However the fact remains that this kid has a future. If only the rest of them showed the same promise. Then the champions would REALLY be uneasy! Monkey Boy: Yeah, well, gold glitters in the front of you while it paints a target on your back. Winchester Smith: GOOD LORD! A flash of ELOQUENCE! I hope someone got that on tape! Send it to his mother, quickly! Monkey Boy: Shut up old man! Get on with this thing... oooooooooooooooooo ~ Singles Match ~ Scottie Rayda vs. BaiN oooooooooooooooooo MATCH OVERLOOK: ------------------------------ -- One word. Slaughter. Scottie Rayda came in and performed every rookie mistake one could imagine. To his credit, the crazy man wouldn't stay down until the very end. BaiN made his fair share of slip-ups but dominated for the most part. After a stiff Nail (spear) Bain locked in his Hades submission lock for the tap-out victory. Afterwards, BaiN grabbed a chair and tied up the near-unconscious Radya in the ropes. BaiN then nailed a series of SICKENING chairshots to the head and face of Rayda which bloodied him up considerably. To the crowd's surprise, an unknown wrestler charged the ring, sliding through the ropes and immediately laying into BaiN with a flurry of kicks and punches. The stray chair was then used to put BaiN over with a Wolf Bite by a man identified as new GWC star Ronnie "The Wolf" Black. With BaiN unconscious and Rayda freed to receive medical attention, Black simply left the ring to a mixed, mostly positive reaction. WINNER: BaiN, via submission, at 4:29 [...backstage, Shizza Hotee catches up with Ronnie "The Wolf" Black for a few words...] Shizza Hotee: I'm here with Ronnie 'The Wolf' Black, one of the many newcomers here in GWC. Now Ronnie, why did you involve yourself in BaiN's match? RB: It's nothing personal. I didn't sign with GWC just to mess with BaiN. But when I see a guy who's that big doing crap like that...nah. I don't hang with that. So, BaiN, you may bash me for sticking my schnoz in your business, but let me tell you this. If you go looking for trouble, then you're going to get it. And especially if it's in my general direction. [...back in announce booth...] Winchester Smith: Well, that was a bit less than what I expected. However, BaiN IS picking up steam. We shall see if he goes anywhere with it. Interesting also, was an appearance by newcomer Ronnie Black. Interesting situation that may become... Monkey Boy: Err...yeah. NEXT. Winchester Smith: Up next we have a relatively new fighter in Dave Fresco challenging for the Regional Championship! Monkey Boy: Nope, not gonna happen. Xtremist is my guy, the man is dangerous and more so because he's INSANE! He'll leave with his gold, guaranteed. Winchester Smith: Bold statements. We shall see...TO THE RING! ooooooooooooooooooooooooooo ~ Regional Title Match ~ The Xtremist (RC) vs. Dave Fresco ooooooooooooooooooooooooooo MATCH OVERLOOK: ------------------------------ -- Dave Fresco BLAZED out of his corner, showing the first real threat to the Xtremist's title reign. He grossly overpowered the champion, starting out with a GORILLA PRESS! This lead to a focus on the back of the Xtremist, with Fresco next nailing a RUNNING POWERBOMB and finishing his first assault with a CAMEL CLUTCH! The Xtremist was able to reach the ropes rather quickly, then rose to his feet. Fresco sent him to the ropes but missed an attempted boot and was taken down by a leg-drag. Xtremist then went to work. An Irish whip which lead to a hurricarana to the outside set-up for the Xtremist's signature. CHAOS! Xtremist hit the skies with a CORKSCREW SUICIDE PLANCHA! He nailed it and after recovering hit two consecutive dropkicks which put Fresco over the Spanish announce table. Xtremist yanked a time keeper's chair, draped it across Fresco and climbed up top! Xtremist NAILS A MOONSAULT ON THE CHAIR, THROUGH THE TABLE! What was left was the two men in a tangled, unconscious mess. Miraculously, Xtremist began to stir and rolled off the mess of table, chair, and Fresco. When Xtremist got to his feet, the crowd got loud as TONY 'THE DRAGON' SERPE made his way down the ramp. Xtremist, oblivious to the presence of Serpe, dragged Fresco to his feet. Xtremist then went to climb into the ring but was met by a SERPE SUICIDE DIVE! Xtremist was laid out and Fresco didn't know where he was. The bell was called for, the match declared a NO CONTEST. With Fresco clearing out the cobwebs and The Xtremist rising to his feet, a three-man brawl ensued. WINNER: Match Declared A NO CONTEST, at 19:27 Winchester Smith: WOW! What a match! Monkey Boy: WOW! What a BRAWL! They were tearing each other apart! This is gonna boil over in the coming weeks, that is for sure! Winchester Smith: Serpe and Fresco both want each other and both want gold! Xtremist is not going to be a happy champion either! Monkey Boy: But, true to my words, HE IS STILL CHAMPION. (grin) Winchester Smith: By a stroke of incredible luck! Regardless, we have the MAIN EVENT right around the corner! LITTLE BLUE SUPER JEW and JOHN ''THE LIBERATOR'' STEELE go at it for a shot at MESSIAH'S Heavyweight Championship!!! Monkey Boy:...TO THE RING! (mockingly) Winchester Smith: THAT'S MY LINE! ooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooo $$ MAIN EVENT $$ ~ Number One Contendor Match ~ John "The Liberator" Steele vs. Little Blue Super Jew ooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooo MATCH OVERVIEW: ----------------------------- -- Match begins with a classic elbow and shoulder lock-up. Steele instantly over powers the much smaller LBSJ sending him stumbling back to the ropes. Steele closes in fast and nails a kick to the gut followed by a VICIOUS powerbomb. Steele drags LBSJ to his feet and whips him towards the buckle. LBSJ counters by using his momentum to scale to the top rope rapidly and execute a moonsault. He is, however, caught in mid-flight by the massive Steele who spikes LBSJ to the canvas with a variation of a tombstone pile- driver. Steele goes for a cover, gets two. Visibly frustrated, Steele yanks LBSJ to his feet violently and backhands him with all his might. LBSJ stumbles back to the corner and Steele follows. LBSJ ducks a forearm, stomping Steele's leg out from under him then bowling him over with a second rope shoulder block. LBSJ rolls through that move, coming up on his feet behind Steele. LBSJ dropkicks Steele's leg out from under him again then wraps it up in a half crab. Steele, fuming, thrusts his leg sending LBSJ flying half of the ring's distance across and down to the mat. Steele gets up, LIVID and limps over to LBSJ. Steele straddles LBSJ and just starts wailing on him with closed fists to the face and temple. The official warns Steele who then begins choking out LBSJ screaming at the top of his lungs, his eyes bugging nearly out of his face; bloodshot. The official again warns Steele who screams at the official to back off, then lifts LBSJ clear off the canvas and plants him in a powerbomb variation. He makes the cover, but only gets two. LBSJ tries desperately to get to his feet, but is scooped up by Steele who plants LBSJ with ANOTHER CHOKESLAM POWERBOMB! LBSJ is covered and kicks out BARELY, right before the three. Steele is stomping around the ring like a mad-man now, yanks LBSJ to his feet again, and executes not one...NOT TWO.. BUT _THREE CONSECUTIVE_ LIBERTIY BACKBREAKERS! Then, he rolls up LBSJ in a pinning combination for the three count. The Messiah's face appears on the big screen over the mat, a wide grin on his face. Under this, Jessica runs to the ring with a look of desperate concern on her face. She gets in the ring and attempts to calm a visibly shaken and angry John 'The Liberator' Steele. WINNER: John "The Liberator" Steele, via pinfall, at 28:11 Winchester Smith: JOHN STEELE SNAPPED! He was trying to choke the LIFE out of LBSJ who didn't know what the hell to make of it! Monkey Boy: Well, he sure wasn't expecting it! I wasn't even expecting it! But to LBSJ's credit, the man WOULDN'T GO DOWN! Two double-handed choke-slam powerbombs and THREE Liberty backbreakers to put the man down! Incredible stamina... Winchester Smith: Well this three-way rivalry just keeps escalating in animosity and intrigue. We'll see you next week where the lid may just blow off this triangle of hatred! GOODNIGHT! Monkey Boy: TOODLES!!! [Steele is walking back up the aisle with Jessica on his arm as she talks in his ear. LBSJ is still lying unconscious on the canvas as trainers look him over, attempting to awaken him. A shocked crowd is silent as the camera flips to the GWC logo.
