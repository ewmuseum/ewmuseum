GWC Titans - Tuesday, October 9th, 2001

9 October 2001
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- Grand Wrestling Corporation presents..... ~ GWC TITANS - Tuesday, October 10th, 2001 ~ -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- TONIGHT'S LINE-UP: ------------------------------ -------------------------------------------------- -- GWC Heavyweight Title Tourney -- -------------------------------------------------- Final: <><><> ***Main evenT*** ~ GWC HEAVYWEIGHT TITLE MATCH ~ Messiah vs. Aj Nin Red Rum -------------------------------------------- -- GWC Regional Title Tourney -- -------------------------------------------- Mike Lawless vs. the Weasel Mr. Jersey vs. Lightning Joe Six-pack vs. Cobra Jim Klown Bowe vs. Axel Edge -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=--=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- =-=-=- Before the card got underway, Dementor had these words for the crowd on-had... -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=--=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- =-=-=- Dementor slowly makes his way to the ring, 'My Plague' blaring the sounds of Slipknot as huge amounts of red fire mark his every step. He climbs into the ring, slowly, with purpose. The fans of GWC aren't yet familiar with this sadistic and popular wrestler. They know that he was one of the first OWF champions, just days after joining, but now, he has to start again. This only gives him more purpose...He absorbs the chants of 'Dementor' as he is in the ring, holding one hand up high, before climbing out and grabbing a microphone from the ring announcers. He then climbs in again, and in his deep, menacing voice, he starts to make his first GWC speech... "I don't know or care who the president here is but...may I ask something? Why the Hell haven't I already got a title match? You know it. I know it...The whole damn world knows it...I - WILL - SEND -YOU..." he trails off to let the fans finish for him "...TO HELL!" The crowd go into a frenzy. He let's them die down before starting up again. "Now that I've said that. I want to set the record books. I will be the only man that has won the GWC title on their first match. You know I'm worthy. But if you feel like hesitating..." He taunts, making an inverted cross with both his hands, first vertically, then with a double cut throat with his thumbs to finish it. The crowd simulates the move. Who would have though that someone as evil as Dementor, the One Man Darkside would be so popular on his first match. He never was in OWF... "Now then. I'm gonna give you to the count of 13 to get your scrawny punk ass down here...and you know what will happen if you don't..." -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=--=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- =-=-=- -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- ~ MATCH 1~ Jim Klown Bowe vs. Axel Edge -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- MATCH OVERVIEW: ----------------------------- Farely quick bout. Edge was looking very out of it when Bowe came in quick. Very fast, very furious offense by Bowe who barely faltered the entire match-up. Edge mounted a small offense with a small package that gained him nothing but a two-count. The rest of the bout was all Bowe. Towards the end, the Klowning put Edge down for the pinfall. WINNER: Jim Klown Bowe, via pinfall, at 4:12 -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- ~ MATCH 2 ~ Joe Six-pack vs. Cobra -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- MATCH OVERVIEW: ----------------------------- The match was one of the better of the evening. Joe Six-pack was very hungry for gold, coming out of his corner swinging like a banshee. He had complete control of the first section of the bout, slaughtering Cobra and quelling every attempt and turning the tide. None the less, the tide turned when Cobra planted a stiff side-kick to the chin of Six-pack, sending him to the outside. An amazing suicide dive off the middle of the top rope (spring board) put Six-pack down. Cobra proceeded to whale on Six-pack with hard boots, and nailed a beautiful vertical suplex right onto the ring steps. The battle spilled into the crowd for a few moments, ending with Joe hitting a piledriver on the concrete. Fans ate it up, huge pops. Battle wound up back in the squared circle, with Cobra and Joe trading blows like boxers. Joe then charged Cobra and nailed a spinebuster. Cobra was dazed, and got back up only to be powerbombed into a pin he could not seem to escape. WINNER: Joe Six-pack, via pinfall, at 12:28 -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- ~ MATCH 3 ~ Mr. Jersey vs. Lightning -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- MATCH OVERLOOK: Another quickie. Jersey came to the ring half asleep and wearing a ball gown. Obviously drunk, Lightning toyed with him briefly. Then, a simple roll up put Jersey away. WINNER: Lightning , via pinfall, at 2:01 -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- ~ MATCH 4 ~ Mike Lawless vs. the Weasel -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- MATCH OVERLOOK: Both men came out strong, though Weasel was definately not looking too sharp. Weasel gained the upper hand in the early going, but lost it quickly when he tried to execute some ridiculous moves based on his baseball obsession. He then exited the ring, screeching at the fans that "He's better than the Babe!" and "Bonds is nothing compared to me!". After trying to convince a SANE capacity crowd that he was a baseball god, he returned to the ring to get clobbered by Mike Lawless. Lawless hit a slew of high impact moves which obliterated any change the Weasel had at victory. Lawless ended it with a big tornado DDT to get the cover. WINNER: Mike Lawless, via pinfall, at 8:10 -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=--=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- =-=-=- Before the title match, Messiah had this to say... -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=--=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- =-=-=- **The lights go down and Tougher Than Leather by Run DMC hits the PA. The crowd immediately recognizes who is coming out and begins to boo. Strobes come on and Lasers shoot all over the arena. As pyro goes off, Messiah walks out in a full suit. He pulls a mic out of his sports coat and begins to address the crowd** Messiah: (excited) Tonight, I Messiah, will achieve greatness here in the GWC. Tonight, I will defeat Red Rum or whatever his name is, and gain the GWC heavyweight title. **chorus of booes** Messiah: All of you out there have waited your whole lives to see me in person, and not only in person but in a Heavyweight title match. You saved your pennies, you saved your food stamps, you saved all of your hard earned money to see my ass win. And you will not be disappointed. OH NO! Tonight, I will stand before you, the locker room, and the whole world and become the biggest,... **crowd begins to chant,"Asshole". Messiah waves his hands like a orchestra conductor** Messiah: Yes I am. And each and everyone of you love me, you know you do, you can't help yourselves. Tonight, I will stand before you, the locker room, the world and God himself and become the biggest name in sports entertainment, and become the GWC heavyweight champ. Now Red Rum, no need to cry. No need to fret. I know you worry about your job because after I break you in the ring tonight, you may never work in this business again. Don't worry. We will just take a cue from other losers in the business. I am sure you could do the job for a few dollars in Ohio Vally Wrestling if need be.(laughs as the crowd booes) Oh I am sure, you could work at EMILL in Mexico. They are always looking for a "few good men" if you know what I mean. As a matter of fact, you could start your own federation Red Rum. Maybe you could call it HOMO. (even the crowd laughes a little) From what I am told in the back, I need to watch my ass. Of course some of the others (censored) in the back seem unimpressed by your "wrestling skills" if you feel me knockin'. (crowd continues to snicker but also boo) But moving on, after the match tonight, after I take out (censored)-boy, all of you in the crowd will have to bow down before me and kiss my feet. And those wrestlers who have never experienced greatness in the back...take a long look at your savior. I am the man that will carry this federation to greatness. And all those who don't believe me, you fans, scrub wrestlers in the back,...you got an itch...I'll make you my bitch. Cause your pain,(aims the mic to the crowd) Crowd:(yells) ... IS MY GAIN!!! **Tougher than Leather plays again, and Messiah continues to play up to the crowd smiling** -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- ~~ MAIN EVENT ~~ ~ GWC HEAVYWEIGHT TITLE MATCH ~ -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- Aj Nin Red Rum vs. Messiah MATCH OVERLOOK: ------------------------------ The FIRST EVER GWC Heavyweight title match was a good one. Messiah came out strong, putting Red Rum on the mat with a drop kick to the right knee and then wrapping up the leg in an leg-bar submission. Red Rum didn't seemed to fazed by the move, though a temporary limp was evident afterwards. Red Rum came back with a huricarana that sent Messiah to the outside, where he hit the concrete hard. Red Rum then climbed to the top buckle, and still in the opening minutes of the bout, hit a SHOOTING STAR PRESS to the OUTSIDE! Both men were on their backs for a good two minutes, the official instructed not to let the match end in disqualification. Amazingly, Messiah was the first to come to, and hit a piledriver on the matted concrete which didn't sit well with Red Rum. It actually _pissed him off!_. He got to his feet rather quickly (considering what had just happened) and his a beautiful crescent kick and followed that by whipping Messiah back first into the steps. This furthered the damage to Messiah's back region, but didn't come near finishing him. Messiah ducked a flying clothesline on the outside, and lured Red Rum into the ring's interior. There, Messiah sweeped that limb out from under Aj Nin and went right back to work. A half crab inflicted some very evident pain, with Red Rum barely reaching a rope. Messiah gave a few good stomps and dropped an elbow on the leg from the second rope. About to lock in a version of the sharpshooter, Red Rum rolled up Messiah in a small package combination for a very close fall. Messiah, frustrated, started viciously kicking Red Rum in the ribs. He sent Aj Nin to the ropes, and missed a boot which lead to Aj Nin hitting a cross body block. Red Rum then hit a hard missile drop kick from up top to the back of Messiah! Messiah was then put in a CAMEL CLUTCH which bore down on the weakened back! Red Rum, nearly with a submission allowed Messiah to reach the ropes. Red Rum was furious, and was screaming at the official. Messiah seized the opportunity in front of him and clipping his targeted leg out from under Aj Nin Red Rum. Quickly, Messiah locked in the Resurrection!!! Red Rum writhed in pain on the canvas with NO WHERE to go! He reached desperately for the ropes but three times REFUSED to submit, spitting in the face of the official on the fourth attempt. Eventually, after nearly THREE AND A HALF MINUTES in the hold, Red Rum passed out from the immense pain and was counted out by the official. WINNER: Messiah, via submission, at 20:45 ! ! !
