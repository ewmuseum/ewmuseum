GWC Titans - Tuesday, October 16th, 2001

16 October 2001

-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- Grand Wrestling Corporation presents..... ~ GWC TITANS - Tuesday, October 16th, 2001 ~ -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- TONIGHT'S LINE-UP: ------------------------------ <><><><><><>>>><<<><><><><><> GWC TITANS - 10.16.01 <><><><><><>>>><<<><><><><><> ***Main evenT*** ~ GWC HEAVYWEIGHT TITLE RE-MATCH ~ Messiah (Champion) vs. Aj Nin Red Rum (challenger) ---------------------------------------------- --- GWC Regional Title Tourney --- ---------------------------------------------- ~ GWC REGIONAL TITLE MATCH ~ Winner of Lawless/Lightning vs. Winner of Six-pack/Bowe Semi-finals: <><><><><> Mike Lawless vs. Lightning Joe Six-pack vs. Jim Klown Bowe ~ DOUBLE-DEBUT MATCH ~ Dementor vs. Dark Prince -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= ...Before the show got underway, our GWC HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPION had these words for the crowd... -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= **After the beginning promo and Titans Begins, Tougher than Leather by Run DMC hits and signals the entrance of the first ever GWC Heavyweight Champion, Messiah. The lights go down and the pyro goes off. Out into the strobes steps Messiah, proudly displaying the title over his left shoulder. He walks to the corners of the entrance and lifts it showing it off to the audience receiving somewhat of a mixed reaction. He slowly walks towards the ring as the chorus of cheers and boos continue. He steps over the second rope and prepares for his first in-ring interview as the champion** Messiah: Thank you for that, somewhat warm reception. (smiles to the crowd still being bet with cheers AND boos) As the first ever, GWC heavyweight champion, the first business I want to take care of is the thank you's. I want to first off thank... (crowd attempts to start an "Asshole"chant) Messiah: Excuse me, I am trying to talk. As I was saying, I want to first off thank the man, who without his talents, I could not have achieved this illustrious title. This man has been with me ever since I could remember. This man was there through the troubles and the trials, and endured no matter what. This man, was the person I idolized, the person I still idolize. This man is the biggest name in this business. His name is....MESSIAH!!!! (crowd cheers and boos) Messiah: Are you kidding me? I did it all by myself. What?! Did you think I was going to thank Buddy Rogers? Ric Flair?? Hulk Hogan?? No!! None of them even compare to me. I am undefeated and not only that, but the first ever Undefeated heavyweight champion in GWC!! Now on to business. I am scheduled for a re-match for MY title tonight live? And why is that?? Cause the brass is forcing me! That's why! I wanna run something by the Chairman back there. As the champion I should be able to make some stipulations. So hear me out and later in the show I will be watching and you can give me an answer. If I have to face Aj Nin again, I think he should work for that re-match. Put him up against some body in the back that doesn't have a match. If he wins, then in the main event I will defeat him again. (crowd cheers and boos) Messiah: I don't know but I seem to remember that (censored)er squealing like a baby when I locked him into the resurrection. He cried and screamed for his mommy. I really (fake crying)...I really felt for him. Aj Nin, if you....(sniffs)...if you want to take my title...(angry)THEN YOU BETTER FIGHT!!! I will not subject my talents to the loosing end of the scrub wrestlers in the back. HELL, I don't even think I should have to defend tonight. But if you want to wrestle me again little man, All you got to do is make me submit...that's right. If I gotta wrestle this guy I WANT A SUBMISSION MATCH!!! I have never quit and I will NEVER EVER QUIT!!! So Aj Nin, You got an itch, I'll make you my bitch! Cause your pain.... (crowd,"IS MY GAIN!!!" Cheers and Boos follow as his music plays and he walks to the corners shouting to the crowd) Messiah: THAT'S RIGHT!!! I AM THE CHAMPION!! FOREVER!!!!!!!! -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- ~ MATCH 1 ~ ~ DOUBLE DEBUT MATCH ~ Dementor vs. Dark Prince -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- MATCH OVERLOOK: ----------------------------- Match started off...competitive. Both men were at each others throats as if they were old rivals. Dementor stepped it up and took control, nailing a spine buster followed by a running bulldog. Dark Prince was stunned, and when he came to his feet was nearly met with the Dark Side Driver. This angered Prince, who slapped Dementor, as if sending a message. He then executed a fine belly-to-belly suplex, putting Dementor on his back. A failed guillotine legdrop put Dementor back on top of things after regaining his composure. Dementor punished Prince for a good while, almost making him submit to a viciously applied arm-bar submission. Prince rebounded with a flurry of kicks, backing Dementor into the corner, then retreated to the far buckle to gain momentum and deliver a huge splash. Dementor, dazed, stumbled out of the corner and walked right into a huge jumping DDT. A close cover lead to Prince gaining steam and setting up for the Dark Side Drop. Before executing the maneuver, Dementor stood up, as if to back body drop his opponent off of him in desperation. Instead, Dementor hung on to Prince's legs, swung him around and up onto his shoulder, and executed a gigantic tombstone piledriver, an assumed version of the Dark Side Driver. This was followed by a decisive pinfall. WINNER: Dementor, via pinfall, at 10:11 -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- ~ MATCH 2 ~ ~ Regional Title Tourney, Semi-Final ~ Joe Six-pack vs. Jim Klown Bowe -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- MATCH OVERLOOK: ----------------------------- A drunken Bowe stumbled to ringside as Six-pack laughed, enjoying his embarrassment. Bowe was assisted into the ring and stood there, wavering. Six-pack toyed with him briefly, giving him a few fresh slaps to the face. Six-pack, finished laughing now, simply kicked Bowe in the gut and executed a DDT. This lead to a quick cover. WINNER: Joe Six-pack, via pinfall, at 3:15 -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- ~ MATCH 3 ~ ~ Regional Title Tourney, Semi-Final ~ Mike Lawless vs. Lightning -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- MATCH OVERLOOK: ------------------------------ Another quick, embarrassing match-up. Lawless looked like he was blind-folded in the ring and Lightning didn't look much better. What would have been more appropriately called a dance recital was finally ended with a small package on the part of Lightning. WINNER: Lightning, via pinfall, 4:02 -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= ~ MATCH 4 ~ ~ Regional Title Tourney, FINAL ~ ~ REGIONAL TITLE MATCH ~ Joe Six-pack vs. Lightning -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= MATCH OVERLOOK: Six-pack, despite having fought a match (if you could call it that) earlier in the evening look sharp. Lightning was less than impressive, though he put up a fight. Six-pack dominated most of the bout, brawling his way through it like he was in a bar of enemies. Six-pack with a series of kicks, put Lightning down in the corner and was met with a Buckin' Bronco. Lightning was dragged to his feet, and got nailed with a hard powerslam, then with a second rope leg-drop. Six-pack, sensing victory hit a hard TRIO of piledrivers. Lightning, miraculously got his leg on the rope. Six-pack, furious, took it to the outside. After hanging Lightning out to dry over a hard steel guard rail, a table was destroyed by Joe using a body-slam to put Lightning right through it. Joe simply rolled Lightning back into the ring and hit a version of the Dukes of Hazzard Decapitator, as Lightning was unable to stand it was simply a top-rope leg drop to the back of Lightning's head. This lead to a pinfall victory. WINNER: Joe Six-pack, via pinfall, at 12:45 ~ NEW GWC REGIONAL CHAMPION! ~ -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- ~~ MAIN EVENT ~~ ~ GWC HEAVYWEIGHT TITLE RE-MATCH ~ The Messiah (C) vs. Aj Nin Red Rum -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- MATCH OVERLOOK: ------------------------------ The match started with the drama of a stare-down. Words that would not be made out were exchanged at close range. Then, a shoving match ensued. At the second shove of Messiah, Red Rum twisted the right arm of the champion and used a drop-toe hold to execute a crossface submission. Messiah was able to quickly reach the ropes, and the battle continued. Messiah hit a series of hard chops and then sent Red Rum to the ropes to be met by a choke slam. Messiah went for a cover, but barely grabbed a two-count. Red Rum, up quickly exchanged stiff punches with the champion, but was unsuccessful. Red Rum sent Messiah to the outside, and climbed to the top ropes. When Messiah got to his feet, he looked around for Red Rum, but could not locate him because he was in mid-air! A FOUR-FIFTY SPLASH TO THE OUTSIDE dazzled the crowd and obliterated Messiah! Both men were out on the mat for a good two minutes before either even began to move! Red Rum slowly got back to his feet, and folded up a chair. The official warned the challenger to no avail. Red Rum draped the equalizer across the chest of the champion. Red Rum got a running start, then leaped onto the announce table while still running and executed a MOONSAULT FROM THE TABLE ONTO THE CHAIR! This move hurt both men, Red Rum writhing in pain on the floor, grabbing his chest. Messiah, AMAZINGLY, was the first to get to his feet. Red Rum was quick to follow, and charged the champion. A back body drop sent Red Rum, arms flailing, into the solid steel ring post HEAD FIRST! This instantly split the forehead of Red Rum open like a cantaloupe! Blood smeared on his face, the champion picked him up onto his knees, and hit three stiff kicks to the face before dragging the limp body of the challenger back into the ring. Red Rum attempted twice to get back on his feet, but collapsed each time. The Messiah laughed at Aj Nin Red Rum's obvious pain. Messiah began to hammer away at the knee of Red Rum. After a significant assault on the right leg of the challenger, Messiah applied a Texas Cloverleaf! Right in the middle of the ring, the champion relentlessly wrenched down on the hold. Red Rum, MIRACULOUSLY survived the hold for TWO MINUTES and then began dragging himself AND the champion towards the ropes! Just before grapping what would free him, Messiah stood and dragged Aj Nin BACK TO THE MIDDLE OF THE RING! Red Rum had NO WHERE to go! He's...is he passing out? WHAT THE!?! HERE COMES JOHN 'THE LIBERATOR' STEELE? What is this NEW COMER ot the GWC coming down to ringside for!?! Messiah instantly releases the hold and comes to the ropes! Steele is yelling some not kind words at Messiah and Messiah is furious! Steele flips him the bird and here come the officials fromt he back folks! Preventing a brawl between the champion and John Steele, an army of officials from the backstage area are escorting Steele from ringside! Messiah now...returning to business. Aj Nin is still on the mat, barely moving. The champion goes to drag him up into a powerbomb set-up...NO! NO! NO! AJ NIN RED RUM WAS PLAYING POSSUM! CRADLE TOMBSTONE PILEDRIVER!!! DEAD WEIGHT!!! THIS ONE IS OVER!!! NEW CHAMPION!!! NEW CHAMPION!!! WINNER: Aj Nin Red Rum, via pinfall, at 22:45 ***NEW GWC HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPION!!!*** -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- [name withheld] Chairman, Grand Wrestling Corp. - http://www.geocities.com/gwcwrestling2000 <><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><> "Dangerous" William Devonshire (IWF/WOW, MLW) <><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><> [detail withheld] "Would you care for a CHOCOLATE COVERED PRETZEL?" - Brody, Mallrats (<--- tied for best movie in history w/ Ferris Bueller's Day Off)
