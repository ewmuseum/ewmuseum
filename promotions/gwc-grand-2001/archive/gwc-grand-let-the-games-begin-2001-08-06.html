GWC Let the games begin...

Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[GWC] Let the games begin... 08.6.01 ===================== Today is the day that the Grand Wrestling Corp. begins it's search for fresh new talent that will occupy the roster of what will ideally be a big part of ewrestling's future. We are not looking for veterans, though you are welcome. We're looking to make names, not take them. The Grand Wrestling Corp. is a booked fed, currently ran solely by Chairman [name withheld]. We are also looking to hire a staff, bookers, writers, etc. We will be contacting certain wrestlers that our scouts find particularlly impressive, so keep an eye on your inbox. If you are interesting in possibly being part of the future, please feel free to contact the Chairman of the GWC, [name withheld], at [detail withheld] . Upon your inquiry, you will be forwarded an information packet which will include a welcome letter, introduction to the GWC, all the information on the running/future plans of the GWC, etc. Thanks for reading, we hope to hear from you all very soon. Signed, [name withheld] GWC Chairman http://www.geocities.com/gwcwrestling2000
