GWC Titans - Tuesday, October 23rd, 2001

23 October 2001

-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- Grand Wrestling Corporation presents..... ~ GWC TITANS - Tuesday, October 24th, 2001 ~ -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- TONIGHT'S LINE-UP: ------------------------------ <><><><><><>>>><<<><><><><><> GWC TITANS - 10.23.01 <><><><><><>>>><<<><><><><><> ***Main evenT*** ~ Regional Title Match! ~ Joe Six-pack (C) vs. Dementor ~ TRIPLE THREAT MATCH for REGIONAL TITLE SHOT ~ Dragula vs. John 'The Liberator' Steele vs. The Xtremist ~ DOUBLE-DEBUT, NO DQ! ~ 'Dragon' Tony Serpe vs. Fusion Dark Prince vs. Cobra Axel Edge vs. Dave Fresco -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=--=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- ...before the card got underway, the Xtremist had these words to say... -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=--=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- The scene cuts up onto the GWCtron as the thousands of packed fans in the arena gaze upon it. The tron turned from a black death colour to a static screen and the fans turning half around, but suddenly it flickers back on again and the front gates of a school were closed shut, with a steel padlock enforcing them. Iron gates, the ones that rust and make the squeaky noise when they are pushed or pulled. The night sky was rotating, turning and moving around, as the moon glistened in the open air and the normal 'safety' face shined through. The darkness swished across the floor and the eeriness wafted down the streets as the abandoned houses creaked as cats jumped on floorboards and bats flew around in the attic. The streets lonely and neglected, not enough people having the pride, nor courage to walk down without some kind of weapon. Not a scene for a school, but a scene for a closed school, a cold abandoned school. The lettering above the Iron gates reading "Park Field Elementary School" and the leaves trapped in the steel letter drop silently just like a fox hunts. The Occasional laughter from the children, the children of the past and the weird whistling of the wind, dwindling in and out of the gate like a weaving needle. The scene that was described in a couple of minutes lasted seconds for the WeW fans as they looked on confused, but yet knowing the showing of The Xtremist was about to occur. And their mind was meeted, when the footsteps of a muscular man walked down the side of the path and walked to the padlocks of the Gate. He wore, an ankle long leather coat. Which shined and glistened in the moon light sky, The Xtremist long brown and blue hair draped down the back of his neck like a snake would on that of a tree. His steady stubby beard, which clearly hadn't been looked at for days had come out of his shell and turned to a full grown goatee. His arms like tree trunks, pacing back and forth as he walked. The fans looked on as The Xtremist held a key in his hand and proceeded to turn it into the padlock, with a grinding crunch the iron gate slung open like that of a flowing dancer on the centre stage. The disturbing atmosphere that it formed, almost death like. The Xtremist swung around and gave off a sinister smile before smashing open the other double door and walking through to the bottom of the road to the bottom where the door, with no window stood. The door of the future, the door of the death, the door that The Xtremist kicked open and the door that flew of its hinges. The Xtremist steadily walked through the pathway before look one side and then the other. He moved to the right of his body and walked in front of the reception desk, where a book filled in dust laid on the desk top. The Xtremist walked towards it before saying in an expressionless voice. The Xtremist: Will Blood Do? *pause* No? Oh Well The Xtremist heinously laughed before walking over to a doorway and opening it before walking in. It was a classroom which was dark and gloomy, the windows where boarded up an the room nearly pitch black. The Xtremist searched the wall for a light switch and pressed it as the light flickered to life and the room lit up with a yellow glow which occasionally flickered on and off. The American flag was popping out the side of the black board, covered in dust and the past. The blackboard was then revealed to the thousands of crammed fans in the arena with "I HATE SIMPLETONS" written in tall high letters across the blackboard and all over the room. The Xtremist walks over to the blackboard before picking up a stick and pointing at the writing on the board. The Xtremist: You see this? This is the writing on the wa.board GWC. And that's what I truly do. I hate them so bad when I clench my fists I don't feel pain, I feel trickles of blood running down my fist and the boiling fury burn up inside of me. *Changing Subjects and clearing Voice* Ut Hum, now children your topic for today will be on History. *pause* Look don't moan at me, that's what its going to be. Now, any questions before we start *long pause* No George, you cant use the fire-hose to squirt the picture of the GWC roster with! *another pause* Very good question, the subject will be in the year 2001, the date wasn't precise but to war hammers locked horns in the great war of Friday Frenzy. There names, the great extreme king, The Xtremist and the schizophrenic asshole Skitzor*pause* Ohhhh.don't be so immature, and you will not tell the headmaster I said that word. Now back to the subject. The two warriors fought in the ring for many hours, it seemed. But in the end.*pause* no...dammit, cheap school don't have another money to pay for repairing books, or get new ones. There's no ending.sooo lets get you to make up the ending.who wants to go first? *pause* Come on, hands up! *pause* Ahh Willy *long pause* YOUR ABSOULUTELY RIGHT! The Xtremist does win the fight and is the European Champion. *pause* that's right Henry. The Xtremist did leave the WeW for bigger things in his career and did win the GWC World Title.oh wait..no.that's the future. *pause* Ohh.that bell goes to early.no no no, don't pack up yet. Repeat after me first.I HATE SIMPLETONS *long pause* And again *long pause* Good Good.now GO and make your dolls of Dracula and stick needles in him! The Xtremist pats imaginary small children on their head and shakes his finger at them as the door of the classroom amazingly opens and closes, opens and closes. At last The Xtremist was left in the class alone and in the shining dim light. The rows of desks in front of him, left and dusty. He walked over towards one of them and sat down, in one of their chairs.. The Xtremist: As the wind blows through the thistle trees and the sun gushes into the meadows, another child is born. Well in my case, the wind smashed through the acorn trees and the sun cascades through the rows upon rows of poisonous plants. When the first baby laughed for the first time, the laugh broke into a thousand pieces and they all went skipping about, and that was the beginning of fairies. And now when every new baby is born its first laugh becomes a fairy. So there ought to be one fairy for every boy or girl. But then again if your name is Sean Chukka AKA The Xtremist they didn't have enough fairies to share out and I landed in the pit of misguidance with no fairy to show the way for a talented piece of human sculpture like me. The child's laughter must have been replaced by the child's cries for their mommy, and the fairies replaced by the mean anger satanic devils that plague the earth like nimrods existence. The plague will end on Titan and a new era will begin an extreme era. I will depart from my enemies.and.my friends.The World isn't big for everybody and soon.if I wait.my time will come to kick the World Champion's jackassed rectum and become the champ.officially. I know I am the champ.but you don't.yet. Watching the peaceful victory of the number one contender-ship for the Regional Championship will remind you of a falling star; one of a million lights in a vast sky that flares up for a brief moment only to disappear into the endless night forever. This Titan I not only face one egotistical b*stard called John 'The Liberator' Steel, who mindlessly thinks he can beat anyone for some tin that I don't need, but I face a so called, the stuck up and corny, not to mention occupanis a lame angle by the name of "Search and Destroy" Dragula. You bought education, I had to learn it off a beggar at the corner of the street once a week, you bought your way into wrestling, I fought my why into wrestling. You live a life, back in those days I just was an existence, but now...I am the Extreme King, and there's one difference between me and you set of freaks...I have ability, and you don't. No I'm not talking about all the ability drugs you buy, I have a special ability that will not be taken away from me, extreme blood runs in my blood vessels! Learned that extreme is only a word...until this coming Monday when I make it reality! Simplicity is for the weak. extremity is for me. The Xtremist stares into the camera as the lights flicker on and off and the words of "I HATE SIMPLETONS" glows in the darkness as the screen fades down...leaving the fans anxious to see what happens at his match on Titan.tune in to find out. -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=--=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- ~ MATCH 1 ~ Axel Edge vs. Dave Fresco -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- MATCH OVERVIEW: ----------------------------- Nothing impressive. Quite a quick bout actually, the newcomer quickly grabbing the advantage. After a view failed offensive attempts, Fresco nailed a big DDT and finished off his opponent, Axel Edge, with the Hawaiian Smash. WINNER: Dave Fresco, via pinfall, at 4:23 ~ MATCH 2 ~ Dark Prince vs. Cobra -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- MATCH OVERVIEW: ------------------------------ Another lackluster match. Both men go back and fourth, not much genuine effort. Cobra comes out on top after a sickening piledriver grounds Dark Prince on the mat. Injury evident, official stops bout. After match concludes, Cobra watches on, concerned, as paramedics load DP onto a gurney and take him away... WINNER: Cobra, via decision, at 3:41 ~ MATCH 3 ~ Tony "The Dragon" Serpe vs. Fusion -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- MATCH OVERVIEW: ----------------------------- Match begins with Serpe over-powering Fusion. John 'The Liberator' Steele comes down to ringside with a folding chair, inciting a huge fan reaction. However, Steele simply unfolded the chair, sat down, and observed the match-up from ring-side. Fusion loses a brief match of mercy, and is sent into the far corner; met with a series of knees to the gut. Fusion stumbles out of the corner into a belly-to-belly release suplex. Serpe climbs up top, calculating...then goes for a flying clothesline which is dodged by Fusion. Now in control, Fusion hits a stiff side-kick followed by a roundhouse which levels Serpe. Fusion goes to the outside, hits a springboard and nails a corkscrew plancha. Fusion exchanges the tide of the match with Serpe several more times, with the crowd reacting amazingly to a failed superplex attempt that sent both men from a STANDING POSITION, crashing down to the concrete arena floor outside the ring. Eventually, Fusion failed to execute a back body drop, which Serpe turned into a quick set-up and execution of the DragonBomb. Afterwards, Steele congratulates both men on their performance with a handshake, and leaves a pair of confused competitors in the ring. WINNER: Tony "The Dragon" Serpe, via pinfall, at 12:47 ~ MATCH 4 ~ ~ TRIPLE THREAT MATCH for REGIONAL TITLE SHOT ~ Dragula vs. John 'The Liberator' Steele vs. The Xtremist -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- MATCH OVERVIEW: ----------------------------- Excitement from the very start. The Xtremist went right for Dragula, leaving Steele by himself. Xtremist focuses most of his energies in the early going on the dismantling of Dragula. Following a vicious leg lariat, Xtremist sent Dragula into the ropes, springboarded of the set of ropes nearest to him and executed a hurricarana that sent Dragula sailing to the outside of the ring. Before John Steele could get his paws on The Xtremist, he was flying to the outside, nailing a suicide senton bomb. Both men were laid out on the canvas and John Steele simply stood in the ring, laughing! The Xtremist drags his body along with Dragula's into the ring...Steele stomps him as he rolls in, and then sends Dragula out of the ring with a gorilla press!!! Dragula is mangled on the outside, and Steele gets a near fall on the battered Xtremist. Frustrated, Steele sets up a seemingly destroyed Xtremist for a powerbomb... XTREMIST TURNS THIS SET-UP INTO A SMALL PACKAGE AND _STEALS_ A WIN! Steele is shocked and cannot believe what just happened! The Xtremist taunts the Liberator, who doesn't take long to react. After he digests it, he yanks Xtremist to his feet, nailing a TRIO, THAT'S THREE! consecutive Liberty Backbreakers. Steele raises his hands in victory, and leaves the ring to a deafening fan reaction. WINNER: The Xtremist, via pinfall, 20:15 ~ MATCH 5 ~ $$ Main evenT $$ ~ Regional Title Match! ~ Joe Six-pack (C) vs. Dementor -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- MATCH OVERVIEW: ----------------------------- Surprisingly uneventful for a title defense. Dementor puts up quite an attempt at the title, nearly taking it on several occasions. Six-pack shows why he is champion however, quelling most attempts easily, others with effort. Dementor fell victim to what has become Joe's signature flurry of punches and kicks dubbed by fans as the "Southern Fury" and also got flattened by a HUGE splash given by the champion. Dementor returned offense by taking out the yellow teeth of the champion with a gigantic booth, followed by a cradle piledriver. Dementor followed up with a second rope legdrop and a double arm DDT for a near-fall. Dementor was then distracted by the presence of the newly named number one contender for the Regional Championship, John 'The Liberator' Steele. He mimicked his previous actions, simply coming to ringside and observing the match. Six-pack stole the win out from under the distracted Dementor's nose, when the champ rolled up Dementor with a fistful of tights for the pinfall. Steele simply laughed, picked up his chair and walked off. Dementor was furious, and began brawling with the champion. Officials flooded the ring and several were actually hit by chairs wielded by both men. Dementor was then seen, chair in hand, seemingly going Steele hunting. Six-pack celebrates win. WINNER: Joe Six-pack, via pinfall, 17:11 ~ STILL GWC Regional Champion! ~ -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=--=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- [name withheld] Chairman, Grand Wrestling Corp. - http://www.geocities.com/gwcwrestling2000 <><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><> "Dangerous" William Devonshire (IWF/WOW, MLW) <><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><> [detail withheld] "Would you care for a CHOCOLATE COVERED PRETZEL?" - Brody, Mallrats (<--- tied for best movie in history w/ Ferris Bueller's Day Off)
