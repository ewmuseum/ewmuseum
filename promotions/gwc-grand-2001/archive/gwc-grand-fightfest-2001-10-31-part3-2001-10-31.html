GWC (Grand, 2001) — Card

GWC FightFest

31 October 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Part 3 of 5] [...the scene before you is one of a jam-packed Joe Louis Arena in the heart of Detroit, Michigan. What seems to be a capacity crowd roars as an aerial shot takes in the jubilant spectators. Various signs read "Messiah is my SAVIOR!", "Red Rum is GAWD!" and "Steele a win, Steele a prize!". The scene then shifts to the announcers booth. Monkey Boy sits scratching his head while Winchester Smith shuffles an assortment of papers...] Winchester Smith: Welcome back ladies and gents, to the first EVER GWC pay-per-view EXTRAVAGANZA, FIGHTFEST! We are well underway here in the Motor City! Monkey Boy: You said it PAWW! [grin] We just finished watching what was nothing less than a simple brawl...which turned out to be between all the promising newcomers in the GWC! Winchester Smith: That it did, chap. We had not ONLY Tony "The Dragon" Serpe, Fusion and Dave Fresco battling it out, but at the end we saw BaiN, "Prime-Time" Andre Harris AND John 'The Liberator' Steele involved! To bad it ended in a no contest, however it was an entertaining contest none the less! Monkey Boy: Well...moving on here...we get to see the THIRST FOR GOLD! DUN-DA-DUM!!! Joe Silly- Hillbilly takes on The Xtremist! This guy I like, Xtremist is a crazy mo' fo', putting his body on the line to destroy his opponents! Now THAT'S, entertainment. Winchester Smith: Not to mention a vicious strap match in which John 'The Liberator' Steele DECIMATED the Dementor! What a confrontation that was! Monkey Boy: Yeah...after the beating he took, I don't think we'll be seeing much of the Dementor in the future. Winchester Smith: Yeeah. Okay then. Sooo...to the RING! [in ring] Shizza Hotee: Ladies....and GENTS! [smile] It is my PLEASURE to introduce to you...the man who will challenge for the GWC REGIONAL CHAMPIONSHIP right here TONIGHT! [crowd pop] Shizza Hotee: He is the KING OF XTREME...he stands 6 feet 4 inches and weighs in at 246 pounds... HE IS.... ! ! ! T H E X T R E M I S T ! ! ! [The Xtremist makes his way to the ring wearing short-tights/y-fronts with X's on the front and Xtreme on the back in blood red writing. He wears black boots with black knee and elbow padding. As he walks, water drips from his wet hair, never shifting his focus from the ring...] Shizza Hotee: And now...the GRAND WRESTLING CORPORATION REGIONAL CHAMPION...he stands 6 feet 3 inches and weighs in at 257 pounds...HE IS... ! ! ! J O E S I X - P A C K ! ! ! [With his greasy hair and yellow teeth, Joe wobbles to the ring in his signature Richard Petty racing tee, stained blue jeans, and cowboy boots and hat. He slaps a goofy grin on his face while dragging the GWC Regional Championship in his right hand...almost seemingly oblivious to the fact that he carries it with him...] [bell tolls] Winchester Smith: This one is underway and the Xtremist is wasting NO time in starting his assault! He charges Joe...RAISES HIM UP LIKE A TACKLING DUMMY... Monkey Boy:...AND _PLANTS_ HIM WITH A VARIATION ON A SPINEBUSTER! GOOD LORDY! Winchester Smith: The explosiveness of the Xtremist is illustrated in the VERY early going here. He climbs casually to the top buckle now...WHAT THE- WOW! Monkey Boy: What in the _HELL_ was THAT!?! Winchester Smith: I've been in this business my entire life and I've never seen a thing like it! It seemed to be some insane variation of a corkscrew plancha! That was incredible! Monkey Boy: Joe Six-pack doesn't think so! He's writhing on the canvas, screaming in pain!!! And look and the cold, calculating look on the mug of the Xtemist! I would NOT wanna be across the ring from that! Winchester Smith: I concur sir! The man seems to have no emotion, he's just destroying Six-pack! What a series of vicious kicks now! He's stomping a hole right through the champion! My gosh! This is amazing! Monkey Boy: Xtremist now...sends Joe to the ropes now...SCOOP...CRADLE PILEDRIVER! WOW! He got AIR on that! Six-pack IS OUT LIKE A LIGHT! Xtremist quick to grab him back up now... Winchester Smith: Xtremist now...UNDERHOOK OF THE ARMS...NEW DISEASE!!! IT'S OVER!!! Monkey Boy: WTF! He's not COVERING HIM! He's got the freaking title won! PIN HIM! Winchester Smith: No...he's...he's...XTRERMINATED! THIS MATCH HAS BEEN A COMPLETE OBLITERATION OF JOE SIX-PACK, THE- ...ONE... . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . ...TWO!... . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . ...THREE!!! Winchester Smith: FORMER GWC REGIONAL CHAMPION! MY GOD! XTREMIST PULLED IT OFF! [bell tolls] Monkey Boy: Wow! Not shabby for your SECOND MATCH! GEEZE! XTREMIST DOMINATED THIS MATCH AND TAKES HOME GOLD! Winchester Smith: An AMAZING showing by the Xtremist who is your NEW GWC REGIONAL CHAMPION! Shizza Hotee: Your winner via pinfall..AND NEW GRAND WRESTLING CORPORATION REGIONAL CHAMPION... ! ! ! T H E X T R E M I S T ! ! ! Monkey Boy: WELL! There you have it, gold changes hands at FightFest! Winchester Smith: But will it again!?! Stay tuned for the defense and possible CULMINATION of the GWC's foremost feud...MESSIAH CHALLENGED AJ NIN RED RUM FOR THE TOP PRIZE WHEN WE RETURN! [camera flips to the ring, where Joe Six-pack lies in between consciousness and not, moving sporadically. The Xtremist simply snatches the Regional title belt from the official, rolls out of the ring and walks down the aisle; disappearing through the black curtains...]
