GWC Titans - Tuesday, November 27th, 2001

27 November 2001
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o Grand Wrestling Corporation presents... ~ GWC TITANS - Tuesday, November 27th, 2001 ~ o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o Tonight's Titans will showcase the talents of... [ MAIN EVENT ] [ TAG - TEAM GRUDGE MATCH ] John "The Liberator" Steele/The Xtremist vs. The Messiah (HC)/Little Blue Super Jew (RC) [ SINGLES MATCH ] "The Gorilla" Chris Hoff vs. Tony "The Dragon" Serpe [ SINGLES MATCH ] Ronnie "The Wolf" Black vs. George De Los Santos [ THREE WAY THREAT ] Asylum vs. Scottie Rayda vs. Calypso [ SINGLES MATCH ] Alex Deadly vs. BaiN [...screen reveals darkness with an unusual metallic-like shine to it. Slowly, it tilts, with the lighting providing the shine pouring over certain sections revealing assorted letters and designs. Then suddenly, a flash of light reveals the GWC logo and then disappears. This screen dissolves to the interior of a studio-like setting. There is a desk with the GWC Titans logo on the front, constructing of steel mesh and textured plating. Two microphones are perched on small stands, sitting behind them are two recognizable faces. Monkey Boy scratches his temple, the stupefied look plastered seemingly permanently on his face. The other chair is occupied by Winchester Smith, who characteristically shuffles papers, then puts on his smile after noticing the camera...] Winchester Smith: WELCOME everyone to the NOVEMBER 27TH, 2001 edition of GWC TITANS! Monkey Boy: The only show on prime-time television where you get to stare at someone as sexy as me for FREE! WHAT A DEAL! Winchester Smith: Someone alert an EMT, this man is HILUCINATING! Monkey Boy: NO... you just THINK you are because you get to sit in presence of such SEXINESS! Winchester Smith: We will start off with some pre-recorded comments from our current Heavyweight Champion... Monkey Boy: Not after Xtremist gets him next week! Winchester Smith:...The Messiah. [Messiah is seen sitting in his locker room on a talking on a cell phone...] Messiah: No! Man, I am telling you. It is the perfect time.---It will be great! No one will see it coming.---I assure you Little Blue Super Jew is cool with it.---We have waited this long, and finally, we are gonna make the sports entertainment world stand up and take notice of us again.---Between all of us, we are the future of this business.---I promise! NO ONE will see it coming. [Messiah folds up the cell and smirks...] Winchester Smith: Well, that was interesting to say the least! Messiah may have an ace in the hold for tonight's main event! Monkey Boy: Maybe its...THE GORILLA! HAHAHAHAHAHAHA....AHHHHAHAHAHAHA... Winchester Smith: Good Lordy...Hey! I have an idea! Monkey Boy: Oh great...another one of these... Winchester Smith: Let's view some wrestling...maybe CALL it...heh? Monkey Boy: What's the catch? Winchester Smith: Sheesh...first match...Deadly and BaiN...roll it... [ SINGLES MATCH ] Alex Deadly vs. BaiN MATCH OVERVIEW-- Match starts with Deadly overpowering the foolish Bain. Bain attempts to body slam the larger Deadly after some stiff punches and falls victim to a powerslam. Deadly then goes to work with a display of his power and exploitation of his power. An incredible powerbomb gave way to a second rope elbow drop and a stomping fit. Bain nearly lost it after a piledriver narrowly escaped. Bain then ducked a dropkick attempt by Deadly and Bain had the upper hand for the first time. Bain pancaked Deadly in the corner with a big splash then used a drop toe hold to set-up for a 747 splash. A near fall seemed to anger Deadly who stole the tide of the match away with a HUGE boot to the face, then a wicked scoop slam. The Deadly Sin driver was hit very quickly, surprising the big man and putting Bain down for three. WINNER-- Alex Deadly, via pinfall, at 11:08 Winchester Smith: Wow! Great debut by the new GWCer, Alex Deadly! Some may even call that an upset! Monkey Boy: Surprised the hell out of me! I sure didn't expect the youngster to take out the monster, but it just happened right in front of our eyes! Winchester Smith: Alex Deadly will surely be a force in the future that should be watched. Things just keep heating up here in the GWC! Monkey Boy: No, it's just that time of the month for you Winchester. I thought I told you to not mention your personal problems on the air...[sigh]... Winchester Smith: Pleeeeaaassseee. We got Mr. Depends Spokesperson over here [points at Monkey Boy] cracking jokes like that. Anyway...next up we have a triple threat match involving Scottie Rayda, Calypso, and a man who has been ruffling the feathers of the GWC heavyweight champion without any actual action as of late, ASYLUM. Monkey Boy: Well we know they have a past, that is about it. Should be exciting... Winchester Smith: TO THE RING! [ THREE WAY THREAT ] Asylum vs. Scottie Rayda vs. Calypso MATCH OVERVIEW-- Asylum came to the ring and DOMINATED the competition. Calypso came at Asylum first and after he ducked a clothesline attempt, Asylum nailed a kick to the gut and set-up for a Straight Jacket Tiger Bomb. Rayda the foolishly jumped in the ring and Asylum dropped Calypso to the mat. Rayda got LEVELED with a vicious kick to the face and then got dragged into the set-up, then PLANTED by a Straight Jacket Tiger Bomb and pinned. Asylum proceeded to set-up Calypso, yelled "YOU'RE NEXT MESSIAH" then planted Calypso WITH AUTHORITY to the canvas. WINNER-- Asylum, via pinfall, at 4:22 Winchester Smith: Well I wouldn't want to be in Messiah's shoes right about now if that man performs like that all the time! Monkey Boy: You're looking at his new biggest fan! He just demolished both men with EASE! That was incredible! Winchester Smith: That it was. Well, Asylum is clearly after Messiah and he knows it. Now we move on to two of the hottest talents in the GWC going head-to-head here tonight. Monkey Boy: THE GORILLA! HAHAHAHA... Winchester Smith: ...Ronnie "The Wolf" Black and George De Los Santos! Monkey Boy: Oh...heh...my bad... [ SINGLES MATCH ] Ronnie "The Wolf" Black vs. George De Los Santos MATCH OVERVIEW-- Black went to work with a quick arm drag then a big back body drop. Santos came up quickly and nailed a high knee and began his assault with a flurry of hard punches. Warned about the fists by the official, Santos dragged Black to his feet and sent him to the ropes quickly and nailed a flying clothesline. Black went down but was up rather quickly then nailed an elbow to the face of Santos. Santos went down and black hit a big leg drop and got a two count. Then, a high paced fight that went back and forth ensued. Neither man could decisively get an upper hand, simply quick blows, fast moves, rapid reversals. This lead to a Wolf Bite out of NO WHERE on Santos! Black was barely able to crawl over and make the cover. Santos got his leg on the rope AFTER the three count, and Black got the win. However the action was not over. "The Gorilla" Chris Hoff made his way to the ring and began yelling things at the fallen Santos. When Ronnie "The Wolf" Black got to his feet, he told Hoff to leave. Hoff grabbed a chair and entered the ring. Black lunged at Hoff who side stepped the still groggy Black and sent him to the outside. Then the attack began. George De Los Santos was viciously attacked with a chair, Hoff focusing mainly on the right leg of the ring rookie Santos. Hoff then partially unfolded the chair and slid Santos's leg inside it. A middle rope leg drop onto the chair sent Santos into convulsions on the canvas as he shrieked in pain. Paramedics and trainers stormed the ring as George De Los Santos's mangled leg remained pinned in between the chair. Hoff wouldn't allow anyone in the ring until Ronnie "The Wolf" Black dove in the ring and began destroying Hoff! The two men brawled all over the ring until a swarm of security separated the two stars. EMT's then got to Santos and removed the chair, helping the fallen superstar onto a stretcher and back to a waiting ambulance... WINNER-- Ronnie "The Wolf" Black, via pinfall, at 10:38 Winchester Smith: Well...I...I don't know quite what to say. That was a deplorable scene. Chris Hoff may have just ended the career of the young George De Los Santos right in the middle of that ring tonight. Monkey Boy: All joking aside, that is something no one ever wants to see. Winchester Smith: We'll be praying for a George De Los Santos recovery and you can bet that Ronnie "The Wolf" Black and "The Gorilla" Chris Hoff have NOT seen the last of each other. Not by a LONG shot. Monkey Boy: Well...the show must go on. Up next... Winchester Smith: An interview with the Heavyweight Champion...The Messiah... **Messiah walks down to the ring. No intro, no pyro, no nothing. Simply walks in the ring and points to the camera** Messiah: I told you! I told everyone that I was gonna shock the sports entertainment world last week. And what happened?! Little Blue Super Jew, was the first to join what will be the defining force behind the GWC. That's right! The Dark Project is underway. We are gonna change the wrestling world and shape it in MY image. Now on to tonight's business. I am not even going to discuss the main event. Everyone knows who is gonna come out on top. But there is someone in the back, in the depths of the boiler room or somewhere...and that person is ASYLUM. Who the hell do you think you are? You step back into my world? Last time I saw you, we were tag team champs of a little federation called the CWFpro...and you remember what you did? Let me remind you "big man". You walked out. Who defended the titles when you weren't there?? Me!! That's right...I did. They tried to force me into other combinations but I always had a little bit of hope that you would come back and we would continue our reign. But you know what...you never did come back. And now I have moved up the ladder and become the number one man in this industry and who shows up?? Asylum? You have no place in MY federation. You come out here and start talking about the past and what was...well let me inform you of a little something....that was indeed.....THE PAST. WE ARE LIVING IN THE NOW! And I am the GWC heavyweight champion, and you are no where near my league. You say you want me, well I never back down from a challenge....never! But there are far better competitors in this federation that I can defend against than you. Just remember, the past is over. Long gone! And since we are on the subject of bringing up the past, I just wanna mention two things...VFCW and the OWF!!! VFCW! How many matches did you win Asylum? **begins to mock counting on his hand** Better still, how many did you lose?? Cause I know you might have won, one single match in all your time of VFCW. And if I remember correctly that was due to a friend of mine losing so you didn't cry to the owners of the VFCW or run down south with the rest of those dinosaurs. Now OWF? Remember. I have it on good authority that you were "BUDDIES" with the owner. Pretty said really...if you wanted it...you got it fed to you on a silver platter. And my question is Asylum, what kinda "FAVORS" did you do for the OWF prez? **crowd laughs** Back to the present, this is a whole different arena, with a whole new set of fans...and I am gonna tell you this ASS-I-LAM, YOU GOT AN ITCH, I'LL MAKE YOU MY BITCH...CAUSE YOUR PAIN....... **holds the mic to the crowd** fans: IS MY GAIN!!!!!!!!!!!!! Messiah: You Mother(censored)ing right!!!! **Messiah walks away from the ring throwing the mic down and receiving a mixed reaction from the crowd** Winchester Smith: We'll get right into our next match...Hoff vs. Serpe... [ SINGLES MATCH ] "The Gorilla" Chris Hoff vs. Tony "The Dragon" Serpe MATCH OVERVIEW-- Tony Serpe began things by quickly focusing on the limbs of Hoff. He nails a drop kick to the right knee of Hoff and began a lengthy assault that seemed futile. Hoff kept squashing the Dragon's attempts at chopping him down. The Gorilla stole the momentum of his opponent with a tilt-a-whirl backbreaker that seemed to open up the flood gates of Hoff's new found viciousness. He nailed a scoop piledriver for a near fall. This lead to Hoff nailing a powerbomb for another near fall. Frustrated by his inability to pin The Dragon, Hoff executed a Gorilla Press Slam to the outside, sending The Dragon into the guard-rails sternum first. A sickening crack put The Dragon on his back, blood dripping from his mouth. Hoff just laughed and climbed to the outside where another Gorilla Press Slam, this time on the pavement put The Dragon out cold. Hoff rolled Serpe back into the ring and made the pin. Afterwards, he went out of the ring to retrieve a chair but was stopped cold in his tracks when Ronnie "The Wolf" Black charged Hoff and another brawl began. After Black nailing a Wolf Bite and Hoff Gorilla Pressing Black through the announce table, security forces swarmed the area once again and physically restrained both men as Serpe was tended to by medics. WINNER-- "The Gorilla" Chris Hoff, via pinfall, at 14:02 Winchester Smith: Well, that animal Chris Hoff may have injured yet another GWC super star with his mean streak that has reared its ugly head for the second time this evening. Monkey Boy: And I LIKE IT! The..heh...the...hehehe...GORILLA! HAHAHAHA.... Winchester Smith: You just can't get over that can you. You don't see me over here falling out of my chair over your ridiculous name. One which is CLOSELY RELATED to the one you have been finding so funny. Monkey Boy: But...it's just so...GORILLA! AHHHHHHAHAHAHAHAHAHA... Winchester Smith: Wow. I can actually feel the points disappearing from my I.Q. just by occupying this chair... Monkey Boy: Annnnd THE TIME HAS COME! The big tag team main event is upon us! Winchester Smith: Whoa! Monkey Boy does his job! Where's those Ripley's Believe it or Not people when ya need 'em! [grins] It'll be interesting enough to just see all these me in the ring at one time. Who KNOWS what will happen! Monkey Boy: CARNAGE! WHOPEEE! [ MAIN EVENT ] [ TAG - TEAM GRUDGE MATCH ] John "The Liberator" Steele/The Xtremist vs. The Messiah (HC)/Little Blue Super Jew (RC) MATCH OVERVIEW-- Surprisingly enough, it started off innocently enough. Some decent tag team wrestling shocked the crowd as Steele and The Xtremist functioned somewhat as a team but were mostly victimized by the fluidness of the tag team flow between the Dark Project stablemates. With LBSJ and The Xtremist in the ring destroying each other, the mayhem began. First a man who was unknown to all at first appeared from beneath the ring and wrapped his arm around the leg of the heavyweight champion. A momentary look of shock was all Messiah had time for as Asylum yanked him from the mat and began destroying him on the outside. He sent Messiah first into what became a tangled pile of flesh and ring steps and then just stomped what many thought may become a hole in the chest of the champion. Minutes into the vicious attack a fan leapt over the guard rail and began assaulting Asylum! Messiah still lie on the floor in God-awful shape as these two men brawled on the outside. A crowd pop alerted all that "The Gorilla" Chris Hoff was making his way down the ramp! All but John "The Liberator" Steele. Steele got the message when Hoff turned him around on the apron and executed a Gorilla Press Slam onto the floor. The fighting between these two men remained on the outside and remained bitter. Chaos spread, with Xtremist obliterating LBSJ on the inside of the ring, The Messiah, Asylum, and our crazed fan brawling on the outside of one side of the ring while Chris Hoff was delivering one hell of a beating to John Steele on the outside of the other side of the ring! If that wasn't enough to quench your carnage withdrawal, Ronnie "The Wolf" Black charges Hoff!!! The Wolf saves Steele's hide and now is exchanging blows with Hoff on the outside of the ring. A battered Liberator slides back into the ring to recover and the crowd literally EXPLODES at what they see next. DAVID STEELE, the brother of JOHN STEELE is making his way down the ramp!!!!! John Steele turns to see what the fuss is about and freezes in mid-step. Shock fills his face, anger his eyes, his face cluttered with too many things to list. The match ends with the fast paced mayhem surrounding the frozen Steele brothers simply staring each other in the eyes. Winchester Smith: I DON'T BELIEVE IT! DAVID STEELE IN THE GWC! CHAOS IN THE MAIN EVENT! TOO MUCH HAS HAPPENED TO EVEN BEGIN TO DIGEST! THE MAYHEM CONTINUES NEXT WEEK ON TITANS! MY GOD! MY GOD!
