GWC Titans - Tuesday, November 6th, 2001

6 November 2001
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o Grand Wrestling Corporation presents... ~ GWC TITANS - Tuesday, November 6th, 2001 ~ o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o TONIGHT'S LINE-UP: ================= :::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::: GWC TITANS - 11.06.01 (Tuesday) Deadline Strats & RP's: Tuesday before 10 pm EST :::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::: $$ MAIN EVENT $$ ~ Non-title Triple Threat ~ The Messiah (HC) vs. John "The Liberator" Steele vs. Little Blue Super Jew :::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::: ::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::: -- The newly crowned GWC Heavyweight Champion goes right into a match (though non-title) against a man who has quickly made it known that he is out to win, John "The Liberator" Steele. Also, the return to the ring of LITTLE BLUE SUPER JEW who is quite obviously eyeing the gold around Messiah's waist as well! Should be nothing less than EXPLOSIVE! ~ Regional Title Re-Match, No Holds Barred ~ The Xtremist (RC) vs. Joe Six-pack :::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::: :::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::: -- The Xtremist snatches Regional Championship at FightFest in only his SECOND MATCH, and looks to successfully defend it for the first time against the man he won it from, Joe Six-pack! This time around however, there are NO HOLDS BARRED! Should be interesting to say the least... ~ Winner Receives Regional Title Shot ~ Dave Fresco vs. Tony "The Dragon" Serpe :::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::: ::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::: -- Two promising newcomers look to get a shot at the Regional strap! Dave Fresco challenges Tony "The Dragon" Serpe in match which gained fuel in the fiery three way at FightFest! ~ No DQ, No Count-Out ~ BaiN vs. "Prime-Time" Andre Harris :::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::: ::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::: -- Two brand new faces in the GWC made their presence known at the PPV, FightFest, AND look to rise in the rankings! They challenge each other to a match, rather than the fist fight they had at Fight Fest in a match where ANYTHING GOES! :::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::: ::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::: The NEW GWC HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPION, The Messiah had these words before the show got underway... :::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::: ::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::: **After the pyrotechnics go off as GWC Titans begin, new music hits the PA. Darkside by Nitocris plays. The crowd is quiet in anticipation of who's new entrance theme this was. The Videotron displays a cross and the word Messiah appears. The crowd surprisingly begins to cheer with very little booing to be heard. The music stops and the arena goes dark and on a beat the lights come back on after a series of explosions, with Messiah standing in the ring and lasers shooting all around. He stands with his hands outstretched and the belt draped over his left shoulder, and a mic in the right hand. The crowd explodes with excitement. The music begins to lower as Messiah smiles at the crowd** Messiah: I told everyone I would regain the GWC Heavyweight title. I told Carson and look what happened to him. Please understand, that all I have done has been part of a master plan that is just now beginning to unfold here in the GWC. Now, first line of business is tonight's main event. Me, Messiah will take on The Liberator (mixed reaction) and Lil' Blue Super Jew(cheers) in a non-title match. Everybody is talking, what is going on with these two. What does LBSJ want with Messiah? And what does The Liberator want with Messiah? Well let me clear it all up for you. See this on my shoulder. This is the GWC Heavyweight Title and I haven't held it just one time, but TWO TIME, TWO TIME,...oops wrong gimmick. I have held this title twice in the past month. And isn't it ironic that LBSJ came down and slammed the door on the head of the former, and I stress former champion Aj Nin Red Rum. Now I am not saying that me and LBSJ are on the same page or anything but.... (smirks) ...well on to new business. Aj Nin, as far as I am concerned, you do not get any more shots at my title unless you work your way back up the ladder my friend. I must give you credit. You gave me one helluva fight at the PPV, but unfortunately you were not any good compared to the likes of me. So good luck on your fight back to the top, I am sure I will be here waiting just to put you back on the bottom again one day. And finally, this goes out to all you in the audience and at home. I change is coming real soon in this federation. Now you ask yourselves what does that mean? Soon....very, very soon...I am gonna unleash a 'dark project' on the GWC. And when it is all said and done, Messiah will not be the only one. Liberator ....LBSJ, you got an itch, I'll make you my bitch. Cause your pain.........is my....(points mic to the crowd) Audience: GAIN!!!!!!!! **New music hits again, as Messiah steps out of the ring and waves the belt infront of a child and smiles raising the childs arm as if in victory. The crowd gives him a very unusual positive response as he leaves** [...screen reveals darkness with an unusual metallic-like shine to it. Slowly, it tilts, with the lighting providing the shine pouring over certain sections revealing assorted letters and designs. Then suddenly, a flash of light reveals the GWC logo and then disappears. This screen dissolves to the interior of a studio-like setting. There is a desk with the GWC Titans logo on the front, constructing of steel mesh and textured plating. Two microphones are perched on small stands, sitting behind them are two recognizable faces. Monkey Boy scratches his temple, the stupefied look plastered seemingly permanently on his face. The other chair is occupied by Winchester Smith, who characteristically shuffles papers, then puts on his smile after noticing the camera...] Winchester Smith: WELCOME everyone to the NOVEMBER 6TH, 2001 edition of GWC TITANS! Monkey Boy: WHOO! Have no fear, the sexiest PRIMATE alive is here! Winchester Smith: Yes, unfortunate news it is. Regardless, we're here to provide a splash of color to your Tuesday evenings, which we truly hope you enjoy. You all saw the line-up several moments ago- Monkey Boy: AND we will start things off with a few newcomers beating the living HELL out of each other in a NO DQ, NO COUNTOUT bout! MY KINDA MATCH! (grins widely) Winchester Smith: You interrupt me again, you'll swallow those teeth son. Monkey Boy: (stops grinning) Winchester Smith: Sooo...here we go! BaiN and "Prime Time" Andre Harris battle it out! These to came to blows at FightFest last week and come to finish each other off on Titans! Monkey Boy: Should be colorful...I'm hoping for red! Winchester Smith: Here's what transpired... o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o ~ No DQ, No Countout ~ BaiN vs. "Prime-Time" Andre Harris o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o MATCH OVERLOOK: BaiN comes into the match strong, overpowering Harris from the start. A series of vicious kicks and punches dazed Harris, making way for BaiN to trap Harris in the corner for a some knees and kicks. BaiN then sent Andre to the other buckle with a HARD Irish whip and charged for a big splash. This he missed, Harris narrowly escaping. Andre quickly clipped out the staggered BaiN's knee then finished the take down w/ a dropkick to head. Harris stayed on the offensive with a perfectly executed moonsault from the top and a nearfall. BaiN later countered a cross body block by catching Harris in mid-air, then sending him to the outside w/ a gorilla press! BaiN waited for Harris to stir, then the 300+ pounder hit a SUICIDE DIVE!!! Both men were chewing on the ringside mats for several moments. Harris was the first up, and used a vicious chair shot to put BaiN back out. Harris then rolled BaiN back into the ring and brought the chair w/ him. He laid it on BaiN and again went up top. Harris went for a shooting star press that he hit...though BaiN was no longer underneath the chair he landed on. BaiN then rolled back on Harris to score the pinfall and the win. WINNER: BaiN, via pinfall, at 12:10 Winchester Smith: Well! Newcomer BaiN pulls out his first victory against "Prime Time" Andre Harris right here on Titans! Monkey Boy: I wouldn't count that Harris out either! He's got style! Winchester Smith: Both men are certainly impressive competitors, the gold bearers of the GWC should definitely keep an eye on them as well as others. Monkey Boy: Err...yeah, sure. WHAT'S NEXT OLD FART! Winchester Smith: Two relatively new superstars to the GWC are becoming increasingly popular with our fans here. Dave Fresco and Tony "The Dragon" Serpe will battle it out for a shot at the GWC Regional Championship! This match will occur next week on Titans, and will be an EXCELLENT opportunity for whomever gets the chance! Monkey Boy: Yeah! And we get to watch them get massacred by The Xtremist! YAHOO BABY! o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o ~ Winner Receives Regional Title Shot ~ Dave Fresco vs. Tony "The Dragon" Serpe o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o MATCH OVERLOOK: A simple lock-up started off the match with the massive Fresco driving Serpe back to the ropes. The lock-up was broken by a kidney punch from Serpe, which shocked Fresco temporarily. When Serpe attempted to advance, he was leveled by a boot to the chin from Fresco. Dave dragged Serpe up quickly, and sent him into the ropes for a tilt-a-whirl backbreaker and a quick pin attempt. Serpe kicked out at two, and Fresco wasted no time. A hard offensive left Serpe in trouble. Serpe finally ducked a clothesline attempt by Fresco and came back with a dropkick to the knee. Fresco went down on one knee, and Serpe quickly got to Fresco's ailing limb and locked in an ankle lock submission. After screaming in pain initially, Fresco quickly got angry and powered out by shoving Serpe off his leg and right out of the ring. This gave Fresco time to rest his leg he was favoring, and upon Serpe's return to the ring, Fresco hit a kick to the abdominal area and a hard powerbomb! This lead to a near-fall, then another Fresco offensive. Serpe came back with an unexpected spring board elbow to Fresco's face. Serpe foolishly attempted a German suplex on the much larger and heavier Fresco, and though he nearly did it, Fresco used his size to botch the move and get another near-fall on Serpe. Serpe, frustrated, charged Fresco who met him with a Hawaiian Smash and a three-count. WINNER: Dave Fresco, via pinfall, at 14:01 Monkey Boy: Now this guy I like. He's massive, dangerous, and takes NO prisoners. Winchester Smith: Fresco is definitely a talent to keep an eye on as well, and he just earned himself a shot at THE GWC REGIONAL CHAMPIONSHIP NEXT WEEK ON TITANS! Monkey Boy: As impressive as he looked here tonight, I don't know if he can topple The Xtremist. That guy is a mean SOB. No kidding there. Winchester Smith: I have to agree with you there. The Xtremist doesn't mess around in the ring, he steps in it and gets things done. SPEAKING of which, we get to see him do just that! However, he faces stiff opposition in the former champion, Joe Six-pack! Monkey Boy: Yeah...drunk stiff. o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o ~ Regional Title Re-Match, No Holds Barred ~ The Xtremist (RC) vs. Joe Six-pack o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o MATCH OVERLOOK: Surprisingly unimpressive match. Six-pack came out with a bit more tenacity that their previous than their previous encounter, but it was pretty much all Xtremist. He put on a devastating offense through the entire contest that, though he tried, Six-pack simply could not compete with. The match ended rather quickly and abruptly when Six-pack attempted to rise from a clothesline and rose up right into an Xtermination. WINNER: The Xtremist, via pinfall, at 6:02 (STILL CHAMPION!) Winchester Smith: In the usual style, The Xtremist SQUASHED the competition. Six-pack looked far better than he did at FightFest, but he was not nearly at the level he was when he captured the championship. Monkey Boy: He's a DRUNKARD! Good freaking luck ever getting gold again you SCHMUCK! Your drunken, wild swings won't work against a man as cold and calculating as The Xtremist. BUFFOON! Winchester Smith: Well...I think we have an Xtremist fan in the audience. And now folks, we move onto the main event. This should be an interesting contest. It is a non-title bout in which the GWC Heavyweight Champion in The Messiah goes up against the man who secured the title win for him at FightFest, Little Blue Super Jew as well as the fastest rising star in the GWC, John "The Liberator" Steele. Monkey Boy: This actually does seem...well...interesting. Lets take a look... o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0oo0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o $$ MAIN EVENT $$ ~ Non-title Triple Threat ~ The Messiah (HC) vs. John "The Liberator" Steele vs. Little Blue Super Jew o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0oo0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o0o MATCH OVERLOOK: Incredible bout from the get-go. First, Steele seemed to make an attempt to not fight in the bout. He exchanged words with LBSJ that seemed to anger him, then Steele turned to walk away. LBSJ instantly pounced on him, a kick to the back followed by a dragon sleeper. Messiah sat back in the corner, laughing! LBSJ was forced to release the hold when Steele got a foot on the ropes. LBSJ was up quickly and began stomping the hell out of Steele. This prompted Messiah to get into the bout. After LBSJ assault of stomps and an elbow drop was complete, Messiah came from behind and executed a release German suplex and an elbow of his own. Messiah followed up with an armbar submission. By this time, John Steele was back on his feet and slid out of the ring. He made his way over to wear Messiah lay, wrenching on LBSJ's arm. Steele dragged Messiah's head under the bottom rope WHILE HE STILL HELD ONTO LBSJ'S ARM!...and Steele jumps onto the ropes, sending the ropes with his weight on it down onto Messiah's throat! Messiah went berserk, grabbing his throat and releasing a hurting LBSJ. Steele went up top, and nailed LBSJ with a top rope clothesline as he rose. Steele then went after Messiah, who was still having trouble with his throat. Steele came up from behind and locked in a Tazmission-style sleeper hold. Messiah is in trouble, and is fading fast Steele rolls out of the ring quickly, grabbing a chair out of reflex. He rolls back in just as fast, raises the chair behind LBSJ who is unaware of his presence and...DOESN'T SWING!?! STEELE DROPS THE CHAIR ON THE MAT!!! LBSJ wrenches on the hold and Messiah...PASSES OUT! The official checks his condition and after raising his arm...ONCE...TWICE...THAT'S IT! WINNER: Little Blue Super Jew, via referee submission, 17:34 Winchester Smith: WELL! That was an UNUSUAL act by John "The Liberator" Steele!!! He could have easily broken that hold, yet HE CHOSE NOT TO! I would LOVE to know what is going on inside that man's mind! Monkey Boy: OBVIOUSLY NOT MUCH! He could've broken the hold, and with the way he was performing in that ring, coulda quite possibly took the win! FOOL! Winchester Smith: I have a feeling that is a man with a plan. We'll just have to see. For my curiously furry side-kick, this is Winchester Smith saying goodnight, and we'll see you next week for another edition of GWC TITANS! Monkey Boy: LATER GATOR! (stupid grin) [The camera fades out to a compilation of clips from nights matches. The final one is of Steele pausing with the chair, then dropping it.
