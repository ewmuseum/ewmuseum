GWC (Grand, 2001) — Card

GWC FightFest

31 October 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Part 2 of 5] [screen fades in from a commercial, and shows the broadcast position. Winchester Smith and Monkey Boy are sitting there, blankly gazing at the camera...] Winchester Smith: Let's come to our next match for tonight. Three impressive rookies here in the GWC will fight each other in a triple threat match. Monkey Boy: And what makes this real impressive is the 'No Disqualification' clause. I hope these three men have enough guts to make this a good match. I want to see blood!!! Winchester Smith: Calm down. Let's go to Shizza. [Cut to the ring.] Shizza Hotee: The following contest is a newcomer no-disqualification triple threat match. Coming to the ring first, weighing in at 267 lbs . ["Same in the End" plays over the PA system as a huge explosion of green pyro-technics rocks the arena.] Shizza Hotee: . TONY ' THE DRAGOOOOON' SEEERPEEEEEE. [The crowd erupts as Tony Serpe walks down he aisle, wearing a blue tanktop with a dragon in a hexagon and orange baggy pants. He has spiked hair, died blue on the left side. He carries a sledgehammer as he slowly walks down the aisle. Some fans raise signs reading "I'm the real dragon" or "I'd like to be dragonbombed".] Monkey Boy: Yeah, there we go. A sledgehammer. This guy knows how to prepare for a no-DQ match. [Serpe completes his walk to the ring, discards his sledgehammer on the outside of the ring, rolls into the ring and quickly makes his way to the turnbuckle, thrusting both arms in the air. The crowd reacts with a large pop. Then the music is replaced by "Get Ya Hands Up" by Jadakiss.] Shizza Hotee: Coming next to the ring, weighting in at 270 lbs, .. DAAAAVE FREESSSSCOOOO!!! [The crowd gives a heel pop as Dave Fresco appears on the aisle. He wears baggy black jeans, black boots, orange elbow pads and black Oakley sunglasses.] Winchester Smith: We saw Dave Fresco last week as he took on Axel Edge and got an easy win. Let's see what he can do tonight against Serpe and the man Serpe fought last week. [Dave Fresco arrives at ringside, exchanges some words with Serpe, smiles evilly and climbs into the ring. "Get Ya Hands Up" stops to play.] Shizza Hotee: And the final contestant, weighting in at 245 lbs . FUUUUSIOOOONN!! [The crowd gives a modest pop as Fusion appears on the aisle. He wears black wrestling pants with neon green stripes on both legs. His short brown spiked hair has blond tips.] Winchester Smith: : All three man weigh nearly the same, but their wrestling style is largely different. Monkey Boy: Fusion takes his time to get to the ring. Come on boy. Let's get this started. I want to see some action. Winchester Smith: Well, Serpe and Fusion fought each other last week like I said earlier. Serpe managed to win that encounter. Let's see if Fusion can fight back and win it this time. Monkey Boy: No way. If there is one person in the ring capable of winning this match, then it's Dave Fresco. Look at him. He's all confident. [Fusion finally makes it to the ring.] Monkey Boy: And Fresco couldn't wait for the bell. He takes both men down with a two-arm clothesline. Reminds me of an airplane . Winchester Smith: . The ref gives the sign for the bell. Fresco took the early advantage. But Fusion jumps back to his legs and Serpe rolls out of the way. Fusion trying some kicks on Fresco who just blocks them. Monkey Boy: You ain't gonna take that man down with kicks. Winchester Smith: But look out for Serpe! He's back to his feet and comes running at Fresco who was distracted by Fusion, and ... a bulldog! Serpe takes Fresco down with a bulldog. That's the problem with triple threat matches, you always have to look out for two guys. Monkey Boy: Serpe back up, and Fusion connects with a dropkick, sending Serpe right back to the mat where he belongs. Winchester Smith: All three men starting at an incredible pace. And . wait..... [Huge crowd pop, as John "The Liberator" Steele appears on the aisle. He wears his wrestling attire, blue pants with the word WOR on the right leg, black boots and his black leather jacket with the picture of Liberty on its back. He also wears an American flag bandana. In his left hand he has a folding chair and he slaps some hands on his way to the ring.] Winchester Smith: John Steele again. We saw him coming to the ring this past week on Titans! Monkey Boy: And costing Messiah his title. John-boy, you'd better keep your nose out of the other people's business, or the true champ will take care of you. Cause your gain will be Messiah's pain. Or whatever. [John Steele gets to the ring, and just unfolds the chair, sits down and watches the action.] Winchester Smith: In the ring, in the meantime, we have Fresco and Serpe exchanging blows. Fresco gaining the advantage, and he sends Serpe down with a big lariat. Fresco turns around, looking for Fusion, who was waiting for this. Kick to the midsection and . DDT!! Fresco's head hits the mat hard. Monkey Boy: But this won't be enough to put Fresco out. Winchester Smith: Serpe up and Fusion tries to go for another kick. NO! Serpe catches Fusion's leg, spins him around, catches him from behind . Atomic Drop! Fusion jumps around in pain. Monkey Boy: Why should that move hurt Fusion? He hasn't got... Winchester Smith: Come on! Fusion visibly hurt, and he doesn't pay attention to Fresco. Dave Fresco scoops Fusion up and bodyslams him to the mat. Monkey Boy: At least that makes him stop jumping around! [grin] Winchester Smith: And Serpe connects with a clothesline to the back of Fresco's head. And he follows with an elbow drop to Fresco's leg. Monkey Boy: Fusion also joins the action. Both Serpe and Fusion now stomping away at Fresco's leg. Looks like some kind of southern tribal dance or something! Winchester Smith: And Fusion talking to Serpe. He points at the turnbuckle. Serpe holds Fresco in place while Fusion climbs to the top rope and comes down with a flying elbow drop to Fresco's knee. Monkey Boy: Did you two play with flies when you were a child? You know ripping their wings out . Winchester Smith: Now Serpe wants the same thing. He climbs to the top rope while Fusion holds Fresco. Serpe comes flying in ... and... Fresco escapes!!! Fresco manages to get out of the way, and Serpe hits the canvas. Monkey Boy: That had to hurt! Winchester Smith: Fresco out of the ring, and Serpe argues with Fusion. And a big fist from Fusion. Serpe and Fusion staring to exchange blows. Monkey Boy: [sings] One for me...one for you . Winchester Smith: Fresco back in the ring, Serpe and Fusion still in the midst of a boxing match there . double headsmash. Fresco knocks Fusion's and Serpe's heads together. Follows up . slams Fusion to the mat and picks up Serpe . HAWAIIAN SMASH!!! Fresco laughs and goes for the pin . ...ONE... . . . . . . . . . . . . . . ...TWO!... . . . . . . . . . . . . . ...KICK-OUT! Winchester Smith: Fusion breaks the pin. Double axehandle blow to Fresco's neck. Fresco gets up and he doesn't look happy at all. Fusion runs into the ropes and comes back...dropkick to Fresco's knee! Fusion continues to work on that knee. Fresco visibly in pain now. Monkey Boy: Pain? Gain? How was it? Winchester Smith: Fusion drags Fresco to the middle of the ring. He wraps his legs around Fresco's. Figure-four leglock!! Fresco yells out in pain. The ref is there to check on him, and Serpe is still out from that earlier action. Monkey Boy: Looks like this will finally end. Oh, by the way: Did anyone tell these guys about the no-DQ? Winchester Smith: Fresco still in the figure-four . Serpe moves! Serpe now notices that both of his opponents are on the mat. He slowly gets up and climbs to the top rope. Monkey Boy: Does the dragon want to fly? His last attempt wasn't very successful... Winchester Smith: Serpe jumps...big splash right onto the mess of legs. That had to hurt both Fresco and Fusion. Monkey Boy: Yeah...yeah .. break some legs!! Winchester Smith: All three men down now. Fresco rolls out of the ring, and he has problems walking. Monkey Boy: Doctor! Nurse! We need a wheelchair! Winchester Smith: In the ring, both Fusion and Serpe get up again. Fusion jumps up...HURRICARANA! Fusion goes for the pin..... ...ONE... . . . . . . . . . . . . . . ...TWO!... . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . THR- NO!!! Winchester Smith: Serpe BARELY kicks out! Fusion looks frustrated now. He picks Serpe up, reverse neckbreaker! Now he whips Serpe into the ropes and starts running himself. Ducks a clothesline attempt by Serpe, they continue running ... BIG BACK DROP by Serpe. Monkey Boy: Aircraft Fusion leaving earth. Winchester Smith: Serpe down with a knee to the floor. He's looks exhausted. Fusion uses the ropes to get back to his feet, Serpe comes running in, tries to clothesline Fusion to the outside. .. BUT Fusion drops down and pulls on the rope. Serpe in the ropes and Fusion levers him out of the ring. Monkey Boy: Was the match changed to a battle royal without me knowing it? Winchester Smith: Serpe wants to get back into the ring, but Fusion won't let him ... baseball slide out of the ring, right into Serpe! Monkey Boy: We had a bit of wrestling, then some boxing, now baseball. When will these guy start playing basketball!?!? Winchester Smith: Fusion grabs Serpe's head and smashes it .. into the ringpost! Serpe is BUSTED OPEN!!! Monkey Boy: FINALLY some blood! It's starting to get interesting. Winchester Smith: Fusion leans Serpe against the ringpost and charges in . Serpe out of the way. Fusion shoulder-tackles the ringpost. Monkey Boy: Smart move. Winchester Smith: Serpe with an armdrag. And he scoops Fusion up, and slams him throat-first onto the guardrail!!! Monkey Boy: Guillotine. Winchester Smith: Serpe's face is now full of blood. He rolls Fusion, who holds his throat with both hands, back into the ring. CHAIRSHOT!!! ! ! ! SMAACCKK ! ! ! Winchester Smith: Fresco is back!! Fresco hits Serpe with a chair...and one more, right onto the head! Monkey Boy: Fresco's the man! Winchester Smith: Fresco rolls into the ring and still has the chair.... ! ! ! SMAAACK ! ! ! Winchester Smith: One more CHAIRSHOT right onto Fusion's head! Fresco's in control now and he raise both arms and the chair high into the air to the approval of the fans. Monkey Boy: Finish it!! Winchester Smith: Serpe climbs back into the ring and he holds something behind his back. It's his sledgehammer . Fresco doesn't notice it, goes for another CHAIRSHOT . Serpe counters with his sledgehammer. ! ! ! SMMAAACKK ! ! ! Winchester Smith: The chair bounces right back from the impact and hits Fresco himself!!!! Monkey Boy: Finally we have some weapons in the ring and these guys manage to hit themselves. AMAZING! And you call ME stupid..SHEESH... Winchester Smith: Serpe picks up Fusion . DRAGONBOMB!!!! I think this is over. Monkey Boy: Finally. ...ONE... . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . ...TWO!... . . . . . . . . . . . . . . SAVED BY FRESCO!!! Winchester Smith: Fresco drags Serpe off of Fusion! Monkey Boy: Dammit! Winchester Smith: Fresco Irish-whips Serpe into the corner .. and he charges right after him . AVALANCHE! Monkey Boy: I don't think this will last much longer...least I hope not...I have to be groomed tonight...this hair isn't LOW MATINANCE ya know... Winchester Smith: Fresco tries to set up something...WHAT THE- ??? [The crowd pops as a big man comes running down the aisle. He wears a black shirt, black boots, black gloves and a mask. On his shirt is written "BaiN"] Winchester Smith: We have another newcomer to the GWC, BaiN, coming to the ring. Looks like he couldn't wait until next week for his debut match! Monkey Boy: Yeah. Look at him, this man is all business! The fire in the eyes of the youngsters filtering in to the GWC is amazing. This should make guys like Aj Nin Red Rum a BIT uneasy... Winchester Smith: He slides into the ring and goes straight after Fresco. Surprisingly, John Steele STILL doesn't move! He looks like a STATUE out there! Monkey Boy: Of course, you fool. He has a match of his own coming up. Do you think he wants to get hurt!?! Winchester Smith: Fresco noticed BaiN sliding into the ring, but he's not fast enough. SPEAR!! BaiN spears Fresco and smashes him right into Serpe, who's still hanging there in the corner. Monkey Boy: BaiN is cleaning the house. Winchester Smith: Fusion is also back to his feet, but he stumbles right into a chokehold by BaiN. CHOKESLAM!! And he continues to choke Fusion, who struggles to get free. [The crowd pops again as another man comes running to the ring. He's a small African-American, but he looks to be quite muscular. He wears street clothes, and upon reaching the ring he immediately starts to climb up the turnbuckle.] Winchester Smith: I think this guy is "Prime-Time" Andre Harris!!! Another newcomer to the GWC. And he comes down with a double-axehandle blow to the back of BaiN! Monkey Boy: The referee looks around and doesn't know what to do! Winchester Smith: Harris continues with a series of chops and uppercuts to BaiN...and now he Irish-whips BaiN into the ropes and Harris catches him on the way back... SIDEWALK SLAM!!! Monkey Boy: Oh, oh. Fresco back up! Winchester Smith: And he catches Harris. Reverse Neckbreaker! In the meantime, BaiN takes care of Fusion, powerslamming him HARD! But Serpe is also still in the game. He clotheslines BaiN .. NO .. BaiN is still standing. Serpe tries again! BaiN staggers but doesn't fall! Monkey Boy: Fusion crawls around on the floor... Winchester Smith: No, .. yes , .. but he crawls behind BaiN, Serpe sees it, goes for another clothesline attempt and Bain falls over Fusion! Monkey Boy: John Steele is still at ringside but he isn't moving at all! This is freaking CHAOS! Winchester Smith: Fusion and Serpe double-teaming now. Double clothesline takes down Fresco. And a double dropkick for Harris. Monkey Boy: Do we have the first GWC tag-team there? Winchester Smith: VICIOUS SPEAR from BaiN to Serpe's back!!!. And Harris and Fresco start to brawl with Fusion! BaiN chokes Serpe, while . Fresco goes for the SLEDGEHAMMER... Harris lifts Fusion . Kneebreaker! And he signals Fresco to use that hammer on Fusion's knee!!! Monkey Boy: That could be it for Fusion's career! Winchester Smith: WAIT!!! John Steele finally decides to join the action. He slides into the ring and grabs the hammer as Fresco draws back. Fresco turns around to check what happened and is met by a knee to the midsection. DDT!! Monkey Boy: I knew he would interfere. Hey, here's my bet. 10 dollars on another interference by John-boy on next week's Titan show. Winchester Smith: Harris executes a beautiful belly-to-back suplex on Fusion. Now Harris taps on Bain's shoulder and as Bain looks up Andre kicks him right into the face. Monkey Boy: That could have broken Bain's nose. But how should we know under that mask. Winchester Smith: Harris taking care of Serpe now, they are exchanging blows. Harris stuns Serpe and lifts him up for a powerbomb. But Serpe with a flurry of fists to Harris' head. Harris lets go and .. LOW BLOW! Out of nowhere! [Ding! Ding! Ding!] Monkey Boy: I think the ref had enough. Winchester Smith: Bain, Fresco, Fusion, all brawling in the ring now. Harris is still knocked out and Serpe rolls out of the ring. His face is full of blood. [A dozen GWC officials are running down the aisle now, and they start trying to separate the wrestlers in the ring.] Winchester Smith: Bain and Harris want some more, but Steele tells Fusion to let go. He walks over to Serpe and helps him back up. Then he raises both Fusion's and Serpe's arm! Monkey Boy: Yeah .. flee from the fight. Shizza Hotee: The result of this contest . due to the amount of outside interference . is a NO-CONTEST! Monkey Boy: Great. Does this mean we have to reschedule this match? Winchester Smith: In fact, I don't know. But I know that Steele made himself some more enemies tonight. Blood's dripping from Bain's nose, but he doesn't care. He yells at Steele to come back and fight like a man. Monkey Boy: But John-boy ignores him. He just walks up the aisle, together with Fusion and Serpe. Winchester Smith: We'll have to wait a bit for the officials to clean the ring and we'll be right back for our next match, which will be a man who has been very present tonight, John 'The Liberator' Steele taking on a new found enemy in Dementor! Strap Match! And it's next! STAY TUNED! [Camera fades out to BaiN beckoning for Steele, Fusion, and Serpe to return to the ring. The trio collectively raise their arms towards the rafters at the end of the aisle way to the approval of the fans. The camera then fades out to black...]
