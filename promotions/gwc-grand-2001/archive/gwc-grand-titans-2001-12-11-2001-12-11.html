We will find out when these two warriors go toe-to-toe for the right to call themselves the GWC'S KING OF EXTREME! [ Settle A Score ] The Messiah vs. Asylum - The thorn in the side of the GWC's royal family, the Dark Project, is a man who calls himself ASYLUM. The man will take on the one he has been chasing since his arrival, former GWC Champion...THE MESSIAH! [ Regional Title Bound Match ] Dave Fresco vs. "The Gorilla" Chris Hoff vs. Ronnie "The Wolf" Black -- Since the Hoff/Black bout yielded no clear victor, a triple threat match has been slated with the third warrior being Dave Fresco to determine who will be the next man to take a shot at the GWC Regional Championship! Alex Deadly vs. Tony "The Dragon" Serpe - ALEX DEADLY is blazing his own trail that seems to have gold at the end of it. He will challenge a man who is on the same trail and would get a good boost by taking a win, TONY SERPE! "Lucky" Adam Wilde vs. "Primetime" Andre Harris - ADAM WILDE wasn't so 'lucky' last week against Alex Deadly but he showed that he is a hell of a talent. [ Singles Match ] "Lucky" Adam Wilde vs. "Primetime" Andre Harris ^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^ ~ MATCH OVERVIEW ~ ^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^ A classic shoulder and elbow lock-up started us off with Wilde taking control, into an armbar and waistlock. A failed reversal attempt lead Wilde to nail a big German suplex, however Harris was up fast. Wilde sent him into the ropes, attempted and missed a clothesline as Harris ducked under and used his momentum to take down Wilde with an Asai moonsault. An attempt at a pinfall failed, and Harris went to work. A few quick stomps gave way to a slingshot into the near buckle. Wilde slumped down on his rear, dazed in the buckle and narrowly fell victim to a bronco buster by Harris. Andre missed however and hurt himself, leaving Wilde ready and poised to capitalize. Wilde nailed some hard, stiff kicks to the chest and head of Andre, then dragged him out of the corner and nailed a DDT. Wilde hit the ropes and dropped a hard legdrop, then went to the air. A flying elbow drop capped off a high paced offense, then Wilde slowed it down. He simply let Harris get to his feet, clipped out his right knee, twisting it. Harris was visually in pain, and Wilde locked in the Shapeshifter Boston crab for the submission victory. WINNER- "Lucky" Adam Wilde, via submission, at 7:01 Monkey Boy: Zzzzz...zzz.... Winchester Smith: [sigh] [ Singles Match ] Alex Deadly vs. Tony "The Dragon" Serpe ^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^ ~ MATCH OVERVIEW ~ ^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^ The two men started it out with a simple, hardly scientific slugfest. Neither men really gained an advantage from the exchange, though Deadly took the tide when he ended the flying fists with a kick to the gut, and a snap suplex. Serpe mocked Deadly a bit, then stomped him back down to the mat several times. Serpe went to the second rope for an double axe but was caught in a reverse waist-lock, then belly-to-belly supelxed. Deadly dragged Serpe to his feet for a traditional piledriver, then a few stomps to Serpe's head. Deadly sent him to the ropes following this, and nailed a jumping DDT which lead to a very close pinfall attempt. Deadly smelled blood, and went for a back body drop that Serpe countered by landing on his feet. He shoved Deadly into the near ropes and went to pull him back with his own momentum for a roll-up but Deadly hung onto the ropes. He then scooped Serpe up upon him rising to his feet and nailed the Deadly Sin driver for the pin. WINNER: Alex Deadly, via pinfall, at 6:13 Winchester Smith: Deadly continues his rise in the ranks here in the GWC, his talent being displayed on the platform of the ring once again this week. [ Regional Title Bound Match ] Dave Fresco vs. "The Gorilla" Chris Hoff vs. Ronnie "The Wolf" Black ^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^ ~ MATCH OVERVIEW ~ ^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^ Match starts with Hoff going right for Black, however he is cut off by a vicious boot to the head by Fresco. Fresco is then jumped from behind and wrapped up in a Black sleeper hold. Fresco quickly breaks the hold with a jawbreaker and then quickly executes a DDT and a cover. Hoff breaks it at one, and the two monsters slug it out. Black has time to recover and a swift martial arts kick to the rear of Fresco's knee brought him down and then Black put him on his face with another kick to the back of the head. Hoff, fire in his eyes, then charged Black but was sidestepped, and Black sent him out of the ring with his own momentum. Black hit the far ropes and nailed a suicide dive that carried the two men, Hoff and Black, right over the guard rail and into the crowd! Fresco, limping, slid from the ring and commandeered Monkey Boy's chair. Black and Hoff were both struggling out of the mess of chairs now, Black beating Hoff to his feet. By now Fresco had joined them and took a fast swing at Ronnie Black. Too bad for Hoff, Ronnie ducked and Hoff ate steel chair for his last meal, crumpling onto the concrete. Black cleared the rail and slid into the ring, Fresco in hot pursuit. Black motioned for Fresco to get into the ring, which he did and before he even stood was met with a stiff karate style kick to the teeth. Fresco was shocked for a moment, leaving black to wrap up grab the previously targeted leg for a legscrew. Fresco, wailing in pain on the canvas then looked up on the buckle to see Black, chair pressed to his chest. Black went for the big splash and nailed it, taking an obvious toll on himself as well. By now the official had dragged Hoff out of the chairs and revived him, returning to the ring. Black struggled over and covered. A mili-second before Hoff could make the save, the three was counted and Black was victorious. Hoff, furious, grabbed the chair left on the canvas and went to work. Black got to his feet first in obvious pain and was swatted down like a fly. Fresco was struggling up as well, and only made it to his knees when Hoff booted the set into Fresco's face. Black was then lifted high over Hoff's head and GORILLA PRESS SLAMMED TO THE OUTSIDE!!! Hoff was then convinced by a heard of officials to leave the ring with out inflicting any more damage. Black was looked at by a doctor at ringside as Fresco came to. Fresco, busted open and a raging monster, swept up his chair and went Gorilla hunting after sending several officials for a ride. Hoff heard, turned, and the two men attempted to but unsuccessfully battle through the sea off security to get another piece of one another. WINNER: Ronnie "The Wolf" Black, via pinfall, 6:48 Winchester Smith: Well, it seems we have ourselves a bit of a "love" triangle here! Three men thirst for Regional title gold and only one will get it. Black seems to be the first one that will receive the shot. Monkey Boy: He took my chair! Well, on to another newly sparked bitter rivalry. Asylum, the man who the Dark Project just can't seem to get rid of takes on their leader and former GWC champion...Messiah! Monkey Boy: This oughta be good...just tell them to leave my poor chair out of it. It's already all lumpy thanks to Fresco's head... [ Settle A Score ] The Messiah vs. Asylum ^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^ ~ MATCH OVERVIEW ~ ^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^ The match started out a fight. Messiah and Asylum simply charged right at one another and began beating the hell out of each other. Messiah got the upper hand early being unusually intense in the early goings. Asylum kept up though, and got the tide turned in his favor with a low blow. Asylum then nailed several hard kicks to the gut of Messiah which backed him into the buckle. Asylum went for the Irish whip into the other buckle, however Messiah reversed it then followed Asylum in with a big splash. Asylum stumbled out, and got a kick to the abdomen, then a snap suplex. Messiah then began working on the lower body of Asylum. A make-a-wish bone lead to a legscrew and then a one-legged atomic drop. Messiah seemed to be obviously setting up for the Resurrection. Asylum knew it however, and when it seemed Messiah was going to finally apply it Asylum stopped him with a roll-up and near-fall. Messiah got frustrated and tossed Asylum to the outside. Messiah planted his head on the guard rail and then rammed him into the ring post. As Asylum staggered back towards Messiah he lifted him up high and pancaked him on the concrete floor. Messiah then rolled Asylum back in the ring. Dragging him to his feet, Messiah tossed Asylum to the ropes and took him down with an altered drop toe hold which seemed to twist the knees of Asylum the wrong way. Asylum let out a scream as he went down and Messiah locked in the Resurrection. Asylum was screeching in pain but did not tap. Several times while wrenching on the hold Messiah screamed some things at Asylum and belted him in the face. Eventually Asylum quit responding to the official and the match was stopped. WINNER: Messiah, via official's decision, 11:02 Monkey Boy: [looking down] Whoohoo! My chair is still here! Yeeeah! Winchester Smith: To bad Asylum isn't! It seems he passed out during the application of Messiah's finisher, the Resurrection. Asylum, however, doesn't seem like the type to go quietly. I'm sure this isn't the end of these two.
