GWC FightFest

31 October 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Part 4 of 5] [camera fades back in to Joe Louis Arena, fans filing back in from a recent visit to the bathroom facilities, concessions, etc. A quick pan of the crowd catches signs like "Liberate this FOCKER!" and "Shane DuShayne is my DAD!". The camera rests, once more, upon the announce position where Monkey Boy tries to talk Winchester into a game of thumb wrestling...] Monkey Boy: C'MON! It's way more entertaining than this CRAP we have to announce! Winchester Smith: (motioning towards camera, complete w/ blinking red light) Congratulations! You just said that on national television! And the World's Biggest KNOB award GOES TO! Monkey Boy: Err...umm...hmm...well I...err..meant...ummm...yeah..well ya see...this is...no...DAMN... Winchester Smith: Just shut up! We have an interesting bout, right here, right now! A man who has been rocking the collective GWC boat in the past week, John 'The Liberator' Steele will climb into the squared circle with someone who he angered along the way, Dementor! Monkey Boy: Don't forget...THEY'LL BE BOUND BY A STRAP! Yummy...guarun-DAMN-teed to be delectably violent and deliciously bloody! GOD I LOVE THIS SPORT! Winchester Smith: Mmm hmm. No comment. TO THE RING! [in ring] Shizza Hotee: Well BOYS (grin)...our next match-up will be a one-fall contests...as well as a STRAP MATCH! [crowd pop] Shizza Hotee: Introducing the first MAN that will slip his hand in the LEATHER... ["Open Your Eyes" by Guano Apes pours out of the arena sound system as the lights slowly dim. A large, muscular man with long blonde hair back in a pony-tail steps out from behind the curtain. He wears blue trousers that are and scattered tears, and also sports black leather jacket with the Statue of Liberty on the back, as well as an American Flag bandana on his head...] Shizza Hotee: He stands 6 feet 7 inches tall, and weighs in at 263 pounds...HE IS... ! ! ! J O H N " T H E L I B E R A T O R " S T E E L E ! ! ! [Steele smiles widely as he makes his way down the aisle, enthusiastically slapping the outstretched hands of the GWC fans. He circles the ring, shaking hands. Steele approaches an old woman who is having an incredible time and he places the American Flag bandana that was on his head, on hers. The old woman, almost in tears, gives the Liberator a hug, and then he rolls into the ring...] Shizza Hotee: And now...HIS OPPONENT... ["My Plague" by Slipknot plays over the PA as Dementor steps from behind the curtain. A sight to behold, he has a "kabuki" (white face mask) as well as black armbands and studded leather gloves and trousers and pull on "monkey" boots...] Shizza Hotee: He stands 6 feet 7 inches tall, tips the scale at 286 pounds...HE IS... ! ! ! DEMENTOR ! ! ! [Dementor steps into the ring and approaches Steele. A tense staredown ensues, while the official fastens an 8 foot long, thick brown leather strap to each man's wrist. Both men, embroiled in their heated comments for one-another seem oblivious to the official completing his work...] [bell tolls] Winchester Smith: AND WE'RE OFF! These to men start us in with a HEATED exchange of words, and NOW FISTS! They're brawling like boxers in there! Monkey Boy: They certainly don't like each other... Winchester Smith: Err....DUH! Dementor getting the better of the exchange...sends Steele to the ropes now...Steele ducks a clothesline...comes back...GIVES HIM A CLOTHESLINE OF HIS OWN WHICH LEVELS DEMENTOR! Monkey Boy: Err...OUCH! Winchester Smith: Dementor is a bit dazed, rising up slowly...Steele poised and waiting...ANOTHER MASSIVE CLOTHESLINE! Steele now...dragging Dementor up to his feet...BELLY-TO-BELLY! Steele follows him down! Steele now, springs up to his feet- ! ! ! WHAP ! ! ! ! ! ! WHAP ! ! ! ! ! ! WHAP ! ! ! Monkey Boy: A sequence of HARD lashes with the strap! That's gonna leave a welt! Winchester Smith: Steele is picking up a struggling Dementor now...sends him to the ropes... ! ! ! WHAP ! ! ! Monkey Boy: OUCH! He swung for the FENCES on that one! Dementor needs to pick himself up and dust himself off or we're gonna be moving onto the next match right quick! Winchester Smith: Nicely said...for once. Steele now...sends Dementor to the ropes again...could we see another home run!?! Monkey Boy: NOPE! Winchester Smith: GOOD GOD! [Dementor ducks under the swing by Steele, quickly wrapping the strap around the throat of Steele and shoving him over the ropes, effectively HANGING Steele who struggles for a foothold...] Monkey Boy: This is HUGE! Steele could be quickly choked out and end the match!... Winchester Smith: OR HIS CAREER! Someone get in there DAMMIT! This is no longer wrestling! Monkey Boy: Why don't you ya old bag of bones! Winchester Smith: MAYBE I WILL! [Winchester Smith puts down his headphones, rising from his chair. He walks over towards where Steele dangles, and begins yelling some inaudible words at Dementor. Dementor shifts his attention at Winchester, dropping Steele to the floor. Dementor steps out of the ring and to the floor, and starts laughing, and taunting Smith. Winchester takes off his tweed jacket and rolls up his white sleeves. Dementor is still laughing...and takes a swing at Winchester!!! Winchester DUCKS IT and plants a fist in the gut of a SHOCKED Dementor!!! The blow doesn't faze him much, but now Steele is up and yanks the strap, dragging Dementor back towards him and NAILS THE LIBERTY!!!] Monkey Boy: THAT BALLSY OLD MAN! I'LL BE DAMNED! STEELE JUST HIT A LIBERTY ON THE FLOOR! WINCHESTER SMITH COULD HAVE POSSIBLY SAVED THE CAREER OF JOHN "THE LIBERATOR" STEELE! WOW! WHAT A NIGHT!!! Winchester Smith (breathing hard): Well, by God I've still it! Ha ha! Arrogant youngster... Monkey Boy: Well, I gotta hand it to you old man, you've got canonizes of steel! No pun intended. (grin) Winchester Smith: John Steele rolls Dementor back in the ring now! COVER! ...ONE... . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . ...TWO!... . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . THR- BARELY ESCAPES!!! Monkey Boy: First near fall of the contest! Steele back up and in control! Winchester Smith: He drags that silly bastard to his feet now...sends him into the far buckle... ! ! ! CLANG ! ! ! Monkey Boy: ...and HARD! Winchester Smith: Steele stalking over to the buckle...what the...what's he DOING!?! Monkey Boy: THAT SLY SON OF A BITCH IS TYING DEMENTOR IN THE CORNER WITH THE STRAP!!! Winchester Smith: Steele has Dementor tied to the ring post!!! AND HE'S LAYING INTO HIM NOW!!! HE'S KICKING THE LIVING HELL OUT OF HIM! DEMENTOR CAN'T FIGHT BACK!!! Monkey Boy: Steele wrapping what slack is left around his right fist now...RIGHT HAND! CLOSED FIST RIGHT HAND! ANOTHER! ANOTHER! GOOD LORD! Winchester Smith: The official is DEMANDING that Steele free Dementor, and is obliging! Monkey Boy: But the damage is done! Dementor's right eye is already swelling from those right hands! Winchester Smith: Dementor is free...he stumbles out of the corner and RIGHT INTO THE LIBERTY!!! IT'S DONE WITH FOLKS!!! ...ONE... . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . ...TWO!... . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . ...THREEEE!!! [bell tolls] Shizza Hotee: Your winner of the STRAP MATCH via pinfall... ! ! ! J O H N " T H E L I B E R A T O R " S T E E L E ! ! ! Monkey Boy: Well, Steele takes home a win..WITH AN ASSIST FROM THIS OLD BASTARD! Winchester Smith: I do what I can! (grins) Monkey Boy: Coming up next, we have REGIONAL GOLD ON THE LINE, so STAY TUNED OR I CASTRATE YOU! YEAH YOU! [camera fades out to Dementor gradually coming too, while John "The Liberator" Steele climbs the buckle and thrusts his fists towards the rafters, celebrating his victory...]
