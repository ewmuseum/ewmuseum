GWC Titans - Tuesday, October 2nd, 2001

2 October 2001
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- Grand Wrestling Corporation presents..... ~ GWC TITANS - Tuesday, October 2nd, 2001 ~ -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- TONIGHT'S LINE-UP: ------------------------------ -------------------------------------------------- -- GWC Heavyweight Title Tourney -- -------------------------------------------------- Round 1: <><><><> Jim Klown Bowe vs. Aj Nin Red Rum "Iron Man" Shane DuShayne vs. Weasel Messiah vs. Franky Dukes Axel Edge vs. Joe Six-pack Semi-finals: <><><><><> Winner of Bowe/AJ Nin Red Rum vs. Winner of Iron Man/Weasel Winner of Messiah/Franky Dukes vs. Winner of Joe 6/Axel Edge -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=--=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- =-=-=- ...Before the event got underway, Messiah had these words to say... ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- ---------------- **Tougher than Leather by Run DMC plays and the lights are replaced by Strobes. Pyro goes off and out of the back comes Messiah. Slowly making his way to the ring, he teases the fans and laughs until a fan throws a beer and smacks him on his face.** (grabs a mic) Messiah: That my friends, was uncalled for. I came to this federation for the simple fact, I was promised competition. I sat over in the TDW and CWFpro and all they did was promote two wrestlers. And let me tell you something, those two losers didn't have enough talent to even be in the same federation as me. I am Messiah dammit! (boos from the crowd) Messiah: Now I have come to this federation, and I will devastate any little person that gets in my way. I have shown the world my talents, and I will continue to do so. So it is time to show THIS locker room, they are nothing to the one and only Messiah. So here is an open invitation to anyone in the back, IF YOU'VE GOT AN ITCH, I'LL MAKE YOU MY BITCH!! CAUSE YOUR PAIN...IS MY GAIN!!!! **Messiah exits and the crowd continues a loud course of boos** -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- ~ MATCH 1 ~ Jim Klown Bowe vs. Aj Nin Red Rum -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- MATCH OVERVIEW: ---------------------------- The match got off to a slow start, both men exchanging blows, brawling. Aj Nin then kicked things up a notch. Tossing Bowe to the outside, a series of devastating kicks lead to a piledriver on the concrete and a back body drop with Bowe landing stomach down on a guard rail. Bowe rebounded, however, with a flurry of punches, and leveling Red Rum with a clothesline that nearly took his head off. The match went long, with Bowe visually tired. Red Rum nailed a top rope suicide dive leaving both men nearly unconscious. Bowe recovered first, amazingly, however upon regaining composure he was met by a stiff low blow by Red Rum. Bowe fell to his knees, and Red Rum rolled back in the ring at the demand of the official. Bowe slipped back in the ring after nearly being counted out. Red Rum met him there with some hard stomps, then a perfect standing side kick. Red Rum nailed some amazing high risk maneuvers including a superplex to the outside. Red Rum brought a chair in the ring, and when attempting to hit Bowe, Bowe countered by dropkicking the equalizer into Red Rum's head, busting him open. The official rang the bell, making this match a disqualification. Red Rum on his knees, got another hard chair shot by Jim Klown Bowe who then left the ring laughing as Aj Nin Red Rum lay unconscious. When awakening, a insanely twisted, blood thirsty look was followed by a bloodied Aj Nin grabbing the same chair that he was assaulted with and seemingly going on the hunt for Jim Klown Bowe. WINNER: Aj Nin Red Rum, via DQ, at 12:38 -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- ~ MATCH 2 ~ "Ironman" Shane DuShayne vs. the Weasel -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- MATCH OVERVIEW: ----------------------------- Shane DuShayne came out with something to prove. A massive onslaught from the get-go kept the Weasel at bay for large portions of the contest. The Weasel countered with his unique baseball related offense which seemed to confuse DuShayne. A baseball slide dropkick started it off, followed by the Weasel 'charging the mound' with a Lou Thesz Press and a series of punches. DuShayne, dumbfounded, simply fell victim to the attack. Eventually, with a huge powerbomb, DuShayne recovered and nearly scored a pinfall. The Weasel tried with all his might to fight back but was his offensive attempts were always squashed. Shane pummeled the Weasel with high impact maneuvers such as suplexes, a bulldog, tornado DDT, etc. Shane came in to finish things off with yet another powerbomb. Right when DuShayne was about to execute what seemed to be his finisher, The Weasel unexpectedly executed a small package and scored the pinfall victory. DuShayne was furious. WINNER: The Weasel, via pinfall, at 10:02 -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- ~ MATCH 3 ~ Messiah vs. Franky Dukes -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- MATCH OVERVIEW: This match was very quick, and very decisive. Messiah came to the ring on a mission. Franky Dukes came to the ring and gave his all, but Messiah quelled every attempt. Dukes tries everything in his arsenal to no avail. Messiah picked the lower back of Dukes, maintaining a constant assault on it. Following a vicious top-rope backbreaker, Messiah locked in the Resurrection causing Dukes to submit. Dukes passed out from the immense pain caused by the move. WINNER: Messiah, via submission, at 4:23 -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- ~ MATCH 4 ~ Axel Edge vs. Joe Six-pack -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- MATCH OVERVIEW: Match began with Egde's dominance. He mounted a very successful offense of martial-arts based moves which seemed to catch 6-pack off guard. Joe eventually absorbed the punishment and came back with very non-technical brawling. He simply punched Edge until he could barely stand. Axel tried to escape by leaving the ring to recuperate but Joe followed him right out of the ring and whipped him into the guard rail. Then, Joe introduced Axel's face to the ring post. Joe then tossed Edge back into the ring to finish things. Edge, however, having other plans threw Joe face first into the buckle, and then executed a big splash. This was followed by a huge piledriver. Edge then hit the high skies, nailing a top rope legdrop and nearly scoring a pinfall. Edge nearly stomped his foot right through Joe's chest, then deciding to hit a spring-board plancha from outside. Caught in mid-air by Joe, a powerslam planted Edge face-up on the canvas. Joe then proceeded to the top rope, nailing a famousser he calls the "Dukes of Hazard Decapitator". Then, a cover scored the win. WINNER: Joe Six-pack, via pinfall, at 11:37 -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- ~ MATCH 5 ~ Aj Nin Red Rum vs. The Weasel -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- MATCH OVERVIEW: Red Rum, head taped up an all came to the ring with blood shot eyes and vengeance on his mind. The Weasel tried to mount a traditional offensive but was overwhelmed by the intensity of Red Rum. Aj Nin devastated Weasel with a flurry of quick kicks and then executed a scoop piledriver. Red Rum dragged Weasel to the corner and nailed a 450 splash which nearly snagged him the victory. Red Rum tossed Weasel to the far buckle and charged him. Weasel narrowly escaped, sending Aj Nin shoulder first into steel. Weasel now capitalized, nailing a piledriver which agitated Red Rum's wound. Weasel then hit a big spine buster but couldn't get the three count. Weasel then sent Red Rum to the outside, and executed an "Going, Going, Gone", going to the top rope and executing a cannonball splash to the outside. Red Rum got his knees up however, and Weasel was hurt. Aj Nin Red Rum then took advantage of this, hanging Weasel out to dry on the guard rail, then send a series of kicks to his pained ribs. Weasel, favoring his left side the rest of the match, unsuccessfully tried to mount yet another offense. Red Rum hit a gut-buster, followed but a long bear hug which seemed to squeeze the last bits of life out of the Weasel. Dead Weight, a cradle piledriver put Weasel down for the three count. WINNER: Aj Nin Red Rum, via pinfall, at 18:22 -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- ~ MATCH 6 ~ Messiah vs. Joe Six-pack -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- MATCH OVERVIEW: Messiah came into this one the same way as the first. Though both men were visibly and rightfully worn down, Messiah came to the ring wanting to finish it quick. Six-pack put up a valiant effort, however. Messiah took quite a beating after his initial lightning quick offense. Six-pack laid into him hard, to his surprise. A barrage of kicks and punches combined with a hard DDT and near fall stunned Messiah. He came back at Joe with some brawling of his own. Messiah came back with a hard fore-arm to the lower back region of Six-pack. This followed by a back-breaker and big power-slam got Joe primed to be finished. Joe came back once again with a surprising series of hard forearms and a nicely timed German suplex but Messiah just wouldn't give. A big German suplex of his own led to a top rope powerbomb and then the locking in of the Resurrection. Joe held out for awhile, trying for the ropes but eventually tapped. WINNER: Messiah, via tap out, at 16:01 -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- (c) GWC Wrestling, 2001 -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- [name withheld] Chairman, Grand Wrestling Corp. - http://www.geocities.com/gwcwrestling2000 <><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><> "Dangerous" William Devonshire (IWF/WOW, MLW) <><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><> [detail withheld] "Would you care for a CHOCOLATE COVERED PRETZEL?" - Brody, Mallrats (<--- tied for best movie in history w/ Ferris Bueller's Day Off)
