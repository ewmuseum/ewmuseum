[name withheld] here. Participation is up, as is membership. Things are looking great this week! I'll continue to recruit, though the influx of new faces should slow as I will be scouting out high quality talent and trying to persuade them to join our ranks. This takes time, but will be fruitful. I encourage all of you to make any and all attempts to IMPROVE ON YOUR ROLEPLAYING! I will recruit quality talent, and the level of competition will rise. In order to keep up and remain a member here, I expect you to adapt, and rise to the occasion. I trust all here are capable of doing so! Moving on, we had a semi-eventful Titans. I do apologize for a few of the lack luster matches, as either one or both of the handlers involved chose to leave the GWC to pursue other activities after the match was booked. I must admit, I am very excited about the coming weeks, as well as our first "PPV", FightFest which is on OCTOBER 31ST! Halloween! This card will be in a PLAY-BY-PLAY format, meaning the matches will be commentated as if the card is live. If anyone has experience writing such matches and would like to lend a hand for the card, it would be appreciated! Thanks. On with the update...
