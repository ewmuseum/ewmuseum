CWF Bash, Day Two

28 September 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Or week two, you decide(Is that spelled right?) This is for the fans of CWF (Hi Syko) Look, our webpage!!! http://www.angelfire.com/wrestling/amw1/index.html Here's my second ever card in my low-rate fed: **Note** Any match with the word's "Guest Written" next to them have, well, been guest written. I'm working on developing a card staff, so if ANYONE wants to guest write, let me know. Place: Elks Lodge Attendance: 1000 Hosts: Peter Johnson and Johnny Freil The show started with the fans getting fired up for what would be a great night of action. All of a sudden "Money Ain't a Thang" played as the former wrestler known as Greed came out to address the fans. Here's a scene from the 6'9, 375 LB man's speech: Greed: Well fans, I hope I'm not disturbing the atmosphere here in New York, but I feel I must address you. My name is Wayne Marquis, but most of you knew me at one time as Greed <cheap pop> Well fans, those days are behind me. I got sick and tired of the one track presidents that built everything around the name of a wrestler, not the talent. So, I decided to change that, I am the reason CWF was started. That's right, I'm the "mystery" president of the CWF. And, as president, I feel there is something important that needs to be addressed. (Mr. Marquis reaches into his gym bag and pulls out a breathtaking title belt like never seen before. He holds it high before he speaks) WE NEED A WORLD CHAMPION!!!! Now I know we have MANY former world champions on our roster, but who are the best, who are the most ELITE of these men??? With careful deliberation, I have only picked four men, of the 8 I wish to battle. The first four men will be Kevin Douglas, Carnage, Barry Bronxs, and "Billionaire" Joe Forbes. Now, as far as the four other men, anyone from the back, you want in, let me know somehow, and we'll see what we can work out!!! "Money ain't a thang" played again as Mr. Marquis left the ring to an uproar from the fans. Rain vs. Chris Young Poor, poor Young, first the attack from Carnage last week, and now, the debut of the furious Rain. The monster of a man just totally dominated Young, who looked like a small child in there. His stiff armshots had absolutely NO effect, and Rain made sure he didn't get to use them much. After decimating him for about a minute, Rain nailed the Rain Drop, and got the easy victory. After the match though, announcer Johnny Freil ran in the ring and nailed Rain with a hellashish chairshot. Winner: Rain via pinfall. Time : 1:07 Next we saw a behind the scenes of CJB and Fatty Jay: (CJB is in his dressing room. He has on his white "Team Captain" tights, his knee pads, elbow pads, and is taping up his wrists. Fatty Jay sneaks in. He has on his black shirt that says "Unleash the Fats" on it in red with blood dripping from the letters. He faces the camera and is holding something behind his back.) Fatty: Hey, Classy. CJB: Oh, what's up Fats? Fatty: Nothing.....I got a new concoction! CJB: Oh yeah.....you changing gimmicks again? Fatty: Nope... CJB: Ok, umm........whats behind your back? (Fatty Jay reveals what is a chair that has a fake stomach taped to the seat.) Fatty: Ta da! CJB:(Laughing) What the hell is that? Fatty: It's my Fattotic chair! The stomach is solid. THis shit would hurt anyone. CJB: Do you really think that's necessary? I mean, I am the team captain...and I know I can beat anyone in the CWF. Fatty: True, but I'm a marked man........I need protection. CJB: Whatever dude...look tonight is my in ring debut, and we both I don't really need Forbes, but he is a good man to have around. Fatty:(Scratching his chin) Yes, Joey is worth a lot of money. CJB: Fats, you seem tense. You need to relax. No one in the CWF hs my talent. I am main attraction and I am the one who is carrying all the weight of this fed on my back. I did it in CWO, and in XMEW. Fatty: I know, it's just that.....Carnage is crazy! CJB: Fats, tonight I'm gonna take care of Carnage, then I'm gonna take care of The Enchillada. Now, go find Forbes and make sure he is ready. (Fatty Jay leaves carrying the Fattotic chair. CJB starts to stretch) Rei Dumas vs. Killa Bee In, in many opinions, the most technical match of the CWF, Rei Dumas and Killa Bee squared off. Both men took eachother down to the mat, and tried to get eachother to tap. Finally, Killa Bee did it, he made a mistake and got cocky, Rei took advantage of the slight error, and out of nowhere, hit the Devil's Advocate. It was the end for sure Winner: Rei Dumas by pinfall. time: 8:12 Deadpool vs. Brian Williams (Guest Written) Brian Williams is seen standing in the ring. "I Might be Wrong" begins to play as Deadpool walks to the ring. Deadpool adjusts his mask and climbs the turnbuckle. Deadpool gives a "Spike" wave to the crowd. He jumps down from the turnbuckle as the bell rings. Deadpool took an early offense, hitting a combo of stiff kicks to Williams' stomach and head. Williams nalied Deadpool with a DDt and threw him outside the ring. Willaims went for a suicide dive, but Deadpool grabbed a chair and nailed Williams with it on his way out. Deadpool hit the ropes and jumped out onto Williams with a springboard moonsault. Williams was the first one up and nailed Deadpool with a sickning chairshot. Williams threw Deadpool back into the ring and set up the chair. Williams sent deadpool into the ropes and tried to hit him with a Belly to Belly onto the chair, but Deadpool landed on his feet out of the Belly to Belly, grabbed the chair, ran to the ropes and as nailed Williams with the People's Chairshot as he turned around. Deadpo ol covered Williams for the 1,2,3. Winner: Deadpool via pinfall Max Power vs. Shawn Price Unfortuantely, after the last card, Shawn Price decided not to show up at the arena, what was Power supposed to do? Well, finally, Stabbing Westward cut through the arena and out ran Venom, chair in hand, belt on waist. A toughman title match was under-way. Oh it was full of highpoints and weapons, with each man standing his own. Venom lucked out with a steel sign long enough to set up a table in the corner. Power was on top of him through, and went for the irish whip, but it was reversed. After Power went crashing through the table, all that was left was the cover and 1,2,3. Winner: Venom by pinfall. Time: 11.42 (Venom retains Toughman Championship) Andy Carter vs. The Enforcer Carter had been hyped up about the World title tournament, and let everyone know in a brief pre-match interview. He also let The Enforcer know the only thing being Enforced tonite was Carter's foot kicking his ass all across New York. Well, Carter almost did. He took the Big man totally to the limit and back. In less than 30 seconds, he has Enforcer tapping to the Chains Of Carter. Winner: Andy Carter by submission. time :30 seconds Another pre-match warm-up between CJB and Fatty Jay aired prior to there match-up. (Johnny Freil is standing by with Classy Joey Boy and Fatty Jay. Fatty still has on his "Unleash the Fatts" t-shirt. While CJB has on his wrestling tights and sunglasses.) Freil: Classy, up next it's you and Forbes against Carnage and The Maquilladora. You have never teamed with Forbes, infact I don't think you have ever seen him wrestle. How do you plan on handling this match tonight. CJB: Well, Johnny-boy, as the CWF Team Captain, I beleive that tonight I am going to single handedly dominate Carnage and The Mastachollie. I know Carnage is tough and maybe just a little crazy, but that's why I have the fat man with me.(Fatty Jay points to himself)You see John John, Fatty has a special gift for Carnage if he decides his insanity is getting the best of him.(Fatty Jay nods his head in agreement) He calls it the Fattotic chair.(Fatty holds up the Fattotic chair) Carnage, we all know your nothing without your sissy boy Venom. And we all know Venom is nothing without ice cream. Neither of those things have anything to do with tonights match, however Fatty Jay!(Fatty poins to himself) And Classy Joey Boy (Fatty points to CJB) will bring the "W" to home team! (Fatty Jay and CJB walk away and get ready to head to the ring) Freil: Well, CJB is very confidant that he will easily defeat Carnage and The Enchillada.....I mean The Maquilladora, single handedly here tonight. Classy Joey Boy & Joe Forbes vs. Carnage & The Maquilladora All four men came out seperately, and as the match was about to start, some god-awful superhero music began to play, and in he ran, CAPSMAN, cape and all. How long did he last?? Oh, 30, maybe 35 seconds. As soon as he made that first WHOOSH sound, it was over. Four of the biggest names in CWF just pounded the guy into oblivion, then turned on eachother and the match was underway. As soon as order was restored, Carnage and Forbes had been the ones left standing. Forbes maintain control, and Joey Boy and himself exchange fast tags. Finally, Carnage nailed the Decent into Insanity out of nowhere on Joey Boy, and then made the tag to Maquilladora. At the site of CJB, Maquilladora went NUTS and it became a wood-shed beating. Finally, sensing the match was nearing end, Forbes distracted the ref, as Fatty Jay tried to nail Maquilladora with the Fattiotic Chair, but inadvertantly hit CJB. Carnage ran and laid out Forbes as Maq got the win. After the match, the brawl just got more hectic. As the show went off the air, Barry Bronx, Dick May, and Kevin Douglas all joined the mayhem.
