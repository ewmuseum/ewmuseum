Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

WFWL's earliest surviving trace is an in-character comment posted 11 January 1996: a wrestler calling himself the Rainbow Warrior announced he and “a long time friend” were joining to “dominate the tagteams,” warning “The Horsemen is coming!!!!!!!!!” The next day, its own operator asked the whole membership to re-mail him after a disk failure wiped his address book, promising a Startup Sheet and a copy of the rules to anyone new, with a first card due that Sunday.

By later that year it had gone quiet: a post looking to restart WFWL “after a six month hiatus from e-wrestling” drew at least one reply (“I would like to join”) and one former member's own assessment, on 3 October 1996: “It was decent but needed a lot of work. The guy who runs it has good ideas and can work at making it great.”

A further recruitment post the following spring still describes the same one-person operation calling for help: “My wrestling federation is desperate for talent and writers!!! I need a lot of help!!! I'm also looking for a Vice President too!!!”

No card, roster, or closure notice of its own survives beyond this correspondence, and its own full name was never stated in any surviving document.
