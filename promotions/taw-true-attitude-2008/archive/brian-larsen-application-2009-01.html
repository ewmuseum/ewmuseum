Brian Larsen Wrestlers Real Name: Brian Larsen Wrestlers In Ring Name: Brian Larsen Height: 6' 1'' Weight: 238 lbs DOB(Date of Birth): 9/23/1986 Hometown: Branson, Mo. Picbase: Eric Young Manager's Name: Devon Manager's Pic Base: Roxxie Chosen Division: Hardcore(Chaosblood Title) Wrestler Alignment: Heel-Neutral Entrance Music: Pretender by Foo Fighters Appearance(In Ring and Out of Ring) In Ring Larsen wears Black wrestling pants, black boots. Out of Ring Larsen wears Jeans, T-shirt, and a cross necklace Finishing Moves(Up to Two): Affirmation- Stunner Moves(At least Ten, No More then Twenty) 1) Spear 2) Reverse DDT 3) Springboard Moonsault 4) Roaring Elbow 5) Swinging Neck Breaker 6) Sling Shot Suplex 7) Swanton 8) Angles Wings 9) Redneck Face Buster (Leg drop from top rope with chair) 10) Spiral Impact (Reverse Neckbreaker Facebuster) (Bio): Larsen broke into the CHZ with Austin Taylor, both graduates of the Plunge, the CHZ's very own training operation. Austin was injured and Larsen went solo. Having, a hard working attitude and a thirst to prove himself Larsen continued to rise in the CHZ. Skylar Riggs took Larsen under his wing and brought him into the group Redneck Express. After a knee injury Larsen came back but strayed away from the remnants of the Redneck Express and took a personal vendetta against the Final Solution. Larsen later hurt his back and when he came back joined the New NRT. After Injuries and kidnaping the group feel apart and Larsen kept fighting the good fight. Then a few months ago Larsen disappeared. Now Brian has returned more focused and with a few new moves to his arsenal.
After Brian jumped right back into the thick of things, doing his best to help carry the CHZ banner, everything changed with the blink of an eye. Enter Lord Riggs, who somehow managed to reconstitute Laura Duncan, once thought dead following a supposed car accident. With Laura being almost within Brian's grasp once more, after seemingly being ripped out of his life forever, he made a "deal with the devil" to get her back. Now Larsen is a member of Covenant, and has been named by Riggs as the heir to his throne. And with both Riggs and Sky Severs sharing their expertise and insight with Larsen, the kid once thought of a "the boy next door", has quite possibly become the most dangerous of them all.
Since moving on from CHZ Larsen, has been moving from organization to organization trying to capture glory that he feels was stolen from him elsewhere.
