Suddenly out from behind the curtains walks The Bombshell of Manhattan. Stopping at the end of the stage. Raising her right hand up and kissing her index fingertip before pointing outwards to the fans on the right and slowly moves her hand straight across until she pointed to the left. Starting to head down the ramp way. Clapping all the hands of the fans on the right side half way down the steel ramp before turning her back towards the ring and clapping all the hands of the fands on the left until she reached the end of the ramp. Spinning around to face the ring and smiled. Rushing forward and sliding underneath the last rope on her knees and swings her hair back from her face and slowly stand up to her feet on at a time and moves towards the ring ropes. Pointing that same index finger outwards towards those fans as she smiled warmly as the fans cheered out. The diva turning around and starting to warm up waiting for her opponent.
