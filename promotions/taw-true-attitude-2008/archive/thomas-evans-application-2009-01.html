Thomas Evans Wrestlers Real Name: Thomas Evans Wrestlers In Ring Name: Thomas Evans Wrestler's Nicknames: "The Hooligan" Height: 6' 0'' Weight: 226 lbs DOB(Date of Birth): 7/5/1987 Hometown: London Borough of Newham, England Picbase: Charlie Hunnam as Pete Dunham Chosen Division: Hardcore(Chaosblood Title) Wrestler Alignment: Heel-Neutral Entrance Music: I'm Forever Blowing Bubbles by Cockney Rejects Description of your entrance: "I'm Forever Blowing Bubbles" by Cockney Rejects begins to play, and almost immediately Thomas Evans emerges from backstage. He is singing along with the song and clapping his hands in the air when the chorus goes "West Ham!" When he reaches the ring he rolls under the bottom rope, and quickly gets to a standing position looking over his opponent with a smile, and then making his way into a corner still singing along.
Appearance(In Ring and Out of Ring) Thomas is usually wearing a jean jacket with a West Ham Crest on the right sleeve. Underneath the jacket is the Claret and Blue colors of his West Ham United Jersey. He is usually wearing either loose fitting jeans or cargo pants, and steel toed boots.
Finishing Moves(Up to Two): Curb Stomp - Standing reverse Indian deathlock surfboard with a head stomp. (Ala Super Dragon)
Moves(At least Ten, No More then Twenty) 1) Punches 2) Kicks 3) Stomps to various body parts, usually on a downed opponent 4) Forearms and Elbows to the face of opponent 5) Headbutts 6) Combination of 3 European Uppercuts 7) Multiple Suplex Variations 8) Arm Trap Cross-Legged STF 9) Rear Chin Lock 10) Piledriver Variations (Bio): Thomas (Tommy to his brothers) Evans is the youngest of three brothers. His family lived in a small flat on the East side of London. His parents were both West Ham supporters, and taught their sons the same ideals. Thomas' dad died when he was seven so he spent most of his life raised by his mom, and his two older brothers. He learned how to fight early on in life, and his brother took notice of this, they decided to take Thomas to a local wrestling show, and he instantly took a liking to it. When he was old enough Thomas began to train and became a huge hit with his brawling style. Thomas Evans will forever be a West Ham supporter until the day he dies.
