Thomas Evans is seen backstage leaning against the wall of the Staples Center parking garage. He is dressed in a West Ham United home jersey, and a pair of torn up blue jeans, and scuffed up boots. He is smoking a cigarette hanging loosely from his bottom lip, and with his right hand he is holding his cell phone up to his right ear.
“Yeah . . . place is a shit hole . . . sluts are on every corner, and I’m pretty sure TAW signed a couple too.”
As Thomas finishes saying that one half of TAW’s double trouble of TAW interviewing Lilly Rose walks up next to him. She smiles and pulls out a cigarette and turns towards Thomas.
“Hey do you have a light?” Thomas nods his head, and pulls out his West Ham lighter, and hands it to her. “Are you Thomas Evans?” Thomas nods as he pulls the phone away from his ear. “Yeah . . . how much?” Lilly raises an eyebrow. “How . . . much . . . for . . . what?” Thomas looks at her. “For a little in out . . . you are obviously looking for a little throw in the sheets.” Lilly glares at him and throws his lighter back into his face, and Thomas shrugs his shoulders.
“The fuck?” Thomas puts the phone back up to his ear, and slides the lighter back into his pocket. “Sorry, just got distracted by some slut . . . I don’t know if she works here, but like I said, I think they hire them.”
Nikki Sixx comes walking but a couple seconds later looking around. “LILLY!? . . .” She waits for an answer then yells again. “. . . LILLY, WHERE ARE YOU!?” She then turns towards Thomas. “Hey have you seen . . .” Thomas pulls the phone away from his ear again. “Fuck you I am on the phone.” Nikki glares and storms off muttering something about a spork, and Thomas goes back to the phone.
“Yeah, my match is in a few minutes. . . I will talk to you later.” Thomas hangs up the phone and looks around the parking lot. “. . . Place is a still a shit hole.” -The scene goes back ringside with Felix Anthony and Michelle Tanner- Felix-Welcome to TAW...-SLAP- Michelle-Shut UP...WELCOME TO TAW INFECTION. Starting to head down the ramp way. Clapping all the hands of the fans on the right side half way down the steel ramp before turning her back towards the ring and clapping all the hands of the fands on the left until she reached the end of the ramp. Spinning around to face the ring and smiled. Rushing forward and sliding underneath the last rope on her knees and swings her hair back from her face and slowly stand up to her feet on at a time and moves towards the ring ropes. Pointing that same index finger outwards towards those fans as she smiled warmly as the fans cheered out. The diva turning around and starting to warm up waiting for her opponent.-

Felix-YOU CAN'T SEE......-SLAM- -Before he can finish the monster brings a monsterious back fist into his face sending him down hard. He grabs him by the back of the head and gets him up then slams him down hard into the F5 and then tosses him out of the ring. He then turns his attentions to the girls. He grabs Ember and hits a chokebomb, and then goes for Sunny and slams her down hard with a Sitout backbreaker rack drop, letting our a roar.-
Michelle-OH MY GOD, ANYONE, ANYONE AT ALL! -What I've Done hits as the huge Monster has already gotten out of the ring letting out a roar of approval. He thrusts up his arm as Loveless makes his way to him. He does not make it and the man vanishes through the crowed. Instead of chasing him he slides into the ring and is handed a mic, he first checks on the girls and then he waves towards the rampway and Orphan, Allister, McKenzie and Chaos make there way on the rampway.-
Alexander Loveless-First off I would like to like to say sorry for Infection to start off like this. With the new security team we should get rid of our "Monster" problem. I have gotten word that we may possibly have some info on who he is, and why he has come, but until I can verify it, I am not going to say one way or another. Now, get some help down here, and Orphan, you and your team get to work. I wont that man in my office by the end of the night.

The camera fades in to show a limo driving into the backstage parking lot area. It is black with red flames going down the side. The limo stops and the driver gets out and walks to the back of the limo. He opens the door and steps back. A man exits wearing a black mask with red flames, like the limo, and matching pants and a black shirt that says "Nos" on it. He takes a look around.-
Limo Driver- Sir, this is your destination. Nos- Did I say you can speak? You're fired! Get lost you bum! The limo driver gives him a bad look and walks back to his door and drives the limo away. Nos walks to the entrance to the locker room area. There is a backstage worker there holding a list.
Staff Member- And you are? Nos- Who am I? Who are you? Staff Member- Sorry, my job is to let only wrestlers in to the arena, so I need your name...
Nos- Read the shirt! Staff Member- Oh, you're Nos...well how do like it so far? Nos- I don't like it much...the staff members are idiots! But I guess you are all just like the fans, IDIOTS!
Nos looks straight at the camera. Nos- Yes, all of you! I am sick and tired of seeing good wrestlers being turned into crap trying to "impress the crowd." I can assure you that I will not risk myself for you people! I am here to become champion and that is the only reason I am here, so next week, get ready! Nos has arrived!
Nos steps into the building and begins to walk down the hall. The camera zooms back and fades back to Michelle ringside.-
Michelle-It would seem I am still alone, so lets just get this show going. The next match is gonna take place outside the Staples Center on the street and will have no offical announcement, only Thomas, Larsen and the ref. So lets get this thing going.
-The camera cuts to the street and Thomas is at one end and Larsen is at the other, Steven Jones, the ref is in the middle. He calls for the bell and the two men make there way towards each other. Thomas begins looking around for something as Larsen is focused on going after Thomas. Thomas smiles as he looks inside a garbage can and pulls out a lead pipe and then begins towards Larsen with eyes focused on him, and the same can be said about Larsen. They finally meet in the middle and the ref tries to explain the rules, instead Thomas uses his free hand and punches Larsen right in the face, he brings the pipe into Larsen's Stomach sending him to his knees. He sends a couple elbows to Larsen's head and then sends the point of his elbow right into the top of his head. He brings him up just long enough to send his head right to the bridge of Larsen's nose and the blood bursts out of it, as he falls holding it Thomas goes for the pin, dropping the pipe.-
1... 2.kickout Michelle-Holy crap this match is brutal already and if it was a First Blood Match Thomas would be the winner, but with the kickout it would seem that Larsen wants some more punishment.
-Thomas does not seem happy but he gets up and goes to grabs the pipe, but as he turns around he is speared down hard by Larsen, sending the pipe flying out of his hand. He begins to fire off lefts and rights down on Thomas as the blood streams from his nose. He keeps punching. At one point he goes down with a hard left and Thomas moves his head just enough to where it slams into the concrete. The moment of hesitation allows Thomas to reverse the possition and comes down with a couple punches. He gets up and looks down and begins stomping on different parts of Larsen's body. He goes down for another pin.-
1... 2...kickout. Michelle-Larsen kicked out again, his hand has to be killing him, but he has not been able to get much offense in this match after the busted nose.
-Thomas gets back up and stomps on Larsen some more. All of the sudden he stops and the camera spins around, he has a metal spork sticking out of his neck, and Nickie and Lilly are standing there. Nickie flips off Thomas and Lilly giggles and they run off. Thomas pulls the spork out of his neck and turns around to a thunderious kick to the nuts by Larsen who it looked like meant to go for a kick to the leg but missed. Larsen goes for a quick pin when Thomas finally falls.-
1..kickout. Michelle-Not much of a move there, but Larsen got something out with the spork attack by our very own Nickie and Lilly. He will have to learn not to mess with the twin terrors of TAW.
-Larsen is pushed off by Thomas and Thomas makes his way to his feet. He still has the spork in his hand and as Larsen goes for him, he brings it up takes it against the forehead of Larsen. The blood begins to trickle down Larsens face. He drops the spork and then hits a release german suplex and as Larsen lands he walks over and grabs his arms. He brings his arms back and then places his foot on the back of Larsen's head. He looks around as the ref warns him against the move, but he tells him to bugger off, and then slams his head down into the concrete as he relases his arms hitting the Curb Stomp. He goes down and rolls Larsen over and goes for the pin.-
1... 2... 3... -The ref raises Thomas's arm, who looks slightly satified, but then his hand goes to the back of his neck and the look changes. He stands over Larsen and spits on him.-
Michelle-Thats it, Thomas was dominating for the majority of the match and his English background seems to helped him in this match, but I'm getting word that we have something going on backstage with Ember.

Ember-STOP...JUST STOP. I had her, I had the match won, I want a rematch..NO I DEMAND A REMATCH! I will not let my first match be fucked up this way. Monster or no monster.

Ember-Get out of my way, I need to talk to one of the GM's. I want my rematch next week. Orphan-Well I guess I should let you know, it's already been signed, they seem to have the same idea you did. Next week you and Sunny will go one on one in a no DQ match, so as you said Monster or no Monster, there will be a winner.

Ember-Fine, I'll go for now, and if that Monster is watching, If it gets involved I'll do whatever it takes to take it out.

Felix Anthony: I need to get back out there. I can't let my fans down. Tiffany Tomas: Felix, I think it's best if you don't go out there. You took a big hit, and besides you don't have any fans.
Felix Anthony: My fans paid good money to see me. I'm going back out there. Tiffany Thomas: For your sake Felix, you should take the night off. I don't care if you want to go back out there, or not. I am ordering you to take the rest of the night off.
Felix goes to say something, but Tiffany stops him. Tiffany Thomas: Don't you say another word. It's final. Felix frowns. Felix: Ok, if you say so. Tiffany Thomas: Good.
