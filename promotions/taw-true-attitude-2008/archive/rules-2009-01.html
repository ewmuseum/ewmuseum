TAW Rules

TAW---TRUE ATTITUDE WRESTLING---Rules WELCOME TO TAW RULES 1) Perhaps the most important, HAVE FUN AND DON'T MAKE ME MAD. What I mean by this is the following- WINNING AND LOSING IN THIS FEDERATION IS ALMOST OF NO IMPORTANCE. What's most important in this fed is how you handle your character. The reason for this is simple, how you handle your character is what wrestling is all about. How many times have you seen The Rock lose a match? Whether you notice it or not, he loses A LOT of matches... but why does it seems like he wins so much, and why does the crowd love him so much if he does lose? Its because he handles his character well. His roleplays are so good, wrestling fans don't even remember or pay attention to his loses. That goes for any good wrestler out there... and so I want it to be with you guys. I'm not saying that if you join you are gonna lose a bunch of matches, but if you do lose some, find a way to use those loses to your advantage. If you just start complaining to me about wanting title shots and so on, all you will do is make me mad. Title shots and title reigns will come, in time, for everyone, so don't be mad when I don't turn you into Bill Goldberg, it WON'T work towards your advantage.
2)Remember QUALITY over QUANTITY, and what I mean is if you carry on for ten pages and do not make sense or you babble and constantly repeat yourself to make your rp look longer it will be notice. A great rp can be one page, but it is very rare. I have nothing against Quantity as long as it's not wasting anyones time, Please I am very very fair and open minded but at the same time I am not stupid so be smart, creative and innovative and there shouldn't be any reason you fail in this fed. Don't quote any other wrestler's catch phases (from this fed or any other fed, e-fed or real, BE ORIGINAL).
3) I want A LOT of segments each week, I want a lot of storyline, BUT matches will be determined by rps and will never be story lined, unless both people in the match agree and your in a established feud, and never the last match for the feud.
A)If you want your person to die, just contact me before you do so. 1)You absolutely must "Roleplay" on the Roleplay board. Failure to do so may result in the automatic loss of a match. If this lack of Roleplays continues, you will continue to lose matches.
2)Do not complain if you lose, everyone loses. If you complain, your character will be suspended or terminated (depending on the situation). Simply participate and increase your chances of winning, but do not cry to me if you lose... it will not be tolerated.
3)Never use anyone else's character in a roleplay unless they e-mail me saying you have their consent or you are in a stable with that person. Never use any staff member without consulting a staff member first, like I said we are open to all kinds of crazy shit. Do not blow up, burn down or totally destroy any part of the fed unless you write me and we agree that it will make absolute sense(Do note that any destruction caused to the fed may lead to having to cancel a show and a suspension in order to carry out the story line)
4)If you do not keep up your character's existence here in the TAW, I reserve the right to use him as a jobber until you either participate, officially quit TAW or wait until I terminate your character.
5)TAW ONLY ACCEPTS ORIGINAL CHARACTERS. Which means NO WWF, TNA, ECW OR ANY OTHER "PRO WRESTLING" CHARACTER. This is simply because I have seen how those characters are usually handled and it hasn't been very good. Also, we see them at least twice every week on TV, that’s enough. When you're more original it makes it more fun for the entire fed. And know that I am a huge Independent fan, I have no problem with a character marking out to a real character just don't duplicate his/her style, moves or catch phrases, and know that all characters are real.(7'1 500 pounds cannot do shooting star presses and 5'8 150 pounds cannot lift 500 pound guys...Very rare exceptions) Also, there is a limit of two wrestlers per applicant, either two singles or one tag team(Please make ONE account on the boards, so if you have a tag team just make the team name, or if it is two singles make it something special to you.)
6)About stables, stables are to have three to six members no less, no more. Interferences by stable mates are to be sent in an e-mail(let's be serious I'm not gonna anything that you don't want with our character) I need to know who will be running the show,if there is a democracy I must know
7)Tag Teams are to roleplay in each others rp's or separate rp's(Note in a tag team match we will add up the combined effort of team members participating into one score.)
8)No attacking or using another person's character in your rps without their permission. 9)Yes, I know, I understand...SEX IS GREAT...BUT keep it off the site. 10)All segments must be approved by me, harming people such as shooting or branding will not be allowed UNLESS approved by both parties and shooting will be a hard sell for me.
11)Champions are REQUIRED to rp each time there posted, not just when there are titles defenses. If a champion no showed twice then they will be stripped of the title.
12)Drama will not be tolerated...first time will be a warning, second time your out. 13)No more then One pic base will be used, no double pic bases, and no one will be able to use the same pic bases as Staff.
Well, that's it, for now. If you have any questions, feel free to ask. Follow these rules and we will try to make this one of the best feds on the net
Last but not least there is a Roleplay limit of 1 per week, I want to be fair for everyone, because I know that people have lives.
Now that you have read the rules you may go forth, check out taken themes and pic bases and fill out one of our two applications.
