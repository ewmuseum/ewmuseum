ARE YOU READY TO GET INFECTED TONIGHT?!? -Otherworld hits and the scenes from the previous Infection plays. It shows the madman running crazy around TAW's first ever show and destorying everything, then it goes to the action inside the ring, It fades out of the scenes and then goes to the main stage where Pyro's are going off like crazy. On the screen TAW appears as the pyro's die down, the cameras go ringside with Felix and Michelle. Shes looking at Felix for some reason holding back a laugh. Felix looks at her and throws up his arms.-
Felix-WHA....-SLAP- Michelle-WELCOME TO TAW INFECTION!! I am Michelle Tanner and this crack head sitting next to me is none other then Felix "Cry Cry I have no fans" Anthony.
-Felix looks like he is about to say something when all of the sudden he stops and listens to something in his ear peice.-
Felix-I'm sorry but there seems to be something going on in the back. -The cameras go backstage and the Big Monster is back there, throwning things around, no one else is there, which might as well be a good thing seeing as what happened last time. All of the sudden on the scene is Orphan, Allister, McKenzie and Chaos. They surround the Monster and all go for him at once. They get the advantage but he manages to power out tossing all four back. He lets out a roar and takes off the opposite direction as Orphan and co follow close by. They fan out and end up boxing him in near a closed loading dock. Before they can all get at him though the gate opens and he manages to get through without much difficulity and it closes before anyone can follow. They rush towards the nearest Exit to see a 18 wheeler driving off and the Monster in the back letting a out a huge roar. Orphan pulls out his cell phone and dials a number. It rings and Alexander picks up on the other end.-
Orphan-He got away, but we are thinking someone is helping him. Alexander-Great. -The cameras go back ringside as the first match of the night is beginning, George Lee is inside the ring with the mic.-
Michelle-Well we are ready for the first of our two action packed matches of... Felix-The night, we are looking forward to -Slap- Michelle-Don't interupt me. The girls are reeved up after the attack last week, lets hope Orphan's security has gotten rid of him for the night.
-Meanwhile inside the ring George lee has the mic and is ready for the intros.- George Lee-The following contest is sceduled for one fall and is a No DQ match... introducing first from Manhattan, New York, she is the BOMBSHELL FROM MANHATTAN....SUNNY DELIGHT!!
-As the music hits the lights surrounding the stadium dim down almost cutting out the lights as the music begins to play. Instantly pink and black spot lights turn towards the stage. The Music Video playing across the screen showing Sunny Delight in different photo shots wearing her bikini and some in her clothes, and finally some for the males eyes. Wearing all sorts of different colors of lingerie. It also showed the diva entering out, and giving her finisher and trademark move to certain of her opponents. Suddenly out from behind the curtains walks The Bombshell of Manhattan. Stopping at the end of the stage. Raising her right hand up and kissing her index fingertip before pointing outwards to the fans on the right and slowly moves her hand straight across until she pointed to the left. Starting to head down the ramp way. Clapping all the hands of the fans on the right side half way down the steel ramp before turning her back towards the ring and clapping all the hands of the fands on the left until she reached the end of the ramp. Spinning around to face the ring and smiled. Rushing forward and sliding underneath the last rope on her knees and swings her hair back from her face and slowly stand up to her feet on at a time and moves towards the ring ropes. Pointing that same index finger outwards towards those fans as she smiled warmly as the fans cheered out. The diva turning around and starting to warm up waiting for her opponent.-
George Lee-And her opponent from Washington DC, she is THE FLAME OF WASHINGTON....SHE IS EMBER!
-This Fire Burns by Killswitch Engage begins to play as Ember makes her way down to the ring smiling and then slides into the ring and throws her hands into the air as the fans cheer.-
Felix-LETS GET THIS THING ON!! Michelle-I should slap you. -Back inside the ring, ref Sabrina Wilson, was calling for the bell as Ember runs right for Sunny. She spears her down hard and begins to rain down fists. Sabrina counts to five and Ember does not let up, Sabrina pulls Ember off Sunny and yells at her. Sunny is back to her feet before Sabrina lets Ember go. She looks at both and restarts the action. They tie up in the middle of the ring but soon the heavier Sunny gets the advantage and hits a DDT. She goes up to the top and hits Ember with a Moonsault. She goes for the pin.-
1.. kickout. Michelle-Oh that was a impressive move by Sunny, but too early for the win. Felix-It was a explosive -slap- Michelle-I'm talking Felix. OH MY GOD WHAT A MOVE. -Inside the ring after Sunny gets Ember back up she goes off the ropes and as she goes for the hurricanranna Ember hits the Matrix Dodge and then recovers and grabs Sunny's leg and goes for a Victory Roll. She kicks out before Sabrina can even go down to make the count. As they get up Ember grabs her wrist and hits a Rope-aided backflip into an arm drag. Ember quickly gets back up and goes to the top and hits Sunny with a crossbody and goes for the pin.-
1... 2..kickout. Felix-A near win by Ember there. I wonder who will w..-SLAP- Michelle-This one was almost done, these girls are giving it there all. -Meanwhile back inside the ring Ember grabs Sunny, but Sunny grabs her by the waist and spears her through the middle rope, Sabrina slides out of the ring and checks on them. Ember is the first to get to her feet as she staggers over to the bell man's seat and grabs a empty seat. She smacks the ground and then metions for Sunny to get to her feet, when she does, she levels her with a HUGE chair shot and then slides her back into the ring and goes for the pin.-
1... 2... 3! -Sabrina calls for the bell and she raises Ember's arm.- George-AND YOUR WINNER....EMBER!! Felix-And thats one is in the books and we FINALLY have a winner of a womens match here in TAW. -SLAP SLAP SLAP-
Michelle-Sexist Pig, what are you trying to say? You wanna call a main event here in TAW? Another remark like that and I'll beat the hell out of you.
Felix-WHA-SLAP- -The camera's go backstage where Thomas Evans is sitting against a wall backstage of The Staples Center. He has a cigarette hanging from his bottom lip, and is dressed to compete wearing his jean jacket with a West Ham crest on the sleeve, with a solid black undershirt on, black cargo pants, and his black steel toed boots. Sitting next to Thomas on the ground is the lead pipe he used in his street fight against Larsen the week before. Lilly Rose and Nickie Sixx slowly walk up to Thomas and look him up and down and with a sigh Nickie begins to speak.-
“Hey Thomas . . . we were told to apologize for our actions last week . . .” -Thomas looks up at them and raises an eyebrow, taking a long drag from his cigarette. Lilly then speaks up too.-
“Errrrr . . . yeah Thomas, sorry about that, how’s your neck?” -Thomas slowly stands up, dragging the pipe up with him, and he pulls the cigarette away from his lips and blows a mouthful of smoke into Nickie and Lilly’s faces.-
“Fine.” -They both look a little irritated at him but they sigh and nod, and Nickie waves the smoke away from her face before opening her mouth again.-
“Well what are your thoughts on the TLC match tonight?” -Thomas shrugs his shoulders.- “A fight is a fight, and I plan on winnin’. Although my beloved Hammers may have just tied Arsenal this past weekend, a draw should not take place in my match tonight. Some people would say the Hammers don’t even have a chance of winning this coming weekend, but no one will say anything like that about me tonight.”
-Lilly smiles and giggles a little bit.- “Well Thomas, lets hope you can win tonight, regardless of the rules, since you don’t seem to need them, or use them.”
-Thomas gives Lilly a smug smile.- “Can I ask you two a question?” -Nickie and Lilly both nod, but are obviously a little confused by this.- “So when you two are going at it, like is obvious you two do, who gets to wear the strap on?”
-Their jaws drop and they both glare at him, and Thomas just smiles flipping them off before walking away dragging the pipe behind him. The camera then goes to another backstage area where Sunny is seen after he fight with Ember. She has a busted lip and is limping as she makes her way down the hallway to Alexander's office. She knocks on the door and a couple seconds later Alexander opens it up. He looks down at the smaller women and raises an eyebrow.-
Alexander-Can I help you Sunny? Sunny-Yes you can, next week I want a rematch. I don't think it's fair the way she won, I knew the match was no DQ and all but she used a weapon, I was not ready for that, if I had been I promise you it would of turned out differenly.
Alexander-So what do you want me to do about it? Sunny-Book another match for next week. Ember vs Me in some sort of match, then I'll know I can use whatever I wanna use,
Alexander-Fine, then next week you and Ember, face to face in a Hardcore match, no rules and the only way to win is by pinfall. The only thing the ref will be good for is to count the one two three.
-Sunny smiles and walks away as the camera cuts back ringside.- Michelle-Looks like the girls are gearing up for round three, so make sure to tune in next week.
Felix-If I'm hearing right production is saying that Alexander is on his way to the ring. -What I've Down hits and Alexander walks down to the ring, mic in hand. He looks around as the fans cheer. He brings the mic to his lips and begins.-
Alexander-Alrighty, first things first, I am getting things settled and will be announcing some things about the Monster after this match, so once it's done don't go anywhere. Now as for the match, as announced it will be a TLC match, Tables, Ladders, and Chairs, but the winner will be the person who can pin the most people. The match will win when either all other opponents are pinned OR if someone gets enough pins that no one can match, and if I'm looking right that would be a single person needing three pins to out right win this match. Thank you and enjoy the main event.
-His music hits again as George Lee gets into the ring and takes the mic. Alexander leaves the ring and vanishes into the back.-
Felix-Wow what a match we have for the main event, I won...-SLAP- Michelle-It's obvious, Thomas is gonna win, he's the best. -The techs begin to set up the ladders, chairs and tables and once there done Lee begins to announce.-
George Lee-The following contest is a ELIMINATION TABLES, LADDERS, and CHAIRS match to determine the Ranking in the BioHazard title picture. Introducing first from Trenton, New Jersey, weighing in at 315 pounds, he is BURNSIDE!!
-Johhny Marconey comes down the ramp with Bill Burns sahdowing him the whole way down. As they get to the ring Bill starts to roll his sholders and hop on his feet stretching for the match.-
George Lee-Introducing next from London Borough of Newham, England, wieghing in at 226 pounds, he is "THE HOLLIGAN" THOMAS EVANS!!
-"I'm Forever Blowing Bubbles" by Cockney Rejects begins to play, and almost immediately Thomas Evans emerges from backstage, pipe in hand. He is singing along with the song and clapping his hands in the air when the chorus goes "West Ham!" When he reaches the ring he rolls under the bottom rope, and quickly gets to a standing position looking over his opponent with a smile, and then making his way into a corner still singing along.-
George Lee-Introducing next from USA TOWN, USA, and weighing in at 256 pounds..he is INFERNO!!
-Inferno walks out on stage with Pyro going off at the start of the song. He makes his way down to the ring staring intently adjusting his shoulder pads.-
George Lee-Introducing next from Branson, Mo, weighing in at 238 pounds, he is BRAIN LARSEN!!
-His music hits and Larsen seems pumped for the match. His eyes never leaving Thomas. He gets to the ring and slides in and stares at him. He pounds to him saying something then slaps his hands three time.-
George Lee-Now Introducing last from Little Rock, Arkansas, he is weighing in at 180 pounds, he is NOS!!!
-The music begins as Nos walks out and begins to walk down to the ring. He enters it while boos fill the arena and begins to stretch for the match. Steven Jones gets into the ring and explains the rules to all five men, and he calls for the bell.-
Michelle-Wow, that was one long intro session. Felix-But it sure did hype up MY fans....-SLAP- Michelle-How many times do we have to tell you, you have no fans. Felix-The champ is here bit -SLAP SLAP SLAP SLAP SLAP SLAP- Michelle-What was that? -Whatever Felix was about to say could not be heard as we see the men eyeing each other preparing there first move. All of the sudden someone jumps the barricade with a steel chair and it's none other then Devon. She slides the chair into Larsen who goes right after Thomas. He swings the chair at him but it gets knocked back into his face as Thomas swings for the fences with the pipe. Larsen staggers backwards dropping the chair then out of the ring. Burnside and Inferno turn there attention on Nos, who got both of them knocked out cold the week before. The close in on him in the corner when all of the sudden Thomas comes out of nowhere laying the pipe into Burnsides side and he crumples. He looks at Nos, and looks at him and then at Inferno and Thomas shrugs and tells him he's on his own, and slides out of the ring. He grabs a table and begins to set it up, but he's not done, he grabs a second and sets it up right next to it. Inside the ring, Nos has managed to get out of the corner but was still being backed against the ropes. He nearly slips on the chair Devon had brought in and he picks it up and in a suprising move tosses it to Inferno, he catches it, but then he also catches it crashing into his skull as Nos hits a superkick unto the chair. Inferno staggers backwards and hits the ropes. Nos goes off the opposite ropes and hits a huge crossbody which sends both to the outside apron, they both hold on as Thomas dodges out of the way. His tables set up right under Nos and Inferno. Thomas gets back to work as he walks around the ring to where Larsen was dumped out at, but is suprised when he isent there. All of the sudden he slides out from under the ring behind Thomas as nails him with a chair shot sending him down hard. On the apron Inferno and Nos are fighting it out when out of nowhere Burnside sends a big boot into Inferno's face, Nos quickly gets out of the way as Inferno goes crashing threw both tables. Nos quickly goes up and comes off with the Nos-atifactsion hitting Burside, sending him halfway across the ring.-
Michelle-This match is brutal. Felix-We've seen Tables and Chairs so far and I think Nos has something planned for the ladder.
-Nos slides out of the ring and grabs a ladder and sets it up. He goes up one end and Larsen goes up the other, as they reach the top they look at each other and then begin to trade punches. Nos gets the advantage being able to throw punches faster and then slams Larsens head into the top rung, turns him around slowly and ends a couple of forearms into his chest and then tosses him off the ladder, by this time Thomas was already on his feet, he watches the blows being traded and he grabs the chair. Devon is about to do something from behind but he notices her in the corner of his eye and slams the chair down hard into her skull, she crumples to the mat and he looks back up, Larsen is thrown off the top of the ladder and Thomas possitions himself just right, Larsen comes down almost head first and Thomas catches him with the chair with a upward chair shot, his head gushes blood as Thomas looks satified. He goes for the pin and Steven counts.-
1... 2... 3! Felix-Oh my god, where gonna need some help out here. Michelle-Oh he'll be fine, what a move by Thomas with an assist by Nos. Speaking of which what is Nos doing up there.
-Speaking of Nos, he has repossitioned himself on the top rung of the ladder and come off with a reverse Moonsault, he slams down hard unto Inferno who is fifteen foot below. Nos hooks the leg and Steven runs over and makes the pin.-
1... 2... 3! Michelle-Another pin in just the matter of minutes. Felix-Nos and Thomas are tied one for one, and the only way to win out right is for one of them to get Burnside and then the other person.
-Steven slides into the ring to check on Burnside who thinking he is someone else punches Steven with a knockout punch and he goes down. Nos and Thomas enter the ring at seperate ends of the ring and go for each other. They throw lefts and rights when all of the sudden Burnside runs at them, they sperate and Thomas ducks behind and grabs him in a victory roll, but as he does Nos goes up top and comes off with a Moonsault, as O'RLY comes sliding back into the ring, he goes for the count as Burnside's shoulders are now down, but at the same time Steven is back up somewhat and can see Thomas with the pin, they both count.-
1...1... 2...2... 3...3... -They both get up Steven raises Thomas's hand and O'LY tries to raise Nos's, they look at each other and argue for a moment.-
Michelle-Looks like our ranking match as come to an end, but who is number one contender? Felix-Its a hard one to figure out but hopefully the man in charge can answer some questions.
-The cameras go backstage after the heated main event ends and Alexander is seen sitting at his desk. Orphan and McKenzie at one side of him and Allister and Chaos at the other. He looks into the camera and begins.-
Alexander-Thank you for tuning into TAW tonight, as promised we have gotten confirmation on who the monster is, but his motives and who, if anyone, he could be working with are still unknown. The Owner of TAW would like to ensure that we are doing everything in our power to keep the situation under hand. As of right now he has not harmed any fans so we are counting ourselves lucky. Now back to the files regarding the monsters name.
-He grabs them from his table and then once again looks back up at the camera.- Alexander-The monsters name is Doomsday, we sadly do not know of any other information but we will keep you informed as we get it.
-He then looks at the TV screen on his desk where the refs are still arguing about the decision.-
Alexander-Now for the rankings match, we hate to have it end this way, but I have come to a decision, both Nos and Thomas are co number one contenders, and will face off at this months Pay Per View Vengence for Valentines for the BioHazard title. Once again thank you for watching TAW Infection.
-The copyright logo appears as the show goes off the air.-
