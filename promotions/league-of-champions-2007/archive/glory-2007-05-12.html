Salt Lake City, Utah May 12, 2007 Dark Match Results Jay Lethal d. Necro Butcher via Pinfall at 13:57 CM Punk d. Adam Pearce via Submission at 10:23 Match One: Fatal Fourway Chris Hero vs. Teddy Hart vs. Kip James vs. James Storm Match starts with all four in the ring at the same time. Storm and Teddy Hart battle and Hero and James round things out. Very solid opener for this edition of Glory, as all four men represent four teams sitting on top of the tag team mountain here in LoC. Kip James was taken out of the match early on by Teddy Hart with a 450 Splash. Immediately upon pinning Kip, Chris Hero rolled Teddy up for a three count. James Storm spins Chris Hero around, EIGHT SECOND RIDE! One, two, three. That finishes Chris Hero off. James Storm gives America's Most Wanted some much-needed momentum.

Match Two: Tag Team Match AJ Styles and Christopher Daniels vs. Paul Birchill and Lance Cade This match wasn't even close. Styles and Daniels flowed like a well-oiled machine tonight in this one. Birchill and Cade never really clicked, never got time to tag one another in, nor did they get a lot of offense in. Lance Cade got knocked over the top rope by Daniels, which was followed up by a plancha by AJ Styles! STO on Birchill sets up the Best.. Moonsault.. Ever~! Birchill kicks out of it, but he doesn't kick out of the Angel's Wings, giving Styles and Daniels an impressive victory.
