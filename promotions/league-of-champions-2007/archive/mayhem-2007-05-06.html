OF THE K-NOX! -[[ Fade. ]]- Match One: Singles Match Lance Storm vs. Ace Steel [[ Lance Storm is seeking revenge from what Colt Cabana did to him last night on Glory by taking out one of Colt's friends and trainer in Ace Steel. Storm wrenches away on parts of the body, overtaking Ace, and ending the match with the Straight Shooter. Lance Storm takes out one of the Second City Saints with ease. ]]
Winner: Lance Storm via Submission (13:46) [[ After that match we fade from Lance Storm celebrating in the ring and we cut to a pre-taped promo. He removes his sunglasses and hangs them on the collar of his shirt. ]]
Edge: Sunday Night Mayhem.. The little show where people with no futures in Solid Gold and League of Champions come to desperately try to save their careers one match at a time. What a waste of air time! I think, right now, I'm the only reason people's even tuning in! And no.. I'm not here to wrestle.
[[ He smirks. ]] Edge: That's what these other jobbers are here for. Me, I'm here to make something perfectly clear.
[[ Pause. ]] Edge: Bryan Danielson, I want your LoC Championship. [[ Edge is very stern in his delivery. ]] Edge: I've been in LoC since day one, just like you have. I've won matches, beaten some of the best the company offers. I have won World titles that mean twice as much as the World titles you've won. And yet, you think you're some big shot because you're undefeated.. I'd be undefeated too if I got to fight Shawn Michaels, Rob Van Dam, and Beef Wellington every damn week!
[[ Edge rolls his eyes. ]] Edge: You sent out these contracts for title matches, knowing some no name jobbers were going to pick them up, sign them, and think this was their one shot at fame. You didn't send them to people who DESERVE title shots, Danielson! Me, me.. I DESERVE A TITLE SHOT!
[[ His eyes go wild. ]] Edge: Jim Cornette has made a match next week.. Kurt Angle versus Christian Cage. Don't get me wrong, there's no dissention in the ranks among Sports Entertainment Xtreme, there's no bad blood brewing between the greatest tag team in the history of professional wrestling.. I just want a shot at the World title. A title shot that is long overdue.

If you're the man who cares about the wrestlers and their opinions.. You'll be on the show. You'll listen to me! You'll then GIVE ME A TITLE SHOT!
[[ The veins in Edge's neck are very noticeable. ]] Edge: And when I get that shot, I WILL become the LoC World Heavyweight Champion.. Because I am.. The Rated R..
[[ Points to himself. ]] Edge: ..Superstar. [[ Fade. ]] Match Two: Ladies Tag Team The Dangerous Angels versus Portia Perez & Tiana Ringer [[ Fast paced, stiff contest that the Dangerous Angels dominate with their superior skills and experience. Tiana Ringer puts up a hell of a fight, working Allison Danger's back when she gets the opportunity. Tiana and Portia tag in and out repeatedly. Allison Danger manages to catch Portia off guard with the Lovelace Choker and makes the hot tag to Sara Del Rey. Sara Del Rey owns Portia with power slams and a series of vicious Yakuza kicks. Portia tags in Tiana Ringer quickly and Del Rey leaves her laying with the Royal Butterfly! As soon as Del Rey gets up, Portia is in the ring and throws a clothesline but Sara ducks it, release German suplex and Portia lands right on her head! Her neck has to be broken! Sara tags in Allison and she immediately puts Tiana Ringer in the STF and pulls back on it for the submission victory! ]]
Winners: The Dangerous Angels via Submission in 14:38 -[[ Tiana and both of the Dangerous Angels, even, circle around Portia and begin checking on her. She's not moving. EMTs are deployed from the back with a stretcher. Once the full extent of Portia's injuries are known, everyone will be informed. ]]-

Match Four: SGW Television Title Jimmy Jacobs vs. John Walters [[ Lacey isn't with Jimmy tonight. He's on his own. John Walters wrestles Jimmy to the mat and completely schools him. John Walters goes for the Hurricane DDT early on but Jimmy slips out of it and gets a roll-up for two. John Walters dominates Jimmy in such a convincing fashion that the fans actually get heavily behind Walters, sensing a title change is going to happen for the first time on Mayhem. Jimmy bails to the outside and Walters follows him out and begins beating him like a grown ass man. He slugs away at Jimmy and turns him around to push him underneath the bottom rope but Jimmy kicks backward into John's crotch.. Walters goes down, clutching his balls and Jimmy begins pulling him toward the ring apron.. Walters tries to get up but can't!! The referee counts John Walters out and Jimmy Jacobs has retained the championship! ]]
Winner: Jimmy Jacobs via Count Out in 18:19 -[[ The referee, after the match, goes to check on John Walters only to find out that Jimmy Jacobs tied John's boot laces to the steel supports under the ring!!
