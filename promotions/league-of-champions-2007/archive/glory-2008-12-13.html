Christian hits a DDT and slaps his chest, already getting cocky. Danielson rolls Cage up with a small package, only getting two. Christian goes on the attack again, dropping Danielson to the canvass with a reverse DDT for a two count. Cage picks Danielson up, Irish whips him into the turnbuckle chest first. Danielson backs out of the corner, UN-PRETTIER! NO! Danielson shoves Cage forward. Cage then turns around into a ROARING ELBOW! Danielson goes to the mat and locks in Cage's arms and begins delivering vicious elbows to his temple area repeatedly! Cage, unable to defend himself from the blows, is eventually knocked out from the blows, forcing the referee to stop the match! ]]
Winner: Bryan Danielson via Knockout [[ Danielson's music hits and his arm in raised in victory as he stares down at the unconscious Christian Cage. The fans are popping, singing "The Final Countdown" as it plays. Danielson points to the belt and yells, "BEST WRESTLER IN THE WORLD!" His celebration quickly turns sour as Tyson Tomko runs into the ring and clotheslines Danielson from behind, sending him to the mat face-first. Randy Orton and Edge make their way down to the ring to join in on the attack. Trish enters the ring to help revive Christian from those vicious elbows he took to the temple to end the match. The fans are booing the actions in the ring, starting a mixture of chants of "CENA! CENA!" and "C-M-PUNK!", wanting them to come out and save the day for the LoC World Heavyweight Champion. Christian gets back to his feet and starts hitting Danielson with rights. And now, to a huge chorus of boos, Chris Jericho slowly walks to the ring, unbuttoning his suit coat. The ringleader of this entire thing enters the ring and watches the destruction of his opponent in two weeks as it happens. Edge and Orton grab Danielson by the arms, holding him still. Danielson spits towards Jericho, bringing a devilish smile to his face as he rolls up the sleeves on his dress shirt. Danielson manages to somehow break free from the hold, and he begins hitting forearm shots to anyone in the area! The fans are rallying behind the once hated and despised champion, but the comeback rally doesn't last long as the numbers game finally gets to Danielson. Christian Cage hits the Un-Prettier on Danielson, allowing Edge and Orton to grab Danielson back without a fight. Jericho pulls out a baton, and sizes up his shot.. Jericho cranks his arm back, prepared to swing, then.. "The Fire Burns" blasts and HERE COMES CM PUNK AND JOHN CENA READY FOR WAR! THE FANS EXPLODE AS THE TWO COME DOWN THE RAMP AND INTO THE RING WITHOUT HESITATION! ]]
[[ Chris Jericho quickly hightails it out of the ring with Trish Stratus in tow right behind him. Punk and Cena start clearing house of the members of The Standard to a huge ovation from the crowd. Punk grabs Christian Cage, GO TO SLEEP! CM Punk is snapping on any person he can get his hands on, wanting revenge for his fellow-Second City Saints John Cena clotheslines Tomko and Orton over the top rope simultaneously! Edge goes to escape, but he's caught by Danielson. Edge takes a vicious head-butt and then gets dropped with a Roaring Elbow! Orton grabs Edge and slides him out of the ring as the remaining members of The Standard retreat with no sight of Chris Jericho at all! ]]
[[ Inside the ring now, Cena and Punk shake hands on a job well done. Danielson grabs his title belt and wipes the blood from his lip. Danielson stands there, staring at John Cena and CM Punk, who both offer handshakes to the LoC Champion. Danielson snarls and them and gives them the middle finger, screaming, "I DON'T NEED ANYONE!" Danielson then exits the ring to a mixed reaction after declining the offer to side with John Cena and CM Punk for the second time tonight. If it wasn't for them, who knows if Danielson would have even been able to leave the ring on his own! The show ends as John Cena and CM Punk discuss Danielson's decline to their offer and what their next move is. ]]
