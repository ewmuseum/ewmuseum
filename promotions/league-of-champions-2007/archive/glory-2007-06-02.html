[[ We go to the ring. ]] Match One: Women's Tag Match Trish Stratus and Melina vs. Gail Kim and MsChif The first women's match in League of Champions history did not disappoint whatsoever. It was an eventful match from the second the bell rang to start the match, til the moment the final bell rang to signal the end of the match. Melina and Gail Kim started, and it was a display of some great women's wrestling. The contest was very evenly displayed for the better part of the match until Trish got the one, two, three on MsChif after hitting her with the Chick Kick as Melina downed Gail Kim. Melina and Trish celebrate, gaining momentum going into the women's tournament next week.
Winner: Trish Stratus and Melina via Pinfall (9:49) May 26, 2007 - After Glory Went Off The Air [[ In this previous recorded footage from last Saturday night, we see Lance Storm and Colt Cabana walking down the street, trying to put their grudge behind them. ]]

Match Two: Singles Match Roderick Strong vs. Steve Corino vs. Petey Williams Roderick Strong dominates this match all the way through. Steve Corino's chest is busted open, and blood trickles down thanks to the super stiff chops from Roderick Strong. Strong is a man unlike any other seen in League of Champions besides Bryan Danielson in terms of sheer brutality. Petey gains some offense, and sends Strong reeling out of the ring. Petey goes to work on Corino, setting him up for the Canadian Destroyer. As he goes to flip, Corino blocks it and sends Petey flying over his back, being caught in the air by Roderick Strong, and then given a backbreaker on his way down! Petey rolls out of the ring, leaving Corino to suffer the Gibson Driver for the one, two, three, giving Roderick Strong a win in his LoC debut!
Winner: Roderick Strong via Pinfall (9:25) [[ Backstage we see Davey Richards in the locker room area lacing up his boots for his match tonight. Shawn Michaels walks up to him, taking his attention away from his boots and on him. Richards looks confused as to why HBK is standing in front of him. ]]

[[ Fade. ]] Match Three: Singles Match Kurt Angle vs. Harry Smith Kurt Angle's experience helped him to have his way with the son of Davey Boy Smith. Harry fought back, though, stunning the former World Champion with a series of power moves, turning the tide in the match completely. Angle hits five German Suplexes in a row on Smith, leaving him down and out on the canvass. Angle drops the straps on his tights, signaling the end of this match. He slaps on the Angle Lock, and Harry Smith is doing everything he can to try to get to the ropes or find another way out of the hold. Angle screams "TAP! TAP! TAP!" at him, trying to get this battle over with. Teddy Hart comes sliding into the ring with a steel chair, and clocks Angle in the back of the head! The referee calls for the bell, giving Angle the DQ win. Harry Smith and Teddy Hart continue the beat down on Angle, leaving him laid out on the mat as the fans boo the Tag Champs.
Winner: Kurt Angle via Disqualification (26:42) [[ After the referees finally clear the ring of the mayhem inside of it, we cut to the interview area as Michael Cole is standing by with CM Punk, who is still showing the effects of his dog collar match two weeks ago. Match Four: LoC ELITE Title CM Punk vs. Jay Lethal CM Punk struggled early in the match, showing that he's not fully healed from two weeks ago. The hungry Jay Lethal and his unorthodox offense dominated the better part of the match, getting several near falls on the champion. The will of the champion stays strong, as he continues to battle back against Lethal, sending the challenger reeling back. Lethal dives off the top rope and hits a double axe handle on Punk. Lethal quickly jumps back on the top rope, he poses to the fans.. ELBOW DROP! One, two, NO! Punk kicked out. Lethal argues with the referee about the count as Randy Orton slides into the ring, looking as rough as CM Punk does. Punk turns around, RKO-No, Punk blocks it and sends Orton running into Jay Lethal! Orton falls out of the ring, STO on Lethal! ANACONDA VICE! Lethal quickly taps, giving CM Punk his first title defense as Randy Orton looks pissed on the outside!

Match Five: Non-Title Match Glory Main Event Bryan Danielson vs. Davey Richards Davey Richards and Bryan Danielson have had some battles in the past. Davey Richards is looking to become a permanent fixture on the LoC main roster. Shawn Michaels is accompanying Richards to ringside to keep an eye on Danielson and Ric Flair. The match starts and Danielson lays in some stiff chops on Richards, knocking him down with an European Uppercut. Danielson paintbrushes the back of Richards' head, trying to get him. The match continues with Danielson in control, comfortably. Danielson is getting real cocky, overlooking his opponent. This comes back and bites him as Richards mounts an incredible return, getting a near fall with a back-cracker! Stiff forearms from Richards has Danielson's nose busted open. Double underhook piledriver! The DR Driver only gets a two count! Richards lifts Danielson on his shoulders, military press now.. He's going for Go 2 Sleep 2.0, Flair on the apron! He drops Danielson and goes for Flair, who drops off the apron before Richards can get him. Michaels clocks Flair! Richards turns around to go back to Danielson, SUPER KICK! One, two, three! After Shawn Michaels stole Danielson's move last week, he steals Michaels' Sweet Chin Music to finish off the man HBK accompanied to the ring!
