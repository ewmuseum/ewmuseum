MAYHEM — Bolder, Colorado, 13 May 2007

13 May 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
May 13, 2007 Bolder, Colorado [[ Mayhem opens up inside of the locker room area. Chris Masters is getting changed into his gear, even though he's not scheduled to wrestle tonight. In the locker room with him is MnM, Monty Brown, and Joey Ryan. Masters looks remotely worried. ]]
Chris Masters: Guys, let me ask you a question. [[ He pauses. ]] Chris Masters: Jillian seems to think Jeff wouldn't come back from retirement to attack me. So tell me, when wrestlers retire.. They stay that way, right?
[[ Laughter busts out among everyone but Masters. ]] Joey Ryan: Yeah, right, dude. Johnny Nitro: Yeah, Chris.. Terry Funk first retired in 1983. He still wrestles to this day.
Joey Ryan: Terry Funk? Whoa, in my era, he was just starting out! Johnny Nitro: Shut the hell up, Joey! You're same age as I am! Joey Ryan: Dy-no-mite! [[ Masters runs his hand down his face. ]] Joey Mercury: Besides, it's Jarrett. You ran 'em out once, why not again? Chris Masters: Good point. Real good point. [[ Masters kind of mans up. ]] Chris Masters: Someone get a phone and tell Jeff Jarrett to show his face right now! He won't break this Masterlock! Hey, you, Mark Henry.
[[ Monty looks up from the magazine he was reading. ]] Monty Brown: Me? Chris Masters: Yeah, call Jarrett and tell him to get his ass here right now.. That is, if you don't break your kneecaps first! Fool!
Monty Brown: I'm 'da Alpha Male! I'm Monty Brown! I ain't Mark Henry! Chris Masters: Whatever. [[ Monty reaches into his bag and removes his cell phone. He dials a number. Suddenly, we hear a ring. ]]
[[ And another ring. ]] Chris Masters: Hah, what a terribly awkward coincidence. [[ Masters sees a phone ringing and vibrating on a nearby seat. He goes over to it and checks the caller I.D. ]]
Chris Masters: What? Who's phone is this!? Jeff Jarrett: Mine. [[ GUITAR SHOT! JEFF JARRETT WAS IN THE LOCKER ROOM ALL ALONG! Masters backs himself against the wall, covered in guitar remains. He looks like he's seen a ghost. Jarrett throws a piece of paper down on Masters' chest. ]]
Jeff Jarrett: Read that, Masters. [[ Masters observes the paper and lets out a howling cry. ]] Chris Masters: NOOOOOO! Jeff Jarrett: That's right.. I just so happened to sign an LoC contract. So next week on Mayhem, Jeff Jarrett versus Chris Masters.
[[ He smirks. ]] Jeff Jarrett: The loser leaves wrestling for good! [[ Jarrett puts his sunglasses on. ]] Jeff Jarrett: Choke on that. [[ Fade. ]] Match One: Singles Match Billy Kidman versus Shark Boy [[ This match is a complete slaughter. Kidman pounds Shark Boy from post to post, kicking him while he's down and even blatantly choking him on several occasions. Shark Boy tries to mount a comeback but Kidman isn't having it, raking Shark Boy's eyes and hitting him with a BK Bomb. Kidman gets down and begins punching Shark Boy in the head repeatedly. He pulls him up and hits him with a spinning heel kick. Shark Boy is completely useless at this point, Kidman is just having his way with him. Shark Boy tries to mount another comeback but Kidman tears his head off with a lariat, climbs the ropes and nails a picture perfect Shooting Star Press for the three count. ]]

CM Punk worked most of the match since his other two partners aren't in the greatest of shape. Punk had no problem manhandling the three opponents to much surprise. [[ AMW walk off as the scene fades back to the ringside area. ]] Match Three: Singles Match Tony Mamaluke versus Delirious [[ Shelly Martinez is at ringside for the match, as is Trinity. This match was a solid, back and forth contest between two extremely talented wrestlers. Tony Mamaluke controls a lot of the match with a good combination of mat based offense and high flying maneuvers. Delirious manages to make a comeback and use his ferocity to turn the match in his favor. Delirious toys with Mamaluke for several minutes until Shelly tells Delirious to end the match, at which point Delirious begins running around the ring in circles, screaming before scooping up Mamaluke and hitting him with a Death Valley Driver and locking in a Cobra Clutch Crossface for the submission victory! ]]

Match Four: Tag Team Match Edge and Christian vs. Val Venis and Ron Killings [[ Edge and Christian definitely give the secondary show some star power in the biggest main event we've seen on Glory. Val Venis has a long-standing rivalry going on with Edge and Christian, so he's going right for either of them every chance he gets. Edge and Christian show no rust as a tag team, showing why they've won seven titles together. Each opponent was isolated for long periods of time, allowing EnC to do whatever they wanted. Christian hit the Un-Prettier on Val Venis after Edge hit the Spear. Ron Killings doesn't even try to break up the pin. He just walks to the back, pissed off. Edge and Christian get the easy tag victory as they roll into PRIDE next Sunday! ]]
