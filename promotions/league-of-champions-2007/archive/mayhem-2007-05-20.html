MAYHEM — Kansas City, Missouri, 20 May 2007

20 May 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
May 20, 2007 Kansas City, Missouri -[[ Backstage, we see Rebecca Bayless standing by with Sean Waltman.. We haven't seen Waltman on television in any capacity since his embarrassing loss to Brent Albright in the first Iron Fist rules match.. Waltman is in jeans and a see through black shirt.. He doesn't bother removing his sun glasses for the interview.. Waltman doesn't look too happy at all to be here on Mayhem.. She gets the interview started. ]]-
Rebecca Bayless: ..Hello, Solid Gold fans.. I'm standing here right now with one of the pioneers of SGW's most prominent wrestling style, Sean Waltman! Sean, ever since your series of matches with the former Axis of Evil and your.. Unfortunate loss to Brent Albright a month ago, we haven't seen you on Shock or any other programming.. Why is that?
-[[ Waltman stands there silently for a moment before speaking. ]]- Sean Waltman: ..It was really a combination of things, Becky.. What went on with Jimmy Rave was just a little part of it.. Jimmy Rave is just a little jerk that couldn't get a win on his own to save his life and quite frankly, I'm not sure how the hell he got hired by SGW in the first place..
-[[ Waltman wipes his mouth. ]]- Sean Waltman: No.. It wasn't just Jimmy Rave.. It was the fact that Bischoff knew he had money in his pocket with me and the James Gang reuniting.. Those guys were great talents.. They were two of my best friends in this business but Bischoff allowed that stipulation at Supremacy to stand even though we got screwed over hard by those... Those.. JACK OFFS!!
-[[ Rebecca takes a half-step back as he yells.. Waltman's anger is becoming more straight forward now as he continues speaking. ]]-
Sean Waltman: How the hell do you think it made me feel to know that when Jimmy Rave pinned me.. PINNED ME.. That I cost my best friends their jobs with the greatest company in the world!?
-[[ She shakes her head, she doesn't know. ]]- Sean Waltman: It felt pretty fuckin' bad!! -[[ There's another pause as he puts his hands on his hips and looks down at the ground.. He doesn't look back up as he continues speaking. ]]-
Sean Waltman: What happened with Albright.. I don't care.. I wasn't all there.. I wasn't myself.. As far as I'm concerned.. He can have that one and if he's as big a star as he probably thinks he is, he'll be around long enough for me to get my rematch and we'll see just who the hell is the better man.....
Rebecca Bayless: What are you saying? Sean Waltman: I'm sayin'.. I lost my way but now.. I'm back and I'm ready to get back in the mix.. Ready to say goodbye to all that D-Generation X rehashing.. I feel bad about costing them their jobs.. But maybe it was for the best.. We can do our own thing now and that'll be best for all of us in the long run.. From here on out, it's a brand new start for Sean Waltman..
-[[ She goes to close up the interview when she looks off camera and looks surprised. ]]- Rebecca Bayless: Hey.. What are those guys doing here?! -[[ Waltman looks over himself and the camera pans over in that direction to reveal BJ Whitmer, Chavo Guerrero, Chase Stevens and Andy Douglas entering the building from the parking lot. Chavo and the Naturals are dressed in decent looking dress clothes, while Whitmer is wearing jeans and a WRESTLE! t-shirt.. Waltman mobs up on them, looking pissed. ]]-
Sean Waltman: Hey!! What the fuck are you guys doing here!? -[[ Whitmer puts his hands up. ]]- BJ Whitmer: Easy, bud.. Bischoff signed us up Thursday night. -[[ Waltman looks down at Whitmer's shirt.. He looks disgusted. ]]- Sean Waltman: Yeah, whatever.. It's bad enough that he signed you goons but you got some kinda' balls comin' in here with that shirt on.. And you, Guerrero.. As far as I'm concerned, you can just go to hell!!
-[[ Guerrero looks surprised. ]]- Chavo Guerrero: Why me!? Sean Waltman: 'Cause you had the chance to come to Solid Gold but you took a WRESTLE! contract instead!! And now you're just gonna waltz in here like it's nothin'!? Screw you, boy!!
-[[ The Naturals step up in Waltman's direction. ]]- Chase Stevens: You got something to say to us, too? Sean Waltman: Yeah.. Who the hell ARE you two jack-offs? -[[ Whitmer puts his arm in front of the Naturals, backing them up. ]]- BJ Whitmer: ..Sean, I think we have some kind of misunderstanding here. -[[ Waltman just stares him down.. ]]- BJ Whitmer: And I think this will settle it. -[[ Whitmer removes his WRESTLE! t-shirt and hands it to Waltman.. Waltman looks down at it, disgusted and Whitmer drills Waltman with a big right hand!! The Naturals and Chavo join in and they pin Waltman against the wall and begin ruining him with sickening punches to the stomach and head!! They stomp Waltman into the ground and the Naturals hold him up.. Whitmer gets a running start and BIG BOOTS Waltman right in the face!! Waltman goes limp in their arms and Whitmer motions at the Naturals to lift him back up. ]]-
BJ Whitmer: Drag his ass out of here! -[[ Chavo and the Naturals go outside with Waltman and Whitmer turns and sees Bayless watching this unfold with horror across her face.. Without warning, he just scoops her up over his shoulder and carries her outside as well as she kicks and screams, trying to escape... Once they're all out in the parking lot, we see their destination.. The Naturals have taken Sean Waltman to the DUMPSTER! Chavo opens the dumpster up and the Naturals dump Waltman inside!! Whitmer begins getting closer to the dumpster with Becky over his shoulder. ]]-
Rebecca Bayless: NO!! DON'T!! DON'T!! PLEASE!! -[[ And Whitmer just tosses her over inside!! Chavo shuts the dumpster lid and locks it down. Whitmer turns and wipes his hands off, looking disgusted himself now. ]]-
BJ Whitmer: I always hated takin' out the trash. -[[ Team WRESTLE! all laugh in unison and walk away as we hear Becky crying out from inside her big, green prison. ]]-
Rebecca Bayless: SICK!! THERE'S CONDOMS IN HERE!! EWWWWW!! -[[ What the hell is going to happen with this new WRESTLE! alliance being turned loose in Solid Gold Wrestling!? ]]-
-[[ Fade. ]]- Match One: Singles Match Billy Kidman, Homicide & Gregory Helms versus Team Rainbow Fish & Lance Starr This match was fast paced with lots of solid action between both teams. Chris Kanyon carried his team well, obtaining a majority of the offense on their behalf. Kidman lays a beat down on Lance Starr and leaves him laying with a BK Bomb but isn't impressed and demands that Starr tag out. Starr tags in Shark Boy and Kidman tags in Homicide. Homicide beats Shark Boy, rips his mask and plants him with a stunner. He throws Shark Boy out of the ring and Kanyon takes his place. The match erupts in a brawl with Gregory Helms going outside to take care of Shark Boy again. He throws Shark Boy over the announce table and into the laps of the commentators. Kidman clotheslines Kanyon over the top rope and follows him out, leaving Homicide alone with Lance Starr, Cop Killa' and good night. It looks like Homicide and Kidman won't have a problem coexisting on May 27th!
Winners: Billy Kidman, Homicide & Gregory Helms via Pin Fall in 15:22 [[ We see the James Gang standing backstage, getting ready for Pride later on tonight with Michael Cole. BG James is holding one of The Hart Foundation's tag titles. ]]
Michael Cole: I'm standing here with the James Gang, one of four teams in the big elimination tag team match for the Tag Titles tonight. BG, we'll start with you. You ended up with one of the Tag Titles belonging to Teddy Hart and Harry Smith that Claudio Castagnoli and Chris Hero swiped from them.
[[ BG slaps the belt with a smile. ]] BG James: That's right, Cole. Them Kings of Wrestling came into our yard tryin' to stand in our way of getting a title shot, and we wasn't gonna' take it anymore. Seems like every team in this company wants to get in our way of our one goal of enterin' LoC.
[[ BG looks to the camera. ]] BG James: The James Gang WILL get our hands on the LoC Tag titles tonight in Kansas City! We got this one belt here, but it ain't enough. All we had to do was beat down Claudio and take that belt. If that's how easy it's gonna' be tonight.. Boys, you ain't seen nothin' yet!
Kip James: That's right! The James Gang is in LoC for one reason, to win those titles! We left SGW on terrible terms, came to LoC for a fresh start.. And all we've done since is sit back and be passed over. WELL, NOT ANY MORE! Those Tag Titles belong to us, and if you ain't down with that..
[[ Pause. ]] Kip James: Too damn bad. [[ Fade. ]] Match Two: Singles Match Ron Killings versus Eric Young Eric Young is fresh from WRESTLE!, making his LoC in-ring debut tonight on the show against the struggling Ron Killings. Ron Killings looked real motivated to get on the winning track here in LoC, after weeks of being pushed around and beaten by anyone and everyone. Eric Young had a lot of ring-rust with the lay-off from WRESTLE!, so he wasn't at his top form, not like it'd mattered against the former NWA World Champion. Killings dominated this short match and ended it with the Lie Detector for three.
Winner: Ron Killings via Pinfall (4:31) SGW Shock Footage - Taped 5 / 16 / 07 -[[ We see Eric Bischoff standing in his office.. In his hands, we see he has a small stack of papers.. Bischoff looks into the camera and addresses the viewers at home.. ]]-
Eric Bischoff: Two weeks ago, a challenge was issued by Nigel McGuinness to "The Canadian Crippler" Chris Benoit for a one on one match at SGW's next pay-per-view offering, Total Destruction on May 27th..
-[[ A pause. ]]- Eric Bischoff: I'd like to say that.. As a wrestling fan.. The prospect of this match excites me and I was extremely happy when Chris Benoit accepted Nigel's challenge for the match.. And I'm even more happy that it will be in a Solid Gold Wrestling ring so that it can be appreciated by the greatest fans in the world..
-[[ Bischoff holds up the papers in his hand. ]]- Eric Bischoff: As a matter of fact, this match excites me so much that I'm going to add a stipulation to it.. No, nothing that will hinder the match quality.. I'd never dream of it.. No, you see.. I have such a great amount of respect for these two men that I'm going to offer the winner of the match a contract... And not just any contract.. I'm talking about a contract that guarantees the winner, upon signing it, an SGW Title shot at any time they want it.. Any month, any week, day, hour or minute... Whenever you want it, sign the contract and it's yours.. And that.. Is an Eric Bischoff.. Guarantee.
-[[ He smiles that trademark Bischoff smile as the camera zooms in on the world title contract and we fade out. ]]-
Match Three: Singles Match Sabu versus John Walters Daizee Haze is at ringside with Walters as she didn't make the trip to England with Nana and Rave. Sabu and Walters go back and forth in a stiff slugfest with Walters trying to take over with some wrestling holds, only to find out that Sabu isn't interested in that at all. Sabu beats Walters down, nails him with a drop kick and goes up top only to have Walters bring him down by crotching him.. Walters follows him up for a superplex but only gets two. The two go back and forth a bit longer with Walters having to up his brutality level to keep the upper hand but that's not enough as Sabu hits him with a tornado DDT and follows up with the triple jump moonsault for the three count.
Winner: Sabu via Pin Fall in 12:27 [[ Jeff Jarrett takes our screen over after that impressive victory by Sabu. He has a guitar in hand, wearing his "King of the Mountain" t-shirt and his ring gear. ]]
Jeff Jarrett: Ya' know, ever since that day I left SGDubya', I've been countin' down the days until I came back and got my revenge.
[[ He rubs his chin. ]] Jeff Jarrett: I shoulda' known that Jillian Hall was the last person I needed to trust. Women, women will do nothin' but bring you down and take everything you have away from ya'.
[[ He shakes his head, almost in disbelief. ]] Jeff Jarrett: That's what she did to me. She took everything I had from me. She took professional wrestling away from Jeff Jarrett.
[[ Pause. His face looks expressionless. ]] Jeff Jarrett: And tonight, here on Mayhem, Jeff Jarrett takes back everything that was stolen from him, and more. Chris Masters, son, you've done got yourself in a world o'trouble sidin' with Jillian.
[[ He's fully focused. ]] Jeff Jarrett: I'm takin' back my career you and Jillian tried takin' away from me all those weeks ago.. And on top of that.. I'm takin' YOURS!
[[ Fade. ]] Match Four: Loser Leaves Wrestling MAYHEM MAIN EVENT Jeff Jarrett vs. Chris Masters This definitely is the biggest main event to come out of this joint-Mayhem venture from LoC and SGW. Jeff Jarrett retired from wrestling after Jillian Hall and Chris Masters beat him down. Shortly thereafter, Masters and Jillian left SGW, citing doing the one thing they planned on, then tried their hand at LoC. The LoC journey didn't last long, as Jarrett came back and got this match tonight on Mayhem. Masters overpowers Jarrett to start, but the veteran Jarrett shows why he's one of the smartest people in the game today, getting out of everything Masters applies. Jillian distracts the ref, allowing Masters to low-blow Jarrett. "IT'S MASTER LOCK TIME, BAY-BEH!" MASTERLOCK IS APPLIED! No one breaks the Masterlock! No one! Nothing! JARRETT runs to the ropes, springs off the bottom rope.. one, two, three! Jarrett has beaten Masters!
Winner: Jeff Jarrett via Pinfall (12:42) Fade out. Solid Gold Wrestling - League of Champions
