Marcus Tate: WELCOME SPORTS FANS! Welcome to the first EVER broadcast of International Leauge Pro Wrestling! The first ever edition of Talk Aint Cheap, and I am your host this evening, Marcus Tate.
Well, let's dive right into this thing, shall we? I'll tell you something. This has been quite a hectic week for everyone involved with ILPW here in the front office, new staff, new wrestlers, new everything it seems. People are simply flocking to become a part of ILPW.
But the new RAW president, Lance Avery, has been working around the clock in order to bring you, the fans, what you rightfully deserve. Lance, in just the past two days, has signed five more _top_ stars into the ranks of ILPW. I've been told I can't tell you who their names, but chances are YOU ALREADY KNOW
THESE PEOPLE! Lance has also been in negotiations with a few other big stars who have yet to decide wether they want to come to ILPW or not. Their reasoning is to see if they like us here. So, expect many many more big names in the near future!!
On today's show, we'll be hearing exlcusives from the Hooligans Y2K, Will
Shelton, and not to mention Logan Gunz! But before we get into that, let's take a moment to look at ILPW's first ever card "Monday Night MisConduct"! The show is gonna be a whopper, I tell ya.
One of the matches headlining this mega-event will be a _LETHAL_ Triple Threat match between The Dave, "The Irish Wolfhound" Orson Edmonds and "Sexy" Fletch
Johnson! This is a match between three of the most well-known stars on the ILPW roster today, the Championship Comittee will be watching this one closely.
In the opening match of the ILPW World Title Tournament, Will Shelton will be facing Malice. I'll tell ya, I've had the opportunity to see these guys in action. Right now, I have no idea who will take the Double-You in this one.
And then in the MAIIIIIIN EVENT! A 12 Man Battle Royale featuring RAW's biggest and brightest stars. This aint your grand-parents battle royale neither. This is a Bring Your Own Weapon Battle Royale for the ILPW DEATH-MATCH
CHAMPIONSHIP! This one will be for _the_ craziest SOB in the ranks of ILPW. Who will be able to walk out of that ring the winner? More importantly, who will actually be able to walk out of the ring period! It's really going to be quite a match and I suggest you don't miss it.
One more interesting tidbit here, Lance Avery has told me that he will be holding a special match on the next event after Friday Night. He's telling me that the four people that have impressed him most will be placed into a special stipulation match... I'm interested too, but they don't like giving me information down here.
And now, the moment we have all been waiting for!!!! Let's let one of ILPW's premiere stars, Orson "The Irish Wolfhound" Edmonds open the show!

Dublin I used te know...I wonder if'n they're related? Far as I know, laddie, until ye own some sort o'a copywright...liken' that foofy-froo who used te be Prince...and by then, the name is aboot as important as the namby-pamby music he plays...which is nae a bit, mind ye. I wonder aboot this Vic Valiant, tho...been me experience that men called "Valiant" seldom are...but I'll just have te wait an' see, won't I?
And Sexy Fletch...heh. [chuckles, "posing" for the camera] I never won no beauty contests, lad...but as ye may or may nae ken, Professional wrestlin' is nae aboot who looks the prettiest when he get his face smashed in...it's aboot the men who meet the challenge runnin', and the men who prove worthy o' the mettle.
It comes te this, boyos. Ye've seen how many others, I dinnae care. Ye're former champs? I dinnae care. We're all on equal footin', and ye're gonna hear a little howlin' from the Irish Wolfhound when he gets to work...and pretty soon, it's won't just be the folk at the Dubh Roison who're howlin'
too. [he smiles and winks to the camera] Take a good look, lads and lassies...and take a good listen. May no' be long afore ye're howlin' along too...
[whips his head back and issues a loud Celtic Roar, which seems to echo through the hills as the camera fades]

_______________________________________________________ (In the center of a well-lit room stands a man leaning over a trophy case. He is wearing a green shirt, and his short blond hair shines in the morning sun.
As he sighs and leans back, his well-defined body shows through his clothing. Standing 6'4" and weighing over 250 pounds, he is a force to be reckoned with. His voice slices through the silence before the camera can react. He speaks without moving a muscle...)
Will Shelton: First impressions. The two most important words in all of wrestling. Today, you'll hear my name for the first time. Tomorrow, it'll mean a lot more to you.
(Shelton turns around, and his green eyes seem to lock with yours. His face is relaxed, and yet determined at the same time...)
Will Shelton: In a way, I'm just like the rest of you. Somebody told Lance
Avery I was pretty good at what I do. You don't have to take their word for it...yet. In time, you'll see that somebody was right. But right now, all you see is a face and 254 pounds of body to back it up. And that'll have to do...til Monday.
I'm just like the rest of you in the way that I don't give a damn about any of you, the same way that you wouldn't care if I never took another breath.
That's okay...for now. Today, I'm just a name...but inch by inch, moment by moment, the name "Will Shelton" is finding a special place inside your heads.
You will remember my name. And you will respect it...one way or another. 99% of the people in this federation are strangers to me. Mr. Avery says I'm
"one of the best of the best." He also sweet talked 49 other people the same way. And there's no way in hell that 50 individuals make up the "best of the best." So we'll find out who the men are...and who the boys are.
I don't have to sit up here and pretend to be somebody I'm not. I don't have to scare you or make a name for myself in your head. That happens when you step in the ring with me. So for now...chill out, relax, and enjoy the scenery. But eventually, you'll have to get in the ring with me. And then the scenery changes. You go from the top of the mountain to the center of the ring, flat on your ass while the referee counts 1...2...3. That day is coming for all of you.
(Shelton steps across the room to a pool table, with the balls already racked. He draws a custom-made cue and strikes the rack...) Will Shelton: That day comes first for 11 men, this Monday Night. It's the same match I began my wrestling career in: a huge royal rumble, no rules, and a weapon of your choice. I like the odds. Because like I said, the 11 other men don't know just who the hell I am...which means respect will come in heaping doses, backed by a spiked chair, a broken beer bottle...or anything else creative that I can think of when I wake up Monday morning. There's 12 pounds of gold staring me in the face, waiting for somebody to claim it.
Monday Night, I will leave everyone else in my wake. That belt is mine. And for the rest of you, you'll have to settle for getting your ass handed to you by someone you don't know nor respect.
Actually...that goes for everybody except one. (Shelton leaves the pool table and steps outside on his balcony. The open air blows the curtains aside, and Shelton puts on his black designer sunglasses...)
Will Shelton: Malice. The one man whose name strikes a chord in my mind. The only name that rings a bell in this entire federation.
Not a bad choice for a first round opponent in the most important tournament in ILPW's short history.
You see, none of the other guys will know it...very few of the fans will know it...hell, Avery himself might not see it...but what you will witness on Monday
Night between Will Shelton and Malice will be historic warfare. Over the 16 months that I've had the pleasure of knowing this man...I've never once stared at him from across the ring. But I've watched him work from afar, and competed in a federation where he held the World Title. I know that he has what it takes to win here.
But just how much do you respect me, Malice? I've been your boss, but never your enemy. Monday Night, when the bell sounds and we lock up, will you know what to expect? I do...but you don't.
The truth is, we're extremely even. This contest will go back and forth, back and forth, until one of us finally grabs the upper hand. And when that happens...the end will be near. But it could take all night before that. And after we've taken each other to our limits, we still gotta go out there and rumble. But I've never had a problem with that. Do you?
So what's it gonna be? I know just what I'm going to get from you, Malice. Be prepared to sit back and take it from me. This is for the World Title. Don't screw it up.
Til then...the entire ILPW is on notice. You say, "big deal." But the bad news is, that's exactly what I want you to say. Because you certainly haven't heard the last from me...and I'm just full of surprises.
(Shelton throws down his sunglasses as the sun dips behind the clouds. He disappears within the mansion as darkness engulfs the camera, and everything fades to black..)
_____________________ **** The people love this guys attitude and I can't blame 'em. He's focused on what needs to be done. Let's see how that attitude will do with the malice of
Malice! _____________________ (Nothing quite stirs the emotions like a breath-taking sunrise. A parade of red, yellow, and orange parading across the sky as if they were trumpeteers announcing that a new day has arrived. All things start anew at dawn, forgetting the transgressions of the days before...and opening themselves to the embrace of a new day, full of new challenges. For some people, however, that embrace is all too familiar. For some, the challenge of a new day has become a regular occurence.)
(Standing along a lone highway, steel green eyes focusing in on the horizon and the brightening of the fresh Kentucky morning, is the man known as "The Lone
Enforcer". His broad shoulders partially blocking the spring morning breeze that blows across the lush green fields. A blue bandanna stationed atop his head reads "GUNZ" across the forehead, his black t-shirt is wrapped tightly across the broad expanse of his chest. Across the front of the t-shirt is the
NWC logo, Gunz almost wears it as his scarlet letter...displaying the one utter failing a career that has reached legendary stature.)
(Cold, steel green eyes gaze out over the lush fields. His eyes display the story of a man who has stood on the pinnacle of his sport, a man who has known what it feels like to stand alone...and to thrive while doing it, and his eyes display the pain of a man who has known betrayal. His eyes are, at the very least, an enigma...powerful and deep, yet cold and unemotional.)
(He lets out a brief sigh as he marvels at a prodigious black stallion grazing in the field. His coat and mane shine in the new morning sun as he has chosen to stand alone, away from the herd which partakes of the Kentucky blue-grass less than 100 yards away.)
Logan: I'm not beat...not yet...and not by a long shot. (Logan wrenches his hands together as they drape over a freshly painted, white picket fence. His gaze continues in the direction of the horses which are backdropped by the rising sun.)
Logan: Once again...the panther is on the prowl. The forest and the territory may change, but the results remain the same.
Throughout the length of my career, one thing has remained a constant...once someone enters the forest, there is no turning back. I have built a reputation, an entire career on the fact that there is no one breathing that can make me quit. Indeed, I am the panther...and now it appears I've found yet another forest to prowl.
(Gunz rubs the panther tatoo upon his left arm as he flexes the bicep. His arms are massive, nearly ripping the shirt he's wearing with each contraction of the muscle.)
Logan: ILPW - The International League of Professional Wrestling. A homecoming of sorts for "The Lone Enforcer", finally I've returned to Seattle. The brass and wrestlers of the ILPW may not realize the storm brewing over their small federation...but in due time, they will all come to understand.
I am the "Lone Enforcer", "The Panther", and "The Annihilator"...not nicknames
I've bestowed upon myself, rather monikers that have been given to me as I've cut a path of destruction across these United States. Man after man, opponent after opponent, I've laid to waste some of the best that have ever stepped between the ropes....
(The morning breeze picks up a little, and blows the tied off ends of the bandanna on Logan's head. Gunz pauses for a second, seemingly collecting his thoughts...then continues.)
Logan: Did I defeat them with skill?? No... Did I have to cheat?? Never... Then, how did I do it?? Simple...I defeated them with heart and intensity. Deep within this body burns an eternal fire, a desire...not a desire for the money, or the cute little title belts, nor a desire for the groupies that hang out at the hotels. Inside of me burns the kindling fire of a warrior, a man bound by honor, perhaps the one person in this sport who cannot be bought...cannot be intimidated...and cannot truly be defeated.
For every time that someone has ever put my shoulders to the mat and gotten a three count...I have returned it upon them three-fold. I have rained down on them a fiercness which they have yet to experience since. Not because they defeated me...but, because they thought that they could escape the forest. And that just does not happen.
(Gunz turns from the fence and begins to walk back towards his truck. A 1999
Ford Ranger, blue in color, that sits along the side of the road.)
Logan: I am the panther...within me dwells an animalistic essence. Each time I search my soul, each time I dig deep within the recesses of my spirit...that panther emerges. As I lay in the center of the ring, bloody from a beating, and
I reach deep down inside to find the intestinal fortitude to get back...that's when the panther emerges. Vicious, ruthless, a predator by nature...that panther has propelled me to heights that I've never dreamed possible.
So, the question everyone wonders is, "Logan, why do you do it?". Why do I continue in this sport that has cast me aside because I refused to "toe the line"? The answer to that is simple, my little sister Misty, and the fans that pay their hard earned dollar to see me compete.
For Misty, I've tried to instill in her that you don't have to "go with the flow" to be successful. I've attempted to teach her that one person can stand alone, on their own two feet, and accomplish whatever they can dream. I've never asked for anyone's help, as I battled the PoA, the Elite, and the Upper
Echelon...I did it on my own. I can, and I will stand on my own two feet. The path of annihilation will continue to be cut, straight through the heart of the
ILPW, and I will do it while remaining "The Lone Enforcer".
As for the fans...they are my backbone. During my days in the NWC, I became a cult-hero of sorts. For some reason the people took to a kid from the streets of Atlanta, a kid with no charisma, no cute gimmick...only a iron will, and the willingness to stand on his own. I do this for the fans, because they are my backbone.
(Logan opens the door of the truck, a slow *ping* from the door being open pervades the air.)
Logan: So, here I am...Seattle, I'm back. This Sunday I must start all over again. The name is Logan Gunz...and at "Monday Night MISCONDUCT", be prepared to witness the panther on the prowl.
Doom On You...Scott Richards, because for eternity you will be remembered as the first in a long line of victims that must, and will, fall to the wayside.
(Logan climbs into the truck and closes the door. The engine starts and the truck pulls into the roadway and tears down the road. As it pulls away, the camera notices the license plate which reads "PANTHER". An image over-lays the truck as it pulls away, the black face of a panther. The panther stares coldly into the camera, then roars as the camera fades to black.)
