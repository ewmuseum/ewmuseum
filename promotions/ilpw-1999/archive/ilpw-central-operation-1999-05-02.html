Role-Playing Information *^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v* -*^v^v^v^v[The Openin' Letter]v^v^v^v^*- *^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v* [ Some funky, yet slow techno-like rap beats begin to play in the background.] You know, I've ran federations in the past, I've been in federations in the past... And honest to goodness, I could have not asked for a BETTER GROUP OF
_EXCELLENT_ TALENT TO WORK WITH!
Although I'm usually a guy who's confident in his abilities, I had an ever-so-slight idea that ILPW wasn't going to attract the attention that it did. (And boy did it ever!) Fortunately, I was surprised beyond belief when
ILPW was blessed with... How should I say this... YOU GUYS! Over the past 4 or so days I've gotten countless applications, some good some bad. But the best, are all in this federation (most of the best.)
I feel honored and very excited to see this flourishing already. Compared to
_many_ federations, this is an amazing start.
But enough of the sweet talk, ladies and gentlemen and others... It's time that _WE GET DOWN TO BUSINESS_.
Based on the looks of things, ILPW has a good chance at becoming one of the funner (I know, it's not a word!) federations you may very well have been in...
NOTHING will be more evident _THIS_ MONDAY_ NIGHT_ when ILPW _BLASTS_ opens the doors to the public on the very first edition of ILPW Monday Night MISCONDUCT.
What a night it will be. Historic to say the least! This card will showcase nearly every piece o' talent the ILPW has to offer. With action filled to the brim, and fantastic matches coming to you right and left, featuring a No DQ encounter, a round one match in the ILPW WORLD TITLE TOURNAMENT, and a lethal
Triple Threat slugfest! The night caps off with a "Bring Your Own Weapon" Royal
Rumble for the ILPW Death-Match Championship!
I expect this to be one of the better events I've ever witnessed. With four brilliant writers on my side, this night will definately be fit for the opening of a federation like ILPW.
Once again, I wish to thank _EVERY_ single one of you who "believed in" ILPW. The effort you put into this federation, you will get back ten-fold. I'm not striving to make this "THE BEST FEDERATION IN THE WORLD", but I am striving to make it the funnest and most enjoyable. So all I ask now, is for you to buckle up and get ready... WE'RE GOING PLACES...

The Legacy Officially begins.The Seattle newspapers and the community itself has been buzzing with the rumors and plans of the ecentric, yet intelligent owner of the ILPW, Lance Avery. Lance has made very clear to the local public, that ILPW will not only make, quote "mucho money for all o' Seattle but a haven for what this place has been lacking for quite some time, SOME DAMN ENTERTAINMENT."
With the ILPW Arena finally being finished a month ago, Lance now brings his exciting array of talent into the ever changing fold. With top stars such as
"Mister Sexy" Brian Gold, Devon Case, "Sexy" Fletch Johnson The Ghetto Thugz,
"Hot Stuff" Scott Richards and the "Lone Enforcer" Logan Gunz. Lance has also been able to bring international superstar Orson "The Irish Wolfhound" Edmonds into the roster. According to many wrestling insiders, this is quite the list of superstars and promises to be one of the premiere wrestling organizations in the vast scope of the sports-entertainment industry.

Participants: Scotty Johnson "Warhead" Shaun Jones "Mister Sexy" Brian Gold Devon Case Tommy Jobber Will Shelton Brian Fury Shadow Mike "The Rat" Rice Bloodlust Malice And A Mystery Man! *^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v* -*^v^v^v^v[Role-Playing Info]v^v^v^v^*- *^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v* Alright folks, here's the scoop. Role-Plays come out 3-4 times a week. Usually every other day. Once we get on a roll, a pattern will develop where the RPs are sent out on Wednesday, Friday, and Sunday.
Matches are not all decided on RPs. Many a times it is angles that plays an important role in the outcome of a match. But let's not just think about wins and losses here.
Feel free to send in strats, angles, in-ringers that will be pasted on cards, almost anything you'd like that you feel would help elevate your chances of a good match.
A few guidelines include: 1) No excessive swearing. You know the obvious words that I'm not gonna list that you don't hear on regular Monday Night Programming, so I want you to try and not say 'em either!
2) When you regularly RP (when you send in your RPs to me) I do not... AND I MEAN I DO NOT ACCEPT RPs that are done in a house show or in-ring capacity. Quite frankly, you never see these on regular TV, they're highly unoriginal and uncreative, and I want to save them for actual in-ringer that can be paste don to our live shows. Now, don't get me wrong, I'm sure we have all written one of these before and they may have been quite good, BUT NOT HERE! :)
3) Have fun and be as creative as you can. And that's about all I can think of with it being 10:45 at night over here on the West Coast (of USA). If I've forgotten anything, I'll be sure to let you know.
