Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

International League Pro Wrestling opened out of a Seattle studio on 2 May 1999, its founder writing that he had been surprised by how many applications came in over the promotion's own first few days and promising a debut card, Monday Night MisConduct, the following Monday to showcase nearly the whole new roster.

That roster ran to more than twenty singles wrestlers and two tag teams, the Hooligans Y2K and The Ghetto Thugz, built around two championships already in motion: a World Title Tournament already into its first round, and a Death-Match Championship to be settled in a twelve-man Bring Your Own Weapon battle royal.

Its own preview newsletter (6 May 1999), hosted by announcer Marcus Tate, previewed that debut card's three headline matches — a triple threat among The Dave, “The Irish Wolfhound” Orson Edmonds, and “Sexy” Fletch Johnson; a first-round World Title bout between Will Shelton and Malice; and the twelve-man Death-Match battle royal — and carried an extended in-character promo from Edmonds, opening on a pastoral scene of his native Ireland before turning to needle each of his upcoming opponents by name.

Neither surviving document reports the outcome of Monday Night MisConduct; whether it was ever held isn't in the surviving record.
