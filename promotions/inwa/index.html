The INWA, a regional TNM-simulated e-fed founded in March 1997 whose president and vice-president each posted their own recruitment ad a week apart, before closing its roster as full three months later. Written by AI, 2026

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

INWA introduced itself on rec.sport.pro-wrestling.fantasy on 31 March 1997, calling itself “the most exciting and realistic e-fed” and expanding its own initials, in that same founding ad, as “international wrestling federation” — though no fuller record confirms that name. It ran on the TNM 6.2 simulator, with roleplay welcomed “whenever and whatever you want.”

Nearly two months later its own president posted a further recruitment call on 21 May, and its vice-president posted one of his own a week after that, crediting the fed's quality to the president dedicating “lots of time to it” alongside “a great VP.”

By 30 June 1997 the roster was full: “please don't try and join... thank you.” No further document survives under the fed's own name. Other community sources corroborate it was still active as late as that August, but no closure notice, card, or roster ever surfaces in the surviving record.
