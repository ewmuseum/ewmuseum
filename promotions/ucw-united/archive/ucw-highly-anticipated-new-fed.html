Well, United Championship Wrestling has a figurehead president named "Tenacious" Ted Murphy. He's in his mid-40s and he's a former wrestler. The broadcasting team of UCW Sunday Showdown consists of Timothy Hyatt and "Spectacular" Scott Taylor. Hyatt is the lead man, who does mainly play by play with a little bit of color. Warwick is young and confident, and has quite a future. He's a real assett to the UCW team, as both an interviewer and co-host of X-Plosion. Lee is an extremely attractive raven-haired beauty. She's very outspoken-- not necessarily a heel, but not a face either (a bit like Sunny, only a lot less of a flirt). d) Anybody else? Sure, there's more... Ring Announcer: "Golden" Gary White. This man is over 60 and is only hired because he is one of the Board of Directors' grampa and he needs a job so he won't bother him at home. He's rather incompetent and forgets where he is most of the time. Backstage Interviewer: "Intellect" Iggy Esmond. He thinks he's a lot smarter than he actually is. He also professes to know a lot about wrestling, but usually makes a fool of himself. He's rather neutral, not liking heels or faces. ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ 3333 33 CARDS AND PPVS 333 33 3333 a) So, how many cards a week will the UCW have? The UCW will have a Pay Per View presentation 4 times a year. These will all be BIG shows. The reason we decided on having only 4 a year is that this will give us more time to build angles and feuds, and it will also add more prestige to each PPV. Here is the lineup: * FEBRUARY (the Sunday before Valentine's Day) - UCW Mystery Date This PPV will hold a tournament called the Mystery Date tournament-- the winner gets a shot at the UCW World Heavyweight Championship at UCW UltraClash in May. The tournament will be run like this: 16 names will be drawn in the weeks prior to the PPV. At the PPV, the 16 names will again be put into a bowl, and 8 teams will be made from those 16 names (hence the name Mystery Date-- no idea who your tag partner will be). FIRST ROUND: Four tag team matches. Winners advance to the second round. SECOND ROUND: 8 members of the 4 winning tag teams are entered into a "sudden death"-style battle royal. The last two men standing advance to the final round. FINAL ROUND: A special bunker match made just for Valentine's Day. This will take place inside a 15-foot high steel cage, and at each of the top corners of the cage there will be a heart-shaped box containing a weapon. Once a weapon is retrieved, it is legal and can be used. The only way to win this match is by pinfall or submission. The winner gets the title shot at UCW UltraClash. * MAY (last weekend of May) - UCW UltraClash This is the UCW's "big one". The equivalent of WrestleMania to the WWF. This will be a huge 3-day event. There will be matches every day, but here are the main events of each day described: FRIDAY - The main event will have all of the UCW's top stars competing in a steel cage. The winner is the one who escapes the cage first. SATURDAY - Saturday's card will be held in the afternoon. The main event will be a War Games match. Later in the night there will be an Awards Ceremony (this will start in 1998). SUNDAY - Sunday's main event will see the UCW World Heavyweight Champion put his belt on the line against the man who won the Mystery Date tournament back in February. * AUGUST (last Sunday in August) - UCW's Survival of the Fittest Survival of the Fittest will see a huge 16-man tournament as well as an 8 tag team tournament. The 16-man tournament can include both Heavyweights and Skyweights. All the titles will also be on the line at this PPV. * NOVEMBER (last Sunday in November) - UCW Twist of Fate Every match on this card will have a random stipulation decided by a wheel that will have 10-15 stipulations on it-- President Murphy will spin the wheel before each match. There will be a great card and the random stips will make it even more exciting. ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ 444 44 44 ROLEPLAYING 44444444 444 444 a) What is roleplaying and how does it effect my success? Roleplaying is basically playing out the role of your character. Anything you make your wrestler do is called roleplaying. This can be writing flashes/interviews, involving your wrestler in angles/feuds, and anything like that. To be a "good roleplayer", you need to really be able to take the roll of your character. You need to really develop your wrestler's personality, as character depth is very important. It's also good to get involved in feuds-- these can get your wrestler noticed and it also makes e-wrestling a lot more fun. b) I thought roleplaying was just writing flashes? Yeah, a lot of people think that. The UCW is run on a "true" booked system-- meaning we look at the entire picture when deciding who wins matches. We don't just look at flashes. Although flashes and interviews *will* have a pretty big effect on who gets pushed and who doesn't, it's not ALL that we look at. To us, roleplaying is much more than just flashing-- it's a lot more than that. c) What is a strat? "Strat" is short for STRATEGY. This is something you'd send in describing what you want your wrestler/tag team to do in their match. We wouldn't ask for one for every match, just when you want something special to happen or whatever. d) Ok, I want to write an interview, but who do I use as an interviewer? Good question. You have a few choices: * For backstage interviews (these will be used on Sunday Showdown as either pre-match interviews or just an interview from backstage), use "Intellect" Iggy Esmond or Sasha Lee. Both are fairly neutral. * For in-ring interviews (these will be used on Sunday Showdown as well, they're just like a regular interview that takes place in the ring), use Brian Warwick or Timothy Hyatt. Both are rather unbiased, but faces. * If you want to have a pre-recorded interview for X-Plosion, you can use any of the four interviewers. e) Who do I send the flashes/interviews/angles/etc to?
