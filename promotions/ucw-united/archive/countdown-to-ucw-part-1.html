[UCW] Countdown to UCW, Part 1

You've seen all the ads Taylor has posted about the UCW. Well it is now finally up and running and here is are first ever broadcast. If you like it and want to join then e-mail myself and I will see what we can do. Enjoy: [ A countdown from 10 all the way to 1 starts up but between each number a scene from wrestling action is shown complete with the commentating and crowd reaction. Seated is a young looking man in his mid-twenties with slick back blond hair in a blue UCW blazer and with a white shirt. At his side is a gorgeous raven haired beauty in a vivacious red dress. Behind them is a large video monitor with the UCW logo. The man looks into the camera and begins to speak...] BW: Happy Easter Sunday and welcome wrestling fans to the first ever UCW broadcast. I am your host Brad Warwick along with the beautiful Sasha Lee. SL: [interrupting] Wait one minute, what makes you the host? BW: [shocked by the outburst] Well...umm...you see...I..uh... Someone as attractive as me will always get high television rankings. There should be a show with just me 24 hours a day. This TV special was designed to introduce you to the newest and what will definately be the hottest wrestling federation around. In the next hour we will be going over the UCW broadcasting schedule, tentative PPV lineup and introduce you to the hot talent we have signed thus far. UCW Showdown is our big two hour wrestling card which will be aired live from the UCW Auditoruim in Seattle, Washington. Another thing that will be worth our fans hard earned cash will be the UCW's awesome PPVs. BW: Ummmmm....No. Anyway lets go back the official Martin Technologies Super Monitor for a rundown of the PPVs. SL: Wow, isn't this thrilling!!!!!!! Has the show started yet or are we still doing the commercials. [ The giant monitor comes back to life as the Logo crumbles away to reveal a rundown of the programming.] Mystery Date This PPV will hold a tournament called the Mystery Date tournament-- the winner gets a shot at the UCW World Heavyweight Championship at UCW UltraClash in May. The tournament will be run like this: 16 names will be drawn in the weeks prior to the PPV. At the PPV, the 16 names will again be put into a bowl, and 8 teams will be made from those 16 names (hence the name Mystery Date-- no idea who your tag partner will be). FIRST ROUND: Four tag team matches. Winners advance to the second round. SECOND ROUND: 8 members of the 4 winning tag teams are entered into a "sudden death"-style battle royal. The last two men standing advance to the final round. FINAL ROUND: A special bunker match made just for Valentine's Day. Once a weapon is retrieved, it is legal and can be used. The only way to win this match is by pinfall or submission. The winner gets the title shot at UCW UltraClash. All titles will be on the line plus a special Bunkhouse Brawl to decided the challengers for the tag straps at UCW UltraClash. UCW UltraClash This is the UCW's "big one". This will be a huge 3-day event. There will be matches everyday, but here are the main events of each day described: FRIDAY - The main event will have all of the UCW's top stars competing in a steel cage. The winner is the one who escapes the cage first. SATURDAY - Saturday's card will be held in the afternoon. The main event will be a War Games match. Later in the night there will be an Awards Ceremony (this will start in 1998). SUNDAY - Sunday's main event will see the UCW World Heavyweight Champion put his belt on the line against the man who won the Mystery Date tournament back in February. UCW's Survival of the Fittest Survival of the Fittest will see a huge 16-man tournament as well as an 8 tag team tournament. The 16-man tournament can include both Heavyweights and Skyweights. All the titles will also be on the line at this PPV. * NOVEMBER (last Sunday in November) - UCW Twist of Fate Every match on this card will have a random stipulation decided by a wheel that will have 10-15 stipulations on it-- President Murphy will spin the wheel before each match.
