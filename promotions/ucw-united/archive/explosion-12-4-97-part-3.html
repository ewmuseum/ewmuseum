[UCW] Explosion!; 12.4.97, Part 3

14 December 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
VOICE - Doing what you have to do, and cheating are two totally different things. So when I'm asked, "Why'd Tommy cheat?" I'll just say, "He did what he had to." [Fade in.] [SCENE: The NPW locker rooms, once again. It's empty and dry, all except for the sounds of packing and bagging up. The camera pans to Johnathan Blade, who is doing the packaging and bagging. Johnathan wears a white Tommy Hilfiger windsuit, and the latest Jordans. Bags are all around him, and the NPW New England Title rests on the bench that juts out from the lockers' base, all nicely shined and buffed. Blade turns to the camera, smiles, and locks up his locker.] JOHNATHAN BLADE - Okay, TD, you wanna rematch at my title, huh? No disqualification. Submission match, huh? And it's signed already? Good. It keeps me from wasting my hand energy. Dragon, you got so mad that you lost, that you _forgot_ that I am rollin' in NPW, grabbing wins left and right. _lncluding_ the one over you, on Thanksgiving's Stampede. Yeah, Tommy may have grabbed the pad. But, like I said, we do what we have to do. Whatever it takes. Doing what you have to do to survive can greatly be in your favor, if you can pull it off. Just like a homeless person stealing a loaf of bread off of a windowsill. In any other regular match, I would abide by the rules. I'd do what I had to _within the boundaries_. But a match like this ... I get to do _whatever_ I have to, _whenever_. Dragon, signing this match could be the worst thing you've ever done. Possibly, me getting _into_ the match could be the worst thing _l've_ ever done. With me never being in a no disqualification match, this'll be interesting, even though I've brawled with people four and five, even _six_ years older than me. And note ... they were all bigger than I was then. I've been pushed into walls, and lockers. I've bled from the top of my head, down to the very _nape_ of my neck. Yeah, I may have been a child prodigy, but that didn't _MEAN_ I wasn't normal. For some, no, it didn't mean that. But for me, it did. That's why I [bleep] wrestled. To make heads roll of the bodies of those who challenged me. To make a person realize, that I'm nothing to play with in the ring. Thunder Dragon, I hope you understand this. [Blade picks up his bags, along with his New England title, as the look on his face is anything BUT plesant.] Thunder Dragon, you're the number one contender for this top prize that Johnathan Blade holds in his hand right now. At United Championship Wrestling's Explosion!, someone will get hurt. Physically, of course. But mentally, and emotionally? Look at the match. Look at who is IN it. And look what's at stake. A title. Over all of the no disqualification crap that is set in stone, this belt is on the line. But I'm not worried, though. Because of the simple fact that you're going to make the mistake all others in my short career have made. And that's the mere fact that I'm nineteen years old. Go ahead take advantage of it while you can, Thunder Dragon. Because I, Johnathan Blade is going to slice through you like a Ginsu. And why, you ask? [Blade proceeds to leave.] Because blades do that. Johnathan Blade is back on his feet and grabs one of the metal stairs. It's above his head....and DOWN ON TD's BACK! Ryo: Thunder Dragon is clutching his back in pain! The ref can't stop the match, this is NO DQ! Johnathan Blade yells to the crowd and the crowd screams back at the champ. This is a fan contest here! Johnthan Blade hops back on the apron and scales the turnbuckles, Thunder Dragon slowly to his feet...HIGH CROSS BODY BLOCK! Mercury: Blade just flattened TD with that one! Blade pulls up Thunder Dragon and drags him over to the Pit. This is where the battle always turns good! Blade with an irish whip...YEOW!! Thunder Dragon just slammed into the ramps! Thunder Dragon stumbles back to Blade...body slam by Blade! Ryo: That was on hard cement! Johnathan Blade scales the ramps and takes a good look down at Thunder Dragon... BIG SPLASH!!! Mercury: Who does he think he is? Jimmy Snuka? Ryo: Johnathan Blade is pounding away at Thunder Dragon! Johnathan Blade stands up and Thunder Dragon is clutching at his ribs. Ryo: TD pulls up the ladder and slams it hard on Blade! Tommy Blade runs over to stop the carnage and jumps on the back on TD. Thunder Dragon pulls Tommy Blade off his back and slams him hard to the ground! Thunder Dragon backs up and is telling Blade to get up. Blade is on his knees and Thunder Dragon looks around. TD starts punching and kicking away at Johnathan Blade! This is the Dragon's FURY! The fans are erupting!!! Mercury: Thunder Dragon is taking control of this match! Bear hug by Thunder Dragon and TD is carrying Blade over to the steel guard rail. STUN GUN! TD just gave a stun gun to Johnathan Blade! That was awesome! TD throws Blade back into the ring. Blade with a spinning heel kick to the chair! TD is down! Blade is down! Both men are down! Mercury: Patricia Hart is pounding the mat! Tommy Blade is out on the mat! I crack myself up. Both men are starting to stir. Blade is dragging himself to the ropes, Thunder Dragon starting to get up. Blade is back up and is slowly walking towards Thunder Dragon. Thunder Dragon swings, Blade ducks...DROPKICK! Thunder Dragon crashes to the mat! Ryo: LOOK! Mr. Blond is out to the ring! He's scouting! He is going to take on the winner of this match! Thunder Dragon back to his feet and so is Blade, TD swings again...DROPKICK!! Mercury: Blade is up again and so is TD. TD takes another swing, kick to the stomach by Blade...DDT!! Ryo: Correction, rather Blade wins or loses, Johnathan Blade will be facing Mr. Blond. Blond is still standing there, looking on! Mercury: Blade is back on his feet and heads to the top turnbuckle. MOONSAULT!!!! He goes for the pin! The ref is reminding him that there are no pinfalls in this match! Mr. Blond shakes his head and walks away! Blade picks up Thunder Dragon, front face lock...SUPLEX! Blade looks like he's telling the ref to ask him. Ref: TD! TD! Do you give up! Thunder Dragon: NO!!! Ryo: Blade back on the attack. Johnathan Blade picks up TD and deposits him on the top turnbuckle....SUPERPLEX!!!! Mercury: Thunder Dragon is grabbing the back of his head!!! If Blade is smart, he'll capitalize on that one! That chair is still in the ring, I didn't notice that! Ryo: Blade did and he's grabbed it. He places it on the mat, picks up Thunder Dragon. PILEDRIVER!!! He just piledrove him on that chair! Johnathan Blade is back up and is signalling to the crowd! They know what's coming up!!!! He's pointing to the chair and the crowd is on their feet!! He's setting him up for the Blade Burner! Mercury: Question. How is TD going to say, "I quit" when he's knocked out? Ryo: Good question....BLADE BURNER! SIDE RUSSIAN LEG SWEEP INTO A REVERSE DDT! Blade is up and is looking towards the crowd! Blade slaps a figure four leg lock on Thunder Dragon! The ref is asking an almost unsconscious TD if he's going to give up! Thunder Dragon is still shaking his head, but it's slower now. He's almost out! Mercury: The crowd is turning towards the aisle! The Hitman is running down the aisle! Hitman hits the ring and knocks out the ref! Blade releases the figure four and is yelling at Hitman. Hitman turns away and is apparently leaving the ring. NO!!!!! HITMAN DRILLED BLADE WITH A CRESCENT KICK TO THE MUSH! Ryo: WOW! He's leaving! Come back! COME BACK! Ryo: Patricia Hart is checking her fallen man. She's concerned for him! Another official is coming down to ringside. Blade is slowly getting to his feet and just falls back down. Patricia Hart is slapping Thunder Dragon trying to help him wake up. Mercury: Rumor has it, TD likes it rough. LOOK!
