BD: Okay... KB: On April 13th, UCW, one advises you to tread very carefully, because with my help, this man is going straight to the top of this federation, and there's nothing _any_ of you low-life can do about it. Duke....? BD: Get outta my face. KB: Ahhh. So eloquent. Well do as he says then... [Cuts back to the studio.] SL: Who was that one, Benny kept talking about? The first one illuminates an image of a man with long pink hair jamming a cattle prod into the side of another man.] VO: For some things in nature... [Another bolt of lightning illuminates the image of the same pink-haired man hitting another wrestler with a brain buster off the top of a ladder to the outside of the ring.] VO: There is just no rational reason for existence. [Another bolt of lightning. This time, it reveals the pink-haired man clutching a chair to his stomach, and performing a sky twister press into a bodyblock on a man standing outside the ring.] VO: But then, just maybe... [Another bolt of lightning. The pink-man is shown, standing in the middle of the ring, arms held straight outwards as if in a crucifix.] VO: Some things are not subject to nature's domain. [Another bolt of lightning causes the screen to go white. When it fades back to black, the pink-haired man is standing there, shown from the waist up. He's wearing a leather coat which goes on beyond his waist.] JS: Welcome, United Championship Wrestling, to Jonathan Shock. [Shock's previously expresionless face twists into a cruel smile, but his eyes remain apparently vacant of any emotion.] JS: [barely above a whisper] And.... welcome to The Revolution. [Another streak of lightning crashes across the screen, and when the white light fades back to black, Shock is nowhere to be found. A driving rock song can be heard faintly in the background as, in large white lettering, the words "There is no reasoning." come onto the screen. A final bolt of lightning crashes, wiping out the words and halting the music. The screen remains black and silent for a few seconds, then cuts back to the studio.] SL: Well that proved it, that kid is nuts.
