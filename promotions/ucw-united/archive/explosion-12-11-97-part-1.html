[UCW] Explosion!; 12.11.97, Part 1

14 December 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The Camera slowly fades from black. A shot of many differnt wrestling superstars begins to flash over the screen. Slowly, steadily more and more are shown. "Back in Black" by AC/DC begins to play as the screen begins to roll and flip faster and faster, barely making some superstars visible... Smoke begins to flow from the sides of the screen... then, without notice, the screen explodes, the camera quickly cuts back to the Rome Free Academy Gymnasium, situated in lovely Rome, New York. The crowd is cheering at the top of their lungs, many can't wait for the action to come... the United Championship Wrestling "Explosion!" Logo shows up on the screen, smoldering a bit.] <=======================================> ____ ____ _______ _____ __ _____ / / / / / ____/ \ \/ \/ / / /_/ / / /___ \ / /_________/ /______/ \____/\____/ United Championship Wrestling <=======================================> ///////||||||||||||||||||\\\\\\\ United Championship Wrestling Explosion! \\\\\\\||||||||||||||||||/////// ///////||||||||||||||||||\\\\\\\ "the Second Coming" \\\\\\\||||||||||||||||||/////// Episode #2 12/11/97 ///////||||||||||||||||||\\\\\\\ \\\\\\\||||||||||||||||||/////// ["Back in Black" by AC/DC slowly dies down as the camera pans around the Rome Free Academy Gymnasium as all the fans scream and cheer in delight. Before long, the camera cuts to ringside, where Mohawk Valley Wrestling's own Martin Grimmer stands, readying to do the intro for the card. Grimmer grins towards the camera and begins to speak.] MARTIN GRIMMER - And United Championship Wrestling provides New England and New York with their second amazing trifecta broadcast! [The crowd erupts.] MARTIN GRIMMER - I'm MVW's host Martin Grimmer alongside my broadcast partner Devon Cannons and we're _very_ excited to be putting on this second wonderful show at our own MVW location. And what a show we have ... DEVON CANNONS - ... First? To open up the second card, GEWA hardcore action at its best as the Fourth Reich takes on the team of Ed Salazar and Hardcore in a non-title brawl! This one's going to be all over the arena, folks! [Crowd erupts.] MARTIN GRIMMER - And what about _THIS_ one from Northeastern Pro Wrestling? A three way dance tag team encounter! It's going to be Malicious Intent, the Motor City Maniacs and the very formidable tag team of the Wave Runners [pop] who are very well loved in the New York area! [Crowd erupts again.] DEVON CANNONS - And then, the finale'. A man who's common to both Northeastern Pro Wrestling _and_ Mohawk Valley Wrestling to boot. I'm talking about the one and only "Stunning" Scott Alexander as he takes on local favorite ... [Crowd erupts.] MARTIN GRIMMER - Tony Ruiz! [Crowd erupts again.] DEVON CANNONS - That's enough stalling, lets get down to ringside for our first encounter as GEWA shows us what tag team wrestling is all about! ___________________________________ \||||Global E-Wrestling Alliance|||/ \||||||||||||||||||||||||||||||||/ \|||||GEWA Tag Team Action|||||/ \||||||Non-Title Match||||||/ \||Hardcore & Ed Salazar|||/ \|||||||||||vs|||||||||||/ \|||The Fourth Reich|||/ \||||||||||||||||||||/ \||||||||||||||||||/ \|||||||by|||||||/ \||||[name withheld]||/ \||||||||||/ \||||||||/ \||||||/ \||||/ \||/ \/ (Cut to the announcers table. Rodgers and McDaniel are seated behind the desk and are wearing their headsets.) [McDaniel] : Good evening from the UCW Explosion. The GEWA was asked to send it's best and brightest stars. [Rodgers] Which serves as absolutely no explanation why we sent these guys. [McDaniel] : Which guys are you talking about? The Fourth Reich are the Tag champs and the team of Hardcore and Ed Salazar is burning up GEWA. [Rodgers] Sort of gives you an idea of the sad state of GEWA, doesn't it. Neo-Nazis for champs and a couple of thugs as their main competition. [McDaniel] : And just who would you rather see as the champs? [Rodgers] Unless GEWA gets some real competition going here I'd just as soon see them get rid of the belts! ["Ride of the Valkyries" begins] [Rodgers] Let the embarrassment begin. [McDaniel] : And here they come, the GEWA tag team champions, The Fourth Reich. (Baron von Falkenberg emerges from the back, however he is alone. Ubermann and von Sippel are not with him. The crowd doesn't know how to react. Then a chorus of boo's starts up. Von Falkenberg ignores the crowd and makes his way to the center of the ring. He calls for a microphone and is handed one.) [Von Falkenberg] Listen up you vorthless scum! (A chorus of boos rains down on von Falkenberg.) [Von Falkenberg] SILENCE! (The crowd silences.) [Von Falkenberg] Now you vill listen to me! I am about to bring out the vinest tag team in wrestling today and I vant you all to give them the recognition they deserve! Achtung! Ubermann, von Sippel! Come out here! Schnell! ("Ride of the Valkyries" starts back up. This time, Ubermann and von Sippel come goose-stepping down the aisle. They stare straight ahead and keep their eyes on von Falkenberg.) [Von Falkenberg] Und now, you lazy, worthless schwine! You will repeat after me! Seig Heil! Seig Heil! (Crowd) Like Hell! Like Hell! (Von Falkenberg turns purple with rage in the center of the ring) [Von Falkenberg] Schwine! You vill pay for this insult! Your precious team shall be made to suffer! [McDaniel] It appears that the crowd has a mind of its own! [Rodgers] [has his head in his hands] God! What were the suits thinking? They send out these clowns! Our only hope lay in the Hardcore Saladbar! [McDaniel] Who? [Rodgers] Hardcore and Salazar! Don't you ever pay attention to anything I say? [McDaniel] I try not to. (Ubermann, von Sippel and von Falkenberg are all in the ring. Ubermann and von Sippel are in their corner, standing at attention, watching von Falkenberg. Von Falkenberg is giving animated instructions and Ubermann and von Sippel are nodding their heads in comprehension giving a guttural "Ja" periodically.) [McDaniel] Where are Salazar and Hardcore? [Rodgers] Perhaps they were as embarrassed by this whole thing as I am! (A drink seller comes to the railing. He has his hat pulled low so his face is hidden.) [Seller] Hey, you three! Yeah, the Teutonic Twits! (Von Falkenberg, Ubermann and von Sippel ignore the concessionaire.) [Seller] Hey, you Aryan Assholes! (Von Falkenberg looks up) [Seller] Ah, I see you recognize your name! Hey, come here! I have a little something for you! All of you! Come on down! (Von Falkenberg exits the ring and heads to the rail. Ubermann and von Sippel remain in the ring. They do a smart about face and stand at attention facing the direction that von Falkenberg went.) [Von Falkenberg] Vat do you want, schwinkopf? [Seller] Come closer. I was asked to deliver a message to you from Ed Salazar and Hardcore. [Von Falkenberg] Vat? Those two cowards are afraid to come forward themselves? (Von Falkenberg closes up to the rail.) [Von Falkenberg] So? Vat is the message? [Seller] Bend down here, I want to make sure you are the only one who gets this message. (Von Falkenberg leans closer to the seller.) [Seller] Here it is, sweetheart! (The drink seller lifts up his tray of drinks and brings it crashing down on von Falkenberg's head, knocking von Falkenberg senseless. Von Falkenberg ends up draped over the railing as the drink seller removes his hat and coat.) [McDaniel] It's Hardcore! Ubermann and von Sippel are leaping out of the ring to come to the aid of their manager. Hardcore leaps the railing to meet their charge. Where is Salazar? [Rodgers] Probably on the other side of the arena trying to hawk pretzels or something! (Von Sippel tends to von Falkenberg while Ubermann attacks Hardcore. They start brawling in the stands.) [McDaniel] Ubermann and Hardcore are going at it here on the floor! Ubermann with a European uppercut and Hardcore is taken back a few steps. Hardcore charges in and catches Ubermann in a clothesline. Von Sippel has now turned his attention to Hardcore and hits him with a big forearm to the back of the head. Ubermann and von Sippel each take an arm and double whip into the ring steps. [Rodgers] And still you have to ask yourself where is Ed Salazar? Ubermann and von Sippel take Hardcore up and get ready to give him another double whip into the ring steps. WAIT A MINUTE! A hand reaches out from under the ring and grabs Ubermann's ankle! Ubermann goes face first into the ring steps! [McDaniel] This match hasn't officially begun and already we have action like you wouldn't believe! Hardcore has recovered enough to take von Sippel and heave him into the ring. Hardcore follows him in. Now the mystery man comes from under the ring and...It’s Salazar! [Rodgers] He must have been studying the Underground's tactics. A good idea for two men who's purpose is to destroy them. Salazar continues to beat on Ubermann on the outside while von Sippel and Hardcore go at it in the middle of the ring. [McDaniel] The referee finally calls for the bell starting this match! Salazar catches Ubermann with a body-slam on the outside and leaves the big German laying on the floor. Salazar finally goes to his corner and waits there. [Rodgers] Listen to the crowd! They're firmly behind the Hardcore Saladbar. [McDaniel] I don't think it's a good idea to refer to them as such. [Rodgers] Well, you're half right. You don't think. [McDaniel] Back to the match. Ubermann is finally back on his feet and makes his way to his corner. In the ring, von Sippel and Hardcore continue to pound on each other, neither man able to get much of an advantage on his opponent. They're standing toe to toe, slugging it out. [Rodgers] Not a very good tactic on Hardcore's part. Von Sippel may not be the sharpest knife in the drawer, but he's one tough customer. Hardcore finally abandons that line of attack and gives von Sippel a big boot to the groin! That one doubled the big kraut over. [McDaniel] Do you think you can lay off the ethnic slurs tonight? Von Sippel is doubled over and Hardcore puts him in a side head lock. [Rodgers] Hey! A wrestling move! I didn't know the black beast from the east knew any! [McDaniel] The what? Where did you ever hear that nickname? [Rodgers] Around. Hardcore drags von Sippel over to his corner and tags in Salazar. Salazar to the top rope and jumps right on von Sippel's back! Down goes von Sippel like a sack of wet cement! Hardcore and Salazar put the boots to von Sippel. Ubermann comes charging out of his corner and is greeted by the referee who chases him back out. [McDaniel] Von Falkenberg is finally moving fairly well now and he's moving over to Hardcore and Salazar's corner where the two continue to stomp on von Sippel. Von Falkenberg gets close and hits Hardcore with his riding crop right across the thigh! That got Hardcore's attention and he is now reaching over the top rope, trying to get at von Falkenberg. Von Falkenberg hits Hardcore right in the face with the riding crop! Hardcore is grabbing at his eyes and von Falkenberg now heads over to the other side where he hits Salzar on the thigh with the crop. [Rodgers] Salazar doesn't make the same mistake that Hardcore made. Instead, Salazar leaps over the top rope and is chasing von Falkenberg around the ring. Von Sippel crawls back to his corner and tags in Ubermann. Ubermann charges across the ring and catches the temporarily blinded Hardcore with a big clothesline. A huge fist to the midsection doubles Hardcore over and Ubermann with a scoop slam! Elbow drop and Hardcore is looking a little shaky right about now. [McDaniel] Ubermann with the cover. 1..2......kickout by Hardcore. Ubermann is screaming at the referee! (Ubermann) EIN...ZWEI...DREI! DREI!!!! [Rodgers] It appears that Ubermann is attempting to give the ref a little German lesson here. The referee is still shaking his head and holding up 2 fingers. Meanwhile on the outside, Salazar has caught von Falkenberg and is getting ready to give him a big fist when von Sippel comes up from behind and hits him with a ferocious rabbit punch! That staggered Salazar for a moment and now Salazar and von Sippel are brawling on the outside. [McDaniel] Von Falkenberg sneaks up behind Salazar – riding crop to the head! That dazes Salazar, and von Sippel quickly climbs up to the ring apron, where Ubermann tags him in. Ubermann leaves the ring and jumps to the floor, taking up the battle against Salazar. [Rodgers] Salazar is still a little bit out of it, smarting from that crop shot… Ubermann takes advantage of that and takes Salazar down with a FIST TO THE NADS! Whoo! He follows that up quickly with a jawbreaker, and Ed’s a hurtin’ unit! [McDaniel] Von Sippel seems to have things his own way in the ring right now. He puts Hardcore into an armbar, Irish whip into the ropes and .....BODY SLAM! Hardcore is motionless on the mat. Von Sippel going to the top rope and gets ready for the Stuka! [Rodgers] If he hits this flying kneedrop, it’s game over – there it is! NO! Hardcore sits up! Von Sippel crashes knee-first into the canvas, and Hardcore staggers to his feet! He stumbles over to make the tag, but Ed Salazar is getting a stomping from Ubermann on the outside, Hardcore will find no help on that end. [McDaniel] Von Sippel has definitely done some damage to his knee, as he limps around gingerly, testing it out. Hardcore is just hanging over the ropes, trying to catch a rest. Ubermann scopes out the situation and runs around the outside of his ring to his corner, where he climbs up and innocently sticks his hand in the ring, looking for the tag. [Rodgers] Von Sippel spots him and ever so slowly gimps over, and makes the tag. Hardcore hears the slap of hands and turns to see Ubermann heading in at full steam! Ubermann barrels into Hardcore, and bounces right back out, leaving Hardcore to fall face-first onto the canvas. There’s some good news for the Hardcore Saladbar, though, as Ed Salazar is coming to his senses finally and is getting into position in his corner. [McDaniel] That’s all well and good, but Hardcore is in bad shape. Ubermann lifts Hardcore to his feet, and gets him up into a bear hug! Impressive power shown by the Nazi, and he’s got Hardcore grimacing, if not screaming. As much as Hardcore’s been beaten on, he’s holding on nicely. Ubermann visibly starts to tire, and is forced to release the hold as Hardcore’s 300 pounds become a bit too much to bear. [Rodgers] Hardcore collapses to the canvas immediately… And now the fans are starting up a "USA! USA!" chant, for no good reason. I mean, the Fourth Reich might be Nazis, but the USA thing is tired. Seeing Hardcore not going anywhere, Ubermann scoots over and tags in Von Sippel, who still has a limp. The two head over and grab Hardcore, lift him up, and whip him off the ropes! [McDaniel] Hardcore on the rebound – ducks under an attempted double clothesline! He comes back and OH! A clothesline of his own on both Von Sippel and Ubermann! All three men his the canvas, but it’s Ubermann who gets back to his feet first, and starts stomping away on Hardcore. Ubermann isn’t the legal man, and Ed Salazar hits the ring and LEVELS Ubermann with a diving shoulderblock! [Rodgers] Ubermann and Salazar take their brawl into the corner, and Hardcore, surprisingly, gets up before Von Sippel, and what’s – hey! Hardcore is trying to get von Sippel in a figure four – he locks it on! He’s got Von Sippel locked in, right in the center of the ring, and Von Sippel already has a bad knee! The referee gets distracted by Von Falkenberg, who is over outside the corner where Ubermann and Salazar are brawling! [McDaniel] The referee gets caught up in between Salazar and Ubermann, trying to break up their battle, and what’s von Falkenberg doing? He quickly scoots over to the other side of the ring, and starts fumbling in his pocket… He pulls out some sort of pouch… And a lighter? HEY! Von Sippel is submitting! He’s screaming for mercy, as Hardcore continues to apply pressure to the leg – Von Falkenberg has gotten some sort of powder out of the pouch and has entered the ring… [Rodgers] With a cautious eye on the referee, who is still embroiled with Ed and Ubermann, von Falkenberg kneels next to Hardcore’s head, fires up the lighter and releases the powder over Hardcore’s face – and brings the lighter – BOOM! Wow! A mini-fireball just exploded in Hardcore’s face! Listen to him scream! Von Falkenberg bails out of the ring, as the ref finally clears out Ubermann, and hauls Ed Salazar back to his corner… Hardcore has released the figure-four, and ha! Screwjob time! [McDaniel] Von Sippel ever so slowly crawls over on top of Hardcore, who is more concerned with the fire damage done to his face then being pinned, and hooks a leg – ref sees it, and here’s the cover! 1… 2… 3! [Rodgers] Salazar tries for the save, but this one is over! The Fourth Reich have won this match, albeit by very underhanded means. I have to respect that. [McDaniel] The concern now has to be the fire damage done to Hardcore’s face. Ed Salazar is assisting him back to the locker room area, as the Fourth Reich beat a hasty retreat as soon as the bell rang to end this one. Well, that’s all from us. Enjoy the rest of the show! ///////||||||||||||||||||\\\\\\\ Continued Part II \\\\\\\||||||||||||||||||///////
