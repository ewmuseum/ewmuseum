[UCW] Countdown to UCW, Part 3

31 March 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
BW: Were back!!!! And the talent juts keeps on rolling on. SL: As do your boring and cheesy lines. * Wrestler: James Brown * Height: 6'7" * Weight: 287lbs * Hometown: London, GB * Status: Face * Picture: He's small and muscular frame. Like James Bond, a lady killer. BW: You must be a fan of this guy, Sasha. SL: Nice looker but a goofy attire. I want a real man not any of these clowns. I want a somebody like the Two Dudes With An Attitude or Nick Nyce. BW: Well, Brown, James Brown has something to say: [Scene: James Brown stands at the UCW Interview Area. ] JB: I've been member of the secret service for 6 years now. During this time I have had many opponents , like killers or kidnappers or terrorists. Well, to say it short: When it came one on one, none of them survived. I don't want to explain this any further... Now I'm looking forward to show a few of you so called 'bad-guys' what makes me the best secret agent walking around... NEVER TURN AROUND! I COULD BE OBSERVING YOU... [Fades back to the studio.] SL: Do you have a clue what he was talking about? BW: Umm..Well..Maybe he is one of those guys whose actions speak louder then words. SL: Or at least clearer. Must be all of those years of tumbling in his mommies backyard trying to be James Bond. * Tag Team Name: Bulldogs * Combined Weight: 500lbs * Manager: Miss Havoc * Wrestlers: Bulldog 1 Bulldog 2 * Height: 6'1 6'1 * Weight: 250 250 * Hometown: Unknown * Status: Heel * Picture: Havoc is a beautiful blonde. The Bulldogs wear masks and look exactly alike. They are also very muscular. BW: The dreaded indentical tag team. Both Bulldogs look exactly a like and that will make them very hard to beat since a lot of illegal swicthes can be made. SL: That mask thing is annoying. BW: Yes it is. It is a tottally unfair advantage for the Bulldogs. SL: No I mean it is annoying that they are wearing masks and I can't see if they are cute or not. But more importantly is, will these guys leave their mark, hehehehe. * Wrestler: The PainMaster * Height: 6'1" * Weight: 324lbs * Hometown: Denver, Colorado * Status: Heel * Picture: He is big and muscular, with a bald head and has a couple of scars on each arm. BW: Frightening individual to say the least. A very big monster and someone who enoys Pain. Receiving it and delivering it. He will be a force here in the UCW. SL: Bradley, you think your pet dog, Chi-Chi, would be a force. BW: Hey, she's a tough doggy. * Tag Team Name: The Harlequins * Combined Weight: 564 pounds * Manager: Comedy Melody * Wrestlers: Tragedy Chaos * Height: 5' 10" 6' 8" * Weight: 220 lbs 344 lbs * Hometown: Sleepy Hollow, Illinois * Status Face * Picture: Comedy is an attractive red head dressed in a provocative version of a clown suit (low cut, fishnets). Melody is a beautiful blond dressed in silver with musical notes on her jacket. Tragedy has a well-defined light-heavyweight type build. He has blond hair and glowing green eyes. Chaos is huge, with a lost, almost innocent look in his eyes. He has shoulder length brown hair. BW: Yet another highly acclaimed pair of wrestlers who are going to call UCW home. The Harlequinns will be a very welcomed addition to our exciting tag roster. They will be early favourites for our tag team champions. SL: Oh goody, more freaks and sluts to join the UCW circus. BW: Sasha!!! Please show some decency. SL: Why? The Harlequinns obviously don't have any. I hope Dudes With An Attitude wipe the mat with these clowns. * Wrestler: Copperhead * Height: 6'0" * Weight: 185 lbs * Hometown: Mexico * Status: Face * Picture: A small framed wrestler in green colored snake-skin patterned body suit. His mask has the pattern of a snake head. Where as the snake eyes are where his eyes are. Two fangs come down on either side of his mouth. BW: This Mexican sensation has entered the UCW to showcase some of his awesome arial skills. The UCW has a fair share of foreign talent which only makes our roster that much more exciting. SL: Copperhead is a Mexican runt. He's probably the ultimate runt -- at only 185lbs. He's nothing to get excited over. People like Mike Sweetser could squash him. BW: You have seem to taken a fancy to the Dudes With An Attitude. SL: Duh, you really think so? * Wrestler: "The Flame" Bruce MacKey * Height: 6'5" * Weight: 243lbs * Hometown: Mississippi * Status Heel * Manager: Miss Tina * Picture: Miss Tina is a beatiful women with black hair down to her shoulders. Mackey has a nice build but not too muscular, tanned blonde hair going down to his shoulders, a small heart tattoo on his left shoulder and blue eyes. SL: Now this guy is more my type. Too bad he has to be one of those guys that has to have a slut escort him to ringside. BW: Pardon me, Miss Tina is anything but a slut. A very fine young lady. Unfortunately she is mistreated by "The Flame". * Wrestler: "Red Raven" Sean O'Brien * Height: 6'7" * Weight: 249lbs * Hometown: unknown * Status: Heel * Picture: He is rather buit and farily tall. He's got red hair, short and kind of curly. BW: Now here is a rather odd individual. SL: Red Raven?!? Don't tell me he comes out decked in red feathers and constantly quotes 'Nevermore'. He'll fit into the UCW madhouse just fine. BW: Ummmm...no. I'm sure he'll fit in juts fine but he doesn't do any of what you just said. What makes this competitor odd is that he wrestles with a mask but doesn't put it on until he is in the ring. One must wonder what the point of the mask is? SL: Yes but one has more of a social life then waste a few hour wondering why some moron wears a mask while he wrestles. Lets go see what this loon has to say. [Scene: An apartment house where there are empty boxes scattered in various places. The camera pans to the various decorative items around the apartment. It then goes to a trophy case where 4 belts and 1 plaque are sitting. Then there is a loud noise in the back end of the apartment. The camera goes over there to see what it was and sees a decent size man unpacking] SO: Don't you hate moving? I do. Always a pain in the neck. But now I have to move closer to where my new fed is. This way I can manage time between my 2 feds. [He pulls something out of a box. It gets stuck and the man pulls harder. The item comes flying out and flying out of his hands. It hits the floor and shatters] SO: Damn! That sucked. Anywho. UCW. I don't have a lot to say because I have yet to see what's going on in this fed. But, rest assured. I will be sticking my nose into some places where you don't think it belongs. Do I care? No, cuz if I stick my nose there, then that's where I think it belongs. Now get outta here, I got some unpacking to do. [Cuts back to studio.] SL: Unpacking? Some wrestlers do some of the most useless interviews. Why would I care if the guy is moving? BW: Because you get paid to. SL: [smiles] I got others ways to make an extra buck. * Wrestler: "Stunning" Steve Studd * Height: 6'6" * Weight: 249lbs * Hometown: Austin, TX * Status: Heel * Picture: He has short Blonde hair, and a muscular body..He has a goatee. SL: UCW: the home of runts, freaks and copycats. Guess which one this guy is. BW: He may have a striking similarity to Steve Austin bu.... SL: He practically is Austin, listen to this: [Scene: "Stunning" Steve Studd is sitting at home in a blue chair. He is reclining back watching television. The chair is placed beside a roaring fireplace. He is wearing long baggy pants, and a t-shirt with his likeness on it. He has a smile on his face, and he is holding a glass of wine, which he takes a drink of when the camera focuses in] SS: Ahh. This is the life. [Sits his glass down on a nearby table] But I can't relax for too long, because I have finally struck it big in the wrestling world. After two hard years of working my ass off, I finally get the respect that I deserve. I have signed a multi year deal with one of the greatest organizations in the World today, the UCW. [Sits Upright in his chair, and gets more serious] That's right wrestling fans. You useless marks. The UCW, United Championship Wrestling, has just signed the hottest free agent on the Wrestling market today, "Stunning" Steve Studd. And every worthless wrestler in the UCW is gonna regret the day that I stepped into the UCW as I am going to kick some major UCW ass on my way to some of the prestigous Championships in United Championship Wrestling. Because I am "Stunning" Steve Studd, and I am gonna kick some ass in the UCW, and thats the bottom line! [Camera cuts back to th studio] SL: Bottom line is this boy is just another pathetic clone. Not like the Two Dudes With An Attitude. BW: Actually, Two Dudes With An Attitude... SL: [gives Warick a nasty look] BW: Are are a great team. SL: Exactly. * Tag Team Name: Imperial Japanese Navy * Combined Weight: 1089lbs * Manager: Hojo Tojomoto, the Japanese Sandman * Wrestlers: BB Musashi BB Yamato * Manager/ Wrestler: Hojo Tojomoto, the Japanese Sandman * Height: 6'10" 6'8" 5'10" * Weight: 563lbs 526lbs 279lbs * Hometown: "from somewhere in the former Japanese Empire" Tojomoto hails fron Tokyo, Japan * Status: Heel * Picture: Hojo is Japanese and completely bald, with no eyebrows even. He is a once fit man who has let himself go somewhat to seed with age. Mushashi has black hair which is worn in a very short (mid-neck length) braid. Huge man, but relatively proportional, except somewhat obese. Yamato is Japanese with hair worn on back of head in a bun or knot. Large and extremely obese. BW: You'll notice that stats were included for Mr. Tojomoto and that is mainly because he is a wrestler at times also. But due to some glitches in his contract he may not be able to be a full time wrestler and be forced to strictly manage. But his Imperial Japanese Navy will be a huge force here on the UCW tag ranks. I've never seen a team this big before. SL: Two fat Japanese in diapers, big deal. BW: We will see how much of a big deal they are in the ring. * Wrestler: Jerod "The Blob" Hodge * Height: 6'4" * Weight: 383lbs * Hometown: Flint, Michigan * Status: Face * Picture: Hodge is large man with medium-short length brown hair. His features are not handsome in any sense of the imagination, but he has a lovable, goofy look about him. BW: Do not let this man's appearance trick you folks, he is an athlete in the truest sense. SL: He is also ugly in the truest sense. BW: Looks don't matter in this sport. SL: Well they matter to me. And that is all that matters. [ Giant monitor returns to the UCW logo. ] BW: That is the enitre roster currently in the UCW. And all that talent and possibly more will be competing in our first ever PPV extravaganza, UCW: A Monster Is Born live. Which will coincidentally be our first UCW card and will make use of the UCW Auditoruim in Seattle, Washington. So call your local cable company in order..... SL: For the UCW to take all your free cash. While your at it make sure to buy all the other crap known as UCW merchndise. BW: That will be enough, Sasha. Be sure to tune in again this Tuesday for our first "true" UCW broadcast, UCW X-Plosion. Which we will get some more comments from some of our superstars, a special spotlight on some of the people who work here at UCW, and give a run down on UCW: A Monster is Born. But for now this is Br... SL: A dumb idiot and his co-host the gorgeous Sasha Lee, saying catch ya later. [ Camera fades out as the light dim on the studio.
