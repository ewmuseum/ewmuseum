I guess that means we should introduce ourselves. I’m Steve McDaniel, and I’m joined by Mike Rodgers – [Rodgers] You forgot to point out that I’ve been nominated as best announcer. [McDaniel] If you had let me finish, I might have pointed that out. But since you interrupted, that’s enough about you. Anyway, we’re the broadcast team from the Global E-Wrestling Alliance’s Wednesday War Zone weekly broadcast. We’ve been working that show ever since the GEWA first opened its doors, back in June of 1996. [Rodgers] And most of the pay-per-views, too. [McDaniel] That’s true. Enough about the past, let’s talk about the present, and the future. Tonight you’ll be seeing a GEWA Global Title match, between the challenger, Red Raven, and the champ, Dr. Hate. Red Raven has been with the GEWA from the beginning, growing into one of our federation’s top stars. [Rodgers] There’s no accounting for tastes, is there? He had a brush with non-mediocrity when he joined the Underground, but for the most part he’s been a piece of garbage. [McDaniel] Dr. Hate has held the GEWA Global Title for 2 months now, ever since defeating J-Man at Business of Punishment II. He hasn't had a lot of competition for the title due to scheduling problems, and that’s been pissing him off. Look for him to get a lot more active in the coming weeks. We’re ready for introductions, let’s get to that now. (Meatloaf’s "Bat Out of Hell" starts up. A generic announcer in the ring does the spiel.) [Kevin Bolland, Jr] This match is scheduled for one fall, and is for the GEWA Global Title! Introducing first, the challenger… Hailing from parts unknown, he stands 6 feet 7 inches and weighs in at 249 pounds. He is a former GEWA Global champion and former GEWA Americas champion… Please welcome the man known as Red RAVEN! (Red Raven appears, dressed in his usual garments: Red pant tights with a black outline of a raven on them; Red boots with black tassels; A black jacket with the words inscribed 'Master of the Figure-Four. The fans in attendance give him a solid pop, as many have seen or heard of him from a variety of federations. Raven’s music stops.) (It starts with the sound of wind, a forlorn sound. The lights in the arena fade to a dark, alien green. "Sudorific" by In Strict Confidence beings sounding across the arena.) [Kevin B] And his opponent… (Raven’s song cuts out, and "Sudorific" by In Strict Confidence starts up) Hailing from Rochester, New York, he stands 6 feet and 2 inches, and weighs 263 pounds. He is the GEWA Global Champion… DR. HATE! (Dr. Hate heads to the ring. Once Dr. Hate reaches where the aisle meets the ring, He stops and holds his arms out in a crucified position. As if one cue, fans right at ringside toss trash and paper wads at Dr. Hate. Dr. Hate stands there, arms out, head bowed with his hair covering his face amid the hail of trash. After only a few seconds, Dr. Hate makes his way into the ring. Hate takes the Global belt from his shoulder and tosses it to a ring attendant.) [McDaniel] We’re ready for action in this one, as both men stare each other down. They’ve got a strange relationship, these two, sometimes bordering on friendship. Other times, it seems that Dr. Hate is going to slice Red Raven’s throat open with that scalpel he’s so fond of. [Rodgers] And there’s a problem with that? [McDaniel] Speak of the devil! The ref just checked Red Raven and Dr. Hate for foreign objects, and found one of those scalpels in Dr. Hate’s boot. He’s giving Hate some trouble about it, but the match hasn’t officially started so there’s not much that can be done about it. The ref seems satisfied that there’s no more dirty tricks afoot, and signals for the bell to start this match. [Ding, Ding, Ding] [Rodgers] Some circling, some feints, and finally we get a lockup – no! Dr. Hate fakes out Red Raven and ducks down, grabbing Red Raven by both ankles and pulling. Red Raven crashes to the canvas, with Dr. Hate holding both his legs… Dr. Hate dives over Red Raven, executing a leg snap! Hate quickly gets back to his feet and stomps a couple times on Red Raven’s right knee, then sits down and executes some sort of ankle submission. [McDaniel] Dr. Hate’s strategy seems pretty obvious, take away the legs and you eliminate the threat of the figure-four and cut down on Red Raven’s aerial attack. You slow things down substantially, too, which is not to Red Raven’s advantage. The ref asks Red Raven is he wants to submit, as Dr. Hate works that hold, but no dice. Dr. Hate releases the hold and springs to his feet. He moves away from Red Raven, and watches warily as Red gets to his feet very slowly. As they circle each other, it’s quite apparent that Red Raven’s legs are smarting some. [Rodgers] Dr. Hate obviously did his homework and came in with a game plan. Say what you will about him, but he’s an intelligent wrestler. Red Raven limps toward Dr. Hate, and this time there’s a lockup for real. Hate notices the referee is standing at an angle where he can’t see much, and Hate shifts his balance – and kicks Red Raven in the nads! [McDaniel] Red Raven collapses to the canvas like a prom dress to the floor of a hotel room, and the referee clues in that something illegal just happened. He comes over for a word with Hate, but he’s ignored by the champ. Hate shrugs and gently moves the ref out of the way, and advances upon the fallen Red Raven. Looks like his dirty tactics are going to pay off. [Rodgers] What doesn’t kill you can only make you stronger. Red Raven can take solace in the fact that he’s a stronger person for having taken a boot to the nads. [McDaniel] I’m sure that’s important to him right now. [Rodgers] Dr. Hate stomps on the right knee a few more times, causing Red Raven to writhe about on the canvas, obviously in severe pain. Hate grins and grabs Red Raven around the ankles, and drags him over toward a corner. Dr. Hate leaves him there and slides outside the ring, where he grabs Raven’s ankles once again and pulls him so that his legs are on either side of the ringpost. Hate shifts his grip to just the right leg, rears back, and SLAMS the leg into the ringpost! [McDaniel] That right knee is really getting worked over. Hate grabs the right leg, and does it AGAIN! He quickly slides back into the ring to beat the ref’s count. Hate scales the ropes and faces out to the crowd – he raises his arms in the air and prep – [Rodgers] Huh, the ref just spotted something strange in the back of Dr. Hate’s trunks and is moving in for a look-see, he’s standing over top of Red Raven… Dr. Hate hears the ref telling him to get down, but comes off the turnbuckle with a moonsault! He levels the ref! [McDaniel] I just don’t think that was accidental. The ref is out like a light, and Red Raven is still lying in the corner… And look at Dr. Hate with the Cheshire cat grin on his face. Hate reaches into the back of his trunk and pulls out a tiny case… He opens it up and pulls out… A scalpel! Oh, this is not good news. [Rodgers] Hate picks up Red Raven, and whips him into the ropes… WHAM! Blunt end of the scalpel into Red Raven’s forehead! Down like a ton of bricks goes double R. Dr. Hate takes a look at the ref, sees he’s not moving, and straddles Red Raven’s chest – and starts driving the blunt end of the scalpel into his helpless opponent’s forehead! Blood starts to flow from a cut just below the hairline. [McDaniel] Dr. Hate stops bludgeoning Red Raven and stares at the scalpel in his hand… He flips it around a couple times, looks down at Red Raven, and then back at the scalpel. He looks like he’s trying to decide something. Oh no! Hate turns the scalpel so the business end is ready for use, his hand approaches Red Raven’s face… [Rodgers] The ref moves! The ref starts to roll around like he’s going to get up, and Dr. Hate quickly slides the scalpel into his boot. Dr. Hate casually hooks Red Raven’s leg, and waits for the ref to notice, which he does, and slowly crawls over to make the count. This one is academic. 1… 2… Kickout! [McDaniel] Red Raven kicked out! Can you believe it? Dr. Hate sure can’t, as evidenced by the look of surprise on his face. But that doesn’t slow him down any, nailing Red Raven with a spinning toe hold… Back to work on that injured leg. [Rodgers] Doesn’t this make you sick? The fans have started a "Go, Red, Go" chant, trying to spur Raven on. Dr. Hate gets to his feet, covering his ears… Obviously this fan support for Red Raven is bothering him. [Dr. Hate] SHUT UP, DAMNIT! [McDaniel] He’s none too pleased, for sure – SMALL PACKAGE! Red Raven catches Dr. Hate napping and here’s the count – 1… 2… Kickout! [McDaniel] Wow! He kicked out just barely in time. We almost saw a new Global champion! What incredible tenacity by Red Raven, to take the abuse he has but still have some fight left in him. Hate gets to his feet, absolutely infuriated that Red Raven nearly pinned him. He grabs Raven by the hair, but OW! FIST TO THE NADS! Red Raven buries a shot into Dr. Hate’s groin, causing Hate to fall to his knees. [Rodgers] A burst of energy from Red Raven, who propels himself off the ropes, and NAILS Dr. Hate with a dropkick to the face! That snaps Dr. Hate backwards, and puts him right down to the canvas. Red Raven rolls Hate onto his back, grabs his feet and steps through – FIGURE-FOUR! [McDaniel] Red Raven, obviously sensing his dire predicament, goes for a home run, slapping on his patented figure-four leglock. The ref asks Dr. Hate if he submits, but gets a negative response. Hate tries to reverse it! He lifts himself up as best he can and starts to roll, trying to make Red Raven roll with him… [Rodgers] He’s doing it, too! Dr. Hate reverses the hold, and Red Raven is forced to break off! Obviously the work Hate has done on Red Raven’s leg has paid off, as he just didn’t have the strength to keep Hate locked in. That may have been a last-gasp effort for Red Raven. [McDaniel] Indeed it may have been, because Red Raven appears to have little fight left in him, lying on the canvas like a beaten man. Hate carefully moves in. Seeing no threat, he pulls Red Raven to his feet. ASSISTED SUICIDE! Dr. Hate hits his short lariat finisher, and this thing is done! Here’s the count, as Hate hooks the leg – 1… 2… 3! [Rodgers] Hate wins! At long last, Hate successfully defends his title.
