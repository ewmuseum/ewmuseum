[UCW] Explosion!; 12.11.97, Part 3

14 December 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
____________________________________ \|||||Mohawk Valley Wrestling||||||/ \||||||||||||||||||||||||||||||||/ \||||||One Stunning Grudge|||||/ \||||||||||||Match|||||||||||/ \|||||Scott Alexander||||||/ \|||||||||||vs|||||||||||/ \||||||Tony Ruiz|||||||/ \||||||||||||||||||||/ \||||||||||||||||||/ \|||||||by|||||||/ \|||||[name withheld]||/ \||||||||||/ \||||||||/ \||||||/ \||||/ \||/ \/ MARTIN GRIMMER - Hello once again, folks, this is ... as always ... Martin Grimmer, here with the uncanny Devon Cannons and it looks like we are in for some more great action here for our main event of this second great UCW Explosion! DEVON CANNONS - I don't know about great, I mean neither one of these guys is quite a Shane Slaughter or anything, but then again few are. But when you throw a couple of guys like Alexander and Ruiz together sometimes some interesting sparks can fly. And then sometimes you can get a 20 minute resthold. MARTIN GRIMMER - Well, lets go to Dave Ferry for the formal introductions. DAVID FERRY - Introducing first ...hailing from the South Bronx, New York, standing 5'6 and weighing 190 pounds, here is the one.... the only..... the great TONY RUIZ!!!!!!!! ["Bad Habit" by Offspring plays over the loudspeakers. Tony Ruiz runs down the aisle, ignoring the fan's hands, slides in the ring and smiles at the crowd. He stands on the second turnbuckle and has his arms to the side, continuing to smile, which get's him a huge crowd pop. He's attired in hot pink baggy pants and black boots. He stands there awaiting his opponent.] DEVON CANNONS - Did you see that guy's pants? DAVID FERRY - And his opponent for this evening ... standing 6'1" and weighing in at 232 lbs ... from Basking Ridge, New Jersey, here is the one and only "Stunning" Scott Alexander [Alexander walks down the aisle. He has on White, Black and Gold wrestling tights that have the words "Simply Stunning" on the back. He wears a black leather "Polo Club" logo jacket to the ring. He also wears silver Oakley's. His fiancee and manager, Veronica, is wearing a stunning blue sequin dress, with a slit up the leg going to her upper thigh. Alexander walks up the stairs, leans on the ropes to let Veronica in the ring. He lets go, then leaps over the top rope, climbs to the second turnbuckle, and gives the Polo Club sign. He takes off the jacket to reveal a clean cut physique. He drops the jacket to the ring attendant, takes off his glasses, and gives them to Veronica, who gives them to one of the lucky fans at ringside, the new owner of a pair of $200 Oakley's.] MARTIN GRIMMER - Boy does this Scott Alexander look like he is all business. This looks like a total mis-match. Alexander towers over his opponent. DEVON CANNONS - Thats right, Tony Ruiz is supposed to be self taught, well I have seen him in action before and he sure does look self taught. MARTIN GRIMMER - The opening bell rings and the two lock up. Alexander with an armdrag take down. But Tony is back up. Tony signals for a test of strength. DEVON CANNONS - Thats gotta be the stupidest thing I ever heard, how does that midget expect to match strength with... MARTIN GRIMMER - Big Drop kick by Tony Ruiz! I guess it doesn't matter now does it Mr. Cannons, it seems that sometimes brain power edges out strength. DEVON CANNONS - What a dirty trick, I can't beleive what I am seeing. This Ruiz character is a real piece of trash. MARTIN GRIMMER - Trash or not, he is really going at it with a series of snap leg drops on the downed Scott Alexander. DEVON CANNONS - Now whats he doing? MARTIN GRIMMER - He's climbing the top rope and..... 450 spalsh!!! I can't beleive he hit that move this early in the match. This Tony Ruiz is a house of fire! DEVON CANNONS - He's picking up Alexander by the hair!! The ref should stop this now! MARTIN GRIMMER - Alexander is on his feet, Ruiz chops him a few times for good measure, and irish whip into the ropes. Looks like that Tilt-o-whirl gut buster that he is famous for, but no! DEVON CANNONS - How in the world did he get an arm drag take down from that? MARTIN GRIMMER - That one relly threw Ruiz for a loop, he is still on the mat in shock. Alexander takes advantage of his fallen foe as he drops into an achilles tendon lock. DEVON CANNONS - Thats right! That little punk is writhing in pain on the mat. If he wouldn't have taught himself how to wrestle, he would have probably known how to get out of this hold by now. MARTIN GRIMMER - How about a thumb to the eye! That got him out of the hold. Alexander is up and complaining to the ref, but here comes Ruiz right behind him Thrust kick to the back of the head! sending Alexander and the Ref both flying out of the ring! DEVON CANNONS - That no good cheater! He should be fired for that! I don't like this guy one bit, if I didn't have to be in this broadcast booth, I would go up there and take care of him myself. MARTIN GRIMMER - Yeah right. Anyways, with Alexander and the ref both out of the ring, Ruiz is taking time to mug for the crowd. DEVON CANNONS - That arrogant little punk. I can't wait to see Scott Alexander slap some respect into him. MARTIN GRIMMER - It looks like Alexander and the ref are back on their feet again. Scott Alexander is whispering something to the ref. DEVON CANNONS - Whatever he said made the ref real happy. Maybe he said that he will let him shake his hand after the match or something. MARTIN GRIMMER - I don't know about that, but Alexander has just caught Tony with a low drop kick. now the Ref is running to Scott Alexander. DEVON CANNONS - I can't beleive it, Scott Alexander just gorilla pressed the ref and dropped him on Tony Ruiz!! I love this!! MARTIN GRIMMER - That has to be one of the oddest moves I have ever seen. Alexander has helped the ref to his feet, and is stomping Ruiz in the gut. DEVON CANNONS - He's giving that cheater what he deserves. MARTIN GRIMMER - Alexander is out on the Apron. Sling shot sumersault senton splash! DEVON CANNONS - That did'nt look too good. Scott Alexander hit his forehead on the way over and he is on the mat holding his neck. MARTIN GRIMMER - Thats why they call that a high risk manuever! Both men are down and the ref starts the count. 1...2...3...4...5...6...7...8.... and Tony Ruiz is up. DEVON CANNONS - This match should have been over! They should call this a no-contest! MARTIN GRIMMER - Ruiz is really digging in to Scott Alexander with a cross face chicken wing. I don't know how much of this punishment his neck can take. He has never been quite the same since that incident back in the AWA. DEVON CANNONS - He's got a hand full of hair! The ref should break that hold. MARTIN GRIMMER - Alexander manages to get his foor on the rope and the hold is broken. He looks really out of it at this point, and Ruiz has a taste for blood! DEVON CANNONS - I can't beleive this Ruiz, he is beating on a helpless man in there. How does he live with himself? MARTIN GRIMMER - Ruiz is picking up this poor young man and he pile drives him. I don't know how much more of this kind of abuse scott taylor can take. DEVON CANNONS - He's gonna do it again! I can't watch. MARTIN GRIMMER - Scott Alexander counter with a back drop! This guy is a machine. Ruiz is back on his feet, he rear waist locks the groggy Alexander. Stunning Scott counters by tripping Ruiz and going into a chicken wing arm lock! This is amazing! DEVON CANNONS - Scott Alexander seems to have gotten a second wind. Ruiz is up and they are exchanging punches in the middle of the ring! These guys are really... MARTIN GRIMMER - Something is going on in the crowd at ringside. Can you tell what it is? DEVON CANNONS - It looks like some kind of a bearded man.... wait it's one of the arena's soda pop vendors causing a commotion with some fans. MARTIN GRIMMER - back in the ring during their exchange of fisticuffs, Scott Alexander caught Ruiz with a kick to the knee. Ruiz buckled but... DEVON CANNONS - He just nailed Taylor with a low blow! I can't beleive the ref is letting this go on! MARTIN GRIMMER - Ruiz takes Scott Taylor by the back of the head, and he throws him over the top rope. DEVON CANNONS - Taylor is down by the railing. He better get up before he is counted out! MARTIN GRIMMER - The ref starts the count 1...2....3...4...5... DEVON CANNONS - Tony Ruiz just slapped the refs hand down and tells him to stop counting. Now he slaps the ref in the face! Tony and the Ref are having a shoving match right there in the center of the mat! MARTIN GRIMMER - There is a near riot in the crowd! That unrully vendor is getting closer to ringside by the minute. Security should intercept him any moment now. DEVON CANNONS - Who cares, I can't beleive that you though that a wimp like this Scott Alexander even had a chance at beating a real star like Tony Ruiz. MARTIN GRIMMER - Lets not get too Wishy Washy here Cannons. DEVON CANNONS - Whatever MARTIN GRIMMER - Scott Alexander getting to his feet now and for some reason that vendor keeps on getting closer to the ring. He must be drunk! DEVON CANNONS - I don't know but Scott Alexander just spat at him since he was walking right towards him! The ref is still arguing with Ruiz! MARTIN GRIMMER - Scott Alexander turning around and set to head back into the rin- .....WHAT THE HELL?!?!?!?! That vendor just bashed his crate of sodas on the back of Scott Alexander's head! DEVON CANNONS - I can't believe this! MARTIN GRIMMER - Wait, that's no vendor....that's "Superstar" Scott Storm!! Storm has removed his fake beard and hat and has pulled some kind of metal object from that vending crate. He is hammering it over the knee of Scott Alexander!! DEVON CANNONS - I love it!! Cmon Scott! MARTIN GRIMMER - Scott Storm knows exactly what he is doing, trying to injure the right knee of Scott Alexander before they face off on Sunday. Alexander is yelling in pain as Storm is just slamming that knee on the concrete floor. Scott Alexander is hurt. DEVON CANNONS - The ref hasn't seen any of this yet. What an idiot! MARTIN GRIMMER - Scott Storm is finally leaving, laughing arrogantly at the crowd, but the damage he did to the right knee of Scott Alexander is already done. He pulled a Tonya Harding with that pipe-like object! DEVON CANNONS - He's the "Superstar", he does what he wants to do. MARTIN GRIMMER - Tony Ruiz has finally decided to stop fighting with the ref, now that he has seen that Alexander is down for good. He throws him back in the ring and pins him.....1....2....3. And once again, we've been screwed out of a decent ending to an MVW match. This is rediculous, we'll see you next week when GEWA host UCW's Explosion!
