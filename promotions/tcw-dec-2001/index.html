Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

This TCW surfaces under two names, three days apart, from the same web address. On 28 December 2001 a post asked the newsgroup to “really appreciate it if someone would join the HCW,” naming a shortage of good tag teams and singles wrestlers and pointing to a page hosted on Angelfire. On 31 December a second post, from the same Angelfire address, dropped the HCW name entirely: “hey.. i need some people to join the TCW,” it read, inviting e-mailed thoughts from anyone willing to check the site out.

Both posts are recruitment calls and nothing more — no roster, card, or result accompanies either one, and neither explains the change of name.

Whether HCW and TCW were the same startup renaming itself over New Year's weekend, or two different plans posted from one shared address, isn't settled by the surviving record, and nothing survives past 31 December 2001 to say how either name fared.
