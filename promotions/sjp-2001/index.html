Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Sekai Joshi Puroresu set out to simulate Japanese women's professional wrestling on its own terms: shows written in an abridged report style, monthly television tapings where the storylines moved, and larger supercards to close each tour. Its in-character staff were drawn in detail — an executive booker forced to retire at thirty-five, a head referee who had called every kind of match — and its FAQ asked handlers to bring characters from anywhere in the world.

Applications opened in late November 2001. In December it staged a spot show at the Yokosuka Athletics Meeting Hall to build interest ahead of an official launch set for January 2002: four matches, a time-limit draw in the tag team main event, and a promised rematch once the promotion was properly under way.

The launch was still waiting on more handlers at the turn of the year. Whether the promotion ran its first tour is not recorded.
