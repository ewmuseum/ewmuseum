Staff 3. Rules 4. Shows 5. Titles 6. Participation 7. Application 1. What's the ACE? *Kayfabe* Kyle Humber, a successful businessman from Memphis, Tennessee, started the ACE in July 1999. The league ran well for a month, but then was forced to fold due to business problems.
In July 2000, after almost a year of sitting back at his home in Dyersburg, Tennessee, Kyle decided to restart the ACE. With new sponsors and a brand-new set of wrestlers, the ACE was ready to go.
*Non-kayfabe* I started this league because I was tired of all the late cards that come with other leagues. The ACE promises that most, if not all, of its cards will be on time.
2. Staff *Real* The actual staff behind the ACE would consist of three people: Me, Myself, and I. I am [name withheld], and I am the President, Owner,
Commissioner, Vice President, Head Booker, and Chief of Dealing With
Your Complaints. I can be reached at the following addresses: E-mail: [detail withheld] AIM: [detail withheld] *Fake* Kyle Humber, President/Owner-The owner of the ACE, Kyle is a man in his mid-40's. He likes the sport of wrestling, but he does not know a great deal about booking. He rarely appears on television, and when he does, it's something big.
Edward Sprinkle, Commissioner-A former wrestler, Sprinkle was out of work when he received a call from Mr. Humber. Sprinkle jumped at the chance to become the Commissioner of the ACE. He knows plenty about the politics and business of wrestling, thus he puts together most of the cards and signs the matches.
Jim Black, Commentator-Jim hosted a sports talk show on a Memphis radio station. He now calls the matches on the ACE's shows, with a decent knowledge of wrestling moves.
Rick Dahler, Color Commentator-A known internet wrestling "smart" based out of Memphis, Dahler was signed on as a color commentator. He knows wrestling, and he tends to like guys who have talent--whether or not the fans hate them.
Bill Mix, Ring Announcer-Previously, Bill was known as Jim Black's wimpy sidekick on his morning radio show. He was signed on after he begged Black to get him a job when he heard Black's show was being cancelled.
Christina York, Interviewer-The weather girl on a local TV station, Christina, a pretty, blond young woman, now doubles as the interviewer for the ACE. She may not have great interview skills, but most of the wrestlers are happy to give her an interview.
Kenny Zachary, Referee-The ACE's only referee. Zachary calls the matches right down the middle, and is not known to accept bribes. Sorry.
3. Rules There aren't many. But let's get right down to it. 1. A handler may have no more than one wrestler or one tag team. 2. No monsters-I'd say about 7'4", maybe 500 pounds is the largest wrestler I'll accept. You have to consider that almost anyone over that height will have a career in the NBA if they can put one foot in front of the other. And if they can't... well, they probably can't wrestle, either.
3. I ask that you send at least one, but no more than three, roleplays per week. They will be sent out in our regular "flash packets." 4. Keep cursing to a bare minimum. You can say "ass," "bitch," and "damn," but anything more than that must be [beep]ed out. No racial slurs, talking about someone's sexual preferences, etc.
5. If you want to complain, I'll listen to your complaints, but keep in mind that it's not going to help you win any matches. If I really have made a bad booking decision, I'll make up for it, but there's no changing a loss.
6. The winner of a match is determined by roleplaying, strategies, and angles. If you lose, there's a reason for it. But it's mostly determined by roleplaying, so stick to that and you should be okay.
7. I frankly don't care that you were ranked in the RSPWF 200 last year, or what titles you've held in ____ federation. Those are nice to know, but they're not going to help you win in the AWL. I care about what you do here, not somewhere else.
8. Have fun! 4. Shows ACE Friday Night Fury-The ACE's main weekly show, shown live on Friday nights from 8-10 PM, CST. It features 5-7 matches, as well as interviews with the ACE's top stars.
ACE American Standard-The ACE's second show. It airs on Monday nights from 8-10 PM, CST, and features 5-7 matches as well. However, it generally features the lesser-known wrestlers of the ACE.
Pay-Per-Views: The ACE will hold a pay-per-view every other month. These pay-per-views have yet to be named, but they will be announced in the future. 5. Titles ACE World Heavyweight Title-The big title of the ACE. The champion has the luxury of only having to defend his title once a month. But he also becomes the most hunted man in the ACE, even though only the number one contender can challenge him for his title.
ACE American Title-The ACE's second title. Seen as a stepping stone to the World Title, the holder of this belt has to defend his title twice a month. The top five wrestlers in the league can challenge for this title.
ACE Tag Team Titles-Currently the only tag team belts in the league. Any two wrestlers can challenge for these titles, which must be defended once a month. (NOTE: The title defenses are the minimums. The wrestler(s) may defend his belt(s) more often than required, if they so choose.)
6. Participation Every wrestler is required to participate once a week. Participation involves roleplaying, strategies, or in-rings.
Roleplaying: The ACE uses a "closed-flash" system. This means that you will send your roleplays to me only, and I will send them out in a packet on a regular basis. I generally ask that all roleplays be at least 15 lines long.
Strategies-Strategies are not required, but they help me write your match a great deal. Include your basic strategy for the match, as well as any spots that you'd like in the match.
In-rings-Only a select few wrestlers will get to do in-rings on Fury. I will contact those whom I would like to send in-rings for the show. 7. Application So, you've finally made it. Anyway, simply cut and paste this into an e-mail, and send it to
[detail withheld] . A couple of notes: -As I've said before, no wrestlers larger than 7'4", 500 pounds. And having an extremely large wrestler does not, in any way, help you win matches. -Entrances should be kept fairly simple. We're a national fed, but I don't want to see a whole lot of laser light shows. You can have some pyros, and maybe a spotlight or something like that. And by the way, we don't have anything resembling a "Titan-Tron."
