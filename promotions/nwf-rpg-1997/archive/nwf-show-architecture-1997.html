NWF: How The Federation Is Run

19 August 1997

Reply to: [detail withheld] (``The_Expert``_) R 37 08/19 04:59 WWIVnet ->8213 R 36 08/19 01:56 WWIVnet 8320->8304 R 34 08/19 01:28 WWIVNet ->8320 How the Network Wrestling Federation is Run: There are "TV Tapings" twice a week. These also setup the "PPV" type specials. Not every wrestler will wrestle on every TV taping, but most wrestlers will be involved in a PPV. All TV Tapings are 4-7 matches. TV Tapings: 1. NWF Monday in Manhattan (Monday) (This show always takes place from a Nightclub-venue in New York.) 2. NWF Weekend Warriors (Friday) (This show takes place from various arenas across the country) Pay-Per-View Specials: 1. Waikiki Wipeout (Early January) 2. May-Day Massacre (End of April) 3. The Wheel of Torture (Early June) 4. Supercard (Mid-July) 5. Autumn Armageddon (Early September) 6. Halloween is Mean! (End of October) 7. Christmas Carnage (End of December) NOTE: Some of the pay-per-views will have a special format, which will be announced at a later time. Cable Specials: 1. Clash of Greats (February, March, May, August, November) House Shows: 1. Ocassionally, House Show Tours will take place. Basically, these will occur whenever the promotors have extra time to create some fresh cards. These generally will not contained as detailed results as the TV tapings, PPV's, or Cable Specials. Each match is determined randomly with dice or a computer by the promoters. You will have to take our word that we are being honest. Then after I know the winner and how they win (pin, countout, DQ) then we can create the details of the match. Some things you can do to participate: Challenge: You can challenge a wrestler to a match. Attack : You can arrange a sneak attack against a wrestler, will only allow one attack per TV taping by a wrestler. Also, on PPV's I will not allow attacks. Accept : You can accept challenges Open Contract: You can post an open contract in the NWF Wrestler's Quote sub. That simply means that you will accept anyone's contract, at any given time. Pair Up : You can choose a tag team partner or join a "corporation." Break up : Fairly or violently, you can break off with your tag team partner. Obviously, this is not all that you can do, but we just wanted to give you the general idea of what can go on in the role-playing wrestling federations! Once a week, we will post the singles and tag team ratings. Usually, after we post the results of the TV taping, the card for the next TV taping will posted as well. Sometimes, we will post something special. This may be a PPV/Cable Special Update or some kind of little editorial. It may be the "Roll Call of Champions" or the NWF Roster, as well.
