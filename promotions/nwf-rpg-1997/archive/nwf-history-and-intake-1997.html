TYPE: NWF001A WWIVNet Host: 8320 NWF Updates & Wrestler's Quotes TYPE: NWF002B WWIVNet Host: 8320 THE NWF'S HISTORY: The NWF was re-established by Stevie Cheesecake in March 1994. It was a Califoria based wrestling modeming role-playing game, and the first of it's kind. He had about 25 wrestlers, and we had our first PPV event, The Wheel of Torture in July 1994. Then tragedy struck. He had to take down his board and let the game go. He pleaded for someone to take over the game, so The Expert did. It didn't change really that much except The Expert tried to make the federation as realistic as he possibly could, with loved fan favorites, and hated rulebreakers. The Expert operated the growing league until November 1994. Due to time constraints, the NWF was forced into a brief hiatus until January 1995. To ensure that the league would not fold, The Expert handed over the reigns to Raging Bull in February 1995. The Raging Bull promoted The Network Wrestling Federation with a little help from The Expert and Starslayer until August 1996. Time constraints caused him to shutdown the game until February 1997. He ran one card and realized that he did not have enough time to devote to the promotion. So, late in the summer of 1997, Raging Bull and The Expert decided to reform the game as co-promotors. The Network Wrestling Federation will resume competiton beginning on September 23rd 1997. We have mostly nodes on the east coast, but have had them as far away as Montreal. The NWF has inspired many other modemers to create their own games, but we are the original. We are the first and foremost of modem wrestling roleplaying games. This is the game that started the craze!!! The Network Wrestling Federation is a pro wrestling RPG. To play you just need to create a wrestler (please be original, copycats of real wrestler will not be allowed due to possible copyright problems --I can allow you to use some parts, (nickname, finisher) but real name use of a wrestler will not be allowed).. To play just hit A at the prompt and leave me this information: A) Wrestler's Name B) Wrestler's Stats (Height, Weight) C) Wrestler's Image (a brief profile of him) D) Wrestler's Finishing Move (and possible nickname for the move) (example: Scorpion Death Lock> Sharpshooter or Reverse Neckbreaker is called the Rude Awakening) Also you cannot duplicate another player's finisher. Everyone now and then I may let a wrestler duplicate another's finisher. E) 3 moves that setup your finisher (there can be duplications here) F) You may have a manager. You must email me with a short profile of him, and what kind of "weapon" he or she carries, if any. In some role-playing wrestling games, each player is allowed to have several wrestlers. In the NWF, this will not be the case. A player may only have more than one wrestler if it is a tag team situation, where one person is using both characters. Also, you must use an entirely new character that you aren't currently playing in a different federation. (A select group of "NWF Greats" will be allowed to use the same characters if they so desire.) Have fun and thanks for playing. Other posts will follow about the game, but first you must subscribe to the necessary subs. Hit "A" now on how to join or just fill out the above, and send it to one of the below email addresses. This type of game is every pro wrestling fan's dream. All wrestling fans should play this game. It is very easy to play. Just email answering the questions, (A-F) and have your SysOp subscribe to the necessary subs, listed above. After you subscribe to the subs, other posts about the game will follow. The first sixteen competitors will be entered into our NWF World Title Tournament, which will be begin on September 23rd.
