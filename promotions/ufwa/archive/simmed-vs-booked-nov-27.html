I will admit you're right to an extent on the simmed/booked thing...maybe the UFWA was just the exception to the rule. When I joined I knew nothing about fantasy wrestling or roleplay (outside of my fantasy and sci-fi experience). I didn't roleplay as much as I should and lets just say my character (Sabbath) quickly went under. I learned swiftly however...my typical RP when I first joined was 2-3 KB...at closing my typical RP was 7 KB up to 20+KB and I was ranked #1. Their were *MANY* great RPers in the UFWA. As for booked being more realistic...to an extent, yes and to an extent, no. TNM allows a lot of customization options...and you can book matches on TNM as well. Booked allows for more strategy...and stuff along those lines.
