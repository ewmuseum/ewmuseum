Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The Alpha Wrestling Alliance began, by its own account, on America Online, where its recruitment post of 14 November 1996 says it had been “running strong for nearly 3 months” and had climbed to number one among AOL's own fantasy wrestling leagues. Its own operator brought it across to the Internet newsgroup, keeping the AOL membership on while opening the league to new signings there. At the time of that post it carried seven active titles, a number it says changed roughly every month as more were added, and it was in the middle of its third signing period, with room for only two or three more entrants.

No further post under this name survives after that November 1996 recruitment notice.

No card, roster or title result from any point of the Alpha Wrestling Alliance's run is on record, and how or when it closed is not known either.
