USPW All American Wrestling

Monday, Week 3, December 2007

Held at Perry Hall

Att. 2,000 (Sell-Out)

Dark Matches

-Trent Shaffer defeated Brett Hall, Brendan Idol and Giant Oak in 6:35 when Trent Shaffer defeated Brendan Idol by pinfall with a Heart Burn. (C-)

-Frankie Perez defeated Reaper in 7:45 by pinfall with a Perfect Parity. (B-) (I'd like to say I know what Reaper is actually capable of already, but then I would be lying.)

Main Show

-And we kick off the follow up to our biggest show and biggest segment in history inside of the Divinity locker room. Seduction sits on the couch while Devine chooses to stand. Across from the two of them are Maxmillion Productions, Zeus Maxmillion, Rudy Velasquez and T-Rex.

DDD: "Now one of you had better tell me exactly how you came to find out how Wolf Hawkins was under the mask? Which he wasn't by the way."

Maxmillion: "I told you already, we had a source."

DDD: "Who?"

Maxmillion: "I can't reveal that."

DDD: "And why exactly is that?"

Maxmillion: "They wish to remain anonymous."

DDD: "Well if you wish to stay alive you'd better tell me."

And all of a sudden the lights go out. Devine shouts out for Giedroyc to turn the lights back on and face him man to man. Then the lights come back on and over at the light switch is Cameron Vessey. Devine stares in his direction looking as though his murderous intentions have switched targets.

Vessey: "What's everyone staring at me for?"

DDD: "You little wise ass son of a bitch! You think that was funny?!"

Vessey: "Huh? Oh, you mean the light thing. My bad."

DDD: "That's all you have to say for yourself?"

And just then the lights go out again. "Don't look at me this time." Vessey can be heard saying in the darkness. Once the lights come back on Maxmillion Productions are gone and in their spot is Reaper. Seduction is freaked out as is Vessey. Devine looks as though he's found what he's looking for as that sadistic grin of his comes back to his face.

DDD: "So he sends you to do his job for him? Well I want you to give him a message from me."

Reaper pulls something out from his sleeve, a rolled up piece of paper and holds it above the table. The lights go out and once they're back on 'The Angel of Death' has disappeared and on the table is the paper he was holding. Devine takes the rubber band off of it, unrolls it and reads it. Once he's done he crumbles it up and throws it at the wall.

Seduction: "What did it say?"

DDD: "It says he's going to have an answer for my challenge tonight."

Seduction: "Oh."

DDD: "Seduction, tell those two little bastards of yours that they've been volunteered to take care of Maxmillion Productions. And tell them that if they f*ck this up, and they probably will, they're dead. Tell them that."

Seduction: "Sure."

Vessey: "Standing right here you know."

(C)

-Backstage in a dark corridor somewhere in the building Giant Oak is waiting for someone. Out of the shadows appears The Flash.

Oak: "Thanks for coming. I'm really gonna need somebodies help on this."

Flash: "No problem citizen Oak. Ridding the world of villains is the primary goal of a superhero such as myself."

Oak: "I need you to prove to Wanda that The Sensational Starfish is actually Pexx. She won't believe me, but you, you she might believe."

Flash: "How is he connected to this?"

Oak: "He's Phlexes partner, and I think Phlex is just using her to get the title. Pexx gives something to Jillefski and then later that night he announces Phlex gets a shot at the National Title?"

Flash: "That does sound like a conspiracy. I will help you solve this case Citizen Oakley."

Oak: "Thanks Steve."

Flash: "Never reveal a superheros super secret identity citizen."

Oak: "Okay, sorry."

Flash: "Apology accepted, don't let it happen again."

The Flash disappears back into the shadows leaving Oak by himself.

Oak: "Am I the only one normal around here anymore?"

(D+)

vs.

-Mantis vs. The Trademark-

This was Mantis' first televised match and he's off to a good start. Mantis looked good in his debut against The Trademark. Morgan got in some offense, but the majority of it belonged to Mantis. Mantis finished the Trademark off with his finishing maneuver, The Preying Mantis. Mantis perches on the top rope and waits for his opponent to stand and turn towards him. He then comes down at them with a chop to the head of his opponent, his hands clapped together.

Winner: Mantis in 6:06

Rating (D)

-After we get back from a commercial break we see The Trademark walking backstage somewhere. He stops as he sees Bulldozer Brandon Smith and clears his throat to get his attention.

Trademark: "*Ahem* The Trademark is here on behalf of the Mantis to lay out a challenge to the Bulldozer. The Mantis says that if The Bulldozer ever wants to see The Bulldozers belt again the Bulldozer will accept The Mantis' challenge for a match for The Bulldozers belt at Winter Epiphany."

Dozer: "You got some nerve coming back here and sayin' that to me boy."

Trademark: "The Trademark is merely a puppet, a messenger. Please don't hurt The Trademark."

Dozer: "Alright, tell you what. I'm going to give you a message to give to that buggy freak."

Trademark: "The Trademark is truly grateful for the lack of physical pain involved in The Trademarks delivery."

Dozer takes his fist, wrapped with the thick chain and smashes it into The Trademarks skull knocking him out with a single blow. "Now you deliver that message to him."

(E+)

-We now go to footage shot earlier in the day showing The Dudez!!!1!!! out at the beach catching some waves. Perez and Hernandez are out in the sea on their surfboards when the camera and the Dudez catch something on the shore. Two men and a White Tiger waiting for them to ride back in. The Dudez decide to wait out in the water until VM go away. Unfortunately for them Force, Oxford and Mr. Snuggleforce end up coming after them anyways. Perez and Hernandez paddle away as fast as they can.

"Dammit, I thought cats couldn't swim dude."

(C)

-And backstage we see Giant Redwood pounding on the door of Wanda Fish and Phlexes locker room shouting out obscenities I could swear the censor told him not to. "Get out here you fishy little bitch! I know what you did to my boy! Now get out here so I can set you straight!" After nearly a minute of pounding and shouting Phlex steps out into the hall.

Phlex: "What the hell do you think you are doing you fat, slobbery son of a bitch?"

Redwood: "Get that little bitch out here so I can beat some respect into her!"

Phlex: "You will not talk about my innocent little flower in that manner!"

Redwood: "And what the hell are you going to do about it needledick?"

Phlex: "You rotund, hebetudinous waste of oxygen, if you say so much as one more ill word of my love I shall myself make bacon out of you to feed the hungry children of the world!"

Redwood: "Tell that little twat of a girlfriend of yours-"

Phlex: "That's it Lard-Lad, you and me in that ring now!"

(C-)

vs.

-Giant Redwood vs. Phlex-

He can save the son, but no one can save the father. That sounds almost poetic. Too bad the match wasn't. Redwoods unique match ruining capabilities shone through here. Phlex was in control of this match throughout and didn't give Redwood a chance to get in any offense. Phlex destroyed Redwood and put him away with a Romeo is Bleeding.

Winner: Phlex in 3:55

Rating (D)

-Backstage we see Human Arsenal in a hallway somewhere in the arena still pissed about last week. He had the champ beat, but things wouldn't work out in his favor. Arsenal is tossing things around letting out steam.

"You know, my mama always taught me to respect others property." Freddie Datsun says after watching Arsenal toss countless objects around for a good minute or two.

Arsenal: "Mind your own business."

Datsun: "Well sorry, but I think I'm gonna make this my business. There are some very nice stagehands whose stuff you're just tossing around like that."

Arsenal: "If you're looking for a fight you picked a very good day to go looking for one."

Datsun: "Now I ain't done come here looking for a fight, but if you don't apologize to the people whose stuff you're tossing around I will come after you."

Arsenal: "Not sure you know this, but apologies ain't exactly my forte."

Datsun: "So I take it you ain't gonna apologize."

Arsenal: "Why don't you go try to pass off this good will somewhere where people might actually buy it."

Datsun: "Now I take offense to that statement."

Arsenal: "Then do something about it."

Datsun gets ready to attack when down from the vents who should pop out but Fumihiro Ota, Super Ninja. Ota holds a shuriken to Datsuns throat.

Datsun: "I see you want to play dirty."

Arsenal: "And I still see you here. Leave willingly or leave in a body bag. It's up to you."

Datsun: "You know, for someone who despises Devine so much you sure remind me of him right now." You can just tell by the look on Arsenals face that Datsun struck a chord. "What's the matter? Truth hurt?"

Arsenal: "Ota, beat the redneck in a match."

(B-)

-T-Rex is in the back at a vending machine trying to get a candy bar, but it unfortunately gets stuck. Rex starts pounding at the machine trying to get it loose and to fall down to him. Then, crack. Rex's head is smashed into the thick plastic from behind. Devine then takes his head and smashes it into the glass repeatedly until Rex is gushing out blood. Devine tosses him to the ground in front of the vending machine. Devine isn't done though. He takes the machine and tips it over crushing Rex under it. Rex lies there seemingly lifeless as Devine crouches down and gives him a message. "Tell your friends, they're next."

(B-)

vs.

-Zeus Maxmillion vs. Cameron Vessey-

This is all good chemistry gets me? What a gyp. Vessey and Maxmillion were very evenly matched, but in the end Zeus got the better of Vessey. He slammed Vessey down with a Thunder Bolt and picked up the win.

Winner: Zeus Maxmillion in 10:43

Rating (C)

vs.

-Freddie Datsun vs. Fumihiro Ota-

And Ota and Datsun put on a great match together. Ota kept up with Datsun, but The American Everyman proved too much for The Super Ninja. Datsun caught Ota when he went for a Ninja Strike and dropped him with a Patriot Press, 1...2...3.

Winner: Freddie Datsun in 17:09

Rating (B+)

-Muffin is walking out of the arena cafeteria with a bottle of water in her hand. She walks down the hall to where ever she may be off to. She's grabbed by the shoulder suddenly and turns around to see who's stopping her.

Muffin: "Oh god Darryl, you scared me."

DDD: "Good."

Muffin: "What do you want?"

DDD: "You knew."

Muffin: "I knew? Knew what?"

Devine clasps down on Muffins shoulder tighter and backs her up to the wall.

DDD: "Don't play games with me."

Muffin: "I don't know what the hell you're talking about!"

DDD: "The Masked Man! You knew who it was all along!"

Muffin: "No I didn't!"

DDD: "Don't play stupid with me bitch, I'm not in the mood. I remember what you said. You told me Hayes wasn't under the mask. You knew."

Muffin: "I didn't know who was under the mask, I just knew it wasn't William."

DDD: "Don't lie to me! You knew."

Muffin: "I swear to you Darryl I didn't."

DDD: "Not gonna admit it, huh? Am I going to have to get it out of you the hard way?"

Muffin: "I'm telling you the truth you psychopath!"

DDD: "You know, you used to be just a whiny little bitch who I could play mind games with. Now, I'm going to have to hurt you."

Devine pulls back his fist ready to strike her, but it's caught on the end of a billy club. One in the possession of Rick Law.

Law: "You strike that young lady and I'm gonna have to take the law into my own hands Devine."

DDD: "And who are you to stop me from going about my business? And this better be good because you're pissing me off."

Law: "I'm the law around these parts now. And you're lucky this is the first time I've run into you because I know about all of the things you've done."

DDD: "Well Johnny Law-"

Law: "Rick Law."

DDD: "Why don't you stop beating around the bush and get on with it? Hit me. Hit me and see how long you last!"

Law: "If you're challenging me to a match I accept that challenge."

DDD: "I hope for your sake you keep a will."

Devine walks off and Law tends to Muffin.

(B)

-Backstage in the Divinity locker room Seduction sits The Hotshots, Cameron Vessey and Davey Wayne down and prepares them.

Seduction: "Congratulations you two."

Wayne: "Congrats on what?"

Vessey: "Did you kill Joss? Or our community service sentence? Or Joss?"

Vessey: "Again?"

Seduction: "Again."

Wayne: "But that's not going to happen, is it?"

Vessey: "But even if we do lose we're still in, right?"

Seduction: "Did you two see what he did to Rex?"

Wayne: "You really think he would..."

Seduction: "*Nods*"

Vessey: "Eep."

Wayne: "Why don't you stand up to him?"

Seduction: "Do you know anyone who can beat him?"

Wayne: "Arsenal had him. We all saw it."

Seduction: "And yet who won the match?"

Wayne: "But he had him beat."

Seduction: "So long as Darryl is in good graces with Jillefski and his goons he can't be stopped."

Vessey: "Isn't Danny kind of pissed about the Wolf thing though?"

Seduction: "Giedroyc has a good idea of what he's doing, but in the end he's still going to lose."

(C-)

vs.

-Rick Law vs. Triple D-

Law and Devine really went at it here. Law kept up well with The Divine One and came close to beating him on a few occasions. Devine pulls out a few dirty tricks to keep Law at bay, which angers Muffin. She tries to inform the ref, but it ends up hurting Law. Law goes for a Long Arm of The Law as Devine turns his attentions back towards him, but Devine ducks under it and pulls the club out of Laws belt. As Law turns around Devine tosses the club to him and then drops to the mat and lays there as though he was hit. The ref turns around to see this scene and puts 2 and 3 together. And gets 4. He instantly DQs Law.

Winner: Triple D by DQ in 17:59

Rating (A)

-Law is still in a state of shock and confusion and begins to argue with the ref that he was framed, but he doesn't believe him. Devine springs back to his feet and from behind Law he yanks the club from his hand. Law turns around just in time to get nailed in the side of the gut. Devine then cracks him in the back of the skull. He tosses the club down and walks toward the ropes. Muffin slides into the ring to check on Law who's bleeding from the back of his head. Devine stops midway through the ropes as he notices what's going on behind him as he looks at the tron. Devine backs up into the ring, Muffin unaware he came back. Devine picks the club up as he walks over. He picks Muffin up by her hair and savors the moment for a second or two, and as he goes for that first blow...

darkness engulfs the arena. Once the lights are back up Muffin is out of the ring as is Law and in Muffins place is The Angel of Death. Devine doesn't looked surprised by it and swings the club in Reapers direction. Reaper catches the club in mid swing and rips it out of Devines hand tossing it out of the ring. Reaper grabs Devine by the throat and prepares to hoist him up, but the lights go out again. As they come up we see Giedroyc has him arm interjected between Devine and Reaper. Reaper lets go of Devines throat setting him free. Giedroyc on the other hand pulls Devines jaw over his shoulder and sends Devine down with a Crashing On (kneeling jaw breaker). Reaper kicks Devines body out of the ring as Giedroyc receives a mic.

Giedroyc: "Now I'm sure you all have questions, and you shall be glad to know I have answers. I know you can hear me out there Devine, so pay attention. The question I shall answer first is a simple one, why did I come to USPW? The reason is to stop Divinity, but more specifically Devine himself. If you'd all remember back to what happened on October 1st 2007, that night Devine showed the world what kind of man he truly was and left Alicia Strong in tears. That however wasn't enough for him. He ended the career of a rising young star and the only man at that time who could've stopped him. You had William Hayes dragged out of the building and fired that night. And then you thought victory was yours, didn't you Devine?

You beat Sam Strong on October 17th and thought that USPW was conquered, that there was no one left who could possibly stop you. And you might have been correct. You see I decided on the 1st of that month that somebody had to stop you. I was to be that person, but not because I wanted to be, it was His willing. And on the 17th, as you thought USPW had hit it's 11th hour, that it was yours for the taking, I made sure you knew it wasn't over. And why didn't I just come as I am you may wonder. It was mind games Devine. I got to you before you could get to me. I played it up like I was Hayes to drive you mad and as everyone knows, I succeeded. And now it's time for me to what no other man can do. Devine, to answer your question, I accept your challenge for Winter Epiphany. I will stop you Devine. He has spoken, and The Wrath of God is upon your miserable soul."

USPW Family Night

Thursday, Week 3, December 2007

Held at The West Texas Coliseum

Att. 2,000 (Sell-Out)

Dark Matches

-Giant Oak defeated Thomas Morgan, Archnemesis and Brendan Idol in 7:07 when Giant Oak defeated Thomas Morgan by pinfall with an Oak Chop of Doom ~!. (D+)

-Reaper defeated Bulldozer Brandon Smith in 8:23 by pinfall with a Fade To Black. (C)

Main Show

-USPW World Champion Triple D enters the building now on what's sure to be another big episode of USPW Family Night. Last week was the unveiling of The Masked Man who turned out to be Jack 'Wrath of God' Giedroyc, the debut of Reaper and the public beatdown of Wolf Hawkins. Devine doesn't get more than two steps through the door when he's smashed in the head courtesy of a very pissed off Wolf Hawkins. Hawkins slams the chair into Devines gut a few times before tossing it aside. He takes what appears to be a sock out of his jacket pocket and stuffs it down Devines throat. Hawkins gets down and starts pummeling away at Devine exacting some measure of revenge on The Divine One. It doesn't take long for USPW security being barked orders from owner Danny Jillefski to step in and pull Hawkins off of Devine. And even more security to keep Devine from now going after Hawkins and finishing the job from a week ago.

(B+)

-And now from a crazy scene to a crazy senile. Robert Oxford prepares to give an interview about his upcoming match with Mainstream Hernandez.

Clare: "So Mr. Oxford, what are your thoughts going into your match with Mainstream Hernandez."

Oxford: "Back in my day the tely-vision bill was only as much as tin foil cost. And back in my day it only cost a nickel a square pound. Nowadays everything's all mainstream and expensive as all bejebus. I remember when a phone was only a dollar and you could stick it to the wall. Now I almost swallowed my fancy new fangled cell-e-lurgh phone-a-majig. And my opponent tonight represents what my generation hates, change. I remember when the movie pictures were only a nickel, and it was 25 miles uphill there and back walking all the way to get there. And I could find a date and put a hole in the bottom of the popcorn bucket and have homemade butter on my popcorn."

Clare: "Uh, could you get back on track Mr. Oxford?"

Oxford: "Back in my day only queers had blonde hair. I shots me a lot of queers back the great intergalactic wars of 2012. And then one of them egg shaped, blonde headed queers shot me with a ray gun. I disintegrated instantly and now here I stand before you the bionic man. My hip is made out of 100% plastic, made in China!"

Oxford walks off for his match leaving Clare confused just what the hell happened.

(C)

vs.

-Mainstream Hernandez vs. Robert Oxford-

A good contest between these two tag teamers. In the end the psychotic geriatric was able to stave off all pain and completely ignore his sense of feeling putting Hernandez away with a Mental Breakdown DDT.

Winner: Robert Oxford in 11:50

Rating (C+)

-Wanda Fish is alone in her locker room at the moment reading her copy of Game Informer. A knock comes at her door and she goes to answer it. "Who is it?" "It is your friendly neighborhood Flash." Fish opens the door and greets The Flash. Flash and Fish both have a seat as Flash has told her there's something he needs to talk to here about. Flash lays the cards out on the table and tells Fish that Phlex is just using her to get the National title and that he's being aided by his partner Pexx under the guise of The Sensational Starfish. Fish doesn't believe a thing Flash says and takes offense to it thinking Oak paid him to say it or something along those lines. Flash states that he's just trying to help the heroes and vanquish the villains. Fish is livid at the accusation that her lover Phlex would do anything to harm her and challenges Flash to a match.

(C)

-Triple D is pacing around the interview area with a few bandages on his forehead and is holding an ice pack up to the side of his head.

Clare: "And I'm now here with USPW World Champion Triple D after the ass whooping he got at the hands of Wolf Hawkins."

Infuriated by what she just said Devine rips the mic from her hands and begins speaking.

DDD: "Ass whooping?! Who the hell are you to say something like that about me you little tramp? It wasn't an ass whooping, he jumped me as I walked into the building and he did it with a steel chair! That little bastard didn't even have the balls to fight me like a man. He had to rely on what that pompous SWF reject of a teacher of his taught him, never play fair. He wants a fight, I'll give him a fight. I'll give him a fight he's not going to forget any lifetime soon. You want me Hawkins, come get me! I'll be in the ring at the end of the night. If you've got a set on you then you'll be there and you'll wrestle me one on one and I'll show you just who in the hell really is the better man. Then you can go back Camp Cornell with your tail tucked between your legs howling the blues.

And while I'm on the subject of people I'm going to kill, let's talk about the man who's the whole reason I had to do what I had to do to Hawkins when I thought he was under the mask. Giedroyc, it won't be long now. In only nine days it will be me versus you in the middle of the ring at Winter Epiphany. And when I beat you to within an inch of your life you'll go crawling back to whatever dark corner you've been living in and stay there. You think I buy that nobility crap you're spewing? 'I came to USPW to stop Devine, I came to save the company,' you came because you saw an opportunity and I'll get you to admit it one way or the other. You came to raise your stock in the wrestling world. You pretended to be someone else to get a World Championship Match in the biggest company in the United States. That's alright Jackie-boy, not like you're going to win anyways. You can take that Wrath of God shtick and shove it because it ain't going to help you when you experience the full wrath of The Divine One."

(B)

vs.

-The Flash vs. Wanda Fish-

Flash didn't want to hurt Fish, but Fish wouldn't return the sentiment still furious that anyone would ever accuse her love of doing her wrong. Fish destroyed Flash until Flash finally started to fight back disregarding his friendship with Wanda. The two were evenly matched throughout the rest of the contest, but Fish pulled through and locked in the Blonde Ambition (figure 4). Flash had no choice but to tap.

Winner: Wanda Fish in 12:54

Rating (B+)

-Backstage Human Arsenal is punching away at a wall still taking his frustrations out on whatever he can find.

"Now what did that there wall ever do to you?" Freddie Datsun asks stepping into the scene.

Arsenal turns around and confronts the American Everyman. "Do you want to get hurt? Is that it?"

Datsun: "No, I'm actually here to challenge you to a match. Since I beat your partner already and all."

A shuriken flies down from the vent and out pops an angry ninja. Arsenal holds onto Ota to keep him from doing something the cops would be looking for him for.

Arsenal: "Hold on Ota, he challenged me to a match. What kind of sportsmanship would that be to just lay him out now? I accept your challenge Freddie, see you in the ring."

Datsun: "I think you may have misunderstood. You're not going to be wrestling me tonight. Since I wrestled your partner already I figured I'd give you a chance to do the same.

Good luck tonight against Umaga."

(C)

-USPW Owner Danny Jillefski is in the tail end of a conversation with someone on the phone, and he doesn't sound to pleased.

Jillefski: "What do you mean no? I've been helping you out for years now and I ask one favor of you and you flat out refuse me? Who the hell do you think you are? I know what you are, but who are you to say no to me? All I ask is to have him here for the PPV and you're going to say no to me? Well you know what, I don't care about getting Peak here anymore. Don't ask me for anymore favors Vibert. What do you mean you'll think about it? No, that's not good enough. It's either yes or you've lost a very powerful business partner."

Jillefski slams down the phone and slumps down in his chair now looking deep in thought. "He said he'll think about it. Can you believe that? After all we've given him." Jillefski thinks some more. "Looks like Operation Justice just got pushed forward."

(C)

vs.

-Umaga w/ Stranton vs. Human Arsenal w/ Seduction-

The Near-Unstoppable Samoan Savage takes on The Wrestling Machine in what turned out to be an amazing match. An unpredictable one too as Umaga has never been pinned and Arsenal has been on a roll as of late. It was a clash of styles to say the least, Umagas animalistic brawling versus Arsenal precision technique. The two fought over the course of 17 minutes never giving an inch to the other and in the end both managers got sick of it. Seduction distracted the ref while Ota got into the ring stealthily and Stranton used that as an opportunity to even the odds. Ota struck Umaga with a kick to the back which he barely felt and Stranton took off his shoe and whacked Arsenal in the back of the head with it. Needless to say it didn't work. The ref turns around to see Ota and Stranton in the ring and calls for the bell.

Winner: Draw by Double DQ in 17:39

Rating (A)

-We now get what starts as a very mysterious video. Words flash across the screen in bold white text against a pitch black background. 'He's a tenth degree black belt.' 'Trained in Muy Thai.' 'NCAA Wrestling Champion.' 'Trained by the best.' 'He's coming.' And now we get video of a man in what looks like an abandoned warehouse practicing his strikes on a punching bag.
