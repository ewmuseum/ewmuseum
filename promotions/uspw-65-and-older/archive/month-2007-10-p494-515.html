USPW All American Wrestling

Monday, Week 4, October 2007

Held at The Laurent Ballroom

Att. 3,394

Dark Matches

-Steve Flash defeated Giant Redwood in 2:58 by pinfall with a Flash Bang. (C+) (Good Chem.) (OMG, this is the greatest match Redwood will ever be part of)

-Akima defeated T-Rex in 4:43 by pinfall with a Savage Headbutt. (C+) (Best match T-Rex has been part of too.)

-Bruce The Giant defeated Davey Wayne in 5:44 by pinfall with a Giant Choke Slam. (B) (Great Chemistry) (As Ron Simmons would say: "DAMN!")

Main Show

-And we start off this show with Alicia Strong, but not unconscious like last time (Poor Muffin, she really does end up in the punching bag role in most dynasties). Alicia enters the arena being helped and watched over by her father, the legendary Sam Strong. Muffin has a bandage wrapped around her head most likely covering up a nasty gash from the attack last Thursday. Sam obviously looks concerned about the well being of his daughter as he isn't letting her out of his sights for a second.

Muffin: "You really don't have to be doing this daddy."

Sam: "Yes I do because I'm not letting those bastards do this to you anymore my little muffin."

(B+)

-And from that we go to a close up of USPW World Champion Triple D. The camera pans out to reveal Devine standing by with our new interviewer Jennifer Clare.

Clare: "I'm standing here with USPW Champion Triple D. Now, Mr. Devine, We all saw what happened to Muffin last week. Are you honestly denying any involvement?"

DDD: "First off, it's 'The Divine One' to you bimbo. Now as for what you just said, Are trying to imply that I did that to my ex?"

Clare: "I never said you did, but anything is possible."

DDD: "Congratulations, you're on my sh*t list after less than a minute. So you're trying to tell me you think I'm some heartless animal that would hurt a woman for some petty little reason? No, you know what? Don't even bother answering that. I know what you women think like, it's always the ex-boyfriends fault. They get a papercut, my fault. They break a nail, my fault. They drop a plate, my fault. They fall down the stairs, my fault. They're a whore, my fault. They inexplicably come out of the closet, my fault.

For all I know she was run over by a bus on Thursday. Now unless you're here to interrogate me or have some sort of DNA evidence and can pull a magical logic-avoiding rabbit out of your ass like they do on TV, I suggest you move on."

Clare: "Alright, fair enough. Lately you've been having trouble with a masked man. Most recently helping Freddie Datsun to capture the #1 contendership to your Championship."

DDD: "Let me cut you off right there blondie. I'm going to address Hayes first. Yes, I said Hayes. I know it's you under that mask you son of a bitch. This is the oldest trick in the book, but hey, I wouldn't expect you to come up with anything more clever than that, or, well, clever at all to be honest. Once I unmask you and reveal that it really is you you're going to be arrested again for trespassing and deported back to that floating rat hole that you're so proud of with all the other Japs that aren't even worthy of holding my jockstrap.

As for Freddie Datsun, I've beaten that stupid hillbillies ass before and I'll do it again on November 22nd. What can he possibly do to beat me, the Divine One, anyways? Unleash his brand new trademark move, The Possum Stomp on me? Puh-lease. Another Pay Per View, another easy mark in my wins column. Nobody's gonna be taking the title of World Champion from me in this lifetime."

Clare: "But Datsun does have physical possession of your belt."

DDD: "Thank you Captain Obvious. Would you like a bitch slap to go along with that smart mouth of yours? Datsun stole that belt from me. That's what poor people do, they steal things and Datsun is nothing more than a common thief. Why the hell people would cheer for a criminal is frankly beyond me. I can tell you one thing for sure, and that is that I will get my belt back. Through hell or high water I will get that belt back from that poor excuse for a role model. Like I said, nobody is going to be taking the title of World Champion from me in this lifetime."

(B)

vs.

-Fish & Oak vs. Jacob Jett & Art Reed w/ Seduction-

Let's face it, in reality this was no tag team match, it was a handicap match. Oak is always a non-factor. Fish fought as hard as she could against her two challengers at Pilgrimage, but if this match is any indication we could very well be seeing a new National Champion in a few weeks. Jett and Reed wore Fish down with double teams and constant tags in and out. To Fish's credit though she was able to catch a second wind and fight off Jett & Reed long enough to make a tag to Oak. To her debit though she made a tag to Oak. Oak came in the ring like an acorn on fire, but it didn't last. Jett hit a superkick to Oaks jaw, Reed caught him stumbling back with a Dark Matter then Jett went up top and nailed the Jettison 450. 1.....2.....3.

Winners: Jett & Reed in 11:41

Rating (C)

-The scene is a quiet one, almost too quiet. The hallway is empty except for crickets. And that's the cue for "The Trademark" Thomas Morgan to come running and screaming through the halls. "Don't kill me, don't kill me!" As Morgan passes by the camera, around the hall comes the person that he's 'talking' to, Bulldozer Brandon Smith. Smith has his chain wrapped around his hand and runs after The Trademark. "Get back here ya little F***er! Nobody costs me a match and lives to tell about it!"

(D-)

-The hallways in the other half of the arena seem to be a quieter, or at least less violent place than they usually are. Although the faint screams of "Help Me!" and "I'm not going to hurt you ya little f***er! Much!" can still be heard in the distance. The camera is live at the locker room door of Divinity. The door opens and out comes quite possibly one of the last people anyone would ever expect to come out of that door. In one piece or without a commotion that could be heard a mile away at least.

Jacob Stranton, manager of the Wild Savages, walked out of the door unscathed and with a smile on his face. "Stranton coming out of the Divinity locker room? What could this mean? This is sure to get interesting folks." Duane Fry speculates.

(D)

vs.

-Frankie Perez vs. Trent Shaffer-

And for those who may not remember, these two were the original incarnation of The Dudez!!!1! Tag team, but due to Shaffers non-clickiness the team was scrapped. 10 Months later and Perez is a star in the making and and Shaffer... is.... um, a work in progress? Yea, let's go with that one. Shaffer had a lot to try to prove to Perez, not being a complete failure for one. Shaffer went right after Perez as the bell rang opting not to tie-up and instead just kick the gut of Perez and go to work. Shaffer was more aggressive than we've ever seen him and he took it to Perez. Shaffer nailed the Heart Burn 5 minutes into the match and made the cover. Perez got his shoulder up at 2.

Shaffer didn't waste time and went back to work wearing Perez down even more. Two minutes later and Shaffer went for the Heart Burn again, but this time Perez was able to reverse it into a victory roll. 1.....2.. Shaffer kicks out and tries to go back on the attack. He charges in, but Perez drop toes him, springs off the ropes and comes down on him with a senton. Perez immediately goes for the P-Clutch, but Shaffer escapes to the outside. Shaffer paces around on the outside of the ring and Perez runs over as if the vault over the top. Shaffer ducks and covers, but Perez only jumps over onto the apron. When Shaffer got up he received an Asai moonsault that took both men down.

Perez is up and tosses Shaffer into the ring by the count of 8 and he controls the match from here on out. Perez sets up Shaffer in the corner for the Perfect Parity a few minutes later, but Shaffer fights Perez off and hops to the outside of the ring. Perez follows. As Perez grabs Shaffer he gets a ring bell to the temple and the ref ends this match disqualifying Shaffer.

Winner: Frankie Perez by DQ in 13:56

Rating (B-)

-In a scene long time readers probably thought they would see, Cameron Vessey is wearing stripes behind prison bars. And on a related note I'm sure everyone also saw Vessey dragging Wayne down with him. Vessey plays his harmonica to a song that I'm sure he doesn't even know. If you could hear I'm sure you would understand why I say that. "Where'd you find that thing?" Wayne questions. Vessey merely points at the corner of the jail cell. Waynes head turns to see a skeleton in prison stripes.

Wayne: "Wasn't he wearing a ring when we got here?"

Vessey: "We need something for bail."

Wayne: "Good point. How are you supposed to sell that from here anyways?"

Vessey: "I got ways."

Wayne: "You got nothing."

Vessey: ".... I got nothing."

The sound of a night stick striking the metal bars of the prison cell echoes as Vessey and Wayne look up. "You both get one call scumbags." one of the officers states. "Is the name calling really necessary?" Vessey asks. The officer unlocks the cell and two officers with cuffs and batons go in. "Cuff 'em boys."

Vessey sticks out his hands, the officers strike him in the head. They continue to smash their batons into the back of Vesseys, well, anything. The young Vessey screams out "POLICE BRUTALITY!!! POLICE BRUTALITY!!!"

Wayne just stands in the corner and watches his partner take a beating.

The officers finally slip the cuffs onto a half-conscious Vessey before turning their attention the Wayne. "Hands out boy." Wayne sticks his hands out and Officer #1 hits him with the baton. Wayne doesn't take that too well and punches the officer in the jaw. #2 rushes in and Wayne fights him off, stealing his baton and striking him in the gut with it. He then turns his attention back to #1 and cracks him over the head with it. The Third officer standing outside the cell calls for back-up.

We cut to 20 minutes later and Vessey and Wayne are sitting at a table in a small room handcuffed. Vessey and Wayne are both bloody and bruised, and Wayne is jumping a bit.

Vessey: "We really showed them. Too bad about what happened to you though."

Wayne: "Damn tas-zzzZZsssSzZs- ers. Can't fight like real men."

Vessey: "Where'd you learn those moves anyways? You take karate as a kid?"

Wayne: "No, I just watched way too much Power Rangers-SsZZsszZzSSssZSzsszZ."

Vessey: "Oh. Well that would explain why you were screaming ai ai ai when those twenty cops had their tasers on ya."

As Wayne and Vessey conversate a detective enters the room. "You have one phone call each. Make it count." "Me first!" Vessey claims. Vessey walks over to the phone and dials a number.

"Hello?"

"Uncle Bryan!"

"Who the hell is this?"

"It's your nephew."

"Which one?"

"Cameron."

"Oh. You again. I'm not loaning you money and that's that!"

"But Uncle Bryan, tha-"

*Click*

Wayne: "Now do you see why you shouldn't gamble."

Vessey: "Shut it and make your stinkin' call."

Wayne goes over to the phone now and contemplates to himself for a moment before dialing. "I've gotta be desperate to be doing this." Wayne mutters to himself.

DDD: "What do you want?"

Wayne: "Darryl, nice hearing from you."

DDD: "How the hell did you get this number?"

Wayne: "Look, Darryl, I know we're not exactly on speaking terms-"

DDD: "Which is why you better give me a good reason not to hang up by the count of three, you're wasting my minutes here."

Wayne: "Funny story actually."

DDD: "I would ask what, but then you would tell me and I'd have to pretend to give a rats left testie."

Wayne: "We're in jail. We're being charged with murder."

DDD: "Really?"

Wayne: "Yes."

DDD: "Well have fun. Don't drop the soap bitches." Devine hangs up in Wayne face.

Wayne: "Sonuva..."

Vessey: "Now do you see why you shouldn't suck?" Wayne glares at Vessey. "Yea, I'll just shut up now."

(C-)

-The scene in the cafeteria is a calm one as it seems everyone can get along fine when they're eating. Bruce the Giant is the most notable figure in that room, and not just because he's ginormous or has a gut that spans four states. It's because his first helping spans four plates. Some chinese guy is yelling at him too in some sort of weird alien language that Bruce can't understand before shooing him away with a spatula.

That's not what breaks up the calm in the cafeteria though. That would be Phlex. The Austrian, orange-skinned, body builder with a product to sell barges into the cafe with a look of disgust on his face. Phlex looks around and steals Brendan Idols karaoke machine. When Idol tries to snatch it back Phlex gives him a blunt shot to the head with the mic. Phlex hops up onto a table and starts ranting about all the fat, overweight and outta shape people in the room, and he's not really overexaggerating either.

Phlex: "Would you big fat pigs looks at yourselves? Looking around this room is like a horror show. I could put money down on any single one of you to die of a heart attack within the month and I would make a fortune. You beached whales should be ashamed of yourselves and your bodies, nobody should have that many chins! What you all need is to have some self-control and Phlex-it-All."

Apparently Phlex isn't out to make any friends, which is a good thing since he ain't makin' any. Bruce the Giant is the first one to stand up and make his way towards Phlex. "(Translated) Bruce Smash!"

Phlex: "Great. Have you been taking vocabulary classes from Warlord Agony?"

Agony stands up from his table with a spork in hand and yells out "Agony kill orange man now!"

Phlex: "No need to get all wordy on me caveman. As for the approaching elephant, I'd like to see you actually catch me. If you want me then you'll get me in a wrestling match, not some silly food fight."

BTG: "(T) You're on."

Phlex: "But I have a stipulation for you to make this interesting. When I win you have to go on the patented Phlex-it-All diet."

BTG: "(T) And if I win you buy me and Agony dinner."

Phlex: "That it blubber nuggets?"

BTG: "(T) For a month."

Phlex: "You're still on you disgusting beached whale. You will be one of my greatest success stories."

(B-)

vs. w/

-Maxmillion Prod. vs. Team Seduction w/ Seduction-

Holy Heck Batman! How the hell? This match was great!

The match starts out with the Super Ninja and Velasquez. The bell rings and Otas super flexible ninja feet connect to the side of Velasquez's head. Ota immediately goes for the cover and it looks as though the match is over early, but Zeus says otherwise as he breaks up the pin before the count of two. Ota picks Velasquez up partially into a sitting position and then springs off the ropes nailing him with a basement dropkick. Ota goes for the cover, but Maxmillion is again in to break up the cover. Ota tags Arsenal in. Arsenal picks Velasquez up and belly-belly suplexes him over the top rope. Velasquez makes a pretty loud thud as he hits the floor.

Arsenal picks up Velasquez on the outside and drills him through the Spanish announce table with a Tiger Driver effectively taking Velasquez out. The ref and Maxmillion check on Rudy with Jez asking Zeus if he wants to continue. Zeus does. Maxmillion rolls back into the ring and challenges Arsenal to try that on him. Arsenal doesn't look intimidated by Zeus and instead tags Ota in. Ota charges in and takes Zeus down with a rolling wheel kick then locks in the bow and arrow lock on Maxmillion. Maxmillion refuses to give up and after an excruciating minute on the hold Ota finally lets go. He tags Arsenal into the match.

Arsenal goes right over to Zeus and grabs him for a belly to belly suplex, but Zeus fights him off with knees to the gut. Zeus then goes for a running clothesline, but Arsenal cuts him off with a thrust kick right to the jaw cutting short Zeus' comeback. Arsenal gets down on the mat and rains down on Zeus with mounted elbow smashes into Zeus's head. Arsenal picks Zeus up and goes to work on him delivering his large arsenal of suplexes and holds. Arsenal tags out to Ota who goes to work on Maxmillion with his large array of kicks and strikes. Ota then lays in wait for Maxmillion to get to his feet before Ota goes for the Ninja Strike, but Zeus ducks it and, Street Cutter to Ota.

Arsenal comes in and goes to clothesline Velasquez, but he ducks it. Arsenal right into a knee lift from Maxmillion. Velasquez up to the top and Arsenal is up again. Missile dropkick to the Wrestling Machine and he's down. Maxmillion pounces quickly and locks in the Olympian clutch. Arsenal writhes in pain trying to make it to the ropes. Out of nowhere (the true calling card of a ninja) a Ninja Strike to Maxmillion breaks the Clutch up. Velasquez goes right after Ota and the two brawl in the middle of the ring. Seduction grabs onto Velasquez's leg and holds him as Arsenal gets to his feet and takes him over the top. Arsenal turns his attention to Maxmillion and delivers an Ammo Dump to him in the middle of the ring. 1......2.......3.

Winners: Team Seduction in 13:31

Rating (B+)

-And we catch back up with Tom & Jerry. I mean, The Dozer & The Trademark. Dozer chases Morgan all the way out to the parking garage and looks to finally have him cornered. Dozer wraps his chain tight around his fist and with a sadistic smile on his face gets ready to put The Trademark out of his misery. And here's the part where that doesn't happen. A steel pipe cracks Dozer in the back and brings him to his knees. On the other end of that pipe is Brett Hall. Hall picks Dozer up and slams him onto one of the equipment boxes with his Butterfly Powerbomb. And from a few crates up Brendan Idol readies himself and then makes the leap. He comes down on Dozer with a huge splash taking The Dozer out of commission.

Morgan: "The Trademark could have taken care of The Bulldozer on his own. The Trademark required none of your assistance." Hall just turns his head towards him. "The Trademark is not complaining though."

(E+)

-Sam Strong walks down the hall on his way to his office for the first time tonight helping his daughter along as he goes. Sam finally gets to the door and opens it to find a real surprise waiting for him. Both Strongs are flabbergasted by what they see. Sam Strongs office has been torn to shreds. Ever hear the term 'It looks like a hurricane blew through here'? Well that wouldn't do justice to this carnage. The bookcases were torn from the walls with pages from the books being torn out and littered across the room. The desk was overturned and smashed in half. The guest chair was hanging out of a broken window while Sams chair, well, half of it is stuck in the ceiling and the other lodged in the wall.

Sam: "The hell happened here brother?!"

Muffin: "It's completely destroyed."

DDD: "Wow, you must have one big ass rat problem around here Sam." Devine walks up behind the Strongs and looks into the room.

Sam: "The only rat I smell around here is you brother. You did this didn't you?"

DDD: "Yes, just like I attacked poor, little, defenseless Muffin over there Thursday. Of course I didn't do it numb nuts. You think I can actually lodge half a chair in the ceiling?"

Strong: "I know you're behind this brother. Who'd you send to do this to my office? Was it Brandon? Is Jillefski behind this?"

DDD: "Slow down there Captain Illogical. If I was even capable of doing this don't you think I would own up to it? And if Jillefski did it don't you think he would be bragging about it?"

Sam: "Then who was it brother?"

DDD: "Like I said last week, I don't know, but that had to be one sadistic motherf*cker." The other half of the chair falls from the ceiling crashing down onto the remains of the desk. "Powerful too."

Strong looks as though he's ready to pick up his fight from aHiA and deck Devine when Jillefski runs up to the champ. Jillefski: "Darryl, we gotta talk."

DDD: "Bout what? What's wrong?"

Jillefski: "Remember how I said that I could..."

DDD: "Handle it, yeah."

Jillefski: "Well I..."

DDD: "Couldn't?"

Jillefski: "And now..."

DDD: "It's out?"

Jillefski: "And about."

DDD: "You think..."

Jillefski: "No, he can't."

DDD: "How about..."

Jillefski: "Not a chance."

DDD: "So basically...."

Jillefski: "Yea."

Devine turns his attention back to Sam "Good luck with that rat problem brother. It's a big one. And out of control." Devine and Jillefski walk off with a purpose and check over their shoulders every chance they get.

This time was a bit different for the American Everyman and the Samoan Savage. As soon as the bell rang both men just went straight after each other, neither planning on making this into a technical encounter, or very pretty either. Datsun held his own brawling with the near unstoppable savage, but it wasn't enough as Umaga caught him with a headbutt and we all know about Samoans and headbutts. From there Datsun was on the receiving end of a savage beatdown from Umaga. Datsun tried hard to fight back, but the #1 contender couldn't catch his second wind and get the momentum on his side. Umaga rammed Datsun into the corner of the ring and went for the Savage Charge. He hits it and Datsun topples out of the corner. Umaga makes the cover,

Rating (B-)

-Datsun pulls out the win over the Samoan Savage. He grabs the USPW Championship belt from the time keeper and celebrates. The celebration doesn't last long as I'm sure everyone expected. As Datsun stood on the middle ropes in the corner Devine slid into the ring with a steel chair and nailed Datsun in the back with it. Datsun fell down to the mat and Devine literally pried his championship belt out of Datsuns fingers. Devine grabbed his belt and took a good long stare at it before turning around to leave.

Too bad The Masked Man stood in Devines way. Devine prepared to nail the MM in between the eyes with the title belt, but he didn't have to. The 300+ lb. Samoan Savage was back to his feet and pissed off. He clubbed the MM in the back of the neck and went to work stomping away at him with his bare foot. Devine cautiously approached Umaga, keeping one eye on him at all times, and joined in stomping away at the Masked Man. Devine picked the MM up and tossed him into the corner of the ring. Stranton gave Umaga the signal to go for the Savage Charge.

Umaga charged into the corner ready to break the MM in half, but it looks to be his cranium that got split in half. Datsun took the chair that was used on him earlier and smashes it right into Umagas head mid-charge. Devine looks furious, but that quickly changes as Datsun turns his attention to Devine. Devine grabs his belt and rolls out of the ring as quick as he can. We fade to black as Datsun challenges Devine to get back into the ring and settle this like a man.
