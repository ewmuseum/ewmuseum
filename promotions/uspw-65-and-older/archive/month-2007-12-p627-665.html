Main Show

-The Show starts off with the normal explosion of pyro and announcers hyping the show you're already watching to get you to stick around when we cut backstage for breaking news. In through the arena doors walks the legendary Sam Strong. The announcers speculate what he could be doing in the arena tonight, but arrive at a sound conclusion, if security didn't stop him it has to do with Jillefski.

(B+)

-The Hotshots are out in the hall warming up for their upcoming tag team match against Joss Thompson's new team, Pure Evil, Archnemesis and Evil Machine.

Vessey: "And how do they understand him? I mean all I hear is beep beep beep, popcorn's ready."

Wayne: "Maybe it's evil speak. Do lawyers have their own language to communicate in?"

Vessey: "Yeah, it's called legal mumbo jumbo and you can never trust it."

Wayne: "Are you still hot about that? It was 2 years ago."

Vessey: "Those sons of bitches made me pay my taxes. I will never forgive them for it. I won that money illegally for a reason."

Wayne: "After putting my car up as a bet."

Vessey: "I won, didn't I?"

Wayne: "After 23 straight losses."

Vessey: "It's the 1 in 24 that really matters anyways."

Wayne: "Yea, and if you ever steal my registration like that again I'll kill you."

Vessey: "Yeah, join the club. You, the psycho and the White Tiger."

"Don't forget the lawyer."

Vessey: "...Ah sh*t, we're getting jumped aren't we?"

Joss: "Very perceptive of you."

And with that Pure Evil jump the Hotshots with chairs and lay them out, only 5 minutes out from their upcoming match against each other.

(D)

-And having spoke of Jillefski earlier we now follow him live as he walks the halls of the arena sipping from his coffee and seeming joyful, which may or may not have something to do with Sam Strong being here tonight. Jillefski struts down the hall like he owns the place, then again he does and he has Big Cat Brandon at his side. Jillefskis walk gets interrupted by the one and thank god only Peter Valentine, who is dressed like.... I can't believe I'm typing this... Cupid. Complete with bow and arrow. And diaper. Jillefski stares at him in disbelief and even Brandon, whose sole two emotions are angry and pissed off, doesn't believe what he's looking at. Valentine interrupts Jillefski to stick up for his 'son' Casey.

Peter: "Mr. Jillefski, with all due respect what you did to my son was wrong and I will not let it go unpunished."

Valentine then holds his bow and arrow up to Jillefski slung back. Jillefski attempts his hardest not to burst out laughing at the idle threat. Did I mention the bow is plastic and spray painted pink? Or that the arrow is Nerf? Yea... Jillefski: "And what are you going to do about it Peter?"

Peter: "I'll shoot you. I don't want to, but I will."

Jillefski: "Peter, may I ask you something?"

Peter: "Sure, anything you want boss."

Jillefski: "Why in the blue hell are you dressed like that?"

Peter: "To commemorate my great ancestor and namesake, St. Valentine."

Jillefski: "Peter, allow me to say this once and only once. It's the middle of f*cking December!"

Peter: "No matter! I am here to avenge my son."

Jillefski: "*sigh* Brandon, take care of him. And be careful not to drop him on his head, it seems to have taken enough trauma."

Brandon walks up to Valentine who points his bow at him now and frantically switches targets between him and Jillefski until Brandon is face to face with him. Valentine shoots the Nerf arrow at Brandon, but of course Brandon being the warrior he is just shrugs off the brutal attack by Valentine. But seriously now, Brandon then grabs the bow and snaps it in two and tosses it aside and then pretty much does the same thing to Valentine. Once Valentine is beaten to a bloody pulp and near unconscious Jillefski and Brandon continue on as though none of that ever happened.

(C)

vs.

-The Hotshots vs. Pure Evil-

The Hotshots were still feeling the effects of the cheap shots before the match, but it wore off eventually and The Hotshots once again proved that maybe they really do deserve those belts. Vessey finished off Archnemesis with a VesseyPlex II and picked up the win for his team.

Winners: The Hotshots in 9:58

Jillefski says back. Through the door comes Sam Strong. "Please have a seat Sam." Jillefski says to Sam with a faux smile on his face and faked sincerity. Strong takes a seat.

Strong: "Enjoying my office Danny?"

Jillefski: "*Ahem* My office now."

Strong: "Not for long brother."

Jillefski: "Always with the jokes Sam."

Strong: "That was no joke brother."

Jillefski: "Let's just get down to business, shall we?"

Strong: "I thought you'd never ask brother."

Jillefski: "Yes, that's in there too."

(C+)

-Human Arsenal is sitting in the Divinity locker room by himself mumbling to himself. His arm seems to be doing a little better as the sling is no longer there, only a light cast. Arsenal speaks a little louder until we can hear what he's saying, something about getting his revenge on Devine, about making that S.O.B. pay, about putting him in his place, six feet under. Arsenal then contemplates not going on a vigilante revenge and listening to Seduction. The lights in the room go out and once they're back on The Masked Man is there. He leans against the wall seeming confident no harm will befall him, Arsenal on the other hand is ready to attack.

MM: "Hello John. How are you?"

Arsenal: "What do you want?"

MM: "I just want to talk. What's the matter, don't trust me?"

Arsenal: "And why would I trust you?"

MM: "Don't think of me as Devines enemy, or your enemy either, look at me as your subconscious pointing you in the direction you want to go in anyways. Why let anyone boss you around John? You're a better wrestler than 99% of the locker room. We both know it's the truth. Why play for someone else's team when you can lead others to success? Or yourself. Why be Devines lapdog? Why be Seductions servant? Why be pushed aside for The Hotshots? Why haven't you gotten any real opportunities around here? Ask yourself that question for a moment. Now realize that you're being kept. You know the saying 'keep your friends close and your enemies closer'? That is this."

Arsenal: "I'm with Divinity because I'm with the winning team."

MM: "But ask yourself why Divinity is the winning team."

Arsenal: "Because I'm on it."

MM: "Precisely."

Arsenal: "What do you mean?"

MM: "The tides will turn and the tides will rise, but only when the moon is on their side."

With that the lights go out and the Masked Man is gone once more. Arsenal looks as though he's about to arrive at his own epiphany now.

(B-)

-In the interview area we have Jennifer Clare ready to speak with Muffin, Alicia Strong.

Clare: "So how are you doing Alicia?"

Muffin: "Aside from knowing my fathers company was stolen out from under him and a group of people who would feel no remorse for ending my career are after me, I'm doing alright."

Clare: "Okay, that was probably a stupid thing to ask after all. How are you holding up under all this pressure?"

Muffin: "I'm managing, but I won't have to for long. I'm going to take the belt from Devine and get control of this company back to my family and then this nightmare will be over."

Clare: "And what about the Masked Man?"

Muffin: "What about him?"

Clare: "He definitely seems to be doing his fair share of work to accomplish those very things. Plus, and no offense intended, he's pulled you out of the fire more than a few times before."

Muffin: "Whoever it is, I have no idea who it is or what they're really after. So excuse me if I don't jump for joy at the sight or mention of him."

Clare: "Alright. And how's your father doing in all of this? I mean he's got probably the biggest match of his career stipulation wise coming up at Winter Epiphany."

Muffin: "My father's going to do what he always does, he's going to train harder than he's ever trained before and he's going beat Brandon."

"And what about your mother?"

Chance Fortune walks into the interview area and seems to start harassing Muffin.

Muffin: "What?"

Fortune: "Your mother."

Muffin: "My mother's dead."

Fortune: "Well that didn't stop me."

Muffin: "Stop you from what? What the hell are you going on about?"

Clare: "You don't want an answer to that, trust me."

Fortune: "It didn't stop me from doing her last night. Ohhh burn."

Clare: "Told ya."

Muffin: "Is there a reason you're here?"

Fortune: "No, not really."

Muffin: "Tell ya what, you just got a reward."

Fortune: "Hold on, let me go grab my condom and then I'll cash it in."

Muffin: "No you disgusting little pervert, you have a match. Against me. Next."

Fortune: "Alright, but if I win you have to go out with me bitch."

Muffin: "Like that would ever happen. And when I win I never want to see you again."

Fortune: "You'll be seeing plenty of me now babe."

Muffin: "Call me that again and I'll knock you out."

(B-)

-Jim Force and Robert Oxford are backstage doing whatever it is old psychos do. Oxford is having a conversation with a soda machine reminiscing about old times and how things were back in the day. Force is turning Mr. Snuggleforce, his pet White Tiger, into a killing machine, as though he wasn't already. Force has a teddy bear with a picture of The Hotshots on it and is teaching Mr. Snuggleforce to kill it, tear it to shreds.

Force: "THAT IS IT MR. SNUGGLEFOOOOOOOOOOORCE!!!!!!!!!! TEAR THEIR HEADS OFF AND SPILL THEIR BLOOD AS THEY SPILLED THE BLOOD OF MY POOR, INNOCENT PIGEON MR SQUIGGLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLES!!!!!!!! BITE HARDER MR SNUGGLEFORCE, THEY MUST PAY WITH THEIR LIVES, NOT WITH THEIR INTESTIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIINES!!!!!!!"

And now what seems like a bit of a reluctant Seduction walks into frame and approaches Force.

Force: "YOU WHO ASSOCIATES WITH MURDEREEEEEEEEEEEEEEERS, WHAT DO YOU DO HEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEERE????!?!?!?!??!?!?"

Seduction: "*gulp* I have... I have information I think you'd be interested in knowing."

Force: "DO YOU KNOW WHAT BLOODTYPE THOSE MOROSE MURDEREROUS MURDERERS AAAAAAAAAAARE!?!?!?!? MR SNUGGLEFORCE DOES NOT LIKE AB, IT GIVES HIM BAD GAAAAAAAAAAAAAS!!!!!!!"

vs.

-Muffin vs. Chance Fortune-

And a very good match from these two. I have to give Chance credit, he can hang with the big boys when put in there against them. And girls. As you would imagine Muffin dominated the match for the most part with Chance getting a few shots to show what he had. In the end it wasn't enough and Muffin put him away with an Angel Driver.

Winner: Muffin in 14:53

Rating (B)

-USPW World Champion Triple D walks out of his locker room in his usual angry mood. As he shuts the door behind him he's stopped in his path by Human Arsenal.

DDD: "What? Come back to let me finish the job?"

Arsenal: "No."

DDD: "Then why are you here blocking my way?"

Arsenal: "To remind you that you don't control anyone."

Ota bursts out of the vents above and drops in on Devine, but it's actually the Hotshots that make the first strike. The four men take out their aggressions on the champ leaving him a bloody mess in the hall.

Vessey: "Clean up. Aisle 4. One Divine jackass."

(B-)

-And the red carpet is out in the ring awaiting the two men about to step in it. The desk and chairs are there too along with a few lawyers. The first to proceed to the ring is Danny Jillefski accompanied by Akima and as always Big Cat Brandon. Jillefski gets in the ring, has a seat and awaits the arrival of Sam Strong. He doesn't have to wait long either. Strong makes his way down the ramp and into the ring accompanied by his daughter, Alicia. Strong takes a seat as Jillefski gets the contract from his lawyer, puts his John Hanc0ck on it, and slides it over to Sam telling him to sign it. Sam takes it, looks it over a bit and then hands it to his lawyer. He then stands up and heads out of the ring. Jillefski looks confused as to what Sam's trying to do and grabs a mic.

Jillefski: "And just where do you think you're going? You have a contract to sign, now sign it."

Strong: "Well brother, my lawyer has advised me to let him have a look at it before I sign it. I'm sure you understand why."

Jillefski: "You sit down right now and sign that damn contract!"

Strong: "And what if I don't feel like it brother?"

Jillefski: "Why must you always insist on doing things the hard way Sam? Brandon, you know what to do."

Brandon and Akima attack Strong and Muffin and we have a preview of what's going to happen at Winter Epiphany with Brandon attacking Sam and Akima going after Muffin. The four brawl fairly evenly until Jillefski involves himself. He pulls a pair of brass knuckles out of his coat pocket and puts them on, he lines up and nails Sam between the eyes, taking him down. That allows BCB and Akima to double team Muffin and takes her out too. Jillefski, Brandon and Akima stand in the ring over their fallen foes and look as though they're ready to finish this for good when someone slides into the ring after jumping out of the crowd. The man is dressed in an officer's outfit and is brandishing a night stick. He takes it to the gut of Akima slouching him over then drives it into the back taking him to the mat. Brandon charges in at him looking for a Big Cat Pounce, but the officer get his foot in the way of that Big Booting him down to the mat.

"The officer looks familiar." Sneer comments, Fry responding "I believe that, yes it is, Rick Law has made his return to USPW!" Law then turns to Jillefski who scatters outside of the ring and grabs a mic.

Jillefski: "You ever want to crash my party again Rick, you think real hard about it because I am neither a nice man or a forgiving man when you get on my bad side. I will make your life living hell now and it only begins tonight. Because right now you have a match... against Akima. Ring the bell."

(C+, C+)

vs.

-Rick Law vs. Akima-

Akima was feeling the gut shot a bit, but all that did was make him angrier and more aggressive. Akima took Law down from behind as the bell rang taking advantage of Law turning his back. Akima dominated the opening minutes savagely attacking Law and not letting up one bit. The big man wouldn't go quietly though and started to fight back leaving Akima reeling after a series of jabs. Law then sprung off the ropes and took the Samoan down with a Big Boot. Law started firing on all cylinders now and made his comeback. Akima would fight back, but it wouldn't be enough. Law hit Akima with the Long Arm of the Law and then Law got hit with a Big Cat Pounce. Brandon continued to attack Law, but Sam and Alicia came to his rescue driving Brandon off. The ref rand the bell and called for the DQ.

Winner: Rick Law by DQ in 10:33

Rating (C+)

-Maxmillion Productions are in their locker room going over some plans or scripts, I'm not sure which. But out of nowhere the door is kicked in by a very pissed World Champ. Devine storms in and pulls Maxmillion off his couch by the collar and slamming him against the wall. Devine is bruised up and still bleeding out of a few abrasions in his forehead.

DDD: "Now you had better listen to me and listen well because none of this is up for discussion. You have the identity of the Masked Man figured out for me by a week from tonight because a week from tonight I am ending somebodies career and quite frankly I don't care whose it is at this point. Now if you don't want it to be yours you three will figure out this mystery and give me the solution to it or so help you god..."

Devine leaves it at that and storms out of the room just as fiercely as he stormed into it.

(B-)

-We are shown Wanda Fish and Phlex in Fish's locker room. They look as though they're preparing to go somewhere. Phlex opens the door for Fish and allows her to go first, but she stops in the doorway as does Phlex. Come screaming down the halls is Jim Force and Mr. Snuggleforce.

Force: "HUNT THEM DOOOOOOOOOOWN!!!!!!!!!! THESE DUDES SHALL NOT GET AWAY WITH NOT ONLY THE MOROSE MURDER OF MY MOST CHERISHED AVIATED AMIGO AND MASCOT OF THE FOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOORCE, BUT FRAMING OTHEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEERS!!!!!!!"

"Should we just stay in again?" Phlex asks Fish who looks a bit distracted. "Phlexie?" She asks, "Yes?" he responds. Fish looks and points up as does Phlex now. The two look each other in the eyes as the camera pans out to see what they saw. Hanging above them in the doorway is a mistletoe. Without much hesitation the two kiss, nothing more than a peck on the lips, but after pulling away for a second the two embrace again in a passionate kiss. The camera stays on them as the two make out, but can't help but catch in the background Giant Oak, who looks like his hearts just been shattered into a thousand pieces.

(B-)

vs.

-Freddie Datsun vs. Big Cat Brandon w/Jillefski-

And what a way to end a show. A fantastic match between two of USPWs top stars and on free TV nonetheless. The match was a slugfest point blank, but an incredible slugfest at that. It was brutal and you could almost feel the pain just by looking at either competitor. The ref was a bit lenient with the rules here as the two brawlers spent a good deal of time on the outside of the ring using whatever they could as a weapon. Datsun really rung Brandons bell with a few shots. No, really, he hit him with the bell. Brandon had set Datsun up against the corner post for a Big Cat Pounce, but Datsun moved out of the way letting Brandon run shoulder first into it.

Datsun saw the opening and took advantage of it working over the shoulder for the remainder of the match. Brandon fought back though, and relentlessly to boot. It didn't seem to be enough as Datsun set him up for the Patriot Press. He had him up on his shoulders when Jillefski pulled at his leg. Brandon got free and sprang off the ropes rebounding with a Big Cat Pounce that sent Datsun flying half way across the ring. Brandon made the pin, 1.....2.....3.

Winner: Big Cat Brandon in 15:56
