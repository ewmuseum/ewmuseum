USPW Red, White, and Blue!

Wednesday, Week 1, 2007

Held at Pennsylvania Park

Att. 2,000

-The Towers Of Power defeated The Hillbillys in 4:46 when Mick Muscles defeated Al The Hillbilly by submission. (D-)

-Jacob Stranton had an interview hyping the upcoming singles match between his client Umaga and Demon Anger.

(D)

-Umaga defeated Demon Anger in 10:21 by pinfall with a Savage Charge. (D-) (Didn't click. Umaga destroyed him anyways.)

-Trent Shaffer defeated Mainstream Hernandez and Frankie Perez in 14:45 when Trent Shaffer defeated Mainstream Hernandez by pinfall with a Heart Burn. (D) (Why? Why not?)

-Citizen X cuts a promo hyping up his upcoming match with Captain USA. (E+)

-Citizen X defeated Captain USA in 9:50 by pinfall with a Flaming Anarchy. Citizen X makes defense number 4 of his USPW National title. (E) (Captain USA was exhausted by the end. Citizen X and Captain USA don't seem to click. Citizen X and Captain USA put on a crappy match. Crowd sections A-F were ready to riot.)

-The Lords Of War defeated Savage Fury in 10:02 when Warlord Agony defeated Java by pinfall with a War Machine. (D)

-Jim Force had an interview hyping his upcoming singles match with Rudy Velasquez. (D) (Force babbles on about gang warfare, the youth of America, social economics and the need to get his ride tricked out. I don't understand either. Oh, and he wants you to buy his merchandise.)

-Rudy Velasquez defeated Jim Force in 9:33 by pinfall with a Street Cutter. (D-)

-Zeus Maxmillion, T-Rex and Rudy Velasquez concoct a plan to do something to someone. It's going to be devious and well planned. Sure it is. (D)

-Darryl Devine had an interview hyping his upcoming singles match with Giant Redwood. (D+) (A few people actually cared. Shocking.)

-Darryl Devine defeated Giant Redwood in 7:41 by pinfall with a Devine Dream Drop. (D-) (Giant Redwood was exhausted by the end. Shocking.)

-Bruce The Giant had an interview hyping his upcoming singles match with Liberty. (B+) (Oh is it nice to see green.) -Liberty had an interview hyping his upcoming singles match with Bruce The Giant. (B+) (Green again, too bad the match will suck.)

-Bruce The Giant defeated Liberty in 14:47 by pinfall with a Giant Choke Slam. (C) (Bruce The Giant was visibly tiring toward the end. The match suffered because there was not enough selling shown. The match also suffered because of who was wrestling.)

Final (C-)

Better than I expected. Maybe I should write more of my shows in less than 5 minutes on toilet paper.
