Seduction: "2 nights ago Ota destroyed you Darryl. He took you out and he took Muffin out. Ota has a newfound killer instinct that I plan to use to end your career Darryl. vs.

-Frankie Perez vs. Trent Shaffer-

And finally, a match that didn't completely blow. I was actually pretty surprised by how good the match was, I was only expecting a D+ at best.

The match itself was a fairly even back and forth exchange of moves and momentum. Perez pulled ahead and took control of the match. Shaffer didn't like that, but he couldn't get on the offensive. So he decides to roll out of the ring, grab a steel chair and nail Perez in plain sight of the ref. The ref calls for the bell and it's over.

w/ vs. w/

-Fumihiro Ota w/ Seduction vs. Triple D w/ Muffin-

A good match tonight. Finally.

Devine was still feeling the effects on that kick to the back of the neck from 2 nights ago. Ota could tell. The match started off with a simple exchange of holds and counter-holds, but a quick kick to the face ended that. The match degenerated into an all-out fight and Ota, the master ninja that he is, was able to take control of the match. Ota went for blood attacking Devines already weakened neck and head area. Ota delivered some stiff kicks to Devines head and then went to finish him off with a Ninja Strike. Devine rushed Ota and took him down with a dropkick. Both men went down and the ref began the mandatory ten count.

Ota was up by 6 while Devine was still struggling to his feet. Ota pulled him up, but Devine fought back. He jabbed him in the gut as many times as he could while getting to his feet and then nailed Ota with some shots to the head. Devine takes Ota down with a clothesline. And then another, and one more for good measure. Devine sets him up for the Devine Dream Drop, but Seduction grabs his leg. Ota attacks Devine from behind and hits him with a back suplex. Ota goes up top. He goes for a 450, but Devine rolls out of the way. Ota was able to roll through though. He charges Devine, but Devine catches him with a 3D. Devine makes the cover, but Seduction hops onto the apron and distracts the ref. Muffin sees enough and pulls her down. The ref now sees the pin. 1....2....3.

Winner: Triple D in 14:43
