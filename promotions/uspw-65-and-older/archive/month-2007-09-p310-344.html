These two had no chemistry together. And I mean absolutely no chemistry together. Normally Flash would be able to drag this guy to at least a D+. Oh well. A pretty easy win here for Flash as he hit a Flash Bang about 5 minutes in and got the pinfall on Archnemesis.

vs.

-Robert Oxford vs. Davey Wayne-

Finally, a good match tonight. About time too.

The ref immediately threw both mens partners out, making them wait in the back. Force seemed especially angry about that. Either way the match was on after that. Oxford and Wayne were very evenly matched up at the start. Wayne was always two steps ahead of Oxford, but Oxford was always 3 steps to the right, 5 steps back, 10 forward and 3 to the other left of Wayne. Oxford gained control of the match and was able to maintain it for a while. Wayne thought fast and raked the eyes of Oxford.

Wayne took control and began to work Oxford over, wearing the old timer down for the STF. Wayne locked it in on Oxford. Oxford struggles to make it to the ropes, but he gets there. Wayne stays on until the ref makes it to 4. Wayne picks Oxford up, but Oxford fights back with shots to the gut. Oxford takes control of the match back and wears Wayne down now. Oxford goes for the Mental Breakdown DDT, but Wayne shoves him off and clotheslines Oxford down. This is where Jim Force comes running down to the ring like a lunatic. Force slides into the ring and goes after Wayne. The ref calls for the bell and awards the match to Wayne.

Winner: Davey Wayne by DQ in 15:19

Rating (C-)

Zeus: "Rex, have you seen Hayes anywhere?"

Jillefski: "It's about time, isn't it Sam?"

Strong: "What are you talking about brother?"

Before Sam can say anything in his defense Big Cat Brandon charges in through the door and blindsides Strong. Brandon viciously pounds on the skull of Strong leaving his forehead cut wide open. (C+)

vs.

-USPW National Championship Match- Rudy Velasquez vs. William Hayes-

Velasquez has really been improving lately, but it was the good chemistry between these two that carried them to a B-.

Hayes took control of the match early on and kept control. Velasquez tried to fight back, but Hayes reversed his attempts easily. Velasquez had to resort to cheap tactics now. Velasquez tried to thumb Hayes in the eye, but Hayes caught the arm and wrung it then moved into a hammerlock. Velasquez snuck in a low blow and then planted Hayes with a DDT. Velasquez made a cover, but barely got 2.

Velasquez kept Hayes down with every dirty trick in the book, but Hayes kept fighting back. He was able to make a comeback and take control of the match again. Hayes nailed Velasquez with a Slick Trick and made the cover, 1......2........3.

Winner: William Hayes in 19:31

Rating (B-)

-Hayes celebration is short-lived as the second the ref raises his hand the music of USPW World Champion Triple D starts to play. Devine comes to the ring with a mic in his hand looking like he has something urgent to say. Devine climbs into the ring and glances over at Hayes with that same look of murder in his eyes as before. Devine begins to speak now.

DDD: "I've got something very important to say right now so, Muffin, if you could come down to the ring. This does involve you."

Muffin makes her way out to the ring a bit reluctantly. She cautiously gets into the ring and stands by Hayes' side.

DDD: "The very man you're standing next to is a fraud. He has set me up since day one to make my life a living hell. This is the man that had me taped and caused what Seduction and her goons have done to me, to you. He's the one who set me up a month ago. He's the one who framed me.

Don't you see what he's doing? He's trying to drive us apart so that he can have you under false pretenses. The man you're standing next to isn't about pride and he isn't honorable. He's been getting Zeus and his cronies to tape me and ruin my life, to ruin your life. To ruin our life. Together."

Muffin looks concerned, but stands next to Hayes regardless.

Hayes: "I may have had them tape you, but I never made you cheat on Seduction or on Muffin. You did that yourself."

Hayes and Muffin turn to leave. Devine looks like steam is about to shoot out of his ears. He drops is title belt from his shoulder and smashes it into the back of Hayes' head. Hayes goes down and Muffin checks on him begging Devine off. Devine backs off and Muffin goes back to checking on Hayes.

And then he nails Muffin with the belt to the back of the head. Devine looks over her with no remorse whatsoever. (B+, B-)
