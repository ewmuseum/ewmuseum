USPW All American Wrestling

Saturday, Week 1, May 2007 (Airs Wednesday)

Held at The Arkansas Coliseum

Att.2,000 (Sold Out)

Dark Matches

-Steve Flash defeated Giant Oak and Bulldozer Brandon Smith in 7:57 when Steve Flash defeated Giant Oak by pinfall with a Flash Bang. (D+)

-William Hayes defeated Wanda Fish in 8:12 by pinfall with a Slick Trick. (C-)

Main Show

-The show starts off backstage in Danny Jillefskis office when Triple D walks in.

DDD: "I want my match with Umaga tonight."

Jillefski: "No."

DDD: "What do you mean no? You said that once I'm medically cleared you'd give it to me."

Jillefski: "I lie alot Mr. Devine. I will give you your title shot in the future, but when I say so."

DDD: "So what did you have in mind then?"

Jillefski: "I'm sure you've heard about USPW Family Night debuting in a little under a month."

DDD: "Yea, I heard about that."

Jillefski: "That's when you get your title shot."

DDD: "I'm not waiting a month to get my hands around Umagas neck."

Jillefski: "Wait a month or be fired. It's as simple as that."

DDD: "Fine, but I will make Umaga pay before then."

DDD begins to walk out of the office when Jillefski calls out to him. "Wait. Don't you want to know who your opponent is tonight?"

DDD: "Who?"

Jillefski: "Acid. And if you don't win I may have to reconsider your title shot."

Devine walks out of the office and makes sure to slam the door on his way out.

(C)

-In another backstage area we see Bruce the Giant, Peter Valentine and Jacob Stranton talking.

Stranton: "Read my lips. You're. Not. Pregnant."

Valentine: "But my doctor told me I am."

Stranton: "You're doctors a friggin' quack."

BTG: "rhyu, rhwe ius."

Valentine: "But he has a medical license."

Stranton: "Did you look on the back of it? I'm sure you would find the kids menu there."

Valentine: "I like the kids menu."

Stranton: "That's not my point."

BTG: "Rhye, rwats noiut is pint."

Stranton: "Stay out of this big guy, you're useless when it comes to speech."

Valentine: "Do you think I can get a happy meal?"

Stranton: "I think you could get some brain surgery, but I'm not sure there is a cure for stupid."

Valentine (now starting to tear up): "I'm not stupid."

Stranton: "Yes you are. You're a damn moron."

Valentine: "No I'm not!"

Valentine, now crying runs into the bathroom and locks himself inside. Stranton goes up to it and bangs on the door. "Get out of there Peter!"

Valentine: "No. I'm not going to come out until you apologize for calling me stupid."

Stranton: (To Bruce) "This coming from a man who thinks he's pregnant?"

BTG: "Ryea. I rohw."

Valentine: "I heard that!"

Stranton: "Well, I'm not apologizing, so what do we do now?"

BTG: "Rhyea I nohw, buiht I hawve to drohp a log."

(C-)

Veteran Mentality are interviewed about Oxfords match with Hernandez next.

Oxford: "Back in my day guys with blonde hair were known as queers. I didn't touch queers, but the city got full of them all coming over on ships and stuff, and cuban rafts. I joined the army to get away from them. I shot me plenty of queers that year. Then one got me. Pistol whipped me right in the back of the head. I lost my short term memory and can't remember a damn thing. Here I stand before you a man on a mission. I will find and kill John G. for killing my wife and taking my memory away."

(D) (Let's see how many people get that reference.)

vs.

-Mainstream Hernandez vs. Robert Oxford-

Old vs. Young. Talented vs. talented, yea, in USPW. Tag team master vs..... tag team dude? Anyways, onto the match. It was a pretty even contest to be honest. Oxford worked over the leg of Hernandez to slow him down while Hernandez prepares to make the big babyface comeback. He does, and goes up top for the Apparition #14, but can't balance with his bad leg. Oxford takes advantage and hits Hernandez with a belly-to-belly off the top rope. He covers and gets the 3.

Winner: Robert Oxford in 9:44

Rating (D)

-We see DDD talking to Muffin backstage. They look to be having a fun time talking about something or other. The camera pans back to reveal Seduction, DDD's girlfriend walking down the hall around the corner. She begins listening and gets upset. She stomps around the corner and goes up to Devine.

Seduction: "You've got a match to get ready for. What the hell are you doing?"

DDD: "Oh, hey Seduction. We were just talking."

Seduction: "Yea, I'm sure. Now get your ass back to the locker room and get ready."

She drags Devine by the ear and he walks off to his locker room. Seduction isn't done as she has something to say to Muffin. "Stay away from him. If I see you near him again, there will be hell to pay. Tramp."

(C+)

-Steve Flash is in his locker room reading some comics, no that isn't a typo, when the lights go out. In the background you can hear the door opening and somebody climbing into the ventilation duct. The lights go back on and a shuriken rains down from the vent. Out pops Fumihiro Ota. Before anything can happen Flash asks "Aren't ninjas supposed to be stealthy? I mean, I could hear you coming from a mile away."

A brawl erupts between the champion and a challenger. They beat the crap out of each other before a security officer steps between the 2. He then cracks Ota in the head with a night stick knocking him out in the process. He takes off his hat to reveal William Hayes. Hayes jumps Flash, taking him down and raining in rights to the head.

(D)

vs. w/

-USPW World Championship Match- Freddie Datsun vs. Umaga (c) w/Stranton-

Another dominant performance from Umaga and another valiant effort from Datsun. I can always rely on Datsun to get a good match out of anyone.

During the match Umaga completely destroyed Datsun before hitting him with a Savage Charge. Umaga made the cover. But he only got 2. Umaga tried the cover again, but Datsun got a shoulder up at 2 again. Datsun came back to life after that and rocked Umaga with right after right and then some lefts for good measure. He takes Umaga off his feet with a 3-point stance. Umaga gets up, but is slammed down. Umaga is up once more. Datsun with a kick to the gut. He gets Umaga up in a Patriot Press, he hits it. 1..2, Umaga kicks out. Datsun gets up and nails Umaga with a right, but Umaga answers with one of his own taking Datsun down. Umaga dominates from here on out. He hits a Savage Charge. Pinfall. 1....2....3.

Winner and still Champion: Umaga in 9:44

Rating (C+)

-Umaga celebrates, Datsun is hurt and Maxmillion Productions make their way out to the ring. Rex and Velasquez beat up the already beaten Datsun. Velasquez puts the exclamation point on this beatdown as he hits Datsun with Street Cutter.

(D+)

-DDD hypes up his match with Acid.

DDD: "Tonight I'll go in there against a mysterious and very dangerous man. Acid is one of DAVEs top stars for a reason, but I'm top of the heap here in USPW because I'm the best around. I'll prove that tonight against Acid and I'll prove that on April 5th when I go 1-on-1 against Umaga for the first time. When I walk out of there with the World Championship around my waist that's exactly what it'll signify, that I'm the best around. I'm not afraid of you Umaga, you should be afraid of me."

(B-)

vs.

-Interpromotional Match- Acid vs. Triple D-

Acid and Devine turned out to be pretty evenly matched. The match went back and forth as neither competitor could keep the other down for long. Acid hit Devine with quick and powerful kicks while Devine worked over Acid with a plethora of suplexes and other moves. In the end Acid was able to slip out of the Devine Dream Drop. He turned Devine around and hit a Mafia kick on him. Acid goes up top for the Acid Rain Bomb. He comes down. Onto the mat. Devine gets back to his feet and stalks Acid waiting for him to get up. He hits a 3D on him. He makes the cover. 1....2....3.

Winner: Triple D in 16:48

Rating (B) (!!!)

-Devine celebrates what was undoubtedly a tough victory for him when Umaga comes out onto the entrance ramp. DDD stands himself up against the ropes and stares back at Umaga. The 2 don't take their eyes off each other, and neither moves. The staredown continues as AAW goes off the air.

(C+)

Final Rating (C+)

-Best show so far.

-Best match so far.

-Best booker Evaaaaar! Well maybe I'm tooting my own horn a little soon. Oh well. Best. Booker. Ever.

-Umaga defeated Freddie Datsun to retain the USPW World title (C+)

-Triple D defeated Acid (B)

Pre-Show News:

-Giant Oak has officially signed with NYCW. He is now the youngest member of their roster.

-In news that could very well be the beginning of the end for DAVE, Eric Tyler was injured at the last DAVE Grappling in a match with Big Cat Brandon. Tyler will be out of action for a little over 13 Months basically meaning that when he comes back it'll be for his last run. To understand how big of a loss for DAVE this is you have to realize Tyler was the only member of their roster that could consistently pull out B- matches, holds win over all of DAVEs top stars and is the most over wrestler on their roster. Here's hoping that SWF and TCW don't scoop up too many of their top names when DAVE goes out of business.

USPW All American Wrestling

Saturday, Week 2, May 2007

Held at The North Carolina Biker Museum

Att.2,000 (Sold Out)

Dark Matches

-Fish & Oak defeated The Hotshots (Davis Wayne Newton & Cameron Vessey) in 7:37 when Wanda Fish defeated Davey Wayne by pinfall with a Blonde Ambition. (D-)

Main Show

-The show starts off with Bruce the Giant, Peter Valentine, Umaga and Jacob Stranton in their locker room. Stranton is trying to cut a promo on his, Umagas and Bruces upcoming match with DDD and Muffin.

Stranton: "Tonight, my monsters will destroy both Devine and Muffin. Umaga will tear them to shreds, rip them apart, show the two lovebirds the meaning of the word pain. Umaga eats Muffins for breakfast. And Devine, do you really think you have a chance of stopping an unstoppable Samoan Savage? Not once, but twice? At the debut of USPW Family Night Umaga will retain the title and send you back to Canada where you belong. Devine, you silly bastard..."

Valentine: "Trix are for kids!"

Everyone in the room shoots an angry glance towards Valentine.

Valentine: "Well they are."

Stranton: "Shut up Peter."

Valentine: "I'm still not talking to you."

Stranton: "Then who are you going to talk to?"

Valentine: "My good friend Bruce the Giant, of course. Right Bruce?"

BTG: "Rym nohrt ure frehnd, nar ghit ure ond ohf mi ohrm."

Valentine: "Ok, umm.... I'll just talk to my good friend Umaga."

Umaga: "Ooga booga (And some more of a Samoan language I can't possibly comprehend)."

Valentine: "Ok, maybe not."

Stranton: "Neither of them speaks English you idiot."

Valentine: "I'm not an idiot, I'm a pregnant man."

Stanton: "You do realize just how much that statement solidifies my point, don't you?"

Valentine: "Sorry, what were you saying? I just felt my baby kicking."

Stranton: "Oh, forget this. Now where was I?"

Camera Man: "Sorry Mr. Stranton, but you're out of time."

Stranton: "Oh, for fu..."

(C)

-Psycho babble time.

Force: "DUUUUUUUUUUUDEZ!!!!!!!!!!!!! YOU DISASTEROUS DEFORMATIONAL DESIGN OF DELINQUENCYYYYYYYYYYYYY!!!!!! YOU WILL FALL TO THE FORCE OF THE FORCE FOR THE FORCE IS A FORCE UPON WHICH I WILL FORCE YOUR LOSS. BUY MY MERCHANDISE. FRANKIE PEREEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEZ!!!!!!! TONIGHT YOU SHALL FEEL THE FOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO OOOOOOOOOORCE!!!!! RARGHHH!!!!"

(D)

vs.

-Jim Force vs. Frankie Perez-

Forces power and paint style of wrestling didn't stand much chance against Perez's strong style. Perez finished off Force with a Perfect Parity to pick up the win.

Winner: Frankie Perez in 7:57

Rating (D+)

-A graphic pops up announcing that at the debut of Family Night on June 5th The Dudez!!!1!!! will defend their tag team Championships against Veteran Mentality.

(D+)

-Steve Flash is in his locker room sitting back reading comics like last week. William Hayes walks into the room.

Hayes: "I just wanted to tell you face to face that after I beat Ota tonight I'm coming after that National title you have."

Flash: "That all came in here to say to me?"

Hayes: "I also wanted to tell you to cherish that belt while you can, because on June 5th you're going to have to shine that belt up real nice, say your farewells and hand it over to me."

Flash: "Well William, I'm not planning on losing this belt. Especially not to you."

Hayes: "Nobody ever plans on losing their championships Steve, they just do."

The conversation is interrupted as both Hayes and Flash turn their attention to the ventilation ducts where a noise is coming from. A shuriken flies out of it. "Great, just what we need." says Hayes sarcastically. Out of the shadows pops Fumihiro Ota.

Hayes: "What do you want? I'm planning on humiliating you later on tonight already, isn't that enough for you?"

Ota hands a note to Hayes. Hayes takes it cautiously, keeping his eyes on Ota even as he reads.

Hayes: "You can talk ninja boy, you know that? Lets see here, there's nothing on this thing."

Hayes lowers the note down only to get misted by Ota who then disappears.

Flash: "Looks like he wasn't in a talking mood."

(D+)

vs.

-Rudy Velasquez vs. Freddie Datsun-

Another good match out of these two as their great chemistry helped them out. Datsun was in control of this match for a good portion of it, but Velasquez was able to cheat momentum his way during the match as well. Velasquez went for a Street Cutter only to have Datsun shove him off. Velasquez turns around for a kick to the gut and a Patriot Press. Before the ref can make it to 3, he's pulled out by Maxmillion. Rex hops in the ring and attacks Datsun. The ref sees it and calls for the bell.

Winner by DQ: Freddie Datsun in 8:17

Rating (C)

-Velasquez gets to his feet and Maxmillion gets into the ring to join Rex. Rex, Maxmillion and Velasquez continue their attack on Datsun leaving him a bloody mess in the middle of the ring.

(D)

vs.

-Fumihiro Ota vs. William Hayes-

And this is just one more reason I shouldn't have anymore heel/heel matches.

These two went after each other hard, unfortunately the crowd had no idea who to cheer for. In fact there was a boring chant going, until the match really picked up that is. a back and forth match saw the momentum switch between Hayes and Ota constantly. This hard hitting encounter ended as Ota misses the Ninja Strike. Hayes picks him up and hits the Slick Trick. He makes the cover and 3 seconds later it's over.

Winner: William Hayes in 13:10

Rating (D+)

-Triple D and Seduction are in their locker room talking, sort of.

DDD: "Look, we were just talking."

Seduction: "I don't want you to be anywhere near her again."

DDD: "Isn't that a bit harsh?"

Seduction: "Not if you love me."

DDD: "Of course I love you. You know I do, or at least you should."

Seduction: "I know you do, but I don't like to take chances."

DDD: "You know I have a match with her later."

Seduction: "And if I even see you look at her any way I don't like you'll be sleeping on the couch tonight."

DDD: "Let's be rational here."

Seduction: "I see the way you look at her and if you so much as think of her like that I won't think twice about making your life a living hell."

DDD: "You actually think I'm going to cheat on you, don't you?"

Seduction: "The thought's crossed my mind."

DDD: "Seduction, you're the only woman for me. Muffin is just a friend."

Seduction: "Like I said, if you're lying to me I WILL make your life a living hell."

DDD: "There's nothing to worry about. You're the only woman I love."

Outside the locker room Muffin has her hand on the door handle, but after hearing that conversation rethinks it and walks away.

(D+)

vs. w/

-Triple D & Muffin vs. Bruce the Giant & Umaga w/ Stranton-

Bruce the Giant and Triple D started this match off. Bruce used his size and power to dominate Devine, who still refused to tag Muffin into the match. Bruce toyed with Devine. Bruce should've learned from his last match with DDD because Devine caught his second wind and started fighting back. He fired off a series of rights that rocked the giant. Devine springs off the ropes, Bruces tries to catch him with a clothesline, but Devine catches Bruce in the knees with a dropkick. Bruce hobbles around trying to catch Devine, but gets chop blocked for his efforts. Umaga storms into the ring and goes for DDD, but gets a flying forearm from Muffin. DDD goes back to work on Bruce wearing down the knee.

DDD takes his eyes off Bruce as he sees Umaga beating on Muffin on the outside. Bruce takes advantage and grabs Devine by the throat. Bruce gets to his feet, but DDD goes down from a chokeslam. Bruce makes the cover. 1....2..., rope break. Bruce struggles to make it back to his feet. He goes to pick up Devine, but gets small packaged. The ref makes the count, 1.... 2..... 3.

Winners: Triple D & Muffin in 10:11

Rating (C-)

-DDD and Muffin celebrate their huge win. Umaga is livid while Bruce tries to apologize to him. Umaga attacks both DDD and Muffin from behind. He lays Muffin out with a spear while he takes DDD out with a sit-out powerbomb. Bruce celebrates with Umaga, only to get attacked by him as well. Umaga lays him out with a Savage Charge before heading to the back with Stranton, who seems to be keeping his distance from the Samoan Savage.

(C+)

Final Rating (D+)

-Yea, it was a step backwards, but it was a necessary step.

-Holy crap on a stick. Devines popularity over the US shot up to a B after last night, except Puerto Rico, of course. Strange thing is Bruce only had a B- across America going into that match.

-Freddie Datsun defeated Rudy Velasquez by DQ (C)

-William Hayes defeated Fumihiro Ota (D+)

-Triple D and Muffin defeated Bruce The Giant and Umaga (C-)

USPW All American Wrestling

Saturday, Week 3, May 2007 (Airs Wednesday)

Held at The Den

Att. 2,000 (Sold Out)

Dark Matches

-Warlord Agony defeated Zeus Maxmillion, Insane Machine and Mean Jean Cattley in 6:36 when Warlord Agony defeated Zeus Maxmillion by pinfall with a Hostile Takeover. (D)

-Bulldozer Brandon Smith and Trent Shaffer defeated The Hotshots in 7:40 when Trent Shaffer defeated Cameron Vessey by pinfall with a Heart Burn. (E)

Main Show

-The show starts off in Danny Jillefskis office where USPW National Champion Steve Flash, and challenger William Hayes are standing.

Jillefski: "Hello gentlemen. It's become apparent over the weeks that you two want to get your hands on each other in the middle of the ring. Tonight I feel generous, which is why I'm going to give you two a match against each other. But, it's not going to be a title match, it's not even going to be a singles match. It's going to be a tag team match and I'm going to let you pick your own partners for it. Choose wisely because the winner of tonights match will get to decide what type of match you have at the debut of Family Night."

(D+)

-Jim Force makes his entrance and stomps down to the ring as he always does. He awaits his opponent for tonight, Mainstream Hernandez, in the ring when music hits the speakers. It's not Hernandez's music though, it's Demon Angers. Anger makes his way down to the ring and grabs a mic.

Anger: "I know almost everyone out here probably hates me, but that really doesn't matter to me. As some of you will have already heard I'm planning on retiring soon. For those who didn't know, well now you do. I got around to thinking though, how do I want to go out? Do I want to just quietly sink into retirement? Do I want to give a speech and let that be all? Do I want to have one last match against some nobody? Or do I want to go out on top? Well the answer is that I'm going to retire as a 9 time Tag Team Champion and win the one belt that's been eluding me for the last 3 years. Force, I know you and Oxford have a title shot coming up and I'm not asking you to give it up, I'm warning you that I'm going to take it away."

Anger hits Force with a big boot that knocks him loopy. Anger tells the ref to ring the bell and we have a match.

(D)

vs.

-Demon Anger vs. Jim Force-

The "Every match I think could be good , but then I find they have bad chemistry dammit" curse is back in full force. Get it? Full FORCE? I'm so punny. Anyways, onto the match. Force fought a forceful fight, but Angers desire to retire as champion was too much for him. Anger finished off Force with a Demon Slam.

Winner: Demon Anger in 6:05

Rating (E)

-Jacob Stranton is backstage with Umaga and a surprisingly scared looking Bruce the Giant. (And, for one night only, we have a Bruce the Giant translator on board.)

BTG: "But why do I have to have a match with him?"

Stranton: "Because you failed. Umaga doesn't like failure. Neither do I. Isn't that right Umaga?"

Umaga: "Ooga booga (And some other incomprehensible Samoan)

Stranton: "And after that failure you have to pay, and you're going to do it with your health."

BTG: "But I didn't do anything wrong, why should I have to get into the ring with it?"

Stranton: "You lost a match Bruce. That's what you did wrong. You have to learn that as a team if you lose, I lose and so does Umaga. Umaga doesn't like to lose. It makes him angry. It makes him volatile. It makes him want to hurt people. Tonight, I let him hurt you."

BTG: "I'm sorry, I didn't mean to lose. It was an accident, I swear."

Stranton: "Sorry Bruce, this is just the way it has to be."

Bruce walks off for his match still clearly afraid of what Umaga was going to do to him. Peter Valentine now walks in. Valentine: "I think my water just broke." Valentine holds up a broken water bottle. "Can I have a dollar to get another one?" Stranton, clearly annoyed, hands Valentine a dollar.

(D+)

vs.

-Bruce the Giant vs. Umaga-

How do you squash a 7' tall man? Just ask Umaga. Umaga dominated this match and put Bruce away pretty quickly with a Savage Charge.

Winner: Umaga in 5:51

Rating (D+)

vs.

-Rudy Velasquez vs. Freddie Datsun-

Their good chemistry helped this match out, but alot of people had a bad taste in their mouth after the finish of this match. Datsun had the momentum on his side and was about to finish Velasquez off with a Patriot Press. Maxmillion distracted the ref while Rex made an impression on Datsun. A boot impression on his chin to be exact. Rex hits Datsun with a Jurassic Power and throws Velasquez on top. The ref turns around in time to count the 3.

Winner: Rudy Velasquez in 9:27

Rating (D+)

-DDD is backstage warming up when Muffin walks up to him. They greet each other with nervous smiles on both of their faces. Muffin and DDD stand silent for a few seconds before both blurting out that they have something to tell the other. Devine says that she can go first, but Muffin insists that he go first. Devine tells her that he thinks it would be best if they didn't talk to each other anymore. Muffin looks a little choked up, but keeps the faux-smile on her face. Devine asks what it was she wanted to tell him, but she says it was nothing. Muffin says "I'll see you around." before walking off.

(B)

-Steve Flash makes his entrance. He grabs a mic and introduces his partner, Triple D. Devine makes his way to the ring to join his partner. Hayes makes is way out onto the stage now with mic in hand. Hayes introduces his partner for tonight, Joey Minnesota. (OOC Note: I really wanted to bring in someone from Japan, but nobody at PGHW wants Bruce.)

(C)

vs.

-Triple D & Steve Flash vs. William Hayes & Joey Minnesota-

It seems Hayes and Minnesota don't work too well together. Dammit.

A good match otherwise though. Hayes and Minnesota spent a good chunk of the match making quick tags in and out wearing Flash down. Flash caught his second win though and made the tag to his partner. DDD exploded into the ring taking down anything that moved. He goes for a 3D on Hayes. Hayes shoves him off. Hayes goes for the Slick Trick. He gets Devine up, but Flash is in from behind with a chop block that takes Hayes down. Minnesota takes Flash out with a back elbow. He gets Devine up for the Minnesota Salute, but Devine turns it into a DDT. Both men go down. Devine crawls over and makes the cover on Minnesota. 1.....2... Hayes breaks it up.

Flash is in and he goes after Hayes. Flash goes for Flash Bang, but Hayes slips out. Devine back up and hits a 3D on Hayes. Minnesota clubs Devine and then lands a Minnesota Salute on Flash. Minnesota goes for the pin, but Flash isn't the legal man. Minnesota turns his attention to Devine, but walks right into a Devine Dream Drop. Devine with the pin, 1....2....3.

Winners: Flash & Devine in 16:12

Rating (C)

-Hayes clotheslines Flash over the top rope to the floor and then goes after DDD. They brawl until Umaga charges to the ring. Umaga tears into DDD while Hayes helps out making sure to keep his distance while he is. Flash gets back in the ring and goes after Umaga, but Hayes intercepts him and the two brawl.

Muffin runs out from the back and goes after Umaga. She hits him with shot after shot, but Umaga doesn't feel any of it. Flash tosses Hayes out of the ring and goes after Umaga. Muffin and Flash double team Umaga, but he just swats them away. He goes to clothesline both of them, but they duck under it. Umaga stops himself with the ropes, turns around and gets taken out of the ring with a vicious clothesline from Devine.

Devine, Muffin, and Flash stand tall in the ring while Stranton, Minnesota, and Hayes try to keep Umaga from jumping back into the ring.

(C+)

Final Rating (C-)

-And the matches for Family Night are now set. If anyone wants to make predictions:

-USPW National Championship Match- William Hayes vs. Steve Flash (c)

-USPW Tag Team Championship Match- The Demons of Rage vs. The Dudez!!!1!!! (c)
