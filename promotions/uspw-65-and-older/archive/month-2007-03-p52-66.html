USPW All American Wrestling

Wednesday, Week 1, March 2007 (Oh, it's so sweet to finally be able to write that line.)

Held at Pennsylvania Park in front of 2,000 people (Sold Out)

Dark Matches

-Jim Force has an interview, in which he goes off on a rant about forces, glitter, the need for you to buy his merchandise, foreign policy, and something about Cameron Vessey. The translator is still working on that part. (E+) -Jim Force defeated Cameron Vessey in 3:48 by pinfall with a Full Force. (D-) (Cameron Vessey and Jim Force have great chemistry. Who would've guessed that?)

-Thomas Morgan defeated Giant Oak in 3:52 by pinfall. (F) (Giant Oak and Thomas Morgan just don't click at all. Well, 1 for 1.)

-Fumihiro Ota defeated Steve Flash in 4:40 by pinfall with a Ninja Strike. (C-) (And in the 'They've both got downsides anyways' match of the night...and for that matter match of the night.)

Main Show

-The show starts off with a shot of Darryl Devines locker room door. Devine enters the room.

DDD: "Hey, Seduc...." He pauses and has a double take. The camera spins around to reveal Seduction and Peter Valentine. In a wig. And dress. And makeup.

DDD: "....tion. What's going on here?"

Seduction: "He's dressed up like me."

Valentine: "No I'm not, I'm the real Seduction. She's the fake."

DDD: "What are doing Peter?"

Valentine: "I am not the great wrestler Peter Valentine, I am Seduction."

DDD: "You are an idiot."

Valentine: "No I am not, I am Seduction. I will prove it now." Valentine lunges after Devine and looks to be trying to make out with him. Devine shoves him away.

DDD: "Get... Get away from me you freak!! What the hell is wrong with you?!?"

Valentine: "There is nothing wrong with me for I am Seduction."

DDD: "No, you're a moron. And don't you have a match to get ready for?"

Valentine: "No I don't for I am not Peter Valentine, I am Seduction."

After a moment of awkward silence Devine lowers his head and shakes it from side to side in disgrace. He then looks to Seduction, the real one, and takes her by the hand. "... Let's just go."

They exit their locker room. Outside they see Freddie Datsun.

Datsun: "There you are. I was looking for you. Something wrong?"

DDD: "You don't want to know."

The camera swings around to reveal Bruce the Giant coming down the hall. Bruce nears DDD and Datsun, but Valentine leaves Devines locker room now.

Valentine: "I am Seduction!"

Devines face has a look of utter disgrace on it while Datsun and Bruce are quite surprised by what they see. Bruce stops in his tracks, covers his face and walks off. Valentine spots him "Where are you going partner?"

Bruce runs off.

(B-)

-The Canadian Crusties def. The Lords Of War to retain the USPW World Tag Team titles-

The Crusties are crusty and the Warlords are rusty. It's kinda sad to think this is basically the best my tag division has to offer. The collective amount of talent in this match would struggle to fill a water bottle. As for what happened during the match there were alot of punches and kicks thrown. In fact I just listed 99% of the moves used in this match. The end of it came as one of the McWades pinned one of the Warlords with feet on ropes.

(D+)

-We see Trent Shaffer walking backstage when out of nowhere a large refrigerator box falls behind him. Shaffer looks at the box and then looks around. Zeus Maxmillion comes out of hiding and begins yelling.

Zeus: "You buffoons. You told me you had the X exactly where the box would fall." Velasquez now comes out. Velasquez: "We did. Somebody must've moved the box." And now Rex comes out.

Rex: "It was Rudy. I saw him move it."

Velasquez: "I thought I told you not you not to tell him carnivore breath."

Zeus: "You just screwed up nearly 2 months worth of careful planning."

Shaffer: "You guys took 2 months to set that lame thing up?"

Zeus: "You stay out of this."

Velasquez: "Yea, get lost punk."

Shaffer: "Whatever dudes."

Shaffer walks off leaving the 3 men to continue arguing.

(E+)

-Trent Shaffer def. T-Rex-

Does Shaffer click with anything? If he does I'm having a hard time finding it. Maybe he can click with a clicky pen. Just saying. A pretty blah affair here. The end of the match saw Mainstream Hernandez distract Rex while Shaffer gets a surprise roll-up for the win.

(D-)

-Rudy Velasquez makes his way down to the ring furious over what just happened. He gets in the refs face and demands he reverse the decision. Rudy then yells at Rex for losing. He then gets on the mic and calls out Shaffer and Hernandez. Hernandez challenges Velasquez to a match right now. Velasquez accepts the challenge.

(D-)

-Rudy Velasquez def. Mainstream Hernandez-

A pretty good match from the midcarders here. The match was a back and forth affair as neither Velasquez or Hernandez could maintain control for long. Hernandez looked about ready to win the match as he went up top for the Apparition #14. Rex distracts the ref as Maxmillion crotches Hernandez. Velasquez recovers and goes up to the middle rope from which he hits a Street Cutter from.

(D)

-Umaga def. Demon Spite-

And from that display of athleticism to this display of.... something. Despite all he tried Spite just couldn't hurt the Samoan. Umaga dominated this match and finished Spite off with a Savage Charge after about 5 minutes.

(D)

-Umaga continues to beat Spite to death, which sadly could probably accidentally happen. Stranton joins Umaga in the ring and puts the boots to Spite. Demon Anger runs from the back as fast as he can, feel free to take a piss break right about now btw, to try to save his partner. When Umaga and Stranton catch sight of Anger they look like deer caught in the headlights while he makes his way down to the ring. When Anger finally gets into the ring Umaga and Stranton bail.

(D)

-Triple D and Freddie Datsun are backstage being interviewed about their upcoming match with Bruce the Giant and Peter Valentine. When asked about Valentine DDD had this to say "No comment." When Datsun was asked about Valentine his response was "Ya know the old saying, if you ain't got nothing nice to say then don't say nothing? That ought to answer your question." When Datsun was asked about Bruce the Giant he said this, "Bruce the Giant is a sick vicious individual. He's big, he's strong, but he's as slow in the ring as Peter Valentine at book learning. You see a chink in the armor you make that chink into a tear and take advantage of it. I've beaten you before Bruce and I plan to do it again tonight. See you in the ring and I really hope you like your view of the lights."

(B-)

-Bruce The Giant & Peter Valentine def. Freddie Datsun & Triple D-

I knew I shouldn't have let Valentine actually do anything. Every time he was in the ring the crowd went mild (Not a typo). Devine and Datsun struggled during this match, struggled to make it good that is.

Bruce was the unstoppable giant in this match taking apart both Datsun and Devine. Valentine made a few blind tags which didn't sit too well with Bruce. It probably didn't help that every time Valentine was in the ring Datsun and Devine were able to turn the tides in their favor. In the end it was a blind tag from Bruce that would finish off Triple D. Bruce caught him with a Giant Chokeslam and got the 3.

(D+)

-With the match over and Bruce victorious he went after Datsun who was on the outside with Valentine. Bruce attacks from behind and shoves him into the corner post. Bruce tells Valentine to get in the ring and take care of DDD. Bruce drags Datsun over to the announce table and chokeslams him through it. Bruce now turns his attention back to the ring where Valentine seems to have lost his chair to DDD. Bruce gets in the ring as Devine is about to cream Valentine with it and punches it back into his face.

Bruce, and I guess Valentine too, stand victorious over their fallen foes as the show goes off the air.

(C+)

Final Rating (D+)

-Aaaaaaaaaand we're back in the slumps. We need to find some pairings that'll draw C- ratings at least. I already know one thing, it doesn't involve Valentine. Within a 50 foot radius at least.

-People thought it was excellent?!?!?!? What the ---- are they smoking?

-Hmm, Giant Oak (I'll explain that one later) is improving. A .02 or 3 increase in every skill. At this rate he could be a great wrestler in about.... oh, a decade.

USPW All American Wrestling

Monday, Week 2, March 2007 (Airs Wednesday)

Held at The Den

Att. 2,000 (Sell-out)

Dark Matches

-The Forces Of America had an interview hyping their upcoming tag match with T-Rex and Zeus Maxmillion.(D-) (And the psycho babble continues. This week Force goes on a rant about the movie industry, impressionable youths, the need for you to buy his merchandise, and somebody feeling the force.)

-Zeus Maxmillion and T-Rex defeated The Forces Of America in 5:47 when T-Rex defeated Jim Force by pinfall with The Extinction. (E+) (The announcing helped this match. That should say something about the actual match quality.)

-Giant Oak is walking backstage, turns a corner, and bumps into Steve Flash. Steve Flash says something that Oak doesn't like, and they start arguing. After some heated words, Steve Flash challenges Oak to meet him in the ring for a match. (E+) (What have we learned here? Old people are easily angered.)

-Steve Flash defeated Giant Oak in 6:38 by pinfall with a Flash Bang. (E+) (I guess even Flash has his limitations.)

Main Show

-We start off the show with the cameras on Triple D and Seduction who are walking the halls. Out of nowhere pops out Valentine. He has on a Darth Vader mask and a cheap looking plastic lightsaber in his hands.

Valentine: "Darryl, I am your father."

DDD: (To Seduction) "Just keep walking, ignore him. Don't look, you'll just encourage him."

They walk off.

Valentine: "Where are you going son? Don't you want to play catch?"

(C)

-William Hayes vs. Warlord Pain-

The "Every match I think could be good , but then I find they have bad chemistry dammit" curse rears it's ugly head for the first time tonight. A pretty competitive squash here as Pain gets in a good bit of offense. Hayes has no problems putting away Pain after a Slick Trick though.

Winner: William Hayes in 6:21

Rating (D)

-Hayes grabs a mic and goes on talking about how great Japaneses people are. He says that his kind would easily wipe the floor with any American. He then issues an open challenge for any American that thinks they could beat him.

(C-)

-Perez, Shaffer and Hernandez are in their locker room. Perez and Shaffer are getting ready for their match against each other. The tension in the air can be felt as neither says a word. Hernandez asks them if they're ready. Both just stand up and walk out the door.

(D-)

-Frankie Perez vs. Trent Shaffer-

The hatred that's been brewing between these 2 for months now can be seen in both competitors eyes. They lock up to start off the match, but it quickly turns into a fight with Perez and Shaffer just trying to beat the living hell out of each other. The match goes back and forth between the 2 with neither having a clear advantage. After 7 minutes Zeus Maxmillion and T-Rex charge the ring and attack Perez and Shaffer. The referee calls for the bell and this match is over.

Winner: Draw in 7:31

Rating (D-)

-Rex and Zeus continue their attack on Shaffer and Perez. It's interesting to note that neither Velasquez or Hernandez are anywhere in sight.

(E)

-Demon Anger vs. Umaga-

2 tastes that don't taste good together. Then again I can't imagine Anger to taste like anything other than dead skin. What was I going to say? Oh right, this match was nothing more than a glorified squash. Anger got a few licks in, but that was the entirety of his offense against Umaga. Umaga finishes Anger with a Savage Charge.

Winner: Umaga in 5:51

Rating (D-)

-Triple D is backstage talking to Freddie Datsun. Datsun vs. Valentine is up next and DDD is helping psyche the champ up.

DDD: "Just remember, it may seem like you're hitting someone who's special, but you're not. He's just stupid. You know you got this one man."

Datsun: "If I couldn't beat this guy do you think I would be the World Champ right now? I got this covered."

(C)

-Peter Valentine vs. Freddie Datsun (c) for the USPW World title-

I hear that Valentine was off his game during this one. He has a game? Wonder if he wants to play Monopoly sometime. As far as the match goes Datsun truly worked a miracle here and carried Valentine to a relatively good match. In the end Datsun was able to slip out of a Heart Breaker and catch Valentine in the Patriot Press.

Winner: Freddie Datsun in 6:50

Rating (C-)

-DDD is backstage being interviewed about his upcoming match with Bruce the Giant.

DDD: "Am I afraid of Bruce? That's the question, huh? Well of course I'm afraid of what he can do to me. I felt it last week. I've seen it before. I've watched it as it happened. Of course I'm afraid. The real question is if I'm going to let that fear get in my way. The answer to that question is of course not. I don't care what I have to put my body through because tonight I will beat Bruce the Giant. No matter what."

(B+)

-Triple D vs. Bruce the Giant-

This match was a true contrast in size, style, speed, skill and quality. Bruce dominated the opening minutes with Devine trying to get away and catch a breather. Bruce was unrelenting though and kept on the attack. Devine tried unsuccessfully to make a comeback twice during that, but Bruces massive fists saw an end to those attempts. Bruce went for the Giant Chokeslam, but Devine began to kick away at the knees of the Giant. Bruce let go of Devines neck and began clutching his own knee.

Devine went on the attack chop blocking the big man until he fell. Bruce went down to his knees after about 3 to the knee. Devine sprang off the ropes, but was caught by the neck on the rebound by Bruce. Bruce dropped DDD with a Giant Chokeslam. Bruce made the cover and the 3 count was inevitable.

Winner: Bruce the Giant in 6:45

Rating (C)

-Bruce is victorious and Devine is down. Peter Valentine comes down to the ring now and starts stomping away at DDD. Bruce joins in and it's a 2-on-1 assault. Freddie Datsun runs down to the ring, steel chair in hand, and chases the 2 heels off. Datsun throws the chair down to check on DDD. Bad idea.

From out of nowhere Umaga levels Datsun with a clothesline to the back of the neck. Bruce and Valentine join Umaga back in the ring and continue their attack. The show ends with Umaga, Bruce and Valentine standing over the unconscious Datsun and Devine.

(C)

Final Rating: (C-)

Well, at least we're finding those C-'s. Now we have to find some consistent C's because we're still leaking popularity. Lucky me that everyone seemed to step up a little. Valentine in a C- match? I knew there was a reason Datsun is champ.

USPW All American Wrestling

Monday, Week 2, March 2007 (Airs Wednesday)

Held at The Grissom Auditorium

Att. 2,000 (Sell-out)

Dark Matches

-Jim Force cuts a promo hyping up his upcoming match with Giant Oak. (D) (Force complains about Oak trees, acorns , maple syrup and squirrels. Yes, squirrels. He thinks their tails are too bushy. Oh, and buy his merchandise.)

-Jim Force defeated Giant Oak in 5:47 by pinfall with a Full Force. (E)

-Citizen X defeated Bulldozer Brandon Smith in 7:31 by pinfall by using underhanded tactics. Citizen X makes defense number 5 of his USPW National title. (D-) (Oddly that's more defenses than I thought he had. Maybe I should pay more attention to my championships.)

Main Show

-Freddie Datsun starts off the show in the ring with his shiny gold belt draped over his shoulder. Datsun brings the mic up to his mouth to speak, but is cut off by the music of Umaga. Down to the ring comes his manager Jacob Stranton. Umaga conspicuously missing from this scene. Stranton tries to grab the mic from Datsuns hands, but Datsun isn't letting go. Stranton settles on getting his own.

Stranton: "Before you bore all of these people to death with your long, drawn on speeches about how grateful you are and all that other bologna let me cut you off. You're a nobody who just scrapes by. Everybody else does the work, you take the credit. You, Freddie Datsun, are undeserving of wearing that belt. That's why tonight I'm here on behalf of the undefeated Samoan Savage, Umaga. I challenge you on his behalf to put that title on the line in a match with him tonight."

Datsun: "I'm a fighting champion Mr. Stranton, and I'm also a man of my word, so believe me when I say that by the end of tonight Umaga is no longer going to be undefeated."

As Datsun finishes that sentence Umaga shows up out of the crowd and hammers Datsun from behind. Umaga savages Datsun until security make their way into the ring and break it up. While they pull Umaga off of Datsun Stranton gets himself involved. He takes one of the retirement home pamphlets, crumples it up and shoves it down Datsuns throat.

(C-)

-The Dudez!!!1 vs. Maxmillion Productions-

The ARSEHOLES thought this match sucked. I do too. That's why I'm not going to spend too much time on it. The end of the match saw Rudy Velasquez get a pinfall on Trent Shaffer after pulling the tights.

Winners: Maxmillion Productions in 6:33

Rating (D-)

-Shaffer, Perez and Hernandez make it to the back where an argument ensues. It seems that Perez and Hernandez are getting sick of Shaffer. Shaffer responds by saying that he doesn't need either of them to be successful, he'll do it on his own. The argument ended with Shaffer and Perez going back and forth saying "Fine." over and over.

(D-)

-Fumihiro Ota vs. Steve Flash-

Great match from these 2 on their television debut and I'm sure we'll be seeing more of them. A hard fought match between these 2 evenly matched competitors saw plenty of back and forth action. It was youth that prevailed today though as Ota slips out of a Flash Bang and nails Flash with a Ninja Strike.

Winner: Fumihiro Ota in 8:45

Rating (C-)

-Flash is about to leave the ring when he's jumped from behind by Citizen X. X nails him in the back with the National Championship belt.

(E)

-Triple D is backstage being interviewed about his upcoming match with Peter Valentine.

DDD: "I'm a bit confused to be honest. I really have no clue. Is this really how Valentine acts or is he playing mind games with me? Is he waiting for me to drop my guard so he can attack? I'm not sure. I always thought Valentine was an alright guy, pretty much sane, but all of a sudden he starts acting like this. I'm getting a bit suspicious, but I'm not going to let my guard down tonight. I'll beat Valentine one more time in the middle of that ring."

The camera pans out to see Valentine, with the Vader mask still on, standing behind Devine. Devine turns around and is startled. Valentine pulls out a flashlight, holds it up to his chin and turns it on.

Valentine: "Darryl, I am your mother."

DDD doesn't say anything, instead just walking off.

(C)

-Peter Valentine vs. Triple D w/ Seduction-

Well, Devine may be no miracle worker, but this wasn't a bad Peter Valentine match. It was a surprisingly even contest throughout. Devine was able to get the upper hand though and finished Valentine off with Devine Dream Drop.

Winner: Triple D in 7:31

Rating (D)

(C)

-Stranton is in the back with Umaga.

Stranton: "Datsun, I hope you like the taste of your own blood. It's gonna be everywhere tonight. Umaga will rip you apart, tear you to shreds, decimate you. You don't stand a chance against the Samoan Savage. You can throw in the towel now and avoid taking a beating the likes of which you have never seen before, but you won't do that. You're too proud of yourself. You're a fighting champion, the American Everyman. Well Freddie, no man can stop Umaga."

(D+)

-Datsun is now being interviewed about the upcoming title match.

Datsun: "You've jumped me from behind 2 weeks in a row now Umaga. Are you afraid to do it to my face? Do you know that I'm capable of stopping you? Well by the time tonight is over you will. I fight for every man and every woman who has ever had a dream. I always wanted to be World Champion and now that I'm finally back on the top of the hill there's no way that I'm going to give it up so easily. You're right when you say that I'm too proud to call it quits Stranton, but I'm proud because I know that I'm the best in this company. I know that your Samoan Savage can't put me away and I know that by the time this night is over everyone else will too."

(C)

-Umaga w/ Jacob Stranton vs. Freddie Datsun (c) for the USPW World title-

Datsuns run of putting on good matches with the younger guys continues. Umaga looked like a monster out there and had the crowd on the edges of their seats waiting to see if they were going to get to see a new World Champion crowned.

Umaga dominated a good majority of this match keeping Datsun down and almost out. Datsun tried to swing momentum in his favor many times throughout the match, but Umaga cut him off every time. 10 minutes into the match Datsun catches Umaga off guard and rocks him with rights. He springs off the ropes and hits a clothesline. Umaga is still standing. Datsun hits another, and Umaga starts to teeter. Datsun hits another and another and finally Umaga goes down. Datsun picks Umaga up and is able to get him on his shoulders. Patriot Press on Umaga.

1...2..., kick out by Umaga. Datsun can't believe it and neither can the crowd. Datsun picks Umaga up again and tries to get him onto his shoulders. Umaga fights it off with some clubbing blows to the back. Umaga backs Datsun into the corner and sends a few shoulder thrusts into his gut. Umaga backs up to the opposite corner and builds some steam. He hits the Savage Charge. Datsuns falls out of the corner. Umaga with a pin.

1,......2,..... Datsun gets his shoulder up. Umaga, Stranton and seemingly the crowd are all surprised. Stranton panics and grabs a steel chair. Stranton has Umaga hold Datsun up and just nails him with the chair. The ref calls for the DQ.

Winner: Freddie Datsun by DQ in 13:02

Rating (C-)

-Umaga continues attacking Datsun after the bell has rung. Stranton nails Datsun with it the chair again as Umaga holds him up. Stranton passes the chair to Umaga who lays it into Datsuns lifeless corpse over and over. Umaga holds Datsun up by the head as he lays a few punches into him. Umaga looks at the blood on his hands and wipes it across his chest as the show comes to an end.

(C-)

Final Rating (C-)
