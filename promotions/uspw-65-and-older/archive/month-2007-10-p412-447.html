Muffin shoots forearms into the gut of Devine until he loosens his grip on her finger. Muffin then lays forearms into the temple of Devine rocking him. Muffin attempts the Angel Driver, but the Divine One doesn't exactly play fair and rakes the eyes of Muffin temporarily blinding her. It's long enough to set her up for a Devine Dream Drop though. He gets ready to hit it, but then turns his attention towards the entranceway as Sam Strong runs as fast as his frail old body can carry him. Sam charges Devine and Devine extends his right arm for a clothesline, but Sam proves he still has something left in the tank and ducks under it.

Devine turns around, and a Double Strong Arm Tactic by father and daughter takes Devine down! Muffin picks him up and finishes him by drilling Devine down into the mat with an Angel Driver. The Strongs celebrate as they head up the ramp.

DDD: "You... you think this over? You think it would be that easy to keep me down? Well you don't get the last laugh. I get the last laugh. I do! Tonight Alicia you've got a match. It's going to be,.... it'll be you and Datsun versus the Wild Savages! Now who's got the last laugh?"

This was a pretty even contest between both teams. The momentum in this match swung back and forth alot, but the heels didn't want it to stay that way. He dragged Fish to the corner and made the tag.

Hall fought with all he had, but it just wasn't enough to take down both men on his own. Dozer dropped Hall down on his head with a Backdrop Driver. Dozer makes the pin, 1....2... Hall gets his foot on the bottom rope somehow. Dozer pulls him away and makes the cover again, 1.....2.... It's broken up by Wanda Fish who seems a little loopy from the shot earlier. Fish gets back to her corner as Hall crawls for it, and he makes the tag. Fish comes in and clears the ring. She nails Dozer with the Dish of the Day. 1.....2.....3.

Winner: Hall & Fish in 10:33

-Veteran Mentality & Steve Flash vs. Team Seduction & Art Reed w/ Seduction-

This was without a doubt Forces career best. Sad, eh?

Mostly your standard 6-man tag affair. Force was kept out of the ring as much as humanly possible while Flash played the face in peril as the heels took turns beating on him and wearing him down. -Muffin & Freddie Datsun vs. The Wild Savages w/ Stranton-

The Savages were extremely dominant here. When you put together two tough as nails Samoans who don't know what pain is that's what you should expect. Muffin was in no shape to be in the ring to begin with and it didn't help her at all tonight. The Savages targeted Muffin as an easy prey, but she put up more of a fight than anyone could have expected out of her. The Savages didn't let up any though. Umaga tossed Muffin into their corner and Akima held her in place. Umaga proceeded to break Muffin in half with a Savage Charge to the injured ribs. Umaga made the cover, but Datsun was right there to break it up.

The Savages continued their battering of Muffin as they seemed to refuse to just finish this thing. Muffin tried to fight back long enough to get in a tag, but it was no use. The Savages then began to argue about something in Samoan. This left Muffin with enough time to get in the tag to Datsun. Datsun came into the ring like a house on fire and was able to knock the Savages off their feet. Datsun went for the Patriot Press on Umaga, but Umaga slid out behind him and held him in place as Akima charged in with a Savage Spear of his own. Datsun moved out of the way and Akima nailed Umaga. Muffin charged in and took Akima out of the ring with a Strong Arm Tactic (Running elbow). Datsun picked Umaga up and then back down with a Patriot Press. 1......2.....3.

Winners: Muffin & Datsun in 19:37

vs.

-Frankie Perez vs. Davey Wayne-

Interesting little side note: Perez and Wayne are at about the same popularity levels as Devine and Hayes were when they pulled off that B match that stood as my best for months. Until Arsenal/Perez at least.

These two went all out to prove that they're the better wrestler. Wayne was desperate to win this since the last win he had (according to DDD) was against Hernandez, so he went all out. Perez didn't hold anything back either. Devine looked on from the outside as an interested observer while Hernandez kept his eyes on Devine and Vessey on the outside. Inside the ring however the match was virtually a roller coaster ride as the momentum went back and forth between the two competitors. Perez was clearly bettering Wayne, which is when he cheated. A back elbow to the nads catches Perez off guard and allows Wayne to work over Perez. Devine looks on approvingly.

Wayne works on Perez's abdominal and lower back regions and then locks on the STF. Perez screams in agony as he tries to make it to the ropes. Perez gets closer and closer and as he's about to grab it Wayne uses his free foot to push it further away. Eventually Perez is able to grab the rope, but the damage has been done. Devine seems to like Waynes tactics. Wayne Picks Perez up and tries for the Fishermans Suplex, but Perez locks his leg around Waynes to stop him. Perez gets in a few shots to the gut and then takes Wayne up for the Perfect Parity.

Vessey grabs onto the leg of Perez to stop him and Hernandez is quick to take Vessey down. Wayne slips out of the Perfect Parity and runs off the ropes, but Perez catches him with a rolling wheel kick. Wayne gets back up and goes down from a clothesline. He's up again and down with a back elbow. Wayne gets up again and Perez catches him with an Explosion Suplex. The crowd is going wild as Perez signals the end. He takes Wayne up with a Perfect Parity, and he drives Wayne down. 1.....2.....3. Devine doesn't look happy anymore.

Winner: Frankie Perez in 22:48
