USPW Family Night

Friday, Week 2, August 2007

Held at The West Texas Coliseum

Att. 2,000 (Sold-Out)

Dark Matches

-Robert Oxford defeated Giant Oak in 5:37 by pinfall. (D)

-Wanda Fish defeated Rudy Velasquez in 7:28 by pinfall with a Dish Of The Day. (C+)

Main Show

-The show begins with a replay of the end Wednesdays All American Wrestling. Devine had just proposed to Muffin when a video from Maxmillion Productions began playing on the tron. The video was of Devine kissing some girl not named Muffin. Devine tried to explain but Muffin teared up and ran up the ramp to the back.

In this replay though we get exclusive footage of what happened after she got into the back. William Hayes found her and helped to console her. Devine ran to the back to find her, but found her in Hayes' arms. Hayes and Devine stared each other down, and that's all of the footage we have.

(B)

-Danny Jillefski comes down to the ring with Big Cat Brandon following closely. Jillefski grabs a mic and Fry gets ready to run.

Jillefski: "It's Friday Sam, it's Friday and I still don't have my answer yet. Brandon is going to hurt you either way Sam, don't make me have to go to the backup plan. Until I have my answer Mr. Fry is going to get hurt."

Fry gets up from his chair at the announce table and tries to hop the guard rail. He's cut off by Jacob Jett. Jett shoves Fry back and hops the guard rail. He tosses Fry into the ring and we have another execution, err... match.

(D+)

vs.

-Jacob Jett vs. Duane Fry-

Jett waits for Fry to stand and then nails him with a Mach 5 Superkick. He puts a foot on the chest and poses while the ref counts to 3.

Winner: Jacob Jett in 0:38

Rating (D)

-Devine walks up to Muffins locker room with a bouquet of roses in one hand. He knocks on the door. "Go away."

Devine doesn't and instead lets himself in.

DDD: "I have flowers for you."

Muffin: "Go away."

DDD: "Just give me a chance to talk to you."

Muffin: "Just leave me alone."

DDD: "Please, just hear me out."

Muffin: "I said leave me alone."

DDD: "Muffin, I know what you think you saw..."

Muffin: "I saw you kissing another girl. I should've known this would happen, after what you did to Seduction."

DDD: "The Seduction thing was different, I..."

Muffin: "Cheated on her. I should've seen this coming. This is what William was trying to warn me about, but I didn't listen."

DDD: "Listen to me, I know you think I was cheating on you, but that isn't true. I know that you're going to have a hard time believing this, but I was set up."

Muffin: "Yea right, just like..."

DDD: "Just like the truth. That girl came up to me and started kissing me. I had no clue what was going on and I had no idea that it was a set up. You have to believe me, I would never cheat on you Muffin, you're the love of my life and I wouldn't do anything to compromise that."

Muffin sits there silently while Devine waits for her to answer back.

DDD: "I hope you find it in your heart to believe me and forgive me."

(B)

-Giant Oak and Wanda Fish (still in cheerleader outfit for those who may have forgotten) make their way to the ring. They grab a mic.

Fish: "After last week I've had it up to here with Bulldozer Brandon Smith. After last week and after all he's done to poor, innocent Oakie for the last 2 months I want to finish this. Bulldozer, I challenge you to a match at Summer of Love."

Oak: "Yea, and she's going to kick your smelly biker ass all the way to Kingdom Come."

Bulldozers music hits and he makes his way down to the ring. Oak hides behind Wanda and keeps his eyes on Bulldozer.

BBS: "I thought you'd never ask. But I think I'd rather have a match with the little chickensh*t hiding behind you."

Fish: "I'm not going to let you hurt Oakie. The match will be against me."

Oak: "Yea, you tell him."

BBS: "Fine, I'll kill him on my own time then. And you want a match with the Bulldozer? You got it babe."

Bulldozer goes to leave the ring, but then turns around and nails Oak with a kick to the head. He bails out of the ring and walks up the ramp laughing all the way.

(D)

-The Hotshots are in their locker room talking. A bird cage with a white-ish sheet over it can clearly be seen in the background.

Wayne: "I can't believe this is actually working."

Vessey: "I told you it would work and it did."

Wayne: "Yea, you did. And I haven't seen any pigs sprouting wings either."

Vessey: "Oh come on, have I ever steered you wro..."

Wayne: "Don't even finish that sentence, I'd rather not be here all night."

Vessey: "Alright, fine. Now that we have the bird and the rematch how are we going to win it?"

Wayne: "I hadn't really put any thought into that part."

Vessey: "I thought that was your end of the deal. I mean, I stole the bird and he almost pecked my eye out. I thought you could at least do this on your own."

Wayne: "We'll come up with something."

Vessey: "Nail them in the heads with a birdcage."

Wayne: "Works for me."

Vessey: "Me too. Now let's get the flying rat and get out of here."

(D-)

vs.

-Steve Flash vs. Art Reed-

A great match from the midcard, which has unfortunately been a little neglected lately. Either way, these two crowd favorites had a great match together. With a shot at Hayes' National Championship in a weeks time on the line both men pulled out all the stops to win.

The match began with a great technical exchange of holds. Flash gets Reed with an arm wringer, but Reed pulls him in and tries to finish the match quickly with a Dark Matter. Flash fends him off and takes him down with an STO. The match goes back and forth from here with neither competitor gaining a clear cut advantage over the other. Flash gets Reed in another arm wringer and Reed tries once more to get Flash with the Dark Matter. Flash fends him off again and tries for a Flash Bang. Reed knees Flash in the head and hits him with a Dark Matter. Reed scrambles and is able to catch Flash in a Dread Lock in the middle of the ring. Flash struggles, but can't make it to the ropes. He taps out.

Winner: Art Reed in 14:58

Rating (C+)

-Maxmillion Productions are laying around their locker room. Rudy's napping, Rex is losing to himself at tic-tac-toe and Zeus is counting his money, all $25 of it. The peace and quiet doesn't last long as Triple D busts into the locker room. He goes right after Zeus and slams him against a wall.

DDD: "Listen and listen well because I'm only going to say this once and I expect an answer and for your sake it better be the answer I'm looking for. I want to know who put you up to it? Who had you tape me?"

Zeus: "Ahem, Customer Privacy Policy."

DDD: "That's not the answer I'm looking for."

Zeus: "Then that's too bad because that's the only answer you're getting."

DDD: "With or without your help I'm going to find out who did it."

Zeus: "You said that same line the last time and I've yet to see any progress on it."

Devine slams him against the wall again while Velasquez and Rex ready their lamps.

DDD: "I really don't care about you or your privacy policy, I want answers and I want them now."

Zeus: "If you don't leave now Mr. Devine, I will resort to physical violence and 3 on 1 just doesn't seem like a fair fight now does it?"

DDD: "I will find the son of a bitch who's doing this."

Zeus: "Sure, just like last time."

(C)

-We see Sam Strong in his office conversing with Freddie Datsun about something or other, old people stuff probably. A knock comes at the door. "Come in brother." In walk Danny Jillefski and Big Cat Brandon.

Jillefski: "You've made your decision I'm sure."

Strong: "Yes I have brother."

Jillefski: "And..."

Strong: "My doctors have told me that with my knee injury I shouldn't be anywhere near a ring, but I think they're all quacks anyhow brother. I'll accept your challenge and put another youngin' in their place."

Jillefski: "Good, then the Big Cat will see you at Summer of Love."

Datsun: "Have you lost it Sam? You can't possibly compete with someone like Brandon. Age has had it's effects on you Sam, you're not the same wrestler you were 20 years ago."

Strong: "You think I don't know that? But when someone threatens me I tend to do something about it."

Datsun: "Jillefski, You're going to have to go through me to get to Sam."

Jillefski: "Why does everyone have to try to make things so difficult? Brandon, take care of this."

Brandon attacks Datsun, but Datsun fights back. Security moves in and breaks the fight up, but in the mess Jillefski lands a sucker punch on Strong. Strong goes after him, but is too restrained by security.

(C+)

w/ vs.

-USPW World Championship Match- Bruce the Giant w/ Stranton vs. Triple D (c)-

This was a pretty one-sided match and not for the side you might expect. Bruce took Devine down with massive shots and power moves, but only the few times he was actually able to catch him. Devine wrestled circles around the Giant and wore him out. He then finished him off with a Devine Dream Drop. 1....2.....3.

Winner: Triple D in 5:39

Rating (B)

-We're back from commercial break and Jacob Stranton is in the back with Umaga and Bruce the Giant. Stranton scolds Bruce.

Stranton: "What in the hell was that? I wanted you to take him out and you got beat. And in 6 minutes no less. Bruce you have been a failure since the day I accepted you into this group. How can a 7' tall man struggle to beat a little pipsqueak like that? You've been slacking and dilly-dallying around too much lately and I'm sick of it."

BTG: "Bwuht Bouws, rhi rhidnt..."

Umaga: "Rarrgggh!!!"

Stranton: "I don't care what your excuse is. You've been a failure to me and that is that. Umaga, you know what to do with him."

Bruce tries to run, but we all know hippos aren't that fast. Umaga catches up to him and beats him down savagely.

(C)

-The Dudez make their way down to the ring and grab mics.

Perez: "After Last Wednesday we can mark down one more successful title defense on the Dudez win/ loss sheet. We beat our former friend Trent Shaffer and his partner fair and square. We've taken on all comers and we sent them all packing. Now we're here to celebrate. So let me just get this out of the way, party at the hotel, room 219. Everyone's invited, and thank you to..."

Perez is cut off by an elbow to the back of the head by Human Arsenal. Hernandez turns to check on his partner and gets the Ninja Strike from Ota. Seduction gets in the ring and grabs Perez's mic.

Seduction: "I've been thinking and I've decided I'd like to add some gold to my team. Dudes, I'd like you to meet your challengers for the PPV, Team Seduction. And Perez, I'd like you to meet your opponent tonight, Human Arsenal."

(C, C)

vs. w/

-Frankie Perez vs. Human Arsenal w/ Seduction-

Hello new best match of all my time. These two had chemistry and damn was it great.

Arsenal controlled the match early on, and through most of it to be honest. Perez was feeling the effects of the cheap shot early in the match and Arsenal made no bones about taking advantage of it. He tossed Perez around the ring easily and wore him out with his arsenal of submission techniques. Perez tried to fight back, but was unable to get past one or two strikes without being cut back off by Arsenal. Arsenal stayed on the attack and Perez continued to take abuse.

Mainstream Hernandez hops onto the apron in an attempt to help his partner. Ota pulled him off and the two brawled around ringside. The ref was distracted by it and so was Arsenal. Perez caught him with a small package. The ref sees it and starts the count, 1.....2.... Arsenal kicks out. Arsenal goes after Perez, but Perez trips him up and starts laying in fists to the forehead. Arsenal shoves him off, but Perez catches him with a shining wizard. Perez makes another cover, but Arsenal again kicks out.

Perez takes Arsenal up in the Perfect Parity. He nails it. Perez makes the cover,

1

.

.

.

2

.

.

Arsenal kicks out. Perez is stunned. Seduction climbs onto the apron and distracts the ref. Ota hops into the ring and nails Perez with the Ninja Strike. Perez and Arsenal are now both down, but Arsenal gets to his feet first. Arsenal picks Perez up and nails him with a Ammo Dump. He makes the pinfall, 1......2.......3.

Winner: Human Arsenal in 11:55

Rating (B+)

-A knock comes at the door of Muffins locker room. "I already told you to leave me alone." The door opens and that person lets themselves in.

Muffin: "I told you to leave me alone."

Seduction: "I think you'd do well to listen to my offer."

(C)

Final Rating (B)

-How many Human Arsenal matches have I had to declare as the new best match of all my time now?

-And just a little FYI, Hayes was used so little on this show because he wasn't there. He was working PGHW that night.
