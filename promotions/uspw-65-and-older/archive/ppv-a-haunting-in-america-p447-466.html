Main Show

-From the cold open for a Haunting in America we go right into another video package. This one specifically focused on tonights main event, the USPW World Championship match between champion Triple D and the challenger, coming out of retirement for this night, Sam Strong.

The video opened with the final moment of Blaze of Glory. Devine held the mic to speak the words "We are here together for one reason; to bring USPW to its knees. No one can beat us and nothing can stop us. We will take over USPW and it all begins tonight. We are the Divinity of USPW." From there the video brings us to the 'Cage match' between Muffin and DDD that ended up leaving Muffin badly hurt and no one being able to get to Devine. From there the video leads us to Strong challenging Devine for the title and the main event being set. The next part of the package is the night Devine revealed his real reasons for cheating on Muffin and Hayes' firing. From here the package goes on like a music video set to some song I don't know by some band I've never heard of. Pretty cool though.

(A)

-The video ends and we go to the announce table where Duane Fry, Shane Sneer and Giant Oak welcome everyone to the show and thank the fans who are watching at home for ordering. Fry goes on to drop his "Tonight is sure to be a barn stormer." line before the announce team are about to run down tonights card. As Fry starts he's interrupted from the other end of his headset. "Folks, I'm sorry for the interruption, but I'm being told something's going on backstage. We're going to go back there live as soon as the camera man arrives at the scene." Fry informs the viewers.

As soon as he finishes that sentence the feed from the camera appears on the screen. The camera man begins his journey down a hallway that looks like it hasn't been used by the owners of the arena in over a decade. The walls are grimy and there are paint chips falling off as far as the eye can see. The lights are blacked out partially from the build up of dust on them and some are flickering on and off. Spiderwebs are in every nook and cranny. I'd also venture to say that if we were to ever see one of these spiders that they would be bigger than spiderman, and I'm not talking about the action figure neither. The cameraman continues forward to find a trail of blood leading down the corridor. He follows the trail to an unconscious security guard. There's more blood trailing off which leads to another of the arenas security personnel, again unconscious and bleeding.

The cameraman pointed his camera down the hall to reveal the end of the corridor. That wasn't the only thing that was there though. A stockpile of security guards, all unconscious, were swimming in a pool of their collective blood. Down the hall was one open door with the police tape that had been keeping anybody in their right mind away from this place ripped away. The cameraman cautiously approached the door, but almost jumped out of his skin as someone had grabbed a hold of his ankle. A barely conscious security guard uttered the words "Help me." before slipping away from his consciousness. The cameraman trembled as he got closer and closer to the doorway.

What sounded like a hyena cackling after a kill could clearly be heard coming from the room. The camera peeked through the doorway to reveal Eddie Peak sitting in a pool of blood rocking back and forth. Peak was mumbling incoherently to himself. In his left hand he held an 8 x 10 of someone, but the cameraman wouldn't be stupid enough to get anywhere near the Hardcore Assassin. Peak picked up a pen in his right hand and feverishly stabbed away at the photo. He stopped and stood up. He then began eerily chanting "You're gonna die." as he walked out of the dark room and past the cameraman without even a slight glimpse his way. As Peak walked down the flickering hall calmly stepping over the bodies he had annihilated the cameraman zoomed in on the face in the pen hole filled picture in Peaks possession.

It was Freddie Datsun.

And to kick off the night after all of that excitement for the video and for Peak we get..... Peter Valentine. I don't think I need to explain that one.

The match kicked off with Casey Valentine and Trent Shaffer squaring off. The two men locked up a few times with neither being able to gain an advantage over the other. Shaffer got frustrated and tagged out to Chance Fortune who decided to forgo the feeling out process and charge in head first. Unfortunately Caseys drop toe hold sent him head first into the canvas. Casey took control in this match from here. It wasn't long though before a blind tag from Shaffer would turn the tides. Shaffer attacked Casey from behind as he was taunting Fortune to get up.

From here it was TBTTLE in charge. Quick tags and cutting the ring in half made sure to keep Casey hurt, TBTTLE fresh and Peter doing what he does best, absolutely nothing. Casey fought long and hard, 3 minutes worth actually before he tossed Fortune out of his way and made the tag to Peter. Valentine came in like a house of fire and immediately stunk up the match, I mean knocked Fortune and Shaffer down to the roars of the crowd. Peter held them off long enough for Casey to recover. Peter tagged Casey in and he came off the top rope with a crossbody onto Fortune. He then went for the legal man, Shaffer. Shaffer fought hard, but in the end Casey caught him with a Heart Breaker and it was 1...2....3.

Winner: The Valentines in 5:44

-USPW Unappreciated Championship Street Fight- Brett Hall vs. Bulldozer Brandon Smith (c)-

In a street fight there are no rules. The only thing to remember is that you have to score a win over your adversary inside the squared circle. I hope these two remember that part.

Smith is finally arriving to the arena moments before his match is about to start. He gets off his bike and gets an earful from some office loser talking about getting here on time as Dozer walks with a purpose towards the ring. He has his chain is his hand and his Championship over his shoulder. He's also about to have splinters in his hair. Out of nowhere Brett Hall comes into the scene and breaks a broomstick in half over Dozers head. It looks like this match started early. Hall picked Dozer up and dragged him towards the ring, but Dozer wasn't going to be led anywhere. He fought back with shots to the gut of Hall.

Hall fights back with forearms to the back of the head of Dozer. Dozer tackles Hall into the wall and starts unloading onto him. Dozer tosses Hall from against the wall and lets him get to his feet before nailing him right between the eyes with a belt shot. Dozer was now in control of Hall and dragging him down the corridors of this arena. Dozer wrapped his chain around Halls neck and led him along with that on their way out to the stage. Hall wasn't going to let Dozers job be easy though. He rammed into Dozer as he sent them both backwards into the wall.

Hall did that repeatedly until Dozer dropped the chain around his neck and let Hall go. Hall got away from Dozer to catch his breath while Dozer wrapped the chain around his fist and charged at Hall. Hall rolled out of the way of the shot and Dozer shot his hand right through a pane of glass. Dozer dropped the chain from his hand and checked on it trying to pull out the little shards of glass lodged into his skin. As Dozer turned around he got a big boot from Hall that sent him through the hole in the wall where the now broken glass used to reside.

Hall, using his brain, decided to use the door to get into that particular room. Hall looked to be lining Dozer up for one final big boot. As Dozer stood up Hall charged in with his foot out. Dozer moved out of the way and got his foot stuck through the wall. Dozer didn't have any problem taking advantage of that as he drove his shoulder into the spine of Hall and tackled the both of them straight though the wall. Dozer went back and got his chain and once again led Hall along by the throat with it. This time Dozer was able to get Hall out to the stage. He then decided to let Hall get on a roll. A roll down the entrance ramp that is. Dozer tossed Hall down the ramp and let him roll to the bottom walking behind him.

Dozer approaches Hall and tries to pick him up, but Hall isn't done yet. He rams Dozer against the edge of the ring apron and starts raining down rights and lefts into the dome of Dozer. Hall then tosses Dozer into the steel steps. He looks under the ring for something and finds a steel chair. Hall tries to smash Dozers head between the steel post and the chair, but Dozer rolls out of the way. He wraps his chain around his fist and smacks the chair out of Halls hands. That doesn't discourage Hall though as he does some quick thinking and gets in a few knees to the gut before ramming Dozer into the post again.

Hall gets set to smash Dozer against the steel, but as Hall charges in Dozer moves out of the way. Hall charges shoulder first into the post and it looks like he's badly hurt. Dozer puts his old football skills to good use and rams Hall between him and the steel post with a tackle. Dozer grabs his chain and gets into the ring. He approaches the corner Hall is leaning against the post on and wraps the chain around his neck. Dozer pulls the chain tight and chokes Hall until he passes out. Dozer lets go of the chain and tosses Hall into the ring. He picks Hall up and as an exclamation mark he drops him with the Backdrop Driver. 1....2....3.

Winner: BBS in 11:38

USPW A Haunting in America

Saturday, Week 2, October 2007

Held at The Louisiana Auditorium

Att. 8,102

-Our non-existent interviewer (I'm working on it) is backstage with Jacob Stranton and the Wild Savages, Umaga & Akima.

IV: "Mr. Stranton, at the close of Thursday nights episode of Family Night you had Umaga and Akima destroy Bruce the Giant and then Sam Strong. But after that you sent them after USPW Champion Triple D. What was your reason for doing that?"

Stranton: "What did you call Devine?"

IV: "USPW Champion Triple D?"

Stranton: "And what are the first two words in that sentence?"

IV: "USPW Champion?"

Stranton: "Exactly. I've said it before and I'll say it again, Umaga and Akima are unstoppable savages and they will lead me to the top of USPW. What I did I did to send a message to everyone in the locker room, we're not on anyones side but our own. When all the dust has settled on tonights main event after Devine turns that fossil into dust, I'm making the challenge right now, at the next PPV Akima is going to bring the USPW Championship back to me."

IV: "And what about tonight?"

Stranton: "How many times have my Savages destroyed Bruce? Can't count it with your fingers and toes anymore? That's what I thought."

IV: "And what of the involvement of Giant Tana, Umaga and Akimas brother?"

Stranton: "Ah, Tana. Tana can talk and eat just fine, but as far as wrestling goes he's weak. He lacks the killer instinct that his brothers do. That's why he'll never be worth anything in this industry while on the other hand the Savages will go down as two of the greatest and most destructive of all time. Tonights match is going to be a breeze."

(D+)

vs. w/

-Bruce the Giant & Giant Tana vs. The Wild Savages w/ Stranton-

False bravado is the phrase I'm looking for. Bruce was full of it. After learning that his tag team partner and friend Giant Tana is, in fact, Samoan (I'm not gonna bother asking the obvious) Bruces confidence in winning this match or surviving even have gone down.

To start the match off we had Bruce, trying his hardest to keep it together, face off against Umaga. The bell rang to start off this contest, but as soon as it did Bruce tagged Tana in and bailed to the outside. He pulled out a paper bag and started breathing in and out of it to help out the anxiety attack he was having. Tana was concerned about Bruce on the outside, but that wasn't a very wise decision as Umaga wasn't concerned about hitting a man with his back turned.

From here the match was on, but unfortunately for the Fun Lovin', blonde haired Samoan it was more like a handicap match as Bruce was now afraid to even tag in. No matter how many times Tana tried. And he tried plenty. Tana just took the beating from his brothers and continued to try to fight back. The Savages were nearly done with Tana as Akima ascended to the top rope for the Savage Headbutt. It was at that moment that Bruce nearly fell off the apron and pulled at the rope to save himself. Fortunately for him he had inadvertently just saved his partner and this match from a sure end crotching Akima on the top turnbuckle. Tana crawled to his corner and made the tag to an inattentive Bruce who just realized he was still in this match and now the legal man.

Akima made the tag to Umaga and it was back to Bruce vs. Umaga. Bruce took out his paper bag, breathed into it a few times then threw it away and put on his brave face. Umaga and Bruce went straight after each other. To accurately describe this battle you would have to have seen what a Whale vs a Mammoth would look like. Bruce went after Umaga and dished it out as good as he was getting it. Tana rallied Bruce on from his corner and Bruce was able to gain the upper hand in the battle. Bruce was on fire now and Umaga couldn't take the Giant down. In the end Bruce was able to hoist the Samoan Savage into the air and slam him down onto the mat with a thunderous Chokeslam. 1......2......3, Bruce has finally done it.

Winners: BTG & Giant Tana in 6:17

Rating (B-)

vs.

-The Dudez!!!1!!! vs. The Hotshots-

Damn. These two teams really pulled it off. One more great match for the night that shouldn't really surprise anyone seeing as I can see future worlds champions in all 4 competitors.

The Dudez!!!1!!! are quite possibly one of the best tag teams in North America right now, but that wouldn't discourage The Hotshots as they knew that winning was the only option tonight. The match started with Hernandez and Vessey squaring off. Hernandez beat out Vessey and took control of this match. Vessey struggled to fight back as Hernandez is the better wrestler of the two, but Vessey resorted to cheating very quickly. A poke to the eyes and a swift kick back into the privates gave Vessey enough time to get in a tag to Wayne who didn't have any problem with the 'whatever it takes to win' methods of his partner.

Wayne and Vessey cut the ring in half and worked Hernandez over. Perez pulled for his partner and got the entire crowd behind him to cheer on Hernandez. Mainstream rallied back and took down Vessey. He lunged for his corner and tagged Perez in. Perez entered the ring and took Vessey down with a charging forearm. Wayne entered the ring now and went after Perez only to get a back elbow to the jaw. Vessey got up for another try but was taken down by a clothesline. Perez kept the Hotshots off their feet for a while. Mainstream set himself up on the top rope and hit the Apparition #14 on Wayne taking him out of the mix and allowing Perez to drop Vessey with a Perfect Parity. 1.....2....3.

Winners: The Dudez!!!1!!! in 16:40

Rating (B)

-Our interviewer is backstage with Alicia 'Muffin' Strong to talk about her upcoming match with Big Cat Brandon.

IV: "How are you feeling tonight Alicia?"

Muffin: "Well enough."

IV: "Are you at all worried about tonights match and that you could possibly be seriously injured by your opponent?"

Muffin: "Every match comes with the risk of an injury, but I know that I can't lose tonight. As hurt as I may get tonight and as painful as it may be for me to get out of bed tomorrow morning I know that I can't lose. My father is going to go out there tonight and put his health, his career, his pride, everything on the line. I know that tonight me losing is not an option."

(B-)

-The Hotshots are walking backstage, Wayne clutching his gut from the Apparition #14 and Vessey grasping at his neck from the Perfect Parity.

Vessey: "You think he's going to be pissed at us?"

It doesn't take long to get an answer as DDD impedes the Hotshots path and he doesn't look to be happy with them.

Wayne: "Wild guess here, but I'm going to say yes."

DDD: "You're damn right I'm pissed at you."

Vessey: "We didn't do anything though."

DDD: "You're not helping your own case much here."

Wayne: "We tried our hardest, but..."

DDD: "But you're just not good enough."

Wayne: "I was going to say that we must've just had a bad night tonight or something."

DDD: "It must be a bad night for you guys every night because I can't remember the last time either of you won a match."

Wayne: "I beat Flash."

DDD: "You beat a 40 year old man after help from Reed."

Vessey: "Remember that 6-man..."

DDD: "That was my win, not yours."

Wayne: "Well uhh, uh...."

DDD: "Exactly. I took you two under my wings and taught you. This is what I get for my investment? An unending stream of disappointment?"

Vessey: "Companionship maybe?"

DDD: "I said it Thursday so let me reiterate, Win and you're in, lose and I kick your asses myself."

Wayne: "We said we were sorry, we were just having an off night."

DDD: "Well then you better hope Monday isn't an off night for you. For either of you."

(C+)

vs.

-Muffin vs. Big Cat Brandon-

And these two bring the goods in another great match tonight. And to think, aHiA was never meant to be much more than a filler PPV.

Muffin was hurt from the beginning of this match and Brandon knew it. Like a shark smelling blood in the water he attacked as soon as the bell sounded. And from the get-go it looked like Muffin didn't have a chance in hell of winning. Brandon was all over Muffin not giving her a chance to so much as breathe. He immediately targeted the left leg of Muffin, the one he had nearly broken just a few days ago and stayed on it relentlessly. Muffin hung in there, but didn't look as though she would be able to fight back. Brandon decided he'd had enough fun inflicting pain on Muffin and tossed her off the ropes in preparation for the Big Cat Pounce. Brandon ran off the opposite side of the ropes and prepared to make contact, but Muffin had held onto the ropes and stopped herself. As soon as Brandon reached the opposite side of the ring Muffin charged in and took him and herself over the top rope with a running clothesline.

The two competitors lay on the outside of the ring as the ref starts to count the both of them out. It was Brandon who got to his feet first. As Muffin continued to struggle Brandon rolled into the ring to break up the count and then rolled back out and picked Muffin up. He tossed her knee first into the steel steps and then rolled her into the ring. Muffin grabbed at her left knee in agony as Brandon readied himself to finish her off this time. The ref intervened however concerned that she may be seriously injured. Muffin waved him off determined to continue. All the while as Muffin tried to pull herself up by the ropes Brandon laid in wait. As soon as she got to a vertical stance Brandon charged in as subtle as a train and rammed into something shoulder first.

Muffin had leapt out of the way just in time. She had hung onto the rope and pulled herself back to her feet. She then hobbled over to the corner and laid some forearms into the back of the Big Cat. Brandon didn't take it for long as he got up and grabbed Muffin with both hands by the throat. He tossed her in and frantically punched away. He then rammed her gut with shoulder thrusts courtesy of his good shoulder. Brandon then hauled Muffin to the top turnbuckle and followed. He set her up for the superplex, but Muffin fought back and punched away at the abs of Brandon. She shoved him off the top and down onto the mat. Muffin pulled herself up to a perch on the top and readied herself, but Brandon wasn't one to stay down. He leapt right back onto the top rope and caught Muffin. He set her up for another superplex, but Muffin fought back again. This time though she didn't push Brandon off. Instead she took them both off the top with an Angel Driver. Muffin crawled over and made the pin on Brandon,

-Freddie Datsun is backstage being interviewed about his upcoming match with Jillefskis mystery opponent who we now know to be former DAVE Unified Champion Eddie Peak.

IV: "Your opponent tonight was hand picked by Danny Jillefski to take you out and we all now know that that opponent is Eddie Peak. What's going through your mind going into this match?"

Datsun: "Well, I'm no stranger dangerous and violent individuals. For those who don't remember I'm a 3-time DAVE Extreme Champion. I've faced plenty of people who are a lot tougher and a lot more violent than Peak and I came out on top back then and I plan on doing the same thing tonight."

Before Datsun can go on that same cackling that was heard earlier in the night can be heard again. Datsun looks down the hall for Peak as the sound gets closer. Freddie may have been looking in the wrong direction though. Out of nowhere one of the reporters from earlier is used to create a new doorway as he's tossed straight through the wall on the right side of the screen. Datsun and our interviewer stare at the limp body of the reporter covered in blood and now drywall. The other two reporters don't take long to follow. One is lawn darted into another as both go through another sheet of drywall. With all 3 reporters bloody and near dead the man responsible makes his way through one of his newly made doors and stares at Datsun.

(B-)

vs.

-Freddie Datsun vs. Eddie Peak-

OH. MY. F*CKING. GOD.

That's all that really needs to be said about my and USPWs best match EEEEEVAR~! I believe it's also looking at best match of the year right now. Take that Canada and Japan!

Still in the back Peak stares at Datsun with his eyes wide open before lunging after Datsun. A brawl breaks out between the two of them, but security doesn't take long to swarm the scene. (What's left of it anyways.) They separate Peak and Datsun and accompany them to the ring so this match can actually begin. As soon as the two competitors are in the ring the ref rings the bell to start off the match.

Datsun prepares to lock up with Peak, but Peak instead delivers a flurry of punches into Datsun and then takes him down with a discus clothesline. As soon as Datsun goes down Peak is on him with a flurry of mounted punches. The ref tries to pull Peak off as Datsun begins to bleed the first minute into this match. Peak stops his flurry of punches and chokes Datsun instead. Peak stops at the 4 count only to go right back to choking Datsun out. He stops again at 4 and goes right back to the well once more. This time though Datsun kicks him away and rolls to the outside to catch his breath. Peak attempts to go after him, but the ref impedes his path. Instead of listening to Jez however, Peak grabs him with both hands around the throat and tosses him away.

Peak gets out of the ring and goes after Datsun. Datsun's prepared this time however and nails Peak with a three point stance. Peak goes down and now it's Datsuns turn to rain down fists into the head of his downed opponent. Datsun picked Peak up and dropped him chest first across the guard rail. Datsun stomped away at Peak before pulling him up again. Peak didn't come unprepared for this match though. He reached into his tights and pulled out something. He put the brass knux onto his right hand and drove them into Datsuns gut multiple times. As soon as Datsun went down to one knee Peak nailed a well-placed shot into Datsuns temple. Peak shoved the knux back into his tights and picked Datsun up, gorilla press style and dropped him face first onto the steel steps. He then picked Datsun up again and shoved him shoulder first into the steel post.

Peak wasn't quite done with Datsun on the outside yet though. Peak went over to the Spanish announce table and looked it over before dragging Datsun over. Peak set Datsun up for a Peak of Perfection (Crucifix Powerbomb) through the table, but Datsun was able to wiggle free and get behind Peak. As soon as Peak turned around Datsun quickly scooped him up onto his shoulders and dropped him through the table with a Patriot Press. Both men were now down, the ref was out and the Spanish announce team was out one table. And to think that was only the first 10 minutes. Datsun was the first to stir back to life, but Peak wasn't far behind. Datsun crawled to the ring apron to pull himself back up while Peak tossed the remains of the Spanish announce table away from himself.

As Datsun got to his feet he went over to what remained of Eddie Peak and dragged him to his feet by the hair. Datsun attempted to toss Peak back into the ring, but Peak had other plans. He sent a few forearms into the kidneys of Datsun to loosen his grip and then picked him up setting him up for what looked like a backdrop. Peak instead ran forward and slammed Datsun into the steel post. Peak kept Datsun in that position and delivered the rarely used Peak of the Devil (Brutal Backdrop Driver) to Datsun onto what little protection the mat on the outside offered. Peak rtied to pin Datsun on the outside, but this match wasn't under DAVE rules. It isn't even supposed to be a hardcore match either, but when ya only got one referee....

Peak picked up the prone body of the American Everyman and tossed him back into the ring. Peak then made the cover again. Too bad he had knocked the ref out about 10 minutes ago. Peak went over to Jez, who was just starting to stir again. He picked him up by the back of the neck and the belt and slammed him down onto the canvas. Peak made the cover again, but Jez was more out of it than he was a minute ago thanks to Peak (again). Peak knew he couldn't win the match, but he could have fun waiting till he could. From there on Peak went to work on Datsun and just brutally beat him down for the Hardcore Assassins demented pleasure. Peak saw that Jez was finally starting to stir again and knew it was time to finish this thing.

Peak hoisted Datsun up for the Peak of Perfection, but Datsun miraculously was able to get free. More so than that he immediately went for a roll up on Peak, but he wasn't even able to get to a 1 count as Jez wouldn't budge. Peak, mad with frustration, charged at Datsun who quickly snatched him up and threw Peak to the outside with a Patriot Press. Peak lay on the outside of the ring while Datsun gets some time to catch his breath on the inside. Peak didn't stay down long, but it was enough time for the former DAVE Extreme Champion to recover. Peak and Datsun stood off in the middle of the ring before the two went after each other. Peak charged in and Datsun caught him to set him up for a Patriot Press. Peak was prepared for that this time and slid out back sending Datsun crashing down with a reverse DDT.

The battle between the two of them raged on for several minutes as both refused to lose this match. Peak went for the Peak of the Devil once more, but Datsun sent rights into Peaks head causing him to set the American Everyman down. Datsun sprang off the ropes and came back with a boot to the side of Peaks head. It was only enough to take Peak to his knees though. Datsun came off the ropes again, this time with a knee lift. Datsun pulled Peak in and planted him with a Patriot Press. Datsun made the cover as Jez crawled over to make the count,

-USPW World Championship Match- Sam Strong vs. Triple D (c)-

Well, the "Every match I think could be good, but then I find they have bad chemistry dammit" curse haunted me here. Plus Strong was tired by the end (I knew I shouldn't have put on that extra minute) and the amount of selling in this match was equivalent to the amount of communication that goes on between a blind man, a deaf man, and a mute man. Aside from those gripes, the crowd was absolutely on fire for this match. It's not everyday you get to see a legend come out of retirement.

Both Devine and Strong had someone accompany them to ringside. In Devines corner was Seduction and in Strongs corner was of course Muffin. The match hadn't even started yet when the ref threw both of them out from the ringside area and to the back. Devine naturally protested, but it didn't do much to help his case. The ref rang the bell and the match everyone had been waiting for all night was finally underway.

The crowd were in anticipation of the first move of this match and Strong was the one to make it. He challenged Devine to a test of strength. Strong held his hand in the air and goaded Devine on until he accepted the challenge. The two locked up seemingly evenly matched for a moment until Strong started to pull into the lead, and then came the knee the the gut. It wasn't Devine who delivered it though, he was the receiver on that exchange. Strong took advantage immediately knowing that if he doesn't now he may never get the chance. He shot some rights into the head of Devine before picking him up by the head. Strong raises his fist in the air and waits for the crowd to chant his name before delivering a Strong punch to the Divine One.

Devine goes down from the punch and Strong waits for him to get back to his feet before running in with a Strong Arm Tactic (Running Elbow). Devine goes down again and Strong makes the cover,

-And Devine retains his title almost as cheaply as the last time. Devine snatched his belt from the timekeeper and went around the ring showboating and gloating, holding his title in the air. In mid celebration though Devine completely stopped in his tracks. He began to stare at something near the entranceway, but nobody was sure what. As the camera closed in on his face Devine looked as though he'd just seen a ghost. One of the cameras turned to the entranceway to try to spot whatever it is and as the camera pans up we find out.

The camera zooms in on the top of the tron as a spotlight shines down. Standing atop the rafters holding the tron up is a man in a black full body suit, arms crossed, staring down at Devine. Devine just stares back as neither takes their eye off the other.
