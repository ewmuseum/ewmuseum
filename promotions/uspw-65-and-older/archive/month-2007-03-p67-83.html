USPW All American Wrestling

Saturday, Week 3, March 2007 (Airs Wednesday)

Held at Pennsylvania Park

Att. 2,000 (Sell-out)

Dark Matches

-Trent Shaffer defeated Giant Oak in 5:49 by pinfall with a Heart Burn. (E-) (So, who thinks Oak is championship material?)

Main Show

-Steve Flash vs. Citizen X for the USPW National Championship-

These 2 had pretty good chemistry which helped the match. I'm not sure I'd have wanted to see the match without that chemistry. Let's see here, Flash has already brought out the best in Giant Oak (Giant Freakin' Oak), Fumihiro Ota and now Citizen X while I've lost pretty much all faith in X. It's easy to see who's more important to this companies future and his name doesn't have X in it. That's pretty sad actually. Flash put X away relatively easily with a Flash Bang.

Winner and NEW USPW National Champion: Steve Flash in 5:44

Rating (D+)

-Flash celebrates the win with his new Championship belt. He's attacked from behind by Fumihiro Ota who proceeds to beat him down and motion that he wants that belt. How did Ota get behind Flash you may be wondering? His special ninja stealth techniques, duh.

(D)

-The Dudez!!!1!!! vs. Maxmillion Productions vs. The Canadian Crusties (c) for the USPW World Tag Team titles-

Unfortunately for the Crusties I need champions who actually show up for work every once in a while. A pretty hard fought match between these 3 teams. In the end it was Hernandez over a McWade with an Apparition #14.

Winner and NEW USPW World Tag Team Champions: The Dudez!!!1!!! in 8:06

Ratings (D-)

-Backstage Rudy Velasquez and T-Rex seem to be angry over their loss.

Velasquez: "I'm angry."

Rex: "Yea, me too."

Zeus: "Calm down, you 2 may have lost, but you shall live to fight another day."

Velasquez: "Yea, but we lost today, and I'm pissed today."

Rex: "Yea, me too."

Zeus: "Then find something to take your anger out on."

Velasquez: "Where's your computer at? I never did like that thing."

Zeus: "You're not smashing anything of mine. Why don't you go find somebodies skull to smash in?"

Velasquez: "You heard him Rex, let's go."

Rex: "Yea, me too."

(D)

-We see footage from last week of Devine telling Peter Valentine that he should see a doctor. We go to a pre-taped video now. We open with a shot of a medical institute. In the waiting room is Peter Valentine who's reading People Magazine. Upside down. Valentine waits around until his name is finally called. The People Magazine falls down to reveal a USPW Magazine. Which is also upside down. Valentine enters the doctors office and we cut to a shot of him with the doctor.

Valentine: "Tell it to me straight doc, am I going to live?"

Doctor: "Yes Mr. Valentine, you'll be alright. According to this you're a bipolar, schizophrenic, narcissist with delirium and mental retardation."

Valentine: "Really?"

Valentine: "I'm going to be a mommy? Thank you doc, you just made my day. Yay, I'm going to have a baby."

Valentine is literally skipping and jumping for joy on his way out of the office.

Doctor: "Mr. Valentine, you didn't take you medication prescriptions with you." the doctor yells out.

(C-)

-We see Triple D backstage getting ready when, from out of nowhere a shuriken whizzes by and pierces the soda machine. DDD looks around wondering where it came from. From out of the shadows pops up Fumihiro Ota who attacks DDD, without the shurikens thankfully. Ota leaves DDD hurting.

(C-)

-Fumihiro Ota vs. Triple D-

A good match here as these 2 can actually wrestle. In USPW. Yea, I know how unlikely that seems. Anyways, onto the match.

Ota started the match off in firm control of DDD, who was still feeling the affects of Otas stealth maneuvers. Ota worked D over with a series of kicks to the head, chest, gut, and legs. Ota weakens DDD and then goes for a Ninja Strike. He's cut off by a clothesline from Devine out of nowhere that nearly flipped Ota out of his mask. Both men go down and the ref starts the 10 count.

Both are back to their feet by a count of 7. Devine goes to work on Ota catching him with a series of punches. Devine is in control of the match now. He wears down Ota for a bit before going for a 3D. Ota shoves him off and hits him with a dropkick to the knees taking him off his feet. Ota springs off the ropes and attempts the Ninja Strike, but DDD rolls out of the way. Ota back up, and gets caught with a 3D. He goes down for the count, 1...2...3.

Winner: Triple D in 8:34

Rating (C-)

-Warlord Pain is backstage watching the monitors when he's jumped by T-Rex. Rex tosses Pain around and smashes a monitor over his head. Rex drags Pain over to the loading gates and presses him over his head. He throws Pain back first down what has to be at least 12 feet to the concrete in what looks like a career ending injury. And you could barely tell it was a stunt double either . I had to get rid of him somehow.

(D-)

-Datsun has an interview hyping up his match with Umaga.

Datsun: "I'll admit that last week I underestimated you Umaga. You're the real deal after all. You may be one of the toughest wrestlers I've ever looked across a ring at. I took you for a chump and would have paid for it if your tamer hadn't lost his temper. This week I'm ready for you, I'm ready to wrestle you again, and I'm ready to possibly take years off my life expectancy to step inside the steel cage with you. 2 men will step into the cage, both will leave bloody and beaten, but only one will leave victorious. That man will be me."

(C)

-USPW World Championship Cage Match- Umaga vs. Freddie Datsun (c)-

This is definitely the best match between 2 USPW wrestlers I've ever booked. These 2 went all out and left the crowd satisfied with their choice to waste $6 on it. I should raise those prices.

Umaga had the advantage over Datsun for the majority of this match, while Datsun was only able to catch Umaga with short, unsustainable bursts of offense. Umaga was relentless the entire time and Datsun was bloodied up by the 3 minute mark. By the 10 minute mark Umaga was in complete control of this match and it looked as though we may have a new champion crowned tonight much to the crowds disdain. Umaga sets Datsun up for a Savage Charge. He hits it, the steel post that is as Datsun was able to move out of the way. Datsun struggles, but is able to get Umaga onto his shoulders. He hits a Patriot Press. The ref makes the count.

1....,2...., kick out by Umaga. Datsun starts working Umaga over with various strikes. Datsun hits Umaga with a running knee lift and then pulls him down and hits a DDT. He goes for the pin, but only gets a 2 and barely at that. Datsun pulls Umaga up and keeps hold of his arm. He gets a short arm clothesline, but it doesn't seem to affect Umaga. He goes for it again and again, but still nothing. Umaga then goes for his own clothesline, but Datsun ducks it and drops Umaga with a neckbreaker. He covers, but barely gets 2. Datsun goes up top and delivers an elbow drop on Umaga. He covers again and only gets 2.

Datsun, clearly frustrated pulls Umaga up and tries for a Patriot Press, but Umaga slips out back. He picks up Datsun and slams him into the steel repeatedly. Umaga tosses Datsun into the corner and hits him with a Savage Charge. He goes for the pin. 1....2......3.

Bet nobody saw that coming.

Winner and New USPW World Champion: Umaga in 14:50

Rating (C+)

-Umaga celebrates his victory as he stands over his fallen foe with the title now draped over his shoulders. The crowd still can't believe what they just saw and are littering the ring with whatever they can throw. The show ends while this chaotic scene continues.

(C+)

USPW All American Wrestling

Saturday, Week 4, March 2007 (Airs Wednesday)

Held at The Arkansas Coliseum

Att. 2,000 (Sell-out)

Dark Matches

-Trent Shaffer defeated Demon Spite in 5:57 by pinfall with a Heart Burn. (D+)

-Bruce The Giant defeated Giant Oak in 4:42 by pinfall with a Giant Choke Slam. (D-) (Notes: There was absolutely no selling involved in this match. And very little talent as well.)

Main Show

-And we kick off the show with the former USPW World Champion making his way to the ring. Datsun is bandaged up and bruised. He grabs a microphone.

Datsun: "Last week was like a nightmare for me, but unfortunately I can't wake up from reality. Last week I took a vicious beating from a large, angry Samoan and in the process lost my World Championship and gained about 9 stitches. I lost fair and square to Umaga. I should probably hang up my boots and call it a career now before end up tarnishing my legacy, huh? I should probably just leave well enough alone and keep my nose out of the World Championship picture, right? I should keep my distance from Umaga before the stitch count increases. These are all things a rational man might do.

It's a good thing I'm not a rational man though isn't it. I will not rest until I get that World Championship belt back around my waist or at the very least get it away from Umaga and Stranton. I will fight to my last breath to get it back. I don't fear death and I damn sure don't fear a Samoan Savage. I want a rematch with Umaga and I'm invoking my rematch clause to make sure it happens tonight."

Umaga comes out onto the entrance ramp with the belt and yells things at Datsun in Samoan. Stranton runs out now and tries to coerce Umaga to the back. Stranton pulls out a mic and tells Datsun "You're on."

(C)

vs.

-The Canadian Crusties vs. Maxmillion Productions-

And what an absolutely craptacular way to start off the show. Remind me not to do any more heel/heel matches. As for what happened during the match, you'd be better off not knowing. Which is why I'll tell you. Velasquez and Rex dominated the match. Velasquez hits a bald guy with a street cutter and it's over.

Winners: Maxmillion Productions in 5:28

Rating (E+)

-Maxmillion Productions are backstage talking. They gloat about how T-Rex took out Warlord Pain last week ending his career. Maxmillion said that's the same kind of killer instinct that Maxmillion Productions needs to have every night. If Rex does that to every challenger to their tag team championships they would have the belts by now and rule USPW. A commotion is heard as Warlord Agony tries to rush them but is being stopped by security. Well, they're trying to stop him anyhow.

Agony gets to MP, but is being restrained by security. He demands a match with T-Rex, but Zeus wants to know what's in it for them. Agony says that he wants to repay Rex for what happened to Pain. "Sorry, I still don't see what's in it for me." says Zeus while Agony is still being restrained by security. This is where William Hayes steps into the picture. "I'm trying to concentrate over here, but if you're in such a hurry to be beaten then I have a proposition for you. If you can beat me tonight then you'll have your match with Rex next week." Agony begrudgingly accepts the challenge.

(D-, D+)

vs.

-William Hayes vs. Warlord Agony-

And good chemistry saves the day. This is easily Agonys best match since I got here. A fairly even match here. Agony hobbled his way through this match, and that was before Hayes started working the leg over. Hayes was able to hit Agony with a Slick Trick and gets the 3.

Winner: William Hayes in 5:48

Rating (C-)

Valentine: "Yup, I'm gonna be a mommy."

Seduction: "......."

Devine continues to shake his head in shame.

Valentine: "Ooo, do you wanna go baby shopping with me?"

Seduction: "....... I am so out of here."

From the shadows Fumihiro Ota attacks DDD. He lays fists into him and some kicks as well. He tosses DDD into a metal door before turning his attention to Valentine who's whimpering in a corner. Ota goes up to him, Valentine asks him "You wouldn't hit a pregnant man, would you?" Ota stares at him before walking away shaking his head.

(C- for the announcement, D+ for the attack)

vs.

-USPW National Championship Match- Fumihiro Ota vs. Steve Flash (c)-

Now this is the definition of disappointment. I was expecting another C- out of them, but the ARSEHOLES only rates it D. Damn.

On with the match. Flash started off the match in control of Ota, but Ota was able to make a comeback due to his stealthy ninja cheating ways. Ota kept control of the match for a while wearing Flash down. Ota was caught with a right from Flash as Flash began making his big comeback. He knocked Ota down repeatedly before hitting a Flash Bang. From there the match was already over.

Winner and still Champion: Steve Flash in 8:03

Rating (D)

-Cameras scurry backstage to see Warlord Agony beating the living crap out of T-Rex. Security swarms over this chaotic scene to try to pull Agony off Rex. Velasquez and Maxmillion only make this situation worse as they attack Agony while security is holding him. More security is needed to separate Velasquez and Maxmillion from Agony.

(D)

-Stranton is backstage with Umaga. Umaga is roaring and snorting and clenching his championship all the while Stranton is being interviewed about the upcoming match with Freddie Datsun.

Stranton: "Datsun, if you keep coming after Umaga and this belt 9 stitches will seem like chump change compared to how many you'll end up with. And I'm glad you're not afraid of death Freddie. Now I won't regret it when Umaga ends both your career and your life in the middle of that ring tonight. You've been given fair warning Freddie."

(C-)

-A video plays recapping the events of last week and the week before during the Datsun/Umaga matches.

(C)

vs. w/

-USPW World Championship Match- Freddie Datsun vs. Umaga (c) w/ Stranton-

This match was just a notch below the quality of last weeks match, but this one didn't involve a cage so I believe that balances it out.

Umaga dominated Datsun throughout this match like he has for the last 3 weeks. Datsun never let up on his 'never say die' attitude and kept trying to get the upper hand on Umaga, but to no avail. Umaga tosses Datsun into the corner and hits him with a Savage Charge. He makes the count. 1.....2.....

Rope break. Datsun was able to get his foot on the bottom rope. Umaga, clearly getting angrier sets Datsun up in the corner again and goes for another Savage Charge. This time Datsun moves out of the way. Umagas shoulder makes contact with the post hard and Datsun sees his opening. Datsun goes after the shoulder. He wears it down with punches and elbows to it and then various armbars. Datsun sets Umaga up for a Patriot Press. He gets him up onto the shoulders and then slams him down. Datsun makes the cover. 1...2. kick out by Umaga. Datsun is shocked and goes right back after the shoulder.

Umaga powers his way out of another armbar and gets back to dominating Datsun. Datsun strikes at the shoulder of Umaga to try to gain the upper hand, but Umaga seems unaffected. Datsun catches Umaga in a standing armbar, but Umaga picks Datsun up and slams him into the corner. He hits a Savage Charge. 1...2...3.

Winner: Umaga in 15:46

Rating (C)

Final Rating (C-)

Another show, another day gone by. And the more I smell like old man. But that's not what I'm here to talk about. Sam told me to meet him in his office at 2. Everybody should know by now that a meeting with Sam is never a fun time. I sat at my desk playing tic-tac-toe to myself until it said 2 o'clock on my watch. I breathed out a deep sigh and went to his office.

I walk in and I'm immediately greeted with one of his old timers stories. "Back when I was wrestling I was so popular I could read a dictionary in the middle of the ring and the crowd would still be on the edges of their seats brother."

"To get into rioting position?"

"No you idiot, to wait in anticipation till I got to the word platymapus."

"Isn't it platypus?"

"They didn't know that brother."

"Sam, is there a point to this?"

"Yeah brother."

"You gonna get to it anytime this week?"

"I really don't like your mouth at times like this brother."

"What are you gonna do about it? Fire me?"

"I already told you, I'll post your address on the website. You know how many 'fans' you have online."

"Hey, I looked that up. It would be a violation of my privacy."

"You find me the law, I'll have my lawyers find the loophole."

"Ya know, this is why people don't like you."

"Plenty of people like me brother."

"Yea, well I haven't met any of them."

"Watch some classic wrestling and you'll see millions love me."

"Sam, was there some reason that you wanted me in here, or did you just wanna ramble on about nothing?"

"I want you to meet my newest hire."

"Who?"

"My kid."

"Isn't PJ a little young to be wrestling? He's only 12 after all." (OOC Question: Shouldn't PJ be in the new workers file?)

"Not PJ, my daughter."

"You have a daughter?"

"Yes, I do brother." And I'm sure she looks like a gorilla.

"You do know that this company is male only."

"Then change it."

"And you do know women can't hold any titles."

"Then change it."

"Do you want me to change your diapers too or do you think you can handle that on your own?"

"Watch your mouth."

"Or wha...oh, right. Message boards."

Sam told me to wait for Alicia in my office, so I went back and looked up some privacy laws. About an hour later a beautiful blonde walked into my office, and this wasn't me daydreaming either.

"You lost miss?"

"Ms. Strong."

"Ms. Strong? Wait, you're Alicia?"

"Yea."

"But you're not a gorilla."

"I'd hope not."

"You're related to Sam Strong?"

"Yea, that's what my birth certificate says."

"You sure you weren't adopted?"

"I'm sure. Why?"

"Because you're actually hot."

"Umm, thank you? Shouldn't we get down to business now?"

"Oh, sure."

"Well?"

"Well what?"

"Aren't you going to do your job?"

"I would but I don't know what it is."

"You're the guy who got hired at the auction aren't you?"

"Is the fact that I'm not 65 or older give that away?"

"I'll ignore that last question. Daddy told me to come to you to discuss my direction with USPW."

"Oh, alright then. You're gonna wrestle."

"That's it?"

"I never said I was any good at this job."

"Can't we discuss my characters direction."

"Ok, you're gonna wrestle. Is that enough direction?"

"I'll ignore that too and get on with what I was thinking. I don't want to live off my fathers legacy."

"So what do you want me to do about it?"

"1. I wanna wrestle in a mask, 2. I don't want to be called Alicia Strong."

"#1 is granted and what did you have in mind for #2?"

"I'm not sure."

We sat for a minute or two pondering that question. Then, I came up with a great idea. "Muffin."

"Muffin?"

"Muffin."

"Why?"

"Old people like muffins."

"They also like prune juice."

"Yea, but you don't really want to be called Prunella, do you?"

"I don't think you get my point."

"I don't think I care about your point."

"I don't want to be called Muffin."

"Why not? Were you attacked by a muffin as a child. Some traumatizing experience?"

"No..."

"Then I don't see why not."

And so it was that after a lengthy debate, and threatening to put her in a romance angle with Giant Redwood, that she accepted the name.
