Jillefski: "Now that the trash has been taken out it's time to show the world just why Divinity have chosen Akima to join our elite ranks. Right now Akima will show his dominance by tearing his opponent to shreds and beating him in that ring 1-2-3 after knocking him out cold. Akima will prove he truly is an Unstoppable Samoan Savage as he takes on one of the toughest men to ever lace up a pair of boots. A man whose list of opponents is numerous, whose list of accomplishments is endless, a man who others fear. Ladies and gentlemen, I give you Akima's opponent,

Giant Oak!"

Oak comes out onto the stage looking surprised and shaking more than one of Dharma Greggs 'toys'. Oak speaks into Jillefskis mic, "I-I can't wrestle Akima. I already have a match tonight."

Jillefski: "Of course you do. You have one with Akima right now so get your ass into that ring."

Oak tries to speak into Jillefskis mic again, but Akima attacks him from behind and sends him rolling down the stage and into the ring.

vs.

-Frankie Perez vs. Human Arsenal-

Perez came out to the ring and waited to find out who TBTTLE had chosen to be his opponent in this pick your poison match. Perez waited anxiously until the familiar music hit the PA system and out he came. Perez got serious the moment he saw his opponent. This was going to be a challenge. Human Arsenal made his way into the ring and the match got underway.

The match itself was fantastic, and any other night would probably be match of the night. Although I know these two are capable of more together. The match played out how you might expect. Human Arsenal was in control for the vast majority of the match, and as you might not expect from a Divinity member it was on his own merit. Perez fought back though no matter how hard Arsenal hit him. Perez tried to take the momentum in this match in his own favor multiple times, but was unsuccessful all but once. Perez caught Arsenal off guard with a flying forearm and swung the match in his favor.

Arsenal got back to his feet and Perez clipped him with a kick to the back of the knee to take him down to one. Perez rushed him with a barrage of kicks to the back and front before finishing it with a roundhouse to the side of the head. Arsenal goes down and Perez takes a moment to rest. As Arsenal begins to stir Perez comes over and whips him into the corner. Perez follows in and picks him up to the top rope. Perez sets Arsenal up for the Perfect Parity (Fisherman Buster from the top), but Arsenal fights back. He knocks Perez off the top rope and drops down. Arsenal charges Perez with a thrust kick that sends Perez down. From there on out Arsenal kept control of the match before finally finishing things with an Ammo Dump. 1....2....3.

Winner: Human Arsenal in 16:54
