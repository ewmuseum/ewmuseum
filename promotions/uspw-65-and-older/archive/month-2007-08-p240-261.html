Held at The West Texas Coliseum

Att. 2,000 (Sold-Out)

Main Show

-The show kicks off with Triple D, Muffin and Freddie Datsun in the ring. DDD grabs a mic.

DDD: "It seems like every week another one of my so-called friends stabs me in the back. The latest being the very man I considered my best friend for the last 3 years of my life. And the worst part is I don't even get an explanation for it, just 'It's nothing personal'."

And who should interrupt but Seduction with Human Arsenal, Fumihiro Ota and Jacob Jett by her side.

Jett: "Look D, I told you, it's nothing personal. She just offered me more money. That's all."

DDD: "I don't believe that for one second."

Seduction: "Well believe it Darryl. I offered him a chance to be part of my team and he jumped at the opportunity."

DDD: "This is between me and him right now so why don't you stay out of it?"

Seduction: "You lying, cheating son of a bitch. How dare you talk to me like that!"

Jett: "Relax. So you don't believe that D. You want the truth?"

DDD: "You're damn right I do."

Jett: "The truth is the second that Seduction called me up and asked me to come down to USPW and beat the living hell out of you I caught the first plane down here. You wanna know why that is? It's because you abandoned me D. You left me to rot up there in Canada. We were partners, we were champions together, we rolled together, we were inseparable. Then, then you started going off on your own down in America. You put them before me. I started seeing you less and less and you started no showing more and more. How the hell you can stand there and say that I was still your best friend up until last week I have no idea. You left me high and dry and now I'm gonna leave you beaten and crying uncle."

Danny Jillefski comes out flanked by Big Cat Brandon.

Jillefski: "Sorry to interrupt this little reunion, but we do have a show to run here. Since it seems to me that there's plenty of pent up rage in there between all of you I'm going to do something special tonight. First off we'll have Muffin take on the "Super Ninja" Fumihiro Ota. And then I think we'll have Freddie Datsun go toe to toe with the Human Arsenal. And to top things off, the battle between former friends. Triple D will take on Jacob Jett. Good luck to all of you. Oh, and the first match begins right now."

Ota attacks Muffin from behind and jump starts this match. The referee throws everyone else out and has them leave the ringside area.

(C)

vs.

-Fumihiro Ota vs. Muffin-

Good chemistry is such a nice change of pace.

This was a great match that went back and forth. Muffin and Ota traded blows, but it was Ota that came out with the short stick in their exchange. Using his super secret ninja abilities (a.k.a. cheating) he took the momentum on his side and started to wear Muffin down with a barrage of kicks and various other strikes.

Muffin caught her second wind and came back fighting. She knocked Ota down a few times and then connected with a Strong Arm Tactic (a running elbow for those who don't know). She waits for him to get up and sets him up for an Angel Driver. This is where Seduction comes down to the ring and hits Muffin. The ref calls for the bell and disqualifies Ota.

Winner: Muffin by DQ in 12:01

(D)

vs.

-Triple D vs. Jacob Jett-

The hatred between these two could be could be cut with a knife. The bell rang and the fight began. Jett and Devine were all over each other and it was pretty easy to forget that this is a wrestling match. The two just rained down punches into each others skulls trying to get one of them down. The match spilled to the outside where Devine got tossed into the steel steps and Jett got dropped chest first onto the guard rail. Jett is flung into the steel steps and then Devine is tripped face first into the guard rail.

The match eventually gets back into the ring, but both men are beaten and battered already. Devine rolled Jett into the ring and Jett plays possum. Devine tries to pick him up and gets two fingers to the eyes. Jett hits a leaping DDT and makes a pin. He only gets two. He then decides to rip the padding off the turnbuckle. He picks Devine up and tries to whip him into the corner, but Devine holds on and reverses it sending Jett flying chest first into the opposite corner turnbuckle. Jett stumbles backwards and Devine drops him with a neckbreaker. Both men are down and the ref is counting.

Devine and Jett are back to their feet by the count of 7 and are trading blows. Jett tries to connect with a right, but Devine blocks it and connects with his own. Jett tries for another and Devine blocks it again then connects with another and another. Devine springs off the ropes and takes Jett down to the mat with a clothesline. Devine waits for him to get up and... he tries for a Devine Dream Drop (his variation of a diamond cutter/ Ace Crusher/ RKO). Jett shoves him off and goes for a superkick. Devine ducks under it and drops him down with a back drop.

Devine goes to the top rope, but takes too long getting there. Jett kips up to his feet and tugs on the top rope crotching Devine. Jett runs up the ropes to the side and delivers a frankensteiner on Devine. Jett nows goes up to the top. He comes down with a shooting star press, but catches nothing but canvas. The ref starts another 10 count. Both men are up to their feet by 8. Devine rushes in with a clothesline, but Jett ducks it. Devine turns around right into a CFTO (backflip kick). Jett picks him up and tries for a Jett Take Off, but Devine back body drops him. Devine picks him up and gets thumbed in the eye. Jett tries to whip Devine into the exposed turnbuckle, but it backfires on him and he gets sent in instead. Jett stumbles out backwards and turns right into the Devine Dream Drop. He makes the cover, 1.....2.....3.

Winner: Triple D in 11:50

vs.

-Freddie Datsun vs. Human Arsenal w/ Seduction-

Holy F***ing sh*t on a pogo stick. We have just topped Hayes/Devine for best match in my USPW history.

Arsenal and Datsun started the match off with an exchange of holds. Arsenal was able to win out on that battle

(hence why he's called Wrestling Machine in a certain other company). Datsun tried to brawl with Arsenal, but Arsenal won that battle too. Arsenal was in full control of the match by now and started to just punish Datsun. There were plenty of times where he could have made a cover, but he instead just picked Datsun up and brought him right back down to the canvas with some impressive power move.

Arsenal finally goes for the Ammo Dump and he hits it. Datsun looks to be done for. He makes the cover, 1.....2... Arsenal lifts Datsuns shoulder off the mat. He pulls Datsun up and sets him up for another Ammo Dump. This time Datsun finds the strength to give him a back body drop. Arsenal gets right back to his feet, but Datsun struggles to his. Arsenal clubs Datsun in the back and gets him set for a belly to belly, but Datsun fights it. He headbutts Arsenal repeatedly and he releases his grip. Datsun connects with a few punches that rock Arsenal and then hits him with a shoulder block. Datsun lifts Arsenal onto his shoulders for a Patriot Press.

Arsenal slips out back and nails Datsun with a German Suplex. Arsenal signals that this match is over, but Datsun seems to disagree. He fights back and grabs Arsenals head slamming it into his knee on the way down. Datsun pulls Arsenal in and tries for a piledriver. Arsenal won't budge though. Arsenal stands up and holds onto Datsun. He slams Datsun down into the mat and then locks him into a Texas Cloverleaf. Datsun struggles but is able to make it to the ropes. Arsenal picks him up and tries to get him set up for an Ammo Dump, but Datsun twists his body and hits Arsenal with the Patriot Press. Datsun makes the cover,

Have I mentioned that I love good chemistry?

The champion and challenger stood off and then came the bell to start this match. Devine was immediately taken down with a heavy punch from the Samoan Savage. He got back to his feet and threw a few punches of his own that rocked Umaga. Devine shot off the ropes and Umaga tried to intercept him with a spear. Devine leapfrogs over Umaga and hits him with a dropkick that sends Umaga stumbling back. Devine springs off the ropes again and goes for a clothesline. Umaga ducks and sends Devine face first into the steel with a flapjack.

Umaga picks DDD up and sends him head first into all four sides of the steel structure. Devine is busted open already this early on into the match. Umaga walks over to the door and orders the ref to open it. Devine grabs onto the leg of Umaga and the Samoan doesn't like it. He pulls Devine up to his feet and picks him up. He tries throwing him face first into the steel again, but Devine grabs onto the steel. Umaga switches position and gets Devine onto his shoulders. Samoan drop from Umaga. Umaga climbs to the top rope. He comes off and squashes Devine under him. He makes the cover, 1.....2... shoulder up from Devine.

Umaga doesn't look happy about that. He locks the Savage Crab on Devine and wrenches at his back. Devine tries as hard as he can to make it to the ropes, but he can't. Umaga eventually lets go of it out of frustration and drops down on the back of Devine. He picks him up and tosses him into the corner. Umaga goes to the opposite side and builds up some momentum. He charges in for a Savage Charge, but Devine catches him with a drop toe hold. Umaga slams face first into the middle turnbuckle. Devine slowly crawls to his feet, but Umaga is back to his long before Devine. Umaga kicks DDD in the gut a few times before pulling him to his feet.
