USPW Blaze of Glory

Saturday, Week 2, September 2007

Held at The Louisiana Auditorium

Att. 9,711

Dark Matches

-Art Reed defeated Giant Oak in 9:37 by pinfall with a Dark Matter. (D+)

Main Show

-The opening video for USPW Blaze of Glory plays. The video highlights all of the feuds and matches from tonight. Most of the time was spent on the huge quadruple main event tonight. First the Tag Team Championship Ladder match between former champions the Dudez!!!1!!! and current champions Team Seduction. Seduction is on a quest to get all the gold in her stable and she's definitely off to a good start. Second is the Last Man Standing match between Freddie Datsun and Big Cat Brandon. It started out with Danny Jillefski being taken out of the announcing booth and has evolved into this. Next is the USPW National Championship Match between Wanda Fish and champion William Hayes. Hayes has been focused on Devine while Fish has been training hard for tonight. Can Hayes retain against Fish again or will this be Wanda's night?

And last but certainly not least, the main event of the night. The USPW World Championship will be on the line when Muffin challenges Triple D for the gold. Former lovers will clash here as they're sure to put it all on the line tonight. Devine cheated on Muffin, Muffin consoled in Hayes, and Devine lost it. Devine has been a different man since then, and has been out to hurt anyone in any way he can. Can Muffin stop Devines reign of terror in its tracks tonight? Only time will tell.

vs. vs. vs.

-USPW Unappreciated Championship Match- Bulldozer Brandon Smith vs. Brett Hall vs. The Trademark vs.

Insane Machine-

This was the worst match of the night for sure, though it wasn't too bad. All of the guys in this can wrestle, it's just the crowd couldn't care less. Thus the Unappreciated Championship.

All four men started out in opposite corners of the ring, but came closer together as the bell rang. Bulldozer told Morgan that he had his back. Morgan went for Hall and we found out what Dozer really meant when he said he had Morgans back. As soon as Morgan turned his back to Dozer he dropped him on his head with a Backdrop Driver. Morgan was out of it and Bulldozer tried for the pin, but he was pulled off by Hall and Machine. The two of them then went for the cover at the same time. They broke up each others pin attempts and then went after each other. Bulldozer just stood in a corner and watched his competition take each other out.

Machines robotic movements were a sight to see for sure, how anyone could wrestle like that I'll never know. Machine drove a large amount of knife edge chops into Halls chest turning it beet red. Machine then whipped him into the other corner and charged in scraping his boot against Halls face. Machine throws him down and moves up top. Machine nails a picture perfect moonsault onto Hall and stays on him for a pin. 1....2.. Bulldozer drags Machine off and drops him on his head with an Inverted Piledriver. 1.....2... Morgan back in to break it up.

Bulldozer unleashes some frustration into Morgans face giving his opponents a chance to recover. Hall was back to his feet first. He tapped Dozer on the shoulder and when he turned around he got nailed with a big boot to the head. Hall was on Brandon like Bruce the Giant on cake, but without the bite marks. Hall stayed on Brandon and was able to get him set up for the Butterfly Powerbomb. That's when Insane Machine got back into this thing. A flying forearm to the temple of Hall takes him down. Hall gets up and gets the Termination Kick from Machine. Machine then gets caught in a Trademark Infringement from Morgan. Morgan gloats and Dozer drops him with another Backdrop Driver. He then makes the pin on Hall, 1.....2.....3.

Winner: Bulldozer Brandon Smith in 9:08

Rating (D)

vs.

-Rudy Velasquez vs. Zeus Maxmillion-

This turned out to actually be a pretty good match, and considering it is a heel/ heel match I'm actually pretty surprised.

Maxmillion and Velasquez went back and forth in this one, neither being able to gain a full advantage over the other. Mostly because they were both trying really hard to out-cheat the other. Velasquez proved to be a better cheater and took control of the match. He beat Maxmillion down and wore him out before trying for the Street Cutter. Zeus grabbed the arm of Velasquez and put him to the mat with an armbar takedown. Zeus flipped over and locked him in the Olympian Clutch.

Velasquez struggled to make it to the ropes, but he was able to crawl there. Maxmillion stalked him and lay in wait. As Velasquez got to his feet Maxmillion kicked him in the gut and hauled him up for a Thunder Bolt Powerbomb. Velasquez fought it and Maxmillion let his grip on him go. Velasquez slipped off the shoulders of Maxmillion, dropped to his feet and dropped Maxmillion with a Street Cutter. 1....2....3.

Winner: Rudy Velasquez in 11:35

vs.

-The Valentines vs. The Best Tag Team Like Ever-

Valentine cowered behind his nephew and bit his nails in terror of losing to the best tag team ever. Too bad all they got was Chance Fortune and Trent Shaffer, The Best Tag Team Like Ever.

Casey wrestled the entire match (done for quality control) not once tagging to his uncle. The BTTLE took advantage of that and didn't even worry about keeping Casey isolated, they just beat him down and double-teamed him. The match continued on like that for 6 minutes before Peter Valentine summoned up some courage and tagged himself in. He stepped into the middle of the ring, got in the face of Trent Shaffer, and then got on his knees and begged him off. Casey didn't like that one bit and went after Shaffer. Shaffer rolled out of the ring and Casey followed. Casey chased Shaffer all the way up the ramp. Then the bell rang. TBTTLE had been counted out. The ref raises Peters hand in the ring and he hugs the ref and runs around the ring excited to have beaten the best tag team ever.

Winners: The Valentines by count-out in 6:33

vs.

-Birdcage of Fun Match- The Hotshots vs. Veteran Mentality-

The structure in which the match would take place was now set up. A cage surrounded the ring, but nothing like your normal cage. This one looked like a birdcage. Thin steel bars wrapped around the ring. The bars were spray painted gold of course. Steel plates spray painted gold as well wrapped around the top, bottom and middle of the cage binding it together. The roof was a convex meshing of steel bars meeting in the top center forming a big gold ring which was used to lower the cage from the ceiling. Inside the ring it looked like tornado week at the local trailer park. Littered around the ring and hanging form ropes dangling from the ceiling were a variety of blunt objects from the traditional chair to the not-so-much recycling bin.

The Hotshots came out to the ring first and looked at it with a 'WTF' expression on both of their faces. They entered the ring through the birdcage door and waited for their opponents. Veteran Mentalitys music started playing and the crowd chants 'FORCE' repeatedly. Then they appear. Oxford is his normal self while Force isn't. Force actually walks to the ring for a change, even if he still has that stupid expression of his face. The strangest part is that Force is carrying an urn. I can only assume what's in there but I'd have to say that's probably the ashes of Mr. Squiggles. Who would get a pigeon cremated you may wonder? Well who would ask for a Birdcage of Fun match? Anyways, Force and Oxford get in the ring and confront the Hotshots. Force then goes over to the corner and puts the urn there, kissing it before he goes.

Force then asks for a mic.

Force: "IF WE COULD ALL OBSERVE A MOMENT OF SILENCE FOR MR. SQUIGGLEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEES!!!!!!"

Force drops the mic, puts his hand on his heart, looks at the urn and is silent for a moment. Oxford is silent as well and so is most of the audience. Wayne and Vessey groan and look at the weapons in the ring. Wayne grabs a kendo stick and Vessey grabs a garbage can. Wayne cracks Force in the back of the head with the stick while Vessey smashes the can over Oxfords head. It probably didn't do any damage. Vessey then rotates the trash can and puts it over Oxford. Wayne gets into position and swings the stick at the bottom of the trash can. Vessey spins Oxford around and Wayne nails the bottom again trapping Oxford inside. The Hotshots turn their attention to Force now.

The Hotshots grab whatever weapons they can and beat the living crap out of Force while Oxford tries to squirm his way out of that trash can. The Hotshots have Force down and hurt and then turn their attentions back to Oxford. They both grab kendo sticks and take turns banging on the trash can as hard and loud as they can. Force tries to make the save for his teammate, but Vessey and Wayne just turn and nail the blood soaked Force with the sticks. Vessey gets c0cky and picks up the urn of Mr. Squiggles, looks inside then smashes it over Forces head. Vessey then pours out the ashes onto Force.

That's when it happens. Force finally snaps. The force begins to surge through his veins. He gets back to his feet and goes after the Hotshots. They both try the kendo sticks, but they have no effect on Force. Force grabs the heads of the Hotshots and slams them together. Then Force goes after Vessey. FOOOOOOORCE Punch!!! on Vessey. Force pulls him up and delivers another. Force then hoists him up in the air and nails him with a Full Force, but with extra Force. Wayne goes after Force, but Force delivers a FOOOORCE Punch!!! on Wayne. Oxford breaks free of the trash can and nails Wayne with a Mental Breakdown DDT. Force hoists Vessey up again and drills him down with another Full Force. Force makes the pin, 1.....2......3.

Winners: Veteran Mentality in 9:31

Rating (C-)

vs. w/

-Bruce The Giant vs. Umaga w/ Stranton-

Bruce cowered in the corner as the bell rang. Umaga walked over to Bruce ready to destroy him, but Bruce stuck himself between the ropes and the ref backed the Samoan Savage off. Bruce told the ref he has Samoaphobia. The ref told Bruce to get over it or some motivational thing like that. Bruce stood up straight, walked to the middle of the ring , looked Umaga in his beady little eyes and punched him in the head. Umaga didn't feel a thing, roared and then took Bruce down with a thrust to the throat. Bruce tried to crawl back to his corner, but this match quickly turned into an extended version of the Umaga/ Bruce beatdowns. Now available with 7 extra minutes of footage and deleted scenes.

Umaga broke Bruce in half with a Savage Spear. Then Stranton hopped up onto the apron. The ref went over to get him down, but Umaga was confused as well. What happened next only added to the Samoan Savages confusion. Out of the crowd and pouncing up to the top turnbuckle was another wild Samoan. Umaga pointed out the other Samoan to Stranton, and also the ref. The other Samoan came down from the top rope with a Suicide Headbutt right into the skull of Bruce the Giant. The ref called for the disqualification and awarded the match to the unconscious Bruce the Giant.

Winner: Bruce the Giant by DQ in 5:31
