vs.

-Mainstream Hernandez vs. Davey Wayne-

The "Every match I think could be good but then I find out they have bad chemistry dammit." curse has come back. I swear I didn't run over its dog. Although, if this is not clicking, I don't think I mind it so much.

Wayne played it smart and targeted the taped up ribs of Hernandez. Wayne went right after them and didn't let up. He worked over Hernandez with plenty of knees to the gut, kicks to the gut, gutbusters, backbreakers and an abdominal stretch. Wayne went to finish Hernandez off with the Fishermans Suplex, but Hernandez blocked it. Wayne tried once more, but this time Hernandez drilled Wayne with a brainbuster. Both men went down. They instinctively tried to tag their partners in, but realized it was 1-on-1. Wayne was back to his feet first. He tried to pick Hernandez up, but Mainstream fought back with his own shots to the gut of Wayne.

Hernandez came back to life knocking down Wayne over and over then springing off the ropes. Wayne tried to clothesline Hernandez, but he ducked it. Wayne tried for a backbreaker on the return trip, but Hernandez instead takes Wayne down with a spinning headscissors. As Wayne gets up Hernandez catches him with a Fever Beat and goes up top. Vessey runs down to the ring and tries to pull Hernandez down, but Hernandez kicks him away. Hernandez hits the Apparition #14. Unfortunately he lands it on the mat. Wayne rolls him up in a la magistral cradle and pulls the tights, 1.....2.....3.

Winner: Davey Wayne in 14:32

vs.

-Freddie Datsun & Muffin vs. Triple D & Human Arsenal w/ Seduction-

To say I'm disappointed with this match would be an understatement.

All four competitors in this match were still hurting from their matches at Blaze of Glory. Devine let Arsenal do most of his dirty in this match. The heels kept Datsun, who was still badly hurt from BoG, isolated from his corner and beat up on him badly. Devine taunted Muffin alot during this match provoking her into the ring long enough to distract the ref and get in a double team on Datsun. Devine and Arsenal continued on like this until Datsun caught his second wind.

He fended off the heels long enough to get the tag in to Muffin. Muffin came into the ring like a house of fire and Devine bailed out. Muffin went after Arsenal and took him down. Muffin was able to take Arsenal down wit ha Strong Arm Tactic and then planted him with an Angel Driver. She made the pin, 1....2.. Devine gets back in and breaks it up. Muffin goes after him, but he bails out of the ring again. Muffin turns her attention back to Arsenal, but Devine sneaks back into the ring and catches her with a Devine Dream Drop. Both Muffin and Arsenal are down. They crawl to their corners and make tags.

Datsun explodes into the ring and takes Devine down, but Devine doesn't go down without a fight. He catches Datsun in the family jewels with an elbow. Devine jumps back to his feet and drops Datsun with a Devine Dream Drop. He makes the cover, 1...........2..............3.

Winners: DDD & Arsenal in 21:01
