USPW All American Wrestling

Monday, Week 1, October 2007

Held at Grissom Auditorium

Att. 2,000 (Sold-Out)

Dark Matches

-Human Arsenal defeated Giant Oak in 5:36 by pinfall with an Ammo Dump. (C-)

-The Dudez!!!1!!! defeated Maxmillion Productions in 6:42 when Frankie Perez defeated T-Rex by pinfall with a Perfect Parity. (C+) (Rex was exhausted by the end. Surprisingly good match otherwise though.)

Main Show

-Sam Strong arrives in the arena coming from the door to parking garage. He holds the door opens and through it come Muffin, sans mask for the first time but with a pretty substantial bandage across her forehead to make up for it, and William Hayes who's helping Muffin. Muffin is still pretty banged up from the looks of it with the forehead bandage, taped up ribs and bruised up everywhere. For those with short-term memory loss this was the result of the cage beatdown number Divinity did to her.

Strong: "Don't worry Alicia, I'm going to make sure Devine gets what he has coming to him for this."

Muffin: "Thanks daddy, but you really shouldn't."

Strong: "What kind of father would I be if I didn't stick up for my little girl?"

Hayes: "Nobody would blame you for pulling out of this Sam."

Strong: "How many times do I have to tell you to stop treating me like an old man?"

Hayes: "I'll stop treating you like an old man when you find the Fountain of Youth."

Strong: "Don't get wise with me brother."

Muffin: "Daddy, William, please just stop this fighting."

Hayes and Strong stare at each other for a moment before both turning their heads forward and moving on.

(B+)

-"The Trademark" Thomas Morgan comes down to the ring and unfortunately grabs a mic.

Morgan: "The Trademark has finally arrived at the Grissom Auditorium. The Trademark is here because The Trademark has a match tonight against the X Citizen. The Trademark fears no man and that includes the X Citizen for when The Trademark is done with you, you will be the ex-citizen. Then The Trademark shall move on to his destiny, the USPW World Championship. In fact The Trademark is this close to joining Divinity. The Trademarks career will be reinvigorated starting tonight."

(E)

vs.

-The Trademark vs. Citizen X-

X's contract is expiring soon and Morgans isn't. I'll let you do that math yourself. And pinfall following a Trademark Infringement for anyone who cares.

Winner: Thomas Morgan in 3:12

Rating (D-)

-And once again Jacob Jett is backstage pounding on the locker room door of USPW National Champion Wanda Fish. "I just wanted to talk to you. Come one out, I won't hurt you, I promise." Jett continues pounding at the door. He then stops and thinks for a second. "I got cookies."

Oak: "Ooo, what kind?"

Fish: "Oakie!"

Oak: "Oh yeah, sorry."

Fish: "I though I told you to stay quiet."

Oak: "Sorry, but cookies."

Jett: "I can hear you in there."

Fish: "Fine. What is it you want?"

Jett: "I just wanted to talk."

Fish: "About what?"

Jett: "I don't know, I actually hadn't thought it out this far ahead."

Oak: "Can I have those cookies now?"

Fish: "Oakie, there are no cookies."

Oak: "What? You mean he lied to us?"

Jett: "No, I have cookies, why don't you open the door and I can give them to you?" Big Cat Brandon walks into shot, but Jett shoos him away. Oak cracks the door open, but Fish pulls him in before he gets attacked. "Oakie, don't do that. It's a trap."

Jett: "If you're not coming out I'm coming in." Jett charges shoulder first into the door. He ends up clutching his shoulder. "Oh that hurt. That always works in the movies. Let's go Brandon. We'll be back!"

(C+)

vs.

-USPW Unappreciated Championship Match- Brendan Idol vs. Bulldozer Brandon Smith (c)-

Idol has been a pleasant surprise since he got here and Bulldozer is Bulldozer, coming out to the ring with his handy chain ready to kill someone for looking at him wrong. And that's pretty much what happened here. Bulldozer dropped Idol with a nasty looking Backdrop Driver onto the back of his neck. 1....2....3.

Winner: Bulldozer Brandon Smith in 8:53

Rating (D)

-Bulldozer celebrates his win over Idol. From out of the crowd comes Brett Hall. Hall grabs Dozers chain from out of the corner, gets in the ring, wraps it around his fist and knocks Dozers' lights out. Hall doesn't stop there as he wraps the chain around Dozers neck and chokes him out, giving Dozer a little taste of his own medicine. Referees and security swarm the ring to pull Hall off of Dozer.

(E+)

-Freddie Datsun is walking somewhere backstage when he bumps into Phlex in the hall.

Datsun: "Excuse me."

Phlex: "You should be excused with that body shape of yours."

Datsun: "You trying to say something?"

Phlex: "I'm saying that your physical form is even worse than your form in the ring."

Datsun: "I know when someone is insulting me, now if you want to take this out to the ring and settle it like men."

Phlex: "I do not need to fight you to know that I am better than you. Just look at my rippling muscles (flexes). You would be able to look almost half as good as me if you were to use my Phlex-it-All vitamin supplement."

Datsun: "My mama taught me not to eat any strange foods, so sorry but no thanks."

Phlex: "You have rejected my product? You have refused to buy my merchandise? You will pay for your arrogance. I want you in a match tonight."

Datsun: "You're on golden goblin. Now can you move out of my way?"

Phlex: "You will regret this day." (Flexes some more)

(C+)

vs.

-Peter Valentine vs. Trent Shaffer-

This was a surprisingly even match. I guess Valentine is wearing thin the rust from his maternity (err.. paternity) leave. Valentine was about to finish Shaffer off with the Heart Breaker, but Fortune held onto his foot allowing Shaffer to slip out and hit the Heart Burn on Peter for the 3 count.

Winner: Trent Shaffer in 7:20

Rating (D+)

-Shaffer is victorious over Peter Valentine, but not without the help of Chance Fortune. Fortune joins Shaffer in the ring and the two stomp away at Valentine. That's when Casey hits the ring with a chair in hand. He nails Fortune and then Shaffer in the head with the chair knocking them down. Fortune and Shaffer roll out of the ring while Casey checks on his Uncle.

(D-)

-USPW World Champion Triple D is in the Divinity locker room with his wannabe apprentices, The Hotshots, Davey Wayne and Cameron Vessey.

Vessey: "So you proud of us now? Are we officially your apprentices yet?"

DDD: "No."

Vessey: "What do you mean no?"

DDD: "No. It's pretty self-explanatory."

Wayne: "Why not? We won the tag match Thursday."

DDD: "No, I won the tag match Thursday. You two just got in the way."

Vessey: "Got in the way? If it weren't for us you wouldn't have won that match."

DDD: "You can believe that all you want, but I did win it. And what's more, I don't know if you two are ready to be my apprentices. Hell, I'm not even sure you're good enough to be my apprentices at all."

Wayne: "What do we have to do now?"

DDD: "Glad you asked that."

Vessey: "I'm not."

DDD: "Remind me to get you a match with Umaga if you keep that up. Anyway, tonight Davey you're going to be wrestling Steve Flash. Don't worry about it though, this should be an easy win for you. Trust me on this one."

Devine walks out of the locker room leaving the Hotshots to themselves.

Vessey: "I'm starting to not like this plan anymore."

Wayne: "What's wrong? It was your plan after all."

Vessey: "Yeah I know, that's what makes it worse."

(C)

vs.

-Steve Flash vs. Davey Wayne-

Wow, these two have absolutely rotten chemistry together. Damn I'm glad I chose not to make this the main event after all.

The match started out as a technical display with Wayne trying to keep up with the man more than twice his age. He couldn't and Flash ended up tying him in a proverbial knot. Wayne forgot about trying to out-wrestle Flash and instead focused on using his speed to his advantage and taking the fight to Flash before he knew what had hit him. Wayne targeted the abdominal and lower back of Flash working that over the best he could to weaken Flash for either an STF or Fishermans Suplex. Wayne went for the Fishermans, but Flash hooked his leg on the inside to keep Wayne from getting him up. Flash fired some forearms into Waynes chin and one snap suplex later both men are down.

Flash and Wayne struggle to their feet trying to beat the refs 10 count. Wayne grabs onto the ref and tries to use him to pull himself up. That was just a distraction though. Art Reed sneaks into the ring and hits Flash with a Dark Matter. Reed bails and Wayne locks Flash into the STF. Flash put up a valiant fight, but ends up tapping out.

Winner: Davey Wayne in 16:43

YOU HAVE TAMPERED WITH THE TEMPERMENTAL TERRIGATIVE TORTUROUS TORQUES OF THE FOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOORCE!!!!!!!! AND FOR THAT YOU SHALL PAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAY!!!!! BUY MY MERCHANDISE. AT A HAUNTED HOUSE IN AMERICAAAAAAAAAAAAAA YOU TWO SHALL FEEEEEL THEEEEE FOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO OOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOORCE!!!!!!!!!!!!"

Pizzles: "*Squawk* Now you're done for bitches, now you're done for bitches *Squawk*"

(C)

-Bruce the Giant is backstage at the food table loading his plate with whatever morsels of food he can find. Someone taps him on the shoulder and Bruce panics sending the plate flying. Bruce turns around to see Jacob Stranton. Bruce looks around frantically for either Akima or Umaga.

Stranton: "Relax Bruce. Akima and Umaga are busy doing whatever it is 2 Samoans locked inside of a bathroom would do. I'd prefer not to think of what they're trying to eat really. Anyhow I'm here on their behalf to challenge you to a match."

Bruce: "Rhuht krhihnd owhf wmatckh?"

Stranton: "A tag team match."

Bruce: "Bruwht rhi dohwnrnt ahve a pawrtnuhr."

Stranton: "If you would let me finish I'll get to that. It'll be the Wild Savages versus you and a partner of your choice. Good luck finding one that's stupid enough to step into a ring with either of them though."

Bruce: (Translated) "Mommy."

Out of nowhere Akima and Umaga attack Bruce and send him through the food table.

Stranton: "I thought I locked you in a bathroom. How did you two get out of there?"

Umaga reaches into his mouth and pulls out a screw.

(C)

vs.

-Freddie Datsun vs. Phlex-

Phlex continues to fit in just fine. Ya know what they say, SWFs' loss is my gain. It was a hard fought match between both sides that see-sawed back and forth many times. Datsun brought the brawling and Phlex brought the, well, more brawling, but the match was good. In the end Phlex tried to knock Datsun out with a bottle of Phlex-it-All, but Datsun ducks under and catches him with a Patriot Press. Datsun pins Phlex, 1.....2.....3.

Winner: Freddie Datsun in 21:19

Rating (B)

-Datsun celebrates his win over Phlex when the USPW World Champion comes down to the ring with Danny Jillefski following behind. Datsun prepares for a fight, but Devine comes out of nowhere with a Devine Dream Drop planting Datsun on the mat. Devine kicks him out of the ring and grabs a mic.

DDD: "As I'm sure all of you know by now at A Haunting in America I'll be defending my USPW Championship against none other than Sam Strong. Now this is normally the part where I talk about my opponent and basically tell you how little chance there is they'll actually beat me. I'm not going to do that this week, because a picture is worth a thousand words. If the lazy bums in the production trailer would be so kind as to put the picture up."

On the tron pops up a picture of Sam Strong in his last televised match against Liberty (Which was property of a magazine and not TCW so we lucked out). The pic isn't too flattering as Strong was pretty out of shape at that point.

DDD: "Now take a look at that. You see that? That's my challenger at aHiA, a geriatric, arthritic 60 year old who lost his last professional match to Liberty of all people. I mean come on, it's Liberty. I could beat that guy with one arm tied behind my back. And look at that body, like a hippo made out of Jello. Face it Sam, you and your family can't stop me or Divinity."

This is where Sam Strong cuts Devine off. He appears on the entrance ramp with his daughter and William Hayes.

Strong: "Well brother, thanks for pointing out the obvious. I know I'm not in the best shape of my career, but it won't take the old Sam Strong to beat you Devine. All of the Strongamaniacs chanting my name will be enough to overcome the odds and get that belt off of you once and for all."

DDD: "Wow Sam, that is such a noble goal. I mean, using your power in this company to give yourself a shot at this title and then using your own little brat of a daughter as a reason to justify it. So noble."

Strong: "You've got to have a lot of guts and no brain to say something like that to me brother!"

DDD: "Then why don't you come down here and do something about it old man?"

Strong throws his mic down and starts on his way down th ramp when he's stopped by Hayes. Hayes picks up Strongs mic.

Hayes: "Why don't you be a man for once Devine and stop attacking women and people twice your age? Why don't you finally grow a set? Why not wrestle someone who'll actually put up a fight?"

DDD: "Why don't you keep your damn mouth shut? You should just be lucky I haven't pressed charges against you with how many times you've been in violation of my restraining order."

Hayes: "You're the lucky one here Devine, lucky I haven't been able to get my hands around your throat you lying, cheating son of a bitch."

DDD: "What is with everyone around here calling me a cheater? I never cheated on anyone."

Hayes: "You have some audacity to say that when you've been caught on tape twice now."

DDD: "Fine, ya know what, I did cheat. I cheated on Alicia, but I had a good reason for it."

Alicia snatches the mic from Hayes before he can say anything.

Muffin: "Then what is it Darryl?"

DDD: "So glad you asked my little muffin. I mean don't get me wrong, I loved our little conversations, and no woman made me laugh quite as much as you. Oh, and those make-out sessions. Whew, you should see your little girl go Sam. Like a tiger I tell ya. But the reason I cheated on you, and that is such a harsh way to put it, is because you never put out."

Muffin: "What?"

DDD: "You never put out. I tried for months to get those pants off you, but daddys little girl is too pure for that kind of thing before marriage. Eventually I just got sick of it, so I found some girls who would. Ya know, let me get in their pants, unlike you. I mean, to be honest, I probably cheated on you with ten different girls and those are just the ones I remember. A man has his needs. You didn't put out so I put you out. Simple as that."

Alicia is on the verge of tears while Hayes, well, let's just put it like this; if you put a piece of coal in his clenched fist right now it would turn into a diamond. Hayes had heard enough. He charged the ring taking Devine by surprise and taking it to him.

Hayes and Devine slugged it out in the middle of the ring with neither man giving any ground to his rival. Hayes and Devine lobbed rights into each others skulls and it was Hayes who got the better of Devine knocking him onto his ass. Devine scurried away, but Hayes caught him. Devine resorted to en eye rake to try to get away long enough to catch his breath. Devine ran out of the corner at Hayes, but Hayes connected with a knee lift into Devines chest. Devine got back up, but got caught with the Hayes Fever backbreaker into downward spiral combo of Hayes. Hayes picked Devine up and set him up for the Slick Trick.

That's when Jillefski made his presence felt. He made a crushing field goal punt right into Hayes', how shall I put this, family jewels. Jillefski grabbed a mic and signaled for police officers.

Jillefski: "You crossed the line tonight Hayes, you just attacked Devine with absolutely no physical provocation. Do you know what that means? It means you're clearly in breech of the restraining order. Officers, arrest this man. Get him out of my building, get that piece of trash out of my building. And Hayes, just to be clear, you're fired!"

The police officers cuff Hayes and drag him out of the arena as Devine and Jillefski sing 'Nah nah nah nah, hey hey hey, goodbye' in the ring trying to coerce the crowd to join them. The last image we see before we go off the air is Muffin crying her way backstage while Devine looks on with a sadistic smile on his face.
