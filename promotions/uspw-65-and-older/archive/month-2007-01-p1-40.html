And so it begins...

It started out like any normal Saturday. As normal as my life gets anyways. I woke up, I ate lunch, I showered and then I did whatever it was my girlfriend was forcing me into this weekend. On the plate for today was going to an auction house to look for some furniture. Why? Because she has no clue what the words garage sale mean.

When we got there we went our separate ways. As fun as trying to outbid some old people for something I don't want to begin with sounds like fun, I think I'll pass. I wandered the halls for a little while and eventually settled on sitting around trying to count the number of little holes there are in the ceiling tiles. Eventually I got bored with that too, so I went to look for my gf. I had no idea which room she was in so I just had to politely walk into each room and survey it. Yes, barging into an auction and yelling for her is polite, thank you very much.

I had checked about 5 rooms now, but still no sign of her. Or maybe she was avoiding me. Who knows. Anyways, I have no idea what was so different about the 6th room I checked but for some reason I didn't just randomly shout out for her, I kept my mouth shut and looked around. It was mostly because my voice was starting to get hoarse, but it's the principle that matters. The auctioneer, the loud annoying guy who talks fast(for those who may not know), was seeming desperate to get rid of whatever it was he was selling, he was even going down on the opening bid of $25. He kept getting lower and lower and I became more and more curious what the hell he was auctioning.

Eventually he starting going pretty low. "$5, $5, only $5. This is a once in a lifetime opportunity. This is the kind that only comes once in a lifetime. $5, only $5. Anyone? Anyone at all? Come on people, I'm practically giving it away"

Then I just had to open my mouth. "One dollar."

"$1, we got $1. Anyone wanna make it 1 fitty? Ah, who the hell am I kidding? Sold for $1. Congratulations sir, you just bought something one of a kind."

"So what the hell did I just buy?"

"A job."

"What kind of job?"

"You're a booker now."

"Wait, what? I work at a library now?"

"No, you're booking for a wrestling company."

"Oh. That kind of booker." After a long pause for contemplation I had to ask "What company am I booking for?"

"You're the new head booker of United States Pro Wrestling."

"Can I have the dollar back instead?"

"No sorry, you just signed a legally binding document."

"No I didn't."

"You did verbally and we have proof. This place is bugged with microphones everywhere."

"And what if I just don't pay you the dollar?"

"Then we'll sue your ass."

".......great......."

Well, at least the gf can't keep bothering me with all the "When are you going to get a job", "I can't pay these bills alone." "We're 3 months behind on the rent.", "I told you the power was going to get shut off today.", "Here's a paper now start looking." crap she bugs me with.

Speaking of the 'ol lady here she is. And she has has that 'I just bought a whole bunch of junk you'll never use and I'll stop using after a week' look on her face. I told her that I just got a job. After the initial burst of joy came the painfully obvious question "Wait, how the hell did you get a job?"

"I bought it, and it didn't come with a receipt either."

"So what exactly does this job entail?"

Here comes the fun part....

"WHAT!!!"

Those were the first words out of her mouth after I told her that I was booking for USPW. She's not much of a wrestling fan, but even she knows of the black hole of talent, skill, youth, and any possible redeeming quality that USPW is. It's not like I want to work there either, but if I don't I get sued. On the bright side it is a paying job. I think. I'll find out tomorrow when I meet with Sam Strong and Danny Jillefski anyways.

The Next Day: USPW Headquarters: Irony of the Day- It used to be a retirement castle.

Old people smell overtakes me and I wish I had a clothespin as I walk inside. I go up to the receptionist who is well past 70 I'd say. I asked her where I could go to see Mr. Strong. She put on her glasses which looked like 2 magnifying lenses welded together and asked me "Do you have an appointment here hot stuff?" After the initial creepiness of that statement wore off I answered "Yeah, I just bought job here and I have to see Mr. Strong about it."

"Second floor, down the hall 3 doors to the right. And do come back now handsome."

I'm not even 5 steps into this place and it's already scaring the heebie jeebies out of me. And now I'm talking like an old person too, dammit. I took the stairs up to the second floor, went down the hall and into the room 3 doors to the right. It was the kitchen. I went back down to the receptionist. "Done already, hot stuff?"

"You just sent me to the kitchen."

"Well where's my prune juice?"

"Huh?"

"I thought I told you I wanted you to get me some prune juice."

"And I thought I told you that I was here to see Sam Strong."

"You're even cuter when you're angry. If you want to see Mr. Strong he's 5 doors down to the right."

Well, that was still creepy, but at least I know where to go now. I went back up to the second floor and went in the door 5 down and to the right. It was the janitors closet. This was going to be a long day. I went back down to the receptionist and asked "Did you want a mop for some reason?"

"What can I help you with now cutie pie?"

"I went up to the second floor and went to the door 5 down and to the right. It was a janitors closet."

"I never told you to go to the second floor."

"You never told me not to."

"I said 5 to the right."

"Fine. Whatever. I'm going."

"I'll be here when you get back hot stuff."

This is just giving me more reason not to like old people. Anyways, I went 5 doors down the hall on the first floor and prepared myself to find the bathroom. This was one time I was glad to be wrong. I found Sam Strong behind his desk flanked by Danny Jillefski and a big angry looking dude. The big angry guy came up to me and looked down on me very intimidating like. Then he opened his mouth and blew the illusion. In a high pitched voice he asked "Do you have a reason to be here punk?"

"I just bought a job here and..."

"Speak up, I can't hear you."

"Say it don't spray it Sasquatch. I said I'm here to talk to Sam."

"Do you want to take this outside?"

"Why? So you can be in your natural habitat?"

"Stop it, both of you. Tiny, stop picking a fight with this man." Sam yelled out very irritated.

"But he hurt my feelings."

"That's no reason to hurt his body though. Mr, I didn't catch your name."

"It's Jay, just call me that."

"Well Jay, why don't you have a seat?"

"Has Tiny sat on it? I don't want it collapsing on me."

"That's it. You're done bitch!!" Tiny charged me but Danny held him back. I'm great at making friends, aren't I? Sam was getting pretty mad at the whole situation. "Stop it now Jay or I'll have you fired." A smile crept its way onto my face "Really? Hey sasquatch, you smell like ****. Standing in the rain isn't a shower, ya know."

"You want to get fired, don't you?"

"Yes sir I do."

"Too bad."

"What, why not? Did I not just prove I'm a classless coward and unfit for working with the public in any capacity?"

"Yes, but we need a scapegoat. You're perfectly qualified for that position."

"A scapegoat? But the receipt said head booker."

"That was just a formality. You're really here because this company is sinking faster than the Titanic."

"Or dying faster than half the roster?"

"I don't like you taking shots at my employees."

"I don't care. Let them hate me all they want. Any of them try to touch me I'll pull their walker out from under them and leave 'em helpless."

"You're very easy to hate."

"It's a natural gift. Look Mr. Strong, I really do not want this job. Is there any way out of it."

"Well, I suppose..."

"What? What is it? Anything, I'll do anything."

"If you really want out of this contract so bad then all you have to do is find a replacement."

"Can do sir."

"A qualified replacement."

"Dammit. I mean can do."

"And no taking a hobo off the street and dressing him up either."

"There goes plan B."

"I await meeting your replacement Jay."

"I'll have them right away."

"Good, because if you don't by Tuesday you're going to have to do your job."

"Wouldn't want that sir."

I walked out of that office with a smile on my face, and possibly some of Tiny's spit on my face too, but I'd rather not think about that one. I walked down the hall ready to leave this building today and get fired tomorrow. Unfortunately I had to walk past that receptionist. She had one leg up on her desk and was leaning back in her chair, she winked at me as I was coming by and then poured prune juice on her shirt. Now that was a true test of my gag reflexes.

And now to get fired...
