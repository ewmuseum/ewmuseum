USPW All American Wrestling

Saturday, Week 1, April 2007 (airs Wednesday)

Held at The Washington State Gymnasium

Att.2,000 (Sold Out)

Dark Matches

-Bulldozer Brandon Smith defeated Cameron Vessey in 8:01 by pinfall with a Backdrop Driver. (D) (They had great chemistry. Well, that's good to know.)

-The Crazy Old Men (Jim Force & Robert Oxford) had an interview hyping their upcoming tag match with The Towers Of Power. (E+) (They're crazy old men. And Jim Force was involved. How do you think this went?)

-The Crazy Old Men (Force & Oxford) defeated The Towers Of Power in 5:53 when Robert Oxford defeated Danny Rushmore by submission. (D+) (Wow. Sadly that match was as good or better than pretty much everything else tonight.)

Main Show

-The show kicks off with Trent Shaffer in the middle of the ring. Yea, Trent Shaffer, that's not a typo. He has a mic in hand.

Shaffer: "For the last few months I've been accused of not pulling my weight around here. I've been called worthless and I've been called talentless. I'm going to prove all of them wrong tonight, because tonight I'm going to win the USPW World Championship. That's right, I'm challenging Umaga for the World Championship tonight."

Jacob Stranton comes out onto the entrance way and laughs at Shaffer. "What's so funny? I'm serious. I want a match with Umaga tonight."

Stranton snickers a little before bringing the mic up to his mouth and saying "Your funeral." and walking to the back. Oh, and cue Umaga who slides into the ring behind Shaffer and hits him with a hard clothesline to the back of the neck before leaving.

(C-)

vs.

-USPW National Championship Match- Corporal Doom vs. Steve Flash (c)-

This was Dooms farewell match as I decided not to renew his contract. He just wasn't worth what he was asking for. I mean, he can't even go 7 minutes without getting exhausted. Flash picks up a clean victory over the Corporal after hitting him with a Flash Bang.

Winner and still champion: Steve Flash in 6:58

Rating (D)

-An unfamiliar music starts playing and down to the ring comes Giant Oak. He makes his way to the ring waving at people and blowing kisses as though people actually like him. He grabs a mic and makes himself useful.

Oak: "Thank you, thank you. I deserve your applause, I really do. For those who don't know who I am, for shame on you. I am the son of the legendary Giant Redwood, I am Giant Oak. My father has left a great legacy, and I, as a second generation star will continue to blaze the trail my father started. I've come to USPW for one singular reason, the USPW World Championship. I will win that title and make my father proud. Then after I accomplish that I'll be off to the SWF to win a title that matters. I digress though, that's not why I came out here. I wanted to thank the millions and millions of Oakies for voting me the most entertaining wrestler of all time. It's definitely appreciated. I will..."

Oak stops as Fumihiro Ota makes his way down to the ring. Oak stands face to face with Ota. Ota thrusts forward as if he's going to attack and Oak runs scared. Otas opponent for tonight makes their way down to the ring. Wanda Fish, in a cheerleader outfit complete with pompoms, makes her way to the ring. Oak eyes her as he lays outside the ring. (E+, D-)

vs.

-Fumihiro Ota vs. Wanda Fish-

If this is bad chemistry I'd like to see some more of it. Well, actually, no I wouldn't. But I wouldn't mind seeing more of this match. Undoubtedly match of the night, and the ARSEHOLES will back me up on that.

Ota was in charge of a large majority of this match using powerful strikes to keep Fish down. Ota made the mistake of taking Wanda lightly and it cost him. Wanda surprised him with a roll-up that almost got her a 3. Ota went after her furiously, but she avoided his attacks and hit with her own. Fish went for a Blonde Ambition (Figure 4), but Ota Kicked her away and hit her with a front dropkick sending her back into the corner. He picks her up and delivers a Ninja Strike nearly taking her head off.

Winner: Fumihiro Ota in 9:01

Rating (C)

-Ota looks down at a helpless Fish and as he's about to pick her up by her hair a clearly frightened Oak steps between her and Ota. Ota tells the ref to call for the bell. He does and we have a match.

(D-)

vs.

-Giant Oak vs. Fumihiro Ota-

Oak stands up to Ota and gets in his face. Kick to the head ~! by Ota. Oak is out cold. Ota puts his foot down on Oaks chest and the refs counts the 3. Shortest match in USPW history.

Winner: Fumihiro Ota in 0:32

Rating (D-)

-Backstage we see Triple D walking. He's staring down at his phone when he bumps into Muffin.

DDD: "Oh man, I'm sorry. You alright?"

Muffin: "Yea, I'm fine. Just watch where you're going next time, alright?"

DDD: "Yea, sorry about that."

Muffin: "I'm Muffin by the way."

DDD: "I'm Darryl."

Muffin: "Well it was nice to meet you Darryl."

DDD: "You in a hurry somewhere?"

Muffin: "Yea, I have a match pretty soon."

DDD: "Really? Who against?"

Muffin: "..." Before Muffin can say anything Peter Valentine pops up.

Valentine: "Where's Seduction? I wanna ask her to go baby shopping with me again."

Muffin: "Against him."

DDD: "Against Valentine?"

Valentine: "Hello. Pregnant man over here. Nobody answered my question."

DDD: "She's in my locker room Pete."

Valentine: "Thank you for finally answering my question. Now I'm off to get my baby some baby stuff." Valentine walks off.

Muffin: "Friend of yours?"

DDD: "I have no clue what he is."

(C+)

vs.

-Peter Valentine vs. Muffin-

Before the match got underway you could see Valentine asking Muffin not to kick him in his stomach so as not to hurt the baby. Muffin agreed to it with a look on her face that could only be described as 'What have I gotten myself into?' You could tell these 2 had chemistry. Somehow. A fairly competitive match that ended with Muffin coming out victorious with an Angel Driver.

Winner: Muffin in 8:31

Rating (D+)

-Backstage in the doctors office we see Giant Oak laying down with Wanda Fish next to him. Oak finally regains consciousness.

Oak: "Where am I?"

Fish: "You're in the doctors office."

Oak: "How'd I get here?"

Fish: "The EMTs dragged you here."

Oak: "Oh. You saw how I saved you from Ota though, right? I stood up to him and he left."

Fish: "Actually he knocked you out."

Oak: "Oh. But you're safe right?"

Fish: "Yes, and that was a very sweet thing of you to do."

Oak: "Yea, I know it was."

(D+)

-Shaffer is warming up backstage when Frankie Perez and Mainstream Hernandez approach him.

Perez: "You wanted us here man?"

Shaffer: "Yea. Tonight I'm going to go out there and prove you dudes wrong when I win that World title."

Hernandez: "You're just gonna get yourself killed dude."

Perez: "Yea, Mainstreams right, how is voluntarily putting yourself in a hospital bed gonna prove anything man?"

Shaffer: "I'm gonna go out there and beat Umaga. Just you two wait and see."

Perez: "Whatever dude."

(D-)

-Stranton is backstage being interviewed about Umagas upcoming match with Shaffer. Umaga is in the background pacing back and forth snorting and yelling in samoan.

Stranton: "Do we really have to talk about his match with Shaffer? I mean, let's face it, Shaffer has no chance of winning tonight. He's what I would refer to as filler. Filler until Umaga meets a real challenger, someone worthy of being destroyed by the Samoan Savage. The Demons, Giant Redwood, Freddie Datsun, none of them have been able to stop Umaga. He will rule over USPW for as long as I decide to let him rule over it. Everyone would do well to remember that."

(E+)

vs. /w

-USPW World Championship Match- Trent Shaffer vs. Umaga (c) /w Stranton-

This is your definition of glorified squash. It's not that I don't like Shaffer, it's just Umaga needs a few more squashes in his resume. Shaffer tried to fight back, but it was no use. Umaga toyed around with Shaffer for a while. Umaga picked Shaffer up and rammed him into the corner. He then lifted Shaffer up top and walked around the ring for a few seconds before looking at Stranton for approval. Umaga goes back to the corner, but Shaffer kicks him away. Umaga now charges the corner, but Shaffer catches him and delivers a tornado DDT. Both men go down.

Umaga is back to his feet by 5 and looks to Stranton for advice. Shaffer kips up behind him and when Umaga turns around, superkick. Well, close enough. Umaga catches it, spins Shaffer around and spears him down. From here on out it's all Umaga. He hits Shaffer with a Savage Charge and gets the pin.

Winner and still champion: Umaga in 7:49

Rating (D+)

-Umaga celebrates his victory with Stranton when Datsun, still bandaged up, hits the ring. Umaga and Datsun brawl before Bruce the Giant rushes, snails pace still btw, to the ring and jumps Datsun. A 2-on-1 occurs until DDD makes his way to the ring. He goes after Bruce and then Umaga. He's holding his own until Valentine gets into the ring and goes after DDD. It's 3-on-2 now. Muffin charges the ring and goes after Valentine. She knocks him out of the ring and goes after Bruce. She's not having much success until DDD steps in and helps her. Bruce rolls out of the ring.

It's now 3-on-1 against Umaga. Umaga manhandles all 3 not letting the numbers game affect him. Eventually though it becomes too much as he's clotheslined over the top. Stranton and Bruce have to hold Umaga to keep him from going back into the ring. The show comes to an end as the faces are in the ring taunting the heels who are making their way up the ramp.

(C)

Final Rating (D+)

Minor setback. Nothing to be worried over. The companies heading in the right direction though. That's all I'm gonna say.

Muffin tagged herself in and went after the now tagged in Umaga. She hit him with punch after punch, but couldn't get him down.
