USPW Blaze of Glory

Saturday, Week 2, September 2007

Held at The Louisiana Auditorium

Att. 9,711

-The Dudez!!!1!!! are backstage to hype up their upcoming ladder match with Team Seduction (Human Arsenal & Fumihiro Ota). Both Perez and Hernandez are taped up, most likely from both the spill to the outside from Family Night courtesy of DDD or the sneak attack from Team Seduction on All American Wrestling.

Hernandez: "Asking the Dudez for a ladder match was your first mistake. Next were the surprise attacks. Those got old real fast. And now your third mistake will be going through with this match tonight dudes."

Perez: "We may not be 100% tonight, but you were the ones stupid enough to totally challenge us in our own element. We're takin back the belts that rightfully belong to us tonight and there ain't nothing you gonna be able to do to stop us dudes."

(C+)

vs.

-USPW Tag Team Championship Ladder Match- The Dudez!!!1!!! vs. Team Seduction (c) w/ Seduction-

The Dudez came into this match bandaged up and hurt while it didn't seem that Ota or Arsenal were feeling any effect from the Dudez attack on them at Family Night. Although Ninjas do hide pain well.

This thing started out as a wild brawl between four men and it didn't evolve into much more than that throughout this thing. Ladders were flying around the ring and going into peoples faces and being broken alot in the early going of this match. Perez and Hernandez were definitely more at home in this match than Team Seduction were. Although Ota wasn't exactly out of place. For all of the Dudez acrobatic hi jinx Ota was there to stop them, that's why the Dudez concentrated on taking Ota out of this thing first. Hernandez tips over a ladder with Ota on it sending him to the outside. Perez gets out of the ring, sets up a table and lays Ota out on it.

Perez holds Ota on the top of the table. Hernandez gets to the top of the ladder and hits a beautiful Apparition #14 off the top of that ladder right through the table. That one's getting the million replays treatment. Hernandez and Ota were out of this for the rest of the match. Perez gets back in the ring and ducks a clothesline from Arsenal. Perez picks up a ladder and drives it into the gut of Arsenal over and over until he had Arsenal backed into a corner. Perez wedges the ladder in there to keep Arsenal in place. Perez goes to the opposite corner then charges in. Arsenal uproots the ladder and Perez jumps sternum first into one end of the ladder. The other end was driven into the gut of Arsenal.

Arsenal clutched his stomach in pain, but tried shrugging it off. Arsenal set up the ladder and started climbing. Perez followed up on the other side. When they got near the top a slugfest occurred. Arsenal won that one and knocked Perez off the ladder. Perez rolled under the bottom rope and pulled himself up. He sprang off the top rope and back onto the middle rung of the ladder. Perez climbed a step and caught Arsenal off guard with a right hand. Arsenal stopped reaching for the belt and he and Perez started to slug it out again. This time Seduction got herself involved. She grabbed onto the foot of Perez and tried to drag him down. It wasn't working too well, but that didn't matter. The distraction was long enough for Arsenal to get the belts down.

Winners: Team Seduction in 15:01

Rating (B)

vs.

-#1 Contender National Match- Steve Flash vs. Jacob Jett w/ Seduction-

This match was thrown into this slot as cooldown from the last match and I believe it accomplished its purpose.

This match was similar to the one they had at the Family Night taping from Thursday. Both men gave it their all and the match see-sawed back and forth. The veteran Flash stayed on the younger competitor and stayed in control of the match. That's when Jett began to use his speed to his advantage outrunning Flash since he can't outwrestle him. Jett also snuck in an eye rake while he was at it. Jett kept Flash down and used Seduction to make sure it stayed that way. That didn't last long though as Flash caught a second wind and nearly finished Jett off. That's when Seduction made the distraction long enough for Big Cat Brandon to come in and knock Flash out of his boots with a Big Cat Pounce. Jett made the cover, 1.....2.....3.

Winner: Jacob Jett in 16:54

Rating (C)

-We go to Sam Strongs office. Strong is watching the event on a monitor when Big Cat Brandon barges into his office. Strong gets up from his seat and stares Brandon right in the eyes. That's when Freddie Datsun pops out of nowhere and attacks Brandon with a steel pipe. Datsun lays into Brandon with that pipe and then calls a ref into the room. The match is now on.

(B)

vs.

-Last Man Standing- Big Cat Brandon vs. Freddie Datsun-

Datsun gets in another shot with the pipe and then goes for payback. He and Strong take the same bookcase Brandon dropped on Strong at Family Night and dropped it on Brandon trapping him under it. Datsun and Strong celebrated while Brandon struggled to get out from under the case. The ref made the count, but only got up to 7 before Brandon tossed the bookcase off of himself and went after Datsun. Unfortunately for Datsun he made the rookie mistake of throwing your weapon down before the opponent is done.

Brandon grabbed the pipe and hit a homerun into Datsuns gut. he then bent the pipe in half over Datsuns back. Now that Datsun was out of the way Brandon went after Strong. To Strongs credit he didn't back down, but he also didn't fare too well against Brandon. Strong stumbled out of his office and looked for something to use on the Big Cat. Brandon come out looking for Strong and as he rounded a corner a broomstick was broken over Brandons head. It barely stunned him. Strong tried some of his patented Strong punches on Brandon, but he just shrugged them off. Then Datsun came to the rescue smashing a stool over Brandons back.

Datsun was immediately back on Brandon and laid rights into his head until he was busted open. Datsun dragged Brandon through the halls tossing him into every wall he needed to to keep the Big Cat down. Datsun then saw an opportunity to finish Brandon. Datsun attempts to lift Brandon onto his shoulders, but Brandon fights him off driving his elbow into the back of Datsuns head. Brandon picks him up and spinebusters him through a table. As the ref counts Datsun out Brandon goes after Strong again. Strong tried to fight him off again and this time it was working. Strong rocked Brandon with some rights and took him down with a bionic elbow. Sam was fired up and wanted Brandon to charge him.

Brandon obliged that request and went at Strong for a Big Cat Pounce, but got cut off with a running knee lift from the American Everyman who had gotten back to his feet at 9. Datsun repeated the punch, punch, toss into the wall routine until he had gotten Brandon out to the stage. Brandon came back to life here and threw some jabs into the gut of Datsun until he had backed him off. Brandon got a head of steam and went for the Big Cat Pounce. Datsun rolls out of the way and Brandon hits Strong with a full force Big Cat Pounce right off the stage. Medical teams scrambled to him while Datsun looked on in horror.

Brandon took advantage and dropped Datsun onto the steel grating with a back drop. Brandon picks him up and tosses him with a release belly-to-belly through the Blaze of Glory set. Brandon wasn't content to leave Datsun breathing though and tossed him down the ramp until they got to ringside. Brandon tossed Datsun into the ring and then went under to look for something. He pulls out a table and sets it up outside the ring. Brandon hops up onto the apron and gets taken right off of it with a three-point tackle from Datsun sending Brandon through the table he just set up. The ref started to count Brandon out, but Brandon was back to his feet by 9. Brandon rolled into the ring and Datsun went right after him stomping on him.

Datsun picks him up and goes for the Patriot Press, but Brandon fights it. He drives his elbow into the back of Datsun as he tries to pick him up. Brandon catches Datsun and picks him up for a powerbomb. He tosses Datsun back first into the top turnbuckle. The turnbuckle breaks separating from the corner. Brandon waits for Datsun on the other side of the ring and when Datsun finally does get back up to his feet, Big Cat Pounce takes Datsun right out of the ring (Thanks to there being no top rope to stop him) and flying through the announce table. The ref checks on Datsun and starts his count,

Rating (B-)

-While the crew cleans up the ring and repairs the ring after that last match we go to Danny Jillefskis office. In walks USPW National Champion William Hayes.

Jillefski: "I'm sure you're wondering why I called you here."

Hayes: "Not really, no."

Jillefski: "Well I'll explain it to you anyways."

Hayes: "Because you like the sound of your own voice too much not to?"

Jillefski: "There are a few rules that I'm changing for your match tonight."

Hayes: "Such as...?"

Jillefski: "Such as making it impossible for you to cheat and retain that belt. For starters, the title can change hands on disqualification."

Hayes: "Well I don't plan on getting disqualified."

Jillefski: "Good for you. Number two, there will be no count-out so you can't just walk out of there to keep that title."

Hayes: "Next?"

Jillefski: "I will be watching the match. If I see you do anything wrong I will personally step in and disqualify you."

Hayes: "Anything else?"

Jillefski: "No, that'll be all."

Hayes: "Good, now I've got a match to go win."

(C)

vs.

-USPW National Championship Match- Wanda Fish vs. William Hayes (c)-

A great match as always from these two. Fish looked determined to win the belt this time while Hayes looked like he just wanted to take his frustrations out on someone.

This match was great back and forth affair like their previous matches with the challenger keeping up with the champ better than anyone might have expected. Fish went after the knees of Hayes to wear him down for the Blond Ambition, but Hayes wouldn't let her get in enough offense to get him in position for a Blonde Ambition. Hayes worked on the back of Fish wearing her down with backbreakers, sidewalk slams, elbows and knee drops. Hayes went for the Hayes Fever Backbreaker and hit it on Fish, he then tried to follow it up with a downward spiral, but Fish had it scouted. She tripped Hayes up and took him down with an STO.

Fish saw her opportunity and locked on the Blonde Ambition. Hayes screamed in pain, but was able to roll Fish over and reverse the pressure. Fish let go. She charged in at Hayes, but Hayes caught her with a powerslam. He went for the pin, but only got a 2-count. Hayes picks her up, but Fish fights back rocking Hayes with punches. Fish springs off the ropes and goes for a crossbody block, but Hayes drops down and Fish flies over him. Hayes gets back up and picks Fish up.

Hayes goes for the Slick Trick, but Fish slips out of it. She dropkicks the right knee of Hayes and he's down on one knee. Fish lays in wait, then Dish of the Day attempt ducked by Hayes. Hayes catches Fish with the Slick Trick again. This time Hayes knee buckles on him and Fish is able to get out of it. The two of them face off in the middle of the ring to a loud ovation from the crowd. Then he appears. Triple D casually makes his way down to the ring. Hayes full attention is on DDD now. Fish could capitalize right now, but doesn't. She probably wants to know what's going on here too. Devine walks to the other side of the ring, steals the National title from the timekeeper and hops into the ring.

Hayes gets in Devines face and tells him to just hit him. Devine looks at the belt and then Hayes. He winds up and goes for it. He smashes it right into the skull of Wanda Fish. Fish drops like a brick and a devious grin appears on Devines face. Hayes is shocked that Devine didn't hit him with it. Devine tosses the National title onto Fish's unconscious body as the timekeeper reads "Your winner by disqualification and NEW USPW National Champion, Wanda Fish." Devine rolls out of the ring laughing all the way up the ramp while Hayes is left to grieve.

Winner: Wanda Fish by DQ; Fish wins the title in 24:48

Rating (B)

-It's time for the champion to comment on his upcoming title defense.

DDD: "Anybody know what it's like to be singled out and called a menace? To be considered by all he used to call his friends a complete a-hole? That's what the last couple of weeks have been like for me. Everywhere I go everyone's all like 'Champ, champ, why ever did you do it? Why did you drive that nice shiny center plate into the skull of Muffin, the woman you supposedly loved? Didn't you know you might mess up the belt over her thick skull?'

My answer is simple. I didn't turn my back on her. I was set up, by William Hayes of all people. And do you know why this happened? It happened to make me look like the bad guy in all of this. Hayes is a conniving son of a bitch. I know deep down in my heart that she cheated on me long before that tape aired. I don't have proof, I didn't hire someone to stalk her like someone did to me, but I know it happened. I'm not stupid, I'm not blind, I'm not ignorant and I'm not oblivious to the obvious. I knew she was cheating on me with Hayes, then that video played. It painted me out to be cheating on her. And do you know what I did next?

I got my revenge. I don't care if I sent you to the hospital Muffin, I don't care if I end your career tonight. You deserve whatever bad things happen to you. That's karma bitch."

(B-)

Muffin: " I've got alot of things I'd like to say to Darryl right now, but I'd rather say them to his face. This was a man I loved for 5 months of my life. I loved him despite knowing he was cheating on his girlfriend with me and that I was the other woman. And then a few weeks ago I find out he was cheating on me too. I feel horrible about that, it makes me want to cry. But I'm not going to. Devine wants to resort to violence, then I'll resort to violence too. I'm capable of alot more in the ring than you ever gave me credit for Darryl and I'll prove that to you when I take the World Championship away from you.

There's only one thing I want to know though Darryl, why did you do it? Why did you cheat on me? And for the record, I never cheat on you with Hayes."

(B)

vs.

-USPW World Championship Match- Muffin vs. Triple D (c)-

Holy flying monkeys on a pogo stick Batman! This match exceeded all expectations for it. Can anyones say new best match?

The crowd was electric for this one before it even began. The ref took the World title belt and raised it up in the air behind Muffin and Devine while the timekeeper read over the rules for this match. Devine and Muffin were in each others faces, Devine talking a big talk. Then Muffin spit in his face, payback for Family Night. Devine looks pissed and decks her with a right and the match is jump started.

Devine goes right to work on Muffin stomping his boots into her as hard as he can and trying to finish her off quick. He pulled her up and went for Devine Dream Drop, but Muffin shoved him away. Devine turned around to get clocked by a Strong Arm Tactic (running elbow). Devine goes down and Muffin takes control of the match. She's able to keep Devine down and keep him under control. She wears him down, but Devine isn't ready to call it quits yet. As Muffin tries to pick him up again Devine grabs hold of her mask and pulls it to the side blinding her.

Devine doesn't take long to take advantage of that planting Muffin with an implant DDT. Devine makes the cover, but Muffin kicks out at 2. As she tries to fix her mask Devine continues to take advantage of her. He lets go of her only to come back at her with a running knee lift to the face. Devine taunts the crowd. Devine picks the still blinded Muffin up by the hair and sets her up for a reverse DDT. He drops her neck down across his knee then picks her up and drives her down with a reverse DDT. Devine goes for the cover, but Muffin kicks out at 2.

Devine showboats to the crowd some more. This time it costs him. Muffin fixes her mask and sweeps Devines feet out from under him. Muffin gets right on him hitting him with lefts and rights to the head. Devine covers up and shoves Muffin away. Muffin gets back to her feet and send Devine back down with a clothesline. Devine gets up again and gets taken back down with a drop kick. Muffin finishes him off with a Strong Arm Tactic. She picks Devine up and plants him with an Angel Driver. Muffin makes the pin,
