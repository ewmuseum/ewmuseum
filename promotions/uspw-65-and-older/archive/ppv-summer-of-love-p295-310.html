Main Show

-The show opens with a video package for Summer of Love. The card is run down from bottom to top covering all the matches and the reasons for them. Force is out to beat the Hotshots in honor of Mr. Squiggles. Muffin takes on Jett as a result of her not joining Team Seduction. Bulldozer Brandon Smith takes on Wanda Fish because Oak can't keep his big mouth shut. Bruce the Giant takes on Umaga after being kicked out of Strantons group. The Dudez!!!1!!! put their Tag Team Championships on the line against Team Seduction as they try to take the gold. Freddie Datsun will take Sam Strongs place in a hardcore match against Big Cat Brandon that stems from Jillefskis anger of being kicked out of the announce booth on national television.

And in our main event of the evening the USPW World Champion Triple D will put his title on the line against USPW National Champion William Hayes in what is sure to go down as a classic. What caused Hayes' hatred of Devine in the first place is still unknown, but Devine shares the same hatred of Hayes now. The driving force in this feud though has been both mens relationship to Muffin. Tonight though, it's all about the gold, possibly.

-USPW World Championship Match- William Hayes vs. Triple D (c)-

Yup, definitely goes down as my best match yet and the best USPW has seen since the Law/ Money feud.

The crowd was electric for this match before the bell had even rang. The match started with the two competitors staring holes through each other, then fists flew. Hayes and Devine took no regard for the fact that this was supposed to be a wrestling match. They brawled around the ring and quickly took the fight to the outside of the ring trying to find whatever they could do to inflict the most pain on their opponent. The steel steps were used, the steel corner post was used, the guard rail was used and the steel grating of the entrance ramp was used. Both men took turns rolling in and out of the ring to stop a count-out. Both men got back in the ring at around the 7 minute mark and they already looked like they had been through a war.

Devine and Hayes stood off. Hayes challenged Devine to lock up with him and try to outwrestle him. Devine obliged. The two locked up in the middle of the ring and what had been an all out brawl only minutes before was a game of one upsmanship as both Hayes and Devine went back and forth with holds. The two seemed evenly match, but Hayes took control of Devine fairly easily this way. Devine knew he wasn't going to win this way and sent some elbows into Hayes' temple. Devine sprang off the ropes and knocked Hayes down wit ha Devine Intervention (Springboard back elbow).

From here the match see-sawed back and forth as Hayes and Devine are both quite evenly matched and both competitors have something to fight for.
