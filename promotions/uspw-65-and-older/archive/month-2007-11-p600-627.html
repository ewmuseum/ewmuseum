Main Show

-We open the show with the music of former USPW Champion Umaga playing, but it's not playing for Umaga, it's playing for Umagas manager the returning Jacob Stranton. Stranton comes out in crutches accompanied by Umaga. Stranton makes his way into the ring and grabs a mic before calling now owner of USPW Danny Jillefski out.

Stranton: “About a week ago I stumbled onto something Jillefski didn't want me to and I'm sure you all know what that is by now. So to keep me quiet he sic that psychotic freak of nature on me and take me out of commission. Problem is, it's not permanent. I'm here tonight to challenge you Jillefski. Why don't you come out here and accept it.”

And Jillefski makes his way out onto the stage with Big Cat Brandon, Eddie Peak and Akima by his side.

Jillefski: “So let me guess, you want it to be Umaga vs Peak tonight. I can assure Jacob that simply isn't going to happen. I mean-”

Stranton: “I wasn't challenging Peak.”

Jillefski: “So then you wanted Umaga vs-”

Stranton: “No. I said -I- wanted to challenge -you-. Let me go slower for you as you apparently need me to. I, Jacob Stranton challenge you, Daniel Jillefski to a wrestling match in this very ring tonight on USPW Family Night. Was that clear enough for you?”

Jillefski: “They let you leave the hospital all drugged up, didn't they? You're on crutches Stranton. You want me so badly, I'll take tonight as my chance to get rid of you for good.”

Stranton: “So you accept? It's set in stone?”

Jillefski: “Yes.”

Stranton: “Good. Then I can get rid of these crutches, they've done their job. I'll see you later Jillefski.”

And the Mean one finds himself on TV once more. Only to get squashed by our most Unappreciated and agitated of champions. Remind me to find something of a character for Jean other than 'angry guy.' Anyways Dozer didn't struggle to put Cattley away with the Dozer Driver in a matter of minutes.

Rating (D+)

-The ref hands Dozer his title and tries to raise his hand, but Dozer pulls his hand away and heads for the ropes when the ring starts to fill up with a green smoke. The arena lights start to dim until they go out completely. A spotlight shines into the ring focusing on Dozer. The arena lights start to come up but of an ominous dark green shade. Dozer looks around trying to figure out what's going on until he sees what he's looking for. Perched on the top rope in the corner of the ring is a man with long black hair and red streaks through it. He's wearing a mask that covers his nose and mouth and a spiked choker around his neck. The man looks at Dozer and points to him or more specifically his title. Dozer lays it down in the middle of the ring and dares the man to come get it, but he just stays perched in the corner.

vs.

-Peter Valentine vs. Jacob Jett-

Okay, either Jett needs to be pushed or Valentine is starting to become a competent wrestler. Since there's absolutely no chance it's the latter I guess it's time for Jett to get a push. The match was a pretty two-sided contest despite what Jillefski probably wanted to see. Jett took Valentine down with a tornado DDT and then finished him with a Jettison 450 getting him the 3.

Winner: Jacob Jett in 10:07

-Team Seduction vs. Umaga & Freddie Datsun-

Not quite up to the standards I was hoping for as I kinda hoped this match would save the rest of the show in terms of match quality. Unfortunately a Peter Valentine match was just as good as this one. That's sad. The match was pretty standard fare as Arsenal spent the most time in the ring in this match pretty much trying to beat these two former USPW champs alone. In the end Arsenal had to tag out to Ota who came in and was beaten fairly soundly by Datsun and Umaga after a Savage Charge from Umaga cut him in half.

Rating (C+)

vs.

-Danny Jillefski vs. Jacob Stranton-

The entrance music of Divinity comes on the arena speakers as USPW owner Danny Jillefski makes his way down to the ring accompanied by Akima, Big Cat Brandon and Eddie Peak. Jillefski gets into the ring and awaits his opponent. Umagas music comes on now to signal the arrival of Jacob Stranton who makes his way to the ring with Umaga, Bruce the Giant and Freddie Datsun with him. The ref clears the extra 6 men out of the ring before sounding the bell and starting this match.

In the opening second Jillefski puts all his weight into it and goes for a right hand to Stranton, but Stranton sidesteps it and delivers a punch of his own to Jillefski. Jillefski goes down and immediately retreats to a corner. He pulls the ref between he and Stranton as Brandon trips Stranton from the outside. Jillefski shoves the ref out of his way and is immediately all over Stranton punching and stomping away. Jillefski stops for a moment to either taunt or admire his 'fans', I'm not really sure which, but as he does Stranton grabs his foot and pulls him down to the ground in the middle of the ring. Brandon distracts the ref as Akima gets in and pulls Stranton off. Umaga doesn't like it and goes right after Akima. Datsun meanwhile goes straight after Brandon and Bruce goes for Peak. The ref sees all of this and throws the match out.

Despite that the six men brawling continue to as Jillefski and Stranton retreat to a safe distance, Stranton in the entranceway and Jillefski behind the announce tables. It looks as though with the help of weapons Jillefskis team has taken an advantage over Datsun, Umaga and Bruce. That's when cavalry arrives in the form of Sam and Alicia Strong. Jillefski is enraged that Sam is even in the building and sends his security guards after him. Muffin and Strong take out Brandon first with a double Strong Arm Tactic and then get in the ring to take care of Akima. Jillefski security guards as well as Peak surround the Strongs as the lights go out. When they come back on 30 seconds later the security guards are all taken out. Sam, Alicia and Umaga stand tall on the inside while Datsun and Bruce corner Peak on the outside. Datsun takes a steel chair and nails Peak with it before Bruce picks him up by the throat and drives him down onto that chair.

Sam asks for a mic and gets one from the timekeeper.

Strong: "Well brother, it looks like we took care of your personal security. Now you're gonna hear me out. You screwed me over and took control of my company. Now you and I both know that ain't gonna last long brother, so I'm making a proposal to you now. Me versus you at Winter Epiphany for control of USPW. You win and I let you keep control of the company, I win and it's mine again. What do you say brother?"

Jillefski steals a mic from the timekeeper and jumps over the barrier.

Jillefski: "Tempting offer Sam, but how about no?"

The lights in the arena go out again and once they're back up we see The Masked Man has his arm securely wrapping Jillefski by the neck. The MM speaks into the mic "I would listen to the offer again Daniel. Or else..."

Jillefski: "Okay okay, you have a match Sam. You have a match. I agree on the terms, but I don't wrestle. You want control you'll have to beat the Big Cat for that. And you know what, since you want to make matches tonight let's make a few more. Bruce, you're going to be taking on Eddie Peak... in a hardcore match. And Muffin, little Alicia, you get to wrestle Akima. That sound like a fun night to you Sam? Happy? Now let go of me you masked freak!"

The lights go out and the Masked Man is gone again.

And to make this story even more pathetic they've challenged each other to see who could get a job with a major promotion first. That's one of those bets that nobody wins. Like who can drink the most battery acid?

One more month down, endless more to go. I sat in my office scribbling over the paper that I was supposed to be writing this coming All American Wrestling on when I looked at the calender. At that moment it struck me, I've been at this lousy job for almost a f*cking year. At the conclusion of Winter Epiphany It will have been a year since that raffle changed my life. I'm still not sure whether to consider that a good thing. I mean I've only made $10 and stolen $102.35. Oh, and I was given $3 Canadian, but let's face it, that's worthless. That's barely enough to pay my electric bill. Which is precisely why I had to steal cable from the neighbors. I'm sure they'll understand. If they ever find out.

This job has given me something to do with my time. And I've made almost a quarter a day, stealings included. But it has left my relationship with my girlfriend a bit strained. I had to sleep in the doghouse last night. That's not a metaphor, I mean I literally had to sleep in the dog's house because she wouldn't let me in. Don't worry about the dog though, he's dead. Stupid chihuahua. Always biting my ankles. And through this job I've made plenty of friends like... Tiny. And ummm... Sam? Well I've got plenty of friends anyways. Who needs more? And I'm close to actually getting paid for work. Only one A* show on the ARSEHOLES scale and I've got me a salary. And a raise coming with it. Son of a bitch won't be able to pay me less than minimum wage either.

And most difficult, I've taken the butt end of every wrestling promotion joke and turned it into the most prestigious wrestling company in the US. Now that's an accomplishment. Now if only I got paid for it. Or didn't have Redwood being given credit for it for the last 6 months. USPW is leading the polls for promotion of the year as well as feud of the year with Devine/Hayes. Peak/Datsun is all but guaranteed match of the year and Devine is without a doubt one of the top five most popular wrestlers in the US. And all of this is because of me. Kind of leaves me with a feeling of accomplishment.

Now if only that came with a feeling of a full wallet.
