-We start this show off in Danny Jillefskis office. Jillefski is sitting behind his desk with Big Cat Brandon standing behind him looking as menacing as always despite his loss at aHiA. Jillefski prepares himself to make an address to the viewers and locker room by clearing his throat and preparing a pre-written statement.

Jillefski: "Hello ladies and gentlemen, boys and girls, people of all ages and welcome to this edition of USPW All American Wrestling. Tonight will be a very special night here because tonight, as acting general manager, I will be in charge around here. For those wondering this is because Sam Strong will not be here tonight due to the ass kicking that was inflicted upon him because he challenged our USPW Champion Triple D for his own selfish desires. Alicia Strong, better known to most of you as Muffin will also not be here tonight.

Tonight I'll be instituting 3 matches right now. Our opening contest will be for #1 contendership to the Unappreciated Championship and it will be between Brett Hall and Brendon Idol. Also tonight will be the two matches our World Champion, Triple D will be announcing involving the Hotshots. As for the rest of the night I will be here to make sure everything runs smoothly and we provide you the best show we possibly can."

-Unappreciated #1 Contenders Match- Brendon Idol vs. Brett Hall-

This is one of those rare matches that starts with a hand shake between the two competitors. And after that Hall nearly took off Idols head with a big boot leading to a two count. Hall took charge of the match early on and was able to keep it throughout most of the match. Idol did have his moments to shine though. Hall attempted to end the match with a Butterfly Powerbomb, but Idol come down on him with a flurry of punches into Halls skull. Hall dropped Idol down to his feet. Idol sprang off the ropes, Hall tries a big boot, Idol ducks under it. Crossbody by Idol gets Hall down for a two count, but Hall just bench presses Idol of the top of him and goes back to work determined to get another shot at Bulldozer.

And speaking of the Unappreciated Champion, He made his way down to the ring at around the twelve minute mark. Hall was instantly distracted by him. Idol capitalized by going to the top rope and nailing Hall with a missile dropkick that takes Hall down. Idol goes for the cover, but he again only gets a two count. Now is where Bulldozer gets into the ring with his trademark chain wrapped around his fist. The ref tries to get him to leave, but Dozer just shoves Jez out of the way and nails his target with a shot to the head. Jez rings the bell and disqualifies Hall as Idol lay on the canvas with a chain impression on his forehead.

Winner by DQ: Brendon Idol in 13:21

Rating (D-)

Dozer stands over his challenger for Pilgrimage confident his title isn't going anywhere. That's when Hall clotheslines Dozer from behind and takes him out of the ring. Hall challenges Dozer to get back in, but Dozer just walks away.

Vessey nervously awaited his opponent tonight in the at one-time unstoppable Samoan Savage. Once they were back in the ring Umaga dominated the young Vessey. Somewhere in Japan Bryan and Larry are cursing at their televisions. Cameron tried to fight for his life, but it was all proving unsuccessful. Umaga broke Vessey in half with a Savage Spear, but didn't make the cover. Instead he decided to toy with him the same way someone would play with their food. Too bad that analogy could be taken literally here. Umaga just toyed with Vessey trying to inflict as much pain on him as possible. Umaga set Vessey up in the corner for a Savage Spear. He charged in, but Vessey had timed it perfectly.

As Umaga charged in Vessey rolled over the back of Umaga and caught him with a schoolboy. Vessey put his feet on the ropes for good measure and the ref made the count, 1.....2.... Umaga kicks out. Vesseys face as he gets up has that 'I'm screwed look to it, and sure enough he was. As soon as Umaga got up he nailed Vessey with a Savage Spear and made the cover, 1....2.....3.

Winner: Umaga in 9:54

vs.

-Rudy Velasquez vs. Big Cat Brandon-

Can you say squash? Well, apparently Velasquez can't. Velasquez fought hard against Big Cat Brandon in this match and at times it looked as if we might be in the middle of one of the biggest upsets we've ever seen. Brandon still dominated a majority of this match although Velasquez really did look like he was fighting with a newfound fire in him. The most impressive spot in this match took place as the Big Cat charged at Velasquez for the Big Cat Pounce, but Velasquez caught him mid-move and dropped him with a Street Cutter. It looked as if Velasquez was going to win, but the Big Cat threw Rudy halfway across the ring after only a two count.

Velasquez fought valiantly after that, but he was no match for Brandon. As Rudy tried to hit Brandon with a hurricanrana Brandon stopped him mid move and drilled him into the canvas with a Brandon Bomb Drop. The 3 count was inevitable after that.

Winner: Big Cat Brandon in 13:09

Then I realize that baby is 5'4" and has more wrinkles than my shirt (And that's pretty damn wrinkly). His tits are sagging too. Yes, USPW is like the geriatric of the wrestling world, actually it IS the geriatric of the wrestling world. It's the grandfather you never visit in the old folks home. It's like the old man that always yells at the kids to stay off his grass. It's like the grandpa in your family that never makes any sense and mixes up names all the time (I'm Jacob, not Melissa dammit!). USPW truly is the geriatric of the wrestling world and it seems to be my job to find the Fountain of Youth for it. I hate my job.
