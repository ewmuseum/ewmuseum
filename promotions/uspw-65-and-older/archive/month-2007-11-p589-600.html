USPW All American Wrestling

Monday, Week 3, November 2007

Held at Irvine Hall

Att. 3740 (Sell-Out)

Dark Matches

Main Show

-And we kick off our post-Pilgrimage show with the new owner of USPW and former GM Danny Jillefski in the ring surrounded by his bodyguards Big Cat Brandon, Eddie Peak and Akima. The crowd clearly detests Jillefski and his actions as they pelt the ring with garbage. Jillefski grabs a mic and speaks.

Jillefski: "Is that any way to treat the man who's providing this show to you? You all paid to see MY company now sit down, shut up and pay attention. Saturday night I did what I said I was going to do Sam, I humiliated you on national TV the same way you did to me. You fired me from the announcing table, I fired you from your own company. I always win Sam, maybe whoever wants to mess with me next will remember that little lesson. Isn't that right Stranton? I know you're watching this right now as you you sip your supper through a straw. And now I'd like to call out those who've helped me on this journey to the top, Divinity."

The first one out to that ring after the announcement happens to be the World Champion Triple D. After him comes Seduction accompanied by Human Arsenal, Fumihiro Ota, Davey Wayne, Cameron Vessey, Jacob Jett and Art Reed. Now that everyone is in the ring Jillefski continues on.

Jillefski: "Things around here are going to be run a little differently from now on. Of course Divinity will receive absolutely no special treatment in this. Of course. I've made a few approvals for tonights show so now I'll announce what you can all look forward to tonight. The Valentines have been granted their rematch for the Tag Team titles tonight against The Hotshots. I've also granted three requests as I'm such a nice guy. The first of which is from our very own World Champion. Tonight Eddie Peak will face off against Art Reed. If Reed loses he's out of Divinity."

Seduction looks outraged at this news while Devine has what's becoming his trademark c0cky grin on his face. Art Reed on the other hand has gone a bit pale.

"I'm sorry Seduction, but he does have a point to it. We can't have losers in Divinity. As for my second request, this one by Seduction, Human Arsenal will compete in a non-title match against Triple D." Devines c0ckiness quickly turns to outrage while Arsenal has a grin on his face. "Sorry Darryl, but you did cost him his title. He deserves a chance to get even. Fair is fair. As for my last request I've decided to grant it as a last wish type of thing. At the end of the night Sam Strong has been given time to make his farewell speech. That will be all for tonight."

(C+)

vs.

-Chance Fortune vs. Zeus Maxmillion-

Two tag teamers with nothing else to do put in a match against each other. What? You expect something that matters in this slot? You obviously haven't been reading for long. Zeus and Chance were one even ground throughout this entire match, but TBTTLE pulled through in the end. Shaffer distracted the ref, and Fortune hit Zeus with a low blow into a roll-up with a hand full of tights, 1....2....3.

Winner: Chance Fortune in 6:35

Rating (C+)

-Freddie Datsun is in the halls backstage with Muffin who's clearly broken up about everything that's happened to her father. Freddie Datsun's there trying to console her on it.

Datsun: "Every thing's going to be alright Alicia."

Muffin: "No it won't, my father was fired from his own company, William got fired because of me and Darryl's the biggest a**hole in the world."

Datsun: "He didn't get fired because of you."

Muffin: "Yes he did, he got fired for standing up to Darryl for me. There's only one thing I can do to avenge them and you know it. I have to beat Darryl and get that belt off him."

Jillefski: "Oh please, Devine's so caught up with the Masked Man I doubt he even remembers you exist. I must give your boyfriend credit though Alicia, he's driven Devine off the deep end."

Muffin: "William isn't the Masked Man."

Jillefski: "Of course you'd deny it."

Muffin: "Let the jackass believe what he wants then. And Danny, I'd doubt that he would forget the girl that beat him in the middle of the ring live on PPV."

Jillefski: "Not according to the record books. Now have a nice night both of you. Oh, and if either of you even think about going to the ring tonight during your fathers farewell speech think again."

(C)

vs.

-USPW Tag Team Championship Match- The Valentines vs. The Hotshots (c)-

The Valentines stood a lot more of a fighting chance this time as opposed to last night. This time they weren't tired out before the match even began. But on the other hand neither were The Hotshots. Casey started the match out in control of Vessey. Casey worked him over decently before Vessey got the tag in to Wayne. The Hotshots and Casey went back and forth for a few minutes until Peter got the hot tag and cleaned house... for about 10 seconds. Davey hit Peter with a German suplex and both men were down. They crawled to their corners and made the tag to their partners. In the end Vessey hit Casey with a VesseyPlex II and picked up the win making quick work of The Valentines once more.

Winners: The Hotshots in 6:16

Rating (C)

Vessey: "Ah man, why couldn't we just get jail time?"

Wayne: "Or a better lawyer."

ATTACK THEM AND AVENGE YOUR FALLEN AVIATOR AMIGO FROM THE ALL FORCEFUL AFTERLIFE!!!! HOTSHOOOOOOOTS, YOU SHALL FEEL A FINAL FORCEFUL FOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOORCE FOR YOUR DEMIIIIIIIIIISE!!!!!!!!!!!!!!"

Force lets go of the leash holding Mr. Snuggleforce, a vicious white tiger who's had to put up with Jim Force his whole life, back and The Hotshots look like deer caught in the headlights. Mr. Snuggleforce lets out a roar and The Hotshots high tail it out of there as they're chased by Mr. Snuggleforce. Wayne and Vessey run through the halls screaming for help for hundreds of feet until a curious Seduction opens her door to see what all the ruckus is about. Wayne and Vessey dive into the room and kick the door shut as Mr. Snuggleforce passes by.

Seduction: "What.. the hell?"

Wayne: "I think I'm just gonna pass out now."

Wayne: "How the hell does he keep getting these animals anyways?"

Wayne: "Somebodies gotta do something about this. Seduction, could you do something about this?"

Fish: "Phlexie, it's you."

Phlex: "She was never your girl you little pansy."

Fish walks out of the bathroom a minute later out of cheerleader outfit and now in jeans and a Dungeons & Dragons t-shirt.

Fish: "I'm ready. Let's go Phlexie."

Phlex: "Allow me to get the door for you my lady."

Phlex opens up the door to leave, but he and Fish stop as they hear the roaring coming down the hall. Jim Force and Mr. Snuggleforce run down the hall in front of them. "SNIFF THEM OUT MR. SNUGGLEFOOOOOOOOOOOORCE!!!!!!!!!!! THEY MUST PAY WITH THEIR BLOOOOOOOOOOOD!!!!!!!"

Fish: "Maybe we should just order a pizza."

(B-)

vs.

-Eddie Peak vs. Art Reed-

A match between two DAVE guys on USPW TV. A year ago who woulda thunk it? And despite these two not clicking this was still a great TV match.

As you would imagine Peak dominated most of the match, but Reed showed a fighting spirit that he hasn't before and did his part to hold Peak off. Peak physically dominated Reed throughout but Reed was able to use his technical expertise in the ring to gain control of the match. It was a contrast of two very different styles and in the end Peak went for the Peak of Perfection (Crucifix Powerbomb), but Reed was able to slip out of it and take Peak down with a Dark Matter. He immediately went for the Dread Lock and put it on Peak in the middle of the ring. Peak crawled for the ropes, but Reed kept pulling him back knowing this was his only chance. After three minutes in the hold Peak finally tapped out.

Winner: Art Reed in 20:33

Rating (B)

-The ref raises Reeds hand after his surprise victory over The Hardcore Assassin Eddie Peak. Too bad he turned his back on a very dangerous individual. Peak grabbed Reed and drilled him into the mat with a Peak of the Devil. Peak punched away at Reeds forehead until he was busted open. Peak then got out of the ring and looked under it. He tossed in a table, two chairs, a trash can, a bag of something and the steel steps. Danny Jillefski comes down to ringside accompanied by Big Cat Brandon as Peak slides back into the ring. He sets the table up in the corner and tosses Reed into it with a Peak of Perfection snapping the table, and possibly Reed in half. Jillefski sets a table up on the outside while Brandon gets in the ring, head for the bag, opens it and pours a massive amount of tacks onto the table on the outside. He then sets up the steel steps to elevate himself all while Peak madly claws away at Reeds face. Brandon pulls Peak off him and then drags him over to the steps. Brandon picks up Reed for a powerbomb, climbs the stairs and sends him through a tack filled table onto the padded concrete floor below.

As Brandon and Peak marvel at their destruction the World Champ makes his way out with a mic in hand. He walks up to Reed and begins.

DDD: "Tsk tsk tsk. I know what the stipulations were for your match Reed, If you lost you're out of Divinity, but you just had to win. Now look at you. You're in no condition to represent Divinity. You're out. I know you can't hear me right now with being unconscious and all, but I'm sure somebody will play this back for you at your hospital room."

Datsun: (To Umaga) "I'm kinda glad to have you on our side now."

Umaga: "Ooga booga."

(C+)

-Human Arsenal vs. Triple D-

As you would imagine this was a fantastic match and a special treat for our TV viewers to help them get an idea of what they're missing on our PPVs. The match was a roller coaster as Arsenal and Devine were on much more even ground than the champ would ever willingly let on.

The two of them went back and forth for over twenty minutes unleashing everything in their arsenals to try to take the other one down for the three. Devine wouldn't go down as he had too much to lose by losing any match, especially after claiming that Divinity needs him more than he needs them as nobody can beat him. Arsenal had a lot to prove too as he was taking on the task of trying to prove to Devine that he wasn't unstoppable. Arsenal would come close to achieving that goal more than a few times through the match, but Devine came close to winning many a time too. By the end of the match the crowd were all on their feet cheering Arsenal on in hopes Devine would get what's coming to him. Arsenal grabbed Devine for the Ammo Dump, but Devine back body dropped him off of him and then waited. Once Arsenal was positioned just right he hit the Divine Dream Drop on him and made the cover, 1.....2.....3. Devine takes the win.

Winner: Triple D in 23:54

Rating (B+)

-We're back from our last commercial break for the night and the ring has been decorated with obvious thanks to Divinity. The red carpet has been rolled out in the ring as well as a podium in the middle of the ring. In the corners facing the hard camera are two pictures of Strong, his loss to Devine at AHiA and his loss at Pilgrimage. Above the ring hangs a banner saying 'Good Riddance Sam'. The unmistakable entrance music of Sam Strong flares up over the arena speakers for the last time as Sam makes his way out to the ring. The crowd cheer 'Thank you Sam' in unison as he comes down to the ring and gets in. He grabs a mic as they continue to cheer for him. He waits a minute for the crowd to settle down before speaking.

Strong: "Boy, when Divinity go all out on decorating expenses they go all out brother."

Before Sam can get anything more out Danny Jillefski appears on the stage clapping for Sam while accompanied by Brandon, Peak and Akima.

Jillefski: "You know, it takes a big man to stand in the middle of the ring and admit when he's been bested so why don't you do that now Sam? Well? What are you waiting for? I gave you an invitation."

Strong: "Ya know brother, you got a lot of something to come out here in front of all these Strongamaniacs and try to get me to be your little ventriloquist dummy. Now you caught me unaware brother, that I'll admit, but my lawyers are working round the clock to get that document saying that you're in charge now voided."

Jillefski: "By that time though Sam the damage will be done and you'll willingly sign this company over to me."

Strong: "Over my dead body brother."

Jillefski: "Watch what you say Sam because that could be arranged, very easily too."

Peak, Brandon and Akima run down to the ring while Sam pushes over the podium, takes off his suit jacket and rolls up his sleeves welcoming the three into the ring. Strong Punch for Akima, Strong Punch for Peak, and then... Big Cat Pounce for Strong. Immediately the cavalry arrives in the form of Muffin, Alicia Strong. Muffin runs past Jillefski and into the ring. She takes Brandon down with a Strong Arm Tactic and then takes Akima back to the mat with a dropkick. She puts Peak on the defense with forearm shots to the head, but out of nowhere Devine spins her around and drops her with a Divine Dream Drop. The four now stomp away at the Strongs until the lights go out.

When they come back up thirty seconds later the ring is cleared out of Peak, Brandon and Akima and in the middle of the ring stands the Masked Man with a chair in hand. Devine looks furious, but at the same time happy in an odd sadistic way as he charges at the Masked Man. The MM ducks under one of Devine punches and hits him with a flurry of rights before taking him down with a snap suplex. The lights go out again and once they're back up Devine is kneeling alone in the ring, the Masked Man gone along with Sam and Alicia Strong.

USPW Weekend Update

-A new contract expiration season has begun and no one in the 'Big 4' is considered to be above a cult status. Most wrestlers are waiting out the down period in the US and only agreeing to PPA contracts. Some big contracts are coming up soon too. But I won't say who.

-And speaking of contracts, the USPW contract of Peter Valentine came up recently. He was of course resigned. When USPW head booker Jacob Stranton was asked about how much Valentine was making his answer was "Enough." No preferential treatment for a friend of Strongs indeed.

-Ricky Dale Johnson, formerly of TCW fame is still jobless. He doesn't like any company in the US and nobody outside the US likes him. Set your standards lower buddy.

-Champagne Lover has once again turned down negotiations from USPW feeling the US is not a good place for his career. You know, seeing as how he isn't a big soap opera star here. I guess 'All My Chihuahuas' doesn't translate well to US audiences.

-Seeing as how title changes don't get covered too often in this column, and I'm running out of material, I'll go back in time and cover a few that haven't been.

The TCW World Tag Team Champions are... Wrestling Machine #2 (Hill) and ........ Texas Pete?!?!? No wonder TCW ain't getting out of cult status anytime soon.

Nothing to really cover in SWF. DeBones is still on top, Money's got the next best thing and the tag division is suffering from Sexual Aggression. Oh, and Groucho Bling is the first champ of their new low level title.

Scout is a double champ over in DAVE holding the Tag Team titles with Guide and the Brass Knux title. Meanwhile Chris Caulfield still won't drop the Unified title. Also, to those who watch USPW and see Peak more and more, he's still a DAVE exclusive. Just wanting to clear that up.

Not much change in NYCW, but hasn't that always been a problem? Flash has upgraded from the Regional Championship to the Empire title. Rick Sanders is a double champ holding that Regional title and the Tag title with partner Honest Frank.

Over in MAW Rip Chord still claims sobriety. And nothing has changed in MAW all year. Parker and Natural Storm still reign supreme. And I said NYCW doesn't know what change is.

The Coastal Zone Championship has seen some change this year. Donnie J started off the year with it before losing it to Devine, yes, that Devine. Devine then left CZCW to concentrate on his USPW career. (A year ago I never thought I'd be saying that.) The title was vacated and picked up by head booker Shawn Gonzalez. A booker giving himself a title? Unheard of, I know. The Tag Team titles were picked up by the team of Ultimate Phoenix and Matt Sparrow. I swear if that team is not named 'Birds of a Feather' I'm slapping somebody. And the Xtreeeeeeeeeeme!!!!! title is around James Prudences waist. no one has yet to care.

And that ought to satisfy you all for another 6-7 months.

And as a reminder, down from hundreds of possible choices, one of these four men is in fact under the mask.
