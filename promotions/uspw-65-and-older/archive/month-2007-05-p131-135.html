USPW All American Wrestling

Saturday, Week 4, May 2007 (Airs Wednesday)

Held at The Washington State Gymnasium

Att.2,000 (Sold Out)

Dark Matches

-Bulldozer Brandon Smith defeated Giant Oak and Insane Machine in 6:46 when Bulldozer Brandon Smith defeated Insane Machine by pinfall with an Inverted Piledriver. (D-)

-Top Secret Dark Match (D+)

Main Show

-The show kicks off once more in Danny Jillefskis office. Standing in front of his desk are Jacob Stranton and Muffin.

Jillefski: "I'm sure you both wonder why I called you here. I'd like to announce tonights main event and I wanted the both of you here." As Jillefski speaks it seems clear that Muffin has put 2 and 2 together and figured out the announcement by the look of worry on here face. "Tonight we're going to have a rematch from a few weeks ago. Tonight Muffin will take on Umaga in the main event."

Muffin: "Why do I have to face Umaga tonight?"

Jillefski: "Because I said so. If it's any consolation though It is a title match."

Muffin: "Fine."

Jillefski: "And by the way, if anyone interferes they'll be punished. For example, let's saaaay, Triple D for instance interferes tonight. If he does I'll take away his title shot against Umaga in 2 days at Family Night."

Muffin: "I don't need any help."

Muffin leaves the office and slams the door on her way out. Once she's gone Stranton slips an envelope to Jillefski. Stranton: "It was nice doing business with ya."

(C-)

vs.

-T-Rex vs. Freddie Datsun-

If anyone remembers that these two didn't click, you have a better memory than me. This is sort of a theme for tonight too btw. Anyways, the bigger T-Rex tried to keep the Everyman down with his power, but the veteran fought through it and dropped Rex with a Patriot Press.

Winner: Freddie Datsun in 4:46

Rating (D-)

-Datsun celebrates his win when Zeus Maxmillion gets in the ring. Datsun gets into his fighting stance, but Zeus tells him to relax as he gets a mic.

Zeus: "Now, you've beaten both of my stars. I give you credit for that which is why I'd like you to star in my next movie."

Datsun grabs a mic of his own. "What are you talking about?"

Zeus: "Every great director should pad out their resume, and I figure I could do a movie about an in-bred pretty easily."

Datsun: "Are you calling me an in-bred?"

Zeus: "Should I speak slower for you?"

Datsun: "No, I'm not stupid and I don't want to be a part of your movie either."

Zeus: "I figured you wouldn't realize what an opportunity you have in front of you. Seeing as how if you want a job done right you have to do it yourself I challenge you to a match in one months time from now."

Datsun: "You're on."

(D-)

-Wanda Fish is in her locker room on her laptop when Giant Oak, mop over shoulder barges in. Wanda closes the laptop and greets Oak.

Fish: "Oakie, you're back already."

Oak: "I'm done."

Fish: "Goodie."

Oak: "I mean it. I'm done with these chores. I've been working for the last 5 hours."

Fish: "Hard works builds character."

Oak: "By character do you mean blisters, because I got those."

Fish: "Well I got a surprise for you."

Oak closes his eyes and puckers up. Pretty obvious what he thinks it is.

Fish: "But I'll wait until next week to tell you."

Oak: "What? I did all that work for nothing?"

Fish: "Actually all that work was just a distraction."

Oak: "So you mean that I spent an hour trying to get bird crap off that car for nothing."

Fish: "Of course not silly. It'll be nice and clean when I take it back to the rental place."

Oak collapses onto the floor from exhaustion.

Fish: "Oakie? You alright? Oh well, If you're gonna take a nap I'll just get back to my game. I need to get my farming and prayer skills up some anyways."

(C-)

-The Dudez!!!1!!! hype up their upcoming matches with the Demons.

Perez: "Anger, dude, I don't care if you're retiring. I don't care if you're like digging your own grave. Hell, I totally don't care if you're going grave robbing Dude. When you go after the Dudez, you're totally gonna get beat."

(D)

-The Demons get some interview time now.

Anger: "Take a good look at me Dudes, because I'm part of what some would consider the greatest tag team of all time. I'm one of the Demons of Rage. I am one of the greatest tag team wrestlers of my generation. As of June 6th I'll be retired and my in-ring career will have finally come to an end, but not without adding one more piece of gold to my collection. I'll win those tag team titles from you and there's not a damn thing you can do to stop me."

(D-)

vs.

-Demon Anger vs. Frankie Perez-

*Sigh* Why is it the good Demon I have to lose? Why couldn't Spite retire? I mean nobody cares about him and I wouldn't have to change my booking plans for his sake. As for what happened during this match, Perez took advantage with with speed and kept Anger on the receiving end for a few minutes. Anger was able to catch him though and slammed him down with a Demon Slam for the win.

Winner: Demon Anger in 6:04

Rating (D+)

vs.

-Fumihiro Ota vs. Steve Flash-

And match of the night goes to...two midcarders. Both of these guys have a bright future ahead of them, even if Flash is 40.

This match was an exhibition, and it was a good one too. Flash and Ota went back and forth exchanging holds in the opening minutes. Flash won out and took control of the match. Ota used his ninja arts to cheat his way into controlling the match. Ota went for a Ninja Strike, but was caught mid-move and flung back for a Flash Bang. Flash picks up a clean victory over Ota going into Family Night.

Winner: Steve Flash in 7:51

Rating (C)

-DDD and Seduction are in their locker room. You can tell Devine is worried about Muffin. Seduction tells him that he's done with her and that he shouldn't even think about helping her out tonight, she's on her own now. Seduction, realizing he's still thinking about it threatens Devine to not so much as think about her or things will take a turn for the worse for Darryl in their relationship. Devine half heartedly agrees that he won't do anything.

(C)

-Stranton is backstage delivering a promo about Umagas match tonight against Muffin and his match Friday with DDD. Umaga is in the background snorting and stomping and trying to bite his title belt like it had a chocolaty inside. Stranton: "Umaga is a monster. Plain and simple. The Samoan Savage hurts people for a living, that's what I trained him to do. He's a blood thirsty beats and anyone who gets in his way will be shoved violently out of his way. Just ask Freddie Datsun. Just ask Muffin. Ask Captain USA, the Demons, Giant Redwood, Trent Shaffer or ask Bruce the Giant. They all got in Umagas way before and they all got hurt because of it. And where are they now? Freddie Datsun can't beat a hoodlum now; Muffin cowers behind Devine for protection; Captain Usa, Giant Redwood, Trent Shaffer, have you seen any of them lately?; Demon Anger is retiring; and Bruce the Giant is now a loyal servant. Once Umaga is done with you Devine, people will see you for the nothing you really are. Umaga is looking forward to Friday, isn't that right Umaga?"

Umaga nods in agreement, title still in mouth. Stranton lets out an eerie laugh as he walks off.

(C)

-Muffin is backstage getting ready to make here entrance. She stops as she hears someone.

???: "I'd watch out for Devine if I were you."

The camera and Muffins head turn their attention to where the voice came from to reveal William Hayes.

Muffin: "What did you say?"

Hayes: "I said that you should watch out for Devine."

Muffin: "Why?"

Hayes: "Stay away and you won't have to find out."

Muffin: "You don't know anything."

Hayes: "More than you do."

Hayes walks away leaving Muffin puzzled by what he said.

(C+)

vs.

-USPW World Championship Match- Muffin vs. Umaga (c) w/ Stranton-

Remember how I said me not remembering who didn't click was going to be a sort of theme for tonight? Yea....

Umaga, as always, dominated this match. He toyed with Muffin and hurt her to send a message to DDD. Muffin tried to fight back against Umaga. I'm not sure whether it was so that she could win, or so that she could survive. Muffin threw everything she had at him except for the kitchen sink. Umaga wouldn't budge. Muffin keeps trying, but a thrust to the throat cut her and her airway off. Muffin made it back to her feet only to recieve a Savage Spear. Umaga set her up in the corner and delivered a Savage Charge. At Strantons advice he did it twice more before finally, and mercifully, deciding to end the match.

Winner and still Champion: Umaga in 11:31

Rating (C-)

-Stranton decides that what Umaga has already done isn't enough. He directs Umaga to 'Finish her off'. Umaga sets her up for the Savage Charge when suddenly somebody stops Umaga and goes after him. And it's... Hayes? You heard that right. Hayes staggers Umaga some but can't get the Samoan Savage off his feet. Hayes springs off the ropes and hits a spinning wheel kick that takes Umaga down. Stranton panics and calls for reinforcements.

Before Hayes can capitalize on the downed monster another one enters the ring in the form of Bruce the Giant. Bruce grabs Hayes by the throat and takes him up for the ride. Bruce is taken down by a shoulder block from Triple D. Devine goes after Umaga now while Bruce is left to Hayes. Hayes sends Bruce out of the ring while Devine brawls with Umaga. Out of nowhere Devine nails a Devine Dream Drop. Umaga is down and rolls outside the ring. Devine goes to check on Muffin, but so does Hayes. You can see animosity between the two as they glanced each other.

(C)

Final Rating (C-)
