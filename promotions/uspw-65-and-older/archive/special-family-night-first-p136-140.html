USPW Family Night

Friday, Week 1, June 2007

Held at Pennsylvania Park

Att. 2,000 (Sold-Out)

Dark Matches

-Zeus Maxmillion defeated Giant Oak in 2:48 by pinfall with a Thunder Bolt. (E-)

-Veteran Mentality defeated The Hotshots in 5:18 when Robert Oxford defeated Davey Wayne by pinfall. (D)

-Top Secret Dark Match (C-)

Main Show

-We start off the biggest event of my booking regime with... Veteran Mentality? What was I thinking? Oxford and Force appear in a pre-taped 'interview' making a challenge to whoever the tag champions may be.

Force: "THE VETERAN MENTALITY SAYS THAT THE BEST ARE THE BEST OF THE BEST WHO HAVE BESTED THE BEST OF THE BEEEEEEEEEEEEEST!!!!! WHOEVER HOLDS OUR TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIIIIIIIIIIIPS WILL BE RAPIDLY RELINQUISHED FROM THE RESISTANT ROGUES UPON WHO UNREPUTABLEY RETAIN OUR TAG TEAM CHAMPONSHIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIII PS!!!!!!!!! BUY MY MERCHANDISE. YOOOOU WIIIIIILL FEEEEEEL THEEEE FOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOORCE!!!!!!!!"

Oxford: "Back in my day scumbags stole stuff. Right now the Dudez have stolen something from us. Those scumbag hippies gots our tag team title belts. The last time I met a hippie I didn't like I shot him in his hippy face with a sun flower in the war of 1854. He now haunts me as the ghost of Christmas Past. Demons, you took what was ours. The last time I met a Demon I didn't like shot him in his demon face with a monkey in the great Heaven/Hell debate of '82. He now works in Hollywood as King Kong. I stand before you today making a challenge to the tag team champions. We're coming for you."

(D-)

vs.

-USPW Tag Team Championship Match- The Demons of Rage vs. The Dudez!!!1!!! (c)-

Win or lose, this would go down as Angers last match and for Angers sake, he really wanted it to be a win.

I let Anger wrestle the majority of the match. The Demons started out in control keeping the Dudez grounded. They worked on the legs to tried to keep it that way. They kept control of the match making some quick tags in and out (as quick as two 90 year olds can anyways). Hernandez was able to kick Spite away with an enziguri and leap for the tag. Perez came in and knocked both Demons down repeatedly. He got caught with a Demon Down from Anger though. Anger makes the pin, 1....2..., Hernandez breaks it up. Anger and Hernandez brawl to the outside while Spite, the legal man btw, goes after Perez. Perez fights him off and hits him with a Perfect Parity. 1....2...3, Anger in to break it up a second too late.

Winners and still Champions: The Dudez!!!1!!! in 6:49

Rating (D)

-The Dudez!!!1!!! have prevailed over the Demons. They celebrate their win jumping from corner to corner showing off their gold. The Demons get up and The Dudez offer a handshake as a sign of respect. Anger celebrates with the Dudez who leave him in the ring to celebrate his last match with the fans.

(D)

-We see Hayes in his locker room getting ready for his match with Steve Flash. Hayes laced up his boots as he heard someone come into his locker room. He looked over his shoulder to see who it was and then looked right back down at his boots.

Muffin: "Why'd you do it?"

Hayes: "Do what?"

Muffin: "You know what. Help me. Why?"

Hayes stood up, turned around and looked Muffin in the eyes. "Contrary to popular belief, I'm not heartless. Now if you'd excuse me I have a championship to win."

(C+)

vs.

-USPW National Championship Match- 2 Out of 3 Falls- William Hayes vs. Steve Flash (c)-

Remember how I said the winner of that tag match got to pick the stipulations of the match? Well, Flash picked 2 out of 3 falls as his stipulation. He's old school like that.

These two are pretty evenly matched and we got a good match out of it. Hayes and Flash let it all hang out in this one. A series of hold and counter hold, won by Flash, was followed up by Hayes thumbing the eye of Flash and getting a quick roll-up for a 2 count. Hayes was in control of the match after that, He wore Flash down with his arsenal of suplexes and strong style strikes. Hayes set Flash up for a Slick Trick, but Flash slips out and takes Hayes down with an arm drag. Both men get back to their feet and Flash sends some knife edged chops Hayes' way. Flash springs Hayes off the ropes and goes for a Flash Bang. Hayes rolls over Flash before he can catch him and rolls him up. Hayes grabs the tights, 1....2...3. The first fall goes to Hayes.

Flash can't believe it and tries to tell the ref that Hayes had the tights, but that just gave Hayes another chance at a roll-up. This time the ref sees him pull the tights. Hayes does the old 'I'm not a cheating heel' complaint routine with the ref. Flash sees the opportunity and gives Hayes a roll-up. Hayes gets out at 2 and the match is back on. Flash is in control and wearing Hayes down now. Flash fires off a snap suplex and goes up top. He hits a flying headbutt. He crawls over and makes the pin. 1....2..., shoulder up by Hayes. Flash gets back to his feet and locks Hayes in a Bow and Arrow Lock. Hayes struggles in the hold. It looks as though he may tap, but he gets his foot on a rope for the break.

Flash pulls Hayes back to his feet, but Hayes comes to life and hits a bridging Northern Lights. The ref counts, 1....2.. Flash gets his shoulder up. Hayes lays some rights into Flashs head. He picks him up and goes for the Slick Trick. He hits it, 1....2....3.

Winner and new Champion: William Hayes in 15:29

Rating (C)

-Stranton is backstage with Umaga who's still snorting and growling and yelling things in some Samoan language.

Stranton: "If I've said it once, I've said it a million times. Umaga is a monster. An unstoppable monster. Think of it like this. He is Godzilla, everyone else is Japan. Get the picture yet? Umaga is an unstoppable force of nature. The Samoan Savage doesn't lose. Devine doesn't stand a chance. What Umaga did to Muffin was only a taste of what he'll do to Devine tonight. Umaga goes in the champion, Umaga leaves the champion. I don't think I can make it any clearer than that."

(C)

-And we get another interview. This time from DDD.

DDD: "Tonight is going to be a war. I go in there tonight against Umaga, a man who has manhandled Bruce the Giant, a guy who has hospitalized most of his opponents, an undefeated Samoan Savage. I'm gonna get my ass kicked, but I'm gonna give back everything I take. I will not stop while there is still an ounce of life left in my body. Umaga is going to have to kill me if he wants to stop me. I'm leaving here with two things tonight. Revenge, and the USPW World Championship and there isn't a damn thing Umaga can do to keep me from getting what I came here to get."

(B)

-We get a video package full of bad stock music (it's all we could afford), and events ~! Namely the events that have transpired between Umaga and Triple D leading up to this match.

(B-)

vs.

-USPW World Championship Match- Triple D vs. Umaga (c) w/ Stranton-

It's nice how sometimes these things work out so well. Umaga and Devine had great chemistry together and you can bet your ass I'm gonna exploit it.

In the seconds before the bell rang Umaga and Devine stared each other down and the crowd ate up every moment of it. Then, the bell rang. All bets were off, this was a fight. The crowd erupted as Devine went toe to toe with the unstoppable Samoan Savage. Fists flew, but neither backed down from the other. Devine endured the barrage of blows Umaga threw his way, but at the same time, he couldn't get the Samoan off his feet hard as he tried. The fight quickly spilled to the outside of the ring where Devine was flung into the guardrail several times before being thrown into the steel steps. Umaga threw Devine back into the ring, but didn't go for the pin. Instead choking him out up until the 4 count then letting go and doing it again. Umaga runs off the ropes and crashes down onto Devine with a senton, once again not going for a cover. Umaga picks Devine up and tosses him into the corner. Umaga charges in with an avalanche that squashes Devine. DDD falls out of the corner face first. Umaga springs off the ropes and drops down with a butt drop right to the small of the back. He pulls Devine up by the hair and starts laying in some rear crossface blows. The ref has to pull Umaga away and check to make sure DDD is still conscious.

Umaga shoves the ref away and goes back to the crossface blows before sitting down for a Boston Crab. Devine tries hard to get to the ropes or to even hang in there. He inches closer to the ropes, but Umaga just lets go of the hold and jumps down onto the small of his back again. Stranton signals for Umaga to end it. Umaga sets DDD up in the corner and hits a vicious Savage Charge. Devine goes down like a sack of bricks. Umaga covers, 1......2..... rope break by Devine.

Umaga goes apesh*t after that and tears into Devine with fists, chokes and forearms. Stranton tells Umaga to finish it, but Umaga doesn't listen. Instead delivering punishment to Devine with every move in his arsenal from a standing spinebuster to a gutwrench suplex. Stranton gets up onto the apron to try to urge Umaga to finish him off, but Umaga goes over and yells some Samoan at him. Out of the blue Devine rolls him up. He only gets a 1 count and Umaga proceeds to tear into him like a fat kid tears into cake. Umaga sends DDD off the ropes, but DDD returns to him with a knee right into the chest. It staggers Umaga and Devine hits him with another. DDD goes for it 1 more time, but Umaga takes a swipe at him. Devine ducks it and takes Umaga down with a neckbreaker. He pins, but only gets a 1.

Devine has come back to life and is on fire, as are the crowd who are calling for the upset. Devine rains rights and lefts into Umagas head. He hits him with a discus clothesline and Umaga goes down. Devine springs off the ropes as Umaga gets up and hits the Samoan Savage with a knee lift. Devine rotates 270 degrees and takes him down with another hard clothesline. He goes for the pin, but only gets a 2. DDD signals for a Devine Dream Drop. Umaga gets back to his feet only to get dropped back down with a 3D. Devine with a cover,
