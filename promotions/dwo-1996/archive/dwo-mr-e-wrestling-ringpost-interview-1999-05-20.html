DWO: The Electronic Wrestling Ringpost — Mike Bilodeau Interview

May 20, 1999, 12:00:00 AM

Adrian Anderson is sitting with a business suit on, next to an chair. There is a man sitting on the chair, but is in darkness until the interview starts. There is a picture of Sick Rick and
Mother Madeline winning the DWO Deadly Rumble on the wall.) Adrian Anderson: Ladies and Gentlemen, this past weekend, the Deadly Wrestling Organization made history. There were many controversial moments on the DWO Deadly Draw 2 card. Let me just say, while I didn't agree with all of them, I have to make it clear that the DWO is for entertainment purposes only, and is a "watch at your own risk" federation. There was call for outrage over an incident between "Macarena" Matt Booth and "The
Handler" Liam Rogan. There was an incident of backstage betrayal, and behind the scenes cover ups and plans of another federation to tarnish the name of the Deadly Wrestling Organization because of this incident.
Tonight, I'll sit down, in a very candid discussion with the President of the Deadly Wrestling Organization, Mr. Mike Bilodeau. He and Shawn Roy made the DWO back in 1995. After Roy's retirement, Bilodeau took the federation all to himself. From there, there were DRASTIC changes. The material became somewhat more risque. The attitude was cranked up a notch.
The wrestlers were more aggressive. It was a different Deadly Wrestling Organization. To open up the interview, first of all, welcome Mr. Bilodeau, my first question has to be, what is your creative picture for the Deadly Wrestling Organization?
Mike Bilodeau: Thank you Adrian. Before I answer that, I just want to make on thing clear. You mentioned the Matt Booth situation. That situation was my decision. I wrote the card. I wrote the ENTIRE card, except a few matches that some of my TRUSTED writers decided to send in on time.
As for my creative picture, and the direction I have taken the DWO in. I wanted a wrestling organization that presented fans with action, entertainment, and non-stop controversy. It is the Howard Stern/Jerry
Springer wrestling organization. I make no excuses for the racey and somewhat extreme content that we present. In fact, MOST people understand it, enjoy it, and can sit back and have a laugh about it. Others sit and criticize. It's funny, because if people didn't criticize, the DWO wouldn't be so popular.
Adrian Anderson: Controversy sells...is that it? Mike Bilodeau: Adrian. When you see an e-wrestling card, what do you see? They're all the same. Everyone has the world title match as the main event, it could be the biggest PPV of all time, or a rinky dink house show card. Everyone has some splinter group claiming they're going to take over. Everyone has the evil-promoter. Everyone has the one wrestler who stands for justice. It's bullshit. What the DWO does, is takes all those stereotypes and makes a mockery of it. We're original. We're unique.
We're the only wrestling organization where you could see a guy rape a guy, and a man fall 300 feet and come back and win the big match. I say, fuck all the other wrestling organizations, they're still ripping off the NWO.
Come to the DWO, and we'll show you what wrestling should be about. Adrian Anderson: You're saying fuck all other e-wrestling organizations? Mike Bilodeau: Well, certainly not all. The UEW is a terrific federation. It's always original. The DWO and the UEW are probably the two best organizations today, and from there, there's just the distant seconds, the place where the DWO stars go to take world titles. The bush leagues.
Adrian Anderson: Most of the people on your DWO card were lesser known names, who came from these so-called "bush leagues."
Mike Bilodeau: That was made obvious by their work. I mean, I open the DWO once a year, maybe more sometimes, so these "lesser known names" get some noteriety with the "legends." Some capitalize, like Nick Leshenn did
last year. Candyman did an excellent job both years. Others fill out the application, and then I never hear from them again. That's until after the card when they whine and bitch that they lost their match. Like it's my fault. I put a LOT of hours into this card, hours when I could have been out having fun, and they complain to ME that they lose. I say fuck them.
You don't work, you don't get the rewards. It's like a job pal, don't work, don't get paid. And DON'T you ever BLAME it on me, because I've done
A LOT for e-wrestling, and I'm not about to second guess my work on the word of some lame, two-bit bush league wrestler.
I'm astonished that a question like that was even asked. There are a certain number of wrestlers that I trust in this whole game. Those men flash when asked to flash. If they can't work for a card, and they know it would be a waste of time, they TELL me, and I back off. Others fill out
these applications and VANISH into thin air. And then we have the men like [name withheld], or "Liam Rogan," who feel they own the whole show. No, Liam Rogan is a lame character, and [name withheld] is just as stupid. He thinks this show revolves around him, because his made-up "girlfriend" was good looking.
No, you get by in e-wrestling by working. And nobody works more than me in this federation. Nobody. So don't you even BEGIN to blame ME for anything that went down on that card.
Adrian Anderson: Two years ago, the DWO was supposedly a "bush league." You came on the Ringpost and started an anti-Loop brigade. You managed to DESTROY the LOOP organizations, claiming they destroyed the bush leagues, like the DWO. You singlehandedly destroyed [name withheld] and his reputation and his fed. Now, you claim the DWO is some kind of super fed? Are you a hypocrite?
Mike Bilodeau: I'm not saying the DWO is a super-federation, Adrian. You have to get me right on this. The DWO has been around for a long time.
Sure, we've had ups and downs. We're not the greatest federation on the net, but we DAMN SURE try to be. We don't get the accolades that most lamers on RSPWF get. But we DAMN SURE deserve some. I'm sick of the DWO being disregarded. The DWO deserves a lot of damn credit for the upswing in e-wrestling lately. No, I DESERVE a lot of the damn credit. The
E-Wrestling Ringpost, once it stopped circulation, the whole damn world of e-wrestling went downhill. Myself and the DWO deserve a lot of fucking credit, and I'm going to get it. Starting now.
Adrian Anderson: How? Mike Bilodeau: By destroying everyone that makes e-wrestling bad. The main man on the hit list is [name withheld], Liam Rogan. Let me tell you about this guy. He makes up a "girlfriend" and he handles a female character through a fake e-mail he set up. He sends around a picture of this "girlfriend"
that another e-wrestler found on a porn-site. He looks like a Quicky Mart attendant. And he goes around e-wrestling thinking he's big shit.
Let me tell you how he handles his character. There is a manager, Sensous Samantha. Not only did Liam Rogan RAPE Samantha in character, he's trying to BECOME a FACE BECAUSE OF IT! Rogan is just as slimey and cheesey as [name withheld].
"Macarena" Matt Booth was NEEDED at DWO. He was needed to show this [name withheld] a lesson. He's NOT ABOVE the game. He's not even a good contributing factor. I showed him that his games can come back to "bite him in the ass."
And the MAIN reason I pulled the "Matt Booth" shot on Rogan was because [name withheld] SCREWED me over on a match he was supposed to write for this card. He worked in conjunction with "Nick Leshenn" aka [detail withheld], and "Magnus Colby", aka
[detail withheld]. They didn't write their matches. They didn't tell me why. And they didn't even apologize. So why do these men gather around [name withheld]? Frankly, because they're just as cheesey and slimey as he is. These three men, can go fuck themselves. This is MY time to get even. This is MY time to get my accolades, and since they didn't HELP me get there with the DWO Card, it looks as if I'm going to have to GET my accolades by KICKING THEIR ASSES, seeing I've JUST SIGNED with the UEW!
Adrian Anderson: You've signed with the UEW? Mike Bilodeau: Yes. As a wrestler/manager type character. You'll never know where I'll pop up.
Adrian Anderson: Rumor has it, this little fued with you, [name withheld], [detail withheld] and
[detail withheld] will be ignited this Sunday at the IEW pay per view. What's going on there?
Mike Bilodeau: Well, apparently, Jay Ralston of the IEW also has a problem with me. He's going to let [name withheld], [detail withheld] and [detail withheld] retaliate on the IEW Pay Per
View. It has something to do with a sodomy incident. That just shows you how original [name withheld] and the IEW are.
To clear the air on this, Mike Bilodeau or ANYTHING affiliated with me, will NOT...repeat....NOT be involved with the IEW this weekend. Anything they do will be without my permission. It's just to start a little fire between us, and I AM NOT EVEN On the IEW MAILING LIST. I would have never even SEEN this if a source didn't inform me of it. It's just how RALSTON and [name withheld] are. Attack from behind.
Adrian Anderson: What is your problem with Ralston of the IEW? Mike Bilodeau: He verbally attacked me this past week. He claims I'm a faggot, and that all faggots MUST die from a horrible disease. Ralston is a narrow-minded bigot. It just shows you how he thinks. If anything is different from him, it must be destroyed. Ralston is the kind of guy that sits outside a teenagers funeral with "God Hates Fags" signs. He is the type of person that should be shot, hung from a tree branch, and fed to wild tigers. When he's burning in hell, he'll reconsider all the evil he has inside.
Adrian Anderson: Are you a homosexual? Mike Bilodeau: I'm not going to lie to you, Adrian. My personal life has nothing to do with my running of e-wrestling, and it's frankly none of
Ralston or [name withheld]'s business. They can attack me any way they please, because I'm secure in my lifestyle, whatever it may be. Nothing they can say or do will hurt me personally. If it damages my e-wrestling "character," fine, I have better things to do with my time.
Adrian Anderson: So you're saying their retaliation means nothing to you? Mike Bilodeau: I think it's a case of grown "men" taking e-wrestling a little too seriously.
Adrian Anderson: Why would the IEW resort to defamation? Mike Bilodeau: Because it's a sub-par federation trying to capture the heat that I and the DWO offer. They're trying to pull their shitty fed out of a rut by capitalizing on my heat.
Adrian Anderson: What are your plans in the UEW? Mike Bilodeau: Everyone will find out in due time. But it involves the destruction of [detail withheld], "Leshenn." [detail withheld], "Magnus Colby," [name withheld], "Liam Rogan,"
and this "king Snake' Ralston person. Adrian Anderson: Anything you'd like to add? Mike Bilodeau: Revenge is always sweeter the second time around. Nobody has a mind like me. I'm sadistic in every imaginable way. The UEW will either welcome me with open arms, or I'll destroy it like I've destroyed
