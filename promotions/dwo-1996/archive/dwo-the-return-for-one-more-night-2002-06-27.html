DWO: The Return for One More Night

27 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Scene opens in a dark studio with announcer Robert Jackson sitting in a chair in a suit. He is sitting next to DWO President Mike Bilodeau, who sits in jeans and a DWO T-Shirt.) Robert Jackson: This is Robert Jackson for the Deadly Wrestling Organization. I am here with DWO President Mike Bilodeau, who has a major announcement. Mike? Mike Bilodeau: Thank you Robert. That announcement is that the Deadly Wrestling Organization has reached an aggreement with one of professional wrestling's top stars to have his return on the DWO One More Night Pay Per View on August 1. While I cannot say who this star is yet, because of his contract terms, I have to say that this card in August is certainly shaping up to be one of the most controversial, stunning and action packed you are likely to ever see. Robert Jackson: Mr. Bilodeau, you retired from actively running this league several years ago. Why should anyone care now about the return of the DWO? Mike Bilodeau: Whether they liked it or not, the DWO has always been the topic of conversation, whether it be on a newsboard, or in emails. The DWO was the source of many stars, and the source of many controversial storylines that you were not likely to see in other federations. The DWO really opened the doors for many of today's organizations. And what I want to do is just sit back and let the stars of today pay tribute to the roots of wrestling, which I believe the DWO is a strong part of. Robert Jackson: Can you speculate on who may be appearing at this card? Mike Bilodeau: Let me just say that, I am currently negotiating contracts with several stars of today and yesterday. But from what I can tell so far, this is going to be one of the greatest cards we have ever seen. Robert Jackson: What are your thoughts on the current state of professional wrestling? Mike Bilodeau: Well, to be honest, I see wrestling as a dying art form. To be honest, it hasn't excited me in several years, and that's why I kind of fell aside and let the younger generation take over. But I think that's about to change. I think this DWO card will give wrestling the shot in the arm it desperate needs. Robert Jackson: That's all the time we have. Thank you for joining us. Again, to get on this spectacular card, please email Mr. Bilodeau at [detail withheld] or at [detail withheld]
