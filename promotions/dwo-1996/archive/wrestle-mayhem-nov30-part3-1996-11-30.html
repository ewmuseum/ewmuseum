DWO Wrestle Mayhem (30 Nov 1996), Part 3

30 November 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Robert Jackson: Get ready for a wild one! This is going to be great!! J'ster versus Sir Giles Cavendish MP, the first time, and most likely, THE ONLY TIME!!!! Johnny Cassimo: Oh boy, this arena is going to be demolished after this match is over!! I gaurentee it!! [DING! DING! DING!] Robert Jackson: J'ster and Sir Giles lock up in the center of the ring!! Cavendish shoves J'ster into the corner! J'ster gets right back up and he DROPKICKS CAVENDISH DOWN!! Johnny Cassimo: Well the crowd certainly behind J'ster on this one, which is something REALLY RARE!! Robert Jackson: J'ster and Cavendish lock up again. Cavendish whips J'ster into the ropes. Cavendish swings for a clothesline but J'ster ducks! ANOTHER DROPKICK BY J'STER GETS CAVENDISH OFF HIS FEET!!! Johnny Cassimo: Cavendish quickly back up. J'ster bounces of the ropes goes for a kick but Cavendish grabs his leg. ENZUGIRI KICK BY J'STER!!!! Robert Jackson: J'ster with a quick cover! 1!... and that's all J'ster can get!! J'ster climbs up to the top turnbuckle!! He's on his way out of the cage!!! Johnny Cassimo: Cavendish gets back to his feet and walks over towards J'ster!! Sir Giles grabs J'ster's tights and pulls him down! Cavendish picks up J'ster, BODY SLAM!! Robert Jackson: Cavendish off the ropes, executes a LEG DROP!! Cavendish picks up J'ster with a hairpull. Cavendish lifts him up in the air!!!!! Johnny Cassimo: GUT WRENCH SUPLEX!!!! Here is a cover by Sir Giles Cavendish!!! 1!.........2!......kickout by J'ster!!! Robert Jackson: Sir Giles picks up J'ster, irish whip into the ropes. Cavendish runs into the ropes as well. BIG LARIAT BY SIR GILES CAVENDISH!! Johnny Cassimo: Cavendish signals for the Referee to open the door!! He's headed OUT OF THE CAGE!!!!! Robert Jackson: J'STER FROM BEHIND!! ROLLS UP CAVENDISH IN A CRADLE!!!!!! 1!!!.......2!!!!........NEW CHAMP!! No! Cavendish kicked out!!! Sir Giles gets up to his feet. So does J'ster. J'ster turns around and gets NAILED BY A DROPKICK FROM THE BIG MAN... CAVENDISH!!! Johnny Cassimo: Sir Giles Cavendish applies a side headlock on J'ster. He runs accross the ring, A RUNNING BULLDOG!!!! He covers!! 1!........ ...2!!...... J'ster's out!! Robert Jackson: The fans are getting up to their feet... somone is coming out of the locker room!! Johnny Cassimo: Who is it?!?! GET THE CAMERAS TO THE AISLE!!!! WHO THE HELL IS THAT!?!?!?! He's over 7 FEET TALL!!! Robert Jackson: Wait a minute, I know that face, I know that man really well!!! And I know Cavendish does too!!! Johnny Cassimo: WHO IS HE?!?! Robert Jackson: THAT'S EXODUS!!! THE ACEW HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPION!!!!!! HE'S HERE AT THE UTICA MEMORIAL AUDITORIUM!!!! EXODUS!!!! Johnny Cassimo: Now, how come you know these people, and I don't?! Are they no namers?!?! Robert Jackson: Are you kidding me? EXODUS IS ONE OF THE FINEST IN E-WRESTLING!!! Anyways, we have a match inside of the ring, can we get back to that?? Johnny Cassimo: Well, yeah. Cavendish climbs up to the second turnbuckle. He comes down with a FOREARM SMASH!!!! Cavendish nails J'ster with a fistdrop! He covers!!! 1!......2!!......kickout!! Robert Jackson: Exodus, has taken a seat at ringside, a few feet away from our booth!!! We have to see what's going on here!!! Cavendish takes a look at Exodus!! Johnny Cassimo: J'STER GETS BACK UP!! SIR GILES AND EXODUS ARE STARING AT EACH OTHER!! J'STER BOUNCES OFF THE ROPES AND NAILS CAVENDISH WITH AN ELBOW SMASH TO THE BACK OF THE HEAD!!! Robert Jackson: A KNIFE EDGE BY J'STER!!! ANOTHER!!! J'ster with a double leghook takedown!!! IT'S A COVER!!! 1!!.......2!!!......kickout by Cavendish again!! Johnny Cassimo: J'ster's climbing out of the cage!!! Cavendish isn't moving at all!! WE MIGHT HAVE A NEW CHAMPION RIGHT HERE!!!! J'STER'S IS WAY UP ON TOP ALMOST TOWARDS THE TOP OF THE CAGE!! Robert Jackson: Exodus' is getting up. LOOK AT THIS SEVEN FOOT TALL MONSTER!! AND WE ARE ABOUT NINE FEET AWAY FROM HIM!!! EXODUS IS CLIMBING INTO THE CAGE FROM THE OUTSIDE!!!! Johnny Cassimo: He's at the top!! HE NAILS J'STER WITH A RIGHT HAND!!! J'STER FALLS DOWN TO THE CANVAS RIGHT ON HIS BACK!!!! WHAT THE HELL IS EXODUS DOING?! Robert Jackson: Exodus, is leaving the area. But he just cost J'ster the match, AND THE MOST PRECIOUS TITLE IN DWO!!!! OH NO!! HERE COMES MORE TROUBLE!! DARK VANITY AND RAZI MAHMOOD!!!! Johnny Cassimo: CAVENDISH IS COVERING!!! DARK VANITY AND MAHMOOD ARE CLIMBING INTO THE CAGE!!!! WHAT THE HELL IS GOING ON!!!! Robert Jackson: 1!!........2!!!....... DARK VANITY SAVED J'STER! DV SAVED J'STER!!!!!! DARK VANITY CAME FLYING DOWN FROM THE TOP OF THE CAGE AND NAILED CAVENDISH RIGHT ON THE BACK WITH A DOUBLE AX HAND!!! Johnny Cassimo: HAS J'STER AND DARK VANITY PATCHED UP THEIR DIFFERENCES??? BUT J'STER IS OUT OF IT!!! RAZI MAHMOOD PICKS UP CAVENDISH... HE NAILS HIM WITH THE LETHAL NIGHTMARE!!! DARK VANITY DRAGS J'STER AND PUTS HIM ON TOP OF CAVENDISH!!! Robert Jackson: MAHMOOD COUNTS!!! 1..2..3!! THAT WAS A FAST COUNT!!! THE REFEREE IS SIGNALING FOR THE BELL!!!!!! DO WE HAVE A NEW CHAMPION?!?!?!? [DING! DING! DING!] Johnny Cassimo: RAZI MAHMOOD AND DARK VANITY CONTINUE TO NAIL CAVENDISH WITH STOMPS!!!! J'STER IS OUT AND IS ON A CORNER!!! THE RING ANNOUNCER AS THE MICROPHONE AND THE OUTSIDE OFFICIAL JUST TOLD HIM SOMETHING.... RING ANNOUNCER: Ladies and gentlemen, the WINNER OF THIS MATCH!!!! AND THE NNNNNNNNNNNNNNNNNNNNEEEEEEEEEEEEWWWWWWWWWWWWWWW DEADLY WRESTLING ORGANIZATION WORRRRLD HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPION!!!!! J'STERRRRRRRRR!!!!!!!!!!!!! Robert Jackson: WE'VE GOT A NEW CHAMPION! WE'VE GOT A NEW CHAMPION! WE'VE GOT A NEW CHAMP!!!! DARK VANITY CLIMBS UP ALL THE WAY TO THE TOP OF THE CAGE AND COMES DOWN WITH AN ELBOW DROP ON TO CAVENDISH!! Johnny Cassimo: The Pscyedelic Slammer and John Sylvia help the new World Champ out of the ring!!!! AND THEY'LL CELEBRATE THIS THANKGIVING WEEKEND!! Robert Jackson: Cavendish is getting beaten up by two damn losers!! And we need some help.. AND HERE IT COMES!!!! THE MASKED MAN ALONG WITH MIDNIGHT JAWAAD MAHMOOD!!!!!! Johnny Cassimo: THEY'RE CLIMBING INTO THE CAGE!! RAZI AND DARK VANITY HAVE YET TO SEE THEM!!!! Robert Jackson: JAWAAD MAHMOOD CLIMBS IN AND HE TOSSES RAZI INTO THE CORNER!! JAWAAD MAHMOOD CLOTHESLINES DARK VANITY DOWN!! LOOK AT THIS PLACE EXPLODE!!! THE MASKED MAN....... LEG DROPS CAVENDISH!!!!!! WHAT THE HELL?!?!?!?! Johnny Cassimo: MIDNIGHT JAWAAD MAHMOOD STOMPS AWAY ON CAVENDISH AS WELL!!!! RAZI WALKS OVER TOWARDS JAWAAD!!! THEY SHAKE EACH OTHERS HANDS!! THERE WASN'T ANY LOVE LOSS!! IT'S MAHMOOD POWER! AND IT'S LETHAL!! HAHAHAHA!!!! Robert Jackson: DARK VANITY, RAZI MAHOOD, JAWAAD, AND THE MASKED MAN ARE STOMPING AWAY ON THE FORMER CHAMPION!!! THIS WAS A PLAN!! IT'S SICK!! TOTALL SICK!!!!!!!!!! Johnny Cassimo: You know, I'm suddenly beginning to HATE English Morons!! Look at that HELPLESS FOOL!!!!!!! NO FRIENDS, NO TITLES!!!! [Suddenly, the crowd ROARS, and it was the BIGGEST pop of the night as someone charged down the aisle....] Robert Jackson: Who's this now?!?!?! ROBSKI!!!!!!!!!!!! ROBSKI!!! ROBSKI!!! ROBSKI!!! ROBSKI!!! THIS PLACE IS GOING INSANE!!!!! ROBSKI IS COMING INTO THE RING!!!!!! DARK VANITY, THE MASKED MAN, AND THE MAHMOODS QUICKLY RUN OUT THE DOOR AND JUMP INTO THE CROWD!!!! ROBSKI HAS COME TO HELP SIR GILES CAVENDISH!!!! Johnny Cassimo: <Sounding scared> WHAT THE HELL IS HE DOING HERE?!?! HE HAD TO RUIN THE PARTY, DIDN'T HE?!?!?! Robert Jackson: ROBSKI HAS COME TO THE AID OF SIR GILES CAVENDISH MP!!! THIS IS GOING TO BE ONE NIGHT IN DWO'S HISTORY, TO NEVER TO FORGET!!!!! TOMMY VAN DAM MAKES HIS APPEARENCE, ALONG WITH EXODUS, AND ROBSKI!! J'STER WINS THE TITLE BUT WE'LL SEE WHAT SHAWN ROY, AND MIKE BILODEAU HAVE TO SAY ABOUT IT!!! <Out of breath> IT FEELS LIKE AND EARTHQUAKE IN HERE!! AND THESE FANS CERTAINLY ARE LIKING ROBSKI!! And trust me, it's never happened before!! FOR EVERYONE HERE IN DWO!! THIS IS ROBERT JACKSON SAYING SO LONG!!!!!!! [The people in the Utica Memorial Auditorium are on their feet chanting "Robski! Robski!" really loud. The scene fades out...]
