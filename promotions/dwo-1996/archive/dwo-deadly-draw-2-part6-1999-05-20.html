---------------------------------------------------------------------- Robert Jackson: This is what it's all about. The night has come to this. Nineteen men, and one woman are going to go through a test like none other. It's the DWO's Deadly Rumble here at the Deadly Draw. The main event of the night. Last year, stars were made. This year, there's so much more at stake. Pride. Revenge. Hatred. This year, the DWO is about who can top who. This year, it's about who will survive. This year, it's all in that ring.
Adrian Anderson: I'd like to just step away from the usual role of the commentator and talk about something very dear to my heart right now. Last year, this event was HUGE! We had over 60 guys on the roster. This year, things have thinned out. Things have not been so good for the business.
The hype just didn't have the same appeal. But I'm telling you all this right now, the Deadly Wrestling Organization and Mike Bilodeau deserve a whole heck of a lot of credit for getting all this talent assembled on one card. They may not be the best of the bunch. Their minds may be elsewhere, but to hold an event of this magnitude, and have it be successful is a tough job in this business. For that, I believe the DWO and President Mike Bilodeau deserve a lot of respect and accolades.
Johnny Cassimo: I agree with that, Adrian. Bilodeau and the DWO have been around for a long time now, and I feel the respect they get really isn't as well as it should be. I mean, this federation has given us top caliber matches, and HUGE names on the same night. We never get RSPWF Awards, but
I know for a fact that DWO Deadly Draw 2 deserves attention. This night has been shocking, entertaining, and down right athletic. We want to send out thank you's to all the wrestlers, creative staff, promotions, and to everyone else in between. It's been a great run, we hope you liked this card so far, and we certainly want to end it with a bang in all your honors. Thanks.
Robert Jackson: If I could just add a little something to that tribute. This card is dedicated to three people, or groups of people. First we'd like to dedicate this card to the families and students of Littleton,
Colorado. It was a horrible tragedy last month, and we send love and admiration to the families who have survived this tragedy with courage and devotion. Secondly, this card is dedicated to the family of Dark
Vanity/Glenn Osborne, a great pro-wrestler who deserves a lot of credit from the business as a whole. And thirdly, this card is dedicated to the innocent people in Kosovo, who are being killed for no apparent reason.
The DWO will be making charitable donations toward these causes with the proceeds of this card.
Adrian Anderson: Well, gentlemen, it's been a rough night for so many people that just didn't make it this far. The Deadly Rumble is about to get underway. Two men start, and every five minutes after they enter, another superstar will come to the ring. The last man standing will win.
To be eliminated, you have to be thrown over the top rope, both feet hitting the floor, you could be pinned, or if you submit you will be eliminated.
Johnny Cassimo: Those latter stipulations were added due to a potential lawsuit threatened by the World Wrestling Federation. They said this event mimmicked their Royal Rumble.
