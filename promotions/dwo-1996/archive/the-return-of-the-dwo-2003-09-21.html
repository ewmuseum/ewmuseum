The Return of the Deadly Wrestling Organization

Sep 21, 2003, 12:00:00 AM
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
It was considered the most controversial federation in the world... It was considered the most original, most creative federation in the world... And now.... It's coming back. The Deadly Wrestling Organization, which was originally opened in the 90s by
Shawn Roy and Mike Bilodeau, had a run which was surpassed by no other federation in the world. It had great champions like Professor Payne, J'ster,
Sick Rick, Apollo, Alex Martinez, and great superstars like Robski, Brody
Thunder, the Mahmood Brothers, and more. It was also a federation that is still being talked about today because of an incident between "Macarena" Matt
Booth and Liam Rogan, an incident that will be talked about for generations to come.
Mike Bilodeau, former DWO President and former WLW Vice President, has decided to bring the chaos back.
The DWO will run much like the WLW. A card every two weeks. Each card will have five matches. There will be a tag division, a singles division and a women's division. There will be THREE pay per views a year, and THREE title belts.
I'm looking toward an end of October first card. The weekly show will be called DWO: Dirty Deeds. It will be broadcast on Tuesday evenings on the Fox
Network.
