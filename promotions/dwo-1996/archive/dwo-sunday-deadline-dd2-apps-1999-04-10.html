DWO: Sunday is Deadline for Deadly Draw 2 Applications

Apr 10, 1999, 12:00:00 AM
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
THE DEADLY WRESTLING ORGANIZATION PRESENTS D E A D L Y D R A W 2 C O N T R O L C E N T E R L I V E F R O M DWO S T U D I O S -------------------------------------------------------------- ("Back in Black" by AC/DC plays as we fade into the DWO Studios. Pictures of Nick Leshenn, Professor Payne, Rick Styles, Roy Rainer, Mike Bilodeau,
Apollo, J'ster, and Sick Rick are hanging on the studio walls. We fade into the front desk, where four familiar men are standing, wearing formal suits. One is Robert Jackson, head announcer for the DWO since it's first card. The other is Johnny Cassimo, the color announcer since the first card. Another is Adrian Anderson, the latest addition to the DWO, who has been like the "Jim Ross" of e-wrestling ever since his debut last year at the Deadly Draw. The last man is DWO Owner, Mike Bilodeau.)
Robert Jackson: DWO Deadly Draw 2 is approaching rapidly. It's amazing that one year ago we were doing this in Upstate, New York. And here we are again going to San Antonio!
Adrian Anderson: Well, we got a call concerning the Alamo Dome in San
Antonio. Apparently some federation had the building booked for that night, and they were going to hold their pay per view event there. Well,
Alamo Dome officials called me this morning and said, they kicked the other federation out, and they know the fans want the DWO! So the DWO isn't postponing anything! Looks like that other federation will have to find some other venue.
Johnny Cassimo: I kind of like that federation too. I mean, where else could you see Hulk Hogan fight Doink the Clown? Last time I watched them, there was this guy called "Who" and he was fighting a plumber. I mean, it's great. But not as great as the DWO!
Adrian Anderson: We understand we're going to be joined by DWO President
Dark Vanity, because the man to my right has a statement to make to him, isn't that right Mr. Bilodeau?
Mike Bilodeau: Well, it seems we have a few changes to make to the Deadly
Draw card. Dark Vanity hasn't been doing the job I expected him to, and now I'm going to deal with him personally.
("Karma Police" by Radiohead plays. The studio lights go out and DWO
President Dark Vanity enters from behind the announcers. He is wearing a suit and tie and does not have his mask on. He is himself, Glen Osborne.)
Glen Osborne: Deal with me? Mike Bilodeau: Oh he's here. Good, I have a surprise for you, Mr. Osborne. As owner of this company, I am officially removing you of your duties as DWO President. Your card making ability was not as efficient as
I thought it might be. I want you out of the DWO effective immediately.
Glen Osborne: You can't do that. At least before I inform you of your NEW referee for the FIRST BLOOD MATCH!
Mike Bilodeau: What? That's my match with Kieran Erotikka. You can't appoint no referee.
Glen Osborne: Yes I can, and I have. PROFESSOR PAYNE, former DWO Champion, will REFEREE your match at the DWO Card. Have a good day, Mr. Bilodeau. Mike Bilodeau: NO! NO! You can't do that! Glen Osborne: I already have. Now, I'll tell you one thing before I go, this is not the last time you'll see me Bilodeau. Throughout my entire career, you've been holding me back, you wanted the limelight. Well, now it's my turn to shine, I'll see you on May 16th.
(Osborne leaves and slams the door behind him.) Robert Jackson: That's not right Bilodeau, and you know it. Mike Bilodeau: Shut up, here are the changes PAY ATTENTION, because your match may be changed!
----DWO DEADLY DRAW 2----- LETHAL LADDER MATCH FOR $100,000 The first team to grab the money off the ladder is richer and going to the
Deadly Rumble! But as of right now, it looks like none of these teams want the money because of their silence!
----DWO DEADLY DRAW 2----- 2 dYnAmiC Wesley Steel & Puppet Master Nicolas Christian vs. "Hot Stuff" Scott Richards & The Trixxster Adrian Anderson: So what's the change in this match? Mike Bilodeau: None whatsoever. Next HUGE change: -----DWO DEADLY DRAW 2------ DOUBLE LETHAL ROUND ROBIN MATCH: Rules to be explained on DWO Inferno -----DWO DEADLY DRAW 2------ "Black Knight" Alex Martinez & Al Robertson vs. Nick Leshenn & Alexander Munroe Robert Jackson: WHY THE HELL DID YOU PUT MARTINEZ IN THIS MATCH WITH LESHENN??? Mike Bilodeau: Because Nick Leshenn will NOT make it to the Deadly Rumble, and Martinez will make sure of that!
Adrian Anderson: Is Rick Styles no longer in your pocket? Mike Bilodeau: Shut up right now Anderson. Next change: -----DWO DEADLY DRAW 2------ LETHAL MILLENIUM MATCH Rules to be explained on Inferno -----DWO DEADLY DRAW 2------ Sick Rick and "Top Dog" Rick Styles vs. Tommy Love & Magnus Colby Adrian Anderson: SICK RICK and RICK STYLES on the same team?? Mike Bilodeau: That's right, they headlined the first DWO Reunion card, and now they'll team up.
Robert Jackson: But why? Mike Bilodeau: Let's just say, Magnus Colby looked at me sideways. It's time for him to pay the reaper. Colby, are you up for the challenge?
Next change: ------DWO DEADLY DRAW 2------ STEEL CAGE SHOWDOWN ------DWO DEADLY DRAW 2------ Mr. Fantastic and Gorilla vs. Nicklaus Kain and "The New Superstar" Cris Rio Robert Jackson: So no change in that match? Mike Bilodeau: Nope, next change: -----DWO DEADLY DRAW 2----- THE ULTIMATE BARB WIRE MATCH Ring ropes wrapped in barb wire. Bring whatever weapons you want. First team to wrap both opponents in barb wire wins.
-----DWO DEADLY DRAW 2----- Apollo & "The Awesome Lion" Moo Kame vs. The Mahmood Brothers Robert Jackson: But why? Mike Bilodeau: Apollo was aksing for it, so I gave it to him. Have fun champ. ----DWO DEADLY DRAW 2---- ROOKIES MUST RETIRE MATCH The losers must leave DWO and never return. They must also retire from wrestling altogether. By the way, the ring will be enclosed by a DEADLY CELL! -----DWO DEADLY DRAW 2---- Jesse Muldoon & Candyman vs. Robbie Kincade & "Tricky Kid" Derek Craven Robert Jackson: You'll destroy two rookies. You can't do that! Mike Bilodeau: Hey, it's the DWO, if they aren't ready for the big time, they will never even make the small time. Next change:
----DWO DEADLY DRAW 2------ LETHAL SCAFFOLD MATCH Seven layers of Glass will be above the ring. Both losers will take a deadly fall through sharp glass. Don't lose this buddies
----DWO DEADLY DRAW 2------ "The Southern Gentleman" Tommy Lane & Crazy Horse vs. Mad Bronco and Mr. Young Adrian Anderson: You're out for blood, Bilodeau. There's something wrong with you. Mike Bilodeau: Bite me, next change: -----DWO DEADLY DRAW 2----- LETHAL FIRST BLOOD MATCH SPECIAL REFEREE: PROFESSOR PAYNE -----DWO DEADLY DRAW 2------ Mike Bilodeau vs. Kieran Erottika Adrian Anderson: What are you going to change there? Mike Bilodeau: THE WINNER GOES TO THE DEADLY RUMBLE! Adrian Anderson: WHAT? You can't enter the Deadly Rumble, and for that matter, neither could a woman!
Mike Bilodeau: She wants it, she's got it. And now I'm going to announce the next match for the card:
-----DWO DEADLY DRAW 2------ WINNER MUST WEAR DRESS IN DEADLY RUMBLE And also, the match will take place in deserts of Texas. No refs, no rules, anything goes, bring your weapons, and whatever team makes it to the highway wins, (or loses depending on how you look at it.)
-----DWO DEADLY DRAW 2-------- The Mercenary & "The Loose Caner" Dark Vanity vs. Johnny Bodd & Eric '99 Adrian Anderson: WHAT? You just fired him, and now you want him in a match in the 200 degree desert?
Mike Bilodeau: I have another announcement, this will be my last appearance on DWO Control Center, so that I may train for my match at
Deadly Draw. TOMORROW is the premeir of DWO INFERNO, make sure your tapes are in, and the FINAL and COMPLETE CARD will be announced tomorrow!
