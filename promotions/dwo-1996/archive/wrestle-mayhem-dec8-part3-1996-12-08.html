DWO Wrestle Mayhem (8 Dec 1996), Part 3

8 December 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Johnny Cassimo: What an UGLY scene this was! Robert Jackson: We are getting set for possibly the greatest battle in DWO History. 20 Men will battle it out for the right to a DWO National Title Match to take place here tonight in Westchester, NY. The American Hero is watching this match in anticipation. Without a doubt, HISTORY will be made here at DWO WrestleMayhem!!! Johnny Cassimo: The GREAT JAMES is in the battle royal..he's going to win the NATIONAL TITLE later on, you watch Jackson!! Robert Jackson: Johnny Cassimo, you are full of shit! This is one extraordinary night in the DEADLY WRESTLING ORGANIZATION and it's about to get HOTTER!! The BATTLE ROYAL is up next!!! (The RING ANNOUNCER is standing in the center of the ring...FIREWORKS go off, the crowd goes nuts....) RING ANNOUNCER: Ladies and Gentlemen....The following contest is a 21 MAN BATTLE ROYAL!!! The winner of this match will fight AMERICAN HERO later on in the night for the DEADLY WRESTLING ORGANIZATION NATIONAL TITLE!!! Another historic night in the DWO is about to get HOTTER!! Introducing the participants..... WALTER CHANCE!!!!!....... MOISTURE!!!!..... FANG!!!.... THE BLACK SCORPION!!!!.... THE "ENFORCER"...RICK WRIGHT!!!!.... GIGANTOR!!!... KNIGHTMARE!!!.... "INHUMAN".....LOGAN RICHARDS!!!... Representing the WCWF......DRAGO!!!!!..... DEVILMAN!!!..... and THE GRAPPLER CRIS RIO!!!!... THE FROZEN WONDER!!!!.... OVERGIVER!!!... THE MISSIONARY!!!.... Representing DESTRUCTION INCORPORATED.....KEVIN AUSTIN!!!!.... "TOP DOG" ..RICK STYLES!!!!.... Representing the LETHAL ALLIES...here is LETHAL WEAPON RAZI MAHMOOD!!!... TOMMY VAN DAM!!!!... SICK RICK!!... The Deadly Wrestling Organization Eastern Champion...SUPER DESTROYER!!! and the former World Champion....APOLLO!!!!! RING ANNOUNCER: LET THE BEST MAN WIN!!!! (DING, DING, DING!!!) (Crowd POPS!!) Robert Jackson: ALRIGHT, here we go!! The participants feeling each other out!! We have a big brawl, it's kind of hard to keep up with all of the action!! MOISTURE dropkicks FANG....APOLLO goes right to SICK RICK and they are slugging it out...SUPER DESTROYER and TOMMY VAN DAM are having a go in the other corner!!! TVD with a corner PISTON punch on SUPER DESTROYER!! These two will meet on CHRISTMAS EVE for the DWO Eastern Title!! FROZEN WONDER and KEVIN AUSTIN are trying to eliminate DRAGO!! Johnny Cassimo: Where is the Great James???!?!?!?! Robert Jackson: SHUT UP!!! Super Destroyer yanks on the tights of TVD and gives Tommy and ATOMIC DROP on the way down!! SUPER D Clotheslines TVD to the mat!!! APOLLO IRISH WHIPS SICK RICK!!! RICK hangs on to the ropes and APOLLO misses a DROP KICK!! RICK on top of APOLLO and pounding away at his head!!! Johnny Cassimo: Look at DRAGO and GIGANTOR!!! The big men are slugging it out!!! RICK STYLES helps out GIGANTOR, they double team DRAGO!! GIGANTOR holds up DRAGO, STYLES throwing rights and lefts to the midsection of DRAGO, and CRIS RIO from behind dropkicks RICK STYLES!! DEVILMAN from behind hits GIGANTOR with a DOUBLE AX HANDLE from the second turnbuckle!! Robert Jackson: APOLLO is on the mat...SICK RICK to the top turnbuckle, he better be careful!! RICK off, but APOLLO MOVES! Rick is down on the mat now!!! APOLLO off the ropes and ELBOWDROPS SICK RICK!!!! Johnny Cassimo: The two newcomers are battling on the ropes....FANG is trying to get KNIGHTMARE over the top rope, but KNIGHTMARE is holding on!! Almost out, Almost out! WOAH!!! KNIGHTMARE has been eliminated...FROZEN WONDER helped FANG to get KNIGHTMARE over the top rope!!! FANG shakes the hand of the FROZEN WONDER and MOISTURE from behind clips the leg of FANG!! That was a set up and that stupid FANG fell for it!! Robert Jackson: We are down to 20 Men!! MOISTURE is kicking away at FANG!! FROZEN WONDER now applies the FIGURE FOUR to FANG!!! Johnny Cassimo: INHUMAN bodyslams BLACK SCORPION!!!! OH!! WALTER CHANCE clotheslines INHUMAN from behind!! WALTER to the second turnbuckle...OH NO!!! RAZI MAHMOOD was just whipped off the ropes by RICK STYLES and WALTER CHANCE was knocked off the turnbuckle and was eliminated!!! Robert Jackson: We are down to 19!!! DEVILMAN and GIGANTOR are fighting, and i mean FIGHTING!! DEVILMAN sends GIGANTOR off the ropes and SIDEWALK SLAMS him!!! DEVILMAN picks up GIGANTOR and DDT'S GIGANTOR!!!! DEVILMAN picks up GIGANTOR and tries to get him over the top, but just cant get him up all the way, OH NO!! GIGANTOR falls on DEVILMAN! He was too much weight for DEVILMAN!!! Johnny Cassimo: RAZI MAHMOOD now battling RICK WRIGHT!! RAZI sends WRIGHT off the ropes and FRANKENSTEINER!!! RAZI picks up WRIGHT by the back of the head and RUNS WITH HIM and sends him over the top rope!! RICK WRIGHT ELIMINATED!!! Robert Jackson: KEVIN AUSTIN and SUPER DESTROYER going at it now!! AUSTIN setting up SUPER DESTROYER for a DDT!!! SUPER D grabs the ropes and AUSTIN falls to the mat!! DESTROYER down for an elbowdrop, but AUSTIN MOVES!!!! AUSTIN applying a CAMEL CLUTCH to SUPER DESTROYER!!! OH!! TOMMY VAN DAM grabs SUPER D'S LEGS while in the camel clutch and pulls up on them to further the punishing effect of the Camel Clutch!! Johnny Cassimo: LOOT AT APOLLO and SICK RICK!! APOLLO has SICK RICK on the top turnbuckle!!! APOLLO going for a SUPERPLEX!!! RICK blocks it!!! RICK ..POWERBOMBS APOLLO off the top turnbuckle!! He is taking moves from DARK VANITY!!! That was a devastating move!! Robert Jackson: FROZEN WONDER and FANG still battling!!! WONDER sets up FANG for the REVERSE DDT!!! NO!!! BLACK SCORPION clotheslines WONDER down....FANG still feels the effects of the hold, but WONDER got what he deserves too!! SCORPION sets up FROZEN WONDER.....SCORPION to the top.....off with a MOONSAULT!!! WHAT A MOONSAULT!!! Johnny Cassimo: SCORPION sets WONDER on the top turnbuckle...HURICANARA!!!!! SCORPION reminds me of someone else with a closer name, but he's less FUNKY!! Robert Jackson: MOISTURE bodyslams FANG!! MOISTURE picks up FANG for a POWERSLAM!!! MOISTURE POWERSLAMS FANG RIGHT OUT OF THE RING!!! FANG ELIMINATED!!! OH MY GOD!! RAZI MAHMOOD clotheslines MOISTURE from behind sending MOISTURE over the top rope to the outside!! MOISTURE ELIMINATED!!! Johnny Cassimo: We are down to 16 men!! THE MISSIONARY with a SHORT ARM CLOTHESLINE on INHUMAN!!! The MISSIONARY has been in the middle of things and it was so hard to see him over there!! MISSIONARY SEES APOLLO!!! MISSIONARY holds up APOLLO for DEVILMAN!! DEVILMAN going for a SUPERKICK!! APOLLO MOVES!! MISSIONARY gets hit with the SUPERKICK!! DEVILMAN turns his back in shame....APOLLO clotheslines DEVILMAN over the top!! DEVILMAN ELIMINATED!!! CRIS RIO charges at APOLLO, APOLLO ducks and BACK BODY DROPS CRIS RIO OVER THE TOP onto DEVILMAN!!! RIO ELIMINATED!!! Robert Jackson: OVERGIVER and TOMMY VAN DAM are slugging away at each other! OVERGIVER has been taking it easy in the battle royal. VAN DAM sends OVERGIVER off the ropes..BIG BOOT!! OVERGIVER hits the mat!! VAN DAM to the top turnbuckle..OVERGIVER staggers to his feet...VAN DAM off and FRANKENSTEINERS OVERGIVER!! Johnny Cassimo: DRAGO has KEVIN AUSTIN by the throat!! CHOKESLAM!! DRAGO CHOKESLAMMED KEVIN AUSTIN right over the top rope to the outside....AUSTIN HAS BEEN ELIMINATED!!! Robert Jackson: SUPER DESTROYER and INHUMAN going at it!! SUPER D has INHUMAN in a sleeper hold!! INHUMAN is going out!! SUPER D APPLIES THE MANDIBLE CLAW!! SUPER D holding INHUMAN in the prone position and GIGANTOR LEGDROPS THE PARALYZED INHUMAN!!! WHAT!! INHUMAN JUST STOOD RIGHT UP!! He has no tolerance of pain!!! INHUMAN grabs GIGANTOR and SUPER D and rams their HEADS TOGETHER!! DOUBLE NOGGIN KNOCKER!! Johnny Cassimo: RICK STYLES with the ASIATIC SPIKE on RAZI MAHMOOD!! That is a paralyzing hold used by TERRY GORDY!! MAHMOOD is going out!! NO!! BLACK SCORPION kicks at the head of RICK STYLES!!! SCORPION picks up STYLES and they are fighting at the ropes trying to get each other over!!!! MAHMOOD clotheslines both men OVER THE TOP!!! STYLES and SCORPION have been eliminated!!!!! Robert Jackson: MISSIONARY and SICK RICK are pounding away at each other!! OH NO WAIT!! THEY BOTH SPOT APOLLO!! MISSIONARY and SICK RICK double team APOLLO!!!! SICK RICK clotheslines APOLLO from behind...APOLLO was kicking a prone OVERGIVER!! MISSIONARY picks up APOLLO and holds him for SICK RICK!! RICK slaps APOLLO across the face!!! RICK off the ropes and attempts a clothesline on APOLLO, but MISSIONARY is still holding him!! APOLLO ducks and RICK clotheslines MISSIONARY over the top rope!!! MISSIONARY has been eliminated!!! Johnny Cassimo: RICK quickly now DROPKICKS APOLLO!!!! RICK knew what he was doing all along!! He wants APOLLO to himself!!! Robert Jackson: OH NO!! DRAGO has GIGANTOR up for a POWERBOMB!!! DRAGO POWERBOMBS GIGANTOR OVER THE TOP ROPE TO THE OUTSIDE!! GIGANTOR is powerbombed onto the SPANISH ANNOUNCER'S TABLE!!! GIGANTOR ELIMINATED!!! Johnny Cassimo: TOMMY VAN DAM VERTICAL SUPLEXES SICK RICK!!!! TVD with a beautiful move!!! VAN DAM picks up SICK RICK and goes to throw him out but RICK slides out of the ring under the bottom rope!! RICK is getting a chair!! RICK brings the chair into the ring!!!!!! RICK HITS FROZEN WONDER WITH THE CHAIR and the WONDER falls OVER THE TOP!! WONDER HAS BEEN ELIMINATED!!!! Robert Jackson: VAN DAM has OVERGIVER hanging by a thread!! OVERGIVER FALLS OUTSIDE!! VAN DAM HAS ELIMINATED OVERGIVER!!! Johnny Cassimo: DRAGO fighting on the ropes with INHUMAN!! DRAGO almost has him over!! NO!!!! SUPER DESTROYER ELIMINATES THEM BOTH WITH A CLOTHESLINE!!! DRAGO and INHUMAN have been eliminated!! Robert Jackson: We are down to the final 5, and just look at who they are!! The former champ, the Easter Champ and his rival, former Tag Team Champ, and a current contender to the vacant World Title!! This should be most interesting!! Johnny Cassimo: All five taking time to regroup!! TOMMY VAN DAM CHARGES SUPER DESTROYER!!!! VAN DAM is met by a dropkick from SUPER D!! These two enemies are going to throw the rule book out the window!! Robert Jackson: SICK RICK and RAZI MAHMOOD are battling!!! APOLLO catching a breather in the corner!!! NOT ANYMORE!! RICK whips MAHMOOD into the corner and APOLLO is squashed!!! MAHMOOD inadvertantly whipped into APOLLO!! RICK into the corner and AVALANCHES APOLLO and MAHMOOD!!! RICK sunset flips MAHMOOD out of the corner....and APOLLO charges out with a CLOTHESLINE!! That was more like a LARIAT!!! Johnny Cassimo: APOLLO picks up SICK RICK into a BACKBREAKER!! MAHMOOD off the ropes and LEGDROPS SICK RICK!!!!! Robert Jackson: TOMMY VAN DAM has SUPER D on the top turnbuckle....HURICANARA!!!! VAN DAM back to the top turnbuckle....NO!! APOLLO shakes the ropes....VAN DAM HAS BEEN ELIMINATED!! What an awesome showing by the newcomer!!!! Johnny Cassimo: VAN DAM is not leaving RINGSIDE!! APOLLO has SUPER D, and whips him into the ropes....VAN DAM PULLS DOWN THE TOP ROPE AND SUPER D HAS BEEN ELIMINATED!!!!! VAN DAM and SUPER D are fighting back to the locker room!!! Robert Jackson: THE FINAL 3!!!! APOLLO, SICK RICK, and RAZI MAHMOOD!!! Each man in a seperate corner!! APOLLO must feel outnumbered!!! Johnny Cassimo: Who cares how APOLLO feels? How do I feel? Two of my favorites are in there! Who do I root for?? Robert Jackson: OH NO!!!!! HERE COMES JAWAAD MAHMOOD, DARK VANITY , and that MAFIOSO!!! Johnny Cassimo: Who was that masked man? Robert Jackson: They are surrounding the ring!!!! APOLLO and SICK RICK are Locked up!! APOLLO SUPLEXES SICK RICK!!! OH NO!!! JAWAAD and DV are on the apron!!! THEY ARE IN THE RING!!! Johnny Cassimo: APOLLO backing off into a corner, RICK is out of it!! JAWAAD and RAZI whispering!!! DARK VANITY has the SINGAPORE CANE!!! DARK VANITY charges APOLLO!!! APOLLO with a big boot to DV's midsection!! APOLLO tosses out DV!!!!!!!!!! Robert Jackson: RAZI picks up the CANE!! RAZI charges to APOLLO!!!! APOLLO back body drops RAZI OVER THE TOP!!! RAZI MAHMOOD has been ELIMINATED!! JAWAAD has the cane and this time APOLLO is hit in the back of the head with it!! JAWAAD MAHMOOD is attacking APOLLO with the SINGAPORE CANE!!!!! [THE CROWD EXPLODES AS ROBSKI RUNS OUT...] Johnny Cassimo: HERE HE COMES!!!! .......ROBSKI!!!!!! Robert Jackson: YES!!! ROBSKI in the ring!!! JAWAAD doesn't see him!!! ROBSKI has a CHAIR!!! ROBSKI hits JAWAAD with the chair!!!! ROBSKI tosses JAWAAD out of the ring!! THIS CAPACITY CROWD IS GOING NUTS!!!! Johnny Cassimo: SICK RICK is getting back up to his feet!! Robert Jackson: ROBSKI CLOTHESLINED SICK RICK OVER THE TOP!!!! RICK ELIMINATED!!!! APOLLO WINS!!!! (DING, DING, DING!!!!) RING ANNOUNCER: Ladies and Gentlemen, the winner of this contest and a shot at the DEADLY WRESTLING ORGANIZATION NATIONAL CHAMPIONSHIP......APOLLO!!!!! (CROWD POPS!!!!!!!) Robert Jackson: APOLLO wins it!! APOLLO WINS!! APOLLO will face his tag team partner later on here tonight!! THE SUPER POWERS WILL COLLIDE!! Robski is walking back to the locker room area and he is recieving a lot of CHEERS!! Johnny Cassimo: This is a travesty. RICK needs a TITLE MATCH! He's the best!!! Robert Jackson: I'm sure after his appearance here tonight, the DWO Championship Comittee will definitely give SICK RICK title matches in the near future!!! WOW! WHAT A MATCH!!! Johnny Cassimo: Well, THEY BETTER!!!
