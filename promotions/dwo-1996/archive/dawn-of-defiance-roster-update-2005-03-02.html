Dawn of Defiance — Updated Card Info and Roster and APP

Mar 2, 2005, 12:00:00 AM
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The DWO Dawn of Defiance Card is shaping up with some great stars signing on the dotted line. With all the events surrounding the card, I've decided to send out a Daily News Feed with all the latest updates and news on the card.
**** DWO Officials have spoken to DWO Champion "The Savior" Jamie Underwood about appearing on the Dawn of Defiance Card. As many of you know, Underwood and DWO CEO Mike Bilodeau had a falling out recently surrounding events in the BWA. Reports of this meeting are sketchy at best, but what we do know is that Underwood has heard the challenge from "Top Dog" Rick Styles, and is also pondering challenging the man he had a run in with at DWO WrestleKade X, the legendary Brody Thunder. Talks are in progress also with Brody
Thunder.
**** DWO/UEW/WLW Legend Rob Magnum has signed on the dotted line and has said he may consider tossing out a challenge to one of his greatest rivals, DWO National Champion The Mercenary.
**** DWO Officials have also been in contact with Heidi Leick and the team of Damage, Inc. Early reports have it that Leick and her team will not be on the card in a wrestling capacity, but maybe in some other capacity.
**** In what could be the most interesting challenge to date, former DWO Champion J'ster has signed on to appear on the card and is considering tossing out a challenge to another wrestling legend, "Hot
Stuff" Alex Martinez, another DWO legend.
**** Also signed is Rick Marley, a WLW superstar who appeard in the WLW Five Pillars Match at DWO WrestleKade X. Marley is pondering who his opponent should be.
