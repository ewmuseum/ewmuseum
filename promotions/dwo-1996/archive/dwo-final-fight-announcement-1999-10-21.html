Hall of Fame Inductions and Card Is Tributed to the following:
Apollo J'Ster Sick Rick Robski Sir Giles Cavendish Shawn Roy "Top Dog" Rick Styles Dark Vanity The Merchants of Chaos Razi & Jawaad Mahmood Kyle Kamikaze Professor Payne Nick Leshenn Alex Martinez ROUND ONE of the FINAL FIGHT Tournament Includes the following matches just signed: Jesse "The Man" Muldoon vs. "Macarena" Matt Booth STIPULATIONS: "I Quit" Match. The winner must get his opponent to say "I
Quit" on the microphone.
IN OTHER NEWS: ---Soon-to-be Hall of Famer, Nick "The Nighthawk" Leshenn has signed for FINAL FIGHT. His opponent is under wraps at the moment, but will be revealed soon! INVITATION: The DWO invites all federations on the net to send their 5 TOP GUYS! If you are a fed head, please distribute this information to your league:
FINAL FIGHT will be a celebration of the Deadly Wrestling Organization as it present's it's last card ever! This event will be dedicated to the careers of all the wrestlers that made the DWO what it was including, Sick
Rick, Apollo, The "Top Dog" Rick Styles, Alex Martinez, Magnus Colby, The
Merchants of Chaos, Alex Extreme, Nick Leshenn, Professor Payne, Shawn Roy,
Dark Vanity, Razi and Jawaad Mahmood, Robski, and J'ster!
All of these individuals will be inducted on this night into the DWO Hall of Fame. The card will be a tribute to them! Whether or not they will compete on the card is still in question!
What will the card consist of? The main attraction will be a 16 man singles tournament. All of the first round matches will have special DWO-Like stipulations. The second round will be a round robin, with wrestlers never knowing who their opponent will be until the final round. The final rounds will be a surprise stipulation, that will be announced the night of the card.
