A New DWO Returns with a New Concept

Feb 25, 2005, 12:00:00 AM
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Ladies and Gentlemen, The Deadly Wrestling Organization is set to return for one more Super
Card. Although, there will be some changes, including a new name.
The Deadly Wrestling Organization is now .... the Defiant Wrestling
Organization. And the first Supercard for the NEW DWO will be scheduled shortly. But first, the concept. (does this sound familiar? can you say rip off? I don't care.) The card will be made up of OPEN CHALLENGE MATCHES! That's right. YOU pick who YOU want to face and CHALLENGE them. IF they accept YOU
GET YOUR MATCH! Unfortunately for you, you won't know until the night of the card if your challengee accepted or if someone else is taking your challenge.
Here's how it works, sign up, fill out your new DWO App.... When a good size roster is assembled, the roster will be sent out for everyone to read over ...
Then comes the first flash package ... you flash talking about who you want to face .... Everyone gets a chance to read over the flashes and see who they want to face .... Then comes the DEFIANT BALLOT! You send in up to THREE wrestlers you want to compete against ... If there's a match with the guy you really wanted, then there will be your match ... If not, we'll go down the ballot making the best matches as possible.
However, here's the "shocking twist." You will not know who you will face until the night of the card. Also, the matches will have mystery stipulations that you won't even know about. So....how are you supposed to flash and strat for this? Flashing will be based on character development and how well you get your character over. Stratting will be based on how imaginative you can be with the unknown. This will all be about creativity and inginiuity.
FLASHES and STRATS will be counted toward your victory/defeat as this is still a COMPETITION!

Most apps will insist that your strengths and weaknesses even out (if you have three strengths you must have three weaknesses). I say bollocks. These are supposed to be realistic characters, and real people aren't products of simple mathmatical equations. Try your best to keep them equal, yes, but if you have one more of one than the other, I'm not going to get angry and tear it up. I've had my strengths and weaknesses be one off like that before, and spent way too much time trying to cut one out or split one up or make a new one to reach this magic equilibrium. The idea is to create balanced characters (and prevent people from sending in Invincible Bill Goldberg type apps, which are boring and WILL be sent into the void where junk emails go to die).
I mean, I'm not going to throw out your app just because you have 5 strengths listed and only 4 weaknesses (aslong as they balance each other). Though I might give preferential treatment to the app that had 4 strengths and 5 weaknesses.
