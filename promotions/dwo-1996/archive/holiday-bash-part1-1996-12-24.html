Welcome to the WORLD'S MOST FAMOUS ARENA, or atleast its theater, MADISON SQUARE GARDEN!! Tonight, all the DWO titles will be up for grabs! Two of them in a cage, and one in a STREET FIGHT IN CORONA, NEW YORK!! With me today is my broadcast partner, ...Johnny Cassimo! Johnny Cassimo: Hey all you punks and pricks!! I am John Cassimo, like it or not, IT'S YOUR PLEASURE!! First of all, SHAWN ROY IS A HELL OF AN IDIOT! He, himself, actually will face THE LETHAL WEAPON RAZI MAHMOOD, right here tonight! Robert Jackson: Yes, the announcement was STARTLING, Razi Mahmood and Shawn Roy FINALLY meet, one on one, right here in DWO, and here at HOLIDAY BASH! Johnny Cassimo: That's not all that's on this great card! SIR GILES CAVENDISH MP GOES UP AGAINST J'STER FOR THE VACANT DWO WORLD HEAVYWEIGHT TITLE! J'ster defeated Cavendish a few weeks back, only to have it vacated because of the controversy he was going to win it in. But Jackson, J'STER WAS GOING TO WIN IF NO ONE HAD INTERFERED IN THE MATCHUP! You have to agree!! Robert Jackson: That, I do. But if you think about it, this would be the main event here tonight anyway, J'ster would've most likely defend his crown, against Cavendish. Johnny Cassimo: But, you don't understand! It's a different thing coming in as the champion, and not. If J'ster was the World Champ, he'd have nothing to worry about, he's slick and always has a trick up his sleeve to win a match, and especially to retain a title! Robert Jackson: Well, on to other things. This event also will be MIKE BILODEAU'S LAST night as DWO Co-President. He's replacement will be announced RIGHT HERE TONIGHT! Several rumors have been thrown around for this situation. Shawn Roy taking complete power, John J. Sylvia taking over, Mark R. Rose, and even SIR GILES CAVENDISH MP!! Johnny Cassimo: And what about ME?!?!?! Robert Jackson: What about you? Johnny Cassimo: I'd be PERFECT as THE DWO CO-PRESIDENT!! You won't see guys like Apollo, American Hero, they can't wrestle, so they WON'T WRESTLE!!! Robert Jackson: Oh, giving you 50% power to the organization is a threat, and severe trouble, I'm sure who ever becomes the President here tonight, will be indeed perfect for the position. Johnny Cassimo: Yeah... right.... Robert Jackson: Fans! Enough talk, lets kick things off here at HOLIDAY BASH!!! RING ANNOUNCER: Ladies and gentlemen, welcome to DWO HOLIDAY BASH!! The first contest here tonight, is scheduled for ONE FALL! Introducing first, from NEW YORK CITY! Weighing in at 400 pounds! Accompanied to the ring by Crack Joint! Here is ...THE GANGSTA! [A mixture of boos and cheers as The Gangsta walks down the aisle.] Robert Jackson: Our first matchup here, and this is the first time The Gangsta enters the ring, remember, a few weeks back. Gangsta took on THREE MEN in ONE NIGHT! If it wasn't for Rick Styles, Gangsta would've faired well in all of his matches, but because of The Top Dog, he ended up in the HOSPITAL! Johnny Cassimo: There isn't ONE DOUBT of who is going to win this matchup here today. Styles is a better man, and a much better athelete. He's going to leave here today, and there was a PACT made, that if J'ster wins the title, RICK STYLES WOULD GET THE FIRST SHOT! And he is the NUMBER ONE CONTENDER! RING ANNOUNCER: His opponent! Weighing 235 pounds! He is accompanied to the ring by MARK R. ROSE!! Ladies and gentlemen, this is ........THE TOP DOG!! ...........RRRRICK STYLES!!!! [A huge chorus of jeers from the crowd starts.] Robert Jackson: Rick Styles is making is way down the aisle! And boy does he look cocky. But that's him, and he'll be watching the main event here tonight, VERY CLOSELY!! Johnny Cassimo: Watch this match. J'ster, if you win tonight, THIS IS YOUR NEXT OPPONENT!! Robert Jackson: Where did you get this information?? You're the king of all marks, NO ONE SHOULD BELIEVE ANYTHING YOU SAY! *************************************** *"TOP DOG" RICK STYLES VS. THE GANGSTA* *************************************** [DING! DING! DING!] Robert Jackson: The bell rings, Gangsta and Styles lock up! Johnny Cassimo: Rick Styles quickly slaps on a SLEEPERHOLD but Gangsta gets to the ropes! Robert Jackson: Styles throws The Gangsta out of the ring! Johnny Cassimo: Rick Styles goes to the outside. Robert Jackson: Styles hits The Gangsta with a kneelift and comes back into the squared circle and Gangsta follows him back in.... Johnny Cassimo: The Gangsta kicks Rick Styles in the midsection. Robert Jackson: Rick Styles slaps on a sleeperhold!! Johnny Cassimo: The Gangsta gets to the ropes once again. Robert Jackson: Gangsta slaps Styles in the face! Johnny Cassimo: Gangsta sends Styles into the ropes. Robert Jackson: The Gangsta catches Rick Styles as he comes off the ropes. Johnny Cassimo: The Gangsta picks up Styles for a backbreaker but Rick Styles counters with a head scissors takedown! Robert Jackson: Rick Styles executes a reverse neckbreaker!! Johnny Cassimo: That was like a jawbreaker right there. Similar to Kevin Austin's move. Robert Jackson: Rick Styles climbs up to the top rope! Johnny Cassimo: Here comes Rick Styles, flying down with a lariat.... Robert Jackson: THE GANGSTA NAILS HIM WITH A FIST TO THE MIDSECTION!! Johnny Cassimo: That's the risk of attempting those high flying moves, it's easier to counter from the ground than hitting from the air! Robert Jackson: Gangsta sets up Rick Styles for a DDT. Johnny Cassimo: Rick Styles counter it with a BACK BODY DROP! Robert Jackson: Rick Styles picks up The Gangsta with a hairpull and runs into the ropes. Johnny Cassimo: The Gangsta catches Rick Styles and executes a SPINEBUSTER! Robert Jackson: The Gangsta covers! 1... 2... kickout! Johnny Cassimo: The Gangsta climbs up to the top! That's over 400 pounds of man on that small turnbuckle! Robert Jackson: Gangsta hits Rick Styles with a flying kneedrop!! Johnny Cassimo: The Gangsta goes for a jumping DDT, but Rick Styles counters it with a backdrop! Robert Jackson: Rick Styles whips The Gangsta into the ropes. Johnny Cassimo: Rick Styles hits The Gangsta with a thrust kick! Robert Jackson: Styles bounces off the ropes and The Gangsta nails him with a MAFIA KICK! Johnny Cassimo: The Gangsta sends Rick Styles into the ropes. Robert Jackson: The Gangsta takes Styles down with a shoulderblock! Johnny Cassimo: Gangsta hits Rick Styles with a kneelift. Robert Jackson: The Gangsta executes a SPINNING DDT!!! Johnny Cassimo: The Gangsta comes off the ropes and misses with a splash as Rick Styles rolled away! Robert Jackson: The Top Dog picks up The Gangsta with a hairpull. Johnny Cassimo: Styles nails Gangsta with a roundhouse right! Gangsta falls down to the mat! Robert Jackson: Irish whip by Rick Styles reversed by The Gangsta. Johnny Cassimo: The Gangsta hits Rick Styles with a spinwheel kick! Robert Jackson: The Gangsta picks up Rick Styles. Johnny Cassimo: The Gangsta slaps on a FULL NELSON!!! Robert Jackson: This could be the end for THE TOP DOG! Styles trips up Gangsta with his leg and escapes the Full Nelson! Nice move by Rick Styles!! Johnny Cassimo: Look at all the offense by The Gangsta, but Rick Styles is too much for him. He used the full nelson, and thought he had Rick Styles trapped. But every man has 2 legs, don't forget that! Robert Jackson: And Styles used his legs and tripped The Gangsta. He knew he could hit with an elbows or fists because he was trapped in the Full Nelson, and he went to the bottom and broke free. Johnny Cassimo: The Gangsta goes for a clothesline, ducked by Top Dog Rick Styles! Robert Jackson: STYLES NAILS THE GANGSTA WITH A SUPERKICK! Johnny Cassimo: Rick Styles covers! Hooks a leg! 1... 2... kickout! Robert Jackson: Rick Styles is setting The Gangsta up for a SUPLEX!! He is going to suplex the 400 plus pounder! Johnny Cassimo: The Gangsta blocks it and executes a suplex of his own!! Robert Jackson: THE GANGSTA BRIDGES BACK!! 1... 2... kickout! Johnny Cassimo: The Gangsta sends Rick Styles into the turnbuckles. Robert Jackson: The Gangsta charges in with an elbow! Johnny Cassimo: Rick Styles lifts up his leg and The Gangsta gets nailed!! Robert Jackson: Styles climbs up to the middle turnbuckle! He has Gangsta in a headlock! Johnny Cassimo: A beautiful backspin DDT by THE TOP DOG!! Robert Jackson: Rick Styles climbs up to the top turnbuckle. Johnny Cassimo: Rick Styles goes for a FLYING LEGDROP!! The Gangsta rolls away!! Robert Jackson: Styles looks as if he has hurt his knee! Johnny Cassimo: The Gangsta wasting no time going to work on Styles' bad knee. Robert Jackson: Gangsta stomps away on Styles' right knee! That's the one he hurt. Johnny Cassimo: The Gangsta picks up Rick Styles with a hairpull. The Gangsta dropkicks Rick Styles right on the back of his injured knee! Robert Jackson: Styles goes down and he is really hurt! Gangsta raises his arm, and he is taking credit for what has happened to Rick Styles. Johnny Cassimo: No, Gangsta did not hurt Styles. Styles missed with a high risk move, and got hurt, if Gangsta does not win this match, he will be a fool. I suggest that he continue to work on Rick Styles... Robert Jackson: Gangsta walks over towards Rick Styles. RICK STYLES ROLLS HIM UP IN AN INSIDE CRADLE!!! 1... 2... kickout! Johnny Cassimo: Gangsta gets back up and charges towards Styles! Robert Jackson: Bad knee and all, STLYES WITH AN ARMDRAG TAKEDOWN! Johnny Cassimo: Rick Styles rolls out of the ring, and Mark R. Rose walks over towards him. Robert Jackson: A discussion going on right now, between Rose and Styles. Johnny Cassimo: STYLES GRABS A CHAIR AND HIS GOING BACK INTO THE RING! THE GANGSTA HAS HIS BACK TURNED ON RICK STYLES! Robert Jackson: STYLES NAILS THE GANGSTA RIGHT ON THE BACK OF THE HEAD WITH THE CHAIR!!! ANOTHER SHOT TO THE GANGSTA'S BACK!! Johnny Cassimo: Styles tosses the chair outside. Styles picks up The Gangsta and places him on the top turnbuckle! Robert Jackson: Rick Styles is going up! I can't believe this!! He's going for a SUPERPLEX! Johnny Cassimo: Mark R. Rose is grabbing a table and setting it up at ringside, near the turnbuckle where Styles and Gangsta are! Robert Jackson: What the hell is going on here?? Johnny Cassimo: Rick Styles lifts up The Gangsta!!! Robert Jackson: STYLES DUMPS THE GANGSTA DOWN FACE FIRST RIGHT THROUGH THE TABLE, ON TO THE CONCRETE FLOOR!! Johnny Cassimo: And Styles comes flying down nailing The Gangsta with a DOUBLE AX HAND!! Robert Jackson: Styles throws The Gangsta back into the ring! Johnny Cassimo: Rick Styles goes back in! He covers!! 1... 2... 3!!! [DING! DING! DING!] Robert Jackson: Rick Styles wins it!! RING ANNOUNCER: The winner of this matchup, is.... THE TOP DOG... RICK STYLES!!! ******************************* *WINNER: "TOP DOG" RICK STYLES* ******************************* Robert Jackson: A big win for TOP DOG RICK STYLES here at Holiday Bash! Styles is now definitely looking for a golden oppurtunity here in DWO! He just might go out looking for the DWO National Title, or the World Title! Johnny Cassimo: He deserves it, he's been fighting on the undercard for too long, it's time he gets his shots at the titles, in the main event matches. Robert Jackson: Well, you can not rush things here, obviously he was focussed on this matchup here tonight, and he will be more concentrated on his quest for gold. Johnny Cassimo: Ofcourse he will. Rick Styles has gone through a 7 foot 4 inch, 400 pounder right here tonight, that takes a lot of courage, and a lot of guts. Can you doubt, for one momment, the he won't be able to take out someone like APOLLO? Robert Jackson: As wise as Styles is, I don't think he'd be able to defeat Apollo. But Sick Rick will have the chance tonight! Who do you think will win at that matchup?? Johnny Cassimo: That's an EASY question, SICK RICK! He's coming into this match, without anything on his back. He's going to be totally focussed in that cage, when he meets Apollo. Apollo, on the other hand, comes in with MANY distractions. The American Hero, J'ster, the list goes on... Robert Jackson: WHAT?! Sick Rick has had heated words with the most annoying people on the face of this planet.... THE LETHAL ALLIANCE!! There going to watch Apollo take on Sick Rick, and might just make it their business! As for Apollo's feud with J'ster, that lasted about 4 months, but both of them have moved on... Johnny Cassimo: Yeah, J'ster is going to get the oppurtunity to fight Sir Giles Cavendish MP for APOLLO'S TITLE! Robert Jackson: It's DWO'S TITLE! And you know something... Johnny Cassimo: What? Robert Jackson: In a one on one match, J'ster has NEVER defeated Apollo! In APW's first card, Sunday Night Slam Masters, both of them met. Apollo suffered a back injury that night as J'ster THREW Apollo out of the steel cage! Then, two months later, they finally met in the finals for the DWO World Title Tournament at WrestleKade! And Apollo won that one. Johnny Cassimo: Well come on, the only reason Apollo won the cage match was because J'ster tiger drived him out of the cage. Then, Dark Vanity TURNED on J'ster at WrestleKade, and COST J'STER THE WORLD TITLE!! Robert Jackson: To the Ring Announcer we go, once again! RING ANNOUNCER: The next match, is for the DEADLY WRESTLING ORGANIZATION EASTERN CHAMPIONSHIP!! It's scheduled for ONE FALL! Introducing first, he is the challenger! Weighing in at 222 pounds! Here is..... LIONHEART! ....TOMMY VAN DAM!!! [Tommy Van Dam walks out on to the aisle way. The crowd just explodes and goes crazy! Tommy Van Dam enters the ring looking very determined.] Robert Jackson: Take a look at Tommy Van Dam! He is definitely ready for Super Destroyer, and Van Dam said it best! It's going to be a WAR! Johnny Cassimo: It's going to be a war. But it's outcome will have Super Destroyer walking out of here with the DWO Eastern Title. It may be brutal, but it's going to get ugly!! AND IT'S GOING TO BE GOOD!!! RING ANNOUNCER: Introducing next! Coming down the aisle at this time. He is the DWO Eastern Champion!! He is led to the ring by THE MASTERMIND!! He weighs in at 269 pounds, ladies and gentleman, this is...... SUPER DESTROYER!!!!! [Crowd jeers. The building formally known as The Paramount fills up with boos, and the fans lets Super Destroyer know that he's not loved. Destroyer walks down the aisle, looking like usual, Stone Cold. Destroyer climbs into the ring and raises his arm with the title belt, the crowd starts to boo louder.] Robert Jackson: Get ready! It's going to be BRUTAL!! *********************************** *TOMMY VAN DAM VS. SUPER DESTROYER* *********************************** [DING! DING! DING!] Robert Jackson: Van Dam and Destroyer lock up!! Johnny Cassimo: Super Destroyer with a headlock takedown. Robert Jackson: Tommy Van Dam gets right back up! Johnny Cassimo: Tommy Van Dam with an irish whip, reversal by Super Destroyer! Robert Jackson: Van Dam nails Destroyer with a shoulderblock! But Super Destroyer doesn't go down... Johnny Cassimo: Tommy Van Dam climbs up to the top turnbuckle. Robert Jackson: Tommy Van Dam nails Super Destroyer with a FLYING LARIAT!!! Johnny Cassimo: Tommy Van Dam slaps on a headlock. Johnny Cassimo: Super D's waiting for Van Dam to get back on his feet. Robert Jackson: Destroyer comes flying down with a cross bodyblock but Tommy Van Dam steps away!! Johnny Cassimo: Super Destroyer crashes down on to the canvas! Van Dam covers! 1... 2... kickout! Robert Jackson: Tommy Van Dam picks up Super Destroyer. Johnny Cassimo: Tommy Van Dam whips Super Destroyer into the ropes. Robert Jackson: Van Dam goes for a backdrop but Destroyer with a leap frog! Robert Jackson: Super Destroyer with a BACKDROP! Johnny Cassimo: Super Destroyer bounces off the ropes and hits Tommy Van Dam with an elbow drop! Robert Jackson: Destroyer picks up Tommy Van Dam.
