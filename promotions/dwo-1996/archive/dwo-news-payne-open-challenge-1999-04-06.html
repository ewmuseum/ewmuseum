DWO: Payne Pulled from the Card, Bilodeau's Open Challenge

Apr 6, 1999, 12:00:00 AM
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
THE DEADLY WRESTLING ORGANIZATION PRESENTS D E A D L Y D R A W 2 C O N T R O L C E N T E R L I V E F R O M DWO S T U D I O S -------------------------------------------------------------- ("Back in Black" by AC/DC plays as we fade into the DWO Studios. Pictures of Nick Leshenn, Professor Payne, Rick Styles, Roy Rainer, Mike Bilodeau,
Apollo, J'ster, and Sick Rick are hanging on the studio walls. We fade into the front desk, where four familiar men are standing, wearing formal suits. One is Robert Jackson, head announcer for the DWO since it's first card. The other is Johnny Cassimo, the color announcer since the first card. Another is Adrian Anderson, the latest addition to the DWO, who has been like the "Jim Ross" of e-wrestling ever since his debut last year at the Deadly Draw. The last man is DWO Owner and President, Mike Bilodeau.)
Robert Jackson: The card is being completed, you don't have much time if you want to be part of the BIGGEST Pay Per View of 1999. Get your applications in today!
Johnny Cassimo: Shut up Jackson, we have HUGE News concerning last years
DEADLY DRAW WINNER, Professor Payne is in some deep trouble, is that right
Mike Bilodeau?
Mike Bilodeau: I'm glad you asked me that Johnny. There seems to be a weak rumor floating around the internet and the DWO offices that Don Thomas, the manager and agent of Professor Payne, the former DWO Champion, and myself had some heated words at the DWO Headquarters in Utica, New York. It is indeed true that me and Mr. Thomas sat down to discuss Payne's involvement on the card, but nothing of the sort came of it. But I want to discuss one thing to put all rumors to rest. PROFESSOR PAYNE will NOT be wrestling on the DWO Deadly Draw 2 card.
Adrian Anderson: What? Well why the hell not? Mike Bilodeau: I also spoke with Payne on the phone this past Sunday,
Easter Sunday in fact. It's no secret that his professional team is undergoing extensive investigations by the U.S. Government for tax evasion and fraud. Well, Payne called here and he said he wanted to be a part of this card, but because of legal and financial burdens, he just couldn't.
Robert Jackson: Will the DWO help with his legal problems to secure him for the card? Mike Bilodeau: I'm afraid that's not possible. At this time, I want to wish Payne and his legal and professional team the best of luck in this trying time, and I want him to know that the DWO is a big supporter of the
U.S. Government in getting every penny you owe them. Payne, welcome to audit hell.
Robert Jackson: What the hell? What's wrong with you? The man was a DWO
Champion? Mike Bilodeau: You have to realize, Robert, this is a business. Maybe not to you, but I own this company and last year Payne cost us a lot of money.
And in fact, he even owes us some for the t-shirts that were made and never sold, and the cheap bastard still has the DWO Championship belt. That's the kind of man Payne is, but let's get off the subject. We have more matches to announce.
Adrian Anderson: Before we do that Bilodeau, I thought you left this evil persona behind? Mike Bilodeau: I did, Anderson. You have to realize that everything I do in this company is for the fans, is for the sponsors, and is definitely for the money. I'm not a wrestler, and I won't even be on camera at the Deadly
Draw. I just want the fans to know what's going on with their hero. I'm not the bad guy here.
Robert Jackson: Let's just get off the subject. We have one more match? Mike Bilodeau: We have, but let's run down the card first! ----DWO DEADLY DRAW 2----- LETHAL LADDER MATCH FOR $100,000 ----DWO DEADLY DRAW 2----- 2 dYnAmiC Wesley Steel & Puppet Master Nicolas Christian vs. "Hot Stuff" Scott Richards & The Trixxster -----DWO DEADLY DRAW 2------ DOUBLE LETHAL ROUND ROBIN MATCH: -----DWO DEADLY DRAW 2------ Rick Styles & Al Robertson vs. Nick Leshenn & Alexander Munroe -----DWO DEADLY DRAW 2------ LETHAL MILLENIUM MATCH -----DWO DEADLY DRAW 2------ Sick Rick and "Black Knight" Alex Martinez vs. Tommy Love & Magnus Colby ------DWO DEADLY DRAW 2------ STEEL CAGE SHOWDOWN ------DWO DEADLY DRAW 2------ Mr. Fantastic and Gorilla vs. Nicklaus Kain and "The New Superstar" Cris Rio -----DWO DEADLY DRAW 2----- THE ULTIMATE FOUR CORNER MATCH: -----DWO DEADLY DRAW 2----- Apollo & "The Awesome Lion" Moo Kame vs. Jesse Muldoon & Candyman vs. Robbie Kincade & "Tricky Kid" Derek Craven vs. The Mahmood Brothers Mike Bilodeau: If you thought that was big, listen to this: TWO RINGS FOUR MEN ONE SURVIVOR ONLY ONE MAN ADVANCES TO THE DEADLY RUMBLE: "THE SOUTHERN GENTLEMAN" TOMMY LANE MAD BRONCO CRAZY HORSE MR. YOUNG Adrian Anderson: So what do they have to do? Mike Bilodeau: That's the good part. Here are the rules: 1. Crazy Horse and Mad Bronco will fight a singles match in one ring until one of them is pinned. Robert Jackson: What are you kidding? THEY ARE TAG TEAM PARTNERS! Mike Bilodeau: I'm not finished. You know, Bodo Kolupa is still around, so shut up or you might be replaced.
2. Mr. Young and Tommy Lane will go in the second ring until one man is pinned. 3. When there are two winners, the two men will meet in a SAN ANTONIO STREET FIGHT that will take place OUTSIDE of the ALAMO DOME and will be
HARDCORE, FALLS COUNT ANYWHERE and only one of them will go to the DEADLY
RUMBLE! Adrian Anderson: That's HUGE! Mike Bilodeau: And I have one more BIG announcement concerning the DWO
Deadly Draw 2. Robert Jackson: What's that? Mike Bilodeau: I know I said earlier that I wouldn't be on camera, and that
I wouldn't be a wrestler, but I changed my mind. I was thinking during the match announcement about something I've been wanting to get off my chest for
quite some time. I'M ISSUING AN OPEN CHALLENGE to any wrestler/manager/promoter/ or anyone that wants to be famous TO FIGHT ME ONE
ON ONE IN A WRESTLING RING at the Deadly Draw. I realize I have my enemies, and here's their opportunity to face me and tell me what they think of me!
THE OPEN CHALLENGE IS OUT THERE!
