DWO: One More Night, More Legends Sign

29 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Scene opens in the DWO Studios. Robert Jackson is standing by.) Robert Jackson: Ladies and Gentlemen, welcome back to the DWO Report. Today we have three HUGE announcements concerning the DWO ONE MORE NIGHT card on Aug. 1. That includes 3 signed talents and one match already signed in the quest to crown the Living Legend. (A graphic of the first match appears on the screen. It is the Two out of Three Falls Ring of Cars Match.) Robert Jackson: The Ring of Cars match will take place outside the Utica Memorial Auditorium. The particiipants will fight inside a ring of cars. The first fall, the men will fight submission style. The second fall, the men will fight for a pinfall, and the FINAL FALL, the CARS WILL BE USED AS WEAPONS. That's right, the cars can be commandeered by the wrestlers for use on their opponent. This match is NOT being sanctioned by the DWO and not being sanctioned by the New York State Althetic Commission. (The faces of the two combatants appears on the screen.) Robert Jackson: There you see it, two living legends will battle. "The Loose Caner" Dark Vanity, making his return to the wrestling ring, with "Macarena" Matt Booth right by his side, will take on the former DWO Champion and one of the most popular faces in all of wrestling, "The Top Dog" Rick Styles, with his manager Mark Rose. (The graphic disappears.) Robert Jackson: Tonight we can also announce that one of the most beloved female wrestlers in all the world has signed for the card. That is none other than Erin McCoys. That's all for tonight, thank you ladies and gentlemen. To be on this historic card, email league President [name withheld] at [detail withheld] or [detail withheld] Thank you
