DWO World Championship: "Top Dog" Rick Styles vs. J'ster (29 Dec 1996)

29 December 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
From DWO Wrestle Mayhem - 12/28/96 ============================= Robert Jackson: We are getting ready for our Main Event matchup right now. J'ster makes his first ever DWO World Title defense, against TOP DOG RICK STYLES!! Johnny Cassimo: This match is occuring because of the deal made between the two men. Mark Rose accompanied J'ster down the aisle, and Rick Styles helped J'ster win the title. In return, J'ster would have to give Rick Styles a shot at the gold! Robert Jackson: Lets get to the Ring Announcer!! RING ANNOUNCER: The following match is for the DWO'S WORLD CHAMPIONSHIP!! Introducing first.... [The theme music to Battlestar Galactica begins to play.] RING ANNOUNCER: He is the challenger. Weighing in today at 235 pounds. Standing at 5 feet and 11 inches!! He is the current UEW Ultimate Champion! Here is... THE TOP DOG!!! RRRRRRICK STYLES!!!!! [The crowd boos as Styles walks down the aisle. Rick Styles wears his usual wrestling trunks and is wearing a white jacket that says, "Top Dog" the back.] Robert Jackson: RICK STYLES, makes his way down the aisle. He looks as ready as ever. The Announcer stated that he is the UEW Ultimate Champion, and that he is. He defeated "Offensive" Alex Adams to win the gold. Johnny Cassimo: I don't care what he did in some other organization, do I work for UBW?? I work for the DWA!!! Robert Jackson: It's UEW, and for goodness sakes, IT' D-W-O!!! Johnny Cassimo: The 'B' the 'E', 'O' and 'A' what's the difference?? Rick Styles has to face J'ster now, not some Offensive or Defensive guy. Robert Jackson: Well, Rick Style beat probably the best wrestler in wrestling right now, Alex Adams. Adams is the current EWA WORLD CHAMPION! He holds the biggest title in the world, and Styles has beaten him! Johnny Cassimo: I liked Rick Styles for a long time, but right now, my money is one J'ster. But it's going to be a great matchup! Robert Jackson: Also, I'd like to point out, this is going to be the first match where Styles is not accompanied to the ring by MARK R. ROSE, who just recently became the DWO's President. Johnny Cassimo: So what is he's the President? Shawn Roy WRESTLED for god sakes when he was President! RING ANNOUNCER: His opponent! ["This is the Life" by Living Colour begins to play.] RING ANNOUNCER: He is the CURRENT DEADLY WRESTLING ORGANIZATION WORLD CHAMPION!!! Weighing 250 pounds, standing at 6 feet and 6 inches. Accompanied to the ring by JOHN J. SYLVIA!! Ladies and gentleman........... J'STER!!!!! [Men wearing black robes with hoods walk down to ringside carrying candles. Once they surround the ring, J'ster walks out. He is wearing a black leather jacket, which is covering his black tights with question marks. His hair is jet black, rather than his usual blonde look.] Robert Jackson: I always thought of J'ster as an unusual person. But this is TOO WEIRD!! These men, who are walking back to the locker room area, came out with candles. Johnny Cassimo: J'ster has to be one hell of a politically correct guy! +-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+- + *World Championship match* J'ster vs. "Top Dog" Rick Styles +-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+-+- + [DING! DING! DING!] Robert Jackson: This match is underway!! Johnny Cassimo: J'ster and Rick Styles lock up. Robert Jackson: Rick Styles quickly goes for a roundhouse right, but J'ster blocks it! Johnny Cassimo: J'ster whips Rick Styles into the ropes. Robert Jackson: J'ster with a backdrop, and that takes Rick Styles down! Johnny Cassimo: J'ster sets up Rick Styles for a side suplex, Rick Styles nails him with an elbowsmash! Robert Jackson: Rick Styles places J'ster on the top turnbuckle! Johnny Cassimo: Rick Styles executes a superplex!!!! Robert Jackson: Rick Styles nails J'ster with a kneelift! Johnny Cassimo: Rick Styles nails him with a roundhouse right. Robert Jackson: J'ster scoops up Styles and slams him down. Johnny Cassimo: J'ster whips Rick Styles into the ropes. Robert Jackson: J'ster hits him with a dropkick! Johnny Cassimo: Rick Styles falls out of the ring! Robert Jackson: The Referee begins the count. 1... 2... 3... Johnny Cassimo: Rick Styles climbs back into the ring. Robert Jackson: J'ster takes Rick Styles down with a belly to back suplex! Johnny Cassimo: J'ster puts on a STANDING FIGURE FOUR LEGLOCK!! Robert Jackson: RICK STYLES TURNS IT INTO AN INSIDE CRADLE!! 1... 2... kickout! Johnny Cassimo: That was amazing! Styles rolled up J'ster into that small package, but J'ster never let go of the figure four!!! Robert Jackson: J'ster runs into the ropes. Johnny Cassimo: Rick Styles hits J'ster with a SUPERKICK! Robert Jackson: Styles goes for a kneedrop, J'ster rolls away. Johnny Cassimo: J'ster whips Styles into the ropes. Robert Jackson: J'ster takes him down with a LARIAT!!! Johnny Cassimo: J'ster covers, hooks a leg! 1... 2... kickout! Robert Jackson: J'ster whips Rick Styles into the ropes. Johnny Cassimo: J'ster NAILS Rick Styles with a JUMPING DDT!!! Robert Jackson: J'ster climbs up to the top turnbuckle. Johnny Cassimo: Rick Styles is back up. Robert Jackson: Styles walks over to the corner. He throws J'ster off the turnbuckle on to the canvas! Johnny Cassimo: Rick Styles with an elbowdrop!! Right to J'ster's midsection! Robert Jackson: Rick Styles picks up J'ster, maybe a power bomb right here. Johnny Cassimo: OH!!!! What a backbreaker! Robert Jackson: A great move right there by Styles, I guess you could say that's a variation of the SAMARTINO BACKBREAKER. Johnny Cassimo: Rick Styles whips J'ster into the turnbuckles, but J'ster reverses. Robert Jackson: Rick Styles tries to reverses it again, but J'ster takes him down with a clothesline!! Great move there on the part of J'ster. Johnny Cassimo: J'ster nails Styles with a headbutt! Robert Jackson: J'ster takes him down with an ENZUIGIRI!! Johnny Cassimo: J'ster puts on an INDIAN DEATHLOCK!!! Robert Jackson: This could be it for Rick Styles, he is yelling out in pain!! Johnny Cassimo: Rick Styles is trying to reach for the ropes, but J'ster is pulling him back!! And as he is pulling him, applying more and more preasure to the hold!! Robert Jackson: Rick Styles giving it his all..... GETS TO THE ROPES!! Johnny Cassimo: J'ster gets up and stomps on Styles. Robert Jackson: Styles grabs J'ster's tights and tosses him out of the ring!! Johnny Cassimo: J'ster hit the guardrail hard! Robert Jackson: The Referee begins to countout J'ster. 1... 2... Johnny Cassimo: This is good for Style letting him take a breather, but you can't win the title via countout! 3... 4... Robert Jackson: Rick Styles goes to outside and throws J'ster back into the ring. Johnny Cassimo: Styles climbs back into the ring and sends J'ster into the ropes. Robert Jackson: Both, J'ster and Rick Styles gets hit with clotheslines!! Johnny Cassimo: They're both laid out!! No one is moving except for the Official!!! Robert Jackson: Wait, RICK STYLES IS CRAWLING OVER TO J'STER!!!! Johnny Cassimo: Rick Styles gets his arm across J'ster!! The Ref gets down for the count!! 1... 2... 3!!! Robert Jackson: No, No, No! J'ster got his shoulder up! Rick Styles is raising his arms into the air, J'ster is in a state of confusion, and the Referee is talking to the Ring Announcer!!! Johnny Cassimo: What's going on here?? The Referee counted the three count. Styles is the new champion! Robert Jackson: Lets take a look at a replay. [The Scene shows Styles getting his arm across of J'ster. The Referee counts, 1, 2, 3. But before the 3 count, J'ster clearly put his arm up in the air, and got his shoulder up.] RING ANNOUNCER: Ladies, and gentlemen, may I have your attention. This match is NOT over. There was NO PINFALL, only a TWO AND A HALF COUNT! This match will continue!! Robert Jackson: Both men are at opposite corners, J'ster looking on, and Rick Styles agrees with the call. It's been a clean match, between two very controversial men here in DWO. Johnny Cassimo: They are two of the NICEST MEN in DWO! Robert Jackson: Rick Styles and J'ster lock up again. Johnny Cassimo: Styles with a wristlock, into a waislock on J'ster. Robert Jackson: Waistlock suplex by The Top Dog, Rick Styles! Johnny Cassimo: Rick Styles applies a sleeperhold!! Robert Jackson: J'STER COUNTERS IT WITH A JAWBREAKER!!! Johnny Cassimo: The sleeper is the hold J'ster knows best, he knows hundreds of ways to put it on, and even more ways to take it off! A sleeperhold will do no good against J'ster. Robert Jackson: Rick Styles goes for a spinning mule kick!! J'STER DUCKS!! Johnny Cassimo: J'ster takes Styles down with a legsweep! Robert Jackson: J'ster sends Rick Styles into the turnbuckles. Johnny Cassimo: J'ster charges in and hits Rick Styles with a Japanese Clothesline!! Robert Jackson: J'ster whips Rick Styles into the ropes. Johnny Cassimo: J'ster goes for a backdrop but Rick Styles counters with a SUNSET FLIP!! 1... 2... kickout! Robert Jackson: Rick Styles nails J'ster with an UPPERCUT!! Johnny Cassimo: Uh, oh. Now it's getting dirty!! Robert Jackson: The Referee is warning Styles about the clinched fist, one more, and he's going to be disqualified! Johnny Cassimo: I don't get it, one minute here you can use chairs and knives, but the next, the Referee is in your face because a punched a man!! Robert Jackson: Rick Styles sets up J'ster, and executes a vertical suplex! Johnny Cassimo: Rick Styles climbs up to the top turnbuckle. Robert Jackson: Rick Styles executes a FLYING SENTON!!!! Johnny Cassimo: Rick Styles whips J'ster into the ropes. Robert Jackson: Styles takes J'ster down with a leaping cross bodylock!!! He hooks a leg!! 1... 2... shoulder up! Johnny Cassimo: Rick Styles hits J'ster with a legdrop. Robert Jackson: Rick Styles picks up J'ster. An inverted atomic drop!! Johnny Cassimo: Rick Styles takes J'ster down with a POWER SLAM!! Robert Jackson: Styles picks up J'ster, side suplex? No, he puts J'ster on the top turnbuckle. Johnny Cassimo: Rick Styles is climbing up! Robert Jackson: Here we go... a BELLY TO BELLY SUPERPLEX BY THE TOP DOG, RICK STYLES!!!! Johnny Cassimo: Look at this!! HE BRIDGES BACK!! 1... 2... .. kickout! Johnny Cassimo: J'STER KICKED OUT!!! CAN YOU BELIEVE IT!?!?! Look at the fight in this man!! He is the TRUE LIONHEART! Not some loser that goes by the name of TOMMY VAN DAM. Robert Jackson: Rick Styles is exhausted, and he can not believe it either. Two time now, he has been less than a second away from becoming, the World Champion! Johnny Cassimo: Rick Styles gets up and picks up J'ster. Robert Jackson: A PILEDRIVER by Rick Styles! Johnny Cassimo: Rick Styles climbs up to the top turnbuckle. Robert Jackson: J'ster is down right in the center of the ring, and he is motion less. Styles is all the way on the top turnbuckle! Johnny Cassimo: Styles comes flying down with a FROG SPLASH... J'STER LIFTS UP HIS KNEES!! Robert Jackson: That's the second blows Styles' ribs have taken in this match! Johnny Cassimo: J'ster is putting on a SCORPION DEATHLOCK!! He has it on!!! Robert Jackson: Styles is in pain but he is fighting!!! Johnny Cassimo: Styles is too far away from the ropes, he has to kick out, or this match will be over!! Robert Jackson: Styles is still fighting it!! Styles has the look of determination on his face!!!! Johnny Cassimo: Rick Styles is powering his way out of the hold, J'ster is doing whatever he can to hold on!! Robert Jackson: RICK STYLES KICKS J'STER OFF OF THE SUBMISSION HOLD!! Can you believe it?? Styles broke out!!! Johnny Cassimo: But that took a LOT out of Styles, and does he have the stamina to become the World Champion?!?! Robert Jackson: J'ster whips Rick Styles into the ropes, reversed by Rick Styles!! Johnny Cassimo: Rick Styles with a HIGH ELEVATED BACK BODY DROP!!! J'ster falls out of the ring!!! Robert Jackson: The fight left in these two men, it's totally out of this world! We thought it would get ugly, and these two men had to give it all they had. But it has been mostly a technical match! And it's been inside of the ring! Johnny Cassimo: Rick Styles is going to the outside!! Can you believe this?? Robert Jackson: Rick Styles wants that title more than anyone! Johnny Cassimo: J'ster sends Rick Styles into the ringpost! Robert Jackson: J'ster catches Styles, and executes a SIDE SUPLEX RIGHT ON THE FLOOR!! Johnny Cassimo: J'ster throws Rick Styles into the guardrail!! Robert Jackson: J'STER GOES FOR A DDT, RICK STYLES COUNTERS WITH A BACKDROP! Johnny Cassimo: Styles throws J'ster back into the ring. Robert Jackson: J'ster gets up. Styles comes back into the ring, but J'ster greets him with a BACK HEEL KICK!!! Johnny Cassimo: J'ster whips Rick Styles into the ropes. Robert Jackson: J'ster catches Rick Styles and he executes a BELLY TO BELLY SUPLEX!!! Johnny Cassimo: J'ster rolls over for the cover! 1... 2... kickout! Robert Jackson: J'ster whips Rick Styles into the turnbuckles, reversal by Styles. Johnny Cassimo: J'ster hit the turnbuckles hard!! Robert Jackson: Rick Styles picks up J'ster and executes SIDE SLAM!! Johnny Cassimo: Rick Styles covers!! 1... kickout! Robert Jackson: J'ster quickly kicked out of that one! Johnny Cassimo: J'ster rolls up Rick Styles in a cradle!!! 1... Robert Jackson: J'ster's shoulders are down too!! 2... Johnny Cassimo: Who's covering who?!?! 3!!! Robert Jackson: The Referee calls for the bell! [DING! DING! DING!] Robert Jackson: Lets get the official word from the Ring Announcer! RING ANNOUNCER: The winner of this bout, and NNNNNNNNNNNNNNNNNNNNNNEW!!! DWO WORLD CHAMPION!!!!! Robert Jackson: RICK STYLES!!! RING ANNOUNCER: THE TOP DOG!!! ......RRRRRRICK STYLES!!! Johnny Cassimo: NO! NO! NO! J'ster should retain the title! Styles' shoulders were down too!!! Robert Jackson: Well, this happened nearly a decade ago, when Rick Steamboat rolled up The Honky Tonk Man, Steamboat lossed the matchup. And appears the same thing has happened here today! Johnny Cassimo: Wait, here comes another Referee from the back! Robert Jackson: They're having a discussion in the ring. The Ring Announcer has something to say!! RING ANNOUNCER: Ladies and gentlemen, BOTH WRESTLERS SHOULDERS WERE DOWN, SO THIS MATCH WILL CONTINUE!!!! Robert Jackson: Style can't believe it, this is the THIRD time in the match, where came SO CLOSE. But both wrestlers shoulders were down, and this match has been officiated very, very well. Johnny Cassimo: And this has been a HELL OF A MATCH! Robert Jackson: J'ster and Styles lock up in the middle of the ring. Johnny Cassimo: J'ster tries to slap on a cobra clutch but Rick Styles with a snap mare takedown! Robert Jackson: AND J'STER WITH A SNAP MARE OF HIS OWN!!! WOW!! What a move by J'ster! Johnny Cassimo: He was down on the mat too! Robert Jackson: J'ster whips Rick Styles into the ropes. Johnny Cassimo: J'ster takes Rick Styles down with a LARIAT! Robert Jackson: J'ster picks up Rick Styles. Johnny Cassimo: J'ster puts on a double underhook, into a PILEDRIVER!! Robert Jackson: J'ster whips Rick Styles into the ropes. Johnny Cassimo: J'ster catches Rick Styles in a SLEEPER!! THE SUPER J SLEEPERHOLD IS APPLIED!!!!!!!! Robert Jackson: J'STER HAS ON THE SUPER J SLEEPERHOLD!!! Johnny Cassimo: THIS IS IT! THIS IS IT!!! Robert Jackson: THE REFEREE IS CALLING FOR THE BELL!!! RICK STYLES HAS SUBMITTED!!! [DING! DING! DING!] Johnny Cassimo: Look at J'ster! He's exhausted and he fall down on his back!!! RING ANNOUNCER: The winner of this match, as a result of a submission... and STILL THE DEADLY WRESTLING ORGANIZATION, WORLD CHAMPION! ..........J'STER!!! Robert Jackson: J'ster has retained his World Title!! For Johnny Cassimo, this is Robert Jackson saying so long!! We'll see you next week on Wrestle Mayhem!!! [The scene shows both J'ster and Rick Styles inside of the ring and on their back. The Referee places the World Title belt over J'ster. The scene fades.]
