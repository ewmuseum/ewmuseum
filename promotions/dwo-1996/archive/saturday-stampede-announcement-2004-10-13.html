Saturday Stampede — Official Announcement

Oct 13, 2004, 12:00:00 AM
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!# THE DEADLY WRESTLING ORGANIZATION presents SATURDAY STAMPEDE #!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!# (Scene opens inside the Control Center of the Deadly Wrestling
Organization at the Deadly Enterprises Headquarters in Utica, New
York. There are pictures of Jamie Underwood on the mat with Justin
Slayne holding the DWO World Title above him, and J'ster slashing
Brody Thunder's leg with a switch blade, and Steve Doulgas upsetting
Rick Styles on the wall. Robert Jackson, wearing a suit and tie is standing in front of the pictures.)
Robert Jackson: Welcome everyone to the DWO Control Center. I am
Robert Jackson. Boy we've got a lot to talk about today, including the absolutely huge DWO WrestleKade X that took place over the weekend and a huge announcement concerning the future of the Deadly
Wrestling Organization. But let me tell you, in my twenty years of broadcasting this great sport, I have never seen a card packed with such great action and surprises as WrestleKade X was on Sunday night. What about that huge main event? J'ster defeating Brody
Thunder because of blood loss making Thunder pass out and being unable to continue. The big man did not give up. He did not submit. The blood loss from a devastating slice of a switch blade caused him to just black out to J'ster's sharpshooter. The match was back and forth and back and forth. It lasted an incredible 45 minutes. Both wrestlers are recuperating from this match at their homes in Arizona and Mississipi respectively. And what about our new
DWO Champion, "The Savior" Jamie Underwood? He outlasted 19 other wrestlers to get the belt, only to have it stolen by Justin Slayne and his new manager "The Top Dog" Rick Styles. It was an amazing night, ladies and gentlemen, and if you haven't already seen it, you better find a tape. It certainly was the card of the year.
(Robert Jackson pauses.) Robert Jackson: Which brings me to a major announcement. After the huge pay per view on Sunday night, DWO President Mike Bilodeau received a phone call from the FX Cable Network. Bilodeau met with the network executives last night in New York City, and FX has offered the DWO a chance to return to cable television on Saturday nights once a month. Bilodeau has jumped at the chance and in
November, the world will witness the return of the DWO to cable television in what's being called Saturday Stampede. It will be a two hour program starting at 9 p.m. and will feature five to six matches a card and will feature the top stars of the Deadly Wrestling
Organization. And the first Saturday Stampede will be held LIVE on
Saturday, November 13th, and will be held LIVE at the PEPSI ARENA in
Albany, New York. Here to run down the card for the inaugaral
Saturday Stampede is your DWO President Mike Bilodeau. (Mike Bilodeau wearing a suit and tie walks into the Control Center and shakes Robert's hand.)
Mike Bilodeau: Thank you Robert. First off, I'd like to thank the millions of fans around the world and the great superstars of the DWO for making WrestleKade X a huge success. And I'd also like to thank the FX Network for giving the DWO a chance to be reborn. Coming off the huge WrestleKade X card, I'm here to announce that the first DWO
Saturday Stampede will be a six-match event. And I'm here today to rundown the ENTIRE card.
#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!# FOR THE DWO WOMEN'S TITLE #!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!# New Women's Champion PHOENIX vs. "POISON BLISS" MYRA BENEDICT w/ Samantha Bevins #!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!# Mike Bilodeau: Phoenix was certainly impressive in her victory at
WrestleKade X. We are still seeking answers to the union between Sam
Bevins and Myra Benedict and why Sam turned on Iris Galiver at
WrestleKade X. Maybe in the coming weeks we'll get those answers.
But Myra Benedict certainly earned her shot at gold here in the DWO with her impressive performance in Deadly Draw 3 over the weekend.
The tag team titles will also be on the line in a very unique situation. #!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!# FOR THE DWO TAG TEAM TITLES #!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!# One half of the DWO Tag Team Champions JOHNNY SLEDGE & A PARTNER OF HIS CHOOSING vs. One half of the DWO Tag Team Champions "RODEO" NEVADA HARRIS & A PARTNER OF HIS CHOOSING #!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!# Mike Bilodeau: Certainly our tag team champions are no longer on friendly terms. They worked together nicely to win the DWO World Tag
Team Titles in that huge ladder match with the Riverhawks, but then
Harris tried to kill Sledge in Deadly Draw 3 after dropping him off the jumbo tron. They certainly are no longer wishing to be a tag team, and therefore they will each pick partners, and the winners will be the new tag team champions. The DWO National Title will also be on the line.
#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!# FOR THE DWO NATIONAL TITLE #!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!# New DWO National Champion THE MERCENARY vs. LARRY GIONET #!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!# Mike Bilodeau: These two men got very physical in the Deadly Draw 3, and certainly Larry Gionet would like a measure of revenge over his elimination in the Deadly Draw. Gionet will get his shot at the
National Title at Saturday Stampede. There will also be a number one contender's match at the Saturday Stampede.
#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!# WINNER WILL BE NUMBER ONE CONTENDER TO DWO WORLD TITLE #!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!# STEVE DOUGLAS vs. K.C. HAGEN #!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!# Mike Bilodeau: K.C. Hagen and Steve Douglas returned to wrestling at
WrestleKade X in huge ways. Douglas came back and upset Rick Styles in his final match. Hagen returned to referee the main event, only to be taken away by Joshua Barnes. These two men will get the shot to get some gold here in the DWO. We will also be bringing back another DWO Title at Saturday Stampede. That will be the DWO Eastern
Title.
#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!# FOR THE DWO EASTERN TITLE #!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!# "SIMPLY GOLDEN" JEFF KEENAN vs. TODD VALENTINE #!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!# Mike Bilodeau: Jeff Keenan was a little underprepared for his National Title contest with Mercenary, but he has vowed to return to the DWO and get his career back on track. And he could do it for the
Eastern Title against a man trying to make a name for himself here in wrestling, Todd Valentine. And now, our huge main event. FIVE MEN will get in the ring to compete for TWO WORLD TITLES.
#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!# FOR THE DWO WORLD TITLE AND THE WLW WORLD TITLE LADDERS TO HEAVEN MATCH #!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!# DWO WORLD CHAMPION "THE SAVIOR" JAMIE UNDERWOOD WLW WORLD CHAMPION KYLE KAMIKAZE FORMER DWO & WLW CHAMPION J'STER "THE LONE WOLF" BRODY THUNDER "REDEMPTION" JUSTIN SLAYNE w/ Rick Styles #!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!#!# Mike Bilodeau: This contest is going to be interesting. Five of the best in the business going after the two biggest title belts in the world today, the DWO Title and the WLW Title. This is how the match is set up. There will be a 20 foot scaffold above the ring. Hanging below the scaffold will be the WLW World Title. Hanging in the rafters above the scaffold will be the DWO World Title. The first man to use a ladder in the ring and grab the WLW World Title belt will be the new WLW Champion. After the WLW Champion is crowned, all five men will then be able to climb to the scaffold and attempt to get the DWO World Title. The first man to use a ladder on the scaffold to get the DWO World Title is the new DWO Champion. Oh, and while the five of them are up on the scaffold, the ring is going to be filled with about 15 table stacked up. It's going to be huge,
Robert.
Robert Jackson: It sure is, Mike. We're looking forward to November 13 live in Albany on the FX network. If you haven't already filled out an application please do so and return them to

Most apps will insist that your strengths and weaknesses even out (if you have three strengths you must have three weaknesses). I say bollocks. These are supposed to be realistic characters, and real people aren't products of simple mathmatical equations. Try your best to keep them equal, yes, but if you have one more of one than the other, I'm not going to get angry and tear it up. I've had my strengths and weaknesses be one off like that before, and spent way too much time trying to cut one out or split one up or make a new one to reach this magic equilibrium. The idea is to create balanced characters (and prevent people from sending in Invincible Bill Goldberg type apps, which are boring and WILL be sent into the void where junk emails go to die).
