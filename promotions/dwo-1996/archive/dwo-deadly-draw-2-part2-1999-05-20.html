---------------------------------------------------------------------------- (We fade into the broadcast booth where Adrian Anderson, Johnny Cassimo, and Robert Jackson are sitting. The fans are surrounding them cheering and clapping.)
Adrian Anderson: This card, in the few short moments it's been airing has been the most controversial night in the Deadly Wrestling Organization in quite some time. Johnny Cassimo, you just went backstage to see what
Martinez planned to do with that tape. What did the man say? Johnny Cassimo: In a nutshell, Alex Martinez said he'd get it to the proper owner, for the proper price. He said if he didn't get the price he wanted, he'd literally tell Bilodeau to fuck himself, and he'd show it to the whole world watching tonight. That videotape is the cause of a lot of tension in the back. The locker room is unstable country.
Robert Jackson: The first match of the evening was hectic. -----DWO DEADLY DRAW 2------ LETHAL MILLENIUM MATCH Each team has a flag, one team a white flag, the other a blue flag. They will keep them in their corner through the match. The first team to get the opposing flag and bring it back to the locker room area, where the referee is waiting will win.
However, during the match, there are no rules, or a referee. It may not be easy to get the flag. And to add some extra stipulations, tables and chairs and assorted hardcore weapons will be surrounding the ringside area. All tables must be broken, all chairs must be bent, and every weapon must be used before a team can go for the other team's flag.
And that's not all. Once all the weapons are used, they must pin one of the opposing team members, before they can go for the flag. This is elimination style.
-----DWO DEADLY DRAW 2------ Sick Rick and "Black Knight" Alex Martinez vs. Tommy Love & Magnus Colby -----DWO DEADLY DRAW 2------- Adrian Anderson: Sick Rick was called down to the ring first, but in typical Sick Rick fashion, he made us wait. We weren't sure how Sick Rick would look after 13 months of hard time in Ryker's Island Prison on an island outside of New York City. He looked in peak physical condition.
Johnny Cassimo caught up with him before the match. Can we see that footage please? (We see Johnny Cassimo standing in the ring as Sick Rick makes his way to the ring for his match. Cassimo wants an interview. Sick Rick agrees as he prepares for his match.)
Johnny Cassimo: His whole life, he's been beaten down, but refused to stay there. When he first made his mark on professional wrestling, critics said that he compensated for a lack of talent with bloodlust, that he brought the gangster mentality to wrestling, that he was nothing more than a criminal in the ring, that he would never amount to anything. But as he learned how to incorporate technical wrestling into his street fighting style, he quickly became both one of the better tactical brawlers in wrestling, and a champion many times over. He has wrestled in a few federations, but tonight, he is back in the federation where he first carved his devil-may-care neck-breaking style, and the federation he is viritually synonymous with...Ladies and Gentlemen...
("Back in Black" by AC/DC plays, and the crowd, awaiting anxiously, sends vibes of anticipation that even the home viewers can feel.)
SICK RICK! (...as AC/DC's volume begins to pick up, we see Sick Rick walking out, in a t-shirt that says "Top Dog" Rick Styles on the front and "Mess with The
Best..." on the back. The crowd is mostly so glad to see him back that they pop huge, although there is still a vocal booing section.
Sick Rick steps in the ring, and takes the microphone.) Sick Rick: Well, well, well... (Looks at the startled Cassimo) Don't worry, Johnny, you'll get it back in a second. Then you can ask whatever you want. But first, I gotta say, "What's up, DWO?"
(big pop) I see most of you seem to have had a change of opinion about me in the last year. I guess you finally realized I'm not a traitor, not a gangster, not a cheater, not a devil?
Well you're wrong!!!! I'm still all of those things! And if you think that
I've mellowed with age, think again. It's been too too long since there was some bloody broken body at Sick Rick's feet in a DWO ring, and I intend to revert to my psychopathic motifs just as soon as that bell rings!!!
(tosses the microphone back to Cassimo) JC: Rick...how can you be so confident? There are so many variables in tonight's match. Your ring rust, your uncertainty of a partner, the rules of
the match, the shaky DWO political situation...what is your mindset? SR: Do you think I care? I've calculated for all those variables, and the plan is still the same--break bones and make things bleed. You're right--there are a lot of uncertainties. Maybe my partner won't show up.
Maybe whoever's running the DWO right now will change the rules right before the match starts. Maybe Mother Madeline will make her way down...(sigh). But there is one thing certain, and that's that this jungle cat is back and will not stop until he has proven that some things will never change!
JC: What is your goal for tonight? SR: There ain't but one. Win. JC: What do you think of the current DWO political situation? SR: Politics? I know all about DWO politics--but let me tell you this, the cream always rises. If there's a pile of bodies, it's going to be at my feet.
I'd forgotten how good it felt to be king. JC: Aren't you a little cocky tonight, Rick? SR: The odds are stacked against me, Johnny, but is that anything new? You ask any man I've stepped in a DWO ring with--Dave Stewart, The Prophet, Kyle
Kamikaze, Rick Styles, J'ster, Apollo, and the list goes on; ask them whether
or not I've earned the right to walk into that ring confident for once. My style used to be based on incredible self-doubt, the thought that I had something to
prove.to a world that's been trying to beat me down since day one. But I proved that, and now I'm just here because it's my nature, because
I like torture. Bring the babyfaces and heels out here, put 'em in a cardboard box and blow 'em all to pieces with an explosive device. Screw good
guys, and bad guys. I'm not a good guy or a bad guy. I'm Sick Rick. JC: What does that mean? SR: It's either evil or it's chaotic; most likely it's both. JC: I still don't understand. SR: (grinning) Well, then, I guess you'll just see the proof in ths ring in a little bit, right? Somebody's getting jaw-shocked on one of these ropes, and
I guarantee it's not going to be me. I'm not in this for a world title or for
glory or fame...I'm in this because I love it, and that's what makes it really dangerous. (takes the microphone again) Are you DWO fans ready to get DEADLY??!?! (big pop) Johnny Cassimo: That was the scariest interview I ever conducted. But the match was much more scary. We were unsure if Alex Martinez would show up for this match. But he actually did. It was rather strange, because he came through the crowd and almost gave Sick Rick a DDT. It didn't happen though. The referee calmed things down a little bit. Alex Martinez brought that videotape to ringside, and we all wanted to run down there and grab it, but we were afraid Martinez would kick our asses.
Robert Jackson: Magnus Colby returned to action. The house here in the jam packed Alamo Dome went crazy for the former DWO Eastern Champion. It was a huge ovation, and I think Magnus Colby may get the bug back and become a full time wrestler again. We'll have to see what his future is.
Tommy Love, his partner, came down to a lesser ovation, but the crowd was so jacked for the opening bout on this card. Colby and Love didn't jaw jack each other, they seemed to have a strategy going in to the match.
The tables and chairs were arranged around the ring. There were four chairs, and five tables. There were also 3 singapore canes, and 3 baseball bats in the ring. When the match started, Sick Rick and Martinez had bats, and Colby and Love took the singapore canes.
Johnny Cassimo: Sick Rick took the early advantage by breaking a baseball bat over the head of Tommy Love, and by weakening him, he was able to toss
Tommy Love over the top rope into a table on the outside. Rick and
Martinez were in the lead for quite some time, busting Magnus Colby through two of the tables. But then Love and Colby got into a double team position on Alex Martinez, after knocking Sick Rick out cold with a steel chair.
Colby placed Martinez on the table, and POWERBOMED his partner through the table and Alex Martinez. It was a huge move that the fans almost DIED for!
Adrian Anderson: Colby then picked up the hurt and bloody Sick Rick and
SUPERPLEXED him from the top turnbuckle through a table on the outside.
There was only one table left at that point, the last thing to be used before teams could go for the flags. Alex Martinez had Tommy Love set for a DDT through the table. But Sick Rick was on the top turnbuckle, and
Magnus Colby was on the opposite turnbuckle. Sick Rick and Martinez were attempting a SPIKE DDT through the table. Sick Rick leaped off, and Magnus
Colby leaped to save his partner by clotheslining Alex Martinez, but ALL
FOUR MEN WENT THROUGH THE TABLE! Robert Jackson: Tommy Love was the first man to his feet. He went to Sick
Rick's and Alex Martinez'z corner and grabbed their white flag. He and
Magnus Colby ran to the back, but Rick and Martinez were right behind with their blue flag. There was a brief fight in the locker room, where another table was used to powerbomb Tommy Love through, thanks to Alex Martinez.
Sick Rick gave Magnus Colby a nice BULLDOG onto the concrete floor. Sick
Rick was going to get the flag through to the referee in the locker room, but Alex Martinez stopped him. Martinez wanted to win the match.
Johnny Cassimo: There was a little scuffle, and Magnus Colby capitalized. He hit Rick with a low blow and back body dropped Martinez. He got into the locker room where the referee was waiting, but APOLLO and MIKE BILODEAU were waiting with a STEEL CHAIR! Apollo hit Magnus with the chair allowing
Martinez to take the other team's flag and win the match for himself and
Sick Rick.
Robert Jackson: We're still confused as to why Mike Bilodeau would help
Alex Martinez would win, but it's the DWO!
Johnny Cassimo: After the match, another strange encounter went down. Three masked men crashed the Martinez celebration. Sick Rick left the scene and went to his locker room to prepare for the Deadly Rumble. The three masked men jumped Martinez. One had a sign that said New Alliance.
One of the other men had a "R.I.P." sign. It was clearly a harbringer of doom toward the New Alliance of the UEW. Alex Martinez ripped the signs, but revealed the men were dummies. They were attached to the third man in the middle.
Adrian Anderson: The third man pulled out a switchblade, remininscent of the old classic J'ster/Sick Rick knife fights. And another masked man ran from behind as Martinez was distracted and hit him with a steel chair, knocking him out cold. It was J'STER! J'ster made his stunning appearance with force, apparently waking Alex Martinez up, and telling him he's here too, and he's not going down at the hands of the New Alliance.
Robert Jackson: What a wild night already. Adrian Anderson: It was an extremely wild match, but not as wild as the next match! ----DWO DEADLY DRAW 2----- LETHAL LADDER MATCH FOR $100,000 The first team to grab the money off the ladder is richer and going to the
Deadly Rumble! ----DWO DEADLY DRAW 2----- 2 dYnAmiC Wesley Steel & Puppet Master Nicolas Christian vs. "Hot Stuff" Scott Richards & The Trixxster ----DWO DEADLY DRAW 2----- Robert Jackson: All four men hit the ring in grand style, all making their impressive debuts into the Deadly Wrestling Organization, the first BIG
TIME federation for most of these men. Wesley Steel seemed to be injured, maybe from his big match last night in a competing organization. Christian was a big prospect we gained from the UEW. Trixxxster has turned a few heads in the independent federations. Scott Richards, we haven't heard much from him, and we just didn't know what to expect. It was a wild brawl for the money!
Adrian Anderson: Richards and Trixxxster were very shaky as a team, not trusting each other. Trixxxster even gave his own partner a DDT during the closing minutes of the match. Trixxxster got to the money and won the match for his partner. Richards got up and demanded his cut of the money, but Trixxxster suplexed him into the ladder and took off with all of the money! Richards chased him to the back, but couldn't find him!
Robert Jackson: So far, Alex Martinez, Sick Rick, Trixxxster, and Scott
Richards have all advanced to the 20 Man Deadly Rumble.
Johnny Cassimo: After these two matches, Adrian Anderson caught up with
Sandy Fox in the women's locker room. It was a wild interview to say the least. Let's see that footage please!
-----------DWO INTERVIEW----------------- ADRIAN ANDERSON WITH SANDY FOX ----------------------------------------- Adrian Anderson: I'm standing by with the "first lady" of professional wrestling, as she's been called by some promoters. Sandy Fox, I'm here to talk to you about your recent marriage, the death of your first husband, and where Sandy Fox goes from here, and her big upcoming match tonight with
a former rival. Sandy, I have to ask you, how did you feel when you heard Glenn Osborne, your ex-husband had passed? (Sandy Fox takes the microphone. She's wearing a long red robe, covering her wrestling gear. She has black lipstick on, and she's died her hair red. She kisses Adrian's cheek and shakes his hand.)
Sandy Fox: Adrian, that night, I was watching you and Johnny on the Control Center, and I had no idea what was going on. And you said there were gunshots in DWO Headquarters, and I knew, I just knew that Glenn was behind it. He was very unstable over the past six months. The last time I saw him alive, I was telling him I was getting married to Matt Booth, his former boyfriend. That kind of hurt him a little. He felt I betrayed him, and he felt that Matt Booth betrayed him. That wasn't the case, me and
Matt fell deeply in love over the past year. We both left the wrestling business and started a catering business. During that year when we were working so close with one another, we fell in love. We weren't betraying
Glenn, it was just we loved each other so much, and I longed for Glenn to say he was happy for me. He didn't say it when I told him, he hung up on me. We didn't talk after that.
Adrian Anderson: Again, I'm sorry for your loss. When did you and Matt
Booth tie the knot?
Sandy Fox: One month ago tonight. Everyone asks me, "isn't he gay?" Well, yeah, he was. But once you've had this for one night, there's no turning back. Matt and I had been friends for so long before though, and love brought us together, not sex. We're married because we love each other. He may be gay, but he loves me, and that's what marriage and friendship is all about. It's funny, because Joan, Glenn's mom, introduced us. This was so long ago, before me and Glenn made it to the DWO. A lot of people were thinking she set us up. She didn't set us up, she just introduced us when Glenn and he were dating. It's a soap opera like happening, I know, but Joan is not an evil person. She just cares very deeply for her job.
Adrian Anderson: You are supporting Joan Osborne in her quest to get the
DWO closed? Sandy Fox: Oh, no Adrian. You have to understand. Joan has been like a mother to me. While I'm not supporting her motives, I'm not going to stand in her way either. Each of us has a path in life, and she's taking hers.
Who am I to tell her to stop? Adrian Anderson: Are Robski and Brody Thunder really in this building tonight? Sandy Fox: If Joan says so, you can take it to the bank. Adrian Anderson: Thank you Sandy, and once again sorry for your loss. Good luck tonight. Sandy Fox: Thank you Adrian. Robert Jackson: It was an interview we could all remember. Sandy actually started to cry for her fallen ex-husband. It's been an emotional roller coaster for that young woman, and her next match would be no getting off point!
------DWO DEADLY DRAW 2-------- SINGLES WOMENS EVENT Winner goes to Deadly Rumble -------DWO DEADLY DRAW 2--------- "The Sunny Killer" Sandy Fox vs. Anita J --------DWO DEADLY DRAW 2---------- Johnny Cassimo: The women were impressive, but too impressive. Sandy Fox managed to injure Anita J with a chair shot. Anita J lay motionless on the entrance ramp. The referee in this match, Mick Gus, tried to stop Sandy from doing any more harm, when Sandy picked up Gus and tossed him into the third row. Sandy got into the ring and claimed to be the best women in the business. She DEMANDED that Kierran Erotikka run to the ring to replace
Anita J in her match, but she got one better, and one certainly BIGGER!
Adrian Anderson: ELLEN DEGENERATE, the former lesbian girlfriend of Sandy
Fox, came from behind jumping over the guard rail. Keep in mind folks,
Ellen Degenerate was a former female bodybuilder, and the former BODYGUARD for Dark Vanity and his "Dysfunction" stable. She's the BIGGEST female bodybuilder in the world, coming to us from circuits all across Europe.
She jumped Sandy fom behind with a dropkick. She even threw her ex-lover through a ringside table.
Johnny Cassimo: The big shock of the night came when SANDY's shirt was
RIPPED OFF by DEGENERATE! Sandy's breasts were exposed to the fans in attendance at the Alamo Dome, and to the world on PAY PER VIEW TELEVISION!
This was what Joan Osborne was talking about earlier tonight! Ellen tied
Sandy in the ropes, and went under the ring and took out a doctor's surgical bag. Inside she had blades and forceps that could REMOVE SANDY'S
BREAST IMPLANTS! Robert Jackson: She sliced one of Sandy's breasts, but Sandy managed to kick Degenerate between the legs. Apparently, as we all found out, ELLEN
WAS A MAN all along. She was so hurt that Sandy was able to fly off the top, with her breasts exposed, with a FLYING HURRICANRANA and that was enough to beat her visious ex-lover! SANDY WON and ADVANCED to the Deadly
Rumble!
Adrian Anderson: After the match, Robert Jackson caught up with Mike Bilodeau, President of the DWO, to see how he felt about Alex Martinez hanging that tape over his head!
--------DWO INTERVIEW-------- ROBERT JACKSON with MIKE BILODEAU ------------------------------ Robert Jackson: President Bilodeau, Alex Martinez, one of your sworn enemies has that videotape from the security camera in your office. He's demanded a HEFTY SUM of money, he may auction it off to Joan Osborne if you don't hand him the dough. He said if no one gets him $20 million by the end of the night, he'll play the tape!
Mike Bilodeau: Robert, I want you to give Alex Martinez a message for me. He has this tape, the tape that everyone wants to see. The tape that shows what really happened behind closed doors the day of Glenn Osborne's death.
I want you to go tell that son-of-a-bitch that me and Apollo are looking for him, and that tape will be DESTROYED!
Robert Jackson: Mike, what's on that tape? Mike Bilodeau: This interview is over. ---------------------- Adrian Anderson: Still a mystery, but we will get to the bottom of what's on that tape before the night is out. I think Alex Martinez will see to that PERSONALLY!
