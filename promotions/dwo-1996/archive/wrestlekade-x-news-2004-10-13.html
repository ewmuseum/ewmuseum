News from WrestleKade X

Oct 13, 2004, 12:00:00 AM
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
WrestleKade X was truly a historic card from top to bottom. The card is posted, so you can read at your convenience. But here are some news tidbits coming out of the card...
The Mercenary defeated Jeff Keenan to become the DWO National
Champion. Johnny Sledge and "Rodeo" Nevada Harris, teaming for the first time, won a ladder match for the DWO Tag Team Titles over The Riverhawks
Tiffany returned as Phoenix for the first time in the U.S., and defeated Sam Bevins' hand picked opponent Iris Galiver, after Sam turned on Galiver with the help of her new protege "Poison Bliss" Myra
Benedict
Justin Slayne attacked Rick Styles before his match with Nick Demola DWO officials pushed back the Styles/Demola match so Styles could recuperate. Kyle Kamikaze defeated four other men to become the WLW Champion. Czon Yozon returned in the Five Pillars match to be the special guest referee. Also WILL GEDDINGS returned for a measure of revenge.
NICK DEMOLA DID NOT SHOW FOR HIS SPECIAL CHALLENGE MATCH AGAINST RICK
STYLES! But Steve Doulgas stepped up to face Styles in Styles' last career match. Douglas upset the Top Dog.
"The Savior" Jamie Underwood won the DWO World Title in the Deadly
Draw 3, but later the belt was stolen from him by Justin Slayne, and his new manager, Rick Styles. Myra Benedict also eliminated 6 MEN by herself in the Deadly Draw 3.
J'ster defeated Brody Thunder in a two out of three falls match. The match was back and forth until Brody passed out from blood loss while
J'ster applied a sharpshooter to his injured leg. J'ster stabbed
Thunder in the leg early in the contest
