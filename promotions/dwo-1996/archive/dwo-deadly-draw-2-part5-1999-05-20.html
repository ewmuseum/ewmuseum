----DWO DEADLY DRAW 2------ "The Southern Gentleman" Tommy Lane & Crazy Horse vs. Mad Bronco and Mr. Young ----DWO DEADLY DRAW 2------- Robert Jackson: Well, we went from DWO President Mike Bilodeau winning his match, to HFW President Cody Young making his pro-wrestling debut in the
Deadly Wrestling Organization. Cody Young has been an outspoken pillar of the wrestling community for quite some time. Just last year, his federation, and himself, were voted the WORST FEDERATION in the WORLD, and he was the WORST PROMOTER in the world. He bounced back, changed the name of his federation, and booked some strange matches, like the winner becomes the husband of the alien that fell from Mars. And he's still the WORST
PROMOTER EVER! Johnny Cassimo: He tried to persuade the fans that he wasn't so bad before the match started!
(We see Cody Young, wearing jeans and a t-shirt in the ring. He has the microphone. The fans are laughing and booing him.)
Cody Young: I have one thing to ask before this match gets started....WHO'S BETTER THAN CODY YOUNG???
(The fans scream "EVERYBODY") Cody Young: NO DAMMIT!!! I'm not HFW ANYMORE DAMMIT!!! NO!!! I DO NOT SUCK!!! (The fans scream "Yes You Do.") Robert Jackson: Well, Cody Young and Tommy Lane found themselves the victims of a Suicidal Stallions assault. Crazy Horse grabbed Cody Young and picked him up over his head. At the same time, Mad Bronco grabbed
Tommy Lane into a bodyslam, and in TRUE STALLION FASHION, THEY THREW THEIR
PARTNERS/OPPONENTS OFF THE SCAFFOLD, THROUGH THE GLASS, INTO THE RING, OUT
OF THE MATCH! Cody Young and Tommy Lane crashed through seven layers of glass before hitting the hard mat of the DWO Ring.
Johnny Cassimo: But then the Stallions had to fight, or the match wouldn't end. As Lane and Young were being stretchered out of the arena, MAD BRONCO managed to dropkick his partner, Crazy Horse, off of the scaffold into the ring. BRONCO and YOUNG would advance!
Robert Jackson: So much great talent has made the Deadly Rumble so far, let's recap. Alex Martinez, Sick Rick, Sandy Fox, Mike Bilodeau, Mad
Bronco, Mr. Young, Gorilla, Mr. Fantastic, Jesse Muldoon, Candyman, Scott
Richards, and Trixxxster. None more shocking, than DWO President Mike
Bilodeau, who's standing by with Adrian Anderson. Adrian?
(The scene switches to the locker room area with a bloody President Bilodeau. Adrian Anderson stands next to him and Apollo for an interview.) Adrian Anderson: Mike you've done it, you're successful in your open challenge match against a woman. Now you're going to the Deadly Rumble.
Mike Bilodeau: That's right I'm going to the Deadly Rumble. This is my company, and I'll do whatever I damn well please. And everyone in that
Deadly Rumble, better sure as hell not touch me, or they'll find themselves in the UNEMPLOYED LINE! Apollo and me are TAKING OVER! Oh wait, we're already in charge. BY ANY MEANS NECESSARY! No, we don't need means, we
ARE the MEANS! NO CHANCE IN HELL! No, we'll give them a slight chance of defeating us in the Deadly Rumble. WE'VE GOT TWO WORDS FOR YA!!! No, no no. Oh, I know. HELLLO LADIES!!!
Adrian Anderson: Are you okay? Mike Bilodeau: I'm feeling great Adrian. I'm going to the Deadly Rumble. And I'm going to get my hands on Alex Martinez and Nick Leshenn. Apollo and myself are taking the whole show. Why? Because I own the show!
Adrian Anderson: That reminds me. Joan Osborne has vowed to shut the show down. She's offered millions.
Mike Bilodeau: She can offer whatever she desires, Adrian. The truth is, what's on that tape won't be enough to shut me down. She has nothing on me, Adrian. So you think that tape is so controversial. I DARE HER to show that tape on the big screen. I DARE HER To try to test me. I DARE
HER! She's out there screaming NO MORE!! NO MORE!! Tonight, I'll prove to the world that we need NO MORE BITCHES! Now, get out of my way.
Adrian Anderson: Back to you Robert! (We fade back to Robert Jackson and Johnny Cassimo in the broadcast booth.) Robert Jackson: Thank you Adrian. Our next match is about to get underway! -----DWO DEADLY DRAW 2------ WINNER MUST WEAR DRESS IN DEADLY RUMBLE And also, the match will take place in deserts of Texas. No refs, no rules, anything goes, bring your weapons, and whatever team makes it to the highway wins, (or loses depending on how you look at it.)
-----DWO DEADLY DRAW 2-------- The Mercenary & "The Loose Caner" Dark Vanity vs. Johnny Bodd & The Widowmaker -----DWO DEADLY DRAW 2--------- Adrian Anderson: Well, this next contest, we didn't know what to expect, since of the men in this match is...well...dead. We thought Mercenary would bring another partner. There's nothing anyone could do about another partner, this match is no disqualification so Merc could do anything he damn well pleased. Our cameras went out to the desert, where no one was around besides MERC, the Widowmaker and Johnny Bodd. Widowmaker, shares the name of a former stable between Merc and Magnus Colby, who we saw earlier tonight.
Robert Jackson: Merc kicked things off in this match with a smoke grenade that blinded his two opponents. It started off as a weird handicap match, with MERC using everything on his opponents, including cactus plants, and then it got interesting.
Adrian Anderson: WIDOWMAKER and JOHNNY BODD tied Merc's hands and legs. They made Merc spread-eagle. But the ropes were tied to the back end of two trucks. I saw this once in FACES OF DEATH PART THREE. They were going to stretch MERC and possibly dislocate his limbs from his body. BUT the unthinkable happened. A SPEEDING BLACK CORVETTE blew a horn and turned it's headlights on and CHARGED FAST INTO THE DESERT CRASHING INTO BODD'S
TRUCK. Bodd got out alright and he and WIDOWMAKER DRAGGED THE DRIVER OUT!
IT WAS MAGNUS COLBY!!! Johnny Cassimo: Magnus Colby would replace the fallen Dark Vanity in this match. He and MERC used a baseball bat from Colby's trunk and defeated BODD and WIDOWMAKER as they escaped the desert in WIDOWMAKER'S TRUCK! It was a wild WIDOWMAKER'S REUNION, but MAGNUS COLBY and MERD ADVANCED TO THE
DEADLY RUMBLE! It's weird because Colby was eliminated from the match earlier tonight, being defeated by Sick Rick and Alex Martinez! But COLBY
DOESN'T REALIZE...he and MERC MUST WEAR DRESSES IN THE DEADLY RUMBLE!
Adrian Anderson: He got his second chance. Well, the next match, someone else got a second chance at an old feud!
-----DWO DEADLY DRAW 2----- THE ULTIMATE BARB WIRE MATCH Ring ropes wrapped in barb wire. Bring whatever weapons you want. First team to wrap both opponents in barb wire wins.
-----DWO DEADLY DRAW 2----- Apollo & "The Awesome Lion" Moo Kame vs. The Mahmood Brothers -----DWO DEADLY DRAW 2------ Robert Jackson: Moo Kame and Apollo came to the ring with the recovering
Mike Bilodeau. Moo Kame revealed his intentions to join Apollo and
Bilodeau in their quest to take the DWO back to what it was before Joan
Osborne, Nick Leshenn and Alex Martinez came to town. Bilodeau took a seat the commentators booth. He didn't say much about what his plans were for the Deadly Rumble though. The Mahmood Brothers returned to great DWO form and the fans loved them! They looked in great condition, and were making the fans in San Antonio mark out!
Adrian Anderson: Unfortunately, this match was just a set-up. Mike Bilodeau didn't commentate. As soon as the bell rang, the three men attacked the Mahmoods and tied them up in the Barbed Wire. The match was ended quickly, due to the interference of Mike Bilodeau. When the Mahmoods were tied up in the ropes, and bloody from the wire, they attacked them more and more with steel chairs.
Robert Jackson: It took ALEX MARTINEZ and NICK LESHENN to come down to the ring to clear off the jackyls. Martinez and Leshenn didn't come out together, don't get us wrong. They took out Apollo and Moo Kame with their patented finishers, the Leshenn Driver, and the Firebomb Chokeslam. After they did this, they just stared at each other, until the officials in the back separated them. It was a wild encounter, and the Deadly Rumble stacks up to be a tremendous event, with wild, extreme circumstances. The DWO
POLITICAL FEUD is enraging!
Adrian Anderson: And this next battle was extremely RAGING! -----DWO DEADLY DRAW 2------ DOUBLE LETHAL ROUND ROBIN MATCH: The assigned teams will start off first. This is an elimination match, both members of the other team must be pinned.
When five minutes pass, the man in the ring for one team, will switch teams and be the partner of the man that is on the outside of the opposite team. And vice versa for the other legal man in the ring. This will continue every five minutes.
When the first man is pinned, the man that is left without a partner will team with the illegal man of the opposing team in a handicap match against the man that pinned his partner.
If the handicapped man is pinned, the two men will be the winners and go onto the Deadly Rumble main event later that night.
If the handicapped man should pin one of the men on the opposing team, he and the other man will go onto the Deadly Rumble later that night.
-----DWO DEADLY DRAW 2------ MYSTERY PARTNER & Al Robertson vs. Nick Leshenn & Alexander Munroe -----DWO DEADLY DRAW 2------- Robert Jackson: Nick Leshenn has been speculating for weeks who this mystery partner would be. It should have been Rick Styles, but he was fired. It could have been Alex Martinez, but he backed out. So now who could have Mike Bilodeau gotten to do his dirty work against Nick Leshenn.
Adrian Anderson: This match is a FIRST for the DWO and the first for ANY
Wrestling Organization ever! It's a Round Robin match, were partners switch, opponents switch, and you just may be put in a Handicap Situation.
Johnny Cassimo: Before the match started, Nick Lesehnn had a few words! ("The King" by Pearl Jam starts up, and the DWO crowd jumps to their feet. All eyes swing to the entranceway, waiting to see their favourite superstar. Finally, the curtains part, and Nick "The Nighthawk" Leshenn steps out, being met with wild cheers. Wearing a black spandex jumpsuit witn a massive silver hawk covering the entire front of his outfit, black elbow and knee pads, and his black "COMIN’ FOR ‘YA!" tee shirt, Nick flashes his trademark devilish grin as he looks over the crowd.)
RA: Finally…Hailing from Vancouver, BC, and weighing in at 305 lbs…He is considered one of the DWO’s biggest stars…He is the master of the Leshenn
Driver…Here is…
…N I C K " T H E N I G H T H A W K " L E S H E N N ! ! ! !… (Grinning, Nick slaps hands with the fans as he makes his way down to ringside. Upon reaching the squared circle, Nick hops up onto the apron with ease, leaping over the turnbuckle and landing sitting. Nick grins to the crowd, tearing off his tee shirt and tossing it to an ecsatic fan, before stepping off and grabbing the mic from the ring announcer.)
Nick: YEEEEEAAAAAHHHHH!!!!! (The crowd roars their approval. Nick continues once the cheers quiet down.) Nick: DWO, it’s great to be back! (The pop intensifies, the crowd absolutely adoring Nick.) Nick: So how ‘bout a little poll? Who here wants to see everyone’s favourite Nighthawk get his clock cleaned by Bilodeau and his special
"Mystery Partner?" (Loud boos replace the cheers from just a moment ago. Nick strokes his chin and grins approvingly.)
Nick: I hope so! Who here wants The Nighthawk pull a victory out of the mouth of despair, seemingly pulling a win from thin air?
(Some cheers are heard, but the crowd seems half-hearted.) Nick: So who just wants me to kick some arse DWO-style? (An absolutely defeaning pop fills the arena. Nick chuckles to himself.) Nick: I thought so… (Nick tosses the mic back to the ring announcer, slumping in the corner and waiting patiently for the match to begin.)
Adrian Anderson: Nothing prepared us for the shocking announcemnt of the mystery partner! RING ANNOUNCER: Here to announce the MYSTERY PARTNER...to be accompanied by APOLLO and MOO KAME...here is the DWO PRESIDENT MIKE BILODEAU!!!!
("Karma Police" by Radiohead plays. Bilodeau walks to the ring in jeans and a Moo Kame T-shirt. The fans boo him loudly. Apollo and Moo Kame follow him into the ring.)
Mike Bilodeau: Ladies and Gentlemen, the time has come. This well-guarded and careful mystery partner is about to come through that curtain and rip
Nick Leshenn's big fat head off.
(The fans boo, Leshenn tries to go at him again.) Mike Bilodeau: Now calm down, we might meet in the Deadly Rumble, if you can survive the onslaught of my mystery partner for Al Robertson. Save it for later. Before I call him down, I want to just say one thing to Alex
Martinez. You have that tape, you son of a bitch. You have this over my head. Now, only three people know what's on that tape. I can't afford having that aired on television, and if you as much as speak what's on that tape, I'll sue your ass beyond belief, understand me?
(The fans boo loudly.) Mike Bilodeau: Now, Nick Leshenn. It's been a while since we talked, with all this tragedy and mis-haps occurring. How have you been? Oh that's great. Last year you almost won this thing, but you didn't. And that was my fault, like everything else wrong with this world. Blame it on the promoter. No, that's not going to work anymore. Your whole summer last year, you almost took my DWO Championship, but you didn't, and that's my fault? I think you are to blame, because you never took the time to learn an effective finishing maneuver or any kind of wrestling move for that matter. But, this year, the attention is all on you. Styles was fired.
Payne was too worn out. So you're the big star in attendance, aren't you. I don't think your capable of pulling off a big show by yourself. You need me to draw you attention. And that's what I'm going to do tonight. Let me introduce you to the mystery man in this match.
(The fans cheer for the announcement.) Mike Bilodeau: The MAN who singlehandedly made the UEW! The MAN that destroyed everyone in his path. The MAN who doesn't take NO for an answer.
The best business person in the free world. That man would be none other than...... ME! MIKE BILODEAU! RING THE BELL!! Johnny Cassimo: We couldn't believe it. It was a huge shocker, DWO PRESIDENT MIKE BILODEAU, who had just completed a HUGE match with KIERAN
EROTIKKA, was going to wrestle again, this time AGAINST VETERAN WRESTLERS, like Nick Leshenn and Al Robertson. I was stunned, but I was in awe of the great stature and courage of Mike Bilodeau.
Robert Jackson: My ass. Adrian Anderson: Alexander Munroe was eliminated in under three minutes by
Al Robertson! Al Robertson using a DEADLY FLYING HURRINCANRANA on Munroe.
Munroe was eliminated so early that no partners had to switch until then. That mean LESHENN and BILODEAU had to team against AL ROBERTSON! Leshenn didn't want anything to do with it, so he DDT'ed Bilodeau on the mat. He was then dropkicked from behind by Al Robertson. Robertson then went over to Bilodeau and put him in his finisher the SHARPSHOOTER DEATHLOCK, and
BILODEAU TAPPED OUT! ROBERTSON and LESHENN WILL BOTH MAKE IT TO THE DEADLY
RUMBLE! Robert Jackson: Leshenn was mighty pissed that he didn't get the cover, so he gave Al Robertson the LESHENN DRIVER to make up for it. That's
Leshenn's flying head scissors into a modified DDT manuever that he made so famous. The Leshenn Driver on Robertson, gave Bilodeau more than enough time to sneak to the back with the help of Apollo and Moo Kame.
Adrian Anderson: This would all lead up to the last tag match of the evening! --------DWO DEADLY DRAW 2---------- SUBMISSION MATCH Must make both opponents say "I Quit." --------DWO DEADLY DRAW 2----------- Alex Extreme & J'ster vs. "International Excellence" Tyrone Parker & "The Franchise" Johnny Rebel ---------DWO DEADLY DRAW 2----------- Robert Jackson: I really think this was the wildest match of the night, hands down. Extreme and J'ster HATE each other, there's no doubt about that. The teammork here was non-existant. They both refused to tag each other. J'ster's manager Khujo got into the action at times as well. J'ster finally got the last laugh on Johnny Rebel, and Rebel was eliminated.
Tyrone Parker fell prey to Alex Extreme and a chair. After the chair,
Extreme got Parker in a Boston Crab, and Parker submitted. J'ster and Alex
Extreme will make it to the Deadly Rumble.
Adrian Andeson: As they were "celebrating", Alex Martinez ran to the ring with a shovel, and hit J'ster from behind in the back of the head, returning the favor from earlier in the night. Alex Extreme's limo driver ran to the ring, for a reason we'd all be shocked over. He threw a FLASH
GRENADE into the ring blinding both Martinez and J'ster. This gave Alex
Extreme an opportunity to put the LAST LAUGH on J'STER! After he let go, he grabbed the shovel, and gave Martinez a shot right below the belt! It was a shocking attack, and Alex Extreme and J'ster will meet again later tonight in the Deadly Rumble!
