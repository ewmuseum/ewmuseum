Deadly Draw 2: Control Center

1 April 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
THE DEADLY WRESTLING ORGANIZATION PRESENTS D E A D L Y D R A W 2 C O N T R O L C E N T E R L I V E F R O M DWO S T U D I O S -------------------------------------------------------------- ("Back in Black" by AC/DC plays as we fade into the DWO Studios. Pictures of Nick Leshenn, Professor Payne, Rick Styles, Roy Rainer, Mike Bilodeau,
Apollo, J'ster, and Sick Rick are hanging on the studio walls. We fade into the front desk, where four familiar men are standing, wearing formal suits. One is Robert Jackson, head announcer for the DWO since it's first card. The other is Johnny Cassimo, the color announcer since the first card. Another is Adrian Anderson, the latest addition to the DWO, who has been like the "Jim Ross" of e-wrestling ever since his debut last year at the Deadly Draw. The last man is DWO Owner and President, Mike Bilodeau.)
Robert Jackson: Ladies and Gentlemen, welcome back to the DEADLY WRESTLING ORGANIZATION! I'm Robert Jackson, an out of retirement for a good cause, Robert Jackson. I got a call last weekend from Mike Bilodeau, asking me for one more run in the federation that I love. And here we are, we already have a HUGE roster forming, and the DEADLY DRAW 2 will happen on
MAY 16th, LIVE in San Antonio Texas, the home of wrestling great, Shawn
Michaels. We have huge news on matches that are being rumored for the
Deadly Lottery that kicks off the Deadly Draw Pay Per View in May, and a
HUGE announcement from Mike Bilodeau that will rock the foundation of
E-Wrestling. Johnny Cassimo: Shut your mouth, Jackson, there are other announcers on this team. I'm Johnny "Too Hot for Too Much" Cassimo. You may remember me from such great feature films as "Cassimo is Coming to Town" and
"Cliffhanger." Adrian Anderson: Johnny, Sylvester Stallone was in that movie. Johnny Cassimo: Sure, the PG Rated one. Think with your other head for a change will you, mister 40-year-old virgin. Anyway, I'm glad to be back in the broadcast booth for, what I feel, will be the crowning acheivment of the Deadly Wrestling Organization. Last year, Professor Payne and Nick
Leshenn stole the show at the Turning Stone Casino in Upstate, New York.
This year, I think a YOUNGER, HIPPER, and FRESHER talent roster will surpass the original. And of course, the legends of wrestling will also be here. There's no telling what we could see in May in San Antonio.
Adrian Anderson: The card is huge. I'm Adrian Anderson, and I can't wait until May 16th, when I think Nick Leshenn, the NIGHTHAWK will finally be avenged for what happened to him last summer. And the reason for that summer of hell for Leshenn is the man standing to my right, the DWO
President Mike Bilodeau. Mike Bilodeau: Mister Anderson, I want to take this opportunity to apologize to the wrestling community for my actions last summer. They were disgusting and very inappropriate. So this spring, I'm going to make it up to those people, and throw a killer card with the Deadly Draw 2.
This brings me to my first major announcement. I would like to announce the appointment of a new DWO President. I am way too busy to take this job this summer, and a GREAT friend of mine will be booking the matches and taking care of the talent. That man is, the former UEW National
Champion....DARK VANITY! Unfortunately, Dark Vanity could not make it to the studio tonight, he's working on a card, but he has given me the information on the FIRST TWO MATCHES to be signed for the HUGE DEADLY DRAW
2 card in May!
Robert Jackson: Huge news coming from the Bilodeau camp. What are the matches, Bilodeau? Mike Bilodeau: The first match of the DEADLY LOTTERY match, where people are paired by the luck of the draw into matches with WILD stipulations.
The first match to be signed is a $100,000 LETHAL LADDER MATCH! The winner will have to climb a 15 foot ladder and grab $100,000 in cash, that he'll split with his or her partner.
and the PARTICIPANTS: "Hot Stuff" Scott Richards of the EWO who will team with: The Trixxxster of the UEW and their opponents will be: "2 dYnAmiC" Wesley Steel of the IWC and: "The Puppet Master" Nicolas Christian, the upcoming UEW Star LETHAL LADDER MATCH FOR $100,000 2 dYnAmiC Wesley Steel & Puppet Master Nicolas Christian vs. "Hot Stuff" Scott Richards & The Trixxster Johnny Cassimo; I've heard tremendous things about Nicolas Christian and the Trixxxster, and I'm sure Richards and Steel will impress us also. That sounds like a great match signed by new President, Dark Vanity. Another match signed?
Mike Bilodeau: The next match will be a: DOUBLE LETHAL ROUND ROBIN MATCH: This will surely be the wildest match on the card, because it is the FIRST match of it's kind. Here are the rules.
1. The assigned teams will start off first. This is an elimination match, both members of the other team must be pinned. 2. When five minutes pass, the man in the ring for one team, will switch teams and be the partner of the man that is on the outside of the opposite team. And vice versa for the other legal man in the ring. This will continue every five minutes.
3. When the first man is pinned, the man that is left without a partner will team with the illegal man of the opposing team in a handicap match against the man that pinned his partner.
4. If the handicapped man is pinned, the two men will be the winners and go onto the Deadly Rumble main event later that night. 5. If the handicapped man should pin one of the men on the opposing team, he and the other man will go onto the Deadly Rumble later that night. and the PARTICIPANTS ARE: "Top Dog" Rick Styles of the UEW and "Synchronicity" Al Robertson of the SRCA and their opponents: Nick "The Nighthawk" Leshenn of the UEW and "Show Stealer" Alexander Munroe of the VAW!! DOUBLE LETHAL ROUND ROBIN MATCH: Rick Styles & Al Robertson vs. Nick Leshenn & Alexander Munroe Johnny Cassimo: That is way too confusing, Bilodeau. Mike Bilodeau: It will be explained in further detail later. As of right now, you can start flashing but please review the flashing policy:
FLASHING POLICY: 1. You must role play for this event, it does not have to be substantial, but I expect ONE every other week! That's about 5 before the event, and I think that's reasonable. You can start flashing now, even if you don't know your match, but the flash show will not debut until further notice.
2. If you don't have 5 flashes for the event, you will have a lesser chance of winning. 3. Strategies for the event are due by APRIL 20. If you don't know your match by then, you will be informed of an extension of that deadline. 4. FLASHING and STRATEGIES are relaxed, but not that relaxed. Remember, I can only decide who wins based on your work and effort. The better the effort, the better the performance of your wrestler.
Adrian Anderson: SEND ALL flashes to [detail withheld] .
