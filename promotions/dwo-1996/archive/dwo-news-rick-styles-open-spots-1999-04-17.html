DWO: “Top Dog” Rick Styles Removed from Deadly Draw 2

Apr 17, 1999, 12:00:00 AM
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
THE DEADLY WRESTLING ORGANIZATION PRESENTS D E A D L Y D R A W 2 C O N T R O L C E N T E R L I V E F R O M DWO S T U D I O S -------------------------------------------------------------- ("Back in Black" by AC/DC plays as we fade into the DWO Studios. Pictures of Nick Leshenn, Professor Payne, Rick Styles, Roy Rainer, Mike Bilodeau,
Apollo, J'ster, and Sick Rick are hanging on the studio walls. We fade into the front desk, where four familiar men are standing, wearing formal suits. One is Robert Jackson, head announcer for the DWO since it's first card. The other is Johnny Cassimo, the color announcer since the first card. Another is Adrian Anderson, the latest addition to the DWO, who has been like the "Jim Ross" of e-wrestling ever since his debut last year at the Deadly Draw.)
Johnny Cassimo: You're kidding me right? Adrian Anderson: No, I'm not kidding. He's not going to be there. Johnny Cassimo: You can't do this to me, to the fans, like this. You can't tell me "Top Dog" Rick Styles has been fired from the DWO!
Robert Jackson: Johnny, Johnny, calm down, I'm sure there's a reasonable answer to that question. Adrian Anderson broke the story here this morning, and that news is "TOP DOG" Rick Styles has been REMOVED from his spot in the DWO's Second Annual Deadly Draw! The details are sketchy, so we have invited DWO Owner and President, Mike Bilodeau here today to give us the news first hand.
Adrian Anderson: Bilodeau hasn't arrived yet, so we can only speculate as to why Rick Styles won't be appearing at the Deadly Draw. This has been a shock to everyone here in DWO Headquarters, the crew and the broadcast team. Rick Styles has been at EVERY DWO Event in one way or another, and it'll be a shame to not see him there in May. It's kind of controversial to hear this news. This reminds me of what happened between Bret Hart and the World Wrestling Federation in 1997. Styles has been the pinnicle of this organization for quite some time.
Johnny Cassimo: That's right Anderson, how will this card be pulled off without the Main Event?
Robert Jackson: Johnny, there are a plethera of talented wrestlers on this card, and simply because Rick Styles will not be on the card, will not make the card any less exciting and entertaining.
Johnny Cassimo: Oh what the hell do you know, you're retired! Adrian Anderson: Now, guys, let's not spread any false rumors. Mike Bilodeau's limo is just pulling into the parking lot and as soon as he gets into the booth, we'll know what's going on.
Johnny Cassimo: This can't be good. We know that Bilodeau and Mark Rose,
Styles' manager, were not exactly the best of friends a few years ago.
Remember the incident? Mike Bilodeau quit the federation, because of Shawn
Roy favoring Rose, and then in a backstabbing situation, Shawn Roy gave
Mark Rose the office of DWO President? Bilodeau was trying to get Styles fired for MONTHS before that. It all stemmed after that fight between former Champion Sir Giles Cavendish, when he started singing the National
Anthem of the UK, and Styles came down and thrashed him. After that fight,
Bilodeau went all out to get Styles fired. Styles was complaining to high heaven about Bilodeau having too much creative control. That could be the situation here today.
Adrian Anderson: Well, to break out of character for a minute here folks, we all know that creative control is the strongest tool for wrestlers to get their characters over. It's unfortunate when creative control of the wrestlers, doesn't mesh well with the promoters creative ideas. It's a harsh business we're in.
(Mike Bilodeau's voice comes from behind the announcers. He is entering the studio wearing a suit that is somewhat disorganized. His tie is out of place, and his shirt is ripped. His sleeve is ripped and hanging by a thread. His lip is busted, and his eye is black.)
Mike Bilodeau: The three of you shut your mouths right now. (The announcers are startled. They move out of the way.) Mike Bilodeau: Rick Styles has been fired effective immediately. He's gone over the line, and NOBODY goes over the line with me. I was meeting with him yesterday in my office, to go over some promotional ideas and character development. The bastard walked out of the meeting after getting a beep on his pager. That was the first mistake, Styles. NOBODY walks out on my for ANY reason whatsoever. Then the son of a bitch tells me my ideas are too old, or unoriginal, and the person that paged him had better ideas.
Adrian Anderson: What's this about Bilodeau? Mike Bilodeau: I'm trying to tell you, Big Show. The man on the pager was the man that I most hate in this business, Jay Saul, owner of the UEW. The point is, Rick Styles said he had an offer to work on a UEW card that same night, and that he was getting paid more money. So here's the deal, he's walked out on a legally binding contract, and I'm going to see to it that
Styles lives the next five years of his life in a legal battle. He's cost me hundreds of dollars. He's cost the DWO a vacancy, and a very hard to fill vacancy. This card depends on every spot being filled in a certain amount of time. The asshole will be hearing from my lawyer.
Robert Jackson: Well, who did this to you? Mike Bilodeau: That's all I have to say at this point. Good day gentlemen. (Mike Bilodeau starts to leave.) Johnny Cassimo: Did Styles hit you? Mike Bilodeau: What? What did you say? Johnny Cassimo: I asked if Rick did this to you. Mike Bilodeau: I suggest that you drop the subject, Mr. Cassimo, or you may find yourself working for the IWF/WOW.
Johnny Cassimo: I just think it's weird that if Styles was the one walking out on his own free will, why he would hit you? That's all.
Mike Bilodeau: Are you calling me some kind of a liar? Johnny Cassimo: Oh no sir. Mike Bilodeau: You want the truth do you? Johnny Cassimo: It would be nice. Mike Bilodeau: Oh would it? Fine, you want the truth? RICK STYLES would
NOT run an interference in the match with Nick Leshenn with a blow torch.
The truth is, Styles was my only chance of making sure that Leshenn never made it to the Deadly Rumble. So Styles walked out of his contract, refusing to do the task at hand. Two days later, in the parking lot, NICK
LESHENN jumped me in the parking lot taunting me that he's going to win the
Deadly Draw! He hit me with a god damn crowbar. Is that what you wanted to hear?
Robert Jackson: Well, how does this change things for the card? Mike Bilodeau: The card? The card?? I could give a damn. But, I'm putting ALEX MARTINEZ back INTO The match WITH SICK RICK....and leaving a
VACANCY in the match with NICK LESHENN until I find a suitable replacement.
HERE'S THE CARD AGAIN, PLEASE REVIEW, THERE ARE CHANGES: SORRY FOR THE
CHANGES, but I HAVE TO DO THESE THINGS!! ;-)
