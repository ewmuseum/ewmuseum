....The old makes way for the new ... Deadly becomes Defiance.... THE DEFIANT WRESTLING ORGANIZATION presents DAWN OF DEFIANCE LIVE! Friday Night April 8th, 2005 Live on Pay Per View from the SkyDome in Toronto ---------------------------------------------------------- (Scene fades into the DWO Control Center live from DWO Headquarters in Utica, New York. A Deadly Wrestling Organization banner is hanging with a red X through it DEFIANCE is written in spray paint over it. Standing in front of the banner is Samantha Schafer, a new commentator for the DWO. She is a young woman with blonde hair and an impressive chest. She is wearing a red dress.)
Samantha Schafer: Welcome everyone to the new DWO Control Center, allow me to introduce myself. My name is Samantha Schafer, I am a graduate of Syracuse University, and I have been working the past several years as a broadcast journalist for CNN Sports. I'm proud to be part of this new era in the DWO and we are proud to bring you several major announcements concerning the upcoming DAWN OF DEFIANCE pay per view from Toronto. First let's run down how this unprecedented card works.
(Scene fades to Samantha in front of a podium.) Samantha Schafer: The roster is growing. We've already signed some great top talent in the world of professional wrestling. But nobody will know exactly who they're facing until the bell rings in their match on the night of the pay per view. In just a couple of days, a complete roster will be sent out for the wrestlers to review. Then they will send an open challenge to 3 other wrestlers. The closest matches wins and there will be surprise opponents for every wrestler on the roster. It's going to be a night controlled by the wrestlers and for the wrestlers. It's going to be controversial. It's going to be dramatic. Most of all, it's going to rip the lid off of this industry. And we have huge news!
(A giant picture of "Top Dog" Rick Styles appears on the screen next to Samantha.) Samantha Schafer: We just learned this morning that DWO Legend "The
Top Dog" Rick Styles has signed on for action. We thought Styles had retired at DWO WrestleKade X after he was defeated in his big match.
But he's back and he's got a big challenge. RICK STYLES has put out an OPEN CHALLENGE to DWO Champion, "The Savior" Jamie Underwood.
(A picture of Jamie Underwood appears on the screen.) Samantha Schafer: Now, rumor has it that DWO CEO Mike Bilodeau and
Jamie Underwood have had a falling out and it is yet unknown if Jamie
Underwood will defend his title on this card. But I can't imagine that Underwood would sit this huge event out regardless of how he feels about Mike Bilodeau. Especially now that the legendary Rick
Styles has tossed out a challenge to him.
(A picture of The Mercenary appears on the screen.) Samantha Schafer: The DWO National Champion, the Mercenary has also signed on. As well as stars such as Ian Walsh, the Jacks, and some newcomers such as The Outsider. The roster is filling up, and a more detailed announcement will be following in the coming days. Until then, this is Samantha Schafer in the DWO Control Center.
