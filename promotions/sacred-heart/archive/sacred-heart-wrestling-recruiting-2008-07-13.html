Sacred Heart Wrestling

13 July 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
13 July 2008 Greetings Primetime Central, My name is "The Sacred Heart" Ronnie Rainbow. You may have heard of me... I have wrestled on all seven continents for about a half a century. Alas, my time in the ring is now over... but my love of the eternal and divine art of wrestling still burns strong. Now, I have gathered up all the riches I could muster to open my own wrestling promotion. Sacred Heart Wrestling is opening it's doors and looking for professional wrestlers with the drive to compete for its coveted championships in front of wrestling fans from around the world. Sacred Heart Wrestling may not be for everybody. We will traveling constantly, cross-country, to bring the best wrestling action to as many people as we can. If you are interested in joining a new wrestling federation run by a ring veteran, stop by Sacred Heart Wrestling headquarters and fill out an application. http://sacredheartwrestling.proboards103.com/index.cgi Dream onward, star-children... \m/ RR ---- 13 July 2008 So.... we meet again! ;) ---- 13 July 2008 Ah, yes, the one known as Argyle. It's good to see you again. Perhaps I shall see you in the Rainbow Ring~! \m/
