Sacred Heart Wrestling: First Three Wrestlers Signed!

22 July 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Greetings traversers of cyber-space and beyond! This is "The Sacred Heart" Ronnie Rainbow speaking on behalf of Sacred Heart Wrestling. I am pleased to inform you that SHW has signed a trio of warriors that have accepted the challenge to come and meet inside the rainbow ring in glorious combat! LOOK OUT~! \m/ First there was The Divine Impala Brotherman Quasar Jones... A holy man... one with the touch... a being of such great stature that his epic crown of curled hair that sits upon his head may reach EVEN THE TOPS OF THE SILVER MOUNTAIN ITSELF!! LOOK OUT~! \m/ /w\ Then came The Father... yet another holy man... Does it surprise you? Not I... For the quest for THE SACRED HEART is a holy quest... INDEED! For SHW is not just a wrestling federation... IT IS A SPIRITUAL PILGRIMAGE! A journey to the very far reaches of one's inner and outer self! A charge that can only be accepted by those willing to give in to the true awesome powers of TIME AND THE COSMOS! FOR WE ARE CHILDREN OF THE STARS AND HUSBANDS OF ROCKS AND THE EARTH UPON WHICH WE TRED! WE ARE DIVINE SPIRITS AND WAYWARD TRAVELLING SOULS! LOOOOOOOOOOOOOK OUTTTA~! ... \m/ Then there is the mountainious mammoth of a man... THE BONNACON! He breathes in your soul and EXPELS FLAMING EXCRIMENT FORTH FROM HIS BOWELS... must I say more? He was forged in myth, raised in legend... and now he lives IN THE SACRED HEART! \m/ ANd so thus, I ask... does anyone else have the spark? The quaintest little flame residing inside themselves to step into the rainbow ring... to cross the rainbow bridge... to find the meaning of it all... to stand atop the silver mountain and shout, "I HAVE FOUND THE SACRED HEART!" Keep dreaming... children of the night... children of the light... and all spirits lost in between... perhaps we shall meet within the rainbow ring... LOOKOUT~! http://www.sacredheartwrestling.proboards103.com/index.cgi \m/ -RR
