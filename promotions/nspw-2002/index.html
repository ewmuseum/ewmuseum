Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

New South Pro-Wrestling opened in August 2002 under the slogan 'It's Time For A Change', a touring promotion calling Tennessee home and running shows through Kentucky, Alabama, Arkansas, Georgia and northern Texas in homage to the southern territories of the 1970s and 1980s.

It billed itself as a three-ring circus, with heavyweights, lightweights and tag teams, and promised three titles and a Sunday night show, Showdown TV, on a small station with a four-week trial.

Its first event, The Change Begins, aired on 9 September 2002 before about 150 fans. Ryogo Matsuda won a Tennessee Triangle, Father Vengeance and Sammy Knight won their matches, and Blackheart beat Chris Virtue in the main event, after which the co-promoter Roy Watts turned on his partner Jerry Quinn and a six-man war was drawn up.

That card, three recruiting calls and its application survive.
