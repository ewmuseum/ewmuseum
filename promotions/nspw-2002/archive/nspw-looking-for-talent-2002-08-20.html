New South Pro-Wrestling: Looking for Talent

20 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
New South Pro-Wrestling It's Time For A Change New South Pro-Wrestling (NSPW) Promoter: Jerry Quinn & Roy Watts Concept: A traveling "three-ring" circus Wrestling promotion that will consistently tour the southern states of North America. An emphasis will be on the concept of storytelling, _wrestling_, and diversity. There will be heavyweights, lightweights, and Tag-Team wrestling. There will be something for everyone, hence the "three-ring circus" tag. If you don't like something one minute, I guarantee if you wait you'll find something else to entertain you. Base: NSPW calls Tennessee 'home' but runs shows in Kentucky, Alabama, Arkansas, Georgia, and Northern Texas. This is to pay homage to the great Federations of the 70's, 80's and early 90's such as Smokey Mountain Wrestling, Continental Wrestling Association, United States Wrestling Association, and the National Wrestling Alliance. Titles: The World Heavyweight Title, the "Beat The Champ" World Heavyweight Television Title, and The World Heavyweight Tag-Team Titles. Shows: Showdown TV, broadcated every Sunday night on station WBCZ. It's a small, weak agreement but they will carry the show for 4 weeks and if it goes well, will continue to carry it - so who knows, if things work out, this could become something. Closing: I think that is all the basics. There is, of course, more information but I'll save you the read. This is just the basic information for the Promotion. If you're at all interested, e-mail Jerry Quinn and let him know. Hope to hear from ya!
