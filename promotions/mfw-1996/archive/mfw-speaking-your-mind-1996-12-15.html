MFW: Speaking Your Mind (12/14/96)

15 December 1996
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
<camera picks up the MFW Speaking Your Mind set as the MFW theme music plays. Followed by a voiceover>
VO: Ladies and gentlemen, please make welcome, your host for MFW's Speaking
Your Mind, newly signed, Dylan Antony III.
<camera pans down to find Dylan Antony standing behind his MFW podium>
DA: Hello MFWers. How are we doing tonight. As you know, the first segment of Speaking Your Mind was scheduled for tomorrow, but because of the participation of the members of this league, we will have one tonight.
Tonight we will here from Tiger Claw, Maze, Chameleon, and Lunatic Louie. Let's get to our first clip: _______________________________________ SCENE: A dingy alleyway. Garbage litters the ground, and various liquids leak from holes in the wall. The noise of cars can be heard in the background, as can the tinny sound of music coming from a portable radio.
Voices come and go, and they're not speaking English. A short Japanese man comes into the shot, dressed in a tailor-made three piece suit with a glittering pin in the lapel. He walks carefully through the mess of the alley, turning his nose up at the stink.]
Man: Not exactly the nicest place in the world, is it? Well, sometimes the greatest prizes in the world can be found in the gutter. People tend to overlook the underbelly of society, thinking that there's nothing of value there. Well, those people are wrong. Allow me to introduce myself. I am
Brian Lau. Some of you might have heard of me. I am possibly one of the greatest managers/promoters in the world. I have managed many atheletes in many sports, but find that unarmed combat brings me the most joy. It was this sport that brought me to the man that I knew could be a star. I have managed him since he was 15 years old. In the nine years we have been together, I have helped him to attain many world championships in many different countries. He became World Champion in the Muay Thai circuit in
Thailand at the age of 17, and actually had to be stripped of the belt in order for someone else to hold it. He has been the IIWF Intercontinental champion on three separate occasions. Now I know what you all may be thinking. "Who cares what he's done before, he's never been to the MFW."
That's right. So I've set up this little demonstration for you to judge for yourself. Behold... Tiger Claw.
[The camera pans around a full 180 degrees to show a group of men watching some kind of fight. Several metal drums hold flaming paper to shed light on the display. The camera moves forward, between a few men, to see two figures battling it out in a cleared area. One of the men is huge. He stands close to 7 feet tall, and must weigh at least 300 pounds. He is pure muscle, and wears a simple pair of pants with a piece of rope used as a belt. The other man is dwarfed by the first. He is about 6'2", 220 pounds, and wears a pair of traditional Thai Boxing shorts. His head is shaved except for a long braid that hangs from the back. His fists are wrapped, and he wears no footwear. The large man charges the smaller one, and the smaller one dodges to the side and throws a hard round forward kick to the larger man's leg.
The smaller man's shin hits the larger man square in the knee, and the larger man howls out in pain as he drops to his knees. The smaller man cinches the larger man's head and begins throwing knees into his torso. The smaller man finishes off with a rising knee to the larger man's head, making the larger man fall backwards. The smaller man backs off to let the larger man get to his feet. The larger man lifts himself up, and the smaller man roars into the air, then closes in. He runs, and then launches himself into the air, jutting one knee out. The smaller man's knee smacks right into the side of the larger man's head, and the larger man slumps down to the ground, unconscious. Several of the men in the crowd cheer, but many curse and throw pieces of paper to the ground. The smaller man walks over to where Brian is standing.]
BL: Tiger Claw... What a vicious man. He may be small in size, but his heart is giant. His endurance is so strong that I have seen him fight for two straight hours without getting beaten. This is just an example of what Claw likes to do best, and that's to slay giants. Nothing is quite as satisfying as watching a huge man flop around like a fish out of water because he can't stand. Tiger Claw is a true warrior. He lives for the fight. The reason I am here is to take care of the business side of his career. Why should Tiger
Claw have to worry about who sells his posters? Why should Tiger Claw have to worry about the ins and outs of contract law? He shouldn't, and he doesn't. Tiger Claw fights, and that is fine enough with him. But don't just take my word for it, ask him...
TC: I have come to the MFW for one thing and one thing only: combat. I wish to fight the best in a no holds barred environment. I wish...
[Suddenly, the large man Tiger Claw fought gets to his feet and runs towards Tiger Claw, screaming like a madman. Tiger Claw calmly turns, assuming a fighting stance. The large man closes in, and Tiger Claw spins with a high forward round kick that hit's the man in the head, and a cracking sound is heard. Tiger Claw leaps forward and grabs the man in a half nelson/hammerlock, and forces his head into one of the flaming barrels. The large man screams in pain, and Tiger Claw lets go. The man falls to the ground once again, now bleeding from one ear.]
BL: Take a good look, members of the MFW. This is your future. Complete and total defeat at the hands of the mighty Tiger Claw. Grim Reaper, you will be the first in the MFW to feel the wrath of the beast. Be prepared. It may help you to keep from getting hurt, but will not save you from defeat. Good day.
[The shot fades.] _________________________________ DA: I sure wouldn't want to be in his opponent's shoes. How will the Reaper fare against him? We're still trying to get an interview with him. Now for our next clip:
_________________________________ (Maze) (Walking through a badly lighted twisting corridor. Maze pops out from around a corner.) "Huh, I see you have enter my Maze, my Maze of Confusion. You've enter a place and time where every decision you make can either make you or break you. You my friend just made the wrong decision, because when you face me, every decision you make will be the wrong one."
(Walks closer, until the screen fades to black) __________________________________ DA: This must be MFW's first master of the mind games. We'll have to see how he fare's against the likes of Matt Carew. Does he have what it takes to win the gold? Now for the next clip:
___________________________________ (Chameleon) (You see Chameleon's back as he is looking into a mirror.) "I bet your wondering why am I standing in front of this mirror. Well I'm doing this give you chumps a glance or should I say fore shadow of what you will experience when you put your fighting skills and style up against me. You will find that the KA-ME-LEE-ON uses your own abilities to crush your helpless body, into flaming heap melting flesh. When you step in the arena to fight me, your fighting yourself. Remember what made you great, now will seal your fate."
(Chameleon punches and shatter the mirror) ______________________________________ DA: The MFW sure has signed some damn strange people. If everybody's like this, I'm not sure I'll stay around long. How about one last clip for tonight:
______________________________________ (The scene is beautiful mansion on tops of and isolated mountain range, you then can see a lush and huge office)
scarface: Send Louie in please (Lunatic Louie then walks in) Louie: Yeah you wanted to see me i heared the bosses had some news./ Scarface: Yeah it was just announced that you'll be taking on Chameleon in the first round of the tourny
Louie: Well that's fine but does it really matter who i take on cause i know i'am going to stomp anyone who gets in my way and if for some reason i can't handle it the "family" is there to back me up
Scarface: True i know you'll bring the gold to us and i'll make sure of that and soon all your oppents will be sleeping with the fishes.
__________________________________________ DA: Is the MFW ready to take on a "family?" We'll have to see later as this story develops. Well, that's it for tonight. See you tomorrow night when hopefully we'll hear from the rest of the league. Until then this is
Dylan Antony III saying goodnight. Goodnight.
