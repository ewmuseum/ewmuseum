MFW: Fantasy Fighting League Idea

10 December 1996
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hey guys..... I've just come up with an idea of creating a fantasy fighting league. This would not be a wrestling league, so to speak, but more of a street fighting league. It would offer championships such as those offered by wrestling feds. I guess the closest thing that this league would resemble would be
Extreme Fighting. It would have 1 weekly show that would host the fights, a daily show to have flashes, a monthly pay per view, random encounters to be determined by me. Titles would not change hands on the random encounters. I need about 16 fighters to start the league (no more than 2 per handler) I have no program to run the fights so I am in the process of developing a formula to determine the fights using dice. Also, roleplays will help alot with the outcome of each fight. If you are interested at all in this league, please throw an e-mail at me [detail withheld]
And I will send you the creation sheet. Cards will be written in Play by
Play format with one or two commentators. I'm looking to start on Monday,
Dec. 23. with a championship tournament.
