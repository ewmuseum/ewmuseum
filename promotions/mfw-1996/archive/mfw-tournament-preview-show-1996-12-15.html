MFW: Tournament Preview Show

15 December 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
<"Where the River Flows" by Collective Soul plays as the participants of the Championship Tournament flash on the screen. "Stunning" Steven Smithe and Johnathan"the Jester"Sysk are seen standing before the patented MFW ring.>
SS: HERE WE ARE...JUST A LITTLE OVER ONE WEEK FROM THE PREMIERE OF THE MFW. THE EXCITEMENT FILLS THE AIR HERE IN DALLAS, WHERE THE FIRST EVENT TAKES
PLACE IN THE DONG JUING DOJO. TONIGHT WE BRING YOU A VIEW OF THE TOURNAMENT
BRACKET WITH OUR PICKS AND OPINIONS. JS: Our first match of the evening features Joselito Santiago versus the
Shining Path Terrorist. Although only 25 years old, Shining Path has been extensively trained in Guerilla Warfare in Latin American Countries. He came home to settle down, but found that life without violence is not life at all. Opponents better watch out for the German Luger he carries in his back pocket. It's a deadly weapon with deadly consequences. He is my pick to win the whole thing.
SS: Not so fast there, John. Joselito Santiago is faster and has more aeriel abilities. It'll be interesting to see how this match turns out.
Santiago practices the martial art of Capoeira and Shining path may have problems with him. I pick Santiago to win this match, but lose later in the tournament. Our next match features Chameleon versus "the Original
Gangster" Louie Lunatic. How do you see this match, John? JS: I pick the Chameleon. He is very well balanced and has an edge in the quickness and aeriel abilities.It's gonna be a close match, but I see the
Chamelion coming out the victor in this match. I have heard that he has a very interesting death move involving fire.
SS: Again I have to disagree with you. Louie Lunatic is sponsored by the mafia and claims to be here to take over the street fighting world. Louie
Lunatic is my pick to win it all. He also has a death move that involves fire. Tell us about the next match up, Johnathan.
JS: The next match involves The Grim Reaper and Tiger Claw. I say Tiger
Claw will take this match. He comes from a rough life in Bangkok and is a master at Muay Thai. He comes into the match looking like the underdog, but his stamina should keep him in the match.
SS: HELL HATH FROZEN OVER....For once I agree with you. Tiger Claw's youth should keep him in the match. At 24 years old, he has a physical advantage over the Reaper. The other fighters should pray that Tiger Claw's wins the match, because if he doesn't, the wrath of the Reaper will surely come on the MFW. The last match of the first round features Matt Crarew against
Maze. Tell us what you think, Johnathan.
JS: I pick the former construction worker, Matt Carew to take this match.Having the 25 pound sledgehammer gives him a definite plus in this match, if he uses it carefully. But he makes one mistake, Maze could quickly turn it on him. But I don't see that happening, so Matt Carew to win.
SS: Well JESUS CHRIST....I agree again. I gotta find a new colleague, this is too scary. Anyone who carries a 25 pound hammer with him to the ring definetly means business. It will be Matt Carew, in a very short match.
Well, that's it......... JS: WAIT A MINUTE....I WASN'T THROUGH YET. They're telling me we have a word from our Vice President Mike Berger.
<via satelite from Chicago, Illinois.> MB: I just wanna say best of luck to all of our fighters. Be prepared for a tough tournament on Monday. We hope our first tournament is a success and that there will be much more league action in the months to come. Now back to Steven and John.
<back to arena> SS: SEE YOU ON MONDAY NIGHT FROM THE DONG JUING DOJO IN DALLAS, TX. <Camera fades as "Welcome to the Jungle" by Guns-n-Roses plays in the background and the commentators mysteriously disappear>
**note from the president** This show is just to give our commentators a personality. It has no bearing on the actual outcome of the tournament. It hasn't even been ran yet. GOOD
LUCK TO ALL...... Bracket: Shining Path Terrorist Joselito Santiago Chameleon "The Original Gangster" Louie Lunatic The Grim Reaper Tiger Claw Matt Carew Maze
