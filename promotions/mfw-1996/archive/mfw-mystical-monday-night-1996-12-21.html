Path's down and Santiago goes for the early cover.

Santiago up....AND THEN BACK DOWN AGAIN WITH AN AWESOME CHOKESLAM!!!
SS: This match started out to be all Santiago, but Path has turned it around. Santiago is still down. Path goes up onto one of our columns. HE

DA: This match is scheduled for one fall. Introducing first...weighing in at 275 pounds, from New York City...here is "The Original Gangster" Lunatic
Louie.
<Louie emerges from beyond the MFW curtain. He's puffing on a cigar and
Scarface walks behind him carrying a briefcase>
DA: His opponent...from Anytown, Kentucky...at 235 pounds, here is Chameleon <Chameleon emerges from beyond the MFW curtain also puffing on a cigar>
SS: This promises to be a great match. The combatants are circling each other, sizing each other up slowly. Louie makes the first move with a kick to the knee.
JS: Chameleon not phased a bit by that move and he counters with a kick of his own. He backs it up with a Russian let sweep.
SS: Louie's pin stripped suit may hinder his performance here. Chameleon locks in a drop leg toe hold. Pain has stricken the face Lunatic Louie.
Chameleon let's go of the hold and climbs up on one of the columns. He comes off with a flying elbow drop
JS: NOBODY HOME!!! That move could've ended his career. Lunatic Louie goes right to work on the injured arem. He locks into an armbar. Chameleon for the ride. HE HITS THE COLUMN HARD!!! Louie pulls him back into the middle of the ring and goes for the cover. One....two...th....kickout by
Chameleon. SS: You won't get Chameleon that easy. He hits Louie with a flying lariat. Louie's down and Chameleon goes for the....figure four? He better check the rule book, you can't win by submission here in the MFW.
JS: Good point, but this move is wearing down Louie. Scarface slides Louie the briefcase and LOUIE HITS CHAMELEON WITH IT, NOT ONCE BUT TWICE. Both men are down.
SS: Louie starting to stir now. He's up but he's hurt. He can hardly walk. Chameleon up now too. He picks up Louie....big bodyslam. He stomps Louie in the head twice. Cover...ONE....TWO...THREE...FO...KICKOUT!
JS: What's he doing? Is he taking a break? Chameleon up on the column. Louie's up and Chameleon springs off of the column and catches Louie in a frankensteiner! FORGET ABOUT IT!! Here's the cover...ONE...TWO...THREE...FOUR...FIVE... Chameleon wins!!!
DA: The winner of this match, after 6:43, Chameleon SS: That was a great match. Both men fought the whole way through. The next match is about to start so let's go up to Dylan Antony III for the introductions.
DA: Introducing first...from Parts Unknown..One of the largest men in MFW at 390 pounds, the Grim Reaper...
<Blue Oyster Cult's "Don't Feat the Reaper" plays and the lights go out>
SS: Who the hell is playing with the damn lights!?!
<Lights come up and Reaper is standing in the middle of the ring> SS: HOLY SH*T....Did you see that? How'd he get into the ring so fast? DA: And his opponent, from Bangkok Thailand...Accompanied by Brian Lau here is Tiger Claw. <Thai boxing music plays as Tiger Claw and Brian Lau make their way to the ring.< JS: It says here that Tiger Claw is Muay Thai figher. Let's see how affective it is against this gian man of death.
SS: Reaper staring Tiger Claw down, but Tiger Claw holds his ground. Reaper charges, but Tiger Claw cathces him in the ribs with a forward round house kick. He backs it up with a punching combo to the rib area. He jumps on
Reaper's back and applieas a chokehold.
JS: Reaper looks dazed...Tiger Claw jumps down now and hits the back of
Reaper's knees with a drop kick. The Reaper is down on his knees.
SS: Tiger Claw is just too quick for the Reaper.Tiger Claw is up on the column..he jumps! IT'S THE GOLDEN TIGER STRIKE!!! That knee to Reaper's head knocked him out. Here's the cover. ONE...TWO...THREE...FOUR...FIVE
JS: Tiger Claw wins. That match only took 2:01. Tiger Claw is making his way back to the locker room.
SS: WHOA!!! REAPER'S UP AND HE LOOKS PISSED!!! <lights black out and when they come back up, the Reaper is no longer in the ring> JS: Wha...? Wherede go? Another great athlete here in MFW...I'm sure we'll hear lot's from him in the future...
