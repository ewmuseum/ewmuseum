Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The Supreme Wrestling Tournament announced itself over two posts, 28 and 30 December 2002, opening the qualifying rounds of what it called a year-long tournament and listing fifty-four wrestlers already signed, with about forty more wanted “in the next three days.”

Its own roster call names wrestlers built entirely around tournament- ready gimmicks and nicknames — “I Hate America” Al Jazeera, “Buzzsaw” Wesson, “The Iron Bull” Jake Demola, “Redemption” Justin Slayne, and “Violent” Victor Frost among them — with five more signings, including “Hedonistic” Jaime Roberts and “New Age Icon” Chris Collins, added in the second post two days later.

No card record of the tournament’s qualifying rounds or any later round, and no closing notice, survive; how far the Supreme Wrestling Tournament actually got before its own record stops is not something its own surviving record answers.
