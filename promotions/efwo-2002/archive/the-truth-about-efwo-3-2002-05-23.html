The Truth about EFWO

23 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The Electronic Wrestling Federation EFWO at www.ragedwar.com is run by [name withheld]. This is the biggest joke of an efed that I have ever seen. If you do not agree with the owner on certain issues, he deletes your account and kicks you out no questions asked. I have seen 6 people kicked out this past month. How does a fed go on like that? The wrestlers in the men's division are decent, but the women's division has become a joke lately since a booker named Linda took back over on booking duties. She only gives push to her friends or those that kiss her ass constantly. As in most cases, the women who have had world title reigns and push are some of the worse rpers one has ever seen. They clone off of each other by putting real wrestlers in their rps and do not put much thought or originality into their work. On top of that, they try to clone such characters as Lita, Trish Stratus and every roleplay sounds alike. Lets go to a break down of the characters... The Women's head booker plays a character named BabyDoll, she of course books herself to win major pay per views and title matches all the time, in fact no one can recall when she actually booked herself to lose. She is a wanna be Becky the Farmer's daughter of WOW or any other GLOW hick image type wrestler. The head booker's other character is Vampire Vixen, a wannabe goth. EFWO is a place where goths are a dime a dozen. The current women's champion is a Trish Stratus wanna be and in fact uses Trish for a picture and speaks to Hulk Hogan on a regular basis, another character talks to Booker T while yet another talks to Stone Cold... notice a pattern? Yes too much real wrestling mixed in with fantasy wrestling. Going back to the head women's booker giving her friends a huge push... A new wrestler has recently been recruited by her, her first match in the ring is a TV title shot at the upcoming pay per view. This new wrestler, Becky Doe appears to be a joke from the get go, might have only written 1-2 rps, has hardly any character description, and with the name sounds like a wannabe Babydoll Bradley. Another woman who is a Lita wannabe, just returned from a 6 month hiatus, but of course is good friends with the woman's booker and is already getting a world title shot. Now the EFWO has its share of orriginal wrestlers too... I am very impressed by Hanmiko X (forgot the spelling). She is a very original rper and has gotten NO push other than a North American title. Her rp's are very deep and way over the head of the typical 15 year old boy who wanks off at Trish Stratus pics all day. That perhaps is the reason she is not getting a bigger push, because she is TOO original Jade and Jessica Hagan are very original and held the world title in the past, yet are also only right now getting a North American Title push. Jade is a crazy lady, but not the typical crazy lady. Jessica, well she started out as a Steph McMahon type bitch, but has developed herself into something stronger, and she is a fan of The Who. Carmen D.- A Latin that pretends she is Italian, umm ok, but hey, havent seen that done. The Best improved wrestler is Heart Break Chick- when she started she was terrible, but she has come a long long way and is one of the best in the league, and is quite original now. The Cazzenotti twins, don't know what happened to them, they were original, BUT very annoying with their constant butt and boob jokes and cartoon references. They were a part of the Bartuzzunnini group which by concept was an original idea with an all female mafia, one of the best I have seen. i would have loved to see the handler go deeper with this, but he/she sold his/her characters out into being face and once they turned face, they were doomed to the sea of unoriginality. There is a couple other characters that might get mentioning.. Citty- just returned, but has potential to be a top again. Salamah Ali- I liked her character, but apparently the handler got pissed off about how this fed was run and did the right thing and quit. In any other fed, this wrestler could have been the most powerful, but since she was original, she got jobbed a lot. If you are into feds where you have no control as to how to run your character, no originality, are ready to kiss the owner and women's bookers ass and dissed for having an unusual original character, then the EFWO is for you!! If you want the bookers to control your angles, if you don't mind writing 10 rps and losing to someone who wrote only ONE, then join the EFWO.
