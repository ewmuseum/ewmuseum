FBW Information Packet

17 June 2000

- F B W - First Blood Wrestling I. What is First Blood Wrestling? FBW is all booked eWrestling federation where creative, if not a tad over-violent, geniuses can come and let out all of their aggression in quite a brutal enviroment through their created wrestlers. A chance to take your very own brain child marauder up and through the ranks to the number prize in the promotion.
The main goal of FBW is to create an extreme eWrestling enviroment where you, the handlers, are actively incontrol of what direction your character will take. This of course requires you to role-play, write strats, maybe do some on card interviews, or who knows what else. First Blood Wrestling isn't about who's got the most wins. Angles shine here.
II. Who runs it, what's it about? - Background Well, from the outside, I'll be running FBW.. the name's [name withheld]. I've been involved, both as an exec and as a participant in eWerstling for quite sometime now. But though I have often helped others run their promotions and often write matches here and there for quite a few different people, I have never really tried full force to erect a federation myself. That of course just changed.
As for within the actual promotion; First Blood Wrestling is your standard indy federation. For those of you who don't know what one of those is, or are shadey on the subject, you won't be seeing thousands upon thousands of fans at any FBW event. In fact, there is no titon tron, no great sound system, or anything of the like. Of course, lucky for First Blood, it is a little better off then most promotion and it does infact have it's very own, just finished, wrestling hall in Bangor, Maine.
III. First Blood Wrestling Employees - Broadcast Team: Tripp Warren: Tripp is probably too intelegent to honestly be apart of the First Blood team. Truth be known with his business savvy and all of his knowledge of the sport, inside and out, he could easily be commentating for a much larger scale promotion. But none the less, for now he finds himself here and you'll find him to be the more knowledgable of the team. Not biased, just a lover of good ole fashion wrestling.
"Luscious" Larry Lawson: Larry is definetly the sleaze of the team and at well over two hundred and fifty pounds of fat, he's hardly luscious. You won't likely here too many smart comments come out of this one mouths, but none the less he adds a bit of comic relief and wit to the broadcast when he can.
- Interviewers Doug Elliot: Straight and to the point, Doug doesn't waste time thinking about the wrestlers' feelings and often asks some pretty blunt questions.
- Other Important Figures Jasper T. Lynch: The one and only President of First Blood Wrestling. Lynch doesn't make too many public appearances and isn't a very camera friendly.
Though you won't see too much of the creative genius behind FBW, he'll make his presence known when he has something to say.
IV. Weekly Events First Blood Wrestling has one weeky show which happens every Tuesday night at around ten or eleven in the evening on public access in and around Maine.. it's shown around two or three in the morning throughout other areas of New England though. It's been dubbed, Tuesday Night Pro Pain. It'll have about five to six booked matches, but int he wild world of FBW who's to say who might end up fighting out of the blue? It's normally about a two hour show and will definetly be the place where angles start and perhaps finish.
As for pay per views? FBW doesn't have them. Mr. Lynch will occasionally call for a super card though, and all that is is basically a night of big action that will be taped and sold (much like some of ECW's older goodies).
V. Titles You'll probably notice there aren't too many titles in FBW, that's because I don't plan on having too many guys within the promotion when it starts. If the ranks do grow you can of course bet on another strap popping up, but for right now I think it's almost more competitive to only have a few.
- FBW Heavyweight Title: The Heavyweight Title is obviously the grand daddy of the straps in First Blood Wrestling. A rather nice looking strap it'll be saved for the best of the best in the league, but will be defended quite a bit more often then most other promotion's.
- First Blood Regional Title: The Regional Title may get a bad wrap for being only the second best in a two title federation, but make no mistake about it, the FBR Belt is nothing to look down upon. Defended probably ever at every single event, the strap is hard to capture and even harder to hold onto.
