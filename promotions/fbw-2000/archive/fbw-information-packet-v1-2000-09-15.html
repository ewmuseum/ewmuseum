The Opening - [About FBW] First Blood Wrestling is a relatively new angle and storyline based booked e-wrestling promotion. Created to add a more fun and exciting, if not violent, enviroment for your characters, FBW is completely dedicated to making sure everyone has a good time. We're trying to put the emphasis back on having a rather relaxed and enjoyable e-wrestling league, instead of just letting a bunch of hot heads try and see who can score more wins.
As I'm sure you can tell from the name, First Blood Wrestling is an indy promotion based on delivering hard-core and extreme pro-wrestling to the fans.
Being it is a small time federation, FBW works out of the New England area of the United States and sometimes does shows in and around the Tri-State area.
What this all means is you'll be pitting your superstars in a league that, like your wrestlers, is just getting on its feet. Struggling to reach the top to say the least. You should probably remember this if you do decide to join
First Blood. Obviously it wouldn't make much sense, unless you wanted to work out some type of storyline that is, to put one of your hall of famer wrestlers into First Blood.
Last, but not least, we here at First Blood assure you we have had extensive training in and around the world of fantasy wrestling. All of us here at FBW have either been role playing, match writting or heading up federations since about '95, and we're all ready to finally deliver a place that really lets everyone shine.
- [The First Blood Style] EXTREME! That about says it all. With the state of pro wrestling today, endorsing as many bloody chair shots and scantly clad women as possible, it's certainly good to know that there's a place like FBW - where everything else just pales in comparrision. First Blood doesn't just endorse violence in the ring, we breed it, package it, and ship it off all over the east coast to the fans that just eat it up like candy. You're thinking of joining a federation where putting your oppenent, wrapped in barb-wire and dosed in gasoline, through a c4 wrapped table is about as standard as an arm drag takedown.
II. Rules and Regulations - [Rules of the Road] Though I am sure most of you have seen all of these a hundred times over, I'm going to include a short list of rules that we here at First Blood Wrestling expect all of our superstars to abide by at all times. I don't think any of them are all that hard to follow, but if you think you'll have a tough time staying in line for some reason it's probably better you just turn your lil'
ass around right here and now. We're not asking much, but what we do ask we expect to be followed.
1. No SPAMMING. I realize a lot of promotions nowadays want you to role play as much as possible and whenever possible, but not here. Don't get us wrong, we expect you to RP for your matches, but we certainly don't want to see a horde of flashes that all basically say the same exact thing.
2. Don't break kayfabe. It's unprofessional. Don't talk about "jobbers" and "heels" in your roleplays and in-rings. We don't like it, and if you think it is cool, we'll kindly show you the door.
3. There will be a role play limit for all cards and events. What this basically means is, we don't want anymore then a certain amount of flashes from you for one perticular matchup. This will keep people that are strong RPers that just don't have a lot of time from being decimated by those that choose to sit and role play constantly.
4. Never use another wrestler in your role play without his/her permission. 5. As far as role play length goes. Keep it neat. Quality of quantity is a good one, right? I don't want to see any four email stories going on. Keep them concise and to the point. This is of course to say that you should also put as much detail and creativity as possible, just don't go Tom Clancy on us..
we're judging flashes not novels. 6. The World Wrestling Federation, WCW, ECW, "Stone Cold" Steve Austin, Bret Hart, and all the rest do NOT exsist and as such should not be mentioned in your flashes. 7. Swearing is fine, just don't get too carried away. - [Inring Rules] This'll be simple.. cause there are NONE! Every standard First Blood Wrestling match is no DQ and no count out, no questions ask. Each wrestler participating need not worry about having the bell called because he brought a steel chair to the ring, cause cracking a chair over your oppenent's skull is as perfectly legal as a suplex.
III. Behind the Scenes - [The Staff] FBW President and Owner: Mr. Jamerson Deal. Mr. Deal has been on all sides of the wrestling industry. From his short stint as a pro wrestler to his more famed days as the manager of some of the greatest tag teams in PWA history.
But now he takes on a different task, a federation's head honcho. Well on the brightside, Mr. Deal just happens to be one of the most business savvy men out there. He knows just what the fans want and he plans on giving it to them and then some. Not exactly what most would consider a "fair man", Mr. Deal expects quite a bit from his superstars and will put his men through some of the most grueling brawls of their life all for the ratings. He's got his mind set.. one day, FBW is going to be a household name.
Commissioner: Jack Holiday. Interns and Interviewers: - Kayla Reynolds - Jeff Akkins - Peter Lawson Commentators: - Bobby Lane - Johnny Midas IV. Television and Live Events President Jamerson Deal has said to be in negotions with east coast access channel Triple X Entertainment.. whether or not this will lead to FBW getting on the air has yet to be seen, but as of right now it's looking pretty good.
- [Saturday Night Hardcore Hangover] Every Saturday night First Blood Wrestling packs in the demented extremist wrestling fans all along the east coast to their most violent of showings.
Though FBW events are held nearly every night, Hardcore Hangover is taped and sold at over live shows and over the internet. It's here where you can expect to see not only all of the big name FBW superstars, but some huge matchups as well. Normally 4-6 matches are scheduled, which leaves room for plenty of fights to just break out in total chaos.
- [In YOUR Face] Though not in practice just yet, if FBW does indeed get it's contract to be on
TV, In Your Face plans to air every Wednesday. It'll be more or less of a recap show.. showing what went down at the latest Hardcore Hangover or houseshows. A few interviews may be taped for In Your Face, as well as up to date news and rumors.
- [The Grinder] The Grinder Report is First Blood Wrestling Bi monthly newsletter. The Grinder features all sorts of interesting articles from the brainchilds in FBW.
Interviews, special news and rumors, upcoming events, and other goodies are all included. - [Supercards] Though obviously not large enough to hold pay per views, First Blood Wrestling will occasionally hype up a big time event, or supercard. Held probably every other month or so, these supercards will feature insane matchups, title defenses, and all sorts of grueling battles between First Blood superstars.
V. Titles and Crowns - [Championship Belts] FBW North Atlantic Heavyweight Title: The grand daddy of them all. The First
Blood North Atlantic Title is THE coveted strap here. It's the one that all of the superstars are vying for. But don't come in mistaken, it'll take one VERY impressive superstar to ever capture the title.. but it'll taken an even more impressive athelete to hold onto it. Especially since according to President
Deal the champion will be defending it as much as every single card! Talk about a fighting champ.
FBW Tri-State Title: The Tri-State Title isn't exactly of the same caliber as the North Atlantic, but nonetheless it should be treated with great respect.
This title will, if you can even imagine it, be defended more so that it's father title and will be put up for grabs in some of the more wild types of matches. Don't even begin to think that this one is for the rookies.
(titles in consideration) FBW Lightheavyweight Title and FBW Tag Team Titles - [Tournaments and Crowns] Being a rather big fan of wrestling over seas, President Deal has been caught saying that he plans on holding plenty of big time tournaments. Though no one is sure exactly how many and how often, it can safely be said that we could be witnessing a few King of Deathmatch Tournaments now and again.
