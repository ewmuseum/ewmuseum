N.E.W. Roleplay: Claire Maynard and Pete Sheridan

30 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
All opinions/flames/whatever welcome. Thanks, James [A hotel room in wherever the card’s taking place. Claire Maynard, back to being blonde after experimenting with hair dye JUST so that she’s been the sexiest brunette and best looking redhead you’ll ever see, is dressed in a black jeans and white blouse combo, which isn’t particularly flattering – that’s to say, only 99.9% of the worthless schmucks who make up NEW’s audience would crawl over hot coals for just a smile from her when she’s dressed like this. It is, however, a damn sight better than Pete Sheridan’s current costume, of just a pair of speedos.] PS: Claire… can’t I put some clothes on… CM: Come on, Pete… how you gonna toughen up if you’re scared of the frigging cold… besides, you can go somewhere nice and warm once you’ve actually cut a decent promo here. PS: Really? CM: Sure… now do it! PS: Okay… hi everyone, I’m the ‘Sex Pistol’ Pete Sheridan, and I’m really excited to be here in NEW! I can’t wait to take on guys of the caliber of my opponents in my first match… [Scowling, Claire storms forward and taps him on the shoulder. As he turns around, she launches forward with a vicious headbutt, sending him stumbling to the floor. As he starts to climb to his feet, a tooth falls out and we see a trickle of blood coming out of his mouth. Bewildered, he looks up pleadingly.] PS: Claire! [His only answer is a kick in the mouth from her high heeled boots, dislodging another tooth.] CM: Listen, Pete. Your opponents this week are of a similar caliber to those tramps who pollute the air outside the shops in Exeter at night. To take men like that on, you wouldn’t HAVE to wait. You could just stroll down a main street in any city in the world, and offer a buck to anyone who’d fight you. [She glares at him.] CM: Shona may have been happy for you to say cute stuff like that, so she could correct you, get the lion’s share of TV time, and feed her pre-pubescent dreams of stardom. I am NOT. I’m as big a star as this joint has got anyway, all I want to do is train you up into something resembling a competent wrestler, and sit back and spend my 90%. Now insult someone. [Quivering, and not just from the cold, Sheri starts to talk.] PS: Ummm… Vik Ventressa, didn’t you used to be in RAW? I seem to remember you weren’t very good… but then again, you were the champion, so I guess you were actually pretty damn great, and my memory’s the problem… [Grabbing Pete’s long hair from behind, Claire pulls him to his feet.] CM: Alright, Pete… time to do this the hard way. On your knees. [Looking at her with a puzzled expression, Pete slowly – too slowly – starts to obey her command. A knee driven hard into his crotch speeds him up.] CM: Put the index finger of your right hand in that drawer. [Even more bemused, Pete does so. Claire smiles approvingly, half turns away… … and then swivels back, hitting a lightning fast kick on the drawer to slam it shut.] <CRACK> [As Pete screams in pain, Claire smiles, looking happy for the first time in this dialogue after she hears the bone break.] CM: I guess I’ll have to do this one, then. Beecher, other guy, I know nothing about you and care less. Vikky V, on the other hand… Vik, are you watching now? Do you see Pete there rolling around in agony? Can you imagine the pain a wuss like him feels now? If you can’t, here’s a way to work it out. It’s about one hundredth of the torture I went through EVERY TIME I had to watch your talentless, charismaless, generally useless, ass attempt to cut a promo. It’s about one thousandth of the suffering I felt every time I saw that nauseatingly hideous wretch of a wife of yours give an interview. And it’s about one millionth of the torment my heart endured every time I saw you holding a belt that men like Jaime Roberts, Mike Masters, and the Andrazoons would never even get a shot at. In case you haven’t worked it out yet, Vik, in those few moments where I’ve wasted even a thought on that decaying carcass you laughably call a body, I’ve come to the conclusion that I despise you. Not because I envy you… because there’s nothing about you that could ever inspire jealousy. Not because I lust after you… sorry, chump, but while I know you’ll try and claim that hate is the flip side of love, and I’ve been carrying a torch for you all this time, the truth is I’d rather screw a rabid porcupine. But for the best reason of all for hate. I loathe you because you represent EVERYTHING that is wrong with this business today. Because you’ve already had opportunities far and beyond those which could be expected by even men of twice your talent. And because, in NEW, you somehow get to start on a level footing with that man over there, who, pathetic though he is, has achieved more than you ever could, simply by being managed by me. That’s why I hate you, Vik. And that’s why I’m making utterly, totally, and completely sure that you don’t have a cat in hell’s chance of going past the first round of this little tournament. Oh, of course, there IS another reason. Because unlike the other losers here in NEW, I’m good enough to do whatever the hell I want. Pete, bring me the handcuffs… PS: But Claaaaaaaaire… [A kick to the cheek stops the wretch from snivelling any more, and he quickly goes out of the room, then returns with a pair of steel handcuffs. Snapping one cuff shut on his wrist, she attaches the other to a table leg.] CM: Night, Pete. Learn to cut a goddamn promo, and I MIGHT let you loose. [As she walks out of the room, slamming the door shut behind her, we… Fade to black.]
