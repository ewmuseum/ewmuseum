N.E.W. On the Spot

Dark room. Folding chair in the center. There's a big guy sitting in the chair. Broad shoulders. Paunch. Black mask. His posture's bad. He cringes with each drawn breath. All the details are right. The pain is real. This is.... (And the caption at the bottom of the screen merely confirms it) EDDY JACKS, NEW SUPERSTAR.] EJ : Shouldn't a' been that tough. But it was. O'Lachlan hurt me. Hurt me bad. [He groans.] EJ : Yesterday, I went out ta buy milk...I'd run out...an' I heard somethin' pop in my back. Couldn't stand up straight fer the next 24 hours. Even now, still hurts.
Tell-tale signs yer gettin' old. Tell-tale signs that ya need ta find a quieter line o' work.
[Jacks sighs.] EJ : I'm not bitter. I'm not worried. I just know...know it in my bones, mebbe, if not in my heart...that I ain't got much longer.
Shit, ya know who I sound like? Lefthanded Lane. Lane an' I went way back, back in the 70's, when we feuded in the mid-south. He was old even then, an' dyin' a little wit' each match, so's, when we went ta work, I always tried ta take it easy on him, outta respect.
[Jacks' voice is alternately sad and bitter. It's hard to hear him...the mask muffles his voice...he's not a particularly loud person...]
EJ : One time, after a really tough card, I comes up ta Lane, an' says, Link-'cuz that was his real name, Link, his momma didn't name him Lefthanded-I says, Link, why ya still wrestlin'? Yer gonna die in the ring, fer chrissakes.
An' he looks at me, an' says, 'Eddy, if I quit wrestlin'-if I turn my back on alla this, if I ferget where I came from, what got me where I was...I may as well be dead.'
Link died six years later, in a sanitarium on the outskirts o' St. Louis. Wasn't there, mind ya, but a friend tol' me what the last words outta his mouth were....
[Long pause.] EJ : 'Get yer friggin' hands offa me, ya stinkin' animals.' 'Cept replace friggin' an' stinkin' wit' more...profound oaths, an' yet get the drift. [Defiantly.] EJ : That's why I'm wearin' this mask. Ta remind me ta never quit. This mask don't come off 'til I'm dead. Mebbe not even then. [Jacks scratches his chin.] EJ : This week, it's one o' my own. Tyrone Parker. We're in the same stable. Share the same lockers. Parker's a hard worker; I respect him. But that don't mean shit or two friggin' dimes in a wrestlin' ring. [Jacks spits.] EJ : Camp Baltimore. United it stands. Indivisible. In the visible. Parker's a worker. He's earned his nickname. He's every bit the man I am, mebbe more. [Another pause.] EJ : Only one difference 'tween me an' him. _ Big difference _. I'm old, half-crazy. Got nothin' ta live fer, everythin' ta gain by pickin' him apart, bone by friggin' bone.
[Jacks shakes his head.] EJ : Parker's one o' those new wrestlers. Approaches each match like it's a mudhole, like ya can just leap around, doin' yer plankachatas an' yer hurrikamama's an' what have ya.
Ain't the way ta go. Y'see, way I learned ta wrestle.... Way da Black Bruiser wrestled, way Lefthanded Lane wrestled.... The ring ain't a mudhole. It's an operatin' table. [Somehow...we don't know how...we can tell Jacks is sneering beneath the mask. There's some cockiness left, yet, in the old boy.]
EJ : An' I'm the surgeon. [STILL another pause.] EJ : There's a distinct friggin' possibility I could die in the ring. Ain't a problem, nossir. Kids at home are in fer a show, either way. An' Parker...get a good night's sleep. Need ya at yer best. 'Cuz, whereas ya may _ be _ a member o' Camp Baltimore... Yer, as o' this moment in time, a _ marked _ man. [Jacks chuckles.] EJ : Check that. Yer a _ dead _ man. VO : At the rebirth of N.E.W., two men that would eventually rise to stardom in the compay did battle. Jack Cody and Madison J.
Valentine were two particpants in a steel cage battle. [The static dissolves into a heading that reads "Saturday Night Shockwaves: 7-1-00". CW : I TOLD YOU, LANE, I TOLD YOU! BL : I cannot believe this! OHHHHHH LORDY!! [Ding! Ding! Ding!] AC : Here is the winner of this match, due to being the first man to touch his feet to the floor.... ....."SMOKING BARREL" MADISON....J..... VALENTINE!!!! [The crowd is still cheering their brains out for the cage breaking, and Kingsley Sweete quickly pulls Valentine to his feet, and the duo quickly dash up the aisle and into the back. The screen then freezes, staying on the image of Madison J. Valentine raising his arm to the sky in victory. The voice over then continues the narration:]
VO : The second time that Cody and Valentine met, they once more were not the only men involved, but there was certainty that their paths would cross once more. [The scene then cuts to a heading that reads "Saturday Night Shockwaves: 8-5-00". From that heading, we go straight to the footage: In the ring,
Cody gets back up to his feet, but as he pulls up Valentine by the hair, he feels a quick thumb jabbing his eye, which sends Cody stumbling back, not seeing the figure on the apron.
As the ref turns to admonish Valentine, Chris Kingsworth swings back behind Jack Cody... *DING!* ..And clocks him in the back of the head with the ring bell! HEEL
POP!] CW : GENIUS!!! BL : COWARD!!! [Valentine pushes the ref aside and dashes forward, hooking Cody for = an inside cradle.. ONE! Cody's legs begin to twitch a little. TWO! Some more movement builds up... THREE? THREE! Just not enough.] BL : DAMMIT! CW : YOU GO, BOY!" [The screen pauses this time on the downed body of Jack Cody, as the voice over continues:]
VO : For the second time, Jack Cody was slighted a win against Mad
Valentine. But this week... this week, they do battle for a third time. And this week, there is much more on the line.
[Images begin to flash across the screen, showing highlights of both
Mad Valentine and Jack Cody.]
VO : Madison looks to make it a perfect 3-0, Cody seeks redemption. Who will the third time be the charm for? But in the end, they both have their eyes set on the same thing... the N.E.W. Rising Star
Championship!
[Fade to black.] [SCENE: We open back up to the N.E.W. Asylum, this time in the high,
"nosebleed" seats. Seated high up toward the roof, is none other than
Jack Cody. Cody wears blue jeans and a black "N.E.W." t-shirt, and just sits there looking down toward the ring, before turning his attention to the camera.]
JC : I'll tell ya, this is gonna be the place to be tomorrow night. 'Cause it ain't long 'til finally, a champion is gonna be crowned in N.E.W. A feat that only few have been given the chance to achieve, and a feat which even fewer have accomplished. And now, one more man'll be added to the list of greats.
The tournament has wound it's way down and now two men are left standing. Two men, have overcome all that've stepped in their way, and are now on a collision course straight toward each other.
Madison J. Valentine, and your's truly. [Jack pauses for a moment, to start down toward the ring again, before turning his attention back toward the camera.]
JC : Yeah, Valentine, me and you one more time. All I can say is that its 'bout time. 'Bout time that its _only_ me and you in the ring. This time, there ain't gonna be a third factor to give ya the edge. This time, MJ, there ain't a steel cage that can fall apart so ya escape with the victory. This time yer little sidekick of a manager, ain't gonna get the chance to swing a bell and clock me in the side of the head.
Nope, this time yer gonna have to rely on somethin' that ya ain't tried to show in quite sometime: yer wrestlin' skills. Yer gonna have to prove to not only me, but to the whole world that yer a wrestler that has enough skill to wear the gold. Yer gonna have to show that you are in fact, one of the top wrestlers that this promotion has to offer. And if ya can beat me in the square circle, fair and square, I'll be the first to tip my hat to ya.
But there'll be no cheatin', no shortcuts, no easy way out. Ya've got one path leadin' ya to that Risin' Star Championship, and that's straight through me.
[Jack pauses once more, perhaps collecting his thoughts.] JC : I know that I've said that I don't care fer championships, that it makes no difference to me whether or not I hold a title. And all of that still holds true. But fuel's been added to the fire.
There's more to it than me either walkin' out as a champion, or me not walkin' out as a champion. Now it's either _I_ walk out as the Risin' Star Champion, or _Valentine_ walks out as the
Risin' Star Champion. And that leaves me with very little choice. Now I _have_ to walk out with that belt 'round my waist. Not 'cause I want the glory. Not 'cause I want my name goin' down in the record books. But because I don't want Madison J. Valentine bein' the first man to hold a title in this rebirth of N.E.W. I don't want the world thinkin' that people like Valentine are gonna be able to run rampant all through the wrestlin' rings. And most of all, I don't want Madison J. Valentine representin' the fans of this company by wearin' _their_ belt.
[Cody rises and is now looking down into the lense of the camera.] JC : Congratulations, Valentine, ya did somethin' nobody else has been able to do... make me care 'bout a title belt. That may be the only positive thing ya'll be able to take out of our match.
I'm walkin' out not only as the Risin' Star Champion... but as these fan's champion. [The camera slowly fades into snow, as it does the screen flickers for amere second then clips from last weeks show flash on the screen. Clips of Nothingface being driven to the floor with a painkiller are shown, over and over again. Then as the camera slowly fades back out an image of Stevens with his face painted in Nothingfaces blood is shown..]
VO : Christian Madison, you are next on my list, enjoy what little time you have left.... [Fade in] [You notice nothing in particular about the room that Rick Stevens stands in, the walls are plain, the floors plain and clear of any items.
The only thing that stands out in the room is a single candle, burning softly in the far left corner. The candle casts an erie glow onto the room, causing the flames to dance off the walls in various patterns.
In the center of the room sits Rick, Rick rocks back n' forth slowly, his hair covers most of his face and half of his face is painted black.
Dressed in jean shorts, no shirt and black boots. Rick begins to speak] RS : Nothingface is no more, the demon is gone. Yet demons still haunt me to this day. This week I will face Christian Madison, will this end thesenightmares?
One can only hope so... But I won't know until I taste your blood, Nothingface wasn't the one.. That would have been too easy [Rick stands, his hair covers all of his face now, leaving very little to see] RS : Madison, I won't promise you a world of pain, or will I promise I will beat you 1. 2.. 3...
Arrogance has never been my style... Instead, I will show you the way to truly enjoy all that my misery has to offer. And you, like the others will soon realize, just how vicious reality can be. Over the years I have come to realize one thing, and that is that life is what you make out of it, for some life is a glorious thing, and for some it is overrated, and they wish to die. Which do you fit into Madison?
Do you want achieve for the best, yet always get kicked in the teeth when you go to grasp that brass ring? Or are you among the smart ones, who wait for all the others to be kicked down, then you step on every last one of the, using them to raise yourself up to the next level.
Come Sunday, we will find out.. It's Madison J's rented studio apartment, 'The Moorings', in the quiet little town of Inverness, Florida. It's recently become his home away from home, some digs a l'il closer to the NEW Asylum than his permanent residence, back in Sacramento. And, for a second home, it's not bad at all... neat-o, shiny decking floor, a coupla leather armchairs, erm... the odd flash of glass and chrome tubing, here and there.]
[And, of course - how could we forget? - the man himself, over by the window. I'm tellin' ya'... it's a spectacular evening out there, bathing him in an orangey, twilighty hue. All very atmospheric, you understand.
We can't really get a good look from the back, but we can see he's barefoot, dressed in grey, dress slacks and a non-tucked black shirt.
Presumably, he's just chillin' after some important meeting, or sum'n, I dunno. Anyway, he's uncharacteristically thoughtful as he, um.. pretty much just stands there. Not the epitome of showmanship we've become used to.]
[Oh no, scratch that. He turns to the camera, and grins, F'N huge, like.] MJ : Welcome back! [He spins around on his heel, making a wee bit too much of a show of it and almost losing his balance in the process. He manages to cover it up pretty well, though, as he paces over towards us. From the front, we can see he's wearing a tie... as you might expect, in a deep, move colour.
_Shaaarp_.] MJ : And once again, the cameras of NEW are invited into 'Chez MJ'. [Gurrr-in!] MJ : And for the slightly more, erm... _insane_, among you, my ever-loving 'l'il Valentines', for those who've deluded themselves into thinking that they're actually my best friend, or my beloved spouse, or whatever... those people might be interested to know that I'm settlin' in just fine, here.
[He nods, now stood right in front of the camera, which looks up at him from a low angle. He actually requested he be filmed like that, to make him look all big and strong and mighty. Now _that_ is an ego, peeps.]
MJ : Yuh-huh. The neighbours are just sort of getting used to having a megastar living next door, y'know? The local kids still point and gawk every time I step outside, but... eh, I'm a man of the people. No problem.
[He chuckles to himself...] MJ : Actually... they're an easy source of money. [The pause allows the chuckle time to escalate into full-on laughter.] MJ : You wouldn't believe this kid today... he gave me five dollars - yes, five, whole dollars - simply for the privilege of carrying
_my_, sacred garbage out. Ha! He's paying me ten dollars to wash my car tomorrow!
[God... you're such a heartless bastard, Valentine! Look at you, smirking away, fighting back another fit of giggles! Don't you realise what you're doing is, um... erm... exploitation, or sum'n? Eh... guess not.]
MJ : Anyway, anyway... enough with the small talk, uh? [He allows himself a last, little chuckle, and digresses.] MJ : There's something I wanna show you boys. Come on, over here... [He reaches down to us, the peons, grabs the front of the camera, and brings it up to an equal height, at last. We spend a split-second admiring his glossy, black hair, as he leads it around, across his apartment.]
[A heeeee_youge_ pile of boxes and assorted crap flits briefly, and accidentally, into, and quickly out of, our field of vision. The sound of triple-footfall on the decking brings us to a door, after a coupla seconds of slightly disorientating cross-room travel.]
MJ : Heeeere we are. [He pushes the camera up, and it focuses on a picture frame. It's pretty plain, just straight, black, your standard quadrilateral shape...
nothing special. Not in the least. Especially since there's no picture in it.] MJ : [off-screen] See that? [Erm... yep. It's a picture frame. Pretty plain, just straight, black, your standard quadrila-- ah, eff it. Yes, we see it, MJ. Now get to the point.]
MJ : You know what that's for? [Oh, for chrissakes, just hurry it up a little!] MJ : That's where I'm gonna put my victory photo. [If we could see him now, you'd bet your ass he'd be smirking his off.] MJ : See, I like to keep records of all my great victories. I like to look back at photos, reports, whatver - and remember some of the many titles I've won, some of the many foes who I've battled and, erm... won.
Against. [A pause, a nostalgic sigh.] MJ : And here's where I'm gonna put the next one. In fact, I even thought about taking a trip down to NEW HQ this week to get the photo taken. It might be nice, y'know? Some proper lighting, get old Ange to do my hair all nice, maybe even a touch of the Max
Factor... bam! One, great piccy.
[And... another pause.] MJ : Then I realised I'd rather have the real thing. After all.. I _hate_ phonies, fakes... stuff like that. [He grabs the camera, pulls it round so it's looking him dead in the face.] MJ : Stuff... like _you_, Jack Co-dee-i-ee-i-o. [Now, he turns away, heads back over to the couch. Boy - he's really all over the place tonight, ain't he? Let's attribute it to caffiene, or sum'n. Anyway, the camera stays put, as he slumps into one of his armchairs.]
MJ : Oh, I hear ya', Jacky. "A fake? Me, Jack Co-day, a fraud?" [He looks at the camera with, uh... indifferent scorn. I think.] MJ : Yeah, Jack, you're a fraud, a phoney, a con-man. Seems you've fooled a lot of folk... but not me, buddy-boy. I mean, you run around, doing your little 'People's Champion' act, like you've got their interests at heart.
[He shakes his head, does a l'il *tut* thing...] MJ : The fact you've tried - and failed, miserably - to turn my fans against me is bad enough. The fact you tried to win them over with that 'lovable loser' thing you do... well, it's almost amusing. But when you claim to be a man of the people, only to lose your rag and cheap-shot moi, attack from behind, simply because you couldn't face up to losing to me... _again_...
[Even from over here... we can _feel_ the smugness. It's almost tangible.] MJ : ... well, that's just bang outta line, Amigo. See, when you were learning the basic ins-and-outs of the 'big boot' at your l'il redneck rasslin' class, I was the face on a million kids'
t-shirts. When you pulled off your first body slam, Jack, I was the face on a million bedroom walls.
[He smirks, makes a big show of stretching in his chair.] MJ : The point I'm trying to make, Ace, is that I'll always be the darling of the fans, whatever you try to take them away from me.
And, even though I've got bigger fish to fry - i.e. - the 'Scam-dee-man' and one World Title - I'm gonna make a point of making it a hat-trick of un-deux-trois over you, and making sure you never, ever, ever wear the Rising Star gold.
[A pause. He looks kinda serious for a fraction of a split-second. Then he thinks better of it, goes back to his old 'mischievous' demeanour, and smirks, his eyebrows dancing the F'N lambada on his forehead.]
MJ : So, I guess I'm about done. And Jack? [A pause, more chuckling.] MJ : I'll see ya' in the ring, chuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuuum......_p_. [Oh boy.
