"Courtesy NRG Archives"]
VO : They were the worst of enemies. [FILE FOOTAGE: Jacks and the Strangler double-clotheslining a stunned
Corey Atari.] VO : They were the best of friends. [FILE FOOTAGE: Jacks caving in the Strangler's knee with a steel chair.] VO : Somewhere in between, 'midst the blown spots and broken bones, the spirit of friendly competition disappeared.
[FILE FOOTAGE: The Strangler tracing a crimson streak in Jacks' prominent forehead with a rusty fork.] VO : All that was left was the _ hate _. [We segue to... SCENE: Darkened room. Three white walls, 'NEW' backdrop on the far wall--in short, your standard interview room.
Jacks is seated on a metal folding chair--hell, he spends half his life seated on metal folding chairs--looking very Jacks-ish.
In other words, he appears old, worn, and dangerous. His left eye doesn't move (why should it? it's glass, fer cryin' out loud!), his right index finger is missing, there's a scar the size of the San Andreas Fault running diagonally across his brow.
And, just in case you're wondering, he's wearing an oh-so-tight "OLD AS
F***, MEAN AS HELL" t-shirt, and faded black jeans.
Talk about a picture of health!] EJ : Ten years ago, kid, I loved ya like I loved m' friggin' mother. We did _ everythin' _ together. We was _ inseparable _. [Pause.] EJ : Hell, I named my second kid after yer wife. I named my pet bulldog 'Strangler.' [Shakes head.] EJ : Ya got greedy. Ya started playin' dirty. Ya did 'is... [Taps the scar along his brow.] EJ : An' thought ya could get away wit' it. Ya _ didn't _. [Sad, knowing smile.] EJ : Shattered yer friggin' knee, I did. Tore the cartilage. Damn near ended yer career. [Long sigh.] EJ : Ya re'peared a few weeks ago, wit' a bang, burnin' m' face. Third-degree burns. Lotta puss, lotta loose skin. Not fun. [Looks away from the camera.] EJ : Ya wuz like a kid brother ta me, Strangy, really ya wuz. [Huge 'pop' as Jacks snaps his left shoulder into place.] EJ : Ain't gonna stop me from 'avin' yer bleedin' yer guts for garters, an' usin' the extras ta choke the life outta that
snake-tongued semi-faggot, Baltimore. [Long-a$$ pause.] EJ : Should'na come ta this. 'Course, at this juncture, it don't make a damn bit a' difference. Ya could be Jesus H. Christ, fer what's it worth, an' I'd still cram m' fist down yer friggin'
gob. [Beat, then defiant.] EJ : Yer a marked man, Strangler. [I smell a catchphrase...] EJ : Check dat. Yer a _ dead _ one. BLACK ------------------------------------------------------------------------- _______________________________________________________________________ |_______________________________________________________________________| _____ _____ _ _ _ | | | | __| | | | | Mr. Black | | | |_ | __|_ | | | |_ |_|___|_| |_____|_| |_____|_| Promoted by: Jeremy Baltimore _______________________________________________________________________ |_______________________________________________________________________| [Fade in on a sidewalk in a middle class neighborhood. The only lighting comes from porch lights and glowing jack o' lanterns at houses along the street... and orange flashlights being waved by hordes of costumed children. Amid the trick-or-treating mirth of the midget vampires, ghosts, and witches (and omigod, some fool dressed his kid like Tinky
Winky!) walks a large figure dressed entirely in black. He strolls the sidewalk like a massive dark shadow. N.E.W. viewers would expect nothing less from Mr. Black.]
MR.B : All Hallow's Eve... the one night when a man like me can walk the streets in peace. And it is a night that so parallels the
N.E.W. Devon Case, Chris Courtade, Eddy Jacks... they have all gotten too comfortable. They knock on a door, are handed a piece of candy, and walk away happy. It's all they want from life.
[Mr. Black pauses and watches a small group of trick-or-treaters opening their bags at a nearby house. Shaking his masked head, he continues.]
MR.B : Case learned the hard way that Jeremy Baltimore's house doesn't hand out candy. Trick! And Courtade should be very careful or he's going to lose his treat to the big, bad boogie man. Mr.
Black may just take that gold candy from the baby. As for Jacks? He's the fat kid who sits in the corner eating candy until he pukes all over himself. I won't even waste my time on him.
[As a pack of rugrats approaches, Mr. Black stands in the middle of the sidewalk. The kids detour into the street and Mr. Black looks over his shoulder, seemingly disappointed that there wasn't an oncoming car.]
MR.B : Courtade, the great champion, is already feeling the pain of living in Mr. Black's shadow. And Case continues to hide behind his promoter. A real man would come out and face me regardless of what his keeper says... instead of trying to injure a man like
Jeremy Baltimore. _I_ put you in the hospital, Case, and now you are unable to live with the thought of facing me again.
[Mr. Black lets out a sarcastic sympathetic laugh.] MR.B : Fools like Courtade and Case are making this entirely too easy. [A boy's voice is heard off camera.] BOY : Hey look, it's a guy dressed like Mr. Black! [A group of three trick-or-treaters approaches Mr. Black, who crosses his arms and stares at the yard monkeys. The lead kid is wearing a black outfit with blue stars and the N.E.W. logo on the center of the chest.
A cheap plastic mask that bears a fair resemblance to a certain "Carolina
Crusher" completes his costume. Standing waist-high to Mr. Black, the half-pint points his index finger at the wrestler.]
BOY : I'm Jack Cody and I can beat you up, Mr. Black! [As the boy's friends giggle, Mr. Black leans down until his masked face is inches from the boy's. He growls at the lad.]
MR.B : Kid... I'll [BLEEP] you up so bad even your [BLEEP]ing whore momma won't recognize your [BLEEP]ing remains. Then I'll take your [BLEEP]ing leg bone and [BLEEP] her with it!
[Quickly realizing that he may have made a mistake, the kid drops his bag of candy and the group runs screaming in the opposite direction. After watching with satisfaction for a few seconds, Mr. Black calmly bends over and picks up a spilled Baby Ruth bar.]
MR.B : Take away the chocolate... take away the caramel... and it does remind me Jack Cody -- a little bag of nuts.
[Mr. Black tosses the candy bar over his shoulder and continues his evening stroll. Fade.]

Some are dancing, others simply stand lonely to the side, while still others try and make small talk. The thing that makes this place stick out from your run-of-the-mill gathering is that all the people are dressed in costumes. A costume party. The camera begins to move about the room, weaving through the inhabitants, and finally coming out of the mass of humanity. The camera ends up on the "outskirts" of the establishment, outside the circle of people, staring straight toward Jack
Cody. Cody stands out not only because of his large size, but also because he is possibly the only person in the room that isn't draped in a costume. Around Cody are some more partiers, all dressed in some rather
odd looking outfits. Cody, in the usual blue jeans and t-shirt, is looking from side to side at the happenings of the event. Finally, he turns back and motions for one of men dressed in a creepy looking costume to come over toward him. The man does so as Cody begins.]
JC : Lately, I've started seein' stuff differently... noticin' things that weren't so clear to me before. And the most glarin'ly obvious is, things ain't always as they seem.
Take a look at this fella here... [The camera turns toward the man that Cody motioned to earlier and reveals his costume in more detail. The man is draped by a large black overcoat, virtually cover his entire upper and lower body. Covering his face is a dark mask, leaving only openings for his eyes and mouth. In the openings around his eyes are white circles, obviously artificually put there. On top of all the clothing, the man is pretty well built and big.]
JC : When ya look at somebody like this ya think 'bout how he must be. Ya look at his size, and think how he really ain't somebody that ya wanna tangle with. Ya take a look at the dark clothes, and it makes ya think o' somebody that goes through life a little differently than most. And when ya see a man coverin' his face with make up and other means, ya instantly think that he's deranged. But if ya dig deeper and look a bit closer, ya might be surprised at what ya find.
If ya take away the disguising coat... [Right after the comment, Cody reaches over and pulls the coat off the shoulders of the man, revealing him to be wearing average blue jeans and white t-shirt.]
JC : If ya pull off the black mask that hides his face... [Cody reaches up and and pulls the mask off of the man's head and reveals some of his facial features and a flowing head of blonde hair.]
JC : And wipe away that paint and make-up... [After this comment the man himself reaches up and wipes away the white circles of paint. The actual man himself now stands before the camera, showing him to look like your normal man off of any street around the country. In fact, he looks a bit timid.]
JC : When ya take away all the extra additives, you'll see exactly what's underneath. A man... no more, no less. Not some larger than life character, not somebody that is immortal, and certainly not somebody that's got the power of a Greek god. A man that has many strengths, but also has limitations. In short, its somebody that is no different than myself.
[Pause.] JC : And that's all ya are, Mr. Black. Nothing more than a man tryin' to simply make himself look bigger than he really is.
Ya put on yer dark coat, yer dark boots, and yer matchin' black mask and make people think that yer some unreal human bein'.
After hittin' one man with one o' most heinous things I've ever seen done in the sport, ever'body thinks that ya've got some kinda super-human power.
But I'm not so easily fooled. Because some time, the truth's gonna come out. Sooner or later, the black coat that you wear will fall. The black mask will be torn from yer face. Then you'll be exposed fer the mere _man_ that ya are. And when this happens, I'm gonna be there to show just what yer limitations are.

HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPION ------------------------------------------------------------------------- _______________________________________________________________________ |_______________________________________________________________________| _____ _____ _ _ _ | | | | __| | | | | "Cold Blooded" Chris Courtade - N.E.W. | | | |_ | __|_ | | | |_ Heavyweight Champion |_|___|_| |_____|_| |_____|_| Promoted by: Steve Miller _______________________________________________________________________ |_______________________________________________________________________| [Camera fade to black] [The warm Texas sun shines upon the camera lens, as it spans out, panoramically to the left and right. As the camera turns to it's right, the etchings of a small, worn down, stone church can be seen. The cracks in the stone, the worn out wood panelings on the windows, and the lack of a roof, more then likely torn away years ago show the true age of the structure. Nothing truly alive grows near this structure, and thus the irony of it all becomes more apparent. Such a once beautiful structure, now, much like it's surroundings, dead to the world. And then slowly, entering from the left, hell comes to ruin a once heavenly structure, just a little more.
His brown, ostrich boots, dusty from the Texas prarie winds click along the dried out, brittle ground. He wears faded blue jeans, snug, dirty from the winds as well. He wears a T-shirt, black, reading simply "HELL'S
KEEPER", and he walks near a dried up water well, and sits down beside it, legs slightly bent, hands in front of him. His head is lowered, blonde matted locks against his forehead, and he slowly raises his head, and focuses on the camera. For he is all that is wrong, for he is the prophecy of doom, and your own personal demise. For he is, N.E.W. Heavyweight
Champion, "Cold Blooded" Chris Courtade]
CC : I have never claimed to be a religous man, nor will I ever claim to be. I believe in what I believe in, fate being one of them. I believe that individuals are pre-destined to have a role in their life, and that role is accordingly, their "duty". No matter what you role is, no matter what impact it plays in life, it's still
_your_ role in life. If you were to say, lay brick, sweep the city streets, become a professional pedophile like, say, Rick Styles, it matters not.
It's still _your_ role in this world. So, I did some entrospective thinking recently. I came to the conclusion that I had a role, and I found what my role is in this life of mine. I looked back upon my life, my career, and where I stand now, and it all became clear. I looked upon this company, and what my role is within it, and _that_ to, became clear.
I am here to make you all my personal bitch. [Courtade slowly gets to his feet, dusting off himself] CC : You see, last week, Baltimore thought that he played himself into the winning hand. He thought that by attacking my lone weakness in this company, my loyality to Case, that he would somehow "get to me". For weeks, he has tried to..."get to me".
Using his pawns like sheep, leading them unwillingly into the slaughter. Using Mr. Black, and yet another brilliant marketing name, "The Strangler" into battles against men like myself. Yet, in time, he too will be...
My bitch. Baltimore, what you started last week, by rubbing your little pompous nose in the pain and agony of my friend is something that you never, _ever_ wanted to start. For weeks, I was driven by the fans, and their mockingly joyous attitude towards seeing Devon being wheeled out on a stretcher. However, with those two striking blows to his back, I felt a change, I felt like it all became clear. It's not them that I shall blame any longer, for it's you.
It's your utter disrespect for human life that I shall hate. It's your contempting little smirks, and smart ass little mouth that I shall blame for my actions. Because Baltimore...
You just pissed off the wrong man. [Courtade spits to his right, and grits his teeth] CC : And by pissing off the wrong man, you yet again, are throwing another sheep to the slaughter. This week, this time, it's no elderly disciple of some heroic retoric. This time, it's actually someone who has a black soul like myself. This time, it's actually someone that just might throw a touch of a fight my way.
But the results will be the same. Steven Bourdo, for months, you have ran around N.E.W. and you have been running people off. You serve as a slave, a true, [bleep]in'
slave to your own personal "massah". You do as your told, you do as your instructed, without a second thought as to what you're doing. You have run out, and even got yourself a catchy little name..."Career Killah" with some [bleep]'d up little sign on the end of it. Congratulations, you might sell a few T-shirts along the way, but here's a bit of news to you.
I have shitted more talented pieces of waste then you have claimed to "end". So, that little game won't do a damn thing to me. I _don't_ fear your punk ass, and I doubt I ever will. Your smug little
"wanna-be" bad ass doesn't unearth thoughts of fright in these eyes boy. [Courtade points to his eyes] All it does is make me wanna prove that you're nothing but a myth.
[Courtade slowly walks foward, and the camera drops to a low angle shot. The church bell tower is seen just over Courtade's head, as the shirt reading, "HELL'S KEEPER" shines off the sun light]
CC : You got that shot kid. You got that shot to actually accumlate something with this match. But, what they didn't tell you is that to get that carrot on the end of the string, to gather your pot o' gold at the end of the rainbow, you must first pass through a fate much worse then hell itself. They conviently forgot to mention that you must roll all your little hate, all that pugnant
"career killah" attitude, and throw it to the side, because that means nothing to me.
I have ended careers much bigger then yours kid. You se Kevin
Slater gracing a buidling anywhere lately? How about Ken Curtis?
You took out Madison, and a few others disciples of Rick Styles, your point? [Courtade grits his teeth] CC : You got no point kid, because that game you run does nothing for me. I feel for you. You're lost, lost in the abyss that is fame, and fortune. The abyss that is a piece of leather, and a gold plate in the center, and now I will show you the light. I will show you that coming after that strap won't get what you want out of life, it will only give you pain, and agony. I will show you that coming after me, because your "massah" told you as such will only bring to you a fate much more painful then death.
Deal with reality son... I am about to make you my bitch. [Courtade looks back over his shoulder, at the cross that hangs just over the church bell, then back to the camera]
CC : Like I said before, I ain't much on religion, but I found this one little quote fitting for you son. My father used to read me it when I was younger. You know Revelations well? The story of how it all ends?
"Let anyone who has an ear listen; If you are to be taken captive, into captivity you go; if you kill with the sword, with the sword you must be killed."
They have used you for their sword for too long, and now... "The sword must be killed" [Courtade merely turns away from the camera, and walks to his right, clicking his boots on the hard, dusty Texas prarie]
