1) EDDY "FLAP" JACKS ------------------------------------------------------------------------- _______________________________________________________________________ |_______________________________________________________________________| _____ _____ _ _ _ | | | | __| | | | | Eddy "Flap" Jacks | | | |_ | __|_ | | | |_ |_|___|_| |_____|_| |_____|_| Promoted by: Nobody. _______________________________________________________________________ |_______________________________________________________________________| [SCENE: We open on Main Street, USA. Nah, not some sort of generic 'everytown' setting, but very literally
Main Street, USA, at Disneyworld. Gaudy pseudo-turn of the century architecture, 434938 gaudily-attired peddlers peddling gaudy, over-sized cups of Italian ice...the camp value here is _ astonishing _.
We pan left. There's a camera crew, and a reporter in a bad suit. The newsman could conceivably be Jimmy Slagle, but this guy isn't nearly obese or dirty enough to be the Fan Site's ace journalist.
Anyway, this reporter-apparently not the esteemed Jimmy Slagle--is conducting 'man on the street' interviews here on Main Street, USA.
Let's listen in.] REPORTER: We're here in Orlando, Florida, the cosmopolitan heart of
_ these United States _, to listen to what the average citizen has to say about legendary senior citizen-and professional
wrestler--Eddy Jacks! [The reporter forces the mic on an elderly man wearing a ridiculous pair of Mouse ears and a "Members Only" jacket.]
R : You, sir! What's your opinion on Eddy Jacks? OLD MAN : Wendell Wilkie, you say? M' pop voted fer 'im, back in '34. Or was it '36? I seem ta remember an election in '36... Plain Groton man, not much of an accent. Used ta wear a thimble on his pennyloafers, which was the style in those days.
[The reporter leaves the old man to his ramblings. We segue to another victim, this time a busty young blonde wearing an oh-so-tight "Indigo
Girls" t-shirt.]
R : And you, miss? What have you got to say about Eddy Jacks? AMBIGUOUSLY GAY FEMALE: Another tool of the masculine power structure, eh? Well, you just wait and see! The patriachoracy is due to fall! Long live the Earth Goddess Mother...
[He cuts her off, too, and moves swiftly to someone else. This time, our speaker is a fat young man in a "Spock Lives: Star Trek IV" muscle shirt. Or, in this case, a 'shapeless tub of lard' shirt.]
R : Anything to say about the Gray Streak? FAT SCIENCE-FICTION FAN: You mean Eddy Jacks? _ Worst wrestler ever _. R : What's your beef with Mr. Jacks, kid? FF : Simply no _ innovation _. I mean, can you imagine that worthless sack of skin doing a quebrada? I don't think he could do a quebrada to save his life!
R : Could you? FF : That's not the _ point _. Jacks is too 'old school.' He doesn't set up his spots effectively enough. He'll segue straight from a kick into a powerbomb. No fluidity, no grace! Not like
TORA. Now, TORA, there's a wrestler!
R : I see. [And, with that, we leave our fat friend-what's that tattoo on his arm say? "Jim Jividen?" Question for another day. Anyway, the reporter moves off, looking for someone else to put on the spot... And runs smack dab into a tall, powerfully-built, hideously ugly young man. He's got perfect blonde hair, a winning smile, and a face like a bulldog. He's wearing a silk Hawaiian shirt and purple leather slacks.]
R : Do _ you _ have something to say about Eddy Jacks? YOUNG MAN: Hi, dad! [And on that note, we segue to a darkened room, away from the glitz and camp of Disneyworld. Jacks is seated on a steel chair, as always. He looks old and fat and mean, and he's won nine friggin' matches in a row.
He's wearing a black "Myers won the D-Cup, But I Wear One" t-shirt, and a pair of faded blue jeans, circa 1884.
Let's listen to what the old bastard has to say.] EJ : Last week, I laid an old ghost ta rest. Settled m' accounts. This week, they're puttin' me in the ring wit' a bum. Now Homeless Joe an' me, we go back. We beat the tar outta that no-talent punk Jeremy Steele-be honest, when's Steele ever done anythin' worthwhile, 'cept get his arse handed ta 'im in the LWC.
[Pause.] EJ : But, as the poofs in Hollywood are fond a' sayin', that was then, an' this is _ now _.
[Chuckles.] EJ : If the last bit didn't hammer 'ome the point, ya friggin' jamoke, I'll spell it out fer ya: I don't care what the people
_ think _. If I win-an' I ain't lost since I set foot in this bleedin' fed-it's 'cause I had the _ desire _. 'Cause I wanted to win. [Long-a$$ pause.] EJ : Body's been smashed ta pieces, dig, but the will ta win's never left. I'm 'ere 'cause I've always wanted ta compete, 'cause I ain't whole wit'out a _ challenge _.
[Shakes head.] EJ : Nobody's ever helped me, least o' all my idiot poof o' a son. I coulda given in, Boxcar, gone yer route an' wound up homeless an' destitute, but I _ didn't _.
I soldiered on, day-after-day, watchin' as body parts broke an' fell ta the wayside. [Another pause.] EJ : Don't need a functionin' prostate, or a knee, or even a brain ta whip yer goat arse, Boxcar. I'm just gonna take 'ese two fists 'ere, an' cave yer soddin' gob in.
[Looks away.] EJ : An' I ain't doin' no _ quebrada _. [We move to fade, and as we do, we hear a faint, off-camera voice…] SCI-FI FAN: _ Worst _ wrestler _ ever _. BLACK ------------------------------------------------------------------------- _______________________________________________________________________ |_______________________________________________________________________| _____ _____ _ _ _ | | | | __| | | | | Mr. Black | | | |_ | __|_ | | | |_ |_|___|_| |_____|_| |_____|_| Promoted by: Jeremy Baltimore _______________________________________________________________________ |_______________________________________________________________________| [Fade in on a shot of everyone's favorite N.E.W. baddie Mr. Black standing across Sullivan Boulevard in St. Louis. The world famous Gateway Arch towers behind the masked Mr. Black, who flips up the collar of his black leather jacket and turns his back to the biting wind. He holds a cell phone to his ear and seems to be having an animated conversation.]
MR.B : Dammit, it's colder than week-old dog crap out here. Will you tell me why you wanted me to stand on the banks of the Mississippi
River and freeze my nuts off?
[Black listens and takes a quick look over his shoulder.] MR.B : Yeah, the arch is still there. [pause] Rising out of the ground... uh-huh... reaching a pinnacle... yeah, got it... and then... what the hell does this have to do with anything?
[As another gust of wind hits Mr. Black from the side, he lowers the phone and stares at it in disgust. Raising it back to his ear...]
MR.B : You just wanted me to freeze my nuts off. What is it, 85 degrees there? You son of a... what? What _about_ Cody?
[pause] You saw that, huh? Well he ain't part of the picture right now. Look, I took care of Case and I whipped his little boyfriend last week. I know I promised to take care of Eddy
Jacks, but the old dude is laughable right now. He isn't worth it.
[Mr. Black listens again and shrugs his shoulders.] MR.B : Yeah, he's like... 80 years old now. But Cody's trying to butt his way into line for an ass-kicking. Well he can take a number and wait with the others because I deserve gold. And that means
Courtade has a bullseye on his back. He's everyone's flavor of the month right now, but he's never faced the future of this sport. Dammit, I'm gonna squat and take a growler all over his career. And I ain't gonna do it as _Mr. Black_ either. I'm really getting tired of this damn mask.
[The masked man snorts loudly and spits a loogie on the sidewalk.] MR.B : What? What the hell are you calling me that for? Look, I'm ready for this. _I'm_ the one who's been out there cracking heads lately. So what happens? They stick me in a match with this Andy
Harden? Jeezus. If Baltimore won't get me a title shot, I'll just have to... huh? What did you say?
[Mr. Black lowers the phone and stares at it in disbelief.] MR.B : When are you gonna be here? Uh-huh... yeah. Okay. Give me a call and I'll meet you at the airport. Yeah, later.
[Mr. Black grins as he snaps the phone closed and slides it into a pocket inside his jacket.]
MR.B : Now it's time to really shake up the damn N.E.W. [With a nod of satisfaction, Mr. Black walks hurriedly down the sidewalk. The sun is vibrant and shining down into the lens of the camera, occassionally causing a small glare to form on it. The camera pans the area, showing the blue sky laying over and tall golden grass that whips back and forth with every burst of wind. Finally, after surveying the surroundings for a few moments, the camera turns back down to a closer scene- that of a cat. The small gray animal is walking around on the land, each slow step taken with incredible care. The cat is on the hunt. Everytime that the grass shakes, the cat lunges toward it trying to catch its prey. This game continues as a voice breaks the silence.]
VO : A cat and a mouse... a hunter and its prey. The cat stalks the mouse all over the country side. Lungin' when it pleases, attackin' whenever it feels the need. It's almost like a game.
Only thing is, its seen from two different perspectives. The mouse thinks thinks he's winnin' all the time. Feels like the cat is doin' everything it possibly can... tryin' to hit, tryin' to kill, tryin' to end it... and just comin' up short on every attempt. The mouse keeps runnin' 'round, havin' as much fun as possible.
Then there's the cat, knowin' exactly what's goin' on when he's on the prowl. He lets the mouse think he's in control the whole time, but the reality is that the cat is the one runnin' things.
The cat is the one who keeps the little game goin'. And the cat, is the one that decides just what happens. Every time he jumps at the mouse, he misses... for a purpose. Every time he tries to catch the mouse, he misses... for a purpose. He lulls the mouse into a trap, makin' him feel like he can escape the attack.
[The voice stops and from the side of the shot, out steps Jack Cody. Cody looks like his normal self- blue jeans, t-shirt, brown work boots. He peers at the camera before turning his attention toward the cat and mouse "game" that is taking place before us.]
JC : This little scene right here sums up everything that's happened
'tween Mr. Black and myself. See, Black, ya epitomize every quality I just described 'bout that mouse. Yer out runnin'
'round, thinkin' that ya run things in N.E.W. Thinkin' that every time somebody stands up to ya, and ya squirm away, that it was 'cause o' somethin' you did. And most of all, I know that ya think ya can keep on goin' wreakin' this havoc on the company fer as long as ya please, 'cause nobody'll stop ya.
But yer forgettin' 'bout the cat... me. Say what ya will about me, but I've made it obvious that I'm not gonna stand by and let ya run rampant. So no matter how many times ya hit somebody from behind, no matter how many times ya make me look like a fool, and no matter how often ya get away from me, I'm still the one that's runnin' things. Just weeks ago ya claimed that ya didn't give two craps about me, but now yer concerned 'bout my every move.
Now yer runnin' off at the mouth and challengin' me... all because ya think that yer winnin' our little battle.
Yer deceived just like the mouse, and eventually it'll come back to haunt ya. 'Cause its a fact that eventually, the cat becomes bored from playin' around...
[The camera zooms in on the mouse, stalking through the grass. The animal carefully places a paw onto the ground, and then pounces. With perfect precision, he catches the small rodent.]
JC : And then the game's over. Eventually yer Pearl Habor antics are gonna stop, Black, and yer gonna reap the whirlwhind o' everything ya've done to N.E.W.

There, sitting inside his locker room, sits the soul fucking reason N.E.W. exists. He sits, slowly sliding on a pair of dirty blue jeans, taking off his various items of ring wear. Slowly, his elbow pads and knee pads come off. He takes off his sweaty T-shirt, and slips on another simple black T-shirt, reading "Me > You". He slowly slicks his blone locks back, wet with perspiration flat against his forehead. Slowly, he grabs the N.E.W.
Heavyweight Title, and slips it over his right knee. He then glares up, with his menacing blue eyes, _into_ the camera, damn near _through_ the camera.
"Cold Blooded" Chris Courtade] CC : Here I sit, in the same position as I do after each, and every
[bleep] damn show. I worked, I put asses in the stands, but I don't feel like I got anything accomplished. Stephen Bourdo...
[Courtade sighs] CC : Whoopty-fucking-doo. Another idiot they rolled in front of me, and another one that will more then likely find himself in the unemployment line.
Another half-dead carcass they fed me, only to have spat back. [Courtade grabs the N.E.W. Heavyweight Title, and simply shakes his head] CC : Hell, it's not rocket science. I _am_ the mother [bleep]in' N.E.W. You take away me, and you got nothing..._ZERO_! I don't give a rat's ass who gets their panties in a wad when I say that, because sometimes the truth just [bleep]in' hurts. You shelf
Devon, week after week, yet you roll out dead air time. Why not just book Simon Prime in the Main Event, that's sure to draw a few hundred people to the arena.
[Courtade pauses] CC : I see you idiots beat me to that. [Courtade slowly shakes his head again, and closes his eyes, taking another deep breath] CC : What next? Maybe now you'll bring in some "big names". Maybe then, you can actually make me break a [bleep]in' sweat in the ring. Arrogant? Egotistical? Try "reality". I came here to make a name for myself, when in realit, N.E.W. is making a name for itself off of me. I ain' getting any younger, and I ain' getting any better. I ain' fighting wars, hell, I doubt I am even fighting anything.
Until now. [Courtade slowly gathers his items, sliding the N.E.W. Heavyweight Belt into his bag, and stands up]
CC : You see, I realized something out there tonight. If you bags of
[bleep] won't give me what I want, then I just might have to go and get it myself. If you won't give me a good damn'd fight, then
I guess I mine as well better start to stirring up some [bleep].
To be honest, I don't blame the "executive committee", not one bit in fact. Look at me, look into my eyes, and you tell me I am of sound mind, of sound body. Look into my soul, and tell me it's a happy place, somewhere where "good things" take place. You won't, you can't, because deep down inside of me...
Is a [bleep]in' monster. I ended the career of several no talent sacks of crap that entered this company. Hell, look at how thin this roster is, that's not coincedence, rather, reality. People just don't want to [bleep]
with me. They don't want to take that walk, and get their asses handed to them. And I don't blame them one bit. Hell, once a coward, always a coward, that'll never change inside the human soul.
[Courtade begins to head towards the camera, towards leaving the locker room] CC : I'll tell you this much, come Sunday, that all will change. Come Sunday, I no longer sit back, and wait for someone to give me orders. Come Sunday, I no longer allow myself to be whored out to this [bleep] ass company, in order to gain it more money. Keep your eyes peeled to my every action, and your ears glued to my every word. And if you got the balls...

Standing on a chipped and aged wooden stool is none other then James Morgan. Attired in torn, grungy, faded blue jeans with black boots and a sleeveless T-shirt reading "N.E.W.'s Bane" across the chest. Morgan cocks his head to the right, resting both arms on his thighes, slouching down. His three metallic eyebrow piercings in his left eye glare in the overhead light as his eyes hang low, obviously heavy form fatigue.]
JM : Intricate surroundings are not needed. Within this simplicity,
I obtain complex settings. It is hard to see but can I imagine the weak minded to see what I do? No, because miracles like that do not happen every day. Whatever higher power or deity you all bow down and pray to could not perform such a task. So how could you all expect Morgan to?
[For the first time, possibly ever, Morgan emits a soft chuckle.] JM : A man once said that other men, himself included I'm sure, fear that which they do not understand. Never a truer statement spoken, says I. There are many examples within this World that wear their ignorance and naivity on their sleeve. Some show mercy upon those because they simply do not know. Others just don't care. I fall in between. Although how you live and whether you do infact live is an issue in which apathy stains me, head to toe, I do see it fit to educate the uneducated. Guide the directionless and teach those in need of teachings.
[Morgan slowly cocks his head to the left, slouching down further, resting both feet on the floor. His tired state shows through.]
JM : One such example, running rampid like the spoiled brat he grew up as and has spawned into adulthood as, is the Devious One himself. Yes, dare I mention a "Headliner?" Me, the pathetic rookie. Am I bending another cardinal rule? Remind me to mourn my errors after, will you? Devon, what you wish to obtain and achieve is beyond me. To be more precise, what you wish to become, I couldn't care. But I do care about your current existance and how you see it right to interject yourself into my affairs. Is this the extent you'll go to gain the respect of the mindless fans or your peers? Will you oppose Morgan to be put in a different light amongst the rest? Do you foolishly take bumps just to be recognized?
[Another chuckle, this one a bit more noticeable and louder.] JM : Pathetic. No one will remember you when you're gone, Devon. Right now, you are the talk of the N.E.W. Entrapped in affairs and issues amongst many men, losing yourself in the soap opera.
How disillusioned have you grown to become, Devon? How ironic. The one thing you used to get away from the every day life is becoming the one thing that is overcoming your life. How fitting. Once you burn out, never to spur from your own expended ashes, you'll scratch and crawl for the last crumbs of your success. From one Indepent Promotion to another, you'll use your name to bring you whatever bit of success the Mistress that is Fate might toss your way, out of pity.
[Morgan leans back, tilting his head up. Both cold, grey eyes stare into the lens.] JM : That is what you'll become, Devon. A man of pity. No one will worry about you. They will feel sorrow for your pathetic being.
And they will cite your existance as a pitiful attempt at being famous.. and trying to be noticed. Your own quest is your own
Waterloo. The life of a fool. I could warn you ahead of time ..
but no, I see it more fit to watch you fall the greater distance. Because it is quite elementary when you break it down,
Case. The higher the fool builds himself up .. the longer he will fall. And in the end, you'll realize how hard it will be to climb back up. You will soon Fall Into a Deep Depression, Devious
One .. but quickly realize one thing ..
[Morgan stands up and begins to step towards the Camera, whispering something.] JM : You're not important.. You're not important.... [Morgans body covers the entire lens as his hands grasp both sides. His voice, still picked up.] JM : You're not important ....... You're not important...........
