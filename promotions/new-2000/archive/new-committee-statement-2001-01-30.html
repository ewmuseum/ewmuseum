N.E.W. Committee Statement

30 January 2001
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Readers, The following is N.E.W.'s stance on the whole scandal Wayne's caused for himself over the past week. When Wayne (Jon Ash) first approached us about entering N.E.W., we looked over his application and sample flash and decided that the character wouldn't fit in as well as we, and the handler would have liked him to. Trust me, "Jon" was just as annoying as Wayne has always been, and the comment was made that he was VERY similar to Wayne in his persistance and outright aggrivating-ness (A new word! :). "Jon" even went to lengths and made the comment to me that [detail withheld] in an attempt to get himself accepted into the fed. Needless to say, a few weeks pass, and Joe read some of Ash's work in GLCW and sent it my way. The work was solid, and he ("Jon") had a genuine interest in the fed and had a lot of ideas for if we used him (Even in a non-wrestling role). Despite being a little aggrivating, we let him know that we'd like to have Ash in the fed. He was ecstatic, and immediately got to work working on an angle with Casey Evans and worked a great show with [name withheld]'s character, Vandal Gomez. Then this whole scandal came out. Joe and I discussed it for awhile, and we decided that we were ready to release Wayne, not for being Wayne, but for the AIDS comment (Which Joe knew nothing of, I hadn't said anything about it) that he had made initially. It was tasteless and selfish of Wayne to use that sort of thing to gain acceptance to the fed. But, we thought twice about it and our decision came to this. We're going to put Wayne on a "probation" of sorts. Now, I know that it probably seems that he shouldn't give enough of a shit about the fed to take some sort of slap on his hand like we're his parents or something, but hey... He gave enough of a shit to [detail withheld] and assume an alternate identity. The probation is pretty much, he's got to lay off some of the other handlers in the fed before they get too aggrivated with him, no more scandals, or stupid shit to get him in trouble with. If he can do that, then he's more than welcome to hang around.. If not, the spot will be easily filled. We don't condone Wayne's actions, but I can see things from his perspective and why he'd pull a stunt like that. He's just can't continue to do the things that aggrivated people about Wayne when being "Jon." It's not that Lionheart sucks, or that Wayne's an ass... He's just a bit overexcited and downright annoying sometimes, and THAT is why a lot of people hold ill will towards him. Hopefully he can use this as a lesson and learn from it. - Jason N.E.W. Committee Member
