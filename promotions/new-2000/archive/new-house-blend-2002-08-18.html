N.E.W. House Blend

18 August 2002

Hey there everybody. It's Cam Cavendish coming back at you for another edition of House Blend. This time from the Elks Clubs in beautiful, historic Charleston, South Carolina. Charleston truly is a wonderful city with a lot of ante-bellum charm. Not to mention a classic hotbed of pro wrestling. Hopefully the next time we come here we can be in a full-size arena for Shockwaves. But hey, Myrtle Beach was a big coup for us and that's going to be a blast. Couple quick notes before we get started. First off, to my good friend, Chris West. I want to thank you for the 50 bucks from the Funk/Winston/Drake/Machado match last Shockwaves. It came in really handy. Got a copy of Cel Damage for my X-Box with it and have been having a real blast. Been playing as Dominique and man I don't know which is hotter. Her body or her car. Gotta love that pink convertible. And yes my friends the rumors were true. We had one of NEW's biggest of cheeses on hand in the form of master promoter Christian Leadfoot. Christian wouldn't share what the family emergency was that tore him away from the NEW Arena last Shockwaves. Just that it was under control and that he'd elaborate when we get to Myrtle Beach. Nor did he seem to be aware of the apparent relationship brewing between fellow NEW promoter and goddess ring announceress Dawn Wells. He was too excited to just be in the city as apparently his parents lived here when they were first married and er go there's a strong family bond for him in Charleston. -------------------------------------------------------------------------- --------------------------------------------------------------------- MATCH 1 ERNEST "BRICK" WALL VS ZEKE CALHOUN Fortunately unlike last week, I managed to arrive at the Charleston Elks Club before the card began. I hadn't had the opportunity to meet Ernest Wall until Christian introduced us. And I could immediately tell why he was brought back. He's kinda like that guy we used to watch going up, Bucksaw John Dugans. A scrappy old cuss with a real heart for the ring that announcers like Chris West like to pick on for being stupid. So when Another Brick in the Wall by Pink Floyd started pumping through the speakers, I was primed to see him knock some heads. And apparently I wasn't the only one as the NEW die-hards in the crowds cheered raucously by opening match standards for Wall. The big guy soaked it in and did some stretches as he awaited his opponents. Then the twangy banjo strains of Foggy Mountain Breakdown by Flatt and Scruggs hit and it was time for the young plowboy Zeke Calhoun to make the scene. Zeke didn't get anywhere near the reaction Wall did although he did receive a couple token cheers from a couple University of Arkansas students. Can't imagine what they were doing in Charleston though. Myrtle Beach would be the better place to scout for next year's Spring Break. But then these were college students from a state consistently at the bottom of national education statistics. So I guess anything's possible. The bell rang and the two big ole boys locked up. But right from the first belly to belly suplex I could see this was just going to be a Mitchell Davis midterm for the young Arkansas plowboy Calhoun. Zeke kept trying to muscle Wall about. A strategy that proved very ineffective as the brick outhouse just started pounding Zeke with short-arm clotheslines, some back hand chops and finally a cross armbreaker. Wall tried to make a total squash of it by slapping on a camel clutch as a follow-up to the armbreaker. But Wall made the mistake of applying it too close to the ropes and Calhoun grabbed out of the bottom strand making referee Ben Johnson call for the break. After that, the youngster flashed some potential as he adjusted his strategy a bit and started using his joints as weapons. Some high kneelifts, elbow smashes and European uppercuts brought Wall to center ring. But Zeke left the well too soon. Instead of sticking with the joint-based offense, he tried to switch back to the stiff-limb brawling and paid for it. Going for a kick to the gut, Zeke got himself spun around and bulldogged right to the mat. Then quick as his veteran legs could carry him, Wall was up on the middle turnbuckle and dropping the elbow on young Zeke just as the plowboy rolled over. Then deciding he didn't want to hold up the card, Wall dropped across Zeke's chest, barred both his arms and started trying to yank them up over the plowboy's head. I'm sure there's a Japanese name for it but since you don't see a whole lot of puro guys wrestling in North Carolina, I wouldn't know. Anyway I don't think Zeke cared what you called it at the moment. All he knew was that he had to yell out his surrender to Ben Johnson as tapping out with his feet wasn't working. Christian smiled broadly like bringing Wall into NEW was his idea and another good show was underway. -------------------------------------------------------------------------- ----------------------------------------------------------- MATCH 2 THE ANDRAZOONS VS THE STYLE COUNCIL I was of two minds going into this match. On the hand, I think I made it pretty clear how I was less than amused by the wild facades of our latter-day Spiders From Mars otherwise known as The Andrazoons. But then at the same time, Red Carter and Curly Cuse had been making life hell for my brothers ever since the days when one could still hope John McCain might get the Republican nomination for the millienal election. So when Nancy Boy by Placebo started up and those garish glam-boys in all their glitter and greasepaint headed down the aisle, I couldn't turn my head. I had to just whip out my trusty Ray-Bans, grin, and bear it. But the sensory pain had only just begun. For no sooner am I given blessed relief from that Placebo trash than I'm subjected to The Style Council's latest affront to good taste. A European techno version of the Petula Clark classic "Downtown" complete with pumping bass and police siren. To say this bastardization of one of the greatest songs in music history was making my ears bleed would be an understatement. This was so bad I still marvel that I didn't die of anemia. I wouldn't even have known Red and Curly were even wearing clothes had Christian not starting dribbling over the respective blue and pink LaCoste shirts (Remember, children. They haven't been "Izods" since 92.) the Council was wearing. As Christian reminisced about wishing he could afford an Izod in its prime the bell rang and all four men met in center-ring. Succubus pairing off with the carrot-top Carter while Glitteris took on Cuse and his perfectly formed ringlets. Yes, people it is safe to assume that Red Carter is the only one in this match who's never had his sexuality questioned. But enough babbling. Seeing as none of the four were winning the power contest they were locked in, Red and Curly decided to kick our Spiders From Mars in their Andrazoons and start peppering them with European uppercuts. But this only set the garish ones off as they dodged the flurry of forearms and slid in between the knees of the fashion plates. Both of whom were low blown in the pass and then dropkicked in the back of their knees. Red and Curly wouldn't stay on the mat long though. As both Zoons peeled Curly off the mat and whipped him into the near corner. There Glitteris and Succubus performed of the sickest acts I've ever seen. They sat Curly on the top turnbuckle and climbed up onto the second rope. Where they then lifted him off the turnbuckle and powerbombed him to the concrete floor. The groan of the crowd told me it would be a miracle if we saw Curly again any time soon. Then the Zoons turned their attentions to Carter who was just getting up after watching his partner get splattered like a mosquito on his windshield. Red was up on all fours and Glit and Suck got to him. So they began to stomp him back down to the canvas again. And once Red was probably battered, they hauled up back up and delivered a two-man Liger bomb, depositing him at center ring. After that, Succubus righted Red again. But only long enough to wish him a Pure Morning and get the one, two, three. And was I ever glad it was over. Beacuse as the Zoons were returning to the dressing area, they stopped to stare daggers into me. And if sensory damage could kill, I'd have died twice over. Leaving some other shmuck the task of delivering these results to you. -------------------------------------------------------------------------- ----------------------------------------------------------- MATCH 3 "AIRWALK" A.J. SUMMERS VS LARRY GAMBLE Needless to say, after that last match I was looking forward to see more normal people and less who thought good taste could only exist in their mouths. So when Larry Gamble stopped by to ask me if I thought he could beat this new guy Summers, it was such a relief. Christian informed Larry that both he and Summers looked like really solid prospects and we both wished him luck. Then the glass that precedes You May Be Right by Billy Joel shattered and Larry was off to the ring with a confidence that completely belied his inquiries about the newcomer Summers As Larry loosened up in the ring, one of the most intrguing songs I've ever heard in a wrestling arena started. Namely Invincible by Edna's Goldfish. I can't say I paid as much attention to the entrance of A.J. Summers as I should have so piqued was my curiosity over the mixture of horns and bass in his theme. Looks like somebody's firing up the old Bearshare after he's finished typing. Anyway, it was the bell tolling to start the match that awoke me from my reveille. And talk about a most unorthodox opening. The newcomer Summers rushed forward to try and get on the offensive right off the bat only to have Larry run up the turnbuckles and backflip over him. Summers turned around to try and face Larry. But young Gamble had already thought ahead, sliding under Summers' feet and double heel tripping him to the mat. A move that he followed up, being so close to the ropes with a fist-drop off the second rope that he buried right in Summers shoulderblade as the newcomer was getting to all fours. But this is where Larry's over-dependence on the aerial offense started to get the better of him. Overestimating the impact of two pretty basic moves, Larry dragged Summers to a more open section of the ring and went for an Asai moonsault. But having wasted so much time, Larry missed and ate only canvas as A.J. rolled out of Gamble's way. A very costly gamble when you consider that Summers was also looking to impress. A task he promptly set about achieving as he immediately rolled Larry up in a magistral cradle for a two count. As Mr. Leadfoot drooled over the fluidity of that near fall, Summers preceded to make an even bigger fan of both of us as he immediately went to the backslide for another two count. We both started wondering if Summers would go for a third near fall when he surprised us and more importantly Larry by planting a superkick right in Larry's rising mush.Then borrowing Larry's lightning quick agility, A.J. ran up the southwest turnbuckles and landed an exquisite shooting star I'm told he calls the Space Jam. After the Space Jam, he hooked the leg and the count was academic. As Summers hand was lifted, the crowd erupted for the Airwalker. Making it immediately clear Jamie Underwood was no longer the only young firecracker around here and really pumping both me and Christian up for their coming meeting on Shockwaves. -------------------------------------------------------------------------- ----------------------------------------------------------- MATCH 4 "SLICK" VIC VENTRESSA VS ROGER PEOPLES Speaking of Jamie Underwood, as a matter of fact, this is when he made his first appearance of the night. The young Roman Candle out both hawking and modeling his home-made Teen Extreme t-shirts. But seeing that this is his time whether the impetuous self-promoter Underwood is there or not, Vic Ventressa makes his usual soundless entrance and climbs in the ring just glaring at the interloper. Even at this, Underwood is not impressed. But then things change radically as the disco classic It's Raining Men by The Weather Girls fires up and out steps Roger, one of the boldest men I've ever known, with his boyish bleached blonde charm and carnation pink tight with matching boots cavorting to the music like he's caught out in the mythic storm. A storm he must feel has come to life as he spies Underwood and Ventressa still locked in their staredown and freezes in mid-aisle. Immediately I know what he's thinking. He's counting his spare condoms in his mind seeing a potential threesome of epic proportions. But Underwood sees what's happening and points it out to Ventressa as the bell rings and Jamie heads for the tall timbers like his ass is on fire. Vic wastes no time in seeing what Underwood has pointed out to him and rushing down the aisle to get at his gay admirer. Sadly for Roger, Ventressa does not drop trou and instead grabs the homo by his blonde locks and throws him into the ring. Following Peoples in, he immediately heads for the north ropes looking to savate kick the taste right out of Roger's mouth for even thinking of him in that light. However this rough treatment awakens Roger. Enabling him to get to his feet and see this kick coming well enough to duck it and yank Ventressa's support foot out from under him. A move he follows up with a couple always exciting (to him anyway) headbutts to the groin and a string of toeholds and leglocks which lead up to a figure four. It soon becomes clear though that Roger hasn't done enough of the right kind of homework on Ventressa as Vic reverses the hold on the homo with ease. Referee Michael Hunt tries to step in and make Ventressa break the hold. But he will only do it after dragging Roger to the ropes and taking a four and a half count for holding them. But the safety of being released doesn't last long for Roger. For no sooner does Ventressa break the hold than he is stomping on the back of Roger's knees repeatedly. Then dragging the young GLAAD lad to center ring Vic cinches in an STF and just starts cranking for all he's worth. Peoples taps out almost immediately. But it makes no difference. Nor does the constant ringing of the bell. Ventressa just keeps cranking, trying to avenge the perceived insult to his manhood. It's only when Corey Irons arrives on the scene and starts beating on Slick Vic that he releases the hold.Then more by force than anything. As Irons nails Ventressa with repeated rights and lefts that back up to and finally knock him out of the ropes to the floor. Ventressa runs off to join Underwood in the tall timbers while Irons checks to make sure the Outer Banks favorite gay wrestler isn't too badly injured. -------------------------------------------------------------------------- ----------------------------------------------------------- MAIN EVENT "SEX PISTOL" PETE SHERIDAN VS JAMIE UNDERWOOD As Irons and our crack medical staff helped Roger Peoples from the ring, we wasted no time heading for home. No sooner were Peoples and his unintentional entourage behind the curtain than Refuse Amen by Amen cranked up and out once again to grace us with his presence was Jamie Underwood wearing the original Teen Extreme t-shirt. Replicas avaliable at the merchandising table for only $15.99. Hate say Christian was too thrilled to see Jamie after he held up the prior match trying to pimp out his t-shirts. But he was coming out as a scheduled participant this time. So what could a body really do? The crowd was pumped up and letting Jamie them hear they liked him or hated him and there plenty of both. But then I didn't hear too many complaints for what happened next even if it was surrounded by the over-wrought strains of Livin La Vida Loca by Ricky Martin. Because what happened was of course the appearance of a raven haired goddess in Claire Maynard and of course her charge, the hapless Sex Pistol Pete Sheridan. Who was getting booed mercilessly by my contemporaries in the crowd for daring to show up in public in a New Kids On The Block t-shirt. Poor misguided slob. If only we could get him a pretty face that didn't hide an evil mind. But while I mourning the company Sheridan so foolishly keeps, the bell rang and Claire Maynard practically shoved Sheridan into the waiting arms of Jamie Underwood. No, not for that purpose, Roger. Although Lord knows she can't be sleeping with him. It was an attempt to spur Sheridan on. But all it really did was put him right in the path of some stiff Underwood knife edge style chops. Which was quickly followed up by a huracanrana and a one count when Jamie kept the legs hooked. And right away Claire Maynard was doing what she does best. Namely, bitch. She was screaming at Sheri like it was some great shame to be on the business end of a lightning quick Underwood huracanrana. I couldn't understand why Sheridan would want to listen to such a bitch. Even if you could lie her on her stomach and bounce quarters off her tight little ass. But for whatever reason Pete seemed to be feeding off Claire's vitriol as he started firing heavy European uppercuts at Underwood's head and dropping him to the mat with a chin-flattening jawbreaker. A move he followed him with a Camp Maynard standard, the Champagne Supernova spinning neckbreaker. And considering how quickly he went to that well, he should consider himself lucky to get the one count. Still Sheridan thought he had something going so he ran up the northwest turnbuckles and went for a senton bomb. But unfortunately for the would-be Sex Pistol, he sent the move Western Union. Thereby giving Underwood plenty of time to get out of the way. And driving the raven haired harpy to scan the ceiling for strength. A moment of distraction which caused Claire to not see Underwood underhook Sheridan's leg and get a two count out of a fisherman's suplex. Thinking he had the wounded duck Sheridan, the nineteen year old Underwood slammed the mat in frustration. More in anger than by any real design, Jamie hurled Sheridan into the ropes and charged after him. The intent being to clothesline the latest Maynard charge out of his boots. But instead it was Underwood's boot that felt the burn as Claire grabbed his right foot and yanked the young cult hero in the making off his feet. Sheridan looked confused by this so Claire shoved him on top of Jamie who received an impulse elbow as the Sex Pistol fell on top of him. The resulting one and a half count for Sheridan seemed to severely irritate Underwood. He toyed with going outside and putting a hurt on Claire Maynard but wisely at the last second turned and superkicked the rising Pete Sheridan right in the teeth. At this point he should have gone for a pin. But seeing Claire Maynard with her back turned berating a fan gave him another idea. He slid out of the ring after her and tapped her on the shoulder. And no sooner did she turn around to berate what she thought was Sheridan than Jamie planted a hard kiss right on Claire's ruby red lips. Meanwhile, Sheridan was back on his feet. Claire, seeing this, tried to wave Sheridan off but the Sex Pistol was confused and thought she was fighting off anoither Underwood advance. Leading Sheridan to attempt a running somersault type plancha. But Underwood guessing what Claire's gestures meant got out of Dodge and Sheridan ended up tackling his manager instead. Seeing this, Underwood had a big chuckle at Sheridan's expense and threw him back in the ring. Claire Maynard tried to hold Jamie back but to no avail. He shrugged the shrew off and scaled the ropes from the outside. Perching himself up there long enough to wait Sheridan back up to his feet and send Sheridan crashing back down with a flying head-on bulldog. And you could tell Jamie was pumped by that results of that move as he immediately jumped back to the top turnbuckle and leapt off with an Ace Splash before Sheridan could even breathe. After that we had another academic one, two and three and our resident cult king was having his hand raised while Claire Maynard was in the ring on her knees screaming her lungs out into Sheridan's face. Another great card for us I'd say. Killer debuts especially by A.J. Summers. Some feud possibilities were born as well as one continued. Was good having Christian around. He had great insight on the show and even suggested that based on Jamie Underwood's interloping he might soon have to face Vic Ventressa and/or Roger Peoples. I queried about the idea of a triple threat to really set that sitch straight and Christian didn't rule that out either. Once again, I can't urge you enough to get out and check out NEW action be it a Shockwaves or a house show like I bring you at House Blend. Because here in NEW, the action's always at it hottest. Good luck everybody and catch ya at the next House Blend. [name withheld] Handler of The Dime Notes (OSW,RSP) Steve Armstrong (XCW) and (under construction) Because life's too short to watch crap. - Adam West, promo for TV Land
