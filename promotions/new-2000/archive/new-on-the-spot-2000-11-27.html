It's as if you're opening your eyes, and then seeing all around you nothing but trees. You stammer to your feet, and look around some more, into the darkness all around you. It's obviously night. And what's that fuzzy mist? Oh. This must be a dream.
Then, for what seems like no reason, you begin to run. Trees fly by you, as you hear the crackle of twigs and leaves underfoot. But it's not just one set of crackles, but two. Something or someone is chasing you. You look behind quickly, the forest zooming by still. Darting in and out of trees is a very human form. It's too dark to make out who or what it is, but it is gaining on you. And then, what you assumed would happen happens. You trip. You fall. You spin.
Facing straight up, you hear the voice.] VO : Here, kitty, kitty. Don't worry... I won't hurt you. I just want to play with you. Ruffle your fur. Burn you alive. Here, kitty, kitty.
[The form gets closer, as you stumble to your feet. But as soon as you are up, you are down again, on your back, using your hands to pull you away from the quickly approaching man.]
VO : Ah, there you are, kitty. Such a bad little kitty. We'll have to punish you. Now how will we do that? Oh... I know.
[The last three words come out almost pleasant. The scream you hear as the pale white hand of your captor descends towards your face is not.
Especially when you realize it is your scream.] [Fade to black.] ------------------------------------------------------------------------- _______________________________________________________________________ |_______________________________________________________________________| _____ _____ _ _ _ | | | | __| | | | | ????????????? | | | |_ | __|_ | | | |_ |_|___|_| |_____|_| |_____|_| Promoted by: ???????? _______________________________________________________________________ |_______________________________________________________________________| [The scene fades in as you open your eyes. Your eyes peer down, as your heavy head does not want to look up. It takes a few seconds to focus, but once you do, you notice that your feet are restrained with leather straps. Going in either direction from the straps is a thick metal chain.
The mist is back. You're still dreaming. Or are you? Your head raises, and you look to the right and the left. Your arms are restrained much like your legs. You feel the cold brick behind your back. That's weird.
You remember learning that you can feel nothing in a dream.] VO : Poor kitty. [The voice demands your attention, as you look straight ahead. You scream to yourself "No, don't look!". But you do anyway. You have no control over your body. Thank God this is a dream.]
VO : I didn't want to hurt you, kitty kitty. But you made me. You ran away. You were a bad kitty. And bad kitty's have to be punished. Bad kitty's have to be hurt.
[You see the black form... and then the white hand reach out from the darkness and take hold of a thick wooden wheel attached to the wall.
Chains stretch from it to the left and to the right.] VO : Goodnight, kitty. [The hand turns the wheel, and the sound of metal coiling around itself is heard. Though it is hard to hear it clearly, because your scream is back. Your loud, brittle scream. This is just a dream... just a dream.
You say it over and over to yourself as the form comes closer, and your eyes fall to his shirt. It is black, with blood-red writing on it.
Wait... no... not blood red... blood. It drips and drizzles down, the letters slowly appearing one after another before your eyes. Your scream gets louder as you make out the words... "I'M BACK". Fire begins to burn the edges of the shirt, slowly licking it's way up at first, and then engulfing the entire dark shirt... or is it a shirt? You don't know anymore. You don't know anything. Just that you're alone... it's dark...
and... You open your eyes. Just a dream... just a dream. But for the NEW... there is no waking up. The nightmare begins anew. And it begins... Judging by the row of iron bars lining one wall, it was a prison cellblock in a former life. The steady drip of water hitting a puddle echoes in the hallway as the shot rolls down the corridor. Then... heavy footsteps.]
VO : Has the N.E.W. learned to stop wishing for that which it does not really want? [The shot turns quickly and comes face to face with none other than Mr. Black. A smirk is evident beneath his black mask.] MR.B : Jack Cody... you wished for my mask and said you would stop at nothing to get it -- even a cowardly attack in my lockerroom. But who paid the price for your wish, Cody? Who felt the beatdown?
Who lost his shot at the N.E.W. World Championship belt? Be careful what you wish for, Cody.
[Mr. Black steps forward and the camera moves back to keep pace.] MR.B : Chris Courtade... you wished to be challenged by anyone in
N.E.W., but what have I done since my arrival? I crippled your friend Devon Case. I made a mockery of his little "hit man."
And I showed you with that chair exactly what I thought of your empty challenge. Do you remember how you got that championship,
Courtade? Do you remember who left the ring and gave you the opportunity to wear the belt you never _earned_?
[Mr. Black suddenly stops. His index finger slowly rises and points at his own chest.] MR.B : Correct. Face it, Courtade, you are nothing but a paper champion... and a paper champion should never play with fire.
[He turns and steps into one of the cells -- a small concrete cubicle in which all fixtures have been removed. Mr. Black runs a gloved hand over the smooth walls that bear scribbled graffiti. He speaks deliberately;
his deep voice muffled in the small room.] MR.B : I promised when I came to N.E.W. that you would all bear witness to the past, the present, and the future of professional wrestling.
At the end of Shockwave you saw the past meet the present in the man they called the Outlaw... [his voice becomes very sarcastic]
...and may I say that _I_ was just as surprised to see him as anyone. And as he looked down at Mr. Black, it was evident in his cold eyes that he saw the future.
[Mr. Black stares at the camera as he steps forward out of the cell.] MR.B : Do you still wonder who I am, Cody? Do you still wish for my mask? Be careful what you wish for, Cody. Be careful what you wish for, N.E.W. It may just become...
[Mr. Black grabs the cell door and slams it shut -- the sound echoing loudly down the corridor.]
MR.B : ...your prison. Various images of several tourists hot spots are shown. Including, Rocky
Mountain National Park. An enormous white capped mountain and the highest point of Estes Park. Suddenly, the scene fades out from the mountain and into view of a rather large log cabin. After a few seconds, we find ourselves inside the cabin. We soon realize that we are not alone. For there is a man in the cabin. Who?
We can't tell. For his face is blurred out for reasons we do not yet know. He is sitting down at a coffee table, so we sit with him. The man now speaks...] MM : This place is so peaceful. [His voice is rather recognizable.] MM : Everytime there's a fork in the road or a fork in my life, I come here. This place helps me clear my head and gets me thinking clearly again. I'm definitely at a turning point in my life.
That's why I'm here. I've traveled through many organizations trying to find my home but to no avail. I've been injured in almost every place I've wrestled in...
..and I can't stop thinking about unfinished business either. Unfinished business that has traveled over 2 different federations. Business that has cost me two knee surgeries in the past 2 years. Business that needs to be finished before I can continue my quest to become a World Heavyweight champion. I'm not going to name names.
But many of you fans out there know exactly who and exactly what I'm talking about. To the person I refer to I will offer this challenge...
Show up on Slam Dance. Come to National Extreme Wrestling, the place where I will make my home. Where maybe, one day, I can fulfill my dream and become a World Champion. Come here and meet me one on one, face to face, and let's get this over with once and for all. What do you say? Will you raise up to the challenge or back down like you have continued to do for the past 2 years?
[The man sighs...] MM : Other business that needs to be addressed. You may all be wondering why my face is being disguised at this very moment.
Well, I have been entered as a mystery man in an 8 man gauntlet match to determine the next N.E.W. Rising Star Champion. The front office of National Extreme Wrestling has decided to keep my appearance a mystery until that very match. I will abide by their wishes. So, until that match takes place you will all have to live with the suspense...
[He laughs.] MM : As far as that very match is concerned...I plan on walking in to N.E.W. 242 pounds and walking out 257. I plan on adding 15 pounds to this well defined body. No, I'm not talking about overeating on Thanksgiving Day. I'm talking about defeating several quality wrestlers to become N.E.W. Rising Star Champion.
I have watched and studied film on the 5 announced combatants in that match. Harden, Mad Goat, James Morgan, Edmond Winston and
Brett Hunter are all very _good_ wrestlers.
But... They aren't _great_ wrestlers. I, am a _great_ wrestler. Pound for pound one of the best wrestlers this sport has ever seen.
The only question left to be answered is if I have the stamina to defeat 7 men in a row, if need be. The answer to that question is simple. Yes, I do.
And yes... I will... [He takes a sip of his coffee and continues...] MM : N.E.W....welcome to a new era. An era where wrestling is just that, wrestling. Screw the drama. If you have talent, prove it, get in the ring with me and we will find out if you really do.
Or, if you are just another bottom dweller scrapping and clawing to get to the top. I'm the real deal. I've have gone out to that ring for you people
365 days a year for the past three years. Blood, sweat and tears
I have given to this business and to you people. Three years I have searched long and hard for my true home. A place where wrestling is appreciated, a place where I am appreciated. I truly believe that I have found that place. Here...
..in N.E.W. [The scene fades from the inside of the cabin back to the outside of the cabin. The snow continues to fall on the log cabin as the scene fades out.]

He's wearing the Standard Jacks Attire-white t-shirt with 'S-CRAP: FUHGEDDABOUDIT' logo, black workman's jeans, and steel-toed boots. In other words, EJ ain't exactly a fashion plate.
But we're not here to describe Armani's spring line. We're here to listen as Jacks pontificates about Christian Ledford, the latest in a long procession of NEW promoters.]
EJ : Got a new _ boss _. Had hundreds a' bosses, overseers, an' trainers throughout m' career. Fer the most part, they're inter-friggin'-changeable. Ya've got yer Osterhouts, yer Jividens, sure, but, when ya get down ta the nuts an' bolts, it's all the same friggin' deal. Everybody-the Jaxxter included-is interchangeable.

Gore, but it ain't my cup a' tea. [Scratches chin.] EJ : So, what's the deal with Leadfoot, yer wonderin'? Seems like a nice enough guy. 'S got a talented, unproven bunch o' folks ta work wit'. Has a rep as an 'old-schooler', but the smarks've got an alarmin' tendency ta throw 'round words like that wit' a bit o' abandon.
[Moving on.] EJ : So I'm in Camp Leadfoot. Means I'm in there wit' Boxcar, who I sorta respect, an' a couple other bums that I _ don't _. 'Long as they give me a wide friggin' berth, there won't be any
_ problems _. [And so.] EJ : Everybody's talkin' 'bout this 'Gray Streak' a' mine. That's always the way, when some career pug balances 'is books an' pulls off a few cheap upsets. Army wins 10 football games? Call out the bleedin' reporters. Navy spanks Notre Dame? Get me the AP, we've got a story!
An' now this. "EDDY JACKS, CINDERELLA STORY." Splashed on the front page a' the Fan Site, the Wrestlin' Insider, the Wrestlin'
Digest. [Adamant.] EJ : I'm damn near 60 years old. [Points to right eye.] EJ : My eye's AWOL. Ditto my left index finger. Both a' my knees are made outta the fancy-dan polymers they use in the space shuttle. An' I owe it all, every last injury, every crummy bump, ta bad bookin'.
[Grins. Several teeth are missing.] EJ : Grin by Gunnar Gaines. Knees by Wick Rilliams. Steel rod in the spine courtesy a' Simon LeBec. Oh, an' the prostate cancer.
Chalk that one up ta Mother Nature. [Back to topic.] EJ : Last thing I am is a _ goddamn Cinderella story _. I've learned a helluva lot in my 40 years in the friggin' sport. I held the
SCRA World Championship fer two bleedin' years.
Ta me, 9 wins is a drop in the bucket. Ta me, 9 wins is akin ta spittin' in the wind. [Snuffs the stogie with a steel-toed boot.] EJ : 'S a man's smoke, _ Winston _. Kinda smoke that knocks yer friggin' teeth out. [Jaw set, determined.] EJ : Ain't here to peddle stogies, tho'. Here ta announce my
_ purpose _. Here ta set the record straight.
[Camera zooms in.] EJ : I've been in a lotta wrestlin' feds. I've won an' lost a great many wrestlin' matches.
Ain't _ never _ been happier than I am right now, 'ere in the N. E. W. I've hit a stride. The tank's near empty, but I'm runnin' on premium. [Fighting back tears.] EJ : This is my _ home _. Leadfoot, fer better or worse, is my new
_ boss _. [Or not.] EJ : An' I'm gonna be his goddamned _ champion _. JC : I could sit here an' tell ya 'bout how I'm the only one that came to answer Courtade's challenge. Or I could tell ya 'bout how I just pushed N.E.W.'s World Heavyweight Champ to limits that he's never been pushed to before in the ring. Maybe I could let ya know how proud I am 'bout competin' in possibly the greatest match in N.E.W. history. I could say how great it is to have a legend o' the sport here. Or I could say that if the ref's hand had traveled a fraction of a second faster, I'd be sittin' here with that gold strap 'round my waist right now.
But not a one o' those things matters. Know why? [Cody sits straight up now, pulling the ice pack off away from him and pointing at his head.]
JC : 'Cause all I can think 'bout is havin' a knot the size of a baseball comin' outta my forehead. All I can think 'bout is havin' to hold an ice pack on my face, to keep it from swellin'

But in yer big moment o' glory, ya made the mistake that's gonna lead to yer downfall. 'Cause ya might've nailed me with that chair, ya might've caused my blood to shed, and ya might've nearly knocked me out cold...
But ya didn't put me away _fer_good_. I might have stitches in my head, I might have a mountain of a knot stickin' out 'bove my eye, and I might be comin' into this at a serious disadvantage... but I'm still right here, my lungs are still takin' in oxygen, my heart's still pumpin' blood... and there isn't a force on this planet that's gonna keep me from gettin' in that ring on Sunday. And there won't be anybody in the arena that's gonna be able to stop me from personally escortin'
