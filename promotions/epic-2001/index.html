Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Epic Wrestling was a website federation on the Slike.com host that opened in November 2001. It billed itself as angle-based and ran no role-plays at all: its members wrote their characters into its weekly show, Monday Night Ground Zero, as storylines, segments and matches, and every show closed with a list of who had written each part. A pay-per-view was to follow every two months.

Its story gave it a past. Its history page tells of Matthew McGrath founding Epic Wrestling in 1983, of Ground Zero on Fox, and of the company closing after his death in 1995, until the nephew of its former champion Trent Morgan revived it in 2001 by buying out the bankrupt CWF and its roster. Its title histories and its Hall of Fame carry that invented past alongside its own run.

Its own run crowned two lines of champions. Seth Anson won the vacant World Heavyweight Championship in a tournament on 3 December 2001 and lost it to Ben Archer at Vengeful Hearts on 24 February 2002. The North American Championship went to Jason Winters on 26 November 2001, and at Epic Warfare VI on 23 December 2001 the Hardcore Isosceles Trapezoid, a character of Sean Simon Williams, took it from him in a Poisoned Pyramid Deathmatch, a barbed-wire pyramid of the challenger's own devising, and held it until Sam Potright beat him at Vengeful Hearts.

Its own event lists run from the Ground Zero of 12 November 2001 to Vengeful Hearts on 24 February 2002, and three of those shows survive whole. How it ended isn't in the surviving record.
