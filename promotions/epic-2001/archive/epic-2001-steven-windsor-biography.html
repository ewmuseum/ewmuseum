Historical Information Epic Titles Won: None Epic Awards Won: None Past Promotions: None Past Titles: None Past Awards/Accomplishments: None |Biography| Steven Windsor was born on December 14th, 1975. He was born at Edmonton General, a city hospital where people went that didn't have medical insurance. He was born to Patricia Adams and Sam Windsor. At the time of his birth his parents were not married. Shortly after he was born Patricia and Sam decided it would be best for Steven if they got married so he could live in a household with married parents instead of just boyfriend and girlfriend. So a week after Steven was released from the hospital Patricia and Sam went to a little chapel in a neighboring city and eloped. Sam also decided that same day they didn't have time nor money for a honeymoon because they would need a place to live now that they had a son. Sam set out to find a small two bedroom apartment that they could afford to live in. Sam found a small little run down apartment in west Edmonton for $200 a month. He quickly looked over the apartment and decided it would have to do for now until they could afford something better. Sam quickly hurried back to Patricia's parents house were her and Steven were staying for the time being. Sam told Patricia that he found them a small little apartment in west Edmonton that would suit their needs for now. It had running water, a stove that worked and a small heater that worked and it also had two bedrooms. At that moment Sam and Patricia realized they'd no longer have the comfort of living with their parents and depending on them for whatever they needed, now they were on their own out in the real world. Sam and Patricia packed up all their boxes and moved them to their new apartment while little Steven was being watched by Patricia's parents. Patricia and Sam had finished the moving of their belongings to their apartment and decided that it was time to go get their baby boy Steven and take him to his new home. So the couple got in their vehicle and drove over to Patricia's parents house. When Sam and Patricia arrived they noticed something odd, the door was hanging wide open and Patricia's parents car was gone. Sam and Patricia quickly exited the car and rushed inside to see what was going on and found Patricia's parents lying on the living room floor knocked out. Right at that instant Sam began to panic, the first thought that jumped into his mind was that Steven had been kidnapped. Patricia tried to wake up her parents and eventually did and asked them what had happened. Patricia's parents told them that they were sitting there watching television, Price is Right to be exact and then there was a knock on the door and Patricia's father Ronald opened the door to find two men with ski masks on standing in the door way with guns in their hands. The two men entered the house and told Ronald and Darleen, Patricia's mother to sit very still and don't move a muscle. The two men quickly searched the house and came back to the living room with Darleen's jewelry, a television and some other items they thought were valuable. The two men were about to shoot Patricia's parents but were interrupted when little Steven who had been laying on the couch with his grandparents the whole time began to cry. This was the first time that the two men notice the baby laying there. They were panicked for a second because they didn't know what the noise was then they realized it was little Steven crying. The men told Ronald to make the baby shut up otherwise they'll make it shut up personally. Ronald did his best to calm the baby down but little Steven was too scared to stop crying so Ronald asked the two men if he could please put the baby in the bedroom, he will stop crying then. The two men thought about it and decided to let Ronald do it, so Ronald took little Steven and put him in their bedroom on their bed. Little Steven still cried so Ronald gave him a bottle and it quieted him right down. Ronald quickly thought to himself if he leaves that room he's as good as dead but if he doesn't leave then so is his wife. So Ronald quickly exited the bedroom and returned to the living room and sat down on the couch next to his wife while the two men stood there and stared at them. After thinking for a few seconds the men decided it would be best if they didn't kill the couple because it would make too much noise so they told the couple to give them their car keys and lay down on the floor facing the ground. Ronald and Darleen quickly laid down on the floor and told the robbers that the car keys were on the table. The men grabbed the car keys and were about to leave until one of the men stopped and walked back over to the couple and hit both of them in the back of the head with the butt of his handgun, instantly knocking both of them unconscious. The robbers quickly exited the house and hopped in the Adams family car and sped off. That is all that Patricia's parents remembered. Sam hearing all of this quickly rushed into Patricia's parents bedroom and found little Steven laying on the bed sound asleep with his bottle next to him. Sam quickly scooped up Steven and rushed out of the room. Sam told Patricia that they needed to leave but Patricia would not hear of it, she wasn't about to leave her parents after they'd been attacked. Sam told Patricia he was leaving and he quickly exited the housed with Steven in his hands and walked to the car and got in and sped off. Patricia was very mad that her husband would not stick around and help make sure her parents were ok but she didn't have time to think about that right now, she called 911 and told them what happened. The ambulance arrived a short while later and it was all over. Patricia returned home to the new apartment later that night to find Sam watching television in the living room and Steven laying in his crib crying very loudly. Patricia asked Sam why he wasn't taking care of the baby and Sam told her simply that the baby didn't deserve his attention. Patricia grew very angry and stormed out of the room and into Steven's room and closed the door and sat with him the rest of the night. A few weeks had passed since this incident and nothing had gotten any better, Sam was still ignoring little Steven because he didn't "deserve" his attention and Patricia continued to be angry at Sam because of it. After only a few short weeks being married the couple was already pondering over getting a divorce. This same situation went on for another month in the Windsor household before Patricia decided she had had enough and asked Sam for a divorce. Sam was bewildered that Patricia had the guts to ask for a divorce but he had no other choice then to grant it because he grew tired of Patricia and the baby. So the couple separated and filed for divorce the following day. Patricia returned home to her parents house to live and she took little Steven with her and Sam decided to stay and live in the apartment. After waiting two long months the day had finally come for Patricia and Sam to meet in court to finalize the divorce. After a week in court the judge decided that Patricia would be granted custody of Steven but Sam would have full visitation rights and get to have Steven on the weekends if he chose to. Sam didn't really care because he was glad to have Steven and Patricia out of his life so he agreed to the terms and the two went on their separate ways out of the court room. Five years had passed since that all went down and now it was Steven's first day of school. He was as excited as he'd ever been. His mom and grandparents took him to school and met with his teacher Mrs. Spillman. Steven was very happy to be in school with other kids, this was the first time that he got to be around other kids because his mother kept him very sheltered at home. Steven was about to enter his class but a hand on his shoulder stopped him, it was his father Sam. A man that Steven hadn't seen since the day his parents filled for divorce. It had been four and a half years and Steven had no idea who this man was in front of him. Sam bent down and told Steven that he was his father and right then Patricia who was talking to Steven's teacher noticed that Sam was talking to Steven and rushed over and grabbed Steven. She told Sam to get out of here, he had no right to be here after neglecting Steven for four and a half years. Sam didn't argue and simply stood up and left. Steven finally went inside the classroom and began his days as a student. Five years had passed since that happened and now Steven was in fifth grade. It was towards the end of the school year and Steven was getting worried about entering Junior High School. Steven had done excellent in school so far, getting exceptional grades and never got in trouble with anyone. He also had a quite a few friends. Steven was on his way home one day from school and was about to turn down the block to walk to his house but he was interrupted by two bigger kids who stopped him and started pushing him and laughing at him. The two kids continued to harass him until they punched him in the face and Steven fell down and the kids ran away laughing. Steven got up while crying and walked home, he entered the house and immediately was surrounded by his mother and grand parents and was asked what happened and he told them to big kids punched him in the face and ran away. Right then and there Steven also decided that he must do something to protect himself if this was going to happen all the time once he entered Junior High. So Steven asked his mother if he could take karate lessons, his mother said no because they couldn't afford it. Steven realized that he would have to teach himself to defend himself so he asked his mother if he could use Grandpa's old weights and start lifting. Patricia wasn't quite sure why Steven wanted to begin lifting weights but she said ok and she brought the weights upstairs from the basement and Steven took them to his room and started training from that day forward. For a fifth grader Steven was actually pretty big. He stood 4'10" and weighed close to 120 pounds. This was almost twice the size of his fellow class mates. The fifth grade school year ended and Steven continued his weight lifting to become stronger. He also started fighting back when those two kids would harass him on his way home. Upon entering the sixth grade the following year Steven had grown to an amazing 5'2" and he weighed close to 155 pounds. His mother was beginning to worry there was something wrong with him because of his abnormal size so she took him to the doctor and had him checked out. The doctor said Steven was in perfect health and there was nothing wrong with him except he was growing a little fast then other kids. The doctor was amazed that Steven only had 3% body fat at the age of 12. Steven continued to lift weights and go to school until the seventh grade came. This year would be totally different from all others. This is the year his father would get back into his life. All his life from the time he saw his father on the first day of school ever he wanted to see his father again and wanted nothing more then to impress his father and for his father to care and love about him. Steven finally got his chance on his 12th birthday, his father came to his house and told Patricia that he wanted to see Steven. Patricia couldn't reject even though she wanted to so she reluctantly let Sam take Steven for the day. Sam took Steven to a river where it was almost completely frozen and told Steven if he wanted to ever see his father again and have his father care for him he has to jump into the freezing cold river and swim upstream fifty yards where his father will be waiting on a little wooden dock. If he fails and drowns while attempting this, then he will disgrace his father and everyone else in his life. Steven really had no choice because he wanted so badly to impress his father, so he threw off his coat and shoes and jumped in the freezing cold December water. Steven struggled against the strong current at first but after about ten yards in the river he gained control of his body and started to easily make the swim. He finally reached the dock where his father was standing and his father helped him out of the ice cold water and gave him the biggest hug he had ever given him. Steven beamed with pride as his father carried him back to the car. Sam and Steven got in the car and returned to Steven's house where Sam dropped him off and told him now he was a man. Although it was the best moment of his life, it was also the last time he would ever see his father. Steven had no idea what was about to happen. Sam slowly backed out of Steven's driveway with Steven standing there waving at his father, his father was waving back not paying attention to the road behind him and as he pulled out into the street a semi came and t-boned Sam's car breaking it in half and instantly killing Sam. Steven stood and watch in horror as his father's car was torn apart, Patricia and her parents heard the ruckus and rushed outside to see Sam's car being thrown everywhere in pieces and the semi slamming on it's breaks. Patricia quickly rushed over to Steven to make sure he was alright while Ronald rushed over to the semi and asked what happened. Darleen rushed back inside and called 911, but it was already too late, Sam was dead. Patricia took Steven back inside while Ronald and the semi driver checked on Sam. They found him bloodied and cut up in the ditch next to the house out of the car, he wasn't wearing his seat belt. Ronald knew instantly that Sam was dead and so did the semi driver. Ronald walked slowly back inside as the semi driver returned to his semi and waited for the cops to arrive. Ronald told everyone the bad news and they all began to cry, Steven the most. From that day forward Steven no longer cared about anything in his life, he felt everything was pointless now that his father was gone forever. The next few years were pretty much empty for Steven, he finished the 8th grade with flying colors and entered high school as a very pissed off and angered young man. Steven wasn't ready to take shit from anyone and the first person to run their mouth to him would pay dearly. By the time Steven had entered the 9th grade he grew to 6'2" and weighed almost 200 pounds. The first year of high school was pretty boring and easy for Steven. He pretty much kept to himself and had very few friends. Mainly spending time on weight lifting and working out. The 10th grade was a totally different story for Steven, he came in contact with a kid named Bruce Anderson. Bruce was the most popular kid in the school and everyone worshipped the ground he walked on, except Steven. One day while eating lunch Steven was approached by Bruce and a few of his buddies and they started making fun of him, saying he was a loner and was a dumbass and had no friends. Steven just ignored the men and continued to eat his lunch, after a few seconds Bruce realized that Steven was ignoring him and he got mad and grabbed Steven's food and threw on the ground and stepped on it. At this time the whole cafeteria turned to see what was going on. They were expecting a fight so they watched in awe as Bruce and his friends continued to make fun of Steven who continued to sit there and stare at the now empty table where his food used to be. Bruce asked Steven if he was still going to eat the food now that he had stepped on it. Little did Bruce know that the lunch he had stepped on was also the only meal his father had ever made for him, a Bologna sandwich with no crust and no miracle whip and a milk. Steven tried to contain his anger while Bruce and his friends continued to joke around about him. Bruce made a grave mistake by saying a stupid comment about Steven's father. Steven stood up quickly and stared straight into Bruce's eyes and said "Don't you ever talk about my father again." and Bruce started to pretend he was scared then started laughing with his friends again and asked why he shouldn't make fun of Steven's retarded father. Steven replied with "because he's dead and if you make one more comment about him, you'll have the same fate." This time Bruce took the comment seriously and backed off a little and told Steven that he was sorry, he had no idea his father was dead. Bruce's friends looked at Bruce like he was an asshole and they all walked away leaving Steven alone at the table. The rest of high school was the same for Steven, boring and easy. By the time he graduated he had no friends and really didn't care. After high school was over his mother Patricia wanted Steven to attend college but Steven had other plans. He wanted to do something to put his strength and size to use, by the time he graduated from high school he was 6'6" and weighed a massive 270 pounds. Patricia asked Steven what he had in mind and Steven told her he was going to be a professional fighter. Right away his mother objected but she knew it was no use because he'd do what he wanted and she couldn't stop him, she hadn't been able to stop him from doing anything since he was 15 years old, no use in trying now. Steven joined a small promotion called ULFC or Underground Lethal Fighting Corporation. After only two fights and two victories Steven decided this wasn't what he wanted to do. Steven returned home and told his mom he was going to go to college. He enrolled at North Dakota University. During college it was the same as high school, he was bored and had an easy time. After he graduated from college with a degree to be a physical trainer he decided to pursue a career in it. He found a job in Calgary where he went to work being a physical trainer for athletes. By the time he was out of college and began work he was now 6'8" and weighed an astonishing 285 pounds. Steven's first and only client was a professional wrestler named Jim Wright. Jim wrestled for Calgary Championship Wrestling. Jim asked Steven if he would be interested in a career as a wrestler and Steven said he would think about it. After thinking it over he went to CCW's training facility with Jim Wright and enrolled to become a full time wrestler. After some training he had his first match and he jobbed to a more well known established wrestler and Steven knew he finally found his calling as a professional wrestler. After wrestling for CCW for six more months Steven decided it was time to move on and seek a better promotion and that brings us to today. Steven is now in search of a promotion to let his name be heard and his talents shown.
