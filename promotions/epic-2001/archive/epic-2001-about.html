Epic Wrestling | Main | About About Epic Wrestling Hello, Visitor Welcome to Epic Wrestling. Epic Wrestling is a fantasy wrestling website created for the sole purpose of the entertainment and enjoyment for those who are apart of our roster. There are actually thousands of other fantasy wrestling sites across the Internet. Unlike other forms of fantasy games, fantasy wrestling has no point system. Nor do it's participants compete against each other in chat rooms. Fantasy wrestling is coming up with an original character and creating a gimmick or personality for that character. Once that is done, the handler (The person controlling the character) works with other handlers to produce storylines. There are three types of fantasy wrestling websites. The first type of fantasy wrestling websites use roleplaying (Either straightforward interviews, or actions in the every day lives of the wrestler, or even parodies of current news and/or movies), as a method of deciding winners. The second type, allow the handlers to decide who wins and loses according to what's best for the storyline. The third type of fantasy wrestling sites, are called resource sites. They serve to provide resources for the first two. Epic Wrestling is somewhat more like the second type which is called an Angle based eFederation. The first type is referred to as a Roleplaying based eFederation. We at Epic Wrestling decided to go the angle based route to allow things to run smoother and give those involved with us a more enjoyable experience. Now don't get us mistaken, Epic Wrestling is a very unique fantasy wrestling website. Unlike, most Angle based eFederations; Epic Wrestling features no roleplaying at all. Since we feature no roleplaying, our roster uses storylines, results, and segments to get their character across to the reader. Our staff works alongside the roster, giving them the creative freedom to input their thoughts on the storylines, results, segments, writing, and booking that we put out on our website. We feel by including them in the creative process of their characters this allows them to better get across their character and how they want that character to be portrayed in the eyes of the reader. Epic Wrestling operates using one show a week and one major pay per view after every two months. During this period, storylines that we have worked on will be unfolded to the reader. The reason we opted for a pay per view after every two months, unlike the WWF's style of a one pay per view each month is we feel the approach we have taken will serve to strengthen the meaning of our pay per views. The current names and months of these Pay Per Views are as follows: August - Unleashed, October - Ashes in the Fall, December - Epic Warfare, February - Vengeful Hearts, April - Reckless Abandon, and June - Deception. We try to give our readers the most enjoyable wrestling experience done through fantasy wrestling. An afterthought of such a task was giving Epic Wrestling a history. Numerous fantasy wrestling websites suddenly open with a large fan base storyline wise for no reason whatsoever. Not even the World Wrestling Federation started that way. By giving Epic Wrestling a history, it's more realistic compared to the generic eFederation or eFed for short. For first time visitors, we recommend visiting the Wrestler Biographies, to learn more about the characters that make up the roster here at Epic Wrestling. Each wrestler's biography helps you get an idea of what makes each individual character unique and what led them here to Epic. If you found that you've missed a lot of recent happenings, don't worry. All of Epic's cards are archived in the Past Events link. However, you won't find the events that occurred in Epic's fictional past when it was under the control of Matthew McGrath. If you wish to give your insight on the storylines and results to the members of Epic Wrestling, feel free to post your thoughts on our Fan Board. Some of your comments can also have an effect on the type of material we put out so don't hesitate to voice your opinions. The Fan Board can also be used to ask our members and other fans like yourself, questions and to discuss some of the general happenings in Epic and eWrestling itself. In return, we ask that you refrain from posting blatant ads to attract attention. This would help save our time of having to remove posts and yours by posting them. Should you have any other questions, please refer to our FAQ. Thank You, Epic Wrestling Staff
