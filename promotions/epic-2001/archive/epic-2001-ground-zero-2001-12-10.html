What this is The Ground Zero of 10 December 2001, Epic Wrestling's weekly show, as its own site published it and the Wayback Machine captured it on 7 February 2002, with its own list of who wrote each segment at the end. Its segment A Rash Decision, in which the Hardcore Isosceles Trapezoid sets the Poisoned Pyramid Deathmatch for Epic Warfare, is credited to Sean Williams.
