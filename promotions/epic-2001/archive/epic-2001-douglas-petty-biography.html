Personality Information Douglas Petty is one of the few genuine nice guys left in the world. When it comes to the fans, he'll do anything he can to please them, no matter how busy he is. But the ladies, thats a different story. Douglas isn't exactly a one lady man, and doesn't care who knows it. In the ring, Douglas loves to use technical moves, as he's always been a fan of PURE wrestling. He isn't the kind to show off during his matches, as he knows that a simple mistake could cost him big. Sure, he may be inexperienced, but he isn't stupid, and he knows the ring just as well as everyone else. Historical Information Epic Titles Won: None Epic Awards Won: None Past Promotions: Past Titles: Past Awards/Accomplishments: |Biography| Douglas Petty is a long time friend of CWF Former Owner, Jessica Jenkins. He was one of her closest friends since birth, as their fathers were good friends and worked together. At age 18, he was pulled away from everything, and put into Shawn Arrows' training camp, just like Charles Lee, Jonathan Mills, and Levond Daniels. Petty is said to have been Arrows' best student, and he was quickly offered a contract with CWF when it opened, and was the first name mentioned for contracts when Epic Wrestling bought out the bankrupt CWF. Douglas Petty now makes his way to Epic Wrestling, hoping to prove to the world that he's not just a normal rookie, and that with the skills he has learned, and will learn, he will be a major star in the wrestling world.
