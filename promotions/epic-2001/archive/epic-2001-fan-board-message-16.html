Posted by Jessica Jenkins (11/13/01) [detail withheld] In Reply to: Re: I like this idea... posted by Stress on November 12, 2001 at 18:57:40 : : : I ain't really going to dog Epic because it's a very original idea, yet I am wondering how can you hype up a card without roleplays? : : Heh. We have a Main Page, to post news on. A Ground Zero Preview link, where can you see the upcoming card. It will be utilized after Epic's first event for suspense reasons. : : For Pay Per Views there will be a Pay Per View site similar to those seen at the World Wrestling Federation, fWo, and IOW. There you can see the history behind the event, the card, the results when they're posted, and other information about the matches and the event itself. : : Don't forget the useful eWrestling resource websites that are dedicated to posting news on latest happenings in eFederations they may know of. : : EW Uncensored - http://www.ewuncensored.com : : Owned by Jonny Trip : : and : : E-Wrestling Network - http://www.ewnweb.com : : Owned by [name withheld], but ran by [name withheld] : All right I see your point there [name withheld] but how will the angle's be strectched more. I mean usually roleplays give the reader more to think about. With there angle's and how good the writer is himself. I see it this way. We're more towards a realistic side of things than most federations are. How often do you see a commercial come on tv with a WWF superstar talking about his match coming up, or a part of his life being broadcast? It rarely ever happens, and WWF angles and fueds USED to be great. : I know angle's can be stressed in cards, and the writers can show there stuff with the promo's they put into the cards, but some things the fans like to see is the fact that they like to see the angle progressed a little more in roleplays. With 8 shows leading up to the first Pay Per View, I think the angles and fueds should be hyped up nicely. We are trying to be different, and if someone thinks there should be roleplays to build them up anymore, I'm sorry. We're not asking for all of that from this group. No roleplaying is also helping our shows. Instead of preasuring roleplays, we can now focus all of our time on the shows, and they're what really matter. Are they not? : It also show's more to the fan on how good that guy really is, and should show why he is getting that push. Everyone in Epic is equal in talent. Some of us have been around longer, some of us have better reputations, but theres not a single person in Epic who's any better than any of the others. As for proving they deserve the push they are getting. Why should they have to? As I said, we're all equals here. We all deserve everything we get, and so far, we're all loving every second of it. Thats what this is all about. Fun. Thats what we're having, and we're trying to let the fans have fun a well, by giving them solid shows, on time, every week. : Now with that said I would like to add that maybe your idea could work. I ain't gonna bitch about it and say that won't work and fuck your whole fan board up because I am not like that. I am a man who goes with my feeling and I feel that ePic might not attract the fans like many other feds due, due to non-roleplays, but there could be a lighter side of ePic. Thank you for not griping and complaining. You're a true man, for being reasonable about it. I wish there were more people like you around, that just voice their opinions in a decent manor. We seem to be attracting plenty of people so far. Sure, there wont be as much to read through during the week, but, who's to say we don't have a few things in mind to take care of that? We might find a way to give people things to do. We certainly hope we can. : After typeing all of this I have now pondered that many other feds have the same wrestlers in it, and if they really wanted to see them roleplay then they can go there, but this idea would lower the fan base here. Many feds don't have the same talent roster we do. I assure you, you wont find Brandon Lopez, Charles Lee, Jonathan Mills, Douglas Petty, Levond Daniels, Seth Anson, and a few others, in any other fed. :) Anyways, the fan base isn't the most important thing to a fed, so I don't see why you keep going at that one point. As I said, we're having fun, thats what matters. I'm finally in a federation with my friends, and it's sucessful thus far, probably will be for some time to come. Nothing else matters, nothing else ever will. I hope I made a few good points in this post, and not just sound like an idiot, as usual. Follow Ups: Re: I like this idea... Stress 11/13/01 (0)
