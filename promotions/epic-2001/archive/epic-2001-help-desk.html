What this is The federation's own help desk, its questions and answers about the site, as the Wayback Machine captured it on 14 April 2002. The owner's and the designer's names are shown by first name and last initial, their e-mail addresses are withheld, and one bare surname is withheld.
Epic Wrestling | Main | Help Epic Wrestling's Help Desk What should I use as a shorten version of Epic Wrestling's name? Being that eW or EW is often used as an abbreviation for eWrestling, you just call us Epic for short. Who owns Epic Wrestling? In character and out of character, Epic Wrestling is owned by William M. William can be contacted through eMail under [detail withheld] Can I join Epic Wrestling? Sorry, but at the time being, our roster is filled up. Please check back after the pay per view. Where are the roleplays? Heh. I suggest that you click the link under main titled "About" and read what it has to say. Who writes out your results? At the end of the results you should be able to find something called "Credits". There you will find a list of the indiviuals who contributed to each portion of the results. Who did the current design present for Epic Wrestling? Epic Wrestling was designed by Steve K. Steve is a very talented web page designer. Unfortunately, if you wish to acquire his services for your own site it costs money. You can contact him by eMailing [detail withheld] [name withheld] is also responsible for designing http://eye.steelramp.com and http://asylum.steelramp.com Would you consider linking to my webpage? Sorry, but the only websites, Epic Wrestling will affilate it's name with at the moment are eWrestling resource webpages. Can I have or purchase Epic? Is your name Vincent K. McMahon Jr? If not, no. Seriously, I am dedicated to this eFed and would not sell it to anyone. Why didn't the song I downloaded, play properly? The files were created on Real Producer 8 Basic. Most likely, the problem is your Real Player Program needs to be updated to version 8. Why are Chris Keaton, Dean Styles, and Greg Doyle reporting news, aren't they fake? While they are fictional characters, the Epic Wrestling website functions as if it were a real promotion. Any out of character activity or news can be found present on our fan board. Do you have any room for affilates? As of the moment, Epic Wrestling will only affilate itself with Professional Wrestling news sites or eWrestling resources sites. I would like to place a link to Epic on my webpage. Do you any pictures I could use to do so? Actually we have two. Our 468x60 graphic (Often used on rotating banners) can be found at http://epic.slike.com/images/epicad.jpg. Luckily for those wanting to take up less space on their webpage, we also offer a smaller 88x31 graphic which can be found at http://epic.slike.com/images/epicbutton.jpg. What happened to the Poser pictures? Our hired artist, Lewis "Unrealsk8er" J. has computer problems and his PC can no longer handle Poser 4. Until his problems are resolved or another artist of equal skill is willing to take the job, Epic won't have any Poser artwork to display for you.
