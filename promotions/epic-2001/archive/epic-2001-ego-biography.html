Historical Information Epic Titles Won: None Epic Awards Won: None Past Promotions: Californian Wrestling Professionals (CWP) Past Titles: CWP Tag Team Champion w/Trent Daniels Past Awards/Accomplishments: |Biography| They met in Hell's Kitchen. One didn't like it there, and got sent packing, the other followed. He didn't follow voluntiraly, he was forced to! Eugene Gregory Oswald always felt that he didn't need JCON's Wrestling School, he always knew that he could make it on his own, Hell's Kitchen was just a place to get scouted. Trenton Daniels always felt that he was still alive, thanks in part to JCON's Wrestling School. Had Hell's Kitchen not been around, he'd still be getting ridiculed at school. Hell's Kitchen taught Trent how to fight back. However, he couldn't fight back in Hell's Kitchen. A fact that didn't go unnoticed by E.G.O. In fact, Eugene would go out of his way to take control of Trent Daniels, and bully him. The very thing Trent came to Hell's Kitchen to get away from. For some reason, Trent didn't take E.G.O.'s bullying, as "bullying". Trent saw Eugene as a mentor, and did as he said, rather than listen to the HK instructors, and even Jonathan Conspiracy himself. However, the instructors weren't going to take it anymore, and Trent Daniels almost lost his spot in the school. But Jonathan Conspiracy saw more than he let on. After conversing with the instructors, followed by a face to face with Trent, Jonathan did the unexpected, he booted the talented "E.G.O." from Hell's Kitchen. Enraged by getting ousted by the "seldom-seen, often-heard" Jonathan Conspiracy, and seemingly losing his chance to get noticed, E.G.O. packed his bags and ventured back to his home in sunny California. He would meet up with some old friends and ink a deal for a regional wrestling promotion, Californian Wrestling Professionals (CWP), a federation that toured up, down, all around and throughout The Sunshine State. Meanwhile, Trent Daniels would become an "animal" in Hell's Kitchen. Alot of regional promotions in the New York City Area wanted "The Animal" in their promotion, but knew that only nationally recognized promotions had a shot at "Hell's Kitchen Hustlers". However, if you were a good friend of Jonathan Conspiracy's, and the money was right, for Jon, as well as the wrestler in question, you could get an HKH on one of your cards. So Trent would get booked in Brooklyn Bad Ass Wrestling, Queens Finest Wrestling, Shaolin Island Wrestling, Uptown Wrestling Association, and Boogie Down Wrestling cards a few times a week. He tended to like wrestling for those promotions, especially since he didn't get close to anyone else in HK, after the departure of E.G.O. Eugene continued to produce results in CWP, but the national promotions still weren't calling. But someone was calling, "The Animal" Trent Daniels. Trent and Eugene managed to keep contact with each other, but no conversation was more important than the one after September 11th 2001! Hell's Kitchen had temporarily shut down, and offers for Daniels to wrestle for any of the five boroughs Hell's Kitchen assisted promotions wasn't offered. Meanwhile, after a couple of canceled cards out west, Eugene and the CWP went back to work; work that Trent Daniels needed! So Daniels packed his bags and headed west once planes were once again allowed in the sky. Trent would sign a contract with CPW, and when his name was announced during the roll call at Hell's Kitchen's Morning Breakfast the morning of October 12th at 9 AM, a silence was heard, as Trent lay sleep in Beverly Hills on the same date at 6 AM. Later in the day, he would team up with E.G.O. and he wouldn't be an "animal", he had become "Self-Professed", seeing as he took it upon himself to free himself from the cage known as Hell's Kitchen! After a few months, the bullying of E.G.O. decreased, as he saw the improvement on the part of the now dubbed, "Self-Professed" Trent Daniels. Trent became E.G.O., Trent became egotistical! Oswald saw this, and used it to his advantage. He and Daniels would face Sunshine Boys, Roy and Ray Sunshine, at Scorching, CPW's big event, which was held at LA's Staples Center, and it became the first event in a major arena. Needless to say, some EPIC talent scouts were in the arena that January night, and saw the birth of EGOtistical! They saw the Sunshine Boys, Ray and Roy, get their "sun set", and watched as EGO and SP's star's begin to shine! E.G.O. has the attention and recognition that he has craved, and hopes that a contract with Epic will become a reality. SP has the big brother that he always wanted, and hopes that a contract with Epic helps him forget about walking out on what he really came to Hell's Kitchen for. Epic is in for one big EGOtrip!
