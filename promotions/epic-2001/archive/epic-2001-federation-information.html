Epic Wrestling: The Rebirth of a Legacy Federation Information Kayfabe Epic Wrestling has no "kayfabe". Which means what occurs on Epic's shows, Ground Zero and Pay Per Views are actually real. The act widely known as "blading" which is basically the cutting of the skin on the forehead to induce bleeding, doesn't exist within Epic. Handler Conduct After joining Epic Wrestling, you and your eWrestler are officially apart of Epic Wrestling. While under membership, we reserve the right to terminate anyone at anytime with probable cause. All handlers shall respect the entire staff, and act in an appropriate manor. Delinquents will not be tolerated. Failure to abide by the following can and will need to dismissal from Epic: 1. Any and all negative comments concerning Epic Wrestling from it's handlers are to be kept off any and all message board services. They are to be done through eMail or an Instant Messaging Service for the staff to deal with. 2. Should a handler need to take time away from Epic Wrestling (Over a week), please notify your fellow handlers and staff through the usage of our message board service that is available through the "Locker Room" link which can be found underneath the "Wrestlers" section. 3. The privacy of other handlers is to be respected at all times. Under no circumstance should any contact information of other handlers be given out. Contact information includes and is not limited to eMail addresses, Instant Messenger user names, and telephone numbers. Taking Time Off 1. If for any reason you should be forced into requesting a "Short Temporary Absence" (One to three months), and your character is currently involved in a storyline, another handler will be assigned to your character who then finishes off that storyline for you. Once the storyline is completed, your character is written off until your return. 2. If for any reason you should be forced into requesting a "Long Temporary Absence" (Four to eight months), and your character is currently involved in a storyline, another handler will be assigned to your character who then finishes off that storyline for you. Once the storyline is completed, your character is written off until your return. 3. If for any reason you should be forced into requesting a "Leave of Absence" (Over nine months), and your character is currently involved in a storyline, another handler will be assigned to your character who then finishes off that storyline for you. Once the storyline is completed, your character is written off and removed from the roster until your return. 4. If for any reason you should be forced into requesting a leave from Epic as a result of a personal matter, and your character is currently involved in a storyline, another handler will be assigned to your character who then finishes off that storyline for you. Once the storyline is completed, your character is written off and removed from the roster until your return. 5. If for any reason you should leave Epic Wrestling to join another fantasy wrestling federation and your character is currently involved in a storyline, another handler will be assigned to your character who then finishes off that storyline for you. Once the storyline is completed, your character is written off and removed from the roster.
