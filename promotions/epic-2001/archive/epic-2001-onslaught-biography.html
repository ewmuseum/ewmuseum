Personality Information Always making time for the fans, Onslaught is perhaps the most intense, exciting, and charismatic superstar ever to grace a wrestling ring. Despite his arrogance, when it comes to what happens in the ropes, he's all business. Onslaught simply views a wrestling match as yet another challenge, a challenge placed upon one's self, a challenge to chart your own boundaries and then attempt to go beyond them. When it comes down to sheer force and intensity, he's simply second to none. From the minute the bell rings, he blazes through the match like a man possessed. The man simply doesn't let up. The saying "Can you stop the Onslaught?" didn't come out of thin air. No, he's not a "Hogan". Like any regular guy, Onslaught feels pain, but he'll continue to go on. He can (and has) withstand almost an ridiculous amount of punishment. However, he won't let it stop him. In the past he's been bloodied up, driven through tables, and has still come out on top, regardless of the situation. It's this will that has caused some commentators in the past to call him a "machine" of sorts. He's sorta like a pissed off energizer bunny. While he doesn't do stupid things inside the ring such as posing when he has his opponent defeated, backstage or in the microphone is a much different story. His ego attracts enemies quick. For example, in a wrestling organization where he once competed, he had extreme pissed off three wrestlers. In order to appease the disgrunted workers, Onslaught was booked in a handicap match with all three of the individuals. Having been placed in submission holds after his mouth had been gagged with a hankerchief during his trainging in the TCA, trying to make Onslaught tap out is like trying to squeeze a eight hundred pound person through a door only two feet wide, it's just not gonna happen. However this can and will hinder his performance in the match or lead to injury if the move is sustained for too long. Throughout time, many opponents have made the unfortunate mistake of making things personal. Bringing him to such a point, he becomes the equivalent of a human whirlwind, wrecking havoc on the life of the unfortunate individual. Perhaps a result of his ego, but no matter the cost or situation, he refuses to cheat by any means. Not even a low blow. Altough being well trained in Jeet Kune Do, Onslaught never took the test for black belt. It was due to his own decision to not have his hands registered as lethal weapons. However, he knows very well he could knock out even the toughest of opponents. Thus he made the decision to wear padded fingercut gloves to his matches, but backstage is a very different story. Along with his determination and in-ring knowledge, Onslaught stands as one of the most innovative offensive wrestlers in the industry today which brings alot of interest to his matches. If given knowledge of whom his unfortunate opponent may be, he researches the background of his opponent, learning about their wrestling style, weak points, and strong points, then using that information to his advantage by finding reversals to compensate for during the match. He holds the position as one of the true masters of the minds in professional wrestling. This gift stems from his mother's profession as a psychologist Onslaught's passion to compete and will to never give up, helps him consistently stay a step ahead of the competition.
