Personality Information Doozer is not a heel or neutral... Yet he isn't known as a face. Throughout his four year career, he's never turned his back on the fans. Doozer also likes to think that he can turn into a Superhero like Clark Kent, but he turns into Doozerman. So, putting the facts that he is a face and a superhero together, Doozer goes by the status "Super Face". Inside and out of the ring, Doozer doesn't take a second of his life seriously. Well, with few exceptions. One of those being when he is on the losing site of a match. Doozer was born competative and will always be. He will end up going on mad rampages after losing a match and especially after being screwed. To Doozer, a win and no titles is better than one loss and all the titles. Because of this, Doozer doesn't go down easily. That and one other belief of Doozer's which is, 'We all die sooner or later... Why not fight like it's my last time?' Doozer stands strong behind that and has been known to stand toe to toe with a guys much bigger than himself while teetering on the edge of a cage, one inch from falling to bad injury or death. Yet, if Doozer is doing good and has the match in his hands, then there won't be a serious bone found in his body. He will spend most of the match mocking his opponent, trash talking him, and taunting to the fans. That usually leads to the opponent coming back, but Doozer won't go down. He'll fight a pyscho in a hardcore match because of another belief he stands strong behind, "broken bones, bruises, and cuts heal over time... but glory goes on forever. Past Awards/Accomplishments: DWF Hall of Famer, First Ever DWF Grand Slam Champion, DWF Best Promo of Ever, DWF Wrestler of the Month (October '00, November '00, March '01), DWF Match of the Month (March '01), DWF Promo of the Month (October '00, November '00, March '01, June '01), DWF Novice Champ of the Year (2000), DWF Promo Series of the Month (December '00), Brain Trust Cockiest Bastard of Interfed Stable, and Brain Trust Best Tag Team of Interfed Stable. |Biography| Scott Lavigne was brought into this world on a cold night at a baseball park. It was during a Boston Red Sox vs. New York Yankees game. There, his dad and his mom were watching the game when Don Mattingly suddenly hit a homerun to win the game in extra innings. Scott's dad had a lot of money in the game, and didn't have as much to give away... So he shot himself on the spot. Not a minute later, and Scott was born. Directly after birth, Scott's mom died. To say the least, it wasn't a great way to come into the world. Amonst all the crazy people going around the stands in a frenzy, Scott Lavigne wasn't even noticed. Until, after a short while, a janitor found him and his parents still at the game. The janitor took in Scott and took care of him. He didn't want to bring the kid to a Foster Home or whatever because he had gone to one as a kid and hated it. Thus, Scott Lavigne was literally born and raised in Fenway Park. He grew up fast, with peanut girls as mother-figures, and beer guys/ janitors as father-figures. It's no surprise that he came to be like he is. He beleives he can turn into a Superhero... More on that later. Five years into his life, Scott got a friend. His name was Kevin Bradford. Kevin's parents did not die; they just abandoned him. Well, it wasn't hard the two were best friends. They did everything together. Everything included a little homeschooling by janitors, watching television, and wrestling. Wrestling was by far their favorite thing on TV to watch (except for Superman movies, which were Doozer's all time favorite... and still are...). Scott had found a favorite wrestler in Hulk Hogan from day one. Yet, Kevin didn't really have a favorite until Mankind started up. The two went through a stage when they didn't care about wrestling. Yet, that didn't last long because it was made for them. At the age of 11, both of them started watching it again. Scott chose a favorite in The Rock, who stayed his favorite forever even when Rock made heel turns. Likewise with Kevin and Mankind, Mick Foley, Dude Love, and Cactus Jack. Kevin's favorite out of the faces of Foley was Dude Love, no competition. The life of the two boys went without change for quite a while. After wrestling with each other and mimicking their favorite wrestlers, they became good. Then, one day, they decided to make up their own wrestlers to add a little more fun to their game. Scott, since he was one or two, was nicknamed Doozer. He got the nickname because, since one or two, he was read these books by the janitor. In the book, the worker people were called Doozers. They were Scott's favorite. Yet, Kevin was having a hard time thinking up a name for his wrestler. He couldn't grasp a name. Scott, or Doozer, tried to think up names, but he was at a loss of good ones. Then, it came to Bradford. His favorite all time wrestler was Dude Love. Obviously, he couldn't have that name. Yet, Dude Love was nicknamed The Dude. Therefore, Kevin chose the name The Dude for himself. Doozer and The Dude later on made themselves a tag team that they called The Bo-Sox Connection, after the name of their favorite tag team between their two favorite wrestlers, The Rock and Mankind of The Rock and Sock Connection. The Bo-Sox obviously stood for The Boston Red Sox. They got creative with their move's names as well. Doozer, who made up his very own move (a Butterfly DDT using his legs to underhook his opponents arms) called it The Green Monster. Yet, he also had another high impact move that had to be his finisher, called The Pesky Poll (named after the Poll in the right field of the Red Sox park). The Dude, who was considerably smaller than Doozer and a lot more agile, came up with great high flying moves that he could actually do and wasn't afraid of doing. He mimicked Jeff Hardy's style so closely that it was a little scary. The Whisper in the Wind, by Hardy, was revamped a little by The Dude and nicknamed The Dudacious Dive. At the age of nine-teen and seven-teen years old. Doozer and The Dude found themselves ready for the real wrestling world. You know as good as the next man, that wrestling schools cost money. Yet, from the first day the beer guys, peanut girls, and janitors saw little Scotty and Kev wrestling, they knew it would happen. Therefore, they saved up every last extra penny they had. By the time Doozer and The Dude wanted to go, the crew presented the two with a suitcase full of money. Doozer and The Dude were surprised, to say the least. It made it harder for them to leave, but they eventually did. It wasn't long in wrestling school before the two were scouted by uTw staffers. The two signed the contracts proposed to them with so much excitement, they didn't care how much money they were making. Yet, they got big enough that they ended up making a good share of money anyway. In the uTw, Doozer was presented with a stables title debut match. It was a six man, frozen hell in a cell match. Doozer, The Dude, and Ice Solid fought against The Hardcore Militia. Doozer eliminated the first man. After him, The Dude was gone. Then Doozer eliminated a second Militia member. It looked like the titles were in the hands of Doozer's team, until Ice Solid tried to eliminate the last member. That made Doozer angry, and he ended up giving the Green Monster to Ice Solid, and eliminating him. This gave the last member of the Militia to gain some power, but it didn't matter. Doozer ended up giving him a Green Monster and eliminating him as well. Doozer was already a confident wrestler, and after starting his career on a good winning streak, he became nothing short of cocky. Yet, he started as a face as well. Anyway, after entering a tournament in the uTw for the Canadian Title, and getting to the final round to face a woman, Doozer was getting confident. Yet, the woman known as The Dark Angel (before the show came out) defeated Doozer. Doozer didn't shrug this loss off, he actually tried befriending such a superior woman who defeated himself (in his mind). It wasn't much longer and Doozer was granted a World Title shot in the federation. He was beating the World Champion badly. Yet, the owner of the federation as well as many other heels of the fed, came down and beat Doozer to a pulp. After the match, the world champ himself admitted that Doozer should have won. Doozer was furious, but granted a rematch. Yet, only to be screwed again. The federation then closed down and Doozer and The Dude were jobless. The Dude, during his stay at uTw, realised that wrestling wasn't exactly for him. Yet, managing a wrestler was. Even though Doozer was considerably smarter and more mature than The Dude, he agreed to let him manage him. The two then, while searching for a federation, found the mother of all federations at the time. They found the Dream Wrestling Federation. Doozer talked with the owner, and was let into the federation. There, he went on a seven match winning streak, winning the Novice and People's titles while doing it. He lost his first match, defending his People's Title, due to interference of his future long time rival, The Big Shot who Doozer ended up gonig udefeated against with three wins in singles competition and undefeated with two wins in tag team. And all five of those matches had titles on the line. Two of the singles with the Hardcore, Doozer winning the first from Big Shot and defending against him later. The third singles with the Dream title on the line, Doozer winning it for the first time out of two. The two tag team matches had the tag titles on the line, Doozer and Mike Robinson (The Emindee Connection) winning and defending. In the Dream Wrestling Federation, staying the fed for one year... one month.. and one day exactly, Doozer won every title at least once, the majority of them twice including the world. For that he became the federations only Grand Slam Champion. He also broke and still holds many records in the federation. To add to his glory in DWF, when he won the Dream Title for the second time (only the 3rd in DWF history to do so), he retired to be immediately placed in a Hall of Fame spot that had his name on for quite some time after holding a #1 spot in the rankings for longer than anybody else had been able to do. Yet, the Dream Wrestling Federation eventually closed down and Doozer was without a job again. He went onto another huge federation at the time, the IWO. There he went seven and two while winning the Extreme Title. Yet, he left that fed in search for another. He ultimately found Legends Of Wrestling. He became the first LoW Tag Team Champions with partner Jason Winters. But, Doozer wanted something else. Doozer wanted to be in a federation that he knew would bring him much competition. Doozer wanted to fight hard fought matches and show everyone that he can beat the best. That is what brought Doozer, along with The Dude, and his champion partner from LoW, Jason Winters to Epic Wrestling...
