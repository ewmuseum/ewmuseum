Personality Information Unpredictable, Untrustworthy, and Unafraid. The Legacy of Ben Archer can be described by these 3 words. With no specific "style" of wrestling assigned to him, Ben Archer is without a doubt one of the most well-rounded guys ever to grace the ring. Archer can Brawl, Shootfight, and Wrestle when the occasion arises and whatever he lacks in technique he makes up with sheer luck. Fans all over the world have come to respect Archer for his "No Bullsh*t" attitude towards life. Not once has Archer backed down from a challenge or made false promises when it comes to fighting. Archer's in-ring persona is one that changes with his anger level. If he is wrestling a regular match, Archer is focused and unusually calm. However, If Archer has pure hatred towards his opponent, Archer is a crazed animal. No longer is he the calm and focused wrestler, he is the all-out mad man that is only concerned about hurting people. This is Archer's "Achilles Heel" if you will. Archer has a tendency to not listen to authority or abide by the rules during one of his infamous "rages". Even if Archer could win a title in a match, the title would not bother him....only the pain and suffering of his opponent would. Outside the ring, Archer is nice, kind, and heart-warming person. He gives up his free time to chill with the fans and promote shows. It has been said that Archer has a "split personality" because of his total change of character inside the ring. Over the years, wear and tear on Archer's body has left him in pretty bad shape. His right ankle and left elbow are constantly giving him trouble and have almost cost him his career on numerous occasions. But no matter, Archer continues to be one of the most respected and influential guys in the business today. Armed with nothing more than a great knowledge of the game, Archer is ready for anything. Historical Information Epic Titles Won: None Epic Awards Won: None Past Promotions: Past Titles: Past Awards/Accomplishments: |Biography| Ben Archer was born just outside of London, England. When he was 6 he moved to the Midwest and that's where he lived until he was 14. On January 22nd just after his 14th birthday, Archer was forced to move to America. He ended up living in Ames, Iowa where he lived a life of hate. Hatred for his family, hatred for his friends, and hatred for life itself. For 5 years he only looked out for himself and no one else. One day he saw a advertisement for a Pro Wrestling school. There he met one of his greatest friends ever, Chris Mezz. Archer and Mezz worked as hard as they could to be the very best in the School. After the School, Archer and Mezz went their separate ways. Archer wrestled in numerous federations such as the RPL, EwA, and WeWF. One Day Archer was contacted by Ralph Richardson and was offered a contract in the UcWF. Little did Archer know that this federation would spark a great Wrestling Career. There he wrestled along side wrestlers like Shawn Arrows, Ice Bullet, Masked Man, Steve Cash, and Chris Mezz. After 2 years Archer decided to leave the UcWF and pursue other organizations. Federations such as the WSW, SWO, and NGW just weren't good enough for Archer and so he applied to IWO. In his short stint in the federation, Archer won the Television Title and went on to defeat DWF Hall of Famer, Doozer to win the Major Push Tournament 2.0. Alongside old friend, Shawn Arrows they dominated the Legendary Tag Team known as Team V.I.A.G.R.A. Despite the accomplishment, the Booking Team refused to acknowledge them as a tag team. In an attempt to find greener pastures, Archer went to join the CWF. It was a family generally owned by those of Arrows blood and Archer thought that he just might have found a permanent home. However, the new owner, Jessica Jenkins wasn't quite the smartest of people. She signed up numerous big time stars. Money became so scarce, getting fireworks for the shows became a hassle. The company soon filed for bankruptcy and was bought out by Epic Wrestling. Ben, aware of the company's successful past, hopes to reach the desired status of legendary wrestlers who once graced the company such as Wayne Carter and "Suicidal" Cameron Wilson.
