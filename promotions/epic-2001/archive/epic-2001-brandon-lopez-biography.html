Personality Information Brandon Lopez is your typical rookie He's new to the ring, and new to big feds of course, which doesn't gain him much respect from his peers. Brandon's one big difference from most other rookies is that he doesn't believe he's the best, and knows he's nowhere near it. He doesn't claim to be better than he really is. Brandon can't move with most of the wrestlers that enter the ring because of his size, but his power easily makes up for it. He doesn't usually use wrestling submissions and never uses any arial moves. He doesn't need them. He can brawl with the best of them, and his power moves are unmatched by most men. Historical Information Epic Titles Won: None Epic Awards Won: None Past Promotions: Past Titles: Past Awards/Accomplishments: |Biography| Brandon Lopez is the younger brother of wrestling superstar, Carlos Lopez. Carlos's history in wrestling has been well documented over the years, thanks to all of his wars, but Brandon is a different story, never really stepping into the ring except for on certain occasions. Epic Wrestling is the first real step in Brandon's career, and he hopes to quickly make a name for himself. Brandon may be a young man, but he's been working out every day, and has been training with his brother Carlos, learning the basics of Power Moves. With his La Bomba de la Perra powerbomb finisher, you can be sure Brandon's going to be a force to be reckoned with.
