JCWF Shadow Interview

23 September 1994
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
This is Gary Adams live from Tokyo--I've been invited here to interview Shadow, an entry to wrestle in the JCWF. But let me tell you, gentlemen and ladies, this place is abandoned and I don't see any sign of Shadow. (Tall man in bright white robes is suddenly standing next to Gary) Shadow:I am here, of coarse, Mr. Gary Abrams. GA:That's Gary Adams. Shadow:Your name does not matter-we are here to speak of me, is that not so? GA:You are right, Shadow. You have entered the JCWF-and so far you are the third competitor-Doctor Feelgood and Punjab the Mad Arab have already announced their entry. What do you think of the competition so far? Shadow:Nothing-I do not consider them to be competition. GA:Doctor Feelgood is a former champion in the U.S.-he stands 6'7" and weighs in at 285 pounds. Surely you have to respect a man of that size and those credentials! Shadow:He is nothing. Americans are weak and lazy, and to excel in America is to be less than adequate here in Japan. I have examined tapes of his fighting-he is nothing to one such as myself. GA:Punjab the Mad Arab-he is fast and a master of a number of maneuvers-what about him? Shadow:He is a master? In my school, the most meager student is greater than he. His great speed is the speed of a slug, moving through the winds of a Typhoon. GA:Well, obviously you consider yourself the top contender for the Japanese World Championship. Shadow:I consider that belt mine already. It is a matter of time-the matches must be signed and fought. I will not bother myself with the Broadcast Title, or with the All Japan Title, I will have the World Title at once. GA:We'll see about that, Shadow-you do have to fight, and WIN the matches. Shadow:Correct. I will make this challenge. Doctor Feelgood or Punjab the Mad Arab, the first of you to accept I will challenge to a match at once. Make it the first match on the first JCWF card. I will be the victor-will the cowards respond? (Shadow is gone) GA:Well, what??!! Oh man, this guy is a spook! He's here, he's not, what is with Shadow? You heard it, Doctor Feelgood and Punjab--you are challenged. I doubt it will be as easy as Shadow thinks, but I think we'll soon find out! So long! -- [detail withheld] Public Access User --- Not affiliated with Teleport Public Access UNIX and Internet at (503) 220-1016 (2400-14400, N81)
