Attention E-Mail Wrestling Admins...

7 December 1993
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Howdy. Since this fantasy wrestling thing is a topic again, I thought I'd take the time to make this little post. This is directed at all the e-mail wrestling game admins out there. Would all of you kindly get together and send me a small 5-10 sentence paragraph describing your league? I'm going to revise and repost the "Guide To E-Mail Wrestling Game" informational post to here and rec.games.pbm. I don't rightly care what information you intend to include to your paragraph, but I would like to see a listing of all "titles" available in your league included somewhere. Also, and this is purely for organisational purposes only, I would like to know the approximate month and year in which each of the respective leagues started up. Example: GCW started in September 1992. If any of the admins cannot read this post, would one of you other admins kindly pass this posting along to him? UeWF guys... get together on one paragraph instead of sending me three different ones. Pete. ps, Quite frankly, I really don't care if anyone posts my phone number. I'm never home, and when I am, I have call tracing--I know who you are before I pick up the phone... on the other hand, if someone does feel the need to post my phone number (ie, some vindicator at an unnamed university in Pennsylvania) and some of you call with sincere requests to talk, by all means, do so. Oxford is boring. The only reason I feel the need to say this is because it would surprise me about this much (holding thumb and forefinger about a millimetre apart) if one of CMU's now-infamous "D-uh Posse" feels the need to flame this posting. Thank you, and good night.
