FWF: Flawless vs. Flash

25 April 1992
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Once again, I will remind my readers of the survey going out. Give me your top three choices for the following awards in the FWF this year . . . Match of the Year Best Face (i.e. the one you most enjoyed reading about) Best Heel (ditto) Tag Team of the Year Wrestler of the Year Dream Match (the winner of this one may well be featured at or before DC6) And so, on the heels of an I-C Title squash by the "Iron Warrior" Blue Steele, I bring you the debut of "Flash" Johnathon Flann, in a match up against former World and U.S. Champion, John "Flawless" Lawless. Comments from both individuals . . . Flash: "It's great to finally make it to the major leagues in the FWF. John Lawless, they say you're a bad@ss wrestler, but today we're going to see if you can top the high-speed, high-flying aerial attack of the Flash!" Lawless: "Where does the FWF get off thinking that some fool like this is going to give John Lawless a serious match? Everyone knows that I'm going to win the Wrestler of the Year award when the survey results come out! Look at the pinfalls I have over some people! Blue Steele, you're on that list, and you know that that Intercontinental Belt is going to be around your waist only as long as it takes 'Flawless' Lawless to get a shot at it. Why? Because if you've got flaws, I've got your number!" --------------------------------------------------------------------------- Alfred Grinchley glances around at the men, women and children who have filled the Portland Memorial Coliseum, home of the NBA Pacific Division titlists two years running, the Trail Blazers. He glances at the man in the ring and begins his announcements for the afternoon. "THE FOLLOWING CONTEST IS SCHEDULED FOR ONE FALL. CURRENTLY IN THE RING, FROM MINNEAPOLIS, MINNESOTA. HE WEIGHS TWO HUNDRED AND TWENTY-THREE POUNDS: HERE IS THE 'FLASH', JOHNATHON FLANN!!" The man with the orange-and-black face paint with lightning crisscrossed over him looks out at the aisle with blue eyes, his pale red hair reaching his shoulders in length. He is a small man, to be sure, and has waited a long time to compete in the FWF. Only the former U.S. Champion, Damien Flayer, is smaller than he amongst the FWF roster. But the Flash is not discouraged by his small size, for Flayer is the only man to have won the United States Heavyweight Championship twice. If a man smaller than he can do it, so can he, in his mind. He sucks in his breath as "Exodus" rolls over the PA system. His opponent seems to have changed his taste in entrance music, and the arrogance of the tune is a tangible thing. As the man with short brown hair in the bright red robe parts the ring curtains, the Grinch continues his work. "AND HIS OPPONENT, COMING DOWN THE AISLE. FROM SEATTLE, WASHINGTON; HE WEIGHS TWO HUNDRED AND SIXTY-EIGHT POUNDS: JOHN 'FLAWLESS' LAWLESS!!!" His azure eyes sparkle with confidence, his hands lightly carrying the infamous "Wrestler Be Cool" stick that has carried him to a screwjob victory time and again before in his career. There is a logical reason for him to keep it, however: when he was in preliminary wrestling, one of his opponents struck him with a set of brass knuckles. When he was U.S. Champion, a challenger once tried to hit him with the belt. He keeps the stick as an equalizer, for the most part, and usually the temptation to use it in a match is resistible. The letters "Utterly Flawless" are written in stylized cursive, silver glittery threads on the back of his robe, and the fans at ringside get a good view of the words as he stands on the ring apron, gesturing with the two-foot-long wooden stick as if it were a sceptre, to the referee. The official calmly walks over and parts the ropes. Lawless smiles, and leaps over them with no effort, darkening the visage of the referee. The marks boo him mercilessly, though the hardcores give a mixed reaction. Lawless has been known to mix it up on occasion, ad sometimes his tactics bely his superb technical skills. He is a former World Heavyweight Champion, and a former U.S. Champion. If he has his way, he soon will be the Intercontinental Champion, and thus the only "triple-crown" champ in the history of the Fictional Wrestling Federation thus far. He has pinned Blue Steele before, in defense of his U.S. Title. He has every belief that it will happen again. He parts his robe, to reveal semi-taut features, looking contemptuously down on his smaller foe. But he is doing it for intimidation, for he knows from first-hand experience what smaller men can do when they get their momentum going. He leaves the "Wrestler Be Cool" stick over in a neutral corner, and unlimbers his arms for the match. The bell sounds, and the contest is under way. Lawless starts to lock up with the Flash, but then backs off at the last moment. He walks over to the ropes and ducks under the top one, facing out and downward, waiting for the official to back his foe off. What actually happens is not to the Flawless One's liking. Flash runs over and leaps over the top rope, dropping a leg on the back of Lawless' outstretched head on his way to the floor! Lawless clutches at his throat, which was hit hard over the middle rope, and flops to the mat within. No sooner has the Flash hit the floor than he is back into the ring, the cheers of all in the Coliseum following him. Lawless crawls away, trying to escape from this half-suicidal opponent. The Flash gives him no quarter, and sends him across the ring in an Irish whip. Lawless grabs onto the top rope, stopping his rebound, and instead channels his momentum into slipping under the bottom rope to the outside. The Flash is immediately out after him, landing a drop kick through the middle ropes to the foe on the outside! Lawless slams against the security railing head- first, cutting him on the forehead. As his blood drips to the floor, he staggers along the strip of real estate outside, only to get hit with a moonsault from inside the ring by his maniacal foe. The Flash cups his hands to his lips and lets out a Tarzan-like call, which is echoed by a spectacular pop from the fans. The high-speed attack has Lawless off-balance and bloody early in the bout, leaving the Flawless One's prospects grim for the near future. Lawless desperately gouges at the Flash's eyes, trying to slow some of this down, getting back into the ring with some haste. Flann shakes his head, and makes his way back inside, only to be ambushed by his larger opponent. Lawless snatches him in a side headlock, only to be shoved away off the ropes. The larger man floors the Flash with a shoulder, then goes to the side. The Flash rolls under his foe, and snares him in a flying head scissors, sending him tumbling head over heels to the mat once more. The Flash goes for a cover. One! Two . . . And a half. Lawless kicks out, dripping blood over himself and his foe, and comes to his knees in the corner. "Noooooo!!" He shakes his head, sprinkling crimson onto the ropes, until the Flash comes close enough for him to deal out a rising knee to the midsection. "Flawless" Lawless stops a moment to catch his breath and a sense of direction, and snaps the Flash over the shoulder. Flash comes to a sitting position, only to have Lawless flip over him, catching his head in both hands, stretching out his spine in ways beyond any creator could have designed. Flash clutches at his neck, coming up to his knees, only to have the move repeated in reverse, bending him back double. Lawless rises to his feet, stretching his hands out, shouting "Flawless!" to the fans. With the blood still coursing down his face, the gesture seems somewhat hollow. Lawless whips Flash across the ring, trying for a drop kick, but Flash is too quick for him, and he flops from six feet up in the air onto the back of his head. The Flash turns himself to a run to the side ropes (from Lawless' perspective) and drops an elbow to the sternum. Lawless rolls away, trying to get out of the ring again. The Flash is after him like a shot and hits him with a hard right, knocking him into the corner. Lawless blocks the next right forthcoming, and turns Flash around into the buckles. A swift backhand chop stuns the smaller man, followed by an uppercut to the jaw. The Flash slumps in the corner, but only until Lawless pulls him out with two handfuls of hair. Lawless makes sure Flash stays standing, then holds him out at one arm's length. He leaps up, and uncoils the full length of his legs right in the Flash's face, in a standing drop kick that shoots smaller man outside the ring with the force of the impact. Lawless is not interested in following. The Flash stands, losing his balance a moment, having to steady himself on the ring apron. Lawless is after him quickly, stomping him loose from the inside until he falls to the mat outside. The official backs the heel away and warns him not to interfere with the Flash's reentry. As Flash slides under the bottom rope, a murmur from the aisle arises. But Lawless ignores it, and sends the smaller man to the ropes. He sets a few moments too early, ducking down, and the Flash turns him over in a swinging neckbreaker before he realizes what is going on. Lawless grabs at the base of his skull, as the Flash points skyward, signalling for the end. He sets Lawless up on the turnbuckle, and leaps up after him, catching him in another head scissors, aiming to Frankensteiner him out of the corner! But Lawless catches the top rope, and when Flash pulls back, he only topples out alone, hitting the back of his head in the middle of the ring. But the hurt isn't for long, as he gets back to his feet. Lawless stands on the top rope and hits him with a flying elbow smash, sending him back to the canvas. Soon after, he whips the Flash into the ropes, and catches him on the rebound in a spine buster, slamming him hard into the mat! He turns to the fans to announce that a Perfect Plex is following, but then he sees what has stirred the fans in the aisle. Coming up the way, as cold and emotionless as ever, is three hundred and thirty-four pounds of motor- cycle clad, shades-veiled Terminator! Lawless' eyes widen in horror, as the huge android steps over the top rope and a huge forearm floors the Flawless One! The official screams for the bell, but Zahorian Systems Model 101, Series 800 cares nothing for the rules at this point. The spectators remember Lawless' pinfall defeat of the Ter- minator in their last encounter, and surmise that vengeance is the motive here. -Target acquired. Cursory evaluation complete. Probability of enemy success: 16.5%.- The Terminator seizes Lawless with two ebon-gloved hands, and hefts him over his head in a gorilla press! He holds him for ten seconds, to the awe of the fans, before sending him onto his back in the middle of the ring. He turns his sunglasses a bit on his face, looking over at the Flash, who stands in awe of the gigantic man who has interceded in this match. A moment longer, and the Flash leaves in a flash. The Terminator turns back to Lawless, and sets him up on the top turnbuckle, much as Flann did a moment before. He catches him there in a front facelock, mounting the second rope, letting the clarion call out: "Hasta la vista . . . baby." He drapes Lawless' arm over his head, and hurls him up out of the corner, letting him fall to the mat from about sixteen feet up in the air in a spine- crushing superplex! Lawless arches his back a moment, then is motionless in the center of the ring. Terminator looks down on him coldly, then announces his analysis. "Terminated." The android assassin steps back over the top rope and back down the aisle to the dressing room, not having even broken a sweat in the brutal assault. -Mission to ensure Lawless' victory accomplished. Return to rendezvous.- As the Terminator leaves the ring area, the Grinch finds his voice and announces the match result. "THE WINNER OF THIS BOUT, AS THE RESULT OF A DISQUALIFICATION: JOHN 'FLAWLESS' LAWLESS!" The official raises Lawless' arm, and it drops like a rubbery noodle. The EMT's make their way into the ring with a stretcher, for no way is this bloodied individual making his way out under his own power. --------------------------------------------------------------------------- And so, for reasons unknown, the Terminator ostensibly gets his revenge- or was he trying to help Lawless in some twisted way? On the tail end of this mystery, there are still matches to come from the FWF: "Rock" Steve Rockwell takes on his former tag team partner, "Scorpion" Stan Golding. "Crimson" Joe Reed defends the U.S. Title against Adrian "the Mauler" Romulus.
