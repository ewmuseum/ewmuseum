The result, 25 December 1989

This page stands in for a document posted to news.announce.newgroups on 25 December 1989 under the heading “rec.sport.pro-wrestling squeaks by!” It closed out the founding vote that began with a Call For Votes on 22 November 1989 and passed through a mid-count checkpoint on 4 December — itself replaced by a companion page in this wing — posting the vote-taker's final tally and declaring the newsgroup's creation. In place of the original document's two full lists of named yes and no voters, the museum serves the counts and the process below.

The final tally, as independently re-counted from the raw article: 159 votes for the newsgroup, 48 against. The post states this margin clears both a 100-vote minimum-difference requirement and the roughly 2:1 yes/no ratio rule, the latter noted in the post itself as a convention not yet formally in effect when this particular vote was called. The vote-taker writes of having examined the list and found nothing irregular enough to affect the outcome, adding the standing caveat that “as a vote taker, I can only trust the pieces of email I receive.” Some no votes are described in the post as objecting to the newsgroup's proposed name; one is described as objecting to professional wrestling as a subject for the net at all, rather than for the alt hierarchy.

Barring serious objection, the post announced the vote-taker would issue the newgroup for rec.sport.pro-wrestling on 27 December 1989 — and the group followed within days, the announcement itself carried through news.announce.newgroups under the same moderator approval as the rest of the process.

The 207 names on the yes and no lists are not served here. Curation rule v109(d) holds documents that are substantially lists of real names back from serving by name by default, and this result post, alongside the 4 December checkpoint, belongs to that class: the fact of the vote, its date, its tally, and its rules belong on the wing's public record; the individual voters do not. The original document, unedited, with every name and address intact, remains collected in the workshop for anyone doing primary-source research into the newsgroup's founding.
