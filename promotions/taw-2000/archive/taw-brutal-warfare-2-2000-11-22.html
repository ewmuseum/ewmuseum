TAW Brutal Warfare #2

22 November 2000
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
TAW Brutal Warfare #2 November 21, 2000 ------------- ..[ Loser Leaves Town match ].. Samael vs. Shiatsu American Psycho vs. The Count ..[ Tag Team Titles match ].. Old School Alliance vs. Extreme Order ..[ BC Title match ].. Jason Jarrett vs. Cobra vs. Ewan Milton ------------- ::After a few commercials, the screen goes black and a logo reading 'VTV' is displayed in the lower right corner. An audio/video feed of TAW wrestling then fades in, showing the TAW arena full of screaming Vancouver wrestling fans. Inside the ring stands newly-hired TAW ring announcer, Darren Vinson.
Darren wears a black tuxedo and jacket, with shiny black shoes and a black tie. He is all decked out for this first ever TAW television broadcast for
sure.:: Darren Vinson: "All rise for the playing of the Canadian National Anthem." ::The fans raise up out of their seats as the national anthem begins to play. After about 30 seconds, the song cuts off and the crowd begins to boo.
'Pour Some Sugar on Me' by Def Leppard begins to play, and the fans continue to boo. Two wrestlers, identified by a name-plate across the screen as the
Old School Alliance, walk down the entrance ramp to the ring. One of them wears a black Slayer t-shirt and red wrestling tights that says 'Iron Mike'
down each leg, and the other wears a pair of cut-off jeans, made into shorts. The one in shorts has his face painted black-and-white, and wears
Road Warriors-style elbow pads with spikes. Closely behind the two men is a beautiful blonde woman wearing a red skirt and a white tube-top. The three of them walk to the ring amid the crowd's boos, and get inside. Iron Mike snatches the microphone from Darren Vinson's hand, and tells him to get out of the ring. The fans begin to quiet down, and Iron Mike begins to speak.::
Iron Mike: "Fans of the TAW, I'd like to introduce you to the newest wrestling superstars in this promotion. Tonight in the first of two main events, you shall see the team known as Extreme Order take on the two of us, the Old School Alliance. Along with the lovely Sara Wyld, you can expect to see my partner Mr. Roboto and I win the TAW Tag Team Titles tonight, the Old
School way. By the way, you can call me Iron Mike!"
::Iron Mike the tosses the microphone to his partner, who continues where
Mike left off.:: Mr. Roboto: "I know what you may be thinking. What kind of name is Mr. Roboto for a wrestler? True, it's a bit unusual, but it's to make a point. Mike and myself are fans of the way things were in the Eighties, especially the way things were in wrestling. Back then everyone wasn't as concerned with titles and being a big name star like they are today. Sure, the wrestlers then wanted fame and glory, but they were more interested in pleasing the crowd. Back then everyone was some kind of comic-book character, or a super-hero, or even a dancing chicken. Nowadays all the wrestlers use their real names, and are just wrestling for the titles. That'
s no fun... no fun at all! So Mike and myself have come here to TAW to bring back the old school ways. You won't see us doing the hardcore crap everyone does these days, you won't see us in some sex-driven storyline, and you won'
t see us fighting each other for #1 contender-ship for a singles title. We are here to please the crowd as a tag team, and that's just what we shall do. That's not to say we _like_ you Canadian scum, but we _are_ here to do our jobs, and that is to put on a good show for you. Now if you'll excuse us, we need to prepare for tonight."
::Their entrance music plays again, and the Old School Alliance leaves the arena amid more boos from the crowd. The view then switches to inside the commentator's booth, which is on the second floor of the warehouse, on the side to the left of the entrance ramp. 'Big D' and Brian Lane are looking down on the Old School Alliance as they exit the area.::
'Big D': "Well, you can sure tell that the Canadian fans don't like _those_ guys!" Brian Lane: "You got that right, D. I sure thought they were cool, though." 'Big D': "I figured you would. Folks, we have quite an event for you tonight. Thanks for joining us here on VTV for the first ever televised broadcast of TAW Brutal Warfare."
Brian Lane: "Yeah, thanks for watching, now get off your couch and buy tickets for next week's show!"
'Big D': "To bring you all up to speed on last week's non-televised event, here is basically what happened. We had the first round of the TAW British
Columbian Title tournament. Jason Jarrett, Ewan Milton and Cobra all won their round one matches, so they meet tonight in our second of two main events. The winner of tonight's match walks away the new TAW BC champion."
Brian Lane: "That is absolutely right, and we also have that Tag Team Titles match tonight, plus a match between American Psycho and The Count."
'Big D': "We'll get to those later, but now let's go to Darren Vinson as he introduces the wrestlers for our first match."
*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^* Loser Leaves Town match Samael vs. Shiatsu *^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^* Darren Vinson: "Ladies and Gentlemen, introducing first, from parts unknown. Weighing in at 208 pounds, Samael!!!" ::The lights go out and 'Forever Fallen' by Bolt Thrower begins to play. Red spotlights light up the stage, and Samael comes out of the back with his head down. He walks toward the ring, and once there his music goes off and he slides under the bottom rope. He makes no notice of Darren Vinson in the ring with him as he walks to a turnbuckle and sits on top of it, with his eyes toward the entrance ramp.::
Darren Vinson: "And his opponent, hailing from the country of China and weighing in at 265 pounds. The mysterious man, myth and legend, Shiatsu!!!"
::'Ice Ice Baby' by Vanilla Ice begins to play, and the fans jump up and cheer. Shiatsu comes out of the back with hands raised, as he jumps around on stage. He gives a few fans at ring side a high-five, and then runs down to the ring, sliding in under the top rope. Shiatsu jumps around with his fists out, taunting Samael, and Darren Vinson gets out of the ring. The bell then dings and the match begins.::
'Big D': "Shiatsu really has the fan's support out there! Hopefully he can use that to his advantage in this match, because the loser is going to be released from their TAW contract."
Brian Lane: "What if they don't _want_ to be released?" 'Big D': "Good question, Brian. Whenever someone signs with TAW wrestling they have a two week probationary period, during which time they can be released without the company having to keep up their end of the contract agreement. TAW is free to release any wrestler for any reason during that wrestler's first two weeks on the roster."
Brian Lane: "Oh... now I feel stupid." 'Big D': "You _are_ stupid, Brian." Brian Lane: "Shutup before I drag _you_ in the ring and start my own wrestling match!" ::Inside the ring, Samael is getting a royal ass-kicking at the hands of
Shiatsu. Shiatsu grabs Samael by the arms and Irish Whips him into the corner. He then gets a running start and Splashes him against the turnbuckle. Samael falls face-first to the mat, giving Shiatsu the opportunity to get on the second rope and Elbow Drop Samael on the back.
Shiatsu then pulls his smaller opponent up and picks him up in a Press Slam maneuver. He walks over to the ropes and drops Samael neck-first onto the top rope.::
Brian Lane: "Whoa, I bet that was painful!" ::Samael is back on his feet and the two wrestlers lock-up. They exchange punches and Samael eventually gets the upper-hand. He punches Shiatsu in the gut and then hits a Bulldog. Shiatsu rolls onto his back with a hand on his face, and Samael attempts to get him into a Figure Four Leglock. Shiatsu kicks Samael away, toward the ropes. Samael bounces off the ropes and tries to Clothesline Shiatsu as he gets up. Shiatsu ducks the move, however, and grabs Samael from behind. He gets an arm around his neck and executes an
Inverted DDT. Shiatsu then pulls Samael back up and whips him off the ropes.
On the return, Shiatsu hits him with a Running Lariat, sending Samael down hard onto the mat. Samael pops right back up onto his feet and kicks Shiatsu in the shin. He then grabs him by the neck and whips him over the turnbuckle to the outside. Samael uses the ropes as a springboard, and jumps over the ropes, nailing the fallen Shiatsu with a Splash on the outside. The two of them get up and exchange punches before Shiatsu grabs Samael by the head and rams him face-first into a turnbuckle. He then pulls him back up and rolls him into the ring before climbing back in himself.::
'Big D': "This is one hell of a great fight here. I suppose this _is_ for their TAW contract, so both of these guys is going to give it their very best to the finish. Whoever wins this match will certainly have earned the right to remain in TAW."
Brian Lane: "Either that, or the winner will be the one who is just a bit less of a wuss than the other guy."
'Big D': "Now what on earth makes you say that? Are you trying to say you don't think either of these guys is putting on a good show right now?"
Brian Lane: "That's exactly what I am saying. I'd rather be on the John reading about the Vancouver Canucks in the newspaper right now, than watching this snooze-fest!"
'Big D': "I can't believe you! Just so you know, the TAW President _does_ watch this show."
Brian Lane: "Your point being?" 'Big D': "My point is, you better watch what you say before you find yourself down in the ring fighting in a Loser Leaves Town match..."
::Again the two lock-up, and Shiatsu kicks his opponent in the gut. He executes a Sambo Suplex from an Indian Stepover. With Samael on the mat,
Shiatsu locks on a Fausforus TCF Submission. Samael tried to fight the hold at first, and then quickly taps out. The fans cheer loudly as Shiatsu releases the hold and raises his hands up in triumph.::
'Big D': "With the Flakang Submissive Flame, Shiatsu wins the match and will stay in TAW." Brian Lane: "See ya later, Samael. Good look out there, wherever you go. I never really liked Samael, but I might as well be nice to the guy if he's getting fired, eh?"
*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^* Winner: Shiatsu via. Flakang Submissive Flame *^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^* 'Big D': "That is very true, Brian. Folks, we'll be back after a short commercial break." *^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^* Weapons match American Psycho vs. The Count *^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^* ::The show returns from the commercial break, with the ring empty and the arena quiet. Darren Vinson stands in the ring again, microphone in hand. A crew-member at ringside nods to him, and he begins to speak.::
Darren Vinson: "Our next match is scheduled for one fall, and is a weapons match. Introducing first, from Great Falls, Montana, and weighing in at 225 pounds. He is, the American Psycho!!!"
::'American Psycho' by the Misfits begins to play, and out comes American
Psycho. He receives loud boos from the crowd as he walks down the entrance ramp and gets into the ring. He flashes an angry look at the crowd before leaning against a turnbuckle and facing the entry-way.::
Darren Vinson: "And his opponent, weighing 385 pounds, the former Prince of
Transylvania, The Count!!!"
::'Blind' by Korn begins to play, causing a mix of both cheers and boos in the arena. Despite the fact that The Count unknown wrestler in TAW, the music still causes a strong reaction from the excited, drunken crowd. Out steps The Count, wearing jeans, combat boots and long, black gloves. He points to someone in the crowd with a smile on his face, and walks down to the ring. Darren Vinson climbs out as the bell dings and the match begins.
At first the two of them look at each other, and walk about the ring. The
Count then lunges for A. Psycho and they lock up. The Count gets his smaller opponent into a Hammerlock, and pulls up with all his might. American Psycho then kicks him in the 'sweet spot', and breaks free of the hold. He then grabs Count by the arm and drops him face-first to the mat. They both roll up to their feet, and again lock-up. A. Psycho gets the upperhand this time, and Irish Whips his opponent into the ropes. He then walks over and tries to set him atop the turnbuckle. The Count punches him in the gut, and jumps to the mat. He then sets American Psycho on the turnbuckle and hits an excellent Superplex.::
Brian Lane: "Whoa, nice Superplex there by the Count! A. Psycho may have trouble getting up from that one."
'Big D': "Both of these wrestlers look in prime condition, and are sure to put on a great fight here."
Brian Lane: "Yes, but I am wondering when they plan to involve weapons, as this _is_ a weapons match..."
'Big D': "I'm a fan of non-hardcore wrestling myself, but I'm sure this match was booked to excite the crowd, so I'll just do my job and announce the match."
::After a few good Suplexes by The Count, the two wrestlers again lock-up. American Psycho kicks his larger opponent twice in the leg and then executes a Standing Dropkick. The Count falls over onto the mat, and A. Psycho pulls him back up. He punches The Count in the face, and then DDTs him back down to the mat. He then rolls out of the ring and goes under the ring apron to find some weapons. There is a banging noise under the ring, and American
Psycho falls to the ground. Someone or something then pushes him back out.::
Brian Lane: "What the hell? I think the ring just tried to eat American
Psycho, and spit him back out!"
'Big D': "I guess he didn't taste too good..." Brian Lane: "This is no time to joke, D! I seriously think this TAW ring is alive!" 'Big D': "You really are a moron, Brian. Obviously there is someone under there who attacked him, and pushed him back out. But I wonder who it could be?"
::All of a sudden a man rolls out from under the ring and begins to stomp on
American Psycho. He pulls him to his feet, and Vertical Suplexes him on the ring floor. He then slides under the bottom rope and gets to The Count just as he is getting to his feet. The mysterious attacker kicks The Count in the gut and then executes a Northern Lights DDT. He then gets on the mat and
Scissor Locks one of The Count's arms with his legs. The man then grabs the other arm and pulls back vigorously. The Count taps three times, and the referee calls for the bell.::
Brian Lane: "Who is this man that just ruined this great match-up? How dare him?" 'Big D': "My God... I think that's Steve Stone!" Brian Lane: "Steve Stone, eh? Who the hell is he?" 'Big D': "A new wrestler on the TAW roster... he's anti-hardcore, so I hear. This _must_ be him!" ::'Boom!' by Royce the 5'9" then begins to play, and the man, identified as
Steve Stone, leaves the ring area. The crowd boos loudly as he walks up the entrance ramp and disappears behind the curtain.::
*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^* Winner: No Contest *^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^* 'Big D': "Well, there is no winner of that last match. Hopefully the outcome of this next match is different, because the TAW Tag Team Titles are on the line."
Brian Lane: "Yes they are, D, and from what I can tell the Old School Alliance means business tonight. I'm sure they'll knock the Extreme Order silly in the ring."
'Big D': "They _did_ have some words earlier tonight to say about this match, but we'll just have to wait and see what happens. Let's go to Darren in the ring."
*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^* Tag Team Titles match Old School Alliance vs. Extreme Order *^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^* Darren Vinson: "This next match is for the TAW Tag Team championship titles. Introducing first, at a combined weight of 450 pounds, and led to the ring by Sara Wyld, they are the Old School Alliance!!!"
::'Pour Some Sugar on Me' by Def Leppard plays as the Old School Alliance makes their second entrance of the night. The fans boo as loud as they can, remembering the interruption made by the Old School Alliance earlier during the national anthem. Iron Mike, Mr. Roboto and Sara Wyld seem to ignore the boos as they walk with smiles on their faces down to the ring. Iron Mike gets inside the ring, while Sara Wyld and Mr. Roboto walk around to side opposite the entrance-ramp. Mr. Roboto gets up on the ring apron, and begins to talk with Iron Mike.::
Darren Vinson: "And their opponents, weighing in at a combined weight of 466 pounds, they are Mac Henri and Seth Dread, the Extreme Order!!!"
::'Keep Away' by Godsmack begins to play, and the response from the crowd is a mix of boos and cheers. From the back steps the Extreme Order. Dread and
Henri walk side-by-side down the entrance ramp, heads held high, and get into the ring. They high-five, and Mac Henri gets out onto the ring apron, leaving Seth Dread in to start the match against Iron Mike. Once Darren
Vinson is out of the ring the bell dings to signal the beginning of the
match.:: 'Big D': "So I assume you're going to go with the Old School Alliance as the winners in this match, right Brian?"
Brain Lane: "You're damn skippy I am!" 'Big D': "Alright, well in that case I'll bet on Extreme Order." Brian Lane: "Wait, we're betting now? How much you got on it? I think I got a few Loons in my pockets, and maybe some Washingtons from America, too..."
'Big D': "No, you can keep your money, Canadian or otherwise. What I meant was I'll predict Extreme Order to win the match, not bet as in place money on them winning."
::Mike starts off by grabbing Dread by the arm and getting him into a Hammerlock Submission. He then kicks Dread in the back, knocking him down to one knee. Seth Dread gets back to his feet, and Iron Mike locks-up with him.
Mike Irish Whips Dread toward the ropes, and executes a Back Body Drop on the return. He then pulls Dread up and does a Scoop Slam. Dread gets up again, this time on his own, and punches Mike in the gut. He then uses the ropes to propel him forward, knocking Mike over with a Running Lariat. Mike gets back up, only to meet a boot to his forehead from Dread. Dread then tags Henri in, and they double team Mike. The Whip him off the ropes, and execute a Double Throatslam on the return. Dread then gets out of the ring and Henri pulls Mike up. Iron Mike kicks Mac Henri in the shin, and then executes a DDT. He then tags in his partner, and goes back to work on Henri.
Mr. Roboto walks across the ring and Vertical Suplexes Dread into the ring. Meanwhile, Mike hits an Atomic Drop on Henri. Mr. Roboto whips Dread off the ropes, and Mike Spears him from the side, totally blindsiding him. Mike then gets into his corner of the ring and Dread rolls to the arena floor.::
'Big D': "So far both teams are doing a good job in the ring and are functioning as smart tag teams. Whoever wins this match will definitely have earned the Tag Team Titles."
Brian Lane: "I'm telling you, Iron Mike and Mister Roboto are gonna win this thing..." ::The action continues, as Mr. Roboto and Mac Henri are still the two legal men in the ring. Henri whips his opponent off the ropes and attempts a
Tilt-a-Whirl Backbreaker. Roboto disrupts the sequence just in time, and rolls to his feet. He then Spears Henri and begins to punch him repeatedly in the face. Eventually Roboto gets up and tags in Iron Mike. Mike gets in the ring and walks over to Dread. He pulls him up by the waist and executes a German Suplex. He then pulls Henri up again and whips him into a corner.
Mike walks over to that corner and hangs Henri upside down, with his feet caught in the top rope. He then tags in Roboto, and runs over to knock Dread off the ring apron. During this time, Mr. Roboto gets in the ring and charges toward Henri, cracking him in the head with a spot-on Baseball
Slide. Henri falls to the mat, and Roboto attempts a pin.::
1 . . . 2 . . 'Big D': "Kick-out!" Brian Lane: "So close, Roboto, so close. One more good maneuver like that and you guys will have the win, and I get twenty bucks from Big D."
'Big D': "What?!? I told you we weren't betting money on this match, and we never _will_ bet money on a TAW match."
Brian Lane: "Calm down, I was joking around. Don't have a cow man, sheesh!" ::Back inside the ring, Roboto pulls Henri up. Much to his surprise, Henri is still full of energy, and Irish Whips Roboto in the corner. He then gets a running start and splashes him against the turnbuckle. Mr. Roboto stumbles a few steps forward, and Henri DDTs him onto the mat. Dread then slides back into the ring and nails Iron Mike in the head with a steal chair. He then walks over to Mr. Roboto, and motions for Mac Henri to get on the top rope.
Dread executes an Electric-Chair Drop on Roboto, and then Henri hits a Top
Rope Leg Drop. Henri rolls him up for the pin and the referee drops to the mat to begin the count.::
1 . . . 2 . . . 3!!! 'Big D': "Extreme Order wins this match, and are our new Tag Team champions. See, Brian, aren't you glad we didn't bet?" Brian Lane: "I had my twenty bucks on Extreme Order, remember? You were the one going for the other guys..."
'Big D': "I think you're mistaken. Anyway, fans, we'll be back with the second Main Event after this."
*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^* Winners: Extreme Order via. Death Row *^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^* ::After the commercial break, we open in the locker-room area. The camera is facing a door that is slightly ajar. Inside voices can be heard talking about something, and on the door is a crude sign that says 'Posted: Keep
Out!". The camera moves inside, and it is revealed as the private locker-room of the Old School Alliance. It is a small room, with only a couch, a love-seat and a desk with an office chair in one of the corners. On the desk sits a computer, a lamp, and a clutter of papers and magazines.
Iron Mike and Mr. Roboto sit on the couch, with Sara Wyld by herself on the love seat. A knocking sounds is heard at the door, and the camera turns toward it. TAW interviewer Lisa Johanson peaks her head in.::
Lisa Johanson: "Hi, are you guys busy? Can I come in?" Iron Mike: "Yes we are, but come in anyway. Is there something you needed, or were you just coming to join us for some milk and cookies?"
Lisa Johanson: "Umm... you guys asked for me to come to your room as soon as possible, remember? You two eat milk and cookies, eh?"
Mr. Roboto: "Damn right, we do! Milk and cookies are totally awesome!" Iron Mike: "Oh yes, I remember. I asked Bart to let you know we wanted to speak about our match just a few moments ago. While I won't deny that
Extreme Order won that match, they did so by cheating. According to TAW rules, using weapons in a match is not cheating, but as far as I am concerned it is a cheap way to knock your opponent senseless. Hell, I admit that was a good knock I received from that chair. Use of a chair isn't so bad, but I know that these Extreme Order guys are into the hardcore weapons match stuff. Earlier tonight I noticed that Steve Stone interrupted a weapons match, because he is against the sort of thing. It just so happens that Roboto and I are against it as well. So my proposition is simple. Steve
Stone, if you want to be a part of the Old School Alliance, so be it. If not, that is fine as well, but seeing as how we have the same views and possibly the same agenda, I think an agreement to help each other would be the least we should do. Just let us know if you want in, otherwise we'll make sure to stay out of your way and we'll give you help whenever you are in need."
Lisa Johanson: "Wow, what will Steve Stone say in reply to this offer? Big
D, Brian... back to you guys."
Brian Lane: "Steve Stone would make a great addition to the Old School
Alliance." 'Big D': "It's time for our main event..." *^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^* British Columbian Title match Jason Jarrett vs. Cobra vs. Ewan Milton *^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^* Darren Vinson: "Ladies and gentlemen, this main event match-up is for the
TAW British Columbian Title. Introducing first, from Austin, Texas and weighing in at 203 pounds, he is Jason Jarrett!!!"
::'Bad Boyz Anthem' by Shyne begins to play, and the crowd begins to boo. Jason Jarrett steps out from behind the curtain and slowly walks to the ring, paying no attention to the loud crowd around him. He climbs inside and
Darren Vinson continues the introductions.::
Darren Vinson: "And his opponent, from _Houston_, Texas, weighing in at 275 pounds, he is the man they call Cobra!!!"
::'Unleash the Dragon' by Sisqo plays and again the crowd boos. Cobra, who is attempting to be a babyface here in TAW will have a tough time doing so, as the TAW fans are not too big on hip-hop music. Cobra walks out from the back, arms raised in the air, and walks to the ring. He receives cheers from a few fans, but they are outnumbered by those who boo.::
Darren Vinson: "And their opponent, weighing in at 310 pounds, and coming from Glasgow Scotland, he is 'Stormy Weather' Ewan Milton!!!"
::'Bat out of Hell' by Meatloaf begins to play, and the majority of the fans cheer. Out from the back steps Ewan Milton, who walks briskly down to the ring. He climbs in and waits as Darren Vinson slips out and the ring bell begins. Cobra and Jason Jarrett both lunge at each other initially. They lock up and Milton watches as Cobra DDTs Jarrett. Jarrett then jumps back up and punches Cobra in the gut. He gives him an Uppercut to the face and then executes a Hurricanrana. Milton finally gets into the mix by Spearing
Jarrett as he works the crowd.:: 'Big D': "Whoa, cheap shot by Ewan Milton!" Brian Lane: "Remember, D, Jason Jarrett is going to win this thing because I say so." ::Jarrett then gets back up and he and Milton exchange punches. Cobra finally gets back to his feet, and all three of them punch and kick each other. Jarrett then grabs Cobra, whips him into the corner, and does a
Splash against the turnbuckle. Milton runs up behind Jarrett and hits a
Bulldog. Cobra gets up on the top rope, by Jarrett lunges for the ropes and knocks him down. Jarrett then gets to his feet and punches Cobra off the turnbuckle, to the ring floor outside. Milton and Jarrett lock-up and
Jarrett whips Milton into the far turnbuckle. He then goes outside the ring and picks up Cobra. He executes a Piledriver on Cobra and then gets back in the ring. Milton waits for Jarrett, and then they lock-up. A kick to the stomach followed by a Cradle DDT, and Milton is back on the mat. Milton knocks Jarrett's legs from under him and then gets up. He stomps on Jarrett'
s chest a few times, and then does a Leg Drop across the throat. Milton then gets Jarrett in a Wrist Lock and holds it as tight as possible. Jarrett taps out, but Milton continues the hold.::
Brian Lane: "Get off him, idiot! Ewan Milton eliminated Jason Jarrett vary unfairly here... but this is uncalled for!"
'Big D': "I'd have to agree, but he's just making sure Jarrett won't be able to interfere in the rest of this match."
