How to apply --------------------- *^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^* Background Information *^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^* > 1.1 - Where is it located? T&A Wrestling is a small independent wrestling promotion based in Vancouver, Canada. T&A Wrestling, or TAW if you prefer, runs out of a warehouse that was previously abandoned, which we were able to buy quite cheap. The warehouse is home not only to the offices of the head staff, but there is also the wrestling ring and such itself on the warehouse floor, and many rooms that were formerly offices have been converted into the backstage
locker-room area. Most all of the TAW events are held in this warehouse, known as the Arena of Aggression. This includes the events held each week, which will be described in detail later in this FAQ. The Arena of Aggression also serves as the training center for TAW wrestlers when no events are going on.
> 1.2 - Can you describe the Arena of Aggression in detail for me? Certainly! The Arena of Aggression includes the offices of the main staff in the front of the warehouse, which looks out onto Jefferson Drive, the street on which the Arena of Aggression is located. The driveway leads to the side of the warehouse, where there is parking for the fans, as well as the front entrance to the wrestling area.
Inside there is a wrestling ring directly in the centre of the warehouse floor. There is a guardrail around three sides of the ring, which leaves a gap of about six feet between the first row of seats and the ring itself. On the fourth side of the ring is a steel walkway, which raises up about six feet to a steel stage, with a television display on the wall and two speakers on each side, which are used for entrances, naturally. Also, to the left side of the stage is the wrestler entrance, covered by a black curtain.
This is where wrestlers will enter for their match 99% of the time. The ring itself is the exact same size as that which is used by Extreme Championship Wrestling of Philadelphia. Maybe you have seen them on television before, no? Anyway, there are four turnbuckles and three ring ropes, just like most other wrestling rings. The turnbuckles are standard black, the ropes are red, and the ring mat is white, with a red TAW logo on it. On each of the four ring aprons is a picture of a Canadian flag, and the words 'T&A Wrestling'.
Behind that black curtain mentioned earlier is a walkway that leads backstage to the locker-room area. This is where all TAW wrestlers get ready for their matches, and also where all backstage interviews will take place.
Placement of objects backstage will change day-to-day, so no further detail is needed here.
> 1.3 - What is the maximum capacity of the Arena of Aggression? Well, good question. T&A Wrestling is a small promotion, and as such it would be ridiculous to imagine a T&A Wrestling event happening in an arena the size of sports venues such as the BC Place Stadium in Vancouver or the
Toronto SkyDome. The Arena of Aggression is 1,330 sq. metres (approx. 14,112 sq. ft.), and can hold as many as 1,600 fans. Quite small, yes, but you must remember that this _is_ an indy-fed.
> 1.4 - Do you ever go on tour in other areas? Yes, T&A Wrestling will _occasionally_ tour around the areas of British Columbia as well as the states of Washington and Idaho, and put on shows in concert halls, bingo halls and large bars. Tour will be rather rare, but will occur every once in awhile. The purpose of these tours will be not only to make money from putting on shows at these venues, but also to gain a fan-base of people who do not live in the Vancouver area.
> 1.5 - What about lights/pyros? T&A Wrestling, as stated before, is a small promotion, and has an equally small budget. When on tour you can expect lighting provided by the venue in which the show is being put on, and nothing more. When doing shows at the Arena of Aggression, however, there will be a limited amount of effects that can be used. There will be spotlights and strobe lights of all colors, which can be used for the entrance of wrestlers. Also, if needed, wrestlers may have a small pyro effect go off on the stage. An example would be an explosion of red fireworks along the edges of the stage, or something to that effect. Explosions in the ring area and/or from the turnbuckles will not be allowed, however, because of safety precautions and because of rigging costs.
*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^* Rules and Regulations *^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^* It is expected that if you are here, you most likely have an idea of what e-wrestling is, and maybe are a well-trained and experienced veteran in the game. As such, the following rules are short and to the point. If you join T&A Wrestling, these rules should be followed at all times.
2.1 - How do you decide who wins? All matches are decided by the quality of the flashes by the wrestlers involved in the match. The one who puts more effort into it will almost always win the match, and will be notified before hand if there is a reason he/she shall lose the match (e.g. for the purposes of an angle). Quality is preferred over quantity in roleplays, but be sure to make them detailed and lengthy enough to defeat your opponent. No simulators or any of that crap is used, just good old fashioned flash rating.
2.2 - Do you allow profanity and such? Profanity in roleplays is absolutely acceptable. Also, as the characters are YOUR creations, you are free to use racism, anti-religion and discrimination to a small degree. Just don't go over-board and offend someone so much that the leave T&A Wrestling or contact our servers and get the site removed... if you do that, well, it's not too cool! TAW will not
_support_ these views, but if you wish your wrestler to do so, go right ahead. 2.3 - I like to whine, is that ok? Alright, so maybe nobody asked this. But I feel a need to address the subject anyway. If you feel like you should have won a match and didn't, deal with it. It's not the end of the world, ya know? Also, please don't ask repeatedly when results will be posted. If they are late, then that would be fine, but if not, don't ask! Not that TAW will feature late cards, of course, as I will be doing my best to get my work done and up on-time for everyone to read and enjoy.
2.4 - Can I book my own matches? Yes, if you request a match with another wrestler, it will almost always be booked. The Executive staff will also book matches when needed, but as all involved are in this to have fun, wrestlers are urged to challenge others for matches each week.
*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^* Executives and Staff *^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^* > 3.1 - Who is the main staff? The main staff, or Executives, are currently a team of only one. That's right, your webmaster and FAQ writer is also the President. Out of Character he is known as [name withheld], but in character he is known simply as 'KSonic'.
No first or last name has ever been given to the press or public, just KSonic. KSonic was formerly the President and CEO of a northern-California based indy-fed between the years of 1998 and 1999. The promotion he ran there was known as KSCW (KryptonicSonic's Championship Wrestling), and went through about three openings and closings during those two years, for various reasons. He has since re-located to British Columbia, Canada, and now runs
T&A Wrestling. > 3.2 - What are the names of the announcer(s)? Currently TAW has two announcers on staff. The first is a 23-year old man named 'Big D' David Vinson. David is a graduate of the University of
North Carolina, who has been a fan of professional wrestling since his early childhood. Never quite large enough to be an actual wrestler, he decided to get a broadcasting degree and get himself an announcing job with an indy-fed somewhere. Well, here he is, 'nuff said...
The second announcer is also 23-years old, and has been 'Big D's best friend since elementary school. His name is Brian Lane, a heel announcer different than the traditional heel announcers of old. These days, however, many heel wrestling announcers seem to be quite over with the fans, because they always drool over the women wrestlers/valets. That description fits
Brian to a tee, as he almost _never_ pays attention to the match going on if there is a hot looking female at ringside.
> 3.3 - Can you give me the names of the referees, interviewers, etc? Referee names? Pleeeaze! Interviewers I can name, though. First off, our head interviewer. Lisa Johanson is T&A Wrestling's head interviewer. This young, red-head bombshell is an expert at persuading wrestlers to have interviews, and also knows how to manipulate them to get all the 'juicy info' out of them that she can. She is a very skilled interviewer, and as such only does interviews backstage at the Arena of Aggression. She does not ever travel to off-site locations to do interviews.
John O'Malley is T&A Wrestling's interviewer sent to off-site locations to meet wrestlers. Off-site locations include bars, wrester's houses, or anywhere away from the Arena of Aggression. O'Malley is a tall and skinny
Irishman who isn't all that bright. He also thinks he is quite funny, cracking odd jokes on occasion that nobody else ever seems to understand or laugh at.
*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^* Championship Titles *^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^* > 4.1 - What titles can my wrestler win? The singles division currently has two championship titles. They are the Canadian and British Columbian Titles. Pretty basic, I know, but oh well! The Canadian Title is the top TAW title, and can be compared to the World Titles of international wrestling promotions. Whichever wrestler holds this title is automatically ranked #1 in the promotion, no matter what his win-loss record. The British Columbian Title can be compared to the
Television Titles of other wrestling promotions. It is a singles title that is second in prestige behind the Canadian Title.
> 4.2 - What titles are there for tag teams? Currently there is only one title for the tag team division. The name? Tag Team Titles, of course! They can be held by more than two people on a team (i.e. a 3 or 4-man team), and as such may be defended in 6-man and
8-man tag matches. > 4.3 - How often must these titles be defended? TAW will not have strict rules regarding how often titles must be defended, but there is a general time length between title defenses that will be enforced more often than not. The Canadian Title shall be defended at least once per month, the British Columbian Title at lease thrice, and the Tag Team Titles once every two weeks, or twice a month.
*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^* Wrestling Events *^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^* > 5.1 - What events happen each week? Each week there will be two events sent out and posted on the TAW website. Brutal Warfare will take place every Sunday night, and will be the event on which all wrestling matches take place. The show will be anywhere from thirty to ninety minutes long, depending on the size of the card. There will normally be between four and six matches on each Brutal Warfare card.
The second event of the week is the one on which all flashes shall be posted, unless they are to take place during the Brutal Warfare show, of course. This flashcard show will be known as Shittalk!, and will be sent out/posted on the website every Friday night. This way all wrestlers will have time to reply to the flashcard at the Brutal Warfare event. Shittalk!
will be hosted by one of TAW announcers, and will also feature a look at the last week's events and a preview of the upcoming Brutal Warfare card.
> 5.2 - Will any of these events be televised? Yes, both Brutal Warfare and Shittalk! will be taped by the TAW television crew, and syndicated to television stations across British
Columbia. Check your local listing for times and availability. > 5.3 - Will there be any supercards? At this point there are no planned TAW supercards, although that is a _definite_ possibility for the future. > 5.4 - What are the flash deadlines for these shows? Ah yes, flash deadlines. The deadline for flashes for the Shittalk! show will be Friday evenings at 6:00 PM Pacific Time. As for strats/in-rings/interviews for the Brutal Warfare card... those will be due by 5:00 PM Pacific Time on Sundays.
