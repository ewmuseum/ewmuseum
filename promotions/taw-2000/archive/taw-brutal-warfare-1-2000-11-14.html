TAW Brutal Warfare #1

14 November 2000
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
TAW Brutal Warfare #1 November 12, 2000 ------------- TAW British Columbian Title Tournament ..[ Round 1 ].. Samael vs. Jason Jarrett NightShade vs. Cobra Cyris Grisham vs. Ewan Milton ------------- ::We open to a house show taking place in the TAW Arena of Aggression. This is the first event for TAW, which has not yet begun on its television syndication deals, so this show is not being taped. We'll just pretend it's available on video from the TAW souvenir stand. Ok, so anyway, back to the show. Alright, the fans are yelling and screaming, awaiting the beginning of the inaugural TAW event. TAW Play-by-Play Commentator 'Big D' David Vinson and his Colour Commentating co-host Brian Lane are sitting at the announce both, also awaiting the beginning of the show. Finally, a voice comes over the PA system.::
# All rise for the playing of the Canadian National Anthem. ::The national anthem of Canada begins to play. Once the song is over, cheers come from the crowd, as this _is_ a Canadian wrestling promotion, and the lights go out. 'Forever Fallen' by Bolt Thrower begins to play, and red spotlights light up the stage. Samael comes out of the back with his head down. He walks toward the ring, and once there his music goes off and he slides under the bottom rope. 'Bad Boyz Anthem' by Shyne then begins to play, and out steps Jason Jarrett. Wearing his pants which fade down from black to silver, Jarrett slowly makes his way to the ring and climbs in.::
*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^* Samael vs. Jason Jarrett *^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^* 'Big D': "Tonight we've had to change a few matches and replace some wrestlers. Jason Jarrett, here wrestling Samael, is one of those additions."
Brian Lane: "This seems odd having wrestlers come out with no ring announcer. When do you suppose that position will be filled?"
'Big D': "Well, I couldn't tell you, Brian. I'm sure my brother Darren would be happy to take the job, but I haven't anything about a ring announcer for
TAW, so I really don't know when someone will take that position."
Brian Lane: "Right well, uhh... let's get this shit started." ::The ring bell dings, and the match begins. Samael springs toward Jason
Jarrett and attempts a Clothesline. Jarrett side-steps it and grabs his opponent by the back of the neck. He hits an Inverted DDT, and the two of them get back to their feet. They lock up, and Samael kicks Jarrett in the gut. He then attempts a Vertical Suplex, but Jarrett reverses it and executes a Waistlock Suplex. Jarrett quickly gets back to his feet and Elbow
Drops Samael. He then pulls him up and whips him into the ropes. Samael is dazed against the turnbuckle, and falls victim to a Splash by Jason Jarrett.
Jarrett then hangs Samael upside-down, and backs up across the ring. He begins to run toward the turnbuckle, and does a Baseball Slide, knocking
Samael's head against the turnbuckle.::
'Big D': "Good God, that was a hell of a Baseball Slide maneuver!" Brian Lane: "Jason Jarrett is officially my favorite TAW wrestler. Have you seen his tattoo?"
'Big D': "The one that says 'PIMP'? Brian Lane: "Yeah, that's it. Do you know what it means?" 'Big D': "I'd assume it means he is a pimp... like he gets a lot of girls." Brian Lane: "I'm sure he does, but that's not what it means. According to
Jarrett himself, the tattoo means Politically Incorrect Mother Phucker...
that's awesome!" ::Back inside the ring, Jarrett and Samael are still fighting back and forth. They lock up and exchange punches back and forth. Jarrett then gains the upper-hand and knocks Samael to one knee after several vicious punches to the chest. Jarrett then pulls Samael to his feet and does a Hurricanrana.
With his opponent down on the mat, Jarrett quickly scrambles up the nearest turnbuckle and waits for Samael to get back up. Once he does so, Jarrett executes XTC, one of his signature moves also known as a Buff Blockbuster.
The fans begin to cheer as Jarrett gets back up and executes his finisher.:: Brian Lane: "There it is, folks! Jarrett just hit Samael with Euphoria, his
Inverted Implant DDT!"
::Jarrett goes for a pin and the referee drops to the mat. He begins the count...:: 1 . . . 2 . . . 3! 'Big D': "There you have it folks. Jason Jarrett got the win, and will move on to the second round of this tournament next week."
Brian Lane: "I think he's gonna go all the way. This guy is amazing!" *^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^* Winner: Jason Jarrett via. Euphoria *^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^* 'Big D': "Okay, up next we have NightShade vs. Cobra. Any comments on these two, Brian?" Brian Lane: "Nope... I've never heard of either of them, and know nothing about them. Let's see how they do in the ring, eh?"
*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^* NightShade vs. Cobra *^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^* ::Once the fans settle down a bit from the last match, 'Smile' by Gorefest begins to play. Out from the back steps NightShade, dressed entirely in black. He slowly makes his way to the ring with an evil look on his face.
His music is replaced by Sisqo's 'Unleash the Dragon', and out comes Cobra. Cobra, who wears black pants held up by a black belt and silver belt buckle, walks briskly to the ring and gets up on the ring apron. He points at
NightShade and shakes his head before slipping in between the second and third ropes. The two wrestlers stare at each other as the bell dings and the match begins.::
Brian Lane: "Well, are they gonna wrestle or just stand there like idiots? Hurry the hell up!" 'Big D': "Calm down, Brian. It looks to me like they are sizing each other up before they begin to fight. They want to get an idea of how to go about this match."
Brian Lane: "Whatever... it looks to me like they have no idea what to do and are looking at each other for help."
::The two lock up, and exchange punches. NightShade then whips Cobra off the ropes, and readies for a Back Body Drop. Cobra slows himself down and kicks
NightShade in the face. He then grabs picks him up and does a Samoan Drop.
Both wrestlers get back to their feet, but a Spear by Cobra takes NightShade back down to the mat. Cobra then gets on the second rope and drops a knee to
NightShade's chest.::
Brian Lane: "Nice Knee Drop there, eh, D?" 'Big D': "While not a high-impact move, it still is sure to have caused major damage. So far Cobra seems to be in complete control of this match."
::Cobra rolls over and gets to his feet. NightShade slowly gets up as well, and spits on Cobra. This sets off a raging fury, causing Cobra to charge toward his opponent. NightShade moves out of the way, and Cobra runs right into the turnbuckle. He staggers backward and NightShade grabs him, executing a powerful Backbreaker. NightShade then slides out of the ring and grabs a chair. Cobra gets to his feet and beckons for NightShade to bring the chair in. NightShade slides under the bottom rope, but Cobra runs over and stomps on his back. NightShade lets go of the chair, and Cobra picks it up. NightShade gets to his feet, only to be knocked back over the ropes by a hard chairshot. Cobra then throws the chair out of the ring and climbs the turnbuckle. Once NightShade gets back to his feet, Cobra springs off the turnbuckle and slams his elbow into his opponent's back.::
'Big D': "Whoa! Nice Elbow Drop there by Cobra. After that charishot in the ring, I doubt that NightShade will be able to continue in this match much longer."
::Cobra grabs NightShade and rolls him into the ring. He then gets in as well and pulls his opponent back to his feet. Cobra punches NightShade in the face, kicks him in the gut, and then takes him out with The Venom, a modified Jawbreaker. He goes for the pin and the referee drops to the mat.::
Brian Lane: "This is it, folks!" 1 . . . 2 . . . 3! 'Big D': "The winner of our second match is Cobra!" *^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^* Winner: Cobra via. The Venom *^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^* Brian Lane: "That was a nice win for Cobra, but Jason Jarrett is still my favorite wrestler so far. Jarrett should have no problem defeating Cobra next week."
'Big D': "Yes, folks... next week will be round two of the BC Title tournament. That means that a new BC champion will be decided in seven day's time."
Brian Lane: "Now it's time for our next match..." *^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^* Cyris Grisham vs. Ewan Milton *^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^* ::'The Scar' by At the Gates begins to play, and out steps Cyris Grisham. He carries two drumsticks [the kind you play the drums with, not chicken bones
:) ] to the ring with him. In the ring he holds up the sticks in the shape of a 'X' and points to the entrance ramp. He then makes does the cut-throat sign across his throat with the stick in his right hand. 'Bat out of Hell'
by Meatloaf begins to play as Ewan Milton enters the arena, wearing green wrestling tights and black boots. Milton slowly walks down to the ring and gives a few fans at ringside high-fives. He eventually climbs into the ring and the bell dings, signaling the beginning of the match.::
'Big D': "You want to predict who will win this match, Brian? I'm gonna go with Ewan Milton."
Brian Lane: "Considering the fact that Grisham was just brought in last night, I'd have to agree. Besides, Ewan Milton looks like he is much stronger than his opponent."
'Big D': "Yes, that is true... oh, nice Shoulder Breaker by Ewan Milton!" ::After a Shoulder Breaker, Milton pulls Grisham up and whips him off the ropes. On the return he nails a Clothesline, and then drops an elbow to
Grisham's chest. Milton then pulls Grisham back up and does an Eye Rake.
Grisham holds his face in pain, only to receive two strong kicks to the gut. Milton then goes behind his opponent and executes a Reverse DDT. With his opponent down on the mat, Milton makes his way toward the turnbuckle and climbs on top. He waits for Grisham to get back up, and nails him with a
Clothesline off the top. Grisham flips over backward and lands on his stomach. Milton then gets up and begins to stomp on his opponent's back.::
Brian Lane: "Get up you stupid fuck!" 'Big D': "See, just like we predicted, Ewan Milton is really putting a hurt on Cyris Grisham."
::Grisham finally is allowed to get back up, and attempts to whip Milton into the corner. Ewan Milton reverses it, and sends Grisham full speed toward the turnbuckle. He runs across the ring and Splashes Grisham in the corner. He then grabs him and attempts to whip him across the ring again.
This time Grisham reverses the move, and sends Milton into the turnbuckle. He also tries to hit a Splash in the corner, but Milton lifts his boot in the air just in time to hit his opponent smack in the jaw. Grisham jumps around in pain and is knocked to the mat by a strong forearm to the back by
Milton.:: 'Big D': "Ewan Milton is really putting the hurt on Grisham here." Brian Lane: "Grisham will certainly lose the match, but he may also get himself a trip to the medical centre!"
::In the ring, Milton continues to completely dominate his opponent. He whips Grisham off the ropes and hits a BodySlam on the return. Then, he Leg
Drops Grisham across the throat. Milton pulls Grisham up and Powerbombs him near the turnbuckle.::
Brian Lane: "I think it's time for the Mine-Digger!" ::Milton climbs onto the second rope and executes a Leg Drop from there. He then makes a pinfall attempt, and the referee begins the count.::
1 . . . 2 . . . 3! 'Big D': "Your winner, Ewan Milton!" Brian Lane: "What a show, what a show!" *^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^* Winner: Ewan Milton via. Mine-Digger *^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^* 'Big D': "That's it for this week, folks. Be sure to buy your tickets now for next weeks' event, where you will see all three of tonight's winners in action, as they fight for the TAW BC Title."
Brian Lane: "Good night, folks!" ::End...:: Copyright © 2000 KSonic Enterprises, Inc. All rights reserved. T&A Wrestling, T&A Arena of Aggression are trademark of KSonic Enterprises, Inc.
