Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

T&A Wrestling ran out of a converted, previously-abandoned warehouse in Vancouver, British Columbia, which its own FAQ calls the Arena of Aggression — 1,330 square metres, holding up to 1,600 fans, with a ring built to the same dimensions as Extreme Championship Wrestling of Philadelphia's. Its own founder-president had run a similarly short-lived California promotion in 1998–99 before relocating to British Columbia and starting TAW; by its own FAQ, dated 29 October 2000, the promotion planned occasional tours into Washington and Idaho on top of its home shows.

Two championships anchored the singles division — a Canadian Title, ranked above all else regardless of record, and a British Columbian Title defended more often beneath it — alongside a single Tag Team title open to teams of three or four. Results were decided purely by roleplay quality, with no simulator, and the weekly schedule split a televised Sunday card, Brutal Warfare, from a Friday-night flash-posting show it called Shittalk!.

Its two surviving Brutal Warfare broadcasts recap a British Columbian Title tournament run over back-to-back weeks. In the first round, on 12 November 2000, Jason Jarrett defeated Samael, Cobra beat NightShade, and Ewan Milton defeated Cyris Grisham; the following week, on 21 November, Milton went on to win the vacant title outright in a three-way against Jarrett and Cobra. The same card opened with a Loser Leaves Town match won by Shiatsu over Samael, saw the debuting Extreme Order (Mac Henri and Seth Dread) take the Tag Team Titles from the Old School Alliance (Iron Mike and Mr. Roboto, managed by Sara Wyld), and ended a weapons match between American Psycho and The Count in a no contest after a masked interloper, later identified on-air as Steve Stone, ran in on both men.

That interference became its own thread by the broadcast's close: backstage, Iron Mike offered Steve Stone a standing invitation to join the Old School Alliance, framing it as a shared stand against the weapons-heavy style Extreme Order had just used to win the tag titles.

No further document survives past the 22 November 2000 broadcast. Whether Steve Stone accepted the Old School Alliance's offer, whether Ewan Milton defended his newly-won British Columbian Title, or how long T&A Wrestling continued to run out of the Arena of Aggression, isn't known.
