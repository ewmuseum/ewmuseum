However, there are a couple points that need to be made here: 1. The existing IIWF is, in fact, called "International Internet Wrestling Federation. You can feel free to check out the Web page if you need proof. 2. The existing IIWF is a stable, established organization, winner of RSPW-F's award for best e-fed. Given that many new feds dissolve within a short time after creation, it would be a potentially bad thing to have another IIWF be created and then fall apart, as there could be confusion, which would detract from the reputation of a truly class organization. 3. Prez [name withheld] didn't say anything about a copyright, which implies legal concerns and whatnot. He was actually pretty damned polite about the whole thing. This is important. E-wrestling has finally come to its own as a recognized phenomena, but still, it's a fairly small group. We need to try to avoid stepping on each others toes as much as possible. It seems to me that the best course of action is for the new fed to simply choose a new name.
