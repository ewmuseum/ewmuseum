IIWF fan board: "IIWF Forever Predictions" (3)

31 July 1998
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Joe Petrow concocted the following diatribe ... >Note, this is done without any knowledge of the booking for this event >whatsoever. It's not even all that serious... > >Future Bowl Match ends in a double countout, meaning that the final >match becomes a four-way dance, won by "Too Sweet" Rick Williams Yes, but Eddy Jacks wears his "Spinebuster Scoops" t-shirt during the match so that the fans can get a good look at it while Gaines/Temple are whipping his sorry ass. And also b/c I'm desperate for interfederational plugs...and only b/c it upsets Ronnie ("too kayfabish"). Miraculously, the match is won by Tonnage, who leaps from the top of the stands and kills all four combatants. > >Alphabet Boys defeat the Prophets of Rage to become new IIWF Tag Team >Titles, simply because to be the greatest IIWF PPV of all time as this >one strives to be, one must have all titles change hands. My prediction? Derek Rage and Marissa Monet "get it on" on the apron while Shadoe and/or DDUA has to slug it out with the ABC boys in the ring. Spreadbury comes out before the matches and announces that it is a death in darkness match, though, so we can't watch Rage and Monet at it but CAN watch Shadoe Rage get killed (oh wait, that already happened). > >"Sychosys" Joe Petrow defeats "Enigma" Takezo Musashi in their God of >Chaos match 80 falls to 0. The first fall will be a Master of >Darkness match, whereupon Musashi will be pummeled to death by four >"creatures" wielding lutes and scythes, turning the match into a >complete squash from that point on. Actually, the two beat Spreadbury to the punch, killing one another in their RP's. Hey, it works in the AOL shitfeds. > >Ronnie Paris is unable to make his flight to Oregon, and is thus >replaced in the King of the Mountain match by the only other Ronnie on >the card, "Playboy" Ronnie D, who becomes the new IIWF Cruiserweight >Champion. Yes....but with one stipulation. Ronnie D must wrestle in the nude, with the words "I LUV MARK CUTTER" painted on his chest. > >Tiger Claw defeats "Showstopper" Simon Lebec to become the new IIWF >Intercontinental champion. (see above) Unfortunately, LeBec no-shows, forcing Casey James to take his place. Mat's head explodes, as he is forced to RP against himself....and the whole e-world mourns another young man dead before his time. > >Brody Thunder defeats Playboy Ronnie D, because Ronnie "owes" Brody >and we know how these things work out. Yes...but with one stipulation. Ronnie D must wrestle in the nude with Creed, also nude, sitting on his shoulders. As if Creed's gigantic package alone isn't enough of a distraction, Ronnie D must also wear socks on his hands. > >Chris Quigley scores the final fall in the Legends match on Subway >Psycho. Because the card is running late, the total time of the match >is 37 seconds. Actually, the match is won by non-participant Poutine Janois, b/c he has done nothing else in the IIWF of any importance whatsoever, simply b/c all four mean either died or tested positive for steroids. Subway Pyscho, who tests positive for steroids, is later killed by Marilyn Manson and KISS, who want their "look" back. > >The mystery participant in the Eternal Rumble turns out to be...IIWF >President Dan Spreadbury! Spreadbury draws #1, and threatens to >withhold pay from all wrestlers unless they eliminate themselves from >the battle royale. Thus, all wrestlers come out, take a final bow, >and eliminate themselves from the ring, making Dan Spreadbury IIWF >World Heavyweight Champion FOREVER! Actually, the mystery participant in the battle royal is........ A SECOND JOE PETROW! Yes, friends, TWO JOE PETROWS! One, a calm, computer-programming nebbish.....and the other a slobbering sex machine. And one final stipulation.........everyone must wrestle in the nude, with every 300-pounder not involved in the match to stand outside the ring, also nude. And whoever wins tests positive for steroids. And is killed by a mine. And then then....uh....the whole colisseum explodes, killing everyone but one of the Joe Petrows and Ronnie D. Nuff said (j/k, to be sure), Oliver L. Bateman SCRA VP Handler of Eddy "Flap" Jacks (IIWF....forever, SCRA, LWC) > >This has been a recording. > >Joe Petrow > [detail withheld] >
