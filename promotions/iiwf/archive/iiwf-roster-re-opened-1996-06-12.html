The IIWF has recently reopened its singles division after a few handlers were forced to drop out because it's coming to the end of term. The tag team division is also still open. But before I merely exort you all to join the IIWF, let me tell you a bit about it: IIWF is a booked fed, where a wrestler's success depends entirely on the quality (although not necessarily quantity) of his flashes and interviews. There are two weekly cards, with PPVs occurring on a six-weekly basis, and also a twice-weekly report, featuring previews of upcoming cards, comments and the like. So the week looks something like this: Monday - Tuesday - IIWF Report Wednesday - IIWF Midweek Mayhem Thursday - Friday - IIWF Report Saturday - IIWF Saturday Night / PPV Sunday - The next PPV goes down on Saturday 29 June. So that you have an idea of what the IIWF is all about, check out the other post "[IIWF Report] 11th June 1996". If you're an active roleplayer and handle a wrestler with an active gimmick, drop me an email - I'd love to have you onboard. Hope to hear from you soon, Daniel S.
