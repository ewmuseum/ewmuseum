[IIWF] Ranks reopened

18 August 1996
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hi there... This is the President of the IIWF here. We're just coming off the back of our third PPV event, and we're looking to expand our rosters with new additions to both our singles and tag team ranks.
The IIWF is a fully booked fantasy fed that doesn't allow real wrestlers, nor does it use simulators. The golden rule of success in the IIWF is:
good writing = good push In other words, if you want to get ahead, you must roleplay often for your wrestler. None of this "x, I'm gonna kick your ass" nonsense. I'm talking about proper roleplay, developing your wrestler's character. In order for your wrestler to succeed, he must stay uppermost in people's minds. And the best way to do that is to be active -- send in angles, send in flashes and interviews. The IIWF has two weekly cards, and also issues reports twice a week, which allow all the other handlers to read your work, so there's plenty of opportunity to get involved.
Many of the IIWF's participants state that the IIWF is the best league they've ever been in... so why not test out their claims for yourself?
Get in touch. Mail me at [detail withheld] or simply reply to this posting, and I'll send you the league's FAQ, the rosters and a creation sheet... you'll be up and fighting within a week!
Why not check out the IIWF's extensive web site. You can read all the IIWF's cards, reports, examine the talent, and more. It's located at: http://www.sisko.demon.co.uk Join the IIWF today -- you'll be glad you did! [name withheld]. IIWF President.
