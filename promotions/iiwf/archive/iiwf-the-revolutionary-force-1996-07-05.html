In fact, things have never been hotter in the IIWF...
We're just coming off the back of our second PPV, Ring Wars, and we have two weekly cards as well as a twice-weekly report featuring analysis, trash talk and previews of upcoming events. If you're looking for a booked fed with the best role-players in e-wrestling, look no further.
The golden rule of the IIWF is: good writing = good push. If you put the effort in, you get the push! There's no other fed quite like the IIWF.
