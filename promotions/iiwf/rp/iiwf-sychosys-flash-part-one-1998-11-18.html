IIWF Sychosys Flash, Part One (Revelations)

18 November 1998
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Orange and yellow dance with the familiar white and blue to form a twisted, ever diminishing haze known as twilight in the Mexican sky. A similar battle of colors takes place between the pounding surf and the defenseless sand of the beach upon which "Sychosys" Joe Petrow walks. Wearing an unassuming grey sweatsuit, and the very assuming IIWF World Heavyweight Championship belt, Petrow runs a hand through three and a half months growth of near hair, before shoving both hands into his shirt pockets, contemplating something...perhaps life, perhaps liberty, perhaps the persuit of 99 cent tequila shots. Not so suddenly, he stops, bends down to pick up a rock, tumbles it around in his hands, and without so much as looking towards the camera, begins to speak.] JP: Three and a half months now, I've been retired. Kicking back, relaxing in paradise, occasionally pulling some practical jokes on the e-wrestling world, just because I can. Having the time of my life. For the first time in a long time, my body is fully healed. For the first time in my life, I'm truly content. For the first time at my life, I find myself completely and utterly at peace. [Petrow gently flips the rock towards the ocean, sending it skipping a couple of times. Petrow reaches down to grab a slightly larger rock.] JP: And every day, in the midst of peace and paradise, I wait for a phone call telling me when I'm going to defend the IIWF World Championship. And the call never comes. It never comes. The god-damned call NEVER COMES!!! [Petrow hurls the rock into the ocean, which disappears in one large splash, then finally turns to look at the camera] JP: Well, enough is enough! I'm not going to sit around passively waiting anymore! People telling me the IIWF ain't around no more. Well I'm here! And I got this here belt that says I'm a champion! So as far as I'm concerned, as long as I'm here and I got this I got enough proof so'as nobody can tell me this ain't around no more! I am so I am, or some such perverbal crap like that! In fact, I'm more than just a part of the IIWF. I *am* the IIWF! I have achived a whole synergy! And yet, it's not complete. Because I'm not a man who can live "happily ever after" Oh yeah, I can be happy forever, and I can be the IIWF champion forever. But not like this. There's got to be a challenge. There's got to be a story. I've *got* to defend this belt. But against who? [Petrow pounds his hand, and lets out a smile] Heh, maybe I ain't so whole after all. There's only one person who can tell me that. And I'm gonna find him. [Petrow turns around, then sprints back towards the resort.] JP: Oy, chico, se habla inglis? No? Well, too bad! Book me the first flight outta here! I gotta visit and old friend! [Fade out] Joe Petrow [detail withheld]
