[Suddenly, Steve Roberts stands bolt upright and shouts out to Sychosys.] SR: What the holy hell is your problem, Petrow!?  You got a captive     audience to bitch about your problems with this league, and you're     just gonna sit there quoting from Sesame Street?  This is the best     that the "Best Interviewee" can do?  You got something to say, then     get your ass up there and say it!     C'mon, Shoot Joe, Shoot! Standing at the podium with the three awards in front of him, Petrow takes off his sunglasses, and looks over the statuettes.  One in particular catches his eye.  Holding the award in one hand, and slowly stroking the scars on his cheek in the other, he addresses the crowd:] JP: You really think I have the Best Face in the IIWF? [Bizarre pop for Petrow, and a very strange look from Becky LaRue. Petrow sets the award down, and looks at another one, seemingly a bit calmer than before] JP: This one...this one I don't quite understand.  Best Interviewee?     Is this supposed to be a compliment?  Is this supposed to mean... [While Petrow is talking, he reaches back behind himself, reaching back at his legs for something] JP: ...that you prefer me standing up and talking to you, rather than     seeing me lace up my boots and get in the ring?  Have I so     mesmurized you with my slightly above average vocabulary that... [It is now apparant that Petrow is taking off his shoes] JP: ...you all have forgotten that I'm supposed to be one of the best     wrestlers in the business?  I mean, [without breaking stride,     Petrow lobs his shoes in the direction of Derek Mota, one at a     time], when you look at it, this award might be more of a curse     than an honor.  It says I'm so damn over with my mouth, that I     don't need to get over in the ring.  So screw what happens in the     ring, right?  Who cares if he's not a champion, look at what     happens when he gets behind the mic!  Well, let's get something     straight tonight.  I am no Larry Morton, I am no Tim Dross.  I am     no Steve Roberts. [Petrow reaches down and quickly pulls his t-shirt over his head, and flings it at 3M, who deals with it as he did the leather jacket.] JP: And, as you can plainly see, I am no Becky LaRue.  I am Joe     Petrow.  I am feared the world over, as the most gifted, the most     ruthless, the most cunning, the most devious, and yes, the most     DISLOYAL wrestler in the world today!  So respect me for my words     if you must, but just remember, if you're in the ring with me,     don't be surprised if this same mouth might be used to bite some     important body part off! [Petrow waits for some sort of response, then sets the two awards aside to concentrate on the final one.  Meanwhile, Petrow reaches down to...waist level.  After undoing a button, Petrow receives a flash of inspirtation.] JP: Hey, check this out! [Petrow takes the microphone of its stand, and places it on the edge of the podium, right by his barely hidden waist level.  Petrow then moves his arm quickly down, and a loud unzipping sound reverberates through the hall] JP: Whoa, cool! [Petrow proceeds to take his pants off, one leg at a time, just like anybody else would.  When finished, he throws them out to the crowd, landing in the middle of the high administration table.] JP: You guys can fight over who wears the pants when this is over,     but take it behind closed doors, I'm tired of hearing about it! [Murmurs go through the crowd, all different, but mainly of the "What the hell is he doing!?" variety] JP: Finally,  [Petrow's voice softens noticably] finally, we got this     award.  When I think of what...what?  What's that?  Wait a minute,     I'm coming over there! [With that, Petrow grabs the cordless mic and leaves the podium, sporting nothing but white socks and bright yellow boxer shorts, and heads over to...a man behind one of the cameras, who appears to be directing the scenes.  The camera changes to one which can better capture what is transpiring] JP: Ladies and gentlemen, let me introduce you to the great executive     television producer for the IIWF, Mr. Dan Kaugleflueffer! [Slight mocking pop, as the balding middle-aged man tries to get back out of the spotlight] JP: Mr. Kaugleflueffer, I wasn't at the meeting for obvious reasons,     so I'm not up to speed on all of these hand signals and stuff.     Would you please explain what this [Petrow points his hand     repeated at his own wrist] is supposed to mean. [Petrow holds the mic in front of the flustered gentlemen, who's name may or may not be Dan Kaugleflueffer, who like many other people, fear Petrow's erratic nature too much to offer the resistance they normally would to this kind of situation] DK: Well, um, the award winners are supposed to wrap up their speech     in, um, two minutes or less, and uh, you've gone a bit over that. [Joe looks at the man for a second, then lets out a slight smirk] JP: Ladies and gentlemen let's have a nice hand for Mr. Dan     Kaugleflueffer! [Smattering of applause, as Petrow leaves the man, talking while walking back to the podium] JP: ...two minutes for the Best Interviewee in the IIWF, do they run a     tight ship here or what?  Now, as I was saying before that slight     interruption, which ironically made this speech go on over longer     than intended, [Petrow is back at the podium, and places the mic     back in the stand] there's one last award that needs to be addressed.     The award, for Most Missed Departed Athlete. [Petrow stands still for several seconds, as if searching for the words that have suudenly stopped flowing in his mind, before he continues.] JP: September 20th, 1997.  Or was it September 22nd?  I can't quite     remember.  Anyway, it was a night when nine months of trials,     agonies, and sacrifices, a night when all of that was supposed to     be justified.  For me, Maurice, the Sychopaths, everyone.  In the     machi I call home, in front of the people who know me best.  Every     indication pointed to Joe Petrow fulfilling his destiny to become     the IIWF World's Heavyweight Champion.  And then... [Petrow reaches down...pulls down...pulling some thing that the podium keeps out of view...and then...a shocked crowd reacts as Petrow's boxer shorts are placed on top of the podium!  And proceeds to speak softly] JP: Every fiber of my being was stripped away.  Not by Requiem, who     nonetheless put up one hell of a fight, and indeed, if it had gone     on, may have actually won through his own ability.  Not by Genesis,     who were just looking out for their man.  Not even by some mask     wearing lunatic, who was just looking out for himself, I guess.     No, I was let down by the living, breathing entity that is the IIWF,     to satisfy a couple of old-timers egos.  You know, I wasn't around     for the aftermath, what ever came of "Masked Outlaw" angle, anyway?     I fought so long to get some respect for this sport and the IIWF,     and I wanted to convey that respect to the Japanese people, the     people who give this sport more respect than anybody in the world.     And yet the very IIWF I sought to protect didn't even want to     protect itself, content to let itself drown in a sea of pandemonium.     They turned my moment of honor into a joke.  And for the second time     in my career, I received complete and utter betrayal from the very     entity I fought for.     I think it's one of those deja vu things, or a dream that's tryin'     to tell me something.  Or will I ever stop thinkin' about it?  I     don't know, I doubt it.  All I know right now, [picks up his award]     is that this little statue cuts both ways.  Apparently, nobody is     missed more in the IIWF than me.  And, nobody misses the IIWF more     than me.  In a perfect world, this means my return, right? Fact is, _I_ am the only success, from that "train-wreck" we called Genesis. And, well, basically I'm the only guy that deserves this award.
