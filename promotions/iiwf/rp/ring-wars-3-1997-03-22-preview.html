IIWF — Ring Wars III

Ring Wars III: The Weekend Starts Here, 21 March 1997

21 March 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
That's right, folks, the IIWF's biggest pay-per-view to date is set to burst onto your screens this weekend. Coming up tonight: the special "Road to Ring Wars III" preview show, featuring interviews and match previews as the IIWF's wrestlers gear up for the biggest weekend in wrestling history. Coming up tomorrow: Ring Wars III's Free For All, featuring interviews, last-minute news, and two special matches... Then, the big one... IIWF Ring Wars III, live from the Skydome in Toronto, Ontario, Canada, headlined by a Submission Match between two legends, the retiring Dan Kauffman, and "Quickstrike" Chris Quigley. The first time. The last time. The only time. And only in the IIWF! Stay tuned, folks! Daniel. IIWF President.
