Lord Byron, congratulations on behalf of the administration, the officials, the assembled wrestlers and the entire IIWF on this award -- your achievements in the ring earlier this year have been nothing short of outstanding.  Congratulations! [The scattered applause from those respectful in the crowd is again silenced as Byron steps forward.  He is dressed as smartly as ever in a pinstripe grey suit, but it is his face which stuns the audience. Gone is the expected sneer.  Gone is the look of supreme arrogance.  His face is pale, his eyes puffed and red-rimmed from lack of sleep.  His face is lined with worry, and he cannot even meet the gaze of Tim Dross for long without looking away.  He smiles slightly, ruefully, and looks down holding the bridge of his nose in his hand as he steps forward.  Slowly he reaches out, taking the award from Dross' hand and looking up for the first time, glancing around the crowd, and then down at the award in his hand.  He takes another long breath, then lets it out slowly, looking back up again...] LB:  Ladies and gentlemen... [Byron pauses, swallowing, and he looks down at the award in his hand again.] LB:  Ladies and gentlemen, this is truly a great honor for me to accept this award... [Byron's voice trails off as the silence in the room is broken.  Broken by laughter.  Byron looks up and around, as does everyone... looks around to the table where Otto "The Butcher" Verhoeven is sitting.  Where the Teutonic Terror is sat, alongside his valet, Nurse Heidi Upperman, laughing out loud.  Byron glances back across at Tim Dross, his shoulders slumping, and he shakes his head, handing the award back...] LB: I can't do this, Mr. Dross.  Not now.  I'm sorry. [Byron tries hard to ignore the laughter of his adversary, looking up and around the crowd.] LB: I'm sorry.  It was a mistake my even coming here tonight. [Byron steps down off the podium, and back down into the aisle, walking towards the exit through the whispers of the crowd, his cane clicking rapidly on the floor.] OV:  Going so soon, Byron? [Byron stops dead at the sound of Verhoeven's voice, yards away from the exit, tapping his cane on the floor so it jumps up into his hand.  His head rises slightly, but he doesn't look around.] OV: What is the matter, "My lord"? What are you running away from? The spotlight? The laughter? Where is your sneer now, Byron, where is the aura of superiority that always seemed to surround you? [He stands up and walks toward the podium.] OV: You are finished. I did warn you, gave you the chance to leave the IIWF with enough of your dignity to still be respected as one of the best. [Verhoeven has reached the podium, his back turned to the crowd. Tim Dross backs off as he is smart enough to sense the volatile atmosphere.] OV: But you didn't listen. You had to get in the ring one more time, trying to humiliate me once again like you always did in the past... [The Butcher whirls around a points a finger at Byron, his face flushed with rage, his eyes glaring brightly.] OV: NOT THIS TIME! You lousy piece of trash are not worthy to even show your face in this promotion again. You snivelling fool make me sick. I broke you, Byron. No, I didn't break any of your bones, didn't do any physical damage to your spine, but... [He thrusts his right hand upward, making a grasping motion.] OV: ...but I took your confidence, your precious willpower, and crushed it like a bug. With that, I destroyed you, destroyed the whole mythos of Byron, the uberwrestler. This here... [He grabs the award.] OV: You do not deserve this award, Byron. Your time of glory is over, once and for all. Go, now, leave like the beaten dog that you are. Return to the hospital, to your ward and tell your whose fault it was that she is now in that condition. Tell her how sorry you are for bringing that danger upon her. Tell her how you failed her. [A vicious, almost sadistic smile appears on his face.] OV: Tell her to stay away from the likes of you, 'cause they bring only pain and suffering. Leave, I say, you have disgraced this show enough for one night! [Byron pauses a moment longer as the crowd cries out in combined outrage and laughter, before stepping quickly to the exit, swinging the doors open and disappearing outside without further comment.] TD: And with that, we bid good night to you all.  And goodnight Becky. BL: Of course. I always enjoy being a mistress... of ceremonies.
