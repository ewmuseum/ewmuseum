IIWF Golden Grapple Awards, 22 November 1997

22 November 1997

[Shots of the Seattle skyline:  The space needle, Puget Sound, The Kingdome.  Suddenly, spotlights slice the night sky like lasers.  The beams cross and flash across the sky above the Meydenbauer Center.  A motorcade of limosines have already cued up in front of the performing arts and convention complex.  The camera quickly shifts to Tim Dross and Larry Morton standing in front among throngs of screaming fans, many waving signs touting their favorite champion.  While Larry and Tim are nattily dressed in tuxedos, their companion, Becky LaRue wears what appears to be a backless coat of paint dipped in sequins.] TD: The superstars have already begun to arrive for this, the penultimate awards banquet of the awards season. LM: I'd just like to add that... [Larry is cut off mid-speech by the roar of a motorcycle as Requiem, in a black leather tux, arrives with Gabrielle.  A valet quickly runs up to the vehicle only to be turned away by a withering glance from its owner.  The former stalks inside, unmindful of the fans who scream for his autograph.] LM: It just occurs to me that... [Requiem's arrival is contrasted by that of Scott "The Fop" Rogers who spills from his limo with with two ridiculously buxom and underdressed escorts.  The three teeter down the red carpet. To the delight of the crowd who ogle the two ladies lasciviously, and to the dumbfoundment of Larry Morton, one of Scotty's companion's dresses stops defying physics, allowing her ample torso to spring free from its overly tight enclosure. She makes no attempt to cover her nudity.  Politely, but not too delicately, Rogers tucks the lady's assets back into their prison.] LM: Aaaaah. BL: Was that a spice girl? TD: I think we're going to regret that this show isn't on delay. BL: Pull your tongue in, Larry, it wasn't real. [Richard "Moxy" Blue is the next out of Rogers' limosine.  His Doc Martens clash with his "Sparkplug Lee Designer Powder Blue Tuxedo". The neons in his tie light up with the command "Kiss Me In The Dark, Baby."  He stops for a moment, the crowd hinges on his next action, but there is only disappointment as he breaks wind.] LM: I don't... BL: Hey, Dross.  Five Bucks says Alex Rio is the next to arrive. TD: No chance.  Ike Sampson. [They are both disappointed as Kevin "The Cavalier" Christiansen arrives. A number of Supersonics fans pelt him with Shawn Kemp jerseys.  There is threat of violence, but Christiansen silences them with an upraised palm. "Sychosys" Joe Petrow arrives in a black limosine, with his companions "Majestic" Maurice McArthur, and Steve Summer.  While Summer and McArthur come dressed to kill in crisp tuxedos, Petrow defies dress code, wearing a leather jacket over a black t-shirt, ripped blue jeans, and low-top athletic shoes, also hiding behind a pair of horn-rimmed sunglasses.  The trio offers no comment as they enter the building.  Tim Dross nods towards security, and Steve Summer is expertly separated from his companions and ejected from the area.  A black and red limosine pulls up to the curb and stops. The attendants open the door. Out steps Brody Thunder, dressed in a western-style tuxedo with a red bolo tie, a smoldering cigar clenched between his teeth. As he walks up the carpet, Tim Dross approaches him, mic in hand.] TD: Mr. Thunder... Mr. Thunder? Any comments on the awards tonight?     Any predictions on whether you won? [Thunder almost brushes past him but Tim stays with him, trying to get a comment from the world champion. Thunder, never breaking stride, finally speaks.] BT: Dross... I couldn't give a tinker's damn 'bout these pats-on-the-back flamin' awards Spreadbury an' his suits hand out.     I'm here cuz I got a message ta deliver. TD: A message? To whom? About what? [Still walking.] BT: Ya want answers? TD: Yes... yes, of course. BT: Then buy a ticket an' plant yer ass in the audience. You'll     get 'em soon enough. Now get the hell outta my way 'fore I     take that mic an' stick it... [Cut to Becky LaRue, standing by Larry, who is listening intently to his microphone to see if it's working.] LB: Cutting edge work there, Tim.  You expect to win "Best announcer" with that kind of mic work? TD: You know I put little stock in these type of awards.  All the IIWF     Journalists are excellent. BL: Bull, Tim.  What's the over-under? TD: If I win, Roberts takes me to the Ballet.  If he wins I take him back to Tiajuana. [The superstars continue to arrive.  Tony Starks and a number of "homies" try to enter the building, but they are stopped until Tim Dross vouches that they are indeed members of the IIWF and not a menace to society.  The Machines arrive, followed by Damage, Inc.  There is a brief staredown as neither will turn their back on the other to enter the building.  The stalemate is broken by Luke Steele who forces both teams through the door simultaneously.  Tim Turner steps out of a gleaming limo with Victoria Von Edward on his arm, and runs into Becky LaRue.] BL: Here's the "Rocket Man" Tim Turner. TT: Hi, Becky. BL: I wasn't sure you would show up after you didn't get any nominations. TT: You see, Becky, first off, I found out why I didn't get any     nominations. BL: Because you weren't good enough? [Turner glares at LaRue.] TT: I am the Cruiserweight Champion! Does that sound not good enough? I'm the number one wrestler in the IIWF! No. I was excluded because I'm _too_ good. IIWF executives were afraid that I would win too many awards and the other wrestlers would quit or something. BL: So why did you come? TT: Simple. No elegant affair is complete without myself and Victoria.     Second, I am here to congratulate my friend Duncan after he wins his     trophy. Third, I am here so Victoria can make her official     announcement. BL: What announcement is that? VVE: I have just signed to be the new host of "Countdown to Saturday Night." It seems that the IIWF felt it was time for a knowledgeable, intelligent, and beautiful commentator to work with Larry to counter the effects of the... other... female announcer in the federation. Oh, by the way, Becky... has Wednesday War Room been renewed? [The two sashay into the building] LM: You mean... BL: I guess this would be a good time to mention that I'm moving to Greece to be the companion of millionaire Aaron Acropolis.  Farewell to the War Room.  Someone advise Vicki... Spreadbury really likes playing Admiral Byrd and the naughty penguin. [A limosine, obviously out of tune and belching smoke, pulls up, disgorging the Jobber Justice Squad.  They pose for the crowd and stay just long enough for people to get tired of them.  Jumpin' Jack trips on a fold in the carpet, rebreaking his nose.  The camera pans to Chris Quigley walking up the red carpet, uncharacteristically dressed in a tuxedo.  He looks visibly uncomfortable in the restrictive clothing as Tim Dross walks up to greet him.] TD: Chris!  Great to see you here.  Um... where is Steve Manning? CQ: I don't have a clue where that little toad is, Dross.     TD: Well, I for one, am glad to hear that, actually.  You're up for a     number of Grapples tonight.  How do you think you'll do? CQ: [shrugs] If there was any justice, I'd at least be _nominated_ for     best singles wrestler, but I'm not going to bitch about that.  The     match of the year should go to my match against Dan Kauffman at Ring Wars III, but considering that it was a match featuring technical brilliance and stamina, rather than explosions and weapons, it probably won't win. There's no accounting for taste sometimes, Tim. TD: If it's any consolation, that match _would_ get my vote. CQ: Yeah, thanks.  These awards are pretty meaningless anyway.  When it comes right down to it, the only awards in the IIWF are the title belts, and I have the "supposedly" second best title, although I've said it before and I'll say it again, Brody Thunder is nowhere near the caliber of champion I am.  I saved him from becoming a huge spot on the mat at Ring Wars when I stopped Hardin from powerbombing his saddle-ass again.  It's just too bad Hardin has rode off into the sunset, I really did want to face him. TD: That much was evident.  Good luck tonight, "Quickstrike"! CQ: This is a popularity contest, Dross, and when it comes to the boys in the back, I usually lose those. TD: Oh, incidentally, where is the lovely Stephanie tonight? CQ: Home. TD: [shrugs]  Well, okay... good luck, once again! [Quigley walks away, stops for a moment to chat with Ronnie Paris, before continuing to stride into the building, a look of indifference on his face.  The flashing cameras suddenly stop, a little shocked, as Steve Manning walks down the red carpet in shredded jeans and a "Grapple This!" t-shirt, with an arrow pointing down at the obvious area.  Tim Dross is about to walk over to interview the youngest Manning, but Becky LaRue shoves the veteran broadcaster out of the way, wanting to get this interview herself.] BL: Stephen!  Delightful to see ya here, babe! SM: Hiya Becks, you're looking particularly slutty tonight. BL: [giggles and snorts]  You charmer, you!  You look so much better now that you're without that ugly growth the IIWF calls a "Quigley".  Without him around, you can finally stand on your own two feet! SM: [smirks]  Not bad.  Not bad at all.  Chris Quigley disappoints me.     Let's face it, he's lost his touch.  There was a time when he was a     great wrestler, he _had_ to be to get through The Living Hell, but I     think those days are long gone.  He needed me to help him beat that drunken Scott, not to mention Serge Annis, Creed, the list goes on and on.  But... all this talk about him is depressing me.  What say we sneak off somewhere and I show ya just how recovered I am? BL: [checks her wristwatch]  Oh, what the hell, I've got a few minutes to kill. SM: [raises his eyebrows]  And they call _me_ twisted.  Later babe, I've     got an awards ceremony to crash. TD: I was about to say biggest pain in the neck... BL: Yeah... sure. TD: Proving once again, Steve Manning boldly goes where Chris Quigley     fears to tread. My boys are still very new here. Of course I don't expect them to be making any great waves just yet. But come next year, expect the Natural Predators to be taking home the Grapples. For this year, whatever happens, it's an honor, as always, to be counted among the members of the IIWF. BL: [yawning] Yeah yeah, sure sure, have fun signing autographs. [Annis shoves past Larry, and makes his way into the building.  Last to arrive is the official IIWF Towncar.  President Spreadbury disembarks, waving to the crowd who cheer approvingly.  He is followed by a familiar, but annonymous bearded gentleman, and the ubiquitous suit wearing high top basketball shoes with his tuxedo.
