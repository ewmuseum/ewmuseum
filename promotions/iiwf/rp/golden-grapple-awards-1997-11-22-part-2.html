IIWF Golden Grapple Awards, 22 November 1997 [Part 2 of 4]

22 November 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
We try at "Inside" to mix in the fun with the current IIWF news, trying to give each wrestler with the promotion some sort of time within the framework of the greater goal of marketing the _Mighty_ IIWF worldwide. It is a difficult balance, trying to be fair to the balance of the roster while giving a sufficient degree of time to the headline matches. We hope we strike the proper balance more often than not. "Inside" is, at its core, an entertainment program and we like to think that the tremendous amount of work that goes into the show each and every week does not go unnoticed.  Sure, occasionally the comments of the "Soundbite" can sometimes be over the top... but we'd like to hope that everyone understands that the spirit in which they are intended is one of fun... one that causes people to want to talk about and watch "Inside the IIWF"... and thereby the Federation as a whole. Thank you again, from myself, Steve Roberts and everyone "behind the scenes" at "Inside the IIWF"... we hope you continue to enjoy our programming and -- we'll see you on Tuesday! [Becky takes to the podium again as "Best Interviewee" appears on the toteboard.] BL: For a time I had a great job:  I'd sit and insult wrestlers and and     get paid for it.  I called that little vacation "LaRue's Lair".  But     the award for my talents will come later.  This one is about you boys. And in case there was any doubt, the "Final Cut" is just a cheap imitation.  Let's see the candidates. [Video, soundless blurbs of wrestlers giving interviews:  Joe Petrow, Dirt Dog Unique Allah, Requiem, Brody Thunder, Steve Kowalski, J.W. Hardin.] BL: ...and your winner is "Sychosys" Joe Petrow. [Upon being announced for the award.  Petrow just sits there stone-faced.  When asked to come up again, Petrow does his "1--2--3!" chant, while "Majestic" Maurice McArthur flips Becky the bird.] BL: Okay, fine.  You want to explain to me what you and McArthur were     doing in that Bronco to make the windows fog over? LM: Um, I think we should just move on. BL: Why?  Scared of what you might hear? LM: Oh please. I'm an adult. BL: Your words, not mine. [Larry can think of no witty rejoinder.]  We     like to give out a few more informal trophies which recognize     achievements other than those normally awarded.  Sparky, you wanna     hand 'em out? SL: Our first Grapplette is for the "Most Nicknames".  It goes to none     other than "Real Deal-Make 'em Squeal-Third Wheel-What's the Deal-     Long Time No Heal-Raw Deal" Luke Steele. [Much laughing from the audience.  Steele takes the statue mouthing "Real Deal... Real Deal!"] SL: This next statue is for "Most Picked On".  Its winner is... Sparkplug Lee!  Hey, that's me!  I'd like to thank my mother and my cousin Buster who drove me to Mr. Charles house of... BL: Get on with it, or I'm going to feed your filet to the Rotundos SL: The next award is for the "Finisher I'm The Most Scared Of", which is Dakota Bundy's "Firearm". [Sparkplug hands over the Grapple, Dakota pats his forearm and sneers.  A chuckle begins in a remote corner, soon that chuckle begins a laugh, the laugh a roar.  Cries of "Oooh..." "Firearm"... "I'm soooo scared!"  Matt Malone reaches for the mic, but 'Plug pulls it away.] SL: The next is for "Best Jobber". [Ned Norton stands, but Sparkplug hands the Statuette to a very unamused Scott Rogers.] SL: Our next award is for "Most Likely To Get Sneak Attacked", whose     winner would have been Marty Warnett. BL: Marty bailed. SL: The "Most Likely to get pushed... off a cliff" trophy goes to... "Soundbite" Steve Roberts! SR: Bring me the biscuit, Sparky. SL: Our last little award for the moment is the "Loose Screw Award" which goes to... Steve Manning?! SM: Damn right, my man!  Always loose and ready to screw. ["Biggest Surprise" appears on the video board.] LM: It seems you can never turn your back in this federation or you'll miss something.  Shoot, I feel like _I_ don't know what's going on half the time. BL: You don't. LM: Um, this last year has seen a lot of interesting things occur, but we can't acknowledge them all, so here are the nominees. [Video:  Cold Spell and requiem leave Genesis, Brody Thunder's "Triple Cross", Requiem winning the championship tournament, Spur revealled as Bily Shakespeare, Dan Kauffman retiring.] LM: The winner, as voted by the wrestlers of the IIWF... Brody Thunder. [Thunder, dressed in a black western-style tuxedo with thunderbolt symbol bolero tie with red ties, stands and makes his way to the podium. Once there he accepts the award, sets it on the podium and shakes his head in sarcastic disgust.] BT: The "Biggest Surprise" huh?     Lemme tell ya what the "biggest surprise" is. It's the fact     that I ain't up here receivin' the "best wrestler" award. This... [Thunder holds up the award, waving it in his hand.]     ..._this_here_ ain't nuthin' but a slap in my face. Was it a_big_     _surprise_that I so-called _triple-crossed_ some folks who'd have     done the same damn thing to me given half-a-chance? Was it some     _big_surprise_ that I won th' flamin' world title after I went out     there an' tol' ya I would?  Well, if it was, my friends, then ya     jus' ain't been payin' attention. So it's time ta prove ta alla o'     you jus' why that world strap's on _my_ waist. An' bein' the generous man I am, I wanna hand out a little award o' my own... [He sets the award back on the podium and stares out at the audience. He then slowly turns in the direction of one man.]     ...ta you, Kowalski. It's a real, real special award. The "Big     Mistake" award. See, that's what ya made the minute ya dropped     me on my head, pal. An' it's an award I'm gonna personally present     ta ya. But not here. Not tonight. So y'all go on an' have yer     little mutual admiration society get-together. Hand out alla yer     precious lil awards. As fer this piece o' trash, I got the perfect     spot ta put it... [Thunder smiles an evil grin. He reaches down below the podium and lifts out a small garbage bucket.]     ...an' that's right where it belongs. [He promptly drops the award into the trash and throws it back under the podium forcefully.  He then walks off, but makes eye contact with Steve "the Fury" Kowalski. Thunder's comments to him are be picked up faintly as he winks and says "Payback's comin'". Thunder returns to his seat as the proceedings continue.  "Nifty" Ned Norton takes to the microphone.] NN: Hey!  Jobber Justice! [The room is deathly quiet.] NN: Um, hi y'all. I've probably been downed by every finisher in this     federation.  I can tell you they all hurt.  Here are the ones that     _you_ claim as the best. ["Best Finisher" flashes on the video, soon replaced by footage of the nominated finishers:  Lord Byron's "Aristoclutch", Mad Dog Watkins' "Every Dog Has a Day", Creed's "Goodnight, Farewell, Amen", The Syndicate's "Syndi-cutter", Steve Kowalski's "Skullpump", and J.W. Hardin's "Cattle Buster"] NN: Are we allowed to show that last one or is it protected as     intellectual property? TD: Let's see him sue. NN: And the winner is, and this one _really_ hurts.  I mean it.  Steve     "The Fury" Kowalski's "Skullpump"! [Upon hearing his name called, Kowalski slides his feet off the table. His table is all the way in the back and he seems to be in no real hurry. This is evident, as he bites a piece off the end of a stogie and starts to light up. Seeing as he will take his time to get to the podium, the production crew runs his SKULLPUMP highlight spot.] [The familiar "Don't Fear the Reaper" begins as the collage of Fury clips start to flash by. Starting off with The New Jersey Nightmare dragging his thumb across his throat. Visions of the Intercontinental belt and the Cruiserweight belt fade in and out. It starts off with Kowalski's debut on IIWF Saturday Night... Kowalski grabs Sandman in a double underhook, then hoists him up in a piledriver position. He drives Sandman's head into the mat and goes for the cover... 1 - 2 - 3! Ding! Ding! Ding! Huge heel pop!] LM: Kowalski just dominated that match! BL: This guy's really going to cause some waves in the IIWF, that's for sure. "Skull-pump! Skull-pump!" [In the next scene: Kowalski and Onslaught go at it in the ring, and the Fury manages to gain the upper hand, nailing Onslaught in the midsection and doubling him over, enabling him to hit the Skullpump. Huge heel pop! Kowalski revels in the incredible heat he receives from the Mexican crowd, and then picks Kauffman up to dish out more of the same. Kowalski executes the Skullpump on Kauffman as well, and the crowd are on their feet, hurling objects into the ring.] TD: This is completely uncalled for! Kowalski has laid out Kauffman and Onslaught... "Skull-pump! Skull-pump!" [Next is the match that set up Kowalski's first title. Kowalski rolls Sexton back in the ring and delivers the Skullpump piledriver on the unconscious Sexton. Kowalski rolls him over for the cover as the ref counts - 1 - 2 - 3! Kowalski then raises his arm in victory to a fair sized heel pop along with some hard-core cheers. "Skull-pump! Skull-pump!" [The screen then fades to Last years Snow brawl: Kowalski puts Shakespeare's head between his legs and underhooks first one arm, and then the other.] TD: Oh no! He's going for the Skullpump! [Kowalski performs the vicious double underhook piledriver on Shakespeare and deliberately makes the cover. The referee counts - 1 - 2 - 3! Ding! Ding! Ding!] SR: Yes! He got him! We've got a new champion! "Skull-pump! Skull-pump!" [It now blurs into the infamous Cruiserweight match up between White Pheonix and the New Jersey Nightmare.] SR: Here it comes! New champ! TD: No! [Kowalski looks around at the Furies, who are starting the all-too familiar chant, and leaps...] SR: SKULLPUMP! Cover that man! [Kowalski covers, and the referee slowly rolls over... 1 -- 2 -- 3! The referee slowly raises his arm, signaling for the bell...] TD: I don't believe it... SR: Yes! New champ! RA: Here is your winner, and _NEW_ IIWF Cruiserweight Champion of the World... Steve "The Fury" Kowaaaalllsssssssskiiiiii!! "Skull-pump! Skull-pump!" [Spreadbury reveals the Fury as the Danny Dynamite's suprise opponent: Kowalski picks Dynamite to his feet -- hooks up the arms and yells "This one's for you, Jersey!" before planting 3D hard into the canvas with the Skullpump and the cover... 1 -- 2 -- 3! Ding! Ding! Ding!] SL: Your winner as a result of a pinfall... STEVE "THE FURY" KOWALSKI! "Skull-pump! Skull-pump!" [Go for the Gold Battle Royal...] TD: That's a Skullpump! Kowalski just Skullpumped Cheshire and dropped him like a cheating girlfriend over the top rope! "Skull-pump! Skull-pump!" [Ending careers...] TD: Good grief, no! Kowalski can't Skullpump Acorn on the outside; that could put Randy out of action permanently! SR: [overjoyed] Go Kowalski, go! [Kowalski drags Acorn up, and hooks one elbow, then the other. The jeers of the crowd reach a crescendo as Kowalski Skullpumps Acron right onto the arena floor, driving his skull into the ground with ferocious impact. "Skull-pump! Skull-pump!" [Finally finishing with recent history...] TD: STEVE "THE FURY" KOWALSKI! SR: Hot damn, Dross! It's the Fury! TD: It's Kowalski -- and he has Thunder... oh my! [As the lights rise once more, the figure of the New Jersey Nightmare, Steve "the Fury" Kowalski, is revealed in the ring, standing with Brody Thunder's head between his legs, and both arms hooked, hooked in preparation... Kowalski jumps into the air, and sits down sharply, driving Thunder's head into the mat with a...] TD & SR: SKULLPUMP! [Huge, huge pop! Even those brawling around the ring pause as they hearth crash of Thunder's head hitting the canvas, Kowalski standing and raising his arms to the crowd, who greet him with a near ecstatic reaction!] TD: Oh my! Oh... my. Words fail me, Steve Roberts. What else can we possibly see here tonight? SR: The Fury is back! Whoo-hoo! [At this point, Kowalski is at the podium, looking back at the screen. He lights his cigar and continues.] SK: That last one was the sweetest. But it won't be the last. Right, cowpoker? [Smiling at Brody Thunder] For the rest o' ya punchin' bags that haven't felt the SKULLPUMP, let me explain it to ya. There's a whole damn process to it. First off, I beat the piss outta ya! Then ya gotta bow down to the man. [Pointing to himself] That's when I'm hookin' ya. At this point yer ears are ringin' an' yer blood is rushin' to yer head, 'cause now yer upside down. It's gotta feel like an eternity, waitin' for me to put yer lights out. Then... Crunch! I drop ya! Yer feet go numb and yer head screams! Neck, turnin' to jelly, just flops over. Ten minutes later, ya wake up. Bloody, embarrassed an' one concussion closer to retirement. [Kowalski snatches his award from "Nifty" Ned Norton and pulls the mic of the podium. He walks down to the front row of tables. He first walks past Tony Starks' table.] SK: For those of ya punks that think ya got meaner finisher than the SKULLPUMP... [puts his cigar out in Starks' drink] then bring it on! [Starks is about to confront Kowalski but is held at bay by others at the table.] SK: I thought so. This fed's been goin' soft since I've been gone. [The New Jersey Nightmare grabs a steak off the plate of Ike Sampson and takes a bite. Then quickly drops it back on the plate. Sampson with a sneer and looks to get up until the Fury says...] SK: Some more than others. Well, I'm gonna change all that. I'm gonna show you pinheads how it's done. Lead by example. [Turning to Thunder's table] Since every team needs a strong leader an' there's none here. [Kowalski stands over the IIWF champion, licking his fingers from the steak. Thunder looks up, eyes smoldering, but keeps under control. Kowalski leans over and whispers in Thunder ear, loud enough for everyone to here.] SK: If yer feeling tuff, tuff guy, I got my own table in the back. Since ya didn't send me a card in the hospital, I figgered ya were hopin' I died. Not this time, cowpoker. [Kowalski back up and tosses the mic to the stage and walks back to his table, shouting...] SK: Enjoy the rest of yer evening, [BLEEP]bags! [As the applause dies down, Tony Starks stalks his way to the front.] TS: Bring it on mother... [A stern glare from the President stops him.]     Listen up.  I been destryoing half the wrestlers in this federation     these last months.  So the suits come to me and say "Tony, if you     stop hurting our superstars we'll let you give out an award at our     little show."  I'm thinking that these boys aren't grovelling enough     before Mama Starks' little boy.  Just another way to hold me down and make me be their slave.  But then I get to thinking, hey, I got a face made for T.V.  And you want "faces"?  Here they are.. [The banner "Best Face" morphs into shots of teh nominees: Marty Warnett, Ned Norton, Chris Quigley, Brody Thunder, Joe Petrow, Steve Kowalski, Dan Kauffman.] TS: Your winner for "Best Face" is Joe Petrow. [Once again, he's asked to come on stage, once again he gives his count, and this time most of the heels join in in the one fingered salute. Meanwhile, Starks quickly heads to his table in the back, kicking his heels up on the table. Again, Billy Shakespeare steps to the lectern.] BS: Those were the "good guys"?  Petrow?  Thunder?  Kowalski?  I'm back     again to present the award for best heel. Every script needs villains to fight the heroes, and the IIWF is no different. Let's bring on the bad guys. [The video morph begins: Requiem, Otto Verhoevan, Lord Byron, Casey James, Mad Dog Watkins.] BS: And the baddest of the bad is... Casey "Blackheart" James.  Well, as we all know, Casey and the Syndicate have left the fed so... [As if on cue, there's a loud noise at the doors at the end of the hall, and everyone turns to see what is causing it. The doors have been swung wide open, and standing there is none other than Casey James, dressed in jeans and a T-shirt with a picture of the front of a tuxedo drawn on the front. Casey strides towards the stage, patting old friends on the shoulder, sneering at old enemies, grabbing a bottle of Mooselips from a passing waiter's tray, causing a Kowalski like voice to yell out "Hey, that was mine, ya freak!" Finally, James reaches the podium, snatches the award, and begins to speak.] CJ: Well, it's nice to see that some things haven't changed. These awards are just as cheap this year as they were last year... So... I'm the best bad guy, huh? Even though I ain't around anymore, I'm still the baddest of the bunch. Badder than Thunder, badder than Requiem... Am I surprised? Hell no. I could have told you that I was the best at what I do without some stupid secret ballot. I figured it might be nice to talk about what it is that makes me so great, so that the IIWF can salvage some ticket sales without me being here. What makes a bad guy bad, and why the hell do I do it so well? It comes down to one thing, pure and simple. I don't give a damn. I go to the ring and I do my job. I win. I don't care if the mutants in the seats like me, or if they hate me. I don't care if any of you wrestlers out there think I'm a stand up guy or not. Do you know why? Because, to be honest, your opinions just don't measure up to a capfull of piss in my books. I do what I do the way I want to do it. _I'm_ the guy that held your world title for the longest period of time. _I'm_ the guy that brought Kauffman down a notch. _I'm_ the guy that is the baddest. If you've got a problem with that, then you're welcome to try and change my ways. I'm not a hard guy to find. I'm here by myself tonight... Well, not by myself... I've got a date, so I need to wrap this up soon, because she charges by the hour. Anyway, the rest of the Syndicate decided not to come. They don't want to. I, however, wanted to, and that's how it works. I look out for number one. Me. That, you boneheads, is what being the bad guy is all about. Now if you'll excuse me, I've got plans with a tramp in red... [Casey walks off the stage and out the door, being sure to rub the award in everyone's faces  There is total silence for a moment which is suddenly broken by a shout as it is realized that the waiter serving fresh rolls is none other than Steve Summer in disguise.  Security quickly closes, dragging the intern back outside.] BL: Well, well, well.  Time fo a few more of those wacky awards.  Take it away, Sparkplug. SL: Our first award is for the "Best Harmonica Player".  No coincidence     that it goes to Leavenworth legend Richard "Moxy" Blue.  Our next     Grapplette is for the "Best Dressed", which is awarded to Scott "the     Fop" Rogers. [Rogers bows, to the excitement of his beautiful companions.] SL: This next award "Loudest man in the IIWF" inexplicably goes to Adam     Smith.  To Dakota Bundy, he of the many facial scars, goes the     Grapple for "Best Mark Messier lookalike".  This next is the "Just     Keeps Going and Going" Grapple, it's for the passing of the Pheonix     legacy to Natural Predator Wolf.  Here's a Grapple for "Best Mascot"     We present it to the late Shock the Taaser.  Our last little award     this last go around was voted on by the inmates of Leavenworth     Penitentiary.  "Best Ass in the IIWF" goes to Richard "Moxy" Blue. [Blue is quite excited to recieve the trophy.  He holds it high above his head, finally climbing upon a chair to do so.  Suddenly the chair is pulled out from under him as Blue is blindsided unexpectedly by Alex Rio. Blue's chin bounces off the table, diced carrots fly, buttered peas pelt Chris Quigley, Reggie Starr receives a lapful of mashed potatoes.  Alex Rio deftly steals the "Best Ass" grapple.  Security is quick to drag Alex away while the crack medical team holds a handful of smelling salts under Blue's nose.  "Moxy" seems to enjoy it. President SPreadbury steps up to the mic. He is about to speak when there is noticed an irritating whine is running through the room.  After a quick search of the air conditioning system, it's noticed that the sound is coming from Sparkplug who is running his finger around the rim of his glass.] DS: In truth, I hate this next award. No single announcer should be     singled out above one another.  Each lends his, or her, talents and     expertise to form one of the greatest teams in wrestling broadcast     history.  But I'm told that you enjoy this award, so I have little     choice. [The video breaks into quarters, each one holding a live action shot of Tim, Larry, Steve and Becky.] DS: This year's "Best Announcer" award goes to Steve "Soundbite" Roberts. [Van Halen's "Running with the Devil" fills the hall and a dozen bikini clad young women, each more buxom than the previous, strut on out to the stage.  Whoops and hollers go up from those who are so inclined as the women bounce to the podium, each notably toting sashes snugly attached around their respective carraiges reading, "Miss Hawaiian Tropic '97". From his side table, Sparkplug Lee places down his cup of mixed fruit, standing and saying:] SL: Ladies and Gentlemen, the Two Time Grapple Award Winning, Steve "Soundbite" Roberts! [The women hold their arms open wide, saying together, "Shoot, Soundbite! Shoot!" The man himself then emerges on stage, the vacation clearly having done him well as he appears tanned and rested, with a several day growth of beard now appearing on his face.  Roberts is wearing his customary leather jacket along with a blue t-shirt with the word, "Who?", his final accessory is a pair of red horns which are prominently affixed to his head.] SR: Hello, Morons, Jobbers, Imps, Wanna-Bes, Has-Beens, Never-Weres, Pre-Operative Transsexuals and Dakota Bundy, the ugliest man on the face of the earth... Thank you. See, without you, I wouldn't be here.  And I accept these two awards, for Best Announcer and Most Likely To Be Admitted Into the Hall Of Fame... on behalf of all you little people.  People who will never get the opportunity to stand here and ever accept even one of these prestigious Golden Grapple Awards... Much less two.  Like me. This award is for Cheshire, the "crying on the inside, jobbing on the outside" clown... For Nightwing, who never met a golf ball he couldn't force through a garden hose without using his hands... For Mr. Robinson, whose stay in the IIWF proved he is indeed the greatest wrestler of all time and well deserving of his 701-0 record... For Billy Shakespeare, you know, the longer he's gone the more I sorta miss the little guy, with his clubberin' fists and his French dog Pepe... And most of all, Dakota Bundy.  The ugliest man on the face of the earth. Without all you losers, White Flight, the Black Pack, Luke "Why Can't I Turn Heel" Steele, the Boyz From the Rage, Boy Requiem and Culture Club, Maggie "The Cat" Paris, Chrissie and Troy, all the gay tag wrestlers everywhere in the world... Without all of you sucking so damn much... I wouldn't look so damn good. Thanks, Morons! [Roberts waves with a flourish as the chant of "Shoot, Soundbite! Shoot!" begins again, Sparkplug Lee standing and saying:] SL: The Soundbite has left the building!  The Soundbite has left the building! RR: They want me to introduce another sorry batch of nobodies and never-will-bes.  Wrestlin' ain't wrestlin' today.  No, it's just a bunch of costumed sissy boys jumping around the ring.  Any of you IIWF superstars would wilt in five seconds against the likes of Eddie Koscuizkowzkalak or El Chingadero Gigante... may they both rest in peace.  Those were real men.  When they put a full nelson on you, you knew your time was up.  Nothing funny, just real wrestling.  I'd pit my leg drop against any of your so called finishers. DDT?  You use that on flowers, not wrestlers. Look at these boys I'm supposed to introduce.  "Creep".  What kind of a name is that? BL: Re-read the card you old fart. RR: "Crud"?  "Greed"? BL: Creed!  His name is Creed! RR: Creed?  Never heard of him.  Is he any good?  Who are these other     guys?  Rec... Rec... lordy, last year I had to say "Enema", now I gotta say this.  No way. [Requiem walks slowly to the front.  He is dresed in diffent attire than earlier, now wearing a shimmering black satin tuxedo, complete with black bowtie and black shirt. He is, in fact, dressed entirely in black. Black glasses conceal his white eyes. A small silver guitar shaped lapel badge is pinned to his right lapel.] RQ: Tonight I would like to thank many people. I would like to thank those who voted for me, I would like to thank the Genesis Generation for believing in me, and I would like to thank the fans of the IIWF. And I would like to thank a few others. I would like to thank Genesis, but alas I cannot for it seems that the once mighty stable that Adam Smith, Nightwing and I formed so many months ago has descended into chaos. Nevertheless, for those members of Genesis who still consider me their friend I say, "thank you." I would _like_ to thank Joe Petrow, for it was he who opened my eyes. Alas, it was also Joe Petrow who decided to stick his nose into my affairs in my last ever match in NLWP, so I won't thank him. Though Soultaker and I rarely see eye to eye, we are both agreed that you must pay. I'm sure that Soultaker will see to that! Joe Petrow, "Dark Saviour", do me the favour of rotting in hell. I would also like to thank Otto Verhoeven. Otto, we faced each other three times in all, and you proved that you are a worthy opponent to any man living. I beat you once, with interference I neither asked for, nor approved of. You beat me once when this "Blind Guardian" punk interfered in our affairs. And I beat you once more, in Japan, when nobody interfered. Do the math, Otto. If I should ever return to IIWF, I will welcome facing you one more time. And, of course, I would like to thank Deathbringer. Deathbringer, I would like to thank you for making me look good on the many occasions that we fought. Deathbringer, you may bring death but that is something I have never had any fear of, as you found out. For a man who relies so much on fear, trepidation and image it must have been quite a shock to stumble into someone who held no fear of you, who was as willing to embrace the darkness as you were. I humbled the once mighty Deathbringer, and you were never quite the same afterwards, were you? And I would like to thank you for an excellent match at Ring Wars IV, Deathbringer. Tell me, was this pathetic "Blind Guardian" a servant of yours? Was this a deliberate collusion on your part to bring yourself back into the embrace of the IIWF? Or was this "Blind Guardian" affair a fortunate moment of opportunity? Regardless, I'd like to thank you for saving the IIWF the embarrassment of watching a half-crippled Requiem beat the hell out of a tired old man. It was a good match, was it not, Deathbringer? The _mighty_ Deathbringer versus Requiem. Deathbringer versus the Angel of Destruction. Deathbringer against the man with a punctured lung and four broken ribs. It was quite a close match, Deathbringer, don't you think? You might like to think of just how close it would be should you ever face a fully healed Angel of Destruction again.
