The Creation of the KWF (Out Of Character Version) One night around 11:30 PM eastern time, I tried to catch some shut eye. But my mind would have nothing of it. I keep thinking about e-wrestling and how federations work. What they're flaws are and how usual they all are, except for a select few. Then I got to thinking, why not start up my own? Why not create the unsual ascept to a federation I've been searching for by never found? So I have....
I am the main man, [name withheld]. I am the one who tells the simulator (for now) what to write. I am the one who books the cards, recruits and does everything. I soon hope to add a vice-president and maybe even a commishioner. But right now, it is just little old me back here trying to run one of the best feds on the net.
You might have noticed the word "crazy" is spelled wrong and yes, it is on purpose. I got tired of seeing E's, X's, H's and C's in federation names. So
I changed crazy to krazy to had a different tune ot the fed. Continue to read on, please. II. The Real KWF (In-character) The dream of a young man who grew up watching wrestling has become true. Playing with Daddy's millions young Ray Thomas has now created himself what he thinks to be a elite wrestling force. He is a very experienced in the business world, but not in the wrestling world. That is his only major flaw.
Ray is currenlty working on signing big names and getting to the big arenas. He already has PPV's lined up. Now he needs the talent and a cable net work willing to carry such a unorthodox type of wrestling.
Ray is working very hard and will make KWF succeed, even if its a tough, tough job. KWF brings a hard working owner that will get into the action. He will make decisions, he will do his best. He brings unique tournaments and ideas to the table. He brings a place for new talent to prosper.
III. The Rules of The KWF Flashing - You will be required to flash at least once a week. You have the chance to flash three times. But doing three aight flashes against a man who does one great one, doesn't always mean your going to win. The flash packet deadline are as followed:
Sunday 8 PM Eastern Tuesday 8 PM Eastern Thursday 8 PM Eastern Strategies - On the site, I hope to get, I will provide a template for you guys to use. It is a simple thing that includes: Overall strategy, offensive and defensive strategy, spots and moves. The stratgy deadline is as follows:
Wednesday 12 AM Eastern The General Rules - They are the standard common sense rules. Keep swearing the min., no TOSing, no badgering a president or another member of KWF. No complaing of a loss without a reason. Other basic stuff too.
IV. The Champions The KWF will start out with a few titles. The are as follows: KWF World Extreme Championship - The main title, the big prize. This is the official title of the KWF. it is defended once every thirty days and is set with a 45 min. time limit with no DQ, no count out rules.
KWF Uncensored Violence Championship - The hardcore type title for the KWF. Defended 1 every two weeks with a 30 min time limit with any thing goes rules. Will normally have a gimmick match if its needed. KWF Small Dude's Championship - The title for the small guys. Defended every week and only for those at 245 and under. Will come into play with KWF gets enough members.
KWF Unified Tag Team Titles - Defended once every thirty days. Will come into play when enough tag teams are created. The titles are easy to get if your a hard working man and have the skill to win them. There is no freebies here in the KWF. You work for everything.
V. The Special Stuff yes, KWF has its own brand of fun times. HardKore to the Bone - A three night tournament to crown the most extreme, most vicious fighter within the KWF ranks. I request people in here and if I feel like it, I might stick you in.
One Krazy Night - A one night only tournament to crown the true King of the
KWF. The winner recieves a world title shot, a gold medal and one big check for $750,000 w/o taxes.
More to come... VI. The Cards If your still reading this, thank you. I really appreciate it. Now I like one card a week. It gets people more involved, more active. THose who are lazy don't get booked and fired soon enough.
Kraziness At Its Best - A friday night card that holds the best of the best in
KWF. Normally everyone is involved. A 3 hour show packed with hardcore action and thrills.
I might had a second card if the KWF gets big enough to support a writing staff. VII. The Application Now, if you've read it this far along. Why not join? Or at least ask to be placed on the list.
