MBC Up Your Arse 5: Overkill, 17 May 1998

17 May 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
It's that grandma on the highway who's going 35 in a 55 zone. You can't pass and no matter what you do, she won't go any faster. It's enough that you wish you had a monster truck to run the old hag over. But that would be....... It's that annoying neighbor next door who blasts his John Tesh and Yanni 24/7. He steals your newspaper every morning and he even kicked your dog. It's enough where you want to take a crow bar and beat the living hell out of his new 1998 Corvette. But that would be......... Its' that guy at work who slaps you on the back and tells you lame jokes. You hate the annoying son of a bitch but you can't get rid of him because he's your bosss nephew. You just wish you had five minutes alone with him and a lead pipe to show him the true meaning of "WHY ME? WHY ME?" But that would be........ It's exactly what you want to do but you've never been able to do it before until now. Fo the first time ever you can shove it some up someone' ass and give them a good taste of..... O-V-E-R-K-K-I-L-L ("Overkill" by Motorhead begins to play as a sped up montauge of differant MBC wrestlers begins to show on the screen. Some violent images are shown as well as some footage of car crashes and train wrecks. The screen suddenly catces fire and a hole burns into the screen revealing the UYA5 logo as it grows larger. The black background fades to a shot of the sold out arena crowd.) ============================ UP YOUR ARSE 5: OVERKILL ============================ May 17, 1998 at Philadelphia, PA ============================ Slush: Explain something to me Pinhead. Pinhead: What is it now? Slush: Why is it that when they make a new movie they'll spend million's of dollars but they can make the same exact movie for 5 dollars. Pinhead: Slush, when I get filthy stinking rich, I'll hire someone to explain things to you. Skullhead: Welcome ladies and gentlemen to Up Your Arse 5: Overkill being brought to you live from Philadelphia Slush: Who are these ECW freaks? Skullhead: For the first time tonight, a MBC Broadcast is being sent to New Zealand and most of South America Pinhead: Are we on in Japan? Skullhead: We have been since Kingdom Come Pinhead: Well good. Slush: Time to go Pinhead: Huh? Where are you doing? Skullhead: Don't bother to ask. (Some demented version of "Frosty the Snowman" begins to play over the PA system as all the house lights go out and the spotlights come up to shine on the Nest O"Love set.) Slush: Yet again I am here to annoy and piss off every single one of you. I don't have any stories about the Chipmunks. I was going to bitch about Disney but I'm sure they get enough hassles from the Southern Baptist Convention. Anywhoo, it's time for..... ============================ SLUSH'S NEST O'LOVE ============================ Slush: So get up on your feet or whatever you Philly freaks do and welcome, Crimson, Infinite Zero and the Beast, THE ALLIANCE (The three members of the Alliance make their way to the stage as "Mister Crowley" begins to play, accompanied by their manager Lord Deimos. Crimson, dressed in his red hooded-robe and carrying his 8-ball staff, leads with his two stablemates on either side of him, the Beast currently unchained. The Beast is only wearing his shorts as usual, and Infinite Zero is dressed in a white full-body glove with only a large black "0" on his chest and a smaller one on his forehead. The audience cheers but gets a little nervous when they see the Beast looming around, not restrained in any way.) Slush: I'd say welcome but you've beaten me up before Infinite Zero: It probably has something to do with you being a goon. The Beast: <grunt>smash<grunt> Slush: Anyway, the Alliance has been quiet but so have the other anti-IOH groups, the Dogs of War and Ministry. However, Ministry and the Dogs of War have added new members. Any new members for the Alliance? Crimson: Patience. We'll get to that in a later. Slush: Later? But I don't want late... (The Beast swings the mike stand and cracks it over Slush's head) Slush: AARRHHHGGH!! Crimson: When I and Lord Deimos founded the Alliance early in this year, we had one goal in mind: destruction of the IOH. When I was part of the HOD we got rid of Vengeance, but apparently we didn't do a good enough job. Deimos: Since most of the HOD is dissolved, retired, or otherwise occupied, we have taken it upon ourselves to destroy Vengeance and his minions once and for all. Crimson: Others have come along with the same goal, and sadly we haven't had much success so far. That will change tonight. Slush: (Getting up) That's fine and dandy but the Alliance is in a good amount of action tonight . First you are challenging Absence for the AWC World title. Crimson: Answered simply enough. Absence is the true enemy of my good friend and long-time tag team partner, Rook. Because of complications, mostly on the IOH's part, Rook was never able to extract his revenge in a proper fashion. Being as close to Rook as I was, I figured it was my duty to take up where he left off. I'm here to destroy Absence. Him being part of the IOH is merely a bonus. Slush: Okay, well what about the Ten man tag match. You're goign to have to get along with the Dogs of War and you haven't gotten along that well before Infinite Zero: There hasn't been much of a quarrel between the three major anti-IOH groups and we hope to keep it that way. The Alliance's only concern is the destruction of the Industry of Hate. We have no bias against anyone else. We respect them all. Slush: Well we'll just have to see, won't we? Crimson: That's it. You're going down, Frosty. (Crimson whacks Slush over the head with his 8-Ball staff a few dozen times) Slush: AH DAMNIT! (Crimson then takes the microphone) Crimson: In our fight against the IOH, we have attracted a new member or two to join our cause and restore the MBC to it's former glory. We have one willing to join now, and we have another who will join us once he gets back..from wherever it is he is. When he rejoins the MBC and becomes a full-time wrestler, he will join us as well. His identity I will not reveal at this time. Slush: So? Who the hell is the new guy? (Lord Deimos takes the microphone and, voice booming, says the following..) Deimos: Ladies and gentlemen, and IOH members, welcome the newest member of the Alliance... SILVER HAND! ("The Theme from Broken Arrow" begins to play as Silver Hand comes to the Nest set wearing an Alliance T-shirt. He walks up to them set and takes the microphone.) Silver Hand: Since I joined this league I've seen the kind of bad things that have been going on. And through it all there's been one shining light that never gives in to the evil. That is the Alliance. When Crimson came to me asking if I wanted to join, I had no hesitations. Now I hope to help them achieve their goal. Slush: So do you all think you have a chance against the IOH? (The Beast leaps on Slush and begins pounding him into the ground. Crimson once again takes the microphone.) Crimson: We have more than a chance. Their members are weak and they're nothing more than a freak show. And not even a good one at that. The time of the IOH is finally waning, and I will be sure that the Alliance is standing over their pitiful remains when they crumble into nothing. (After everyone gives one last kick to the bastard snowman, they turn and leave, Deimos bringing up the rear, speaking with the newest addition to the Alliance) Pinhead: It's always such a good feeling when Slush's get a beaten like a dog. Skullhead: I think Slush is more along the lines of a rabid dog Pinhead: No. I'd say we was like one of those three legged dogs Skullhead: No. He's one of those dogs on wheels because they can't use their hind legs Pinhead: No. He's that ugly snaggletooth tog that drools Skullhead: He's that chiwawa from the Taco Bell comercials Pinhead: I don't think we can get lower than that Slush: I need hazard pay. Skulhlead: You signed a waiver for that in your contract Slush: Where is this so called contract? I've seen it once and I don't remember all of these clauses that you mention Pinhead: You were most likely in a drunken stupir from having to much NyQuil. Slush: You can never have to much NyQuil Pinhead: Yeah maybe if you're a horse Skullhead: Let's look over the card for tonight. ============================ UP YOUR ARSE 5: OVERKILL ------------------------ May 17, 1998 at: Philadelphia, PA AWC WORLD HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPIONSHIP MATCH --Crimson vs. Absence TEN MAN TAG TEAM MATCH --The Widowmakers/Happysayer/Superfly/Primetime ---vs. ----Ogre/Blackstarre/Thufir/The Beast/Infinite Zero NO DISQUALIFICATIONS TEAM BASTARD CHAMPIONSHIP TITLE MATCH --Champions: The Firebirds ---vs. ----The Assassins --"Big" Ernest Nodine & Jimmy "The Pitbull" Walker ---vs. ----"The Shotgun" Stan Crawford & ??? (Crawford's partner will be drawn at random) (If Nodine's team wins, he gets a SBC Title Shot) (If Crawford's team wins, his partnr gets a SBC title shot) STANLEY CUP TOURNAMENT CHAMPIONSHIP MATCH --Brigade vs. The Eternal Bachelor MIGHTY BASTARD CHAMPIONSHIP TITLE MATCH --Champion: The Disgruntled Postal Worker ---vs. ----Simon O'Neal ============================ Skullhead: Moving on RING ANNOUNCER: The following contest is scheduled for one fall and is for the AWC World Heavyweight Championship. Introducing first, here is the challenger. Representing the Alliance, here is CRIMSON ("Mister Crowley" by Ozzy Osbourne begins to play over the PA system)
