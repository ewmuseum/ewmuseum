PWC Rulebook, May 1997 Edition

11 May 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Greetings! As you can figure out from the subject of this post, I am here to announce the opening of a new E-fed League, the Professional Wrestling Coalition, or PWC for short. But what makes this league any different then the others? Why would you want to join? I hope to have the answers to these and more questions below. The PWC has 4 titles available. They are: - PWC Championship Title This title is open to any wrestler. It is defended at least once a month at PPV events, and wherever else required. - PWC Tag Team Titles These belts are available to Tag Team competitors. Defended once a month at PPV, and where else appropriate. - PWC Trans-Continental Title This title is equivalent to the WWF Intercontinental belt. Open to any wrestler, and defended at PPV, and at least one card show a month. - PWC All-Star Team Titles. These titles are for a 4 man tag team, called All-Star Teams. Defended annually at one PPV in April, and wherever else appropriate. There is 1 PPV per month in the PWC, and 3 cards per week. This may change, but will remain steady for now. The PWC is a mixed gender fed, accepting wrestlers that are male or female. If you interested in joining, or in obtaining more information about the PWC, please email at the address below.
