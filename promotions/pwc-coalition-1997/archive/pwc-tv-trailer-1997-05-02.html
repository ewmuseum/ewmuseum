PWC Television Trailer, 2 May 1997

2 May 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
{A black and white camera shot of some wrestlers doing their thing in the ring. This is followed by a shot of other people wrestlting.} [Voice Over] "For years, the world has watched many wrestling feds come, and it has seen many feds go." {Scenes of wrestlers being defeated, then the screen goes into full colour, as the sounds explodes into what sounds like a heavy metal band. The logo of the PWC zips onto the screen, and is bolted down.} [Voice Over] "Now, the PWC intends to rock the wrestling world forever! With top notch competitors, action packed cards, and plenty of mouth watering excitement, you wont want to turn anywhere else. And if you want to be a part of this legacy that will take the entertainment world by storm, then send your resume to the Board of Directors. Positions are limited, so hurry up and move, cause the world wont wait. Join the PWC today! PWC, the Authority in Wrestling Entertainment!"
