The PWC is an excellant fed that been running well considering the low number of wrestlers, however, it has recently came in need of good quality wrestlers.. this fed needs people who will at least flash once a week to keep active;) so if you are interested in trying something new, with a spice of life.. give this fed a try;) email [detail withheld] with this application if you are interested:) *--* 7/23/97 - 4:32:04 PM *--* Hey Gang Just Virgo's handler here. Forgive me for using the OWA mailing list for self gain, but since the OWA is on two month vacation, I couldnt think of another time to do it. A new e-fed is opening, and is need of wrestlers. Now, far be it for me to to push things on anyone, but since it is my fed, I would like to have some real talent in this fed, and the OWA has been by far some of the best RP talent in E-feds I have seen. So, take a look at the following, and if you are interested, send me the form, and we'll be in touch. -------------------------- PWC- Professional Wrestling Coalition -------------------------- The PWC is a new e-fed that will hopefully become one of the more known feds around. We accept wrestlers from all genders (male/female/other), from all walks of life. The PWC accepts only original wrestlers, and no ripoffs of existing wrestlers, please. Titles ----- The PWC currently has 3 titles: The PWC Championship Title, the PWC Tag Team Titles, and the PWC Continental Titles. This may grow, but only when the fed grows in size. The sooner it does, the sooner new titles will be introduced. The PWC Championship Title is given to the best wrestler in the PWC, and is defended at least once a month. Can be won by anyone. The PWC Continental Title is initially earned in a tournament, then defended at least twice a month. Can be won by anyone. The PWC Tag Team Titles is earned initially through a tag team tournament, and defended twice a month at least. Can be won only by Tag Teams. Referees --------- The PWC has private referees on their payroll. Here is a list of them. Jeff Maxwell - A fair referee, who knows the rules, but knows how to please the crowd as well. Mikal Postovich - A ref from Russia, with experience in less strict federations. Has been known to have a high tolerance of rule violations. Ron Eddings - A guy who likes to go by the book, but not always. Will not let much slide unnoticed. Stewart Asten - The most popular ref so far in the PWC. While not about to stop a match when it starts getting good, Stewart will keep things from getting out of hand completely. A real fan favourite. Cards ------ The PWC will have 3 cards a week. Wild Wednesday, Friday de Force, and Sunday Night Savage. Wild Wednesday - A house show that is your basic Raw is War type deal. Has a nice array of matches, some news and updates, as well as a few surprises from time to time. Friday de Force - An 'All Action' card, with great matches, some out of the ordinary. A good show to watch. Sunday Night Savage - The perfect way to end the weekend. A great card, with some great action. Most often, this is the card where titles are put on the line. This show will also be pre-empted some weeks in the event of a PPV. PPV ----- The PWC will host Pay-Per-View events currently once ever 5 to 6 weeks. As the fed grows, that may change to once a month. Some titles we have so far include House of Havoc, Summer Storm, and Xcessive Xmas. Flashes ------- Flashes are a vital part to your remaining active in the PWC. You are expected to make at least one flash a month, with emphasis on Quality, rather then quantity. Also, if you are part of a main event match, or in a PPV, you are expected to have flashes sent in to me to be aired during the card. Censoring is in place, so no cursing or foul language in your flash. If you are caught cursing in your flashes, it will cost your wrestler, so dont hurt your career in the PWC, and censor. Remember, kids watch this ^_- Wrestlers ---------- You are entitled to 2 wrestlers per handler. That means you can have 2 singles, 2 tag teams, or 1 single and 1 tag team.
