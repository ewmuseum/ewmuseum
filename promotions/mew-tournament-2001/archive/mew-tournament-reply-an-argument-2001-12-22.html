"[name withheld]" < [detail withheld] > wrote in message news:[detail withheld]... > What in the blue hell does being mature have to do with running a tournament? > I think that your opinion says a lot about you as a handler. Simply put, if > you don't intend on playing in the tournament, then why should you throw your > two cents into the argument? Because it's an argument. Albeit, as foolish as the "Michinoku Armbar" argument -- but still, it's an argument in which I felt I should add my two cents and describe how spelling "tournament" as "tornoment" would incite a negative reaction from me as a handler.
What does being mature have to do with running a tournament or a fed, you ask? Someone who is mature has a good grasp on things; the person knows what he/she can or can't do. They know their limits, whereas some people just throw out insane guarantees that they couldn't possibly pull off for an extended period of time. Two cards a week? Three, maybe? A PPV/supercard each month? Please. If you make a guarantee, get ready to back up your guarantee.
Maturity also sets the standard for the quality of work you put out. If your shit comes out sloppy, it says a lot about you. I'm not saying you have to slave over a card and put it as first priority, but at least put in an effort to make it, well, *not* incoherent. I can tell you that nobody enjoys a card that looks as if it was done in one night in one whole sitting.
That's why I follow e-feds such as AVW, EMWC and am in e-feds like GIW, KCW, and RCW. They're good at what they do and I scarcely find myself saying,
"God, that was a shitty card."
While I might be a bit eccentric at times, I believe that my opinions are reasonable. > As far as a tournament being a "tall order" for a young handler or > fed-head...speak for yourself. Maybe_YOU_couldn't do it, but there are a ton > of other guys, and kids, who can. What I actually said was that it was a tall order in general, regardless of age. I also understand that there are a ton of other "guys and kids" who are able to pull this off; but if you ask them, they'll tell you that -- while it's a fun job -- it's not an easy one.

Ask anybody who's worked with me before; I'm one of the most easiest handlers/fedheads to work with.
