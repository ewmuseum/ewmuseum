Basic Information. The Tornoment: This is a tornoment to crown an outstanding wrestler. There will be 32 participants. First come first serve so apply right away if you are interested. There will be one wrestler allowed per screen name. The winner will be posted on a well known Wrestling
Site. But the main point of this is to see who the best Wrestler of
2001 really is.. There will be 3-5 judges judging each match, I have
personally already selected them. This way there is more then one person deciding who wins each match. The Tornoment should last about 3-6 weeks. I would also like to invite anyone who does not have a fed there are currently partiapating in to go to National Championship
Wrestling. SPOTS WILL FILL UP FAST SO SIGN UP RIGHT AWAY!!! Rules 1. There will be no arguing witht the Outcome of the match. 2. No spamming at all will be excepted. ( Spamming is waiting until the last day to send your roleplays. IF you wait until the last day to send any Role Plays they will not be counted. if you send more then 2 Role Plays within 3 Hours of the deadline The longest Role Play will not count)
3. Please this is too have fun so lets try and do so. and let the Fighting begin!!!! Quick App Wrestlers Name: Wrestlers Weight/Height: Manager yes/no: Enterence: (Fill in blanks) Announcer: Comming Down to the ring Weighing in at [ Blank] he hails from [Blank]. He is the [Blank]
