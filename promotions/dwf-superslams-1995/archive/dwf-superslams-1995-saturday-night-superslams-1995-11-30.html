DWF (Nov 1995) — Show

"DWF Saturday Night Superslams 11/18"

30 November 1995
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(camera opens on the Hartford Civic Center.) AL TYSON: You're looking live at "the Mall", the home of the Hartford Whalers. And tonight, it's the home of DWF Saturday Night Superslams!!! (camera cuts to inside, where Tyson and his broadcast partners are seen standing in front of a raucous crowd. The camera pans to show fans holding various signs, like "Spike Lewis is a god!!" and "army of Darkness Won't ever Forget tonight".) TYSON: Hello, everyone, we're live from the Mall, and welcome to another edition of Saturday Night Superslams!! With me as always are my broadcast partners, former DWF World TV champion "Pistol" Pete Romero (crowd pops loudly for Romero) and the self-proclaimed "manager of the year" Lord Byron Adams (crowd boos lustily, for Adams' Empire Club is currently engaged in a brutal feud with the ever-popular Red River Bandits)!!! And gentlemen, we're less than twenty-four hours away from our pay-per-view extravaganza, November Knockout!!! ROMERO: That's right, it's all going down tomorrow afternoon from venerable Fenway Park in downtown Boston. The main event as you all know, is a steel cage tag-team grudge match: DWF World Heavyweight Champion JD Power and his mystery partner take on two members of the Army of Darkness, Apocalypse and the Mongolian Brute. ADAMS: But you won't have to wait until tomorrow to see the Army in action, as they'll square off against the World Tag-Team champions in a non-title match in our main event tonight!! TYSON: That's right, Hiro Nogami and Jack Sampson will be here in the building tonight!! We'll also see another member of the Army of Darkness, Fear, as he takes on the red-hot Spike Lewis!! And we'll also have comments from the world champion himself, JD Power!! With all that in mind, let's get up to the ring for our opening bout. [RING ANNOUNCER] Ladies and Gentlemen, our first match on Saturday Night Superslams is scheduled for one fall. (A pulsing guitar riff starts up.) Coming down the aisle, from the Sunshine State of Florida, weighing in at a combined weight of 472 pounds.... "Unbelievable" Jeff Bennett.... "Sensational" Shawn Garrison.... THE HEARTBREAK EXPRESS!!!!!! (crowd reacts wildly.) TYSON: Listen to the ovation for the Heartbreak Express!! These two young heartthrobs have been capturing the hearts of imaginations of fnas across the world. (Indian war drums begin to play over the loud speakers, and the crowd reacts violently.) [RING ANNOUNCER] And their opponets, accompanied to the ring by Warpath, weighing in at a combined 507 lbs., here are Chief Joseph and Bloody Feathers.... APACHE NATION!!!! ADAMS: One of my personal favorite tag-teams. They are currently waging war everyone down in the Carolinas, and they're my pick to win the Carolinas Tag-Team belts in the upcoming tournament. TYSON: In fact, both of these teams have to be considered favorites to capture those tag straps in that tournament, which gets underway Monday night at Minges Coliseum in Greenville, NC. Plenty of good seats are still available, I'm told. ROMERO: And the Heartbreak Express has a big match-up tomorrow night at November Knockout against the former World Tag-Team champions, Flesh & Blood. TYSON: All right, the action in the ring has started out fast and furious, with Bennett and Bloody feathers starting things off. They're exchanging chops in the center of the ring, irish whip, Bennett off the ropes, duck under a clothesline, off the other side, BIG clothesline of his own!! Bloody Feathers is sent flying out of the ring. Here comes Chief Joseph--dropkick, and he's out to the floor. And Apache Nation needs to regroup here in our opening bout. ADAMS: We haven't seen Bloody Feathers in a DWF ring since he ran your buddy "Rugged" Ricky Starr out of wrestling a few months back, have we, Romero?? ROMERO: That's not how it happened at all, you little-- TYSON: Gentlemen, let's keep the action in the ring. And by the way, your Lordship, are you referring to the same Ricky Starr who's currently signing your check as commissioner of the DWF?? ADAMS: The one and the same!! --WE MOVE TO FURTHER ACTION-- ROMERO: Garrison really needs to make the tag here. He's been taking a pounding from these two wahoos for a good ten minutes now!! TYSON: Chief Joseph going for the suplex--reversal!! Garrison lunges for the tag--he gets it!! Bennett in the ring now, cleaning house!! Dropkick!! Another one!! Picks up Feathers--slams him down!! Picks him up again--tosses him out onto Warpath!! ADAMS: He wasn't bothering a soul out there!! ROMERO: Yeah, not since he threw a coupla cheap shots earlier on!! TYSON: Garrison's up on the top turnbuckle now--Bennett comes over, looks like a rocet launcher coming. Chief Joseph staggers over.....Garrison launches, hits him, goes for the cover, 1...2.... ROMERO: 3!!! He got him!! ADAMS: A travesty of justice has been perpetrated here!! Almost as bad as Little Big Horn!! [RING ANNOUNCER] Here are your winners, THE HEARTBREAK EXPRESS!!! --------SQUASH MATCHES-------- SHANE OUTLAW had a final tuneup for his match with American Heavyweight Champion Jason Stormcloud at November Knockout. The world-renowned suplex master was too much for the game youngster BOBBY SANCHEZ, who had score an upset victory earlier in the week over veteran superstar "Outlaw" Frank James. Outlaw, who along with Christopher Fantasy is regarded as one of the finest wrestlers in the DWF never to have held a World Title, appears to be primed and ready to grab his first-ever championship belt. SIMEON PARADISE continued his roll Saturday Night, defeating veteran preliminary star SPOT JOHNSON in under two minutes. This massive member of the Syndicate has had so much success it's hard to believe that he's just a rookie. He has a big match with fellow rookie "Hammerin'" Hank Jackson tomorrow night at November Knockout. Jackson was in the broadcast booth for this match, and promised the fans that he would take care of "the big nasty", as he is fond of calling Paradise. This should be an explosive matchup. GINO PASCARELLI defeated former friend and sometimes tag partner STEVE MARSHALL in a surprisingly competitive match. Pascarelli, who's career has skyrocketed from preliminary status since the arrival of his cousin Vincenzo Scarpacci and his manager Don Paolo from Sicily. Paolo led Pascarelli to victory in the tournament to crown the DWF's first Light Heavyweight Champion. He has since lost the belt to the popular superstar from Japan, Akira Nagasaki, but has a huge rematch scheduled for tomorrow night at Knockout. ------------------------------ [RING ANNOUNCER] This match is scheduled for one fall. Coming down the aisle, accompanied by his partner Dread, representing the Army of Darkness, here is FEAR!!! (crowd boos, as the Army of Darkness is the most hated organization in the DWF, even more than the Empire Club and the Syndicate.) TYSON: The 280 pound masked man steps into the ring, and one has to wonder why else would Dread be out here besides to get involved somehow. ADAMS: He's probably out here to watch the match, 'cause if he watched it in the back, he'd have to listen to Romero babble. ROMERO: I'll show you babble-- TYSON: Gentlemen!! Let's get back to the ring for the introduction of one of the young lions here in the DWF, Spike Lewis. [RING ANNOUNCER] And making his way to the ring, from White Plains, NY, weighing in at 268 pounds........ SPIKE LEWIS!!! (Crowd roars for Lewis. The young superstar has been an instant fan favorite since his ring debut back in June.) TYSON: Wait a minute, Fear is attacking Lewis before the bell!! Clothesline, now's he choking him!! Picks him up, tosses him out to the floor!! ROMERO: Lewis better watch out out there, Dread's lurking around like he might try something--Oh, no!! TYSON: Did you see that?!? Dread just suplexed Lewis on the floor!! All the while Fear has referee Bobby Scott distracted. Turn around, ref!! ADAMS: I love it!! Wait a minute-- TYSON: Tommy Victory has made his way out from the locker room!! He's attacking Dread. They're going at it like two rabid dogs here on Saturday Night!! Wait, now Scott's out on the floor, trying to break them up. ROMERO: I guess Victory couldn't stand by and watch his good friend take a beating like that, so he had to save him. ADAMS: You have to wonder what kind of shape Lewis will be in for his World Television Title match against "Dirty" Doug Simpkins tomorrow night at November Knockout!! TYSON: I wouldn't be surprised if Simpkins has orchestrated this entire sick episode!! Meanwhile, Fear has dragged Lewis back in the ring--he's going in the tights!! He's got something--it's the Death Spike!! He blasts Lewis in the throat with it!! Lewis is down. ADAMS: He's not moving!! I love it!! TYSON: Dread is pointing the referee towards the ring!! He gets in, starts the count-- 1....2......3!!!! Oh no, he got him. [RING ANNOUNCER] here is your winner, FEAR!!!!!! ROMERO: Wait a minute, here comes Simpkins!! TYSON: The world television champion is pounding on the helpless Lewis!! ROMERO: Maybe it WAS all a set-up!! ADAMS: I love it!! TYSON: Victory is back in the ring now--he's being held off by Fear and Dread!! Now here comes "Hammerin'" Hank Jackson!! He's attacking Simpkins!! He chases Simpkins out, followed by Fear and Dread!! Jackson is cleaning house!! ROMERO: I guess we have to admit that Simpkins accomplished what he wanted, softening up Lewis for tomorrow night!! TYSON: Let's get up to the ring, where Lord Byron is set to interview the World Champion. [ADAMS] Ladies--and I use that term very, very loosely-- (crowd boos lustily.) and Germs, will you please welcome my guest at this time, from Tallahassee, Florida, the DWF Heavyweight Champion of the World, JD POWER!! (Power comes out towards ringside, with the championship belt draped across his shoulder. Half the crowd boos mightily, for Power is one of the most hated men in the history of the DWF. The other half cheers softly, because Power is their favorite in his on-going feud with the even more hated Army of Darkness, led by Power's former ally, Apocalypse.) ADAMS: Now champ, every one here wants to know, who is your mystery partner for tomorrow night's steel cage showdown with the Army of Darkness?? POWER: Lord Byron, if I told you, it wouldn't be a secret anymore, would it? Let's just say he's...... a monster. ADAMS: I've heard rumors that he's a former World Champion. That narrows it down considerably. Is it Big Van Vader?? The Ultimate Warrior?? Sgt. Slaughter?? Who is it?? POWER: You'll just have to wait until tomorrow, there, your Lordship. ADAMS: Well, let's talk about Thanksgiving Thunder, just six days away. You're putting the title on the line against Apocalypse. (crowd gives their largest heel reaction of the night.) Don't you think he may try and use tomorrow night's tag match to injure you before the title match? POWER: Don't you think I've thought about that?? But don't worry. My "mystery" partner's behind me 100 percent. It's in the bag. Now if you'll excuse me, I've gotta get to Fenway. (with that, Power leaves the ring and heads back to the locker room.) ADAMS: Well, no new insight into the main event tomorrow night from the world's champion. Back to you, Romero. ROMERO: Well, we've come to our main event. It's a non-title match between the world tag-team champions Hiro Nogami and Jack Sampson and the Army of Darkness, Apocalypse and the Mongolian Brute. TYSON: Let's get up to the ring announcer for our final match-up here on Saturday Night. [RING ANNOUNCER] Ladies and Gentlemen, tonight's main event is a non-title match, scheduled for one fall, with TV time remaining. Introducing first, representing the Army of Darkness, accompanied by Famine, first from the barren wastelands of the Gobi Desert, the MONGOLIAN BRUTE!! And his partner, hailing from Armageddon, the former DWF World Heavyweight Champion, APOCALYPSE!! (the crowd boos like never before. Apocalypse is undoubtedly the most hated man ever in the DWF.) ROMERO: What's famine doing out here?? I hope we don't have a repeat of that last match!! What--the Brute is spitting into the crowd!! Buncha Savages!! ADAMS: I love it!! [RING ANNOUNCER] And their opponents in this non-title event are the World Tag-Team champions--first from Minneapolis, Minnesota, "Thunderbolt" Jack Sampson!! and from Japan, Hiro Nogami!! (the crowd reacts with a huge roar. Aside from Jason Stormcloud and Spike Lewis, the tag champs could be the most popular men in the DWF.) TYSON: And we'll be back with our main event, right after we pay the bills!! (cut to commercial) ...............continued......
