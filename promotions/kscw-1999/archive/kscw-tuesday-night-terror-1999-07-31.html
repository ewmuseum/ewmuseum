Tuesday Night Terror

31 July 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Tuesday Night Terror from the Arco Arena in Sacramento,
California -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- [As the show begins the Rock is seen in the back walking around. Some guy asks for the Rock's autograph and hands him a hat. The Rock takes it and then looks at the guy.
  Rock: "Do you want me to take this, turn it sideways and shove it straight up your ass?" Guy: "No... umm.. I want you sign it." Rock: "What's your name, boy?" Guy: "It's Ri..." Rock: "It doesn't matter what your name is!"   The Rock then puts the hat on the man's head and pulls it down over his eyes. The guy tries to take it off, but the Rock pushes him and he trips over a garbage can. The Rock laughs and then walks away.]
  [The KSCW introduction then plays. It shows different clips of KSCW wrestlers doing extreme moves like Powerbombs through chairs, DDTs on the cement, etc. During the entire clip the KSCW's new intro music, 'It's Extreme,'
by the Slashtones plays. An explosion then occurs and the screen is covered with red flames. The flames then blow away and the KSCW logo comes into view. A loud sound is then heard and the words 'Tuesday Night Terror,' which are in a blue font, slide in underneath the KSCW logo.]
  Big D: "Welcome to Tuesday Night Terror, ladies and gentlemen." Brian: "Why do you have to say that all the time?" Big D: "What?" Brian: "You say 'ladies and gentlemen' too much." Big D: "Oh, shut up." D-Vin: "Would you two calm down. The show is starting and I want to see Booker T."   Stevie Ray vs. Booker T   [Lovely Day by Kurt Franklin starts to play and Stevie Ray walks out from the back. He struts down to the ring and slides in. The music continues playing, at which point Booker T comes out. He raises the roof, getting a big pop from the fans, as he walks down to the ring. Booker T then slides in and jumps around the ring, looking at his brother. The match starts when Stevie Ray gives Booker T a kick to the midsection. Booker T doubles over and then Stevie whips him off the ropes. On the return Stevie hits a
Tilt-a-Whirl Backbreaker. He then pulls Booker T to his feet and whips him off the ropes again. This time on the return he gets ready for a Back Body Drop, but Booker T kicks him in the head and then hits a Spinning Heel Kick.]
  D-Vin: "Wow, what a tough battle these two are having." Big D: "Some of the best matches in wrestling history have been between family members." Brian: "This is sports-entertainment, not wrestling." D-Vin: "Speaking of entertainment, I hear that Kimberly and Fyre are doing a dance routine again tonight."
Brian: "Yeah! Whoo hoo!"   [Stevie Ray is getting the crap beat out of him by Booker T when Justin Credible walks out. He is followed by Jerry Lynn, Tommy Dreamer and
Francine. They watch the match for awhile, as Booker T takes Stevie Ray down with a Drop Kick and then sets him up for a Powerbomb. Francine then scrambles up the turnbuckle and hits a Missile Drop Kick onto Booker T. This allows Stevie
Ray to make a pin and get the 1... 2... 3! He is then announced the winner, and raises his hands in the air. Justin Credible, Jerry Lynn and Tommy Dreamer then get into the ring and beat up both Stevie Ray and Booker T. Tommy Dreamer gives
Stevie Ray a Dreamer Driver and Jerry Lynn hits a Facebuster on Booker T. They then push both of them out of the ring and Francine gives Dreamer a mic.]
Winner: Stevie Ray   Dreamer: "Now that we took care of business and got rid of those two punks, it's time to get extreme. In the past I was allied with Ken
Shamrock and Steve Blackman. The Torture Den was with me in the Prez's
Franchise. But now I am the Franchise, and little punks like Shamrock and
Blackman will be destroyed.
The Triple Threat has power that wannabies like the Torture Den can't even dream of. Anyone who gets in our way will be destroyed, make no mistake about that. So, you two punks get the hell out here and lets get this thing started. And don't forget to bring that old bitch of yours, because I'm sure Francine will love to kick her ass!"
  Triple Threat vs. The Torture Den   [The Torture Den's music begins to play and Steve Blackman comes running out to the ring. He slides in and quickly begins to fight as
Shamrock and Debra slowly walk down to the ring. Shamrock gets in and helps out
Blackman. Blackman gets Lynn into the corner and punches wildly at his face.
Shamrock, on the other hand, is not faring so well against Dreamer. Dreamer gets
Shamrock into the corner and kicks him in the gut a few times. He then sets him on top of the turnbuckle and hits a Superplex. With Shamrock out on the mat
Dreamer goes over to the corner and helps Lynn. They whip Blackman into the corner and hit a Double Splash on him. He then falls to the mat and Lynn hits a
Corkscrew Moonsault of the top rope. He gets Blackman in a headlock and Dreamer goes back to Shamrock.]
  Big D: "The Torture Den is looking terrible out there." D-Vin: "What about Debra?" Brian: "Puppies!"   [Jerry Lynn and Steve Blackman have both gotten out of the ring now, and Dreamer is fighting Shamrock. Shamrock whips Dreamer off the ropes and hits a Powerslam on the return. He then gets set to do the Sharpshooter, but
Dreamer kicks him off. Jerry Lynn gets tagged in and quickly goes after
Shamrock. He knocks Blackman off the ring apron, but then is tripped up by
Debra. This enrages Francine and she walks around the outside of the ring. She grabs Debra by the hair and slams her into the guardrail. Justin Credible then runs over and starts to fight Blackman. He Evenflows him onto the concrete floor and then gets him into the Texas Cloverleaf. Meanwhile, Shamrock hits a
Hurricanrana and then gets Dreamer into the Ankle Lock. Dreamer is able to grab the ropes and break the hold. He then, with a little help from Jerry Lynn, gives
Shamrock a Dreamer Driver and picks up the win. Triple Threat walks away, leaving the Torture Den behind, and then a few medics come out with a stretcher to help Blackman.]
Winners: Triple Threat via. Dreamer Driver   [After the match the show goes to the first break of the night. First an advertisement for the KSTV channel comes on. It is followed by a short ad for the upcoming WCW Pay-Per-View. The next ad is for KSCW t-shirts.
Goldberg’s music plays in the background as various t-shirts featuring the
Souljas, the Bladerunners, the Showstoppers and Booker T are shown.]
  [When the show returns from the break, "Livin' La Vida Loca" starts to play and Kimberly and Fyre come out. They are both wearing white short-shorts and light-blue spaghetti-strap tops. They do a little dance routine on the ring entrance ramp, and then walk back to their locer room, recieving a pretty large pop from the men in attendance.]
  [Wrath's music begins to play and he and Test step out from the back. They stand at the top of the entrance ramp, looking out into the crowd, before walking down to the ring. The Rock's music begins to play and the crowd goes crazy. He steps out of the back and slowly struts to the ring. Test and Wrath stand in the
corner as the Rock grabs a mic and starts to speak.]   Rock: "You little jabronies look scared. Testicles, Wart, I'm sure you want to know who the Rock's partner is going to be. I'm sure you two are pissing your pants just waiting to see who has the privilege to team with the Great One. Well, he calls himself 'The Greatest,' and can kick your collective asses in more ways
than one. He and the Rock will kick your two candy asses.. if ya smmelllll what the Rock... is cookin'"
  [The Rock then drops the mic and over the loud speakers is heard 'I'm the greatest!' Most of the fans start to boo as the Cat, Ernest
Miller, walks out from the back and down to the ring. He slides in and does a little dance before giving the Rock a high-five and standing on the ring apron.
Test then steps forward and Wrath gets out onto the ring apron. The bell dings and the match begins.]   Wrath and Test vs. The Rock and The Cat   Brian: "Who would have guessed that the Rock would team with the Cat?" Big D: "I don't know, but the fans didn't seem too happy to see
Ernest Miller." D-Vin: "I wasn't too happy to see him either."   [Test charges toward the Rock, but is taken down when the Rock trips him. The Rock then grabs Test by the waist and Suplexes him over his head.
The Rock kicks Test in the leg a few times before tagging in the Cat. Miller kicks Test in the gut and then chokes him with his boot. Wrath runs into the ring and Clotheslines Miller. The Rock then comes in to help his partner, but
Test trips him. Wrath Press Slams Miller and then he fights the Rock. Meanwhile,
Test gets up and sits in the corner, holding his neck.]
  D-Vin: "I wonder if Test hurt his neck. He seems to be in a lot of pain." Brian: "That’s what happens when you mess with the Great
Ones."   [The Rock throws Wrath outside the ring and they begin to fight again. The Cat then charges Test in the corner and nails him with his elbow. Test falls to the mat, and the Cat hits a Standing Side Kick when Test gets up. The Cat then rolls Test up for the pin and the ref counts 1... 2... 3!]
Winners: The Rock and The Cat via. Standing Side Kick   D-Vin: "Folks, we have just recieved word from the hostpital that Steve Blackman has been badly injured and possibly may have had his career ended here tonight."
Big D: "This certainly comes as bad news. We'll keep you posted on his condition."   [The show goes to another break just before the main event. First comes another KSTV ad. It is followed by a short ad for Michael’s World
Wrestling Federation’s upcoming Fight Night ‘99 Pay-Per-View. Then, a short preview of the main event for tonight plays, and the break ends.]
  Television Title Triple Threat match Raven vs. Goldberg vs. Konnan(c)   [Back from the break, the fans are on their feet as Raven’s music begins to play and he comes walking out to the ring. He sits in the corner and waits as Goldberg’s music begins to play. The fans go nuts as Goldberg does his usual entrance, fireworks and all. He then charges down to the ring and quickly attacks
Raven. During all of this, Konnan comes down to the ring with the Television Title, to no music, and watches the two fight. Goldberg whips Raven off the ropes and Spears him on the return. He then starts to pull
Raven up, but Konnan comes from behind. Konnan tries for a Drop Kick, but
Goldberg catches him and hits a Backbreaker. He then whips Konnan off the ropes and Spears him as well.]
  Big D: "Wow! Look at him go... what an animal!" D-Vin: "I hate Goldberg. I hope Raven and Konnan kick his ass." Brian: "Yeah, like that’s gonna happen."   [Goldberg pulls Konnan to his feet and picks him up. He hits the Jackhammer and goes for the pin. Just then, Raven slides in with a chair and whacks Goldberg in the back with it. This breaks the pin, and Raven starts to fight with Konnan. They fight fairly equally while Goldberg limps around outside the ring. Raven
then eventually hits Konnan with the Evenflow, but when he gets back up, Goldberg Spears him yet again. With Raven on the mat, Goldberg takes the opportunity to pin Konnan and gets the 1... 2... 3!]
Winner and Television champion: Goldberg
