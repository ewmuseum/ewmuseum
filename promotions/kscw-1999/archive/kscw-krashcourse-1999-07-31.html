KSCW — Card recap

KSCW Krashcourse

31 July 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[As a commercial break ends, a green background comes into view and then the KSCW logo is stamped into the background. The screen fades out and then D-Vin can be seen sitting at a news desk. He pushes a few papers out of his way and starts to speak.] D-Vin: "Good morning, ladies and gentlemen, and welcome to KSCW Krashcourse. It seems as if having this show become a review of KSCW Friday Night Face-Off is turning into a weekly thing, as once again KSCW was having troubles last night with the telecast. So, I will be here to cover all of the results for you. Now, lets get started."
  The Big Show vs. Tommy Dreamer   D-Vin: "Most avid KSCW fans know about the Big Show calling Val Venis out to a clean, friendly fight tonight. However, Val Venis decided to decline the match at the last minute by simply not showing up at the arena. As you can imagine, the Big Show still wanted to fight someone. So, the
President convinced Tommy Dreamer to come out. Once Dreamer got in the ring, the
Big Show quickly took all the energy out of the Triple Threat member, eventually hitting the Showstopper and adding another win to his record.”
Winner: The Big Show via. Showstopper   Test vs. Ernest “The Cat” Miller   D-Vin: "This one was going just perfect for Test. He was having one of the best matches of his life against The Cat, beating him all over the ring, inside and out. However, Test’s luck turned for the worst when
The Rock came out and attacked him. It was two on one for awhile, as the Rock gave Test a Rock Bottom and then got set for the People’s Elbow. Then, Wrath came from out of nowhere and blindsided the Rock with a chair, knocking him out cold on the mat. Wrath hit the Meltdown on the Cat and put Test on top of him for the 1... 2... 3!"
Winner: Test   Raven vs. Road Dogg   D-Vin: "This one’s really not even worth talking about. The Road Dogg was out with Val Venis, and Raven had Mysterio by HIS side. After
Raven and Mysterio both tasted a little Shake Rattle n’ Roll and the Money Shot,
Road Dogg made a pin and the ref counted 1... 2... 3! Then, as the two Warriors members left, the President came down to the ring and gave Raven and Mysterio both the finger and their pink slips."
Winner: Road Dogg via. Shake Rattle ‘n Roll   Street Title match Stevie Ray vs. Booker T(c)   D-Vin: "The next match saw Stevie Ray and Booker T taking each other on in a dark, industrial area. This was easily the most exciting matchup of the night, as they hit each other with everything imaginable. It looked as if Stevie Ray had the match won when he hit his brother over the head with a metal pipe. Then, a car came from out of nowhere and hit
Stevie Ray. Booker T rolled him up for the win, but then medics came to help
Stevie Ray. It was later announced that Stevie had been hit in the left leg and that it has been broken and he will be out for two and a half weeks at the very least.”
Winner and Street champion: Booker T   Lion’s Den match Konnan vs. The Godfather   D-Vin: "After the horrible hit and run, we came back to the live show inside the arena and went straight to the Lion’s Den match between
Konnan and the Godfather. Despite the efforts of the Godfather, I’m sad to say that Konnan was able to pick up the win in this one. Although, it’s about time he got a win over the former leader of the Nation."
Winner: Konnan   Tag Team Titles match Triple Threat vs. The Showstoppers(c)   D-Vin: "And finally, we came to the Tag Titles match. Triple Threat was Lance Storm and Jerry Lynn this time. The two should explosive effort out in the ring, but were unable to match the power of the Showstoppers.
Two Spears and a Showstopper later, and the duo of Goldberg and the Big Show both added another win to their records."
Winners and Tag Team champions: The Showstoppers
