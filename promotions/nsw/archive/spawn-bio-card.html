Spawn bio card

April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Name: Spawn Height: 6'2" Weight: 217 lbs Home Town: Wichita, Kansas Style: High Flyer Titles held: Stealth Title Finisher: ENDCAP (Buff Blockbuster) Information on Superstar: Spawn started out in NSW as a rookie. His career was stuck in the low cards until he fought Chris Crosse. During the match it looked like they would do anything until Spawn was pounded down by a sledgehammer. But that wouldn't stop Spawn from going on. He fought back and won the Stealth title in a Cage Match at Bone Bash 2003. His career was just taking off when NSW had problems and shut down. But he's back and says it's time for a new evolution of SPAWN! What can we expect from Spawn? Just about everything. Defiantly another title reign in the Stealth Division but more importantly the up rise of a high flyer to take the style to new levels. Many don't understand Spawn. For a light weight he can fight but there are things that he can't do and he knows it. His style is high flying as said before but that's not all. He told Jesse Friend in the old NSW that when push comes to shove, the little man can pack some heavy hits as well take them. Only time will tell for this man. Flash Back ::The show comes back on as the cage is being lowered for the next match :: “Folks this is one hell of a feud. Crosse attacked Spawn to win the title and beat him bloody. Spawn hasn’t been able to get his hands on Spawn since but wants to… This battle may be the end, but I doubt it.” – Kay “Damn straight. These guys are going to walk out of there looking like raw meat. May the best raw hamburger win! Ewe, look at that nice fresh meat over there Kay… The third row with the huge break-“ – Rick “No thanks. Let’s start the match.” - Kay :: The arena goes black and Boom by P.O.D. blasts over the PA System. The crowd boos and Spawn steps out on the stage. He walks slowly down the ramp stopping to cuss at the fans. He gets about half way down the ramp and then runs and jumps up to the edge of the ring. He does a flip over the top rope and runs over to the turnbuckle and jumps to the top of it. He takes his glasses off and puts them in his leather jacket, then takes his jacket off and tosses it outside the ring as his music slowly fades. :: “Last week we seen Spawn fly from one ring to the other.” - Kay “We also seen him get a broken nose.” – Rick :: Chris Crosse just walks down to the ring, Trash talking the fans, he climbs through the door and gets into the ring. :: “This young man has been all over Spawn these past few weeks.” - Kay “Well, Spawn may be able to turn his career around, and if he does.. This would be the perfect match to do it in.” – Rick :: The cage is lowered around the ring and Spawn and Chris are both inside... Spawn runs at Crosse for a clothesline, but Crosse ducks and Spawn bounces off the ropes. Crosse dropkicks Spawn, Spawn flies into the ropes again, but grabs onto them. Crosse gets up and Spawn runs at him again, Crosse ducks and dropkicks Spawn again. Spawn becomes angry and runs at Crosse again, this time hitting a dropkick to the legs of Crosse. Crosse falls to his knees as Spawn hurries to his feet and laugh. Crosse gets up and grabs Spawn, Crosse nails a DDT on Spawn and then picks him up.. Crosse then smears Spawn's face into the side of the cage and Spawn screams in pain. Blood is dripping from Spawn's face as he falls to the ground. :: “Crosse doesn't put up with Spawn's shit anytime... And he is proving it now. ” - Rick “Crosse should show a little respect though, After all... Crosse has been trying to attack Spawn all week.” – Kay :: Spawn wipes his face and gets up slowly, Crosse has begun to climb the cage. Spawn jumps up and grabs onto Crosse, Crosse is still climbing to the top carrying Spawn on his back.. Spawn then yanks on Crosse and slams him into the mat hard. Spawn picks Chris up and launches him to the corner. Spawn runs at him and Spears goes for the spear, but Crosse moved and Spawn nailed his shoulder on the turnbuckle. :: “That may have broken his shoulder.” - Rick :: Spawn drops to his knees holding his shoulder, Crosse then runs at Spawn and dropkicks him in the side of the head. :: “We may need an ambulance here soon. Spawn is taking a serious beating and may soon need some serious medical attention. ” – Kay :: Crosse gets up and picks the battered Spawn up. Crosse then jumps up to the top rope, and jumps off towards the dazed Spawn; Crosse nails a flying dropkick on Spawn as Spawn flies down to the mat. Crosse grabs the legs of Spawn and locks him up in a Sharpshooter. Spawn screams in pain and Crosse just keeps pulling back harder. Spawn manages to crawl slowly to the rope and lift himself up enough to flip Crosse off of him. Spawn slowly climbs to the top tope, but Crosse grabs him and throws him off. :: “Spawn is just getting tortured in there.” – Rick “He is going to need a great come back or else Crosse will defend his title once more. ” – Kay :: Spawn gets to his feet and Crosse is running towards him for a clothesline but Spawn drops to his stomach and Crosse jumps over him and flies right into the corner turnbuckle. Spawn then kicks Crosse in the gut and nails a Hangman’s DDT. Spawn runs to the turnbuckle and jumps to the top rope, Spawn then jumps high into the air and nails an elbow drop to Crosse's chest. Crosse screams in pain as Spawn jumps to his feet and picks Crosse up. Spawn bounces off the ropes and nails Crosse with a spinning heel kick... Blood flies out of Crosse's mouth. :: “OH MY! I think I just seen a tooth fly out of Crosse's mouth. Looks like Spawn is finally getting in the hang of this. ” – Kay “Looks like Spawn may snag the win, All he has to do is climb out of that cage while Crosse is down.” - Rick “But when he gets out, he has to worry about Crosse's manager.” – Kay :: Spawn climbs up the cage and gets to the top... He looks like he is going to jump out of the cage.. But he turns around and jumps. :: “HOLY SHIT! Spawn just nailed Crosse with a moonsault from the top of the cage.” - Rick “Spawn may want to get his ribs checked out after that high fly!” – Kay :: Both men are down and Spawn slowly crawls to the ropes, as well as Crosse. They both get up slowly using the ropes; they run at each other and nail each other with a clothesline. :: “Now that was funny, It looked like something you would see in baseball! ” – Rick :: Spawn and Crosse just lie there for a few minutes... Then Crosse gets up and picks Spawn up, But Spawn kicks Crosse in the gut, Crosse comes back with a right hook... then Spawn nails Crosse in the face with a left hook and they continue to exchange fists. :: “What is this?! A boxing match?!” – Kay :: Crosse then dives at Spawn and hits a diving clothesline, Spawn flies backwards... Crosse gets up and starts kicking Spawn in the head. Spawn then grabs Crosse's foot and pulls on it, Crosse falls on his ass and jumps to his feet in pain... Spawn gets up and spears Crosse, He then goes to the top turnbuckle and waits for Chris to get up.... Crosse stands up and Spawn jumps off and nails Crosse with the ENDCAP!:: “I think Spawn just broke Crosse's neck!” - Rick "Some one call an ambulance!” – Kay :: Spawn climbs up the side of the cage, and jumps out. :: “The winner of the match and your new Stealth Champion... SPAWN!” – Nancy Jane
