Rules

February 2003

Rules NSW prides on creativity. Instead of having people who are the average Joe we look for those who have the talent to express creative Promos (RP's), storylines, in-cards, and match writing. NSW will give you what you want as long as you bide by the few rules we set. No buddy shall join another efed within the PTC community as the same character they are here, if so they will be talked to and arrangements will be made. One being that if you do you will be fired immediately if so not wanting to change your name in one of the selected efeds. Work with others and ask them for help with a storyline you are in with them. That's a stepping-stone to being more creative. If you and your advisory can work together that angle can become one beyond what we can hope for. All the great feds do it, and we will do the same. NSW will allow you to join any time and place. Your app may be deleted if we do not like your character. Places to go instead of NSW are PWA, the WO academy. We feel this is a place to get a lot of experience and learn new ways to write. If you are accepted you agree to write 2 promos a week average. No less, if so you will be warned the first time and fired the second time. No in between breaks or such, (Like if you RP once then RP twice the next week, then once after that week. Not allowed) as well you also agree to write one match whenever needed and help with staff ideas if asked for them. You must be active on the forums of PTC. PTC FORUMS HERE . Register right after you join NSW and are accepted. Make sure not to link images to your avatar spot or signature. NSW will have certain images for those who want them in the gallery sooner or later. Make sure not to bug the Poser Artist about your poser. Posers are done and not changed UNLESS it's a major wrong or the Poser Artist chooses to fix it. This goes for banners as well and much more.
