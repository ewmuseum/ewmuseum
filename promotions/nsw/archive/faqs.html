F.A.Q's Q: What is NSW? A: NSW is an efed called No Surrender Wrestling. Q: Who owns NSW? A: The original owner is Jesse Friend AKA Jesse Jamester. Q: How long has this fed been open? A: Started back in June of 2002 as SCW (Suicidal Championship Wrestling). From there it generated into NSW near the late August of 2002 and closed the beginning of January 2003. The owner redirected NSW to a new server and bought a new domain. Q: Has NSW ever been apart of an interfed? A: Yes, we are apart of PTC and were apart of eWc, which was TKW, SWEET, and Lights Out Wrestling. Q: May I have a poser picture made custom from your poser artist? A: No… The owner is also the Poser Artist. He creates images for Lights Out Wrestling and NSW. Q: Does anyone get into NSW or is it judged by a certain criteria? A: We try and base it on a creative view. No average Joe can get in. They have to show work of their Promos and have a nice well-balanced background. Q: Hey I know you will you let me in? A: Surprisingly, no. NSW doesn't want to be known as feeder friendly. We welcome all to join but only few for acceptance. Q: Could I become apart of the staff, such as update? A: No. We don't want anyone who we don't know to staff. Jesse Friend chose the staff he has and those upon the staff will do there best to work for him.
