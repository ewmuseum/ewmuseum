My name is [name withheld]... I am the president of Championship Wrestling Federation... My league is called CWF and it runs two cards a week and two reports... We only accept good roleplayers... We need just 3 single wrestler's and two Tag Teams... You are limited to one each.... The application is below... Here's the URL: The application on homepage doesn't work... http://www.angelfire.com/ga/cwfwrestling/index.html To submit a wrestler for entry into the CWF, simply fill out the form below and send it. It's very important that you type your email address correctly; failure to do so will result in no reply. To submit a tag team, don't forget to fill the form out for each member. Every application is judged on its own merits, and you may be asked to submit a sample roleplay before being accepted.
