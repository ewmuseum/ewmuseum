Walls ======================== Wow... nice show. Lots of angles starting to get going now, lots of cool little feuds, some characters really standing out... things are really shaping up. One thing I can really use is RELIABLE writers. Please, help me out. Anyhow, this next show is a BIG one... it's gonna f'n rock! =============== Perhaps the most anticipated show of the "Stand and Deliver" tour is here! From the famous... and infamous... Viking Hall in South Philadelphia, NEO-Pro brings... ....THE SKY'S THE LIMIT 3! Not only are we going to see the actual eight man tournament to crown a new International Cruiserweight Champion but a number of other wrestling companies have accepted invitations to have thier own matches on the show, making it a true supercard... A TWO NIGHT SUPERCARD!! From one of the, if not _the_, best wrestling companies in the world in IWF/WOW we will see "TIGERHEART" RALLY JACKSON defend his SPOTLIGHT CHAMPIONSHIP in a three way dance against "THE SPITEFUL SUPERSTAR" TRIPP SHADE and "THE NATURAL" TRAVIS CADE! Woman's fed DSW will bring us a SIX WOMAN TAG MATCH as the team of NINA GRIMSSOM, TARA SMITH and TRINITY EXTACY take on IRIS GALIVER, THE BLOODSUCKING EDITOR and NINA LARUE! The newest kid on the block, GLORY, will be sending THE ARMANI EXCHANGE to take on the aptly named SOUTHLAND KILLERS! Plus... IWE, WAVE and ICON have been invited but have yet to formally announce their decision! Then, of course, there is the third annual tourney with arguably it's strongest lineup yet. With competitors from FSW and WAVE, this is sure to be a scorcher! The first match will have TJ ANDREWS battling WAVE's near legendary TASOGARE NO PRINCE! FSW will send "THE JERSEY DEVIL" PAK, the popular high flier taking on two time competitor and the favorite in the tournament, PSICOPA! The third match could be the absolute show stealer as the returning KRISTOFF ST. LIVINGSTONE takes on former Pacific Champion "DEADLY" DEREK IRVIN! Maybe KStL will avoid a beating this time. And making his debut in the final first round match SHAUN BORDERS will debut a new look and attitude against "THE TECHNO DRAGON" TRANCE EXTACY! All that plus... We will hear from Shogun Z, Jacob Cross, Mike Waldrop and Billy Rock... the semi finalists in the Triple Crown Tournament! Rum Barton debuts! Social Distortion will be in the house! And who knows what else might happen in Viking Hall! DEADLINES: EVERYTHING is due on Friday the 15th of August. Send strats, rps, etc to [detail withheld] and/or [detail withheld] And I need writers!
