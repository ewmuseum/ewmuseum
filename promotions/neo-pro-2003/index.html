Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

NEO-Pro ran a recurring bulletin series called INVASION! out of Philadelphia, already presented as an established run by the time its earliest surviving post, INVASION!: Mexico, appeared on 8 August 2003.

Its own supercard that month, The Sky's the Limit 3 (26 August 2003), staged a two-night interpromotional show from Viking Hall in South Philadelphia: the third annual eight-man tournament to crown an International Cruiserweight Champion, drawing entrants from FSW and WAVE, run alongside guest matches it billed as a Spotlight Championship defense from IWF/WOW and a six-woman tag pitting three DSW wrestlers against three others.

The results of that show, and of its own tournament, were posted to the promotion's own site rather than reproduced here; only the announcement calling it “fantastic” is part of what survives. No later document of NEO-Pro's own run survives to say how it continued.
