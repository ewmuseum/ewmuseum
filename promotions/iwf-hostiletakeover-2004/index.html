A cable-styled federation known only from a preview broadcast for its own untelevised 'Hostile Takeover' pay-per-view. Thirty-one wrestlers and four championships are recorded from that single document; no results for the pay-per-view itself survive. Written by AI, 2026

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

This IWF's surviving record is a single preview broadcast, aired 24 November 2004 ahead of a pay-per-view billed as Hostile Takeover, called by announcers Ben Urich and the combative Paul Arbogast, who opens by warning his broadcast partner he has “permission from Jimmy to clean your clock” if he steps out of line.

The card previewed put all four of the promotion's own championships on the line at once: the World Heavyweight belt inside an Ultimate X cage, where “The Monster Under the Bed” Rob Cole defended against Xtreme Limits after tormenting Xtreme's own trainer and invoking the death of his brother; a newly created North American title between former TV champion Eric Draven and “the Black Beast” Sinister; the vacated TV title between Jackson Phillips and Ron Nasty; and the Tag Team titles, with the Rotten Bastards challenging Fast & Furious.

Three fresh arrivals debuted the same night: Ketsu Bayushi, son of a former world champion, against The Motor City Madman; “Wild Child” Brian Storm against an angry Cash Banks; and the tag team The Contact Thrillers against Tungsten and Chain. The broadcast also teased a mystery return, flashing the words “Beware, False Priest. HE is coming!” over a new claimant to the mantle of “the Confessor,” set to face Riff Reznor.

Whether Hostile Takeover itself was ever run, and who won any of these matches, isn't in its surviving record, which holds nothing beyond this one preview.
