Free Per View Show before IWF Hostile Takeover

24 November 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
IWF FREE-PER-VIEW PREVIEW SHOW!!!!! ['Bodies' by drowning pool begins to play as we see a collage of superstars in the IWF ring. We see action shots of Rob Cole, Xtreme Limits, Rocky, Craddick, Riff Reznor and the rest as we lead up to the big pay per view!!! The music is loud, the action i violent, and the IWF logo lights up the screen before fading out as Ben Urich steps out from behind a monitor. The new reporter is dressed in a suit and tie, smiling broudly... stepping out from the opposite side, dressed in a teeshirt and jeans and wearing a pair of shades is Paul Arbogast.] Urich: Hello, IWF fans, and welcome to the Free-Per-View showcase of the IWF. Tonight, we're getting ready to show you one of the most incredible cards ever seen on cable television... the greatest athletes in the world are in the IWF, and tonight promises to be explosive. Arbogast: There's a brutal night on the way, Ben... and you better not forget who has seniority here tonight! I've been given permission from Jimmy to clean your clock if you get out of line... courtesy of an Arbogast Trademark slap across the face! Urich: Well, uh... let's just try to get along and do our jobs for the fans sitting at home. And what better way to start off this show than in talking about the new blood here in the IWF! Arbogast: I couldn't agree with you more, Ben... we've got the first ever match for Ketsu Bayushi in this company, the first appearance of Brian Storm, and the first appearance of The Contact Thrillers! Urich: We don't know much about the Thrillers yet, but we've seen Tungsten and Chain as a tag team before. They've been giving one hell of a showing in our Regional company where they've managed to win one hell of a tag team tournament in order to earn their spot in Hostile Take-over. Arbogast: Yeah, but the Contact Thrillers have gotten a pretty big contract for coming to the IWF... you don't give a contract like that to guys you haven't seen, and I think The T And C connection are about to find a whole new world of pain! [Screen comes out with a pose photograph of both teams as the voice overs continue... ] Urich: These two teams are out to prove exactly how good they can be in this company, and I expect a real knock down fight. There's alot of pressure riding on this match for both teams... [The image comes back to both men, nodding. They turn to look at one another and Urich continues... ] Urich: And then we have a guy who I've admired for a few years now, the one and only Brian Storm! Arbogast: Yeah, you /would/ admire that lame duck piece of crap! Urich: Storm is a former champion in the south where he's gone toe to toe with some of the biggest names in our sport. They call him the Wild Child, and he's one of the single most charismatic wrestlers to ever step into the squared circle. He makes his debut tonight against a man who is definitely no push-over! Arbogast: Now we're talkin', buddy... Cash Banks came into this company on the last show, where we saw the Maverick pull a dirty trick in order to win. We know Cash Banks is angry, he's upset, and he's going to take out that frustration on Brian Storm! I have to tell you, I saw Banks take the fight to Maverick last week and the dude seriously impressed me. He's no stranger to big names, and this guys star is gonna' shine right in the eyes of Brian Storm! [We see another image, during Arbogasts' speech, of both Cash Banks and Brian Storm. Both men roll their necks in preperation for the match, with Cash Banks suddenly pulling off his wife beater and tensing his chest in angered anticipation.] Urich: And finally, the last debut of the evening will be the first ever match of Ketsu Bayushi when he takes on The Motor City Madman. Arbogast: And before you start singing the praises of your good ol' boy Hero, let me just say that it's going to be my pleasure to watch a legend like Bayushi put that glorified idiot right in his place. Urich: The Motor City Madman has been a force of reckoning in the IWF since the day it opened. His return this past autumn has been one of the highlights of the company, but he came with the Man From Japan trailing right after him... for several months, The MCM has been learning under the learning tree of Matsu Bayushi, and the former world champions' son has taken exception to it. There's alot of bad blood between these two... that's for damn sure! [The footage changes to the image of Bayushi and Madman, both prepared for their match in a split screen shot as they roll their necks in preperation.] Urich: And all of this is brought to you by Crisp-O-Thud, Acme Chairs, and Fred Duckbill Enterprises! Three sponsors a company can truly be proud of... Arbogast: *belllchhh!* Urich: Always a class act Paul... Arbogast: Shut the hell up, punk! Let's get on to the next set of previews... Urich: Whatever you say, tough guy.... because the next is a match alot of people have been looking forward to. The "Confessor" makes his pay per view appearance against the brother of a man who once carried the same name in this very company! Arbogast: But the shadows are falling in a new light, Ben... and this new Confessor has a fresher attitude and a more violent outlook on life. Forget the "dark hero" crap... this guy is here to punish the wicked, punish the righteous, and devestate anything in his path! Riff Reznor doesn't stand a ghost of a chance... Urich: Speaking of ghosts, there's a cold wind blowing through the arena tonight... and everyone's whispering about "him". Tonight could spark the return of a Legend in this company, a legend brought back by the whisper of a familiar name. Riff Reznor stands more than a 'ghost' of a chance! [Another screen shot of the two superstars as Riff Reznor prepares to face off against the "Confessor"!] Arbogast: That crazy kid doesn't know what he's in for! The new Confessor will finish what he started last week. Urich: Well, it seems that there's someone else out there that takes exception to the new Confessor. [Video footage of the last Smashvision plays, showing the Rotten Bastards beating down both men, then the lights going completely out and the MegaTron showing the flaming letters "Beware, False Priest. HE is coming!" Urich: What could this mean for the man donning the Confessor's cloak? Were sure to find out. Arbogast: For the first time ever, all four of our esteemed IWF belts are put on the line. There's gonna be some gold grabbin' done here tonight. And I just know that my boys, the Rotten Bastards are gonna dish out as much pain as possible. Urich: Well, that may be true, Paul, but The North American belt has never been defended before. Arbogast: Hey, never correct me! I'll have the Bastards down here so fast... Urich: At least you'll be in good company, then. The newly created North American title will be on the line tonight, as former TV champion Eric Draven will meet the Black Beast, Sinister. [Shots of Sinister and Draven are shown, both men looking ready for a fight.] Urich: Draven lost the TV title to Jackson Phillips and now Phillips will face off against Ron Nasty for the title! Arbogast: Nasty's due for some singles gold and he'll be glad to mangle anyone who disputes him. Even you, Urich, you spit monkey! Say something and I'll send you back to the Daily Bugle so fast... [Split Shot of Phillips holding his shiny TV title, as Ron Nasty looks smugly at the camera.] Urich: Speaking of the Bastards, they have quite a task for themselves, as they've won the chance to face Fast & Furious tonight for the IWF Tag Team Titles. Barry Wom and Dirk McQuickly will be facing down Mark Ferrari and Jason Sensation! [The camera shows split shots of both teams, ready and eager to go. Mark and Jason smile and fake air guitar, while Barry looks stern and Dirk smirks behind him.] Arbogast: That match is almost over right now! Both men have been in the house circuit, as Jason's just coming off of an injury! The Bastards have that one wrapped up tighter than your old lady, Urich. Urich: Uh, right. Well, there's still tag contentions to be settled, as the dangerous Bushi Boys are set to face off against the monstrous team of The Psyachotic One and the Tower of Power, the Army of Darkness. [Split scene of Lady Kachiko in front of the Bushis, Bully looking dnagerously mean, while Kiddo strikes a martial pose, a scream on his face. TPO stands in front of the masked Tower, TPO with his arms crossed and an angry glare from behind his shades.] Arbogast: And the sisters are doin' it for themselves tonight as the heated rivalry between Rocky and newcomer, Diana Prince, goes to the next level. A lot of words and some rather hurt feelings have gone back and forth between these two. Tonight, they meet once again in a No DQ Falls Count Anywhere match. Urich: These two ladies have some heavy tensions between them and the place is gonna explode when the former champ gets her hands on the brash upstart. [Another screen shot of the two superstars as Diana Prince prepares to face off against the Rocky!] Arbogast: Rocky! Pheh! She's a man in a good disguise! And don't forget ....this pay per view happens to have the paneled 5 judge match between "The Cornish Crippler" Kenan Craddick and "The Working Stiff" "Iron" Mike Steele! Steele will finally get to show Kenan Craddick just how real his deal is! Urich: I'm not so sure I'd count that one out just yet. There was a very brutal match last week that led up to this! [Video footage from last Smashvision of Craddick dangling from the scaffolding, as Mike Steele is locked in a headscissor by Craddick. Craddick begins to cut the rope. Cut to footage of Craddick nailing Steele with the Tiger Driver and the scaffolding collapsing beneath them.] Urich: These two men have such hatred for each other and tonight is the match that will decide it all. After this, no more match-ups allowed between these two! [Split screen shot of the two superstars. Mike Steele holds his hands up in a fighting stance, while Craddick taps a finger at his temple with a cocky grin on his face.] Arbogast: And what could be more violent than a Rob Cole match? Urich: Not much. Arbogast: Tonight, The Monster Under The Bed dusts himself off and climbs into an Ultimate X Cage against the World Champion, Xtreme Limits! There has been so much bad blood between these two, and most of it has been someone else's. Urich: Yes, Johnny Marcetti, Xtreme's trainer, was taken and tortured by Rob Cole. Cole even went so far as to insinuate that he dug up Xtreme's dead brother, who died in a wrestling accident. Arbogast: Xtreme Limits will learn the true meaning of anguish as he has to win against Cole over the ring, on a cable, trying to free his precious belt! It's gonna be brutal! [Video footage of spots between Cole and Xtreme play on, then cut to a shot of a schematic of the Ultimate X Cage. The scene switches to a split screen shot of Cole wringing his wrist with a feral grin on his face, while Xtreme bounces and throws a few martial arts punches.] Urich: That's all the time we have for now, folks. Enjoy the show and good night!
