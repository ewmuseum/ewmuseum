[AWI] Explosion 05/18/96, Pt. II (of III)

18 May 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
{Fade back after the commercials to Alan Kinsman and Alex Matthews in the ring...} [AK] Our next contest is for one fall, with a ten minute time limit... our first competitor... already in the ring... Alex Matthews! His opponent... stands 6 feet even, and weighs 241 lbs... here is... JIMMY SPRINGHEEL!!! {"Springheeled Jim" keys up over the loudspeakers, and Jimmy Springheel slaps hands down the aisle to definite fan approval, particularly from the ladies...} [HR] So, what has your "sources" told you about Jimmy Springheel, Mike? From what I understand he's a pretty talented boxer as well as wrestler... [MH] He's a Street-Fighting-Kick-Boxing Joe wanna-be, and he's a pretty boy to boot! 'Til he learns how to use a pair 'a brass knuckles, I refused to be impressed. [PS] Michael Helics -- always one to view things with an open mind. [HR] Springheel and Matthews circling each other now... Matthews darts in... but Springheel catches him with a bodyslam! Lifts the youngster up... and a knee lift to take him down again! Irish whip into the corner... and the Jade Tiger's coming to the ringside... [PS] To continue his scouting for potential partners in the tag team of "Crystal Heels"... [HR] Springheel follows in, but Matthews is out too quickly! Alex goes for a punch... blocked by Springheel... who returns with a European Uppercut... [PS] No, that's THE European Uppercut... Springheel's key knockout punch! [HR] Well, Alex seems pretty out of it... Springheel with the cover... 1... 2... 3!!!! [AK] Here is your winner... JIMMY SPR..... {Kinsman is cut off, as the Jade Tiger takes the microphone and enters the ring...} [Jade Tiger] {Looking at the pummelled jobber, turning to Jimmy Springheel} Very impressive, Mr. Springroll. [JS] (Springheel pulls the mic over to him) That's SpringHEEL, mate! [JT] Whatever....very good work there dispatching a near invalid. You are a weak, talentless slob. You have no future in the AWI, leave now little boy before you get hurt. Your skills are an embarassment to the sport I created. What can someone like you do against the likes of 'The Olympian' Gary White, Lord Thomas Imperious, or....a legend like me...{Jade Tiger slaps Springheel across the face, as Springheel staggers back Jade Tiger removes his kimono. Springheel picks up the microphone...} [JS] Now look 'ere old man... [HR] Jimmy Springheel just BARELY ducks a back-fist from the old... I mean from the Jade Tiger... [MH] Who's doing what I've always wanted ta do... beat the crap out of a pretty boy whose ruinin' what I worked hard to build! [HR] Or not, as Springheel drops the microphone and starts unloading on the Tiger with rights and lefts... [PS] Which shows exactly what he CAN do against someone like the Jade Tiger... [HR] Springheel whips Jade Tiger into the corner... he mounts the turnbuckles... but Jade Tiger with a thumb to the eyes to get him off... and the Crystal Crow out of NOWHERE... Springheel doesn't see him... TIGER'S POUNCE Scissors Piledriver by the Crystal Crow! Now the Crow starts choking him... but Jade Tiger seems to be telling him to get Springheel up... Jade Tiger wants to give him a Tiger's Pounce himself! [MH] Man, who needs a Legends reunion when you have this? The Tiger showing his student personally how it's done... brings a tear to my eye... [HR] Again, or not, as Dacia Blackthorne, Justice, and Dick Decker are in the ring are in the ring, and the Crow and the Tiger head for high ground... someone's got to sort this mess out... fans, we'll be right back! *************************************************************************** And now, today's subliminal message. {Jerone needs some more commercials. Write up some cool AWI Commercials. Get Lotsa Fan Support. Any fan favorite that's of Hero or higher standing, or rulebreaker that's of Villian or higher standing is eligible.} That's the end of today's subliminal message. Thank you. :-) *************************************************************************** {Fade back into the ring, with Kieth Matthews and Alan Kinsman standing by... } [AK] Our next contest is for one fall, with a ten-minute time limit... our first contestant... already in the ring... Kieth Matthews! His opponent... from Estonia... he stands 6'1".... weighs 255 lbs... accompanied to the ring by his student, SARAH BARKER... he is... "THE PHILOSOPHER" MIKHAIL TZSKOVA!!! {A wave of boos goes throughout the crowd, as the Philosopher and his student come down to the ring...} [HR] Tzskova and Matthews eyeing each other now... Collar-and-elbow tie-up... No, the Philosopher breaks... Matthews approaches... but Tzskova backs off... Matthews rushes him this time... but Tzskova takes to the outside of the ring! [PS] Umm... Mike... correct me if I'm wrong, but this isn't exactly the most efficient way to start, is it? [MH] It's called "ring psychology"... but, yeah, a steel chair upside the head is a much better form of psychology... an' quicker at that! [HR] In any case, Mikhail takes his time getting back into the ring... Matthews rushes him again... but Mikhail catches him with a leg sweep! the Philosopher with an armbar now... but Kieth's out, and cinches on a headlock of his own! Tzskova grabs the legs... reversal into an Indian Deathlock... [PS] Or an Estonian Deathlock, as it were... [HR] Matthews manages to get out... Tzskova to his feet... and NAILS a fistdrop to Matthews' knee! That leg has GOT to be hurting right now... [PS] A high-velocity fist hitting it has a habit of doing that to you... [HR] Tzskova lifts him up... whips to the ropes... Matthews with a leapfrog... and a bodypress on the return pass! ... No count, as the Philospher's quickly out, and cinches an inverse leglock... Matthews worms his way out... this has been an excellent display of wrestling on both parts... [MH] Blahblahblah... yeahyeahyeah... Dammit, when are we gonna see some WRASSLIN'?! [HR] I don't know about that... but Sarah's dragging a table into the aisle for some reason... The Philosopher back to an Indian Deathlock... Matthews out, and the Philosopher's looking a bit frustrated... Tzskova with an armbar... he releases... and locks on a Figure Four! [PS] I guess that's the Final Word!!! [HR] How do you know that? [PS] Every great up-and-coming "Best Announcer In The World" has to have his own sources, ya know!!! [HR] Well, whether it's the figure four or the Final Word, the pain looks excrutiating... and Matthews quickly submits! [AK] Here is your winner... "THE PHILOOOOOO"... {He's interrupted, yet again, as Tzskova grabs the microphone... } [Philospher Mikhail Tzskova] My dear Mr. Matthews, are you hurt? Oh, my, are you feeling....pain? That certainly isn't any fun, now. Do you know wish you had not though of joining such a violent sport like wrestling? Do you not wish you had followed a different path? {Kieth looks around at the crowd, trying to elicit an answer... after few moments, he shakes his head "no", to a wave of cheers...} [PMT] Ahhh...still arrogant in your violent ways. You will learn. [HR] Tzskova just foot swept Matthews again... amd he's locked on the Final Word... Matthews is screaming in pain... and Tzskova's asking him again if he wants a different path... but Matthews is screaming too much to answer! [MH] Ya know, this "wrestling" stuff may have some potential yet! [HR] Matthews screaming in pain... he may have a permanent injury... here comes the referee brigade... but Sarah's turned that table to it's side, and is using it to block them off!!! I don't believe it... this is actually PRE-MEDITATED! [PS] Either that, or Sarah's a REALLY quick thinker! [HR] Tzskova's screaming that question again... Matthews finally shakes his head "yes"... Tzskova lets him go, and now he's grabbing the microphone again... [PMT] That is good. You have learned the price of violence...you realize now that violence causes nothing but pain, anguish, and misery...those exact feelings you have right now {sees medics coming in, but they can't get past the refs and Sarah} No.....no.....he needs to feel this pain, to soak in all the throbbing anguish he feels right now...every second he feels it, the longer it will stay with him...the longer it will be that he will not cause violence on someone else. As a matter of fact, you aren't screaming as much as you were before....are you getting accustomed to the pain? Tsk tsk tsk.....that simply won't do. [HR] HE'S PUT HIM IN THE FINAL WORD AGAIN!!!! Matthews is out from the pain... wait... what's that?.... JUSTICE IS WADING THROUGH THE CROWD TO GET TO THE RING!!! [PS] Neither rain, nor hail, nor wooden table... will keep Justice from helping the innoce... {Paul is interrupted by a loud cracking sound coming from the ring... or, specifically, from Kieth Matthews leg...} [HR] DID YOU HEAR THAT!!! I think he just broke Kieth Matthews' leg!!!! Justice is in the ring now, and the Philosopher and Sarah Barker are beating a hasty retreat! I can't wait to hear what Daki Chamberlin has to say about THIS!! Folks, we'll be back!!! **************************************************************************** What was that Jerone said about commercials again? ****************************************************************************
