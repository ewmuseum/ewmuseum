[AWI] Armageddon '97, Pt. III

14 August 1997

[RJr.] "thr...NO! SIM KICKS OUT!!! Amazingly Sim kicked out after the punishment he's taken and missing that elbow drop." [CH] "Amazing? More like stupid if you ask me. I would have laid there and died myself." [SA] "That's why Sim is the wrestler and you're the announcer." [RJr.] "Sim apparently bleeding from the head, presumably as a result of one of Mischief's forearm shots... Mischief with another kick...followed by a second short arm clothesline. Scoops Sim up...and a nails a sidewalk slam. But Sim fights back with..." [CH] "Let me guess! An elbow smash. Man let me tell ya. That Sim sure knows a truckload of wrestling moves. God forbid he add a few more to his arsenal." [RJr.] "Mischief shrugs off the elbow and responds with a neckbreaker that lays Sim flat. Mischief lifts him to his feet...irish whip...AND THERE IT IS!!! "BIG TROUBLE" Tilt-a-Whirl Backbreaker. 1....2....3!!! That's all she wrote for Chris Sim tonight. Mendoza quickly into the ring to stave off any further damage to his fallen comrade.' [SA] "A truly impressive win by Ken Mischief... He managed to make sure that Sim Sim never established any sort of game plan in that one." [CH] "On the contrary Stephen. I think the idea of absorbing all of that punishment until Mischief couldn't beat him anymore was a good idea. Too bad Sim couldn't last long enough until Mischief tired himself out." [RJr.] "Always the unbelievable insight from you Chris... Sim appears to be fine, though he's holding his back a bit... Fans let's go to another commercial break and when we get back, words from tonight's participants in the NA Tag Team Tournament." ============================================================================== {The screen goes black, then a computer graphic of a cherry bomb appears. After a second the fuse sparks, burning down quickly until the cherry bomb explodes into flames.} Cherry Bomb Pro Wrestling is an e-wrestling fed based in Japan, as well as a regional promotion of GDWA. We're currently looking for Japanese female wrestlers, especially heels. So if an e-fed with a distinctly Japanese style of wrestling interests you, check out our way cool webpage at http://www.indra.com/~michiku and see what we are all about. If you have any questions, contact Miko at [detail withheld] ============================================================================== [RJr.] "Fans we've just been informed that there is a new developement in the North American Tag Team Tournament concerning the belts and their *previous* owners...the team Known as Perfection has re-emerged on the scene and are supposed to be somewhere in the building. We have a camera*..." {"The Perfect Fanfare" keys up and a loud chorus of boos greet the unexpected return of Perfection, accompanied by Johnny Rooks} [CH] "AND THERE THEY ARE!!!" [SA-vo] It seems that the former North American Tag-Team Champions have returned.. Kim Lee seems to have even bulked up a bit in their absense... [Kim Lee] We're ba-ack! [Jilliam Bole] Did you miss us? [KL] I should hope so since the tag-team ranks have been pathetically devoid of quality competition since we've left. [JB] True, but what can one expect without us, after all, we are perfection. [KL] How true Jil... yet I can't but help feel something is missing... [JB] Yes... what could we have forgotten? [KL] We? Nothing, we are perfection. [JB] Yes, you see, Kim and I were taking a much deserved vacation, relaxing, eating the best foods, seing the best shows, visiting the best sights, meeting the best women... [KL] We did all this confident that our man here {pulls Rooks over and pats him on the back} could take care of buisness. [Johnny Rooks] That's right, and... {Kim not-too gently snatches the mic back} [KL] And, for the first time I can remember, we were wrong. [JB] Yeah Kim, my waist feels a bit drafty... {At this point Rooks starts looking nervous, but Kim has a strong grip on him} [KL] Well, we can't begrudge him too much... after all, he's not us.. [JB] True... well, since we are pefection, I suppose we'll have to let him get off... with about a month. [KL] No, Jil, we owe Mr. Rooks a chance to save himself. Johnny, you have to the count of five to leave the ring, or we'll leave you in a condition that will make the Plague Dog envious. [JR] WHAT??!! Boys, I know... [JB] One [JR] I made some mistakes, but I thought.... [KL] Two [JR] we had covered that. You *need*... [JB] Three [JR] me and.... oh, hell! [RJr.-VO] And Rooks *bails* out of the ring at high speeds! He's headed for the hills! [KL] Well, we've learned from our mistakes, and this {points to Rooks' retreating back} will never happen again. [JB] Being that we are perfection, we do not need a manager to screw up our buisness affairs. [KL] Instead, we're sure, Mr. Chamberlin, that you shall be much wiser than your father was and treat us fairly, lest you share his fate. [JB] Chamerlain, we, the former and should be current North American Tag-Team champions are not even in the tourniment for the belts that are ours. At the same time, teams such as the War Birds, whom we have defeated, are not only allowed in this tourniment yet get a shot at the world tag-team titles? Mr. Chamberlain, we're sure you will see the lunacy in this and grant us what should rightfully be ours, the shot at Intensive Care. [KL] We held the belts, we never lost them, we should thus be the #1 contenders. Give us what we rightfully deserve based upon our accomplishments and record, you will not like the alternative. {"The Perfect Fanfare" keys up and a loud chorus of boos greet the exit of Perfection, minus Johnny Rooks} [RJr.] "WOW! I think that caught /EVERYONE/ off guard. Who expected Perfection to be making a return on the eve of the tournament!?" [SA] "Don't forget relieving Johnny Rooks of his managerial duties." [CH] "Man WHAT are those guys thinking? Rooks can't be blamed for the situation at hand. Chamberlin is a total goof ball and had /NO/ right even starting this stupid tournament!" [RJr.] "Well, all in all, that's a little bit to late to debate now. Even as we speak Team Stevens' newest mix and match of mayhem is ready to head down to ringside for action. Let's head to the interview spot with Chad." ============================================================================== [Cut to Chad Duncan in the interview area where Robbie Stevens and Agony & Ecstacy are standing by.] [Chad] Ladies and gentlemen, joining me at this time are the members of Team Stevens. But before we get started, Robbie. What exactly is going on with you, Jessica Perkins and Sarah Victory? [Robbie] I dunno Chad.... I didn't think having two women fighting over you would be so painful. Look, I will handle it, trust me. [Chad] Well, judging from the events that happened last week, you've got your hands full. Tonight, Tank Bradley and Toshiaki Hasegawa, you've got a match tonight with members of the Flock. [Bradley] Chad, before we even get started on them, let me clear up about what I said last week. I am playing games with Mike Piersall. I gave him a small sample of what level of pain is waiting for him when he steps in the ring. I let the guy slide last week. If I wanted to, I could have broke his neck... but I didn't. I asked for a match, he gave me a match, I'm not going to hold it against him for being stupid. The fact is, I destroyed him. I hit him with three moves and he threw in the towel. Now, Augustus can say I'm not making a smart move... Garcia can say I'm not making a smart move... but here's the straight facts. I am not giving Mike Piersall an out. I'm not about to let him and all the AWI fans say that Tank Bradley beat him only because he wrestled Springheel first. I don't need to beat a tired near cripple. I can beat a full rested near cripple. Piersall, the ball is now in your court. I got you in the ring. I embarassed you. You have the choice. I ain't afraid of you at all. I'll take you on at anytime and any place you name. I'm not a hard man to find. I'll let you slide. You gave me the match I asked for. You can try to redeem yourself to the eyes of the fans or you can go find a rock to hide under, either way, I don't care. As far as the Flock goes. I don't sweat them. James, I don't care who you bring. You're just a bunch Jehoviah Witness knocking on my door on Sunday Morning at 8 A.M. And I'm gonna do to you exactly what I do them. {Bradley cracks his knuckles and draws his thumb across his throat}. [Chad] Mr. Hasegawa.... [Joey stands directly in front of Chad. Chad's tone becomes slightly annoyed] You're thoughts on facing the Flock tonight? ['Smilin' Joey] The big man is right ... Flock, we have nothing against you. All I know is I look /FAR/ too good to be losing this early in the tournament. Our friend Carlos Mendoza can sort of illustrate my point here ... he has no sense of style. That's why I beat him so easily - you caught that match right Chad? [Chad] Yes, though I don't think it was such an easy victory. ['Smilin' Joey] Are you kidding - I was toying with him. That we got more TV time. Anyway, Flock, I'm treating you like a warmup for my big match coming up with the Sensei here. Tank is far too dangerous, and I'm far too suave though ... you don't stand a chance. [Chad] Well, there you have it from a confident Team Stevens. Back to you Raul. [Fade out.] ============================================================================== [RJr.] "Well let's not waste anymore time Chad. Let's send this one straight to the ring for our Main Event!" [AK] "Ladies and gentlemen the following contest is the opening match to crown a /NEW/ AWI North American Tag Team Champion!!!..." {crowd pops big for tournament to crown new champions} "...Our first team...representing Team Stevens...weighing in at a total combined weight of 589 lbs..."Tank" Bradley..."Toshiaka "Smilin Joey" Hasegawa...AGONY AND ECSTACY!!!" {"You're So Vain" by Faster Pussycat blares over the PA as Robbie and Heads to ringside with "Tank" Bradley and Smilin Joey right behind him.} [AK] "And their opponents...weighing in at a combined weight of 523 lbs...hailing from Salem, Massachusetts and Estonia respectively...Rev. Jerimiah James..."The Philosopher" Mikhail Tvskova...THE FLOCK!!! {"Onward Christian Soldiers" keys up as James and Tzskova walk down the aisle to the ring. Both appear to be very stern and focused.} [AK] "Your referee for this bout is Marty Wood." [DING!] [RJr.] "And folks that bell sounds for another first on Armageddon, the beginning of our North American Tag Team Title Tournament. It looks like Hasegawa and will start for A&E, and The *good* Rev. for The Flock." [SA] "As far as team work goes this match is a toss up, but if I have to give anyone an advantage it would have to be The Flock. With their size and experience, they should have the decisive edge in this match." [RJr.] "Good point. Our two combatants ready to lock up, but wait...not before Smilin Joey goes into his runway strut...much to the disamy of the fans and James. Ane now Joey with a leg drag take down...but the Rev. Quickly back up to his feet...and curses Hasegawa with a choke... and a chokeslam from nowhere! Wood with a warning..." [CH] "Let the good reverend tell you it came from the heart." [SA] "Well he /IS/ a man of God. Why would we ever doubt his word?" [RJr.] "James with a hammerlock...but Joey counters with one of his own...lifts the reverend up for a slam...and nails him with a dropkick on the way up. And a flurry of kicks from Hasegawa. Might be laying off the groin as so not to get a DQ loss in the tournament this early." [SA] "Well you know good ol Marty is one who is quick to pull the trigger. I'll bet this match means a little too much for him to call it this soon." [RJr.] "Right you are as Wood let's the match go on, aware of the tactics of Hasegawa. Lifts James and sends him for an Irish Whip...Sunset Flip on the return...he has a hand full of tights...but Woods sees it and calls for a break!" [CH] "Well I guess he's not gonna let *everything* go. Although I do believe Joey's hands *accidently* got caught in the Reverend's pants." [SA] "I'm not /EVEN/ going to comment on /THAT/ one..." [RJr.] "Joey now biting at James' face...which brings in Tzskova...which in turn brings in Bradley! But both go back to their corners as Wood gets Joey to break and gives him a stern warning." [CH] "Oooooooooo! I bet Joey's scared of him now. Probably didn't understand a word he said. You know he doesn't speak English" [SA] "But Hasegawa is very fluent in English." [CH] "Yeah but Wood ain't!" [RJr.] "James with an irish whip...and Joey with a sleeper on the reverend on the return trip. James having trouble...but breaks the hold. And Joey donates a low blow to the groin for his offering to the reverends cause... and now sends the reverend on his way to the outside to receive more offerings from Robbie." [CH] "Hey at least they are being generous. What did Mendoza ever give the good reverend? Joey's got more class and an even bigger heart." [SA] "Well Robbie is being pretty generous with that chain as he lays into James." [RJr.] "Tzskova not to happy about the way that Team Stevens pays worship and he goes around to help his partner...and Tank Bradley rolls James back into the ring. Joey goes for his patented stomp flurry...but James rolls out of the way and gets to his feet...only to get dropped by a low blow from Hasegawa. This guy disgusts me." [CH] "Yeah he does. You'd think that after James gave him the Television Title he'd be a little bit more appreciative." [RJr.] "Joey rolls James up into a small package after he doubles him over with that low blow...He's got the tights again!!! WOOD ISN'T IN POSITION TO SEE IT!!! 1....2....3!!! And Team Stevens has stolen another victory via treachery and deceit." [SA] "Well James and Tzskova got what they deserve. It looks as if A$E practice what the good Rev. preaches, and it payed off." [RJr] "That it did. And Folks we are out of time on this third edition of AWI Armaggedon. For Stephen Augustus and Chris Hairie, I'm Raul Garcia Jr. saying, so long everybody!"
