[AWI] Video Magazine 05/09/96, Pt. I (of II)

9 May 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
{A montage of clips fly by the screen.... Dr. Hyde with the Victoria Cross on The Olympian... "Condor" Eric King with the Dive Bomb on John Robertson... Jack Fury with the Jackhammer on Crystal Crow... "Bulldog" Bryan Bachman with the Top Rope Bulldog on "Olympian" Gary White... The Mist Angel with the Cradle Superplex on Danny Boy McGill... John Robertson with the Cradle Suplex on the "Devastating" Dan Lea... D.A. Bookthrower with the Instrument Of Justice on Oval Office... Danny Boy McGill with the McGill Leglock on Mist Angel... War Machine with the Powerplex on "the Awesome One" Kerry Masters... The Olympian with the Gold Medal Hold on Dr. Hyde... Justice with the Summons on Little Vincent... and finally Lord Thomas Imperious with the Yappian Sleeper on Bryan Bachman... then we fade into what looks like a newscasters desk as a logo appears... --------------- /\ \ / / / \ \ /\ / / / \ \/ \/ / -------------- V V IIIII DDDD EEEEE OOO V V I D D E O O V V I D D EEE O O V V I D D E O O V IIIII DDDD EEEEE OOO M M AAA GGGG AAA ZZZZZ IIIII N N EEEEE MM MM A A G A A Z I NN N E M M M AAAAA G GG AAAAA Z I N N N EEE M M A A G G A A Z I N NN E M M A A GGG A A ZZZZZ IIIII N N EEEEE {A close up shows the two "anchorpersons", "Mastermind" Alliyah Johnston, a well-toned attractive Hispanic woman, and Chad Duncan, a slighlty overweight Black man. Both are dressed in nice business attire.} [CD] Good evening, fans, and welcome to the AWI Video Magazine! I'm your host Chad Duncan, and with me is "The Mastermind" Alliyah Johnston for some expert commentary! Tonight's show will see some of the fallout from the shakedown that occurred at Ultimatum. [MAJ] Yes, we'll see interviews from {A picture of Justice shows up news-style behind her} the man that tried just a bit too much, and ended up a martyr... yes, I do mean Justice, and the man that reaped the rewards of that, "The Olympian" Gary White {OGW in the inset}. [CD] Also today we'll see the AWI TV Championship up for grabs as "Theodocious Ferrocious" Wilson takes on "Bulldog" Bryan Bachman... [MAJ] This one could be interesting... Wilson is a solid power wrestler, but he can come out of nowhere with moves like his "Noose" sleeperhold. Conversely, you have the guy that NEVER runs out of steam, Bryan Bachman. You have to give this one to Bachman. [CD] Also in action tonight we'll see the Crystal Crow take on "Predator" Adam White! [MAJ] What we're going to see today is an excercise in the effects of a good manager. Adam White is coming into this mad, so he won't totally be on his toes, and... well, let's just say the Jade Tiger is batting 1.000 with his Kendo stick. I would expect the Crystal Crow to secure a victory with some... assistance from his mentor. [CD] Interesting calls... now the question is whether they'll come true. Let's go to the ring! ****************************************************************************** Crystal Crow vs. "Predator" Adam White After introductions, both men circled each other warily. Crow made the first move inside with a karate kick, following it with a savat kick knocking White down. A quick stomp, and Crow sent White to the ropes for a jumping spin kick which couldn't down Adam. A second double irish whip, and this time it was the Crow that was downed with a thunderous drop kick from the bigger Predator. Predator picked him up for an atomic drop, and then, after a whip to the ropes, gave him a taste of the sidewalk-side of his boot. Finally, a side headlock on the downed Crow slowed things up a bit... For all of two seconds, as the Crow quickly manuevered out and landed a quick punch. He then landed a second karate kick as White tried to stand, and finally downed the Predator with a roundhouse. Did I mention that ALL of these shots were to the head? Silly me. The Predator moved out of the way of a second stomp attempt, but got caught by the third. Jade Tiger then jumped to the ropes to discuss stock options the referee, while Crystal Crow planned to toss Mr. White outside the ring. Unfortunately, teh Predator decided he didn't like the taste of ringside mat, and reversed the throw, sending the Crow crashing to the outside. Jade Tiger stood vigil over his protege, until a six count, at which point the Crow made it back in... ...to the waiting arms of the Predator's half nelson. White lifted both to their feet, then lifted Crystal Crow up for a backbreaker. Not satisfied, he picked him back up for a HARD piledriver, but caught an oriental spike as he went to capitalize on his next move. The Crow kept trhe spike on just long enough to toss White out, into the waiting arms on Jade Tiger. Or, more specifically, the waiting arms of the Tiger's kendo stick. After a couple of shots which thoroughly equalized things, Tiger rolled White in. Crow lifted him up, sent him into the ropes, and promptly hit the Tiger's Pounce Scissors Piledriver. It was a simple 1, 2, 3 from there. ****************************************************************************** Rhea Perlman coach basketball? Check out "Sunset Park".... all the music you want is at Sam Goody's. Are you afraid of the pultry police? If not, go to Long John Silvers.... ****************************************************************************** {fade back to the Mastermind and Chad} [CD] Ultimatum II has come and gone, wrestling fans, but we will feel the repercussions of it for a very long time. One of the specific situations involves the... well, twisted web surrounding the North American title belt. I had a chance to talk to two of the people involved in that situation. Here's what the ex-champ had to say... {switch to Chad Duncan in the middle of the ring.} [CD] My guest at this time is the *former* North American Heavyweight Champion. HE..... IS..... JUUUUUUUSSSSSSSSSSTICE!!!!!!!! {big pop, as Justice joins Duncan} [CD] Justice, thank you for joining us today. {Justice nods} At the Ultimatum Pay-per-view of a few days ago, you were slotted to face 'The Awesome One' Kerry Masters in the first match of the North American title mini-tournament. Instead, you wrestled *Robbie Stevens* first, then lost the belt to 'The Olympian' Gary White. Your thoughts on this odd series of events. [J] Odd, indeed. First of all, I'd like to wish 'The Awesome One', as well as Deric James and Madeline Freechilde, a speedy recovery. Although we were the bitterest of rivals, Masters proved, at times, that he could be a worthy combatant. It is to *that* man I give my best wishes. Secondly, Gary White. {pauses} That man was handed a golden opportunity, when he was named Masters' replacement in the tournament. It became more of an opportunity when, through my own pride, he became the default favorite to win the title. For that, I offer my most sincere apologies to the War Machine, Greg Gardner, and, especially, to Jack Fury. [CD] So, what does that mean? Do you plan to pursue 'The Olympian', and attempt to win back the North American championship? [J] Not at this time, no. I imagine both the War Machine and Jack Fury are unimpressed by his hold on the title, and will take steps to relieve him of the burden. What's more, while White obtained the belt unfairly, it was through no actions of his own. He didn't cheat to win any of his matches, nor did he arrange for anybody else's difficulties. But White, I know your kind better than that, and believe me when I say, I *will* be watching you very closely. [CD] That leaves one matter left over from Ultimatum, Robbie Stevens. [J] {Justice's normally grim face becomes even more foreboding} Oh, yes. Robbie..... Stevens..... I say I regret my decision to wrestle Stevens that night, but not because of the match itself. Somebody had to shut his mouth, especially after all he said that night. And it was not only my duty, but my *pleasure*, to beat him like the cowardly dog that he is. Your man on the outside couldn't stop me, Stevens. That chain you used on my head couldn't stop me. I showed that when I say 'Justice will be served!', that NOTHING can stop me!!! But Stevens, it is very obvious, by Bradley's attack on me after the match, that you haven't learned your lesson yet. So listen closely to what I say, for my words bring tiding of your doom. Very soon, I will declare war on you, and all your people. Chris Revi. 'Tank' Bradley. And you. I won't lower myself to assault a woman. That would be your tactics. But I will be sure that Tori Johannessen will have nobody left to guide her. And as for the Honor Guard.... I respected you, and still do, to an extent. But every time I see you with Stevens, every time I hear you fall for one of his lines, it makes me think that you are more fools than true men. If you get in my way when I finally decide to finish with Stevens, then so help me, the wrath of *Justice* will fall on your heads as well!! [CD] You say that you will deal with Robbie soon. What are you planning to do until then? [J] To be honest with you, Mr. Duncan, I'm not really regretful that I lost the title belt. My gaining it was the end result of a long and complicated series of events, and what those events did was distract me from my true purpose: to bring fairness, and true justice to *everyone* in the A.W.I. That is why, for the time being, I am going to rededicate myself to those goals. To show that whenever anyone tries to take a shortcut, whenever *anyone* tries to obtain victory by underhanded means, that they must reckon with JUSTICE!! For every wrestler out there, listen to me: Consider what you do very carefully, in the coming weeks. If you do not, judgement will be passed upon you, immediately, and with no appeal. Once again, JUSTICE SHALL BE SERVED!!!!!!! {Big pop, then fade back to commercials...}
