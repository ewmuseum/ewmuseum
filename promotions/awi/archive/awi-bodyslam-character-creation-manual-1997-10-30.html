BODYSLAM! Basic Character Creation Manual v1.1 Written by [name withheld] *** I. About This Manual *** This section provides the BARE BONES required to create a BODYSLAM! wrestler. For more clarification, see the BODYSLAM! Advanced Character Creation Manual. For an example of character creation, see the BODYSLAM! Sample Character Manual. If you have not read the Advanced Character Creation Manual, sections of the BODYSLAM! Wrestler Sheet will be unfamiliar. Simply skip them until you actually read the Advanced Manual. *** II. Creating An Image *** If you are playing as part of a role-playing campaign, please see the appropriate campaign information for guidelines as to what would be an "acceptable" character. In either case, figure out what you're character is "like" -- height, weight, name, personality, wrestling style, finisher, etc. *** III. Creation Points *** Creation Points are spent to determine the exact statistics of your character. You spend creation points to buy your character's attributes, height and weight, merits or flaws, etc. Every character starts off with 50 points. *** IIIA. Merits cost Creation Points to get; Flaws give Creation points. Available Merits are: *Ally (1CP) -- the character has one friend which will always be there whenever he can to help the character (Sting and Lex Luger) *Contacts (2cps) -- the character has Front-Office connections, and deals with officials more easily (Eric Bischoff) *Established Star (3CPs) -- the character was big-time before he came to the federation, and is more popular/hated as such (Kevin Nash entering WCW) *Fast Learner (3CPs) -- the character picks up skills and techniques faster than anyone else ("Diamond" Dallas Page) Fear Inducer (2CPs)-- the character possess an object which makes EVERYONE run for cover (Jake "The Snake" Robert's snakes) *Invulnerable (3cps) -- the character can shrug off all but the hardest blows at will (Ahmed Johnson) *Manager (1-4cps) -- the wrestler has a valet or manager (Vader has Paul Bearer) *Manager's License(2CPs) -- the character is a licensed manager, and gets paid by his charges, and cannot be removed from ringside normally (Paul Bearer) *Mentor (1CP) -- The character was trained by another wrestler(past or present), and gets more moves because of it (Bret/Owen Hart being trained by Stu Hart) *Miracle Man (2CPs) -- the wrestler recovers from injuries extrodinarily quickly (Sting) *Referee Slack (2CPs) -- the character tends to not get caught by refs, and punished more lightly when caught (Jerry Lawler) *Rich (2CPs) -- the wrestler has a lot of cash, and his money goes a long way (Ted Dibiase) *Set-Up Move(1CP) -- the wrestler has a move he does which sets up for his finisher (Hulk Hogan's Big Boot Kick before the legdrop) *Special Weapon(3/5CPs) -- the wrestler has a special weapon he can use which will pass a ref search (Lex Luger's steel forearm) *Striking Appearance(1CP) -- the wrestler has something about his looks which strike an emotional note with the fans (Shawn Michaels) *Styles(2CPs) -- the wrestler is adept at one particular style of wrestling. (Terry Funk as a heel). *** IV. Manuevers *** *** IV.A -- The Manuever List *** Once you've spent all of your Creation Points, you must choose which manuevers your character is skilled in. Manuevers fall in five classes, analagous to the Attribute used to determine their success -- Strength, Technical, Dexterity, Brawling, and Savvy. The wrestler gets a number of manuevers in each class equal to their Rating in that Attribute divided by ten. Savvy is a dual exception to the rule -- the wrestler gets a number of Savvy manuevers equal to their SAV divided by seven (rounded down), and the wrestler may choose to buy manuevers from ANY class with the SAV "slots", or add a +5% bonus to perform the manuever for each slot invested (+20% maximum bonus to any manuever). For example, a wrestler with a 40 SAV gets 5 SAV "slots" rather than 4, and may get manuevers from ANY class with those 5 slots, or add up to 5 +5% bonueses to moves (up to 4 on any one move), rather than having to get 5 SAV moves. In addition to being described by Class, Manuevers are also described by Level, which describes how hard it is to utilize. Levels are described by a number, with very simple manuevers (like a punch) being Level One, and complex manuevers having much higher levels (a surboard is Level Five, for example). However, there are a number of additional "flags" which can be placed on moves in the above list which describe new ways to use these moves. One example is the difference between a normal Elbow Drop, and "Macho Man" Randy Savage's Flying Elbow Drop. In this example, the "Flying" portion is a Manuever Variation. Manuever Variations cause the mechanics of a manuever to change. Manuevers with variations must be chosen seperately from those without (i.e. elbow drop, flying elbow drop, and suicide elbow drop would require three DEX "slots"). Avaliable Manuever Variations are : *Assisted -- The manuever involves using outside help for "cheatin' leverage" on a specific hold. Adds a level to the manuever. *Corner -- The Manuever is performed on an opponent stuck in the corner. *Combo -- the manuever is performed twice on an opponent in one round (punching someone twice quickly). Usable on BRA moves *Crossface -- the manuever gets added damage from grabbing and twisting the opponent's face and neck. Usable on TEC holds, and adds a level. *Dashing -- the manuever involves running first, then performing the mauever (dropkicking someone after running to the ropes, for example). *Elevated -- the TEC hold gets added damage by lifting the opponent in the air. Adds one level and makes the manuever a STR move *Flurry -- as Combo, repeated three times. (Kicking someone two or three times). Adds two levels to a manuever *Flying. -- the opponent jumps first before performing a manuever which could be done without jumping. Makes the manuever a DEX manuever *Handspring -- the wrestler does a running handspring before performing the manuever. Adds two levels to a manuever, and makes it a DEX manuever. *Hipbuster -- the character runs along the apron to perform a manuever on someone on the outside floor. Adds a level to the manuever, and makes it a DEX move. *Moonsault -- The wrestler performs a back somersault before performing the manuever. Adds a level to the manuever, and makes it a DEX manuever. *OffTheTopRope -- the Manuever involves flinging/throwing your opponent off the top rope. Adds a level to the manuever. *OverTheTopRope -- The Manuever involves flinging/tossing the opponent over the top rope. Adds two levels to the manuever. *Pinfall -- The manuever is held at the end to keep the person in a pinfall position. *Razzle -- the manuever is performed with an extra, unnecessary step which makes it "look cooler" (The flip Marc Mero does before the Merosault). Adds a level to the manuever. *Running -- the manuever is performed WHILE running (for example, British Bulldog's Running Powerslam). Adds a level to the manuever. *Screwjob -- the pinfall manuever has some cheating aspect to make it more effective. *Shooting Star -- the manuever involves performing a forward backflip before doing it. Adds a level to the manuever, and makes it DEX. *Slingshot -- the wrestler pulls the ropes for momentum, and launches himself over (into the ring). Adds a level to the manuever, and makes it DEX. *Somersault -- the wrestler performs a somersault before performing the manuever. Adds a level to the move, and makes it DEX. *Springboard -- the manuever involves the wrestler jumping ONTO the top rope first, then launching himself at the opponent. Adds a level, and makes the manuever DEX. *StandingOnTopRope -- The manuever involves flinging your opponent off the top rope while BOTH wrestlers stand on the top rope. Adds TWO levels to the manuever. *Suicide -- the manuever involves jumping from inside the ring to the outside floor. Adds TWO levels to the manuever. *ThroughTheRopes -- The manuever involves knicking the opponent through the ropes to the floor. Adds a level to the manuever *Top-Rope/Second-Rope. The manuever involves jumping on someone from the top or sencond rope (only one can be chosen), then performing the manuever. Adds a level to the manuever, and makes it DEX. ***III.C -- Finishing Manuevers *** Finally, the wrestler chooses one manuever as his/her "Finisher" (unless s/he has the Finisherless Flaw). That manuever gets an automatic +5% to hit, does more damage than a regular version of that move, and is harder to kick out of. The Stone Cold Stunner is the finisher of Steve Austin, for example. *** IV. Where to go from here? *** Of course, there is more to character creation than merely the above.
