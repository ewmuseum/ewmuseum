[AWI] Explosion 05/19/96, Pt. III of III

19 May 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Camera cuts in on Carnage and Destruction in the familiar darkened room.) [Destruction] We're wanted men! Knock Around the Clock seems like an eternity ago, but the memories of that night still linger in the minds of both our opponents and our partners from that night alike! [Carnage] We've done what we set out to do after Knock -- to cut a path of destruction through the entire AWI! First we fought Cutting Edge again, and they still couldn't beat us! Then Perfection, our unfortunate partners from that night got their chance. They went down to defeat! Concrete Jungle -- They didn't even show UP! And now, Cutting Edge wants a rematch. [D] Consider it done, Cutting Edge! We don't shy away from anybody. And this time, we're gonna finish the job we started in our first match -- pound you two into a pulp of quivering jello! [C] This is only the beginning. We don't intend to stop here! We want it all, and we want it NOW! {Fade out into the ring with Alan Kinsman...} [AK] Our next contest is a tag team attraction... our first competitors... are from St. Louis, MO... at a total combined weight of 460 lbs.... led to the ring by their manager, MR. STEVEN WILLIAMS... "BOMBIN'" BOB JONES... and TERRY "The TACTICIAN" JONES... THE CUTTING EDGE!!!!!! {"Tom Sawyer" by Rush keys up, and the crowd gets on it's feet for the Joneses...} Their opponents... from Parts Unknown... at a total combined weight of 541 lbs... CARNAGE... DESTRUCTION... they are DAMAGE, INC.!!!!! {"Damage, Inc." by Metallica rushes up over the loudspeaker, as the two big men walk down the aisle to crowd boos and some trash...} [HR] Folks, this is a grudge match to end all grudge matches... Damage, Inc. has been trying to put away the Joneses for a long time, but Bob and Terry have proven to be their equals in the ring... [MH] Yeah, it's like stompin' on roaches... no matter how hard you squish 'em, they just keep gettin' up and comin' back for more! [PS] Now THAT was a picture that I like to keep fresh in my mind. Thanks, Mike! [HR] It looks like Terry and Carnage are going to start this one off... collar-and-elbow tieup... Terry with a leg takedown to get the bigger Carnage down to the mat... but Carnage punches him off... [MH] Which, of course, is the best way to end that technical crap... [PS] Ever the master strategist, huh Mike? [HR] Terry returning with a half-nelson as both men rise... hey! {Switch to a shot of the announcer's table, as "Condor" Eric King has pulled up a chair and procured a headset...} [CEK] Good Evening, Ms. Rasputin... Paul... [HR] What brings you out here? [CEK] Scoping things out... studying different styles... [MH] And runnin' from the Iceman! Ran outta places to hide in the locker room? [HR] Michael! Carnage out of the half-nelson... Terry whips him in... and hits a BIG backdrop! [CEK] Good solid move... when going against a bigger man, it's always best to use their weight against them... and Carnage rolling outside is a good idea also, as it gives him a moment to regroup and rethink strategy... and no, Helics, the Condor runs from noone. I fought the Iceman in three of the hardest matches of my career, and when all was said and done, my hand was raised on all three occassions... [MH] It ain't quite the same when it's raised from a hospital bed! [HR] Gentlemen, let's get back to the match... Carnage rolls back in... tags to Destruc... no, the Tactician with a small package before he can tag out! 1... but Carnage is out! Both men up... Terry with the irish whip... but Carnage grounds him with a clothesline! Carnage lifts Terry to his feet... lifts... no, Terry blocks it, and hits a bodyslam! [CEK] Carnage probably should have tagged out while he had the chance... now, Mr. Helics, I have nothing more to prove against Chris Revi. If you have some problem with me... well, you're welcome to teach me how you all did things back in the day, or at least try to. [PS] Oooooooooo... are you gonna take that Mike? I wouldn't take that! [HR] Paul!!! Double irish whip... Terry with a tag... I don't think Carnage saw it... DOUBLE DROPKICK by the Edge!... followed by a savat kick by Bombin' Bob as Carnage rises... and Carnage rolls outside again! [PS] The Cutting Edge is really looking sharp today folks! But Mike, are you just gonna take that? [MH] Look, if I didn't have this neck injury... [PS] Blahblahblah... yeahyeahyeah... whatever. [MH] Look, Stone, don't think I can't still whip your... [CEK] Actually, I did come out here to announce one other thing -- the Condor is the newest competitor to throw his name into the Long Hot Summer hat! And I even got a dream first round matchup. [PS] Which is? [CEK] I'm getting a second shot at "Devastating" Dan Lea, and Dan was even nice enough to put the Light Heavyweight belt up for grabs while he was at it. [MH] I would too. You can always use an easy title defense. [HR] Carnage rolls back in the ring now... Tags in Destruction... but Bob Jones with a Bodypress as Destruction enters! No count... but Destruction BITES Bob as both men rise! [MH] I guess he really wants to sink his teeth into the Cutting Edge! [CEK] Paul, you are the humor man out here, right? [PS] I count my blessings that I am. [HR] Destruction lifts Bob up... no, Bob blocks it... but Destruction with a fallaway slam... and then locks on an armlock on the smaller of the Edge... [CEK] Impressive manuever from such a large man... most power wrestlers don't put a lot of weight into the technical side, and I think that may make Damage, Inc. and even more formidable team. And, on that note, I think I shall take my leave. Mr. Helics, if you ever want to come out of retirement, you know where to find me. {With a slight smile, the Condor leaves the annoucing table, to crowd cheers.} [MH] That's what's wrong with America today! No respect for your elders. [PS] Actually, I think he simply doesn't respect YOU. [MH] Hey! [HR] Bob out of the armlock... Destruction with a punch as both men rise... and he tosses Bob to the outside... [MH] And right into a beating! Ha! [HR] Destruction now talking to the referee... Carnage is picking up Bombin' Bob... a whip to the railing... NO! Bob sends Carnage into the railing! Bob crawls back in... but Destruction goes to the eyes as he enters... and then bodyslams the much smaller Jones... and now it's back to the armlock... [MH] As much as I like Damage Inc. in ass-kickin' mode, this is good. Bombin' Bob can't fly all over the place if he's stuck on the ground. [HR] That seems to be very apt... Bob finally gets out, but Destruction grounds him again with a punch... Destruction grabs him by the hair... lifts him up... NO! Bob spins and manuevers into a monkey flip to take the big man down! Bob going for the tag... but DEstruction tackles him to keep him in the ring! [MH] A sound technical manuever if I ever heard of one! [HR] Bob with a punch this time to get Destruction away... drops a quick elbow... Bob to the ropes... and floors the big man as he rises with a clothesline! Bombin' Bob is on fire now! He tags in big brother Terry... they whip Destruction in... DOUBLE BACKDROP! The referee's putting Bob out... Terry with an armdrag takedown on Destruction... and Carnage comes in and chokeslams Terry! What kind of move was that? [MH] The effective kind. Destruction's back in control, isn't he? [HR] Yes he is, but the referee's putting Carnage out of the ring... and Destruction throws Terry outside while the referee's back was turned! [MH] Even more of the effective kind of manuever. [HR] You are sick, Mike... Destruction now jawing with referee Pete Allen... Carnage has gotten around to Terry... and Carnage whips Terry into the railing! Allen's getting played like a fiddle right now... [MH] And what a sweet song it is! [HR] You ARE sick! Carnage lifts Terry in the air... press slam... ONTO THE RAILING! That could have caused some serious injury! Steven Williams is over by his man... Carnage has spotted him... he grabs Williams... but Bob Jones out of nowhere with a dropkick for the save! Now Destruction rolls outside... [PS] I don't think this is looking good for the Edge... [HR] Bombin' Bob to the apron... he runs off the apron into an elbow drop on Carnage! [PS] Okay, perhaps I was mistaken. [HR] Bob setting Carnage up for a DDT... but Carnage backdrops him into the crowd! Mr. Steven Williams is wading through to help Bob... while Damage Inc seems to be setting up for the DOA on the ringside area! They're in position... Terry blocks the crucifix shoulderslam! And dodges the Flying Chokeslam! Terry Jones has just avoided the DOA! [PS] And added ten years to his lifespan in the process! [HR] The Tactician with a hard DDT on Destruction... rolls himself and Destruction back into the ring... both men to their feet... but Destruction with the shoulderbreaker! Williams has finally gotten Bob up and back to the corner... Destruction goes for the camel clutch... but Terry grabs a leg to stop it... Terry with an armdrag takedown... but Destruction with a VICIOUS eye gouge... their setting up again... this time Terry's up.... DOA ON TERRY JONES!!!! [MH] Well, that was an entertaining match. satisfying conclusion, too! [HR] Carnage with the pin... 1... 2... TERRY KICKS OUT!!! TERRY KICKS OUT!!! [Bell] DINGDINGDINGDINGDING!!!! [PS] Ummm... I thought you said Terry kicked out? [HR] I thought he did... let's get the official word. [AK] Ladies and Gentlemen... THE TIME LIMIT HAS EXPIRED... this contest has been ruled, a DRAW. [HR] Damage Inc. is FURIOUS!!! Steven Williams has herded his men out of their, but the Joneses seem ready to keep this one going... I'm sure we haven't seen the last of this... [MH] I hope not. The Edge's good luck streak can't last forever... [HR] I don't quite think it's luck... Let's have some words from our Light-Heavyweight champion, and we'll be back! ***************************************************************************** {screen fades to what you hopefully recognize as the Rooks Enterprises' gym. "Devastating" Dan Lea is doing arm curls on a complex piece of weightlifting machinery. Johnny Rooks stands nearby, and notices the camera.} [JR] Ah, good, I see you've finally arrived. Come on, come on, you're late, and you know, time is money. My time, and that of my men, is especially expensive. Dan's had a few things on his mind, and I thought we'd get them straightened out now. First thing. We have received notification that Dan is to be one of the AWI representatives at the Tiger Mask Invitational, to be held soon. What do you think, Dan? [DDL] I think that that Tiger Mask guy was a pretty good wrestler .... for a scrawny wimp. And I think that every other guy there'll be doing the same high-flying, acrobatic *crap* that the Mask used. But here's the deal. I'm the strongest light heavyweight in the AWI, and I got the belt to *prove* that makes me the best. {releases the hand grips to the weights, and a crash follows} And when I go to the Invitational, I'm gonna show the rest of the world that I *am* the strongest light heavyweight *anywhere*, and that I am the *best*!!!!! Bring on the mat wrestlers! Bring on the high flyers! I'll take them all out! You know, they call some of those aerial guys 'suicidal'. Well, they're right, 'cause when you get in the ring with me, it's suicide!! [JR] You've seen the announcements for 'The Long, Hot Summer' tournament? {Dan nods} Good, because I've entered you into it. [DDL] Yeah, actually, I was going to talk to you 'bout that. The payoff for that match is pretty big time, isn't it? 25 thou, and a World Title shot. I'm not sure I should have to go through this thing to get the shot, considering how easily I pounded McGill before, but I don't mind a light workout before I get the shot. [JR] Dan, do *not* get over confident. This tournament is filled with men who you haven't wrestled before, and a few of the bigger ones could probably test even *your* considerable strength. [DDL] Johnny, give me a *break*. It'll be like walkin' through the park. Only problem I see is if I get saddled with a partner who can't hold up his end on the random tag thing. So who's the first victim supposed to be? [JR] 'The Condor' Eric King. *And* we got a note from King, asking that the match be for the Lightheavyweight title. [DDL] You have to be kiddin' me! He wants to get pummled *again*?? That match with Revi must've addled his brains, 'cause if he were smart, he would've made for the hills as soon as he was told about it! What's the situation with the title? [JR] You have to defend it again, and soon. [DDL] {sighs} Yeah, I suppose so. Alright, sign it for a title match. King, you were stupid enough not to run, and you were stupid enough to ask for this title, I really hope you weren't so stupid that you forgot to arrange for your funeral, because once we get to the first round of this thing, I'm going to DEVASTATE you!!!! {fade out} ****************************************************************************** You want commercials? Exactly how late is this card again? And when do you think I'll get around to getting it out if I try to actually put COMMERCIALS in it? Man... some people just want everything. :) ****************************************************************************** {The scene is the Power Tools' workout area. A lot of equipment has been damaged severely.} [BLACK] Hey folks...as you can see we were a little upset at being eliminated so quickly from the Square Dance World Tag Title match. [DECKER] We could make excuses about being rusty from Bill bein' on the shelf for so long, but the fact is, we weren't as ready for this as we thought. Warbirds, congratulations. [BLACK] But don't think that just cuz we have common enemies that you shouldn't watch out for us, cuz we're STILL hungry for gold! [DECKER] Bill, I think you should be warning the Toxic Twins about that. [BLACK] That's right, Tyler an' Perry! We're gonna tear you limb from-- {Black takes a swing at Decker, who blocks it.} [DECKER] Yo, Saw...save it for the Twins! {Both stare at each other, scream, and attack each other as we fade out} ****************************************************************************** {Cut back to Alan Kinsman in the ring...} [AK] Ladies and gentlemen... the next match is our MAIN EVENT... it is a tag team attraction... and it is for the AWI NORTH AMERICAN TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP!... {Cue "Teaser" by Motley Crue as Tamara skips to the ring with a big smile on her face} [Tamara] Somebody in the AWI Head Office must really like me. After the way I was treated last week, it's poetic justice that my boys get this North American Title match tonight. So without further ado, weighing in at a combined weight of 561 LBS. They hail from Bala Cynwyd, PA and are /YOUR/ next AWI North American Tag Team Champions. Perry and Tyler... the Toxic Twins! {"Walk This Way" by Aerosmith fills the arena as Perry and Tyler Toxic step out from behind the curtains sporting Armani suits and smoking pipes instead of their usual cheap cigars. The Toxic Twins walk calmly to the ring, then take the mic from Tamara, who makes her exit from the ring.} [Perry] Hey Tyler, explain something to me. Last week we were beating the crap out of those chumps the Honor Guard pretty good. What I wanna know is how that weasel Robbie Stevens got his hands on Tamara. [Tyler] It's simple dear brother. That scum sucking, <censored> is just too damn sneaky. Robbie Stevens, we tip our hats to you. You got your hands on Tamara and distracted us and the ref enough to keep us from getting our rightful victory. [Perry] But the same thing we tip our hats for will also cost you your career. Nobody touches Tamara! So if we have to go through 'Ice Stick' Chris Revi, that Dump Truck jerk or the Honor Girls, big flippin' deal. You made our hit list Stevens and unlike Madeline Freechild, a car wreck won't keep up from kickin' your scrawny ass. [Tyler] But first, let's kick those Power Fools' asses. These suits need gold to look right. {With that, Tyler throws the mic back to Alan Kinsman...} [AK] Their opponents... hail from Detroit, MI... at a total combined weight of 575 lbs... "BUZZSAW" BILL BLACK... and DICK "THE DRILL" DECKER... THE POWER TOOLS!!!!! {The sounds of machinery grinding, hammering, etc. come up, as the Power Tools make their way to ringside... pound on each other for a few minutes halfway up the aisle, and continue into the ring.} [HR] Tyler and Perry have removed their jackets and ties... could this be a new image for the Twins? "Tyler" with the ta... no, Black with a forearm from behind prevents the tag... and the Honor Guard have appeared at ringside! Anybody kickin' ass like this ain't that bad...
