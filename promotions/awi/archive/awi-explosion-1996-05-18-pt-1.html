[AWI] Explosion 05/18/96, Pt. I

18 May 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Open to camcorder-like footage following Robbie Stevens and the Honor Guard walking through a parking lot towards a hotel.] [Robbie] Guys, I don't blame you for not to talk to Hubert. I mean, it's a good sign, you're not as blindly trusting everyone, but I think this is a mistake... [Sam] There's just no way he could do something like this. No offense, Robbie, but I know Paul, and I know Paul would never do anything like this. You have got the wrong person. [Robbie] And I respect that... You want to know.... I think it's a mistake because this is a distraction and I want you guys focused, but if that's what you want, that's what we'll do. [They enter the hotel lobby.] [Robbie] You find out what room he's in I'll get the elevator... Tank, the camera working OK? [Bradley] (from behind the camera) It's working fine... [camera shot pans to follow one of the maids walking down the hall.] [Robbie] Hey, don't waste the film! [Tape stops and comes up again with them stepping out of the elevator.] [Robbie] OK, what room did they say he was staying in? [Sam](looking down at a piece of paper) Room 504. [They walk down the hall to Room 504 and knock on the door. Two women, dressed in rather skanky clothes, answer the door. They're both drinking beers.] [Woman #1] Alright! Paul's back... (notices who they are) Oh, you're not Paul. [Woman #2] He better get back soon, we're running out of beer. [Sam] Excuse me... I think we've got... [Robbie] (Cutting him off) Is this Paul Hubert's room? [Woman #1] Yes it is. He went out to pick us up some more brews. You here to party with us, too? [Robbie] No, we came to talk to Paul.... excuse me for asking, but you two girls look a little young to be drinking. [Rich] They don't look that young, Robbie. [Robbie] Guys, I'm 20 years old, do you think I ever get carded at clubs? [Bradley] They don't check. Robbie has to tell them not to serve him alcohol. [Robbie] That's right, because underage drinking is wrong and against the law. Can I see some ID please? (They fish their ID's out of their pocket books and hand them to Robbie) YOU TWO ARE 17!?!?!? [Sam] What? Let me see that (looks at the IDs) That's what it says. [Robbie] What are you two doing here, drinking beer, in Paul's hotel room... on a school night! [Woman #1] If you can't figure that out.... [Woman #2] We're not gonna tell you! (giggles) [Robbie] This is just plain wrong... (walks into the room and walks out with what's left of a six pack) You girls should be home right now, you're parents are probably worried sick about you! And I'm confiscating your liquor. Tank, get rid of these... [Bradley] No problem... [Bradley takes the six pack, the camera gets put down and you can see Bradley starting to open a bottle.] [Robbie] Not right now! Get the camera.... Guys... I think we'd better get out of here, OK. I think we've seen too much already. [HG and Robbie start walking down the hall, Bradley picks up the camera and the beer. As Robbie and HG turn the corner, Chris Revi walks around the other corner carrying a case of beer. He enters the room.] [Woman #1] Alright! Paul's back! [Robbie] (yelling from down the hall) Tank! Let's go! [Camera shot cuts back to the lobby.] [Rich] Lying, no good, cradle-robbing... [Sam] Man.... I can't believe he would do....my God, are we so naive to have trusted Paul with our finances? He could be-- [Rich] Embezzling funds from us! Stealing. If he's doing...that with underage women, there's no TELLING what he could be doing! [Robbie] Guys, I don't want to say I told you so... but I told you so. Look, I'll take care of this. I'll make a few phone calls. You guys go back to our hotel and get some sleep, we've got a lot of training to do tomorrow. Remember, focus. OK? Talk to you tomorrow.... [Sam](walking away) I need to tell Danny Boy McGill about this...he needs to know what his manager is doing...he might look into how much of a cut Paul really IS taking from him. [Rich] Good thing ve haf a good, HONEST manager like Robbie to vatch our backs for us. [Sam] You got that right, Rich. We can't let that affect us, though...remember what Rob says, Focus......... [Robbie] (to the camera) Tank, think they bought it... (camera nods) Good... this was too easy. Let's go back up stairs. I love that color printer I got for Christmas, those ID's were easy to do. [Robbie and Tank start walking through the lobby] [Person working the desk] (to Robbie) Excuse me, Mr. Hubert. When will you be checking out tomorrow? [Robbie] Tank, turn that off.... {Fade into a computer-animated aerial view of a sports stadium; the camera view "flies" down to ground level into the parking lot, and through the arena doors which open for it, then up the aisle and into a wrestling ring where a bomb sits, timer ticking away... the screen cuts to clips of wrestling action with the bomb's digital readout superimposed on the screen, beeping as it counts down...} :10 Crystal Crow moonsaulting the Mist Angel... :9 Intensive Care and the Honor Guard in a four-man brawl outside... :8 Dr. Hyde manuevering out of "The Olympian" Gary White's holds.... :7 "Condor" Eric King leaping over the ropes to bodypress "Iceman" Chris Revi... :6 Megaton double-splashing the members of Big Time Wrestling... :5 Lord Thomas Imperious with the Yappian Sleeperhold on Deric James... :4 Jack Fury with the Jackhammer on Crystal Crow... :3 "Theodocious Ferrocious" Wilson raises his hands in victory... :2 "Tank" Bradley with the Nail In The Coffin on Justice... :2 Danny Boy McGill throwing the figure four on John Robertson... I'm your host, Heather Rasputin, and with me, as always is the ever-irrepressible Michael Helics and the ever-irresponsible Paul Stone... [MH] Okay, who lied to her and to her she was a comedian... [PS] Hey, don't stop her. The more she goes on the better I look! [HR] ANYWAY, as I said, we have a full hour's worth in excitement, as we'll see newcomers Jimmy Springheel and "The Philosopher" Mickael Tziskova in action! [MH] We're also gonna see Damage, Inc. take out the Jones' once and for all! [HR] Indeed, the Cutting Edge will be taking on Damage, Inc., but we'll also see the North American title get re-established tonight! [PS] That's right! The Doublemint Terrors, Perry and Tyler, are gonna try to take out the evidence that early release from the psycho ward is a BAD thing, the Power Tools! [HR] Also, we'll have a SPECIAL edition of "The Courtroom", where D. A. Bookthrower HIMSELF will come out to have an interview with the two men mixed up in the World title situation, Lord Thomas Imperious and Danny Boy McGill! [PS] Hey... I thought LTI was refusing any interviews! [HR] So did I, but apparently, something's changed... But first, let's get to the ring, where Spitfire Peters awaits one of the newest competitors to the AWI! {Cut to Spitfire Peters and ring announcer Alan Kinsman in the ring...} [AK] Ladies and Gentlemen.... WELCOME TO AWI EXPLOSION!!!!! Our first match is for one fall, with a ten-minute time limit.... out first competitor.... from Los Angeles, CA....SPITFIRE PETERS!!! Her opponent.... stands 5'8", 180 lbs... from Loch Ness, Scotland.... "THE RINGMASTER" DACIA BLACKTHORNE!!!!!!!! {Bagpipes playing "The Scotish Funeral March" key up, as Blackthorne comes to the ring to a favorable crowd reaction...} [HR] Both women circle each other... collar-and-elbow tie-up... and Blackthorne takes Peters down with an armdrag... Peters quickly moves into an armbar... but Blackthorne reverses it into an armbar of her own... she picks Spitfire up.... Short-Arm Clothesline... No, Spitfire ducks it... and lands a dropkick! I think she caught Blackthorne totally off-guard! [PS] Well, if the Ringmaster knew it was coming, I think she would have, like, moved or something. [HR] Spitfire picks up Dacia... double irish whip... Blackthorne with a sunset flip! 1... but Spitfire's quickly out... both women up... Dacia with a dropkick... NO! Spitfire's just TOO quick... [PS] Either that, or she knew it was coming... [HR] Peters looking to capitalize with an armbar, but Blackthorne quickly reverses her attempts... Blackthorne to the ropes... big kneedrop on Spitfire's arm... she picks Spitfire up... backs her into a corner... and starts WAILING on her with kicks... two to the face... one to the stomach... another to the face... two more to the stomach... [MH] And Blackthorne shows that there may be some hope for her yet! [HR] Blackthorne pulls Peters out into the center of the ring... both women struggling... neckbreaker by Blackthorne! She lifts Peters to her feet... whip into the ropes... connects with "The Claymore" Discus Punch! Blackthorne is simply on fire! [PS] Actually, I think "All Over Fire" is a better description, given Peters nickname... [HR] Dacia lifts Spitfire to her feet... double underhook pwerbomb!!!! [MH] Also known as -- to anyone that does their research -- the Loch Ness Drop! [HR] Well, the Loch Ness Drop seems to be effective, as the referee counts the pin... 1.... 2.... 3!!!! [AK] The winner of this match... "THE RINGMASTER" DACIA BLACKTHORNE!!!! [HR] A great opening win for the Ringmaster... let's look at those highlights again... {Switch to a replay of Blackthorne simply abusing Peters with kicks in the corner.... another shot of The Claymore.... and finally, Blackthorne finishes Peters off with the Loch Ness Drop...} {Dacia Blackthorne joins Chad Duncan in the Interview Spot...} [CD] An impressive debut from an impressive woman. What has made you come to the AWI? [RDB] Thank you Chad. Why am I here you say? Well, I'm here to put the wrestling back into women's wrestling. Enough of these perverted sex romps posing as wrestlers, I need a place where I can show the world that women are REAL wrestlers too. [CD] Who does your first challenge go out to? There are a lot of impressive female talent -- Monster 13... Tori Johannsen... Brenda Storm... and, of course, the Women's Champion Tiger Z... [RDB] Challenges right now are open to anyone. I'm not here to step on toes, but let me make it clear to all the ladies here in the AWI: I do want the championship belt. And by Loch Ness I'll be having it sooner or later. {Dacia walks off the platform...} [CD] A lot of challenges flying furiously in the AWI today, including a BIG one by these two men who, after AWI head Daki Chamberlain's announcement about the North American tag title situation last week , demanded a few moments to speak on this weeks show. So without further ado, here they are, Carnage, Destruction, Damage Incorporated! {Damage Inc. by Metallica comes up, and the crowd reacts accordingly, which, of course, means negatively...} [CD] Gentlemen, tonight you face the Cutting Edge, in a rematch from-- [Destruction, cutting Chad off rather abruptly] We didn't come out here to talk about our match with the Cutting Edge! We came out here to talk about this farce called a North American Tag Title match tonight! [Carnage] That's right! We're sick and tired of getting lost in the shuffle here in the AWI! We've been here ever since Union, blazing a trail of destruction through the tag ranks. We faced not two, but THREE teams at Knock around the Clock, and beat all three of them-- [CD, trying to interrupt] I would think Perfection would have something to say about that... [Carnage] Shut up little man, unless you wanna get thrown off this platform! Like I was saying, we beat THREE teams at Knock, and then, all three of them wanted rematches. So we beat them all again! [CD] Actually, by my count, it's one victory, one draw, and one no show. [Destruction] And why do you think Concrete Jungle no-showed their rematch with us? They were afraid of us, plain and simple! We kicked their tails so bad at Knock, they were a little woozy still when they signed for a remtach. When they came to and realized what they'd done, they put their tails between their legs and ran! [Carnage] And as for the Cutting Edge, we'll settle that little buisiness right here tonight. All of this points towards one thing! WE should be in that title match tonight, not the Toxic Twinkies and the Power Fools! What kind of joke was that, Daki, with the fish bowls? EVERY name in the Toxic Twinkie bowl should have read "Damage, Inc.", and that's that. We've been around longer than the Twinks, and we're tougher than the Twinks and the Fools put together. Plus, we've beaten the <censored> out of better teams than both of them. [Destruction] Bottom line, Chad, we'll take care of Cutting Edge, then we'll be in the house for the Title match. Don't be surprised if you see us again. {Damage Inc. leave to another round of boos, this time conspicuosly louder than the last, as we cut back to the announcing table...} [HR] Damage, Inc. seems to have quite a bit to say about the North American title... do you think they may be overlooking the Cutting Edge? [MH] Of course not! The Toxic Twins kick ass and take names... the Edge puts people in small packages. Now, who would YOU worry about? [HR] Depends on whether or not I want to walk away with a win... fans, we'll be back right after these messages! **************************************************************************** ******************************************************************************
