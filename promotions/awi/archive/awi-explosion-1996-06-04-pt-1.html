[AWI] Explosion 06/04/96, Pt. I (of II)

4 June 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
{The camera opens on Perry and Tyler Toxic being mobbed by reporters as they exit UNO Lakefront Arena in New Orleans.} [Reporter 1] Perry, how do you feel after winning the AWI North American Tag titles? [Perry] I got the crap kicked out of me by the Power Tools, then I got nailed with a chair by that jerk Destruction. What the hell do you think? My head hurts you jerk! [Reporter 2] Tyler, same question. [Tyler] I feel freakin' great! You jerks in the media have been calling us pretenders. Everyone said we got lucky with the Warbirds. <Censored>! We beat their asses and we can do it again. Everyone said there was no way in hell we could hang with the Honor Guard. Well, I saw the tape and it looked like we schooled those putzes pretty good even after that weasel Robbie Stevens carried our manager Tamara to the back. People said we were stomped bythe Power Tools the first time we met them and we'd get stomped again. Doest my eyes deceive me or do /WE/ have the belts? We've proved you jerks wrong from day one and that's why you can call us champs! (The camera turns back to the door as Tamara comes out and is surrounded by reporters) [Reporter 3] Tamara, how does it feel to be the hottest manager in the AWI? [Reporter 1] Tamara, who's next on the Toxic Twins' hit list? [Reporter 2] Tamara, care to answer the reports that you're in the hunt for a job in the USeW? [Tamara] Gentlemen... if you have any questions, you'll have to wait for a bit as I have an exclusive interview with SHOOT! video magazine tonight, but I will say this: The Power Tools found out that you don't one up me. They got a lame win over the Toxic Twins. Well, payback's a bitch and we one upped you when you got DQed tonight. For a title no less. And I am more than happy to say it was /MY/ pleasure. As for idiots like Robbie Stevens and the Honor Guard, the WarBirds and Damage, Inc, you punks are just that... PUNKS! The Toxic Twins are the new breed of AWI superstars and I manage them. We'll take on whoever the AWI Championship Committee sends our way. We don't care who we have to kill. We're the champs so get used to it. Now I'm outta here.
