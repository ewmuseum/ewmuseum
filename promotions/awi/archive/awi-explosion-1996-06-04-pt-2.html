[AWI] Explosion 06/04/96, Pt. II of II

4 June 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
{Open up to Kyle Esprit, in the locker room area...} [Kyle Esprit] OK, I've managed to track down one of the lone wolves of the AWI who I've heard may not be such a loner after all, The Mist Angel, who will be facing John Robertson tonight. I can tell that the Mist Angel is focussed on giving old John-boy a horriffic beatdown tonight, eh Misty? [Mist Angel] You have shown great powers of observation in noticing that I am prepared and focused for this match. This is a battle which is of monumental importance to the Mist Angel's quest for the top of the LightHeavyweight ranks in the AWI. John Robertson is one of the top contenders which the Mist Angel must defeat to regain the championship which was taken from me just a short time ago. [Kyle Esprit] So, Mist Angel, there's been talk, you know, rumours floating around the dressing rooms and stuff, that you've changed since we last saw you. That you've become more agressive, more...how shall we say...brutal in your methods. [Mist Angel] The Mist Angel is not one to give away the secrets of his preparations. Just know that the Mist Angel is aware of the degree of his opponent's talents. John Robertson is one of the top athletes here in the AWI, and like myself, if he comes to the ring in the right frame of mind, can defeat anyone. As it may be, my perceptions tell me that John Robertson's methods might not be so gentlemanly as we are accustomed. [Kyle Esprit] Well, Robertson's never been a gentleman, but what about those things everyone's saying about... [Mist Angel] (interrupting) Who is saying? [Kyle] Well...um...you know, THEM. People. Anyways, they've been telling me that you've been considered for membership in the Syndicate! So tell me...are you full-fledged member, or is Doctor Lupo just giving you a try-out this evening. [Mist Angel] I know of this Lupo of which you speak. The Mist Angel has never been in association with his Syndicate. But it is now time that I leave your questioning and return to my preparation. This is a most important match, and the Mist Angel will not be distracted from his goals. (as the Mist Angel leaves interview area) [Kyle] OK, well, I guess he wants to keep it a secret. ****************************************************************************** {Switch to Alan Kinsman, in the ring...} [AK] Our next contest is for one fall, with a ten minute time limit... our first competitor... from Sweetwater, Texas... accompanied to the ring by his manager, PAUL HUBERT... weighing in at 237 lbs... JOHN ROBERTSON!!! {"Chariots of Fire" plays over the loudspeakers, as the fans nearly explode out of their seats for the man from Sweetwater...} His opponent... hails from the Isle of Wight.... he stands 6'4".... weighs 237 lbs.... he is... THE MIST ANGEL!!!!! {"Dead Souls" by Nine Inch Nails starts up over the loudspeaker, as the crowd goes crazy, though not quite as loud as it was before...} [HR] John Robertson offers out a handshake to start this one... Mist Angel accepts... and it looks like we may actually get a fair wrestling match going here! [MH] Ya say that like it's a good thing... [PS] Not if President Chamberlin wants to collect any more money anyway! [HR] Collar-and-elbow tie-up to start... Mist Angel gains control... and whips John Robertson into the turnbuckles! He follows in... but Robertson catches him with a forearm! Robertson now out of the corner... manuevers around the Angel... the Mist Angel blocks a small package attempt... and counters with a roundhouse kick which floors Robertson! I think Esprit is right -- the Mist Angel IS more aggressive right now! [MH] Yeah... anybody that can go lick-for-lick with Street Fighting Kickboxing Joe is alright by my book! [HR] Interesting method of measurement you have there, Michael... Mist Angel to the top rope like a cat... FLYING BODYPRESS! Robertson's pinned... but he wastes no time in getting out! [MH] Well, just lying there on your back like that would be kinda stupid. [HR] Robertson with a forearm to regain control... and he slips on a toehold... waitaminute... that's Dr. Lupo... and Little Vincent! What are THEY doing here?!?! [PS] Ummm... scouting for competition? [HR] But there's not even a Light Heavyweight in the Syndicate! [PS] Maybe Little Vincent's planning on losing a LOT of weight? [HR] Do the words "Not bloody likely" mean anything to you? Mist Angel finally out of that toehold, but Robertson switches to an inverse leglock... John Robertson proving to be the consummate technician, as usual... [MH] And look... Lupo's even taking notes... maybe he's going to have Intensive Care come out and beat both of these guys into the ground... nope, gotta stop saying that... don't wanna get my hopes up... [HR] ANYWAY... Mist Angel squirms out, and manages to land a karate punch... double irish whip... and the Angel lands a picture perfect dropkick! Angel to the ropes... hard kneedrop! [PS] If he had soft knees the Angel would have more serious problems than dealing with John Robertson... [HR] Angel to the ropes again... BIG splash! The Mist Angel is HOT right now! John Robertson staggers up... the Angel with a karate kick... double irish whip... and a SECOND hard dropkick! John Robertson's reeling... the Mist Angel climbs to the top rope... Robertson's back on his feet... FLYING SAVAT KICK... ROBERTSON DUCKED!!! The Angel lands HARD on his head that time! [MH] See, you don't have to worry about that kinda stuff when you're hitting somebody with a steel chair! That's why I never did any of that flying stuff... [PS] I thought you never did it due to lack of ability... [MH] HEY!!! [HR] ANYWAY... John Robertson with a WRENCHING inverse leglock... Angel out... Robertson lifts him to his feet... Mist Angel blocks a DDT... The Angel manuevers around Robertson... no, Robertson reverses... no... the Angel behind him now... [PS] I'm getting dizzy here, y'all... [HR] Spins John Around... and the Mist Angel with an inverted atomic drop! Mist Angel lifts Robertson... whip into the coner... no, Robertson counters... belly-to-belly is blocked by the Angel... and the Angel manuevers around for a reverse rollup! 1.... but that's all, as Robertson quickly gets out... what is Lupo up to anyway? [MH] Hey, Dr. Lupo hasn't done a thing! You make him sound like some kinda criminal! [PS] If the prison stripes fit... [HR] ANYWAY.... Mist Angel with a karate punch to regain control... double irish whip... and a jumping spin kick! The Angel to the ropes again... FLYING SOMERSAULT BODYPRESS... INTO A ROBERTSON TILT-A-WIRL SLAM!!!! John Robertson with an INCREDIBLE manuever!!! Robertson picks the Angel up... and tosses him outside! [MH] That's okay... now Lupo can give the Angel the necessary advice to whip up on Robertson... [PS] So why is Little Vincent holding the Angel up like that? [HR] Hey, you're right! He's holding the Angel's arms back... the referee's too busy admonishing John Robertson... neither Robertson nor the ref sees this... Lupo's pointing his finger at the Angel and yelling at him... now he slpas the Angel... this is humiliating... LITTLE VINCENT WITH "THE END" SPINEBUSTER ON THE OUTSIDE FLOOR!!!! Robertson's got his back to it all, and the ref can't see past John!!! [MH] Oh, my. Isn't that a problem.... tsk tsk. [HR] You're making fun of it, but this is SERIOUS! Vincent rolls the Angel in... Robertson turns around... but Vincent and Lupo have already moved away! Robertson thinks that the Mist Angel just crawled back into the ring! [MH] Oh my. Isn't that a problem.... tsk tsk. [HR] How can you make fun of this?! Have you no compassion?!?! [PS] Probably not. [HR] ANYWAY.... Robertson's lifting him up for the Robertson trademark Cradle Suplex... Mist Angel with a standing scissors piledriver! What heart by the Angel! What intestinal fortitude!!! [MH] What extreme desire for a further beating... [HR] The Mist Angel is dragging Robertson into the corner... he's lifted him up... could he be?... HE'S GOING FOR THE CRADLE SUPERPLEX... Robertson blocks it! Robertson with a forearm... the Angel blocks it! Mist Angel with a karate punch... and a backfist... tries again... and again Robertson blocks it... and this time Robertson knocks him away with a forearm! [PS] Actually, I think all Robertson wanted to do was knock him away... the forearm was just a good way of doin' it... [HR] John Robertson whips the Angel in.... Angel with a leapfrog... John Robertson with another tilt-a-wirl! Robertson picks the Angel up... sets up for the cradle suplex... and there's the bell?!?! Robertson throws the Angel to the ground... everyone's confused... Hubert's on the apron... he's trying to figure out what's going on... let's get the official word from Alan Kinsman... [AK] Ladies and gentlemen... THE TIME LIMIT HAS EXPIRED... this contest has been ruled... a DRAW!!! [HR] Hubert's talking to the referee now... Mist Angel's back up... and he's looking for Lupo... BUT ROBERTSON HITS HIM WITH A CLOTHESLINE FROM BEHIND!!! I don't believe it! JOHN ROBERTSON JUST BLINDSIDED THE MIST ANGEL!!! [MH] God, it's a shame ol' Stan Jurgens couldn't see this now... [HR] John Robertson puts him in the scorpion deathlock... this is crazy... why's he doing this... the Mist Angel can't get out... Paul Hubert's pleading with him... and now Lupo and Little Vincent have entered the ring! Vincent's approaching Hubert... Hubert with a hasty retreat to the back... now Little Vincent with elbow drops to the Mist Angel, while Robertson's got him in that Scorpion... this is a set-up!!! [PS] Man, you're a quick one, aren't you?! [HR] Dr. Lupo is all in the Mist Angel's face... Mist Angel may be darn near unconscious... but HERE COMES PAUL HUBERT!!! AND HE'S BROUGHT THE WAR MACHINE AND JACK FURY WITH HIM!!! The Syndicate... NEWEST MEMBER AND ALL... head for the hills... but the damage has been done! [MH] Heck, that's the best time ta head for the hills... the person you want beat down's been beat down... you're gone before their friends can help 'em... great strategy if you ask me! [HR] You would think that's a good strategy... fans, let's get some words from some of the newest competitors in the AWI... hopefully we'll have this one sorted when we get back... ***************************************************************************** {Camera pans up a lush, green hillside to a huge castle sitting on a cliff overlooking the ocean. It is midday and the sun is glowing faintly behind a very dreary and cloud ridden day. Cue "Amazing Grace" on bagpipes. Camera switches to the inside of the castle with a roaring fireplace and in the background, a beautiful woman in black leather pants and a white blouse. She is holding a golden goblet in one hand and gently stroking a white kitten on the mantle with her other hand. The kitten is laying on it's back and is purring contently.} [Blackthorne] Ah've not been 'ere a whole month yet, and what do ah see before me lovely green eyes? This "Billion Dollar Babe" tramp comes trouncing to de ring like sum sort a cheap floozie! Aye lass! Do you not be hearing what de "Ringmaster" Dacia Blackthorne's saying to ya? In case ya missed it /BABE/, yer not living up to what we ladies 'ere in the AWI need to be looking like now are ya? Come on kitty! {takes the kitten in her arms and heads to a velvet couch. Now eyeing the kitten} Now what do YOU think about this Nessie? {Kitten looks up to see the camera for the first time and arches its back and hisses in surprise} Ahhhh yes! My thoughts exactly Nessie! Run along now. {puts kitten down and Nessie makes a beeline for the stairs}. Well now {sarcastically blunt} /BABE/, it looks to me like yer heading for a rude awakening now, eh? Ya see it's quite simple lass. Ya don't come into the AWI, or any division with me in it, and be demanding title shots and what not. It just doesn't work that way. First off girl, put some clothes on that frail body 'for ya catch ya death of cold! Second off, ya have to move up in the world before ya can look down. Since ya want to be moving up so fast, why don't cha just prance into the ring with likes of me and see if ya can't tease me into givin' ya a victory? When it comes to me lassie image is nothing, wrestling is everything! In other words girlie, ya mouth just wrote a check that ya big fat butt can't cash! It looks like the first floozie to get pricked by the Blackthorne is you /BABE/. Oh, and don't be backing down lass! If ya put enuff of that green stuff under the ring,it might cushion yer fall when ya get hit with the "Loch Ness Drop". Since ya got so much of that green stuff, jus place a bet and see if it's really worth somethin, eh? Ta ta luv. See ya in the ring! {Blackthorne gets up and heads to the fire place and pours the remaining contents of the goblet into the fire. As she walks toward the stairs she mutters...."Ah yes. Tis time to test the mettle of "Ringmaster" and see if she still has "Ye olde desire to win" still within her."} ****************************************************************************** (Ken Mischief standing in front of a tall mirror. Turns and faces the camera.) [KM] I have been looking at this body and saying that there is something missing from this picture. That is what I am here for. To make this picture right. [Camarman] What are you talking about. [KM] A belt is what I am looking for a championship belt. I know that I cannot expect to get one right away and I am prepared for the road ahead but I also know I have what it takes to take any man out here in the AWI. So whether you are friend or foe. I am not afraid of a little bit of trouble so if you have a championship belt, beware -- you are only another person who should be afraid of KEN MISCHIEF!! ****************************************************************************** [Camera pans over a rising sun over a hot and dry desert landscape with a mesa in the background. A black scorpion crawls in front of the camera and the camera pans back quickly!]("Saeta" by Miles Davis starts playing) [Voice] No no no! Do not be afraid! He won't hurt you, he is merely curious. Much like yourself I suppose. {Camera pans right to a black silver tipped, boot. Then camera moves up the leg to the waist.} Ah yes! Mexico! The land where everyday is a stuggle for some. For others, such as myself, it is home of the lovers. Those who love to live. {Cut to scene of a grand fiesta in the courtyard of an extravagant villa at sunset.} And those who live to fight!{Cut to scene of a handsome Mexican fighter in a bar fighting in a circle of cheering patrons and bartenders. Camera pans up to the head of the stranger only to have him turn his back and spread his arms as if to embrace the landscape.} [Voice] If only the entire world could see what I see everyday! If only my eyes could show you! Wouldn't you like to see it all, eh? Come closer friend. {Stranger turns to reveal his face with a playful smile on it.} There is much to be seen here. Allow me to introduce myself. I am Carlos Mendoza. "El Scorpion" if you will! {takes a bow}. I have much I'd love to show you, but only a few of you could ever see it. Alas that leaves me but one choice: To bring it to you. Or at least part of it. {Bends down and picks up the scorpion} Yes little diablo, it is time for the grand Diablo to leave you. Others deserve what you have so enjoyed so freely and for so long, eh? {Puts the scorpion on a rock and stands to face the camera.}Yes, AWI, you have been so lucky as to have The Scorpion, Carlos Mendoza, choose you as his next grand fiesta! Your lives and way of viewing wrestling will change forever! I will give you what you have so longingly deserved. Flair! Mystique! Romance! And above all a man to give you the thrill you have been missing! Yes my friends, your time is now. Until we meet again, Adios! {Camera pans away from the scene backwards to reveal a huge mesa with Mendoza on top of it. As camera fades to black, you hear Mendoza yell...} And a man to thrill your beautiful women, yes! ****************************************************************************** {Switch back to Heather, Paul, and Michael...} [HR] We're back, and the ring's finally been cleared... the Mist Angel was able to leave the ring under his own power, so he'll be alright, but he clearly will want a piece of John Robertson! [MH] Hey, Robertson's finally done some good, and now the Angel's going to want to start somethin' with 'im... [HR] ANYWAY... let's go to the ring for our main event... {Switch to Alan Kinsman, in the ring...} [AK] Ladies and gentlemen... the following attraction is a LONG HOT SUMMER qualifying match... it will be a NON-TITLE match. Our first competitor... hails from Usa, Japan... he stands 7'1"... and weighs 390 lbs... I give you... "THE WAR MACHINE" GREG GARDNER!!! {"War Pigs" by Faith No More blasts over the loudspeakers, and the crowd goes absolutely nuts for the BIG man hailing from Japan...} His opponent... is YOUR AWI NORTH AMERICAN CHAMPION!!! He hails from Seattle, Washington... stands 6'6"... 280 lbs.... I give you... "THE OLYMPIAN" GARY WHITE!!!!! {"The Olympic Theme" by the AWI Orchestra plays, and the crowd boos as loudly for the Olympian as it cheered for the War Machine... Gary White takes his gold medal and prances it around the ringside for all to see, then gets in the ring, holds it to the sky, and gives it to the referee to extreme crowd boos...} [HR] Both men lock up now... collar-and-elbow tieup... Gary White with an armbar... no, the War Machine counters it and lifts White up for a press slam... and DOWN goes the Olympian! Gardner now lifts White to his feet... but Gary White now with a half-nelson... hey... what's Jack Fury doing down there!?! With... is that a sledgehammer in his hand?! [PS] Maybe he's going to hammer out the problems he's had with both of these men... dum dum ding! [HR] The War Machine out of that hold... retaliates with a knee lift... and... Jack Fury's got the gold medal... He's throwing it to the ground... the Olympian with an armwringer... he doesn't know Jack's out there... BUT HE JUST HEARD THAT SLEDGEHAMMER HIT THE MEDAL!!! JACK FURY'S GOING MEDIEVAL ON GARY WHITE'S GOLD MEDAL!!! The Olympian drops the hold and heads outside... he just slapped Fury... and... he's got the Gold Medal Hold on Jack Fury!!!! [MH] Serves him right... Fury's just mad he couldn't get into the Olympics!!! [PS] Yeah, darnit, he'll just have to settle for all of his college awards. [HR] Jack Fury with a hip toss to escape... he retaliates with a forearm... but White with a DDT... and back into the Gold Medal Hold... the War Machine doesn't know what to do... Fury's quickly out again... and the referee's called for the bell!!! The referee brigade's come out of the back... Fury's taken over with a flurry of punches which drops the Olympian... the referee brigade's trying to hold them back... now the War Machine is in there, and he's holding back Jack Fury... let's get the official word from Alan Kinsman.... [AK] Ladies and gentlemen... "The Olympian" Gary White has been COUNTED OUT... the winner of the match... "THE WAR MACHINE" GREG GARDNER!!! [HR] The Olympian is INCENSED!!! He's jumping back at Fury, but Fury's getting the better of him with punches and kicks... the ref brigade and the War Machine are struggling to keep them apart... we're out of time, fans... but be here next week for more AWI Explosion!!!!
