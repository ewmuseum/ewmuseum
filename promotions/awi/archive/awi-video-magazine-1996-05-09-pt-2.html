[AWI] Video Magazine 05/09/96, Pt. II (of II)

9 May 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Chad Duncan stands before the AWI fans on the interview platform. The fans are going crazy in the background, waving Fury Flames, Danny Boy clovers and such....} [CD] Ladies and gentlemen, I have been asked here tonight by "The Olympian" Gary White, who tells me he has an important message for all the AWI. So, without further ado, here is your NEW AMERICAN STATES CHAMPION (the boos begin).... GARRRYYY WHIIIITE!!!! {The Olympic Fanfare plays as Gary White walks out with a large smirk. He turns to some fans and motions to his belt, and then his medal. This infuriates the fans even more, except for two guys waving mock gold medals and cheering the Olympian. He high fives these guys on the way to the platform. Finally the boos subside as White climbs the platform} [CD] OK, Mr. White, you told me you had something important to say to all the AWI.... [OGW] {grabbing the mike} ...yes, Chad, I do. You know, the Olympian has been on a roll. First I was an Olympic champ, then UWA TV champ, then AWI TV champ, and now...(pausing).. AWI North American champ. Just like I said, if I got a shot at this title, it would be mine. Justice, War Machine, Fury.... I bet you all got a sick feeling in your gut when the call came in saying The Awesome One was out and Gary White was in..... you knew you couldnt duck me any more." [CD] I wouldn't say you've been "ducked". Why, the War Machine defeated you not long ago in a match... [OGW] I have *ALWAYS* contended that was a FLUKE! {regaining composure} But I didnt come out here to relive the past... I am here to tell the world that I, The OLYMPIAN GARY WHITE, WILL REPRESENT THE UNITED STATES IN THE OLYMPICS IN ATLANTA!!!! {The crowd roars with boos} [CD] Mr. White, that is impossible. We both know that the tryouts for the US team were months ago, and I know for a fact you weren't there. [OGW] Since when does Gary White need a TRYOUT!?!? With my credentials all I need to do is just ask the committee - I'm a shoe in for the GOLD AND.... in preparation for this event, I need sparring partners for training purposes. So....I am, as of now, issuing an OPEN CONTRACT in which ANYONE can take on the OLYMPIAN, FOR THE TITLE, IN A *SUBMISSION* MATCH! {White pauses as the crowd reaction dies down} [OGW] Anybody who has the guts, just sign on the line. This is a great oppurtunity for wanna-be's out there - Bachman....War Machine.... and possibly most importantly, Jack Fury. {The crowd erupts in cheers at the mention of the faces} [OGW] Fury, you're a loser. I've watched you lose over and over since the beginning of the UWA. Always the challenger, never the champion. YOU HAVE THE GUTS TO COME OUT HERE AND INSULT ME AND MY MEDAL?!?! I hold you personally responsible for my teams loss at KOTC. YOU AND YOUR PATHETIC JACKHAMMER!!! YOU WANT TO FACE ME?? YOU GOT IT!! 'CAUSE JUST LIKE ALWAYS, YOU'LL END UP.... A LOSER!!! {Suddenly Thedocious Ferrocious Wilson's music starts up and the fans go wild. White has a puzzled expression on his face for a second, which soon turns to an expression of anger as he sees TFW coming down the aisle towards the podium...} {Wilson slaps the hands of the fans as he jogs down to the podium. He mounts the steps of the stage, walks up and gets right into Gary White's face.} [TFW] White, you want a challenger?! You want someone to take you on for that {points to North American Belt} title?! You got one! {White waits a moment, while the fans' cheers die down, before answering.} [OGW] {with a very annoyed look on his face} Why are you *wasting* my time, Wilson? I've already *devestated* you once in the ring. Even *you* can't be so much of a massochist that you want it to happen again! [TFW] White, I've been demanding a rematch with you since we first met. All I heard from you were excuses about how I wasn't good enough! Then, I kicked your ASS at Knock Around the Clock! Now, I want the chance to do it again, for a TITLE! [OGW] Wilson, you're a no-talent brawler. The only reason you won at KAtC was because that wasn't a *real* wrestling match! Even then, you still couldn't beat me without the help of Dr. Hyde! But I'm a man of my word. If you really want a shot at this title, I'll give you one! [TFW] {pointing his finger in White's face} You'd better *believe* I want that match! I'm gonna take you out, and I'm gonna take that belt! {Cheers from the crowd.} [OGW] {brushing TFW's finger away} Hold on a minute, Wilson. I said I'll give you a shot. Still, I'm a true sportsman {crowd boos}, and I wouldn't want to take advantage of a man who's obviously *not* in his right mind. So, let me spell this out for you. You will be *wrestling* me for the North American Title! This will be a *submission* match, so you actually have to have the *skill* to apply an actual wrestling *hold* on an opponent! Plus, you will be matching submission holds with the OLYMPION! So, just in case you were too dim-witted to realize the implications of all that before, I will give you this last chance to save face and back out. [TFW] White, I don't care what stipulations you put on this match! I want you IN THE RING, FOR THE TITLE, and I want it NOW! [OGW] {smirking} You'll get your match, don't worry. It won't be now, but you will get the chance, once again, to be HUMILLIATED by the Olympion! Also, if you pay real close attention this time, you may actually learn something about WRESTLING when I tie you in knots! {"The Olympic Fanfare" keys up, as Gary White leaves the podium, and we fade back into Alliyah and Chad at their newsdesk. Alliyah appears to be barely containing laughter.} [CD] {looking at her quizically} What, pray tell, do you find so funny?... [MAJ] {slightly giggling} The lengths men go through to release their testosterone. Chad, I do not mean this as a disrespect to "Theodocious Ferrocious" Wilson, but to accept a challenge from an Olympic class wrestler in a match of pure technical ability?... I believe the vernacular phrase would be "White's gonna tie him in knots"... [CD] Only time will tell about THAT particular match... however, TF Wilson did have the chance to face the man that did put Gary White down for the three count for the AWI TV Title, "Bulldog" Bryan Bachman... let's go to that match! ***************************************************************************** "Theodocious Ferrocious" Wilson vs. "Bulldog" Bryan Bachman Both men met in the ring, and shook hands to start the match, to quite a bit of crowd approval (except for a band of guys on the side that proceeded to chant "We Want Blood!!!"). Colar-and-elbow tie-up, and Theo took control with a bodyslam. He picked up the Bulldog, double-irish whipped them both, and caught an incredibly solid dropkick to the chin from the Bulldog. Bachman picked him up to his feet, but Wilson threw in a quick hip toss to take down Bachman. Wilson ran to the ropes as Bachman rose, but caught a headbutt for his troubles. As Bachman went to lift him again, Wilson threw on a quick armlock, but Bachman just as quickly elbowed out of it, and hit a shoulderbreaker to make the first chain of more than one move in a row for either man. Bachman, having decided that picking Wilson up is a bad idea, ran to the ropes, a came down with a hard elbow drop. Both men up, and a double irish whip saw Theo hitting his own running headbutt. He picked Bachman up, and landed a flurry of rights and lefts, then went for a double irish whip, which saw Bachman leap over for a sunset flip and a one count. As both men rose, Theo hit a solid forearm, then whipped them both in to nail a clothesline. TF then went to the ropes for an elbow drop of his own, but the Dog was too fast for him. Bachman tried to lift him for a shoulderbreaker, but Wilson quickly took control with a whip to the corner, then walked in for a non-Wilson Express forearm smash, then a double irish whip. Unfortunately, Wilson had not learned his lesson yet, as Bachman hit a HARD bodypress, but for no count. Bachman hit a quick forearm as both men rose, but Wilson managed to get him in "The Noose" sleeperhold.... ...and held it... ... and held it..... ...and held it..... ********************** Commercial Break ********************** ...and held it.... ....and finally Bachman escaped. Noone could have been as frustrated as TF Wilson was after that VERY long sleeperhold proved ineffective. Undaunted, though, Wilson whipped the Dog in for a HARD powerslam, and then, after a double irish whip,hit a sunset flip of his own for a 1 count. He lifted the Dog then whipped him in, but the Bulldog reversed it and put him on the top rope for his patented Bulldog off the top rope, but Wilson held him up, and then reversed it into a powerslam off the top rope. He held for the pin, but only got a two count and a bit more frustrated. Wilson then hit an elbow smash as both men rose, and managed to whip Bachman into the corner. Wilson backed up, and managed to mail him with the "Wilson Express" Running Forearm smash and covered, and this time got the three count and the AWI Television Championship. ****************************************************************************** {Switch back to Alliyah and Chad. Chad has quite a smirk on his face...} [CD] So you were saying what about Wilson losing? [MAJ] I said Bachman would win. You've managed to find my once-a-year mistake. However, that still does not mean Wilson will out-wrestle the Olympian -- take into account the the Wilson Express will serve no purpose whatsoever in that match. [CD] True, but the Noose will, as it did to help make T.F. Wilson our new TV Champ. And speaking of "new" things, let's take a look at some of the new faces we'll be seeing very shortly in the AWI! ****************************************************************************** {Fade in on Mickael Tziskova, the Philosopher, standing in mists. Next to him is a woman who looks to be between 19 and 24, wearing robes similar to his.} [Philospher] The true path to enlightenment is not a easy one. It is one strewn with sharp rocks, winding roads, and steep climbs. So many fall from it, and take the easier path, think it goes where they wish, but it does not. Only a few has the will and the inner peace to reach it. My prime student has that Will, she has that peace. She will be the instructer now, for the gentler of the the species...she will be the teacher of my word, and she will teach it well. Rejoyce, for my Student has arrived with me. Her name is Sarah Barker. ****************************************************************************** {The scene is a lavish estate with fountains and sculptures around a garden in full-bloom. A voiceover of Robin Leach begins...} [Robin Leach] "We're here today to bring a special edition of `Lifestyles' to the AWI. We're at the home of the self-professed `Billion Dollar Babe,' and as you can see here, the lovely tycoon has invited us to have a look at her lovely garden. There are many lovely bushes and trees here, many of which are rare plants from the four corners of the globe. As we look for our hostess, notice the beautiful..." {The camera stops and zooms in on a naked blonde women (with appropriate FCC areas distorted who looks up angrily.} [Woman] "Excuse me, but weren't you supposed to be here tomorrow for the interview? And get that camera out of the sun! I don't want a tan line, you know!" [Leach] "Oh, of course. Join us again next time on Lifestyles of the Rich and...ahem...Famous!" ***************************************************************************** (We are in a small, run down training center. There is a woman working out in the ring with a man who looks to be about 300 pounds. The woman picks him up into a rack, but sets him down almost instantly.) [WOMAN] Ah told ya ah could get you up, Jimmy! (The man, "Jimmy", notices the camera and nudges the woman.) [JIMMY] Hi there, I'm James Mason, and I'm here to introduce you to the hottest property in woman's wrestling today. I know, you've heard it all before...but you're talking to a woman who has done it all...cage matches, handicap matches, matches against men. She's taken the best wrestling could dish out, and she's stood tall. It is my pleasure to introduce you to the Southern Belle of e-wrestling...Dixie Hart! (The woman steps forward) [DIXIE] Thanks, Jimmy. Ah don't have much t' say that Jimmy ain't said already except that th' one thing ah ain't done yet is get a title. Ah've tried th' shortcuts, an' they ain't gotten me any closer. Ah've come t' bring th' honor of the South t' AWI. So look out...an' NEVER underestimate th' power of a woman! ****************************************************************************** Packed with peanuts, Snickers really satisfies.... Bud..... Bud..... Bud.... Wei..... Bud.... Bud.... Wei.... Ser...... I heard that when Grant Hill drinks Sprite, he isn't thirsty anymore!.... ****************************************************************************** {Fade back in to Alliyah and Chad at the newsdesk.} [CD] June 10th is D-Day for the AWI, folks, as we bring you the Long Hot Summer. What is the Long Hot Summer, you may ask? Well, for one lucky AWI competitor, it will be the longest night of action in his or her life! In the weeks leading up to The Long Hot Summer, 32 competitors will meet up in qualifying matches. These competitors will be randomly paired up to form tag teams -- these tag teams will then meet up, with the losers getting eliminated, and the winning 8 being reshuffled into two four-way matches. That'll leave us with two competitors, who will meet in a two-out-of-three falls match, with the stipulations for each fall being chosen by the competitors before the night even BEGINS! And what does the winner get? TWENTY-FIVE THOUSAND DOLLARS, and the title of "Ironman (or Woman) of the AWI"! PLUS, they'll get a shot at the AWI Champion at at the Union '96 Pay-Per-View! [MAJ] AWI competitors are lining up left and right for the oppurtunity. Here's some of the qualifying matches already signed... {Graphics of each competitor come up as she mentions them.} Steve the Insane will challenge Big Poppa. Jack Robertson wrestles with newcomer Jimmy Springheel. The Mist Angel does battle with fellow martial artist Street Fighting Kick Boxing Joe. The War Machine will compete against rival "Olympian" Gary White. Reverend Jeremiah James will wrestle former AWI Champion D. A. Bookthrower. Dr. Hyde will do battle with Jack Fury. "Theodocious Ferrocious" Wilson will challenge one of the largest men in the AWI, Heavy Duty of Megaton. Finally, Terry "The Tactician" Jones of the Cutting Edge will meet up with "Bulldog" Bryan Bachman. These matches, and more, will be leading up to our Long Hot Summer spectacular! [CD] We'll be giving you more information on The Long Hot Summer in the coming weeks, but we're almost to the end of our show, and it's time to get "Down and Dirty" with the AWI again! Today's topic is "What one wrestling manuever, besides your own, would you NOT want to be in the most?" What would you say to that, Alliyah? [MAJ] Well, the move that I have scouted out the most is the Flying Dropkick / Flying Splash combination of the WarBirds. However, given the fact that neither myself nor anyone I manage will ever be on the receiving portion of that manuever, I don't worry that much about it. [CD] Interesting statement... let's get Down and Dirty! ****************************************************************************** [Jade Tiger] That is easy! "The Condor" Eric King's Dive Bomb, it is executed so poorly that I'd be afraid Crystal Crow would be arrested for assisting in a suicide. * * * [JohnR] The Galacti-plex. * * * [JackR} John's cradle suplex. * * * ['Devestating' Dan Lea] Y'know, I've been doing some practicing with the Real Americans and I gotta say, that Stars and Stripes is *really* a double whammy! Power does press slams as well as I do, and even for a scrawny guy, Glory knows how to get elevation, before coming crashing down. * * * [Justice] The various and assorted suplexes off the top rope always concern me. Besides the normal suplex effect, the height achieved can be very destructive. So, the Mist Angel's cradle superplex would have to be my choice. * * * {Tyler and Perry Toxic} [Tyler] Which finishing manuever do I least want to be put in? Who comes up with this stuff? [Perry] Probably some dweeb that thinks the perfect date is him, Tamara and a bottle of Jack Daniels. [Tyler] I got no problem with that. [Perry] Me either, but that's beside the point. The point is finishers we don't want to be put in. Not that it matters, since we can kick out of anything, but I definately don't want to get caught in Heavy Duty's moonsault. [Tyler] What about B-52's Flying Splash? That dude's so fat he has his own zip code. [Perry] And Heavy Duty isn't the size of a Saturn? The point of the matter is, they have to hit the move, and those two lard asses can't get the job done. [Tyler] Face the facts lame-oids! It doesn't matter what anyone says they're afraid of. We know nobody wants to get hit with our finishers. That and winning are all that matters. [Perry] So get out of our faces! * * * [Condor] Well, unfortunately, I've already been subjected to a lot of the finishing moves I really feared when I first came to the AWI. I've taken Revi's Icepick and Iceplex many times... I've taken two Devastators from Dan Lea... I've been in a scorpion deathlock, courtesy John Robertson, and an Oriental Nerve hold, courtesy of the Crystal Crow... But right now, if I had to pick the hold I most fear... with my history of neck problems -- mostly because of Revi -- I'd have to go with D.A. Bookthrower's Instrument of Justice. The strain that hold puts on your back and neck... {Condor Shudders} I don't know how long I could last in that hold. * * * [Carnage and Destruction of Damage, Inc.] [Destruction] Oh for the love of... That's it, no more of these stupid questions. (Destruction reaches up and grabs the camera from the stunned camera man, and then shoves the man out of the way. The camera then swivels around to focus on Carnage.) [Destruction, from behind the camera, in his best mock announcer voice.] Hello, AWI fans, and welcome to the Damage Inc, show! Our guests today, as always, are Carnage and Destruction, Damage Inc! (sound of one person, namely Carnage, clapping). Ok, and here he is, Carnage of Damage Inc! Tell me Carnage, who's the biggest, baddest, most ass-kicking wrestler in the AWI today? [Carnage] Why, that would be you, Destruction. (Carnage reaches up and takes the camera from Destruction. The view swivels around until we see Destruction -- and behind him, the stunned camera man.) [Carnage, from behind the camera, in his own announcer voice] And now fans, here he is, the other half of Damage Inc, Destruction! Tell me, Destruction, who do /you/ think is the biggest, baddest, most ass-kicking wrestler in the AWI today? [Destruction] Well, it's you, Carnage, of course. (The camera swivels again, as Carnage holds it out and gets into the field of view himself. (The camera goes flying as Carnage heaves it away.
