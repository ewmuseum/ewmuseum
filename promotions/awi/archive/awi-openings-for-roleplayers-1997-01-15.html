AWI — Rules

Openings for Roleplayers Available

15 January 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hey all, My name is [name withheld], and I'm one of the co-GM's for the AWI. We have some openings available for wrestlers, and are currently accepting submissions. So, here's a short synopsis of what the AWI's about, and, if you're interested, an e-mail address to contact. The first question that seems to pop up with joining feds is "Is it role-played (booked) or simmed"? The AWI is, frankly, neither. It is, at it's basis, a role-playing game ala Champions, Vampire, or Dungeons & Dragons. The AWI uses the BODYSLAM! ruleset, which is based heavily upon M. David Clark's WWF RPG and the Coliseum RPG writeen by [name withheld] (one of the co-GM's). The final product is a dice-based system where you have /TOTAL/ control over your characters, but not only rewards, but requires solid role-playing. Nearly every aspect of wrestling is covered, from popularity to rankings to wrestling style to character quirks that actually make a mechanics difference. No, this is not a "Take five minutes to calculate numbers and create a character" fed. This does not use a computer simulator. Conversely, the GM's don't decide who wins or loses. You create the character, you submit the strategy, and the only things that decide who wins are your strengths and weaknesses, your opponent's strengths and weakness, your strategy, his strategy, and the weird quirks of ten-sided dice (well, of course, your popularity, managers, cheating and stuff all make a difference, but they fall under "your strategy" :).... ). Sound like a lot? Well, frankly, it is. As I said, this is a role-playing-game -- yes, winning matches and titles are cool, but what is stressed is /DEVELOPING AN ENTERTAINING CHARACTER/. Copycats of real wrestlers are not allowed. In fact, they aren't even looked at twice. Originality and creativity are what's required, though the mechanics for actually wrestling matches are equally in-depth. Everything affects everything else, and you have control of your character's destiny. Again, this does not come cheaply. If you've ever played any of the above-mentioned RPG's, you know that it takes a while to create a character and get him down pat. Expect spending a half an hour (at least) the first time you create a BODYSLAM! character. It's worth it later down the line. So, if I haven't managed to scare you off (I'm not trying to -- I just don't want to present something that my fed is not), e-mail me at [detail withheld] , and I'll send you a starter package to create a character. Thanks for your interest, and I hope you enjoy the AWI. -[name withheld] -AWI GM in charge of Miscellaneous. :) -------------------==== Posted via Deja News ====----------------------- http://www.dejanews.com/ Search, Read, Post to Usenet
