IWF: The New Beginning

23 May 2001
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
V^v^V^v^V^v^V^v^V^v^V^v^V^v^V^v^V^v^V^v^V IWF INTERNATIONAL WRESTLING FEDERATION "NOTHING NEAR THE ORDINARY" V^v^V^v^V^v^V^v^V^v^V^v^V^v^V^v^V^v^V^v^V [ INTRODUCTION ] Hey guys, it's me, [detail withheld] .. some of you that I've sent this to may know me pretty well, others may barely recognize my screename. As many of you know, I've been in the making of starting up a new REAL/FAKE Wrestler E-mail Fed .. a fed I plan on witholding with the best cards, the best booking, and fair judgement, no matter what. My basis for the fed is for everyone to keep a happy medium .. I know sometimes the fed will be at it's peak, and sometimes barely any participation .. I expect this, and just hope that you members will entrust my word that I will put forth 110% in this fed. I want everyone to be involved with the fed's storylines .. I always love seeing people putting up new angles with their own characters .. creativity is key, and just think of any ideas that come to your head, no matter how stupid they may be .. to me, it's better than putting your character in a situation where he rambles on about his opponent .. that's why I like to see more conversation, rather than a monologue .. To me, I know some of you have great RPlaying capabilities, and others are fairly new .. I want to see this diversity in the fed .. it's very similar to the WWF .. they have their seasoned veterans (Triple H, Stone Cold, Undertaker, etc. ) .. and then they have their up and coming talent ( The Hardys, Rhyno, Chris Jericho, Benoit, etc. ) .. To me, this always means I can work with the younger guys to reach a better capability, while the better guys can compete with their other opponents .. All I ask is for your help in the beginning .. This way we can get things rolling easily, because I've seen a lot of feds die out quickly right in the beginning because no one RPlayed .. I won't be strict on RPlaying each week .. People have regular schedules and lives, so that's fine .. Just an extra sense of ability put forth in the beginning would be most appreciated .. Other than all that, to any of you that don't want to use a Real character, e-mail me and tell me, that way I can send you an application .. I'll keep one handy for all the guys who want Fake characters .. Thanks for reading this, and please consider joining .. I think it will be a fun experience, and one you'll love to participate in .. ~[detail withheld] (:-) [ FEDERATION RULES ] 1. Try to keep the cussing on a minimum .. regular words like d*mn, a**, and hell are fine here and there .. an occasional b*tch .. just try not to get too carried away.. 2. Using other characters in your RPs is only permitted if you have made sure that it's ok to do so with the character's handler .. otherwise, please don't use any characters in your RPlays .. 3. Sending out any other e-mails to the fed besides your RPlays needs to be checked with me .. I don't like having all this spam mail in my mail box, so check with me in what you'd like to tell the fed, and I'll do so myself for you .. 4. Finally, just keep an overall good attitude between other RPlayers .. I've seen some people hold a grudge against another RPlayer because of a loss that they took against him / her and hated them for that .. Remember this is only a GAME .. don't take things personally .. [ ROLEPLAY SCORING ] The way that I grade a person's RPlaying is somewhat simple .. it revolves around basically a few key items .. ~ I look for a certain STORYLINE or ANGLE that the person is working with .. if he / she seems to move a storyline more and more ahead, I'll add points .. ~ Conversation and Interaction with people .. I like seeing this as opposed to a character talking about his opponent by himself .. keep things interesting and have dialogue .. ~ Be creative .. don't keep your character stuck at House Shows all week, take him to restaraunts, parties, anywhere .. just be creative .. ~Lastly, make the most of your character .. if you have the Sandman for instance, have him do and say the things he would do .. have him react to certain situations as he would .. sometimes I pretend as though I AM the character when I'm writing my RPlays .. be your character and have fun being him .. That's all I can really say about my grading .. I don't grade RPs individually, but rather throughout their weekly performance .. So keep these factors in mind, but HAVE FUN! [ CHAMPIONSHIP BELTS ] World Heavyweight Title PLAIN and SIMPLE .. if you hold this title, you ARE the best .. you are the top man in the federation, and along with holding this title, you must show that you are worthy of holding it .. stay on top of angles, keep your character involved, and don't slack .. or else the belt might end up in the hands of another person .. Intercontinental Title This belt is the STEPPING stone to the WORLD TITLE .. I like to consider this division as one of the most interesting because you have many of your soon to be Main Event wrestlers .. after holding this belt, and showing your capabilities of being a TRUE IWF Superstar, you will soon be bumped up to the BIG SPOTLIGHT .. Hardcore Title This is where the BIG BOYS play! The IWF Hardcore division I want to be one of extensive creativity.. this division always keeps me open for huge ideas and stipulation matches .. This is a division of all skill levels of RPers .. Cruiserweight Title Always a fun division to watch on TV, this is where I like to keep some of my newer RPlayers that are just getting into RPing .. the Cruiserweight title is one of prestige, but holds a great deal of competition amongst many of the lower level RPers .. this division is where the future superstars are located .. Tag Team Titles As always, these titles are rewarded to the best duo in the federation .. I've adopted a certain way to go about this division (Thanks to a good friend of mine: [detail withheld]) .. rather than having one person use two characters to win the titles.. the Titles must be held by two different people with one individual .. meaning Screename1 and Screename2 have to team up and win the titles together with one person, rather than Screename1 compete against Screename2 with two characters of their own going up against each other .. .. For right now, that's all the titles that the fed will be holding, and what I believe to be a sufficient amount .. Future titles to be brought out could be: Cruiserweight Tag Team Titles, Woman's Title, Television Title .. So just remember guys, this fed is gonna' great, and all I need is your help .. e-mail me at [detail withheld] if you'd like to join .. Thanks..
