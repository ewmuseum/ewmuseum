NLW's First Encounter With the Wrestling Press

26 April 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The scene fades up to backdrop which reads, "Next Level Wrestling," and an empty set of tables and chairs in front of the banner. There are reporters from many, if not all of the wrestling magazines on the market. The crowd sits in near silence as there is no movement anywhere near the table. Then, from a small PA in the room, the song "Blind" by Korn come on.] # ARE YOU READY!? # [Slowly, four men walk out, and all the camera flashes go off, seemingly simultaneously. Three of them take their seats, as the four, stands behind the podium. The music cuts as the man looks into the small crowd, ready to speak.] Man: Hello, and welcome to the Next Level of professional Wrestling. Let me introduce myself, and the three men by my sides. [He points to a black haired, young looking man.] Man: That is Josh Orloski, the boss, as he is fondly called by most of the staff,.. He in the operator and CEO of Next Level Wrestling. [He points to an older looking man, dressed in a very expensive looking suit.] Man: This man is none other then the man that helped NLW get it's wings flapping, this is Ron Copeland, the man who offered funding for this project. [He points to a rather large man, he also seems very young. Most likely still in his very early 20's.] Man: This, this is NLW first star,.. "The Cornerstone" Sean Steel! [Sean, seeming like the showboating type, stands up and takes a few bows, even though he is a complete unknown.] Man: And myself, I am Paddy Martins, long time advisor to Mr. Orloski, and head of personnel.. I'd like to give the podium to Mr. Orloski now, who has a few brief words to say. [Paddy sits down in an empty chair, as Josh Orloski stands up, and walks to the podium. He clears his throat, and bows his head for a second, seemly trying to collect his words.] JO: I think Paddy put it best when he said, "Welcome to the next level." The name of Next Level Wrestling comes from our product. Our wrestling is the next level, and we are planning on raising the bar for all the federations now, and those to come. We will take the action to the next level, and the show to the next level,... You really have no idea how good it feels to know that you are at the head of a project that easily could change the way we look at our sport forever. The feeling is even better because I know, Paddy knows, Sean knows, and Ron knows that it _is_ an attainable goal, and we _will_ do it. I've always wanted to be able to say these words, but because of lack of funding, I was never able to finish the things I started, but now, with Mr. Ron Copeland as a private financier, we can do it. No,.. We will do it. NLW _will_ take wrestling to the next level. We hope that you will be at the arenas, watching your TV, and spreading the word. [Josh Orloski sits down, as the people in attendance start a small applaud. Ron Copeland stands up, and he to walks slowly to the podium.] RC: Like Josh said, I am the financier of NLW, He is the gears that make things run, but I'm what keeps him oiled. [Small laughter pop :) ] RC: When Josh came to me with the idea for NLW, there was no way I could say no. I have been along time fan of wrestling, and this could be my way to help the business further itself. Josh has many good ideas and I'm sure he will bring in 2 dollars for every one he ends up spending. [Josh Orloski stands up to join him.] JO: The next level is about to begin, are you ready? [The scene fades out as Josh and Ron shake hands and smile towards all the cameras.] --- Josh - NLW President - FAQ in the works - Join up now! :)
