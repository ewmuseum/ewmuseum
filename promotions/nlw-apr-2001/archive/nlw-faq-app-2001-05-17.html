What is NLW? 1.1 Fantasy Explanation - NLW, which is the acronym for Next Level Wrestling is just that, the Next Level of pro-wrestling. It is not like most of them other federations out there in the fact that it is not a nationwide fed. Instead, NLW mainly caters to the wrestling needs of the East Coast, and sometimes, but rarely they will visit the Mid-West. NLW is different in one other aspect as well, they way they run the show. NLW is a very in-your-face, action based wrestling promotion. NLW is an "extreme" change from the sometimes tame action found it the bigger "global" feds. You will see violence here, and lots of it. That is, if you can handle it? 1.2 Real Explanation - NLW is an e-federation (a fantasty wrestling federation played by e-mail) which is open to just about anyone who wants to play. The matchs will most likely not be like the ones you see on WWF TV every Monday Night, but more like the old days of ECW (Extreme Championship Wrestling). The angles will be more adult orientated, and the in-ring action will be a pinch more violent, but I'm sure there is no complaints about that. :) NLW was formed because I wanted to run am e-fed in the style I would like to play one. If this style isn't for you, no hard feelings. Again, I have to ask.. Can you handle it? :) xxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxx 2. Who runs NLW? 2.1 Fantasy - NLW is run by a plethora of people. Josh Orloski is the CEO/President, Ron Copeland is the main financer, Paddy Martin is the head of talent, Buck Buck Shaw is the head of training, and Shawn Avery is the head of advertisement. All of these men's jobs are different, yet they all come together and most them work on backstage things together. They will be gone over more in their respective sections towards the end of the FAQ. 2.2 Real Life - NLW is run by, well, pretty much just me, Josh Orloski, but I'm sure I'll have the help and input of everyone in NLW. xxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxx 3. What do you have to do? 3.1 Flash (Roleplaying) - Well, this is usually a given, but in NLW you must flash (roleplay) at least once every two weeks. If possible, one a week would be even better. I will expect the guys who are higher in the card to at least RP once a week. 3.2 Strategies - A very important part of NLW is your strat for your match. Not writing one is the same as not training for a real life athletic event. What a strat needs is.. YOUR WRESTER: YOUR OPPONENT: YOUR ENTRANCE: (What will happen as you walk to the ring?) SPOTS: (Thinks that you would really like to see in your match, moves, ref bumps, manager stuff even stuff that the announcers and fans might say or do. This part should have the most beef to it.) ENDING: (What you will do if you win, lose, anything that would happen at the ending of your match.) 3.3 Angle Ideas - The reason most of us play this game is for the angles that we will get to play out, and see. The president can't make up every cool angle (although I am very good at it.) :). That is where you come in, if you want to feud with someone or team with someone all you gotta do is tell me. Almost anything you want to see is doable and I am VERY easy to get in touch with. So, hit me with your ideas everytime you get one, I'll use a good number of them. 3.4 Other - Ahh, other. Well, I will need help writing the matchs, so if you want to volunteer to help, well then, don't hold back. I'll take all the help I can get. Other than that, come to our IRC chat room, which is on the Starchat server, and the channel is #nextlevel Don't worry, someone will usually be be there. Hopefully. :) xxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxx 4. NLW Shows 4.1 NLW "Body Count" - This NLW's main show, very similar to WWF's RAW is WAR. Well, not that similar. NLW's Body Count is a lower grade version, there is no "Jumbotron." Instead, we have a big screen TV, and any fan that is caught throwing stuff at it will pay a rather large fine. :) The show usually runs from one hour to 90 minutes, and can be found on most of the East Coast syndicated TV. 4.2 NLW "Step up!" - This is NLW's "talking" show, where there will not be any wrestling (for now) and there will just be about 30 minutes of promos. 4.3 Supercards - Most feds have PPVs, well, we can't afford that yet so we will have supercards, usually every 2 months and once in a awhile, there will be 1 a month. A Supercard is where everything comes together. These are the big boys where everything is on the line. Champions are made, and broken, and this is where NLW will hit it's highest excitment. xxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxx 5. Names and Faces 5.1 Josh Orloski - Josh is one of the most creative and personable men in the wrestling business. He ran a rather successful promotion in Colorado area, but was forced to close the doors for money reasons. He is open to just about anything, and is not at all afraid to do something shocking. He will give his all for NLW, no matter what. He is a fair guy and gives everyone what they deserve. BASIC GIMMICK: Fair, Just, Dedicated. 5.2 Ron Copeland - Ron is easily described as the money behind Josh's ideas. He will rarely, if ever, be seen on camera in an acting role. He helps run the company, but he doesn't know enought about the business to do it himself. BASIC GIIMICK: Nothing, rarely on TV. 5.3 Paddy Martins - Paddy is Josh's long time friend and advisor. He is the "face" commentator for the action that takes place on "Body Count," and he is the head of talent. He controls most of the contracts, and problems with the roster. If you have a problem that has to be solved in the back, you talk to him BASIC GIMMICK: Wrestling Fan, Fair. 5.4 Shawn Avery - Shawn is the head of marketing, advertising and he is the "heel" commentator on "Body Count." He is your typical little brat rich kid, who likes what he likes and nothing else. BASIC GIMMICK: Jerk, Asshole, Kinda Moronic. 5.5 Buck Shaw - Buck is the head of the training department. If a new guys comes to NLW, and he is not up to standard, give him a few weeks with Buck, and he will usually come out a very polished worker. He will wrestle once in awhile on the shows, and will sometimes help with the commentary on the Supercards. BASIC GIMMICK: Skilled Wrestler, Former Star. 5.6 Chloe Clemens - [gulp.] Chloe is, well, beautiful. He is the head interviewer for NLW, but the only problem is that the wrestlers usually can't keep their eyes off he body long enough to look her in the eyes! Don't get her wrong though, he is all business when she does an interview. BASIC GIMMICK: Beautiful, Professional. xxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxx 6. Titles 6.1 NLW Atlantic Heavyweight Champion - This is _thee_ belt in the NLW, as of right now NLW is not big enough to have a second title so when you have this.. EVERYONE will be shooting to take you out. This belt will be defended quite often, and every defence will be important. 6.2 NLW Tag Team Titles - Like the heavyweight title, but for a tag team. :) xxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxx 7. Joining 7.1 Rules - Next Level Wrestling is NOT a place for egos. If you think you are so much better than everyone else maybe you are right, there's the door. Buh bye. - You have to participate every week, and try to keep your character fresh, if you just sit on your ass and do nothing, you're as good as gone. - Try not to break kayfabe, which means, please try to stay in character, unless we have a really cool shoot thing planned, then it's all good. :) - No useing anyone else's characters in your promos unless they helped write it, or they gave permission and yes, I will ask them. - Don't expect ass-kissing you get you anywhere, but we really don't forbid it. :) 7.2 The Application (finally.) - Player Information - Full Name: Your email address: E-wrestling experience: AIM Screen Name: IRC Screen Name: Will you write matches if needed?: - Character Information - Name: Height: Weight: Age: Gender: Hometown: Alignment: Theme Music: Appearance (be thorough and descriptive): Biography (be thorough and descriptive): Gimmick (be thorough and descriptive): Wrestling Style: Real Life Wrestler Whom You'd Compare Him/Her To: Finishing Move: Name: Description: WHEN FINISHED SEND TO [detail withheld] - That's it, a simple easy application.
