THE EWWF PROUDLY PRESENTS.. KINGDOM OF THE WICKED... LIVE AND ONLY ON PAY PER VIEW.. WEDNESDAY AUGUST 21ST... FROM THE US BANK ARENA... CINCINNATI OHIO.... EWWF CHAMPIONSHIP MATCH C-4 EXPLODING MATCH... SIMON SOLKOFF (C) V DESPAIR V SIMON UNDERWOOD [If you have been watching Surrender for the past two months, you understand the history that has engulfed these three men. Wednesday, August 21st, live on pay per view, their war will be settled, with a step beyond what you have already seen. C4-explosive placed around the ring...should make for the tasty Cincinnati style chili explosion.] UNITED STATES CHAMPIONSHIP MATCH... FOUR WAY ELIMINATION JIMMY SLADE (C) V RANDY LOVE V LARRY SPIKOWSKI V TREVOR SALADIN [Four Americans, one title, one chance to represent this country. I suggest they get busy.] SEX CHAMPIONSHIP MATCH *stipulations to be announced by Ms.Summers ASHLYN SUMMERS (C) V HOMELESS JOE BOXCAR [Current champion, meet former Champion] CRUSIERWEIGHT CHAMPIONSHIP MATCH.. TOMMY KAIN (C) V ASH V DAVID RHEAUME [Tommy Kain has had the Crusierweight championship since he killed Grant Morrison for it. He hasnt' defended it. His Hulk Hogan days are OVER Nick Demola v Junya Kontani. *stipulations to be announced* [Blood, sweat and tears,and some barbed wire has all been used. Who knows what's next?] Rune Tao& Liam Zen v Riot Squad [Tao and Zen have wreak havoc. No one wreaks havoc like Syko and Andrew Krumm. Kingdom of the Wicked, havoc holds no boundaries.] DEBUT MATCH Kyle Archer v Jerry Gordon v Tian Jumming. [Three guys, one chance to make a good impression. Blake and Mitchell WILL be watching] Debut match #2... Albert Miller v Mr.Badman [These two wrestled in the six man tag that closed the show. Now they wrestle each other. I smell money. A-train v Da Bad Guy] TAG TEAM BATTLE ROYAL... WINNERS TO BE THE NEW TAG TEAM CHAMPIONS... PARTICPANTS THUS FAR... JACK PAINE/DALLAS WINSTON 'HAZMAT AND RAGNAROK JERSEY CITY STREET MACHINE (ROCKY CAMARELLI AND SAMMY PAULISON) RYAN SANDERS/STEPHEN T.DUNSK IAN BLADE & DANIEL GRAHMN RIOT SQUAD (PULLING DOUBLE DUTY) RUNE TAO&LIAM ZEN(DOUBLE DUTY) OTHER ENTRANTS ARE ACCEPTED!!
