EWWF: a Syko roleplay

13 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
This one was hastily titled... "Are you stupid or somethin'?" Enjoy, and C&C the shit out of it. And if you don't understand all of it.... go watch this past weeks EWWF: Surrender. Good shit right there. ~Syko ____ [Stupid is as Rune Tao does. Fade up like a box of chocolates on a chilly
Chicago morning.... sitting out on the balcony of his 23rd floor hotel room,
Syko is enjoying a cup of coffee while sitting in a lounge chair, already fully dressed and ready so early in the morning..... ]
Syko: It's cold out..... just like those fleeting moments when you know something has changed your life.... but what's done is done.
[Flashback.] Geoff Jordan: RUNE TAO ADVANCES!! RUNE TAO FUCKING ADVANCES!! I CAN'T BELIEVE IT!! SYKO HAD THE DAMN THING WON!! THREE TIMES HE HAD THE DAMN MATCH
WON!!! THREE TIMES! LIAM ZEN PULLING THE REFEREE OUT WHEN THE DAMN MATCH
WOULD HAVE BEEN WON!! THEN, THOSE BASTARDS RYAN SANDERS AND TYLER DARDEN IN
THE HOSPITAL ROOM!! GODDAMNIT SYKO WAS ROBBED!! SYKO WAS FUCKING ROBBED!!!
[Ahem.] And what is yet to be done will be seen soon enough. Yes, Tao... you *fingerquote* beat me. [More tasty goodness from surrender.] [Tao grabs Syko by the arm, irish whip, reversed by Syko with the good arm,
Tao goes crashing into the steel steps. Syko grabs the chair from a ringside person and smashes it into the midsection of Tao. Tao stumbles out, Syko waits...]
*CRACK* GJ: WHAT A CHAIR SHOT BY SYKO!!! *CRACK* *CRACK* *CRACK* *CRACK* *CRACK* GJ: SYKO, SUMMONING UP WHATEVER HE HAS LEFT, THROUGH THE BLOOD IN HIS EYES,
THE PAIN IN HIS LEFT ARM, JUST SMASHED THE CHAIR INTO RUNE TAO'S FACE AND
THE FANS HERE IN MINNEAPOLIS ABSOLUTELY LOVE IT!!! [Flash] [Syko brings Tao to his feet, delivers another Syko-kick to the jaw of
Tao....] GJ: SYKO UP TO HIS FEET..... *CROWD POP...FLASH PHOTOGRAPHY* CJ: SYKO'S DOING THAT STUPID RAISIN THE ROOF THING!! GJ: 450 SPLASH ON TAO!! [Flash again, quick now!] [Miller comes through the crowd and places a chair in front of Tao's face...] GJ: OH GOD.... CJ: WHAT? GJ; SYKO TALKED EARLIER ABOUT THIS MOVE..... CJ: No..it's physically impossible! [Syko hulks up, Tao starts to move and Miller gets on the apron and bashes
Tao in the face with the chair to knock him back into the corner...]
GJ: Syko is going to attempt to go corner to corner, the long way, across the ring, AB CD..... He's going A to D folks..... CJ: It can't be done!! [Syko "hulks up" in front of the crowd and leaps to the top rope...] GJ: YOU CAN'T MAKE IT SYKO! DON'T DO IT! IT'S NOT WORTH IT!! FIND ANOTHER WAY!! [Syko raises the roof some more...] [BULBS FLASHIN... PEOPLE POOPIN.. OOOH'IN AND AAAHH'IN SYKO HIT IT POP] GJ: SYKO HIT IT!! HE WENT 'COAST TO COAST' ON RUNE TAO!!!!! [Now back to our show.] Syko: Yeah... funny how that is Tao.... You 'handing' it to me. And you're absolutely right, how getting dropped on your head changes things..... you would know, of course...... what with your selective memory.... Let me put it in simplest terms so you can understand. It's obvious you haven't picked up the hint from that interview I did with Geoff Jordan the other day. It took you... your tag team partner.... an attempt on the life of my best friend, and the grace of Adam Mitchell to set it all up.... just for you to get another cheapshot in on me... and pick up yet another tainted 1....
2.... 3. And I bet you're proud of yourself too, you wormy little fag. Laughing all the way to the bank, and moving on in the tournament..... one step closer to a belt you don't have the right to touch. Man, if I were you, I'd be on cloud nine right now. Of course, I'd also be a horrible wrestler, and less than a turd of a man to boot. But hey... I'm sure you can live with that.
After all, you can't change who you are. Yeah, Tao, life is good ain't it? Knowing that you made a name for yourself, beating me the only way you could. Gee, the whole wrestling world should get down on their hands and knees and kiss your masked ass.
[Feel the love? Well, that ain't love, that's sarcasm like a motha-] Be proud. More proud that you are when you march in those Rainbow Coalition parades. More proud than you are when Zen bends you over before bed.... And enjoy this moment. Take it in.... smell success. Smells nice, huh? Take another whiff.
It may be the last time you get to enjoy it like this, Rune. I don't know who you really are. The mask I've hardly seen, the voice I've long forgotten, just like everyone else has.... you could be anyone.... but it's easy to tell you've been around and you know your shit....
And you know you can never really 'beat me'. Sure, a win is a win is a win for you. Just something to drive your stock up.... so you can get just that much closer to Mitchell's right nut. YOu twist the facts to try and make it all seem right....
'He's just a loser anyway... he'll never amount to anything. His partner never did.' And you'd know, right? You're such a fuckin' expert, why don't you go and do commentary? Then you can tell your little jokes about corpses and how it correlates to the careers of the wrestlers that you know could take you out on your best day. I've said it already... it's that fear... that jealousy.... You wanna become a SOMEBODY, just one more time....
So go ahead.... fight in the tournament. Hell, I want you to go on and win, lest my friend's death be in vain.... But then, you wouldn't care about that. You might even lose out of spite. Or maybe by some likely chance you'll be beaten cleanly.... like you should have been.... and knocked all the way back down the ladder, into obscurity, where noone will know you, and noone will care.
But at least you'll always have your biggest fan. Yourself. Are you still laughing? You won't be. Tao.... you can have the title. They come, they go. You can have the fame and the paycheck to go with it. It's fleeting. Nothing in this world lasts forever.... not even my hatred for you.... because after Surrender.... I won't have any reason to. Because somehow, Tao... my yin is gonna catch up with your yang... and when it does, you'll wish you've never had the opportunity to hear me preach it to you like gospel..... the simple truth..... that when you fuck with Syko.... Resistance... Is.... Futile. And you WILL be taken care of.
[Syko sips at his coffee, which grows colder by the minute. Chicago's a cold place.] But this week, I have a match, against one Derek Irvin... another man who's been wronged by the bloodthirsty maniacs that reside in this business.
I feel for you, brother. I really do. Granted, I don't know much about you or your situation, but I know for a fact that if Xian was burned the way you describe what happened to your girl, I'd be just as filled with rage as you.
And it's similar, what's happened to our lives since, so to speak.... You've come to seek your retribution for it all.... to right the wrongs done to you. You want to hear him scream in pain like she did, you want him to feel the agony of it all, jus tlike she did....
And so do I. Revenge is a dish best served cold, bringing out the bittersweet taste of it all. But I don't have quarrel with you. No reason to fight you, besides the normal 40 acres an a mule that comes along with being a professional wrestler.... with a dangerous man like you across the ring from me, perhaps it's a message fromMitchell.... he wants you to hurt me real bad before I face Despair, in the slight bit of hope that one of you two does me in. Or perhaps... jsut another one of his schemes, set up while throwing darts at my picture on the back of his door..... having his men wait for the right moment to bum rush the ring and not only inflict their pain upon me, but you as well.
[Syko lets out a heavy sigh.] So let's just dispense all the bullshit. You need to win to show your worth to the EWWF. I need to win just to survive another week, so I can throw it all back in the faces of those who would spit on the grave of me and those I cared for.
As fucked up as things are.... the way it's all come crashing down around me.... back to reality.... all iit does, is make me want it that much more.
I my not seem like the same old Syko anymore... and truth be told, I'm not. But I'm still just as good. And I'm still just as ready for any and every obstacle that'll be put in my part from now until I decide to leave this business, on my terms....
Just remember, folks.... You may have killed Andrew.... but I'm still alive and kickin'. And baby.... I ain't goin' nowhere anytime soon. Believe it. [Fade to cold coffee and stale donuts. Gotta love those continental breakfasts.]
