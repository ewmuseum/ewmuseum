[MSWA] Championship Wrestling is on the air

30 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The November 30th edition of Championship Wrestling is on the air and available for viewing.. It was a wild show, "wild" in the sense that it had the return of Wildlive with Wilde and Rebecca Tanke and their guests the KWF's heavyweight Champion Tommy Rogue as MSWA newcomer/IWA announcer Cito Conarri. As well as some wild twists and turns including the arrival of the CAL World Heavyweight Champion Thomas
"Think Tank" Turner and the unveiling of his plans for the MSWA if his attempt to create a new umbrella group called National Pro Wrestling comes to fruitition. The card also saw the return of "Smooth
Criminal" Brandon Reeves, "the Main Event" Chris Stratford defend his
Smokey Mountain Heritage Title against "Quiksilver" Quinn Tate and
Mid-South Heavyweight Champion Psycho Steve defend against everybody's favorite hillbilly Cledus T. Yokel IX, in the main event.
