[CAL] Reindeer Games II: Official PPV Lineup

14 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE MEMPHIS, TN The Coalition of Affiliated Leagues [CAL] In Association with National Pro Wrestling [NATPW] has announced the official lineup for the CAL's annual December PPV Spectacular. Reindeer Games II: A New Years Revolution will be held on Tuesday Night December 31st at the American Airlines Center in Dallas, TX. For more information visit. http://www.mswawrestling.com/rg2002 Ten matches, one war : the Coalition of Affiliated Leagues versus National Pro Wrestling. Each match is worth one "point". The team - either NATPW or the CAL - with the most points at the end of the card wins total control of the CAL name brand... and the future of wrestling... MAIN EVENT CAL WORLD HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPIONSHIP Psycho Steve [MSWA-CAL] versus Think Tank Turner © [NPW-NATPW] CAL WORLD TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP The Rock Brothers [NPW-CAL] v. The Axis [Cross/Loki] [IWA-NATPW] IWA TITLE MATCH THREE-WAY DANCE Heidi [IWA-NEUTRAL] v. Mr. Dude [IWA-CAL] v. Daeriq Damien [IWA-NATPW] *Should Heidi win, she will be allowed to choose which side - NATPW or the CAL - will get her "point" for the total end tally of the night. [MID-SOUTH HEAVYWEIGHT TITLE MATCH] LAST MAN STANDING Mace [MSWA-CAL] v. Nighthawk [MSWA-NATPW] [KWF TITLE MATCH] STANDARD MATCH Tommy Rogue [KWF-CAL] v. Jaime McNulty [KWF-NATPW] [NPW TITLE MATCH] STANDARD MATCH Timmy Fantastico [KWF-CAL] v. Rob Brimstone [NPW-NATPW] ["CHEUNG-CARNAGE II"] STANDARD MATCH Sam Carnage [CAL] v. Lawrence Cheung [NATPW] [NO. 1 CONTENDERS MATCH - CAL WORLD TITLE] FOUR CORNERS MATCH Calvin Astroth [KWF-CAL] v. Ric Owens [NPW-CAL] v. Chris Stratford [MSWA-NATPW] v. Kai Scott [IWA-NATPW] [NO. 1 CONTENDERS MATCH - CAL WORLD TAG TEAM TITLE] ELIMINATION MATCH Ron Havok, Xavier [NPW-NATPW] v. Alternative 2.0 [IWA-NATPW] v. Violence Inc. [KWF-CAL] v. Quinn Tate & Cito Connarri [MSWA-CAL] [CAL-NATPW GAUNTLET ELIMINATION MATCH] [CAL] Apathy, Gazz Maybury [IWA], Alex Constantine, Cledus T. Yokel IX [MSWA], Johnny Thunder, Kid Xtreme [KWF], Danny Vicious, E-Normus Norman [NPW] v. [NATPW] Neo, Jonathan Laslow [IWA], Johnny Five, Chris Wright [MSWA], Marty Sugar, Dominic Demonstone [KWF] Brutal Lee Frank, Keith Edwards [NPW] Two teams of eight. It starts with two people in the ring. At three minute intervals, someone new is allowed down to the ring, alternating between CAL and NATPW. In the end, the team who gets all 8 people eliminated first loses. Eliminations by pinfall, submission or KO. --------------------------------- Joe B.
