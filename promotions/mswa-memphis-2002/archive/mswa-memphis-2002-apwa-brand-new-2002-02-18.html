APWA

Brand New E-fed opening out at http://theapwa.tripod.com INTRODUCTION My name is Joe B. and I have been involved in the hobby of ewrestling for about three years now. My roots in the hobby go even farther then that, to almost 15 years ago when I used to play in the old IWA, play by mail fed that you could find inside wrestling magazines.
As far as ewrestling goes, I was extremely lucky in starting out, because the first fed I was ever accepted in was also the greatest fed I was ever in. It was called the NAWA, and run by the best President and Vice President you could wish to have. Unfortunatley the burden of handling a roster of almost 30 wrestlers, holding two cards a week (never more then half a day late) and a pay per view a month, on top of juggling a family and a career and a life proved to be too much. And after 16 months the North American Wrestling Alliance closed it's doors.
A few months after the NAWA closed, the WWA opened it's doors. Another great fed, with excellent guys that was run by and filled with former handlers from the NAWA. Unfortunatley after 6 months the same fate befell it. The NAWA and
WWA were both a place were people who enjoyed creative writing and wrestling were able to bring both of those into one great hobby.
About eight of the former NAWA/WWA handlers surfaced in the various fed's of the National Wrestling Council, for the next few months. Despite having an undefeated record during my tenure there, I did not have any fun, because the dominant rp style in the NWC was so much different then what I had grown accostomed to in the NAWA/WWA. It was never about the Win's and the Losses to me, it was about developing a great ewrestling character and telling a good story.
So that being said, I announce the creation of the APWA APWA Mission Statement If you have participated in ewrestling for any length of time , then you know how hard it is to find a good fed that actually stays open more then a month. A lot of people in this hobby, dive right into running a fed and feel like they need to make it the best , most professional federation on the internet. This usually causes a huge constraint on time and clashes with life in the real world..
Many of us in the hobby, forget that ewrestling is supposed to be about having fun and meeting and interacting with other people, who have the same enjoyment in wrestling and writing as you do.
Hopefully the APWA will be about nothing but having fun. And not about making our hobby seem more of a sure, then a fun distraction.
The APWA is not going to be one of those feds that expects more from you then you can and/or are willing to give. We are a casual fed , which means your participation is based around your time schedule. As long as you remain active in some way , we will continue to include you in everyday events.
This fed is catered around those who feel ewrestling is meant to be a haven for fun , not total and complete seriousness.
How the "APWA" will operate The APWA will be for the most part a roleplay board fed e-fed. Because having a board, makes it easier for everyone to have access to everyones rp's and not causing clutter and confusion through mass emailings.
The "APWA" is a booked fed. The outcomes of matches will be determined by angles/storylines with roleplays playing a smaller role. Roleplays will serve more as a way to set up storylines and angles and develop characters. That does not mean that roleplays are irrelevent, that just means that one roleplay a week or 5 roleplays a week, does not matter. Quality over Quanity. It is a term that all efeds use, but va=ery few actually mean. The APWA does mean that.
The "APWA" will have an open door policy to everyone involved. All handlers will have alot creative control over your characther, and the storylines and angles they are involved in. Your suggestions will be taken in and used to the fullest possible extent. You must take into consideration however , that the other person(s) involved in your feud may have ideas as well and even outsiders wanting to get involved , but rest assure we will do everything we can to listen and use your suggestions and requests.
We will feature one weekly card. These cards will be either full commentary OR in detailed summary. This will depend on my time limitations. I will make no promises.
