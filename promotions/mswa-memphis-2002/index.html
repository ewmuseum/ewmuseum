Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

A Memphis promotion opened in February 2002 under the initials APWA, its own introduction describing a territory-style card called Memphis Championship Wrestling. Its earliest posts advertised open applications and a growing roster, with a first produced show following within a month.

By May the promotion joined the Coalition of Affiliated Leagues, a multi-federation umbrella, and by summer it was posting under a new name, the Mid-South Wrestling Association. Its Gravity Division tournament that August built a division around high-flying, style-specific competition rather than a weight class.

Through the rest of 2002 its weekly Championship Wrestling broadcasts carried a Mid-South Heavyweight Title, a Smokey Mountain Heritage Title and crossover matches against the coalition's other member federations, closing the year with a multi-federation pay-per-view lineup against a rival coalition.
