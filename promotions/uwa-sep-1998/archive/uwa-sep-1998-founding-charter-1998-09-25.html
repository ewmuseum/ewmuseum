Now I've ran maybe 10 feds, most of them folding ina week, but with your help I would like to keep the UWA long past a week, maybe even a year. So if your going to join, please devote a little online time to this. Thanks. Rules 1: No Tossing or complaining. I don't take any BS. 2: Rp Limit is 40 lines. No less. I judge the rp's by 90% Quality and 10% Quanity 3: Real and Fake Wrestlers only. You can have 4 wrestlers and unlimited mangers. 4: Can't use other people's wrestlers except if you have their permission. 5: No threatning, and don't take something seriously, it's roleplayng. Championshsip Belts World Championship Pride and joy of the UWA. A Lightweight and Heavyweight can win this. This is the big-time baby. the top of the Ladder, the jewel of all jewels. It will only be defended twice a month. At a Regular card and Pay-Per-View. So get ready to roleplay your ass and off and win this. Heavyweight Championship The best belt a Heavyweight can get if he's not going for the World. Will sometimes be in the main event, but a great belt for a great roleplayer. Will be defended three times a month. At the Pay-Per-Viw, and two Weekly Cards. UnCeNsoRed Championship The most EXtreme belt in the UWA. Will be defended every two weeks in the most extreme ways. If there is an Extreme Wrestler out there, you need this belt. The best extreme belt in Wrestling. Television Championship Think your a performer? Well then this is your belt. It will be defended every three events. Be ready to become the best performer in the UWA. Lightweight Title For the Lightweight out there. The top rung for them. This beatiful title will be defended two times a month just like the World. A good title for the Lightweights. Weekly cards UnCeNsoRed Tuesday Night You want to see something Uncensored? Well you came to the right place. the main card of the UWA, and everyone loves it. In every major arena and coliseum across the US and the globe. So from 8:00 till 11:00 Eastern time, get ready to rumble. Friday Night Fury Pop open and beer and get your favorite Lazy Chair beause you'll wanne see what happens on FNF. With all the major superstars in the UWA on FNF, there's sure to be a brawl.
