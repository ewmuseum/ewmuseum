Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Another Universal Wrestling Alliance announced itself on 25 September 1998 with a complete founding charter rather than a bare recruiting post — belts, rules, a weekly schedule and a full year of planned pay-per-views, all before a single card is known to have run.

Its charter named five championships: a World title open to any weight class, a Heavyweight title, an “UnCeNsoRed” title for the promotion's most extreme matches, a Television title, and a Lightweight title, each with its own defense schedule laid out in advance.

Two weekly cards were planned, “UnCeNsoRed Tuesday Night” and “Friday Night Fury,” alongside a full year of named pay-per-views running from an October “Judgement Night” through a September “School's In '98,” by way of “New Year's Stable Wars,” “WrestleCrazia '98,” and “Hell's Cell.”

Its roster policy welcomed both real and invented wrestlers, capped at four per handler with unlimited managers, and its charter closed with a full staff list under its own president, a vice president, and a webpage designer.

No card, title change, or wrestler is attested beyond the charter itself. A further recruitment thread under the same name surfaced within the following month at a different address, but no show, roster, or result survives from either.
