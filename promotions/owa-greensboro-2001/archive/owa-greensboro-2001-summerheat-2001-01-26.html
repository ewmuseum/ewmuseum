OWA Last Summerheat card...a new era..a new site...check us out!!!

January 26, 2001, 12:00:00 AM
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(A mass of fireworks go off, as the camera shot scans the crowd, and picks out signs such as "Pac man tried to sell me crack!", "I like cheese", "Buffy
World Order" as well as a mass of AUSTIN 3:16, bWo and DX signs. The camera eventually settles on our announce team, Jerry "The King" Lawler and Good old JR.)
JR: Welcome everyone to the first Summerheat of the millennium, right here in the Anaheim pond, where over 15,000 crazed OWA fans jam pack the arena!
Hello everyone, I'm Jim Ross, and next to me here is Jerry "The King" Lawler. King you still trying to calm down after Thursday night? King: It was incredible! Trish's puppies!!!!!! JR: Well Trish is here tonight, hopefully fully clothed after what happened on Thursday. King: Are you kidding?? I hope we see more PUPPIES!!!!!! JR: Well lets forget about that for now please King, and focus on the incredible lineup we have for tonight. We will see four titles on the line tonight, including the much awaited match up between the new OWA champion
HHH and the worlds largest athlete, and apparently Shane McMahon's
"business" partner, the big show. King: Boy that one will be incredible. Vince and Shane will both be at ringside!!! JR: Well all hell is going to break loose in that one. As it will in our tag title match. King: Can you imagine how much beer the Acolytes have consumed since they won the titles on Thursday??? I doubt they'll even be able to stand up straight, let alone fight!!!
JR: Also we are awaiting to find out just who Commissioner Fusions opponent tonight is. Rumors have been circulating around the back, and all signs point to Tha Kid.
King: But what about SummerZ??? He laid out a challenge last week, and
Fusion never answered it!! Vince may give SummerZ the shot!!!
JR: Well whatever happens tonight, it sure as hell won't bring back the rattlesnake. ("No Chance" hits, as a chorus of boo's fills the pond. The boo's soon turn to cheers as Shane McMahon emerges from the top of the ramp, mic in hand. He slowly walks down into the ring, and begins to speak.)
Shane: Hello everyone, may I be the first to formally announce myself, Shane
McMahon, as the official co-owner of the company. But since the handing over of power last week, I was slightly confused. For you see what both me and my father discovered is that there is apparently a third party in the ownership of the OWA. Now I'm thoroughly vexed by this, as former owners Alex Fowlis and SummerZ both assured me there was only two parties involved in this process. Now I guess we'll just have to wait and see who this third party is, but lets just hope they know there role, and understand who the true owner of the company is.
("No Chance" hits again, and Vince steps out from the ramp to the disdain of the crowd. He makes his way down into the ring to confront his son Shame.)
Vince: Hello Shane. Now son, after Thursday night I would have thought you'd have learnt your lesson. You saw what I did to Austin. You saw the power I hold in the OWA. So why don't you just give it up right now and hand your share of the company over to me.
Shane: You know what dad? I did see what you did to Austin. And I did something to Austin as well.
(Vince gulps as "Disturbed" hits, and the rattlesnake receives an enormous pop from the capacity crowd. He walks down into the ring, flips Vince the bird, then stands behind Shane.)
Vince: What the hell are you doing here??? You're fired you son of a bitch!!! Shane: Wrong dad...cos you see after you fired Austin, I thought "what the hell is he doing, firing the biggest name in the business today?" and you know what I did then dad? I rehired Austin to a new five year contract with the OWA!!!
(The crowd go insane as Vince's face floods with anger.) Vince: YOU STUPID LITTLE BRAT! (Vince tries to get to Shane, but is stopped by Austin.) Austin: Hold up there Vince. You know what, I'm glad ya fired me. Because with my new contract...there was a special stipulation. It guaranteed me at least one title shot.
(The crowd go wild as Vince gulps again.) Austin: So tonight Vince, it'll be a triple threat match for the title, and
I'm gonna step into that ring and do what I do best. I'm gonna go out there, kick some ass, raise some hell, win the title...and drink a hell of a lot of beer! And Shane....DTA.
(Austin turns around and stuns Shane, then gets up and stuns Vince. He salutes the crowd, then walks to the back to a huge ovation from the crowd.)
JR: MY GOD KING!!! THE RATTLESNAKE IS BACK!!! AND HE'S GOT A TITLE SHOT TONIGHT!!! King: Who cares!!! Someone go and help Vince!!!! JR: The hell with Vince!!! This night is going to be chaos!!!! Folks join us after the break for the four way match to decide the vacant European title!!!
**************************************** (The Unholy, Scott Steiner and Jeff Jarrett are already in the ring, eyeing each other up. "The Kings" hits, and the fans go wild as Da Brick makes his way down to the ring, accompanied by Trish. As soon as he enters the ring the fight begins.)
King: ITS TRISH!!!!!! ARRRRGGGHHHH!!!! PUPPIES!!!!!!!! JR: For the love of god calm down King!!!! Someone get him some saltpeter!! King: It's ok JR...I'm ok. It's just....PUPPIES!!! JR: Well lets concentrate on the action inside the ring please. Da Brick is dominating Jeff Jarrett here as Scott Steiner takes the unholy to the outside. Remember this is NOT elimination rules, this is one fall to a finish.
(Scott Steiner drops the unholy across the security rails, and enters the ring. He begins to take out Da Brick, and he and Jarrett double team him.)
JR: Well some teamwork going on between Jarrett and Steiner, but how long can that last. (The Unholy re-enters the ring, and whips Jeff against the ropes, and nails him with a stiff forearm. Da Brick is back up, and is battling with
Steiner.) JR: Well there are two huge guys going at it inside the ring. King: (looking at Trish) talking of huge....HUGEEEEE!!!!! (The Unholy takes Jarrett down with a side russian leg sweep, and goes for the cover. 1..2.. Steiner breaks up the pin attempt with a knee to the skull. Da Brick hits Steiner with a big boot to the back of the head as
Jarrett attempts to recover his balance. Da Brick tosses Steiner to the outside with relative ease, and then slides out to meet him. Meanwhile
Jarrett has gone to the corner, and has grabbed the acoustic equalizer.)
JR: King...he's go the guitar!! The referee is on the outside trying to control Da Brick and Steiner!!!
King: Hit him Jeff!!! (Jeff holds the guitar above his head, waiting for the unholy to regain his composure. The Unholy gets a big low blow in on Jarrett to the delight of the crowd, and takes the guitar off him. He smashes the guitar over
Jarrett's head, then throws the remnants outside the ring. He goes for the pin.) JR: There's no referee!!! King: Hey ref!!! You idiot!!! (At that moment Nash and Hall run down to the ring. Nash grabs the unholy, and he and Hall perform a huge double power bomb on him, leaving him in a heap on the floor. They drape Jarrett's arm across the unholy, as the referee slides back into the ring.)
JR: No!! Not like this!!! (The referee counts. 1...2...3.) JR: Dammit! Jeff Jarrett is the new European champion!!! Thanks to the help of the outsiders!!!
King: Well the unholy cheated, so Jeff was just repaying the favor!! JR: Dammit Jarrett brought the guitar in!! King: But the unholy used it on him!!! JR: Now what???? Nash Hall and Jarrett are helping Steiner beat on Da Brick!! Here come DX!!!!!! (The crowd erupts as DX come out to help Da Brick. They clear the ring, and
Jarrett taunts Da Brick, holding the belt up high as "the kings" hits again.)
JR: Could this be a new faction????? What the hell is going on here????? (The camera cuts to the back, showing SummerZ talking to Buffy. The crowd give them a big pop.)
SummerZ: Hey did they get my new entrance music yet? Buffy: Yeah Vince said something about it before. Anyway shouldn't you get ready for your match?
SummerZ: What match? I'm not booked tonight. Buffy: Well all Vince told me was to tell you to get ready for your match. SummerZ: Oh what the hell? I thought I had the night off! He didn't say who
I was fighting?
Buffy: Nope, just to get ready. SummerZ: Dammit. I'd better go get ready. For all I know he's booked me against the Jobber.
Buffy: Well he is a former television champ. SummerZ: Well yeah I guess. Did he actually ever lose that belt? Buffy: For all I know he's still got it. SummerZ: Probably sold it to buy some scotch. Take a memo. Buffy: I'm not your secretary. SummerZ: Well at least give me a piece of paper and a pen! (she hands him a notebook and a pen.) SummerZ: (speaking out loud what he's writing on the paper.) Make....Jobber...defend....belt. Ok I'm gonna go give this to Vince and see who my opponent is. Come on lets go.
(They exit the room as Summerheat cuts to a break.) *************************** JR: Welcome back to Summerheat, and King what do you suppose all that was about? King: Well I wasn't really paying much attention to what was being said...I can tell you exactly what Buffy was wearing though.
JR: Well anyway as we get ready for the handicap dumpster match next let me tell you about our next Pay Per View, OWA Aces High which will take place less than a week away on Sunday 28th January, it will take lace live at the
Houston astrodome, and some tickets are available for this huge event.
King: Boy I can't wait for that one, as the saying goes, it's in the luck of the draw, especially when 15 of the hottest superstars in the game today will compete for that place at OWA Wrestlechaos 2001 against the OWA champion. Of course the 15 participants have yet to be revealed, but rumours are circulating that Vince and Shane have decided on the 15 superstars to compete, and could even be announcing them tonight!!!
JR: That one will be HUGE!!! ("Darkness death" hits, and Cobra emerges from the top of the ramp alongside sexy linda, pushing a dumpster.)
JR: Well this was a challenge laid out by Cobra on OWA corrugated cardboard, he wanted any 2 members of DX in a dumpster match, and Y2J and HBK were more than happy to accept the challenge.
("The Kings" hits once more, and DX walk out to a huge ovation. They high five, then run down into the ring. Cobra slides out the other side of the ring, and a confused DX call him back in. Cobra smiles, and beckons for them to turn around. To their shock, a hooded figure stands behind them.)
JR: King...that's the beast!!! (The beast starts to pound on DX, as Cobra slides back in and begins to help him out. DX regain the advantage though, and whip both men against the ropes, and deliver a double back body drop. Y2J goes outside the ring, and goes under the ring, where he re-emerges with a table. He lifts the lid off the dumpster, and places the table across the top.)
JR: uh-oh. That does not look good for the person who gets tossed onto it. They'll go through the table then into the dumpster!!! (HBK has been beaten down in Y2J's absence, and now the beast and cobra begin to double team Y2J. The beast whips him across the ropes as Cobra stands at the opposite end of the ring. Y2J is flung into Cobra, and Cobra launches him to the outside, where he plunges through the table and into the dumpster. The beast then lifts up HBK with relative ease, and tosses him like a small child into the dumpster. Cobra stands on the outside, and slams both of the lids shut.)
JR: Well it wasn't a handicap match when it was said and done, but regardless of that, Cobra just defeated DX!!!
King: Hey wait!!! There's the rest of DX! (The arena lights go out, and when they go back on Cobra and The Beast are no-where to be seen. Da Brick and Mr. Ass help Y2J and HBK out of the dumpster, as the fans look around the arena for Cobra and The Beast.)
(The camera cuts to the back, where Kevin Kelly is seen with Nash, Hall,
Steiner and the new European champion, Jeff Jarrett.)
KK: Well gentlemen we saw Jeff Jarrett become the new European champion before thanks to your help...but what is your relationship with each other?
JJ: Dammit Kevin Kelly, you do not call me Jeff Jarrett, you call me the chosen one, because slappy I have the stroke around here, and now that i've captured the european championship people will start to recognize that! And as for our relationship....you don't need to know about that yet. Now get out of our way.
(Jarrett pushes Kelly out of the way as the four men walk out of the shot.) (The shot then cuts to Emerald Fusion walking down the hall with his belt around his waist, looking pumped up for his match.)
JR: Well next up that man will defend his United title...but against who????? Find out after this break!!
*************************** ("King of rock" hits, and the fans give Emerald Fusion a lot of heat as he strolls down to the ring decked out in his long leather jacket, and trademark black shades. He enters the ring and removes his jacket, and his shades, revealing emerald green eyes beneath. He grabs a mic off Tony
Chimmel.) Fusion: Well it looks like another screw job on Fusion. Dammit I'm the commissioner around here, I should be getting to make the matches!!1 I shouldn't be put in matches where I don't even know who I'm supposed to be fighting!!! Dammit whoever McMahon has lined up to face me, get your ass out here right now!!!
(The lights begin to flicker, and "Nitro" by the Offspring blares throughout the arena. The fans look puzzled trying to figure out who is going to appear, and give a huge pop when they see the familiar face and seven foot frame of SummerZ, accompanied by the ever gorgeous Buffy. SummerZ gives a huge grin as Fusion protests to the referee inside the ring. SummerZ raises his arm up high as the fans give him a huge pop, then he rushes down to the ring, and slides in, only to be jumped by Fusion.)
JR: My god King!! It's SummerZ!!! King: I guess we know who he's fighting now!! Obviously SummerZ and Vince are still on good terms!!!
JR: Well these two definitely have a history, SummerZ and Fusion used to be the two main men in the business until SummerZ gave up the company. Now could we see SummerZ make a huge impact on his return to the ring by capturing the United title?!?!?
(The two men are still going at it in the ring, trading lefts and rights, until Fusion gets in a stiff boot to the abdomen. SummerZ is hit with a huge scissor kick from Fusion. Fusions turns to taunt the crowd as SummerZ is down.)
JR: What an idiot. If you've got a man down the pin him!!! King: Ah shut up JR, those idiotic fans are provoking him. (SummerZ gets a huge boot to the face of Fusion, and Fusion goes right down. SummerZ drops a huge leg onto the head of Fusion, and the fans cheer even louder. SummerZ picks him up and tries to lift him onto his shoulders to set him up for the SummerZ End, but Fusion counters with a backslide. 1..2...
SummerZ kicks out.) JR: These two may be huge, but the agility of them is incredible!! (Fusion and SummerZ lock up, and SummerZ' strength shows as he lifts Fusion up for a huge brain buster. He raises the arm signaling for the end of
Fusion. )
JR: He's going up top!!! (He leaps onto the top rope, and signals for the SummerZault. He leaps through the air and goes for his version of the frog splash, but Fusion moves out of the way at the last second.)
JR: He risked it all with that move! SummerZ is out!!! (Fusion slowly crawls back over to SummerZ, and limply puts an arm across him. The referee counts 1...2.. SummerZ somehow manages to get a shoulder up, much to the annoyance of Fusion.)
JR: How the hell did he kick out????? King: Uh-oh, Fusion isn't happy. Where's he going???? (Fusion exits the ring and grabs his belt off of the timekeeper. He lunges at SummerZ with the belt, but SummerZ manages to duck the shot, and nail
Fusion with a huge right hand, causing him to drop the belt. He then scoops him up on his shoulders as the referee disposes of the belt, and SummerZ starts to climb the ropes. He ascends to the second rope, and lowers Fusion into a tombstone position. He leaps from the second rope, and hits the
SummerZ End on Fusion. He places an arm across Fusion...1...2....3.)
JR: King can you believe it????? We have a new United champion!!! King: I don't believe it!!! How could this happen???? Noooooo!!!!!! JR: SummerZ is ecstatic!!!! And here come the bWo!!!! They face the APA next for the tag titles, but at the moment they're down here celebrating with
SummerZ!!!
King: Oh no JR, I just had a scary thought. What if the bWo won the tag titles???? Then every member of the bWo would hold titles!!!
JR: Of course Buffy is already the woman's champion, and it would be great if the bWo won the tag straps, but never ever underestimate the Acolytes.
These guys love to fight more than they love to eat! King: Not more than a cold brewski!!! JR: That ones up next, join us after this break!!! ******************** (SummerZ and Buffy wish the bWo good luck, and go back to the locker room. As soon as they have gone the Acolytes music hits, and they receive an incredible ovation as they stroll down to the ring, tag titles around their waist. They enter the ring and relinquish their belts to the referee. Farooq and Da Phat Guy start it off.)
JR: Well I hate to overuse this term...but this one is gonna be a SLOBBERKNOCKER!!!!!! King: Uhhh...I wouldn't bet on it. JR: What? Why not? King: Well the bWo suck!!!! JR: Well they are probably the most underrated team in the OWA, but these guys can put up one hell of a fight.
(Bradshaw is tagged in with Da Phat Guy down. Bradshaw drops a huge leg, but
Da Phat Guy dodges it and manages to tag in Hollywood Snow. He starts to pose for the crowd, and is hit with the clothesline from hell. Bradshaw goes for the pin as Edge and Christian run down to the ring. Edge pulls down Da
Phat Guy as Christian goes inside the ring and breaks up the count.
Christian nails the unprettier on Bradshaw as Edge nails the edgeamatic on
Da Phat Guy on the outside. Farooq takes down Christian with a big spinebuster, but then DX run down to the ring, and chaos ensues.)
JR: What in the hell???? This is madness!!!! These four teams all want tag gold, but only one of them has it!!! And now DX are doing a number on everyone!! King we have to take a break, this one is a dq win for the bWo.
Up next, the main event, HHH vs The Big Show vs AUSTIN!!!! ********************* ( "Sweet home alabama" hits, and the largest athlete in sports entertainment, the big show, makes his way down to the ring along with his business partner Shane McMahon.)
JR: Well this man certainly has to be the favourite in this one. Seven foot, five hundred pounds, he's a monster!!!
King: But JR it's gonna take a hell of a lot to beat the game, especially with Vince in his corner.
( HHH's music hits, and the OWA champion makes his way down into the ring along with Vince McMahon. Vince stares down Shane outside the ring, and HHH and The Big Show start it out with rapid lefts and rights inside the ring.)
JR: This match has started!!! And we haven't even got our third participant out yet!!! (The glass shatters, and the fans go mental as "disturbed" hits and Austin walks down into the ring, and joins in the fight.)
JR: WILL YOU LISTEN TO THIS OVATION!!!!! THIS IS AMAZING!!!! King: Argh!!! (The fight grinds to a halt as "wreck" hits, and the vision of Mick Foley appears on the SummerZtron, much to the fans delight.)
JR: What the hell??? What is IWEF president Mick Foley doing here???? (Vince and Shane look equally shocked as Foley starts to talk.) Foley: Hello OWA!!!!! (the fans go wild, and the fight inside the ring starts again as Triple H takes advantage of the distraction.)
Foley: I guess your all wondering why I'm here....well Shane I heard what you said before about the third party in the ownership...AND IT'S ME
SHANE!!! IT'S ME!!! JR: What in the hell????? Mick Foley is the third owner of the OWA!!!!! This is chaos!!!! Foley: And my first ruling as OWA joint owner? To make this match a four corners match!!! JR: What???? Who will be the fourth participant?????? Foley: I think you know the guy....he's been held down for a hell of a long time.....THA KID!!!!!!
(The fans seems less than ecstatic with Foleys announcement, but Tha Kid sprints down to the ring with a chair, and starts to raise hell. He takes out a battered Austin, then knocks HHH out. He hits a huge low blow on the
Big Show. He hits the delusion on the chair while the referee is detained by
Vince on the outside, who is protesting. Tha Kid signals the referee, and covers the show. He gets the three.)
JR: OH MY GOD!!!!!!! THA KID IS THE NEW OWA CHAMPION!!!!!!!! THIS IS AMAZING!!!!! THA KID'S UNDEFEATED STREAK REMAINS ALIVE...AND HE HAS FINALLY
ACHIEVED HIS DREAM!!! WE HAVE A NEW UNITED CHAMPION....WE HAVE ANEW WORLD
CHAMPION....AND WE HAVE A NEW OWNER!!!!!! WHAT THE HELL WILL HAPPEN THIS
THURSDAY ON DEZTRUXION?????? (The OWA copyright logo appears, and Summerheat goes off the air.) check out the site at http://www.summerzuwf.com if you like it....sign up..we accept both real and original wrestlers...so plenty room for everyone
