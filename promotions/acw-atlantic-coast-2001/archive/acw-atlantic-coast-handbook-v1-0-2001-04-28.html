THE APPLICATION ~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/ INTRO ~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/ Atlantic Coast Wrestling is an east coast-based independent federation that revolves around the "old school" extreme style. Basically, hardcore wrestling, so-called "sports entertainment" and the old-school style of wrestling are intertwined (kind of like the early days of ECW or the current XPW). We don't need to rely on television; our underground fanbase is quite big... however, ACW does have a weekly show that airs each week at 3AM, which is explained later in this handbook. ~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~ HOW THIS SHIT WORKS ~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~ 2.1. ROLEPLAYING AND STUFF Well, here's the basic scheme of things: - Roleplays will be posted by YOU, the handler, on the board (at Yahoo! Groups -- http://groups.yahoo.com/group/atlantic_coast_wrestling ) designated for ACW stuff. Yeah. - These roleplays, or "promos" if you will, will usually take place in or around various locations at the venue. If you've ever seen a batch of ECW promos, they're kinda like that. - It's YOUR responsibility to go to that God damn board and send out your promo. :-) You are allowed _one_ promo a week. - Swearing is allowed, but please "bleep" it. - If you're not booked on a card, you don't really have to roleplay. However, if you want to advance an angle you're "working," feel free to do so. - Strats aren't required for ACW; we're more of a "side" thing just for fun. Not something to be taken absolutely seriously, but hey- at least care enough to roleplay. ;-) - What if you want to do in-rings on ACW TV tapings? Well, send me the gist of what you want to say in-character in a summary and I'll put it there for you. Example: After Jason Noname got settled in the ring, Joe Blow made his entrance, as usual, to "Enter Sandman". Right before the match, he picked up the mic and told Noname that he wasn't going to let this win slip away and that Noname was nothing but a, "punk bitch who only got a push in this Goddamn company by sucking dick." Or... Joe Blow came out to the ring and announced that he would put his Atlantic Coast title up for grabs against all comers. He claimed everyone in the back was afraid of him. He called Jason Noname a bitch over and over and over. - The only things you'll get through e-mail are the arena reports, the update, and important announcements. Other things such as roleplays go on the board, as mentioned before. Once again, if you've forgotten, the addy to the board is (say it with me now) http://groups.yahoo.com/group/atlantic_coast_wrestling . - If you're not a member of Yahoo! Groups, please become one. It only takes a few minutes, people. :-) 2.2. ACW TV Well, we're not really on television except for a local station here in New York City; ACW can be seen for only an hour early Sunday at 3AM on WNYW, FOX-5. Only God knows if that slot will even last. Fortunately for you people, you get detailed "arena reports." That's right. We _don't_ have fully-written cards here in ACW; instead, we have detailed arena reports. Atlantic Coast Wrestling is purely an independent promotion that has little (if any) television exposure. Consider us like the East Coast version of the California-based Xtreme Pro-Wrestling. 2.3. SUPERCARDS ACW doesn't have pay-per-views, God dammit. We're too cheap for that. Instead, we've got supercards. And no, I haven't come up with names for them yet. :-P ~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/ WHO REALLY RUNS THIS JOINT ~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/ I: PRESIDENT: JIMMY T. ACW titles might not be the most prestigous titles in the world, but hey- it'll make you feel more important. That helps especially if people think you're shit. ACW ATLANTIC COAST CHAMPIONSHIP: The big mamma jamma. Win this bitch, and you're God. Of the federation, at least. Anyway, just defend it and you'll be well off. Don't? You get stripped. ACW INTERNET TITLE: Hey, it's the internet title. What can I say? ACW TAG TEAM TITLES: Pair up with a buddy, beat asses, and then you can get this. These titles won't be defended as much, however, you'll get the occasional tag team match here and there. ~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~ ACW BOOBS ~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~ 4.1. YOUR BOSSES I: ACW PRESIDENT/CEO: JAMES CROSS Mr. Cross is a handsome, young entreprenour who thinks he deserves what he wants. That's a little hard, because his dad cut him off from any further financial, uh, stuff. The Cross family kind of disowned him. Ironically, Cross is the _humblest_ member of his family. Mr. Cross isn't around a lot, but when things go awry, expect him to be there. Expect him to be pissed. Expect him to get straight in your face and be brutally honest. Oh, and uh, he's a part-time stoner. Beware. II: CREATIVE DIRECTOR: STEPHEN LOWERY Stephen Lowery is just a suck-up to Mr. Cross. Simple as that. Cross dubbed him the "creative director," but, really, Lowery doesn't do anything for the fuckin' company. ~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~ The Application ~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~ NOTE: You HAD BETTER have read the handbook... there's some pretty unique shit in there.
