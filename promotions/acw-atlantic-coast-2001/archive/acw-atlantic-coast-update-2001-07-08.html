That's right, you heard it here first... then again, this is the only place you'll hear it. Atlantic Coast Wrestling will hold its inaugeral TV taping outdoors and ON THE BEACH at Queens, New York's Rockaway Beach. So, get ready, bring your suntan lotion... and a weapon to give away. =-=-=-=-=-=-= OOC NOTES =-=-=-=-=-=-= Hmm... nothing much to say except welcome to the fold, I guess. The card will be made up soon, probably sometime this week. Hope y'all read the handbook, know what to do, and all that jazz... I'm really anxious to get this started. :) Just to remind you guys, I'm not the only one keeping this fed alive; you guys are, too. You guys play a MAJOR part in keeping ACW afloat, so, you've gotta put in some effort. I'm not saying put your real life on the sidelines; I'm just saying do what ya can. Get in RP's, get in in-rings/backstage promos, get in strats (if ya want). Anyway, that's it. Just telling you guys that this thing is indeed alive and well. <steps off soapbox> -j. =-=-=-=-=-=-=-= ACW IRC LIST =-=-=-=-=-=-=-= IRC list will be formulated soon. In the meantime, we're at #acw.
