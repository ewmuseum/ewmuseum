Joining 7.1 Rules 7.2 Application xxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxx 1. What is ACW? 1.1 Fantasy Explanation - Of course, ACW is the acronym for Atlantic Coast Wrestling. ACW is a very small, independent promotion that is based on the East Coast, usually states nearby the Atlantic Coast (duh). ACW goes by one rule when it comes to most things: there are no rules. Atlantic Coast Wrestling combines the beautiful footwork of aerial/technical/lucha styles with the gritty, no-nonsense, violent approach of garbage (hardcore) wrestling. Expect to see a good wrestling match... as well as some blood. 1.2 "Real" explanation - Well, ACW is an e-federation. Like, duh. You didn't think this was a real fed, did you? If so, you're an idiot. xxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxx 2. Who runs ACW? 2.1 Fantasy - The mysterious James Cross is at the helm of Atlantic Coast Wrestling. He has personally funded the company himself, is the founder, as well as the CEO. The only help he gets is from the so-called "creative director," Stephen Lowery, who seemingly has an odd (but sometimes good) taste in talent. 2.2 Real Life - ACW is run by me, James (or Jimmy, whatever floats your boat). T., that is. I don't give out my last name, so, like, don't ask me. :) xxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxx 3. What do you have to do? 3.1 Flash (Roleplaying) - Well, this is usually a given, but in ACW you must flash (roleplay) at least once every two weeks. If possible, one a week would be even better. I will expect the guys who are higher in the card to at least RP once a week. - As far as the length of roleplays go, I don't want epics. Simple as that. I just need something short, sweet, and straight to the point. - Roleplays SHOULD take place backstage at the venue or nearby the location of the venue. It seems to make more sense that way. This basically means no roleplays taking place in London, England if your match will be at, say, Cataret, New Jersey. 3.2 Formatting (in in-rings, flashes, etc.) - Yes, Atlantic Coast Wrestling DOES have a specific format for roleplays. This means you can't just use whatever format you want. Here's what it'll look like. It's fairly simple: [Tommy Grayson walks into the room, sporting an EVIL~! smirk on his face.] TG: Atlantic Coast Wrestling sucks. Get out of here while you can, you stupid bastards. [Fade.] - Use the [ brackets ] around your actions and use your wrestler's name in initials, unless he has one name. In that case, your wrestler's name would be capitalized. - You know these things? "..." Yeah, those. I only want THREE of those. I don't want, "..........". That's just stupid. 3.3 Strategies - A very important part of ACW is your strat for your match. Not writing one is the same as not training for a real life athletic event. What a strat needs is.. YOUR WRESTER: YOUR OPPONENT: YOUR ENTRANCE: (What will happen as you walk to the ring?) SPOTS: (Thinks that you would really like to see in your match, moves, ref bumps, manager stuff even stuff that the announcers and fans might say or do. This part should have the most beef to it.) ENDING: (What you will do if you win, lose, anything that would happen at the ending of your match.) 3.4 Angle Ideas - The reason most of us play this game is for the angles that we will get to play out, and see. The president can't make up every cool angle (although I am very good at it.) :). That is where you come in, if you want to feud with someone or team with someone all you gotta do is tell me. Almost anything you want to see is doable and I am VERY easy to get in touch with. So, hit me with your ideas everytime you get one, I'll use a good number of them. 3.5 Other - Ahh, other. Well, I will need help writing the matchs, so if you want to volunteer to help, well then, don't hold back. I'll take all the help I can get. xxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxx 4. ACW Shows 4.1 ACW TV - This is basically ACW's main and ONLY show. ACW TV airs weekly on your local FOX or America One affiliate along the East Coast. The show contains various matches taped from ACW's house shows. Sometimes, they will be shown in sequence; in other words, there is one set card, certain matches will be shown over a course of a couple of weeks. Hope that made sense. Usually, up to three matches are shown on ACW TV; the rest of the airtime is taken up by promos and excess amounts of shilling. 4.2 Roleplay Packet - No fancy name. It's just a roleplay packet. 4.3 Supercards - No. ACW _doesn't_ have pay-per-views. After all, we ARE independent and stuff. ACW will have a supercard almost every two to three months, depending on the schedule. There is no set number as to how many matches will be on a supercard. xxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxx 5. Names and Faces 5.1 James Cross - Cross is definitely an odd individual (if not the oddest) in Atlantic Coast Wrestling- and he's the Goddamn PRESIDENT/CEO. While he looks strange and pimped-out on the outside, Cross can maintain a serious demeanor and make fair, just decisions. A wrestling fan all his life, as well as a Japanese garbage wrestling fan, Cross wishes to make ACW one of the most controversial promotions in the region. BASIC GIMMICK: El Presidente, Numero Uno, and El CEO. 5.2 Stephen Lowery - Stephen is the creative director of ACW. Rumor has it that his job is actually a form of indentured servitude to Mr. Cross. As much as Lowery denies it, it's fairly obvious; Lowery is often Cross's "yes" man... but he does tend to screw things up, possibly on purpose. BASIC GIMMICK: Creative Director, Mr. Cross's indentured servant. 5.3 Chris Lansberry - Chris is the face commentator of ACW, basically. He's a fan of the sport and was hired by Cross because of his enthusiasm... and also because of the way Chris's face contorted in disgust as he watched a Japanese wrestler being powerbombed into a fishtank of pirhanas. BASIC GIMMICK: face commentator. Fair. Actually intelligent and doesn't call a back bodydrop a "slam." 5.4 Josh Davis - Josh Davis used to work as a backstage interviewer for James Pillow's old LVPWA. He, unfortunately, never started because Pillow had forgotten Josh was an employee. As a result, Davis became disgruntled and urinated in Mr. Pillow's coffee... and, of course, was immediately fired. Davis briefly found work in the AWP, however, when ownership changed once again, his services were no longer needed. So, here he is. BASIC GIMMICK: heel commentator. Stoner. An idiot. Digs old school rap music. 5.5 Kennedy Michelle Chambers - Kennedy is basically the third wheel of the commentating team. Just to, you know, add some feminine point of view to the matches. And some flavor. KMC used to work for ICW, but recently, she had been trying to get her own talk show, which she did... and it didn't last too long. Damn Oprah and Rosie. BASIC GIMMICK: neutral commentator. She thinks certain wrestlers are cute. Supposedly digs Josh Davis. xxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxx 6. Titles 6.1 ACW Atlantic Coast championship - Equivalent to the world title of a "big league" federation. 6.2 ACW Television championship - It's the Television championship. It's defended on almost every card, I guess. 6.3 ACW Atlantic Tag Team championship - Equivalent to any tag team title. Like, duh. xxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxx 7. Joining 7.1 Rules - Atlantic Coast Wrestling is NOT a place for egos. If you think you are so much better than everyone else maybe you are right, there's the door. Buh bye. - You have to participate every week, and try to keep your character fresh, if you just sit on your ass and do nothing, you're as good as gone. - Try not to break kayfabe, which means, please try to stay in character, unless we have a really cool shoot thing planned, then it's all good. :) - No useing anyone else's characters in your promos unless they helped write it, or they gave permission and yes, I will ask them. - Don't expect ass-kissing you get you anywhere, but we really don't forbid it. :) 7.2 The Application (finally.) - Player Information - Full Name: Your email address: E-wrestling experience: AIM Screen Name: IRC Screen Name: Will you write matches if needed?: - Character Information - note: fill out twice for a tag team. Name: Height: Weight: Age: Gender: Hometown: Alignment: Theme Music: Appearance (be thorough and descriptive): Biography: Gimmick: Entrance (no huge entrances; we're an indy!): Wrestling Style: 10-20 Wrestling Moves: Finishing Move: Name: Description: Sample Roleplay (required, unless I asked you to join!): - Tag Team Section (tag teams only, duh!) - Team Name: Combined Weight: 5-10 Double Team Moves: Finishing Move: Name: Description: WHEN FINISHED SEND TO [detail withheld] Note: mucho "thanks" to [name withheld], whom I ripped this handbook off of.
