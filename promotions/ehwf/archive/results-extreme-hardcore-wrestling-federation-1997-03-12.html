EHWF SHOW 1 EHWF Areana, March 14 1997 Ron Brow: Welcom everyone to the fist ever Extreme Hardcore Wrestling Federation show right here at the EHWF Areana. I am the Prez Ron Brown and Joining me tonight will be a man who has been in this sport for many years. Would you please welcome Dick The Giant!!! DTG: Well thanks very much Ron it's good to be here tonight, we have a great matches for you all tonight so lets take you to our man with the mic Tony Roo. TR: Ladies & Gentalmen our first match is between 2 men who have been around the Indys for a while now. First on his way down to the ring the man who is a co-holder of the Extreme Global Westling Federations Rocky Mountian Tag Titles Bubba Ray Studly. His opponent making his way to the ring weighing in at an hugh 578lbs The Beasty. Ron Brown: Yes we are about to start not much is known about Beasty but his opponent for tonight is more of a tag wrestler than a singels wrestler. DTG: Ok the bell has rang and they lock up. RB: Beasty grabs a headlock, Studly throws hiw into the ropes and clothes lines him. DTG: Studly is stomping on his head, the ref warns Studly RB: Oh my god, Studly just punched out the ref. DTG: Studly grabs Besty and throws him over the tope rope and follows him out to the floor. RB: That must of hert cause we don't have mats on our floors. Bubba Ray climbs to the top rope, DW grabs a chair and hits Beasty with it once, twice, three times. RB: Beasty is bleeding like hell, he is going to need stitches after this one. DTG: DW places Beasty on the table, Bubba faces the crowd to do the Studly moonsult while DW sets the table on fire. DTG: The ref counts one,two,three RB: So who would of thought Bubba Ray Studly gets the first win here in th EHWF. Right now lets get down to the ring with my broadcast buddy Dick The Giant as he hears what Bubba has to say. DTG: Well Bubba you just had your first win here in the EHWF what do you have to say?
