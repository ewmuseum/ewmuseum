Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The Extreme Hardcore Wrestling Federation staged its first card, EHWF Show 1, on 14 March 1997 with a roster of just six wrestlers, its own president describing the promotion as still building toward the sixteen to twenty wrestlers it needed before holding tournaments to crown champions. It was played entirely by e-mail rather than a live simulator — “that way you don't have to go and search for results, they come to you” — and outcomes were decided by the quality of each entrant's own roleplay.

That opening match pitted Bubba Ray Studly against the 578-pound Beasty, and ended when Studly and a partner, DW Studly, set a table on fire and drove Beasty through it with a moonsault. Interviewed afterward, Studly claimed the Extreme Global Wrestling Federation's own Rocky Mountain Tag Team Championship as his and his partner's current title elsewhere, and issued a standing challenge to any team from any promotion willing to face them once the roster grew large enough to make a match of it.

A recruitment post five days later still describes the promotion as taking new members and playing exclusively by e-mail. No further card, result, or roster update survives; whether the promotion ever grew past its first six wrestlers, and how or when it closed, isn't in the surviving record.
