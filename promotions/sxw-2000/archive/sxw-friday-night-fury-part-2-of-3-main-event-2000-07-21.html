Friday Night Fury (Part 2 of 3, Main Event)

" referee is Ernie Chang ' Eve of Destruction ' by Tears for Fears begins to play as the lights go out and the music starts.
A huge letter Omega appears on the X-Tremetron and lightning strikes each ring post. The lights come back on and Omega is standing in the ring.
Cheryl - " Now making his way to the ring with his manager Alpha ...... he is ...... OMEGA !!!!!! " Brimstone's intro music begins to play and the sound of a motorcycle exhaust can be hear, soon
Brimstone is seen riding his Harley from the garage, through the halls and stops at the top of the entrance ramp, just under the X-Tremetron Video Screen, he puts the kick stand down and dismounts his bike and makes his way to the ring.
Cheryl - " Now making his way to the ring, he hails from London, England and weighs in at 550 pounds he is ..... BRIMSTONE !!!! "
*** The Bell Rings *** Talk about the clapping of thunder, which has nothing on these two giants, as the echo of each chest shot rings through out the arena, then the rope tossing begins and each stands his ground and the ropes have no ricochet effect on these big powerhouses.
Alpha is cheer Omega on as he tries to nail a clothesline, but the huge Brimstone ducks the attack, bounces the ropes and comes back with a clothesline from his own hell, which sends Omega spinning in a circle before he lands flat on the mat, stomach first.
Brimstone walks over and helps Omega to his feet, just so he can fall victim to a powerslam, that is a set up for Brimstone's guillotine leg drop off the tope rope, but
Omega moves at the last second and Brimstone crashes to the mat, Omega is to his feet and lands a baseball slide face kick.
Omega catches his breath as Brimstone is slow to his feet, Omega locks in and lands a underhook DDT and tries for a cover.
referee - 1....2." William - " Either of these guys can be the spear head for the SXW, this match up is unstoppable ! "
Brimstone kicks out the fans let out a ahhhhhhh, as Omega lifts Brimstone to his feet again for a suplex, but he cannot lift Brimstone, on the other hand Brim can lift Omega, and he does, into a weak powerbomb, he then tries for a cover. referee - " ...1..." Omega gets his shoulder up no problem, he then rolls to his feet and again the fist fight is on, ending quickly when Omega scores a super kick that sends Brimstone into the corner, Brimstone moves right out of the way when Omega spears the turn buckle, then Brim grabs
Omega and hits a few knife
hand chops then flings Omega into the opposite corner and hits him with a running corner splash.
Styles - " These guys are monsters, they cannot get the other down for the count ! " Omega stumbles from the corner and hits the mat thanks to a clothesline from hell, Omega is then picked up into the Last Ride Powerbomb, it was not as powerful as one might suspect it would be on say a heavy weight, but Omega is almost as heavy as Brimstone, the impact shacks the ring.
Tely - " Holy moly, mother of pearl, that was intense ! " Brimstone goes for the cover. referee - " ..1....2." Omega gets his shoulder up again, Alpha is ecstatic, Brimstone is less then happy, as he reaches down and lifts Omega up by his hair and signals for the sky high choke slam finisher, but Omega is not willing to take the ride, he bashes the arm of Brimstone until he breaks the hold, then a few boots from Omega and then it starts ....
Styles - " Oh oh, Brimstone is screwed now, Omega has his second wind ! " Omega lands a suplex which he could not pull off just a few minutes ago, then Brimstone takes another super kick which spins him in circles, just as he stops spinning
Omega runs at him and slams
the giant with a choke slam, Omega keeps it going and lands Brimstone with his Omega Destroyer-
Gorilla Press Slam, then chokeslam opponent while in mid air. The weight of Brimstone prevents the move from being spectacular, but non the less, still amazing to see,
Omega makes the pin.
referee - " 1....2" Brimstone kicks out, Omega gets angry and applies the Crossface Scorpion
Deathlock, Brimstone is in
undeniable pain, but he fights his way to the ropes, the action is intense, Omega barely lets go once Brimstone has the rope, this angers Brimstone to where he turns right around and applies the cobra clutch.
Now Omega is on the ropes, Brimstone breaks quick, only to find himself locked in a bear hug, Omega then forces them away from the ropes, Brimstone must use his strength to break free, pounding the shoulders of Omega until he breaks the hold, then Brimstone grabs him and hits a choke slam.
Styles - " This is epic, the mountain giants trample everything in sight and are unstoppable ! "
Brimstone clotheslines Omega over the top ropes, Brimstone then runs across and bounces the rope,
Omega simply landed on the ring apron, otherside of the ropes, he is just getting his balance when
Brimstone comes back at him and bumps Omega off the ring apron and sends Omega sailing 5 feet across, in the air and finally landing back first on to the Spanish announcers table and it collapses !
Alpha runs over to see if Omega is all right, even Brimstone seems concerned, but then Omega moves and soon is actually on his feet .
Tely - " Incredible, Omega is standing after going through the Spanish announcers table ! " Alpha is begging for Omega to stay out of the ring ... referee - " 6......7.......8.......9.." Omega never gives up, he climbs back in, and right into another chokeslam, this time it's the Hell's
Gate- Middle of ring sky high choke slam, Omega crashes to the mat and
Brimstone makes the cover. referee - " 1...2.. " William - " Yes, he got his shoulder up ! " Tely - " This has turned into quite the little battle ! " Styles - " LITTLE !? " Brimstone huffs as he picks Omega up one last time for a Samoan drop, that sets up the jumping elbow drop of the turn buckle and finally another Last Ride
Powerbomb and cover. referee- " 1....2.....3 " *** The Bell Rings *** Cheryl - " Winner of this match via pin fall ......... BRIMSTONE !!!!!!! " ( 10:09 minutes ) After the match, Brimstone celebrates and the fans cheer him on, Alpha enters the ring, helps Omega to his feet, he then gets and accepts the hand shake from
Brimstone ..... William - " Folks, that'll do it for us, our first show ended as it started, great sportsmanship, and respect for there follow CO-workers and comrades in arms, thanks folks for making SXW your choice for ring action and we will see you in just 4 days for
Turmoil !!!!!!! " Brimstone, Omega & Alpha all grabs beers and start to chugg in the ring with referee Ernie Chang and even Styles gets in the ring for a beer, he tries to fire up a blunt and then .....
....... was then cut off and the camera went to Cheryl Hunter for the 'Now the Stage is Set' report [Image] NOW THE STAGE IS SET.. Matches where set, now the SXW is in motion as the results may lead to rivalries or alliances !
Will we learn what might have happened to Legionnaire when he went through Voivod's portal & see more gadgets !?
Ahala & Damona may have formed an alliance as Slayer Red observed Legionnaire & Voivod, or just one of them ?! Loci & Cyric may have unfinished business, the blood has being drawn the line was crossed, but both still live !
Raging Bull wins his match, but Hex makes the wound, Christina is the victim of his vicious attack with a chair !
From Kamikaze & Cheyenne to Omega & Brimstone this card saw it all, what will the next week ...
