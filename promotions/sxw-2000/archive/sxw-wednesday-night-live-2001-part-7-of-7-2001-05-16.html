Full card and graphix at http://sxw.50megs.com/results/05_16_01_WNL.htm SXW SECRET ORIGINS: FEATURING OVER FIEND[Image] Most may be unaware of how the Super Star from SXW known as Over Fiend came to be the man he is today, it all started nearly 30 years ago when children born like him where not as common as they are today, born into the world as Freddie Roundhorse his light gray skin would always make him stand out.
Born as a mutant or power born as some call them, Freddie has gray skin, no body hair would ever grow from him and he was like touching a slob of concrete, bumpy and tough, needles broke when he was first given vacines, which they skipped, once they realized he didn't need to eat either, assuming his body would not come under normal human deseases.
Unfortunatly Freddie's mother died hours after birth, the damage done by his stone like skin had been damaging her internally for months before he was born and once was has out, he nearly took everything in her with him.
While the child was tested and probed for the first few months of it's life, he soon was given to his father, who had been blaming himself for the mutant gene's in his son and that killed his wife, 2 days after gaining custody of his child, Freddie's father tried to kill him with a pistal, in a murder suicide, but Freddie survived, unscratched, his father was so quick to assume he'd killed his son, he didn't even blink before turning the weapon on himself, when the police showed up Freddie was trying to wake his father up, his head nearly gone, blood all over.
Month later Freddie's last name was changed by adoption officials and he was then put up for adoption, but nobody would take him and to prevent him from becoming a goverment ward and tool he was then bounced around from foster home to foster hom, never staying with one family for to long, all to scared of him or what people thought of them for having such a strange child.
And in school, once Freddie was 14, he was being made fun of by boys 4 years older then him, Freddie got suspended on his first day in high school for hanging all 4 older boys from a fence by their under pants.
Freddie also could never get himself a date, not even in the schools for children much like himself, he was an outcast among outcasts.
When Freddie became his own man, he signed on for the marines and got kicked out weeks later for beating up his superiors, he then would lose job after job from his anger towards women or normal men, then when he was
22 Freddie fought his first street match, highly illegal and justly so as he took his first life, killing his opponent with a haymaker that broke the guys neck, Freddie was then driven out of the state by locals and then hunted by police.
During the chase Freddie got cornered but still did not give up, soon lethal force was used and it did no good, Freddie found out he could even take bullets and walk it off seconds later, but he was soon out numbered and taken to jail. From there he earned hte name Over Fiend by taking control of his cell block in his first hour there.
Years had gone by and his time was served, Over Fiend as he soon even called himself was loose and Freddie Roundhouse was never to be seen again. Fiend soon joined the XWF, making history as the 1st signed, also as the first big mouthed heel, he even taked down to the biggest man he ever faced, that man was Brimstone !
XWF was good to Over Fiend, he won many titles and broke many bones, he may not have made any freinds, but he would rather have enemies and that is what he got, from claiming to be A-Train's true brother, to faking alliances with Brimstone, Ahala & The Red Raven while all along a tool of
Lucas Myphis, but making a name for himself.
Most know that in June of 2000, 7 different timelines merged into one( If not read the very end segment to this WNL quick and come back to this is you want to understand a bit more, yeah right Mikee B), making the SXW timeline, where things where still the same, but yet different.. It was in the SXW timeline where Over Fiend may have learned his true roots, when the Dominion came into the picture. Learning he was not a human mutant with the X cromisone, but a half breed alien, his mother was abducted and impregnated by Fiend's father, she never said anything since nobody would believe her back in those days, so she hid it, hoping for the best.
Fiend had always blamed himself for both his parents deaths, not because he was alien but a freek super natural, once his mind was clear of the only guilt he had ever felt it was like an all new Over Fiend, one who betray all of the human race once he learned he was not full one of them, since then the Dominion has left earth alone for now, and Over Fiend stayed behind, his name is making waves and his threats taken more seriously, he is proving himself worthy once again and no more ties to bind him down !
BLAST FROM THE PAST: This weeks blast from the past t is a recap of last years X-tremeMania
2000 listed as one of the greatest PPV's of all time, this years may have leveled that claim as Damona destroys the SXW super stars in a single melee, by herself with one hand, but yet to END IT ALL, like she did a year ago .. in another timeline, with alternate allies and a different
Ultimate plan, but a few lose screws called Heroes, weather intentional or not, saved more then the day in the eye of destruction and hope to do it again, even if just a fluke !
Nexus of All Realities ! In space, but unlike anything even imagined, there are planets and whole galaxies orbiting around a single nexus, the point of all realities.
On the outer side of the galaxies are giant beings known as celestials, bigger then any 100 planets they stand vigilant, the gods of all kinds can be seen as if the higher powers have gathered at this time for a great purpose.
The beings knows as eternity, darkness, death, oblivion, apocalypse, and many more surround as the Nexus, their attention is focused on the smallest being in their group, as the view closes in, it is Kamikaze, is astral form floats in space, he is no longer your typical human, he does not need air, or food or anything, he simply is and may always be.
[Image] The entities closes in as The Chosen appear before Kamikaze who awakens from his slumber, he is shocked at what he sees, but he cannot be for long, the Chosen must speak with him.
