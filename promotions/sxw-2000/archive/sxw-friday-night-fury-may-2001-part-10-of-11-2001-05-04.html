Futile, Damona blatantly uses her powers to whip Despair in the air from ring corner to ring corner then back right into a flying Driving Knee Lift and ripe for a pin which Damona ignores.
Damona is superior even without the ring as she whips Despair into a Neck
Breaker then
a Belly to Back Waist Lock Suplex(German Suplex) and finally a Belly to
Back Double Chickenwing Suplex(Tiger Suplex) but Despair busts out of the sliding pin. Damona is enraged by this and thrown off guard taking a Somersault Flying Head Scissors , Damona was quick to kick out and then lift Despair into a one handed Choke slam and drivers her down, not once, not twice, but trice .. then a forth pick up still holding on and using just the left hand, the Infinity Ring hand she raises her girl up high and with a lethal strike from her right hand, drive through her chest and out her back, blood and organs fly everywhere and Damona then kisses her daughter good bye for ever and drops the dead green corpse to the ground.
[Image] * * * The Bell Rings * * * Dark Wind - " .... and your winner via death strike ..... " Fans have little reaction, probably for the best but then a simply nasty look from
Damona makes them all and give he biggest pop of the night !.
Dark Wind - " ... She is all powerful and all that is beautiful ...... " Dark Wind - " .... Our goddess and master ........ DAMONA !!!!!!!!!! " 3:23 minutes Back in the dungeon they wait for Despair to return and for Kamikaze to disappear for his match, which is the only thing that happens as they all know what
Damona must have done to her own kin. None the less, Kamikaze is going to do his part during his match, the others will have to fight one more battle, this one may be the last.
KAMIKAZE vs. DARK WIND [Image] [Image] Extreme Dumpster Match [Image] no time limit [Image] referee - [Image] - Kelly O'Delly Kamikaze is whisked away and into the ring, where his dark brother stands across from them, they bow with complete respect, head down, not looking for a sneak attack from the other, it'll never happen.
* * * The Bell Rings * * * After a futile lock up and grapple melee, Dark Wind gets into his powers and starts dishing out the energy balls, firing wildly at his agile brother who flips and dodges them all, the blast hit area's of the stone coliseum breaking holes into it.
Dark Wind is trying to blast Kamikaze who gets up on a ramp where the a dumpster lies just below, also putting Kamikaze up about 50 feet.
Dark Wind fires more blast from below, up at his brother who again dodges them all, then Dark Wind teleports up with Kamikaze, both into a blur of a melee, finally
Kamikaze blocks a lethal Soul Reaver- Claw hold to face, drains victim's energy, can kill if held too long with a cross hand block and not only that but but reverses the absorbing so it is he who takes all of Dark Wind's energy and power for a few minutes.
Dark Wind falls to the ramp top at his brothers feet, who not only has Dark Winds massive power level right now, but added to his own which is at it's peek from him saving it up, blast through the dampening field in place around the dungeon and teleports all held with in it to select locations around the arena, surprising Damona, her Dark Elite & The Four Horsemen, with the core group including Cyric,
Beast, Fiend, Bull, Piranha, Curio & Gomez right into the balcony in direct combat the the core evil group.
The battle is outrages, energy blast and powers firing everywhere, Damona not only has android guards but she uses the ring of Infinity to force the fans to turn on the wrestling super stars, it's more then a peer six brawl, more like a peer 19.
The Four Horseman jump into their mechanical flying steeds and zoom out to attack the anti-Damona forces, while the Dark Elite try and take down the core team in the balcony. Damona flies herself to safety high above all a force field deflects all attacks on her and then all hear as she says ...
[Image] DAMONA - " ENOUGH !!!!!!!!!! " Damona raises her hand with the Infinity ring and all in the coliseum are frozen in place, only Damona has freedom to move around as she levitates over to her balcony ...
Damona goes up to Cyric & Beast who cannot move but all hear. [Image] Damona - " It is time you two fought for the SXW One World Title, if one of you does not kill the other, free of will, free of my control then I will, and do not test me Cyric .. you know I mean it .. when I say all of earth save for me and my minions will fall into the sun and burn into nothingness as that explosion destroys the entire milky way galaxy !!!!! "
[Image] Damona - " I will create my own world from the ashes and no who breathed life before my world will, I shall restart the human race in my image and as my slaves, think about it, the two of you currently hold all of earth and it's races fate in your two hands, as long as one of you dies by the others ...
BWAHAHAHAHAHAHAHA !!!!! " In the blink of an eye the fight is over, all is in order as the once would be world saviors are all seated in the same section, held down by the power of
Damona, the newblood, the old school and the arch enemies all who put it aside from the last strike and failed, now sit with the fans and villains to watch as the alternate timeline father of Beast the Crusher defends against him in a deathmatch for the fate of all mankind, how ironic some would say.
