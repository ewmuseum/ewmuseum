Jake Styles accepts the shake and fans go wild. Crow does the arm lift for Styles the winner and another fan pop as he leaves letting
Styles get one more pop and then make his exit as well. Biggest pop- The end when Styles and Crow shook hands. Biggest Moment- When Styles turned around Crow was up still in the ring after the bell rang.
Possible Outcome- More good matches with these two, possible tag team, again the match made many things possible.
Biggest Heat- When Jake Styles first came out, a section of fans had Styles sucks signs front row. Jake saw this and tore the sign up. Cheryl - " Now in the ring, he is the riff master & playdoh disaster ... .... the mind numbingly .... GUMBY !!!!! " Gumby punches Heenin in his glass jaw and d00d goes down. * * * The Bell Rings * * * There are ladders all around and after Gumby puts a beating on Heenin and nails a Flying Clothesline From Hell he then exits the ring and slides in a 10 foot ladder. Gumby then stands it up under his guitar and then stomps a mud hole in Heenin before he starts his climb.
Maxx - " Gumby is not messing around with this match folks. " Gumby is half way up when Heenin stumbles to his feet and drops down at the base of the ladder, quickly punching up and nailing
Gumby right in the nads. Gumby falls off the ladder in pain. Heenin is slow to start his climb up and then Gumby in his pain kicks the ladder leg and it tilts, Heenin gets off and avoids real injury.
Gumby is still down when Heenin slams that ladder across his back and then uses it like a poker and starts to poke Gumby into rolling and falling out of the ring. Heenin lays the ladder down and then off the rope Baseball Slide Kicks it right when Gumby started to get up, the bottom step nails Gumby right in the forehead and knocks him back down.
Tely - " Now that was a telegraphed move if I ever saw one. " Maxx - " I didn't think Heenin had it in him, Marcos took a chance with this kid, his moves may earn him some respect in SXW after all.
" Heenin sets the ladder up and climbs it. Fans pretty much boo him the whole time then a cheer begins to build and build, Heenin looks just a bit to late to see Gumby outside the ring still, but with his own ladder and he uses it folder up and under the bottom rope as a '
POKER' and slams the legs of Heenin's ladder, you guessed it, the ladder tips over. But the laws of psyics says the ladder falls towards Gumby ... Heenin goes over the ropes and BodyPress Suicide Slams right on top of
Gumby, both men down and outside the ring, this is looking bad for the two involved. Maxx - " Oh jeez, that looked bad, I hope neither of them is injured? " Gumby is up first and he slides into the ring and sets up the ladder he put in. While he goes up, Heenin is revived as well and in the ring, he starts to climb up the other side of this ladder. Gumby and Heenin exchange blows while peering just over the top of the ladder, each fighting to get one more step higher then the other, finally both are waist high over the top of the ladder.
Tely - " That ladder is wobbling back and forth, it's going to fold ?! " Gumby and Heenin both now fight just to stay on the ladder as it's legs start to wiggle like jello and then it starts to tip, but Heenin and
Gumby both roll it 160 degrees, like walking on stilts and in circles to stay balanced. This goes from one end of the ring to the other ...
the other being nearest to the Fitzgerald league announcers. Maxx & Tely - " OH SHIT !!!!!!
