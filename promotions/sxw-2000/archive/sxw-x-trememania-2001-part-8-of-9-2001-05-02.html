referee - [Image] - Ernie Chang A lone wolf starts howling, before the first few bars of "Stairway to
Heaven" scream
through the speakers.The fans are on their feet, booing their heads off, as Whitesnake jumps from the scaffolding high above the arena, and lands hard in front of the
Xtreme-a-tron, slightly bending the metal. [Image] He walks to the ring, a look of evil on his face, pausing every couple of feet to stare down a fan, finally, he enters the ring, where he lifts his head back, and lets out a howl that makes the hair on the back of everyone's neck stands up.He removes his long leather trench coat, and prepares for battle.
Cheryl - " Now in the ring, from places unknown, he is .... WHITESNAKE !!!!!! " ' Dragula ' by Rob Zombie starts to play and fans begin to boo, but a faint section of Hex fans cheer, as Brandon Hex ... [Image] ... makes his stalking like walk into the arena, and checks out the fans, for food maybe, then he walks to the ring, almost undead like !
Cheryl - " Now making his way to the ring, from places unknown, the walking undead .. ... BRANDON HEX !!!!! " * * * The Bell Rings * * * Whitesnake laughs at his one armed quasi-undead opponent. Whitesnake simply owns Hex in the ring, he tosses him around like salad and then plants him over and over with moves like a Bulldog Lariat , a Flying
Somersault Neck
Breaker , a Belly to Back Powerbomb and his powerful Snake Bite- Underhook Powerbomb (Tiger Driver) but Brandon still kicks out of pin attempts. Whitesnake scores his Snake Venom- Spinning Cobra Clutch Sleeper Hold but nothing doing as Hex Mule Kicks a Low Blow and then nails a Jaws of Undeath-
Tilt-a-Whirl Head Scissors Takedown (Hurricanrana) , used mostly to flip opponents out of the ring. Hex is out also and tries to use his one arm to chair shot Whitesnake, who catches it and slams Hex across the back with it a few times. Whitesnake is tossing
Hex around
but missing when he tries further chair shots, finally it is knocked out of his grasp in a missed shot leading to the ring post deflection.
Hex then uses his agility to lure Whitesnake into the ring stairs and ring posts, over and over, but still gets knocked around the ring like a light weight from Japan.
Hex is falling back as Whitesnake moves forwards, relentless as he stalks his weakened prey, Hex is tossing things at Whitesnake left and ring, chairs, camera's, coffee mugs and finally a fast ring bell special which whacks Whitesnake right between the eyes, knocking him out cold, hex leaps on him after a few seconds of disbelief and wins to his and fans surprise.
* * * The Bell Rings * * * Cheryl - " ... and your winners via TKO pinfall.... " fans boo with a mild cheer Cheryl - " ... he is .......... BRANDON HEXXXXXXXXXX !!!!!!!!! " No point spread, Mikee B's call - Match time 8:42 minutes

[Image] YOU CAN NOT HALT ... THE MARCH OF TIME !!!!!!!! [Image] Cheryl - " Now making his way to the ring, leader of the Fallen One's and SXW chairmen, he is ..... AEON !!!! "
Aeon - " The moment of truth is upon us all folks, he cannot deny me any longer, it is time the fans found out who is taking all there money and booking these death matches because of some vendetta he has on his chip of a shoulder.... so come on out ...
coward !!!! " Fans are silent until they here GWAR ..." Jagermonstah ! ", then out walks the man, the myth, the legends.... the LEGION !!!!!!
[Image] Cheryl - " Now making his way to the ring, he is the 0wn3r of SXW and X-Treme Island .... he is .... LEGIONNNNNNN !!!!! " Aeon is shaking his head as Legion walks down the ramp, into the ring and right up to
Aeon's wheelchair.
* * * The Bell Rings * * * Aeon is shouting at Legion until ... Aeon is picked out of his wheelchair and slammed with a Belly to Belly Suplex and then a Pump Handle Slam and finally Aeon is driven to the mat in the Dominator (Pedigree) and placed into a Reverse Ankle Lock.
Aeon is thriving in pain as Legion tightens his hold, it seems the SXW 0wn3r was still able to take Aeon by surprise, but then fans get even more shocked when Aeon starts to stand up. Aeon kicks Legion in the face over & over until h is free from the grasp of Legion. Aeon's turn as he pummels Legion into a corner, works over his mid section and then starts in with the Shoulder Breaker and a Suplex into a
Powerslam(Jackhammer) Aeon is now walking around as if his legs where no damaged, like he was playing possum with the boss. Aeon & Legion lock into mortal kombat until finally electricity flies in all directions as Aeon rips both of Legion's arm right off.
Aeon uses Legion's arms like club, beating them against his chest and head, Legion is twitching and chunks of his synthetic alloy body rip off and drop to the mat, by the time Aeon is finished Legion looks like a worn and tattered rag.
Aeon drops the arms and grabs Legion in a Pump Handle Slam then lands a
Ravages of
Time- Leg Drop from the Top Rope and finishes this with his March of
Time- Reverse Sidewalk Slam(Reverse Rock Bottom), but Aeon does not cover Legion, instead he reaches down and starts to rip Legion's head off the beaten shell.
Aeon is finished, Legion is finished, his head in the palm of Aeon's hand as he holds it up to fans who cheer & boo, Aeon gets a microphone.
Aeon - " Folks, what you have just seen here tonight ... was nothing more then a ploy by the SXW 0wn3r to remain hidden while this half assed ROBOT of a legend comes out here to do his dirty work, well if that is how we must play this game, then this is how we shall play ... as of right now I am signing the 1st match for SXW
