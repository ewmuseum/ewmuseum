X-treme island, a state all in it's own with amnesty from all law and trade embargoes. The last things fans and the world knew about SXW and the island was that Damona had single handily defeated and killed them all,
[Image] .. save for a few who have been trapped on the island outside the Super
Dome , as it
is protected from intruder and from those who wish to leave, by a huge half sphere shaped force field placed on it by the omnipotent Damona.
The scene goes into the arena where the corpses of all the fallen SXW super starts still lay, untouched, the crowd seats are riddled with dead fans, announcers and all staff in the Super Dome also killed, many of these deaths are gruesome and unholy, not wished on any man or women or thing.
[Image] [Image][Image] Else where on the island, in a new building, an ancient castle which she has moved from its original location to be where he administrations building once stood, now gone, as if it was never there, Damona's powers know no bounds.
Inside she sits in her thrown, her minions stand by, waiting for her next step towards universal domination ....
[Image] Damona - " Ahhhh the power!!!!!!!!!..hahahahahaha." [Image] Damona - " The world got a small preview of what's going to happen but I think our fun with the SXW is not at a end yet."
Dark Wind leans over to her........... [Image] Dark Wind - " What do you plan all powerful goddess?" [Image] Damona - " It is Friday is it not ? (everyone sept the four horsemen nod) [Image] Damona - " So I feel like having my very own Friday Night Fury!!!!!!!!!"
[Image][Image][Image][Image][Image][Image] [Image] All of the Dark Elite laugh as they nod their heads in agreement to Damona's plan and soon Damona gestures for them to watch as a picture of X-Treme island is set before them.The island begins to glow soon the entire island begins to float up from the water into the sky above floating above the ocean.
The picture then goes to that of the island as all the buildings are removed and in their place is a massive coliseum is put in dead center of the island and in the center of the coliseum which soon like the island, with a chunk of earth under it, rips clean from the island separating itself from it !!!!
[Image] Inside the coliseum Damona and her minions all teleport into the main balcony for watching, Damona sits in her thrown which is also there the best st in the house ...
her horsemen and elite all stand or take seats by her side as Damona then raises her hand, the Ring of Infinity shines and in the blink of an eye, all the dad fans, all the dead staff, all the blood and chunks of brain left around the ring area and in the stands are suddenly all alive, back in place as if never torn apart or disintegrated.
[Image] They are startled until they see Damona ,the one who killed them once already, they all sit back down, half the seats are empty, the staff wonder what is going on asking
[Image] Tely & Styles [Image] who say Damona must be playing with them and this is her game, if we all do not want to die again, suggest we all play along.
Else where in the coliseum is a dungeon, where all of the fallen SXW roster appear ... [Image] ... All back, only a few changes, for one, none have any weapons, or powers, Damona has taken them away, but not the few titles they hold.
Other then that, [Image] Brandon Hex is human and so is [Image] Gomez, who is also ... alive, Hex is still missing his left arm and Gomez is still very much blind ... all the ones who tried to stop her and failed !
A handful still down on the island below, can only wonder what is going on up in the floating coliseum of Damona...
[Image] Damona -" Let Friday Night Fury begin...
