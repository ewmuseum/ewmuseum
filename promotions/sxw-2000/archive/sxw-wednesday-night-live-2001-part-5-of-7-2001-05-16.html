Full card and graphix at http://sxw.50megs.com/results/05_16_01_WNL.htm DAMONA vs. THE UNIVERSAL CELESTIALS [Image] Damona still in outer space, not aware of how things have unfolded on
X-Treme Island, due to her self indulgence in destroying an entire empire she thinks to herself ... " Can nobody stand against me ?! "
[Image] Voice - " We can Damona ! " As Damona turns she is no longer in the middle of the battle she just claimed victory in, but rather another place in space, the edge of the universe to be exact. Damona is surrounded by massive beings the like never seen before by mortal eyes. On the outside of the circle, beings who have existed since the dawn of time, stand over a galaxy tall each and make Damona look like a grain of salt, they are the Prime Celestials.
Inside the circle closer to Damona not only in distance but size are more commonly known celestials, some thought of only as myth and folk lore, the gods .. Norse, Japanese, Chinese, Indian, Greek, Roman, you name it they are there in all forms, there is the roman god of fire and with him the greek god of fire and so on, no boundries, no beliefs to divide them.
Ego, the living planet who is in itself an entire world, stands next to it's greatest enemy, Galactus, the planet eater who only exist to feed on entire worlds, he has killed trillions in the process, but now Damona could prove to be far more destructive.
Other beings known across the universe by the names Death, Hope, Mercy,
Eternity, Oblivion, Fate, Chaos & Order, even the several incarnations of the devil himself, his sons, his daughters, his higher archey and all his enemies of the light, unitied for one cause, to stop Damona, while they can.
The powers strike, a blast so powerful and destructive it could have collapsed the galaxy there are in, simply absorbed by Damona and fired back at them. Several beings perish in the backlash, entities that can never die, do and the stakes just got higher.
Galactus fires his powerful eye beams at Damona, she disappears and the blast destroys Thor god of thunder. Chaos & Order are wipped from existance with a mere touch as Damona appears between them. Ego the living planet then swollows Damona whole, all stop and space is silent again.
Suddenly Ego the living planet looks like he just ate a bad million mile long burrito when he explodes into billions of small chunks of dirty and clay, Damona floats in the middle unharmed after just blasting an entire planet to bits. The gods attack, from all fronts, Damona looks to be in trouble but when all is said and done .. hundereds of once immortal corpses float all around her and she holds the heads of Zues & Odin in her hands, laughing at them all.
Death is the last minor entity left besides the Prime Celestials, Damona has destroyed them all, even Mephisto and all of Hells most powerful monarchs. Damona speaks to Death.(Note- Death here is aka the grim reaper not ahala.)
[Image] Damona - " And even you oppose me, when I can give you all the dead your non existing heart desires, the whole universe delivered to you and all you have to do is sit back and count the heads that roll in ...
why, of all of them why you ?! " [Image] Death - " Because Damona ... without life there can be no death ! " Death strikes at Damona, the lethal heart strike that non has ever survived .. Damona laughs it off.
[Image] Damona - " So be it ! " You know when the entity known as Death, dies, that cannot be good ... ... Damona still a float in her bone garden, all the most powerful forces to exist, now dead to all, lifeless in space, Damona looks around and then like a speck of salt she looks up to one of the Prime Celestials ...
[Image] Damona - " Just bring it ! " As the Prime Celestials mentally start there attack on Damona, the melee is so strange and intense, that mere words cannot do it justice, only the end result can give hint to the power of Damona.
As the all powerful Damona drifts back towards earth, taking her time, enjoying her univrse, she never looks back once at the mess she left behind, as the corpses of gods and prim entities float around a huge, huge wall, forged out of the Celestials, who Damona could have destroyed, maybe, but instead she imprissoned them by morphin all there bodies into each other and creating the huge Source Wall.
Without the Prime Celestials there would be nothing ness, so as to not oppose Damona, she trapped them forever in a wall made by there own bodies, there expressionless faces pushing out of the walls sides, looking outwards into the vastness of space, but never able to move freeely again, stable on the edge of the universe, for all eternity.
[Image] SXW: FOREVER INTERLUDE As the being known as The Master of Time, Anti-Aeon sits in his time line monitoring chambers punders over his current quest to rid the earth of the
Monarch, a man from 2002 who turns on his friends and morals to rule the world as he sees fit, with regular humans been deemed weak and executed at birth if not refugees found much later and the end for them is still the same.
[Image] Anti-Aeon does not view his monitors, which are open to outrages time peroids, he thinks of bigger fish,
[Image] to Logan Eagle, while he sent back in time, and has yet to return , something is wrong but he does not know what, [Image] Damona's current reign of power was never history in his time peroid which is currently
2099, nor was the real identity of the Monarch.
[Image] Anti-Aeon thinks back to the day he saw himself through anothers eyes, a victims eyes, his own eyes, as he to, powerful both super natural and business was still fresh meat to a ruthless monster .. named Whitesnake
... who tortured Aeon, and didn't even ask him any questions .. just caused him pain ... caused him to question his life and past deeds and to make up for them .. until the end of time !
