Harvey - " Bring all the fancy magic powers you want and whatever weapons you want but at the end of the night you'll feel even more dead than you look.

Harvey - " Now, bring out Coolman "I'm a sausage-jockey who can't touch myself as well as I'd like with a finger missing" P ... for our match of epic proportions, I hope you're watching Damien, this'll happen to you next Friday. "
'The Real Slim Shady' by Eminem starts to play and CP's video reel starts .... [Image] as CoolmanP walks to the ring. He jumps to the 2nd turnbuckle, and puts up his fist as a pyro goes off.
[Image] He gets down and waits for the match to start. Cheryl - " Now in the ring, will you please stand up , for he is the one, the only .. REAL ....... COOLMAN........ PPPPPPPP !!!! " * * * The Bell Rings * * * Things starts as a wrestling match where CoolmanP has the fans and the moves, he gets in his 3/4 Face Lock Bulldog (Stunner) and The People's Kick- Thrust
Kick(superkick) but Ben Harvey is hard-core and gets up from both finishers. Ben Harvey uses his Gutwrench Suplex and CoolmanP rolls out of the ring. Outside the ring Harvey scores a Press Slam Back Breaker landing CP across the announcers table, then a Elbow Drop from a chair and cover.
CoolmanP is able to kick out but is quickly nailed with a few chair shots, he still kicks out of another pin. Ben Harvey then wraps electrical cords around
Coolman's neck
and cuts off his air.
CoolmanP is out of it when Ben Harvey does a Harvey Wallbanger- Full Nelson Slam to CP and onto two folded out chairs then pins him in the mesh of steel. * * * The Bell Rings * * * Cheryl - " ... and your winner via pinfall in this hard-core match ...... " fans boo Cheryl - " ... he is ...... BEN HARVEY !!!!!!!!!!! " No point spread, Mikee B's call - Match time 8:01 minutes
After the match, Ben Harvey was not gonna leave just yet. He grabs a chair and waited for CoolmanP to stand up and then delivered three stiff chair shots to the back of
CoolmanP's head. Ben Harvey was still not finished he rolled CP into the ring, around the ring post with a Double Leg Grab & Post Low Blow, twice, then a Figure Four Leg
Lock Around the Ring Post, SXW staff had to pull Harvey off of CoolmanP who then later was carried out on a stretcher by EMT's with a dislocated hip joint and knee.
