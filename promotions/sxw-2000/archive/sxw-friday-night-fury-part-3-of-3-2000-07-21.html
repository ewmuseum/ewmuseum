referee - " 1...2.." Ahala kicks out. Styles - " Ewwwwwww, I thought it was over right there ! Damona rolls and now she is pinning Ahala ! referee - " 1.....2.." Ahala turns it around again and now is pinning Damona ! referee - " 1....2.. " Damona again twist the hold around, but this time into a figure four leg lock, which Ahala breaks after a few seconds !
Ahala is lifted to her feet by her hair and into a spinning hair drag take down, then another one, on the third attempt Ahala nails a somersault DDT turning the toss around on Damona, who plays possum her self to snare Ahala into her Visions- Crossface Chickenwing with Body Scissors, in the middle of the ring, Ahala resist for near two minutes, but passes out from the sheer pain.
