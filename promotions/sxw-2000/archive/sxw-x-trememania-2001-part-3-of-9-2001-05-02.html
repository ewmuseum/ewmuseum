Defender or Bull against either HKK or Wildthing. Hardkore Kid held his own much better then Wildthing did who took most of the pins and needed HKK to save his ass.
Defender was in and nailed Wildthing with a Final Justice- Gorilla Press into a Chokeslam but Hardkore Kid risked a DQ to give Defender the Kore-Anium- Flying headbutt off the top rope to prevent another pin. Raging Bull was in against Hardkore Kid and it was an impressive melee but ended with
Hardkore Kid taking in order a two Raging Gore- Double Shoulder
Tackle(Spear) and then a Raging Charge- Running Crossbody Splash in the Corner(Ho Train) but when he nailed that Wildthing clocked him with the SXW Hard-core title and the referee did not see it.
Raging Bull yanked Wildthing into the ring over the ropes with one hand and toss him across the ring, this then knocked out the referee who Wildthing landed on.
Raging Bull had HKK pinned for well over three but no referee. As Defender tried to get the referee out from under Wildthing suddenly, from the rafters above the ring just atop the hell in the cells down flew
Piranha ... [Image] Now a vampire he was here to cheat his former stable mates out of tag team gold, by nailing Bull with his Tropical Plunge- Belly to Belly Pump Handle
Brainbuster Suplex and put Bull down, just as Defender had gotten the referee to come around, Piranha then slipped away back the same way he came as neither Defender nor the referee where wise to his attack.
Hardkore Kid nearly sucker pinned Bull but he kicked out. Defender however never to back in, though he tried, now the referee was being stupid and stopping him while allowing the sneaky Duo to double team Raging Bull several times and finally scoring their The Deadly Double- A Double 3/4 Front Face Lock Bulldog ( Stunner )
and Hardkore Kid made a three count pin to win this one. * * * The Bell Rings * * * Cheryl - " .. and your winner via single fall pin.....
