Cyric opens with a chop, Grimm fires one back, Cyric with another and Grimm is pushed back, but still returns fire, a third chop and Grimm is flung in to the ropes, Cyric follows with a Knee Lift and then a Choke Toss in to the corner where Cyric starts to pummel
Ryan Grimm. Tely - " Cyric is pure power folks, there is nothing funny about that ! " Cyric points to Lucas Myphis as he nails Ryan Grimm with the Inferno's Snare- Rock Bottom and cover. referee - " ...1.. ....2. .... " Lucas Myphis pulls Cyric out of the ring by his leg. Once Cyric is out he lands on his feet and is looking right at Myphis and then
'POW', he smacks him in the jaw and Lucas is out cold, in the air and landing flat on his back.
Styles - " Cyric just laid Lucas Myphis out cold and in out very first match ! " Tely - " Something tells me we are going to see that happen allot more in SXW ! " Ryan Grimm from across the ring does a Running Baseball Slide Kick and under the ropes hitting Cyric in the back of his head.
Outside the ring all legal and Ryan Grimm is nailing right and left hooks, Cyric is popped around left to right but then out of nowhere he catches a punch and then catches the second one also, then Cyric uses his strength to crush the hands of Ryan Grimm, who falls to his knees in pain.
Styles - " Cyric is in complete control here ... " Tely - " Hold on just a second folks, guess who just showed up in the arena ? " Theo Blitz gets a pop as he runs out from the entrance and down towards the ring. The Destroyer of Perfection, Theo Blitz runs around the ring and behind the unsuspecting Cyric, who takes a nasty shot to his stomach when Theo hits him with a Sledge Hammer !
Styles - " Chaos at ring side folks ! " Both Cyric & Ryan Grimm are down, Lucas Myphis is yelling to the back for somebody to come down and just as Theo Blitz is walking backwards up the ramp, Johnny Grimm is seen running by him towards the ring.
Tely - " First Theo Blitz, and now this, what are the Grimm's going to do and where is Jake Styles ? "
The referee is checking Cyric & Ryan Grimm when Lucas Myphis distracts him, and as that happens Johnny Grimm switches places with his twin brother Ryan who rolls under the ring to hide.
Styles - " What's this, a switch-a-roo at ringside !? " Johnny Grimm is 100% and lifts Cyric to his feet, Lucas Myphis is done distracting and tells the referee to turn around, just then he sees
Ryan Grimm walking Cyric and punching him as they climb the ring steps, then Ryan Grimm signals for his finisher and moves into a mighty The Grimm Crusher- Ace Crusher, but Cyric was playing possum, in mid move he reverses and delivers a Jaw Jacker to
Grimm. Tely - " Holy crap, Cyric countered, Cyric countered ! " Cyric has the stunned Grimm still standing on the ring stairs as Cyric lifts him over his shoulder and then Cyric climbs up the stairs and right up the turn buckle with a 300 pounder slung over his shoulder.
Tely - " Cyric possesses amazing strength, I cannot believe my eyes ! " (screaming) Lucas - " WHAT ARE YOU DOING ... WHAT IS HE DOING !!! " " REFEREE THIS IS ILLEGAL, YOU CANNOT LET HIM DO THAT, STOP HIM ... STOP HIM ! " Lucas is pulling hair out of his head as Cyric now signals the fans who cheer him on as he then slams Johnny Grimm with a Touch of
Cain- Front Faced Suplex from Top Rope. Fans go crazy as Cyric rolls over, stunned but to his feet and walking around in a circle, he gets his bearings and drops into a cover and leg hook. Theo Blitz still stands on the entrance ramp watching and is not to happy right now.

Grimm's grab there head and fall in pain, Cyric is out of the ring to celebrate his victory and Theo Blitz has gone backstage as the bell rang.
Cheryl - " .. and your winner via pin fall .... " Cheryl - " ... he is ..... " Cheryl - " ...... CYRIC !!!!!!!!! THE DESTROYER OF PERFECTION THEO BLITZ vs. JOHNNY GRIMM Standard Match
15 minute time limit referee - - Phillip Doober Lights go out! Red flashes of light go out across the arena, as Theo Blitz descends from the rafters when his music hits! Cheryl - " Now in the ring, he is THE DESTROYER OF PERFECTION .... THEO BLITZ !!!!! " Cheryl - " ..... and his opponent ..... " ' Ace of Spades ' by Motorhead starts to erupt throughout the arena as Johnny Grimm ? makes his appearance and walks to the ring. Cheryl - " Now in the ring, one third of The X-Treme
All Stars .... .... he is .... JOHNNY GRIMM!!!!!!!! " * * * The Bell Rings * * * Again start with both men circling the ring, staring each other down. Johnny Grimm makes the first move as Lucas Myphis near ring side of course is shouting for him to punch.
Theo Blitz also hears the orders and is able to duck a hand chop and lift Grimm in to a Neck Breaker quickly scoring a power move,
Theo makes no cover and instead give it up for the fans.
Styles -" Wow, Johnny Grimm dropped quicker then his brother against Cyric ! " Theo Blitz lets Johnny Grimm stand up and then Theo starts to jook & jive him with punches and set up for The Van Daminator but
Johnny Grimm ducks the kick and lifts Theo Blitz right up into a
Pumphandle Slam and then he gets Theo again with a Spine
Buster off the ropes and then Grimm makes the first cover.
referee - " ..1.... ....2.. .. " Theo Blitz gets his shoulder up and Johnny simply lifts Theo's head up and punches him a few times and back into a over.
referee - " ..1.... ....2.. .. " Again Theo gets his shoulder up. Lucas - " Give him another Pump Handle ! " Johnny Grimm lifts Theo back up for another Pump Handle Slam but this time Theo drops out behind Grimm and lifts him into a
T-Bone Suplex, then an Irish Whip off the ropes setting Johnny
Grimm up for yet another Van Daminator.
Styles - " Ewww, that was a nice shot and now Theo is going to the ropes. " Theo top ropes waiting for Johnny Grimm and then Lucas Myphis is up on the ring apron, referee again distracted, when suddenly ...
.. Cyric climbs out from under the ring. Styles -" How the hell did Cyric get under the ring ?! " As Theo Blitz stands up for his top rope move, Cyric leaps up and chops his left leg, this puts Theo off balance and he falls off crotch first on the ropes and then flips and lands outside of the ring.
Cyric is clearly seen as the referee turns around. Cyric calmly walks up the ramp and stands at the top watching just like Theo did in his match.
Lucas Myphis is yelling for Johnny Grimm to get up and take advantage and when the referee has his back turned looking over the ring ropes at Theo Blitz, that is when Ryan Grimm comes running out and slides into the ring just as his twin brother slips out near the ramp side of the ring.
Tely - " Folks, I cannot believe what we are seen, the Grimm's have just pulled yet another switch-a-roo ! "
Ryan Grimm pushes the unsuspecting referee to the side and reaches over the ropes grabbing Theo Blitz by his hair and pulling him back into the ring just so he can take a Fallaway Slam and then Grimm performs a Asia Moonsault and cover.
referee - " ...1. .... ..2. ....." Theo Blitz kicks out at the last possible moment. Ryan Grimm is in a rage as he lifts Theo Blitz up and into The Grimm Gutter- Gutwrench Powerbomb leaving Theo down mid ring so Grimm can go up high and try his Five Star Splash but Theo
Blitz rolls out of the way and quickly back to cover Grimm. referee - " ...1... ......2.. .... " Ryan Grimm kicks out just in time, so Theo then twists Grimm and locks him into a Sharpshooter and Grimm is in pain, but as he resists Lucas Myphis assists and pulls him to the ropes causing
Theo Blitz to break even though it's a no DQ match. Styles -" Yeah good call, Johnny ... I mean Ryan was not going to tap with rope leverage ! "
Theo Blitz stomps Ryan Grimm and then scores his The Skull Crusher- Pedigree. Theo Blitz then goes to the top ropes and shows Lucas Myphis how a Five
Star Splash is really done and then covers his tag team stud.
referee - " ..1.... ....2. ....... 3.. " * * * The Bell Rings * * * Cyric begins to clap, Lucas Myphis is enraged and enters the ring, trying to strike down Theo Blitz with a cane, but Blitz nails Lucas with
The Van Daminator and puts him out, turning to see Cyric clapping for him. Cheryl - " .. and your winner via pin fall .... " Cheryl - " ... he is ..... " Cheryl - " ...... THE DESTROYER OF PERFECTION ... THEO BLITZ !!!!!!!!!
