Full card and graphix at http://sxw.50megs.com/results/05_16_01_WNL.htm BACK ON X-TREME ISLAND .. LET THE SHOW BEGIN .. In the absense of Damona, the Dark Elite & The Four Horsemen of Damona are running the show, booking mis-matches and stacking the odds in their favor. In the colisium the fans are forced back in the aisles, while
Damona's storm trooper search the island for tonights special guests, including .. Sleet, Frost, Hardkore Kid, Crazy Noodle Boy, Over Fiend and
SXW One World Champion Cyric, who will defend up to two times tonight, if he survives the first stipulations and deadly opponents.
In the private balcony of Damona her thrown left empty as she is not there to fill it, next to hers is Dark Winds, he sits and rules the game today, his mistresses SXW Light Weight title hanging over the arms of nboth throwns.
Also in the balcony are The Red Raven & Whitesnake, the SXW World Tag Team champions, they have been given gifts by Damona, the managers of the
Trinity ... Trish who Raven has marked with his RR initials and Lita, who is beaten and bruised by Whitesnake who tests her threshold for pain.
[Image] Dark Wind - " Let the entertainment begin ... " Death grabs Sleets SXW Cruiser Weight title as the Storm troopers usher not only Sleet into the ring, but also his partner and all former team mates in the Hand, Frost will be the special referee, as the Dark Elite laugh in smug supremesy !
On thing to note, while the entire island is under a power dampening field, in this colisium, in the arena zone and aisles including the balcony, all powers work, the field has a hole in it for gladiators to use in battle as weapons, this will come in handy soon enough ....
SLEET vs. DEATH To the Death Title Match - no time limit [Image] [Image] XWF Cruiser Weight Championship Title Belt [Image] Referee - [Image] - Frost The theme from Dragon Ball Z begins to play as Blue lasers and pyro's light up the entrance way and once Sleet appears he blows smoke from his mouth.
[Image] Cheryl - " Now in the ring, the storm before the blizzard .... he is ..... SLEET !!!!! " ' Pulling Teeth ' begins to play as Death makes her way to the ring
[Image] Cheryl - " Now making her way to the ring, she is part of the four horsemen of Damona ... . .. she is ...... DEATH !!!!!!!!! " * * * The Bell Rings * * * Death held onto the SXW Cruiser Weight title to force Sleet her former stablemate into this to the Death match, for a strap already his by rights.
Death was trying to grab Sleet but his high flying ability was able to avoid her strikes, even when Death started to use her symbiotic battle suit to make sharp stabbing weapons, and Sleet even snuck in a 3/4 Face
Lock Bulldog ( Diamond Cutter version ) and his tag partner and match referee made a three count ...
.. but Dark Wind informed him to win meant to the death. The match continued until Sleet was getting sliced up and forced to use his powers, blasting Death with a Kame Hame Ha- A huge beam of energy that can knock his opponent out( Dragon Ball Z Style ) but the Horseman was strong enough to still stand.
Frost jumped in when Death almost had the perfect kill shot on Sleet, he grabbed her arm and was back handed, then Death turned to him, when suddenly Frost started to use his powers, making a misty snow storm around the ring, Death lost focus and then took a Kame Hame Ha- A huge beam of energy that can knock his opponent out( Dragon Ball Z Style )
right in her face, temperarily blinding her. As Death whipped around trying to regain her vision Frost & Sleet started to both use their powers, blending them together and starting to form a shield of ice. As Frost contunied to sculpt the shield Sleet was blind sided by Death's hand, he was knocked down next to the shield which was more and more looking like a snow man only more realistic then most.
Death had then regained some vision, enough to spot her target as she extracted two large blades from each of her wrists, then turning to the ring she swung both hands together, making each blade slice through her opponent .. to the death !
As the snow man crables just after Death stabbed into it and assumed she had won her match, killed her former friend and that's about all the further she gets, with her vision still impaired, her rage had taken a hold of her and she thinks she just killed Slteet, the match says that is how you win, but inside this Horseman still beats the heart of a human who is struck with over welming regret.
In rage and realizing what she has become, Ahala or Death as she is called now, retracts large metal wings from her shoulder blades, lets out a yell .. " NOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO !!!!!! " And then flies straight up in the air, breaking through the Super Dome roof and flying off perhaps never to be seen again.
* * * The Bell Rings * * * Ralphy - " ..... and your winner via no contest ...... " Fans cheer ! Ralphy - " ... and still your SXW Cruiser Weight Champion ...... " Ralphy - " ... he is ...... SLEEEEEEEEEEET !!!!!!!! " 75 to 102 point spread in Sleets favor - match time 7:04 minutes
Just after the announcement Sleet stands up from behind the ice pile.
Sleet - " Well, looks like our plan worked, if she doesn't snap out of
Damona's control, at least she will not stnad against us any longer ! "
Frost - " Yeah, unless she realizes we tricked her and then we are both totally dead ! " Dark Wind - " The Trinity is it ... seems Damona gave your wenches to the SXW World Tag Team Champions, but since you cheated Death so to speak .. You will face The Red Raven & Whitesnake not only for the titles, but for the wenches as well ... until then learn your place and learn well ice manipulators ... you have no true power ! "
Sleet is finally handed his SXW Cruiser Weight title, fans cheer like it was their last day on earth and then the troopers usher the Trinity out of the ring by Dark Wind's orders and the next match gets under way !
