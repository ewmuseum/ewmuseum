Full card and graphix at http://sxw.50megs.com/results/05_16_01_WNL.htm [Image][Image][Image] Wednesday Night Live May 16th 2001 On Today's Edition: Post X-TremeMania 2001 & Friday Night Fury Recap. Damona vs. The Domination Sleet vs. Death in a to the Death Title Match HKK vs. CNB vs. Pestilence for Hard-core gold Cyric & Fiend vs. War & Whitesnake OWC title with heavy stipulations Damona vs. The Celestials Mikee B & Crippler Calhoun with SXW / WVW Secret Origins: Featuring Over Fiend A Blast from the Past : THE END of the XWF timeline SXW: POST X-TREMEMANIA 2001 RECAP [Image] [Image] Well it seems not so long ago Loci and the Inferno was going to kill us all, but then along came Damona, having paid very little attention to her questing the globe for the Ring of Frost could lead to this, let alone the warnings of Dark Cyric that where also over looked have come true.
[Image] [Image] Damona gain all the sister rings to her prized Ring of Frost into the
Gauntlet of Orion, once all the rings where locked into place she was bestowed the Ring of Infinity and so far her power since seems unending as she kills and revives who she sees fit for more punishment.
[Image] [Image] In the course of her quest for legends from days of old or the XWF timeline where changed into Horsemen, hounds to do Damona's bidding ..
Ahala became Death the leader, Brimstone became War, perhaps the most powerful for sure destructive [Image][Image][Image][Image]
Post became Famine and still carries a child in her womb and finally
Mentally Insane, who is now Pestilence and he brings pain to all Damona's enemies.
[Image][Image][Image][Image] In their quest they found all four rings, each held by a protector who was oblivious to having such a powerful piece of a might prize around their finger, invisible until removed, only by death or dis membering the finger can this be done, and it cost, CoolmanP, Frost, Lugg-Nutt and a man from
WVW known as Marcos, a parlor magician and escape artist but he could not get away scott free from the Horsemen who road into WVW and took what they came for.
[Image][Image][Image][Image] Damona has other allies in her quest, Dark Wind the evil brother to
Kamikaze, who Damona has an obsession with and uses Kamikaze's brother for more then dirty work, well two kinds of dirty work anywise. Whitesnake a victim to his own hunger for blood and supreme control over his prey,
Whitesnake was actually once part of The Elements of Destruction, able to half control the animal within thanks to Zeebo, who was thought dead during the Inferno, but has since re-appeared. The Red Raven, once part of the XWF timeline elite and history or whatever repeats itself as The Red
Raven went from hero to horror, perfect for the New Elite and the power that comes along with it.
[Image][Image][Image] Now, Damona is all powerful, she single handily destroyed all life in the
SXW Super Dome at the end of X-TremeMania 2001, all who where brave enough to stand against her, fell, heroes, villains, people who where just there to see a good show, killed for her amusement and pleasure, as she abuses her new found omnipotence and holds all of the SXW roster hostage on the now floating X-Treme Island.
The world can only watch and hope, as Damona keeps them out or the super stars in with a powerful force field, allowing them to roam the island, without their powers and most communications cut off.
Damona held her own Friday Night Fury show, making the matches and forcing her enemies to fight each other, in many cases to the death. Those not willing to oblige her commands where forced to do so or killed for trying to resist. Now she has held them captive for nearly 1 week. Left a score of dead souls in her wake and with no end in sight of her downfall, Damona plans to stop all threats in her path to universal conquest ..
.. Powerful alien races will not allow Damona to rule them, whole worlds will fight against her, even the mighty alien alliance the Dominion will be forced to stop their galactic empire from expanding to stop her, or they will having nothing for themselves to conquer and claim as Dominion territory.
But more powerful beings will also stand against Damona .. they are known to all races by many names, but all are thought only as myth or hope of something higher in life then mere mankind, they are celestials, ranging from the incarnation of death herself, to the very 1st beings to ever exist, the Prime Celestials, who stand a whole galaxy tall each, looking over all, to the race of Watchers, sworn to never interfere, but only observe, now for the 1st time in a millennium, they all agree they must act, they must interfere, to the gods of myth .. Norse, Greek, roman,
Japanese, African, to the beings called Nightmare, Hope, Destiny, Love, even Hate, all have something to lose if Damona is not stopped, even Death who would finally have all the life force ever to exist in her play pen can see how Damona's power and ego mis-balance the order of all that is
.... .. Damona, now omnipotent senses the threat to her power, she holds earth, know for its super naturals and ability to over come impossible odds, already in her grasp, she toys with earth but moves into space, to stake her claim on all and destroy any who oppose her will ...
[Image] DAMONA vs. THE DOMINION Damona leaves X-Treme island, allowing only her self to exit the force field as she flies high into the sky and then teleports herself into the vastness of outer space, in search of her first prey, she will attack the
Dominion and Destroy them, if the Celestials are unwise enough to attack her after the Dominion falls and as Earth is held in her grasp, then she will not ignore them any longer and they shall all perish or serve under her will, weather they like it or not !!!
Far from the Milky Way galaxy fly a fleet of Dominion world invader space crafts, massive in size, numerous in numbers, the ships are stocked with several alien races
.. the Krillions [Image] , the Benzites [Image], the Sleethians [Image], the Nebullians [Image] .. four powerful races, each with their own homeworld allied in the Dominion, along with the field leaders, the
Zzzaaxxxians, high up in the ranks is Voivod, former SXW super star and current galactic conqueror, along with the strangely unique entity known as Syrinx the only one of it's kind, and Scorpio Bliss [Image], a lofty pirate who sold out her own homeworld to the Dominion, all of whom allied with the Dominion serve under a almost mythological races, some even considered gods, the Primemortals [Image].. none of which have ever been seen, living on some secret world and they rule the Dominions, but on this day, their grasp on the universe closes as Damona appears on front of the prime attack fleet, headed by [Image] Voivod & Syrinx.[Image]
Zzzaaxxxian - " Sir, a single energy signature just appeared in front of us, it's reading is off the scale, but sensors are picking up nothing and visual cannot confirm target, it's smaller then a lifepod ! "
[Image] Voivod - " Impossible, nothing in existence is that small and that powerful ... visual, magnify .... closer .... closer ... damn it you fool, magnify by 100, it's out there somewhere ... "
Just then, on the huge monitor the energy signature is seen ... .... Damona, she smiles . [Image] Inside the main cruiser Voivod is enraged as he orders all ships to lock on target and open fire, at the same moment Syrinx the most powerful next to Damona herself, teleports out in space, next to her, engaging in battle, blinding energy blasts from all angles and then a alien scream is heard even n space a Damona uses her hands to rip the entity's very fabric into shreds like a paper towel.
[Image] Voivod - " OPEN FIRE!!!!!!!! " The huge vessels open fire and a barrage of laser fire flies right at
Damona, she again smiles as it all is absorbed into her energy field around her body.
[Image] Voivod - " Impossible !!!! " Damona then fires the laser blasts back, going right into the cannon barrels and into the ships hauls, in seconds several ships explode from within, others lose power or engines, the mother ship untouched.
[Image] Voivod - " FIRE TORPEDO'S !!!!!!! " The ships that can fire torpedo shells at Damona do, the target seekers fly in spirals as they all soon impact on Damona's location ... the blast is blinding, nearly a minute passes as the wait to see if Damona is finished .... the could gone and Damona still stands in mid space, she now holds up her finger and signals to BRING IT !
[Image]Voivod - " FIRE, FIRE EVERYTHING ... NOTHING CAN SURVIVE THAT ... NOTHING .... ! " Damona no longer humors the Dominion, she reflect all torpedoes back into their launching bays, more ships explode from within, while laser fire is simply deflected, some hit targets and do damage, mainly to the mother ship which by the end is all that is left of the Dominion fleet, power off, weapons fried, crew nearly all dead but a handful of bridge staff and the captain.
[Image] Voivod - " Computer .. open hailing channel ... computer .. COMPUTER ..... ! " [Image] Rancor Varn- " Captain ... we must abandon ship .. tell the
Primemortals before it is too late ! "
[Image] Warchild - " Screw that, lets just get the hell out of hereee..... " As Warchild turns to exit the bridge she bumbs right into Damona ... [Image] Damona - " Going some place ?! " Warchild falls dead to the floor, after damona shovers her hand into her stomach and grabs some guts, pullig them out. Voivod leaps at Damona and is caught in mid air via a choke hold, while Rancor Varn tries to grab his energy pistal is simply atomized by Damona's mental force of command.
[Image] Damona - " Pity, I thought you being the supreme humanoid force in the universe would prove more of a challenge, but alas, that is something
I shall never have again ! "
Voivod is trying to claw at her but as she speaks, he never hears the end of her wisdom, she twists her wrist and digs her fingers deep into his iron skinned neck and kills him, he drops dead as she lets go, laughing and looking around at the dead bodies laying around the bridge, the last craft of the mighty Dominion left a drift in deep space as Damona teleports just outside of the craft and observes the scraps that float around, so massivewas her path of destruction even stars are blocked out by bebrees.
