Full card and graphix at http://sxw.50megs.com/results/05_04_01_FNF.htm Back on the other floating chunk of earth, in the coliseum ... the Orc guards grabs
Fire out of his holding cell with DRI and take him to his match against
.. Whitesnake. As the Orc guards stand by, Omega, Kamikaze, Cyric, Curio, Piranha, Bull
& Rage have
all figured out how to signal each other and devise an escape plan, each then reveals the plan to the others in the holding cells.
Meanwhile .... WHITESNAKE vs. FIRE [Image] [Image] [Image] Hard-core Match no time limit [Image] referee - [Image] - Larry Segal Much like down on the island in the parking lot, the original intro bits are gone, but this time a mighty trumpet of horns echoes the introduction of a champion as they walk out into what is now very much like the roman gladiator arena, only with a wrestling ring in the middle and allot of technical stuff hanging around, it's a weird mix of the distant past and present, even a sky dome !
* * * The Bell Rings * * * Whitesnake & Fire where matched even, but after the wild side of Whitesnake cut lose, Fire was losing the battle. Whitesnake slams Fire with his Snake Bite- Underhook Powerbomb (Tiger Driver) and then after a few to many trash can lids to the mask and head. Fire takes a lethal Fury Swipes- Lights in the arena disappear, when they return, a heavily breathing Whitesnake stands over a totally knocked out opponent.
* * * The Bell Rings * * * The Red Raven - " .... and your winner by destroying his opponent ..... " Fans forced to cheer against their will by the power of Damona and the Infinity Ring ! The Red Raven - " .... The mighty and powerful ..... WHITESNAKE !!!!!!!! " Match time 5:01 minute After the match Fire is taken from the arena by Orc guards and brought back to his cell, they then remove The Jackal from his cell and tell him to get his titles, they then lead him to the arena by force, although he willingly goes with them.
Kamikaze signals that once Bull & Omega fight is when they will make the move to escape. But that move is just a ruse to fool Damona into dropping her guard, even more so when they make the real attempt during Kamikaze & Dark Winds match which may distract her enough for this to work, but what is the plan ?!
Meanwhile Over Fiend is giving the One armed Brandon Hex a really hard time, Fiend is separated from the Reavers, he acts like his old self and picking on the weakest one of all, and Brandon is already in enough self pity.
[Image][Image][Image] Gomez cannot believe he is alive, he has trouble remembering his past and how he died, he remembers Hex telling him that Cyric(dark) killed him and Hex saved him from eternal slumber. Gomez is not to happy about the whole Plague thing, but at least he is now alive and can fight the good fight again.
