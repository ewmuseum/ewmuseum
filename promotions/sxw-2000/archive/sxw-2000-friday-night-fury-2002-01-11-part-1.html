THE WOLF vs. JUSTICE SCOTT Standard Match
15 minute time limit Cheryl - " The following match is the standard for all SXW standard matches, pinfalls and submissions in the ring, wretlers can be DQ'd or counted out of the ring. This is a non title match and has a 15 minute time limit."
referee - - Phillip Doober ' and Justice for All ' by Metallica starts to play and soon after Frank
Justice wheels out in his wheel chair and fans begin to cheer. As he passed down the ramp pyros begin to explode and soon Scott
Justice enters the arena to a huge fan pop.
Cheryl - " ... Now making his way to the ring lead by his brother ... ... he is ..... SCOTT JUSTICE !!!!!!! " Cheryl - " ... and his opponent .... " The opening riff of 'Seek and Destroy' by Metallica blares through the building and the images of a wolf appears on all monitors around the arena.
Scott Justice stands in the ring with his arms crossed as most of the intro plays but Wolf does not show up. As Justice is looking down the entrance ramp, the referee starts a 10-count or else Wolf forfeits the match
referee - " ... 1 ..... " referee - " ... 2 ..... " referee - " ... 3 ..... " Maxx - " Well so much for The WOlf, is he doing this to piss of my father ? " referee - " ... 4 ..... " Tely - " I think that sneak attack by Scott Justice may have put The Wolf out of action for real ?! "
referee - " ... 5 ..... " referee - " ... 6 ..... " Suddenly the lights go out in the building. Maxx - " What the ? " When the lights come on, Wolf is standing behind Justice. As Wolf swings Justice around, he hits him with a Sledgehammer.
Tely - " My god, that was a nasty shot from The Wolf, maybe you wish he didn't show up after all aye Maxx ?! "
Maxx - " Are first match has already gone to hell ?! " * * * The Bell Rings * * * Wolf continues his assault until Justice, then throws him out of the ring. The Wolf does a Suicide Dive through the ropes Goring right into Scott Justice, both men go down.
Referee finally starts an out of ring count when Scott Justice gets up first and starts beating on Wolf's cast .
Tely - " hat's the arm Scott Justice already injured !" As the cast shatters, a brace is revealed and Wolf gets up. Justice sets up Wolf for Final Justice- Fireman's Carry into a
Brainbuster, but Wolf counters it with a Diamond Cutter. Wolf sets up Justice for the Dream Catcher - Suplex (Jackhammer like) into a Powerbomb but his knee gives out, and Wolf rolls out of the ring.
Justice gets up on the turnbuckle and showboats for the crowd. When he comes off the turnbuckle, he sees Wolf has crawled back into the ring. As Justice sets Wolf up for another Final Justice- Fireman's
Carry into a Brainbuster, but Justice did not notice The Wolf grabbing his Sledgehammer and then as they spin around the referee doesn't see as The Wolf hit Justice in the head with the
Sledgehammer as they swing around and then both men fall, with Wolf landing on Justice to score the pinfall and the Sledgehammer slides to the edge of the ring still not noticed by the referee.
referee - " .. .. one .... " referee - " .. .. .two ..... " referee - " ... .. threeeeeeeee .... " * * * The Bell Rings * * * Cheryl - " .... and your winner via pinfall .... " Cheryl - " ..... he is ..... " Cheryl - " ...... THE WOLF !!!!!!!! " The Cartel run down the ramp, and Wolf stands there with the sledgehammer, holding them off just outside the ring. Justice comes up from behind and hits Wolf with a Forearm Smash to the back of the neck, flooring him, and the Cartel jump into the ring and beat on
The Wolf. The beating lasts a good 2 minutes until .... ' Crawling ' by Linkin Park starts to play and the True Power video on the X-Tremetron ...
Praxis, the True Power is seen running down the entrance ramp and sliding into the ring. First he knocks Prince Neptune out of the ring with a Clothesline, then turns and runs at Ronny Storm simply lifting him up into a Toss Outside the Ring. Praxis turns again to see
Cherry Snow and Buffy daring to get in his face, he grabs both their heads and makes them Three Stooges Headbutt each other.
Scott Justice is the last Cartel member left and he is still stomping on
The Wolf until he notices the silence and absense of his fellow roid users buddys. Justice turns to see Praxis and then takes about 4 free shots to Praxis' jaw, but this does very little, only making Praxis head jerk around a bit.
Scott Justice then tries a Low Blow and then a Throat Chop but still
Praxis is still standing, barely phased. Scott Justice steps back and then turns to run away, but bumps right into the now standing Wolf.
Scott Justice runs back again and bumps into Praxis. Then Scott Justice turns back around and runs into a Clothesline with the Sledgehammer and is out, he is then stomped out of the ring by The Wolf. Fans cheer like crazy as The Wold stands 1 foot across from Praxis, holding the sledgehammer and not striking. They simply stare as
The Wolf's music play and he exits the ring. Praxis is almost like a statue until he finally leaves once The Wolf is on his way up the ramp.
Maxx - " What do you make of that ? " Tely - " I was hoping you could tell me ? Looks like The Wolf has no clue about Praxis' part in this 'angle' either. "

Justice's attempt with his finisher.
