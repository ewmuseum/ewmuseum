Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Super X-Treme Wrestling opened its doors on 16 July 2000, built from the merged rosters of two closing federations arriving, its own recruitment copy promised, on equal ground. An initial roster of thirteen had grown past two dozen within two weeks, and a debut card was already booked for the promotion's first Friday night on the air.

That card, Friday Night Fury, aired on 21 July 2000 from the promotion's own X-Treme Dome and carried eight matches to a crowd its own broadcast put at twenty-five thousand: a costumed superhero against an interdimensional traveler, a mistress of the Indian Death Grip broken only at the ropes, and a main event pitting two of the roster's largest competitors, Omega and Brimstone, to a clean finish and a handshake. The broadcast closed on an unexplained warning of its own: the letters "XB" found freshly spray-painted across the arena wall as the crowd filed out.

The promotion kept up a steady newsgroup recruitment drive for the rest of the year, advertising two cards a week, a monthly pay-per-view, an awards show and ten championships across its roster, while a running "Ring of Frost" tagline became one of its recurring calling cards. Its own recruitment copy credited its founding owner with an earlier run leading another federation it called simply "the infamous XWF."

By September the roster had already turned over at least once: a departed player's signature character was written out and recast onto Brimstone, and the promotion's own posters aired the dispute publicly on the newsgroup, arguing over who should have held the federation's commissioner post. A December recruitment post closed the year by teasing one wrestler's own profile alongside it, a hardcore and reigning rumble champion sharing a tag team billing with another.

No closing notice survives in this packet's own record: the promotion's last surviving document, posted Christmas Eve 2000, still describes it as hiring for the year ahead.
