== B A N N E D F R O M T E L E V I S I O N == == W R A S S L I N' == Handbook, Version 1.0 1) Introduction 2) What is BFTW? 3) Rules and Roleplaying 4) Titles 5) Cards 6) Staff 7) Application 8) Conclusion =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= 1) Introduction Welcome to Banned From Television Wrasslin'. I know what your thinking..."Another e-wrestling federation starting up?!"...yeah, another e-wrestling federation, but this isn't your normal e-wrestling federation. This is for a special breed of wrestlers that can raise their standards to compete in some wild places. BFTW is run by three men who are all experienced in the e-wrestling department and hope for everyone that joins, we will make your stay in BFTW a fun one. This isn't a long boring handbook that is full of promises that the fed head won't keep, its to the point, and truthful. So please read the handbook and hopefully you'll be interested...enjoy :)
=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= 2) What is BFTW? BFTW stands for Banned From Television Wrasslin' if you didn't pick it up earlier. Its not your normal wrestling federation, more of a gimmick fed. We are obviously not allowed on television due to some of the things we perform in our federation but we are a local attraction from all over. Maybe once a year, our "Mega-Card" is on Pay Per View, but thats as close as we get to television.
BFTW has some explicit content that includes some hardcore wrestling as well. But if you're up for having fun, then BFTW is for you. =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= 3) Rules and Roleplaying There aren't many rules in BFTW, but here are the few: 1) NO TOS'ING! We're here to have fun and not start any trouble between anyone. If you have a problem, come to any three of the fed heads. If we can't help you then if you want, you can leave BFTW or try and deal with the problem. We will do our best to help you out though...
2) We don't mind cussing as long as the sentences make sense and don't have a curse every other word so its too hard to even understand. 3) There will be a 4 roleplay a week limit for packets, that doesn't include on card interviews. 4) Please do not complain to the president for the following reasons: Match Outcomes, Booking, etc. Just do your best to roleplay and it may not work everytime, but sometimes it will win matches and we will give you the opportunity to win here in BFTW.
5) HAVE FUN! If you're not here to have fun, then I recommend you don't join... =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= 4) Titles BFTW Hardcore Heavyweight Title - Most prestigious title in BFTW. Not every match has to have some extreme stipulations, but in BFTW, you're expected to go a little crazy in the ring. This title is defended at Mega Cards (every 6 weeks) unless of a special gimmick match and sometimes at the weekly shows.
BFTW Northern Hemisphere Title - Second most prestigious title in BFTW. Stipulations vary for this title and this title is defended more often than the
Hardcore Heavyweight. Its defended at Mega Cards and usually at weekly shows.
BFTW Welterweight Title - Most prestigious title for guys under the weight limit of 245 lbs. Stipulations also vary and this title is defended just as much as the Northern Hemisphere. Defended at Mega Cards and weekly shows.
BFTW Tag Team Titles - _IF_ we can get enough tag teams, there will definitely be a Tag Team title so tag handlers, please send in an application and we'll notify you whether or not there will be a tag division, I'll try my best to recruit and get it going. Only title for tag teams. Is defended at Mega Cards and weekly shows.
=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= 5) Cards In BFTW, there will be two cards a week. One will be a _HUGE_ show with about nine matches along with a few dark matches, if the darks are necessary. The on-card matches will be in full commentary while the dark matches will be summaries. Then there will be another card where all the matches are summarized except the main event which is in commentary. On-Card interviews, Strats, and
Angles are encouraged on both cards. Here are the two cards:
Saturday Night Slobberknocker - Weekly card that is the bigger card of the two, in the real world, more like the RAW or Nitro. Slobberknocker is very rarely on television, and if it is, its only on local. This card contains up to nine matches a card and includes on-card interviews as well. All matches are in full length commentary as well. This is where the majors angles and storylines take place.
Cruisin' for a Bruisin' - Weekly card that has about nine match as well, but only the main event is in full commentary. The rest are darks in short commentary. On-Card interviews can also be used on this show and a few storylines and angles brew here.
The BFTW will hold a "Mega-Card" about every five to six weeks. This would be like a Pay Per View, just its not on the actual PPV channel on TV. Once a year, the owners of BFTW may be able to persuade the Pay Per View broadcasting company to let us on television but this is not often. The Mega-Cards will hold almost all titles on the line and where major feuds enter the ring. There is also a chance of there being "gimmick" Mega-Cards like a Survivor Series, Royal
Rumble, etc.
=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= 6) Staff Fictional Staff - Co-Presidents/Owners - Jerry Dylan - A man who wrestled in premiere federations at one time known as
"The Jackyl" but then came into the industry of extreme, backyard federations and came into enough money with his friends to start his own federation. Dylan loves to have fun and everyone loves him.
Brady Black - An associate of Dylan's, but also a close friend. Both men wrestled together in their early days and have known each other for quite some time. Black along with Dylan put their money together and now there is BFTW.
Black has gone psycho ever since starting the federation though and Dylan doesn't like it one bit.
Commissioner - Alex Cote - The man in BFTW responsible for booking matches, having control over most wrestlers, etc. Cote used to wrestle in many federations and still does under "The Hammer." Cote decided to join two friends of his, Dylan and
Black, in starting a backyard type wrestling federation so put his money into it. The fans have no problem with Cote because he gives them what they pay for.
Commentators - (NOTE: There can't be commentary if its not on TV right? Wrong,
BFTW tapes all its shows for later use if we are ever allowed on
TV and wanna replay it :)) Todd Powers - Play-by-Play announcer. The crowd doesn't mind him, but doesn't like him too much either. He gives it to you straight as he sees it with some catch phrases in his vocabulary.
Taylor McGlocklin - Color Commentary man. Also the man people love to hate. He always roots for the bad guys, typical heel. Justine Summers - Also Color Commentary, but this is a girl! The fans absolutely love her not only because she's drop dead gorgeous, but because she has a great personality as well.
Interviewers - Will Wellington - African American interviewer that the fans absolutely love. He can imitate the wrestlers and just make you laugh. He's a former wrestler that had a career ending injury so stays in the biz any way he can.
Vic Vernon - A man most people hate due to what he says about the fans. He's known to most as "Slick" and wants to interview any heel in BFTW.
Ring Announcer - Bronson Bloxon - Introduces the wrestlers as they enter the ring and is known for his great voice and ability to drag words out.
