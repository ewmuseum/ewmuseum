Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Banned From Television Wrasslin's surviving record is its own Handbook, Version 1.0, posted 3 March 1999, pitching itself as “not your normal wrestling federation” but “a gimmick fed” too extreme for TV, save for an annual pay-per-view “Mega-Card.”

On screen, it staged itself as the creation of two co-presidents: Jerry Dylan, once a premiere-federation wrestler billed as “The Jackyl” before turning to backyard extreme wrestling and pooling his money to start his own promotion, and his old friend and fellow investor Brady Black, whom the handbook itself says had “gone psycho ever since starting the federation.” A third founding partner, Commissioner Alex Cote, still wrestled himself under the name “The Hammer” while booking every match.

It ran two weekly cards — the flagship Saturday Night Slobberknocker and a lighter Cruisin' for a Bruisin' — called by play-by-play announcer Todd Powers and color commentators Taylor McGlocklin and Justine Summers, and set four championships: the Hardcore Heavyweight, Northern Hemisphere and Welterweight titles, plus a Tag Team title contingent on finding enough teams to fill it.

No roster, card or title reign survives beyond the handbook's own plans; it is the last surviving document under this promotion's own name.
