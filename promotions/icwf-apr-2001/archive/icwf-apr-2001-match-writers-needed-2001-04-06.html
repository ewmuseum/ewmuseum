ICWF (Apr 2001): Match Writers Needed

6 April 2001
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The ICWF just recently opened, and has had overwhelming success for one week. No one has quit and everyone has RPed! Good RP's to! The only problem is, our staff is somewhat small and we're not great match writers. And our RPers try so hard, we feel they deserve better, so we need a few good match writers, who will take a little bit of time to write good, entertaining matches. We have 4 cards a month. (including the PPV) Some cards are big, some are small. Right now our cards have a fairly large amount of matches on them, as we are just getting opened, and developed and everyone wants to RP.
But if you are interested, please e-mail me back at [detail withheld] . Thank You.
