Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

ICWF was one week old when its own staff posted a call for help on 6 April 2001, describing “overwhelming success for one week”: no one had quit, and every wrestler had turned in a roleplay. The problem, in its own words, was quality rather than quantity — a small staff that felt it wasn't yet writing matches good enough for the roleplayers it had.

Its own schedule ran four cards a month, pay-per-view included, and the call asked specifically for match writers willing to take the time to write matches its own roleplayers deserved.

That single call for writers is everything that survives of ICWF. No card, roster, or result is held, and no closing notice is known.
