It is a BOOKED e-wrestling league. In other words, the angles and winners are chosen by the President based on roleplaying, character statistics, and whatever is best for the league as a whole. So, please try to understand all of this if your wrestler or team does happen to lose a match here and there, which will happen if you stay with us long enough. -- WHAT WE HAVE TO OFFER -- We at the RWA are here to serve you, the e-wrestling fan and participant. Our objective is to bring you the best, most exciting, and most realistic e-wrestling action on the 'Net, no strings attached. As we grow, more cards and extra features will be added, but as of now, we will have one card a week and a Pay Per View Extravaganza every two months. Our weekly card is Tuesday Night UNLIMITED, and will be written by [name withheld] with the announcing talents of Gary Knack and Scott, himself. Don't let Gary get on your nerves, though. He just "tells it like it is." YEAH, RIGHT! -- TITLES -- As of press time, there are two titles (one for the singles, one for the tag team division) slated to be competed for in the RWA. For the singles division, there is the RWA World Heavyweight Title (That means no SCRAWNY FOURTEEN YEAR OLDS!), which will be given to the winner of a thirty-two man single elimination tournament. And for the tag team division, there are the RWA World Tag Team Titles, which will be put up for grabs in an eight team, single elimination tournament in the near future. Both the singles and tag team titles MUST be defended EVERY TWO WEEKS (Provided there's a card both weeks)!!! -- !REQUIREMENTS! -- Here are some MUSTS that really have to be followed in order to become successful in the RWA: 1) ROLEPLAY! As stated in the "WELCOME TO THE RWA!!!" section, roleplaying is mandatory in order for your wrestler(s) to win. But the foremost reason to roleplay is to MAKE THE RWA FUN, FUN, FUN!!! Without the interviews, the matches would be virtually meaningless. And remember, all roleplays should be sent directly to President [name withheld] at [detail withheld] , who will either package them or send them out separately to the RWA mailing list via Blind Carbon Copy (BCC.) 2) CHALLENGE! How in Tarnations do you expect to win if you don't wrestle? In other words, your guys will NEVER wrestle if you don't challenge people and roleplay. But the worst concequence of all resulting from no roleplays or challenges... EXPULSION! That's right, no sense keeping any dead weight around when we could be accepting new and talented wrestlers. Of course, we could feed you to Rabid Dog ECCH!, but we like to keep him hungry. -- OPTIONS AND LIMITS -- As of press time, the RWA is accepting two singles wrestlers and one tag team per e-mail address. We realize that some people have more than one internet account, or are allowed to create numerous "screen names" or "handles" on a commercial service accounct, but we would appreciate it if you would be honest with us and only enter the limited amount of wrestlers or less than that, if you please. The RWA is also open to stables. If you would like to create a personal stable including your two singles and one tag team, that's great. Another option is to combine powers with other RWA members to make a joint stable. The joint stable is recommended, due to more options with angles (e.g. Stable break-ups. What fun is it to break up with yourself?) Yet another RWA option is to enter a singles wrestler and find a wrestler owned by someone else, and join forces to make a tag team. This can be good to get away from your normal cookie cutter-type tag teams (i.e. Frick and Frack, Penn and Teller, Pee Wee Herman and ... Pee Wee Herman.) -- RWA ENTRY FORM -- To enter, cut and paste this entry form in a nice, new e-letter in your mail program, fill it out in full, and then send it to [detail withheld] , with it titled RWA STATS: <WRESTLER OR TAG TEAM NAME>. -- HANDLER INFORMATION -- (Note: All of the HANDLER INFORMATION is optional except for the E-Mail Address.) E-Mail Address: Real Name: Age: Prior E-Wrestling Experience: Other E-Wrestlers Created In Past: If So, What Leagues? Hobbies: Added Notes [Things I Should Know About You (Criminal Record, etc. ;) )]: -- WRESTLER INFORMATION -- Wrestler's Name: Height: Weight: Hometown: Entrance Music: Gimmick: Looks: Build: Outfit, Wrestling Attire: Allignment (Face, Heel, Neutral): Describe personality, motivation, etc.: Experience and Training: Wrestling Style (Aerial, Brawler, Mat Wrestler, Technical, Powerhouse, Jobber, Blob, Sumo, Daredevil, Lunatic, etc.) *CHOOSE UP TO THREE, BUT NO MORE!!!*: Style #1: Style #2 (Optional): Style #3 (Optional): Finishing Move: Discription of Finisher: Favorite Moves (Include AT LEAST Twenty of them): Manager, Valet, or Bodyguard (Optional): Description, if Applicable: Items Brought to Ringside (Optional): Added Notes: ______________________________________________________________________ -- !NOTE! -- As of press time, ALL Rabid Wrestling Association text products will be best read in Microsoft NOTEPAD for Windows, or any equivilant text editor, Macintosh or IBM PC. This is where the logos and text allignments will look their best. Also, it is recommended that the editor be sized to the full screen, and if your editor gives you the option, use Word Wrap. ______________________________________________________________________ -- !ENJOY! -- Well, thanks for taking the time to read over this RWA Overview and Application. We hope to see you joining what is destined to be the best roleplay e-wrestling league on the 'Net. The RWA will surely knock your socks off!
