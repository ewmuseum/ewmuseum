BBWF Sunday Slamfest (28 December)

28 December 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PZ: Hello everyone to this addition of Sunday Slamfest! I’m the President and I’m join in the broadcast booth with the Vice President and Luke Mills! VP: Tonight we will see a mixed tag team match, that will be unbelievable!! A No DQ match between Apocalypse and a new comer to the BBWF Sandman!! LM: That should be a match to remember! PZ: Well let’s get started! Our first match is between Luca Brasi and War Path! [ Luca Brasi and War Path make their way to the ring] LM: The bell rings and this match is underway!! War Path takes to early advantage! VP: War Path picks Luca Brasi up and slams him to the canvas!! War Path covers 1 ............... 2 .......... kickout!! That was the first nearfall of the contest!! PZ: War Path whips Luca Brasi into the far side corner!! War Path climbs the turnbuckle!! Rights and lefts to the head by War Path 1 ............ 2 ........... 3 ....... 4 ............ 5 ............ 6 ............... Luca Brasi counters with an atomic drop!! LM: Luca clotheslines War Path down! Luca with the cover 1 ................. 2 ............. kickout!!! Luca hooks War Path for a suplex .............. small package!! 1 ........... 2 ............. 3!!! War Path wins the match!! PZ: The big man, War Path, can mat wrestle quite well!! Now for our next match it will be........ [The crowd goes silent. "Machinehead" by Bush blares over the PA system as the crowd is in stun. Out walks brother of Xavier DuPrix.. MATT DUPRIX!! Matt DuPrix walks out with a flannel shirt and jean shorts and he has a microphone, the fans go wild. Matt heads into the ring wearing a DETROIT TIGERS hat. Matt looks around the arena.] Matt: I am here tonight to finish what my brother couldn't succeed at. My brother Xavier, the man who rests up in Heaven right now is watching down and thinking I should do what he didn't finish. I am not going to allow thugs like D.U.I. to degrade what he represented. So at NEW YEAR'S NIGHTMARE on January 4th, XTC if I am so lucky.. I want to you inside that electric steel cage. [ Fans cheer loudly. Moments later D.U.I. members, DOOM, and XTC both walk out onto the ramp. Matt DuPrix takes the microphone.] Matt: BRING IT ON!!!!!!!!!! [XTC runs down the ramp as then Matt DuPrix clobbers him with a clothesline. DOOM runs down to the ring, DuPrix hits him with a clothesline. Matt DuPrix begins to hammer on XTC as "Machinehead" by Bush blares. DuPrix decks DOOM one more time and then jumps into the crowd.] PZ: Matt Duprix appears to be starting where his older brother left off!! XTC’s problems never seem to end!! LM: Matt Duprix is fired with revenge!! I pity XTC, Matt is very upset!! PZ: Now as I was saying our next match is a tag match between Bad Asses and Hell’s Fury!! [Bad Asses and Hell’s Fury make their way to the ring] VP: I hope this match will be a violent one!! The bell rings and we are under way! LM: It appears that Mark and Fury are going to start things out! This is a debut for the Bad Asses! PZ: Mark slams Fury to the mat! He covers 1 .......... kickout!! LM: He’s going to have to wear down Fury before he can cover him! Mark whips Fury into the ropes! Mark hits Fury with a shoulder block! Fury just stopped and shoved Mark to the mat! Mark bounces off the ropes clothesline!! Fury just stands there and shakes his head! PZ: Fury won’t be pushed around. Fury grabs Mark by the neck and lifts him off the mat!! Fury throws Mark into the corner. VP: A quick tag to Final Justice. Final Justice starts punching Mark in the mid-section! Final Justice is pummeling Mark!!! LM: Chris comes in and hits Final Justice in the back. Final Justice turns and levels Chris with a clothesline!!! VP: Final Justice is dominating!! LM: Final Justice heaves Mark up over his head!!!! Final Justice throws Mark through the time keeper’s table!!! Final Justice steps over the top rope and jumps to the arena floor!! VP: Hell’s Fury seems to have this match well in hand!! Final Justice and Fury are trying to climb back out of the tag-team cellar! Final Justice picks up Mark and slams him onto the concrete!! PZ: Chris climbs the turnbuckle!! Chris leaps but Final Justice catches him in mid-air!!!! Chris goes flying into the crowd!! LM: WOW!!! PZ: Final Justice is reaching over the safety rail for Chris!! He had better watch the legal man! Mark is staggering back to his feet! VP: Mark has found the ring bell!! It was under the broken table!! Mark smashes the ring bell into Final Justice’s head!!! LM: That will take the big man down!!!! It does!!! VP: That is why they are called Bad Asses! LM: Chris jumps over the safety rail and helps heave Final Justice back into the ring!! VP: Mark jumps in and covers Final Justice!! 1 ................ 2 .................. Shoulder UP!!! Final Justice kicked out!!! PZ: Mark and Chris don’t know what to do!! Mark starts stomping away on Final Justice’s forehead!! LM: That is stopping Final Justice!! Final Justice gets up and shoves Mark back to the mat!!! Mark quickly tags in Chris. Final Justice also makes the tag!! PZ: Fury charges, but Chris leapfrogs!! Chris bounces off the rope and Fury leapfrogs! VP: Chris bounces off again, Fury puts his head down and ........................????? LM: That LOOKED like the Wave of XTC!!! PZ: Chris covers and 1 ................. 2 ..................... 3!!!!! Bad Asses win!!!! [ “Boom” blares over the PA system!! DUI appears through the curtain. War Path, Chris Taylor, DOOM, and XTC walk down the ramp! DUI climbs in the ring and XTC takes the mic. ] XTC: Ladies and Gentlemen, let me introduce to you, DUI’s newest tag sensations, Bad Asses!!! [ Crowd boos as Chris and Mark raise their hands in victory ] XTC: DUI already has the US Championship and we want all the gold!!! Addiction!! Give Bad Asses a title shot!!! They beat the BIGGEST tag team in the BBWF!!! DOOM: Jay Voodoo!! I don’t know who you cried to but you got your title shot!!! I will crush you come New Year Nightmare!!! XTC: I want some title shots!!! Bane!! This is a calling out!! Put up your strap!! CT: Hey Prez!! You suspend DOOM and XTC and they are here anyway!! DUI, Deviants Underground Incorporated, doesn’t play by your rules!!! We do what we want, when we want it!!! XTC: Lets go! [ “Boom” blares as DUI walks up the ramp ] PZ: If these guys won’t play by the rules, I will have to take drastic action. LM: Those guys are worthless pieces of garbage! I think you should fire them all!! [ Mac Voodoo and Volcano come down to the ring ] LM: This match has been a long time coming!! VP: Well, I don’t know what sparked this feud, but I do know it is a hot one!! I have a feeling that this will continue until The Voodoo-Clan or Volcano are out of the BBWF!! PZ: The ref is calling for the bell. The match starts as Volcano tackles Mac and starts to pound on him!! VP: Mac rolls Volcano off and starts to pound on him!!! This is a fist fight!!! LM: Here comes Jay and Carl. I wondered why they weren’t out here. Mac picks up Volcano and slams him to the canvas!! Mac drops an elbow but Volcano moves out of the way!! PZ: Look at the top of the ramp!! It is Apocalypse!! VP: What is he doing here?? He is just standing there. PZ: Meanwhile in the ring, Volcano has taken the advantage and has Mac in a front face lock. VP: That is the key to winning. You need to wear down your opponent. If he can’t move, he can’t win. PZ: Now Cardiac and Magus are at the top of the ramp! They are in a huddle with Apocalypse! LM: Mac is now on his feet. Volcano is still holding on with that head lock. SIDE SUPLEX!! Volcano lets go now!!! PZ: Cardiac and Magus are heading down the ramp!! Jay and Carl see them coming. They turn and start walking up the ramp!! LM: Look!! Plague jumps out of the crowd! He is up on the apron!! The ref runs over to stop him from coming into the ring!! VP: The Stranger steps over the safety rail!! He gets into the ring!! RUDE AWAKENING on Mac Voodoo!! The Stranger looks at Volcano and slides out of the ring and leaves through the crowd!! LM: Plague jumps down and runs up the ramp!! He ran right by Jay and Carl!! PZ: As Volcano goes for the cover, Jay and Carl turn around!! They quickly jump in the ring and the ref calls for the bell!! Volcano grabs Jay and throws him over the top rope!! Carl grabs Mac and drags him out of the ring!! LM: Look at Volcano stare at Apocalypse!! Apocalypse just nods his head. What does that mean??? VP: The Voodoo-Clan are coming up the ramp as Apocalypse ducks back through the curtain. Volcano has won this match! LM: What is the connection between Apocalypse and Volcano?? PZ: I don’t know and I’m not to sure I want to find out!! VP: Death Machine is coming to the ring!!! He steps in a starts to attack Volcano!! [ Volcano hits Death Machine with a low blow!! As Volcano pounds on Death Machine, Pyscotron by Megadeth plays over the loud speakers. Red and Blue lights start flashing and then a loud explosion happens as Pyscotron comes down the ramp and help Death Machine. Then the lights go out and coming down the rafters comes Stargazer! ] LM: These two men are helping Death Machine!! They must be two new members of Addiction!! VP: The three men leave Volcano beaten in the ring!! I can’t believe what just happened here!! PZ: It is time for our next match! VP, I’m sure you will love this one! Its a no DQ match between Apocalypse and Sandman [ Apocalypse comes down to the ring ] VP: That is odd! Apocalypse comes down to the ring alone. He usually is accompanied by HIS Insane Asylum! [ Sandman makes his way to the ring. He is carrying a bat, a roll of barbed wire and a steel chair!! ] VP: YES!!!!!! LM: It IS no DQ! Sandman throws that stuff in the ring and attacks Apocalypse!! PZ: The ref is calling for the bell!! Sandman picks up the bat and hits Apocalypse in the head with it!!! Apocalypse goes down but Sandman picks him back up! LM: Sandman scoops Apocalypse up and slams him to the canvas!!! PZ: Pyscotron is coming to the ring!! I heard that Addiction was interested in acquiring Sandman, but I didn’t hear anything else about it!! VP: Sandman picks up Apocalypse and piledrives him right on that steel chair!!! Sandman pulls Apocalypse up and piledrives him on the chair again!! LM: Here comes Pnemon!! He comes up to the ring and puts something in Apocalypse’s hand! I can’t see what it is. PZ: Pyscotron chases Pnemon back to the locker room! Sandman bends over to pick up Apocalypse, but Apocalypse throws some sort of powder in Sandman’s eyes! LM: Sandman staggers back as Apocalypse staggers to his feet! Apocalypse picks up the coil of barbed wire and starts whipping Sandman!! PZ: Sandman is cut!! Blood is everywhere!!! LM: WHO ARE THESE GUYS??? [ Two masked men walk down to the ring. One is carrying a metal trash can and the other is carrying a ladder. They set the objects beside the ring and walk off. ] PZ: I don’t know. VP: Apocalypse clotheslines Sandman to the mat!! Apocalypse bounces off the ropes ................ Pyscotron hit Apocalypse in the back with the trash can!!!! Apocalypse crumples to the canvas!! LM: Sandman reaches down and pulls the ladder into the ring!! Sandman slams the ladder onto Apocalypse!! PZ: Sandman rolls Apocalypse over but doesn’t pin him!!! Sandman sets up the ladder. He now grabs the barbed wire and begins to climb! VP: Sandman is perched on the top of the ladder!!!! He is wrapping the barbed wire around his body!!! Sandman leaps and BIG SPLASH!!!! The ref counts 1 ........................... 2 ......................... 3!!!! Sandman wins!!! LM: Neither of these men are moving!!!! Sandman REDEFINES HARD-CORE!!!! PZ: Pyscotron has left!! The two masked men return to the ring and help Sandman back to the ring! VP: What a match!!! That was worth every penny I paid!! PZ: Ummmm, we are paying you. VP: I LOVE MY JOB!!!!!!!!!!!! LM: It is now time for our main event! This is the first mixed tag team match in the BBWF’s history!! [ Gwar and Mandy come down to the ring ] PZ: WOW!! Look at Mandy’s ring attire!!! [ Bane and Amber come down to the ring ] VP: Check out Amber!!! [ VP whistles ] LM: You guys done?? PZ: How can you sit here, with beautiful, scantly clad women in the ring, and not say anything? [ VP and Prez look at each other ] VP Ohhhhh. PZ: Sorry, I didn’t know that you were ................. ummmmm. LM: I am a happily married man!!! Lets call the match please!! The ref calls for the bell and we are underway!! PZ: Bane is in the ring but Mandy wants to start the match. Bane shrugs and gets out. Amber steps in the ring! VP: Mandy quickly tags Gwar! PZ: Mind games by Mandy and Gwar! The rules say that Bane HAS to come in now! He does and Gwar and Bane lock up! LM: These two could possibly meet again on January 7th!! If Bane retains the BBWF World Title at New Year Nightmare! PZ: So far, both men have traded moves! Neither wrestler has been able to get an advantage! LM: Gwar tags in Mandy!! Amber has to get into the ring!! VP: YES!! Now we get to see a cat fight!!! PZ: MEOW!! LM: “sigh” Amber scoops up Mandy and slams her to the canvas!! Amber pulls Mandy up by her hair!! PZ: OUCH!! VP: HA HA HA!! Amber is spinning Mandy around by her hair!!! [ Brad Morris runs down to ringside!! He grabs Bane and starts to beat on him!! Warfare runs down and starts to attack Gwar!! ] PZ: What the hell??? LM: That is Brad Morris!!! He must be trying to soften Bane up!!! VP: Warfare is attacking Gwar!!! PZ: Mandy and Amber have stopped fighting!! They are trying to help their men!! LM: Love Doll is here!!! She grabs Amber and slams her on the concrete!!! She turns her attention on Mandy!! LOVE DOLL DDTed MANDY!!! VP: I bet Love Doll could kick your ass Luke!!! PZ: The ref is calling for the bell! This match is out of hand!!! Here comes more trouble!!! [ The Insane Asylum rundown to the ring to help Bane. Addiction comes to the aid of Gwar. Brad Morris, Warfare and Love Doll exit through the spectators. The Insane Asylum help Bane and Amber back to the locker room as Addiction grabs a mic. ] Gwar: You must want trouble!! Morris! Warfare! You won’t get away with that!! I want to introduce everyone to Psycotron and Stargazer!! They have something to say!! Psycotron: Me, Stargazer and Death Machine want to take this time to challenge DOOM, Zeus, and Volcano in a BATTLE OF THE GIANTS match!! Stargazer: Accept if you DARE!!!! Death Machine: DIE!!!!!!!!!! [ Addiction leaves the ring ] PZ: Well, I’m not really sure what a Battle of the Giants match is, but I’m sure it will be interesting! LM: That mixed tag match ended in a draw. The feud between Bane and Gwar is far from over! I hope to see Bane and Gwar wrestle again in the near future! These two stars always put it all on the line when they step in the ring!! PZ: That is all for tonight!!! For everyone in the BBWF, this is the Prez saying “Goodnight”!!!
