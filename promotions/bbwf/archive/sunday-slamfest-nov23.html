BBWF Sunday Slamfest (23 November)

23 November 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
*** Camera fades in to show a jammed packed Kiel Center in St. Louis, Missouri. Fireworks explode as the camera pans around the arena. People in the crowd hold signs that read “Why, War Path, Why?”, “Apocalypse is the undisputed King of Hard-Core”, “IFW Sucks”, and “The Castoldo Family RULES!!!!”. Luke Mills is in the ring. *** LM: Ladies and Gentlemen, please join me in welcoming Consigliere!! *** The crowd boos as Consigliere makes his way to the ring with Johnny Boy. Consigliere has the US Belt draped over his shoulder. *** LM: Welcome Consigliere, I was... Consigliere: Address me as the United States Champion!!! LM: You are not the BBWF US Champion, so I will not refer to you as such!! All you are is a dirty rotten thief!! Consigliere: I kicked your ass once!! Don’t make me do it again!! LM: I don’t need to take this from the likes of you!! I am out of here! *** Luke drops the mic on the mat and leaves the ring. Johnny Boy picks up the mic and proceeds to conduct the interview *** JB: What I would like right now is for all of you St. Louis losers to stand up and show respect for the UNDISPUTED BBWF United States Champion, Consigliere!!!!! *** BOOOOOOOOOOOOO *** JB: Well my dear friend, what to you think about Flyin “Higher than a kite” Brian’s challenge to a loser leaves the BBWF match??? Consigliere: Well John-Boy, I have a lot to worry about now. I have to defend MY belt and worry about those mysterious individuals that insult me in the Interview Center. Those pussies are too afraid of The Castoldo Family to take credit for their disrespect. I was so concerned, that I went out and hired myself a bodyguard!! Let me introduce, DOOM!!! *** A large man slowly stalks to the ring *** JB: Consigliere, do you or don’t you accept Flyin “Cryin” Brian’s challenge?? Consigliere: Well JB, I would have to say ................................... *** DOOM grabs Consigliere from behind and puts him in a full nelson. DOOM then tosses Consigliere around like a rag-doll. DOOM picks Consigliere up in the full nelson and drives Consigliere into the mat face first! He then picks up the US Belt and grabs the mic from Johnny Boy! *** DOOM: I am the only athlete in the BBWF worthy to wear this gold belt! Brian, I will accept your loser leaves match!! Sign it!! Do it now!! As for The Castoldo Family, watch out!! You interfere in my business and you will be DOOMED!!!! HA HA HA HA HA HA HA HA HA HA HA HA HA HA HA HA HA!!!! *** The light go out in the Kiel Center. When they come back on, DOOM is gone and Johnny Boy is flat on the mat. The US Belt is also gone *** PZ: I don’t know what to say!! That US Belt has been in more hands than a two dollar whore!! VP: HA HA HA HA HA HA HA HA HA!!! Yeah in more hands than Luke’s wife!!! LM: Leave my wife out of this!!! All I have to say is that The Castoldo Family now knows what it feels like to be double crossed! PZ: Our first bout features the debut of Chris Taylor’s prodigy, XTC. He takes on a tough challenge in Angelo Saturn! *** Angelo Saturn w/Fonze and XTC w/Chris Taylor make their way to ringside *** VP: I have noticed a change in attitude in Chris Taylor!! I can’t believe he interfered in the World Title Match last Wednesday! PZ: Darkhawk is also not happy about that situation. He wanted to win the belt with honor and defend the belt with honor! VP: I think Darkhawk wants to get XTC in the ring to teach him a lesson in respect and honor! Their goes the bell and XTC and Angelo Saturn lock up! XTC pushes Angelo back into the corner. LM: The ref is calling for the break. XTC slowly backs up. He kicks Angelo in the stomach!! XTC Irish whips Angelo to the opposite corner and charges in!! VP: Angelo is staggering!! XTC measures him and executes a textbook drop-kick!! PZ: Chris Taylor has this young man trained and ready for the BBWF. XTC picks up Angelo and backs him into the corner. XTC whips him into the farside corner! XTC bounces off the nearside ropes. LM: XTC catches Angelo in the Wave of XTC as Angleo staggered out of the corner!!!!!!! XTC goes for the pin!! 1 ........ 2 ........... 3!! XTC is victorious!! VP: That was a very impressive win for XTC tonight! LM: Chris has predicted great things for this young man!! With a little hard work and the right guidance, this man could be wearing BBWF gold in the near future!! PZ: Our next match is for the BBWF World Tag Team Championship!! The two team of Suicidal Mafia and Total Annihilation have much animosity between them!! *** Total Annihilation and the Suicidal Mafia make their way to ringside *** VP: Both teams have brought insurance policies so no one will interfere in this match!! PZ: Before this match I informed Zeus and Armageddon that if they get involved in the match they will be suspended indefinitely!! VP: Hopefully this will be a FAIR match. The bell rings and the match begins!! LM: Ash and Capone start it off! Both men are very cautious, neither wants to make a mistake!! VP: Ash charges at Capone!! Capone hits him with a clothesline!! Down goes Ash!! PZ: Capone picks Ash up and whips him across!! Capone doesn’t see it but, Ash tagged in Star666!! Capone drop-kicks Ash out of the ring!! LM: Capone turns to follow Ash to the outside!! Star666 taps Capone on the shoulder!! PZ: Capone doesn’t know that it is Star666 behind him!! Star666 grabs Capone by the throat, Chokeslam!! Star666 picks Capone up and slams him down to the mat!! LM: These two teams were two of the seven teams that fought in the Abandoned Building match!! That match will be a classic match in BBWF history. These teams hate each other!! They both want this match to be fair!! That is why they both brought GAURDS!! PZ: Capone got the advantage and now he tags in Louie!! Louie whips Star666 into the ropes! Louie clotheslines him down!! LM: These two teams could become two of the greatest teams in our great sport!! Louie covers Star666 1 ........ 2 ........ 3 NO NO Ash makes the save!! Capone runs into the ring and clotheslines Ash out!! VP: They are setting Star666 up for the Mob Drop... they hit it!! Star666 is out cold!! Capone goes for the cover 1 ...... 2 ...... *** Armageddon jumps up on to the ring apron to try to stop the count!! Zeus pulls him down and smashes him head off the ringpost!! *** ........ 3!! The Suicidal Mafia win the match!! They are the NEW BBWF tag team champions!! LM: Congratulations go out to The Suicidal Mafia!! They deserve the BBWF tag team belts!!!! PZ: What a match!! Now I must go and prepare to draw the names for the “King of Hard-Core” Match!! *** CUT TO A COMMERCIAL *** LIVE FROM THE MGM GRAND IN LAS VEGAS, THE BBWF PROUDLY PRESENTS ------ BBWF HARD-CORE I!!!!! SIXTEEN OF THE TOUGHEST WRESTLERS IN THE SPORT TODAY COMPETE FOR THE RIGHT TO BE CALLED---- “King of Hard-Core” AND A SHOT AT THE BBWF WORLD CHAMPION!! ALSO ON THE CARD WILL BE COUNTLESS OTHER MATCHES WITH SPECIAL “HARD-CORE” STIPULATIONS!! THIS IS NOT YOUR AVERAGE IFW PAY PER VIEW!!! CATCH BBWF HARD-CORE I ONLY ON PAY PER VIEW!!!!!!!! Presented by The PWBB, were America goes for fun. *** CUT BACK TO SUNDAY SLAMFEST. Prez is standing in the ring with a barrel of names *** PZ: We are set to begin the drawing for the “King of Hard-Core” match at BBWF Hard-Core I! We will draw sixteen names. The match will begin with numbers one and two!! Every two minutes, another wrestler will enter the battle. An athlete will be eliminated by either pinfall or submission. The battle will continue until there is ONLY ONE, UNDISPUTED, “KING OF HARD-CORE”!!!!! Here we go: 1. XTC 2. Guido 3. Luca Brasi 4. Il Capo 5. War Path 6. Brad “Love Machine” Morris 7. Consigliere 8. Lethal Injection 9. Cardiac 10. Cinder 11. Pancakeboy Jr. 12. Prophet 13. Plague 14. Tiny Castoldo 15. Armageddon 16. GWar One of these 16 men will become “King of Hard-Core” and will go on to face the BBWF World Champion at BBWF NEW YEAR’S NIGHTMARE on January 4th 1998, presented by Company Connected, the best place for jerky!!!! LM: That match at Hard-Core I will be good match!! Speaking of good matches the next match is the finals of the Cruiserweight Tournament!! *** Il Capo and Anaconda make their way to ringside *** VP: These two men will not let us down tonight!! This match should be a good one!! The combatants both want to become the first BBWF Cruiserweight champion!! PZ: Wasn’t that news great?!?!?!?!? LM: Shut up Prez me and the VP already talked about Hard-Core I!! The match appears to be starting and there is the bell!! PZ: Anaconda quickly gains an advantage by taking out the knee of Il Capo!! Il Capo is limping around the inside of the ring. Anaconda is viciously attacking that knee!! LM: Il Capo slides out of the ring to try and get the cobwebs out!! Anaconda bounces off the ropes flies over the top rope and knocks Il Capo into the safety rail!! Anaconda rolls Il Capo back into the ring!! Anaconda climbs up on the turnbuckle and connects with a missile drop-kick!! Anaconda goes for the pin. 1 .. 2 .. Il Capo got the shoulder up!! PZ: Anaconda attempts an Irish whip but Il Capo reverses it and back body drops Anaconda to the arena floor!!! *** A voice fills the arena *** ??: Il Capo!!! Il Capo!!! HA HA HA HA HA HA HA HA HA HA HA HA HA HA HA HA!! I bet you never thought you would here this voice again!! I am coming to get you!!! I am coming!!!! I will be there soon!! I am there!!!!!!!! *** The lights go out in the arena. After a few moments they come back on and Il Capo is nowhere to be found. The ref is confused and Anaconda urges him to start his ten count. *** PZ: What? ..... 2 ......WHO? ...... 4 ....... Where? .......5 ......... WHY? ......... 7 ............ HOW? .........9 ...........10!! Anaconda wins! Anaconda is the BBWF first Cruiserweight Champion! He beat Il Capo in a very controversial match! VP: That must be the understatement of the year! Was Il Capo attacked?? Did he run!??? Where is he??? LM: I wish I could answer one of those questions!! I haven’t a clue!!! VP: No, THAT IS THE UNDERSTATEMENT OF THE YEAR!!!! PZ: UHHHH, Our next match is a special bedlam grudge match between The Voodoo-Clan and Apocalyptic Events! *** Apocalyptic Events w/Terrax and The Voodoo-Clan make their way to ringside *** VP: It is no secret that these men do not get along! After what Terrax said about Jay and Mac Voodoo last week, I’m surprised he is even out here! The bell sounds and we are underway! PZ: This is not official but I think the winner of this match may be owed a title shot in the near future!! These teams are awesome! VP: Oh Boy, we have company! The Army of Darkness is strolling toward ringside! They have promised revenge on the Insane Asylum for costing Lethal Injection the Cruiserweight Championship! LM: Meanwhile in the ring, the action has been going back and forth with no team having a major advantage! Mac Voodoo just ducked an attempted clothesline by Magus! Magus hits the farside rope ...... no wait!! Magus hits the arena floor!! Jay Voodoo pulled down the top rope!! PZ: Lethal Injection has a steel chair!! He smacks Magus in the head with a steel chair!! The ref didn’t see a thing! He is trying to keep Apocalypse in his corner! Ash is crawling under the ring. LM: Apocalypse runs over to his fallen partner as the ref begins to count 1..... Apocalypse is trying to wake Magus ...... 3 ........ Ash is sneaking up behind Apocalypse! ...... 5 .........Apocalypse turns to confront Ash! ....... 7 ...... Kick to the stomach and Apocalypse Forever on the floor!! ........ 9 .................10!! Magus has been counted out!! Apocalypse is furious! He is stomping away on a prone Ash! VP: He better watch out! Star666 has a frying pan! CLANG!!!!!!! That is going to leave a mark! LM: The Voodoo-Clan are motioning for the Army of Darkness to roll The Apocalyptic Events back into the ring!! AoD heaves the two big men back into the ring and head back to the dressing rooms! PZ: Back in the ring, Jay and Mac are beating Magus and Apocalypse into a bloody pulp!! VP: Oh my god, look who is coming through the curtain!! Thrasher and Stranger!! These two men HATE The Apocalyptic Events!! This is going to get nasty!! PZ: Look at The Voodoo-Clan hold the ropes open! This is making me sick!! Thrasher and The Stranger are standing over a motionless Apocalypse and Magus! Thrasher clenches his fist and ......... punches JAY VOODOO!!!! The Stranger kicks Mac in the groin!! VP: The Stranger and Thrasher in The Asylum???? No Way!!!! Mac is set up by The Stranger and RUDE AWAKENING!!! Mac is picked up and thrown out of the ring! Jay takes a dive and retreats to the safety of the outside! LM: They had better turn around because here comes Plague and Cardiac! The Voodoo-Clan is surrounded! Jay tries to grab Mac and pull him over the safety rail! VP: He will never get away is he has to drag Mac!! Jay is leaving his brother!! He is letting him for the wolves!! The Asylum are beating on Mac Voodoo as Jay escapes through the crowd!! PZ: The Aod are headed back down the aisle! The Asylum are in the ring helping The Apocalyptic Events back to their feet! The Voodoo-Clan have won the battle, but the WAR is far from over! VP: As the officials restore order, I want to wish Darkhawk good luck against Godfather in our next match! The Castoldo Family is obnoxious enough as is. I can’t imagine what they would be like if they had the BBWF World Championship! *** Darkhawk and Godfather w/Tiny make their way to ringside *** LM: The bell sounds and this match is underway! The two men start by trading blows to the head! Darkhawk has Godfather reeling and Irish whips Godfather into the corner! PZ: Darkhawk charges in and gets a boot in the face!! Godfather hops up to the middle turnbuckle and executes a flying lariat! Godfather goes for the pin! 1 ... Kick out by Darkhawk!! VP: Only one Castoldo out here tonight???!! PZ: Guido is suspended, Consigliere was attacked by DOOM, Il Capo disappeared, and Luca Brasi is out with injuries until Hard-Core I. Thing aren’t going good for The Castoldo Family! VP: It is about time!! LM: Darkhawk has Godfather in the corner! He attempts an Irish whip but Godfather reverses it and Darkhawk squishes the ref in the corner!! The ref is hurt!! The ref is down!! Darkhawk is trying to help. He is trying to wake the ref up! VP: Look! XTC is running towards the ring!! XTC slides into the ring and spins Godfather around and kicks him in the stomach! Wave of XTC on Godfather!!! PZ: XTC taps Darkhawk on the shoulder and points to Godfather. Darkhawk is jacked! He doesn’t want to win this way! He pushes XTC down to the mat and goes back to helping the ref. XTC did not like that at all! LM: XTC taps Darkhawk again and as Darkhawk turns around, Wave of XTC!!! XTC just dropped Darkhawk!! XTC rolls out of the ring and heads back to the dressing rooms! PZ: Look at Tiny!! Tiny slides Godfather on top of Darkhawk!! The ref is waking up. He sees the cover and is crawling over to Darkhawk!!!! 1 ......................................... 2 ................................................................. 3!!!!! Godfather wins!! Godfather is the new BBWF World Heavyweight Champion!!! We will see you Wednesday Night!! Good Night Everyone!!!!! ***** FADE OUT *******
