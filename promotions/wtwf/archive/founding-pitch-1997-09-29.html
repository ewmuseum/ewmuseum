BRaND NeW WReSTLiNG FeD!!! WTWF HAS HIT THE STANDS!!!!!!!!!!!

29 September 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
WTWF The Next Generation In Pro Wrestling World Titan Wrestling Federation WELCOME Welcome To The Next Generation In Pro Wrestling. This is World Titan Wrestling. This is the big time. For Months upon months me and my co-prez have been formulating the perfect federation and after five months, we finally found the perfect time to unleash the WTWF. Me and my co-prez have ran many feds before that were good but were lacking something, and we`ve finally found out what we were lacking. And we`ve put it in to here. World Titan Wrestling Federation is a very unique and very original Wrestling Federation. You see all these other new feds pop up left and right but they`re all the same! And after about six months of that I`ve decided to start a brand new riveting and exciting federation that will be like no other ever seen. Here in World Titan Wrestling Federation we tell it like it is, We don`t hide nothin from our members and we Explain EVERYTHING so players get a full idea of What WTWF Truly is. Like I said, WTWF IS VERY ORIGINAL even our app is original becuase if you don`t agree why I have chosen a certian system, a certain day a certain anything, I explain why I chose it any why it is better then the other choices. So please read ALL of the app. If you like what you hear so far, read on because it gets alot better! WRESTLERS Okay, this just my opinion, but I think 24 singles wrestlers (plus a few jobbers that I make) and about 8 tag-teams is More then suffecient. At this number, Our federation is not too big, and not too small. It won`t be confusing either. It is a very managable number. Also, becuase we`re doing two cards a week, we should be able to put out very readable, exciting cards that everyone will be able to get the swing of. There will be 4 singles matches and 2 tag-team matches On the smaller card and seven singles matches and two tag-team matches on the other card. If anyone doesn`t get a match in a weeks span they will be put on one of the two interview shows that we have. Hope fully, This will create a fed that will keep a majority of players intrested. So I guess what I`m trying to say is that you may not get a match a week but you`ll be involved somehow and If I don`t have enough time to give everyone a match I will give matches to those who deserve them. Also, We`re only allowing 1 singles wrestler or tag-team to each person. Usually In new federations The president allows a person to have two or three people thinking that it will fill up the numbers, the truth is, there isn`t any hurry. And as a wise man once said "Good things come to those who wait." By us allowing you guys to have one wrestler per player we`ll get a better character development. Furthermore, there is less chance of one of the characters being neglected. I know Of alot of handlers who control two or more wrestlers in one fed and they do it well, but two other reasons have to be considered. (1) The more wrestlers a player has, the more power they have over other wrestlers, using two as one unit. (This is allowed when players combine their wrestlers to make stables and things) To be fair to all players you will all have the same number of wrestlers except if you choose a tag-team. (You can also team up with other players to form tag-teams.) (2) The more players we have in the fed, the more interpersonal dynamics will play a role. Think About it. Would you perfer a 24 man fed with 12 players or 24?The more players we have the more chances of different approaches and angles and storylines. BOOKED OR SIMMED? I see the benefeits of TNM (SIM), I perfer HJS because of it`s flexiblity. Besides, TNM is probably responsible for 75% of the feds out there that are formed and flopped anyway. Kid comes along, Plays TNM and Thinks WOW! and then thinks again WOW, I can do this with other people. Only to find they haven`t got the time. HJS- The Human Judgement System. Hideously refereed to as "Booking", usues the most advanced computer processor in the entire world - The Human Brain. This only works for presidents who are not biased. And I am not biased at all. HJS is whereI Fairly judge the outcome depending on stats, strats, angles, heat, involvement, etc, HJS remains the most flexible system, allowing any type of match, and allowing for absolutely anything to happen. It can include any factor and is quick to program. And it can take use of something a computer doesn`t have - Imagination. Imagination is often the distinguishing this between HJS and SIM Feds. And It`s a HUGE difference if you ask me. OUR FLASH SYSTEM (ROLE-PLAYING) Well, I got a choice of two. There is the Closed Flash System, used by very succesful federations such as the APOW. Alternatively, there is the Open Flash Sytem, Used by exciting, fun federations such as IEUW. CFS is great for that, you know, professional feel. Basically, everyone sends their flashes/interviews into one main person who edits them together. Sometimes commentary is added to give the finnished article a "magazine" kind of feel, but more often then not, the flashes are simply edited together. I`ve found the CFS limiting to the devolpment of feuds. BUT Much more heat can be generated through a system called... OFS- Open Flash System. Basically, everyone has the distribution list and you individually send out your flashes. It`s the system I`m most used to, and it gives the fed a 24hr feel, with stuff happening with out me having to be involved. Sometimes it is difficult to keep up with all the things and sometimes there are just too many flashes, but genrally, this is the most fun and from what I can tell, develops stronger bonds and famiarity between players. Feds are often more active this way and... I think thats why I`m going to use the OFS. OUR TITLES These are what everybody works for. What everybody works their butts off for. This is what it`s all about! The World Titan Wrestling Federation World Belt- This is the most prestigous belt known to mankind. NUFF SAID! The World Titan Wrestling Federation North American Belt- This is the Second Highest Belt known to Mankind. If you`re not too sure of yourself about going for World Title try for this. The World Titan Wrestling Federation Television Title- Thius Title Will Get you going on your way to Some higher belts but This truly is the working mans title. The World Titan Wrestling Federation Tag-Team Titles- This is for the Greatest Tandem In The Wrestling Buisness Today. Only for the GOOD Tag-Teams! OUR CARDS Okay. Our cards are where the real war begins. I believe that two cards a week Is a good number. We`re not overdosing, and there is not too long of a gap for things to go quiet. Perhaps when we start one a week is neccesary but I think Two is good. We`re gonna have a midweek card and a weekday card. I`m gonna try my absolute best to get everyone on a card in a week. But I can`t promise this. I will give more time and attention to matches with greater significance or importance (grudge matches, title matches). If people are role-playing extensively, I repay you with a full well written match. If people are not role-playing, I`m not gonna waste my time writing a long match for you. It`s a reciprocal process. Now about the PPVs. PPVs here at WTWF are different. First of all, PPVs are getting less rare every month. PPVs suffer from overabundance. There used to be a time when A PPV Was actually something special, nowadays we got three a month. THAT IS WAY TOO MUCH! I want to recapture that specialness and excitement that PPVs once had. I think PPVs should be used sparingly, to close fueds, to start feuds and for big devlopments. Four a year sounds good to me and it will keep the fed More exciting and running longer. Here are the days when the fights are: SUNDAY NIGHT MAIN EVENT!!!!! Here it is ladies and gentlemen! This is the hottest 2 and a half hours of wrestling to ever hit Television! SNME is hosted By WTWF President Warren White, wrestler Joe Madison and Gary Adams. Sunday Night Main Event contains 6 LIVE matches. Including An Interview in WTWFs very Own Heart And Sole Interview show hosted by Jimmy Heart and Slicksta Sole. SUNDAY NIGHT MAIN EVENT is Brodcasted all over the world on The World Wide Wrestling Channel, Each Sunday Night at 8:00 PM WST and 11:00 EST. WEDNESDAY NIGHT BomB!!! The very best Wrestling show On The weekDays! Hosted By Tommy Maggs, James Cook and Rob McBride. BomB Holds 5 Live matches!!! Sunday Night BomB Also has an interview show but instead of Heart And Sole we have WTWF`s very own Kelly Star hosting her very own 10 mintue interview show where three of World Titan Wrestling Federation`s Top Stars tell us some of the things on their mind in sort of a Talk show fashion. This show is also Broadcasted on The WORLD WIDE WRESTLING Channel every Wednesday night at 9:00 EST! ALSO, HERES A SPECIAL TREAT! WORLD WAR WRESTLING WIRED!!! Sick of those boring news shows? Well the WTWF has something new for you! Once a month, on a Monday night just before Raw and Nitro hit the air the WTWF will bring you WTWF Wired. A hip and brand new wrestling news show. Get all the big stories, the rumors that float around the locker room and even chat with some WTWF superstars. All of this hosted By The Beutiful Kelly Star and Rob McBride. Dont miss WTWF Wired, Once a month on a Monday night at 7pm est (4pm on the west cost) THE PPV`S! SOUL OF THE SURVIVOR!!! Every March there is two teams of five... Squaring off in full force elimanation combat... only the Tough Survive. All Of The Winners of each Survivor Series match are put into a Survivor Series of The Survivors. The Survivor or survivors Of The Final one will be Known as The Fittest men in WTWF untill the next March when it all happens again.. The Soul Of The Survivor Will prevail... SUMMER SPECTACLE Every Summer WTWF Likes to show it`s finest. Giving everyone one Big BANG To bring in the new Summer! Feuding people will meet here to try to get even with Enemies in a night full of Stipulation and Gimmick matches. One of the most exciting and EXTREME nights in WTWF History! It truly is a Summer Spectacle. ROYAL WAR Each and every Wrestler in WTWF Competes in This. The Ultimate Fight to decide The One man who can throw each wrestler over the top rope. The winner will get a match at Wrestlefest against The Champion of WTWF. This can be the very stepping stone to launch A Rookie in to Championship Status. Anything caN happen and ANYBODY CAN WIN!! WRESTLEFEST This is the one. This is The Grand daddy of them all. This is the very event which everyone waits for. This is where Arch Enemies face each other in one on one competition to finally settle the score. To finally prove whos the best. This is also where The Champions must face the number one contenders of their belts. Can They Keep them?
