Monday Massacre, Part 1

25 January 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[We fade into from a black screen to the outside of the arena, where a camera is following "Sheriff" Damon Hayes to the front doors. Hayes isn't dressed for action, a pair of simple blue jeans and a green windbreaker being the extent of his attire. His dreadlocks are tied back neatly, the coarse hair bouncing against his shoulders. Hayes makes that annoying "svish-svish" sound as he walks, but that's the price for wearing a windbreaker. Despite not being dressed to wrestle, Damon carries a bulging duffel bag in one hand and a similarly filled backpack on his shoulders.]
[As Damon reaches the doors, a member of the LSW security team steps into his way. The man, his name-tag reading 'Buddy,' is actually a few inches taller than Damon and is relatively muscular. It's hard work controlling those fans.
Damon comes to a halt and looks quizzically to Buddy as the security guard speaks.] Buddy: You aren't scheduled to wrestle tonight, Mr. Hayes. DH: [Grinning.] Hey, good call, champ. Buddy: [Gesturing toward the bags.] What's with the equipment? DH: Oh, this? [Pause.] It's Steve Sire's supply of kiddy porn. He sells it on weekends. I'm going to expose him! The bastard!
Buddy: [Scratching his head.] Uh. I don't think I should let you into the building, Mr. Hayes. See, there're stipulations for the mainevent. If you interfere I'll have to arrest you. And, you'll be fired.
DH: [A hearty laugh.] Oh, Buddy. Poor, sweet, dear, inbred, Buddy. I'm not going to interfere. Besides, you have to let me in. I bought a ticket. I'm a fan tonight. [Deadpan.] Go Pelican Boy.
[Indeed, he did. Damon reaches into his coat and flashes the rectangular piece of cardboard that is a Monday Massacre ticket. He then smiles and pushes past the security guy.]
Buddy: Oh. Um. Have a good time, Mr. Hayes. DH: I will. You're doing a bang-up job, Buddy. Have a good night. [Damon ducks into the arena. With that, the usual Massacre intro kicks in. The infamous Massacre logo charges onto the screen in supersonic fashion. It slams down onto the screen, engulfing in flames. Once it cannot take it anymore...
BOOM! The screen shatters and we cut to the interior of a jam-packed Astrodome
Convention Center. The camera pans the sea of fans. It's now that we get a chance to witness that enormous steel cage for the main event, hanging from the ceiling. Just about every audience members sports their LSW merchandise, holding up their color signs for the camera to see. Some of the more notable ones include: "Cooper FEARS Desade!", "Pelican Boy Must Die!", "Match of the
Century--Curtis versus Scott", and "'Sissy' Steve". The camera seeks out the broadcast booth. And when it finds it, we zoom in. Curtis and Scott stand by, headsets on and all.]
Curtis Miller (CM): Welcome to Monday Massacre! Hello everyone, I'm Curtis
Miller. And as always, he's Scott Waters. Tonight, we've got a condensed
Massacre format. But that shouldn't detract from the great action we already have planned for you here tonight.
Scott Waters (SW): Yeah, and the powers that be are forcing two of the greatest athletes in all of the NWC to wrestle each other!
CM: Althought I don't totally agree with Scott's statement, I'm sure he's talking about the main event. It'll be "Perfect" Raymond Pierce, the challenger... he meets the champion, "Sexy" Steve Sire! The NWC title will be on the line tonight! And we already saw the number one contender to the title enter the building tonight and--Wait! Here he comes!
SW: Gimmie' a break! Don't stop the show for that idiot! [We cut to the crowd, front row to be exact. "Sheriff" Damon Hayes, bags slung over his shoulder, nachos in one hand, coke in the other... takes his seat in the front row. Many of the fans seated nearby crowd Hayes, looking for autographs and such.]
SW: Somebody needs to get him outta' here! CM: He did purchase a ticket. Folks, tonight we've also got Pelican Boy defending the Television Title up against "Blitzkreig" Jerry Olsen! Should be a helluva' match! Olsen is a former TV champion himself, and Pelican Boy... he's had a pair of successful defenses since becoming champion. Als--Hold on a second! Folks, folks... I believe we have some commotion in the back. Um... do we have a camera back there?
SW: What?! What happened? CM: Yeah, they've got a camera. Let's cut to the back. [And we do so. What do we find? The NWC champion, Steve Sire laid out in the back, bloodied. He's not moving at all and looks to be in very, very bad shape.]
CM: Whoa! [Some members of the security as well as the medical team rush to Sire's aid. The "Sexy One" continues to lay on the cold, concrete... not moving an inch. It's definite that he's unconscious. With that, we cut back to the arena.] CM: My God! "Sexy" Steve Sire... the challenger for tonight's event... has been layed out! Sire is hurt!
SW: Who could've done such a thing?! CM: Y'know, I don't wanna' point any fingers... but the Elite are the obvious choices. SW: No way! That's proposterous! CM: Like I said, I don't want to point any fingers. And there's definitely no proof that it was the Elite. Oh man, strange happenings already.
SW: So what happens? What is Steve can't wrestle? Huh? Huh? CM: I'm not sure. We've little to no information about Sire's condition. And when we do get any information, we'll bring that to you promptly. But Scott,
Si-- [Without warning, the Astrodome Convention Center erupts into jeers. The camera cuts to the entranceway, and who's our guest? LSW's own, commissioner David
Cooper. Boy, does he look angry. Cooper marches down to the ring, sporting beige slacks and a white shirt.]
SW: Ut-oh. [He doesn't look too happy, not one bit. Coop' marches up the stairs, the crowd still jeering madly. He enters the ring, snatching the microphone out of Cole's hand, practically out of spite. Poor Adam. He does his best to quickly exit the ring. And Cooper? He ignores the crowd, looking to say what he wants to say.]
David Cooper (DC): First of all, I'd like for everyone to shut their mouths. [Oh yeah, like that'll happen. The jeers only increase.] DC: Tonight, I'll have a number of items to get to. However, as of right now, there's only one topic on my mind. Whether you saw SCCW's High Stakes last night, or not... you probably heard that Desade--
[Loud pop for the director of the reigning LSW Tag Team champs.] DC: Yeah, yeah. Nonetheless, you might've seen her showing her face on camera. That doesn't sit well with me, not at all. You see, Desade seems to believe that Cobra and Croc are her property. No, she's dead wrong.
[Only more jeering from the crowd. But hey, anyone who insults Desade won't get any cheers.]
DC: She can't seem to realize that the Black Ops are MY team. They're MY creation. They're MY employees. Desade, I'm not exactly aware of what your problem is. The fact of the matter is, you have no say here. It is I who signs the checks for Cobra and Croc. It is I who books them for matches, not you. You tag along, and pretend to actually serve a purpose. Well, I'll make this very clear cut for you. Either you stop laying claim to the Black Ops, or--
[Cooper's immediately cut off by the thunderous pop from the fans. We cut to the entranceway, and there she is, the Director of the LSW Tagteam Champions.
Desade stands at the entranceway, her impassive features locked on Cooper in the ring. It's as if she doesn't hear the fans, as if she and the LSW's owner are the only two people in the building.]
CM: Oh, she won't be happy about this. Not one bit! SW: Have you ever actually _known_ her to be happy, Miller? [But the fans don't think that. They're booming for the unorthodox woman as she all but stalks to ringside, those frosted steel gray eyes on the commissioner who waits motionlessly. The Director's dressed simply tonight in a double-breasted, navy blue man's business suit, dark hair pulled back away from her face, away from her eyes.]
[She climbs into the ring and gets face-to-face with the commissioner, showing no fear... she's actually showing no emotions whatsoever. From inside the sportscoat, she produces a microphone, and, without ever looking away from
Cooper, the mic is raised to her lips.]
Desade: Or what, David? What will you do? Will you fire me? Release me to seek employment elsewhere? Ban me from the arena on Sunday at Malicious Intent? [A slight, slight smile.] You won't do that, Commissioner. You won't do that because I am making you money. Every fan who buys posters with me on them... or
Black Ops t-shirts or hats... puts money in _your_ pocket. And you are far too much of a money-grubbing coward to ever give that up.
[The crowd "Oooooh's" for Desade's harsh words. Cooper's eyes widen and he steps close to the nearly-motionless Director.]
Desade: So go ahead. Fire me. Before you had Black Ops, you had the American
Avengers and All Play and No Business as your tagteam champions. If you wish to return to the days when the tag team division in Texas is the laughingstock of the entire Council... or, worse yet, if you wish to prevent the Outlanders from reigning because they _blackmailed_ you... then fire me. I will go and you will never find Black Ops again.
[She seems to be amused by this, judging from that small glint in the corners of her eyes.]
Desade: But. If you do so, I trust you've knowledge of the darkness? Do you think you can control Croc and Cobra without me? Are you willing to delve into those places that no one wants to go to? The nooks and crannies of this world that my men dwell in? Do you? Are you willing to face your greatest fears to step into my world? I don't think you're any more ready than Ray Pierce is to know the truth past what is in your delusional mind.
[Finally, _finally_ the Director takes a step back away from Cooper, that slight ghost of a smile trailing back onto her features.]
Desade: Black Ops are _my_ team, Cooper. I found them... I trained them in the ways of shadow and light, in the myths of smoke and mirrors... I control them.
Without Desade, there would be no Black Ops in LSW. Remember that and remember it well, dear, sweet David. Wish for a happy ending, David. Because I don't often offer second chances, and this... this is yours.
[Loud pop for Desade, yeah... she told him alright. Cooper appears slightly startled. But egoism surpasses astonishment as David simply chuckles in front of Desade's face. He brings his microphone to his lips, ready to reply.]
DC: Alright, first off all... what you need to do is back up, and outta' my face. You understand?
[Lots of jeering for Cooper. Desade looks on, not the least bit intimidated by
Cooper's "threat." Nor does she step back even a hair.]
DC: You proposed some interesting options, Desade. Can I fire you? Damn right. Would I fire you? In a drop of a hat. But you're correct. You do put money in my pocket. Don't get me wrong, it's like the saying goes... women like you...
are a dime a dozen. [Whoa... Desade doesn't look to happy about that. Then again, she wasn't happy to begin with. The crowd continues it's jeering, Cooper does his best to speak over them.]
DC: And rather than going through the hassle of searching for a replacement... I've actually formulated something better. So I won't fire you. [Sigh of relief from the male Desade fans.] SW: [Low.] Thank you! DC: Fine you? [Cooper slowly turns his back to Desade, as he follows through with the next statement. Some fans chant "Hit Him! Hit Him!", but Cooper pays them no mind and Desade only lets loose with a bit of a smile.]
DC: Yeah, ten grand should be enough. [Cooper turns back to Desade, the crowd booing unmercifully. Desade? Probably doesn't even care about the fine.]
DC: But that's not enough, oh no. Y'see, tonight... I will not take exception to your absurd claim of the Black Ops being yours. No, not at all. Y'see, tonight you'll definitely get that claim. Because tonight, you'll be managing the Black Ops, but not in a tag team title match.
CM: [Low.] Non-title tag bout? DC: I know what you're all thinking, perhaps a non-title match? [Cooper grins slightly, almost as if he read Curtis' mind.] DC: Not that either. Y'see tonight, Desade... you will manage the Black Ops. But you will manage the Black Ops against... [Cooper pauses, the crowd awaits in anticipation.] SW: Who? Who? [David smiles, one of those devilish smiles.] DC: ... EACH OTHER! CM: What?! SW: HA! DC: Yes, that's right. You will be at ringside. We'll find out where your loyalty lies, Desade. As it'll be Cobra against Croc! Black Ops member versus
Black Ops member! Now they'll see what your [miming quotes with one hand]
"guidance" has gotten them into. Have a blast, Desade. Oh, and to prevent you from worming your way out of this... it's no-disqualification and no-countout.
And if they don't wrestle, they will _lose_ that precious NWC title shot! [The crowd gasps as Cooper backs off, exiting the ring, grinning like a madman. He hops off the apron, heading towards the back, completely ignoring Desade and the crowd.]
Desade: David, dearheart... if I told you I was surprised, I would be lying. And if you think for a moment that this will weaken my power base... if you think I'll lose my grip on my men so you can step in and guide them... if you think you've won... well, then you're nothing but wrong, Mr. Cooper.
[Cooper doesn't even seem to be acknowledging that Desade's speaking, just slowly walking up the ramp.]
Desade: It's a lesson that's difficult to learn... hard to understand... and easy to forget. But take heart, my dear commissioner. It's a lesson I shall very much enjoy teaching you. Sleep lightly, David. You've not seen the last of me, and... beware the shadows.
[A small, indulgent smile crosses her features as she looks around the crowd, many of whom speak along with her next words.]
Desade: They move when you're not looking... CM: There you have it, folks! Croc vs. Cobra, no-DQ, no-countout here tonight! Which side will the Director be on? SW: Well, she SHOULD be on my bedside but I -- CM: --will you stop! Fans, we'll be back with more Massacre, as well as a
Television title defense... after this commercial break!
[Fade to commercial--"Malicious Intent: Dangerous Destiny" on January 24th
1999. Triple main event... NWC Title match, NWC Tag Title match, LSW
Heavyweight Title Ladder match. Order now and receive a free Desade poster!]
