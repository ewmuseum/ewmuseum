NWC Releases Face Off at High Noon 2

14 April 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Nick Radkowsky here again, from the NWC promotional department, with yet another great NWC success. This one roots out of Texas via Lone Star Wrestling. The LSW was one of the charter members of the NWC, and remains the only fed that has not undergone a change in commissioner. David Cooper of the LSW has been at the helm for over a year of incredible service to the NWC, and continues this great tradition as they release the highly anticipated Face Off at High Noon 2. Not only did this card feature a preview card which set up the excitement for this huge event, it also featured the defense of the most coveted belt in the National Wrestling Council. The Heavyweight title, held by Mr. Sinister, was put on the line against the LSW's Avalon Britton.
If you only read the portion posted here, you will be depriving yourself of an incredible supercard. So head over to the promotion's own show page (lsw.hypermart.net/fahn2show.html). Once you're done with that, check out the NWC's own home page (members.tripod.com/~nwcouncil/).
Thanks again, —Nick Radkowsky, NWC Publicity Director
