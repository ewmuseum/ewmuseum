[NWC] (LSW) Monday Massacre 2

30 December 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Continued results from Part One.) ------------------------------- [David Cooper]: For the Lone Star Wrestling Regional Title, the champion Trevor McWade will meet the number one contender "Deadly" Dave Morgan! [The fans let out a loud cheer for the hometown hero] [David Cooper]: In a casket match, B.A.D. Blood will meet Mr. Frankenstein! In a no disqualification, pinfalls count anywhere match, "The Extremist" Chuck Kelley meets "Old School" Tony Mac! Then, the Lone Star Wrestling Tag Team Champions will be decided in a Four Way Bunkhouse Dance between The Ukrainian Brothers, The Desperados, and the LSW's two new tag teams: Too Hot To Handle, and Pain and Agony! [The fans react with louder cheers] [David Cooper]: More matches to come very soon. Now, as some of you may know, The National Wrestling Council will hold their first ever interpromotional card on January 17th and 18th, from what I hear. They're requesting three singles wrestlers and one tag team from each promotion to compete in that extravaganza! In Lone Star Wrestling, the Heavyweight Tag Team champions will attend the NWC's event. The Heavyweight Champion and the Regional Champion. Therefore, after Disturbance In Dallas, anyone not holding a title will be placed in a battle royal to fill the third spot! That's all at this moment, thank you. [The fans go wild as Cooper steps through the ropes and out of the ring] [Curtis Miller]: WOW! What announcements from our Commissioner! Disturbance In Dallas will be huge! [Scott Waters]: I'm actually agreeing with you, Miller. Cooper even surprised me this time. [Curtis Miller]: [Looks at Waters with a stunned look] [Scott Waters]: What? Its not like a respect the old bag, though. HAHA! [Curtis Miller]: I knew it was too good to be true. Folks, we're outta' time. Make sure you tune in for Friday Warfare this Friday night! Check your local listings for time and availability. Well, for Scott Waters... [Scott Waters]: [Motions at the camera] [Curtis Miller]: ... Adam Cole, Commissioner Cooper, and the rest of the LSW Staff, so long everyone! We hope you enjoyed Monday Massacre! [The screen fades to black, and in gold lettering the words... "Lone Star Wrestling" http://members.aol.com/cleverref/LSW.html ... Are presented. The full screen then slowly fades to all black once again, as the broadcast goes off the air] ------------------------------- Check out the websites!
