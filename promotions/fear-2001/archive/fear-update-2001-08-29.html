Vince Knight -3 0 1 0 The way I will do the rankings is how Gordon Adair did them in the NWWA. If you win or lose by one of these methods, you will either gain or lose the specified number of points. Submission: 5. Submissions, I feel, are honorable, and in my opinion, the best possible outcome. Also, in FEAR, it's okay to submit. It's not shameful or anything like that. Pinfall: 3. This is what most outcomes will be. CO/DQ: 1 Draw: 0.
