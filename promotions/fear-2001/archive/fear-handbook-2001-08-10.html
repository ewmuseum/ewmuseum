This is a preliminary handbook for Frontier Extreme American Rasslin'. This is my new e-fed, if you haven't figured that out yet. Um, join. Yeah. Do that. Shitty introduction, yes? 2. MANIFESTO ------------ You've heard it before, and you're about to hear it again: FEAR is an e-fed meant to have fun in. But this time, we mean it. Egos here will not be tolerated. Mine is big enough as it is, thank you very much. The atmosphere is going to be very laid back (i.e., if a card's late, don't bitch about it. Go live your life), so don't worry too much about things. It ain't no thang, as I always say. Okay, so I don't say that always. Go die. NEXT! 3. DO YOU KNOW FEAR? ------------------------- A. WHAT'S IN A NAME? F - Frontier, because we hope to forge new ground in the world of pro wrestling, taking you, the fan, into a world never before dreamed of. E - Extreme, because we make our own rules. FEAR is a privately owned company, fully backed by Micheal Price and his associates. A - American, because FEAR is not a world wide wrestling show, or even a totally national one. All its shows will originate from the USA. R - Rasslin', because it's what we're all about. B. WHO'S DA BOSS? Surprisingly, this is a bit of a difficult question. Do you mean in or out of character? Well, OOC, it's me, [name withheld]. That's it. I'm running this the way I want to run it. Of course, I may call on handlers to write matches or help out in some form or fashion, but all decisions are ultimately mine. In character, the promotion is ran by Micheal Price, aka "The Walking Contradiction," Sabbath, as well as his wife, Sayaka. There are others, however, and the chain of command goes something like this: Micheal Price: The president and owner of FEAR, Micheal Price is more commonly known as Sabbath in numerous other promotions. Went into retirement during his wife's 2nd pregnancy, and out of active competition indefinately, due to several serious knee injuries dating back to 1997. He is the backbone of FEAR. Randy Steele: Randy is FEAR's "commissioner," a figurehead who will do most of the TV shows, leaving the PPVs for Price. He also doubles as FEAR's talent scout. Sayaka Daikin-Price & Tony McMoore: The "bookers" for FEAR, they come up with a majority of the angles, and supervise the television programs. Tony also hosts "The Smell of FEAR," every Wednesday night. Stephen Reynolds: The retired wrestler formerly known as Requiem has taken on the dubious task as chief of security. However, pity the fool who crosses the 6'8" 290 lbs. giant from nothern California. Jack Scull: A legendary professional wrestler, Scull was a perfect choice for head referee. He has been a referee since his retirement, and calls things straight down the middle. He also doesn't hold much respect for either of the two Jr. Referees, and will be more than happy to come to ringside to admonish those "punks." Jake Steele & Patrick McMoore: Jake is an alright guy. He really is. He's just too easily wowed by the ladies in the crowd to pay attention to the match. And it doesn't help that he's a handsome guy at that, and the ladies are almost always trying to get his attention. Patrick, on the other hand, is an asshole, and not the lovable type. At only 18 years old, he's the son of booker Tony McMoore. He rather dislikes his job, as his father forced him to take it to pay for a vehicle for school, and as a result, doesn't give two craps as to what goes on in the ring. B. A long, long time ago... FEAR is a concept that sprung from the twisted minds of Micheal and Sayaka Daikin-Price, the former better known as Sabbath. During Sayaka's 2nd pregnancy, Sabby suffered a career ending knee injury, forcing him out of wrestling. As the injury healed, however, he became bored with sitting around his phat palacial Seattle estate, and with the help from a few old friends, brought his tiny creation to life. Are you scared? C. Championships FEAR currently recognizes three titles, (with a secondary heavyweight belt to come later, perhaps) all of which must be defended at least once a month, as well as every supercard. Frontier Heavyweight Title: This belt will be held by the top dog in the promotion, and can be won by anyone...so long as they can hang in the upper echelon of FEAR. Super Juniors Title: Perhaps the most exciting matches in FEAR will be contested over this prestigious belt. Only wrestlers 230 lbs. and under may participate. Tag Team Titles: (NOTE: The tag titles will not be brought into play until we have at least five active teams) E. FEAR Productions FEAR will only have one show a week, entitled "The Smell of FEAR." It will be a live action card, typically with 6 fully written matches. Occasionally, there will be a dark match or two if necessary. "The Smell of FEAR" is broadcast every Wednesday night at 10 pm on Channel 23, and rebroadcast every Saturday morning at 10 am for those who might've missed it Wednesday. The program can be seen by anyone with a satillite, and by most everyone down the West Coast. The show will generally be taped in the Seattle, Washington, area, and will occasionally branch out up and down the west coast, and maybe into the mid- west as necessary. "The Smell of FEAR" will be hosted by Tony McMoore, and his partner in crime, "Devious" Dan Richardson, who, despite the scary sounding name, is not scary at all. He's quite pansy-ish, to be honest. As far as Pay Per View's go, there are none. C'mon, FEAR uses equipment donated by Knights of Kazrick TV in Schaan, Liechtenstein. You think we can afford PPV right now? Please. There will be, however, supercards! Every three months, "The Smell of FEAR" will be pre-empted by a supercard, which will feature a particular gimmick, plus mandatory title defenses. They are, as follows: January: Frostbite April: SLAM! - If you want to get attention, this is your card. At each SLAM! there will be an 8-man tournament that any wrestler may enter, with the winner receiving a Heavyweight title shot at the end of the night. July: Beat the Heat October: Devil's Night - The theme here is evil. Every match will be a sort of gimmick match, with the heavyweight title being contested in the most dangerous of them all. The wrestlers pick the gimmicks here. Also, after every card, there will be an update sent out, featuring results from shows held in the area, but not televised. Only the main shows will be held in the Factory at first. You know the drill with updates, though. 4. Rules & Regulations ---------------------- A. What they are NOTE: Both IC and OOC rules will be strictly enforced. One violation will result in a warning. A second will result in a lack of push for two weeks. A third will be a one month suspension. A fourth will rseult in termination of your services. 1. Your wrestler must be at every show he is scheduled to perform at, unless there is a suitable excuse. It's okay to miss a RP occasionally 2. It is the responsibility of your wrestler to make challenges formally (more on this will be explained later). Otherwise, your match lineups will be left up to me and me alone. 3. Your wrestler may defend his title at any time, however, if no matches are made, I will set them up personally. 4. Your wrestler will not attack any of FEAR's staff (without my OOC permission). 5. YOU will not use other wrestlers belonging to someone else in your RPs and such without permission from the handler. However, attacks are fine, so long as they aren't rampant. 6. DON'T BITCH! 7. DON'T BITCH! 8. DON'T BITCH....got it? 5. The Challenge System ----------------------- FEAR's challenge system will hopefully bring a new aspect to the worl of e-wrestling. Every update will include a challenge board, where I will post challenges made by handlers. The match will not take place until the wrestler being challenged accepts. For example: The Rock challenges Mankind to a Falls Count anywhere match. That's it. All challenges must be made to me and me alone. I will post them. 6. The Application Process -------------------------- A. A few words about the app: please don't waste our time. Only apply if you intend on putting effort into your stay here. Believe me, I've done it before, and wound up pissing some people off. If shit comes up though, and you decide we'r enot for you...just let me know. B.
