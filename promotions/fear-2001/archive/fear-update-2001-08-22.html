FEAR owner Micheal Price has promised an action packed card, and it seems that's what we're going to get! Price has spared no expense in finding the finest talent wrestling has to offer, and, wasting no time, declared that the opening round of the Frontier Heavyweight Title 8-man tournament be held on this landmark episode. In what's sure to be a classic, two old friends are brought together for the first time in years. THE WASP will take on "The Apprentice of FEAR," CHRIS LUPSA! Two other acquaintences will make their mark in FEAR as well: the UFWA's first ever world champion "THE DEMON" VINCE KNIGHT will go head to head with the man many consider to be the most dangerous man in wrestling... "The Black Beast from the East".....SINISTER! Also, the man who wants us to call him our Savior, the incomperable ACE HOLLYWOOD locks horns with a true BMF (that's Bad Muthafucka)..."The Unbeliever" LANCE ORMANDE! And last, but not least, we will see two of the world's finest junior heavyweights look for gold. Neither man may be defined as a heavyweight, but size does NOT mean anything. International sensation EIJI YATSU takes to the sky against EMWC & KCW mainstay, BILAL MASAI! All this and more, coming to you LIVE on THE SMELL OF FEAR! The deadline for this card will be Sunday, August 26th. This includes RPs, strats, angles, and in-rings. -- The Smell of FEAR 09/12/2001 TBA ---------------------- No matches have been signed yet for this card. -- NEXT SUPERCARD!
