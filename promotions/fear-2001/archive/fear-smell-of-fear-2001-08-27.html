After a stop in GAMMA, I was offered a Junior Heavyweight title in the Tokyo Organization of Puroreu against Shoji Suzuki, a legend in puroresu, and a man I defeated before the start of the JSL. I was going to take the offer, but then after thinking I refused it. What had I done to earn that shot? Nothing really. I'd defeated Suzuki months before. I'd tied twice and lost twice to two of the top junior heavyweights in Japan, then had a few wins over lesser wrestlers. I had no real reason to receive a title shot. I knew I needed to prove myself, but TOP wasn't the place to do it. I needed to venture out to a place I wasn't comfortable with. That place is here in FEAR. I'm very grateful for getting the opportunity to compete for the Frontier Heavyweight title. While I promise nothing, I will state that I am extremely driven in this point of my career and wish to succeed at every chance. Bilal Masai, you are my opponent in the first round of the tournament. I wish you the best, but be ready to fight like you've never have. Despite my cool appearance outside the ring, intensity flows through me. When we face off in this arena, I will all I can to prove what I'm capable of. [Masai pauses and catches his breath, then looks down briefly, before setting his dark gaze on the camera and speaking with authority.] Masai: I want to go ahead and tell you this is going to be easy, or that you've got a great match ahead. [The youngster looks down a little, head shaking side-to-side and eyes downcast. It makes no difference to me where your allegiances lie. You're on the opposite side of the ring, and I'll do whatever it takes to put you on the canvas. I have no ground rules about it. I've faced my share of cutthroats, and watched men who said they were choir boys prove to be the worst of all. I walk into this not expecting you to respect me or believe me when I tell you I can do things you've never seen before. [Masai's eyes stay fixed in a way that punctuates his dead serious expression. The crowd seems to sit on edge as Masai starts to pace again, now agitated.] Masai: They doubted me when I came into virtually every company, until I clawed my way to where I wanted to be. And here I am, one of the hardest working men in this business -- unafraid to take that leap, break that bone or go just that far to prove what I've proven time and again. You can build muscles. You can reate dark hearts. You can't fabricate determination, desire or a will to achieve. [Nice pop! Masai pauses, lips pursed as he considers the task before him. Skeptics become believers in the presence of Bilal Masai, Eiji. That's not ego talking. That's history, my friend. And tonight you become part of it. Believe that.
