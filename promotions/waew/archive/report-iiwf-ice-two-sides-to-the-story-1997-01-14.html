[REPORT] IIWF-ICE. Two Sides to The Story

14 January 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
************************************************************************** *** IIWF, ICE Report by [name withheld] Handler of ICE's J.P. Steele and match writer Handler of IIWF's Danny Boy Joseph (replacing Steele on the wait list and match writer ************************************************************************** * Hello RSPWF The following report is my ideas (keyowrd MY. Opinions only) on the IIWF, ICE. Recently, there were claims that ICE "copied" the IIWF web page. Perhaps that's true. I could deal with that, which Daniel had said before. But when one [name withheld] ranted on, predicting ICE would follow suit, and claiming it was a "second-rate federation"...that I could not deal with. As an active member of ICE and a big fan of the IIWF...this took me by surprise. There were no flame wars or such between feds...just Daniel saying ICE had copied his IIWF page. I could live with [name withheld]'s comment on that. However, when one [name withheld]--a man whom to my knowledge has never stepped foot on ICE soil--made some claims this report became nessacary in my mind. For the remainder of this writing, I will remain impartial. I can see both sides of the story, seeing I am in both leagues and understand both. While I may have been in ICE longer, and maybe know [name withheld] better than I know Daniel, I will remain impartial. First, the copying web page claim. This is one that definantly I can see eye to eye with on IIWF with. True, the IIWF page was up before ICE's (though the leagues have bee around since practically the same day). And yes, the ICE page did resemlbe the look of the old ICE one.So IIWF, chalk up one point for yourselves in this. Right on that account. ICE's page in my opinion IS a copy. But to that I say...so what? Is it in some constitutional law that one may not copy the look of another's fantasy wrestling internet web site? Is the IIWF page copyrighted the old look? NO. It's kind of pointless to make a big deal out of THAT. I have noted that both Daniel and Mike have " co.uk " in their addresses. While I am not 100% sure on this...I believe that has something to do with their server. Perhaps I am wrong. BUT, if I am right...wouldn't it make sense that this particular server supplies an editor for a home page...which allows BOTH Daniel and Mike to make similiar pages for their respective leagues? Nod if you're awake people. That part was easier to remain impartial...and not on ICE's side, since I do agree on the IIWF with that. But what's next is [name withheld]'s claim that ICE was a second rate league, and probably copies IIWF's cards and wrestlers. Rog...perhaps you should pay close attention in this thing before you jump to conclusions. Second rate? Hmm, shouldn't one be active and constant in both leagues for a considerable amount of time before passing judgement? I think so. And, if my memory of ICE (dating back 'til June when I joined) serves me correct I have yet to so you in ICE. Hmm, looks like someone's just taking sides with no true basis. Starting a little war when nothing was needed. ICE "supposdly" copied IIWF's page. Daniel changes his. End or story. Or so it seems...because soon enough Roger jumps in with nill comments. By now, you may be wondering my opinion of both leagues. Which do I feel is better and the like. Honestly, at this point and time--IIWF. It does have better cards, bigger names, and is more consitent. But does that mean ICE isn't good? HECK NO! Another thing to keep in mind...Daniel has multiple match writers to work on his cards. Mike doesn't. ICE's cards are done by Mike--with an occasional match or two being done by me (or at least that's now what is being done). With less help, it's hard to match IIWF's ten matches a card live object. (Though I do think that's a little too much, seven or eight is quite enough for a weekly card IMO). I must say that the IIWF does a fantastic job with things, but so does ICE. Maybe not as good...but remember:IIWF _is_the number one fed out there in 1996 going by the RSPWF Awards. So Roger...wouldn't that mean that ANY OTHER fed is "second rate" when compared to the #1 league in 1996? Including your TOWL. (Don't even get me started on that, or impartiality goes out the window.) Roger even had that gaul to say think that ICE copies cards and wrestlers. Oh Roger...how much fact in things do you have in this matter? ICE's cards--Sunday Night Freeze (Arena card), Monday Night Carnage, Thursday Night Mayhem, and Roadkill on Ice (being done by me until Feb. when my league WAeW [formerlly CeWF but we had to change names]) in fact do NOT resemble ANY IIWF cards. As for wrestlers...sorry but there are no imitations of the IIWF's top notch stars. The only "similiarities" in the roster are Deathbringer and the Armed Forces who joined ICE this past fall. Both Ron Nanko or Nathan Novak have yet to complain on anything to Mike or the rest of ICE on anything to my knowledge. Both flash often. Such intrest tells me they enjoy the fed--thus WANTING to participate. But, I won't speak for them since I don't know their views on this matter. The fact remains that this shouldn't even be an issue. ICE "supposedly" copied the look of the IIWF's page. Daniel changed the look. So, what's the problem now between the feds? Nothing. Yet, [name withheld] chose to stick his nose into the matter without knowing both sides of the story. I know those two sides. I stick up for both the ICE and the IIWF. It's my opinion that both leagues--unique and exciting in their own ways--are great. Yeah, maybe ICE isn't as good as IIWF...but how many feds are? To my knowledge, their were no comments made from neither Daniel or Mike DIRECTLY to the other on anything on this matter. There ARE no problems between the leagues...despite Roger's flimsy claims and my own retalitations. Just let them be, and enjoy. Whatever you fancy--IIWF, ICE, or any other league just relax. After all, it's just e-wrestling not life or death. If there's one thing I cannot stand it's passing judgement something before you even involve in both things. Weather it be an ICE member passing judgement on IIWF before being in it, or vice versa I belive it's not right. Sure, Roger is entitled to his opinion...but to make it without even being in ICE, just reading a card from RSPWF and seeing a "copied" web page he passes judgement? Can't side with that view in that case. IIWF and ICE are both fantastic leagues. If you're not involved in either (or both) you're really missing out. IIWF may be tthe better of the two, but ICE is a bit easier to get into without a waitling list. Both feds have their own brand of e-wrestling which I for one enjoy on both sides of the fence. IIWF is fantastic, ICE is great. Both are two leagues I enjoy top to bottom. Rant on EITHER one and usually take some offense. I stood up for ICE backing down on claims...but I have also stood up for IIWF on IRC when people from the LOOP leagues (such as DPW and EWA, both of which I am in as well. But THAT's another issue =^). claimed IIWF was over-rated. So, if you chose to like one fed over the other--or neither or both...all for it. Your opinion is yours, and you're free to have it. But please, I hope that you have some proof and truth if and when you make claims. Putting down one league is not really a smart thing IMO. Instead, seeing both sides of the story with fact and logic as I have done is a little more civil and just. Live a little in the other man's shoes...pardon the cliche. Look before you leap. In basic terms...know what your saying before jumping to conclusions if you want to be taken seriosuly by people ('specially me). As of now, there is no "war" or "problem" between federations...despite [name withheld]'s flame throwing. Just let the two feds be sepretly and not cause problems on either side. International Internet Wrestling Federation and Independent Championship E-wrestling are both fun and entertaining leagues in my opinion. And should you state your disagreement...try to have SOME valid points in your case. If you can't back up your opinions--right or wrong--just what worth are they to anyone? Now, anyone here on RSPWF who has had no idea on anything with IIWF or ICE may be confused or bewildered. But those who do, read, comment, enjoy. E-wrestling is just a "game" for fun. Enjoy. --[name withheld] =============================================================== \
