PART TWO: 1.How do I Create a wrestler? 2.What is the Character Creation Sheet? APPENDIX: 1. Acknowledgments ------------------------------------------------------ WHAT IS E-WRESTLING? E-wrestling is a fantasy/imaginary wrestling game based over the Internet. It's foudning father is the old play by mail leagues such as IWA. The leagues have imaginary wrestlers created by "handlers" (members) wo participate in action. Cards are written up, usually in play by play format. The main way to be succesful is roleplaying. The main part of roleplaying is a written interview "in character" as your wrestler. The better the writing, the better the "push" (how your wrestler does overall). ------------------------------------------------------- WHAT IS WAeW? They are: [name withheld] ( [detail withheld]) and "Millennium" ( [detail withheld]). -------------------------------------------------------- WHAT TITLES ARE THERE IN WAeW? WAeW currently sanctions three titles. They are as follows: WAeW World Heavyweight Title: The big one. Universal weight limit. Defended only on pay per views, unless at fed-hed's request. WAew North American Title: The secondary title. 290 pounds and under is the weight limit. Defended on ppvs, and on live cards every 4-6 weeks. WAeW World Tag Team Title:The big one for tags. Universal weight limit. Defended only on pay per views, unless at fed-hed's request. -------------------------------------------------------------- WHAT IS THE WEEKLY SCHEDULE? This is the format of the schedule: Sundays:WAeW Sunday Night Live show, 5 or more matches a week. Mondays:Nothing Tuesdays:Nothing House show, ten or more matches a week. Wednesdays:WAeW Rewind Report Report show with a look at cards, interview,etc. House show, ten or more matches a week. Fridays:Nothing Saturday:Nothing. ---------------------------------------------------------- WHEN ARE PAY PER VIEWS? In order to ensure a sense of "spectacular supercard"ness, we have them every three months. In 1997, the following pay per views will occur. Ten great matches, including the following format:three title matches, one grudge match, two survivor series matches, a final survivor battle royale for a title shot, and three extra bonus matches! SUNWAVE MELTDOWN:WAeW's answer to WrestleMania, this event begins on Independence Day, July 4th. Where else, but from the Bayfront Center in St. Petersburg. This card promises to have everything you could ask for...AND MORE! AUTUMN ARMAGEDDON:November 5th, 1997...from The Igloo in Pittsburgh it's the wildest night of your life. No, nothing "extreme" but expect the un-expected, with such speciality matches as:cage, two out of 3 falls, Texas death, falls count anywhere, and more. ----------------------------------------------------------- HOW DO I DO GOOD IN THE RING? The main factor in deciding pushes is roleplaying. Roleplays can be many things. Written interviews are the main ones, and these are required to be more than 1k for Wednesday, and no more than 10k unless you feel absoultly nessacary. You can also do locker room interviews or "pre-recorded" comments during cards, but you have to let [name withheld] know ahead of time. Also, stratagies of how your wrestle will wreslte that match are accepted. Above all, it's consistancy and quality. If one person goes three weeks with 5k roleplays, strats, and interveis on cards...and is consistant and doing good, they will succed better than a person who does a roleplay every other week and never does a strat or locker room interview. ------------------------------------------------------------- HOW DO I CREATE A WRESTLER? To create a wrestler and/or tag team, you will have to fill out and return the creation sheet below. BUT, we have a few guidelines. 1)BE ORIGINAL. Do not enter any Sabus or Shawn Michaels. 2)BE CREATIVE. Don't rip off a real life character either.
