International Cruiserweight Tournament of Champions — Founding

"ICTC Press Conference #1"

24 October 1998
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[OOC NOTE: If you're good =) and you would still like to be in (Mad Goat, where are you?), you can be placed in a grudge match on the undercard. An Update will be out Monday. Mail me at [detail withheld] .] ---------------------- (The scene fades into a press conference room. This is the Pennsylvania Convention Center at the corner of North 11th Street and Arch Street in Philadelphia, Pennsylvania. This is one of the smaller rooms, but it is filled with reporters and a few fans. Many of the fans are wearing shirts from some of their favorite wrestlers. As the camera pans around, we see shirts saying "1:900 says I just ripped you off!" ... "Step into the Spotlight!" ... "Kore wa sakete toore me, anata no unmei ... You can not avoid your destiny." ... and of course, "I'm Too Confident!" The camera then pans around to other fans, who are wearing shirts from some of their favorite organizations. We see shirts from UEW, IWC, IWF/WoW, SCRA, SJPW, NEW, and G-Pro. As the camera pans around even more, we see that the room is a bright white with a large screen in the front. There are many more fans than originally expected. Suddenly ... the room goes black ... and the fans erupt in a scream and a cheer. The screen lights up to a bright white ... suddenly, four red blood letters run down the screen ... ___ _______ _______ _______ / // _____//__ __// _____/ / // / / / / / / // / / / / / / // /____ / / / /_____ /__//_______/ /_/ /_______/ Then ... a crack of thunder. The screen goes to a black background with the four red letters still staying on the screen. The screen flashes quickly to white and back to black faster and faster, with the four red letters staying constant. Then ... the scene changes to an open prarie. The land is empty, barren, but full of nice, green grass. The sun shines brightly overhead. Suddenly, like traveling overhead high on a plane, the camera moves quickly forward, faster and faster, repeating the movement as if traveling on a globe, until words suddenly come on the screen. They are on top of the land, and fly by quickly ... __________ /___ ___/ / / / / / / / / / / NTERNATIONAL / / / / / / ___/ /___ /_________/ _________ / ______/ / / / / / / / / / / RUISERWEIGHT / / / / / / / /______ /_________/ _________ /___ ___/ / / / / / / / / / / OURNAMENT / / / / / / / / /_/ _____ _____ / ___/ / // /_ / // // _/ /____//__/ _________ / ______/ / / / / / / / / / / HAMPIONS / / / / / / / /______ /_________/ The lights brighten, and standing at a podium in front of the room, is "No Gimmicks Necessary" Al Robertson! The crowd gives a large pop upon seeing this. He is going for the "Sepowicz Look" as he is wearing a short sleeved shirt with a plain blue tie. He is also wearing khaki slacks and brown dress shoes. His blonde hair is now complimented by red highlights on the sides, but the blonde is still the main portion of his hair. He grabs the microphone and begins to speak ...) Al Robertson: Welcome to the International Cruiserweight Tournament of Champions Press Conference! (Large crowd pop.) Al Robertson: We are here today to announce the preliminary tournament matches for ICTC. Let me say that I had a huge turnout, much greater than I expected, so I have decided to do a 24-man tournament rather than a 16-man tournament. Let me explain the tournament. (The screen shows brackets and divisions. The fans look for names, but none are listed yet.) Al Robertson: There are three divisions. One taking place at an yet to be determined site in America, most likely the First Union Center in Philadelphia, Pennsylvania. The second taking place at a yet to be determined site in Mexico, and the third taking place at a yet to be determined site in Japan. Each division is an eight man tournament. The winners of the three divisions will face off in a three-way dance for the trophy in the final match of ICTC. (Al pauses, and lets the fans take in the rules.) Al Robertson: And now ... the brackets ... (The screen shows the words ... AMERICAN DIVISION "Kill Devil" Hakisho Shaniko -| Andrew "Flash" Tucker -|____________ | The Trixxster -| |____________ "Confident" Lawrence Williams -|____________| | |AMERICAN Tasogare no Prince -| |CHAMPION "Money Driven" Mike Sebastian -|____________ | | |________ Sal Summiter -| |____________| "The Spotlight" V.D. Vitrone -|____________| The fans look at the words and let a loud cheer out.) Al Robertson: Yes, that's right. In our opening matchup, the hot newcomer from SCRA and NEW, "Kill Devil" Hakisho Shaniko, will face the established cruiserweight and former cruiserweight champion of INWI, SWP, and NEW, Andrew "Flash" Tucker. And, in our second matchup, the master of jokes and the former IWC Can-Am Champion and SWP Juniorweight Champion, The Trixxster, will lock up with the IeWC World Champion Forever, "Confident" Lawrence Williams. (The screen shows clips from these four mens' careers: A shot of "Kill Devil" Hakisho Shaniko running down the aisle in the SCRA, a shot of Andrew "Flash" Tucker pinning The Trixxster to become the SWP Juniorweight Champion, a shot of "Confident" Lawrence Williams holding up the IeWC World Title, and finally a shot of The Trixxster making The Twilight Prince tap out to become the first ever IWC Can-Am Champion.) Al Robertson: Next, the bottom portion. In the next matchup, my former stablemate Tasogare no Prince, ala The Twilight Prince, the former IWC Can-Am Champion and a top SJPW contender, will face off against "Money Driven" Mike Sebastian, the NEW Cruiserweight Champion. Then, the famous announcer from UEW, Sal Summiter, will debut in the ring in a matchup against the always tough "Spotlight" V.D. Vitrone, the NEW World Champion and #5 ranked Cruiserweight in the world. (The screen shows shots of Tasogare no Prince winning the IWC Can-Am title in the two out of three falls match against the Trixxster, then a shot of "Money Driven" Mike Sebastian covering for the NEW Crusierweight Title. Then it shows a shot of Sal Summiter holding a microphone, then cuts to a shot of "Spotlight" V.D. Vitrone holding the NEW World Title high in the air.) Al Robertson: Now ... we move onto the Mexican Division. (The screen shows the brackets once again, but this time it is the Mexican division ... the fans POP at the huge talent of this division. MEXICAN DIVISION "Ladykiller" Chris Tyler -| Kabuki Kid??? -|____________ | "Mr. Excitement" Alex Extreme -| |____________ "Real Deal" Shawn Harisson -|____________| | |MEXICAN "Mr. Main Event" Chris Douglas-| |CHAMPION "Friar" Michael St. Paul -|____________ | | |________ "Mr. Sincere" Johnny Glamour -| |____________| El Carretera Estrella -|____________| The crowd pops again!) Al Robertson: No one was more surprised than me after signing "Ladykiller" Chris Tyler. This former UEW American Champion and UEW Points Leader will square off against Kabuki Kid. The Kid is a question mark for the tournament, as he contacted me to sign up, but has since vanished, and I am not sure whether or not he is signed. Stay tuned for details. Next up former UEW Television Champion and AeWF World Champion Alex Extreme will take on former King of Cruisers participant "Real Deal" Shawn Harisson. Could Extreme's enemy and Harisson's friend Jeff "Madfox" Matthews be far behind? (The screen shows clips of "Ladykiller" Chris Tyler holding up the UEW American Title and then a shot of him pinning "No Worries" Rob Magnum and then a shot of him defeating Joe Malloy yet again. Then the camera shows a shot of the Kabuki Kid, with a big question mark over it. Then, a shot of "Mr. Excitement" Alex Extreme winning the AeWF World Title is shown and then a shot of him smashing the Stanley Cup over Magnus Colby's head in the Match of the Year from UEW Ides of March '98. Finally, a shot of "Real Deal" Shawn Harisson participating in King of Cruisers is shown.) Al Robertson: Now, the bottom half. "Mr Main Event" Chris Douglas, a man who is known throughout the world for his "Night of Glory" and who competes in SCRA, will take on a newcomer, "Friar" Michael St. Paul. And, in the final match, "Mr. Sincere" Johnny Glamour, a former IWC International Champion, will take on El Carretera Estrella, aka The Highway Star. This is Highway's return to the ring after a long absence, but he is a former IWC North Eastern and International Champion, so he is good. (The screen shows clips of "Mr. Main Event" Chris Douglas pinning Vedder to become one half of the IeWC World Tag Team Champions is shown, then a shot of "Friar" Michael St. Paul working out in the gym. Then shots are shown of "Mr. Sincere" Johnny Glamour winning the IWC International Title and then a shot of El Carretera Estrella winning the IWC North Eastern Title.) Al Robertson: And now, the final division ... (The screen shows these brackets ... Bad Boy McCade -| Juan Garza -|____________ | "Stray Cat" Sid Starr -| |____________ Greg "The Millionare" Miller -|____________| | |JAPANESE "Sudden Death" Shane Matthews -| |CHAMPION "Keen" Kurt Orr -|____________ | | |________ Ivan "The Iron Curtain" Ramius-| |____________| Banshee -|____________| The crowd pops for a third time!) Al Robertson: First off, we will have Bad Boy McCade, a prankster and former rival of mine from IWF/WoW, take on Juan Garza, a two time EWO World Champion. Then, we have "Stray Cat" Sid Starr, the former WAR! World Champion and current EWC Skinnyweight Champion, take on Greg "The Millionare" Miller, a current SJPW wrestler and one of the most enthusiastic men to sign a ICTC Contract. (The screen shows shots of Bad Boy McCade winning the Newcomers' Battle Royal at IWF/WoW Summer Explosion III, then cuts to a shot of Juan Garza winning the EWO World Title. The screen then shows a shot of "Stray Cat" Sid Starr holding up the EWC Skinnyweight Title and then a shot of him duking it out with Tiger Claw and then "Armitage" Steve Spector in the JRMC. Then it cuts to a shot of Greg "The Millionare" Miller delivering a moonsault in the SJPW.) Al Robertson: Next, we have a matchup between "Sudden Death" Shane Matthews, a very highly talented individual and former AEWA and TOWL World Champions, and "Keen" Kurt Orr, a man who supposedly holds 15 Ph.D's ... or maybe I just made that up. In fact, I did. Anyway, after that, we have the #1 Cruiserweight in the world Ivan "The Iron Curtain" Ramius in a match against Banshee, one of the Keening brothers and a competitor in G-Pro and SJPW. (The screen cuts to a shot of "Sudden Death" Shane Matthews delivering a powerbomb to an unsuspecting opponent, then cuts to a shot of "Keen" Kurt Orr standing next to his manager The Scholar. Then the camera cuts to a shot of Ivan "The Iron Curtain" Ramius waving the Russian flag with pride, and then to a shot of Banshee doing a suicide dive in G-Pro.) Al Robertson: Well ... that's it for the brackets. In addition ... we will have this match ... (The screen shows the words ... OFFICIAL INSANE WRESTLING CORPORATION MATCH FOR THE IWC CAN-AM TITLE ROBBY BLAZE (c) TAKES ON ??? The crowd pops.) Al Robertson: In addition ... (The screen shows the following words ... OFFICIAL SJPW MATCH SPECIAL SIX-MAN TAG!!! PARTICIPANTS TO BE ANNOUNCED The crowd pops!!) Al Robertson: And ... we may even have a UEW official match, and some other grudge matches. They will be announced soon. As for interviews, they will start later. I'll be back Monday with an update and more information ... (Al walks out as reporters shout questions.
