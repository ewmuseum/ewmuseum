Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The International Cruiserweight Tournament of Champions announced itself on 24 October 1998 at a founding press conference staged at the Pennsylvania Convention Center in Philadelphia, hosted by “No Gimmicks Necessary” Al Robertson. Turnout for the invitational ran so far past what Robertson said he had expected that he expanded the field on the spot, from a planned sixteen-man bracket to twenty-four.

The tournament split its field into three eight-man divisions — American, Mexican and Japanese — each fought at its own yet-to-be-determined site, with the three divisional winners set to meet in a three-way dance for the tournament trophy. Robertson introduced each entrant with a highlight reel of championships they said they had won elsewhere, billing the field as talent drawn from more than a dozen other e-feds.

Among the pairings he announced: “Confident” Lawrence Williams against The Trixxster, El Carretera Estrella marking his return to the ring after a long absence, and Ivan “The Iron Curtain” Ramius, billed as the world's number-one-ranked cruiserweight, against Banshee. One entrant, the Kabuki Kid, had already gone missing before the brackets were even set, and Robertson told the room he wasn't sure the man was still signed.

Robertson closed the conference promising a written Update the following Monday and interviews to follow. Whether the tournament played out as announced isn't in the surviving record; this founding press conference is the International Cruiserweight Tournament of Champions' own surviving document.
