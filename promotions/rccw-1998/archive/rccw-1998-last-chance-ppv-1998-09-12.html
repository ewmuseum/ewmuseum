"{RCCW} Last Chance PPV"

12 September 1998 (posted 14 September 1998)
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
This is my first attempt at actually writing out a card, tell me what you think, please. ----------- RCCW Last Chance PPV September 12, 1998 Fort Lauderdale, Florida {The scene opens up to the beach in Fort Lauderdale. People all over the beach have signs about the RCCW. The camera pans the beach to see people surrounding the RCCW ring set up on the beach. Friends in Low Place by Garth Brooks begins to play as President Paul Dorchak comes out from the entrance curtain down to the ring.} Paul Dorchak: Hey Fort Lauderdale! Thank you for welcoming the RCCW to your beach!! Tonight we have a bit of a different for mat, so let us here at the RCCW What you think! First announcement of the night concerns the next Pay Per View, also known as Devils Night, October 30th from the Los Angeles Memorial Colosium. Every match will have some sort of gimmick to it. Stay tuned for details. Now I turn it over to Trevor Randolf and Mike Johnson for the rest of the show! Trevor: Thank you Mr. President, and welcome every one to the RCCW's Last Chance PPV. Two matches tonite will be last chance for the combatants. Firty. Freefall and Heroine will go at it to see who will walk out the final Womens World Champion. Plus, Clutch and Maverick will go at it in thier final meeting ever. They will be in an enclosed cage. It will be a Taipei Death Match in a cage! Plus.. Mike: Wait a minute. Here comes Octane down to the ring. He wasnt even scheduled to wrestle tonight. I wonder what he is doing? {Octane steps into the ring and takes a microphone. The fans boo him excessively. He is wearing the RCCW European Title around his waist.} Octane: If you would all please give me a moment to speak. First of all, I would like to thank the fans all around the world that have come out to cheer on the superstars of the RCCW. I have been wrestling here for a long time, but now, the desire is gone. I have done everthing I have wanted and more, so as of right now, I am announcing my retirement and taking this title with me. Thank you!. Mike: Can you believe what has just happened? Octane has left! Octane has retired! This is amazing! Trevor: I wonder what Paul is going to do about the European title now, I am sure we will find out later! Our first match of the night is Buff Butterfly taking on a rookie, The Loose Cannon, Brian Anthony. Buff Butterfly vs The Loose Cannon, Brian Anthony Mike: They are in the ring now. They lock up. BB takes the advantage with a side headlock. Anthony sends him into the roles and BB comes back with a flying clothesline. He picks him up by the hair and nails him with a right to the head. Trevor: BB sets Anthony in the corner. He nails him with an elbow to the head. Buff picks him up in a scoop slam then climbs to the second rope and jumps off with a knee to the head. Anthony gets to his knees and BB kicks him in the head. Mike: Buff is really taking it to the rookie. He is hitting him with a lot of technical moves. Anthony sends Buff into the corner face first. That had to hurt. Wait, Anthony isnt paying attention. Buff hits him with the Buff Buster and pins him! Buff Butterfly wins with the Buff Buster at 5:03 Trevor: Buff with an easy win over the rookie, but I wouldnt count out Anthony yet. This was only his first match here in the RCCW. Mike: Thats right. Well, Buff is still in the ring as Dancing Jalapeno enters the ring. Trevor: Tim Mulany makes his way down to the ring as Queezy comes out of the curtain with a chair. He runs up and smashes him over the head with a chair. What do they care about this man. Maybe they are making a point or something. Mike: I dont know....Wait. There is some commotion in the crowd. It cant be. No way, it cant be! {Demon Within and Grendle step out of the crowd just behind where Queezy is Buff and DJ come running down at them as Grendle runs up to them and hits them with a double clothesline. Queezy stands up in amazement. DWI taps him on the shoulder. Queezy turns around and is met with a chair to the face! He then picks him up and executes the Dance with the Devil. He pulls a mic out of his jacket.} DWI: Hey Queezy, you should watch your back a little bit better than that, punk. {DWI and Grendle leave to boos} Trevor: I cant believe they are back. Two of the baddest world champions are back in the RCCW. Paul must have done a lot to get them back. Mike: What ever you did, Mr. President, Good Job! WEll, I guess that take care of the of that match. I guess its time to get to the the first title match of the night, and the last of its kind here in the RCCW. The First of the two Last Chance Matches, Heroine vs Freefall for the World Womens Title! Heroine vs Freefall World Womens Title Trevor: Both of these women really want this title. They want to be known as the last world champion inthe womens division here in the RCCW. They lock up at the beginning and freefall takes down Heroine with a hair pull snap mare. They get back up and lock up again, Heroine take Freefall down with a verticle suplex. Freefall gets up and is hit with a drop kick falling out of the ring. Heroine follows her out and they battle outside. Freefall whips Heroine into the guardrail then runs up with a splash and Heroine moves and Freefall eats the railing. Mike: Neither woman listening to the count by the referee as he counts both of them out. Referees decision, double count out and Freefall will be known as the final RCCW Womens World Champion! Double Countout at 2:34 Trevor: L-Mo is in the ring awaiting his opponant, Carnage. L-Mo vs Carnage World Television Title ::::Superbeast by Rob Zombie kicks in over the loud speaker as the fans turn to the entrance ramp. They wait for a minute, but no one is coming out. L-Mo walks around the ring. He's a little paranoid since no one is making his way into the ring and can tell the something is wrong. The fans suddenly jump to their feet as Carnage is spotted making his way through the crowd. He jumps over the guardrail and stands on the floor, staring at L-Mo. Carnage jumps up onto the ring apron and continues staring at L-Mo. A few words are exchanged, but can not be heard. Their argument quickly heats up as L-Mo yells at Carnage. Carnage finally gets fed up and slaps him. L-Mo charges at Carnage, seemingly unphased by the slap. He begins to choke Carnage, but fails to notice Crackshot crawling out from under the ring. Crackshot grabs a chair, slides into the ring and nails L-Mo in the back of the head. He pulls him up to his feet by his hair and yells a few words at him. Crackshot grabs L-Mo's head and hits him with the Overdose. Carnage, who's face is still blood red from being choked, climbs to the top rope. He flies through the air, hitting L-Mo with the Guilotine. Crackshot is handed a mic as the arena fills with boos.:::: Crackshot-L-Mo, at Showdown you tried to kill me but didn't have what it takes....an error that will definitely prove fatal!! ::::Crackshot goes to the floor and grabs a couple of chairs. He returns to the ring and places one on each side of L-Mo's head. He walks over to the ropes and jumps the top rope, landing on the ring apron. He stares at L-Mo's motionless body as a huge smile crosses his face. Crackshot leaps to the top rope and flies through the air with a springboard shooting star press that connects with the chairs covering L-Mo's head. Crackshot stands over L-Mo yelling at him as Carnage begins screaming and pointing to the entrance. Crackshot looks over to see Freefall and Creed racing down to the ring. They enter the ring as Crackshot and Carnage scatter into the crowd. Crackshot pauses amongst the fans and faces the ring.:::: Crackshot-L-Mo, get your pathetic ass up and prepare youself cause the worst is yet to come!! ::::Crackshot and Carnage exit as L-Mo and Co. are left in the ring. Freefall flicks them both off as Creed checks over L-Mo. He picks L-Mo up and carries him to the back.:::: No Contest Trevor: Well, surprise after surprise tonite. No match for the TV Title tonite, but Carnage and the rest of the Group have made thier point here tonight. Mike: That is right. I have just been given word that the President has a word for us up on the Sonytron. {The Sonytron comes on and Paul Dorchak is sitting behind a desk.} Paul: Surprise after surprise tonite so far. First, I would like to comment on the title situation here in the RCCW. First, as you all know, the Womens Title is no more. Octane has walked out with the European Title. Well, he can have it! The Cyber title question has been up in the air lately too. It is now gone also. The only titles here are the World, TV, Hardcore, Cruiserweight, and Tag Title. Now onto what just happened. I am making the first scheduled match for Devils Night. Carnage and L-Mo in the Thunderdome! Then another match that has been asked for as of late. Night vs Riot in a Scaffold Match. For safetly reasons, I am not going to allow the dumpster add on to is. Sorry. There you go, thats if for now. {The Sonytron goes off and the scene goes back to Trevor and Mike} Trevor: More great and shocking announcements by the president. For those of you out there that dont know, Devils Night is the One year anniversary for the RCCW/PMCW. It will be a great show. Mike: Well, its time to get back to the action tonight. The next match up is for the Hardcore title, though, the winner will not get the belt until the night is over. Queezy take on Creed. Queezy vs Jason Creed World Hardcore Title Trevor: Both men lock up and Creed takes the advantage with an eye poke then starts kicking a downed Queezy. He picks him up by the hair and spikes him down with a vicious DDT. Mike: That had to hurt. Queezy is barely moving. Creed stands above him and and drops down on him with a double axe handle to the back. Creed is just taking it to Queezy. Trevor: I figured Queezy would do more in this match, but just keeps on him. Queezy, drops Creed with a lowblow. He gets up and starts kicking Creed in the head. Creed grabs Queezy's leg and stands up. Whats he going to do? Mike: He just twisted his leg off almost. Creed is going for the pin. One, Two Three!!!!!!! As soon as the referee's hand hits the mat for three the lights go out. All of a sudden the entire ring the walkway and the entrance are consumed with fire. The fire goes down and all is black once again. A voice is heard. Voice: That's right Quezzy, I am back and I welcome you to your own personal HELL. All of a sudden music begins to play. It is faint at first and then gradually gets louder it can be made out that its "Frances Farmer Will Have Her Revenge on Seattle" by Nirvana. The darkness is broken by fireworks shooting out of the entranceway and pinwheels start to spin and send sparks flying around everywhere. Out of the entranceway steps a big man 7' 0" 356 lbs. Immediately the crowd recognizes the man as the beast known as Grendle. Grendle points to the entranceway and from behind the curtain steps the 7' 6" man known appropriately as the Demon Within. He has a microphone in his hand. The arena remains pretty dark except a spotlight shining on DWI and Grendle and another shining on the ring where Quezzy is recovering from his defeat and using the ropes to pul him up. Quezzy looks up and a look of surprise is seen on his face. DWI: That's right Quezzy. I left in disgrace after you beat me last time and now I come back as my redeemer. You see when I heard my best friend Grendle took a leave of absence I felt I must also take a rest and help out my friend. Until now, the world of wrestling has pretty much forgot about the two Towers of Terror that once ruled the RCCW. Well we're back. Isn't that right Grendle? Grendle nods his head in approval. DWI: And to prove we are back. Well as you know talk is cheap, so why don't we just show you what we are here to do. Grendle runs into the ring and Quezzy is finally up on his feet. The Demon Within is walking slowly to the ring. Grendle tries to pick up Quezzy but Quezzy DDT's Grendle. DWI is now on the apron. Quezzy runs over to DWI and tries to punch him. DWI blocks Quezzy and pushes him off. Grendle still lays on the ground and DWI steps over the top ropes and enters the ring. Quezzy immediately gets back up and starts to attack DWI. DWI remains for the most part unfazed. DWI after about a minute of defense, picks up Quezzy and chokeslams him. DWI then picks up Quezzy and gives him the Dance with the Devil. Grendle comes to during the chokeslam and after the DWD Grendle begins to dance around the ring pointing at DWI. DWI pics up a microphone in the ring. DWI: And in case you haven't heard yet, WE'RE BACK. DWI throws the microphone on the limp body of Quezzy and DWI and Grendle leave the ring. The lights come on fully after DWI and Grendle have left. Winner is Jason Creed by pinfall after standing corkscrew leg whip. 6:12 Mike: I cant believe they are back. I knew Paul was out looking for talent, but I didnt think he would be able to get those two back. The last time DWI was around, him and The Psychotic One were tag champs, but they are back! Trevor: Damn straight and DWI has some sort of problem with Queezy. Those two have gone rounds in the past and it seems if DWI wants to rekindle the flame. I cant wait to see where it goes. Mike: Well, we have two matches left this evening. First up is Piston vs Jack Dealer. Dealer is already in the ring, lets go down for Pistons introduction. Piston vs Black Jack Dealer World Cruiserweight Title Piston: Jack...you're the only guy that I've faced in any fed that I haven't dominated...in PMCW you always had my number but in RCCW I've came to life and got a few wins on you...I've watched Big Oct call it a day here in RCCW and I'm gonna do the same...so here...Jack...here is my LightHeavyweight Title (Piston hands the belt to Jack and they both raise it above their heads...Jack takes the belt and turns away from Piston and raises it above his head...Piston executes a spinning kick to the back on Jack's head...immediatley knocking him out...Piston, mic in hand, goes up to the top rope) Piston:: Ha ha ha JACK...you thought I was gonna give you MY belt?? Fuck that...THIS is what I think of you (Piston executes the Underhaul (Shooting Star Press) onto Jack, lying face down...Piston picks up the LH belt and leaves the ring...and RCCW) Mike: What a surprise! I was wondering what he was going to do with Octane leaving, but this is not something I didnt expect. All that leaves left for the MCM are Clutch and the Chop Shop. Trevor: Speaking of Clutch, the huge main event. Taipei Death Match, in an enclosed cage. This one will be brutal. Well the cage is down and the combatants are in the ring, so lets get to the match. Clutch vs Maverick World Heavyweight vs World Hardcore Mike: Both men are each starting out with kendo stick in thier hands. Clutch takes the first shot by hitting Maverick in the leg with his stick. The big man is down. Clutch is goiing to have to keep him grounded to win again over him. The last time these two met, it was in a contoversial ending in which Clutch was finally awarded the victory. Trevor: Neither men liked the tainted match, so Paul resigned the match, in a cage in this situation. Clutch is still going to work on Maverick. He is relentless with the stick. Oh my god, he just hit Maverick over the head and smashed his stick into pieces! Mavericks hair has instantly turned red from the blood. Clutch is going for the other stick and Maverick pushes his face into the cage. This aint no steel cage folks, it is a chain link fencing cage with barb wire mixed in. Maverick is scraping Clutches face across it cutting him open! Mike: I knew this match was going to be brutal, but I never expected this much blood. The only way to lose this match is if you can not go on any longer. Both men going for the kill right away. Clutch using the kendo stick to draw blood on Maverick and Maverick using the cage. Clutch knocks Mav down with an elbow to the had. Both men losing a lot of blood. Clutch throws Mav through the air with a butterfly suplex then is right on him with an ankle lock submission hold. Trevor: Maverick is in some emence pain here. I dont know how much longer he will be able to hold on. Clutch lets go and grabs the other stick and climbs to the top rope. Maverick hobbles over to the ropes and bounces into them to make Clutch fall. Maverick grabs the kendo sitck. Mike: He's winding up. BOTH: Its homerun number 63. Big Mac and Slammin Sammy, watch out! Maverick has taken the lead with a kendo stick to the face! Trevor: Clutch's face is completly covered in blood. If you have children watching this match, put them in thier room, this isnt for the weak stomachs. Maverick climbs up on the ropes and takes Clutch down with a DDT from the top! Mike: This has to be the most brutal match I have seen since The Psychotic One and A-Bomb in thier series of Thunderdome cages. Trevor: Dont forget Freightlines guillotene leg drop on Onyx from the top of the thunderdome. Mike: That was nothing compared to what A-Bomb and TPO went through. Clutch sends Maverick into the ropes and they hit each other with a double clothseline! Both men are down. What happens if neither of them get up. Trevor: Well, the former blue mat is now a blood red! These two might as well just take this match to the hospital, because that is where they are going to end up tonite. Mike: Both men have stood up. They are looking eye to eye! They are shaking thier heads and are heading rot the door of the cage. Clutch kicks the door open! Trevor: Whjat is going on? they are both walking out of the cage. They are not going to continue! Maverick grabs the time keeper and slams his face into the bell. Mike: Clutch has grabbed the announcer and executes a DVD on the time keepers table destroying it. They each have grabbed the belts. Trevor: Maverick has the world title! That could cause a problem Mike: No it isnt they have switched belts and are shaking hands! What a developement. {Clutch and Maverick walk to the back with the fans cheering as the scene fades to black.}
