WSW — Card

WSW Saturday Night, September 25, 2004 (Part 8 of 8)

Sep 29, 2004, 12:00:00 AM
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Jen Babes - The following contest is set for one fall with a 20-minute time limit, and it is our MAIN EVENT!
["45" by Shinedown begins to play, and the crowd boos.] Jen Babes - Introducing first, accompanied down the aisle by Vixen, from
Peoria, Illinois, weighing 215 pounds, ERIC LEEEE!
[Vixen and Eric Lee run down the aisle, with Lee yelling at the crowd to rile them up the whole time.]
Lampley - Eric Lee has had a lot of trashtalking and cockiness about him the entire week, but he's got his hands full tonight! Mr. Ed's a former
WSW Interstate champion, and one of the greats in WSW!
Gambler - Yeah, but every horse eventually gets turned into glue, and
Lee looks like he's an Elmer's fan!
["Good Reason" by Seafood plays, drawing cheers.] Jen Babes - And his opponent, from Liverpool, England, weighing 285 pounds.. the former WSW Interstate champion.. MIIISTER EEEEEED!
[Mr. Ed begins walking to the ring, slapping hands with the fa-] Lampley - AND ERIC LEE WITH A TOPE CON GIRO OVER THE TOP TO MR. ED ON THE FLOOR! Gambler - WHOA! Lampley - Lee pounding on Mr. Ed with rights and lefts! Ed trying to get back up, Lee totally surprised him!
[Lee kicks away at Ed, who is getting back to his feet, and tosses him back into the ring. Lee taunts the crowd in the front row, who starts booing him heavily. Lee rolls into the ring and stomps away at Ed. Lee bounces off the ropes and drops a quick legdrop and covers...
1, kickout by Ed. Lee picks Ed up by the hair, but Ed shoves him back into the corner, and begins shoulderdriving into his gut. Ed slugs away at Lee, then hiptosses out of the corner, but Lee lands on his feet, runs off the ropes--into a side slam!]
Lampley - Mr. Ed begins making his comeback now, but Lee is back up..he's just too fast for Ed to keep down!
Gambler - And he's too good! Just listen to him! Lampley - Ed misses a right hand, sidekick by Lee! Lee springs off the second rope, DROPKICK TO THE BACK OF THE HEAD! And Lee poses AGAIN!
Gambler - He's that damn good and he knows it! [Lee measures up on Mr. Ed, and begins kicking him HARD in the chest! Ed begins yelling at the kicks, and Lee continues unloading..until Ed catches one of the kicks, and clotheslines Lee down! Ed leaps on the smaller Lee and starts pounding his head down into the mat. Ed picks Lee up, sets him up..and hits the snap suplex! Lee begins getting to his feet, and Ed punts him into the corner. Ed hits big forearms in the corner on Eric Lee, then whips him into the buckle...Lee springs out of the corner with a Super Swinging DDT, but Ed counters into a Northern
Lights suplex!
1.. 2..kickout! Ed waves his arms to get the crowd into it, and waits for
Lee to get up..he grabs him by the throat, and chokeslams him! He covers..
1.. 2.. 3-no, kickout by Lee!] Lampley - Eric Lee may have bitten off more than he can chew! Mr. Ed whips Lee into the ropes...BAAAAAAAACK BODYDROP!
Gambler - Nice throatcall there. Lampley - Lee begging off from Mr. Ed...Ed after him, LEE LOWBRIDGES
HIM! Ed to the floor! Gambler - And Lee's waiting for him...WHOA! Lampley - Plancha by Eric Lee, AND ED CATCHES HIM AND THROWS HIM INTO
THE RING! Ed sliding back in..and an elbowdrop!
1.. 2.. Kickout by Eric Lee! Gambler - Lee, get up! Lampley - Lee is up..and he elbows Ed in the gut! That looked dangerously low! Gambler - Like Ed would notice. Lampley - I'll let that slide! [Ed staggers to his feet, but Lee is right on him with an enzuigiri! Lee goes to the top rope and waits for Ed...then takes him down with a spinning bodyblock and covers!
1.. 2.. ...kickout by Ed! Lee begins choking Ed with his boot against the ropes, and the referee counts to get the break. Lee backs off..then flips off the ref and the crowd behind the ref's back, then runs off the ropes and running bootscrapes Lee. Lee taunts the crowd even more and begins stomping Ed as Vixen applauds her man at ringside. Lee backs off, and
Vixen begins choking away at Mr. Ed! The referee turns around right as
Vixen stops the choking.]
Lampley - And now it's a damn handicap match! Ed is in trouble here, and
Lee is letting him know it!
Gambler - Man, that's the worst part - to get your butt kicked and have somebody let you know it!
Lampley - Lee is being VERY cocky here so far and he looks like he wants to finish this! Lee is measuring him for Total Insanity..ONE KICK HITS!
Gambler - OW! That just nailed Ed in the chest! Lampley - AND ANOTHER! And he's yelling at Vixen that this will be number three! Gambler - Here it comes, waaait for it.. Lampley - The third kick HI-NO! Ed blocks it with his elbow! Lee staggering, ED FROM BEHIND, ED SLAM! ED SLAM!
Gambler - HEY! Lampley - Ed picks Lee up again, hooks him from behind again..EDACHE! Another of his big moves, and back up, no pinfall! Gambler - Ed's unloading on him! Lampley - Ed with a kick to the gut...hooks the arms, UP, PAPERCUT LANDS WITH AUTHORITY! COVER BY ED! 1.. 2.. 3! ED WINS IT! Jen Babes - Here is your winner, Mr. Ed! Lampley - Lee got cocky late in the match, and Mr. Ed made him pay for it! Mr. Ed with the big victory tonight!
Gambler - I can't believe he got that! Lee had him offbalance the whole match but Ed shut him down!
Lampley - Mr Ed celebrating a big win in the ring, could he be the next
WSW Champion? We'll see you next week, everyone!
[The camera shifts to "The Flagship" Richard Stark and Will Mason in the back, watching Mr Ed celebrate, and taking notes all the while. We then fade to black.]
