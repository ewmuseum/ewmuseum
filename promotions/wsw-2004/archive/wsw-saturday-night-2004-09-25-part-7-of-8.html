WSW Saturday Night, September 25, 2004 (Part 7 of 8)

Sep 29, 2004, 12:00:00 AM
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Jen Babes - The following contest is scheduled for one fall, with a twenty minute time limit. Introducing first, accompanied by his manager,
Tommy Starr...
"TIME MARCHES ON... FOR WHOM THE BELL TOLLS..." [The crowd boos as The Bomber walks out to the ring, Tommy Starr following him.] Jen Babes - Weighing in at 262 pounds, here is THE BOMBER! [The Bomber walks out, focused on the ring, while Tommy Starr talks trash with the fans. The Bomber snarls at the camera as they walk by to go up the stairs to the ring, and Tommy Starr shoves the camera on the way past.]
Lampley - The Bomber and Richard Stark have been going back and forth over the past week, and this ought to be a good, back and forth match.
Gambler - What exactly are you seeing, Lamps? This guy's gonna destroy
Richard Stark. He's bigger, stronger, and younger.
Lampley - Stark has a lot more experience, though, Gambler, and he's got a lot of tricks up his sleeve.
Gambler - So he's an old, efficient car, like a Celica, and he's about to get run over by a Hum-vee of a man!
Jen Babes - And his opponent, from Pensacola, Florida, and accompanied to the ring by his manager, Will Mason...
[Beethoven's 5th Symphony plays over the PA and Richard Stark walks out, dressed in a red sequined robe. Behind him is Will Mason, wearing a blue suit and carrying a briefcase. On the back of Stark's robe is "The
Flagship" spelled out in sequins.]
Jen Babes - Weighing in at 258 pounds, here is "THE FLAGSHIP"... RICHARD STARK! Lampley - Richard Stark bring a lot of wrestling knowledge and experience with him. Gambler - He should have brought his last will and testament too, judging by the look on The Bomber's face!
[Stark climbs in the ring as the referee holds The Bomber back. Starks unties the sash on his rope and slips it off, handing it to a female worker at ringside. Stark pulls his pads up from his ankles to his knees. Stark slowly checks the ropes in his corner.]
Gambler - Seems this Richard Stark fella isn't too eager to lock up with
The Bomber. Lampley - Stark taking his time, making sure the match starts on his own terms. Gambler - Nice spin on it. One of the presidential campaigns should hire you. [Stark finally approaches the center of the ring, and the bell sounds. The two men move to lock up, but Richard Stark ducks under the lockup and tousles The Bomber's hair. The Bomber turns around, and Stark steps back into the corner. The Bomber taunts Stark, motioning Stark to come back to the middle of the ring. Stark returns and they lock up. The
Bomber powers Stark into the corner and delivers a couple of crisp forearm shots to Stark's chest. The Bomber whips Stark into the opposite corner and charges in for a clothesline. Stark slides out of the way and
The Bomber crashes into the ringpost! Stark rolls The Bomber up, and pulls the tights...
1... 2...... and The Bomber kicks out and gets back to this feet. He complains to the ref about Stark's actions.]
Gambler - Richard Stark is a cheater, I knew it! Lampley - Stark using the ring to his advantage in the early going in this match. Gambler - Isn't that what I normally say, then you whine about? Lampley - Just because the man thought of it before The Bomber did is no reason to take out your hate on him.
[They lock up again, and The Bomber backs Stark into the ropes. The
Bomber whips him off across the ring. Stark rebounds and ducks a clothesline from The Bomber. On the rebound, Stark goes to clip The
Bomber's knee, but The Bomber steps aside and Stark smacks his face across the middle rope. The Bomber drops a double axehandle across
Stark's back, sending Stark prone to the mat. The Bomber stomps away at
Stark, attacking Starks' neck and upper back. Stark gets to the ropes, and the referee orders The Bomber away. As The Bomber backs off, distracting the referee, Tommy Starr chokes Stark across the bottom rope!]
Lampley - Now this is uncalled for! Tommy Starr has no business involving himself in this match!
Gambler - Oh, so now the cheating is unacceptable! If it was that wannabe lawyer Will Mason doing it, you'd be cheering!
Lampley - Neither manager is supposed to be part of this match! Will
Mason coming over now and pulling Tommy Starr off of his charge! Mason decks Starr!
Gambler - Managers aren't supposed ot be involved! Come on now! Lampley - Would you just shut up? The Bomber pulling Stark up off the canvas.. lifts him up... Brainbuster! The Bomber covers...
1... 2...... And Stark gets the shoulder up! Gambler - He's a lucky man to get out of that! [Stark slowly staggers to his feet as The Bomber waits for him to get up. Stark gets to his feet, and The Bomber takes him back down with a vicious clothesline. The Bomber covers and puts his feet on the ropes...
1... 2...... And the ref spots the feet and stops the count. The Bomber argues with the ref over the count, giving Stark enough time to get to his feet...]
Lampley - The Bomber's got to focus on the match and not the ref! Gambler - For once, Vin-man, I agree with you! Turn around, Bomber! Lampley - Bomber still arguing with the ref... LOW BLOW BY STARK! Gambler - Disqualify him! Lampley - Stark goes around Bomber... butterfly suplex! Stark covers! 1... 2...... And Bomber kicks out! Tommy Starr giving the referee an earful! Gambler - Of course he is! The referee's biased! Lampley - No he's not... Stark over to The Bomber again, and picks him up.. shinbreaker! Stark smells blood! He's calling for the Side Indian
Deathlock!
Gambler - Oh great. Lampley - Stark is setting him up... he's got it locked on! The Side
Indian Deathlock is locked in!
Gambler - Bomber's too close to the ropes, though, I'm not worried! Lampley - Tommy Starr is, though... he's moving over there! Wait a minute! He's pushing the rope a little closer to The Bomber!
Gambler - Always there to lend a hand! Lampley - Maybe not! Here comes Will Mason again! We've got another fight on the floor! Gambler - Can't these two play nice with each other? Lampley - Apparently not! The referee's trying to get them to break it up! They're brawling all over the floor here!
Gambler - Kick his ass, Tommy! Lampley - The referee trying to get some control on the floor, meanwhile, The Bomber has gotten to the ropes!
Gambler - Good, now break the hold, ref! Lampley - The referee still trying to get the fight broken up on the floor! Stark sees that Bomber's in the ropes! He's pulling him off the ropes!
Gambler - At least Stark respects the rules of the ring! Lampley - Not quite! He's pulling him farther towards the center of the ring! The ref's back in!
Gambler - Damnit, no! Get out, Bomber! Lampley - Bomber reaching for the ropes, he's got nowhere to go... and... YES! Bomber gives up! Gambler - Damnit! Those cheaters! Jen Babes - The winner of this match... "THE FLAGSHIP" RICHARD STARK! Lampley - Stark makes a successful WSW Saturday Night debut, but the
Bomber gave a great effort tonight.
Gambler - Stark cheated! He's a cheating cheater! Lampley - And The Bomber didn't? Gambler - We'll be right back, folks! *cough* ---- (C) 2004 WSW Productions - http://wswfed.com/
