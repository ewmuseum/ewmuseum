Gambler - Southward showing his Japanese influences there... Lampley - Southward adjusts his elbowpad again and climbs up top... sizing Krim'nal up... SUPERSTAR ELBOWDROP! Southward connects with an elbow from about 9 feet in the air it seems! Southward covers, this could do it right here... 1... 2.... and Krim'nal rolls the shoulder again! Southward is visibly frustrated!
Gambler - He was THAT close to getting the win! [Southward picks up Krim'nal and tries to whip him into the corner, but Krim'nal reverses the whip and Southward slams HARD into the corner, flipping onto the apron! Krim'nal charges at Southward with a lariat, but
Southward ducks it and catches Krim'nal by the neck, dropping and guillotining Krim'nal across the top rope! Southward gets on the apron and slingshots in with a somersault senton on Krim'nal, but Krim'nal gets his knees up!]
Lampley - These guys keep going back and forth! It's a matter of who wants it more right now!
Gambler - Sean Southward does, of course! [Southward grimaces, laying on all fours after the blocked senton. Krim'nal gets up and picks Southward up by the waist, German suplexing him over with a bridge - 1... 2... and Southward barely gets the shoulder up! Krim'nal gets back up and whips Southward into the corner. Krim'nal charges in with a spear, but Southward moves out of the way and Krim'nal collides with the ringpost, falling all the way to the floor!]
Lampley - Sean Southward remains one step ahead of Krim'nal on that spear! Gambler - Both these guys have taken tons of abuse so far tonight. Lampley - And Southward's going out to the floor to dish out more punishment! [Southward dives off the apron with a double axe-handle to the back of Krim'nal! Southward whips Krim'nal into the guardrail, crashing the steel! Southward rips off his elbow pad and deliver an elbow strike to Krim'nal's head and a second strike! Southward loads up for the Roaring Elbow, but
Krim'nal duck and drops Southward with an inverted Russian legsweep faceslam on the floor!]
Lampley - Southward's face just bounced off the concrete! This might be the opportunity Krim'nal needs!
Gambler - No! C'mon, Sean, get with it! [Krim'nal rolls Southward back into the ring and climbs in himself as the crowd turns to the entranceway and begins booing...]
Lampley - Well, well, well, would you look at who just showed up... Gambler - Donovan Astros! My favorite wrestler! Lampley - Astros slowly walking to the ring with that telltale steel chair... [Astros slowly walks to the ring, but right behind him is Nemesis! The crowd cheers as Nemesis attacks Astros, but the cheers only last for a second as
"Stunning" Steve Southward comes from the entranceway with a steel chair of his own and cleans Nemesis's clock!]
Gambler - Hey, hey, the gang's all here! Lampley - Why do these two need to be out here? Gambler - To make sure Sean Southward remains champion! [Astros and Steve slowly walk to ringside as Krim'nal sets Sean up on the top turnbuckle facing the crowd. Krim'nal climbs up top with him...]
Gambler - Steve! Donovan! Save Sean! Lampley - Krim'nal's got Southward set up... DEATH PENALTY! That's gonna do it! We're gonna have a new champion! Krim'nal covers! 1...
Gambler - Sean's foot's on the ropes! Lampley - 2.... [Astros slides into the ring at the 2 count, chair in hand...] Lampley - THR- *THWACK* Lampley - Astros just destroyed Krim'nal with that chairshot! The referee's calling for the bell!
Azaelia - The winner of this contest, as a result of a disqualification... KRIM'NAL! However, the WSW Championship cannot change hands on a disqualification, therefore STILL WSW Champion, "SUPERSTAR" SEAN SOUTHWARD!
[Krim'nal rolls off of Southward to the side. Astros drives his chair into the gut of Krim'nal repeatedly as Sean slowly gets to his feet. Astros kicks
Krim'nal to the floor and turns around with Sean Southward right in his face!] Lampley - This has been brewing for weeks, folks! Gambler - Calm down, Sean, Donovan was just trying to help! [Southward is screaming at Astros, pointing out that his foot was on the ropes. Donovan looks non-plussed and inordinately calm. Steve Southward slides into the ring behind Sean as Donovan and Sean continue arguing...]
Lampley - Sean Southward and Donovan Astros are about to come to blows here! Donovan has dropped his chair and brought up the fists and... *THWACK* Lampley - STEVE SOUTHWARD JUST LEVELLED HIS OWN BROTHER WITH A CHAIRSHOT ACROSS THE BACK! Gambler - It was a setup all along! This is great, Lamps! Lampley - That god damned Donovan Astros has turned brother against brother! Astros is stomping away at Sean! Steve is working that chair over on Southward's side and back! This is sick! This is disgusting! Gambler - This is beautiful! Finally Stevie's standing up for himself! Lampley - How can you condone this? Astros is SCREAMING at Sean! Now, Steve picks Sean up while Astros grabs his chair...NO! NO! GOD DAMNIT NO!
*THWAAAACK!* Lampley - A vile Conchairto to the head of Sean Southward! Someone needs to get out here! Come on!
