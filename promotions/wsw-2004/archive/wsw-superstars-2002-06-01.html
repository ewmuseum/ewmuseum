[Astros points to Jen Babes at the announce desk.] Astros - It's been like this from day 1, Sean! Whether it be the Movement, or the Saviors, it's always been "What can we do for Sean?" And you know what? I shouldn't HAVE to ask for a title shot, Sean, because everyone in this hellhole knows that when it comes the best wrestler in WSW... Sean, right now, YOU'RE LOOKING AT HIM.
[Southward stares right into Astros' eyes, and for a few tense moments, they're almost nose to nose. Then Southward slowly raises the WSW
Championship belt in his hand to be between him and Astros.] Southward - This right here..begs to differ. [The crowd "ooooh"s.] Southward - Let me make something clear to you, DEVON. You WERE the WSW Champion before, but now, it's mine. And you keep bitching to me, and bitching to me about not being the WSW Champion, and about how I screwed you against Krim'nal! Well, if you would've taken care of Krim'nal before, I
WOULDN'T HAVE HAD TO BOTHER GETTING INVOLVED! [Astros turns away for a moment, gathering his thoughts. The crowd murmurs in anticipation, expecting a blowup any second.]
Astros - Well... [Astros pauses again, his face seething in anger. The crowd at this point is getting louder, sensing the impending train wreck...]
Astros - Well then, SIMON... since you're God's gift to professional wrestling, I guess you won't need my help Wednesday... so DON'T expect it.
[Southward shakes his head a bit, then turns to the ropes to walk out. He stops halfway through the ropes and turns back. Azaelia walks up to him to get the mic to him.]
Southward - Unlike you, Don...I know how to beat Krim'nal. ["Overnight Sensation" starts back up as Southward exits the ring, carrying the WSW Championship belt in his hand and slinging it over his shoulder.
