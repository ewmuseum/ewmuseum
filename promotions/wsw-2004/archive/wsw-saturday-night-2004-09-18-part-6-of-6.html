WSW — Card

WSW Saturday Night, September 18, 2004 (Part 6 of 6)

Sep 19, 2004, 12:00:00 AM
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Jen Babes - The following contest is set for one fall, and it has a 20 minute time limit! Introducing first...
“IT’S EVOLUTION, BABY!” [“Do The Evolution” by Pearl Jam blares as Jen Babes stops talking. The lights dim a bit as Shima Takamura and his agent, Aaron Vick, make their way to the ring. Takamura has a calm look on his face, while Vick is giddy like a schoolgirl.]
Jen Babes - Being led to the ring by his agent, Aaron Vick, from
Sapporo, Japan, weighing 238 pounds, here is SHIMA... TAKAMURA!
[The lights come back up as Takamura and Vick enter the ring. Takamura poses on the top turnbuckle as Vick points to his pledge. The crowd reaction is very mixed.]
Gambler - This Aaron Vick guy has gotten on my nerves the last week or so, Lamps. Lampley - Why’s that? He’s just excited to be representing someone as talented as Shima Takamura.
Gambler - He doesn’t need to show it as much... [The lights dim out again...] “I FEEL IRRATIONAL - SO CONFRONTATIONAL - TO TELL THE TRUTH AGAIN I’M
GETTING AWAY WITH MURDER!”
Jen Babes - And his opponent, from Glasgow, Scotland, weighing 295 pounds, here is a two-time WSW North American Champion... KRIM’NAL!
[“Getting Away With Murder” by Papa Roach blasts as Krim’nal makes his way out to the ring. The crowd pops on name recognition, but then begins booing as Krim’nal taunts the crowd.]
Lampley - This guy’s a nutcase. He has snapped since the last time we saw him two years ago.
Gambler - I’m surprised you haven’t snapped, Lamps! I mean, you had your livelihood taken away from you for two years, he has every right to be pissed off!
Lampley - And what have you done over the past two years, Gambler? Gambler - I’m legally not at liberty to divulge that, Vin-man. [Krim’nal slides under the bottom rope and stands up, staring down
Takamura midring. Referee Ryan Stevens calls for the bell and both men lock up. Krim’nal throws Takamura off into his corner, where Aaron Vick looks distressed from the get-go.]
Gambler - Krim’nal showing his superior strength to open this match up! This isn’t Japan, Shima, the guys here are big! Lampley - Takamura’s giving up almost 60 pounds and three quarters of a foot to the big man, Krim’nal. He’s going to have to use a lot of his speed and technical knowledge to beat Krim’nal tonight.
Gambler - So he’s screwed is what you’re saying. Lampley - I didn’t say that at all! [The two wrestlers lock up again, and Krim’nal delivers a knee to the midsection of Takamura, doubling him over. Krim’nal drops a double axe- handle to the back of Takamura, and another takes Takamura to the mat.
Krim’nal stomps on Takamura’s back a couple of times, then pulls him up. Krim’nal whips Takamura into the ropes. Takamura ducks a clothesline on the rebound, but gets caught by the throat! Takamura elbows his way out of the chokehold and legsweeps Krim’nal to the mat! Krim’nal ske-daddles to the floor and Aaron Vick claps at ringside.]
Lampley - Shima Takamura escapes a potentially dangerous situation there. Gambler - Potentially? One chokeslam from Krim’nal, and it would have been all over. [Krim’nal slides back into the ring and is stomped as he gets in by
Takamura. Krim’nal slowly gets to his feet, and Takamura grabs one of
Krim’nal’s legs and dragon screws him to the center of the ring.
Takamura bounces off the side ropes as Krim’nal gets up and charges in with a dropkick, staggering Krim’nal and sending him to the ropes.
Takamura charges in again and Krim’nal back body drops Takamura over the top rope, but Takamura catches the rope and holds on, landing on the apron.]
Lampley - A dangerous situation here... Takamura turns around, Krim’nal does as well.. Takamura blocks a right hand! Right by Takamura, but
Krim’nal knees Takamura in the gut again!
Gambler - He’s found Shima Takamura’s soft, chewy center. Lampley - Krim’nal cinching Takamura up for a suplex, Krim’nal’s got him up... and drops him over the top rope!
Gambler - That’s a quick way to break a rib, those ropes are not the most forgiving things in the world!
[Takamura flops to the mat, holding his stomach. Aaron Vick looks distraught as Krim’nal taunts Takamura, holding his gut in mock pain.
Krim’nal picks Takamura up and slams him to the canvas, then drop an elbow into the chest of Takamura. Krim’nal covers...
1....... 2........... Takamura rolls the shoulder at the last second! Krim’nal gets up and pulls Takamura back to his feet. Krim’nal goes for the throat again for a chokeslam. Takamura elbows in desperation, but Krim’nal kicks Takamura in the midsection, doubling him over again.]
Lampley - Krim’nal has been targeting the midsection all match, and so far, it’s been sound strategy!
Gambler - And people were calling Krim’nal psychotic. Lampley - I hate to break it to you, G-Man, but he’s still psychotic. Krim’nal now setting up Takamura... could this be a piledriver attempt? Gambler - It’s either that or the Krimewave! Lampley - Krim’nal lifts him up, looks like a piledriver coming u-NO! Takamura kicked him in the face! Krim’nal brought Takamura’s legs up for that piledriver attempt and Takamura kicked him clean in the face!
Gambler - You can’t do that! Lampley - He just did! Takamura back on the ground, Krim’nal’s been staggered by that kick...
Gambler - Watch out, Krim! Lampley - Takamura traps him... EXPLODER! Somehow he got Krim’nal over with an Exploder suplex! Takamura covers!
1.... 2...... [Krim’nal punches Takamura in the gut to free his shoulders. Both men slowly get up to their feet. Takamura bounces off the ropes, Krim’nal tries a big boot, but Takamura rolls under it and clips Krim’nal’s other leg! Aaron Vick shouts out encouragement to Takamura as he now mocks
Krim’nal, faking a limp before stomping repeatedly on Krim’nal’s knee.
Krim’nal slides to the ropes and the referee forces Takamura to back off.] Lampley - Takamura is all over Krim’nal’s knee! Gambler - Get that monster off of him! He’s in the ropes! Lampley - Monster? Takamura’s half Krim’nal’s size, and he’s taking the fight right to the big man!
[Krim’nal struggles back to his feet, limping towards Takamura. Takamura circles Krim’nal, working to get behind the slowed opponent. Takamura gets up alongside Krim’nal, grabs his leg and rolls forward, trapping
Krim’nal in a legbar! Krim’nal struggles over to reach the ropes, and finally does. Takamura releases the hold at the referee’s 4 count.]
Lampley - Takamura continuing to punish the knee here, and it’s obvious that Aaron Vick has got a winner here.
Gambler - What do you mean, obvious? This match isn’t over yet. Lampley - It’s getting close. Krim’nal back up to his feet, favoring his leg once again. Takamura coming back over and-
[Krim’nal grabs Takamura’s throat a third time, and this time finally does get the chokeslam to connect!]
Gambler - See what I told ya? Lampley - Chokeslam by Krim’nal! He covers! 1.... 2........ Takamura kicks out! Wow! Gambler - That was 3! Lampley - Takamura able to kick out, you have to think that Krim’nal couldn’t get the force he wanted with that chokeslam on his bad knee.
Krim’nal is frustrated now, and he’s going outside... Gambler - He said earlier this week he was going to compress Takamura’s spine! Lampley - Krim’nal’s got a chair! Aaron Vick is trying to stop him, and
Krim’nal decks him!
Gambler - Thank goodness! Now maybe he’ll shut up! Lampley - Krim’nal back in the ring with the chair... he’s setting it on the mat. Gambler - This is going to be beautiful! Lampley - No, it’s not... Krim’nal signaling for the Paralyzer, but he’s having problems getting Takamura up because of that knee!
Gambler - You can do it, Krim! Lampley - Takamura is punching that bad knee! Krim’nal lets go, and
Takamura trips him! Takamura trying to lock in an STF...
Gambler - His knee’s trapped on top of that chair! Ref! Break the hold! Lampley - Takamura gets the stepover toehold in... crossface! This is the Sin Tamer! Krim’nal’s got nowhere to go!
Gambler - Break the hold! Lampley - Krim’nal can’t get to the ropes... and he gives it up! Gambler - No! Jen Babes - Your winner, Shima Takamura! Lampley - Shima Takamura with a big debut win here tonight! Gambler - He cheated! Lampley - He did no- [All of a sudden, the lights cut out.] Gambler - Did you forget to pay the electric bill? Lampley - You’d have to talk to the Executive Committee about that... ”WELCOME... TO... THE... DARKSIDE...” Lampley - What in the heck? [The lights come back up and standing behind Shima Takamura is a man dressed in an ornate black outfit with a black and silver mask. Takamura turns around and gets leveled by the masked man!]
Gambler - I like this guy! Lampley - This must be the Darkside Tiger we’ve heard a lot about! Tiger’s got Takamura... Darkness DDT! That springboard spinning DDT just dropped Takamura right on his head! What an opportunist!
Gambler - When opportunity knocks, you better be ready to take it,
Lamps! Lampley - Darkness Tiger has Takamura again... reverse crucifix... into a face first powerbomb! Takamura’s hurt, finally we have some officials down here!
[Darkness Tiger rips the microphone from Jen Babes as he goes to the floor.] Darkness Tiger - Takamura! Welcome to the Darkside! Lampley - I have no idea what this was all about... Gambler - I don’t know, but I like it! Lampley - We might find out more next week! Until next week, this is
Vince Lampley for the Gambler, and thanks for joining us here at WSW!
