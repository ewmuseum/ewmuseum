WSW Saturday Night, September 25, 2004 (Part 5 of 8)

Sep 29, 2004, 12:00:00 AM
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Gambler - OK, im backstage here at the WSW Arena. I am attempting to interview The new recruit on the WSW curcuit, JW Wilde. Now i've knocked on his locker room door a few times to no avail, so i will try one more time now.
[Gambler knocks on the door again. He waits for 5 seconds before giving up.] Gambler - Well, it looks like he is out at the moment, but when we get in touch with..... [The door swings open as JW walks out.] Gambler - Ah, Mr Wilde. Sorry to interupt you. I just wanted to ask what your strategy is against the high flying Airimir tonight?
[JW stares at The Gambler for 10 long seconds before speaking.] JW Wilde - You want to know.... my strategy? G, i don't have, a strategy. When i get inside a ring, i don't need a strategy. I go out there, and i beat the living crap out of anyone STUPID enough to get in that ring with me. And tonight, i will............. are you listening?
Gambler - uh, yeah. JW Wilde - Do i scare you? Do i make you feel small....... GOOD! Now you know what i went through as a child. I was beat, battered and humiliated. Well, tonight, Airimir, he is gonna feel that pain. He is the first victim. He is a stepping stone to the WSW Championship. He is the first man...... to take a walk....... on The Wilde Side. Now, is that all?
Gambler - Yeah, that's all. JW Wilde - Good. Now do me a huge favor and get the hell out of here. [JW watches The Gambler walk away before slamming the door shut. The camera switches back to the ringside area.]
Lampley - Well, I sure hope Gambler takes his time getting some stuff for that errant chairshot. I'll finally be able to call a match!
[The bell sounds, and the sound of heavy winds begin blasting over the PA.] Jen Babes - The following contest is scheduled for one fall, and it has a 20 minute time limit! Introducing first, from St. Louis, Missouri, weighing in at 152 lbs, here is AIRIMIR!
[Airimir spins out from the entranceway, wearing a powder blue singlet top and baggy pleather pants, designed with clouds. He slaps the hands of the fans as he walks down the entranceway.]
Lampley - This young kid's got a lot of heart, but he's giving up a lot in the way of pure size tonight. It's going to be a very interesting matchup.
[Airimir makes it to the ring, and the music on the PA switches to...] "SOMETHING TAKES A PART OF ME.. YOU AND I WERE MEANT TO BE..." Jen Babes - ANd his opponent, from Leicester, England, weighing 298 pounds, here is JW WILDE!
["Freak on a Leash" by Korn plays over the PA as JW Wilde steps out of the entranceway, flexing and smiling.]
Lampley - JW Wilde actually asked for this match, and you have to admire
Airimir's gumption for accepting this match, but this seems like a suicide mission for the cruiserweight tonight.
[The bell rings, and JW Wilde begins stalking the smaller Airimir around the ring. Airimir ducks under several swings by Wilde, but gets trapped in the corner, and Wilde begins unloading on him with heavy forearm shots. Wilde whips Airimir across the ring hard, bouncing him out of the corner and down. Wilde picks up Airimir and throws him into the ropes..but Airimir ducks a big boot and answers with a dropkick to the back, staggering Wilde! Airimir dropkicks him again and sends him down...and then sliding dropkicks Wilde to the floor!]
Lampley - Wilde to his feet on the floor...Airimir off the ropes, PLANCHA, NO! WILDE CATCHES HIM! Wilde turns with him..no, Airimir pushes off him, and Wilde goes into the post! What a counter!
[Wilde slides back into the ring, and Airimir goes to the top rope..and is caught by the larger Wilde! Airimir tries to wiggle out, but JW Wilde drops him down with a thunderous powerslam! Wilde covers..
1.. 2.. ...and a kickout by Airimir. Wilde picks up Airimir and whips him into the ropes...and picks him up in a press slam! Wilde presses Airimir a few times, then throws him up and down facefirst to the mat! Airimir tries to get to his feet, but Wilde stomps him down repeatedly. The referee begins counting as Wilde chokes Airimir, but he won't break it until the referee gets between them. Airimir gets tossed into the corner again, but moves out of the way of a charge...he bounces off the ropes, and gets a big boot to the face!]
Lampley - This has been a total beatdown thus far here...Wilde throws
Airimir into the ropes...and a SPINEBUSTER! Cover..
1.. 2.. ....WILDE PULLS HIM UP! He's saying that's not it...hooks the arms, up and DOWN in the X-Factor! That's the Wilde Side Drop! He hooks the leg..
1.. 2.. 3, Wilde gets the pin! [The camera zooms out at JW Wilde celebrates his victory. We can see The
Gambler walk out to the announce desk, holding an icebag up on his head.]
Gambler - So, what did I miss? Lampley - JW Wilde Just got done dominating Airimir, where were you? Gambler - The match is over already? I was getting ice for this lump on my head. Stupid Brian Hill. Can I get a replay of what happened?
Lampley - Maybe we'll give you one during the break. Gambler - That and some painkillers. Lampley - Before we take a break, we've got Shima Takamura and Aaron
Vick backstage with some comments about last week.
[In a locker room in the back Shima Takamura has his back to the camera as he punches at the wall and rants incoherently in Japanese as he remains unfocused in the background. In the foreground Aaron Vick has a displeased look, he paces back and forth listening to Shima scream at the top of his lungs. Aaron stops and pats his star on the shoulder as he tries to calm him down, the sports agent turns back to the camera.]
Aaron Vick - You all saw what happened last Saturday! It was an uncalled for premeditated attack on the future of this company. You saw how they fans reacted to him, everything was going our way. We beat Krim’nal like we said we would, then this guy dressed in a mask has the nerve to attack Shima from behind.
[Shima turns to the camera and starts to scream out loud, and Aaron Vick tries to quiet him down again.]
Shima Takamura - COWARD!! Aaron Vick - You heard the man Darkness Tiger, you want to come out here talking nonsense about a greater power sending you. Well we all knew you were a few fruit loops short of a breakfast. I know there’s a lot of jealousy with the press Shima’s been getting but from here on out Tiger watch yourself because if you ever see this man face to face it’ll only be a second before you see nothing but the ceiling lights. Unlike some
Shima Takamura isn’t a victim and he doesn’t attack people from behind.
You can be sure that we’ll be there at ringside watching you, Shima lets go. [Aaron Vick starts to walk off camera as he calls Shima to go with him, but Takamura lets out a primitive growl before uttering his final threat.]
I…WILL…MAAAAAKE…YOU… VIIIIIIICTIM!!! [Shima takes the lens of the camera as he slowly closes his hands together crushing it leaving nothing but darkness on the screen.]
