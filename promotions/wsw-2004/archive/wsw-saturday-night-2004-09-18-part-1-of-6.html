WSW — Card

WSW Saturday Night, September 18, 2004 (Part 1 of 6)

Sep 19, 2004, 12:00:00 AM
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[We fade into a wide shot of the WSW Arena. The stands are full of fans, and a spotlight is shining on the WSW logo in the middle of the ring.]
Lampley - (on voiceover) Tonight, after two long years, WSW returns to action right here on WSW SATURDAY NIGHT!
[The view changes to a skyline shot of Seattle at dusk. The Space
Needle, Columbia Tower, Safeco Field, and Qwest Field are all visible.
You can hear the wind blowing in the background until...] "READY STEADY GO!" [Paul Oakenfold's "Ready Steady Go" plays as the camera slowly zooms in, interspersed with cutscenes of various wrestlers...
Jayson Reynolds hitting the Sinister Edge Krim'nal double arm DDTing an opponent "The Flagship" Richard Stark with a butterfly suplex Diablo flshing a sinister smile behind red mirrored glasses Shima Takamura being patted on the back by Aaron Vick The Bruiser flexing for the camera Ronnie Blades and Sunshine standing over a fallen opponent... The camera continues to zoom in closer and closer to street level, and it finally arrives at the front door of the WSW Arena. Superimposed on the door is the WSW Saturday Night logo.]
"R-R-R-READY... GO!" [The doors slide open and we go back to the wide pan shot of the crowd. It pans over to Vince Lampley and The Gambler standing at the interview stage as "Ready Steady Go" fades out .]
Lampley - Welcome, everyone, to the WSW Arena here in Seattle, Washington! I'm Vince Lampley, and alongside me, my broadcast co-hort for six years now, The Gambler. How's it feel to be back, G-Man?
Gambler - Please don't ever call me that again, Lamps. This is a great atmosphere, though, a packed house here at the WSW Arena, and inside that locker room, there are 25 or so guys ready to make an impression not only to the fans, but to the Commissioner and the Executive
Committee, because they're here tonight, too, and we're gonna find out exactly how WSW is going to name its new champion!
Lampley - Right you are, Gambler, and later tonight, in our main event, it's a battle of old versus new, as Krim'nal, the two time former WSW
North American Champion, takes on Shima Takamura!
Gambler - Yeah, all the matches today are old guard against new guard except for this Paul Doom and Diablo matchup. But Diablo's changed his look so much over the past two years, it might as well be old against new.
Lampley - Well, ring announcer Jen Babes is getting in the ring, so it's time for action! Jen Babes - At this time, I would like to introduce the commissioner of
Western States Wrestling, Mr. Byron Ellis!
["Cochise" by Audioslave begins to blast over the PA, and a black male of average build in his mid-30's walks out wearing a black pinstriped suit and carrying two velvet belt bags. He reaches the ring, climbs up the steps, and steps through the ropes into the ring, taking the microphone from Jen Babes.]
Ellis - I'd like to thank you all for coming out tonight to our re- opening gala. Thank you, everyone!
[The crowd cheers.] Ellis - At this time, I'd like all the wrestlers here tonight to come out onto the rampway.
[From the entrance walk out about 25 wrestlers, from the 6 foot 10 inch
Krim'nal to the 5 foot 11 inch Airimir.]
Ellis - These are the men that are going to make WSW the best wrestling federation in North America. And these are the men that will be fighting to earn this.
[Ellis unzips one of the velvet bags and pulls out the WSW Heavyweight
Championship belt, freshly polished. The belt shines and sparkles under the arena lights. Ellis sets the belt down on top of the bag on the mat.]
Ellis - The man that will hold this title will earn it by winning three matches in three weeks. There will be no second chance to be the first
WSW Champion of the new era. And to get a shot at those matches, you are going to have to prove to me, and to the Executive Committee, that you deserve it.
[Ellis pauses for a second letting this sink in with the wrestlers.] Ellis - For the next few weeks, from now to October 10, your performance in and out of the ring is going to be evaluated. The 6 best of you, the ones who win your matches, and prove that you're going to act like a champion and represent this company with dignity and respect, you will be part of an 8-man single elimination tournament starting October 17 and ending at our first supercard of 2004 on Halloween night from the building right across the street, Key Arena!
[The crowd pops.] Ellis - For the rest of you, all is not lost. On the 17th of October, there is going to be a Championship Scramble match. Everyone who doesn't make that top 6 will be in this match. Entries come in every 2 minutes, two at a time. You are eliminated from this match when you are pinned, made to submit, thrown over the top rope to the floor, or are disqualified. The match will continue in this manner until two men are remaining. Those two men will be entrants 7 and 8 into the Heavyweight
Championship tournament The men that survive the first two rounds will face each other at the Key Arena on October 31 in what will be a staple for Championship matches in WSW.
[A dramatic pause stirs the crowd.] Ellis - On October 31, the two finalists will face off in a best of 3 falls match. Someone is going to have to win twice on Halloween to be the WSW Champion!
[The crowd pops again.] Ellis - And as for this... [Ellis unzips the other bag, pulling out the WSW Interstate Championship title belt.] Ellis - The Interstate Championship, the belt that signifies that you are the number 1 contender to the WSW Championship... the last four men eliminated from the Championship Scramble will participate in their own tournament, culminating at Key Arena, October 31. Good luck to you all, and I'll be watching.
[Ellis puts both belts back in their bags as the wrestlers file back to the locker room. We pan back over to the announce table, now occupied by
Vince Lampley and the Gambler.]
Lampley - Some big announcements there by the commish, and it looks like these first few weeks of WSW are going to be real important for all these guys.
Gambler - Yep, if these guys want to be WSW Champion, they're going to have to prove it. Lampley - Well, coming up next, we'll get this show kicked off in style, as Jayson Reynolds takes on the man called "Your One True Fantasy", Carl
Blair. That's coming up next on WSW Saturday Night!
