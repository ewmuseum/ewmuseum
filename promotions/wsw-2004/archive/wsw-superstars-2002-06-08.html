WSW Superstars, June 8, 2002

8 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

— Part 1 of 4 —

[A WSW logo flickers in and out of fade to the tune of a generic guitar solo. At the crescendo of the solo, the logo gets its brightest, then shatters. Fade to...
Sean Southward Psycho Drivering Krusader at Retribution. Soundgarden's "Pretty Noose" plays in the background as various action clips flash by...] "I caught the moon today, pick it up, and throw it away all right..." [Krim'nal executing the Outtake on Donovan Astros.] "I got the perfect steal, a cleaner love, with a dirty feel all right..." [The New Midnight Express walking down the aisle.] "Fall out and take the bait, eat the fruit, and kiss the snake goodnight..." [Astros and Kid Dynamic beat down Krim'nal at High Stakes.] "Common ruse, dirty face..." [Dean King and Eros Fate grapple midring.] "Pretty noose is pretty hate..." [Sean Southward holds up the Heavyweight Championship, followed by Knuckles and DeJa Sku fighting atop a ladder.]
"And I don't like what you've got me hanging from..." [Kano and Karnage staring off, Nova Johnson hitting the Novabomb on Mr. Chang, and The Crippler planting Alternativ with the Crippler Buster.] "And I don't care what you got!" [Krim'nal pinning Donovan Astros.] "I don't care what you need!" [Dean King planting Fyre with the King of the Castle.] "I don't want anything!" ["Stunning" Steve Southward walks down the aisle.] "And I don't like what you've got me hanging from!" [The last clip fades to... ...and the background switches from black to the live crowd in Provo, Utah. WSW cameras pan across the crowd until they get to a tight-in shot of our announce crew, Jen Babes and Motormouth Dave.]
Jen Babes - Welcome to WSW Superstars live from Provo, Utah! I'm Jen Babes here with Motormouth, and..
Motormouth - And tonight, on my show, we get to see some of the newest and brightest stars in WSW, especially me!
Jen Babes - In addition, we'll hear from a man making his return to WSW, a former WSW Champion, The Mutt..
Motormouth - And the CURRENT WSW Champion, "Superstar" Sean Southward! Quit living in the past!
Jen Babes - All that and a lot more, but let's get down to Bill Dunn for the opening match tonight live from Provo!
["Everything Zen" by Bush plays over the sound system, and the crowd gives little reaction.]
Dunn - The following contest is scheduled for one fall! Making his way down the aisle... [The loner Rune steps through the curtains to a modest reaction. Rune raises his arms and elicits a modest crowd response as he walks down the aisleway to the apron.]
Dunn - From Stonehenge, weighing 303 pounds, RUNE! Jen Babes - Rune is definitely a strange competitor, sort of a protector of the down-trodden.
Motormouth - The bad news for him tonight, though, is that Masahiro Tabaka is going to make him one of the downtrodden himself!
[Rune climbs up the ringsteps and through the ropes, posing to the crowd. "Everything Zen" is abruptly cut off and replaced by "Faze" by Fuyima Tanaka. The crowd instinctively boos.] Dunn - And his opponent, accompanied to the ring by Lady Yung... [Lady Yung steps through the curtain, a devious smile on her face.] Motormouth - I really like Lady Yung, she's got it and knows how to use it. She's my kind of girl! Jen Babes - Thankfully, there's women in this business with higher standards than that. Motormouth - Show me one. Jen Babes - *hmph* Dunn - From Tokyo, Japan, weighing 237 pounds, MASAHIRO "ONI" TABAKA! [Masahiro steps through the curtains himself as the crowd boos the man behind the Devil mask.]
Jen Babes - What this guy and that fake Kid Dynamic did to the real Kid Dynamic at Retribution was sick and uncalled for! Motormouth - You're starting to sound just like this Rune fella. Jen Babes - Why, because I have a heart? [Masahiro climbs onto the apron and hops the top rope as a light display greets him midring. He walks back over to the ropes and parts the bottom two ropes for Lady Yung to step in the ring with him. They pose midring amidst all the boos as the lights return to normal. Lady Yung exits the ring to the floor as Masahiro sizes up Rune.]
Jen Babes - Although Masahiro has the experience advantage, he's giving up almost 70 pounds to Rune.
Motormouth - Normally, that'd be a grave disadvantage, but Masahiro excels at picking apart slower, bigger, and dumber opponents.
Jen Babes - You being the autority on stupidity here in WSW, I'm sure you're a great judge of people's intelligence.
Motormouth - Right! I mean... [Referee Ryan Stevens calls for the bell to start the match. Rune tries for a lockup, but Masahiro ducks away. Masahiro poses and smiles. Rune charges in and again Masahiro avoids the contact. Rune turns around right into a zerosen kick from Masahiro! Rune staggers back and Masahiro charges in with a Touch Of Class! Masahiro covers - 1... and a very quick kickout by Rune.
Both men get up at about the same time, and Rune takes Masahiro down with a big clothesline, eliciting a cheer from the crowd! Rune picks up Masahiro and slams him back to the mat with an old-school bodyslam! Rune backs into the ropes and comes off them with a body splash, but Masahiro rolls out of the way at the last second, causing Rune to crash to the mat violently!]
Jen Babes - Masahiro having his problems in the early going, but he looks to have the advantage now.
Motormouth - Even if you're bigger than him, you can't out-power a guy like Masahiro. He's too fast, and knows how to attack too well for you to make your size advantage work.
[Masahiro quickly goes to Rune's legs and attempts to lock in an Achilles tendon hold, but Rune kicks Masahiro into the ropes. Masahiro rebounds back into a clothesline by Rune, then drops an elbow into the chest of the "Oni".
Rune covers - 1... 2... and Masahiro kicks out again. Rune picks Masahiro up and whips him into the ropes. Rune ducks his head for a back body drop, but
Masahiro catches Rune's arm and flips him overusing his legs, locking in a cross-arm breaker! Rune uses his 6'9" frame to quickly get a foot on the ropes, and referee Ryan Stevens calls for the break.]
Motormouth - What a great tactical maneuver by Masahiro! Jen Babes - Normally I'd have to disagree with you, but that was an excellent takedown. [Rune gets up favoring his arm. Masahiro attacks it with a spinning inferno kick, staggering Rune into the ropes! Masahiro puts the arm behind the top rope, pulling the forearm through under the bottom rope, and applying a wristlock! Ryan Stevens counts Masahiro to five to break the hold. Masahiro breaks the hold and attempts an irish whip on Rune. Rune reverses the whip and sens Masahiro into the ropes. On the rebound, Rune catches Masahiro with a bearhug!]
Jen Babes - Look at Rune squeeze the breath out of Masahiro there! Motormouth - Not only does this hold work over the back and wind of your opponent, it allows you to catch your breath at the same time. Really a potent weapon for the big man.
[Masahiro struggles as Rune keeps Masahiro's feet off the ground. Masahiro connects with a double chop to the neck of Rune, but it doesn't phase the big man. A second double chop gets Rune to drop Masahiro back to his feet, still in the hold. Masahiro tries a third double chop, and connects, causing
Rune to break the hold! Masahiro chops away at the stunned Rune and runs into the ropes. Masahiro comes back on the rebound, but gets PLANTED with a powerslam by Rune! Rune covers - 1... 2... and Lady Yung gets on the apron!]
Jen Babes - What's she doing on the apron? The referee had to break his count because of her!
Motormouth - Civilian refereeing! It's obvious Rune had the tights on that pin. Jen Babes - Rune gets up off of Masahiro and goes over to Lady Yung, and boy, he does not look happy.
*SLAP* Jen Babes - Lady Yung just slapped the taste out of Rune's mouth! Rune rearing back to deliver some retribution...
Motormouth - Don't hit a lady, Rune! You're a defender of the weak, remember? Jen Babes - Is that supposed to be a comment about women? Motormouth - Ummm, uh... look! In the ring! Jen Babes - Lady Yung jumps down off the apron and Rune turns around into a kick to the midsection by Masahiro! Masahiro grabs Rune's head... MAS-A-MAN!
That X-Factor delivered by Masahiro might just be all he needs! [Masahiro hooks up Rune in a pin with his feet on the ropes behind Ryan Stevens's vantage... Stevens counts - 1... 2... 3!!!] Jen Babes - What a cheater! Masahiro gets the win here, but it's a rather tainted one for the "Oni".
Motormouth - It's only tainted in your eyes. What a great win for Masahiro here tonight! Dunn - Here is your winner, MASAHIRO TABAKA! (c)2002 Western States Wrestling, Inc. - http://wswfed.com/

— Part 2 of 4 —

Dunn - Ladies and gentlemen...please welcome the former WSW Champion...and self proclaimed Best Ever WSW Champ Past Present or Future....THE MUTT!
[Break Stuff by Limp Bizkit is playing on the Sound System as the crowd is in a frenzy cheering for The Mutt as he makes his long awaited comeback debut in the WSW. The Mutt comes out under the WSWTron and stands at the top of the ramp and raises one hand with a clinched fist in the air, as the crowd continues to cheer him, and some fans are doing a woof woof chant. The
Mutt then makes his way down to the ring. He then steps into the ring and stands in the middle of the ring and raises his clinched fist again at the crowd. The Mutt then walks over and gets a microphone from the announcer table.]
The Mutt - Well...Well...Well....It sure is nice to be back in the squared circle in an arena, in the great state of Utah *thumbs up* (Cheap POP!) and in the wonderful city of Provo *thumbs up* (Cheap POP!).
[The crowd roars as The Mutt mentions the city and state.] The Mutt - Well you see just as the legendary Brigham Young circled his wagons here in the state of Utah...The Mutt's career has come full circle, leading him back to the squared circle...and you know what? It feels DAMN
GOOD to be back. You never know how much you miss a thing until it's gone, and to be able to have a chance to recapture it, is a blessing. And tonight
The Mutt is here to let everyone know, that it is time for them to pick up their game, and be ready. You see this is where the whole happy go lucky...touchy feely stuff ends...it ends now. Yes it's great to be back, but now its time to be serious. And when the Mutt gets serious, everyone should stop what they are doing and pay attention, especially the ones running around with gold they do not deserve.
[The crowd cheers loudly for The Mutt as he has become serious. The Mutt is looking up at the fans and acknowledging them, as he takes the mic to his mouth to continue.]
The Mutt - Yeah that's right...the fans here all know it. We have ourselves a bunch of CHUMPS for CHAMPS! Well be warned, your championship days may be numbered. I do not care what belt it is, or who I take it from. I am hungry for GOLD, and when The Mutt is hungry, he usually goes on a HUNT, and his hunts are always successful. The HUNT starts on Wednesday, when The Mutt faces BURGER BOY...Spatula. Need The Mutt say anymore about this retard and his chances of winning...I think not. Let's just say he will be "OVER EASY!"
ha ha ha [The Mutt laughs as the crowd cheers loudly for him and at his comments.] The Mutt - Now...most of you probably watch WSW TV and saw that The Mutt said he had a major announcement to make. Well let The Mutt address that right now. The Mutt's sources tell him, that another major WSW star from the past will be making his debut before WSW Wednesday Night. Who is that star?
Well I guess we will all have to stay tuned to see who it is...ha ha ha...and with that said, The Mutt has one thing and one thing only left to say...COME GET SOME!
[The Mutt's music hits as he raises his fist to the crow again and they cheer loudly for him as he exits the ring and walks back up the ramp.]
Jen Babes - Who exactly could the Mutt be talking about? Who's coming back to WSW? Motormouth - I have a good idea who it is, Jen, and I don't like it one bit. Jen Babes - Who? Motormouth - We- Dunn - This contest is scheduled for one fall! Motormouth - Dammit! He interrupted me! Now I'm not going to say. Jen Babes - Oh, come on.. Dunn - Making his way down the aisle... from Kobe, Japan... weighing 219 pounds... KENDO... NAKAMURA!
[The debuting Kendo Nakamura enters the arena and walks down the aisle, staring straight at the ring. He slides into the ring and walks into his corner, turning to point toward the entrance.]
Jen Babes - Not much is known about Kendo Nakamura.. he's been trying to come to America for some time, wrestling in various promotions in Japan and perfecting his craft.
Motormouth - And tonight, he gets to perfect his style on Fyre! [Speaking of, Creed's "My Sacrifice" begins to play, elicting some very half-hearted cheers.] Dunn - And his opponent... from Dublin, Ireland... weighing 235 pounds... FYRE! [Fyre runs into the ring and comes at Nakamura..who leaps up and snaps Fyre over with a hurricanrana, ending up on Fyre's chest. Nakamura then smoothly transitions into a cross armbreaker, hyperextending Fyre's arm. Fyre squirms in pain and reaches the ropes, causing the hold to be broken. Nakamura gets up and goes to center ring, telling the kneeling Fyre to bring it on. Fyre charges at him and gets struck with a spinning roundhouse kick, staggering him into the corner. Nakamura advances on him, walloping him with stiff kicks to the stomact and chest and finishing with a huge highkick to the head.]
Jen Babes - Thus far, Kendo Nakamura is just whittling Fyre down to size! Motormouth - The last time I saw something that st- Jen Babes - STOP! Motormouth - What? [Fyre staggers out of the corner, and Nakamura gives him a jawbreaker, followed by a kick to the gut and a DDT. Nakamura covers... 1... 2...
kickout by Fyre. Nakamura bounces off the ropes..but the flashing elbowdrop misses its mark. Fyre armdrags Nakamura off his feet once..twice...then cinches in an armbar. Fyre transitions into a top wristlock. Nakamura bridges out of the move to a vertical base...then executes a standing enzuigiri to take Fyre down! Nakamura moves behind the standing Fyre and executes a snap belly to back suplex, then covers. 1... 2... kickout.]
Jen Babes - Nakamura with a scoop..and a backbreaker! He's going up top, but Fyre's moving..and the moonsault misses! Motormouth - But he's on his feet! Jen Babes - And off of it, Fyre with a dropkick! Fyre off the ropes..but Nakamura with a standing spinkick takes him back down again! Motormouth - No matter what he does, Fyre CANNOT get anything going against Nakamura! [Nakamura kicks Fyre down into the corner, then scrapes his face with the side of his boot. Nakamura waits for Fyre to get up and goes for another high kick, but Fyre ducks and bulldogs Nakamura! Fyre goes to the top rope for The Flame, but Nakamura's up..and springs off the second rope to strike
Fyre in the skull with a kick, sending him to the floor!] Jen Babes - What a shot by Nakamura, Fyre slow to get to his feet... Motormouth - Fyre's slow to get up in the morning. Jen Babes - Nakamura's on the apron..SPRINGS, ASAI MOONSAULT BY NAKAMURA! What a move! Motormouth - That might just finish him! Jen Babes - Fyre thrown back inside, Nakamura back in as well..Nakamura measuring him... Motormouth - BINGO! Jen Babes - Nakamura just PASTES him with a spinkick! He might be out! Motormouth - He's not done with him, though! Jen Babes - Kendo Nakamura on the top rope...FLIPPING LEGDROP CONNECTS! And a cover, one, two, three! It's over!
Dunn - Here is YOUR winner... KENDO... NAKAMURA! Jen Babes - Kendo Nakamura debuts with a DECISIVE win over Fyre, a former Intercontinental champion! Motormouth - This is the kinda guy we need more of in WSW - completely merciless, goes in there to win and not kiss babies!
Jen Babes - You WOULD like him.. (c)2002 Western States Wrestling, Inc. - http://wswfed.com/

— Part 3 of 4 —

[The screen fades to black and the following words appear]
"The spirit I have seen/ May be the devil: and the devil hath power/ To assume a pleasing shape." ---Shakespeare: Hamlet [The screen fades to black once again.. then fades into a cemetary.. with a coffin and a person in a cloak.. hiding his face]
Cloaked Man - We are gathered here today to morn the passing of Diablo... The Diablo as you knew him...He is gone... SHUT UP.. the voices in my head are yelling at me... Fine.. I'll take off this damn cloak..
[The man removed his cloak.. revealing... Diablo?] Diablo - The real reason I am here is to.. completly and utterly destroy WSW! wait.. where the hell did that come from.. will you guys just shut up and let me finish this? I mean it... OK.. you asked for it!
[Diablo starts banging his head against the wall..] Diablo - Sorry about that.. Those guys dont know when to leave me alone.. You see... I want WSW to know that I am not the same Diablo that was part of the so-called Movement. I was different then.. I am sick of being lumped in with the same gang of idiots as the so called Saviors of WSW. They have become a parody of themselves.. They have become what they didnt want to become.. SPORTS ENTERTAINERS.... but that isnt important right now.. I am told by several of the voices that I should be talking about my upcoming match with Krusader.. but.. why should I? I mean.. He hasnt been around since his loss to one of those Southward idiots.
[Diablo suddenly gets a strange look on his face..] Diablo - ATTENTION ALL WSW SUPERSTARS... and we use the term superstars lightly. We shall use this perfect specimen of a professional wrestler to destroy this pitful federation called WSW. You have all been warned.
Krusader will be the first of many to feel our wrath.. our pain.. the pain that we love sooo much.. the pain that brought us to Diablo in the first place... STOP IT... STOP TAKING OVER...
[Diablo rams his head into the wall and passes out... as the screen fades. Cut back to a rather stunned Jen Babes and Motormouth. Motormouth is just shaking his head.]
Jen Babes - He's totally gone. Motormouth - To quote Hank Hill..that boy ain't right. Jen Babes - Diablo's disappearance after losing at WSW Retribution obviously did a lot more damage to him than we suspected..
Motormouth - OBVIOUSLY? He's more wacko than Wakko, Yakko AND Dot put together! Jen Babes - Let's go up to Bill Dunn while we sort things out here. ["Schism" by Tool plays in the arena, elicting a modest response from the capacity crowd.] Dunn - This contest is scheduled for one fall! Making his way down the aisle... from Las Vegas, Nevada.. weighing 225 pounds.. here is.. THE FALLEN
ANGEL! [The Fallen Angel walks to the ring, slowly ascending the steps and entering the ring. His music cuts off.]
"LET THE BODIES HIT THE........FLOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOR!" [The crowd boos the playing of Drowning Pool's "Bodies" quite loudly.] Dunn - And his opponent... accompanied down the aisle by Kristen Jean... from Altoona, Pennsylvania.. weighing 224 pounds... "THE REASON"... RICK.. CAREY! [The beautiful Kristen Jean escorts the bleach blond hair, tattooed, nipple ring bearing Rick Carey to ringside, carrying Carey's Singapore cane in her hands. Carey jumps up onto the ring apron and enters the ring, snapping at the jeering fans. Referee Raquel Devine calls for the bell. Fallen Angel moves in for a lockup but Carey goes low and bulls Angel into the corner.
Carey executes consecutive shoulderdrives, then punches away at Angel, clubbing him down in the corner. Devine counts to get Carey out of the corner, but Carey grabs Angel and heaves him over the top rope.]
Jen Babes - Carey has just overpowered Angel to start out..wait, off the ropes, SUICIDE DIVE BY RICK CAREY! What a move!
Motormouth - This guy'll put his body on the line just to take this bum out! You gotta love it! [Carey backs Angel hard into the steps, then Irish whips him into the ringpost. Carey throws Angel into the ring and follows him back in, then kicks him as he's getting up and DDTs him in mid-ring. Carey picks Angel back up and executes a second DDT...then picks him up AGAIN and DDTs him for the third time! Carey covers... 1... 2... Carey yanks Angel up by the hair!
Devine reprimands Carey, who ignores her and whips Angel hard into the corner. Carey follows in with a vicious clothesline. Carey whips Angel across the ring again and follows in..but Angel ducks the clothesline! Carey stops his momentum by grabbing the buckle, then turns, grabs Angel by the hair and pulls him into an inverted DDT.]
Motormouth - Fallen Angel has just been demolished so far! Any more of this and he WILL be a fallen angel!
[Carey sets up Angel..and powerbombs him down! Carey points to the sky, then climbs to the top rope...and executes a picture-perfect shooting star press!
Carey covers... 1... 2... he pulls him up again! Carey waves off Devine and whips Angel into the corner. Angel hits chestfirst and staggers backward...Carey gets behind him and sets him up on the top buckle. Carey climbs to the second rope..and executes the Aftermath, an Outsider's Edge off the top! Carey stands up and puts one foot on Angel's chest, telling
Devine to count. She does so.. one... two... three!] Dunn - Here is your winner.. "THE REASON"... RICK... CAREY! Jen Babes - My god, what a complete and total slaughter by Rick Carey! Motormouth - And I don't think he's done! Jen Babes - Kristen Jean just threw him that Singapore cane... and Carey is waylaying Angel with it! Come on, stop this!
Motormouth - I doubt Raquel's going to mess up her manicure to stop him, do you? Jen Babes - Carey is wearing out Fallen Angel with that Singapore cane..now he's got him on the top rope again, and he canes him again...come on, no!
Another Aftermath! Motormouth - Here come the officials...there they go! Jen Babes - Carey swings at the officials to clear 'em out, that'll cost him some money! Here come the medics to take away Angel, who's in a lot of pain..
Motormouth - Gee, ya think? Jen Babes - The officials are trying to hold back Carey as the EMTs take Angel away...no, Carey breaks away, HE LEAPS OVER THE TOP ROPE AND CANES ANGEL ON THE WAY DOWN! Motormouth - YES! Jen Babes - We've got more officials out to stop this man, what was the reason for this attack?!
Motormouth - Rick Carey's all the Reason we need! (c)2002 Western States Wrestling, Inc. - http://wswfed.com/

— Part 4 of 4 —

[For the final segment of WSW Superstars, we cut backstage to the locker room after. Our intrepid reporter, Lil Jimmy, is sitting down next to the
WSW Champion, "Superstar" Sean Southward. Southward is dressed in a leather jacket with faded jeans and a black Motörhead England "Boneshaker 2001 Tour"
t-shirt. He's also sporting the WSW Championship belt lying on his knee, and a bandage on the side of his head.]
Lil Jimmy - "Superstar" Sean Southward, last Wednesday night..your Saviors partners, "Stunning" Steve Southward and Donovan Astros attacked you, giving you a conchairto after your match with Krim'nal ended. Let's go back and show what happened..
[Jimmy turns toward a television monitor on the table next to him and Southward, but Southward simply lifts his foot up and shoves the monitor off the table. It lands with a loud crash next to the table.]
Southward - I don't need to see the footage, Jimmy, I was there. Lil Jimmy - Alright...well, I guess the question is, how do you feel right now? [Southward turns toward Jimmy.] Southward - How do you think I feel, Jimmy? How do you THINK I feel? Two guys that were basically blood to me..a guy I've known for almost five years now, and somebody I've known my whole life..cracked my head with a couple of steel chairs and left me for dead. How do you THINK I feel?
Lil Jimmy - W- Southward - No, shut up, Jimmy, and listen for once. I hear you people out there saying stuff like there's no honor among thieves, and I had it coming.
And maybe I did, for actually getting to the point where I trusted Astros and I trusted my brother Steve. I trusted them to watch my back and help me if I needed it. Well, on Wednesday, I *didn't* need it, and I let them know it. And for it, I got a conchairto. Does that sound right to you?
Lil Jimmy - Y- Southward - No, it SHOULDN'T. It's not about any of that. It's about one thing - the simple fact that I'm the WSW Champion, and that I'm the best wrestler in the world. I don't say all that because it sounds good, I say it because it's the goddamn TRUTH. I've proven it time and time again. And
Astros and Steve..they can't handle it. If they wanted a chance to become the WSW Champion, all they had to do was ASK ME. They just had to have the guts to come to me and ask for a title shot. I even outright GAVE THEM THE
CHANCE, and they didn't take it. Instead, they've gotta go around me and pull this crap. Especially Astros.
Lil Jimmy - Well, what happened? Put yourself in their shoes..why did they do it? Southward - Donovan Astros is real simple. I succeeded where he FAILED. I've got the WSW Championship now, he doesn't, and that STICKS IN HIS CRAW. He can't handle that fact. Well, now he's going to have to handle it, because now he AND Steve have done something they should've known better about - they have PISSED ME OFF. So now, bravo, boys, you're going to get your chance - your chance to see what I'm like when I'm REALLY angry.
Lil Jimmy - Since it looks like the Saviors have crumbled.. [Southward rolls his eyes.] Southward - Brilliant deduction, Watson. Lil Jimmy - ...what are you going to do now? [Southward tils his head for a moment and pauses. He picks the WSW Championship belt up off his leg and holds it in his hand.] Southward - What am I going to do? As far as my dear brother Stevie goes, it's going to be just like we were kids again..only this time, I'm not going to beat him like the redheaded stepchild he was then, I'm going to beat him like the *blond*headed stepchild he is NOW. And Donovan Astros....
[Southward slowly gets to his feet with the WSW Championship belt in his hand.] Southward - Well, for months here, all everybody's been saying is that Donovan Astros is Sean Southward's bitch. [Southward leans down and gets right into Jimmy's face, dead serious.] Southward - Well, NOW..he's going to be. [Southward walks past Lil Jimmy, who watches Southward exit out of view. A door slamming is heard, punctuating Southward's exit, as well as the end of
WSW Superstars. Fade out.] (c)2002 Western States Wrestling, Inc. - http://wswfed.com/
