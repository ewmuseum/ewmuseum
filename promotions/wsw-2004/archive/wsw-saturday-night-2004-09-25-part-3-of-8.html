WSW Saturday Night, September 25, 2004 (Part 3 of 8)

Sep 29, 2004, 12:00:00 AM
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Jen Babes - The following match is set for one fall, with a 20 minute time limit! [The lights go down, and "Cemetary Gates" by Pantera filters into the arena.] Jen Babes - Introducing first, making his WSW debut, from HELL, weighing
328 pounds.. VENOM!
[The behemoth known as Venom slowly stalks his way to ringside, looking down at the mat on his way down. He stops at the ringside area and turns back toward the entrance, waiting.]
Lampley - This monster Venom will be quite an undertaking for Diablo, who seems to have undergone a change of some sort since his return to
WSW last week.
Gambler - And he better have more changes in mind if he wants to beat
Venom..look at this guy, he's huge!
[The crowd begins to cheer as "The World without Logos by Ishii Yasushi begins to play.] Jen Babes - And his opponent, from London, England, weighing 270 pounds, the former PCCW Pacific Northwest champion.. DIIAAAABLOOO!
[The curtain parts..but nobody appears. The lights turn back on slowly, as Venom braces himself for Diablo's imminent entrance.]
Gambler - So where is he, Lamps? Lampley - I have no idea..this is Diablo's second match here after his close loss to Paul Doom last we-
[The lights go completely out.] Lampley - WHAT THE? Gambler - MOMMY! Lampley - The lights are out in the arena again tonight! Gambler - Can we PLEASE get the electric bill paid?! Lampley - We can't see..,. NOW WE CAN! DIABLO IS BEHIND VENOM! Gambler - BEHIND YO-OW Lampley - DIABLO THROWS VENOM RIGHT INTO THE RINGPOST! HEADFIRST! Gambler - HE'S DOWN! [Diablo begins stomping away and raining rights and lefts down on Venom, who's busted wide open from the ringpost shot. Diablo picks up Venom on his shoulder..and rams him into the ringpost, then drops him on the steps! With a murderous look in his eyes, Diablo throws Venom into the guardrail, and then sandwichs him with a clothesline! Diablo positions
Venom near the rail, then picks it up and drives it down on Venom's throat, choking him with the rail!]
Lampley - I swear, Diablo is trying to destroy Venom! Gambler - It's what he does, Lampley! Lampley - The referee down, pulling at Diablo to pull him off of Venom! I don't think this match has even started! Gambler - And Diablo's after the referee! What the hell did Venom do to piss Diablo off?! Lampley - By the looks of thinking, EXISTIN- [The lights dim.] Gambler - AGAIN WITH THE LIGHTS! Lampley - We're in dimness here, folks-- Gambler - --appropriate, considering how dim you are-- ["Original Sin" by Ishii Yasushi plays. The crowd gets on their feet.] Lampley - Oh NO! That's--that's the music of that man we saw last week! Gambler - What's going on here?! Lampley - We've got officials down here to help Venom, and Diablo just slid into the ring! He's waiting for that man to show up!
[Several referees and officials are carrying out the demolished Venom up the aisle...as a blaringly white spotlight shines..and he appears.]
Gambler - THAT'S ISCARIOT! Lampley - What? Gambler - ISCARIOT! THAT'S HIS NAME! I've heard of him! Lampley - He's coming for Diablo again! [The man in white, named Iscariot by Gambler, has a crooked half-smile as he walks up the aisle, looking at Diablo. The referees with Venom stand between him and the ring. Iscariot nods to the officials, and stands aside to let them pass...but looks at them and catches a glimpse of Venom. Iscariot's smile quickly turns to a cold, hard stare.]
Lampley - WHAT THE?! Gambler - ISCARIOT JUST ATTACKED VENOM! AND THE REFS GO FLYING! Lampley - Iscariot just nailed Venom from behind with a kick to the head! He's kicking and throwing Venom up the aisle to the ring!
Gambler - He just pointed at Diablo..look at the look in his eyes! Venom's on EVERYBODY'S bad list today! Lampley - Iscariot throws Venom into the ringpost, he's barely standing...OH MY GOD! ISCARIOT JUST SANDWICHED VENOM'S HEAD AGAINST THE
POST WITH A ROUNDHOUSE KICK! Gambler - That could have crushed Venom's skull! Did you HEAR that impact! Lampley - The officials are down here again, and they're trying to get to Venom...what the..
Gambler - Iscariot's backing off! [Indeed, he's backing off, as Diablo is staring right into his eyes. Iscariot wags a finger and smiles at Diablo, then backs up the aisle once more. EMTs come down with a stretcher for Venom, being sure to get a large number of bodies around him to prevent any more damage to the possibly-crippled Venom. Diablo yells in anger as we fade to a break.]
