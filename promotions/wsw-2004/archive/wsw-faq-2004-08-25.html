WSW Frequently Asked Questions

WESTERN STATES WRESTLING (http://wswfed.com/) In September 2002, Western States Wrestling (WSW) closed its door for what many thought was forever. Two years later, everything has changed.
WSW Frequently Asked Questions How many wrestlers is WSW looking for? WSW is looking for 12-16 talented players to fill its roster. The first show will not run unti we get to the 12 wrestler mark.
Will wrestlers from the original WSW be automatically added to the roster? No! All applications will be reviewed based on their talent and quality of roleplays. No one will be getting a free pass into WSW.
How many titles will there be in WSW? WSW will start out with two title belts: WSW Championship - The top belt in the promotion, this is what every wrestler in WSW should strive for. Either you want this, or you go home.
WSW Interstate Championship - The secondary title in WSW, holding this title means you are the #1 contender to the WSW Championship, and will get a title shot within 30 days.
How many shows a week will WSW run? WSW will run 1 scheduled show and 1 syndicated show a week: WSW Saturday Night - On or about 6:05 Pacific on Saturday nights
(if we're not preempted by Mariners baseball), WSW Saturday Night airs on KSTW11 in the greater Northwest area. You'll see the top superstars of WSW battling it out for the right to call themselves WSW Champion.
WSW Power Hour - The Power Hour showcases the superstars of WSW with exciting matches, interviews, and action you won't see anywhere else! Check local listing for time and station.
What sets WSW apart from other promotions? We are looking to make this as enjoyable for the players as possible, because without good roleplayers, no fed can exist. Wrestlers will be booked, most likely, in a match every week, unless a player requests otherwise. If we can't find room for you on WSW Saturday
Night, you'll have a match on the Power Hour against one of the WSW Dojo trainees to showcase your talents. We have dedicated staff with (sadly)
almost 20 years in combined experience in roleplaying, writing, and booking. What are the deadlines/expectations for each show? Both shows will have a interview/strat deadline of 6PM Pacific time the Thursday before WSW Saturday Night. WSW Saturday Night is where storylines and angles will advance, and interviews and strats for that show should focus on advancing issues. WSW Power Hour, as a syndicated broadcast, is much less specific, simply because it may air at different times on different weeks, or perhaps get pre-empted.
Interviews on the Power Hour should be general and shouldn't have any specific angle advancement. Also, Power Hour RPs shouldn't reference anything that will be happening on the accompanying Saturday Night.
I expect something out of each player every week, whether it be a roleplay, a strat, an interview, or just some communication about why you couldn't get something in that week. If you don't RP, strat, or interview in a week, expect to lose. If you disappear for weeks at a time, expect to lose to the WSW Dojo trainees a lot. If you need to leave for an extended amount of time, if you're going on vacation or whatever, just communicate with me.
More will be fleshed out in the coming weeks as we get more applications. If you have any questions, feel free to email me at
