WSW Saturday Night, September 18, 2004 (Part 5 of 6)

Sep 19, 2004, 12:00:00 AM
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[We come back to the WSW Saturday Night interview stage, just off of the entrance. Vince Lampley is standing in front of a WSW Saturday Night logo holding a microphone.]
Lampley - Ladies and Gentlemen, we have a surprise for you here tonight. One of WSW's finest sons recently returned after a 12 month-plus absence from wrestling. Please, welcome back... Mr Ed!!
[A hush falls as a lone spotlight focuses on the curtains atop the ramp. They briefly shake and then Mr Ed bursts through, standing in the light, while "Good Reason" by Seafood plays over the PA. Suddenly, the audience erupts to a rapturous welcome of applause and cheers. Mr Ed strides confidently over to the interview stage and stands next to Vince
Lampley.]
Mr Ed - Thanks for having me out here, Vince. Lampley - Well, let's not keep these folks waiting, they wanna know - what brought Mr Ed back to WSW?
Mr Ed - C'mon Vince, you know as well as I and all these other people do... as soon as that phone rang and someone told me that WSW was not only back up and running, but preparing for a re-opening in a matter of weeks, my mind was made up instantly.
Lampley - So, what's happened since we last saw you? Mr Ed - There was a short, 6 month spell with ACCW - but that didn't really pan out. The company didn't have the same draw, couldn't attract the big name stars and many viewers and audience members were seeking bigger and better things. So, I did the same. The lack of challenge really got to me, I had words with the boss and there was a mass exodus of the higher level stars and ultimately the company folded due to extreme lack of interest.
Lampley - And then, what? Flipping burgers? Washing cars? Mr Ed - Ha! You wish, monkey boy. I went back home to England, spent time with my old friends and family, caught up on lots of things I'd missed from spending so much time travelling but all along - I was looking for a way back into wrestling. My training regime never once let up - every day has been spent working out as if I had a match the very next night and thanks to that I'm still in the same shape I'm always in.
Ready and raring to go. Lampley - Well, it certainly is good to see familiar names - yourself,
Diablo, Jason Reynolds, JD Money and then there's that Krim'nal chap.
Mr Ed - Aaahhh yes, I did wonder when we'd get to that one. i think, in the current roster, we have a nice blend of experienced guys to take the top slots and newer, fresh talent to work the earlier matches but also to develop, pressure the guys at the top and ultimately challenge them.
Lampley - That's so very true. But, the question on everyone's lips, what about Krim?!? Mr Ed - You really don't give up do you? Were you a piranha in a past life? Or maybe a praying mantis?
Lampley - It's definitely one of my more... endearing qualities. Mr Ed - Well, Krim and I haven't spoken in a long time. We've had so many ups, so many downs and so many hilarious nights between these walls. I look forward to seeing him again, fighting him again and, as always, stomping his little scally ass into the ground.
[The crowd lets out an audible chuckle.] Mr Ed - But, this is about now. WSW present, not WSW past! Mr Ellis has decided to give me a few matches to get my match-game back and that suits me fine. After that, it's business as usual. I'll be back on the hunt for gold, busting heads, kicking ass and entertaining all these dedicated Ed-Heads in here!!
[The crowd now rises to its feet, cheering and clapping.] Lampley - Anyone in particular in your sights? Maybe settle some old scores? Mr Ed - Wait and see, Vince, I'm going after whoever has the title. If that happens to be Krim, then even better.
[Mr Ed shakes hands with Vince.] Lampley - Thanks for coming out tonight, Ed, I hope we see you back out here soon. Mr Ed - Oh believe you me, you will do, Vince, I'll be back out sooner than you think. Night folks, enjoy the rest of your night!!
[Mr Ed high fives some fans around the entranceway, soaking things in before disappearing behind the thick black curtain.]
Lampley - Coming up next is our main event, as the man we were just talking about, Krim'nal, takes on Shima Takamura! Stay tuned, folks!
