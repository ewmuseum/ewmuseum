WSW Saturday Night, September 18, 2004 (Part 3 of 6)

Sep 19, 2004, 12:00:00 AM

[Over the WSW airways we hear.. "Let's take some calls from the request line caller number 1"...........as the music of Petey Pablo (Freek-A-Leek) is playing in the arena. We see Sunshine appear from behind the curtain wearing a white sexy mini skirt and a short white top exposing her vulptuous breasts and toned stomach. She has a mic in her hand. The crowd is whistling loudly and cheering loudly for Sunshine, who rolls her eyes, and taps the mic.]
Sunshine - "Now that I have your attention, will you please stop making those outrageous noises. You guys sound like a pack of wolves in heat!
Have you never seen a beautiful woman before, that's right, only fat slobs live in this town...my bad!"
[The crowd begins to boo!] Sunshine - "Well, let me first make something perfectly clear, none of you dead beats could ever stand a chance with a woman like me, the sun doesn't shine on any of you, but I can tell you who the sun does shine on...and that's the future heavyweight champion of WSW......RONNIE
BLADES!" [The crowd boos loudly as appearing from behind the curtain is Ronnie
Blades. Decked out in his R.B hooded sweatshirt, the initials R.B on the front and on the back "Unleash the fury of the Blades". Ronnie Blades walks towards Sunshine, grabs her by the waste then kisses her passionately on the lips. He then let's her go as the crowd boos
LOUDLY!] Sunshine - "Now this is what you call a REAL MAN!" [The crowd chants "Slut...Slut...Slut"... R.B doesn't seem phased by the chant, and whispers something in Sunshine's ear, who then nods.]
Sunshine - "I've just been informed by Blades, that any of the guys that are chanting Slut, that if you really want to prove how much of a man you are, that if any of you can beat him 1...2...3 in the middle of the ring, then you would get to see how much of a slut Sunshine really is!"
[Sunshine's voice lowers as she says the last part, and goes back to
Ronnie Blades as if to say, are you sure about this. Ronnie nods his head in approval to signify yes, it's cool.]
Sunshine - "Well, I have no worries, as there is no one in the back, and no one in this audience that stands a chance against Ronnie Blades! Let me just give you guys a brief history lesson..."
[Crowd boos....and the chant of slut starts up again, as Sunshine is evidently upset at the chant. She looks at Ronnie and says they are calling me a slut...I'm not a slut. Ronnie shows no emotion, and doesn't respond and Sunshine pouts then beings to speak again.]
Sunshine - "Do any of you people even know that Ronnie has held championships in Australia, Japan, and in many indy feds around the
United States? He's no newbie to the wrestling world, my baby has one every championship known to man....except the WSW championship! That's why he's here, to add this championship to his long list of achievements."
[The crowd chants...Bull*bleep*...] Sunshine - "Hello, this is a family friendly show, you people have no CLASS" [The crowd changes the chant again, and now are chanting SLUT
SLUT...SLUT!] Sunshine - "Well, to make things short and to the point, we are here in
WSW to stay! We are going to dominate, and destroy every single superstar that dare faces us in that ring! Ronnie is a superior athlete, he's quick, he's agile, he's powerful, and he's darn well good in bed! I just had to throw that in there, since only him can satisfy me, not like anyone in the back or any of you deadbeats ever could!"
[The camera goes to show R.B standing with his hands folded eyes wandering around the crowd. Sunshine passes the mic to Ronnie.]
R.B - "This party is now getting started, my time is now, and the others in the back, and all you people in the audience, your time for greatness will NEVER COME, not as long as Ronnie Blades is here in WSW...now with that said......the sun has just set on WSW, and the reflection is on
ME!" [With that Ronnie drops the mic, grabs Sunshine by the arm as they walk to the back with the crowd booing loudly!]
Jen Babes - And his opponent... ["Wanksta" by 50 Cent plays in the arena. The crowd begins to stand up and cheer.] Jen Babes - ..being led to the ring by his manager, Mr. K, from Ottawa,
Ontario, Canada, weighing 225 pounds... J.. D.. MONEY!
[Mr. K opens the curtain and leads the way to the aisle for JD Money. K clears his way through the aisle, with Money close behind, until they get to the ring. Money slides in and goes to the corner, standing on the second buckle to the cheers of the crowd.]
Lampley - JD Money, the former Mr. Ca$h Money, former member of 3GX, now the head of Money Inc! Quite a change for this young man!
Gambler - Money Inc?! I don't see Mike Rotunda anywhere in this arena! Lampley - *sigh* [Money turns his back to adjust his boots, but it's a mistake, because
Blades nails him from behind before the bell, sending him into the turnbuckle!]
Lampley - Ronnie Blades with a cheap sneak attack right before the bell! And he just sent JD Money into the other buckle, and he's laying into him with those kicks!
Gambler - I like this guy already! Bell to bell action, gets going right at the bell! Lampley - But the bell didn't ring! Gambler - Just a minor technicality! [Blades whips Money hard into the buckle, sending him staggering back out...and then takes him right down with a huge spinebuster! Blades covers nonchalantly..
1.. 2.. ...Money kicks out, and Blades immediately picks him up and tosses him to the floor.] Lampley - Blades follows him out...MONEY SURPRISES HIM! CA$H MONEY KICK! Gambler - How'd you pronounce that?! Lampley - That caught Ronnie Blades totally off guard! And now Money whips Blades into that steel ringpost! Blades rolls back inside on instinct..Money in on the other side..CLOTHESLINE BY BLADES!
Gambler - He totally waylaid him with that clothesline, took him right outta his socks! [Blades shakes out the cobwebs in his head and goes back on offense on
Money. As Money gets back to his feet, Blades knocks him right back down with a big right hand. Another vertical stance, another right hand, and a legdrop for good measure. Money covers..
1.. 2, and a kickout! Blades immediately wraps him up in a reverse chinlock.] Gambler - This match has been kinda different than I expected, I thought
Blades would whack him around a bit!
Lampley - It's early, and thus far, Blades' girl Sunshine hasn't been involved. Gambler - I know I'm more involved just watching her instead! Lampley - Will you stop?! [The crowd begins to get behind JD Money, and he gets back to his feet, and begins elbowing out of the chinlock.. and Blades immediately shuts him down with a big kneelift to the gut. Blades shoves Money into the corner and begins unloading with right hands, with the referee getting in between them to break it up, but not before Blades hits a right hook over the ref to Money.]
Lampley - Ronnie Blades is just pounding away at JD Money..now moving along the ropes, Blades sending him there with more punches right to the head!
Gambler - Money's head's gonna be lumpier than pizza dough when this match is over! Lampley - Money against the ropes, Blades is loading up...NO! MONEY LOWBRIDGES HIM, BLADES OVER THE TOP TO THE FLOOR! Gambler - Right in front of Sunshine! Help him! Lampley - JD Money going after Blades..and Sunshine's in between the two of them! And Money's going after her!
Gambler - Don't you DARE touch her! Lampley - Sunshine's backing up..AND BLADES SHOVES MONEY INTO THE STEPS! He went headfirst into the steps! Gambler - I think he might be out! Lampley - And Sunshine's LAUGHING at JD Money! It was a setup, pure and simple! Blades tosses Money back in..he's climbing up top...GUILLOTINE
LEGDROP! 1! 2! HE PULLS HIM UP! Gambler - He went after his girl, Lamps, he ain't done! Lampley - Blades with the thumb across the throat, this could be up...scoops him up...sidewalk slam, no, around, around, AROUND AGAIN,
AND DOWN HARD! THAT'S THE VIBRATOR! Blades on top, hooks the leg!
1! 2! 3! Blades with the win! Jen Babes - Your winner, Ronnie Blades! Lampley - Ronnie Blades picks up the decisive victory here on WSW Wednesday Night! Both he and Sunshine standing victorious over JD Money! Gambler - That was an impressive win! JD Money's got history here in
WSW, and Blades just took him down and out in his first match!
Lampley - We'll be right back! --- (c) 2004 WSW Productions
