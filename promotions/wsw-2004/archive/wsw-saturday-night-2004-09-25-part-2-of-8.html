WSW — Card

WSW Saturday Night, September 25, 2004 (Part 2 of 8)

Sep 29, 2004, 12:00:00 AM
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Lampley - Our next match is supposed to be Eric Hunt and James Knight, but we're getting some word from the back that... yes... we've got a camera back in Eric Hunt's locker room, can we get back there?
[The camera shifts to Eric Hunt's locker room, where everything is tore up. Eric Hunt is lying unconscious under a stand of lockers, and spraypainted on the wall is "The Krimewave Is On". EMT's are working to lift the lockers off of Hunt.]
Gambler - Krim'nal is one not very happy camper. Lampley - We're getting word that the scene is the same in James
Knight's locker room, Krim'nal has just destroyed these men's debut!
Gambler - Krim'nal's out for revenge! Lampley - Well, Ronnie Blades has asked for some interview time, so I guess we'll, um, go to that.
"Let's take some calls from the request line...caller #1" [Freek-A-Leek by Petey Pablo is heard playing in the arena as Sunshine appears from behind the curtain wearing a pink robe and pink high heels.
The crowd starts to boo as emerging from behind the curtain as well is
Ronnie Blades, in his usual attire R.B hooded sweatshirt and his personalized R.B jeans, with a scarf tied around his head! They walk to the ring as they have a scheduled interview with Lampley.]
Lampley - First of all, I must say Sunshine, that you look absolutely gorgeous! Sunshine - Aren't I always? That's nothing new Lampley, but that's not what we are here to talk about now is it Lampie Boy?
Lampley - That is true...., Ladies & Gentleman standing before me is
Sunshine & Ronnie Blades, two of the newest members of the WSW roster! I must say Ronnie had an impressive debut last week against the now seemingly forgotten J.D Money! My question to you guys is that Ronnie is more known as a Brawler but he looked quite technical in his match against J.D, has he been training?
Sunshine - Of course my baby has been training! Do you think we settle for mediocrity? We've seen the workouts and the skills of many of the
WSW wrestlers, and based on who we are fighting, Ronnie can change his style to suit! He can brawl with the best of them, get technical if needed and my baby is strong, so don't EVER under estimate what he brings to the table every time he steps foot in the WSW ring!
Lampley - Ok, but if you take a look at the video footage here, we'll see Ronnie executing a move that you have patented as the "Vibrator". It looks like a very painful move for the opponent, but does Ronnie have the strength to lift 300 pound guys, twirl them around and slam them to the mat each and every week?
[Sunshine pauses, then walks towards Ronnie and whisper something in his ear. Ronnie nods in approval.]
Sunshine - I see that you are not quite on the R.B bandwagon as yet huh
Lampie Boy? That's okay, we are more than willing to provide proof of the power of Ronnie Blades...
Lampley - How do you plan to go about doing that? Sunshine - It's pretty simple, all you fat slobs in the arena, I have a proposition for you! The fattest slob in this arena who is willing to allow Ronnie Blades to perform the Vibrator will get a kiss from..........ME...SUNSHINE! Not only that, but under this robe, guess what I'm wearing? That's right my pervs, ABSOLUTELY NOTHING!
[The crowd roars in approval and starts a chant HOLY SHIT...HOLY
SHIT..HOLY SHIT, that changes to STRIP....STRIP...STRIP...STRIP!]
Sunshine - Not so fast, only ONE person will get to see what lies under this robe, and that person is the fattest slob weighing at least 300 pounds that will allow Ronnie Blades to perform the Vibrator showcasing his awesome power! To add to this, before we peform anything you must give a verbal agreement which clearly says that WSW or Ronnie Blades will not be held liable for any injury that could take place by your participation in this event.
[Before Lampley can make it official a rush to the arena floor takes place with Security having to hold back some of the crowd as Sunshine picks out the fattest guy in the arena. Security leads the guy into the ring. He's wearing a dinged out Mutt T-Shirt, at the back of the shirt has the words "Come Get Some".]
Sunshine - What the heck are you wearing....nevermind...Do you agree not to hold WSW or Ronnie Blades accountable for any injury that may or may not occur with your participation?
Fat Guy - Yes. Sunshine - Are you also declaring that under no circumstances will you sue WSW or Ronnie Blades as a result of what takes place here tonight?
Fat Guy - I agree. Sunshine - Last, but not least, do you understand that your fat ass is going to get annihilated in a mere matter of 1...2...3 seconds?
[Sunshine shakes her head as Ronnie Blades steps forward! Ronnie swings the fat guy into the ring ropes, then backdrops him to the mat....]
Lampley - What the heck is going on here, he was to just execute the
Vibrator and that's it...
Sunshine - Shut up Lampley... [Ronnie Blades picks up the fat guy and gives him a Spine Buster to the mat, then climbs the top rope and hits his devastating leg drop! Ronnie
Blades picks up what's left of the fat guy and punches him repeatedly in the face busting him open as Security runs down the aisle to break it up! Ronnie fends off the security guards and picks up the fat guy and plants him with the Vibrator. Sunshine is seen applauding as Lampley is back to his announcing position with a shocked look on his face! The crowd is booing loudly!]
Sunshine - Let this be a lesson to all you pervs in this arena and watching all around the world on t.v! I am not a cheap slut, you actually think I would show my body to you people? Ronnie has also clearly shown how powerful he is, that guy weighed at least 350 pounds if not more, don't you ever, EVER under-estimate myself or Ronnie, or you will be on your way to the hospital just like this fat slob!
[Ronnie Blades is standing behind Sunshine and grabs the mic from her.] Ronnie Blades - This is all about business, this is all about making it to the top in WSW! I will not stop until I've accomplished my goal of being the WSW Heavyweight Champion! Seeing that T-shirt of that washed up has-been "The Mutt" on that fat slobs body, only gave me more motivation to whip his ass!
I'm looking to dominate, anihilate every WSW superstar that stands in my way! Let it be known that I'll be watching every match that takes place tonight, one match especially, that being The Bruiser's match! Why that match? That's for me to know and the rest of WSW to watch and find out!
These last words will be reknown soon, so listen attentively and memorize! The sun has just set on WSW...and the reflection is on.....ME!
[With that Ronnie Blades drops the mic..."Let's take some calls from the request line...caller #1" is heard again in the arena as Freek-A-Leek by
Petey Pablo plays as Sunshine & Ronnie Blades get out of the ring as the medics try to put the fat guy on the stretcher! Lampley makes his way back to the announce table.]
Lampley - What a disgusting display by Sunshine and Ronnie Blades here tonight, and now they're going after The Bruiser?
Gambler - Seems fair to me! Fans, don't try this at home, unless you want to get your butt kicked like that poor sap just did.
Lampley - Well, folks, coming up, this match was originally scheduled for the Power Hour this week, but now that we have a gap thanks to
Krim'nal, it's going to be Venom and Diablo, one-on-one, next here on
Saturday Night!
