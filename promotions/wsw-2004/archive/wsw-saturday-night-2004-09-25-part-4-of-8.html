WSW Saturday Night, September 25, 2004 (Part 4 of 8)

Sep 29, 2004, 12:00:00 AM
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Jen Babes - The following contest is set for one fall with a 20-minute time limit! Introducing first..
[A voice booms “Are you ready….Are you ready for the BIG TIME? Are you ready for PRIME TIME? Are you ready for The Bruiser”. "Enter Sandman" by
Metallica plays, causing the crowd to look toward the entrance..cheering.]
Jen Babes - From Los Angeles, California, weighing 310 pounds.. THE BRUISER! Gambler - Heeey... where's Ben? Lampley - What.. you were expecting a celebrity to show up here? Gambler - uhh.. yeah! Lampley - Maybe in your little dream world..last week.. you thought Tony
Blair was here.. this week.. uh.. forget it.
[The Bruiser comes down the aisle, playing to the crowd, and rolls into the ring. "I Stand Alone" by Godsmack plays, diverting the crowds attention to the entrance.. who immediately start booing.]
Jen Babes - And his opponent.. from Vancouver, British Columbia, weighing 285 pounds... BRIAN.......HILL!
[Hill stares out to the crowd, then starts walking toward the ring.] Gambler - It's him! The guy who made Reynolds wrap! Lampley - ...yeah. The guy who had no business even being at the show last week. [Hill gets in the ring and the bell rings.] Lampley - Hill immediately takes the offensive and throws Bruiser into the ropes... Bruiser rebounds off the ropes.. Hill picks him up...
POWERSLAM. Hill covers.. 1.... and Bruiser kicks out quickly. Hill back up and starts stomping on
Bruiser. Bruiser rolls away and get up.
[Hill whips Bruiser out of the ring then follows him out. Hill picks up
Bruiser and slams him into the announcer's table, then grabs a nearby folding chair.]
Lampley - He's holding the chair like it was a baseball bat. Gambler - Here's the windup.. Lampley - Bruiser starts rising from where he was resting over our table... [Hill swings the chair at Bruiser's head...] Gambler - And here comes the P-*THWACK* [Bruiser somehow ducks and the chair hits Gambler. Gambler is out cold. Bruiser takes advantage of the distraction and whips Hill into the ringpost. Bruiser picks up Hill and rolls him back into the ring.
Bruiser gets back into the ring but Hill is already up.] Lampley - Hill grabs Bruiser ... hits the Samoan Backbreaker and goes for the pin. 1... 2...... And Bruiser kicks out! Both men are up and taking punches at each other! [Gambler finally crawls back into his chair.] Gambler - Did someone get the license of that truck? Lampley - Not now Gambler.. Bruiser throws Hill into the ropes.. and hits the DDT.. Hill goes vertical before falling to the mat.. Bruiser with the cover..
1... 2...... And Hill gets the shoulder up at the last minute.. Bruiser is not happy.. He pick up Hill and slams him back down with a spinebuster. He covers again...
1... 2...... AND HILL KICKS OUT. Bruiser is beyond livid at this point... Bruiser goes to the corner and waits for Hill to get back up. Hill slowly gets up and stumbles a bit.. Brusiser charges Hill and hits Hill with the
Lights Out!
Gambler - We having an earthquake? Lampley - No. Bruiser covers and hooks the leg.. 1... 2...... 3!!!!! Jen Babes - Your winner, The Bruiser! Lampley - and The Bruiser gets a win over the guy that knocked out Jason
Reynolds last week!
[The screen cuts to the lockerroom. Jason Reynolds is sitting at a monitor watching as the Bruiser hit Hill with Lights Out! Reynolds stands up and slowly claps.]
Lampley - A big win for the Bruiser right here tonight and... wait a minute... what's she doing out here?
[Sunshine walks out to the entranceway wearing, well, barely anything! A pink bikini set in the strictest sense of the clothing word is covering her most sensitive bits, and Sunshine begins walking to the ring. The
Bruiser is yelling at her, wondering what she's doing there...]
Gambler - This view makes my head feel better already! Lampley - Ronnie Blades and this tramp had some harsh words for The
Bruiser earlier tonight, maybe Sunshine wants to deliver that message face to face...
[The crowd starts booing as someone is hopping the guardrail... someone who looks a lot like Ronnie Blades.]
Gambler - Get that fan out of here! Lampley - That's no fan, Gambler, maybe you should go get some aspirin... that's Ronnie Blades! Blades sliding in the ring behind The
Bruiser... Blades from behind with an elbow to the back of the head!
Blades signalling for the Vibrator.... WOW! He got The Bruiser up! And dropped him down! Gambler - This is great! Lampley - Not really, but I'm impressed with the raw strngth of Ronnie
Blades! Fans, we'll be right back with JW Wilde and Airimir one on one!
["Freek-A-Leek" by Petey Pablo plays as we fade to commercial.] ---- (C) 2004 WSW Productions - http://wswfed.com
