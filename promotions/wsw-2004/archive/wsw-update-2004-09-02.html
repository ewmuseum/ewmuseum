/--------------------\ | Saturday Night and | | Power Hour Cards | \--------------------/ 09/18/04 - WSW Saturday Night #1 - WSW Arena - Seattle, WA - Jason Reynolds has already made his determination known; he wants to be known as more than just the last Pacific Northwest Champion. This time, he has his eyes set on WSW Championship gold. But right off the bat, he's got a huge challenge ahead of him in the cocky, confident man that some call "Your One True Fantasy", Carl Blair. Can Jason Reynolds prove he's got the in-ring skills to match his determination? Can Carl
Blair and his high-flying style knock off the Sinister One?
- Diablo says he's out to clean up WSW of the 'freaks' of professional wrestling. Seems a bit hypocritical if you ask me, but the man who gets the first chance to prove his worth against Diablo is the returning Paul
Doom. At 6'9, 290 pounds, Paul Doom is a genetic freak, but we'll see if Doom has the skills to overcome the dangerous veteran in Diablo.
- Shima Takamura is a young Japanese import ready to take it to the mat in the United States. If you read the press clippings his agent, Aaron
Vick provides, Shima is the next superstar to be launched by WSW. All the hype has gotten the attention of one of WSW's most celebrated superstars, Krim'nal, who seems to have lost it a bit upstairs since the doors closed 2 years ago. The last North American Champion sees the WSW
Championship that eluded him in his sights, and Shima Takamura had better be as advertised if he wants to win his debut match.
- Two street tough individuals take to the ring looking for their piece of the WSW Championship as JD Money and Ronnie Blades clash in the squared circle! For those of you who like their wrestling full throttle, this match is gonna be an all out brawl for pride and status here in WSW. Ronnie Blades might have the advantage in the beautiful and devious Sunshine at ringside, but JD Money has experience between the WSW ropes, and a street-tough image to live up to.
- Former Interstate Champion Mr Ed will be in the WSW Arena as well, and you can be sure he wants to know what has happened to his former
Points of Authority co-hort, Krim'nal! Plus WSW Commissioner Byron
Ellis will be on hand to explain how the WSW Heavyweight and Interstate
Championships will be decided!
