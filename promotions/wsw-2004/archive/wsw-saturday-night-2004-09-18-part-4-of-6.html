WSW — Card

WSW Saturday Night, September 18, 2004 (Part 4 of 6)

Sep 19, 2004, 12:00:00 AM
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Jen Babes - The following contest is scheduled for one fall with a 20 minute time limit! ["The World Without Logos" by Ishii Yasushi plays over the PA as the lights dim and tint red.]
Jen Babes - From London, England, weighing in at 270 pounds, here is former WSW Pacific Northwest Champion.... DIABLO!!
[Diablo steps through the curtain wearing a long black trenchcoat, red mirrored spectacles, and his trademark smirk. He strides confidently to the ring at a slow pace, looking over at the fans every so often until he reaches the ring.]
Lampley - This certainly isn't the same Diablo we knew a couple of years ago. Gambler - I think that Diablo is somewhere writing poetry and mutilating cats. That guy was dark and brooding, this guy is just twisted.
Lampley - I can't argue with you there, except maybe for the cats thing. "ARE YOU UNFORGIVEN TOO?" ["The Unforgiven II" by Metallica plays over the PA, and the crowds cheers a little bit as Paul Doom steps through the curtain.]
Jen Babes - And his opponent, from Parts Unknown, weighing 290 pounds, here is PAUL... DOOM!
Lampley - Paul Doom has had some harsh words for Diablo over the past week, calling Diablo a freak...
Gambler - He is a freak, Lamps, just face it. Lampley - And why is that? Gambler - Just look at him, Lampley, it should be obvious. [Paul Doom climbs into the ring while Diablo takes off his jacket, revealing a one-piece jet black outfit. Diablo takes off the spectacles revealing blood red eyes. The bell sounds and both men circle each other slowly. Diablo flashes a smirk and locks up with Doom. Both men struggle for position, with Diablo backing Doom to the ropes. Diablo whips Doom off the ropes, and shoulderblocks Doom down. Diablo goes off the ropes himself, and Doom gets up and Shoulderblocks Diablo to the mat! Diablo gets up to his feet and smirks again.]
Lampley - These two monsters going at it, two of the biggest men in WSW, and neither one can keep the other down here in the early going!
[The two men lock up again, and this time Paul Doom forces Diablo into the corner. The referee asks for a clean break, but Diablo cheap-shots
Doom over the top of the ref. Doom staggers back and Diablo goes on the attack, firing off left hand strikes which back Doom into the side ropes again. Diablo whips Doom out one more time, and this time levels Doom eith a clothesline. Diablo drops a leg across Doom's throat and goes for a cover...
1.... 2...... and Doom gets the shoulder up. Diablo picks up Doom and cinches him in for a belly-to-belly suplex which connects. Diablo covers again....
1.... 2...... And Doom kicks out again. Diablo picks up Doom and whips him into the ropes, then bounces off the back ropes. Diablo goes for a shoulder tackle, but Doom sidesteps it, and Diablo runs himself face first into the top rope, staggering him! Doom comes up behind Diablo and takes him down with a reverse DDT! Doom covers...
1.... 2...... and Diablo kicks out!] Lampley - Paul Doom was a little slow getting out of the blocks, but he has the advantage now.
Gambler - I still don't like either of these guys. Paul Doom is a big ol' goody two-shoes, and Diablo is a freakshow on legs.
Lampley - What is it with your irrational hatred of people, Gambler? Did
Diablo kick your dog or something?
Gambler - I'm pretty sure he skinned and ate it. Lampley - All complaint letters can be sent to The Gambler in care of
WSW Studios... [Doom picks up Diablo and slowly turns him around, and drops him with a hangman neckbreaker! Doom poses for the crowd a little bit, and turns around... right into a standing Diablo, who slaps the taste out of Paul
Doom's mouth! Doom fires back with a series of rights, and the two warriors trade punches midring, Doom getting the upper hand. Doom backs
Diablo to the ropes and whips him across. Doom charges in and takes
Diablo down with a clothesline. Doom covers...
1.... 2...... and Diablo kicks out with force! Doom shakes his head for a second, then picks Diablo up and drops him with a sidewalk slam! Doom covers again...
1... 2...... and Diablo powers out!] Lampley - Paul Doom cannot figure out how to put Diablo away! Gambler - Here's a hint. Wooden stakes and holy water, Doom! Lampley - Paul Doom is signalling of the Doomsday now, maybe this will seal the match for him... Diablo blocks! He trips Doom up... ANKLELOCK!
Gambler - Oh man, Paul Doom has nowhere to go! Lampley - Diablo has it cinched in tight! Doom struggling over to the ropes, he's almost there!
Gambler - C'mon. grab those ropes! Lampley - Diablo dragging Doom away and he cinches down on the anklelock again! Doom is in serious trouble!
[The lights suddenly go out.] Gambler - I'm BLIND! AGAIN! AAAAAH! Lampley - The lights have gone out in this arena again! We have a match going on! ["Original Sin" by Ishii Yasushi begins to filter into the arena.] Gambler - What is that music? Lampley - Fans, we have no idea what's going on here...Diablo's still got that anklelock on, we can see that from camera flashes..
[About thirty seconds later as the music picks up, a spotlight shines down at the entrance..and a man, dressed in white, saunters out from the backstage area. He has an almost regal, holy aura about him. The crowd is reaching out to him, but he is ignoring them, simply walking toward the ringside area. The man has a crooked half-smile, not laughing, but a bemused look. From the camera flashes and spotlight, Diablo can be seen in the ring leaning against the ropes in surprise and confusion, staring at the man, who finally stops about ten feet from the ring. The music cuts off. By now, Paul Doom has pulled himself into the corner and is clutching his ankle. Diablo and the man are in a sort of staredown -
Diablo with a serious look, and the man simply holding his wrists down and smiling. He looks like the cat that ate the canary.]
Gambler - Who the HELL is that?! Lampley - I have no idea! The lights are back on... Diablo has his radar locked on this mystery man on the outside... Paul Doom is back to his feet, Diablo doesn't even notice him!
Gambler - I think this mystery guy welched on a bet or something with
Diablo... he looks bloodthirsty!
Lampley - He's still got someone to deal with in the ring! Paul Doom with a free shot from behind... right to the back of the skull! Diablo's dazed! Doom doubles him over with a kick to the midsection.... DOOMSDAY!
He got him up, bad leg and all! Gambler - The man outside has seen enoguh! He's out of here! Lampley - Doom covers! 1.... 2....... 3!!!! Paul Doom owes that man a victory, because without him distracting
Diablo, he would have never escaped that ankle lock!
Jen Babes - Your winner.... PAUL DOOM! Lampley - Diablo's already back to his feet, and he's headed to the back.. him and that man in white have some sort of history, and I understand we've got something in the back...
[The camera cuts to the back, where the mystery man in white is walking out of the building. A production woman is in his path. The man comes across the petite woman and looks down at her.]
Man - God bless you, child. [We fade to commercial as the man in white walks out the bck door of the arena.] --- (c) 2004 WSW Productions
