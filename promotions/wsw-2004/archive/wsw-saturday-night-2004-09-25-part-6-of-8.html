WSW Saturday Night, September 25, 2004 (Part 6 of 8)

Sep 29, 2004, 12:00:00 AM
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Lampley - Last week, on the Power Hour, we got some comments from Simon
Thurman, who has joined the WSW roster after being trained in the Steele
Dojo. Let's take you back.
[Sharply dressed Kylie Thompson stands in front of a large banner with the WSW logo firmly printed in the middle. She looks off camera as she prepares for her interviewee. Simon Thurman walks out dressed in his red, white and blue stripped singlet pumped up as he turns to the camera giving everyone out in TV land the “All-American” smile. Superimposed in the corner is the Power Hour logo and the words "LAST WEEK".]
Thompson - “Ladies and Gentlemen my guests at this time seems to be a rather enthusiastic and dare I say glowing newcomer Simon Thurman!”
Thurman - “Well Kylie, if you want to say I’m enthused or happy, go ahead, but I can’t say I’m glowing cause there’s no bun in this oven. I can tell you one thing though; I am thrilled to be here in the WSW after all the training and dedication I put in at the Steele Dojo.
Thompson - “That you did, but here you are on the WSW Power Hour set to wrestle a fellow Dojo Trainee Alexandre LeBoux.”
Thurman - “You know what, Alexandre has a lot more experience than me in a professional ring, but this is my chance to shine and make my way up to the main roster and compete with the best in the world. I’m use to competing with the best in high school and then in college. You can’t forget I was an All-American.”
[Simon moves about the interview area excited clapping his hands together and hopping around on one spot while stretching his neck before finally settling down once again.]
Thompson - “It certainly looks like you’re ready to get out there and compete here tonight.”
Thurman - “Let me tell you, I have been ready to compete my entire life, from day one I was putting on wrists locks while the other kids were busy strapping on their wrist watches. I’ve lived and breathed this sport and I’m more than ready to show these fans what I can do.”
Thompson - “Before you head out to the ring how do you size up the man from Paris France?” Thurman - “All I have to say to Mr. LeBroux is that yeah I’ve seen you backstage, in the dojo, you like to keep to yourself and give us younger guys a hard time. Heck I see you walking around with your nose stuck high in the air like the French snob you are. Buddy here in the good ol’ U-S of A it’s about sportsmanship and comraderie and that’s what I’m gonna show you when you step in that ring with “The All-American” Simon
Thurman!”
[Simon holds up one finger to the sky, blessing the lord for his opportunity as he chants out U-S-A leaving a big smile on the interviewer who watches the youngster makes his way to the ring area.]
Lampley - He got the win last week on Power Hour, but this week, he's got a real dangerous customer in Darkness Tiger.
Gambler - Yeah, this guy absolutely destroyed Shima Takamura last week after Takamura's lucky win over Krim'nal.
Lampley - It wasn't lucky, Gambler, Krim'nal gave up. Gambler - Luckilly. Jen Babes - The following contest is set for one fall, with a 20-minute time limit. [The crowd begins to cheer as "Simon Says" by Drain STH begins to play.] Jen Babes - Introducing first, making his WSW Saturday Night debut, from
Ames, Iowa, weighing 220 pounds... the first Steele Dojo graduate..
SIMON... THURMAN! [Simon Thurman runs down to the ring highfiving the fans as he goes, then climbs the ring steps and goes between the ropes into the ring.
Thurman raises both arms to the crowd, who begins cheering more for him!] Lampley - Simon Thurman, the first graduate and trainee of Donovan
Astros and Derrick Steele in the Steele Dojo, making his WSW Saturday
Night debut!
Gambler - Yeah, but this is the big time now, not that pa-and-pa shop! He better show something special to make it here! [The lights go out...] Gambler - WHOO! "WELCOME... TO.... THE DARRRRKSIIIIIDE!" Jen Babes - And his opponent, from the darkness, weighing 223 pounds.. DARKNESS... TIIIIIGERRRR! [A spotlight shines on the entranceway, as the black-masked Darkness
Tiger walks down the aisle, staring at the ring and Simon Thurman.
Thurman stretches on the ropes as Darkness Tiger raises his arms wide at the aisleway, drawing boos from the crowd before sliding into the ring and rolling to his feet, his eyes never leaving his opponent.]
Lampley - This is the man that blindsided the Japanese sensation Shima
Takamura last week on WSW Saturday Night! That's all we know about this man... that he's a cheap shot artist!
Gambler - Hey, he made an impact and he did it early, and that deserves props, Lampley! He's going for it all right off the bat!
[The bell rings and we're underway. Tiger and Thurman circle each other tentatively, until engaging in a collar and elbow tieup. Thurman grabs a quick side headlock, into a double leg takedown, and then leaping to a side headlock on the mat, earning some applause from the crowd. Tiger turns himself and cradles Thurman for two, which Thurman reverses back to the headlock. Tiger gets to his feet with Thurman and shoves him into the ropes into a shoulderblock, which takes Tiger down. Thurman goes off the ropes and over Tiger, but Tiger catches him with a roundhouse kick right to the mouth, taking Thurman down!]
Lampley - That was a huge move right off the bat by Darkness Tiger! Tiger off the ropes, and a sliding dropkick to Simon Thurman's head! A cover! 1.. 2.. Kickout by Thurman! Darkness Tiger to his feet, and he kicks Thurman right in the back! And again!
Gambler - He's focusing on that big noggin of Simon's! It'll take a lot more kicks than that to get through that thick skull!
[Darkness Tiger whips Thurman into the buckle, but Thurman reverses the whip. Thurman charges at Tiger in the corner, but gets two feet to the face! Tiger springs to the top rope...and leaps and catches Thurman in the Darkness DDT! Tiger covers nonchalantly...
1... 2... ...and Thurman kicks out just in time! Tiger gets back to his feet and waits for Thurman to get up..and catches him with another kick to the head! Thurman staggers into the corner..and Tiger rushes in with a
Yakuza Kick! As Thurman goes down to the canvas, Tiger springs to the top rope..and executes a senton bomb!]
Lampley - Darkness Tiger is just ALL OVER Simon Thurman here! What's that, he just looked to the entrance..
Gambler - Is somebody coming out? Lampley - No, just a glance by Tiger..he must be expecting an appearance by somebody, maybe Shima Takamura, who Tiger attacked like a rat last week!
Gambler - No, a rat doesn't completely take you down and out like Darkness Tiger did! HAHA! Lampley - Will you stop?! Darkness Tiger measures Thurman..and nails him with a legdrop! Now Tiger going up top, he's had great success so far......NO! THURMAN ROLLS OUT OF THE WAY OF THE DIVING HEADBUTT!
Gambler - Good thing he has that mask! He probably couldn't see how hard he hit the mat! [Thurman and Tiger are both on the mat, as Thurman begins pumping his arm slowly, and the crowd begins clapping along! The referee gets to
4... 5... 6... Darkness Tiger is up and measuring Thurman... Thurman gets up, and ducks the buzzsaw kick! Thurman waistlocks Tiger and executes a beautiful release German suplex! Thurman yells to the crowd and charges Tiger in the corner! He climbs to the second rope and begins punching down! One... two.. three.. four.. five.. Thurman picks up the pace, six, seveneightnineTEN! Thurman waits for Tiger to stagger out..and executes a side backbreaker!
1.. 2.. Kickout by Tiger! As Tiger gets up, Thurman gets behind him and hooks him up..and executes a kneebreaker!]
Lampley - Thurman has started going for the knee of Darkness Tiger..another kneebreaker and he's down! Gambler - He wants to lock in that STFF! Lampley - Thurman with a grip on that leg..and he snaps it over! Tiger is in serious pain, and he's going for it! STFF..YES, he's over!
Gambler - Get to the ropes, Tiger! Lampley - Tiger is in trouble here..but NO! He's in the ropes! Thurman breaks the hold at the ref's count!
Gambler - Tiger was able to get to those ropes right away, but he's limping a bit! [Tiger gets up to his feet with the ropes..and Thurman executes a double leg takedown! Thurman gets to his feet and grabs the bad leg of Darkness
Tiger..he turns him over for a half Boston crab, but Tiger twists himself and hooks Thurman's leg! Thurman releases the hold and elbowdrops Tiger in the back! Thurman picks up Tiger and forearms him repeatedly in the back of the head.]
Lampley - Thurman shoves Tiger into the buckle, and back out...Simon hooks Tiger, GERMAN SUPLEX, NO! Tiger flips and lands on his feet!
Gambler - But he came up lame! Lampley - Tiger limping from that landing! Simon after him, no, spinkick to the gut by Tiger!
Gambler - He's signaling for something! Lampley - He hooks him, Diamond Cutter, NO, DARKNESS TIGER FLIPS OVER HIM, ASAI DDT! ASAI DDT! Gambler - And D.T.'s pissed! He wants to finish this NOW! Lampley - Tiger stomping his bad leg, trying to get feeling back into it... Thurman groggy, up to one knee... TIGER CHARGES, SHINING WIZA--
THURMAN DUCKS, SCHOOLBOY BY SIMON! 1.. 2.. 3!! THURMAN GOT HIM! SIMON GOT HIM! Jen Babes - Here is your winner, Simon Thurman! Gambler - What the hell was that?! Lampley - Simon Thurman ducked the Shining Wizard and rolled up Darkness
Tiger for the win! The Steele Dojo graduate wins his first major WSW matchu-NO! C'MON! DARKNESS TIGER WITH AN ATTACK FROM BEHIND!
Gambler - That was a fast count, Tiger! Get him good! Lampley - Tiger throws Thurman into the ringpost shoulderfirst between the buckles! Thumb across the throat...he has Thurman up..AND DOWN,
DARKNESS DRIVER 2K4! COME ON, STOP THIS! Gambler - He's going to do it again! [The crowd begins to cheer loudly.] Lampley - IT'S SHIMA TAKAMURA! SHIMA TAKAMURA IS HERE! Gambler - DARKNESS, BEHIND YOU! Lampley - Shima is standing right behind Darkness Tiger..Tiger turns around, they're face to face, eye to eye!
Gambler - HIT HIM! EITHER OF YOU! Lampley - Tiger is SCREAMING at Shima, but Shima's not moving...SIMON THURMAN FROM BEHIND ON DARKNESS TIGER! Gambler - CHEAP SHOT! CHEAP SHOT! Lampley - TIGER DAZED...AND THURMAN CLOTHESLINES DARKNESS TIGER OVER THE TOP TO THE FLOOR! YEAH! Gambler - YEAH?! What kind of biased announcer are you?!
