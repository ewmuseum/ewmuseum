WSW — Card

WSW Saturday Night, September 18, 2004 (Part 2 of 6)

Sep 19, 2004, 12:00:00 AM
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Jen Babes - The following contest is set for one fall with a 20-minute time limit! Introducing first..
["What's Yo Fantasy?" by Ludacris plays, bringing the crowd up to look toward the entrance in wonder..]
Jen Babes - Being led to the ring by Vanessa Right, from Orlando,
Florida, weighing 235 pounds.. CARL... BLAIR!
[Vanessa Right leads Carl Blair down the aisle to boos from the crowd and some whistles for Vanessa. Blair rolls into the ring.]
Gambler - Wow, this is quite an honor! Lampley - Yes, Blair is a promising young star here in the WSW revival.. Gambler - I mean, having the British Prime Minister wrestling here on
WSW Wednesday Night! Lampley - ....uh, Gambler, that's CARL Blair. Not Tony Blair. Gambler - Huh? Geez, I had way too much to drink at the WSW social last night. Lampley - There wasn't any such thing! Gambler - Well, it wasn't so much a social as it was me and a six-pack of Coors. [Cue up "Debonaire" by Dope, as the bass line kicks in and the crowd looks toward the entrance again, cheering the announcement to be made..]
Jen Babes - And his opponent.. from Toronto, Ontario, Canada, weighing
245 pounds.. he is a former WSW Pacific Northwest champion... JASON...
REYNOLDS! [Jason Reynolds pops out of the entrance, slapping hands with a couple of fans on the way to the ring, staring at Blair the whole time.
Reynolds climbs the steps and gets into the ring, and is immediately attacked by Blair! The referee calls for the opening bell as Blair keeps the attack up. Blair begins pounding on Reynolds in the corner, but
Reynolds shoves him off easily. Reynolds misses a clothesline, and turns around into knife-edge chops by Blair. Blair goes up top..missile dropkick!]
Lampley - Blair has surprised Reynolds so far here! Blair with the cover! 1.. 2, kickout by Reynolds with authority! Reynolds back up, Carl Blair with a snap mare..off the ropes--AND REYNOLDS NAILS HIM WITH A CLOTHESLINE!
Out of nowhere! Gambler - He nearly 360ed the guy with that! [Reynolds shoves Blair into the corner and begins stomping a mudhole in him. The referee counts but Reynolds just waves him off and continues the assault, dragging him out of the corner and executing a picture perfect double underhook suplex. Reynolds gets up, bounces off the ropes and drops a forearm right across the nose, and covers for a quick two- count. Reynolds hooks up Blair..and drops him in a DDT! Reynolds gets back up and goes to the ropes..but Blair punches him in the gut!
In surprise, Blair tries a Northern Lights suplex, but Reynolds counters that into another DDT!]
Lampley - Reynolds now going to the top rope, this is odd for him...and
Blair kicks the top rope! Reynolds crotched, and down in the ring! And now Blair choking him in the corner!
Gambler - Blair's gonna make him pay for that! Lampley - Blair CONTINUING the choking, the referee breaks it up..and
Blair kicks at Reynolds! Blair now up to the top rope, Reynolds is dazed out of the corner..CROSS BODY--NO, POWERSLAM BY JASON REYNOLDS!
Gambler - He shook the ring with that powerslam! I'm surprised he didn't dent the ring! Lampley - He's signaling..he's going for a torture rack, he's got it! Gambler - No, Blair's wiggling..OH! Lampley - Reynolds just drops back and slams him down from the torture rack! A very smart move, Blair was going to get out otherwise! And now he's got him set up again, the crowd's on his feet...UP, SINISTER EDGE!
That's his move! Cover! 1.. 2.. 3! Jason Reynolds wins it! Jen Babes - Your winner, Jason Reynolds! Lampley - Jason Reynolds, the former Pacific Northwest champion with a decisive victory over a tough Carl Blair!
[As Reynolds breathes heavy and hang's over the ropes from awesome match, the lights in the arena go out.]
Gambler - Wait, what the heck? I'm BLIND! Lampley - The lights just went out! Gambler - Doesn't anybody care that I'm BLIND?! Lampley - You're not blind! [Red lights flash across the entrance way as "I Stand Alone" begins to blare through the speakers. A fan in the crowd can be herd yelling "It's him! He's here!" Out from the back steps Brian Hill. The fans roar as
Blair tears down the ramp and enters the ring. He takes a long look at
Reynolds and gives his trade mark cocky smile before raising his hand acting like he wants Reynolds to give him a high five. Reynolds shrugs his shoulders and raises his hand in a gesture of friendship to the newly signed Hill. As Reynolds goes to slap his hand, Hill pulls it away and levels Jason with a hard left. Hill stands over the WSW mainstay and begins to boot him.]
Lampley - What the heck is this?! Brian Hill's attacking Jason Reynolds! What is this about?! Gambler - I like this guy already, he knows how to make an impression! [Carl Blair notices what's going on and approaches Hill smiling and pointing at Hill as if Hill was on his side. Hill then sneers and boots
Blair in the gut. Blair doubles over and Hill puts him over his shoulder executing The Ratings Spike send Blair hard to the mat. Hill smiles and climbs the ropes calling for a mic.]
Hill - Now, all of you must be wondering 'why is the most predominant member of the AGW standing in a WSW ring?' The answer is simple my people, Brian Hill hates to watch an un-entertaining show and he knows his people also hate watching some boring dribble. So I, Brian Hill, have come to give all you fans something this fed is sorely lacking, a little entertainment.
Lampley - By beating down Jason Reynolds and Carl Blair?! What right does he have! Gambler - The one on his arm, and a left too, judging by what he just did to Reynolds! Hill - I mean look at this guy, (pointing to Blair). How can you possibly have a decent fed with flamers like this guy strutting around in the main event. Buddy, the only time you feel cocky is when you keep your hand in your pocket all day! And as for you (pointing at Reynolds)
why in the hell do you deserve the title tiny? You come out and please the fans with you high flying crap, no one wants to see that, NO ONE! So you want that shiny world title you're going to have to go through me.
Ladies and Gentlemen, Your Saviour, Your Entertainer and your One and
Only has come to the WSW, and he's here to stay.
[Hill drops the mic and climbs the turnbuckle throwing his arms in the air to a chorus of boo's and hisses.]
Lampley - Brian Hill has made an impact here tonight on WSW Saturday
Night! Jason Reynolds will not forget this night any time soon! We'll be right back with Ronnie Blades and JD Money in action!
