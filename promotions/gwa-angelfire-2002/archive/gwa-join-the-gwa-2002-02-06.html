Yes, thanks to Google Groups and the miracle of communication that is rec.sports.pro-wresting fantasy, the Global Wrestling Alliance (approximately the 87th E-fed to take that name, if anyone is keeping score)should be up and running soon. There will be fundamental differences between this fed and others, at least others I have been in. In the fed I am coming from, an all-tyrannical owner turned down all angles, booked no interesting matches, had at most two Pay-Per-Views a year, ignored handler requests for storylines, and fired everyone who argued with him or encouraged others to do likewise. Ignoring that totalitarianist, though, the fed was pretty fun ( www.nefwwrestling.com - I highly recommend it!). The Global Wrestling Alliance will be an e-fed working from the ground up, starting in a small arena and hoping to grow from there. I will run angles, and try to create storylines and feuds for every wrestler, so everyone always has a place. In the beginning, there will be only a World Title, but after awhile new titles will be introduced as competition picks up. There will be one card a week, split up between four writers so no one ever complains about being burnt out or having not enough time. By the way, I am still looking for a fourth writer if anyone is interested in writing two matches a week. Only dedicated roleplayers need apply, those that are willing to be creative, imaginative, and frequent in their flashing. After the NEFW, I'm out to establish an e-fed like we all love them to be; a place where you can create a wrestler, have fun, have a few laughs, enjoy some roleplaying competition, and never walk away bitter or feeling like you've been screwed. That's how E-feds ought to be, right? If interested, go to http://www.angelfire.com/wrestling3/gwa6/ and fill out the application there. Don't pay too much attention to the website, it's still under heavy construction, though I do greatly appreciate the work done on it so far and commend it's creator for helping me out. I look forward to seeing you all in the Global Wrestling Alliance!
