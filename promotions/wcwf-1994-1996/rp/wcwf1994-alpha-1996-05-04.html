[WCWF] Alpha...

4 May 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
WCWF Alpha ---------- "Lightning Kid v. Bret Hart....FIGHT!" My name is Sean Waltman, and those are the scraiest words I've heard in my life. Across the ring from me is a big, mean-looking bastard from the Northern Region. They call him the "Excellence of Execution" because he used to work in one of the Soultaker's "purification" camps a few months ago. I heard he killed 26 men with his bare hands, including his own brother. He lunges towards me, his once-white tights dyed bright pink from the blood of his victims. What a sicko this one is. I even heard he has this bizarre method of killing people where he crosses their legs over, then bends them back until they break, then keeps bending until their neck snaps like a twig. Even calls it the "sharpshooter." What kind of deranged mind would name the move they use to kill someone? "Hey, kid," he yells at me. "You don't stand a chance. You're in with the best there is, the best there was, and the best ther ever will be." What a blowhard. He's stalking me now, preparing to begin the barbaic street fight we're forced into as part of the Soultaker's big plans. He lunges forward now, striking out at my face with a pair of spiked knuckles. I instinctively throw up my hands to protect myself, and when he sees this he drops down and stabs me in the calf instead. It hurts like a bitch, but aside from all the blood there isn't much damage. At least I hope that to be the case. He steps back and grins at me, his eyes hidden by those creepy wraparound sunglasses. I leap up and slam a foot into his mouth. This catches him totally off-guard, and he falls to the ground, more stunned than injured. "Good, someone with spirit." He says, spitting out a tooth. The crowd is yelling furiously, bets rapidly changing hands in the stands. I, however, waste no time on small talk, springing at Hart with sudden, animal ferosity. I am reasonably sure this guy killed one of my family members somewhere in his career. I slam fist after fist into his mouth, until the blood gushes forth. Hart is pissed. He tosses me off. "Hey, you little shit!" He screams as teeth fly out like spittle. "Do you know who you're fucking with here?" He pulls a knife out of...somewhere. It was pretty quick, so I'm not sure where it came from exactly. Big one, though. He makes a quick pass with the blade, opening a mild gash on my arm, but not doing any serious damage. "There..." he babbles, "how's that? I'll carve up your dead body with this after I slit your throat with it!" He grins, which looks really sick, sans teeth and all. But he's just called his next spot. At least I hope he's that dumb. He is. He makes a swipe at my throat, and I quickly duck under it and grab his arm, then smash an elbow down on his tibia, breaking it in two. Or maybe three. He screams. I grab the knife and slit *his* throat. Oh, god. I've just killed another man. Hart is kind of helplessly clutching at his neck as the lifesblood pumps onto the floor. The Soultaker sits on his throne at the opposite side of the room, watching the proceedings like a robot. "Very good, pup." He drones. "Your first kill. One down, four to go." he takes a sip from a very over-blown gold-and-diamond goblet. "I'll kill you last!" I yell out, overcome by bloodlust. Bret *finally* finishes bleeding to death at my feet. Some people have no manners. As the body is dragged off by the attendants, I am left with a few minutes to reminisce on the circumstances that brought me here. It was only a few short years ago that Soultaker introduced himself to the world, waving his oh-so-precious urn around like a trophy. The urn seemed to make him infinitely powerful, yet the power was almost completely evil and negative, as though the urn was supposed to belong to someone else and was rebelling against him in its own way. It was more than anyone else could do, however. No one could oppose him. He entered the wrestling federation known as the WCWF, and began killing members or using his evil influence to subjugate their will. No one suspected anything, because it was wrestling, and everyone assumed it was just fake. But it wasn't. Soon small government figures began "disappearing," and later larger leaders. Soultaker went public shortly after, literally *daring* anyone to stop him. Man, did they try. My thoughts are interrupted as handlers lead a very wild-looking man into the fighting area with me. His whole body is covered with scars, of every size and shape, most self-inflicted. It's the wildman known as Sabu, who supposedly is just learning the fine art of using the toilet after being hunted down and captured from the Sudan just for these tournaments. They give something like could have been a dog biscuit, presumably as incentive, then release him on me, like a dog. "Lightning Kid v. Sabu...FIGHT!" I've heard rumors about this guy, sure, but he look even weirder in person. He starts pointing to the lights and mumbling something about the moon and salt, an odd combination to say the least. I decided to take advantage of his seeming distraction. I spring at him with a foot aimed at the mouth, but he catches my leg, out of nowhere, and flings me at the wall. Luckily my nose was there to break the fall. Yes, Pinky, this is a pain that will linger. I pull myself to my hands and knees, struggling for air. he punts me in the ribs like a football. I think a lung might have deflated, but I could be wrong, what with all the broken ribs influencing my judgment and all. I clutch at my torso, protecting my ribs, so he kicks me squarely in the face. Blood explodes everywhere. God, I didn't know I had that much in me. He picks me up and stands me up against the concrete wall, and shoots a leg out, nailing me in the shoulder with a crescent kick that shatters my shoulder bones. The pain is excrutiating. I wish for death, and he looks happy to oblige. "Finish him." Soultaker barks, tired of watching such a rout. The animalistic Sabu bows to his Master, then turns to finish me off. And in a funny twist of fate, I guess I was allergic to whatever Sabu dragged in with him from the Sudan, because I sneezed then. Blood and phlegm, but mostly blood, sprayed from my mouth and nailed him right in the eyes. He was blinded by it, so I did the only thing that came to mind. I grabbed both of his legs, lifted him in the air, and fell backwards as fast and hard as I could and smashed his head into the concrete like an overripe pumpkin. He seemed to still be breathing, so I broke his neck for good measure. This cold-blooded killing stuff seemed to be getting easier. Then I threw up. "Very innovative. Is the vomiting bit symbolic of anything in particular?" The Soultaker asked. I was going to say something witty like the action heroes always did, but the pain of lifting someone with a broken collarbone was so intense I just passed out right there. Anyway, with this chance to think back again, I'll continue. Soultaker challenged the US government to stop him if they could. And did they try. Armies, navies, you name it. They even hired psychics to try and counter his power. Nothing worked, and, even worse, Soultaker had 75% of the military under his thumb and the US was fucked. President Rush Limbaugh officially surrended July 4, 1995, and the Canadian Prime Minister officially followed two days later. He never paid much attention to Canada, though. It was just a formality in case ever did. But I guess ruling started to get to him, because his urn told him that some pale guy was destined to fight him for all eternity. That spooked him, so he... I am awakened to find myself on a medical table, with an odd-looking fellow in a white coat standing over me. "Good morning, Mr. Waltman. I'm Dr. Vincent B. Baats, and you're quite the sight." He giggled, which I found vaguely disturbing. "I can't feel my arm." "That's because it's not there anymore." Giggle. This guy definitely gave me the creeps. I looked to where my arm last was, more than a little apprehensively. There was this metal robotic arm where *my* arm should have been. "What the FUCK have you done to me?" I yelled. "Improved you, young man." He said, as though chiding an impertinant child. "Your arm was no longer fit for combat, so it was replaced by a better one." Giggle. Two flunkies picked me off the bed and escorted me back tot he arena for my next fight. Soultaker was still there, ever watching. "Feeling better, pup?" "FUCK YOU!" "My, aren't we the mouthy one? Have to fix that. But I'll cherish it anyway when I rip out your throat -- personally. Bring out my warrior." The doors slowly opened, and the backlight silhouetted the most magnificent physical specimen I'd every seen. He stepped out, face painted with blood and blood streaked in his hair. And he had more cybernetic attachments than a Daily Mixer. In short, he looked like one badass, heavy duty motherfucker. "Lightning Kid v. Jim Hellwig...FIGHT!" I didn't even see him move. He was just that fast. He slammed into me like a Mack truck. I literally flew across the arena, smashing my *other* shoulder into the wall. The fucker was a cyborg, no doubt about it. But then, any more shoulder operations and I'd be one too. I mean, I'd heard rumors about the Soultaker's "doctors" turning men into machines in an effort to build a better warrior, and in this case I guess they built the ulimate warrior. I grabbed one of the numerous indentations in the wall, and used it as a foothold, climbing the wall, and launching myself like a missile at him. He simply caught me by the throat. And snorted at me. "Teach him a lesson." The Soultaker growled. I could hear emotion creeping into his soulless voice. Hellwig held up his index finger, and something sharp and nasty looking emerged from it. I didn't like the looks of that. And he was slowly bringing it closer to my left eye, smiling all the way. Ah, shit, no... The cynical audience even cringed at my plight. I look a tiny bit of solace in seeing looks of pity and concern in their faces as this monster carved out my eye like a piece of poultry in cold blood. I, however, was now so immune to the mindless, senseless violence of this world that I simply shut out the hellacious, excrutiating pain. I simply prayed they hadn't replaced his genitalia and brought my knee up as hard as I could. If they did replace them, they worked the same as the old ones. He doubled over in pain, dropping me, and with a total lack of emotion I raised my high-tech cyberarm, with a few thousands pounds of force per square inch behind it, and smashed his skull in with one blow. "You should wear a helmet next time." I commented. Oh, god, what have I become? This was all beginning to get to me. At this point, I didn't really care if I lived or died or both, as long as I had the Soultaker's fucking head on a platter. "Contestant's eye will be improved while we take a break." Someone injected me with something, and I returned to the memories... Upon learning of his final fate from the urn, Soultaker began setting up elaborate "Mortal Combat" tournaments, in order to properly screen his potential adversary, adn to also find capable warriors for his army. In one of the first such tournaments, my brother Barry was drafted into service, and killed in the first round by one of Soultaker's champions. Apparently Barry had been distributing anti-Soultaker literature, and was seen as some sort of threat. So he was killed in a public forum. Then the rest of family was discretely killed in a more public forum. Except me. I got sent to the training camps to prepare for...this. I woke up again, not surprised to find I had a cybernetic eye. Baats was yammering something at me, but there seemed to be another voice speaking to me. Soultaker is good. I will serve the Soultaker. Yes, it all makes sense now... <no, this is wrong!> ...I've been fighting for the wrong side all along. He really is looking out for my best interests. <no! he's planting things in my mind!> I think I hear another voice, but it's not His so I ignore it. <baats put something in my brain! fight it!> I walk, determined to fight for Him, to the arena again. A tall redhaired man waits for me there. Soultaker, my Master... <he's not my master! FIGHT IT!> ...seems pleased to see me. "Lightning Kid v. Mark Callous...FIGHT!" This is apparently my Master's champion, and the one who killed Barry. That information is put in my brain from somewhere else, almost taunting me. He charges me but my new eye tracks his every motion, one by one, allowing me to lash out with my new arm as though in slow motion... <i'm just another machine now! just another machine!> ...and grab him by the jaw. He squirms a bit, but is helpless to fight the pressure I apply. With no emotion, I examine his face...his *pale* face... <this is the one! the one who can beat soultaker!> ...and toss him over to where Soultaker sits. I'm not playing anymore. "This pathetic fool is not a challenge for me. Fight me yourself." <yes! fight it! stand up to it!> My Mast..Soultaker seems very surprised by this sudden show of resistance from his servant. "Very well, pup. Prepare to die." He leaves the urn on the throne and comes for me personally. I feel sudden urgings. <i am not important. the other must get the urn.> I gace him down. His fist lashes out, faster than humanly possible. My face shatters, but I am no longer important. In fact, the blow dislogdges the implant and now I can think again. I back myself towards the far wall, trying to get him as far from the throne as possible. He falls for it, following me away from the urn. Mark climbs to his feet, unsure of what to do. Soultaker has forgotten about him. "The urn!" I yell. "Get the urn!" Not totally moving last words, I know. But even if they did ensure my death, they served their purpose. Soultaker picked me up and snapped my neck in two. But it was too late for him, even though I was done like dinner. Mark grabbed the urn. And a miraculous transformation took place. I seemed to be floating above the proceedings like a ghost. I saw Soultaker throw down my increasingly lifeless form like a doll, and turn his attention to what stood on his throne. "I am now the Undertaker." it said in a voice that resonated from beyond the pale. "It is my destiny to destroy you." I tried to call out for help, but my body wasn't responding anymore. I was floating higher above the scene, as my body shed a tear of joy -- or pain, who knows -- before I died. It is a far, far better thing I do... I forget who wrote that. Soultaker burned all the books quite a while ago. I think it's appropriate, though. Hey, the light...it's really bright...peaceful...good, I need a loooooong break... [end WCWF Alpha] First name Scott, last name Keith. Don't make me say it again. Don't take it personal, I'm just having a bad life.
