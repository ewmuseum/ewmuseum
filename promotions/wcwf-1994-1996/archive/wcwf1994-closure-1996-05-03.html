[WCWF] No, it's not back, but...

3 May 1996
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Afternoon, WCWF fans. Thought I'd try a little experiment in creative writing here. As those of you who've been following my rantings during the WCWF's run knew, I've been saying I'd write a sort of alternate-reality version of the wrestling world I created called WCWF Alpha.
Well, I finally wrote the damn thing, so here's how it'll play itself out: Basically this is the last WCWF stuff I'll ever do. Period. This is going to be the story of the big, final climatic battle between the Undertaker and Soultaker as they (sort of) battle over the fate of the 1-2-3 Kid. Those of who actually know what I'm talking about here, congrats. Those who don't, don't worry...it's not absolutely necessary to understand this part to follow the whole story.
The intro and first part (WCWF Alpha) will be posted now. Then, in the next few weeks, I'll do WCWF Gamma, Delta, and however many I feel
I need to, all building up to WCWF Omega, where EVERYTHING gets wrapped up. Then that'll be it for the federation, so don't ever ask again.
I'll be hanging around [name withheld]' DEWF, though, where my talents can be better put to use. ;)
Enjoy. First name Scott, last name Keith. Don't make me say it again. Don't take it personal, I'm just having a bad life. All bad jokes and/or sarcastic remarks above (c) 1996 LPK Enterprises. Everything is more profound when you're standing in a big field of stuff
