[WCWF] Halloween Havoc 1994

reposted 7 June 1995 (for an October 1994 card)
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Live from the Pontiac Silverdome, it's phase 1 of Halloween Havoc 94! In honor of the memory of late CWF president Wendi Richter, all participants (except for the Unholy Alliance) will be wearing black CWF armbands. RIP, Wendi.
The great Wakkorotti burps "America the Beautiful" to start us off as Gary Michael Capetta takes his place in the ring! "Detroit, let's get ready to rumble! Our opening contest is scheduled for one fall, and it is for the United States heavyweight championship!"
[The video screens light up with a mushroom cloud as Adam Bomb prepares to make his entrance.]
"Coming down the aisle, to be accompanied by his manager, the Grand Wizard, and also by Virgil, from Three Mile Island...weighing 292 1/2 pounds...the CWF United States heavyweight champion.....ADAM BOMB!"
Bomb climbs into the ring as the pyrotechnics go off around the ring. While that's going on, let's go backstage for this interview with the Soultaker... Mean Gene: Soultaker, this is undoubtably the biggest match of your career, as you face Adam Bomb for the US title! Any thoughts?
Soultaker: I have no need of thoughts, worm. I have the Master to my left, controlling nature itself, and the CWF World champion to my right, preparing to destory Lex Luger. I do not think -- I do. [Shoves Gene to the ground.] Let the end of the CWF commence tonight, my allies.
[The trio of Soultaker, DemoCrush and the mysterious Master step on the wrestling ring shaped golf cart that carries them to the ring.]
"And his opponent, and challenger...from the jungles of deepest, darkest Rwanda, weighing 349 pounds...to be accompanied by the Master, and the CWF World champion, Crush...the Soultaker!" The three arrive at ringside, and after the Master steps off the cart Soultaker drops to one knee and asks for his blessing before the match. As the Master blesses Soultaker, Virgil runs over and bangs their heads together to signal the start of the match. Crush clotheslines Virgil for his insolence as Soultaker rolls into the ring. Bomb immediately goes on the offensive, stomping away at 'Taker with a vengeance. He picks him up and slams him to the mat, hard, then follows up with a quick elbow. He poses for the fans, but Soultaker is already back on his feet. Bomb, a little unnerved at the quick recovery, tries a clothesline, but 'taker grabs his arm and falls on it, putting the champ in a Kunze Armbar (tm). Bomb is screaming in pain, and he barely makes it to the ropes to break the hold. Soultaker continues his assault on the arm, grinding his boot into Bomb's shoulderblade, then dragging Bomb into the center of the ring and dropping a high knee on his shoulder.
Now deciding to toy with Da Bomb, Soultaker steps back and allows Bomb to stand up. He offers Bomb a free shot at him, which Adam Bomb gladly accepts, by using his uninjured arm to attempt another ill-fated clothesline. This time, faster than the eye can follow, Soultaker grabs the arm and slams a forearm into it from below, nearly separating Bomb's shoulder in the process. Soultaker pulls Bomb over to the ropes, then steps over the ropes, taking Bomb's arm over the top rope. He then drops down, yanking hard on Bomb's arm and causing him to snap back.
Now quick as a cat, Soultaker scales the top rope and dives down with a boot to the newly injured shoulder of Adam Bomb. To add humiliation, he stands up Bomb and applies a double-chickenwing, then leans back as Bomb screams in pain. But just as Bomb is about to submit -- he lets him go.
Both of Bomb's arm are now useless, and Soultaker decides to take advantage of that, by boxing Adam Bomb's ears ruthlessly, then spitting in his face. But again Bomb fights back, and boots Soultaker in the gut. No effect, but it got the message across. Soultaker just stands there grinning, so Bomb boots him again. And again. Still no effect.
Finally Bomb takes a run off the ropes...and Soultaker drops down like a bolt of lightning and clips Bomb in the left knee. Bomb, not seeing the attack at all, drops like a sack of rocks and writes in pain, trying to grab his injured knee but not being able to use his arms properly due to the injuries sustained. Now Soultaker really goes to work, battering
Bomb's knee from one side of the ring to the other, until finally he puts a spinning toe-hold on Bomb. Bomb is now in some serious pain, but being a fighter, he does the only left that he can -- he kicks Soultaker in the head with his only useful limb -- his right leg. That is enough to break the hold, but now Soultaker is really mad. He stands up Bomb
(who is hopping on one leg), and hits him so hard with a right hand that Bomb is left clutching the ropes for support while his ears ring. But now Virgil decides to lend a little moral support, by slapping him repeatedly until that nuclear power starts flowing again. The Soultaker is having none of that, however, and drives a knee into Bomb's gut, then puts the clawhold on his face. However, Bomb grabs Soultaker by the throat in retaliation and chokeslams him to the mat. Now we get some damage. Bomb scales the top rope and clotheslines the recovering
Soultaker with the Neutron Bomb, putting him down for the count. Finally, he picks him up and powerbombs him. Then again. Then one more time, until finally he goes for the cover and gets 1.......kick out!
Adam Bomb is in shock, as he has fired every gun he had at Soultaker, and he has just kicked out. Completely in shock now, Bomb runs to the corner and springs out with a cross-bodyblock, but Soultaker grabs him by the waist in mid-air and falls backwards to the other corner, smashing Bomb's face viciously into the ringpost. His face seems to explode with blood as many of the fans at ringside scream in terror, but
Soultaker pays no attention, choosing instead to ram his knee into Bombs' back as he stands in the corner. Soultaker then picks up Bomb and goes to the next ringpost, and drops him facefirst again! Soultaker is no longer concerned with winning the match, but merely ending the life of Adam Bomb. Now Virgil runs into the ring, but meets a clawhold from Soultaker. The referee has had enough, and stops the match immediately to get medical attention for Bomb.
"Ladies and gentlemen, the referee's offical decision: He awards the match, as the result of a stoppage of match, to the NEW United States heavyweight champion....THE SOULTAKER!"
It would seem that that is enough for the Unholy Alliance, as Crush takes the US title from ringside and puts it around the waist of
Soultaker, and the evil trio head back to the dressing room to prepare for Crush's match with Luger.
"Our next match is a special challenge, scheduled for one fall." Now, with a pre-match interview backstage, here's Craig DeGeorge... Craig: Guys, I'm here with Jim Cornette, and wouldn't you believe, he claims to be injured. The "doctors" have locked his dressing room door so we can't get pictures, but I don't think it's too much of a stretch to guess that he's planning something here. Back to you.
[Back in the arena, "Sexy Boy" begins blaring over the loudspeakers] "Coming down the aisle, with his manager James J. Dillon...from San Antonio, Texas, weighing 235 pounds...'the Heartbreak Kid' Shawn Michaels!" Michaels and Dillon emerge from the dressing room, with Michaels sporting a snazzy new outfit, bought with the bonus money paid out by
Dillon. "And his opponent...from Louisville, Kentucky, weighing 148 pounds...James E Cornette!" No one comes out of the dressing room. "James E Cornette!" Now there's some movement, as Cornette fights his way through a team of doctors with a cast on his leg. He hobbles down to ringside and asks for the microphone.
"Hey you mutants out there, I know you've been anxiously awaiting my triumphant return to the wrestling ring, but I had a little accident on the way here. You see, I was driving a group of orphans to a Raffi concert, when a two ton cement truck sideswiped me, and sent me flying over the edge of the I-95 bridge into the river. I managed to pull each and every orphan out of the water and carry them to shore before the bus exploded, almost losing my life in the process, but suffering no serious injuries. Then it happened -- I was carrying a four year old orphan who was missing both his arms and legs across the street, when that *punk*
Michaels came flying down the street on his Harley and tried to run me down! He missed the first time, but he had a gunsight mounted on his windshield so after his second run he hit me. Luckily I was able to leap out of the way just in time to save the orphan, but I broke my leg in the process. [During the monologue, Michaels fails to notice who has made his way to ringside.] So Sid Vicious is taking my place. Bye!"
And as Cornette runs for his life from Michaels, Vicious rolls into the ring, preventing Michaels from taking off after Cornette. He hammers the Heartbreak Kid with two forearms to the back of the neck, then whips him off the ropes and backdrops him about 12 feet in the air, and over the top rope. Michaels lands headfirst on the STEEL railing outside, and goes for guidance from Dillon. He rolls back into the ring and hides in the ropes, but Vicious grabs him by the boots and pulls him out, then hooks his legs and slingshots him into the opposite corner.
Michaels hits his head on the ringpost and goes flying over the top rope again, this time landing in the lap of a patron in the first row. It would appear to be Pat Tanaka, obviously there to watch Michaels' back after the last Spotlight when Cornette's men attacked Tanaka for no reason. Tanaka offers Michaels a hand up, then helps him over the railing again and back into the ring. He then goes over to
Michaels' corner with Dillon, just in case Cornette tries anything funny. Vicious comes after Michaels again, but Shawn slips in a quick savate kick to the chops, stunning Vicious long enough for Michaels to scale the top rope and nail a fist to Vicious' head. One more savate kick to the head, and Vicious is staggering. Michaels catches him with a quick cradle for 1....2....kick out. But now Cornette emerges from the dressing room again...with the Barbarian! He is apparently
Michaels' replacement in the stable. Michaels comes off the ropes for something, but it doesn't matter because Barbarian hooks the leg and trips him up. Vicious slams a boot to Michaels' head, then drops a huge leg on his neck. Vicious scales the second rope, after some choice words to Tanaka, and is about to drop something completely useless, because Michaels lifts his feet and nails him in the forehead. He then bounces off the rope and hits him with a high knee. Tanaka cheers him on as he signals for the teardrop suplex. Barbarian, however, jumps up on the ring apron as Michaels prepares for the move, which gives
Cornette the opportunity to run into the ring and remove his "cast", then waffle Shawn with it in the gut. Cornette rolls out of the ring, and Vicious powerbombs the stunned Michaels as the ref turns around in time to count 1....2.....3.
"Here is your winner, Sid Vicious!" Tanaka, however, begins arguing the point with the referee. That gives Barbarian and Vicious the chance to, well, let's be frank here, beat the crap out of Michaels. Vicious holds him as Barbarian delivers a big boot to the face, then they whip him off the ropes and deliver a double-big boot. They then take turns powerbombing him in the center of the ring, while Tanaka goes after Cornette. Finally he sees what's going on in the ring, and rolls in to stop the violence. He starts by throwing salt in Michaels' face, then...hey, wait a minute! Tanaka is on Cornette's payroll too!
[Extended beating of Shawn Michaels by Tanaka, Vicious and Barbarian deleted for space reasons and good taste.]
So anyways the Usual Gang of Idiots (referees, officials, etc.) finally run out to save Michaels, and now he's really in bad shape. Facial damage, broken ribs, bruises, the whole nine yards. The officals escort him back to the dressing room for medical treatment as we go back to
Craig DeGeorge for this interview with Cornette... Craig: You are truly the sickest group of thugs I have ever the misfortune to interview in my life.
Cornette: Normally I'd respond to that, wimp, but I'm in too good of a mood tonight! We showed that pretty boy Michaels just what happens when you betray a Cornette! And Diesel, don't think we've forgotten about your worthless hide now that you've joined the Dangerous Alliance!
You'll get yours too! Tell 'em all about it, boys! Vicious: Craig, I am truly and deeply sorry for any damage I may have caused Mr. Michaels, and I hope the fans can find it in their hearts to forgive me.
Craig: You're full of it. Vicious: Yeah, I know. But it's still fun to kick your blond ass, Shawn! Bet you won't be looking in no mirrors now, pretty boy! Hope you enjoy the food at Detroit General Hospital! I hear it's 'specially tasty through a straw! Hahahahahahah!
[Barbarian growls his agreement] Tanaka: You thought I was your *friend*, Shawn? That I cared whether you got your ass kicked from here to England? Unlike you, traitor, I know just how good an offer Mr. Cornette here was making, and I took him up on it! You made the mistake of your *life* and that's what it's gonna cost you, one way or another!
Craig: Back to ringside. Get me outta here. "Our next match is a special tag team attraction, scheduled for one fall." Edgar Winter's "Frankenstein" begins blasting as the Heavenly Bodies and the Young Stallion's ex-girlfriends emerge. The girlfriends appear to have a glazed look in their eyes, as if under someone else's control.
"Coming down the aisle, with their manager James J. Dillon....at a total combined weight of 487 pounds...Gigolo Jimmy Del Rey and the Doctor of
Desire Tom Pritchard...the Heavenly Bodies!" The crowd of course boos the duo as their new girlfriends make goo-goo eyes at them. "And their opponents..." Jimmy Hart's "Crank it Up" replaces "Frankenstein" "Being led towards the ring by Rene Goulet, from New York City, New York, weighing a total combined weight of 500 pounds...Pretty Paul Roma and Jim Powers...the Young Stallions!"
Roma takes the initiative immediately, charging into the ring and clotheslining both Bodies, then dropkicking them in succession. The girlfriends merely turn up their noses and depart back to the dressing room, however, unimpressed with the display. Powers climbs to the top rope very quickly and leaps off with a fist to the head of Del Ray, then sits on him and pounds away at the side of his head with closed fists.
Roma gives Pritchard a flying head scissor that sends him over the top rope and to the floor, where he stops to confer with Dillon. Powers then whips Roma off the ropes and hiptosses him onto Del Ray, for
1....2.....kick out. Roma then gives Powers a boost onto his shoulders, where Powers moonsaults onto Del Ray for another 1.....2....kick out.
Powers then climbs through the second rope and waits on the apron, as Roma whips Del Ray off the ropes and picks him up for a stun gun. But as he falls back, Powers slingshots himself back into the ring and clotheslines Del Ray, to the great delight of the crowd. Again,
1.....2.....kick out. They attempt the same move again, except this time after Powers nails the clothesline Roma holds onto the legs and turns Del Ray over into a Boston Crab. Pritchard, now recovered, rolls back into the ring and breaks it up with El Thumb to El Eye (an old
Mexican wrestling maneuver). As Roma covers his eye in pain, Pritchard climbs the top rope and hits an axehandle off the top rope, putting Roma down on the mat. But Powers, like a cat, climbs the top rope and leaps off with a flying bodypress, for would be a three count if the referee thought he was the legal man. But he was more inclined to think Roma was, so he ushers Powers out of the ring, which gives the Bodies a chance to do their dirty work. The first five minutes are all Bodies, as they assault Roma with a variety of moves. At the end of their assault, Roma makes a try for a tag, but Pritchard drags him back to the center of the ring and puts a Boston Crab on him, then tags Del Ray with his head. Del Ray quickly climbs the top rope and moonsaults Roma, who is still in the Crab. I think we'll call this the Crab Nebula, shall we? It fits the Heavenly Bodies more than the Quebecers. At any rate, things are looking pretty bad for the Stallions now...but there is hope.
Because Alundra Blayze and Rockin' Robin were at ringside, and while they were hoping to keep the announcement secret, now seemed like a good time to reveal themselves. For you see, after the Stallions' old girlfriends dumped them, they found new ones in the form of these two lovely ladies. And these two ladies are two you don't want to mess with in a fight. Blayze and Robin jump over the railing and begin screaming encouragement to their men, much to the chagrin of Del Ray and
Pritchard. While Pritchard maintains the Crab on Roma, Del Ray wanders over to the corner and shakes his goods at the women. Blayze responds in a very lady-like manner -- she spits in his face. Now enraged, Del
Ray sticks his head through the ropes and screams obscenities at the women...and that was mistake #1. Blayze, faster than the eye can follow, lashes out with a judo kick to the side of his head, then a quick chop to the throat. Mistake #2 was clutching his throat and blindly staggering backwards, inadvertently knocking his partner off
Roma. Not wanting to blow it, Roma immediately rolls to his corner and tags Powers, who springs over the top rope and dropkicks Pritchard over the top, leaving the weakened Del Ray in the ring. He whips him off the ropes and powerslams him, then whips him off again and gives him a jarring clothesline. Then he tags Roma in again, and sets him on the top rope for a Superplex. Roma climbs the top rope for the splash portion of the move as Powers hits a beautiful superplex, but Dillon smacks him in the back with his shoe, knocking Roma off before he can complete the move. Robin immediately chases Dillon around the ring, as
Powers, not realizing the move wasn't completed, leaves Del Ray lying in the center of the ring and goes to find Pritchard. Del Ray, now totally confused, stands up to get his bearings...but that's mistake #3. Roma has recovered and now comes flying off the top rope with a bodypress for
1.....2.....but Del Ray reverses it for 1........2......but Roma reverses again for 1........2......but Del Ray reverses again for
1......2.......3....but now they've gone too far and are tangled in the ropes. Del Ray thinks he's won and begins celebrating, so Roma schoolboys him for 1.....2....but Pritchard jumps in and smashes an axehandle blow onto Roma's back, breaking the count. Dillon decides to even up the score (after escaping Rockin Robin) and he throws his shoe into the ring, which Del Ray deftly catches. Pritchard hooks Roma's arms to hold him still as Del Ray reaches back with the shoe....and has his arm caught by Powers. Powers kicks him in the gut, sending the shoe flying into the crowd. With Del Ray doubled over, Powers sunset flips him...but Powers isn't the legal man. Roma is. So Powers releases Del
Ray and gets escorted out by the referee as the Bodies double team Roma again. But now Roma fights back, staggering the Bodies with lefts and rights, slamming each in turn and now rolling over and making another tag to Powers. He dropkicks each of the Bodies, then whips Del Ray into the stunned Pritchard, knocking both down. He then scales the top rope and leaps with a splash onto the pair, who are now in total chaos. Del
Ray staggers to his feet, and Powers clotheslines him. Pritchard carefully navigates his way to his feet, and Powers clotheslines him too. Finally, Powers throws Del Ray out of the ring and piledrives
Pritchard, then makes a cut the throat gesture. The Stallions are about to debut their new finishing move. Roma climbs to the top rope, as does
Powers. Roma sits down on the top rope, and Powers sits on his shoulders. Then Roma stands on the top rope, with Powers on his shoulders...and Powers stands up too! Powers is now 15 feet in the air, and he dives off of Roma's shoulders and hits Pritchard with an earth-shaking splash. The crowd is in shock and applauds wildly as
Powers covers for 1......2.......but the bell rings. The crowd groans with disbelief. "Ladies and gentlemen...the time limit has expired. This match is a draw." The crowd cheers as the Stallions and their new found lady friends make their way back to the dressing room with Goulet, just one second short of their first big win in the CWF.
"And now -- the retirement match!" Also Sprach Zarathrustra fills the arena. "If Ric Flair should lose this match by any means, he will no longer be allowed to wrestle in the CWF ever! Coming down the aisle, from
Charlotte, North Carolina, weighing 245 pounds..........Nature Boy, Ric Flair!" Flair emerges from the dressing room, alone. Sherri is in England along with the Enforcers, and both Pillman and rumored new member Hulk Hogan are in Tokyo. Win or lose tonight, Flair does it on his own with no outside help. Flair soaks up the mixture of boos and applause as he faces what could be his last match.
"And his opponent, being led towards the ring area by Fifi...from Pittsburgh PA, weighing in at 225 pounds...The Franchise, Shane Douglas!" The crowd begins chanting "Flair is Dead" as Douglas jumps on the apron and simply points one finger at Flair, then makes the throat cutting gesture. The crowd goes nuts. Flair simply gives a big "Whoo" in response, as Douglas charges into the ring. The two exchange trash talk back and forth, until Douglas takes the offensive by shoving Flair to the mat, much to Fifi's delight. Flair bounces up and shoves him back onto the ropes, where Douglas rebounds and nails Flair with a forearm shot to the head. Flair crumples and Douglas drops an elbow for the first two-count of the match, but Douglas knows deep down inside that it's gonna take a nuclear explosion to put Flair down for good (no Adam
Bomb jokes, please.) He clamps on a side headlock to maintain the pressure, but Flair rolls him over onto his back for two. Douglas stands up, maintaining the headlock, and begins pounding quick punches to Flair's head. Flair tosses him off the ropes to break the hold, but
Douglas responds with a dropkick. Flair tries to roll out but Fifi blocks his path, and Douglas dives into Flair with an elbow to the back.
Douglas rolls him into the center of the ring and methodically drops a knee on his head, then picks him up and slams him. Another knee, then another elbowdrop. Cover for two, but again Flair will not be beaten so easily tonight. Douglas whips Flair off the ropes, and goes for the belly-to-belly to finish him off fast, but Flair sees it coming and drops down with a clip to Douglas' knee. Douglas crumples in apparent agonizing pain, and Flair is delighted. He grabs the knee and prepares for the figure-four, but he only gets to "Now we go to..." before
Douglas reveals he was faking and rolls Flair into an inside cradle for another two count. Douglas jumps up and does a few jumping jacks to emphasize the point. Flair gets really pissed now and makes a blind charge at Douglas, but now Douglas surprises Flair by military pressing him above his head, then dumping him outside on the concrete floor!
Flair lands on his knee, and Fifi, concern in her face, rushes over to lend a hand. Seeing he's still alive, Fifi lends her left hand...and slaps him in the face. She picks him up and throws him back in the ring so Douglas can continue the beating. Flair, however, is now seriously hobbled. Douglas takes immediate advantage, kicking Flair's knee relentlessly over and over. Douglas puts a legbar on Flair to further intensify the pain, then releases it and puts a grapevine on Flair instead. Flair is screaming in agony now, but he won't submit his career away just yet. Now, however, Douglas drags him into the center of the ring...and calls for the figure-four! However, as he starts to put in on Flair, the Dirtiest Player in the Game pulls one last trick out of his bag and just as Douglas is at his most vulnerable, Flair kicks him in the back of the head and cradles him with a Japanese variation of the small package that would put any other man on any other day in the losing column. However, this was not any other man. This was The Franchise. And the Franchise was in a career ending type of mood, so he had the audacity to kick out at two and seven eighths. He simply stood up with smugness so thick it nearly choked out the front row, and waggled a finger patronizingly at Flair. Flair, at that moment, gave up. Not literally, mind you, but in his heart he now knew that this was his last match. Douglas whips Flair off the ropes and hits the belly-to-belly for 1......2......but Douglas picks him up.
With a smug look on his face, he drags Flair to the center of the ring and slaps the figure-four on him, this time securing the hold without incident. And after about 3 minutes of enduring the hold, Flair is about ready to give up...until Mr. Perfect emerges from the dressing room, that is. He takes up residence at ringside, and begins screaming at Flair.
"Come on! If you're such a legend, why are you letting this smart-ass punk kid kick your ass? Get up and prove you're still a man! I know
Space Mountain is just empty bravado, but you must have *something* down there! Of course, Fifi knows you don't!"
That does it. Flair suddenly sits up with new conviction and decides to channel his energy towards winning the match out of spite to Perfect, rather than to beat Douglas. And he hates Perfect much more than
Douglas. With vigor so overwhelming that even Shane looks shellshocked, Flair pulls himself to the ropes and ties himself up in them, causing a break of the figure-four. Perfect reaches up and begins slapping Flair, insulting his manhood even further, until finally Flair, forgetting all about his knee injury, leaps up and uncorks a right hand so hard that
Douglas goes flying across the ring. Douglas staggers to his feet, but Flair is a madman. He chops viciously at Douglas' chest, drawing blood. He rams Douglas' head into the turnbuckle 15 times, then gives him a European uppercut followed by another chop. A quick snapmare, then the running knee to the forehead. With Douglas down, Flair extends a hand of friendship to Perfect for helping him out, and Perfect shakes it.
The war is over as Perfect's respect for Flair wins out over his bitterness about lack of title shots. Back to business now, Flair grabs the younger challenger by the hair and slaps his face. Then backhands him one to teach him respect. Then, in his biggest moment of triumph, grabs his left leg and puts the figure-four on him. Fifi is immediately stunned, and jumps up on the ring apron to try to buy her man some time.
Shane is apparently in great pain, but the referee isn't there to count Shane's submission. Finally Flair releases the hold and decides to venture to the top rope for a bodypress. But as he gets to the top rope, Perfect hooks his leg and drops Flair on the top rope, um, you know where. So much for that respect crap. Yep, it was all another
Perfect ploy to destroy Flair. And this one worked. Flair drops to the mat in pain, suddenly feeling the effects of the knee injury as well, and now Douglas is back up on his feet. He taunts Flair to stand up again, and as Flair tries to shield both his groin and knee at the same time, Douglas simply shakes his head in pity at the aging and broken down ex-champion, then executes a perfect belly-to-belly suplex. He covers for 1......2.....but Flair will not yield. He picks up Flair and gives him a shoulderbreaker for 1......2.....still not enough. He climbs the second rope and drops a knee on Flair's throat for
1......2.....but not quite there yet. He picks up Flair and piledrives him, but again no dice. Finally, Perfect decides to take matters into his own hands. He yells instructions at Fifi, who jumps onto the apron to distract the referee. Perfect grabs a chair and asks Douglas to hold
Flair. With all the frustration of years of living in the shadow of Flair, and being second to the Man, and being passed over for title shots in favor of Flair, he lets loose with a home run swing with the chair right at Flair's head. And he connects. The steel chair breaks over Flair's head, and Flair is now done like dinner. Perfect takes the evidence with him out of the ring, and for the final humiliation Douglas puts the figure-four on the greatest wrestler ever to step into the ring. The crowd is deathly silent as the referee begins the count.
Many images flash through Flair's mind. 1...............and Flair thinks back on his first ever CWF World title reign, beating Bret
Hart.........2.........Flair recalls the formation of the Four Horsemen, the defection of Mr. Perfect, winning another title after dubious tactics by the Horsemen...........and as the ref's hand seems to approach the mat in slow motion, not a single person in the whole arena dares breathe for fear of missing one of the single most historic moments in the history of the CWF..............the air is silent, the crowd is silent, time is standing still for one single moment; the moment that separates what was and what will be for the Nature
Boy....and the silence is shattered not by a scream of triumph by Douglas, or the release of frustration by Perfect, or the throes of ecstasy by the 95,000 people jam packed into the Silverdome, or even the cough of someone daring to disturb the moment....but rather by much less dramatic thump of the referee's hand hitting the mat for the third and final time in Flair's CWF career. And the crowd remained silent, shocked by what they had just witnessed. Douglas quietly released the hold, unbelieving of the magnitude of the deed he had just perpetrated.
Not just something so petty and selfish as winning a title. But rather ending a man's career, and indeed his livelihood as he sees it. Perfect is so shocked that he finally got the last laugh that even he cannot fathom it. Then one voice, that of Dave Scherer, rises out of the crowd.
"Flair is dead!" And as if opening a flood gate of emotion, Douglas begins to celebrate. Fifi jumps into the ring to congratulate him. The crowd cheers and chants and cheers and chants some more, now that their wish has finally come true. Perfect offers Douglas no congratulations, given the fact that his motives were purely selfish, but merely nods in his direction, as if to say "This could be you." Douglas nods back, thinking the exact same thing. And Flair merely lays there. Until this point, he identified himself as simply The Man. The Champ. He defined himself as a wrestler and vice versa. Now he is no longer a wrestler. He merely counts the ring lights one by one after recovering from the blow to the head. He counts 28. He sees Douglas and Fifi triumphantly returning to the dressing room, he feels the fans jubilantly celebrating his demise, and he hears the words that are doomed to echo through his ears from this moment and forever...
"Here is your winner...Shane Douglas!" Referee Tommy Young, still filled with respect for the greatness still filling Flair, simply looks down and asks "Are you alright, champ?"
