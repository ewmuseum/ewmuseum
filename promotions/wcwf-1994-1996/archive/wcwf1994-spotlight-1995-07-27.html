[WCWF] Spotlight for end of July, 1995

27 July 1995
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
WCWF Spotlight for sometime around the end of July, 1995... [We open in the offices of the Four Horsemen, as their secretary, Elizabeth, answers phone calls.] Liz: Hello, Horsemen Central...please hold...no, we do not sponsor benefits...no, we are not the Four Horsemen of the Apocolypse...please hold...oh, hello Mr. Douglas. I'm not sure Ric wants to talk to you, but I'll put you through.
[We cut to Flair's office, where a huge rear-projection TV screen displays porno videos while Flair cuddles with 5 very young ladies.
Elizabeth's image interrupts the movies.] Liz: Excuse me, Ric, but Shane Douglas is on Line 1 for you. Ric: Damn his timing! Okay, put him through. [Shane Douglas, in a business suit with the WCWF World title belt on the desk, appears on the screen.]
Ric: Shane, I see that the Takamatsu Corporation has visual phones installed as well. Shane: Cut the crap, Flair. The Takamatsu Corporation wants the Four
Horsemen name and the legal status that goes with it, and we're willing to pay any price.
[Flair seems a bit stunned.] Flair: Look, pal, I already beat Ted Dibiase's little band of "Four
Horsemen" last year at Starrcade, and to tell the truth I'm getting a bit sick of it. Ric Flair, Lex Luger, Sting and Brian Pillman are the
Four Horsemen now and forever, and there's not much you can say to convince me otherwise.
Shane: Okay, how about we settle this once and for all then...men to men? Flair: What the hell's that supposed to mean? Shane: You bring your Horsemen, I'll bring the Takamatsu Corporation. We wrestle a round-robin tournament. Whoever has the most points wins, and gets the Four Horsemen name. And if you don't win the round robin part, I'll even give you another shot to keep your name -- you and me in
an "I Quit" match for the WCWF World title. But if you blow that shot -- you retire, and so do the members of your Horsemen. Forever. No more legal loopholes, and I get the Four Horsemen name. Forever. But then again, if you win, you not only get the most prestigious title ever created -- namely my World title -- you also get majority ownership of the Takamatsu Corporation. And that would make you my boss.
[Flair's eyes light up.] Flair: So who's your little group of losers gonna be? Shane: Ah ah ah...couldn't give away my little surprises before Summerslam, now, could I? That's what you get in exchange for a one-on-one shot at me -- you won't know your opponents until next week at In Your Mailbox II. Deal?
Flair: Where do I sign, big man? [A fax machine on the bottom of the screen spits out a contract.] Shane: Right on the dotted line, old man. [Ric Flair signs on the dotted line, and In Your Mailbox II for next week is set.] [We cut to the Bodydonna health resort, where JJ Dillon escorts Shawn
Michaels in.] Shawn: This place blows. [Sunny hippity-hops out of her office, clipboard and whistle in hand, to show Shawn around.]
Sunny: Hi! I'm Sunny, your guide to health, fun and fitness for the next two weeks! Shawn: Up yours. Sunny: Oh. I see we'll have to work on your attitude... Shawn: And I can name two or three things I'd like to work with on you... [JJ smacks him in the back of the head.] JJ: Behave yourself while you're here. [Shawn gives Dillon a Nazi salute and goose-steps into Sunny's office.] JJ: [To Sunny] I don't want to see him again until he's in fighting shape! Sunny: Oh, I can *guarantee* that! [Sunny hippity-hops back into her office.] JJ: What a nuthouse... US tag title match: Barry "1-2-3 Kid" Horowitz & Sean "Lightning" Waltman v. The Razor Boys: Paul Diamond & Al Perez w/ Razor Ramon. Because of Jackie Fargo's edict that any two of the three Razor Boys could defend the US tag title, the Razors choose to go with Paul Diamond and Razor Ramon, with Perez acting as the cornerman. Barry Horowitz starts out with Da Bad Guy, offering a goofy grin and a handshake. Sean
Waltman groans with embarrassment at ever having acted like that. They lock up, and Ramon immediately slams Horowitz, only to have it reversed into an inside cradle very quickly for 1...2....kick out. Ramon is stunned, and Horowitz follows with a quick rolling cradle for
1....2....kick out. Barry whips Ramon off the ropes and nails a leg lariat, then tags in Waltman, who grabs the top rope and slingshots in onto Ramon, with a legdrop. Ramon drags himself to his feet as Waltman goes to the second rope and comes with something, but it doesn't matter because Ramon catches him and reverses it into a blockbuster slam.
Ramon drops three quick elbows, then tags in Diamond. Diamond uses his brawling skills to the fullest, keeping Waltman in the heel corner as the Razors hammer the challengers for the next five minutes. At around the 7 minute mark, Waltman is tossed over the top rope, where Perez breaks a chair over his head -- literally. The thing breaks in two.
Horowitz rushes over to help, but gets the same. Barry, however, collapses to the ground and doesn't move. Perez tosses Waltman into the ring, where Ramon picks him up and sets up for the Razor's Edge. Sean struggles in the hold to get to the corner, however, and braces his feet on the top rope, trying to push off. However, Perez reaches up and un-braces his legs, and Ramon hits his finishing move for
1......2......picks him up! Horowitz is now struggling to his feet, and seems different. He sees the 1-2-3 Kid tights and screams in horror, trying to rip them off. The Razors think he's a little weird, but don't seem to care. Ramon whips Waltman off the ropes, and Diamond jumps onto the top rope and comes off with a spinning leg lariat. He covers for
1....2...but Horowitz rolls in and breaks up the pin. Ramon and Diamond try to double-team, but the ref escorts them back to their corner.
However, while that is going on, Horowitz stands up Waltman...and gives him a superkick right over the top rope! Diamond doesn't see this, so goes after Horowitz again. He gives him two quick right hands, but
Horowitz fires back with a big boot to the gut. Diamond doubles over, and Barry nails a DDT. Waltman, stunned, climbs back up to the ring apron, and Horowitz picks up Diamond and whips him viciously right at
Waltman, slamming their heads together and sending Sean flying right into the front row. Diamond is about to collapse, but Barry hooks the
Winnerplex and nails it for 1.......2.......3!!!!!!
"The winners....and NEEEWWWWWWW UNITED STATES TAG CHAMPS...LIGHTNING WALTMAN AND THE 1-2-3 KID!" Horowitz angrily snatches the microphone away from Finkel. "I don't know where you got the STUPID idea that I'm the 1-2-3 Kid, because I'm sure as hell NOT that pimply faced little puke! Last thing
I remember, I'm on KooeyKooeyKooey hanging from a friggin' PALM TREE, and now I'm back in the ring with THESE friggin' tights on, and now you're telling me that I'm one half of the US tag champs?"
The referee diminutively hands Horowitz a tag belt, and goes to give the other to Waltman, but Barry grabs it from him.
"I'm the US tag champs, jerk! If he wants his half of the belts, he can come and get it!" Horowitz storms off, back to the dressing room, slapping fans the whole way... [We cut to Las Vegas, where Paul E. Dangerously stands outside the Hilton Hotel & Casino] Paul E: I can't believe they'd sink this low for money...how could they betray me like this...
[He walks into the lounge, where god-awful disco music is blaring in the background. On stage...well, it's too horrible to describe.]
Paul E: Guys, what are you doing? The WCWF is back -- you don't have to degrade yourselves like this! Come back to the Dangerous Alliance again and we'll be back where we were before!
[The camera suddenly cuts to the stage, where Stan Hansen and Big Van
Vader are dressed in sequined, skin-tight jumpsuits, while a karaoke machine blares "Hot Stuff"]
Vader: Aw, Paul E, we needed the money! Paul E: But there's lots of perfectly respectable ways to earn money...extortion, murder, prostitution....anything but DISCO KARAOKE!
Hansen: Feel this silk! It's very soft on my skin... Paul E: SHUT UP AND GET DOWN FROM THERE! [Vader and Hansen hang their heads and come down.] Vader: Okay, but we wanna keep the suits. [Paul E buries his head in his heads and leads his team to the waiting limo...] [We cut to a gym in Philadelphia, where Sandman punches a large slab of frozen meat, whilst puffing away on a cigarette.]
Sandman: One...OW!...two...OW!....three....OW! Woman: You know, hon, you might try using a punching bag like a SANE person. Sandman: I'm not a sane person. Not while those pretenders to the hardcore name -- the Dangerous Alliance -- are still around. I just gotta imagine this is Vader's ugly face....OW!....and hit it until my knuckles break.
Woman: Won't be long at the rate you're going. How's it coming with you, Tommy? [We see Tommy Dreamer, being caned by Beulah.] Dreamer: Fifty more lashes, and I'm ready for anything 911 can dish out. [Whack! Whack! Whack!]
Woman: That's sick. Chris, you okay over there? [We see Chris Benoit throwing jobbers in the air, breaking their necks.] Benoit: Yeah, I just gotta cripple a few more guys. Then I can take on that rotten Calgary Connection. Teach them to fire me for being "too violent." They abandoned me at In Your Mailbox, so now I'll show 'em.
Woman: Keep it up. Uh, are you guys going to be ready to take on the
British Bulldogs? [We see Raven and Steve Richards, Los Gringos Locos, sitting on lawn chairs watching the proceedings while listening to Pearl Jam.]
Raven: Sure, babe. We're on an intense training regimen here, doncha know? [Raven takes a drink of his beer. Richards is reading Playboy, sideways of course.] Richards: Wow. Holy shit! What a pair of...interesting articles. [He turns the magazine the right way around again, as if no one had noticed.]
Woman: Whatever. [She turns to the camera] It doesn't matter anyways, because pretty soon the WCWF will belong to...the Extreme Team!
[Finally, we cut to the locker rooms, where Sean Waltman is depressed over his title win. Well, you know Sean....]
Sean: I can't believe that Barry did that to me! I thought that after he got hit on the head he might have been permanently nice. Who would've thought that another shot to the head could've brought him back again?
[Suddenly, we see a dark form appear in front of Waltman. It is the
Undertaker.] UT: ........help meeeeee...... [Waltman runs over to try anything, but it disappears just as suddenly.] Waltman: Well, that was certainly strange. [Just as suddenly, another darm form appears in front of Waltman. It is the Soultaker.] ST: ........help meeeeeeee too........ Waltman: Get bent. ST: ........wellllllll you don't have to be ruuuuuuuuude about it....... [And he disappears.] [Finally another dark form appears. This time it's Dan Spivey.] Spivey: Hey, kid, which way are the washrooms? Waltman: Down the hall and to your left. Say, aren't you supposed to be moaning or something spooky like that?
[Spivey rolls his eyes and moans menacingly.] Waltman: Okay. Just wanted to make sure you weren't slacking off or anything. [And Spivey disappears.] Next week: IN YOUR MAILBOX II! THE FOUR HORSEMEN V. SHANE'S CORPORATION WITH MORE STIPULATIONS THAN YOU CAN SHAKE A STICK AT! BE THERE!
