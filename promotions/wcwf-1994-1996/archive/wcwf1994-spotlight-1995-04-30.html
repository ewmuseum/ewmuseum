WCWF (1994-1996) — Roleplay / Spotlight

[WCWF] Spotlight, April 30/95

30 April 1995
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Welcome to a kind of weird edition of WCWF Spotlight. The WCWF has now grown to a point where it's getting harder and harder to keep things in order, so it's time for a Crisis on Infinite Storylines.
Let the games begin... The Quest Part One: Sean Waltman Seeks a Second Opinion... [Camera opens with "Lightning" Sean Waltman sitting the dressing room with the Undertaker and Paul Bearer.]
Sean: Guys, I *need* to talk to Rene again! You're the only ones I know who can do that sort of stuff.
UT: My...rapport with the departed is no longer there. When the urn disappeared last year, so did my ability to speak with those in the Next
World.
Paul: I'm afraid you're out of luck, young Mr. Waltman, ooooohhhh yesssss... Sean: No way! I can't accept that! There's gotta be someone out there who can still do that. Who has the urn?
UT: No one knows but the possessor. Sean: Fine! Then I'll find the urn and bring it back to you. *Then* will you help me? UT: I will....do my best. Sean: Good. Then I'm going to take a plane trip to England, where the urn was lost in the first place.
[Waltman marches out of the dressing room, and we cut to...] The Quest Part Two: Virgil Seeks Some Professional Help... [We open with Virgil in the offices of WCWF's resident psychiatrist, Dr. Sigmund Fraud.] Virgil: It's been awful recently, Doc. First I discover that Mr. Dibiase was really the Master all along, and then he FIRES me and replaces me with that big goon Mr. Hughes.
Fraud: And how does that make you feel? Virgil: Crappy! It's bad enough that I've been saddled with every bad gimmick in the history of the CWF and WCWF, plus the Loser title, but now I don't even have an employer.
Fraud: Mm-hmm. And your lack of a last name can't help much, either. Last time I checked, you were calling yourself Virgil X, were you not? Virgil: Yeah, that's right. I was trying to get in touch with my African roots, but it didn't work. Fraud: So maybe you should go to Africa, then. Virgil: Yeah, but I've got this great new gimmick worked out, Doc! Check this out... [puts a videotape in the doctor's machine] [We hear horrible rap music blaring in the background, while Alundra Blayze and Bull Nakano dance in the background, showing a total lack of soul. Virgil walks in, dressed in a gangsta rapper outfit.]
"He's Snoop Virgil V...oh yeah..." The girls sing in the background. Virgil starts to rap... "Yo! Snoop Virgil V is in the house, and you know I ain't no Mickey Mouse..." [Dr. Fraud jumps over and turns off the tape before permanent damage is done. Virgil buries his head in his hands.]
Virgil: Oh, who am I kidding? I need to be a simpering lackey for my life to have meaning! I need to be a worthless bodyguard!
Fraud: So go to Africa to find your roots, and find someone to be a spineless boot-licker for as well.
Virgil: Yeah, good idea, Doc! I'll do that! Thanks! [Virgil runs out of the office, and we cut to...] The Quest Part Three: Just Sit Right Back And You'll Hear A Tale... [We open with Bret Hart floating in his coffin at sea off the island of
KooeyKooeyKooey.] Bret: 76,193,394,389 bottles of beer on the wall, 76,193,394,389 bottles of beer, if one of those bottles should happen to fall...oh, man, this is so stupid. I've been out here for weeks now, eating birds and drinking rainwater. If I had any luck at all, a huge cruise vessel would sail by and pick me up on the way to Tahitti.
[Oddly enough, a huge cruise vessel chooses that moment to sail by, on its way to Tahitti.]
Bret: Hey! Hey, stop! [Lord Steven Regal and Hunter Hearst Helmsley poke their heads out the door to take a look-see.]
Regal: Oh, my. If my eyes don't deceive me, that's Bret Hart, floating adrift at sea in a coffin with no food or water.
Helmsley: I do believe you are correct, good sir. I suggest we take action at the earliest convenience.
[They whip out their electronic organizers.] Regal: Uhhhh...Thursday good for you, chap? Helmsley: No, no, shuffleboard finals then. How about Saturday, noonish? Regal: No, I'm afraid not. Water polo finals. Helmsley: Oh, yes, but of course. Silly me. Regal: [To Bret] Sorry, old, chap, but we're really rather busy for the next few days! We'll throw you some food and water and have our lawyers get in touch with you sometime next week. Cheerio!
Bret: Thanks heaps. *Sigh* 1 bottle of beer on the wall, 1 bottle of beer... [Bret floats into the sunset as we cut to...] The Quest Part Four: Barry Horowitz Redefines Himself... [We open with Barry still hanging from the tree we left him in at the conclusion of Wrestlemania V. (Haven't read it? SHAME ON YOU!)]
Barry: Somebody HELP ME!!! Hey, you seagull, get away from there! [A seagull is pecking at...a delicate place. Barry begins thrashing around to protect himself, and does so enough to loosen the knot around his leg and drop him to the ground!]
Barry: Yes! Free! I'm free! I'm...naked. Oh, dammit. [He looks around frantically, only to see the only available clothing are the tights left behind by Sean Waltman, the former 1-2-3 Kid. He changed outfits before Wrestlemania.]
Barry: How humiliating. But we are the same size, so.... [He puts on the 1-2-3 Kid tights.] Barry: Ugh. Okay, now I just need a way off this island. [He notices a gleaming over a hill.] Hey! That looks like gold over there!
[He runs over to the hill. It's really just the side of a very steep slope, almost a cliff.]
Barry: Oh, damn, it's the just the... [Before he can identify the object, a rock gives way, and Barry goes tumbling down the hill, with the object plummetting after him. He makes it to the bottom relatively unscathed.]
Barry: Whew. That was close. Luckily I didn't get hurt. [The object hits him in the head. And we'll be back next week, when he wakes up...] And with that over with, let's go to our regularly scheduled Spotlight... (Vince McMahon stands on a podium with Al Perez and Paul Diamond, who are wearing the U.S. Tag belts around their waists. They are accompanied by the lovely Tessa and Razor Ramon)
Vince : I'm standing here with the U.S. Tag team Champions the Razor Boys, and, say, Paul Diamond, glad you were able to find some new tights, were you able to bleach the stains out of your last few pairs?
Diamond : In the immortal words of Jesse Ventura, "Shut up, McMahon!" By the way, Vince, that's a very nice suit. Somewhere a Century 21 dealer must be running around awfully cold. That worthless snot, the Lord High Anvilcutioner, Jim Neidhart, decides to pull a little prank on the Razor Boys. Ha bloody ha. Well Neidhart, you and your
Law and Order pals are going to have to realize that there ain't no law that applies to the Razor Boys but the law of the Jungle :kill or be killed! We showed that to your buddies the Twin T-backs of Terror, now we're gonna show you!
Vince : I see. Al Perez, any comments? (Al Perez just scowls at Vince McMahon) Diamond : Al isn't talking today, he's taken a vow. Vince : I see. Well, notable by his absence is your manager, Lord Robert, or should I say Dustin Rhodes.
Ramon : We were about to come to that, Chico. May we present ... the former manager of the League of Gentlemen ... Dustin Rhodes!
(Dustin Rhodes walks to the podium) Vince : Former manager? Dustin : You got it, Vince. There's some big changes due for the League of Gentlemen. The first is that I'm returning to active duty as a wrestler. The second is that Razor Ramon's services as a bodyguard are no longer required.
(Razor raises an eyebrow) Vince : What? You don't mean your firing him just because he lost to 911, are you? Dustin : Not in the least, Vince. You see, with Razor Ramon, I made 2 big mistakes. Firstly not using him to his full potential, and secondly sending him to do what honour demanded I should do myself. Razor, you did good work getting rid of Rex King and Steve Doll. I'm asking that you become a full time member of the L.O.G. and help get rid of
Jim Neidhart. Will you accept my offer? (Ramon scowls , then grins an evil grin) Ramon : Chico, Neidhart thinks he's the executioner ? Well, he's gonna find this Razor is a guillotine with his name on it!
Dustin : Glad to hear it. You see Vince, several months ago I was almost put out of wrestling entirely by that oversized goon 911. The same night, he killed my daddy, the finest wrestler that ever was. (sniffs back a tear)
After that day, I planned my revenge. I put together the League of Gentlemen for that purpose. Then, an old friend offered his services. He brought me secrets of healing from the Far East to speed my recovery. He found agents to aid in my revenge. And now that I am 100% recovered from my injuries, he has agreed to take over the reins of the League of
Gentlemen. I give you the new manager of the League Of Gentlemen ...
the Sensei, Ricky "the Dragon" Steamboat! (Ricky Steamboat walks up to the podium, accompanied by the Ninja Dragons,
Masa Chono and Ricky Choshu. Steamboat then breathes flame all over Vince
McMahon's suit. Vince runs backstage, where he is saved from incineration by Gorilla Monsoon, who dumps a bucket of water over him. Steamboat picks up the mike.)
Steamboat : Sorry, but if I had to look at another powder blue or mustard yellow suit, I was going to lose my lunch. Dustin and I are tired of being pushed around by these steroid addled troglodytes. No more!
I brought the Ninja Dragons back from Japan to help make our mark on this federation. We stated by getting rid of that fool, Jimmy
Snuka. Now we will teach that same lesson to the rest of the federation. Before warned of the rise of ... The League Of Gentlemen! Vince McMahon: With Wrestlemania V now in the history books, one of the bigger *wrestling* stories to come out of it is the fact that we have a NEW WCWF Intercontinental Champion. So without further ado, ladies and gentlemen, accompanied by his manager James J. Dillon the WCWF Intercontinental
Champion "The Heartbreak Kid" Shawn Michaels. [As his music blares in the backgroud, Michaels, still a little bandged from the landmine match, struts to the interview platform with an overjoyed J.J. at his side.]
Vince: Shawn, congratulations on your victory over Barry Horowitz for the I-C title. Shawn Michaels: Well ya know McMannequin, it should come as *NO* suprise to you or to anyone else in the WCWF to see the HB
Kid with the gold once again strapped around his waist, although as we both know this reign came a couple of months later than we hoped.
Vince: What about Atsushi Onita and his challenge? Was it a set up the whole time? Shawn: Well Vinnie Mac, after that 1-2-3 punk fluked out a victory over me at Starrcade, J.J. put the wheels into motion. We brought in
Onita and, well, in the end the result is just what we wanted with The Heartbreak Kid strappin on the gold again. But, for once, there's something more important than Shawn Michaels we need to take care of today, so give the mic over to J.J. and let him take over.
[Vince hands J.J. Dillon the microphone and slis into the background] James J. Dillon: At Wrestlemania, Barry Horowitz unveiled the new I-C belt which is around Shawn's waist. After the victory party,
Shawn, the rest of the FFOW and myself had a little talk with Wendi Richter and...well...guys come on out. [The other members of The First Family of Wrestling, Dr. Tom Prichard,
Jimmy Del Ray, and Atsushi Onita make their way to the interview set looking very somber]
J.J.: Two months ago, the wrestling world lost one of the best there ever was. But more importantly, we lost a very good and close friend. Tonight, we'd like to make a presentation to his remaining family. Tommy, Peggy, Doug, if you could join us at this time.
[From the backstage area Tommy & Peggy Gilbert, the parents of Hotstuff
Eddie Gilbert, along with his brother Doug make their way to the stage and shake hands with the FFOW]
J.J.: I personally knew Eddie for a good many years. After Shawn won the
Intercontinental title, he said "J.J., you may think I'm going insane, but when I was in that match- I could feel Eddie's presence. I could feel him telling me what to do." We all sat around and talked about it, and we made a decision. Peggy,
Tommy, & Doug we would like to give to you this retired World Championship Wrestling Federation Intercontinental Championship belt in memory of your son Eddie, who ment more to us than words can say. [Shawn gives the Gilbert family the retired belt] Shawn: And one more thing [looks up to the heavens] Eddie, I know you're up there...and I'm dedicating this title reign to you my friend.
["Hot stuff" by Donna Summer begins to play as all return to the dressing room] [Missy Hyatt takes Vince's place on the platform] MH:Ladies and gentlemen, I introduce to you, Mr. Harley Race! (Race makes his way to the platform) Harley, you say you have an important announcement, so, let's hear it. HR:Well, I certainly do have big news. In fact, it's fantastic, unless you're one of the five Midnight Street Patrol fans.
MH:Harley, what are you talking about? What do you have planned for the Midnight Street Patrol?
HR:Hahaha.....nothing much.....just the END of their careers!! MH:Harley Race, how do you plan to end a team like the Midnight Street
Patrol's careers? HR:With the Race Task Force!!! MH:The what!? HR:The Race Task Force!! My new team!! MH:Would you mind telling us more? Like, say, who's in the group?? HR:The Race Task Force was brought together by yours truly to destroy the Midnight Street Patrol!!
MH:And who are they?? HR:Patience Miss Hyatt, patience.....I will now give them a proper introduction. Ladies and gentlemen....I introduce to you the Race
Task Force, Brutus the Barber Beefcake and Greg the Hammer
Valentine, the Dream Team. Tito Santana and Rick Martel, the Strike
Force, and the NEW Rock N Roll Express, Rick Morton and Paul Roma!!!
Ladies and gentlemen, THE RACE TASK FORCE!!!! (They all leave) MH:Back to you in the booth. [We now cut to the offices of Wendi Richter, where we see WCWF World champ Shane Douglas with manager Ted Dibiase, former champ Ultimate
Warrior with manager Tammy Fitch, former champ Steve Austin with manager
Roddy Piper, and former champ Sean Waltman with interim manager Gary
Hart. They're all yelling.] Hart: I demand the results of the four-way ladder match at Wrestlemania be reviewed by this office IMMEDIATELY! My client, Mr. Waltman, clearly had the match won, but for the tactics of Jackie Fargo! I demand he be recognized as World champion!
Dibiase: Horse shit! My man Shane won the match fair and square, and
I'll challenge any ruling that says otherwise!
Piper: All's I gotta say is Stunning Steve shoulda been given the title back while you folks sorted out this whole mess. He was champion going in, and after all that crap he shoulda kept the belt until you made up your minds. I say he's the rightful champion.
Fitch: Mr. Hellwig never should have lost the title to Mr. Austin or
Mr. Douglas or whoever beat him on that Spotlight. The title should have remained with him, since the match was never sanctioned in the first place. I will recognize him, and him only, as the official World champion.
Wendi: Tammy...Ted...Roddy...Gary...calm down. Please. As president, it's my job to ensure that the rules are maintained, and Jackie Fargo was the legal referee of that match, and his decision, no matter how tainted, was to award the match and title to Shane Douglas. That is my stance.
Fitch: Fine. Then effective immediately, the Court of Last Appeal hereby withdraws from the WCWF and will pursue its own interests in the form of a new federation in the Mid-South. We will choose to recognize
Mr. Jim Hellwig as the rightful World Champion. I'll have the papers on your desk Monday morning, along with, I'm 100% sure, the support of at least four other stables who sympathize with my cause.
Wendi: Tammy, you don't know what you're saying... Hart: Hey, I like what she's saying, too! In fact, if I can liquidate the right stocks, I think I can manage to set up MY own federation, too!
And we're gonna recognize "Lightning" Sean Waltman as the REAL champion of the World! Wendi: Oh god, why me... Piper: Hey, I think you guys are onto something here! I don't see why
I shouldn't start up a federation, too! Move to Philadelphia...buy a bingo hall...but first, I've got BIG plans for the WCWF! The Hardcore
Alliance may be moving to the Tri-States, but we're gonna go out with a
BANG! [Piper runs out, a demonic gleam in his eye.] Wendi: I suppose you, Ted, are going to start up your own federation, too. Ted: Hell, no. I already own this two-bit federation as it is. Why would the Million Dollar Man need competition for himself? Besides,
Shane Douglas is already recognized as the official World champion. [Jim Cornette bursts into the room] Cornette: THIS IS A TRAVESTY OF JUSTICE! My Midnight Express were supposed to be wrestling for the tag team titles at Wrestlemania, and we got SHAFTED! I'm going to...
Wendi: Let me guess...you're going to start up your own federation in the Smoky Mountains and recognize the Express as the World tag team champions, right?
Cornette: [Turning to Dibiase] Wow, she's good. Wendi: Okay, fine, I don't care anymore. When y'all get the details worked out, call me. I'm going for coffee with my financee...
World tag title match: Adam Bomb & Warlord v. The Gangsters. [Note: for those of you who are not familiar with the Gangsters, they are Paul E. Dangerously's team of Vinnie Vegas and Brian (Lee) Lenardo.
And just because Vinnie Vegas looks exactly like Diesel, it doesn't mean they are the same person. Just thought I'd clear that up.]
The Gangsters are waiting in the ring for the champions to enter, and when they do it's no contest. Vegas and Lenardo, still pissed off at losing their chance at the US tag belts, are in no mood to lose tonight.
They give Warlord a double-boot over the top rope right away, into the arms of Harvey Wippleman. Bomb charges towards them for a double-clothesline, but the Gangsters each hold out an arm and double-chokeslam him instead. Warlord climbs onto the apron, but the challengers whip Bomb into Warlord, sending him crashing into the barricade. Bomb staggers into the middle of the ring, and Lenardo gives him a big foot to the face while Vegas gives him a big foot to the back of the head. Warlord crumples and Vegas covers for 1....2.....3.
"Here are your winners...and NEEEWWWWWW WCWF WORLD TAG TEAM CHAMPIONS...THE GANGSTERS!" Next week: The Quest Parts 5,6,7 and 8...Roddy Piper's Big Plan comes to fruition...We learn more about the four new regions...and some matches and stuff...
