[WCWF] The end of the WCWF...

27 May 1995
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I just wanted to let everyone know that In Your Mailbox will be the last ever WCWF card written/booked by yours truly.
I was planning on doing IYM, followed by King of the Ring to finish off the WCWF, but I'm getting totally tired of doing this whole thing, so I'm just going to wrap it all up on Tuesday. Given the way it's going to end, it won't matter whether all the subplots are tied up anyways.
I'll take a well-deserved vacation from the WCWF until about September, then probably start up a totally new federation with a full booking committee from the start.
[name withheld] will have full rights to the WCWF, to do with as he sees fit. He currently runs the DeWF, and if he wants to call it the WCWF from now on, more power to him. However, my version of the WCWF will end completely with In Your Mailbox, and any further use of the name will not be connected in any way with the continuity of the current WCWF, or old CWF.
I'l still be on the 'net, of course, but now that I've created the fantasy group and gotten the WCWF to the point where I want it, I can go back to just lurking again without worrying about the pressures of votes or running an e-fed.
The WCWF home page will still be up, as usual, at http://gpu.srv.ualberta.ca/~skeith/wcwf.html , and I'll update Steven
Regal's History of the WCWF to bring it right up to date later on. I encourage current WCWF members to contact [name withheld] (whose address is [detail withheld] ) about joining the DeWF (officially sanctioned by the WCWF, I might add...) in the interim.
I'd just to thank [name withheld] for creating wonderful little fake federation, [name withheld] for helping to maintain it, and everyone who was a member, or currently is, for all their ideas and support.
And I'm outta here... [detail withheld] - or - [detail withheld] Hockey is life -- The rest is details... "Spoon....SPOON!" - The Tick WCWF Homepage: http://gpu.srv.ualberta.ca/~skeith/wcwf.html
