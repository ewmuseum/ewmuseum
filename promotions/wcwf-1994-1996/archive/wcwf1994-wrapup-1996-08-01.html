Hi ho, mindless followers. :) Just thought I'd let whatever straggling supporters I have left after bowing out of running the premier (okay, only...) fake wrestling federation for a few months know that I'll be putting as much of the old stuff up on my homepage as I can for y'all to peruse.
I'd be really spiteful and point out how many angles the WWF and WCW have stolen from me to date, but that's beneath me, I feel.
I'll probably just do the best of compilation, WCWF Alpha, a few choice PPVs and couple of episodes of Tuesday Night Undercooked. Just so everyone can get an idea of what I was doing at the time. If you like it, try [name withheld]' DEWF, which is along the same lines, and which I hang out around from time to time.
