Capitol Punishment 1998 (part 1 of 6)

14 June 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
ICWA Capital Punishment '98 June 14, 1998 MCI Center / Washington, D.C. [The scene opens showing the face of Mount Rushmore.. The faces of the American Presidents are replaced by the stars of the ICWA, Adam Mazur, The Knick Man, Axon Blade, and Mike Wick, as we hear the voice of Nick Klien...] Nick: [V/O] It started out with eight men... Eight men with hopes of becoming the first ever ICWA World Heavyweight Champion... [The camera begins to focus in on the four faces...] Nick: [V/O] Eight, soon became four, and four, soon became two.. Those two men, The Knick Man and Axon Blade... [The camera focuses in on the two center images on the face of Mount Rushmore, The Knick Man and Axon Blade...] Nick: [V/O] Over the course of the past 21 days, these two men have had their battles... [The camera fades in to show various slow motion clips of Axon Blade and The Knick Man getting into physcial encounters on Monday Night Heat..] Nick: [V/O] But tonight, the ultimate battle.. In our nation's capital, Washington, D.C., these two men will right it out to see who will be come the first ever ICWA World Heavyweight Champion, HERE AT CAPITAL PUNISHMENT '98!!!!!! [As a rock 'n' roll version of "America, the Beautiful" plays in the background, the scene opens up inside the sold out MCI Center in Washinton, D.C. Fireworks go off near the entrance way, and the camera pans around the crowd, holding up various signs, and then finally closes in on the announce position, where Nick Klien, wearing a black tuxedo, and "Big Nasty" Paul Agren, wearing a navy blue suit, are seen standing...] Nick: Ladies and gentlemen, welcome to Capital Punishment '98!! We are LIVE from Washington, D.C. and here tonight, we're gonna crown our first even World Heavyweight Champion, when Axon Blade takes on The Knick Man... Paul: I'll tell ya what, they're two of my favorite wrestlers here in the ICWA, but I gotta tell ya, over the past few weeks, we've seen The Knick Man gain the advantage over Axon Blade, in my opinion, beating him at his own game! Nick: Also tonight, Kontra will meet Adam Mazur for a shot at the World Television Champion, "Wild Card" Ricky Watts!! Paul: I saw Kontra in the back just before we went on the air tonight.. He is ready, and he's my pick to walk out of here with the gold!! Nick: In a STRETCHER MATCH, Mass Destruction meet The Enforcers in a rematch from last Monday night!! Paul: It was a fluke last Monday night, and it's not gonna happen again! The Enforcers are walking out of here victorious tonight! Nick: Plus more great action as well!! Right now we're gonna kick it over to Jason Garrison for the introductions for our opening match!! [DING DING DING] Garrison: The first contest here at Capital Punishment is schedueled for one fall, with a 20 minute time limit!! [The theme from "Buffy, the Vampire Slayer" begins to play over the arena sound system...] Garrison: Introducing first, from Boston, Massachusetts, weighing in at 240 pounds.. PLLLLLLLLLAYBOY JIMMMMMMMMMMMY MAAAAAAAAAAC!!!!!!!!! Paul: Here's my boy!! Nick: Jimmy Mac schedueled to come out first.. But that's Warblade's music!! [As the entrance darkens, blue and red lights take over the arena, and the crowd cheers as they await the entrance of Jimmy Mac... A walks out from behind with curtain in army fatigues, hiding his face with a tinted shield on the army helmet. This man enters the ring, and slowly takes off his helmet, and "Whatcha Want!?" by the Beastie Boys cuts off the previous theme as the man reveals himself to be Playboy Jimmy Mac...] Nick: Cute, real cute... Paul: Shutup Klien, this guy is great!! Playboy: [laughing, and comically swinging his hands in a karate fashion] Take a guess on who I'm supposed to be... I'M WARBLADE!! [taking off the Army jacket, and throwing it into the crowd] Why cover myself up?! There's no need for that jacket, just like there is no need for you, WARBLADE!! You couldn't get the job done with Ricky Watts, and now you have to deal with me! I ridiculed, humiliated, and beat the ELIMINATOR on Monday night!! And speaking of Monday night, Tennessee will never be the same, and neither will those Southern Belles if you catch my drift! Anyway, The Eliminator was the first wrestling to step in my way, and now YOU are in my path, Warblade!! I caught wind of the TV Title chase this past Monday, and I understand that the winner of this match is gonna face the Champion this Wednesday... Well, we all know Wednesday is HUMP DAY, and THE MAC will be ready to go!! I want a strap!! That's what every wrestler wants damn it! I want to throw a shout out to the man behind the truth... "Big Nasty" Paul Agren at the announce position!! Paul: YEAAA!!! BIG NAS-TAAAAAAE!!! Nick: Please... Playboy: Paul, keep your eyes on Mac, cause there will be plenty to commintate on! Paul: You know it baby!! Nick: And what's this guy doing now?! He sees a fan with a Warblade sign... And he's taking the sign and ripping it up!! C'mon, that's just a fan!!! Playboy: You don't know what you're in for, Warblade!! The Eliminator was my first stepping stone, and you know what that makes you!! So, in the land of D.C., after I execute you in Capital Punishment fashion, and beat you with my arsonal of moves, Billy Boy and myself have to meet some ladies in the Oral... [pausing] Excuse me, OVAL Office!! [laughing] Warblade, I'm here in the ICWA to attack what I seek, and seek what I want!! Tonight in Washington, I want to to beat you within an inch of your life and ATTACK my goal of becoming the number one contender! I may be new, but I'm more than ready to back up the hype on PLAYBOY JIMMY MAC!!! I'm not even thinking of Wednesday right now, I'm not looking past you WARBLADE... You're another opportunity to make my statement in the ICWA that the ATTACK IS HERE!! Get ready D.C., there's TOO MUCH MAC!! Hope your ready for me... Paul: I'm tellin' ya, Klien.. That's my boy!! Nick: Yea, sure.. But I'd like to make reference to something Jimmy Mac said, and he is correct in saying that this is somewhat of a "Number One Contenders" match, because yes, the winner of this match will be fighting for the World Television Title, no matter who the champion is, this Wednesday at ICWA Wreckage Wednesday!! Paul: This is great!! I've got Kontra vs. Playboy Jimmy to look forward to!! Garrison: And his opponent... [The theme from "Buffy, the Vampire Slayer" once again begins to blare throughout the arena...] Garrison: Accomapanied to the ring by Abigail, from Dallas, Texas, and weighing in at 260 pounds.. WAAAAAAAAARBLAAAAAAAAAADE!!!!! Nick: Abigail leading Warblade down the aisle as he slaps hands with some of these great fans here in Washington, D.C... Paul: Please!! What's so great about 'em?! Nick: First, they jam-packed the arena here tonight to see great ICWA action, and second, they're paying out salary, so be nice!! Paul: Well, uhh... Nick: Warblade now climbing into the ring.. He looks ready for Jimmy Mac! [DING DING DING] Nick: This match is underway, and Playboy Jimmy Mac and Warblade and just circling each other here in the early goings. They lock up... And Warblade slams him down! Warblade has the strength advantage here. Paul: Yea, but Mac's got the intellect! Warblade's is an idiot! Nick: Mac back up, and he quickly locks up with Warblade again.. And Warblade catches Mac with a hard kneelift to the midsection, and again quickly pulls off a snap suplex. Warblade off the ropes now... Nice kneedrop, and he's staying right on top of Jimmy Mac!! Paul: THE MAC's got him right where he wants him! Nick: Yea, OK... Mac now being brought up to his feet, and he just caught Warblade with an thumb to the eye gouge! Mac quickly tosses Warblade into the ropes and nails him right under the chin with a dropkick! Nice moves by Mac, but Warblade only stumbles back, and now Mac moves in on him again... Northern Lights Suplex by Jimmy Mac! Into a bridge... ONE.......... One-count only!! Jimmy Mac now following up, draping Warblad over the second rope... Guillotine legdrop! NO!! Warblade moved out of the way! Mac goes down hard, and Warblade once again taking control, sending Mac into the ropes... Warblade puts his head down, and he pays for it!! Swinging neckbreak by Jimmy Mac!! Paul: I told ya, this Warblade's a big dummy!! Jimmy's got him! Nick: And the cover! ONE.......... TWO.......... NO!!!! Mac almost had him right there! Mac not letting up though, he slams Warblade down to the mat. And now he's heading up to the top rope! He may be taking too much time right here... He goes for a flying elbowdrop, but Warblade once again out of the way!! The high-risk style not paying off for Jimmy Mac thusfar... And now Warblade picking Mac up off the mat, into a waistlock, AND A GERMAN SUPLEX!! ONE......... TWO.......... THREE!!!!! HE GOT HIM!!!! NO!!!!! Jimmy Sanders now saying that it was only a two count, that was close!! Paul: That's right, good call by Sanders!! his shoulder was up! Nick: Warblade looking a little frustrated, once again picking Jimmy Mac up... And sending him into the ropes! A clothesline misses, and Jimmy Mac ducks down.. But Warblade goes right back after him, sending Jimmy Mac over the top rope and to the outside!! Warblade looking out to the fans.. And now he's going out after Jimmy Mac, not allowing Sanders to make the count! Mac now running away from Warblade on the outside, and he runs behind Abigail!! What a lowlife!! Paul: Abigail was going to cheat!! All Mac's doing is stopping the interference!! Nick: Oh please! Jimmy Mac shoves Abigail in the direction of Warblade, and Warblade has her.. And "Playboy" thows in a cheap right hand! Warblade now trying to get Abigail out of the way, fighting back with his own rights and lefts... And Jimmy Sanders laying the count on both men! Paul: Watch the count, Mac!! Nick: Oh no!! Low blow on the outside by Jimmy Mac.. Kick right to the groin! And now Mac pushing Warblade back into the ring! Mac follows him back in, and now lifting him up.. YES!! Piledriver by Jimmy Mac takes Warblade down hard! Jimmy Mac going for the cover!! ONE......... TWO.......... NO!!!!! Warblade kicks out just at the last second!! Jimmy Mac arguing with Jimmy Sanders now, but that's not gonna do him any good! Mac now heading back up to the top rope, this hasn't paid off so far.. But Warblade's up!! Warblade shaking the ropes, and Jimmy Mac is crotched on the top turnbuckle!! Paul: So much for his night with the Mac Girls... Nick: Warblade now climbing up to the top rope with Jimmy Mac.. Could it be?! He's going for a..... YES!! SUPERPLEX BRINGS BOTH MEN DOWN, AND THEY'RE BOTH HURT!! It's now a matter of who can get to their feet first! And I think Warbalde may have actually taking the brunt of that blow... Playboy Mac now fighting up to his feet, and he's up! Warblade also fighting up to his feet, but a Russian legsweep by Mac brings him back down!! And now Mac looking out to the fans... Paul: No, don't look out to these morons!! Get back on your game! Nick: And Jimmy Mac now looking down at Abigail.. I think he said "This one's for you!!" But look, Warblade's up!! WARBLADE'S UP!! And Jimmy Mac arguing with Abigail.. Warblade bounces off the ropes, Jimmy Mac turns around.. OMNISPLASH!!!!!!!!! WARBLADE HITS THE OMNISPLASH, AND IT'S OVER!!!! ONE............ TWO........... THREE!!!!!!!!!!!!!! [DING DING DING] Nick: Warblade pulls out the win here in our first match at Capital Punishment!! Paul: Awww, man... Poor Jimmy!! Nick: Jimmy Mac is out, and Warblade now celebrating with Abigail, on his way to the back... Our next match here tonight is going to be a big one for The Student Body, because if they don't win it, they're outta here!! Paul: Well I don't know if it's exactly fair for The Student Body to have to face Juicy Fruit and his gay pals if they want to salvage their careers, but still, that's Zane for ya...
