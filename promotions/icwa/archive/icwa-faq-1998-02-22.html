If you are an experienced e-wrestler you may simply shrug and take this minor change in your stride. However, if you are new to the sport of fantasy wrestling you may not understand the basics of e-wrestling. Here, therefore, before we get into too deep a discussion of the minor variations ICWA employs we present for your delight and edification a basic introduction to e-wrestling. Experienced e-wrestlers may prefer to skip this section.... ________________________________ | | | 4.1 What is Fantasy Wrestling? | |________________________________| First, a secret known to only an elite few.... Ready? Professional Wrestling is fixed. Yes, it's true. I know it's a painful truth to have to learn, but we must all learn it sooner or later. "Real" professional wrestling is like a soap-opera, only with more outrageous and outlandish violence in it. Colourful characters compete with one another, either to win the "prize" of a championship belt, or to gain vengeance on one another for imagined or real slights, to prove who is the best, and so on. In effect, a series of "Scriptwriters" known as the "bookers" determine the storylines, the plots, and what is going to happen. The professional wrestlers then go out and, within the confines of the plot, put on the most amazing show of bizarre and outlandish violence. Don't be mistaken into thinking that the matches are choreographed and that wrestlers don't get hurt, because they can be, and badly too. But the outcome is never in doubt. That's why it's usually called "Sports Entertainment" and not simply "Sport". In "fantasy" wrestling, much the same thing occurs. You create a professional wrestler who competes with other wrestlers. The people running the wrestling league act as the "scriptwriters", deciding who will win and who will lose, what the plot will be, et cetera. However, in fantasy wrestling the people controlling the wrestlers have a lot of input into the "scriptwriting" process. You decide how wrestlers react, who they face, what they say, and so on and so forth. How is this done? Through: * Angles * Flashes * Strategies ____________ | | | 4.2 Angles | |____________| What is an 'Angle'? Real wrestling would be really boring if all that happened was one wrestler walked into the ring, beat the crap out of the other, and then walked out again. Real wrestling involves intricate plots, schemes, conspiracies, alliances, friendships, enmities, betrayals and feuds. Those are angles. We use them in Fantasy Wrestling, too. For example, you might have a wrestler who is some kind of American patriot, a man who loves his country and who weeps tears of happiness whenever he hears the Star-Spangled Banner. Now, imagine if you will a new wrestler coming to town, an ardent commie who wants to bring down civilisation as we know it and replace it with an all-new Soviet Regime. How do you react to that? Do you rush down to ringside and attack him before his first match? Do you challenge him to a match so that you can "kick his commie butt"? Do you do an interview in the centre of the ring, and dare him to come down to the ring and talk to him about what a despicable country he comes from? All of these (and many more options, limited only by your imagination) are available to you. To arrange an angle, whatever it may be or whoever it may involve, you should: * Check with the person(s) running the league (aka 'fedhead(s)'). * Check with the "handler" (the person who runs the wrestler) that this is OK with him. You should always check with the fedhead(s), just to ensure that what you want to do doesn't interfere with any other angles. If it does conflict you might not be allowed to do it, or you might have to change your angle slightly. Perhaps you might have to delay your plans for a while, or bring them forward. Perhaps the other angle(s) might have to be modified in some way. Whatever. Just check with the fedhead(s), just to ensure that we can all keep our stories matching :) If in any doubt about anything, always check with the fedhead of your wrestling league. They're there to help you, and they're willing to listen. If at all possible (and sometimes it won't be, necessarily) you should also check with the handler(s) of the other wrestler(s) the angle will involve. Putting your heads together will often improve the angle, as the two (or three, or four, etc) improve upon and expand the angle, in ways that you might not have conceived. Going back to our Super-patriot/Commie example, contacting the handler of the commie might make available more ideas. For example, perhaps it is the Commie who attacks the Patriot, not the otherway around. Perhaps it is the Commie who calls out the Patriot... Remember that fantasy wrestling is, in many ways, a "team" sport. Your primary duty as a handler is not (necessarily) to become the champion of a wrestling league, but rather to present good storylines, to entertain the other handlers. In short, to "tell" a good story. But remember, good things come to those who do good. By working to tell good stories you will be (hopefully) improving your wrestler's chances of winning gold. Angles are important things in fantasy wrestling. Interesting, entertaining angles are the lifeblood of every wrestling league. You can have the most eloquent, entertaining wrestler known to man, and if all he does is walk up to a wrestling ring, fight a match and leave, then he's not going to get very far. The crowds will soon be chanting "Boring!" Remember, be _entertaining_. _____________ | | | 4.3 Flashes | |_____________| You've come up with an excellent angle. It's the business, guaranteed to get you noticed. You and your opponent have discussed things at length, and you know exactly what you want to do. The fedhead(s) of your league think it's a great idea, and can't wait for you to start the ball rolling... You've come up with "the plot"... now you've got to do the "screenplay" :) That's where "flashes" (sometimes referred to as "role-playing", or "rp" etc) come in. Every so often you produce a written 'flash' detailing your wrestler being interviewed by a reporter, or "video promo" type material (such as an American Patriot standing in front of the White House, tearfully extolling the virtues of America...), or indeed anything your mind can conceive. If you've seen the sort of video footage and interviews commonly used in professional wrestling already (and I assume you have! :) ) then you know the sort of thing you can do here. Flashes are an important part of fantasy wrestling. The most brilliant, inspired, downright devious angle can be completely ruined by bad flashes. If the angle is the plot, then the flashes are the most important bit - the acting. A brilliant plot let down by poor acting is not much of a plot. Conversely, brilliant acting does not usually hide a weak plot. But when the the two combined are red hot --- it's Oscar time, buddy! Good angles combined with good flashes may well steer you toward the wrestling Oscars --- the championship belts! ________________ | | | 4.4 Strategies | |________________| The angle has worked fine. The interviews were a big success. You've managed to lure your hated opponent somehow into a match. Maybe you insulted his sainted white-haired mother, or maybe you stole his favourite CD. Whatever, yuu're now standing in the ring. Standing opposite you is a lean, mean wrestling machine. Now what do you do? This is where Strategies (or "strats") come in. The fedhead(s) decide who will win and who will lose (based upon the quality of your flashes, whether your winning or losing will advance the storyline/angle, and a host of factors). The fedhead(s) decide this, and then the match is written. It is impossible (for time reasons) for the match to be played out 'in real time', and so you, the handler, should submit a strategy outlining, in general terms, what your wrestler will do in the match. For example, to go back to the Commie/Patriot example, the Patriot might be an amazingly skilled ring technician, whilst the commie is a huge powerhouse who does nothing but brawl. Your strat should outline how you plan to defeat this monster. Perhaps you would attempt to outwrestle him, avoiding punches and going on the offensive with intricate moves you hope will tie up the opponent, leaving him unable to do anything but submit. Perhaps you will attempt to "rope-a-dope", trying to tire-out the bad guy, leaving him too tired to defend himself later in the match. Try to put yourself in the mind of your wrestler. You know the sort of wrestler he is, you know what he can do, you know how he thinks. Try to briefly describe what sort of plan he would come up with to defeat his opponent. As well as general strategy, you can perhaps come up with some specific things you'd like your wrestler to do before, during and after the match. For example, your Patriot character when he comes down to ringside might be carrying Old Glory, proudly swinging her in the air. Perhaps you'd quite like your character to execute a backbreaker on your opponent when he is weakened? Put it in the strat. Perhaps you've scouted your opponent, and know a good counter to his finishing move? Describe how your wrestler will outwit his opponent in the strat. Strategies need not be overly complex, nor need they be too short. A single paragraph saying "I beat up my opponent" is just as bad as a twelve page second-by-second account of how you would like the match to progress. As per flashes, more or less anything goes, within the bounds of reason. If your strategy says your wrestler is going to bring a flamethrower to ringside and toast your opponent, please do not be too surprised if your fedhead(s) ignore it. Once you've come up with your strategy for your match, send it to your fedhead(s). Then sit back and wait for the match, to see if your exquisite plan worked perfectly, or whether your opponent threw a monkeywrench in the works... _______________________ | | | 5.0 ICWA Requirements | |_______________________| This section will detail some of the things which ICWA require of you, or things that ICWA does that are different from 'normal' fantasy wrestling. _____________ | | | 5.1 FLASHES | |_____________| As detailed above, flashes are the interviews, video segments, etc that compose a large part of the entertainment in ICWA. There are two basic avenues for flashes in ICWA. * Flash Packets This is the main avenue for getting your flashes out to the world of ICWA. Flashes are mailed to Mike, who sends them out each day (Normally...) to the entire fed. * The Cards You can do "In-Rings" for both of the ICWA's cards each week. These need to be sent to fed head responsible for the card in question. ___________________ | | | 5.1.1 FLASH RULES | |___________________| ICWA has flash rules somewhat different to most of the feds that are around today. The only limitation we place on the quantity of your flashes is that of ONE per DAY. This does not mean that we expect you to do 7 flashes a week, but we do not say you can only do 2 a week either. Bad language should be avoided wherever possible. Remember, this is nominally a family show. Just as in regular "family" wrestling entertainment, bad language should be the exception, not the norm. By the way, more or less anything (within reason) goes within ICWA. However, if in any doubt please contact the ICWA Presidential Team. Attacks upon other wrestlers is permitted only with the permission of the ICWA Presidential Team, or the permission of the other handler(s) [Preferable]. Attacks upon the ICWA announcing staff, or other members of the ICWA administration, _are_ permitted but should be used sparingly, and never gratuitously. All such attacks should be cleared with us first. If in _any_ doubt over whether part of your flash will be permitted, or is acceptable, please contact us. 99 times out of 100 it will be fine, but it's the 100th time that makes the checking worthwhile! ____________________ | | | 5.1.2 FLASH POLICY | |____________________| ICWA "Flash" policy is as follows: All handlers of ICWA wrestlers are expected to produce a flash at least once a fortnight. Though we would like to have frequent flashing, we do _not_ want you to throw together a junkie flash just to meet the quota. This is all in the name of having fun, so if you are going to do a flash to meet the requirement, make it short and interesting so that we're happy that you are active, and you're happy with doing a flash that is fun. If there is a reason why you can't flash regularly, ie perhaps you are going on holiday for a month or something, please contact the ICWA Presidential Team. We're flexible. We're here to help you :) However... Handlers who _persistently_ fail to comply to this flash policy and cannot offer a decent reason will be "jobbed" out of ICWA. Namely, your wrestler's career will suddenly take a sharp downward turn as he begins losing to other ICWA stars with perhaps alarming regularity. Put simply, flashes and angles are the absolute lifeblood of ICWA. ICWA depends on good flashes and angles, as does your wrestler's career. Those who regularly come up with interesting, entertaining angles and flashes will prosper. Those who don't --- well, they won't. __________________ | | | 6.0 ICWA TITLES | |__________________| Where would any federation be without it's titles? Well, here at ICWA Wrestling, we to have our titles, and boy oh boy are these babies prestigous! What have you got to aim for? Well, let's not keep you in suspense for any longer... All of these titles can only be won by pinfall or submission, unless special stipulations exist for the match in question. ICWA World Title: As you have probably guessed, this is the mother of all the titles here. This is the big kahuna, the big momma, the... yeah, ok, you get the point. This belt can be won by anyone, regardless of height, weight, or hair colour.... ICWA North American Title: The "Second" Title in the ICWA. Yes, it's not as prestigious as the World Title, but then, it's a hell of a lot more prestigious than nothing at all! This title also has no limitations on it. ICWA World Tag Team Titles: As per the statements for the singles variation of this title, this is the big one when it comes to the tag team circuit. All of the ICWA Titles are defended at all major Pay Per View Events, and at the descretion of either the fed head, or the handler. ______________ | | | 7.0 RANKINGS | |______________| ICWA Rankings work as follows for both singles are Tag Team divisions. Wrestlers are ranked with the Number of Victories less the number of defeats dictating their positions. Should a tie be in effect, the Number of Wins breaks ties. If a tie still exists, draws break the difference. Example: The Spunky Lobster has a record of 11-4-2 His total would be 7 Steven Spunk has a record of 9-2-2 His total would also be 7 However, The Lobster would rank ahead of Steve Spunk, due to the greater number of wins. _______________________ | | | 8.0 PEOPLE AND PLACES | |_______________________| The following are the fictional ICWA officials and places in the ICWA Universe.
