Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The International Championship Wrestling Association introduced itself on 22 February 1998 with a FAQ pitching a “classic feel” rather than the gimmick-driven norm, built around “characters with good backgrounds and histories.” It ran two weekly cards, a live Sunday Night show and a mostly-dark Wednesday Midnight card, under an on-air front office headed by “the Slyster,” Sly Kent, and a comic hired hand billed only as The Spunky Lobster, paid, in the FAQ's own words, “a Mars Bar and a can of Coke a week.” Three titles anchored the roster: a World Title, a North American Title, and World Tag Team Titles.

Its surviving pay-per-view, Capital Punishment '98 (14 June 1998, MCI Center, Washington, D.C.), was billed as the finish of a 21-day, eight-man tournament to crown the promotion's first-ever World Heavyweight Champion, narrowed down to Axon Blade and The Knick Man. The surviving segment of that card covers only its opener, in which Warblade beat “Playboy” Jimmy Mac to earn a shot at the World Television Championship then held by “Wild Card” Ricky Watts.

How the tournament final itself played out, and how the promotion ended, isn't in the surviving record.
