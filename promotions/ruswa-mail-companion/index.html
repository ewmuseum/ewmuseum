A play-by-mail companion federation announced by RUSWA's own operator in August 1997, its own name never given in the surviving record. Written by AI, 2026

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

This promotion carries no name of its own in the surviving record. Its operator, already running an e-mail federation called RUSWA, announced on 28 August 1997 that he was starting a second, separate promotion by mail: “I have decided to start a play by mail wrestling federation in addition to my e-mail fed the RUSWA.”

As announced, it would run on a monthly mailed booklet, fifty cents a match rather than the usual dollar or two, because the booklets cost its operator nothing to send — each carrying a roster, rankings, champions, interviews and match results, plus a Wrestler of the Month and a Tag Team of the Month award worth ten free matches, and twenty more for any wrestler holding a title across three straight booklets. Nine championships were planned: five singles titles (World, Intercontinental, Television, Cruiserweight and Extreme) and four tag titles (World, Intercontinental, Television and Six Man).

No further trace of it survives — no name of its own, no roster, no booklet, and no result or closing statement of any kind.
