It will take place on February 26, 2017 at the Amway Center in Orlando, Florida. It will be the first official PPV event held by OWA as well as the first since it's rebirth. Every match will also be contested inside of a Steel cage. Background Rage in the Cage will consist of professional wrestling matches that involve wrestlers from pre-existing scripted feuds or storylines that play out on OWA's primary television program, Anarchy. Wrestlers will portray heroes or villains as they follow a series of events that build tension and culminate in a wrestling match or series of matches. On the January 23, 2017 episode of Anarchy a #1 Contenders match took place to determine who would challenge Mason Wolfe for the OWA Intercontinental Championship. Chris Dunn won the match to become the #1 Contender. On January 27, 2017 on OWA.com it was revealed that Dunn would get his title opportunity at the first-ever OWA PPV, Rage in the Cage and the match would be a Steel cage match. Also on the January 23, 2017 episode of Anarchy, Angelita Henderson became the #1 Contender for the OWA Women's Championship and will face Erin Spencer-Wolfe inside of a Steel cage at the PPV. On the January 30, 2017 episode of Anarchy during a Champion vs. Champion match between Owen Grady and Tyler Chandler the latter was attacked by a hooded figure before they were revealed to be new OWA signee, Hunter Gage who proceeded to lay out the champion. Six days later on OWA's official Twitter account it was revealed that Gage would challenge Chandler for the championship at Rage in the Cage after Chandler demanded the match. On OWA.com it was revealed that Women's wrestling history would be made at Rage in the Cage as 6 women would compete inside of a Steel cage for the first time ever and whoever was victorious would become the new #1 Contender for the OWA Women's Championship. On February 8, 2017 it was revealed that Abigail Dresden would not be able to compete due to an injury she suffered at the hands of Hazel and was removed from the match. Daniel Riley said that the sixth and final participant would be revealed on the February 16, 2017 episode of Anarchy. On the February 9, 2017 episode of Anarchy, Adam Dunn defeated Laifai Anafili, Antonio DeLuca and Aaron Keller in a Fatal Four Way to become the #1 Contender for the OWA Universal Championship and will face Owen Grady for the championship at Rage in the Cage.
