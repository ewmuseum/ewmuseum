Bio name | Alyssa Davies image | ALYSSADAVIES.png birth_name | Lisa Alyssa Davies born | May 9, 1991 (age 25) Minneapolis, Minnesota resides | Orlando, Florida ring_names | Alyssa Davies billed_height | 5 ft 7 in (1.70 m) billed_weight | 120 lb (54 kg) billed_from | Minneapolis, Minnesota trained_by | Sara Del Rey debut | 2012 Lisa Alyssa Davies (born May 9, 1991) is an American professional wrestler and valet currently signed with Orlando Wrestling Alliance (OWA) under the ring name Alyssa Davies. Davies was trained by Sara Del Rey and spent five years training with her after graduating from college with a degree in Business. Early life Davies is the youngest and only girl of four siblings. Davies' older brothers would always watching wrestling and soon after Davies herself began to watch with him and fell in love with professional wrestling, idolizing the likes of Trish Stratus and Lita as well as Molly Holly. Davies' older brothers wanted to enter the business however none of them were cut out for it and instead one day it was Davies who decided that she wanted to become a professional wrestler. Davies however wanted to complete college first so she would have something to lean back on if professional wrestling didn't work out for her much like it did her older brothers. Shortly after finishing college with a degree in Business, Davies sought out Sara Del Rey and managed to meet her at her final show with Chikara and managed to convince her to train her, even willing to pay her. Professional wrestling career Training (2012–2015) Upon meeting Sara Del Rey at her final Chikara show and convincing her to train her. Davies trained with Del Rey, waking up early every day and spending at least eight hours a day training. Davies learned the ropes fast and impressed Del Rey with how quick she was able to learn things. Davies would spend five years training with Del Rey before signing with her first wrestling promotion with Del Rey's blessing. Orlando Wrestling Alliance (2017–present) In February 2017, Davies signed a contract with Orlando Wrestling Alliance.
