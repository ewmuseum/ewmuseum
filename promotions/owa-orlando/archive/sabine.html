Early Life Artemis Constantinou was born in Athens, Greece to a Greek mother (Christina) and American father (Daniel). She lived with her family in the famous island of Santorini for the first 5 years of her life before the family moved to Tarpon Springs, Florida for 2 years before finally settling in Orlando, Florida. She speaks Greek, English and French fluently. Growing up, Constantinou was a talented singer, winning numerous talent contests throughout her high school years. She passed on becoming a professional singer in the hopes of being a professional wrestler. Professional wrestling career Orlando Wrestling Alliance (2017-present) Artemis signed her contract with Orlando Wrestling Alliance (OWA) on February 18, 2017. Personal Life Artemis is married to Australian Ryan Turner.
