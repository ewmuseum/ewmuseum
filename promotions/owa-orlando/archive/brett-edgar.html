Professional wrestling career Orlando Wrestling Alliance (2017–present) In February 2017 along with Shane Miller, Edgar signed a contract with Orlando Wrestling Alliance and will be apart of their tag team division. At Rage in the Cage (2017) during the Kickoff show, Edgar and Miller defeated Jake Anderson & Duke Knight as well as Drew Connolly and Eric Connolly in a Triple Threat Tornado Tag Team Steel Cage match to become the first-ever OWA Tag Team Champions. Personal life McClarin is close friends with his tag team partner Shane Miller with the two having both gone to high school together. Both bonded over their love for professional wrestling and got into the business together having both been trained by Brian Kendrick and Christopher Daniels. It was revealed in February 2017 that McClarin is currently dating OWA Superstar, Ricki Wilde.
