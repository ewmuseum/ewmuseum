Dunn is known for her time in World Elite Wrestling where she briefly competed in their developmental territory Top Notch Training (TNT). Early Life Rebekah was born to Christian missionaries in Cape Town, South Africa. She was raised there predominantly but would travel to the United States to visit family every summer. It was here she met Adam Dunn. They were friends all through high school and it was him who got her interested in professional wrestling. Professional wrestling career World Elite Wrestling (WEW) TNT (2013–2014) On the October 30, 2013 episode that aired on tape delay on December 5, 2013 of TNT, Bekz defeated Lena Galante and forced her out of TNT. On the December 19, 2013 episode, Northman defeated Sierra Dailey in the opening round of the TNT Women's Championship tournament, advancing to the next round. Dunn left the company when the TNT folded. Independent Circuit (2014–2017) Rebekah and her husband currently make periodic appearances on the independent circuit. Orlando Wrestling Alliance (2017–present) On January 27, 2017, Rebekah signed a contract with Orlando Wrestling Alliance. Personal life Northman began dating Adam Dunn in 2011 and the pair married on January 1, 2014. The couple lives in San Jose, California with their cat Marcel. Marcel passed away in 2014 and the couple has since acquired 4 other Persian cats. It was revealed on December 1, 2014 that during her absence from the ring, Rebekah had given birth to a son named Adam Christian Dunn Jr. Rebekah is good friends with Ariane Jacobs and her sister-in-law Ella Dunn.
