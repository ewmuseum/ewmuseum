Williams after graduating college and getting a bachelor's degree in Journalism, pursued his dream of becoming a wrestler. Williams started wrestling at small shows that had at the very least 20-30 people inside of school gyms watching. Afterwards Williams seeked out British wrestler, William Regal and managed to train with him a little bit mainly the psychology of the business. Williams continued to travel throughout Europe, sleeping in his car before moving to Ireland for a bit. In 2016, Williams decided that he wanted to go to the United States and learn a new wrestling style. Professional wrestling career Training and Europe (2010–2017) Orlando Wrestling Alliance (2017–present) Williams signed a contract with Orlando Wrestling Alliance in February 2017. Williams officially made his in-ring debut on the February 23, 2017 episode of Anarchy as the opponent for Hunter Gage as chosen by Tyler Chandler in a Pick Your Poison match. Williams would win the match after a distraction from Chandler.
