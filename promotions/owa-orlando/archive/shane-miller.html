Professional wrestling career Orlando Wrestling Alliance (2017–present) In February 2017 along with Brett Edgar, Miller signed a contract with Orlando Wrestling Alliance and will be apart of their tag team division. Personal life Miller is close friends with his tag team partner Brett Edgar with the two having both gone to high school together. Both bonded over their love for professional wrestling and got into the business together having both been trained by Brian Kendrick and Christopher Daniels. Miller is currently in a relationship with OWA Superstar, Sunny Ducruet.
