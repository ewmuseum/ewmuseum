Olivia had her debut match against Brooklyn which ended with a double count out as Brooklyn attacked Rhodes outside of the ring. After the match's conclusion, Laifai Anafili made his debut, attacking Brooklyn as he saved her. Personal Life Olivia is currently in a relationship with Laifai Anafili.
