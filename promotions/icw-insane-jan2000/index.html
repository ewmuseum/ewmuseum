Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

A federation styling itself Insane Championship Wrestling surfaces in this record on 18 January 2000, already an ongoing concern with no founding date of its own stated. Its lone surviving recruiting post billed the promotion as a running clash between old-school and hardcore styles, inviting readers to "come watch" or "take sides" at a pair of free-hosted web addresses.

The federation's own operator resurfaces eight months later, on 3 September 2000, announcing a wholesale change in booking staff: with the operator himself departing for college, the promotion sought two to three new writers to carry two shows a week and a monthly pay-per-view, with its outgoing staff providing direction on where the promotion was heading next.

No card, roster, or title history survives from either end of that span. Both documents are recruiting-facing, naming no wrestlers and describing no results.

A held record elsewhere in this museum already carries an unrelated, December 1997 federation of the identical full name, itself already flagged as having a further, unrelated same-named restart under a different operator the following year; nothing here links this federation to either.

No further document survives after the September 2000 staffing call, and no closing statement of any kind was posted.
