Ten Questions with Kid Nitro: Most Holy

12 November 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Most Holy Kid Nitro: Most Holy is a man who I've been able to watch since the beginning of his career. I'll be the first to say that he has come a very long way in this business, in just a very short time. He's won numerous titles in his career, including the NEWF U.S. Title, among others. Although I don't have a clue as to what this whole Mustard thing is all about, many people do, as he is one of the most popular wrestlers today. He dropped by recently, and answered the ten questions. 1. What is your most memorable moment in your career? The first time Most Holy won the World Heavyweight Title in the WAWA, especially considering it was only his sixth or seventh match of his career. Sometimes Most Holy can still hear the referee slapping the three count.... Although, when Most Holy finally heard the Mustard....that is a special moment too. 2. Who is your greatest enemy/rival of all time? When Most Holy first started, it was Mark Paris....he put Most Holy in the hospital more times than anyone else...the bastard. Brolly was always a tough fight, but other than that, when Most Holy starts feuding with someone, he doesn't stop until they are dead or gone. Everyone Most Holy feuds with has left wrestling....HBK, Garvin....the Fransoos.... 3. Who is your greatest ally of all time? Mark Paris, who else? Through good times and bad times, we have helped balance each other out and make us the champions that we are. As the LoS and now as 25:17, we show as a team that anything can be accomplished. Doomsday is quickly turning into one of the more brilliant wrestlers and allies that Most Holy has. 4. Which Title that you have won means the most to you and why? Which Title? There have been so many Precious.....Two time World Champion and US Champion in the WAWA, TV champion and four time Cruiser Champion in the AGW, the Cruiser and US Champion in the NEWF....But Most Holy is probably most fond of his US Title here in the NEWF, defeating Speed Racer for the first time in Most Holy's career means a lot to him. 5. What is the greatest match you have ever been involved in? Brolly Vs Most Holy in an iron man match....Most Holy lost the match, 16-0 but Most Holy kept getting up...not that Most Holy remembers much of it after the second pinfall.....But Most Holy is very proud that he didn't give up, that he kept getting up and trying. 6. If you could change one thing that has happened in your life, what would it be? Most Holy doesn't need to change anything. When you are bigger than the Pope, you have lived a near perfect life.....except for maybe ignoring the Fransoos.....and smacking Mike E. Clark when Most Holy had the chance....finishing off Shitwa...um...Shockwave Intl. when the opportunity was there.... 7. Who is your favorite wrestler to watch wrestle? There is so much talent in wrestling today, it's hard to pick just one. Most Holy enjoys watching Kommon Sense and that damn Mexican, Campos. Mark Paris, of course, is always a joy to watch, the way he can pick his opponent apart is incredible....Xloj...Most Holy doesn't know why but Xloj is always entertaining.... 8. Who was the biggest influence on you as a wrestler when you were growing up? Most Holy is about to date himself....People like Harley Race, Arn Anderson, Bulldog Bob Brown, Pat O'Connor....men who didn't need to jump off the tope rope and weren't afraid to fight bigger opponents, wear them down, break their spirits and pin their asses to the mat. 9. If you could pick anywhere in the world to wrestle in, where would it be and why? Most Holy noticed everyone else answered Japan, Most Holy has no use for those....Japs....If Most Holy could wrestle anywhere, he would reopen the Central States Wrestling and do the Midwest tours, where people truly appreciate wrestling. 10. If Hollywood were to make a movie based on your life, who would play you? Bruce Willis if they hurry, he is getting to old to play Most Holy but we do look alot alike. John Wayne would have to teach him Most Holy's manly demeanor but Most Holy would have to teach him how to talk....everyone always says Most Holy talks funny...Most Holy didn't know he had an accent.
