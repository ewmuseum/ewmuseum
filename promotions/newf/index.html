Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The Nitro Extreme Wrestling Federation ran its own dated 1998 season, from a June debut show through a December Iron Man Match, staging its own in-fiction interview column, "Ten Questions with Kid Nitro," across at least ten instalments.

The NEWF World Title changed hands seven times in the held corpus. Dirt won the belt's founding reign at NEWF: The Beginning (21 June 1998). Most Holy won it last, defeating defending champion Alex Star in an Iron Man Match at NEWF: Pandemonium (1 December 1998) — a title change the fed's own served broadcast closes on directly: "WE HAVE A NEW NEWF WORLD CHAMPION!!!"

That same Pandemonium broadcast also crowned a new Hardcore Champion (Blackheart Paul Vega) and a new Television Champion (KC "Loose" Cannon) inside a Lethal Lottery tag-team tournament card.
