Application 9.1 Singles Application ==================================================== 1. What is the ACW and who runs it? Atlantic City Wrestling is a fantasy wrestling federation created solely by [name withheld]. Being involved in E-Wrestling for a while Chris decied to open up a federation of his own. The federation was created in late 1999 and is looking to make an impact on the E-Wrestling world.
2. Where is the ACW based? The ACW is based in Atlantic City, New Jersey. Eack week the ACW will put on a show from the wonderful Hotel & Casino, Bally's Park Place. ACW will move around on occasions but it's heart is in Atlantic City.
3. What match writing style does the ACW use? The ACW is based mainly around summary matches. In summmary matches, The action is summarized up, Instead of writing a lengthy match. Though, The summaries are pretty in depth. Also, Every match will not be summary. Every now and then, I will throw a live match in there in the ABS(Action Block System) for a special occasion.
4. What are the weekly shows put on by ACW? Here at Atlantic City Wrestling, There are really 3 weekly programs the ACW puts out. A flash show, A weekly card and a weekly update. Here they are...
4.1 ACW SNAKE EYES This is the ACW's BIG show of the week. This is where all the matches,interveiws and latest happenings take place. Every Monday night from
Bally's Park Place in Atlantic City, New Jersey. The ACW brings you the hottest wrestling action around. The whole city is rooting for Snake Eyes.
4.2 ACW SPEAK YOUR MIND This is the show where the ACW's finest run their mouth. Every Saturday night,
Anybody who is anybody is on this show. You wanna be heard? Grab a mic and..SPEAK YOUR MIND!!
4.3 ACW UPDATE Every Monday following Snake Eyes, The ACW Update will come out. It will give you news on Roster,Rankings and upcoming events in the ACW.
5. Who are the personalities of the ACW? 5.1 The Higher Office The Higher Office of the ACW, Consists of many people behind the scenes. But only one can be called the head honcho, The president of this great organization...
- JAMES LEXINGTON - (ACW President) The man in charge of the ACW. The head honcho of the ACW. He makes all the decisions and his decisions are final. James is a former professional wrestler himself, So he knows how to run a succesful federation. James is 27 years old now, He started wrestling when he was 21. He never had the chance to make it big. He was sidelined with a career ending injury while attempting a high flying move. James suffered 2 hurniated disks in his back at the age of 25 and was told to not return to the ring. But he vowed to get back into the wrestling world somehow. Hence the ACW. James saved up enough money over the past couple of years, Along with the money he made in wrestling and now he is trying to make Atlantic City Wrestling the best promotion around.
Personality wise, James is one of the nicest guys around. He never tries to make enemy's unless he has a good reason. But if you get him mad, Don't expect him to just sit back and relax. He will take action.
As far as appearance goes. James is 6'1" tall, 245 pounds. He sports his long brown hair which he slicks back into a pony tail. His hazel eyes complement them nicely. A dark tan and and average build make up the man that is James
Lexington, President of the ACW.
5.2 Commentators Here at Atlantic City Wrestling, There are 2 main men who call all the In-Ring action for this organization. Here they are...
- ALEX GORDON - (Face Commentator) Alex makes up one half of the ACW commentating duo. Alex is one of the nicest guys you'll ever meet, Opposed to the other half of the duo. Alex grew up in the wrestling buisness so he knows a lot about wrestling.
Alex is 37, Has short black hair which he slicks over into a part and physically, Is not much to look at, 5'9" 160 lbs. Alex was never a wrestler, He just worked behind the scenes.
- RICK MARSHALL - (Heel Commentator) Rick is the opposite of Alex. He is cocky, Arrogant and basically, Thinks he is better than everyone. He will constantly make outrageous comments and try to overule Alex.
Rick is 35, He also was never a wrestler. He worked behind the scenes as well. He is 6' tall and 210 lbs. Many thought he could become a wrestler because of his size but Rick never pursued a wrestling career. Along with Alex Gordon,
These two make up one of the hottest wrestling duo's in the world today.
5.3 Interveiwers The ACW has 2 personalities that do interveiws. Here they are..... - STEVE MULLEN - Steve is the male interveiwer for the ACW. He is a humble and ordanary man who just likes to get down to buisness. He always tries to get the news out of someone.
- MICHELLE STEVENS - Michelle is the female interveiwer for the ACW. She is much like Steve Mullen,
Always tries to get the scoop out.
5.4 Ring Announcer There is only one ring announcer in the ACW... - JOHN MORTEN - The ACW's designated ring announcer. He does all the announcing. 6. What titles does the ACW offer? 6.1 Singles Titles - ACW WORLD TITLE - The grandaddy of em all. This is the ACW's top prize. Men from all weight divisions among the ACW battle it out for the right to be called the best.
There is no doubt that the man who holds this title is the top dog in the ACW at that period in time. Do you have what it takes? Step up to the plate and find out.
- ACW HIGH ROLLER TITLE - Hey, Second best isn't bad. This is the ACW's secondary title. The man who holds this belt should hold his head high and has the right to be called champion. This belt is often defended on Snake Eyes.
- ACW TELEVISION TITLE - The ACW's Ironman title of sorts. This title is defended weekly on Snake Eyes against a worthy contender. The man who can hold onto this for a long period of time, Deserves to be the TV champ.
- ACW CRUISERWEIGHT TITLE - This is for all the guys who are under 240 lbs. The ACW prides itslelf on a good Cruiserweight division so you can bet you'll see a lot of high flying action.
6.2 Tag Team Titles - ACW TAG TEAM TITLES - These belts are held by the best tag team in the ACW. 7. How many Pay-Per Veiws does the ACW hold? The ACW will hold Bi-Monthly Pay-Per Veiws. This gives more time for a build up and makes them more special.
8. Rules & Regulations 8.1 Roleplaying Obviously, Roleplaying is a must in order to win matches. The ACW holds one flash show a week and you are only allowed 1 flash and 1 flash ONLY. This is so the roleplaying is judged on quality instead of quantity. If more than one roleplay is sent, The second will be discarded. So I am only asking for one roleplay a week, That isn't much.
Second, No attacking of announcers,officials etc. in roleplays. This really makes no sense to me.
Next, There should be no swearing in roleplays. This is lenient, Anything you wouldn't hear on tv shouldn't be in there. And it would make it more realistic if you just [BEEP] things out. If you do not roleplay for a certain period of time I will give you a warning. If it continues, You will be jobbed and then possibly removed from the ACW roster.
8.2 Strategies Strategies or strats for short are another important part of success in the
ACW. It is HIGHLY RECOMMENDED that you strat for all of your matches. This is the tie breaker of sorts for close matches. Starts should contain things such as, Suggested Spots, What you want to do before and the match, Your approach to the match and so on.
8.3 Angles Roleplays and Strategies are an important part to a successful federation. But a even bigger part is Angles. Nobody is going to remember a roleplay you did,
Ten years later. But angles they will. Without angles, The federation goes nowhere. So, I can work out some angles but not all. If you have any suggestions are idea's for angles I suggest you e-mail me and tell me. Same thing goes with matches, If you want a match, The easiest thing to do is ask.
8.4 Other things All decisions are made by me. If you have a problem with a decision I made,
Talk to me about it. Don't e-mail me saying "I SHOULD HAVE WON!! I ROLEPLAYED
BETTER THAN THAT GUY!!". There is a logical reason for everything.
