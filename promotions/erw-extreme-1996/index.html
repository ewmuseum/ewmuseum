Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Extreme Regional Wrestling announced itself on 17 September 1996 with a structural sheet rather than a card: six championships — Regional, Television, World, United States, Extreme, and a tag title — and three weekly shows, Extreme Monday, Thursday Night Brawl, and Sunday Night War, plus a magazine promised out every two weeks. Its president, Chris, and vice president, Jason, ran it as a roleplay league strict enough to drop anyone who didn't post at least once every two weeks, filled any vacant title by battle royal, and capped membership at forty wrestlers, two per member.

A week later it announced a web page, admitting plainly it hadn't worked out how to use it yet: “The erw has a web page but im not sure how to make links on it for like cards and such.”

A first pay-per-view, “Big Bash 96,” followed that October, and the promotion's own later record describes it still running into November 1996.

No card, result, or closing notice under its own name is in the surviving record.
