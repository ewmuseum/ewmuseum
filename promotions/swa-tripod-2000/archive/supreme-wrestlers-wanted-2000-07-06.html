Supreme Wrestlers Wanted

6 July 2000

< a href=" http://www.egao.com/roomwithaview/swa/index.html "> Supreme Wrestling Alliance</a> Web site was just started lately. And is under developement. It should be done in the next few weeks.
About the League The SWA is a booked league.All matches are won by roleplay. I dont want anyone
IM'ing me to ask if they will win. or why they always lose without roleplaying.
By the way, if you do that, then you're gone. I hate whiners. Remember, BETTER
ROLEPLAYERS WILL WIN MORE MATCHES! Not only that, but DO NOT IM ME at all!
Email me the question or comment, but don't IM me because I'm very busy as of late! Creating a Wrestler New players are allowed only one singles wrestler. The reason we have set it at one is because of WRESTLER DEVELOPMENT. Most people will roleplay better if they have fewer wrestlers. The more wrestlers you have means the more time you have to divide between them. It's better to spend more time on one wrestler so that you can create more of a PERSONALITY for the wrestler. We will make an exception to this rule IF you want to have a tag team or form a tag team with someone using a different wrestler. We allow wrestlers to compete in both singles and tag team competition at the same time. It is possible in the SWA to hold both the World Heavyweight Championship and World Tag Team Championship at the same time.
Please use the application at the bottom of this sheet to apply to the SWA. The SWA is a FANTASY LEAGUE meaning we allow only imaginary wrestlers. No wrestler will be accepted if his name is the same as a real person. That means no using real wrestlers from the past or present, or anyone's name that is famous. No Bill Clintons or Ric Flairs. Also, we frown upon using patented finishing moves like the Stone Cold Stunner or Diamond Cutter.
Roleplaying Roleplaying will be done in the familiar fashion as in many other fantasy organizations, and that is Flashing. We will have a plain open mailing list where you can just reply to a roleplay at any given time. Becoming a member of the league will constitute the following rule, however. If you are an AOL member, YOU CANNOT TOS ANY MEMBER OF THE LEAGUE! If we find out you TOS someone, you will be removed from the league. Also, if you leave the league, notify us immediately so that we may remove your name from the mailing list.
Please don't get angry if you still get mail when you leave, because it does take time to get off everyone's mailing list. You can not use another wrestler in your roleplay unless you are quoting something he said in a previous roleplay. This means no attacking another wrestler in your roleplay. If you need to use profanity to get your point across, you must EDIT IT YOURSELF or it will not be permitted. An example is "Goo Boy, I am going to kick your
(CENSORED) when we meet in the ring!" or, "I'm going to kick your *** on Sunday!" Cards and Booking The SWA will hold ONE card during most weeks. All cards will be e-mailed to all members and old cards will be archived on the website. All SWA Wrestlers are promised one match per week. (A wrestler who wrestles singles and tag team will normally get at least two matches.)
The card will be held on Tuesday where full results and attacks will be given. Sometimes the card may not be written up with commentary due to lack of time, but some of the time it will have commentary.
On some Saturdays, a second card may be held. This card will feature only 6 or
7 matches. Wrestlers may be choosen to wrestle on Saturday cards by roleplaying, being involved in intersting feuds, earning title shots and defending titles. Saturday's cards will have full comentary, with the extra events. Saturday matches are usually set up between the release of Tuesday's card and Friday evening.
Every four to five weeks, the SWA will hold a PPV on a Sunday or a Saturday. Matches will be announced throughout the month before the PPV, and often will change before the card, as title changes often change matches. However, a final card is usually posted a week before the PPV.
Pay Per Views The SWA tries its best to emulate professional wrestling. The SWA will have 10
HUGE PAY PER VIEWS in the course of a year. These 10 pay per views will have
EVERY competitor in the SWA competing!
New Year's Brawl: A 30 Man Battle Royal taking place on January 1st of every year WrestleStock: The SWA's premier event of the year, taking place in February March Madness: Our big event which has all matches having a gimmick, taking place in March
King of Hardcore:
Our big event featuring a 16 man tournament in which the last match is a hardcore match and the winner is declared the King of Hardcore, taking place in April
May Mayhem: Our big event featuring a birthday party for the President of SWA Independance Day: Huge PPV taking place on July 4th, has all titles being defended, plus a 20 man submission/pin battle royal for a shot at the World title the next night.
Rock N' Wrestling Music Fest: Special performance by a top music artist, and all titles defended, takes place in August. Hardcore Halloween: Big event where all title matches have an eery gimmick to them, usually taking place on Halloween night.
The Texas Deathnatch Massacre: Card featuring many high profile matches. With a Texas Death match main event. And a U.S. Title ladder match. Christmas Bonus: The only PPV that is only a Netcast. Internet users can order for $10.00 and they watch it on the Real Player, usually takes place on Christmas Eve.
Attacks during Cards If you want to attack someone during a card you are more than welcome to, in fact you are encouraged to. Attack refers to actually attacking someone, an interview during the card, or whatever you want inserted in the card. There are a few regulations, however. Attacks will only be used if they make sense with the matches on the card and attacks already sent in. This usually means first come, first served. You may only send it two attacks per card, and you must write the attack yourself. Also, when you attack, you may hurt someone, but not so badly as to severly injure them or kill them. Do us a favor as well. We want our cards to look well, try to proof-read your attacks and write them up in the format we use to write cards. Sloppy attacks most likely wont make it into the card. DON'T USE COLORS AND SPECIAL FONTS EITHER OR THEY WILL NOT BE USED.
Titles and Title Defenses There are four singles titles and one tag team title in the SWA: SWA World Heavyweight Championship- Best of the best hold this title. If your the SWA world champion your the best in the business at the time.
SWA World Tag Team Championship- Tag team counterpart of the world title. Goes to the best tag team in the SWA.
SWA Extreme Championship- This title goes to the induvidual who is most willing to spill his blood and others. Deviosion of its own.
SWA Asian Championship- For wrestlers not quite at the main event level. Well respected title that is defended often.
SWA US Championship- Beginers who are looking to make a name for themself. No wrestler can hold more than one singles title at a time. However, a wrestler
CAN hold both a singles title and be one-half of the World Tag Team Champions.
