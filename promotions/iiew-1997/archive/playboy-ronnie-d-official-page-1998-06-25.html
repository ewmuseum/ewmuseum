What this is The organizer's own page for his wrestler, “Playboy” Ronnie D: statistics, ring entrance, background, titles held and a list of career matches, as the site printed it. The page's own “Last updated” date is 25 June 1998; the pictures are not served.
Special thanks to Brian B. of the AWMC for the truly awesome pictures of Ronnie D! #6 The offical page of the 8th best heel and 6th best wrestler in e-wrestling today! Who knew DTP and PRD were twins?! :^) "Playboy" Ronnie D signs with the Simply Canadian Rasslin' Alliance! "Playboy" Ronnie D was voted 8th best heel and 6th best wrestler in the 1998 RSPWF Half-Year Snapshot Awards. Thanks to all who supported him! Statistics Height: 6'2" Weight: 214 lbs. Alignment: Heel Finishers: The Heartbreaker (Knee-drop from the top rope to the chest.) The Ronnieplex (Spinning underhook face first suplex from the top rope.) The 69er (Sitdown Tombstone Piledriver) Hometown: Your Daughter's Dreams Theme Music: "So Wha'Cha Want" by The Beastie Boys Description: "Playboy" Ronnie D is a caucasian with skin of regular colour and complexion with long sandy brown hair that is let loose. He has a small tattoo of a red maple leaf on his right arm. The "Playboy" is of a medium build, like Chris Benoit or Eddie Guerrero, only slightly less muscular. He has a small birthmark on the right corner of his upper lip and very few scars on his forehead, because of his avoidance of any opponents that might make him less attractive to the ladies. To the ring, "Playboy" Ronnie D usually wears long, black tights with mettalic mother-of-pearl vinyl leopard spots on them with a huge mettalic red vinyl broken heart on the seat. His boots are black, with a small, mettalic red broken heart on the sides. He also has black fingerless gloves with a mettalic red vinyl broken heart on the back. Ring Entrance: [The lights extinguish, and pink spotlights pan the arena, causing the little children to scream. A red heart-monitor graph appears on the big screen, as slow beeps go faster and faster, causing the heart monitor to go faster. Suddenly, the heart monitor flatlines, as a piercing beep resounds through the arena. The long beep stops, and the flatline forms the words MARQUEE MAN The words quickly scroll off to the side, and just as soon, the flatline forms ICON As soon as those words scroll off, the words GOD OF WRESTLING blip across the screen. Then, as "So Wha'Cha Want" by the Beastie Boys kicks in and the pink spotlights converge around the top of the aisle, the infamous logo hits the big screen The girls scream and the rest of the crowd boos as the curtains burst open and out steps the one, the only, the oft-imitated but never duplicated "PLAYBOY" RONNIE D! He stands at the top of the aisle, posing for a pictures, before making his way down the aisle. The pink spotlights follow him down the aisle, as he steps onto the apron and through the ropes. He stands in the center of the ring, posing for more pictures, before the lights come back on.] Other Information: "Playboy" Ronnie D had a middle-class upbringing and had an uneventful childhood. Due to the monotony of everyday life, he became a skirt-chaser, a darned good one, too, and by the time he reached high-school, he didn't exactly have to chase the skirts to nab 'em. He never really got involved in sports, because he didn't want to risk injury. In fact, his only involvement in school sports was towel boy for the school football and basketball teams, so he could tell the girls he was on the team. He became interested in wrestling fresh out of the University of Toronto as a means of getting attention. Ronnie D is known for wanting to get attention, as his parents never gave it to him. The girls did, because of his body, and wrestling happened to be a well-paying way of getting attention. He set to work trained in several small organisations under different gimmicks until February of 1997 when he joined the Violence Fantasy Wrestling Federation and the Extreme Hardcore Wrestling Association under the moniker of "Playboy" Ronnie D. He drew tonnes of attention in both leagues, partly because he realised that the "extreme" style was not for him, and was different in that he used mostly technical and high-flying moves during his long stays with both federations. He won several championships in both promotions before the VFWF folded in June. He then packed his bags and left the EHWA in August of 1997 when he decided that his skills were such that he could compete in the NeCW. In the NeCW, he soon became noticed in the internet community with the release of 'The Best of "Playboy" Ronnie D,' a compilation of his various video flashes and matches. Soon after, his star began to rise due to all the hype surrounding him, and he was very soon courted by several organizations. Shortly after, he decided to join up with the PCWF, Pro Championship Wrestling Federation. He never got started in the PCWF, and neither did it. After a fiery backstage blow-up in the NeCW, involving Ronnie and the president's wife, he joined NOW as it folded. He has joined the EMWC and is enjoying success there. It hasn't folded yet, and let's hope that this trend continues in the SCRA, IIWF and HOWL, which he has just joined.
