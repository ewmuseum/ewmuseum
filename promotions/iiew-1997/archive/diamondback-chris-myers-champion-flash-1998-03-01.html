IIeW (The International Invitationals of e-Wrestling) — Roleplay

Diamondback Chris Myers — Deschenes Cup Champion

1 March 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(It's Sunday night [for the sake of the flash, let's play pretend], after the IIeW has brought to us a damn fine night of e-wrestling. The fans still can be heard through the curtain, filing out, but talking about one of the best nights of wrestling they have ever seen. Where we are, we see roadies, techies, and suits, all trying to break down the evening and set up for blue jays baseball.
We also see the Deschenes Chp, in all its glory. As we get closer, though, we see a few marks...blemishes, brought on by the sweat, blood, and tears of one man.
Diamondback Chris Myers. Chris Myers stands backstage....well, actually, he's leaning on a wall on one arm, forehead pressed against it. Sweat and blood drip down from his face, which has been stripped clean of the facepaint by the strain of the matchup with J.W.
Hardin. His face shows more strain then should be on the face of a 22 year old. Of to the side, Dallas, make-up still streaking her face, talks to the reporters, letting her man collect himself after the evening of hell.
Chris takes a deep breath, collects himself...and looks at the camera) CM: Hey, Mom, Dad....I did it. I'm still in a state of shock...right now, the magnitude of what I just did it hitting me like a freight train. I beat Simon Lebec...I beat Creed...and I beat the Outlaw.
I know I was saying it all along, but....wow. (Chris looks down at the Cup, which rests at his feet) CM: Two months ago, I get a phone call...and for one night, I'm atop the wrestling world. But there's something I can admit...it took three guys to make me the king of the hill for one night.
Lebec...I told you I'd win. I said I wouldn't fall for your mind games...I took the Devil's Advocate and the Pearly Gate, and I still won.
Creed....you're right. I ain't minor league no more. I said I'd beat you...I said you were overlooking Chris Myers...and that it'd turn out to be a mistake.
Don't you go blaming the bookers...don't go blaming the fans...look inward, chummer. And last...and definitely not least....Hardin. I've been through hell and back...and a good chunk of that trip was spent on Interstate-Jay Dub. I took everything you could dish out...I took a Cattle Buster right in the center of the ring...J.W. Hardin threw everything INCLUDING the kitchen sink at me...
...and I won. Hardin....I accept your thanks. Who knows? The future might hold a rematch... and remember this....this blood on the Cup? I think some of it's yours... But most of all, I thank the fans. When I was down, trying to pull myself up for that ten count, it was them screaming to get up that got me. When I countered the Goodbye, Farewell, Amen, it was the chant of MYERS that gave me the strength to flip Creed over.
Thanks to you...for one night....I'm better then Verhoeven...I'm better then Petrow...I'm better then Creed...and I'm better then Hardin. And yeah...I'm better then Ronnie D. (Chris cracks a small smile...one that gets bigger and bigger) CM: WOOOOOOOOOOOOHOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO! I DID IT! I DID IT! LOOK AT ME, MA! TOP OF THE WORLD! (Myers picks up the Cup...and hoists it in the air, one handed, screaming his thanks to the wrestling gods. A camera flashes...
...and when wrestling fans pick up the paper Monday morning, they all see a bloodied, battered, torn, and worn, but deriously happy Chris Myers....holding the golden Deschenes Cup in the air....and the headline....
BEWARE THE RATTLER!!!!
