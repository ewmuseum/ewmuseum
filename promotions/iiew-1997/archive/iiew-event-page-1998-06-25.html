IIeW (The International Invitationals of e-Wrestling) — News

IIeW Event Page

25 June 1998

What this is The IIeW section of the organizer's own site: a summary of the 28 February and 1 March 1998 supercard, its line-up and federations, and a news item announcing IIeW '99 for 9 and 10 January 1999. The page's own “Last updated” date is 25 June 1998; the pictures are not served.
(Click the above image to download the card.) The International Invitationals of e-Wrestling was a supercard run by Ronnie D. that combined the talents of several federations to make one of the largest e-wrestling extravaganzas ever! The IIeW took place over the weekend of February 28th, 1998 and March 1st, 1998 was an event that sported one of the most impressive line-ups of e-wrestling stars ever. Men like "Blackheart" Casey James, "The Angel of Destruction" Requiem, Gunnar "Grizzly" Gaines, "Crimson" Joe Reed, "Superior" Sean Stevens, Steven Spector, "Cowboy" Ken Curtis, Bishop, Serge Annis, "Sychosys" Joe Petrow, "The Perfectionist" Nik Nyce, "Outlaw" J.W. Hardin, Creed, Otto "The Butcher" Verhoeven, "The Savior" Simon Lebec, LOCO, Diamondback Chris Myers, Curtis "Thunder" Hansen, Captain Midnight, Mister Robinson, Tony Starks, Mad Dog Watkins, Steve "The Fury" Kowalski, "Playboy" Ronnie D and "Lone Wolf" Brody Thunder participated in the star-studded showcase of e-wrestling's best! Federations like the IEWA, the UWF, the SCRA, the EMWC, the MLWO, and the IIWF all took part in making this one of the most successful interfederational supercards EVER! [name withheld] has announced that due to popular demand, there will be a second IIeW! The Olympics of e-Wrestling will swing into a site to be determined soon on the 9th and 10th of January, 1999! So far, all that has been announced for the card is the Deschenes Cup tournament. More activity as to the planning of the event is expected to take place during the course of the summer of '98. Hopefully, IIeW '99 will be even BETTER than IIeW '98! Is that even possible? We'll have to find out on the 9th and 10th of January, 1999!
