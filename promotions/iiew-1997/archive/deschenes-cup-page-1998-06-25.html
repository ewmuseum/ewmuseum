What this is The Deschenes Cup section of the organizer's own site: the results of the first cup (1 March 1998, at the IIeW) and the second (7 June 1998), with captions for the second cup's wrestlers. The page's own “Last updated” date is 25 June 1998; the pictures are not served.
The Deschenes Cup; the most revered trophy in e-wrestling. Once every six months, e-wrestling's best are invited to participate in the grandest tournament of them all. Only one man emerges with the Cup. There are two main reasons why the Deschenes Cup is the most unique and revered tournament in e-wrestling: - It is by invitation-only: No one who asks shall be entered. - It is a one-night only event, meaning that you never know just how far you will make it, whereas other tournaments play by rounds, meaning you know your opponents ahead of time. With this system, sometimes even the best may fall. Listen to the Deschenes Cup theme song! Click here!
