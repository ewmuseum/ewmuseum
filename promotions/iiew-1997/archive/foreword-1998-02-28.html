IIeW Foreword — 28 February 1998

28 February 1998

________________________________________ --------------- | _ | ------------- | ELECTRONIC MAIL |_| | ----------- | | --------- | From: [name withheld] | ------- | To: The readers of the IIeW | ----- | | --- |________________________________________| Dear reader, Enclosed is Night One of the International Invitationals of e-Wrestling. (The IIeW for short.) Many of you have inquired as to HOW I did this thing,

so before you read the card, read this foreword to find out how it came to be

so that you can appreciate it that much more.

In late December, a few days before Christmas, in that hellhole ( :) ) known as rec.sport.pro-wrestling.fantasy, people were talking about how to

assemble several of e-wrestling's best into one federation. I thought about

it, thinking that it was a swell idea, and I decided that I didn't want the

hassle. Then, on the 23rd or so, I found myself a new idea. What if I

organized a once-only card with all the top stars? Couldn't be too hard, I

said. Little did I know...

After a posting about it that recieved a bit of encouragement, I sent

first batch of invitations for the Deschenes Cup. To my dismay, although not

really surprise, I didn't get many responses. Two or three, I believe. (Thanks

to [name withheld] for being one of the first to join... And a DOUBLE thanks for

sticking through it all the way!) I pretty much gave up when not many

fedheads expressed interest in joining.

And so, it appeared that the dream was dead. A Christmas break project

shot out of the sky. So sad, so sad. And then, one fine day in the middle of

January, my good buddy [name withheld] sent me a mail asking when this thing

would happen. I thought to myself, "It's over, Goetz. Leave me alone," but

wrote down "I'm getting around to spreading the word right about now." Oh

well, I thought, he'll forget in two days.

The nails were in the coffin of the IIeW. But of course, like any

success story, the nails are ALWAYS in the coffin and then along comes a hero

to pry them out. Well, the hero was my imagination, as one night I had a wierd

dream. I dreamt of a complete wresting supercard. Ric Flair battled Shawn

Michaels in one main event; Andre battled the Hulkster in the other main

event. Ricky Steamboat battled Randy Savage again and The Undertaker took on

The Giant. There were so many matches with top talents, and when I woke up, it

got me to thinking about the IIeW again.

So, I dug in that night and went to Dejanews and found the addresses

of some top talents. I sent out more invitations, and this time, teh response

was MUCH better. Nearly all accepted!

At this point, I was planning on a one night deal. I sent messages to

a few fedheads, and amazingly, the responses were great! I even had the MLWO

and IIWF in now. This was when I realized that the IIeW, with a lot more work

than I originally estimated, was going to happen!

By February 1st, all systems were ready to go. I got flashes from the Deschenes Cup participants, and all seemed to start going well. And then,

things got better. When fedheads saw the calibre of competition, they started

begging to get in a match or two on the card.

Then I realized one on the main principles of the card: For people to

find a fed that would be right for them. They'd read this thing, and on top of

thinking that it was a kicking PPV, would say, "Wow! The XYZ federation put on

a great match! I want to join!" And I said, well, I wouldn't want people to

have to sift through crap, so let's only allow in feds that I, and any other

self-respecting e-wrestler, would join: Feds with a quality roster, quality

writing, and a record for being fairly punctual. So, I had to reject quite a

few people, and then issued invitations to a very select few that I checked

out. In doing that, I was also able to justly keep the word "Invitational" in

the name.

At that about that time, I realized that I'd need a main event. So, I

invited [name withheld]' to bring Brody Thunder over for a match. And who would it

be against? Well, I looked up and down, high and low, and all the guys

that could wrestle matches against Brody that could be justified main events

were in matches already. So, what better to do than get "Playboy" Ronnie D in

on the hot action? That's in short the story of how the main event was formed.

As the event grew in size, I realized that with 20 matches signed, it

wouldn't be realistic to have it all on one night. So, I made it a two night

deal. When I did this, I realized that I had to find a new main event. I asked

Marty if Requiem would fight someone. He gladly obliged. I looked at the RSPWF 200, and realized that I hadn't invited a man that been at the very top and

very near the top, Gunnar Gaines. So, I asked Curt if Gunnar would fight

Requiem. The match was signed. Then, after having sent an invitation to

him almost a month prior, [name withheld] replied to my invitation expressing

interest in Casey James participating in the Deschenes Cup tournament. At this

point in time, the tourney was booked up. I told Mat there wasn't much I could

do, but if something came up... And besides, I felt sorry for a guy that

spends his day cooped up in a garage.

At this point in my long-winded story, I realized that two guys that

had never met each other was NOT an interesting main event. I suddenly

remembered how Requiem lost the IIWF World Title... Due to the interference of

Casey James and the Syndicate. That's when I invited Mat to bring Casey in

there to have them settle (or continue) an old grudge. And with Gunnar in

there, the possibility of starting another.

And so, flashing continued, and then we went on to booking, and then

we went on to writing. And then I took it all, rolled it up in a ball and

said, "Let there be e-wrestling!" And it's coming up. And then on the second

day, Ronnie will say "Let there be e-wrestling!" And it will come quick. And

on the third day, Ronnie will rest 'til the umpteenth day. :)

The moral of this story? For you to be able to appreciate the work

that went into this and why I can truly call this "my labour of love for

e-wrestling." Enjoy, [name withheld] IIeW Coordinator
