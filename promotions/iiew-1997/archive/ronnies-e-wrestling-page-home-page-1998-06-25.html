Ronnie's e-Wrestling Page (home page)

What this is The front page of the IIeW organizer's own site: a welcome, the news of mid-1998 (the RSPWF Half-Year Snapshot Awards and the site's updates) and a tour of its sections, the IIeW, the Deschenes Cup, the organizer's wrestler and a links page. The page's own “Last updated” date is 25 June 1998; the pictures are not served.
Welcome to Ronnie D.' e-Wrestling Page. You may remember me from endeavours such as the International Invitationals of e-Wrestling, the Deschenes Cup, handling "Playboy" Ronnie D, and controlling rec.sport.pro-wrestling.fantasy. :^) This page was made so that you would have easy access to the pages of the International Invitationals of e-Wrestling, the Deschenes Cup and more. Enough small talk, though. As they copyright in show business... Let's Get Ready to Rumble! Can you believe that anyone could be offended by this little muchacho? Yo quiero Taco Bell! You have to love this picture of Mr. Garrison dressed up as Ric Flair. Free him, too. NEWS [name withheld] sweeps up at the RSPWF Half-Year Snapshot Awards! [name withheld] virtually swept the floor of the RSPWF 1998 Half-Year Snapshot Awards! He took home awards for Best Event (IIeW), Best Match (Brody Thunder vs. Ronnie D), and the Vince McMahon Jr. Award for services to e-wrestling (For the IIeW). He also placed 6th for the Best Player award. His character, "Playboy" Ronnie D, also placed highly, coming in 8th place for the Best Heel Award and 6th place for the Best Wrestler award. Also, congratulations to [name withheld] who is the handler of the first Deschenes Cup winner, Diamondback Chris Myers. He grabbed the Best Face award. And a BIG congratulations to [name withheld] who handles J.W. Hardin, our second Deschenes Cup winner. He took Best Player, Best Heel and Best Wrestler. Deschenes Cup, "Playboy" Ronnie D, and Links pages all updated! The Deschenes Cup page recieved a major overhaul, with all the matches of both Deschenes Cups uploaded and pictures of all the wrestlers in this year's Deschenes Cup uploaded. The "Playboy" Ronnie D page was also updated, with new pictures of Ronnie D uploaded, along some new news. The Links page was also updated, with tons of new links. OTHER SECTIONS By far, the International Invitationals of e-Wrestling is my crowning achievement. Never before has such a large interpromotional event gone off so well. Many, many people consider this to be the biggest event in e-wrestling history. Check it out! The Deschenes Cup is a bi-annual invitational tournament which accepts only the best in e-wrestling. The first Deschenes Cup was held at the International Invitationals of e-Wrestling, with the young rookie Diamondback Chris Myers taking home the Cup. The second Deschenes Cup is already underway, with some of the top stars vying for e-wrestling's most prestigious prize. "Playboy" Ronnie D is the character that I handle. He is possibly what I am most famous in e-wrestling for. He captures, for me, all the glitz, glamour and style that is professional wrestling. Check out the self-proclaimed Icon of e-Wrestling's home page, voted 6th best e-wrestler web page on the 'net! This is a collection of links to several e-wrestling sources. Great federations, informational sites, etc. are listed here. Click above to mail me! Thanks to Trace P. for use of some graphics.
