IIeW Press Conference — 28 February 1998

28 February 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
____________________________ / _____ _____ __ __ \ | |_ _|_ _| \ \ / / | | | | | | __\ \ /\ / / | | | | | | / _ \ \/ \/ / | | _| |_ _| || __/\ /\ / | | |_____|_____\___| \/ \/ | | INTERNATIONAL INVITATIONALS | | OF e-WRESTLING | \ ____________________________ / PRESS CONFERENCE [We open up in a conference room. There are several men seated at a long table

draped in a purple cloth. At the middle is a podium, a gold-and-purple podium,

the colours of the International Invitationals of e-Wrestling.

The camera pans the table. There are suits a plenty there, and security guards

are all over. But the notables in the room are: Brody Thunder, "Playboy"

Ronnie D and IIeW president Ron Deschenes. [Ron Deschenes, in his mid-40's, wearing a brown suit and red tie, with

wire-rimmed gold glasses, and thinning hair, steps up to the podium. He looks

a tad nervous.]

Ron Deschenes: Welcome to the International Invitationals of e-Wrestling Press

Conference! We organized this so that you, the press, can get a better idea of

what this whole thing is all about.

I, Ron Deschenes, am the president and co-ordinator of this whole operation. I'd like to start off by introducing our two wrestlers with us today... [He points at Brody Thunder, who sits in a chair, scowling.] Ron Deschenes: In this corner, "Lone Wolf" Brody Thunder! [Cameras snap like crazy as Brody barely acknowledges the press. He seems

ticked off.] Ron Deschenes: And, in this corner... [He points at "Playboy" Ronnie D, who sits with his feet up on the table.] Ron Deschenes: "Playboy" Ronnie D! [Again, the cameras fly off as Ronnie waves a bit and flashes a few smiles for

the camera.]

Ron Deschenes: Now, I know that most of you came here to find out what this is

about and find out what these two guys are about, so I won't disappoint you by

talking too much. Now, if you'll focus your attention to the movie screen

behind me...

[Ron Deschenes steps out of the way to reveal a movie screen behind him with

the IIeW line-up in gold lettering on a purple background. He gives teh press

a few minutes to read it over...]

THE INTERNATIONAL INVITATIONALS OF e-WRESTLING ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ LIVE! Saturday, February 28th, 1998 and Sunday, March 1st, 1998 On Pay-Per-View around the world on from SkyDome in Toronto, Ontario, Canada -= NIGHT ONE =- [FREE-FOR-ALL] World Invitational Championship Match-up [UWF TAG TEAM TITLE MATCH] The Fabulous Ones [c] vs. Anarchy [EMWC FOUR-WAY TABLE DANCE -- FOR THE EMWC TV TITLE] "Precious" Pietro Sandini vs. "Crimson" Joe Reed vs. "Dangerous" Danny Dynamite vs. Dirt Dog Unique Allah [IEWA FEMALE FRACAS -- TV TITLE MATCH] Jessica Starbird vs. Current IEWA Women's TV Champion [MLWO KING OF CHRISTMAS FINALS REMATCH...PLUS A STEVENS] Sean Stevens v. The Man From Heck v. Kevin Legend [MLWO MATCH] Captain Midnight v. Daemon Dempsey [MLWO TAG TEAM WAR TO END ALL WARS] Matt Harris/Steve Spector v. Paradox/Tommy Marauder [SCRA MATCH WORLD TITLE MATCH] "Cowboy" Ken Curtis vs. Gansta Impasse [UWF MATCH -- CAGE DUEL] Bishop vs. Serge Annis [MAIN EVENT -- ELIMINATION-RULES TRIANGLE MATCH ] Requiem vs. Gunnar "Grizzly" Gaines vs. "Blackheart" Casey James -= NIGHT TWO =- [FREE-FOR-ALL] World Invitational Championship Match-up [DESCHENES CUP TOURNAMENT -- QUARTER-FINALS] "Sychosys" Joe Petrow vs. "Outlaw" J.W. Hardin Otto "The Butcher" Verhoeven vs. "The Perfectionist" Nik Nyce "The Savior" Simon Lebec vs. "Diamondback" Chris Myers Creed vs. LOCO [UWF WORLD TITLE MATCH] Sebastien Jericho vs. The Reformer [c] [EMWC MATCH] Curtis "Thunder" Hansen vs. "Wild Thing" Kevin Slater [DESCHENES CUP TOURNAMENT -- SEMI-FINALS] Winner of Petrow - Hardin vs. Winner of Verhoeven - Nyce Winner of Lebec - Myers vs. Winner of Creed - LOCO [MLWO TAG TEAM TITLE REMATCH -- NO DQ OR CO] Good Knights v. Rich and Infamous [c] [MLWO MATCH -- NON-TITLE] Mister Robinson [World Champion] v. Mystery Opponent [EMWC WORLD TITLE MATCH] Gabriel Whitecross [c] vs. Lord Byron [IIWF WORLD TITLE MATCH -- PROGRESSIVE DEATH MATCH] Steve Kowalski vs. Mad Dog Watkins [DESCHENES CUP TOURNAMENT -- FINALS -- 2 OUT OF 3 FALLS] Semi-final winners duke it out, not once, but twice -- and possibly thrice! [MAIN EVENT -- STEEL CAGE WARFARE] "Lone Wolf" Brody Thunder vs. "Playboy" Ronnie D Only $59.95 to see BOTH nights on PAY-PER-VIEW! The stars of e-wrestling

are going to shine for TWO NIGHTS in a row!

Don't miss the supercard of kings and the king of supercards -- THE INTERNATIONAL INVITATIONALS OF e-WRESTLING! (Card subject to change.) [Ron Deschenes steps back into view as the screen goes blank.] Ron Deschenes: Now, as you saw, the main event of the last night is "Playboy" Ronnie D versus "Lone Wolf" Brody Thunder, which is why these two men are

here. I'm sure that these men are many times more interesting than myself...

[A few chuckles rise from those in attendance.] Ron Deschenes: So, I'll hand it over to Brody... [Ron walks over and takes a seat. Brody Thunder stands up and walks to the

podium. He is wearing his trademark "EVIL, MEAN & NASTY" t-shirt and black

jeans, with a large belt-buckle.]

Brody Thunder: Fer those o'you puck-suckin',ice-kissin',cuzzin-lovin' idjits

up here who don't know me..I'm Brody Thunder. I come from the great state o'

Arizona an' I'm here fer one reason an' one reason only. [Thunder points over at Ronnie but continues to look at the audience.] Ta kick that lil runt's canadian ass. I been in this business now fer the better part o'thirteen years.I've been all

over the flamin'world. I've held straps in some o'the toughest wrestlin'

organizations in this sport. I fought pretty-boys, egomaniacs, psychos, dead

men, heroes an' legends. But I ain't _never_ looked forward ta tearin' up

someone's hide like I am this punk's.

[Thunder looks at Ronnie briefly,smiling an evil grin.] That's right...you keep on smilin' at me,ace. Cuz come March 1st I'm gonna

slap that smile right off yer wiseass lil face. An' I'm gonna do it on yer

home court,runt. I want alla these so-called _fans_ o' yers ta see what

happens when ya step through them ropes with a man who can plant yer canadian

bacon right through the mat. Ya facin' no goodie-two shoes 'Flash' Kauffman

now "D". Yer facin' the Wolf. An' if ya don't know the difference by now...ya

will when that flamin'bell rings,amigo.

See Kauffman wanted ta beat ya. I wanna beat ya _up_. So all o' you media boys better get yer photo ops today cuz after March 1st I

guarantee...

...he _won't be a pretty picture. Bank on it. [Brody takes walks away from the podium and sits back down. He glowers at

Ronnie D as he steps up to the podium. Ronnie has on a black vest and white

shirt, with a black suede vest. He also has on a string-tie and black cowboy

hat, possibly to tick Brody off. A few teenage girls who have probably skipped

school for this shriek as Ronnie takes the hat off and tosses it into the

seats.

He starts clapping his hands, rolling his eyes.] Ronnie D: Oh, BRAVO, Brody! Couldn't have put it better myself. Now, if you're from Toronto, and I'm assuming most of you are, except that guy

with the turban in the back, you know who I am. For you guys that aren't from

Toronto, you also probably know who I am, because the marquee man, the icon,

the god of wrestling, me, is on the magazine covers, the billboards and even

the milk cartons worldwide! But in case you've been hiding under a rock, and

it'd have to be a big one at that, I'm "Playboy" Ronnie D: the single most

talked-about man in the wrestling world. And some of you rock-dwellers are wondering why I'm so darned talked-about. Well, you gotta know that I've got the lady-killer looks, the right attitude

about myself, and everyone else for that matter, and all the talent in the

world. That's three things that SOMEONE here doesn't have, and it's not

Turban-boy that I'm talking about. Now, Brody says he wants to "kick my Canadian ass." He wants you to take your

pictures now, because in two days, I won't be a pretty picture. Why? Because

he's said he doesn't like my "struttin' 'n' preenin'" and all that... You know

what, Brody? There have been a lot of people that want to slap the smile off

my little face. And guess what? I'm still smiling!

[Ronnie turns to Brody and flashes a quick smile at him.] Ronnie D: Brody, this may be a cage match, but the story's still gonna be the

same. Guy walks in, wanting to stomp a mudhole in my ass and walk it dry, and

I run circles around the never-been.

Now, Brody, I'll admit is not a never-been. Hell, he's got "thirteen years of

experience" and "straps in the toughest organizations in the world!" Brody's

been around the horn... But after March 1st, Brody's going to be a has-been.

He'll need a walker to get around, maybe he'll even get his little Syndicate

friends to wheel him around. Because, you know, Brody used to be a part of the

Syndicate. What ever happened to that?

[Ronnie scratches his chin.] Ronnie D: Oh yeahhhhh... [Ronnie smiles at Brody, while Brody glowers forbodingly...] Ronnie D: Brody and all you media-types, take notice: I back my words. And

when I say that I come out on top all the time because I am the icon and the

god and the marquee man of wrestling, you better believe it. Get your ass

ready, Brody, it's gonna be fun kickin' it.

[Ronnie sits down and seems contented. IIeW president Ron Deschenes stands up

and goes to the podium.]

Ron Deschenes: Alrighty! These two are gonna get it on on March 1st, but

first, we'll give you a chance to go at them with questions. Ask away!

Reporter: Brody, do you think Ronnie's popularity in Toronto will play against

you? Brody Thunder: Well last I checked it weren't no handicap match. It was jus' me an'the runt. Hell all o' Canada can show up an' cheer'em on if they want. They ain't gotta wrestle me. _He_ does. An' I'm tellin' you he can't beat me. Don't believe me? Either show

up at the match or watch from yer laz-e-boy,cuz I'll prove it.

Reporter: Ronnie, what could you attribute your popularity in Toronto to? Ronnie D: Well, it's a well-known fact that I'm not that well-liked anywhere

OTHER than Toronto! And the reason they love me here is because I am Toronto's

only truly succesful athelete or sports-associated person.

They cheer me because they can be proud of me and my success. I mean, what, do

you expect them to be proud of the Argos, the Bluejays or the Craptors?

[A few chuckles come from the reporters at the last comment.] Reporter: Is wrestling gay? [Thunder scowls at the man in disgust.] Brody Thunder: Think o'that one all by yerself,didja? Next. Reporter: Brody, what's your gameplan? Brody Thunder: My "gameplan"..is ta give this arrogant idjit the beatin' o' his life. D'ya'll think this is gonna be some kinda classic scientific

wrestlin' match? I'm comin' ta put his hindquarters on the end o' my Tony

Llama's an' boot him the hell outta my sport. _That's_my_ gameplan.

Reporter: Ronnie, how can you call yourself "the icon" when you're in the

presence of men like J.W. Hardin, Creed, Lord Byron, Mister Robinson, Requiem

and Otto Verhoeven? And dare I say, Brody Thunder?

Ronnie D: Umm... Because I am the icon? I mean, you look at them: They each

have their good points, their courage, their strength or whatnot, but then you

look at me. And I have all that they have and more. I'm the total package. I'm

the icon. Hey, I like the sound of that... "Total Package." Oh, wait. Someone

already took that.

Brody Thunder [Under his breath]: Ain't never stopped ya b'fore... Reporter: What do you believe would happen should you win? Chaos? Riots? Brody Thunder: Y'mean _when_ I win...well the business will be shy one more

lil primadonna pretty-boy. Then o'course the ignorant canadian fans might

throw a hissyfit after seein'one o'their own got his head handed to'im by me.

Fact is he's goin' down an' yer lookin' at the man who's endin'the myth o' "Playboy" Ronnie D. Reporter: Do either of you feel overwhelmed to be in an event of this

magnitude? Brody Thunder: Son,I've wrestled before hundreds o'thousands o' screamin' idjits jus' like ya got here. Overwhelmed? I don't get overwhelmed. Is this

the biggest event o'my career? Maybe. Maybe not. But it'll certainly be one

o'the tops.

Ronnie D: Me, I'm not overwhelmed, because I can handle pressure. But this is

one of the biggest gigs I'll have ever done. It can be nerve-wracking, being

in the main event and all, but I don't get paid to piss my pants, now do I?

Reporter: How will it feel to meet friends from other feds? Brody Thunder: Well first..ya hafta _have_ friends in other feds. I don't. In this business the fewer friends ya have the better. Y'see everyone's out

fer themselves. Everyone wants to be on the top o'that mountain. Friends?

Yeah...I got a few men I trust with my back. They're appearin' at this event. I'm sure 'fore we leave here they'll be a few longnecks emptied an' a lil hell

raised. Reporter: Brody, are you gay? [Thunder again shakes his head while staring a hole through the man. He

turns so as to direct his full attention to the man as he answers.]

Brody Thunder: I'll tellya what,punk. If yer lookin' fer a date,ace yer

pissin' in the wrong stall. Why doncha ask ol' "Playboy" over there. Maybe he

can help ya out...

[Ronnie bolts up to his feet. He strides over to Brody, and Brody meets him

halfway at the podium. They stare each other in the eye for a good thirty

seconds as the tension rises.]

Ronnie D: You wanna repeat that, Brody? Brody Thunder: Yeah... I said yer a pansy-ass homesexual. [Ronnie looks down, and breathes in, long and hard...] Ronnie D: Yeah? Well, I heard stuff about you and Casey Jam -- [Ronnie doesn't get to finish his sentence as Brody's fist connects with his

jaw! Ronnie falls backwards onto the table, and Brody immediately jumps him as

cameras flash! Brody starts pounding on him, when Ronnie suddenly shifts the

momentum and rolls them over so he's on top! He plants a punch in Brody's face

as security pull them off each other.

Suddenly, Brody lashes out from the security and jumps Ronnie! He pounds his

fists into Ronnie's face and jumps up. He then kicks him in the gut for good

measure and walks off. The camera focuses on Ronnie cussing Brody out, barely

audible, and staggering to his feet...
