More than to handle "Playboy" Ronnie D again. More than to run a federation. More than anything else, I'd like to run the IIeW again and that's why the
International Invitationals of e-Wrestling, the only true interfederational e-wrestling event is back and better than ever!
Back on February 28th, 1998, the e-wrestling world witnessed an event of unprecedented magnitude... The International Invitationals of e-Wrestling. Bringing together 6 of the greatest federations of the time, the IIeW pioneered a new era in e-wrestling... The era of cooperation.
Where once federations stood as islands, they now united to put forward the best show imaginable. And what a show it was! Stars from all corners of the globe stepped into the ring with the sole purpose of demonstrating e-wrestling at it's best.
Little did anyone know the trend it would spawn, but soon after, several federations cooperated on cards, tournaments and more.
Quickly, other events trying to bring the e-wrestling world together popped up. However, no one event stands out as more historic, more awe-inspiring, and more of a testament to the game of e-wrestling as the International Invitationals of e-Wrestling.
And now, the IIeW returns to kick off a brand new year, to create and to capture memories, to showcase the best that e-wrestling has to offer.
