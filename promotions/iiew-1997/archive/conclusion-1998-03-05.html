IIeW Conclusion — 5 March 1998

5 March 1998
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
________________________________________ --------------- | _ | ------------- | ELECTRONIC MAIL |_| | ----------- | | --------- | From: [name withheld] | ------- | To: The readers of the IIeW | ----- | | --- |________________________________________| Dear reader, You have just witnessed one of the grandest spectacles in e-Wrestling

history. I just felt that it is important for you to know that...

Now, onto the good stuff! :) In my foreword, I bitched and whined. Bitch, bitch, bitch; whine, whine,

whine. I told you how much work it was. But what I neglected to tell you was

how great an experience this also was.

To tell you all the truth, this is easily the most rewarding thing I've

ever done in e-Wrestling. How? Well, obviously, to quote [name withheld], my

"legacy has been cemented." I'm not known to be that modest, and I don't

intent to start now. :) When people think of great moments in e-wrestling,

they're probably going to think of the first International Invitationals of

e-Wrestling. Maybe, if there's another IIeW, they'll think of it, too. But the

mere fact that this is the first one ever makes it historical...

I was also complimented several times. "Great guy," "incredible," and

many other words were batted around. That and the thanks I recieve make it

pretty worthwhile...

But those are jealous reasons. Another reason I have loved doing this is

because I love the game of e-Wrestling, and this will give all of us who are

ever searching for a fed a starting point to find out who is the best. This is

also a great way to rank e-wrestlers in your mind: Who put in the most work,

the best result, etcetera?

I also got a frightening taste of what it is like to be a fedhead. I can

say that I have an extremely higher regard of those beings now... :) Muchos

gracias, amigos!

But the most important thing I got out of this: I made several new "Net.Buddies." I used to be... Leery of big-time e-wrestling, because of all

the egos, etc. But, underneath the egos and all, there are some pretty great

guys. I've discovered that it's much better than death threats and

mailbombs each time you win a match. :) I want to thank all you guys whom

I got to know well, you made this all worthwhile. You know who you guys are. :) Overall, this was a great experience... I really want to thank each and

every one of you who made this possibly the greatest supercard of all time,

with huge participation, great writing and great friends.

Thanks, guys, I mean it... [name withheld]
