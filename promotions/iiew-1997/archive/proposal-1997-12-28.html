The International Invitationals of e-Wrestling — 28 December 1997

28 December 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
HELLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLO, everybody! It's me, your old pal [name withheld]! What am I writing about NOW, you ask? Well, read on. The International Invitationals of e-Wrestling. The supercard to end all supercards. I intend this to be a HUGE event where feuds can come to a head and where feuds can start. This will be a card where federations can put on their best matches and where interfederational feuds can start. I guess "card" wouldn't be accurate, "megaevent" is what we're looking for. But before I go ahead with it, I'd like to get as much approval as possible for it and hopefully make it almost fully RSPWF-endorsed.

How will I go about this? Let's put together a rough plan: - Discussion on RSPWF. Here is where I would like to discuss possible matches in the event, etc. Length: Probably from now 'til January 14th.

- Picking up volunteers. No, not as in PICKING UP. I really don't have an interest in other e-wrestlers... :) I mean as in getting match writers, maybe a flash aster, etc. Anyone? This will go on from now until February the 1st, most likely.

- Signing of matches. OK, this will go on from right about now until about February 15th: two - three weeks before the event.

- Roleplaying for the matches. Everything will be sent out in a roleplay packet once every two days or so. This will go on from January the oh... 20th to the night before the event, most likely.

- Writing of the matches. Duh. This will go on for about two to three weeks before the event. This gives wrestlers three to four weeks to roleplay with their opponent, jockeying for a win and two or so weeks to build up their matches, jockeying for position on the card. (Good heat = match later on in the card.)

- Actual posting of the matches. This will probably be sent to the fedheads for distribution to their members and the individual e-wrestlers as well as posted to RSPWF.

OK, that sounds good. Now, you're all wondering a bunch of stuff. I'll try to answer some questions now.

Q: How long will this event last? A: Depending on response, four to seven days. Q: OK, so you will send out one card each night. So that means there will be how many matches per card?

A: Depending on response, six to eight matches per card. Maybe even more if I get a REALLY great response. I'm setting my goal at forty matches over five days meaning eight matches for each of the five cards.

Q: Where will the event be held? A: Probably somewhere like SkyDome, Wembley Stadium or the Super Dome. Somewhere big, preferably with open air.

Q: OK, sounds great! How can I help? A: Well, one way to help is to give me ideas on special things to happen during the event. Here are some other positions that you can apply for, as well as application forms to apply with.

Other positions: - Independant match writer I want as many of these as I can get. These people will write matches between wrestlers from two different feds. They may also be asked to write a few extra things. You know, fun things, like ads, etc.

Application ----------- Send me:     * A few matches you've written in the past.     * What feds you write or have written for.     * If possible, a recommendation or two. People that have references from other players or fed-heads will have priority.

- Fed-heads Yes, I want to help showcase other federations at this event by offering them time to host matches at the International Invitationals of e-Wrestling. Your fed can hold matches at the event, etc. Of course, fed-heads, you or your writers would have to write these. You'd probably want to, as well.

Application ----------- Send me:     * A card your fed has held in the past.     * What your fed could do at the event. - Flash master I will most likely need someone to gather and send out the flashes in a packets. Not a really hard job, but I need someone else to do it as most of my time will be spent recruiting and communicating with fed-heads and wrestlers.

Application ----------- Send me:     * What feds you've been flash master for before.     * A recommendation. Applicants with recommendations will get priority. - Bookers I'll need about five of these. I'll probably hand pick these guys, probably fed-heads, but you can still send in applications for this. Don't worry, I WILL let you handle a wrestler, you just won't book his matches.

Application ----------- Send me:     * What feds you've booked for before.     * As many recommendations as humanly possible! I need to be able to feel confident that you are a competent and resonably unbiased booker. No recommendations means that I can't really consider you.

OK, now, for all of you (jerks ;) ) that don't want to do any of the above for me and just want to 'rassle, contact me. I'll probably need some form of reference. Send me a flash of yours and get someone to mail me telling me how great you are or show me that you made this year's or last year's RSPWF Awards (Not a nomination) or the RSPWF 200. Tell me if you want to wrestle in the tournament that is open to all or if you have an opponent that you would like to battle. This isn't anything final, I just want to get a loose card ready.

Now, here is what I have planned so far: - The [name withheld] Invitational Cup. Don't bother asking to be in here, I'm inviting the combatants in this tournament personally. And no, "Playboy" [name withheld] D will NOT be involved in the tournament.

- Un-named Tournament Anyone have any ideas for names for this tournament? It will be a tournament, open to all, that will be contested at the megaevent. It could possibly be for a belt that could be defended all over, sort of what the TNM World Title was supposed to be for TNM feds except for booked feds.

Anything else? Come on, suggest stuff! More will surely follow, and I really would encourage you all to participate somehow. -- [name withheld] -=- [detail withheld] Handler of renowned e-wrestler "Playboy" [name withheld] D   #32 in the 1997 RSPWF 200 Playboy's page:   http://[name withheld].home.ml.org/          RIP Seinfeld 1988 - 1997 As soon as they idiot-proof the world, a better idiot will come along.
