The new mixed with the traditional. The IEWL and NIWF are over, but from the ashes rises the NEW ICWF. The ICWF is the place for handlers that want to be a part of something they can be PROUD of. Where the WRESTLING still means something. Also, where you can still have a social life and not have to dedicate 10 + hours a week to keeping on top of the card.
Want excitement, want wrestling, want free time, too?? Then the ICWF is for you!!! The ICWF is a booked fed, but the principles are a little different. It not how MUCH your roleplay, or even how WELL you roleplay. Its how well you can develop your charachters, without dedicating your life to it.
The ICWF also carries the history of the IEWL and the IWF. A merger between the two has created this great new fed, and along with that comes a great history.
The ICWF Website will be up soon, but you can submit wrestler apps immediately. The Tournament to crown the ICWF North American Champion will be underway soon.
