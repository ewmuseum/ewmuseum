A Challege

4 April 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Adam_Benjamin — 4 April 2006 *A dressing room door in the backstage area is seen. The Sign on the door reads "First Class". As the camera man knocks the door slowly opens, seen exiting are three femals ladies.... The table is filled with bottles of wine on ice. There is a table set up with food on it.... In the corner Adam Benjamin sits lacing up his boots...
Benjamin: "Tonight we dance... Tonight we light the flame once again... Tonight.... "First Class" Chandler Maxwell enters the rooms soaking wet from a shower. Maxwell: Three ladies wow, you missed out on a good time Adam... Benjamin: "Look tonight is about Business... Maxwell: Yeah that is true but by god Adam I could not go out there with a full tank. I had to let out some of my juices....
Benjamin: "You just make sure you take Doe out. I can not stand that guy.... Maxwell: Doe is going down Adam... Benjamin: Great and I will win my match... Then Afterwards Sands will come out and give my Belt back...
Maxwell: Yeah that is right thing to do. I mean you never lost it.... Benjamin: But there is one thing I want do. Sands I want you to personally give me Karl Brown for the first PPV...
Maxwell: Wow Adam now you are really thinking.. What a match that would be.. Benjamin: Karl I know you are out there watching this. I am not sure if all the hype that follows you these days has allowed you to forget were you came from.
However I am calling you out. Now in any other fed. No lets do it again Karl. Lets re live the memories.... Sands give the fans a match to remember....
Maxwell Match of the year.... Benjamin: That it would be..... (Fade to black)
