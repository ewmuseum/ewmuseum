MCW (2006) — Roleplay

From the desk of...

3 July 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Linguistic — 3 July 2006 (This is an "In Character" roleplay for MCW's imaginary chairman, Jalen Latham...) Hello everyone. I am MCW Chairman Jalen Latham, and I this is straight from my desk to you.
There have been a ton of rumors running around over the last few weeks about the future of Major Championship Wrestling. Most of which is revolve around an insane notion that MCW doesn't have what it takes to compete on the big stage with some of the larger wrestling organizations. After Zero Hour, I hope it is painfully obvious to our detractors that our talent is some of the best that the wrestling world has to offer.
We came into Los Angeles and the Staples Center and put on a show that will be remembered for quite some time. Whats more is that we haven't even got started!
As I write this, the top members of our organization are headed toward one of these "top organizations" to decalre war on them. We've shown the world what we can do in house... now it's time to take out one of the big dogs as a tremendous act of aggression.
In the following weeks, I will be asking you to help me wage this war against the so called Ultimate Championship Wrestling. As rebels, we will take over their shows and make them feel the power that is Major Championship Wrestling!
On that note, we are pushing forward with our own card at MCW Arena. Center Stage 5 will broadcast live from MCW Arena. Here is the lineup:
Pulsar vs. Reno Kidd Rob Franklin vs. Joseph Justice vs. Nakita Dahaka in a one fall Triple Threat match Pietske/Insurgent vs. Dakota Smith/Blade in tag team action Sanket Desai vs. Justin Sane MAIN EVENT: John Doe vs. Bryan Storms (c) in a NON TITLE match I will be letting the roster know what is expected for them in the coming days. If anyone has questions... you know how to address them to me using the appropriate channels.
Jalen Latham
