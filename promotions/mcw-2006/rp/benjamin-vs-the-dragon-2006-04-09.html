MCW (2006) — Roleplay

Benjamin vs. "The Dragon"

9 April 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
JABolich — 9 April 2006 Special challenge match called for by Christian Sands. Could "The Dragon" be Karl Brown? Or does the boss have something more devious in mind?
Adam_Benjamin — 16 April 2006 (Fade into "Yours Truly" Adam Benjamin riding in his car on his cell phone) Benjamin: "Hey Chandler what's going one man?" Adam flips the phone from his left ear over to his right. "Nice did you get in a good work out? You have a big match this week." Adam turns off the radio in the car "Nice this is a huge week for us. I have a few matches for some other feds, so I will meet you up at the hotel this weekend."
Adam rolls his neck as he pulls up to a red light "Yeah now that is what I am talking about! We really have to let last week go and move full steam forward. Hey Mate I am going to let you go I have to go pick up some new wrestling trunks. Talk to you later Chandler!"
Adam hangs up the phone and looks into the camera "So Sands you are bringing in a Dragon to face me. Its about time! I have been waiting to face the Dragon for a long time Sands.
Dragon its I knew you would answer the call. I knew you would relive the magic we once put on inside the ring.
See you this week Dragon! (Fade to black) Adam_Benjamin — 16 April 2006 (Fade into "Yours Truly" Adam Benjamin riding in his car on his cell phone) Benjamin: "Hey Chandler what's going one man?"
