Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Linguistic — 3 May 2006 Kenny Harwood vs. Drunken Tiger Drunken Tiger faces another debuting opponent. Will his size and strength overwhelm another debuting opponent in the unknown Kenny Hardwood?
RP GOES HERE! RP Deadline is Wednesday, May 10th by midnight. TH — 5 May 2006 WARNING: The following is the uncensored, unedited promotional segment from Kenny Hardwood. The following footage will be edited and cleaned up for presentation on TV.

I see you, all trying to act tough because you're all big and all, well it ain't the size of the dog in the fight, and it sure ain't the size of the dog in your fat frigging belly either. It's the size of the fight in the dog. I may not look big, but I can handle myself, which is probably more than you can say for yourself.

Cuz there's one thing a wrestling crowd loves, and it's not some fat-frig foreigner. It's the blue collar American worker. I paid my dues as a worker, and now it's time to start kicking ass and taking names, and taking this sport back where it belongs. Into the arms of the blue-collar, American worker.
Alright you f ucking *****. Is that good enough? Director: (off-camera) Yeah, yeah it is. KH: Yeah, that's right it is. Yeah. I'm gonna go get wasted now. F ucking Pabst Blue Ribbon, that's a man's beer baby. F ucking A. You better hope I don't run into you after I'm done drinking it. F ucking *****.
Kenny storms off-stage. Director: Sh!t... this one's gonna need a lot of work. cut reel Linguistic — 7 May 2006 (Scene opens to Drunken Tiger in front of an MCW backdrop...) Drunken Tiger: You have got to be joking. As one of the top athletes in the world, I have come to Major Championship Wrestling to make my mark on the wrestling world and bring attention to the plight of my people in a divided country.
First they give me a submission specialist who can't get the job done, but at least puts forth an effort that I can admire. Not many Americans can do that these days. Blade made a valiant effort and I wish him luck in his attempts this week to obtain victory over a new opponent.
Then they give me this deok-deok-obneun saramiguyo? (Drunken Tiger takes a second to compose himself... He's started speaking Korean in the middle of his English promo, and he's a little embarrassed...)
My apologies. What I am trying to say is that the man they send me is not worthy to be in the same league as a Korean Traditional Wrestling Champion, much less one that won the honor three years in a row. This man, this Kenny Hardwood, is devoid of intelligence.
Mr. Jalen Latham, I am insulted that you would give your permission for this match to take place. I graduated from the Korean Ssireum environment and progressed into the K1 Mixed Martial Arts Arena, defeating the best. This man from one of your most worthless states in this country, New Jersey, failed miserably in his first attempt at Ultimate Fighting... and then came to professional wrestling.
Yes, Mr. Hardwood, I saw the tape. It must have been quite embarrassing for a tough guy such as your self who beats up "the most faggy of fags" and the like. You just continue to delude yourself. The end result will be the same...
Me using you as a political statement when I execute the REUNIFICATION on the back of your head. Maybe your government will take notice when one of their "prize" individuals is accosted at the hands of a South Korean.
The more I think about that last statement, the more I want to die of laughter. Kenny Hardwood - A prized individual?
(chuckles...) To my people in Korea, don't fear. This man poses no threat to our end goals. With you behind me, there is nothing in Major Championship Wrestling that can stop us from reunifying our country and putting an end to the stereotypes that we face every single day of our lives.
Stereotypes that rather than avoiding, Kenny Hardwood has embraced as a form of attack on our race. Whether the joke be about my body parts, the typical size of my people, our eating habits, or just the usual ethnic slurs... the man you see before you will systematically break Kenny Hardwood before the MCW Arena crowd.
Kenny, it's less than a week away. The two of us will then step into the ring and let our words take a step back for our actions to do the talking.
Then we'll see what that third world body of his can do to back up his weapons grade mouth.
(Scene fades...)
