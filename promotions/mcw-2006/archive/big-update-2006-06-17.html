Big Update

17 June 2006

Linguistic — 17 June 2006 I'm trying to keep everyone in the loop as to what is going on with MCW and the Zero Hour card.
First things first, I've got Adam writing up the main event and I have started work on the three-way elimination match and all segments. I don't think I'll be able to get it all done by the end of the weekend, but I'll be putting forth a big effort. What I am looking for is some people who feel confident in match writing stepping forward. Even if there isn't a match left to write, we're also hoping for character segments that further storylines. Contact me with ideas... I'm all ears.
For the most part, I was extremely happy with the RP turnout this time around. There are a few people who could have put forth an effort (any effort...) and maybe let me know why they no showed so I could be prepared for some sort of angle... but I'll work through it.
The main point I need to make to all of you is that as I have put a lot of energy into MCW, I won't be around to continue the MCW mission after the end of July. As many of you may know, I am a 7 year Army man with deployments to Iraq, Korea, Kuwait, and Turkey... and now they are calling me back over to the sandbox to kick Al Qaeda in Iraq's ass a little more.
I have enjoyed working on this fed and I have no intentions to let it die a horrible death. Some of you have put a lot of faith in the idea of MCW for me to let that happen. I am in contact with a few people to merge feds in some sort of storyline that will keep all belt holders intact with their belts... trust me, this is a REALLY GOOD THING! The talent pool will increase and there will hardly be a skipped beat in the overall operation of things.
What I need is patience from the crew, continued participation, and I'll continue to work my ass off to get you all what you deserve.
Leave comments here, or drop me a pm/e-mail if you prefer. Word to your mother! Josh Phenomenal — 17 June 2006 Josh, I have had no DSL for a week. Since you are leaving soon... Just keep Frankie Scott out of the picture. I really can't give you the time you need from me.
You are doing a great job. Be Safe. Scott True Life — 17 June 2006 you can change Justin Sane to heel
