MCW (2006) — Card

MCW Center Stage 4 (May 29, 2006)

29 May 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Linguistic — 29 May 2006 OOC: This is a very shortened version of Center Stage. I apologize, but work, school, and lack of RPing in most of the matches left this one short. Let's turn it up a notch for this PPV Ya'll!
Linguistic — 29 May 2006 (MCW Center Stage 4 opens to a max capacity MCW arena... The crowd is going absolutely nuts in anticipation for one of the biggest weekly cards since the MCW restart earlier in the year... Various signs are being held up in the stands, with the majority of them giving Bryan Storms and Chris McMillan encouragement for both their tag match tonight and the huge main event at Zero Hour... As the camera pans the crowd it fades out...)
(Fading back in to the broadcast booth, two individuals are visible... On our right is a smallish man with a mousey appearance, distinguished by poofy brown hair, big glasses, and an outfit consisting of an old-fashioned brown sports jacket over a white turtleneck. His look is almost professorial. The fellow on our left is the polar opposite of his companion. Fat, dark-haired, and characterized by a double chin and a receding hairline, he seems barely able to fit into the expensive black pinstripe suit he wears over a baby-blue silk shirt and a blue and gold tie. Subtitles identify the man on the right as Simon Van Helder and the fat man as Creek Wineberg.)
SVH: Hello everyone! We’re live from MCW arena and wasting no time getting things ready to kick off this week as we have a huge card for you. I’m Simon Van Helder and my assistant is none other than Creek Wineberg.
CW: How many times do I have to tell you that I am nobody’s assistant? SVH: I guess I just can’t get that through my head, Creek. In action tonight we have all eight participants in the North American title tournament kicking things off properly with first round matches. Let’s not forget the huge Main Event, either. The Zero Hour Main Event participants, Bryan Storms and Chris McMillan will be teaming up against a dangerous duo in Chandler Maxwell and Steve Marlay.
CW: You can’t forget that those two will have the just as dangerous Adam Benjamin in their corner to keep everything on the level.
SVH: More like, he’ll be there to level McMillan and Storms. CW: That too! SVH: We can’t forget that last week at Center Stage Bryan Storms and “First Class” Chandler Maxwell were supposed to have a match. Adam Benjamin appeared on the MCWtron and proceeded to tell Storms that the match wasn’t going to take place.
CW: Bryan Storms got a free ride to the Zero Hour Main Event because of the actions of Chandler Maxwell. You can’t tell me that he and Benjamin don’t have something up their sleeve.
SVH: All that I see is that Maxwell and Benjamin are trying to make sure that when the dust settles at Zero Hour, Chandler emerges as the number one contender.
CW: As much as I like “First Class”, I’m sure Dakota Smith might have something to say about that.
SVH: Dakota might end up with gold regardless. He’s got a great opportunity to grab the North American gold if he can get past his first round opponent... “Phenomenal” Frankie Scott.
CW: Frankie is well known here in California. He’s got quite a reputation nationwide for being willing to do whatever it takes for a win.
SVH: It’s time for a commercial break folks... when we get back, it’s John Doe versus Justin Sane. These two have been jaw jacking all week long!
(Scene fades to commercials...) Linguistic — 29 May 2006 North American Title Tournament First Round: John Doe vs Justin Sane (Scene fades back in from the commercial break...) (The camera pans all across the jammed packed stadium giving the MCW fans some television time. "Nann" by Trick Daddy and Trina hits through the PA system and the frenzy turns into a riot, as the arena is engulfed with boos as Justin Sane walks out from behind the black curtain with Shawnna at his side... The mood aggravates as Justin Sane grabs the hot Latina next to him at her hair and gets himself a kiss, but the two don't let themselves get disturbed and smile into the crowd on their way down the ramp...)
CW: What the hell am I doing next to you, Simon? For that mamacita I would start smoking again.
SVH: You stopped smoking? CW: No, but for such a goddess I would stop and start again. SVH: Oh, what a joker you are today, Mr. Wineberg. (With an athletic rollover jump over the top rope Justin enters the ring from the apron and Shawnna slides into the ring under the bottom rope and through the legs of Justin. While she stands up she pushes her back against his groin... In the same moment Justin Sane takes his time by doing his trademark pose and a row of white pyro explodes in the ring... The music stops playing and he gets himself a microphone and then again flares a nostril at the crowd in disgust before he starts to speak...)
Justin Sane: SHADDAP! I wouldn't have even come out here if there was an easier way to get my message across. But unfortunately I have to deal with this condensed stench arousing from each and everyone of you for a few moments. Oh, just stop your booing. Just look at yourself, hating me for what I am, what I achieved and what I own. Matter fact you guys paid to get in here and see me, but when I take a closer look at some of you, you should've rather invested that money in some Weight Watcher lectures.
CW: Haha... so true. Where did they find that awesome dude? SVH: I don't know, but the fans can't stand his arrogant ways. If you say dude again, Creek, I think I’ll have to kill you.
Justin Sane: I came out here for a reason... I want to introduce to you some friends of mine. First off all hailing from Ecuador and standing next to me in this moment the Latin Love Goddess Shawnna. Most of you pervs may know her already from the adult movies she formerly starred in. You got any words for them, baby?
(Justin holds the microphone for her...) Shawnna: No, because I don't f-beep-k with classless jerks. (The continuous booing gets even louder. Justin is just laughing...) Justin Sane: (Still laughing...) I have never seen so many bubbles busted at the same time. Well baby, we'll rock the sheets when daddy's done with business. Now, let me introduce to you another big friend of mine. My partner in crime, so to speak, when it comes to the oldest business of mankind. Some of you may have subscribed to his magazine. Ladies and Gentleman, here's a true self-made millionaire we all can learn from.... Hugh Hefner!
(Again Justin's theme music hits in and playboy owner and founder Hugh Hefner stands on the Center Stage ramp with two cute blondes in bunny outfits hooked in each arm... As always, Hugh Hefner is wearing a bathrobe and has a big cigar in his mouth... Hugh gets a immensely positive reaction from the MCW fans...)
CW: Hugh, where's my copy of this month Playboy? SVH: How about you just ask him later, when you attend to Justin's after –party that you keep talking about 24-7?!
CW: Good idea, Simon, good idea. I could get it even signed. (Commentator Simon van Helder just shakes his head while Hugh Hefner grabs a microphone on his way up the steel steps... After the two bunnies are done hugging Justin, both of the men in the squared circle shake hands...)
Justin Sane: It's great to have you here, Sir. Hugh Hefner: Thank you, son. It's an honor to have been invited. I've been loving wrestling since I was a kid and wish the best for the MCW and of course for you. Well all you may boo him, but I've seen this kid wrestle already and he always puts on a great show. And to tell you the truth, enjoying the fruits of one's life is no crime either. That's all I really got to say.
(Then he continues to smoke his cigar...) Justin Sane: Okay, you heard what the old man said people. So, instead of getting on my nerves get a life. And Jonny boy to you I have to say that our clash is just minutes away and I hope your family is sitting somewhere in the front rows, because we'll be creating a moment that people will remember for a long time: You are going to get your ass whooped through the whole stadium live on national TV by a television debutant. Isn't that great b*tch?
(Justin drops the microphone, which results in a dull sound echoing through the whole arena... Again the booing gets extremely loud and even "John Doe"-chants start loudly echoing through the crowd... "Nann" starts playing again as Justin Sane and his group make their way out of the ring and leave backstage again...)
SVH: Justin Sane better should prepare himself for his match with John Doe. CW: What makes you think he isn't prepared? He sounds more than prepared to me. SVH: Well, to me he sounds cocky. And the reaction by the fans just underlines that. CW: Forget the fans, I heard he has a great style of wrestling. SVH: We'll see if he can back up his big words. Next is... ("Hypocritical" blasts through the arena, prompting John Doe to dash through the curtain and rush to the ring... The crowd goes wild...)
GL: His opponent, and the number one seed in this tournament... hailing from parts unknown... he stands six feet and one inch, weighing in at two hundred and ten pounds... he is JOHN DOE!!!!!
(Another crowd pop as Doe hits the ring and jumps atop a turnbuckle... He plays to the crowd...)
RESULTS: The match started with Justin Sane immediately attacking John Doe from behind as he played to the crowd. The first part of this match was all Justin Sane, as he used every underhanded tactic imaginable to maintain control of the tempo. To John Doe’s credit, he was able to keep Sane off balance at times with amazingly daring moves.
The middle of the match saw the fans really getting behind John Doe as he got his second wind and staged a comeback. On three separate occasions it looked like Doe had the eighth seed put away, much to the crowds delight. However, he never accomplished this due to outside interference from Justin Sane’s girlfriend, Shawnna.
In the final moments of the match after a back-and-forth few minutes, Doe looked to put Sane away once again and went to the top rope. Shawnna came to her man’s aid but was sent to the arena floor for her efforts. It didn’t matter, though. She had bought her man enough time. He powerbombed John Doe to the canvas into a pinning predicament... 1... 2... 3!
The winner of this match as a result of pinfall... Justin Sane!!!!! He advances to the 2nd round!!!!! Linguistic — 29 May 2006 Blade vs Insurgent RESULTS: Blade came down to the ring, but he just didn’t get the job done. The broadcast team commented on how hit and miss he has been lately in matches.
Insurgent was almost able to beat Blade faster than Drunken Tiger did a few short weeks ago. A couple of offensive moves later, Insurgent hit his finisher - The Patriot Act for the pin.
The winner of this match as a result of a pinfall... Insurgent!!!!! Insurgent advances to the 2nd round!!!!! Linguistic — 29 May 2006 Kenny Hardwood vs “The Judge” Joseph Justice RESULTS: This match never took place. Kenny Hardwood was introduced but instead of Kenny coming down to ringside, Jalen Latham made his way to the ring. He announced the winner to be Joseph Justice due to a no-show.
The winner by no show... “The Judge” Joseph Justice!!!!! He advances to face Insurgent in the second round! The crowd booed but were informed that the reason Kenny couldn’t make it to ringside was because after last week’s match in which he leveled Drunken Tiger with multiple chair shots, Drunken Tiger got revenge in the parking lot, putting Kenny in the hospital.
Jalen repeatedly apologized for the inconvenience, and said he had a way of making things better for the fans tonight. Since Chandler Maxwell had been released, the match in the Main Event would not take place... however Steve Marlay and Chandler Maxwell had replacements that would take on Chris McMillan and Bryan Storms in the Main Event: Blitz!
The crowd went crazy at this announcement. The stage was set for what looked to be a great Main Event. Two highly skilled singles wrestlers in McMillan and Storms taking on one of the best tag teams around in Blitz...
Linguistic — 29 May 2006 Dakota Smith vs Frankie Scott RESULTS: Before the match started, Frankie Scott actually called the broadcast booth and informed him that he hadn’t signed his contract when the match had been made, and that due to a huge wrestling match on pay-per-view for another wrestling organization he hadn’t had time to fly back out to California for this match. He apologized, but stated that he would make it well worth MCW’s while once he made his in-ring debut.
The winner of this match as a result of a no-show... Dakota Smith!!!!! Dakota Smith advances to face Justin Sane in the second round. Linguistic — 29 May 2006 The announce team was stunned, to say the least. They commented on how last Center Stage was so action packed and this one was slow to really get started. The fans were in a restless stir... the writing was on the wall... Enter: Steve Marlay!
Marlay came down to the ring and talked about how he was in a funk but was really upset that he had been taken out of the Main Event in favor of two guys that hadn’t wrestled in MCW since it’s restart earlier in the year.
“They go by the name of Max and Jecht” he said. “I deserve to be in that Main Event!” The cameras cut from a still ranting Steve Marlay and back stage, where Dakota Smith is going nuts. He keeps saying over and over again how he wants to fight. Blade makes the unfortunate mistake of being in the wrong place at the wrong time and is destroyed by Dakota. Dakota continues slamming things into the ground and punching the wall as he storms around backstage with Rico in tow, then suddenly stops when he spots a television monitor broadcasting Steve Marlay live.
A smile goes to Dakotas face and then the cameras cut back ringside. SVH: I don’t know what to expect after seeing the look on Dakota’s face. CW: I know what to expect, and I think that Marlay would do good to get out of the ring before what I expect comes to fruition!
Dakota and Rico appear through the curtains in the arena and the fans start getting excited. After Marlay tells Dakota that he doesn’t belong in the same ring as him, Dakota charges the ring. For his part, Marlay stood his ground but it was a case of Dakota Smith being much too angry to feel any punishment that Steve Marlay had to dish out. A few seconds later , Dakota has Marlay up in the air anddoes his 336 from the seond rope, successfully piledriving Marlay to the mat. He makes the cover, and Rico makes a count... even though this wasn’t any form of sanctioned match.
Dakota celebrates and Rico informs the world that Dakota Smith will be the next MCW North American Champion!
Linguistic — 29 May 2006 Main Event: Chris McMillan and Bryan Storms vs Blitz RESULTS: During the entire introductions, the fans were absolutely ecstatic. There was an electricity in the arena that hadn’t been felt to date in the new MCW. After the introductions, all four men went to their separate corners and prepared for the match.
The team of Chris McMillan and Bryan Storms was just a little shaky at first. Both men were trying to show that they were the true World Champion, but that tactic worked very well to Blitz’s advantage. Blitz controlled the first half of the match with awesome power moves... eventually isolating Chris McMillan on their half of the ring. For what seemed like an eternity, McMillan endured the full force of Blitz’s offense. The crowd cheered Storms and McMillan on, and eventually “The Wolf” was able to make a tag.
After the tag, Storms kept Blitz off balance with lightning quick moves. Dropkicks were the name of the game. McMillan rolled outside to catch his breath, but made it back into the ring just in time to help Storms as he eventually was overwhelmed by the two mastodons.
All four men brawled for a few minutes with the referee trying in vain to gain control. Frustrated, he called for the bell.
This match has been declared a no-contest! To think that this stopped the men from brawling would be foolish. SVH: Folks, next week we come to you live from the Staples Center here in Los Angeles for MCW’s first Pay Per View: Zero Hour!
The scene faded to black as security rushed to the ring to break them all up... MCW logo appeared... The end!
