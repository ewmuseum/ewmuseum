Linguistic — 6 December 2006 Ok. Hit me hard with your thoughts on round 1. I put a ton of hours into posting this, and I'd love for you to at least address the following.
Was the card easy to read? Was the card entertaining? What did you think of the shortforming? What match was most entertaining? What match was most disappointing? What segment of broadcast team dialogue did you enjoy, if any? Did you like any of the three segments I threw in there? (Opening segement, Ending Jalen Latham segment or the Coming to LA"video package")
Anything else? Appreciate it. The no-shows put a little wrench in my gears but I think it'll be worked out shortly.
Hell_Fighter — 10 December 2006 I'll start this badboy out. I know that I am a little late. But better late than never. Was the card easy to read? Actually it was and for a shortform card it still had a lot involved and made everybody that invest time into it look really good. I was most impressed with it. I really felt the emotion and could actually feel like I might actually be in at least a live houseshow event (like WWE or TNA or something)
Was the card entertaining? Again, much to my surprise it kept me very entertaining for a shortform card. What did you think of the shortforming? This could really work and be a good template for how matches are written in the future. To have a quick matches but enough detail enough to show that a person's time doesn't go to waste.
What match was most entertaining? All of the segments is what sold this and put the gave this card its entertaiment value. They weren't long but still were able to advance potential storylines and feuds. They more than got the point across.
What match was most disappointing? Definately the Cameron Cruise Project vs Christian Soldiers match simply because of the no shows, but even then they both still looked good and you managed to explain what happened to each team without them losing credibility, and that shows that you know of each character individually and that's always a good thing for a fedhead. Their absense was reasonable and within character that was way better than have them "to be jobbed miserably" sort of thing, and that was a good thing.
It could of been worse, you could of fed all four wrestlers to be sexually accosted by the three foot midget with the ten foot penis!
What segment of broadcast team dialogue did you enjoy, if any? Frankie's mom...Frankie's mom...Frankie's mom was a fun little chant that I remember. Did you like any of the three segments I threw in there? (Opening segement, Ending Jalen Latham segment or the Coming to LA"video package")
Very fitting...I dug them. Anything else? Nah, I think that I pretty much covered everything
