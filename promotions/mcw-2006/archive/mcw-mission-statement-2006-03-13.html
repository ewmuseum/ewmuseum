MCW (2006) — Rules

"MCW Mission Statement"

13 March 2006
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
First of all, welcome to the Major Championship Wrestling forums. I'm Bolich and I'll be your stewardess for th- wait a minute.
The vision behind Major Championship Wrestling is one intended to emphasize 'new blood'. The federation is designed to break away from the trend of having the same group of talents jumping from promotion to promotion and instantly making it into the main event. With MCW I envision a fed where the talents who aren't in that select group have the chance to compete at a top-tier level - where the guys who are trying to make a name for themselves CAN make a name for themselves. This federation is all about giving the lesser known talents of the wrestling world a chance to ride the lightning and about creating a distinct, original product featuring wrestlers you might not be entirely familiar with - new talent, older talent that never got the chance to shine... the young guns of the wrestling world, really.
To that end MCW will not accept anyone who currently holds or formerly held a world championship for A1E, MBE, or any other FW Central fed currently hosted on the main forums. That means no EPW World Champs, no GXW World Champs, no CSWA World Champs, no A1E world champs, and so on and so forth. (Former MCW Champions are exempt given that the fed's current mission statement and its reality back in 2003 have not changed drastically. If you held a title in MCW, by all means app.) As a fed owner I believe that trading the same main event group across several feds cheapens the game at some level, and though a lot of these characters are handled by people I respect and consider friends I still want to give a new generation a chance to shine.
This, of course, does not mean you're barred from entering if you handle a World Champion, it just means you can't apply with that character. If, for example, I wanted to join I couldn't join as Christian Sands (a former EPW Champion), but I COULD join as Tariq Ismail, who has never held a title in his life.
That leads me to another disclaimer: Though I will have a presence on-screen as the fed owner I will not be competing in-ring. I've done it before, and if there's one thing I've learned it's that there is inevitably a conflict of interest when the fed owner is handling his own guy in his own fed. I don't want anyone to get the idea that I'm in this for politics: I'm in this solely for the fun.
So that's the gist of what I'm aiming for. Interested? Go check out our website: http://fwrestling.com/host/majorcw/index.html If you want to join, fill out an application and send it in to [detail withheld] - I'll get to it ASAP.
Danke! - Jeff, MCW Big Cheese
