Josh JABolich — 24 October 2006 I'd get behind you finishing the Finale. Two matches don't take a month to write. Linguistic — 24 October 2006 Thanks for the great post in the open forum. One of those matches is a freakin 60 minute iron man match and the other one I have been told would be finished "the next weekend" for over a month. I have no control over when the matches get to me, and I could short form it myself... but I think that would cheapen everything.
Once again, I thoroughly appreciate you adding to the discussion. The ending of the card is out of my hands right now. What do you sugest I do, go to these peoples houses and force them to finish up? I'm sure they are doing what they can.
Thanks for being a dick. Now go **** yourself. Your friendly neighborhood fedhead, Josh JABolich — 24 October 2006 You got a bug up your ass or somethin'? I think we're entitled to a little impatience given that we've been waiting since July for the card.
Try setting a deadline for the matches. Or better yet, try it this way. If the matches aren't getting to you it's time to put out a cardwriter call and give them to someone who can write them on short notice.
robishott — 24 October 2006 Hey guys, chill. no need to fight over this it's merely a game. Linguistic — 24 October 2006 Trust me... I've been there. Three guys bailed so I wrote those matches plus the ones I already planned to write. I just don't like people sharp shooting my ass. Trust me, I bust my ass for this... but you should know this.
I don't call it a bug in my ass when somebody is insulting me almost two weeks after I wrote this post and between now and then didn't send me any messages asking me what was going on or offering any solutions (in private, perferably). God damn, what did I ever do to you?
We'll get this done. I've even got your segment on stand by for when the matches get to me.
JABolich — 25 October 2006 You seem to have forgotten that I used to run EPW as well as this fed until real life intervened, so don't lecture me about working hard to get the cards out. Back in the olden days I used to pump out cards in five days with no help whatsoever. Believe me, I know all about hauling butt to get a card posted (and it's part of why I'm staying gone: I don't have the motivation to spend hours writing up elaborate cards that'll be read by maybe six people).
If people really are jumping ship... I'm not really sure what to tell you. And complaining about a three-month wait on the card isn't so much insulting you as venting about my wanting to see how the show ends.
Linguistic — 25 October 2006 I said it before... my problem is you sharpshooting. I've seen it elsewhere with you... only a post when there is something smart ass or negative to say. Dude, nobody ****in railroaded you when you ducked out. Have a little respect and do the same.
I'd get behind you finishing the Finale. Two matches don't take a month to write. Tell me that was warranted without so much as a PM to let me know what you were thinking. I stepped down when I realized my life was unpredictable and it would be unfair to the handlers. I just thought I'd throw a big final show together for them.
I would sit here and defend my life's schedule, but working 14 hours (yes, that is correct) a day speaks for itself. I find time to do this because I need a hobby away from work and family. Condescending remarks do nothing but piss me off.
All I asked was a damn PM before you sent that message which was, once again, almost two weeks after I posted the message to begin with.
As for my match writers... Adam, keep working on the card and send me an update when you can. You haven't contacted me in a week about the card so I figure I need the update.
Karl, don't sweat this. You're writing a LOOOOONNNNNNGGGGG match and some of us still understand that if we have busy lives that means others have busy lives as well. Thanks for the routine updates as to the status.
Anybody else that has a problem with it, keep it to yourselves because I can't promise a very friendly response. Everyone else who has actively participated in this card has voiced concerns privately and we have all come to a resolution... which is why I posted the rest of the card up and to the double-main event.
Josh Thread closed in respect for MCW and its handlers. This is not how I want to end MCW in the minds of everyone around. Trust me, Karl Brown vs Adam Benjamin will be well worth the wait and we sincerely apologize for it for the billionth time.
