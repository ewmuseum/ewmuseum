It's a shame that Nakita and Karla went to the double countout, especially with the incredible RP battle they had .. but as a fedhead I know sometimes its very difficult to pick a winner and that this is the only route to go (I'm assuming as such although it could have been an angle submitted by the two of them). So while it may be disappointing to not have a winner, the mere RP war between them definitely made this round something to watch.
I loved the line about how Creek had yet to have been proven that Lindsay Troy was indeed a female.. one of my favorite lines I have read all year by an announcer.
Congrats to NAPW for having two teams going into the next round.. I don't get much of a chance to read their stuff outside of the TEAM and MCW events, but they've done incredibly well in both events and it really makes me want to start following their product more often. (And congrats to NAPW for their placing in Holzer's fed of the year standings ... you know you're hot stuff when Holzer ranks you ahead of MBE, A1E and AWC ... take that and the fact that D! and New and Improved DX took home his top places in two other categories and you know the league is smoking!)
You are much more forgiving about no showing than I am by having the teams not show up... when someone no shows for me I job them horribly.. maybe yours is the more diplomatic approach.
I'm looking forward to the semi's and the finals.. NEW's going to try to get a tag division started up soon, so maybe we'll throw a couple teams in the next go around.
Linguistic — 1 January 2007 Thanks for the feedback! It really bolstered my self-confidence. After this tournament is over with, I plan on taking a break from e-fedding to make sure my life and career are all in order. I'll attack the e-fed scene right afterwards!
I'm hoping that the match between Nakita and Karla served to continue feuds in other feds. From what I was getting from the handlers involved, that seemed to be the idea behind it. I hope that the three of them will get involved.
I'm throwing in a special match for the next round. Announcements will be going up with the RP threads!
