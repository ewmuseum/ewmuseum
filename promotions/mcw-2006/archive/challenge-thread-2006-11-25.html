Linguistic — 25 November 2006 This is the thread for any of you to issue and accept challenges, or to inform me of matches that you and the opponent have already agreed upon.
None of these matches will be booked for the first round, as there are already 8 matches set up. However, we will need a few matches to fill out the quarter finals, semis, and finals.
Any match you can dream up will be considered. I am especially interested in Survivor Series style matches (dream matchups or interfed rivalry matches) or classic one on one matches for sheer RP-competition.
All matches will be judged by myself and at least two other people. Keep in mind that the results will be shortformed, but in a very well written way on an actual card.
