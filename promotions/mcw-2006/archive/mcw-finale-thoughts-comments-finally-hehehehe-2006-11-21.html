MCW Finale Thoughts & Comments (Finally..hehehehe!)

21 November 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hell_Fighter — 21 November 2006 Match of the Night? Segment of the Night? Angle of the Night? Funniest Moment of the Night? Impending/forshadowing future Feuds of the Night? Shocking Moment of the Night? Face of the Night? Heel of the Night? Overall Thoughts Hell_Fighter — 21 November 2006 To start things out and get this ball rolling I will start this survey out. Match of the Night? Karl Brown verses Adam Benjamin definately was the match that was worth the wait and made the fans and MCW allumni leave home happy. Both men should be proud, then to have the match end in a draw and award both men with the title of MCW LEGEND was a good way to end FINALE on a good memorable note.
Segment of the Night? Christian Sands segment was a good one Angle of the Night? No real angle that I can spot other than Dahaka/Franklin and you can always see what transpires from that in UCW.
Funniest Moment of the Night? Professor Tremendous verses Roderick McRatrick match was very entertaining. Especially with Team Tremendous getting involved and helping with the assist.
Impending/forshadowing future Feuds of the Night? Since this was a Finale no real feud stuck out since most of them were "settled" or resolved.
Shocking Moment of the Night? Christian Sands NOT announcing his big return to active in-ring competition perhaps. Face of the Night? Benjamin & Brown I think share in this honor. Heel of the Night? Professor Tremendous because Team Tremendous saved his ass and yet he will take all of the credit.
Overall Thoughts Great match to remember MCW all around and everybody who either participated or just plain helped out should be proud of this. It was a great occasion for everybody and we should remember it fondly.
All of the match writers involved should get the MVP honors for finally getting this badboy puppy out. It was worth the wait.
Linguistic — 21 November 2006 Match of the Night? The mo'fo'n MAIN EVENT! GREAT WORK KARL! Segment of the Night? I don't know where to even start. Having Christian Sands drop a segment for the card was awesome... and I thought the Jalen Latham closing was cool.
Angle of the Night? The Professor Tremendous CSWA Unified Title fiasco. Roderick McRatrick in a dress will do that, ya know.
I also enjoyed my little brainchild for Sarge/Drunken Tiger teaming against "The Brooklyn Brawler" and his mystery partner.
Funniest Moment of the Night? The fact that James Irish wasn't the comedic focal point of a match he was involved in. Impending/forshadowing future Feuds of the Night? Had it been out any sooner, the three-way elimination match could have foreshadowed a great feud in UCW between Dakota and Storms.
Also, I know that Rob and Nakita are tag team partners... but you can see with that whole foursome that there is animosity that will eventually come to a head. Great job by both.
Shocking Moment of the Night? The 60-minute match. I really think that was worth the wait. Face of the Night? William Hung? Heel of the Night? I think Christian Sands at the end of the card did a great job of playing the heel. Overall Thoughts God guys, I really wanted to get this one out to you a lot sooner than this. You all deserved so much better than that. We've already been here before, so I'll just close that by saying that I'm sorry.
I want to let you all know that your really friggin good at this whole e-fedding thing. Rob Franklin improves every day thanks to everyone helping him out, and I take pride in knowing that the whole lot of you have numerous belts in your individual characters' futures.
Keep in touch. You guys know I'm trying to put some stuff together under the MCW banner, and you are always welcome to participate and help out.
Adam_Benjamin — 22 November 2006 I am trying to type, but I am still am feeling the chills of reading a efed match. Karl, I personally want to thank you. You wrote my dream match.
I felt like I was there, it was Flair vs. Steamboat, Milinko vs. Eddy, Danileson vs. London, Samoa Joe vs. Angle, or CM Punk vs Chris Hero
Personally I have been a little down with e fedding, however after reading that, I can honestly say the fire is back.
Thank you ("Yours Truly" Adam Benjamin looks down at the MCW logo, and in honor takes his boots off and leaves them in the ring.)
Hell_Fighter — 26 November 2006 [quoting] Linguistic said: > Funniest Moment of the Night? > > The fact that James Irish wasn't the comedic focal point of a match he was involved in. [end quote] Sorry about this Josh, James Irish, and everybody, but I gotta take full responsibility for this.
At the time when I was writing this match. I was putting so much focus on McRatrick and Team Tremendous, that I actually forgot that Irish was the special guest referee until like three/fourths of the way through the match and then I was like "Oh CRAP!" I can't believe that I forgot. So sadly I went back and tried to correct it. I didn't get a chance to do much with Irish after that simply because I was in the middle of one of my writing zones. I tried to make it up as much as possible but I kind of thought that having Irish as rather laxed in enforcing the rules was kind of funny in itself (or at least that was what I was trying to convey)
I do want to apologize for not getting James Irish more involved. I am sorry guys.
