MCW (2006) — News

Just letting you know...

24 May 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Linguistic — 24 May 2006 I'm sitting on 24 hour duty here at my unit, so I can't work on the card tonight... but it's in the works. I'm hoping to have it all done by Sunday.
Anyway, I just wanted to let you know that because of lack of time we have lost two characters in Chandler Maxwell and Kenny Hardwood. In the near future that has know major effects on MCW as we are still in good shape for the PPV and immediately after to work angles, feuds, and titles.
What that does mean is that in the long term we are going to need both a little more participation from the roster that is active right now and to also get you guys out there trying to actively recruit for us. You all should know by now that I am hard worker who will do what he can to make this fed great... it can only be as good as you want to make it though.
I did want to say that we had nice participation for this last card and I'm hoping to make Zero Hour one hell of a first pay per view.
Let's do this! Josh True Life — 25 May 2006 Aight, sir! Let's do it! I'll try to find some potential members! Linguistic — 26 May 2006 Final Word The Army has really handed it to me this week, as I just came off of 24 hour duty and I was just told to pull another 24 hour duty on Saturday. This being said, I still plan on getting this card out by the end of the weekend.
However, since I am not a huge fan of full write up matches to begin with I have decided to short form the matches on this particular card in order to continue our progress toward the pay-per-view and of course to keep everyone involved.
The Pay-Per-View WILL be a full write up and I aim to make it the best card so far in MCW since I have taken over.
I have everyone's ideas for match segments and angles for this Center Stage. They will be full write ups... the only thing that will be short form will be the results.
I promise it will still be an enjoyable read! Josh
