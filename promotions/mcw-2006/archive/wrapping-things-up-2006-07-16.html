Wrapping things up...

16 July 2006
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Linguistic — 16 July 2006 With the MCW merger with UCW in full swing, and Center Stage 5 posted, I thought it would be a good time to drop a few notes by everyone to make sure everyone is tracking.
Personal [detail withheld] Center Stage 5 I was very pleased with how everything turned out with this past Center Stage. Most everyone RP'd, which is a step in the right direction. Not only did they RP, but two of the matches where really hard for me to decide the winner.
I would like to take this time to applaud the handlers of Nakita Dahaka, Rob Franklin, Joseph Justice, Sanket Desai, and Justin Sane for the awesome work on RP'ing.
With the merger, there is no doubt in my mind that MCW competitors can hang with their UCW counterparts.
Special thanks goes out to Patrick (handler of Dahaka) and Shane (owner of UCW) for their assistance with writing matches for the card.
MCW's Future While MCW will be closing it's doors in the storyline, I'm hoping to hold a special show within the next month or so that properly sends all of the wrestlers off to their futures in UCW. Ideas are welcome, but I'm thinking that I will hold some sort of SUPERSHOW along the same lines of TEAM's currently being produced SUPERSHOW.
It will be MCW's farewell, and will be open to any character that chooses to participate. Obviously, I will be focusing a lot of attention on current and former MCW superstars.
The immediate future will be a posted lineup for a special House Show with short formed results to keep everyone involved till their big matches. This should help people with RP skills and make for some sort of entertainment.
Firings This being my sixth show posted in the last couple of months, I think it's safe to fire some of the dead weight before it contaminates the rest of the roster. Therefore, the following characters are hereby fired.
Steve Marlay Pulsar Reno Kidd John Doe Blade I am deleting their profiles from the database as we speak. Thanks Everyone has been a blast to work with and I apologize that my life interfered with our all having tons of fun. This was my first attempt to run an E-fed, and I'd like to think that in a short period of time I had some success. I tried a few things that haven't been tried often to my knowledge such as more short forming of matches, more of a ranking system, a proper fed cycle with faster turnaround for cards, and more interaction between handlers and staff.
Stay tuned folks, as I am helping Shane behind the scenes with everything and you have definitely not seen the end of my involvement in e-fedding. I'm around!
robishott — 17 July 2006 Good to hear you aren't leaving as soon as expected man.
