JABolich — 6 April 2006 http://fwrestling.com/host/majorcw/tv/centerstage01.html The results for the first show are up. If you check the TV Guide section of the main site you'll find a set of standings for the title scoring.
I'll be posting the new lineup on the weekend, most likely. I want to allow a couple of days for people to read the card and hopefully pork up the roster a bit. In the meantime, feel free to offer feedback!
Linguistic — 6 April 2006 This was a decent card and a good way to get MCW's feet wet again, in my opinion. The swerve at the end of the night with Benjamin was really entertaining. I can't wait to see what's next.
Josh (The Sergeant) Mister Dread — 9 April 2006 It was nice to see a card come out when promised. Good card, I can't wait to see what happens when the roster fleshes out a little. Of course, by then I'll have the MCW heavyweight strap around my waist.
