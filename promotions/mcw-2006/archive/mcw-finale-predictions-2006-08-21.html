MCW Finale PREDICTIONS...

21 August 2006

Hell_Fighter — 21 August 2006 While we wait on the card to be produced... Lets look into our crystal balls and guess who we think might win what and guess what will happen.
This is for fun. ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Kelly Clarkson to sing the National Anthem, lead to the ring by Andrew Gilkison! (Let us guess what will happen here.) MCW Owner, Jalen Latham makes an announcement! (What do you think this will be, this can be fun or serious prediction...preferrably fun) The Sergeant & Drunken Tiger vs "The Brooklyn Brawler" Steve Lombardi and ????? Two total opposites will be forced to step into the ring together as a team when they face the infamous "Brooklyn Brawler" and a tag team partner of his choice!
"Ice Cold" Sanket Desai vs "First Class" Chandler Maxwell Current MCW Star vs Former MCW standout trained by main eventer Adam Benjamin, himself! "The Judge" Joseph Justice vs Pietske Justice says that he will dish out his own brand of justice to a man many believe to be a pervert and a pedophile, Pietske!
”Phenomenal” Frankie Scott vs Steven Shane Frankie Scott has told the world that he will be escorted by Carrie Underwood! Will Steven Shane care?
CSWA Unified Title Match (or not): Professor Tremendous (Champion?) vs Roderick McRatrick... IN DRAG AND WEARING A FUNNY HAT! Special Guest Referee: James Irish! It's true, it's true! In a Non-Sanctioned match, Professor Tremendous makes Roderick McRatrick put his money where the dress barn cashier is. This is sure to be the most fashionable match of the night, and that's based only on the guest ref!
Loser Is My Soldier Match: Rob Franklin vs Nakita Dahaka The offers and counter-offers have been thrown back and forth for days now. The winner of this match will have the services of the other in their "stable" until no longer required!
Settling The Score: Insurgent vs Justin Sane A Title Shot Hangs In The Balance! At MCW’s inaugural pay-per-view, Zero Hour, Insurgent was on the verge of becoming the MCW North American Champion. Justin Sane interfered, costing Insurgent the gold and setting himself up for a future title shot. Before that can happen, Insurgent aims to bring the power of all of the anti-Bushamaniacs everywhere to the ring. A future title shot hangs in the balance!
The Faces of MCW Falls Count Anywhere, No DQ, Elimination Match: Bryan Storms vs Dakota Smith vs Chris McMillan Without a doubt, these three have epitomized what is great about Major Championship Wrestling. At MCW Finale, no belts will be on the line... this will be about RESPECT! The winner will undoubtedly come out of this match as the superior wrestler!
MCW Founder Christian Sands will be in the ring to say a few words. MAIN EVENT: 60 Minute Time Limit Match for the Honorary Title of MCW Legend! "Yours Truly" Adam Benjamin vs Karl "The Dragon" Brown This is the match every MCW fan has wanted to see for a long time. The originals. Jalen Latham has signed this match out of respect for what MCW was and what it hopes to be in the eyes of it's fans for the rest of their lives. Karl Brown and Adam Benjamin step into the ring for the chance to be called MCW LEGEND!
Hell_Fighter — 29 August 2006 While we wait for the card to be finished and posted. I will start this thread out. Kelly Clarkson to sing the National Anthem, lead to the ring by Andrew Gilkison! (Let us guess what will happen here.) Clarkson starts to lipsync the national anthem and Gilkison FUBARS and causes some sort of wardrobe malfunction
MCW Owner, Jalen Latham makes an announcement! (What do you think this will be, this can be fun or serious prediction...preferrably fun) Latham will retire and forever disappear from professional wrestling. He will move out into a log cabin somewhere out in the wilderness with his new bride...
A three foot midget with a ten foot penis They will get freaky deaky and do whatever it is that people like them do. (Just kidding Josh ;-) ) Seriously though, Latham will announce that he is the new co-owner of the UCW and will sit on the board of directors.
The Sergeant & Drunken Tiger vs "The Brooklyn Brawler" Steve Lombardi and ????? Mystery Partner: Gary Coleman Sergeant and Drunken Tiger will win via huge squash. Brawler and Coleman will come in, get utterly squashed, collect their paycheck, and move on back to wherever they from (Stanford and the Post Office)
"Ice Cold" Sanket Desai vs "First Class" Chandler Maxwell Sanket Desai should pull off a nice decisive win, unless Maxwell has something else planned so I am not totally counting him out, but unless that happens Desai with the win here, but this could be something that would be great in the newly merged UCW.
"The Judge" Joseph Justice vs Pietske The Judge executes the pervert and alledged pedophile....keep in mind I said...ALLEDGEDLY! However, I kind of predict some funky stuff to happen during the course of this match in one way or another.
”Phenomenal” Frankie Scott vs Steven Shane If Frankie has Carrie Underwood in his corner. Steven Shane will have other American Idol winner Fantasia...however, she will not be a factor in this match because prior to the match, she will get excited, break her shoes, and cry the whole time while the match is going on.
Should be a good match but I kind of think that Shane will take this VERY close one. CSWA Unified Title Match (or not): Professor Tremendous (Champion?) vs Roderick McRatrick... IN DRAG AND WEARING A FUNNY HAT! Special Guest Referee: James Irish! This match should be really funny, but I am predicting that Team Tremendous will somehow prevail in some form or another, although knowing the Professor he will take all of the credit.
But the wild card could be James Irish. You never know. Loser Is My Soldier Match: Rob Franklin vs Nakita Dahaka No comment since this is my match, but once again my ongoing rivalry with Franklin will continue. Win or lose I think that I will continue to have lots of fun playing off of Rob. Keep up the awesome work Rob man. May the best man win.
Settling The Score: Insurgent vs Justin Sane A Title Shot Hangs In The Balance! This would of been a good match but due to neither man able to participate or rp, I think that this will either go no contest or maybe even thrown out all together. Hopefully both men will be able to show up in the new UCW to continue this feud and pick this match up once and for all.
Either that, or they will both get jumped and both anally accosted by a three foot midget with a 10 foot penis.
Oh the humanity. The Faces of MCW Falls Count Anywhere, No DQ, Elimination Match: Bryan Storms vs Dakota Smith vs Chris McMillan This should be a great match and since no title is on the line but more for pride. The true winners are us fans as these guys will put on a clinic.
But in the end, I am predicting Dakota Smith to come away with a close win. I really do wish that McMillan got in something though, I miss him. MCW Founder Christian Sands will be in the ring to say a few words. Christian Sands comes out and says that he was just accosted by a three foot midget with a ten foot penis.
(Just kidding Jeff, all in good fun man. Don't worry, I'll show the love and respect.) Seriously though, I am predicting that Sands will UNRETIRE and return to active wrestling in the newly merged UCW.
Now that would be instant ratings spike. MAIN EVENT: 60 Minute Time Limit Match for the Honorary Title of MCW Legend! "Yours Truly" Adam Benjamin vs Karl "The Dragon" Brown The match that will send the fans home happy and let us all forever remember MCW for months and years to come. This will be the wrestling clinic of all clinics.
But sadly, in the end, I think that Karl Brown will received the honor of MCW Legend and narrowly defeat Adam Benjamin in sudden death, but don't be surprised if these two see each other again in the newly merged UCW.
