Linguistic — 18 July 2006 What do we want to do, people? Would you like to see a short formed house show while we wait on the Revolution card to get running, or would you like me to go ahead and start working on a huge MEGASHOW featuring everyone from MCW and the rest of the FW Circuit?
I want thoughts and I want them now! Josh robishott — 18 July 2006 I personally would like a house show if only to drag out the MCW experiance a little longer.
Hell_Fighter — 18 July 2006 If it means keeping you busy until you officially ship out Josh, I'm all for it. ;D Just kidding, I think that we need to keep the MCW open for as long as possible, and if the houseshows are easy to produce and post then by all means go for it. Do like one or two leading up the one big mega supercard/farewell MCW show to send us off.
Not to meantion that with the posting of Center Stage 5, there are some feuds that have stemmed and began that I think we need to help fan the flame so to speak. (I.E. Densai/Sane being the big one)
So to kind of keep the MCW momentum going as we slowly ween off of MCW and ease into UCW with some strong momentum, we need to have something like a direction to go off of. So I am all for doing a house show (shortform) leading up to the megasupercard which will have both MCW and UCW superstars as well as several other invitations from other feds on FWC and whatnot. Maybe some possible dream matches and the like.
Well that is all I am gonna say for now, anything else just email or PM me. Patrick
