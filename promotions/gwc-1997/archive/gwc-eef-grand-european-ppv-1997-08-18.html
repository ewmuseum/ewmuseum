[EEF] The Grand European PPV (8/8)

18 August 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The European E-Wrestling Federation http://home4.swipnet.se/~w-45120/Index.htm JOHN: We have a battle here for you, fans. Josef "The Cavalier" Tadeuscz... LUKE: The Polish Sausage. JOHN: ...is set to face "Classic" Andrew Archer... LUKE: Our soon to be European Champion. JOHN: Why do you insist on interrupting me? LUKE: Get over it, Fitzy. They want to hear me instead. FRANK: Ladies and Gentlemen....this match is our MAIN EVENT! (big crowd pop.) FRANK: Scheduled for one fall. Introducing first. Advancing by defeating "Dark Rider" Dusty Knight, Jeff "The Rocket" Wrobleski, and Ray "Your Idol" Stevens...accompanied by his manager, Lady Solidarity. From Lodz, Poland....weighing in at 260 lbs.... JOSEF..... "THE CAVALIER".... TADEUSCZ! (fans cheer loudly as Qkumba Zoo's "Child Inside" Plays for the fourth time tonight.) LUKE: I really am starting to hate that song... JOHN: Oh, come on, Luke. Josef getting into the ring with Lady Solidarity. He's soaked with sweat. LUKE: Slimy and hairy. Classic Andrew is going to need decontamination after wrestling this Polish sausage... FRANK: And his opponent... (fans begin to boo loudly.) Undefeated here in the EEF...a former titleholder in three other federations....Advancing by defeating William Madison Gage in his only match tonight... From Kent, England....weighing a total of 251 lbs... CLASSIC ANDREWWWWWWWWWW ARCHER! (BOOS) JOHN: Quite a pedigree for the Classic one. This should be a good matchup. A rematch of a few weeks ago. Here are some clips from that match, as you see the European and German Championship belts being exhibited before the crowd. LUKE: As you see here, Archer was devestating, controlling the match for a long long time. JOHN: But Josef fought back, and nearly earned a victory with a full nelson weardown hold... LUKE: Then those animals pounced on the Monarchy, keeping them from seeing a fair match. JOHN: Whatever. Before a double clothesline put both competitors down for the ten count. Back to the action here, they're about to lock up. LUKE: Lady Solidarity out of the ring, and the bell is rung. (DING!!DING!!) JOHN: Archer quick to grab Tadeuscz by the head and ram it hard into his knee. Classic Andrew hits him with a standing dropkick, and Tadeuscz down. Archer still hasn't broken a sweat. LUKE: Meanwhile, Tadeuscz could drown if he stands on his head right now. Sponge boy. JOHN: Classic Andrew with a snap elbow drop, and another, and a quick pin. ONE!!!! TWO!!!! JOHN: Kickout by Josef! LUKE: Well, he lasted longer than I thought he would. JOHN: Archer with Josef's left leg...knee drop. That'll slow Josef down. LUKE: Lady Solidarity waving that flag around, the fans cheering. Choosing the Polish man over a good hearty example of British life. Noble, charitable, heroic... JOHN: You don't know anything about British Nobility! LUKE: I saw Princess Di once...That counts. JOHN: Archer with a Northern lights suplex. Off the ropes, drives the knee. Josef has to mount some attack here, or the match is over. LUKE: Archer lifts him, puts him in an armbar. JOHN: JOSEF with a short clothesline! The crowd erupts with cheers! Archer is down! Tadeuscz winded, but still standing! LUKE: Unbelievable! He went deep inside and pulled that move out of nowhere! JOHN: Archer up, Tadeuscz with an armdrag! And another! Archer up, Standing dropkick! Tadeuscz is on fire, and the Classic one backs into the corner! LUKE: Josef following him in, ah....Archer suckers him! Dropkick with the ropes for leverage! JOHN: Good move by the wiley Englishman to regain his advantage. Rolling Neckbreaker, and Tadeuscz is hurt badly. Archer to the second turnbuckle, leaps with a fist and drives it into Josef's head! LUKE: Josef is out of it. Cover! ONE!!! TWO!!! THREE!! Yes Andrew Archer is... JOHN: Wait! Referee Harvey Smiths says no! Josef's foot is on the ropes! Archer is angry, stomps Josef and drags him into the center of the ring! ONE!!! TWO!!! KICKOUT!! LUKE: I don't believe it! Smith must have lost a screw when Kegonite hit him, Archer had him pinned! He could have counted 50! JOHN: Josef's foot was on the ropes! You saw it! Everyone saw it! LUKE: Eh, whatever. Archer now with a little sweat as he sets Josef for a gutwrench suplex. JOHN: JOSEF counters! Full Nelson! Fans are cheering! LUKE: Archer grabs his hair and hits Josef with a jawbreaker. Nice try, Polish boy, but not even close. JOHN: Lifts Josef, throws him to the ropes, down for a back body drop...Josef leapfrog, off the ropes, Archer misses with a clothesline, Josef off the ropes, Flying Bodycross! ONE!!! TWO!!! Kickout! LUKE: Archer not letting Tadeuscz get up! Stomping on his legs and knees. I've never seen Andrew Archer so angry, so wild... JOHN: Josef now in a sitting sleeperhold. He's not breaking free.... ref raises his hand once, it falls. Twice...it falls... Archer pulls him to his feet, throws him off the ropes... LUKE: There it is! The Classic Touch! You can hear a pin drop! All the little Might for Righters are silent! JOHN: But Archer doesnt go for a cover he lifts him up. Tadeuscz firing two quick punches to his stomach, but like a child's punches. Archer throws him to the ropes again... LUKE: One more! For good measure, Josef's spine has to feel like gum right now. JOHN: Cover in the center of the ring! ONE!!!! TWO!!! HE PULLED HIM UP! LUKE: Andrew Archer wants not only to beat Josef, he wants to put him out of contention! JOHN: Archer with a standing dropkick, sitting on Josef's chest...slapping his face, spitting on him, come on ref... LUKE: Why? Spit going to make Josef even more out of it? JOHN: He's humiliating him! LUKE: And Lady Solidarity and the fans can do nothing about it. HA! I love it! Clean sweep for the Monarchy! JOHN: Lifts Josef, throws him to the ropes again, setting for the spinebuster...got him! LUKE: WAIT! Tadeuscz... JOHN: Crucifix circle! He has Archer's head tucked under his arm, hooks the leg. LUKE: He wouldn't... JOHN: Trying to hit him with the Polish Pride! LUKE: He's too spent to ever lift... JOHN: ARCHER IS UP! SLAMMED AND HELD IN THE PIN! HARVEY SMITH IS ON THE JOB!! LUKE: NO! ONE!!!!! TWO!!!!! THREE!!!! (DING!!!DING!!!DING!!!) (THE CROWD ERUPTS!!!!!!!!!) JOHN: Josef "The Cavalier" Tadeuscz wins the title! His Fishermanbuster puts Archer away after all seemed lost! The fans are going wild, Lady Solidarity is going wild, and Andrew Archer is livid! LUKE: I don't believe it! The Classic is defeated! JOHN: What a night. LUKE: Let's look at the big picture before we celebrate over this little victory, shall we? The Monarchy are the new Tag Team Champions. Classic Andrew Archer, robbed in the last moments of that match, carries the German Title with the dignity that the European Title no longer has.... And Josef Tadeuscz? A one shot wonder. Listen up, Cavalier. You got lucky tonight, but in the words of another one shot wonder, Archer will have the European title and you'll be "In an Mmm-bop, Gone." JOHN: Give me a break, Rock. Josef "The Cavalier" Tadeuscz carrying on the fine tradition of Eastern European wrestlers to achieve greatness, following in the footsteps of Ivan Smolecki. Classic Andrew Archer will be a force to reckon with as the German Champion, and The Monarchy looks to hold the tag team championship with their skill and determination, for a long time to come. Look in the ring!!! (Josef offers a handshake to Classic Andrew, who refuses it. Andrew snatches the German title and walks back to the boos of the crowd. Lady Solidarity walks to Josef's side, Josef kneeling from exhaustion on the mat. The European Championship belt is handed to him, and still on his knees, lifts it up for the fans to see. They cheer wildly and explosions in the rafters send streams of confetti throughout the arena.) JOHN: Before we begin the interview, I would like to announce the five EEF wrestlers scheduled to compete for the GWC WORLD TITLE! (fans cheer) First, a man who lost early tonight but exemplifies what the EEF is about. ICEMAN DEAN SCHLOTZHAUER! (crowd pop) A man who has earned the reputation of a giant and a dark soul.... HOLOCAUST KEVIN CHAMBERS! (crowd boo pop) He'll fight anyone who he doesn't respect... RAY "Your Idol" STEVENS! (half boo half cheer) Your German Champion.... CLASSIC ANDREW ARCHER (LOUD boo pop) And.... (cheers begin.) This man....your European heavyweight Champion... JOSEF.... THE CAVALIER... TADEUSCZ! (Loud cheers.) [And with that the first ever EEF PPV ends to clips of a celebrating Josef Tadeuscz holding the EEF European Heavyweight title and the fans cheering him.]
