Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

RAW-Florida opened in March 2002 as one of the baby RAWs, a regional territory of the RAW universe built to bring newcomers along and to send its best upward. Its founding FAQ promised cards written as reports, fictional wrestlers only, and three Sunshine State titles, with a Fight Fest every April in Miami as the year's crown jewel.

Its owner staged the move himself. On 25 March 2002 the last WWC OvEr DriVe, from MGM Studios in Orlando, closed with a Winner Takes Roster match between the owner and his brother Bryan; the owner won, and the whole WWC roster left for RAW-Florida in RAW-F t-shirts, its Primetime champion Ryiot carrying his belt over as a Caribbean Islands title.

That card, its FAQ and one recruiting notice are what survive. No RAW-Florida card of its own is held, and how it ended is not in the surviving record.
