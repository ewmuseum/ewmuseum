Results: WWC OvEr DriVe -- March 25th, 2002

26 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
--- March 25th, 2002 The WWC proudly brings to you... WWC OvEr DriVe!! LIVE! From MGM Studios in Orlando, Florida! --- WWC OvEr DriVe As the camera faded in to the weekly dose of WWC's OvEr DriVe disclaimer writing. A huge orange and white logo of RAW-F flashed on the screen and then on to the show into. The promo quickly ended and then was followed by thousands of fans screaming their heads off as pyros and fireworks busted off as the weekly ritual of WWC's weekly show, OvEr DriVe. As soon as the pyros ended the fans went wild again as the theme of the new found owner of RAW-F, Jesse Teague, blasted onto the PA. Jesse strutted down the walkway and climbed into the ring. After a few seconds of waiving at fans with a smile on his face,
Teague took to the mic.
Teague: " Ladies and Gentleman, I'd like to welcome you to the last show of WWC under my ownership. "
With that, the fans showed how they felt with a roaring line ofboos. Teague: " Yes, it appears that Bryan Teague, my ever so beloved, baby brother will step into the spotlight and continue the grand organization that I have devoted my life to for the past four years. "
The fans suddenly filled the arena with chorus of cheers. Teague: " Honestly, I believe this company will still survive as it has done for the past four years. Hopefully, this federation will grow under the tenure of my baby brother and volute more than it has ever done. "
Before the owner could get anything else out, Bryan Teague's music hit the PA. Bryan then joined his bigger brother in the WWC squared circle.
B. Teague: " Brother, you don't know how much I thank you for giving me so a gift as the ownership of this enterprise. I thank you. " J. Teague: " Your very welcome, Bryan. " B. Teague: " I don't know how to repay you. I wish I could do somethi-..." J. Teague: " Oh. You don't have to wish. I've already returned my favor back. Thats why I'm taking the entire roster of WWC with me to the sunshine state with me! "
The fans totally shocked with Jesse's statements, gave a mix of boos and cheers. B. Teague: " What the hell?! " J. Teague: " Yes my dear brother...So you don't have to worry about repaying me back. " B. Teague: " Hell no! I'm not going through with this. Your taking property of WWC and the fans. Your stealing! You can't do this! " J. Teague: " Oh yes, I can. I will tell you why. My signature is accompanied with every man on this roster's contract. So yes, I can take anything I want to. "
B. Teague: " Well...lets make a deal. I've got my stuff in the back. I came prepared tonight, just incase something came up such as something like this. I'm ready to go. How about a...shall we say...'Brother vs. Brother' match right here tonight in this ring on OvEr DriVe?!? " The fans then let another pop go as Jesse Teague looked stunned with the daring attitude of his younger sibling.
J. Teague: " Well... " Bryan Teague stood there waiting for answer from his brother. Looking at the fans and then looking back at Jesse, as he spoke again.
J. Teague: " Okay Bryan, for the roster of WWC...tonight! You...and I." Another pop let go. Jesse Teague climbed out of the ring and headed to the back. Bryan Teague left after his brother. After the ring was cleared, the ring announcer climbed in the ring and introduced the first match of the night.
Carlos Smith vs. Ryiot The opening match was for the Primetime Championship, currently held by Ryiot. Before the match Ryiot took to the mic, insulting Carlos
Smith by calling him The Hawaiian Poverty Stricken Kid due to his folks not having enough money for buying him some wrestling skills.
Carlos then ran out before his entrance and pummeled the big man Ryiot before the bell even rang. Carlos connected with a few dropkicks and then took the monster down with a superkick that not only put the monster down but sent him clashing over the top rope landing on the ringside area cement floor. The Hawaiian Nature Boy then bounced off the ropes and delivered a plancha to the outside. Both men lay on the cement climbing to their feet slowly, very slowly. The referee began to count. 1...2...3....4... Carlos climbed back into the ring. He then got on his feet as the ref counted 6 simultaneously along with Carlos bouncing off the ropes again (!!) this time delivering a 450 splash to the outside (!!) landing on the battered Ryiot. The ref started his count again. 1!......2!......3!......4!......5!......6!.....Carlos then climbed to his feet. He picked up Ryiot and threw him into the ring then he followed. Carlos, shaken but managed enough of strength, applied a boston crab. Ryiot lay almost unconscious and didn't show no sign of pain. Carlos let go, shocked that Ryiot was not showing any sign of harm. Carlos then began to stomp Ryiot. A costly mistake as
Ryiot opened his eyes and caught the foot of Smith. Ryiot climbed to his feet with Smith's foot in hand. Ryiot then delivered a clothesline that setup for his finisher, Black Heart Plunge, a High Angle Falling
Foward Uranage Suplex. With that, Ryiot covered Carlos for the pin -
1!...2!...3! Winner: Ryiot, retained Primetime Championship ( Black Heart Plunge ) After the match Jesse Teague greeted Ryiot at the entrance way. Jesse Teague: " Ryiot, you know we've worked together for a long time. Yes, I'm moving on to RAW-Florida. But...I was thinking. How about you carry that Primetime Championship over to RAW-Florida, and you still being the champion, we'll call it the RAW-Florida Caribbean Islands
Championship. How about it?
Ryiot: "Sure, why not?" Jesse: "Ahhh man, your a good one. Thanks." Jesse, overjoyed with the fact he has recruited two of WWC's champions, shook hands with Ryiot then walked back to the backstage area.
The Bronx vs. Shadrick Robinson As the Bronx made his entrance Shadrick Robinson's theme music cut in the middle of The Bronx's entrance. Shadrick then made his way out with his crew of bodyguards following making sure no gang members attack Shad. The Bronx stood at the middle of the ramp. The Bronx then threw a punch at one of the bodyguards that told The Bronx to go ahead and get in the ring. The Bronx didn't get no where with his faint attack as the whole crew beat the living hell out of The Bronx,
Shadrick watching as he continued to make his way to the ring slapping the hands of the fans looking on. The group of bodyguards then carried
The Bronx to the ring. The Bronx, almost dead, as soon as the bell rang was quickly pinned by Shadrick for the shortest match in professional wrestling history. Shad then celebrated his victory and then left the ring with his crew.
Winner: Shadrick Robinson ( Pinfall ) Bucks Inc. ( Money & Kid Ca$h ) vs. The Outlaws ( Jonathan Badd & Urban Outlaw ) Money and Kid Ca$h, WWC tag team champions, battled the duo of Jonathan Badd and Urban Outlaws, the Outlaws. The Outlaws started the action with their customary ferocity, but were quickly neutralized by
Ca$h's aerial moves. It was an awesome missile kick the move that gained Ca$h the control over Jonathan Badd. Ca$h continued applying serious punishment to Badd until this one tagged Outlaw. The referee was unable to stop Money from coming into the ring, and at some point the four men got involved in a titanic battle. The referee re-established order and Outlaw and Ca$h were the ones fighting in the middle of the ring. Outlaw viciousness and power controlled Ca$h and stopped him from imposing his aerial style. After delivering a series of powerful elbows and kicks, Outlaw covered Ca$h for the count of two only, due to the interference by Money. Outlaw and Ca$h continued their brawl until they simultaneously hit each other with powerful clothelines. They ended up almost unconscious in the middle of the ring, while the thrilled crowd chanted, Outlaws! Outlaws! Amazingly
Bucks Inc. had gotten the crowd behind the most hated team in the WWC,
The Outlaws. Both contenders tagged their respective partners, Money and Badd, who took each other to the limit. Badd delivered his finishing move, Rules Broken and a couple of DDTs. That brought in the foreign object, Ca$h grabbed one of the title belts and tossed it in to his tag team partner, Money. Money then smacked Badd in the forehead with the belt causing a DQ and retaining their titles.
Winner: The Outlaws ( DQ ) Dynamite vs Octavious Davis -- Hardcore Match This was a match up for the extreme fan in all of us. These two guys started out in the ring, and stayed there all of two minutes before finding a garbage can full of assorted plunder under the ring, and using all of it! Before the match had reached the 5 minute mark,
Dynamite Mercy was busted open with two head wounds (one cause by
Davis bending a cookie sheet over his head, and one caused by a self inflicted shot with a steel garbage can) and a large gash in the arm, caused by a barb wire wrapped baseball bat. At one point, Dynamite laid Davis out on a table, and busted a fluorescent light bulb across his head! After several minutes on the floor, they went back to the ring, where Octavious Davis finished the match off with a 450 Leg Drop and managed to get the pinfall. Once again the Octavious Davis showed up, and decimated everyone in the ring.
Winner: Octavious Davis ( 450 Leg Drop ) Deion Austin vs. The Rebel Warrior The next bout featured two of the fastest and most technically competent performers anywhere in wrestling today, as Deion Austin took on the 38 year old veteran The Rebel Warrior. This match was a terrific blend of old school wrestling and amazing show of knack that had to be seen to be truly believed. Warrior dominated the proceedings for the first five minutes or so before a low blow turned the tide in
Austin's favor. Austin wore Warrior down with a nearly suffocating chinlock, then connected with a textbook quebrada moonsault for a two count. Austin then sent his opponent outside in order to suplex him into a table, but it was Warrior who ended up reversing things and sent Austin into the table instead, as referee Michael Hartman pleaded with both men relentlessly to return to the ring. Reluctantly, following the official's instruction, Warrior sent Austin back inside, then climbed the top turnbuckle and nailed Austin with a missile dropkick for a count of two. Austin soon countered with a scorpion deathlock on Warrior, but was unable to gain the submission. There followed a flurry of rapid pin attempts by both men before the twenty minute time limit expired, resulting in a classic "well earned draw"
by both Austin and Warrior -- and a healthy round of applause from the appreciative audience.
Winner: Draw ( Time Limit Expired ) After the match Austin didn't like the result and wanted a win over the veteran so he decided he would continue the battle at his own will delivering a chair shot to the back of the head as Warrior made his way to the backstage area. Austin then left the ringside area to a chant of D A Sucks! Officials helped Warrior to his dressing room after the absence of Austin.
Keon Williams vs. Machine Machine was in the hunt for revenge against WWC Heavyweight Champion
Keon Williams, for Keon's defeat of him several months ago here in the same building. However, it was Keon who opened things up in their match tonight with a flurry of fists to Machine that sent the nefarious Machine to the mat and out of the ring. Keon then rammed
Machine's head alternately into the guard rail and the ring post, and then finally into the boot of Keon. Keon then tried to put Machine away with a flying shoulder tackle and a running powerslam, but netted only a count of two. Keon continued his path of destruction with a powerbomb, then went to work on Machine's leg by wrenching it several times on the apron. This set Machine up for the reverse inverted boston crab-- the move he had used to defeat Machine in their last encounter. After spending what must have seemed to him like an eternity trapped in the hold, Machine was able to reverse it, but with the illegal aid of his friend Jonathan Badd who made his way to the ring, Machine managed to gain the ropes and force a break. Keon then regained control of the match with a running bulldog followed by a frog splash. That setup for another inverted boston crab in the center of the ring. Machine then called in Badd to make the save but Keon met
Badd with a picture perfect rock bottom. Machine still lay on the mat where Keon left him. Machine was then wrapped in a third inverted boston crab by Keon. That ended the match with Machine tapping furiously.
Winner: Keon Williams and still Heavyweight Champ ( Inverted Boston
Crab ) After the match Jesse Teague greeted his heavyweight champion at the entrance way as Keon was heading to back to watch the next match in his locker room. Teague invited Keon to ringside to watch the defeat of his younger brother Bryan. Keon took up the offer and found a seat ringside to watch the match.
Bryan Teague vs. Jesse Teague -- Winner Takes Roster Bryan entered the ring wearing his regular wrestling gear that he had put on every week to fight on his bigger brother's events. A confrontation started the match off with the two brothers exchanging a few heated remarks and a continuous stare that has never been witnessed by the brothers in public. Bryan initiated the fight with a right hand to the jaw of Jesse. The brawl kept steady with a lot of your basic wrestling moves insisted by Jesse which surprisingly over powered the younger, smaller Bryan Teague. Bryan then showed signs of his wrestling skills that he hath developed over the years working as a superstar for his older brother, Jesse Teague. Backstage, the WWC superstars watched impatiently for the foresee of their future. On the
MegaTron of the arena, the fans watched every action of the wrestler's backstage. Meanwhile, in the ring, Bryan was pulling out all the stops, beating his brother from pillar to post. Worried, Keon climb out of his ringside seat and climbed into the ring connecting with a super kick on Bryan unexpectedly as he bounced of the opposite side ropes. The ref set back and watched the event transpire. With the fans attention off the Megatron, the dressing room was empty. Suddenly the
WWC superstars sprinted down to ringside acting if they were about to attach the Heavyweight Champion but instead tossed about Bryan Teague!
Even head of Officials, Michael Hartman got into the mix, taking off his officiating shirt to reveal a RAW-Florida t-shirt! The superstars cleared the ring to act as lumberjacks as Jesse pinned his baby brother in the center of the ring as Michael Hartman counted,
...1!...2!...3! Keon then climbed into the ring and handed out RAW-F t-shirts to everyone. The wrestlers celebrated in the ring as the crowd didn't know how to handle all the events taken place in the match.
Winner: Jesse Teague ( When Superstars Unite ) As the superstars continued to celebrate the camera faded to black with the conclusion of WWC's OvEr DriVe.
