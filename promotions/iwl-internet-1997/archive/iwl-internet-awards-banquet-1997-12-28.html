IWL Awards Banquet - Dec. 28, 1997

28 December 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

Many IWL wrestlers are seated at some tables, while other tables are surrounded by Championship Committee members, along with many friends & family of several personalities. As the camera begins to pan down to a side angle view, the 'TIC Only' table comes into view, surrounded by The Capitol, Fire Mexica & Mr. Vasquez, Oriana, Slam, and the IWL World Champion, Spazzz. Many tuxedo-clad waiters & waitresses are working in various points in the hall, as IWL President JP Décosse stands up from the head table, and begins to make his way to the stage podium. The busy crowd begins to hush down, as JP speaks into the microphone ...) JP: "To the Wrestlers, friends & family thereof, Championship Committee, and to all of the dedicated IWL fans watching at home, I would like to welcome you to the 1997 Winter Season Awards Banquet!" (Spattering applause & a few hoots are heard from the guests, as JP cracks a friendly smile.) JP: "Now, before we get anything underway here, I would just like to state that due to last term's fiasco between John Marc and The Capitol, neither man will be presenting an award this evening, for security purposes." (A great deal of chuckling and whispering takes place, as The Capitol is seen uncomfortably wriggling in his seat. On the other side of the room, John Marc just smirks, while one of his tablemates, Deathcall, points and laughs at him.) JP: "Tonight we have 15 special awards and recognitions to distribute, so without any further adieu, let's get our first presenter up here! (General Pentagon walks up, and stands behind the podium wearing a dress United States Marine Corps uniform. Colonel Alexandra escorts him, but she is wearing a shiny, red designer evening gown that has a seductively low cut. She smiles devilishly at the camera.) IDP: "Well, this time around the IWL has seen fit to have me present the Most Underrated Wrestler of the Term award. I can think of one of the nominees in this category who fits into the group that has been legitimately underrated. And that's my former tag team championship partner--the Vindicator. You can say I'm biased in giving out this award but hey that's just too bad. Well, let's go through the nominees and see whether or not the members of the IWL voted correctly. Colonel, if you would please do the honors of listing the nominees and then revealing the winner, I'm not really a fan of these flashy awards shows." (The Colonel is all to happy to be the center of attention.) Colonel: "Well, first we have the other half of the former IWL World Tag Team Champions, the Vindicator! He has been most definately underrated by many in the IWL who have learned to regret it. The next nominee is Slam.
