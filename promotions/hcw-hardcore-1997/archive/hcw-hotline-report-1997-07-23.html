HCW Huckins’ Hotline Report, 23 July 1997

23 July 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
.......[Voice of Tony Huckins crackles on] ....Heeeeelo fans, I am T MAN Tony Huckins, with your HUCKINS' Hotline Report for Tuesday, July the Twenty Second. I have a preview of tonight's card, some HOT and weird Rumors, and the latest NEWS to the HCW that only the T MAN the TERMINATOR can give ya... Starting things off, Tonight will see Malibu Mike face Vince Viper for the Television Title..if Vince wins he gets 5 minutes with Sara in the ring! Good luck Mike! Also, the King of Snakes added the stip that should he win the barbwire matchup, No one can help Sara in those allotted five minutes..or face a two week suspension, and that has been granted by the HCW Presidents. ALSO, you'll see Hiro Yuko and Damon Zex take on the Punc Rokkerz- Austin Starr face Buzzkill, Master Blaster face CyberTiger, and Two-faced Steven Stryfe face Michael the Punisher. Personally, LAUREN LANE could whup Two Face... unless Stryfe brings the She-male Ivanna with him! That girl could make RIGHT GUARD turn left! HA ha ha. Thursday's card is very important to a few careers- the lineup has Poe receiving a TV title shot- and then the Johnny Blaze/Ben Toussi fight..this match is MOST important as to WHO will take the momentum into the Pretty Boy Match at the BeachBlast PAY PER VIEW. Also, a new team of HIGH IMPACT will face the American Connection, and Flex, fresh off a win over Malibu Mike, will face Rabbi, another man who's on a roll, besting Clyde Conrad last weekend, AND the man called SADISTIC Steven Storm..aka the wrestler formerly known as BBBLIZZARD. Whoever gains a victory in this one, is headed for great things! Also, the TRIANGLE Tag Match, will see The NEW Team of Perfect Poison, AKA Cyanide and Malibu Mike face The Dark Lords known as the TWIN TOWERS and the new team of the ALLIED POWERS! That means Malibu Mike could very well be wrestling TWO HARDCORE matches in the SAME night! NAMES headed to compete in the SIX man tag match for XCW- Lord Kevin Kingsley, and Bad Boy Brian Boston. Names headed to compete in the WOWA Six man tag, "Confessor" Rey Jeran, Chris Cartwright so far.....more later. NOW the hottest rumors, the juiciest gossip. Word backstage is that YES, Makaveli WILL show up tonight as he personally threatened a member of Disaster Relief, our Security Team, telling him he will try to be on the show- now we know he was fired..but he claims some legal matters say he can stay as long as he wants..we'll see if he tries something tonight. ALSO, I have heard that the MAIN EVENT for NEXT WEEK'S NIGHTMARE, already signed- will feature El Diablo and HADES taking on HEARTBREAKER and a mystery partner....maybe we'll see who it is this Weekend on Pay Per View. I also heard that Tyson Tercell... after a CRU meeting with HCW Executives..caught a cold -or Flu from the Co-President [name withheld]..hopefully he'll be alright to face Mike Kegonite in his DOUBLE HELL Exploding Dynamite board matchup at the Beachblast. Also, CYANIDE was miffed he did NOT have a match with anyone at BEACHBLAST, and has stated he WILL wrestle there if he FEELS like it...so we'll have to wait and SEE What's up with THAT. My Stooges-AH, SOURCES tell me ALSO that a man was at the Queens office this weekend, meeting with Recruiters and an Executive about re-joining HCW..who?? Well let's wait and see- actually, I was not able to find out, but I WILL before the Pay Per View, trust me! AND you wouldn't BELIEVE who just bought a Front Row ticket to the BeachBlast Pay Per View...wait till you see who is standing come Sunday Eve in the crowd with a hotdog and a "LAST cup of Sorrow!" Speaking of comings and goings... I'd like to welcome ALLIED POWERS, HIGH IMPACT and AMERICAN CONNECTION to the ranks of HCW ....good for you guys, you just joined the HCW- now the hard part...SURVIVING. -Hey, many have tried! NorthPac, we hardly knew ye! ...AND CHAD ROGERS, welcome buddy! Now I can wake up in the morning WITHOUT depleting my Sanka trying to stay awake through Austin and Black! And as a side note, wrestlers touring from Super Japan will be here on the NIGHTMARE following BEACHBLAST, for a week or so...stars like the SJPW Cruiserweight Champion SUPER TENGU, "Too Sexy" LANCE SMITH...KYU KABUKI, "Demon Boy" ISHIRINKU and the TAG TEAM of Keiji Asamiya & Kazuya Asakino. LET'S SHOW them what NORTH AMERICAN HARDCORE is ALL about! Well that's it for now, I will be back on Thursday with BEACHBLAST updates, and the FULL CARD... and SEAN RUSSELL will be spewing his venom on SATURDAY. ANY rumors you think you got for me? Mail me and send them in...and I'll read them on the air. Well, I am the T MAN, Tony Huckins, and until Austin Starr says something coherent ...HAVE a good one! ..........Click! ..[Dial Tone.......]
