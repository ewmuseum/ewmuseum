This weeks CrossFIRE will focus on the NEW World Champion. I fully believe El Perfecto will become a hall of famer. He outlasted Patty Babes Carter, and The Heartbreaker last night. He is one of the only real Fan Favorites in the HCW, in a time of heel turns. El Perfecto has proven time and time again he has what it takes to be a major player here in the HCW, and should prove to be a fighting Champion as well. He is already slated to defend this MONDAY NIGHTMARE against the man replacing Shane Dreamer, Survival. At first, he had a bit of an ego problem, but this win should help keep him down to earth, and a regular nice guy. Counterpoint: El Perfecto will be worse then that other guy [Mr. Explosion] I fly to Chicago last night, and what do I see? HCW's version of Curt Henning capturing the World Title. While he's a good talker, beating PBC was a fluke, and beating the Heartbreaker NEVER should of happened without that loser yutz Python interfering. Nevertheless, he's our New HCW World Champ. How long will he keep the belt? Who knows? He's gotta face Survival of the bWo and if he survives that far, he has title defenses against the Top competitors here on the line too. With a roster full of Talent and men willing to sacrifice life and limb to capture that Title, you are a marked man Perfecto. Now my summary, In short the last HCW champ we had allowed the win to go to his head, he beacme egotistical, and in short, nothing but a punk. He became demanding that the HCW careen to please him, and defended his belt a whole THREE times. When faced with a controversy, he cried and went home. So what's to make me think that pressures of becoming World Champion won't change El Perfecto? And remember folks, Perfecto already HAS a healthy ego, actually, an ego the size of Montana, so what's to stop him from developing a Superman complex? I wouldn't bet on him focusing, and I may even take odds that in a week or two, he'll be lynching someone with barbwire, look out Enforcer!! That's it, go away, see ya next week [Lauren Lane] The HCW is not responsible for the comments made by Mr. Explosion and thinks he should get a better ventilated office. Have a good week folks!
