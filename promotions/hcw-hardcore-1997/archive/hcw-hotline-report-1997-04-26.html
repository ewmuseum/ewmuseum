HCW Huckins’ Hotline Report, 26 April 1997

26 April 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[dial tone]... This is the HCW and this is the voice of Tony the Tiger Huckins, a man who is the ONLY real journalist in the HCW, a man who'll tell it like it is. Let's go the hot topics tonight, Survival and the bWo title shot, Python/Heartbreaker, the major influx of talent arriving in houseboatloads to HCW shores, and the real reason Iron Maniac and NutCase are fired. In addition, predictions for tomorrow's Saturday Night Fever, behind the scenes gossip and weekly dirt. Not the dirt in the Ruffneck's underwear, the hot gossip ONLY the HCW's real journalist can bring you. Survival has been rankled as of late, as he has a title shot. Many wrestlers were spotted behind the scenes complaining that Survival does not warrant a World Title shot yet. Well here's the real deal folks, WAY back when the World title was held up, and the three way dance was established so the winner would take the title, Shane was promised a title shot rematch the day after W.A.R. But then personal circumstances prevented Shane from working with the HCW, and rather then send the bWo into Deathgames a man short, HCW officials SCRAMBLED to find a replacement for Shane. Enter Survival. He was in the right place, and in exchange for joining the bWo for Deathgames, Survival was handed the HCW World title shot that Shane would have had, had he been able to fulfill his match. But when Shane returned early, the contract was already etched in stone, and rather then face a lawsuit, the HCW agreed to let Survival fight the Title shot on the Saturday Night Fever after W.A.R. The bWo, who won the world title shot the THUNDER! before Mega Fight STILL have it at their disposal, and as far as I know, still have not named a man to face the Champ. One man who isn't focused on the title anymore, that's the popular Heartbreaker. HB is furious with Python for sending snakes all over Lisa during a THUNDER! interview. He may get his match with Python at Mega Fight. Another tag team, this time the bWo's Underworld, have thrown their voices in, pressing HCW to re-instate the former World Tag champs. Time will see. Tomorrow's card features a double main event, Flash Flood defending his Hardcore title against HCW's most improved wrestler, Michael Martin Murphy, and El Perfecto defending his World Crown against Survival in a Thunder Cage, i.e. a Roofed Steel Cage. I look for a title change or two, as i heard a few SURPRISE appearances tomorrow night. Now for the dirt; rumoured to be entering HCW; Mammoth, Jarred Tokarek, Masked man, Clyde Conrad, Bad News Brawner, Phrentic, Jack Bradshaw, Dreamwalker, DJ Johnson, Mechanic Mike Malone, Dreadnaught and BlackJack Bill. Not to mention the DIA, Poe, Zex and Lord Doomsday. Rumoured to be on their way out??? Terminator X, Chris Kanne, Jimmy the Gusher, Balsack the Jaws of Death, Daemon, House of Fire, and Awesome Alliance. Fatal Alliance are being whispered about in the HCW offices, and a possible full time contract maybe in the works. And popular wrestler Flex is currently competing again on the independent circuit, fresh from an injury at one time thought to be career-ending. Will there be talks with HCW again? Time will see. Now that the hcw webpage has almost completed it's renovations, their computers should be corrected in terms of mailing lists. If you find any discrepancies, please let them know immediately. AND for all you slackers in the HCW, you have until TOMORROW NIGHT to get in your new roster sheets or risk expulsion. Here it is, and I'll see ya later. This has been the only real journalist, and PITTSBURGH SUCKS!! Tony the STUD KING Huckins. Have a great week, as long as mine's better!!
