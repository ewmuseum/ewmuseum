HCW Roughneck Report, 30 March 1997

30 March 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
...Hello everyone this is Sean, Roughneck Russell, THE only real journalist no matter WHAT Tony Huckins thinks. ...And how about Tony? Hopefully Miss Stephanie, Jonnie Adonis' hot valet powerbombed some sense into him. We shall see. ...Iron Maniac has fired Most Reckless Force, and in a time where he's losing friends almost as fast as our World Champ, that's not a smart idea. If the Iron Maniac really wants to capture the HCW World Tag Team Titles at We Aint Rolemodels, then a feud with a disgruntled (albeit popular) Reckless Force is NOT a good business maneuver. ...We ain't rolemodels is really shaping up to be a big card. Let's look at sides for the Main Event. Deathgames that is, only in the H.C.W.. On one side you got What's left of Wildside, namely Jonnie Adonis, El Luchador Violento and the Canadian Vampire, one of my favorite International teams, and then the team of Blizzard & Avalanche. Then on the other side, you have the remnants of the bWo, led by Shane (No relation to Tommy) Dreamer, then Paul Disney and the Hotshot Mike Stewart. They've teamed with members of the Fatal Alliance, which leaves a slot open on each team yet. "Hardcore" Jon Stilwater, who recently picked himself up a title, not HCW mind you, is rumoured to be the final man for Shane's team, but no word on who the final man for Jonnie's team is. More word on the rest of the card next week. ...It AIN'T gonna be Terminator X. Oh no, not after what our illustrious Champ did to him tonight. Terminator has been taken to the Pacific Gateway Hospital, the E.R. unit, and they're gonna need better then Eric la Salle and George Clooney to fix him up. No, Seriously, I called the ward and spoke to Doctor Andrew James, and the word so far is that Terminator X has received minor injuries to the skin surrounding the Larynx. No permanent damage, but it's safe to say Terminator won't be able to talk for awhile. We'll keep you updated. ...In other medical conditions, The Man of A Million Holds Chris Kanne is already out of the hospital, after a brief checkup saw him without any real injuries, nothing exceeding a few bruises. Kanne was reported however to have long red welts on his back, and a large bruise on his shoulders, which looked like whoever attacked him probably hit him with a splash, plancha or moonsault of some kind. ...For Thunder!, the matches are: The Underworld vs. Simon Fiasco & Rancid Rick Ruby Duke Douglas & John Stilwater vs. Da Punk Rockerz Iron Maniac & Nutcase vs. The Night Stalkers MMM & The Deacon vs. FIRE Education Crew vs. Terrorize & Stryfe and Party Animals vs. The Blood Merchants In addition, look for Official word concerning possible penalty for Jonnie Adonis' actions, as well as an interview with Michael Martin Murphy. ...Well that's all tonight!! Of course later on in the week is Tony Huckin's Hotline report, but tune in next week for the RoughNeck Report! This is Sean "Rougneck" Russell saying have a great week, even if you are Dylan Dice.
