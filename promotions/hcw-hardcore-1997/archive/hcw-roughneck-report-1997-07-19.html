HCW Roughneck Report, 19 July 1997

19 July 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
 [Dial Tone... then voice of Sean Russell comes on] ..Hello Mat Fans..Roughneck Sean Russell talkin' atcha...Tonight's HOTLINE report is gonna go over the latest happenings at THUNDER! last night..the comings and goings, and News and Rumors as well. Comings and goings abound the HCW-get this fans.. CJ BRAWNER has left to become MINISTER! Yes fans, he seeks to be ordained...so NOW HCW is without a Vice President. Who will take over??? We will see! Someone we WISH would leave us alone...Makaveli, has been demanding to be let into HCW, as he was let go before the Summer Break, but he has complained he was entitled to stay longer, in a verbal agreement. I'LL Let the Lawyers handle this, but I say KICK the bum! In other notes...Injuries have struck a chord in the Hardcore Championship Wrestling. President Chris Smith has come down with a serious Flu...Michael Murphy is doing much better with his rehabilitation, he should be back in HCW soon. Also, Three new tag teams have been signed to HCW ranks...the First Team, Kamikaze and Madcap...ok. They will be known as HIGH IMPACT. ..The Next Team...David Johnson And Dany Austin..they will be known as "Allied Power" .. I have heard good things about these guys...the LAST Tag Team most recently signed was a team called American Connection, former Champs, they are made up of American Man, and "All American" Mark Thompson. What role will they play? ...it just looks worse for the Title reign of the Party Animals more and more EVERYDAY. Also...Hiro Yuko AND his partner-Vince Viper? -will face the Tag Champs The PARTY ANIMALS this Sunday on Extreme Weekend Review. Non TITLE, by the way. ..Going over THUNDER! last week...WHO WAS that guy that saved ZEX from a beating that night?? Remember- Tom E. Extream, the Official's pet as Zex calls him...and The HUGE tandem of the TWIN TOWERS Attacked the Black King during the Zex/Tom E. match. Also, three men in Ski Masks showed up..and beat on Zex too-Zex tried to fight-but The Twin Towers were TOO much, and THEN a Young Man with blonde hair and sneakers stormed the ring, clearing out The Dark Lords with a Steel Chair. Now Zex didn't see him, but that man didn't tell anyone why he was there-and WHY he saved the Black King of HCW. In other results, Malibu Mike lost to Flex when Johnny Blaze tripped him, Vince Viper BRUTALIZED Stevie Starr, Doomsday SQUASHED two men, Master Blaster annihilated Red Ledret, and Bad 4 Life beat Akira Saito in a handicap match, when Timothy N Turner refused to wrestle. Last I hear, TNT was seeking a singles career in some other fed, and Akira was abandoned! This week, Vince Viper has demanded that he defend his title EVERY show. He has challenged Mike on Tuesday- Poe on Thursday, and next Weekend Extreme, he challenged the man going against DOOMSDAY-Austin Starr. Maybe he Doesn't want this title? Speaking of the Pay Per View- the WHOLE card has not yet been determined..but it seems only 7 matches and the WOWA and XCW matches are signed..the executives expect to sign at least two more matches. I want to see Ben Toussi and Malibu Mike get their HEADS SHAVED! Ha ha. That and the VIOLENT Double HELL Exploding Dynamite board match that Tyson Tercell and Mike Kegonite will face off in! ...and the Heartbreaker/Vince Viper Orlando Beach DEATHMATCH will be worth the price of admission ALONE! I was on the wrestling channel the other day, RSPWF, and caught some of the competition...Get this fans, some of these feds out there CLAIM to be HARDCORE, or EXTREME, but with the "WRESTLING" action their wrestlers- and that term is used VERY loosely, go through, in another 3 years their guys will be BRAINDEAD and sitting in some old fogies home wetting themselves after they break every nervous grip in their bodies! We realize that limbs break, and above all else, make sure the well being of our wrestlers is guaranteed. THAT's why when a Wrestler steps out of line, he is fined. YES, we do put on some BRUTAL action, but it's NEVER Life Threatening. -At least OUR wrestlers know a bit about WRESTLING! These so called Extreme feds -their ah...(coughs) TALENT wouldn't know a ARMBAR from a CROWBAR! It takes discipline, training and SKILL to be a good wrestler..something these other guys DON'T have. It takes a special breed of man to be a good wrestler, and THEN a KICKASS Brawler! ...THAT's HARDCORE. ANYONE can smash another's head into a CAR Window, we at least can use a Front Facelock for a DDT THROUGH the window. Like the wise man once said..There's being EXTREME, then there's just being plain STUPID! Well that's my news and views for THIS WEEK, and I'll see you all next week. Look for a NEW FACE this week on Extreme, and of course, Vocal Explosion- James O'Casey's got HAWK, DEMONA CARFAX, and the PUNC ROKKERZ. Have a good one. [Dial Tone....]
