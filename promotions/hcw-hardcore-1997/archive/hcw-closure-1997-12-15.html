HCW Closes; Merges With WOWA Into a Superfederation

15–16 December 1997

HCW is no more, I have taken it apart for bigger things. HCW, Hardcore Championship Wrestling will be merged with [name withheld]'s WOWA to form a Superfed, with cards beginning this new year. You could call it a Phoenix's birth, from the ashes of the HCW arises a new and soon to be powerful force in EWrestling. So for all the "BIG" leagues in the world of eWrestling, live it up now, because I've been around for almost a year now, and the SuperFed is the revolutionary Federation of the internet, and it hasn't even been born yet! All cards Will be posted on the RSPWF and for those of you who want to join, after we have our fed handlers send in applications, then what spots we have left will be up for grabs. BUT don't think you won't be doing your part. I don't write 60+K great cards to have handlers not participate. No Participation- you're fired. And no coming back either. Also, I have been asked by Future President [name withheld] to let the RSPWF know that we are looking for another Vice Pres. to help with booking and possibly Card writing. IF you have a great imagination, and don't mind working with friendly and dedicated people, and DON'T have your Own side fed, by all means, contact him at [detail withheld] Any questions, ask me. I'll be back. BUT come next year, we'll see which federation has what on the Lists... I'm tired of seeing guys I don't KNOW on these things! ;> AJ Black ************************************************************ "You're a mean one, Mr. Grinch" "You love us, you REALLY love us!" *************************************************************
LOST HORIZONS TO MERGE REMNANTS OF HCW WITH THE WORLD ONLINE WRESTLING ASSOCIATION; **Merger to happen after the WOWA/NWF Pay Per View ----------------- BY SEAN RUSSELL Sports Writer CHICAGO IL. --The powers that be at Lost Horizons have decided to put what's left of the HCW into the WOWA, to create a Superfederation. The Complete merger is not to be set into finality until after the WOWA's showing against the NWF. But then the World Online Wrestling Association will close its doors for good, combining with the elements of HCW and begin operations in the New Year. This means there will be a giant federation of sorts, running out of Chicago that combines both leagues into a jumble- but how to keep the vastly different styles intact? The WOWA has long been prided on it's technical and athletic sports entertainment product, whereas the HCW has been known for it's sadistic violence and daredevil wrestling maneuvers. Reports have already been received that [name withheld], in exchange for selling WOWA to the Lost Horizons company, is headed to be the new President of the Superfederation. While we have not gained specifics on to who much else has been hired, it is apparent that AJ Black will be kept on in some fashion. There will be a Press Conference Wednesday Morning to answer most questions of the press, and I will try to get a special interview with AJ Black to probe in depth as to the reasons for the merger. Rumorhounds have already been saying that federations like the Atlanta-based SAW and the Vancouver-based EWF are in line to be a sort of- "Minor League" federation chain for the Superfed's talent that may be left out... that remains to be seen. Some report that in disconnecting HCW, Lost Horizons has also dissolved the alliances with the SJPW and ULWF, but this has not been confirmed. Check your news Wednesday for my in depth report on the Lost Horizons Press Conference, straight out of the Omni Hotel in uptown Chicago, and hopefully I'll get the word straight from the former President.
