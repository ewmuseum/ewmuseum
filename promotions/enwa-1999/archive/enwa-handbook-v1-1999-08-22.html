Results Writers. You can still be a newsletter writer or results writer and still use a wrestler in the fed to Roleplay. ------------------------------------------------------- Rules of the EnWa: 1. You can have 1 fake wrestler or 1 fake tag team with 1 manager. No rip offs like "Stone Cold" Bob or "Heartbreak" Bill. The name of the game is originality. 2. No TOSing any member or me of the fed! If you do, I don't care if your the World Champ, your fired! People join this fed to have fun and do some role-playing, not to get there AOL account terminated because someone used foul language in there Role-play.
3. All foul language is permitted, but if your on a TV show or something, keep it real and censor it out like f[beep]k or sh*t. 4. No racist remarks whatsoever. No one knows if your opponent in real life is black, white, Hispanic, so don't say anything your not sure of. If you let one slip I won't be mad, but, don't do it all the time. 5. No spamming throughout the fed anything. Whether your selling your Playstation or advertising your fed, don't do it. E-mail me your advertisement and I will put it in the commercials I will have on the cards. 6. If you have a problem with something, Don't start sending one liners like "You copied that Role-play from another fed" Either E-mail me or put a little
OOC (Out Of Character) Note before or after your Role-play.
7. Do not attack me or any other wrestler that isn't yours in your Roleplays. You can use me in your role-play like your talking to me or having a meeting with me, but don't use anyone else without there permission. If you do use someone in an RP without there permission, I will delete the Role-play and it will not count in your match.
8. No stealing anyone's pics, macros, disclaimers, or anything else. If you have there permission then it is fine with me, but, you should give some credit underneath whatever you take to the person who let you use it.
9. We allow any gimmicks as you want here in the EnWa, But don't use real wrestlers sayings or alter them and use it. Example, DON'T use "That's the bottom line because Blaze said so!" 10. If you lose, you lose. Don't whine to the whole fed about it. Simply E-mail me, in a respectable manner, and tell me about it. I will not screw anybody. 11. Seeing that eleven is my lucky number, I will end the rules here with one final rule which is the most important: HAVE FUN! ------------------------------------------------------- Title Information: The EnWa World Heavyweight Title: This is the biggest and best title of the federation. Whoever wins this will have had to put in a lot of hours and a lot of hard work. Since the EnWa is somewhat ran under ExTrEmE rules, the championship can be decided in an extreme match. This title is truly for the
only ELITE wrestlers of the EnWa. It's holder will have to defend it at least on two regular cards a month and every PPV. The EnWa United States Title: Whoever is the keeper of this title is not one to be messed with. This person has also worked really hard for this title. It is the second best in the federation. If you have this people will be trying to take it away from you constantly. Since the EnWa is somewhat ran under
ExTrEmE rules, the championship can be decided in an extreme match.. It is defended at least three regular cards a month and every PPV.
The EnWa Hard-core Title: The third most respectible title in the game. It's designed for the sicko's and the nutcases out there. Whenever defending this championship, the champion and/or the booker, will decide the stipulations of the match. The title must be defended atleast three times a month, and if applicable at the PPV.
The EnWa Television Title: This is the lowest belt in the fed, but, in my opinion, the hardest to keep. For it is defended every card, no matter what.
I will give the champion a break every once in a while and will not force him to wrestle sometimes, but only at the champions request.Since the EnWa is somewhat ran under ExTrEmE rules, the championship can be decided in an extreme match.. The person who holds this nice piece of gold, is surely not one to mess with.
The EnWa Tag Team Titles: These pair of titles is for the two men who can work together the best. They click when it comes time to wrestle. These are really the second best titles you can have, because in order to achieve these, you gotta work your butt off. Since the EnWa is somewhat ran under ExTrEmE rules, the championship can be decided in an extreme match.. They are defended at least three times a month on regular cards and every PPV.

I have been in feds where everyone may have role-played, but not well. The EnWa is gonna be different. That is why there is some info you need to know about how to role-play before you join the EnWa:
---All role-plays must be at least 50 lines. A role-play less than 50 lines, will not be counted. ---This is how I will rate all role-plays for a match. 10% Quantity 75% Quality 15% Originality My Definition of each: Quantity: Simple enough, How big it is. To get these 10 percentage points, all you have to do is meet the Role-play Line Minimum.
Quality: Also simple enough, Don't put out a 500 line role-play thinking your gonna win with that, because no one can put 500 lines of quality stuff in a role-play, if you think you can, try. Also good font size, font style, and very few typos and spelling errors help you here. Please use periods also.
Originality: This is where those of you who do an original role-play. Something I have never seen before. If you have just one where the wrestler comes out and goes to the ring, speaks, and leaves, you won't get too many points here.
---Only size 8 to 10 font size. And try to not use some huge font style, you will lose points in the quality rating. ---Only two RPs a day. And depending on how many days to RP for a card there are, will depend on the RP limit. We don't want any SPAMMERS! ---Always put EnWa somewhere in the subject. Like this "{EnWa} Joe Blow Speaks {EnWa}". Then people will know that your in the EnWa and won't delete your Role-play... ---Don't do something fake, like kill somebody or something. Keep it realistic. ---For those of you who would like to do an interview for a card, write out the interview, and I will take care of it. Only 5 or 6 questions please... ---Use my role-play list and my role-play list only. I will frequently send out new lists so people who have quit don't TOS everybody. ------------------------------------------------------- Pay Per View Information: The Pay Per Views are the biggest events of the month.
