Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The ExTreme National Wrestling Association (EnWa) introduced itself with a recruiting handbook posted 22 August 1999, styling itself “an all FAKE fed” and taking its tagline from its own name: “Some think they are eXtreme, but we _KNOW_ we are Extreme!” The handbook named an on-screen President, a Co-President, a Vice President, and a Commissioner among its staff, alongside separate ring announcers and a full slate of face and heel interviewers.

Five championships were laid out with their own defense schedules: a World Heavyweight Title held to two defenses a month and every pay-per-view; a United States Title defended three times a month; Hardcore and Tag Team Titles under the same three-times-a-month standard; and a Television Title billed as the hardest to keep because, unlike the others, it was defended on every card. Each, per the handbook, could be settled in an extreme-rules match if the champion or booker chose.

Its own card calendar ran six shows a month: three editions of a Monday-night flagship, PaNdEmOnIuM, carrying ten to thirteen matches, a live band, and at least three title shots; two smaller Thursday Night Thrashings cards; and one monthly pay-per-view. Roleplays were graded on a fixed formula — ten percent for meeting a fifty-line minimum, seventy-five percent for quality, and fifteen percent for originality — with a house rule against copying another wrestler's gimmick outright (the handbook's own example: no “'Stone Cold' Bob”) and a cap of two roleplays a day per member.

A follow-up post two days later, on 24 August, promoted the coming title tournaments by naming three wrestlers already signed — Johnny Rebel, Juvy, and Tim Beck — while noting its own website was still under construction.

No card, tournament result, or further notice survives; nothing in the record shows whether the EnWa's own title tournaments were ever run, or when the promotion opened or closed beyond these two documents from August 1999.
