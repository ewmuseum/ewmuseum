Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The Extreme World Wrestling Federation opened its own pitch, on 2 September 1997, by disowning its own name: “It's not extreme!! We are extreme GOOD that's what the extreme stands for!” It named two fed heads, one billed as a veteran of two prior federations who wrote its matches, the other a returning hand from the RWF who built its page; between them, the post promises, one card would run each week, judged purely on roleplay quality rather than length: “we don't use TNM! we create the story.... and the roleplayer the roleplays the best..... WINS MATCHES!!”

Its roster policy was exclusive and stated outright: “WE ONLY USE REAL WWF, WCW and ECW WRESTLERS!!” By the following day it had already signed roleplayers for Stone Cold and the Headbangers, and kept recruiting daily through 4 September for further WWF, WCW or ECW talent, single or tag, with a writing sample required of every applicant.

Its last surviving document, 5 September 1997, is a call for a roleplayer to portray Sycho Sid. No reply survives, and no show, card or result of any kind is in its surviving record.
