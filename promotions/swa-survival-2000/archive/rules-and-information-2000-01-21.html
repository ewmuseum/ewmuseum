Survival Wrestling Alliance Is Up and Running

21 January 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hello there, i see you must be interseted in joining the swa: Survival
Wrestling Alliance, if so. just have a little read of these simple rules and check out
http://www.angelfire.com/wrestling/theswa its that easy! once we have over 8 people we will start rping! ohh how sweet it will be, just send in your app if your rp is good enough your in, i havnt refused an app yet
EVER so you must be good enough! see you on the other side!
Prez Blank Rules and Information 1.) Your Wrestler must be anything, *BUT* you must take itno account that a origional wrestler has a better chance of winning over a non origional. I will let it slide for you to be origional if your name is say Elvis "The
Rock" Prestly or something.
P.S No South Pakr Wrestling... Im F*@#ing SICK OF IT!!!! 2.)Members must be able to produce at least 1-2 Role-Plays per week. These
Role-Plays must be at a minimum of 15 lines (in the text box on rp board), and must be quality work, no crap. If you can not get this much done, give me the reason and I'll more than likely let it slide. If there is no reason presented, I will warn, suspend, or eventually fire you.
3.) Members must be willing to lose some matches for the sake of keeping what I see could be a good angle alive.
4.) Champions must be able to role-play for their matches. If the champion doesn't role-play for their match one of two things will happen: 1-The opponent will automatically win, or 2-The champ will be stripped of the title wich would then be put up in a tourny or battle royal.
5.) Some cards a wrestler will wrestle more than once, sometimes 2 or 3 times. You must be prepared to do so.
6.)All Championship (except world) matches will be held atleast once a month. World Championship matches will be held once a month at a PPV. The only exception with this rule is if the Champion agrees to a match.
7.) Matches are decided strictly on QUALITY role-playing. Do not expect to win with a bunch of words with no meaning.
8.) The card for an event will be posted 4 days before the event. Matches will then be added up untill the day before in which all match forms can no longer be turned in. Results for events will be posted between 1:30 and
10:00 pm, Australian EST. 9.) Cards will be held on Monday (Monday Night Massacre), and Thursday
(Thursday Night Terror). Everyonce in ahwile I might have to skip a show due to being extremely busy in my personal life.(hey i do have a personal life too ya know!)
10.) Role-Plays should not consist of repetitive vulgarity or Profanity. Also, Racial slurs will not be tolerated. Sure this is supposed to be fun, but sometimes being TOO immature is kinda stupid.
11.) Spamming will not be tolerated. I WILL find out who did it and make the proper procedures which is telling the board managment which will find your computer # and eventually catch up with you. Bottom line - don't spam!
12.) All out of character (OOC) messages should be posted on the OOC board. This is what seperates reality from the game. If you want to advertise, go to the OOC board.
13.) Please do not complain over a loss. Hey, you win some you lose some. Besides, if you do lose there has to be an explaination... either you didn't roleplay as good or this is for the angle I talked about earlier. If complaining becomes a problem, I will not hesitate to fire you.
14.) If you come into a fued with an SWA employee (example: Vince McMahon and Stone Cold in the WWF) Do not worry, I will still be fair about
EVERYTHING. 15.) A member may have either two single wrestlers, or 1 tag team and a single wrestler. If you do have more than one wrestler, you may not fight yourself (especially for a title). And you must keep up with your roleplays.
If not, I will cut the wrestler you do not roleplay with. 16.) There will be 4 titles. They are: World, United States, Hardcore and Tag Team, this is because i was in another fed and you really had to work for the titles and thats what im looking for here!
17.) Try not to get TOO into this thing. I mean, I want you to be a loyal member and all, but this is meant to be fun. If it is too much of a hassle, tell me you want a break and relax for a week or so.
