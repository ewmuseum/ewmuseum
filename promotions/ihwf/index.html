Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

International Hardcore Wrestling Federation staged a monthly PPV and a weekly TV show, Saturday Night Slaughter, under five title divisions (World, Intercontinental, Associate, Extreme, Tag Team). Its own rules page states the fed's own founding principle plainly: "NO REAL WRESTLERS!!!!! We think it's important to create your own persona." The held corpus documents two eras: a fully dated launch running 27 January–31 March 2001 on the fed's own served Champions page, and a later reopening — the fed's own served news article, "The Insider," carries a CEO press-conference announcement of the fed's return after two years of dormancy, bylined 8/2/2003, and a three-hour Saturday Night Slaughter broadcast opens on the same announcement ("the return of the IHWF").

The fed's own served Champions page runs a fully dated World Title chain from its own vacant start. Apocalypse won the belt's own founding reign ("Beginning of an Era") on 18 February 2001, defeating the Godfather. After an intervening reign for Vaughn, the Godfather won the title back from Vaughn on 31 March 2001 — marked "Close" on the fed's own ledger, its own last-attested 2001 reign. The same page dates fully chained three-reign histories for the other four divisions across the same stretch.

At the later reopening, the Godfather — by his own words no longer champion — defeats Chaos to open the broadcast's own third hour, then is ambushed by Enforcer with a lead pipe. The broadcast closes on its own main event, Jason Slade against Max Powers, ending with no official winner after a stable the fed's own announcers name on-air, Ultimate Justice, runs in twice.
