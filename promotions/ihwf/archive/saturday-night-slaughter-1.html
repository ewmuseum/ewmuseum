IHWF's Saturday Night Slaughter, Hour One

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Saturday Night Slaughter "Fuel" by Metallica blares overhead as the jampacked arena in Chicago is going wild. Fans are holding up there various signs, supporting there wrestling heros as the camera pans over the tons of wild fans. The camera focuses on the announcers, Chip and Marcus as they begin the new era of the IHWF Chip: Folks, welcome to the return of the IHWF here as tonite, we have one hell of an opening card. Marcus: I'm glad to be back in the helm here in the IHWF..I was getting tired of sitting around, worshipping Slade all the time...and speaking of Slade.. Chip: Enough already about Slade, who is back here in the IHWF, along with many other people here..Ultimate Justice, Enforcer, Chaos, and many, many more superstars. Marcus: But enough about that, more about Slade. Chip: God, I think we've had enough already...but the question is, will the wrestlers here make this IHWF stand memorable? Will something good come out of all of this? Marcus: I truly hope so..and there is bound to be more talent introducing themselves into this federation in the upcoming weeks. Chip: So without further ado.. Suddenly, "Dead Bodies Everywhere" by KoRn plays overhead as the fans go wild. Atomic Mass comes strolling out, business suit and all as the fans chant "Mass" loudly thruout the entire arena. Mass strolls into the ring, staring at the crowd around him as he holds the mic to his mouth Mass: Ladies and Gentlemen, I'm glad to give to you all..THE IHWF!! The fans go crazy as Mass circles the ring a couple of times, taking in the applause Mass: Yes, the IHWF is back in business and this time around, I hope things can be different.this time around. I hope the IHWF holds..and tonite, we will see the beginning of those roots starting to take hold. To grasp this company and to branch out across the United States and further. We will become something once more, and with all the talent in the back room..and more to come, things are looking up here in the IHWF...so with out further ado, our very first match tonite.. Mass leaves the ring as the many fans are anxious to see who's up first tonite. William McCoy vs Mayhem "Born on the Bayou" by Credence Clearwater Revival as the fans are a bit mixed. William McCoy comes walking out, wearing some high water jeans as the fans are beginning to get into him a bit. He climbs into the ring as he awaits for Mayhem to come out. "Are You Ready?" by KoRn plays overhead as the fans are awaiting for Mayhem. But Mayhem doesn't come out as McCoy is storming around the ring. Chip: Where the hell is Mayhem right now? Cameras pan backstage as they find Mayhem resting a pile of plywood, his bodies splattered across the broken pallets..Blood is pouring from his forehead..The camera pans back onto McCoy as he is standing there, laughing his head off. He asks for the mic as he looks at the crowd. McCoy: You see that? People around here take me for a joke..but no sir-ee...I did that to Mayhem, yes, me!! He didn't show up all week and then attempted to waste my time by wrestling? Ain't happening. That's my little show of gratitude towards him. Next week Mass..I want a real challenge, one that will actually give me my time worth spending..and that's all I've got to say on that one. With that McCoy walks out of the ring..the fans not quite sure what to make of him. Chip: Strange indeed..but he proved his point here tonite. Chip: Well, this next match ought to prove interesting. Marcus: In one corner, we have the man formely known as Suicide..who in turn would like to now be called Stevie Rich. Chip: Then we have Scotty Richards, a man seemed, well, how can I put this...distraught in his promos. Marcus: This match is definetly going to be worth watching as we see who exactly get the upper hand here today. Scotty Richards vs Stevie Rich All the lights in the arena shut offas complete silence and darkness has taken over. The silence soon ends when the word "SCOTTY" in big blue, bold letters can be seen on the Hardcore-tron, Fading in and out 5 times. After the 5th fade out, complete darkness takes over the arena once again, as the fans start a huge: "SCOTTY SUCKS" chant and then over the speakers a very loud: FUCK OFF!!! is heard, followed by a huge explosion of pyro in the entrance way, as Nookie by Limp Bizkit hits the speakers, the fans going completely insane. From behind the curtains, "Sensational" Scotty Richards struts out, an ice cold Molson Canadian in his right hand, and his left hand raised high in the air, giving the crowd , the wrestlers, and the entire wrestling industry, the middle finger. He struts to the ring, the fans giving him all kinds of hell as he can only laugh. "King of the Closet" by Blindside comes on as the fans reactions are mixed. Stevie Rich comes walking out, wearing blue jeans and a Blindside T-Shirt as he tries to get the crowd going. Stevie Rich slides into the ring, taking his shirt off and tossing to the floor below. He stares contently at Scotty Richards.. Scotty Richards nails Stevie Rich with a clothesline as the ref calls for the bell. Scotty Richards picks up Stevie Rich and nails him with two quick punches and tosses him towards the ropes. Stevie Rich reverses and nails Scotty Richards with a shoulder block, sending him hard to the mat. Scotty Richards gets up as Stevie Rich grabs him and executes a body slam on Scotty Richards. Stevie Rich bounces off the ropes and drops an elbow on Scotty Richards as he goes for the cover.. 1..kickout Stevie Rich picks up Scotty Richards but Scotty Richards squeezes out an eye poke, sending Stevie Rich a little confused. Scotty Richards throws him to the ropes and attempts a gorilla press slam but Stevie Rich counters and nails Scotty Richards with an inverted DDT. The crowd goes wild as Stevie Rich gets on the top ropes and taunts to them. He leaps off, attempting a huge frog splash, but Scotty Richards is quick to move out of the way. Stevie Rich lands hard as Scotty Richards rolls out of the ring and lifts up the ring apron. Chip: Well, what are we going to pull out from underneath the ring today? A table, chair, what?! Marcus: You never know what those ring boys leave under there!! Scotty Richards pulls out a ladder as he slides it into the ring. He jumps into the ring as Stevie Rich is there to greet him on the ring apron. Stevie Rich attempts to suplex him back into the ring, but Scotty Richards reverses, sending Stevie Rich onto the floor below. Scotty Richards jumps down as he grabs Stevie Rich and sends him into the guardrail. He grabs the chair from the ring announcer and gives Stevie Rich a good "whack" over the head. Stevie Rich falls to the mat as Scotty Richards gets back into the ring. He sets the ladder onto the turnbuckle as he slides back out and tosses Stevie Rich before he got counted out. Scotty Richards picks up Stevie Rich and tosses him into the ropes and nails him with a vicious atomic drop. He takes Stevie Rich and tosses him hard into the turnbuckle, sending him crashing into the ladder. Scotty Richards runs to the other turnbuckle and attempts to splash Stevie Rich, but Stevie Rich reverses, catapulting Scotty Richards right into the ladder. Scotty Richards falls down with the ladder landing on top of him. A smile emits from Stevie Rich as he climbs the top ropes. He jumps off and nails Scotty Richards with a top rope Guillotine Leg Drop. Stevie Rich throws off the ladder and goes for the cover... 1..2..kickout Chip: I'll tell you what, he barely made it out of that pin there Marcus. Marcus: What a devastating move there by Stevie Rich, taking advantage of the situation and showing who truly deserves to be the victor here! Stevie Rich takes the ladder and sets it up in the middle of the ring. Stevie Rich attempts to pick up Scotty Richards, but Scotty Richards rolls Stevie Rich into a small package. 1..2.. Stevie Rich kicks out as both men get on their feet. Scotty Richards nails him with a few massive rights and then nails him with a huge vertical suplex as Scotty looks around, noticing the ladder. He picks up the ladder and begins to climb to the very top of it. Suddenly, the crowd is going wild as Mercenary comes running out from the back, carrying a chair with him Marcus: Who's coming out now? Chip: I don't know, but it seems like we are going to have some company... Stevie Rich gets up as he begins to talk with the referee. Mercenary slides in and shoves the ladder, sending Scotty Richards flying. He lands on the ropes, nailing him in the crotch with some fierce intensity. Mercenary turns Stevie Rich around and plants a chair right into his face. Stevie Rich is out cold as Mercenary looks down towards Scotty Richards and mutters a few choice words. Winner: None
