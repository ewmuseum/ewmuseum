RULES OF THE IHWF Welcome to the IHWF!!! Please read the following as I, the president of the IHWF, shall explain exactly how the IHWF functions and why the IHWF promises to be the most fun and easiest to compete in e-feds on the Internet today. First things first NO REAL WRESTLERS!!!!! We think it's important to create your own persona. You can use the gimmicks but we feel it's more important to be creative. The most wrestlers one person can have is four (4). We will have a PPV once a month. The PPV is where all the headline events and matches will be taking place. There are also one weekly show, which take place on Saturday night. Saturday Night Slaughter. Only the biggest brightest hardest working wrestlers get main event time. At the moment there are only 5 different title divisions: WORLD HEAVYWEIGHT BELT INTERCONTINENTAL BELT ASSOCIATE BELT TAG TEAM BELTS EXTREME BELT Wrestlers can not hold more then one singles title at one time. A wrestler can though hold a singles title along with half of the tag team titles. If you have the chance to win two singles titles and you succeed at doing it, you will be forced to drop one of the titles. Which one is your choice. Any wrestler can challenge any of the champions for any title. However you can only do it if you are on the rankings. A few ways to get on the rankings is to generate heat. You can generate heat two ways. Number one is by competing regularly. Get a match on every show every show every single match you can the more you wrestler the more the crowd knows you and the more the bookers will see your talent. The second way is to get in the ring. When you get in the ring you are actually roleplaying making challenges and talking about how you got screwed or you completely blew someone away. These next two thing are probably the biggest rules that the IHWF has. Number one...You are not allowed to attack another IHWF wrestler in your roleplays unless you have the guys permission. People don't like it when you use them so try to avoid it unless you have there permission. Number two is that all roleplays must exceed fifteen lines. This should be up around twenty-five to thirty though. Twenty-five to thirty is the recommended minimum of lines that we think you should have. If you can't roleplay over fifteen lines of work, then you shouldn't be rolplaying at all. That's the way we see it. Violation of these last two rules may result in your wrestler begin suspended from the IHWF. If you contantly break these rules, you wrestler will be fired. This is everyone's warning! But most importantly, let's try to remember this is just a game. Some people take this way to seriously and in turn, one bad apple spoils the rest of them. Let's not turn this into a warzone...accusing others of something that isn't true or is just "character". This is a game, lets just have fun!! Go back to IHWF!
