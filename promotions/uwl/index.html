Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Formed on America Online on 8 January 1997 by a founder billed "Krazy Dogg," the Ultimate Wrestling League moved to the web that August. Its own rules page states plainly, "Real and Fake wrestlers allowed" — a blended roster it kept through a 2003-era relaunch and a rules revision as late as December 2004.

The UWL World Heavyweight Championship's own served history runs a fully dated chain of fifteen reigns across fourteen holders. Masked Menace held the belt's own founding reign from 11 January 1997; Black Shadow alone held it twice, in April and again from July to September that year; Chris Jericho won it last on 29 October 1999, before the ledger's own final entry records it vacant from the turn of the millennium.

Five further titles change hands or stand defended across the fed's own Summer Blast '99 (20 June 1999), a nine-segment PPV that survives whole but for its own main event: Frost won the United States Championship from Stone Cold Steve Austin, the Double "A" Express retained the Tag-Team Championship twice in one night, Chris Jericho defended both the Hardcore and Cruiserweight Championships, and Ripper retained the Television Championship. The card opens with Taz submitting Dean Malenko to the Tazmission in a Hardcore, Falls Count Anywhere match, and turns mid-card on the rWo unveiling three new arrivals before they turn on their own mouthpiece. Its own World Heavyweight main event breaks off at a link-out before the finish — the fed's own title-history ledger alone records Punishing One winning the belt from Psycho that night.
