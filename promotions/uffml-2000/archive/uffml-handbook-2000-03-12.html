UFFML Handbook

12 March 2000

Contents   1  -  Intro 2  -  Rules 3  -  Join 4  -  Titles 5  -  Shows 6  -  Fighters   1 --- Intro    The UFFML was fromed on the 10th of March and was officially opened on the 12th of March. It's basically a fed to have fun in and also a place where newcomers to the e-fed scene can prepare themselves for the
"harsh-reality" of big-time e-feds! Roleplaying is not really accounted for as ya can choose to write 2, 4, 20 or even 15 lines for your rps
--- cos the results are simmed. Even if they are written, the winners will be simmed. The winners are NOT pre-determined, even in the case of an angle, which will not happend every now and then.
  2 --- Rules   Here are the complete set of rules; slightly outdated by a few hours but they have meaning :
  1. The UFFML is NOT really a wrestling fed; we have some basics of a wrestling fed but we are mostly a fighting-fed!! Look it up a dictionary if ya don't know what that means. We accept any kind of wrestlers but originals are preferred! And interferences will be minimal as all Standard Matches will be fought under No-Holds-Barred Rules!
2. If you have sent in your application, please wait for a reply stating you have been accepted before you start roleplaying! And after you've been accepted, remember that I will only update the Singles Roster on Saturdays so ya don't have to wait till I put ya up to start rping!!
3. Upon notification of the fighter's acceptance, they will be put on a one week probation. Their performance will be evaluated by RPs. If they are not up to scratch, they will receive a warning before they are
"forced" to be a jobber. 4. Don't curse too much and keep down insulting the other person's parents! 5. After you've roleplayed and set up a match,please inform the staff at : [detail withheld] of the match or else the match won't take place!   6. If you wanna interfere in a match on a particular card, or do an interview on a particular card, e-mail the booker of that respective card! But not all interferences will be accepted; read Rule 1.
7. You can only handle 4 wrestlers at one time and each wrestler can only have 2-5 valets! If you do handle 4 wrestlers, only 2 of them can be single-wrestlers; the other 2 must be a tag-team.
8. If for some reason,you are unable to roleplay for some time, please inform the staff! 9. For those E-Fed owners who wish to advertise their site,please do so on the OOC Board! 10. If you want to reproduce any part of this site,please e-mail Mr X at : [detail withheld] !! 11. Please do not pretend to be someone else because the IP numbers will reveal everything! 12. I pick the winners based on roleplays and creativity. For your rps,you have to write above 5 lines but don't get carried away and write a 200-liner which is out-of-point for at least 150 lines. If your rp is long but doesn't make sense because of spelling and grammatical mistakes or it isn't to the point,I'll more likely pick a short but more sensible and direct rp!!!For creativity,ya can use music,videos,photos and different fonts and colors for your rps but stick to the point and don't overdo it. Remember, you can write just up to 10 lines for your rps!
13. If ya lose a match,don't bitch about it saying you got screwed or that your rps were better then the other guy's!! It may be better to you but I and the other bookers make the final decision!!!
14. Only The Commish and The Prez can book title-matches. The only way the other staff-members can book title-matches is if the person higher than him/her is out-of-action.
15. Only The Commish has the right to appoint special refs for any kind of match,even a UFFML
Ultimate Heavyweight Title Match!!!! He can even choose himself to be the ref and there isn't anything even the Prez can do about it!!!
16. Only The Prez has the right to suspend an UFFML Superstar if he has committed a dastardly act!!! 17. If you lose a title,you have up to 4 days to ask for a rematch! 18. I get more Internet time on weekends so expect major site-updates on the weekends!! 19. Other than signing-up or signing matches,all other stuff including angles and interference-requests should be directed to :  [detail withheld]   ! 20. These are the ways results will be done ; Match-Summary (WCW.com), Short Match-Summarries (WWF.com) and Simming. Match-Summary means that we will only write the important things that happened in each match and in each backstage scene. Short Match-Summaries means that we will only write a few lines on what happened in each match; see WWF.com TV's section to see what we mean. Simming will be done using a Zeus Pro Simulator and i'll touch up the results cos Zeus tends to repeat the same thing over and over again. Most of the time, we will use technique 1 or 3 but Simming will be done most of the time.
21. Have fun!       3--- Join   Tell me these things if ya wanna join :   Real Name E-Mail   FIGHTER'S NAME : anybody's acceptable but original characters preferred. no spin-offs. HEIGHT : make it reasonable and make sure it co-ordinates well with your weight; the shortest ya can be is 5'7". WEIGHT : make it reasonable and make sure it co-ordinates well with your height; the lightest ya can be is 200 lbs. HOMETOWN : either a real one or a made-up one will do. this is a MUST. BIRTHDAY (day/month/year) : don't give me your birthday; i want your wrestler's b-day. and yes, this is a
MUST. VALET : once again, either a real one or a made-up will do and this ain't a must. but if ya wanna have a valet, please check to see that your choice has not already been taken.
ALIGNMENT (face/neutral/heel) : this ain't hard to understand, right?   ENTRANCE MUSIC : either a real theme or a made-up theme will do. if it's real, supply me with a URL if possible. also, check to see that your choice of theme hasn't already been taken.
RING INTRO : this is a MUST and make sure it's real good cos it will be on your profile, if ya get one.
GIMMICK : is this too hard to understand? tell me what kind of character your wrestler has.
APPEARANCE : please be clear and precise cos your "physical-profile" may be done. do everything from facial-features to scars in unimaginable places.
HISTORY : this is a MUST and must be done in 2 parts. first, tell me all the titles you've got in other feds and please tell me the full-name of those feds. secondly, you can tell me that he was brought up a tortured soul and was arrested for something he didn't do, stuff like that.
WRESTLING-STYLES : well-seasoned brawler, technical-submissionist; you tell me what styles your wrestlers excels at. REQUIRED. FAV. QUOTE : <i>not a must but preferred. and just give me ONE quote.   TRADEMARK MOVES (between 5 and 9) : moves your character does regularly and is unique to him (describe all created-moves concisely). put your set-up move first.
FINISHER : the move that wins the match for your character. you can only supply me with 2 finishers (describe created-move/moves concisely).   SAMPLE RP (at least 10 lines) : this is a MAJOR-MUST. please do it in normal-sized font; don't try to cheat. ADDITIONAL-INFO : tell me whatever ya wanna tell me here; whether my fed rules, the fact that u can only rp on certain days, u'd like to be involed in certain divisons; anything.
  4 --- Titles   Ultimate Heavyweight Title --- the biggest title. Win this twice and u'll be promoted to the UFF.   Gang-Fight Titles --- the tag-team titles of the fed; fought under 4-Corner Elimination Rules
  United-States Title --- whoever holds this title is automatically the #1 Contender for the UH Title; all US Title Matches will be fought under Ladder Match Rules
  Television Title --- whoever holds this title is can only be the #1 Contender for the US Title if he beats the Mad-Man Champion in a non-title match. Fought under Dumpster Match Rules.
  Mad-Man Title --- the Hardcore Title of the fed; whoever holds this title can only be the #1 Contender for the US Title if he beats the TV
Champion in a non-title match. Fought under Hell-House Match Rules (only in the UFF & UFFML).
  Lightweight Title --- only for wrestlers under 230 lbs.   3-Men-Gang --- the 3-Men Tag-Team Titles of the fed       5--- Shows   We have 2 weekly shows; Wednesday Night Storm & Sunday Night Hell. Storm is like the NITRO of the UFFML. We are also thinking of having house-shows where matches are made up on the fly and are non-title.
  6--- Fighters   So far, we have 3 jobbers to pick on and 5 bona-fide fighters - Jack,
Boy-Z, Jo, Zo & Bo. All the fighters have good promise and if ya join, ya can choose to be a fake fighter or a real one; it's up to ya!!!
      SO, IS YOUR MOUTH WATERING ALREADY? IF NOT, JUST GIVE THE uffml A TRY AND SEE HOW IT'S LIKE! COME ON, IT'S JUST FOR FUN!!
