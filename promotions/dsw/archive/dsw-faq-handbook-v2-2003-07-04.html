IN CHARACTER - Diamond Star Wrestling is a small, indepdenant women's ONLY wrestling federation based out of Carson City, Nevada. We operate on a very small budget, financed only by revenue and our owner's cash flow, so you won't see any three story projection screens, massive fireworks displays, or sold out 100,000 seat venues (or 10,000 seat venues for that matter). Hell, it'll probably be awhile before we expand beyond Carson City, even! B. OUT OF CHARACTER - DSW is a booked (for any definitions you don't under- stand, see Section IX: HELP! I don't know what _______ means!) e-wrestling federation, ran by ME, [name withheld] (see next section). Read on for more info! DSW was created by [name withheld], who stepped down from the promotion in June of 2003, upon which I took the sum'bitch over! ======================== II. FINE, WHO RUNS IT? ======================== A. [name withheld] - Out of Character, I'm the Diamond Star Wrestling fedhead, which means I'm the boss! I give out title shots for sex! Er...yeah... Anyway, I'm the ultimate authority as to what goes on here. If I don't think an angle is suitable, I can drop it. All decisions are made by me...ME ME ME!!! If you need to reach me, feel free to drop me a line on AIM: [detail withheld], or email me at [detail withheld]! I've been in e-wrestling for going on seven years, handling the below character since my inception. I've got a good deal of experience as a fedhead, running the World Internet Wrestling Federation, a simmed league, in 1998, the New Wave Wrestling Alliance, which I took over from Gordon Adair, at various intervals in 1999-2000, and FEAR, which I ran shortly in 2001. B. MICHAEL PRICE - Michael Price, better known, perhaps, as "The Walking Contradiction" Sabbath, purchased Diamond Star Wrestling from it's former owner, Brenden Bowmer, in June of 2003, for an unknown sum of money. The former owner, Bowmer, was suffering a severe case of burn out, and as such sold out the company to Price. Price has wrestled the world over, and currently competes in OWE, IWF/WOW, IWE, and KAWF, and is perhaps best known for his stints in the UWF and NWWA, the latter of which he also ran for a time. Aside from DSW, he owns the FEAR Academy, a VERY small promotion/wrestling school trying to make a name for themselves. Just a note: Price will VERY rarely be on television, as his wrestling schedule prevents it. Therefore he will only VERY rarely take part in any angles, and that's ONLY if a handler (that is, you guys) requests it. ================================= III. WHAT TITLES DOES DSW RECOGNIZE? ===================================== A. DIAMOND STAR TITLE - The Diamond Star Championship is the most prestigious title in the DSW. Title holders must defend it at least once per cycle (spanning from the beginning of the first show after a supercard, to the end of the next supercard itself). If you can grab hold of this, consider your self to be the best of the best in DSW! B. SHOOTING STAR TITLE - Secondary to the Diamond Star title, the Shooting Star Championship is for the up and comers of the promotion! Normally, the lady who holds this belt is considered the heir to the throne of the Diamond Star Title! This belt must be defended at least ONCE a month, but more than likely, every UNLEASHED! If you can hold onto this one, you can be sure that a Diamond Star Shot is coming up for you one of these days! B. GEMINI TAG TEAM TITLES - The only tag team titles in DSW, the Gemini Titles are currently suspended until we can get a more active tag team roster. But they are the tag team equivalent to the Diamond Star Title. ============================= V. WHAT SHOWS DOES DSW RUN? ============================= A. UNLEASHED! - The flagship show of DSW, running bi-weekly on Thursdays, on Channel 29, UNLEASHED! is where the major happenings of DSW will take place! It will normally feature 4-6 matches fully written, and oncard segments from all the top DSW Superstars. As DSW grows, we will try to add more matches as we get the writers. DSW will be hosted by Kenny Callighan and Freddy Nuckels. B. However, if DSW grows to the point where we need two full time shows, Beauty Calls will ultimately become similar to UNLEASHED!: fully written matches and major happenings. Beauty Calls will be hosted by Freddy Nuckels. C. SUPERCARDS - Supercards are where feuds are finished and the really big matches take place, and will take place on Sundays, typically every 3 or so months, at the end of each cycle. All titles will be on the line at these shows, and they will be hosted by Kenny and Freddy. D. OTHER MAILINGS TO WATCH FOR 1. Now remember, these are just rumors, so they may not be true! 2. This is VERY important...always pay attention to the Updates! ============================ VI. WHAT'S EXPECTED OF ME? ============================ A. ROLEPLAYING (NOTE: See "Helpful Hints" section for more info on Roleplaying) 1. FLASHES - Flashes, or promos, are where characters build up heat for their upcoming matches. running smack on their opponents and basically trying to further the angle. These are typically pre- recorded segments, taking place in ANY number of situations, and will be shown on the Beauty Calls show, every other Thursday. 2. STRATEGIES - Strategies, or Strats, are perhaps the greatest bit of roleplaying one can do for DSW, as without them, when your matches are written, all we'll have to go buy is your application. Strats are basically what you want to do during matches: including, how your character enters the ring, what she will do during and after the match, and maybe some spots. 3. ONCARDS - Oncards are, well, anything that takes place on a card, or show. In-ring interviews, backstage interviews, attacks...you get the picture. The point of oncards is to help further your character's Angle.... 4. ANGLES - Angles are the lifeblood of wrestling: they're the story, or the feud, between two or more wrestlers. Remember "Stone Cold" Steve Austin vs. Mr. McMahon a few years ago? That's an angle. The Rock vs. Hulk Hogan? Angle. Triple H marrying Stephanie McMahon and gaining an obscene amount of backstage power? An...no, wait, that's really happening. Well, you get the point. 5. MISC. - Any other form of Roleplaying you can think of? Commercials for your character's merchandise? Writing commercials period to make a show more realistic? Music videos? B. OTHER PARTICIPATION 1. MATCH WRITING - Okay, these matches on UNLEASHED! have to come from somewhere. That's why we need MATCH WRITERS!!! YAAAAAAAAAY!!! Now, I know people don't like writing matches, but all DSW needs is EACH handler to write ONE match per month. That's all it takes to make DSW successful. However, Match Writing is strictly optional. It's NOT a requirement, although it can help your push. 2. TAKING AN INTEREST - What does this mean? Well, basically, it means caring about Diamond Star Wrestling. Not just YOUR angle or character, but about the fed as a whole. Offering suggestions. Getting roleplaying material in on time. Writing matches. Anything that helps make the fed a better place! ========================== VII. WHAT ARE THE RULES? ========================== NOTE: MORE MAY BE ADDED LATER! A. IN CHARACTER - Just a few... 1. HELP! I DON'T KNOW WHAT _______ MEANS! ============================================ Okay, I know many people reading this know e-wrestling (and wrestling) for that matter, inside and out. However, that may not always be the case. I also know that there's not many newbies who come into e-wrestling will want to create a female character. But, it has happened. So...for those of you who aren't familiar with some of the terms, here's some help. Those who are, just skip this section. More terms may be added later, as I get the chance to. But for now, I'm sticking with just the basics that apply to DSW. E-WRESTLING - Electronic wrestling, or play by email wrestling. E-FED - E-wrestling Federation. A play by email wrestling promotion. FEDHEAD - The guy (or girl) who books the matches, decides the winners, and puts the show together, as well as other duties. BOOKED E-FED - An e-fed where the winners are determined based on the work the participants have done, as opposed to a SIMMED or DICED e-fed. DSW is a booked e-fed. Booked feds also have fully written matches usually. SIMMED E-FED - An e-fed where winners are determined through a simulation program, such as TNM or Zeus, often randomly. DICED E-FED - A fed where the winners are decided through a set of rules and dice rolls, based on wrestler statistics. Similar to Dungeons and Dragons. HANDLER - You...the person who creates a character and plays out her career. CARD - The show. Monday Night RAW and SmackDown! are real life cards. MATCHES - Where the action takes place. There are several different types of matches, including Play By Play (Commentary only, with no action written out), Clipped (in which only the high spots are shown, w/commentary), Summary (a short description of what happened), and Action Block System (commentary and action are seperate, and the action takes place between brackets). DSW uses ABS for UNLEASHED! and Clipped for Beauty Calls. SPOT - Or, highspot. A major move in a match, such as a chokeslam from the top rope, shooting star press, etc. JOB - To lose a match. HEAT - The reaction a wrestler gets to her actions. JOSHI - Japanese for women's wrestling. ========================= X.
