Kugi [0-2-0] Not Ranked: Tag Team Rankings =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= Gemini Champions: - Extreme Measures [1-0-0] =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= SCHEDULE =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= DSW UNLEASHED! -------------- July 10, 2003 -------------- DSW is hot off the heels of one of the hottest editions of UNLEASHED! since it's inception! We can only assume that this week will be even better, as KUGI locks horns with LARA LEIGH LOVE in our opening contest! LOVE has to be thinking of what KUGI did to TARA MARSHALL last week, and will also have to be looking over her shoulder for TALIA WICHZINI! "THE GODDESS" NINA LARUE puts her skills against those of "BATTLE PRINCESS" MISAKI ISHIKAWA in the second match of the evening! Can LARUE handle the tazer wielding "BATTLE PRINCESS?" Will the BLOODSUCKING EDITOR show up? Guess we'll have to see... The DSW SHOOTING STAR TITLE is up for grabs again as THE BLOODSUCKING EDITOR takes on NAOMI ISHIKAWA! These are two of (in this intrepid reporter's opinion) two of DSW's cutest stars, but this match should be anything BUT. Expect a very high flying encounter here! But of course, the BSE has to be on her guard for her rival, MISAKI ISHIKAWA! The question is...does MISAKI want the title for herself, or will she be happy putting it in the hands of her older sister? The MAIN EVENT this week promises to be something, as the alliance of DSW DIAMOND STAR CHAMPION IRIS GALIVER and TALIA WICHZINI tries to fend off the duo of former champ POLARIS, and women's legend "TORNADO" TARA SMITH! The hatred here is intense, folks...expect a lot of blood and broken bones... ....when DSW is UNLEASHED!!! DSW BEAUTY CALLS ---------------- August 24, 2003 ---------------- Beauty Calls will be seen in a special pre-show format in the hours before Clash of the Valkyries! Expect to hear comments from everyone involved in the show, as well as a few matches perhaps! DSW CLASH OF THE VALKERIES! --------------------------- August 24, 2003 --------------------------- DSW returns with it's second supercard! No plans have been set, but expect to see some wild and wooly action, as well as title defenses from The BLOODSUCKING EDITOR and IRIS GALIVER....IF they can hold onto their gold! =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= DEADLINES =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= NOTE: Everything is due at 11:59 PM on these days!
