IAWF Charter, and Heat Wave Free-per-View card, 15 July 1997

15 July 1997

_________ _______ | /\ / | / \ /\ / | /____\ / \ /_____ | / \ / \ / ___|___/ \/ \/ International Allied Wrestling Federation What is the IAWF? -What does the IAWF stand for? -How are matches played out? -Rules and guidelines -Titles -Cards and Free-per-Views -Signing up - What does IAWF stand for? The IAWF stands for the International Allied Wrestling Federation. It is a new federation created from the ashes of 3 pen and paper federations I managed while attending college. Taking what I consider to be the best of the best in professional wrestling, mixing them in with "homemade" superstars, and tossing the rulebook into the shredder, this league stands for hardcore athleticism, extreme competition, and an Iron Man will to survive. It is up to you to create your champion, and originality of gimmick can be just as important as your skills inside the sqaured circle. - How are matches played out? At the risk of being uncool, this is a simmed federation. Matches are played out in TNM 6.2 based on statistics you have provided for your wrestler. That means that no outcomes are fixed in any way, so your wrestler had better be damn good in order to compete. The reason I have decided to avoid fixing matches is to give all contenders a fair shake at the gold, as well as settling "who would beat who" scenarios based on how good a wrestler really is, rather than jobbing off superior athletes. This applies to all contenders of the IAWF, so have no fear that El Presidente (me) will always be wearing gold. That said, an interesting twist has been added to the mix in order to please the fans of role-play wrestling. Alongside the TNM simulator, I have written a program that takes numerous factors into consideration, and provides me with the statistics that I enter into TNM's simulator database. Extra factors such as ring presence, interview ability, gimmick originality, etc., all add (or subtract from) to the total points of the chractesristics that the TNM simulator uses. Posting interviews, issuing challenges, match interference, and a general participation beyond joining the federation will add points in favor of your wrestler. The flipside of this coin being that too much participation can be detrimental to the success of your competitor, as he/she will subbcumb to the effects of fatigue. Your wrestler will be as good as you make him/her, and overworking will play as much a part of your success (or lack of) as underworking. This is not official, but I *may* add 1 or 2 role plays per week. This will be for experienced role players to showcase their talents. This will be done if there is a great enough interest. - Rules and guidelines 1) Do not ask for a victory. The match results are based on how you have created and develloped your wrestler. The more time you take to devellop your competitor, the more likely your database stats will show positive growth, resulting in more victories. As a guide, try to give an interview or post a challenge (see below for challenge guidelines) at least once every one to two weeks for your wrestlers. 2) Posted interviews don't have to be overly fancy. In most cases, interviews will be as simple as responding to another wrestler's challenge or comments. In fact, the easiest way to write interviews is to make challenges to other wrestlers. That said, I've noticed that there is sometimes a problem with trying to post an interview. If you get message saying you have already signed the guestbook, try to add your interview the next time you connect to your internet provider. If you are still having trouble, email the interview to [detail withheld] 3) When posting, remember that swearing is allowed to a certain extent. If your interview relys on your wrestler's ability to use profanity as much as possible, your interview will be edited. I do not wish to be responsible for any complaints to Geocities because of excessive foul language. Consider it a challenge to you to come up with an intelligent way to say what you want to say without venturing into Steve Austin territory (think Hawk of the Road Warriors). 4) When making a challenge or when asking for co-operation, remember that you can only challenge 5 ranks above or 5 ranks below your wrestler's current ranking for a match. Any challenges made outside of the viable range will be considered a feud in the making. In this case, it will up to the lower ranked wrestler to generate heat by keeping the character active in the ring (either in matches, or through intereference in other matches) and posting interviews to keep the fued going. When the matches finally play out (usually between 3-5 matches, with a possibility for up to 9 in the case of a very hot feud, in order to see who the "better" wrestler is) a set of "closing" interviews will be expected from each participant in order to signal to other competitors that he/she is now ready to move on to a new feud. 5) There will be a new form for posting in the event you want to interfere in another person's match. When interfering in another match : - No beatings resulting in hospitilization - No insanely one sided brawling (the person(s) you attack will be able to fight back) - Must be someone you are currently feuding with or have posted a challenge to - Titles The title situation in the IAWF is realitvely straight forward. One active title for every 20 contenders. A beta run has led me to the conclusion that 20 per title creates the maximum amount of title opportunities while allowing champs to keep the gold for a reasonable amount of time. While 20 wrestlers doesn't create a nice easy tournament layout in order to determine who will fight for the gold (all championship titles are initially awarded via single elimination tournament), I have worked a solution around the seeding problem. Before the tournament, a battle royal will be held to determine the top 16 competitors (20 man battle with the first four eliminated out of the tournament and seeding based on inverse order of elimination) for the title tournament. Current Active Titles : IAWF World Iron Man Championship Title IAWF International Women's Championship IAWF World Tag Team Championship - New As this is an Iron Man style of league, the highest prize is the IAWF World Iron Man Heavyweight Championship title. This belt goes to the toughest of the tough and the best of the best. In order to make sure this belt is awarded to the conteneder with the right stuff, this belt can, and will only be defended in one of four ways ( for now ): - Iron man match : He who wins the most falls after 60 minutes - Submission/I Quit match : No countout, no DQ. make your opponent give up, you get the gold. - Street fight : Again, no countout or DQ. Anything goes and falls count anywhere in the building. - Death match : To win this match you must take 3 consecutive falls over your opponent. - Exception : a triangle match may be ordered in the case of there being two equally legitimate contenders for the championship. For the Iron Ladies, there is the IAWF International Women's title. The holder of this belt is the best of the best when it comes to women's wrestling. This title can be defended in any manner, at the discretion of the champion. The IAWF is expanding it's talent base in opening up a tag team division. While the Iron Man spirit is still what drives the IAWF, tag team wrestling is a must-have if we are ever to attain #1 status among internet sports leagues. For these teams we offer the IAWF World Tag Team titles. For the tag team division, teams must be a single unit, rather than two single's wrestlers teaming up. Teams should have a finisher (not manditory) and a unified gimmick. Unlike the single's ranks, the tag team division will be 10 teams per pair of belts. These titles can be defended in any manner, champion's choice. As new talent pours into the league, new divisions will be opened. Possible divisions include a Light-heavyweight, and Mixed Tag Team divisions. - Cards and Free-per-Views Cards are divided into 3 categories : - Weekly - Bi-weekly - Free-per-View Weekly card : Monday Night Meyhem - 5-7 matches - One of two times for wrestler debuts Bi-weekly card : Friday On Fire - 8-10 matches - Minimum 1 World title defense Free-per-Vews : June - War Zone Aug. - Heat Wave Oct. - Harvest Moon Horror Dec. - Best Of the Best Feb. - Winter Wasteland Apr. - Battlebrawl - All titles defended - One of two places for wrestler debuts - Each F-p-V will have at least one specialty match specific to the F-p-V - F-p-V's will be posted on the website for "live" viewing - Signing up The sign-up form is your ticket into the ranks of the IAWF. When creating your new wrestler, think long and hard as to who you want your wrestler to be. Some general guidelines (based on a test league I created in TNM to check how variables worked in matches) for wrestlers are : - Originality counts. The biggest reason for the popularity among e-feds is the originality of the wrestlers involved. - Keep heights and weights in check with reality. That means no 9 foot giants, 700 lbs high flyers, or 200 lbs 7 footers. - Remember the style of wrestler you're creating and don't pick trademark moves that don't fit into the style of wrestling he/she is accustomed to. A brawler wrestler using a figure four as a finisher will be marginally effective at best. Using a more appropriate finisher (say, a powerbomb) seems to have better results. - Keep in mind that smaller and lighter wrestlers may not always be able to get the job done when it comes to power moves. A recent departee from the fed was frustrated that his 225 lbs wrestler was unable to perform his superplex finisher and was unable to put away bigger opponents. Use small guys for speed and arials, medium sized guys for some arials and technical wrestling, and big guys for brawling. That way, you get to take advantage of your wrestler's natural physical abilities (as I said before, 700 lbs daredevil flyers will go nowhere fast). - Keeping wrestlers active is the key to success, so long as it is not overdone. Participating in interviews, making challenges, running interference, etc. will all help you attain victory int he long run. This league is what you make of it. If you don't participate actively, then your chances of success become that much less hopeful. If you plan to be inactive for a period of time, please inform me and steps will be taken to ensure that you will not suffer an inactivity penalty.
