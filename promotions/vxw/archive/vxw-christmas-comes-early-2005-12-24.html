Find us at: http://games.groups.yahoo.com/group/vegasxtreme/ --------------------------------------------- VXW Presents: Christmas Comes Early December 24, 2005 Sam's Club, Las Vegas, NV --------------------------------------------- If you weren't at Sam's Club this afternoon you missed quite a show from the VXW, who celebrated the holidays in style with their special untelevised show (available soon on DVD!), Christmas Comes Early. The early start helped make for a strong turnout, and still allowed everyone to get home in time to spend the evening with friends and family. What more could you ask for? The show opened with "Lean" Darlene Orion, complete with a rather fetching Santa outfit (~!), welcoming everyone to the show and reminding them that all proceeds would be going to various homeless and children's' charities in the Vegas area, a classy gesture by the Beauty Brothers that received a good response from the crowd. ------------------------------------------------------------- NEWCOMER OF THE YEAR AUDITIONS MATCH "Cutthroat" Kyle Sturgis & Roman "Money" Anderson vs Iyasu Saku & Cobra ------------------------------------------------------------- We were treated to some pre-match mic work from Roman Anderson, who stated his claim (in his own inimitable style) for the Newcomer of the Year award in the VXW, getting in all his catchphrases before wrapping up by telling "his" fans to DIG DEEP IN THEIR PANTS for the needy this Christmas! Both Kyle Sturgis and Cobra also stated their claim to the award, although they chose to do so through their performances in what turned out to be a good showing for all four men involved. Saku and Cobra were the more noticeably effective unit in this match, working together against a team that wasn't exactly on the same page for the most part. In fact, Anderson spent most of his time while not in the ring shouting instructions to Kyle Sturgis, with a predictable lack of compliance from the massive Sturgis. The match was a back-and- forth affair, although the balance of the first few minutes probably went to Saku and Cobra, thanks to some frequent tags and a couple of nice tandem offensive moves including a hiptoss into a backbreaker on Roman Anderson and a pair of double dropkicks on Sturgis that slowed his attempt an an illegal intervention. But it's hard to fend a monster like Sturgis off for long, and he was often dominant whenever he was in the ring, legally or otherwise. He showed off his overwhelming strength with a 20+ second hanging vertical suplex on Cobra, no mean feat on a man who weighs over 260 pounds himself, and a short while later scored a nearfall on Saku with an impressive gorilla press into a gutbuster. Cobra showed off his offensive skills too including several suplexes, one of the release German kind that almost folded Anderson in two. Saku's vaunted submission skills were on display as well, a painful-looking Surfboard/Dragon Sleeper combo in particular, also on Anderson, quickly brought Sturgis in to make the save. Anderson himself also had impressive offensive moments of his own, although his tended to be more opportunistic ones. The young rookie was more than content to pick his shots while allowing Sturgis to do the bulk of the inring work, something which appeared to be more than fine with Sturgis and his manager, David Xavier. But Anderson made a mistake at around the eight minute mark, after he and Sturgis had been successful in isolating Cobra for a spell of sustained offense, wearing him down to the point where Anderson attempted to put the man in the snakeskin mask away with his own SNAKEBITE finisher! But as he tried to hook him up for the patented running Tiger Bomb, Cobra suddenly broke free and backdropped out of it, before spinning around, hooking up Anderson... and showing him the Snakebite done right! The crowd POPPED~! bigtime for the finisher, but Cobra is a veteran and instead of going for the cover he used the opportunity to make a much-needed tag! Saku came in with a flurry of strikes on Roman Anderson, and then took him down with a spinning heel kick. Sturgis came at him too, but Saku evaded the monster's lunge and opened up with stiff kicks before hitting the ropes and leaping into a flying bodypress... which was caught by Sturgis! Or at least it was until Cobra hit the ropes too and piled on with a bodypress of his own, which toppled the giant to the mat!! Sturgis was back up almost instantly, but so too were Saku and Cobra, rocking the big man with a barrage of right hands, and then a double dropkick that sent him staggering back against the ropes. Cobra sized him up, rebounded off the opposite side for momentum... and then drilled Sturgis with a Cactus clothesline that sent them both up and over to the outside! Meanwhile the brief respite had allowed Roman Anderson to recover somewhat from the impact of the Snakebite, and he came charging in on Iyasu Saku... .... and straight into the GOOD AND EVIL CROSSFACE!!! Anderson was in real trouble, and David Xavier quickly reacted to the danger, jumping up onto the apron as Anderson struggled with everything he had to escape the deadly hold. Entering the ring, Xavier drew the referee's attention just as Saku wrenched back even harder on the crossface... and Anderson began to tap!! Irate as he saw the referee ordering Xavier from the ring, Saku gritted his teeth and piled on even more pressure... until suddenly he was dragged from the ring by Kyle Sturgis! Cobra was still down, having unfortunately taken the rough end of the tumble to ringside when the 344 pound Sturgis landed on top of him, and was unable to stop Sturgis from hoisting Saku up in a high pumphandle... and then spiking him outside the ring with a vicious sitout facefirst driver! Saku was completely out of it after than move, and Sturgis quickly rolled him back in the ring as the referee's attention belatedly returned to the match. Sturgis made his way around to his corner and demanded the tag from the battered Roman Anderson, who was just then climbing painfully to his feet. But Anderson took one look at the bellowing Kyle Sturgis, another at Iyasu Saku laying prone nearby, and instead of making the tag, or even the cover for that matter, he headed to the corner, and the top rope! Looking down to see Sturgis and Cobra brawling at ringside, a grin spread across Anderson's face and he leapt off... ... hitting a beautiful somersault legdrop (stolen from Valec Corso, for those keeping score at home) on Saku!! Cobra tried to lunge in to break up the pin, but was pulled back by Sturgis as Anderson got the 1-2-3!!! Winners: Roman "Money" Anderson & "Cutthroat" Kyle Sturgis (Leap of Faith) Time: 11:17 Postmatch, while Cobra assisted Iyasu Saku to the back with some parting words aimed in Kyle Sturgis' direction, David Xavier and his client returned to the ring for the official decision, where they were met with a jubilant handshake from a beaming Roman Anderson. Xavier hesitantly accepted the offer, but not so much Kyle Sturgis, who with a nod to his manager suddenly yanked Anderson into a boot to the gut... and then planted him with a Mount Sturgis Driver!!!! Then, satisfied with their day's work, they left the ring with the "victorious" Roman Anderson laid out cold in the middle of it! -------------------------------------------- The Rodeo Boys & Team Brittania vs Team Essington & Purgatory -------------------------------------------- The next match was billed to be a slobberknocker and it delivered - sort of. Before the match, VXW's own "Lean" Darlene Orion confirmed what was being speculated all week.......that indeed Simon and Justin Davidson had no-showed the charity event, thus putting The Rodeo Boys into a 4 on 2 Handicapped Match. But since the theme of the show was 'Christmas Comes Early', the Beauty Brothers gave Kenny Kidd and Billy Bones an early Christmas present......new tag team partners. It was then revealed that their new tag partners for the match was none other than an up and coming team here in Vegas, Tap Out City. The action was all over the place, as Team Essington (represented tonight by their technical expert Mr. Jefferson and his midget partner Mr. Smith) looked to get a jumpstart on Kidd and Bones, hoping to humiliate them even more. Chad Essington's boys seemed to be focusing their attack on Bones, and utilized several of their double-teams on him, including a nasty Sidewalk Slam/Reverse DDT combo that saw Rude charge in to break up the count. Perhaps it was the lack of chemistry between the partners or perhaps it was the fact that Smith and Jefferson didn't know them enough to trust them yet. Either way. Synn and Psyklone spent much of the match watching anxiously on the apron. But one of the most decorated tag teams in RAW-Carolina history couldn't be kept on the outside looking in for long - several times they charged into the ring to get a piece of someone......just anybody on the opposite side of the squared circle. After over ten minutes of wild action, a frustrated Synn flew through the middle ropes at Rabid- who sidestepped, sending Synn crashing headfirst into the safety railing. All hell broke loose at that point, as PsyKlone rolled out of the ring to go after Rabid. Mr. Jefferson took Billy Bones over the top rope with a clothesline, just as Mr. Smith crushed Rude in the corner with a Bronco Buster. Kenny Kidd charged the ring and planted the midget wrestler with a DDT!!! Only to turn around to get his head taken off with a steel chair by Tanara Skott!!! Synn recovered just in time to re-enter the ring and apply the CARDINAL SIN to an unconscious Kidd for the submission victory. Winners: Team Essington & Purgatory (Cardinal Sin) Time: 11:03 No sooner had the bell rung than Tap Out City and Team Essington began brawling back towards the locker room. As the trio of Skott, Synn and Psyklone celebrated in the ring, the crowd popped tremendously, as Sebastian crawled into the ring from behind and nailed Psyklone with the steel chair!!! Not wanting to play Scrooge on Christmas Eve, Sebastian also gave Synn a Christmas gift.....a NASTY chair shot to the face. As Tanara Skott ran for higher ground, Sebastian stood in the ring over her fallen tag team once again, demanding the head of The Equalizer. ------------------------------------------------------ VXW SPIN THE WHEEL TITLE MATCH "Sadistic Insanity" Damian Payne © vs Omega ------------------------------------------------------ If there's one title in the VXW that truly personifies the "Xtreme" mantra instilled during Jason Storm's short ownership of the company, then the newly-reminted Spin the Wheel Championship is certainly that title. Throw it in the mix with two irresistible forces of nature like Omega and the recently crowned champion Damian Payne, and what you apparently get is a contest that resembles something less like a wrestling match and more like outright open warfare! Let's backpedal for a moment though, because before we got underway it was time to spin the new and improved Xtreme Wheel (~!), to find out exactly what rules, or possibly lack thereof, would be in place for this particular match. Being the champion's prerogative, Payne gave the wheel a big spin and it passed over various wild and often downright brutal stipulations until it finally came to rest on... .... a TABLE FOR TWO MATCH??? A short consultation between Darlene and the official led to her announcing that this variant on the "regular" Table Match could be won in only one way -- by not only putting your opponent through a table, but in the process of doing so, you yourself had to also go through that very same table! That announcement brought a POP~! from the fans, and evil smirks from both competitors, who faced off nose- to-nose in the center of the ring as the bell rang to get this battle underway. For a moment we got a brief calm before the impending storm between these two huge monsters, who seemed to be in the midst of an intense verbal exchange... and then a right hand from Omega was returned in kind by Payne, and suddenly it was on! Blow after blow flashed back and forth from both men, but neither would back up an inch. Omega gained a brief advantage with a knee buried to the gut, but Payne reversed the resulting Irish whip and the two bulls met head on with huge shoulder tackles... and again neither man gave an inch! Omega hit the ropes, but ran straight into a big lariat from Payne... which had absolutely no effect! Omega egged on the champion, who ran the ropes and hit another lariat... and again no effect! With Omega laughing in his face, Payne came in a third time... straight into a huge spinebuster (~!) from Omega... which he immediately shrugged off and popped right back up from!! This set the tone for the whole of this contest, which developed into a brutal game of one-upmanship between these two alpha male behemoths, both of which exhibited an apparent outright refusal to show any sign of pain or weakness to the other man. Clotheslines and shoulder tackles escalated to slams and suplexes, and from there to ever more impactful moves and combinations. Payne had a nice flurry with an inverted swinging neckbreaker followed by a spinebuster, and then followed up THAT with a big full nelson faceslam that left Omega bleeding from the mouth... only to see Omega pull himself upright by the ropes and flash him a wicked red-toothed grin! A minute or so later, Payne too was busted open when the action spilled outside the ring and he ate the steel steps face-first, courtesy of an Omega Irish whip. But as Omega shouted his dominance to the crowd, he turned around in time to take the very same steel steps straight to his own face, swung wildly by a bloodied but defiant Damian Payne! Payne tossed Omega back into the ring and dragged a table out from under the apron, bringing the key to victory into play for the first time. But after setting it up in the corner, his attempt to send Omega into the table was reversed, leaving Payne to impact the as- yet- unbroken wood. It didn't stay that way for long however, when Omega charged in with a spear... and plowed straight through the table (~!!) when Damian Payne sidestepped!!! He knew that wasn't enough to win this particular match, and quickly dragged Omega out of the rubble and scooped him up into a big shoulderbreaker. He followed with a Russian legsweep attempt but Omega countered with a couple of stiff back elbows... and then was promptly taken down by a vicious short-arm clothesline by Payne! Somehow Omega again climbed back to his feet, and was shot into the ropes by Damian Payne and met by a boot to the gut... and then the leaping DDT he calls the Last Stop! The fans POPPED~! loudly for the move they knew as the precursor to the ChokeBomb, but Payne had other business to attend to first, quickly heading back outside where he retrieved not one but two more tables (~!), sliding them both into the ring. He set them up so one overlapped the other at ninety degrees, forming a cross pattern, and then scooped up Omega, dumping him onto the tables. Then with a gleam in his eye he climbed up to join him! Obviously figuring that the two tables, combined as they were, would have a much better chance of holding up under the 600lb+ strain of the two monsters combined, Payne decided to put that theory to the test when he carefully positioned himself and pulled Omega up into a standing headscissors! Giving the signal for his deadly ChokeBomb (~!), he hoisted Omega up... .... but the move was blocked! He tried again, but another block... and then Omega stood up with a glazed look in his eyes, backdropping Payne down to the canvas below! He roared his crazed defiance to the sky as the fans recognized his infamous "zone", and then as he looked down at Payne with veins bulging in his forehead, he saw the Spin the Wheel champion climb to his feet... and dove off with a huge spear (~!), pounding away at the face of Damian Payne with huge right hands. Yanking him back upright he sent him to the ropes, and then took him up with a modified flapjack that he turned into a powerbomb on the way down! Eyes bulging, he held on, pulling him back up into the air (~!), where Payne fired back with hard rights straight to the skull! Omega staggered under the onslaught, stumbled slightly... .... and powerbombed Payne onto the tables (~!!), sending him straight through one of them!! The crowd BOOED heavily, then POPPED~! as the referee waved it off as again not being sufficient to end the match. Omega shot him a deadly glare, heading for the official who quickly backed away and bailed outside. Omega finally turned his attention back to Payne, pulling him up and dropping him onto the remaining table. Then with a sinister cut-throat gesture, he turned and headed towards the corner! Climbing slowly to the top turnbuckle, he set up seemingly for the rare but deadly frog splash he calls the DFA... .... when somehow Payne found the strength to slip off the table and clamber up to join him! Omega drilled him with a headbutt, leaving him teetering on the top, but he fought back with a forearm as these two titans perched precariously on the top rope! They rocked each other with desperate blows... and then Omega grabbed Payne around the throat (~!), to which Payne retaliated by grabbing Omega's throat! They struggled and fought and battled for position... and then twisting in mid air, they both came off the top... .... and CRASHING through the table!!!!!!! The fans cheered the carnage, but then fell quiet as the referee finally reentered the ring and checked to see which man had actually landed on top. And then he gave his decision to Darlene at ringside, who announced the WINNER OF THE MATCH... .... and _STILL_ VXW SPIN THE WHEEL CHAMPION -- "SADISTIC INSANITY" DAMIAN PAYNE!!!!! Obviously stunned and awed by the display from these two warriors, the crowd began to chant "REMATCH! REMATCH!" as out from the back came the VXW medical personal to check on both combatants. But it wasn't them that was needed so much as security a couple of minutes later as both men shoved away any attempts at assistance and appeared ready to restart the hostilities! Fortunately said security did arrive in the nick of time to separate them, and finally they were escorted out of the ring with the fans no doubt wondering if their Christmas wish would be granted by VXW management any time in the near future. Winner: "Sadistic Insanity" Damian Payne (table for two) Time: 17:22 During a very brief 15 minute intermission, the youngsters in the crowd recieved a treat as 48% VXW owner Rob Beauty came to the ring.....with Santa Claus!!! As Santa went around the ring passing out stocking filled with candy and other various VXW goodies, Rob thanked the near sold-out crowd for attending and proceeded to thank each and every person who took time out of their busy schedules to donate and/or support this charity show. And now on to the second half!!! ------------------------------------------- TRIPLE THREAT ELIMINATION MATCH "The Biz" Mike Bisignano vs Lark Fenriz vs Arron Iron ------------------------------------------- The bell rang and the three men began stalking each other, just waiting for someone to make the very first move. Lark then made a move towards Iron, but the crafty veteran took "The Wolf" down with a Drop Toe Hold, followed by a quick elbow to the back of Lark's head. "The Biz" finally got back on his feet, but Iron immediately knocked him back down with a stiff lariat. Iron pulled Lark to his feet and whipped him hard into the corner, but Lark moved out of the way in time to see Iron crashing shoulderfirst into the corner. Lark then picked "The Biz" up and whipped him hard into the ropes and connected with a Powerslam and went for the pinfall but Iron BARELY broke it up at the count of 2. Lark stood up to meet Iron, but AI knocked him down with a stiff right.....only to recieve a low blow afterwards courtesy of "The Biz". With "The Jamaican Superstar" still doubled over in pain, "The Biz" followed his low blow with a DDT. "The Biz" then sent Lark into the ropes and knocked him down with a dropkick. It was then at this time that some of the fans SHOCKINGLY began to boo "The Biz", as he slowly began his ascent to the top turnbuckle. Lark rolled out of the way in time for "The Biz" to come crashing down ribs first on the harsh canvas. Lark didn't waste no time at all, as he applied an Abdominal Stretch to the "The Biz". Just as Fenriz was able to get the move sinched in tightly, Iron flew through the air out of nowhere, knocking both men down with a Missle Dropkick, knocking all three men down to the mat. Iron quickly scrambled to cover Fenriz, but Lark was able to get a shoulder up at the count of 2. (Iron eliminated via Fenriz) (Time: 9:48) With the match down to Lark Fenriz and "The Biz" Mike Bisignano, Lark went on the quick offensive on "The Biz", who was still recovering from Iron's finisher. Chris Wheeling wasn't too happy either about having to be cuffed to Jessica Jennings at ringside, per the match stipulation set by Rob Beauty. It was nothing but out and out brawling in the early going, with a high tempo blitz that Knight and Lee really struggled to match. In fact, Hot Property really couldn't get anything going at all until about two or three minutes in, when Lee bought some brief respite with a cheapshot on Anthony. Knight however was having no such luck with Marcus. Marcus took his shirt off and blatantly choked him with it at one point too, before throwing it to the fans. The referee, Jon Gale, seemed to be letting a lot of this go, which was fine with the fans who were loudly behind the fired-up O'Malleys. Lee tried to turn things around for his team with a flying double axehandle off the top to the outside, only for Marcus to nail him in the gut on the way down. Marcus was on all kinds of fire here, going after both guys, and the crowd was eating it up. The best spot was probably a slick double slingshot suplex on Lee and then a double backdrop on Knight that had him land ON Lee. That almost brought a title change right there, and there was another close call when they got Lee with a suplex/crossbody combo, only for Knight to break it up. The challengers were really wiping the floor with the champs at this point, and it was looking more and more like this might just be the O'Malleys' night. Wheeling was redfaced by now from all the frantic shouting at his clients to get their act together, but there wasn't much he could do with Jessica cuffed to his wrist. Instead he was helpless to look on as the O'Malleys delivered more of the same for the next couple of minutes, gaining two more narrow nearfalls in quick succession, first with a camel clutch/dropkick combination on Stevie Knight, and then by a Gory Bomb from Anthony to Austin Lee that came particularly close to gaining the three. But Hot Property hadn't achieve the distinction of holding the VWF/VXW's longest title reign record without being able to dig deep when they had to, and they were finally able to turn the tide somewhat thanks to a desperation low blow by Lee to Marcus O'Malley that the referee somehow missed. Jessica tried to draw the official's attention to it, but Wheeling quickly slapped his hand over her mouth, at least until she stomped him on the foot and had him hopping around in pain, drawing the cheers of the fans. This finally gave the beleaguered tag team champions a chance to mount some much-needed offense, and they used their temporary numbers advantage to work over the feisty Anthony O'Malley. He gamely kept fighting back though, at least until they caught him with a nice double front layout suplex, but when they again wasted time signaling for the Property Boom, Marcus O'Malley returned to the fray with renewed vigor, and once again the champs were on the backfoot. The closing minutes saw ever-more furious action with Austin Lee almost snatching the win for his team with a reverse DDT, and the O'Malleys coming close on two occasions to connecting with their deadly Irish Thunder. Another time they caught Stevie Knight with a double Superkick that knocked him through the ropes to the outside, only for Lee to immediately take Anthony out with a signature Brain Freeze kick of his own. The finish came a minute or so later after Marcus caught Lee with an Enziguiri and then signalled for his patented Kiss of Death, which drew a big POP from the fans. But before he could execute it, Knight suddenly appeared and blindsided him with a shot from one of the tag title belts (~!), as a dazed Lee grabbed at the ref to provide the distraction. The crowd was all over Knight with a wave of BOOS as he tossed the belt to the outside and then applied the ultimate humiliation by cockily pinning Marcus with one foot placed on his chest, and his eyes locked smugly on the helpless Jessica Jennings at ringside. Or at least that was the plan, but to everyone's confusion Anthony O'Malley suddenly came out of nowhere with a surprise rollup on an oblivious Austin Lee! The referee counted to two, and then he froze, looking back and forth between the two pins... .... and then he slapped the mat again for the three! The place erupted at the apparent title change, then fell silent as an argument flared up in the ring, with the referee in the center of it. Anthony O'Malley claimed one of the title belts in the confusion, while Knight had the other, and both teams were furiously stating their claims... when the flustered referee, who apparently had no answer for any of them, threw up his arms and walked out! No one knew what was going on, least of all the fans who tried to do their part by chanting for the O'Malleys, along with some other less choice chants. In the end security had to escort both teams from the ring, and the match ended in confusion with no official announcement being made, something that no doubt the Beauty Brothers will have to resolve. Numerous times in the early going, Valenkov tried to tag Kagedo in, but he would either jump off the ring apron or raise both of his hands high in the air to avoid the tag. This allowed Jennings and Harper to gain the early advantage, as they used numerous double teams move to keep the big man grounded. Despite this being the first time the SoD stablemates had tagged together, they worked like a fine oiled machine. Harper would ground Thunder and tag in "The Hype", who would begin pounding the champ and tagged in Harper again. For the first five minutes, it was the same routine. Lather. Rinse. Repeat. Oddly enough, it frustrated Kagedo to the point that he LITERALLY WALKED OUT ON THE MATCH!!!! With Chris Wheeling screaming at Kagedo to come back out of the locker room, the champ was forced into a 2 on 1 Handicap Tag Match. The prime focus of Jennings and Harper was the big legs of Thunder, for if they could keep him flat on his back, he couldn't use his strength and height to his advantage. But Thunder didn't win the 24- Hour Title on a fluke win. And despite having his legs take most of the attack from his opponents throughout the match, Thunder eventually made it to his feet once again and slowly went on the offensive, using his monsterous strength to slowly put a beating on both of his opponents. Just as it seemed that momentum was fully swinging in the direction of Valenkov, he made a fatal error. Whipping Jennings into the ropes, Thunder stuck out a big boot in hopes of taking Adam's head off. Instead, Adam sensed it coming and grabbed the leg of Valenkov and Dragon Screwed it......RIGHT INTO A HYPEREXTENSION!!! In the middle of the ring, Jennings had his long time nemesis in the middle of the ring in his patented Single Leg Boston Crab. The fans wanted a tap out to go home happy, and the pain was painted all over the face of Valenkov. Outside the ring, Chris Wheeling was screaming and begging for his man not to tap out. Numerous times, the big man would raise one hand in the air as if to tap.. ...then suddenly put the hand back down. Suddenly, a rush of wind blew by Chris Wheeling.... ....and for some reason, knocked Justin Harper off the ring apron on the other side of the ring. Kagedo had finally returned. After ramming the head of his Iron Man opponent at New Year's Eve into the guard rail, Kagedo rushed the ring... ....and saved Valenkov from tapping out!!!! Grabbing a handful of hair, Kagedo tossed Jennings over the top rope, then pointed at him while looking at Valenkov!!! Instantly, Thunder got the message and climbed over the top rope and went after Jennings on one side......while Kagedo continued the assault on Harper on the other side!!! With Chris Wheeling in the ring distracting the referee, Kagedo tossed Harper back in the ring. With the martial arts expert now busted open, Kagedo went on the offensive as he attempted to cause further damage to the open wound of Harper. Much to his surprise, Harper made a comeback with several stiff kicks and chops to the abdomen. Harper irish whipped Kagedo into the ropes, but missed the clothesline attempt as Kagedo bounced off the other side and nailed Harper with a shoulder block. The impact knocked Harper backwards into the ropes, causing him to get tied into the ropes. At this point, the ref rang for the bell as ruled it being a Double Countout since Jennings and Valenkov were the legal men. Decision: Double Countout Time: 13:11 And that's when things got out of hand...... With Harper still in the ropes and Valenkov and Jennings brawling on the outside, Kagedo and Chris Wheeling began putting the boots to Harper. For some strange reason, the fans began to pop wildly as through the crowd and into the ring entered......SANTA CLAUS!?!?! Jolly Old St. Nick climbed in the ring and gently put both his hands on the shoulders of Kagedo and Wheeling, only for both men to shove him away. With it being the night before all good little boys and girls got the gifts they've wanted all year, Santa once again tried to break up the fight and put his hand on the shoulder of Wheeling. What happened next caused the crowd to gasp in unison...... CHRIS WHEELING SLAPPED SANTA CLAUS!!!!! Santa Claus staggered backwards for a moment, then calmly adjusted his big red coat. He walked back to Wheeling, grabbed him by the nap of the neck and jerked him back...... Underhooking his neck with his left arm, he tore off his hat and white beard to reveal...... KEVIN SIMMONS!!!! The crowd blew the roof off Sam's Town when his identity was revealed.....KEVIN SIMMONS WAS SANTA CLAUS!!!! The look on Chris Wheeling's face said it all as Simmons dropped him violently with a Durham Deathdrop!!!! As soon as the fans begin shouting and screaming, Kagedo stopped his attack on Harper to turn around and once again stare his adversary in the face. Instantly, Kagedo climbed out of the ring and staggered backwards up the ramp and on the stage, as Simmons and other VXW officials helped to untie Harper from the ropes. As "Lean" Darlene Orion thanked the fans for coming out, Jennings and Thunder continued their brawl in the stands until more VXW officials had to finally separate the two. The show officially ended with Simmons raising Harper's arm high in the air, as "Hells Bells" blared throughout the arena. HAVE A VERY MERRY CHRISTMAS!!!!
