SIMMONS: But then again, I also thought about a man who was once my friend, my tag team partner......the man who oddly enough was the one that found me in a crumpled heap backstage. I'm talking about Terry Jacobs. (A second shock pop makes it way through the crowd.) CK: Geez. What's Simmons gonna do?? Accuse EVERYONE here in VXW? If that's the case Mike, I accuse you of doing it in the garage with the cresent wrench. MD: Will you stop Chris?? After all, the man also has a second point there. After all, it was Simmons who had to leave Jacobs to the wolves to save his ex-wife from a chokeslam from Bass she didn't deserve at the time. Jacobs had to defend and soon lost those same RAW-C Tag Team Titles without help from Simmons. SIMMONS: But then I was sitting at home when I got an interesting package in the mail. And what I found was a videotape that had a very troubling surveilance footage on it. The footage of me getting my ass kicked. So it's time that the world found out the truth, and it's gonna be found out when I ask this simple question..... (Simmons immediately turns a snap 180 degrees and faces Bass.) SIMMONS: Why?? (The crowd seems to gasp in unison at the accusation of Bass.) MD: WHAT?!?! Bass was the attacker??? (Bass faces Simmons with this puzzled and questioning look on his face.) CK: That's it. Put Simmons in a damn nursing home. There's no way The Outlaw did it. He's been telling Simmons he's got his back. (Bass grabs the mic from Simmons as he removes the toothpick from his mouth.) BASS: Excuse me, Kev? I attacked you??? You're kidding, right?? (Simmons simply stands there stone faced, just glaring a hole through Bass.) BASS: Why the hell would I attack you? We're family...we're blood. You know better than I do that when you're in the SoD, you're blood relation. What reason would I have to attack my own brother, especially if he's retired? SIMMONS: For starters, don't bring my personal life into this mess just to change the subject. You know as well as I do that you were the one who attacked me. (The posture of Bass says it all: Not only is he getting defensive but you can see he doesn't appreciate being interrogated at all.) BASS: That's bullshit Kevin and you know it!!! (Both men just stop and glare at each other as Kagedo just stands there as a third party.) SIMMONS: Is it Morris??? Am I making all this shit up??? Well maybe I am and maybe I ain't. But then again, why should the fans take either one of our words for it when they can see the footage themselves!!! (FACE POP FOR SIMMONS) MD: Look at Kagedo!!! He is livid!!! He's right up in the face of Bass!! And the big man is trying to plead his case to the Dragon!!! CK: I can't believe this.....is Bass really the attacker?? (Simmons turns away and faces the VXW-tron.) SIMMONS: Without further adieu, folks....roll the footage please. (The VXW-tron comes to life with the surveilance video from Xtreme Prejudice IV, as Simmons is standing alone as if waiting for someone when suddenly, a man with Bass's height, build and long curly hair jumps Simmons from behind, grabbing him by the shirt collar and ramming him facefirst into the nearby garage door. Or was it just someone wanting to frame you?? (Bass turns around and shakes his head in disbelief as the 'Asshole' chants get louder and louder. Suddenly, Bass removes the toothpick again from his mouth and turns back around and faces Simmons.) BASS: YOU'RE DAMN RIGHT I DID IT!!!! (MASSIVE HEEL HEAT FOR BASS.) MD: The attacker was none other than Simmons's former stablemate!!! But why??? BASS: But you know something Kevin?? I may have done it, but unlike all the other times in the past......I wasn't working alone. Isn't that right.....old friend??? (Simmons has this shocked and stunned look on his face, as if he figured out the puzzle. He immediately turns around but it's too late.....) WHAM!!!! (Simmons falls face first to the mat like a sack of potatoes.) (MAMMOTH HORROR POP THROUGHOUT THE CROWD.) MD: OH MY GOD!!! KAGEDO JUST LAID OUT SIMMONS WITH THE STEEL CHAIR!!!! CK: IT WAS AN INSIDE JOB ALL ALONG!!!! MD: KAGEDO AND BASS CONSPIRED TO TAKE OUT KEVIN SIMMONS!!!! BUT WHY???? BASS: Tsk, tsk, tsk Kevin....we go through this every single damn time!!! And you STILL don't learn your lesson, now do you?? But things are fixing to change, isn't that right Kagedo?? (Bass hands the mic off to the Dragon, one of the founding forefathers of the SoD.) KAGEDO: Prophetic, isn't it Kevin? One day all of your hopes and dreams are materialized and standing right before you, and the next second they are as impermanent as a simple desert mirage. (The Dragon reaches down and wipes a stream of blood that has now begun to trickle down the forehead of Simmons, who is lying prone and helpless) KAGEDO: Blood begets blood, after all. Tonight, my old..'friend'..is just the beginning of the end of something that has taken me years to do. Mo and I, along with a few other people of consequence along the way that you will come to know soon enough, have been waiting patiently. Patiently, Kevin; for your ego to finally bring you to the pinnacle of success. And tonight, what I began when I first arrived in Carolina so long ago has been fulfilled. The destruction of the Predator. (Kagedo picks up the crumpled heap that is Kevin Simmons in a full nelson.) MD: Oh my God!! He's gonna Dragon Suplex the Predator!!! CK: Somebody needs to stop him!!!
