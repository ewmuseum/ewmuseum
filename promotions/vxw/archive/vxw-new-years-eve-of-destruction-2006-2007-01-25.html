VXW — Show

New Years Eve Of Destruction 2006

January 25, 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Diedra - Hell-lo, Amerika. I yam Diedra and deez are my Dierdorfs. (She then puckers her lips, rubs the Dierdorfs, makes a climaxing like noise and they walk off) .. .. .. [Eve Of Destruction plays and we see shots of past VXW action, highlighting each of the stars on the New Years show] New Years Eve Of Destruction is finally here and what a great night it was. Things kicked off with a big tag team match. ########################################################### PROTECTORS OF TRUTH [vs] JOEY YOUNG & TOM 'TOP OF THE LINE' ROGERS ########################################################### Lark Fenriz and Foodstamp were off their game right from the start and the two young kids took advantage. Young, the flashier of the two kept the fans on the edge of their seats with his high flying action while Rogers used his well rounded mat skills to wear down either Fenriz or Foodstamp. But at the four minute mark, a clearly frustrated Fenriz came into the ring and broke up a FULL NELSON by Rogers. Young came into the ring to help his partner but Fenriz caught him in a SPINNING SIDEWALK SLAM. >From here, Fenriz and Foodstamp just began a full out beat down of Young and Rogers. The referee tried to regain order but was thrown over the top rope by Foodstamp and POT was disqualified! But Fenriz and Foodstamp continued, they even brought a table into the ring and were set to put Rogers through the table when the crowd went ASB! Kenny Kidd and Billy Bones, THE RODEO BOYS rushed to the ring and sent POT packing with some well placed shots. Fenriz and Foodstamp retreated to the back and the crowd let out a HUUUGE 'WELCOME BACK!" for the Rodeo Boys. They helped Young and Rogers to their feet and the fans gave a huge ovation for all four men. ########################################################## WINNERS = Joey Young and Tom Rogers via disqualification ########################################################## The action then moved backstage, where Sebastian was seen talking with Miles McKinley. [Sebastian is right in the face of McKinley] SEBASTIAN: Sebastian won't tell you again, tonight you're job is to make sure Valenkov leaves the ring WITH the 24 Hour Title. Not Cobra and not YOU! [pokes McKinley in the chest to emphasize the point] Sebastian is going to become 24 Hour Champion again, but Sebastian has to do it against Valenkov. MCKINLEY: But I could. . SEBASTIAN: I!?!? There is no I in Sebastian. MCKINLEY: Actually. . SEBASTIAN: Look here, if you want to keep that secret of yours then you will do what Sebastian says. Tonight, Thunder Valenkov leaves here as 24 Hour Champion! [Sebastian walks off as McKinley stares at him hard] .. .. .. Sebastian made his way to the ring for his own match of the night, against The Equalizer in a no disqualification match. ################################# No DQ, No Count Outs SEBASTIAN [vs] THE EQUALIZER ################################# E took the charge and beat The Starmaker all over the ring, sending him hard into each of the corners. The Equalizer hit a SLINGSHOT SUPLEX on Sebastian and then E climbed to the top rope and hit a MOONSAULT! That's when The Equalizer went outside and got a table and stood it up in the corner of the ring! The fans were chanting as E set up Sebastian for a RUSSIAN LEG SWEEP through the table, but Sebastian fought off and used a HIP TOSS to get out of the leg sweep. The Equalizer turned and charged and Sebastian stepped out of the way and The Equalizer SPEARED the tabled! Sebastian then began waving to the back and out to the ring came The Dierdorfs! Kris and Brian rushed into the ring as Diedra sauntered her way to ringside. The three on one beatdown began, and it was brutal! The Dierdorfs whipped E to the ropes and Sebastian CLOTHESLINED him with a JAGGED PIECE OF THE TABLE! Sebastian then left E for the tag team and they hit their PULVERIZER! The Starmaker then picked up E and used his DOUBLE UNDERHOOK PILEDRIVER now known as the STARMAKER! Sebastian made the cover for the ONE! TWO!! THREE!!! The fans were let down around ringside. Most knew Sebastian would have something up his sleeve, but they also thought The Equalizer would have something up his sleeve, but as Sebastian led his troops away from ringside the bloodied Equalizer struggled to his feet. ################################# WINNER = Sebastian via pinfall ################################# [Backstage, the shot opens onto the face of none other than the Starmaker himself, Sebastian, still a bit sweaty from his win. He's standing outside a door, looking down the corridor to where a large tuxedoed figure is currently in the middle of a physical disagreement with a Sam's Town vending machine. The burly figure is instantly recognizable as one of Chris Wheeling's personal goons. Not Lou, the other one. Yeah, him. Sebastian looks around slyly at the camera, then opens the door and enters. The camera moves to film the inside of the room, which reveals itself as none other than the private locker room of the Wheeling himself, along with the man who looks up instantly from the bench at the intrusion, the reigning Twenty-Four Hour Champion. The Soviet Monster. Thunder Valenkov.] SEBASTIAN: Easy there champ. Just came in to see how you are doing. Did you see Sebastian defeat former champ The Equalizer? [In the midst of his pre-match preparations, Valenkov discards a roll of black tape and immediately draws up to his full 6'11" frame, looming over the Starmaker. Cold gaze flashing to the camera and beyond, it quickly settles onto Sebastian.] SEBASTIAN: Calm down, Sebastian can see what is going on in that primal brain of yours. You are face to face with the real greatest 24 Hour Champion of all time. Its okay to be number two. Two. [holds up fingers] Two, you. You, number two. [Valenkov's face set and unreadable, he finally looks down at the contract in Sebastian's hand, dark eyes narrowing with... curiosity?] SEBASTIAN: We both know what this is, this is the match of all time. Valenkov, four time champ versus Sebastian three time champ. The two greatest of all time, one on one in the ring. Ghost Town Blues! [pulls out a pen] You can do this yourself right? You don't need some mouthpiece to make all your decisions do you? [Sebastian's words seem to be having little effect on the stoic champion, and the Starmaker looks like he's starting to get a little frustrated.] SEBASTIAN: [gets close to Valenkov, looking all over his face] Is there someone in there? Maybe you are a Wheeling Robot? [Grinning, Sebastian suddenly raps Valenkov on the forehead with the pen to emphasize his point. He gets in two taps before Valenkov's large hand suddenly snaps closed around his, pen and all. He holds it there, gripped tightly as his face twists into an angered snarl. Sebastian's eyes widen, as... ....Valenkov's other hand closes around the contract, and he snatches it from Sebastian's grasp! Breathing low and forced in Sebastian's face, the massive champion suddenly turns his attention to the contract... and scrawls his mark upon it, without ever letting Sebastian's pen hand go! He finally releases his grip as Sebastian realizes what he just did.] SEBASTIAN: HELL YEAH! HELL F***ING YEA. . Voice: WHAT THE HELL IS THIS!?! [And a figure bursts past the camera man and into the room, soon followed by a huffing-and-puffing couple of goons. Yes, better late than never, it's Chris Wheeling!] Wheeling: What do you think you're doing? Who gave you the... SEBASTIAN: You almost made it, but it is to late. [Wheeling's eyes about pop out of his skull as he realizes what Sebastian's referring to. He tries to snatch the contract from Sebastian's hand, but the Starmaker is too quick and pulls it away.] Wheeling: Give me that! You, I... no, you know that's not legal anyway. Are you kidding me? Sebastian: Looks legal to me, Chris. Wheeling: Don't be ridiculous, look at him, he doesn't know what the hell he's signing. He's freaking RUSSIAN, you idiot! Sebastian: He knows what he's doing! Wheeling: The hell he does, why don't you just shut up and... Sebastian: Why don't you make me? Wheeling: Oh, I'll make you... Sebastian: I'm right here, bring it! Wheeling: You piece of... Sebastian: Come on, little man! Wheeling: Don't you dare... [By now the two men are right in each other's faces, and the juvenile dialogue becomes almost non-understandable. It looks like they're about a second away from exchanging blows... ....when Valenkov steps between them, pushing the two men apart. Wheeling seems totally taken aback by this, is mouth wide open and flapping in the wind. Valenkov holds him at arms' length with a hand on his manager's chest, then turns back to stare right into Sebastian's face again. His other hand grabs a handful of the Starmaker's shirt, and then...] Valenkov: FIGHTT... YES. [And then he just shoves Sebastian back against the wall of the locker room, turns and exchanges the briefest glance with Wheeling, and then pushes past the camera and goons, and exists the suddenly silent room. Wheeling and Sebastian look at each other.] Wheeling: NO, WAIT, THAT'S NOT... Sebastian: THE HELL IT ISN'T... [Good lord, cut!] .. .. .. The fans were partly abuzz and partly in shock, we are going to see Valenkov and Sebastian one on one at Ghost Town Blues. The fans were set for more tag team action next up. ################################### THE O'MALLEYS [vs] THE DIERDORFS ################################### This match started with the obligatory four man melee, but once things settled Anthony was taking the fight to Kris Dierdorf. Anthony ground him with a FLYING HEADSCISSORS that turned into an ARM BAR and from there the O'Malleys went to work on the arm of Kris Dierdorf. Using quick tags and nice double teaming The O'Malleys really put the hurt on Kris. Kris battled out of an ARM BAR at one point and clocked Marcus with a FLYING AXHANDLE, but Marcus was able to get him by the ankle and drag him back to the corner before he could make the tag to Brian Dierdorf. Diedra finally got up on the apron, and Anthony brought her into the ring. During the chaos Kris tagged in Brian Dierdorf. The referee was getting Diedra out of the ring, but she had tossed Brian her RIDING CROP and Brian went to work on Anthony, welting up his back! It was sick! Marcus charged into the ring and took the RIDING CROP from Brian, and turned the tables, but the referee saw it and disqualified The O'Malleys! ############################################# WINNERS = The Dierdorfs by disqualification ############################################# As Ken Young made his way to the ring for his match with Omega, Chad Essington walked out behind him. These two men have had some troubles and Young seemed to want to take this challenge on alone. While he and Essington argued, Omega made his entrance. ####################################### OMEGA [vs] KEN 'THE PERFECTION' YOUNG ####################################### Omega hit the ring and ripped Young off his feet, knocking The Perfection into his manager sending Essington through the rope and to the floor. Omega did not let up on Young for a moment, it was a total one sided match, if you can call it a match. Omega picked up Young hit his THREE X GORILLA PRESS SLAM! >From there Omega locked on the ANKLELOCK FROM HELL and Young tapped out! ############################### WINNER = Omega by submission ############################### But Omega did not let up, he refused to break the hold. Chad Essington got into the ring and tried to pull Omega off but the small weak manager had no chance. That's when Matt Payne hit the ring, to a huge ovation from the VXW faithful! Payne rattled Omega with a few shots, then finally got Omega to break the hold before breaking the ankle. Payne got in a couple really good shots that rocked Omega, before Matt Payne charged Omega, but Omega pulled Richard Masterson in front of him and Masterson took the SPEAR! Security hit the scene and kept it from escalating any farther. But Matt Payne, former VXW student has made his presence known and it looks like he is ready to take the next step! Fans were then sent outside as The Hype was arriving at Sams Town! <We find a jet black Dodge Charger pulling into the parking garage and from within it emerges the one and only Adam Jennings. His blonde locks are pulled back into a tight ponytail, and he is wearing his trademarked violet-lensed sunglasses. To meet him is VXW's own, "Lean" Darlene!> LDO: Adam! Hey, Adam! We have all got to know--is your partner here tonight? And are you ready for the big contract signing? <Without breaking stride, Jennings moves on past the lovely interviewer and camerman who is almost always in tow. Still, ever the journalist at heart, she falls in to pace with him and he grins> AJ: Never give up, eh Darlene? Alright, here's the sound bite for now. Is my partner here? No idea. I've not spoken to him since the voice message I left for him Christmas Eve. Hell, he may be in a gutter dead for all I know. But one thing is for damn certain---thats what it would take to keep him from getting a piece of his old rival. LDO: So is it safe to assume you have reached back into the past of Kevin Simmons to procure one of the former Society of Doom members? Kagedo? El Jefe? Violent Jack? <Displaying nothing but a solid poker face, Jennings continues towards his locker room> AJ: Sorry, Darlene. But you, me, and everyone else will just have to see if he shows up before midnight or not. But for the sake of Simmons and Bass, they'd better pray to any god who will still listen to them that he doesn't. Hell's coming to Rhyolite to meet them, and some things may just best be left buried. <He grins at her one last time as he enters his locker room> .. .. .. The Outlaw Morris Bass walked out of the Rage In The Cage match as a one man tag team champion. He would put his titles on the line against the new team, the hot team of Roman Anderson and John Manning! ############################################## VXW Tag Team Title Match 'THE OUTLAW' MORRIS BASS © [vs] THE OUTCASTS ############################################## Anderson started the match and for some reason tried to match power with Bass. Lets just say it didn't go well and quickly The Outcast team was in trouble. Anderson was getting his head kicked in by the Outlaw and Manning was pissed at his partner for trying to fight with Bass. Bass sent Anderson into the corner and brought him out with a VERTICAL SUPLEX. Anderson lay on the mat and Bass mocked Manning, which brought him into the ring. This gave Bass a chance to LOW BLOW Anderson, and follow that up with an INVERTED ATOMIC DROP. With Anderson digging deep in his pants to relieve the pain, Bass came off the ropes and used a BIG BOOT to knock him off his pins. Bass made the cover for ONE! TWO!! But Manning made the save. Manning ducked a charging Bass and hit a DROPKICK on him before the referee sent Manning back to his corner. Anderson struggled to his feet and caught Bass coming with a KICK to the midsection, he followed up with another and then hit a KNEE to the face before rolling over and tagging in Manning. John Manning used the advice of his partner, he started to slowly chop away at Bass. A hit and run kind of tactic that started to pay off, a clearly frustrated Bass was now chasing Manning around the ring, while Manning was working on the midsection of the big man. Manning locked Bass in an ABDOMINAL STRETCH, well as best as he could get it, but Bass was able to power his way out and he used a few solid right hands to soften up Manning before hitting a RUNNING POWERSLAM on Manning. Bass picked up Manning and was going for a POWERBOMB when Manning countered with a HURRICANRANA, but during the flying bodies the referee was knocked to the canvas! [REF BUMP!] Manning got to his feet and Bass CLOTHESLINED him to the mat, then charged over and knocked Anderson off the ring apron and into the ring barriers! The Outlaw shook his head and got out of the ring and took the chair Lean Darlene Orion was sitting on and returned to the ring. Bass was ready to BRAIN Manning when Cocky by Kid Rock hit and Adam Jennings walked out onto the stage area! Bass dropped the chair and turned, Jennings smiled at him and waved his finger. Bass showed Jennings a finger of his own. As the two men exchanged pleasantries, John Manning hit a SCHOOLBOY on Bass. The referee roused moments before the schoolboy and Manning grabbed the ropes and got ONE! TWO!! THREE!!! The crowd went ASB! Hell, they went double ASB as an enraged Bass flew to his feet only to see the referee handing The Outcasts, Anderson and Manning the tag team title belts! !!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! WINNERS AND NEW TAG TEAM CHAMPIONS = .. . . The Outcasts by pinfall !!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! Security quickly rolled in and secured the area as the fans were treated to a quick video segment. [Viva Las Vegas is played, the screen shows the old VWF logo, which dissolves into the RAW-Vegas logo and then into the current VXW logo.] VOICE OVER: [as he talks we see in ring action shots of the men he mentions] Sebastian was the first. Classy Joey Boy set the bar. Revolution Randall Crane took the bar from CJB and beat him with it. The Maquilladora, Fenriz, The Equalizer, Jennings, Valenkov. The names are many. But who will be first? [The screen becomes black and we see 'VXW Hall Of Fame' appears on the screen. 'Coming Soon'] .. .. .. The ring was then set up for a contract signing. And the fans were on their feet, saliva dripping from their mouths in anticipation! <The ring is cleared except for a folding table and a foursome of folding chairs on either side of it and the owner of the VXW, Ric Beauty. He lifts his microphone to speak to the fans> Ric: As you know, at this time we are going to have a contract signing for a very special grudge match at the upcoming Ghost Town Blues spectacular, just up the road in Rhyolite! <Promo Pop> Ric: At this time, I would like the first two participants to come to the ring please. <"Hell's Bells" starts to play, and the fans groan and boo and begin to pelt the two figures of Morris Bass and Kevin Simmons as they make their way to the ring. Grinning like the proverbial chershire cat, Simmons enters the ring confidently. Looking like he is about to punch a baby or two after his tag team championship loss to Roman Anderson and John Manning...thanks to Adam Jennings, Bass follows moodily> Ric: And now, if I could call out one of their opponents please. <Kid Rock's "Cocky" starts to play and the arena explodes with the force of millions of fans of the man known to many simply as 'The Hype'. Jennings emerges from the backstage area, and the entire building seems to vibrate with anticipation and excitement as he slowly makes his way to the ring and steps inside. He grins, the old trademark cocky as he wants to be grin, at Bass who returns it with a glare> Ric: Ok, boys. Here it is. Both parties have had attorneys to go over it. As you will notice, the name of Mr Jennings' partner has been left blank pending his arrival tonight. Mr Simmons, if you would please. <Smirking with pleasure, Simmons drags the contract to the side of the table that he and the 'Outlaw' stand at. He signs and hands the pen to his compatriot who adds his own signature as well. With that, he slams the pen on the table and shoves the papers back towards Jennings who pulls down his sunglasses to the edge of his nose to peer over the top of the violet lenses at his opponents. Slowly, he picks up the contract and peers intently at it. Taking the microphone from Ric Beauty, who quickly exits the ring, Jennings begins his taunts> AJ: Wow. This is really something. Ghost Town Blues, eh, Kev? Seems like you want to steal the show there every year man. But just one thing you seem to have overlooked....this match is a LOSER LEAVES VEGAS MATCH! So much for your great lawyer, I guess. Hell, he may be great at reading the fine print, but right here as bold as hell...stipulation, the team declared the losing team will NEVER COMPETE IN LAS VEGAS AGAIN! <Mad face pop as Simmons stomps the ring angrily. Bass, however, stands motionless with his eyes fixed on Jennings as he signs his own name to the contract..causing the fans to erupt even more> AJ: Hell, it's spelled out so clear that even that neanderthal to your left there could underst... <He doesn't even get to finish the insult as Bass explodes at him and sends him sprawling to the canvas. The two on one beating commences as the fans begin to turn expectantly to the entrance to look for the mystery guest, though no one seems to appear. With Jennings now lying facing Dan Aller, who cannot help but comment on the blood now trickling from the former 24-Hour Champion's forehead, Bass sits in with a very impressive looking camel clutch as Simmons taunts their target for so many months> Simmons: Well, Adam, looks like there was way too much hype for this! And now we know that you will be all alone at Rhyolite. Hell, I suppose that bald headed bastard decided he was better off in St Louis after all. Maybe he did finally learn his lesson when I crippled him last year! And now, it's your turn! <In a mockery of all things good and decent in the world, Simmons picks up one of Jennings' legs and wrenches back on it in Jennings own Hyper Extension and a look of triumph and pompous arrogance on his face. A look that is matched by the raging fury that is seen on the face of 'The Outlaw' as the camera gets a close up of him wrenching back on Jennings' neck. And yet, this looks is quickly dissolved and reversed into panic and fear by one sound.....> DTMA: Oh my god!!! It's.................. <"LaGrange" by ZZ Top begins to hit the house speakers and is almost immediately drowned out by an explosion or cheers that could well be heard four states away> <And the one and only Travis LaGrange slowly begins to head towards the ring. The face of Morris Bass seems to be drained of color as he slowly releases his lock on the chin of The Hype and erects himself, almost as if afraid to run and visually confirm what his mind is telling him is happening behind him. Kevin Simmons releases Jennings as well and stands in recognition of the foe yet unknown to him> Stone: Dear Christ, Dan! No one has seen or heard from Travis LaGrange in years now! And you just KNOW that Adam Jennings couldn't have been more true to his word about hell coming for Simmons and Bass at Rhyolite! The history between Bass and LaGrange superceeds legendary status, and I'm personally in awe that we're going to get to see it ourselves this time! DTMA: No, doubt, Johnny! And look at this as Bass finally turns around to see the ghost of years past! Who can forget that epic Buried Alive match these two hooked up in back in the old days of WILD? And it was brought to a head because of Bass' actions regarding LaGrange's deceased wife! You would be hard pressed to find anyone on the planet who hates Bass more! <And sure enough, as Bass turns to face the awful and slowly approaching truth, he flees the ring and escapes through the crowd as quickly as his legs can carry him. Simmons, watching his henchman high tailing it from the scene, turns back around just as LaGrange enters the ring. What seems like an eternity passes as these two men look at one another before Simmons decides to take action, but his wild swing is blocked and he is quickly knocked flat on his back by the still unbelievably calm and collected LaGrange. Rolling from the ring and escaping in Bass' wake, Simmons gets himself to safety and leaves only LaGrange and Jennings in the ring. As the latter regains his vertical balance again, the former picks up the fallen clipboard and contract. Pausing only a brief second, LaGrange's name is signed and the arena explodes once again. No one can hear what is being said at the announce position, but the fans calm enough to hear the following as Travis LaGrange takes a mic in hand> LaGrange: Hey Bass, looks like hell did freeze over. <With nothing more to say, he drops the mic and finds the extended hand of Adam Jennings, and as the two exchange a handshake, the clock strikes 12 and the pyro flares on the entrance ramp and the last thing seen as the show ends is LaGrange standing in the center of the ring staring at the area that Morris Bass fled through the crowd> .. .. .. Needless to say the place was abuzz. Travis LaGrange! The Outlaw himself. Here in VXW! And in a huuuuge match! Fans were still talking about it when the three men made their way to the ring for the main event. ########################################################### VXW 24 Hour Title, TV Title Match THUNDER VALENKOV (24) [vs] COBRA (TV) [vs] MILES MCKINLEY ########################################################### The rules of this match were a little strange. McKinley could win only the TV Title. Valenkov could only defend his title, but Cobra could lose the TV Title to McKinley or win the 24 Hour Title from Valenkov. Got it? Good. But McKinley was under orders to make sure Valenkov defeated Cobra, and that is exactly what he set out to do. Valenkov and McKinley double teamed Cobra right from the start and it was looking like a long night for Cobra. McKinley held him so Valenkov could get in some free shots, Valenkov held him for McKinley. Valenkov even whipped Cobra into a SAVATE KICK from Miles McKinley. The plan was working. Cobra was struggling. But the TV Champ had something in his gas tank, he reached deep down and ducked a Valenkov CLOTHESLINE and then hit the 24 Hour Champ with a FLYING SHOULDER that sent him reeling, then Cobra spun around and hit a BELLY TO BELLY on a charging McKinley. But McKinley knocked down the ref! [REF BUMP!] Valenkov got caught by another FLYING SHOULDER from Cobra who followed up with a DROPKICK that took Valenkov off his feet. Meanwhile Sebastian was giving McKinley a chair! The Scot made his move and WHAM! [SWERVE POP!] McKinley clocked Valenkov in the back of the head and the champ went down. An enraged Sebastian got in the ring and grabbed the chair away from McKinley and began to bark orders at him. Cobra climbed over and made the cover on the champ, but there was no one to make the count. That's when Omega came charging out from the back! Omega hit the ring and pulled Cobra off Valenkov by the ankle and went right into the ANKLELOCK FROM HELL! But Omega would get stopped again, this time by Matt Payne! Payne tackled Omega! Valenkov got to his feet and went straight for McKinley and Sebastian! Valenkov was joined by Wheelings goons at this point! The referee got to his feet and saw the total chaos in the ring and called for the bell! Valenkov exchanging punches with McKinley, Cobra joining in, with Sebastian while Omega and Matt Payne brawled on the other side of the ring. Then, Morris Bass walked out onto the stage laughing at the chaos inside the ring. His presence was soon explained. That's when Viva Las Vegas hit and VXW President Val Beauty came out onto the stage. .. .. .. [The action in the ring comes to a momentary stop as the leader of the VXW stands on the stage] VAL: I was hoping we could do this in a more prestigious way but I guess this is what we have. It is time to announce the VXW Team for Wrestlebowl. Since we have three of those men in the ring right now, I thought this would be a good time. This team will compete under the leadership of CHRIS WHEELING! [WHA??? POP!] The team will include. . THUNDER VALENKOV!! OMEGA!! COBRA!! And this man behind me, THE OUTLAW MORRIS BASS are all on the VXW team! But the team is a five man team. [Sebastian stands tall in the center of the ring and smiles] The last man on the VXW team is . . [Nookie by Limp Bizkit hits and an old friend to the VXW comes walking out on stage to a HUUUGE SHOCK POP from the crowd] .. . . SADISTIC INSANITY! DAMIAN PAYNE!!!!!! .. .. .. So the show came to an end, with security charging the ring as the brawl started all over again. Sebastian came up the aisle to ask Val about the choice of Damian Payne, and Morris Bass threw up his hands and walked off. WrestleBowl is coming up!
