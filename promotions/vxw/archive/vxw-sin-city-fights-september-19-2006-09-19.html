Sin City Fights September 19

September 19, 2006

[Cold opening to Sebastian sitting in his locker room. A courier walks in and hands an envelope to the Starmaker] SEBASTIAN: What the hell is this? COURIER: Hey man, I just deliver. [Sebastian takes the envelope and signs for it, he opens the envelope and reads the paper inside. A big smile breaks out on his face} SEBASTIAN: Finally! [Viva Las Vegas hits and we cut to action from past VXW shows, then we cut to JD Southerns sitting at the VXW desk} [JD Southerns! Where in the hell is Darlene?] SOUTHERNS: Welcome to Sin City Fights! This is our final show before Vegas Violence. At Vegas Violence we will crown champions. Legends will perhaps be made. I am JD Southerns and I am the new host for Sin City Fights. For those of you hoping to see Lean Darlene Orion I have some bad news. Lets just go to the footage. [Lean Darlene Orion is backstage. Delila walks toward her with her tag team Brian and Kris Dierdorf] LDO: Can I get a quick interview? Is it true you will be entering the TV Title open match to- [Before she can finish Delila shoves her, Darlene, a fighting spirit, comes out swinging and connects with a right hand to Delila. That sets off Kris and Brian who beat her down until security rushes in to make the save] {CUT back to JD] SOUTHERNS: Darlene was taken to UMC Medical Center and she will be okay, but she will not be here tonight. So you are stuck with me. [half the VXW male audience begins flipping channels] SOUTHERNS: The Polish Powers and Purgatory. These two teams have been the puppets in the attempts of Sebastian to draw out The Equalizer. But both teams are legitimate contenders to the tag team titles. Lets see who got the upper hand. .. .. .. ========================== PURGATORY (vs) THE POLISH POWERS ========================== [CUT to just after the bell rings, Synn and Papa Joe square off] STONE: I think these two could tear it down in a one on one match. DTMA: I agree, submission artist Synn and former MMA fighter Joe. [POP! As Sebastian and Robbie Caldwell rush down to the ring] DTMA: HERE WE GO!!! [Caldwell uses his tazer on PsyKlone and drops the big man to the floor, Sebastian joins Joe and Klenski in a triple team of Synn] STONE: This is not what I wanted to see! DTMA: Joe and Klenski holding Synn open as Sebastian takes free shot after free shot! [Caldwell grabs a chair and CLANG! Nails PsyKlone over the head] STONE: Robbie Caldwell owes Sebastian. [Caldwell slides into the ring with his tazer gun. Sebastian orders him to tazer the defenseless Synn] [Bad To The Bone hits and the place ERUPTS! All eyes look to the curtains and out from behind walks, the former heavyweight champion, the man Sebastian has been waiting to see for nearly a year. . . THE EQUALIZER!!!!] DTMA: IT'S THE EQUALIZER!!!! STONE: THIS PLACE IS GOING CRAZY!!!! [The Polish Powers drop Synn, the four men in the ring watch as The Equalizer lifts the mic to his mouth] E: "No, Sebastian, this isnt an illusion. I've tried to enjoy my retirement, and leave the task of shutting your mouth to those in the VXW locker room. But alas, that proved to be fruitless. Omega didnt do the job. Harper didnt do the job. And even Purgatory didnt do the job. Which just goes to show...if you want something done right, you need to do it yourself." {He paces back and forth across the stage} E: "I've had to listen to your drivel for the past year. I've had to listen to your arrogance, to your lies...and your lame ass attempt at a challenge. But even my patience has limits, Sebastian. So, it seems the only way to shut you up, is to accept your challenge, and beat you into a bloody pulp myself!" {He pauses, the crowd CHEERS!} E: "You want a tag team match at Vegas Violence? That's great. And I wont make you wait to find out who my partner is. Now, granted, I'm sure a lot of people will be expecting me to name one of the members of Purgatory to be my partner, but not this time. In fact....my partner is going to turn some heads...not to mention really piss you off, Sebastian." {He grins, scratching his chin mischievously.} E: "The last time I set foot in a VXW ring..it was the VWF, and I was its World Heavyweight Champion. Let me take you back to that night. I was fighting Adam Jennings for my title, and I was just about to claim victory. Do you remember what happened, Sebastian?" {he resumes his pacing, as he wanders down memory lane.} E: "And then...just as I was about to reach my title belt....do you remember what happened? I was debilitated by a tazer...wielded by none other than your acquaintence, Robbie Caldwell. And you see, I am a firm believer in keeping my friends close....and my enemies closer. You seem to be quite chummy with Mr Electricity lately...so I feel its better to keep your new ally right where I can keep an eye on him...and more importantly, keep him away from you. So...at Vegas Violence, the Starmaker will have to find himself a new partner....because I claim Robbie Caldwell as mine! Expect the unexpected, Sebastian, and you'll still be surprised!" {"Bad to the Bone" plays again, as "The Equalizer" turns and leaves, returning backstage, leaving a furious Sebastian in the ring.} DTMA: The Equalizer is back! And he has taken Caldwell as his partner for the match at Vegas Violence! STONE: No one could have predicted this! [CUT back to JD Southerns] SOUTHERNS: Now the question is who will Sebastian pick as his partner for the tag team match at Vegas Violence. The Equalizer and Robbie Caldwell will face Sebastian and a yet to be named partner at Vegas Violence. So we have one match set. Now lets move on to see which two men will meet for the TV Title at Vegas Violence! The rules for the open match were simple. The eight men who entered were split into two groups of four, winner of each group would meet for the TV title at double V! .. .. .. =========================================================== TV Title Open Match RING ONE -------- BLAKE HAMILTON (vs) JOHN MANNING (vs) JUSTIN HARPER (vs) KRIS DIERDORF RING TWO -------- ROMAN ANDERSON (vs) LANCE VOLTAR (vs) BRIAN DIERDORF (vs) MILES MCKINLEY =========================================================== [CUT to moments after the bell] DTMA: Two rings, eight men! Brian Dierdorf and McKinley start to mix it up in Ring 2] DTMA: Justin Harper and Kris Dierdorf have found each other! RING ANNOUNCER: Roman Anderson has been counted out of ring two and has been eliminated! [Anderson clotheslines Manning out of ring one and to the floor] DTMA: Roman Anderson is about getting his revenge, he doesn't seem to care about the TV Title. [Harper gets a vicious elbow from Kris Dierdorf. Dierdorf then yanks Blake Hamilton out of the corner, lifts him above his head and then drops him to the mat] STONE: And Manning and Anderson are fighting up the aisle and to the back! RING ANNOUNCER: John Manning has been counted out of ring one and has been eliminated! DTMA: We are down to six! [CUT FORWARD in the match. In Ring One Kris Dierdorf has been dominating the action while it has been back and forth in Ring Two. McKinley climbs to the top rope of Ring Two] STONE: ITS FLYING KILT TIME!!! .. . . SENTON ONTO BRIAN DIERDORF!!! DTMA: ONE!!!!!!! TWO!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! VOLTAR MAKES THE SAVE!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! [In Ring One Kris Dierdorf hits both Harper and Hamilton with clotheslines] STONE: Lance Voltar on his feet and McKinley charges him! DTMA: SMALL PACKAGE BY VOLTAR!! ONE!!!!!!!! TWO!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! THRRRRRRRREEEEEEEEEEEEEE!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! RING ANNOUNCER: Miles McKinley has been eliminated from Ring Two! DTMA: Ring Two is down to Voltar and Brian Dierdorf! [Kris Dierdorf comes up behind Hamilton and locks him in KRIS' DELIGHT!] STONE: Harper on his feet and he stands back, no reason to make the save! DTMA: AND BLAKE HAMILTON IS TAPPING OUT!!! RING ANNOUNCER: Blake Hamilton has been eliminated from Ring One! DMTA: We are down to the final four. [CUT FORWARD, the action between Brian and Voltar has been becoming more and more one side toward Brian Dierdorf while Justin Harper has fought back and is now controlling ring one] DTMA: Justin Harper off the ropes, .. . . CORKSCREW ELBOW!!! (LETS GO HARPER! Clap clap clapclapclap!) STONE: Harper picks up Dierdorf! .. . . RUNNING POWERSLAM!!!!! [Voltar flips over and manages to hit a LEGDROP on Dierdorf as they hit the mat} DTMA: ONE!!!!!!!! TWO!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! THRRRRRRRRRRREEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEE!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! RING ANNOUNCER: Brian Dierdorf has been eliminated! The winner of ring two, .. . . THE BIG V! LANCE VOLTAR!!!!!!!!! [Justin Harper off the ropes and hits the . . ] STONE: WIPEOUT!!!!!!! DTMA: Harper hooks the leg! ONE!!!!!!!!!!! TWO!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! THRRRRRRRRREEEEEEEEEEEEEEEE!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! RING ANNOUNCER: Kris Dierdorf has been eliminated. The winner of ring one .. . . JUSTIN HARPER!!!!!!!!!!!! [FADE with Harper walking over and staring at Lance Voltar, Voltar tries his best tough guy stare back but breaks into a smile then lifts up his arms to the crowd] .. .. .. .. .. ((((( COMMERCIALS ))))) .. .. .. .. .. [Cut to--] V/O: Perfection... [Ah yes, that voice. Wrapped within it's familiar welcoming tone of insincerity, we know it all too well. The sight that fades up before us is also more than familiar. Six feet, eleven inches. Three hundred and twelve pounds. A specimen of physical humanity truly honed for the task at hand. Or in this case, *in* hand, two heavy dumbells, driven by will and exertion and lightning-spiraled musculature through their well-worn, unrelenting course. Yes, he's lifting weights. Geez.] V/O: Such a fleeting, unattainable thing, don't you think? Many of us, we strive for it, few if any attain it. But all of us, we all recognize it when we see it. A combination of elements, that fused together can bring us all the success and riches we could ever hope for in this world. An entity, a state of being that can come in many shapes and forms. Take this business of ours for example-here we know what perfection entails, that flawless blend of MIGHT... [Our voice pauses, letting the shot linger on the towering titanic form of the man known as Thunder Valenkov.] V/O: ... and MIND. And of course that would be where *I* come in. [Indeed he does, stepping before Valenkov with that ever-present knowing smirk. Your friend and mine, the manager of future champions. Chris Wheeling.] Wheeling: You know, there's a saying where I come from... that while the best of us innovate, the rest of us immitate. No, actually I just made that up, but it's rather fitting don't you think? In this little wrestling nirvana of ours, the best... well we all know who deserves that particular label. The man who turned this company on its head and remade it in his image. Who even as we speak stands one step from the brink of regaining all he lost. Regaining everything that was stolen from him, by ill fate and dirty deeds. And for those keeping score at home, that would of course be _me_. [He flashes our favorite faux-warm smile from within the shadow of the Soviet monster, Valenkov, who still unrelentingly lifts and drops those weights, his focus on this one task and no other.] Wheeling: But there is of course another saying-immitation is the deepest form of flattery. And so, to my greatest fan, Chad Essington, I say this-I'm flattered. Flattered that you would attempt to mold yourself in my image, take the lessons you have learned from the success that I've achieved... and apply them to yourself, and your own lofty goals. Unfortunately, when those goals happen to intersect with my own... well, let's just say that flattery may get you many places, but the Twenty-Four Hour Championship... *MY* Twenty-Four Hour Championship... will certainly not be one of them. I'm sure you understand, Chad. After all, we're cut from the same cloth, are we not? [That one draws a subtle smirk.] Wheeling: I've worked to0 hard these past few months, I've fought too long for the latest player in your fool's parade to interfere with that now. Although I will say this, Chad... you're representing a better class of bottom-feeder these days. [Wheeling lets out an amused chuckle.] Wheeling: No, that's unfair, because even I'll admit that you may have actually found something this time. Mr. Ken "The Perfection" Young, while he may not yet deserve that self-appointed moniker, certainly is not without potential. The kind of potential that all too easily could be spun into something great, with the right amount of guidance... and the *appropriate* representation. [And there, a knowing wink, and again the insincere smile.] Wheeling: But that I'm afraid will have to be for another day. Because tonight you want the same thing I want, Chad. Unfortunately you can't have it. You've come a long way, my friend, and so has Mr. Young in a very short space of time. But you haven't come far enough, and tonight will highlight that fact. But by all means, don't look at this as a setback in your quest. Instead, I suggest you look at it as an opportunity... the ideal opportunity to see how far the both of you still have to come. In fact, dare I say it may turn out to be... [He pauses, casting a glance over his shoulder at the human machinery of destruction that still towers over him, Valenkov finally turning his attention to the camera.] Wheeling: ... the *perfect* opportunity. [And with, as ever, that final smirk, our scene of might and mind fades out.] .. .. .. [Fade in on JD Southerns at the VXW desk] SOUTHERNS: The TV Title match is set, Justin Harper takes on The Big V Lance Voltar. The 24 Hour Title match will be set by the end of this show, but what about the tag team titles? Well lets go to our president Ric Beauty to find out about that. [Ric Beauty is sitting at his desk at VXW headquarters] RIC BEAUTY: We are getting closer and closer to Vegas Violence, we are going to crown champions. Since Justin Harper was man enough to step up and be the TV Champion we here at the VXW have decided to recognize Harper as the champion from before. He will have to defend that title at Vegas Violence against The Big V Lance Voltar. And of course, the 24 Hour Title will be awarded on that night. And for the tag team titles, we are going to have a Six Pack Tag Team match! Purgatory, The Polish Powers, The O'Malleys, The Dierdorfs, Team Brittania and a special team of my own making. The Surfer and The Outlaw! Blake Hamilton and Morris Bass! Six teams, first one to get a pinfall or submission gets the dubya and the titles. [CUT back to JD Southerns] SOUTHERNS: Whoa! Ric Beauty putting together Bass and Hamilton? Blake Hamilton has been talking about Bass all around the locker room about his win over Bass. How will the Outlaw take to it? Well The Perfection has been on a quick rise here in the VXW, how would he fare against a man who has been to the top of the VXW mountain three times? .. .. .. ============================= Final Four Match THUNDER VALENKOV (vs) KEN 'THE PERFECTION' YOUNG ============================= [CUT FORWARD, an early back and forth match has now become a test of power that Valenkov is winning. Young has rolled out of the ring after getting tossed by Valenkov] STONE: Young has not been manhandled like this since coming to the VXW, and I think he is not sure how to take it. DTMA: Valenkov is the picture of confidence, there is a reason this man has been a three time 24 Hour Champion. [Young rolls into the ring and they lock up again, Young hits a quick knee to the midsection and hits a facebuster as a follow up.] STONE: Well, he has the upper hand, now is the time. DTMA: But Young poses! [The crowd boos him as he poses, Young doesn't notice Valenkov getting to his feet behind him. Valenkov locks him in a FULL NELSON!] STONE: Valenkov cranking up the pressure! DTMA: Young trying to break the hold, but I think Valenkov might be to powerful! [Young begins to break the hold, the crowd is shocked!] STONE: I dunno Danny, it looks like he is going to break the hold! [HE DOES! And then uses an arm drag to take down the big man. Young stomps on him.] DTMA: Ken Young is a force folks! [CUT FORWARD, Young has been working on the shoulders of Valenkov using a nerve hold most of the time] DTMA: Young is working to try and take the power away from Valenkov, smart wrestling. STONE: Well Chad Essington has come a long way from his Mr. Smith/Mr. Jones days. [Valenkov gets to his feet and begins to elbow Young in the gut. Young returns with a forearm smash to the base of the skull] DTMA: Valenkov is not as effected by those shots as Young or Essington would like. [Young comes off the ropes, but is caught by Valenkov for a BIG TIME POWERSLAM!] STONE: Whoa! [Valenkov gets to his feet, shakes off the cobwebs and tries to get the blood running back to the shoulders] DTMA: The former champion has the look of a man possessed now! [Valenkov stomps on Young, then pulls him to his feet] STONE: Whips him to the ropes, .. . . RUSSIAN SICKLE CLOTHESLINE!!!!!!!!!!! DTMA: Young bouncing on the mat like a fish out of water. [Chris Wheeling motioning that he wants this one to end] STONE: Valenkov pulls up a weary Young, that clothesline took a lot out of him. [Valenkov picks up Young in the bearhug] DTMA: HERE IT COMES! STONE: SOVIET JUDO SU- [HUUUGE POP AS SEBASTIAN AND ROBBIE CALDWELL RUSH TO THE RING!!!!!!] DTMA: Now what!?!?!?! THE STAR MAKER WITH A CHAIR! [Wheeling screams at Valenkov who moves] <<< CLANG! >>> DTMA: SEBASTIAN NAILED THE REFEREE!!!!!
