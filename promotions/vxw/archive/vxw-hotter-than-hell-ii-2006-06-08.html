Hotter Than Hell II

(We see a replay of Hotter Than Hell I, 24 Hour Champion Adam Jennings walking through a door, then we hear a scuffle, then camera then went behind the door and found Jennings on the floor holding his head) .. .. .. (Hotter Than Hell by KISS hits and the screen comes alive with scenes from The Gauntlet PPV. Jennings in the ring with Omega and The Outlaw Morris Bass. Thunder Valenkov clearing out the ring Roman Anderson jumping out of the ring, scared of Valenkov Miles McKinley eliminating Valenkov Marcus O'Malley as the winner of The Gauntlet 2006 CUT to Lean Darlene Orion at the desk) LDO: The question is still out there, who jumped Adam Jennings on our last show? The 24 Hour Champ has a host of men who think they should get the next shot at his title, but would any of them try this kind of stuff? Would Omega? Bass? Or could it even be Marcus O'Malley? Only time will tell, but fans I am on the case. I am Darlene Orion and this is Hotter Than Hell! And it is Hotter Than Hell in Las Vegas. Things are heating up all over. Lets go some footage from backstage earlier. .. .. .. * Papa Joe and Stan Klenski storm through a dressing room door marked "Sebastian" * KLENSKI: THIS IS THE BEST YOU COULD DO FOR US??? SEBASTIAN: Calm down boys. KLENSKI: Let me get this straight. You pulled us out of the Gauntlet, you had us pass up shots at the tag team titles, and now the best match you can get us is against Los Diablos???? SEBASTIAN: (stands up on his crutches) Remember who brought you here! Sebastian! Do you think Sebastian makes you into stars overnight? Do you think Sebastian has some magic wand and POOF you are at the same level as The Equalizer? No, it takes time. Sebastian made The Equalizer, and Sebastian will make the two of you into a great tag team. Patience. KLENSKI: The last thing I saw had you talking about making Synn and PsyKlone into main eventers, no mention of us! PAPA JOE: (moves toward Sebastian) SEBASTIAN: Its all part of the big plan. Patience Klenski, practice some patience. (Sebastian limps out of the room leaving Klenski and Joe alone) KLENSKI: I am getting sick of that guy. PAPA JOE: We make ourselves stars. We make our own path. (CUT back to LDO) .. .. .. LDO: Papa Joe and Klenski seem to losing patience with Sebastian. The Star Maker still seems intent on drawing out The Equalizer. .. .. .. ++++++++++++++++++ POLISH POWERS (vs) LOS DIABLOS ++++++++++++++++++ (CUT to the beginning of the match, the bell rings and The Polish Powers rush the Diablos and pound them) DTMA: Klenski and Papa Joe taking the fight to the masked boys from the Academy! STONE: Don't worry, we will be coming back strong! VXW ACADEMY!!! (CUT forward, the beating has continued, Los Diablo #1 has been knocked to the floor) DTMA: Klenski tags in Papa Joe, the former Polish street fighter! (Papa Joe and Klenski whip Diablo #2 to the ropes and hit him with a STIFF DOUBLE CLOTHESLINE!) STONE: Joe looking confident here, I think he is thinking.. .. . . JOE CHOKE!!! THE PAPA JOE CHOKE!!! (Papa Joe slaps a REAR NAKED CHOKE on Diablo #2 then pulls him off his feet, Klenski dives off the apron to make sure Diablo #1 does not interfere) DTMA: AND DIABLO #2 is OUT!!!! <<< DING! DING! DING! >>> STONE: Choked him out! RING ANNOUNCER: Winners of this match, PAPA JOE and STAN KLENSKI, .. . . THE POLISH POWERS!!!!! (Klenski and Papa Joe leave the ring and make their way backstage. We cut backstage and they come around a corner and they see Sebastian standing with some of the VXW crew. They stop to hear Sebastian finish up a bad Polish joke. One of the crew members points and Sebastian turns around) SEBASTIAN: Hey, great match. Great umm.. work out there. PAPA JOE: I think I have had enough of you Sebastian. SEBASTIAN: Slow down there tough guy. Sebastian is just having some fun. No hard feelings. (Sebastian walks, on his crutches off, giving a knowing smile to the crew he was joking with) KLENSKI: I am getting sick of him. PAPA JOE: We make our own path, through him. (Fade) .. .. .. (CUT back to Darlene at the VXW desk) LDO: It looks like there might be trouble in Sebastian paradise. No Equalizer, and now his hired help seems to be having enough of him. Lets change gears and talk about John Manning. Manning had a huge debut at The Gauntlet, but lost on our last show to Cobra in a match for Cobra's Spin The Wheel title. Manning was looking to right the ship against VXW Academy member Roddy Barber. .. .. .. ++++++++++++++ JOHN MANNING (vs) RODDY BARBER ++++++++++++++ (Cut forward to just after a powerslam by Manning) STONE: Manning strutting around the ring like he is beating the champ. DTMA: He is a picture of confidence. (Manning waits for Barber to get up, then hits an INVERTED ATOMIC DROP on him then goes to the ropes) DTMA: SCISSORS KICK!!!! (Manning springs to his feet, he slicks back that jet black hair and then pulls Barber up and sets him up for the . . ) DTMA: FORWARD FLIPPING PILEDRIVER!!!!! STONE: IMPACT! DTMA: Manning makes the academic cover ONE!!!!!!!!! TWO!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! THRRRRRRRRREEEEEEEEEE!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! <<< DING! DING! DING! >>> RING ANNOUNCER: Winner of this match as the result of a pinfall, .. . . JOHN MANNING!!!!!!!!!!! (Fade to commercial with Manning in the center of the ring, slicking back his hair and talking trash to the crowd) .. .. .. .. .. ((((( COMMERCIALS ))))) .. .. .. .. .. (Rod Beauty is sitting at his desk, surrounded by the four members of Team Essington, his new bodyguards. Chad Essington walks in) CHAD ESSINGTON: No sign of Fenriz here tonight. ROD BEAUTY: Figures. So there is no way anyone can collect my bounty if no one can find Fenriz. I heard he was released from Shootfire. I think his demons might have finally gotten the best of him. CHAD ESSINGTON: Rod, I wanted to ask for a favor if I could. My guys are ready to defend you at the drop of a hat in case Fenriz, or anyone tries to get to you. But we do have some business we need to take care of. KAOS. They have stuck their noses in my business and I want a chance to take at least one of them out. I am asking, with all respect for a match at Hotter Than Hell III. A tag team match would be best, but I'll take whatever you give me. ROD BEAUTY: (thoughtful) Well Chad, right now things are still hot with Fenriz. I have problems all over the company. I am reluctant to send two of these guys away from my side. But one would be ok. CHAD ESSINGTON: Great! ROD BEAUTY: (sizing up the Team Essington members) And Chad, you would want to take out the biggest one right? So lets see.. the seven foot Dynamo against. . Mr. Smith!!! (Chad's jaw drops to the floor! The seven footer against the four footer) CHAD ESSINGTON: But.. ROD BEAUTY: This will keep the bigger guys here right by my side. CHAD ESSINGTON: B-b-b- ROD BEAUTY: Now get back out there and see what the word on the street is about Lark Fenriz. Remember our deal Chad. I would hate to have to review the contract between the VXW and Essington Sports. CHAD ESSINGTON: (snorts) Fine. (CUT back to LDO) .. .. .. (Darlene is sitting at the desk, her legs are crossed and she caresses the one on top and takes a deep breath. Umm. . . back to the show) LDO: I need a massage. But there is one guy who I would not want anywhere near me, Omega. The big man has hit VXW like a storm. He injured Robbie Caldwell, Iyasu Saku and Sebastian. He even took out a rookie from the academy named Matt Payne. At Hotter Than Hell One Justin Harper and Omega went to a time limit draw in a great battle. Harper got this night off, but Omega drew the lucky rookie from the academy Miles McKinley. .. .. .. ++++++++++++++++ OMEGA (vs) MILES MCKINLEY ++++++++++++++++ (Cut forward, Omega has been dominant. He has beaten McKinley all over the ring and has just whipped him to the ropes) DTMA: BACK BODY DROP!!!! STONE: McKinley lands on his feet! DTMA: WHOA! SPINNIGN HEEL KICK BY MCKINLEY!!! (Omega sinks to one knee and McKinley hits him with a series of kicks then runs to the ropes, nailing Omega with a SHINING WIZARD!!!) STONE: I am telling you, McKinley has got a ton of potential. (McKinley comes off the ropes, but gets caught by Omega in a BEARHUG, that turns into a VIOLENT SPINEBUSTER!!!) DTMA: Omega now looking at the ankle! (Omega begins to just yank and pull at the ankle of McKinley) STONE: Omega is blind! He gets in these rages and .. . . ANKLELOCK FROM HELL!!!! (Omega pulls McKinley off the mat and is swinging him around) DTMA: The referee trying to see if .. (REF BUMP!!!) (McKinley knocked into the referee) STONE: Omega still with the anklelock! <<< DING! DING! DING! >>> RING ANNOUNCER: The winner of this match as the result of a disqualification, .. . . MILES MCKINLEY!!!!! (Omega breaks the hold and McKinley rolls out of the ring, grabbing at his ankle) (HUUUUGE POP!!!) DTMA: JUSTIN HARPER RACING TO THE RING!!!!! (Harper slides in and spins around Omega and the two men begin to punch at each other, VXW security runs down and tries to separate them) STONE: Harper really wants to take out the mighty Omega! (Omega breaks free of security and nails Harper with a HIGH KNEE in the forehead while Harper is restrained by security) DTMA: UNFAIR!!! Omega taking advantage!! (Security loses control of the action and Omega grabs a chair) <<< CLANG! >>> <<< CLANG! >>> (Harper drops to his knees, blood pouring from his head) DTMA: Omega needs to be stopped! (Omega seems satisfied and drops the chair in front of Harper. Security gets between them and leads Omega away from ringside) STONE: He just wants to hurt people, Omega needs counseling. DTMA: You want to have him sitting on your couch. STONE: Not a chance! (Fade to commercial with medics tending to the heavily bleeding Justin Harper) .. .. .. .. .. ((((( COMMERCIALS ))))) .. .. .. .. .. (Fade in on Darlene Orion sitting at the VXW desk) LDO: Thunder Valenkov has been in a small war with the VXW Academy since losing the 24 Hour Title to Adam Jennings. Valenkov has made examples out of academy members week after week, well Johnny Stone has had enough and he got a match for two of his rookies against Valenkov and his unlikely partner Roman Anderson. .. .. .. +++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++ THUNDER VALENKOV & MR MAIN EVENT ROMAN ANDERSON (vs) JOEY YOUNG & TOM 'TOP OF THE LINE' ROGERS +++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++ (Cut forward to about four minutes into the match. Young and Rogers have had moments against Anderson, but once he tagged in Valenkov it has been all one sided) DTMA: Anderson whips Young to the ropes. STONE: KICK TO THE FACE!!! Anderson telegraphed it! (Anderson stumbles backwards, Young charges in and hits a MONKEY FLIP on Mr Main Event!) STONE: These youngsters are really beginning to jell! (Young off the ropes, Anderson sidesteps the dropkick and tags in Valenkov) DTMA: Young has to tag! (Valenkov comes off the ropes and hits the RUSSIAN SICKLE clothesline on Young) DTMA: Valenkov is so powerful. (The former 24 Hour Champion pulls Young to his feet and then sets him up for the . . . ) .. . . SOVIET JUDO SUPLEX!!!!! STONE: Anderson tagged him!! Roman Anderson tagged Valenkov while Thunder was performing that move! (Anderson climbs in the ring and makes the cover on Young) DTMA: ONE!!!!!!! TWO!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! YOUNG KICKS OUT!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! STONE: These kids are tough, they are ready to make their move! (Anderson turns to Valenkov and begins to yell at him for not putting them away, just then the crowd POPS!) DTMA: DAMIAN PAYNE!!! SADISTIC INSANITY!!! (Payne tackles Valenkov off the ring apron and they roll to the floor, Valenkov landing awkwardly) STONE: Payne looking for payback, Valenkov cost him a match on our last show! (Young manages to make the tag to Tom Rogers. Roman Anderson is watching Payne and Valenkov when Rogers gets him in a CRUCIFIX PINNING COMBO!!!) DTMA: ONE!!!!!!! TWO!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! THRRRRRRRRRREEEEEEEEEEEEEEE!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! <<< DING! DING! DING! >>> RING ANNOUNCER: Winners of this match as the result of a pinfall, .. . . JOEY YOUNG and TOM 'TOP OF THE LINE' ROGERS!!!! DTMA: A major upset!!! STONE: HELL YEAH!!! This has been a pretty good night for my boys! (Fade back to Darlene, with shots of the jubilant Young and Rogers) .. .. .. LDO: So a big, BIG upset for the rookies. Thanks in no small part to Damian Payne. The bad news is that doctors are taking a look at Valenkov, who landed awkwardly on his shoulder. As of right now it looks like Valenkov might be out of action for a few weeks. That brought us to the nights main event, a non-title match between Team Brittania and the O'Malleys. .. .. .. ++++++++++++++++++ Non-Title Match TEAM BRITTANIA (vs) THE O'MALLEYS ++++++++++++++++++ (Cut forward, TB has isolated Marcus and has been using quick tags and some shenanigans to keep control of the match) STONE: Anthony trying to rally this crowd! (O MALLEY! O MALLEY!) DTMA: Marcus getting to his feet, using the ELBOW!!! (Marcus continues to elbow Simon in the ribs until he breaks the chin lock, Marcus turns and hits a JAWBREAKER!)
