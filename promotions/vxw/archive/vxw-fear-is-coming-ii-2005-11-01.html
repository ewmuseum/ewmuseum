Fear Is Coming II

Nov 1, 2005

(The Beautiful People hits and the screen begins to fill, one by one with pictures of the various VXW superstars once they are all on the screen the word FEAR blazes over them, then we fade to Dan The Man Aller) DTMA: Welcome to Fear Is Coming Two. Earlier tonight Sams Town was rocking with matches picked by the fans of the VXW. But two of the matches that scored high in the voting were not held tonight, but will be held on future VXW shows. At Fear Is Coming Three, Thunder Valenkov and Adam Jennings will meet in a Dream Partners Match. Who will they pick as their partners? And at Fear Is Coming Four, Jennings teams with Marcus O'Malley to face off against Kagedo and The Outlaw Morris Bass in a Vegas Streetfight! So now you are wondering, which matches were picked to be held tonight? A title match? Yes! But before we get to that one, lets check out the non title tag team four way! Made into a tornado match by Rob Beauty! Voted on by you the fans! .. .. .. ================================= Tag Team Four Way Tornado Match HOT PROPERTY (vs) TEAM BRITTANIA (vs) PURGATORY (vs) THE RODEO BOYS ================================= With all four teams in the ring, the bell rang and the chaos began! Justin and Simon Davidson went after the Rodeo Boys while HP slid out of the ring, trying to get the bigger Purgatory boys out of the ring. Synn went to the outside to get double teamed by HP while PsyKlone stayed in the ring and went to work on Billy Bones, giving Team Brittania a chance to double team Kenny Kidd. HP rolled into the ring after hitting a DOUBLE SLAM on Synn and they hit side by side SWINGING NECKBREAKERS on Team Brittania. PsyKlone used a SPINNING SIDEWALK SLAM to put some serious hurt on Bones. HP then went to the double team of Kenny Kidd. Kidd was already in trouble after the beating he took from Team Brittania. Knight held him up for a VERTICAL SUPLEX while Lee DROPKICKED him over to the mat! But when Lee jumped to his feet he was tossed over the ropes by PsyKlone. Knight spun around only to end up face to face with the seven footer, Knight dropped to the mat and tried to call for a time out. PsyKlone smiled, and then got DOUBLE TEAMED by the Brits who were back on their feet. Justin and Simon picked up the big him and hit him with a DOUBLE DDT and made the cover ONE!!!!!!! TWO!!!!!!!!!!!!!! BUT SYNN BROKE UP THE FALL!!!!!!!!! Synn and Simon tangled into the corner while Justin Davidson came off the ropes, looking to hit a SPLASH on the big man but before he got close enough he was hit with MISSLE DROPKICKS from the Rodeo Boys!!! Kenny Kidd made the cover on Justin ONE!!! TWO!!!!!!!!!!!! ONLY TO HAVE AUSTIN LEE PULL HIM OUT OF THE RING!!!!!! HP went back to double teaming of Kenny Kidd as Billy Bones came off the ropes and hit a SOMERSAULT SUICIDA PLANCHA on all three men. In the ring, Justin and Simon had gone to work on Synn and seemed ready to hit the HAIL BRITTANIA on him when the lights went out! The shadowy figure appeared once again in the upper reaches of the arena, but this time no wave of the arms to set the ring on fire. He only bowed his head. When the lights came back up, Justin Davidson was bleeding badly in the center of the ring and Synn was on top of him ONE!!!!!! TWO!!!!!!!!!!! THRRRRRRREEEEEEEEEE!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! ((((( WINNERS - Purgatory ))))) Big win for Purgatory. On our next show the main event is going to be a Tag Team Gauntlet match with the winners earning a place in the Rage In The Cage match. I think Purgatory has to be considered favorites. .. .. .. (Backstage, Rod Beauty is sitting at his desk when Rob Beauty walks in) ROD: Get out. ROB BEAUTY: Have you seen the latest? ROD: On this stupid idea of yours to let the fans pick the matches? No. ROB BEAUTY: You might want to put your tights on boy. ROD: WHAAAT? ROB BEAUTY: You and me tough guy, winner controls the VXW for 30 days! (Rob walks out with a spring in his step, Rod gazes at the floor) .. .. .. .. .. ((((( COMMERCIALS ))))) .. .. .. .. .. (CUT back to DTMA) DTMA: Well Rod and Rob Beauty each own 49% of the VXW, we still have no idea who owns the other 2%. But Rod and Rob met with the winner getting full control of the VXW for the next 30 days. And the special guest referee was Ric Beauty, Re-RAW star. For those of you who have been following Ric in Re-RAW you know he has become a bit of a drinker. .. .. .. =========================================== Winner Gets Control of the VXW For 30 Days ROD BEAUTY (vs) ROB BEAUTY =========================================== Ric stumbled to the ring in his referee shirt and called for the bell before with Rod or Rob had come to the ring. Once the match was underway the strategy of the younger Beauty was easy to see, he was trying to keep the pace of the match fast to wear out Rob. Rob has little in ring experience and does have that bad knee and Rod was quick to keep the match in the fast lane. But the pace, which was in the favor of Rod, was broken up when Ric called for the bell during a COLLAR AND ELBOW. When asked why he called for the bell he informed the ring announcer he would be back, and he made his way into the crowd, calling for the beer man. Another referee climbed into the ring and the match continued, but during the strange exchange with Ric, Rob had taken Rod to the mat and started to work on his neck and shoulders. Rob got Rod to his feet and used a SHOULDERBREAKER into a NERVE HOLD to try and get the submission. But Rod was not about to tap out. Rod hit a SNAP MARE and seemed to have taken some of the wind out of Rob, but a charging Rod got a SPINNING DOUBLE LEG LARIAT for his trouble. Rob then picked him up and hit the BEAUTY BRAINBUSTER!!! Rob then climbed to the top rope, and leaped off for the legdrop known as the BEAUTY BOMB!!!! ONE!!!!!!! TWO!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! BUT RIC PULLED THE REFEREE OUT OF THE RING!!! Ric let the other referee know he was the 'real' ref in the match and then knocked him down with a stiff right hand. Ric rolled in the ring, careful not to spill his beer and was confronted with an angry Rob. Rob then shoved Ric, who spilled his beer. Rod rolled up Rob in a SCHOOLBOY and Ric made the fastest count in VXW history. ONE TWO THREEE!! ((((( WINNER = Rod Beauty ))))) Ric then slurped up some of the spilled beer before stumbling his way to the locker room. Well the match that seemed to peek an interest for nearly everyone in the voting was the Halloween Mask Match. Four men in a one fall four way, but each man would be in a Halloween costume. We would only find out who was who after the fall! .. .. .. ======================= Halloween Mask Match JASON (vs) FREDDY (vs) DRACULA (vs) THE WEREWOLF ======================= As the four men made their way to the ring, fans were trying to figure out who was who. It seemed like the Werefolf was the smallest of the four. But once the bell rang this was a full on fight! Jason and Freddy teamed up to go after Dracula. While the Werewolf stayed his distance from the fighting. Freddy picked up Dracula and hit him with a SPLASH SLAM. When Freddy got to his feet he got a ENZIQUIRI from The Werewolf! The Werewolf then moved to Jason. But Jason tossed him out of the ring. Jason then was dumped over the top rope by Dracula. Dracula came off the second rope and hit Freddy with a TWISTING ELBOW DROP as The Werewolf and Jason were fighting on the outside of the ring. Dracula then made the cover on Freddy ONE!!!!! TWO!!!!!!!!!!!! BUT THIS WAS NOT A DREAM! FREDDY KICKED OUT!!! Dracula threw a few punches into the mask of Freddy, as The Werewolf sent Jason hard into the ring steps. The Werewolf followed that up with a GUT BUSTER on the ring barriers and left Jason hanging. But we all know Jason doesn't go away that easily. The Werewolf joined the action in the ring and SPEARED Dracula. The Werewolf then kicked Dracula in the gut and went for the HYPER DRIVE but was pushed off by Dracula. The fans exploded for The Werewolf! Dracula caught The Werewolf coming off the ropes for a SPINEBUSTER!!! But it was time for Nightmare on Elm Street XX, Freddy versus Dracula! Freddy powered into the blood sucker and drove him out of the ring. That's when Jason grabbed Freddy and pulled him out of the ring. Freddy versus Jason! And yes folks, this battle was much better than the first one. They were really going after each, and then Jason turned the tide his way with a KICK TO THE GROIN! The Werewolf was on his feet in the ring and he came off the ropes and used a BASEBALL SLIDE to kick Jason into Freddy! The Werewolf then ducked a charging Dracula, and hit him with the HYPER DRIVE!!! The place went A S B! (ape shit bonkers) The Werewolf then tossed Freddy and Jason into the ring. Sensing the end near both Jason and Freddy ducked a DOUBLE CLOTHESLINE by The Werewolf and then the two men went to work on him as a team. Freddy and Jason joined forces. They sent him to the ropes and Freddy hit a TILT A WHIRL BACKBREAKER on The Werewolf. Chants of Caldwell began, as Mr Electricity uses the tilt a whirl taught to him by Sebastian. Jennings, err... The Werewolf was in trouble. Jason then picked up The Werewolf and hit him with the .. . . SAMMAEL!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! The crowd let out a moan, as we saw the finisher of Lark Fenriz! But when Jason went to make the cover, Freddy knocked him out of the ring and made the cover himself. ONE!!!!!! TWO!!!!!!!!!!!!! THRRRRRRREEEEEEEEE!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! ((((( WINNER = Freddy ))))) Freddy then reached down and pulled off the mask of The Werewolf to reveal .. . . IYASU SAKU!!! Jason then pulled off his mask on the outside of the ring to reveal .. . . ADAM JENNINGS!!! Freddy then took off his mask in the ring to reveal .. . . LARK FENRIZ!!!! And an angry Dracula stormed to the back without ever taking off his mask! But Fenriz scored the win, after Adam Jennings hit the Sammael in near perfect fashion! This left Fenriz and Jennings in a long staredown. .. .. .. .. .. ((((( COMMERCIALS ))))) .. .. .. .. .. (We are once again in the office of Rod Beauty, he is sitting at his desk, still sweaty from his match but he looks much happier. A VXW official walks in with videotapes) VXW OFFICIAL: Here you go sir, tapes on all possible number one contenders. ROD: Great. I am going to pour over these and name my own number one contender for the Rage In The Cage match. (The official walks out, Lark Fenriz walks in) ROD: What do you want? (Fenriz just stares at Rod) ROD: You think I should put you in the cage right? Are you crazy? Listen up wolf boy. You are the kind of guy I don't want in MY VXW. The old way of thinking around here, and just like The Maquilladora you are on your way out. So if you think.. (Fenriz pulls a piece a clothing from behind his back) ROD: (near trembling) Is that what I think it is? (Fenriz nods) ROD: What the f*** kind of monster are you? (Fenriz places the piece of clothing on the desk and then hold up his finger to signify he wants to be named the number one contender then he walks out. The camera swings back to Rod who picks up the piece of clothing) (CUT back to DTMA) DTMA: That gives me the shakes. Fenriz has always been a strange guy but I haven't got the foggiest idea what the hell that was all about. Lark did pick up the win in the Halloween Mask Match, so I can see where he want to be named the other number one contender. Rob Beauty has named Adam Jennings, and Jennings has already signed to be in the match. Later on we will see the match RITC Qualifier between Justin Harper and The Outlaw Morris Bass. But first a TV Title Match! Usually we spin the wheel to see the stipulations but the fans got to choose those too. .. .. .. ================================ VXW TV-Title Match Ladder Match MIKE 'THE BIZ' BISIGNANO © (vs) MR ELECTRICITY ROBBIE CALDWELL ================================= Caldwell came to the ring with more fire in his eyes than he has in recent matches, but The Biz came to the ring looking like a champion. The Biz and Mr Electricity locked up and the match was quickly taken control of by Caldwell. Using his size advantage he powered The Biz back into the corner and applied some well places elbows before whipping Bisignano into the opposite corner and then hitting a CLOTHESLINE on the champ as he came out. Caldwell then hit The Biz with a PUMP HANDLE SLAM. Mr Electricity showed some signs of confidence as he climbed to the top rope and dove to the mat, only to miss whatever the hell it was he was trying to do. Bisignano DROPKICKED Caldwell in the knee three times, before Caldwell hit the mat, and then The Biz went to work on the leg and knee of Mr Electricity. Mike Bisignano used all the tricks in the book over the next four to five minutes to wear down the knee of Robbie Caldwell. Elbow drops, spinning tow holds, wishbone around the ring post, each time with an intensity about him that had the fans roaring for the TV Champion. The Biz was going for the figure four when Roman Anderson rushed out from the back. The Biz saw him coming and jumped to his feet. While The Biz and Anderson were exchanging words Caldwell slid to the outside of the ring and grabbed a chair. The Biz turned into a MASSIVE CHAIR TO THE FACE!!! Anderson then pulled the ladder out from under the ring and helped Caldwell get it in the ring. Caldwell set up the ladder and began to climb. But the champ got to his feet and started up the ladder after him. Caldwell turned hit a SUNSET FLIP off the ladder driving The Biz into the canvas. Mr Electricty then folded up the ladder and placed it upright in the corner, he grabbed The Biz and executed a GERMAN RELASE SUPLEX onto the ladder, and Bisignano took a SICK landing! Caldwell looked very happy with himself, and he placed the ladder on the mat in the center of the ring. He put The Biz on the top rope and signaled for the SHOCK AND HORROR!! But when he went for the move, his knee buckled and both collapsed onto the ladder with Caldwell taking the brunt of the fall. Roman Anderson got into the ring and tried to help Caldwell get to his feet. The Biz roused himself and saw Anderson shoving away the ref and trying to help up Caldwell. The TV Champion then attacked Anderson! The Biz worked him over and then set him up and hit the FORWARD FLIPPING PILEDRIVER!!! The crowd exploded and The Biz rolled Anderson out of the ring. Bisignano then set up the ladder and started the climb, as Caldwell rolled on the canvas below. But as The Biz neared the top Caldwell got to his feet and he started to climb up the other side. The Biz reached up and started to unhook the title when Caldwell stopped him as he reached the top. The two men exchanged blows but Caldwell was getting the better of the exchange, and then Caldwell locked up and the champ .. . . AND HIT THE SHOCK AND HORROR OFF THE TOP OF THE LADDER!!!!!!!!!! The arena was in shock! Neither man was moving! Finally Caldwell rolled out of the ring and threw up his hands and walked off to the back! In a strange move, maybe because its Halloween, Caldwell who seemed to have the win in his hand left the ring area. The Biz got to his feet, climbed the ladder and unhook the title! ((((( WINNER = Mike 'The Biz' Bisignano ))))) The actions of Robbie Caldwell have left a lot of people wondering just what is going on in the head of that young man. The win goes to The Biz. But before The Biz could leave the ringside area he was greeted by David Xavier and his client Kyle Sturgis. Both men scoffed as they passed Bisignano. .. .. .. ================= $1,000 CHALLENGE ================= David Xavier getting on the mic and issuing his challenge once again...$1,000 per minute that anyone can last in a match with his over seven-foot tall monster, Kyle Sturgis. Johnny Stone walked out with two of his students, Tom Rogers and Joey Young. Stone talked about how the money Xavier had could do some real good for the VXW Academy. Xavier laughed at Stone thinking any of his students had a chance against Sturgis. Up first was Rogers. At the bell, Sturgis powered Rogers immediately into the corner and pummeled him with forearm shots to the face. Rogers was already dazed and confused. Sturgis pulled Rogers to the middle of the ring and lifted him into his arm-across-the-throat inverted DVD he calls the Cutthroat Driver! A sick head dropping move, and Rogers is OUT. But since Sturgis still has time, he picks up Rogers and hits his SICK head dropping finishing move, the STURGIS DEVASTATION. 1...2...3. Time of the match was 40 seconds. The second match immediately starts, with Joey Young trying to get an advantage by coming off the top rope at Sturgis. The big man just catches him and lifts him HIGH into the air and down with a vicious chokeslam. He then lifts Young up, holding him under his arms and knees and hits his package piledriver he calls the Mount Sturgis Drop. Young is done. 1...2...3. Time of the match, 29 seconds. After the match, Xavier gets on the mic and makes fun of Stone for his students doing worse and worse each week. Xavier then says if Stone wants money for the VXW Academy so badly, at Phear Phantastic it can be Kyle Sturgis vs. JOHNNY STONE in the $1,000 challenge! Stone nods and seems to agree to the match as the segment ends! .. .. .. .. .. ((((( COMMERCIALS ))))) (FADE IN on Dan The Man Aller) DTMA: Well it has been signed if you will, Johnny 'Hands Of' Stone has accepted the $1,000 Challenge. He will take his shot at it during Phear Phantastic. This Sturgis guy is a real monster, but one punch from a guy like Stone and not only could he go a minute by Johnny could actually pin the big man. The main event at Phear Phantastic is the Rage In The Cage match. Ten men and all the titles are on the line. The winner of the final match of the night would get a place inside the cage. .. .. .. ======================== Steel Cage Match THE OUTLAW MORRIS BASS (vs) JUSTIN HARPER ======================== Harper made his entrance before the cage was lowered from the ceiling. As he was standing in the ring, Bass and Kags came out of the crowd and attacked him. Kags then moved to take over the controls of the cage and Bass held Harper so that his head was over the side of the ring and it looked like the cage was going to be lowered onto Justin Harpers throat. But Adam Jennings and Marcus O'Malley came out from the back and chased off Kagedo, Harper then rolled over and took over on Bass. Harper pulled The Outlaw back into the ring and Jennings lowered the cage. Harper sent The Outlaw into the sides of the cage, each of the four so that all of the fans could enjoy the sight. He then went for a DROPKICK but Bass pushed him off and hit a quick ELBOW. Bass then used a FULL NELSON SLAM to further hurt Harper, and give him some time to shake off the earlier onslaught. Harper reversed an irish whip but got nailed by a CLOTHESLINE as Bass came off the ropes. The Outlaw began to scream at the crowd that Harper was his. The fans seemed to disagree, as did Harper himself who ducked the next punch and turned it into a RUSSIAN LEG SWEEP! Harper then started on the legs with a HAMSTRING PULL on the man from El Paso. Bass, who has bad knees anyway fought his way to the ropes and the referee called for the break. Harper backed off and let Bass get to his feet, a move which had Bass a bit confused. Harper just smiled at him and called him to come face off with him in the center of the ring. They interlaced fingers and the test of strength was on. Though Bass would be considered a favorite, Harper got out to the early lead, but The Outlaw slowly took over and had Harper almost down on one knee, when Harper kicked Bass in the leg and then hit him with a DRAGON SCREW LEG WHIP!!! Harper then went for a SPRINGBOARD MOONSAULT but got a GUT FULL OF KNEES from Bass. Sensing he needed a big move to turn the tide of the match Bass lifted Harper up over his head and hit him with the .. . . OUTLAW SLAM!!!!! Bass then made the cover for the ONE!!!!! TWO!!!!!!!!!!!!!! HARPER GOT A SHOULDER UP!!!!!!!!! But some serious damage had been done. Harper barely got his shoulder up and as Bass pulled him to his feet Harper had lost the fire he had for the previous parts of this match. Bass slapped him across the face before hitting him with a FISHERMANS NECKBREAKER!!!! Bass then paraded around the ring as the fans tossed trash and boos down on him. In the history of this company Bass and Kagedo may be the most hated by the fans. Bass then continued to punish Harper with some FOREARMS and STIFF UPPERCUTS before sending Harper into the steel cage posts! (BLOOD POP!!!) And Justin Harper was bloody and in trouble. Bass whipped him to the ropes and nailed him with that BIG BOOT TO THE FACE!!! (FINISHER IS COMING POP!!) The Outlaw then picked up Harper for the .. . . WIDOWMAKER!!! But Harper countered and hit Bass with a SHOULDERBREAKER!!!! AND THE PLACE WENT CRAZY!!!!! Bass got to his feet first, but Harper took him down with a LEG SWEEP and locked on the .. . . CEDAR RAPID DEATHLOCK!!!! Bass reached but was not close enough to get to the ropes! The Outlaw was grimacing in pain as Harper just turned up the pressure!! Bass lifted his hand and nearly tapped! But pulled up the hand before he hit the mat! For everyone in the arena time was going slow, the fans who waited to see Bass tap, for Harper who wanted him to tap and for Bass who was in so much pain. But The Outlaw is not a man who gives in easily and he found it deep within himself to get the ropes by his fingertips and it was enough for the referee to break the hold. Harper pulled him to the center of the ring by his legs but Bass kicked him off to the ropes, Harper then jumped over Bass and went to the opposite ropes and came off with the .. . . WIPEOUT!!!!!!!!!!! The tribute to Terry Jacobs, Harpers former tag team partner!!! Harper made the cover ONE!!!!!! TWO!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! KICKOUT!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! >From somewhere, somehow, Morris Bass managed to kickout of the Wipeout!! Harper was on his knees looking at the ref and you could see it on his face, he was at a loss as to how to put away The Outlaw. Harper then began the climb up the ropes. Looking to hit a top rope move, Bass followed him up. Both men were on the top rope in the corner when Bass got the upper hand and .. . . CHOKESLAM OFF THE TOP ROPE!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! Bass and Harper lay beside each other in the center of the ring, neither moving. Finally Bass lifted his arm and draped it over Harper ONE!!!!!!!! TWO!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! THREEEEEEEEEE NO!!!!!! SHOULER UP!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! HARPER GOT HIS SHOULDER UP!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! The Outlaw slowly rose to his feet and picked up Harper. Bass then sent Harper to the ropes and nailed him with that BIG BOOT!!! Bass then signaled for the WIDOWMAKER!!!! Bass had him but Harper reached down and grabbed the leg of Bass, turning it and causing Bass to fall backwards, and in the blink of an eye, Harper had him in the CALIFORNIA NIGHTMARE!!!!! Harper himself has called it a last ditch finisher!!! Bass was stuck, not close to the ropes. That's when Kagedo came down and began to climb the outside of the cage. Harper looked as if he was going to break the hold, but stopped when he heard the ROAR of the crowd as Adam Jennings came out and pulled Kagedo off the outside of the cage!!! Harper turned it up and Bass.. TAPPED OUT!!!! ((((( WINNER = Justin Harper ))))) Jennings sent Kagedo into the crowd and the Dragon took off into the night. Justin Harper not only won the match but has also earned himself a spot in the Rage In The Cage match at Phear Phantastic! After the ring cleared, Rod Beauty came out with mic in hand. .. .. .. ROD BEAUTY: I would like to say the next thirty days are going to be great! Without my meddling brother things are going to be done right! Now, I cannot take Adam Jennings out of the cage since he has already signed a contract, but I can name my own number one contender. I have spent a lot of time looking over tapes, won loss records as well as rankings in the respected magazines and there can only be one man who should be. . .
