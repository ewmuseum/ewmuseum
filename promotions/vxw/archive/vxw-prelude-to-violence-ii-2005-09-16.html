Prelude To Violence II

Sep 16, 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Balls To The Wall hits and the screen comes alive with past VXW action # Jason Storm hitting the Perfect Storm on Kenny Kidd # # The Maquilladora pinning Rod Beauty at Birthday Bash # # The SOD standing as one, in better times # # Team Brittania hitting the Hail Brittania # # Hot Property standing with their tag team titles # # The Biz and Valec Corso fighting in the steel cage # # Thunder Valenkov getting his hand raise as 24 Hour Champion # CUT to Dan The Man Aller) DTMA: Welcome to Prelude To Violence Two! Earlier tonight fans were treated to a big show over at Sams Town, the new home of the VXW! The night started off with tag team action, a rematch between the Dudes and the hot young team of Joey Young and Tom Rogers. .. .. .. =========================================== DUDES OF DESTRUCTION (vs) JOEY YOUNG & TOM 'TOP OF THE LINE' ROGERS =========================================== Young and Rogers immediately protested the arrival of referee John Lyon. Lyon was the ref in each win that the Dudes have had in the past few weeks and it seems like he might be on the Dudes payroll. Lyon called for the bell and the match started with Rogers in the ring with Lance 'The Big V' Voltar. They locked up and Voltar went behind Rogers and hit a GERMAN SUPLEX with bridge and Lyon made the SUPER FAST COUNT! ONE!! TWO!!! THREEEE!!! ((((( WINNERS = The Dudes of Destruction ))))) With Hamilton and Voltar walking up the aisle, the two youngsters made their way up the ramp and jumped the Dudes from behind! Young and Rogers brought both Hamilton and Voltar back to the ring and rolled him in the ring. They hit a DOUBLE DROPKICK to Voltar and then they picked up Hamilton and hit him with a DOUBLE SHOULDERBREAKER!!! Young made the cover and Rogers made the count as the crowd counted along! ONE!! TWO!!! THREE!!!! The rookies then rolled out of the ring to a big ovation from the crowd. On Prelude to Violence One, at the end of the night Lark Fenriz made his return to the rings of Vegas, and he did it in a big way. Lets see how his first official match back went. .. .. .. ======================= LARK FENRIZ (vs) CARL 'THE COBRA' SMITH ======================= The Cobra, another VXW Academy rookie, looked nervous as Fenriz made his way to the ring. Smith decided to try and jump Fenriz before the bell. That was a big mistake. Fenriz took the only real punch Smith got off and then plastered the kid. Fenriz beat Smith, there is no real better way to say it. It was brutal and at times hard to watch. Fenriz picked up the much smaller Smith and hit a POWERSLAM off the second rope but never even hinted at making the pinfall. The fans began a SAMMAEL! chant and the Wolf seemed ready to hit that move, but then he suddenly tossed Smith to the outside of the ring. Fenriz got on the outside with him and was going for a CLOTHESLINE when familiar music hit! Thunder Valenkov walked out onto the staging area and toward the ring! Fenriz let go of Smith and walked toward Valenkov. The two men were exchanging, pleasantries, meanwhile Smith was pulling himself to his feet. As the ref counted, Fenriz got nearer Valenkov, Smith rolled into the ring! When the bell rang Fenriz turned to see Carl 'The Cobra' Smith lying in the ring, with his hand raised. Security came out and got between Fenriz and the laughing Valenkov before they could exchange any blows. ((((( WINNER = Carl 'The Cobra' Smith ))))) But what a huge win for the rookie. This might move him to the head of the Academy class! .. .. .. .. .. ((((( COMMERCIALS ))))) .. .. .. .. .. (CUT back to DTMA) DTMA: Once upon a time in the VXW there was a group called the New Age Army. That group is now a shell of their former selves, and it looks like the last two remaining members of that group are set to finally go their separate ways. Tai Angela attacked Saku during a recent match and tonight was their big match. .. .. .. ============================ THE DARK MONSTER TAI ANGELA (vs) IYASU SAKU ============================ In a surprise this was a one sided match. Angela missed his first attack and Saku was all over for the rest of the match. The submission specialist went right after the shoulders of the big man and never let up. At the seven minute mark Saku signaled for the GOOD AND EVIL CROSSFACE, but that brought Blackbird up on the apron. Saku went over and knocked her back to the floor but Angela was waiting right behind, much the same way he hit the END OF THE LINE heart punch before. But Saku reversed it and put Angela in the GOOD AND EVIL CROSSFACE and Angela tapped out with little delay! ((((( WINNER = Iyasu Saku ))))) The fans seem to have taken a liking to Saku and gave him a nice ovation as he left ringside. Angela was tended to by medics as he and Blackbird walked to the back. That's when Rod Beauty came out to the ring, the 49% owner of the VXW clearly had some things on his mind! .. .. .. ================== ROD BEAUTY SPEAKS ================== (Rod steps into the ring and lifts the mic to his lips) ROD BEAUTY: Where should I begin? Should I complain that my older brother Rob is the one who decided to hire back Lark Fenriz and ruin my party last week? Should I talk about the fact that The Maquilladora is a cheater and should have lost at Birthday Bash? No. All that is water under the bridge. Lets move on shall we. Rob Beauty, my brother is the only reason I haven't simply FIRED The Rhombus outright! He feels loyal to the group of misfits. Sad man my older brother. But he feels more loyal to the bottom line! And with the help of out silent partner, the guy who owns that other 2% of the VXW, I am here to make a match for Prelude To Violence III! But first, could I have The Rhombus, all of them, come to the ring. I know you guys are here tonight. Maq, Sonic, CAPSMAN, EVIL CAPSMAN! Come on down to the ring. (The Maquilladora by Radiohead hits and the Rhombus comes to the ring. Everyone but CAPSMAN gets in the ring, CAPS is on the outside running around and goofing off with the fans) ROD BEAUTY: Thank you gentlemen. I am so happy about the news I have for you guys. You see at Prelude To Violence III, we are going to have a RHOMBUS RUMBLE!! That's right! All four of you in a battle royal format and the winner gets a shot at Thunder Valenkov at Vegas Violence! (They all nod, sounds good) But the losers, well the losers will be fired! (HUUUGE BOOS) Hey, the VXW needs to trim the roster a bit so that is the plan. And one last thing boys, whoever wins has to win the 24 Hour in order to keep their job! Lose to Valenkov and LOSE YOUR JOB! (fade back to DTMA) .. .. .. DTMA: Well Rod Beauty has set his sights on the Rhombus and he has what he wants, three of the four members of the Rhombus will lose their jobs on the next show, and then whoever wins will have to defeat Thunder Valenkov to keep his job. In many other companies, they are slim in the tag team division or its non-existent, but in the VXW we have a pretty nice tag team division. Two of the top teams in the division faced off, with interesting results. .. .. .. =============== TEAM BRITTANIA (vs) PURGATORY =============== Things kicked off with a four man brawl, with Synn and PsyKlone getting the better of the Brits. Finally things settled down and PsyKlone and Justin Davidson were the legal men in the ring. PsyKlone used his size and power advantage to take charge of the match. PsyKlone hit Justin with a VERTICAL SUPLEX and tagged in to Synn who came in and drove a SERIES of ELBOWS into the sternum of the fallen Brit. Then made the cover... ONE!! TWO!!!! BUT JUSTIN GOT HIS FOOT ON THE ROPES!! Synn pulled him to the center of the ring, where Justin rolled under a punch and tagged in Simon. Simon hit the ring and took Synn off his feet with a variation of a ROARING ELBOW and then DROPKICKED PsyKlone, who tried to hold on the rope to keep from falling to the floor. But Simon used the ropes and hit him with an ELBOW that sent the big man crashing to the floor. Simon turned and ran into a SPINEBUSTER from Synn! And once again the match was back in the hands of Purgatory. Synn let out a huge scream and then scooped up Simon and hit the SNAKES EYES, followed by a sharp stiff NECKBREAKER, then a tag to the big man. PsyKlone hit a nice LEGDROP and made the cover.. ONE!!! TWO!!!!! SAVE BY JUSTIN!!! And this brought all four men into the battle. An old fashioned donny brook followed. Team Brittania knocked PsyKlone out of the ring and they were set to hit Synn with the HAIL BRITANIA when a hooded man walked out onto the stage. PsyKlone immediately stood up and faced the man, as if under his control. Justin and Simon then hit Synn with the HAIL BRITTANIA!! But they never got a chance at a pinfall attempt as PsyKlone came into the ring with a chair. The ref stopped PsyKlone and an angry Justin leaped up from his pinfall attempt. Simon rolled out of the ring and made his way up the ramp as the hooded mystery man walked off. Then from out of the crowd came the VXW tag team champions, Hotstuff Stevie Knight and All That Austin Lee, and they were swinging gold. They double hit PsyKlone, then nailed Justin Davidson. Synn, still woozy from the Hail Brittania made his way to his feet only to go back to the mat after a double title belt shot. Simon came back to the ring and ducked the first try by Hot Property and for a moment looked like he had a chance, but they got him on the second try. Knight and Lee then stood over the four men with their titles over their heads. That is until the four men began to stir. Then HP high tailed it outta there! The match was declared a no contest by the referee. .. .. .. .. .. ((((( COMMERCIALS ))))) .. .. .. .. .. (We cut backstage and find the O'Malley brothers preparing for their matchup against Bass and Kagedo, when a quick knock at their door grabs the attention of Marcus. He motions to Anthony to see who it is as he continues to lace up his boots. A large shadow appears over him, and as he looks up he sees none other than the man known to millions simply as 'The Hype') AJ: Well, Marcus. Tonight's the night. You sure you and Anthony are ready for this? MO: Ready? I'm a-boot as ready as tha virg'n girl at prom night. (Marcus tilts his head over towards Anthony.) MO: Anth'ny, on tha oth'r hand, is anoth'r story. He's a bundl' o' nerves right now. Still tryin' ta figure out why K'gedo picked him at Vegas Violence. Kinda alot on tha young'n's mind ye know. (Adam nods and follows Marcus' glance at the younger O'Malley who is engrossed in a PS2 game at the present) AJ: We'd all like to know why, but I'm damn sure not gonna be the one to try and figure out what that psychopath has in mind. You just keep him focussed. SoD style, we've got one another's backs. Harper has his deal with Bass, you and Anthony have all those screwy tag teams to wade through, Kev's got that sick ass bald bastard to worry about, and I have to waste my time dealing with a wet fart of a human being named Caldwell when I should be working on taking back my 24-Hour championship from Valenkov. (Marcus chuckles and then hears a second voice coming from around the corner where Anthony and his game console are. A female voice. With a look of anticipation, Marcus walks around the corner to see....) MO: Now what tha bloody hell are you doin' here? (There she stands, none other than Adam Jennings's baby sister, and former girlfriend of Marcus, Jessica Jennings. Decked out in a standard SoD T-Shirt and blue jeans, she walks over next to her older brother. Remembering the stiff left hook he recieved the last time he saw her, Marcus stands in somewhat of a defensive position, only to have Adam motion for Marcus to take it easy.) MO: Ad'm, ye tell me ta keep mine an' me broth'r focused. An' ye got ta bring her along? Are ye crazy man? (Jessica slightly steps forward in front of her brother.) JJ: Marcus, I can't believe how I acted when you came back. You gave me an honest explanation and I was blinded by my own illusions on what I thought was the truth that I refused to listen. I've been such a fool and I now you really needed me with all that's been going on with McQueen's death and I feel bad for not being there for you. If it's alright with you, I'd like to start over and pick up where we left off. (Marcus looks around her right shoulder to see Jennings grinning from ear to ear as he slightly nods his head. Marcus can't help but crack a smile on his face.) MO: You sneaky sumbitch!!! (With the smile still on his face, he turns his attention back to his old flame.) AJ: Just keeping the team intact, Marcus. Say hello to the new valet for the Society. MO: Valet, eh? Reckons it beats Bubba anyday. (Marcus turns and looks back at Adam.) MO: Alright, I'm willin' ta forgive an' start ov'r myself. But I don't want her at ringside t'night. It's gonna get ugly out there an' I don't want her in harm's way. Is that a deal Ad'm? (Adam looks to his baby sister who is giving both he and Marcus an incredulous look of defiance. Without stirring, he nods) AJ: He's right, sis. You don't need to be mixing up with assjockeys like those two. Not yet at least. (Jessica rolls her eyes as only little sisters can around their big brothers.) JJ: Alright, fine. I guess I'll stay in here then. Don't know what the hell I'm gonna do to cure my boredom. MO: Simpl'. (Marcus hands her the TV remote.) MO: Ord'r porn. (Adam gives Marcus one of those 'I didn't near to hear that' looks as Jessica blushes and snickers quietly.) .. .. .. (CUT back to DTMA) DTMA: So the Society of Doom has a new valet, Jessica Jennings. In my opinion, she is just what the VXW needs, someone who is young and hot. Some would say that The Biz is young and hot too. One of the hottest shooting stars in the wrestling business today. Mike Bisignano came to the VXW, rose right up to challenge for and eventually win the TV Title. He gave a non title shot to the top rising star in the VXW Academy, Javier King. .. .. .. ========================== Non-Title Match MIKE 'THE BIZ' BISIGNANO (vs) JAVIER KING =========================== King was nervous, but excited. Both are about the same size to fans were looking for an exciting high flying match. Which we got, for about two mintues. Both men were chaining along until King missed a MOONSAULT and The Biz grabbed him and hit a SAMOAN DROP! >From there Bisignano picked apart the youngster with ease, the ease of a champion. At near the six minute mark The Biz hit a FORWARD FLIPPING PILEDRIVER that brought everyone out of their seats! King was clearly done. But The Biz made the slow deliberate climb to the top rope for THE DELMAR DIVE!!! He made the academic cover for the ONE!! TWO!!!! THREEEEE!!!! ((((( WINNER = Mike 'The Biz' Bisignano ))))) But The Biz called for the mic after the match. Bisignano, first gave a short speech about how he is the greatest TV Champion in the history of the VXW, but then he moved on to the masked man who has been attacking him. He then called The Outlaw Morris Bass to the ring. The Biz jumped Bass last week when The Outlaw was confronted by WILD President Sandsbury. The Biz told Bass he knew it was him, jealousy etc... but then from out of the crowd came the masked man. He slid in the ring behind The Biz and took him out with a violent CHAIR SHOT!! The masked man jumped back into the crowd and rushed off and Bass just laughed at The Biz. The final match of the night was filled with emotion. Since the death of Mark McQueen the matches between the Society of Doom have been hard to watch. .. .. .. ================================ THE O'MALLEYS (vs) THE OUTLAW MORRIS BASS & KAGEDO ================================ The match started with Marcus and Kagedo, it was obvious that Marcus was looking to protect his younger brother. Kagedo picked Anthony for his match at Vegas Violence and the question that everyone is asking is Why? Why pick Anthony O'Malley and not a title shot? Or a match with Kevin Simmons? Kagedo kept clear of Marcus and continued to point to Anthony on the ring apron, wanting him in the match. The younger O'Malley wanted in the match as well and finally Marcus relented and tagged in Anthony. Anthony was on a mission to show Kagedo he was not going to be a pushover and he took the fight right to Kagedo. Anthony started off with some KNIFE EDGE CHOPS and some stiff right hands but he changed it up and began to use his feet. He caught Kagedo coming out of the corner with a DOUBLE LEG LARIAT, and followed that up with a SPINNING HEEL KICK and went for the cover. But it was to soon, Kagedo kicked out quickly. Kags then fought him off long enough to make the tag to The Outlaw. Bass ducked Anthony and then took him down to the mat and used some KNEE STRIKES to soften up Marcus' brother. Bass got the kid on his feet and hit him with a BELLY TO BELLY suplex and then dropped an ELBOW on his chest and made a cover. ONE!!! TWO!!!!! But the kid kicked out! Bass pulled him over and tagged in Kagedo. Bass held him open, but Anthony got free as Kags was coming off the ropes. Kags stopped just short of hitting Bass and Anthony got over and tagged in Marcus! Marcus hit both Bass and Kagedo with a CLOTHESLINE!! He tossed Bass out of the ring and went to work on the legal man Kagedo. Marcus was on fire, using mostly his fists, but this was his chance to exact some revenge. The referee tried to break it up but Marcus would just go back to the closed fists. Marcus then put Kagedo in a CHOKE HOLD and refused to break it up! Bass started to get in the ring to save his partner when the crowd began to stir. Standing on the VXW stage was WILD TV Champion, Omega!! The place exploded! Bass turned away from Kagedo and O'Malley and made his way up the ramp to Omega. Just as Bass and Omega began to exchange blows Justin Harper came out from behind the curtains and joined in! Bass, Omega and Harper were throwing punches as Kagedo was still being choked out by Marcus. The ref, watching the donny brook on the stage like the rest of us, finally got Marcus to break the hold. Lets switch over and see how this match finally ended! .. .. .. MD: As Lester Bash would've said over across the street, Good Gravy!! Look at the carnage!! CK: Oh great! You're using BWWA catchphrases on a VXW show. Leslie Stanton will sue your ass for that. MD: We've got action all over the arena!! We've got Morris Bass brawling not only with Justin Harper but WILD's TV Champion, Omega, has showed up to get himself a piece of The Outlaw....and they're all in the aisle!! CK: Yeah, but got a match still taking place in the ring still. Focus Mikey, focus!!! (Kagedo and Marcus O'Malley are exchanging fists right in the middle of the ring. Marcus nails Kagedo with a stiff forearm across the right side of Kagedo's face.) MD: Marcus irish whips Kagedo.....Clothesline!! (Kagedo ducks the clothesline.) MD: NO!!! HE MISSED!!! (Kagedo sneaks up behind Marcus and grabs him in a Full Nelson.) CK: DRAGON SUPL--- (Anthony O'Malley sneaks up behind Kagedo on the other side of the ring apron and leaps up on the top rope.) MD: Springboard Dropkick!!!! Springboard Dropkick to the back of Kagedo's head!!! And Marcus's little brother broke up the Dragon Suplex!!! (MAMMOTH FACE POP) (Both Marcus and Anthony stand over Kagedo, as Marcus shouts instructions to his little brother and points to a turnbuckle. As Anthony positions himself upon the turnbuckle, Marcus points his index finger through the crowd and then looks up in the air and points his index finger in the air. He then suddenly rips off his trademark O'Malley Brothers T-Shirt to reveal a sleeveless Black T-Shirt with McQueen's picture on the front of it and 'One Tough Bastard' written in blood on the back.) (MAMMOTH FACE POP x 10) CK: What are they planning?? (Marcus proceeds to pick up the crumpled heap of Kagedo off the mat and slings him against the ropes.) CK: DON'T TELL ME!!!! (Kagedo bounces back, as Marcus picks him up in a Flapjack.....) MD: IT IS!!!! (Anthony leaps off the top rope with a Leg Drop across the back of Kagedo's head, as Marcus falls backwards.) MD: OH MY GOD!!!! IRISH THUNDER FROM DOWN UNDER!!!! (MAMMOTH FACE POP x 100) MD: THE PLACE IS GOING CRAZY!!! AND ANTHONY HAS KAGEDO PINNED!!!! 1...... 2...... 3....... (Winners: The O'Malley Brothers) CK: I CAN'T BELIEVE WHAT I JUST WITNESSED!!!! THE O'MALLEY BROTHERS USED THE OLD ALCOHOLICS ANONYMOUS FINISHER ON KAGEDO!!! MD: AND LOOK AT LITTLE ANTHONY O'MALLEY TALKING TRASH TO KAGEDO!!! YOU CAN'T SAY HE'S NOT READY FOR HIS MATCH WITH KAGEDO AT VEGAS VIOLENCE!!! CK: What a night!! And the brawl in the aisle is still going!!! MD: Folks! We're out of time!! What a way to close out the show with bang!!! (The camera switches fro security separating Bass, Omega and Harper and then focuses is on The O'Malley Brothers hugging each other in the ring as the scene slowly fades to black.) © 2005 Beauty Productions
