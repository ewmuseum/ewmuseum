Post Vegas Violence V Update

Oct 11, 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
VXW Update ============ Vegas Violence Results Team Essington defeated The Dudes, Joey Young & Tom Rogers Iysau Saku defeated Matt Payne Lark Fenriz defeated Kenny Kidd Kagedo defeated Anthony O'Malley Justin Harper defeated The Outlaw Morris Bass Adam Jennings defeated Robbie Caldwell Team Brittania defeated Hot Property, Purgatory by DQ Mike Bisignano defeated The Masked Invader Thunder Valenkov defeated The Maquilladora For the second straight year no titles changed hands at Vegas Violence. Though VXW fans were sorry to see the final appearance of The Maquilladora. Fans should feel better that Anthony O'Malley is improving. Also, Blake Hamilton and Lance Voltar have been released from their VXW contracts. Hamilton and Voltar are looking to join the Pro Surfers Tour in Australia. UPCOMING EVENTS ================== Fear Is Coming I October 19, 2005 PURGATORY (vs) JOEY YOUNG & TOM 'TOP OF THE LINE' ROGERS Synn and PsyKlone step into the ring with the top team in the VXW academy. Ok, the only team. ROMAN ANDERSON (vs) CARL 'THE COBRA' SMITH Smith takes on the man we used to know as The Masked Invader. Anderson is the man who did the dirty work for Valec Corso, attacking The Biz over the past few months. THE RODEO BOYS (vs) TEAM ESSINGTON Kidd and Bones are back! The three time former tag team champions will see if they still have the magic as they face the undefeated team of El Satanico Loco and Buck Wild. JUSTIN HARPER (vs) IYASU SAKU Two great submission artists tangle in this match. Harper might be looking forward to his match on Halloween, and Saku might use that to continue his winning streak. LARK FENRIZ (vs) MR ELECTRICITY ROBBIE CALDWELL Caldwell looks to rebound from his loss to Jennings while Fenriz will be looking to continue to make his case for a title shot. THUNDER VALENKOV, VALEC CORSO, HOTSTUFF STEVIE KNIGHT (vs) ADAM 'THE HYPE' JENNINGS, MIKE 'THE BIZ' BISIGNANO, MARCUS O'MALLEY The night ends with a big six man tag team match. Can Corso work with the Wheeling protegee's and how will The Biz fit in with the SoD members? Also, VXW leadership will announce the #1 Contender to the 24 Hour Title. That man will automatically be awarded a place inside the Rage In The Cage match at Phear Phantastic on November 22, 2005! ========== Fear Is Coming II Hells Night! October 31, 2005 Signed so far JUSTIN HARPER (vs) THE OUTLAW MORRIS BASS This match will be a rematch of the cage match from Vegas Violence. It will also be held inside a steel cage and winner will qualify for the Rage In The Cage match! ========== Fear Is Coming III November 9, 2005 ========== Fear Is Coming IV November 16, 2005 ========== Phear Phantastic RITC 2005 November 22, 2005 Main Event! Rage In The Cage! All title holders with challengers in a 2 story steel cage! Pin a champion and take his title! Plus other matches as well!
