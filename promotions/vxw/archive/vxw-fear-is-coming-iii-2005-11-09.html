The fans showed their love as Jennings played to them, but behind him he didn't see Ripper shrug off the DDT and get straight back up! Jessica tried to warn him, but he turned around into a BIG BOOT to the face! Ripper tagged in Thunder Valenkov and they showed some teamwork, hitting a big DOUBLE SHOULDER TACKLE! Valenkov made the cover ONE!! TWO!!! BUT JENNINGS KICKED OUT!! That set the pace for the next few minutes of action, Chris Wheeling pulling the strings as his two monsters began to take apart the outnumbered former champ. Valenkov wore him down with some STIFF UNDERHOOK KNEE STRIKES and then a STALLING UNDERHOOK SUPLEX! Ripper soon added a couple of RIB BREAKERS of his own, and then tried to CHOKE OUT Jennings again, this time with his huge gloved hands! Ripper was in no hurry to pin Adam Jennings, but Valenkov had him in trouble again after another double team by the two giants, this time a HUGE DOUBLE FLAPJACK that left The Hype free-falling from over TEN FEET UP! Valenkov made the cover ONE!!! TWO!!!!!!! BUT JENNINGS KICKED OUT AGAIN!!!!!!! Valenkov was looking to put Jennings away, setting him up for his SOVIET JUDO SUPLEX, but Chris Wheeling was having too much fun seeing his two monsters dismantle Valenkov's top challenger, and he demanded another tag! Valenkov did as he was told, and he and Jack Ripper combined to send Jennings HARD into the turnbuckle. But then the double team broke down when Valenkov tried to send Ripper charging in with an IRISH WHIP on his own partner... ....but Jennings moved, and Ripper ran headlong into the turnbuckle! Jennings tried to take advantage of the confusion, and went for a HYPER-DRIVE on Valenkov! But Valenkov shoved him away, and Jack Ripper knocked him down with a BIG FOREARM BLOW! Ripper tagged Valenkov back in, and the champ went for a RUSSIAN SICKLE off the ropes! But Jennings ducked the big LARIAT and hit a SPRINGBOARD REVERSE ELBOW! He was looking for a RUNNING HIGH KNEE... .... but Chris Wheeling TRIPPED HIM behind the referee's back! The crowd wasn't happy and Jessica was complaining to the official too, especially when Valenkov hit a BIG LEGDROP across the back of Adam Jennings' neck! Valenkov covered again ONE!!! TWO!!!!!! BUT JENNINGS GOT A SHOULDER UP!!!!!! Valenkov tagged Ripper in again, and Jack shouted that it was about to be over! He picked The Hype up in a MILITARY PRESS SLAM... and then dropped him THROAT-FIRST across the top rope with a MODIFIED HOTSHOT! And then Valenkov followed up with the RUSSIAN SICKLE! Jennings had to be out cold! Ripper made a cover ONE!!! TWO!!!!!!!! BUT JESSICA JENNINGS MADE THE SAVE!?!?! She kicked Jack Ripper in the side of the head... and then ran for her life as Jack went after her! Jack chased her around the ring, but Jessica was too fast... until Chris Wheeling blocked her path! Jessica was in trouble, but then Valenkov called Ripper back to the ring, and hit the SOVIET JUDO SUPLEX on Jennings! Ripper was the legal man, and Valenkov pointed to Jennings. Jack made the cover ONE!!! TWO!!!!!!!! THREE-NOOOOOOOOO!!!!!!!! JENNINGS KICKED OUT!!!!!! Jessica was safe for now and cheering her brother on, but Jack was ready to finish Jennings off, and called for the MOTHER OF ALL BOMBS!! But again Chris Wheeling thought he had a better idea, and shouted for Jack Ripper to make the tag. Jack wasn't happy, but he made the tag in the end, and under Wheeling's orders they lifted Jennings up in a DOUBLE HANGING SUPLEX, and they were looking for a devastating DOUBLE TEAM MOTHER OF ALL BOMBS!!!!!!! But Jennings reversed it into a DESPERATION DOUBLE DDT!!!!!! All three men were down, and the fans were trying to get The Hype back into it! And somehow he got back to his feet, and was waiting for Jack and Valenkov. They tried a DOUBLE CLOTHESLINE but Jennings ducked! But then Chris Wheeling TRIPPED HIM AGAIN!! The crowd was all over him, and this time Jessica Jennings had had enough, and she went after Wheeling herself! He laughed her off... so she SLAPPED HIM IN THE FACE and knocked him down! And then she jumped on him and started beating him down! Valenkov and Ripper were STOMPING AWAY on Adam Jennings in the ring, while outside suddenly STEVIE KNIGHT appeared and dragged Jessica off of Chris Wheeling! Knight pinned Jessica's arms so Wheeling could wind up for a BIG SLAP of his own... .... but here came MARCUS O'MALLEY to save his girlfriend!! He started taking it to Stevie Knight, but then AUSTIN LEE arrived to back up his partner, and they beat down Marcus! And in the ring it looked to be the end for Adam Jennings, when Valenkov and Ripper set him up for a DOUBLE CHOKESLAM! But Jessica Jennings JUMPED ON VALENKOV'S BACK! Valenkov threw Jessica down to the mat and stood over her, and Wheeling shouted for him to THUNDER BOMB her! Outside the ring, Marcus was fighting back, and SLAMMED STEVIE KNIGHT'S FACE INTO THE STEEL BARRIER! Then Marcus and Lee started brawling all over ringside! Chris Wheeling was still shouting at Valenkov, who backed away from Jessica! Wheeling was mad, but Jack Ripper grabbed her instead! He had her by the hair, and lifted her up for the MOTHER OF ALL BOMBS!!!! But Jennings saved his sister with a SPEAR!! And he opened up on Jack Ripper with RIGHT HAND after RIGHT HAND! The big man was being rocked, and Jennings hit a CLOTHESLINE! But Ripper was still up... .... until Jennings CLOTHESLINED HIM OVER THE TOP ROPE!!! Even then, Ripper still landed on his feet, but Jennings was on fire and shouted for Jack to come get some more! But then he turned around... .... into the THUNDER BOMB from Valenkov!!!!!!! Jennings didn't see it coming, and Valenkov made the cover ONE!!!! TWO!!!!!!!! THREE!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!
