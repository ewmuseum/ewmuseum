Xtreme Prejudice VII

Aug 4, 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Iyasu Saku is standing outside Sams Town) SAKU: It shall be in here tonight that my quest to win the 24 Hour Title begins. Tonight the road to the top starts in a match with Sonicman. We have met many times old friend, and like so many times before this should be one of the best battles of the night. But Saku needs to become champion. I need to win the title. So you shall not stop me. Tonight, it begins. (fade to the parking lot at Sam's Town) (Mark McQueen is signing some autographs for some fans when The Outlaw Morris Bass and Kagedo rush in and break it all up. They viciously attack McQueen, beating him to the ground. Bass then picks him and CHOKESLAMS him on the concrete of the parking lot. The fans are screaming for security. The guards rush over but no in time to stop Kagedo from hitting the DRAGON SUPLEX onto the hood of a car.) .. .. .. (Master of Puppets hits and we see scenes from recent VXW events) = The Dudes of Destruction in the ring with The Rhombus = = Kenny Kidd getting drilled by Jason Storm = = Valec Corso and Mike 'The Biz' Bisignano battling in the steel cage = = Kevin Simmons talking at his retirement = = A close up of Morris Bass and Kagedo = = Shots of the Triple Threat 24 Hour Title, Caldwell slamming Adam Jennings = = Thunder Valenkov pinning Caldwell after the Thunder Bomb = = Valenkov, standing with Hot Property, all three men holding up their title belts as Chris Wheeling looks on = (CUT to JD Southerns, that's right folks JD Southerns sitting at the VXW desk) SOUTHERNS: Welcome to Xtreme Prejudice Seven! That address what most of you are thinking, where is Dan The Man? Rob and Rod Beauty, owners of the VXW, have decided to take over as commissioners of the VXW and Dan has been moved back to his spot at ringside calling the action along with Johnny 'Hands Of' Stone. I am happy to be back. We saw seven big matches tonight, so lets get right to it and see how the night played out. .. .. .. ================================= THE SURFER BLAKE HAMILTON (vs) TODD YOUNG (VXW academy student) ================================= The ref for this match was the same ref who helped Hamilton and partner Voltar to a win last week. Referee John Lyon made his way to the ring. The two men locked up, Hamilton placed Young in a side headlock and Lyon called for the bell! No one had the foggiest idea what the hell was going on, until the announcement was made that Hamilton won the match as the result of a submission!?!?!?! ((((( WINNER = The Surfer Blake Hamilton ))))) Voltar then hit the ring and he and Hamilton began a brutal assault on Young. Ending with DOUBLE LEGDROPS by the Dudes. Sickening! The Dudes were then leaving the ring area when Justin and Simon Davidson, aka Team Brittania came out early for their scheduled match. The four men exchanged words until separated by VXW offcials. Team Brittania moved to their match. .. .. .. ================ TEAM BRITTANIA (vs) THE WIZARDS ================ Justin and Simon disposed of the two men dressed like rejects from a Harry Potter movie in only two minutes. It was HAIL BRITTANIA for the pinfall at 1:56. ((((( WINNERS = Team Brittania ))))) They took some time to head over to section 113, home of the rowdy TB fans. The loud gong sounded that is was time for a long time VXW star to return to the ring, Iyasu Saku made the return walk to the ring. But his opponent was revved up for this one on one match! .. .. .. =========== IYASU SAKU (vs) SONICMAN =========== Sonicman came out and hit Saku with a FLURRY OF PUNCHES, ending with a ENZIGUIRI!!! Sonic, still showing some signs of rust from his time off, was out to prove something to Saku and himself. Sonicman kept the action fast, sending Saku into the corners, MONEKY FLIPPING HIM OUT and then hitting him with some stiff FOREARMS!! Saku looked more than the man with ring rust than did Sonicman. Sonicma nailed a RUNNING BULLDOG and climbed to the top rope, leaping off and hitting a SOMERSAULT SPLASH that brought crowd to its feet. But savvy ring vet Saku placed his foot on the ropes and saved himself from the three count. Sonicman pulled him up and went for his trademark DDT, THE CLOSE SHAVE!!! Saku reversed it and picked up Sonicman and drove him into the corner! Saku used some KNIFE EDGE CHOPS to soften up the excited Sonicman, and hit him with a BRAINBUSTER out of the corner!!! Saku then sent Sonicman to the ropes and hit him with a ROARING ELBOW that seemed to take off the head of Sonicman!!! Saku made the cover, and got the ONE!! TWO!!! THREEE!!!!! ((((( WINNER = Iyasu Saku ))))) When we come back, Austin Lee and PsyKlone, Kenny Kidd in the ring with VXW legend The Maquilladora! Stay tuned! .. .. .. .. .. ((((( COMMERCIALS ))))) .. .. .. .. .. [We cut backstage to pre-recorded footage of the ever-lean Darlene Orion alongside none other than one half of the VXW tag team champions, the man who is All That and so much more -- Austin Lee! Lee, as is typical for him, is dressed in a black "You ain't All That" t-shirt and faded jeans, and leans casually against the wall, green-tinted shades tucked into the neck of his shirt and familiar smirk firmly in place. Oh, and of course let's not forget the nice shiny platinum tag team title belt draped over one shoulder. Take it away, Darlene!] LDO: Austin...later tonight we'll see you step in the ring with the monster known as Psyklone, one half of one of the three teams that are currently staking their claim for a shot at your VXW Tag Team titles. Now considering that Psyklone is... LEE: Yeah, alright...let's remember who's interview this is, okay? Save that pretty little mouth for things it's better suited to, and leave the talking to me. [Darlene, unsurprisingly, shoots Lee an evil EVIL glare, which doesn't exactly get any better as he takes the microphone from her grasp.] LEE: We all know what you're gonna say anyways -- Psyklone, he's so big. He's so tough. He's a MONSTER, Darlene. A psychopath, a killer. A man without remorse, and all those other bull*BLEEP* "buzzwords" the marketing guys throw around to make us think we care. Well take look at me, Darlene -- do I LOOK like I care? Do I look like I'm SCARED? Do I look like I'm breaking a sweat about this match tonight? [He smirks.] LEE: Exactly. And I'll tell ya why -- it's 'cause I've been there, Darlene. I've done that, slain the giant, got the t-shirt, AND the shiny damn belt to prove it. Go ask Simon Davidson, when I pinned his jacked-up ass 1-2-3 in the middle of the ring. And after tonight, you can go ask Psyklone too. Because there's a saying, Darlene -- the bigger you are, the dumber you look. And Psy, I'll tell ya right now, man -- you're gonna look pretty *BLEEP*in' dumb when I hit you with the "It's All Over Now!", and I leave you rocking in the corner, muttering over and over to yourself... "I'm supposed to be a *BLEEP*ing monster, but now I know..." "I ain't All That." [Fade out to that signature smirk.] .. .. .. (CUT to JD at the desk) SOUTHERNS: For PsyKlone this match could get he and partner Synn off probation. For Lee this was a chance to show the world he is not just a tag team wrestler. .. .. .. ==================== PSYKLONE (vs) ALL THAT AUSTIN LEE ==================== Lee tried to get the quick jump by attacking PsyKlone from behind before the bell. Lee used his fists and some well placed kicks to the gut to get PsyKlone in some trouble. Lee came off the ropes for a RUNNING BULLDOG, but the big man turned on him and hit a SIDEWALK SLAM. PsyKlone then used sheer power to work over All That as the match moved on at the pace of a tank. PsyKlone tossed Lee out of the ring and then followed him out, but Lee used this as a chance to get back into the fracas and ducked a PsyKlone CLOTHESLINE, then hit the big man with a SAVATE KICK!!! Lee then used the ringpost to soften up the noggin of PsyKlone. Lee continued this tactic, rolling into the ring to break the count and then heading right back out to the floor and sending PsyKlone into the ring post, ring steps and once into the announce table. Austin Lee had a big smile on his face and rolled his opponent into the ring and climbed in himself and made the cover. But PsyKlone kicked out! Lee rolled the big man back to the floor and climbed out on the apron. Lee then went for a SOMERSAULT PLANCHA!!!!!! But PsyKlone caught him. Lee desperately punched the big man, knowing he was about to get POWERBOMBED!!! Success came and Lee was able to turn it into a HURRICANRANA!!! All That then climbed back onto the apron and tried again, a CROSS BODY!!! Only to be caught again!!! This time PsyKlone rammed Lee into the ring post, nearly snapping the tag team champion in half!!! PsyKlone then picked up Lee and SLAMMED him to the floor!!!! But the ref was counting away!!! As PsyKlone was pulling Lee to his feet, Lee hit him with a BLATANT LOW BLOW, doubling over the big man. Lee rolled in the ring and listened as the referee counted out PsyKlone! ((((( WINNER = All That Austin Lee ))))) So Purgatory is still on probation. On the last Xtreme Prejudice, Kenny Kidd lost to Jason Storm and lost his knickname, well all three knicknames. Kidd came to ring without any music, without any fanfare at all. The man seemed lost. Lets take a look at how his match went. .. .. .. ================= THE MAQUILLADORA (vs) KENNY KIDD ================= As the bell rang, Kidd rolled out of the ring. He threw up his hands and made his way to the back. The ref counted him out and this match was over. ((((( WINNER = The Maquilladora ))))) But as Maq was pumping up the fans, 49% owner of the VXW Rod Beauty walked out onto the staging area. (Rod Beauty, the brother who turned on Ric and Rob and teamed with Kenny Kidd a few weeks back, walks out on the stage) ROD BEAUTY: Listen to you idiots, cheering this relic. (CHEAP HEAT) It is time to purge this company of the likes of guys like this. I made sure we didn't renew Classy Joey Boys contract! I made sure that Randall Cranes appearance last week was one time only! But you. (points to The Maquilladora) you signed a long term deal right before Storm left. You are the past Maq, and the future has no place for you. No one wants to see you anymore. It was funny back in the day, but now, now you are a shadow of the man who once walked the halls of the Vegas. You and the whole Rhombus, pathetic! But like I said, I have a problem. The Rhombus is under contract. Well Mr. Maquilladora have I got a deal for you. At Birthday Bash, you go one on one with ME! Lose and you and the whole Rhombus crew are DONE! FIRED! Win. HA! You're not going to win! But if you do, hell, I'll give everyone in the Rhombus a title shot on the first show after Birthday Bash! I would give you some time to think about it, but I am the boss. So its gonna happen! (Rod walks off with a smile on his face) .. .. .. (CUT back to Jd Southerns) SOUTHERNS: So Rod Beauty, the youngest of the Beauty Boys wants to take on Maq at Birthday Bash, with Maqs job on the line. When we come back, the number one contenders match and the split of the SOD comes to a head. .. .. .. .. .. ((((( COMMERCIALS ))))) .. .. .. .. .. (CUT back to JD) SOUTHERNS: All one needs to say is Jennings versus Caldwell. This time, winner gets a shot at Valenkov at Birthday Bash. .. .. .. =============================== ADAM 'THE HYPE' JENNINGS (vs) MR ELECTRICITY ROBBIE CALDWELL =============================== An angry Jennings took to the fight as soon as the bell sounded. He drove a stunned Caldwell back into the corner and drove fist after fist into his head. Jennings used a RUNNING POWERSLAM to bring him out of the corner and went for the quick fall. But Caldwell managed to kick out and then get the hell out of the ring to try and compose himself. But The Hype followed him and sent him into the ring steps before rolling Caldwell back in the ring. Mr Electricity was favoring his left shoulder after the collision with the ring steps. Jennings went right to work on it with a series of DEEP ARM DRAGS that ended in an ARMBAR. Jennings placed Caldwell on the mat and used some KNEES to further soften up Robbie. Caldwell made his way to his feet, and sent Jennings to the ropes with an IRISH WHIP, but Jennings reversed it and hit a DROPKICK on Caldwell. As Robbie stumbled backwards he ended up tied up in the ropes!!! Jennings came over and pulled on the ropes near that injured shoulder, but the ref forced him to stop that. Then Jennings punched Caldwell in the head while the crowd counted to TEN! The ref finally got Caldwell out of the ropes and Jennings hit a SHOULDERBREAKER on him and followed up with a SECOND ROPE FIST DROP!!! But the cover only got him a 2 count! The Hype then signaled for the HYPER DRIVER, but as he turned Caldwell pushed him off and Jennings and the referee butted heads!! That brought Kenny Kidd, carrying his cowbell, to the ring!!! Jennings, turned and ***CLANG*** got nailed with the cowbell!!! Kenny Kidd then placed Jennings on the top rope, helped Caldwell to his feet, and then woke the ref. While he was waking the ref, Caldwell was hitting the SHOCK AND HORROR!!!!! He made the cover and the groggy ref made the count ONE!!! TWO!!!! SHOULDER UP!!!!! The crowd went crazy!!!!! Kidd was stunned, and so was Caldwell. Mr. Electricity went nearly insane pounding on Jennings. Caldwell pulled Jennings to his feet and nailed him with a DDT!!! Kidd got up on the ring apron and began to take off the turnbuckle cover in the opposite corner, as he and Caldwell began talking to each other. The ref then moved to the corner, pushed away Kidd and went to fixing the turnbuckle. The ref now had his back to the action in the ring!!! Kidd then climbed in the ring with a chair, he slammed it on the mat, Caldwell fell down and Kidd tossed the chair to Jennings!! The ref, hearing the chairshot turned to see Caldwell on the mat and Jennings holding a chair and called for the bell!!! ((((( WINNER = Mr. Electricity Robbie Caldwell ))))) Caldwell rolled out of the ring, holding his head as if hit by the chair and was helped to the back by Kidd. Jennings pleaded his case but to no avail. As they reached the stage area, Caldwell and Kidd turned and laughed at Jennings, who had just watched his change at a rematch with Valenkov disappear! So at Birthday Bash Thunder Valenkov will defend the 24 Hour Title against Mr. Electricity Robbie Caldwell. Also on that card, TV Champion Valec Corso will meet Mike 'The Biz' Bisignano in a rematch from their hard fought cage match at XP VI. Lets take a look at the footage. .. .. .. ========================= FROM XTREME PREJUDICE XI CORSO vs BISIGNANO ========================= Bisignano fought his way to the ropes before Corso had a chance to really get the hold on. Corso choked The Biz with the ropes and then hit a GERMAN SUPLEX out of the corner area. Corso pulled The Biz over and he climbed up to the top rope, but The Biz got to his feet and was up there with him in a shot! Bisignano tossed Corso to the mat then hit a SPLIT LEG MOONSAULT for the ONE!!!!!! TWO!!!!!!!!!!!!!! But Corso got a shoulder up!!! The Biz now had the look of someone who was on their way, he placed Corso on the top rope and climbed up with him. Using the cage The Biz was clearly going for a HURRICANRANA off the top rope when the lights in Sam's Town went out!!! When the lights came back on Corso and The Biz were still against the cage up in the corner, but a masked man was standing at ringside holding a pair of jumper cables in his hands, the cables connected to some kind of machine he had with him! The Biz looked down just as the man placed the cables on the cage, electrifying the cage!!!! The sight, was sickening! Both Corso and Bisignano were electrified and finally fell into the ring. You could smell the flesh. The masked man disconnected the cables and walked toward the dressing rooms. Neither man was able to answer the ten count and the match itself was declared a draw. ((((( DRAW ))))) .. .. .. (BACK TO SOUTHERNS) SOUTHERNS: No one has any idea who the masked man was or why he would end such a great match. Rob Beauty has said that at Birthday Bash, no matter what the spin of the wheel says, that anyone who interferes will be dealt with harshly! Speaking of harsh, it is harsh to step on a man's retirement party. It is even harsher when it is someone you called a friend. The Outlaw Morris Bass and Kagedo attacked The Predator Kevin Simmons on the night of his retirement. And of course, they attacked Mark McQueen earlier tonight, trying to even up the odds. But their was a surprise in store for them! .. .. .. ================================ THE O'MALLEYS (vs) THE OUTLAW MORRIS BASS & KAGEDO ================================ With Bass and Kags in the ring, The O'Malleys came out to Have a Drink On Me but stopped on the stage area. Rob Beauty walked out behind them! (Rob Beauty, 49% owner of the VXW, smiles at Bass and Kagedo) ROB BEAUTY: We all know that earlier tonight you attacked Mark McQueen in the parking lot, and we all know you wanted to unhandicap this handicap match. Well do I have a surprise for you boys! (The music starts back up and Justin Harper walks out and stands beside the O'Malleys) (A WELCOME BACK chant begins) .. .. .. SOUTHERNS: The three crusaders for justice rushed the ring and the fight was on! .. .. .. ================================ THE O'MALLEYS and JUSTIN HARPER (vs) THE OUTLAW MORRIS BASS & KAGEDO ================================ Harper went after Kagedo and the O'Malleys double teamed Bass. The Outlaw did his best but a DOUBLE CLOTHESLINE sent him out of the ring. Kagedo was doing well until the O'Malleys came over and he was overwhelmed by the numbers game!! A double DDT from the O'Malleys followed by a SPLIT LEG MOONSAULT from Harper and Kagedo was in deep trouble. The ref got The O'Malleys out of the ring and his match was on! Harper went for the quick fall, but Kagedo kicked out almost instantly. Justin Harper sent Kagedo to the ropes and hit a SPINEBUSTER on him, then drug him over to the O'Malleys! The three men then cut the ring in half, worked on Kagedo with quick tags and with the man advantage they were able to hold off a charging Bass on a few occasions. Harper and Anthony O'Malley went for a DOUBLE RUSSIAN LEG SWEEP but Kagedo was able to find the strength to flip over during the move and land on his feet. He dropped Anthony with a quick LEFT JAB and his Harper with a SPINNING HEEL KICK and was free enough to make the tag and bring The Outlaw into the ring. Bass grabbed legal man Anthony O'Malley and began to pummel him with FOREARMS and FISTS!!! Bass then hit Anthony with a SICK DOUBLE UNDERHOOK BRAINBUSTER!!! But Bass did not go for the pinfall, instead he climbed to the ropes and went for a SWANDIVE HEADBUTT!!! He missed!! Anthony tagged in Marcus O'Malley and the older O'Malley turned the tide on Bass. O'Malley hit him with a few good KNIFE EDGE chops, an IRISH WHIP into the corner, and a TORNADO DDT coming back out of the corner!!! The cover and a TWO COUNT!! Harper then shouted out that Bass has bad knees and Marcus locked him up in a SPINNING TOE HOLD!!! O'Malley pulled Bass over, continuing to hit the SPINNING TOE HOLD and tagged in Harper! Harper continued to punish the legs and knees of The Outlaw. Again, Harper and the O'Malleys cut the ring off and used quick tags and a focus on the knees to punish Bass. This went on until Anthony tried to lock Bass in a FIGURE FOUR, the veteran Bass reached up and GOUGED THE EYES of Anthony. Both men were on the mat but Bass began to make his way back and he was able to tag in Kagedo!!! Kags grabbed Anthony before he could tag and hit him with a GERMAN SUPLEX SERIES, ending in a GERMAN SUPLEX WITH BRIDGE!! But only got a two count! Kagedo then knocked Marcus and Harper off the apron, climbed up on the top rope and CLOCKED Anthony with a MISSLE DROPKICK!!! By this time, the weary Bass wanted back in the action and reached in and tagged himself in. Kags held Anthony open and Bass hit him with a FOREARM SHIVER that had the first three rows shivering! Bass then picked up Anthony for the CHOKESLAM but his knee did not hold!!! Bass crumbled to the canvas. Anthony tagged in Justin Harper!!! Harper locked on the CEDAR RAPIDS DEAHTLOCK!!!! But Kagedo got in the ring and made the save, that brought the O'Malleys into the ring. They knocked Kags out of the ring, Marcus followed him out. Anthony then picked up Bass and hit him with his RUNNING VERTEBREAKER CALLED THE FALLING STAR, SPIKED BY A GUILLOTINE LEGDROP FROM HARPER!!! Justin Harper rolled over Bass, made the cover and got the ONE!! TWO!!! THREEEE!!!!!! ((((( WINNERS = Justin Harper and The O'Malleys ))))) What a great show!
