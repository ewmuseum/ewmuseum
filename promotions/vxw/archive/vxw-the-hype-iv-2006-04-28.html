The Hype IV

Apr 28, 2006
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Six feet, eleven inches of destructive humanity. Three hundred and twelve pounds of restrained ferocity. This figure that stands before us, stoic and silent, head bowed, sculpted chest rising and falling with each deliberate breath... this figure is these things and many more. But this should be no secret to those within this organization, for this figure is one that ploughed an undefeated swath through every challenge that stood before him for nigh on a year in the VXW. But yet, but still...] V/O: How easily they forget. [We know that voice. Of course we do, we've certainly heard it often enough over the past year. Even so, it's changed somewhat from the familiar tone of yesterweek. The confident edge, that smug certainty, is conspicuous by its absence, replaced with frustration and submerged resentment. The source of the voice paces into view. Gelled brown hair, five o'clock shadow, open shirt collar. The deposed manager of champions himself, Chris Wheeling. He stops before his pet behemoth, Valenkov, for of course it must be he.] WHEELING: How easily they FORGET. They forget the era of greatness that I bestowed upon this place. They forget their proud and noble champion who defeated everyone in his path. The man who first brought the tag team titles singlehandedly back to prominance, who lifted the late Mark McQueen to far greater heights than he alone could scale. The man who rescued the Twenty-Four Hour Championship from the hands of a crude street-cred messiah, who even now once more drags that title through the dirt. [Bitterness? Nah, not our Chris.] WHEELING: They forget the domination of our current "champion" at every turn... but one. They forget the career of the Maquilladora, snuffed out at this man's hands. [Those hands that ball into mighty fists behind him, impatient to be put to work.] WHEELING: They forget it all, all of it. They forget it all because of three "defeats" in as many matches, as many as this man has suffered in the entirety of his past career. They look at this streak of misfortune, these flukes, betrayals and abberations, and they forget the fear, the intimidation, the awe that they all felt when they trembled before this man's might. They forget all this, and in its place they see... weakness. Opportunity. The chance to use our names to further their own. [His left eye twitches slightly, and he grinds his perfect teeth.] WHEELING: Morris Bass, "The Outlaw", drops our names... just another notch on his list of conquests. He proclaims himself and Omega as the most talented "big men" here in Vegas... [His lips curl into a derisive sneer.] WHEELING: How easily they forget. Stone's punk students, Barber and... [shrugs] whoever. They luck out to survive their first encounter with this man... [He turns and nods towards Valenkov, who's cold blue eyes flicker upwards towards the camera.] WHEELING: ... and what do they do? They come back for more. Stick their noses in my business. _MY_ business. No respect. No fear. How easily they forget. [He glowers into the camera. One begins to get the impression he's not in the best of moods.] WHEELING: And now, tonight... [snickers] "Mr. Jones." The second-rate henchman of a third-rate manager who once thought he could actually threaten my turf. Didn't take him long to be shown his real place in this company, but tonight... tonight, what? No doubt tonight he sees his big chance at last to climb the ladder, to claim _my_ throne as the greatest managerial mind in Las Vegas. To use us as a stepping stone to rise to the top of this company. [Wheeling looks up and cocks a questioning eyebrow at Valenkov, who simply narrows his gaze and glares into the camera.] WHEELIING: How easily they forget. [And then, finally, a smirk creeps onto his face.] WHEELING: But not any more. No, tonight I think it's way past time that we gave everyone a reminder. A reminder of our true place in this world, and more importantly a reminder of theirs. Because you've all, every one of you, been living in a fantasy world these past few weeks. A world in which we don't matter, a world in which we barely exist. But that time is gone. Reality is about to return, and it starts with a reminder. A reminder for Mr. Jones and Chad Essington, and a reminder for any others who may find themselves sadly in the wrong place, at the wrong time. A reminder they WON'T easily forget.
