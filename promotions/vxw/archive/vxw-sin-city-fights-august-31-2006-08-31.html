Sin City Fights August 31

August 31, 2006

(We go backstage as the camera focuses upon a VXW banner when suddenly, a familiar voice begins to speak.) VOICE: Aye!!! I'm ov'r here. (Mammoth face pop ensues as the fans know exactly who that is. The camera scrolls over to the left to find Marcus and Anthony O'Malley sitting on a couple of jet black road rack cases. Both men are in their normal attire: Marcus with his vodka bottle and kendo stick firmly attached in each hand, while Anthony sits there wearing a VXW trademarked pea green O'Malley Brothers T-Shirt.) MARCUS: 'Aye kidd'es. Did ye miss us? (The fans pop crazy as Marcus just sits there and smiles.) MARCUS: Before we go any furth'r, I gotta answer a quest'n that ev'ryone keeps askin'. An' that is 'Where on Earth is Ad'm Jenn'ngs??' (MASSIVE GODLY INSANE POP FOR JENNINGS) MARCUS: Ye see, ol' Ad'm has tons o' free time on his hands ev'r since he was susp'nded. (The crowd all in unison beings to boo.) MARCUS: An' th'm fancy people down'n Holl'wood took an' interest in ol' Ad'm an' called'm up. (Marcus starts snapping his fingers violently.) MARCUS: Anth'ny, what's that comp'ny that fired ol' Tom Cruise? ANTHONY: Par'mount. MARCUS: Right!!! Par'mount. That's tha company that call'd Ad'm. Said he remind'd 'im o' a combo between Tha Rock an' a 70's version o' Burt Renyolds. (The crowd starts laughing.) ANTHONY: (singing) # East bound an' down# # Load it up an' truckin'# (The crowd begins laughing as Marcus slaps his little brother on the shoulder.) MARCUS: An' as ye can see, Jess'ca ain't with us t'night since she's out there in Holl'wood a-visitin' her broth'r. Which is probably a good thing since there's one person I need to address here. An' that's you Chad Essin'ton. (The fans start to stir with anticipation.) MARCUS: It took ye one year to FINALLY find a client with some talent. It took ye one year ta find someone ta help ye make a name for ye self. An' what did ye an' Ken Young do?? Not only did ye make a name for ye self BUT ye managed ta get on tha bad sides o' a couple people. Ours. (Marcus opens the top of the vodka bottle and places it in his mouth and tilts his head back as the bottle violently begins to bubble with each gulp. Finally, Marcus takes the bottle out of his mouth and wipes his chin and his lower lip with his left arm.) ANTHONY: Ye see Ken Young. You an' ol' Chad there have managed ta do somethin' that very few people here have managed ta do. An' that's get our attention. So go ahead. Pat ye selves on tha back fer that one. But realize that what ye've done is ye've found yerself in a fight that ye won't win. My broth'r an' I are BOTH two-time Vegas Tag Team Champions. Ma'cus won tha feckin' Gauntlet match this year. We're consider'd one o' tha BEST tag teams in tha business t'day. (The camera zooms in on Anthony as for the first time in a long time, Anthony seems to be upset.) ANTHONY: Ask any wrestlin' organization out there ta list tha top 10 Tag Teams out there an' you'll find me an' my broth'r on ev'ry single list. This ain't Villano 23454 and Villano 970987 you're dealin' with. THIS IS THA ASSASSIN' AN' KID LIGHTNIN' BABY!!!!! (The fans start popping crazy as Marcus just looks at his little brother, grinning from ear to ear, just expecting Anthony to take a page out of the old school playbook and let out a WHOOO!!!!) MARCUS: So boys, here's tha Gauntlet we're throwin' down. It don't matter if I'm in tha finals or if it's Anthony that's in tha finals for tha 24-Hour Title. Regardless, at Vegas Violence, Me an' my broth'r want you two boys in tha ring. An' find ye selves a partn'r. (The crowd buzzes as Marcus nods his head.) MARCUS: That's right. Ye heard me. 3 on 2 Handicap Match. Find ye selves anyone ye think is tha meanest badass around an' put him in yer corn'r. Chad, baby, we know ye got tha money ta burn so choose wisely cause...... MARCUS AND ANTHONY IN UNISON: If fear's all that's stoppin' ye then damn ye soul cause tha Lord hates a coward!!!! .. .. .. [Richard Masterson quietly enters a small business office somewhere deep within the bowels of Sam's Town. Chris Wheeling, having apparently appropriated this office for his own purposes, is currently on his cellphone with his back to the door and the towering figure of Thunder Valenkov standing off to the side. Richard sits down quietly and waits for Wheeling to turn around. Wheeling finishes the business call and turns, a little surprised to see Masterson sitting at his desk. With a quizical eyebrow raise, Wheeling waits.] Masterson: We have a problem. [The quizical look does not fade.] Wheeling: A problem? How so? Masterson: (looking a little anxious) Caldwell. You saw what he did. [Wheeling steeples his hands and leans back in his chair looking at Masterson.] Wheeling: Of course. Not that it should have been unexpected... surely you had to know your neanderthal spawn's fondness for snapping limbs would have consequences down the line, even if he was too blinded by testosterone and whatever ungodly concoctions he has running though his veins to know it himself. But, and with all due respect... I fail to see how _your_ unfinished business is any concern of _ours_. [Masterson, by no means, is a man use to being discarded so easily. With a slight beading of sweat on his brow, he leans forward a little.] Masterson: Our plans, Wheeling. That is what I mean. This constant upheaval of power is just ripe for the pickings. You want Valenkov back in the champion's seat and Omega is the tool to get you there. If Caldwell distracts him enough, I don't know how well I can keep Omega under control and focused on that goal. Wheeling: I don't have time for your distractions, Masterson. _That goal_ is the only thing that concerns me right now. So might I suggest not only do you take care of whatever distractions are keeping you from focusing on that goal, but also I might suggest you learn to keep a tighter leash on _your_ pet monster. [Wheeling smirks and glances pointedly in Valenkov's direction, who simply returns a blank stare.] Masterson: Wheeling, now is not the time for this! I held up our part in keeping that little waste of talent Anderson in the ring for Valenkov to destroy. Nobody, and I mean nobody, even knew that Caldwell was in the arena. Wheeling: (with a smug look) So what are you proposing? Abandon our _plans_ to squash one trifling little bug? Masterson: (looking down a little bit) No, we just need to watch out for him. Ya know, watch our backs a little more. Wheeling: Watch our backs? [He pauses in thought for a moment] No... I think perhaps you're right after all. This distraction of yours shouldn't be allowed to concern either of us any longer. With the forces at our command, _we_ should be the last to worry about looking over our shoulders. But perhaps... perhaps it's time we underlined that fact. [Wheeling stands up and looks Masterson in the eye. Thunder Valenkov stares at Masterson, making him feel a little uncomfortable. A smile spreads across Wheelings face and extends his hand.] Wheeling: Perhaps it's time, as the cool kids say... we unleash hell. [And with a smirk from Wheeling and a knowing smile from Masterson, the two men shake hands.] [ftb] .. .. ..
