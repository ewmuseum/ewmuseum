Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Backstage at the Sam's Town casino. In a quiet corridor away from the VXW mid-show hustle and bustle, we find a solitary soda machine. Nothing too exciting perhaps, in and of itself, but what draws the average viewer's attention is not the machine, exactly, but instead the rather shapely female figure that stands before it. But alas, all is not well in shapely female land, for it appears that her beverage of choice has had the nerve to get stuck in place halfway-down, forcing our friend to do what rather shapely female figures in this situation have a tendency to do, and that of course is to bend over in a provocative manner (and in tight leather pants, no less!) while attempting to retrieve her soda. But fear not, fair damsel, for your knight is about to arrive... or should I say, Knight, because it is indeed the man who puts the "Hotstuff" in "Hotstuff" Stevie Knight... er, "Hotstuff" Stevie Knight! Never the type to look a gift horse in the mouth, or some other more appropriate metaphor, a smirking Stevie approaches our shapely female from behind, laying his hands on the small of her back... well, at least somewhere in that region...] KNIGHT: Easy, baby... how about you let a man take care of that... [The shapely female pulls away and reveals herself as Jessica Jennings. She looks into the eyes of Knight with a seductive grin on her face.] JESSICA: Sounds like a good idea. Why don't you go get Marcus for me, please? [Knight gives her an evil look for a split-second before regaining his smug composure, leaning forward against the soda machine, and over Jessica.] KNIGHT: Yeah, that's real funny, sweetheart, but let's be serious for a second... I've seen the way you look at me, Jessica, when you think I'm not looking. And hey, I understand. Hot chicks like you aren't supposed to be with drunk out-of-shape slobs like Marcus, it just ain't natural, babe. You're supposed to be with guys who look like _this_... [He nods down at his muscular physique, and seems quite impressed with what he finds.] KNIGHT: So whaddya say, honey? How about you kick that Irish loser to the curb, and trade up for a sleeker model, huh? [Yeah, Stevie's never exactly been good at metaphors. But hey, at least he adds an oiled bicep flex to underline his point... that's gotta count for something, right?] JESSICA: Stevie, Stevie. [Jessica slowly runs her soft fingers up and down his right bicep, then she looks up and down the man they call "Hotstuff".] JESSICA: Is that the only bulge you can get at a time like this? [Jessica looks down and examines the waist of Knight and just smiles as she looks back into his eyes.] JESSICA: Looks like the survey says yes. [Jessica rolls her eyes and turns around and finally manages to get her dented can of soda out of the machine.] JESSICA: Looks like I didn't need a man after all. [Jessica opens the can and begins to drink it as she walks away. A rather irked Knight watches her depart, and calls out after her.] KNIGHT: Your loss, babe. You just made a big mistake, sweetheart, but you'll come back, they all come back to the Hotstuff in the end... I'll see you later... [Fade out.] .. .. .. (The camera moves in between two cars and we see the Classy Joey Boy lying on the ground, a pool of blood around him) WOMAN: I just found him like this, who would do such a thing? Folks, CJB, Classy Joey Boy was attacked in the parking garage here at Sam's Town and has sustained some serious injuries. He was taken to UMC Medical Center and it looks like he will be spending some time there. But no one saw the incident, but two suspects jump out. Lark Fenriz and Mike Bisignano. Fenriz and CJB have old business from the past, which is what CJB was looking to settle at Phear Phantastic, but he inadvertently cost The Biz the TV Title. Strange things are afoot in the VXW. Speaking of strange, Team Essington was at Phear Phantastic and it was an all new team. Mr. Smith and Mr. Jones were in a match with the Rodeo Boys when they decided to just attack the Rodeos with chairs. Earlier, Kenny Kidd stepped in the ring with the seven foot Mr. Smith. .. .. .. ============ KENNY KIDD (vs) MR. SMITH ============ Kidd was unsure of how to attack the big man, so the big man attacked him first. Smith pounded on Kidd, who used his speed to get away but Kidd was unable to mount any real offsense. Smith caught a flying Kidd and drove him into each of the four turnbuckles. Smith then dropped a HUGE ELBOW on Kidd and then the big man climbed up to the top rope. That's right, all seven feet of him on the top rope. But before he could leap, Billy Bones came out from the back, carrying the Rodeo Boys bull rope. Smith got down and rolled out of the ring, but was hit clean on the nose with the cow bell! That brought out the four foot Mr. Jones. Jones jumped on Bones' back, but Bones flipped him to the ground and began to choke him with the bull rope. That's when a man in a mask came out from the back, his mask read Mr. Jefferson, and he grabbed Bones, spun him around and planted him with a JUMPING DDT! Jefferson, slid into the ring and hit Kidd with a FACE FIRST SIT OUT VERTICAL SUPLEX while the ref was trying to sift through the chaos on the outside of the ring. Smith slid into the ring, made the cover for the ONE!!!!!!! TWO!!!!!!!!!!!!!! THREEEEEEE!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! But now there are three men on that team, for the Rodeo Boys the fight is now uphill. The next that was scheduled was AI Arron Iron taking on Iyasu Saku, but neither man was able to make it to the arena. Both were stuck in other parts of the country. So the fans got a VXW Academy match. Matt Payne defeated Tyler Shaw with a POWERBOMB. Payne was accompanied to the ring by Cobra, who seemed to be playing the role of coach for the youngster. Payne dominated the match and is quickly becoming the top man in the Academy. Speaking of the VXW Academy, Johnny Stone was on hand for Setting Sun and is doing well after his loss to Kyle Sturgis at Phear Phantastic. After Matt Payne won his match over Shaw he and Cobra were making their way to the back when David Xavier and Cutthroat Kyle Sturgis walked out onto the stage area. The four men exchanged words before security came out and kept the heated exchanged from becoming physical. Fans were on the edge of their seats for the debut, at least the official one since the man has been around for about three months now, of Omega! .. .. .. ================================ OMEGA (vs) MR ELECTRICITY ROBBIE CALDWELL ================================ Omega came to the ring first and the fans were wild for the big star. Caldwell came out, and did not look confident at all. Caldwell climbed into the ring but never left the corner area. The bell rang and Caldwell gave Omega the finger and jumped out of the ring and made his run toward the back. But when Caldwell got to the top of the ramp, he ran into Spin The Wheel Champion Damian Payne! Sadistic Insanity pointed to the ring then turned Caldwell around and gave him a little 'encouragement' shove to return to the ring. Omega climbed out and decided to help Caldwell come back to the ring. Omega dragged Mr Electricity kicking and screaming back to the ring. Omega blasted him with a CLOTHESLINE!!! Then picked him up and hit him with a BACKBREAKER SERIES!! Omega was intense, he had the look of a man determined to make an impression not only on Caldwell but on the fans of the VXW as well. Omega then tossed Caldwell over the ropes and the beating continued on the outside of the ring. Caldwell went into the ring steps, and then into the ring post. Omega had bloodied his opponent, and tossed him back into the ring. Omega climbed to the top rope and waited, and then hit Caldwell with a FLYING CLOTHESLINE!!!! Omega then signaled to the crowd that he was going to end this match. That's when Money by Pink Floyd hit and Roman 'Money' Anderson walked out and jumped Damian Payne from behind! Omega turned and he got a BLATANT LOW BLOW from Robbie Caldwell! Which the referee saw and he quickly disqualified Mr Electricity! [We open to footage shot earlier in the night. Backstage at Sam's Town, outside a door labeled with the name of one of the VXW joint owners, Rod Beauty. "Lean" Darlene Orion is there with a microphone, but also notable by his presence is one of the two burly bodyguards that have been accompanying Chris Wheeling wherever he goes of late. Darlene moves forward to ask him a question, but he shakes his head and raises a single finger to his lips to "shush" her. Just then the door to the office swings open, and we see both Rod Beauty and Chris Wheeling, in the midst of a handshake.] WHEELING: So glad we could see eye to eye on this, Rod. ROD: No problem, Chris, any time. [They exchange friendly smiles and Rod retreats back into his office, closing the door behind him. Wheeling turns to Darlene with his familiar transparent smile.] WHEELING: Ah, Darlene... sticking that pretty little nose where it doesn't belong as ever. LDO: Of course. So tell me, Chris... rumor has it that your meeting with Rod Beauty was because of the confusion surrounding the tag team titles, after Austin Lee pinned his own partner, Stevie Knight, in the Rage in the Cage match at Phear Phantastic. In fact, rumor has it that the titles may even be STRIPPED from Hot Property here tonight, due to the clause in the rulebook that Randall Crane used several years ago to strip the Maquilladora of the tag titles when he held them by himself. What do you... WHEELING: Woah, woah, woah. Easy, Darlene... let's forget about rumors and stick to the facts, shall we? After all, not only do loose lips sink ships, but they can also cause pretty announcers to lose their jobs... and we certainly wouldn't want that now, would we? [Darlene gives Wheeling an evil look.] WHEELING: Excellent. Now, as I was saying, Darlene, let's just stick to the facts. While there may indeed be a clause in the rulebook that allowed for the stripping of Maq's titles "back in the day," that really has nothing to do with the current situation. In fact, there isn't even a situation to start with. As you all saw, and as I explained to Mr. Beauty just moments ago, the simple fact is that Hot Property walked into the match with the tag team titles, and Hot Property walked OUT of the match with the tag team titles. No confusion. No controversy. No situation to address, and certainly no titles to strip. [Darlene rolls her eyes.] DARLENE: And he bought that? Figures. [Wheeling smirks.] WHEELING: Now, now, Darlene. Of course he did. Rod Beauty is a very reasonable and intelligent man after all, and he also happens to be a close friend of mine. So we discussed the situation, as friends often do, and that was the end of it. So I'd suggest, Darlene, if you're looking for a story to report, you'd best move along and look elsewhere, because I'm afraid there's nothing to see here. [And on that note, Wheeling turns sharply on his heels and departs. His bodyguard steps up to Darlene with an odd look, then smiles widely and gives a little wave goodbye before following after Wheeling. Fade out. .. .. .. [AC/DC's "Sin City" suddenly kicks in over the speakers as an image of a spinning roulette wheel appears on the big screen, gradually slowing until it stops on the spot where the green zero usually is -- except here it's gold plate instead of green, and the symbol is the dollar sign... # Diamonds and dust # # (home at last) Poor man last, rich man first # # Lambourginis, caviar # # Dry martinis, Shangri-la # [And the jeers of the fans turn to full-blown BOOS as out from beneath the screen step none other than the reigning VXW Tag Team Champions! HOT PROPERTY!! # I got a burning feeling # # Deep inside of me # # It's yearning # # But I'm going to set it free # [Knight and Lee mug for the cameras, prominently displaying their shiny platinum title belts, and step to each side as out next appears... Six feet eleven inches, three hundred and twelve pounds of pure destructive intent. The Soviet monster. The killer from Kazakhstan. The man who ended the VXW career of the legendary Maquilladora. And with his gleaming golden title belt draped over one mighty shoulder, the reigning VXW 24-Hour Champion!!! THUNDER...VALENKOV!!! The BOOS intensify as the fans lay eyes on the man who pinned Adam Jennings in the Rage in the Cage match at Phear Phantastic. But if this verbal abuse has any effect upon Valenkov in the slightest, he does not show it, staring out impassively across the gathered throng. # I'm going in to sin city # # I'm gonna win in sin city # # Where the lights are bright # # Do the town tonight # # I'm gonna win in sin city # # I'm gonna rule you baby # [And of course, this little group that could, and always does, would not be complete without the greatest manager in Vegas wrestling history. The man who makes things happen. Your friend and mine! CHRIS "BY GOD!" WHEELING!!! Yes, yet more BOOS as Wheeling struts out onto the stage with with a wide self-satisfied smirk firmly plastered upon his face. Mock-bowing in response to applause from Knight and Lee, he gives them his most endearing "oh you really shouldn't, but you really should," look. As he does so, Wheeling's two recently-aquired black-dressed heavies appear onto the stage also, staying in the background while they scan the area for trouble. Satisfied that all is well, they nod to Wheeling, and all six men make their way down to ringside, Lee and Knight strutting and flexing with every step while Valenkov strides purposely ahead. The monster climbs up onto the apron and steps over the ropes, which are soon also held open by Knight and Lee for Wheeling to join them in the ring. The two heavies take up position around ringside, and then with the tag team champions flanking him either side, and the 24-Hour Champion towering over him from behind, Wheeling flashes that familiar faux-smile out across the fans and raises a microphone.] WHEELING: What a year... [He grins.] WHEELING: What a year it's truly been for the fans and wrestlers of the VXW. Changes in ownership, a changing of the guard, new stars, new attractions, and the acheivement of unheralded heights for this company. And of course, as the sun sets on 2005 and we look forward to another new year, I can't help but reflect on what a great year it has also been... for _ME_. That even before that night, the wheels had been set in motion for the formation of a group, that would bring about the domination of this company. And in a business in which such grandious claims are made ever day, in every corner of the world, of course there were skeptics. Of course there were disbelievers, who were sure I wouldn't be able to back up such extravagant words. [His smile turns up a couple of notches.] WHEELING: But just three months later, in this very ring, that claim became a reality. The Twenty-Four Hour Championship, the greatest prize this company has to offer, was pryed, through my own brilliance, from the undeserving grasp of "The Hype" Adam Jennings, and delivered into the hands of the most dominant force in wrestling today, Thunder Valenkov! [The crowd BOOS at the mention of Jennings getting screwed out off the title, while Wheeling gestures proudly towards Valenkov, who simply sends his impassive gaze out across the arena.] WHEELING: And alongside him, of course, where would I be without the hottest tag team in wrestling today, Hot Property!
