(The Beautiful People hits and the screen begins to fill, one by one with pictures of the various VXW superstars once they are all on the screen the word FEAR IS NEAR blazes over them, then we fade to Dan The Man Aller) DTMA: This is it, the final show before Phear Phantastic. We awarded the final spot inside the cage tonight, we saw one spot up for grabs and we had a street fight. But we also heard from Omega. The big man from WILD who has been after Morris Bass hit the ring before the show could get started. .. .. .. ============= OMEGA SPEAKS ============= ["Scum of the Earth" erupts over the P.A. System with Running WILD and pictures of Omega destroying opponents on the screen. A chorus of boos rains down as Omega, dressed in boots-blue jeans-tight black tee and his DV8 sunglasses, strolls down to the ring. Omega's head doesn't turn to look at the VXW fans. Omega climbs the steps and knocks down a piece of trash thrown at him. Omega removes his sunglasses and stares at the area the trash came from. Omega is given a microphone and steps into the ring.] Omega: Being here in Vegas has given me a new respect for the shows that go on here. [Mild VXW and Vegas Pop.] You see, I've seen shows with dragons, hyper-active idiots, and those with gay moments I couldn't stand. You see, I've found what I've been watching to lack as certain...edge. A certain...ferocity. You can keep your "Hype", your Drunken brotherly love, your versions of evil. Give me carnage...give me blood... give me Bass. [Boos start at the sound of Bass's name. Omega glares around the ring.] You sheep don't even know why you boo anymore do you? [Again boos for Omega.] I promise you retribution against all the wrong Bass has done and you boo me? You all probably sat there in wonder as to why, after "I" cost Bass his match that I allowed Sandsbury of WILD... [Boos for the competiton, Omega has to wait. An almost sneering snarl is formed on his face.] Like I was saying, you all were probably wondering why I let that suit do the talking. Its simple. I knew that none of you would understand. No one here woulD evER unDERSTAND! [Omega's muscles tense. Omega starts pacing the ring, an intensity building in his eyes.] Bass has run from me at every chance he had. The most recent time we faced each other he did enough to keep me busy while another lost the match. His time is coming to an end. Our match, his ass (pointing to the ceiling) hanging by that thin line on a piece of paper not even worthy to be considered toilet paper, and the ladder will be his end. [Omega looks wildly around. His breathing becomes deep and an animalistic snarl creeps up on his face.] Not knowing what I'll do to him must have him scared. Not knowing what I'll do to him, what I can do to him, probably has him running behind his friends. Bass, I know you're here and I know you can here me. [Omega stops pacing. Omega looks down the ramp.] Bass, your friends will not help you. Your friends can not save you. (pointing again at the ceiling) And when I take that contact down...hehehehe...I'll be waiting for you when you get back. Why? [Omega glares around the arena.] Just to keep kicking you while you are down. [Omega toss the mic and vaults ropes landing nimbly on his feet. Omega puts on his DV8 sunglasses and walks up the ramp, spitting on a rowdy fan as he passes.] .. .. .. (CUT back to DTMA) DTMA: Not to be outdone I suppose, the fans got another unscheduled appearance, this time from David Xavier and his massive client, Kyle Sturgis. .. .. .. ================= $1,000 CHALLENGE ================= David Xavier came out and ran down Johnny Stone, calling him an old man and a coward. He said Sturgis would destroy him at Phear Phantastic. Xavier then called out anybody to try and win $1,000 for every minute they could last in the ring with "Cutthroat" Kyle Sturgis. Nobody came out. After some more goading, the VXW cameraman hit Sturgis in the back with his camera to a huge pop! Sturgis stumbled forward to the ropes. The cameraman dropped his camera and pounded Sturgis in the back with forearms, and then dove onto him and grabbed onto his leg. Sturgis fought to kick the cameraman free. After he did, the cameraman tried to run, but Sturgis quickly reached down and grabbed him by the throat. Sturgis pulled up the camerman, shook his head, and then chokeslammed him. After that, it was just trademark-move time for Sturgis. He lifted the cameraman up and demolished him with the Sturgis Devastation, just dropping the poor sap right on his head. Cover for 1...2...3. Sturgis gets the pinfall at 42 seconds. After the match, Sturgis lifted the cameraman up one more time and dropped him with the Mount Sturgis Drop (package piledriver). Xavier and Sturgis posed, and then left. The cameraman was stretchered out. .. .. .. (CUT back to DTMA) DTMA: Lark Fenriz and Rod Beauty have something going on, but for some reason Rod named Fenriz the number one contender and gave him a place in the cage. But Rod had promised it to Caldwell. So Mr Electricity demanded a match with Fenriz and he got it. But Fenriz and Caldwell are not strangers, and each time they have met in the past, Robbie has left the ring when things started going bad. He didn't have a place to run in this one, it was a lumberjack match! .. .. .. ================================ Lumberjack Match MR ELECTRICITY ROBBIE CALDWELL (vs) LARK FENRIZ ================================ The lumberjacks were in place and Caldwell made his entrance. Then Fenriz came to the ring, looking very serious. At the bell Fenriz went right Caldwell who slid out of the ring and ran right into a hard right hand from COBRA!! Caldwell spun around and then got a shot from Damian Payne. Sturgis tossed Caldwell back in the ring and Fenriz began to pound on the smaller Caldwell. Fenriz slammed his head into each turnbuckle before hitting him with a SLINGSHOT SUPLEX! Lark then threw Caldwell to the wolves. On the outside, Caldwell ended up face to face with The Rodeo Boys and Team Brittania and the two tag teams had a lot of fun pounding on the arrogant Caldwell. Fenriz came out to join in but he got nailed from behind by Payne. Cobra then pulled Payne off Fenriz and that started a fight between Cobra and Payne. Caldwell crawled back in the ring after ducking a RUNNING CLOTHESLINE from Iysau Saku, a clothesline that knocked Fenriz off his feet. Billy Bones of the Rodeo Boys rolled Fenriz in the ring and Caldwell started to work on his lower back. On the outside of the ring, referees pulled apart Cobra and Payne, and then sent both men to the back, since it was clear they were not ready to give up the fight with each other. Caldwell sent Fenriz to the ropes and a TILT A WHIRL BACKBREAKER on The Wolf, the signature move of Caldwell's mentor Sebastian. Mr Electricity made the cover ONE!!!!!! TWO!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! BUT FENRIZ KICKED OUT!!!!!!!!!!!!!! Caldwell continued to work on the small of the back of Fenriz, trying to take away the ability of the big man to hit the SAMMAEL! Caldwell got blocked during a GERMAN SUPLEX attempt and Fenriz broke with with a few well placed ELBOWS. Fenriz then came off the ropes with a FLYING SHOULDERBLOCK that sent Caldwell out of the ring and into the hands of newcomer JC Brawley. Brawley, making his first VXW appearance had some fun roughing up Caldwell but he was soon knocked down by Tyler Rude and Rabid, aka Tap Out City. They sent Brawley into the first row and went for a DOUBLE DDT on Caldwell only to have it broken up by the Black Knight, Brawleys tag team partner. This brought over Team Brittania, much to the delight of Section 113. Caldwell climbed back in the ring during the chaos, but he ran into Fenriz who picked him up and hit .. . . SAMMAEL!!!!!!!!!! The fans were going crazy, Fenriz made the cover ONE!!!!!!!! TWO!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! BROKEN UP BY DRACULA!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! The man in the Dracula mask came out of the crowd slid into the ring and broke up the fall. Dracula rolled back out of the ring and made his way to the back, and Fenriz decided to give chase, but he was cut off by the lumberjacks. That's when things got weird. Roman 'Money' Anderson, who had not come out with the lumberjacks, came from the ceiling of the arena, lowered down by a rope. Anderson grabbed Caldwell, who was still out in the center of the ring, gave a thumbs up to someone high in the arena ceiling, and both men were raised out of the ring. Fenriz fought off the lumberjacks and got back in the ring only to see Caldwell rising to the ceiling. The referee had no choice but to award the match to Lark Fenriz, by count out. [Backstage at Sams Town, we find the reigning Television Champion Mike "The Biz" Bisignano chatting with a couple of VXW workers. As always, he's wearing his ring/street gear and has a black VXW baseball cap on top of his head. The TV title is slung over his right shoulder. Suddenly, a rather large shadow looms over him.] VOICE: Mr. Bisignano... just the person I was looking for. [Biz looks around, into the hardened glower of a rather large and burly man, dressed all in black, including a leather jacket and gloves. The voice however, doesn't belong to this bear of a man, but rather to the man who stands behind him. And as the black-clad fellow steps to the side, taking up a position to survey the area, we see that familiar transparent smile that could only belong to one man...Chris Wheeling!] BIZ: Really now? And what pray tell can I do for ya? [Wheeling flashes a pearly-white grin.] WHEELING: Actually, Mike... can I call you Mike? [Biz shrugs.] BIZ: I prefer Biz. WHEELING: Excellent. Actually, Mike, it's more what I can do for you. [Wheeling nods to his ape-like associate, who fixes his glare upon the two backstage workers. They quickly take the hint and depart the scene.] WHEELING: You see, Mike... I'm a businessman. And like any good businessman, I'm always looking for the right opportunity. The opportunity to expand, and conquer new horizons. To grow my franchise, so to speak. And although my domination of the VXW championship scene has brought great reward over the past six months, both to myself and of course the clients that I manage, a smart businessman is never satisfied with mere greatness, Mike, as I'm sure you can understand. BIZ: And I take it that's where *I* come in, right? [Wheeling's grin grows wider.] WHEELING: Very astute of you, Mike. VERY astute. Yes, that is indeed where you come in. Because I've been watching you, Mike, ever since you won that Television title. And I'll confess, I didn't think you'd do as well for yourself as you have done, Mike. I thought you'd be a flash-in-the pan champion, who'd drop the title back to Valec Corso within a matter of weeks or even days. [The Biz stares at Wheeling and his face tightens.] WHEELING: But you didn't, Mike. I was wrong, and I'm happy to admit it. I've been impressed with you these past few months, in fact I've been very impressed. The growing confidence, that burgeoning air of a champion, your undeniable ring skills... they all add up, Mike, to what I see as the missing part of my equation. The final piece of the puzzle as it were. That fleeting quality that makes the difference between mere greatness and total domination of the VXW. [Wheeling offers his hand.] WHEELING: So what do you say, Mike? Will you accept my offer and take your place in the most dominant group this company has ever seen? [Wheeling smiles expectantly. The Biz just continues to look Wheeling in the face.] BIZ: The Biz has and always will answer to one person and one person only. And that's The Biz. [Wheeling actually seems somewhat taken-aback, but soon recovers.] WHEELING: Now, now, Mike... let's not be hasty here. Think about everything we can do for YOU. Rage in the Cage is right around the corner, after all, and that cage can be a dangerous place for a man without allies. Just take a look at the list of entrants. My people will certainly be watching each others' backs, as I'm sure will the Society of Doom. What about you, Mike? Who's going to watch your back? Who's going to protect that shiny morsel of yours, against a cage full of ambitious, hungry lions? BIZ: That's what being a champion is all about. If I were to take the backdoor route, I'd be no better than the king of the backdoors, Roman Anderson. [Wheeling sighs.] WHEELING: You're making a mistake, Mike. A _BIG_ mistake. This is a once in a lifetime offer I'm giving you here, please don't make me look foolish for thinking you'd be smart enough to take it... I don't like to be made a fool of, Mike. BIZ: Well you should've thought about that before you woke up this morning and chose to put on that K-Mart suit and Payless shoes. [Wheeling's smile slips from his face for a moment, before quickly finding its way back. He nods understandingly and produces a business card from his pocket.] WHEELING: Okay, Mike, I'll tell you what we'll do. Here's my card. Sleep on it, think it over, don't rush into any hasty decisions, and then give me a call, okay? It's a once in a lifetime opportunity, Mike. I wouldn't like to see you just throw it all away. Call me... I'll be waiting. [And with a parting smile, Wheeling spins on his heels and walks away. His hefty compadre moves to follow, but stops first to give Bisignano a curious look. And then he raises his hand, extends his pinkie and his thumb, and makes the wiggling "call me" sign with a knowing wink, before turning to trail behind Wheeling. The Biz looks at the card and then at Wheeling before he just shakes his head in disgust.] BIZ: Damn...and I thought Roman Anderson was the only guy around here who could always be found one step behind another man but that guy DEFINITELY takes the cake. [The Biz looks at the card once more before walking away and letting it slip from his fingers as he heads down the hallway.] .. .. .. (Backstage, we find "Lean" Darlene Orion standing in front of a VXW backdrop with microphone in hand.) LDO: I'm standing here with one of the participants in tonight's Vegas Street Fight, "The Assassin" Marcus O'Malley. ("Lean" Darlene turns and faces Marcus.) MARCUS: Well hell, if ye found out that ye had ta face me an' Ad'm in a street fight tonight, wouldn't ye be a wee bit antsy an' gettin' all pissed at ye partn'r too? (Marcus removes his hat to reveal the permananet scar that runs down the middle of his head.) MARCUS: An' tha result? 125 stitches. Did it piss me off? Ye bet ye damn ass it did. But ye know what else it did? It made me hungry.....hungry for a piece o' that Tex'n's ass. An' personally I want to thank tha fans o' Vegas who voted fer this match. It may not mean a whole hell o'lot ta Ad'm but it sure as hell means alot ta me. (Marcus turns his attention back to "Lean" Darlene.) MARCUS: An' aft'r it's all said an' done, I'm gonna make damn sure that Bass is soften'd up for ol' Omega at Phear Phantastic. (Marcus starts to walk off but "Lean" Darlene grabs him by the arm.) LDO: Just one more question Marcus. How do you feel about being in Rage in the Cage with Kagedo as #1 Contenders to the VXW Tag Team Titles, and what is your gameplan going into the cage? MARCUS: If ye askin' me do I like bein' forced ta pair up with Kag'do, tha answer's hell no. Matter o' fact, I hope he gets tha livin' shit kicked out o' him while he's in there. It's the only time in my life I'll ev'r hope an' wish that Lark Fenriz would rip out his tongue through his bloody ass. But if ye askin' me what my gameplan is goin' in, it's real simple. Believe it or not, I ain't in there ta win gold. (The camera slowly pans in on the face of Marcus.) MARCUS: Ye see, I got two SoD broth'rs in there with me......Ad'm Jenn'ngs an' Just'n Harp'r. If Ad'm truly wants tha 24-Hour Champ'nship I'll do ev'rything in my pow'r ta help him get it. If Harp'r wants tha Spin tha Wheel TV Title, once again, I'll do ev'rything in my pow'r ta help him get it. LDO: But what about the VXW Tag Team Titles? Aren't you interested in those? (Marcus just looks at "Lean" Darlene and smiles.) MARCUS: Ye let me worry a-boot that. (Marcus turns and walks off leaving "Lean" Darlene in a state of confusement.) .. .. ..
