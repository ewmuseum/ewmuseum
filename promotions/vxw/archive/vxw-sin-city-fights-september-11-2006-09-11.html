[The camera cuts backstage to pre-recorded footage, shot outside the private locker room reserved for Chris Wheeling and Thunder Valenkov. One of those men, the Soviet Monster himself, is conspicuous by his absence. The other, you'll be glad to know, is standing by alongside none other than "Lean" Darlene herself. He is of course your friend and mine, the incomparable manager amongst managers, Mr. Christopher Wheeling. He greats the camera with a signature transparent smile.] LDO: Chris, there's several things that... WHEELING: Yes, of course there are, Darlene. Of course there are. I'm sure we're all busy people here, so allow me to cut through the pointless preamble and get you where you're hoping to go. Yes, there's several things that you'd like to know, several questions burning deeply in the very pits of your soul that I and I alone can answer. Am I on the right track so far? [Darlene gives Chris a somewhat evil glare, before her professionalism as ever takes over.] LDO: Well, yes. In so far as... WHEELING: In so far as, let me see... one, you'd like to know exactly what was going on last week with that quite frankly unprovoked, outrageous and barbaric display of physicality at the hands of Omega and Thunder Valenkov. Well I can answer that right now, Darlene, and save your sweet lips the trouble of asking. Because what that was, in its most basic form, was a show of strength. A reminder for those that may still be dwelling in the past, that the power in today's VXW lies as firmly in my hands, and of course the hands of my associate, Richard Masterson, as they ever have. An example of the forces we wield, and the destruction they can wreak at any time we simply give the word. And above all else, a warning... of the fate that must surely befall any poor soul foolish enough to get in our way. LDO: So in other words, two men with tiny... conciences, getting off on watching their pet monsters do the things they can't do themselves. Am I on the right track so far? [Wheeling's smirk waivers for a moment, but he as ever recovers well.] WHEELING: Touche, Darlene, touche. So then, let me wrack my mind for a moment, and see if I can't come up with the second question you no doubt wish to get off your delightful chest. [That last is accompanied by a letcherous glint within the eyes of one Christopher Wheeling.] WHEELING: Because tonight, a few short minutes from now, my "pet monster" steps into the ring with a man I remember all too well. A foe with whom you might even say we share a common past. In fact, can you even believe this, Darlene, but someone who actually once though he could steal something that belonged to me... and who instead almost had his career stolen from him, as worthless at that may be. A man who if he had any sense would have stayed buried in the past, but instead we find him once again blindly peddling the same brand of juvenile idiocy as ever, and just as blind to the situaiton he finds himself in tonight. LDO: That's tremendous, Chris. But let's cut through the "pointless preamble," shall we? What stipulation have you chosen for Valenkov's match with Lance Voltar? WHEELING: I like you, Darlene, but let's not push it. One word and I could make your life a misery around here. LDO: Really? So imagine how I'm feeling right now, after a couple of hundred. [That one he doesn't manage to hide quite so easily, the smile disappearing from his face.] WHEELING: Okay, if that's how it's going to be, fine. You want to know my stipulation, Darlene? I'll tell you. Because you know, I've thought long and hard about this, these past few days. Oh, of course I could have simply stacked the deck so favorably that even young Mr. Voltar would have realized the gravity of his dilemma... even if he couldn't spell it. A chokeslam match perhaps, or an intelligent thought on a pole, something so far outside his capabilities that it took any enjoyment out of the match. But no. Never let it be said that I'm not a giving man. Never let it be said that I didn't give our Mr. Voltar a good, honest, fighting chance. Because this match I've picked tonight, Darlene, is going to give Lance Voltar the chance to get closer to greatness than he's ever gone before. A match that no matter how hard he tries to run from destiny, it will keep on dragging him back. A match that, as I believe his kind might like to say, will almost certainly be "off the chain." So to speak. [A smirk at last from Wheeling, it's back baby!] WHEELING: Yes, I think tonight, Darlene, we're going to give the fans a treat. Because tonight, we're going to kick it, OLD SCHOOL style. [And with that, he raps lightly upon the locker room door, which swings open as if on cue. And there in the doorway, no... filling the doorway, is the near seven foot, three hundred and twelve pound force of nature known as Thunder Valenkov. Primed, ready, muscular lightning-spiralled arms straining with unreleased destruction. Grim visage unreadable, as it looks down at his hands... and the eight feet of solid steel chain that hangs in that effortless grip.] WHEELING: Now what's that saying? Oh yes... sweeeeet... [And with a merry chuckle and the clank of hefted chain, we fade out.] .. .. ..
