(OOC- Big thanks to [name withheld] and [name withheld] for writing the Irish Street Fight) ******************************************************* Irish Street Fight for the VXW Tag Team Titles Hot Property vs. The O'Malley Brothers ******************************************************* For the first time ever in Vegas history, the fans were about to witness a match where no pinfall, submission OR disqualification would determine a victor. Instead, the victor(s) would be determined based upon a point structure. In a match that seems to be right up the O'Malley Brothers alley, the rules of the match were simple: No countouts and No Disqualifications. For every weapon shot a person lands, that respective tag team gets 1 point. For every time a person is drove through a table, that respective team earns 3 points. If one tag team partner accidentally hits the other with a weapon, 1 point is deducted. First team to 10 points win the VXW Tag Team Titles, but you must win by at least 2 points. The referee in the ring is only responsible for keeping score.....that's it. But going into this match, there was alot of things weighing on the mind of Marcus O'Malley, particularly his girlfriend Jessica Jennings, who along with older brother Adam, is still mourning the loss of her father over the weekend. But unfortunately, one constant thing that's said in times like this is that 'the show must go on.' With that said, Hot Property (minus their "injured" manager Chris Wheeling) entered the ring first. Stevie Knight, as always, was proud to show off his half of the VXW Tag Team Titles. His partner however, Austin Lee, acted as if he was naked in front of a couple thousands screaming fans as they made their way to the ring. The fans were obviously behind The O'Malleys, who entered last. Holding his half of the VXW Tag Team Titles high in the air, Anthony O'Malley had a smile that spread ear to ear. His older brother, Marcus, followed close behind pushing a wheelbarrow full of weapons ringside. The brothers climbed in the ring, but Marcus immediately found himself back outside the ring and flat on his back, thanks to a swift superkick by Lee. This left young Anthony alone to recieve numerous double team attacks from Knight and Lee as they seemed to use EVERY dirty trick in the book without using a single weapon. Anthony soon found the courage within himself to fight back, eventually culminating in a pair of clotheslines that sent both Knight and Lee over the top rope and out of the ring near where Marcus was slowly getting to his feet. Without missing a step, he reached into the wheelbarrow full of goodies and pulled out a staple gun. After catching a quick breather, Knight dragged Marcus back in the ring and sandwiched him inbetween the ladder, as Lee went up top. With flashbulbs popping, Lee executed IT'S ALL OVER....NOW!!! on the sandwiched "Assassin", trimming the lead back down to 6-5. Knight grabbed the trusty kendo stick of Marcus as Lee stood Marcus up. Knight practiced his batting swing...... ........and hit Lee instead!!!! Doing so caused Hot Property to be deducted a point, putting the score back down to 6-4. As Knight stood over Lee apologizing, Marcus staggered out of nowhere and nailed Knight with a desperation KISS OF DEATH!!!! Enter Chris Wheeling, neck brace and all......with a ROLL OF BARBED WIRE!?!?! He tried to escape in time, but he was caught by Anthony!!! With a firm grip on the screaming and pleading Wheeling's neck brace, Anthony raised his fist to deck him, but Wheeling kicked him in the balls, making it twice that Anthony had suffered Blunt Testicular Trauma tonight. Marcus saw this and ran to his aid...... ......but he was cut off by Thunder Valenkov, who drilled Marcus out of nowhere with a Thunder Bomb on the Barbed Wire, making the score again 6-5!!!! As Thunder vanished through the crowd, Wheeling stayed at ringside to gloat and cheer. Suddenly, the fans got on the edge of their seats once again as this time, Jessica Jennings ran to ringside to confront Wheeling. But Wheeling made a bad error in judgement when he KISSED JESSICA JENNINGS!!!! Back in the ring, Marcus placed a 'YIELD' sign over the face of Lee as Anthony leg dropped the sign into the face of Lee making the score 7-5. Knight immediately crawled out of the ring and grabbed the one thing everyone wanted to see.....A TABLE!!!!! Matter of fact, Lee was trying to set him up for a Property Boom through the table!!! Just as Knight and Lee got him in position to 'Drop The Boom'....... .......Marcus came out of nowhere to spear Knight, as Anthony countered with a Headscissor, just BARELY missing the table!!!! With Knight and Lee not moving perhaps from physical exhaustion, the lights when out!!!! The fans began popping LOUD as they sensed an appearance by Kevin Simmons. One of the thugs quickly grabbed the steel chair and drilled both Marcus AND Anthony with nasty chairshots, giving Hot Property the lead at 10-9. But since you had to win by 2 points or more, the other thug saw the unblemished and unfolded table in the middle of the ring. He immediately jerked Marcus up off the mat and the two thugs hoisted him up and DROVE him viciously through the table with a crude double slam, giving Hot Property the win and BOTH VXW Tag Team Titles! Winner (and ONCE AGAIN VXW Tag Team Champions): Hot Property via a 12-9 score.
