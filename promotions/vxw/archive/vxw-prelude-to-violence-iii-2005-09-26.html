Prelude To Violence III

Sep 26, 2005

(Balls To The Wall hits and the screen comes alive with past VXW action # The trio of Jason Storm, Randall Crane and Ric Beauty in the ring # # All the members of The Rhombus from past action # # Robbie Caldwell and Kenny Kidd in front of the Riders banner # # Team Brittania and Purgatory fighting in the ring # # Lark Fenriz hitting the Sammael on The Equalizer # # Adam 'The Hype' Jennings defeating Kevin Blair # # Hot Property standing with their titles # # Mike 'The Biz' Bisignano standing with the TV Title # # A close up of 24 Hour Champion Thunder Valenkov along with Chris Wheeling # CUT to Dan The Man Aller at the desk) DTMA: Welcome to Prelude To Violence Three! Our final show before Vegas Violence. And tonight we had a big time event! The TV Title was on the line, a Rhombus Royal with the losers losing their jobs, but lets start things off with a match that was scheduled just a few days ago. The debut of Team Essington, the highly touted tag team by the new company in sports management, Essington Sports Inc. .. .. .. ========================= TEAM ESSINGTON (vs) JAVIER KING & MATT PAYNE ========================= King and Payne came to the ring first. Matt Payne is the newest rookie in the VXW Academy and this kid is big. Well built and nearly six nine he has the look of the future star. And teaming with King, many think he is the best of the young prospects, they looked like a formidable team. Then the lights went down and spotlights bathed the crowd, as Team Essington made their way to the ring. The fans stood in silence at the strange team. Up on the apron first was the masked luchadore El Santanico Loco. A top star in Mexico, but some have said he is a little past his prime. His partner, a man standing six ten, Buck Wild. They slid into the ring and took apart the rookies. King tried to fight off El Satanico and Payne used his fist but Buck Wild was able to overpower him. Wild tossed Payne out of the ring, and then he and ELS double teamed King. The ref got ELS out of the ring and the big man lifted King over his head, and turned it into a BACKBREAKER!! Then he tagged in El Satanico Loco. Wild got up on the second rope and ELS stood on his shoulders then dove off with the Essington End Somersault Frog Splash for the ONE!! TWO!!! THREEE!!!! ((((( WINNERS = Team Essington ))))) An impressive debut for this strange team. A luchadore satan and a guy from the backwoods of West Virginia. On Prelude To Violence II a rookie scored a count out win over Lark Fenriz, after Thunder Valenkov walked out to the ring. They had a rematch tonight. .. .. .. ======================= LARK FENRIZ (vs) CARL 'THE COBRA' SMITH ======================= This was hard to watch at times. Smith, who came to the ring looking very confident and even tried to start a Cobra chant, was quickly knocked off his feet by the charging Fenriz. The former Heavyweight Champion then beat Smith senseless. Fenriz was hitting the kid with everything he had. Fenriz then hit the SAMMAEL and made the cover ONE!! TWO!!!! BUT FENRIZ PULLED SMITH UP!!! Lark then hit the youngster with a T-BONE SUPLEX. Fenriz climbed up to the top rope, not a place he normally goes, and came off with a DEVASTATING KNEE DROP!!! Then Fenriz picked him up and signaled for another SAMMAEL!! Johnny 'Hands Of' Stone, lead teacher of the VXW academy, rushed to ringside and tossed a white towel in the ring. The referee called for the bell and Stone got in the ring and pleaded for Fenriz not to hit his powerful finisher on Smith for a second time. Fenriz, eyes wild with rage, seemed on the verge of hitting the move but he dropped Smith to the mat and walked off. ((((( WINNER = Lark Fenriz ))))) .. .. .. .. .. ((((( COMMERCIALS ))))) .. .. .. .. .. (Backstage, Lark Fenriz comes out of Rod Beautys office. Fenriz nearly rips off the door as he leaves and the camera moves in the room to see a sweaty and scared look on the face of the 49% owner of the VXW. Then, Mr Electricity Robbie Caldwell and Kenny Kidd walk in the room) CALDWELL: We need to talk chief! ROD BEAUTY: What about? KIDD: Vegas Violence is coming up and I don't have a match. How can that be? Hell Rod, most of the people out there tonight came to see me, back in action once again! WHOO! CALDWELL: I think Kenny here deserves a shot at Valenkov! KIDD: Hell yeah! ROD BEAUTY: The winner of the Rhombus Royal gets the title shot. CALDWELL: Look Rod, you gotta give Kenny a match. I need him on the card at Vegas Violence. KIDD: Come on Rod, give me anybody. I'll beat the hell out of any man on the rost.. (Lark Fenriz walks back in the room) ROD BEAUTY: Lark.. er.. Mr. Fenriz. I have been thinking about what you asked.. umm... for a match at Vegas Violence and I know just the man to give you a great battle. KIDD: (wildly shaking his head NO!!!) CALDWELL: (trying to get Rod to shut up) ROD BEAUTY: Kenny Kidd here was just.. (Fenriz turns to Kidd and looks him up and down, then he nods and walks out of the room) CALDWELL: What the hell were you thinking Rod? ROD BEAUTYS: It's done. Fenriz versus Kidd at Vegas Violence. KIDD: Oh God. (fade) .. .. .. (CUT back to DTMA) DTMA: So Lark Fenriz will take on Kenny Kidd at Vegas Violence. That should be a good match, both Kidd and Fenriz are back in the VXW after some time off. Fenriz took some time and Kidd was injured, so it should be a good match for both men. Ok, who are we kidding, Fenriz might kill Kidd, but keep this in mind folks, you can see it on October 4! Also at Vegas Violence the continuing saga over the tag team titles continues as Hot Property defends against Team Brittania and Purgatory. But tonight we had one member from each team in a triple threat match. .. .. .. ======================= HOTSTUFF STEVIE KNIGHT (vs) JUSTIN DAVIDSON (vs) SYNN ======================= The other members of the respective teams were barred from ringside so fans were hoping for a fair fight. Knight began exchanging words with Davidson just as the bell rang. Synn took a chance and charged both men but they got out of the way and Synn went chest first into the turnbuckle. Knight hit him with a NECKBREAKER as he came back out only to get a SAVATE KICK from Davidson during his KIP UP! Davidson used the ropes to CHOKE Knight until the ref called for the break. Justin turned to Synn who was back on his feet, but Synn caught him coming off the ropes and hit him with a FALLAWAY SLAM!! Synn rolled Knight out of the ring to the floor and sent Davidson to the ropes, hitting a BACK BODY DROP to the Team Brittania member. Synn followed up with a LEGDROP and a cover ONE!!! TWO!!!!! BUT KNIGHT BROKE IT UP!! Hotstuff came over the top rope and hit a PLANCHA onto the pin attempt. Knight then went off the ropes and DROPKICKED Synn to the mat. Then he hit a series of three SNAP SUPLEXES on Justin Davidson. But instead of going for the pin, he stood in the center of the ring with his arms outstretched soaking in the fans reaction. That got him a FOREARM to the back of the head from Synn and a DDT as well. Justin Davidson pulled himself up in the corner and moved out of the way of a charging Synn, making Synn 0-2 on the night for corner charges. Davidson turned it into a SUNSET FLIP and got a ONE!!! TWO!!!!! BEFORE SYNN KICKED OUT!!!! Justin got Synn with a SPINNING KICK and then leaped up and hit him with a STANDING MOONSAULT. But he couldn't make the cover due to Knight making his way over. Justin hit a DROP TOE HOLD that sent Knight face first into the second turnbuckle. Justin Davidson was now in control of the match, or as much control as you can get in a triple threat. He picked up Knight and tried to slam him on top of Synn but the Purgatory member rolled out of the way. Justin went over and got an EYE POKE from Synn. Synn then picked him up and POWERBOMBED Justin Davidson in the center of the ring. Synn was standing over Justin when Knight DROPKICKED him out of the ring! Hotstuff then pointed to the top rope and he climbed up to hit his HEATSEEKER splash. But, as he usually does, he spent to much time up there enjoying the scenery and Synn was able to push him off, and Knight landed hard in the center of the ring. Justin Davidson looked to hit Knight with his flipping piledriver aka THE ALBION SMASHER but Synn broke it up and sent Justin to the outside of the ring over the top rope. Synn then hit a JUMPING DDT on Knight and made the cover ONE!!! TWO!!!!! (And Justin was just so close to breaking up the fall) THREE!!!!!!!!!!!! ((((( WINNER = Synn ))))) So a bit of a surprise as Synn wins the triple threat and maybe gives Purgatory a bit of a psychological advantage going in to the Tag Team Title match at Vegas Violence. That's when Blackbird made her way to the ring, in an unscheduled appearance. .. .. .. ================= BLACKBIRD SPEAKS ================= (Blackbird climbs into the ring and slowly lifts the mic to his red lips) BLACKBIRD: So my monster is gone. Tai Angela shall not wrestle again. Blame! It rests on the shoulders of Iyasu Saku. The man who is a traitor to me. The man who turned his back on me. The man who will be cry the tears of defeat. Iyasu Saku, this I promise you. You will feel the pain that my monster feels, you will feel the hurt that I feel. You will not walk out of Vegas Violence Iyasu Saku. This I promise you. (She places the mic on the mat and walks out of the ring) .. .. .. (CUT back to DTMA) DTMA: Well, Iyasu Saku is scheduled to meet one of the VXW Academy students at Vegas Violence, and it looks like Blackbird will be there for that match. The four men in our tag team match each have big time matches at Vegas Violence. Jennings against Caldwell, Harper will meet Bass and both of those will be cage matches and Kenny Kidd will stand face to face with Lark Fenriz. But earlier tonight they all four met in the ring with one thing on their minds, except Caldwell, who was mentally not in the match at all. .. .. .. ============================================ ADAM 'THE HYPE' JENNINGS & JUSTIN HARPER (vs) MR ELECTRICITY ROBBIE CALDWELL & KENNY KIDD ============================================ Jessica Jennings accompanied Jennings and Harper to the ring and that took Caldwell right out of the match. The lovelorn Caldwell sent Kidd in the ring against Jennings as he made eyes and blew kisses at Jessica Jennings. In the ring Kenny Kidd had taken control of the match and had Adam Jennings on the mat and in a REAR NAKE CHOKE after they had deon some nice chain wrestling. Kidd was trying to get the attention of Caldwell to they could make a tag, but Caldwel was leaning around the ring post trying to get a good look at Jessica. Kidd broke the hold and came over and tagged in Caldwell. Mr Electricity smiled at Jessica then rushed into the ring, only to be on the wrong end of a SERIES OF ARM DRAGS then a couple of DROPKICKS, and Caldwell slid out of the ring to regroup. During his regroup, Justin Harper came over and tossed him back in the ring. Jennings picked him up for a slam, but Caldwell slid down his back and hit him with a MODIFIED BACKBREAKER!! Caldwell leaped to his feet and smiled yet again at Jessica Jennings. Caldwell then used a RUNNING HIGH KNEE on Jennings and he made the tag to Kidd. They hit Jennings with a DOUBLE CLOTHESLINE and Kidd made the cover. ONE!!! Jennings kicked out!! Kenny Kidd sent Jennings to the ropes and got a KICK IN THE FACE when he went for the BACK BODY DROP!! Jennings used a SNAP MARE and a KICK TO THE BACK OF THE HEAD before tagging in to Justin Harper. Harper blew over and knocked the googly eyes Caldwell off the apron and then nailed Kidd with a BULLDOG!! Harper came off the ropes and DROPKICKED Kidd who stumbled back into the corner. Harper went over and got up on the second rope and the fans counted to TEN as he punched Kidd. Harper then MONKEY FLIPPED Kidd out of the corner!! Harper rushed him, but was hit with a ROARING ELBOW!! Kidd had turned the tide and tagged in Robbie Caldwell. Caldwell went over and knocked Jennings off the apron and then turned to Harper. He sent Harper to the ropes and hit him with a TILT A WHIRL BACKBREAKER!!! A signature move he learned from Sebastian. Caldwell was on top of the world. He got out of the ring and began to use some of his best lines on Jessica Jennings. Much to the shock of the crowd she was smiling right back at Mr Electricity and motioning him to come closer with her finger. Then. . . KICK TO MR ELECTRICITY'S POWER PLANTS!!!! Caldwell turned and Adam Jennings hit him with the HYPER DRIVER!!!! Jennings rolled Caldwell into the ring, where Harper made the cover. ONE!!! TWO!!!!! THREEEEE!!!!!!!! ((((( WINNERS = Adam 'The Hype' Jennings & Justin Harper ))))) Kidd tried to make the save but Jennings was able to cut him off and Adam Jennings and Justin Harper pick up the win. .. .. .. .. .. ((((( COMMERCIALS, including this one))))) [A still shot on a tombstone, a voice comes in.] V/O: They thought it was dead and buried. [Zoom into the tombstone, showing Wrestling International League of Destruction.] V/O: The controversy was what was supposed to kill them. [Camera shakes a little.] V/O: But no one...saw this coming. [Camera shakes violently as dirt flies everywhere turning into clips of some WILD: Wrestling Unleashed superstars.] V/O: Now, they're taking no prisoners in an effort to REVOLUTIONIZE pro-wrestling once again. [Camera stops on a metallic crimson and silver logo.] V/O: It's WILD: WRESTLING UNLEASHED. Join us for Inferno One Point One, Set it Off. [Fade.] .. .. .. .. .. (Back in Rod Beautys office, he is now glaring at the screen which just showed a commercial for WILD) ROD BEAUTY: WHAT THE F***???? They tell me I can't use Morris Bass because of some no compete clause, then they come into my building and threaten me and my people with that Neanderthal Omega and now they have commercials on my show??? (picks up the phone) ROD BEAUTY: Is this the advertising department? Good, this is Rod Beauty. You are ALL FIRED!!! (fade) .. .. .. (CUT back to DTMA) DTMA: Well if you know anyone with some advertising sales skills tell them to give the VXW a call, because Rod Beauty has fired the whole advertising staff of the VXW. Rod Beauty has been on a tear lately, and the advertising staff are not the only people he was looking to pink slip. Losers of the next match will be in the unemployment line. .. .. .. =================== THE RHOMBUS RUMBLE =================== The Maquilladora, CAPSMAN, EVIL CAPSMAN, Sonicman, Dennis Franz and even Yakabi were all in the ring. The rules were explained, over the top rope and touch the floor and you lose your job. Last man in gets a 24 Hour Title shot at Vegas Violence against Thunder Valenkov. EVIL CAPSMAN was the first to strike, going right after Yakabi. This brought Maq and Sonic in to make the save. Franz made a move on CAPSMAN but CAPS sidestepped him and tossed him out of the ring. ((( Dennis Franz has been eliminated ))) Maq and Sonicman got EVIL C off Yakabi and they began a double team beatdown of him. This caused CAPS to rush in and help his former partner EVIL C and in a flash the match became a tag team soft of battle with Maq and Sonic taking on CAPSMEN! while Yakabi looked on from the corner. A double CAPSFACTOR to Soincman had him in trouble, but when they looked to toss him out The Maquilladora, who had been on the end of an earlier DDT, got to his feet and rushed over and DROPKICKED both men to the floor! ((( EVIL CAPSMAN has been eliminated ))) ((( CAPSMAN has been eliminated ))) Sonic got to his feet and offered his hand to The Maquilladora, Maq extended his arm and got a BOOT to the GUT and then a PILEDRIVER!!! Sonic then chased around Yakabi until he finally caught him and tossed him over the top rope and to the floor! ((( Yakabi has been eliminated ))) Sonicman and The Maquilladora have been at odds many times over the control of The Rhombus so it was fitting it came down to these two men. Sonic picked up Maq and used some UPPERCUTS to drive him back to the ropes, then an irish whip and a beautifully executed HURRICANRANA!! Sonic then climbed to the top rope and went for a TWISTING ELBOW DROP!! And he hit it!! Sonic then pulled Maq to his feet and took him over the dump him over the ropes. And he did! But The Maquilladora landed on his feet on the ring apron, Sonic went for a SHOULERBLOCK to knock Maq off the apron but Maq flipped over Sonic and landed behind him. Sonicman turned and got his with those FEET OF FURY!!! That series of fast kicks Maq uses had Sonicman on dream street. The Maquilladora then came off the ropes and hit Sonicman with a SPINNING HEEL KICK that sent him over the top rope and to the floor! ((( Sonicman has been eliminated ))) And The Maquilladora still has a job, as long as he defeats Thunder Valenkov at Vegas Violence. ((((( WINNER = The Maquilladora ))))) As Maq was walking up the ramp, Valenkov came out and nailed the masked fan favorite with a CLOTHESLINE!! Valenkov then began to stomp on The Maquilladora. The big champion then picked him up, clearly to hit the THUNDER BOMB off the stage!! But Lark Fenriz came out and clobbered Valenkov, breaking up the move before Valenkov could hit it. Fenriz and Valenkov began to exchange punches until security came out and pulled the two men apart. That's when Kenny Kidd hit the scene, with Fenriz being held by five security guards Kidd came out and BLASTED Fenriz with his COW BELL!!! It split open Fenriz and dropped him to one knee before Kidd hit him again, and then again. More VXW officials came out and got Kidd out of there but the damage to Fenriz was apparent. Lark was able to walk out on his own but was bleeding very badly. And that is a great segue into the final match of the night. A mysterious masked man has been leaving TV Champion Mike Bisignano on the floor almost each week. Starting way back during The Biz's first match with then TV Champion Valec Corso. The Biz will meet this Masked Invader at Vegas Violence, but on this night he would have to defend against The Surfer Blake Hamilton. .. .. .. ============================== Spin The Wheel TV Title Match MIKE 'THE BIZ' BISIGNANO © (vs) THE SURFER BLAKE HAMILTON ============================== Well the referee for the match was John Lyon, the man many people think is getting pay offs from Hamilton and Voltar. Lyon had a big smile for The Biz and it was obvious that something was up. Then The Biz spun the wheel and it landed on a No Disqualification Match! The Biz slid in the ring and POUNDED THE REFEREE!!! Hamilton looked on stunned as The Biz pummeled John Lyon and they was nothing Lyon could do about it. Hamilton came over to break it up but he got a PUNCH IN THE MOUTH and The Biz went back to beating up the referee!! The Biz ended his business with the referee with a SPINNING HEEL KICK!! The champ then went outside the ring and grabbed a chair. That brought The Big V Lance Voltar charging to the ring! But he was cut off from ever getting to the ring by VXW Academy students Joey Young and Tom Rogers!! Young and Rogers took down Voltar and The Biz began to batter Blake Hamilton with the chair! <<< CLANG!! >>> And then again! <<< CLANG!! >>> The Biz then sent Hamilton to the ropes and hit a DROP TOE HOLD that sent Hamilton face first into the chair and further split him wide open! Bisignano then pulled up referee John Lyon and told him this match was just about over! The Biz climbed to the top rope and hit THE DELMAR DIVE!!! Then he jumped to his feet and told John Lyon that if he didn't count the first beating would feel like a day at the beach! The Biz made the cover.. ONE!! TWO!!!! THREEEEEEE!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! ((((( WINNER = Mike 'The Biz' Bisignano ))))) But then once again, out from the crowd came the Masked Invader! He slid in the ring behind The Biz, grabbed the chair from the floor and <<< CLANG!!! >>> <<< CLANG!!! >>> Two chair shots and The Biz was out on the mat, bleeding from his head. Young and Rogers slid in the ring and the Masked Invader slid out, laughing. >From where I was the sound of that laugh seemed very familiar. Well folks, that's all for tonight.
