VXW — Show

Prelude To Violence I

Sep 6, 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Balls To The Wall by Accept hits over a dark screen. In one second flashes we see some highlights of past VXW action The Maquilladora pinning VXW 49% owner Rod Beauty Iyasu Saku and Tai Angela waiting in the ring Hot Property and Team Brittania fighting it out Mike The Biz Bisignano holding up the TV Title Thunder Valenkov hitting a slam on Robbie Caldwell CUT to Dan The Man Aller) DTMA: Welcome to Prelude To Violence! We are marching forward toward Vegas Violence V! Can you believe it? The show that really kicked off the VXW, back when we were called the VWF. Tonight things get heated up as one man will make his own match for Vegas Violence. Eight men will fight an Octogon Match and we also have the Tag Team and 24 Hour Titles on the line in a huge show! The Octogon Match works like this, eight men come to the ring and are randomly paired off in the outer four rings. Score a fall in one of the outer rings and you move to the center ring. First man to score a fall in the center wins the match and the right to make any match he wants for Vegas Violence. After the 20 minutes it took to get all the men to the ring, the lovely Lean Darlene Orion pulled names from a hat. In Ring 1 it would be ADAM THE HYPE JENNINGS vs ROBBIE CALDWELL! In Ring 2 it would be THE OUTLAW MORRIS BASS vs DARK MONSTER TAI ANGELA! In Ring 3 it would be KAGEDO vs VALEC CORSO And in Ring 4 IYASU SAKU and JUSTIN HARPER Lets see how it all went down! .. .. .. ================== THE OCTOGON MATCH ================== Jennings and Caldwell tore right into each other, their former feuds for the 24 Hour Title now brewing into some kind of hatred for each other. These two have been battling since the beginning of the year in a feud that is now rivaling Crane and CJB from the old days! Corso took the upper hand on Kagedo, who was getting booed more than any man to ever step foot in a VXW ring. Harper had taken down Saku and they were going for a mat match. But the surprise was Bass, who ducked a charging Tai Angela, hit a FLYING CLOTHESLINE as the big man came off the ropes and then set him for and hit the WIDOWMAKER!! For the ONE!!! TWO!!!! THREEE!!!! ((((( The Outlaw Morris Bass moves to the center ring ))))) Bass would have to wait as the rest of the men still fought on. Caldwell and Jennings spilled to the floor, each man ripping, and biting at the other. Harper and Saku dueled move for move. And Corso was working over Kagedo in the corner. The referee was trying to get Jennings and Caldwell back in the ring when both men came up off the ground with chairs and (DOUBLE CLANG!!!) Nailed each other with simultaneous chairshots! The frustrated referee called for the bell and both men were disqualified from the match! Meanwhile Valec Corso hit Kagedo with a POWERBOMB out of the corner and made the cover, but Kags was able to kick out at two. Justin Harper came off the ropes, but was caught by Saku who hit a SHOULDERBREAKER on Harper. As Saku moved in for the kill, Blackbird jumped up on the apron. She got the attention of the referee, and Tai Angela snuck in behind Saku and hit him with the END OF THE LINE heart punch! Angela rolled out and Blackbird got off the apron. Corso sent Kagedo to the ropes and hit a BACK BODY DROP and followed that with a FIST DROP from the second rope. Harper regained his composure and shook off the sting in his arm and locked the unconscious Saku in the CEDAR RAPIDS DEATHLOCK!! As that referee was checking on Saku, Kagedo ducked a Corso CLOTHESLINE and used a CRUCIFIX SLIDE to get the pinfall!! ((((( Kagedo moves to the center ring ))))) And then Saku was eliminated when his hand fell three times while in the Deathlock! ((((( Justin Harper moves to the center ring ))))) This left Harper in the ring against the two men who have voved to end the SOD. Jennings was getting pulled apart from Robbie Caldwell by VXW security, Harper was getting double teamed by Kagedo and Bass. Bass and Kagedo were more focused on beating Harper than winning the match. As the minutes passed it was clear that Harper was having more and more trouble mounting a comeback. Each time he would show a flash of fire, The Outlaw and The Dragon would put it out. Bass would hit a HUGE CLOTHESLINE, SLAM or SUPLEX and then Kagedo would come off the ropes with a SENTON, PLANCHA or a KICK of some kind. Justin Harper was slowing getting brutalized! With the fans screaming for someone to come save the now bloody Harper, Kagedo walked over and took a seat in the corner. He and Bass exchanged smirks as The Outlaw gave the thumbs down signal to the crowd to finally bring this to an end. But as Bass went to pick up Harper, Harper caught him with a LOW BLOW and applied the CEDAR RAPDIS DEATHLOCK!! Kagedo did not move, even shouting to the ringside announcers that Bass would be able to slip out of the hold. But The Outlaw was not going anywhere and his hand was going up in the air! Kagedo came out of the corner and broke up the hold. The Outlaw rolled out of the ring and to the floor and Kagedo picked up Harper and hit him with a SLEEPER DROP. Kagedo then ended the match himself with the ENDGAME for the ONE!! TWO!!! THREE!!!!!! ((((( WINNER = Kagedo ))))) A mic was handed to Kagedo and he was asked who he wanted to face at Vegas Violence. The fans began a loud SIMMONS! SIMMONS! chant. Kags let them cheer and chant for a while until he said the name with a smile. (SHOCK POP!!!) .. . . ANTHONY O'MALLEY!!! (Fade to commercial with Kagedo standing in the center of the ring) .. .. .. .. .. ((((( COMMERCIALS ))))) .. .. .. .. .. (Backstage, we find the lovely Renee Anderson backstage with a certain irate individual, who is furiously pacing back and forth....) RA: Ladies and Gentlemen, I'm backstage here with.... (The irate individual, who turns out to be "The Outlaw" Morris Bass, stops dead in his tracks and spins around to where he's facing Miss Anderson. He rips the microphone out of her hands and violently screams at her.) BASS: GET THE HELL OUT OF HERE YOU IGNORANT BITCH!!! (Obviously scared and trembling with fear, Renee Anderson slowly and quietly exits off to the side as Bass turns his attention to the camera.) BASS: Justin Harper...you annoying little pissant. It's very obvious that you have decided to be NOTHING but a royal pain in my ass. You chose to steal my destiny....MY DESTINY....of winning the 2005 Dog Days Tournament. You chose to rear your ugly head in a situation that didn't involve you in the first place.....you chose to align yourself with the VERY same faction that is at war with Kagedo and I. (Bass chuckles somewhat psychotic with a half smile on his face.) BASS: And now.....now.....you chose to take away my opportunity to headline Vegas Violence and defeat Thunder Valkenov for the VXW 24-Hour Championship. My chance to drink from that silver chalice.....and once again, you took it away from me. YOU TOOK IT ALL!!! (The camera zooms in on the angered look of Bass.) BASS: Use all the sayings you want......Lightning doesn't strike twice.....Slap me once shame on you, slap me twice shame on me.....I DON'T CARE!!! (Bass stops momentarily to take a deep breath.) BASS: However you choose to say it, it won't happen a third time. I'm going to Vegas Violence.....and so are you. And at Vegas Violence, whether you want to do not, we're gonna settle our differences. And we're gonna do it inside the harsh confines of a Steel Cage!!!! (SHOCK POP GOES THROUGHOUT THE ARENA.) BASS: I vow to make your life a living hell inside that unforgiving steel. I will make you bleed. I will make you suffer. I will beat you within that very inch of your life. I will force you so close to the point that you can see Mark McQueen himself....... (MAMMOTH BOOS ECHO THROUGHOUT THE ARENA.) BASS: And when you reach out to touch him, that's when I will stop and bring you back into reality. Cause I want you to be able to witness something that McQueen will NEVER be able to do.....and that is Kagedo and I standing atop the rubble that WAS the Society of Doom.....feasting on the spoils of our defeated enemies. And then what, Justin? Was robbing me of that Dog Days Trophy or taking away my chance to be VXW 24-Hour Champion been worth all the suffering and agony you will suffer? Was being a martyr for the SoD worth the price of your life? It's time to pay the piper.....except this time, you can't afford it. (Bass slams the mic down and walks away.) .. .. .. (CUT back to DTMA) DTMA: Bass has put out the challenge to Justin Harper. Speaking of challenges, Rod and Rob Beauty have decided that Adam Jennings and Robbie Caldwell will meet at Vegas Violence, the stipulations of that match are yet to be determined but the match has been signed! Well our final two matches were the result of a match at Birthday Bash. Rod Beauty (49% owner of the VXW) had challenged The Maquilladora to a match. If Rod had won, Maq and his Rhombus friends would have been fired from the VXW. But Maq won the match and secured title matches for the Rhombus. Those matches took place earlier tonight. .. .. .. ========================== VXW Tag Team Title Match HOT PROPERTY © (vs) THE RHOMBUS ========================== Rod Beauty came to ringside, to 'watch' the battle to make sure it was fair. Stevie Knight started this match off with the always strange but popular CAPSMAN. Knight took the brunt of the punishment for the early moments until he was able to reverse a GERMAN SUPLEX attempt into a GERMAN RELEASE of his own. >From there Knight and Lee isolated CAPSMAN and began the double team on CAPS. Sonicman tried to rally his partner but each time CAPSMAN made a move, either Knight or Lee was able to get him just before he could make the tag. Knight and Lee hit a DOUBLE BACK SUPLEX and made the cover for the ONE!!! TWO!!!!!! SAVE BY SONICMAN!!!! Sonic clocked Lee on the back of the head and then nailed Knight with a SAVATE KICK. Sonic went back to his corner and CAPSMAN, leaped to his feet, posed, and then tagged in Sonicman! Sonic cleaned house on Lee and then took Knight to task as he tried to get in on some of the action. The former tag team champions suddenly had the momentum and Sonicman hit Lee with a FLYING CLOTHESLINE and made the cover for the ONE!!! TWO!!!! (NEW CHAMPS GASP!!) LEE KICKED OUT!!! Sonicman then set up Lee for THE CLOSE SHAVE, but Lee used a BACK BODY DROP to get out of the DDT finisher of Sonicman. Lee tagged in Knight and Hotstuff was able to capitalize for his team. Once again the two on one double team beatdown was in order. After four minutes of pure Hot Property beating, Sonicman came up off the canvas, after a DOUBLE BACK BODY DROP, and hit both men with DROPKICKS!! He was clearly going to be able to make the tag when Rod Beauty left his seat and pulled CAPSMAN off the ring apron! When Sonicman looked up there was no one to tag! Sonic turned and got hit with the PROPERTY BOOM. ONE!!!! TWO!!!!!!! THREEE!!!!!!!!!!!! ((((( WINNERS = Hot Property ))))) .. .. .. .. .. ((((( COMMERCIALS ))))) .. .. .. .. .. [Through the curtains from the backstage comes a man in his late 40's wearing a black business suit and sunglasses. He is carrying a briefcase in his right hand. The crowd doesn't really know what to make of this. He walks down to the ring and up the steps in to the ring. He grabs a microphone from the ring announcer and takes off his sunglasses.] Man: For those who don't know who I am, allow me to introduce myself. My name is Michael Sandsbury and I am a representative of the Board of Directors for the wrestling promotion, Wrestling International League of Destruction, better known as WILD. I'm not here to waste anyone's time so let me get down to business. Some of you may be asking just how come someone from WILD would be at a VXW event. Well, I am here on official WILD business to speak with TWO distinct people and I would like them to come down to the ring RIGHT NOW. So as soon as someone can send Rod Beauty and Morris Bass down here, the sooner I will be out of this ring. (O Fortuna hits and the 48% owner of the VXW Rod Beauty walks to the ring with The Outlaw Morris Bass at his side. Both men smirk at Sandsbury as they slowly walk up the ring steps and get in the ring) SANDSBURY: Mr. Beau.. (Beauty grabs the microphone out of Sandsbury's hand and begins to speak) ROD BEAUTY: Hold it right there pal. (an arrogant laugh and his tone becomes sarcastic) I'm not really sure where to begin. Which question should I ask? (Rod looks over the crowd) ROD BEAUTY: Maybe a little history lesson first, for these people. Ladies and gentlemen, Mr. Umm... (Sandsbury tries to speak but with no microphone, he clearly can't be heard) ROD BEAUTY: Whatever. This guy is here because over in the WILD promotion, some of you may have heard of it, it's the show that on here in Vegas at 4 am on Sunday mornings. They decided to let go of Morris Bass. Of course we have The Outlaw under contract here in the VXW. This guy seems to think that The Outlaw had in his contract a no-compete clause. And that we, here at the VXW, violated that clause when The Outlaw appeared at Birthday Bash, is that right so far? (Sandsbury nods) ROD BEAUTY: Then that brings me back to my questions. (Rod turns a bit more serious) Do you really think I give a SH** about WILD? AAAAND (now very serious) Who in the hell are you to be in MY RING? (Violently, Bass rips the mic out of the hands of Rod Beauty, as the cascade of boos engulf the sold out arena.) BASS: You mean.....MY RING????? (Massive heel heat for Bass as he looks all over the arena with a shit eating look on his face, as he focuses his attention back on Sandsbury.) BASS: Micheal Sandsbury.....the undigested piece of corn in the vomit that is WILD. Why would you of all people come to Vegas and ruin both my time and yours?? I got bigger and better things to worry about right now, like the demise of the Society of Doom and making the life of Justin Harper a living hell. (MASSIVE HEEL HEAT CONTINUES.) BASS: And you got better things to worry about other than trying to sue me and the VXW for every penny we have. You should be more concerned with trying to kiss Sid Starr's ass.....keeping that WILD Heavyweight Title of his all nice and shiny, cause it's obvious it's not coming off his waist anytime soon. You should be more concerned about keeping dumbasses with no talent like Larry Gionet off WILD programming so you can get higher ratings. (Bass takes a step closer in Sandsbury's direction.) BASS: And you should be more worried about your stars like Mark Pure, who can't get a damn break in that company cause of all the political bullshit that goes on backstage. You should be more worried about trying to figure out how the hell you're gonna bring all that disorderly conduct and mismanagement to an end. You should be more worried about trying to keep what was once a prestigious promotion from going down the same shithole as BWWA, MSWA, and the CAL. (Cheap pop for yet again, another insult at VXW's former competition across the street, as Bass takes yet another step towards Sandsbury.) BASS: You, of all people, should know for a fact that I am not a follower. Never have. Never will. I do my own decision making. So it would be best for you....and your company...to get the hell out of Vegas. Or else, both YOU and WILD will regret it. (Bass shoves the microphone into the chest of Sandsbury. He catches his breath and begins to speak) SANDSBURY: I should've known that this wouldn't be easy. I guess i'll just have to switch to "Plan B". (Sandsbury exits the ring and heads up the aisle. Beauty and Bass just look at him retreating and laugh to themselves. Halfway up the aisle, Sandsbury stops and turns back to the ring.) SANDSBURY: Oh yea...I almost forgot. SAY HELLO TO PLAN B...AS IN THE BIZ! (Out of nowhere, the VXW Television champ, Mike `The Biz' Bisignano runs through the crowd and jumps the railing and slides into the ring. He hits Rod Beauty with a spinning leg lariat that sends the co-owner to the mat. He then runs the ropes and goes to hit Morris Bass with a flying dropkick but Bass catches him and puts him down with a fallaway slam. ) (Without any hesitations, Bass picks up the crumpled heap that was Mike Bisignano and prepare him for a Widowmaker. As Bass picks The Biz up, he stares directly into the eyes of Mr. Sandsbury, who is staggering up the ramp in shock and disbelief. Bass points a large bony finger at Sandsbury, then violently drops Biz on his head with the patented Sit-Down Tombstone Piledriver of Morris Bass.) (Immediately, Mr. Sandsbury spins around and starts running up the aisle, as both Bass and Rod Beauty leave the ring together to a mixture of cheers and boos.) .. .. .. (CUT to DTMA) DTMA: For those might not know, Morris Bass was under contract with WILD, another federation. Bass was let go from WILD but it looks like they want to enforce that no-compete clause. The Outlaw is surrounded by controversy no matter where he is or what he does. He has helped Kagedo with the SOD, he and Justin Harper are at odds and now he has WILD and The Biz to worry about. Our last match of the night was The Maquilladora's turn at his title shot. Maq held the old heavyweight title and had classic battles with Classy Joey Boy. But could Maq stand in the same ring as the monster VXW 24 Hour Champion Thunder Valenkov? .. .. .. ========================= VXW 24 Hour Title Match THUNDER VALENKOV © (vs) THE MAQUILLADORA ========================= Rod Beauty (49% owner of the VXW) was still at ringside and he and Maq had words before the bell, this gave Valenkov a chance to jump The Maquilladora from behind and get the early edge in the match. Valenkov used his power to slowly beat down Maq with FOREARMS, CHOPS and some stiff punches. But The Maquilladora would not be held down and mounted a comeback of his own with some CHOPS and a SERIES OF THREE FLYING SHOULDER BLOCKS, the last one of which took Valenkov to the mat. Maq made the cover but Valenkov kicked out easily. The Maquilladora then went to work on the arm of the big man, looking to weaken a limb. Maq was focused on hurting the right arm of the big man. Valenkov pushed Maq back into the corner and whipped him into the opposite corner, but Valenkov missed the charging corner splash and Maq got him in a DEEP ARM DRAG then ARM BAR and was once again working on the arm of the champion. This went on for the next few minutes, Valenkov coming back and Maq going right back to the arm. Valenkov, at the urging of Chris Wheeling who was at ringside, finally reversed into an ARM DRAG of his own and then a SNAP NECKBREAKER on the challenger. With the tide turning the champion hit a devasting BACKBREAKER on The Maquilladora and then came off the ropes for a HIGH FLYING KNEE DROP and then the cover ONE!!! TWO!!!!!! KICKOUT BY THE MAQUILLADORA!!!!! Valenkov then placed Maq between his legs for the THUNDER BOMB, but Maq grabbed his legs for the takedown and turned it into a BOSTON CRAB!!! This brought Hot Property to ringside. And right behind them were Sonicman and CAPSMAN! The four men began to brawl on the outside of the ring and the referee scrambled to the outside to try and regain some order. Austin Lee had hit CAPSMAN with a SCOOP SLAM and climbed up on the ring apron when Maq broke the BOSTON CRAB and went over and stopped him. Sonicman tossed Knight into the crowd, Maq held Lees arms and Sonicman got on the apron and HIT A CLOTHESLINE!! But Lee was pulled free by Chris Wheeling and Soincman hit The Maquilladora!!! Maq spun around and into a URANGE SUPLEX by Valenkov who made the cover for the ONE!!! TWO!!!!! THREE!!!!!!!!!!! ((((( WINNER = Thunder Valenkov ))))) Knight came out of the crowd and he and Lee then hit CAPSMAN with the PROPERTY BOOM and then started a double team of Sonicman on the floor. Valenkov, under orders from Chris Wheeling, hit the THUNDER BOMB on The Maquilladora! Lets take a look at what happened next. .. .. .. [Maq lies motionless in the middle of the ring, the big Russian and Rod Beauty standing above him, celebrating. And the crowd is letting the two men know how much they really, really hate them. As the referee hands Thunder his title, the lights suddenly darken and the entire arena falls silent. Strobe lights begin to go off, creating quite a chaotic scene. The fans are glued to their seat in anticipation, as Rod and Thunder look at each other and then stare at the rampway, waiting for something to make some sort of sense.] [And then a voice comes over the PA system, a voice dripping with... evil.] VOICE OVER: Rod... Valenkov... [A wolf's howl can be heard.] VOICE OVER: I have waited in the shadows for long enough to strike. Tonight marks my return... And the ink I shall use? [An evil, bone chilling laugh.] VOICE OVER: _YOUR BLOOD_! [Whoever is speaking lets out another bone chilling laugh that echoes throughout the arena. Rod and Thunder stand in the middle of the ring, staring at the entrance way, waiting for _someone_ to come through. Maq has rolled over to the one corner, trying to figure out exactly where he's at. And then... Right behind Rod and Thunder, straight from out under the ring, comes a figure on some sort of rigged trap door elevator thing. Whoever it is and however it go there, the bottom line is that he is standing three feet behind Rod and Thunder, who have no clue.] Then the man in the trench coat drops the trench coat, revealing his identity as... "THE WOLF" LARK MOTHERFUCKING FENRIZ! This gets a _RESOUNDING_ pop as Rod and Thunder finally turn around, only to get met with stiff left's and right's. Fenriz quickly clotheslines Rod over the top rope, leaving Thunder all alone. He then takes off the championship belt around his waist, and begins whipping Thunder with it. Eventually Thunder makes it out of the ring, taking his title with him. He joins Rod on the rampway, as Fenriz gets a mic, still holding the unknown championship belt in his hands.] LARK FENRIZ: I was the Vegas Wrestling Federation Heavyweight Champion for 196 days. I bled, sweated and busted my ass for that 200 day stretch, reeking havoc and causing chaos the whole way. I left in my tail tucked between my legs because I just ran out of gas. I couldn't take it anymore. But now... [Lark paces around the ring, fans sitting on the edge of their seat,] LARK FENRIZ: But now I see what happens when I leave town. WIth the big draw gone, it's time to push the young talentless hacks to the spotlight, just so somehow the sales balance off. And, just to make sure everything runs smoothly, they attach a little stooge to the talentless hacks side, as a sort of insurance policy to make sure the young punk never fails! [MEGA POP~!] LARK FENRIZ: And I'm not sorry to say, seeing the once illustrious Vegas Wrestling Federation being turned into this three ring circus? It does make my blood boil. It does make me hot under the collar. And it _DOES_ MAKE ME DREAM OF SKINNING YOU BOTH ALIVE! [Even bigger of a face pop. Maq is now too his feet, standing behind Fenriz. Rod and Thunder just stare on, trying hard not to act scared, as the welts from Lark's belt are beginning to form.] LARK FENRIZ: So to appease the demons instead my head, I dusted off _MY_ custom made Vegas Wrestling Federation Heavyweight Championship <Lark holds his title in the air.> and hopped on the next flight to Vegas, vengeance the only thing on my mind. Tonight marks ONLY the beginning sheep... [Lark laughs an evil laugh.] LARK FENRIZ: Just wait to see _ALL_ that I have in store for you! (Fade with the camera on the face of the former Heavyweight Champion) © 2005 Beauty Brothers Production
