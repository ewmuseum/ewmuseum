Sin City Fights February 7

(Backstage, a black limo pulls up in the parking garage as the driver's side door opens. To a tremendous chorus of boos steps out Kevin Simmons and "The Outlaw" Morris Bass. The anger on Bass's face just fills the air as you can just feel it sweeping over your body. Hesitating to do her job, "Lean" Darlene Orion quietly sneaks over to the limo to the two men.) LDO: Gentlemen, what are your thoughts on-- (Bass angrily rips the mic out of Darlene's hands.) BASS: My thoughts?? MY THOUGHTS!?!? Let me tell you what MY thoughts are. (Bass turns and looks at the camera.) BASS: That was nice Adam, real nice. Pretty damn good trump card you played wasn't it? Bringing back an old bag of bones to back you up. (Bass stops for a moment to do some deep breathing in attempts to calm himself down.) BASS: Travis LaGrange.....you should have stayed out of this. This didn't involve you. And this sure as HELL don't concern you. You should know better than anyone else that some things in life....Hell, some PEOPLE are better left off dead...... (Bass just stares deep into the camera lens.) ........and buried. (Bass 'politely' shoves the camera back into the hands of Orion as Bass walks off, leaving Simmons there by the limo.) LDO: So Kevin, what is the strategy now for you and Bass? (Kevin looks at Darlene with a puzzling look on his face.) SIMMONS: What's the strategy? Darlene my dear, you ask that question as if we HAD no strategy. You see, Adam just once again, played right into our hands. ("Lean" Darlene looks at Simmons, obviously not understanding at all as Simmons plays with his tie.) SIMMONS: Remember, Ric Beauty told him nobody within VXW or any former association with the dead SoD. So that automatically narrowed down Adam's choices quite drastically. And besides, everybody knows that Travis's win over Bass in Russia was a fluke and he's been ducking the monster ever since then. So what better way to kill two birds with one stone than to request that stipulation to Ric Beauty in order to get Bass what he's been asking for for a long time??? (Simmons smiles a confident grin.) SIMMONS: It's all about mind games my dear. And nobody does them better than me. Now, if you really want to know what's the next step in our plan, well, let's just say that we're gonna travel down memory lane and play our own little game of "This is Your Life", except in the end, we're not playing with Adam's life (chuckles) oh no. Next week, we're gonna select two people that are close to Adam and force him to make the ultimate decision......which one does he save??? And which one does he sacrifice??? (The crowd boos as Simmons playfully adjusts his tie.) SIMMONS: Now THAT is a mindgame. (Simmons walks away as we go to ringside.) .. .. .. [Sebastian is standing with Miles McKinley and The Dierdorfs] SEBASTIAN: Look, it is the same plan as with The Equalizer. Tonight Sadistic Insanity is Damian ‘Feel The’ Panye! [fade as they all walk away] .. .. .. [Viva Las Vegas hits and the screen blazes with the new VXW logo (thanks Jon)] Sin City Fights had the Sams Town arena jammed packed as fans we excited to se ethe first card after New Years Eve of Destruction. A man who got jumped, hell mugged was in the ring and ready for face PJW, the man who has the plan to change wrestling as we know it. .. .. .. ====================================== THE EQUALIZER [vs] PAUL JOESPH WARREN ====================================== PJW walked out onto the stage and called for a mic. Lets see what he had to say. [PJW paces back and forth, looking at The Equalizer standing in the ring] PJW: Mr. Equalizer, I like these fans watched New Years Eve Of Destruction, I watched as you were brutally assaulted by Sebastian. And I was sad. [PJW wipes a fake tear from his eye to a loud moan from the crowd] PJW: Mr. Equalizer, I know that you are in pain. I know that you are hurt by the actions of Sebastian. You see Mr. Equalizer what Sebastian did is exactly the kind of thing that makes me sick about this great sport. I want to see two men, getting all sweaty and pulling and tugging at each other until one is on TOP and the other is on the BOTTOM! [The statement brings YOU ARE SICK POP from the crowd] PJW: Because of your pain Mr. Equalizer I have decided NOT to face you tonight. Please take the night off, you can thank me later. [With that PJW walks off and the referee shrugs and calls for the bell, and awards the match to The Equalizer via count out] !!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! WINNER = The Equalizer via count out !!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! .. .. .. So PJW gave The Equalizer a gift by letting him have the night off, strange, but it will be a win for The Equalizer. At New Years Eve Of Destruction Roman Anderson and John Manning picked up a big win and got the tag team titles. For much of the past few months The Dierdorfs have been the top tag team in the VXW but have yet to get their shot at the titles. They would get a chance to prove themselves on this night. ****************************************** ROMAN ANDERSON [vs] ONE OF THE DIERDORFS ****************************************** Anderson came to ring with Manning and they waited for the Dierdorfs. As they watched the stage, The Dierdorfs came out of the crowd and jumped the Outcasts from behind! Getting the upper hand, they threw Manning out of the ring and hit a DOUBLE DDT on Manning, then Kris let the referee know he would be the Dierdorf in the match. Kris went to work on Anderson, Kris Dierdorf send him to the ropes and hit a HIP TOSS into an ARM BAR. Dierdorf then used the next minutes of the match to keep Anderson on the mat and wear on his arm. Dierdorf had Anderson in a STANDING ARM BAR when Anderson hit a go behind and nailed Kris with a BELLY TO BACK THROW SUPLEX. Anderson got to his feet and tried to get the blood flowing back in the arm when Brain Dierdorf got up on the ring apron. Manning slid into the ring and dove between the top and second rope taking Brian Dierdorf to the floor. During all this, Diedra gave Kris her riding crop and he went to work on Anderon while the referee was breaking up Manning and Brian. Kris then hit a SLINGSHOT SUPLEX and went for the fall. He got . . ONE!! TWO!!!!!!!! BUT ANDERSON KICKED OUT!!!!!!!!!!!!! Kris then slammed Anderson on the mat, on the back that felt the sting of the riding crop and Kris then locked him in the CAMEL CLUTCH!!!! Roman Anderson reached for the ropes but he was not close enough, then he tried to power out, but he wasn’t able to do that. That’s when Manning got up on the ring apron, distracting the referee. It was to much for Brian and Diedra to resist and they got into the ring to cheap shot Anderson, and when they did Manning turned the referee around. Seeing the three on one attack the referee called for the bell, Manning then got into the ring, and cleaned house using his title belt. !!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! WINNER = Roman Anderson via disqualification !!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! The fans then turned their attention to the video screens to watch a news conference. .. .. .. [A police captain is in front of a podium] POLICE CAPTAIN: I have a short statement and that will be all. Stan Klenski was arrested on murder charges. Mr. Klenski was involved in a bar fight and one of the men in the fight was killed during the altercation. Mr. Klenski is currently in the Clark County Detention Center. We will not accept any questions right now. [the police captain walks off and we return to Sin City Fights] .. .. .. We here at the VXW have looking into this matter to get all the facts. Fans were pumped for the return of the Rodeo Boys. And they gave a huge ovation as they appeared on the screens in the moments before their return match. [Lean Darlene, stands with Kenny Kidd and Billy Bones, both wearing ‘Last Ride’ t-shirts.] KIDD: HOOOOOW DEEEEEEEE! How the hell have ya been Vegas!? [HUGE POP!] BONES: It is damn good to be here Darlene. LDO: You guys said you have a bit of an announcement tonight. . KIDD: Slow down Dar, we’re a getting to it, but we got some business first with POT. BONES: Them boys need to learn a little it bout respect. And we are just the boys to teach ‘em! [The Rodeos show off the t-shirts and head for the ring] .. .. .. [WELCOME BACK!] The place was crazy for the return of the Rodeo Boys and the fans were ready to boo the POT boys. ***************************************** THE RODEO BOYS [vs] PROTECTORS OF TRUTH ***************************************** Fenriz and Foodstamp hit the ring ready for a fight, but the Rodeos were prepared. All four men battled for a bit and then it settled to Foodstamp and Bones in the ring with Bones hitting a STANDING ENZIGUIRI to stun Foodstamp. Bones then hit a SPRINGBOARD REVERSE ELBOW. Bones made the tag and they double slammed Foodstamp and then hit DOUBLE ELBOWS to the chest. Kidd then grabbed Foodstamp by the ankle and pulled him to the center of the ring and locked him in the ELKO LASSO! The anklelock was working, until Fenriz broke it up by clobbering Kidd from behind. Fenriz turned to get hit by a FLYING Billy Bones, who hit Fenriz with a LEG LARIAT! Kidd got to his feet and kicked Fenriz in the midsection and then Bones leapfrogged over and face planted Fenriz. Foodstamp got up and he found himself in a DOUBLE RUSSIAN LEG SWEEP. Fenriz got up and the Rodeo Boys CLOTHESLINED him out of the ring and they climbed to the top rope and both waited for Foodstamp to get to his feet for the .. . . EIGHT SECOND RIDE!!!!!!!!!!! Kidd made the cover ONE!!!!!!! TWO!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! THRREEEEEEEEEEEE!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! !!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! WINNERS = The Rodeo Boys via pinfall !!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! After the match Kidd and Bones asked for the mic. [The Rodeo Boys are in the center of the ring, they look pretty serious] KIDD: Yep folks, this is our last ride. Bronze medals in the Twelve Days tournament and now we are back in the VXW for one last ride. BONES: One last ride, and we figure there is only one way to end that ride. KIDD: We are here to put together the all time tag team match. BONES: Damn right. KIDD: The match everyone wants, the match that never happened. But it needs to happen. BONES: So listen up out there, this is what we want, this is our open challenge. KIDD: One night, Ghost Town Blues maybe. THE RODEO BOYS Against THE O’MALLEYS! Against TEAM BRITTANIA! Against HOT PROPERTY! Yeah we know. Two of those teams are missing in action. But we are here to get the word out. BONES: Damn right Kenny. The four best teams in VXW history, the great wars. Lets do it one last time boys. However ya’ll want to do it. KIDD: Round robin, four way, TLC, it don’t matter. BONES: So there is the Gauntlet boys. Lets see who picks it up! [CUT] .. .. .. .. .. ((((( COMMERCIALS ))))) .. .. .. .. .. Well the fans were buzzing about the challenge set out by the Rodeo Boys. Next up in the ring was Chad Essington, who had a HUUUGE present with him. Lets take a look. [Chad Essington stands in the middle of the ring and he has a big present standing in the ring with him.] ESSINGTON: Can I get my client out here? Ken, come on out here. [Ken Young walks out onto the stage and makes his way to the ring as the crowd gives him some cheers. Young eyes the box and then faces Essington] ESSINGTON: You and I have had a bad couple of months. We haven’t been on the same page much. [Young nods] ESSINGTON: So I am here to make a peace offering. This box is a late Christmas present just for you! [Young looks on with suspicion] ESSINGTON: Come on Ken, open it. [Young looks to the crowd, who don’t want Young do get anywhere near box] ESSINGTON: Would it make you feel better if I opened the box? [Young doesn’t have a chance to answer as Essington begins to rip the paper off the box, and then he opens the box and peaks his head in the door] ESSINGTON: It’s just what you always wanted! [Young moves over, and gets TAKEN OFF HIS FEET by the man who storms out of the box] ESSINGTON: OOOOOH YEAH! [The man, who is at least six eight picks up Young and tosses him across the ring. The shocked Young gets to his feet only to be met by a charge from this mystery man. He crushes Young into the corner and his sheer power has taken the wind and life out of The Perfection. The man then grabs Young by the head and begins to press, trying to CRUSH HIS HEAD!] ESSINGTON: FINISH HIM! [The man lifts Young off his feet for a moment, then drops him to the mat, Young is not moving as Essington stands over him] ESSINGTON: No one! NO ONE MAKES A FOOL OUT OF ME! Kenny, meet my new client, The Sudanese Nightmare! NO ONE MAKES A FOOL OUT OF ME! I know what is best for my clients! [Essington and The Sudanese Nightmare leave the ring] .. .. .. The next match had a bit of a grudge to it, Sebastian asked for this match because he felt slighted by not being a part of Team VXW for WrestleBowl. ************************************************* SEBASTIAN [vs] ‘SADISTIC INSANITY’ DAMIAN PAYNE ************************************************* Before the bell, the referee pointed to Sebastians cronies and tossed them from ringside! Sebastian was incensed! But while he was screaming at the ref, Payne came in and began to pummel Sebastian. Payne rocked him with a series of right hands then picked up Sebastian for the SNAKE EYES! Payne continued to punish Sebastian, hitting him with a TWISTING FISHERMANS SUPLEX and made the cover for ONE!!!!! TWO!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! BUT SEBASTIAN KICKED OUT!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! But Sebastian was able to turn the tables when he countered a BACK BODY DROP with a SWINGING NECKBREAKER! Sebastian then went to work on the lower back of Payne with some KNEE DROPS. Sebastian then kept Payne in the CAMEL CLUTCH for a few minutes before letting him out of the hold and driving a KNEE into the small of his back. Sebastian then went for the TILT A WHIRL BACKBREAKER but Payne countered and hit a DDT on Sebastian in a beautifully done counter. [REF BUMP!] But the referee got knocked to the mat. Payne then hit Sebastian the INSANITY CLAUSE! But noticing the ref was out Payne then got out of the ring and grabbed a chair. That’s when The Equalizer rushed out to the ring, he climbed into the ring and asked for the chair. Payne, nodded, knowing that The Equalizer was looking for some revenge over what Sebastian did to E at New Years Eve Of Destruction. The Equalizer waited for Sebastian to get to his feet and then. . . [WHAM!] [WHAT THE F POP!} And The Equalizer hit DAMIAN PAYNE IN THE FACE WITH THE CHAIR! The referee, saw it and called for the bell, awarding the match to the unconscious Damian Payne. !!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! WINNER = Sadistic Insanity Damian Payne via disqualification !!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! The Equalizer dropped the chair at Sebastians feet and left the ring, leaving a stunned Sebastian in his wake. Adam Jennings is back and the fans were pumped for his in ring return, but his opponent is an old friend, The Outlaw Morris Bass. ******************************************************* ADAM ‘THE HYPE’ JENNINGS [vs] ‘THE OUTLAW’ MORRIS BASS ******************************************************* The two men came face to face in the center of the ring and just started swinging! It was violent and fast and much like a streetfight. Then referee tried to break it up but he couldn’t. Bass rocked Jennings and then used a SHORT ARM CLOTHESLINE to take down the former 24 Hour Champion. The Outlaw then hit a RUNNING SLAM on Jennings that left The Hype in the corner of the ring, in a perfect position for a RUNNING BOOT SCRAPE by Bass. Bass then taunted some pretty ringside Jennings fans, but that left him open for a FOREARM SHIVER from Jennings when he turned. Jennings sent Bass for the ride and sent him up to the lights with a BIG TIME BACK BODY DROP! The crowd was on their feet as Jennings signaled for The HYPER DRIVER!!!! But Bass turned and rolled out of the ring before getting hit with the patented finisher of The Hype. Jennings came over and Bass grabbed him by the ankles and pulled him out of the ring, both men then once again began to pummel each other. With Jennings beginning to get the upper hand in the fight Bass hit Jennings with a BLATANT LOW BLOW!!! Bass then sent Jennings into the ring steps and then grabbed a nearby chair! The referee had already disqualified Bass for the LOW BLOW so Bass had nothing to left to lose, until the screens came alive. [Immediately,Travis LaGrange is seen on screen. He’s wearing a goofy kid’s birthday party hat and has a noise-maker in his mouth. He tosses a handful of confetti into the air and with a blast of air the noise maker sounds, unfurls and returns to its original position.] LaGrange: SUR-PRISE BASS! (LaGrange rips off the party hat, as if that's disguising him.) It’s me ya dumb bastard! Did ya miss me?! I know it ain’t yer birthday, cowboy, but I thought I’d drop by and see how ya were doin’. You know, since we’s tight and all. Alright, you got me. I lied my ass off. We ain’t tight at all, but man, you shoulda seen tha look on that ugly puss of yers, Mo’. Yer face looked like Kate Moss’ – well, if someone dropped a carton of cigarettes and a pound ah’ coke in her lap. Shit, I wish I woulda had a Polaroid camera to capture that Kodak moment. You know, save it for prosperity and such … (LaGrange frowns. Stops realizes and smiles his gold-toothed grin.) On second thought, wait! I remember that look! And I got a little picture of it, which is good enough. Now it ain’t current and all, but I swear you ain’t aged a day. (A journalist’s picture of dirt-embossed Bass being buried alive during a PPV in WILD is shown.) You remember don’t you? The dirt cascadin’ down yer face? The world closing in. The lights bein’ blotted out by the ton of dirt? (laughs) It’s gettin hard to breathe just thinkin’ about it, ain’t it Mo? (LaGrange loosens his collar. Begins to act as if he can’t draw air. He’s mock frantic.) Huh, huh … I can’t see … huh, huh … I can’t breathe … huh, huh … somebody poke a straw in the dirt! Call an ambalance! Get a back-hoe! A team of gophers! Anything! Lord have mercy, Travis LaGrange done buried me alive! (LaGrange drops character and laughs through the next sentence.) You was squawlin’ for yo’ momma, boy under that dirt. Sounded like Jack “Fuckin’” Twist takin’ it up door number two up on the mountain. Trouble is, yer headed for the same damn type ah beatin’ that ended that sugar-britches’ life. Thought yer ass woulda learned your lesson, tryin’ to mind-fuck with the great Travis LaGrange? It can’t be done! But I guess you’re just too gawddamn hard-headed to give up that shit, ain’t ya. You just had to go and pull Adam Jennings’ card, didn’t ya? Son, ya done lit the fires of hell now. ‘Cause Jennings? He ain’t no dummy. He knew who to call. He knew, same as the wrasslin’ world, that there would only be two things I’d come out of retirement for: 1. A Suicide Kings reunion. And 2. Whuppin’ yer sorry, no-good yellow-tinted spine. One outta two ain’t all that bad. It’s 50 percent. And after I get done with you and yer Brokeback Buddy? That’s all you’ll have left … 50 percent. ‘Cause after steppin’ in the ring with me and Jennings, you’ll be half the men you were. Shit, ya might as well be women. Cause ya’ll ain’t got that much of a high percentage of testicles and testosterone between the both of ya to begin with. But it’ll be a barrel of drunken monkeys to take what ya got left. Are ya ready, boys? Have ya wrote yer wills? Have ya kissed yer wives and boyfriends? Looked at that vacation spot you always wanted to go to? ‘Cause the clock is tickin’. And ya ain’t got that much time left. Why? The redneck reaper with the gold-tooth twinkle is comin’ … and he’s dyin’ to meet ya. (LaGrange smiles. Gold tooth twinkles. Fade.) .. .. .. And then a stunned Morris Bass turns for the .. . . HYPER DRIVER FROM JENNINGS!!!!!!! Much to the delight of the crowd who then started a TRAVIS IS THE REAL OUTLAW chant. With the crowd hot, it was time for the main event. The alliance of Valenkov and Omega and management taking on Cobra and his young partner Matt Payne (no relation to Damian Payne) ************************************************** THUNDER VALENKOV & OMEGA [vs] COBRA & MATT PAYNE ************************************************** Omega and Cobra started it off and Omega used his power to take advantage, and once Omega had the control he was ordered from Wheeling/ Masterson to get Cobra into the corner and tag Valenkov. The double team beating began, Omega holding Cobra while Valenkov took some cheap shots, then Valenkov would hit a SLAM or SUPLEX and make the tag, hold Cobra while Omega took some cheap shots then Omega would hit a SLAM or SUPLEX. And so on and so on, all while young Matt Payne was forced to watch from the corner. Cobra fought back after ducking a double CLOTHESLINE from Valenkov and Omega, he hit a STANDING ENZIQUIRI to the legal Omega and then came off the ropes for a SPRINGBOARD BACK ELBOW than knocked the mighty Valenkov to the mat. Cobra then made the HOT TAG and Matt Payne came into the ring with a lot to prove. He cracked Valenkov, knocking him from the ring, then he tackled Omega and mounted him and began to deliver a blistering series of shots to the face of the man who put him on the injured list for such a long time! Valenkov began to make the walk back to the corner, but Wheeling ordered him into the ring to break up the beating Omega was taking. A reluctant Valenkov did as he was told and got into the ring, but before he could stop Matt Payne. . [SNAKES CAN FLY!] Cobra came off the top rope and hit a FLYING SHOULDER to the champion rolling him back to the outside of the ring. Cobra dove over the top rope onto Valenkov and the two title holders of the VXW began to brawl. In the ring, Omega powered up and tossed off Payne and both met in the middle of the ring, Omega going for a CLOTHESLINE but Payne ducked it and caught him in a BACKSLIDE for ONE!!!!!! TWO!!!!!!!!!!!!! [THREEEEE!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!] But the referee was distracted by Masterson who jumped onto the ring apron. Wheeling took the title off the announcers table and came up behind Cobra and [TITLE SHOT THE BACK OF THE HEAD!!!] And Cobra dropped to the mat. Valenkov picked him up and was going to roll him back into the ring when Wheeling began barking orders and pointing to the announcers table! Wheeling wanted Cobra THUNDER BOMBED through the table! Valenkov did, once again as he was told. Back in the ring Omega hit a SPINEBUSTER on Payne and had picked him up into a BEARHUG! Cobra kicked and was able to keep himself from being THUNDER BOMBED through the table, he broke free and SLAMMED the head of Valenkov into the table! Valenkov shook off the slam and kicked Cobra in the GUT and picked him up for the .. . . THUNDER BOMB!!!!!!!!! .. . . COBRA COUNTERED IT INTO A MODIFIED DDT!!!! And both men crashed through the table!!! Omega drove Payne into the corner and then left him to see what the hell was going on outside the ring. With Omega having his back turned he did not see Payne, who hooked him in the BACKSLIDE for ONE!!!!! TWO!!!!!!!!!!!!!! THRRRRRRRREEEEEEEEEEEEEEEE!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! The crowd went crazy, as did the young Payne who rolled out of the ring while pointing at Omega. But Wheeling was pulling Valenkov to his feet and ordering him to continue the punishment of the TV Champion Cobra! Omega then joined in and it wasn’t until Payne was near the stage area that he heard the fans who were trying to tell him that Cobra was getting double teamed. But by the time Payne got back to the ring he was to late to stop Valenkov who .. . . THUNDER BOMBED COBRA INTO THE BROKEN TABLE!!!!! Payne chased off Valenkov and Omega, but the damage had been done. Payne stood over Cobra as the four men who have ruled the VXW began to walk up the aisle, but Omega was dragging behind, a strange look on his face. He turned and returned to the ring. .. .. ..
