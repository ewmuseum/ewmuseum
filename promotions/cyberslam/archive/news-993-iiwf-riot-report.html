iIWF Riot Report

14 August 2000

Rayge =|( iIWF RIOT REPORT )|= Well, well, well.. Here it is, an official reporter for the iIWF, its about time. My name is Rayge and I'll be checking in almost daily with my Riot Report on the happenings of the iIWF. You'll be seeing card previews for Mayhem and Turmoil as well as results, everything inbetween will be interviews, news, feature articles and the such. If you're interested in contacting me to be on my interview list or if you have news, my contact info is always posted at the end of my article. Let's get to it, shall we? Monday Mayhem World Title Match the_korn_kid (c) Vs Ivysaur slam each person twice. This is going to be a great match. The two were former stable mates. When The_Korn_Kid left GOV Ivysaur went crazy on him and many beat downs followed. As of late they've both been in the World Title hunt as the Championship changes hands by the week. My pick is Ivysaur simply because this is a slamming match and Ivysaur is the better of the two at that. TV Title Match Onemanarmy(c) Vs extreme_x_ Vs Thorn We got no contender for the TV title so i stuck these two into the match. slam each person twice. Over the past month this title has somewhat lost a lot of its prestige it once held. Now its a belt used as a stepping stone to the World Championship. Extreme_x, a sleeper through July is hungry for a promotion and has rekindled that fire that once drove him. Thorn and Onemanarmy(c) both seem like decent contenders but don't flash nearly as much as they should, leaving me to go with Extreme_X. Hardcore Title Match con_victor(c) Vs Kryptonite_ re-write counts towards the win, so make this good. slam each person twice. This match has violence written all over it. Both men are worthy of the Championship, certainly Con_victor has proven so. Kryptonite has been around the block a bit and looks to actually be staying around this time. However, he's no match for Con_victor whose an excellent re-writer and hence why he holds this title. Underdog Title Match Chameleon (c) Vs Dexter slam each person 2 times. Chameleon is the champion of Underdog Champions. This is his belt, an although he sits barely above the .500 plateau, there's no way he'll let this title slip from his grasp once again. Dexter is much above the mark at approximately 58% and has asked Commishener Bazorro, that if he were to win this title and have to forfiet that he put as a #1 conteder for a bigger belt. Both these guys are surely on the rise and I think Dexter will pull this one out for the GOV. Tag Title Match Ivysaur/Con Victor(c) Vs Announce/Krohn slam each person once. This match is very interesting. Announce and Krohn are relatively new to the game and really stand no chance againt the former team of GOV leaders. So Ivysaur and Con Victor will undoubtly win. However, there's a hole in this wall. Since Ivy and Con are opposite sides of the table now, Con has chosen his newest GOV addition, Dexter to be his new partner. More on this oddity in my Tag Conteders preview. World #1 Contender's Match extreme_x_ Vs Con_victor slam each person twice. This is a future headliner! Con undoubtedly will reach the top of the mountain one day in the iIWF but him and his GOV has one problem, Impact Playerz, lead by Extreme_x. Its a battle of stables for contendership to the World Championship and I think Con_victor will take it this time but that title won't be safe for long from Extreme_x. TV #1 Contender's Match Tchosengame Vs dawgmedic_ slam each person twice. This is one of the duller matches on the card. Both are relatively decent flashers and their standings aren't bad either, in a way, under rated potential. If either one can grab that TV Title, they better make the best of it 'cause it'll make the best of their careers. Tchosengame, make the most it. Hardcore #1 Contender's Match Announce vs red_dragon Vs Krohn vs Rockrolen slam each person once. A four way dance for the Hardcore contendership. No matter who wins they'll have a hard time taking that title from Con_Victor, but these four individuals definately are prospects. Red_Dragon was suspended today and Rockrolen was thrown in this match after the intial card posting. He had a solid outing on Turmoil last week and I think it will continue to tonight, Rockrolen. Underdog #1 Contender's Match hellhammer vs harddcorek slam each person twice. Not much to say about this match. Harddcorek has the edge of experience and their difference in the standings is fairly slim, but I have to say my pick is Hellhammer. Tag #1 Contender's Match Con_Victor/dexter vs chameleon/Korn Kid dont have many teams, so i stuck Korn Kid and cameleon together. slam each person twice. Ok, as I was talking about earlier. Afro_Thunder has retired, leaving his partner Dexter out in the cold. However, Con_Victor has come to the aid of his GOV stablemate. So say Con and Ivy retain their Tag Title tonight and Con and Dex win this match, Ivy will hand his belt over to Dex, making the #1 contenders, the new Champions and Baz has promised Ivysaur he can challenge with whoever he wants as a partner at the next chance for those titles, making him the #1 contender. Confusing, yes, but guess what, it'll happen, Con_victor/Dexter will win. "Contract" on the line Battle Royal Mrsatnight Vs Rock Vs Haker Vs Leadberg vs hellraizer Vs Creed_h Vs Johnny_rocke Vs Dorito Vs Ghetto_fab Vs Razmiga this is our weekly "contract" on the line battle royal. this is real simple. you do PRE-MATCH comments, slam any 4 of the people in the match, then post your results on the board. if you people no-show this match, your fired. My fav match of every monday card. A brilliant idea to motivate or heave the slackers in the fed. My picks for those being fired? Rock, Creed_h (who was suspended I believe), Dorito and Johnny Rocke. Get ready to clear some space on the list. Alright thats the Monday Card and that's all for my intial article here on Hammerlock. I will follow up with Results hopefully tomorrow and will be absent till Saturday. Once again for those who wish to contact me, my info is below. Let Monday be Mayhem because nothing else matters...
