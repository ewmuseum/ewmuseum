The man with Tyme on his side, Justin Tyme(CBF)

Hammerlock News — undated in the source

Twenty Questions with Justin Tyme of CBF We come across a young man seated in a modest chair. He has a youthful smile and a dramatic flair to him. Another man walks in, and takes a seat across from him. Reynolds lights a cigarette, readies his notebook and the camera, and begins. Blast Reynolds: I am here with Justin Tyme of the CBF. Anything you'd like to say before we begin? Justin Tyme: I'd like to thank all my fans for getting behind me and staying there as I make my way up the ladder here in Cyberslam. Blast: Speaking of climbing ladders, I hear you were one of those few chosen Majors Slammers who managed to clear the indies in just one month. How does it feel to have gotten straight to the Majors in such a short time? JT: Well, the obvious answer is that it feels great. However, I've never been one to be too humble. I fought hard, had some great slammers behind me, and earned my right to make it to the CBF. It was my tyme. Blast: That seems very apparent in your relationship with the Knights. What brought this about? JT: Well, I pride myself on basing my career on the great faces of the wrestling world. Guys like Bruno Sammartino, Hulk Hogan, and Andre the Giant. It's tough to be on the side of right in Cyberslam because everyone wants to be the heel. I was lucky to have found a group of guys like the Knights to get behind because they are the only ones with enough guts to do what's right...at least, most of the tyme. Blast: Yes, Rembrandt's dealings with his father-in-law seem to bring a strange feel to the Knights. It was Rembrandt who recently saved you from Dr. Douglas of Big Brother's intended kidnapping, yet Al Bradd, Rembrandt's father-in-law, was fine with kidnapping a Knight, until Rembrandt interfered. How does this affect your view of the Knights and Big Brother? JT: Well as far as Big Brother is concerned, I obviously would live to take them all out one by one. It was Dr. Douglas who gave me the poison that still threatens my life today. Al Bradd turned on me just when I thought I could trust him, and he has been trying to keep me and the other young slammers down. Frankly, those guys have gotten under my skin one too many tymes. There's a storm coming and BB better be prepared to zip up and find shelter when it does. As for the Knights, well, I never left them. I just need some tyme to figure out if our goals are still the same. Rembrandt needs to do more to earn my trust back, Destroyer has to get his head on straight, and Chevalier needs to spend more tyme with us helping develop our careers. Blast: Well, Chevalier was highly involved with his feud with Dark Knight, which saw him standing victorious by the conclusion. How was your take on that? JT: I knew that Chevs would come out on top. Was there any doubt? Dark Knight was a man I found myself in a number of conflicts with, and although he put up a great fight, I can't say I'm sorry to see him go. Perhaps now, though, Chevalier can spend more of his energies on his fellow Knights. Blast: Perhaps...he has yet to reply to my request for an interview...now, you mentioned that Dr. Douglas poisoned you? JT: Yes. Last month he injected me with some sort of serum. He used a variation of the same serum to enslave the entire population of Blasttown by spiking the water supply. Oddly enough, the House of Pain helped the Knights fight off the mobs and save the day. I discovered later that the injection I received gave me incredible strength whenever I got angry. What I didn't know was that every tyme I exerted myself this way, my body would deteriorate. Now, if I am far apart from Buffy, I could slip into a coma and die. Scary thought. Blast: Why the connection to Buffy? JT: Well, Douglas had a crush on her and he knew that she and I were kind of interested in one another. As some sort of psychotic twist of fate, he injected the cure to my condition into her and tried to enslave her too. I guess he figured that he could watch me die as he held my cure close to him. The plan backfired, however, as Buffy resisted his mind control and fought against him. The only thing that keeps me alive is contact with her. Luckily, Buffy has promised to stay by my side until we figure out a permanent cure. Blast: Wow...a truly romantic story. Quite the gentleman. And a sad tale...I wish you all the best in finding your cure. What are your goals with the new month? JT: Well, to get cured, of course. I also want to win and hold onto the Heavyweight Championship. Oddly enough, it is the one title that I have yet to ever wear around my waist. I'd love to beat Al Bradd for it too, that would be icing on a huge cake. Blast: How long have you been in CBF? JT: About four months now. Blast: So, you've really made quite an impact. What has been the key to your success? JT: Well, I have to admit, that I have been following the advice of Chevalier. When I first came to the CBF, I was always in the middle of the rankings. Since Chevy trained me, I have held a top spot for the last two months. Blast: If the Knights weren't with CBF, who WOULD you join, just out of curiosity? Or would you work solo? JT: Well, that's a tough question. Right now, I'd probably have to say that I'd start my own stable. I believe in fighting for what's right and I know of a number of slammers out there who feel the same way. I think I could scare up a pretty impressive roster in short order. cont... Blast_reynolds
