iRCW rules and policies

a member fed's own page, captured 17 January 2001

Date: 11/08/100 Policies ... by The Eli Contact Info: AOL SN: [detail withheld] Main Email: [detail withheld] ICQ [detail withheld] MSN Messenger: Eli About Myself: I am a long time Cyberslam slammer. I will give you the respect that you give me. I don't want you guys to beg me for anything. You have to earn your respect. Feel free to talk to me about anything in the federation. Please do not disrespect me on the boards or anywhere. If I read or find out about this, you will be terminated no matter how good you are. 1)Inactives: Here in the iRCW, it is extremely important that you are an active slammer and flasher. If you are not active on the boards, you will not be considered for any titles, cards, or promotions. Being Active: Active does not mean one or two liners. Put some thought into them. Activity is actively interacting on card days, non card days, and engaging in fueds of some sorts. 2) Padders/Jobbers: Do not slam another slammer more than two times a day, unless I inform you to do so on card days. Do not slam those slammers that are more than 15-25% below you too much. Keep it to a mimimum. (3-5 times a week). 3) Punishments: If you break any of the rules, you will be subject to suspension. Usually there will be an Email and/or Flash warning. 1st offense: Flash Warning and Email 2nd offense: 2-Day suspension 3rd offense: 5-day suspension 4th offense: Termination 4) Champions/Titles: There are 7 Titles in all: World Title, Intercontinental Title, European Title, Hardcore Title, Tag Team Title, Sucka Title, The Man’s Man Title The World Title is the most prestigious Title to hold in the iRCW. You have to be a very active flasher, but can be anywhere in the standings to earn or win this belt. The Intercontinental Title is the second most prestigious title in the iRCW. This belt is for the slammer who can be better than he/she thinks. To earn this belt you have to be active on the boards and near the top of the standings. The Toxic Title is for an active slammer who makes his presence felt on the board. This belt can mean big things for your future here in the iRCW. The Hardcore Title is struggling slamming wise but not flashing wise. Remember, there are NO DQ’s, and no countouts in hardcore matches. The Tag Team Titles are for the best tag team in the iRCW. Both members of the team MUST be active on the board. The Sucka Title is for the slammer under 50%. If you hold this belt and go over 50% you will be moved up to the Hardcore division. To hold the Sucka Title, you must be very active on the board, not matter what your standing rank is. The Man’s Man Title is a rewrite title. To win this match, you must be VERY active on the board. Matches will take place every other card. Even if you are lower in the ranks, you must still roleplay, if you are high in the ranks you must roleplay as well. If you remain VERY active you will get a shot at the belt. 5) How to Slam, the Cards, & No-Shows: On days of cards, do not slam anyone besides your oppenent in your match assigned. If you do, you will be disqualified. If this continues, you will get a 3 day suspension. Any other days besides card days you are free to slam anyone, just keep in mind the policies. Cards will take place on Wednesday’s, entitled Wednesday’s Wire, and Saturday’s, entitled Saturday Fire. Free Per Views will take place in a three day period. I will tell you the time when it is to happen. 1st No show: Exclusion from the next show. You must write me with an explanation on why you no-showed. 2nd No show: 2-day suspension. 3rd No show: Terminatation. Cards will work as following: On both cards, there will be an Under-card, Middle-card, and Main Event. That Undercard is ONLY for the brand new slammers as well as those who only flash a little bit. If you no show, you will be terminated from the iRCW. Middle-cards will be for everyone else. The main event will be the main event of course! As for the other matches, they will be as followed. The Wire Card Will Consist of… ALL TITLES AND CONTENDERS MATCHES The Fire Card Will Consist of… GRUDGE MATCHES & FOLLOW UP MATCHES 6)Slamming Your Matches on Card Days: In case of any tie, first you go to points, and if there is still a tie, the challenger or the 2nd slammer listed initiates the tiebreaker. In Tag matches, Tiebreakers go to Points (see Points System) 7)Point Systems: A Point System will be used in case of ties for all matches… Point System: 5 pts for a Injury victory, 4 By winning via Finisher, 3 pts for a pinfall victory, 2 pts for a victory via Countout/Interference, 1 pt for a victory via DQ., 8) The Schedule: I will provide a callender at the beginning of every month to inform you about upcomming events. There will be no blitz’s unless I give the okay. 9) The Crew: The iRCW Announcing Team: Color commentary/PBP: Jim Ross & Mike Tenay Interviewes: Jack Son Ring Announcer: “Triple Crown” Tom Brown Referee: Jack “Big Moose” Horrington 10) Final Statement(s): Cyberslam is for all ages, don’t use foul language. Do not even think of covering up foul words like so: @$$, @%#^, etc. I don’t want to see it on my board. If you do, you will be suspended for 2-days. If you continue, you will be terminated. If you must use parenthesis (censored). Resets of the board will be every Monday. You may use me in your roleplays but if it gets out of hand I will have to step in. If you continue to use me after I ask you to stop, you will be suspended for 5-days. If you are dead weight in the iRCW you will warned, then terminated if you continue to be dead weight. If you are posting 1 liners, you will be suspended without warning due to ignorance. On card days, post a prematch, and then in a reply, post your results. In your prematch comments before you slam your match, put your match and what kind of match it is in the prematch comments box. I hope to have the iRCW extremely successful but that can only happen with your help on the board. DEAD WEIGHT WILL NOT be accepted! Before you slam your daily matches, look on the board. There may be a important announcement. Thank you and good luck! Eli, iRCW Commish
