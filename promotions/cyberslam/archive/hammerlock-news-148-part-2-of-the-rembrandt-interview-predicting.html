Part 2 of the Rembrandt interview/Predicting CBF future and more

Hammerlock News — undated in the source
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Blast shifts in his chair, and extinguishes the cigarette. Scribbling something in his notepad, he continues. Blast: "Fair enough. You have mentioned the Executive a few times. He was a legendary commish in the CBF for quite some time. How does Limit stack up to him?" Rembrandt: "Well, they are very different, so it's hard to compare. However, I like what Limit is doing with this fed. He had growing pains like any new commish will, but he had a plan and stuck to it. I truely feel that he is one of the major reasons this fed is getting the respect it does right now. However, he has a solid core of active workers, which helps. But just like he can't take credit for the good workers, we can't penalize him for the stiffs. He likes to keep everybody as involved as possible, and that is why the undercard of the FPV seemed a little dry. He gives us the opportunity to prosper, but some just don't take advantage of it." Blast: "Speaking of the FPV, you played a pretty major role in it. Of course, many thought that once again you were in the shadows of the Chev/DK showdown. How do you rate your performance?" Rembrandt: "Well, for starters, I LOST MY WORLD TITLE!!! Thanks for reminding me. However, I did manage to hold on to my tag title, so that was pretty good. I made a decent showing in the Stable Wars, and I beat Meltdown in a brutal RP Match. Overall, it sucked because I LOST MY WORLD TITLE!!!" Blast: "I see. It's good to see you aren't bitter about losing the World Title. You mention Meltdown. It seems you have a lot of trouble with the newcomers because you won't give them the respect they want. Why is that?" Rembrandt: "Sawdust, Meltdown, the list goes on. The thing is, respect is something that is earned over time, or at least should be. If they want my respect, they need to show me some real effort. And not just 1 good month or running a simple angle to get over. I mean put in the time to make me respect them! Some of these kids come in and just expect that because they had a good month in the minors they deserve my respect. It doesn't work that way. I need to see it for myself over the long haul." Blast: "What do you think of the talent coming up from the iRWF and the iAWO? Does anybody stand out in recent memory as being a potential star?" Rembrandt: "I think it's the same as it's always been. Some of the newbies really give an effort, like Dark Knight, Mysterioso, Dr. Douglas, Meltdown...while others seem to just fade away and not be able to compete at the major league level. Of all the new talent, I think Justin Tyme has the true potential to be a major player. That is why we recruited him into the Knights. He really is that good." Blast: "Do you think eliminating the flashing promotions will hurt the quality of the new talent coming in?" Rembrandt: "No doubt. I don't care what their record was, a good flasher and RP'er is worth more to a fed in the long run than someone that had a lucky month. I think that flashing should be the ONLY way to get a promotion!" Blast: "How many questions are we at? I lost count." Rembrandt: "Don't look at me. I didn't realize I was expected to take that kind of responsibility here." Blast: "How about just a few more. The playoffs are coming up. You went to the Ultimate finals last month. Do you expect a repeat?" Rembrandt: "Well, when it comes to the playoffs, anything can happen. I always say hope for the best, expect the worst. I look forward to going back to the Ultimate, perhaps even winning it one day, but who doesn't? I'm glad I just got there once. Of course, that doesn't mean I'm happy when I lose. But it was a long road to the top, I'm just thankful for any success I have." Blast: "You have held so many titles and won so many tournaments. Does any one match stick out in your mind?" Rembrandt: "Well, a lot of them do. From my first televised card to losing the title at the FPV, I remember most all of the big ones. However, the CBF match of the year for 1998 is the one that continues to stand out. The DOD against the One & Only's in a barroom brawl. We had fought to a draw the week before and wanted to settle things. So we met them on their home turf and kicked them around and won our first tag title. It was the first time anyone in this fed had ever seen a tag team ironman match, and it was extra sweet because it was my idea. It was without a doubt the most awesome match this fed has ever seen." Blast: "Hmmm, how about some word association and then I'll let you go. I have a list of names from your past and present and you give me your thoughts on them....." Chevalier - "The ultimate face & the greatest stable leader in the modern era." Thrill - "Always gave maiximum effort, loved to force you to be creative. A real contributor." Meltdown - "Has potential, let's see if he uses it to grow or self destruct." Dark Knight - "Very active, very talented. A glimpse at the future of the game." Paperbagman - "Fun to be around. He was the most electric flasher I have ever seen. Pure humor and always exciting." Justin Tyme - "Future superstar" Dionysus - "My Brother-In-Law. Partied like no other." MaskedJobber - "My greatest, and most respected, opponent...bar none." Mintwist - "The ultimate champion. Was great at everything." Executive - "The greatest commissioner EVER!" Al Bradd - "When he has the time, he is the smartest player in the game and an all-time great flasher." Raje - "Once was pretty good, but he just couldn't hang on and is now a non-active slug. A terrible waste of talent." Granny - "Hot, Hot, HOT!! Man when she takes her teeth out...WOW!!" Executioner - "The hardcore Icon. He was the innovator when it came to brutality." Sawdust - "Fun to work with. I wish he had stayed around longer." Bigsexyt - "Awesome in the ring. A true intimidator." Sarcasm - "A major player. Master of mind games. Nobody pissed off more slammers." Limit - "Great commish with potential to be one of the best." Skullcrusher (Drimstone) - "Raw talent when I first met him, but eager. Loved to go on the attack. He was taken from us way too soon." Destroyer - "My best friend in the game. A saint. He has to be to have put up with me for the last 16 months." Blast: "Great! Any others I missed?" Rembrandt: "Too many to count. Michaelsolo, Satanspimp, & Mil Mascaras come to mind. But if I review them I will just keep going and we will be here for hours." Blast: "Maybe some other time, I do have a schedule to keep you know. But it has been a real education to the CBF. I thank you greatly for your time and sharing your vast knowledge with me." Rembrandt: "Any time Blast. It is I who should be thanking you." Blast: "You are welcome. Good luck in the playoffs!" Rembrandt: "Thanks, I'll need it." Blast_reynolds
