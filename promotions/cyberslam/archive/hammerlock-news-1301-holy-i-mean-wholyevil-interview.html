Holy...I mean Wholyevil Interview

Hammerlock News — undated in the source
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hey there, Jimmy Bear here bringing you another interview with the current US title Holder in the cWo, Holyevil. Holy Evil, Thank you for your time. HE: "You should thank me. I'm a busy man. And it's Wholy Evil. Don't forget that! Now, First off, why don't you tell the cWo fans a little about yourself, Like Titles held, where your from, that sort of thing. HE: "Well, I'm from Maryland originally. I loved wrestling as a kid and trained in Japan, which is where I met Undertow, my fiancee and Valet. If you notice, I tend to incorporate a very Japanese style of slamming, using High flying moves to ground an opponent and incorporating a variety of submission holds to wear them down. I tend to stick to high flying state-side. It tends to catch people off guard easier and taking down big guys is a lot simpler than trying to make them tap out. I've held numerous title. I was the third person under CRAZY IVAN in the iAPWF to hold all five singles titles, behind ICE HAWG and SGT. SMASH. I was there a little under ten months. I joined Cyberslam April 4th, 1999. So I just celebrated my one year anniversary a few weeks ago. In the cWo I've held the US title and should have held the People's title, but due to a technicality, it was first held up then awarded to my opponent. I'm always gunning for something bigger though." Your Feud with Nakoma dates back to your days in the iAPWF. Exactly what is your problem with her? HE: "Well, she's a cocky *****. She's Annoying too. She's constantly embellishing her own accomplishments and making herself seem grand and great. She also has cut a couple of growths off my stable in the past. But they were both cancerous, so it's not that big a deal. And now she's proclaiming women are better than men. What? since when? Women in slamming are useful for three things; Shaking their breasts and making the men in the stands hoot and hollar while being a valet for men, fighting with women, or being a hideous freak of nature and fighting men. You can't be any more than one of those, but Nakoma wants to be all three. Plus, she's boring and constantly spits out the same digested bile over and over about how magnificent she is. Plus, I think she's threatened by my title as King of the US and King of Evil." Jimmy_bear
