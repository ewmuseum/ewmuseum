iUWA: Predictions back with Avengence

30 August 2000
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Shamus The buzz around the iUWA is the New Begining. So why wouldn't the August FPV be named just that, A New Begining. There are two things that make this a big thing, 1 is obviously it's an iUWA FPV, and 2 this is Arcadia's first card. So why not give you my opinion on what I think of the matches. Thats right, It has been awhile and I figured what the hey I do some good ole predictions. But first tidbits. Well I want to start off this report by righting a wrong. You see sometimes even a world class reporter like myself... well not me..but Even a semi-decent reporter like...no that's not me either... Even a guy with an old typewriter that when you hit a J it also types H and K, earning lower than minimum wage and doing interviews in an old broom closet like myself makes a mistake. In my last report I may have hurt some people's feelings, and Shamus never wants to hurt anyone, So I decided I am going to do something I never do...Apologize. I want to appolgize to Dangerous Minds, You see if you don't remember I said last week "Dangerous Minds, well there isn't anything dangerous about them and the Minds part is in question as well." I can see how that could have been taken the wrong way, So I want to appolgize and let me reiterate that. If your a member of DM you probably don't know what that big word means. Reiterate~To Retell, repeat in a different manor. But anyway, what I meant to say was, The Dangerous Minds have never been a threat to this fed, it's nice to see them keep it that way. And boy they sure are plenty dumb. You see that's what I was trying to say. But I just put nicely. Now if there is anything else I can spell out for you be sure to let me know. Whew, that was long. It appears the bWo is gaining some numbers in it's early stages of returning. As now we see Ov back in the bWo tee. What's the matter Ov, couldn't hack it up in the majors? I know what your gonna say, skip it, rewind it an play it back in your head, I don't care just don't say it on the board. But enough bashing for now. I am in rare form tonight, the doctor gave me the wrong pills for my anger fits. Instead of giving me something to sudate me, he gave me Midol. So I have violent mood swings and I hate men, but my cramps are gone. Nothing to do with iUWA sorry. Next I would like to ask all the slammers of the iUWA to help your pal Shamus with something. I would like to know who you guys think did the best in Augest, I know some are new, and some have left. But I would just like a top five guys list and hopefully be able to do something with it. So send me a list at [detail withheld] . If at all possible I would like to get this list done by the begining of next month which is a few days. Sorry I make this stuff up as I go along. But enought rambling on with... Shamus Predicts: A New Begining iUWA FPV World title G_Ride vs. The Rick Well this day was sure to come, IP vs IP for the world. G_Ride has come a long way and if my dog didn't pee in my hat I would tip it to him, but in this match I will once again side with the experienced one. So the prediction goes to... Shamus Prediction:The Rick US title: Angelhunter vs. Electabuzz The US title is on the line in this one. A member Dangerous Minds goin up against a big shot, no not that big shot, an IP member and Leader of the Fold. Now I just wanna clarify one thing, Electabuzz has my vote for most improved slammer in iUWA. If he wasn't in Dangerous Minds I might even root for him. Now don't let this fool you, by no means am I an IP mark. We all know where I stand, it just so happens that the prediction goes with... Shamus Prediction:Angelhunter JOB title Tamongo vs. Stronguymike Tomango, the man who saved the iUWA by convincing me not to retire. Well not exactly saved the iUWA, but atleast I do a good job keeping the bathrooms clean. At any rate, Tomango has taken a leave of absence, it happens, and I know all to well. So it would be foolish for me to pick him to win this bout. Stronguy Mike on the other hand, I know pretty well, and he would be pretty good if he just threw in some extra time. And on another note, with the bWo back it would put the UHA just slightly above DM. Hmmm. But regardless my prediction goes with... Shamus Prediction:Stronguy Mike Tag titles Jack Wolf and Electabuzz vs. The Rick and Jenerez Storm vs. Lyonell & EZombie. Forgive me for overstating the obvious but we have Dangerous Minds vs IP vs bWo. Well, one of these things is not like the other. I'll stop there. We have 4 legends doing battle. But I do belive the prediction is gonna go with... Shamus Prediction:b.W.o ~ Lyonell and Ezombie Skull crusher Supersoviet vs. EZombie The infamous iUWA own, Skullcrusher championship. There is alot of meaning behind this belt, and I am very happy to see the tradition being carried on. I was not apart of it's begining, but I do know why it is such a prestigous title and why it is more of a memorial than a belt you sling over your shoulder. What was I doing, oh yes the predictions. Supersoviet is one funny SOB and I wish him the best of luck in this match, but he is in this match against a legend all his own. A man by the name of Eric Zombie. And what better way to honor a legendary belt than by putting it around the waist of a iUWA legend, So I predict... Shamus Prediction:Ezombie Slammer title Knibby vs. Pheonix Well this is a new belt. What I am gaining from the policies, I hope everyone is read up, is that the winner of this belt will gain a title shot at there choice of a championship. Well only a fool will use this oppertunity to go for the JOB title. But it would be kinda funny. And once again we have a match with two great slammers. Well this decision will not be easy so I will go to the Shamus Automatic Predicition Device. (SAPD) What this does is it takes the stats of each slammer, multiplies it by the number of nose hairs Montana Kid has, adds two, divides the total ammount of times Lt.Loco quits a week, fractions in a percentage of times we seen that same old UHA video and gives me a prediction. So here we go. Shamus Prediction: Tails... I mean Knibby USA vs. The World Vinny Vegas, G Ride, and Sabit vs. Knibby, Supersoviet, Slammin Stu. Help I have stepped in a time warp and I am stuck in an 80's WWF angel. Just joking, kinda. Has the Iron Sheik retired or will he come down as well. But I must say this, there is nothing wrong with remaking an angel, ecpesially when it is a burn your opponents flag match. This is a great idea, I do not promote the burning of flags (but bras are ok), but this is a very unusual twist to the norm. Now the question lyes here, where does Shamus stand? Well I'm glad I asked, I hail from the marvelous world that has brought some wrestling legends. Some true greats of our time. Some great music as well. I come from a place where the Boss is still the Boss, we don't have great beer but we have a bunch of hics. We have Hockey, but just one team. I AM NEW JERSIAN! Shamus Prediction:USA Montana Kid vs. Lt Loco If you really have to even wonder who will win this match I have one question, at exactly what age did you stop eating paint chips? Shamus Prediction:Montana Kid Jack Wolf vs. Angelhunter See Above match for comments... Shamus Prediction:Angelhunter Greydeath vs. King Crab Well, your guess is as good as mine, if not better. I know these guys have talent. But who are they... I don't know. Who will win... I said I don't know... What is the air velocity of an Unmaiden Swallow... Stop asking me questions. Shamus Prediction:Graydeath And that about does it for me, I need to fall asleep. Or else the warden makes me shower with all the guys in cell block 9, and they don't play nicely. So I bid my favorite fed goodnight and get me those lists. Shamus is out of Heeeeeerreee! none
