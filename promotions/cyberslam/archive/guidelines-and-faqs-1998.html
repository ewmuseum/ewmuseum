Guidelines and FAQs

the hub's own rulebook, captured December 1998

1. MEMBERSHIP There are two types of memberships a Premium Membership (Paid) and a Standard Membership. .. Premium members have certain added privileges including: .. They can not be dropped from CyberSlam (unless they violated certain rules) They may transfer from one Fed to another at the turn of the month. They get an additional Free Slammer. .. All Slammers start off as Standard Members, whether or not they paid to jump the list. If you have jumped the waiting list you get the next month free. If you continue to pay $10.00 for the following months you will become a premium member and receive an extra slammer free. .. If you now have a free slammer and want additional slammers each will cost $10.00. Then all your Slammers will become premium members. 2. HOW TO BECOME A PREMIUM SLAMMER Go to the join area and sign up your additional slammer. Make sure to fill out the payment info accurately. 2. HOW TO SLAM CyberSlam is made up of 7 major federations and 2 indy federations. Each federation has a commissioner. All the action for your Slammer occurs within your slammer's fed only. To begin Slammin go to http://www.scoopscentral.com/cyberslam/. Then click on the FED button, this will take you to our main Fed page. All the Feds are listed there. Find your Fed and click on the link, this will bring you to your Fed page. Read and follow the instructions on that page. .. VERY IMPORTANT: Jobbing Type A: The first type I've seen is SLammers bringing in a Second slammer to the fed they are in and jobbing to himself. For Example Slammer A and B are owned by the same person. Slammer A constantly fights Slammer B in order to gain an advantage. Type B: The second such jobbing, which is quite popular, is stable jobbing, that being the slammers within a stable saving up like 10 or more matches and expending them all on the top stable member to help with the win. Type C: Next and last is the kind of a mix of things, it's the non'flasher or 3 flash per week man who just jobs twice a day to their friend, or just half their matches 6 days per week to hide it. these are the harder ones to spot, and in addition to this there are the ones who try and hide the jobbing in an angle. Penalties Due To Jobbing Commissioners will come down very hard on slammers who job. The program has also been altered to negatively impact upon those who job. Make sure you stick to your commissioners guidelines regarding jobbing, a number of slammers have been suspended due to jobbing. 3. ABOUT COMMISSIONERS The Commissioners have absolute authority to suspend any slammer within their own fed, for any reason. If you have a problem contact your commissioner. These are the emails so that you can contact your commissioner. If you don't, then the commish can and very well may suspend your slammer. 4. HOW TO USE THE FLASH BOARD Each Fed has a flash board. This is where the role play takes place. Each fed's board has its own rules for flashing. This is the number one reason for slammers getting suspended, for they blow off the guidelines set by the Commissioner. You should know that all flash boards do not allow what would be called "Vulgar" language. There is a censor built into the game that filters out this type of language. Now if you choose to creatively bypass this filter you WILL be suspended. Repeat, YOU WILL BE suspended. Now most of you know this, but a few always seem to try to push the limits. If you have a creative idea for enhancing the role play for all, pass it by your commissioner first. Make sure its OK. Feuds are great!! But if you overstep the boundaries and your commissioner is not filled in, it may be taken the wrong way. Also, the Commish has the experience to bring your ideas to life. Using another slammer's "Real" name or "Information - Age, sex, ...." while flashing is not allowed. Remember this is role play here. This is considered a major violation and violating this will lead to suspension. Trying to guess how many slammers are owned by a particular person and posting your guesses is considered a major violation 5. HOW TO EMAIL Don't email any slammer directly by "slammername@cyberslam.com", it will not get to them. The only exception is me, [detail withheld] and Commissioners. You need to use the MAILBOX feature within CyberSlam to contact other Slammers. If you use the MailBox feature and want your email to be answered, then you better select the option which allows the receiver of your email to respond. Otherwise the email will be sent anonymously with no way of being returned directly. If you receive a CyberSlam email that says Do Not Reply to This Email Directly, then please don't. It means the slammer who sent you the email, sent it anonymously and unless you know their direct email, there is no way to reply directly, you must go to the MAILBOX area to reply. The rules for email are the same as those for the Flash Board. Violating these rules by sending offensive email to other slammers is not tolerated. You will be suspended if you violated these rules. 6. INACTIVITY The key to CyberSlam's success is your participation. The program now automatically scans for Slammers that are inactive and automatically drops them from the game. Be sure to remain active by Slammin on a regular basis. YES, if you go vacation or if you are ill and can't slam, the program will still drop. You would then need to contact your commissioner for reinstatement. 7. NEWS CyberSlam has a staff of excellent journalist who keep you up to date on what's going on. The news is updated weekly (we try our best). If you want to have items posted on the News, then email [detail withheld] with your News. 8. CHANGING FEDS You are NOT allowed to change feds during the month. Free Slammers are not allowed to jump Feds at all (even at the turn of the month). The only way a free slammer gets to jump feds is if he is kicked out by his commissioner and is lucky enough to be sent to the iCCW or iRCW. Paid Slammers (those with a Premium Membership) may transfer Feds at the turn of the month only. Must receive the following form filled out before the beginning of the new month. Playoffs Check out CSlam area for all the details regarding when playoffs start and the rules. The iCCW and the iRCW will now have a slightly different feel to it. In order to make it to a major fed, a slammer must either win his federation's belt at the end of the month OR hold his fed's top title (see your commissioner to find out which title gets you in). There are no more drafts. Only way up is to win. 12. INDY FEDS There are currently 2 indy feds and one major fed within the CWF league. Each FED has a commissioner, the league has a president. Each commissioner reports to the president. The president and the major fed commissioner may or may not be the same character. Currently Nassau is the commissioner of the CWF fed and the president of the CWF league. The duties of the presidents of each league are to keep tabs on their indy feds, scouting for talent, watch for inactivity of any of the indy commishes and other smaller duties. . There are four ways that a slammer gets from an indy fed to a major fed. First, a slammer wins the federation championship at the end of the month. Second, a slammer wins that federations top title. Third, a slammer gets a recommendation from his commissioner. Fourth, a slammer pays to jump to a major fed. In turn however, Slammers can be sent down to the indys from the major feds by breaking Cslam or Fed policy. This "demotion" can be done at any point during the month if the major fed commish AND The Slammer agree to it. . An indy to major fed relationship is modeled after the NWA to WWF relationship. Like the NWA, the indy fed may have veterans as well as rookies. It has it's own championship, but the champion can not compete for the Ultimate CyberSlam Championship. However, the champion does move to the major fed the following month. . The commissioners of the indy feds are considered on an equal level with all other commissioners. They have the full power of all other commissioners and are treated as equals. What they say goes. However, the presidents are expected to communicate with their respective indy fed commishes and vice-versa. They must be able to work together to give eachother ideas, help with problem slammers, or anything elce that may occur. . Each indy fed's have their own set of policies that you as a slammer in that fed must follow, however, these policies are based losely on the main feds policies, thus pomoting a smooth trasistion if the indy slammer moves up to the parent fed. . Currently the iRCW and the iCCW have real world counterparts known as the RCW and the CCW. There is an interaction between the virtual and real world feds that is currently being developed. 13. DO NOT MAIL us your join info. ========================== FAQs 1) Does C-SLAM take any real skill? ie; quick hitting buttons like on a video game --> It's takes strategy skill not quick hitting buttons skills. Some of our best Slammers have managed to make the playoffs repeatedly and one even won three championships. 2) Who comes up with the matches? Does each FED have a "booker" ? --> You select who you want to wrestle, by clicking on a pop down menu that shows all the wrestlers in your FED. 3) Who comes up with the angles? --> All the players (we call them Slammers) do. The angles are developed through our Flashboard and by emailing other Slammers. 4) Are the winners pre-determined? --> No. Our online program looks at your Slammer's inherent talent (which are based on the moves that the Slammer has chosen when they signed up), number of wins, number of losses and number of matches. 5) Can I pick the Slammers that I would like to go up against? --> You select who you wrestle, but by the same token they can select you as their opponent. 6) When do the playoffs or Ultimate CSlam start --> This month they start on August 29th. On the 29th all the Slammers in all the Feds will have their records reset to zero. Over the next 24 hours all of the active slammers within each fed battle it out to see who ends up with the best record. On the 30th , the slammers with the best records (winning percentage - this means even to a tenth or a hundredth or a percentage point - DO NOT GO FOR A TIE - unless you have identical records only one slammer will advance) in each fed are selected to fight for the CSlam Belt. Because of the nature of the Internet there are no hard rules, it may start at 3 or at 8 or end by 3 or by 8. We're flexible, so should you. 7) I heard you win money if you win the belt? --> No. You win a Scoops Tee Shirt if you win the belt. 8) I am having trouble posting. For some reason, I can reply to a post but I can not post to the flash board. --> The problem is that you are not entering the flashboard, you are just clicking directly to it. If you do not go through our Enter page, then it will not allow you to post. You must first go to the nter page, then type in your slammer name and click on the button "Enter The Board".
