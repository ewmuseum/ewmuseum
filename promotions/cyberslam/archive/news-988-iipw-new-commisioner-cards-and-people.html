iIPW: New commisioner, cards, and people

14 August 2000
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Arcadia Hello I'm Arcadia and your not. Well since I last reported we have Blackdawg a new commisioner and he is on a rampage, he stripped all champs, and has begun to attack the BWO. He held a world title tournment, to decide who was the world champ it was all rewrite but the rewrites were 100 words or less, the winner of this is A^12, he is the world champ, the Gold Strap is the top slamming title with Firestorm orginally holding it but losing it to Ody Mandrell. Blackdawg has banned all the iIPW against the iBWO, with the numbers on the iIPW side, the BWO has some tension, Imperial is obviously out of the group as Sandman and Firestorm kicked the snot out of him. Now the qeustion is Big Daddy was left there after coming out of the bathroom and finding Imperial laying there. The card is also posted. Main Event A Twelve vs Sandman - No DQ Full REwrite, non-title matchup. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Upper Card Imperial vs Nickbones - Streets Title Rematch, Full rewrite. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Ody Mandrell vs Kiwi - Gold Strap match 2vs2, rewrite required. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Snuff/Nickbones vs Firestorm/Big Daddy - Slam each opponent once. Prematch required. stipulation may be added on Sunday. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Under Card Lee Burg vs Malicious - Newcomers initiation match. Full Re-write. No slamming. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Savedge vs Painmaster - Full rewrite. If Painmaster wins, Blackdawg will pay to have his old Bike put back together. I don't really know how much I like mandatory rewrites for all this stuff, they are good on occasion but I don't like having to do them every week or every 5 days Also the BWO vs. The IIPW begins to heat up with many matches in there Kiwi vs Nickbones. Firestorm vs Ody. Big Daddy vs Olympus. Snuff vs. Sandman. Imperial vs A^12 This should be odd. But back to mandatory rewrites, I don't mind the 100 word one but full ones can take up alot of space. That is also a hassle for all the slammers that have to work. I just recieved this email from the horse mouth Sandman ---------------------------------------- My fellow iBWO'ers, we began a new today, the Imperial was being very stupid and didn't know what was going on. So he has been beaten to a bloody pulp. Also Kiwi is the only one to get a title shot, no offense but everyone should wake the **** up. The guy is not active, and he does not deserve to wear the BWO shirt, I know this was a direct order from Tiny and I have no problem with Kiwi but to be BWO you have to stay active. So the BWO is down to four. ----------------------------------------Thank you and good night. That's news to me Arcadia(didn't think I was active, Blackdawg didn't even know there was a reporter) [detail withheld] [detail withheld] Bar Room Brawl
