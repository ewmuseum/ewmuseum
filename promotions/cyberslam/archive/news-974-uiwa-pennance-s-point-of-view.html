UIWA - Pennance's Point Of View

11 August 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Ebediah Springfield Its time once again for Pennance's Point Of View. This is the totally one-sided views of the UIWA's most outspoken members. The views in this report are of Mr. Pennance alone and do not express the opinions of C-Slam, UIWA Commissioner Swampking, Hammerlock News, or myself. Welcome once again to Pennance's Point of View. The column where the Lord of Extreme makes his opinions known about the UIWA's 'haves' and 'have-nots.' First this weeks Pennance Top Ten. 1(Tie). Pennance 1(Tie). JordanZombie 1(Tie). Whipple_Jr. 4. Sirstyles 5. Marley 6. Mr_splooge 7. Editor 8. Rasputin 9. The_Bowler 10. Shockmaster 1,343. AbdulIrefusetoreadswampkingspoliciesandgetsusupendedfarouk 12,123. (Falling another spot from last week, for the 2nd week in a row) Miss Tickle (Mystikal for all you non-followers of the UIWA) 15,200,250. Twinkieman (making the list for the first time in his career for defeating The_Bowler) This week, AbdulFarouk, better known as AbdulIeatglueandIamslopeheadedFarouk found himself on the receiving end of yet another suspension for not following the policies set forth by Swampking in his never-ending quest against idiots in the UIWA. This fool comes out week after week, and embarrasses himself in those long drawn out rants that mean absolutely nothing except, "I'm great, you suck." He is a laughing stock, along with his fellow sheep, Mystikal. Pennance's point of view is this: Enjoy your suspension. Please make it permanent. And take Miss Tickle with you. Some freak known as Rasputin this week decided to claim he is the holy avenger of this fed. However, no one has bothered to tell him that I am the resident dealer of God's will. He claims he has found people guilty of sin, and that he will make them pay. Sound familiar? It does to me. Pennance's Point of View is this: Please, keep on being the cheap Pennance knock off. You will never achive half the greatness I have. But it is rather flattering to have you pattern your career after one such as I. Take notes, child, you shall learn what separates a good gimmick from a star. Tuesday brought about the teaching of a lesson. Weeks ago, the Brat Pack lead by one Editor made its way to the arena, denouncing the Marquis Players, a.k.a. The Establishment. I set a challenge out for the three. The first one folded and ran away, we know. The second accepted, then retired. The third, Editor, accepted, and the match went on. However, he found that stepping into the ring, or parking lot in that case, was much different from talking about it. Pennance's Point of View is this: Editor surprised me with skill I didn't think he possessed. I am impressed, and that is a rare thing. However, it wasn't good enough to dethrone me. You may now return to your worthless career facing Shockmaster in dark matches on every card. Tonight, I look across the street and see a closed down Big Boy Restaurant with a sign stating it as the future sight of the Elite Lounge. Now, I have seen this before, with 160 Proof opening a bar to combat the Establishment Lounge. But Miss Tickle seems intent on making a fool of himself yet again, by trying a stunt that already failed. Another good stable with its weak links making fools of themselves. (NOTE: This is not a shot at the 160 Proof. There members screwed up in the UIWA and got kicked out of the Proof) Pennance's Point of View is this: Stop embarrassing the Elite and retire. A newcomer to the UIWA in SirStyles has denounced Extreme wrestling, opting for his own style, ancient greco-roman wrestling. While it in itself is an art form, it has no place in the UIWA. The sport I started my career in is much more brutal than any high school sport. And the tendency to use foreign objects to one's advantage is part of surviving in those kinds of matches. SirStyles claims that shows a lack of skill. Pennance's Point of View is this: Please feel free to ask the many people that have failed to win the King of Extreme title from me about my skill. I am just as dangerous without a weapon as I am with. Your stipulations have been laid before you, sheep. If you aren't too old fashioned to take them, I suggest you put your skill where your mouth is. Finally this week, I had the opportunity to sit in on the wonderful win Twinkieman had over The_Bowler this evening. The Bowler looked promising, but just couldn't stay foucused on the match to win against the unknown jobber. After I try to give the young lad some pointers on his skills in the UIWA, I get a very rude rebuttal. Now, he has dropped the Establishment name several times, trying the typical unknown's tactics. But this took a different twist. Now Cooper, a man claiming to be a former Establishment member, thinks he will form a new Establishment in the UIWA. Whipple Jr. quickly educated the poor slob, and at press time, we have yet to hear from Cooper or Bowler. Pennance's Point of View is this: You can get all the people you want. You still will not surpass the greatness of the real Establishment. Please, get your third man, call Swampking, and at the FPV this month, let us end your careers in utter shame. That is all for this week. Pennance is through with you. You may return to your pathetic lives. UIWA Website
