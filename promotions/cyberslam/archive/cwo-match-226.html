CWO match 226: Kristian vs Warmachine

PreMatch Comments I don't recall beating you up yet .. oh well! Kristian gets slammed in the middle of the ring. Here comes Warmachine off the ropes, a big splash...nobody home! Good timing by Kristian! Kristian slowly gets to his feet...and gets leveled by a devastating shoulder tackle!!! Kristian takes a swing...Warmachine blocks it! Legsweep and a BIG splash! Kristian is flattened! Kristian tosses him out of the ring and onto the timekeeper's table. He's nuts! Kristian climbs the ropes...a flying headbutt!!! NO! Warmachine gets out of the way and Kristian crashes through to the floor! Warmachine grabs him and tosses him back into the ring! Warmachine whips him into the ropes. He sets up for a backdrop. Kristian rebounds and nails him with a flying headbutt! They lock up in the corner...neither is giving up the advantage...oooh same thought! They rake each other's eyes! Both are blindly reaching for the ropes. We could see a bear hug here...Warmachine can't hold him! Kristian breaks the hold and slams his head into the turnbuckle. One way ticket to Turnbuckle-City as Kristian executes a picture perfect slingshot! Impressive displays from both men at this point. Kristian with the speed advantage while...Warmachine rocks him in the corner with an avalanche! No place to go!!! Warmachine rolls out of the ring for a breather...here comes Kristian flying between the ropes with a clothesline! No...Warmachine saw in coming and ducks out of the way! Kristian hits the floor hard and Warmachine tosses him back into the ring. Warmachine has got him up for a Samoan Drop...Kristian grabs his head! Warmachine takes him down, but in return was devastated by a kind of neckbreaker! Kristian whips Warmachine into the ropes...he's waiting in the middle of the ring with a backdrop... Warmachine saw it coming! He grabs him and drives him into the mat with an awesome piledriver! Warmachine with the headbutt...he stunned himself! Warmachine falls down! Kristian to the top rope...BIG legdrop from way up there! 1...2...NO! Close! Warmachine is trying to get him up...Kristian blocks it and is trying to lift him up! No!!! Both go sailing over the top rope! Slowly they climb back into the ring. Kristian undoes some of the tape on his wrist...he chokes out Warmachine!!! The ref doesn't see it from his angle! Warmachine with a stiff chop to the chest...goes for a second and Kristian rocks him with an enziguri kick to the head! It's a standoff in the center of the ring! Both men appear to have some kind of foreign objects! They swing...they connect! Both hit the ground! I think the ref saw them, but he's not doing anything! Warmachine has him in a headlock...spins him around...Kristian escapes and backs off. We could see a bear hug here...Warmachine can't hold him! Kristian breaks the hold and slams his head into the turnbuckle. Warmachine with a roundhouse right...Kristian catches it and executes an armdrag takedown! Great reflexes! Warmachine has got him! He's signaling to he crowd for a piledriver...Kristian counters with a backdrop! Kristian gets slammed in the middle of the ring. Here comes Warmachine off the ropes, a big splash...nobody home! Good timing by Kristian! Kristian off the top rope. He took way too long to get up there...axe-handle attempted and Warmachine delivers a big fist to the midsection on his way down! Kristian off the ropes. Warmachine is waiting and executes a textbook fireman's carry. Kristian is thrown into the corner...Warmachine is right behind him! A devastating Vader Attack! Rocked his world! Kristian whips Warmachine into the ropes...he's waiting in the middle of the ring with a backdrop... Warmachine saw it coming! He grabs him and drives him into the mat with an awesome piledriver! Warmachine with a stiff chop to the chest...goes for a second and Kristian rocks him with an enziguri kick to the head! Kristian gets slammed in the middle of the ring. Here comes Warmachine off the ropes, a big splash...nobody home! Good timing by Kristian! What does he have there? A roll of coins! Kristian clocks him in the jaw and about twenty bucks in change flies all over the ring! Warmachine slaps on a headlock. Kristian can't seem to power out...he lifts him into a side suplex! Up to the top rope and...BAM! Big elbow from fifteen feet up! Warmachine is climbing to the top rope...Kristian shakes the ropes! Warmachine falls right onto the turnbuckle allowing Kristian to climb up...superplex!!! Warmachine stuns him with a punch...bounces off the ropes...leapfrog by Kristian...on his way back...Kristian nails him with a Frankensteiner! The crowd goes nuts! One way ticket to Turnbuckle-City as Kristian executes a picture perfect slingshot! Warmachine stuns him with a punch...bounces off the ropes...leapfrog by Kristian...on his way back...Kristian nails him with a Frankensteiner! The crowd goes nuts! Warmachine is climbing to the top rope...Kristian shakes the ropes! Warmachine falls right onto the turnbuckle allowing Kristian to climb up...superplex!!! Kristian looks like he's going for an inverted atomic drop here. Wait! Warmachine is biting his face! This is a side of Warmachine that I've never seen before! Kristian is perched on the second turnbuckle waiting for Warmachine to turn around...flying clothesline! Right on the money! Kristian slaps on a headlock. Warmachine picks him up...bell to belly suplex! Kristian with a small package! 1...2...kickout! It's going to take more than that! Bear Hug!!! Kristian is in alot of pain right now!!! Warmachine going for the belly to belly...couldn't do it! Kristian has fallen on top of him! Kristian has Warmachine in a headlock. He's giving the sign for the bulldog! Oh...a rake of the eyes and Warmachine breaks the hold! Kristian goes for a punch, but Warmachine ducks and grabs him! He’s up on his shoulder and Warmachine nearly powerslams him through the mat! Flying fistdrop from the top rope...connects! I can't really see from this angle but I think Warmachine is busted open! Kristian with a roundhouse kick. Warmachine traps the leg...Kristian tries for the enziguri kick...missed! Warmachine with a stiff chop to the chest...goes for a second and Kristian rocks him with an enziguri kick to the head! Warmachine grabs a handful of tights. Looks like Kristian is going for a ride...No! He blocks it! Warmachine slaps on a sleeper...but Kristian drops down with a jawbreaker! He springs to the top rope, BIG double axe-handle to the head! Warmachine wraps his fingers around his throat...way up!!! And crashing down to the canvass! What a choke slam! Kristian looks pretty confident. He climbs to the top and...moonsault! Warmachine catches him and slams him to the mat! Great Power! Warmachine with a boot to the stomach...powerbomb! Pin attempt! 1...2...kickout! Doesn't get much closer than that! Warmachine rolls out of the ring. He saw someone holding a Kristian poster and grabs it! He begins tearing it up! Warmachine rolls back into the ring and attempts to make Kristian eat a piece! This match has been going non-stop and...oh! It spills out of the ring again! Warmachine whips Kristian into the guard rail! Now he tries to ram his head into the metal steps...reversted! Warmachine gets his head bounced off the hard steel and both roll back under the bottom rope! Warmachine slaps on a headlock. Kristian can't seem to power out...he lifts him into a side suplex! Up to the top rope and...BAM! Big elbow from fifteen feet up! Kristian in control. Throws Warmachine into the ropes...up for a Frankensteiner! Warmachine holds on to the top rope and Kristian comes crashing down head first! Kristian is in trouble! Warmachine grabs him and is pointing to the crowd! Kristian connects with an elbow to the face! Warmachine took way too long! Warmachine whips him into the corner...here he comes with a big avalanche! NO! Kristian ducks out of the way! I swear the ring moved a good three feet with that impact! Kristian in control. Throws Warmachine into the ropes...up for a Frankensteiner! Warmachine holds on to the top rope and Kristian comes crashing down head first! Kristian slaps on a headlock. Warmachine picks him up...bell to belly suplex! Kristian off the ropes...he leaps...he misses! Cross-body block attempted, but Warmachine ducks under it! Kristian whips him into the ropes...looking for a backdrop...Warmachine sees it coming. DDT! Kristian’s head bounced off the mat like a superball! Kristian gets slammed in the middle of the ring. Here comes Warmachine off the ropes, a big splash...nobody home! Good timing by Kristian! Warmachine looking pretty cocky...goin' for a figure four leglock, but he's posing for the fans! Kristian hooks the tights and rolls him up! 1...2...3!!! Cardinal mistake and Warmachine pays for it!!! Kristian with the win! Winner: Kristian
