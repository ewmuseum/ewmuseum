CWO commissioner's policies

a member fed's own page, captured 20 February 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Commissioner's Background Policies ... by Rictor Things I will not tolerate 1). Inactive: I define this as a slammer that rarely flashes and doesn't slam much. C-Slam doesn't need these guys and here in the IRCW we don't need that either. C-Slam has one purpose and that is to have fun. People who don't participate take away from the fun and I don't like that. If you don't participate, your gone. Plain and simple. 2). No shows: Something I absolutely hate. If you are scheduled to fight someone in a card or agree to fight and you no show, your out of here. But I understand that we are all busy outside of C-Slam, so if you can't make it for ANY REASON, Email me, have your buddy email, I don't care just let me know. Then we can work something out. 3). Match Hoarding: I don't like this one bit. You always get some guy who likes to save all his matches up and use it on some poor jobber. Not cool. I catch you doing this and I'll fine you. 4). Padding: Don't do this. I will fine you for this. I don't mind if you SOMETIMES beat on a jobber, but don't make a habit of it. They are trying just as hard to get to the top as you are, and it isn't fair when the top guy beats the crap out of the lower guy. If you do this I would GLADLY knock your power down, then let the guys you use to beat on beat on you! Also, if you have two slammers in the same fed and enjoy jobbing one out to the other, I will fine BOTH your guys. Role Playing To me the most important aspect of C-Slam is the story lines, AKA role playing. THIS is what makes it fun. My favorite slammers are those who do this often (HINT HINT, if you want to get on my good side...ROLE PLAY!!!). Role players get title shots, tournament spots and drafted, so the word to the wise. Flashing I don't want to see anything that wouldn't be heard on WCW TV. So no sexual innuendoes, no talk about families, and no 4 letter words. I don't care if you bash the other guy, just do it with out doing the above. If I see you doing anyone of these, I will first email you and warn you. After that I will fine you depending on how bad the offense. Email Same rules apply for email. No vulgar stuff, or I am going to either kick you out or fine you BADLY!!! Other stuff I demand respect from my slammers. I will not tolerate disrespect, you will be gone if this happens. I am more than willing to help with angles. If you are having trouble angles or need help in areas of your character, email me. I will help you as much as I can. If you want to include me in your role play, just let me know, and we can work something out. I want to make this as much fan for you guys as I can. So if I email you with some ideas for your character, don't get offended. I am just trying to help. Titles Here are the rules the Champs must follow. Note that these are for the singles titles here You must be an active flasher a total of about, oh, 5 flashes per week minimum is mandatory. The same goes for the contenders for the belts. The champs CANNOT slam anyone under 50%. This rule is mainly to ensure the champs don't pad. I will strip titles away for this, so don't temp me! There are only three exceptions to this rule, these are 1) If you are in a tournament, I will allow it. Thursday Melee will be a regular show every Thursday. If a Melee card is missed, there will usually be a sort of house show to make up for it. There will also be a weekly show on Monday's called, "After Burn". The nature of this show will change every week, with each week having a different type of stipulation kind of match. i.e. - 1st week will be a title decider type of show. - The next week will have a battle royal. - The next week will have a stable wars and so on. And we will also have one FPV every month. - They will mostly be on the next to last, or last Sunday of the month. - All titles will always be decided in some sort of way on FPVs. - I will be the one to make the matches, and all official matches made by me can be slammed any time on the day of the event. Types of matches. - Grudge Matches. All regular 1 on 1 matches are grudge matches. These are easy to fight with both men using one match against the other. In the event of a tie, the man with the higher percentage will fight the tie breaker. All types of wins count. -Tag Team matches. All regular tag team grudge matches are done by each member of each team fighting the two members of the other team once. If you and Joe are fighting Regis and Kathy, you use one match against Regis, and one match against Kathy. Joe will do the same. Regis will fight you and Joe, I think you get the idea. All wins will count. - World Title Matches. Regular grudge match rules. In the event of a tie, the champion initiates a tie break. Champions will defend 2 to 5 times a month. - Television Title Matches. The challenger initiates one match against the champion. You can win the title only by winning with pins and submissions. Champions will defend at least once a week. -North American Title matches. Grudge Match Rules. Same stuff as World Title. Defended 3 to 6 times a month. - Tag Title Matches. Grudge Match Rules. Challengers Must win more than 4 matches. A tie results in a successful defense for the champs. #1 contender matches. To be considered in one of these matches, you must do ok in the standings, but good on the board. Like I said, I look at flashing. That's your ticket to title shots. Rictor, CWO commissioner
