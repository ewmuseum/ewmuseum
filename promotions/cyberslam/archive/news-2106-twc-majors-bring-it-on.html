Come on guys, lets get moving. Some people have said their goodbyes, like Crazyfool, Test_, and The Crow are a few. Nature Boy though is not leaving. Here is what he had to say WOOOOOOOOO! I'll never leave. . .I love this place. Another World title for the Naaatcha boy!!!! WOOOOOOO! I think support will grow behind Nature Boy considering he is usually their UT rep. They are having a roleplay day, so you see, CSlam is still alive. Finally the UIWA Swampking is there, one person has said goodbye, they are starting to slowely return. This fed could show alot of strenth Kris Jerico has posted a couple of times, Stryfe turned tail and ran, Johhny Xs is following along with the KJ angle, Freak is there and so is Mr Splooge. This fed is showing some life, good for them. So lets have a run down shall we, I will give you ratings of what are the best feds. So here we go.
