Rapid Fire: Arazi here, and I figured I should clue your jabroni @ss in on why I did not participate in Saturday's match that I was thrown into. I verbally threatened the participants of the match, and also later offered to be the ref of the match - so they took it upon themselves to assume I wanted to be "in" the match. I would never involve myself in a match with a wannabe like Jojoke, Bubba or Carnage b/c their reputations bring me down. If you really want to know what I was doing on Saturday, I suggest you go ask Stuman himself (yeah, that's right - Dave freaking Stuart). He and I had a nice chat on AOL about things over even your head. I will part here by chastising you a bit. As a supposed reporter, and I use the term loosely, your job should be to objectively show all angles of anything you report. This does not mean you kiss everyone's booty, but that you tell the story from all perspectives. By grossly neglecting to even inquire why I was gonzo from that match was so reflective of the shoddy quality of CSlam reporting today that you represent. I dare you to print this for others, and I hope you can get someone to read this mail for you. I know it is hard to read when you're eyes are closed kissing someone's behind! I'm out, Arazi Rapid_fire
