IQFWC: Interview with Mad Hatter

23 January 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Great One Great One: This is Great One with the interview IQFWC fans have been waiting for. I am joined with Mad Hatter, the "Legend" of C-Slam. Mr. Hatter, its nice to meet you. Mad Hatter: It is nice to meet me isn't it? GO: Well yes I guess it is. MH: Look, I don't have all day. Let's get this thing over with. GO: ok ok. You have claimed to be part of C-Slam for a long time. Do you have any past info you would like to share with us? MH: I've been a part of C_Slam since 98. But did I do much then? No! IT took me a while to understand this so called game. GO: About how long? MH: Lets just say that certain people needed to teach me a thing or two. I always had the talent, just needed a kick in the *** to know how to use it. GO: Just like many slammers... I have always wanted to know... why do you refer to yourself as the "Legend" of C-Slam, if you are only in the indies? MH: Hey! I've been in the pros before. GO: Then why did you rejoin the indies? MH: Quite frankly, my slammer wasn't doing ****. HE didn't have the right attitude to deal with the damn politics that are involved in the majors. So I took my experience and knowledge, said screw you, and in the fall of 1999, Mad Hatter was born. GO: I see. But you still didn't answer my question. MH: What question? GO: Why do you refer to yourself as The "Legend" of C-Slam? As far as I know you haven't done anything grand. MH: Have you ever seen me in the ring? GO: As a matter of fact.. MH: That was a rhetorical question you idiot! I have the skill, and have shown it over and over again. Yet no one will show me the respect I deserve. That is why I am the Self-proclaimed Legend. GO: But the fall of 99 was a long time ago and you are still in the indies. MH: Hey, I was up for a promotion three months after I made my Mad Hatter debut. Hell, I evened helped a guy get in the CEW, you may have heard of him. He goes by the name of Hairy? Maybe not. Indie reporters don't know jack... GO: Ok, I think we got the point. But a quick qustion. Why anger? MH: Anger? ANGER? THe world was being handed to me on a silver plater and I was forced to leave this sport. That would make anyone pissed off. GO: Yeah, I do see your point. Now is there anythingin particular that made you join the IQFWC? MH: There was an opening. And CEW is the best major. Put two and two together. GO: Yes, CEW is known as the best. However, there is a LOT of competition for the promo. Can you give me a rundown on the certain people who you think have a shot at it, that is of course if you were removed from the running? MH: hat's easy, no one deserves it. But if i had to pick.... Randy has a shot, if he gets his act together. (LOL) Otherwise there is only one title I think the guy incharge of promotions will be looking at. GO: That of course being you. Now about Randy. You are being blamed for turning him into the biggest heel, when he was easily the slammer of fans choice. Why did you do this? MH: He was a high profile star in this fed. and didn't have a clue what this sport is all about. GO: Exactly what do you mean by that? Because you costed the man the World Title and threw him over the edge. Come on, there had to be something else. Jealousy maybye? MH: Jealousy of what? the world title? I am sick of people thinking that what I do is out of Jealousy. There is a reason to behind what I do. GO: And for the last time what?! MH: Hey, I'm really getting pissed off here. If you haven't figured it out by now, then just stay tuned. GO: And lastly, should we expect anything from you that will catch the fans eye? MH: You should always expect something from me. As far has catching a fans eye, like I give a rats *** what they think. But I will always be a force in any fed I'm in, Major or minor. Everyone has to learn to, DEAL WITH IT! (Hatter gets up and leaves) GO: Well I guess this concludes our interview. Check back later for exclusive Mortal Monday results. Coming soon. none
