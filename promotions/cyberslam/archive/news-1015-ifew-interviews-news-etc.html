Dustin Bones Thursday Terror Live!!! From The Convention Center Asbury Park, N.J. TRIPLE MAIN EVENT!!! Gauntlet Match MIAFOSO VS PREACHERMAN, CROCHUNTER, STAN, AND DIABLO Miafoso was tired of these guys calling him out. So he challenges all four of them to a Gauntlet Match. Can the former world champion over come these odds? 100% rewrite, no slamming. Miafoso must do a rewrite for each of the 4 people and they must do one for Miafoso. Loser Leaves Fed Match MAD SWINGER VS EL_BUFF_ONE One of these 2 will make the roster slimmer. The loser will have slammed his last match in the iFEW. 100% rewrite, no slamming. Doggystyle Match WOLFDOGG VS HELLSRAGE Well this is Wolfdogg's made up match. It is 100% rewrite, and it is a inferno barbwire table match. The ring ropes will be replaced with barbed wire, the ring will be surrounded by flames, and you must win by putting you opponent through a table. It will be interesting to see how this one turns out. Upper Card Hardcore title Match VANHAMMER VS CROCHUNTER Vanhammer couldn't wait till Monday but instead decided to defend on Thursday. Crochunter has a chance to be the HC champion instead of the number one contender for Monday. Slam your opponent 2 times each. DQ's and CO's are reversed. Roleplay is 2 extra wins and best rewrite is 3 extra wins. Cage Match GREATONE VS CHRISAUSTIN Chris wants Greatone and now he has him. No where to run inside a 15 foot high steel cage. Slam your opponent 2 times each. CO's and Dq's are reversed. Roleplay is 2 extra wins and best rewrite is 3 extra wins. Ladder Match FIERDOGG VS STINGER Suspended high above the ring will be a taser. FIrst one to climb the ladder and get the taser and zaps the other person will be the winner. Slam your opponent 2 times each. Roleplay is 2 extra wins best rewrite 3 extra wins. Mid Card World Title Contender Qualifing Match APOSTLE VS MAD DOGG The first of 3 qualifying matches for the World title contender match on Monday. Slam your opponent 2 times each. Roleplay worth 2 wins and best rewrite worth 3 wins. World Title Contender Qualifing Match HELL_SPAWN VS RACHEL The second of 3 qualifying matches for the World title contender match on Monday. Slam your opponent 2 times each. Roleplay worth 2 wins and best rewrite worth 3 wins. World Title Contender Qualifing Match BUBBIEJOE VS RAINE MADIA The final of 3 qualifying matches for the World title contender match on Monday. Slam your opponent 2 times each. Roleplay worth 2 wins and best rewrite worth 3 wins. LW Title Contender Qualifing Match TZAEL VS EARL VS SHAME The First of 3 LW contenders matches to see who will compete Monday for the number one contender spot. Slam your opponent 2 times each. Roleplay worth 2 wins best rewrite worth 3 wins. LW Title Contender Qualifing Match ATROCITY VS KOOFOO The Second of 3 LW contenders matches to see who will compete Monday for the number one contender spot. Slam your opponent 2 times each. Roleplay worth 2 wins best rewrite worth 3 wins. LW Title Contender Qualifing Match KRONIC VS GOOFBALL VS SUPERHOMBRE The Final of 3 LW contenders matches to see who will compete Monday for the number one contender spot. Slam your opponent 2 times each. Roleplay worth 2 wins best rewrite worth 3 wins. #1 Tag Team Title Contender DARKENED DREAMS (ARAWN AND TOD_S_ENGEL) VS THE IMPACT PLAYERZ (STAN AND SJKASH) This is to see who will Face the False Prophets on Monday. Slam your opponents 1 time each. Under Card Prove Yourself Matches MAXAM VS REVOLUTION_ Slam your opponent 4 times each.
