CWO match 81: Mr_mojo vs Coroner

undated in the source
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PreMatch Comments slamming Coroner with a stiff chop to the chest...goes for a second and Mr_mojo rocks him with an enziguri kick to the head! Coroner off the ropes...leaps over Mr_mojo with a headlock applied...flying bulldog!!! Very impressive maneuver! Coroner gets up from the mat and Mr_mojo is right there with a strong running shoulder block! Levels him! Test of strength in the middle of the ring now. Mr_mojo twists his wrists...hands to the mat...and Coroner gets his fingers stomped! Mr_mojo whips him into the ropes and goes in for the clothesline...Coroner ducks it...off the other side and a beautiful cross-body block! Only a one count. Mr_mojo in control. Throws Coroner into the ropes...up for a Frankensteiner! Coroner holds on to the top rope and Mr_mojo comes crashing down head first! Coroner whips him into the ropes. He sets up for a backdrop. Mr_mojo rebounds and nails him with a flying headbutt! Mr_mojo is perched on the second turnbuckle waiting for Coroner to turn around...flying clothesline! Right on the money! Mr_mojo looks pretty confident. He climbs to the top and...moonsault! Coroner catches him and slams him to the mat! Great Power! Mr_mojo connects witha European uppercut...Coroner nails him with one of his own! They're going punch for punch in the center of the ring! Coroner rolls out of the ring for a breather...here comes Mr_mojo flying between the ropes with a clothesline! No...Coroner saw in coming and ducks out of the way! Mr_mojo hits the floor hard and Coroner tosses him back into the ring. Mr_mojo is perched on the second turnbuckle waiting for Coroner to turn around...flying clothesline! Right on the money! An irish whip and Mr_mojo is going for the ride...Coroner off the ropes from the other side...and Mr_mojo takes him down with flying headscissors! Great move! Coroner slaps on a headlock. Mr_mojo can't seem to power out...he lifts him into a side suplex! Up to the top rope and...BAM! Big elbow from fifteen feet up! Mr_mojo whips him into the ropes. He sets up for a backdrop. Coroner rebounds and nails him with a flying headbutt! Coroner sets up Mr_mojo on the top rope for a superplex! Mr_mojo rams him with an elbow! Now from the top rope, Mr_mojo with a moonsault!!! But he's not through with Coroner yet... Mr_mojo undoes some of the tape on his wrist...he chokes out Coroner!!! The ref doesn't see it from his angle! Coroner slaps him! Mr_mojo is just standing there laughing. Now he starts to walk away...turns and NAILS Coroner with a flying forearm! What does he have there? A roll of coins! Coroner clocks him in the jaw and about twenty bucks in change flies all over the ring! Mr_mojo goes for a suplex...reversed...reversed again...Coroner blocks it and slaps him! Coroner climbs to the top rope! But Mr_mojo is right there and tosses him clear across the ring! Coroner swings and misses! He catches a boot to the gut for his troubles...and Mr_mojo slams him with a jumping DDT! Devastating! Coroner goes for a backdrop...Mr_mojo puts on the brakes and nails him with an underhook suplex! Coroner rebounds out of the corner with a flying forearm...but Mr_mojo is waiting with a stiff boot to the face. An irish whip and Mr_mojo is going for the ride...Coroner off the ropes from the other side...and Mr_mojo takes him down with flying headscissors! Great move! Coroner off the ropes...leaps over Mr_mojo with a headlock applied...flying bulldog!!! Very impressive maneuver! Mr_mojo off the ropes...leaps over Coroner with a headlock applied...flying bulldog!!! Very impressive maneuver! Coroner with a roundhouse kick. Mr_mojo traps the leg...Coroner tries for the enziguri kick...missed! Coroner off the ropes...leaps over Mr_mojo with a headlock applied...flying bulldog!!! Very impressive maneuver! Mr_mojo with a roundhouse right...Coroner catches it and executes an armdrag takedown! Great reflexes! Coroner sets up Mr_mojo on the top rope for a superplex! Mr_mojo rams him with an elbow! Now from the top rope, Mr_mojo with a moonsault!!! But he's not through with Coroner yet... Coroner with the dropkick...misses! Mr_mojo grabs a hold of his ankles...BIG slingshot! Coroner sails though the air. His head connects with the steel post!!! Out cold! Mr_mojo rolls him up...1...2...3!!! You could've counted to a hundred!!! Winner: Mr_mojo
