CWO match 239: Ammodawg vs Bad_karma

undated in the source

PreMatch Comments So i'm your ticket well I don't have time to fight with you on the board so I will fight you in the ring! come get some come get some Bad_karma gets him up for the sidewalk sla...no! Ammodawg slips out the back door. Look at Ammodawg fly! He's going for the head scissors, but Bad_karma grabs a hold of the ropes! Not going anywhere, and Ammodawg crashes to the canvas head first! Bad_karma gets up from the mat and Ammodawg is right there with a strong running shoulder block! Levels him! Bad_karma whips him into the corner...here he comes with a big avalanche! NO! Ammodawg ducks out of the way! I swear the ring moved a good three feet with that impact! Bad_karma stuns him with a punch...bounces off the ropes...leapfrog by Ammodawg...on his way back...Ammodawg nails him with a Frankensteiner! The crowd goes nuts! Bad_karma swings and misses! He catches a boot to the gut for his troubles...and Ammodawg slams him with a jumping DDT! Devastating! Bad_karma is complaining to the ref about a hair pull. I don't think he's buying it. They lock up...the ref sees a hair pull and breaks it up. Ammodawg off the ropes. Bad_karma is waiting and executes a textbook fireman's carry. Bad_karma lifts him up for an atomic drop! Ammodawg levels him with a short clothesline! Bad_karma is prone in the center of the ring! Ammodawg is on the top rope...big splash! 1..kickout! Bad_karma whips him into the ropes and goes in for the clothesline...Ammodawg ducks it...off the other side and a beautiful cross-body block! Only a one count. Bad_karma slaps him! Ammodawg is just standing there laughing. Now he starts to walk away...turns and NAILS Bad_karma with a flying forearm! Bad_karma swings and misses! He catches a boot to the gut for his troubles...and Ammodawg slams him with a jumping DDT! Devastating! Ammodawg looks like he's going for an inverted atomic drop here. Wait! Bad_karma is biting his face! This is a side of Bad_karma that I've never seen before! Ammodawg whips him into the corner...a running start for a shoulder ram and Bad_karma dodges the hit! Nothing but turnbuckle! Ammodawg into the corner...Bad_karma is right behind him...and meets a set of knees! Bad_karma goes down, but I think Ammodawg got hurt with that last move! Ammodawg off the ropes...leaps over Bad_karma with a headlock applied...flying bulldog!!! Very impressive maneuver! Ammodawg off the ropes with a cross-body...Bad_karma catches him. But he can't hold him! Both spill over the top rope! Hard bump, but both climb back into the ring. Ammodawg is leaving! He's had enough and is heading back to the dressing room. The ref's count is up to six and here comes Bad_karma! He dropkicks him from behind and now he's dragging Ammodawg back to the ring...by the ear!!! Bad_karma catches Ammodawg with a legsweep...he bounces of the ropes and gets way up there for a legdrop. Doesn't connect! Ammodawg rolled out of the way! They lock up in the corner...neither is giving up the advantage...oooh same thought! They rake each other's eyes! Both are blindly reaching for the ropes. Bad_karma slaps on a headlock. Ammodawg can't seem to power out...he lifts him into a side suplex! Up to the top rope and...BAM! Big elbow from fifteen feet up! Bad_karma climbs to the top rope...signaling for a double axe-handle. Ammodawg gets a running start and counters with a drop kick! Big mid-air collision! Bad_karma is climbing to the top rope...Ammodawg shakes the ropes! Bad_karma falls right onto the turnbuckle allowing Ammodawg to climb up...superplex!!! Ammodawg with a roundhouse kick. Bad_karma traps the leg...Ammodawg tries for the enziguri kick...missed! Ammodawg off the top rope. He took way too long to get up there...axe-handle attempted and Bad_karma delivers a big fist to the midsection on his way down! Bad_karma has him up. Loks like he’s going for a piledriver! No...a shoulderbreaker. Wait! Ammodawg grabs the ropes and takes him down with neck scissors! Ammodawg slaps on a headlock. Bad_karma picks him up...bell to belly suplex! Bad_karma gets him up for the sidewalk sla...no! Ammodawg slips out the back door. Bad_karma is trying to get him up...Ammodawg blocks it and is trying to lift him up! No!!! Both go sailing over the top rope! Slowly they climb back into the ring. Bear Hug!!! Ammodawg is in alot of pain right now!!! Ammodawg into the corner...Bad_karma is right behind him...and meets a set of knees! Bad_karma goes down, but I think Ammodawg got hurt with that last move! Ammodawg whips him into the ropes...looking for a backdrop...Bad_karma sees it coming. DDT! Ammodawg’s head bounced off the mat like a superball! Bad_karma rolls out of the ring for a breather...here comes Ammodawg flying between the ropes with a clothesline! No...Bad_karma saw in coming and ducks out of the way! Ammodawg hits the floor hard and Bad_karma tosses him back into the ring. Bad_karma is running towards Ammodawg...he doesn't see him! Ammodawg was setting up for something and just got crushed! But Bad_karma missed the mark and has fallen out of the ring! Those metal ring steps must have hurt! Ammodawg takes him down with a leg trip. Like a cat to the top rope...Big elbow from way up...but Bad_karma rolls out of the way!!! Nobody home! Bad_karma slaps on a sleeper...but Ammodawg drops down with a jawbreaker! He springs to the top rope, BIG double axe-handle to the head! Ammodawg goes for a punch, but Bad_karma ducks and grabs him! He’s up on his shoulder and Bad_karma nearly powerslams him through the mat! Bad_karma sets up Ammodawg on the top rope for a superplex! Ammodawg rams him with an elbow! Now from the top rope, Ammodawg with a moonsault!!! But he's not through with Bad_karma yet... Ammodawg rebounds out of the corner with a flying forearm...but Bad_karma is waiting with a stiff boot to the face. Bad_karma rolls out of the ring. He saw someone holding a Ammodawg poster and grabs it! He begins tearing it up! Bad_karma rolls back into the ring and attempts to make Ammodawg eat a piece! Ammodawg with a small package! 1...2...kickout! It's going to take more than that! Bad_karma gets him up for the sidewalk sla...no! Ammodawg slips out the back door. Ammodawg whips him into the ropes...looking for a backdrop...Bad_karma sees it coming. DDT! Ammodawg’s head bounced off the mat like a superball! Bad_karma slaps on a sleeper...but Ammodawg drops down with a jawbreaker! He springs to the top rope, BIG double axe-handle to the head! Bear Hug!!! Ammodawg is in alot of pain right now!!! Ammodawg is perched on the second turnbuckle waiting for Bad_karma to turn around...flying clothesline! Right on the money! Mule kick from Ammodawg...but Bad_karma cartwheels out of the way. A little showboating going on! Bad_karma with an atomic drop...blocked! Ammodawg connects with a powerful spinning mule kick! That'll turn your lights out for good! Ammodawg off the ropes...leaps over Bad_karma with a headlock applied...flying bulldog!!! Very impressive maneuver! Ammodawg has Bad_karma set up for the Ammo Bomb!!! This could be it...wait! Bad_karma's manager has just jumped up on the ring apron with a steel chair! Wham!!! Stiff shot to he head and the ref is calling for the bell! Ammodawg takes home a win by DQ...but this war is far from over! Winner: Ammodawg
