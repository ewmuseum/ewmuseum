CWO match 234: Mr_mojo vs Daos

PreMatch Comments Moving up... That's a fact. You don't see Daos go to the rope very often, but there he goes! He takes too long! Mr_mojo grabs him and launches Daos across the ring!!! Daos goes for the pin! One...foot in the ropes. Is that a "SCOOPS" sign in the third row? Daos sneaks behind...chicken wing! Mr_mojo manages to slip out before he could lock it in. Very Lucky! Daos is prone in the center of the ring! Mr_mojo is on the top rope...big splash! 1..kickout! I've never agreed with some of Mr_mojo's tactics, but this is nuts! He's pulled off the top turnbuckle pad and is trying to ram...Daos turns it around and slams Mr_mojo's head into the exposed metal! Mr_mojo looks enraged! He rams Daos's head in! They're both busted open! Mr_mojo looks pretty confident. He climbs to the top and...moonsault! Daos catches him and slams him to the mat! Great Power! Mr_mojo whips him into the corner...he's right behind him. Daos leapfrogs behind and catches him in a chicken wing! Mr_mojo is going out! No. He manages to slip his foot between the ropes. Mr_mojo off the ropes...leaps over Daos with a headlock applied...flying bulldog!!! Very impressive maneuver! Daos rolls out of the ring for a breather...here comes Mr_mojo flying between the ropes with a clothesline! No...Daos saw in coming and ducks out of the way! Mr_mojo hits the floor hard and Daos tosses him back into the ring. Daos climbs on his back...camel clutch! Wait! He couldn't cinch it in and Mr_mojo is able to power out. Daos is trying to be sneaky by untying the top turnbuckle while Mr_mojo is on the outside setting up a table! Where are all of the referees?!?!? Daos whips him into the ropes. He sets up for a backdrop. Mr_mojo rebounds and nails him with a flying headbutt! Daos swings and misses! He catches a boot to the gut for his troubles...and Mr_mojo slams him with a jumping DDT! Devastating! Mr_mojo goes for a headbutt...Daos latches on a nasty claw hold! Great reflexes! It almost looks like he's palming a basketball in there. An irish whip and Mr_mojo is going for the ride...Daos off the ropes from the other side...and Mr_mojo takes him down with flying headscissors! Great move! Daos gets him on the mat with a well placed right hand...he's going for the claw! Mr_mojo rolls out of the ring to avoid the hold. He rolls back in before the ref begins to count. Daos looks a bit dazed. Mr_mojo climbs the turnbuckle...Daos snaps out of it and catches him in a great fireman's carry! Mr_mojo with a roundhouse right. Blocked! Daos grabs the arm and throws it into a hammerlock! Into the corner, and the crowd starts chanting as Daos rams his head into the turnbuckle again and again! Daos grapevines the leg...going for the head to complete the STF...Mr_mojo rolls over for a pinning combination! 1...2...kickout! Daos was lucky he didn't get pinned right there! Daos is prone in the center of the ring! Mr_mojo is on the top rope...big splash! 1..kickout! Daos with a roundhouse right...Mr_mojo catches it and executes an armdrag takedown! Great reflexes! Daos slaps on a headlock. Mr_mojo can't seem to power out...he lifts him into a side suplex! Up to the top rope and...BAM! Big elbow from fifteen feet up! Daos gets him down on his stomach and locks in the camel clutch! I can hear those vertabrae popping from here! He lets Mr_mojo up! He's not finnished punnishing him! Daos sets up Mr_mojo on the top rope for a superplex! Mr_mojo rams him with an elbow! Now from the top rope, Mr_mojo with a moonsault!!! But he's not through with Daos yet... Impressive displays from both men at this point. Mr_mojo with the speed advantage while...Daos rocks him in the corner with an avalanche! No place to go!!! Mr_mojo is heading back to the locker room! The crowd is really giving it to him...WAIT! Here comes Daos with a fire extinguisher! He sprays him in the face and carries him back to the ring! Mr_mojo looks pretty confident. He climbs to the top and...moonsault! Daos catches him and slams him to the mat! Great Power! Mr_mojo applies a headlock. Now Daos with a forearm to the kidneys and he executes a picture perfect Russian leg sweep! Daos climbs on his back...camel clutch! Wait! He couldn't cinch it in and Mr_mojo is able to power out. Daos is prone in the center of the ring! Mr_mojo is on the top rope...big splash! 1..kickout! Daos stuns him with a punch...bounces off the ropes...leapfrog by Mr_mojo...on his way back...Mr_mojo nails him with a Frankensteiner! The crowd goes nuts! Irish whip into the corner, reversed, reversed again...Daos levels him with a short clothesline! But man does he look dizzy! Mr_mojo applies a headlock. Now Daos with a forearm to the kidneys and he executes a picture perfect Russian leg sweep! Mr_mojo with a roundhouse right. Blocked! Daos grabs the arm and throws it into a hammerlock! Into the corner, and the crowd starts chanting as Daos rams his head into the turnbuckle again and again! Daos gets him on the mat with a well placed right hand...he's going for the claw! Mr_mojo rolls out of the ring to avoid the hold. He rolls back in before the ref begins to count. Daos gets up from the mat and Mr_mojo is right there with a strong running shoulder block! Levels him! One way ticket to Turnbuckle-City as Mr_mojo executes a picture perfect slingshot! Daos whips him into the ropes and goes in for the clothesline...Mr_mojo ducks it...off the other side and a beautiful cross-body block! Only a one count. Mr_mojo is perched on the second turnbuckle waiting for Daos to turn around...flying clothesline! Right on the money! Daos slaps on a sleeper...but Mr_mojo drops down with a jawbreaker! He springs to the top rope, BIG double axe-handle to the head! One way ticket to Turnbuckle-City as Mr_mojo executes a picture perfect slingshot! Mr_mojo shoots in with a European uppercut. Blocked! Daos turns it into an armbar. He's really applying the pressure. We'd like to take this opportunity to remind you that for your own safety, as well as the safety of the wrestlers in the ring, please refrain from throwing food and other objects at your computer. They lock up...both men looking for an advantage. WOW! Mr_mojo tosses Daos into the corner! Great show of strength! Daos slaps him! Mr_mojo is just standing there laughing. Now he starts to walk away...turns and NAILS Daos with a flying forearm! Daos with a stiff chop to the chest...goes for a second and Mr_mojo rocks him with an enziguri kick to the head! Daos looks a bit dazed. Mr_mojo climbs the turnbuckle...Daos snaps out of it and catches him in a great fireman's carry! We'd like to take this opportunity to remind you that for your own safety, as well as the safety of the wrestlers in the ring, please refrain from throwing food and other objects at your computer. Mr_mojo is going for a crucifix. Can he get it!?!? Daos breaks free...spins around...full nelson! Great counter! Irish whip into the corner, reversed, reversed again...Daos levels him with a short clothesline! But man does he look dizzy! Daos slaps on a sleeper...but Mr_mojo drops down with a jawbreaker! He springs to the top rope, BIG double axe-handle to the head! One way ticket to Turnbuckle-City as Mr_mojo executes a picture perfect slingshot! Forget about it!!! Mr_mojo ties up Daos in the ropes and he's using him like a human punching bag! Oh NO! Daos connects with a solid kick to the groin! OUCH! It's Daos with a nerve pinch. He's digging those fingers in...and Mr_mojo takes him down with a shoulder roll. Nice counter! Daos stuns him with a punch...bounces off the ropes...leapfrog by Mr_mojo...on his way back...Mr_mojo nails him with a Frankensteiner! The crowd goes nuts! Daos with the dropkick...misses! Mr_mojo grabs a hold of his ankles...BIG slingshot! Daos sails though the air. His head connects with the steel post!!! Out cold! Mr_mojo rolls him up...1...2...3!!! You could've counted to a hundred!!! Winner: Mr_mojo
