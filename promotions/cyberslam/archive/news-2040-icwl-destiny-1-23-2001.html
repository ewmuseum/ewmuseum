iCWL - Destiny - 1- 23 -2001

22 January 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Eaves Dropper Well can you believe it is already time for another Destiny? My how time flies when your, umm, nevermind. On with the matches and predictions. Match #1 - IC Title Match Jabroni(Champ) vs Drifter Jabroni continues to dominate all aspects of the IC title. Can a new guy put an end to his run? Well even though Drifter is an untested new guy. This reporter is going to predict something unheard of. My Prediction - Drifter pulls off the upset and dethrones the self proclaimed Greatest IC Champ ever. Match #2 - Any Title Shot Match Fat_Guy vs Akuma In a match that Fat Guy may or may not have wanted he will face Akuma in a "first blood" match with the winner receiving a shot at any title they want on Friday How can you go against a guy with that much girth? My Prediction - Fat_Guy wins Match #3 - Special Stips Match Law vs Crystalmeth Crystalmeth has called out all stables as he plans on ending them one by one. UU was his first call out and Law is one of UU's main men. So the commish is placing them in the ring with this stipulation. If Law wins then Crystalmeth may not sneak attack or beat down any UU members for 2 weeks. If Crystalmeth wins he will get a shot at any UU member he chooses. When it comes down to it, how can you go against a guy with a name like that. My Prediction - Crystalmeth wins Match #4 - Grudge Match Afro_Thunder vs Fsp FSP likes to sneak up and attack people. One of the people he picked on wants revenge in the ring. Afro_thunder is not one to be messed with so he gets his wish. The Afro man should get his revenge in this one. My Prediction - Afro_Thunder wins Match #5 - Stable Rep Battle Switchblade vs Rage In a preview to what the commish has planned out for Friday's Mayhem Clutch member Switchblade will face UU member Rage in a battle of the stables I think its safe to say that this one will be an interesting match to pay attention to. The first official, Clutch v UU match. And the vet should prevail My Prediction - Switchblade Wins Match #6 - Underdog Title Match TheRock(Champ) vs Soulreaver Really not much can be said about this match. Must be why its the Underdog title. Sticking with the champ on this one. My Prediction - TheRock retains. Match #7 - World Title Match Raze(Champ) vs Zylo This match has everyone on edge, can the current iCWL champ keep the Greatness at bay? Or will the man who held the belt longer than anyone retake what he thinks is his? The second of the Clutch v UU matches might see something of a surprise to all. This reporter doesn't see it that way. My Prediction - Zylo wins his belt back. Ok well there is the action packed Destiny for the 23rd. Be sure to not miss this one as the Stable Wars are just heating up and the possession of that World Belt could stay or go with one. Alright I'm out, be back next time. Eaves none
