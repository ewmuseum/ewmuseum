CCWe Chevalier leaving the CCW Elite?

19 January 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
CCWe The Gossip Column Gossip back with a correction Yesterday I said Luvgate left the Knights when Bomberman got a boost off of him, wrong Knight, it was Freud who slammed him instead upon his death, thats correct Freud has retired to the big ring in the sky. Due to another suspension for violating the jobbing/padding policies Maxx Justice has suspended another slammer first was Luckee indefinitely, and yesterday was Massacre Sr both of the 160 Proof. Hardleg of Xtasy wasn't to thrilled with the recent happening, if you're gonna break the rules don't do it in the CCW Elite. Justice will be swift! The change to the Friday Fury card due to the supension follows as such.The last match on the card was the Triple Threat Match, thats been yanked. With Massacre Sr being suspended and stripped of the Underdawg Championship our Triple Threat Match was turned into a FATAL 4 WAY match CCWe UD Title Match Aragorn vs Hardleg vs Sapper vs Deathwish Slam each slammer TWICE each, point system used in case of a tie. [Gossip]Hardleg will take the UD Title hands down In other news, Bomberman snapped yesterday and turn into foul mouth psychopath, attacking Sapper backstage with a chair, these two slammers go back along way and a little mudslinging started, but if Bomberman holds true to form, we won't see much of him after the card today. In other news, Big poppa graced the CCWe with his appearance also, not denying he was contemplating RETIRING, but his friends and family talked him out of it [specualtion] They shouldn't have, if you have seen Big poppa on the mic you will see indy talent at best. In other news, its the old version of the Miller Lite commercial between the CCWe and the CFL about the Face of Faces Chevalier CCWe - Taste Great CFL - Less Filling Read the following insertion from Chevalier from the CCWe boards Chevalier: You poor deluded boy. You're right when you say I can opt to stay or go. But since Lars tore up my contract the CFL slammers have gotten a case of weak knees. Do you hear them call my name now that I can show? NO! You don't. SE sounds more like Michael Jackson than he does Barry White. Hey at least he can hit the high notes when he sings now. I don't go by THEIR schedule, I don't go by anyone's schedule... least of all yours. When I go to the CFL it will be because it's the right time. I will listen... if they call me.... if they challenge me NOW then I will go! The only bad publicity is no publicity, will Chevalier leave the confines of the CCWe and venture to the CFL? Write it down on your calendar, don't count on it!!! In other news, Prototype continues with his school of evil, his latest was telling Ashlira she was just an experiment of Nocturnal Supremacy. When Proto sees someone down, he kicks them even more, gotta give the kudos for that. Will he teach Alex asylum the fine art of kicking someone when they're down? Will Ashlira postpone Retirement and fight Prototype back? Last week if you had asked Gossip which of the big 3 were better, Last week would have ranked CWFe, CFL, CCWe This week its ranks CFL, CCWe, CWFe. A little more activity and the CCWe will claim the top spot. I'm not one to spread rumors Gossip none
