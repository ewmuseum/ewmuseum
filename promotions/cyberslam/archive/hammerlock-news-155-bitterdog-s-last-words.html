BitterDog's Last Words

Hammerlock News — undated in the source
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Well, I guess, that I should go ahead and make my retirement speech. A lot of you knew this was coming. I have been in Cslam for almost a year. I have had a great time. I have met a lot of people from this game that I am glad to call friends. I have had minimal success in this game, but that is not why I joined. I joined because, quite frankly, this is a game. And that is how I would like to think that I played Cslam. Like a game. There are a few people I would like to thank that made my stay here fun. Bagelguy and Dark_knight: What can I say? We started out together. We were each other’s constant in the iRCW with that lame brain Lord. You guys drove me to raise my game up to levels I never thought possible. We never pulled off the swerve of all swerves, but it was fun talking about it. To the H.O.P. and all those that were ever in the H.O.P.: Thanks for making the H.O.P. the best stable that I have ever been in. The drinks are on me boys. MJ and Dio: Thanks for giving this snot nosed, rookie punk a chance. I’ll never forget it. Limit and Myth: Two of the best commishes out there. Both gave me advice and showed that being a commish can be fun. Also showed me that commishes aren’t jerks, they’re just not all there. LOL. iRWA: What can I say? A fed that didn’t give the commish too much grief. I don’t care what the voters think. You are the best indy fed out there. And finally, Chaosbill, you were my mentor. Showed me the ropes, and even now, you are still teach the game to others. And to everyone else out there that play this game because it is fun, not because they want to win, win, win. CBF, CEW, and every other fed, just because I am retiring, doesn’t mean you won’t see me again. This is the D-O-G and it’s been real. Freedom
