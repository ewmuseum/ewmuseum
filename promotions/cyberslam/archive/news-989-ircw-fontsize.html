Typeface Typeface here, a man with a rhyme, let's get right to results and ntoe waste any time, 'cause you wanna know who's got the belts, and this'll hit you so hard that it'll leave welts. Word. **Thursday Rage** Keebler defeats Tr*CENSORED*ke... Syrus no-shows, allowing NBK to defeat Snoball... Exterminator trashed Reinhardt and Goalie Bill... waitasec, The Stickman got trashed? Oy... Speed managed to defeat Manowar... yet another upset... Static beat his way through Bloodknight, Deathknight, and Thunderbolt... Title news! Jolt remain the champs only due to outside interference by face stable extraordinate, The Knights. In a match that has lead to some serious trash-talking, the Phenoms claim the Knights are hypocrits, calling themselves faces but resoting to outside DQs to win the titles. Even stranger, Sugar seems to agree with them. Freak defeated Page to retain the Richmond Title... erm, excuse me, "The Shiny". Kiddpain destroyed The Infinite to retain the Blood and Guts title, and I'm with Maverick on this one... nobody's dominated this belt since Da Franchise had it. And he only lost it because he planned it that way. Kiddpain may have to be crowned the new iRCW King of Hardcore. "The Prodigal Son" Andy Macdaddy, the Southern States champion, was unable to appear for his Southern States triple threat defense, and so it turned into a regular match. Your winner, and new Southern States Champion... and setting a record for Fastest Trek From Rookie To Titleholder... Mister Zinc! And finally, in the Hevyweight title match, "The Toxic Avenger" Haz shows just why he holds the title, as Sugar really had no chance at all here. Good thing the Knights didn't interfere here, too. **** That's what happened, it's in the past, but hey the weekend flew, Monday got here fast, I got tonight's card for you to see, now e'ryone watch Fire and have a par-tee! **Monday Fire** It's the weekly rookie rumble... Butchclaymor, Morrigan, Execution, Hogan, and T Bone in one ring, Syrus, Fat Tony, Mech, Exo Skull, and Buffdude in ring two, and Yokosunu and Maxim in ring three. Most of these guys are rookies, a few have shown signs of life, Morrigan just seems to have vanished, Exo is already gone... when all's said and done, nobody really cares about his match unless someone shows signs of life. After that, it's JRC taking on Snyper, and Kurse (apparently the iRCW's new fashion adviser) taking on Snype's partner Panic. Hey, it's a new version of the rookie rumble... only it's a rookie tag-team warning match. Kinda. The Amazing Jiggalos take on The Canadian Alliance... both semi-inactive, one had a history of no-showing, all need some work on thier mic skills... hey, something good might come out of this. Might. Your Hardcore fans will get a treat tonight, as Freak takes on Keebler and Hfly in a hardcore match... technically, a three way match, but Keebler and Hfly are stablemates, so Freak might be in trouble. Next, it's a mid-card match worth of a main event, as Heavyweight Champion Haz takes on TV Champion Greenhell Junior. We'll make it an inferno match just to keep things hot. What's this doing in the middle of the card? It's a triple-threat tag team match next, as Resident Evil, The Evil Knights, and The Coldbloods (with Maverick subsituting snoball in place of The Infinite). The winning team just might get a contendership match. Even more importantly, all three teams are seeking to gain (or re-gain) respect. That's what's up for grabs here. Contendership time! It's The Original Gangstas taking on Iron and Exterminator, the newest Tag With No Name. Will the Gangstas stay together, with new heel Reinhardt and face-keeping NBK? Hey, if you wanna know, look at the main event... Sugar takes on Knight for a shot at the Richmond title... hey, it's a Knight fight. Cool. The Southern States contendership is up for grabs between Static and "The Last Boyscout" The Infinite. There's talk of blackmail and other stuff going on, too, so things should get strange. And finally, the Heavyweight Contendership match... we've god Thunderbolt, The Rookie Phenom (wait, I think Zinc deserves that title more... T-Bolt's been around longer) in one corner... Iron in the other corner... and Speed, if he can recover from the beating at the hands of Goalie Bill, will be in the third corner. ... waitasec. Static beat Thunderbolt as part of the four corners match on Rage... hey, Static, if you wanna air any views, let me know, 'kay?... And now, the Main Event... First, a little setup. Reinhardt had decided he's getting no respect, and 'gone heel'. NBK, his tag-team partner, tells him he's sick of his whining... and challenges him to a Balcony Deathmatch? Last Man Standing rules, and the winner gets a shot at Kiddpain for the Blodo and Guts title at Rage. **** Just one last thing before I go, a bit of info I've got to know, There's still a tie on who, you want me to interview, Knight or Coldblood, tell me who I should, sit down and chat, so we can... can... aw, crud. Okay, okay, I romise never to rap again. Until then, you keep slamming, and I'll keep reporting. none
