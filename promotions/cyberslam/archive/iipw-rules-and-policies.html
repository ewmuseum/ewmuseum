Date: 12/20/99 Policies ... by The Xpocalypse Inactives Here in the iIPW, it is "VERY" important that you are an "ACTIVE" slammer. Not just in slamming your matches, but in flashing as well. If you are not active on the boards, you will not be considered for any titles, nor will you be put on any kind of Pay Per View Cards. Flashing Let’s get right into it, flashing is the number one reason to play this game. Not every great slammer is a 80% winner. Some of the all-time greats were just average in the ring, but special on the boards. What makes a great flash? Having a valid point is the key. Flashes that just take up space will not only be ignored by other slammers, but I will frown upon them. Flashing is a way to make the game fun and draw heat and thus draw matches. A quiet 80% slammer is a chore, while a noisy 40% slammer is a joy. Hopefully both ends will live up to being flashers. Role-play is important. Let me say it again. ROLE-PLAY IS IMPORTANT! It creates feuds and sets up matches, it makes reading the board a joy and it makes playing the game fun. There is nothing more dreary than a dead flash board filled with flashes that say nothing. . Some basic rules of the flashboard, if you can’t say it on TV, don’t say it here. If you get a message saying you used a “bad word” you must change it and either ----. or ****. You must remember, many young people play this game, and what would you think if your little brother read something like that? I've watched the OOC explosion and the trouble it has caused. Because unlike some leagues, I think you are all adults, I will not outlaw OOC posts unless forced to. But know this, I will not tolerate using OOC for personal attacks on this board! The slammer caught doing this will be suspended IMMEDIATELY! You think someone is cheating, bring it to me. Or discuss it off the board. Jobbing and Padders 1. I feel jobbing has taken over the game of C-Slam. This will stop. 2. First we have the low man that slams the high man to get the high man more powerful. This will result in Match reduction and or power fines. I realize the High ranking slammers will try to get the Lower slammers to slam them. That will happen. 3. Second you have the Padder that Slams only the low ranking slammers to bulk up there ranking. This also will be a fine of Power and or match reduction. 4. In all if you push the jobbing and the Padding you will get fined. 5. Then you have the Stables that job to the Slammer that has the best ranking in their stable. There is no reason that stablemates should slam stable mates. Unless it's either a match for a Card. Or a legit dispute within the Stable. I've seen cover ups of Jobbing and Padding before and I see right through it. 6. If you participate in these ILLEGAL activities, you will ONLY get one warning. After that, the consequences will be heavy and harsh... Events After all of the rules, we finally get to something fun... Monday Madness This will be our main card, all titles will be on the line, and some specialty matches. You must work to get put on this card, a spot just won’t be given to you. Smashing Thursday- Our other weekly show, will be toned down a bit, some titles may be on the line, but mostly specialty matches and contender matches. This is where some lower class slammers can show off their stuff and get that long awaited push. End of the Month This is the time for the FPV and the major tournament. Near the end of the month, an eight man tournament will be started, these 8 will be the top eight slammers of hte iIPW for that month.(Both slamming and RPling are considered.)The winner of this will move up to the majors along with the man who recieves the flashing promo. Before the touramnent, it’s everyone’s favoritve time, the FPV, the biggest card of the month. This is where the best players in the game put on a show for millions of people around the world. This is when I expect the most of you, both slamming and flashing. Now of course, there are always some rules to go along, when you slam matches for a card, you must preceed them with the correct pre-match comments. Just state the card, and the type of match you are in. That’s not too difficult is it? Also, on the day of a card, YOU MAY NOT slam matches, unless they are booked matches. This makes it a lot easier on a lot of people, abide by this rule and I will stay happy with you. Last, but not least, emailed results would be appreciated. This is not a must, but it would make my job a lot easier. Titles You knew this one was coming, we will have 4 major single titles, and 1 Tag Team Championship. World Title The most presitgous title in the iIPW, you win this, and your on top of the FED. Extreme Title Our FED’s Hardcore Title, some think this is more important then the World Title, all of these matches will be 3 vs. 3, no holds barred, anything goes. TeleVision Title By far, the hardest title to hold for long period’s of time, this belt is defended at every event. UnderDogg Title For all slammers under 55%. After three days above that mark, you will be stripped of the title, and two worthy contenders will get a shot at it. Tag Titles A great stepping stone for you career, you can show that you “play well with others” and that you can hold a title. In these matches, you slam each opponent once. In total that will come up to 8 matches. In the case of a Tie, we have the ever important points system... Points Sytem This will only come into affect if we need a tiebreaker, otherwise number of wins will decide the winner. 4 points for injury or interference 3 points for Finisher win 2 points for win pinfall 1 point for win by DQ/Countout Board Resets Will happen once a week, sometime Sunday most of the time. Final Thoughts Every day youshould check the boards before you slam a match. There may be an IMPORTANT announcement. When I post announcements, I will use indicators such as *****"MESSAGE*****so that everyone will know. When I am participating in "Role Play", I will use a regular heading for the title. "DO NOT USE ANY HTML'S in your TITLES, only in the body of your Message. This was a ruling handed down by the Higher Powers in the CyberSlam. Suspensions will be handed out if this is violated. All final decisions are made by me and that is the word. If you need help with developing a story-line or developing your character, then contact me via Email.. iIPW Staff Color Man-Jamie “Uno” Owen Play by Play-Josh “Doggy Style” Quinn Interviewer/Journalist-”Scoops” Andres Camera Men-Erick “Corky” Thomas and Al “SideBurns” Smith Ring Announcer-Tom “TL3” Lappin Security-Blake “Scoob” Severson and Gabe “Guerilla” DeLong Trainor-”Tiny” Tim Frank Secretary to theCommish-Miss Nicki Background info Xpocalypse is a former Minor League baseball player, who eventually got a job working for Fox Sports. When talks began of Fox starting their own wrestling corporation, he jumped ship and joined the CSlam crew, becoming a commish of the iIPW. He has a wife, but no kids, often his wife will accompany him, along with his two body guards, Pat and Andy. Contacts You can always contact me, either through email, AOL(Jordan332), or ICQ [detail withheld]). I am always willing to help, and make you better in any way.(That sounds really corny) XPOCALYPSE
