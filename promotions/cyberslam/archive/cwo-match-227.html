CWO match 227: Azrael vs Aragorn

undated in the source

PreMatch Comments Gotta win some day ... Azrael has his legs...trying to roll Aragorn over for the Boston Crab...and Aragorn powers out! Looks like it's going to take alot more than that to put Aragorn away! Azrael is charging Aragorn with a clothesline...Monkey flip! Man that Aragorn is fast! This crowd is really divided between Aragorn and Azrael. All managers have been barred from ringside so hopefully we can settle this feud once and for all! Azrael slaps on the sleeper! Aragorn is fading...the ref comes over to check! 1...2...the hand comes up! Azrael releases the hold and delivers a forearm to the kidney for good measure. Aragorn whips him into the ropes...Azrael ducks under the clothesline...from the other side...Azrael with a dropkick to the knee! Azrael off the ropes for a shoulder block. Aragorn sidesteps and applies a painful nerve pinch that drops Azrael to his knees! They're circling..Aragorn locks him up from behind for a Russian leg sweep. Azrael grabs the ropes to break the hold. Aragorn sneaks behind...chicken wing! Azrael manages to slip out before he could lock it in. Very Lucky! A clothesline to the back of the head sends Azrael to the mat! Incredible speed as Aragorn is right on top of him...STF! Again, Azrael refuses to submit and the hold is broken after 1 minute. Aragorn whips him into the ropes...Azrael ducks under the clothesline...from the other side...Azrael with a dropkick to the knee! Aragorn slaps on a painful armbar...a duck under and Azrael turns the tables with an arm lock. Aragorn off the ropes for a shoulder block. Azrael sidesteps and applies a painful nerve pinch that drops Aragorn to his knees! Azrael slaps on the sleeper! Aragorn is fading...the ref comes over to check! 1...2...the hand comes up! Azrael releases the hold and delivers a forearm to the kidney for good measure. Aragorn has him down, knees on his back...trying to roll him over for the bow and arr...can't get it locked in. Azrael is too close to the ropes and breaks the hold. Aragorn slaps on a claw hold. Azrael counters and slaps on a claw of his own! How long can these two keep this up? Somebody had better get some aspirin! Aragorn is screaming at the ref that there was a hair pull on that last move. Azrael sneaks up...and jams his fingers down Aragorn's throat! Mandible claw!!! You don't see Azrael go to the rope very often, but there he goes! He takes too long! Aragorn grabs him and launches Azrael across the ring!!! Azrael off the ropes...sleeperhold! Aragorn doesn't have it completely locked in and Azrael slips out the back door. Azrael off the ropes...sunset flip! Can he pull him down? No! Aragorn drops a big fist to the face. Now he's got the legs...rolls Aragorn over...Scorpion Deathlock! Azrael reaches the rope after 45 seconds! A clothesline to the back of the head sends Azrael to the mat! Incredible speed as Aragorn is right on top of him...STF! Again, Azrael refuses to submit and the hold is broken after 1 minute. Wow! Azrael is getting into it with a fan on the outside! Apparently the kid is wearing a Aragorn t-shirt! Azrael executes a belly to back suplex...steps over...into a Boston Crab! Aragorn manages to get his arm over the bottom rope. Lucky! Irish whip into the corner, reversed, reversed again...Aragorn levels him with a short clothesline! But man does he look dizzy! Aragorn has him down...he grapevines the legs and tries to roll him over...can't do it! Azrael kicks out with authority! Aragorn has him down...he grapevines the legs and tries to roll him over...can't do it! Azrael kicks out with authority! Irish whip into the corner, reversed, reversed again...Azrael levels him with a short clothesline! But man does he look dizzy! Aragorn reaches around...looking to lock in an abdominal stretch...BIG hiptoss by Azrael! Aragorn off the ropes...sunset flip! Can he pull him down? No! Azrael drops a big fist to the face. Now he's got the legs...rolls Azrael over...Scorpion Deathlock! Aragorn reaches the rope after 45 seconds! Aragorn goes for the pin! One...foot in the ropes. Is that a "SCOOPS" sign in the third row? Aragorn goes for a headbutt...Azrael latches on a nasty claw hold! Great reflexes! It almost looks like he's palming a basketball in there. Azrael looks a bit dazed. Aragorn climbs the turnbuckle...Azrael snaps out of it and catches him in a great fireman's carry! Azrael is digging down deep...Aragorn coming off the ropes...Azrael catches him in the fall from grace!!! This crowd is on their feet as the ref hits the three count! That was one for the record books!!! Winner: Azrael
