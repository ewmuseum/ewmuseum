iFEW- Interviews, news, etc.

15 August 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Dustin Bones This Dustin here reporting for the hottest ticket in town, the iFEW. Today iFEW slammer MAFIOSO, is my guest. Mafioso, Thanks for joining me. Alright Dustin thanks for having me, who better to represent this fine fed, iFEW. Nobody but you of course. So Mafioso you have been in iFEW longer than most slammers here now. How have things changed since Juevenile took charge? Well, I think the fed talent has improved. He has brought in many more slammers for me to tear apart. Some good, some not so good. Also, he has really improved the quality of the cards. I think he is a good commish. He could very well be the reason the fed is so active, the slammers enjoy working for a commish of this caliber. Basically he's a kick{censored} commish, not to be imitated or copied, in any way. I am glad he's our commish, he has really helpe the fed out. A few months back you and Bubbiejoe had a thing going against Wolfdogg what ever become of that? Well, as times changed so did Mafioso. I began to become better and felt that I was not in Wolfdogg'd league. I mean I could've beat the guy with just my right foot. As you probably already know. All you'd have to do is watch one of his rants on TV. as for Bubbiejoe, gimmie a call honey! Oh a little romantic interest I see. Well I don't blame you there. Who then would you say is the person you've had the most difficulty with? I can't really say anyone an iFEW has given me real difficulty yet. There are a group of guys who like to annoy me and talk about how they could beat me in a match anytime any place. But, if I had to choose a person who will give me trouble, it's definitely got to be Hellsrage. We've fought one other time, and we tied, so if we are booked in a match again, it'll be one for the record books. Who are those people you mentioned? Well they aren't really worth mentioning, but Preacherman has challenged me, Diablo has been annoying me, the rook Crochunter wants to fight me, and the loser Stan wants to get in the ring with me. So, what I did was I asked Juevinile to book a gauntlet match. It should be this Thursday, so if any of you iFEW fans, wanna see CSLAM at it's best tune into that very match. Mafioso will win another one to go down in history. You're currently with Darkened Dreams now, what happened to KORRUPT? To tell you the truth, KORRUPT fell apart. We used to be strong with many elite members, but our leader Karnage got promoted to AWO, and everyone else disappeared. I was left to lead a group with no members. So, one day I decided to join the one stable that had given me the most trouble, DARKENED DREAMS. Speaking of the AWO you may be one of the top candidates for the promo. If you do get the promo will you rejoin KORRUPT with Karnage or will you stay with DD? I'm not really sure just yet. It all depends on what AWO is like. Karnage will always be one of my friends, and so will Mantus. Who knows, Karnage might even want to join DD, because I think KORRUPT is done for. DD has been growing rapidly, new members are joining each day, Karnage could be the next. So do you have anything you want to ask me? Yeah Dustin, I wanna know when your going to fight "Bad" Bill Sharpdon for title of "Supreme Reporter". You know Sharpdon did help me in the fight against Diablo last night. I don't know if you can take him. What do you say? Well first of all I'm a reporter, not a slammer, so I don't see a fight happening. Bill is my understudy you see, I am the teacher, he the student. He's got a long way to go. I am the Superslinky reporter, the only thing he is good for is for sharpening my pencils and ordering lunch. One more thing. Why did you choose to report for iFEW? I'm sure a "superslinky" reporter like you had many offers from other feds. Truthfully with all the Majors wanting me, Rolling Stone, Spin, and Deer Hunter's DreamHomes. I felt like I needed a challenge. So what's more challenging than an upstart indy fed with a rookie Commish? I had to take it. Another challenge is living off this pathetic salary that Juevi's got me on. I'll have to talk to him about that. Up next is the segment known as Superslinky's Word Blitz. It's the word comparison with a new name. Ready? Alright shoot IMPACT PLAYERZ Elite stable, need more slammers in iFEW Canadian Wrestlers They eat, sleep, and talk wrestling 24/7 Mcdonalds or Burger king Burger King, I'm the Bacon Double Cheeseburger man. The future of the iFEW and C-slam? One word. ME. Well thanks once again. I know you are a busy man. Keep up the good work. Thanks for having me and I look forward to seeing a real article about the true star of the iFEW. Phew some people. And the nerve of him, making passes at Bubbiejoe like that. Doesn't he read. She went out with me last week. ME...Superslinky!!! Not some ego-maniacal, all though successful slammer. But alas, my work is never done. Arawn 's interview is up next in Rollin da Bones with Dustin Bones. Goodbye, and thanks for reading. Dustin none
