bs Four Way Elimination Match Titles on the line Big_Daddy(WC) vs Jay_Biess(TVC) vs Fire_Blade vs British_Kid slam each of your opponents once winner will walk out with the Heavyweight title. Second place slammer walks out with the TV title. Result: British_Kid comes out on top in this one Big_Daddy came in second Your new Heavyweight Champion ... British_Kid!!! Your new TV Champion ... Big_Daddy!!! Four Way Elimination Match Contender spots Anaconda vs Murt vs Darealman vs Cuto slam each of your opponents once winner walks out with a #1 contender spot for the Heavyweight title. Second place slammer will get a shot at the TV title. Result: Anaconda was the victor and will gets the number one spot Murt came in second to get a shot at the TV title Pitfighter Title Match Jiffy Store Brawl Mr_T vs Dave_Sims vs Drater vs Sundown slam each of your opponents once. This fight will take place in a local 7-11 convenience store. Winner will walk out as the new Pitfighter champion. Drater walks out of the store with the Pitfighter title in hand Result: New Pitfighter champion ... Drater!!! Revolution vs Sav_Edge (Sav_edge wins via a mistake on Revolutions part) Sorrow vs Mr_Champion_ Neither Bothered to show Nastays vs Dr_Death Nastays wins via no show Bittrbubba vs Gorehound No show on both parts Ragedemon vs Thelord Thelord wins on a no show weapon_omega vs Greenbert Omega showed but didnt do anything and Greenbert was no where to be found. That's the card. Look for something else later this week. none
