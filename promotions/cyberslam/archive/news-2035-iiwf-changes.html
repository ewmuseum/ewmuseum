iIWF: Changes

20 January 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Flash_Lightning Yes that's no typo. The iIWF is getting better day by day. The mid carders are now nipping at the veterns' heels. They are using their newly aquired experience to get into the world title contendership. So once again I have to ask, what is this story about? This time it's about the slammers. The champions. Underdog: The underdog champion, Twiztid, is a newly aquired asset to the iIWF. This rookie has a very promsing future ahead of him, but since I'm not one to give compliments without something else I have to say that Twiztid's posts aren't as "enjoyable" to read. I know he's a rookie but if he's gonna be a champion he's got to stop being so rookie-esk. This means at least four paragraph posts and two sided fights with exciting twists. Tag Team: Once again I have to compliment the tag team division. This is by far the best tag division I've seen in the iIWF. The tag Champions are Chameleon and Agent Orange other wise known as KONA. Chameleon and Agent Orange are the two "vets" in the fed. Agent Orange by far the leader of the stable. Chameleon, although, one of the best slammers in the fed, is in a bit of a slump and can't compare to experience of Agent Orange Cham will be lucky if he makes the promo list this month. All I can say about Agent Orange is that he comes off as a little lazy when it comes to the re-writes. I think that if it mean defending he world title he'll write like a pro. Hardcore: The hardcore divsion is ummm... well... wait a minute they're good! The hardcore champion, Frozen, is definately moving up in the rankings of the iIWF. Since my article saying he could do better, well, he's done better. (And you all critisized my critsism.) Frozen still has a long way to go if he wants to reach his full potential. If you ask me, when he reaches that potential he'll be in the CRF. He's like a freakishly strong little kid and growing strongly into a strong adult. TV: The television champion, Sho Kosugi,is a great slammer. He's grown greatly this month. He too has a lot of potential and is moving into the high rankings of the fed. All sho needs now is to expand on his abilities and he will be on top of the fed in no time. World: The world champion is Agent Orange. I would compare him to the genius kid who does Alebra in elemetry school. His abilities and resume is amazing. Although most people consider him to be a "rookie" or a vet who just switched personas. Agent Orange is well-known around the iIWF so well known me critsizing him would be like me saying Freedom can't report. The iIWF is growing and changing everyday. I have taken everyone into consideration and I have come up with a promo list. Agnet Orange Chameleon Uhaul Tchosengame TheJan Finale The six of these people will have to step it up if they wanna eliminate two. My predictions for promtions are Chameleon (if he can get out of his slump quickly) and Agent Orange (the host of the up coming award show). So in all the iIWF is still and will always be (until I say otherwise)the best indy fed in C-slam. I'm Flash Lightning and that's the latest Shocking News!!!!! none
