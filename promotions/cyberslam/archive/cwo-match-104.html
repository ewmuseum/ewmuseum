PreMatch Comments Hi again Ebola goes for a punch, but Warmachine ducks and grabs him! He’s up on his shoulder and Warmachine nearly powerslams him through the mat! Warmachine grabs him with a big bear hug! Those ribs are...no! Ebola nails him with an earringer! What a wake up call!!! Say goodbye to that spine! Warmachine rocks Ebola with a nasty atomic drop! Warmachine rolls out of the ring. He saw someone holding a Ebola poster and grabs it! He begins tearing it up! Warmachine rolls back into the ring and attempts to make Ebola eat a piece! Warmachine is running towards Ebola...he doesn't see him! Ebola was setting up for something and just got crushed! But Warmachine missed the mark and has fallen out of the ring! Those metal ring steps must have hurt! Ebola leaves the ring and returns with a chair! He goes to swing, but the ref grabs it! Ebola undoes some of the tape on his wrist...he chokes out Warmachine!!! I can't really see from this angle but I think Warmachine is busted open! Ebola off the ropes. Warmachine is waiting and executes a textbook fireman's carry. Warmachine grabs a handful of tights. Looks like Ebola is going for a ride...No! He blocks it! Impressive displays from both men at this point. Ebola with the speed advantage while...Warmachine rocks him in the corner with an avalanche! Those metal ring steps must have hurt! Warmachine lifts him up for an atomic drop! Ebola levels him with a short clothesline! With a shoulder block, Warmachine knocks Ebola to the canvass. Ebola with a sunset flip! 1...2...Warmachine rolls it over! 1...2...Ebola turns it into a small package! 1...kickout! Ebola in control. Throws Warmachine into the ropes...up for a Frankensteiner! Warmachine holds on to the top rope and Ebola comes crashing down head first! Ebola goes for a punch, but Warmachine ducks and grabs him! He’s up on his shoulder and Warmachine nearly powerslams him through the mat! Ebola goes for an armdrag takedown...Warmachine rocks him with a short clothesline to the back of the head! Warmachine slaps him! Ebola is just standing there laughing.
