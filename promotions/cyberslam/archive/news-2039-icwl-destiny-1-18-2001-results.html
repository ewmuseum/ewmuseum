iCWL - Destiny - 1-18-2001 - Results

22 January 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Eaves Dropper Well another Destiny has passed and some interesting events have taken place before and after Thursdays Destiny. Everyone who knows the iCWL, knows that The Clutch is becoming a well established Stable here. Zylo, the former World Champ, has formed his own little stable to try and steal some, if not all, of the gold from The Clutch. He has gotten the services of Law, Fat_Guy, Rage, Noreaster. If you notice, all four of them are newcomers to the iCWL and truly only Fat_Guy has had an impact. If you know what I mean. It should be interesting to see just how this all goes down. Ok, and with that here are the matches, results and how I did. Match #1 - IC Title Match Jabroni(Champ) vs Noreaster Jabroni continues to notch up the successful title defenses. Can he eclipse the run Zylo had as world champ. Only time will tell. As usual I start off the card with a bullseye. So I'm 1-0 so far. Match #2 - Triple Threat Tag Match Infinite vs Eddie_Payne vs Switchblade Switchblade tried to throw a monkey wrench into this match when it was announced by naming, Payne, his partner for the match. This would have made it Eddie_Payne & Infinite vs Switchblade & Eddie_Payne. Being the wily commish that he is, Wallace switched it up, so to speak, to a triple threat match where the winner would get the belts and name his own partner. Clutch have the advantage right? Wrong, Infinite pulls out the upset and has to name his new partner now. Considering that this match was changed from when I predicted this one doesn't count against me. Still 1-0 Match #3 - Underdog Title Match TheRock(Champ) vs Deathshift Well not really much to say about this one, it was a good match with the champ retaining easily. Thats two for the reporter, whooo hooo. Im 2-0 Match #4 - IC Title #1 Contendership Rumble Maple vs I_Dont_Flash vs Superloco vs Paperbagboy vs Drifter vs Fury vs Perfect Storm Lots of people lose their rights here in the iCWL as only 3 show and one gets himself DQ'd for slamming matches not on the card. The three who showed, Superloco, Drifter and Fury. Drifter wins a shot at the IC title while Fury gets no cards for a week for not following directions And the streak stops, couldn't have seen that one. Im 2-1 Match #5 - Handicapped Title Shot Match Zylo vs Rage & Law Well Zylo may have proved once and for all his dominance over two guys at once. Although it must be said that both Law and Rage are stablemates of Zylo's. Did the champ persuade them to lie down for the match? Who cares, Zylo would probably have won either way. Back on the winning track now, that'll put me up to 3-1. Match #6 - World Title Match Raze(Champ) vs Afro_Thunder These two Clutch members wowed the crowd from start to finish but in the end it was the Champ prevailing and starting his run as champ. Well, can't win em all so to speak, Im 3-2 so far. Match #7 - Pink Slip Rumble Thrash vs Stalin vs Falcon vs Firestarter vs Craven Everyone gets their walking papers, sad, truly sad. Well I thought about this and considering no match took place. It doesn't count against me. Besides, who could have possibly predicted all five would no show? Well there you have it, I finished up 3-2. Not my finest hour but still respectable as it was over 50%. This puts me at 22-8. Can you say, Outstanding. Now to answer those burning questions from last week. Yes we did and alot. No one yet has done it. Neither, The titles are outside The Clutch He sure can Not just yet, but I will Alright so with that I leave you with the questions for the upcoming week. Can the Unholy United start to take some of the Gold that The Clutch has away? Have we heard the last from Stalin? And does anybody care? Truly no titles changed hands this time, can all the champs continue this trend? Can Fat Guy get any bigger? Is it alright to mix brights with whites? And thus another action packed report is coming to a close. Normally I would thank all those who assisted me in the production of this, but well lets face it. I did it all myself. Aren't you proud? Alright, I'll be back real quick with the next Destiny card. Don't miss it. Eaves_Dropper none
