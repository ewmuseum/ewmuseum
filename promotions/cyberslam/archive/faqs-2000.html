FAQs

the hub's own FAQ page, captured May 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
FAQs 1) Does CyberSlam take any real skill? (ie; quick hitting buttons like on a video game) It's takes strategy skill, not quick hitting buttons skills. Some of our best Slammers have managed to make the playoffs repeatedly and one even won three championships. It is a combination or role/playing game and online wrestling game. It features a simulator, as well as flash boards where you can role play. 2) Who comes up with the matches? Does each FED have a "booker" ? You select who you want to wrestle, by clicking on a pop down menu that shows all the wrestlers in your FED. However, each fed does have a commissioner who does book the title bouts and tournaments. Now this aspect of CyberSlam is run totally by each commish, who develops the tournaments and the fed titles. These titles are separate from the playoff belt which takes place at the end of the month. The playoff belt is an important belt, in that the holder of that belt is promoted to a major fed OR is allowed to particpate in the Umtimate C-Slam (read guidelines for more detail). 3) Who comes up with the angles? All the players (we call them Slammers) do. The angles are developed through our Flashboard and by emailing other Slammers. 4) Are the winners pre-determined? No. Our online simulator looks at your Slammer's inherent talent (which are based on the moves that the Slammer has chosen when they signed up), number of wins, number of losses and number of matches. 5) Can I pick the Slammers that I would like to go up against? You select who you wrestle, but by the same token they can select you as their opponent. 6) When do the playoffs or Ultimate CSlam start This month they start 2 days before the last day in the month. So if the month has 31 days it would start on the 29th. On the 29th all the Slammers in all the Feds will have their records reset to zero. Over the next 24 hours all of the active slammers within each fed battle it out to see who ends up with the best record. On the 30th , the slammers with the best records (winning percentage - this means even to a tenth or a hundredth or a percentage point - DO NOT GO FOR A TIE - unless you have identical records only one slammer will advance) in each fed are selected to fight for the CSlam Belt. Because of the nature of the Internet there are no hard rules, it may start at 3 or at 8 or end by 3 or by 8. We're flexible, so should you. 7) I heard you win money if you win the belt? No. You win a Scoops Tee Shirt if you win the belt. 8) I am having trouble posting. For some reason, I can reply to a post but I can not post to the flash board. The problem is that you are not entering the flashboard, you are just clicking directly to it. If you do not go through our Enter page, then it will not allow you to post. You must first go to the enter page, then type in your slammer name and click on the button "Enter The Board".
