CWO match 211: Annihilator vs Brainchz

undated in the source
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PreMatch Comments none With a shoulder block, Annihilator knocks Brainchz to the canvass. He’s off the ropes...BIG Legdrop!!! Brainchz slaps on the sleeper! Annihilator is fading...the ref comes over to check! 1...2...the hand comes up! Brainchz releases the hold and delivers a forearm to the kidney for good measure. Brainchz whips Annihilator into the ropes...he's waiting in the middle of the ring with a backdrop... Annihilator saw it coming! He grabs him and drives him into the mat with an awesome piledriver! Annihilator is charging Brainchz with a clothesline...Monkey flip! Man that Brainchz is fast! Annihilator goes for a roundhouse right...backdrop by Brainchz over the top rope! Brainchz climbs out after him and slaps on a cobra clutch! The fans are on their feet! Now he throws Annihilator back into the ring at the ten count. They're circling..Brainchz locks him up from behind for a Russian leg sweep. Annihilator grabs the ropes to break the hold. Annihilator with a roundhouse right. Blocked! Brainchz grabs the arm and throws it into a hammerlock! Into the corner, and the crowd starts chanting as Brainchz rams his head into the turnbuckle again and again! Annihilator is going for a crucifix. Can he get it!?!? Brainchz breaks free...spins around...full nelson! Great counter! Test of strength going on...no clear advantage. The ref calls for a break as they end up in the corner. Brainchz has his legs...trying to roll Annihilator over for the Boston Crab...and Annihilator powers out! Looks like it's going to take alot more than that to put Annihilator away! Brainchz goes for a crucifix! Will he get the pin? NO! Annihilator brings him down with a Samoan Drop! Irish whip into the corner, reversed, reversed again...Brainchz levels him with a short clothesline! But man does he look dizzy! A clothesline to the back of the head sends Annihilator to the mat! Incredible speed as Brainchz is right on top of him...STF! Again, Annihilator refuses to submit and the hold is broken after 1 minute. Brainchz slowly gets to his feet...and gets leveled by a devastating shoulder tackle!!! Annihilator gets him up! The crowd is up on their feet and BAM! Sidewalk slam! Brainchz has got a one way ticket to Suplex City...and Annihilator is supplying the ticket! Way up and just as long down! What power! Annihilator shoots in with a European uppercut. Blocked! Brainchz turns it into an armbar. He's really applying the pressure. Bear Hug!!! Brainchz is in alot of pain right now!!! Annihilator goes for a headbutt...Brainchz latches on a nasty claw hold! Great reflexes! It almost looks like he's palming a basketball in there. Brainchz executes a belly to back suplex...steps over...into a Boston Crab! Annihilator manages to get his arm over the bottom rope. Lucky! Annihilator whips Brainchz into the ropes...Brainchz grabs onto the top rope. He's not going anywhere. Brainchz takes a swing...Annihilator blocks it! Legsweep and a BIG splash! Brainchz is flattened! Brainchz gets him on the mat with a well placed right hand...he's going for the claw! Annihilator rolls out of the ring to avoid the hold. He rolls back in before the ref begins to count. Brainchz has got a one way ticket to Suplex City...and Annihilator is supplying the ticket! Way up and just as long down! What power! Annihilator off the ropes...Brainchz with a great leapfrog! Annihilator puts on the brakes and rocks him with a swinging neckbreaker! Annihilator whips him into the ropes...he gets Brainchz up on his back. Can't do it! Brainchz slips out of a potential Samoan Drop. Lucky move! Annihilator off the ropes...sunset flip! Can he pull him down? No! Brainchz drops a big fist to the face. Now he's got the legs...rolls Brainchz over...Scorpion Deathlock! Annihilator reaches the rope after 45 seconds! Brainchz looks a bit dazed. Annihilator climbs the turnbuckle...Brainchz snaps out of it and catches him in a great fireman's carry! Brainchz whips Annihilator into the ropes...Annihilator reverses it...Annihilator nails him with a big knee lift! Brainchz goes down! Annihilator grabs a handful of tights. Looks like Brainchz is going for a ride...No! He blocks it! Brainchz is in trouble! Annihilator grabs him and is pointing to the crowd! Brainchz connects with an elbow to the face! Annihilator took way too long! Annihilator throws Brainchz into the ropes. He goes for a hiptoss...but Brainchz puts on the brakes! Slaps on an abdominal stretch! Annihilator is complaining about a hair pull and the hold gets broken. Annihilator looks ripe for the picking! Brainchz grabs his ankle and it appears we have a figure four on the way...No! Annihilator stops him with a boot to the lower back. Annihilator whips him into the corner...he's right behind him. Brainchz leapfrogs behind and catches him in a chicken wing! Annihilator is going out! No. He manages to slip his foot between the ropes. Brainchz off the ropes. Annihilator is waiting and executes a textbook fireman's carry. Brainchz gets him on the mat with a well placed right hand...he's going for the claw! Annihilator rolls out of the ring to avoid the hold. He rolls back in before the ref begins to count. Brainchz off the ropes...cross body block! Annihilator catches him! Turns it into a nasty shoulderbreaker! A clothesline to the back of the head sends Annihilator to the mat! Incredible speed as Brainchz is right on top of him...STF! Again, Annihilator refuses to submit and the hold is broken after 1 minute. Brainchz whips Annihilator into the ropes...he's waiting in the middle of the ring with a backdrop... Annihilator saw it coming! He grabs him and drives him into the mat with an awesome piledriver! Definite bad blood between these two! Annihilator is complaining to he ref about a hair pull and...NO! Brainchz cheap shots him with a fist to the back of the head! Why doesn't the referee disqualify him!?!? Annihilator with a boot to the stomach...powerbomb! Pin attempt! 1...2...kickout! Doesn't get much closer than that! Brainchz has him down, knees on his back...trying to roll him over for the bow and arr...can't get it locked in. Annihilator is too close to the ropes and breaks the hold. Annihilator throws Brainchz into the ropes. He goes for a hiptoss...but Brainchz puts on the brakes! Slaps on an abdominal stretch! Annihilator is complaining about a hair pull and the hold gets broken. Brainchz is out of it and...Oh! Annihilator applies the The Annihilator..It's all over!! Annihilator has sent a message to all the other wrestlers that he's back. Winner: Annihilator
