Up is down: Black is white

16 August 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Doc Doc Holidaze, Sr Newsman and a helluva nice guy...walks in through the Front door of The CCWe arena. He puts his bags down and lights up a Fat stoogie... Doc: Ahhh, Home...Yo, you there - You seen Lars?! Reject: Lars, I thought you were looking for the Navy Demon! Doc: What?! Aren't you reject - The former Nice guy? Reject:Not anymore...Bwahahahahahahahaha Doc continues down the hall past the Bickering Merc and PE...he looks in.. Doc: Hey fellas! Still feuding with the All-stars?! PE: All stars!? Damn du...They're dead...Now we have to put up with the Knights blantant OOR crap. Doc: Knights are doing out of ring stuff!? Geez, that means they are bad now right!? Merc: Ahhh..no, they are still wussy do-gooders. Doc: I think I'm getting a headache... A little further down the road, Daos pops out in front of Doc... Doc: OH JEEZUS! Don't hit me Daos! Please for the Love of Cabo, don't kill an old man... Daos: Aww, do not be afraid fellow evil-slayer. I mean my breathren no harm...Here let me help you up from down there... Doc: No way! I know of the Back-Stab! Forget you.... Doc runs away looking back at the "Good" Daos? He runs smack into the chest of Mark Nelson aka The Family Man... Doc: MARK! Thank God! I'm so frickin' lost...Daos good, Knights Feud, Holyevil is good, Wolff, Toy and BBM of the AG are ruthless, Reject is bad, I don't know what the Fudge is going on... FM: Are you Amish?! Doc: No, you know I'm not..I'm just a little sheepish over all the face-changes At least you never change... FM: SAPPISH?!! Did you say SAPPISH!? YEARGGGHHHH! FM picks up Doc and throws him into the wall. The Plaster cracks as Doc slides down the wall. FM quickly picks up the Vet Newsman and chockeslams him through a table. Doc groans in pain as FM sneers and walks off... From down the hall, Lars sees the heap that is Doc and rushes to the table... Lars: Doc! Hey man, welcome to the CCWe... Doc: $#@#$ You! We have to talk about renegotiating my contract...I'm too old for this SH#$#! none
