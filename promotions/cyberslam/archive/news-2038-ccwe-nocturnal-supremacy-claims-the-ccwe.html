CCWe Nocturnal Supremacy Claims the CCWe

21 January 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
CCWe The Gossip Column Gossip here to eat crow, on my predictions I was hit and miss on the Friday Fury card. Nocturnal Supremacy stole the show with Brutal swerving Ashlira (who officially said goodbye yesterday) Brutal talked the talk, and he walked the walk. Joining Prototype, Deathwish, and Alex Asylum in being a force in the CCW Elite. The school of evil 101 is now in session Right now the NS has to be the stable to be measured by, here me out Assassins Guild - Aragorn (they have fled like rats on a sinking ship) Knights - Luvgate,Bomberman,Chevalier (reduced to a 6 man tag team) 160 Proof - Luckee,Massacre sr,Sapper,Krotch (Suspensions hurting the Proof) Xtasy - Rattlesnake,Hardleg (the smart thing would to align with another stable) Righteous Violence = (who? look at the stable page) Vengence,Bigbad_momma,Deathkiller Nocturnal Supremacy - Well oiled cohesive unit right now, can they keep this momentum going? I asked the Commish on the stable situation in the CCWe [Comments by Maxx Justice] "At the time NS is the dominate stable in CCWe...no questions about it." Freud is gone, Ashlira retires, leaving Reject, Fey lucifer, and Big poppa as free agents, next month of promos will have to step up and get right in the mix. I'm not one to spread rumors but There are some big names coming to the CCWe next month and they are not promos Gossip none
