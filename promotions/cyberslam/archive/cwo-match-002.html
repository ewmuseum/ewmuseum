CWO match 2: Alucard vs King_zero

PreMatch Comments Here comes KZ! Alucard stuns him with a punch...bounces off the ropes...leapfrog by King_zero...on his way back...King_zero nails him with a Frankensteiner! The crowd goes nuts! Impressive displays from both men at this point. Alucard with the speed advantage while...King_zero rocks him in the corner with an avalanche! No place to go!!! Alucard undoes some of the tape on his wrist...he chokes out King_zero!!! The ref doesn't see it from his angle! From the second turnbuckle, Alucard with the flying fist drop...King_zero gets the leg up! Alucard gets a jaw-full of boot! Alucard is climbing to the top rope...King_zero shakes the ropes! Alucard falls right onto the turnbuckle allowing King_zero to climb up...superplex!!! King_zero with a roundhouse right...Alucard catches it and executes an armdrag takedown! Great reflexes! King_zero with the headbutt...he stunned himself! King_zero falls down! Alucard to the top rope...BIG legdrop from way up there! 1...2...NO! Close! King_zero connects witha European uppercut...Alucard nails him with one of his own! They're going punch for punch in the center of the ring! One way ticket to Turnbuckle-City as King_zero executes a picture perfect slingshot! King_zero with a small package! 1...2...kickout! It's going to take more than that! King_zero tosses him out of the ring and onto the timekeeper's table. He's nuts! King_zero climbs the ropes...a flying headbutt!!! NO! Alucard gets out of the way and King_zero crashes through to the floor! Alucard grabs him and tosses him back into the ring! Alucard looks like he's going for an inverted atomic drop here. Wait! King_zero is biting his face! This is a side of King_zero that I've never seen before! King_zero off the ropes...leaps over Alucard with a headlock applied...flying bulldog!!! Very impressive maneuver! King_zero connects witha European uppercut...Alucard nails him with one of his own! They're going punch for punch in the center of the ring! Alucard looks pretty confident. He climbs to the top and...moonsault! King_zero catches him and slams him to the mat! Great Power! King_zero slaps on a sleeper...but Alucard drops down with a jawbreaker! He springs to the top rope, BIG double axe-handle to the head! Alucard off the top rope. He took way too long to get up there...axe-handle attempted and King_zero delivers a big fist to the midsection on his way down! King_zero is climbing to the top rope...Alucard shakes the ropes! King_zero falls right onto the turnbuckle allowing Alucard to climb up...superplex!!! King_zero off the ropes...leaps over Alucard with a headlock applied...flying bulldog!!! Very impressive maneuver! One way ticket to Turnbuckle-City as Alucard executes a picture perfect slingshot! Alucard with a stiff chop to the chest...goes for a second and King_zero rocks him with an enziguri kick to the head! King_zero backs him into the corner with a series of knife-edge chops...climbs the ropes for what looks like a...jumping DDT! No, wait...Alucard has a firm hold on those ropes and King_zero jumps down. No damage. Forget about it!!! King_zero ties up Alucard in the ropes and he's using him like a human punching bag! Oh NO! Alucard connects with a solid kick to the groin! OUCH! King_zero with a roundhouse right...Alucard catches it and executes an armdrag takedown! Great reflexes! King_zero whips him into the ropes. He sets up for a backdrop. Alucard rebounds and nails him with a flying headbutt! Alucard swings and misses! He catches a boot to the gut for his troubles...and King_zero slams him with a jumping DDT! Devastating! Alucard backs him into the corner with a series of knife-edge chops...climbs the ropes for what looks like a...jumping DDT! No, wait...King_zero has a firm hold on those ropes and Alucard jumps down. No damage. Alucard slaps him! King_zero is just standing there laughing. Now he starts to walk away...turns and NAILS Alucard with a flying forearm! King_zero goes for a backdrop...Alucard puts on the brakes and nails him with an underhook suplex! An irish whip and King_zero is going for the ride...Alucard off the ropes from the other side...and King_zero takes him down with flying headscissors! Great move! King_zero whips him into the ropes and goes in for the clothesline...Alucard ducks it...off the other side and a beautiful cross-body block! Only a one count. An irish whip and Alucard is going for the ride...King_zero off the ropes from the other side...and Alucard takes him down with flying headscissors! Great move! King_zero with a roundhouse kick. Alucard traps the leg...King_zero tries for the enziguri kick...missed! Alucard looks pretty confident. He climbs to the top and...moonsault! King_zero catches him and slams him to the mat! Great Power! Alucard is prone in the center of the ring! King_zero is on the top rope...big splash! 1..kickout! Test of strength in the middle of the ring now. Alucard twists his wrists...hands to the mat...and King_zero gets his fingers stomped! Alucard has King_zero right where he wants him...a fistfull of tights and they're up on the turnbuckle. Superplex? NO! King_zero pokes Alucard in the eyes and down they go! King_zero rebounds out of the corner with a flying forearm...but Alucard is waiting with a stiff boot to the face. King_zero off the ropes...he leaps...he misses! Cross-body block attempted, but Alucard ducks under it! Alucard whips him into the corner...a running start for a shoulder ram and King_zero dodges the hit! Nothing but turnbuckle! Alucard is perched on the second turnbuckle waiting for King_zero to turn around...flying clothesline! Right on the money! King_zero with the dropkick...misses! Alucard grabs a hold of his ankles...BIG slingshot! King_zero sails though the air. His head connects with the steel post!!! Out cold! Alucard rolls him up...1...2...3!!! You could've counted to a hundred!!! Winner: Alucard
