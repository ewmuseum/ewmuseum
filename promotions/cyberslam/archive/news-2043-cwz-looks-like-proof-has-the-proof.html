CWZ: Looks like Proof has the Proof.

23 January 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Phantom Joe Malone Good afternoon CWZ about twenty minutes ago I was given this video by one of the grips for the CWZ, it shows some very strange happens in the CWZ. Just watch for yourself and see if you can figure out who Mr. X is. The Manta-tron inside the CWZ arena flickers to life across the screen runs the message "Proof TV...... Proof TV..... Proof TV....." as the words fade out Gator is seen in the hall through the new "Proof TV" chatting with someone who is hidden slightly by the shadows. The two men are in a somewhat heated argument but it is obvious that they are not going to come to blows. As the "Proof TV" camera focus in on the two men the sound equipment is better able to catch what is being said.... Gator - Look man, that is not what we agreed on. You said that it would only cost Proof the regular amount. Stranger - Look what I gave you was worth at least 3 times what I normally receive Gator - I am not arguing that, what I am saying is that you should not be changing your prices after the fact. Stranger - Hey if you do not want anymore-just say so and I'll walk Gator - No no I must admit your presence is a great benefit to us. So here take your blood money Gator tosses a huge wad of money at the stranger... Stranger - Thank you and do not worry what I have for you is well worth the cost. Gator - It better be As the stranger turns away a glimpse of some lettering is caught on his jacket...Across the Manta-tron once again Proof TV.... Proof TV flashes then as quick as it arrived it is gone... As you can see for yourself there was three numbers on that jacket thats right 187 has a mole feeding info to the Proof. Now I don't want to start anything but take this as you see it. Thats it from me for now. THE PHANTOM FADES.................................. none
