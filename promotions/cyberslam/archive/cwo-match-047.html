Coroner rolls out of the ring for a breather...here comes Ammodawg flying between the ropes with a clothesline! No...Coroner saw in coming and ducks out of the way! Ammodawg hits the floor hard and Coroner tosses him back into the ring. Coroner whips him into the ropes and goes in for the clothesline...Ammodawg ducks it...off the other side and a beautiful cross-body block! Only a one count. Coroner slaps him! Ammodawg is just standing there laughing. Now he starts to walk away...turns and NAILS Coroner with a flying forearm! Ammodawg is pinned..1..2.shoulder up and what's this. Yes it's Ammodawg's patented Ammo Bomb move. 1...2...3 That's it Ammodawg has won in a stunning upset Winner: Ammodawg
