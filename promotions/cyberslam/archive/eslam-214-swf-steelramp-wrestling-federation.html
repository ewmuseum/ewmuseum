[name withheld] Greetings, My name is Mr Steel, and I sort of inherited this privilage from the past site owner. His e-fed was In The Ring, and it was highly successful but due to personal reasons the past owner agreed to our deal..[ finally ] We bought out the steelramp.com domain, and with that came a whole load of hits, contacts, members, and exactly the right sort of audience that we needed. What would we do with this space?? Hmmm... an e-fed. But not any other e-fed..oh no, we have seen the SouthPark wrestler federations, the Money Systems, the combined e-feds, and more recently, the Poser feds.. Well.. you will have to wait and see the next in the series after our federation is released. Our site is underwork as we speak, with all the scripts being created by a top team of designers, our second domain is getting registered right now, and we already have a full roster. After recieving 64 applications in 4 days. So, what are you waiting for, check out the federation, and I look forward to posting here again, Mr Steel SWF: Steelramp Wrestling Federation
