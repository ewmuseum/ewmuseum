CEW: Hardcore Hell: Jan. 01

23 January 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Extreme:Your source for unbiased news HardKore Hell Opening up the camera shows Extreme outside of the CEW arena. Wearing his usual polo shirt and blue jeans he looks a little more agitated than usual. If that's possible. "Hello Cslam and both of my adoring fans. I'm here outside of the CEW arena with a very special report on HardKore Hell: Jan. 01. I told you Cslam, you ask for a report, your going to get one. Now without further delay let's look at this month's participants." Crimescence Savannah Gob Paxs Reno Assistant Vlad Jb_Famous "A very diverse group of slammers in a very unique tourney. From my time in the CEW a while ago I recognize 2 of the 8 names. This could be interesting. I'm going to simply put rate the performances of all 8 combatants. Starting with the first round losers, and then work my way up the ladder to the HardKore Hell: Jan. 01 champion." One and Done Jb_Famous:Well Jb was a newcomer to the CEW that I heard had alot of potential. Well in HardKore Hell he didn't show hardly any of that potential. He was quickly dispatched of by Vlad in the first round and the man hardly put up a fight. I've heard rumors he's gone AWOL from the CEW and after a performance like the one he had, I would to. Assistant:A Right to Censor member in a HardKore Tournament? Jesus that's like having Terry Funk be your teacher for Technical Mat wrestling. Well as to be expected Assistant didn't make it past the first round but she did happen to put up a good fight. After her first round loss Assistant has picked it up a notch in the CEW and is a force to be reckoned with. Watch out guys this is one tough lady. Paxs:"The one time UT winner is now just a shell of his former self as this match showed. The ancient one as I like to call him seems to have little in the tank and should maybe consider a retirement party. If he's going to be serious about becoming a threat again in the CEW he's got one hell of a big hill to climb in front of him. The rookie Gob made quick work of him in the first round and Paxs still shows no signs of life. Come on man you had a good ride, it's time to call it quit's. Savannah:Well the other slammer that was around the last time I was exits in the first round as well. Savannah though makes the best of her time in the tourney by giving the best match of the first round. She took the young rookie Crimescnece to the limit and then some before finally bowing out. This lady said she had finally found that killer instinct again and I think I have to agree. But with the recent departure of her lover Nakoma, it's going to be hard for her to carry on. Semi-Finals: Good not Great Crimescence:Although he's only a young pup in the CEW this guy has some skills. After beating a very good Savannah in the first round Crimescence gave another very strong performance in the semi's before finally bowing out at the hands of Gob. Crimescence seems to have a very big upside and it appears the CEW has a major player for some time to come. Now whether or not he can back up my words is left to be seen. But if his HardKore Hell showing is any indication he may do just that. Reno:Simply put this man lost in the best match of the entire tourney. This match could have easily been the final but with the luck of the draw it was only a semi-final. Reno took out the Assistant in the first round and then battled the Dark Vampire Vlad to one of the best matches I have seen in quite sometime. Reno has a tremendous upside but what remains to be seen is whether he coasts after his initial month like so many in the past. Or whether he continues strong and moves his way up on the CEW ladder. Finals: Just couldn't close the deal Gob:A 800 lb freak in the Finals!! Where does the CEW get these guys. After stomping the uninspired Paxs in the first round the carnie freak eked out a victory over Crimescene in order to make the finals. His first to matches were impressive but his finals match left alot to be desired. Did he burn out over the 3 days of tourney, were the first two a fluke. I hope the first is the case. When the freak is on his game he's tough but if this is any indication he may be hit and miss. Too bad for him because if he stays on his game he could be a force. The Champion: Nuff Said!! Vlad:Your champion of HardKore Hell: Jan. 01? None other than "The Dark Vampire" himself. That's right folks Vlad is now the #1 contender for CEW Extreme title. How'd he get there? Glad you asked. Crushing the very uninspired Jb Famous Vlad moved to the semi's with ease. There he and Reno had the match of the tourney. I have to feel that whoever won that match, pretty much had the finals in the bag. After slipping by Reno it appeared he had a very big obstacle in his way, literally. But not even the 800lb Gob could stop "The Dark Vampire" from attaining his destiny. Now as he moves on to a match with the CEW Extreme Champ one has to think, Was this a fluke? I'll tell you straight out, No CEW it was not. "There you have it CEW a recap of the HardKore Hell Tournament that was just completed. All in all a very good tourney but not a great one. The one good thing that came from HardKore Hell: Jan. 01 was the fact that the CEW's future is bright in deed. 6 of the 8 participants had their game faces on and gave it everything they had. Now CEW follow their example and show me and everyone else what you can do. Until next time this is Extreme." (Have a event, or new worthy item you want covered. Drop me a line and I'll see if it's newsworthy.) "I'm not passive, I'm not timid, I"M EXTREME!! none
