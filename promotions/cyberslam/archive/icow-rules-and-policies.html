Date: 01/03/101 Policies ... by The Peers iCOW Policies Your Commish: Peers ([detail withheld]) (AIM: [detail withheld]) Your Major Commish: Grayson ([detail withheld]) Your OOC Website:icowcslam.homestead.com Below are the list of policies for the iCOW. These are in addition to the CSlam-wide rules that every fed follows. As a general guideline: Play nice, and you won't have any problems. IC/OOC Please keep in mind that this is a game. There is In-Character (when you speak as your slammer) and Out-Of-Character (when you speak as yourself). Please keep the two seperate. You're Rocky Maivia on the flashboard, and Dwane Johnson on the OOC board and email. So's everybody else. Violating this will result in punishment. Inactivity You will be considered inactive in the following cases: -You do not flash in the space between two board resets. -You only flash for card matches. -You consistently no-sell other people's flashes. -You no-show a card match. In all these cases, standard punishment will be given. Padding/Jobbing These are two closely related slamming problems. Padding is the act of slamming people much lower ranked than you to improve your rating. Jobbing is slamming someone just higher than you in order to boost their rating. To prevent these acts of bad sportsmanship, we have two policies. First, you can slam someone else only twice per day, and no more than six times a week. Second, you cannot slam someone more than 15% (or 5 slots on the ranking list, whichever is more) above or below you. Flashboard Rules -Profanity must be kept to a reasonable level. 'Damn' and 'hell' are allowable. Anything else should be done as ****, #%!#%, or (bleep). If you can't say it on TV (network, not cable), then don't say it here. -Likewise, 'mature themes'... sexual, overly gory, racist... they don't belong here. Partially because we have children playing the game, and mostly because they just plain don't belong here. If you like that kind of thing in your games, go play somewhere else. -OOC flashes are a big no-no. We have an OOC board for that. -Committing crimes. This covers anything that would be a felony. Murder, rape, robbery, etc, are all grounds for immediate suspension. Why? You've been arrested. This includes assault on anyone who isn't part of the iCOW. Tazz doesn't go around choking out fans, neither should you. -Using the commish in your flashes. This is not allowed unless I tell you via email or chat that it is. -HTML in your flash titles? Nope. This is a CSlam-wide rule, but I'm repeating it here. Only the commish can use HTML in flashboard titles. If you use it, it's an immediate suspension. Punishments In general, there are three levels of punishment in the iCOW. First, I'll give you a warning, either in email, or over the board. In very serious cases, this step will be skipped. Second, you'll be suspended temporarily. If you email me after three days and ask politely, I'll reinstate you. Third, you'll be suspended permenantly. It's assumed that if you get this far, you're obviously not willing to play by the rules. Shows The iCOW has two weekly shows, Monday Night Firefight and Thursday Throwdown. Show cards are posted the night before the show. Each match will be given, along with how the people involved should slam.
