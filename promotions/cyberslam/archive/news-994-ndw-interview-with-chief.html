NDW: Interview with Chief

14 August 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Flash Recently a super star has retired from the NDW. A man who helped the iNDW go to the NDW. A legend and a myth. The person I am talking about is Chief. I had the honor of sitting down with Chief and talk to him about his retirement. Flash: Why have you decided to retire? Chief: I have decided to retire because there is nowhere left to go. Cslam is no longer about the game. It's who's --- you can kiss, what fed you can get in, who you make friends with, and who you were in the past. Flash: So you feel Cslam has been corrupted? Chief: I would say that, it no longer has heart to it. There is no integrity behind the scenes, and that's why the game is bound to fail. Flash: Where do you see Cslam in five years? Chief: I don't. Flash: Any names you would like to talk about? Chief: There's a lot of names out there. What kinda names you want? Leaders? Teachers? Flash: Any body you would like to talk about. Chief: Well, a few names stand out. Luvgate, really doesn't know it that well, but I wouldn't have stayed around if it weren't for him. Myth has always been a great commish for me. Highway taught me what it was like to actually have rage against. He made me WANT to kill him, and I learned from that....The Assassin's Guild has always been home for me. The Law has taught me a lot also... Flash: Why did you join the Assassin's Guild? Chief: Because they asked me to. I was about a week into CSlam, and had no idea what the ---- I was doing. They took me in, and it just went from there. Flash: How did your wrestling career start? Chief: How did it start? It started when I felt I could kick some ---, and like it. Flash: Why did you join the Syndicate? Chief: I am quite fond of Reject, and felt like helping him out. Flash: What is your favorite moment in Cslam? Chief: That really is a hard one. After winning the Rewrite title in the iNDW, I felt on top of the world. It was really exciting, and the comments I got from people were ego boosts. There's someone I forgot to mention up there, Cujo, the guy is by far one of my most favorite in Cslam. Nothing but encouragement from the guy, and I owe most of what I have done to him... Flash: Do you feel you helped the iNDW turn to a major? Chief: I don't know how that decision was made. If it was the slammers, or commissioner, but I know, either way we had to go. The fed was really on the top of it's game then, and I felt Bullgod was also. If I had to answer that question yes or no, YES, I took it there myself. Flash: What was your favorite belt to hold? Chief: Either the Xtreme Rewrite Title (Peoples Title) or the Native (North) American Title. With the rewrite belt, I liked it, just 'cause I felt I couldn't lose. Native American Title, just because it felt perfect. And I couldn't lose it *wink* Flash: Who was your favorite wrestler to work with? Chief: Hmmmm, that is hard. My favorites have been, Highway, Wonko, Frigid, Reject, Cujo, and uh, of course Massacre Sr. Flash: Who were your worst enemies? Chief: Hmmm, again Highway...That guy was a prick. Major of the iIWF was always a favorite of mine to go against. Flash: Do you think you will be remembered in the future? Chief: Only time will tell! Flash: If you were me, what would you ask you? Chief: Um, I'd ask me...Why are you such a stud? Flash: What's your answer? Chief: Because I can be. Flash: What would you like to see changed in Cslam? Chief: I'd like to see it downsize. I'd like to see Commissioners working their ---es off. I'd like to see some engine changes, and I think it's time to ban the morons. This game is supposed to be fun. Make it that way. Flash: What is your opinion on The Slammer? Funky? Chief: The Slammer? I have no opinion on the Slammer, why? Because I have never seen a thing of his to be opinionated on. This is his game right? Well, act like a man and interact. Funky, also a man I have never personally talked to, I think he is good at what he does. He takes a lot of ----, but he does what he can. Flash: Anything you would like to add? State? Chief: I'd like to add that everyone in Cslam has made it fun for me, at times. But the game needs to shape up, or it will die. Games are supposed to be fun, make it that way. And politics really need to be left out of this place, please. Flash: How would you like to do some word association Chief? Chief: Word what? Flash: Association Chief: That like a spelling hospital? Flash: No, I say a word and then you tell me what it makes you think of. Flash: Sound good? Chief: Oooh, you mean Word Association! Flash: That's what I said! Chief: No you didn't! Flash: Yes, anyways....ready? Chief: OK shoot. Flash: White man Chief: Xcalibur is a white man, he's pretty cool. Flash: Buffalo Chief: Would you shut the **** up and play this game for real? I don''t have time for your silly games. My sponge bath at the retirement home is in an hour. Flash: Retirement Chief: About time. Flash: Women Chief: the reason I am here. Flash: Steel pipe Chief: hurts Flash: Snoogans Chief: Snoogles Flash: Bullgod Chief: I learned the game from him. He made it fun. Flash: Insane Chief: Fuji Flash: Stuman Chief: Don't know enough about Stuman to go into detail. But I respect him. Flash: Storm Chief: I think he deserved his win. Flash: Blayze Chief: he's been a friend for a while, I like the guy. Flash:Tazz Chief: I don't know. Sounds original *wink* Flash: Senior Citizen Chief: Massacre Sr. Flash: Thanks for your time, and good luck in the future Chief Chief: No prob, thank you. This has been an in depth interview with NDW super star Chief. Thanks for listening. --Flash-- none
