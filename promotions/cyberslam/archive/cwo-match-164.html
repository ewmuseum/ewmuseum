PreMatch Comments Kiss the Sky Purple_haze goes for a roundhouse right...backdrop by Patriot over the top rope! Patriot climbs out after him and slaps on a cobra clutch! The fans are on their feet! Now he throws Purple_haze back into the ring at the ten count. The crowd is deafening here tonight! Patriot climbs out of the ring and grabs a chair from ringside. Purple_haze catches him coming back in and...dropkick! Patriot slammed HIMSELF in the face with the chair! Purple_haze is screaming at the ref that there was a hair pull on that last move. Patriot sneaks up...and jams his fingers down Purple_haze's throat! Mandible claw!!! Wow! Patriot is getting into it with a fan on the outside! Apparently the kid is wearing a Purple_haze t-shirt! Purple_haze throws Patriot into the ropes. He goes for a hiptoss...but Patriot puts on the brakes! Slaps on an abdominal stretch! Purple_haze is complaining about a hair pull and the hold gets broken. Patriot off the ropes. Purple_haze is waiting and executes a textbook fireman's carry. While there's a break in the action here are some pre-recorded comments from Patriot..."Purple_haze, when I get done with you I hope the doctors have an old picture of you on hand so they know where to sew all the pieces back on!" Tough words from a tough man! Patriot is in trouble! Purple_haze grabs him and is pointing to the crowd! Patriot connects with an elbow to the face! Purple_haze took way too long! Purple_haze off the ropes...sunset flip! Can he pull him down? No! Patriot drops a big fist to the face. Now he's got the legs...rolls Patriot over...Scorpion Deathlock! Purple_haze reaches the rope after 45 seconds! Whoa! Low blow by Purple_haze ! And the crowd is letting him have it! Purple_haze grabs a handful of tights. Looks like Patriot is going for a ride...No! He blocks it! Patriot gets him down on his stomach and locks in the camel clutch! I can hear those vertabrae popping from here! He lets Purple_haze up! He's not finnished punnishing him! Patriot gets him on the mat with a well placed right hand...he's going for the claw! Purple_haze rolls out of the ring to avoid the hold. He rolls back in before the ref begins to count. Patriot slaps on the sleeper! Purple_haze is fading...the ref comes over to check! 1...2...the hand comes up! Patriot releases the hold and delivers a forearm to the kidney for good measure. They're circling..Patriot locks him up from behind for a Russian leg sweep. Purple_haze grabs the ropes to break the hold. Patriot goes for a crucifix! Will he get the pin? NO! Purple_haze brings him down with a Samoan Drop! Purple_haze goes for a roundhouse right...backdrop by Patriot over the top rope! Patriot climbs out after him and slaps on a cobra clutch! The fans are on their feet! Now he throws Purple_haze back into the ring at the ten count. Off the ropes...Patriot grabs a hold and goes for a fireman's carry. Purple_haze holds on and rolls him into a small package! 1...2...kickout! Purple_haze with a boot to the stomach...powerbomb! Pin attempt! 1...2...kickout! Doesn't get much closer than that! Bear Hug!!! Patriot is in alot of pain right now!!! Patriot has his legs...trying to roll Purple_haze over for the Boston Crab...and Purple_haze powers out! Looks like it's going to take alot more than that to put Purple_haze away! Definite bad blood between these two! Purple_haze is complaining to he ref about a hair pull and...NO! Patriot cheap shots him with a fist to the back of the head! Why doesn't the referee disqualify him!?!? Patriot rakes his eyes across his boot laces! The ref must be as blind as Purple_haze is now to have missed that! Patriot side steps the dropkick...and slaps on the figure four leg lock! Purple_haze reaches the rope after one minute. A clothesline to the back of the head sends Purple_haze to the mat! Incredible speed as Patriot is right on top of him...STF! Again, Purple_haze refuses to submit and the hold is broken after 1 minute. I've never agreed with some of Purple_haze's tactics, but this is nuts! He's pulled off the top turnbuckle pad and is trying to ram...Patriot turns it around and slams Purple_haze's head into the exposed metal! Purple_haze looks enraged! He rams Patriot's head in! They're both busted open! Purple_haze going for the belly to belly...couldn't do it! Patriot has fallen on top of him! Patriot has his legs...trying to roll Purple_haze over for the Boston Crab...and Purple_haze powers out! Looks like it's going to take alot more than that to put Purple_haze away! Patriot is looking to take a breather in the corner...here comes Purple_haze! BIG avalanche! Patriot crumples to the mat. Irish whip into the corner, reversed, reversed again...Patriot levels him with a short clothesline! But man does he look dizzy! Purple_haze is going for a crucifix. Can he get it!?!? Patriot breaks free...spins around...full nelson! Great counter! Purple_haze whips him into the ropes...Patriot ducks under the clothesline...from the other side...Patriot with a dropkick to the knee! Patriot looks hurt. He falls to the mat. Purple_haze comes running off the ropes for a legdrop...Patriot pulls the ref in the way! It's a three way collision! I don't think that's what Purple_haze had in mind! Purple_haze applies a headlock. Now Patriot with a forearm to the kidneys and he executes a picture perfect Russian leg sweep! Purple_haze off the ropes...sleeperhold! Patriot doesn't have it completely locked in and Purple_haze slips out the back door. Patriot down to the canvass...Purple_haze comes off the ropes with a big splash. Patriot rolls out of the way! Nobody home! He gets up and slaps on a bow and arrow submission hold! The ref is asking...but Purple_haze refuses to submit! Patriot slaps on a headlock. Purple_haze picks him up...bell to belly suplex! Patriot slowly gets to his feet...and gets leveled by a devastating shoulder tackle!!! Patriot down to the canvass...Purple_haze comes off the ropes with a big splash. Patriot rolls out of the way! Nobody home! He gets up and slaps on a bow and arrow submission hold! The ref is asking...but Purple_haze refuses to submit! Patriot executes a belly to back suplex...steps over...into a Boston Crab! Purple_haze manages to get his arm over the bottom rope. Lucky! Hammerlock slapped on Purple_haze...he counters with a strong elbow to the head! Patriot has got a one way ticket to Suplex City...and Purple_haze is supplying the ticket! Way up and just as long down! What power! Test of strength going on...no clear advantage. The ref calls for a break as they end up in the corner. Purple_haze whips Patriot into the ropes...Patriot grabs onto the top rope. He's not going anywhere. Patriot slaps on a headlock. Purple_haze picks him up...bell to belly suplex! They're circling..Patriot locks him up from behind for a Russian leg sweep. Purple_haze grabs the ropes to break the hold. Purple_haze whips him into the ropes...he gets Patriot up on his back. Can't do it! Patriot slips out of a potential Samoan Drop. Lucky move! Purple_haze with a boot to the stomach...powerbomb! Pin attempt! 1...2...kickout! Doesn't get much closer than that! Patriot whips Purple_haze into the ropes...he's waiting in the middle of the ring with a backdrop... Purple_haze saw it coming! He grabs him and drives him into the mat with an awesome piledriver! Bear Hug!!! Patriot is in alot of pain right now!!! Purple_haze lifts him up for an atomic drop! Patriot levels him with a short clothesline! Purple_haze gets him up for the sidewalk sla...no! Patriot slips out the back door. Patriot slaps on a headlock. Purple_haze picks him up...bell to belly suplex! Purple_haze goes for a roundhouse right...backdrop by Patriot over the top rope! Patriot climbs out after him and slaps on a cobra clutch! The fans are on their feet! Now he throws Purple_haze back into the ring at the ten count. With a shoulder block, Purple_haze knocks Patriot to the canvass. He’s off the ropes...BIG Legdrop!!! Purple_haze gets him up! The crowd is up on their feet and BAM! Sidewalk slam! Patriot has his legs...trying to roll Purple_haze over for the Boston Crab...and Purple_haze powers out! Looks like it's going to take alot more than that to put Purple_haze away! Patriot executes a belly to back suplex...steps over...into a Boston Crab! Purple_haze manages to get his arm over the bottom rope. Lucky!
