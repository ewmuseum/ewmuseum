WWA - Just who is the WWA?

15 May 2000
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[name withheld] Well we here at the WWA have been working hard the past week to get our newest site re-vamp up to date. Our hotline is still in the works but when released will feature a ton of features including a couple of our WWA Real Audio Shows as well as Real Audio Shoot Interviews with WWA members complete with transcripts for those of you who don't have Real Player. We will have awards, DC-TV listings, the whole deal. Lately we've been growing a bit, adding new affiliates such as the FWF and the NEW. Our criticly acclaimed graphics have gotten us a lot of recognition recently as well. We have worked hard on our graphics and so far they are paying off. but the graphics are there not only to attract people at first but to give us that real fantasy wrestling feel, we want you to feel like this really is the WWF, the WCW or the ECW. We have a few great roleplayers that can really hold their own in the E-Wrestling world. Wether it's the crack-me-up antics of 'Crybaby' Carter Hayes or the no-nonsense tough-guy himself Butch McGraw there's personalities of all kinds here at the WWA. Our prime time show Wednesday WAR can be found on DC-TV around 8:00 central. It's our biggest event of the week and quite the ratings draw I might add. Our next big Pay Per View event is coming up on June the 5th entitled WWA Revolution. It should be a great show and we'll be signing more matches for it in the days to come. That's about it so far, now that you know just a tad about us the least you can do is check out the site. Thanks. SO WHAT ARE YOU WAITING FOR? :-) -[name withheld]
