CSlam: More than just a game! ALL!

3 February 2001
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Chubbs To me I never look at Cslam to be nothing more than a game...but it was a game I was damn good at! I never really thought about it leaving us...or going away...but it lasting forever...it was more than a game...now that its gone I have nothing to do...but this... The night I heard of the news...I lied awake untill 3 in the morning...thinking of the time, the effort, and most of all the heart felt words I put into Cslam. Cslam was a way to get my personal life into words...and transmit them into a game...making them iron out... I think Cslam is one thing...and nothing else... A legend... A legend never dies in my eyes...and this will not...I personally hope Snowball.com reads this and sees what Im saying... Screw emailing Save Cyberslam...Im emailing Snowball.com...Screw them...I say make a rebellion...Cyberslam has the domain name of www.cyberslam.com so I say we redesign the site and start over...try to get as many slammers back in the right spots...but if we can get snowball.com to let us keep the site...then so be it...sounds good... Words from the wise... Chubbs - RWF The Ultimate - NDW Contact me via email at [detail withheld] with your views... [detail withheld]
