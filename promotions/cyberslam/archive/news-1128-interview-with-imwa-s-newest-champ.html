Interview With iMWA'S Newest Champ

30 August 2000
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Edge Congratulations on winning the iMWA World Champion belt. Now that you have won it do you think that you have accomplished your goals in iMWA? Not totally. I still have to win the tag gold from the crew. It isn't about the gold, it is about finally beating the Crew as a team in the ring. I mean, I kick there butt all the time out of the ring, but it has to be an official win in the ring. I also want my name to be known as one of the best champions the iMWA has ever had, and a legend in this fed. Now that you have the belt you are the center of attention. Everyone wants a shot at you now. Who do you think is the most worthy? If you had a chance who would you pick? Of course the next best is LaMagra. He is the Underdog champ for god sakes, and should be the hardcore as well. But there is many top contenders. Two that stand out are Mr. Arse and Shocker. They both have what it takes and are very active. Of course they are not up to standards with me, but they are worthy of a shot. Bolt is always good, and Crow is moving up as well. The Sickness, you and Lamarga, tried at to beat the CrEW but failed. Now that you have the World belt do you see yourself leaning more towards single competition or still going after the CrEW? Oh, I will not leave this fed until we beat the Crew. The last match we had, I honestly have to say, we gave the Crew the best match of their lives. It is far from over, because if you want to make a name for your self in the tag business, then you have to beat the Crew. But LaMagra and I will do our own thing as well, as I'm the World, and he is the Underdog champ. Now that you have won the belt has your attitude changed, do you feel more superior then the rest of iMWA? I do feel a little superior. I feel that I do deserve this belt, and that I am the best in the fed. If you don't think so you don't belong here. I thought that of myself and in one quick month, look where I'm at now? You and Bolt went at it hard. You beat him but is it possible Bolt will become eight time iMWA World Champion by defeating you? Bolt is a great competitor, and yes he will probably become 8 time champion some day, but it wont be by taking the belt from me! Now to my last question Amundson. Right now you are on fire. How much of a flame do you have left? Im not on fire Edge. Fires sooner or later go out. I am not on fire, Im just that damn good, not to rip of HHH, but I am! Im not hot at the moment I'm just doing what I will always do and that is be the best at what I do, because I am the best at what I do! Well I thank you for this chance you have us Amundson and we hope you continue to entertain us. Also fans I am trying to get together a Top Ten flashers so send in your votes to [detail withheld] Your votes will be greatly appreciated. So stay tuned for more news from iMWA!! none
