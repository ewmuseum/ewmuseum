PreMatch Comments None Annihilator with a stiff chop to the chest...goes for a second and Ebola rocks him with an enziguri kick to the head! Annihilator gets him up! The crowd is up on their feet and BAM! Sidewalk slam! Annihilator with a stiff chop to the chest...goes for a second and Ebola rocks him with an enziguri kick to the head! Ebola slowly gets to his feet...and gets leveled by a devastating shoulder tackle!!! What does he have there? A roll of coins! Ebola clocks him in the jaw and about twenty bucks in change flies all over the ring! Annihilator with a stiff chop to the chest...goes for a second and Ebola rocks him with an enziguri kick to the head! Ebola rebounds out of the corner with a flying forearm...but Annihilator is waiting with a stiff boot to the face. Ebola off the ropes. Annihilator is waiting and executes a textbook fireman's carry. Ebola looks hurt. He falls to the mat. Annihilator comes running off the ropes for a legdrop...Ebola pulls the ref in the way! It's a three way collision! I don't think that's what Annihilator had in mind! Ebola goes for a suplex...reversed...reversed again...Annihilator blocks it and slaps him! Annihilator gets him up for the sidewalk sla...no! Ebola slips out the back door. Annihilator whips him into the ropes and goes in for the clothesline...Ebola ducks it...off the other side and a beautiful cross-body block! Only a one count. Annihilator is trying to get him up...Ebola blocks it and is trying to lift him up! No!!! Both go sailing over the top rope! Slowly they climb back into the ring. What does he have there? A roll of coins! Ebola clocks him in the jaw and about twenty bucks in change flies all over the ring! Ebola with a small package! 1...2...kickout! It's going to take more than that! Ebola looks pretty confident. He climbs to the top and...moonsault! Annihilator catches him and slams him to the mat! Great Power! Annihilator rolls out of the ring for a breather...here comes Ebola flying between the ropes with a clothesline! No...Annihilator saw in coming and ducks out of the way! Ebola hits the floor hard and Annihilator tosses him back into the ring. Ebola whips Annihilator into the ropes...Annihilator reverses it...Annihilator nails him with a big knee lift! Ebola goes down! Annihilator is climbing to the top rope...Ebola shakes the ropes! Annihilator falls right onto the turnbuckle allowing Ebola to climb up...superplex!!! Bear Hug!!! Ebola is in alot of pain right now!!! Annihilator with the headbutt...he stunned himself! Annihilator falls down! Ebola to the top rope...BIG legdrop from way up there! 1...2...NO! Close! Annihilator throws a big elbow, Ebola rams him with a knee at the same time! They both hit the mat and the fists are flying! Throw the book out the window, this one's becoming a brawl! Ebola goes for a punch, but Annihilator ducks and grabs him! He’s up on his shoulder and Annihilator nearly powerslams him through the mat! Ebola is looking to take a breather in the corner...here comes Annihilator! BIG avalanche! Ebola crumples to the mat. Ebola connects witha European uppercut...Annihilator nails him with one of his own! They're going punch for punch in the center of the ring! Ebola is perched on the second turnbuckle waiting for Annihilator to turn around...flying clothesline! Right on the money! Annihilator whips him into the ropes...he gets Ebola up on his back. Can't do it! Ebola slips out of a potential Samoan Drop. Lucky move! Ebola off the top rope. He took way too long to get up there...axe-handle attempted and Annihilator delivers a big fist to the midsection on his way down! Ebola takes a swing...Annihilator blocks it! Legsweep and a BIG splash! Ebola is flattened! Ebola is walking on the top rope! Agile!!! But here comes Annihilator with a dropkick! Ebola goes sailing through a table and Annihilator is tied up in the ropes! Ebola takes a swing...Annihilator blocks it! Legsweep and a BIG splash! Ebola is flattened! Ebola looks pretty confident. He climbs to the top and...moonsault! Annihilator catches him and slams him to the mat! Great Power! Flying fistdrop from the top rope...connects! I can't really see from this angle but I think Annihilator is busted open! Annihilator is prone in the center of the ring! Ebola is on the top rope...big splash! 1..kickout! Ebola off the ropes with a cross-body...Annihilator catches him. But he can't hold him! Both spill over the top rope! Hard bump, but both climb back into the ring. They lock up in the corner...neither is giving up the advantage...oooh same thought! They rake each other's eyes! Both are blindly reaching for the ropes. Ebola whips Annihilator into the ropes...he's waiting in the middle of the ring with a backdrop... Annihilator saw it coming! He grabs him and drives him into the mat with an awesome piledriver! Ebola takes a swing...Annihilator blocks it! Legsweep and a BIG splash! Ebola is flattened! Annihilator with a stiff chop to the chest...goes for a second and Ebola rocks him with an enziguri kick to the head! Bear Hug!!! Ebola is in alot of pain right now!!! Annihilator gets him up! The crowd is up on their feet and BAM! Sidewalk slam! Ebola whips him into the ropes...looking for a backdrop...Annihilator sees it coming. DDT! Ebola’s head bounced off the mat like a superball! Annihilator slaps on a sleeper...but Ebola drops down with a jawbreaker! He springs to the top rope, BIG double axe-handle to the head! Say goodbye to that spine! Annihilator rocks Ebola with a nasty atomic drop! Annihilator stuns him with a punch...bounces off the ropes...leapfrog by Ebola...on his way back...Ebola nails him with a Frankensteiner! The crowd goes nuts! Annihilator swings and misses! He catches a boot to the gut for his troubles...and Ebola slams him with a jumping DDT! Devastating! Annihilator has got him up for a Samoan Drop...Ebola grabs his head! Annihilator takes him down, but in return was devastated by a kind of neckbreaker! Ebola is in trouble! Annihilator grabs him and is pointing to the crowd! Ebola connects with an elbow to the face! Annihilator took way too long! Ebola off the ropes...leaps over Annihilator with a headlock applied...flying bulldog!!! Very impressive maneuver! Annihilator slaps on a sleeper...but Ebola drops down with a jawbreaker! He springs to the top rope, BIG double axe-handle to the head! Ebola is perched on the second turnbuckle waiting for Annihilator to turn around...flying clothesline! Right on the money! Annihilator rolls out of the ring. He saw someone holding a Ebola poster and grabs it! He begins tearing it up! Annihilator rolls back into the ring and attempts to make Ebola eat a piece! Ebola whips Annihilator into the ropes...Annihilator reverses it...Annihilator nails him with a big knee lift! Ebola goes down! Ebola connects witha European uppercut...Annihilator nails him with one of his own! They're going punch for punch in the center of the ring! Annihilator whips Ebola into the ropes...Ebola grabs onto the top rope. He's not going anywhere. Annihilator slaps on a sleeper...but Ebola drops down with a jawbreaker! He springs to the top rope, BIG double axe-handle to the head! Annihilator lifts him up for an atomic drop! Ebola levels him with a short clothesline! Ebola goes for a crucifix! Will he get the pin? NO! Annihilator brings him down with a Samoan Drop! Ebola takes a swing...Annihilator blocks it! Legsweep and a BIG splash! Ebola is flattened! Ebola connects witha European uppercut...Annihilator nails him with one of his own! They're going punch for punch in the center of the ring! Annihilator climbs to the top rope...signaling for a double axe-handle. Ebola gets a running start and counters with a drop kick! Big mid-air collision! Annihilator stuns him with a punch...bounces off the ropes...leapfrog by Ebola...on his way back...Ebola nails him with a Frankensteiner! The crowd goes nuts! Annihilator is prone in the center of the ring! Ebola is on the top rope...big splash! 1..kickout! Annihilator rolls out of the ring for a breather...here comes Ebola flying between the ropes with a clothesline! No...Annihilator saw in coming and ducks out of the way! Ebola hits the floor hard and Annihilator tosses him back into the ring. With a shoulder block, Annihilator knocks Ebola to the canvass. He’s off the ropes...BIG Legdrop!!! We could see a bear hug here...Annihilator can't hold him! Ebola breaks the hold and slams his head into the turnbuckle. Annihilator slaps on a headlock. Ebola can't seem to power out...he lifts him into a side suplex! Up to the top rope and...BAM! Big elbow from fifteen feet up! Annihilator has got him! He's signaling to he crowd for a piledriver...Ebola counters with a backdrop! Annihilator is out of it and...Oh! Ebola applies the Simple Death..It's all over!! Ebola has sent a message to all the other wrestlers that he's back. Winner: Ebola
