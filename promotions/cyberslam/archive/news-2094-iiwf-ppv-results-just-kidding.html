iIWF:PPV results... just kidding

2 February 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Flash_Lightning Well that's the last ride, the end of the road, the last train stop, the... well the END! It's been great being a reporter. It's been great having more sway than a regualar slammer. It's also been great reporting for the iIWF. I loved hearing the comments of the slammers when I said something bad about them. I loved seeing slammers prove me wrong and change their ways. Beyond the reporting, there's C-slam as a whole. I always wanted to know what was behind C-slam what was behind the scenes, but not like this. This is the worst thing that could have happen, and for what something that's obviously more important than people themselves! MONEY! I have no idea how much it will take to run C-slam, but I bet if everyone sent in five bucks (I have no idea to where) or something like that, we can keep it alive for just a little while. For now I guess we have to settle for other web sites like Cyberbrawls, but it's okay we'll live, but the big picture is that C-slam won't. It's been fun, but now I guess I'll be signing off. Just when the rumors started to flow... I'm Flash Lightning and that's the latest Shocking News!!! It'll be okay
