Edge THE ROAD TO CWZ "The whole Fed starts the race, but only one man finishes it." September 29th & 30th Live from Madison Square Garden OPENING MATCH Fatal 4 Way In the Parking Lot The_darkness vs Darkstalker vs Axel_ vs Nightdragon The_Darkness takes the win here and will be rewarded next month for the effort.As a great reporter and scout I predicted this match easily. ROUND ONE OF THE HOLY SCRIPTURE TOURNAMENT: MATCH # 1 Falls Count Anywhere Match Bobby Boats vs Crazy guy Rewrite Only Bobby Boats was fired right before the match as Crazy Guy emailed ahead no contest Who would have known he would have got fired and the other guy screwed up. Throw that one out of the books. I am still 1-0 for now. GRUDGE MATCH Steven_Flash vs Wes White Wes_white with the win.Once again I am right. Wes White showed his ex-partner that he can make it own his own. ROUND ONE OF THE HOLY SCRIPTURE TOURNAMENT: MATCH # 2 I Quit Match Damion vs Bolt Rewrite Only Damion moves on to the final round.Well who said I was perfect. I thought Bolt had this one but Damoin proved everyone wrong. What looked like a upset to many was only a another victory to Damion. Damion had this to say "I never thought it was a upset. i went in knowing I was gone to win and I did. Where is the upset there?" TAG TEAM TITLE MATCH The CrEW vs Sickness 2K(c) This was no disappointment at all, The_Crew regain the iMWA TAG TEAM TITLES One of the best matches of the night next to the World Title match. These two went at it hard. What was a regular Tag Title match turned into a no holds bar cage match. Sickness 2K had troubles in the match and in the end split up. What a shocker that was. I really thought the Crew were washed up but they proved they are still the best team in the iMWA. Well that bring me to 2-2 so far. EXTREME TITLE MATCH Ladder Match Error vs Ynk Ynk had no chance to win as Error keeps the title!Like I said the first time Ynk CHOKED. Raise my record another notch. FRIDAY'S MAIN EVENT WORLD TITLE MATCH Mr Arse vs Amundson Mr Arse tops day 1 off with the iMWA WORLD TITLE in hand.I knew it. I end up the Day 1 card going 4-2. After fighting a no holds bar cage match Amundson had to defend his World Belt against a fresh Mr.Arse. Some critics claim that this was not a fare match. The match before Amundson was driven through four tables. I ask Amundson if he thought this match was a fair one after being in a brutal tag match with the Crew. He replied, " NO!". He did not have to much to say. He still seemed upset when I asked him this. In all this was a Card to go down in history and be remembered by all. This month is already shaping out to be a good one. Stay tuned for day 2 results. -Edge- none
