iFEW- Interviews, news, etc.

17 August 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Dustin Bones Hello, this is Dustin Bones with another edition of Rollin Da Bones. Today it's my pleasure to welcome Arawn. Who in many eyes is the considered the very best in iFEW. No you know me, Dustin calls it right down the middle and I pick no favorites. So ladies, gents and those who are undecided at press time, say hello to Arawn. What's up? Thanks for having me here. How long have you been in the iFEW and do you like the way things are going since Juevenile took control? I've been with the iFEW almost since it inception. Things were slow and the slammers at the time had no clue what they were doing. The commish at the time was good, but Juevinile has made us what we are today. His entrance into the iFEW was the turning point for many of our careers. You've held many titles here Arawn and had many memorable moments, which moment meant the most to you? That's an easy one. The debut of Darkened Dreams. When Mantus made his first appearance in the iFEW and DD was formed during my inferno match with another slammer. It was a great moment in the iFEW. The iFEW may become a MAJOR fed soon. What's your opinion on that? I think it's well deserved. The slammers here have busted their butts for the last couple of months as well as Juevinile. We will take C-Slam by storm. Many in the fed consider you as one of the top talents here. Is there anyone you think of in that same capacity? There are actually a few, Mafioso and Mad_swinger of course, Hellsrage is another. For someone such as yourself who has done everything there is to do, and held every title there is to date. What keeps you going, what's your motivation? Simply put, Darkened Dreams. I'm determined to make DD a force to be reckoned with in all of C-Slam, not just the iFEW. We've already infiltrated the AWO and made our mark. But there's more to be done. Obviosly you've had a few tough matches here. Which one would you say has been the most difficult? Actually Dustin, I'm going to give you credit for that. Your triple threat strap match was brutal. I mean, how do you beat a stablemate with a strap and keep the peace within the group. It wasn't exactly an easy thing to do. Have you got any rivalries going right now? Is there one person in the fed that you really can't stand or get along with? Well, as far as rivalries go, I would have to say the False Prophets. No one has given us as good a fight as the False Prophets have. Not even the great IP's there anyone I can't stand. Yeah. Mr. Schizophrenic himself Stan. He crossed DD and will pay for it. Do you have any role-models in the sport? Well, not really. If I had to point to any one slammer it would be hard. I can point to slammers such as Caz, Batman, Onefatmotha, Abyss Lord. They were my first introduction into the game. And I have nothing but respect for them all. Do you think that the slammers here in the iFEW can compete with the slammers from the other Major feds should iFEW become a Major fed? Definitley. We'll have to weed some of the bad seeds, but for the most part we'll be a MAJOR force. Ok Arawn this is my personal favorite part of the interview. It's The SUPERSLINKY WORD BLITZ. This is where I say a word, amd you say the first thing that comes to mind. Juevenile? Great commish, but needs to keep his nose out of DD's business. Rachel or Bubbiejoe? Great to look at, painful to watch in the ring. Darkened Dreams? Best stable in the iFEW, and we've just begun. Canadian wrestlers? Way too serious. Rookies Hey, you gotta start some how. Anchovies? Rather watch Greatone try to cut a promo. Arawn this just about wraps it up here. It's been a pleasure having you as a guest. Do you have any words of wisdom, or suggestions for the ladies out there who think they might want to become a wrestler? Yeah, the more skin ... the better. Just one final thought ... I suggest you use Minnesota in your Madden match against Juevi .... Moss catches everything. Thanks once again everyone for reading. This is the iFEW's own Superslinky reporter reminding you to keep. Rollin da Bones none
