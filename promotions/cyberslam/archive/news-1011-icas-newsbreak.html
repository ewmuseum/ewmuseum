ICAS: Newsbreak

16 August 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Bizkit Welcome to your iCAS Newsflash for Wendesday, August 16, 2000. I am the newest reporter for Hammerlock, and I will be reporting the happenings inside the Independent Cyberslam All Stars. Today, the card for Thursday Thrashing was announced, and the card will have major implacations on the FPV this Sunday night. Double Main Event iCAS King of the Ring Round 2 Matches Stasis vs Oz Grim vs Jackhammer Two weeks ago on Thrashing, The team of Some Good (Stasis/Grim) defeated NC-17 (Jackhammer/Oz) for the Tag Team titles. Jackhammer and Oz will try to extract revenge in the form of two singles matches. Non-Title Hardcore Match Pornstar_x vs Dbell Dbell has come onto the scene as of late and will defenintly be a challenge for the Hardcore Champ. TV Title Match Kurtz(c) vs Marshmauler The champ will make his first title defense against a strong up and comer who came off a strong win against Tazz_ on Monday. Light Weight Title Match Champion(c) vs Big_Zep The three time Light Weight Champion will make his first title defense of his new reign against the man he defeated on Monday in a four way dance for the belt. Contender's Match Paradox vs Inquisitor The winner of the match will get a shot at a title belt at either the FPV or Monday Massacre. Getting to Know the Fed Match Bigdaddy_ vs Deejay Both men have grown a strong dislike for each other over the last few days. They'll settle the score on Thrashing Other Singles Matches Moe vs Life Dreamdevil vs Mizery Black_angel vs Lastangel Millerman vs Vocablaryboy Some matches that are just thrown together, but should be exciting. Inactive Battle Royal Death Match Toohot vs Hellangel vs Thedevil vs Wolf vs James_c Ah, will there be blood? Who will win? Will anyone show up? We will find out! Sigh, that's all of the news for today, I'll bring ya some more tomorrow night. So Sit back, relax... And remember Y'all gotta have faith. none
