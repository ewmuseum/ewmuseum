iRCW Fontsize

16 August 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Typeface Typeface here... and honestly, I'm not sure quite how to start today's Fontsize. I guess there's no good way to do it. Mister Peak, a 12-month veteran of the iRCW, "The Franchise", The King of Hardcore, and a true legend, has left CSlam. There's not much I can say about the Peakster that wasn't said as part of my 'cry wolf' goodbye to him last month, when he didn't truely leave... everything said before applies here, too. We'll miss you, Peak. Those wanting to learn more can check the iRCW Flashboard and iRCW OOC Board. With this sad news, all I can really do form here on out is give you the preview for Thursday Rage. Our 'Meet Your Champions" feature will be postponed until next week, possibly Friday if I'm feeling up to it. Godspeed, Peak. Looks like I'll keep on reporting, even without you slamming. **Thursday Rage** Card opening matches pit Keebler against JRC, Kris Jericho against Snyper, and Angryguy against Thunderhead. Look for wins from JRC, Snyper (yes, Snyper... what can I say, I hate people who steal 'real' wrestler's names), and Angryguy. Next off is a three-way dance with The Infinite, Johnny XS, and Speed. Two minor stable leaders and a guy who's done pretty good for himself so far this month. Typeface's pic: Johnny, as much as I hate to say it. The guy's on a roll. On Monday Fire, Kurse and Panic fought to a draw. It's not gonna happen again, 'cause we're sticking them in a steel cage. Typeface's pic: Well, I want Kurse to win, since he's at least somewhat entertaining. Four corners, four tag teams, eight men, one big lump of chaos. The Evil Knights, Dragon Slayers, Original Gangstas, and Amazing Jiggalos all face off... hrm. Two midevil-themed teams and two street-life-themed teams. Interesting. Typeface's pic: The Evil Knights. I'd've said the OGs, but they're still recovering from that fall on Fire... TITLE TIME! Freak defends "The Shiny" against Sugar. If Sugar wins this one, look for Jolt to lose the tag-team titles... but I think Freak will be retaining. Right after that match, Sugar and teammate Twelvevoltman, "Jolt", have to defend their tag-team titles against "The Future". Hey, look, Ma, I named a tag team! Again, if Sugar wins the Richmond, look for Jolt to drop the tag titles. And if Jolt does drop the tag titles, then look for Twelvevoltman to win over Greenhell Junior for the TV Title. But again, I don't think that's gonna happen, as "Mister Green Jeans" seems to be a monster in the ring when there's gold on the line. The B&G contendership match betweel Reinhardt and NBK ended in a draw on Fire, so Kiddpin had to defend against... "The Big Stick", Goalie Bill! And for somethign special, they'll be fighting it out in WWF New York. I wonder how many people are going to be there watching Smackdown... I mean, it's not like it's live... ah, anyways, Kiddpain's been unstoppable lately... I think The Stickman will just be the next to fall before the new crown prince of hardcore. The Southern States title may change hands yet again, as Rookie Phenom, Mister Zinc, has to defend his newly gotten title against... Static, who has been dominating nearly every match he's been in. This is going to be one hell of a match... I favor the champ, but only because it'd be a shame for him to lose in his first defense. And finally, the Heavyweight Title is on the line as Damage Inc's Haz has to defend against... the newest Knight, Thunderbolt. Yes, T-Bolt has gone and joined up with CSlam's premire face stable, and he'll be representing them tonight as he tries to take the title from "The Toxic Avenger". If anyone can do it, Bolt can... but I don't think anyone can do it. Haz with a close win here. **** none
