Alisterfiend catches Rocke with a legsweep...he bounces of the ropes and gets way up there for a legdrop. Doesn't connect! Rocke rolled out of the way! Alisterfiend into the corner...Rocke is right behind him...and meets a set of knees! Rocke goes down, but I think Alisterfiend got hurt with that last move! Alisterfiend catches Rocke with a legsweep...he bounces of the ropes and gets way up there for a legdrop. Doesn't connect! Rocke rolled out of the way! Rocke off the ropes...cross body block! Alisterfiend catches him! Turns it into a nasty shoulderbreaker! Alisterfiend looks hurt. He falls to the mat. Rocke comes running off the ropes for a legdrop...Alisterfiend pulls the ref in the way! It's a three way collision! I don't think that's what Rocke had in mind! Alisterfiend rolls out of the ring. He saw someone holding a Rocke poster and grabs it! He begins tearing it up! Alisterfiend rolls back into the ring and attempts to make Rocke eat a piece! Rocke off the ropes...cross body block! Alisterfiend catches him! Turns it into a nasty shoulderbreaker! Rocke wraps his fingers around his throat...way up!!! And crashing down to the canvass! What a choke slam! Alisterfiend has got a one way ticket to Suplex City...and Rocke is supplying the ticket! Way up and just as long down! What power! Alisterfiend whips him into the ropes...looking for a backdrop...Rocke sees it coming. DDT! Alisterfiend’s head bounced off the mat like a superball! Alisterfiend looking to slap on a powerbomb here! Rocke panics and slips out. If Alisterfiend had locked it in this would've been over! Alisterfiend slaps on a headlock. Rocke picks him up...bell to belly suplex! Rocke whips Alisterfiend into the ropes...Alisterfiend reverses it...Alisterfiend nails him with a big knee lift! Rocke goes down! Rocke whips Alisterfiend into the ropes...he's waiting in the middle of the ring with a backdrop... Alisterfiend saw it coming! He grabs him and drives him into the mat with an awesome piledriver! Alisterfiend whips Rocke into the ropes...Rocke reverses it...Rocke nails him with a big knee lift! Alisterfiend goes down! Alisterfiend looks hurt. He falls to the mat. Rocke comes running off the ropes for a legdrop...Alisterfiend pulls the ref in the way! It's a three way collision! I don't think that's what Rocke had in mind! Rocke has got him up for a Samoan Drop...Alisterfiend grabs his head! Rocke takes him down, but in return was devastated by a kind of neckbreaker! Rocke wraps his fingers around his throat...way up!!! And crashing down to the canvass! What a choke slam! Rocke slowly gets to his feet...and gets leveled by a devastating shoulder tackle!!! A kick to the gut and Alisterfiend is buckled over...DDT! Alisterfiend grabs the rope to block it and Rocke takes a hard fall! Rocke is leaving! Bear Hug!!! Alisterfiend is in alot of pain right now!!! Alisterfiend is looking to take a breather in the corner...here comes Rocke! BIG avalanche! Alisterfiend crumples to the mat. Nobody home as Rocke ducks down leaving nothing but air! Alisterfiend is mocking Rocke. Alisterfiend is about to slam Rocke with a chair!! StoneCold jumped into the ring... too late. Rocke is injured. This will definitely upset the race towards the belt!! Winner: Alisterfiend
