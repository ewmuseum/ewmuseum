Sharpshooter Hello...This is Sharpshooter bringing you tomorrows card for the iIPW...This isnt any regular card either...It is the first round of the Tag Team Title Tourney...Below is the card and my choices to win... -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Tag Tournoment First Round Painmaster/Firestorm vs Million Man/Blackjak I am going with the vets (P-master and Firestorm) to take this one... -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Sandman/vixen vs Kyle Karusso/Halifax Although Vixens a girl...She has mroe bite than bark...Her and Sandy will advance... -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Jake Fury/Magnma vs Downfall/Bloodz Downfall and Bloodz can talk the talk...but JF and Magnma know how to win this match...Its going to them -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Jaxxx/Big Daddy vs Trendkill/TheVoice The bWo is reunited since Daddy's arrival...They are looking good and should win this match with the two Rookies... -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- First round should look good for the bWo...They have two seperate teams in the tourney which gives them a good chance to bring the titles back to the stable... Ill be back Wednesday with results and an Interveiw with a slammer...God knows who... This is Sharpshooter...Always shootin' from the hip... none
