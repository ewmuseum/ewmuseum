Newbies: the story of CyberSlam

the hub's own Newbies page, captured December 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
It all started April 1997. Then there were a few brave soldiers who were willing to take on the establishment. They were willing to risk their careers and reputations to follow a dream of an online wrestling world where wrestlers could battle each other in a virtual ring. So Chaos Bill, CatWoman, Hardcore, Tough Guy, Studd, Eliminator, DynamiteKid, TheJester, Marksman, DigiSlam, Dog, TheBat and Thawk pulled their life savings together, kissed the establishment good bye and started CyberSlam. Those early days were rough, there was only one federation, The CWF. The stadium seats were empty but the enthusiasm ran high. Unfortunately, these brave soldiers ran into problems and soon feuds began to develop. Tough Guy attacked CatWoman and dissension began to creep in. It was decided that CyberSlam needed a leader to take control and take it to the next level. And like a gift from God, The Slammer walked through the stadium doors and became President. The Slammer quickly brought order to CyberSlam, he banned Tough Guy from CyberSlam and sanctioned Hardcore. He decided that online wrestlers be called Slammers and online wrestling, Slammin. That once a month a special event would be held. it would be the Ultimate CyberSlam Event and it would be named CSlam. That the winner of CSlam would be CyberSlam's Official Champion and would take home a purse of $100. And a legend was born out of the first CSlam event, Chaos Bill. He devastated the competition and set the standard for Slammin?. In the months that followed Chaos Bill tried to repeat but was unsuccessful. Instead new champions emerged TheBat, CometMeeker,Disease, Smash Mouth and our reigning champion StuMan. The Slammer, on a mission to make CyberSlam the best thing that ever happened to the Net, created a new federation, CCW(CyberSlam Championship Wrestling). These two federations were joined by a third hardcore federation, CEW. Now the stage was set for CyberSlam 3.0, the brainchild of The Slammer. A virtual world were Slammin could take place without any restrictions, no log in, no hassle, just pure adrenaline pushing fun! It took a few months to put together but CyberSlam 3.0 debuted October 1, 1997 and with it came the introduction of a fourth federation, CBF (CyberSlam Blast federation). CyberSlam is made up of four main areas FEDS, NEWS, SLAMMERS and JOIN. JOIN. To become a slammer a user must first become a member of CyberSlam. You first Slammer is free, your second Slammer cost $10.00 for one month of Slammin'. CyberSlam has become quite popular, so there is no guarantee that your free Slammer will be accepted, we currently have over 1,000 people on a waiting list. You can always decide to jump the waiting list by paying $10.00 for one month of Slammin'. FEDS is where you will find the CyberSlam federations. It is within each federation that you can look up your favorite Slammer?s standings. You can also review past matches by going to the Review area within the federation. if you?re a Slammer you can use the Mail Box area to contact other Slammers. There are weekly Title Bouts, where the current federation title holder must defend their title against a top contender. SLAMMERS area contains a listing of all Slammers and Stables. Yes, Stables. Stables are alliances set up by Slammers with other Slammers. We have LOD, Revolution, BWF and many others. Slammers have the ability to edit their profiles and Stable leaders can control their stables. NEWS by the greatest staff of journalist anywhere. We have had many great newsmen The Legend, Shooter and Jerry Mack. Our editorial section is dedicated to editorials and interviews. By following the News, you can follow what?s going on within CyberSlam. Another great feature of CyberSlam is the Mail Box. The Mail Box allows Slammers to email each other via CyberSlam.
