iMAW Tuesday Terror

14 August 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Jamhead Yo Yo Yo, this is Jamhead. In the Evening when we start to Jam... here is the card. Tuesday Terror --------------- For the iMAW World Championship Mattskeee (c) v. Nemasis Slam your opponent 3 times. Tie goes to points. Nemasis earns a second chance at a first chance at the title. Does that make sense? Don't worry about it ---------------------------------------- Pred: Mattskeee will pull it off hands down. ---------------------------------------- For the iMAW North American Championship Flannigan (c) v. Heat_streak Slam your opponent twice. Tie goes to rewrite. This match is a Picnic Match. Inside the ring is a picnic basket full of your normal picnic goodies, from silverware to food to a bottle of champagne. Also, there's a grill in the ring with steaks cooking. You can use any of these objects in the match with your opponent. I expect the winner to do a rewrite. ---------------------------------------- Pred: Heat_Streak will finally win the N.A. Title in his 4TH atempt ---------------------------------------- For the iMAW Bloodlust Championship Taurus (c) v. Kosmic Slam your opponent twice. Tie goes to points. ---------------------------------------- Pred: Taurus will pull it off against this newbee. He has held the belt the entire month. ---------------------------------------- Undercard --------- Chunkman v. Syco_hardy Slam your opponent twice. Tie goes to points. ---------------------------------------- Pred: Syco_hardy ---------------------------------------- Goldmask v. Trainwreck Slam your opponent twice. Tie goes to points. ---------------------------------------- Pred: Goldmask ---------------------------------------- Kurtt_angle v. Rusty Slam your opponent twice. Tie goes to points. ---------------------------------------- Pred: kurtt_angle ---------------------------------------- Manicmailman v. Jaques Slam your opponent twice. Tie goes to points. ---------------------------------------- Pred:Manicmailman ---------------------------------------- Jack v. Masterpiece Slam your opponent twice. Tie goes to points. ---------------------------------------- Pred: Jack ---------------------------------------- Alright, there's your card and early at that. Even though I don't say it, I'll use the results of a lot of these matches to determine #1 contender's for titles. And even if you lose, if you put up a good effort on the boards it will look rather favorably on you in the future. Are you gonna call 911? and spoil all of our fun. This is Jamhead signing off. none
