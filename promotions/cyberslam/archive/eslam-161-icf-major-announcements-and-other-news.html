{ICF} Major announcements and other news

22 May 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Tha 'N Phoremir Some news on the new superstars here in the ICF. Tru Killa Black Gizmo looks as if he will take the ICF "Extreme" division to new limits. Kniq Clayton looks to be the next promising star here in the ICF as $cott Revenue, Mike Sensation, and Kommander Kill have recently announced their retirements from ICF. Razool and his Guards of Babylon.....Everybody thought it was the stupidest idea ever when "Then Commisioner of ICF, now President" Hargens announced that the team would be formed. Now, Everybody is wanting in. Jordan Jericho still looks to be one of the top superstars in the ICF though. New superstars, The Unknown, Inferno, Wildchild, and Dragonfire, should be VERY promising stars in the ICF. Mach 9, well, I don't know, but he should also do good. Which brings me to my next point, Why was Friday Fright Night cancelled for a month? Well, you are about to find out! It was cancelled, because there was to much "Hardcore" activity seen on ICF television for a local group, so they reported it and ICF Friday Fright night has been cancelled for about a month. Oh well, it was going to be delayed anyways due to Owner Ryan Smith's vacation departure. Also, The Informer site has been doing almost TOO awesome as of late. He is getting more hits than ever on his site. This should go over well. Also, Vega and Zombie have been getting better and better as time goes on. Zombie was this close (--) to getting fired at Fright Night last week , but overcame the odds and pulled off a victory. The Bloodfest updated cards and this week's Tuesday Torture updated card will be posted later on tonight! International Combat Federation
