PreMatch Comments Deep Freeze Aragorn slaps on the sleeper! Ice_storm is fading...the ref comes over to check! 1...2...the hand comes up! Aragorn releases the hold and delivers a forearm to the kidney for good measure. Off the ropes...Ice_storm grabs a hold and goes for a fireman's carry. Aragorn holds on and rolls him into a small package! 1...2...kickout! Ice_storm looks ripe for the picking! Aragorn grabs his ankle and it appears we have a figure four on the way...No! Ice_storm stops him with a boot to the lower back. Ice_storm has his legs...trying to roll Aragorn over for the Boston Crab...and Aragorn powers out! Looks like it's going to take alot more than that to put Aragorn away! Aragorn slaps on the sleeper! Ice_storm is fading...the ref comes over to check! 1...2...the hand comes up! Aragorn releases the hold and delivers a forearm to the kidney for good measure. Aragorn has his legs...trying to roll Ice_storm over for the Boston Crab...and Ice_storm powers out! Looks like it's going to take alot more than that to put Ice_storm away! Ice_storm grapevines the leg...going for the head to complete the STF...Aragorn rolls over for a pinning combination! 1...2...kickout! Ice_storm was lucky he didn't get pinned right there! Figure four by Aragorn! Extremely punnishing...now Ice_storm is trying to reverse it! He rolls over and the pressure is put back on the legs of Aragorn! Both men feeling the effects! This is unusual! Ice_storm appears to be attempting a mandible claw! Aragorn bites his fingers. So much for that idea. Aragorn with a dropkick to the knees...sidestepped by Ice_storm! Aragorn with a dropkick to the knees...sidestepped by Ice_storm! Aragorn goes for a roundhouse right...backdrop by Ice_storm over the top rope! Ice_storm climbs out after him and slaps on a cobra clutch! The fans are on their feet! Now he throws Aragorn back into the ring at the ten count. Ice_storm goes for a roundhouse right...backdrop by Aragorn over the top rope! Aragorn climbs out after him and slaps on a cobra clutch! The fans are on their feet! Now he throws Ice_storm back into the ring at the ten count. Ice_storm has him down, knees on his back...trying to roll him over for the bow and arr...can't get it locked in. Aragorn is too close to the ropes and breaks the hold. Ice_storm whips Aragorn into the ropes, setting up for a monkey flip. He telegraphed it WAY too early! Aragorn gives him a stomp on the head for good measure. Aragorn shoots in with a European uppercut. Blocked! Ice_storm turns it into an armbar. He's really applying the pressure. Ice_storm goes for the pin! One...foot in the ropes. Is that a "SCOOPS" sign in the third row? Ice_storm reaches around...looking to lock in an abdominal stretch...BIG hiptoss by Aragorn! Ice_storm down to the canvass...Aragorn comes off the ropes with a big splash. Ice_storm rolls out of the way! Nobody home! He gets up and slaps on a bow and arrow submission hold! The ref is asking...but Aragorn refuses to submit! Aragorn off the ropes...sleeperhold! Ice_storm doesn't have it completely locked in and Aragorn slips out the back door. Ice_storm gets him on the mat with a well placed right hand...he's going for the claw! Aragorn rolls out of the ring to avoid the hold. He rolls back in before the ref begins to count. Looks like Aragorn is hiding a foreign object in him boot. Ice_storm doesn't seem to notice, though. Aragorn shoots in with a European uppercut. Blocked! Ice_storm turns it into an armbar. He's really applying the pressure. It's Ice_storm with a nerve pinch. He's digging those fingers in...and Aragorn takes him down with a shoulder roll. Nice counter! Aragorn goes for a headbutt...Ice_storm latches on a nasty claw hold! Great reflexes! It almost looks like he's palming a basketball in there. They lock up...both men looking for an advantage. WOW! Ice_storm tosses Aragorn into the corner! Great show of strength! Aragorn shoots in with a European uppercut. Blocked! Ice_storm turns it into an armbar. He's really applying the pressure. Aragorn gets him on the mat with a well placed right hand...he's going for the claw! Ice_storm rolls out of the ring to avoid the hold. He rolls back in before the ref begins to count. This crowd is really divided between Ice_storm and Aragorn. All managers have been barred from ringside so hopefully we can settle this feud once and for all! Ice_storm has his legs...trying to roll Aragorn over for the Boston Crab...and Aragorn powers out! Looks like it's going to take alot more than that to put Aragorn away! Irish whip into the corner, reversed, reversed again...Ice_storm levels him with a short clothesline! But man does he look dizzy! Ice_storm side steps the dropkick...and slaps on the figure four leg lock! Aragorn reaches the rope after one minute. Aragorn off the ropes...sunset flip! Can he pull him down? No! Ice_storm drops a big fist to the face. Now he's got the legs...rolls Ice_storm over...Scorpion Deathlock! Aragorn reaches the rope after 45 seconds! Ice_storm throws Aragorn into the ropes. He goes for a hiptoss...but Aragorn puts on the brakes! Slaps on an abdominal stretch! Ice_storm is complaining about a hair pull and the hold gets broken. Aragorn is going for a crucifix. Can he get it!?!? Ice_storm breaks free...spins around...full nelson! Great counter! Aragorn slaps on a claw hold. Ice_storm counters and slaps on a claw of his own! How long can these two keep this up? Somebody had better get some aspirin! Aragorn slaps on the sleeper! Ice_storm is fading...the ref comes over to check! 1...2...the hand comes up! Aragorn releases the hold and delivers a forearm to the kidney for good measure. Ice_storm is out of it and...Oh! Aragorn applies the Vertabreaker..It's all over!! Aragorn has sent a message to all the other wrestlers that he's back. Winner: Aragorn
