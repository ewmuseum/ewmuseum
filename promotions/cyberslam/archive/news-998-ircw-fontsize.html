iRCW Fontsize

15 August 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Typeface Typeface here, rockin' at you a bit later than usual due to some real-life heiniosity, if I may borrow a phrase from Edge and Christian. Let's cut right to the videotape, shall we? **Monday Fire** As usual, Maverick wins the rookie rumbles, but there's a surprise here... Fat Tony has managed to save his job by actually showing up. It's amazing. The rest of the rookies are all gone. Some (Hogan, Buffdude, Butchclaymor) won't be missed. One of them will be. Alas, Morrigan, we hardly knew ye... The card openers gave us two extremes of wrestling. Snyper had an amazingly lopsided victory over JRC, while Kurse and Panic had such a close match, nobody's quite sure who won. Maverick is considering a rematch... The Rookie Tag Team match happened... wait, did I say Rookie? I must mean 'no-show'. And that's what Lance Storm and Qwee Wee did. Coupla'a posuers if you ask me. Amazing Jiggalos with the win here. The Hardcore Match was supposed to be a three-way event, but Keebler didn't spend much time doing anything other than being beaten on, as Freak forces his way past Keebler and Hfly to win it. In the Mid-Event, it was Haz against Greenhell Junior, Heavyweight Champ against TV Champ... And Greenjeans kicked, if I may coin a phrase, "All holy *bleep*." Amazing. Just which belt is the big deal, anyways? The Tag Team Triple Threat match had a no-show as well, as Page left his partner Snoball hanging in the breeze for what would be Snoball's last match. The Evil Knights get a close win over Resident Evil. Snoball has announced his retirement from slamming. While almost always quiet to the point of inactiveness, when he spoke, it usually didn't suck. We'll miss him... well, we'll probably think he's still there, just not doing anything. Contendership time! And a whole lot of matches to go through here. The Original Gangstas apparently had their minds on the main event, because "The Future," Iron and Exterminator, totally crushed them... just like Sugar crushed Knight for her shot at the Richmond title. She's had shots at the Television Title and Heavyweight Title in the last week or two, so hopefully she'll win this one... Static likewise dominated over The Infinite for a shot at the Southern States title, and we'll have words from him in a bit... Thunderbolt, one of the rookie phenoms we've been graced with lately, fought his way past Iron and Speed for a shot at the Heavyweight title. And finally, the main event... well, we don't really have a winner, or a Blood and Guts title contender. Why? Well... both men are hospitalized after one hell of a brutal match. The match ended in a double count-out as hese two friends with differing ideals went at each other tooth and nail (and various other sharp objects). Look for this match to get released into the iRCW Archives soon, it'll be a definate fan-favorite. **** And so, with a bit of a delay until Thursday Rage's card is posted, we here at Fontsize decided to bring you a special quickie-style rundown on just who your contenders are. **Meet Your Contenders** Richmond Contender: Sugar The leader of the iRCW branch of the Knights, very close friend (one might say intimate friend) of Damage Inc's Manowar, and the only still-active female in the iRCW, Sugar has been the embodiment of the Knight philosophy since her arrival. Her talent matches her spirit, as her recent matches for the Television and Heavyweight titles have shown. Should she defeat Freak for the title, her reign will probably be a long one. Southern States Contender: Static A relative rookie to the iRCW with a distinctive voice, Static is determined to get the respect he feels he deserves, ready to blackmail the current Southern States champion, Mister Zinc, for a title shot if he didn't win the contendership. And the man deserves one. I had a chance to sit down with Static briefly earlier today. ** Typeface: Static, you were booked in a Southern States contender match on Monday Fire. Now, just a week ago on Thursday Rage, you deafted Thunderbolt, Deathknight, and Bloodknight in a four corners match... and yet Thunderbolt goes on to a Heavyweight contender match. Any thoughts on this? Static: I have wanted a shot at the Southern States Champ Mr. Zinc for quite along time. I won that 4 corners match figureing that if I won Mav give me a shot at the title. Well he came through on that front. I get a contendership match with The Infinite. Then for some reason he gives Thunderchump a chance to face Haz for the Heavyweight Title. I am wondering what in the hell did he do to deserve that?! I thought guys like Iron, Thunderbolt, and I were still on the Southern States level. I guess getting his butt kicked by me promoted Thunderbolt to main even Status. Maybe I should take a dive in my next match. Losing seems to work for everyone else. ** Television Contender: Twelvevoltman Announced as the contender last week, when the champion was on vacation, Twelvevoltman makes up the other half of the iRCW Knights. A man with an overused gimmick of getting people's names wrong and speaking like Captain Kirk, this slammer's raw staing power is imposible to ignore, having dominated the charts since his arrival in the fed. But recent events may make one wonder just where the loyalties of a drunkard lie. Tag Team Contenders: Iron and Exterminator Exterminator was declared to be "The Future of the iRCW" by Sickboy upon his promotion. Iron is a rookie with a good mix of luck and talent. Together, these two teamed up out of nowhere to win their title shot. To be honest, I don't think anyone's worried about "Jolt" losing the tag titles quite yet... but these two have a better chance than anyone of making me eat those words. Heavyweight contender: Thunderbolt What can you say about this man... the iRCW's Rookie Phenom until Mister Zinc arrived, Thunderbolt had shown amazing skill inside the ring and on the mic, tussling with Haz in an Iron Circle match, going after Manowar, and in general being one of the fed's fastest rising stars. He's got a chance to rise to the ultimate height... and hhe may just be able to take it. Blood and Guts Contenders: ??? Nobody's sure who won the contendership for the B&G title, Reinhardt or NBK... both men are decent slammers, although their hardcore skills didn't get a chance to shine until last night, a night that may have ended their careers. Best friends until Reinhardt became disillusioned with being a good guy, these two showed the world that they both deserve the Blood and Guts title... the question is, will either of them be in any shape to take on Kiddpain? **** We hope you enjoyed this look at your contenders. Join me tomorrow when the Thusday Rage preview will be coupled with a look at your current champions. And then on Thursday, we'll announce the winner of the 'Who Should Typeface Interview' contest. Until then, you keep on slamming, and I'll keep on reporting. none
