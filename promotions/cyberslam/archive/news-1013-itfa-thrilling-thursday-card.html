iTFA: Thrilling Thursday Card

16 August 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Sprite Thrilling Thursday Thursday, August 17th MAIN EVENT World Title vs Hardcore Title Lumberjack Match Kronus_ (C) vs Freezer (C) Simply put, title vs title. The winner walks out the World Champ. The loser is the Hardcore Champ. But how do we make this match more interesting? We make it a lumberjack match. I invite EVERY slammer in the iTFA to join this match at ringside. One guy gets tossed out of the ring? Toss him back in. What? You want more impact? Hmmm, okay. Those of you who wish to be involved in this match, you can slam *ONE* match vs either Kronus_ or Freezer. How do your slams effect the outcome? Simple. If you beat one of these 2 men, the other will receive a victory added to their total. Interesting, eh? Iron man match rules (slam each other TWICE) MID-CARD Peoples Title Match Eyeless (C) vs Vapor Vapor is nearing the 50% cutoff so why not just elevate him to a new level and allow him to face off against one of his nearest and dearest foes. These two know each other pretty well. Enjoy! Grudge match rules w/ Vapor slamming the tiebreaker, only if needed TV Title 4 Corners Match Arrow vs Jaw vs Jadakis vs Bowe_davis With Vapor going after the People's title, the TV title is vacant. How do we fill it? We put 4 men in the same ring and make them touch ALL 4 corners in succession to gain the victory. Slam each other ONCE "To Be, or not To Be" a Contender Matches Deaman vs Pimp_man vs Oi_boy Something new and exciting. These 3 slammers will fight for the right to be a #1 contender. To what title you ask? That will be upto me. Work your butts off in this match, on the boards, and if you win, you could find yourself with a World Title shot. Slack off and win this match, you just might not get a title shot at all. Slam each other TWICE (Oi_boy, see this. 2x each!!!) Darque vs The_evil_one Same thing as above. You both are slightly lower in the standings thus a World Title shot may not be quite fair, but a People's or Hardcore Title could be on the horizon. Slam each other THREE (3) times! UNDERCARD Strap Match Mr_ppv vs Aight What happens when you tie two slammers together by a leather strap? Well, they beat each other senseless until one can not answer the 10 bell. Slam each other TWICE Welcome to the fed Match Twiztid vs Ruffryder Twiztid is new so we need to introduce him to the fed. Who gets the duty of showing this newcomer around the ring? Ruffryder! Slam each other TWICE Image is nothing, News is everything. Listen to Sprite. none
