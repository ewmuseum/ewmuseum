iNCW THURSDAY THRASH

6 December 2000

xx STEPPING STONE RING TITLE MATCH Loverboy vs Johnnye vs Anarchy This is the title that pushes the rookies to the spotlight. Anarchy last month showed he could handle the show. Both Loverboy and Johnnye has the chance to wear the ring. Should be a great opener. US CONTENDER MATCH Outcast_ vs Darkrider Darkrider, one of the vets of the federation who has always just been there. Just so close...but the brass ring always seem to slip away. Outcast is another dark rookie who showed great promise. The US title, only second to the world title, are in their sights. WORLD TITLE CONTENDER MATCH Lier vs Psycho_Dad Lier, a champion several times over. Psycho_Dad, a man who ruled last month only to slip due to a technicality. He wants what he considers his belt back and Lier wants that shot to become a grand slam champion. TLC BLOOD TITLE Stigmata vs Dell Again, two vets of the federation. Stigmata last month held off an offensive flurry by newcomer Leatherface to keep his Blood title. The Hardcore slammer now faces a major talent in Dell. A former world champion, he and Stigmata face off in a TLC match Tables, Ladders, and Chairs oh my! BURIED ALIVE US TITLE Evilhell vs Electric_Kid Evilhell been the trademark name of midcarder. So has Electric_Kid. Both men have the talent, the drive, just not the notice. Time to change that. On Evilhell's request, this US title match is a Buried Alive match. Which man will become the #2 guy in the federation. Electric_Kid been there before...is it Evilhell's turn? LIGHT vs DARK MATCH Psycho_Dad and Anarchy vs Heat_ and Evilhell The background here is simple. Heat and Evilhell ushered in the Era of Darkness. Psycho_dad wish to make that the Era of Light. Having brought Anarchy under his wing, these four men will start what I'm sure will be an epic fued. WORLD TITLE SHOT Heat_ vs Velocity They grow up so fast. Last month, Velocity was just starting and in a short time has earned a World title shot. Great right? Problem. Heat has been on an unbelievable winning streak. Velocity is going to have to push it past the limits to win..but as we all know..anything can happen. Experience the Great White North! iNCW
