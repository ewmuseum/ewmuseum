CWO match 83: Coroner vs Brainchz

undated in the source
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PreMatch Comments What's shaken Corney Brainchz with a stiff chop to the chest...goes for a second and Coroner rocks him with an enziguri kick to the head! A clothesline to the back of the head sends Coroner to the mat! Incredible speed as Brainchz is right on top of him...STF! Again, Coroner refuses to submit and the hold is broken after 1 minute. Coroner shoots in with a European uppercut. Blocked! Brainchz turns it into an armbar. He's really applying the pressure. Coroner backs him into the corner with a series of knife-edge chops...climbs the ropes for what looks like a...jumping DDT! No, wait...Brainchz has a firm hold on those ropes and Coroner jumps down. No damage. This is unusual! Brainchz appears to be attempting a mandible claw! Coroner bites his fingers. So much for that idea. Impressive displays from both men at this point. Coroner with the speed advantage while...Brainchz rocks him in the corner with an avalanche! No place to go!!! Brainchz whips Coroner into the ropes, setting up for a monkey flip. He telegraphed it WAY too early! Coroner gives him a stomp on the head for good measure. Brainchz with the go behind...full nelson! But a very slippery Coroner is able to slide out before any damage could be inflicted. Forget about it!!! Coroner ties up Brainchz in the ropes and he's using him like a human punching bag! Oh NO! Brainchz connects with a solid kick to the groin! OUCH! Brainchz is climbing to the top rope...Coroner shakes the ropes! Brainchz falls right onto the turnbuckle allowing Coroner to climb up...superplex!!! Brainchz is prone in the center of the ring! Coroner is on the top rope...big splash! 1..kickout! Brainchz gets up from the mat and Coroner is right there with a strong running shoulder block! Levels him! Coroner with a roundhouse kick. Brainchz traps the leg...Coroner tries for the enziguri kick...missed! Brainchz gets him down on his stomach and locks in the camel clutch! I can hear those vertabrae popping from here! He lets Coroner up! He's not finnished punnishing him! Coroner off the ropes...sunset flip! Can he pull him down? No! Brainchz drops a big fist to the face. Now he's got the legs...rolls Brainchz over...Scorpion Deathlock! Coroner reaches the rope after 45 seconds! Brainchz stuns him with a punch...bounces off the ropes...leapfrog by Coroner...on his way back...Coroner nails him with a Frankensteiner! The crowd goes nuts! Brainchz with an atomic drop...blocked! Coroner connects with a powerful spinning mule kick! That'll turn your lights out for good! Brainchz with the go behind...full nelson! But a very slippery Coroner is able to slide out before any damage could be inflicted. Brainchz whips him into the ropes and goes in for the clothesline...Coroner ducks it...off the other side and a beautiful cross-body block! Only a one count. Wow! Brainchz is getting into it with a fan on the outside! Apparently the kid is wearing a Coroner t-shirt! Brainchz down to the canvass...Coroner comes off the ropes with a big splash. Brainchz rolls out of the way! Nobody home! He gets up and slaps on a bow and arrow submission hold! The ref is asking...but Coroner refuses to submit! Brainchz side steps the dropkick...and slaps on the figure four leg lock! Coroner reaches the rope after one minute. Flying fistdrop from the top rope...connects! I can't really see from this angle but I think Brainchz is busted open! Brainchz sets up Coroner on the top rope for a superplex! Coroner rams him with an elbow! Now from the top rope, Coroner with a moonsault!!! But he's not through with Brainchz yet... Coroner with a small package! 1...2...kickout! It's going to take more than that! We'd like to take this opportunity to remind you that for your own safety, as well as the safety of the wrestlers in the ring, please refrain from throwing food and other objects at your computer. Coroner off the ropes...sunset flip! Can he pull him down? No! Brainchz drops a big fist to the face. Now he's got the legs...rolls Brainchz over...Scorpion Deathlock! Coroner reaches the rope after 45 seconds! Coroner looks ripe for the picking! Brainchz grabs his ankle and it appears we have a figure four on the way...No! Coroner stops him with a boot to the lower back. Brainchz slaps on a sleeper...but Coroner drops down with a jawbreaker! He springs to the top rope, BIG double axe-handle to the head! Brainchz throws a big elbow, Coroner rams him with a knee at the same time! They both hit the mat and the fists are flying! Throw the book out the window, this one's becoming a brawl! Brainchz goes for the pin! One...foot in the ropes. Is that a "SCOOPS" sign in the third row? Brainchz with the headbutt...he stunned himself! Brainchz falls down! Coroner to the top rope...BIG legdrop from way up there! 1...2...NO! Close! Brainchz is out of it and...Oh! Coroner applies the Nail In The Coffin..It's all over!! Coroner has sent a message to all the other wrestlers that he's back. Winner: Coroner
