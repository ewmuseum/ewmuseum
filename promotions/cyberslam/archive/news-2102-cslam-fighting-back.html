Cslam: Fighting Back!!!

3 February 2001
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Extreme:Your source for unbiased news Opening up the camera shows Extreme standing outside the RWF arena. For an arena that was supposedly shut down there is an awful lot of activity. "Hello Cslam I come to you today live from the RWF arena, where some very exciting things are happening. This fed like so many others is going to press on until there is no hope left or they start cslam back up. Yes you heard me right the RWF is still a fully functioning fed, just like the CEW, CWFe, and any other fed out there that's still holding cards and slamming matches. Now Cslam here is what I propose. Let me state that again. He is what I expect from each and every one of you. If you truly love this game, if you truly want to help bring it back. Contact your Commish, get him running cards again and show Snowball and the rest of the world Cslam will not go down without a fight. For now I am EXTREME." I'm not timid, I'm not passive, and I'm sure as hell not giving up yet, Because I'm EXTREME!! (Thank you Lordmaster for bringing this to my attention. If your a Commish r if your a slammer and have news you want me to post, drop me a line at [detail withheld]. I don't care if you’re an indy or a major. Just drop me a line.) none
