[cew] Rooftop Report w/ Wes Slater

22 May 2000
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[name withheld] Damn the old methods, it's back to reality. The baseline is the goal and mothas better start running faster to catch up to NBK. Seems to me that kelpers in the CEW are truly more ignorant than I thought. The fools keep lining up for their shot at greatness. Their chance to step the best. Some of you would come to the conclusion that I am like every champion before that has become egotistical, self-indulgent, and power hungry. Problem with that is, NBK was all those things way before I got a hold of the World Strap, and so assuming that I have all of a sudden turned into a mega-maniac with dictatorial intentions and image only goals is ludicrous. That is why so many of you are not ready to be who I am and so many of you can not get back to were you once were cause I'm changing the whole idea of World Champion. For so long so many things I did went unnoticed and for that, everyone paid a big price when it came back to haunt them. Even now things I do go unnoticed and yet again the whole CEW will rein in the pain that is NBK. As usual, NBK will have to name names, cause I frankly don't care who gets what out of this as long as they go away enlightened. Chris Powers. If ever the CEW needed to see exactly what I mean by not noticing what the deal is with NBK, Chris Powers is the man. Hey Powers, thanks for all your help and keep doing what your doing and oh yeah keep believing you got me under wraps. Then of course there is Jeff Atkinson. This young buck seems to believe he can go out and get anyone and throw them at me and get back at me in some way. JA, you need to think long and hard about what you do before you react to my verbal beat downs. You have to have all your bases covered, cause when all is said and done, NBK knows all your tricks, yet you know nothing of me. Phobos, Brock Williams, Scott Slade, Jerry Gallows, whoever you want me to smack around for a few minutes, I’ll be glad to JA. Cause they just don’t get it either. AC. The man, the myth, the legend. Not likely. I embarrassed you AC. I took your title and now you are busy bitching with the only other man in the CEW who even comes close to you when it comes to being embarrassed by yours truly. Nick Charman and AC deserve each other. There time has gone. They flashed in their pans and now the light is gone. Gentlemen, it's time you step back and look at who you see before you. The man who has his own personal NBK signature CEW WORLD Title Belt. The man who is the one and only active CEW wrestler ever to be in the CEW Hall-of-Fame. The Vice President who wields more power round the CEW on a weekly and monthly basis than Powers and JA combined. I am simply the Talent. I am the Talent AC and Charman used to be. I am the Talent countless CEWer’s will never be. I am the Talent no one will ever come close to being again. When I sleep you don’t know what I dream. When I breathe you don’t know how it hurts. What makes it worse for all of you CEWer’s is you had your chance. You could have taken advantage of the smaller, weaker version of NBK, but no longer. It simply won't happen. I got my mind made up and every last time you think of the CEW, every last time you see the CEW, every last time the CEW exists there will be three other letters attached to it. NBK!
