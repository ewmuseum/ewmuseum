Oswald Welcome back your friendly neighborhood reporter. Now doing three times the action. the AWO, the CGW and the IUWA. So lets get right too it. First off. The iUWA Commisioner Arcadia announced that they are building a new arena, where you ask? Well in Wakonda Oregon. now, even though it seems that CSlam is over. it isn't because not every fed is still up and running. Arcadia has seized back control of his fed and he promise a card tommorow. Now onto the AWO. This fed is in peril. The problem is most everyone is saying goodbye in HTML. But atleast it is a major and they know when to close their tags. But I think there are some signs of life as I recieved an email from California just moments ago. No fed I am in will die. Not until my script is deleted along with the others i will not let a fed slip into death. the AWO is full of talent. Whoa California said something good. Come n guys lets not let the fed die. Don't let either fed die, show Snowball what slammers are made of. If you want me to cover your fed, email me. [detail withheld]. If you want an interview email me or contact me at [detail withheld] on my ICQ. Come on i will cover every fed anybody asks me to. Until next time(because there will be a next time) Thats new to me Oswald none
