CWO match 218: Volume vs Purple_haze

undated in the source
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PreMatch Comments Dawg Pound. They lock up...the ref sees a hair pull and breaks it up. Definite bad blood between these two! Purple_haze is complaining to he ref about a hair pull and...NO! Volume cheap shots him with a fist to the back of the head! Why doesn't the referee disqualify him!?!? Volume slaps on a headlock. Purple_haze picks him up...bell to belly suplex! Purple_haze is climbing to the top rope...Volume shakes the ropes! Purple_haze falls right onto the turnbuckle allowing Volume to climb up...superplex!!! Bear Hug!!! Volume is in alot of pain right now!!! Purple_haze gets up from the mat and Volume is right there with a strong running shoulder block! Levels him! Purple_haze whips him into the ropes and goes in for the clothesline...Volume ducks it...off the other side and a beautiful cross-body block! Only a one count. Purple_haze throws a big elbow, Volume rams him with a knee at the same time! They both hit the mat and the fists are flying! Throw the book out the window, this one's becoming a brawl! Test of strength in the middle of the ring now. Purple_haze twists his wrists...hands to the mat...and Volume gets his fingers stomped! Volume off the ropes...he leaps...he misses! Cross-body block attempted, but Purple_haze ducks under it! Purple_haze rolls out of the ring. He saw someone holding a Volume poster and grabs it! He begins tearing it up! Purple_haze rolls back into the ring and attempts to make Volume eat a piece! Purple_haze is prone in the center of the ring! Volume is on the top rope...big splash! 1..kickout! One way ticket to Turnbuckle-City as Volume executes a picture perfect slingshot! Purple_haze wraps his fingers around his throat...way up!!! And crashing down to the canvass! What a choke slam! Volume with a small package! 1...2...kickout! It's going to take more than that! Volume whips him into the ropes...looking for a backdrop...Purple_haze sees it coming. DDT! Volume’s head bounced off the mat like a superball! Purple_haze off the ropes...Volume with a great leapfrog! Purple_haze puts on the brakes and rocks him with a swinging neckbreaker! Volume is leaving! He's had enough and is heading back to the dressing room. The ref's count is up to six and here comes Purple_haze! He dropkicks him from behind and now he's dragging Volume back to the ring...by the ear!!! Volume with a small package! 1...2...kickout! It's going to take more than that! Mule kick from Volume...but Purple_haze cartwheels out of the way. A little showboating going on! Bear Hug!!! Volume is in alot of pain right now!!! Volume leaves the ring and returns with a chair! He goes to swing, but the ref grabs it! Purple_haze gets him up! The crowd is up on their feet and BAM! Sidewalk slam! Volume is in trouble! Purple_haze grabs him and is pointing to the crowd! Volume connects with an elbow to the face! Purple_haze took way too long! Purple_haze is running towards Volume...he doesn't see him! Volume was setting up for something and just got crushed! But Purple_haze missed the mark and has fallen out of the ring! Those metal ring steps must have hurt! Purple_haze gets him up on his shoulders....NO! Volume slips out and catches him with a forearm to the back of the head! Volume off the ropes...cross body block! Purple_haze catches him! Turns it into a nasty shoulderbreaker! Flying fistdrop from the top rope...connects! I can't really see from this angle but I think Purple_haze is busted open! We could see a bear hug here...Purple_haze can't hold him! Volume breaks the hold and slams his head into the turnbuckle. Volume with a sunset flip! 1...2...Purple_haze rolls it over! 1...2...Volume turns it into a small package! 1...kickout! Purple_haze gets him up! The crowd is up on their feet and BAM! Sidewalk slam! Purple_haze wraps his fingers around his throat...way up!!! And crashing down to the canvass! What a choke slam! Purple_haze slaps on a sleeper...but Volume drops down with a jawbreaker! He springs to the top rope, BIG double axe-handle to the head! Purple_haze whips Volume into the corner. Volume sidesteps and Purple_haze gets a shoulderful of turnbuckle! Say goodbye to that spine! Purple_haze rocks Volume with a nasty atomic drop! Volume is leaving! He's had enough and is heading back to the dressing room. The ref's count is up to six and here comes Purple_haze! He dropkicks him from behind and now he's dragging Volume back to the ring...by the ear!!! Purple_haze has him up. Loks like he’s going for a piledriver! No...a shoulderbreaker. Wait! Volume grabs the ropes and takes him down with neck scissors! Purple_haze is complaining to the ref about a hair pull. I don't think he's buying it. Purple_haze slaps him! Volume is just standing there laughing. Now he starts to walk away...turns and NAILS Purple_haze with a flying forearm! Volume whips him into the corner...a running start for a shoulder ram and Purple_haze dodges the hit! Nothing but turnbuckle! Purple_haze stuns him with a punch...bounces off the ropes...leapfrog by Volume...on his way back...Volume nails him with a Frankensteiner! The crowd goes nuts! We could see a bear hug here...Purple_haze can't hold him! Volume breaks the hold and slams his head into the turnbuckle. Volume whips him into the corner...a running start for a shoulder ram and Purple_haze dodges the hit! Nothing but turnbuckle! Purple_haze with a stiff chop to the chest...goes for a second and Volume rocks him with an enziguri kick to the head! Volume slowly gets to his feet...and gets leveled by a devastating shoulder tackle!!! One way ticket to Turnbuckle-City as Volume executes a picture perfect slingshot! Purple_haze gets him up! The crowd is up on their feet and BAM! Sidewalk slam! Say goodbye to that spine! Purple_haze rocks Volume with a nasty atomic drop! Purple_haze gets up from the mat and Volume is right there with a strong running shoulder block! Levels him! Volume is heading back to the locker room! The crowd is really giving it to him...WAIT! Here comes Purple_haze with a fire extinguisher! He sprays him in the face and carries him back to the ring! Purple_haze climbs to the top rope...signaling for a double axe-handle. Volume gets a running start and counters with a drop kick! Big mid-air collision! Volume off the ropes. Purple_haze is waiting and executes a textbook fireman's carry. Flying fistdrop from the top rope...connects! I can't really see from this angle but I think Purple_haze is busted open! Purple_haze with a stiff chop to the chest...goes for a second and Volume rocks him with an enziguri kick to the head! Volume leaves the ring and returns with a chair! He goes to swing, but the ref grabs it! An irish whip and Volume is going for the ride...Purple_haze off the ropes from the other side...and Volume takes him down with flying headscissors! Great move! Volume goes for a crucifix! Will he get the pin? NO! Purple_haze brings him down with a Samoan Drop! Purple_haze is trying to get him up...Volume blocks it and is trying to lift him up! No!!! Both go sailing over the top rope! Slowly they climb back into the ring. Purple_haze gets up from the mat and Volume is right there with a strong running shoulder block! Levels him! Purple_haze swings and misses! He catches a boot to the gut for his troubles...and Volume slams him with a jumping DDT! Devastating! Purple_haze with a stiff chop to the chest...goes for a second and Volume rocks him with an enziguri kick to the head! Purple_haze with an atomic drop...blocked! Volume connects with a powerful spinning mule kick! That'll turn your lights out for good! Volume into the corner...Purple_haze is right behind him...and meets a set of knees! Purple_haze goes down, but I think Volume got hurt with that last move! Volume whips Purple_haze into the ropes...Purple_haze reverses it...Purple_haze nails him with a big knee lift! Volume goes down! What does he have there? A roll of coins! Volume clocks him in the jaw and about twenty bucks in change flies all over the ring! Volume is thrown into the corner...here comes Purple_haze! Vader Attack! Nobody home as Volume ducks down leaving nothing but air! Volume is leaving! He's had enough and is heading back to the dressing room. The ref's count is up to six and here comes Purple_haze! He dropkicks him from behind and now he's dragging Volume back to the ring...by the ear!!! Purple_haze whips him into the ropes and goes in for the clothesline...Volume ducks it...off the other side and a beautiful cross-body block! Only a one count. Purple_haze looking pretty cocky...goin' for a figure four leglock, but he's posing for the fans! Volume hooks the tights and rolls him up! 1...2...3!!! Cardinal mistake and Purple_haze pays for it!!! Volume with the win! Winner: Volume
