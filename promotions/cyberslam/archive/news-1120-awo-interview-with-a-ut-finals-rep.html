AWO: Interview with a UT Finals Rep

29 August 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Norm Peterson I was sitting there noticing that the AWO has managed to have two BRB finalists in a row, when in walks Mr. Snake. Hey Mr. Snake, can I buy you a round? Sorry, I don't drink! Then why did you come down to Cheers than? I heard there was a Peanut Butter and Jelly sandwich special. Oh I see, well while your waiting for your sandwich, care to answer some questions? I think I have a few spare moments for you Norm. Great, Sammy can you get me another one, and give my friend here one of those grape nehi's. Mr. Snake you are this month's AWO Final reps? Yes, I am. Proud to be that too! You should be, you are only the third AWOer to make it to the finals, any thoughts about what you will do different? Hopefully win, other than that, give the AWO a quality showing. We have come a long way in our three months Norm, Antispawn and Positron making the final rounds in the BRB's, now if we can wrap up the UT, we'll gain the respect others fail to give us. I hear you, nobody likes to be like Cliffy. So how long have you been in the AWO? have been there for four months now, however, I have had part in the AWO since it's existence as an Indy fed in one way or another. The AWO is Snake's home away from home. Really, what sticks out in your mind about the AWO that keeps bringing you back? Well, being a part of the slammers that helped build the fed, I try to give whatever I can to it, the AWO made me who I am. Duke, what can I say that hasn’t been said about him, he's top notch, he's the AWO heart and soul, he's another reason I stay there, Duke needs me! Hey Norm, it's taking a long time for a PB&J sandwich isn't it? That's partly my fault, let's do some word association, and it should be here. Word association, this could be interesting Norm! Yes it could. Let's see how about The King. He's New Lead, that about sums it all up in two words! MattOcarmore If you asked me that last month, my answer would be different, however, right now the word that comes to my mind is "Houdini" Positron A legend in a past life, has to be! Positron=Positive all around for the AWO. Fatsam Fatsam, let's just say he's like a Father to me! *Snake chuckles* Sam is the best all around slammer the AWO has. Period. Stables Horse manure. AWO Underrated And finally Peanut Butter and Jelly sandwiches. THE BEST! Speaking of, where the hell is mine? If you need a cook, I got one you can rent out, the best around! You'd have to ask Sam about that, but it looks like your sandwich is on its way, and I need to see a man about a horse. Thanks for your time Mr. Snake. Thank you Norm, and I would like to apologize now, to the 12 fools I am going to make look absolutely ridiculous in the Ultimate Finals, look out, PB&J is on it's way! AWO
