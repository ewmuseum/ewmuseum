CWO match 30: Aragorn vs Daos

PreMatch Comments Thank you. This crowd is really divided between Daos and Aragorn. All managers have been barred from ringside so hopefully we can settle this feud once and for all! Daos has him down, knees on his back...trying to roll him over for the bow and arr...can't get it locked in. Aragorn is too close to the ropes and breaks the hold. Daos reaches around...looking to lock in an abdominal stretch...BIG hiptoss by Aragorn! A clothesline to the back of the head sends Daos to the mat! Incredible speed as Aragorn is right on top of him...STF! Again, Daos refuses to submit and the hold is broken after 1 minute. They're circling..Daos locks him up from behind for a Russian leg sweep. Aragorn grabs the ropes to break the hold. Aragorn off the ropes...sleeperhold! Daos doesn't have it completely locked in and Aragorn slips out the back door. This feud has been heating up for awhile now..they lock up...double eye rake!!! Both men are blinded! Aragorn shoots in with a European uppercut. Blocked! Daos turns it into an armbar. He's really applying the pressure. Aragorn with a roundhouse right. Blocked! Daos grabs the arm and throws it into a hammerlock! Into the corner, and the crowd starts chanting as Daos rams his head into the turnbuckle again and again! Daos goes for a headbutt...Aragorn latches on a nasty claw hold! Great reflexes! It almost looks like he's palming a basketball in there. A clothesline to the back of the head sends Daos to the mat! Incredible speed as Aragorn is right on top of him...STF! Again, Daos refuses to submit and the hold is broken after 1 minute. We'd like to take this opportunity to remind you that for your own safety, as well as the safety of the wrestlers in the ring, please refrain from throwing food and other objects at your computer. Aragorn goes for a headbutt...Daos latches on a nasty claw hold! Great reflexes! It almost looks like he's palming a basketball in there. Daos slaps on a claw hold. Aragorn counters and slaps on a claw of his own! How long can these two keep this up? Somebody had better get some aspirin! Daos executes a belly to back suplex...steps over...into a Boston Crab! Aragorn manages to get his arm over the bottom rope. Lucky! Daos throws Aragorn into the ropes. He goes for a hiptoss...but Aragorn puts on the brakes! Slaps on an abdominal stretch! Daos is complaining about a hair pull and the hold gets broken. Aragorn rakes his eyes across his boot laces! The ref must be as blind as Daos is now to have missed that! Aragorn gets him down on his stomach and locks in the camel clutch! I can hear those vertabrae popping from here! He lets Daos up! He's not finnished punnishing him! Aragorn with a dropkick to the knees...sidestepped by Daos! While there's a break in the action here are some pre-recorded comments from Daos..."Aragorn, when I get done with you I hope the doctors have an old picture of you on hand so they know where to sew all the pieces back on!" Tough words from a tough man! Aragorn with a roundhouse right. Blocked! Daos grabs the arm and throws it into a hammerlock! Into the corner, and the crowd starts chanting as Daos rams his head into the turnbuckle again and again! Aragorn whips Daos into the ropes, setting up for a monkey flip. He telegraphed it WAY too early! Daos gives him a stomp on the head for good measure. Daos gets him on the mat with a well placed right hand...he's going for the claw! Aragorn rolls out of the ring to avoid the hold. He rolls back in before the ref begins to count. The crowd is deafening here tonight! Daos climbs out of the ring and grabs a chair from ringside. Aragorn catches him coming back in and...dropkick! Daos slammed HIMSELF in the face with the chair! Aragorn goes for a headbutt...Daos latches on a nasty claw hold! Great reflexes! It almost looks like he's palming a basketball in there. This feud has been heating up for awhile now..they lock up...double eye rake!!! Both men are blinded! Daos has a hold of him...cobra clutch! Aragorn has presence of mind enough to run backwards into the corner and the hold is broken! Aragorn whips him into the ropes...Daos ducks under the clothesline...from the other side...Daos with a dropkick to the knee! Daos with the go behind...full nelson! But a very slippery Aragorn is able to slide out before any damage could be inflicted. Daos down to the canvass...Aragorn comes off the ropes with a big splash. Daos rolls out of the way! Nobody home! He gets up and slaps on a bow and arrow submission hold! The ref is asking...but Aragorn refuses to submit! Daos down to the canvass...Aragorn comes off the ropes with a big splash. Daos rolls out of the way! Nobody home! He gets up and slaps on a bow and arrow submission hold! The ref is asking...but Aragorn refuses to submit! Daos throws Aragorn into the ropes...runs off the other side...double clothesline!!! Figure four by Aragorn! Extremely punnishing...now Daos is trying to reverse it! He rolls over and the pressure is put back on the legs of Aragorn! Both men feeling the effects! Off the ropes...Daos grabs a hold and goes for a fireman's carry. Aragorn holds on and rolls him into a small package! 1...2...kickout! They lock up...both men looking for an advantage. WOW! Aragorn tosses Daos into the corner! Great show of strength! Daos is trying to be sneaky by untying the top turnbuckle while Aragorn is on the outside setting up a table! Where are all of the referees?!?!? Incidentally...we have a late breaking story on Daos, but it's too hot for Java! You'll have to call the hotline! Aragorn throws Daos into the ropes. He goes for a hiptoss...but Daos puts on the brakes! Slaps on an abdominal stretch! Aragorn is complaining about a hair pull and the hold gets broken. I've never agreed with some of Daos's tactics, but this is nuts! He's pulled off the top turnbuckle pad and is trying to ram...Aragorn turns it around and slams Daos's head into the exposed metal! Daos looks enraged! He rams Aragorn's head in! They're both busted open! Aragorn with the go behind...full nelson! But a very slippery Daos is able to slide out before any damage could be inflicted. Aragorn gets him down on his stomach and locks in the camel clutch! I can hear those vertabrae popping from here! He lets Daos up! He's not finnished punnishing him! This feud has been heating up for awhile now..they lock up...double eye rake!!! Both men are blinded! Aragorn shoots in with a European uppercut. Blocked! Daos turns it into an armbar. He's really applying the pressure. Daos grapevines the leg...going for the head to complete the STF...Aragorn rolls over for a pinning combination! 1...2...kickout! Daos was lucky he didn't get pinned right there! Aragorn is dizzy it looks like he's about to go down!! Daos is going on top of the ropes - OH NO he's going for a flying bodyslam..Aragorn just moves out of the way..SLAM Daos hits the mat. OH my Aragorn is applying his feared Vertabreaker move. That's it...Aragorn is here to stay. I don't know about you, but I was impressed with Aragorn's ability to come back. This wrestler is a force to be reckoned with... Winner: Aragorn
