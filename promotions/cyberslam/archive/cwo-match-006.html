CWO match 6: Volume vs Lasher

undated in the source
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PreMatch Comments Lash this. Volume with a sunset flip! 1...2...Lasher rolls it over! 1...2...Volume turns it into a small package! 1...kickout! Lasher is climbing to the top rope...Volume shakes the ropes! Lasher falls right onto the turnbuckle allowing Volume to climb up...superplex!!! Lasher slaps him! Volume is just standing there laughing. Now he starts to walk away...turns and NAILS Lasher with a flying forearm! Lasher swings and misses! He catches a boot to the gut for his troubles...and Volume slams him with a jumping DDT! Devastating! Volume takes him down with a leg trip. Like a cat to the top rope...Big elbow from way up...but Lasher rolls out of the way!!! Nobody home! Lasher gets up from the mat and Volume is right there with a strong running shoulder block! Levels him! Volume goes for a punch, but Lasher ducks and grabs him! He’s up on his shoulder and Lasher nearly powerslams him through the mat! Volume is thrown into the corner...Lasher is right behind him! A devastating Vader Attack! Rocked his world! Lasher is prone in the center of the ring! Volume is on the top rope...big splash! 1..kickout! Mule kick from Volume...but Lasher cartwheels out of the way. A little showboating going on! Volume is perched on the second turnbuckle waiting for Lasher to turn around...flying clothesline! Right on the money! Volume looks like he's going for an inverted atomic drop here. Wait! Lasher is biting his face! This is a side of Lasher that I've never seen before! Volume with a small package! 1...2...kickout! It's going to take more than that! This match has been going non-stop and...oh! It spills out of the ring again! Lasher whips Volume into the guard rail! Now he tries to ram his head into the metal steps...reversted! Lasher gets his head bounced off the hard steel and both roll back under the bottom rope! Volume is thrown into the corner...Lasher is right behind him! A devastating Vader Attack! Rocked his world! Lasher off the ropes...Volume with a great leapfrog! Lasher puts on the brakes and rocks him with a swinging neckbreaker! Lasher gets him up! The crowd is up on their feet and BAM! Sidewalk slam! We could see a bear hug here...Lasher can't hold him! Volume breaks the hold and slams his head into the turnbuckle. Lasher whips him into the ropes...someone has thrown something into the ring! Lasher ducks to avoid it and gets crushed by Volume off the ropes! Big collision! Lasher rolls out of the ring for a breather...here comes Volume flying between the ropes with a clothesline! No...Lasher saw in coming and ducks out of the way! Volume hits the floor hard and Lasher tosses him back into the ring. Lasher climbs to the top rope...signaling for a double axe-handle. Volume gets a running start and counters with a drop kick! Big mid-air collision! Volume has his legs...big slingshot coming up! Lasher won't budge! He turns it into body scissors and powers out! Great move by Lasher! Volume has Lasher in a headlock. He's giving the sign for the bulldog! Oh...a rake of the eyes and Lasher breaks the hold! Flying fistdrop from the top rope...connects! I can't really see from this angle but I think Lasher is busted open! Lasher whips him into the ropes. He sets up for a backdrop. Volume rebounds and nails him with a flying headbutt! Lasher whips him into the ropes...he gets Volume up on his back. Can't do it! Volume slips out of a potential Samoan Drop. Lucky move! An irish whip and Volume is going for the ride...Lasher off the ropes from the other side...and Volume takes him down with flying headscissors! Great move! Volume backs him into the corner with a series of knife-edge chops...climbs the ropes for what looks like a...jumping DDT! No, wait...Lasher has a firm hold on those ropes and Volume jumps down. No damage. Lasher is climbing to the top rope...Volume shakes the ropes! Lasher falls right onto the turnbuckle allowing Volume to climb up...superplex!!! Lasher gets him up on his shoulders....NO! Volume slips out and catches him with a forearm to the back of the head! Lasher stuns him with a punch...bounces off the ropes...leapfrog by Volume...on his way back...Volume nails him with a Frankensteiner! The crowd goes nuts! Volume backs him into the corner with a series of knife-edge chops...climbs the ropes for what looks like a...jumping DDT! No, wait...Lasher has a firm hold on those ropes and Volume jumps down. No damage. Lasher grabs a handful of tights. Looks like Volume is going for a ride...No! He blocks it! Lasher with a boot to the stomach...powerbomb! Pin attempt! 1...2...kickout! Doesn't get much closer than that! Volume off the ropes...leaps over Lasher with a headlock applied...flying bulldog!!! Very impressive maneuver! Lasher is complaining to the ref about a hair pull. I don't think he's buying it. Volume takes a swing...Lasher blocks it! Legsweep and a BIG splash! Volume is flattened! Lasher gets him up on his shoulders....NO! Volume slips out and catches him with a forearm to the back of the head! Lasher looking pretty cocky...goin' for a figure four leglock, but he's posing for the fans! Volume hooks the tights and rolls him up! 1...2...3!!! Cardinal mistake and Lasher pays for it!!! Volume with the win! Winner: Volume
