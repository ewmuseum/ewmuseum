iPWL: TNT Results

9 August 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Guybrush Threepwood Hi again, Hammerlock. This is Guybrush Threepwood and I have the newly printed results for Tuesday Night Terror. Let's get to it! UNDERCARD LETHAL LOTTERY 1. Regulator & Lieutenant v J Money & Krush Compton Glory must go head to head, with Pantheon members as their partners. Prediction: J Money & Krush. Regulator has not been seen since he fell from the Tower of Doom on Friday. If he does not show then it’s a handicap match, which despite how good Lieutenant is he cannot beat J Money and Krush by himself. Of course, that is if J Money doesn’t take out Krush by himself. Winner: J Money & Krush. A fake Regulator showed up and partnered up with Lieutenant, this did not help him. J Money made the pin against Lieutenant, will there be friction between the two? 2. Kindred & Nancy Boy v Barnibus & Dusk Kindred and Dusk are in the middle of the AofA/POD war. Barnibus hates Canadians for stealing his sheep. Nancy Boy is….*sigh*….never mind. Just fight. Prediction: Barnibus & Dusk. Nancy Boy has done nothing in the iPWL and will get whupped. That leaves Kindred against 2 guys who do not have any major problems against each other. Winner: Barnibus & Dusk. The rookies fought valiantly but could not overcome the experience the vets have. 3. Hour Father & Bulbasaur v Superfly & Disco Fever Hour Father and Bulbasaur are at war with each other. Fly was swerved by Disco and they share a long history. I sense nastiness! Prediction: Superfly & Disco Fever. Hour Father and Bulbasaur teamed up last time and lost. They are teamed up again and now have more hatred for each other as Hour Father broke Bulbasaur’s undefeated streak in the TOD. As for the team of Superfly and Disco Fever. Well, they were former members of Cabal and have now joined different sides in the AofA/POD war. Who knows what will happen between these two? Winner: Hour Father & Bulbasaur. There was more fighting between Superfly and Disco Fever than there was fighting between the two teams. 4. Deathwish & Crazyphuk v Rev & Calupa Wish & Rev go waaaay back. Old feuds are always the best feuds. Prediction: Rev & Calupa. As Deathwish is teamed with another rookie to the iPWL. Rev still has to answer Bulbasaur’s questions about his no showing at Indy Revolution. Calupa may force Rev to answer them! Winner: Deathwish. Grayson removed his partner from the card, yet Deathwish still was able to get the win. 5. Neo & Zoll v Texas Ranger & Aftermath Coincidence? Three Pantheon members in the ring with one AofA member. Again! Prediction: Neo & Zoll. The whole fact that there are 3 POD members in the ring, gives Aftermath no chance of winning this match. Of course, he also disappeared during Friday Fury, so who knows if he will even show up. Winner: Neo & Zoll. When Aftermath doesn’t show, Texas Ranger rolls over for his fellow New Breed. MID-CARD 1. Major Lord v Lunatic In a rewrite match. Prediction: Lunatic. He seems to be the adept flasher here but Major Lord could surprise. Winner: Lunatic. Both showed which is a good thing. While both did flash, Lunatic was way ahead of Major Lord in this one. 2. Calupa v Barnibus In a rankings match. Prediction: Calupa. With every outing since his return, Calupa has gotten better and better. Of course, with Barnibus you never know what will happen. Winner: Calupa. After going 21-0 early on, Barnibus has been hit with a losing streak. Calupa strengthens his position in the World Rankings. 3. Kindred v Zoll The Pantheon rookie versus the Crimson Warrior. Rankings match. Prediction: Kindred. He has taken this federation by storm, winning the Cruiserweight title and on Friday going for the Hardcore title. Winner: Zoll. The veteran takes this one from his fellow stable-mate maybe Kindred was thinking about the Hardcore Title match on Friday. 4. Deathwish v Nancy Boy Will the iPWL rookie stand up against the Sheep Herder? Prediction: Deathwish. He is facing a rookie, plain and simple. Winner: Nancy Boy. This rookie pulls off an upset as he beats the veteran fair and square. HIGH CARD 1. Big Poppa v Bulbasaur To determine the #1 Contender to the World Title Prediction: Bulbasaur. He has been hinting at a new gimmick that might get him more movie roles. If he debuts it here, will it give him good luck against Big Poppa? Winner: Bulbasaur. As Big Poppa does not enter the ring as he has other stuff to deal with. 2. J Money v Regulator To determine the #1 Contender to the North American Title. Prediction: J Money. Again if Regulator is still missing, then who does J Money fight? Winner: J Money. He beats the fake Regulator and will take on Hour Father at Friday Fury. 3. Neo v Superfly v Deathwish In a triple threat to determine the #1 Hardcore Contender. Reranking rules apply. Prediction: Neo. He won against Superfly last time. I think he can do it again against two opponents. Winner: Superfly. He beats his fellow stable-mate, Neo after Neo beat him last week. Deathwish came in last in this match. 4. Miller v Armagggeddon To determine the #1 Contender for the Television Title Prediction: Miller. These two have been quiet for a while. If they show up then I give the edge to Miller who almost beat Rev for the title Friday. Winner: Armagggeddon. Armagggeddon gets his first shot at the Television Title on Friday. Main Event CRUISERWEIGHT TITLE MATCH Texas Ranger v Malibu Bob To determine the new Cruiserweight Champion. Sidx and Oatmeal are off serving suspensions so the #1 contender will face the #4 contender. Prediction: Texas Ranger. He went from the World Title Division to the Cruiserweight Division. That should have been more than enough practice to defeat this rookie. Winner: Texas Ranger. He wins this one when Malibu Bob doesn’t show. Congratulations to Texas Ranger as he wins the Cruiserweight Title. Friday Fury with be full of title matches as it always is. One match that might not happen is the World Title match as Kryptik as been sent to jail for scalping tickets to TNT. Will there be a match or will Bulbasaur win by default? Find out that answer and more when Friday Fury hits us. So, for now This is Guybrush Threepwood signing off. none
