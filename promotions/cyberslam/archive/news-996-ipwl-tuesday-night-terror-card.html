iPWL: Tuesday Night Terror Card

15 August 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Guybrush Threepwood Told you I'll be back. This is Guybrush Threepwood and I have the iPWL TNT card. Let's get to it! UNDERCARD LETHAL LOTTERY 1. J Money & Lieutenant v Superfly & Calupa Compton Glory teamed together? That spells trouble for two matched-up enemies. Prediction: Compton Glory. With Compton Glory together and the fact that Superfly and Calupa do not like each other. This is a no-brainer. 2. Neo & Texas Ranger v Barnibus & Disco Fever Sheep Herders, Architects, and Pantheon. Could get wild. Prediction: Neo & Texas Ranger. Barnibus beat Disco Fever on Friday, will there be past aggression? I think so. Anyway, they’re facing two POD members. 3. Deathwish & Zoll v Kindred & Dusk Interesting pairing that could lead to one of the most explosive matches. Prediction: Deathwish & Zoll. Even though Kindred is on an impressive tear through the fed, the fact that he is teamed up with an AofA member will make this one interesting. I say they don’t get along and that leads to the Sheep Herders/POD team winning it. 4. HourFather & Sidx v Krush & Regulator HourFather finds himself outnumbered and surrounded. Will his partner help win this thing? Prediction: Krush & Regulator. HourFather teams up with a rookie and they have to face two POD members. The question is…How will Krush handle the news of his brother, Impact was the one playing the fake Regulator. RANKING CARD Ranking Matches: 1. Unknown v Maxx Power Two rookies vie to see who will earn a spot in future cards, and who will move closer to the #1 Contendership. Prediction: Unknown. He has been seen while Maxx Power has not. That’s the only reason why I choose him. 2. Dusk v Disco Fever They may be Good Ole Boys…..but they’re on a collision course in the World Rankings. Prediction: Dusk. If neither jobs to the other, then I pick Dusk to win. 3. The Lost One v The Pothead More rookies trying to prove they belong. Prediction: The Lost One. Neither has shown up, which makes this hard to pick but I got to, so I choose The Lost One as The Pot Head is well, The Pot Head. 4. The Plumber v Calupa Calupa has been sliding in the World Rankings. Can he defeat the rookie and start rebounding? Prediction: Calupa. Calupa stops his slide as The Plumber will not put up much of a fight. He hasn’t been seen either so there is a strong possibility of a no-show. MID CARD 1. HourFather v Oatmeal Oatmeal requested a match against the reigning North American Champion. No titles….but there is a cage involved. Prediction: HourFather. HourFather proved he is a worthy NA Champion. He should easily beat Oatmeal, who might have too much on his mind. 2. Bulbasaur & Vinesaur v Major Lord The brash newcomer has called out the Pokemon stars. Now he’s gotta face them handicapped. Prediction: Bulbasaur & Vinesaur. Major Lord proved one thing at Friday Fury and that is he has a big mouth. Bulbasaur & Vinesaur are very capable of shutting it. 3. Zoll v Rev Former Architect leader, faces the current Architect leader. Is there a war brewing here? Prediction: Zoll. Rev hasn’t been the same since skipping Indy Revolution. So, I choose Zoll, if Rev shows up as the Rev of old then I would choose him. HIGH CARD 1. Lieutenant v Regulator v J Money To determine the #1 North American Contender. Prediction: J Money. Regulator seems to be the odd man out in this one as he faces both members of Compton Glory. The real question will be which of Compton Glory will win it. I choose J Money. 2. Bulbasaur v Big Poppa v Barnibus To determine the #1 World Contender. Poppa has left the ring until the fed gets financially squared away. Will Grayson be able to lure him back with chances at the big title? Prediction: Big Poppa. Bulbasaur has been the #1 Contender for a while, desperately trying to get his third World Title Reign. I see him losing that status for a while. Big Poppa hasn’t been seen in a while. If he shows up then I pick him because he is fresh and he is the CWO World Champion. Barnibus has been calling out Big Poppa because he thinks BP is avoiding him and he stole Barnibus' sheep. What will happen? 3. Genocide v Oatmeal v Vinesaur To determine the #1 Cruiserweight Contender. Prediction: Vinesaur. This rookie has quickly risen to #1 Contender status, can he defend it? MAIN EVENT GRUDGE WAR!!! Dusk v Bulbasaur After the events from last week’s title match….these two have decided to settle their own differences in a Chair on a Pole match. Get it, use it. One problem: Kryptik is the guest referee!!! Prediction: Bulbasaur. Dusk has pissed off Kryptik by injuring his hand. That should sway Kryptik towards Bulbasaur’s side. However, Bulbasaur is Kryptik’s major threat at his losing the World title, so who knows? As with all other TNT cards, none of the titles are on the line but their #1 Contenders status is. Will Bulbasaur get a third shot at the three-peat? Will Big Poppa return to the iPWL Arena? Will J Money get another shot at HourFather or will his partner Lieutenant? Will Regulator pull off the upset and beat both members of Compton Glory for that shot? Find out when iPWL presents Tuesday Night Terror. This is Guybrush Threepwood signing off. none
