iMWA: Commish Skull Gone!?

21 January 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Christian There hasn't been a card since the January 12, 2001 edition of Suicide Friday and haven't heard from Commish Skull since he announced the results of that card. People have been complaining about jobbing, padding, and the like. Obviously something has to be done about the situation. Once again, CWZ Commissioner Martini has decided to take action. This Friday, he announced that he had made an agreement with Funky Nassau that Skull would have until midnight to show his face in the iMWA or he would be fired! The time has passed, and we have not heard from him or a new commissioner yet. In the meantime, he along with Overseer and CCWe commissioner Maxx Justice have decided to watch over the iMWA. As for the jobbing and padding situation, Martini and Maxx Justice are going to take care of it, watching closely for violations. The recently promoted CCWe commissioner has been known quite well for his policy against jobbing and padding, and if this is any indicator of how the situation will be handled in the iMWA, this should no longer be a problem. Of course, with the absence of a commissioner, there is always the problem of a lack of cards. Slammers, of course, are not allowed to book matches for titles, but Martini has allowed one slammer, Dyingbreed, to book the following card for Tuesday Terror: Steven Richards vs. Mcbain vs. Joey Inzane Here we have a man who fights for the right to censorship, a foul-mouthed Scot, and a new slammer trying to make a name for himself in the iMWA. Apparently Dyingbreed hasn't been very pleased with Richards' activity, although the RTC frontman has been a bit more vocal as of late. In any case, this should be an interesting three-way match to start the card off. Rookie Three-way Harley Quin vs. Headcrusher vs. Canadian Man All three of these people are fairly new to the iMWA (That's why this is a rookie match, of course!). While Harley Quin has been active, meeting with Maskedfeuder and SniperX, neither of the other two participants have said much. How will this match turn out? Will it reek of awesomeness or of stinktitude? It's all up to these people, the future of the iMWA. . . "Where is my Sledgehammer?" Match Ishboo vs. Lostsoul Recently Lostsoul (of the stable BAD) was found with a sledgehammer. Ishboo (of the Hatebreed) recently lost his trademark sledgehammer. Coincidence? Maybe, maybe not. We'll just have to find out. . . Table Match JJ Rider vs. Anton Both of these men have asked Skull for a title shot, but neither have been answered. Dyingbreed has decided to answer them, except he can't give out title shots. Instead, this will be just another grudge match, except with the stipulation of a table match! MAIN EVENT Steel Cage Match SniperX vs. Cege Cege is yet another newcomer, but he has targeted one of the Hatebreed's top members in SniperX. Apparently one of the members of the Hatebreed interfered in a match between SniperX and the young rookie, causing him to go after the stable. SniperX really doesn't like Cege either, but his stablemate has apparently decided to put both of them inside a steel cage for what could be an exciting main event! Coming soon: an interview with Harley Quin! Stay tuned! none
