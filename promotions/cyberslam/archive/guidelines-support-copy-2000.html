Guidelines (support copy)

the rulebook as the hub's own Support menu served it, captured May 2000
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
1. GENERAL Everyone is allowed to join for FREE. When you join you get placed within an Indy FED (indy feds are denoted with an "i" before the Fed name.) If you would like to transfer, then you must go to our Transfer area and fill out the form. The cost is $15.00 per transfer. It is one time fee. This applies to both indy and major fed transfers. This means that you pay only once and you are then moved to that fed. This is considered a permanent move. You will remain in this fed unless: --> You lose a stipulation match. --> Become inactive. --> Violate any of CyberSlam's Guidelines or Policies to the point where a commissioner request that your member ship be terminated or that you be sent to an indy fed. You are now allowed multiple slammers per computer/trooper. However, you are only allowed two slammers in any one fed per any one computer. This means if the program detects that you are trying to sign up a 3rd slammer to a particular fed, it will not allow it. No slammer is allowed to transfer or become active after the 20th of the month. Any new slammers joining after the 20th will automatically start the first of the following month. Slammers within the Major Feds are eligible to participate in the Ultimate CSlam Event. This event takes place once per month and the winner becomes CyberSlam's Ultimate Champion and a prize is awarded. There is only two ways to get from an indy Fed to a major Fed. One is to transfer in. The other is to win the opening round of the playoffs at the end of the month. For more help on this contact us at [detail withheld] 2. HOW TO SLAM CyberSlam is made up of 8 major federations and 16 indy federations. Each federation has a commissioner. All the action for your Slammer occurs within your slammer's fed only. To begin Slammin go to http://www.cyberslam.com, select the fed from the pop down menu, then click on the Go button, this will take you to that Fed. VERY IMPORTANT: Jobbing Not Allowed Type A: The first type I've seen is Slammers bringing in a Second slammer to the fed they are in and jobbing to himself. For Example Slammer A and B are owned by the same person. Slammer A constantly fights Slammer B in order to gain an advantage. Type B: The second such jobbing, which is quite popular, is stable jobbing, that being the slammers within a stable saving up like 10 or more matches and expending them all on the top stable member to help with the win. Type C: Next and last is the kind of a mix of things, it's the non flasher or 3 flash per week man who just jobs twice a day to their friend, or just half their matches 6 days per week to hide it. These are the harder ones to spot, and in addition to this there are the ones who try and hide the jobbing in an angle. Penalties Due To Jobbing Commissioners will come down very hard on slammers who job. The program has also been altered to negatively impact upon those who job. Make sure you stick to your commissioners guidelines regarding jobbing, a number of slammers have been suspended due to jobbing. 3. ABOUT COMMISSIONERS The Commissioners have absolute authority to suspend any slammer within their own fed, for any reason. If you have a problem contact your commissioner. To contact your commissioner, go to the commissioner's fed page and click on his name. VERY IMPORTANT If you have an angle that incorporates the idea of challenging your commish, then run it by him first. If you don't, then the commish can and very well may suspend your slammer. 4. HOW TO USE THE FLASH BOARD Although each fed has their own guidelines for flashing, it is illegal to comment, insult or speak in an undignified manner about any commissioner or sheriff past OR present from your own or another fed (ie., if your slammer was in the CWO and you insulted a sheriff from the CWZ you would be penalized). This to ensure a degree of respect for those who volunteer their time to CyberSlam. In addition to this, if such an event does in fact occur, the commish of that fed will contact the Head Commish, Funky Nassau and The Slammer. The slammer who committed such an offense may be permanently banned from CyberSlam. Each Fed has a flash board. This is where the role play takes place. Each fed's board has its own rules for flashing. This is the number one reason for slammers getting suspended, for they blow off the guidelines set by the Commissioner. You should know that all flash boards do not allow what would be called "Vulgar" language. There is a censor built into the game that filters out this type of language. Now if you choose to creatively bypass this filter you WILL be suspended. Repeat, YOU WILL BE suspended. Now most of you know this, but a few always seem to try to push the limits. If you have a creative idea for enhancing the role play for all, pass it by your commissioner first. Make sure its OK. Feuds are great!! But if you overstep the boundaries and your commissioner is not filled in, it may be taken the wrong way. Also, the Commish has the experience to bring your ideas to life. Using another slammer's "Real" name or "Information - Age, sex, ...." while flashing is not allowed. Remember this is role play here. This is considered a major violation and violating this will lead to suspension. Trying to guess how many slammers are owned by a particular person and posting your guesses is considered a major violation 5. HOW TO EMAIL Don't email any slammer directly by "slammername@cyberslam.com", it will not get to them. The only exception is me, [detail withheld] and Commissioners. You need to use the MAILBOX feature within CyberSlam to contact other Slammers. If you use the MailBox feature and want your email to be answered, then you better select the option which allows the receiver of your email to respond. Otherwise the email will be sent anonymously with no way of being returned directly. If you receive a CyberSlam email that says Do Not Reply to This Email Directly, then please don't. It means the slammer who sent you the email, sent it anonymously and unless you know their direct email, there is no way to reply directly, you must go to the MAILBOX area to reply. The rules for email are the same as those for the Flash Board. Violating these rules by sending offensive email to other slammers is not tolerated. You will be suspended if you violated these rules. 6. INACTIVITY The key to CyberSlam's success is your participation. The program now automatically scans for Slammers that are inactive and automatically drops them from the game. Be sure to remain active by Slammin on a regular basis. YES, if you go on vacation or if you are ill and can't slam, the program will still drop. You would then need to contact your commissioner for reinstatement. 7. NEWS CyberSlam has a staff of excellent journalist who keep you up to date on what's going on. We have a NewsBoard that will keep you up to date. Just click on the News button to enter. All slammers have access to the board, although only reporters have posting privileges. We now have an Editor-In_Chief, Freedom, who is in charge of our news staff. 8. STABLES A) Stables do have an effect on the outcome of matches. B) Stables are run by stable leaders. Stable leaders are required to keep track of their members, and maintain their stables. if a member does not show up on your list it may because that member has become inactive. Leaders must submit an email address which is made public for anyone to be able to contact the stable leader. C. PLAYOFFS Check out CSlam area for all the details regarding when playoffs start and the rules. The indy feds will now have a slightly different feel to it. In order to make it to a major fed, a slammer must win his federation's belt at the end of the month. Also, indy commishes are responsible for selecting the top flasher from their indy for a promotion to their major fed. The selection must be sent to your league president (ie., the commish of the major fed). 10. INDY FEDS There are currently 16 indy feds. Each Indy fed is actually based on a "Real World" fed. For example the iRCW is based on Richmond Championship Wrestling, an indy fed based in New York. Each indy FED has a commissioner and is part of a CyberSlam league. There are 8 CyberSlam leagues. Each league has a president. For example the president of the CWF is and he is also the commissioner of the CWF fed. Within the CWF are the iCCW and the iRCW. So, in total you have 3 feds (1 major and 2 minors) within the CWF league. Each commissioner reports to the president. The president and the major fed commissioner may or may not be the same character. Currently Papaguido is the commissioner of the CWF fed and the president of the CWF league. The duties of the presidents of each league are to keep tabs on their indy feds, scouting for talent, watch for inactivity of any of the indy commishes and other smaller duties. . There is ONLY THREE ways that a slammer gets from an indy fed to a major fed. A slammer wins the federation championship at the end of the month, the slammer transfers in or a slammer gets promoted in by being the top flasher in their indy. . An indy to major fed relationship is modeled after the NWA to WWF relationship. Like the NWA, the indy fed may have veterans as well as rookies. It has it's own championship, but the champion can not compete for the Ultimate CyberSlam Championship. However, the champion does move to the major fed the following month. . The commissioners of the indy feds are considered on an equal level with all other commissioners. What they say goes. However, the presidents are expected to communicate with their respective indy fed commishes and vice-versa. They must be able to work together to give each other ideas, help with problem slammers, or anything else that may occur. . Each indy fed's have their own set of policies that you as a slammer in that fed must follow, however, these policies are based loosely on the main feds policies, thus promoting a smooth transition if the indy slammer moves up to the parent fed. . 11.
