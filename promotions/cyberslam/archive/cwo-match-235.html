CWO match 235: Mr_mojo vs Bad_karma

PreMatch Comments you're next. Mr_mojo has Bad_karma right where he wants him...a fistfull of tights and they're up on the turnbuckle. Superplex? NO! Bad_karma pokes Mr_mojo in the eyes and down they go! Mr_mojo off the ropes. Bad_karma is waiting and executes a textbook fireman's carry. Mr_mojo is perched on the second turnbuckle waiting for Bad_karma to turn around...flying clothesline! Right on the money! Mr_mojo off the ropes...leaps over Bad_karma with a headlock applied...flying bulldog!!! Very impressive maneuver! Bad_karma with a boot to the stomach...powerbomb! Pin attempt! 1...2...kickout! Doesn't get much closer than that! Mr_mojo goes for a suplex...reversed...reversed again...Bad_karma blocks it and slaps him! Bad_karma off the ropes...Mr_mojo with a great leapfrog! Bad_karma puts on the brakes and rocks him with a swinging neckbreaker! Mr_mojo goes for an armdrag takedown...Bad_karma rocks him with a short clothesline to the back of the head! Mr_mojo undoes some of the tape on his wrist...he chokes out Bad_karma!!! The ref doesn't see it from his angle! Bad_karma whips him into the ropes and goes in for the clothesline...Mr_mojo ducks it...off the other side and a beautiful cross-body block! Only a one count. Bad_karma is running towards Mr_mojo...he doesn't see him! Mr_mojo was setting up for something and just got crushed! But Bad_karma missed the mark and has fallen out of the ring! Those metal ring steps must have hurt! Bad_karma has him up. Loks like he’s going for a piledriver! No...a shoulderbreaker. Wait! Mr_mojo grabs the ropes and takes him down with neck scissors! Mr_mojo off the ropes. Bad_karma is waiting and executes a textbook fireman's carry. Mr_mojo into the corner...Bad_karma is right behind him...and meets a set of knees! Bad_karma goes down, but I think Mr_mojo got hurt with that last move! Bad_karma is prone in the center of the ring! Mr_mojo is on the top rope...big splash! 1..kickout! With a shoulder block, Bad_karma knocks Mr_mojo to the canvass. He’s off the ropes...BIG Legdrop!!! Bad_karma catches Mr_mojo with a legsweep...he bounces of the ropes and gets way up there for a legdrop. Doesn't connect! Mr_mojo rolled out of the way! Mr_mojo has Bad_karma in a headlock. He's giving the sign for the bulldog! Oh...a rake of the eyes and Bad_karma breaks the hold! Bad_karma stuns him with a punch...bounces off the ropes...leapfrog by Mr_mojo...on his way back...Mr_mojo nails him with a Frankensteiner! The crowd goes nuts! Bad_karma climbs to the top rope...signaling for a double axe-handle. Mr_mojo gets a running start and counters with a drop kick! Big mid-air collision! Mr_mojo is thrown into the corner...here comes Bad_karma! Vader Attack! Nobody home as Mr_mojo ducks down leaving nothing but air! Bad_karma has him in a headlock...spins him around...Mr_mojo escapes and backs off. Mr_mojo looks pretty confident. He climbs to the top and...moonsault! Bad_karma catches him and slams him to the mat! Great Power! Bad_karma with a roundhouse right...Mr_mojo catches it and executes an armdrag takedown! Great reflexes! Mr_mojo off the ropes. Bad_karma is waiting and executes a textbook fireman's carry. Ladies and gentlemen...we remind you that the use of flash photography without the written consent...wait. Why would you take a picture of your computer anyway?!?! Mr_mojo takes a swing...Bad_karma blocks it! Legsweep and a BIG splash! Mr_mojo is flattened! Bad_karma gets up from the mat and Mr_mojo is right there with a strong running shoulder block! Levels him! Bad_karma has him in a headlock...spins him around...Mr_mojo escapes and backs off. Ladies and gentlemen...we remind you that the use of flash photography without the written consent...wait. Why would you take a picture of your computer anyway?!?! Bad_karma gets up from the mat and Mr_mojo is right there with a strong running shoulder block! Levels him! Mr_mojo whips Bad_karma into the ropes...Bad_karma reverses it...Bad_karma nails him with a big knee lift! Mr_mojo goes down! Bad_karma with a boot to the stomach...powerbomb! Pin attempt! 1...2...kickout! Doesn't get much closer than that! Bad_karma rolls out of the ring. He saw someone holding a Mr_mojo poster and grabs it! He begins tearing it up! Bad_karma rolls back into the ring and attempts to make Mr_mojo eat a piece! Bad_karma whips him into the corner...here he comes with a big avalanche! NO! Mr_mojo ducks out of the way! I swear the ring moved a good three feet with that impact! Mr_mojo off the ropes...cross body block! Bad_karma catches him! Turns it into a nasty shoulderbreaker! Mr_mojo is perched on the second turnbuckle waiting for Bad_karma to turn around...flying clothesline! Right on the money! Mr_mojo is looking to take a breather in the corner...here comes Bad_karma! BIG avalanche! Mr_mojo crumples to the mat. Bad_karma gets him up! The crowd is up on their feet and BAM! Sidewalk slam! They lock up in the corner...neither is giving up the advantage...oooh same thought! They rake each other's eyes! Both are blindly reaching for the ropes. Bad_karma is running towards Mr_mojo...he doesn't see him! Mr_mojo was setting up for something and just got crushed! But Bad_karma missed the mark and has fallen out of the ring! Those metal ring steps must have hurt! Mr_mojo in control. Throws Bad_karma into the ropes...up for a Frankensteiner! Bad_karma holds on to the top rope and Mr_mojo comes crashing down head first! Mr_mojo goes for a punch, but Bad_karma ducks and grabs him! He’s up on his shoulder and Bad_karma nearly powerslams him through the mat! This match has been going non-stop and...oh! It spills out of the ring again! Bad_karma whips Mr_mojo into the guard rail! Now he tries to ram his head into the metal steps...reversted! Bad_karma gets his head bounced off the hard steel and both roll back under the bottom rope! Bad_karma whips Mr_mojo into the ropes...Mr_mojo grabs onto the top rope. He's not going anywhere. Mr_mojo off the ropes...leaps over Bad_karma with a headlock applied...flying bulldog!!! Very impressive maneuver! We could see a bear hug here...Bad_karma can't hold him! Mr_mojo breaks the hold and slams his head into the turnbuckle. Ladies and gentlemen...we remind you that the use of flash photography without the written consent...wait. Why would you take a picture of your computer anyway?!?! Mr_mojo is looking to take a breather in the corner...here comes Bad_karma! BIG avalanche! Mr_mojo crumples to the mat. One way ticket to Turnbuckle-City as Mr_mojo executes a picture perfect slingshot! Bad_karma whips him into the ropes and goes in for the clothesline...Mr_mojo ducks it...off the other side and a beautiful cross-body block! Only a one count. Forget about it!!! Mr_mojo ties up Bad_karma in the ropes and he's using him like a human punching bag! Oh NO! Bad_karma connects with a solid kick to the groin! OUCH! Mr_mojo off the ropes...he leaps...he misses! Cross-body block attempted, but Bad_karma ducks under it! Mr_mojo off the ropes...cross body block! Bad_karma catches him! Turns it into a nasty shoulderbreaker! Bad_karma with a roundhouse right...Mr_mojo catches it and executes an armdrag takedown! Great reflexes! They lock up in the corner...neither is giving up the advantage...oooh same thought! They rake each other's eyes! Both are blindly reaching for the ropes. Bad_karma has got him up for a Samoan Drop...Mr_mojo grabs his head! Bad_karma takes him down, but in return was devastated by a kind of neckbreaker! Mr_mojo is looking to take a breather in the corner...here comes Bad_karma! BIG avalanche! Mr_mojo crumples to the mat. Mr_mojo takes a swing...Bad_karma blocks it! Legsweep and a BIG splash! Mr_mojo is flattened! Mr_mojo is in trouble! Bad_karma grabs him and is pointing to the crowd! Mr_mojo connects with an elbow to the face! Bad_karma took way too long! Mr_mojo is thrown into the corner...Bad_karma is right behind him! A devastating Vader Attack! Rocked his world! Bad_karma whips Mr_mojo into the ropes...Mr_mojo grabs onto the top rope. He's not going anywhere. Mr_mojo is digging down deep...Bad_karma coming off the ropes...Mr_mojo catches him in the The Bad Mojo!!! This crowd is on their feet as the ref hits the three count! That was one for the record books!!! Winner: Mr_mojo
