iPWL: Friday Fury Results

14 August 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Guybrush Threepwood Hello again, Hammerlock. This is Guybrush Threepwood and I have the results from Friday Fury. So, without further adieu. UNDERCARD LETHAL LOTTERY 1. Dusk & Superfly v Barnibus & Disco Fever Good Ole Boys on opposite sides? Superfly the only POD member present? Barnibus looking for sheep? Prediction: Dusk & Superfly. This should be a close one but I see Dusk & Superfly edging out the team of Barnibus & Disco Fever. Of course, the fact that Superfly, Pod member and Dusk, AofA member will probably not get along makes my prediction worth nothing. Winner: Dusk & Superfly. They totally whip their opponents, who didn’t even put up a fight. 2. Regulator & Sidx v HourFather & Zoll POD outnumber the AofA in another match. How will the rookie figure in? Prediction: HourFather & Zoll. Not sure if Regulator will return. That makes it a rookie coming off of suspension versus another team that is made up of POD/AofA. Winner: Draw. This match didn’t end as hostilities flared. 3. Kindred & J Money v Rev & Neo Again, two Pod members in a match that has one member of the AofA. J Money is representing Compton Glory. Should be interesting. Prediction: Kindred & J Money. The hottest rookie teamed up with a member of Compton Glory. Those two sides don’t have a real strong feud going. The other team of Rev and Neo is again an AofA/POD team, so you’ll never know what will happen. Winner: Kindred & J Money. They barely win this match in another close bout. 4. Krush & Bulbasaur v Calupa & Deathwish A ring full of wild cards, and Web of Deceit on opposite sides of the ring. Prediction: Calupa & Deathwish. Don’t know how this will end up. But what I can say is that a member of the WEB will be victorious. Winner: Krush & Bulbasaur. They win when Deathwish doesn’t send in his results. 5. Texas Ranger & Lieutenant v Oatmeal & Kryptik Kryptik is added due to the sudden disappearance of Aftermath. This match is an odd mix to begin with. Prediction: Texas Ranger & Lieutenant. Oatmeal hasn’t been seen since he came back from suspension, Kryptik will be wrestling in the lottery for the first time. If Oatmeal doesn’t show up look for Texas Ranger and Kryptik to team up and beat Lieutenant. Winner: Oatmeal & Kryptik. The World Champ stole the show in this one as he one-handedly won this match. RANKING CARD Ranking Matches: 1. Barnibus v Disco Fever Struggling for World Title contention. Prediction: Barnibus. This one is just a pure guess as it could go either way. Maybe it will depend on whether Disco Fever is Canadian or not. Winner: Barnibus. In a close one, Barnibus takes it on another “Canadian”. 2. Kindred v Neo POD battle to move up in the North American division Prediction: Kindred. He has been having a great month and I don’t see it stopping now. However, Neo is due for a victory so don’t count him out. Winner: Kindred. This match could mean nothing as Kindred goes up for the Hardcore Title later. 3. Vinesaur v Malibu Bob A rookie against a no-show artist. Prediction: Vinesaur. This is based on the fact that Malibu Bob hasn’t shown once. I don’t see him breaking that streak. Winner: Vinesaur. The no-show artist did what he did best and now he will never show in the iPWL again. 4. Puppi Dog v Lunatic Two relative rookies battle to move upwards. Prediction: Lunatic. Don’t know about this one either, but I pick Lunatic because he won a rewrite match and that’s about it. Winner: Puppi Dog. Lunatic quits because he feels he isn’t getting over, so Puppi dog wins. 5. Scott v Malibu Bob_ Two more newbies trying to get ahead. Prediction: Scott. Different spelling, same person, this one also no-shows. Winner: Neither. They both no-show. MID CARD 1. HourFather v Major Lord In a “READ THE DAMN POLICIES” match. The winner is the person who incorporates the majority of the policies into their roleplay. Prediction: HourFather. This match was made for Major Lord as he never follows the policies. I say he still doesn’t. Winner: HourFather. He layed the smackdown on Major Lord as he used the policies in his role-play like no one could. 2. Geneside & Major Lord v Compton Glory (Lieutenant & J Money) In a Hardcore tag team match, requested by the World’s Most Hardcore Team! Prediction: Compton Glory. A veteran tag team would have trouble with Compton Glory, a team of rookies is almost impossible to get a win. Winner: Compton Glory. They win as both of their opponents no-show. 3. Calupa v Texas Ranger As the other half of the WEB starts to seek his own revenge! Prediction: Calupa. This should be a close one but I just have a feeling Calupa will pull this one off. Winner: Calupa. He gets his own kind of revenge on this Legend Killer. HIGH CARD 1. Kryptik (c) v Bulbasaur As the leader of the WEB tries to become the first three time champ! Prediction: Bulbasaur. I would really like to see someone get crowned three-time champion. Bulbasaur has that chance again! Winner: Kryptik. Kryptik defeats Bulbasaur, yet again. Will Bulbasaur ever get the trifecta. 2. HourFather (c) v J Money In what appears to be a growing feud between HourFather and… the whole damn fed… Titles are on the line. Prediction: J Money. HourFather has pissed off a lot of people in this fed and it wouldn’t surprise me if someone interfered in this match just to teach HourFather a lesson. Winner: HourFather. He solidifies his Champion status as he dispatches J Money, who always seem to run out of luck in this division. 3. Lieutenant (c) v Superfly v Kindred A triple threat cage match. Prediction: Lieutenant. He has held onto this belt for a while and even though I have a feeling it will change tonight, I still see it around Lieutenant’s waist. Winner: Kindred. He wins the belt on his first try, after having the Cruiserweight title a week before. It seems gold will always be around this slammer’s waist. 4. Rev v Armagggeddon The newcomer earned his shot at the longest reigning champ in the iPWL. Prediction: Rev. He has defended the title amongst all challenges and still has kept it. I see Armagggeddon putting up a fight but will eventually be defeated. Winner: Rev. He has had the longest title reign in iPWL history and it seems like it will never end. 5. Texas Ranger v Sidx Fresh off of suspension, Sidx finally gets a title shot. Prediction: Texas Ranger. The veteran is just too much for this rook. Winner: Texas Ranger. But he has to give it up to Sidx as he is ruled to good for the Cruiserweight title. I'll be back later as the Tuesday Night Terror card is announced. This is Guybrush Threepwood signing off. none
