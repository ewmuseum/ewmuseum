PreMatch Comments This is for bringing in your playmates Ammodawg takes him down with a leg trip. Like a cat to the top rope...Big elbow from way up...but Alisterfiend rolls out of the way!!! Nobody home! Alisterfiend has got him up for the big suplex...wait! Ammodawg shifted his weight! They're both heading over the top rope! Through the timekeeper's table!!! I can't tell who took more damage! They both crawl back to the ring. Alisterfiend stuns him with a punch...bounces off the ropes...leapfrog by Ammodawg...on his way back...Ammodawg nails him with a Frankensteiner! The crowd goes nuts! Flying fistdrop from the top rope...connects! I can't really see from this angle but I think Alisterfiend is busted open! Ammodawg whips Alisterfiend into the ropes...Alisterfiend reverses it...Alisterfiend nails him with a big knee lift! Ammodawg goes down! Ammodawg goes for a punch, but Alisterfiend ducks and grabs him! He’s up on his shoulder and Alisterfiend nearly powerslams him through the mat! Ammodawg goes for a crucifix! Will he get the pin? NO! Alisterfiend brings him down with a Samoan Drop! Alisterfiend is prone in the center of the ring! Ammodawg is on the top rope...big splash! 1..kickout! Ammodawg gets slammed in the middle of the ring. Here comes Alisterfiend off the ropes, a big splash...nobody home! Good timing by Ammodawg! They lock up...the ref sees a hair pull and breaks it up. Alisterfiend lifts him up for an atomic drop! Ammodawg levels him with a short clothesline! Ammodawg is thrown into the corner...here comes Alisterfiend! Vader Attack! Nobody home as Ammodawg ducks down leaving nothing but air! Ammodawg with a roundhouse kick. Alisterfiend traps the leg...Ammodawg tries for the enziguri kick...missed! One way ticket to Turnbuckle-City as Ammodawg executes a picture perfect slingshot! Alisterfiend is trying to get him up...Ammodawg blocks it and is trying to lift him up! No!!! Both go sailing over the top rope! Slowly they climb back into the ring. Ammodawg slaps on a headlock. Alisterfiend picks him up...bell to belly suplex! Alisterfiend stuns him with a punch...bounces off the ropes...leapfrog by Ammodawg...on his way back...Ammodawg nails him with a Frankensteiner! The crowd goes nuts! One way ticket to Turnbuckle-City as Ammodawg executes a picture perfect slingshot! Alisterfiend is climbing to the top rope...Ammodawg shakes the ropes! Alisterfiend falls right onto the turnbuckle allowing Ammodawg to climb up...superplex!!! Ammodawg goes for a suplex...reversed...reversed again...Alisterfiend blocks it and slaps him! Ammodawg is thrown into the corner...Alisterfiend is right behind him! A devastating Vader Attack! Rocked his world! Alisterfiend slaps him! Ammodawg is just standing there laughing. Now he starts to walk away...turns and NAILS Alisterfiend with a flying forearm! Ammodawg has got a one way ticket to Suplex City...and Alisterfiend is supplying the ticket! Way up and just as long down! What power! Ammodawg has his legs...big slingshot coming up! Alisterfiend won't budge! He turns it into body scissors and powers out! Great move by Alisterfiend! Ammodawg with a roundhouse kick. Alisterfiend traps the leg...Ammodawg tries for the enziguri kick...missed! Alisterfiend going for an atomic drop! Ammodawg grabs him in a headlock and they both go down! Forget about it!!! Ammodawg ties up Alisterfiend in the ropes and he's using him like a human punching bag! Oh NO! Alisterfiend connects with a solid kick to the groin! OUCH! One way ticket to Turnbuckle-City as Ammodawg executes a picture perfect slingshot! Ammodawg rebounds out of the corner with a flying forearm...but Alisterfiend is waiting with a stiff boot to the face. Ammodawg is perched on the second turnbuckle waiting for Alisterfiend to turn around...flying clothesline! Right on the money! Mule kick from Ammodawg...but Alisterfiend cartwheels out of the way. A little showboating going on! Alisterfiend is complaining to the ref about a hair pull. I don't think he's buying it. Alisterfiend whips him into the ropes. He sets up for a backdrop. Ammodawg rebounds and nails him with a flying headbutt! Ammodawg has Alisterfiend right where he wants him...a fistfull of tights and they're up on the turnbuckle. Superplex? NO! Alisterfiend pokes Ammodawg in the eyes and down they go! Definite bad blood between these two! Alisterfiend is complaining to he ref about a hair pull and...NO! Ammodawg cheap shots him with a fist to the back of the head! Why doesn't the referee disqualify him!?!? Alisterfiend has got him up for a Samoan Drop...Ammodawg grabs his head! Alisterfiend takes him down, but in return was devastated by a kind of neckbreaker! Alisterfiend gets him up! The crowd is up on their feet and BAM! Sidewalk slam! Alisterfiend whips him into the ropes...he gets Ammodawg up on his back. Can't do it! Ammodawg slips out of a potential Samoan Drop. Lucky move! Ammodawg is walking on the top rope! Agile!!! But here comes Alisterfiend with a dropkick! Ammodawg goes sailing through a table and Alisterfiend is tied up in the ropes! Ammodawg has Alisterfiend right where he wants him...a fistfull of tights and they're up on the turnbuckle. Superplex? NO! Alisterfiend pokes Ammodawg in the eyes and down they go! Ammodawg slaps on a headlock. Alisterfiend picks him up...bell to belly suplex! Ammodawg takes a swing...Alisterfiend blocks it! Legsweep and a BIG splash! Ammodawg is flattened! Alisterfiend with the headbutt...he stunned himself! Alisterfiend falls down! Ammodawg to the top rope...BIG legdrop from way up there! 1...2...NO! Close! Alisterfiend catches Ammodawg with a legsweep...he bounces of the ropes and gets way up there for a legdrop. Doesn't connect! Ammodawg rolled out of the way! Ammodawg goes for a crucifix! Will he get the pin? NO! Alisterfiend brings him down with a Samoan Drop! Alisterfiend grabs a handful of tights. Looks like Ammodawg is going for a ride...No! He blocks it! Alisterfiend going for the belly to belly...couldn't do it! Ammodawg has fallen on top of him! Alisterfiend has him in a headlock...spins him around...Ammodawg escapes and backs off. Bear Hug!!! Ammodawg is in alot of pain right now!!! Ammodawg off the ropes...leaps over Alisterfiend with a headlock applied...flying bulldog!!! Very impressive maneuver! Alisterfiend climbs to the top rope...signaling for a double axe-handle. Ammodawg gets a running start and counters with a drop kick! Big mid-air collision! Ammodawg whips Alisterfiend into the ropes...Alisterfiend reverses it...Alisterfiend nails him with a big knee lift! Ammodawg goes down! Ammodawg off the ropes...cross body block! Alisterfiend catches him! Turns it into a nasty shoulderbreaker! Ammodawg whips Alisterfiend into the ropes...Alisterfiend reverses it...Alisterfiend nails him with a big knee lift! Ammodawg goes down! Alisterfiend gets him up for the sidewalk sla...no! Ammodawg slips out the back door. Bear Hug!!! Ammodawg is in alot of pain right now!!! Ammodawg looks like he's going for an inverted atomic drop here. Wait! Alisterfiend is biting his face! This is a side of Alisterfiend that I've never seen before! Alisterfiend rolls out of the ring. He saw someone holding a Ammodawg poster and grabs it! He begins tearing it up! Alisterfiend rolls back into the ring and attempts to make Ammodawg eat a piece! Definite bad blood between these two! Alisterfiend is complaining to he ref about a hair pull and...NO! Ammodawg cheap shots him with a fist to the back of the head! Why doesn't the referee disqualify him!?!? Alisterfiend whips him into the ropes and goes in for the clothesline...Ammodawg ducks it...off the other side and a beautiful cross-body block! Only a one count. Ammodawg climbs to the top rope! But Alisterfiend is right there and tosses him clear across the ring! Ammodawg with a roundhouse kick. Alisterfiend traps the leg...Ammodawg tries for the enziguri kick...missed! Alisterfiend sets up Ammodawg on the top rope for a superplex! Ammodawg rams him with an elbow! Now from the top rope, Ammodawg with a moonsault!!! But he's not through with Alisterfiend yet... Alisterfiend just caught Ammodawg's Ammo Bomb move. Alisterfiend is not giving up. The ref checking to see if the arms are limp. They are. It's all over Ammodawg win's the match. OH...Ammodawg won't release Alisterfiend. I'm afraid here..someone should get in there, this wrestler is going to need some medical attention unless...here comes Toughguy and his bodyguards, Wham! They just caught Ammodawg by surprise, now they're kicking ans stomping. Oh boy it's going to be some time before Ammodawg tries that again! Winner: Ammodawg
