Doc Doc: Welcome to Biography....Today we look at the early days of Slamming Superstar Daos pronounced "Day - Os"... In his rookie years Daos found hmself not quite suited for the regiment that is CSlam.... Commissioner Lars Magnuson Daos was commomnly seen sneaking Jelly-doughnuts into the CWO lockerroom and wearing huge neon-green Cowboy hats. Although he foud it quite humorous, Lars did not... Lars telling Daos about Steers and ...... A local Superstar Ebola took the fledgling star under his wing. He tried to train the CWO recruit in the ways of the Slam.... Ebola and the struggling Daos Although Daos mic skills were increasing his constant antics with electrified dog collars and making half the Platoon of the CWO "Disciples" did not please Lars... Yet another scolding from Lars Daos carried on desperate for an angle and friends to help him along the way. He met 2 young superstars in the Form of Vigilante and Aragon. Together they formed Ex-Com... Ebola helps Daos in another of Lars' marahon Roleplay Ebola even tried to help the CWO budding superstar get in touch with his "kinder" "Gentler" side... Ebola: "Like a doily, only Bigger" Although he was progressing, Daos kept up his antics. He rode hippotami and constantly claimed he was the Commish when Lars would step away. Lars was not pleased But the confidence of the Young Daos was soaring. Unfortunately the lack of jelly doughnuts was deteriorating the fragile mind of the would-be Legend. Lars losing his touch with the King Daos. Soon Lars knew that he could hold the Future HOFer back no more...He released the somwhat deranged superstar on the rest of CSlam.... "I am....in a world....Of sh@#.... Doc: So we come to the end of Chapter One....Daos the CWO years....Join us again we we follow the footsteps of Daos as he joins the Blackballs and makes the CEW his personal playpen....Later. none
