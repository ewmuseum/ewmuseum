Eyeless Hello Cyberslam!!! This is your new iXMW reporter, Eyeless!! Reporting for the best up-n-coming indy fed in CSlam. The iXMW has a new commissioner, in Hayabusa_X. He looks promising so far, already dishing out a suspension since he took over earlier this week. The "Original Cowboy From Hell" looked over the fed, determining the first card. He finally ha smade it official, and here is the first iXMW card under Hayabusa_X. Cowboys From Hell Live from Dallas, Texas!! iXMW ????? Title Match Crash Nasty v.s. Papa Ghede This is for the Rewrite Title, 100% Rewrite. Will take place inside the Alamodome. But not the arena, the historical landmark!! The winner gets to rename the title whatever they want. iXMW Ironman Title Match: Papa Ghede v.s. Morrigan This match is a hockey stick match. Both men start out with hockey sticks as weapons. Slam your opponent 3 TIMES iXMW Brass Knuckles Title Match: Crash Nasty v.s. Dirk Nasty This match is just like it says, a Brass Knuckles On A Pole Match. The first one to get the Brass Knuckles off a pole in a corner, and beat your opponent senseless until he can`t answer the ten count. Slam your opponent 3 TIMES. iXMW Bloodcore Title Match Talon v.s. Dragon This match will be inside a larger Lions Den, with weapons. The only way to win is to make your opponent submit. Slam Opponent 3 TIMES iXMW Independent Title Match Bush v.s. The Truth This is for the under 50% title. This match will take place in horse stables!! Slam Opponent 3 TIMES iXMW Hardcore Tag Team Title Triple Threat Match The Nasty Bros. (Crash & Dirk Nasty) v.s. The Chosen (Dragon & Talon) v.s. The Carney Crew (Papa Ghede & Showbiz) This is for the iXMW Hardcore Tag Team Titles. It will be a free for all, inside of a Triple Tier Cage. The first TEAM to escape first with the Tag Titles inhand, and through the door will be declared the winners and champions. Slam each member of the opposing team ONCE. iXMW Hell In A Cell I Quit Match Pharoah v.s. Axe The match they wanted, here infront of them. It is as stated above. Let`s make it a REWRITE MATCH. 100% Rewrite iXMW Contract On The Line EVERYONE ELSE The people not stated above are in match. Just slam any TWO PEOPLE once and post it on the board with a brief prematch. That also means that your promise that you will be active and flash. All Rewrites are due before 11:59pm Monday. I want to see 500% from all of you since this is my first card. Show me what you all got. Minimum 2 RP posts are required for every NON-REWRITE Match. Don`t finish the matches in your RP!! I hate that!! Slam all matches before 11:59pm Monday Have Fun Guys!! Good Luck!! The Original Cowboy From Hell, Hayabusa X To be a new commish, the card is solid with good and unusual stips. Thats it for now, as later I will be have an interview with the commish, the card results, and more!!! Now I`ll Go Lookin For My Eyes.. Eyeless none
