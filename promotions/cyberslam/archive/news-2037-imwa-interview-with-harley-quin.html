iMWA: Interview with Harley Quin

21 January 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Christian Greetings to all our fans in CSlam! Today I am here with yet another new iMWA slammer, Harley Quin! Christian: So, if I'm not mistaken, you're a female clown, right? Harley Quin: Yeah that's Me! Christian: What brings you here, to the iMWA? Harley: Well I saw this line outside and I waited and then some guy said you must be the new girl, and I said ummmmmm...no, and then I was hit on the head and woke up in a lockerroom,and here we are. Christian: Interesting. . . So what do you like most about your experience here? Harley: Stupid people....stupid people abound in iMWA. They're easy to entertain. Christian: Speaking of stupid people, is there anyone whom you don't like? Harley: Ummmm I'm a clown I like everyone...... Christian: Damn, I reek of stupidness now, don't I? Harley: It's ok we all have bad days! You could be like Lostsoul. He's about one step from picking fleas off himself. In the circus we had this monkey named Jojo, but he died. Well, he didn't die; he was put asleep for biting children! Sorta like Lostsoul should be.... Harley: Be right back.... Christian: Uh, OK. . . ::Harley leaves and comes back juggling knives as Christian looks on quietly.:: Harley: What? You don't like juggling? Christian: Oh, I love juggling! Juggling has quite the coolnosity factor! Harley: How about knife throwing? I've only missed twice today.... Christian: Um, what are you going to be throwing knives at? Harley: Well, usually I'd pick some ugly kid, but you'll do! It'll be fun! Just don't move! Christian: Um, sure! Harley: You stand against the wall,and feel free to ask questions. Just don't move, and you gotta sign a waiver.... ::Harley hands Christian a pen and a piece of paper with words written in crayon. Christian reads the paper, signs, and stands against the wall.:: Christian: Anyway, you have established quite an impressive record in your short time here. Recently, however, the issue of jobbing and padding has been a major concern, with a few of the slammers complaining to Martini about this issue. Finally, in the absence of Skull, the CWZ commissioner took action. How do you feel about jobbing and padding? Do you think Martini is handling this situation properly? Harley: It's just like in the in the cicrus when the overseer was stepped in by the Jumbo the Elephant. The big top was a mess! We had a vendor peeing in the lemonade, but then he came back and everything was good. People need someone to watch over them. Harley: Ready? Christian: Yup. ::Harley begins throwing the knives, barely missing until she only has one left. She then does a back flip and throws the last one, striking the wall inches from Christian's head. Christian wipes the sweat off his brow and begins to applaud.:: Harley: Thank you. Thank you. Christian: That performance rocked! Now, more than ever. . . CLOWNS RULE! High five! Harley:We do, but some people don't like clowns....it's sad.... Christian: Some people just don't appreciate good entertainment when they see it. Harley: True. I blame Steven King. He wrote that book It and ever since people have hated clowns. Someone should run him over or somethin'. Christian: Well, SniperX and Maskedfeuder seem to enjoy your company. Harley: True, they're nice even though MF is Jobber Fodder! BWHAHAHAHA! That is still funny! Christian: So you were recently released from a mental institution, correct? Harley: Yeah, it's kinda sad you live in a place for two years. You take your meds and they throw you out. They say I'm not as violent as I was, whatever that means. Christian: There are quite a few stables in the iMWA, including BAD, the Hatebreed, and the Bombers. What do you think of stables? Harley: They smell, and they are no fun to clean. Christian: Quite true. If you did have to gather a band of slammers to have fun and interfere in matches, whom would you choose to join you? Harley: Rumor in CWZ, Hapytheclown in CEW, Mr.Arse, and Psyduck in CWZ. Christian: Do you have any particular reasons for your choices? Harley: Well, Hapy rulez 'cause he's a clown too. Christian: But of course. . . Harley: Rumor needs to get away from Mr.Twinkie. Have you heard what he says? Christian: Not directly, but Rumor tells me some of the things that he says. Weird stuff. Harley: Well, I've heard him. He's mean.... Harley: Mr. Arse has a funny name, and Psyduck is a Pokémon. Enough said. Christian: At Tuesday Terror you are scheduled to face Headcrusher and Canadian Man, neither of which have had much of a presence outside of the ring. What kind of approach are you going to take to this matchup? Harley: I'm not sure.... I think it's going to suck with neither of them showing up at the arena, but I'll come up with something.... Christian: What are your goals for the future? Harley: Well, more fun, to get something to eat, that's about it, and watch the Greatest Show on Earth! I love that movie! Christian: Do you have anything else you would like to share with us? Harley: Hmmmmmmmmmm let me see.....Loutsoul sucks, cleaning stables suck,Jobberfodder,Going to eat,....Nope that's it. Christian: Thank you. Harley: No problem. This concludes our interview with Harley Quin! Stay tuned for more from the fed that most totally reeks of awesomeness, the iMWA! none
