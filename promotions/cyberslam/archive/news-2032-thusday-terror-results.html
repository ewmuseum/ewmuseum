Great One Here are the results for an awesome edition of Thursday Terror. Thursday Terror Results Live...from the Altel Arena in Little Rock The iQFWC Presents Thursday Terror -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- US Title Match Mad_hatter (c) vs Deth Deth earned this shot on Mortal Monday, now let's see if he can step up to the challenge. Deth defeats the Mad_hatter 6-2 and becomes the new iQFWC US Champion. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Hell in a Cell...Contenders Final Lordy vs Hex After the battle royal, only 2 remained. Let's see who shall be left standing now. As expected it was a tough match but in the end Hex took home the contenders Title on points...15-13. Good job to both Slammers. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- World Tag Team Title Match Last Rites (Cripler/Deth) vs Black Magic (Voodoo_death/Voodoo_lord) Last Rites won the Titles by defeating the team of Psychotic Tendancies but Black Magic has been chomping at the bit to get the gold. Last Rites ward off the Black Magic and retain the Tag Team Titles. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Blood and Guts Title Match Wendell vs Mikey_ripper vs Randy Well, the masses have spoken and you want a rewrite. Let's see what the talent level is. I will be emailing 5 Slammers at random to judge the rewrites and email me with their votes. No Slams, just writing ability. Let's see you shine. Unfortunately Wendell had some travel issues and did not make the card, but Mikey_ripper and Randy put up an excellent battle, and when all is said and done and the votes are tallied, the new iQFWC Blood and Guts Champion is none other than Mikey_ripper. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- World Title #1 Contenders Match The Hangman vs The Blueflame Two individuals with good ability, now let's see them prove it to the rest of us. Winner gets No Warning at Mortal Monday. It came down to points on this one and Theblueflame takes it home 6-5 and gets his shot at No_warning at Mortal Monday. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Contenders Title #1 Shot Cactus vs Mr_mist vs Conquistador Can't call it a #1 Contender for the Contenders belt, just doesn't sound right. Anyway, let's see who will get the shot at Lordy or Hex. Cactus takes home the win and will face Hex at Mortal Monday -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Tag Title Rankings Match Psychotic Tendancies (Sandmanman/No Warning)vs The Brood (Big Dave/Icekwik) Someone has to be in line for the Tag Titles, lets see if it will be one of these two teams, or if Drunken Hangover really has a legitimate claim as they say they do. Psychotic Tendencies takes the win 5-3 over The Brood. WOW! My predictions sucked! Cominsg very soon, will be the interview with the self proclaimed "Legend" of C-Slam, Mad Hatter. I will be asking him a bunch of questions about his carrer as well as what he thinks oif the roster. Be sure to check in for it. But unil then, See ya next time. none
