CWO match 223: Volume vs Alisterfiend

undated in the source

PreMatch Comments let's pick up where we left off Alisterfiend with a stiff chop to the chest...goes for a second and Volume rocks him with an enziguri kick to the head! With a shoulder block, Alisterfiend knocks Volume to the canvass. He’s off the ropes...BIG Legdrop!!! Alisterfiend lifts him up for an atomic drop! Volume levels him with a short clothesline! Volume undoes some of the tape on his wrist...he chokes out Alisterfiend!!! The ref doesn't see it from his angle! Volume whips him into the ropes...looking for a backdrop...Alisterfiend sees it coming. DDT! Volume’s head bounced off the mat like a superball! Alisterfiend is prone in the center of the ring! Volume is on the top rope...big splash! 1..kickout! Volume is looking to take a breather in the corner...here comes Alisterfiend! BIG avalanche! Volume crumples to the mat. Alisterfiend wraps his fingers around his throat...way up!!! And crashing down to the canvass! What a choke slam! Alisterfiend catches Volume with a legsweep...he bounces of the ropes and gets way up there for a legdrop. Doesn't connect! Volume rolled out of the way! Impressive displays from both men at this point. Volume with the speed advantage while...Alisterfiend rocks him in the corner with an avalanche! No place to go!!! Alisterfiend going for the belly to belly...couldn't do it! Volume has fallen on top of him! Volume goes for a punch, but Alisterfiend ducks and grabs him! He’s up on his shoulder and Alisterfiend nearly powerslams him through the mat! Alisterfiend gets up from the mat and Volume is right there with a strong running shoulder block! Levels him! Mule kick from Volume...but Alisterfiend cartwheels out of the way. A little showboating going on! Volume is leaving! He's had enough and is heading back to the dressing room. The ref's count is up to six and here comes Alisterfiend! He dropkicks him from behind and now he's dragging Volume back to the ring...by the ear!!! Alisterfiend slaps on a sleeper...but Volume drops down with a jawbreaker! He springs to the top rope, BIG double axe-handle to the head! From the second turnbuckle, Volume with the flying fist drop...Alisterfiend gets the leg up! Volume gets a jaw-full of boot! Alisterfiend stuns him with a punch...bounces off the ropes...leapfrog by Volume...on his way back...Volume nails him with a Frankensteiner! The crowd goes nuts! Alisterfiend has him in a headlock...spins him around...Volume escapes and backs off. Volume is in trouble! Alisterfiend grabs him and is pointing to the crowd! Volume connects with an elbow to the face! Alisterfiend took way too long! With a shoulder block, Alisterfiend knocks Volume to the canvass. He’s off the ropes...BIG Legdrop!!! One way ticket to Turnbuckle-City as Volume executes a picture perfect slingshot! Volume goes for a suplex...reversed...reversed again...Alisterfiend blocks it and slaps him! Volume is perched on the second turnbuckle waiting for Alisterfiend to turn around...flying clothesline! Right on the money! Volume undoes some of the tape on his wrist...he chokes out Alisterfiend!!! The ref doesn't see it from his angle! Alisterfiend with the headbutt...he stunned himself! Alisterfiend falls down! Volume to the top rope...BIG legdrop from way up there! 1...2...NO! Close! Volume is thrown into the corner...Alisterfiend is right behind him! A devastating Vader Attack! Rocked his world! Alisterfiend whips him into the ropes. He sets up for a backdrop. Volume rebounds and nails him with a flying headbutt! Alisterfiend is running towards Volume...he doesn't see him! Volume was setting up for something and just got crushed! But Alisterfiend missed the mark and has fallen out of the ring! Those metal ring steps must have hurt! Alisterfiend lifts him up for an atomic drop! Volume levels him with a short clothesline! Alisterfiend is trying to get him up...Volume blocks it and is trying to lift him up! No!!! Both go sailing over the top rope! Slowly they climb back into the ring. Alisterfiend grabs a handful of tights. Looks like Volume is going for a ride...No! He blocks it! Alisterfiend lifts him up for an atomic drop! Volume levels him with a short clothesline! Volume whips Alisterfiend into the ropes...he's waiting in the middle of the ring with a backdrop... Alisterfiend saw it coming! He grabs him and drives him into the mat with an awesome piledriver! Alisterfiend gets him up for the sidewalk sla...no! Volume slips out the back door. Volume slaps on a headlock. Alisterfiend picks him up...bell to belly suplex! Alisterfiend slaps on a headlock. Volume can't seem to power out...he lifts him into a side suplex! Up to the top rope and...BAM! Big elbow from fifteen feet up! They lock up...the ref sees a hair pull and breaks it up. Volume looks pretty confident. He climbs to the top and...moonsault! Alisterfiend catches him and slams him to the mat! Great Power! Forget about it!!! Volume ties up Alisterfiend in the ropes and he's using him like a human punching bag! Oh NO! Alisterfiend connects with a solid kick to the groin! OUCH! Alisterfiend gets him up! The crowd is up on their feet and BAM! Sidewalk slam! What does he have there? A roll of coins! Volume clocks him in the jaw and about twenty bucks in change flies all over the ring! Volume is in trouble! Alisterfiend grabs him and is pointing to the crowd! Volume connects with an elbow to the face! Alisterfiend took way too long! Volume takes a swing...Alisterfiend blocks it! Legsweep and a BIG splash! Volume is flattened! Volume off the top rope. He took way too long to get up there...axe-handle attempted and Alisterfiend delivers a big fist to the midsection on his way down! Alisterfiend has got him up for the big suplex...wait! Volume shifted his weight! They're both heading over the top rope! Through the timekeeper's table!!! I can't tell who took more damage! They both crawl back to the ring. Alisterfiend goes for a fireman's carry...Volume hangs on and turns it into a pinning combination! Only a one count. Alisterfiend swings and misses! He catches a boot to the gut for his troubles...and Volume slams him with a jumping DDT! Devastating! Bear Hug!!! Volume is in alot of pain right now!!! One way ticket to Turnbuckle-City as Volume executes a picture perfect slingshot! Say goodbye to that spine! Alisterfiend rocks Volume with a nasty atomic drop! Alisterfiend stuns him with a punch...bounces off the ropes...leapfrog by Volume...on his way back...Volume nails him with a Frankensteiner! The crowd goes nuts! Volume is dizzy it looks like he's about to go down!! Alisterfiend is going on top of the ropes - OH NO he's going for a flying bodyslam..Volume just moves out of the way..SLAM Alisterfiend hits the mat. OH my Volume is applying his feared Sub Woofer Drop move. That's it...Volume is here to stay. I don't know about you, but I was impressed with Volume's ability to come back. This wrestler is a force to be reckoned with... Winner: Volume
