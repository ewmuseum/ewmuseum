Newbies: the story of CyberSlam

the hub's own Newbies page, captured April 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
It all started April 1997. Then there were a few brave soldiers who were willing to take on the establishment. They were willing to risk their careers and reputations to follow a dream of an online wrestling world where wrestlers could battle each other in a virtual ring. So Chaos Bill, CatWoman, Hardcore, Tough Guy, Studd, Eliminator, DynamiteKid, TheJester, Marksman, DigiSlam, Dog, TheBat and Thawk pulled their life savings together, kissed the establishment good bye and started CyberSlam. Those early days were rough, there was only one federation, The CWF. The stadium seats were empty but the enthusiasm ran high. Unfortunately, these brave soldiers ran into problems and soon feuds began to develop. Tough Guy attacked CatWoman and dissension began to creep in. It was decided that CyberSlam needed a leader to take control and take it to the next level. And like a gift from God, The Slammer walked through the stadium doors and became President. The Slammer quickly brought order to CyberSlam, he banned Tough Guy from CyberSlam and sanctioned Hardcore. He decided that online wrestlers be called Slammers and online wrestling, Slammin. That once a month, a special event would be held. it would be the Ultimate CyberSlam Event and it would be named CSlam. That the winner of CSlam would be CyberSlam's Official Champion and would take home a prize. And a legend was born out of the first CSlam event, Chaos Bill. He devastated the competition and set the standard for Slammin? In the months that followed Chaos Bill tried to repeat but was unsuccessful. Instead new champions emerged TheBat, CometMeeker, Disease, Smash Mouth and our reigning champion StuMan. The Slammer, on a mission to make CyberSlam the best thing that ever happened to the Net, created a new federation, CCW (CyberSlam Championship Wrestling). These two federations were joined by a third hardcore federation, CEW. Now the stage was set for CyberSlam 3.0, the brainchild of The Slammer. A virtual world were Slammin could take place without any restrictions, no log in, no hassle, just pure adrenaline pushing fun! It took a few months to put together but CyberSlam 3.0 debuted October 1, 1997 and with it came the introduction of a fourth federation, CBF (CyberSlam Blast federation). CyberSlam has grown since those early days, currently we have 8 major federations and 16 indy federations. There are two indy feds associated with each major fed making those three feds a league. Each league is run by a league president who also happens to be the commissioner of the major federation within that league. For example the iNCW, iIPW and CCW are in the CCW league. The CCW league president is Lars, who also happens to be CCW commissioner. CyberSlam is run by the President, Funky Nassau. Funky began his career as the CWF commissioner and was promoted to president after an outstanding career as commissioner. The Slammer then moved into a front office position as CEO. CyberSlam is also supported by the ever reliable Lars as VP in charge of Slammers, the controversial StuMan as VP in charge of Development, the creative Papa as VP in charge of Promotions, the consistent Freedom as Editor-In-Chief, the overworked Slam Ma'am as Chairperson and the under appreciated Ironhead as assistant to the CEO.
