Cslam: Got News?? Maybe I'll cover it

23 January 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Extreme:Your source for unbiased news In the darkness of a underground parking garage the end of a lit cigarette glows bright red. Zooming in the camera picks up the shadowy outline of a man. Exhaling the smoke the man begins to speak. "Cslam, the first time I graced your presence I was called to controversial for Hammerlock. So the higher ups let me go and I was forever to be remember as a man who pushed the envelope with his reports." Taking another long drag of his Cigarette the man pauses for a second. "But like all things that are ahead of their time, I was brought back when it was deemed that Hammerlock and Cslam could handle me. Well folks that time is now, I'm back and boy am I ****** off. I'm ****** that the higher ups just now decided to bring me back when all this time I could have been making waves with my reports!! But I guess things come to those who wait, because now is my time." Throwing his cigarette away the man steps from the shadows and into the light. As his face hits the light we can all clearly see that it is none other than the CEW's former reporter EXTREME. "By giving me the green light Hammerlock and our editor in chief Freedom have unleashed a force like no other in journalism. I tell it like it is. I don't care who you are, what you've done in the past. I'm all about the here and the now. As of now I await assignment from Freedom. So commisioner's and slammers alike. You have something you want covered drop me a line. But I'm warning you right now, be prepared for what I have to say, because if I don't like what I see I'm going to tell you." I'm not passive, I'm not timid, I"M EXTREME!! none
