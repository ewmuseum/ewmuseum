iAPWF board guidelines

a member fed's own page, captured 3 October 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The concept behind the IAPWF Board is to simulate the flashes that wrestlers give each other before matches. It is not an everything goes in your face type of board. You'll notice if you watch enough matches that the language used by wrestlers is pretty tame. Sure you get the "I'm gonna kick his rear all over the mat" type of stuff, but that's the extent of it. Well guess what, that is the extent of this board. Absolutely no sexual references are allowed, especially not terms such as jack off, cum...anything or any other term that implies or is slang for what most would perceive as a sexual act. Absolutely no knocks or put downs of people's cultures. That includes using terms that most people would find offensive about their cultures or nationalities. Terms such as redneck.... or so on are not tolerated. Stay away from religious put downs or knocks. No family references in your flashes, such as "I kicked your Mother's butt all over...." No circumventing the censor filter. This means if one of the words not allowed is "Good" then you can not type in "Gxxd, G#%d", Good### or any variation which tries to indicate that the word "Good" is being used. Now this is just the same policies that are imposed on professional wrestlers from the WCW and WWF. Now if Brett and Mankind can refrain from this type of language so can you. It is the spirit of these guidelines that I want to be kept, not the letter of the guidelines. I will decide when the spirit of these guidelines have been violated. Now that this has been cleared up, go rant, rave and flash. Have fun. Go slammin! The Slammer
