[cew] Weich Report w/ Tim Weich

22 May 2000
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[name withheld] Ladies and Gentlemen two weeks ago we saw a sad sad happening. Bloodbath a multi crowned champion and true legend of the game, met the end of his career against the greatest opponent and best friend he has ever had in the game. But that wasn't the only thing that ended with a sick crack two weeks ago. The greatest feud in the history of E Wrestling came to a violent and bloody halt. Bloodbath of course faced the two time CEW Champion AC in that career ending war. AC and Bloodbath have had five matches over the course of two years. In this day of titles are everything, and that you become a name by winning one...so few recognize what made this feud so great. After this column I hope that some people look back on the AC/Bloodbath series with a little different opinion of what it takes to be a legend. Bloodbath: Medium size, muscular build, and the ability to fly with the greatest of ease. Bloodbath was in the classic sense of an all arounder all that and a phenomenal step more. Bloodbath came into the NWF a bit of an enigma, starting out a cruiser weight, he literally worked his way past EVERYONE in the NWF, he took on Destruct, Xior, The Godfather, Scott Diamond, and the list goes on and on. When the dust settled the NWF could never claim a more important or resilient figure then Bloodbath. When the time came that Bloodbath was bigger then any title...he made his own. The NEW world title. He brought along with him a group of NEW wrestlers and NEW titles. Bloodbath’s final match in the NWF was a victory over an upstart who he had once made his protoge a young wrestler named AC. Bloodbath and this upstart would feud across four companies. AC: Large build, muscular build, an authority on high impact power moves. AC came into the NWF strait out of some lower level indy federation where he had been shown the basics by another legend Scott Diamond. AC came into the NWF, a run away train he scored 6 victories in a row before he won his first title, from a man that Bloodbath brought in from the NEW...AC became the NEW’s IC Champion and Bloodbath’s new heir apparent. Bloodbath worked hard to make AC a champion and a great wrestler Bloodbath used to muse how one day AC would take his place atop the NWF. But when AC lost the NEW title to Zot, the cold shadow of Bloodbath’s turned back struck a chord of hate and resentment in the upstart. He challenged his master to a one on one match, Bloodbath found this the perfect time to hang up the boots. And made AC vs. Bloodbath his retirement match. AC felt true defeat at the hands of Bloodbath. For Bloodbath it wasn’t about a title belt it wasn’t about anything other then cementing his dominance over the wrestling world by beating AC he had shut the door on his career complete, a list of victories over some of the greatest and most skilled athletes the world has ever known. It wasn't about gold for AC either. Bloodbath represented at the time, and folks...to this day. What a great wrestler is. He has all the tools. All the words all the moves and all the mental toughness to topple the giants. To AC beating him would mean more then any damn title belt ever would. Beating Bloodbath meant the world. The fire to beat Bloodbath burned in AC. When AC jumped to the HCW he wanted to win a world title, but couldn't let that seed of rage in him to his former mentor burn out. AC and Bloodbath would meet again in the HCW. AC had spewed his venom for years that Bloodbath saying he could beat him now. That things had changed that AC was the man now, and Bloodbath was a coward for leaving him without another go around. Bloodbath debuted in HCW with one mission to silence AC’s volatile mouth. Bloodbath and AC met for a second time. The match was brutal and went the distance...one hour. When the batch. And they got it. The bloody battle that followed finally evened the score. AC and Bloodbath both held a win over one another. For both the hurt, and celebration were short lived. Neither man had proven they were the better man. AC beat Bloodbath, Bloodbath beat AC, they had a tie...1-1-1...something hd to give. The two would meet again in Bloodbath’s promotion, he reopened the NEW and changed it to the UEW. AC showed up and took his first world title. After a car wreck put AC out of action he came back and with support from Akona and a few others, forcibly took over the UEW effectively making it the ACW. Bloodbath stood up for his company to take on AC. AC and Bloodbath fought a hell of a fight but in the end AC’s knee blew out off a missed spot and the match was ruled a draw after Bloodbath refused to make the cover. The feud settled a bit as both found a sort of peace with the tied up statistics. AC and Bloodbath formed an alliance and Bloodbath would even come to AC’s aid when he needed it. When AC formed a group called the Crew the first man he signed was Bloodbath. The Crew dissolved after AC blew up Chris Powers in an exploding cage match. But when Bloodbath came back and asked for another retirement match...the fire stoked. AC and Bloodbath met one final time in the squared circle...this time in the CEW. The match was an epic highs and lows...shared kick outs...shared finishers...One slip at the last moment...cost Bloodbath the match...and his career. AC refused to pin him. When higher ups forced him to, AC made the cover...but broke it at two. Bloodbath’s words were enough. “You Win.” AC had finally over come his greatest rival, and Bloodbath had the finale his career sadly demanded. A violent end to a violent man. The feud between the two was brilliant. AC attacked Bloodbath first for his betrayal of him for turning his back after he lost the NEW IC title. Bloodbath claimed it was to set him free...and that in the first match it was time to see if he had learned all the lessons. The second bout was all about Bloodbath shutting up a hot arrogant would be champion. The third encounter...both men NEEDED to win...Bloodbath to prove to himself he had it...AC to finally just win. The next two were bloodbath’s both played on the who is the man themes...but the thing about his feud is...not one time...not once in the five meetings...not once in the two years...did a title ever come into it. It all came down to who was faster...smarter...better. It came down to two legends...two men who were bigger then titles...and yet still champions. Two men who had to play the game like they never did against any opponent...two men with a rivalry that could excite millions. It wasn’t about gold it was about pride...that is lost these days. Right now the CEW does show some signs of people that see the difference. Right now there are some feuds that could go far. Chris Keanan vs. Tyler Simono. This one right now is the king #$@$. This is the one sitting on the mountain. These guys have heat...and they play one another like fiddles. You never know who is up or down in this one...and this is one that could go the distance. Kadeem Jones vs. Jayson Solo. This one is so ripe. Backstage egos are the only thing keeping this thing from taking center stage. One thing both contestants should keep in mind is...a good feud will get you noticed...it makes you interesting...it makes us want to go places with you. Keep that in mind. Guys I want to see in feuds...and think could make great ones. Phobos vs. Chaos! This one would be GREAT...Chaos a strait shooter talented dark, and intelligent. Phobos a joker a clown and brilliant. This match up would have so many drastic contrasts it wouldn't fail to shock and amaze folks...God just think of the rps...Chaos...GET BETTER SOON!!!!! DAMMIT!!!! Top Gun vs. Hotrod vs. Davey Scott. This could be good all these guys are GREAT and need a great feud to go along. I don’t know how to set it up but with all the similarities here the way the previous match works of contrasts this one works off similarity. SDH and ANYONE!!!! SDH is the most underrated guy around. SDH Slater? Yeah dog....what a match up that would be. Or SDH Rockwell...come on this guy is gifted....one good one will be all it takes. Feuds...not titles...are the life blood of the game. Just my take...I may be wrong...but watching AC and Bloodbath kill one another for an hour to see who is the better man is better then any title match I have ever seen. Cyber Extreme Wrestling
