[IWO]- Official Mayhem 'Press Release'!

25 May 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Johnny Shallow CHICAGO, IL.--(IWO ONLINE E-WIRE)--May 25, 2000--IWO/IWO Online, Inc. proudly present the Pay-Per-View extravaganza, May Mayhem, live Sunday, May 28, 2000, at 8 P.M. Eastern. In the main event of May Mayhem, the entire Internet Wrestling Organization roster will compete in the second annual "Mayhem Match." The rules for this match state that a network of 4 scaffolds will be set up side by side high above the ring. Each IWO super star will draw a number prior to the match. As each one minute, thirty seconds pass in the match, the super star who drew the corresponding number will enter the match by climbing up the scaffold and battling. The only way to be eliminated from the match is to be knocked off the scaffold. The last man remaining will earn a title at next month's pay per view. The past few weeks have seen numerous IWO super stars, including Hardcore Isosceles Trapezoid, Psycho Jay, G-Dogg, and others duke it out in an effort to gain a mental advantedge over one another coming into this match. Also on the card for May Mayhem, the Internet Wrestling Organization Intercontinental Tag Team Titles will be on the line as the lone champion, Fenix, defends against both Hellfire & Brimstone and Enemies of the State in a Flaming Ladder Match. In this match, the first team to set a ladder on fire and climb to obtain the title belts hanging high above is declared the winner.
