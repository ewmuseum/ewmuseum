CWO match 52: Bubbajones vs King_zero

PreMatch Comments Sucka Flying fistdrop from the top rope...connects! I can't really see from this angle but I think Bubbajones is busted open! Incidentally...we have a late breaking story on King_zero, but it's too hot for Java! You'll have to call the hotline! You don't see Bubbajones go to the rope very often, but there he goes! He takes too long! King_zero grabs him and launches Bubbajones across the ring!!! Mule kick from King_zero...but Bubbajones cartwheels out of the way. A little showboating going on! Bubbajones has him down, knees on his back...trying to roll him over for the bow and arr...can't get it locked in. King_zero is too close to the ropes and breaks the hold. Bubbajones with an atomic drop...blocked! King_zero connects with a powerful spinning mule kick! That'll turn your lights out for good! One way ticket to Turnbuckle-City as King_zero executes a picture perfect slingshot! Bubbajones climbs on his back...camel clutch! Wait! He couldn't cinch it in and King_zero is able to power out. Bubbajones whips him into the ropes. He sets up for a backdrop. King_zero rebounds and nails him with a flying headbutt! Flying fistdrop from the top rope...connects! I can't really see from this angle but I think Bubbajones is busted open! Bubbajones whips King_zero into the ropes, setting up for a monkey flip. He telegraphed it WAY too early! King_zero gives him a stomp on the head for good measure. A clothesline to the back of the head sends King_zero to the mat! Incredible speed as Bubbajones is right on top of him...STF! Again, King_zero refuses to submit and the hold is broken after 1 minute. Bubbajones is climbing to the top rope...King_zero shakes the ropes! Bubbajones falls right onto the turnbuckle allowing King_zero to climb up...superplex!!! This match has been going non-stop and...oh! It spills out of the ring again! Bubbajones whips King_zero into the guard rail! Now he tries to ram his head into the metal steps...reversted! Bubbajones gets his head bounced off the hard steel and both roll back under the bottom rope! King_zero looks pretty confident. He climbs to the top and...moonsault! Bubbajones catches him and slams him to the mat! Great Power! King_zero throws Bubbajones into the ropes. He goes for a hiptoss...but Bubbajones puts on the brakes! Slaps on an abdominal stretch! King_zero is complaining about a hair pull and the hold gets broken. King_zero is screaming at the ref that there was a hair pull on that last move. Bubbajones sneaks up...and jams his fingers down King_zero's throat! Mandible claw!!! Bubbajones slaps on a painful armbar...a duck under and King_zero turns the tables with an arm lock. While there's a break in the action here are some pre-recorded comments from Bubbajones..."King_zero, when I get done with you I hope the doctors have an old picture of you on hand so they know where to sew all the pieces back on!" Tough words from a tough man! What does he have there? A roll of coins! King_zero clocks him in the jaw and about twenty bucks in change flies all over the ring! Bubbajones climbs on his back...camel clutch! Wait! He couldn't cinch it in and King_zero is able to power out. King_zero is charging Bubbajones with a clothesline...Monkey flip! Man that Bubbajones is fast! Bubbajones slaps him! King_zero is just standing there laughing. Now he starts to walk away...turns and NAILS Bubbajones with a flying forearm! King_zero has Bubbajones right where he wants him...a fistfull of tights and they're up on the turnbuckle. Superplex? NO! Bubbajones pokes King_zero in the eyes and down they go! King_zero is walking on the top rope! Agile!!! But here comes Bubbajones with a dropkick! King_zero goes sailing through a table and Bubbajones is tied up in the ropes! Test of strength in the middle of the ring now. Bubbajones twists his wrists...hands to the mat...and King_zero gets his fingers stomped! King_zero goes for a roundhouse right...backdrop by Bubbajones over the top rope! Bubbajones climbs out after him and slaps on a cobra clutch! The fans are on their feet! Now he throws King_zero back into the ring at the ten count. King_zero off the ropes for a shoulder block. Bubbajones sidesteps and applies a painful nerve pinch that drops King_zero to his knees! King_zero goes for a roundhouse right...backdrop by Bubbajones over the top rope! Bubbajones climbs out after him and slaps on a cobra clutch! The fans are on their feet! Now he throws King_zero back into the ring at the ten count. King_zero with a roundhouse right. Blocked! Bubbajones grabs the arm and throws it into a hammerlock! Into the corner, and the crowd starts chanting as Bubbajones rams his head into the turnbuckle again and again! Off the ropes...Bubbajones grabs a hold and goes for a fireman's carry. King_zero holds on and rolls him into a small package! 1...2...kickout! King_zero off the top rope. He took way too long to get up there...axe-handle attempted and Bubbajones delivers a big fist to the midsection on his way down! King_zero takes him down with a leg trip. Like a cat to the top rope...Big elbow from way up...but Bubbajones rolls out of the way!!! Nobody home! Bubbajones climbs on his back...camel clutch! Wait! He couldn't cinch it in and King_zero is able to power out. Look at King_zero fly! He's going for the head scissors, but Bubbajones grabs a hold of the ropes! Not going anywhere, and King_zero crashes to the canvas head first! Bubbajones slaps on a headlock. King_zero can't seem to power out...he lifts him into a side suplex! Up to the top rope and...BAM! Big elbow from fifteen feet up! Bubbajones has him down, knees on his back...trying to roll him over for the bow and arr...can't get it locked in. King_zero is too close to the ropes and breaks the hold. Bubbajones rakes his eyes across his boot laces! The ref must be as blind as King_zero is now to have missed that! King_zero is screaming at the ref that there was a hair pull on that last move. Bubbajones sneaks up...and jams his fingers down King_zero's throat! Mandible claw!!! Bubbajones with the go behind...full nelson! But a very slippery King_zero is able to slide out before any damage could be inflicted. Bubbajones executes a belly to back suplex...steps over...into a Boston Crab! King_zero manages to get his arm over the bottom rope. Lucky! Bubbajones reaches around...looking to lock in an abdominal stretch...BIG hiptoss by King_zero! King_zero with a roundhouse right. Blocked! Bubbajones grabs the arm and throws it into a hammerlock! Into the corner, and the crowd starts chanting as Bubbajones rams his head into the turnbuckle again and again! Bubbajones with a stiff chop to the chest...goes for a second and King_zero rocks him with an enziguri kick to the head! A clothesline to the back of the head sends King_zero to the mat! Incredible speed as Bubbajones is right on top of him...STF! Again, King_zero refuses to submit and the hold is broken after 1 minute. Bubbajones is pinned..1..2.shoulder up and what's this. Yes it's Bubbajones's patented Belly Flop move. 1...2...3 That's it Bubbajones has won in a stunning upset Winner: Bubbajones
