CWO match 242: Arcwing vs Freud

PreMatch Comments I'm going to kick you in your freud. Freud off the ropes...he leaps...he misses! Cross-body block attempted, but Arcwing ducks under it! Freud whips him into the ropes and goes in for the clothesline...Arcwing ducks it...off the other side and a beautiful cross-body block! Only a one count. Freud swings and misses! He catches a boot to the gut for his troubles...and Arcwing slams him with a jumping DDT! Devastating! Arcwing in control. Throws Freud into the ropes...up for a Frankensteiner! Freud holds on to the top rope and Arcwing comes crashing down head first! An irish whip and Freud is going for the ride...Arcwing off the ropes from the other side...and Freud takes him down with flying headscissors! Great move! Freud off the top rope. He took way too long to get up there...axe-handle attempted and Arcwing delivers a big fist to the midsection on his way down! Arcwing is climbing to the top rope...Freud shakes the ropes! Arcwing falls right onto the turnbuckle allowing Freud to climb up...superplex!!! Freud in control. Throws Arcwing into the ropes...up for a Frankensteiner! Arcwing holds on to the top rope and Freud comes crashing down head first! Freud sets up Arcwing on the top rope for a superplex! Arcwing rams him with an elbow! Now from the top rope, Arcwing with a moonsault!!! But he's not through with Freud yet... Forget about it!!! Arcwing ties up Freud in the ropes and he's using him like a human punching bag! Oh NO! Freud connects with a solid kick to the groin! OUCH! Freud is complaining to the ref about a hair pull. I don't think he's buying it. Arcwing stuns him with a punch...bounces off the ropes...leapfrog by Freud...on his way back...Freud nails him with a Frankensteiner! The crowd goes nuts! Arcwing off the ropes with a cross-body...Freud catches him. But he can't hold him! Both spill over the top rope! Hard bump, but both climb back into the ring. Freud stuns him with a punch...bounces off the ropes...leapfrog by Arcwing...on his way back...Arcwing nails him with a Frankensteiner! The crowd goes nuts! Freud slaps on a headlock. Arcwing can't seem to power out...he lifts him into a side suplex! Up to the top rope and...BAM! Big elbow from fifteen feet up! Arcwing rebounds out of the corner with a flying forearm...but Freud is waiting with a stiff boot to the face. Arcwing whips him into the ropes. He sets up for a backdrop. Freud rebounds and nails him with a flying headbutt! Freud takes him down with a leg trip. Like a cat to the top rope...Big elbow from way up...but Arcwing rolls out of the way!!! Nobody home! Arcwing whips him into the ropes. He sets up for a backdrop. Freud rebounds and nails him with a flying headbutt! Arcwing with a roundhouse right...Freud catches it and executes an armdrag takedown! Great reflexes! Freud with a sunset flip! 1...2...Arcwing rolls it over! 1...2...Freud turns it into a small package! 1...kickout! Flying fistdrop from the top rope...connects! I can't really see from this angle but I think Arcwing is busted open! Freud stuns him with a punch...bounces off the ropes...leapfrog by Arcwing...on his way back...Arcwing nails him with a Frankensteiner! The crowd goes nuts! Look at Arcwing fly! He's going for the head scissors, but Freud grabs a hold of the ropes! Not going anywhere, and Arcwing crashes to the canvas head first! Freud has Arcwing right where he wants him...a fistfull of tights and they're up on the turnbuckle. Superplex? NO! Arcwing pokes Freud in the eyes and down they go! Freud with a roundhouse kick. Arcwing traps the leg...Freud tries for the enziguri kick...missed! Freud whips him into the ropes and goes in for the clothesline...Arcwing ducks it...off the other side and a beautiful cross-body block! Only a one count. Arcwing off the ropes...leaps over Freud with a headlock applied...flying bulldog!!! Very impressive maneuver! Look at Freud fly! He's going for the head scissors, but Arcwing grabs a hold of the ropes! Not going anywhere, and Freud crashes to the canvas head first! Freud off the ropes with a cross-body...Arcwing catches him. But he can't hold him! Both spill over the top rope! Hard bump, but both climb back into the ring. Impressive displays from both men at this point. Freud with the speed advantage while...Arcwing rocks him in the corner with an avalanche! No place to go!!! This match has been going non-stop and...oh! It spills out of the ring again! Arcwing whips Freud into the guard rail! Now he tries to ram his head into the metal steps...reversted! Arcwing gets his head bounced off the hard steel and both roll back under the bottom rope! Forget about it!!! Freud ties up Arcwing in the ropes and he's using him like a human punching bag! Oh NO! Arcwing connects with a solid kick to the groin! OUCH! Freud rolls out of the ring for a breather...here comes Arcwing flying between the ropes with a clothesline! No...Freud saw in coming and ducks out of the way! Arcwing hits the floor hard and Freud tosses him back into the ring. Freud throws a big elbow, Arcwing rams him with a knee at the same time! They both hit the mat and the fists are flying! Throw the book out the window, this one's becoming a brawl! Mule kick from Freud...but Arcwing cartwheels out of the way. A little showboating going on! Arcwing with a roundhouse right...Freud catches it and executes an armdrag takedown! Great reflexes! Freud in control. Throws Arcwing into the ropes...up for a Frankensteiner! Arcwing holds on to the top rope and Freud comes crashing down head first! Arcwing goes for a suplex...reversed...reversed again...Freud blocks it and slaps him! Freud looks like he's going for an inverted atomic drop here. Wait! Arcwing is biting his face! This is a side of Arcwing that I've never seen before! What does he have there? A roll of coins! Arcwing clocks him in the jaw and about twenty bucks in change flies all over the ring! An irish whip and Freud is going for the ride...Arcwing off the ropes from the other side...and Freud takes him down with flying headscissors! Great move! Arcwing tosses him out of the ring and onto the timekeeper's table. He's nuts! Arcwing climbs the ropes...a flying headbutt!!! NO! Freud gets out of the way and Arcwing crashes through to the floor! Freud grabs him and tosses him back into the ring! Freud slaps on a headlock. Arcwing can't seem to power out...he lifts him into a side suplex! Up to the top rope and...BAM! Big elbow from fifteen feet up! Freud connects witha European uppercut...Arcwing nails him with one of his own! They're going punch for punch in the center of the ring! Freud tosses him out of the ring and onto the timekeeper's table. He's nuts! Freud climbs the ropes...a flying headbutt!!! NO! Arcwing gets out of the way and Freud crashes through to the floor! Arcwing grabs him and tosses him back into the ring! Freud with the dropkick...misses! Arcwing grabs a hold of his ankles...BIG slingshot! Freud sails though the air. His head connects with the steel post!!! Out cold! Arcwing rolls him up...1...2...3!!! You could've counted to a hundred!!! Winner: Arcwing
