iUWA: The Shamus Standpoint

16 August 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Shamus The Shamus Standpoint Welcome to this weeks Shamus Standpoint. Unfortunatly I will not be reviewing cards, giving an interview, or even a top ten list. I am going to do something Shamus has not done. I am gonna rant a bit. You see, the iUWA has always been more or less a c-slame home for me. I started here, I strived here, I won here, I lost here, I got promoted here. I came back here, and I will stay here through commish after commish. I have been through alot of them. Not them all but quite a bit dating back to Jerry Lee. The day Jerry left the iUWA was the fed's saddest day. It was when we were our strongest. We held together for a little while. But how long can an unsupervised group of sheep stay a flock. Not very long. Soon slammers I respected and admired were leaving the fed. I chose to hang through it. Soon after the Iron Mike era began. I have said this before, Iron Mike was and is still one of my friends. Was he a great commish, I wouldn't say that. Was he unbiased, I wouldn't say that either. I will tell you this, at the time talent was at an all time low. I became the reporter under Iron Mike's term. Am I the best reporter, No. But regardless, Soon after Iron Mike was on his way out the talent really started to pick up, I decided to stay in the iUWA even though my slammer has been granted to start in the CGW. The day I strived for since I began. I turned my back on friends to hope I get there and I finnally made it. But I still felt like I was missing something, the iUWA. I come back to a new commish named Exile. Who I feel was off to a great start. I don't want to nor will I badger him because he has the ability to become a very good comish. But I must say this, if he doesn't do the right thing soon, he will be losing alot of great talent. Alot! What is the right thing you might ask, the right thing is to put more into the iUWA, until it can stand again on it's own. We need some good cards. I can't argue with the matches I get because I am fine with them. But some of the other matches seem to have been tossed together. Let new guys prove themselves before awarding them with title shots and what have you. At the same time, give those that have proven themselves more time on the top. I have never been one to say keep the vets with the vets and the rookies to themselves, Hell I'm going through the same thing in CGW. I am saying this, the ones who have ruled this fed for as long as I can remember deserve to have more than an Underdogg title match. Or for that matter a match at all. We have already seen one decent slammer leave, with a possibility of 3 or so more on there way out. Exile, do something about it before it is to late. Before this fed is in shambles again. And this place in where I call home is burnt down to the ground. Don't take the easy way out by resigning, we have seen that before. Instead make it right. I know it is impossible to please everyone, but just listen to "your" slammers. For the most part they know what is best. This doesn't mean every Jack $## jobber that requests a title shot gets one. (i.e. Jobberx) A title shot is supposed to be a great thing, not something you give away in a Mcdonalds kid's meal. So as a service I am gonna just run through who I think deserve a shot at each belt. Starting with The Underdogg Current Champ: Tomango Jobberx Vinny Vegas Jack Wolf Jr Jacco Now this belt is never one to be happy with, and I really don't feel the right guy is holding it. He is to talented and even though his slamming record is not up to par, he still has beyond proven himself. The only one on this list I think deserves and will become much more is Vinny Vegas. The only reason I put him on here is because he really hasn't proven himself. So throwing him a World Title shot is just a waist of the other talent that has done more than proven themselves. The SkullCrusher Curent Champ:Sabit Sidious Jenerz Storm Super Soviet Half Real The Skullcrusher is more of a tradition which should always be honered. And I definatly feel Sabit is the right guy for this belt. Nuff said. TV Title Current Champ: Zabingu Electabuzz Maple Leaf G Ride Stronguy Mike For starters, I really don't know anything about this Zabingu. He is real new, but he is also real good. So I will say ok about this one. And same with the current #1 contender, Electabuzz. He is definatly deserving. As for the other 3, well I think they deserve to be atleast given a shot. The World Championship Current Champ: Slammin Stu The Rick Tomango Angelhunter Montana Kid The most coveted world title. I know there is some controversy around the current champ, but he is deserving, and he is very talented. But this is who I see as other very over looked canidates for this belt. Granted there are two IP members but they are a benefit to have in this fed. I know ALOT of people will not agree with my thoughts. It also may seem at times I am a IP mark, I'm not. I have always been more of a bWo man myself. So why side with IP in this. I hate seniority, And I hate when respect is supposed to be given because they were here the longest. But I honestly feel that the men that are ready to leave are the men that are the ones really ready to lead this fed. They are apart of my past. Will there be more to come. Absolutly. Will they be as good, perhaps. But will they give it as much as most of the "vets" have given it, not a chance. In closing I would like to say this to Exile and the rest of the iUWA. I know how it is being new, I know how it is to try so hard to earn respect. And even though it is almost like the iUWA is a brand new fed to some, even the comish, it has been a home for alot of the "vets" So why make them prove there selves yet again. There is only so many times you can until you say F it, and let the new guys have the stage. And for the vets I say this. Don't turn your back on this fed now. I understand the reasons, but try to deal with it. It has only been a few weeks, give it a little more time, if not than you can have a Shamus Hanging day in the iUWA. And for all you smart guys who know who I am from this report, good for you. They are two seperate people, keep them that way. Right now this is more or less an OOC on both parts. Ov is not Shamus, and Shamus is not Ov. I have always been very unbiased with my reports and never boosted myself. NEVER! So for me to say all this is not to better benefit myself, it is to benefit the iUWA. I know I'm not the greatest reporter. I know it is hard after 4 days or so since my last report to see this. I appologize, if anyone thinks I should retire let me know. Until than I'll continue being Shamus. And the iUWA will continue to have a reporter that truly gives a damn about this fed. **Note to Exile** I appologize for seeming like I am ripping you apart or judging you. I'm not, I just know you can give the iUWA more. You have the potential to be a great commish. Don't let this get to you, it is only a game after all. none
