iNWA: The Icon leaves

2 October 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Defcon Well its been on the cards for a while now but Strykar has finally booked his ticket to the big leagues. Before he left he managed to spare us a few words ---------------------------------------- Defcon: Hi this is Defcon here and today I am sat with a man whos bags are packed for the big leagues. Yep thats right folks Strykar is going up in the world leaving the iNWA and setting up sticks in the CRF. Thankfully the legend has politely agreed to answer us a few questions before he leaves .. So Strykar how are you feeling about the promotion? Strykar: Well, I have mixed feelings about it, naturally. As you know, I was supposed to move up to the CRF in June originally, but my agent wasn't able to come to a contract agreement. So, now that they're under new management, with a new direction, we're going to give it another try. I'm not especially thrilled over leaving the iNWA, it's always been my home under the C-Slam umbrella, but at the same time it's a great opportunity to showcase what I do to a bigger crowd. Defcon: Do you feel that the CRF is really going to challenge you or do you think you will find yourself in the same situation that you have in the iNWA which is of course total domination? Strykar: As you know, Defcon, I'm a perfectionist when it comes to stepping into the ring. I expect it to take a little while to adjust to the different styles and personalities of the Slammers, but you can bet on my rising straight to the top again, sooner than later. I won't accept any less than that. Defcon: You will leave a huge hole behind in the iNWA. Who do you think will manage to fight themselves to the top and take over your mantle the best in the fed? Strykar: Well, you have my stablemates in MD2K to take into consideration first. Cyberpunk, Dogox, H_Court, Payne_Valley and Sicofly. All are capable of carrying the mantle of 'The Man'. And, so are the two Myth Ladies as well, Miss_U and Granny_S. Trust me, I've been in the ring with them, and it wasn't a pleasant experience. Other than that, the only other Slammer to really show me anything in the ring has been Ikon, who could have a decent career. With some experience, both he and The_G_a_m_e, who is saddled with a tired old gimmick, could work up to that level. And perhaps, just perhaps .. JBomb. Defcon: JBomb? Why do you say that? Strykar: Because, Defcon, the man isn't smart enough to know when he's completely outclassed. That utter lack of mental activity makes his dangerous. Look at this supposed challenge he laid out. The guy is playing 'Pin Me, Pay Me'. HE loves having his backside kicked around the ring by every man on the roster. Defcon: On a side note do you think that the MD2K will still dominate in the fed or will the influx of new talent perhaps spawn a decent sized army to take them down? Strykar: Contrary to what some guys and girls might tell you in this game, it's quality that matters, not quantity. They can gather as many as they want, but unless they're all superstars in their own right .. well .. in that case, they're not going to accomplish anything. Defcon: Ok lets look back at your long career in the iNWA what for you has been your best moment? Strykar: The best moment? Wow, it's hard to nail something like that down to just one thing .. Now, this might sound kind of strange and all, but I think that it was my last Tag Title run with Cyberpunk. The first with Hardcourt was memorable, but this time around the other Slammers were actually showing up to lose their title shots. Defcon: And your worst? Strykar: Another good question .. I can't really say it's any title loss or anything like that. I think that the worst moments were the chaotic ones, where the iNWA was lacking direction, or was having a very poor direction shoved at it by shortsightedness in the office. But I think that's also really on the mend now as well with the arrival of Faded. Defcon: We have seen a lot of people come and go. Who sticks out in your mind as the your most dangerous foe? Strykar: Most dangerous foe .. This is a real trip back in time I think. Discounting anyone in my own stable, who are all dangerous on any given night, I would have to say Creed, simply because the man is a thinking man's Slammer. That, in and of itself, made the man as dangerous as anyone in C-Slam. Defcon: Well thanks for your time Strykar. And good luck in the majors. Strykar: Thanks, Defcon. It's going to be a different road, but I've been ready for it for months. none
