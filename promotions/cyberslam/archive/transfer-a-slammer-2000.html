Transfer a Slammer

the hub's own Transfer page, captured April 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
If you would like to transfer your slammer, then fill out the form below and click on the "Continue" button. The cost per transfer is $15.00. It is a one time fee, which allows you transfer this slammer (only this slammer, it does not apply to any other slammer you may have) to the fed of your choice. The Slammer Important: Transfers submitted near the 20th of the month will not be completed until the turn of the following month. In other words if you submit a transfer on the 20th, your slammer will not be transferred until the 1st of the following month.
