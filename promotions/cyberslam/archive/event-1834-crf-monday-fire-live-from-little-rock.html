CRF "Monday Fire" Live From Little Rock

11 December 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
xx Live From The Alltel Arena in Little Rock Arkansas...... CRF MONDAY FIRE MAIN EVENT CRF WORLD TITLE MATCH Champion Redtide Vs Challenger Spoom Spoom earns his shot in tough fashion. Can he gun down the new Champ or will he drowned in the "High Tide"? CRF CAN-AM TITLE MATCH Triple Threat Match Champion The Korn Kid Vs Challengers Koala And Thor The Korn Kid finds himself defending his title against two gold hungry men in Thor and Koala. Can The Can-Am Champ survive in a match where he doesn't have to be pinned to lose his title? CRF ENFORCER TITLE MATCH "BATTLE AT BILL'S HOUSE" Champion Carnage Vs Challenger Pagan Carnage wants a piece of Pagan but that's not the only thing he wants. He puts his Enforcer Title on the line with the stip that if he wins, Pagan must give up his magic powers. If Pagan wins he gets the Enforcer Title. Not a good trade off in my book but what do I know.....I'm just the Commish. These two will do thier best to Trash Bill Clinton's house. Seems Bill is having a meeting with Gore and Bush trying to help get this whole presidential Fiasco fixed. Carnage and Pagan get to "Crash" the party. Watch out for the Secret Service. This match is rewrite as always and is due by Midnight Board time on Monday. CRF TELEVISION TITLE MATCH Champion Bick Vs Challenger Payne Valley Payne Valley defeats two othe men to gain this shot. Will he make Bick's Title reign a short one or will "Enraged" get involved to insure the new stable keeps the TV title? CRF TAG TEAM TITLE MATCH Champions "Technocrancy" Cyberpunk & Pagan Vs "Luchadore Lunatics" Bick & The Korn Kid "Technocrancy" has been quite the dominate tag team as of late. They're on a month long tear as the champs. This week they face a tough challenge in the form of the "Luchadore Lunatics". Will the champs make it out alive or will the Lunatics be "Running the asylum" after this match? CRF RAPID FIRE TITLE MATCH Champion Manowar Vs Challenger Wok King The CRF's Resident Chef goes for the gold against the Man Built for War. Will The Chef Bake the champ or will Manowar unload the big guns? TRIPLE THREAT TAG TEAM MATCH "The Method" Delusion & J Dark Vs "The CrEW" Juggernaut & Powershock Vs The "Dynamic Duo" Batman & Rancid The More Tag Teams The Merrier! These 3 teams do battle to see who gets braggin rights over the other two. "DO THE *JOB* MATCH El Jobre Jobs...er...Vs Triple C Seems "Uncle Trips" is suffering a dry run in the win department. Let's see if the resident CRF Jobber Specialist can't help him out and get the former World Champ back on track. GRUDGE MATCH PART 2 Juggernaut Vs Mystikal Speaking Of "Uncle Trips" It seems his stablemate Mystikal has a special request. I found a note under my door from Mystikal saying that he would take care of what Triple C couldn't. Is this the typical "Elite" Ego at work or is it something more? Well, Ask and so shall you receive. Mystikal gets to battle Juggernaut in a Falls Count Anywhere match. BATTLE OF THE BIG DOGS Lord O Flies Vs Twlvevoltman Two men sit at the top of the standings. Who's the best? Let's put them in the ring and find out. To the winner? A #1 contender's match for the World Title On Thursday. DO I HATE YOU? MATCH Cyberpunk Vs The Monk It seems Cyberpunk is having some sort of memory problem. Could "Enraged" member The Monk have anything to do with it? Does Cyberpunk even remember who The Monk is? maybe this match will jog his memory. REGULAR MATCH Dogox Vs Krynn Two of the Younger CRF Superstars do battle in what should be a clean match. HARDCORE CARD OPENER Powershock Vs California A Hardcore match...Gotta love'em. The Official Website Of The CRF
