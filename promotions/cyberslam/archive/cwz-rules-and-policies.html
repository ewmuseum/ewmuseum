CWZ rules and policies

Date: 01/05/101 Policies ... by The Martini MARTINI CWZ Commisioner [detail withheld] Flashing- This is very important. If you don’t flash you do not belong in the CWZ. This is key in my fed. The harder you flash and roleplay the farther you’ll get. No HTML is allowed in the title of your post, this is against Cyberslam Policies. As for the post itself...Go For It. Be Creative. Cyberslam doesn’t allow you to post pictures either.You are not allowed to include me in your posts unless it is cleared by me first. I will watch this closely. In most cases I will allow you to do so, but ask first. No Racism of ANY KIND is allowed. I will watch this closely. As far as swearing goes you can do a few things. If you feel the need to swear in your post use things like (bleep), ------, ******. Do not try and go around the C-Slam Filters with things like @$$ or F—K or S---. You will be penalized. Kids like to play this game to. Just don’t do it…OK?? Revealing other slammers identities or guessing at them is not allowed and is cause for immediate suspension. NO ONE LINE POSTS ARE ALLOWED. This is NOT considered a flash. I will warn you once for it and then after that it will be an automatice suspension. After your warning, if you are new I will e-mail you and explain what to do. But I do not want to see them. It’s a waste of space. Your Crew: Play by Play – Bobby “The Body” Jones Color commentary- “Lazy Boy” Larry Tyler Interviewer-Scheme Gene OOC Post I hate Out of Character Posts. Do not do them on my board. If you have a problem with another slammer do it elsewhere. Do not waste my board space with OOC. If you have a question for me…E-mail me or talk to me on ICQ. Email- Making threats to me or other slammers on e-mail is highly illegal. If you have a problem talk to me like a human…I’m pretty reasonable. Remember this is a game…Have fun with it but DON’T take anything personal. Jobbing/Padding- Another big No-No.. What is jobbing and or padding in my book? If you slam the same slammer more than 9 times a week or more than THREE times in one day. . You also may not slam anyone 20% lower than you in the standings unless it is a booked match on a card. There should be NO REASON to job or pad. There are plenty other slammers to slam you don’t need to slam the same guy over and over again. Weekly Shows- The weekly cards will be Monday Mayhem and Thursday Throwdown. If you are booked on the card you CANNOT slam other matches except for those booked, if you do you will be DQed and lose the match.If you have a match request, email me or ICQ me and Ill do my best to get it on there. Ill have the cards posted at least a day before they are to take place. FPV Toward the end of each month will we will have a FPV. Any matches will be accepted but not always booked due to matches you have as a slammer. I love to see gimmick matches so if you have one email me or ICQ me and we will work on the specifics, if you already have them let me know and we will see what we can do. Playoffs All slammers who are at or above 50% and ACTIVE will compete in the CWZ Playoffs. I will also add UP TO 2 additional slammers from under 50% who have been active all month to compete as well. This does NOT mean that I will do this. I will use this option when necessary. Titles You can only hold one singles belt excluding the Doc Pain Respect title which you can hold and have another singles. You can hold a tag title as well as a singles title. World Title-Defended at Monday Mayhem & The FPV Tag Team Titles- Same As World Title. Intercontinental Title - Defended at all shows Hardcore Title-Defended at all shows. TV TitleDefended at all shows. The TV Title is for those slammers under 50%. As with the other 3 singles titles, only slammers who flash are eligible for the TV Title. Once a slammer goes over 55%, he will be awarded a Hardcore Title shot and stripped of the TV Title. Doc Pain Respect Title Awarded every Wednesday! This is one of the highest titles in the CWZ. It is for that slammer who has worked his/her butt off in the CWZ to help improve and keep the fed moving and advancing.. ROLEPLAY & FLASHING is key to winning this title. This is the title you should all shoot for holding. It doesn’t get any better than the Doc Pain Respect title! Point System -In case of a tie in tag team matches and those matches where there is not a tie breaker the following point system will be used. 4pts-Win via finisher 3pts-Win via a pin or injury win (despite outcome) 2pts-Win via countout or Interference 1pt-Win via DQ Prematch Comments- When fighting on a card you must put in the name of the match and/or the Card Name. Example: HARDCORE TITLE MATCH or Monday Mayhem Match. Failure to use Pre-Match comments will result in an automatic LOSS via finisher. Just do it.. This is done in the GO Slammin’ Section not just on the boards. Roleplay on the boards. Discipline -Any violation of the policies will result in a warning for the first offense then 3 day suspension for the next. Should you continue to violate the policy after that then you’ll be watching from the outside. I also reserve the right to skip the warning and move right on to suspension depending on the severity of the violation. When doing this it should be expected that you’ll be suspended for more than 3 days. Board Resets -The board will be reset late Wednesday Mornings around 8am c/t.. Should I see it needs to be reset more I’ll add another reset day and will let everyone know when it is before I do it.
