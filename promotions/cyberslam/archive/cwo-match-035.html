CWO match 35: Brainchz vs Pegasus_kid

undated in the source
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PreMatch Comments Not so lucky this month huh! They lock up...both men looking for an advantage. WOW! Pegasus_kid tosses Brainchz into the corner! Great show of strength! Brainchz gets him down on his stomach and locks in the camel clutch! I can hear those vertabrae popping from here! He lets Pegasus_kid up! He's not finnished punnishing him! Pegasus_kid whips him into the ropes...Brainchz ducks under the clothesline...from the other side...Brainchz with a dropkick to the knee! Pegasus_kid whips him into the corner...he's right behind him. Brainchz leapfrogs behind and catches him in a chicken wing! Pegasus_kid is going out! No. He manages to slip his foot between the ropes. Pegasus_kid is charging Brainchz with a clothesline...Monkey flip! Man that Brainchz is fast! Whoa! Low blow by Pegasus_kid ! And the crowd is letting him have it! Brainchz with a dropkick to the knees...sidestepped by Pegasus_kid! Brainchz gets up from the mat and Pegasus_kid is right there with a strong running shoulder block! Levels him! Pegasus_kid has his legs...big slingshot coming up! Brainchz won't budge! He turns it into body scissors and powers out! Great move by Brainchz! Look at Pegasus_kid fly! He's going for the head scissors, but Brainchz grabs a hold of the ropes! Not going anywhere, and Pegasus_kid crashes to the canvas head first! Brainchz throws a big elbow, Pegasus_kid rams him with a knee at the same time! They both hit the mat and the fists are flying! Throw the book out the window, this one's becoming a brawl! Pegasus_kid backs him into the corner with a series of knife-edge chops...climbs the ropes for what looks like a...jumping DDT! No, wait...Brainchz has a firm hold on those ropes and Pegasus_kid jumps down. No damage. Pegasus_kid leaves the ring and returns with a chair! He goes to swing, but the ref grabs it! While there's a break in the action here are some pre-recorded comments from Brainchz..."Pegasus_kid, when I get done with you I hope the doctors have an old picture of you on hand so they know where to sew all the pieces back on!" Tough words from a tough man! Pegasus_kid looks ripe for the picking! Brainchz grabs his ankle and it appears we have a figure four on the way...No! Pegasus_kid stops him with a boot to the lower back. Brainchz with a dropkick to the knees...sidestepped by Pegasus_kid! You know, Pegasus_kid's contract is up after this match. Between you and me, if he loses I think he's headed to that other federation. Brainchz side steps the dropkick...and slaps on the figure four leg lock! Pegasus_kid reaches the rope after one minute. Brainchz with a dropkick to the knees...sidestepped by Pegasus_kid! Pegasus_kid shoots in with a European uppercut. Blocked! Brainchz turns it into an armbar. He's really applying the pressure. Pegasus_kid slams him to the canvass...he goes to climb the ropes and Brainchz grabs the back of his tights! Rolls him up! 1...2...shoulder up! Brainchz gets him on the mat with a well placed right hand...he's going for the claw! Pegasus_kid rolls out of the ring to avoid the hold. He rolls back in before the ref begins to count. One way ticket to Turnbuckle-City as Pegasus_kid executes a picture perfect slingshot! Pegasus_kid off the ropes...sunset flip! Can he pull him down? No! Brainchz drops a big fist to the face. Now he's got the legs...rolls Brainchz over...Scorpion Deathlock! Pegasus_kid reaches the rope after 45 seconds! Pegasus_kid is going for a crucifix. Can he get it!?!? Brainchz breaks free...spins around...full nelson! Great counter! A clothesline to the back of the head sends Pegasus_kid to the mat! Incredible speed as Brainchz is right on top of him...STF! Again, Pegasus_kid refuses to submit and the hold is broken after 1 minute. Brainchz throws Pegasus_kid into the ropes...runs off the other side...double clothesline!!! Brainchz grapevines the leg...going for the head to complete the STF...Pegasus_kid rolls over for a pinning combination! 1...2...kickout! Brainchz was lucky he didn't get pinned right there! Pegasus_kid whips him into the ropes...Brainchz ducks under the clothesline...from the other side...Brainchz with a dropkick to the knee! Brainchz is prone in the center of the ring! Pegasus_kid is on the top rope...big splash! 1..kickout! Pegasus_kid leaves the ring and returns with a chair! He goes to swing, but the ref grabs it! Pegasus_kid in control. Throws Brainchz into the ropes...up for a Frankensteiner! Brainchz holds on to the top rope and Pegasus_kid comes crashing down head first! Brainchz looks a bit dazed. Pegasus_kid climbs the turnbuckle...Brainchz snaps out of it and catches him in a great fireman's carry! Brainchz sneaks behind...chicken wing! Pegasus_kid manages to slip out before he could lock it in. Very Lucky! Pegasus_kid throws Brainchz into the ropes. He goes for a hiptoss...but Brainchz puts on the brakes! Slaps on an abdominal stretch! Pegasus_kid is complaining about a hair pull and the hold gets broken. Pegasus_kid is out of it and...Oh! Brainchz applies the Chz cutter..It's all over!! Brainchz has sent a message to all the other wrestlers that he's back. Winner: Brainchz
