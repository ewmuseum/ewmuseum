CWO match 82: Daggybaby vs Coroner

PreMatch Comments slamming Daggybaby has him in a headlock...spins him around...Coroner escapes and backs off. Flying fistdrop from the top rope...connects! I can't really see from this angle but I think Daggybaby is busted open! Daggybaby with the headbutt...he stunned himself! Daggybaby falls down! Coroner to the top rope...BIG legdrop from way up there! 1...2...NO! Close! Coroner backs him into the corner with a series of knife-edge chops...climbs the ropes for what looks like a...jumping DDT! No, wait...Daggybaby has a firm hold on those ropes and Coroner jumps down. No damage. An irish whip and Coroner is going for the ride...Daggybaby off the ropes from the other side...and Coroner takes him down with flying headscissors! Great move! Flying fistdrop from the top rope...connects! I can't really see from this angle but I think Daggybaby is busted open! A kick to the gut and Coroner is buckled over...DDT! Coroner grabs the rope to block it and Daggybaby takes a hard fall! From the second turnbuckle, Coroner with the flying fist drop...Daggybaby gets the leg up! Coroner gets a jaw-full of boot! Coroner is thrown into the corner...here comes Daggybaby! Vader Attack! Nobody home as Coroner ducks down leaving nothing but air! Daggybaby slaps on a sleeper...but Coroner drops down with a jawbreaker! He springs to the top rope, BIG double axe-handle to the head! Daggybaby swings and misses! He catches a boot to the gut for his troubles...and Coroner slams him with a jumping DDT! Devastating! Daggybaby is prone in the center of the ring! Coroner is on the top rope...big splash! 1..kickout! Daggybaby slaps on a headlock. Coroner can't seem to power out...he lifts him into a side suplex! Up to the top rope and...BAM! Big elbow from fifteen feet up! Daggybaby gets him up on his shoulders....NO! Coroner slips out and catches him with a forearm to the back of the head! Coroner gets slammed in the middle of the ring. Here comes Daggybaby off the ropes, a big splash...nobody home! Good timing by Coroner! Coroner whips Daggybaby into the ropes...he's waiting in the middle of the ring with a backdrop... Daggybaby saw it coming! He grabs him and drives him into the mat with an awesome piledriver! Coroner is looking to take a breather in the corner...here comes Daggybaby! BIG avalanche! Coroner crumples to the mat. Daggybaby has got him! He's signaling to he crowd for a piledriver...Coroner counters with a backdrop! Coroner takes him down with a leg trip. Like a cat to the top rope...Big elbow from way up...but Daggybaby rolls out of the way!!! Nobody home! Coroner whips him into the ropes...looking for a backdrop...Daggybaby sees it coming. DDT! Coroner’s head bounced off the mat like a superball! They lock up...the ref sees a hair pull and breaks it up. Daggybaby is climbing to the top rope...Coroner shakes the ropes! Daggybaby falls right onto the turnbuckle allowing Coroner to climb up...superplex!!! Daggybaby sets up Coroner on the top rope for a superplex! Coroner rams him with an elbow! Now from the top rope, Coroner with a moonsault!!! But he's not through with Daggybaby yet... These two slammers are having one heck of a match. It looks like Coroner is setting up Daggybaby for his ultimate Nail In The Coffin move...the fans are screaming...the ref looks around and sees None. The ref is telling the manager to get out of the ring. Oh NO! Palvenis has jumped in the ring with a bat..the fans are going wild...the ref is busy with None. BAM!! Palvenis has whacked Coroner with the bat! Palvenis has jumped out of the ring...the ref turns around and catches Daggybaby applying his patented TOP ROPE TOMBSTONE PILEDRIVER move. 1...2...3 it's over. Daggybaby could have applied his grandmother's move, it wouldn't have mattered. Once again the Bad_company stable has changed the outcome of a match. Winner: Daggybaby
