[ CRF ] Maverick says fight back Cslam

3 February 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
CCWe The Gossip Column Gossip here after chatting with CRF Commish Maverick I found that dedication to your craft and profession can show that chivalry is not dead. Maverick is continueing to run the CRF as normal, he asks of the CRF slammers, no he demands that you step up to the plate and continue to work as normal conditions would allow.Your CRF commish hasn't given up, so don't give up on your Commish and your CRF. As Maverick this may be in anarchy times but the CRF will hold true to tradition and show why the lineage of history has made the CRF stand out from its peers. Can you accept the challenge? What do you as a slammer get out of this? You get to prove that you are the better person! The CRF board has been reset already, Maverick says follow the rules , and Thor has chained himself to the ring. CRF and all of Cslam do not give up, if Cslam is going to shut down, at least you will be able to go down with a fight I'm not one to spread rumors Gossip none
