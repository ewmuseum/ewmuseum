CCWe Friday Fury Card and more

18 January 2001

CCWe The Gossip Column Gossip here with the new CCWe OOC BOARD,(link at boottom of page) Maxx Justice has been busy reshaping the CCWe, at least you could do is check out the site and voice an opinion, and now for the Friday Fury Card and Gossip's own predictions and commentary. Live Friday January 19th From The Kemper Arena Kansas City, Missouri CCWe Presents CCWe BK Title Match Brutal(c) vs Chevalier 3 vs 3, point system used in case of a tie. [Gossip] This will be as close as they get, can the new BK Champ retain or will the power of Chevalier overcome Brutal? Look for Chevalier to give a good performance but fall short as Brutal retains the belt. CCWe UD Title Match Massacre_sr(c) vs Aragorn 2 vs 2, Aragorn slams the tie. [Gossip] The UD Champ will take an easy win here. Aragorn will be overmatched in this title contest. #1 Contenders Tag Titles Bad Meets Evil (Prototype & Alex_asylum) vs Righteous Violence (Chevalier & Bomberman) Slam the other teams slammers once each, point system used in case of a tie. [Gossip] Bad Meets Evil is gonna stomp a mudhole in Righteous Violence and walk it dry, why Bomberman remains in the CCWe is without a doubt a crime, someone call the paddy wagon and haul his carcass away. B-Man there is a reason they call it ELITE, you are taking up valuable space. #1 Contender US Title Bigbad_momma vs Big poppa 2 vs 2, Big poppa slams the tie. Big poppas last chance to prove himself on the board. [Gossip] Once again this is a no brainer, the Higher Powers of BBM will prevail, words of advice Big poppa get to the arena or Maxx Justice will bring out the hook, again its the Elites activity is a must. Send the CCWe a postcard when you get booted back to the majors. #1 Contender BK Title Krotch vs Alex_asylum 3 vs 3, point system used in case of a tie. [Gossip] HAve to give the nod to Krotch on this one, the only way Krotch doesn't win the BK Contenders shot is if Prototype can somehow manage to set the tempo of the #1 Contenders Tag Team match. Non-Title Match Luvgate vs Deathkiller 2 vs 2, Deathkiller slams the tie. [Gossip] Did you know that Luvgate quit the Knights over Bomberman getting a boost off of him, and then recently decided to stay, talk about Fickle as a woman, I'm going against the odds on % and give the win to Deathkiller. Cage Of Justice Match Rattlesnake vs Vengence 3 vs 3, point system used in case of a tie. Interference wins reversed. For those that dont know, the Cage of Justice is a steel cage wrapped in barbed wire. To win you have to climb out over the top and reach the floor. [Gossip] This is going to be the match of the card to watch, this feud heated up over the weekend and continues as we speak, if both slammers perform as well as they were trash talking look for this match to overshadow the rest of the card. Who doesn't love a cage match, good call Maxx Justice on this one. Winner will be if he survives , ah who cares get in there and rip each others heads off. Parking Lot Brawl Reject vs Fey_lucifer 2 vs 2, Fey_lucifer slams the tie. Both former BK Champions and both have a liking for the extreme matches. [Gossip] Both former BK Champs, both former CEW slammers, back then Fey Lucifer schooled a young Reject, or does the fact that Reject went to the CCW Elite before Fey Lucifer eat away at him? Got to go with experience here, Fey Lucifer will come from behind to school Reject once more. Triple Threat Match Hardleg vs Sapper vs Deathwish Slam each slammer TWICE each, point system used in case of a tie. [Gossip] Will this Triple Threat Match turn into a handicap match? Look for Deathwish to be the deciding factor in this match, remember Hardleg never underestimate your opponents or overlook the obvious. Notice everyone in the CCW Elite is on this card except Ashlira ? Guess she found out the hard way, its a man's world sweetheart. [rumor] Will ASHLIRA retire, thats the rumor going around. This has been the gossip column, direct all questions and news to Robert Yell (CCWe Reporter) [detail withheld] I'm not one to spread rumors Gossip CCWe OOC BOARD
