xx The new month in iHEL gets rolling on Thursday. Thursday Chaos Thursday December 7'2000 From the iHEL arena in Tampa Florida World Title Contender Three Way Dance Unicron Realsexy Sure_Thing Slam each of your opponent once TV Title Contender Four Way Elimination Match Guest Referees:Sure_Thing&Ignoramus Scruffy Juggby Ember Sturgeon slam each of your opponents once With two guest referees this should be pretty interesting Each referee will have one slam they can use. They can use it against anyone and if they win, the person they are helping out gets the win added to their total. If they lose the person they slammed will get a win added to their total. I want you to role-play those slams (preferably do a role-play for the initial interference and then a role-play for the results). Pitfighter Title Bout Shawn_King vs B_twelve 3x3 (slam your opponent 3x) Dafunkster is stripped of the title due to not being active. Shawn and B will battle to see who gets to be the new Pitfighter champion. Grudge Match Spam On A Pole Match Kydd vs Hyper_Blade 3x3 There will be a pole tied to one of the corners with a can of spam attached to it. Whoever grabs the can of spam is the winner. Other than that there are no rules in this match. If Hyper_Blade wins i'll put him in the World Title match(making it a three way dance) If Kydd wins he gets to have Pamela as his valet for two weeks. Lethal Lottery Round One Matches Hardcore Rules Scruffy&Realsexy vs Ignoramus&Unicron Shawn_king&Sturgeon vs Fire_flame&Mr_blonde Sure_thing vs Kydd&Ember Thepete&Hyper_blade vs Sloose_canin&Sic_freak In each of these bouts you will slam each of your opponents once. Sure_Thing will fight handicapped because his partner had an unforseen emergency. In that bout Kydd and Ember will slam Sure_Thing 1x each and Sure_Thing will slam each of them once. iHEL OOC Board
