CWO match 216: Daos vs Volume

undated in the source
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PreMatch Comments Dawg Pound. The crowd is deafening here tonight! Daos climbs out of the ring and grabs a chair from ringside. Volume catches him coming back in and...dropkick! Daos slammed HIMSELF in the face with the chair! Daos with an atomic drop...blocked! Volume connects with a powerful spinning mule kick! That'll turn your lights out for good! Figure four by Daos! Extremely punnishing...now Volume is trying to reverse it! He rolls over and the pressure is put back on the legs of Daos! Both men feeling the effects! Volume goes for a backdrop...Daos puts on the brakes and nails him with an underhook suplex! Volume whips him into the ropes...Daos ducks under the clothesline...from the other side...Daos with a dropkick to the knee! Volume is going for a crucifix. Can he get it!?!? Daos breaks free...spins around...full nelson! Great counter! Volume whips him into the ropes...Daos ducks under the clothesline...from the other side...Daos with a dropkick to the knee! Impressive displays from both men at this point. Volume with the speed advantage while...Daos rocks him in the corner with an avalanche! No place to go!!! Volume looks like he's going for an inverted atomic drop here. Wait! Daos is biting his face! This is a side of Daos that I've never seen before! Daos slaps on the sleeper! Volume is fading...the ref comes over to check! 1...2...the hand comes up! Daos releases the hold and delivers a forearm to the kidney for good measure. Volume goes for a roundhouse right...backdrop by Daos over the top rope! Daos climbs out after him and slaps on a cobra clutch! The fans are on their feet! Now he throws Volume back into the ring at the ten count. Test of strength in the middle of the ring now. Daos twists his wrists...hands to the mat...and Volume gets his fingers stomped! Volume with a small package! 1...2...kickout! It's going to take more than that! Daos looks a bit dazed. Volume climbs the turnbuckle...Daos snaps out of it and catches him in a great fireman's carry! Volume whips him into the ropes...Daos ducks under the clothesline...from the other side...Daos with a dropkick to the knee! Volume off the ropes with a cross-body...Daos catches him. But he can't hold him! Both spill over the top rope! Hard bump, but both climb back into the ring. Volume shoots in with a European uppercut. Blocked! Daos turns it into an armbar. He's really applying the pressure. Volume looks pretty confident. He climbs to the top and...moonsault! Daos catches him and slams him to the mat! Great Power! Daos whips Volume into the ropes, setting up for a monkey flip. He telegraphed it WAY too early! Volume gives him a stomp on the head for good measure. Volume throws Daos into the ropes. He goes for a hiptoss...but Daos puts on the brakes! Slaps on an abdominal stretch! Volume is complaining about a hair pull and the hold gets broken. Volume off the top rope. He took way too long to get up there...axe-handle attempted and Daos delivers a big fist to the midsection on his way down! Daos is complaining to the ref about a hair pull. I don't think he's buying it. This crowd is really divided between Volume and Daos. All managers have been barred from ringside so hopefully we can settle this feud once and for all! Volume slams him to the canvass...he goes to climb the ropes and Daos grabs the back of his tights! Rolls him up! 1...2...shoulder up! Daos stuns him with a punch...bounces off the ropes...leapfrog by Volume...on his way back...Volume nails him with a Frankensteiner! The crowd goes nuts! Volume takes him down with a leg trip. Like a cat to the top rope...Big elbow from way up...but Daos rolls out of the way!!! Nobody home! Volume whips him into the ropes...Daos ducks under the clothesline...from the other side...Daos with a dropkick to the knee! Daos with the headbutt...he stunned himself! Daos falls down! Volume to the top rope...BIG legdrop from way up there! 1...2...NO! Close! Volume slams him to the canvass...he goes to climb the ropes and Daos grabs the back of his tights! Rolls him up! 1...2...shoulder up! Looks like Daos is hiding a foreign object in him boot. Volume doesn't seem to notice, though. Daos with the headbutt...he stunned himself! Daos falls down! Volume to the top rope...BIG legdrop from way up there! 1...2...NO! Close! Volume takes him down with a leg trip. Like a cat to the top rope...Big elbow from way up...but Daos rolls out of the way!!! Nobody home! A clothesline to the back of the head sends Volume to the mat! Incredible speed as Daos is right on top of him...STF! Again, Volume refuses to submit and the hold is broken after 1 minute. Daos whips Volume into the ropes, setting up for a monkey flip. He telegraphed it WAY too early! Volume gives him a stomp on the head for good measure. Wow! Daos is getting into it with a fan on the outside! Apparently the kid is wearing a Volume t-shirt! Daos with a stiff chop to the chest...goes for a second and Volume rocks him with an enziguri kick to the head! Daos reaches around...looking to lock in an abdominal stretch...BIG hiptoss by Volume! Volume has his legs...big slingshot coming up! Daos won't budge! He turns it into body scissors and powers out! Great move by Daos! Daos goes for the pin! One...foot in the ropes. Is that a "SCOOPS" sign in the third row? This feud has been heating up for awhile now..they lock up...double eye rake!!! Both men are blinded! Volume rebounds out of the corner with a flying forearm...but Daos is waiting with a stiff boot to the face. Volume is screaming at the ref that there was a hair pull on that last move. Daos sneaks up...and jams his fingers down Volume's throat! Mandible claw!!! Daos down to the canvass...Volume comes off the ropes with a big splash. Daos rolls out of the way! Nobody home! He gets up and slaps on a bow and arrow submission hold! The ref is asking...but Volume refuses to submit! Volume is charging Daos with a clothesline...Monkey flip! Man that Daos is fast! It's Daos with a nerve pinch. He's digging those fingers in...and Volume takes him down with a shoulder roll. Nice counter! Daos goes for the pin! One...foot in the ropes. Is that a "SCOOPS" sign in the third row? Daos slaps him! Volume is just standing there laughing. Now he starts to walk away...turns and NAILS Daos with a flying forearm! Daos slaps on a headlock. Volume can't seem to power out...he lifts him into a side suplex! Up to the top rope and...BAM! Big elbow from fifteen feet up! Volume is perched on the second turnbuckle waiting for Daos to turn around...flying clothesline! Right on the money! Volume connects witha European uppercut...Daos nails him with one of his own! They're going punch for punch in the center of the ring! Volume backs him into the corner with a series of knife-edge chops...climbs the ropes for what looks like a...jumping DDT! No, wait...Daos has a firm hold on those ropes and Volume jumps down. No damage. Daos whips him into the ropes and goes in for the clothesline...Volume ducks it...off the other side and a beautiful cross-body block! Only a one count. What does he have there? A roll of coins! Volume clocks him in the jaw and about twenty bucks in change flies all over the ring! Daos with the headbutt...he stunned himself! Daos falls down! Volume to the top rope...BIG legdrop from way up there! 1...2...NO! Close! Incidentally...we have a late breaking story on Volume, but it's too hot for Java! You'll have to call the hotline! Daos executes a belly to back suplex...steps over...into a Boston Crab! Volume manages to get his arm over the bottom rope. Lucky! Volume whips him into the corner...he's right behind him. Daos leapfrogs behind and catches him in a chicken wing! Volume is going out! No. He manages to slip his foot between the ropes. Daos looks tired. I don't think this wrestler is going to last much longer! Wait a second, did you see that. DID YOU SEE THAT!! Daos must have leaped 10 feet off the mat, landing on Volume. Now both wrestlers are down...1...2.. Hold on Daos has muster up what little strength this wrestler had left and is applying the Nocturnal Emissions. It's all over!!! Daos has won a match to go down in the record books. Winner: Daos
