Edge THE ROAD TO CWZ "The whole Fed starts the race, but only one man finishes it." September 30th Live from Madison Square Garden LOSER LEAVES THE FED TAG MATCH Highway 4:10 vs Strange_Seed/Dyingbreed Someone get the hook Strange_seed & Dyingbreed takes the Highway 4:10 out of the iMWA I saw this before it happened. Seed and Breed take the win. Could they be your next tag champion contenders? PUT UP OR SHUT UP MATCH Rentahero vs ICON ICON wins The winner has bragging rights and the loser can only say good things about the winner for 24 hours.I never would have known. I miss this one. This was a great match. Icon showed that the hero doesn't always win. HOLY SCRIPTURE FINAL Last Man Standing Match Winner of match one vs winner of match two Damion made it to the final to walk away the new Holy Scripture ChampionI could not prdict this one after the first round was no contest. Damion wins one match and take sit all. Easy win win for Damion, but he is not complaining. I know I wouldn't. EVENING GOWN MATCH Amundson vs Living Dead Girl/ Rumor Amundson takes away the win and kept the dress. Once again I was right. I enjoyed this match the most. Seeing Liv was .....Watch the encore. TELEVISION TITLE MATCH Rayne(c) vs Swat Pup Swat_Pup you better have gotten plenty of rest to no show this card Rayne has once again proved why he deserves the promo, good luck in the CWZI was screwed with this one. You are on my list now Swat Pup along with Moses. SATURDAY'S DOUBLE MAIN EVENT PEOPLE'S CHAMPIONSHIP MATCH 3 Story Cage Match The_Crow(c) vs Ynk vs Morbidangel vs Icon Crow has proven that he is unstoppable since winning this belt. Can nobody derail the PEOPLES CHAMP, The_Crow puts another win in the history books. I know my stuff. Crow wins easily. I would put my money on Crow everytime. LOSER LEAVES THE IMWA MATCH Mr Arse vs The_Crow Not only did Mr Arse lose the iMWA WORLD TITLE but lost the loser leaves fed match as well (ironic isn't it) good luck in the CWZ Mr Arse and The_Crow will be giving up the iMWA WORLD TITLE , which now is in my office, who will start the month off as the World Champ?I did not pick a winner but Crow took away the win thanks to some help by Shocker. Also making an apperance and helping out Crow was no other then Rap start Eminem. This was the match that had people on there feet. Amundson came out after Crow was declared the winner and put his finishing move on Crow. Then he took the belts and laid them on Arse. Crow still won the match though. There was tension between these two as a stare down happened once Crow was back up. Many questions came from this match. tune in to iMWA to see the answers. A great card and a sold out crowd. This was an exciting card and you can bet on more greta cards to come with a commish like Moses. The heat is rising in iMWA right now as slammers try to get ready for a cut. After winning this match Crow was stripped but he keeps his People's belt. Moses is now getting ready to choose who will get a chance at the belts. Look for a great month by iMWA and expect this fed to cause some ruckus. none
