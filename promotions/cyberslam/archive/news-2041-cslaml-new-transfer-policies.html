CSlamL New Transfer Policies

22 January 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Freedom Cyberslam used to allow transfers via Credit Card, however some people violated the system with fraud & we no longer accept card payments. We devised however a transfer system which will alleviate this. The following are the rules: #1 No minor to major transfers. #2 You may however transfer down a level. #3 You are allowed to request a lateral transfer with one exception. If you already have a slammer in that fed, you are going to be denied transfer. #4 In order to transfer you need the permission of the commish of the fed you are in & the commish of the fed you wish to transfer to. #5 Once permission is granted, each of the commishes sends a quick email to Our President of Promotions (Currently Brodie) to state the transfer is occurring. #6 The slammer making the request also emails Brodie using the following form: Slammer Name: Email: Trooper: Current Fed: Fed to Transfer to: Permission Granted by: (Put commishes names here) If you falsify this in any way your transfer will be denied. #7 At the start of every month, Your transfer will take place then and ONLY then. Do not request for an immediate transfer. #8 The only way to get to a major fed is by earning a promotion. #9 Feds are allowed ONLY 3 transfers in. Which means if a fed already has 3 people approved to transfer, all other requests are either denied outright UNLESS the commish who approved the transfer in dropped one of the transfers he had already approved. The commish is responsible for informing the dropped slammer of this. #10 In addition to this, Majors now may draft a maximum of 6 slammers. The Elites will still be based on application & the CFL will still be based on Invitation. #11 Requested Demotions count as draft picks. Slammers will need to arrange this with feds & commishes. #12 Commishes may deny a transfer in for valid reasons only, commishes may deny transfers out ONLY if their fed is low on slammers. none
