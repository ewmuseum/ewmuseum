UIWA - An End Of An Era

2 October 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Ebediah Springfield The end of an era has come in the UIWA as the UIWA is losing arguably the greatest slammer in its long history dating back to its days as an independent federation. And that slammer is none other than the UIWA Ultimate Finals Representative for September Whipple Jr. Whipple jr was extended an invitation to the Elites by CWFe commissioner Judge just before he went to the finals. From what I was told, Whipple really struggled with the decision to accept the invitation because of his loyalty to the UIWA. Whipple jr is a man that was given 5 consecutive promotions to the majors while the UIWA was still an independent fed only to have turned them down or transfer back to the fed her loves. Whipple was more than just a slammer. He was the man that led this federation. He claims the the UIWA is the house that Whipple Built and those claims are not that far fetched. Although he was a constant at the top of the standings, that did not define Whipple. Whipple was the most active slammer in the UIWA. He is what made the federation go around as just about ever angle in the fed revolved around him. A weekly article by me was the UIWA Top 10. It was a list of the top 10 flashers in the federation as voted on by the slammers of the UIWA. Ever since I have done this article, there has never been another slammer at the top of this list other than Whipple Jr which proves although he may not have been liked by his peers, he was respected for his hard work and dedication. A man that held the Universal Heavyweight Championship Belt for 4 consecutive months, and for 4 separate times for a total of 7 months, It was the times when Whipple DIDN'T have the Universal Title that truly made him magical as he was a better flasher without the belt. Whipple Jr will always be a fixture in the UIWA as his legacy will continue on. And it is safe to say that the UIWA will never been the same without him. UIWA Website
