iRCW Fontsize

10 August 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Typeface Typeface here and, well, we've managed to throw together a show for you today. Don't ask me how. In Rage news, "Jolt" has selected their tag-team opponents for tonight: The team being called "The Phenoms", Thunderbolt and Mister Zinc. This coudl very well be a title-reign-ending decision for the Knights tag-team, but whoever wins this match will definately prove to be a dominating tag-team. In other news, Andy MacDaddy has been seriously injured folowing an attack by a large masked man who powerbombed him twenty feet through a table. No clues as to who this masked man is yet, but right now the MacDaddy is expected to be too injured to defend his Southern States title. But then, when was the last time a doctor caused a title to change hands? And in Fontsize news, there's currently a poll up at the iRCW webpage for who I shoudl interview next, and the winners are... well, it's a tie. So, everyone who voted, go back and vote again, but only for Infinite or Sugar, the two highest vote-getters at this point. After the weekend, I'll set up an interview with whoever has the most votes for next week's Thursday Fontsize. Now, for our semi-monthly... **Stable Report** As has been the trend in recent months, Damage Inc is still the dominant stable in the iRCW. Their in-your-face attitude and comradeship has made them the centerpiece of the fed, even enduring the recent betrayal by stable re-co-re-founded Mister Peak. Their current roster consists of Manowar, leading Freak, Haz, and Andy MacDaddy. Rumor has it that Thunderbolt and Exterminator have both applied for membership in this fan-favorite stable. Running nearly equal with Damage Inc in the fan-favorite area, but lagging far behind in ring strength, is the Coldbloods. Once the iRCW's "Best Stable" for several months, the group seems to be unable to recover from a string of losses and off-and-on inactivity. The roster is made up of The Infinite, leading Page, Snoball, Keebler, and Hfly. Rumored possible recruits include Sugar, Goalie Bill, and Mister Zinc. A distant third on the list is Resident Evil. This fairly new stable has been around for roughly a month, and although they only have three members, have shown that they an be quite a powerhouse, even if their mic skills need some polishing. If they ever find someone to teach them the rules of the game, they could be one hell of a force. Their membership right now is Speed leading Zangeif and Darius. Possible future members may include Static and Greenhell Junior. And finally, we have the Knights. Technically, they qualify as a stable, even though there's only two of them. We all know about the Knights, CSlam-wide, so there's no pont going into it here. Sugar has shown time and again her great promo skills through her relationship with Manowar, while Twelvevoltman is expected to beginr epeating his long title reign as TV Champion. And then we have the others, the non-stabled. Kiddpain, the last remaining member of the iRCW Mythdaddies and The Infernal Machine, would appear to be the most likely of all the independants to form a new stable (after all, two's a feud, three's company, four's a crowd, five is just plain fun), and he's got a wide number of possible recruits to choose from, including The Evil Knights, Made For Syn, and the names mentioned above as possible recruits. That's all for today's iRCW Fontsize. Fontsize may or may not be heading your way tomorrow with Rage results... and if we do air tomorrow, we'll have quick profiles on each of your iRCW champions. If not, we'll be coming at you with a double-card Fontsize on Monday. Until then, you keep on slamming, and I'll keep on reporting. none
