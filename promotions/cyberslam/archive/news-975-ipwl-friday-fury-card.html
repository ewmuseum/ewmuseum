iPWL: Friday Fury Card

11 August 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Guybrush Threepwood Hello again! This is Guybrush Threepwood and I have the Friday Fury card for the iPWL. I know it's late but it wasn't my fault(like the website being down). So, not to make it anymore late, let's get to it! UNDERCARD LETHAL LOTTERY 1. Dusk & Superfly v Barnibus & Disco Fever Good Ole Boys on opposite sides? Superfly the only POD member present? Barnibus looking for sheep? Prediction: Dusk & Superfly. This should be a close one but I see Dusk & Superfly edging out the team of Barnibus & Disco Fever. Of course, the fact that Superfly, Pod member and Dusk, AofA member will probably not get along makes my prediction worth nothing. 2. Regulator & Sidx v HourFather & Zoll POD outnumber the AofA in another match. How will the rookie figure in? Prediction: HourFather & Zoll. Not sure if Regulator will return. That makes it a rookie coming off of suspension versus another team that is made up of POD/AofA. 3. Kindred & J Money v Rev & Neo Again, two Pod members in a match that has one member of the AofA. J Money is representing Compton Glory. Should be interesting. Prediction: Kindred & J Money. The hottest rookie teamed up with a member of Compton Glory. Those two sides don’t have a real strong feud going. The other team of Rev and Neo is again an AofA/POD team, so you’ll never know what will happen. 4. Krush & Bulbasaur v Calupa & Deathwish A ring full of wild cards, and Web of Deceit on opposite sides of the ring. Prediction: Calupa & Deathwish. Don’t know how this will end up. But what I can say is that a member of the WEB will be victorious. 5. Texas Ranger & Lieutenant v Oatmeal & Kryptik Kryptik is added due to the sudden disappearance of Aftermath. This match is an odd mix to begin with. Prediction: Texas Ranger & Lieutenant. Oatmeal hasn’t been seen since he came back from suspension, Kryptik will be wrestling in the lottery for the first time. If Oatmeal doesn’t show up look for Texas Ranger and Kryptik to team up and beat Lieutenant. RANKING CARD Ranking Matches: 1. Barnibus v Disco Fever Struggling for World Title contention. Prediction: Barnibus. This one is just a pure guess as it could go either way. Maybe it will depend on whether Disco Fever is Canadian or not. 2. Kindred v Neo POD battle to move up in the North American division Prediction: Kindred. He has been having a great month and I don’t see it stopping now. However, Neo is due for a victory so don’t count him out. 3. Vinesaur v Malibu Bob A rookie against a no-show artist. Prediction: Vinesaur. This is based on the fact that Malibu Bob hasn’t shown once. I don’t see him breaking that streak. 4. Puppi Dog v Lunatic Two relative rookies battle to move upwards. Prediction: Lunatic. Don’t know about this one either, but I pick Lunatic because he won a rewrite match and that’s about it. 5. Scott v Malibu Bob_ Two more newbies trying to get ahead. Prediction: Scott. Different spelling, same person, this one also no-shows. MID CARD 1. HourFather v Major Lord In a “READ THE DAMN POLICIES” match. The winner is the person who incorporates the majority of the policies into their roleplay. Prediction: HourFather. This match was made for Major Lord as he never follows the policies. I say he still doesn’t. 2. Geneside & Major Lord v Compton Glory (Lieutenant & J Money) In a Hardcore tag team match, requested by the World’s Most Hardcore Team! Prediction: Compton Glory. A veteran tag team would have trouble with Compton Glory, a team of rookies is almost impossible to get a win. 3. Calupa v Texas Ranger As the other half of the WEB starts to seek his own revenge! Prediction: Calupa. This should be a close one but I just have a feeling Calupa will pull this one off. HIGH CARD 1. Kryptik (c) v Bulbasaur As the leader of the WEB tries to become the first three time champ! Prediction: Bulbasaur. I would really like to see someone get crowned three-time champion. Bulbasaur has that chance again! 2. HourFather (c) v J Money In what appears to be a growing feud between HourFather and… the whole damn fed… Titles are on the line. Prediction: J Money. HourFather has pissed off a lot of people in this fed and it wouldn’t surprise me if someone interfered in this match just to teach HourFather a lesson. 3. Lieutenant (c) v Superfly v Kindred A triple threat cage match. Prediction: Lieutenant. He has held onto this belt for a while and even though I have a feeling it will change tonight, I still see it around Lieutenant’s waist. 4. Rev v Armagggeddon The newcomer earned his shot at the longest reigning champ in the iPWL. Prediction: Rev. He has defended the title amongst all challenges and still has kept it. I see Armagggeddon putting up a fight but will eventually be defeated. 5. Texas Ranger v Sidx Fresh off of suspension, Sidx finally gets a title shot. Prediction: Texas Ranger. The veteran is just too much for this rook. Well, there you have it the card for iPWL Friday Fury. Hope this card lives up to expectations. So, for now this is Guybrush Threepwood signing off. none
