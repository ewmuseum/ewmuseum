Rayge =|( IIWF RAYGE REPORT )|= Its finally been decided! The decision has been made and four indy slammers' lives are gonna be changed forever as they are brought up through the ranks of the indies into the major, the CRF. Of the four promotees chosen by CRF Commishener, Kronus, each month as of late the majority has been taken from the iIWF. Last month it was Scourge and Redtide, the month before, Idiotstick and OneFatMotha. Who has gotten the call this month? Its Wednesday August 30th of the year 2000 and this is your iIWF Rayge Report. Instead of me telling you who got the promotions, I've decided to just let them speak for themselves here. I contacted each of the individuals and asked for them to make some comments, so here they are. CON_VICTOR: My name is Victor... CoN Victor. I am a average sized man, with above average skills. I have been in the iIWf for about a month... I started toward the end of July. By the end of my first week here, I had a tag belt, the Hard-core Belt, and the infamous Legacy Belt (a belt given to a newbie by a veteran who is going to be promoted... in hopes that the belt will later be pasted to the next prospect.) But I knew this was just the beginning, at one point, I held The Hard-core Belt, The Tag Belt, The Legacy Belt, and The Heavy Weight Belt.... Yes, I earned all this during the course of one month. I am just that great. When I first got here, I had serious heat with Scourge and Redtide... members of the GoV. Our egos were to big... we could not exist together. But eventually, Redtide, stable leader of the GoV, gave me an opportunity I could not refuse... He offered me a position in the GoV, To stand on Ivy's side (a member of the GoV) and help him lead the GoV after Scourge and Redtide were promoted. After the two men left, The GoV went down hill. Ivysaur was a terrible leader, he was letting people in and out of the stable like he was a revolving door. He was shuffling the belts around, taking belts from one slammer and handing them to another. Eventually, Ivy left the GoV, joining its archrivals, the Impact Playaz. Ivy left me alone in the GoV with the worst flasher in the league.. Thorn. I remained loyal, and took the full burden on my shoulders... I tried my hardest to take the GoV back to the top of the league, cause right now, they where scraping the bottom. It was hard to recruit the first member into the GoV, but eventually, I found a great flasher... his name was Dexter. He was the biggest nerd I have ever met. Our whole angel was to make him cool. Eventually, we did... he became cool in his own way. All though we never go him to move out of his mother's house. The GoV grew to three men when I recruited a midget named Andre... That's right, Andre the Midget. He was a former friend of Dexter who wanted to join the GoV. I thought he would be perfect. We met our fourth member, Moe in Baskin Robin's... he was working there for a second job. The poor guy got a pay cut from the commish, and was forced to take an extra job. He eventually joined the GoV, and is now getting paid enough to buy anything he wants. The GoV has transformed many lives... it took in the men nobody wanted and made them into forces that no one could handle. The GoV made Dexter cool, it made Andre have the courage of a giant, and it gave Homeless Moe a home. The GoV came form the bottom and now has become the most influential stable in the league. Later on the GoV re-recruited Ivy... It was a shaky decision, but inside I felt that he could only help us. Now that I have been promoted, I am worried. I don't want the GoV to fall back to the bottom... I want its legacy to reign in the iIWF for ever. I needed a new leader... a man who can keep the GoV in the promise land. After mush deliberation, I picked a man, a guy who I met in iDSLW... Max Factor. He is a man who I can trust to lead the GoV... I have no regrets. Over my month stay at the iIWF, I have developed many allies and enemies. The GoV members have become my closest friends...We have done everything together. We go to parties, drink beers, and [edit] women. But then there is the flip side... the enemies. I have develop a lot of heat with slammers such as Extreme X, The Korn Kid, Dark Angel, and Death Knight... these people knew how to irritate me. Extreme X is the biggest {edit} in the FeD. He is constantly whining about how he never gets promoted... about how he has been here for a year, and is constantly getting passed on the promo by newbies. He thinks the whole world is plotting against him... I think he sucks. Then there is Korn. If I could explain him in a few words I would say he is a hard working terrible flasher... He is constantly posting, but every thing he flashes is boring and uncreative. Next on my list of foes would be the team of Dark Angel and Death Knight... these guys are sad slammers with an even sadder story line. I don't know what they are trying to pull off. They try to act like some demons from the darkside... please, the closest you have come to the darkside is the shade under a tree. These slammers are terrible. I have been in a lot of great matches... a lot which can go into the record books... but only one stands out to me. It would have to be the total rewrite match between myself, The Korn Kid, and Extreme X for the strap. This match, in my opinion, was the best main event in iIWF history. It took the three best flashers in the league, who have been involved in constant heat, and put them in a skill only match (rewrite) to see who would come out the Victor. At the end, when all was said and done... I came out on top... I won the belt... it proved just how great I was. My favorite memories, where with the GoV... transforming Dexter into the suave guy he is. I took Dexter shopping, and taught him how to act cool. It was a lot of work, but after awhile, Dexter grabbed hold of the concepts... now this guy gets more girls then me. I also had fun on the night I first found Moe... he met him in Baskin Robin's... we helped him get a pay increase. It was fun taping him to the roof of my car and driving him around. But before I go, I would like to say a couple of good byes. I would like to say bye to my commish... Baz, defiantly the best indy commish there is. I would like to say bye to The GoV... Max Factor, you have a lot of work ahead of you. And lastly but certainly not least...I would like to say bye to The Korn Kid and Extreme X... you helped me create very interesting. I am looking forward to the CRF... I have a lot of friends up there, like Redtide and Scourge... I have heard a lot about the great flashers like Idiotstick, and Batman. I am really looking forward to kicking some [edit] in the majors. So... here I come. FADE TO BLOOD THE_KORN_KID: Ive been in the iiwf for about 7 months. The only belt i have ever held is the iiwf world title on 2 occasions. Ive tried to be cocky as it goes for the game, but thats not really me, i just do the opposite of what i would do in real life. Ive never really liked or hated anyone, seeing as i can never stay in any type of tag team or stable for longer then what seems like 3 weeks. My favorite match would have to be my first major title shot, that was the hardcore title match against scourge. When i joined and turned on the gov would be my favorite moments. In the crf, lets just say i dont plan on staying there long. I will win that ultimate title before the end of the year, thats a promise. Id like to thank everyone for giving me something to do with my time other then sit around or get drop on my head(hehe), especially baz since he was the one to bring me back from my suspension that had gone over. I guess thats about it, thank you. The 4th man to get the promo, that's right, all four indy promo spots went to the iIWF, is Death_Knight. Now, Death_knight was not available for comment and as much talent as this guy has (and trust me, he oozes it) this reporter feels he needs more time in the iIWF before "earning" a spot in the CRF. He has been here less than two weeks and as one half of BoA has torn the league apart and captured Tag Team gold. Where does this leave his partner Dark_Angel? It's amazing that the other three indies, the iNWA, the iHSWA and the iMGF, are SOOOO horrible that a guy whose been in the iIWF less than two weeks gets a promotion over a top star in any fed, such as, the iNWA. I'm proud of Death_Knight's accomplishment, he certainly would've gotten the promo next month along with his friend. But now is not supposed to be his time and I think it should be made an official all across CyberSlam rule that you must be in a federation for one complete month before being promoted. It's good the iIWF has a large enough new talent pool to be able to survive this 4 man promotion and next month I expect at least three more will be promoted. There you have it, so to recap, the Promotions from the iIWF to the CRF for the month of August are Con_victor, The_Korn_kid, Extreme_x and the man who snuck in the back door, Death_knight. Congrats to all you've done a great job this past month and are all deserving. It seems once again the iIWF is tapped to its roots for talent. Who will get the nod next month? Well there's many options, its going to come down to the hardest worker that's for sure. So till the time for speculating arrives again, don't turn the page, till you've read the rayge, take it easy!
