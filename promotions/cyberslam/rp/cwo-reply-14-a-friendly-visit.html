A friendly visit

a CWO flash, captured 23 May 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Paingod The cameras catch up with the Master of Cheap Heat as he approaches the locker room of Big John. *Knocks on door* *Impersonates Hickish voice* Hey! Is a Biggie John here? Who's there? I didn't order anything. This is a delivery from a Sweet Sue to a Big John here. Umm okay then, hold on. As BJ heads to the door, PainGod grabs firing estinguisher from the glass safety box to most likely rock the daylights out of him. Okay whe..!! HERE'S YOUR PACKAGE! BJ gets a rude "hello" as PainGod nearly takes his head OFF with the extinguisher! As PainG gets ready to make another attack with the weapon, BJ regains his senses and gives the Master of Pain something to think about with a low blow! As PainG lies in pain on the floor, Bj grabs the extinguisher, brings it over his head and brings it donw... ONLY for it to be sent STRAIGHT BACK at his FACE! PainGod kicks the extinguisher to give BJ a nice implant into his head! With the second shot, Big john is finally out cold after surviving through a massive hit from Asylum. *Holding crotch* Ahh you son of a monkey's uncle!!! That HURT! *Still holding crotch* Heh, at least your arse isn't going to be 100% at Fury HAHAHAH! PainGod heads out of the locker room holding his injured groin and laughing on his way out. Who will get the last laugh at Fury?
