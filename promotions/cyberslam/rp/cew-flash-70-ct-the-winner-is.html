CT: The Winner is?

a CEW flash, captured 28 May 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Arlo: Fish is rushing at Fati.. Erick: Clothesline to the back of the head by the DreamWild champion! (Fish picks his belt up, and begins to walk towards the arena. Fati begins to show signs of life, as the fans motion for Fish to turn around.) SMACK! Arlo: Fish just nailed Fati with that title! Fati is back down! Erick: Figure Four! Fish is applying the pressure to Fati's leg...that's the same leg that was injured at the FPV! (Fish applies the pressure for quite some time, and then let's go and makes his way to his feet. Slowly, he picks Fati up, and places Fati's arm around his head.) Arlo: Wait, I've seen this before...SMURF KILLER! Erick: Fati is down, but there is no ref. Fish can't win the match like this. Arlo: Wait, look at that! (Fish motions for a kid on the streets, wearing a referee's shirt by chance, to get on his hands and knees and count the three.) Erick: 1....2....3! Fish did it, he stole another victory from Fati! Arlo: And this time he's got the belt...He's getting out of here just as fast as Fati did on Monday! (Firefights appear in the background, approaching to check on Fati...life slowly begins to come into the Samoan as the Volunteer workers bring him back into the real world. Then, the firefighters turn around and remove their gas masks...) Arlo: WAIT! Those aren't FireFighters! Fish had better look out! Oh Geez....Who could it be?? Bigfish
