At the 160 (Amundson - All)

a CWZ flash, captured 24 January 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Gator sitting at the 160 is fuming at his ex-partner Amundson Gator sitting at the 160 is fuming at his ex-partner Amundson. Tending bar like most always Crazyfool is just passing shots of "Rumplemintz" down to his friend and fellow Proofer. Gator - That son-a-gun may have cost me my shot at the next round. He shows up and gets whooped by a couple of Xtascy members, my god how 187 has fallen. Crazyfool - What did you expect? I mean he is 187 and they are all worthless Gator - Yeah, I know that but still he normally puts up at least somewhat of a fight. Gold God my rump, more like Golden Shower. Crazyfool - Hahahaha, good one Gator Gator smiles at his friend who for some reason can always get the Cajun cheerful. (must be all of the drinks that he shoves down the bar). Just as the two Proofers are laughing it up at the expense of Amundson, Martini comes on the air to announce the winners of the first round of the Lethal Lottery. Gator - Here we go now I have to listen to Martini tell me I lost. Crazyfool - Stop being so pessimistic Martini - First I want to thank all of you for supporting the CWZ Gator - Man he sure likes to kiss backside Martini - The first round of the Lethal Lottery is over and here are your winners. The winners will now move on to the second round and one step closer to the prize, a shot at the CWZ title currently held by Proof member Test Gator and CF raise their glasses in a toast to their brother. Gator - Alright already get on with it... Martini - In the A pool, moving on will be Crazyfool, Nature Boy, Damien, and Bulldog Gator - Way to go man (as he slams another shot) Martini - Now on to pool B, Psyduck and Test Gator - At least two Proof made it in Martini - Khris & Ibe_man Gator - Darn it Martini - And Gator & Amundson Crazyfool - See you panicked for no reason Gator - What the heck he let both of us move on? Crazyfool - Guess he knows talent when he sees it. Gator and CF both raise their glasses and slam their drinks down in celebration of there moving on to the second round. Gator
