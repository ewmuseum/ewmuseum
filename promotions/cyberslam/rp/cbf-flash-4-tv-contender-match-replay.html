CyberSlam CBF flash board

TV contender match (replay)

a CBF flash, captured 17 May 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Michael Solo enters the arena dressed in his street clothes. No mask, no wrestling attire, only his black denim shorts, leather jacket, an Outkasts t-shirt, and his trademark shades... and for some reason he also carries a nice shiny metal Easton ball bat. As he makes his way down the hallway, he's absconded by Scheme Gene. Gene: Michael Solo, you had said you were NOT going to be in this arena tonight... and now, here you are... what gives?? Solo acts as if he is about to put the bat over his shoulder only to cock it back and bring it full force around with a swing that almost knocks Gene's head off. Gene slumps to the floor like a sac of potatoes. No longer hindered, Solo makes his way to the arena entrance. OnceBeaten stands in the ring, calling out Solo. As Solo walks thru the curtains, the crowd gives him a mixed reaction of mostly boos. Solo hops on the apron and Once goes to his corner to prepare for the match. Solo walks purposefully straight across the ring still carrying the bat and as OnceBeaten is talking to ring girl, Solo walks up behind him, and brings the bat full force around the back of Oncebeaten. Oncebeaten doubles over and is met with another bat blow to the back of the head. He slumps to the floor unconcious. Solo grabs the Referee by the collar and tells him to call for the bell to begin the match. The referee refuses until Solo brings the bat up close to his cheek. The referee calls for the bell, Solo tells him to start a knockout count. As the referee counts, Solo exits the ring and heads to the back. The referee hits 10 and the bell is rung once more signifying the end of the match. Festee: Well.. he showed up, but, he sure as hell didn't seem happy about it. Lord Mayes: That man is a menace. But, he is also your winner by way of knockout. Michael_solo
