Spitting Mad! (From Krynn)

a CRF flash, captured 9 November 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Backstage Baggie can hear a loud *BANG* come from the arena Baggie: Alright that is ENOUGH!. Baggie stomps off to the general direction of the ring, just before he gets through the curtains another loud *BANG* goes off, making Baggie jump backwards. Baggie: eep... Oi You... Sound person. Baggie walks over to a small geeky kid... Maverick only employs people who dont want much wages Baggie: Who is out there making all of that bloody noise? Trainee: That would be Krynn sir... it's time for his daily interview. Baggie: This... SUCKS! Not only does he get TWO bloody big pyros, but the crowd are BOOING him. I had to run Thunderbolt out of the fed to even sell one damn t shirt... ok, I have had enough of this. Baggie storms off towards the ring, grabbing a maic backstage before he walks out through the curtains Baggie: Ok Cryin, It's about time you and me had a little... huh? Baggie looks at the ring and sees there is nobody there Baggie: Oh great... and he has the whole "teleporting to the back" gimmick too... I am really gonna have to see Mav about this.... newbies should NOT be allowed to have all of the cool gimmicks... Baggie waves to the crowd who are wondering why he came out here, then slowly slinks back off to the backstage area... to find either Krynn or Maverick Paperbagman
