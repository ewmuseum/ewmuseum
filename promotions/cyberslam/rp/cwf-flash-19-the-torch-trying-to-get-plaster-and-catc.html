The Torch, Trying To Get Plaster, And Catching A Movie(whoever)

a CWF flash, captured 28 October 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Inferno sits on the Couch in the Proof locker room. Between his legs sits an almost empty bottle of "Jack Daniels" On the floor are empty bottles of "Maker's Mark", "Southern Comfort 100 Proof", "Bacardi Spiced Rum", and 12 empty Budweiser bottles. The Human Torch picks up the bottle of Jack and puts the straw in his mouth.....(Yes, I said Straw......He wears a mask with a slit over the mouth.....DUH!, How else could he drink it?) As he sips the Jack, He turns up the Volume on the Remote.... Batts: Now Go get your ****in Shine Box! Tommy: MOTHER****ER!...I'm gonna Kill... Batts: Come on! Come on Tommy! Henry: Come on Tommy, Back off.... Tommy: You keep that Mother****er here! You hear me Henry?(Tommy leaves). Batts: What the hell is his problem Jimmy? He Disrespects me and I'm just kiddin around over here! Jimmy Conway: Nope....Nope...You insulted Him... Batts: No I din't, I didn't Insult him. Jimmy Conway: Yes you did....You insulted him a little bit there... Inferno continues to watch as Tommy soon comes back. He and Jimmy start to kick the ever lovin Dog **** out of Batts. Inferno smiles under his mask a bit.....then looks down at the almost empty bottle of Jack..... Inferno: I just don't get it.......All this booze and I'm still not drunk.....Go figure....I'm probably the only Proofer in history that can't get Drunk.......*sigh*.....Wonder how those Playoff's are going? The Torch pushes a button and changes the channel to CWFTV. In the ring, We see Justinsane pinning My ***...er...I mean Your_Butt. He downs another sip of the Jack and kills the bottle....He reaches over and grabs a bottle of "Crown Royal". Inferno: God I feel Bloated....Oh well, I must try.... Inferno spins the top off and drops in the straw. He switches the channel back to his Movie as he continues to sip his beverage... Tommy: Hey Ma, Can I borrow this Knife? Tommy's Mom: What do you need that for? Tommy: The Deer got it's....what's that called....The foot... Henry: The Hoof. Tommy: The hoof, It's stuck in the grill and I gotta pry it out... I wonder, Anyone know what Movie I'm watching? It's pretty easy. Anyway, Tbc by anyone who wants to start something....I'm bored now that Freeze has gone into seclusion. Inferno
