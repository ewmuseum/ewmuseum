Natch Meets with Doc Pain

a CWZ flash, captured 21 October 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Natch is dressed in his $3,000 suit. . .wearing his $5,000 shoes (never understood why the shoes were so expensive . . .but I digress)and riding the elevator to the 22nd floor to talk t Doc Pain. . .the elevator opens. . and there is the CWZ headquarters. Natch walks off the elevator. . .and he is greated by a lovely blonde secretary. . . Secretary: Mr. Flair. . .Doc Pain is waiting to see you. . .walk this way. The secretary slinks along in her short skirt and stilletto heels and Natch smiles and says: Natch: Honey if I had a swing like yours it would be on my porch. Scretary: *glares at him* Cute. Opens the door and there is Doc Pain drowning in paper. . .charts on the walls. . .his staff buzzing all around him. Secretary: Mr. Flair is here to see you Doc. Doc: Good! Natch! Come on in. Have a seat. How's my Heavyweight Champ getting along? Natch: Real fine Doc. Real fine. Doc: Natch its time to make your decision and I know you will continue to defend the CWZ belt with honor. . just like you have done in the past. Natch: Doc, I'm glad to have this belt back. . .its been a loooooong time since I've had a chance for this belt. . .and I won't be giving it away. Doc: Then you aren't going to the CXZ? GREAT!!! Natch: I didn't say that Doc. Doc: What? Natch: Look Freebird has committed to the CXZ and Prophecy has committed to the CWZ. SCUM is divided. I have to choose between my tag team partner and my friend for the last two years. Its not easy. Doc: Yes. . .you and Prophecy go way back. Both great members of the CWZ. Natch: Thanks for the compliment Doc. Prophecy and I go back to the days before Stickler. . .we hung out when there was a Plant Reaper and an Atomic ZimZum. We were CWZ all the way. Doc: I know that, and I'm glad I have your support now. .. I certainly need it. . . Natch: Not so fast Doc. Through all that time. . .while the federation was promoting the Proof. . .and Test and Lepercon and DeadGirl. . .and Mystique and Baccarol and Black Prince and Sephiroth. . and even Phantom "freaking" Joe. . .who got the money? Who got the money and the title shots Doc??? Do you know??? Doc: Well. . it always changed. It depends on so many factors. . . Natch: It didn't change Doc. You saw to it that the Naaaature Boy wouldn't get the belt. . .you tried to keep SCUM down! We aren't paid adequately. . .we aren't given the proper respect. . .and when it all boils down. . .respect and money is all that matters. Doc: But Natch. . . Natch: Don't "but" me Doc. For all those reasons. . .the Nature Boy is heading for the CXZ. Doc: You are kidding. . right? Natch: *standing up . . .and pointing his finger into Doc's chest* I'm deadly serious. I'm going to the CXZ and I'm keeping the CWZ title until someone beats me for it. So take your loyalty crap and shove it up you ***. WOOOOOOO! Natch stands up and looks at a stunned Doc. . .and smiles. Natch: Now where's that secretary with the fine rack? WOOOOOOOO! Natch turns and walks out of Doc's office. . .and looks for the secretary on his way to the elevator. Nature_boy
