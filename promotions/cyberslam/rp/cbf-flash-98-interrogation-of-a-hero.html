Interrogation of a Hero

a CBF flash, captured 16 January 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The Masked Hero sits alone in a dark room somewhere in the BlastTown Police Department. A door opens and the police commissioner and an FBI agent enters. Masked Hero: Finally! Gentlemen, what is going on here?! Commissioner: We were hoping you would answer that for us. Masked Hero: What? FBI Agent: Mr. Hero, it is no secret that BlastTown has been the victim of a crime spree. A crime spree involving a man in your uniform. Commissioner: We understand you have been more than helpful in bringing criminals to justice. Look, why don't you just become a cop in your spare time? We don't appreciate this vigilante stuff. Then something like this crime spree comes along and look at the mess we've gotten ourselves into. FBI Agent: Commissioner, you digress from the business at hand. Masked Hero, is there any way you can prove to us that you did not commit those crimes?! Masked Hero: I thought we had a justice system that stated one was innocent until proven guilty. Commissioner: We're not saying you're guilty of anything, Hero. Just give us a reason to send you home. The Masked Hero turns away angry and thinks. FBI Agent: Can you prove your innocence? The Masked Hero turns back around and pulls off his mask. The police commissioner stares at him in shock. Commissioner: L-Let him go. FBI Agent: What?! Commissioner: It can't be the guy we're looking for. I'm letting him go. Get out of here, Hero. The Masked Hero puts his mask back on and walks over to the door. Masked Hero: I don't want any of this to leave the room, gentlemen. And I never, ever want my integrity questioned again! Is that clear? Commissioner: Yes sir. Masked Hero: Good. Now stand aside! There is villainy to vanquish! And a villainous impersonator to aprehend! The Masked Hero walks out of the BlastTown Police Department on his way back to the arena. He only hopes he can figure out who has been behind this crime spree before they srike again. Masked_hero
