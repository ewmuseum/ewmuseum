Top Story, 12 February 2003

12 February 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Top Story - Wednesday 12th February 2003 Revolution Wrestling Set To Revolutionize PTC? 2 Weeks after the owners announced Revolution Wrestling will open rumours have been running wild whether or not RW will enter the formidable PTC organisation. We caught up with the new owners who had this to say. "Well we can confirm we have had contact with the owners of Primetime Central but no deal has been made yet, we are hopeful a deal will take place within the next week or two." Show Latest - Wednesday 12th February 2003 Adrenaline #1 Thw owners also confirmed today that Revolution Wrestling's first edition of Adrenaline will be one to honour the past xCw stars, Revolution Wrestling replaced xCw on the map last month and officials confirmed that they shall indeed do this.
