Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Revolution Wrestling took over a federation abbreviated xCw in January 2003 and announced itself two weeks later. Its own top story of 12 February has rumours running wild about whether it would enter Primetime Central; the owners confirm contact and say no deal has been made yet, and that they are hopeful of one within a week or two. By April the alliance’s own affiliate wall lists it.

Its first Adrenaline was carded for the Fargo Dome on Wednesday 26 February 2003 and booked no matches at all. Instead the federation gave the night to the roster it had inherited: Wrestler of the Year, Feud of the Year, Tag Team of the Year and Best Moment, each with a list of nominees drawn from xCw’s own October 2001 to October 2002. The nominees for Best Moment include finding out that one of its own wrestlers owned half the company, and the night xCw went solo.

The card ends by saying more awards could be scheduled and that the wrestlers due to appear were not yet confirmed. Two pages of this federation were ever captured, and on the second of them the results line still reads 00/00/00. How it ended isn’t in the surviving record.
