BBCW Trick or Treat to a Beatdown pt3

Nov 1, 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Narcissist is sitting at pool side he is wearing black jogging pants with the word "Legend" down the left leg and the word "Killer" down the right they are written in white. He is also wearing a red bandana, and black running shoes] Narcissist - Sometimes I wake up in the morning and think to myself, damn I'm I really this good, then I look in the mirror and the answer is YES I am this good. Let it be a lesson to all the superstar in the Big Balla Championship Wrestling, I took a virtual Super man and the Unknown, I am talking about the Super Unknown and I took him out of action permanently. Lets have a little flash back here in my short yet very fruitful stay in the BBCW... second match in the BBCW I win the intercontinental belt, then I go on and on on the road to virtual beating everyone that steps in my way, and despite all odds with everyone jumping and beating on me...at the Last Drive By I took out a legend of the sport. Super Unknown you were the smart one for hitting the road, well smart and scared of Narcissist, the rest of the BBCW should follow your example and get the fuck out. Now at Trick Or Treat To a Beat Down I'm in the match of a life time, the top 10 BBCW superstars get the chance of getting a glimpse of the true meaning of perfection and when your all old and on wheel chair you can all brag to your grand kids that you once had the chance of getting into the ring with the greatest and BIGGEST megasuperstar of all professional wrestling. Let me put it to you this way, I came to the BBCW to become the very first grand slam winner. This sport is becoming saturated with over the hill as beens, you got guys who think they are Hollywood stars and the biggest name in wrestling, well the big foot and the leg drop arent in my wrestling arsenal...but I am the true biggest name in the game. I aint the best there is the best there was or the best that there ever will be...but I am the better of then all. Tiny and all of your Castaways feg friends can all just go hang yourselfs, I want your belt and what I want I get, even if I have to go through all the Castaways to get at you Tiny the job will get done... [camera fades out]
