(12 noon in Auburn Hills, Michigan. A Chartered Bus arrives at the Palace. There are fans standing outside waiting to get in to see WCW World War 3. The people outside look over at the Charter bus. On the side of the bus BBCW is written. Another charter bus pulls up behind it. 200 people get out the buses all wearing BBCW merchandise. It looks like a stand offthe thousands of WCW fans are on one side and the 200 BBCW fans are on the other. A BBCW fan yells, "We're taking over!" A WCW fans yell, "This is NWO country and we are just Too Sweeeeet!" Tension begins to build. President Davis stands ontop of one of the buses. ) President Davis: We are not here for trouble. We are here to help you out. These 200 hundred are just a sample of the millions of fans BBCW has. These 200 hundred wanted to help you guys out. They saw what a great day it was ond htey didnt want you to sleep through it. And thats what would happen if you watch WW3. He are here to bring you THEE Alternative! (A truck comes behind Davis with a huge TV. Scenes from last years WW3 are shown. ) President Davis; This is what you are paying to see. If you give us the opportunity this is what we will give to you. (Scenes of BBCW action begin to show. The fans look interested. ) President Davis: But you paid to see this. (Shots of Hogan and Piper) President Davis: So we will leave you alone. Fan: NO!!! We dont want the Old Boys! Fan: Fuck the Big Boys we want the Big Ballas!!!! BBCW! BBCW! BBCW! BBCW! (WCW/NWO fans start tearing up thier posters and merchandise. A helicopter drops BBCW merchandise and the fans put it one. They all chant BBCW as they head into the Arena) (Outside with AJ Parker, Andre Beauman and Ms. Leonard) AJ: Well it looks like BBCW has taken over Auburn! Only Presidnet Davis! AB: Thats the truth. Its a good thing. What better place than the Palace for a team of royalty a team of class like IFS to take thier spot as the most dominant stable. AJ: What ever there are 12 other teams who have something to say about that. ML: I have a question was that match between Hogan and Piper shown 12 years ago. AJ: No Ms. Leonard that was last month. ML: I could have sworn I saw it before. AB: You did! AJ: Lets take you to this special package on the teams participating tonight. Voice: Stable Warfare started in BBCW between two factions the Castaways and the Tourniquet. (Shots of The Tourniquet and Castaways brawling) This spawned group after group to be formed in BBCW. (Shots of each stable) Before long gang warefare had gotten beyond control. The two begining factiosn will be in the house tonight to try to prove that the foundations of BBCW stables is the strongest. But which is the foundation the Castaways or the Tourniquet? Tonight the Castaways have selected the former US Champ Tiny, former US tag champs Stealth and Crow and the leader of the stable Rat. Tourniquet has the former two time TV champ Havoc, the showstopper Vigilante, former Ultimate fighter Leviathon and the current IC champ Extreme Exodus Adonis. But that's not all other stables in BBCW fell they have equal claim to the most dominate stable title. The first being No Limit Souljahs. No Limit had two teams going in but due to the injuries of the world tag team champs and the Evil Souljahs leaving No Limit have consalidated in what stil is a pretty effective unit. Nas will join Carnage, the new Cruiserweight champ Tanaka and the World Champ C- Murder. Da Posse on the other hand started with one team but they have too grown. D Willis, Dre Samson, Heavy G, and Da Pantha will represnet one team as Kool, Zavier, Maverick and a yet to be named man will bring up the rear. A new stable has formed and have caused nothing but headaches for everyone in BBCW. The champions from other organizations feel they canrun all over the big balla's because they are Simply Irresistable. And they have seleted 4 men to represnt them tonight. They are Nature Boy Nick Bamond, Hot Shot Rex Carter, Sadistic Bowler and former TV champ Bounty Hunter. A sworn enemy of SI is the family of Mo Thug. Curse, Mangler, Redeemer and Devil's Child will try to prevent SI form winning while keeping their hopes alive. There will no "Amore" shown to the Itailan Facist Stable. Il Duce's troops have been on a roll in single competito but how will they fare as a unit? Will Class be enough to take them to victory? Light up the refer the Third World Order is here. The Regulator, Sicillian and Un Civil War better be high as a kite to survive the brutal environment that they will be in tonight. A brand new team Dungeon of Death has come to BBCW. We dont know anything about them but the President was impressed with them when he saw them in a trip to Vegas. Howver this isnt Vegas how will these men fare? Some men dont have allegance to established stables and thugs President Davis has formed two Wildcard teams. The second is made up of 3 new comers all making theier debut. Crusher, Spoonmaster, and Dynamite will use this forum to mae a beinging impression on BBCW. Super Unknown was advertised to be on this team but that was a misprint. Super Invader an establihsed wrestler in BBCW hopes to guide this team to victory. The final team had no alligances but after Cris Taylor joined SI his moties ae in question. We have already seen him fight with Wraith. can the TV champ and Mr. Hearbreaker but theier differneces aside to team with SilverBlade? And more importantly can these 3 deal with the egomaniac Narcisisst? Not only are bragging rights on the line but so are title shots. Yes title shots!!! But he has changed it to every member of the team will get a shot at the title of thier choice and only a member of the participating team can get a shot! All this in one match with 4 rings?
