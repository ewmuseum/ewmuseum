Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Another federation calling itself the International Wrestling Organization opened from a personal Xoom-hosted page in May 1998, its own founding notice dated 14 May and corroborated by a later post, five weeks on, stating the federation had been running for a month. It described itself plainly: “a booked federation made of only fake wrestlers.”

It planned a single weekly show, Saturday Night Madness, with a Wednesday card promised once the roster grew large enough, and meant to open with an IWO title tournament at its first event. “The IWO will be as realistic as possible, with the main focus on international wrestling,” its founding notice said, before asking recruits for singles and tag-team wrestlers alike.

Five further posts followed over the next seven weeks — calls for a vice president, tag-team entrants, and staff, and an application form — restating the same recruiting drive without adding a card, a result, or a named wrestler to the record.

No tournament, show, or closure notice survives. Its own last trace, an application call from 3 July 1998, is still an open door.
