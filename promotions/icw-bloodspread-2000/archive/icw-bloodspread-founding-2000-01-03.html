{ICW} - International Championship Wrestling

3 January 2000

[name withheld] here, once again, looking for more grapplers for ICW. ICW
Bloodspread, our innaugural card is on the 8th, and we already have a stellar card with lots of top names. There's still room on the roster for anyone that wants to join, and we need tag teams. Let me stress that REAL wrestlers WILL
BE REJECTED! This is a FANTASY league, with FAKE wrestlers. No "Steve Austin" no "The Rock" no "Rob Van Dam" or "Chris Jericho". And no rippoffs. Don't come in calling yourself Tripple L and using a Pedigree as your finisher and doing crotch chops.. We want quality roleplayers with good characters. We need tag teams, so if you handle a tag team or have been thinking about it,
ICW's doors are open. ICW's titles are very prestigious, but ICW isn't just about the titles. It's about angles, it's about fueds, and it's about having fun. However, roleplaying will ALWAYS have priority over angles, unless all involved handlers agree to otherwise. Roleplays are sent to a mailing list, or can be sent to myself, which will be forwarded to the league. ICW Bloodspread this Saturday will host a Battle Bowl to crown a new ICW World Heavyweigth
Champion, so sign up before you miss the opportunity of a lifetime! ICW Already has around 23 wrestlers, but that's not enough. We're always looking for more. The bigger the mailing list, the bigger the roster, the better. ICW has just gotten into swing, and people are roleplaying, and it's looking like we're going to be a very prominate league. Thanks for all of you helping me out, and special thanks goes out to ICONOFECW and BUDICERULZ for plugging ICW in When World's Collide 3, and making this all possible. You guys don't know just how much of a hand you really had in all of this ;p
International Championship Wrestling is out to get more roleplayers, but let me stress this... recently I had to remove somone from the mailing list because they had an attitude of "I don't have to impress you with my skills, your league has to impress me". Now.. he was on the mailing list, but would not fill out an application, because he was "Waiting to see what the league had to offer". Now, i'm a nice guy, I try to be, but I just don't want to put up with stuff like this. ICW is a fun league, it was in the past, and it will be again. If you want to be added to the list just to check out some stuff, that's fine, but don't have the "I'm above you all, i'm just seeing if you can hang with me" attitude about it, then you're gone in the blink of an eye. We don't have to impress anyone. If you feel that we're not "good enough" for you, then look elsewhere by all means. E-wrestling was never meant to be like that, it was meant as a hobby for fun, not your life. ICW is all about having fun, and having an enjoyable experienceas an e-wrestler. If you would like to be a part of ICW, or help out with booking, cardwriting, or whatever you think you could help in, then please contact me at
[detail withheld] . Thanks [name withheld] ICW President
