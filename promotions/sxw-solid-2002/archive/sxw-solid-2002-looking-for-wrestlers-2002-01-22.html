Welcome to the sXw (Solid eXtreme Wrestling). We here in the sXw are not to strict about our rules, but we do ask that you follow them. If you think you may like to join after reading the rules, scroll to the bottom and fill out the app. To see the rest of the site, check out
http://sxwrestling.cjb.net/ 1.) Roleplaying Roleplaying is the key to having a successful federation. The Owner(s) of the federation can only do so much to get his wrestlers to roleplay. But it is really up to you. All we require from roleplaying, is just that. You need to roleplay to keep your spot in this fed. Seeing that this is a real and fake federation, we will be a little more stricker with this rule then an all fake federation would be. There are many people out there who can RP really well with someone like The Rock. And I would like to put on of those people on our roster. But if you want to keep the spot of the Rock.. you need to roleplay well, better than the other person.
2) Content This is another must. When you roleplay, you need to have content. Plan it out before. For those of you who don't understand what I mean by content, here's a small definition. Content is what you talk about in your roleplay, whether it be a challenge or an invitation, content makes an rp a piece of art.
Anyone, Anywhere can send out an RP and have it say.. Triple H I challenge you at the Royal Rumble. That's not content.. that's crap! We know you are talented and you can RP well if you put your mind to it, and that is why you are in this federation.
3) Language Most wrestlers tend to use bad language, or curse words, in their interviews.. well we here do not mind if you curse.. all we ask is that you stay in character.. you will never see Kurt Angle say the 'F Word' on TV. So we'd expect our Kurt Angle, not to say the 'F Word'. Now if you have a created character.. well, we don't know his character to well, so if your character curses every other word, all we advise is to slim it down as much as possible.
We don't mind cursing, but there is one thing we do not tolerate, and that is Racial Comments. You will be fired on the spot, that is the only guarantee I will provide. 4) Strats Strats are a good way to let us know your character is still alive. And a good way to start an angle. Strats are anything you would see a wrestler do outside the ring.. except rps. In other words.. A strat is either an interview, an attack, or anything else. We don't require strats, although it is a good way to let everyone know who you are and that you are with us here on earth.
Unless of course, you don't want your character here with us (mentally) on earth. Please make strat's realistic, don't make your wrestler talk from space, or make him give an interview 3000 miles away from the arena in which an event is taking place. The perfect place for a strat is in the arena.
5) Email Everything is done through email, if you do not like that, then you can leave. Please, don't send out 5 emails a day.. try and include everything in one email. And no Mass Mailing. Do not take the fed's email list and start sending out those crappy forwarded messages, we all know none of those are true.
Those are the rules, we don't ask for to much as you see above, but we do ask that you follow the rules as closely as possible.
