Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

ICEWF opened on 4 July 2001 with a founding post that got ahead of its own newsgroup's fatigue at yet another launch: “Yes, it's another new fed. Drop by and read the rulebook. If you like, join. If you do not like, don't. Simple as that,” hosted first at a go.to address.

The very next day, its operator posted a change of address, moving the promotion to a new efeduniverse.com page and noting the move the way a postal forwarding form would: “we have to put those in at the post office, so I guess I'll put one in here, too.”

Those two posts, a day apart, are everything that survives. No rulebook, roster, or card is held, and no closing notice is known.
