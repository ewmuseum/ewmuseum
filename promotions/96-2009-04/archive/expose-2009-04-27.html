- - - - [Commonwealth Championship: Chad Vargas vs. Makayla Fox vs. Salim Raj] - - - - The bell rings and the three competitors circle each other, each eyeing the other two to the best of their ability. Fox decides to strike first, lunging towards Raj, but he’s intercepted on his way in by Vargas, who slams him down to the mat. Raj nods to Vargas, and they both pick up Fox and drop him again with a Double DDT. Fox attempts to get away and crawl to the other side of the ring for a breather, but Raj and Vargas each grab one of his legs, dragging him back into the center of the ring. They apply a single leg boston crab each, and after a few seconds of Fox attempting to fight out of it, seem to realize that they can’t win working together on a submission hold, each dropping their leg and locking up above Fox.
Raj manages to win the grapple with a quick knee to the gut, followed by an uppercut. Vargas stumbles back, and Raj charges in, just in time to get caught with a stiff clothesline. Vargas is on the offensive now, lifting Raj up and then executing a picture perfect suplex, which Raj rolls through into a pinning predicament for a one count. Both men stand up and the audience is firmly behind Vargas, chanting his name. They prepare to lock up once more, but Fox is up and runs at them for a double clothesline, perhaps realizing too late that they see it coming.
Both men lift Fox up by one arm and drop him down to the mat with a sickening thud, executing a double team Razor’s Edge. They proceed to begin to focus on one another again, exchanging lefts and rights, uninterrupted as Fox is right out of it. Raj ducks a wide left from Vargas and attempts a neckbreaker, but is reversed and put into position for a DDT, which is then reversed again into a dropsault from Raj! The crowd shows it’s appreciation for the maneuver as both men get to their feet, circling once more.
With both Fox and Vargas down, Salim Raj lines them up beside one another. He then climbs to the top rope and calls to all of the fans. Raj leaps off the top rope with a corkscrew moonsault and lands on both Vargas and Fox. He then pins Fox.
1..2..! He just barely kicks out but Raj isn't deterred. He gets Fox back up and shoots her into the corner. He runs in and hits Fox with a wheel kick, causing her to stumble out of the corner. Salim then shows amazing atheleticism by jumping up to the top rope and leaping off with an inverted flipping neckbreaker. Fox is down and Raj pins him again.
1..2..! Vargas breaks up the fall and then gets Raj up and dumps him over the top rope to the outside.
Vargas gets Fox up and whips her into the ropes. When she comes charging back Vargas turns Fox inside out with a clothesline. Fox is down but Vargas gets him back up. He grabs Fox's arm and lifts her up for a backdrop but instead of dropping her backwards, Vargas spins her out into a powerbomb and pins him!
1..2..! Fox just barely gets her foot on the bottom rope. She gets back up and dumps Vargas over the top rope to the floor. Raj charges at Fox, who hops onto the apron, hits Raj with a shot and then springboards into the ring and goes for the Elysium but Raj pushes her off his shoulders. When Fox turns around, Salim Raj nails hr with the Jai Ho. Makayla is down and Salim pins her. 1..2..3!

- - - - [Elite Championship Match: Ahriman vs. Jinx vs. Kent] - - - - Jinx and Ahriman immediately runs towards Kent, double teaming him with punches to the back. Kent stops at nothing to defend himself as he exchanges punches with each of them. The two newcomers then kick Kent together in the gut before sending him towards the ropes. They set up for a double clothesline, but Kent counters the attempt with a double clothesline of his own. Kent then looks at them as the crowd gives him a pop for the move. Jinx and Ahriman both slowly get up and Kent then goes for Ahriman, hitting him with chops to the chest. He throws Ahriman into the corner and goes for a shoulder tackle, but Ahriman then moves out of the way, having Kent connect with nothing but metal. Kent falls on his knees, holding his shoulder as Ahriman turns around to a sudden dropkick from out of nowhere by Jinx. Jinx goes and connects with a follow-up leg drop. He goes for an early cover, but Ahrima kicks out before the count of two. Jinx then grabs Ahriman’s arm and locks on an armbar submission, having Ahriman struggle to get to his feet. Ahriman tries to get out of it, but each time, Jinx makes the hold tighter. After a couple of minutes, Ahriman slowly gets onto his feet, making the armbar even more painful for him. He falls back onto the mat as he screams out in pain. Suddenly, Kent runs over and hits Jinx with an elbow drop, making him unlock the submission. Kent starts stomping Jinx to the ground as Jinx struggles now to get onto his feet. Kent then picks up Jinx and sets him up into a suplex, but Jinx slides out of it and connects with a single arm DDT. He goes for a cover…1 – 2 – kickout by Alex Kent! Jinx looks at the referee and grabs Kent’s head as he sends him into the ropes. Kent stops himself at the ropes, but Jinx hits him with a clothesline before he can even turn around. Jinx starts to hype the crowd up as he turns his attention to Kent, who suddenly gives him a kick to the gut followed by an enzuiguri.
Ahriman makes his way towards the top rope, in which he notices Kent slowly getting back up on his feet. He turns his attention towards him and flies off the top rope with a flying cross body, landing right on Kent. The two superstars struggle to get back on their feet as the fans go wild. They both get back on their feet and start exchanging blows with each other, but then get knocked back down as a rejuvenated Jinx runs towards the outside, landing on both with a flying body press over the top rope. All three are now laid out on the outside as the fans start chanting “96” throughout the arena. Jinx slowly makes his way back up to his feet and grabs Ahriman’s head. He then goes for an irish whip towards the ring steps, but Ahriman counters it and whips him back towards him, connecting with a nasty looking big boot, nearly taking Jinx’s head off. Ahriman picks Kent up, but Kent pushes his hands off and start throwing punches to the head of Ahriman. He then irish whips Ahriman towards the barricade, which causes Ahriman to fly over into the crowd. A groggy Jinx slides back into the ring as Kent makes his way back in and kicks him in the abdominal area, causing him to fly about a feet in the air. Kent bounces off the ropes and connects with an elbow drop to the chest, then picks up Jinx’ head and locks on a sleeper hold. Jinx struggles for breath as Kent adds more pressure to the hold. The fans start stomping their feet and clapping for Jinx in order to hype him up as Jinx slowly fades into unconsciousness. The referee picks up Jinx’s arm and drops it, signaling a 1 count for the submission. He repeats this as he watches Jinx’s arm drop to a 2 count.
The ref repeats this one last time in which Jinx’s arm stops in mid-drop and the newcomer starts trying to get more and more energy into his system. He slowly rises up from the ground and hits Kent in the gut with a couple of elbows before running towards the ropes, connecting with a flying elbow. Jinx springs back up to his feet as he sees Ahriman slowly getting back up on the apron and he runs towards the ropes and connects with a flying elbow on Ahriman, having him fall back onto the outside floor. Jinx jumps up onto the top turnbuckle and waits for Kent to get back onto his feet. He then flies off the top rope and connects with a missile dropkick onto Kent. Jinx goes for the cover…1 – 2 – kickout by Kent! Jinx then grabs his head and screams out in frustration as he hovers over Kent and starts laying him out with punches to the temple. He then picks up Kent and sets him up for a front suplex, but Kent then gathers up enough energy to counter the move with a northern lights suplex, setting Jinx up for a pin…1 – 2 – kickout by Jinx.
Ahriman slides back into the action with frustration and hits both men with a standing moonsault as they are laid out on the mat, setting up a pin on top of both men…1 – 2 – both Jinx and Kent push Ahriman off, sending him flying into the air a couple of feet. Ahriman looks at the men in shock as he lands on his knees and storms towards Jinx, hitting him with a headscissors takedown. he then turns and hits Kent with a clothesline. Ahriman screams out as the crowd gives him a huge pop. He slides out of the ring and grabs a couple of chairs from under the ring as Kent and Jinx slowly get back onto their feet. Jinx notices the weapons being thrown in the ring and runs towards Ahriman, connecting with a baseball slide onto the chair, sending the chair straight into Ahriman’s face. Ahriman falls back onto her back as Jinx slides out of the ring to get him. He tosses Ahriman back into the ring, which makes Kent take advantage of the situation and go for the pinfall, but Jinx immediately pulls Kent out of the ring and slides back in to try to make the cover. Kent pulls Jinx’s leg and sends him out of the ring. The two men look at each other with anger and start having a shoving bout before Maximus lays Lariett out with a haymaker from out of nowhere. He slides back into the ring and goes for Marie, but Marie immediately jumps out of the way and gets Kent with a sidekick, having him spreadeagled on the floor.
