> > Ok, I'm just as much against short lived so-called "hardcore" feds, > > but every single time a fed opens, you flame them and assume it'll close > > in a month. Just because a fed falls under the hardcore category does > > not mean it'll fold in a matter of weeks. Case in point: Extreme Fantasy > > Wrestling. EFW is and always will be a hardcore fed, despite the fact > > that it's a real wrestler fed and it's hardcore, doesn't mean it was > > necessarily short lived. EFW has been open since January 1, 1997 and > > continues to remain open to this day. So, Jammer and whoever else is > > jumping on the Anti-SHEF bandwagon, lay off the flaming. Not every > > hardcore fed will fold within weeks. Give them a chance to prove > > themselves before you pull out your flame guns. > > I must agree with you on this one Yeti. These idiots really dont know > what they are talking about most of the time. Lets look over the facts. > The EWA most likely the best fed ever ran. By the Infamous Rick Baptist > is still going strong and is as extreme as ever. Its called Extreme > Championship Wrestling for a reason boys. As for you idiots flaming > peoples feds. What gives you the right? At least these guys are getting > out there and trying. You just sit around critizing where you have no > right. Hell if you want to critize so much why dont you come to the EWA > (Considering they let you in) and see how a real fed is run. The > Hardcore stuff isnt bad its just done alot. But what hasnt been done > alot. The NWO has been done to death. And yet you still do it. Well I > went off enough thanks. Please note (AGAIN) a few things about Anti-SHEF postings: 1. We only tell a league that it will fold if it INSISTS on calling itself "the greatest", "the best", or something similar before it puts out a card. I think prisoner109 is the BEST example of setting your sights too high, too soon. Many fold in a month. 2. The Anti-SHEF has no problem with Hardcore OR extreme feds, but when these seem to be the ONLY selling points, the product trades blood for quality. I've seen how good extreme wrestling can be, but when someone advertises that the debut match of the fed is a battle to the (literal) death... We are against the extremely extreme. 3. Unlike certain other e-fed critics (whose initials are JJ), the Anti-SHEF is made up primarily of fedheads who KNOW what we're talking about. There have been a number of times that I've offered pointers to fedheads who decide to email me directly to defend their fed (i.e. try to start a flamewar). When they realize that I'm not trying to troll, many ask my advice on how to better run their fed. Just a few clarifications...
