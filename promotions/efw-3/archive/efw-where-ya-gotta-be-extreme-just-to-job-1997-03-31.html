EFW is without a doubt, one of THE MOST EXTREME feds on the net. We are a real wrestler fed and roleplay based. (meaning the lazy bastards out there can't sit on your ass and still win.) We have one card a week (until I get more writers) and a PPV every month. EFW features some of the most innovative ideas (Four ring, Six team War Games match) and most extreme angles (A manager kidnapping stable called The Stable) and a good mix of excellent RPers and beginners. We are completely uncensored and commentary is done by the Insane Clown Posse (when they ain't beating the shit outta someone) The fed has been open for three months now and is run by "Hollywood" [name withheld]. Although we are not accepting apps for new wrestlers at this point, e-mail me if you want on the mailing list. After you get just a taste of EFW and the extremeness of it, you'll be hooked. If you want good family entertainment, then go elsewhere ya silly little puss. But if ya want blood, gore and kick-ass action, then send an email to me and say YES to EFW and NO to wholesome feds!!!! -----"Hollywood" [name withheld] Handler of NWA and PIW's Maniac Mike Manson, VCW's Johnny Briggs, DEWF's Wimpy Ford, Bungholes, Elmo Todd, and Spanky the Talking Monkey God.
