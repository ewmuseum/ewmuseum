Extreme Fantasy Wrestling... is *BACK*

10 November 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
*********************************** * _______ _______ /\ /\* *| _____ \ | _____ \ || ||* *| | \/ | | \/ || ||* *| |___ | | || ||* *| ___> | |___ || /\\ ||* *| | | ___> || // \\ ||* *| | | | ||// \\||* *| |____/\ | | |// \||* *|_______/ /___\ |/ \|* *********************************** * Extreme Fantasy Wrestling * *********************************** On Monday, January 13, 1997, a television show aired which would change the way people looked at wrestling, and totally redefine the term "hardcore." The show, was Hardcore TV. The organization that ran said program, was Extreme Fantasy Wrestling. On that night, fans saw the Horsemen and the Crips meet for the first time, beginning a war which would last for five months... Two days later, EFW went to the extreme again, and this time, it was the Horsemen and the Crips in a gang war, on Wednesday Night Wars. Kevin Nash, Sting, and Steve Austin all went to the hospital that night, with deep lacerations that required a grand total of 37 stitches. Extreme Fantasy Wrestling, however, never made alot of money, especially under the iron fist of Jack Tunney. But it was at the 2nd EFW pay-per-view, Bloodlust, that Extreme Fantasy Wrestling hit the bigtime. The card was highlighted by many incredible events. It was on this night, that the Insane Clown Posse joined EFW. Dennis Rodman made his professional wrestling debut, against Rob Van Dam. The battle between the Lethal Alliance and The Stable raged on, as Diamond Dallas Page became the only person in EFW history to win the US Heavyweight title twice, with his victory over Xanta Klaus. It also saw Public Enemy winning the World Tag Team Championship, Jim Ross dying of goat-inflicted injuries, the Anaheim Highway Brawl between The Yeti and JunkYard Dog, and a huge war between the Horsemen and the Crips, in a cage. Also on this night, The Giant defeated Taz for the EFW World Heavyweight Championship. As time went on, EFW got more exciting, more violent, and the proceedings in the ring got more bizarre, as managers were kidnapped, lines were crossed, and power was shifted. For the stars that disappeared, new ones were born. Clarence Mason, leading The Stable, soon became the leader of the most hated, and successful group of wrestlers in EFW history. Their hellish fued with the Lethal Alliance showed the world what hardcore really meant. Not to be outdone was Team Nobody, an almost equally successful, and even more hardcore group of athletes. Eventually, EFW started to fade. Money was disappearing. Mini-Vader and his band of mini CIA agents soon uncovered the reason. EFW President Jack Tunney was using EFW's funds in lavish ways, and even funding an underground child pornogrpahy ring with the money. It seemed that with Tunney behind bars, EFW would be able to continue. However, on June 14, 1997, EFW held its last television broadcast. Extreme Fantasy Wrestling had run out of money, and would be unable to hold the Faces of Death pay-per-view the next night. This PPV was to be headlined by an End of the World match between Bastion Booger, (the US Champ) and The Giant. The main event, was a match that the fans had waited 5 long months to see... Raven, the EFW World Heavyweight Champion, was to take on the EFW Television Champion, The Undertaker. EFW however, disappeared as the money was gone. The formerly great organization was never to be heard from again... UNTIL NOW. On September 18, 1997, EFW Owner [name withheld] settled a lawsuit, against a major wrestling promotion, due to breach of contracts. The payoff was in the millions. That, combined with various stocks and bonds sales, brought [name withheld] the money to sign a long term television contract, to bring back Extreme Fantasy Wrestling.
