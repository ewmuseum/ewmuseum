A revived independent fantasy-wrestling promotion whose title lineage traces to a first encounter six years earlier, relaunched for one documented television taping at New York's Hammerstein Ballroom. Written by AI, 2026

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

New Revolution Grappling was revived on 31 July 2006 by its own returning operator, who announced the relaunch as a low-key summary fed and reported having lined up several returning characters from the promotion's earlier run.

A television taping from the Hammerstein Ballroom in New York City, posted the same day, opened on a highlight package of the promotion's own returning stars before a four-match card: the Bonecrushers beat Girlz on Film in a tag opener, Team Rumble Fish beat a trio led by the Anti-Gravity Kid, and Ketsueki Ikeda retained the NRG World Heavyweight Championship — a title he had first won from Christopher Cannon six years earlier — by shoving Cannon off a ladder in the title match. “Head Trauma” Inoue and “Executioner” Eddie Mills entered the night already holding the NRG World Tag Team Titles, unchallenged on the card.

No further document survives past that broadcast; how the revived promotion continued, if it did, isn't in the surviving record.
