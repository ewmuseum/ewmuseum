Wednesday Heart Attack! - 3-3-99 - From Gettysburg College, PA [Shots of the ongoing feud between Bloodlust and Joe Six-pack is shown as the following message is all you here. Also, as the speech progresses, we see many old NEFW clips.]
VOICE: "Two and a half years ago Simon Pollinetti brought forth on this industry a new federation, conceived in responsibility and dedicated to the proposition that all wrestlers are created equal. Now we are engaged in a great civil feud, testing whether the NEFW or any federation so conceived and so dedicated can long endure.
Bloodlust and Six-pack are set to meet on a great battlefield of a war some consider to be long past. We have come to dedicate a portion of that field as a final fighting place for those who here gave their blood that federation might survive. It is altogether fitting and proper that we should let them do this. But in a larger sense, we cannot dedicate, we cannot consecrate, we cannot hallow this ground. The brave men, past and present who wrestled for the
NEFW have consecrated it far above our poor power to add or detract. The world will little note nor long remember what we say here, but it can never forget what they did here. It is for us still in the
NEFW rather to be dedicated here to the unfinished work which they who fought here have thus far so profitably advanced. It is rather for us to be here dedicated to the great task remaining before us--that from these honored wrestlers past and present we take increased devotion to that cause for which they gave the last full measure of devotion--that we here highly resolve that these wrestlers shall not have bled in vain, that this federation under Flashmaster shall have a new birth of freedom, and that federation of the handlers, by the handlers, for the handlers shall not perish from the net."
