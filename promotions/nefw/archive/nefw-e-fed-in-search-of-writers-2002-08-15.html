E-Fed in search of writers

15 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hello all, this is Little Blue Super Jew, a member for about a year and half now of one six-year-old e-fed, the North Eastern Federation of Wrestling. This fed came into Google Groups in 1996, with founder
[name withheld] breaking off from a larger e-fed to start his own, which became very successful and a happy place to be. Such greats as
Rude Dude, Mondo Somatic, Dr. Melancholy, Capital, and many others were part of the NEFW during it's heyday.
Unfortunately, that heyday has long since passed. Paul left the fed in the capable hands of a [name withheld], who then passed it on to one [name withheld], and from there it went to [name withheld]. [name withheld], otherwise known in the NEFW as Flashmaster, was an intolerable tyrant with no concept of how to run an angle, promote a storyline, book a show, or even hold a Pay-Per-View. The only thing he had going for him was that the members listened to him. Now, however, Flash is gone, and in his place is one [name withheld], who doesn't garner enough respect from the roster to get anything done. The six-year tradition of the NEFW is about to end.
I, however, intend to halt that process. In a very short while, I'll be taking over the NEFW, being hostile if necessary. [name withheld] just has to go. But once he does ,it will be up to me to rebuild the NEFW from the ground up in order to restore it's greatness. To that end, I turn to the glut of talent that is RSPWF. I need writers who are willing to help with the NEFW workload by contributing three or more matches a week on a regular basis. Experience with my own e-fed,
AWE, has taught me that the writing ability of some of the people who frequent this group is simply incredible, and if the NEFW is to survive, it might just need that kind of amazing skill.
So if interested in helping to restore the glory of this once-great e-fed, contact [detail withheld] - that's me - and let me know. I, in the meanwhile, will continue the task of setting things right in the fed and getting the NEFW back to the greatness it lost so long ago.
LBSJ
