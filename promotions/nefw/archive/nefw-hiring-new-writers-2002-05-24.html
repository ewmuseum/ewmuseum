NEFW-hiring new writers

24 May 2002

— Part 1 of 2 —

Hey all, The NEFW is looking for a few good men, or women. I have 2 writer spots open. I am considering writers both in and out of the NEFW. If you are interested please send me an email with a bit about you and your efed experience and 2 sample matches. Thanks and I hope to hear from you soon.
[name withheld] NEFW Owner and President

— Part 2 of 2 —

What's up all? It's been a few weeks, but I'm back. Unfortunately,
I'm still not up to speed with my computer problems. Until I get a new one, I won't be doning any e-fedding for awhile.
So, while I'm down and out like this, I thought I'd try to help out my old stomping grounds, the NEFW. Many of you probably haven't heard of it, because of a few reasons. One, the writing is, by and large, crap, and two, it hasn't been on Google since 1998. It did start here, and can claim a history all the way back to 1996. It's still around, though in pretty bad shape. Bottom line is that it NEEDS YOUR
HELP. Contact [detail withheld] and let him know you're interested in being a writer for the fed. The more, the merrier, because sooner or later this guy's got to realize that the more writers, the more of a chance the cards will be out on time (which is becoming an increasingly rare NEFW occurance).
In any event, thanks for considering it, and it's good to be back. Not that any of you noticed I was gone. ..... Bastards!
