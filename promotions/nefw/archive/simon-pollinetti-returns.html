NEFW: Simon Pollinetti Returns

8 November 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
That is correct, the man who started and ran the NEFW is now back on the Executive board. That can mean only one thing, one of the best feds around will be getting even better. That is correct, the man who booked the famous EMT and Paramedic 'Dead Medic Society' title reign is heavily involved in booking again. The man who booked the unforgettable 38DD win for Douglas in the Frat HouseParty Battle Royal during Irritating the Neighbors is back at it. Who can forget Bruiser Terry Orton losing the match as he was trying to fix the flat tire of the Bud truck out front, after drinking the sand out of a coke bottle and drying the fish tank, since he had already dried up the kegs in the basement? Oh, and H2O spent the night pulling his head out of the toilet after that DDT. Some of the stars of the NEFW: Chris Myers, Mark Fulcrum, Wild cards, the Syndicate, Mondo Somatic, Sickamoa, Rude Dude all started here. Dublin daredevil, Devastation Inc, Dr. meloncholy, Brandon Charm, Terry Orton, Chris Douglas, The Capitol, Creed, Future Warrior, Old Brown heineken, Ethan DeSade, and too many others to count all spnt time here. This fed will soon have its 2 year anniversary party. How many feds have lasted that long? This is one of the strongest and longest lasting feds that is around right now. And to think it started as a fed for newcomers to develop their skills? What this post is about is this: Join this fed, it is awesome and will continue to get better. Two yaers and counting. Come and make it work. Imagine that? A fed looking for top handlers without begging for writers or execs? NEFW Home Page - http://home.sprintmail.com/~flashmaster/index.html
