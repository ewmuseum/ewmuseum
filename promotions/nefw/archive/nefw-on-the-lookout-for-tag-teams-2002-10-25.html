On the lookout for Tag Teams

25 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

— Part 1 of 5 —

Got a Tag Team kicking around doing nothing? Well you're in luck, friend! The NEFW is re-opening it's Tag Team division and is on the hunt for Tag Teams of all shapes and sizes to fill the ranks. Don't have a tag team but always dreamed of handling one? Then this is the place for you! Have a tag team that was brutally murdered by hungry wolverines some years past? Have them resurrected in the only e-fed with Jesus Christ on the active roster (that I know of)! In short, the NEFW is looking to start a tag team division completely from scratch, and in on the lookout for anyone with a team they want to put in competition. So stop on by the website at www.nefwwrestling.com and feel free to check out the archived matches, which can also be viewed right here on RSPWF. The Tag Team division is crying out for someone to come fill it! Will that filler be . . . . . . YOU? LBSJ "Stand back, I know the Heimlich manuever!" (Stares at the choking man, then punches him in the face) "Okay, so I panicked."

— Part 2 of 5 —

Hmmm... I've been considering a number of "concept" tag teams...
Would any of these interest you? Race Card: "White Power" Ryan Vinyard and "Black Rage" Talik Malcolm. A skinhead and a New Black Panther who are court ordered to tag team after their hate-crime brawl injures a small child. Managed by their Jewish probation officer. The GOD Squad: Ace Virtueson and Racer Thriftmore: Recent graduates of a Christian Highschool, and now graduates of a Christian Wrestling School, these two are trying to bring the Lord back into wrestling! The Mex-Am Connection: One's Mexican, and the other is American... and neither speaks the other's language! Further, they neither one realize that they don't like each other, so they're constantly talking trash about each other in their native tongues... and the other never suspects a thing! Super Terrific Dudes (STD): John Area and Sid Fillus. Use your imagination: They hail from the Free Clinic. Disturbing Romance: A young wrestler, and his "14 year old girlfriend." Everyone notices the fact that his "girlfriend" is actually a large transvestite... except him. He's not a tag team but: Muhammed Weinburg- Half Palestinian and half Jewish. Should explain itself. :) I've handled tag teams before: The Outpatients have won multiple tag team and singles titles, including a 111 day NAWA Tag Team Title reign. :)

— Part 3 of 5 —

Heh, I really like these ideas, especially the racial tag team. Anyone in the NEFW can tell you that I've always been big on Racially Motivated Violence. I came up with a similar concept myself awhile ago with the team of Whiteman and Nitro Negro . . but anyway. Just out of curiousity, weren't you in the NEFW once before? I seem to remember a kuribo on the mailing list at one point. And our former President, [name withheld], handled The Christian Youth Fellowship - and the members were Ace Virtueson and Racer Thriftmore. Anyway, this kind of thing is exactly what the NEFW needs. If anyone else wants to get in on it, just e-mail me at [detail withheld] , and you can be on your way to revitalizing a defunct Tag Team scene. Spots are always open!
