Apology from the NEFW for the actions of it's former President

18 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

— Part 1 of 2 —

Hello, all. I'm Little Blue Super Jew. Some of you might recognize me from such e-feds as Hardcore Garbage Wrestling, and the Global
Wrestling Corporation. Today though, I'm here to let you all know about the truth behind the recent idiot bungling up the NEFW and offending you all by being a presence on this board.
His name was [name withheld]. He took the fed over from a President who stepped down (retaining his status as Owner, just so he could vote, like a nudnik...)and proceeded to subject all of us to the kind of idiocy he just put all of you through in his attempts to "recruit"
people. But as of today, that rulership he extended over the federation is no more. I gathered together all the enemies he's made over his years of trying to hold everyone back and push his own characters, and take over the fed, and they amounted to a considerable lot. Together, we took back the fed. I am now in charge, along with
Jake Steed, the NEFW's new hero, and Flashmaster, the man who was President for four years before all this came about. So now, Chad is gone. In a matter of hours, we banded together and took back the website and the Yahoo! Group the fed was based off of, kicked out Chad and the old Owner, and now seek to reparate those who were offended and put the fed back on track. I hope you can forgive the NEFW for [name withheld], and we promise you that we'll keep things like that from happening in the future.
To those who were interested, however, the offer to join the federation still stands. Ignore the boasts of being "elite" or the claim of it's legacy. The truth is, that despite it's 6-year history, the NEFW has taken several steps forward and backward, and has much to learn. It could profit from the help of any one of the extraordinary talent at RSPWF. We are a simple, growing fed now, but we seek to be great, and finally obtain the status which has eluded us for six long years. I hope you can help us be that great. As President, I'll be doing my part, as will the rest of us.
It's a long, hard road to recovery, but we've already taken the right steps. If you're interested in the NEFW, or would like more information, feel free to contact me at
[detail withheld] . I'll be waiting to hear from you. And thank you for your patience.
