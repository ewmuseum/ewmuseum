Writing Positions Open

19 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hello again, LBSJ's handler here. If any of you remember the North
Eastern Federation of Wrestling, it's about to go under a massive overhaul as the result of the executive committee forcing the current
President to resign. He just out-and-out sucks as a leader. But that's neither here nor there, the important issue at hand is that once the deed is done, the federation will be in need of a new team of writers. Once again, I'm making a plea to the minds and talents of
RSPWF and the match writers which reside here.
Rest assured I'm not asking anyone to leap headfirst into a cesspool fresh off of a civil revolution. After the dethroning of the current
President, there will be a long restructuring period during which time the NEFW will be reordered and rebuilt from the ground up. Any writers coming in now would not have to deal with characters or angles they were not familiar with, and in fact would recieve a long waiting period during which time they can familiarize themselves with the talent and talk with the new President - being
myself - on which paths to take. I wouldn't want anyone to go in blind. In closing, an e-fed that I feel once had astonishing potential and was very great in its time is now slowly seeping down the tubes, and though I've done what I can, I can't truly save it without new writers on board.
So if anyone has interest, contact [detail withheld] , and we can get this thing started. Thank you. [name withheld] Little Blue Super Jew Soon-to-be NEFW President
