Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
This is a Sample Card of the NEFW. Allow me to put in some backstory -
This is the Last NEFW card before the fed "scales down" to atone for the greivous financial errors of the previous two Presidents. As such, the only card on the match is a bout for the NEFW's UIC Title, kind of a farewell for the national scene as the fed retreats back to it's New
England stomping grounds.
The NEFW will be resuming cards in a little over a week. We would love to have wrestlers of all kinds aboard, though our cruiserweight division in particular is suffering a depletion in numbers.
