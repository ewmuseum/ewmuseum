NEFW Wet 'n Wild Weekender (19 July 1998)

20 July 1998
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Larry Jawler: Here we are in beautiful downtown, TBA.. Smat: That's not where we are. Jawler: Have you seen the schedule? The weekender is in TBA. See it says it right here. Smat: Forget it. Just get to the lineup. Jawler: I only got one question. Smat: Which is? Jawler: Is Pammy here?!?!? Smat: Don't worry about that, just get to the lineup. Jawler: Okay, first we have Blackheart against Tony RomeDawg. Smat: A battle of newcomers. Jawler: Boring. Smat: They may surprise you. [Indeed they did. This match went back and forth, neither wrestler able to get the pin. Blackheart had the advantage, and was going for the pin when the bell rang for the time limit. Would Romedawg have kicked out? We'll never know.] ================================================================= Winner: Time limit draw. Time: 20:00 ================================================================= Jawler: Told you. Smat: That was anything but boring. These men are up and coming stars. Jawler: Yeah, yeah. Smat: Up next we have a couple more newcomers, Ultimate Solution and Showtime. Jawler: Owwww, there's Pammy. Smat: You are going to get into trouble with Showtime. Jawler: Like I care. When he wins as many championships as I have, then he can run his mouth. A woman like her deserves a man that loves her more than he loves himself. Smat: Can't argue with you there, but they seem to be happy. Jawler: Well, he is, anyway. Smat: Anyway folks here we go. [Ultimate Solution and Showtime lock up and Ultimate Solution gives a good showing until Pammy distracts him by untying her top. Showtime grabs him for a sunset flip and gets the pin.] =============================================================== Winner by pinfall: Showtime. Time: 13:53 =============================================================== Smat: Another win by Showtime. Jawler: Looked like another win for Pammy. Has Showtime gotten an honest pin yet? Smat: I'll let you take that up with him. Jawler: The Rude one is up next with a match against another newcomer. Smat: Mass Destruction is a tough customer, but Rude deserves better than this. Jawler: That's what happens when you screw over your boss. Rude might as well follow OBH and leave. Smat: He is a charter member of the NEFW and will be here long after Willy Slick is gone. [Once again Rude Dude shows why he is one of the best wrestlers in the fed. Mass Destruction gets a few good punches in but Rude responds with chops of his own, a drop kick to the knee and a figure four. Rude let's go before MD can tap out, and leaves the arena in disgust.] =============================================================== Winner by countout. Mass Destruction. Time: 4:30 =============================================================== Smat: Once again, the highest paid wrestler is mistreated by the fed he loves. Jawler: Rude could learn from Double D. Dublin Daredevil was once the highest paid wrestler in the fed, now look at him. Smat: This next matchup is an interesting one. Two wrestlers we haven't heard from in awhile in a loser leaves match. Jawler: Willy Slick is trying to cull some deadwood to trim the payroll. [Arzax and Billy Bob Smith fight with desparation. Arzax going to the top ropes on several occasions, eventually making a mistake and giving Smith the chance to put him in the Smithlock. Arzax was able to get to the ropes, but Smith pulls him back into the center of the ring. Arzax again gets to the ropes but he's wobbly on the one leg. Smith hits a piledriver and goes for the top ropes. Arzax back to his feet and hits a top rope hurracanranna for the pin.] Jawler: Looks like pig-lover is headed back to the hills. Smat: Arzax earns his right to stay. =================================================================== Winner by pinfall: Arzax. Time:12:33 =================================================================== Jawler: Next up an interesting match of APW proportions. Brandon Charm and Terry Orton. [This match never started. Orton was in the ring with his bottle, when Charm was attacked on his way to the ring by Rude and York. Orton finishes off the bottle then runs to Charms aid, breaking the bottle over Rude's head. Ms. Flowers had security break it up, she looked at Orton with disgust, but then left without saying anything.] Smat: Next up is the Professor's chance at revenge. Northern Exposure vs Jonny Rocka and Frankie Senshall. [Northern Exposure have a good showing, and Rocka & Senshall hold their own. The match took an interesting turn of events when the Professor got up onto the ring apron to distract the ref so that Northern Exposure could double team Rocka, but #IV jumped in and Champion and Ringmaster with his brick, "Socrates". When the ref turned back around Rocka had an unconscious Ringmaster pinned for an easy 3 count.] =================================================================== Winner by pinfall: Jonny Rocka and Frankie Senshall. Time: 14:35 =================================================================== Smat: #IV outsmarting the Professor. What an interesting turn of events. Jawler: He was just doing what Socrates told him too. Smat: Please. Rocka and Senshall had that one set up from the start. Jawler: Those three are becoming a serious thorn in the Professors side. Smat: Speaking of thorns, the next two matches feature M4. Jawler: First up, its Mondo Somatic going against Goatman in a non-title match. Smat: Mad Goat comes to the ring with Mother Earth, giving out goat masks. He's a real fan favorite. Jawler: Mondo isn't though. He's accompanied by a chorus of boos. Smat: The bell rings and Mondo goes right to work with a series of chops followed by an armbar submission. He's using his weight to wear down the smaller man. Mad Goat finally gets a hand on Mondo and punches him twice, three times to get out of the hold. Jawler: Took him long enough. Smat: Mondo goes nuts, or whatever it's called when a madman loses control. He has Mad Goat in a chokehold. He's not letting up, the ref pulls him away and admonishes Mondo. Mondo pushes the ref out of the way and goes for the eyes of Mad Goat. Jawler: Mondo doing whatever it takes to win. Smat: Mondo fires Mad Goat to the ropes Mad Goat comes off with a flying back elbow, catching Mondo by surprise. 1 kickout. Jawler: That's just going to make Mondo mad. Smat: It seems to have done just that. Mondo sends Mad Goat to the turnbuckle and follows him in with a running lariat. A few kicks to the stomach softens up Mad Goat and Mondo goes to work with the Cobra Clutch. Jawler: That really works the abs. Mondo grabs the ropes for extra leverage. Mad Goat is screaming in pain. Mondo laughs and turns it into a gut wrench suplex. Smat: The Somatic Solution. This one is over. 1 2 3 ============================================================= Winner by pinfall: Mondo Somatic. Time:18:28 ============================================================= Jawler: M4 continues to roll. Mondo even won that one without any help. Smat: And he is staying around to greet Dr. Melancholy as he makes his way to the ring for a title defense against FFOD. Jawler: He's just making it even, because the Professor is coming to the ring with FFOD. Smat: FFOD asking for a test of strength and the Doctor complies. FFOD gets the leverage and sends Melancholy to his knee. Oh, the Dr. gets out by headbutting FFOD's privates. Jawler: At least he didn't bite 'em. Smat: Melancholy follows it up with an atomic drop. He sends FFOD to the corner and follows him in with a clothesline. Melancholy send him to the other turnbuckle, but FFOD's knee was waiting on him this time. Th Dr. steps back and punches FFOD. Jawler: 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9 Smat: The Dr. stopped at nine and laughed at the fans when they continued to count. Jawler: He has fun with this sport. Smat: A belly to back earns the doctor a 2 count. The doctor yells at the re f, then sends FFOD to the ropes. Jawler: Spinebuster, It's over! 1 2 kickout. Jawler: No!!! Smat: The Dr. can't believe it either. He goes back to work, sending FFOD back to the ropes. The Doctor sets too soon and gets a swinging neckbreaker for the trouble. Jawler: FFOD signaling to the crowd. He sets the Yokmission. Smat: The Dr. refusing to give. Here comes Mondo, he kicks FFOD in the back of the knee. Jawler: He owes FFOD for his knee injury. Smat: The Dr. is fading, and FFOD still not letting go. Here come Militant and Mortesque with chairs. Jawler: This is going to get ugly. Smat: A chair to the back of the leg gets FFOD to release as he goes down in a heap and the bell rings. ==================================================================== Winner by DQ: Five Fingers of Death. Time: 19:20 Dr. Melancholy retains the belt. ==================================================================== Mortesque is holding FFOD's legs as Militant and Mondo go to work on his knee with the chairs. The Professor is yelling to the back. Heaven & Hell coming running out and Militant and Mortesque leave the injured FFOD to Mondo and suicide dive H&H. All four men going at it on the outside. Jawler: Uhoh, here come the three jokers. Smat: And Socrates. Jawler: Too bad the weren't smart enough to look behind them. They just got ploughed by Northern Exposure. Smat: Folks we're out of time. We'll sort this one out for you Wednesday night. -- **** [name withheld] The Flashmaster [detail withheld] [detail withheld] President of the Northeast Federation of Wrestling NEFW Application - http://home.sprintmail.com/~flashmaster/nefwapp.html General of AT-AT - [detail withheld]
