Interlude

18 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
This here is the NEFW's unofficialy "show," Interlude. Not so much a show as more of a post-game rundown of the future bookings, and some things thrown in by the handlers relating to their characters. This is the most recent Interlude, members of the NEFW get to enjoy all the antics weekly.
___________________________________________________________________ (The scene opens up once more in David Fineberg's office, well-rested after a good night of NEFW Impact. Sitting at his desk, the NEFW President spins back and forth in his swiveling chair, then sees the camera on him.
He comes to a stop, gives a brief smile, and gets down to business.) FINEBERG: "Hello once more, NEFW Faithful. I think we can all applaud ourselves once again for a successful Impact. However, this show did not go without it's problems. For one, "

"DIAMONDBACK" CHRIS MYERS: "You put Equalizer back on camera?" WRAACK: "You didn't put in any barbed wire?" FINEBERG: "Shut up! For one, I was going to say, we now have an unresolved conflict that begs being taken care of. The Crimson Christian will have his revenge at Day of the Dead, when he'll face Tommy McKnight once again, with the Unified Intercontinental Title on the line! But that's not the only tension that came about as a result of Impact. That's why, on this coming
Impact, we will see A Six-Man tag match, featuring Butcher Ben, The

Dead, as Ripper faces off against The Goth!!" WRAACK: "Speaking of that match . . . " FINEBERG: "Eh??" (Wraack tilts his head, indicating the Rage Lottery machine still poised next to his desk. Fineberg stares at him.)
FINEBERG: "Is there something wrong with your neck?" WRAACK: " . . . . . uh . ." "DIAMONDBACK" CHRIS MYERS: "The Rage Title match, Mr. President?" FINEBERG: " . . . . . . . .oh! Oh, right. It's that time again!" WRAACK: "Geez . . ." FINEBERG: "Hold your breath everyone - we're going to find out today who Ripper's Tag Team partner - and possible opponent - will be! Here we go!" Blue Super Jew and The Crimson Christian still battling it out. The two robots exchange furious blows, and as the Christian rolls his mecha aside, a barrage of missiles from the Mechanorah blows apart several crowded apartment buildings. The Christianator quickly gets back to it's feet and whips a giant cross-shaped laser rifle from it's back, blowing apart dozens of highway overpasses as he attempts to hit the Mechanorah. The two continue their spree of wanton destruction until . . )
"YOU WILL CEASE THIS IMMEDIATELY!!" It's eyes flare to life, and it's scrawny mechanized limbs burst from the cross, leaving giant holes in the hands, leaking burning hot oil across the fleeing Japanese people. The Je-Gundam stares at the two warriors and lances it's hand out, with a word of warning.)
FMJ: "You are wreaking unruly havoc across this plain!! This will end here!!" LBSJ: "Oh yeah?! (Punching the Christianator in the head a few times) Who's gonna stop me?!" (The ocean boils. AAAAAAA!!!" (Surely enough, The Cross, grown to over four hundred feet in height, looms over the combatants. The four stand still, eyeing one another uneasily, each one ready to attack. MOR-MAN: "HA HA HA!!! HA HA HA HA HA HA HA HA!!! Finally!!!" (The camera spins around, and we find none other than the vicious Mor-Man, equipped with a Mecha of his own! Mormanism also bears out enough weaponry to blast all four to atoms on the spot.) MOR-MAN: "At least, I have lured all of you into a vulnerable position!! Now will be my finest hour!!" FMJ: "I HATE MORMANS!!" CROSS: " !" MOR-MAN: "HA HA HA!!! HA HA HA HA HA!!! PRAISE JESUS!!" FMJ: "I AM Jesus!!" LBSJ: "Man, I hate these goys . . ." CRIMSON CHRISTIAN: "What can we do?!"
