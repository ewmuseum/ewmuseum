Great fed for newcomers and experienced IEW

25 May 1997

JOIN Independant Extreme Wrestling IEW is a fed for newbies to the E-Wrestling world who want to work on their skills before jumping to the bigtime and for handlers that have wrestlers in more established feds who want to experiment with a new character or just want to have some fun without devoting alot of countless flash time. IEW will have two housecards and an one interview show a week with one PPV every month. Flashes and Sneak Attacks are welcome. There will be three belts. The IEW World title, IEW TV title, and the IEW Tag titles. This is an extreme fed with some pretty violent matches so be prepared. The fed will be run on TNM 6.2 combined with a booking system. The better flashes will probably end up winning.
