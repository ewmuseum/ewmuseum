Marquee Men of Wrestling, an Oklahoma City e-fed hosted on the Wrestling Channel, opened its first Sooner and Spotlight Championships at a debut supercard called Summerfest I in the late summer of 2000. Written by AI, 2026

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Marquee Men of Wrestling introduced itself to the newsgroup in the closing days of August 2000 with Prelude, a live studio preview show hosted from its own Marquee Studios in Oklahoma City. Two commentators carried the broadcast, previewing an eight-match debut card and cutting away to a press conference where the promotion's own president, Jack Daniels, welcomed The Wasp — fresh off a run with the UEW and a spot in Wrestlebowl II — onto the roster.

That card, Summerfest I, aired a week later and crowned the promotion's first two champions on the same night. A Sooner Championship tournament ran through a two-match first round into a ladder match, which "The Elite Sensation" Eric Emerson won to become the inaugural Sooner Champion. A parallel Spotlight Championship tournament ran three first-round matches into a three-way "Brutal as Hell" cage match, which the returning veteran Fang won to become the promotion's first Spotlight Champion — only to be jumped after the bell by two debuting newcomers as the broadcast closed.

Through the following weeks the promotion kept building its roster, naming new signees and putting out an open call for writers to keep its weekly Monday Night Mayhem broadcast fed. A federation calling itself the ASCW appeared inside Marquee Men of Wrestling's own broadcasts as a cross-promotional guest, sharing airtime and, later that autumn, a merger: the two promotions combined to form a new organization, the PWO, by that October.

No document survives from Marquee Men of Wrestling itself narrating its own closing; the last surviving word from the promotion under its own name is a mid-September call for new talent, still describing itself as an organization on the rise.
