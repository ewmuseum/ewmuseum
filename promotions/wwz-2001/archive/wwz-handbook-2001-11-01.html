WWZ Handbook

1 November 2001
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
If your interested go to http://www.geocities.com/worldwrestlingzoneze/index.html The Shows : We run two shows a week Sunday Night Dagger and Wednesday Morning Carnage. The former of these shows will be completely hand written and in relatively deep detail - interested writers email here - the Wednesday show however will be very short, four matches will normally be the maximum, for those under main event status and will include very brief reports and is just a 1 hour show compared to the 2 and a half hour show on Sunday. The PPV's will be held once every 6 weeks and will be long and very detailed and hand written, adding to the quality. How The Matches Are Determined : Matches will be determined by a combination of three things. The first, containing 85% of the decision, will go on the quality of your roleplay. The second, containing 10% of the decision, will go on the quality and strategies shown in your match strategies. The final 5% will go to a pre-match interview [trash talk]. The three crucial things combined give you your final mark, they are explained later. So for example someone scoring 60% for roleplay, 8% for his/her match strat(ergy) and 2% for their pre match interview, then they will beat some one who gets 68% for the roleplay but does not send in their match strat or interview, unless one of the people has agreed to lose, to increase the tension in the feud. Roleplaying : As explained before, roleplay contains 85% of your final mark for each show, and therefore is the most important trait. You only have to do one roleplay for each match you have, consisting of a minimum of 10 lines. Your roleplay mark will be taken from the best roleplay you do within the roleplay window for each show. So for example you do three roleplays with in the window and you score 30%, 40% and 35%, your 40% will be taken for your match. Obviously the more roleplays you do the better chance you get of getting a higher mark [your mark however will not be revealed to you at any point]. The roleplay windows are simply the dates between which you can roleplay for each event and the window for each event will be announced 7 days before the event. For example for Sunday Night Dagger, the previous Sunday we may announce the roleplay window is from Monday until Saturday morning [we will give times] and therefore any roleplays before or after the window will not count. In the subject of each roleplay you will be required to put the show name in for example you may put 'Dagger : Raging Bull prepare to die' in the subject of the roleplay. Any more questions on the roleplay idea of this fed email me at [detail withheld] Roleplay Rules : Roleplays must be posted on the roleplay(RP) boards linked from the site and must abide to the following rules. Any breach of the rules and/or regs of the RP board can result in a stiff punishment handed out and decided upon at the time of the offending RP. And, on another note, people do NOT need to tell me what RPs are offensive and what RPs are not. I make the decision there, whether to punish or do whatnot to the offending poster. It is between me, that poster, and the victim of the RP (if there is one). It is NOT anyone else's business, otherwise. - you cannot use another person's character without direct permission from that person. I will be giving out a list of people to contact, who they handle, and various contact methods at the beginning of next week. - you cannot book matches on the RP. That means you can't say that there will be a match on a certain date. Only Zeebo and Mike Moss can do this, as they are the two with the booking power - you cannot make any racial, religious, or basically any offensive and harassing remarks to other on the boards. - acts of violence cannot become too violent. Any acts involving such things that would permanently or severly injure another wrestler are prohibited. Examples of the such are shootings, stabbings, killings, paralyzing, and others along this line. You need permission from me before you can post such thing. It wouldn't make much sense to shot someone, then wrestle them the next day, would it? - foul language is fine. - you cannot write an RP for another person - you may include any of the interviewers in your roleplays but no other staff and no dating any of the staff but they can come on to your wrestler, slightly. - final rule is to have fun and be creative with your RP. The more unique it is, the more attention it will get Match Strategies : Strats are good tools to control how your wrestler acts in a match. Below is guidelines to writing a quick strat., don't spend more than 5 minutes on a strat unless you really want to, it is just a basic way of controlling how your wrestler works, we are looking for short sharp quality here and a maximum or 1 per match. Include the following : - wrestlers entrance (if this isn't included, a defualt one will be used) - wrestlers strategy for the match - suggested spots or ideas for things to happen in the match - what your wrestler would do if he or she won or lost (both reactions here please) - other interferences or run ins you want to make during that card That is all that is needed for the strat form but unlike the roleplays, we do not have a board for them and they must be emailed to us two days before the event [the exact time will be set before every event so there is no confusion] Interviews : Two days before each event, every wrestler in a match has to send in an interview, which will make up 5% of their final mark. It is basically trash talk and 5 line is set as the recommended level although more or less will be accepted. Basically you will come out to the crowd and say what you want to say about your opponent etc. A good one will give you that little extra chance of winning your match and the best three-four for each show will be selected and used during the show. Other Message Boards : Any other message boards up will have the first message ever posted on them just explaining what they are and why they are there. Newsboard : The newsboard will give in character news, either updating you on the site or reporting the news, from one of our many reporters, we will try and update it as often as we can. It will also have interviews with the wrestlers out of kayfabe. Ranking System : At the end of the last Wednesday show, before the Pay Per Views, the person top of the rankings will become the no.1 contender for the PPV and the World Wrestling Zone's World Heavyweight title holder will have to defend against the no.1 contender. This is the same for the tag division. To get into the rankings you must have at least wrestled five televised matches. For a win you get 3 points, a draw earns you 1 points and a loss will earn you 0 points. The person with the best points per matches will be top of the rankings. But remember there is the 5 match minimum. I believe that is all you need to know for now any problems, email us at [detail withheld] and be sure to enjoy the site. Have fun. -- Tom Rubashow
