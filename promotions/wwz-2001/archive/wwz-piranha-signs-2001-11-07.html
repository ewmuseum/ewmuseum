WWZ: Piranha Signs on the Dotted Line

7 November 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
report by Jimmy Morrison of the Daily Testament (from the WWZ newsboard) In a press conferance this morning, Mike Moss, the World Wrestling Zone's ambitious founder announced the signing of the seasoned veteran David Page, better known as the Piranha. Moss was delighted with the signing stressing the fact that Piranha, has the drawing capabilities to knock the WWF aside. Others are more sceptical though saying Page has only made it big in one federation, where he walked out after the owner suggested a zombie angle. Zeebo, WWZ's commishioner and Piranha's adopted father, is also pleased to see Page on the payroll saying 'His wrestling abilities will come on no end with a good run in WWZ, and he could be a real backbone to the federations success.' Page, 23, has been described as 'an awesome submissionist, whose skills on the mic rival any in the buisness.' Can this man help Moss's dream become a reality or is he as Jim Ross says 'A blue haired freak, with no talent' only time will tell? report by Jimmy Morrison of the Daily Testament For more info go to http://www.geocities.com/worldwrestlingzoneze/index.html -- Tom Rubashow
