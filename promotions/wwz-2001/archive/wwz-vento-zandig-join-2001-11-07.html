WWZ: Vento and Zandig Join WWZ

7 November 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
report by Deano Ward of Wrestling Pages (taken from the WWZ newsboards) Mike Moss, once again has opened his wallet, this time to Aki Vento(Dan Wickens) and to Zandig. Zeebo, WWZ's hired commish, had the following to say on the two: Speaking on Zandig first he stated 'Zandig will play the perfect heel, with a twisted character and a big frame, he definatly has main event potential. Although we may not see five star matches from him, I feel his extremist ability with many high spots will help get him over. Vento - real name Dan Wickens and close with the already signed David Page (Piranha) - has the unique talent of being a high flying 270 pounder. His unique style will definatly make the crowd ooooo and ahhhh even if the internet fans will not be impressed. He, like Zandig has time on his side and looks as though he could help us, the WWZ, overtake Vince McMahon's WWFE as the world's most popular wrestling federation.' This was all Zeebo had to say and both stars will debut at this Saturday's house show (Starting 19:00 CET at the World Wrestling Zone Arena). But with wrestlers not applying as regularly as President Mike Moss would like, WWZ might be starting to worry about their first televised show a week on Sunday. report by Deano Ward of Wrestling Pages (for more details go to http://www.geocities.com/worldwrestlingzoneze/index.html ) -- Tom Rubashow
