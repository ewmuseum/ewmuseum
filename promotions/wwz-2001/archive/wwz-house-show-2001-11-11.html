WWZ House Show, 11/10/01

11 November 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
To see it on the site go to http://www.geocities.com/worldwrestlingzoneze/ho1.htm The House Show Date : Saturday 10th November 2001 Starting : 20:00 CET From : The World Wrestling Zone Arena Attendance : 1,501 (capacity 30,000) The Card :- Wrestler(s) One Wrestler(s) Two Title Stipulations Aki Vento 'Polished Leather' Jimmy Bailey None None Zandig Jack Inglis None Table Match Piranha Hacker Myers None Steele Cage Match Reported by Richard Page of the Daily Account The show begins with a few pyros going off and the WWZ theme plays. Mike Moss, WWZ's president appears at the top of the ramp. And begins the show with a few words 'I would like to thank you all for coming and hope you enjoy the show, we have a television taping coming up on Saturday, which will be shown on BBC and NBC on Sunday, so please make sure you come back to this arena for that. In terms of action, we have some super gimmick matches and I hope you will join us, hopefully with some new wrestlers, next Saturday. Have fun. I now pass you over to Miss. Jones.' Aki Vento in a standard match vs. 'Polished Leather' Jimmy Bailey with Mary Brookman refereeing In the ring stands Sarah Jones 'Ladies and gentlemen, the following match is set for one fall and has a 20 minute time limit. Already in the ring weighing in at 243lbs, 'Polished Leather' Jimmy Bailey.' She pauses to little crowd reaction. 'And his opponent hailing from Finland and weighing in at 270lbs Akkkkkiiiiiiii Vennnnnnto'. The arena goes dark as Fat Lip starts up. The fans wonder and as the guitar kicks in blue and red fireworks explode and Vento runs down to the ring. BELL RINGS Vento and Bailey lock up with Vento's strength advantage already showing through as he pushes Bailey into the corner where Jean-Claude Laurent splits them up. As Vento turns to gloat to the crowd, Bailey re-couperates before giving Vento a harsh Running Bulldog slamming his head into the mat. Bailey makes a rookie mistake by not going straight for the cover as he runs to the ropes before climbing up to the top. It is too early on in the match and Vento, who was up quickly, walks over to a stuttering Bailey, on the top rope and hurls him off. Vento then delivers his favourite Face Driver before giving Bailey a Running Side Arm Take Down as he tried to get to his feet. Again Vento taunts the crowd in his home Finish language and again Bailey has time to re-couperate but this time Vento is back concentrating before 'Polished Leather' can get any advantage. Picking up Bailey he body slams him to the floor before going for the pin. Laurent counts 2 before Bailey kicks up. As the count is being made, the beautiful woman seen earlier with Vento makes her way to the ring, in a buisness women's outfit and a scowling look on her face and a black, shiny handbag under her arm. She gets a seat and starts watching, from ringside. After Bailey's kick out Vento makes his way to the top rope but misses and attempted Flying Sommersalt. As he lies their Bailey slowly makes his way to his feet but before he can do anything the official World Wrestling Zone's theme music plays and Mike Moss walks out with a mic in his hand, for his second appearance of the night. He says 'I do believe Zeebo stated this would be ladder match, so Laurent get outa there and let the ladder action commence'. With that the theme plays again and he has gone as quickly as he came. As Bailey stands in the middle of the ring Vento has already clicked on and has seen the lowering slip above the ring, he slides out of the ring, with Vento still shell-shocked and grabs a ladder from under the ring. On sliding it into the ring and following it though, he finds the unforgiving foot of Jimmy Bailey who pounds, with his feet into Vento. He picks up the heavy Vento and with great strength throws him back to the outside, right next to the businesswoman. Bailey sets up the ladder and starts climbing with Vento on the outside its all over. The businesswoman gets off her seat and slides into the ring with here chair in hand, she hits Bailey pretty harshly with it before running back and trying to help up Vento. As Bailey lies motionless, Vento gradually gets to his feet and into the ring. He delivers the VENTOBUSTER before setting up the ladder. He climbs to the top and grabs the slip, the BELL RINGS The women helps Vento down from the ladder and dusts him off, she walks to the back with him by her side and content on her face. Sarah Jones gets into the ring as Mary Brookman helps Bailey out of it. 'And here is your winner, in a time of 5:33 Aki Vento' The crowd boo. 'Now onto the nezt match. Zandig in a table match vs. Jack Inglis with Mary Brookman refereeing Again the voice of Sarah Jones can be heard 'Ladies and gentlemen the following match is a table match with a 15 minute time limit. Introducing first standing to my left hailing from Tunbridge Wells, England and weighing in at 175lbs Jack Inglis. And his opponent hailing from San Diego in the US, weighing in 230lbs he is the twisted Zannnnnndigg'. Little reaction comes from the crowd comes when the lights go out and 'Left Behind' by Slipknot plays when they come back on Zandig stands in the ring and Mary Brookman calls for the bell. BELL RINGS As Mary Brookman is checking the boots of Inglis Zandig charges into his back knocking the small Brit to the ground. On picking him up he delivers the Inverted Shoulder Wrack Brainbuster followed by a horendous Belly to Back DDT. He gloats to the crowd who are already booing him before continuing his assault on the youngster with a Reverse Russain Leg Sweep, an Underhook Leg Sweep and a Brainbusting Face Driver. At this point his relentless attack stops and before more gloating he decides to go to the outside and get the table. On sliding out, he lifts up the ring banner and searches for a table, on eventually arriving from under the ring, the plucky young Brit, Inglis is on his feet and he spins over the top rope landing a 360 degree turn on his opponent. As his opponent lis on the outside mat, Inglis makes his way to the top rope and delivers a harsh frog splash to his opponent. On recovery, Inglis sets up the table and sets up Zandig for a suplex onto it, still on the outside. Zandig however blocks it and manages to hit the Belly to Belly Tombstone Piledriver onto the outside mat. He rolls Inglis back into the ring and hits a Body Slam Side Piledriver. He goes for the pin, obviously forgetting it is a table match and hits his hand against the floor in frustration. He gets up, turns to the crowd and signals with his arms it is over. However, when he turns around he is hit by an Inglis Spinning Heel Kick the crowd erupt to the local lad who is spilling over with adrenaline. As a groggy Zandig gets to his feet he is met by a Hurricarana and then a Flapjack. Inglis brings the table inside, and on laying his opponent on it, he goes to the top rope. If he hits the frogsplash Zandig will be first through the table and will take the match, if not however, then he will go through the table and Zandig will win. He takes a deep breath and leaps from the top rope. As he flys through the air Zandig rolls off the table and Inglis smashes through it. BELL RINGS Sarah Jones gets into the ring as Mary Brookman helps Inglis out of it, as Zandig sits huddled in the ring corner, shaking with glee. 'And here is your winner, in a time of 7:09 the Twisted Zandig' The crowd boo. 'We now have a short break where and you can come and meet the stars.' Piranha in a steele cage match vs. Hacker Myers with Mary Brookman refereeing Again Sarah Jones stands in the ring 'The following match is a steele cage match, with the only way to victory by getting out of the cage. Standing to my right hailing from Belfast, Northern Ireland, weighing in at 210lbs, Hackkkkkker Myers and his opponent, hailing from Amazon Delta, Brazil, weighing in at 230lbs the Deadly Piranhaaaaaa' The lights slowly fade to black. A cool wind starts to blow and a few twinkling lights scatter the audience. The first strum on the strings of the Jaws theme makes the audience shiver. Blue roman candles flare up surrounding the entrance. Piranha slowly walks out. More roman candles light up as he walks down the runway. He climbs into the ring and falls to his knees in prayer. On finally standing, the BELL RINGS The two lock up. Neither man wants to give away any advantage on the opening night of WWZ. Myers gets the first advantage by pushing Piranha to the ground straight from the lock up much to the audience's displeasure. Myers turns to the crowd boasting about his strength as Piranha gets up behind him. He taps Myers on the shoulder and as he turns he applies the NERVE HOLD. Myers struggles to get out of the move using a foot takedown but eventually escapes via a sneaky EYE RAKE. As Piranha holds his eyes Myers bounces off the ropes and delivers a powerful CLOTHESLINE sending Piranha crashing to the mat with a tremendous bang. Being inexperienced, Hacker Myers decides that it is early enough to start climbing the cage. He's only just off the ground when Piranha grabs him from behind and throws him to the mat. Piranha picks Myers up and gives him a PYRAMID DRIVER followed by his RAZOR TOOTH but Myers wriggles out, Piranha stops him quickly, converting it to his CROOKED FIGURE 4. Eventually Myers manages to wriggle out but he has badly hurt his knee and he is having trouble walking or even standing. As Myers begs for time out Piranha kicks the injured knee of Myers. He asks for the door to be opened and casually walks towards it. Myers reaches into his tights and pulls out some knuckle-dusters, he pulls Piranha back into the centre of the ring and hits him as hard as he can in the face with his smuggled in weapon. The crowd calls for a DQ but the referee informs the ring announcer cage matches are no DQ and there for the match must continue. The ring announcer relays this information to the crowd who boo in unison. Hacker Myers picks up a now busted wide-open Piranha and attempts a BODY SLAM. As he hoists him into the air his knee gives way again, sending both wrestlers tumbling to the mat with Piranha sitting atop Myers. He pounds him several times until he appears unconscious. He goes to one of the corners and opens up the turnbuckle pad, pulling out the rope that keeps it attached to the ring post. Swiftly he ties one end to Myers left wrist and the other end to the cage. He then takes Myers knuckle-dusters and throws three strong punches to Myers, which bring immediate swelling around the eye and nose. He climbs the cage as Myers struggles to untie himself. Piranha makes it to the outside and drops to the floor winning the match. BELL RINGS With Myers still tied to it, the cage rises back into the air. Myers is about ten feet in the air when the rope snaps and he falls to the outside matting. Piranha exits through the crowd to a chorus of cheers. Myers is assisted to the back by security and medics to a barrage of boo. And the fans begin to filter out of the first house show, as Sarah Jones announes the winner in a time of 8:55. End of show -- Tom Rubashow
