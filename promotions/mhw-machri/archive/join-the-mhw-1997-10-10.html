Join the MHW!!!

10 October 1997

****************************************************************** MACHRI Hardcore Wrestling ****************************************************************** I think the M.H.W. is one of the best e-wrestling feds in the World!!! Why, because in the M.H.W. almost anything you want in E-WRESTLING, can actually happen! If the President and his assistants let you of course. That's not very difficult to do, just be original!! How can you make a name for yourself and climb the ladder of the M.H.W.?? First of all; - THINK; - SPEAK; - DO; The 3 secrets of succes, before you do something, make a plan. Give good interviews or comments or challenges. Interfere in matches, make sneak attacks, win the support of the crowd, help other wrestlers, etc. THE 3 most important things when you're a acting wrestler, tag team or manager in the MHW. Before that you have to prove that you've knowledge of (e)-wrestling. What I mean is, know your moves, know your limits and be original. * You may have 3 wrestlers and 1 tag team in the MHW each wrestler and tag team may have a different manager. * The MHW has the following titles: MHW World Heavyweight title MHW International title MHW World Tag Team title * Every week, two shows: MHW Wednesday CLASH (house show) (#1 contender matches, special challenge matches) MHW Saturday LIVE (something like NITRO/R.I.W)! (special interviews, a few title matches, special matches) * Every month a PPV Super Card (title matches, fued matches, special matches, battle royal) * On the pages of the MHW you can find info like ratings, title histories, upcoming cards, roster, etc. All interviews, statements and results you get by E-mail! What we need is: (remember THINK!!!) *NOTE: I ask you more than once for some info of your wrestler's talents, because we use not one but THREE (3!!) wrestling simulators for the MHW The MHW Form: ********************* Your name: Your E-mail adress: Wrestler's name: Wrestlers weight: Wrestlers height: Is your wrestler: Heel or Face: You got 70 points to give to your wrestler: Strength: Dexterity: Technical: Stamina: Toughness: Cheating: Tagging: Give me your 5 moves: Submission Holds: Top Rope Moves: Define Special Moves: Your favourite kind of match: (for example Lumberjack match): What kind of style has your wrestler: (for example; All-rounder, Brawler, Submission wrestler) Name your wrestler's finisher: Description of your finisher: In what situation can his finisher move be done? Can your wrestler do his finisher when on the rebound from the ropes? Can he be thrown off the turnbuckle while performing his finisher? How much is the maximum weight an opponent can have so he can perform his finisher? (be real!) Give us at least one counter for your wrestler's finisher: You have 250 points to give to (no more than 100 for each) Worker: Push: Stamina: Give us your wrestlers background: Hometown, history, titles, former federations, etc. Does your wrestler use a foreign object? If yes, what does he use? Does your wrestler have a manager? If yes, give us his name: Does the manager interfere? Give us your managers background: Give me a second-best finishing move: In what situation can his second-best finisher move be done? Choose two specialty's: Strength, Toughness, Conditioning, Intelligence, Cheating, Brawling, Technical, Mat Wrestling, Aerial, Quickness, Teamwork. Which specialty you like to work on (training)? Has you wrestler a tag team partner? (If yes, fill in the form for his tag team partner!) What's the name of his tag team partner? What's the name of the tag team? Do they have a manager? If yes, what's his name? Does he interfere? Does the wrestlers also wrestle single matches? ************************************************************************** Now, I ask you to SPEAK! Give me some comments of your wrestler, tag team or manager! Be original, that's the way you have a bigger chance of success in the MHW!!! Give me more than 10 lines, but no more than 25 lines, please! Introduce yourself to the MHW, make sure that everyone remember you! **************************************************************************** Last but not least, I want you to DO something!! For example; do something to win the crowd for you, win their support. Invite someone for a tag team, attack somebody, etc. And you know........BE ORIGINAL!! **************************************************************************** So, THINK, SPEAK and DO! Join the MHW today!! Join us, fill in the form and I'll get back at you! If you have any questions, ask me. Join us and enjoy the ride.............. **************************************************************************** MHW President [name withheld] [detail withheld] http://callisto.worldonline.nl/~nwo/efed.html
