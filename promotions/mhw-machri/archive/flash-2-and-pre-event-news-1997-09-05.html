OTHER STUFF ------------------------------------------------------------------ Some words from the MACHRI World Order Champion 'El Perfecto Body' Jacques Castonguay and hismanager the lovely El Sexy Girl. El Sexy Girl: "Hello fans, El Sexy Girl here, Steelman you hurt me with that Superkick but I'm happy you paid for it, you know what you are a LOOSER, is Man-O-War in your family?? Barry Horrowitz was your wrestling teacher??" Right Perfecto? El Perfecto Body: "Yes, it's right, Steelman is out, I put it out of the Rumble and beat him up in my match, now it's Badmuts this week, and I'm gonna kick his butt. So, next show, Badbutts I'm gonna whip you out, you know why, cause I'm the champ and you're a wimp!! By the way if you are European champion go in France and defend it! Dumb!!" (A Bad Scene in the locker Room) Shocker: I want a rematch with the Badmuts guy let me at him (Mark Steelman Comes In Mad and Sweete) Mark: Jessica Were you at Jessica: Sorry Mark Steelman: You should of been there to keep El Sexy girl from messing with me, I want my title shot with El Pefecto and untill I get it I will interfear with every match he has, get that camera out of here (Push the camera down) A word from the MACHRI World Order President: "Allright Shocker, if Badmuts loses his match tomorrow against 'El Perfeto Body you get a BRAWL title shot on Wednesday CLASH! If not, you will face Mark Steelman on Wednesday, the winner will face the BRAWL Champ on Saturday LIVE (Sept, 13)!!!" "Here's the card of tomorrows Saturday LIVE:" * MACHRI World Order European title match: Champion 'Dildo Bob' -vs- Eric 'the Crow' Draven * Tag Team Tournement for the vacant MACHRI World Order European Tag Team title. * MACHRI World Order Tag Team Championship match: Champions Bloody Blue -vs- The Ditchers. * Champion -vs- Champion non-title match: MACHRI World Order World Heavyweight Champion 'EL Perfecto Body' Jacques Castonguay -vs- MACHRI World Order BRAWL Champion Badmuts (if Badmuts wins this one, he gets a World title match at next Wednesday CLASH, Sept. 10.) * Shocker -vs- Father John II. * Mark 'Sexy' Steelman -vs- Domino. * The Assassing -vs- Brain Bond.
