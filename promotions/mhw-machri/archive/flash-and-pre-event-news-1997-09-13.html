MACHRI World Order FLASH September 12, 1997 1. Interviews. 2. Report. 3. Saturday LIVE card 4. Other Stuff 1. INTERVIEWS ============================ 'El Perfecto Body' Jacques Castonway: "El Perfecto Body here, Steelman, you want me now, I can't wait. You make me lost my match. I'm gonna kick your butt. I want a brawl, no dqs, no countouts, Because I want to beat you, put this and iron match the most pins in two hours. I'm gonna prove to you that the best technician in the world is here and that's me" A word from the MACHRI World Order President: "Allright, It's allright with me, if Steelman accepts, this one is on for the title at MACHRI World Order, DISORDED! Sunday September 21!!" "Of course if Perfect has the title at that time!!" 2. REPORT ============================== Last night Derek W. Dick went of to meet our newest wrestler Insanity!! (Fade into a dark basement, Insanity is standing holding a stuffed dummy.) Derek W. Dick: "Um, here we are in Insanity's home... Mr. Insanity could I have a word?" Insanity: Oh.. I see the MACHRI World Order wants to know what I'm thinking. They've brought a camera down to the bowels of the earth to conduct just one interview. WELL LADIES AND GENTLEMEN- YOU'RE NOT GETTING ONE. INSTEAD YOU MAY WITNESS MY STRATEGY SESSION- A PREPARATION FOR MATCHES TO COME!!!" (Insanity takes the dummy by the legs and hits it against a table repeatedly. He throws it against a wall, and finally tosses it against a bed of nails. He then turns back to the camera.) "NOW YOU SEE? I WILL DO THIS TO EACH AND EVERY ONE OF YOU IF I HAVE TO!!! I WILL TEAR YOU ALL LIMB FROM LIMB!!! NOW EVERY WRESTLER UNDERSTAND THIS- I AM SUPERIOR TO YOU ALL!!! MY ABILITY FAR SURPASSES ANYONE I'VE EVER MET!!!!! AND DO YOU KNOW WHAT THE WORST PART ABOUT THIS IS?!!!... (calms down)... people think I'm crazy..." Derek W. Dick: "I, uh, can't imagine..." (Insanity DDTs Derek W. Dick and then moonsaults onto him after springing off the wall.) Insanity: "I'LL DESTROY YOU!!! I'LL DESTROY YOU ALL!!! AND YOU WITH THE CAMERA--" (Camera suddenly goes dead) ************* (The screen opens up with a sign that says the Summit. As the camera man makes his way inside he pans across the large ring. Inside the ring two wretslers are taking on four wrestlers in a handicap match. Making his way to the ring is a guy with a tuxedo on with the name Mean Bean on his shirt.) Mean Bean: Well folks I am here in Houston, Texas where Little Big Men are paraticing against four other wrestlers. The match has just been going on about three minutes and has been dominated by Little Big Men. (The camera turns back towards the ring where Flash is nailing on two wrestlers with a fury of left and rights. He then dropkicks one wrestler in the other. Both wrestlers go down. Flash makes the tag to his partner Clash who gets in the ring and does a gigantic moonsault on both wrestlers. Finally Clash gets the mic and says that he has enough of this match. So he signals for the WhipClash and sure enough he hits it. The ref is counting 1-2-3.) Mean Bean: Can I have a word with you guys. Flash:(Not even out of breathe.) Yeah but make it quick. Mean Bean:Congratulations on your win over these guys. Clash: Shut up and get to the questions. Mean Bean: Allright then, Flash first of all you have just came off a successful debut against Domino. How did you feel about the match. Flash: Well lets just say that it was a whole lot easier than what I expected it to be. I mean I expected to at least have to break a sweat but I guess a normal person who is not in the extradornary shape that I am in at this very moment would have at least broke a sweat. Everybody knew after the bell had run it was just a matter of me playing games with Domino, I mean me making it look like he had a chance to beat me. I knew inside of my head that I could put the Power Outage on him and make him submit easily. Eventually I got tired of playing so I just ended the match with the Power Outage. Mean Bean: Well sounds like to me you have a lot of confidence in yourself. What do you think about your upcoming match with Father John II? Flash: To answer your first question you would be as confident as me if you had the body and the looks I have. Father John II I know that you are a big man but that does not mean that I will be intimadated by you. If I was intimadated by you that would mean that I wouldn't be in wrestling today. I have beaten a lot of people bigger than me and that will not stop me from either getting the one, two, three over you or if I have to make you submit via the Power Outage. It doesn't matter which way but I figure it would be more easily by the Power Outage because it takes down everyone no matter what the size is of the person. Hey I have just had this terrific idea how about Father John II since you are co-holder of the European Tag Titles that if I beat you in a singles match then you have to give us, Little Big Men, a match for the titles on any card in the next week. Mr. President is that stipulation signed for the match? Mean Bean: Do you have anymore comments on anything else? Flash: As matter fact I do. It is about Dildo Bob and the European Title you have. Personally I have nothing against you, but I do have a problem with you having the European Title. So I am here by issuing you a challenge for the European Title at the PPV. Since my schedule will be full up to the PPV. I will figure you won't accept because I have noticed that you will not fight anybody when the belt is on the line. So come on punk do you accept? Mean Bean:Well Clash I have just got word that you have added another belt to your collection? Clash:Yeah that is right. It is the EWF World Tag Team Titles with another one of my friends Anaconda. He might join this league it just depends on if he feel like he needs more respect than what he is already getting since he is the most feared man in the world. Mean Bean: How do you feel about your debut match with Brian Bond? Clash: Well frankly I am not to worried about the match because Brian Bond could not get a win against Dildo Bob without my friend Flash's help. Even though I am not worried about him I always prepare for any match the same whether it be a debut match, a regular match, or even a title match. I always prepare for them. Brian Bond I guarantee you will go down just like those other wrestlers in the ring up their did. By the WhipClash I know Brian Bond every time I say that word you start shaking and regreting you have to face me well I have to debut against somebody and lucky for everyone else it was not them but you. I tell you what I will not make it as hard on you okay. Not!!! I take it hard on every competitor no matter what. I have just got one more thing to address and that is Flash's match with Father John II. Mr. President if you accept the stipulation well then we will become the new European Champions by at least Saturday. If we do not get the match Wednesday then me and Flash want to make our tag team debut against the Ditchers. Do you accept Ditchers? Both:This interview is over. A word from the MACHRI World Order President: If Hell & Heaven accept, and Flash defeats Father John II this Saturday, Little Big Men gets a title shot at MACHRI World Order, DISORDERD!!! If Hell & Heaven don't accept it, Little Big Men will not get a title shot!!! If the Ditchers accept your challenge, then you make your tag team debut on Wednesday CLASH, September 17, 1997!! About Flash's challenge to 'Dildo' Bob. If Flash beats Father John II, who's #1 contender for the European title, and 'Dildo' Bob accepts it, you get a title shot!! If Father John II wins, he gets a title shot at MACHRI World Order, DISORDED! ***************** (The screen opens on a limo as the doors open the All-Americans come out of the car.) John "American" Smith: Hey all you guys feeling fine today. Tomorrow Bald Eagle and me have a match against the Bloody Blue it will be our first match of showing people how great the United States of America really is. Bloody Blue you are from England and well we will be giving you all a butt whooping American style. Guys England is a good country and all but it never be like the United States of America. The best country in the world. I figure we will see how good we really are since we are going up against the best tag team the MACHRI World Order has to offer. I also want to start my singles career fairly soon since I figure I need a belt by the end of the first PPV. I figure there is no one better to start my singles career off against then the Assasing. He is another one of those people who are not from the best country in the world, the United States America. Bald Eagle:You know this would be a perfect opportunity for me to try out my new finisher the Ole Glory. I don't know which one of Bloody Blue I will do my finisher on but it will be the end of the match. I know that it is a non-title match but the way I understand it is if we beat Bloody Blue then we automatically get a title shot. I know that we said that we wanted the American I mean the European Tag Titles but I have decided that the World Tag Team Titles are good enough. I mean the MACHRI World Order should have the best tag team and the best country representing them. Well luckly we are both the best tag team and from the best country. Hey John you know that we should take out anyone not from the United States of America. Do you agree with me John? John "American" Smith:Yes I agree with you Bald Eagle watch out everyone not from the United States of America. Both:All other countries watch out we are declaring WAR ON YOU!!!!!!!!!!!!! A word from the MACHRI World Order President: If you beat Bloody Blue tomorrow night, you get a title shot at Wednesday CLASH, September 20, 1997!! 3.
