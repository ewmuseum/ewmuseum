MACHRI World Order FLASH #3 1. A word from the President 2. Ratings & Title history 3. Interviews 1. A WORD FROM THE MACHRI WORLD ORDER PRESIDENT ------------------------------ YO, I believe the action in the MACHRI World Order is amazing! You all do a terrific job. Starting this Wednesday we gonna use a brand new points system. With this system your succes in the MACHRI World Order is your own hands. Much more important then before is roleplay, challenges and actions!!! That's you're only way to success!!! I want your interviews, roleplay or actions on Monday. Expect the pre-event news on Tuesday!!!! Matches for this Wednesday CLASH: * Father John II -vs- Mark 'Sexy' Steelman * Brain Bond -vs- 'Dildo' Bob (non-title) * Triangle Elimination match: The Assassing -vs- Eric 'the Crow' Draven -vs- 'El Perfecto Body' * The Ditchers -vs- Bloody Blue (non-title match). * MACHRI World Order BRAWL Championship Ladder match: BRAWL Champion Badmuts -vs- Shocker. 2. INTERVIEWS --------------------------------------- Shocker: Eric 'the Crow' Draven, making me a enemy was the worst thing you could have done and costing me a match is the dumbest thing you could have done I want you in the ring. Lumberjack Match, No countouts, just Pin or Submission. Because when you get in the ring with me that will be you last thing you have done. Ha ha ha ha. Mark 'Sexy' Steelman: Badmuts I'n should say I'm sorry....... But I'm not. I was trying to make you lose, but if you want to argue about it bring it to the ring and I'll do the talking. Now as for you Perfecto I told you I'll be there every match until I get a Title shot at you. A word from the MACHRI World Order President: Well it's allright with me, Shocker you'll meet Eric 'the Crow' Draven on Saturday LIVE, September 13. It's gonna be a Lumberjack match, only pinfall and submission counts. Shocker, if you win the BRAWL title at Wednesday CLASH against Badmuts, the title will be on the line on Saturday!!! If Badmuts wins the match then he have to defend the title on Saturday LIVE vs. Mark 'Sexy' Steelman in an I Quit Match!!! So, if Shocker wins the title, this match between Badmuts and Steelman is still on, no matter what!! An interview with........... Badmuts and 'Dildo' Bob! Badmuts: Hello motherf&%$kers, here the greatest wrestlers of the MACHRI World Order and the whole world. 'Dildo' Bob: I guess you all ask yourself who that guy was on Saturday? Badmuts: Bob, don't tell them, he will introduve himself on Wednesday! 'Dildo' Bob: Yeah, you're right! F%#k you FOLKS! Badmuts: Let's hit the town, for some P%$SY and some beers!
