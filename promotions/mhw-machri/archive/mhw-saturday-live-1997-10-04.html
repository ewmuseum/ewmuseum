MHW Saturday LIVE!!

4 October 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
the MACHRI Hardcore Wrestling proudly presents.... October 4, 1997 M.H.W. SATURDAY LIVE !!!!!!! Derek W. Dick: Ladies and gentlemen let me welcome you to another edition of MHW Saturday LIVE!! Unfortunately there was no Wednesday CLASH this week, but we do our best to make it work this coming Wednesday!!!! We have a great card tonight! But first of all, we show you a recorded interview with Insanity! My good buddy! (Fade into Insanity's basement) Insanity: HELLO MHW, WELCOME TO THE PIT OF INSANITY!!! THIS IS MY HOME- AND TODAY I HAVE SOMETHING TO DISCUSS. MYSELF AND DOMINO!!! WE SHOWED ON WEDNESDAY THAT WE ARE THE BEST ANYONE CAN OFFER, WE COMPLETELY DESTROYED THE ALL-AMERICANS AND I LOVED IT!!! (Insanity calms for a second) Insanity: Well, Domino and I will have tag gold before you know it, and Domino, I will always be there to assist you. Big Al, Domino beat you on Saturday, and Eric Draven, YOU'RE NEXT!!! EL PERFECTO BODY, I WANT YOU IN A MATCH SOON. AND THAT'S ALL I GOTTA SAY! Derek W. Dick: Insanity has...., no I think it's better to say nothing. Our first match is a non-title match between MHW International Champion 'Dildo' Bob and Bald Eagle. Highlights: Dildo bounches off the orpes and starts running at Bald Eagle. Bob is nearly decepitated by Eagle's clothesline. Bald Eagle climbs to the top rope. Bald Eagle nails Dildo Bob with a Flying Double Axe Handle. Bald Eagle nails Dildo Bob with the Ole Glory. But can't get the 3 count. After slamming Bald Eagle to the mat Dildo Bob climbs the ropes, Bald Eagle gets up, Dildo Bob executes a Flying Drop kick from the top rope. Dildo Bob slaps on the Cross Face Chicken Wing, Bald Eagle refuses to give up! Bob picks up the stunned Bald Eagle and nails him with the Slob Drop! One...Two.kickout! Dildo Bob slaps on the Sharpshooter! Bald Eagle submits immedialtely!!! The winner by submission: 'DILDO' BOB! Great match! It's was fifty-fifty. I like to this one for the title!!!! Our next match is between Flash and Badmuts. Here are the highlights: Badmuts whips Flash into the ropes, Badmuts nails Flash with a Leaping Clothesline. One...kickout. Picks him up and stuns Flash with a powerful knee lift. Badmuts nails Flash with a Powerbomb, One...Two...shoulder up! Badmuts is stunned by a hard shot right to the nose. Flash slaps on the Figure 4 leglock, Badmuts refuses to give up. Badmuts whips Flash into the ropes and takes him down with a Spinning Leg Lariat. Badmuts lock on the Figure 4 Leglock. Flash has given up. The winner by submission: BADMUTS!! (On the video screen appears Eric 'the Crow' Draven) Eric Draven: Tonight, Domino, Insanity, you D.E.A.D. I want World title! Domino, I want match for title!! I #1 contender!! I destroy you!! Father John II destroys Insanity!! AMEN! Derek W. Dick: Yes, Eric Draven is the #1 contender for the MHW title, I wanna know when Domino wants to put his title on the line against Draven!! Up next: 'El Perfecto Body' Jacques Castonguay -vs- John 'American' Smith. Highlights: John Smith whips Perfecto into the ropes, Perfecto nails John Smith with the Diamond Cutter! 1,2,2½... Perfecto locks on the Sharpshooter! Smith refuses to give up. Domino comes to ringside! Perfecto goes to the top rope, Smith gets up, Perfecto Body nails John 'American' Smith with a Flying Clothesline! El Perfecto Body slaps on the Sharpshooter! John Smith...... Domino gets in the ring and stomps on.. John Smith's head!!! The referee Mr. Robin disqualifies El Perfecto Body!!! Domino leaves with a big smile on his face!!! So winner by DQ: JOHN 'AMERICAN' SMITH!! Perfecto is still a wanted man in the MHW!! Up next Mark 'Sexy' Steelman -vs- 'Big' Al Marvelous. Big Al nails Mark Steelman with the Marvelplex, only a 2 count. Big Al locks on the Boston Crab, Steelman refuses do give up. Big Al executes a Powerbomb. Steelman is standing against the ropes. Big Al goes for a clothesline, Steelman steps a side and Big Al flies over the top rope. Big Al is lying on the concrete floor. Steelman follows him outside, picks him up and whips him into the guardrail. They're back in the ring. Big Al whips Steelman into the ropes, Steelman is coming off the ropes, Big Al elevates Steelman into the lights with a huge back drop. Big Al goes for the cover! One...Two...Three! Winner by pinfall: 'BIG' AL MARVELOUS! A surprise to me, I thought Steelman was favorite in this one. Up next is the awesome tag team of Hell & Heaven -vs- the new tag team of MHW champ Domino & Insanity. Highlights: Insanity whips Eric Draven into the turnbuckle. Insanity slaps Draven in his face. Draven is upset and goes after Insanity, Insanity tags his partner. Domino slams Draven to the mat, floowed by a elbow. Domino picks up Draven and push him to his corner. Draven tags in Father John II. Father John II whips Domino into the ropes and elevates a huge clothesline. Picks him up and locks on an Full Nelson, Domino can reach the ropes. Domino tags his partner. Father John II does the same. Draven naisl Insanity with a Vertical Suplex and clims up the top rope. Insanity gets up and Draven nails him ith a Flying Clothesline. Eric 'the Crow' Draven slaps on the Crow. Insanity has given up. The winners by submission: HELL & HEAVEN!! Ladies and gentlemen, Hell & Heav................... (In the ring Domino is yelling at Insanity) Domino nails Insanity with a DDT! Goes outside and comes back in the ring with some barbed wire. Put the barbed wire on Insanity's chest and goes to the top rope. Domino comes down from the top rope on to Insanity with a huge leg drop. There's blood everywhere!!!! Domino picks up the bleeding Insanity and throws him over the top rope and graps a mic. Domino: "Ladies and gentlemen, another SOB has just been eliminated!!!!" "Insanity, I don't need quiters!! You're just another looser, just like the Assassing!! And he's nowhere to be found, right? Go ahead like your wounds and get another job. Maybe McDonalds needs someone!" "I don't need anyone, I am the MHW World Champion! I did it by myself and I keep the title for a long time! BY MYSELF! Insanity, go home, FOOL!" Derek W. Dick: What should I do laugh or worry about Insanity's condition?? This is the second tag team partner, Domino has dumped in the MHW. First The Assassing and now Insanity! What a story!!! Our next match is between Brain Bond and Clash. Clash whips Bond into the ropes and brings him down to the mat with a well placed drop kick. Picks him up and slams him to the mat. Brain Bond is down in the ring. Clash stomps on Brain Bond's head. Clash goes for the cover and has a handful of tights. Mr. Bombsquad is counting. One..Two..Three. Winner by pinfall: CLASH in 2 minutes and 34 seconds. Well that's it for today! Wednesday CLASH is coming up! I hope to hear more of the condition of Insanity!! Let me leave you with a word from the MHW President. Goodbye! MHW President [name withheld]: I am not happy how things go here in the MHW! Only a few wrestlers send in their interviews and their actions. That's not a way to make it in the MHW!!! What I like to have is at least one interview a week and 1 action. That's the only way to earn a major push!! And tell your friends that the MHW still need some wrestlers/tag teams. But only if they like to do interviews and do some roleplaying!!! Thanx, see you later!
