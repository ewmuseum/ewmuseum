MHW Saturday LIVE

27 September 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
MACHRI HARDCORE WRESTLING proudly presents............ MHW Saturday LIVE!!!!!! (some clips of MACHRI World Order Wednesday Clash were showed) Derek W. Dick: Hello everyone, I'm Derek W. Dick and welcome to MHW Saturday LIVE! Yes, history in the making! A new chapter in wrestling! After a few weeks and 1 PPV, I know for sure, we're here to stay! Tonight the MACHRI World Order titles will be unified to MHW titles! With me tonight Barnesstormer! Barnesstormer: Thank you Derek, I'm glad to be here! MHW Saturday LIVE!!! Our first match is between Mark 'Sexy' Steelman with Little Bad Girl -vs- Clash! Derek W. Dick: Steelman is on a roll! Defeated Perfecto in that 2-hour Iron Man match at DISORDED! Highlights: Steelman nails Clash with a Piledriver, followed by a suplex, 1,2 kickout! Clash slams Steelman to the mat! Picks him up and delivers a German Suplex. Clash goes to the top rope. Steelman gets nailed by a double axe handle from Clash! Clash goes for the cover! Clash has got the 3 count! Winner by pinfall: CLASH! Time of the match: 5 minutes and 30 seconds! Barnesstormer: Surprise, surprise! That's all I can say! Derek W. Dick: I agree, Jimmy! Up next a #1 contender match for the MHW World title! 'El Perfecto Body' Jacques Castonguay with El Sexy Girl -vs- Eric 'the Crow' Draven with The Priest! Highlights: Before the match begins the Inforcers come to the ring, Brian Bond, Curt Vander, Lord Lander, Mark Steelman and Shocker! Who's in trouble? Perfecto? Draven? After a DDT, Eric Draven goes for the Crow! Bond, Vander, Lander and Shocker attack Draven from behind. Brain Bond executes a piledriver, Steelman distracts the referee! Vander nails Draven with a Daimond Cutter! Steelman goes after El Sexy Girl! Lander hits Draven with his Flag Pole! Steelman kisses El Sexy Girl on her mouth! Shocker slaps Draven in his face! The referee is back on the job! Perfecto picks up the stunned Draven and elevates Draven with a Vertical Suplex! Shocker distracts the referee! Steelman gets in the ring and nails Perfecto from behind! Perfecto and Eric Draven lying on the floor! The ref counts: 1,2,3,4,5,6,7,8 Perfecto gets up. Perfecto nails Draven with a DDT! One..Two..shoulder up. Perfecto whips Draven into the ropes, Shocker trips Draven, the referee sees it. He disqualifies El Perfecto Body! The winner by DQ: ERIC 'the Crow' DRAVEN!!! Barnesstormer: Eric 'the Crow' Draven is now officialy the #1 contender for the MHW World title. He gets a title shot at next week MHW Saturday LIVE!!!! Derek W. Dick: The Enforcers said the WAR is on! We just saw it!! Both men had a rough time!! Up next is Insanity -vs- Flash! Highlights: After a bodyslam Insanity locks on the Buzz Killer! Flash refuses to give up! Insanity climbs the ropes. Insanity nails Flash with a Frog Splash! Flash is down in the ring, Insanity locks on the Headscissors! Flash submits immediately! Winner by submission: INSANITY!!!! Barnesstormer: Insanity dominated the whole match! No joy for Flash! Up next a MHW World tag team Championship match!! Current MACHRI World Order World tag teams Champions The Bloody Blue with Little Bad Girl -vs- current MACHRI World Order European tag team Champions Hell & Heaven with The Priest. There are no lost love between these two teams! The winners will become the MHW World tag team champions! Highlights: Father John II attacks Lord Lander before the bell rings! Little Bad Girl hits Father John II with her Whip! Lander goes for the cover, 1,2, kickout! Lord Lander locks on the Boston Crab. Father John II refuses to give up! Lander tags his partner, Vander locks on the Figure 4 leglock. Father John II has given up! The winners of the match and MHW World tag team champions BLOODY BLUE, Curt Vander & Lord Lander! Derek W. Dick: Bloody Blue! A really great tag team! MHW tag team champs, allright! Up next is for the MHW International Championship and is between current MACHRI World Order BRAWL Champion Brain Bond with Little Bad Girl and current MACHRI World Order European Champion 'Dildo' Bob with Batfinger! Highlights: Brain Bond whips Dildo Bob into the ropes, Bob nails Bond with a Frankensteiner! Dildo Bob is on the top rope and Brain Bond is down in the ring. Dildo Bob flies of the top rope and lands on top of Brain Bond! Dildo Bob goes for the cover. One..Two...Three!! Winner by pinfall and undisputed MWH International Champion 'DILDO' BOB!!!! Barnesstormer: Great match, I like to a rematch of this one!! Bec................ (suddenly Eric Draven, Father John II and The Priest come to the ring and attack Brian Bond. The Priest hits Bond with his Iron Cross. Eric Draven locks on his finisher The Crow!. After this he throws Brain Bond out of the ring and spits on him, he graps the mic and start talking) Eric 'the Crow' Draven: "Bond, you fool, go to gay friends! You Dildo Bob, like you! John II, me, October 11 against Inforcers! Badmuts and you, help us???" (Dildo Bob leaves the ring and leave Draven and his friends in the ring) Derek W. Dick: No answer!!!! I hope to hear from Dildo Bob and Badmuts very soon! Yes, ladies and gentlemen, this match is already signed. The Inforcers, Brain Bond, Curt Vander, Lord Lander and Shocker will meet Eric Draven, Father John II and 2 other wrestlers in a WAR-Games kind of match at Saturday LIVE, October 11, 1997!!! We keep you up to date on this one!!! Barnesstormer: I can hardly wait to see this one! It's time for our Main Event. It is for the MHW World Heavyweight title. Current MACHRI World Order Champion Domino -vs- 'Big' Al Marvelous. The winner will become the undisputed MHW World Champion! Highlights: Big Al whips Domino into the ropes and goes for a clothesline. Domino ducks and nails Big Al with a SC Stunner! Big Al is out cold! Domino picks up the stunned Big Al and goes for a Powerbomb, Big Al counters it with a Small Package, One, Two, Thr.... kicked out! Big Al complains about a slow count! Domino comes from behind and give Big Al another SC Stunner. Picks him up and executes his Slingshot, Face First, Powerbomb. One....Two...THREE!!! Winner by pinfall and undisputed MWH World Champion DOMINO!!!! Derek W. Dick: This means that Domino will defend his title at MWH Saturday LIVE, October 4 against Eric 'the Crwo' Draven! A great night! Let me leave you with our new champions: MHW World Haevyweight Champion: DOMINO MHW International Champion 'DILDO' BOB MHW World Tag Team Champions THE BLOODY BLUE Thank you for tonight, for Barnesstormer and myself, GOODBYE!
