Down Under Wrestling Association Rulebook - Version 1.1 -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- ** INFORMATION ON THE DUWA ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ The DUWA stands for the "Down Under Wrestling Association", and is run by President [name withheld]. I, [name withheld], have experince in the world of E-wrestling, as I have held titles in the now defunct UWWA, and the ever popular AEWF. I have owned a league before, the WRL but because it was owned by me AND another person, I was unable to do what I needed. It was also a simmed fed, and did not allow me to express angles the way I would have enjoyed. The Co-Owner quit on me, without notice, and the fed fell through, since he was the person with all of the wrestlers names, the roster, and all of the information. But don't worry, that fate will not end the Down Under Wrestling Association, instead of three shows a week, plus a monthly PPV like in the WRL, I have spaced it out with less shows. Read on for that. I hope you enjoy reading this, and feel free to use it as a guideline for writing your own rulebook for your federation. When you finish, and if you think youy can handle it, send in your application. I just want to leave you with this. You send all flashes to [name withheld], email address at end, who will then send it out to the whole group twice during the week. Your name and e-mail address will not be given out to the other handlers, so don't worry about spam. The start date for the DUWA is tentatively scheduled for Late-August, but could be pushed back as far as October, depending on how the applications go. ** APPLICATION ~~~~~~~~~~~ Each handler can enter ONE singles wrestler *OR* ONE Tag Team. This will allow the handler to concentrate on leading their particular wrestler to the title they want. There will be a maximum of 25 singles wrestlers in the DUWA, and 8 Tag Teams. This will allow good tounrmanets in both if the need arises, and enough men to fill out a Pay Per View. When applying, do NOT make a stupid or unbelievable gimmick like "Jim the Soldier", or "The Clown". They will not be accepted in this federation, although men like Vader, Raven, and the like will. They may also have names that sound real, like Kevin Harris, Chris Harding, and Harry Samadichiadis. Keep in mind, since this is a booked fed, there will be no REAL wrestlers accepted. The federation is for MADE UP wrestlers only. ** DUWA PROGRAMMING ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ Unlike many other e-mail wrestling federations, the DUWA will NOT be gone in one month because of the result of "Burnout". For that reason, we will be having ONE weekly card, plus two "Flash Updates", on Wednesday and Sunday, which will contain all the flashes sent in during that time period. The weekly show, held on a Tuesday Night, is called "DUWA Tuesday Night Timebomb". It is sent out Wednesday day Australian Time, and you will read it Wednesday Morning or Night. There are bi-monthly Pay-Per-View events. This means there is a major show every two months. They will be held in February, April, June, August, October and December. Their names have been determined, and are below. The DUWA's version of "Wrestlemania" will be the October Pay per View, titled "DUWA Starslam". It is an outdoor event, held in Australia, or the United States. The weekly show contains 5 matches, while the Pay Per View will alternate, with 8 being the least amount on the card. The two other weekly shows are Challenge shows, in which Flashes will be sent out. There are Flash/Compilation shows before the Pay Per View's are on, to recap the events surrounding a particular match. Here is a listing of the shows and PPV's: Pay Per Views: Valentines Day Massacre (February) Collision of the Contenders (April) Winter Rumble (June) War to Settle the Score (August) Starslam (Outdoors; October; 12,000+ Attendance) Christmas Combat (December) Weekly Shows: Tuesday - Tuesday Night Timebomb (5 Matches) [Wrestling show] Thursday - DUWA Challenger [Flash show] Sunday - DUWA Sunday Night Slammers [Flash show] ** MAIN RULES ~~~~~~~~~~ During Flashes: - Do NOT attack any other wrestlers, or any of the staff of the DUWA (listed below) as that will cause injury, and make a new man come in. We don't need that, yet. The ONLY exception to this rule is if the President has OK'ed it to attack a staff member, and the player has OK'ed it to attack their wrestler. If a wrestler is on the way out, then the President will make up a roleplay interview, or attack, if the player doesn't. - There is NO swearing during a roleplay. You may type "[CENSORED]" in your flashes if you wish, but there will be no "four letter words". The only swearing that is accepted is "ass". This is not to be overused. It may be said up to twice in an interview, once a week, per HANDLER. If you wish to suggest an angle, e-mail the President, [name withheld], with your request. He can be reached at [detail withheld] and make sure the subject line is "DUWA". Each HANDLER must present one flash each two weeks. That is not to many, and will give enough time to get a good one out. If your wrestler gives more flashes in a week, then he will be rewarded with a better push than most others. If your wrestler, on the other hand, doesn't give a flash, then he will be penalised with what we at the DUWA offices call "Job Duty" for that two week period. ** OPERATION ~~~~~~~~~ The DUWA is a "booked" federation, meaning that the match results are determined by the wrestling council. The result of a match depends on several things: - Angles (Storylines) - Quality/Frequency of Flashes - PPV/Weekly Show - Type of Match If two wrestlers, equal in the Flashes department are having a match, and it is a non title, or title match on PPV, with the match not necesarilly suiting either of them, then the Presidnet, [name withheld], will use TNM v6.2 PLUS to Simulate the match, to make it fair. He will then take an angle from there. ** TITLES ~~~~~~ The titles currently in the DUWA: Singles + DUWA Heavyweight Championship + DUWA Lightweight Championship (210 pound Weight Limit) Tag Teams + DUWA Tag Team Championship All titles can change hands by Pinfall or Submission, unless there is a special prematch stipulation.
