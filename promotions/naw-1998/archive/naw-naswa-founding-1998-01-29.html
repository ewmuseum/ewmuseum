Here...

29 January 1998
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
People have advertised many federations... Some are good, some just plain suck. Do you thrive on quality action? Do you look for great matches and interviews? This isn't some wacked fed. This is the NASWA. Can you stand the competition? Here we go...
North American Supreme Wrestling Alliance They said that they wanted a revolution... They claimed that they didn't have the competition... Who knew that it would come down to this? You wanted it... Now... You are going to get it! Ian Walsh presents: N.A.S.W.A. Somebody is going to get their butt whipped tonight. Cards: Supreme Monday: This card will consist of 4-5 matches. These matches will be written out. There will be roleplays included in this and there will be interviews. There will be surprizes and other things. This is supreme...
House Shows: These are just shows that come on from time to time. There are two of these per month. I will have 3-4 matches on each of them.
Super Cards: (PPVs) These are once a month and there are not some pathetic names for them.
January: Winter Blast February: Cold War March: Wrestlefest April: Time to plant the pansies! May: Original Masters June: Super Slam July: Summer Heat August: Summer in the City September: Bungle in the Jungle October: Hauntings November: Thankgiving Harvest December: Holiday Horror Titles: No stupid trash here! You want real titles? You got em... N.A.S.W.A. World Heavyweight Title- Yeah, you win the title and think that you are safe... Not! You win this title, you will defend this title! I want this title on the line once a month! If you can't hack it, pack your bags and go to some pansy fed. I don't have time for losers.
N.A.S.W.A. Tag Team Titles- Don't think that you are safe here... This has the same rules as the World Heavyweight Title. Your team has to be able to work hard or be losers. In order to win, you must be good.
I bet that some of you think that there should be more titles. Well, there is really no point. Do I have hardcore in the title? Do I have extreme in the title? This isn't a place where there needs to be a million titles. Let's keep this good, but simple. If you are good enough, you will earn a title.
Roleplays: This is pretty simple. Your roleplay will consist of the following: Scene Talking Lines from end to end No giant fonts At least 30 lines Introductions are optional. I don't want people being tossed. Limit the swearing. All roleplays will be sent to me. I will send the roleplays out in a newsletter type thing. I will keep you updated on cards and news. Roleplays are due the day before the event. No exceptions. As a matter of fact, I want them in by 7:00 the day before the event. You can have challenges and requests in your roleplays.
Other things that are important: 1.No junk mail! I don't want stupid things filling up my mail box. I don't want chain letters, petitions, complaints sent to everyone. If you have a situation that we need to discuss, talk to me.
2.Use the lists that I send out for your roleplaying. I will send a roster every so often.
3.If I put you in a match and you don't roleplay, I will job your wrestler and then fire you. I don't take garbage in my federation. If you quit the federation before an event, I will replace you or just not have the match.
These wrestlers are your copyright and not mine. 4.Proofread what you send me. I don't want to have to do everything. 5.You can scroll down and see an application. For you people who want to use real wrestlers, leave right now! I mean that! Just delete this mail and go away. I want original wrestlers. By the way, if you put for your wrestler
"Heartbreak Kid" or "3:16" or any other run offs, I will not accept it. Come up with original things. You must have some kind of imagination.
6.Listen to whatever I say... I know what I am doing. Application: (Fill out and send to me, [detail withheld] ) 1 singles and/or 1 tag team: E-mail address: Wrestler(s): Height: Weight: Description and attire: Entrance music and what you do: Setup maneuver(s): 1. 2. Finisher: Other Maneuvers: 1. 2. 3. 4. 5. Manager: Attire for manager: Description of manager: Have some fun! (C)-N.A.S.W.A. 1998
