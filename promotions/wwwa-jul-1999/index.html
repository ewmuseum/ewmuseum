Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The World Wide Wrestling Alliance opened for business on 5 July 1999, the day after its own predecessor's closing pay-per-view, as a direct continuation under a new name and new titles. Its Handbook v1.0 introduced its own president in his own words: “My name is Joe Eckert, and I am the president of the World Wide Wrestling Alliance... As the former president of the very successful OWWA, and a co-president of the now defunct High Octane Wrestling, I decided that I again wanted to run my own federation.” It ran booked and by e-mail, with four titles on offer from the start — a World Heavyweight Championship, World Tag Team Championship, Cruiserweight Championship and Hardcore Championship — each required to defend at least once a month.

Its broadcast slate ran Wednesday Wargames as the flagship show, a Sunday Night Special built to set up angles rather than settle them, and a roleplay-only daily flash show, WWWA Trashtalk, with pay-per-views running monthly.

Its 22 September 1999 Wargames created the Hardcore Championship outright, Cryptic Saber winning an eight-man battle royal through the arena's own parking garage, and turned the Cruiserweight title over the same night: “Traditional” Tommy Brooks announced his retirement live on the air, vacating it with no notice, and on-screen commissioner Chris Bane handed it by decree to “The Rocket” Jarry Rice. Elsewhere on the card, Alec Empire won with his finisher, Scar Tissue, after Mark Jones missed a top-rope leg drop.

Two weeks later, from Madison Square Garden, the World Tag Team titles changed hands when a referee knocked out moments earlier only just recovered in time to count Prophet's cover of Chris Hopper after Oracle shoved Hopper off the top rope — the Dark Forces (Prophet and Oracle) taking the belts from the Too Cool Connection. Cryptic Saber defended the Hardcore title against Empire in a boiler room brawl, escaping through its own third door with a Saber Slam onto a garbage can. And Jarry Rice lost the Cruiserweight title to a masked challenger who unmasked afterward as Mike Holmes, helped to the win by a second masked man revealed as “Too Extreme” Walker Quinn, who blinded Rice with powder and pulled the World Heavyweight Champion's own ankle out from under him at ringside.

That Heavyweight Champion, Razor, is billed as champion throughout the whole of the surviving record without ever being shown defending the title, still holding it when the record ends. No further broadcast survives past 6 October 1999; how much longer the World Wide Wrestling Alliance continued isn't in the surviving record.

Hardcore World Wrestling Alliance (HWWA)

Predecessor to WWWA (Jul 1999)
The promotion's own operator announced, 29 June 1999, that his prior Hardcore World Wrestling Alliance would cease to exist the day after its own closing pay-per-view and be replaced in full, under this new name, effective 5 July 1999 -- the same rename the held predecessor's own registry row already records from its own side.
