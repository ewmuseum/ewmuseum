I. Introduction First off, let me introduce myself. My name is Joe Eckert, and I am the president of the World Wide Wrestling Alliance, or WWWA for short. The
WWWA was created to try and take the next step to providing e-wrestling entertainment by taking the best ideas from all the federations I was in, and putting them all together into one. As the former president of the very successful OWWA, and a co-president of the now defunct High
Octane Wrestling, I decided that I again wanted to run my own federation, and that's how the WWWA was born.
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
II. About the WWWA As I said, the WWWA took the ideas from a number of different federations, as well as people's posts on RSPWF, and I put these ideas all into one in the WWWA. For example, I wasn't very happy with a lot of ways federations decided their standings, so I created a pay system for the WWWA that was totally unbiased when determining standings. This pay system is one of the unique things about the WWWA. Because of my past experience with both simmed and booked federations, I was sure I wanted the WWWA to be a booked federation, because booked is the only way to have a fair decision on who wins and loses matches. Booked federations also attract more attention, and in my opinion are more fun to play because angles are able to be more developed because there is no simulator that screws up the storyline during a match. The WWWA is run by email, which some people do not prefer, but I have found that the majority do like email federations better, because they are much more convenient than having to check a message board to see if there are any new roleplays. The WWWA currently runs a closed-flash email system which I just began, so I don't know if we are going to remain this way or return to open. If you join, your comments are always welcome, and will be taken into consideration.
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
III. Rules and Regulations Ever fed needs it's rules, and the WWWA has them too. I'm not going to go into them in extreme detail here, because they are posted on the website, but let me give you an overview of them. What the rules say are that we're here to have fun, so don't do anything that will make people not have fun. I can tolerate some swearing in roleplays, but keep it real. If you have a wrestler who swears every two words, he's not going to make it very far before I tell you to make a new character or leave. One thing that won't be tolerated is bringing up someone's sexual orientation (ie calling someone gay). All this does is makes your wrestler seem unintelligent, and will also cost you by way of a fine (another good thing about the pay system we have). Other rules include no real wrestlers, the neccessity to roleplay at least once a week, and that you can only handle up to three wrestlers.
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
IV. Broadcasts 1) Wednesday Wargames- Wargames is the main show for the week, like WCW's Nitro or the WWF's Raw Is War. This is where the majority of the title matches will take place, and where everything big will take place. There will be between four and eight matches on Wargames, and it will be written in summary format with commentary.
2) Sunday Night Special- Special is the equivalent of WCW's Thunder or WWF's Sunday Night Heat, in that some big matches will take place, but it is mainly to develop angles going into Wargames and pay-per-views.
On weekends of WWWA pay-per-views, Special will not take place. There will be between three and five matches on Special, and it will be written in summary format with commentary.
3) WWWA Trashtalk- This is the WWWA's official flash show. No matches will take place on this show, and there will be no commentary. It is strictly roleplays, and will be sent out every day or every other day.
4) Pay-Per-Views- Pay-Per-Views are where all of the "storyline-ending" matches will take place. All of the legendary matches of the WWWA will likely take place at a pay-per-view. All of the matches will be main event matches, and it is likely that all of the titles will be on the line. Pay-Per-Views will be approximately once every month, with between six and ten matches, and will be written in
play-by-play format. --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
V. Titles 1) World Heavyweight Title- The WWWA World Heavyweight Title is the most sought-after belt in the whole wrestling world. Whoever holds this belt is the best of the best. This title must be defended at least once every month, or the belt will be vacated.
2) World Tag Team Titles- The team wearing the WWWA World Tag Team Titles can call themselves the best, because they are. The tag team titles, like the World Title, must be defended at least once a month.
3) Cruiserweight Title- Have a guy who weighs 235 pounds or under? Then he qualifies as a Cruiserweight. This title is for the smaller guys in the fed, and although it may not be as prestigious as the World
Heavyweight Title, the Cruiserweight Title signifies the best lightweight athlete. Again, the Cruiserweight Title must be defended at least once a month.
4) Hardcore Title- The Hardcore Title isn't for the weak of heart. If your guy is willing to turn himself and his opponent into a bloody mess trying to win a title, then this is the title for him. Don't expect to see too many wrestling moves in this division, because this title signifies the best brawler in the wrestling world, and it too must be defended at least once a month.
