WWWA (Jul 1999) — Card Archive

Wednesday Wargames

22 September 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The scene opens to a black background with the WWWA logo set in front of it, centered on the screen. A fast bass line begins, and is soon followed by guitar and drums as the logo shatters and gives way to a number of video clips. These opening credits quickly end, leaving us with a birdseye view of the Hartford Civic Center. Thousands of screaming fans pack the arena as the camera cuts to the broadcast location where Sandy Karsen and "Dangerous" Dan Santos sit.]
SK: Welcome to WWWA Wednesday Wargames!!! I'm Sandy Karsen... DS: And I'm Dan Santos. SK: We're coming to you live from the Hartford Civic Center, and man is it packed! It's standing room only here in Hartford, and these fans are pretty rowdy.
DS: They came to see some action and they're going to get it! Just look at our main event tonight; a Hardcore Battle Royal, which will be held in the arena's parking garage! 8 men will go in, one man will come out, and that man will be crowned the new WWWA Hardcore Champion.
SK: That's not the only thing! We've got "Traditional" Tommy Brooks putting the Cruiserweight strap on the line against Jarry "The Rocket"
Rice! Hold on fans, I'm being told there's something going on in the back. Let's see if we can't get a camera back there, huh?
[There is a pause, when the screen cuts to the office of WWWA Commishioner Chris Bane.] CB: What do you mean you're not wrestling?! TB: I told you, I'm through. I've done everything a man could do in this sport, and now I want out.
[The camera cuts back to the broadcast location.] SK: He's out? "Traditional" Tommy Brooks, the WWWA Cruiserweight
Champion is handing in the belt and hanging up the tights?
DS: I guess so. But he is right, he's done everything a man could do in this sport, and it's about time he takes some time to enjoy what he's earned.
SK: I guess you're right, but what's going to happen with this match coming up tonight? [As if on cue, "Kids of the K-Hole" by NOFX begins to play, and Chris
Bane walks from the entranceway. The fans begin to cheer as he makes his way down the aisle toward the ring. He steps quickly through the ropes, and grabs a microphone from ring announcer Lance Mayson.]
CB: Ladies and gentlemen, as you saw just moments ago, Tommy Brooks has decided to officially retire from wrestling. But that leaves us without an opponent for Jarry "The Rocket" Rice tonight. Seeing as how the president, Joseph Eckert isn't here tonight and left me in charge, I've decided that the title is going to be automatically handed over to Jarry
"The Rocket" Rice!!!
[Some fans begin cheering, at the same time many others boo.] CB: Hold on, I'm not finished. As I said, the title is going to be handed over to Jarry "The Rocket" Rice, so Rocket, if you'd come down here now to get your title...
[We wait a few seconds, when "Running With the Pack" by Bad Company begins to play. Jarry "The Rocket" Rice makes his way out, beaming, and makes his way to the ring. He climbs through the ropes, and greets Bane with a handshake.]
CB: Mr. Rice, your Cruiserweight Title... [Bane hands Rice the title as fans begin to boo and throw garbage into the ring.] SK: Come on! He didn't earn that title! Make him fight for it! CB: Now, I'm sure a lot of you are unhappy with my decision, but let me finish. Because the title is simply being handed over to Jarry Rice, he'll be expected to defend it next week against the number one contender. But who is the number one contender? All of the cruiserweights in the WWWA roster will battle it out next week, and the only man standing will be the number one contender and will face The
Rocket later on in the night!
[The fans begin to cheer as Jarry Rice gets a concerned look on his face.] CB: And seeing as how I have the power for tonight, there's one more thing. Next week, during the main event, I will be the special guest referee to make sure things go the way they should, and security will surround the ring to prevent any outside interference. Sandy, Dan, back to you!
SK: Thank you Mr. Bane. So no Cruiserweight Title match here tonight, but how about this?! Rice will have to defend the Cruiserweight Title against the number one challenger next week on our program!
DS: But how fair is that to his opponent? You can't actually expect a man to fight twice in one night and be in the best condition for his second match, can you?
SK: If they want it bad enough, they'll fight twice in one night for it. Now, without further delay, lets go to the ring for our opening bout.
LM: Ladies and gentlemen, introducing first, from Seattle, Washington,
Alec “The Destroyer” Empire!!!
[Alec Empire makes his way to the ring and awaits the arrival of his opponent.] LM: And his opponent, from our nation’s capital, representing the
Razor’s From Dollywood, “The All American” Mark Jones!!!
[“Real American” blasts through the pa as The All American makes his way to the ring. He climbs into the ring after slapping a few fans high fives.]
SK: There’s the bell! They circle each other, and as they go to lock up Empire nails Jones with a knee to the gut. Empire is raining blows across the head and neck of The All American as he backs up into the corner!
DS: The referee asks for the break, and Empire begins to back up. He rushes quickly back in, going for a clothesline, but Jones ducks out of the way and Empire hits the turnbuckle! Jones rolls him up in an inside cradle!
MO: One! Two! SK: Jones almost had him there! Jones pulls Empire up, and chops
Empire back against the ropes. Jones springs off the far ropes, and both men go over the top!
DS: That clothesline may have hurt Jones more than Empire, and Jones’ shoulder hit the ring apron and he is now holding it in pain. Empire gets to his feet, and pulls Jones up. Empire whips him into the ring post shoulder first!
SK: That’s not going to help Jones’ shoulder get any better! Empire tries to whip Jones into the other ring post, but Jones reverses it, and
Empire collides head first with the steel post. Jones rolls into the ring to stop the referee’s count, and then goes back to the outside.
DS: Jones with a stomp, and another! Empire is making his way to his feet, but Jones begins firing blows at Empire. Empire rakes the eyes, and that stops Jones in his tracks. Empire falls back, grabbing Jones’ tights and sending him head first into the steel guardrail. Empire rolls back into the ring.
SK: Empire doing the smart thing, as he knows he can’t win the match if he stays on the outside. Empire goes toward the ropes, prompting the referee to stop his count.
DS: Now here’s a show of inexperience by Alec Empire. He should let the referee continue his count, as now The All American has more time to recover on the outside. Jones finally climbs up onto the apron, and
Empire goes to meet him. Jones catches him with a knee to the stomach, and then gets him in a pinning predicament with a sunset flip.
MO: One! Two! SK: Empire kicks out after two, and both men are to their feet! Empire goes for a running clothesline, but Jones ducks, and as Empire turns to meet him, gets hit with a boot to the stomach. Jones goes off the ropes, and takes Empire down with a perfectly executed spinning neckbreaker.
DS: Jones is heading towards the turnbuckle, and this is very unlike him. He’s more of a brawler than a high flyer, but it will be interesting to see what he tries here. Jones to the top, but Empire is up to meet him. Empire sweeps Mark Jones’ legs out from under him, putting him on the top turnbuckle. Empire climbs the turnbuckle, but
Jones fights back with a low blow.
SK: Empire is reeling, and Jones in turn sets him up on the turnbuckle. He may be going for it all here folks! Jones climbing to the top, signals to the fans, and – it’s The Fourth of July! Jones with the cover!
MO: One! Two! DS: Empire is in the ropes! Jones pulls Empire up, and sets him up for a tombstone piledriver. Alec Empire is fighting desperately to escape, and reverses the tombstone, planting Jones in the middle of the ring.
Alec Empire quickly goes to the turnbuckle, and climbs to the top. Seeing his opponent still on the ground, he jumps off the top with a kneedrop, and connects with the side of Jones’ head. Empire with the lateral press.
MO: One! Two! SK: He didn’t quite have him there. If he hooked the leg, this match may have been over with. Empire pulls Jones up, and sends him into the turnbuckle. Jones hits the turnbuckle and bounces back, and Empire catches him by the hair and places him under his arm.
DS: Looks like it’s Empire’s turn to go for the win! The Destroyer moves to the middle of the ring, where he drops Jones with the Scar
Tissue! Empire with the lateral press, and he hooks the leg!
MO: One! Two! Three! LM: Ladies and gentlemen, your winner as a result of pinfall, Alec “The
Destroyer” Empire!!!
SK: Alec Empire with a very convincing win over “The All American” Mark
Jones. I’d say that match could have gone either way, as both men made their share of mistakes, but Empire capitalized at the end and ended up with the win.
DS: I thought it was all over with after Jones hit the Fourth of July, but Empire was still clear-headed enough to find the ropes and break the count. After that, things just went downhill for The All American.
SK: Fans, we’ll be right back after these commercials from our sponsor! [Commercial.] Where do real wrestling fans go for the inside information? The Wrestle-Factor, that’s where! Find out wrestler’s birthdays, or take part in the World Wide Wrestling Alliance, the official e-federation of the Wrestle-Factor. The Wrestle-Factor – Where real wrestling fans go for news!
[End Commercial.] DS: Welcome back. It’s time for that match we’ve all been waiting for, where 8 men will battle for the ultimate prize in the Hardcore wrestling world, the WWWA Hardcore Title!
SK: I’m told the parking garage is being opened now, and one by one the athletes are making their way in through separate entrances.
DS: It looks like Razor and Cryptic Saber have spotted each other first as they advance towards each other. Ooh, The Carleton County Kid with a chair to the head of Razor! Cryptic Saber and The CC Kid begin stomping on Razor as Dan Davidson comes to his friend’s rescue.
SK: Davidson with a football tackle on Cryptic Saber, taking him down to the concrete floor! Meanwhile Riot and Hardline are going at it!
The two men trade punches, and Riot hits Hardline with a front-faced jawbreaker, taking him down hard. Riot goes in search of a weapon, and pulls a barbed-wire covered baseball bat from behind a trash bin!
DS: I wonder if that’s one of the weapons Razor hid in this parking garage? Riot advances toward Hardline, but Hardline nails him with a kick to the gut, causing him to drop his weapon. Hardline picks up the baseball bat, and just as it looks as if he’s going to bring it down onto Riot, “The Suicide Machine” Tommy Jobber nails Hardline from behind with a garbage can!
SK: This is the first time we’ve seen Tommy Jobber in a while. For those fans who haven’t been watching our program for long, Tommy Jobber is the former Hardcore Champion of the World! Jobber pulls Hardline up, and with the help of Riot, they double ddt Hardline onto the trash can, causing it to fold from the impact.
DS: There’s some cooperation on the part of Tommy Jobber and that
“redneck” from Dallas, Texas, Riot. Prophet and The CC Kid are now battling it out on top of a police car! Prophet sets the Carleton
County Kid up, and smashes him into the hook with the Prophecy! Prophet makes the cover on the hood of the car, and the referee makes the count!
MO: One! Two! Three! SK: Now we’re down to seven men, but it doesn’t look like that’ll last long. Carved In Stone now has Hardline up, and they take him down with a spike powerbomb onto the cement floor! Dan Davidson makes the cover and Razor prevents Cryptic Saber from making the save.
MO: One! Two! Three! DS: Now there’s only six men left, and half of them are RFD members! Prophet and Tommy Jobber lock up, and Jobber whips the man from San
Diego into the back of an armored truck. Jobber picks up a “No Parking” sign, and nails Prophet with it, knocking the man out cold. Jobber goes for the cover.
MO: One! Two! Three! SK: Three down, five to go! Cryptic Saber and Dan Davidson are going at it. Saber slams Davidson into a car’s hood head first, and then takes him down with a reverse ddt! Cryptic Saber sets up a section of steel fence against the back of a truck, picks Davidson up by his head, and throws him into the fence. Cryptic Saber goes for the pin, but
Razor breaks it just before being nailed by Riot with a shovel!
DS: Riot still has that shovel, and this time he brings it over the head of Cryptic Saber! Riot with the lateral press on Cryptic Saber.
MO: One! Two! SK: Cryptic Saber is busted open! Riot pulls Saber up but takes him back down with a piledriver. Meanwhile Jobber and Dan Davidson are going at it. Davidson tries to whip Tommy Jobber into a cement support, but Jobber reverses it and Davidson is laid out. Jobber with the cover!
MO: One! Two! Three! SK: There’s only four men left, and Jobber is going after Riot with a piece of pipe! He takes a swing at him, but Riot ducks and Jobber connects with a car window, shattering it. Riot grabs some of the glass and throws it into the face of Jobber. The small shards of glass cut
Tommy Jobber’s face open as he begins to bleed profusely. Riot jumps on top of him, and begins rubbing glass across the bleeding face of Tommy
Jobber, tearing the skin even more.
DS: Razor and Cryptic Saber both get to their feet at the same time! Razor and Cryptic Saber begin trading blows as Riot pulls Tommy Jobber to his feet! Riot backs off, grabbing an orange construction cone, and rushes at Tommy Jobber, who meets his running opponent with the Pearl
Necklace! He goes for the cover!
MO: One! Two! Three! SK: The only men left are Cryptic Saber, Razor, and Tommy Jobber! The bleeding Tommy Jobber makes his way over to his two opponents, and grabs
Razor. He whips Razor into a cement support, and Razor drops to the floor. Jobber pulls him up, and just as he is about to grab him Cryptic
Saber runs into both of them carrying a barbed-wire covered table! They both go head first into the cement support, and Razor falls on top of
Tommy Jobber!
MO: One! Two! Three! SK: Cryptic Saber rolls Razor off Jobber’s bleeding body, and makes the cover! MO: One! Two! Three! LM: Ladies and gentlemen, your winner, and new WWWA Hardcore Champion of the World, Cryptic Saber!!!
SK: Your winner, a bloody, broken Cryptic Saber. DS: That match was pure chaos. I mean that was the pure epitome of
Hardcore! SK: Dan, you ain’t seen nothin’ yet. Fans, we’re out of time, so until next week, I’m Sandy Karsen…
DS: And I’m “Dangerous” Dan Santos, sayin’ good night everybody! [The words “Copyright 1999, World Wide Wrestling Alliance (WWWA)” flash across the screen, and then it slowly fades to black as Cryptic Saber stands in the parking garage holding the Hardcore Title high above his bloody head.]
