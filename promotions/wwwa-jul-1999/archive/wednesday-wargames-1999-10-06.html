Wednesday Wargames

6 October 1999 (posted in three parts, the same day)
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The scene opens to Madison Square Garden in New York, NY. The camera zooms in to ringside, and pans across the fans. The camera comes to stop on the broadcast booth, where Sandy Karsen, "Dangerous" Dan Santos, and Tom Gross sit. Karsen and Gross are wearing identical WWWA golf shirts while Dan Santos wears a multicolored Hawaiian button up.]
SK: Fans, welcome to Wargames! I'm Sandy Karsen, beside me as always is "Dangerous" Dan Santos, and lookie what we got here! Back by popular demand, possible the best wrestling announcer in the history of the business, Tom Gross!
TG: Thank you Sandy, it's great to be back commentating. After getting my feet wet filling in for you last week, I knew I couldn't stay away much longer, and when WWWA President Joe Eckert himself approached me with an offer, I was quick to sign with the company.
DS: Oh great, now I've got tweedle-dumb and tweedle-dumber to work with. Who's who? Who cares?
TG: Thanks a lot Dan, you sure know how to make a guy feel welcome. But anyway, we've got some great wrestling action for you tonight in this action packed hour long program! Three titles will be up for grabs as the Too Cool Connection will defend the tag straps against the Dark
Forces, Cryptic Saber will try and hold onto his Hardcore Title in a boiler room brawl against Alec "The Destroyer" Empire, and the masked man will try to beat Jarry "The Rocket" Rice for the second week in a row to become the WWWA Cruiserweight Champion!
SK: This masked man is awfully familiar. I just can't piece together the voice and the wrestling style.
TG: Well my inside connections have let me know who this man is, but
I'm going to keep it a secret, because here tonight he'll be unmasked, whether he wins or not!
DS: I thought that this was a title vs. mask match. If he wins, he gets the title and keeps his mask, but if he loses, Rice takes the mask off of him. SK: That's true Dan, but if you were listening at all to what he said yesterday on Flashtalk, no matter if he wins or loses, the mask is coming off, and it's as simple as that.
TG: But if he loses, Rice gets to take the mask off of the masked man, and he'll be publicly humiliated by the defeat.
DS: That's true. Ahh, humiliation, is there any greater way to totally destroy your enemies?
SK: There's always barbed-wire, but let's no get caught up in that right now. We're going to go straight to the ring for our first match of the evening!!!
LM: Ladies and gentlemen, this next match is scheduled for one fall, with a 20 minute time limit. It will be for the WWWA World Tag Team
Titles!!! Introducing first, the challengers, representing the RFD,
Prophet, Oracle, The Dark Forces!!!
[The lights dim and the Dark Forces appear from the entranceway. They make their way slowly and confidently to the ring, where they wait for the arrival of the champs.]
LM: And their opponents, from Calgary, Alberta, Canada, representing the Canadian Connection, the WWWA Tag Team Champions of the World... The
Carleton County Kid, "The Iceman" Chris Hopper, the Too Cool
Connection!!! ["You Start The Fire" begins and the fans erupt. Everyone in the Garden is on their feet as the Too Cool Connection appears from the entranceway. They make their way to the ring, slapping high fives with fans. They slide into the ring, where they hand the tag team titles to referee Mike Ocoboc, who hands the titles to Lance Mayson outside the ring and signals for the bell.]
TG: And this match is under way! It looks like Chris Hopper and Prophet are going to start it off. They circle each other, and lock up. Chris Hopper shoves Prophet backwards, and he falls to the mat, but quickly gets to his feet. The two men lock up again, and this time it is Prophet who shoves Hopper to the mat.
DS: Both of these men are trying to out-do the other one, but that is a fight Prophet doesn't want, as Chris Hopper is just towering over him.
SK: Prophet rushes in, but Hopper catches him with a boot to the midsection, and sends him head first through the ropes to the floor.
Hopper pursues him, and comes off the apron with a double axhandle which levels Prophet. Hopper picks his opponent up, but puts him right back down to the concrete with a bodyslam.
DS: Prophet has to get back into the ring if he wants to have any chance at winning this match.
TG: Hopper with an irish whip into the ropes, and takes Prophet down with an elbowsmash to the face! Hopper makes the tag to his partner, the Carleton County Kid!
DS: This is a little more of a fair fight, as both of these men could wrestle in the Cruiserweight Division, although Prophet chooses not to.
SK: The CC Kid and Prophet lock up! Prophet goes for an irish whip into the ropes, but The Carleton County Kid reverses it, and sends
Prophet into the opposite ropes. He ducks under as Prophet comes back, and then takes him down with a drop toe hold. The Carleton County Kid immediately applies a camel clutch, keeping his opponent on the mat.
DS: This submission maneuver isn't much of an attempt at the win as it is an attempt to wear Prophet down so his team can make an attempt at the win.
TG: Here's a bit of inexperience by the Carleton County Kid as he releases the hold, allowing Prophet to make his way gingerly to his feet. The Carleton County Kid takes a wild swing at Prophet and misses, and Prophet takes him down with an atomic drop!
DS: Prophet's got to try and make the tag now. SK: Prophet is inching toward the corner as The Carleton County Kid makes the tag. Chris Hopper rushes in, and pulls Prophet away from the corner just before he is in reaching distance of his partner. Hopper pulls Prophet up, grabs him by the throat, and slams him into the canvas with a chokeslam! Hopper with the cover, and he hooks the leg.
MO: One! Two! TG: Oracle breaks the cover, and the Carleton County Kid tries to rush into the ring only to be stopped and admonished by referee Mike Ocoboc.
This allows Oracle and Prophet to double team the monster Hopper, and chop him into the ropes. The Dark Forces double irish whip Hopper into the far ropes, and take him down with a double back body drop! Prophet leaves the ring, leaving Oracle and hopper in the ring.
DS: He didn't make a tag! Prophet is still the legal man! SK: That's true, but the referee had his back turned, and he didn't notice. Oracle off the ropes, and he collides with Hopper with a dropkick, sending the big man tumbling through the ropes to the floor!
DS: The action goes to the outside again! TG: Oracle runs towards the ropes, springing off the top rope and lands on Hopper with a corkscrew plancha! The Carleton County Kid runs into the ring, dropkicks Prophet off the apron, and then goes over the top rope, nailing Oracle with a cross body block!!!
DS: This action is getting out of hand. I'm getting motion sickness from seeing all of these guys fly through the air!
SK: The Carleton County Kid pulls Oracle up and rolls him into the ring, and then rolls his own partner into the ring near the Too Cool
Connection's corner. He then jumps up on the apron and waits for the tag!
TG: "The Iceman" makes the tag, and The Carleton County Kid runs into the ring, stomping Oracle to the mat. He then runs up and punches
Prophet in the face, which leads Prophet to try and enter the ring, leaving the referee's back turned. The Carleton County Kid whips Oracle into the ropes, but Oracle collides with the referee, squashing him between the two Dark Forces and knocking him out cold.
DS: Did you see what The Dark Forces did to the referee?! They should be disqualified for that!
SK: That was totally unintentional, I'm sure. The Carleton County Kid climbs to the top rope, and jumps off with a missile dropkick, but
Oracle catches him and slams him down to the mat with a short powerbomb! He makes the cover, but there is no referee to count! Chris
Hopper nails Oracle with a boot to the head, sending him rolling out of the ring. Prophet runs up from behind and goes for a clothesline on
Chris Hopper, but Hopper spins around ducking, and catches Prophet by the throat!
TG: Here it comes! He's got him set up, and he puts him down in the middle of the ring with the Icepick! Hopper makes the cover, and the referee begins to stir!
MO: One! Two! Thr... SK: He kicked out! Prophet kicked out of the Icepick! Hopper pulls him up, whipping him into the ropes, and takes him down with a clothesline! He goes toward the turnbuckle, and very slowly makes his way to the top buckle.
TG: Oracle is on his feet on the outside, and he jumps up onto the apron. Just as Hopper is about to drop all of his weight onto Prophet,
Oracle shoves him off the turnbuckle, and he lands short of Prophet and knocks himself out cold! Prophet somehow rolls over onto Hopper, and the referee counts!
MO: One! Two! Three!!! LM: Ladies and gentlemen, the winners, and new WWWA World Tag Team
Champions, The Dark Forces!!!
DS: I can't believe they pulled it off! That was quite a match from beginning to end! TG: Can you believe Prophet was able to kick out of the Icepick?! Not only that, but he was able to make the cover at the end and win the
World Tag Team Titles for his team and for the RFD!
SK: Now the RFD is in possession of the World Heavyweight Title, World
Tag Team Titles and Cruiserweight Title! All they need now is someone who can capture the Hardcore Title from Cryptic Saber!
DS: Cryptic Saber or Alec "The Destroyer" Empire, whoever survives the boiler room brawl coming up next! Stay tuned!
-- World Wide Wrestling Alliance President WWWA- http://home.earthlink.net/~eck007/index.html SK: Fans, welcome back! Next up we're going to be going to the depths of Madison Square Garden, the framework of this great arena as two men will battle it out in the dreaded boiler room brawl!
TG: The rules of this match are simple. Two men will be released into the boiler room through separate doors. The winner is the first man to make it out of the boiler room through a third door. But this is no easy task as both men will have a variety of weapons at hand, and shouldn't be afraid to use them if they want to be the WWWA Hardcore
Champion!
LM: Ladies and gentlemen, I would now like to direct your attention to the video wall as both men are being released into the boiler room!
DS: It looks like these two guys are on opposite sides of the boiler room. Cryptic Saber grabs a pipe from a dark corner and stalks around looking for his opponent. Meanwhile, Alec "The Destroyer" Empire grabs a metal folding chair and carries it through the boiler room with him.
SK: Here they come! They've spotted each other, and the two men are running towards each other! Cryptic Saber takes a swing with the pipe, but Alec Empire slaps it away with the chair and smashes the chair over the head of Cryptic Saber. He pulls the Hardcore Champion to his feet, and whips him into a sizzling pipe!
TG: Ooh, that'll blister. Alec Empire pulls Cryptic Saber up, but as he goes for a tombstone piledriver, Saber reverses it, and piledrives
Empire right onto his metal chair! Saber walks away, grabs a metal garbage can, and rushes toward The Destroyer.
DS: Saber with an attempted splash onto Alec Empire with the trash can, but Empire rolls out of the way and the can crushes under the weight of
Cryptic Saber! Empire gets slowly to his feet, and runs through the boiler room looking for the exit. He turns a corner, but is met with a shovel to the face by Cryptic Saber! Saber drops an elbow on him, and then turns around to grab a screwdriver.
TG: Oh my god he's going to put a stake through his heart!! SK: Saber jabs downward with the screwdriver, but Empire kicks it out of Saber's hands and nails him with a lowblow! Empire grabs another metal garbage can, and throws it over the head of the Hardcore
Champion. The Destroyer dropkicks his opponent, sending him sprawling into a mass of pipes, can and all. The pipes break open, sending scalding hot water all over Cryptic Saber!
DS: I have a feeling this match isn't going to have a pretty ending. Luckily that garbage can prevented the boiling water from hitting anything but his legs. Empire with an elbowdrop onto the garbage can, crushing it around the Hardcore Champion. Empire pulls up the mass of metal and man, and throws him into a pile of scrap wood!
TG: Empire is looking for the exit! He's wandering around aimlessly, but wait, I think he found it! He's rushing towards a door, he's almost there, and wham!!! Cryptic Saber with a metal garbage can shot off that beam up in the ceiling!
DS: How did he get up there, and how the hell did he get that garbage can off him without the use of his legs?
SK: Saber pulls his opponent's unmoving body up, sets him up over the garbage can, and delivers the Saber Slam on his lifeless body! Saber approaches the door, throws it open and falls out into the hallway!
LM: Ladies and gentlemen, your winner of the boiler room brawl, and still WWWA Hardcore Champion, Cryptic Saber!!!
TG: He's battered, he's bloodied, he was almost boiled, but he made it out alive, and he's still the WWWA Hardcore Champion! He's Cryptic
Saber!!!
DS: What a match! That one will go down in history as one of the most brutal boiler room brawls ever to take place on live television!
TG: Fans, don't go away, because after this short commercial break we'll finally find out who the masked man really is! Stay tuned!
-- World Wide Wrestling Alliance President WWWA- http://home.earthlink.net/~eck007/index.html SK: Welcome back! During the break there was some commotion in the back, so let's see what happened!
[Cut to a shot from during the break, out in the parking garage. A black stretch limo rolls up to the doors. The driver steps out and opens the back door, allowing the masked man to get out. Suddenly,
Jarry "The Rocket" Rice runs up from the side and tackles the masked man into the door of the limo, denting it. Security quickly rushes in and separates the two men.]
TG: This just goes to show you how bad these two guys want to get at each other's throats. Rice couldn't even wait a couple minutes for the match before going after that guy in the mask.
DS: Well it looks like he's going to get his chance now, because Lance
Mayson is in the ring with a microphone! Take it away Lance!
LM: Thank you Dan. Ladies and gentlemen, it is time for our main event of the evening! It will be for one fall, with a 30 minute, time limit.
The WWWA Cruiserweight Title will be on the line against the masked man's mask!!! Introducing first, hailing from parts unknown, "the epitome of hardcore, the master of disaster, the most ELITE wrestler ever to step into the squared circle", the Masked Man!!!
[The masked man appears from the entranceway without any music. He saunters cockily to the ring, and rolls under the bottom rope. He reaches for his mask as if to take it off now, and as the fans gasp he only turns it slightly.]
LM: And his opponent, representing the RFD, the WWWA Cruiserweight
Champion, Jarry "The Rocket" Rice!!!
[Rice appears at the entranceway, and at his side is the WWWA World
Heavyweight Champion Razor. They both make their way to the ring, and both climb into it. Rice and Razor advance toward the masked man, but referee Mike Ocoboc sends Razor out of the ring before signalling for the bell.]
TG: The bell has been rung, and this match has begun! Rice darts in quickly, and the men lock up. The masked man comes out on top, and irish whips his opponent into the ropes. Rice goes over the top with a sunset flip, but he can't get him over and the masked man pulls Rice to his feet by his hair.
DS: And you thought male pattern baldness was bad. SK: The masked man with a kneelift, and takes Rice down with an axe kick! DS: A nice little display of martial arts from the masked man, showing that he has a wide array of moves, from devastating high flying moves to lethal martial arts.
TG: The masked man goes quickly to the top, waits for Jarry Rice to get to his feet, and then takes the Cruiserweight Champion down to the mat with a huricanrana off the top rope! The masked man makes the cover!
MO: One! Two! DS: He almost had him! If only he had hooked the leg... SK: The masked man pulls Rice to his feet, and irish whips him into the ropes. The masked man ducks down as Rice comes back, but Rice nails him with a kick to the head, goes off the ropes, and nails the masked man with a clothesline!!! The masked man rolls out of the ring, and the referee begins to talk to Jarry Rice.
TG: What's the masked man doing?! He rolled under the ring, and here comes another masked man from the other side! He's got a chair in hand, and levels Razor!!! The masked man rolls back under the ring, and the original masked man rolls out from the other side with something in his hand. Razor jumps up onto the apron when he sees the masked man. As the referee admonishes Razor, the masked man throws a handful of powder into the eyes of Jarry "The Rocket" Rice, temporarily blinding him.
DS: That was a nice move by the masked ma... err Masked Man 1. SK: The masked man to the top rope, jumps off, Shooting Star DDT!!! Razor jumps up on the apron, but the other masked man rolls out from under the ring and grabs Razor's ankle, driving him face first to the mat! He pulls Razor out of the ring as the original masked man makes the cover.
MO: One! Two! Three!!! LM: Ladies and gentlemen, your winner, and NEW WWWA CRUISERWEIGHT CHAMPION!!! [The masked man pulls his mask off to reveal... Mike Holmes!!!] TG: I knew it!!! I knew it all along! LM: The new WWWA Cruiserweight Champion, Mike Holmes!!! DS: Well the fans are loving it here in Madison Square Garden as Mike
Holmes makes his return to wrestling! But who's his partner in crime?!
[The other man pulls his mask off to reveal... "Too Extreme" Walker Quinn!!!] TG: This is just too much! "Too Extreme" Walker Quinn makes his return to wrestling in the same night, and on the same team as Mike Holmes!!!
DS: Fans, I don't know what to say, except that we're out of time!!! Don't expect these two to remain quiet for long!!! SK: Good night everybody!!! [The scene closes as a still shot of Mike Holmes and "Too Extreme" Walker Quinn standing together in the middle of the ring is shown. The words "Copyright, 1999, World Wide Wrestling Alliance (WWWA)" flash across the screen, and then it fades to black.]
