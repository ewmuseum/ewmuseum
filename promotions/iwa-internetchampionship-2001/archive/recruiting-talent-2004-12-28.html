IWA (Internet Championship Wrestling) - Recruiting Talent!

28 December 2004

Hey folks, I'm just scouring the net in hopes of finding new talent for our e-fed. Since closing our doors about six months ago after a great four years and ending it all with a fantastic show (we decided to move on and try something new), some of us have banded together to reopen the e-wrestling doors and start over. We have affiliated ourselves with most of our old staff and some of our old crew but have also been joined by some new 'excellent' talent - namely our artist/designer (he's just been hired on as an official NWA TNA artist.) Anyway, come and check us out and if you're interested, sign up! Make sure you read all the Rules and FAQ's though because this isn't your average e-fed. Roleplaying is only a part of it. It's very different than what most of you are used to and the competition can get quite intense. * We've only got 3 spots left in the Tournament of Champions. We are accepting 20 members in total but only the first twelve are accepted into the tournament.. IWA Staff Check us out at [detail withheld]
