NWN Weekly Report, Issue #1

25 October 1996

NWN WEEKLY REPORT Issue #1 Oct 24rd 1996. Welcome fans, after a lengthly layoff, the NWN is back! There are alot of changes going on around here that's why the NWN schedule has been messed up and hasn't been in production for a while. Lets get to the news! NWN'S 1st PPV The NWN will be holding it's first PPV on December 1st! It'll be called, RINGSIDE RAMPAGE! It'll be live from Skydome in Toronto, Ontario. The World, TV and Tag champions will be decided on this night, you'll find out how in next weeks newsletter. POWERSURGE HAS BEEN MOVED! Saturday PowerSurge has been moved to Wednesday nights. Hopefully this will allow me some time to do some of the card over the weekend. More on PowerSurge later. ROLEPLAYING IN THE NWN In case there has been any confusion on how to roleplay, I will clear that up for you right now. Roleplaying doesn't only mean trash talk, it also means sending in strategies for your matches like what you want to do before, during or after a match. If you don't send in anything, you'll be jobbed. This is a roleplaying fed, you can't just sit back and expect to win because it won't happen, you must be active to have a chance of winning. HAPPENINGS AROUND THE NWN... HAVOC BREAKS LOOSE! Last Saturday there was mayham in the ring at the end of the PowerSurge broadcast. The New Rockers and Ready For This were going at it and Carnage and Eyeball were going at it. At the end of Carnage's match, the two masked men with him revealed themselves to be Marty Jannety and Tony Ruiz which was a big shock to all their fans. Ready For This who were scheduled to face the Rockers next, decided to come out a little early and attack the Rockers. Their stablement, Eyeball came from the dressing room as well and decided to join in on the action and there was a big brawl which brought out tons of officials to break it up. All men were fined heavily. SANDMAN JR. MAKES HIS NWN DEBUT! Sandman Jr. of the VWA(NWN is VWA's regional fed) made his debut against Big Buster Johnson. Sandman lost to Johnson in his NWN debut. Johnson had a big advantage in that match because Sandman just hasn't been himself at late. He has been stressed out over his ever lasting feud with the VWA Horseman plus he's been nagged by a old knee injury. Maybe he should take some time off from one of these organizations but knowing the competitor he is, he'll take on the world if he has to. UPCOMING CARDS... Wednesday November 6th 1996: READY FOR THIS VS NEW ROCKERS: This match has been resigned for obvious reason's due to what happened the last time they were scheduled to fight. This time President Baldwin has firmly stated there will be a match or there will be heavy consequences to pay. CARNAGE VS EYEBALL: After the brawl on PowerSurge, Baldwin has decided to let these two go at it. Added stipulation - they will be put in a 20ft high steel cage where they can beat the crap out of each other. THE MALONEY BROTHERS VS MAXIMUM FORCE VS THE SUPERPOWERS: This was original supposed to be The Maloney's and Max Force but Baldwin decided to throw in The SuperPowers because they were the only tag team that wouldn't of been in action on the card. This wil be a tag elimination style match. BRETT YOUNG VS "THE SPARTAN" TROY WALTERS: The Spartan will finally make his NWN debut against a dangerous individual in the form of Brett Young. These two know each other from the WLW and know each others style. This should be an interesting match. TOM LANDIS VS SANDMAN JR: Fan favorite Tom Landis will be taking on the seasoned veteran Sandman Jr. If Sandman can keep his mind off the VWA Horsemen and stay focused on Landis, this should be a very exciting and classic match to watch. --------------------------- RING RAMPAGE - DECEMBER 1ST --------------------------- Lineup...
