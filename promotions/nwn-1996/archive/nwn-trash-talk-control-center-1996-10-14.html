NWN Trash Talk Control Center

[We fade into the NWN Trash Talk Control Center with Ken Daniels and sitting Jeff Costas are sitting behind a desk. The NWN logo is on a backdrop behind them] [Daniels] Welcome everyone to our first ever Trash Talk control center! I will be your host for NWN Trash Talk. There has been a number of changes here recently in the NWN. We have a new president and Steve Sampson and Al Grant have been fired. Reasons for these changes are unknown at this time. [Costas] Well, what a first week we've had here in the NWN! I look forward to many more exciting action! Here is the schedule for this weeks PowerSurge: [The schedule appears on the TV wall beside them] Tom Landis vs Brett Young Ballkicker vs. Eyeball Big Buster Johnson vs. Sandman Jr. Carnage vs. Junkyard Dawg Ready For This vs. The New Rockers Z-Man vs Super Dragon [Daniels] We have some great action coming up for you this week. The NWN is working on having a second card a week, possibly a Wednesday card. It'll have one or two superstar matches and the rest will be superstar vs jobber matches. Two great cards a week! [Costas] That will for sure be a good move by the NWN, the more exposure of the league, the better. [Daniels] Mike Baldwin, our new president, has been in the wrestling business a longtime. He really knows how to run things and is a very tough man. [Costas] Well since this is a trash talk show, lets get to the trash! [Camera turns on to two figures in the NWN studio.They are The New Rockers Tony Ruiz and Marty Jannetty.Tony is looking at program magazine for Saturdays card] [Tony Ruiz] So, are you ready world. Are you ready to be amazed. I thought you would. I just wanna say a few things. First,I told you I would beat Bret Young and I did. I dont wanna be a b****,but I told you so. On Powersurge, we take on Ready For This? Sounds like your too regular to be in this league. You see you gotta be special in order to be here. Thats why I'm here. And is everyone else. From the insane Carnage to Eyeball. And even though I hat to say it even Bret Young is special. So why dont you just forfit and it will make your life a whole lot easier. Marty,would you care to take it from here? I wanna get something from the back. [Tony goes to the back of the studio to get something] [Jannety] Sure. While my partner goes to the back to get a surprise I'm gonna say a little something. We,the Rockers, will kick your butt on Saturday. Now we may not be the biggest or even the baddest wrestlers here but, we are the best damn tag team you ever saw. Now before any matches go on the air, we're gonna hold a autograph table inside the building. There will be us the Rocker, and a few others. [Tony returns with cardboard cut-outs] [Tony Ruiz] You see this. These two are Ready For This. When we're finished with them they're gonna be like this. [Tony and Marty do two karate kicks to the cut-outs. Both cut-outs are now in two pieces each] [Marty Jannety] Be prepared for the beating of your life!!!!!!! [They leave as the camera fades out] ================================================ [Daniels] The Rockers look pretty convincing. [Costas] Yes they do indeed. I wonder what Ready For This is thinking right now. Next up we have The Spartan! ================================================ "The Spartan" Troy Walters- Hey... this is the newcomer to the NWN.. but no newcomer to e-wrestling! I am a 33 time champion... soon to be 34! I challenge any "top contender" to a match.. and I'll prove my status! ======================================== [Daniels] I've seen this guy in other feds, he is incredibly to watch in the ring. He is a 33 time champion, that is impressive! [Costas] Yes indeed it is. I can see gold down the line for The Spartan sometime soon. [Daniels] Next up we have Buster Johnson. [The scene plays in a dark backyard in Harlem, NY. The whole backyard is wet, because it is raining. The camera pans over the whole backyard; and suddenly the 0camera gets closer to a black powerful man, who showes a very evil face expression. He is folding his powerful arms. You can see, how muscular he is. Suddenly, a well dressed man enters the scene. He is smaller than Big Buster Johnson, is smoking a cigar and carries a briefcase] Mr. J. Executioner: "Welcome to Harlem, NY. My name is Mr. J. Executioner, and I'm one of the best managers in the whole wrestling world. And this powerful, strong man is none other than BIG BUSTER JOHNSON! Some might know him from the IWWL or UWC. And they certainly remember the terror, he caused. Now, it's the NWN's turn to get to know this terror. But now, lets hear from BIG BUSTER JOHNSON himself !!!" Big Buster Johnson: "Hey, you little worms out there!!! I'm Big Buster Johnson, one of the powerfulliest and strongest man in the wrestling circuit. First, I ruled the most parts of my hometown Harlem. Then I searched for a new challenge. I started in the IWWL. What was the result? The answer is quiet easy. I was the never beaten IWWL World Heavyweight Champion. The I conquered the UWC by storm. As captain of the notorious Executioner's Brood, we terrorized the UWC. Nobody couldn't stop us! Only the heads of the UWC tried to keep us down. But it didn't work. Now, I'm in the NWN. What will happen in the NWN? Well, what a difficult question. Ha,ha!! I'm gonna terrorizing the NWN and I'm gonna capturing any gold in the NWN. And the first step to it will be Sandman jr. Sandman, mark my words! It's never a good idea to meet me first. You're going down like anybody else before. You're gonna feel the power and strength of Big Buster Johnson! When I'm finished with you, all your stinking bones are crashed. Prepare for your devastating destruction! Brother, it's time to cut your neck!!!! Ha, ha, ha ,ha, ha, ha!!!!!!!" This laugh sounds even more terryfying because of the echo, you can hear in the backyard. (fade out) =============================================== [Daniels] Freaky but very convincing. This guy looks like a force to reckon with. The NWN better be prepared because this guy looks like he can reign terror just like he says. [Costas] Johnson is huge! Right now, I can't think of anyone off the top of my head who can beat this guy. Eyeball would give him a good fight I bet. [Daniels] I bet he can. Two of the biggest men in the NWN going at, that'd be a treat to watch. Up next we have.......Sunny??? [Costas] I never knew Sunny was in the NWN. Lets take a look. ========================================================================== [The camera turns on to the NEW Carnage and his NEW manager Sunny. They are in the NWN Studio. Two men with black outfits and black maskes are behind them] [Sunny] Hello NWN, I am Sunny, Carnages new manager. R.N.and that old Carnage just weren't what the NWN wanted so we came here and we just threw them out onto the street. R.N. is probably at some strange mans house and Carnage is now at the bottom of some lake or something. This is the new Carnage and I'm his manager. Take it away Carnage. [Carnage] Thank you Sunny. Now since the old Carnage is dead I have to take over and face JYD. Now the JunkYard Dog I heard about was this great wrestler in the WWF and all that. But now JYD your in the NWN, where there are no rules. I could splash you through a table or hit a referee if he gets in my way. I'll do everything in my power to make sure that you will not win.Now the rest of Sunnys wrestlers are behind us. They'll be escorting me ,along with Sunny, to ringside and they'll stay there. But I dont want to let the cat out of the bag about who they are. We'll get back to that later. But back to JYD. Now I'm gonna tell you my gameplan right now so you can be warned. I'm gonna come at you like a ton of bricks. I'm gonna hit you with everything including the kitchen sink! But I still dont wanna make enemies, yet. So JYD, when we get in that ring I wanna shake your hand before and after the match. So what do you say? [Sunny] Just remember this JYD: I'll be at ringside so dont try anything funny or I'll have my two "friends" come out and make sure everythings alright. When Saturday night Power Surge comes, I'm going to reveal my two mystery men and the whole world will be shocked! [The camera fades to black as Sunny is smiling] ==================================== [Daniels] A New Carnage? Two Masked Men? Sunny? What is going on? [Costas] This is very strange. These two have peaked my interest. We'll just have wait and see what's this all about on Saturday Night PowerSurge. [Daniels] There's something to look forward too. Up next, we have the Z-Man! ======================================================================= [We fade in on the NWN Soundstage. The Z-Man, Brett Powers, and Julie Jones are all standing in front of the camera. The Z-Man is in his wrestling attire. Brett Powers is in a business suit, and Julie Jones is wearing a "Z-Man" t-shirt, blue jeans, and black high heels] [Z-Man] My debut here in the NWN wasn't exactly what I had in mind, but I think I gave the fans a good indication that the Z-Man is here to stay. Franchise, the next time we meet in the ring, you're going down, PUNK! Now then, on the match at hand..the Sable Dragon. Mr. Karate Man, huh? Well, Mr. Sable Dragon...when you step into the ring with me..you're little fire will be EXTINGUISHED! You can think of me as the DRAGON SLAYER! And I'm not gonna need a sword and a shield to take you down, Dragon. All I need is my two good fists! You think you can take me out! I don't think so! You're going down at PowerSurge! [Brett] That's right, Sable Dragon. I know for a fact that this man (points to the Z-Man) can beat you..1..2..3..in the middle of the ring. And if you're manager tries to interfere..I'm got a little surprise for him. [Z-Man] I'm got two things on my mind, Dragon...Z-Plex...Z-Thang.....Z-Plex... Z-Thang. Once I hit anyone with either of these two moves...it's all over! And Dragon...one way or another..at PowerSurge...I'm hitting you with one of those moves. Dragon...get ready cause you're GOING TO SLEEP, PAL! COURTESY OF THE Z-MAN! [The Z-Man and Brett Powers give intense looks at the camera, while Julie Jones smiles. We fade out] ========================================================= [Daniels] Z-Man looks determined to make up for that lost last week agains Franchise as he takes on Super Dragon this week. [Costas] That should be a tremendous match. Up next, we have "Ready For This." [Daniels] Yea, I'm ready. [Costas] No, that's their name, really. It's "Ready-For-This." [Daniels] What? Ok, anyways, lets take a look. =============================================================== [Scene starts with leroux and crosscheck walking down a back alley somewhere in pittsburgh] [Crosscheck] Hey Francois,whatcha think about those rockers women we gotta wrestle? [Leroux] (laugh) who???? (laughing harder) [Crosscheck] Heh heh...i know what you mean! They can't possibly match our extreme talent. [Leroux] Hey maybe we'll even break a sweat.......(laughing Hysterically)MAYBE!!! [Crosscheck] I'll tell you what rockers!! Pray we have an ounce of mercy in our system when we wrestle you because we are in this league for 2 reasons and 2 reasons only,tell em leroux! [Leroux] We're are here to bring the tag titles to the Regulators and our friend Eyeball the Big Man is here to bring the regulators the world title! So all of you in the league may call us three the regulators and don't worry.....heh heh...we'll be formally introducing ourselves to each and every one of you all in good time. The second reason us 3 are here is to Hurt people and hurt them permanently! [Crosscheck] Rockers listen up!! Don't dare come to the ring thinking you have a chance because we will dominate you! So until your date with the stretcher comes...(laughing)Enjoy your health! [Leroux] Rockers.....bring it on!!!Yeah Bring it on!!! [Scene ends with both of them smashing each others head with hockey sticks] ================================================================== [Daniels] These guys remind me of the Goon. Other than that, I hear they are a great tag team and can compete with the best of them. [Costas] If these are even half as good as what I hear, the tag team division could heat up with all this talent. The Maloney Brothers, SuperPowers, Ready For This, The Rockers, Maximum Heat, all great tag teams plus I hear we have more on the way! Things are really starting to get competitive here as of late. [Daniels] Yes they are. Up next, we have a member of the Regulators stable, Eyeball. =================================================================== [Standing on a NWN soundstage, Eyeball looks into the camera] [Eyeball] Well well. My first match in NWN, and it's against Ballkicker. Gotta tell ya, I love the name. You've got guts, I'll give you that. But, it isn't enough. You won your match last week, but it isn't enough. I am going to show every wrestler in the NWN what eye can do. You, Ballkicker, are going to be an example. Don't take it personally though, it's all business. The only way to get a title shot, is to win matches, and that is what eye am gonna do, one body at a time. You are going to be the first Ballkicker. You should consider that an honor. [lights fade out on the stage] ==================================================================== [Daniels] Short but I think everyone got the message from this big man weighing in at 399lbs and 7 feet tall! [Costas] Unbelievable indeed. Up next, the Maloney Brothers. ===================================================================== [Brian Maloney] I thought they had found another tag team to compete with us here in the NWN, but I was mistaken. [Kelly Maloney] If the Super Powers are the best you guys could dig up, I don't see how things are any different from when we were the only team on the roster. We'd say more, but we need to get our waists measured. There's nothing worse than a belt that doesn't fit just so. ======================================================================== [Daniels] They are bad mouthing the NWN competition. [Costas] They want more competition, well, they're gonna get it with the whole slew of new tag teams that have arrived. Lets see what they say then. I don't know who's who or what's what in this league, but I'm gonna find out, and there's gonna be some pain. [Kellen] You want it, you need it, you HAVE TO HAVE IT!
