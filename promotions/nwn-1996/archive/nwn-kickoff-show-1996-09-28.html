NWN Kickoff Show

28 September 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
<The NWN Logo appears on the screen then a voice begins to speak> Voice: It has come. The time to prove you can wrestle with these big boys. The time to see who will wrestle in the NWN Championship Bunker Match. Boys this is the Official NWN Kickoff show!!! <A shot of a NWN Sound Stage, Sam Springer, NWN President, is sitting down> SS: Welcome and hello to the kickoff to the NWN! My name is Sam Springer and I am your president. I am here to tell you just how we are going to decide the Champions of the NWN. NWN World Championship: Top 5 men in the rankings are entered in the Championship Bunker Match. RULES: 30 foot steel cage. 4 wooden boxes with weapons in each. Wrestlers can wrestle with weapons. Only way to win is by climbing over the cage. SS: Now if that is good this will be great. To decide the NWN Television Championship. All of the other people in the ranking will be entered into a massive 3 ladder match!! RULES: 3 Ladders TV Title suspened above ring First one to grab belt and bring it down wins SS: Ok, ok, you want to know the tag team situation right? Well here it is: The Maloney Brothers will win the titles by defult if no other teams are in the NWN one week prior to the PPV. Now if a team appears here is what will happen: A tag team battle royale!! Both men from every team go into the ring Battle Royale Last man in ring takes titles for his team SS: Ok, well my time is done. Let's goto NWN Announcer "Scammin" Steve Sampson. SSS: Welcome ladies and Gents and here is the NWN!!! We are going to give you guys a show tonight that will leave you screaming for more. Let me introduce my second half of the announcing crew, Al Grant. AG: Thank you "Scammin" Steve. Let me give you guys a little update on the whole thing we got going on. Our only card is called Saturday Night Powersurge. The first powersurge happens October 5. THe PPV is called, check this out, Nightmare in Cyberspace. It will happen Halloween Night. Yes Thursday HALLOWEEN!!! SSS: Ah ha! Well let me introduce a few wrestlers already making a name for themselves. First up lets introduce a man named "Z-Man" Brian Zordich. AG: Now this is 6'2.. 245 pounds. He isn't any shrimp. SSS: But he aint no monster either. AG: Now he and Franchise this guy is one bad ass. He is ub 4 other feds. THey will remain nameless due to legal agreements. Holds many titles, tag and singles, but he wants NWN gold. SSS: Now I think I am gonna make a prediction. I think this Franchise guy will take it. He looks like he is hot as hell. AG: No no. I am going to go with the Z-Man. Now lets goto out next match for the NWN's debut. "Rocker" Tony Ruiz. THis guy is a third generation wrestler. He's held a few titles. A few tag titles with his partner, Marty Jannetty. SSS: He is gonna wrestle with the man who I think is a real man, "Fantasy" Brett Young. This young guy is a real butcase. I have seen him in action before. This guy will do everything to win. AG: Well he is gonna need it because Tony Ruiz isn't and picnic. After that we will see Junkyard Dawg go up against Ballbreaker. I haven't seen either of these two before. But this Ballbreaker guy really likes to hurt his opponents. This Dawg is a real brawler. He looks tough!!!! SSS: Now we goto our last signed match, Tom Landis vs. Mr. Sandman. Mr. Sandman is 7'1 and over 400 lbs! Our biggest man yet!! But this Landis guy is from my hometown of Chicago Illinois, I gotta go for Landis. AG: Not me this monster of a man, Mr. Sandman is gonna be hard to tangle with!! SSS: Wait this just it, my camera man, Bert, just gave me a note. It tells us that there are two more tag teams in the NWN. Contracts just signed!! This means that the Maloney Brothers will not win by defult!!!!! AG: Ohh boy!! This is just heating up. See us Saturday Night, October 5 for POWERSURGE!! This is Al Grant and "Scammin" Steve Sampson. See you there!!
