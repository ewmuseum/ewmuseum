NWN PowerSurge

12 October 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
************************************************************************** [We fade into a jam packed NWN arena in Toronto Ontario. Over 25,000 fans are screaming their heads off. We pan into Eric Erickson and Tom Vickers at the announcing table] [Vickers] Welcom ladies and gentleman to Saturday Night PowerSurge! I am your host Tom Vickers along with Eric Ego Erickson. Tonight we have 6 great matches! We have Tom Landis up against Brett Young which should be exciting! [Erickson] Young will take that punk Landis. [Vickers] Ballkicker vs Eyeball, Big Buster Johnson vs Sandman Jr. [Erickson] A couple of sub par matches. We have Z-Man up against Super Dragon. Plus Carnage will be in action accompanied by Sunny. I can't wait to see *her* in action. Haha. [Vickers] Ahh nympho, control yourself in the back. We also have a great tag team match to top it all off as the Rockers will take on Ready For This! [Erickson] What a dumb name. Original but dumb. [Mic] Introducing first, weighing in at 200lbs, from Tokyo, Japan, SUPER DRAGON!!! [He comes to the ring to Yokozuna's music. Wears white pants with a big red dot on the side. His manager Hyachu Lee accompanies him He has a cane] [Mic] And his opponent, weighing in at 245lbs, from Detroit, Michigan, "Z-MAN" BRIAN ZORDICH!!!["The Immortals" by Techno Syndicate starts up and Z-Man walks to the ring accompanied Brett Powers and Julie Jones. The crowd cheers him on.] As Z-Man was making his way to the ring, Super Dragon, who was in the ring, did a springboard splash onto Z-Man on the outside. SD picked up Z and dragged him into the ring. SD picked up Z and slammed him down. SD dropped a couple elbows. SD then went to the top ropes and went for a somersault splash but Z rolled out of the way. Z got up and delivered a kick to the stomach of SD. Z whipped SD against the ropes and nailed him with a dropkick that almost knocked SD's teeth out. The crowd really started to get behind Z. Z started posing for the crowd then SD came from behind and rolled him up for a 2 count. Z went to the outside to recouperate with his manager. SD leaped onto Z and Powers off the top ropes. SD got back in the ring. The crowd was in shock. Z got back in the ring at the 8 count. SD whipped Z against the rope and went for a clothesline but Z ducked and nailed SD with one of his one. Z picked up SD and slammed him to the mat. Z set SD on the top turnbuckle. Z nailed him with a frankensteiner. Z picks up SD and nails him with a vicious DDT. Lets join the match in progress... [Vickers] Z-Man is signaling for his finisher the Z-Plex! He has Super Dragon locked in. [Erickson] Come on Super Dragon, get out his grasp before it's too late! [Vickers] Z-Man with the Z-Plez! 1.....2.....3! Z-Man is the winner! [Mic] And the winner of this match, Z-Man! [Vickers] Wait a sec, what's this? The SuperPowers have come out and are going to work on Z-Man. SuperPowers nail him with a double DDT! Offficials have made their way out to stop this. [Erickson] Z-Man might of won the match, but has paid for it at the hands of The Super Powers. [Vickers] SuperPowers and SuperDragon are forced to leave. Brett Powers and Julie Jones help their man Z-Man back to the dressing room. What an exciting first match we have had tonight! Alot of highflying action by SuperDragon that has really impressed me. Even though he lost, he has potential here in the NWN. [Erickson] It was all the stupid fans fault! They wouldn't shutup and let Dragon concentrate! [Vickers] Up next we have...wait, I have just received word there is something going on in the back, lets get some cameras back there! [We cut to the locker rooms where we see Tom Landis and Brett Young going at it] [Vickers] These two were scheduled up next. I guess they didn't want to waste anytime and decided to get in on back here. Young just nailed Landis with a chair! [Erickson] This is great! Two men beating the hell out of each other, what more can you ask for. [Vickers] Young is dragging Landis out of the dressing room down the aisle to the ring. He whips Landis into the guardrail and Landis is hurt! Young now, throws Landis into the ring. Young off the ropes, missing with a clothesline, off the other side, Landis ducks gives Young a backdrop. [Erickson] Young just went about 10 feet in the air! [Vickers] Lanids picks up Young, swinging neckbreaker by Landis! Landis now delivering a series of punches to the head of Young. The ref steps in and threatens to dq him. Landis looks pist. [Erickson] Those were closed fists, how cheap of Landis. [Vickers] Landis picks up Young and delivers a reverse Thrust kick to the throat! Young is gasping for air! [Erickson] Landis thinks he's Jackie Chan all of the sudden? [Vickers] Landis, not letting up, whips Young to the buckell. He comes charging in but Young nails him with a kick to the midsection! Young just reached into his pockets and nailed Landis with some kind of object! He covers 1....2....Landis kicks out! [Erickson] That was a pretty good move there by Young, I like his style. [Vickers] How could the ref have not seen that??? Young now climbs the top ropes. He drops an elbow and misses! Landis gets up and puts Young into a Boston Crab! Young has given up!!! [Mic] And the winner of the match, TOM LANDIS!!! [Vickers] Smart move by Landis, taking advantage of a stunned Young. [Erickson] If ya ask me, that was cheap! [Vickers] Well that was a great match indeed. Alot of brawling which is not normally Landis' style but he was victorious and that's all that counts as far as he's concerned. [Mic] This next match is scheduled for one fall. Introducing first, from Boulder Colorado, weighing in at 399lbs, BALLKICKER!!![He comes to the ring attire like Vader. "Big Balls" by AC/DC blares over the speckers.] His opponent, weighing in at 400 pounds, accompanied by Crosscheck and Francoix Leroux, the team of Ready For This, here is EYEBALL!!![He wears an outfit like Diesel with a white mask on his head and an eyeball on the front of the mask. He is 7'6" so he towers over everyone.] [Vickers] This man means business. Look at him, he is huge! Eyeball wastes no time and goes after Ballkicker! He whips the 399lbs Ballkicker into the corner and charges in with a vicious clothesline! Eyeball manages to pick the heavy Ballkicker up and slam him to the mat. Eyeball stands on Ballkicker's chest! [Erickson] 7'6", 400lbs right ontop of Ballkicker! That's gotta hurt. [Vickers] The ref warns Eyeball to back off. The ref checks Ballkicker to make sure he's ok. The two lockup. Ballkicker rakes Eyeball in the eyes. Ballkicker off the ropes, nails Eyeball with a belly butt! Ballkicker then just sits on Eyeball! [Erickson] These two are really throwing their weight around! [Vickers] Eyeball is hurt. Ballkicker is signaling for his finisher The Ballkick! The crowd is going crazy! [Erickson] Ah shudup! [Vickers] What's this? Leroux is on the apron and distracting the ref, Crosscheck comes from behind Ballkicker and nails him with his hockey stick! It doesn't affect Ballkicker! Ballkicker, grabs the hockey stick and Crosscheck gets off the apron. The ref turns around and sees Ballkicker with the stick and dq's him! [Mic] And the winner of this match, as a result of disqualification, EYEBALL!!! [Vickers] Ballkicker is upset and decides to go after the 3. The 3 see him coming a jet. Ballkicker is chasing them back to the locker rooms. What an ending to a great match. [Erickson] Ballkicker is so dumb. This is wrestling, not hockey moron! No wonder you got dq'd, you can't even tell the difference, haha! [Mic] This match is scheduled for one fall. Introducing first, from Harlem, N.Y., weighing in at 320lbs, accompanied by his manager Mr. J. Executioner, here is BIG BUSTER JOHNSON!!![He wears red trunks with a black fist on the back side. "Gangsta's Paradise" by Coolio is playing.] And his opponent, from Philly, PA, weighing in at 235lbs accompanied by Kellen, SANDMAN JR!!![He is wearing a t-shirt and weighlifting pants and has Mr. Perfect like attire under them. His manager Kellen is a beautiful blonde who carries a cane with her.] [Vickers] Johnson attacks Sandman before the bell! Kellen gets on the apron and tries to take a swing at Johnson but misses. Lucky for Sandman the ref didn't see. Johnson, in the corner, delivers a series of chops to Sandman! [Erickson] Those are more than Rick Flair chops he's giving there. [Vickers] Johnson, arm drags Sandman out of the corner. Johnson goes over to Sandman, Johnson gets nailed in the groin by a punch from Sandman! Sandman delivers a superkick that sends Johnson stumbling but not down. Sandman, off the ropes, flying clothesline and sends Johnson down! Sandman goes for the pin, 1.....2....kickout by Johnson! Johnson rolls out of the ring to take a breather. Sandman sneaks up on Johnson on the outside and gives him a bulldog! Sandman rolls back in the ring. [Erickson] Sandman is really going to work on Johnson here. [Vickers] Johnson gets back in the ring. Sandman tries to pick up the 325lbs Johnson but it backfires and Johnson falls ontop of him...1...2...Sandman kicks out. Johnson is the first one up and he grabs Sandman and nails him with a belly to belly suplex! Johnson, picks him up and nails him with the Neck Cutter! [Erickson] I've never seen a reverse neckbreaker like that in my life! [Vickers] Johnson covers...1...2...3! [Mic] And the winner of this match, BIG BUSTER JOHNSON!!! [Vickers] Johnson is victorious but Sandman gave it his all, with him being the underdog and all that. Johnson was just to big and powerful. [Erickson] This Buster Johnson guy is gonna be something to reckoned with here in the NWN. [Vickers] Well folks, it's time to take a commerical break, we'll be back here in a few minutes with some more exciting action! [Vickers] Welcome back ladies and gentleman for the second half of PowerSurge! Up next we have Carnage up against Junkyard Dawg. Any thoughts Erickson? [Erickson] I don't know either of these too well but I'm going to go with Carnage because he has Sunny as his manager and you all know what she's capable of, hehehe. Maybe she'll flash a little something something your way Vickers, it's probably not too often you get to see the other side of women, haha. [Vickers] Oh you're a riot tonight....Lets get to the ring. [Mic] This match is for one fall! Introducing first, from Victoria Tx, weighing in at 252lbs, accompanied by Sunny, here is CARNAGE!!![Sunny walks down in front with Carnage behind her. Carnage is wearing a red mask with silver eye holes and black pants with red stripes going in different directions. There also two men in black with masks on accompanying Carnage] [Vickers] Who are those two men with Carnage? What's going on here? [Erickson] I'm as clueless as you Vickers. [Vickers] Here comes JYD! Wait! Carnage just leaped over the top ropes onto JYD on the outside! [Erickson] Carnage is wasting no time here at all! Look at Sunny, she is loving it! HAHA HAHA! [Vickers] Carnage just rammed JYD into the guardrail two times face first! JYD is bleeding! Carnage just gave what he calls the TMT, a rude awakening onto the cement floor! Carnage just rolled into the ring. [Erickson] Carnage is just going to work on JYD. He wants to end this early. [Vickers] Carnage brings JYD back into the ring and covers him...1...2....3! [Mic] And the winner of this match, CARNAGE!!! [Vickers] I've never seen anything like this. Officials are now taking away JYD on a stretcher. This is just unbelievable. [Erickson] Sunny and those two men in black just got in the ring. They are congratulating Carnage. What's this?!? The two men are gonna unveil themselves! [Vickers] It's....it's..... [Erickson] Tony Ruiz and Marty Jannety? [Vickers] What??? Why would these fans favorite have an alliance with Carnage??? I can't believe they turned their backs on the fans. The fans are throwing garbage in the ring. [Erickson] I love this. This is great! Who needs the fans anyways, thay don't help you in anyway, they just mess you up. Look at what happened to Super Dragon earlier tonight when the fans wouldn't shutup. He lost. [Vickers] Here comes Ready For This out of the crowd! The are scheduled to face Ruiz and Jannety next. Leroux and Crosscheck of Ready For This are going to work on Ruiz and Jannety! They just double dropkicked Carnage over the top ropes! [Erickson] Here comes Eyeball! The stablemate of Ready For This. He just got in the ring and chokeslamed both Rockers! Carnage has just gotten back in the ring and he's going after Eyeball! The two start brawling! Ready For This is brawling with The New Rockers! [Vickers] We get kaos on the ring! Here come officials to try and break this up. Eyeball and Carnage are now brawling into the crowd and the fans are going crazy! Sunny is freaking! She doens't know what to do. The officials finally separate Ready For This and The New Rockers. [Erickson] The match didn't even happen! I was looking forward to this match. Pres. Baldwin better reschedule this match. [Vickers] Well it has been a great evening here live from VWA arena in Toronto. This is Tom Vickers and Eric Erickson signing off. We'll see you next week everyone! [Fade To Black]
