Welcome to the RWL. This New Federation is bound and determined to be one of the best internet based federations in the world. But to do this, we need your help. I need staff to write cards, help upkeep this site, add their own feel to it.. update and design it to be the best out there. Right now, the RWL site is very simple. But we will improve and redesign it over time. I did it in this quick, easy way to get us established! This Federation will work strongly on the best roleplays, but at times, I will add some action of my own to help push an angle I may see developing. You're efforts as much as mine, will either make or break this federation. Staff writers, bookers and promoters can NOT handle a character. But at times, if they wish to pass the torch and get into the action, then thats wonderful too. The federation will not open until we are fully prepared with the proper staff and characters.
The following staff is needed to complete the backbone of this federation: 4 card writers (2 main, 2 backups) Co-Booker Artists (2 or 3) Web Designers (2 or more) Vice President (to handle crisises when I can not) ALSO: Unlike many feds, who throw together matches to decide title holders.. the first single wrestler to sign up will be the World
Champion, the second, IC, the Third US.. until the 6 titles are taken.. the first Tag Team will be Tag Champs. The second set of players will become #1 contenders and challenge at the first card. 3rd set will be
#2 contenders and challenge the winner of each match. If we can get this to happen ASAP.. that's 18 players already!! and we're well on our way to being HUGE!
