Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

thReat — “fight back” — was a website federation on the theewe.net host. Its weekly show, Sunday Night Flaw, began on 24 March 2002, and its first pay-per-view, ReLoad, on 5 May 2002, crowned Xaxon World Champion, Takisuma Ogama International Champion and Quincy Brown Fighting Champion.

The first run lasted three months. At Flaw 12 its owner, Jon Hackett, booked a Malevolence for 23 June and billed its World Championship match as maybe the last match the fans would ever see from thReat; its title history retired four belts that day, “due to closure of company.”

It came back on 6 April 2003 with Flaw 13 at the ARCO Arena, under a new owner, Troy Mason. The Malevolence that had waited a year was staged that May, setting Troy Mason’s Resistance against Vincent Pembridge’s Vagabonds five on five, and Payback and Double Or Nothing followed in July and August. Six championships ran on one title history across both runs, with General David Sanders its World Champion from Payback on.

Its last staged event was House Show 02 in Salt Lake City on 12 September 2003. In June 2004 the site announced “thReat Returns!”, now based in Dallas, with a new roster and a new backstage, and promised that the first Friday Night Flaw was coming soon; no show of that return survives, and how the story ended isn’t in the surviving record.
