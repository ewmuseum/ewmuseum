thReat Flaw 25 -- Finally Airs!

10 August 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Network officials once again lied to Colin Gear, as the show only aired on Friday night after having been taped nearly a whole week prior. In any case, the show turned out to be a chokeful of surprises and intense matches, as the road to Las Vegas continues to wind down. Rivalries explode, and top personalities get shocked by what others can do to them. With Flaw 26 set to return LIVE next week, what more can be expected? Highlights included; * Colin Gear enjoying his reign as Commissioner. * The Board Of Directors receiving a rude welcome. * Vincent Pembridge continuing his stranglehold on the company before the General decided to fight back. * Lawrence Neumeyer having to use his years in the Army to overcome some obstacles. * The International Title Tournament Quarter-Finals. * Wolf torturing Ernie French in the sickest way possible. * Newcomer Carson Nash's popularity not going down well with several members of the roster. * And an ending that could prove to be a foreshadowing of events to come.
