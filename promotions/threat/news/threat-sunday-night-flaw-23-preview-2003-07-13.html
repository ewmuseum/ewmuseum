[thReat] Sunday Night Flaw 23 Preview

13 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Robert Sole here, with the preview for Flaw 23, the post-Payback show! Our post-Malevolence show proved to be a raging success with endless surprises, so Flaw 23 could possibly overshadow that! General David Sanders, our new World Champion, will address the crowd regarding his unprecedented victory at Payback! Sanders, who not only defeated Xaxon to claim the title but drove the maniac out of the company, is said to be completely overjoyed at winning the World Title... and will thank the fans for the rousing encouragment they have given him throughout! Our opening match will be one between two newcomers to the federation. It seems every week, we've got new blood in the company, but the combatants in this match -- R.A.T and Christian Elijah -- are certain to put on a brilliant display for everyone. R.A.T debuted at Payback and the outlandish way he portrayed himself as gone down well with the fans. Christian Elijah, meanwhile, has had history with notable companies such as WWR & SE... and is now looking for a place to really cement himself as a true player. Watch out for this potential blockbuster of a match! Following that, the INTERNATIONAL TITLE TOURNAMENT will commence, with the first qualifier pitting Phil Atken against haTE. Atken is already bitter as it is, following the loss to Tyler Davidson at Payback, so we can expect fireworks here as well! Another match featuring two newcomers will showcase the talents of Brady Watts & Rune Winters. Both men are believed to be in the best shapes of their lives, and this will certainly make for an interesting contest. Tyler Davidson, our new Cruiserweight Champion, will apparently have an open challenge of sorts going around. Not just to challengers for his title, but to everyone, basically. Three more INTERNATIONAL TITLE TOURNAMENT matches will take place on Flaw 23, with the tournament being aimed at climaxing at the Double Or Nothing PPV. In the second of four matches, Chris McCluskey will face off against Dane Manetta, and one must wonder if Dane can snap his losing streak against the man who wrapped up a controversial victory at Payback. The third match will feature Jere Casarella fighting against Vandal, with rumours of a police investigation hanging over Jere's head, over the supposed murder of one Rio Nexan. The final ITT contest for the show will showcase Rent-A-Hero locking horns with Candor, in what will surely be one hell of a technical feast! With already so matches booked for Flaw 23, you'd expect that be all, right? You're wrong! A heartbroken Levond Daniels, who will NOT be allowed to use his legs, will take on the impressive Jesse Ramey... as well as a Tag Team Titles showdown, with Born To Die defending against The Trenchrunners! And with Vincent Pembridge heavily rumoured to be present at the show, for a little meeting with Troy Mason, how could Flaw 23 not be the most highly-anticipated thReat event in history? All this, and so much more, on Flaw 23! The show kicks off at 8 PM, at the Hara Arena in Dayton, OH. Tickets are on sale at your Local Box Office or call TicketMaster for more information. Remember, the card is subject to change... since how ABSOLUTELY ANYTHING can happen in thReat.
