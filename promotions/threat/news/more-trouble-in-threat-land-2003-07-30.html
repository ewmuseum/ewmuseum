More Trouble in thReat-land.

30 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Vincent Pembridge's attorneys, the friendly people at The Patterson Agency, have somehow managed to prevent Flaw 24 from being aired live on Sunday night, due to the ongoing lawsuit Vincent Pembridge & Xaxon have filed against the company for the accusation of the kidnapping of Troy Mason. The show did go on as planned, and thus, it'll be aired on a delayed basis sometime this week. When exactly? Well, Troy Mason's meeting with network executives todaydidn't go too well, and it seems that The Patterson Agency's political warfare will mean that Flaw 24 will air ONLY THIS FRIDAY. A whole five days after it was taped. And as if Troy Mason wasn't stressed out enough, Commissioner Gear has clarified that the reason he claimed Flaw 25 was in jeopardy of being not being able to air LIVE was because he has a hot date on Sunday. And by the powers invested in him, Flaw 25 will now be scheduled for taping this SATURDAY. When will it air? Network executives don't want to share. Maybe we should find a new network?
