thReat Flaw 20 Preview!

14 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hello, my name is Moe Sole. I am the oldest member of the Board Of Directors, and I am bringing to you this week's preview! Okay, here we go! Right, there are several matches set to go down. Like, for example, Chris Pinash taking on Wolf. Pinash had a good victory at Malevolence, and will want to return to thReat after a slight injury with a victory over Wolf, who'll have one eye out for Steve Solex. Wolf's been in a rich vein of form, although the results to the matches he's taken part in don't really reflect that. But anyway, it should be a good battle. Rejection's comeback to thReat; and the industry, for that matter; will be against newbie Jay Jaxxen. Another highly interesting match-up, with both men wanting to prove their respective points. Rent-A-Hero had a smashing debut last week, leaving many people hailing him as the freshest new thing in the industry. In contrast, former Cruiserweight Champion Vertigo's been a bit jaded lately. Will this affect her when she takes on RAH? Or will the superhero's punking out of Remix the prior week turn out to be costly? The last match that I know of that will be taking place is a Cruiserweight Title showdown. Tyler Davidson will go toe-to-toe with Phil Atken, and even though both men aren't too popular with the fanbase, this match is set to send hearts racing. Finally, all the talk will be put aside and these men can finally beat the living hell out of each other. There's rumoured to be more matches, but my memory at this age is pretty shoddy. Anyway, you can expect to see most of the other superstars out in full force, as they aim to roll on with their lives & personal agendas! All this and so much more on Flaw 20. The show kicks off at 8 PM, at the Galiano Arena in Louisville, KY. Tickets are on sale at your Local Box Office or call TicketMaster for more information. Remember, the card is subject to change... since how ANYTHING can happen in thReat. ANYTHING.
