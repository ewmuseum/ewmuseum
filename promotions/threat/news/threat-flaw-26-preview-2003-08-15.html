thReat Flaw 26 Preview

15 August 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
We're down to four men. Well, not really. Due to R.A.T's no show last week, blind & R.A.T will have their Quarter-Final match on Flaw 26, and the winner will have to compete on the same night against Jere Casarella in the second Semi-Final! Tough work for whoever wins, but news of Jere Casarella having to defend his newly-won Fighting Title against DeVante Stevenson should balance things up. Touching on that matter; Vincent Pembridge was none too pleased about having lost the title, and is counting on DeVante to possibly regain is. Question is, with Rio Nexan now officially back and no longer 'dead', will he have an impact on the night's proceedings? The first Semi-Final pits Chris McCluskey & haTE. haTE isn't exactly super-popular with the fans but he has proven to be of absolutely top calibre in this tournament, and has slowly built up his own small fanbase. There will be no surprises if he does beat McCluskey, but the latter has been on a roll himself. This match will be a titanic struggle, and the winner of this match... may just go on to win the International Title. We're also expected to have some tag team action on the show, after weeks of our tag teams being missing in action due to Commissioner Colin Gear's trickery. The Commissioner also has something to say regarding the #1 Contendership to the World Title, and Phil Atken's status in the company. It'll be interesting to see what the moron has to say, and if he can stop driving the thReat budget to the ground. We'll also, hopefully, get to hear from the Board Of Directors & Troy Mason. All this, and so much more, on Flaw 26! The show kicks off at 6 PM, at the Toledo Sports Arena in Toledo, OH. Tickets are on sale at your Local Box Office or call TicketMaster for more information. And remember, the card is subject to change... since how ABSOLUTELY ANYTHING can happen in thReat.
