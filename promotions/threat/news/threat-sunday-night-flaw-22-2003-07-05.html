thReat Sunday Night Flaw 22

5 July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Flaw 22, the event preceeding the Payback PPV, proved to be one laden with surprises and slight controversy. Firstly, the fact that half of the roster weren't present due to travel problems placed immense stress on the shoulders of Moe Sole & the returning Troy Mason, who *were* supposed to have a meeting late in the show. The keyword being 'supposed', as Troy Mason decided to forego it. Some tension there, possibly? Also, (psycho)Rio Nexan & Jere Casarella continued their intesifying rivalry, with shocking consequences. You would expect both men to be thrown together into a match at Payback, to end it once and for all... but that hasn't been the case. Instead, the Sociopath by the moniker of psycho-Rio will duel with the returning Ampske, while Jere Casarella bust heads with Rent-A-Hero. Speaking of whom, the latter had a big role to play on Flaw 22... but what was it? And yes, more questions can be tagged to Flaw 22. Did General David Sanders find someone to be his partner against Vandal & Candor? Is Phil Atken the smartest man alive, or does he just irritate the heck out of Moe Sole? Did Steve Solex do the right thing in saying that Wolf is a Nazi? Could it be that Drake Thanas loves messing with the head of Chris McCluskey? What will Ernie French do now that he has no friends and Stephen Lee does, in Jay Jaxxen? Will Levond Daniels be successful in his quest to save Jessica Jenkins from the clutches of the monster, Anubis? Why did haTE suddenly become so important to ORB & Steven Fury? Did Dane Manetta make a wise choice with his request? How will blind retaliate to the sneakiness of DeVante Stevenson? Would Tyler Davidson have gotten the hint that Moe Sole doesn't really fancy him? And finally, the question to end all questions -- did Troy Mason finally find a way to deny Xaxon his prize, the thReat World Championship? And what will be the show-stealer at the Payback PPV? Indeed, it was a show that, even without half the roster, ended up being one hell of a rollercoaster ride. Payback should be even greater, but be sure to update your schedule; Payback has been brought forward by a day, and now falls on Saturday, July 5 2003! With so many matches slated to take place, you can be assured of a wonderful show! Signing off, Sergey Kovhachek
