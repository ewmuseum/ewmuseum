thReat Sunday Night Flaw 21!

25 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
A delay of the online release was due to the shocking events that capped off the night, but finally, it's up for your viewing pleasure. With the Payback PPV approaching, all the rivalries seemed to heat up, and show no sign of ending even at Payback. On the positive note, the kidnapping saga finally comes to an end, but that's as good as it gets. Highlights; 01. Daniel S. & Moe Sole having the same ideas. 02. The debut of Fire's talkshow. 03. Rio Nexan giving insight into Jere Casarella's past. 04. A lawsuit being filed against thReat? 05. Rent-A-Hero being interrogated. 06. A show-long saga revolving around Stephen Lee & Jay Jaxxen & Ernie French. 07. General Sanders finding out how lethal his new enemies appeared to be. 08. Steve Solex making things way too personal. 09. A finale that left everyone sickened yet desperate to know more. And much, more more! Check the show out NOW!
