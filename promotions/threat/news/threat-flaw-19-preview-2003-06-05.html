thReat Flaw 19 Preview!

5 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Following a week's break off the backs of Malevolence, thReat programming is set to return with Flaw 19. Now, once again a pure wrestling entertainment company, thReat won't have to worry about the likes of Vincent Pembridge & Ty Hughes taking over, although... all members of The Vagabonds will now have to adjust being wrestlers. For Ampske, due to his impressive performances since his debut, he has earned a shot at the Aggression Title and new champion Steve Solex. Solex, who defeated Wolf in an epic contest at Malevolence, will be looking to prove that the victory was anything but a fluke. And with the new rulings over the Aggression Title enforced by Troy Mason, the clash between the two fighter-like competitors should be one to watch. Also at Malevolence, a newcomer by the name of Jere Casarella got everything sorted out, and he's ready to get stuck into action. One without a professional wrestling background, the Spanish native is a wee bit worried about his wrestling debut. And facing Tyler Davidson in your first match sure seems like a frightening prospect. Nonetheless, should be interesting to see this match unfold. What will Davidson's mood be like, seeing how he won the IOW Cruiserweight Title -- which is worthless -- but didn't manage to claim the thReat Cruiserweight Title? Speaking of which, Phil Atken is, finally and actually, the thReat Cruiserweight Champion. Will he have anyone to defend it against, or will he use Flaw 19 to mock Troy Mason? Darren Anthony and nØt have been at each other's throats for a while now, and when Darren's manager turned on him to back nØt, the equation got a wee bit more deadly. Now, with nØt fully recovered, Troy Mason has signed a match to hopefully put an end to the feud. So, it will be The Dreamer versus nØt, with Graves in the corner of the latter. What will go down? Anything, really. And finally, with the sudden change in demeanour of Xaxon at the end of Malevolence, it seems that Troy Mason has a fresh set of problems on his hands, and it all revolves around the thReat World Title. Will this issue be resolved on Flaw 19? Only Troy & Xaxon know, but the consensus is the two men will only agree to disagree! All this and so much more on Flaw 19. The show kicks off at 8 PM, at the Tio Pepe Hall in Fort Campbell, KY. Tickets are on sale at your Local Box Office or call TicketMaster for more information. Remember, the card is subject to change.
