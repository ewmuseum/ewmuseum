thReat Sunday Night Flaw for June 8th!

8 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Following the Malevolence PPV, not many people expected Flaw 19 to be as groundbreaking as it eventually was. Quite a number of matches, overshadowed by the developments that unfolded and the numerous in-ring segments. Highlights included; 01. Ty Hughes providing an explanation. 02. A new interviewer being hired. 03. General David Sanders possibly coming across a new foe. 04. Jere Casarella in his pre-match preparations. 05. Levond Daniels making a bad choice, possibly. 06. The war between Darren Anthony and nØt climaxing. 07. Phil Atken showing off his new title. 08. Vertigo being offered drugs. 09. Jere Casarella and his encounters with an old friend. 10. Dane Manetta receiving some bad news. 11. Kyle Knowen throwing a jealous fit. 12. A new hero popping up to stop Remix and his funky rapping. 13. Wolf & Solex redefining their feud. 14. More newbies making their voices heard. 15. The return of a former champion. 16. Sanders cheering Troy Mason up. 17. A triple-threat tag-team match as the main event of the show. 18. Troy Mason's night ending on a horrific note.
