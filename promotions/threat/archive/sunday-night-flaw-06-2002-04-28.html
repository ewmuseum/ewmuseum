04/28/02

Tonight, a BIG night

"This is a big night," Jon Hackett told himself.

"My booking decisions tonight will make or break thReat."

Hackett sat at his office desk, twirling a pen between his index and middle finger. A sheet of paper laid on his desk. All the thReat competitors names were on it. It looked like...a checklist. Perhaps to check off who's been booked for ReLoad next week. It seems two names have already been crossed out. Lennox Dawson was one, but Hackett's left hand covered the other name. Who was it?

"Let's get this party started."

Another Debut

A man arrived in the building. He stood at 6'6", and weighed approximatley, 276 pounds. He walked with a cocky strut, and a smirk on his face. He seemed to say, "I'm better than you all," with his eyes, as he was approached by security.
"Sir, excuse me," the security guard said, catching the man off guard. The man stood there, crossed his arms, and looked into the security guards eyes.

"Hmmm, yes?" He responded. "Are you supposed to be here? What is your name? How did you get this far into the building," the security guard attacked the man with an onslaught of questions.
"Excuse me?" The man asked, very arrogantly. "Who - are - YOU!?" The security guard shouted, spreading his words apart, as if the man was a bit slow in the head.
The man's arms went down to his side, and he looked at the security guard, with a look of frustration on his face.
"I'm Steven Solex you, you imbicile. Get out of my face, you pathetic fool." The security guard backed away, as Steven walked past.

He makes his way around to the boot, bangs a fist on the top and it raises slowly for him to reach in grab his gym bag.

"You're...aren't you...but you're..." he spits out but our guy interrupts.

"Yes, that's right. Now stop drooling on my goddamned shoes and open that friggin' door!"

"Have a good night, sir." The guard grins cheesily as the man stares at him with a glare that would make Satan himself get a cold shiver up and down his spine.

With that, he steps through the open door into the brightly lit halls of the United Center.

Close Escape

"phYre's gone, Quincy's about to be dispossessed as well.. my uKw debut was smashing. Everything's hunky dory. Now, where's Mr Hackett?" Jakks murmurs to himself while strolling around in the backstage area.

The self-proclaimed Scorpion Of Manchester made a shocking appearance on uKw's latest edition of Main Event, interrupting a tag-team match, to signify his entry into the FUKW. At the last minute, however, Jakks took himself out of the FUKW equation and ultimately cost the FUKW-sponsored tag-team their match.

Tonight, he was looking to even the score against Quincy Brown, and further impress thReat Chairman Jon Hackett. Maybe a chance at the thReat Fighting Title would be given to Jakks at the ReLoad pay-per-view?

"You there, have you seen Mr Hackett?" Jakks asks of a backstage worker.

Finding it hard to comprehend Jakks with his thick British accent, the backstage worker shakes his head and walks away.

"Ah well, I'll catch up with Mr Hackett after I destroy Quincy Brown."

Jakks then turns towards his locker-room, and just as he enters the room, Jon Hackett sticks his head out from behind a pillar.

"Whew," Hackett sighs, relieved that he managed to evade Jakks and his never-ending requests for once.

Takisuma Ogama VS Riot

Darkness engulfed the arena whilst a Japanese symphonic piece reverberated from the PA system. Out stepped Takisuma Ogama from behind the entrance curtain, with that same familiar red robe covering his entire anatomy from view. Those in attendance immediately provided an immense roar of jeers, almost drowning out the symphony. Following behind Ogama was none other than “Beautiful” Bobby Blair, waving that Japanese flag high above his cranium once again, causing more disapproval amongst the crowd. Upon entering the ring, Ogama stood in the centre, and unrobed himself to reveal his uniquely painted face and black karate pants, whilst Bobby looked on from the outside.

“Whole” by Flaw suddenly hit, and the boos superceded that of the man currently in the ring. Riot, the Aggression Champion, made his way out from the backstage area. He walked purposefully down the ramp, staring down Takisuma Ogama, his every footstep practically echoing off the metal ramp way. He finally reached the ring and slid in, hell-bent on revenge for the steel chair shot he received last week courtesy of Ogama.

The fans booed the two men as they came toe to toe, and exchanged some pre-match words with one another. Ogama turned away from Riot, seemingly walking back to his corner to strategize himself for the match to come. The bell rang and the boos slowly began to fade.

Suddenly, Ogama spun around and attempted a hard right lariat, but Riot had somehow anticipated it, and grasped a hold of the oncoming leg. Riot smiled arrogantly, until Ogama took the initiative and connected with a standard enzuguiri. Riot collapsed to the mat instantly, whilst Ogama leaped back up to his feet. Bobby Blair praised his protégé from the outside, whilst the fans in attendance seemed mildly impressed. Riot, however, was furious, as he gradually ascended to his feet.

The two locked up, as Riot used his strength to gain the advantage, whipping Ogama to the ropes. On his return, he was met with a hard powerslam to the canvas. Ogama bounded back up immediately, only to be hit with a clothesline. This time Ogama was slower of the re-draw, allowing Riot to regain his composure. Riot sent a quick boot into the stomach of Ogama, before dropping him with an inverted DDT.
The fans watched in somewhat of a confused state as the two heels battled it out. In The Big Leagues (Part 2)

Standing backstage, watching a monitor, the mysterious Gremlin driver stands. Gym bag by his feet, hands in pockets, is fixed to the screen.

A tap on the shoulder and he grunts at the tapper. He continues to stare at the monitor as the man tugs on his shirt. This time, the Gremlin driver turns to face him.

He spins around to find himself face to face with thReat's owner, Jon Hackett. His look changes a little and then becomes a little more gruff.

"Yes?" he questions the owner. Looking a little annoyed that his concentration on tonight's events was broken.

Hackett chuckles to himself before extending a warm and friendly hand.

"I'm glad you made it," he replies, "I'm Jon Hackett, I own this promotion."

The man before Hackett, laughs and places his own hand inside Hackett's to succeed the shake.

"I'm glad you made it too, Jon. I'm August Monday, I own your Fighting Championship."

Jon looks at him confused a little. August returns the glare with another annoyed look.

"I don't mean literally, Jon," he replies. "Well, not yet anyway!"

Brandon Blade VS Xaxon

"Highway Star" by Deep Purple hit as the crowd jumped to their feet. Xaxon came from the back, clads in his red tights with the "X" on the crotch. He stretched his arms as he was walking towards the ring. He stepped in and awaiting Brandon Blade's music. It didn't come on. Xaxon was furious. Blade had refused to fight him on two seperate occasions because of his age.

Xaxon motioned for a microphone. He was handed it and he started screaming.

"'Vat is your problem?! My Age does not mean anyving! Get out here now so I can show you 'vat a man like myself can do! NOW!"

Some music began playing as Xaxon seemed a bit more satisfied although it turned out not to be Blade's music. It was Jon Hackett's. Jon ran to the ring with anger and was thrown a microphone. He caught it in his right hand and turned simultaneously in the direction of the rampway.

"Blade, I warned you! You either wrestle Xaxon tonight or you're fired!," yelled Hackett.

Suddenly Brandon Blade came through the crowd and attacked Xaxon and Jon from behind. He threw Xaxon out of the ring and laid Hackett out with a right hand and grabbed the microphone, now lying on the canvas. He got in his face.

"Heh, you want to fire me? You've gotta be conscious first! I'm sick of you and grandpa playing these booking games. Next week, I'll be at ReLoad and I'll be there with a plan. It's all up here, Jon. All in my head. It's planned out to perfection. Now that you are out of the way, SHIT!"

The Crowd Erupted.

Xaxon was back in the ring and start hammering away at Brandon Blade. All of the sudden.

BAM

Bleed

Xaxon hit his trademark manuever. Xaxon, the man who has been told all his time here in thReat that he was "too old" just literally fucked up one of the best in the business. Xaxon raised his arm in glory as he helped Jon Hackett to his feet.

"Jon, don't you have zomsing you 'vould like to tell the thReat fans?"

Jon held the microphone up to his mouth and screamed in fury.

"Brandon Blade! You! Are! Fired!"

The crowd with berzerk!

"Now, I'm not finished. I still haven't announced the World Title match for ReLoad."

"YEEEEEEEEEEEAAAAAAHHHHHHHH!," yelled the riled up crowd.

"This match will feature the man standing next to me, the only TRUE veteran of thReat, Xaxon!"

Xaxon pumped his fist in excitement.

"Who am I 'vacing Mr. Hackett?"

"I'm glad you asked that Xaxon."

Click.

The light went off. Blue flashes of light flew around the arena. After a good 3 minutes of this the blue lights stopped as it was still pitch dark in the arena.

"I present to you Xaxon, your opponent at ReLoad for the thReat Championship..."

The lights turned on.

"Disciple X!"

The crowd was pumped as hell. Disciple X stood in the ring, right infront of Xaxon. The main event for ReLoad was set. Disciple X versus Xaxon. Xaoxn looked stunned for a split second then inched closer to Disciple X's face. Xaxon stepped back one step and extended his hand.

They shook.

The match was set as Flaw 6 went off the air.
