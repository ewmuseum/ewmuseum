Biography

Sergey Kovhachek was born on June 3 1982, in the troubled capital city of Ukraine -- Kiev. Not the greatest place to consider being raised in, but nobody chooses the life they want. His father, Hendrei, doted on Sergey quite a lot. Mainly because Hendrei & Junnah -- Sergey's mother -- had been married for five years, and finally, a child was theirs.

Hendrei & Junnah met in 1972, when they were both twenty two years of age, and looking to start up a business of their own, finding the job market in the area to be absolutely hell. So, a delivery business between the two was set up, and within five years, they got married.

Five years later, Sergey came along.

By the time, the Kovhachek family had become pretty well-to-do, due to the delivery business. But that meant nothing, and didn't chase the misery of living in Kiev. Which was always rocked with turmoil-filled situations -- civil war, food shortage, international sanctions, corruption, high crime-rate, and more of that juicy stuff.

So, mother Junnah suggested to hubby Hendrei that maybe a move to another country, such as the land of opportunity & freedom, would be beneficial to the family's survival.

And as the family touched down in New York, Sergey found that he would have to adjust to the chaos that was the Big Apple, on the eve of his 4th birthday.

The apartment that Hendrei found -- in Brooklyn, no less -- was small and supposedly haunted, but it was the only one available within the family's budget. That wasn't the biggest problem faced by the family, however. Overcoming the frightening language barrier was the issue, but it was helped by friendly & helpful neighbours.

Another problem soon required the attention of Hendrei & Junnah. Money. This wasn't Kiev, where they could live with the peace of mind that money would NEVER be a problem. Right off the bad, the finances were tight. So, it seemed that Sergey would be left unattended at home, with his toys and periodic visits from neighbours and such, while his parents worked to put food on the table.

Junnah as a dishwasher at a pretty high-class restaurant, Hendrei as a construction worker.

The Kovhachek family soon came to realise that maybe New York wasn't THAT different from Kiev.

For the remainder of 1986, nothing much happened within the family. Hendrei's exceptional love for his son never waned, while Junnah sought to be around Sergey more. The family was getting along with their neighbours well, and Sergey really felt comfortable being taken care by them whilst his parents were busy at work. The language barrier issue was slowly being resolved.

Yeap, everything seemed hunky dory, as even the financial situation improved.

And then, Junnah became pregnant again. Must have been that celebratory romp after Hendrei found himself getting a promotion at work.

So, with the finances under threat again, Hendrei was forced to take up a second job. As a cleaner, at a nearby gym. This gave him the option of bringing Sergey along with him sometimes, since it was pretty safe.

With his mother working less now, with the baby due, Sergey found himself interacting more with his parents. Something he truly enjoyed. Junnah became even more caring of her son, while Hendrei began to impart some details of what exactly his jobs entailed to his kid. For the following seven or eight months, Sergey found himself relishing life with his parents, and the other kids in the neighbourhood.

And for his 6th birthday, sister Melehna being born was one of many wonderful presents he received.

However, all wasn't perfect in the Kovhachek camp. Mother Junnah suddenly became very ill following the birth of Melehna. This required Junnah to take even more time off work, while Sergey was handed the responsibility of helping raise his sister. Money, as usual, became yet another problem.

And for Sergey, kindergarten was a terrifying notion.

The neighbours were called in to help look after little Mehlena after news of yet another promotion for Hendrei was made available. This time, it was the job at the gym. From a cleaner, he'd become a technician of sorts, having helped the regular technicians on his shifts. It was just about this time that Hendrei realised that the gym he worked at was a hangout for boxers & wrestlers who were pretty popular in the independent scene.

All the time, he thought it was just a hang-out for fat people to attempt getting into shape.

The situation was beginning to improve, however. Junnah recovered and eventually got back to working, while resuming her role as a housewife, doting on her daughter as much as Hendrei doted on Sergey. And at the same time, the family's circle of friends grew, as Hendrei & Junnah became more involved in the little gatherings the neighbourhood had every now and then.

By then, it was already the middle of 1989. And it seemed that everything in the Kovachek camp was hunky dory. The rough bumps had been overcome, and there was no cause for panic, as even the financial situation in the household was finally stable.

It stayed quiet for a few years. Junnah got a promotion, becoming a runner in the restaurant -- one who'd plate the food and transport them to the waiters and waitresses, for serving. Hendrei became quite the popular figure in the gym, as he began to catch a few wrestling shows in the area. Sergey followed in his father's footsteps, with regards to popularity, forming several cliques with friends. Mehlena was enjoying being brought up by her mother.

Then, on the eve of Sergey's 10 birthday, Hendrei had a near-death experience while working at his construction job. A steel reinforcement pillar almost fell on the man. Thankfully for the Kovhachek family, Hendrei only escaped with a few scrapes on his arm.

But a few days following that incident, Hendrei took a risk and gave up the construction gig, after six long years.

That was the catalyst of the mini-revival of the hard times.

Junnah began to fall ill frequently again, right after another promotion, that saw her become a waitress at the restaurant. However, it wasn't all bad.

Sergey started in take an interest in journalism, intrigued by the various writing styles that he was exposed to. And Hendrei actually became officially involved in the wrestling industry, following years of dedication at the gym, as a roadie.

All this, though, made little Mehlena feel neglected and left out. With her parents becoming more and more busy, and Sergey focused on doing well in school, as well as trying to land some girl friends, there weren't any influential figures in her life paying much attention to her.

Except the neighbours, of course.

One year to the day of Hendrei's brush with death, the man of the Kovhachek family slapped on the tights and the boots for the first time in his life, as he graced the squared circle of the NY Conference Wrestling under the moniker of 'The Russian Storm'. Of course, he started out at the bottom of the ladder.

But a little bit more money was made, and the whole wrestling phenomenon had Sergey hooked. Hendrei duly explained to his son the whole concept of wrestling -- about how it was staged, but also, played out to be absolutely real.

That only improved the relationship between Hendrei & Sergey.

So, for the following couple of years, nothing much happened within the family. Until the year 1995 rolled around.

And after nine years of staying in the apartment in the heart of Brooklyn, the family moved to a nicer and admittedly bigger home, following Junnah's rise to power in the restaurant.

See, being the hardworking woman she was, Junnah actually got promoted to become general manager of the place. That didn't go down well with some people, that a foreigner actually became the GM of the restaurant.

But after nine years of complete dedication, even while she was pregnant and ill, Junnah still gave everything she had into the job.

Mariah Nozarella didn't like this at all, and swore revenge on Junnah Kovhachek. Simply because she'd started out as a waitress eight years ago, and was the headwaitress for about two years to the day. Obviously, she figured that she'd be appointed General Manager when Herman Bosch stepped down.

Apparently, she figured wrongly.

The years following that rolled by quickly. Hendrei started to move up the ladder, Junnah was doing a fantastic job of running the restaurant, Sergey stepped foot in high school and learnt about the Internet, while Mehlena started school as well, becoming interested in witchcraft at the same time due to the influence of television.

All was well.

June 1998. Sergey became 16 years of age, Mehlena became 10 years old. Mehlena also got her first taste of love, getting close with a boy who lived in the area and went to the same school she did.

June 1998. Hendrei won the second-most important title in the wrestling organisation, which had been renamed All-Star Alliance Wrestling two years prior.

June 1998. Junnah Kovhachek was murdered.

June 1998. Hendrei Kovhachek, stunned and confused over his wife's death, suffered a stroke.

July 1998. All was not well.

Within a month, the Kovhachek family was ripped to shreds. Sergey was completely baffled with the plague of events that left him a shell of his previous self. He felt angry, betrayed by the forces that made the universe.

Why him, he wanted to know.

He didn't have time to wait for an answer. Mehlena needed to be taken care of, and money had to be found to take care of Hendrei, who was almost as effective as someone in a comatose state. All the family friends amassed over the years stepped up to the plate here, helping Sergey & Mehlena survive with their rushed entry into adulthood.

Or rather, the responsibilities of adulthood.

Sergey got a job as a writer for a teenage magazine, as well as working the night shift at a nearby drug store. He had to juggle those two jobs with school and the taking care of Mehlena & Hendrei. At least his sister had Jefferey, the boyfriend. But his father only had Mehlena, who was still too much of an emotional wreck.

But then, Hendrei's contacts and pals made while working as a construction worker and at the gym and even as a wrestler came through for the main man of the Kovhachek family.

And in late 1998, some form of revenge was gained, when Mariah Nozarella and husband Granje Nozarella were sentenced to seven & nine years in prison respectively, for the combined murder of Junnah Kovhachek.

That did little to lighten the mood in the Kovchachek family. But Sergey & Mehlena knew that they had to move on.

So, they did just that.

For the next five years, Sergey & Mehlena handled themselves well, despite the massive pressure on their shoulders. But with the help of family friends and Junnah's friends and Hendrei's friends, they survived.

Sergey graduated from college with a journalism degree, and started work with a more recognised publication, focusing on the lifestyles of people in North America. Not exactly what Sergey had in mind, but the options for him were limited at the time. Plus, he was one of the lucky few to even get picked up for a job.

Of course, doing the job interview, Sergey was told about how his written English was better than his spoken English, and that most people formed impressions based on their verbal grasp of the language.

This didn't deter Sergey from following in his father's footsteps and venturing into the wrestling industry, receiving training from the same people that worked alongside his father. At the same time, Sergey was well aware of the fact that the industry had changed.

More and more people were discarding the traditional rules of wrestling, and going into wrestling with the frame of mind that they could do whatever they wanted.

Sergey, however, didn't wish to go down that route. That was further proved when, while in the midst of training in late 2002, he was offered a chance to become an internet journalist, in the field of professional wrestling.

Naturally, he accepted. And started by covering some of the smaller promotions in the tri-state area.

It was hard for Sergey to juggle training and his journalism career -- on both fronts -- while he had other pursuits. Such as starting work on attaining an IT degree and helping out several mates with a car maintenance business. Not to mention taking care of father Hendrei, who was actually looking to recover from his stroke.

But he still had time to be with his sister, Mehlena. Who had blossomed into quite the angsty and moody teenager, with a fetish for goth now. The murder of her mother obviously affected her the hardest, and a break-up with Jefferey in early 2002 didn't help matters much. High school was something very enjoyable for Mehlena, however.

And she was just starting out.

Now, it's 2003. Mehlena's 15, Sergey's officially a man now at 21. With their whole lives still ahead of them, Mehlena & Sergey are looking to take their relationship as siblings to a new level, being the remaining survivors of the Kovhachek family.

But for Sergey, a whole new spin on his life was about to occur.

He'd been snapped up by thReat to become a reporter, and now, he was ready to begin his wrestling career in thReat as well.

What beckons? Wait and find out.
