Biography

So, you little pricks wanna know all about the life of me, Erik Andrews, eh?

Well, to start everything off, I hate all of you, because I’m better than you, period. But anyways, I was born on December 31st, 1974. My mother and father were supposedly the happiest people on the planet that day… Too bad the fuckers were shot two weeks later by some serial killer that was stalking my neighborhood at the time, taking out everybody who lived there. Thank God I was still in the hospital then.. Why, you ask? Well, I was born without eyesight in my left eye, and with blurry vision in my right. So basically, visually, I was really fucked up. I had no color in my left eye, only the black pupil. I was a really scary looking kid.

By age 5, I was hooked on wrestling. At that time, I was staying with my cousins, all the way across town. My aunt and uncle hated wrestling, but they supported my habit of watching it and all of that shit. They would let me stay up to watch the weekly shows, and in return, I’d be a good little boy for Aunt Florence and Uncle Nick. In reality, I really didn’t like either of them, but that’s beside the point. They gave me food, clothing, and a roof over my head, so I guess I have to be semi-respectful towards them, ya know?

For the next 7 years, nothing exciting really happened to me, except for I got this contact for my right eye, that made my vision clear.. Basically, for me it was like getting a completely new eyeball, and I could finally see clearly. Sure, I had glasses before this, but FUCK, like I wore ‘em, haha… Also, I was starting to become the big, tough, bully-type character that I am today. I was starting to get muscular (thanks to the hours and hours of work that my uncle to pile onto me in between home-schooling sessions…I still hate that fucker for that.), I was getting that punk-ass attitude that I have now (don’t you just love it?), and I was starting to get in fights (and most of the time winning…believe me, there were some times where I’d get my ass handed to me, but that’s nothing that a knife, or 2 by 4 can’t fix, right?) with other kids. Yes, I was finally starting to grow up.

Eventually, by age 18, I had moved out of the house, and was looking for employment. I had tried getting jobs at McDonald’s, Burger King, Wendy’s, Taco Bell, and Subway, but unfortunately, no one liked the fact that I was more interested in stealing food, than making it. *shrug* Haha, oh well…

I actually started getting jobs at various clubs and hotels as a bouncer/security officer. Sure, it wasn’t fun, but fuck, it paid some good cash for me to live off of. I worked at that for about 7 years, until I got caught stealing from some old bitch in a hotel. I mean, she had enough gold on her to pay for fucking Madison Square Garden, for Christ’s sake! Needless to say, it got me a good few years in jail.. *shrug*

And now, I find myself looking for a job… Where will I end up? Only time will tell, fuckers!

And yes, I am still better than ALL of you…period.
