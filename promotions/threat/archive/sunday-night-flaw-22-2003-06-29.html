You would think that a normal man, after having been kidnapped and physically tortured to no end, wouldn't even risk going to his workplace, where the bane of his existence would have the chance to maim him again. But for Troy Mason, it didn't matter the number of broken bones he had. It didn't matter the number of cuts and gashes he had ALL OVER his body.

It didn't matter.

Inside his office, Troy stared at the stack of paperwork on his table. All as a result of the weeks where the Board of Directors -- Moe Sole, more specifically -- were left in charge of the actual day-to-day operations of the company. Of course, with none of them actually having prior experience as THE owner of a promotion, there were bound to be mustakes made. Such as contractual details, for instance.

But Mason never expected the mess that was made to be of such great magnitude. Moe Sole and company had actually done a good job, but the little things had been left undetected and uncared for. And it was all the little things that were coming back to haunt Troy Mason, as it did in the past. All the small things, as always, would snowball into one massive mess.

It looked neverending for Troy Mason.

Grabbing a single file off the table surface, Troy slowly leaned back in his seat and began reading the contents of the file. He'd decided that having dealt with paperwork the last two days and resting the days prior, there was more urgent business to attend to. A loophole HAD to be found.

There were only so many ways to say NO & I DON'T KNOW to the lunatic himself, Xaxon.

Without the thReat World Title, Xaxon couldn't re-begin his reign as champion. And while Troy had kept saying that he had no idea where the title was, it was secretly in his possession the whole time. Unlike the thReat Fighting Title, stolen by Vincent Pembridge & Bobby Knickerson in the early weeks of the company's re-emergence, Troy was ordered to keep the title with him or someone he absolutely trusted. Who ordered for this?

Jon Hackett.

Now, assuming that Mason had someone to trust the title with; who would he or she be?

Simple. His mother, half-senile in her nursing home.

Frowning, Troy shook his head. Prior to the kidnapping, he had hoped that everything would have been able to be resolved in an amicable manner. No need for violence, and all that bollocks. Apparently, that wasn't the case. This was about Xaxon's greed and ego, and his quest for power. The kidnapping opened a whole new can of worms, one of the most damaging results being a lawsuit of some sort, for false and unsubstantiated accusing.

"He's... not gonna... win."

Troy Mason was on a mission; he'd just sworn to fight until victory was finally his, once and for all.

One way, or another.
