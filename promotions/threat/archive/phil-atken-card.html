Biography

Every day you go onto one of these wrestling sites and you read the stories of peoples life, and they were all beaten like a red headed step-child. They were bullied, they had sex with their mother, they're depressed, they're drug addicts, they faked the moon landing, they once ate some cheese cake that gave them special powers. They always have some special reason for getting into the hell hole that is the sport of wrestling.

Me? Oh I got into this sport by being a drunken fool. I never had any interest in this sport ever, but one drunken night out I signed up for some wrestling "Skool", yes that's right the owner couldn't even spell school. This is the sort of idiots you work with in your everyday life. Most of them are, if I'm totally honest, in-bred hicks and if they didn't go into wrestling they'd probably be selling chickens on the Motorway. (Do they sell chickens on the motorway anymore? I mean that has to be one of the dumbest ideas ever, after professional wrestling of course). Now what the hell was I talking about? Ah yes, so I had signed up for these lessons, and they were quite pricey so I thought I better make use of them. It was there I met one of the dumbest men in this sport, Colin Gear, my freaking trainer. Now Gear isn't exactly one of the most respected men in wrestling, he's a former jobber with a huge ego, power hungry moron, who kills any company he's in charge of (see cWo). Totally off topic tangents are great, get used to them bitchcakes. Now, Colin didn't expect to be dealing with anyone with such extreme raw talent as I had, and after about four weeks of training, he threw me out into the lions, and of course I kicked those lions arses all over the place. Those poor fools had no idea who they were dealing with, I'm the smartest man in this game god damn it. And to think people spend all their time bitching about HHH, they have no idea I have had every company I've worked for in the palm of my hands.

So, after I beat up those bunch of loser, Colin quickly hired me for cWo, a company he had recently found a powerful role in backstage. Not a lot of people know this, but Wendel was only "on-air" talent, Colin Gear and some other loser whose name escapes me at the moment, those were the guys running that damn place. Sure, Wendel had SOME power, but Gear had the power to book the matches how he wanted them and the way he wanted them, which resulted in me becoming cWo TV Champion. You see, Colin may have been the one in power, but he was my puppet, I controlled him… that was until the bitch fired my ass due to budget cuts. Apparently the company had went over-budget, something about spending too much money of clocks or something, be damned if I know what that was about. Of course without my talent, and my ratings drawing powers, cWo quickly sank, and the cWo Board left a monkey in charge of the company.

Okay, re-read that statement.

THEY LEFT THE COMPANY IN A MONKEYS HAND.

Those are the sort of morons I constantly deal with in wrestling. They have no idea what the fans want, they only know what they want. They never thought that the fans wanted me, but they did, oh yes, and they paid for firing me. Paid a heavy price indeed, CLOSURE. So there you go cWo fans, that's the sort of idiots who ran your favourite company, and you wonder why it closed...

So for many months I was left jobless, with no direction to go, sure I had made an ass load of money on my cWo salary, and no I'm not going to tell you what I was making, lets just say it was "enough" and move on shall we. I thought I might return to my true love, taking pictures of nude ladies for lots of money, but alas it was not to be...

You see folks, I got a phone call from Colin asking if I was interested in coming to some fighting company he had recently signed a contract to. He claimed he was trying to take it over from the inside, using backstage politics and such but I refused, knowing that Colin was a total moron who had fired my ass from cWo. I mean what did he expect after firing me? Forgive him and pretend the whole incident never happened? God, he was a moron, no wonder he's unemployed AGAIN. The next night I got another phone call, this time from IOW, they said they'd seen my cWo stuff and were short of cruiserweights, they wondered if I'd be interested in signing for them. I thought about it, but I had to ask the most important question of all: "How much for?", and the amount they replied with was too good to true, which was very much the case. You see, unlike Wendel, Scum actually did run the company in and out the ring, and maybe I'll tell you all about the IOW incident one-day but it ain’t today. What I will say though is that Scum hated the damn place and wanted it closure, and after I had two show appearances the arsehole shut down the fucking place. I never did get one cheque from that place, but it was no biggy.

Apparently thReat scouts had been watching my IOW stuff and were quick to snap me up straight after the closure, and here I am. There's only one problem with thReat, I have no friends in power, but that doesn't matter, I still have this place wrapped around my little finger. thReat doesn't like people talking about the backstage antics that go on around here, and know that I'm not afraid to tell anyone what's really going on, so what do they do to shut me up? Put a title around my waste, (a crappy one at that, but I managed to make it mean something. Something I can't say about that bitch Vertigo) but I still mouthed off on the company so they were quick to try and get that title off me. I managed to fight them off for a month, but the buggers finally found a way to do it. They had to make sure I was exhausted and out of it before they even dared put someone against me, so what do they do? Book the match to happen at an IWO event I happen to be debuting at. Not only that, but they booked the match to take place AFTER my first place. Not only that but if I didn't defend it, I would be suspended. You see, the thReat Board of Directors and Troy Mason hate my guts, they try to make me look like the bad guy just because I'm a little smarter than most of the other talent. Just because I don't fit into the thReat mould, just because I'm not a complete idiot who's only in this sport to get revenge for his parents death, or whatever the feck these cunts whinge about nowadays. Of course the problem with screwing me is that I hit back twice as hard Mason, oh yes I do Sole. You both better watch out, as should most of your talent, I'm not getting screwed again. You shall all see...

Oh and by the way, the only reason David Sanders won the world title is because he and Mason are often found in the janitors closet together, I'm not at liberty to say what they do, but I think you can guess.

I am the smartest man in wrestling...
