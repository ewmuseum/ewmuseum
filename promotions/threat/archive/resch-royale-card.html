Resch Royale

Nickname: "The 'Royale' One"

Born: December 14th, 1976 Place of Birth: Berlin, Germany Hometown: Hamburg, Germany

Height: 6'5'' Weight: 260 lbs. Blood type: AB Eye color: Blue

Theme song: "Whisper" by Evanescence

Style

Competing style: Powerhouse / All-Arounder

Signature moves:
Powerbomb, Pump-Handle Slam, Big Boot to the Head, Powerslam, Belly-to-Belly Suplex, Headbutt, Clothesline, Choke

Finisher: Spinning powerbomb into pin. Finisher name: the Knightship

Historical Info

Other/Previous promotions: ROGW, HoF

Other titles won: ROGW Universal Champion (x3), ROGW Tag Team Champion, ROGW Hardcore Champion (x2), HoF World Champion, HoF US Champion (x3), HoF Hardcore Champion (x2)
Other awards won: ROGW Hall Of Famer, ROGW 2000 Wrestler of the Year, HoF Rookie of the Year 2001, HoF Royal Rumble 2001 Winner

Biography

The Royale family is respected. Starting nearly into the late 1800's the Royale's have been the foundation of German wrestling since the turn of the century. Adolf Royale began the first large German wrestling corporation, the German-Alliance of Honor. The family was then well trained through many generations. The Royale family is feared and noble...their latest protege is Resch Royale, a young and hopeful yet unmodest wrestler. He has potential, but also flaws. Resch is determined to leave the family name untarnished. Will he?
