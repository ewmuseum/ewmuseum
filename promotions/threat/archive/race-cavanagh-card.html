Race Cavanagh

Real Name: Race Cavanagh (Ray-s Cav-a-nar) Nickname: "The Essence of Intense, The Detroit Velocity, The Innovator of High Risk," Also known as: Jaxx Cavanagh (fWo Survivor 3 applicant)

Born: 3rd August 1982 Place of Birth: Hartlepool, England. Hometown: Detroit, Michigan, USA (From 3 Years old)

Height: 5'11 Weight: 208lbs Blood type: O+ Eye color: Hazel

Team: ---

Manager: --- Trainer: Sinclair Roberts and Savio Chavez

Style

Competing style: Fast-Paced Extremist/Innovative High Flyer

Signature moves:

Race-buzzter = After delivering a toe-kick to his opponents mid-section, Race takes a step back then jumps onto their shoulders. From there Race pushes on their head sending himself backwards and bringing them down in a facebuster fashion, landing in a sit-down position and driving the opponent face first into the mat.

If done with enough force, this can count as a finishing move.

Inverted Russian Leg Sweep = Race grabs his opponent in an armbar position then brings their arm to his chest while they are facing him. He then uses his free arm to hook around their neck and trips them with his foot (same side as opponent) sending him/her face first into the mat.

---

Common Moves = DDT, swinging DDT, bulldog, crucifix pin, neckbreaker, some kind of suicidal flip over the top rope and onto his opponent, running flying lariat, middle rope leg drop, Russian leg sweep, various hurricanrana, punches/jabs, talented in reversal

Rare Moves = Diving Sun-set flip from the top rope, Shooting-star press, pulling piledriver

Impact Moves = Tornado DDT, diving inverted neckbreaker, Enziguri kick, unorthodox neckbreaker

Finisher: 1) Race perches on the top rope whilst waiting for his opponent to stand. When they do so and face him, he leaps from the top rope and grabs hold of their head. With his opponents support both him and Race pull a 180-degree rotation before Race snaps them down with a DDT, planting their head square into the canvas.

Done in a very quick fashion to ensure total impact

2) With opponent stunned on the mat, Race runs at the centre of the ropes and jumps up onto the top rope springboarding/bouncing off it before turning 180-degrees and planting a leg drop across his opponents neck.
Finisher name: 1) Thrill Kill 2) Detroit Drop-in

Biography

At twenty years of age, Race Cavanagh had a lot of good and bad things to look back on.

He was a well-known figure in many parts of Detroit, even at the age of fifteen. This reputation was because of his “anything goes” attitude to towards…well everything. You might think a person like this would have left school as soon as he started and refused to take part in any sort of educational activity. But no, Race enjoyed school for some unknown reason and his true form was only released after the school bell rang for the final time of the day.

At sixteen, Race performed at many underground heavy metal concerts, imitating his favourite rock artists. Even though his five foot eleven, 215-pound figure had no resemblance at all to any of the imitated artists, he was deeply respected for what he did.

His parents on the other side hated what he loved, hated what he did and…well…hated him. Even as a child they used to bully and verbally abuse him but he was always to plastered with alcohol to remember what they had did. He never did have the most perfect of childhoods, though his parents horrendous behaviour didn’t pass to him, he kept as himself and acted how has his heart told him.

Besides his passion for music, Race loved wrestling, he loved it to the point were he would watch a match 10 times before becoming in any way bored of it. But training was out of the question, every time he went, he couldn’t help but turn a practice match into a “no holds barred” brawl. This fact annoyed Jaxx and it was those facts, which lead him to making a backyard wrestling federation. Because of his popularity in the underground, many used to turn up to see his wrestling shows consisting of 3 matches, wrestled on four unwanted mattresses. There was no doubt in any watchers mind, that Race had talent, that Jaxx would one day live up his dream and become a professional but the question was…When?

By his seventeenth birthday, Race had lost his feelings for music and was totally concentrating on his wrestling career. He had become more tolerant towards others beating him and was finally accepted into a wrestling school. There he trained and trained, harder than any other member, his belief was too strong to miss this chance. His temper was still a factor when speaking or planning with others and any personal comments would instantly, without any tolerance get to Race. He attended the gym regularly and had grown into a muscular physique, well a small 5’11, 210 pound wrestling figure.

2 years passed and Race Cavanagh had learned nearly all he had too, he had lost weight and grew into a body he was proud of.

His cousin, Jamie Bond had been in the same wrestling organisation as him for around the same length of time. However Race had been the first opponent Jamie had ever met in the ring.

Race had adapted to the rough side of wrestling, he often forgot what talent he had and used his brawling attitude to impress the crowd. Not one fan could say they didn’t enjoy it when Race Cavanagh’s music hit and he came out holding a steel chair in his hand. But it wasn’t always that common piece of steel he used; tables, ladders and a fan’s bottle of beer often came into play, in other words violence and aggression were the suiting words to race’s gimmick. To say his brawling got him as far as he had came would be a lie, Race loved takedowns of any sort and also quick, spinning moves. His well known finishing move; “The Thrill Kill” was a high-risk yet match-ending leap from the top rope into a swinging DDT. Race would ascend to the top rope then leap off connecting with his opponent and spinning them around in some degree of rotation before planting them skull first into the mat. Most of Race’s high-risk moves where of a fascinating standard, whether the move was executed with force or not.

Anyway…

At twenty years of age, he had become a regular match winner at the wrestling schools weekly event. There was nothing left for him to do there, save sit get in the ring and perform. He wanted a new way, a new perspective, he found thReat.
