Biography
Leader.

What Tristan Carter had been since he first led his 2nd grade class into a snowball fight with the 3rd graders. Leading the football team to four straight playoff appearances in high school. Two time state champion in wrestling. A bright student and one of the most popular student council presidents ever. Tristan is the ideal leader.

When Tristan's father bought a wrestling gym for his brother, Lincoln Carter, in 1988, Tristan and his four other siblings would most often be found there. They loved the sport as much as they loved their uncle. And Lincoln had the desire to train all five of them. When things began to crumble in 1998 - who kept things together? Tristan did. Because even in his late teens he was a fantastic leader and had family values. And now in 2002 - you see Tristan with those exact same traits. Because he is the glue.

Despite beginning his professional wrestling career, Tristan has managed to hold the Carter family intact. The one who got the whole family to come back for Thanksgiving and his father's birthday before that. But will professional wrestling change that? Will Tristan become more concerned with his career than to worry about his family? Some answers are obvious - others will be a nip in the butt - yes, that surprising.
