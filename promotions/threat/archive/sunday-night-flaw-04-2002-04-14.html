Arrival

“Keep the change . . . “

Stepping out of the taxi is none other than thReat’s two newest superstars “Beautiful” Bobby Blair and Takisuma Ogama. Bobby Blair looks calm and collective, he puffs on a cigar while Takisuma Ogama removes the gym bag from the trunk.

They’re standing outback of the thReat arena in which will be a highly anticipated night. Ogama makes his debut and this is do or die, first impressions are important. Yet, Bobby Blair seems unworried.

He glances at the camera and slowly takes a drag of his cigar before dropping it down to the side of his business suit. Exhaling he blows smoke into the lense creating a small cloud.

“You must be here for the kid?” he questions.

Now focused on Ogama who stands wearing his sweat suit from earlier, he holds a gym bag and looks rather emotionless. Not understanding the English language is rather rough, Ogama stands there with some confusion.

“He might not look like much, but just wait ‘till later tonight when he gets his hands on Noah. Right Ogama?”

Attempting to speak English Takisuma sounds out “R-i-g-h-t!”

The two slowly turn and enter the back of the arena.

Another Favour? Geez, Does This Guy Ever Stop?

"Mr Hackett! Mr Hackett!"

For the third consecutive week, the man known as Jakks is running up to Jon Hackett; chairman of thReat.

The first time, Jakks attempted to ask Jon Hackett if the latter would like to be his 'partner' of some sort. But the former UFF superstar couldn't bring himself to actually go through with it. Last week, Jakks subtly asked to be treated like royalty. Hackett said he could get just that if he defeated Amp Canon later on in the show.

And guess what? Jakks is travelling in style now.

"Jakks, what's the matter now? You wish for the walls of your locker-room to be bright green? You want the dog outside the arena shot?" Hackett answers rather agitatedly.

If you were him, you would be agitated too.

Yeah, you. Sitting on your fat ass, drinking your expired milk and eating your tasteless chips.

"Actually, I just wanted to say how incredibly refined you are looking tonight.." Jakks replies solornly.

Hackett's face tells the story of embarrasment, as he looks down at his feet while slumping his shoulders.

"Sorry."

"It's quite okay."

A few moments of awkward silence pass, with both Jakks & Hackett looking down at their respective feet. Jakks's face, as he raises it, suddenly lights up.

"Also, I just wanted to ask if I could get a shot at the thReat Fighter's Title. I think I deserve it," the man from Manchester says.

Hackett sighs, and scratches his head. Raising his head up, he looks Jakks in the face and nods.

"You deserve it, you're correct. Beat phYre tonight and you have yourself a deal!" Hackett replies after several moments of pondering.

Jakks grins maniacally and mouths his appreciation just as Jon turns and walks down the hall. Jakks too turns and walks down the opposite passageway.

"Geez, does this guy ever stop?" Hackett mutters under his breath, with Jakks safely out of earshot.

Yeah, does that Jakks fella ever stop? Bloody idiot.. who does he think he is?

'Fucking censors, make him suffer some more!'

And he continued to make QB suffer. But the whole time the network bleeped out 'nigger', Quincy heard the word loud and clear. The words hurt more than the stinging kicks that Amp Canon continued to deliver into his ribcage. Quincy soon took a hold of Amp's left ankle and tripped Canon to the mat. He then scrambled to his feet, and started to unleash some kicks of his own, followed by...

Canon lays on the mat trying to cover up from the stomps, but only ends up feeling the weight of the boots coming down with force on his left arm. Quincy would soon stop stomping to the delight of Jimmy.

Amp's face was now fully covered in blood, masking his skin. There was really nothing more he can do but use his primal instincts, and fend off QB.

Quincy reared back for "Shoot Em In The Head", but Amp sidestepped and kneed QB in the ribs that he had practically kicked out of his back, earlier in the contest. Quincy clutched his ribs as Amp reared back for what looked liked "Bull's Horn".

Riot VS Lennox Dawson

Flaw's "Whole" hit as the fan erupted in boos.

The hated Riot slowly stepped out from behind the curtains as the fans continued to jeer. Unfazed, Riot started for the ring, walking with a purpose. Tonight he would be facing Lennox Dawson for thReat's Aggression Championship, a title match that Riot had demanded his way into, and received said shot. Riot had never been a stranger to title matches, however his list of accolades was well short of anyone that had spent as much time wrestling as he did.

As Riot reached the ring he slowly walked up the black, metal steps and stepped in the ring. He demanded a microphone and saw that a mic was being handed to him. He snatched it up and smacked the top to make sure it was on. He signaled his music stop and waited for the crowd to quite down. Chants of 'asshole' sprung up in places throughout the crowd. Riot sneered as he raised the microphone to his lips.

"You know, miserable cunts like you aren't going to bring me down with your little chanting. So I advise that you stop wasting your breath, sit the f*ck down, shut the f*ck up and listen to what I have to say."

The fans booed as Riot paused a moment to look up at the crowd. He raised his hand and pointed the mat below.

"Tonight Lennox Dawson will get his. Lennox Dawson, I am no Brandon Blade, I will not bring you out here and demand you to lick my boots. Instead I will keep it simple, I simply ask you to come out here and receive the beating you so rightfully deserve! Lennox Dawson, I am a man of action, I am here for one thing and one thing only... the thReat Aggression Title! And no man, living or dead, will stop me from wearing that belt!"

The Indomitable

A voice echoed through the vaguely open door of locker-room 434. Laughing and giggling of a deep voice blurred softly, just being competent to here. Deeply, as the same individual over and over gasped, speaking to himself faintly. A dim light begin to smolder through the windows, taking over the obscurity in the crack of the door.

"Creeek"

A strong noise was heard, as the door jolted open. A figure was in view, smiling as he looked into the camera’s lenses. He was muscular built, with strong blue eyes, which stood out in the dazzling light over him. Black hair, spiked and groomed, with a smirk on his face and squinted eyes.

"When you look into my eyes." He paused, and then continued.

"You’ll see the devastation that I bring to thReat."

He stood there with coldness embedded on his face. The figure stepped forward, motioning his head straight into the camera.

"Who am I?"

He began to walk the other way, as the camera stood frozen in the exact same spot. Not moving, at all. With no intension, he lugged his head facing the camera just before turning the corner.

"Disciple X"
