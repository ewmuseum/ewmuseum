The Upperhand Jakks: Kage Fighting Incorporated. The organisation in which Alex Sanders supposedly rose to superstardom. Bollocks. Absolute shite. Sounds like the brother of that git, Mike Sanders. You know, the bum in WCW?

Jakks is talking to his new lady friend Helena.

In his dressing room, Jakks continues to flip through the pages of phYre's Kage profile. One that he got through illegal means.

Helena shrugs in response to Jakks' question, and the Scorpion Of Manchester looked a little angry that his partner couldn't enjoy the joke.

Jakks: August Monday was a big factor in helping me acquire this precious folder.

Helena takes a sip of her Pepsi.

Helena: Yeah, I know. I did make the call, you know?

Jakks smiles at Helena's comment, and turns to wink at her. Helena blushes, before she gets up and walks over to the mirror in the room. Wiping the dust off her rather short dark-blue velvet skirt, Helena admires her body in the mirror for a few seconds.. before she notices that Jakks is looking at her.

Helena: I'm going to the ladies room.

She turns around and smiles at Jakks.

Who smiles back as his partner leaves the room. Sighing, Vincent Pembridge's eyes continue to scan the contents of the folder.. before abruptly closing it.

Jakks: My mother couldn't handle my father's death. She began drinking, and drinking...and drinking. She soon became abusive and beat me. I had to leave, but before I could go on my own, I was thrown out. Jessica Sanders, my mother, threw me out of my own home, and left me for dead.

Repeating the words printed in phYre's resume, Jakks sniggers as he remembered what he did to his rival last week. Throwing him off the stage may have seemed.. a tad bit too violent. But after all the things he had to endure over the past couple of years, that move seemed like nothing to the Scorpion Of Manchester.

Cracking his neck, Jakks winced. Obviously still feeling the effects of the previous night's action in England, the Englishman throws the folder down on the floor and continues to ponder.

Jakks: Mess with me, will me? I'll show him. And once I'm done with this wanker, it's time to set the record straight with Quincy. Bleeding hell.. that title is rightfully mine. No matter, patience is of the essence..

Jakks gets up from his chair and leaves his dressing room.

Not before locking it, however.

The Scorpion Of Manchester's biggest weapon against phYre is in the room.. and he wouldn't want to lose it now that he is firmly in the driver's seat.
