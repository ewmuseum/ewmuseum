Historical Info

Biography

Randall Alder-Tangk was born in the heart of the Mississippi. Living the luxury life, well not really rich and famous lifestyle, but to a kid living on a house boat which cruised up and down the Mississippi, could you want anymore? So yes, he lived a luxury life to him.

Though life was tough for his parents, they weren't poor, but they weren't living the high life. They worked hard trying to pick up as much work as they could wherever they might stop. Randall's father, Dieter Alder, was a jack of all trades, master of none.

Randall never really knew how he got into wrestling, it just seemed to fall in his lap and if you're given an opportunity like this, you take it. You don't look a gift horse in the mouth. It was an uncanny circumstance, but good stuff happens to good people.

It was one sunny day as the H.M.S. Alder floated happily down the river, Randall's father had just done some mechanical work in the last town they'd visited and they were cashed up. They could live happily for a good two months without his father having to work, but of course, he always worked anyways. As they floated by, they noticed a small dingy with a little Hispanic man fishing. Politely, they waved to the man, he waved back.

They were floating by, young Randall was aged all of ten, quite a strong boy for his size, but would be referred to as a runt by most. He watched happily as the Hispanic man casted into the river, it wasn't long before he'd gotten a bite...

"Daddy! Daddy! Slow down, I want to watch him catch the fishy," he cried to Dieter.

The boat slowed so his son could watch the little man catch his fish. In all his years on the Mississippi, Dieter had never seen such a big fish in the river. One of those mythological fisherman's "the one that got away" type of tale.

The fish leapt out of the water and over the small dingy, knocking the man into the river. He did not come up.

Before Dieter could stop his son, he was overboard and swimming against the current and had managed to get the man to the shore, safe and sound. Dieter, by this time, had leapt overboard and had swam to meet them at the shore. He performed CPR on the little Hispanic man as his wife called for the ambulance on their two-way radio.

The little Hispanic man was taken to hospital, Randall wanted to make sure this little man was safe and sound and was actually breathing and living. Dieter wanted to make sure as well, they were just that kind of down to earth people.

They spent two days in the hospital, Randall never left the little man's side. As the man came to he looked up and saw the smiling face of his ten year-old saviour.

"My little angel," he said to Randall.

"I owe you my life!"

Randall, happy the man was safe, turned to leave, back to live the life of a river rat like his father and mother had. But the little Hispanic man grabbed Randall by the arm, stopping him in his tracks.

"I'm not a rich man," he said, "but maybe I could share my wisdom with you to make you wealthy, yes?"

Dieter had come into the room to see what they were talking about. The man explained that when he was younger he was a famous wrestler in Mexico. He had excelled in the lucahdore nature of wrestling. An art exclusive and kept to many Mexican families in his time.

He went under the name of Bull Mask II, his father had taught him to wrestle. He would have taught his sons to wrestle, but the one son he had suffered severely from cerebral palsy. And he'd had a daughter as well.

He explained that his son lived until the age of twenty-one, but had died two years ago, his body was just too tired.

He had come to Mississippi to visit his daughter, Ezmerelda. She had just birthed a grandson for him. He had come alone as his wife had left him several years ago. He promised his daughter he would catch the biggest fish for their feast, but unfortunately the river nearly took his life.

Except for Randall Alder-Tangk. His angel.

And all he had to offer was his knowledge in perhaps training Randall to become a wrestler. He didn't have to make a living out of it. He didn't even have to if he wanted to. But the little Hispanic man begged he let him teach him for a week, for he knew that Randall had the heart of a great wrestler. He could tell by the look in Randall's eye he was destined for great things.

Dieter said they had a spare room on the H.M.S. Alder if he wanted to travel the Mississippi with them, there would be no charge. In exchange for his knowledge they would give him a roof, food and a family.

The little Hispanic man, Pedro Ramirez as his name turned out to be, agreed. As he was close to his daughter and her family and would be able to visit them anytime. And who could deny the great life on the Mississippi.

So as the days wore on, Pedro taught Randall who to wrestle. Randall was quite quick, he was strong, but he didn't have the power other wrestlers would have, so he had to do things his own way and work his best around his small stature. He worked hard on his technical wrestling as well as his luchadore teacher's way, if he wanted to get into the business he had to make sure that every move he did slowed down his opponent.

The days wore on and he had turned fourteen years old, Pedro still travelled with the family and was more excited than ever with Randall's skill. They rolled down the river and they had come across a carnival. Pedro, Randall and Dieter headed into town for the carnival.

They noticed a sign saying spend two minutes in the ring with the Spine Splitter and win three hundred dollars. They had heard about this guy, Pedro had remembered him off the circuits in Mexico. He was banned for his stiffness in the ring, he never fixed his ways and was probably harder now that he had to earn his living travelling around America with a carnival.

Randall wanted to enter Spine Splitter's tournament. Pedro wasn't sure if he could do it but Randall said one thing to him that made him certain he could do it...

"I could win as Bull Mask III."

It made Pedro's heart melt. The innocence of a child. As Bill Cosby says, kid's say the darndest things.

They headed back to the boat where Pedro presented Randall with a mask he had made for him. It was brilliant with it's colours. It's browns and golds and reds, and when he put the mask on he felt empowered with the spirit of Pedro and his father before him. He was fulfilling an old man's tradition and he was proud.

He arrived at the carnival wearing the mask and a pair of sweat pants. He stood all of about 5'3" tall and probably just made his weight over 120 pounds. He stood in the middle of the ring and the people pointed and laughed. Spine Splitter entered the ring and he pointed at Bull Mask III laughing his head off. The bell rang and he charged at the young boy, four swift kicks to the thigh had knocked Spine Splitter to his knees as his thigh cramped almost immediately. He cringed as his felt the tautness within his thigh.

A dropkick to the face, stiffer than he could ever have imagined from Bull Mask III. His nose began to bleed. Spine Splitter grew angry and chased him around the ring. Mockingly, Bull Mask III skipped around as the man chased him. He was the attraction at the carnival and he was being upstaged by the 5'3" 120 pound nobody.

Bull Mask III rebounded off the ropes and hit a dropkick to the knee off Spine Splitter. He dropped to his stomach as Bull Mask III quickly swung into his training. He lifted the legs up of Spine Splitter and got the knees under his arm pits, he stood on the shoulder blades of Spine Splitter and delivered his variation of the elevated Boston Crab. The people who had paid to watch Spine Splitter do what his name said he did, but they roared with approval when this nobody from out of nowhere made the monster tap.

Spine Splitter tapped the canvas hard as the fourteen year-old boy rejoiced with his teacher and his father. The trio jumped around in the ring as they were rewarded their three hundred dollars.

A man approached them as they headed back to the boat. He had a fancy suit and an accent they weren't really sure of. But they could tell he was trying to sell them something.

He was kinda.

He ran an independent wrestling promotion in the heart of North Carolina and he would pay handsomely for Bull Mask III to wrestle. He was amazing for his size. "Fantastic" was his exact word. When Bull Mask III removed his mask and became Randall Alder-Tangk again, the man's heart nearly skipped a beat when he realised he were only fourteen years-old. He said he'd pay another 50% of what he'd already offered. With accomodation provided for Randall and one supervising adult.

He told them he would wait for them in this exact spot 9 o'clock tomorrow morning to get their answer, as he was haeding back to North Carolina then.

The trio went home to discuss whether or not this was a good idea and/or opportunity for Randall.

They sayed up all through the night trying to figure why he shouldn't go to North Carolina with this man. Afterall, one of them would be with him. That was another decision.

9 o'clock the next morning, Randall walked up to this fancy man from North Carolina with Pedro carrying his bags. His father had decided it would be best if the man who had taught him this talent went with him, to teach him more and make sure that nothing bad happened to him, as he knew the business.

They were in North Carolina.

Randall dazzled the crowds as Bull Mask III. Sure, he didn't win all the time, but he was a true favourite in the hearts of most fans that had seen him. He was a fantastic spectacle and a big attraction for the promotion. Eventually, Bull Mask III began to speak on the mike and he had some talent talking on it. Sure, he was a little immature, but he had the bearings of someone who had twenty years experience around the ring. He was proving that he could make the successful tradition of the Bull Mask true.

It was 2002, Bull Mask III was becoming a household name around the North Carolina area. But there was always room for improvement. Randall came home from school one day to find a strange man waiting in the home he and Pedro stayed at.

The strange man's name was "Sugar" Steve McGowen. He was not only a wrestling trainer, but a manager and promoter. When Randall asked about Pedro, he had handed Randall a letter.

"Dear Morty,

You are my angel. My saviour. You have such a bright future and there's one thing that's stopping you from being the best. From getting onto televisions. And that anchor is me.

Anchor's away, Randall.

I've left you in good hands. Sugar has spoken with your parents on my approval. He wants to take you to the big time. Show you what I can't. Show you around sports entertainment and take you to places I never dreamed I could take you.

You can do it Randall. Do it for me.

I will pray for you my angel.

Pedro."

Randall looked up at Sugar and gloomily said...

"Bull Mask III is dead. Let Randall Alder-Tangk take the spotlight now."

And thus was born...

RAT!
