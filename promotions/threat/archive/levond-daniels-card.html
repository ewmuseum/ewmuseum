Levond Daniels

Real Name: Levond Daniels

Born: February 03, 1978 Place of Birth: Greensboro, North Carolina Hometown: Greensboro, North Carolina

Height: 6'3" Weight: 214 Blood type: Come Find Out Eye color: Brown

Trainer: Shawn Arrows

Theme song: "A Place for my Head" by Linkin Park

Style

Competing style: True Fighting

Signature moves:
HeadRush (Daniels charges at the opponent and catches them with a spear, driving his head into thier stomach, and instantly follows it up by pushing up on the opponents thighs, elevating himself enough that he can bring the backs of his heels crashing down into the back of his opponents head.)

5 Strike No Touch Combo (Daniels comes up off the ground first with a knee, just above the opponents groin. He plants the foot on the opponents leg as his other comes up with a knee to the gut. Daniels pushes the leg he was balancing on the opponents knee with up and catches the opponent with a knee to the face, followed by a forearm to the back of the neck, and then another knee to the face/elbow smash to the back of the head to knock the opponent out.)

Repeated Kicks to Throat (Usually done while opponent is on knees.)

Jumping Knee Strike, Ax Kick, Crescent Kick, Tornado Kick, Jumping Back Kick, Roundhouse Kick, Thrust Kick, Hook Kick, Baseball Slide Roundhouse Kick, Leg Sweep Kick, Step Kick, Straight Leg Jumping Side Kick, Spinning Backhand, Elbow Smash to Back of Head, Straight Punch to nose

Finisher: Reverse Tornado Kick Finisher name: Reverse Tornado Kick

Historical Info

Other/Previous promotions: Carolina Wrestling Federation, Epic, IOW

Other/Previous managers: Jessica Jenkins

Other titles won: IOW Television Title

Biography

Levond has never been one to tell very many people about his past. A lot of illegal doings seem to force him to protect his past from everyone, thus only the fighting career matters.

In which case, it all started with CWF. Levond was one of many scouted from Greensboro to become the next wave of superstars to hit the independants, but unfortunately, that company soon after went bankrupt.

When selling the company to try and make what little bit of money she could back off of it, Jessica Jenkins signed over the rights to the owner of Epic Wrestling, [name withheld]. Included in this were the actual wrestlers contracts, atleast part of them.

The group then went to Epic, travelling around the country putting on shows. They once again came in contact with their old trainer, Shawn Arrows. Daniels still remembered that night, oh so long ago.

A kick to Shawn's leg was all it took to take him down. His knee was still sore. It probably always would be. Daniels was just damn sure he was going to finish that someday.

Then Epic closed.

Levond Daniels went home and waited, eventually getting the telephone call he had been waiting on, and appeared in IOW as soon as he could.

This led Jessica Jenkins to follow him. Shortly after her arrival Christmas rolled around, and with it, a lot of changes.

Levond and Shawn found a new respect for each other. Shawn broke things off with his wife Samantha, to be with Jessica. Gary finally let everyone see the asshole he had buried inside.

In the end, Jessica became Levond's manager, trying to keep he and Shawn on good terms. That plan just brought Levond and Jessica a lot of trouble though, when Anubis reshowed his hideous mask.

Everyone thought he retired when Epic closed down, but after kidnapping Jessica, and then costing Levond the Television Title the next week, everyone was sure he was back for good.

Neither man was seen again though. Nor was Jessica.

Another few months have come and gone. Time to rest, to think, to prepare.

Levond finally realized it's time to Fight Back.
