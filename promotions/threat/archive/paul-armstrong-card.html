Style

Competing style: Technical Supremist

Signature moves:
Paul is a unique worker in many ways. He doesn't have signature moves, he has a wide range of techncial wrestling moves that usually taget some sort of limb. Through the match he will continue to target the limb untill applying one of his three finishing submissions. The three limbs of target are the knee, neck, and lower back area.
