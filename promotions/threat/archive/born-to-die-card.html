Bio: SinFyre and X-Ploder were born on the streets of China Town in NYC. X-Ploder, the older brother, became a member of the Born to Die street gang at a young age and do to his athletic ability and sheer arrogance and contempt for life, became one if its core members and chief amongst the enforcers in the group,even though his 6'1" size made him appear smaller and less of a threat to his opponents or to local business men. But if anyone crossed him, didn't pay up the extortion fees, or was part of a rival gang, they would quickly find out that X-Ploder was one very evil young individual with a sinister cunning mind. X-Ploder's younger brother SinFyre joined Born to Die because he looked up to his brother as a role model. Not as malicious as his older brother, or even as loyal to the gang, SinFyre has been practicing martial arts his entire life, from karate to kung-fu. But the hard life of gang membership has taken its toll on Sinfyre and jaded him, making him cynical toward the world and everyone in it. Both men have perfected the art of using fire, from arson to blowing fire, throwing fire, breathing fire, small explosives, and other various techniques they use in both their gang activities as well as in the ring. A recent gang war with the Shadow Dragons has decimated the Born to Die gang, and both SinFyre and X-Ploder have gone into hiding, staying way out of Chinatown as their territory is being split up by other gangs, the triads, and the tongs. Wrestling has been good to them as far as money, so they intent to continue their young and burgeoning careers, although once a gang banger, always a gang banger, and X-Ploder is looking forward to the day he can return to Chinatown and rub out the Shadow Dragons and reform Born to Die. With the help of the "Manager of Champions" Johnny O, they just might reach their goals, especially with Olympus' sudden movement in underground circles.
