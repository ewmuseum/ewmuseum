Underhanded Tactics

Troy Mason opened the door to his office and switched on the lights. Almost immediately, a scowl of godlike magnitude was seen on his face. Mason wasn't having a great week, despite his charges getting one over The Vagabonds last week. Storming into his office, Troy threw his briefcase & his coat onto this table, before he opened the fridge and grabbed a can of ginger ale.

It seemed that Vincent Pembridge had now gotten near-unaminous support of the thReat board of directors, and the Scorpion Of Manchester single-handedly booked the show, leaving Troy Mason with the chance to make only ONE match. One match; those words kept coming back to hound Mason.

He was boiling with rage, and the intensity in his bloodshot eyes showed it. The battle between himself & The Vagabonds was clearly taking its toll on him, but tonight, Troy wished to continue on the good mini-streak his charges were on. Having outplayed the team of fighters sans Vincent last week, it seemed a weak link had been established.

Ty Hughes wasn't the force he seemed to be. His recent feuds in tA must have left Hughes worse for wear, and now, Troy knew where the focus of his attack would lie. However, something in the back of his mind kept nagging him about the idea. Like a warning, or something.

And as Mason sat down in his chair, he realised just what that warning was. And how foolish he would have been, had he acted rashly and gone ahead with it in the morning, as intended. Troy breathed a sigh of relief, and placed the can of ginger ale down on a coaster on his table, before thinking.

If he had one match to make, what would it be? He himself, actually, didn't know what Vincent Pembridge had cooked up for the night but since he was the rightful owner, his decision would overrule over that of The Brit's. And seeing how Pembridge was busy fighting at the ACW Pay-Per-View at the moment, Mason had the chance to bring down the hammer on The Vagabonds. "Time to play the game, my friends."

And that is how, ladies & gentlemen, the main-event came to be 'The Dreamer' Darren Anthony versus Ampske. Wrestler versus fighter, in a Standard Wrestling Match. Ampske would be headlining for the second straight show, but Darren's vast experience would surely make him more of a viable & tougher challenger.

But Troy had more planned. Oh yes, he did. Keep an eye out for that, kiddies.

An Offer She Can't Refuse

Joshua Smith knew exactly what it was that he had to do tonight and no matter what it took he would get the job done.

There was no way that Geneva would be able to resist him tonight after he shows the world what he can do to an arrogant jackass like Tyler Davidson.

First things first though. He must first find a way to get to Geneva without her pitbull like companion and bodyguard, Marcus Escobar, picking up on it again and he had the perfect plan to do it.

A stake out.

Sure it might take him a while to accomplish but sooner or later Geneva would have to either leave the locker room on her own or she would be left in it by herself. And the moment she was he would be right there to keep her company.

It didn’t take quite as long as Joshua had expected though. He stood outside of The Faithless locker room for maybe five minutes before Geneva herself had walked around the corner and towards the locker room and most importantly she had walked towards the room without Marcus Escobar. Joshua saw it and he knew it was his opportunity to finally move in. He quickly cut right in front of Geneva as she stepped towards the door and flashed her, his biggest smile he could manage before a look of disgust could cross her face.

“Haven’t you learned your lesson yet?” Geneva asked as she tried unsuccessfully to get by Smith.

“I’m a slow learner,” Joshua responded. “Let’s get straight to the point now though baby when are me and you going to get together?”

“How about never?” Geneva quickly replied.

“I thought you might say that,” Joshua came back without missing a beat and keeping his smile going. “I got an offer for you that you certainly can’t refuse however.”

“And what would that be?” Geneva asked suddenly intrigued by the idea or at least pretending to be.

“I’m going to head out there tonight and teach Tyler Davidson a lesson,” Joshua answered. “You just sit back and watch me in action and I’m confident that you would be dying for the chance at some of this.”

“Is that so?” Geneva muttered with a grin on her face. “Get to work then.”

“You got it,” Joshua responded before he turned with one last grin and grabbed hold of Geneva before he planted a kiss right on her lips and very much against her will or so it seemed. Joshua pulled back still smiling before he turned and walked off leaving Geneva behind him with what seemed like a satisfied grin on her face as she watched Smith walk off.

The look quickly disappeared though as Geneva turned away from him and opened the door to the locker room and walked in. Before she was able to close the door inside of the locker room there was a shot of two very distinct figures.

One was Marcus Escobar as he sat on a bench in the room and was looking up at another man.

Tyler Davidson. Joshua Smith’s opponent later tonight. What could the two of them possibly have been talking about?

Standard Fighting Bout Tyler Davidson vs Joshua Smith

vs
“Lying From You” by Linkin Park began to play in the arena and the crowd who normally wouldn’t have cared for the man who was coming out began to cheer for the man that they all hoped would beat the hell out of Tyler Davidson tonight. As the fans continued to cheer him Joshua Smith walked out from the backstage area with a wide grin on his face. His earlier encounter in the night with Geneva had instilled him with quite a bit more confidence than he should rightfully have for his first match in thReat and his first ever fight inside of an octagon.

Smith wasted no time as he quickly made his way down the ramp and began to look around at the massive octagon which sat in place of the ring. He began to test out the walls from the outside before he opened the door and stepped right into a right hand from Tyler Davidson that knocked him right back out the door. Smith staggered backwards and away from the octagon as Tyler grabbed the cage door and slammed it shut apparently trying to keep Smith out of the octagon and himself safe from the possible abuse of a fight inside of the octagon. Smith grabbed onto the cage door from the outside and began to try and yank it open but each time Tyler pulled it right back shut intent on not letting Smith get into the octagon.

It was then when what obviously had been planned beforehand began to unfold.

As Smith tried to force himself into the octagon against the will of Tyler, Marcus Escobar came from nowhere right behind him and leveled him with a vicious clothesline that drove Smith’s head right into the steel mesh on the door. Escobar grabbed Smith by the back of his head and slammed his face right into the mesh again before he pulled his head back and began to pound him with right hands to the forehead. Tyler backed away from the door and was laughing the entire time as he began to watch what was happening on the outside. Escobar grabbed the door of the cage and opened it just enough so he could squeeze Smith’s head in the opening. Escobar yanked the door all the way open and then slammed it shut with all of his strength right across the head of Smith who quickly fell to the ground and grabbed his head in obvious pain as he rolled around.

Inside of the octagon Tyler had grabbed hold of a rope that was already tied into a noose. Escobar picked Smith back up and tossed him inside of the octagon right to Tyler who quickly began to tie the noose right around the neck of Smith. Escobar stayed on the outside as Tyler dragged Smith towards the wall of the octagon. Tyler stuck the loose end of the rope out through the octagon wall to be grabbed by Escobar and he yanked it tight as he pulled Smith right into the wall and began to choke him out. Smith struggled and tried to get his hands under the rope and free himself but the second he tried Tyler put a stop to it with a right hand straight to his head.

Tyler was still laughing at the entire thing as he backed off of Smith and then quickly lunged forward with a picture perfect superkick that he called The Assassination. Officials were trying to get Escobar to release his grip on the rope but he wouldn’t do it at all as Tyler continued his attack inside of the octagon. Tyler grabbed a chair up off the mat and as Smith continued to struggle the chair Tyler held was smashed right into the skull of Smith.

Smith’s head felt steel on both sides as the back of his head was knocked into the steel mesh of the octagon and the front of his head was damn near caved in by the steel of the chair. Tyler wound up again and drove a second chair shot right into the head of the now motionless Smith.

And again.

And again.

And a fifth time Tyler slammed the chair into Smith’s head as Escobar choked him out with the noose. Tyler backed off and threw the chair back down to the mat and just watched as the officials finally pried the rope out of Escobar’s hand and the motionless body of Smith collapsed to the mat. Tyler stood over him and just looked down with a satisfied grin on his face before he sat down next to Smith and applied the Future Shock submission move.

There would be no tapping out for Joshua Smith however and the match was quickly called as a knockout victory for Tyler as there was no movement of any sort coming from Smith.

Winner: Tyler Davidson via knockout


The Meeting

Troy Mason is a very busy man, and with his recent problems with the fighters coming forward he had a lot more to deal with in thReat. As he was a busy man, he couldn’t be bothered to put up with crap, the trouble was... he was just about to.

Troy sat in his office trying to think of his next step to take when there was a knock at the door. Before Troy could(or probably couldn’t in this case) invite the person in, the door swung open and in walked Phil Atken with the IOW Cruiserweight Title around his waist and the thReat Cruiserweight title slung over his shoulder. He didn’t look too happy, and neither did Troy for that matter.

“What the HELL do you want?” sneered Troy.

Phil let out a nervous laugh and gave a half-hearted smile.

“I want to talk business Troyey, I want to talk about bringing in the ratings, I want to talk about me. I am quite possibly the greatest addition to thReat ever and you let me get screwed last week!”

“Who are you and what are you talking about?”

“Who am I? Troy, look at this belt over my shoulder, I am YOUR Cruiserweight Champion!”

“Err, I’m look at that belt over your shoulder, and the one around your waist and they both belong to the same person. It isn’t you though.”

This statement didn’t sit very well with Phil.

“Listen here Troy” Phil said, trying to sound angry and failing. “I earned these damn titles but not once, but twice that disgrace to the females of this world screwed me out of them. So what did I do? I took what was rightfully mine.”

“And how were you screwed?”

It was obvious that Troy didn’t care, and was getting more & more frustrated with the conversation.

“Troy, do you really want your very first champion of cruiserweights to be a no good, cheating, bitch who needs to promise sex to men to win matches? It’s no wonder she’s earned the name Vertihoe.”

Sections of the crowd cheered, for some reason, as Troy sighed. He just wanted to be rid of Phil, and as quickly as possible.

“Alright, since you’re boring my ass off, and you’re a little bitch, I’m going to book a match for Malevolence and you’ll get your hands of Vertigo then. Until the rightful champion will remain the champion. Now if you’d kindly piss off, thanks.”

Phil left Troy’s office with a huge grin on his face and rubbed his hands together.

“The rightful champion eh? This is gonna be a fun night! Windmills!”

Injured

"Wake Up" by Rage Against The Machine began to blare over the speakers, which was the signal for the entrance of one Steve Solex, who got a taste of poetic justice last week. The war between Wolf & himself had been brewing for a while, but finally, after weeks of back & forth banter with Solex always seeming to get the last laugh... Wolf one-upped him last week. Now, with the challenge for Malevolence having been laid out, everybody wanted to see if Steve was man enough to accept it.

They were stunned, as they saw the Hunnington Beach local limp from the back and down the ramp, with the help of... crutches.

The timekeeper immediately bolted from his seat and rushed over to Solex, to try and assist him. Steve was pissed and humiliated at the use of the crutches, but he didn't object to being helped down the ramp. The jeers quietened down, as the timekeeper helped Steve Solex into the ring, and making sure that the longtime servant of thReat was alright, before returning to his position.

Solex looked out at the sea of humanity, who he never really cared for. Why let a bunch of people with conflicting opinions affect his professional career, he thought? Snorting, he pulled out a microphone from the back pocket of his jeans, managing to balance himself with the crutches.

It was a sad sight to see, but now, some of the smarter fans were wondering if his apparent injuries were true. He hadn't been attacked viciously by Wolf, but maybe, it was legitimate. Maybe there was something the fans didn't know.

"Last week, what you people didn't see was Wolf attacking me as I was being tended to due his earlier attack on the night. And he went ballistic on me, cursing like a madman. It's a sorry thing to see happen to a person who's obviously jealous and sick of getting outsmarted, but really, it's not my fault. That I'm brighter, stronger, and generally better. But I got too cocky.

Yes, I'm admitting it. I got too cocky. I thought I was untouchable.

And look at me now! Look at what that bastard did to me! He's almost crippled me, damn it! THE END OF MY EXISTENCE IS NEAR!!"

The crowd jeered, not appreciating the derogatory comments directed towards Wolfgang, not caring anymore about how pitiful Steve Solex looked with the crutches. Also, they had a suspicision that he was faking, although he genuinely did seem injured, and the vibes enemating from him psychologically seemed to back him up.

But still, everything seemed too fishy. Why was there no mention of this unaired attack, at all? Not by the thReat officials, not on the website, not by the paramedics, not by the other guys in the back; no word at all about this was breathed.

"So, Wolf. Regarding your little challenge you made the other day... fine. I'll accept it. I'll accept it, and train hard for it, because I want to put you away... once and for all. I want to end all this. But hey, wait a second. If we're going to do this, it's on my terms. After all, I'm the one crippled here. So, what I say is this.

30 minutes. Instead of the usual 60. And... Street Fight rules, baby. Weapons, ability to roam anywhere in the city during the fight, no disqualifications... just the two of us, raising some hell. That's about it, Wolf. I hope you're not too much of a pussy to agree to my terms, but if you DID cripple me like a coward, I guess anything's possible, heh.

And that's about it, Wolfie. I guess I'll see you at Malevolence."

Solex threw the microphone down and sneered at the crowd, attempting to get himself out of the ring now. But before he could even move from his spot, before the timekeeper could even get up & slide into the ring to assist Steve... the crowd's cheers indicated that Wolf had arrived.

And there he stood, on the stage, dressed to fight for some reason. Of course, the spectators had been kept in the dark with regards to the fights that would take place on the night. But that didn't seem to matter at the moment, for Wolf had a microphone in his hand... and a highly bemused look on his face, like as if he couldn't believe what had just transpired.

"Solex, you're a liar. Your injuries are freaking phoney but you know what? I'm not gonna argue with you about it. I've got my match with Marcus Escobar to prepare for, and I've only just learnt about it, heh.

All I wanna say is this; if you're gonna keep ducking me until Malevolence, fine. I can wait until then. And as long as I get my hands on you at the PPV, I don't care what kind of match it is. You can have it favour you to the ninth degree, or you can make it an Evening Gown Match. Really, I won't mind. All I wanna do is kick your sorry ass over & over again.

So, I guess I'll see you at Malevolence, you coward. Hope you heal quickly."

With that, and the news that Wolf would be battling Marcus Escobar later, Wolf turned around and returned to the back. The crowd celebrated jubilantly, at the fact that Steve Solex had just been dissed and out-verbalised. Steve just stood there, in the ring, unable to respond. The anger was building up inside of him, and was threatening to explode there and then... but a thin and creepy smile that broke out on his face had everyone confused.

And as the timekeeper climbed into the ring, attempting to help the veteran out of the ring, a couple of the observant fans got more confused. The limp that Solex had carried... seemed to have shifted.

From the right leg, to the left leg.

... Mysterious or just plain idiocy? You decide.

Darren Anthony was soon to find himself experiencing more pain, as Ampske quickly picked his opponent up and connected with a ferocious right hook, that sent The Dreamer tumbling out of the ring. Smiling, Ampske jumped through the ropes and landed with a double-footed stomp to the spine of Darren, causing the latter to holler out in pain. Early on, the battle was already getting heated.

Ampske slowly got up and picked up the struggling Darren to his feet and knocked him with a flying forearm. He went for another… and another… Nearing the steel steps, Darren knew he was in for big trouble if the vicious shots that he was receiving would go on. He saw Ampske lunge… but he ducked…

DROP TOE HOLD!

The tremendous thud of Ampske's face onto the steel steps was a horrific crack. The fans gasped in horror as they saw the steel steps flying, before they cheered, egging on Darren. Anthony snarled and looked at Ampske in anguish as he picked him up by his soft, flattened hair and began to pummel him onto the bottom steel steps…

Again… and again… and again.

Ampske, amazingly, was still not bleeding from these vicious strikes. The pain was truly immense… almost searing to the point of total unconsciousness… but he couldn't lose. There was no way in hell that could happen; that would be total disgrace for The Vagabonds.

Darren finally stopped his relentless assault and picked the limp body of Ampske off into the ring and followed. The fans were at fever pitch now, especially when Darren quickly went for the cover;

TWO...

THR...

The Dreamer then began an onslaught of elbow drops, finally relenting for a brief moment only to bounce off the ropes and into an unreal-esque springboard, only to moonsault back in dazzling fashion. He held on for the pin;

ONE...

TWO...

THR...

Tonight was his big chance to prove himself, and Anthony wasn't going to waste it. He picked up Ampskes' lagging body and whipped him to the ropes. Ampske returned sluggishly only to be hit with a terrific looking moonsault dropkick. He got up slowly and was hit once again, right on the button, with the same dazzling move. Darren had terrific steam now as he went over to Ampske and jumped over him, bouncing off the ropes and hitting a text book leg drop. He went for the cover;

ONE..

REVERSAL. WHAT? Yep, you read correctly.

Too sluggish with his pinfall attempt, Darren had too loosely held the legs of Ampske and was not suffering the consequences in a deadly armbar… to no avail for Ampske, Darren had been to close to the ropes and easily reached it with his left foot. However, Ampske could now feel his energy coming back… and that could only mean bad news for The Dreamer.

Ampske slowly got up with the help of the ropes and looked to the side to see Darren charging at him from the other side… Ampske ducked his opponent's intended clothesline, and immediately hit the bouncing and off balance wrestler to his back with a vicious springboard flying knee. Although Ampske was a pure pit-fighter, he used the ropes, which had never been available in the realm in which he fought in, to his advantage. Many a days, he would think about how much he could've humiliated his opponents by using the ropes for more leverage… this was his chance.

Ampske landed hard onto the mat on one foot and felt the electric pain once more. He went over to Darren and kicked him with his in-seam onto the shoulders, trying to weaken it for a crucifix armbar. He quickly fell to the mat on his knees and began to contort The Dreamer's body with an abdominal stretch. However, he could feel his grip slowly loosening… his back was an important strength in performing his many suplexes and submissions, and this one was no exception.

Darren sensed an opportunity… he slowly got up to his knees and reversed the submission with a snap mare takeover followed by his own neck wrench. Holding it for several seconds, he saw a bigger opportunity… he quickly jumped back up to his knees and jumped once more over the body of Ampske, whipping himself off the ropes, coming back and hitting a VILE dropkick straight onto the face. He laughed as he saw Ampske squirming in his pain… then he looked at the top rope…

He climbed up and screamed belatedly… the fans responded, cheering as loudly as they could. Darren jumped high into the night air and landed on Ampske full force with a tremendous elbow drop. He went for the cover;

ONE...

TWO...

THRE... Almost.

Darren was amazed, equally with the fans, with Ampske's tenacity. So was TROY MASON as he came down the ramp, sleeves rolled up, watching the match with every right to. He stopped at the bottom of the ramp, and nodded his head, urging Darren on. As Anthony picked up Ampske with malicious wishes, he first nailed Ampske with a straight jab and threw him to the ropes. Ampske ducked under The Dreamer's clothesline attempt and ran against the opposite ropes to come back with a spear that only happened to turn into a huge, flipping DDT.

Heh, wait. No.

Ampske flipped right onto his feet and maintained his posture as he heard The Dreamer's footsteps, amidst all these voices and nailed him in the back with a tremendous heel first straight kick. Darren stumbled to the ropes before turning around and seeing Ampske running at him with full force, knocking him over the top with a huge clothesline.

The fans rose in a huge collective show of disdain, not appreciating the brutality shown by the former pit fighter. Ampske grabbed his back in pain, but the adrenaline was overtaking it now… the pain was going away temporarily… especially with the sight of Ty Hughes & Bobby Knickerson jumping over the barricade, and joining the announce team in commentary.

Darren slowly rose to his feet only to see the feet of his opponent come sailing into him, but he moved out of the way just in time and pulled Ampske down onto the mat and then nail him straight in the back with a vicious fist drop. He picked up Ampske and hit him with stiff punch before grabbing Ampske by the arm and throwing him to the ropes. Ampske reversed the whip… but Darren now reversed with his own and gave a vicious knee shot to the gut and slammed Ampske with a flapjack right onto the barricade. Ty & Knickerson scowled in horror, seeing their team member fall down viciously onto his face once more.

The only thing Darren could do was laugh… and he did. He laughed while he was being cheered on by thousands of fans.

That's when Knickerson jumped out of his seat and rushed at Anthony, but the referee got in the way. However, as the referee stood between a raving Knickerson & a smug Darren Anthony, nobody except Troy Mason noticed Ty Hughes sneaking around the ring, and with a chair in hand.

*CRACK*

A vile chairshot to the back of The Dreamer's head, who slumped down to his knees and hit the ground face-first. Ty tossed the chair into the crowd and returned to his seat, just as Bobby Knickerson was being forced to return to the announce table. Troy Mason screamed in disgust, but he knew that's all he could do. If he even dared to walk over to the representatives of The Vagabonds, the owner of thReat would have been mauled.

And Vincent Pembridge would have been able to break out the champagne, once his business in the ACW concluded.

Ampske arose and grinned, delighted at the assist he'd gotten from the tA star, as he dragged Darren by the hair and tossed him inside the ring. He jumped onto the canvas before stepping up to the top rope, lurching like an eagle for his prey… The Dreamer slowly got up to the concern of the fans and turned around…

Amspke flew for the cross-body block…

Darren saw… and reversed…

The fans gasped in amazement that even Darren could come out of a reversal, much less believe how Ampske managed to even reverse a bridge pin into one with his legs pinning Darren's shoulders to the mat. More concerning was Ampske's back… Ampske grimaced with pain getting up and went towards the fallen body of The Dreamer, picking him up and nailing a big forearm shot, sending him stumbling backwards. Ampske pulled forwards, whipping The Dreamer across…

As Darren came back, Ampske extended his arms… as did The Dreamer… the two clashed together and fell onto the mat onto their tired and aching backs. The referee, after a while, had no choice but to start counting;

Ty Hughes shouted out instructions to his team member, while Knickerson jawed to a few fans who teased him about his two defeats earlier in the night...

THREE.

FOUR.

Troy Mason urged the crowd to make some noise, for the sake of Darren Anthony...

FIVE.

SIX.

SEVEN.

Darren had finally managed to get onto his feet to see Ampske still lying limp on the mat. He walked over still dazed from the fallaway slam and sluggishly picked up Ampske to his feet.

UPPERCUT PALM STRIKE. Ampske had played possum all along. The fans were furious as Ampske got up to his feet in stunning fashion with a kickflip and then went running towards the corner.

It was coming… a springboard knee attack.

'The Dreamer' Darren Anthony laid on the opposite turnbuckle, his chin in immense pain, his mind blurred from the outside world. Ampske slapped his knee and brushed against his mat with his right foot, much like a bronco and ran…

REVERSAL. Darren ducked just in time sending Ampske out of position and onto the ropes, now tied between his legs. Ampske howled in pain as he looked at Darren with vicious fury, but all The Dreamer could do once more was to smile. He made his way up to the adjacent turnbuckle and started to climb onto the ropes, walking across to Ampske howling in pride.

TURNBUCKLE HURRICARANNA.

Awww, what a miss.

On the outside, Troy Mason bit his lower lip, praying that Darren would be able to pull out the victory.

That Weekly Thing They Do

So, another edition Flaw is winding down. 17 may have seemed shorter than the previous editions of the show, but it was just as turbulent and emotionally-charged as ever. nØt and Christian Embers were planning on making the evening a living hell for Darren Anthony.

They slid into the ring, and quickly overpowered Darren Anthony. And with the 3 other members of The Vagabonds all itching to get their hands on the man they would face at Malevolence in the Fighters/Wrestlers Showdown... Darren Anthony was in a world of trouble. Troy Mason jumped out of the ring when he could, and watched as the first member of his Resistance was getting the shit getting kicked out of him.

But there was a back-up plan, as always. And this time, it was in the form of General David Sanders. He'd come out to the stage, wrists taped, ready to go into battle. Sanders himself knew, though, that if he went into the ring all by his lonesome... he was dead. He would have been mauled. His limbs would have been ripped apart. So, the General turned around, and pointed to the backstage.

"BRING OUT THE ARTILERY!"

And out they came. Not many of them, but enough to give The Vagabonds a run for their money.

Dane Manetta.

Vertigo.

The Faithless.

Wolf, although he was limping and had a bandage wrapped around his head.

Jason Semps.

Six members of Troy Mason's resistance, including Darren Anthony in the ring. With an almighty roar, General Sanders let the troops down the ramp, and into the ring. And almost immediately, in accordance with the increasing roars of approval from the crowd... the tide turned. The fighters were now getting overpowered, as Troy Mason shouted instruction.

Sanders & Wolf battled Ty Hughes. The Faithless were taking Embers on. Semps & Vertigo were beating Ampske down, while Manetta was kicking the shit out of nØt & Bobby Knickerson, as Darren Anthony slowly recovered and joined Manetta in the ass-kicking of his nemesis.

But then, a new twist in the chapter between The Vagabonds & The Resistance occured. This was New Orleans, after all. And on that very night, another promotion was having its extravaganza. One that had featured a certain British Degenerate. One who had engineered the takeover of thReat, and had actually successfully turned the company back to a hybrid entity. One who'd somehow gained the trust & support of the Board Of Directors, to allow him the power of booking the events however he wished to.

"Time" by Taproot.

Vincent Pembridge came sprinting out, with a chair in hand, and in one swift motion... he jumped into the ring, and cracked the chair over the head of Jason Semps, before hammering Vertigo. Slowly enough, the fighters began to regroup, with their insipirational leader back in the fray. Troy Mason watched from the outside, as the real preview between the two teams unfolded. It was evenly matched, as Vincent's troops battled his troops tooth and nail.

Of course, Vincent was proving what a fighter he really was... and Ty Hughes began to impose himself physically, fighting through whatever pain seared through his muscles. Embers & Ampske were co-ordinating well as a tandem, while Bobby Knickerson's kung-fu skills began to shine through. And with the help of nØt, it seemed the Vagabonds were slowly taking the lead in this feud.

And then, it was settled. The Scorpion Of Manchester & Ty Hughes began to take the wrestlers out. Darren Anthony, Wolf, General Sanders... the others followed suit.

As Flaw 17 came to a frenzied end, Troy Mason had his face buried in his hands. His Resistance had faltered in the face of overwhelming physicality in terms of the 6 men that represented his worst nightmare. They were standing tall, arms raised in the air, victorious in this round.

The showdown at Malevolence... would overshadow everything. And by then, either Troy Mason or Vincent Pembridge would have won.

Question was, who would be the biggest losers? Confused? Don't be.
