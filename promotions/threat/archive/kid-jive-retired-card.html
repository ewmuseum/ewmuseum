Biography

I was born in 1998, on the date of September 16 to be precise. I was called a birthday present for my mother. Her birthday was on the 17th, of course it wasn't on purpose you know. I mean, the day I was to be born, I think its pretty much random.

I think I turned out to be more of a White Elephant than a present to tell you all the truth. I was a hellacious kid. You know the type, not a ghetto boy, but the type of hellraiser you'd see on Leave it to Beaver or something. Except with more cussing. I'd steal shit, I'd lie like a bitch. And you know what... I don't think I ever cared. I stole from my friends for god's sake.

Hell, by the time I went to junior high, I was smoking. But I was a superstar. I was a 13 year old superstar. We had one of those traditional square trampolines. That bitch was about the size of a typical wrestling ring, and you can guess what we used it for. Thats right, wrestling. I was the youngest. My oldest brother Chris made sure we did everything right. We being me, Kid Jive, my brother Mel, The Bleak... he had a disease. And my brother Xris, he was... well, we couldn't come up with anything better than what ma and da chose for him at his birth.

I was a risktaker. I didn't care, I would backflip off the trampoline.. and when we got that pool... man it took us hours, but we never swam in that pool again after the first week. We lifted the trampoline and dumped it into the pool. It was a huge pool. Like, every five years ma and da took all their savings and got somethin' cool. That pool, even with a huge square trampoline in it, we had a good ten feet between the edge of the trampoline and the side of the pool. We'd leap off. We'd backflip off it.

And thats what taught me caution. My brother Mel was on the outside (ie-in the drink) and I thought to try a new move I'd been messing with. A moonsault bodyblock is what the mexicans call it now, damn mexicans. I called it the Backflip of Pain. Now that was a cool name man. Mexicans ruin everything. They're about as useful as blacks...

Well, now their telling me not to take that road... damn, oh well... I really should get talking about how I learned caution. I learned it when I jumped too far, I damn near cleared the pool edge, 10+ feet, but my chin slammed it. I landed on my feet and took two feet backwards... and then it all went black.

I woke up in a fuckin' hospital. I didn't want to be there, but hell, my parents made me go. Turned out, there were wires in my jaw. It was so fuck.. no way man.

NO WAY.

Aww man, these bi...znitches, they just told me to tone down the swearing. I guess I can do that, but they said they wanted "me" in this thing. And ... the swearing thing is really limiting me ... Well ...

Anyway... I woke up the next day. I wanted to fight some more. A few friends were coming over for a big match for the inter-Universal title. And it was me against Sam... aka Samuel B. Igjohnson. I'm serious, that was his nickname. We were 13 years old, you know the names are gonna be like that. The only reason my brothers and I were so normal was my oldest brother, Chris was 18. He was pretty mature. He knew a good name, and said these names would get us jobs in the pros if we wanted them.

Anyway, I suppose I keep getting off subject, probably the reason would be my stay in the CWL and the CWL Minor leagues. Devastating time for me. My high school years weren't really huge. And I really didn't do anything through college. I played ball for Louisianna State. We won the nats two outta my two years there. Two outta the two I was playing varsity. I was a second baseman. I didn't have the arm to play short or third, but damn I was good at second. I was a low order batter, but not because I sucked. That was because I hit better there. I batted .487 my senior year, and had a fielding percentage of .997. I led the NCAA in hitting that year, and was second in fielding. I ran pretty well too. I had 37 stolen bases. Fourth in the NCAA there too. I was the total package they said.

Five tool players are hard to come by. I wasn't one of them. I was only three tools. I didn't have the arm. I could hit, run, field, and all... but I didn't have the power or the arm to be a five or even four star player. I was drafted low, but I was conceited.

I thought only a top spot would be good enough for me, so I went and wrestled for a year, going to various showings and tryouts.. never accepting a contract. I was waiting for the next draft. With all these tryouts, I couldn't lose!

I lost. I couldn't believe it, I was drafted in the 27th round. That was down a whole 21 rounds from the year before! 27th round! I couldn't believe it, I was devastated. I said to the team that drafted me, I said to the Cardinals, SCREW YOU. I was 20 then.

Now I am 22, that seemed so recent, the hurt of being drafted so low. It humbled me. I had learned caution, and I had been humbled. My matches on the independant circuit tripled in quality after that. I was suddenly willing to learn to take a hit, to take a major bump. I was willing to lose the matches occasionally.

People took notice. The World Wrestling League took notice. My brother Xris was wrestling with them, he pointed JaKob Kruise in my direction. I signed, and wrestled on the Tag Scene. I wrestled a bit part as Cyril. The name was shitty awful. Suddenly, after losing his first shot at the Television Title, Xris stole a gimmick. A little gimmick infringement, and looked into his past. He took the name The Bleak. JaKob encouraged me to find a new name, and for the next few months they called me The Light. My finisher? Seeing. It was awful. Absolutely brutal! I left, and so did Xris. He was taking impossible bumps. He took a forty foot bump after the Dudleys hung him with flaming barbwire from the top of "The Big TV." He was out, I was gone.

I went back to the minors. I signed with the Championship Wrestling League. Not the big new thing CWL, but the CWL. They had three minor leagues, much like the WWF's current minor league. I wrestled in No Limit Wrestling. I went 13-1 there, that is until I was forced to job away the World Title upon leaving. They didn't want a vacant title. Big deal, I didn't care. I was in the big time. I hit the CWL like a Hurricane. I won the CWL Cruiserweight Championship on my first appearance. I took second in the Ringmaster Battle Royale to Justin Style. I could have won it, but I got too cocky.

I stayed for only three weeks however, I had problems with the management, and they were forced to drop me. They vacated the belt. They embaressed me with a lookalike. It was absolutely awful in my opinion. It was brutal. It was skanky. It was something... I can't say because the producers of this show won't let me.

You want to know what they did the week before they released me and put out the lookalike? They sent a hitman after me during a match! The sonofa..shut up, I'll call him what I want! He fucked my career up! That sonofabitch, he slammed me with a lead pipe! For the next six months, up until this past August I had recurring concussions. Honestly, one of these days I'll find out who did it. I narrowed it down to three men. Justin Style, Gillesp, or Adam Reeves.. one of them... I'll find out, and they will be deader than dead. They will be gone. They won't know the meaning of the word hello anymore. They'll be damn vegetables.

And you know what. When I came back, I showed the world that I could still entertain, sure only three episodes were aired... but that was because I made a comeback! I aired The Jive By! I beat that damn Claude Mitchell in the ratings three weeks in a row! But I made a comeback, my brother Xris made me do it.

He dragged me back into Pure Talent Wrestling. I wallowed around there for two months. I fought a few meaningless matches. I beat them, then I let them win on occasion. I didn't care actually. It was boring to me.

I nearly won the International Title... but my own brother pinned me! I didn't think he would do it.. but he did. And then he left the place upon losing. He said Monte Burns had cheated. Ha! I don't think so. Xris was a very sore loser.

But, it all worked out for him. He has a big money contract with j O l t. The lucky bastard. Didn't see that word coming did you? Heh, didn't even have time to bleep it out.

Anyway, I was tired of wrestling. I went home, richer than hell, determined to enjoy retirement. But I couldn't. I was tired of wrestling, but I knew there could be more. I knew there had to be a way to test my body further. I found that way. A friend told me about it.

He told me about an Asylum. I entered into the Asylum in February of 2001 and immediately began to show what I could do. I was there to prove to the world that I was a true fighter. I went out and ended up with the Television Title in under a month. I was forced out due to what has become a reoccuring theme in my career. Trouble with authorities. Joe Campbell is not the type of guy you mess with. I tried to take him on backstage, and I paid with my job.

I've been without a job, looking for a place to wrestle since April of 2001. Its been over a year now since I last cut a promo with the Asylum. And now I'm low on funds.

I've been drinking.

A lot.

Want to sign me?

Enjoy the ride.
