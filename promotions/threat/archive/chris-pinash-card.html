Biography

Chris was born in 1981, January 2nd. Born into a happily married couple, life was destined to be the average normal stuff, followed by the customary death, and eventually realising just what faith was right.

Unfortunately, people didn't quite take to Chris as easily at he'd hoped and by 12 he was a social outcast. He wasn't weird, he was just unwanted. So at 13 he declared into himself that he was a rebel acainst social order. He went straight into action, spray-painting the streets, boarding through the mall, hitting on girls that were obviously going to reject him - but copping a feel whilst he could. This - to him at least - was rebellion at that stage.

But, like so many other rebels who seem to make themselves more likable through being an asshole - he pissed off the local large dairy products [or big cheese's]. What ensued were a number of muggings, random attacks, property damage... basically he got himself wiped out - the big guns of society made him look like an idiot, and a weak one at that.

Determined not to give up what little status he'd gained from his rebellion, he started taking lessons in various fighting techniques. This time ready to take on the kids headlong, with a newfound secret weapon. As you can imagine, there aren't many variations of fighting compatible with a skinny 13 year old kid that's spent his life getting beat up and bullied. All he could learn effectively was Karate and kick-boxing. But what he learned - he learned well, and by the age of 14 was already fast on his way to taunting his jumbo-sized milk-products a second time.

Later in the year, near his 15th birthday, it started again. His house was de-windowed as the social foe's started brick each pane one-by-one while the family was out. When Chris made it home - he went on a one man revenge mission.

Using his newly aquired combat techniques, Chris started taking a measure of revenge on each and every one of his 'oppresors'.

Of course, beating on the social king - makes you king for a day in these sort of stories. But determined not to comprimise his views - he settled for a middle-of-the-road social status, but to those who always wanted to do that, and never had the guts... he's an Icon.

After conquering the world of social ladders, Chris realised he had TOTALLY forgot about passing school exams and so on. So he put his talents in fighting to good use. at age 17 Chris set out into the wrestling world - of course he started at the lowest of the low, but that's only to be expected. What he also thought he could expect - was fair treatment, but yet again Chris found himself in another social ladder of sorts. The rebellion started again, and Chris found himself getting noticed before too long with it. He made a name for himself, and moved on, and he's been doing the same ever since.
