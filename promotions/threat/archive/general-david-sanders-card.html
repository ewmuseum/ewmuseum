Biography

The History of David Sanders. David’s dad was taken away one mysterious night by four men. As David has been told, none of them spoke English. Police Officers and Crime Scene Investigators said it was somehow linked to World War II. A few days after searching for clues, David’s mother found out she was in the very beginning stages of pregnancy. When she got back from the hospital, there were four members of the United States army at her door. David’s father, Major Matthew Sanders, was announced a Prisoner of War.

They got enough evidence and tipoffs from suspects and ended with the conclusion that his father was taken to Japan. This story was taken lightly because the US Government did not want an uprising in the American people against the Japanese US citizens. After all, the peace treaty was made just a few decades ago from the terrible bloody war. It was noted that the people that captured the Major was a Japanese Cult. However, no one does know how they managed to find the Major. It was assumed that someone told them the information on where he was located, because earlier in the week, a coast guard sited a boat coming in from the waters, but didn’t reach it in time to see what was going on.

Nine grueling months later, David Sanders was born. Still, however, no sign of his father. The only relationship he had with his father was through past pictures. David was specially schooled, because he was no normal child. He had something in his head that just wasn’t “right.” Doctors said it could be because he had no father. His mother never did remarry. But anyway, David was specially schooled. His house and other belongings were paid for by the government. They said they were still in deep search for Major Matthew, but David and his mother knew they stopped looking.

David was now 21. He never attended college. Instead, he lived in his mother’s basement doing nothing. The only thing he did was work at a fast food restaurant getting paid minimum wage. One thing that he did extremely excel in was the History of WWII. He lots and lots about it. He lived in Arlington, Virginia, near the Arlington Memorial gravesite. In 1992, when David was 22, his father was announced dead. There was no real proof to this, but they had told David and his mother he was a goner. He had a casket buried in Arlington. Inside, David and his mother put pictures and other objects of remembrance in there.

From this point on, David lost it. He had so many thoughts in his mind, so much anger. He went crazy. He thought that everyone was out to get him. His father was taken from WWII almost over 30 years after it had ended. He knew now that anything could happen. He did join the army for five years, but it wasn’t what he wanted to do. He wanted fighting. All they did was train. He wanted real combat, no test dummies or drills.

He was practically insane now. His mother was getting old, and he had nothing in life. His mother died in ’99. He now had full possession of the house. He had no life experience whatsoever. Three years later, the government stopped supplying the money that was being sent for the past three decades. They argued a good case. David was 32. He needed some money. A job. Anything... And he needed it quickly.
