One Down

The arena darkened, thrown into a night that could only mean one of many possibilities. The murmuring crowd scanned every inch of the arena, excited. This was Sunday Night Flaw 19, and not the opening they quite expected. Already, they were shocked with the words of Ty Hughes that opened up the show and wanted more. They watched the stage and the tron, the ring and the barricade, waiting for the right moment when the spotlight would come to life and reveal the mystery before their eyes.

But the light wouldn’t come to life just then.

Instead, “Angel” as performed by the band Massive Attack started at the level of a whisper, and the mist started to rise from the chasms of the ramp. The blood-colored radiance resurfaced, and the lights slowly descended on the stage.

The figure, large and standing tall, emerged through the mist, and the fans cheered lightly for the man that had defeated Malik Roland on that legendary night, Malevolence. Sure, it was by no means the highlight of the evening, but it was a moment worth mentioning and one that the fans would remember as his debut match.

They would never know his name though. And all they had to call him was the one word he used to describe existence.

blind

He looked over the audience, most on their feet and cheering for him, others just clapping, and still others that booed. He stared through all of them, and realized the truth that none of them would ever comprehend.

Unless he forced them to see. The first step had been taken. There were many more to go.

blind stepped down the ramp, the same blank expression on his mouth and the sunglasses shining the red of blood. Most wrestlers would pay attention to the fans other either side of them as they walked down this path. Some enjoyed this, the fans being the only reason they wrestled anymore. Just to feel their cheering on their back. It was their drug.

He figured that he was there for the fans as well, ironically. But the reason for his presence was far more specific and derisive then the others. They were there to suck the respect out of them. He was there to make sure they would see the sins that their lives were.

Malik had seen first hand.

And now there was only the question of who would be next.

blind stepped into the ring, his music dying down and the lights returning. He grabbed his microphone, and set the promo into motion. He was about to say something, but his eyes looked up and he shook his head.

“That fucker didn’t think that I would win, did he?”

Tremendous cheer, with almost every person and his brother with his arms in the air and his voice joining the roar.

“He thought that I was going to sit by and let him rape me over. That I would let him beat me until I begged him to stop. That I would lose, without a shadow of a doubt, and be forced to apologize for what I did to him so many weeks ago.

“That’s what made him blind. That’s what makes them all blind.”

Cheer, with very little jeer.

“He thought he was perfect. I showed him that he is the embodiment of nothing.

“One down. The rest of humanity to go.”

This time, rather then the somewhat continuous cheer, the crowd gave a mixed reaction. Now, even after a month’s time, they still didn’t know what to make of this man. This being that seemed to hate all who stepped before him, for whatever reason they were these so-called sinners. Little did they know that blind held dark secrets, deep down in the pit of his soul, that they would never understand.

He raised the mic up once again, silencing the crowd and bringing a million eyes on him.

“He is only one of many. One of many that must be taken care of before the stench is too unbearable. I made sure that he got what was coming to him. And now all he can do is sit and rot for what he did. His crimes have been punished. Malik is now sulking in his miserable existence.

“Just like I will ensure the rest will.

“Reality is coming to take you all sinners to your graves. You simpletons and your fancy dreams, you can’t see anything, can you? Life, the complex bitch that she is, is nothing to any of you pieces of trash. You just go about, pushing life away and taking the money and the wealth and the charms instead.

“What you need, my fellow beings, is an eye opener. Something to tie you down and rip open those sockets. Something that will force all who sit and ignore to watch the screen and make you cry tears of blood for what you will realize. Something that will choke you with its taste of reality and give you the chance to repent for all that you have done. Something to open your fucking eyes, or someone if you know what I’m getting at.

“The time has come, fuckers. The world is about to bear witness to a lesson it never wanted to acknowledge or endorse. A man will stand over all of you like a king stands over his empire and proclaim the word of the new god.

“The revelation to end all revelations.”

Now the fans didn’t know what the hell to think.

“Malik Roland was only a hors d'oeuvre. The main course is going to be so much darker. So much realer. So much of a realization that many of you will die just trying to make the journey and many of you won’t accept the truth.

Prepare for what you are about to see. Because, ladies and gentlemen, boys and girls, you will realize that one and only fact.

“Beyond lies innocence, not death – the lie that blinds you all.”

Massive Attack hit, and that was it. blind, standing in the ring without a smile or a frown, dropped the mic with an echoing thud, and stepped through to the apron. The arena, more confused the anything, participated in their mix of taunts and applause, more of the latter.

blind gave no sign of caring. No sign of callousness. No sign of fear or unawareness or irritation or anger. He only stepped up the ramp, pushing through some of the mist that remained from his entrance and bearing the weight of his words.

blind had given his speech, and now he was on to the next step of his mission. Whatever that may be.

He's gotten those three things, but with added extras all around that make all of it seem worth nothing. The constant attempts to crush his self-esteem have plagued him, and he's at his boiling point.

Now, it had been revealed that all but Ampske & Vincent were spared from having to wrestle in thReat. the cryptic beginning - 3

Jere Casarella placed his bag down on the floor of the locker-room he had been assigned and smiled. thReat, his new home. And on this night, a match to ease him into the new line of work he'd embarked on awaited. Since Malevolence, Jere had been waiting anxiously for this night. The moment where he'd venture into something so unknown, yet so familiar in some sense.

Originally, June 1 2003 was supposed to have been the big day. But since the thReat Board Of Directors saw it fit to postpone Flaw 19 for a week, Jere had been driven to the point of almost tearing his hair out. He was officially obsessed over this new job of his. Why? That might be the question some people might be asking.

The answer? Nobody knows, for sure. One can only offer theories.

Maybe he's simply trying to move on. The life he's lived might not be the life he wants anymore. Casarella might want the past to be buried, and any recollection of it to be burnt. Maybe that's why he has that enigmatic nickname, which basically serves as a reminder of days gone by for Jere.

Anjo Atormentando. Tortured angel.

Now, that opens up a whole new can of worms, doesn't it? Yeap, it sure does. But flash-forward to the present.

Jere Casarella was on the verge of kicking off his wrestling career. And to do it against an established competitor such as Tyler Davidson? It made Jere a wee bit doubtful. The entire notion of wrestling was making him very conflicted, but suddenly, as Jere opened up his bag and pulled out his boots, the man stopped caring.

The fire in his eyes was extinguished, and only pure focus could now be seen.

Casarella had brawled with men with more malicious outlooks on life. Wrestling was filled with men who were, in his mind, acting to put forth a creative and entertaining show. Jere wasn't going to do that. He was going to be one of the few who had now broken away from the point of entertaining.

And doing this gig for their own selfish needs. But if you knew Jere well, you would know that his needs aren't selfish.

The man just wants to live.

Your Newest Nightmare

Malevolence had come and gone.

There were the winners, losers, and then of course there were the Leslie Voss’s. The fate of the company was not set and the new blood was ready to wreak havoc upon sole owner, Troy Mason. He had already signed multiple new names but who would stand out amongst them? Who had the spark that would catch Mason’s eye and make a name for themselves?

For this, you need know only one word: Candor.

As we opened our scene backstage, things looked fairly bland, by all accounts. People associated with thReat in one way or another passed throughout this area for days leading up to any edition of Flaw. However, this crowd held a man who is more significant than most of these Joe Nobody’s. The man who’d won the semi-main event of Malevolence, THE General David Sanders. He was passing through the crowd.

As he passed a certain man, carrying a generic manila folder, the hustle and bustle of the mass of people forced Sanders into the young worker, bringing the folder to the floor. The African-American worker quickly was mumbling apologies and bent over.

The General was in a rush and apologized quickly, but had to keep moving.

“Don’t do it.” The voice came from somewhere along the gray wall of the arena’s interior. It came, specifically, from a tall and well-built man, also African-looking in descent, but with a darker skin tone. The young worker was unsure as to whether this man was talking to him and he pointed to his chest, which brought a nod from this mysterious man. Sanders’ attention was now focused on whoever this was that chose to make a spectacle, while he turned his head.

“And you are?” Sanders asked, his curiosity had become piqued by this man who had so rudely brought himself into this two-person affair.

“My name is not of consequence to a lower being, a parasite to this world…such as yourself,” was the venomous reply that came from this man, still leaning against the wall and looking at the worker, who seemed about to piss himself in fear. “Your disrespect of this fine young man is what really boils my blood Mr…?” His voice trailed off as if he was unsure of the General’s name.

“Sanders, but to you I’m the General, since you seem to have such an issue with manners.” This brought simply a sneer from the man who had referred to himself as Candor. Ignoring Sanders, ‘Candor’ gave his directions to the young worker. “Leave those papers to Mr. General here. He’s not up to your level… he doesn’t deserve to have someone else clean up his mess.” The voice of this man was as snotty as snotty got, but the worker disobeyed him, handing the papers, with a shaking hand to Sanders. The General shook the worker’s hand vigorously, giving him a smile as Candor spat after him, “Impurity…”

“So...” The General stumbled on his name, “Buddy? What exactly is the issue that you have? Perhaps you didn’t see what happened to Voss at Malevolence?” Candor let Sanders feel the uncomfortable tension for a moment, then answered Sanders with the same sneering tone he’d been using thus far.

“Troy Mason signed my contract and that made me an employee of this... place. As I’m a superior entering a land of those who should be my minions, I expect you to know me. Rather, I’m expecting to be known very quickly as a man to be taken quite seriously.” However, while Candor was speaking, he didn’t notice Sanders mocking him by moving his hands in the gesture that usually means someone can’t shut up.

“I see you are a comedian, General. Perhaps you would like to show me your comedic skills in the ring later tonight?” This brought a small chuckle to the General’s face, and he responded with a tone that seemed light, strangely disattached from the tone of the conversation.

“Kid, sorry, but I’m not interested in being your ladder to stardom. I’ve worked hard in this company to get where I’m at, and I don’t have time to deal with the likes of YOU – Especially tonight.” Sanders then went to turn and leave but the voice of Candor beckoned him back.

“I fear that turning and leaving may be a very large mistake. I did not wish to preach Olorun’s gospel quite so quickly, but if I must, then I must. General, you and all those who are not direct descendants of the first man…no, not your Christian Adam, but the REAL first man, evolved to a high state of being... you and all of those impurities within these country that you represent, you are nothing but specks. The brilliant puzzle which Olorun has created will destroy those billions of anomalies that you represent. And then…we will have the perfect world, which He designed. Then…we will live in paradise.”

Of course, Sanders looked at Candor as if he was insane, which he very well might be.

“Okay, then. I’m gonna go turn and walk away now, and you can... well... go cry to whoever wants to hear your story. You’re not getting a match or anymore of my time.”

With that, GDS left, but Candor stood his ground, glaring after the man who represents American ideals. “David... that is a grave mistake. I may be new around this place, but this only makes me your newest nightmare… just as terrifying as any.”

That brought the sneer of Candor into the camera’s view, right before we faded out.

Definitely A Bad Decision

The Vagabonds. Total failures. thReat was now a pure wrestling promotion, but that didn't seem to effect most of the group. Most were wrestlers to begin with, and would prove to be just fine without the fighting.

One of the newer men on the roster however, wouldn't have it so easily. He hated wrestling, and had declared war on the so called sport since day one. Everything about wrestling made him sick, and now he was trapped in another wrestling promotion.

Atleast he had a backup plan.

Tonight no one was sure, but they would soon be let in on where he stood.

Levond slowly made his way down the ramp, glancing around the arena at all the fans as if he was searching for a certain someone. He slid into the ring as soon as he got to it and popped quickly back up to his feet, absorbing the mixed reaction from the crowd with a small on his face.

"You've all been wondering just where my motives are tonight, right? We all know, at Malevolence, I tried to join the Vagabonds, and aid them in turning thReat into a full fighting promotion. We all also know I was rejected. Dumbest mistake Vicnent Pembridge ever made. Now look at this company. Wrestling? Wrestling fucking sucks. Anyways, back to what I was saying. What you don't know however..."

Levond paused as the fans went silent. What he said next would easily decide where he stood with the fans.

"What you don't know, is that the only reason I tried, is because in order to win the war thats about to begin against a masked freak named Anubis, I have to be able to fight him."

The crowd finally united in a round of cheers for Daniels as he let his motives be known.

"So far he hasn't accepted the invitation to show up here, but rumor has it he's here tonight. Now Anubis, if the rumors are true, and you're stumbling around back there, I say we do this tonight, and you let Jessica go after I win."

Levond wouldn't have to wait long. The lights in the arena dimmed, then went to a complete black. Following the darkness was a golden light, shining down atop the entrance ramp. A few seconds later, the light was occupied, but not by one person as expected. By two.

One was a mammoth man, overly built and wearing a plastic mask shaped like a dog's head. The other was a teenage girl. Her golden red hair was more than noticeable in the light, as was the silver collar and leash, and the silver leather outfit she was forced to wear.

When the lights came back to normal, everyone who hadn't figured it out, did so quickly.

Anubis...

and Jessica Jenkins.

Anubis carried a microphone in his free hand, holding onto the leash tight with the other, and brought the microphone up to the mouth of his mask, glancing over at Jessica.

"You. Tell."

He pushed the microphone into Jessica's hands and turned to stare back at Levond, jerking forward on the leash. Jessica kept her eyes locked on Levond as well as she brought the microphone up.

"He. He says that if you really want to fight him for my freedom, it can't be just for that. You have to find something to put on the line as well. The most important thing in the world to you Levond."

Jessica paused for a second, taking a deep breathe before continuing.

"He wants you to let him know, next week."

With the message delivered, Anubis backtracked into the backstage area, taking Jessica with him with a jerk on the leash. Levond just stayed in the ring, staring up at the top of the ramp with obviously no clue what he was going to do now to get this match.

It was definitely a bad decision to go down for Levond to resort to this. Too late now.

Wrong Answer

Troy Mason slapped his right thigh in delight, and squealed like a little girl. No longer was he worried and an emotional wreck. He was actually happy, for the first time in two weeks. Quite surprising, considering the problems that were still on his plate, but he had every right to be pleased.

His decision to push the Triple-Threat Tag-Team Match to the main-event slot had paid dividends, judging by the fan reaction. And the other matches on the show turned out to be pretty good as well. Not to mention the plethora of new stars that made their voices heard, and the various developments that unfolded.

Along with that, however, came a few more issues. Like the snapping of Darren Anthony, and yet another injury befalling nØt. But putting aside that, there was another reason for Mason to be as giddy as a schoolgirl who'd just bought new grandma-style panties.

He'd been expecting big bad Xaxon to show up on the night, and kick his ass, but it hadn't happened. All his worrying was a waste of time. General Sanders was right, Troy had to focus on the positive. Look on the bright side of life. Like that annoying song that many people seem to life, for whatever reason.

Troy got up from his seat and started whistling, a new spring in his step. He'd faced stress before, and overcame it. This new wave of problems was another obstacle he would overcome. Much like Vincent Pembridge. Sure, in the end, it looks as if The Scorpion of Manchester has triumphed. But the fact that thReat was free from his stranglehold was the important thing. Nobody thought it was possible.

Jon Hackett's dream was still alive.

Walking to the front of his desk, Mason began to shake his booty. The ratings for Flaw 19 was set to be the highest ever, for the show. And the fact that the team division was flourishing made things all the more worthwhile. It demanded a celebration of some sort.

"I'm doing a little jig, cuz I'm so happy... the ratings for this show, are gonna shock ol' Uncle Vinnie!"

Just then, the door to the office was kicked open. And in stormed Xaxon, who looked generally pissed off and drenched. It seems the rain had some part in him arriving so late to the Tio Pepe Hall, but finally, he was there. And with Troy consumed in his nonsensical singing, Xaxon took advantage.

Punch to the face.

"OWWWWW!"

Troy fell to the ground, but Xaxon pulled him back up, and smashed the owner's face against his own desk.

Twice.

Thrice.

Four times.

Five times.

Six times.

Seven times.

Help, I'm losing count.

Finally, Xaxon threw Troy Mason back down onto the floor, as blood covered the man's face. He was whimpering pathetically, as well, trying to find the words to beg for mercy. There wasn't about to be any dished out by Xaxon, as he stomped Troy's face, before kneeling down and bringing his face as close as possible to the owner's.

"My title. 'Vhere iz it?"

Troy blinked. He had to find the right answer, he knew that.

"I-I... d-don't k-k-know! For r-real! I s-seriously don't know!"

Xaxon smiled. He knew it was a lie. He could smell the fear on Troy's breath, and the lie he was being fed. The rebirth; it made the man nicknamed Azre become a little more astute. He had the innate ability to judge who was being nice, and who was being naughty.

"Wrong answer."

So, expectedly, it was lights out for Troy. Just as Flaw 19 officially came to an end. However, that's not where the story ended. See, while everybody filed out of the Tio Pepe Hall, someone happened to stumble into Troy's office by accident. And seeing the state it was in, a call to the police was put forth.

Almost an hour after Flaw 19 went off the air, police arrived on the scene. Storming into the office of Troy Mason, they were stunned to see blood on the walls, with the entire office trashed. One other thing was pretty much noticeable. It really couldn't be missed. The most critical piece of the puzzle, fellas.

...

Troy Mason was... nowhere in sight.
