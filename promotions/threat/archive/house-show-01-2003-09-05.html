thReat House Show 01

Date: 05 September 2003
Location: Antonio's Sundry Warehouse, San Diego, CA

The Story So Far
thReat was officially on suspension-mode, but in a meeting with the roster a few days after Double Or Nothing, Troy Mason swore that nothing would be able to keep thReat down. Money problems are the least of his concerns; the roster was in a horrid shape, and the danger posed by Vagabonds v2 was getting fearsome. Many meetings between Troy & the Board Of Directors ensued, and finally, it appeared as if Mason was reinstated as the Owner of thReat, and was allowed to hold THREE House Shows while the legal issues were sorted out. This basically meant thReat was given a lifeline, even though it wasn't much. Secretly, though, Troy Mason had to ensure that the House Shows worked. Or else, by the end of September, the Board Of Director would liquidify their shares, and thReat would be dead. Forever.

--

The warehouse had been abandoned for two years, but still kept by Antonio -- a friend of Troy Mason's college friend -- for use in his new line of work; filming of pornographic movies. On this day, Antonio had no intention to do any filming since his entire crew demanded higher pay. Good for thReat. Although, the conditions were pretty horrid, with the musty smell and the slight mould infestation. Money problems had hit thReat hard recently, thanks to Commissioner Colin Gear. So, our audio recording was pretty poor and our video equipment was bought second-hand. Hell, we didn't even bother setting up a video wall. But, this was the best we could do, and Troy Mason informed the fans -- all 450 of them -- that thReat was in a bad state. Down, but not yet out. Not quite. He went on to stress that thReat would be making a comeback soon enough, no matter how hard it would prove to be, and he expressed his hope that the fans stay loyal to the cause. Mentions of many meetings with the Board Of Directors were made, before he once again remarked that it wouldn't be long before thReat was back on its feet. It had to be noted, however, that security was at an all-time high as Troy returned to the back.

--

thReat Aggression Title Match: Filthy XIX vs Ernie French [c]
Ernie French had dethroned Wolf at the Double Or Nothing PPV, and that just made him more popular with the fans. Filthy XIX was a newcomer to the company, and having been mentioned in the programme sheet sure did help him. Instantly, he was a hit with the fans. Why? Well, probably because he was described as a street-fighting hobo looking to rise to superstardom. This match started off at breakneck pace, as both Ernie & Filthy dazzled each other with hard-hitting power moves, culminating in a sick bump where Ernie sent Filthy XIX soaring out of the ring with a back body-drop, and the hobo landed awkwardly on his neck. Thankfully enough, he was alright, and the battle resumed, with weapons being bought into the fray. This is where Mr Filth really started to shine, as he wore the champion out with a wide array of weapons.

The battle ended up in the crowd, where Filthy scored with a double powerbomb on the concrete. However, due to his inexperience, he hadn't realised that a 5-count was required under Aggression Title Rules, and he started celebrating after the referee counted to 3. This allowed Ernie to regain control of the match, courtesy of a sick double-arm DDT on the concrete. As the match retreated back towards the ring, a ladder was used by Ernie to try and knock Filthy out, but the Lord Of The Landfill managed to use the ladder to his advantage, after a flying kick into the ladder which rebounded off Ernie's face. However, Filthy XIX then decided to scale to the top of the ladder and attempted a flying headbutt. French rolled out of harm's way, and managed to connect with a suplex through a table, to pick up the victory after about nine minutes. Great opener to the House Show, leaving the fans ecstatic at the prospect of seeing more of Filthy XIX in the future.

Result: Ernie French won at 9:32 to retain the Aggression Title

--

In his little makeshift office, Troy Mason was talking to Two-Face & Nathan Brady. Two of the four men duped by Vincent Pembridge & Colin Gear, to simply cheat the company out of money by being on payroll but not being involved in active competition. Instead, the four men -- the two aforementioned guys with Casper Cardiel & Race Cavanagh -- were made to do grunt work for the Scorpion Of Manchester, which involved messing with the Board Of Directors. Troy informed Two-Face & Nathan that despite what went on, he bailed them out because he believes in second chances, and officially welcomed the two into the thReat family. Nathan thanked Troy, but then insisted that he wanted Vincent Pembridge in a match, since he did see the British Degenerate arriving at the warehouse earlier.

And you know what Troy did?

The sneaky thReat Owner made a match; Two-Face & Nathan Brady vs Vincent Pembridge. Handicapped Match. Even though Troy knew that Two-Face & Nathan Brady weren't the best of friends. The two men left the office of Mason's giving each other dirty looks, while Troy smiled to himself, knowing that he'd just done a very smart thing.

--

thReat International Title Match: RAH vs Chris McCluskey [c]
After the end of a long tournament that saw a lot of surprises and tons of action, Chris McCluskey came out of the Double Or Nothing PPV as thReat's International Champion. He made his way to the ring imitating Kurt Angle in his early days in the WWF, which drew massive heat from the crowd. Chrissycakes went on to thank himself for the support given while taking part in the tournament for the title, and also said that he was going to offer an open challenge to any AMERICAN wrestler, for his very first title defense. The crowd of 450 people went ballistic when Rent-A-Hero, who wasn't at the PPV due to a flu bug, answered the challenge. And he certainly kicked up a storm as he hit the ring and kicked the crap out of Chris, before McCluskey managed to gain some sort of reprieve following a tremendous overhead belly-to-belly suplex.

The pace of the match dropped significantly following the opening minute or so, as both men tried to carve out each other's weak spots for exploitation, which saw a lot of submission holds being used within a short period of time. It was clear, however, that McCluskey was the more confident of the two, as he began to tighten his grip on the match. Almost pulled out the victory, even, after a fisherman's suplex from the top rope. Eventually, the return of REMIX saw RAH get clocked with a chair while the referee was momentarily distracted by Chrissycakes, allowed the latter to pick up the victory with his Reality Check submission finisher.

After the match, Remix beat on Rent-A-Hero a bit more, before laying down the challenge for the next House Show; Remix versus Rent-A-Hero in a Steel Cage Match. The crowd popped big time at that, but with RAH concussed, they never did get confirmation on whether that showdown would really happen.

Result: Chris McCluskey won at 7:48 to retain the International Title

--

Elsewhere in the warehouse, Sergey Kovhachek were leading a bunch of backstage workers in some sort of mourning ritual for Jere Casarella, who died at the Double Or Nothing PPV in the Fight To The Death with psycho-Rio. It was meant to just pay respects to Jere, who had given it his all while working for thReat. However, his murderer showed up halfway and started laughing at the photo of Jere that was mounted on a wall. Sergey took great offense to this and told the Cuban Sociopath, calling him a sick murderer. psycho-Rio retorted, remarking that Commissioner Gear had all the paperwork approved, and thus, the murder was oh-so-legitimate. In a way, he was correct, but it was the murder of Jere Casarella in the Fight To The Death that was one of the causes of thReat's suspension. Anyways, psycho-Rio left the mourning ritual chuckling to himself, with the Fighting Title he'd won as a result of winning the Fight To The Death around his waist.

Needless to say, Sergey Kovhachek was mighty pissed.

--

Outside the warehouse, Tyler Davidson & Rune Winters were bemoaning not being involved in matches, when the two started talking about how General David Sanders screwed thReat with the paralysing of Lawrence Neumeyer. Another one of the factors that got Troy Mason's ownership license revoked and thReat suspended. Both agreed that Sanders took the personal matter too far, and that getting Lawrence paralysed was NOT the honourable thing to do.

--

Vincent Pembridge then graced the ring, looking as cocky as ever. He started off his spiel in the ring by 'hyping' his showdown with Quinton May in ACW the following Sunday, before briefly touching on the subject of how Levond Daniels wasn't around because he was given the night off. The crowd remembered what had happened between Vincent & Levond & 'Anubis' at the Double Or Nothing PPV, but the Scorpion of Manchester quickly changed topic, talking about the two defining events that got thReat suspended.

The murder of Jere Casarella, and the current comatose state of Lawrence Neumeyer.

He chuckled at the thought of thReat's favourite superstar, General David Sanders, resorting to doing something so heinous to get thReat suspended, before offering his condolences to Troy Mason. The night he returned to thReat, the Board Of Directors decided to pull the plug. But, the British Degenerate also mentioned that he was happy that Troy Mason was taking measures to rebuild thReat, after having been given his ownership license back, albeit temporarily.

Simply because it'd allow Vincent the chance to carry out his ultimate masterplan. All thanks to the Board Of Directors, who agreed upon a set of rules laid down for thReat by the American government, and decided to stick with thReat, reinstating power to Troy Mason & Colin Gear. Seemed that Pembridge knew an awful lot about what was going on. Vincent went on to say that although he felt elated at the end of Double Or Nothing, he knew that the suspension would be short-term thing, but was still overjoyed because he wasn't even the reason behind the suspension.

But, before the former thReat Fighting Champion could go on any longer, psycho-Rio decided to interrupt, as he limped down to the ring -- still brandishing his wounds and scars from the PPV -- and called Vincent 'a pile of sodding shite'. The animosity between both men had been rumoured, and now, the crowd were getting to see the two men stare down at each other. However, Vincent didn't retaliate with words. Instead, he charged at psycho-Rio and a brawl was on.

The melee was broken up by security rather quickly, however, and the two fighters were quickly escorted to the back. psycho-Rio received a surprisingly loud chorus of cheers for brawling with the Scorpion, which left many of the fans thinking that a match between the two was imminent in the near future.

--

In the back again, several of the backstage officials were talking about how so many people were injured. Like Brady Watts & Rejection & Jason Semps. There were also rumblings about Christian Elijah possibly leaving thReat after the mix-up at Double Or Nothing which saw the Tag Team Match involving him & Frankie Focker & haTE & R.A.T not materialising. Chris Pinash was also speculated to have fully recovered but not wanting to return just yet. Disciple X was another name brought up, and one guy mentioned that he doesn't want to come back because he can't be assured that some of the current roster members won't end up killing him or hurting him really bad.

Obviously, referring to the likes of psycho-Rio & General David Sanders.

The conversation then revealed that a lot of the wrestlers/fighters on the roster currently shared Disciple X's sentiments, and that they were worrying about their futures. Then, Twizzy came along and invited the officials to join him in a dance.

... Naturally, they fled, and Twizzy trudged back dejectedly to the catering room.

--

thReat Tag Team Titles Match: We Iz Special vs Born To Die [c]
Something unexpected happened next. "Say My Name" by Destiny's Child began to play over the speakers, and every member of the crowd began to sing along. Which led to name-calling breaking out in the crowd. But, once KEITH ANAL-WEDGED & ALAN ABEL WADE came out to the ring, massive jeering took over.

The voices of 450 people suddenly sounded like 45000 people.

Most of the jokers in attendance remembered Keith & Alan from Icons Of Wrestling, where the likes of Phil Atken & Tyler Davidson & psycho-Rio came from. And, since the brothers were wearing pink bikini trunks, it wasn't hard for the crowd to take an immediate disliking to them. Alan had control of a microphone and talked about pies for a good three minutes, before handing it over to his brother, Keith Anal-Wedged. Keith expressed his delight at having been signed to contracts by Commissioner Gear. Iron-clad contracts. Following which, Keith challenged the Tag Team Champions to a match.

So, Born To Die came out, bewildered. After checking with the referee, Johnny Olympus gave the go-ahead to his boys to wrestle the match, and it was on. SinFyre almost murdered Alan Abel Wade with a variety of kicks, that was followed by a catapault sommersault legdrop. X-Ploder, meanwhile, went absolutely postal on Keith Anal-Wedged. Annihilating him with a running bodyslam piledriver after punching him silly for almost a minute. And with both Alan & Keith out cold in the middle of the ring, Born To Die picked up the easy victory after both X-Ploder & SinFyre scored with swanton bombs; Double Dew Feather. We Iz Special had no chance at all, and were dragged out of the ring by paramedics.

Poor fuckers.

Result: Born To Die won at 2:09 to retain the Tag Team Titles

--

Mere moments before Pembridge's match, that Kaylee Young girl; the Mormon; went to his door. She started talking about how she was a missionary, and that she felt that Vincent's soul needed to be saved from sin, and that she was here to help Vincent. He politely told her to leave, but she stayed and continued to ramble. The Scorpion retalitated cheekily, giving some obscene gestures with his pelvic region. She ignored them, and kept going on about how Vincent was evil, and he yawned. Nothing he hasn't heard before. But then she started saying that he obviously needed help, because in his current state, there was no way he could beat Quinton May in ACW. He didn't like that, especially when she said it with a smile on her face. What happened next was pretty damn shocking to me, but he decked her. The Arrogant Bastard decked Kaylee and left her for dead. Medics quickly rushed to her assistance, and while they treated the sobbing Kaylee, remarks about a nasty bruise were confirmed.

--

2-On-1 Handicapped Match:
Nathan Brady & Two-Face vs Vincent Pembridge
After a short intermission where Twizzy came to the ring and mooned the crowd, the fans got a real treat. No, not the rumour spread around that Alan Abel Wade & Keith Anal-Wedged were being gang-raped outside the arena by a couple of grumpy blind men. The 2-On-1 Handicapped Match, silly gooses.

Nathan Brady & Two-Face came out together, receiving cheers. Obviously. But the two kept their distance from each other in the ring, while Vincent Pembridge strolled down to the ring, his usual arrogant self. Match started off with a double-team, as Nathan & Two-Face completely overwhelmed the Scorpion, and left him reeling with a variety of double-team moves. One moment of miscommunication, however, allowed Vincent to behead Nathan with a flying thrust kick, before he almost got the pin on Two-Face following an inverted piledriver. But, Nathan & Two-Face quickly got back together, and continued to wear the British Degenerate out, as the match spilled out of the ring.

It was then that Vincent really began to turn the tide, isolating Two-Face from Nathan after a spinning chokeslam onto a chair. Brady still managed to match Pembridge move for move, even with his partner out cold, but the Arrogant Bastard used every cheap trick in the book to put himself in the driver's seat. As he eventually got Nathan back into the ring, Vincent gambled on a missle dropkick, which was surprising considering that the Degenerate is an out-and-out brawler. Nathan evaded getting struck, but he was too exhausted to take advantage of the situation.

Two-Face eventually got into the picture, and climbed back into the ring after Brady accidently clotheslined the referee. With the chair in hand, Two-Face tried to clobber Vincent with it but the latter ducked, and Nathan got hit. The Scorpion of Manchester quickly followed up with the Scorpion Bite, and pinned Two-Face for the victory. After the match, Two-Face & Nathan Brady brawled all the way to the back, with security trying their hardest to break it up.

Result: Vincent Pembridge won at 9:21

--

DA MAIN EVENT! WHOO HOO!
Troy Mason came out again and expressed his displeasure at Vincent Pembridge having won, before announcing that for the next House Show, Vincent would get his first shot at the thReat World Title; Triple Threat Match. psycho-Rio versus Vincent Pembridge versus General David Sanders. Troy assured that Sanders WOULD show up for the next House Show, before he touched on the topic of finding a #1 Contender to the General's World Title.

And, as a result of a backstage poll, was somewhat proud to announce the two men who'd be involved in this situation; blind and Phil Atken. Mason said that since opinions were split on both men, he had decided to make things interesting. A 2/3 Series, with each match in the series being contested on each of the 3 House Shows planned. Unless, of course, one man won the first two falls unanswered. The first match would be PINFALLS ONLY, the second match would be SUBMISSIONS ONLY. The third match, if need be, would be PINFALLS & SUBMISSIONS.

Simple, yet rather interesting. With that, Troy left, having set the stage for the two combatants.

Ready at their shot at glory.

Phil Atken came out to the ring first, and even though the sound-system was screeching all through Atken's now famous pre-match promo I did manage to pick up something.

"Gear. Screwed me. PPV. Bumsex."

I hope he was talking about the guest referee spot; then again, we were at a porn warehouse.

Anyway, blind came down to the ring, and fresh off his loss at Double or Nothing to Chris McCluskey. did not want to waste any time or put the opportunity to waste as he took Atken down with a flying chop-block to the knee which sent Phil tumbling in a 360 manner and was as cool as hell to watch. Atken was yelling, "Ah, my freaking knee! You asshole!" as loud as possible. blind backed off and Atken hobbled to his feet, but didn't get any time to recover as he was quickly nailed by a snap suplex. Atken pulled himself up using the ropes, and one of those boring technique sequences of holds that are a pain to describe happened and ended with blind nailing Atken with that Sightless move. It only got a two.

Anyways, throughout the match, Atken sold the whole "knee in pain" thing; it looked like it could be legit. That or us reporters were fooled again. The ending of the match came when blind kicked Atken's knee, and as Atken bent over, clutching it, blind managed to nail him with 'And Now You Can See' at a very quick speed. Awesome spot! blind was quick to get the pin after that, and the crowd went wild. Atken remained in the ring for a while, as everybody filed out of the warehouse, and he swore to get revenge on the next House Show.

Result: blind won at 7:43 to take a 1-0 lead

--

All in all, it was a rather decent House Show, considering that the morale in the federation is quite low. The entire saga with Showtime cancelling the television deal and the suspension, and the mere fact that House Shows are even being held -- it's had a negative effect on many members of the roster. But, considering the quality of the matches on display, it seems as if thReat definitely has the starpower to make a roaring comeback.

Whenever that is.

--
