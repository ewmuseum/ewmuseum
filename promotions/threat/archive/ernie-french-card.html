Biography

On the 14th of October 1975 A child was born in L’Hôpital St. Roch, Nice, France, the child was named Ernest Fredrick Noble II, son of Ernest Fredrick Noble and Lorraine Noble. The innocent child would grow up to become Ernie French, dropping his real name after discovering the shameful secret behind his conception.

By the time Ernest had reached the age of three, his mother had decided to leave his father for another man, the problem with this was that Ernest Fredrick Noble Senior was a very violent man, not only towards her, but towards her child, Ernie.

Ernie was her nickname for Ernest Fredrick Noble II, and it is also where the first name of Ernest’s ‘Screen name’ came from. He loved his mother dearly and missed her once she was gone.

For a few years Ernie lived alone with his father in their small home and was abused constantly from his alcoholic father, not only was he beaten, he was starved for days on end, mentally tortured each and every day and raped often. He was a quiet child and at school, the few times he was allowed to attend by his father, Ernest would sit outside on his own and watch the other children play, he never joined in.

At the age of thirteen Ernest Fredrick Noble II realised that his home life was not that of a normal child and quickly decided to run away. Two months passed and Ernest had grown week and tired from living off the land in a forest and stumbled out towards civilisation looking for a place to eat or drink. Police took him to the station and at a later stage the same day returned him back to his residence. His father raped him five times that night and locked Ernest in a closet for almost a week after that. Indeed he realised that if he was going to run away it would have to be far, far away.

The 14th of October 1990 marked Ernest Fredrick Noble juniors fifteenth birthday and to celebrate he packed a bag and ran away. This time, a more mature mind was able to think through a plan. He would get a ferry across to England and stay there until he had earned enough money to use his newly attained passport to fly across to America and find a job doing something, anything.

A successful ferry ride later and Ernest Fredrick Noble II was fresh out of money. His first job would be to find a job and then a home. He walked the streets of London for three days, sleeping rough at night until finally he entered a restaurant named ‘Guiseppe's’. He was hired to wait on tables and the owner allowed Ernest to sleep in the empty flat above the restaurant until he got enough money to rent a place of his own.

At the age of nineteen Ernest finally had enough money for his plane ride and he purchased a one way ticked to America. He would land in JFK airport three weeks later.

June 21st 1995 was the day that Ernest Fredrick Noble, now known to most as Ernie French, met his wife. The two dated for two years until she found out that she was pregnant. Ernest the proposed and the two got married. Her name was Rebecca Logan-Noble. She dropped Logan and became Mrs. Rebecca Noble. Three months after the wedding to the day Rebecca went in to labour and Ernest’s daughter was born.

Ernest Fredrick Noble currently worked at a car wash and due to the new arrival he decided that he needed a better paying job, he packed his bag six months later and promised his wife he would return soon a rich man. Ernest landed a job in an office, hating his job he quit and was put out on to the streets. Too ashamed to tell his wife and child that he was homeless he decided to begin stealing and selling the stolen goods so that he could send money home to his family, the thefts got him in to trouble at times and he learned to stick up for himself, and then, after a gruelling fight which he had somehow managed to come out on top of, it hit him… he would fight for money.
