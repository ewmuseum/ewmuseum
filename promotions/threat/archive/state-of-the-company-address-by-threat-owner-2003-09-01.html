State Of The Company Address by thReat Owner
September 01 2003

Hello, fans of thReat and other curious parties. I am Troy Mason, Owner of thReat. On August 23 2003, as our Double Or Nothing PPV reached its end, it was revealed that due to the death of Jere Casarella on the show and the severly-injured state of Lawrence Neumeyer, thReat's safety record was completely horrendous. With the power of the Board Of Directors, who had the support of the United States government, my ownership license was revoked and all operations were suspended.

Over the past week, I've been in numerous meetings with members of the roster, the Board Of Directors, my own legal team, with the legal team from The Patterson Agency, and law enforcement officials all the way up to the FBI. thReat has become a hotbed of criminal activity, and now houses an unsafe working environment. This is partly my fault, due to my vision for thReat. I've always wanted thReat to be different than most promotions in the industry. Traditionally, companies revolve around the basic concept of throwing two people together and telling him to entertain the crowd. Now, it doesn't matter if the product is worked or real. As long as it still entertains, but on a larger scale, and brings in money... my job is done.

That's not the way it's supposed to be. But it's too late now. And, with Vincent Pembridge making it his mission to use this company to fund whatever diabolical plans he has for the future, the only thing I can do now is fight back. But if this company did indeed close down, it'd take a lot of the pressure off my shoulders. Jon Hackett wouldn't approve, however. I made a promise to the man, and I am going to stick by it.

I'll keep fighting until the day I win.

The Board Of Directors have worked out a deal with me. I am to hold three House Shows this month, and if things look promising, my ownership license will be reinstanted permanently. The investigations will continue to carry on behind the scenes, and our safety record will be under tight scrutiny. If thReat does indeed survive past the next month, I have until December of this year to show the American government that the working environment in thReat is not damaging at all. Simply put; thReat will either fizzle out by the end of September, or by the end of December, or go on to last as long as I do.

Sure, there are many external circumstances that are dragging thReat down. I've already touched on some of them. Police investigation, the government keeping a close eye on our safety record, the Board Of Directors wanting to see a positive product being churned out. A few days ago, Showtime officially cancelled our television deal. So, another struggle is on my hands; I have to find a new network to air our shows on.

Our roster's dwindling pretty quickly as well, due to injuries and such. I'll have to scour the nation for fresh talent. In addition, I've been given the go-ahead to set up a developmental federation called thReat:Miami that will be housed completely in Miami, Florida. This will see the development and training of talent, so as to prepare them for the unbarred and uncut atmosphere we have. thReat demands a lot from each and every one of its roster members, and with the way we're structured, it's not surprising in the least bit that we have a lot of injury problems to deal with.

Finally, to stabilise the budget, there will be an arena built especially for thReat events. The Jon Hackett Arena in Los Angeles, California. The official thReat HQ will also be settled down in Los Angeles, and the bulk of events we have in the future will be held in the Jon Hackett Arena. For us to reach out globally, we must start small, and work our way up.

As of now, those are the only details I can provide you with, regarding the current status of thReat. Other than the fact that most of us here are mourning the loss of Jere Casarella, and also praying for Lawrence Neumeyer. He might have been a tool of destruction, but he's still a human being, and being paralysed as a result of manipulation from a psychotic individual is not what he deserves. Fans, I plead that you stick by us. thReat will once again arise from the ashes, and in due time, we shall soar like an eagle.

After all, it is our slogan.

We shall FIGHT BACK.

Thank you, Troy Mason
