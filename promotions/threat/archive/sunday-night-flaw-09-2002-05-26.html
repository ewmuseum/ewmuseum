Colvin: Of course. We’re rounding up Flaw 9 here with a lumberjack match!

Perry: This is the first in the history of thReat, so it ought to be memorable. Our Lumberjacks tonight include the new signee, “Superstar” Vince Jacobs, and the current thReat World Champion, Xaxon.

“Highway” by Deep Purple plays. Red strobe lights shoot around the entrance area as Xaxon steps out. He’s not in his usual wrestling attire, as the crowd begins to laugh hysterically.

Colvin: He’s wearing a flannel shirt! SKIN TIGHTS JEANS! AHHH!

Perry: Oh calm down.

Colvin: Yeah, easy for you to say. you ENJOY old men in tight pants.

Perry: Dale, shut up. Here comes the other lumberjack for tonight. Vince Jacobs. The nerve of this man. He came out last week, and just interrupted the main event to sign a contract!

Colvin: This is The Superstar, Chad! He can do whatever he damn well pleases!

Perry: No he cannot! Interrupting a main event match of that magnitude last week was a stab to the ratings!

Colvin: Put a sock in it. SVJ boosts ratings. He doesn’t give them a downfall.

Perry: Tell that to UPN.

“Heresy” by Nine Inch Nails plays. Gabriel Oliver Danielson, clad in black, walks down the ramp. The arena is nearly pitch black with a spotlight hitting G.O.D. on his way to the ring.

Perry: This guy is a monster. A MONSTER.

Colvin: Yeah, he’s quite the competitor.
Perry: I’m talking about his size! He’s amazing, no doubt. He’s got only good things ahead of him in thReat. With G.O.D. now in the ring, we’ve got only one more competitor left to bring to the ring.

Disciple X’s music hits, as “The Indomitable” strolls to the ring. He hops up with his vertical onto the apron and steps through the ropes. All four men are ready. Xaxon stares across the ring, into the eyes of Vince Jacobs. The two in-ring competitors lockup.

Perry: Here we go and there’s the bell.

Disciple X rolls around to the back of G.O.D. and hooks him up, giving him a belly-to-back suplex. Disciple X picks Gabriel back up and hits him with a hard right hand to the jaw. The Indomitable whips G.O.D. into the ropes and executes a back-body drop.

Perry: Wow, Disciple X is really infuriated!

Colvin: Heh, this is only the beginning. G.O.D. is toying with him.

Disciple X picks G.O.D. up and throws him over the top rope, to SVJ. SVJ just picks G.O.D. up and rolls him back in. Disciple X goes nuts. G.O.D. takes advantage and whips DX into the turnbuckle then follows it up with a splash, knocking DX to the mat. G.O.D. taunts to him to get back up. DX sits up but is greeted with a dropkick right to the jaw.

Perry: Oh, geez Louise.

Colvin: Don’t talk about Louise like that!

Perry: *sigh*

Gabriel throws Disciple X over to Xaxon’s side of the ring. Xaxon drags him over to SVJ and they begin an assault on DX. SVJ holds Disciple X in a full nelson so that Xaxon lands a right hand to the breadbasket of DX. Disciple X falls to the ground, but quickly gets back up. HE grabs SVJ’s head and bounces it off the steel steps. He then goes to Xaxon and rips open his new flannel shirt.

Colvin: Oh shit! They better be censoring this!

Perry: The wrinkles! THE WRINKLES!!!!!!

Colvin: That’s what happens when you get old, Chad. Can you imagine what his bal---

Perry: I BEG YOU NOT TO FINISH THAT SENTENCE!

Colvin: *smiles*

DX gives 6 consecutive chops to the chest of the thReat World Champion. The seventh is blocked then Xaxon kicks DX in the stomach and executes a DDT. Meanwhile, G.O.D. is on the top rope, ready for take off.

Perry: High risk maneuver coming up here!

Colvin: Get him! GET HIM!

G.O.D. flies off, double leg dropping both Xaxon and Disciple X.

Colvin: GOT HIM! GOT HIM!

Perry: He connected! What a risk taker!

Colvin: You only regret the risks you don’t take, Chad!

G.O.D. is slow getting to his feet and once he does, he’s chunked into the crowd by the now standing Vince Jacobs! Jacobs picks up Xaxon and throws him over as well!

Perry: SVJ is kicking some ass!

Colvin: He’s the Superstar baby! thReat entertainment is the best in the biz! SVJ is going after Disciple X! Revenge time baby!

Perry: Wait a second! It’s another developmental talent competitor is making an appearance! It’s, It’s White Lightning! He’s coming to the ring!

White-Lightning runs to the ring and taps SVJ on his shoulder. SVJ turns around and is surprised to see White-Lightning again.

White-Lightning: Answer my questions!!!

SVJ: Get the f*ck away from me!

Perry: Disciple X hooks him! SCREWDRIVER! SVJ was occupied with White-Lightning and DX capitalized!

Colvin: NO DQ?! NO DQ?!

Disciple X sends White-Lightning to the back as G.O.D. and Xaxon are fighting it out in the crowd. Disciple X goes to join them. He makes his way through the foam fingers and old nacho trays and reaches the two men. He grabs their heads and bangs them together.

Colvin: I can’t see what’s going on! It can’t be anything interesting anyways, SVJ is knocked out cold!

Disciple X leaves Xaxon out in the crowd but takes G.O.D. back to the ring. He whips G.O.D. but it’s reserved. DX heads for the turnbuckle but he hops over the top rope and lands on his feet on the apron. He leaps to the top rope and jumps off with a missle dropkick!

Perry: Oh my God! Oh my God! Great action here!

Disciple X picks G.O.D. up now. Gabriel can barely stay on his feet! XOA!

Perry: X'ed on Arrival! That's it! The pin!

1....2.............3!

Colvin: NOOOOOOOOO!

Perry: YEEEEAAAAAHHHHH! That's all the time we've got folks! Goodnight!

Disciple X raises his arms in triumph as Flaw 9 goes off the air.
Winner: Disciple X via pinfall
