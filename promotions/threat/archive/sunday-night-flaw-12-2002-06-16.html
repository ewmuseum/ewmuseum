Sunday Night Flaw 12

16 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Move Bitch! Get out the way! Get out the way bitch Get out the way!

That could only mean one person. The owner of thReat, Jon Hackett. Tonight, no matches were scheduled, but there would be announcements for PPV bouts. With the main event already being set in stone, Hackett walked non-chalantly to the ring with a microphone in his right hand. He brushed off his sleeves and asked for a chair.

Once he set it up, he began to make his speech.

"Hello all.

Tonight you will see no action, so let that be a warning. Tonight, I will be booking next weeks matches for Malevolence on the 23rd. To start off, we've got a couple of new thReat superstars that have made their debut. I clumsy young Colombian by the alias of Banderas and Dean Bronson. The two have run into each other and seem to have a bit to prove for themselves, therefore, these two fighters will square off in the Opening match of the evening in a fighting style matchup."

The crowd began cheering as they knew things were being promoted for this event. They wanted the best entertainment, and they were going to get it.

"For the second match of the night, we've seen CashnMoney and Kid Jive put up with each others bullshit for so long, and frankly I'm tired of seeing it. So, I'm booking a special "Put Up or Shut Up" Match. This match will consist of tables, tables, and upon that, more tables. Let's call it, a table match instead. But in this particular match, the first person to put their opponent through 5 tables in the winner."

BOOM. The roof was blown off. The fans cheered their heads off for the table matches they so dearly love.

"Now, we've got a little midget running around backstage. Conan. Conan the Leprechaun. Him and one of the premiere superstars in thReat, Joey Starr have been bumping into each other quite a bit. Since Conan is so HaRdKoRe~!, I'll let him have "The STARR" in a harcore style matchup. This is for the number one contendership for the International Title."

Joey Starr would get his chance to prove himself. He'd get his chance to make a name for himself in thReat after his past PIW run.

"Here now, is another fighting matchup. The Scorpion of Manchester, Jakks against the man you all know as phYre. Jakks tampered with past Kage Fighting Inc. files and got into the mind of phYre as of late. I'm sure that phYre wants to get his hands on the dirty thief."

The crowd booed at the sound of Jakks' name. They hated that man. The crazed maniac from the UFF was definetly not a fan favorite on this night.

"Handicapped Match is next."

The crowd knew what we going down.

"Araknis and Takisuma Ogama against the biggest, baddest, motherf*cker thReat and this whole industry has ever seen, Riot. To make this interesting. This is also for the International Title. If Riot can pin either Ogama or The Forgotton One, he will become the new International Champion."

The crowd jeered with glee.

"thReat Fighting Championship match. August Monday will take on Quincy Brown in what could be the final Fighting Match thReat will ever see. August Monday has battled his way through hard times and I believe he's earned his shot. QB's definetly got his hands full with this man."

A mixed reaction spilled from the crowd, some chanting for Quincy and some for Monday. Next week was boiling up.

"Three men, one title belt, what may be the last match of it's kind in thReat. Disciple X, "Superstar" Vince Jacobs, Xaxon. thReat World Championship on the line in a triple threat match in maybe the last wrestling match you fans will ever see held by thReat. Strap yourself in, because this will be one high octane match."

The crowd got quiet. They knew there was one match left to be announced. The match that would indeed, decide the fate of the company.

"Oh yeah, start cheering."

They did so. The fans jumped to their feet and began to cheer.

"Time for the main event. Your four wrestling superstars are...

The baddest man in the industry! Riot!

The one and only, the Starr! Joey Starr!

Your Hardcore Champion, Rejection!

Your current International Champion, Takisuma Ogama!"

Four of the best in the game had been chosen to compete for the right to keep their division alive. Now for the others.

Your current tFC, Quincy Brown!

His challenger that very same night, August Monday!

The young blood that thinks you're all a bunch of weak sauce, Steven Solex!

The Scorpion of Manchester, Jakks!

There you have it ladies and gentlemen. Thost eight superstars will battle in out in an over the top Battle Royale to determine the direction of thReat. I hope you all have bought your tickets...Malevolence is just around the corner."

Fade to black.
