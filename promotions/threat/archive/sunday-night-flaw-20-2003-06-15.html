Accomplice

Officials were scooting all over the place, getting ready for the production of Flaw 20. There was little time before the show hit the airwaves, live, and there were no plans for a video montage highlighting the weeks gone by in the company. A repeat of last week's antics by Ty Hughes, where he hijacked the airtime for that montage, wasn't what anyone wanted.

Hence, interim guy-in-charge and Board Of Director member, Moe Sole, decided to scrap that for once, until Troy Mason returned. There were many things that couldn't be done without Mason around, and although Moe basically had more power than Troy... the latter was the man making things work.

With two other members of the Board Of Directors, Sucino Hass and Jackson Mahon, Moe Sole was in the backstage area... going through final discussion over production details for the show. Quality, camera angles, whether or not to really activate the spycam in the public toilets -- that kind of shit. It was something that was second nature to Troy Mason.

But he wasn't there on the night.

Hence, the massive stress on Moe Sole's shoulders. Elected by the rest of the Board Of Directors to be interim guy-in-charge, his experience in the industry being the biggest factor. But maybe it was because of his age that he wasn't quite able to really all the stress that came with filling Troy Mason's shoes.

Moe Sole was a survivor, however. And he wasn't about to give up.

"So, everything's settled?" he asked of a stage technician, as Sucino Haas and Jackson Mahon signed various orders for equipment. The technician nodded, before walking away, talking over his wireless walkie talkie.

Smiling, Sole breathed a sigh of relief. It seemed everything had been taken care of, and with no time to spare, as well. Flaw 20 was about to get underway, sans Troy Mason and all. Moe turned and traded congratulatory high-fives with Sucino & Jackson, who too were relieved that everything had looked to be picture-perfect.

That was before the arrival of three police inspectors on the scene.

"Mr Sole? Inspector Andrews here. We're here to continue the investigation into the disapperance of Troy Mason. As it stands, Xaxon is still our number one suspect..." the burly inspector began, as he flashed his badge at the Board Of Directors trio.

Moe felt his heart stop for a nanosecond, almost worrying that he'd done something wrong or maybe there was a serial rapist on the loose in the federation. As it was, the matter concerned Troy Mason.

"But?" Sole asked, as Jackson & Hass leaned forward, eager to find out more about the situation.

The look on the face of Inspector Andrews should have tipped them off as to what was to come.

"We believe he had an accomplice. An accomplice who is right here, in his building, at this very moment.

And a very influential accomplice, too."

Stunned would be a good word to describe Moe Sole, as Flaw 20 officially hit the airwaves.

Great timing, eh?

Pride

Marcus Escobar had done what many considered impossible at Malevolence.

He had stepped into the ring on behalf of Troy Mason and The Resistance and he had single handedly eliminated Christian Embers and Ampske from any possible contention in that match.

As though that wasn’t enough for Marcus and Geneva to be proud of he had also managed to stand toe to toe against The Scorpion of Manchester, Vincent Pembridge, himself before he finally fell victim to the Fight Champion of thReat. Even with falling in defeat to Pembridge though, Escobar knew that he had nothing at all to be ashamed of. He had done something in this business that many other men couldn’t even claim to doing and had he not already had to go through two other guys before getting to Pembridge things just might have turned out differently.

That was all in the past now however.

Tonight was Flaw and it was a new day for thReat and most importantly for The Faithless. Marcus and Geneva sat together with their legs crossed in their own form of meditation but Marcus’ thoughts were focused primarily on actions of pride. Malevolence was his biggest night to date but tonight he would have the chance to make an impact for The Faithless in the tag team ranks of thReat with a victory over Risto Dizdarevic of Bosnia’s Finest.

The task would not be a simple one but Marcus knew that after what he had gone through at Malevolence he would not fail tonight. Not against someone like Risto, a relative newcomer to the business. Marcus opened his eyes slightly and saw that Geneva was no longer seated next to him but stood not far off to the right and stared down at him. She knew that his mind was not focused on the duties at hand.

“I’ve told you before you must focus,” Geneva lectured him in a rather soft voice showing that despite her appearance she wasn’t very angry over it.

“My apologies again,” Marcus told her as he nodded his head. “I just have a hard time with it when a show comes around.”

”Obviously,” Geneva replied. “You are quite pleased with the performance at Malevolence and as you already know I too was proud of you on that night. Tonight is a whole other matter though. I will soon step into the ring alongside you and together we must push the plan forward.”

“I know,” Marcus assured her. “Tonight I will take care of business with Risto and then we can worry about what will come next. You know that it will not go over well.”

“I do,” Geneva told him. “I am prepared for it.”

No further words were spoken as Geneva again sat on the ground and crossed her legs to go back into her meditation. Marcus remained where he was and closed his eyes again as well but his thoughts ever remained on the events of the recent past and what his task would be tonight. He wasn’t quite as certain as Geneva was that things would go as planned but he trusted her and that was all that mattered.

Business As Usual

That evening in Louisville, the site for yet another thReat event, was a beautiful one. As comfortable as any could be. The event that just happened to take place on this evening was Sunday Night Flaw 20, which, for those keeping count, was the second night thReat could enjoy being a federation of nothing but one hundred percent wrestling.

blind, one of many so-called wrestlers involved in this federation, walked through the parking lot. He carried nothing with him. No bag or clothes or any of that typical crap you see your everyday wrestler carrying as they entered the building. Shit, he didn’t even carry a smile. He was, as he had claimed indignantly many times before, on a mission of sorts. Nothing, not even his own pleasure, could stand in the way of this.

He paced along the lot, eyes locked forward, shredding the arena standing before him apart with his sinister stare. No sounds were made and no words were muttered aggressively. Only the gravel flustering below his boots and the low rumble of the world could be heard.

And then, out of the corner of the cameras eye, appeared a man, wearing baggy jeans and a leather jacket, leaning against the trunk of a black car, cigarette smoke drifting above his head. He stepped out, instantaneously and naturally as if he had been waiting for this moment for awhile, lightly pressing his right hand against blind’s chest. He never looked up, only staring down towards the ground as if something interesting just happened to be crawling passed. Once blind came to a complete stop, the black man looked into his eyes, confidently and arrogantly.

The tension rose almost immediately.

“And what do you want, DeVante?” asked blind, staring down at the man he had about three or four inches on. DeVante stood silent for a moment, his left hand pulling the cigarette out of his mouth and his right gesturing a quick point of acknowledgement.

“I’ve seen you. You look like the kinda guy who knows just what he wants.”

blind, unflinching, wasn’t flattered or impressed. He simply remained, waiting for the point for this sudden disturbance.

“Would you like to talk business with me quick?”

Again, blind stood, uncaring and wondering what else he could be doing with his time.

“Well, let me rephrase that. Would you be interested in somethin to take your mind off all these blind motha fuckers for awhile?”

A sudden smile etched across blind’s previously sullen face, as his thoughts and memories gave him a reasonable idea of what was going on. DeVante Stevenson, once of Flatlined fighting, gave out a little laughter, realizing that he had just scored himself another customer.

“Yeah, I knew you were a reasonable guy.” he said, absently lifting the trunk of the black BMW. Inside laid a spare tire, a pair of battery cables, and an open gym bag full of clothes. Nothing special at all, but blind knew better. DeVante placed his hand on the rim of the trunk, struggled through a small crack, hit a switch of some kind, and opened up a compartment hidden underneath.

Now the ‘products’ were in full view for the world to see. Lying inside was a good amount of many diverse drugs, most notably a bag full of heroin, about the size of a fist. blind’s eyes scanned across the selection before locking onto this one item. A smile larger then any fan had ever seen on his face rose out from the grave, teeth shining in the light emanating from inside the trunk.

“Ahh, yes. That’s some of the best stuff you can afford.”

blind picked it up off the floor of the trunk, and brought it to his side. He stared down at the bag, studying the powder within and making sure it was genuine. When his senses were satisfied, he looked up into DeVante’s smiling face. DeVante nodded, and took another drag of his cigarette.

blind, never taking his eyes off of DeVante, smiled accordingly, and then opened the bag. He lifted the bag in the air, turned it upside, and watched as grains of heroin fell to the ground serenely. With half the bag empty, blind quickly tossed the rest over into some bushes of the parking lot, landing upside down and officially emptying the bag for good.

If DeVante was mad, you couldn’t tell by looking at him. He simply watched the bag’s contents fall to earth, calmly inhaling the smoke and starring. Once the bag was drained of its expensive filling, DeVante simply looked up to blind, who was already back into his frozen state of mind.

“It’s the scum like you, DeVante, that must be washed from the cracks of this world. You are the kind of human being that lives off the suffering of others, and lives through the vices of those who can’t control themselves. You speak of yourself as if you are divine, but all I see is shit. Shit that must be flushed, because you’re diffusing through this world like a disease.

“DeVante, you are just one of the blind.”

blind stepped forward as if on cue, stubbing the shoulder of DeVante Stevenson as he walked. Many other people in this world, including the previous Malik Roland, would take that as a sign of disrespect and say a bunch of words to make themselves sound tougher then they were ever meant to be. DeVante, on the other hand, simply stood there for a few seconds, waiting for blind to step farther away.

When a few seconds had passed by, DeVante suddenly shot out his right hand and violently closed the trunk of his car. He turned, stared coldly at the arena standing in front of him, and took the final puff of his cigarette. He then continued to toss the now lifeless cigarette to the ground aggressively, amongst the remains of some of the heroin that had just been thrown down.

DeVante kicked the small pile into dust, wiped his jeans off quickly, and stepped forward, walking towards the arena where he now had other business to attend to.

For the two of them, Flaw 20 had just begun.

The long night had come to an end, finally. For Moe Sole, he could take pride in the fact that Flaw 20 had been nothing short of breathtaking, sans the sham of a main event, ruined by one Phil Atken. Also, the fact that the accomplice to Troy Mason's supposed kidnap was within the ranks of the organisation left a bitter taste in the mouth of the Board Of Directors.

In the office of the BOD again, Inspector Andrews shook his head, as he'd just finish his report for the night. Ampske was taken off the list, although Andrews had promised to continue keeping tabs on him. Ty Hughes had been raised as another suspect, due to him hijacking airtime on Flaw 19. During which, he made it known that he had no love lost for Troy.

And of course, Vincent Pembridge, although that certainty was ruled out once Andrews found out that the Scorpion had the best alibi ever -- he was miles away, involved on ACW's Sunday Courage show.

So, whodunnit? Nobody knows. Where's Troy? Nobody knows.

Jackson Mahon's cellphone rang, and he excused himself to answer it, while the rest of the Board began to discuss the next steps amongst themselves. Inspector Andrews, meanwhile, filed all his reports and shoved them into his briefcase. He'd have to retire back to hotel home and get some rest, for the next day would be another long day of fact-finding and such.

Troy Mason, bound and gagged to a chair, completely bloodied and almost devoid of life. With explosives wrapped around him.
