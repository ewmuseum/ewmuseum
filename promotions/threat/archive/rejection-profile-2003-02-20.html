Rejection

Real Name: Trevor Wilson(Only to Lucretia)

Born: November 30th, 1977 Place of Birth: Salem, Mass. Hometown: Salem, Mass.

Height: 6'11 Weight: 345 lbs. Blood type: AB Eye color: Green

Manager: Lucretia

Theme song: "Ain't Love Grand" by Atreyu

Style Competing style: Powerful/Brawling

Signature moves:
"Denied" - Rejection gets his opponent in a full nelson. He lifts them up, and slams them down face-first and keeps the full nelson locked in.
"Ballad of Denial" - Several stiff-arm punches to the head
"Inner Hatred" - Rejection lifts his opponent up, and drops him over his knee on their stomach.


Finisher: Rejection Finisher name: Rejection grabs his opponent by the throat, lifts him up, and slams him down into a sit-down powerbomb.

Historical Info Other/Previous promotions: thReat

Other titles won: thReat Aggression Title

Biography To get the big picture, you have to glance at all the small ones.

Trevor Wilson was born. Big whoop. This would be the first in a series of rejections.

Trevor Wilson was born on November 30th, in Alexandria Bay, New York. Trevor wasn't planned at all, and was more of a nuisance than a son. However, Trevor didn't show any contentness either. He was a troublemaker, and was in a constant phase of the terrible two's. Breaking things on purpose, defacing paintings and the walls, disrespecting his parents. He was considered a "horrible son, a monster", among other titles. He just happened to not give a damn. His parents thought he would soon grow out of it, but to no avail. Then, school started.

School was a nightmare. Not for Trevor, he rather enjoyed it. It was a nightmare for his parents, Jacob and Laura. They were there at least once a week, if not more. "Trevor hurts the other children; Trevor makes messes and doesn't clean them up; Trevor is braking crayons and ripping books." His parents grew more and more hateful towards Trevor as the days went by. But, Trevor kept advancing in school. He, after all, was a very bright boy.

Middle school didn't get any better. In fact, it gave Trevor a more wider range of victims to terrorize. He even tried beating up on kids older than he, and even if he got shoved down on his ass, he'd get up and do it again. There was one count, where he broke two noses while serving a detention. This granted him three days OSS. Which meant three days, in the house, with his parents at work, alone. A broken TV and speaker system left his parents vengeful. Growing hatred for him inside replaced physical violence, but they were being pushed too much.

High school came far too quickly for young Trevor, who was now a 15 year old teen.
Little did he know high school was going to be dispensable.

He came home one day, early of course, due to disruptions in class. His parents were done. Especially with him. As his father unlocked the door, he let Trevor walk in ahead of him. Mistake. As soon as Trevor cleared the door, his father let loose. One hard chop to the back of the neck took him out, and he lay on the floor unconscious. Jacob had little time, and moved fast, with Laura's help.

He awoke with a screaming headache. It was dark out, but there was lots of light on the bench he was sitting on. He had a note taped to his hand: "Go away." He looked up, then re-read the note. They were serious. He ripped the note off, and crumpled it into a ball.
He had two duffel bags with him, which he assumed were his clothes and anything else he had. At least they gave him his stuff. They dropped him off at what looked like the train station. He didn't know how to get here from home, or to get home from here. So, he was stuck. How ironic, that this was his easy getaway, but he had no money to take it. So, he sat there for a while, trying to figure out what to do.

Then, he remembered. His mother's sister Sara was 24. So, he decided somehow to get to his Aunt Sara, who lived in Salem.

And so he took off. Well, not really. Through weeks of hitchhiking, getting meals from kind passerby's, and searching, he finally found Aunt Sara. He had other family he could go to, but none this close to his age. He knew she would understand. She would get him.
And he'd change, he'd change for her, because he knew she'd accept him.

And she did. She took him in with arms wide open. None of that cute, big hug and kiss stuff. She strutted out, gave him a look, and let him inside, giving him a half-hug as they walked into the house. She was the mom he had wanted. The one he needed. She registered him into school in Salem, and he fit right in. He wasn't the out of bounds shit-starter he used to be. Instead, he was just some cool kid everyone seemed to want to get to know. He talked a ton of shit, but he backed it all up. And he had got friend after friend after friend. Some of them all idolized him, and he got a small group of lackey's right away.

The one thing he didn’t notice though, were girls. He paid them no attention, just like when he was younger. Oh they paid attention to him. He was just too stupid to realize it.
Until one girl. Her name was Erica. She was the typical wet-dream walking: medium height, blonde hair, blue eyes. But, it might not have been her looks that attracted him to her, rather the fact that she was one of the few girls who didn't notice him at all. So, he set out to make her his own. It was hard, but he became her friend eventually, taking different routes in the hall and on the way home to "accidentally" bump into her. And she started to take a liking to him. The other girls who made advances on him stopped, and just remained casual friends.

Because he loved Erica. Something inside of him had clicked, and the two had become a single unit. They were forever together, and his bad influences started rubbing off on her.
They started skipping classes, sometimes whole days, just to lay around together. But, there years of high school were coming to a close. It was their graduation party, and it all went downhill.

Everything was going as planned. They showed up together, broke off and started talking with some friends. To Trevor however, that meant get plastered and joke about all the geeks and retards they would miss harassing. And the alcohol got to him too strong though. He went looking for Erica, and saw her with another guy. Casually talking, a laugh here or there, but it meant a threat to Trevor, and he lost it. He took off, and tackled the guy from behind. He rolled him over, and started punching his face with a clenched fist. Whack after whack, he spilt his blood over the green grass of the front lawn, until he finally heard the screams. He looked up, and was plowed by three people, stopping his attack. They restrained him down, and he kept yelling to get off of him. And he kept looking for Erica, but she was gone.

She was gone. He was carried home that night, dragged more than half the way, and slumped onto Sara's front deck. She was the one who woke him, and delivered him a letter. His eyes were barely open, and she guided him inside. She set him down on the couch, and gave him a glass of water with two Advil. After a few minutes, he started to read the letter. It was brief, but told him everything. The bottom of the page was hard in a way, and a very dark red. And he read silently:

"Trevor,
I'm sorry, but I can't do this anymore. There was a time when I was absolutely in love with you. Seeing you made my whole day complete. But, you've changed, or maybe I've changed. But there isn't anything there anymore. I've missed more school in this one sole year than three times my school education. My parents and I have grown farther apart with all your shit, and I'm sick of it. Look at this paper. All this at the bottom is the blood of an innocent friend of mine. You can't even handle yourself anymore. It's over.
Good-bye."

And that was it. He re-read the last line again. "It's over. Good-bye." Suddenly, he felt 15 all over again. He folded up the letter, and went into his room. Sara didn't even try to stop him, or talk to him. She knew what he was going through, and knew lecturing him wouldn't do anything. He closed the door behind him, and turned the lock.

He didn't come out for a total period of two days, 48 complete hours. He had missed his graduation, but none the less, Sara, as sweet and kind as she was, had gotten his diploma for him. He came out completely changed. He had dismissed the whole situation with
Erica, and was back to the attitude he had held as a child. Hell had re-broken loose. But, this time he was done here. It was time for college. And Sara, the loving guardian she was, put him through it. And he truly did love her for that.

College. There it was, in all it's glory. He loved it here. He was his old self again, taking fights on and off campus, keeping the grade, kegging it literally every night, and this time, he was noticing girls. He was well built, and quite attractive, only these girls meant nothing to him. All he did was keep a tally on the back of his closet door in his dorm room. Every day, or at the least, every other day he put up a new tally mark. Then, he was hit with a shock. His grades had actually started slipping. And not the good slipping, he was risking failure in several classes, ending in his removal from campus, and being kicked out. So, he decided to give up on sex, his friends giving him a long talk on why a keg every night was a good thing. Once he stopped paying attention to girls, they did the same. Just like before, they were simply piles of walking flesh. As gross an analogy it was, it practically became his motto. Until the grades got back up that is. But, of course, the meaningless sex had nothing to do with it, and it was the heavy partying. They went up a little, enough to keep him in, but he was content with that. Until his next rude awakening.

His grade increase lasted a mere week or so, and he was gone. Kicked out of college, and back home with Aunt Sara. A tension had grown between them, and he was sorry for the waste of her money on his short-lived college education. But she would be mad at him forever for that. So, he got a cheesy job as a McDonald's worker, and gave all his paycheck to her. It was all he had to give her, but she took it from him. He didn't even know if she spent it or not, just that he gave it to her, and she took it from him. Things went like this for a while. He mostly stayed inside the house, or just sat around outside, killing the time until the next day. Then six months later, out cold.

Sara was done with him. She told him she loved him, and would always love him, but it was time he got out on his own. She waited in the living room while he got his things packed. Once he was finished collecting his things, she handed him a small bag. She told him he could open it whenever. She gave him a hug, and had to stretch way up to kiss him, and he left. He stood out on the deck for a few minutes, and just started walking. He went to the main part of town to the park. It was turning into night, but there was this shining of light purple and orange coming from the sun, and clouds. It almost set the mood for him. He sat down at one of the benches, and laid down his two duffel bags. All he ever carried with him on these little "adventures" were clothes. He sat there, pondering what to do, where he could go, how he was going to...While these thoughts ran through his head, he was undoing the strap on the bag Sara gave him. All the paychecks he had given her. So, she knew what she was doing the whole time, ever since he first started working. Just a way to get him going. But all this money was just the excuse she had been looking for to get rid of him.

Fine. He'd make it on his own. And he knew how. He got in contact with old friends from college. They were all going to wrestling school in Rochester, New York. He had the build for it, and he knew he could make it there. At the least, he could get some good experience, and hang with his old friends.

But he wasn't happy there. He was excelling at everything they were being taught, and he had the cash to keep paying. But, he just felt out of place. They sent out wrestling videos of the guy's once a month, to different independent promotions. He was dying for a response every time. And every time, nothing. Until one time. He made it his own personal agenda to make a flawless performance. And that he did. He finally got a response. It had taken six months, but he was finally going somewhere. A place called
AECW. He didn't even look to see what it was fully called. He just packed up, and hitchhiked to there headquarters in Philadelphia. Most of his money was scrapped, but he had enough for the occasional meal. He would ask total strangers at their home if he could take a shower. Surprisingly, he got lucky on several occasions. This actually gave him a bad idea, that things were going to fall apart.

But finally, Philadelphia. They were definitely an independent federation, but that was fine with him. He figured if he got his ass beat, then he'd at least learn something from it all. He worked out a deal, and made an extra couple bucks working with the road crew.
These people also became his traveling guru, and he found a way to make it to all the shows, hitchhike free. He was set now. He had a steady job, and wrestled whenever he could, for as much money as possible. He still worked extra, and traveled with the road crew, so he started getting a lot of money left over. He wired most of it to is bank account back in Salem.

Slowly though, he was getting over with the fans. Every night he started pulling off newer and more dangerous moves, and started offering himself for any high-risk match-ups. He was even getting over well with the guys backstage, showing he'd do anything to make it in the business. Then, a big match came up. He was put into a hardcore main event match. This was what he was dying for.

And it came with a blast of agony. During the match, when he was supposed to pull off a
Moonsault while his opponent was lying on a chair, he rolled out of the way. The pain ripped through his body, and his back was seriously aching. But, he continued the match, only to be screwed in the end, and lose the biggest match of his career on a screw job. So, he left. His brow was still wet from sweat as he walked out of the arena. This wasn't really a problem, but the show that was just put on was in Oklahoma. So, he had no idea how to get home.

He took a gamble, and bought a one way ticket to Salem. It took all the money he had with him, leaving only a couple dollars to spare. But, the spoils at home were excellent.
He had been with AECW for eight months, and though it wasn't a half-million dollar a year job, he had lots of money now. And the first thing he did was get an apartment, and fill it with all the things he needed. He did this for one reason: He was planning a huge party. He was making all the plans, everything that needed to be done. He invited as many friends as he knew, excluding everyone from Salem itself. He even let friends bring their uncles neighbors daughter. And then, the party...

Ended, as far as he knew, with him being arrested for disturbing the peace. He himself barely knew what was happening he was so hammered. But, he spent that one night there, and bail was posted. But, he was shocked and amazed at who had done it.

Not Aunt Sara. But, rather, a girl from the party. He had given her looks throughout the night, and had found her very attractive. But, for some reason this girl, whose name he didn't even know was setting him free from the county jail. She had no reason at all for doing it. But, he would soon find out. She drove him back to Salem, the jail was only a town or so over, and to his apartment.

Trashed. Most everything inside was broken, cups were laying around everywhere, vomit was spewed across the bathroom where drunken idiots had missed the toilet. It was one big shithole. And he hated it, and his blood started boiling. He was about to turn around and start trashing the rest of it, when he bumped into her. She purposely stood behind him, watching him survey the damage the whole time.

"So, wanna place to stay?"

This puzzled and bewildered him, but he didn't question it. Some girl, whose name nor identity he didn't even know was offering him residence. But, in his state, why would he turn her down. So, he unpacked the clothing in his beat up dresser drawers, filled his duffel bags, grabbed the few dollars he had left in case of emergency, and followed her out the door.

This was how he came to live with Lucretia. He didn't mind that she listened to black metal, and it soon grew on him as well. He rather enjoyed her alchemy paintings o the wall, or her choice of black for most everything. He lived with it, and soon became content with it. Their whole nature of being together began as a physical relationship only, as making love most of the time was all they really did. But soon, there was something more there, and both were content with their lives.

Except one thing. Trevor was done with the rest of it. One day, he was sitting there thinking.

His parents rejected him.

Erica rejected him.

His school rejected him.

His Aunt rejected him.

AECW rejected him.

His friends rejected him.

The world had rejected him.

"What're you thinking of?", she asked half-wondering if the answer would be a good one with the intensity in his eyes.

"It's time I paid back the world, for rejecting me." He had a stern, cold look on his face.

"Back to wrestling again?"

"Only this time, I am Rejection."

She smiled. She loved the violence for some reason, and now that he finally had the chance to payback all the bitches he could never touch, it was time for the return of
Trevor Wilson.

As Rejection.
