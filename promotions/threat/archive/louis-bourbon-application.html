Character Information

Character Name: "The Sun King" Louis Bourbon (XIX) Type of Wrestler: Dignified heel, with technical acumen, knowledge of how to cheat (and get away with it), and an arsenal of submission moves that is unparalleled.
Height: 6'3" Weight: 232 lbs. Entrance Music: "1812 Overture" by P. I. Tchicovsky (spelling butchered, I know) Character Gimmick: Louis Bourbon is a descendant of the great Bourbon line of kings in France, which reached its greatest height with Louis XIV, the "Sun King". Louis is a Frenchman of the modern day, and upset with France's lack of prestige in the world community, has vowed to restore it. He declared himself king of France, Louis XIX (the last Louis in France to be called by a number was Louis XVIII), but often simply goes by Louis Bourbon. Somewhat delusional, yet possesses strong athletic attributes and a nearly Mensa level mind. Also acts like the Sun King, forming a cult of personality around himself.
Character History: Born in Calais, France, on the English Channel, Louis Rousseau was, for ten years of his life, your perfectly average young Frenchman. That is, until he discovered a course in his younger years on French history.

He was astounded by France's past glory days under Napoleon Bonaparte and other great rulers, but was most enthralled by Louis XIV.

The Sun King, as he was called, led France to heights not yet seen. Fear of him dominated Europe, as he held his own nobles under his control, moved the French court to Versailles, and generally ruled well.

The young Louis devoured any material he could on the man, and went on to major in History at the University of Paris. Now 22, and out of university, Louis had to decide what to do with his life.

He began working for the French government, in a desk job regarding agriculture, but he was not happy. Even in the government, he could see the pride of France declining. De Gaulle's attempts in the 70s to restore French prestige had only served to annoy foreigners even further.

Louis was shocked at every France-bashing development that occurred, ESPECIALLY the situation over Iraq. France had no REASON to fight Hussein. How would America have liked it if France would have went to go fight Japan, and France demanded US aid?

An enraged young Louis began to be driven to desperation. He wanted to restore French pride, rebuke the US for its actions, and do it..with himself as the head. And thus, so it was, that before a small gathering of like minded individuals that he was proclaimed Louis XIX, King of France.

But the new King needed a territory to rule over. With a fair amount of amateur experience on his hands from university, as well as a quick mind and dexterous body, he set out to learn the mat game, and did so rapidly.

Now The Sun King has set his eyes on thReat to be his kingdom. Woe be to any who cross him, because, in a slight paraphrase of Louis XIV's immortal line, "Le fed'tat, c'est moi!"

Also, one of my closer friends in the e-fed business (Jamar) joined up recently, and I'd like to work with him again someday, preferably here.
Why should you be in thReat? I should be chosen because I've been doing this e-fedding thing for six and a half years now. I get my responsibilities done on time, can write matches, segs, and roleplays, and am generally a pretty nice guy. I also think The Sun King brings an added dimension to thReat as a guy hellbent on world domination, but with an interesting twist.
