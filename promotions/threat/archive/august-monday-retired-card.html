Biography

Rage has lead an interesting life. Coming straight out of high school and into a scholarship at the University of Portland for wrestling. Studying psychology as a major there, Rage was named NCAA Wrestling Champion and All-American in his first year. His high school sweet-heart, Ellen Jameson, went to UoP with Rage and the soon engaged to be married.

On their way to the engagement party, the rain soon came to cover the roads, the coldness of the night soon turned the road's watery coverage to ice. A stray deer in the middle of the road causing Rage to swerve or die, soon came back to haunt him. He slide off of the road and into an oncoming truck. Luckily, only for him, it was the passenger side of the vehicle to hit. Killing Ellen instantly with severe head trauma. The accident threw Rage through the windscreen and his fairytale life was over.

Rage woke up two months later, out of his coma. The doctors told Rage that he may never walk again, due to a back injury. Rage's exact words to the doctor were: "If I don't ever walk again, the world will stop turning." Months on months of his own physiotherapy and training, Rage soon learned to walk. The doctors were amazed but his next feat would stun them forever more.

Rage put himself back into training for wrestling, he was still on his scholarship at UoP and wanted to continue his wrestling. Rage was back on track and he was stronger than ever. He devoured opponents at wrestling meets and was to be named NCAA Wrestling Champion and All-American again but one fracard after a meet turned his fairytale wrestling career sour.

Rage and his team mates went to a local bar, talking about the meet they had a Rage's glorious win. They drank for hours on end, eventually, the bartender would serve them no longer. This infuriated Rage, triggering something which had been dislodged in his mind, from the accident. Rage leapt the counter and slammed the bartender onto the bar, his buddies cheering, having great fun. Rage was quite serious. Rage lifted up the bartender and delivered a devastating orang suplex down onto the bar, dislocating the bartender's shoulder and dislodging three vertebrae. Bar flies in on the night called 911 immediately and somehow detained Rage.

Rage was to go to court at age 22. He was sentenced and found guilty by the Justic of the Peace and sentenced by the judge to two years imprisonment, probation to be looked into after nine months minimum. Rage's sour temper had just been ignited, his new found strength just into form. What was to happen to Rage next.

In prison, day after day, all times possible, Rage pumped the weights. Becoming strong than he ever was. His impecible weight training was soon a defense in a scary, fearsome prison with murderers and rapists. Rage was not scared one bit, but it did however scar his life. Rage was given probation on a good behvaiour bond, he was out of trouble the whole time he was in their. His weight training in the "hole" was what saved his life and his anus virginity.

He went into indy circuits and was soon signed by President Gabriel. He was signed for his great mike skills and phenomenal wrestling skill. His in ring reading was second to none and his temperament was better than ever before. But Gabriel saw something that he may regret signing Rage for. One night, after his weight training, a janitor bumped into him and did not apologise. Rage flew into, well, uh, Rage? He took the janitor's broom and snapped it over the janitor's head, realising he was breaking his bond, Rage fled from the scene immediately. His inner Rage was to some degree controllable, but is it safe anymore?

One day whilst training with Wood, Rage copped an off target lariat which gave him a bloodied nose, he then lifted Wood above his head and slammed him down hard for a scoop slam onto the turnbuckle. Upon helping Wood up, Woods fist words were, "Wow, Augie, look at you. The Raging Fear, August Monday!" Rage took that in to the back of his mind, realising what he missed was the thrill of competition. But would he ever hit the squared circle again? Only time will tell, he may appear doing what he does best, brawling for life and limb.

Now he just fights. Having been unemployed for a while since Kage, August comes to thReat.
