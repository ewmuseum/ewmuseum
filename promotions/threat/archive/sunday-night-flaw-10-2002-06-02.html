Not Him Again (Part 1)

"Come on X! It would be fun! I mean, I got you the win the other day, didn't I? We could be the best team in thReat! I could see it now, Disciple X and White - Lighting! Guess what the headline would be?! Oh I'll tell you...! 'The Best team in thReat'! Yay!" It was the same voice which startled the 15,000 fans last week on Flaw 9. Although, it came from such a small person. White - Lighting stood there, face to face with thReat superstar, Disciple X.

"No... we wouldn't. Don't you have anything else too do?"

Disciple X began to stroll down the hallway, the little critter following close behind. It was time for DX to prepare for his match, and if for one minute he lost focus, there would be trouble. He sighed, and turned around, coming face to face with White-Lighting, supposingly 'Boy Wonder'.

"Listen kid. You helped me out, yes. ...It's over now. That show was fucking over a week ago, and now, it's over! I appreciate you trying to... well... harrass me and everything, but just please, get the fuck away from me! ...And take your stupid costume with you!" Disciple X muttered out his words, as he spat to the ground.

"...After all that we've been through!" White - Lighting stood there, shocked.

"blah?...." DX was slightly confused, a faint chuckle was heard..

"Is that all you have to say to me X? ...After we spent so much time together! Fine, you know what, I'm leaving now. I'm not even going to look back! Okay?"

"Fine with me...."

White Lighting wipes a tear from his left cheek, while Disciple X covers his mouth, trying not to burst out in laughter. Slowly, he begans to walk down the hallway. Not turning around, like he stated. Disciple X waited there, holding his patience, as White - Lighting was just a inch from turning the corner. ...It never did happen, and it never would.

"Before I go..".

White - Lighting turned around, and began to walk towards Disciple X. He smiled, but the same facial expression given by Disciple X. It was more like a evil grin, a grin which was eyed most often by strangers.

"You want my money or something! Take it! Just... go away!" DX pushed away in misery, trying to get away from White - Lighting, the small, pathetic, White Lighting.

"Please X! I can't leave you so alone...!"

"Trust me, I'll be fine...!"

"I don't know X... it's a tough world out there, people like you need people like me."

Disciple X turned around, he brushed his hair back with his left palm and looked straight into White - Lightings eyes. He was mad, he was pissed off, and he couldn't deal with this shit anymore. ....Or could he?

"You know what... fine! You proved your point kid! You can hang out with me for a bit, but I'm telling you right now, it'll never be forever! You understand?"

"Yeah, Yeah! Wow! Where going to have so much fun together. I planned out the things we can do for the next... couple of monthes, look!" White Lighting's face turned bright, smiling as they began to walk down the hall.

"Geeze, what I do for little kids... dressed in costumes..."

Scene Slowly Faded, as the two entered Disiciple X's lockeroom.

The camera zoomed in on the lone wooden door on the long bare corridor and concentrated on the sign positioned near the top of the door.

It read -- Costume Area.

The sound of smashed glass could be heard.

“I know Im your agent, just calm down. You can’t go out there tonight! What type of impression are you trying to make here?”

The camera edged slightly into the doorway. You could see a tall white man, very clean looking. He was a man with quite a serious look about him, his arms were the size of great pillars, and his shaven head almost glistened in the dim lighting. He was a man of authority that was for sure.

“I got you this job”

There was a long pause.

“What do you want from me?”

“Damn…Would you ever hump off?”

“Fine.”

The large man emerged from the room and shot of at a fast pace down the corridor. Only seconds later another figure emerged from the room. He was nothing like the first man. This is what the term chalk and cheese was made for. He couldn’t have been larger than 5 foot tall. He had scruffy brown hair under what was seemed to be a warrior helmet of some kind and a definite unshaven look. The rest of him was green. He wore a nicely tailored green jacket, green trousers and a nice buckle belt holding them up. His feet were covered with nice black buckled shoes. What a sight he was. He seemed to have bottles of liquor stuffed into his pockets and one attached to his chops.

He stumbled down the corridor seemingly missing the cameramen, swaying from one side to another. At the end of the corridor he headed right towards what looked like the way to the stage area. As he turned the corner he hit what seemed to be an unmoveable object. He crashed to the floor with a thud.

The unmoveable object was none other than former PIW World Champion Joey Starr.

“Watch where your going toilet brush head.”

“What’s with that stupid helmet you got on??? Who do you think you are, Conan or something???

With that he turned off down the corridor, you could hear him chuckling to himself as he went.

“Conan………………..Yeah.”

Steven Solex vs. phYre

"Fuel" played by Metallica hit the sound system, and out came phYre. The fans were quite pleased to see the masked man, as they cheered him down to the ring. Once in the ring, phYre stuck his arms straight into the air, perhaps telling the fans.....he would win. phYre put his arms down, and walked to the corner of the ring, when "Awake' by Godsmack began to play, and the much hated Steven Solex came out to an onslaught of boos. He walked down to the ring taunting the fans. He slid into the ring, and was ready to go.

He ran straight toward phYre who nailed him with a thunderous right hand to the cheek, and down went Solex. The fans jumped to their feet, as the referree began the very early count. 1.....2.....Solex began to roll around the ring, holding the side of his face, in horrible pain. The ref continued....3......4.....5......6.....Solex got up to one knee, much to the displeasure of the fans....7......8.....Solex was up, but very woosy.

A bruise had already began to appear on Solex's face. Solex put up his fists, and phYre began the attack. phYre came in with a huge right hand, but Steven ducked and hit several right hooks to the kidneys of phYre. Steven then followed up with a headlock, and numerous punches to the forehead, until phYre swept out the legs of Solex, who fell hard to the mat. Solex got straight up, however, phYre began hitting him, and hitting him in the head with lefts and rights. Solex, seemingly frustrated, pushed phYre away from him.

This angered phYre who ran at Solex, but only got a hard right hand to the throat for his troubles. phYre was down but not out, as he got up after a count of 5. Solex continued to beat on phYre, with a kick to the shin, followed straight up with a hard overhand right to the nose of phYre. It was apparent that phYre was bleading, as blood dripped down and out of his mask. Yet, Solex still appeared to be much more hurt, as the bruise on his face now began to swell.

phYre played possum, as he walked around the ring. Solex attempted to seize the oppritunity, and ran toward phYre, who tripped Solex. Solex got back up, infuriated, as phYre was waiting. phYre leaned back, and went for the WildphYre, but missed. Solex then grabbed phYre, who was suddenly Caught In The Mix, and went down....hard.

A ten count was ensued, and Solex was victorious. The fans booed, as Steven stuck his arms into the air, and walked out of the ring. The bout had ended.

"I wouldn't go as far as saying that, Scott.." Katanya interuppted, he shot his head towards her, a cold stare glared from his eyes.

"You're right Scott," She corrected, as Araknis returned the mirror, the scene faded to black.

Takisuma Ogama vs. Riot


International Title Match

"Whole" by Flaw began to play as Riot made his way down to the ring. The former Aggression Champion looked around, obviously confident that he would beat Takisuma Ogama tonight. It was given that Riot was cocky, and some times a little overconfident... but he somehow felt in his gut that one man wouldn't leave under his own power tonight. He just assumed it was Ogama. A Japanese Symphony began to play, and as it's haunting melodies made their way through the arena, Takisuma Ogama walked out.

He had his title buttoned around his waist and displayed it proudly to the camera, like he was showing off baby pictures to a total stranger in a crowded airport. He unbuckled it and tossed it to the ground as he slid into the ring. Riot ran over to stomp on Ogama, but Ogama grabbed the leg he was standing on and pulled it from under him. Riot fell over, giving Ogama plenty of time to get up. As Riot tried to get up, Ogama kicked him in the ribs.

Riot fell back once more, and Ogama grabbed both of his legs, pulled them up under his arms and sling shot him out of the ring, sending him crashing to the mats around the ring. Riot got to his feet, and out of nowhere Ogama hit him with an Asai Moonsault.

Ogama got to his feet, walked over to the ring apron, and rummaged around for a little bit, coming out with a steel chair in hand. Riot got to his feet, and turned around just as Ogama swung the chair down. Riot rolled out of the way, causing Ogama to slam the chair into the guardrail. Riot got on his feet and swung a fist hard at Ogama, catching him in the ribs. Ogama dropped the chair as he winced in pain. Riot moved in behind him, picked him up, and tossed him under the top rope, back into the ring. Riot climbed up on the apron, but Ogama was already up.

Ogama suddenly drop-kicked Riot in the face, forcing him back out. Riot braced himself against the guardrail while he stood up, and once more Ogama attempted an Asai Moonsault. This time, however, Riot was prepared, and he sidestepped Ogama, causing him to slam into the railing. Ogama screamed out as Riot rolled into the ring once more. Ogama pulled himself off of the railing and limped to the apron, where Riot grabbed him by the head and pulled him in. Riot stood Ogama up and whipped him into the ropes. As Ogama ran back, Riot ducked his head, obviously trying to backdrop Ogama out of the ring. Again.

Ogama saw it coming and rolled over Riot's back, landing right behind him. Ogama reached under, grabbed Riot's head, and flipped him over onto his back. With Riot looking up, Ogama jumped into the air and leg dropped Riot, forcing the air out of his body. Ogama pulled Riot up and whipped him hard into the turnbuckle. Riot bounced off the turnbuckle a little, but he fell back into it, and then into a seated position. Ogama, seeing an opportunity, ordered a chair be thrown to him. He caught it, ran to the opposite corner from Riot, and then sped towards him. He jumped at the almost last possible moment, and while he was in the air, he put the chair in front of his shoulder.

A flying shoulder block into the corner. With a chair.

Riot's nose busted open, covering the lower half of his face in a crimson mask. Ogama once more picked him up and sent him into the ropes. When Riot came back, Ogama kicked Riot in the right knee. Then he kicked him in the left knee. Riot slumped down, and Ogama took a few steps back, then once more drilled Riot with a double seated drop-kick. Riot flopped backwards, resting so that he was facing up but still laying on his calves. Ogama saw another opportunity. He clapped his hands, turned around, and nailed a perfect Asai Moonsault. Once more he pulled Riot to his feet and whipped him into the ropes. Ogama ran to the opposite set of ropes, coming back with a spinning back elbow. Just as his elbow would have hit Riot, Riot ducked, turned so he was facing Ogama, grabbed him around the waist, and threw him as high and as far as he could, almost out of the ring. Ogama thudded on the mat, writhing around in pain.

Riot walks over to the fallen champion, sneering at him. Riot pulled him to his feet by his hair, kicked him in the stomach, and put his head between his legs. He grabbed Ogama around the stomach and hoisted him to his shoulders, looking for a powerbomb. He turned a few times to disorient Ogama, then dropped him right on his head and neck in the center of the ring. Riot looks out to the crowd as he pulled Ogama to his feet. He knew this was going to be it... he was going to be the next champion. He hooked Ogama for a reverse DDT, the prelude to the Crowd Control. As he did that, a man dressed in a long black coat pushed his way through the crowd and hopped the rail. Security rushed towards him, but he ran right towards the ring, chair in hand.

He hopped up onto the apron and into the ring, just as Riot had gotten Ogama turned. As soon as Ogama's head touched Riot's right shoulder, the man in black tattooed Riot in the face with the chair, denting it. The force of the swing blew open his coat, revealing Araknis in the ring, slamming Riot with a chair. The referee called for the bell as security rushed in and pulled Araknis out of the ring.

He didn't fight back.

Meanwhile, in the ring, Ogama had taken this chance to lock in his finisher, the Ogamacity. Riot was in obvious pain as his face turned purple from oxygen depravation. Ogama released the hold, walked out of the ring and grabbed his belt. Confident in the fact that he had earned this win.
