Biography

[ large unknown chunk ]

One day, while at a pub in Atlanta(Georgia) on May 30 2003, Lawrence Neumeyer came across a man named Vincent Pembridge who provoked him. Dared him into a fight. Lawrence was willing to let it go, before the Scorpion Of Manchester insulted him heavily, marking him as 'another Yank pussy'. That drove Neumeyer over the edge and drew him into the fight with Vincent Pembridge.

The thing was, Lawrence won.

However, police showed up on the scene and raided the pub, mainly because the owner had been using his establishment to push drugs for months. As a result, Lawrence got arrested as a suspect and also because the cops had barged into the pub during the final moments of the fight. Vincent Pembridge somehow managed to escape though, leaving Neumeyer very bitter.

A few hours later, Lawrence was bailed out by a lawyer from a firm known as The Patterson Agency. That lawyer, Norman Ilijah, then invited Lawrence to a safehouse, where Vincent Pembridge awaited with a proposition. Naturally, Neumeyer was confused and demanded to know what kind of scam was being pulled. But after hearing out The Scorpion, Lawrence understood the game.

And took Pembridge up on his offer.

For about a month, Lawrence disappeared. Assumedly, he went to Japan for a fact-finding mission, as well as to train under one of the British Degenerate's friends, a reverred martial artist in East Asia. Neumeyer returned to the States on July 10 2003, fitter and tougher. Armed with the knowledge he was ordered to check out, and also well-trained. His fighting skills, having already been superior before he even met Vincent Pembridge, were labelled by the Brit himself as 'extraordinary'.

Finally, Lawrence Neumeyer was deemed ready to embark on the quest for revenge.
