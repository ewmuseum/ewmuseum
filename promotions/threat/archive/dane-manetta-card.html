Dane Manetta

Real Name: Dane Manetta Also known as: The Pariah

Born: 18th October 1980 Place of Birth: Parma, Italy Hometown: Miami, Florida

Height: 6ft2 Weight: 251lbs Blood type: B+ Eye color: Green

Theme song: The Place Where You Come From, performed by Boy Hits Car

Style

Competing style: Technical Brawler

Signature moves:
Armbar, Arm Breaker, Atomic Drop, Belly to Belly Suplex, Boston Crab, Chop Block, DDT, Fisherman Suplex, German Suplex, Inverted Powerslam, Knee Breaker Drop, Neck Breaker, Piledriver, Reverse DDT, Sitout Powerbomb, Snap Suplex, Texas Cloverleaf.

Flap Jack Gutbuster Drop - The attacker lifts the victim up in the air as if for a back drop but instead of tossing them over, the attacker pushes them up in the air when the victim is horizontal to the mat. The attacker kneels down so the victim lands across one of the attacker's knees as they fall to the mat.

Inverted Face Lock Elbow Drop - The attacker stands behind the victim and applies an inverted face lock. The attacker quickly releases their grip on the face lock and turns 180 degrees towards the victim with their far arm. The attacker drives their elbow into the victim's chest and drives them down to the mat back first.

Leg Sweep Inverted Facelock DDT - The attacker stands behind or to the side of the victim. The attacker applies an inverted face lock on the victim. The attacker wraps their inside leg (that is, if they are applying the face lock with their right arm, they hook the victim's right leg with their right leg). The attacker sweeps out the victim's leg and falls backwards, driving the victim to the mat on the back of their head.

Sweet F*ck You - With his opponent directly in front of him, he kicks him in the stomach before setting him up in a powerbomb position. As he lifts his opponent up, he quickly brings out his knee, and drives his opponents back into his knee.

Finisher: When Dane is facing the victim, Dane catches the victim across their chest so Dane is standing to the side of the victim with their one arm across their chest and under the victim's arms. Then Dane lifts the victim up and falls forward, driving the victim back first to the mat. This can be done as a counter to a charging attacker.
Finisher name: Driven Under

Biography

October 18th, 1980. The place was that of Parma, Italy. Being a city rich with culture, art, tradition and history, one would think that Parma would be the ideal place to raise a young, new born child. The parent’s of young Dane thought otherwise. Little more than a week after Dane's birth, his father flew to the United States of America, leaving Dane in the arms of his mother. Little did little baby Manetta know, he would soon be taking his first plane trip soon after.

---

Being from a family which bled wrestling through many generations, the Manetta family was shocked to hear the news that Dane had found work in the form of The Fighting Zone, a fighting federation that was well known around the world for its brutality and its unique ability to spot some of the top sports entertainment talent around the world. Dane was proud to have been signed to such a well known company, and it as his break into the world of Sports Entertainment.

His father however, now head of a family which was known for producing wrestlers, objected. The way he saw it, Dane had been trained a wrestler, he grew up in a wrestling family… he would be a wrestler, not a fighter. Fortunately for him, TFZ mysteriously closed no more than a few days before Dane was scheduled to debut. Dane’s family saw this as a blessing, as Dane gained a little something extra to his repertoire having trained within the company for a couple of months.

Dane took his knew found fighting knowledge and shoved it straight into the mix, wrestling a few low key Indy shows before being hand picked by a man named Troy Mason to become part of a promotion that was rising from the ashes.

thReat

thReat stood to be Dane’s first real foray into mainstream wrestling, and he proved to become an instant hit with the fans after defeating Damon West in his debut match. Bad and good times followed as thReat struggled to stay afloat over its 7 months existence, but Dane fought on through feuds with Chris Pinash and Rejection. It was at the final thReat PPV that Dane finally defeated the previously unbeatable Rejection, but unfortunately, Dane was seen as being responsible for an injury that Rejection suffered during the match.

Dane’s thReat tenure then came to an end as he was told his contract was not to be renewed, much to his upset. thReat however soon closed and news of Dane’s departure never made it to the press.

The move by thReat officials to brand him responsible and terminate his contract left Dane feeling betrayed by people he had looked up to, for letting him get a start at his career. Dane was bitter because he felt in thReat he had been forced to act a way he didn't want to, a way he wasn't comfortable with. Dane's attitude towards work and wrestling in general took a new turn.

With his new attitute Dane was contacted by hWo, a prodominantly hardcore based promotion. Dane looked at this as a way of vending his frustrations, and getting to swing his beloved steel chair around at a few heads. It wasn't before long that Dane made an impact on hWo, winning the Impulse championship after defeating three men on one night to reign victorious.
