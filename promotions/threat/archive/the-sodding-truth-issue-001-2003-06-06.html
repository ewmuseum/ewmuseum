The Sodding Truth [Issue 001]
June 06 2003

Hello cunts,

You lot are probably wanting to know why I did whatever the bloody hell I did. Firstly, holding back the online transcript of the Malevolence show, then removing the sodding main-event from the transcript. And finally, changing the passwords to the website so that all you Internet junkies who are too bloody poor to have bought the show would have been crying at your computer tables.

Bloody pathetic, you lot are.

Some might say that I'm a bleeding sore loser, for doing all that. Well, feck you, gits. That was a horrendous ending to a match that I would have eventually won on behalf of The Vagabonds. I simply had to spare you people the atrocity of the match, and hence, did everything in my power to prevent you blokes from reading it.

I mean, look at the facts. Xaxon is an old tard. How long more could the twat have gone on for, eh? With everything he had thrown at me, I soaked it up and frustrated the cunt. No matter what he did, I simply dug deep down and kept the fighting dream alive. I was simply using his offense as a way to build the adrenaline inside me, and once his feeble body ran out of steam, I would have pounced on the chance to finish the bugger off.

Alas, it is all over. But contrary to popular belief, I am not a sore loser. I don't have much to be sore about, in actual fact. Because, cunts, you all have overlooked one thing.

In defeat, I have won.

I won't say anything more than that. Use your tiny little brains and figure that out for yourselves, you bloody arselickers. Me, a sore loser? Bollocks. Troy Mason is the bigger loser than me, and no matter what the result at Malevolence would have turned out to be, the ramifications for him would be massive.

Let's review, shall we? Xaxon headbutted the shite out of him. Ty Hughes basically walks away scot-free because the contract he was signed to was under the name El Crackerooni and under that moniker, Ty was only contracted to the company until Malevolence. Christian Embers has apparently retired, and there isn't a bloody clause in the contract that prevents anyone from retiring. In which case, Embers GETS money instead of losing money from 'walking out' on his contract.

Bobby Knickerson was signed as a janitor, and his bleeding contract was never upgraded to that of a fighter's or a wrestler's. In any case, Knickerson is with me now, as the young lad lacks direction in life. So, Troy, who are you left with? Simple; myself and Ampske.

You can keep Ampske. As for me, I'm bloody rich. I don't care how much money I lose from walking out on this sorry promotion, because I can jolly well earn it back very easily. ACW's paying me large sums of money due to the contract Ethan Winters signed me to, before his untimely demise. And guess what? The contract's bulletproof.

See, Troy, I always have contingency plans. Unlike you, I think of the future. And the future of thReat is, for now, all in your hands. I'll still be stopping by every now and then, due to the matter of several business decisions I made during my brief time in charge having to be fulfilled.

Other than that, I have nothing to say about my assailant at Malevolence, and I have nothing to say about Xaxon defeating me to gain the victory. All I have to say, Troy, is one very simple thing.

Have fun with the old bugger.

-VP
