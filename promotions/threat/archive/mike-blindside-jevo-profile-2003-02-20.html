Biography Chapter I - In The Beginning:

It all started on a stormy night, on July the 29th, the year of 1974, the city of New Orleans. A child by the name of Michael Vincent had arrived, born to Pauline and Greg Vincent. The time was exactly 4:54 A.M, the child being exactly 6 pounds and 8 ounces. Yeah, they named him Michael Gregory Vincent. Being named Michael after his deceased uncle, and the name Gregory coming from his father’s full name. His blood type, O Positive, and his eye color, green. A normal child indeed, and a beautiful one at that. Who was this child? It was me.


Chapter II - A Living Hell:

By the age of 2, I began to adapt to the environment in which I lived in. A 3 bedroom complex with my mother, father, and older siblings.. one brother, and one sister. Just on the North side of New Orleans, where the town never slept. Drugs, Sex, Scandal... 3 perfect words to describe the North of New Orleans, a place where many would rather not reside. Too feeble of a child to understand, or know what was happening around me, my mother being a drug addict and my father being an alchoholic never struck me once. But, soon enough as I grew older and older, I would be quite compatible of everything concurring in my household, including the abuse from my father to my mother.


Chapter III - New Horizons:

By the time Kindergarten had arrived a 2nd year, I was actually in preparation to go. Now, exactly 6 years old, I had everything I'd need via hand me downs, and my grandparents buying me school supplies. My un-caring parents unfortunately made me miss my original Kindergarten year, when I was actually 5 years old, but I didn’t give a fuck. I’d looked at schools on the big screen in the living room, and my brother Shawn had told me a lot about school. He was in the 5th grade, and my sister was in the 4th grade, which made it capable for me to actually know some one at the school. When first attending school, I immediately didn’t adapt to some bitch, who I was not even related too being able to tell me what to do. Numerous times, when she’d piss me off, I’d mumble swear words at her that I had heard around the house from my parents and siblings. Of course, one of my classmates would tell on me and get me time out. I’d kick their ass afterwards though, so it never really mattered. Yes, the woman’s name was Miss Slovak, and I’ll never forget that wench either.


Chapter IIII - Payback:

Two years later, now in the 2nd grade, I’d gotten used to school. It was fairly easy too, and I began to find it as my turn-around from that hellhole of a home. In fact, I’d picked up a friend through my years of going to Shadyside Elementary School. His name was Jason Jennings, we called him Pallie most of the time. He had just moved to New Orleans earlier in the school year. I was the head of the 2nd graders, their bully if so to say. But, one thing they didn’t know that only Jason knew, was that after hours at the home of mines.. I was the one being bullied. My asshole of a brother, Shawn, was always kicking my ass every single night at home for no particular reason. But what could I really do? Me, being 8 years old, and him being 12 years old. But, Shawn took it too far after he pushed Jason out of a tree, breaking his arm.. Shawn denied it though, of course. I had to get him back, the ultimate payback. So, I stole my teacher’s, Mr. Grover’, pet snake and took it home. I put it on my brother’s chest that night while he slept, and stood at the door with a camera in hand. He woke up, saw that snake, and screamed his fucking head off. He pissed his pants too, it was hilarious. Sure, he beat me up afterwards, and my dad killed Mr. Wiggles (the Snake) and condemned me to my room. But, at least I had gotten my Payback.. with a picture to prove it.


Chapter V - Death:

By time I was 11 years old, I’d permanently turned my back on father. Now older and smarter, I realized he was nothing but shit. Recently, last year, I’d been placed in my correct grade. I was now in the 6th grade. My mother’s drug habits had gone further, to the point that I could not even stand being around her. Instead, I lived most of my time with my grandparents, who cared more about me than anyone and also made sure I got to school every day. But, something horrible happened. My grandfather, who owned and worked at a steel mill.. died. It was the most horrible thing to ever happy in my life. The most influential man in my young life, was dead. What was more fucked up about it, is that my father showed zero remorse to the death of my grandfather. In fact, the drunk banned me to my room the day of my grandfather’s funeral, instilling even more anger inside me. After I’d been let out of my prison, I witnessed once more my father beating my mother, and this was the last straw. I grabbed my brother’s baseball bat out of the broom closet, and before I knew it, I was beating my father half to death, getting back at him finally. Afterwards, my mother knelt over him crying. My brother and sister both looked at me, as if I were some god or something. Only, I didn’t realize I had earned their respect. I was now the new man of the house.. for now.


Chapter VI - The Stepdad:

James Corliss, a.k.a my mother’s drug source soon moved into our house, recognized as our mother’s new boyfriend. I hated him right off the bat, and so did my siblings. His petty attempts at trying to be our father, only angered us all even more. It hurt me to watch my stupid mother accept heroine, and other drugs from him every night. Now 12, I knew more about the world than most children my age, and I knew this Corliss guy was a bad seed. Soon enough, just like my father, Corliss began to beat on my mother. Numerous times, my 16 years old brother Shawn tried to defend our mother.. only for Corliss to beat him down. This man was pure fucking shit, and I wouldn’t take his bullcrap anymore. When my father had lived with us, he had a gun hidden at the top of him and my mother’s dressing closet. One night, I saw Corliss continuing on with his wrong doings, now for about the 4th time, trying to have sex with my 15 year old sister Jessica. I took action. I held that cold pistol in my hands, shaking, and begged Corliss off. He did not listen. So, I shot his ass.. and in all humour, I shot the man straight in his fucking right buttcheek. I watched him fall over in pain. That was the end of Corliss, so we though.. our mother had a choice to choose either him, or us, and to our suprise.. she chose her drug source.


Chapter VII - Mardi Gras:

Shawn, Jessica, and I moved with our widow grandmother. The best of our lives were with her, and I grew up living with her for the rest of my life. Four years later, now 16, and living with my grandmother alone after my siblings left to college, I went into a life of trouble. I joined a gang, me and my best friend Jason, called ‘The Nodj Boyz.’ Hanging out late, smoking weed, drinking, stealing, girls, no one telling us what to do.. the gang scene was my kind of life. The gang did everything together. We were a huge fucking family. Our biggest plan to cause trouble, was the night of Mardi Gras, in which we planned to hotrod a few cars, and steal them. That night though, I met the most beautiful girl I had ever seen in my life. She was 16 years old, just as myself, and her name was Sara Summers. Just her period made me want to just run away with her, and leave that gang. We talked for hours, even through the loud celebration, and we then retired by talking with one another on her porch after I had walked her home. I got her number, and that was that. This was the girl I wanted to spend the rest of my life with.


Chapter VIII - Goodbye:

Two years later, now 18, and out of school and the gang I was in, I planned to go to a distant city and live with Sara and we did. My grandmother paid for the first class airline tickets for me and Sara to Yonkers, NY and we left. We had enough money saved to rent out a small apartment, and we rented out a two bedroom home on the Westside of Yonkers. I picked up a job, working at a local Best Buy store, and Sara began working at a Hollywood Video store. Our big life in the big city had begun, and nothing could hurt it. One night though, things all changed for me after I was mugged by four men. After that, I began attending classes to help me learn to defend myself better on the streets of New York. After a while, I had picked it up perfectly, and mastered all manuevers of defense. In fact, one of the people in my classes, who told me he was an agent for a distant independant wrestling promotion called ICWF told me I could be a great prospect to the federation. I considered, and for two weeks attended ICWF’s weekly shows and training classes. After a few months, now 19 years old, I felt I was ready to wrestle and I went into the ring a week later renounced as Michael “The Man” Vincent. After a few matches, all wins, I was actually getting my name out into the air also becoming a fan favorite. But, after letting my emotions get the best of me against an opponent who I didn’t like because of backstage problems, I hit him with a few deadly punches to the jaw that weren’t actually sanctioned in the match. This resulted in his firing back, and ending in an on-air actual fight which angered most, and in a sense boosted ratings. After one last punch, I rendered the guy unconscious, and got the full blame for what happened. That same night, I got a call from the Owner stating I was fired, and that was it.


Chapter VIIII - A Second Chance:

A year since I’d been let off my contract in ICWF, now married to Sara, and a worker of a law firm, I recieved a miraculous call from some one one night. He was none other than Jason Jennings, my best friend from my lifetime in New Orleans. He called telling me how he had saw me wrestle for ICWF, and heard about what happened. He told me he had gone pro, and had been wrestling for about a year now in a federation called Old-School Championship Wrestling (OCW). He also stated he was going the alias XTC, and that they were looking for young and new talent. He told me the place where the shows and what not happened was at some theatre in the Red Light District. I realized this could be my big break, and I went down to the OCW theatre. I immediately signed up with OCW, and the federation involved me in a storyline with a mid-carder in the federation by the name of Sean Angst. The beginning of the storyline involved me attacking him during his International Title Match. After the attack, the fans noted me as ‘The Bad Guy’ of OCW, and referred to the attack as “The Blindside.” This brought up my alias, Mike “Blindside” Jevo. After a few months the character got over with the fans, and I became not only one of the highest paid wrestler’s in OCW soon enough, but a main eventer as well. After a year in the business, I proclaimed the name as the OCW ‘Rookie of the Year.’ Six years later, at the age of 27, I retired from OCW after a long record of title wins, and awards. I left the mark behind as being recognized as one of the best tag-team members in OCW, forming my tag-team Original Sin with XTC, and also landed myself as being ‘The Best’ to ever be in OCW, and a spot in the OCW Hall of Fame. Yeah, I had done it all, and I was done with wrestling for good.. so I figured.


Chapter X - The Final Chapter:

After a year of retirement, at the age of 28, with two children and still married to Sara Summers-Vincent, I felt as if life couldn’t be any better. But, I knew it wasn’t as good as I pretended it to be. I loved to entertain people, and I loved to work my ass off for millions of people each night.. I loved being the main headliner of each event. I wasn’t prepared to retire, but I did anyways and now it was time for my big return. It was time for Mike Jevo to get back into the business, and do what I did best. I knew it would be a long, fiersome road to make it back to the top, but I was willing to do whatever it took. I was willing to do anything to get back to the top of the mountain.. Yeah, I’m ready to do whatever it takes.. I just hope the mountain is ready for me.
