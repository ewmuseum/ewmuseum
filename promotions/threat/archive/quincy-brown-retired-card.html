Quincy Brown

Born: 12/16/1979 Place of Birth: Brooklyn, NY Hometown: Philadelphia, PA

Height: 6'4 Weight: 245 Eye color: Brown

Manager: Trent Weathers (doesn't come to ring/cage) Trainer: Trent Weathers

Theme song: Fight Club - Fat Joe, M.O.P., Petey Pablo

Style

Competing style: Boxing/Brawler

Finisher name: Trip Downtown

Historical Info

Other/Previous promotions: theSTORM, ECLIPSE

Other titles won: current thReat Fighting Champion Other awards won: Philadelphia Daily News Golden Gloves Winner, New York Daily News Golden Gloves Runner-Up

Biography

Quincy Brown is an ex-amatuer boxer, who fought in the New York Daily News Golden Gloves before getting into some trouble with the law after beating up his opponent following a lose by decision in the finals. He would spend six months on Rikers Island not being able to put on any boxing gloves, and needing to stay out of trouble in order to even have a shot at getting inside another boxing ring. However Quincy was banned from competition in New York, which led to him heading out to Philly.

In Philadelphia Q finally captured a Golden Gloves title, but many professional boxing promoters felt that his technic wasn't good for pro boxing, so Quince would once again abandon the boxing gloves, this time by choice, to fight on the streets of Philadelphia for money, against anyone who felt they had what it takes to be tough on the mean streets. This would eventually lead his trainer, Trent Weathers to show him tapes of various fighting federations like the Asylum, CLUB and TFZ, and had got a tip from some reliable sources about a new fighting fed called theSTORM, and let QB know that he could make a 'killing' in his new profession.

The only thing Quincy knew about growing up was how to fight. A punch here, and kick there and every now and then, find the nearest object and hit the person with it. In the beginning it was out of fear, and due to the fact that Q was smaller than everyone else in his grade, and was frequently picked on, but the growth spurt would soon arrive, and a vengeful Quincy would use it to his full advantage.

After holding his own in Elementary and Intermediate School, Quince would not be the bullied, but the bullier in High School. This would lead to Q getting expelled from school and eventually landed him a juvenile detention center where a fellow by the name of Trent Weathers would work with him, and with his help Quincy was on his way out of the detention center and headed for The Brooklyn Zoo, Weathers' boxing academy.

QB would just demolish the other students in sparring sessions, even without the proper technique of a boxer, and after 'convincing' Trent that the time was right, Quincy would land himself a spot in the New York Daily News Golden Gloves Tournament.

Quince would dismantle opponent after opponent on his way to what seemed to be The Golden Gloves in the 200 pound division at the tender age of 19.

However controversial judges, atleast in Q's eyes, gave the finals and the Golden Gloves to his opponent, a no-name in boxing after his win over Mr. Brown.

The reason this person is a no-name is because Quincy Brown attacked him after the fight,during the post match press conference after some comments directed his way, and after this display of poor sportsmanship Quincy landed himself in Rikers Island for six months, and his opponent laid in a hospital bed, yet to awaken.

This is the act that got Quincy Thomas Brown banned from the New York Daily News Golden Gloves.

Then upon his release from Rikers Island, and not donning boxing gloves for what seemed like years, Trent set Quince up with a place in Philadelphia, allowing QB a second chance at becoming a Golden Gloves Champion.

In that very event, Quincy would once again dismantle all who faced him, and this time he left no doubt about who the Golden Gloves champion was going to be, by knocking out his opponent in the finals.

The professional promoters didn't feel that QB had what it took to make it in the pro ranks.

Fed up with the critics, Quincy Brown took it back to 'the streets' and fought any and all comers on the streets of Philly, making a pretty decent living for himself.

Finally after much thought, and wanting to keep Quincy out of trouble, Trent Weathers figured that if QB was going to fight in the streets of Philly to earn a living, he might as well show him the tapes he had collected from such promotions as Asylum, CLUB and TFZ.

Quincy would study these tapes and would always say that one day he would be an Asylum champion, but Trent had other plans, what is that plan?

These plans led Quincy to theSTORM and a shot at their Hardcore Title.

He would dismantle his no-name first round opponent, but the business side of things in theSTORM weren't right for Quincy Brown, and there was no fame coming from being there. And that's when Trent got a phone call.

It was from a member of ECLIPSE Fighting, he wanted Quincy Brown in the federation and held out of signing his contract until Quincy Brown was made a member of the roster. Details were worked out between Trent and the anonymous caller, and eventually money from this person was given to Trent and then Quincy Brown.

An ECLIPSE Fighting contract soon followed, but it was to be short lived as the fed experienced some problems after it's first show as well, and Quincy Brown was once again left unemployed.

This led QB to attempting to get the name of his 'backer', but Trent knew he couldn't do it especially since he's getting paid quite a bit of hush money, as well as a reliable source for openings in the 'sports entertainment' and 'fighting' worlds.

This would come into play as Trent was directed by his 'source' to thReat, a fed that plans on having a wrestling division and a fighting division.

So now QB is here, and he plans to not make this federation one and done, just his opponents.

Prepare for a 'Trip Downtown', and that's not a thReat, it's a PROMISE!

And it all started with Amp Canon. A bigot, a cracker, a person that even white people hated. On camera Canon had defeated QB, but off it Quincy got the upper hand.

Canon didn't feel that way though, and soon the two were fighting in another match a few weeks later. This match would escalate into an all out war, with Q getting the W.

After battles with Jakks and phYre, both men would soon find themselves in the Alamodome battling for the thReat Fighting Championship. It was a night like no other. QB came in full of energy and would defeat Amp Canon once and for all; but there was a problem.
