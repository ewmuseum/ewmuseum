Biography

Nathan Brady was born in a local hospital, found somewhere in Apollo, Pennsylvania to his parents, David and Marie Brady.

The story begins on February 13th 1980, the time and moment where Nathan was finally born into this world. After an intense thirteen hours of labour, David and Marie had their first 'blessing' from the man in the sky... a young boy. The creation of Nathan had been something in the making for the last few years, although he was not the first try of the couple. In fact, Nathan entered his family as the forth child... the other three, all members of the female population.

David Brady was an old-school 'Professional Wrestler', working the independent circuit as a mid-card name. He'd been a success on some of the territories, playing his trade as a callous heel who took revenge on all those who opposed his self-proclaimed right to being the 'Champion' of whichever organization he chose to work for. David was a good man at heart though... but wanted one thing from his wife... one thing that he felt, would make his life complete and allow his tradition to be carried on for more generations.

Sure, a little girl might not have been the worst thing in the world to have... but to Brady, he didn't think that a woman's place was inside the ring and to be honest, he didn't want to be remembered for having this 'girl' as his 'future' mark on the 'Sports Entertainment' industry. So, when his hope was brought a little step further with Nathan's birth, you could only imagine the kind of joy that was surging through the veins of his father!

It was so much, that David would take Nathan to his independent shows and allow his fellow wrestlers to have a look at his son and hold him. For a short while, Nathan was the talk of the locker room... and all of this being accomplished before the age of fourteen months!

As Nathan began to progress, his father's workload began to lower... all the way down to the point where David felt it would be better for him to help Nathan develop as a child and a person in general... before of course, settling him on the road that he too had taken in his early teens.

It was Nathan's 13th Birthday that his father chose to reveal to him the plans and ideals that he wanted his son to follow. The very moment of his birthday, that these goals and ideas had been wedged into the mind of a fragile youngster. He had no say in this matter... not even in the sense of 'accepting' this life-plan that had been laid out for him already.

However... despite this, at the age of sixteen, after three years of running down this path of being a 'wrestler', Nathan couldn't take the pressure anymore and muttered the heart-breaking decision to his father.

"... Dad, I know you've spent the last few years trying to get me running down this street like you did... and really, I appreciate everything that you've done for me, but... I don't want to be this wrestler that YOU want me to be. I want to be able to have my own life... with my own choices made by me and nobody else."

David was torn apart by this statement. He'd thought... well, assumed that he son would have wanted to be the same way as him. Unfortunently, that wasn't going to be the case, for either member of the family. It was at this point, that David flew into a blind rage... unable to control the pure emotion that was running through his battle-worn body. In a freak showing of frustration and strength, David battered his only son... throwing him around their gym like he was something of a little rage doll.

Nathan was placed into hospital for three months, his injuries forcing him into a coma.

"... Oh My God... I'm so sorry son, I didn't mean to do what I did to you. I was just so hurt that you didn't want to be a wrestler like m-..."

Those were the words uttered to Nathan by his father, minutes before he awoke from his three-month coma. Those were the words that David felt would make everything right... make everything the way that it should have been.

... but, it only made things worse between father and son.

David chose to carry on with his own business as his son plunged himself into debt, dicing with death... the only route that he thought would save him from the pain of having to relive that savage beating. Nathan started to use illegal substances by the time he was seventeen and quickly became 'hooked' on weed. He would smoke every day, hoping that something would come along and strike him dead.

Be it a debt-collector... be it a junkie looking for some gear... or the almighty God himself.

At eighteen, Nathan decided to try and condition his body again... and even start the whole route of 'Professional Wrestling' once more. At the time of his choice, he had, for some reason, decided that kicking his 'addiction' and getting back into physical shape would be the only way he could go. It'd been long enough since he broke communication from his family... and even longer since he'd thrown away his fathers dreams of having a son in the business.

At the age of twenty-one, Nathan finally broke through his 'Wrestling Training School', working a few dates with some of the indy federations his father had worked for years before.

The fans could see who he was...
The wrestlers could see what he wanted...
Nathan Brady could see what he had become...

A Professional Wrestler... with a desire and skill to push through as many barriers as he could possibly handle. His ego began to develop from his point, and as the smaller wrestling sheets started to recognize him... Nathan claimed himself to be the best that Wrestling was going to have in the future. He claimed that he was the 'Future of Wrestling'... the man that would save it from the point of extinction.

Would that mean that Nathan would become somebody Hellbent on making it known that a single performance would save even the most bankrupt of companies?

Unfortunently, No...

... but what it did mean though, was that Nathan Brady was beginning to discover his own personal talents. On paper, he sounded like the absolute machine, with a brain that many wrestlers wished was theirs... but in reality, with an empty tray....

The Journey, had only just begun...
