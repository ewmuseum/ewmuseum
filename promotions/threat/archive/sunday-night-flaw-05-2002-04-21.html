Finders Keepers

It was his first time, his first time in a big time promotion. For the first time, CashnMoney had the chance to gain the money he wanted when he began this wrestling life. CashnMoney stepped out of the new lavish limousine acquired from Jon Hackett. Wearing only jeans, and a wife beater, the massive monster began walking.

A man, neatly dressed in a navy blue suit, walked up to CashnMoney.

Hello, Mr. CashnMoney. My name is Chris White, and I’ll be showing you around this place., he explained.

"Where’s my locker room?, said CashnMoney in a deep voice.

"Right this way sir." Chris White began walking, while CashnMoney followed closely behind him. They arrived in a narrow hallway.

"This is the entrance to the ring area. The curtains are right there." Chris explained.

They began walking again. They pasted a corridor, which lead to the hallway with the locker rooms. They past the first door, closest to the ring entrance, and kept walking.

"This my locker room?" questioned CashnMoney.

"No, yours is the one at the end." Chris White said.

"No, this one is mine." declared CashnMoney.

"I’m sorry Mr. CashnMoney; your locker room is the one at the end of this hallway."

CashnMoney grabbed Chris White by the back of the head, and threw him into the wall, face first. Chris White laid there bleeding from his nose. CashnMoney kicked the door open. He then took the name off of the door, and threw it to the floor.

The name read, Lennox Dawson.

"Your just one opponent, through many. I've already caught your key elements, and your weakness. I'm the bigger man, and your the fish. Soon, you'll be hooked."

The door slammed shut, as the scene faded.

Xaxon VS Avo Chavez

Age, not a factor. Size. Most definitely. Xaxon, perhaps the most experienced man in the wrestling industry today was set to face Avo Chavez for the second time in as many matches he’s had in thReat. Burdened by the lack of speed and strength to his opponents, his knowledge is equivalent. It all balances out.

Anger leads to failure. Failure leads to destruction. Avo Chavez, full of angst and hatred, is ready to unleash on the bigger, wiser man in Xaxon. His "manager" was brutally...bitten...three weeks ago in the last time these two men met. Would Avo triumph over carnivorous warfare? Can Avo even reach the top of the pole?

"Highway Star" by Deep Purple filled the arena. Cheers rang through the crowd as Xaxon stepped onto the stage. The crowd jumped out of their seats and cheered for the veteran. The German Wonder walked down the ramp and stepped in slowly as if not to hurt himself.

He’s fragile.

Avo Chavez’s theme rang through the ears of the screaming thReat fanatics. The cheers quickly died down as the fans began to sit down. Avo Chavez didn’t care. All he wanted was his manager. Rocco the Taco. Avo slid into the ring with a purpose.

The pole was set up. Rocco the taco hung 15 feet above the ring canvas. Avo stared up at it, wondering how he could get up there. Avo quickly dashed up the turnbuckles trying to get up the pole and snatch a huge upset, but Xaxon threw him off. Avo fell on his back in the center of the ring and Xaxon, as a traditional competitor, didn’t attempt to go for the quick victory, but to actually hold the match.

Xaxon picked Avo up and irish whipped him and Avo flew under the top rope and out of the ring. A surprised look filled Xaxon’s wrinkled face. Xaxon got out of the ring slowly and looked down.

"’Vhere are you Avo..."

Xaxon ran around the next ring post.

BAM

Avo connected with a jumping clothesline to the neck of Xaxon, sending him down. Avo jumped on the apron but much to his dismay, he was pulled back down by the back of his tights. Xaxon then rolled Avo back inside the ring. He followed. Xaxon hooked Avo in a suplex position and landed and inverted suplex. This took more of an effect on Xaxon, knocking the wind out of him.

They both lay motionless on the mat until Xaxon was able to make it to one knee. Avo was already up and kneed Xaxon in the mouth. Avo whipped Xaxon into the ropes and executed a dropkick to the stomach of Xaxon as he flew back. Avo began stomping on Xaxon. Avo then headed to the turnbuckles. He got to the top but lost his footing and racked himself on the top rope.

That HAD to hurt.

Rocco was all the way on the other side of the ring...awaiting a rescuer. Avo got off the top rope and walked dizzily in the ring. The now upright Xaxon, punched Avo in jaw. Not once, not twice, three times, DDT. The crowd roared! A move such as that gets the crowd in a frenzy. Building adrenaline and motivation. Something that fuels your victory fire.

Elbow drop, by elbow drop, Xaxon gained more of an advantage. Xaxon then began his trek to the pole. He got on the middle rope and reached up then suddenly grabbed his back. Maybe a cramp or something. He couldn’t continue up. Avo lay down but turned to Xaxon and smiled.

"Old bastard."

Xaxon got down from the ropes and saw Avo charging towards him. Xaxon side-stepped him, grabbed by the back of his tights and threw him into the pole.

Rocco fell to the ground...so did Avo. The throw strained Xaxon as he fell to the mat as well. There lay the taco. Nearly in the center of the ring. Both men down. Avo crawled closer. Xaxon did too. Closer, and Closer, and Closer. Xaxon was almost there but lost his strength and his hand knocked the Taco out of the ring.

Avo got to his feet and walked to the ropes, about to get out, holding his back. Xaxon was now up, and behind Avo. He held him from behind and gave him a German suplex...how fitting. This took everything Xaxon had. Xaxon rolled over and fell out of the ring...right next to Rocco.

He laid his weak hand on top of the soft shell manager, and grasped it. The bell rang and the crowd erupted. The match was over with age and experience winning this battle.

 

And you are?

Lennox Dawson strolled backstage after arriving to the arena. He walked around, looking for his lockerroom with his bags over his right shoulder.

CRACK

Lennox Dawson looked down to see that he had stepped on the nameplate that CashnMoney had threw to the ground. HIS nameplate. Lennox Dawson's face looked a bit frustrated as he opened the door. CashnMoney was tieing his boots up with his back to the door. The door slammed against the wall and CashnMoney turned around slowly.

"Can I help you?"

"Who the hell are you?", quizzed Lennox.

"CashnMoney. Get used to it."

"Well you're in the wrong locker room, CashnMoney."

"That's where you're wrong. This is MY locker room now."

"Did you not hear me?", asked Lennox in anger.

Lennox threw his bags down and threw CashnMoney out of his chair. A brawl broke out in the locker room. Punches and kicks flew around as the heat was building between these two in a matter of minutes. A security squad broke up the fight.

"Fuck, Lennox!"

"Me and you! Tonight!", yelled the restrained Lennox.

"You're on!"

The camera fades as the two superstars were escorted away.

Disciple X falls over once again, and Araknis picks him up and rolls him back into the ring.

Araknis crouches down, putting his hands in front of him, like he's waiting to strike. Disciple X staggers to his feet, and Araknis kicks at his head once more.

But Disciple X caught it.

He pulled in Araknis, grabbed him around the waist, and hoisted him up once more. This time, however, he dumps Araknis over the top rope and onto the floor. Disciple X does a little taunting to get the crowd into it, and they explode. Araknis struggles to get to his feet, but manages to. He rolls under the bottom rope, and Disciple X lets him go past him. Araknis gets to his feet once more, and the two men met in the center of the ring with a collar and elbow tie up. Disciple X got the upper hand, and pulled Araknis off the ground. He held him up for a few moments, then dropped him face first on the mat. Araknis flopped around the ring for a little while, until Disciple X grabbed him and wrapped him up for the pinfall.

Something to Discuss Pt. 1

"I'm coming with you."

That is the sound of an argumentive woman, and to be more specific, that is the sound of Katanya Lee, the wife of thReat wrestler, Araknis. The cameras finally faded in, Araknis stood in his casual wear clothes, apparently he didn't plan on wrestling again tonight, and there was a reason for that A reason he'd like to discuss with thReat's Owner, on a unscheduled basis, a meeting that will discuss not only who they had the audicity to book him against, but also how they've treated him since he got here.

"No, I'll take care of this myself," Araknis assured her, "I'm just going to...talk."

"Who do you think you're trying to fool, Scott? I'm not the girl you knew back when you suddenly decided to visit Japan, to see...." She stopped, Araknis turned around and faced her, a "Don't say It' look graced his face, but she decided to continue anyway, "Is this what this is about, Scott? Your father?"

"This has nothing to do with him, this has to do with me, getting what's due." Araknis said sternly, correcting whatever false accusations she may have formulated in her mind.

"You're lying."

"Am I?" He asked, "Maybe I am, I could be, and since I'm lying on that, maybe I lied when I said 'I do.' Maybe..maybe.." He looked into her eyes, she had already started to shed tears, he lowered his head sadly, disgraced at what he had the nerve to say, "Look, I'm sorry."

"I know," She turned around, fully prepared to walk away.

"Where are you going?" He asked, concerned that she may not have accepted his apology.

"Back to the locker room, go and find Hackett, afterall you do have something important to tell him.." she walked away, exiting down into the bowels of the arena. Turning his back, Araknis continued his way towards Hackett's office.

"Right, something to discuss." He said under his breath, as the scene faded away to black.

Riot kicked out, almost sending Krone out of the ring again.

Both men get to their feet and charge at each other. Krone goes for another spinning heel kick, but Riot sidesteps it and clotheslines him. Krone falls hard, and Riot, not missing a beat, pulled him back up. He whipped Krone hard into the opposite corner, and followed him, smashing his body into Krones. Krone falls face first out of the turnbuckle, and Riot drops an elbow on his back. Riot gets back to his feet and pulls Krone to his feet. He puts one arm over his shoulders, and he puts the other under Krone's right knee. RIot then lifted him up as high as he could and held him there for a few moments... then he threw him across the ring. Krone landed in the center of the ring, and started to get up, when Riot ran over and kicked him in the chest, forcing him back down. Riot climbed on Krone's chest and began punching him in the head, but Krone rolled over and got on top of Riot. He got to his feet and kicked Riot in the throat, which put him on his back. Krone got to his feet and looked like he was about to put Riot away when a Japanese symphony began to play. The haunting music confused Krone, who turned his attention away from the slowly rising Riot to the entryway. Suddenly, Ogama came sprinting down with a chair in hand. Krone got spun around by Riot, who is subsequently whipped into the ropes. Krone turns around again, and this time, the Japanese man is right in his face. He swings the chair, but Krone tumbles out of the way. The man, however, catches Riot flush in the face, which is ironic. Riot falls over in a heap, and the man once more turns his attention to Krone. Krone executes a picture-perfect standing sidekick, which forces the man out of the ring. Krone turns his attention to Riot once more. He lifts him to his feet and kicks him below the belt. Riot doubled over and Krone lifted Riot up onto one shoulder, like he's going for a shoulder breaker. Instead, he jumped into the air as high as he could, and spiked Riot's head into the mat, knocking him out. Krone made the cover.
