Have You Seen Him? pt 1 ft. Katanya Lee

"Have you seen my husband?"

"No ma'am, I haven't."

Once again, the fans are given a view of the backstage area that hides beneath the glitter and lights of the current pay per view extravaganza, Reload. Katanya Lee, the wife of Scott Lee aka Araknis sits down ontop of a stranded chai. Running her fingers through her long jet black hair, that glistened under the roof lights, she breathes heavily, letting a sudden rush of air escape her lungs.

"Where the hell is he?" She mumbled to herself, "He said he would leave earlier than I did, but yet I'm already here, with him no where to be found.."

Her husband was involved in a very vital match up tonight, his chance against Riot for the Aggression Championship, something he had most deserved over the past few weeks. However, if you had asked her, she would be the first to tell you that, this wasn't what crossed her mind at the moment, in fact, it was the farthest.

Was he okay?

Where was he?

Why wasn't he here?

Those were questions that rushed at her, but she managed to maintain, standing to her feet once more, ready to continue her search, and even though she had asked these men and women once before, she was more than willing to ask them again, because it's not very hard to miss a man, covered in red/white/black makeup..

..Right?

Fin.

"Awake" by Godsmack began to play as Steven Solex made his way to the ring to little fan fare. This was Solex's debut match, which just happened to be on a pay per view. Monday rolled under the swing and came back up with a leg sweep. He grabbed the bat and swung down, smacking Solex hard in the ribs. Solex jumped off the mat, shaking, turning in midair. When Solex came back, Monday swung the bat at his midsection, but Solex spun to the side and evaded the blow. Monday turned slightly to get a look at Solex, and Solex spun and nailed Monday with a spinning backfist into his throat.

Monday fell backwards, choking for air as Solex pulled a stop sign down from the wall. He picked Monday up to his feet, and whipped him hard into the corner. Monday hit so hard that he fell out a little, then fell to a seated position. Solex set the stop sign in Monday's face, pulled the bat back, and swung it as hard as he could into the center of the sign, effectively flattening Monday's nose. Monday pushed the sign away from his face, clutched his face and rolled around on the mat. Solex swung the bat down again, but Monday rolled under the ropes and right into the cage wall. Solex dropped the bat at his feet, grabbed the cage wall for leverage, and started kicking Monday anywhere he could.

He kicked him repeatedly in the head and chest, but somehow Monday managed to grab his ankle, spin it, and force Solex to the ground. Solex spun around and got to his feet slightly before Monday could, and he kicked him in the ribs. Monday rolled with it this time, flipping over Solex's leg and landing in a squatting position. He got to a standing position and nailed Solex right in the bridge of the nose with a stiff right punch. Solex stumbled back, and Monday reached up to the wall and pulled down the first weapon he got his hands on.

A chain with a lock on the end.

Just as he looks back up, Monday catches him in the upper left arm with the lock. Solex screams out, and turns around gripping his arm. Seeing his opportunity, Monday swung again, hitting Solex in the back with the chain, leaving a huge red welt on him. Solex buckled down, hitting both knees. Monday threw the chain around the front of Solex's face, planted his foot in his back, and pushed forward, trying to choke him out. After a few seconds, Monday got bored and let Solex go. Solex started to get off his knees, rubbing his throat, trying to catch his breath.

The referee saw as the spit flew from the mouth of Gregor Xaxon. The effects of the kick sent little trickles of blood from the mouth of Xaxon. The busted lip shown through. DX watched as the blood painted the floor a crimson glazed red. Disciple approached him and dropped a boot into his face. Making the bleeding even worse. Disciple X was kind, he picked up the now bloody opponent. Then dropped him to the ground with a punch to the open wound.

Disciple X went for the pinfall.

1…

2…

3…

No! Xaxon had his foot on the bottom rope. The ref signaled for the match to keep going. Disciple X got in the face of the referee. He was whipped around by the now standing Xaxon. He was kicked in the stomach and snap suplexed. Xaxon was winded. He had to end it. He couldn’t go on.

He crawled to his feet slowly and waited for DX to get up. Disciple X was now to his feet.

BLEED

Xaxon connected with his finishing maneuver and fell on top of DX, for the pinfall.

1…This was it.

2…Xaxon’s whole career flashed before his eyes.

His mind flicked through thoughts of competing in fWo Survivor 2.

Winning the PCW World Championship…

3.

He did it again. The fans shot out of their seats! Xaxon had won! He had done the inevitable! Once again he was on top of the game, the best in the game. Xaxon was the man of the hour.

thReat World Champion

The referee held Xaxon’s arm in the air and handed him the new thReat title belt. It was his and his to defend. He was helped up the ramp and pumped his fist in glory as he reached the peak of the stage. The fans gave him a standing ovation as he left Disciple X lying on the mat.

The camera panned to "Superstar" Vince Jacobs again, being a part of this standing ovation. He loved this show. His wife, Victoria, let out a loud whistle of enthusiasm.

The fans were in an uproar, ReLoad had been a smashing success and it didn't seem like the end was coming, but the night was supposed to be over. The crowd's cheering got louder and louder; almost to a deafening pitch. That was until the lights went out.

Darkness everywhere.

The announcers, event staff, thReat staff, everyone thought there were some sort of technically difficulties. The lights began to slowly dim in; right before pitching out again. The Jumbotron flickered on to the look of black and white static. It flickered again and went black.

Technical difficulties, right?

No.

The Jumbotron flickered to life again. Three words appeared in big white letters:

GOD IS NOWhere

The crowd was still silent and the Jumbotron began to static in and out, but the words remained on the screen. Soon the deafening silence was replaced by music. Nine Inch Nails' "Heresy" slowly came in, louder and louder. The fans were still bewildered by the turn of events.

The lights underneath the metal grating of the entrance ramp began to flash red and black. The arena began to collectively sense that something big was about to happen. Smoke began to fill the air around the ramp and beneath the Jumbotron.

Disciple X lay motionless in the center of the ring; completely unaware of what was going on. Again, the lights went out. The words and static remained on the Jumbotron. A few moments later the house lights flickered on. The fans strained their eyes; adjusting to the new light. Something was completely wrong.

Disciple X was no longer alone. In the ring stood someone no one had seen before. He had black hair, going down past the shoulders. His face was caked; ghostly white makeup, black lipstick, two large red eyes. He was wearing a long black trench coat, leather pants, combat boots, black gloves.

He moved his head to face the crowd, he smiled at them, showing his large white teeth. From the trench coat he pulled out a microphone. "Heresy" abruptly came to a stop as everyone waited.

"Before you lies a loser," his harsh words echoed through the arena. "A loser that is BLIND to the sins that he and everyone of you create each and every day. A loser that laughs at men who eat themselves silly. A loser that gets excited from watching two human beings have sex on the televisions. A loser that revels in the contract he is being paid by this company. A loser that is PRIDEFUL of himself. A loser that takes out his aggression on the weaker. A loser that tolerates laziness.

A loser that enjoys the forbidden fruits of love. This loser is like all of you. He is a SINNER."

The crowd began to boo loudly at the unnamed.

"You're against me because you're all afraid to admit the truth," he said calmly. "This ‘Disciple' as he calls himself that lies here, pathetically on the mat, a man that you love. And for what? I'll tell you why you love him. Because he does what you all do. He SINS. Every single one of you sloth-like fools sicken me to my stomach for what you've done. You should all be on your knees and praying to your God."

He paused and soaked in more jeers.

"Your Gods that don't exist. Your God is your flaw. Your God is your fears, lies, and cheats. Your God is the embodiment of everything that is wrong with your world. Your God is dead, useless, and no matter how much you want to believe in him, he will NEVER exist."

The unnamed laughed as he looked down upon Disciple and put one foot over his chest.

"Here lies your hero, your champion of the day. And more importantly; your flavor of the week. The flavor fades from your taste buds, your hero is dead. Your hero means nothing anymore. He's just another face in the crowd. Your hero belongs to GOD."

The crowd began to jeer loudly and debris began to slowly pile up inside of the ring as the unnamed bent down and lifted Disciple X's lifeless body in his arms. The unnamed laughed voraciously as he threw Disciple X back to the ground. He slowly brought DX back up and put him into a standing head scissors. He lifted DX up into a powerbomb position and then; in a split second dropped him down into a DDT.

The unnamed stood up; picking the microphone back up with him and sneered.

"Your hero has been crucified. I am the Anti-Hero. I am the Anti-Christ. I am Gabriel Oliver Danielson. I am GOD."

"Heresy" flooded back over the loudspeakers and the lights went out. The screen remained the same as ReLoad went off the air.

GOD IS NOWhere

Xaxon wins the match by pinfall
