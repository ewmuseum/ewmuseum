Victory Speech
July 15 2003

Hello recruits.

I’d like to take this time myself to thank all of those who were rooting for me on Sunday at Payback. I appreciate every cheer, every clap, and every god damn hoot. As thReat champion, I’ll promise to take my duty seriously. I’ll show respect to all around me, or at least to those who deserve it. I’m going to carry this title with dignity and honor.

There’s not going to be an extra stride in my step, but rather, a big smile on my face. Why? Because I’ve won and achieved the absolute number one goal in this company. It’s something that Vincent Pembridge, Rio Nexan, Jere Casarella, and J. Leslie Voss have never done. I’m special.

You might be wondering who will I be defending my title against first.

To me, it doesn’t matter. If it were up to me, I’d let everyone get a chance at it, but my body would forbid me to do so because of the toll I must pay during matches. The first competitor that will get a shot at the title will be a man or woman that is an excellent athlete and respects this company. Troy Mason and I may have a discussion with the board of directors of who we have in mind. The most deserving will get a shot.

Phil Atken, anyone?

No, I said most-deserving.

This extra week off for Flaw is turning out to be good, for my injuries have extra time to heal. I suffered a large concussion from the match at Payback, as well as countless bruises and fractures. Xaxon put up a hell of a fight, but there was no way I was letting a chance like that get blown away.

You may be thinking to yourself if I’m worried about Vincent Pembridge coming after me. I’m sure he’s got plans to take me out to get this title that he wants, and I’ll be ready for him. I’ve fought against him before, and he got away with a cheap victory. He’s always got something up his sleeve, so I’m going to try my best to be ready for it.

To the rest of thReat; you’ve all been doing a great job. Any one of you could be where I am right now with this title in my possession, I was just blessed with the opportunity. I look forward to seeing many of you in my future ventures in thReat. I understand I’m not the only military man here, as Jay Jaxxen is also a part of thReat. He must’ve followed me here, that stupid ass.

Anyway, I thank you for your time. And...

God bless America.

Signed,
General David Sanders
