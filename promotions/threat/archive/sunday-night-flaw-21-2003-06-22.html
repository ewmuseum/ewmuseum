He's just a young man, trying to make his dreams come true. To run a wrestling company, to become wealthy, to become famous. He's gotten those three things, but with added extras all around that make all of it seem worth nothing. The constant attempts to crush his self-esteem have plagued him, and he's at his boiling point.

What made everything worse was that with the defeat, Vincent Pembridge still seemed like a winner. All those weeks planning, Troy could have checked up on the contracts on the Vagabonds' members. Now, it had been revealed that all but Ampske & Vincent were spared from having to wrestle in thReat. With two other members of the Board Of Directors, Sucino Hass and Jackson Mahon, Moe Sole was in the backstage area... going through final discussion over production details for the show. Quality, camera angles, whether or not to really activate the spycam in the public toilets -- that kind of shit. It was something that was second nature to Troy Mason.

But he wasn't there on the night.

Moe Sole was a survivor, however. And he wasn't about to give up.

"So, everything's settled?" he asked of a stage technician, as Sucino Haas and Jackson Mahon signed various orders for equipment. The technician nodded, before walking away, talking over his wireless walkie talkie.

Smiling, Sole breathed a sigh of relief. It seemed everything had been taken care of, and with no time to spare, as well. Flaw 20 was about to get underway, sans Troy Mason and all. Moe turned and traded congratulatory high-fives with Sucino & Jackson, who too were relieved that everything had looked to be picture-perfect.

"Mr Sole? Inspector Andrews here. We're here to continue the investigation into the disapperance of Troy Mason. As it stands, Xaxon is still our number one suspect..." the burly inspector began, as he flashed his badge at the Board Of Directors trio.

Moe felt his heart stop for a nanosecond, almost worrying that he'd done something wrong or maybe there was a serial rapist on the loose in the federation. As it was, the matter concerned Troy Mason.

"But?" Sole asked, as Jackson & Hass leaned forward, eager to find out more about the situation.

The look on the face of Inspector Andrews should have tipped them off as to what was to come.

"We believe he had an accomplice. An accomplice who is right here, in his building, at this very moment.

And a very influential accomplice, too."

Moe Sole scratched his forehead, the entire situation causing him to have even more wrinkles in his old age. The mere mention of the Scorpion's name ran shivers down his spine. At least the cancer was rectified quickly, but the damage had been done. And its ramifications were being felt by EVERYONE now. Jackson Mahon's cellphone rang, and he excused himself to answer it, while the rest of the Board began to discuss the next steps amongst themselves. Troy Mason, bound and gagged to a chair, completely bloodied and almost devoid of life. With explosives wrapped around him.

Then, static again.

Everybody gulped, completely stunned. Before they turned to look at Inspector Andrews, who had one thing to say.

"Well, fuck me dead."

Two Weeks And Counting [1]

It had been two weeks since the disappearence of Troy Mason. And still, no clue about his whereabouts. Following the revelation at the end of Flaw 20, it was confirmed that Troy was alive -- but for how long, was the new question. Seeing how he WAS covered all over with explosives.

Now, in the Board Of Directors office, Moe Sole quietened down the discussion that been ongoing -- regarding the fact that there apparently was an accomplice that helped Xaxon kidnap Troy Mason two weeks ago. However, the notion of Xaxon behind the kidnapping, while entirely plausible, was not even confirmed.

But if it wasn't him, who else would it be?

The two main suspects, after another week of investigation, were Vincent Pembridge & Xaxon. But as Inspector Andrews' arrival to the arena was awaited, Moe Sole had told his board that the Inspector was now ordering investigations into everyone who had a major part to play on the shows since after Malevolence.

Seeing how Andrews had reached a dead-end more than a dozen times.

"Alright, people. I've been doing a mini-investigation of my own. Vincent Pembridge has every motive to kidnap Troy, seeing how he lost the war to him. However, the guy's has a great alibi, which is competing in the ACW. This doesn't mean we can rule him out. For all we know, he's the mastermind being everything.

I personally think that Vincent & Xaxon worked out some kind of deal, regarding Xaxon wrecking some havoc in thReat. That's the only way to explain Xaxon's sudden change in behaviour. Now, maybe they worked out this deal before Malevolence, which would make Pembridge the ultimate mastermind.

Or they met up AFTER Malevolence, making Vincent the accomplice. In which case, he would have gotten someone to do the job of kidnapping Troy, if Xaxon himself didn't do it. Ampske was thought to have been the accomplice, but he's been ruled out indefinitely. The directors were launched into a fresh wave of discussions again, wanting to end this ordeal as quickly as possible. If Troy was seriously injured, he had to be found quickly. If he was, in fact, dead... the point of the case would be severely diminished.

But the problem was, nobody knew anything.

"Phil Atken was my other choice, but he has an alibi -- he was forced out of the arena two weeks ago, and thus, he retreated to a bar to engage in copious amounts of alcohol consumption..." Inspector Andrews announced, as he walked through the doors of the office, carrying his briefcase in his right hand and a cup of coffee in his left hand.

His eyes were bloodshot, and the unshaven look didn't look too great on him. The man was going through hell, but could you blame him?

This was a case which appeared as if even Sherlock Holmes couldn't solve.

Moe Sole looked up and smiled on Inspector Andrews. The man who would solve the case, he hoped, was finally here... and ready to get down to business. Which was the unenviable task of grasping at the straws, in the form of interviewing people who had remote connections to the case...

But had been impact-players in the organisation since Malevolence.

"Now, I've got some news.

Troy Mason IS alive. This past Wednesday, we finally managed to track the location of the place in which Troy Mason appeared to be last Sunday, thanks to a live feed from a private detective that is a personal friend of Mr Mahon's. However, we did not find Troy, nor anybody in the area.

However, following a thorough investigation of the place, we can safely say that while he does look as if he's been badly injured, he is still alive. We can also say that Troy Mason is still in the state of Kentucky, and so is Xaxon. Unless Xaxon is NOT the kidnapper, then we have to be led to believe that the actual kidnapper is also in Kentucky, with this supposed accomplice.

That's about all I do know."

The BOD seemed relieved at the news and information that Inspector Andrews had gathered, none more so than Moe Sole. He scribbled something down on his pad, before the sixty-seven year old stood up and folded his arms, looking directly at Andrews, gulping down the rest of his coffee.

"So, what's the plan for tonight?"

The good Inspector smiled, and threw his coffee cup away, before unlocking his briefcase.

"You do your job, I'll do mine. Simple plan."

And with that, everything was set.
