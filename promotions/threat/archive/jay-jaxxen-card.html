Biography

Jay was born to a pair of hippies in Seattle, Washington in 1970. His parents we're all about peace and love, and at first Jay was all for that. But, soon after entering Middle School in 1983 he realized that he had no friends and that he was made fun of for being an unusual size. By the age of 13 he was 5' 8" and he was still growing rapidly. He was never liked by the kids in his classes and was basically an outcast. Jay was never as smart as his teacher's and his parents thought he was. By age 16 he was 6ft 6in and he was playing on the High School Football team. He found that football was the perfect outlet for his rage against the the popular people. By 18 he was repeating the 11th grade and his attitude about peace and love which he had once shared with his parents had now faded. He was now a hateful and bitter person.

In the spring of 1988 he went off to basic training. He then went into the US Armed Forces in 1990 after two years of the rising in the ranks and going into Special Forces training. In early 1991, Jay joined the Special Forces. A year later he was sent to Desert Storm where he met his now friend and partner, Dean McDaniel. The two men were thick as theives and they watched each others back in battle. In one time of battle Jay noticed that an Iraq soldier had shot at Dean's head. Jay immediatly jumped at took the bullet for him, which saved his life. To this day, Jay still says that Dean owes him.

In the end Jay was honorably discharged from the military due to this wound, and his friend Dean soon quit. In 1995, Jay and Dean were reunited at a Special Forces Reunion. The two had become interested in Wrestling and had both been thinking of entering the profession. So a month later, the two men joined a wrestling academy owned by Sal E. Simmons. After 4 months of training they graduated Sal's Wrestling Academy in early '96 and they went on to almost two years of independent work.

In 1997, Jay joined BJWC, also known as Billy Jay's Wrestling Company. He had little success there and after a short Television Title reign went on to Storm Wrestling v2 in late 98'. After another stint of little success he joined WWW or World Wide Wrestling. At this point Jay started to shine winning two Tag Team Titles under the name Harcore Killah' and one Canadian Championship. He left WWW in mid 2001 after giving it three years of his career.

Soon after this, Jay came apon theStorm which was Storm Wrestling v4. The new owner which took up where [name withheld] had left off saw Fighting Talent in Jay and signed him up immediately. Jay took the fed by storm and immediatley went after it's Hardcore title. Sadly, Jay never got his shot as the fed closed three weeks after his entering the federation.

Jay was out of work and he needed something, so he ventured to town of Concord , Conneticut and there he found an old boxing gym. Jay worked out there, sparring with other boxers and even another wrestler every now and then. Then, one day Dean McDaniel walked through the door. Dean said he had a fed lined up, it was called Franchise Wrestling. Jay said he'd check it out at the two men were back together.

In FW, Jay and Dean formed a team known as FTE, or F*ck The Establishment. FTE did nothing but joke around and have fun with other wrestlers. After a stint in FW, FTE thought that they needed to just seperate. And the two men went there seperate ways.

It's now 2002. Jay has become an over 30, ex-military major turned professional wrestler, turned fighter, turned wrestler again, who is out of a job. He has been hanging around the indy circuit for a few months, but he hopes to make it big in thReat.
