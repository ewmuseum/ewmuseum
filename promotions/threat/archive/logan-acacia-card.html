Historical Info

Biography

On the 29th November 1979 a very special child was born, Logan Acacia. Logan was special because he was born as the second human manifestation of the Greek God, Orion.

Growing up Logan knew he was Orion's second coming on Earth. Perhaps it was his strength, swiftness, intelligence, good looks and how daring he was that brought him to believe in his God given abilities.

However his overconfidence and general arrogance has separated him from others and has done through his entire young life. But this has not affected Logan, he is very happy being alone knowing he is The Orion.

Throughout his childhood, Logan's God given intelligence enabled him to do very well in school. In fact he did so well that he graduated from his local high school in Green Bay, Wisconsin with very high grades in English, Psychology and History. Logan never pursued College though, instead opting for a career in wrestling.

His quest to become a wrestler began when he was only seven years old, when he had no friends and started to watch local wrestling shows. It seemed an ideal step for Logan to take, as where better to show and prove that he is the second coming of Orion on Earth than to wrestle on live television and prove his God given talents to the World, and that they make him better than everyone else.

Before arriving in thReat Logan wrestled a lot in Japan to gain experience. He wrestled literally hundreds of matches there since he was eighteen up until his debut in thReat in July 2003.

Logan never mentions anything of his family, leading everyone to believe that his arrogant and egotistical attitude forced them to disown him.

Other than this information, very little is known about the twenty-three year old man who calls himself "The Orion."
