Some thought that Vincent Pembridge was the biggest and baddest around, and many were right. But Lawrence Neumeyer could be argued to be an even BETTER fighter than the British native. Tonight, David Sanders had to go against him for his beautiful World Title.

Lawrence Neumeyer came from the back, to a theme song especially picked out for this occasion. Why? Nobody cared. The crowd jeered wildly, but at the same time, they also felt sick for jeering. There was nothing they could do that would express how they felt about this man. There have been some really sickening things in thReat. Stuff involving kidnapping, firearms, hostile takeovers, and riots. One of the things that you can add on top of that is leaving a man inside a fiery inferno. That’s what this guy did the thReat’s World Champion, all for revenge on a little mishap many years ago in the United States army.

Lawrence sneered before walking to the ring. It would be the second time he’d wrestled since being “in” thReat. He was wearing black boots and black and white camouflage pants with a dark red sleeveless shirt on. He put his knee on the outside apron and grabbed the ropes to repel himself into the ring. He stood in the center of the ring and raised both of his arms into the air. He was ripped. He layed down on the mat and began doing knuckle push-ups. Although appreciative for his duty in the army, the fans spat at the enemy.

They couldn’t bear looking at him anymore. So, you something grabbed their... attention.

“ATTENTION!”

Biggest. Pop. EVER~!

The entire arena leapt on their feet, cheering at the sound of “Headstrong” by Trapt. They all clapped and hollered as General fucking David Sanders came out from the back! ...An American flag in hand! He came out waving the flag of his country while looking dead in the eyes of Lawrence Neumeyer.

“U – S – A!”

“U – S – A!”

“U – S – A!”

Sanders walked towards the ring, his championship belt round his waste, and the American flag resting over his shoulder. His two most-cared for items in the entire world. He looked around, and handed the flag to a nearby security guard. He nodded, before looking back at Lawrence. He slid into the ring as the referee took his belt off. The General took off his black shirt, revealing his six-pack of abdominal muscles.

He and Lawrence Neumeyer inched closer to each other. There eyes hadn’t left each other. It was like a tractor beam. Now standing right in front of each other, Neumeyer had an inch over Sanders. Lawrence suddenly stepped back, relieving tension from the moment. He extended his hand for a handshake.

The crowd booed and urged Sanders not to do it.

He did. Although Sanders knew nothing about Neumeyer’s capabilities, he knew that he wouldn’t pull a cheapshot off at a fair handshake. Back in the army, it was drilled into their heads not to give their country a bad name. They weren’t going to start now.

The two shook hands before stepping back and circling the ring, both of them ready to strike the other. The bell had rung earlier, and everything was set.

“SANDERS IS MY HERO!!!!!”

A young fan yelled out in the front row. Sanders looked away, and...

WHAM!

A superkick to the side of the chin knocked Sanders out. He seemed to be out cold with the outrageously hard kick to his jaw.

Neumeyer laughed and walked over to Sanders. He put his boot over his chest.

1...

2...

Sanders’ eyes opened. He grabbed Neumeyer’s leg and wrenched it sideways hitting a dragon screw. Sanders got up and set himself up in an attack stance, screaming obscenities that children under the age of five have never heard. Neumeyer got up and saw the General charging towards him.

Drop toe hold into the corner turnbuckle!

The General clutched his jaw again. It was definitely broken, maybe twice. Sanders turned around on the ground only to be stomped on vigorously. Neumeyer began choking him out with his boot. The referee counted to five, and ordered the move to be stopped. Lawrence was a fighter and a wrestler, with styles collected from places around the world.

He grabbed Sanders by the hair and brought him to his feet. Sanders fought back with jabs to the stomach!

A stiff elbow to the back sent Sanders back to his knees. WHAM! A knee to the throat! Picking Sanders up, Neumeyer put him between his legs and perfectly executed a powerbomb!

The brute strength of this man impressed the entire audience.

He was ‘pwning’ Sanders.

He let the General get up by his own will power and taunted him towards him. Sanders went for a right club, but Neumeyer ducked. Sanders went for a clothesline, Neumeyer avoided it. One last desperation punch, missed.

“Don’t you think I know all of your moves by now, David?” Lawrence said, in an antagonizing voice.

Sanders was almost winded, when he realized he had to complete change his wrestling style, in the middle of the match. Lawrence had tapes to watch of Sanders’ fighting, but the only thing Sanders could do to prepare was train, but he was constantly hurt with injuries.

Putting his hands up to his shoulders, Sanders looked into the eyes of Lawrence.

“The hand is quicker than the eye, you fucking bastard.”

WHAM!

A kick to the gonads.

Lawrence was stuck watching Sanders’ hands, that he never saw the low-blow coming. The crowd erupted into applause. With Lawrence clutching his nuts and on his knees, Sanders spun around and hit a spinning heel kick to the side of Neumeyer’s head!

Neumeyer fell over with the crowd now chanting...

“GO BACK HOME!”

“GO BACK HOME!”

“GO BACK HOME!”

The Swedish native ignored their pathetic rants. He had a match, after all. You know, against Sanders. For the world title and all. Anyway. Sanders got up to take a quick breather. Lawrence retreated to the corner. Sanders followed him there, and climbed on the second turnbuckle. With Lawrence below him, D. Sanders jumped down and crushed Neumy’s chest!

Sanders grabbed Lawrence by his collar and ripped him to his feet.

“Remember this move of mine?”

Lawrence got an entire carton of knuckle-ness, as the General laid him out with a stiff right hand. General grabbed both of Lawrence’s legs and dragged him to the middle of the ring. The fans in Boston all cheered as Sanders put Lawrence into a Boston crab!

Lawrence screamed in pain. However. He’s seen Sanders do this move before, and knew his own proper counter. Pushing as much as possible with his legs forward, Lawrence slowly stretched his back more and more like an arch. He got far enough to reach Sanders throat and pull in the opposite direction. Sanders was forced to let go of the hold before Lawrence tore a hole in his throat.

Both men were on the ground in pain and exhaustion. Both men slowly collected themselves and got to their feet. Lawrence went for a punch.

Blocked.

He went for another.

Blocked.

Once more.

Blocked.

Sanders went for the Prisoner of War, a clothesline from hell.

NO! Lawrence dodged it, and went behind Sanders hitting a German Suplex into a bridge pin!

1...

2...

KICKOUT FROM SANDERS!!

Lawrence spat at Sanders as he got to his feet. He sized up the General as he slowly stood up. David groggily turned towards Neumeyer before practically getting decapitated with a clothesline!

While on the mat, Lawrence slid out of the ring. He reached under the ring and pulled out a sledgehammer.

The history of the sledgehammer to Sanders? Last month at Payback, anyone? Xaxon used it as a deadly weapon, taking Sanders inches to his life. Countless shots all over put Sanders in the hospital for a few days, and Lawrence looked to do the same thing.

He got into the ring and the referee immediately tried to the take the weapon from Lawrence. After moments of struggling, Lawrence gave the referee the sledgehammer. The ref walked in the other direction to bring it outside the ring.

Cue axe-handle smash to the back of the head.

Lawrence laid out the referee. He picked up the weapon as Sanders was getting up.

BAM! A hard shot to the stomach. Sanders fell to the mat and began bleeding from the mouth. The crowd booed hysterically while Lawrence smiled. He began stomping on Sanders. Kick after kick. He picked Sanders up and set him up for a vertical suplex… Bam! He connected.

Lawrence went over to try to revive the referee. The General used every ounce of strength to crawl upwards towards the sledgehammer. Lawrence was distracted in his attempts to get the referee back. The wooden handle of the sledgehammer was now touching his head. He just couldn’t grab it behind him. Lawrence went back over to Sanders and grabbed his legs, trying to drag him to the center of the ring. He did, but not after Sanders grabbed the sledgehammer and cracked it over Lawrence’s head!

The crowd exploded into applause. They roared in approval.

Blooded poured from Lawrence’s head as he immediately fell to the ground unconscious. Three men in the ring, all down and out.

General David Sanders.

Lawrence Neumeyer.

The referee.

NEUMEYER KICKED OUT! TWO finishers and a sledgehammer to the head still did not keep this man down! The entire arena, including Sanders, was in complete denial. How could he have done it? Hopefully there wasn’t much more fight in Lawrence.

Getting tired, Sanders staggered to his feet. He picked up Neumeyer hitting some jabs here and there. Sanders brought him into the corner. Suddenly Neumeyer lifted his leg back and hit a kick to Sanders’ gut. Sanders doubled over in pain as Lawrence went to the turnbuckle. Knowing he had to react quickly, Sanders hit a hard right hand to the spine of Neumeyer. Lawrence was on the second turnbuckle, now with his head laying on the ring post. Sanders went through the ring and to the outside apron. He quickly climbed to the top of the turnbuckle, moving Lawrence out of the way.

Sanders sat on the top turnbuckle and pulled Lawrence up towards him. He lifted him... Onto his shoulders.

The entire audience stood on their feet. The referee was going to stop Sanders, but it was perfectly legal.

Picture on the top turnbuckle a man standing, holding someone else in a crucifix powerbomb position. Sanders rocked forward.

But.

In mid-air Lawrence tried to wiggle out and counter the move. It didn’t work. Sanders’ brute strength held him tightly, with the move slightly deformed.

THE FUCKING LAST SURRENDER FROM THE TOP-ROPE!!

Thousands of cameras flashed during the move, almost blinding the camera. No matter what, Lawrence was done. He hadn’t moved. Blood stained over his body. He was a beat man.

1...

3!!

No joke. Sanders had defeated his number one enemy. The crowd roared in approval, as Sanders was handed his world title from the referee, who helped Sanders to his feet. David held the title high and walked over to the turnbuckle. He showed the crowd his title and posed on the turnbuckle. He had defeated his own threat.

Focusing off Sanders, the camera went to the referee who checked on Lawrence. He hadn’t moved since impact. The referee signaled for paramedics to come to the ring.

They rushed out. The crowd hushed up. Sanders hopped down from the turnbuckle and walked over towards Lawrence. He saw he wasn’t moving. Paramedics immediately checked Lawrence and put him on a stretcher. Sanders watched with little concern. This man wanted to kill Sanders, it was hard to have compassion.

Being placed on the gurney, Lawrence was carted up the ramp quickly. Sanders was left in the ring, victorious, feeling like...

The champion of champions.

But the night wasn't quite over.

RESULT: General David Sanders via pinfall to retain the World Title

Never Saw THAT Coming

Man, it was weird. On one hand, we were all happy to see Sanders beat Lawrence, but when the medics came out, we knew something was wrong. And we all knew it wasn't planned or anything. Seeing how we'd seen so much mayhem on the night, it was obvious that Lawrence laying lifeless in the ring wasn't planned.

Hell, a man had died on the show. And it looked as if there was gonna be another one.

The medics stopped on the stage, and attended to Lawrence, while Troy Mason came out and sorta did a celebratory dance with Sanders. The two are good friends and all, so it wasn't unexpected. But suddenly, we all watched as those Board Of Directors dudes, led by Moe Sole, got up and climbed over the barricade. They were looking to join Troy & Sanders in the ring, but in the end, only Moe Sole went in.

The other 9 guys had to be content to stand around the ring, as another dude jumped out from the crowd. He looked damn familiar, and once I saw he had a badge, I knew it was that Inspector from many months ago, who was investigatin' the kidnapping of Troy Mason. Aaron Andrews or somethin', his name was.

On the stage, we heard a lot of shouting, and the people around me distinctly heard one of the medics saying that Lawrence had been paralysed or something. He couldn't move at all, it seems. And hell, I think Troy Mason heard it all the way in the ring, as he & Moe Sole began to argue. Finally, one of the medics came down to the ring and had a private conversation with Moe Sole.

Then, the weirdest shit happened. Fuckin' unbelievable.

That Inspector dude also went into the ring and talked to Moe Sole, before the old guy got a microphone and began rambling about how things were out of control in thReat. He said that the Board Of Directors had been working with the police & the American Government for the past week, and based on what they'd seen on the show...

thReat was suspended from all activity, effective immediately. And he blamed the murder of Jere Casarella, in a match that was never legalised, and the new development of Lawrence Neumeyer being severely hurt and possibly paralysed, coupled with the fact that his contract was an absolute sham & he'd failed a medical exam earlier in the year that officially restrained him to his home.

Troy Mason was definitely shocked as hell, man, but Moe Sole continued to say that Troy's ownership license was revoked, and thReat was now officially under police investigation again for a number of reasons. All of us in the crowd were then urged to leave the arena quickly, as streams of security officers appeared outta nowhere.

In the ring, Sanders was pretty stunned, and some of the fans began to swear and cuss at him, which was fuckin' weird. But as we were all leaving, that asshole Vincent Pembridge appeared on the stage. For a moment, we thought he was checkin' on Lawrence or something. But then, he pointed to Troy & Sanders in the ring. Before he started to clap like a crazy bastard. Then it hit us, man. This was the fucker's masterplan all along.

The fucker finally won the war.

And he was lovin' every damn second of it.
