House Show 02 — Morgensen Gymnasium Complex, Salt Lake City, UT

12 September 2003

thReat House Show 02

Date: 12 September 2003
Location: Morgensen Gymnasium Complex, Salt Lake City, UT

The Story So Far The race was on for Troy Mason. A man who had a lot of work to do with thReat, but within a very short window of time. And with all the police & government investigations hanging over the company, Mason realised that even if he could do something magical with the three chances he had, thReat might yet disappear from the face of the planet due to the fact that he was basically harbouring fugitives. It would be easy to fire the likes of psycho-Rio and such, but the compensation that would have to be paid would cripple the already horrendous financial state of the company. Marred by one Colin Gear, the Commissioner. At least the signs of a revival were encouraging, with the overall product of House Show 01, with the only blemish being Vincent Pembridge assaulting a female member of the roster. Time wasn't on Troy's side, however, and he now had to use all he had to keep the company afloat. By hook, or by crook.

--

Morgensen knew Troy Mason's older brother & Jon Hackett. And naturally, became a fan of thReat since the company's revival earlier in the year. So, when Troy booked the show to take place in Salt Lake City, Morgensen gave him a call, and that was the end of that. Now, with 617 people packed into Morgensen's Gymnasium, Troy Mason came out to the ring and once again reiterated what the Main Event of the show would be -- Sanders defending his World Title against Vincent Pembridge & psycho-Rio. Mason basically informed the crowd that the General WAS in the building, preparing for his match. Surprisingly enough, half the crowd didn't really respond, still blaming David Sanders for being one of the causes for thReat's fall from grace. Troy pleaded with them, saying the General wasn't at fault at all, before announcing that the expected Steel Cage Match between Rent-A-Hero & Remix would have to be postponed, due to a flu bug affecting both men. He did promise a night of great action nonetheless, and left to a rousing ovation. Be noted that the video & audio equipment seemed to be of top quality this time 'round, we actually even had a video wall, and security was tight as ever.

--

thReat Cruiserweight Title Match:
Sergey Kovhachek vs Tyler Davidson[c]
A rematch from their battle on Sunday Night Flaw a few weeks ago, the first match featured the promising Sergey Kovhachek, still winless in thReat, taking on Tyler Davidson. Tyler came out first and had a little announcement to make -- he rocked. Tyler was in a foul mood, mouthing off about the lack of restraint General David Sanders possessed, and the lack of competition for his title. Before he could go on any further, though, Sergey came out, and the match came to life. Kovhachek controlled the first minute or so, a tilt-a-whirl reverse DDT almost gaining him the victory following a flying headscissors. Tyler finally used his years of experience to gain control of the contest, even going as far as to scoring with a 720º legdrop. Alas, Sergey kicked out, keeping the fans on the edge of their seats. The match got a little messy then, as the two fierce competitors tumbled out of the ring following a weird defense tactic; Tyler had scaled to the top of the turnbuckle, with his back turned to Sergey, but the Ukrainian suddenly recovered and jumped to the top, before hitting a suicidal flying bulldog down onto the concrete. Needless to say, the crowd went crazy. After brawling on the outside, the two men re-entered the ring, and after a series of near-falls, Tyler Davidson finally got the victory after applying his Future Shock finisher. Fantastic match; these two will be doing battle again soon. They just have to.

Result: Tyler Davidson won at 10:33 to retain the Cruiserweight Title

--

In the back, Colin Gear arrived wearing an expensive suit, finally having the guts to show his face in the vicinity of a thReat event. However, as he strutted towards his office, almost as if his testicles were the size of watermelons, several repo men appeared and informed the Commissioner that Troy Mason had managed to somehow overturn all the crazy spending. Colin was adamant, stating he didn't know what that meant. I have a feeling he wasn't kidding, I've talked to him before. Anyways, within seconds, Colin Gear was left with his red boxer shorts and pink socks. Undoubtedly, Troy Mason's taking steps to restructure the financial despair the company was in. And he was going straight to the source.

--

psycho-Rio was then seen entering the building, with that trademark sadistic & cock smirk plastered on his face. The Sociopath sneered at all the security officials crawling all over, before he was blindsided. Two men started attacking him with lead pipes, and security was called into action, but a group of British teenagers made their presence felt, causing a mass brawl. It was obvious by then that it was the handiwork of one Vincent Pembridge, and the two attackers were his cohorts; DeVante Stevenson and current tA fighter, Santos Salvatore. The two cronies to the Scorpion dragged psycho-Rio out of the building and into the waiting arms of police officials, who thanked Santos & DeVante for the apprehension of the fugitive. As the police car sped away, Santos & DeVante was greeted by Vincent Pembridge, who expressed his delight at the Cuban finally out of the picture.

--

Tag Team Match: haTE & Christian Elijah vs R.A.T & Frankie Focker This match was supposed to have happened at the Double Or Nothing PPV, but Colin Gear messed up their travel arrangements and all four men ended up in Canada. After doubts over their own futures considering what eventually transpired on the PPV, all four men were finally convinced that they would never find themselves in a situation that Jere Casarella & Lawrence Neumeyer were, unfortunately enough for the two men. All four men had bad blood with each other, and both teams were comprised of individuals that hated each other. Elijah squared off against Focker first, and the latter opened up a can of whoop ass. Old school versus new school, and Frankie was completely wiping the floor with Christian, looking like the deceased Mr Perfect while at it. Elijah eventually managed to get the tag in to haTE, who looked disgruntled the entire time. The Giantkiller himself got into the ring and found Focker still firing away on all cylinders, before a hard spinning belly-to-back suplex brought an end to Frankie's advantage. However, haTE didn't build on it. Instead, he insisted that the charismatic R.A.T be tagged in, and he got just that, much to the crowd's delight.

A brilliant battle between the two men ensued, with R.A.T using his speed to great effectiveness, until he was thrown out of the ring with an overhead belly suplex! All hell broke loose, and all four competitors brawled around the ring for a while, with the referee desperately trying to control things. Focker & Elijah ended up back in the ring first, and the latter absolutely maimed his Mr Flashback with his Skullfucked move, before attempting his patented Asai Moonsault. Frankie moved out of the way and drilled Christian with a powerslam, before executing Mr Perfect's very own Perfect-Plex. haTE slid back into the ring just in time to break up the count, and went on to destroy Frankie Focker with a tilt-a-whirl inverted piledriver. However, R.A.T -- who'd received quite a beating on the outside -- came back in and almost pulled off his Rabid X finisher on haTE, only to get a pump-handle backbreaker for his troubles. What followed was haTE pulling off R.A.T's Rabid X finisher on Randall himself, which was enough for the victory. Post-match, haTE got hold of a chair and attacked Chirstian Elijah & Frankie Focker, before obliterating the young Randall. R.A.T had to be carted out of the ring. Good match, although the fans certainly wished it had lasted longer.

Result: haTE & Christian Elijah won at 7:39

--

Colin Gear paid a visit to Troy Mason, wearing a janitor's outfit, and demanded to know why all his belongings were being repossessed. Mason responded, stating that only the crap Colin bought with thReat funds were being repossessed. The two men started arguing; rather childishly, it has to be said. They were interrupted by Keith Anal-Wedged & Alan Abel Wade, however, who had ideas on how to improve thReat. Before either Troy or Gear could react, Keith stripped down to his boxers and started dancing, as Alan sang a Britney Spears song. For one time only, Gear & Troy combined to kick the asses of the brotherhood tandem known as We Iz Special, and security eventually took over. Thankfully.

--

Santos Salvatore & DeVante Stevenson were seen in the parking lot, smoking. They were discussing about how cool it was that they managed to pull of their task for the night without breaking a sweat, before DeVante asked Santos about life in theAsylum. But before Sonny could even answer, a man in a trenchcoat jumped out of nowhere, and attacked the cronies of Vincent Pembridge. And within seconds, it was revealed that the man was indeed psycho-Rio.

But.

The Sociopath's fun was shortlived, as police officers swarmed the scene and took him down, much to the surprise of everyone. As psycho-Rio was being beaten by batons and such, Santos & DeVante recovered, getting in the Cuban's face and taunting him. It had been a sneaky set-up all along, and the Cuban swore revenge as the officer-in-charge began to list off EVERY SINGLE CRIME committed by Rio. Seemed like a bloody long list to me.

--

thReat International Title Match: Vandal vs McCluskey[c] Chris McCluskey came struttin' out to the ring with the biggest smile on his face, as he quickly got control of a microphone and began mouthing off about how the thReat roster was spread so thin, that Troy Mason was about to give Rent-A-Hero a rematch at the International Title. McCluskey pointed out that Americans can't be creative geniuses with people like Troy around, and stated that most Europeans were so much smarter, with the possible exception of Colin Gear. This brought VANDAL out. The man who'd really had a quiet time in thReat recently, but blatantly expressed his disgust at the International Champion's words. Chris expressed his disgust at Vandal's face, leading to the eventual challenge.

And acceptance.

A very entertaining speech from Chris McCluskey, but the match wasn't too bad as well. Chris used his quickness to take control early in the match, but Vandal's sheer power brought him back into the contest, after a flapjack into the turnbuckle. Vandal almost had the match won with a death valley driver, but McCluskey got his foot on the rope. The match continued with Vandal dominating, quickly putting McCluskey's short bursts of revival with his raw power. Finally, it seemed all was over when Vandal executed his Remains Into Ruble move, but Chris somehow kicked out! This sparked a war of words between Vandal & the referee, allowing Neverwhere to recover and hit a hammerlock DDT. The 3-count was made, and Chris retained.

Or did he?

Seemed Vandal had his right foot on the ropes, and after viewing the evidence on the small video-wall, the referee forced the match to continue, overruling his own decision. McCluskey was furious about this and almost got stunned when Vandal rolled him up. Neverwhere saved himself in the nick of time, however, and an even contest developed, with a series of near-falls. Most thought McCluskey had it won after a massive bridging northern lights suplex, but Vandal kicked out. Seconds later, Chrissy had his opponent locked in the elevated Texas cloverleaf known as REALITY CHECK and Vandal tapped out, finally giving Neverwhere the official victory. An enthralling match, which didn't seem boring at any point. Surprising, considering Vandal's limited array of moves. Neverwhere was awesome, as usual.

Result: Chris McCluskey won at 12:21 to retain the International Title

--

In the back, medics were treating R.A.T and Troy Mason was rather furious, upon hearing that his young star was the latest member of the dreaded injury list. Storming off in a fit of rage, Troy continued to curse loudly, just as haTE arrived on the scene and checked on R.A.T, but without saying a thing. haTE seemed devoid of any emotion whatsoever, and walked off shrugging his shoulders. He muttered something about finally being able to move on, and it did seem that the man was less tensed up than usual. A new haTE on the horizons? If it means more giantkilling feats, I'm all for it.

--

Outside the building, a bunch of Mormons started protesting. They had pickets and all, and were really vocal about their protests. Of course, due to their apparent speech impediments, nobody could understand what the hell they were saying, but their signs were printed with the same old slogans. You know, ABSITENCE BEFORE MARRIAGE, JESUS IS MY BOYFRIEND, MOSES WAS A GOOD LOVER; crazy stuff like that. Security outside the building kept a close watch on them, but it seems there were a few Mormons in the crowd. Although, they were reasonably quiet, and were simply conversing with each other quietly.

--

PhD vs 'The Sun King' Louis Bourbon When both men entered the company, there was an immediate disliking, and several spats ensued. Bourbon's introduction to thReat was interrupted by PhD, but Dawson was taken care of after a slight verbal exchange, as Louis clocked him with his scepter. On the following show, there was a retaliation by PhD by means of a sneak attack, but again, the Frenchman triumphed in the backstage brawl. However, after Bourbon won his match on the night, PhD finally got sweet revenge. Unknowingly, he'd injured the Frenchman, and Paul got suspended for a month. That didn't prevent Louis Bourbon to make many scathing remarks towards his new enemy, and with both men having passed recent medicals, this match was signed.

Louis Bourbon made his grand entrance, as expected, and he looked to be in fine shape. The scepter was given a kiss before he placed it aside, and PhD came out to a rather decent ovation, but he didn't bother with the fans. The match started off rather slowly, with both men using basic technical moves to try and gain an advantage. Louis took down his opponent with a unique armbar submission where he also focused on the neck of PhD, but Dawson got to the ropes quickly. The two continued to tease each other with simple moves, before PhD got physical with a fallaway slam. That was the catalyst for the match to spark into life, and both men threw a variety of hard-hitting moves at each other. So much so that Louis Bourbon looked seriously injured after PhD's 3/4 falling neckbreaker. The match soon saw submission moves being applied, before Louis managed to sidestep Dawson's Full Term finisher, sending him into the turnbuckle. The Frenchmen followed up with a French Suplex, but only got a 2-count.

So, he hoisted Paul onto the top of a corner turnbuckle and scored with an amazing dragon suplex; Revokation Of Nantes. Of course, Bourbon couldn't help but waste time strutting around the ring arrogantly, before making his equally cover; a foot on the chest of Dawson. That allowed Paul to kick out at two and instantly slap on an ankle lock, much to the surprise of everyone. Louis tapped, PhD got the victory. End of story. Decent match, and PhD went on to use the scepter on The Sun King again. It appeared, however, that Bourbon's ankle was twisted badly. What an injury-prone guy. Decent contest, by the way. PhD looked to be breathless a couple of times in the match, so that might not go down with Troy Mason.

Result: PhD won at 9:06

--

Kaylee Young made her way out to the ring as Louis Bourbon was helped to the back, and the young lady - no pun intended - was pretty upbeat. No sign of a bruise anywhere, although with a microphone in hand. As she waited for the hooting and cat-calls to stop, Kaylee began to talk about Vincent Pembridge, and what an evil man he was. She didn't get very far on the subject, though, as she spotted the Mormons in the crowd and went absolutely ballistic. She growled and pointed at them. Angrilly.

Of course, before she started to scream at them, calling their religion a sham, and claiming that they were too weak to open their eyes. A vicious verbal war was the result, and security had to be called in to escort Kaylee out of the ring and to the back. A very weird & confusing situation for most to comprehend, really. But one thing's for sure; Kaylee has not forgotten about the Scorpion of Manchester.

--

General David Sanders was finally spotted backstage, as he came out Troy Mason's office. As he came out, a referee went in and informed the Owner that Louis Bourbon had really twisted his ankle, drawing another agonised groan from Mason. But anyways, as David retreated to his locker-room, tons of backstage officials were giving him dirty looks. They felt that it was because of the World Champion and him paralysing Neumeyer that they were almost without a job. With families to support and bills to pay. Sanders ignored all of them, however, and simply proceeded to enter his locker-room. The tension that was built up there was pretty frightening.

--

Match 2 Of 2/3 Contendership Series: blind vs Phil Atken It was time, for blind & Phil Atken to do battle. Atken needed to win if he stood any chance of becoming #1 Contender, while blind would have to play it smart and win to secure his status. The mist rising was the cue for the crowd to cheer wildly, but as blind walked towards the ring, the cowardly Phil Atken clocked him from behind. That signalled the start of this match, which was contested under SUBMISSIONS ONLY ruling. The two men brawled with each other for a while on the outside, Phil seemingly no longer carrying that knee injury. The referee tried to get the two combatants into the ring, but Atken show the referee what he thought by suplexing blind onto the unforgiving concrete. Once Phil threw blind into the ring, though, the latter turned the tables on the former Cruiserweight Champion with a release german suplex. For the next few minutes, blind tried to get Phil to submit in a variety of holds, but Atken always managed to find a way to squirm out of sure defeat. Finally, as blind set Phil up for his Way Of The Blind move, Atken countered and nailed blind with his own fall-forward piledriver, before going upstairs and executing a phenomenal moonsault. Seconds later, blind was trapped in Phil's trademark submission finisher, termed Humiliation. The enigmatic blind didn't want to tap out and eventually passed out, which was enough for the referee to call the bell and award the match to Phil Atken. Disappointment for blind, but he showed a different side of himself on the night. Phil has a very possible chance of actually winning the series now, however. Match 3 would be the all-important decider, then.

Result: Phil Atken won at 9:46

--

thReat World Title Match:
Vincent Pembridge vs General David Sanders[c] Troy Mason came out as Atken danced to the back, and he quickly addressed the crowd, informing them that due to psycho-Rio's apprehension by police, the match would now only feature Sanders & Pembridge. He went on to say that all week long, people were asking him a question. About why did he give the two big troublemakers of the federation a shot at the World Title. He answered, stating that many people had also begun to criticse General David Sanders since the Double Or Nothing PPV, and Troy was certain that Sanders would walk out the winner, no matter what. With that, the Owner took his position outside the ring, and got ready for the match.

Which really epitomised the words BLOODY FUCKING INTENSE, and summed up what thReat was all about. Good versus evil.

Vincent Pembridge came out first and he was in a pretty jovial mood. The General, however, was a picture of sharp contrast, and once he hit the ring, all hell broke loose. David opened a can of whoop ass to the Scorpion, outbrawling him, before the Arrogant Bastard struck back with a series of suplexes. The two men would go on to have a gazillion near-falls in this high-paced match-up, with Vincent intent on proving his wrestling prowress and Sanders eager to show what he brought to the dance. Quite a lot of the fans, surprisingly, were jeering Sanders; not when he almost got the victory with a small package, however. The Callous Fighting Machine kept his cool, and nearly went close with a -- surprisingly enough -- springboard moonsault. The atmosphere generated by the crowd made this match a million times better, and when the referee was knocked out by an inadvertent spear from David, we all took it as a bad omen. Vincent managed to hit his patented inverted piledriver, and covered the General. With no option, Troy Mason used his status as Owner and slid into the ring, counting.

ONE.

TWO.

THRE - hang on. Why did he stop?

Because he could and he wanted to. Troy Mason effectively screwed Vincent Pembridge. The Manucian Predator had Sanders pinned, and David wasn't moving an inch. Vincent had the World Title won. But Mason got up and gave Pembridge the ol' finger salute, before he started mouthing off at Vince. The crowd couldn't believe their eyes, and went ballistic with joy.

The Scorpion of Manchester was mighty incensed, but suddenly, psycho-Rio jumped out of the crowd with a steel chair and jumped into the ring. Vincent turned around instinctively, caught the chair that was being flung at him, and ate steel, as psycho-Rio executed Vinnie's own finisher on him. Sanders, who had started to recover, was on the receiving end of the Sociopath's wrath as well, getting a Screwed Over for his own troubles. Troy Mason had done the wise thing and ran out of the ring, ordering security to surround the ring, as the referee finally came to and called off the match. Pure mayhem brought an end to a brilliant match, full of psychology and mouth-watering action.

Result: Draw after 15:47

--

In terms of entertainment value, the fans definitely got their money's worth. Kudos to Troy for managing to be a part of such an engrossing show. However, the security lapse at the end that allowed psycho-Rio to re-enter the building and storm the ring wouldn't be an encouraging sign for the Board Of Directors. And with a couple of fresh victims of injury being made on the show as well, it seems the progress Troy has made is minimal. There's only one shot left for Troy Mason, and as the building emptied, the general consensus was that thReat might not be able to make it. Too much to fix, too little time.

Will Troy Mason go on to prove all of us wrong? Time will tell.
