Biography

Simon Krask... Mike McHale... Simon has been suffering from split personality since he was 18. Faced with a very scary past, this man has been through the worst. Seeing his mom murdered by his father? Living on the streets when he was 12? What would that do to you? Simon lived in Downtown Boston all his life, with his mom and his drunk father. His father used to beat him and his mom, but Simon took most of the beating because he would try and stop his dad from beating his mom. His mom, Christina Tript, married his father, Dale Krask, and they had Simon, and one other child but put the other child up for adoption because they knew they wouldn't be able to afford him.
When Simon was 12, he saw his drunken father beat his mother to death... Simon had no idea what to do. He was in shock. He loved his mom, but hated his dad. So he did what he thought would be the right thing to do, and he tried to kill his dad. Luckily Simon's neighbor heard all the commotion, and called the cops, because Dale was about to kill Simon as well. Dale put up a fight with the cops, and pulled out a shotgun, and shot one of the officers. Dale was shot 6 times in the chest, and killed.
After that, Simon ran away, and lived on the streets for two months. After being found by the Boston Police, Simon was put with a foster family. Chris and Amy Walker were his foster parents, and they did everything they could to try and make Simon happy. But they knew he was up too no good when he wasn't at home. Hanging with the wrong crowd, involved with drugs, and drug dealing, Simon found himself in trouble when he was 16. He was caught trying to sell cocaine. He spent a year in jail, and when he got out, he went right back to drug dealing. Simon hit it big. He made some major deals, and was now a multi-millionaire. When he was 18 he met Clarissa, his current girlfriend.
But when he was 18, he was told he was suffering from Split Personality. When Simon didn't take his medication, he turned in a man that you wouldn't wanna be around. He hated everyone and everything except for Clarissa, and one other person. Simon's long time friend, and fellow drug dealer... Matt Davies. They were best friends. They sold drugs together, and they did basically everything together.
Simon wanted to try something new when he turned 20, so he bust onto the wrestling seen a year later, after some intense training from long time friend, Trevor McKinnis. He had his own wrestling league, and gave Simon exposure. Simon didn't make it big until he joined FSW (Five Star Wrestling) a widely known wrestling organization, where he won the World Championship two times, along with the Five Star title, and the Tag-Team titles 4 times with E.O.E... Epitome of Evil. Simon finally stopped selling drugs, and focused just on wrestling. After the FSW closed down, Simon has been living with Matt and Clarissa in there Mansion in Amherst, NH. Simon has been trying to come back onto the wrestling scene, and he hopes that when he does, he wants to be in control once again.
