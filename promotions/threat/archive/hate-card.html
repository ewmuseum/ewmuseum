Biography

Born in Baltimore, Maryland, haTE and his family had to move to Japan after he was born. Ever since he was old enough to comprehend anything, he was working. With going to school AND trying to make money with limited oppertunities in Japan, haTE had a plateful.

Since he was 7, he was doing things he could in delhi's, getting little money for his hard work.

At the age of 11, he was shipped back to the States to get more oppertunities. He did all he could for his family while living with an aunt, but it was barely enough to help. It made little difference.

At 16, he moved back with his family and did a little more than what he could when he was younger.

haTE started wrestling in Japan at 18. He grew up in a family with 7 children, him being the oldest. There was one before him, but the baby died at birth.

With both parents working two jobs and haTE wrestling, they made just enough money to make it through. While wrestling in Japan, haTE had some nasty matches, where he broke bones and has some nasty scares to show for it.

Now twenty, haTE moved back to the States where more oppertunities lie for him. Still trying to support his parents and family back in Japan, haTE is trying to make it big. He debut in Professional Wrestling in Advantage Wrestling, but only made a short stint.
