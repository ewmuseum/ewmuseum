Welcome, and Hello avid readers. [name withheld] here, laying down the building blocks for a politically correct online.manual. There is never much to my introductions, so I'll just make this short and sweet. DON'T GIVE UP!
Concept
thReat is a combination, more so wrestling than fighting but they are a bit inter-twined. thReat has 4 championships altogether. Three wrestling titles and one fighting title. The wrestling championships will be defended in your run-of-the-mill wrestling matches from Standard rules to Hell in a Cell. The fighting title will be held in a steel cage with weapons strapped to the cage walls on the inside. There is one doorway in which the competitors enter then once they are in, the cage is locked shut. The only way to win in the fighting match is to knock your opponent out or make them say "I Quit".
Guidelines
- Section 1.1 (Character Development) Character Development is the single most important thing to keep in mind when producing flashes, or running angles. If your character develops, thReat develops. They go hand in hand in making thReat successfull and giving your character pushes. C.D. as I'll dub it, will be a good-sized part of who is pushed. So please try to have some story to your flashes, as I know that most (if not all) already do that, but this is just a little stone reminder. Also when developing your character, please try to keep it as real as possible...murders and such are alright, but make what happens real as well with the cops and judge etc. - Section 1.2 (Character Flashes) Ah, the joyous occasion when someone posts a valuable, angle & character furthering flash. We have a few simple guidelines for your flashes, but I think that all of you already know this, but for those who have not been apart of thReat, it may be of some value to them.

         Do not use anyone else's character in your flashes, unless permitted.      Keep Swearing to a minimum.      Keep your flashes real and interesting, as I will be reading them all!      Have fun writing your works and having others read them.
- Section 1.3 (Character Matches) This small section is only to deal with when your character has a match on the FLAW or PPV card. As you all know, angles are the biggest part of deciding who gets pushed, and who wins, etc. Flashes though further angles and characters and they are also a key factor in who wins. I don't care if you can describe a dog's butt to a tee, as long as you further your character and angle, you'll get good pushes. - Section 1.4 (Character Support/Attitude) Supporting characters for main characters in thReat are welcomed with open arms, it shows creativity. Just don't go too far out of hand with it, like having a supporting character pop up on every show, etc. Also, the attitude of you, the handler is very important. I have spoken to most of you and understand that you all very fun-loving people and your attitudes are very well-rounded. Keep it that way...LOL - Section 1.5 (Character Originality) Please do not copy anyone's work. If this is found out by any member of thReat or the thReat team, or even an innocent fan, we will ask for proof and then deal with the offender in a thReat court trial; more than likely, you will be kicked out on your booty. So keep that in mind, because we have the motto, "If you do the crime, you do the time." - Section 1.6 (Fun) The whole idea of thReat is to have as much fun as humanly, and for some, inhumanly possible. Please enjoy being a part of thReat in some way or another, you all are the building blocks to a hopefully successful promotion...let's make it happen!
