Biography

Frankie was born in a small southern town of a small population. Like most kids he was active in sports, although always hating the fact there was only one or two sports to participate in. In other words, the school where he attended didn't have a wrestling program. Fact is the school he attended barely had enough kids to field a team let alone have more than one form of athletics. Nevertheless Frankie's father thought football suited him his kid best and he wanted Frankie to follow in his footstepst. Everyone played in this one horse town. Fact is they played "Ironman Style." Even though they barely had enough kids to sport a team, Frankie spent the majority of his time riding the pine, even though it wasn't like his school could afford not to place him. Frankie's heart really wasn't into football, but he played just because his dad wanted him to. Needless to say Frankie's laziness, didn't exactly impress the coach much. But as fate would have it, Frankie was destined to play one way or the other. When the only blue chip player on the team, the quarterback went down, Frankie was called upon. Fourth and long, that little team from Star, Mississippi was down by two with the district title within reach and a shot at the state playoffs up for grab. Frankie stood at the 35 yard line ready for what was the biggest point in his life. With one swift kick he'd be hero, with another he'd end up in peril. Frankie ended up making the field goal, but in the process was physically anniliated by three of the other team's line backers. In the end, Frankie came through, but his offensive line gave way to Frankie suffering a concusion. Thus the Star High School's football team with it's lack of stars had their cinderella story ended in failure when it came state playoffs.

In the process of being hit by the larger players, Frankie lost consciousness and ended up in a coma for almost a year. Yes my friend, they knock the living daylights out of this lackadazial kid. During this time, Frankie's relatives would always come and set with him at his bedside. Although they had little population, the town was lucky enough to have a pretty good cable company which offered such channels as Turner South. Frankie's relatives loved this channel as it allowed them to watch wrestling classics like the Ric Flair/Ricky Steamboat match from days gone by just to name a one or two. There was all sorts of personalities from the 80's on the old NWA broadcast. Months past and out of the blue, Frankie suddenly woke up with no memory of who he was, where he was from, much less anything else other than knowing his name. And that being only from his uncle holler it so loudly when Frankie awoke from his year long slumber. The first thing he saw was the wrestling show, which sort of kick started something in Frankie. The moniker "flashback" came from the fact his grandma told him he needed to flash back to reality. Along with his parents, his other relatives thought Frankie was faking his amnesia just to get attention. Therefore Frankie was sort of ostracized by his family, who had a strong pride and couldn't deal with the fact they now had a "handicap" member. Sad to think that once loving family could turn their back on their own, but that's basically what it come to.

Indeed there was no place like home, but home was beginning to become hell. Not having much to keep him home anymore, Frankie struck out on the road looking for a place to call home. Thus trying to make a living on his own in the process. Striking out in a rusted two-tone 1972 Chevy pickup and barely enough gas money in his pocket, much to his chagrin, the truck ended up breaking down and Frankie ended up hitch-hiking. He luckily got a trucker who was on his way to New York to stop. He and Frankie shared several stories before they ended up at a truck stop in Jamestown, Virginia. Unfortunately Frankie got caught with his pants down and the trucker left Frankie standing in the truckstop bathroom with his penis in hand. Needless to say while Frankie was draining the main drain, the trucker grew impatient and left Frankie. Perhaps he was just lucky or it was just blind fate, but Frankie ended up telling his story to one of the locals who had been catching a bite to eat. This stranger and Frankie soon hit it off and after introductions, Frankie came to realize that the man he was speaking to was former wrestler, Darren James, now propieter and trainer of Jamestown Massacre Wrestling school. A few days later, Darren introduced Frankie to a few of the local farmers, who found a few odd jobs on the farms enabling Frankie to earn a some money. A month later, Frankie was training to become a professional wrestler, his life long dream. However the lack of money from being unable to find a decent job only allowed Frankie to spend about a half a year at the school. However Frankie's willingness to learn and ability to pick things up quickly helped him. Now Frankie finds himself jobless and looking for work in his "new love" professional wrestling. Through a few of his contacts, Darren learned that thREAT was hiring and now Frankie finds himself perhaps lucky or then again unlucky depending on how his future goes.
