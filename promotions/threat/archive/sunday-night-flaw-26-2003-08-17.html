Twizzy was finally fighting back against DeVante, ducking a roundhouse kick and scoring with an unorthodox belly-to-side suplex, before he pulled DeVante up and planted him with a vicious german suplex! Stevenson was naturally shocked, and as he wobbled to his feet, Twizzy continued to stun him with a reverse face-lock, before lifting DeVante up in the air...

And dropping him on his face in a reverse suplex! Massive move, that had DeVante grounded.

Rune Winters, meanwhile, had finally picked himself up and knocked the recovering Tyler Davidson with a forearm smash, before he pulled the Cruiserweight Champion up and tried to toss him over the topes. Davidson wisely held on to the ropes for dear life, however, and gave Rune a taste of his own medicine... with a mule kick, connecting spot-on with Rune's gonads. The former PIW man collapsed like timber to the canvas, as the fans went ballistic.

Before Tyler Davidson turned around and for some reason, locked in the Lockdown -- his version of an Indian Deathlock, designed to break the legs of any poor sucker in the hold.

Well... poor Winters.

Across the ring, Sergey had delivered the third elbow drop in succession, and pulled Neumeyer up to his feet, with massive crowd support spurring him on. Another kick to the midsection, and Sergey was all set to dish out more pain to Lawrence, who was breathing rather heavily. But surprisingly, as Kovhachek lifted him up for what seemed to be a suplex, Lawrence effortlessly floated over and landed behind Sergey.

Following which, the Harbinger of Revenge grabbed the back of the rookie's neck, sent his right knee crashing into the lower-spinal area, before brutally headbutting Sergey in the back of the head, sending him down to his knees... howling out in agony! Atken, being escorted up the ramp, applauded the move.

But, broke free once Lawrence pulled Sergey up by the hair and very easily threw him over the top rope. Phil was making a beeline for Sergey, not wanting to wait any longer until their PPV battle.

Eliminated: Sergey Kovhachek, by Lawrence Neumeyer

With Atken attacking the defenseless Sergey on the outside, and Twizzy grappling with DeVante over who'd eliminate who... there was enough entertainment to keep the crowd satisfied. However, Neumeyer himself was slightly bored. He looked around, and saw nothing that could pose a challenge to him.

So, he charged at Tyler Davidson and drove his foot into the back of his head, courtesy of a running sidekick. Rune Winters, who'd been tapping out for the past 30 seconds, was relieved... and overjoyed as he saw Lawrence pull Tyler up by his long bangs, and within a flash, the Cruiserweight Champion was flying over the top rope.

Eliminated.

Or so everybody thought. His feet hadn't touched the ground.

But Neumeyer didn't care. Instead, he pulled Rune Winters up and punched him harshly in the side of the face, before the Harbinger of Revenge slapped on a blatant choke, hoisted the former PIW man up in the hair, and effortlessly tossed him out of the ring like a banana skin. Just as Tyler stumbled back into the ring, holding the back of his head.

Eliminated: Rune Winters, by Lawrence Neumeyer

On the other side of the ring, DeVante had sent a mammoth uppercut crashing into Twizzy's jaw, which was followed up with a spinning backheel kick, and a running thrust kick... that naturally, sent Twizzy tumbling backwards. Eventually toppling over the top rope and onto the ground.

His little duel with DeVante having ended so suddenly, and with him on the losing side.

Shame.

Eliminated: Twizzy, by DeVante Stevenson

As DeVante turned around, he grinned at Lawrence Neumeyer who was still under the assumption that Tyler Davidson had been eliminated. And thus, it was only down to two. The thReat Cruiserweight Champion was all set to spoil the party, however, and he intended to do it in a big way.

A very big way.

First, he dived at Lawrence's knees, taking the red-eyed unremorseful monster with a chop block, before he rolled back up to his feet, finding himself standing in front of a baffled DeVante Stevenson.

DeVante, of course, got over the surprise of Neumeyer being taken down so easily and swung at Tyler. Who basically duck and retaliated with an uppercut, a kick to the ribs, a series of five stinging chops to DeVante's chest, and to top it all off, a wonderful Five Star Slam which the Five Star Superstar had been working hard to perfect over the last couple of months. With half the crowd cheering for him, the former(and last ever) IOW Extreme Champion pulled Stevenson up...

Wary of the fact that Lawrence was recovering...

And quickly tossed DeVante over the top, drawing one of the biggest pops of the night.

Eliminated: DeVante Stevenson, by Tyler Davidson

With the field having now been reduced to two, Tyler quickly turned around and rushed at the recovering Harbinger of Revenge, aiming to level him with a clothesline. Lawrence swiftly ducked it, and smacked his lips as the Cruiserweight Champion came bouncing off the ropes.

Because...

...

Lawrence exploded out of the starting blocks...

And without a care for his own safety...

...

Executed a picture-perfect butterfly kick.

INTERNECINE.

And just like that, it was all over. Tyler Davidson had soared out of the ring, over the top rope, and landed on the ground awkwardly. The scene resembled a car wreck, and the crowd were silenced. Lawrence had managed to come out victorious, and in his first ever official match in thReat. In... hell, the bloody industry.

He was going to Double Or Nothing as the #1 Contender.

So, it was only fitting that the champion decided to come right out, aye?

Winner: Lawrence Neumeyer, eliminating Tyler Davidson to confirm it

One Last Showdown

"Headstrong" by Trapt.

The crowd went wild. General David Sanders marched out, with two items of particular importance to Lawrence Neumeyer in his hands. One, was the thReat World Title. The other, was a video tape that was labelled 'JAPAN'. Sanders had viewed it earlier in the show, and he apparently thought it had a lot of significance to what was brewing between him and Lawrence.

Neumeyer remained in the ring, smirking, as he carefully eyed David... who'd quickly rolled into the ring and demanded for the music to be cut.

So, the music was cut. And the fans were silenced again. This was a side of the General they weren't used to seeing.

David stared at Lawrence.

Lawrence stared back.

The General looked at his World Title and sniggered, before tossing the tape onto the canvas...

And...

Crushing it with his boot.

"See you in Las Vegas."

And so, with that, the General quickly left the ring, leaving Lawrence Neumeyer to look down at the broken pieces of the video tape, David's words continuing to ring in his head. The crowd were definitely confused. After weeks of end-show mayhem brought about by the Harbinger of Revenge, he seemed amazingly tame on the night.

In truth, he was tired of the games.

"Looking forward to it."

It was time to roll the dice, and gamble on his future. Simple as that.

DOUBLE OR NOTHING.
