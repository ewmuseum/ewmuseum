Biography

Copenhagen, the capitol and pride of Denmark. Home to many of the most wealthy inhabitants this fair country holds. Included in this fold, are the family that bears the name Krone. This name is known countrywide, not only because it shares the name of Denmark's currency, but the family has also made themselves virtual celebrities. Immanuel Krone, Noah's grandfather founded Denmark's most well known chain of hotels, Ocean Centre. After his passing, 51% of the company went to his son, Noah Krone I. Noah was a well respected businessman, and not the usual cutthroat most of the other individuals in the business were. Unfortunately, this led to his demise. Shortly after his sons birth in 1977, he was set up by the remaining 49% vote of the company, which led to his eventual impeechment.

With a solid bank account, but little respect left in his own country, Noah took his wife Elena, and 1 year old son Noah II to the land of the free, America. The transition wasn't too difficult for the family, for Noah and Elena already spoke fluent English. Rather then moving inward, they remained on the east coast, making their home in Queens. It was here that Noah took his previous fortune, and invested it into a computer company. His wife called him crazy ... that was until he made his next million. The Krone family lived well in their new home, Noah I telling his son of stories back in their homeland. By the age of 13, Noah II had grown quite the distaste for tyrany. He loved his father dearly, and despised basically anyone who had done the man wrong. It was around this time when Noah found the internet, and let his reign of terror begin.

By the age of 16, Noah hacked his first site. It was his school of all places, and a simple hacking at that. The rush of destroying all that made this world terrible, was tremendous for Noah. Within the year, he hacked an oil companies bank account, and transferred 25 million dollars in funds to various foundations. This hack alone gave him the nickname 'dRill.' It went on for two more years, Noah developing skills few had posessed when it came to hacking. He had this amazing ability to see the patterns behind numbers. With a simple glimpse, he could pick apart a code like it was nothing. Unfortunately for him, he slipped up on a single job. During an attempt to hack into the stock market, he got caught up in a firewall. With heightened security surrounding the servers, Noah was caught.

The young man was taken to court at the age of 19. By the end of the trial, he somehow was found not guilty of all charges. Apparently he was able to destroy all traces of evidence, so no proof of his crimes were revealed. However, his computer was destroyed ... and his mother felt it was time the young 'punk rock' kid found another hobby.

Prior to the age of 19, Noah had no formal fighting or wrestling experience. It wasn't until his mother made him attend a Tae-Kwon-Do class, simply to avoid other 'demons' that were in his life, that he got involved in any athletic sport. It did however intrigue Noah, and he quickly excelled. The martial arts led to other contact sports, one being shootfighting. He saw a group of guys participating in this phenomenon in the gym's backroom, and was immediately hooked. Over the next 3 years, Noah spent most his time in the Dojo, training under the tutelage of his sensei, Natsu Guyen. Noah began attending, and winning tournaments. Noah made the competitions fun to watch. His spiked hair and unorthodox style gained a lot of attention. It was at an event in November '98' that a man by the name of Nick Cyprus attended a show.

This was no ordinary scout, this man was the World Champion of a little known wrestling promotion called the GWI (Gladiator Wrestling International). He spoke with Noah some after a match, and convinced him to attend a GWI training session. Noah did just that, and a whole world of destruction was opened for him. This was Noah's chance to restart what he had began 8 years earlier. This time it would be the Sports Entertainment industry that would be his target. So he trained, and in less then a year, was on GWI shows. After a string of good fortune and victories, Krone recieved a contract from the NAWA, GWI's parent company. It was then when Noah was able to unleash his true power. In less then a year, the NAWA's front office was placed behind bars, due to illegal use of fund management. To this day none of them every admitted to it. Oddly enough, young Noah had gotten his computer back.
