Carys

Real Name: Carys Callicker Nickname: "Cannon Fodder" Also known as: --

Born: 20/11/1985 Place of Birth: Manchester, England Hometown: Philadelphia, Pennsylvania

Height: 5 Foot 7 Inches Weight: 112 pounds Blood type: A Negative Eye color: Sky blue

Team: None

Manager: None Trainer: Jade

Theme song: 'Trigger' by InFlames

Style

Competing style: 'Cangurú feroz de guerra' (A fierce Luchadoristic and Technical style, blended with a high damaging use of her knees and elbows, taught to her by her mentor Jade)

Signature moves:
Falling from Grace - Submission: Carys uses the bottom rope as a springboard to spear the knee of the opponent, when the opponent is down on the ground she then hooks them in a Reverse Figure Four Leck Lock.

Special delivery: Carys slings the opponent against the ropes and runs against the opposite ones. She bounces of the second rope and hits a springboard temple kick to the face with her let foot and follows it with a roundhouse kick to the temple in the while still in the air.

Star Chaser: Carys runs up the ropes to the top one and balances as she runs across the top one before reaching the middle of the ropes (abut ten feet) where she leaps of the rope and hits a Senton body splash to her opponent. (Kinda like Jeff Hardys run across the rail thing only she runs across the ropes.)

Precision: She hits a Very fast release german suplex and then catches him in a neck breaker!

Cannon Fodder: Carys dazes the opponent with a quick jumping knee to the chin or general facial area. She then jumps to the second rope and spins in mid air. She managaes to twist so that her feet land under the chin of the opponent, she then backflips keeping the feet under the chin and staggering them backwards.

Carys' fighting methods:

Larger Opponents: Carys will use her speed and technical ability to dissasemble her opponent as fast as she can before they get a grip on her.

Smaller Opponents: If there ever is a smaller opponent then Carys will use ONLY striking moves to take them apart until the finish.

Heels: When Carys faces a heel her first and only method is to attempt to out cheat them. General focus areas on the body will be groin, eyes and nose.

Faces/Neutrals: Carys will always cheat in her matches. Whether she is winning or not. Always have her cheat.

Males: Carys cheats an awful lot like any other match, will try her very best to insult and undermine any respect they have...again lots of groin shots.

General: Carys will always take the oppurtunity to make a springboard something or other. Whether it is from the ropes, top turnbuckle or the apron.
