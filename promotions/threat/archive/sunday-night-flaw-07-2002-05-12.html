Sunday Night Flaw 07

12 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
We're far from there. Far from the cheers of thReat's Flaw. Far from Jakks, Jon Hackett, phYre and crew. Far from the cage that he owns.

Quincy Brown is far from it all.

He's here...

...San Antonio, Texas...

The place of his biggest accomplishment to date, the place where Quincy Thomas Brown made his mark.

The place where Quincy Brown escaped death!

Quincy lifts up his head, and raises his eyes to a placard at the top of a doorway.

"SAN ANTONIO CORRECTIONAL FACILITY"

"One final fight, its me and you motherfucker!"

Quincy climbs up the steps, and shoves through the doors and disappears inside.

The door shuts, as we finally arrive to Hartford, Connecticut...

The home of Flaw 7.

Pumped

"Sell-out!," yelled the ecstatic Jon Hackett.

He has been this way since ReLoad last week. Ratings were through the roof, and was for at least one night, the most watched program around the globe. He was on a complete high. thReat has hit it's peak since ReLoad. The Shopzone has sold a new record of merchandise for a week as well as ticket sales. Hartford was holding thReat's largest capacity in it's short history.

"Life is good, life is good. I'll call my momma! Today is her day!," shrieked Jon. Hackett closed the door in his office and headed backstage as the scene faded.

Steven Solex vs. Frank Nock

"You ready?"

Steven Solex looked on to Frank Nock, who sat on a production trunk just behind the 'curtain'. Nock held his head down, he was focused, he heard nothing, just the slow breathing coming from himself. He felt a hand touch his shoulder, "I said, are you ready?" He looked up, Steven Solex...a fighter like himself, well almost. He was younger; and to the older man, he looked 'harmless'.

Frank just nodded, he didn't want to speak, he couldn't. "Awake" by Godsmack started, as Steven took one last look at Frank, then walked off. Solex trudged down the ramp, his blue eyes surveying the crowd; he slid into the ring using the apron for leverage, as he loosened himself up, smiling.

"Eye of the Tiger" by White Snake blurred, the old copy was still useful to Frank. He hopped off the trunk, splitting the curtain, wasting no time getting towards Steven. He slid under the ropes, no time to get up. Solex rushed him, delivering stomps to the weary back of Frank.

Nock went down again, and again, he smiled at the pain, back to normal. Frank again tried to escape by punching Solex. Steven laughed at the old man, picking him up by the hair, bear hugging him. He did this for a few more seconds, then belly to Belly Suplexed him down to the mat. Fatigue began to set in. Steven picked Frank up, then began to bring him to one of the four corners of the ring. He began to punish him in the corner.

Solex then threw him to the other side of the ring. He stayed on the opposite side of the ring. He then began to take of the turnbuckle padding from the top turnbuckle. While he was taking the padding off, the referee was busy checking to see if the old man was able to continue. Frank stepped forward, as Solex turned around into a drop kick, Steven fell to the floor, but got up quickly was given a Stalling Suplex by the forty-one year old man.

Frank stomped away at Steven’s legs, in attempt to keep him grounded, something he learned in the underground. Solex got up, flooring Nock to the ground, and went for a leg drop, only for Nock to roll out of the way. Solex grabbed the ropes to hall his massive body back up vertical as Frank, trying to end it, with a schoolboy pin.

Almost; not quite.

With Nock now grounded, Solex went to work on his lower back. Stomp, followed stomp, followed stomp. Then a splash; each pound of his 276 pound mass driven down across Frank’s spine, sending his nerves into a frenzy. Then, whilst Frank groaned, Steven returned to his feet. Despite taking than most would he still had time to copy his glance to one side, then to the other; to acknowledge.

Both men rose at roughly the same time, but Solex beat Frank to the kick in the stomach. Frank keeled over, “Fuck you,” were the only words Nock could get out before he was subjected into vicious, match-ending contact with the knees of Steven Solex.

He was in the mix now, and he loved it.

Solex let the old man drop, then secured his knockout.

Bitter

Jakks sat in his lockerroom, staring at himself in the mirror, messing with his blonde goatee. He turned away from the mirror and quickly darted to his locker. He opened it up and pulled out his gym bag. He pulled out a file folder with the name of Alex Sanders, phYre, printed on top of the manila organizer. He opened it up as phYre's birth certificate fell out.

"Whoops."

Jakks put it back in the folder as he sat down, reading all of phYre's information on file. He had been tampering with confedenitial files.

"Interesting...This will come in handy tonight."

Jakks shut the folder and threw it back into his locker then slammed the door shut as he looked very, very amused.

Showtime

Backstage at Reload, "Beautiful" Riot sat hunched over in a chair in his locker room. One week prior he had made an open challenge to the public for someone to face him for his Aggression title.

No one took him up on the offer.

Riot was infuriated that no one would face him for his title.

He wouldn't be turned down again.

Riot looked over at his title belt, laying in a pile on the corner of his gym bag. He shook his head as he ran his fingers over his face, stopping to poke at the bandage that covered his gash from Araknis. Araknis had already learned his lesson, he had his chance and blew it. Now Riot was offering some other random slob a chance to pick up where Araknis had failed, to become thReat's Aggression champion, but it seemed no one wanted it. Almost as if everyone was afraid to face Riot.

Well no more.

Riot was going to lay down another challenge, he wasn't leaving until someone accepted. Even if it meant he had to pull some bum from the crowd to fight him, he was going to have a challenger.

"Showtime."

Aggravated

Araknis stomped backstage, angry over his loss at ReLoad.

"Hackett!"

Hackett turned around with a danish stuffed in his mouth. He took his time to swallow what he had bitten off and responded.

"Hey Scott, what's up?"

"This is what's up. I want a match with Takisuma Ogama, TONIGHT. Title or no title, it doesn't matter, I'm going to prove myself one way or another.

"Oh are you?"

"I am."

"Well then Araknis, you've got it. After phYre and Jakks, you're up."

"About damn time things are going my way," said Araknis as he walked away to get ready for his matchup.

Grudge Match

phYre vs. Jakks

"Fuel" by Metallica hit as phYre ran to the ring, ready to gain some more revenge on Jakks. The man who sent him to jail by framing him for possession of illegal drugs. He got to the apron before he was abruptly interrupted.

"Stop right there!"

It was the voice of Jakks. phYre's theme had died down to nothing as Jakks stood ontop of the ramp with a microphone in hand.

"So phYre, or should I say...Alex Sanders. You've had a rough life haven't you."

phYre looked puzzled as he nodded to the question and motioned for Jakks to get down to the ring so they could start their match.

"Hold on you stupid bastard."

The crowd booed as Jakks was onto something.

"Mother's Day must be hard for you Alex, you know, not having a good mother and all."

phYre's expression turned to rage in mere seconds. Jakks pulled the folder seen before from behind his back and opened it up and began reading.

...My mother coulnd't handle my father's death. She began drinking, and drinking...and drinking. She soon became abusive and beat me. I had to leave, but before I could go on my own, I was thrown out. Jessica Sanders, my mother, threw me out of my own home, and left me for dead.

"There's a little excerpt of the resume you turned into Kage Fighting Incorporated last August. Sad isn't it?"

"HOW THE FUCK DID YOU GET THAT?!," screamed phYre.

"Aww, calm down you ignorant orphan."

phYre snapped and sprinted full speed up the ramp at Jakks. The began trading punches and Jakks got the upperhand, then threw phYre off the stage, 20 feet down onto the concrete and chairs below.

"This is a knockout, correct? Heh, who cares, I'm going to leave him...just like his mother did."

The Deal

Flaw returned from the commercial break, but we aren't in the arena; we're back where we started, San Antonio, Texas.

"Well, well, well; just the man I was looking for, you got my money?"

The chief of police had a smug look on his face, rubbing his hands together in anticipation of the "dead presidents" he's about to recieve.

"Yeah, I got your money!"

It was Quincy Brown. Not customary to payoffs, he reached into his pocket and pulled out a wad of cash and handed it over.

"Right this way, I know exactly who you want to see."

Brown followed the chief of police, who counted the wad, as we faded back to Hartford, CT.

I have control

"Fuck, my head hurts.. damn migraine."

Mathew Alan Woj stepped slowly through the doors of the Hartford Civic Center. Cradling his head in his hands, he grimaced in pain. His expression changed little as he looked at the lineup for the night. Three matches had been signed. Steven Solex taking on Frank Nock... phYre versus Jakks... And some kind of mystery man taking on Riot.

"Three matches.." he muttered to himself, "Three fucken matches, this place doesn't even know how to set up a decent card for the fans. So long as the mystery man isn't a chink or a nigger, I'm good."

Who was he talking to?

"Talkin' to myself again... people are going to think I have problems..."

And so the mystery was cleared up as he walked beyond the cameras. The fans at the Hartford Civic Center gave him a mixed reaction throughout it.

Flip.

Switch. Another camera.

He walked upon the doors of Jon Hacketts office, just busted right in. He knew full well the cameras had been trained on him throughout his sojourn into the arena. He knew that Jon Hackett would be waiting for him with a small stack of papers. He had had his lawyer look them over himself.

He walked up to the desk of Hackett...

"Mr. Woj," begins Hackett, "Welcome! I'm very happy to..*"

"Shutup. Give me the paper, a pen, and shut your mouth."

He is handed a pen and paper and a stack of papers. He signs the papers and takes them for later perusal, he has three days. If he feels like it, he can nullify the papers, so long as he tears the contract in under three days.

"Mr Hackett, the card tonight is nothing more than a disgrace. I expect it to be quite better next week. As you have given me some minor booking powers in our agreement," the crowd reacted with some minor shock, anticipating this reaction, Mr. Woj continues, "for my own matches. I would like to book myself against ... well, this guy. I noticed he hadn't competed yet. A Mr. Studd... spelled with a dollar sign."

"Alright," said Mr. Hackett, "JJ $tudd is a developmental talent, I suppose I could sign you with him next week. Good luck, we're happy to have you on."

Jive exited the room with a slight swagger, knowing he was fully in charge of the situation. Mr. Jon Hackett dropped his head into his hands, and sighed, this was a bad bad decision...

Araknis vs. Takisuma Ogama

A Japanese sympathy played over the system as Takisuma Ogama made his way down the entrance to the ring. The now somewhat arrogant champion maked the statement clear that he wanted to pick up a victory over his opponent Araknis. Ogama strutted somewhat down to the ring as his International championship shown in the eyes of the fans.

At this time, "Forgotten" by Linkin Park blared over the audio sound waves as Araknis made his way out into the entrance way. The fans stood to there feet to watch as Katanya Lee made her way out standing in front of Araknis. She studied his body before he walked on by her and down into the ringside area.

Araknis glanced up at his opponent before he hopped into the ring with him. Takisuma smiled as he realized that his title wasn't on the line in this match. He laid his valuable item beside the ring and challenged his opponent to enter the ring. Araknis didn't look too scared. He climbed eagerly into the ring and looked into the eyes of the International champion.

The referee signaled for the bell and the match began. Ogama started it off with a lock up, attempting to bring his opponent down quickly before the match got too far underway. Ogama slammed Araknis down into the ring. His shoulders bounced off the mat and Ogama pinned him down with his knees. Araknis looked into his face and laid a stiff hand into his jaw. Ogama fell backwards into the ring ropes, and tangled himself up.

Araknis stood to his feet and watched as the champ tried to pry his way out of the ropes. The champion, Ogama, couldn't do it in time. Raky bounced off the ropes and headed in the direction of the entangled Takisuma. The Forgotten One laid a stiff drop kick down into the face of the champion, it freed his arms from the brutal entanglement.

Scott Lee laid down across his opponent, hoping for a quick victory.

Ogama raised his stiff boot into the face of the high flyer. Lee flew backwards holding his face. The blow he sustained was enough to bust his face open quite a bit.

The blood was in a small puddle by the time that Ogama made his way over to Araknis. It seemed to build the confidence of the champion. He picked up the now Bloody Forgotten One and sent him flying over the ring to the outside.

Ogama bounced off the ropes awaiting the arrival of his opponent.

He didn't notice anything had happened as the champion seemed to trip and fall. Raky approached him and gave him an armbar. He wrenched up on Ogama's arm attempting to eliminate the champion from the match. Takisuma's arm burned as Scott Lee pried up on it forcefully. He showed absolutely no remorse as he tried to take the arm completely out from his opponent.

No, once more the champion pried his shoulder up off of the ground. He stood to his feet quickly, even before Raky could. Ogama waited as Araknis turned around and met him with a stiff standing kick. Knocking him off balance and into the ropes. He wasn't tangled up, just resting basically. Now, Ogama ran at him and gave him another clothesline to the outside of the ring. Katanya was on the other side of the ring. She tried to rush around to get to him, but Ogama was already on the outside of the ring, beating on Raky.

Mrs. Lee came from behind and attempted to take Ogama off of him, but it didn't seem to work. Takisuma continued to beat on him as the wife tried and tried to pull him off of Araknis. The blood started to become deeper as the two men pounded eagerly on each other. It wasn't a one man battle now, they were both swinging. The referee was already to the five count as the two kept on beating. Katanya gave Ogama kick to the midsection. Just swiftly enough to avoid contact from the referee. He fell backwards and she threw him up into the ring. The referee was up to seven, Katanya picked up Scott Lee and tossed him into the ring before the two men were both eliminated.

Ogama stood over his lifeless opponent and watched as the blood poured from his forehead. Takisuma stood up and bounced off of the ropes. The referee stood in the way and was wiped out. Ogama continued towards his opponent. Ogamacity. TO hooked on his finishing move, but the referee is down. Araknis kicks around and had both of his feet on the ropes trying to break loose. Katanya from behind. She blasted Takisuma in the head with a chair. The blood trickled from his forehead. Raky woke up the referee in time for the Strangled Web. The referee saw no sign of conscience behavior in Ogama so he rang the bell.

Another Guest Appearance?

Camera panned through the crowd to see all the thReat fans with their signs. Suddenly the camera panned to a big commotion on the right side of the ring. The camera saw five security guards escorting 'Superstar' Vince Jacobs and his wife Victoria walking to two empty seats in the front row. The fans went crazy to see the superstar walking among them. They tried to touch him as the security tried to keep their arms and hands back so Vince and his wife can get to their seats. Vince seated his wife in the chair and turned around and acknowledged the crowd's reaction to his appearance. Vince sat down to enjoy another thReat presentation.

Let's Chat

Wandering around backstage, we catch a glimpse of "Beautiful" Bobby Blair. Sporting a cheap blue business suit, he struts his stuff down the hall. He turns the corner, takes a drag from his cigar, then stops in front of a door.

The camera flows to the door, which reads "Araknis". Bobby knocks then decides to let himself in, catching the Japanese superstar offguard.

"Out," Araknis didn't even have to look at the man standing in his doorway, he just wanted him gone.

Bobby stops dead in his tracks, extending his hand he holds out a VHS tape.

"Now, there's no need for hostility. I'm here on good terms. I have something that I'd like you to take a look at."

Araknis just stares at the tape in his hand.

"First off, you violate my time by just waltzing in here, and second, why would I take anything from you? I have no idea in the slightest want you want with me. I already defeated your man."

A little frightened he sets the tape down on a table and begins to plead his case.

"Take a look at your record Araknis, then take a look at Ogama's. Neither are what you would call dominant numbers. We both know the hardcore Japanese style struggles in America, that's why I have a proposition for you. . ."

"....Keep talking.." Araknis ponders.

??? vs. Riot

"Whole" by Flaw began to play as the Aggression champion made his way out. His head was heavily bandaged from his match at Reload. He held his title high over his head, and the fans booed. He sauntered to the ring, confident in his open challenge. He felt that after his systematic destruction of all challengers, no one would be fool enough to take him up. He ordered the ring announcer to give him the microphone.

"Well, it looks like no one had the balls to accept my challenge. Not that I expected any different. I mean... fuck, look at the way that I took apart all of my other opponents... in my first match in thReat, I took Bavin up high and dropped him to the concrete with a top rope Powerbomb. I've sandwiched Lennox Dawson's head between a chair and a fucking van. I defeated Araknis last Sunday, despite a whole in my head the size of my fucking thumb. I beat him soundly, as a matter of fact... I made him my bitch. And then I issue a challenge. I didn't expect anyone to be stupid enough to take it... it was more of a time killer. Something to do while the blood fell from me. But tonight, I'm fucking serious. I want one of you backstage to come out here and face me for the Aggression title... I want to show the fans what an exercise in futility looks like."

The fans booed. And then Lawrence Clamentia made his way down. The fans cheered, but not because they liked him. They cheered because he shut Riot up. Clamentia rolled under the bottom rope, and Riot whipped him with the belt. Clamentia yelled out in pain. and rolled back out of the ring. He got onto his knees, but Riot stood on the bottom rope and whipped the belt at him again. The belt whizzed by Clamentia's head, and he reached back and caught it. He pulled it as hard as he could, managing to pull Riot over the top rope, right on top of him. Both men laid sprawled out on the mats surrounding the ring, but Riot moved first.

He crawled towards the ring skirting, tossing it out of his way. His upper body disappeared for a few minutes while he rummaged around, looking for a suitable weapon. While he was under the ring, Clamentia got to his feet. He was staggering, but he braced himself against the guard rail. Finally Riot reappeared, skillet in hand. He hurried to his feet and noticed Clamentia standing dizzy. He swing the skillet at his head, but Clamentia ducked. As Riot finished swinging the pot, Clamentia came up underneath him and pulled him off his feet, delivering a fireman's carry, tossing him over the rail. The back of Riot's head hit the top of the rail as he fell, opening up a small wound. Clamentia got up and hopped the rail, following Riot. He landed close to Riot, and grabbed a chair from a fan.

He held the chair up high above his head, and swung it down to Riot with great force. Somehow, Riot managed to do a back roll and evade the chair shot. He got to one knee, and Clamentia swung the chair at his head. Riot ducked backwards, bending so that his head almost touched the ground. Clamentia noticed this and stomped on Riot's midsection, forcing him down on his head. Riot fell back down, and Clamentia tried to stomp on him again. Riot caught his foot, twisted it, and forced Clamentia to the ground. Riot got to his feet and pulled Clamentia up. He grabbed Clamentia by the back of his head and tossed him into a large group of fans. They moved quickly, and Clamentia was spent sprawling into a row of empty chairs. Riot walked after Clamentia, breathing heavily.

He pulled Clamentia to his feet, but Clamentia punched him in the stomach, forcing him to double over. Clamentia quickly got up and grabbed Riot in a front face lock, right before driving Riot's skull into the concrete floor. Clamentia grabbed Riot and pulled him up again, once more kicking him in the stomach. He put Riot's head between his legs and tried to lift him for a powerbomb. Riot powered out and back dropped the back of Clamentia's head onto an open chair seat. Clamentia grabbed the back of his head in obvious pain, while Riot took a moment to catch his breath. Riot grabbed Clamentia's legs, putting a foot under each of his arms.

He fell backwards violently, sending Clamentia hurtling through the air, and barley over the guard rail. Clamentia spilled out onto the other side, landing close to one of the ringposts. He followed through on the toss, though, and managed to roll to his feet. Riot came bounding towards the railing, and before Clamentia knew what was going on, Riot leapt over and gave Clamentia a flying clothesline, forcing his head into the turnbuckle. Clamentia crumpled up, and fell into a seated position.

Riot reached down and picked up the skillet he dropped earlier, and wielding it like a baseball bat, he swung it at Clamentia's head once more. Clamentia ducked and rolled to his feet, getting into an offensive stance when he got up. Riot turned around, and Clamentia ran towards him. Just a few feet away from Riot, Clamentia put out his right leg, trying to execute the Cutlass Edge. Riot sidestepped the move, but barley. While Clamentia's leg was still in the air, Riot grabbed it, pulled him closer, and kicked him below the belt. Clamentia fell to one knee in obvious pain. Riot put him in a front face lock, and slowly turned him over, obviously going for the Crowd Control. He snapped Clamentia around, and dropped him with an Ace Crusher, effectively ending the match. The count was just a formality.

This Is What Happens Afterwards

Xaxon held the title in his arms as he sat in the middle of the ring. The pleasure he felt as he looked down upon another belt to add to the vast collection. He could hear music coming from some unknown source as the people screamed loudly. What were they chearing for? Were they cheering for his good performance for his age? What?

His head spun around faster and faster as he circled the arena. It revolved so many times he could tell what everyone was wearing. He memorized them all.

It was just an imagination.

He fell over face first to the mat, breathing heavily, jaw ricocheting off the mat. Blood poured from his mouth.

Nothing.

Xaxon was underwater. Everything around him was black. He sat underwater, with the blood still pouring from his mouth and coloring the water around his face. His eyes were open, and he stared into nothing. He just wanted to see something. He no longer breated, he long moved. He just hoped to stare into something, or see something. It had been like this for too long.

When you're old, and you push your body to the limits, this is what happens.

Light.

"FUCK!"

Xaxon sat in his bed with an ice pack on his head. For the past week he had been treated by his long time private doctor. The doctor said he had a weak head, from being dropped on it so many times.

"'Vhen can I get back in the ring doc?" Xaxon asked.

The doctor was finally going to break it to him after all these years. It took a lot of courage to say it...

"Gregor, I think it's time...I think it's time..." he trailed off.

"'Vell fucking say it man!" Xaxon shouted, and then held his chest in pain.

"I think it's time you called it quits. You're too old, and you're too damn old man. Get your head out of your arse, your too damn old, and if you do this much longer you're going to die man."

Xaxon didn't reply. He knew this was true. He knew his time was coming to an end, and look at him, he's the thReat world champion. Xaxon cursed as he rolled over and looked at the thReat title laying across the room on the table. Was it really worth it? Was his life hanging on the edge of a cliff really worth it.

Yes it was.

Xaxon closed his eyes, and he found himself once again drowning in the black water. He once again searched for something to see, and after an eternity of realizing he wouldn't find anything, he closed his eyes, and then opened them back to the world and remembered he was champion.
