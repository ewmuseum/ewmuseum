Historical Info

Biography

Drake has always been known as a loose wire. Always being dragged up on his attitude problem. He never took any notice of it. From a young age he wanted to get into the military. After hard work and intense training he qualified into the military. However there was one problem....

Drake was always insulting those less talented then him. In the military everbody is told to trust the man next to him; but this wasn't the same with Drake. Nobody could trust him because he was always looking out for number one and that was all.

There was a fellow military member who slept on the bunk below him, these two guys had it in for each other since they met eyes. They were both known as the most competitive members of the military, and everybody knew these two wouldn't get along.

Drake always wanted to be the best at everything; and he made sure he was no matter what it took. But Paul (Drakes enemey) was probably as talented as Drake was in every aspect. When they had obstable courses these two would always be head to head. However this one time Drake had dripped Paul in one of the courses to make sure of his victory; this didn't go down well with anybody.

Drake's attitude which everyone had thought had calmed down was provoked by the general's shouting at him for his childish antics. Drake was made to stay out all night in the rain running through the same course over and over again, and was forced to apologise to Paul for the incident. Drake was severly embarrased at this point and didn't take kindly to it.

The Generals knew that Drake was a huge problem; but like everyone else believed the military would bring out the better in him. But after the embarrasment Drake did something that risked his life....

There was a live ammo drill; Drake was lying there on the ground behind some sand bag's watching Paul with so much animosity withing him. That is when Drake made the biggest mistake of his life, Drake carefully positioned himself where he wouldn't be on focus by everybody, and with one pull of the trigger, hit Paul directly in the chest. Nobody realised at first because it wasn't obvious; with all the noise and all. However when it came to the time when they were asked to stop they noticed Paul there. Everybody ran to Paul checking his pulse to see if he was alive; Drake just stood there with a smirk upon his face. He soon lost the smirk and tried to act upset and surprised about what happened.

The next day Drake was requested to go to the head man's office. They knew that Drake was trouble and they all knew that it was him who killed Paul. When they asked Drake about it he denied it at first, but once the generals told him that they knew it was him he soon admitted to it.

However Drake wasn't reported to the police because the military knew what would happen to them, they knew that if it got out that one of there men was killed by his supposed 'Team Mate' then all hell would break loose. They told Drake that he was excused from the military, and he was never aloud back. Drake took this chance as he knew going to prison is the worse thing that could happen to him.

Drake tells this story everytime another Paul comes along, everytime somebody challeneges Drake he lets them know what they are in for..
