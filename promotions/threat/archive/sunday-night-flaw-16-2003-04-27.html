Finalising Details

"So, Troy. Ready to talk business?"

Troy Mason walked into Mason's office with five of his bodyguards. Teenagers, of British descent. Obviously the lackeys of one Vincent Pembridge, who apparently didn't feel like talking to Mason, and thus had Hughes do the honours of making the match that would decide the fate of the company. Just like it was meant to be many months ago, when Jon Hackett was at the helm.

Mason looked up from the stack of documents on his table, and frowned as his eyes met those of Ty's. Who had, for weeks, been impersonating as a Mexican wrestling superstar by the name of El Crackerooni. Only last week was it revealed that Crackerooni was a front. One devised by Vincent Pembridge from the start, no doubt. Which really goes to show the lengths to which the Scorpion of Manchester has gone to. All for the sake of power, and revenge.

They do say vengeance is an aphordisiac of some sort.

"Yes, I am. Let's get this over with, I don't wish to have scum like you in my office much longer than I have to."

"Aww, feeling a wee bit bitter, are we Troy? Come on, how could you not have seen this coming? Wait, I guess you don't catch tA programming. Lucky. Even if you did, I bet Joe would freak out if a pussy like you was watching the likes of me tearing new ones. Not that I have any respect for that wanker. But I digress. Let's go over the basics, shall we?"

Mason bit his lower lip, before he opened the folder that was at the top of the stack of documents and looked at it. Inside was the contract which Vincent Pembridge had his lawyer type up, with regards to the match at the upcoming PPV. One which would really set the tone for the rest of thReat's existence. Either it would be 100% wrestling or 100% fighting. It was that simple.

"Alright, Hughes. Five fighters versus five wrestlers. The fighters will be Vincent, yourself, Christian Embers, Ampske & Bobby Knickerson. You guys won't be allowed to name any replacements should any unprecendented injuries occur. The group of teeangers that work for Vincent Pembridge will NOT be allowed at ringside.

The five wrestlers will be the ones I choose, and at my disposal. As per MY status in this company, I have the right to withold who my team will consist of until whenever the hell I want to. Furthermore, I will be allowed to be at ringside during this Fighters/Wrestlers Showdown.

If you, The Vagabonds, happen to win... thReat will become an actual fighting promotion. No more wrestling matches, unless stated otherwise. The board of directors will appoint Vincent and yourself as the new owners as thReat. I will step down as owner.

However, if the wrestling team end up winning, this hybrid nonsense ends, and thReat returns to the wrestling-oriented company it was when I brought it back. The Vagabonds will be forced to become wrestlers, and your contracts are thus iron-clad. Meaning in the case of a walk-out, you gentlemen will suffer huge losses. The only way there will be a breakeven is if in the event that you get fired.

And the only person who can fire you... is ME."

Ty Hughes nodded, as an evil grin crept onto his face, almost as if he realised something. Mason noticed this and frowned again, wondering exactly what was going on in Ty's mind. He didn't have much time to do any investigating at all, as Hughes grabbed the folder out of the owner's hands and grabbed a pen off the table, before signing the document. Throwing the folder back, Ty Hughes watched as Mason too signed the contract, which pretty much set the stage for the upcoming PPV in thReat.

Which, in a strange sense of foreboding, was named... Malevolence. The exact same name Hackett had given the PPV which never transpired last year.

"That's all there is to it, then. See you at Malevolence, Mason. And oh yeah, Vincent made some fights for the night. Hope you don't mind... boss."

Troy leaned back in his seat and cursed, remembering that the hybrid activity started from that night on. No, he hadn't forgotten it. The cage had been in place since morning. What Mason had forgotten, however, was the tiny bit of power Vincent Pembridge had squeezed out of the board of directors, who were always easily swayed.

And due to that, a main-event which was a Fighting Bout. Between... Ampske & Jason Semps.

"Fuck".

Anger Builds

A vicious chair shot to the side of the head… a fate that Dane Manetta suffered following his unscheduled victory against Chris Pinash last week, just like his sister Dane was struck down by Pinash’s example of Calculated Mindless Violence as he called it. And to sum the night up… to add to a chair shot… add a massive brawl at the end of Flaw which saw Dane suffer a wicked DDT and it all sums up to a bad day at work for Dane Manetta.

Upon entering this business Dane knew what it could be like, after all he and his sister had grown up watching his great, great grandfather, great grandfather… his grandfather and of course his own father. The wrestling industry was as you could say in his blood… he had been told all about violent attacks… vicious beatings and about making enemies.

He had expected something to happen… but not to this extent. For a week now Dane has sat silently, locked away from the world, thinking… Thinking about what is too happen to thReat… thinking about this allegiance that Vincent Pembridge has brought to thReat to once again make thReat a hybrid promotion. He also thought about Chris Pinash…

Dane was angry… he was angry with Maria for getting into a match with Pinash… he was angry at Pinash for attacking his sister and then Dane himself… he was angry that thReat was turning into a Hybrid promotion and the fighters were back… he was angry with Vincent Pembridge for bringing this upon everyone, and also for cheating to win a couple of week ago.

But most of all he was angry with himself, for allowing his sister to be in a situation that he himself can’t currently handle never mind Maria doing so. Built up anger can bring the best of people down… but to some it makes them stronger… more competitive and determined.

Releasing it would be a terrific pleasure.

The woman slowly turned around and stared into Fire’s eyes with an unimpressed look on her face, she didn’t appreciate Fire’s comments and was obviously not interested in pursuing a relationship with him but she wasn’t the sort of person who enjoyed embarrassing people so she quickly came up with an excuse.

“I would but I’ve got so much to think about, I’ve got a title match on my debut and I have to concentrate on that.”

Fire getting excited, he knew that she was playing hard to get. He knew that she was intimidated by him, who wouldn’t be? But he had to pursued her that he was serious and that she had an opportunity to go on a date with every woman’s dream.

“Oh, you’re a wrestler…How could a beautiful thing like you be involved in such a rough sport? How about I take that problem out of your busy schedule so that you can leave space for more important things? And by the way what’s you’re name?”

She thought to herself for a second and wondered what Fire was up to but his intentions soon occurred to her she wasn’t happy about his idea but she knew that it was the only definite way of achieving her dream.

“Well the important things are what matters and my name is Vanessa, Vanessa Vasquèz but most people call me Vertigo.”

Vertigo walked out of the refreshment area with a smile on her face she couldn’t believe how stupid Fire was. She couldn’t believe that he actually thought that his pathetic chat up lines would actually win her over. Fire however had a different interpretation of what happened, from his point of view it looked like he had just made another conquest and Vertigo was now yet another name on his list of admirers.

Breaking Point

"The Man Without Fear" by Drowning Pool ft. Rob Zombie began to play over the speakers, and the fans got on their feet, cheering for a thReat superstar they had grown to like over the past couple of weeks. One who'd capped off Flaw 15 wrestling in the Main Event... and unfortunately, losing. As he appeared from behind the curtains and steadily made his way down the ramp, the look on his face was evident.

He was pissed and disappointed at the same time.

Nevertheless, the crowd still cheered for him, but hushed as Wolf immediately got a microphone once he entered the ring. He, to the best of anybody's knowledge, wasn't scheduled to fight on the night... with injuries from the previous show carried over being the apparent excuse. But it seemed as if Wolf just had a couple of choice words to get out of his system.

Before it ate him alive and destroyed everything he'd worked hard for.

"Alright, this is going to be short and sweet. Last week, I lost to some jackass named Fire. Don't really care about that, because shit happens. Then, Vincent Pembridge and his gang come out... and decide to raise some hell. For no real reason, I get drawn into the mayhem. Again, I don't really care about that. What I do care about, however, is Steve Solex.

A man's who obviously bitter at having lost to me.

Twice.

And he just can't seem to let go. The jackass keeps coming back for more, and last week... he crossed the line. He got mixed up in Vincent P's little war against us 'wrestlers' and took the chance to kick the crap out of me!"

The crowd naturally jeered, simply due to the fact that Steve Solex & Vincent Pembridge had been mentioned. Solex, most remembered, did attack Wolf earlier in the show last week... which could have attributed to Wolf's lagging performance in his showdown with Fire. But for those who knew the man born in Germany, they would tell you one thing.

Wolf doesn't blame any external factors. He picks himself up, and moves on.

Of course, it was hard, considering it was his first loss. Not just in thReat, but in his career. Two solid victories put him on cloud nine, and the loss to Fire brought him back down to Earth. It was something that was hard to swallow for Wolf, but he was trying his best.

"So Solex, I propose we end this. At Malevolence. You and me, in a match. Nope, not a normal wrestling match. Not a submission match. Not a fighting bout, no way. I'm talking about something big. Something that incorporates all of that, really. Something used for special occasions. But you know what? Malevolence is gonna be special to me.

I'm talking about... an Iron Man Match!"

The fans popped loudly, at the mention of those last couple of words. Iron Man Match? Sixty minutes of backbreaking labour, with each move having to be planned carefully. Strategy was the key... and Wolf was planning to end his war with Steve Solex in the most strategical way possible. He stood there, in the middle of the ring, waiting for a reply as the crowd started to chant his name.

Before, without any music, Solex sauntered out from the back with a microphone in his hand. The cheers quickly morphed into boos, and Wolf smiled thinly. He was hoping his enemy would make a quick response, and his eyes gleamed as Solex put the microphone to his mouth.

"I'll have to think about it, Wolf."

The native of Port Charlotte almost collapsed at the words of Steve Solex, who laughed and retired to the back, admist a huge barrage of jeers. Once again, he'd outsmarted Wolf and made him look like a fool. Another proverbial low-blow.

Wolf had reached his breaking point.

"Screw this."

The Guilty

There he was, standing above them, realizing that they had no idea of his presence amongst them or of his position or that he would soon be apart of their world.

Completely blind to his existence.

He watched from above, staring down into the crowd and watching as lights scanned their environment repeatedly. Signs danced and faces smiled. All was well in their eyes. And he just sat there in his haven and thought.

How happy you all must be. How thrilled you’re little souls must be to see us in our unimaginable world of recognition and honor. I can only imagine how this must all feel. And you think that it’s going to stay this way forever.

Oh man do you have another thing coming.

His eyes watched the little people cheer and hold up their signs and smile and laugh. They were all so innocent, yet so very guilty. All of them played some role in the mistake that was society. None of them were completely safe. All would have to take part in his mission. Nothing was going to stop him from showing them the error of their ways.

Maybe they would fall in line. Maybe they wouldn’t.

Either case, they had little choice. They would have to bear witness to his lesson.

He stood up, giving one last glance to the people below in their safe little refuge, and then walked along the rafters.

The time had come to begin.

Again? NO. No, it didn’t happen, right? He never knew that was the ring, he wasn’t told these were the conditions… Chris played by the rules - there was no rules in fighting. This never happened.

“This didn’t happen.”

Chris whispered silently to himself, face-down on the mat… in the ring… no, not a ring, it wasn’t a ring - Chris would have won if it were a ring. He pushed himself up to his knees, just in time to see the victor retreat.

“This Didn’t Happen!”

Louder now, talking, almost shouting it. Now kneeling before a jeering crowd… why were they jeering. He stood up and screamed above their hollers and boos.

“This Never Happened! It Doesn’t Count!”

He stood, infuriated at losing to Dane again. It wasn’t right. He started to think to himself about what that loss meant… two defeats in a row, to one man… it wasn’t right. He demanded a microphone… he felt it was his duty to explain this defeat. Reluctantly, Pinash was given control of a mic.

“Dane, you and I both know that this match doesn’t count! Chris Pinash is all about the rules. He plays by the rules, and he wins by them. This match here - it had no rules. That match there… wasn’t even a match - it was a fight.”

Chris composed himself, shook his shoulders down, took a short walk to stretch his legs. Then spoke again, looking disgusted, at the fans, the ‘ring’, the opponent… everything.

“Wrestling… at it’s best it’s an art form, an intricate art. Fighting… at it’s best it’s a crime, and a violent crime at that! I am not a criminal. Dane Manetta is, he’s proven that two times in two weeks. Last week, he couldn’t contain his aggression, and after I fairly beat his sister, in a sanctioned match - he ran down to the ring, demanding that I face him… do you know why? Because he can’t live without mindless, useless violence. This week - he challenges me to a match, he gets it sanctioned - and what doesn’t he tell me… what does everyone forget to tell Chris Pinash? That this so-called ‘match’ wouldn’t have any rules. That this ‘match’ would be enclosed in unforgiving steel. But Dane, not notifying me was your initiative, and I honestly am fine with that.”

Chris took the mic down from his mouth, he licked perspiration that had formed on his upper-lip from his match, and wiped sweat from his fore-head with his right sleeve. The mic was raised again.

“I’m fine with it because I know you wouldn’t be fine without it. I know you’d never be satisfied if it was a completely fair fight, if it had rules. What you want, and crave, is a match where you have the upper hand from the beginning, where you can waltz in, dish out needless violence, cheat and deceive your way to victory - then high-tail it out of the area before your opponent wakes up to give you what’s for. Well, this week I can live with it… what’s done is done Dane. But if I were you… I wouldn’t be in such a hurry to get to Flaw next week. You may have won this… mockery of a match… but you can take it from me Dane - this was your last.”

The thump of the mic hitting the mat, then the few seconds of high pitched squealing before the signal regulated its-self again. “Big Cheese” by Nirvana started again, and Pinash left the ‘ring’.

In the eyes of the masses, he’d suffered a loss. In his eyes, he was the victim of an assault.

Peacemaker

He sat amidst the jumbling feet of infidels. In his little place he sat, contemplating… his face without a sarcastic grin… with good reason…

How can you grin at such a gruesome site that was laid forth before the thReat viewers the past week?

How can you grin at such malice?

How can you grin at Christian Embers?

He turned away from his fans… he turned away from what drove him to wrestling… he turned away from the people that supported him.

But now… Christian? Christian just didn’t care… nor did he give a fuck about what they thought of him nonetheless.

He would vividly remember last week when he saw Eric Morgan in the face and said the casual hello before unleashing a torrent of forearms and knees to the helpless victim’s face.

It had been dismembered… bloodied and left aside for some idiot to clean up.

He loved the RAGE that evolved within him from this new found fighting spirit.

“Wrestling fucks everything up.” He said solemnly as he several wrestlers walking past him in cautiously, guarding there backs with somebody else’s and those shields took other people as their shields… it was a never ending cycle of fear.

Fear of fighting.

Fear of fighting Christian Embers.

They knew all too well what Christian Embers could do… they knew all too well what Christian Embers would do.

And that… that scared them the most.

The fact that he WOULD.

Christian continued to sit in his little place for himself… his small place of providence and peace where he could rest and just simply reminisce about the memories of the pain he enacted to all his foes.

Eric Morgan was just another statistic of many.

“YOU!” screamed a familiar voice.

“What the fuck do you want Eric?!” screamed back Christian, quickly getting outside his sanctuary and into hostile grounds.

“THE CAGE. NOW. NOW!!!!!” responded back Eric with a strong, rebellious voice.

Christian finally grinned with his usual sarcastic style; his eyebrows were raised like a devious troublemaker while his smile was attributed with a charm that was quite indescribable. His smile may have reflected charm, but the snarl that soon followed quickly erased all memories that his mouth once symbolized charm. Christian looked at Eric with burning eyes, his hands ready to pounce on Eric’s sweaty face…

But he didn’t. “Ok.” He replied calmly to the surprise of Eric. “You want me? You got me!”

Eric smiled and looked at him straight into the eyes soon there after, his nostrils snorting a mad vapor. Eric soon turned around and headed for the Cage.

“Oh wait!” said Christian excitedly, grabbing the hand of Eric, turning him around. “Umm…”

Eric looked at Christian awkwardly, infuriated that Christian touch him. “Before you…”

BAM!

Eric fell down to the concrete with a tremendous thud, the ground tremoring with a violent shake of a small earthquake.

“NEVER CHALLENGE ME TO A FIGHT THAT YOU CAN’T WIN.” Screamed Christian, sadistically, into the ear of Eric before kicking him in the kidneys with a toe kick and leaving to the ring.

To Christian there were two places of providence.

That small corner that he found wherever he went.

And the cage…

Because in the cage… he could NEVER be beaten…

Because in the cage… HE was the peacemaker…

Or the breaker in this case.

Departure

Now, his actions already setting up a chain of events that would lead to the first lesson he had in mind, he walked down the hall, not staring into the eyes of anyone that passed by. He simply stepped through the backstage area, no emotion locked into his face, and stepped outside.

The sky was clear and the moon was streaming down like a headlight, but he imagined it was raining, just like it had been before. He could see his hair, soaked and painted to his face, and he could see his boots, stepping through the streams along the sidewalks and splashing the water around like a fish in a shallow puddle.

But the rain never fell. The earth remained dry. The night wind never ceased.

Most men would have showed up at the arena in a car. Or have paid a limousine company a good amount of money to drop them off. Or, if worst comes to worst, called a taxi to bring them to their event.

He had walked, from the airport all the way to the Alamodome, with only his feet to carry him.

That had been in the afternoon, when the sun was out and the city was alive. The sun had long since set, and the city had long since died down to a dreary state, where only the nocturnal roamed the streets with their cigarettes and their liquor and their lives, as pointless as they were. It was now the evening, and the sinner’s time to shine.

His boots echoed in the alleyways and his breathing was heavy, but his job was done.

And now it was time to move on to the next portion of his plan.

Christian approached Eric and nailed with him with a vicious palm strike to the chest that sent Eric back a few steps before he retaliated with his own hook to Christian’s left arm, the arm that wrestlers are trained to work with at all times (for you idiots out there, you never grab your opponent’s right arm in wrestling, only his left). Christian looked at Eric quixotically, “What the fuck you think that’s going to you idiot?” screamed Christian before he gave a swift middle kick to the right thigh of Eric.

Eric fell in pain on his right knee as Christian quickly took his stance and kicked him in the same right arm that he had plagued the week before. Eric howled in pain, his arm smarting from the inside with an indescribable pain. Christian approached closer, not getting too close to his opponent to be hit with a punch or a forearm. Eric opened his arms slightly, giving Christian the opening he was looking for. He quickly grabbed one arm and quickly tightened it into his left armpit before sending a quickly legdrop like move to his face before locking the other arm of Eric in with his legs.

It was the crucifix armbar.

It was the pain all his victims had faced…

It was the END all his victims had faced.

Christian quickly pulled his body, stretching Eric’s body, slowly pulling the bones out of their sockets while contorting one arm with his arm.

But Eric didn’t give up. He simply screamed; his pride could not be shattered. As Eric screamed Christian laughed and as Eric screamed loudly Christian laughed louder.

“YOU’RE FUCKED!” he screamed into Eric’s face, Eric looking back with a sorry image that he once stood as.

ONE.

FIVE.

Christian picked his right elbow up as he pounded at Eric’s face. Eric fell limp… and limp…

And then… it just looked as though he had died.

But Christian didn’t care… he wanted to prove a lesson.

“FUCK WITH ME AND I’LL FUCK WITH YOU!” he screamed, not to Eric, but to the fans who had supposedly, because of their previous betrayal, cost him his father’s life.

Christian felts the hapless body of Eric strewn on his, his arms spread out, his life sacrificed in the same manner just like another man who had spread his arms out and died.

No, Eric had not died.

But he was hurting. He was hurting bad.

The winner… emphatically… Christian Embers.

Christian Embers stood laughing as he gave a small nudge to Eric’s body with his foot.

The referee pushed Christian back as he tended to Eric. “GET OUT!” demanded the referee to a celebrating Christian Embers.

There was no “out” now anymore in Christian Embers’ life here in thReat.

Why… there was no out anymore for anyone.

Why? Christian Embers was the guardian, the Cerberus to the doors of freedom… and until the day he defeats everyone that stands in his way… he will never give freedom to thReat…

He will never see them see the day of light…

HE WILL NEVER LET THEM SEE WRESTLING!
