Rejection

Real Name: Trevor Wilson Also known as: The Rejected One

Born: November 30th, 1977 Place of Birth: Alexandria Bay, New York Hometown: Salem, Massachusetts

Height: 6'8 Weight: 287 lbs. Blood type: O+ Eye color: Hazel

Theme song: "I, Voyager" by Nevermore

Style

Competing style: Powerhouse/Roughneck

Signature moves:
"Denied" - Pumphandle Slam
"Ballad of Denial" - Several hard chops to the chest ending with a Short-Arm Clothesline.
"Face of Wrath" - Falling headbutt from the second rope
"Inner Hatred" - Rejection lifts his opponent up, and drops him over his knee on their stomach.
"Undying Torment" - A continual onslaught of of knees to the stomach, usually with the opponent backed inot a corner. The goal here is to cause internal bleeding.

Northern Lights Suplex
Inverted Fisherman's Suplex
Full Nelson Slam
Pendulum Back Breaker
Inverted DDT
Yakuza Kick, usually followed by an attempt to grab the opponents legs in time to hook them up, and then deliver a Falling Powerbomb.

Finisher: Rejected Finisher name: Rejection lifts his opponent by the throat, and drops him into a Sit-Down Powerbomb.

Historical Info

Other/Previous promotions: thReat

Other titles won: thReat Aggression Champion
