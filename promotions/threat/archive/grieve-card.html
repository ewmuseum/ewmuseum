Style

Signature moves:
Misery: Grieve wraps both hands around the victims throat and squeezes causing much misery, if the opponent does not tap nor pass out, he slams him hard to the mat followed by a pin fall, usually.

Hurt: Grieve firmly wraps his arms around the foe and lifts him off that mat, squeezing with all his power until he weeps of pain.

Despair: Grieve chargers at his opponent, connecting with a devastating spear.

Heartache: Grieve holds the victim in as if it was a gorilla press slam. After showing his surplus power, he tosses the victim in the air and as he is falling, he uses his hands to drive the victim's chest into his knee causing severe pain to inflict around the heart thus being a Heartache.

Tragedy of Love: Grieve positions the victim in a suplex with his head facing the armpit of Grieve. As Grieve falls to the mat, he slides the victim’s neck underneath his pit and as the both connect to the mat, Grieve crushes the victim’s throat making it difficult to breathe.

Purgatory: Grieve shoots the foe off the ropes as he shoots to the opposite and meets the victim in the middle of the ring for a monstrous clothesline.

Finisher: Grieve drapes the back of the victim over his shoulder and firmly wraps his arms around the body to where there is no escaping. As the opponent is draped around the shoulder, Grieve tightens the hold causing much pain and agony to vastly inflict. Before the befallen foe taps, Grieve slams the shoulder of the victim onto his knee causing much more pain to inflict upon the foe.
