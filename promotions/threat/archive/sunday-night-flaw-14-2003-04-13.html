Time To Get An Explanation

Security guards were all over the arena. They'd gotten specific orders as to what to do on the night, and having been paid handsomely, they were obviously inclined to carry out their jobs to the fullest extent of the ability. No solo duty, they had been broken up into pairs. And as it were, there were two pairs keeping watch outside Troy Mason's office.

Where inside, Troy Mason was reading the day's papers. Sure, he was still reeling from what happened last week. He was still feeling the effects of Vincent Pembridge's attack. In fact, Troy's hip still hurt. It cost the man a chance to get jiggy with some lolitas the past Wednesday, when he accompanied a friend to go cat pimping. Amongst other wonderful things.

Flaw 13 went very well but the warning sounded out by The Scorpion Of Manchester left him worrying. What exactly did Vincent mean? What was his intentions? All these things bugged Troy, especially with the show nearing. And now, it was 7 PM.

It was time for Flaw 14 to kick off.

The words of wisdom from Vincent last week were continuing to haunt Mason. Now, they became more harrowing. The time had come for the second show in thReat's revival, one where Vincent Pembridge was ORDERED to reveal his true intentions. All he had was that one chance. Hence, the need for extra security. This night was going to be one with a chokeful of surprises. Question was, would they affect the company in any shape or form?

Veil Of Darkness

Darkness had engulfed everything

With each passing second the light had appeared to come closer and closer before it could be seen for what it truly was. A candle’s wick being burnt down to the wax as surrounding it were several people robed in dark cloaks and hoods as they sat with their heads bowed and faced the candle’s flame.

“The time has finally arrived for us,” the man began to speak. “I, Marcus Escobar, along with Geneva will begin our plan of conversion in a matter of days now. It is important that each and every one of you maintain your support for The Faithless and you will be rewarded when the time is right.”

Another murmur went through the group who still sat bowed in front of the candle and looked up at Marcus Escobar and watched patiently as he stepped to the side and let the woman known simply as Geneva now step to the forefront and look around at those who had pledged their allegiance to her and her cause.

“All of you no doubt have questions that you wish to be answered,” Geneva began as she looked around at the bowed heads of her loyalists. “Rest assured that all of your answers will one day come to you when you least expect it and you will eternally grateful for the loyalty with which all of you have so far served with. There will be much doubt that many of you will no doubt experience but if you persevere and maintain your course you will be rewarded with benefits greater than any you could have ever dreamed of. Remember that you are now forevermore my Faithless and you serve me whether it is in life or death from this day forward.”

With that said Geneva again fell silent and she turned slowly to walk away from the vigil and Marcus led The Faithless back into another of their rituals around the candle as Geneva watched from a seat behind them all constantly watching and plotting the best ways to achieve her cause and fulfill her destiny.

The question still remained to all that were not in her circle of just what her cause truly was. It seemed that even many that currently sat bowed in front of her could not be entirely certain of the truth of it all but regardless they had displayed fierce loyalty towards her on many occasions and now with the true start of their endeavors their loyalty will be put to the ultimate test.
