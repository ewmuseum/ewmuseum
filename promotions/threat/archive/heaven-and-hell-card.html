Heaven and Hell

Wrestler 2: Hell Birthday: 07.29.75 Hometown: Queens, New York, USA Weight: 297 lbs

Combined Weight: 516 lbs Entrance Theme: "Earthbound" by One Nation Wrestling Style: Heaven is an energetic Aerial Martial Arist

While Hell is a Powerful Rulebreaker

Hell

Bio: Through the sands of time

So goes the days of our lives....

In the begining of time, we lived in nothingness. Then god said, let there be light.

Flash.

Then there was one; a pure good hearted soul. Following only as he creator saw fit. He followed the lord, obeyed the lord, lived through the lord. A kind and gentle man, thinking only of others; never for himself, no matter what the consequences. As a young boy, he followed the laws of life as well as the laws of the lord.

Growing up in Albany, New York.. He was truly tempted. Lust, greed, along with so many other sins surronding the boy. He did not give into temptation. He remained pure, no matter what obstacle came to him. Though he was given help, coming from a proper family, living in "good" section of the neighborhood giving all the privledges that could come his way.

Attending a private school, having his own chaeffur. He grew up happy and content. An honor roll student no.. A role model student, the boy was often called The Saint, or Heavenly. This did not bother the boy much, as he felt the same way. A Volunteer for many organizations he kept himself quite busy, in fact the only thing that he ever did for himself would take karate lessons. To strengthen his mind, but more so his soul. His parents could not of been happier.

If there was one thing that they frowned upon of their son's doings.. It would have to of been his choice in friends.. Or his one friend. You see, their boy as sweet and good hearted as he was.. Was not exactly socially skilled. Having only one friend, if you would call that a friend..

Only the other end of the neighborhood, more exacly Queens, New York there was another boy. Not exacly the right sort for the sweet boy to be meddling with. There lived an older boy, not as kind or generous. In fact, quite the opposite. A big and brawny boy, feared by a few the the neighborhood childeren, as he was referred to as a bully: of the worst sort. Lying, cheating, stealing, not at all like his friend. In fact, the friendship of the two was not instant. The bully use to always pick him, knowing who he was and were he came from, he knew he could get more than a lunch with robbing him.

It went on for a couple of weeks, then one day the two were seen coming home together. With the sweet boy telling his mother this is my new friend.

Nobody knows just what happened that day, just why the two of them became friends or even how? Two people so opposite, yet so close.

Flash.

Years Later.

Not much has changed with the two boys, especially their bond. The two acted the same way, neither changing for the other.. Perhaps for the best. Not to say they didn't try it to one another. Having the best of both worlds can have its advantages. The two acted alike so much, by this day and age when the two walked down the street, many refered to them as "Heaven and Hell" The Good/Evil duo. This really didn't phase the two, in fact they rather encouraged it. The two were in seperable, until school was over that is. Heaven having the money and grades to go to college and better himself, did. Where Hell, had neither and remained home in Queens. The two seemed to have lost contact for the most part, Heaven's parents were thrilled by this. Yet, Heaven remained to have his social problems, but yet his good hearted nature did not change. There were some doubts in his mind that his good nature is what kept him socially isolated, but did not let up. The gap that the lack of friends left him, he filled with his martial arts. Hoping to one day fill the status of his soul purity.

Four years later, Heaven had finally graduated from college; his parents couldn't of been happier. Heaven made his way back home to Albany, New York. Hoping to find a career, with his newly aquired degree. For days Heaven walked from corporation to corporation handing in resumes taking interviews with corporation resources. But one night, he was held up. A large man, jumped out of an alley way behind Heaven, screaming at him to hand his money and any valuables over. Heaven pondered just what was going on in his mind. He thought about using his martial arts skills that he had learned over the years, this being the first time he was forced to use them. Then he thought to himself, that this was self defense. He made up his mind, Heaven then turned around and knocked the assilant's knife ouf of his hand with a spinning back kick; he then proceeded to hit him with a crane kick. The two then rolled around fighting for a bit, not really getting a good look at one another. Until they finally did, Heaven.. Hell..? The two spoke to one another.

Both men stood at ease for a moment until embracing one another. The two then met for a drink, talking about what the other had been up to. Heaven explained how the past four years were going for him, that he was now finally trying to find a job. While he listened as Hell told him he never quit his job, that being a bully. He's just now a corporate bully. Hell made the purposal of Heaven joining him, after seeing just what Heaven is capable of doing. But Heaven would have nothing to do with stealing or anything of the sort. Heaven was in disgust even or the suggestion.

The two sat there for awhile, not discussing anything. But then Hell had another idea, Professional Wrestling. Heaven just gave Hell a look. Hell quickly gave an explantion of how they are not stealing and how its not real. It's just a show, almost like an actor. Somehow, Hell talked Heaven way into it.

Present Day.

Finally Heaven and Hell are truly born.. A whole other Genesis.
