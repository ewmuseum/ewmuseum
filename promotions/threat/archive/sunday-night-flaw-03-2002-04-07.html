Sunday Night Flaw 03

7 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

04/07/02

Another Night

White walls once again, as Xaxon walked in the arena's valet office, where Hackett was located. It had just been one week since Xaxon had made his return to Threat, but it felt like years. Time travels slower when you're older...

"You 'vanted to see me?" Xaxon asked with a bit of violence in his voice. He was staring at Hackett, who sat comfortably in his leather chair.

"Yes," he said, "Avo Chavez-you've met him-has missed two straight Flaw appearances. I can't tolerate things like this. I..."

Xaxon cut him off.

"I will take care of 'it," he said, with his deep, German accent in place.

Xaxon walked out of the office before Hackett could say anymore.

No Problem

Quincy Brown sat next to his manager, Trent Weathers, tieing up his shoes. Trent Weathers picked his teeth with his tounge, with something on his mind.

"Quincy, you know that you must do well here in thReat."

"Yeah, I know or it's, back to jail."

Amp Canon was walking by and heard the words "back to jail", so he stopped to listen through the door.

"You can't afford to make any mistakes here or you'll be back to fighting for your life rather than your money."

"How about giving me a pep talk rather than bumming me out, okay?," said the bit peeved Quiincy Brown.

"You're right. Just keep doing what you're doing, and you'll be fine."

Amp Canon's face lit up and he grew a mischevious smile as he walked off-screen.

Xaxon VS Avo Chavez

Avo Chavez ran at Xaxon, but Xaxon moved out of the way. Avo Chavez then jumped onto the ropes, and did a sunset flip, onto Xaxon. Xaxon immediately got up, as did Avo Chavez. Chavez then drop kicked Xaxon, he fell to the mat. Xaxon again got up. Avo Chavez then ran at Xaxon, Avo was met with a big boot to the face. Avo fell hard to the mat.

Xaxon grabbed Avo Chavez’s head and picked him up. He winded up, and threw a right hook into Avo’s face. Avo Chavez slammed to the mat. Xaxon again grabbed Chavez, and threw him into a corner. He began throwing fists at Chavez. Chavez fought Xaxon off. He pushed him back about 4 feet. He then jumped on to the top ropes, and did a hurricanrana to Xaxon! Avo Chavez then went for the pin…

…1

…2

KICK OUT!

Xaxon slowly got up, while Avo Chavez waited. Chavez then delivered a DDT to Xaxon. Avo Chavez again went for the pin.

…1

…2

.Kick OUT!

Xaxon got up. Chavez tried to cross body block him, but Xaxon caught him in the act. Xaxon went for the gorilla press slam, but Xaxon’s old legs gave out. Avo Chavez landed on the floor. Xaxon fell to his butt. Xaxon again grabbed Chavez and whipped him to the ropes. Xaxon then clotheslined Chavez to the mat. Xaxon grabbed Chavez again, and applied an arm bar. The move was on for about 20 seconds, before Chavez got to the ropes. Xaxon let go. But then grabbed Avo’s legs and dragged him to the middle of the ring.

Xaxon then applied a camel clutch to Chavez. The move was on for a minute before Xaxon let go. Xaxon threw Avo Chavez to the ropes, Xaxon went for a clothesline, but Avo ducked under. He turned around, and dropkicked Xaxon. Xaxon fumbled over the ropes to the outside!

Avo Chavez ran to the ropes, then ran back to the side where Xaxon was standing on the outside. He jumped over the ropes and landed on Xaxon!

As both men got up, then slid into the ring. Xaxon kicked Avo Chavez in the gut. Chavez hunched over, as Xaxon grabbed his head and applied a DDT. Xaxon then hooked Avo’s leg and went for the pin.

…1

…2

..KICK OUT!

Xaxon then grabbed Avo, and did another DDT. He again went for the pin.

…1

..KICK OUT!

Xaxon was mad at the referee. He felt it was a slow count. As Xaxon was arguing, Avo Chavez was gradually getting up. Avo then ran at Xaxon, and drop kicked his legs. Xaxon fell to the mat. Avo Chavez then jumped onto Xaxon’s back. Xaxon got up, as Avo scissor kicked Xaxon. Xaxon fumbled back to the ropes, and bounced off. He then gave Avo a big boot to the face.

Xaxon then grabbed Avo and suplexed him back to the mat. Xaxon then threw Avo onto his shoulder, and did a running powerslam. Avo was hurt. Xaxon had the upperhand.

Avo punched Xaxon in the groin, as he got up. Avo then jumped onto the top ropes. He waited for Xaxon to turn around. Xaxon turned around. And Avo delivered a drop kick from the top ropes! But Xaxon moved out of the way!! Avo fell to the floor. Xaxon then waited for Avo Chavez to get up. He then did his finisher, Bleed to Avo Chavez. He then hooked the leg, and pinned Chavez.

…1

…2

…3

The match was over! Xaxon won the match. Xaxon then began to walk over to Chavez’s corner. He grabbed Rocco the Taco, Avo Chavez’s manager! He took a big bite out of Rocco the Taco!!

Once Again...

"Mr. Hackett, sir?," asked Jakks.

"Ah, you again."

Jakks sat down in Jon's office, then stood back up in uncertainty. He put his hand by his chin and rubbed it a bit.

"You know, Jon, I was thinking."

"Yes?"

"Could I maybe get a first class ticket to Flaw instead of riding coach all the time? All the fighters get the coach seating, and I...I...I think it's a bit unfair.

"I can't say you haven't got a point," replied Jon Hackett. "I'll make you a deal. If you can defeat Amp Canon tonight, fighters will know have first class transportation to every show."

"Great. First Class..."

Jakks left the room with a smile on his face.

My Graceland

A door opened. Hackett's office door once again. It was becoming a busy night for Hackett. A masked man walked through the door. He was a muscular man, with scars on his chest and a tribal tattoo over his left pectoral.

phYre

"Ah, welcome phYre!," greeted Jon Hackett. "Glad to have you here."

"Good to be here. When do I get started?," asked phYre anxiously.

"Hey there, calm down. You've got a fight next week."

"Outstanding. With who?"

"Jakks," replied Jon. "I figured I'd give you a good challenge."

"You know me too well," said phYre with a smile on his face.

phYre and Jon shook hands as phYre stepped out of Hackett's office.

Fucking Prick

His pride had been damaged, it wouldn't happen again. Noah Krone stood patiently at a buffet table backstage. His hands lay rest on the warm table in front of him, as he awaited his 'dinner' in the microwave. The night had gone pretty smoothly for thReat in its third installment of FLAW. It would all lead up to the main event, a match including Noah Krone himself. Triple Threat, balls on the table ... Noah would make a mockery of his previous attacker.

The ring sounded, and Noah reached in to remove his 'hot pocket'. Before the paper was unwrapped, a sharp pain raced up and down his spine as hot liquid splattered across his back.

"Jesus fucking Christ!" Noah spun around with every intention of socking the assailant in the face. To his suprise, and not to mention shock ... it was a small man. A very small wan. He had tripped over some stray wiring on the ground, and spilled a half full cup of coffee onto his less then appealing 7-11 shirt. He had forgotten about the liquid searing through the cloth, and a smile coats his face.

"And you are?" He asks with a cockiness in his voice.

"Ugghh, too much for your wallet, scrub." He had spunk!! Noah became pissed about the blatant remarke.

"What the hell did you ju ... " Noah was cut off by the presence of another. It was a man, a familiar face to Noah. He recognized the person, but couldn't put two and two together. The men stared deep into eachother's eyes, an already obvious distaste for one another.

"Who is your friend?" The lash back from Noah brought a look of disgust to the little man's face.

"This, although I'm sure it is of no importance to you ... is Takisuma Ogama. I suggest you remember the name. With my guidance, he'll be thReat's first World Champion."

The stare off continued, once again being interrupted by Noah.

"He doesn't say much does he?"

"Ogama speaks very little english. Now if you will excuse us, we have business to attend to."

The well dressed man spoke a few words of Japanese to Ogama, and he redirected his glare from Noah's eyes. The two newest additions to thReat walk down the hall in stride, as a now perturbed Krone stood alone.

"Oh by the way ... the name is Bobby Blair. I have a feeling you won't be forgetting that." The harsh voice echoes through the halls towards Noah. His hand clenched as all his energy turns back towards the table.

"Fucking prick."

Amp Canon VS Jakks

Insane, am I the only motherfucker with a brain? I'm hearing voices but all they do is complain How many times have you wanted to kill everything and everyone? Say you'll do it but never will

"Eyeless" by Slipknot. Amp Canon makes his appearance to the crowd and they jeer wildly. Cries of "Racist!" and similar comments are sporadically shouted. Amp had been labeled a heel in his mini-feud with Quincy Brown, who was just as racist. He was black, though, so it's OK.

Fucking double standards.

The instrumental of "American Woman" by Lenny Kravitz hit and Vincent "Jakks" Pembridge comes out to a somewhat cheering crowd. He wasn't necessarily a "face" but he wasn't a racist like Amp, making him the lesser of two evils. Because of this he was cheered to a small degree.

What a great fighting division thReat has.

The bell rang to start the match up. The two brawlers settled into their fighting stance and approached each other with caution. Amp attempted a jab-straight combination; the jab connected, but the right straight was caught by Jakks and countered by a forearm shot to the back of the head. Jakks followed this up with a knee to the back that sent Amp staggering a few steps. Amp shook the counters off and settled down once more.

Jakks took the initiative this time going for a right toe kick into the abdomen. Amp grasped the right leg of his opponent and pulled it in forward. As Jakks face flew towards Amp, it was introduced to a right hand cutting through the air at an enormous velocity. Jakks fell to the canvas covering his nose as blood trickled down. Amp stomped the shoulder of his downed opponent twice and then picked him up. With a grunt, Amp threw Jakks across the ring and smirked. Jakks got up and both brawlers, once again, settled into their stances.

The fighters circled, both looking for the right moment to step in for an attack. Instantaneously, both of them stepped in at the same time attempting a series of unsuccessful punches, knees, and tosses. Finally, after about fifteen seconds of parrying, Amp slipped in a right cross that staggered Jakks. Amp backed up and rushed in attempting his signature move, "Bull's Horn." Jakks recognized this, stepped out of the way, and guided Amp into slamming into the corner of the cage.

The steel rattled and Amp's head provided a sickening thud. Amp stumbled aimlessly, and was introduced to the move knows as "Jakked Up" - an adaptation of D'Lo Brown's "Lo-Down." The ten count started, but Amp made no attempt to move. Jakks was declared the winner via knockout.

Plans

  "Fuck yes."

Riot was happy he'd get his hands on both Noah and Araknis tonight. His devious ways were only the beginning of what he was gunning for. The Aggression Title. He's been swinging that chair around like Barry Bonds.

"This is NOT going to be pretty...my kind of match. I'm going to take my best friend...my chair and smack it across the faces of those two fucking weaklings. I'll make my statement tonight which will lead to me becoming the FIRST thReat Aggression Title holder, and I'll be holding on to it from quite some time."

Riot begins walking towards the entrance area.

"Jon will be expecting another visit tonight..."

Noah Krone VS Araknis VS Riot

"Disposable Teens" by Marilyn Manson began to play as Noah Krone made his way down to the ring. He stood in the center, and performed a little kata as his music faded out. It was replaced by "Whole" by Flaw. The crowd booed as Riot made his way down to ringside, probably because of what he did on flaw 2. He got into the ring and advanced towards Noah, until the referee separated them. Without any notice, Araknis showed up in the ring. No fanfare, no nothing. He just showed up. Riot started off the match with a quick knee to Noah's gut, which made the big man double over. He raised his arms to deliver a axe handle smash, but Araknis kicked Noah hard in the face first. He fell flat on his back. Riot turned to face him.

He was greeted with a thunderous hook to the face.

Riot fell to the ground, holding his face, as Noah Krone stood back up. Krone saw Riot, and saw Araknis, and decided to try a standing pump kick to Araknis. Araknis side stepped it, and delivered his own standing side kick. Noah fell down. Again. Araknis stood over both of his fallen foes, just staring at them. He grabbed the wriggling Riot's arm, then stepped over, applying a twisting arm bar. Riot screamed out. Noah stood up, slowly.

And then shocked everyone but running off of the ropes, running back towards Araknis, and kicking him full on in the back of the head...blood shot out of Araknis' nose. Riot screamed out, as that caused his arm to be nearly ripped out of socket. Noah grabbed the almost unconscious body of Araknis and stood it up. He whipped him into the ropes, and as it came back towards him, he attacked with a palm strike to the heart, forcing him down again. Riot stood up again, ran towards Noah, and smashed him into the turnbuckle. Riot backed off, screaming, then ran back towards him, leg fully extended, smashing Noah's face with the bottom of his boot. Noah slumped over in the turnbuckle, and Riot stomped on his chest. Araknis slowly stood up, almost blowing in the slight breeze of the air conditioning. Somehow finding strength to move forward, he advanced towards Riot, who snapped around and gave him a hard spear. Araknis' head bounced off of the mat. Riot climbed on top of him, and proceeded to give him forearm shots to the head. Noah clambers to his feet then climbs up the top rope. Riot got off of Araknis, screamed, and then Noah jumped on him with an OCL.

Noah rolled over Riot and tried to get the cover.

1...

2...

KICKOUT!

Riot threw his shoulder up, and Noah fell somewhere beside him. All three men made it to their feet at the same time for the first time since the beginning of the match. All three men stared at each other.

Then Noah and Araknis both side kicked Riot.

Araknis looked at Noah, then back to Riot. Noah nodded his head, then stepped back. Araknis grabbed Riot to his feet, then gave him a snap suplex. And another. Then he lifted him for a vertical suplex, but dropped him face first. Bite of the Spider. Araknis stepped back, and Noah stepped up. He lifted Riot to his feet, lifted him up for a vertical suplex, then dropped him with a falcon arrow. The referee slid into the count.

1...

2...

And Araknis kicked Noah in the head, stopping the count. Apparently, and team effort stopped then. Noah then tried to hurricanrana Araknis, but Araknis caught him, lowered him to the ground, and applied a Tarantula. Riot slowly got to his feet as the referee checks for the submission. He yelled out once more, then charged Araknis. He grabbed his arms around his head, lifted him as high as he could, and bulldogged him. Noah got to his feet, and extended his hand to Riot. Riot looked at it, accepted it, then pulled him up onto his shoulders for a flapjack DDT. Riot got up, looked around, and obviously not satisfied with the damage he had already caused, slid out of the ring and fumbled around for something.

A chair.

He came back into the ringcand set the chair by the turnbuckle. He picked up Araknis and "swung" at him, intentionally missing the hit the ref. The ref got knocked in the mouth and fell to the ground. Riot then DDT'ed Araknis to the floor and headed back to his turnbuckle with the chair. Araknis has just started to stand up, groggy, and Riot laid him out with a heavy chair shot. Araknis fell over once more, and Riot kept slamming him with the chair. Noah got to his feet, spun Riot around, and got hit under the chin with an uppercut...from the chair.

Noah flew backwards, and was helped down by another shot from Riot. Riot continued to slam the chair down on Noah. Araknis once more got to his feet, and Riot turned around and slammed his chair so hard on Araknis' head that the seat popped off. Araknis slumped over.

Riot lifted him to his feet, and grabbed him for a reverse DDT. He spun him around, and dropped him with an Ace Crusher. Crowd Control.

Riot ran over and awoke the ref then laid over top of his nearly dead enemy, and the referee made the count.

1

2

3!

"Whole" by Flaw began to play again as Riot climbed out of the ring. Instead of leaving, he went under the apron and grabbed another chair. He climbed into the ring and began to slam it down on Araknis and Noah again. After a few seconds of the carnage, referees and authorities storm out of the back, rush the ring, and hold Riot back. As he was forced from the ring, he tossed the chair back in, catching Araknis in the ribs.

Riot ran up the ramp, through the curtains, and into the backstage hallway searching for Hackett's office. He slams open Jon's door and slams his hand firmly on Jon's desk, startling him.

"I want my shot!"

"I WANT, I WANT, I WANT!," mocked Jon. "Haven't any of you heard of EARNING things?!"

"I've earned a shot!"

"At what, Riot?"

"The thReat Aggression Championship!," exclaimed Riot.

"You want it?"

"You fucking bet I do."

"Then you'll get your chance on Flaw 4. Now get out of my office!," yelled Jon.

Riot slams his fist on Hackett's desk one more time.

"Sir, you've just made the best investment of your life," stated Riot as Flaw 3 went off air.
