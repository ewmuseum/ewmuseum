Biography

Christopher Elliot Ryan was born on July 5, 1978 in Riverside, California. He was born the son of a professional wrestler and a kindergarten teacher, and took an immeadiate liking to what his fater did for a living. Any memorabilia he could get his hands on, he had to have, whether if it was his father's or his father's "worst enemy". To say that Christopher was a fan would be a vast understatement, he was obsessed. He had decided by the age of four that he wanted to be a wrestler and do exactly what his father was doing throughout the West Coast. He got so bad his mother even banned him from watching the sport until he was older because he was tearing up not only himself, but all the furniture in her house. This, Christopher likes to refer to as....the dark ages.

By the time he was sixteen, he had joined the local amateur wrestling team, and his school's football team. . .The Red Lightining. He soon, with coaching from his father, and his football coaches, became one of the best wrestlers and defensive ends in the California area. He was being sought after by colleges all over the United States. Schools like Florida State, Iowa, Oregon State, and Texas A & M, were showing mass interest in the young prodigy, and he was reveling in it all.

As his senior year of high school came to the end he had a decesion to make. Stay home and keep close to his beloved parents, or stake out on his own. With the coaxing of his father, Christopher enrolled in the college of Texas A&M. Yet, not long after his school year started, disaster struck, he learned, that his father, his hero, his pillar of strength had succumbed to a brain tumor which had been festering inside him for the better part of fifteen years. Christopher was plunged into a world of depression and sadness, his grades slacked and his performance on the field lacked greatly. Off the field he was even worse, drinking was a daily pastime, and partying was numero dos. It was on the night of July 4th, Independance Day, that Christopher got a little too inhebriated and tried to drive his car home unsuccessfully, he crashed into a tree, and broke a leg and suffered some fractures to his ankle. This signaled the end of Christopher's football career.

As he worked through the horribly painful rehabilitation process, Chris moved back home to live with his mother to save money. He had a lot of time to reflect on his life, and the shit it had deffinatley become. It was on one of these days, that he witnessed, a mugging just outside his house. A man was trying to take an old ladies purse. Christopher tried to get out of his bed and help, but to no avail, he was powerless to do anything but watch as the old woman put up a fight and was stabbed in the chest twice, and then stabbed once more in the head.

It was on this day that Christopher Ryan knew exactly what he wanted to do with his life. He wanted to help others. But not in the traditional way. He was going to do it his father's way. By being a wrestler. Driven by the new fire he had inside Christopher completed his rehabilitation process on time and soon after enrolled in a wrestling school.

Now fresh out of wrestling school, Protocol has set himself on a mission to help the meek, the hopeless, and the weak. He has a lot on his hands now, but he will not stop until his mission is complete.
