Erik Hellfield

Real Name: Unknown Also known as: Hellfield

Born: 04/09/78 Place of Birth: Sydney, Australia. Hometown: Maple Ridge, BC, Canada.

Height: 6'4 Weight: 263½lbs Blood type: O Eye color: Green

Trainer: Shane Fenton/Jason Hellfield

Theme song: "Everytime I Die" by Children of Bodom

Style

Competing style: Extreme/Power

Signature moves:

Signature Moves: Gehenna #2 is Running Jump DDT, usually on a steel chair, the bare floor, or from the top rope.

The Soul Slayer is a Tombstone Piledriver, from the second turnbuckle.

Redemption is a Choke Sleeper, dropped down into a backbreaker while still in the hold.

Eternal Suffering is a very stiff Death Valley Driver onto the nearest hard object.

Finisher: Kick to the back of the knees, catches opponent as they fall to their knees with a lifting reverse DDT.
Finisher name: Hellbound

Historical Info

Other/Previous promotions: HCW, SWF, uKw.

Other titles won: SWF I.C. Title
