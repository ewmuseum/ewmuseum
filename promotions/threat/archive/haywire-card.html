Haywire

Real Name: Dominic Crazy Nickname: "n/a" Also known as: n/a

Born: June 15,1980 Place of Birth: Fort Bragg, North Carolina Hometown: Fayetteville, North Carolina

Height: 6'1 Weight: 224 lbs Blood type: O Eye color: Icy Blue

Team: n/a

Manager: n/a Trainer: n/a

Theme song: Tha' Voices by FTE

Style

Competing style: Unorthodox

Signature moves:
Whiteout - Grabs groggy opponent by arm and climbs to top rope. Mc then walks across ropes before jumping and hitting opponent in heart.

Blackout - Cross face sleeper hold

Crazybone suplex - Grabs opponent by both arms and lifts him in air. Crazy continues to hold opponent in air before dropping him face first to the mat.

2 way drop - Opponent swings (right hand) at MC. Mc ducks it and catches him under the arm, lifts him in the air & slams him to the mat (rock bottom/book end)

Downtime - Sharpshooter

The Crazy Lean - Moon Sault off top rope.

Cross + Breaker - Piledriver

Snap Shot - Frog Splash from top rope

Ring Around (the rosy) - while opponent is getting up from floor, MC runs around the apron or ring barrier and flips off onto him.

Silencer - Missile dropkick

Crash Board Stretch - Surfboard Stretch

Raindrop - Flying elbow to opponent on mat

BasePlex - Single German Suplex

Multiple BasePlex - Does 4 BasePlex’s without letting go. On fifth one he holds it for a pin

Banana Split - Wishbone

Egg Roll - Hurricanrana

Back Down - Throws opponent into corner and screams at him

Bust Stop - Running forearm splash (into flip up)

911 Call - Kicks opponent in stomach. Crazy then turns and grabs opponent by the neck and drops to the mat (stunner)

Hammer Time - Kick opponent in stomach and bounces back of ropes to connect with a scissor kick to the back of the head.

Mandible Claw - takes two middle fingers & sticks them down the throat of opponent.

The Gore - Spear

Elevator Drop - Atomic Drop

Fake out - Sometime during match, crazy falls on ground, acting as if he is hurt. When his opponent comes over to see what’s wrong (or to pick him up) crazy rolls him up for the pin.

Pain Killer - Figure Four leg lock

Tornado Slam - Belly to belly suplex

Unsolved Mystery #1 - While ref is down, MC takes out a pair of brass knuckles and hits opponent with them. Crazy then falls to the mat and crawls toward opponent (as if he is hurt) for the pin.

Unsolved Mystery #2 - While both men are outside of ring, Crazy crawls under the ring. MC comes out later on for a sneak attack.

Oz Kick (Wiz of oz kick) - Sidekick

2 Face Slam - Reverse DDT

Downtown Leg Drop - Atomic Leg Drop

Call-ATT Strikes - punches opponent straight down the middle; starting at their head and ending with a low blow (screams "1 800 Call-ATT" with each hit)

Finisher: Superkick (Out of Nowhere) Finisher name: Flashback

Other/Previous promotions: TW, VW, eW. Inc., OWL, NTICW
