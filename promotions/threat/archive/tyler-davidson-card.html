Biography

Tyler grew up in a family where both of his parents were lawyers at a very prestigious law firm in the New York City area. While most of the business was done in the New York area the family actually lived in Danbury, Connecticut in their own private estate far removed from the hounds of people that often travel throughout the area. Growing up Tyler was at a lack for very little as his family was capable of affording him anything he wanted whether it be entertainment or schooling. Tyler went to some of the best private schools in the state before he started high school and began participating more actively in sports.

At first his true interest laid in baseball and football and for his first two years of high school he had great years becoming one of the top players in the state in both sports. In his junior year of High School Tyler got his first taste of the wrestling world when his friends forced him into trying out for the team. To Tyler’s surprise he wound up making the varsity wrestling team. He immediately began training harder for the wrestling competitions than he did baseball and football and after a few weeks he quickly began to fall in love with the sport. He continued on the wrestling team for his remaining two years of High School in the meanwhile to the chagrin of his parents he dropped his baseball and football careers along the way. After his graduation from High School Tyler managed to convince his parents to put him through wrestling school in South Carolina while he also attended college there at Coastal Carolina University. Tyler eventually packed his bags and headed off for the sunny beaches of South Carolina where his life would take a totally new turn.

While training at the school this is where he met the man that would go on to have the biggest impact in his so far short career. That man would be “Suicidal” [name withheld], a man who would later on go on to win the World Heavyweight title on four occasions. He was about two years ahead of Tyler at this point in time but the two sparked up a very strong friendship that carried on through the years. In his remaining time there [name withheld] would often work extra with Tyler trying to give him an extra edge over the rest of the students. By the time [name withheld] graduated Tyler was far ahead of the rest of his class and was already receiving offers from small promotions around the region to begin competing but with his promise to his parents of remaining in school he opted to turn them down and continues his training there. During his schooling at Coastal Carolina he also met another person that would influence much of his life. That person would become in time his girlfriend Heather. The two struck up a friendship when sharing a journalism class together, which in time blossomed into more. Heather also from the Connecticut area has remained with Tyler for the past three years and now continues to support him in his quest to become one of the best in the business today.

After college Tyler made his debut wrestling debut. The owner of the fed he was part of at the time took a look at his background and upbringing and decided that it would be best for Tyler to play the role of a rich snob. It was a role that he didn't fill very well and resulted in him picking up a streak of losses. Things continued this way for Tyler until the return of his friend and trainer [name withheld]. [name withheld] came back into the promotion for the second time and joined with Tyler helping to break him free of the horrid gimmick and allowing him to be himself again. Since then Tyler has traveled the country and Japan training himself to be the best wrestler that can be. At the age of 24 Tyler made his debut for $oldout Wrestling and rapidly propelled himself into one of the companies top stars. Just as he seemed on the verge of capturing the Global Title however tragedy struck. While in California preparing for his big match his girlfriend Heather, left behind in New York City with the wife of [name withheld], was killed. With so much on his mind Tyler would come up short in his match and depart shortly after with the help of Victor Blood and T3 assaulting him and stuffing him in the back of a car. That was the last Tyler had been seen by anyone. Until now. Now he is back but as a much different man than he once was. Taking his place atop his father's company of Davidson Enterprises, it was Tyler who has since financed some of the newer financial acquisitions of [name withheld].

Since that time Tyler has fluctuated quite a bit as a person and a performer having stints in Five Star Wrestling and Icons of Wrestling before now taking a spot in thReat.
