The Legacy Begins [1]

Troy Mason sat in his leather-chair, in his wonderfully furnished office. Stacks of files were on his table, and he realised he would have to work faster if he was to enjoy a night out on the town following the show. His show. Flaw 13.

Flaw was his show, and thReat was his property.

Dressed smartly; wearing a long-sleeved blue shirt with a maroon tie, black pants with a belt that possessed a shiny silver buckle, and polished black shoes; Mason got up from his chair and walked over to the door of his office, opening it ever so slightly.

And he peeked out, his eyes observing the scene.

Tons of officials rushing about, paperwork to be sorted out. This was the first show since June of last year, and majority of the officials had been retained to ensure a tightly-knit backstage. They wanted the show to be the success, and with mere minutes before the big speech, everything had yet to be settled. The pyrotechnics had been screwing up all night, one of the referees now had food-poisoning, some idiot puked in the cranberry fruit punch in the catering room... but Troy Mason managed to smile.

He was a wrestling fanatic. Ever since he was a kid, Troy was addicted to wrestling. And when Jon Hackett called him out of the blue one day, with a favour to ask for, Mason couldn't possibly refuse. It was like a dream come true for the young man.

Now, here he was. Living the dream. thReat was his now, and Troy was going to make sure he led it right to the top. And had fun while doing so.

"The legacy begins. Tonight."

It certainly would. thReat was back, and looked to pick up where it left off last year. A smashing success, making waves all over. Sure, Jon Hackett was the architect then, and a stellar cast helped thReat gain recognition. But Troy Mason quickly used the files he inherited from his friend Jon, and established a star-studded roster of his own. One that would be unveiled over the course of the night.

Closing the door to his office, Troy returned to his seat, contemplating over how he was going to address the crowd later. A lot of things had to be said, a lot of points had to be raised up. But Mason knew it would be fine.

thReat's ride to the top was going to be smooth and painless. He just had a feeling it would be.

It was a hectic night thus far and was about to get a little more hectic.

J. Leslie Voss!

I'm not like your General Jackass and Wolf and Solex and God knows who else you let in here. I'm J. Leslie Voss dang-naggit!

J. LESLIE VOSS!" he roared at Mason.

Troy Mason looked up from his chair at the 6'6" man who was leaning over him and leaning on his desk. Mason shook his head with a grin of satisfaction.

Voss's jaw dropped and his eyes widened. He let it sink in for a second before he straightened himself up and his gaze became one of anger and rage.

I'm a major draw!"

The former thReat icon against a new-generation superstar.

Disciple X versus Fire.

The two combatants had each made their grand entrance moments ago, and were now in the ring. Both were just about ready to get the clash started. For almost a week, they prepared, with Troy Mason making the card available for quite some time now. It all came down to this one match. The first main-event of the revival. The capacity crowd... in awe of the atmosphere that had been generated. It was simply surreal.

The staring contest continued for a few seconds, before Disciple X struck first, with a quick and hard right. Fire came right back with his right hook of his own, and it was on. Disciple, Fire, Disciple, Fire, Disciple... Fire finally got a block in and took a slight advantage, connecting with two hard rights.. both of which unanswered.

But instead of going for a big move, Fire decided to throw another right. DX had recovered his marbles and blocked, before firing back with three of his hardest hitters. Going for the fourth proved to be greedy of the thReat icon, for Fire ducked and countered with a perfectly-executed falling neckbreaker. The crowd went wild.

Disciple X was back on his feet quickly enough, and managed to evade a clothesline attempt from The Hot One, before knocking his adversary down with a clothesline of his own. Fire sprung back up, and Disciple knocked him back down with another clothesline. Again, DX got greedy and went for a third clothesline. The 21w newcomer ducked and his momentum took him into the ropes. It was the same for the Fire, and the two were set to collide.

Fire had other ideas; he soared through the air and drove his left shoulder into Disciple's face. The crowd popped loudly, and Fire took advantage of the fact that he landed right on top of Disciple X.. and began hammering away with his clenched fist. However, being more experienced, the thReat Icon knew what to do and simply rolled over and on top of Fire, before he let loose with his own fists.

Naturally, the hardcore thReat fans in attendance loved it.

With Fire reeling, DX peeled himself off his opponent and pulled Fire up. The crowd got even more riled up when he began to viciously drive his right knee into Fire's gut. Repeatedly. The last one sending The Hot One staggering backwards a bit.. giving Disciple X the chance to measure Ralf up.

For a axe-kick to the back of Fire's head.

He decided he didn't want to wait anymore, and helped the 21wrestling newcomer up to his feet, before connecting with a couple of forearm strikes. Following that, Disciple X whipped his opponent into the corner. Due to the impact of the irish-whip, Fire's body bounced off the turnbuckle.. and as he grimaced from the sudden back pain he was experiencing, DX made matters worse for him.

Courtesy of a belly-to-belly suplex.

Reeling, Disciple X was now experiencing a moment of deja vu as he was whipped into the turnbuckle. The force behind the irish-whip was tremendous, as was DX's one moments earlier, and as the latter stumbled out of the corner... Fire knocked him down with a powerful shoulder-thrust.

Fire, sensing a golden opportunity with Disciple X flat on his back, quickly ascended the turnbuckle.. and with the crowd urging him on, took flight with an axe-handle smash in mind. Unprecedented

Troy Mason grinned, before he grabbed the remote control off his table and turned the television off. Flaw 13 had come to a wonderful ending and Mason was proud. Of himself, of everyone backstage, of all that competed in the ring... everybody who had a part to play tonight, he was proud of. The revival of thReat was truly unprecedented.

And the feedback he was receiving; truly phenomenal. It was raining down from every corner of the country. So much so that Troy decided to unplug his phone. Sure, he loved hearing the comments about how his roster was perfectly picked and so forth. But it got too overwhelming.

Looking around, Troy Mason finally realised this was his true calling. An owner of a wrestling company. His dream had come to pass and now, he was having the most fun in his life. Not many people actually accomplish their dreams, although Troy had Jon Hackett to thank. Again, all of this was truly... unprecedented.

Satisfied with the fact that he'd completed all of his paperwork and that Flaw 13 had gone off without a hitch, the young man removed his eye and placed it into the right breast pocket of his shirt. It was time to go out and celebrate with friends and colleagues, over a round of beers. He had earned it. So had everyone else who participated today.

But as he got up from his chair and turned off his table-lamp, now looking to pack his things, the door to his office swung open. And there stood a man that looked very familar to Troy.

To all thReat fans.

"Hello, Mr Mason."

Troy had jerked his head to the right to see what the commotion was and his mouth gaped open, as his brain tried to register the face he was looking at and to also come up with a name.

"You're... you are Jakks. Vincent Pembridge!"

Indeed he was. A major player in the thReat fighting division during the company's first run in the sun, Jakks; or now going by his real name, Vincent Pembridge; had shown up in an organisation two weeks ago. All-Star Championship Wrestling. And quickly stamped his authority there.

Like he did in thReat the first time around, but with limited reward. Or so everyone thought. They didn't know the whole story.

"Yes, you're correct. Heard about thReat and figured I'd stop by. Someone told me that you've scrapped the hybrid format?" Vincent said, sneering, while advancing into his office.

Troy Mason's heart began to skip a couple of beats, as he saw The Scorpion Of Manchester tread into the office with a sadistic smile. He had heard from many people about what Vincent was capable of. Even read his file.

"Also read that you are, or were, friends with Jon Hackett? Your file has some very interesting information, Mr Mason."

Then, to everyone watching, it all made sense. Vincent was the man waiting in the wings and babbling about some takeover. Beads started to form on Mason's head as he straightened up and cleared his throat, unsure of where this whole thing was going. In Pembridge's head, however, the whole scenario had been accumulately planned for weeks.

"Yes, I knew Hackett very well."

And with those words uttered, Vincent charged forward, grabbing Troy by the throat and pinning him against the wall. Troy Mason's eyes almost bulged out of the sockets as he began to struggle and choke. The British Degenerate began to laugh like a madman, his voice filling the entire office.

"Listen, twat. Hackett betrayed me. A deal was made but he backstabbed me at the last minute. Now, me felt that Hackett was a wee bit unreasonable but this latest string of developments... I won't stand for it. I'm back to claim what's rightfully mine, and more. You won't stop me. No cunt will stop me. Shite, anybody who even dares to try will get annihilated and their knobber chopped off."

Following that, Vincent rammed his knee into Troy's stomach, before throwing him forcefully against the boss's own table. The impact was thundering but Troy was still conscious. Just in a whole lot of pain and agony. Vincent Pembridge looked down at Mason and spat at him, before the smile and the laugh disappeared. He had one last thing to say...

"The FIGHTERS are coming back. And taking over. That's all there is to it, you stupid cunt."

Watching as Vincent coolly walked out of the office, Troy rolled to his side and began to reflect on what the hell had just gone on. Vincent Pembridge was back, pissed at hell, wanted revenge, power... and he intended to do it by bringing back the fighters.

Truly... unprecedented, you have to admit.
