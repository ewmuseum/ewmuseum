Previously

Previously, on thReat programming.

There was this poll on some lame forum-driven website, where this Nazi-wannabe named K3NNY posts about his enermous dick, about the top "bad guys" in the land. And I'm right up there, with Vincent. Quite a shock, but understandable.

Now, following Malevolence, do you all still think I'm a bad guy? I'm the one that saved thReat, with Vinnie's little sister, Karen. Not Xaxon. All he did was make it official. I did all the hard work. For two months, I schemed my ass off. Why do you think, on Flaw 16, I smiled when Troy laid down the law for the Showdown and the aftermath?

Because I knew no matter what, even if my plan didn't go according as planned, I'd be out of thReat. I had no intention of staying, I was simply helping Karen get one over her brother. Send out a message on her behalf -- that she was nearly ready to take him on. And Vince, it didn't break my heart to turn on you.

Fact that you're pals with Campbell made me wanna do it all the more.

So, Troy, Karen & myself expect a "THANK YOU" card sometime soon. thReat fans, likewise. I'm gonna focus full-time on tA now, because THE FOREVER CHALLENGER has a lot of work to do over there. Maybe I'll make a comeback someday, as El Crackerooni, seeing how that's the contract I'm signed under. But until then, have fun with Xaxon, Troy. Vince really did beat you after all. Heh. Who would have guessed it? But you know what? Don't expect me to help you out there.

My job in thReat... is done.

---

His face was lying on his desk. The man was sobbing. Ah damn, he was crying his little heart out, for God's sake. Just as he thought he had everything well in hand with the outstanding defeat of the Vagabonds and Vincent Pembridge, the very man who won it for the Resistance had now turned on him. Perhaps Mason's head was on that desk to cover up the damage down by that brutal headbutt given to him at Malevolence.

He's just a young man, trying to make his dreams come true. To run a wrestling company, to become wealthy, to become famous. He's gotten those three things, but with added extras all around that make all of it seem worth nothing. The constant attempts to crush his self-esteem have plagued him, and he's at his boiling point.

What made everything worse was that with the defeat, Vincent Pembridge still seemed like a winner. All those weeks planning, Troy could have checked up on the contracts on the Vagabonds' members. Now, it had been revealed that all but Ampske & Vincent were spared from having to wrestle in thReat. And as for the Scorpion himself, he'd made it known that he didn't mind losing money from walking out on the company.

He had his contingency plans.

---

The General stood there in the shadows.

He turned the lights on while Troy shuddered rubbing his eyes from the light.

"You should be ragingly happy."

"Should I?" Troy said. "Why are you in such a good mood? I thought you were in a new mood or something when you wanted to kill everyone you saw?"

"I was. But I took it out on J. Leslie Voss. See, I'm trying to control my inner self. But that's now what I came to talk about... I wanted to congratulate you, sir, but apparently you are not in the mood."

"How could I be?"

The General began to list.

"One: Vincent Pembridge is off you back and no longer up your ass. Two: ThReat is still fully yours and a wrestling company. Three: Your world champion has returned with a twist, so ratings are going to sail through the roof. Four: NO MORE VOSS!"

The General would have danced, but it would've been embarrassing and just... odd.

Troy thought about what Sanders had to say. He made sense.

"Yeah, I guess you're right, Sanders. I've got to look at the good things."

"That's right. Not the bad."

"I own this company." Troy began his revival speech. "This is fully mine. There are no more problems. This is the best thReat has been since Week One!" Troy stood up with a smile. "Practically all my troubles have been resolved!"

"Well..." Sanders said in a low voice. "Not everything."

Troy looked up at Sanders.

"Someone left a floater in the bathroom."

Troy acted serious to the joke of Sanders. "Well, damnit! That ruins everything!" Troy slammed his fist on his desk sarcastically, as Sanders and Troy shared a laugh.

---

Walking to the front of his desk, Mason began to shake his booty. The ratings for Flaw 19 was set to be the highest ever, for the show. And the fact that the team division was flourishing made things all the more worthwhile. It demanded a celebration of some sort.

"I'm doing a little jig, cuz I'm so happy... the ratings for this show, are gonna shock ol' Uncle Vinnie!"

Just then, the door to the office was kicked open. And in stormed Xaxon, who looked generally pissed off and drenched. It seems the rain had some part in him arriving so late to the Tio Pepe Hall, but finally, he was there. And with Troy consumed in his nonsensical singing, Xaxon took advantage.

Punch to the face.

"OWWWWW!"

Troy fell to the ground, but Xaxon pulled him back up, and smashed the owner's face against his own desk.

Twice.

Thrice.

Four times.

Five times.

Six times.

Seven times.

Help, I'm losing count.

Finally, Xaxon threw Troy Mason back down onto the floor, as blood covered the man's face. He was whimpering pathetically, as well, trying to find the words to beg for mercy. There wasn't about to be any dished out by Xaxon, as he stomped Troy's face, before kneeling down and bringing his face as close as possible to the owner's.

"My title. 'Vhere iz it?"

Troy blinked. He had to find the right answer, he knew that.

"I-I... d-don't k-k-know! For r-real! I s-seriously don't know!"

Xaxon smiled. He knew it was a lie. He could smell the fear on Troy's breath, and the lie he was being fed. The rebirth; it made the man nicknamed Azre become a little more astute. He had the innate ability to judge who was being nice, and who was being naughty.

"Wrong answer."

So, expectedly, it was lights out for Troy. Just as Flaw 19 officially came to an end. However, that's not where the story ended. See, while everybody filed out of the Tio Pepe Hall, someone happened to stumble into Troy's office by accident. And seeing the state it was in, a call to the police was put forth.

Almost an hour after Flaw 19 went off the air, police arrived on the scene. Storming into the office of Troy Mason, they were stunned to see blood on the walls, with the entire office trashed. One other thing was pretty much noticeable. It really couldn't be missed. The most critical piece of the puzzle, fellas.

...

Troy Mason was... nowhere in sight.

---

With two other members of the Board Of Directors, Sucino Hass and Jackson Mahon, Moe Sole was in the backstage area... going through final discussion over production details for the show. Quality, camera angles, whether or not to really activate the spycam in the public toilets -- that kind of shit. It was something that was second nature to Troy Mason.

But he wasn't there on the night.


Hence, the massive stress on Moe Sole's shoulders. Elected by the rest of the Board Of Directors to be interim guy-in-charge, his experience in the industry being the biggest factor. But maybe it was because of his age that he wasn't quite able to really all the stress that came with filling Troy Mason's shoes.

Moe Sole was a survivor, however. And he wasn't about to give up.

It seemed everything had been taken care of, and with no time to spare, as well. Flaw 20 was about to get underway, sans Troy Mason and all. Moe turned and traded congratulatory high-fives with Sucino & Jackson, who too were relieved that everything had looked to be picture-perfect.

That was before the arrival of three police inspectors on the scene.

"Mr Sole? Inspector Andrews here. We're here to continue the investigation into the disapperance of Troy Mason. As it stands, Xaxon is still our number one suspect..." the burly inspector began, as he flashed his badge at the Board Of Directors trio.

"But?" Sole asked, as Jackson & Hass leaned forward, eager to find out more about the situation.

The look on the face of Inspector Andrews should have tipped them off as to what was to come.

"We believe he had an accomplice. An accomplice who is right here, in his building, at this very moment.

And a very influential accomplice, too."

---

"It's quite simple, as I've explained. By influence, I don't mean anyone with any real power. The investigation I've carried out with my team have linked the accomplice to the events of the past two months within the company. Now, I'm not a fan of all this wrestling thing, but there was a little... invasion of sorts going on?

Led by a man by the name of Vincent Pembridge?"

Moe Sole scratched his forehead, the entire situation causing him to have even more wrinkles in his old age. The mere mention of the Scorpion's name ran shivers down his spine. After all, the Board Of Directors were the ones that gave Vincent the crucial advantage in his war with Troy Mason, falling for his 'sweet' words... and eventually giving him complete power, albeit for a short period of time.

Dark days in the company, that. Giving control of thReat to a madman hell-bent on the organisation's destruction, for his own ego to be boosted? Not good. At least the cancer was rectified quickly, but the damage had been done. And its ramifications were being felt by EVERYONE now. Moe Sole knew that, and wished he could have turned back the hands of time.

And prevented the rest of the Board from entrusting the Brit with that kind of power.

"Yes, a little invasion. But Vincent Pembridge can't be the one behind this. I mean, it's a little complicated than it sounds, Inspector..."

Andrews sighed, realising just how long and drawn-out this case would be, while Moe rubbed his temples, feeling a migraine coming on. Suddenly, it hit him. There were a few pieces of the puzzle he hadn't yet considered, and one of them was sure as hell influential over the last two weeks.

"But I think I might know of a probable suspect!" Sole exclaimed suddenly.

The Inspector's eyes lit up, as he tore a new page from his notepad and got ready to scribble information down.

"His name would be?"

Sole cleared his throat, proud that he had figured this out all on his own.

"Ampske."

---

"What?" Ampske muttered back, as he winced, with the therapist putting more pressure on his ankle. See, he was getting it massaged, to help it recover. See?

Andrews frowned, knowing right off that if Ampske had an ankle injury and wasn't afraid of showing up to an event WITH the injury, that would rule him out as Xaxon's accomplice. Still, he was obligated to try and find SOMETHING that would trace a connection between Ampske or Xaxon. Or anything at all which was relevant.

"Since when has he been like this? Injured, I mean..." Andrews posed to the therapist. "Last week." ---

The good Inspector took all of this down, and had several ideas running through his head as a result of Ampske's response, which didn't quite put the pitfighter out of the running just yet. Maybe he DID have a motive for being Xaxon's accomplice, seeing how he basically screwed himself. But peharps, in Ampske's mind...

Troy Mason screwed him, by not briefing him on Aggression Title rules.

"Vagabonds, eh? Vincent Pembridge was the big dog of the group. What happened to him?"

Again, Ampske gritted his teeth, due to a combination of the sudden burning pain in his ankle and the question that was posed to him. Why was his time being wasted, the enigmatic pitfighter with a mysterious past wondered.

"He left. Simple as that. After Malevolence, the fucker blamed all of us for the defeat and basically disowned us. If you want to find out where he is, give the office of the All-Star Championship Wrestling a call. He's basically committed to that place now, so you'll probably find out his itenary or something."

Inspector Andrews wrote all of this down, a million thoughts blazing in his head. None of them, however, were helping him to inch any closer towards the climax of this case. So many loopholes were still floating about... and none of them looked to be dismissed by Ampske.

So, Andrews got up and thanked Ampske for his time, before walking out of the room.

The investigation continued. Who was next, though?

---

Jackson Mahon's cellphone rang, and he excused himself to answer it, while the rest of the Board began to discuss the next steps amongst themselves. Inspector Andrews, meanwhile, filed all his reports and shoved them into his briefcase. He'd have to retire back to hotel home and get some rest, for the next day would be another long day of fact-finding and such.

Just as he was about to take his leave from the office, Jackson Mahon turned off his cellphone following the conversation and returned to the table with a look of urgency on his face.

"Moe, that was a detective friend of mine! Turn on the big screen!"

Sole grabbed the remote and turned on the big screen television in the office. Static greeted everybody for the first few seconds, before an unfamiliar setting came into view. And what everybody saw then shocked them down to their bones.

Troy Mason, bound and gagged to a chair, completely bloodied and almost devoid of life. With explosives wrapped around him.

Then, static again.

Everybody gulped, completely stunned. Before they turned to look at Inspector Andrews, who had one thing to say.

"Well, fuck me dead."

We can also say that Troy Mason is still in the state of Kentucky, and so is Xaxon. We at The Patterson Agency carried out an investigation of our own, and the results are most interesting.

" Inspector Andrews nodded his head, as his eyes widened in surprise. Folding his arms, Andrews rested his spine against the edge of the table, interesting to hear more.

"So, what are the results of your little investigation? I'm dying to know, maybe I can learn where I've gone wrong in my entire career..." he replied sardonically.

Norman simply grinned, as he placed his briefcase down the table and slightly loosened his tie.

Jon Hackett.

Pure lunacy.

Result: GENERAL MOTHERFUCKING DAVID SANDERS VIA CLIMB-OUT TO WIN THE MOTHERFUCKING THREAT WORLD CHAMPIONSHIP

All Over

Man, it was incredible. That backheel kick, Xaxon crashing through the ring... the Matrix-esque way Sanders got himself over onto the other side of the cage... and the way he just jumped down to the ground with only a few feet left. Man oh man, it was fucking sweet.

I almost went deaf as my section started celebrating like crazy fuckers. Balloons fell from the sky, and Troy Mason cried as he rushed over to Sanders, picking him up and hugging him! It was totally cool... especially when the referee handed Sanders the World Title. He could barely stand, but he grabbed it and raised it in the air anyways.

Xaxon? That fucker stayed still, amongst the debris. He looked fucking dead.

Vincent Pembridge then got up and clapped as he walked over to Troy Mason & Sanders. When they saw Vincent coming, Troy & David Sanders wiped the smiles of their faces, staring at the British fucker. Our celebrations looked like they were gonna end, but you know what happened?

Vince simply nodded his head and walked off, a smile on his face.

Troy & the General watched him leave, before they hugged each other again. An amazing sight to behold, I tell you. Truly wonderful. thReat was finally free from the crazy fucker, Xaxon, and we had ourselves a true American as our World Champion! It was golden, man!

So golden, we got to our feets and sang along, as they played our fucking national anthem.

Our IDay was here. Bless the lord.

Bless the lord.
