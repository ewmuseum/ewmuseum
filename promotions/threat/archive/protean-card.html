Biography

On the 14th of November 1979, the world turned upside down then inside out, and Jacob Seldon was dragged kicking and screaming into a world he would soon learn to resent with a passion. Son of proud parents Lisa Seldon and Richard Joshua Seldon, homesteading in a cramped Ottawa farm house. The family, while not particular well off, were never what you could call poverty stricken and Jacob always seemed to get what he wanted, perhaps because as he said himself "I never wanted much, except my privacy".

Two years later the Seldon family welcomed a new arrival, a sister for Jacob, Laura Richard Seldon was born on July 19th 1981. Jacob, who was very cautious show affection for anyone took an immediate liking for his new baby sister, one that would last to the very end.

Schooling was very important to the children's parents, and both Jacob and Laura were sent to the best school that the family could afford. This payed off very quickly, and both children showed an impressive aptitude for learning. When only seventeen Laura sold her first novel to a prominent American publisher. Jacob, however, showed no passion for literature and announced his intention to become a professional wrestler almost daily.

Although his parents were skeptical they allowed Jacob to enroll in a local Candian Training Program. His dilligence payed off and Jacob was noticed by Jack Whitton a recruiter from The Gene Pool Professional Wrestling Training Centre For Exceptional Individuals, located in Nevada. While initially his parents forbade it, they soon came around when Whitton assured them that Jacob was one of the best he had seen in a long time.

Sadly Jacob's father never lived to see his son live out the dream, when he died of a fatal heart attack suddenly while working. This, it would appear was no great loss to Jacob or Laura, as it transpired that Richard Seldon was something of an abusive and violent father.

Taking up room and board in a beaten up motel, Jacob's time was split between wrestling under the name "Silver Sentinel" (A local Ottawa comic book hero), and drinking himself towards an early grave. It is possible that Jacob would have happily spent the next five years in training, but tragedy brought him back home.

Laura fell ill, of what was later diagnosed as cancer. Jacob remained at her side until she finally died on January 3rd 1997.

However before he returned he had made one new aqquaintance that would follow him, he met Natalia Elysa Taylor, a manager in training, and the two fell instantly in love. Natalia followed him north to Canada and was present, at Jacob's request, for the funeral of his sister. Natalia and Protean began living together in a small one bedroom apartment in central Ottawa, where they remained for a while until Jacob won a contract in Liberty Wrestling Federation.

He wrestled under the name Protean, a character that he and Natalia had invented together.

Shortly before winning the LWF Hardcore Title, Natalia became troubled with their relationship and disappeared. Jacob immediately assumed that she was headed to find a Gene Pool partner who wrestled as Psycho-Mantis. After a tip off from an old friend of Natalia's, Monique, Jacob was thundering down south to Lost Wages, Nevada, in search of his manager.

He found her in the ruins of the Gene Pool training center, and the two have made a shakey future together.....

The story continues.....
