Biography

Mathew William Brendan Walton was born to Jeffery and Tina Walton on the 28th of May in the year 1985 in Corner Brook, Newfoundland. For as long as he or anyone else for that matter could remember for that matter, Mat had always loved wrestling. He along with his Uncle, former star in the wrestling world, Andrew Martin would always go down to the Civic Centre and watch the wrestling cards on the rare occasion any promotion would come to Newfoundland, especially that part of Newfoundland.

As was the case for most Newfoundlanders, both Mat's and Andrew's parents were laid off. Again, like most Newfoundlanders, they moved to a place that was like a second home for the loveable bunch from the East Coast, Fort McMurray, Alberta when Walton was just three years of age and Martin, six.

Their postal code may have changed, but their love for wrestling didn't waver at all. The two were introduced to a new, more professional looking type of promotion...Stampede Wrestling.

This is when the two of them began seriously considering wrestling as their profession, and due to the lack of size on Walton's behalf the two were uncertain about their future.

At the age of seventeen Martin went off and joined the World Wrestling Revolution. With his new found fame and success, Martin all but forgot about his roots, and most importantly, his promise to his Nephew.

The WWR closed just over two years later and Martin returned home to Fort McMurray to find his Nephew still wanting to be a wrestler. Martin reluctantly accepted, giving his Nephew a chance at stardom but having the thought in the back of his mind that his Nephew was much too small to go past his inital training.

But much to the surprise of Martin, other workers, and even himself at times, Walton succeeded and Martin cheerfully offered him a match at a promotion in Calgary, Alberta during Stampede week.

However, during Walton's match, something went terribly wrong. Walton went for a suicide dive over the top rope without grabbing onto it but his foot got caught as he was going over and he fell to the concrete below on the top of his head.

The fall had knocked him out cold. When he awoke in the hospital, he had found out he had suffered a severe concussion, and had broke two vertebrae in his neck. Doctors told him that he should never wrestle again. Never one to listen to advice however, the head strong Walton has joined thReat looking to make a name for himself, and.....most importantly...... make his Uncle proud of him.
