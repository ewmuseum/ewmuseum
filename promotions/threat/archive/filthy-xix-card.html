Filthy XIX

Real Name: King Filthy Grimy Disgusting Pulsating Puss-Filled Malformed Horowitz XIX Nickname: "King of the Trashheap, Lord of the Landfill" Also known as: Mr. Filth, Grimez, FILTHIMANIA!(only used by fans), and basically anything you can think up that involves him being a worthless, stinking hobo

Born: Unknown (appears to be mid to upper 30's) Place of Birth: Unknown...Dumpster, perhaps? Hometown: Nomadic

Height: 6'2 Weight: 266 lbs. Blood type: O+ Eye color: Green and really cat-like

Team: None

Manager: Ted and Shane Fawcett Trainer: In all actuality, the person(s) who trained him now want nothing to do with him. They refuse to divulge the name of the school for fear of losing stock value in their school. Sowwy, Filthy XIX :-(

Theme song: "Let's Get Dirty" by Redman

Style

Competing style: Dirty, Filthy Hobo-Style with some elements of technical, power, hardcore, and a smidgen of high-flying skills rarely

Signature moves:
Puke: Sitout Fire Thunder Driver

Wretch: Mandible Claw

Disgust: Dragon Screw into the Anklelock

Contamination: Double Powerbomb

Epidemic: Stiff Uppercut to the cleft of the chin

Sleaze: Inverted Suplex into Ace Crusher [Osaka Street Cutter]

Disease: Running Spear to the spine of an opponent

Tainted: Stiff knife-edge chop to the throat

The Landfill: Diamond Cutter out of a suplex [suplex into cutter]

Scuzz: Double Underhook Brainbuster

Slimer: Kick to the nuts

Boogers~!: An all-powerful poke to the eyes!

Decay: Boxing Combo [left hook, right hook, left jab, right cross, spinning right punch to the nose]

Crud: Starts like a suplex, but quickly spins into a wicked neckbreaker [Zach Gowen does this move]

Contagion: Spike Piledriver

Pollution: Death Valley Driver

Dregs: Reverse DDT backbreaker into Dragon Sleeper

Muck: Over the shoulder powerbomb with opponent landing face first into turnbuckle

FILTHIMANIA: Hulk Hogan BIG ASS BOOT/LEG DROP COMBO!

That's Just WRONG: Filthy pulls a cup from the front of his pants and shoves it into the face of an opponent, hence the name![rare...thank GOD!]

Basically, any goofy thing you can think up, Filthy will do it. He has a completely spontaneous in-ring style where he can brawl, he can crawl, he can fly, he can die...well, I don't THINK he'll collapse and die, but I betcha you want him to, don't you?

OTHER MOVES: Spear, DDT, Russian Leg Sweep, Back Drop Elbow Driver (Matt Hardy's Richochet), belly to belly suplex, t-bone suplex, Germ Suplex (renamed German Suplex), backbreaker, tornado back breaker, vertical suplex, leg drop, knee drop, moonsault[rare], top-rope splash[rare], powerbomb, basic grappling holds, scratching himself, laughing insanely, lighting things on fire, spitting on people, picking his nose, eating his boogers, and any gross thing you can think of.

Finisher: GRIME TIME! Finisher name: Filthy picks the opponent up in a Crucifix Powerbomb position and lifts the opponent up, but as they go down, he flips them around and blasts the opponent with a stiff DDT on the way down [Crucifix Powerbomb DDT]

Historical Info

Other/Previous promotions: IWO, IWO-Black, IML, PWA, NTICW, CDC (all as other characters, still in CDC as other character)

Other/Previous teams: Again...none!

Other/Previous managers: None.

Other titles won: IWO Black First and Only Xtreme Champion, IML Extreme Champion, IWO-Black Tag Titles, IML Tag Titles (None for Filthy, though. :-( )
Other awards won: Graduated from PWA without competing in a single match

Biography

FEAR THE POWER OF THE MAJESTIC HOBO CALLED FILTHY XIX! (that's Filthy the 19th to you mortals!)

Watch the meteoric rise of a hobo from the depths of Central Park trash to Park Avenue Trash!

More to come, a-holes!

All you need to know is that the father/son team of Ted and Shane Fawcett needed to rise to superstardom, they couldn't find anyone but Filthy XIX while he was tying bricks to seagulls' legs and throwing them in the harbor in NYC.

They taught him to wrestle, he was extremely good, and now here they are in thReat looking to find their first chance at superstardom.

BOW DOWN TO FILTHIMANIA! For whatcha gonna do when FILTHIMANIA slobbers all over you?!?!

Well, what WILL you do?

To paraphrase Phil Atken...

BOW DOWN! That's what~!
