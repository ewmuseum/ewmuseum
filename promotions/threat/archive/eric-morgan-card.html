Eric Morgan

Real Name: Eric Morgan Nickname: "The Survivor"

Born: 08-21-82 Place of Birth: Watts, California Hometown: Drifter(mainly about the metro L.A. area)

Height: 6'1" Weight: 198 Blood type: A+ Eye color: Blue

Team: None

Trainer: Hector "Chongo" Garcia

Theme song: "Changes" by 2pac

Style

Competing style: Kung Fu/Luchadore/Street

Signature moves:
Combos:
1.Eric hits the opponent with a straight jab, grapples their shoulder in a front underhook and then hits them with an upwards elbow strike to the chin/nose.

2.Eric right crosses, then backfists the opponent, then hops sideways and rolls in midair to hit the opponent with a side jumping roundhouse to the face/chest that goes a little bit higher than where the backfist struck.

3.Eric intercepts a punch, drives his knee into the opponent's elbow, then side elbows them to the face. He then moves under through the arm he intercepted to deliver a back sweeping hook kick to their back/rear of skull. This last kick is often not neccessary, but is used sometimes.

4.Eric blocks a kick by driving his knee into the opponent's leg and with the opposite leg, does a jumping inside crescent kick across the side of the jaw or chin.

Regular Moves:
1. Back flipping kick-Eric jumps upwards and hits opponent with both legs at different or the same time. If he uses both legs in cycle, he can strip a weapon from the opponent with one and then kick them in the chin with the other.

2.Jumping Spinning Outside Crescent Kick

3.Aerial Kick-basically a kick that is a handless cartwheel kick

4.Single Shoulder-Underhook sitout face buster. (Can be done as a counter to a move, and very quickly)

5.Lightning quick extended roundhouse to the side of the skull.

Finisher: Eric swings with all his might in a lightning quick roundhouse, which he stops about an inch from his opponent's face. He then smirks, and amazingly does hops into the air, rolls sideways and brings down in a side spinning a motion, an axe kick on the crown of his opponent's head.
Finisher name: The Fakeout

Historical Info

Other/Previous teams: X(w/ Vash)

Biography

Eric Morgan was born in Watts, California, the son of two Berkeley radicals who went into the harsh blue-collar ghetto to hide from authorities. They were found, however, and in an ensuing gunfight, were killed. They had expected this to happen, but could not leave the area because of the mother being pregnant with Baby Eric. So they waited until the birth and left young Eric with a neighbor, a kindhearted elderly black lady by the name of Faith Jordan. Eric was raised from the age of 1 month to age 11 by Faith, whereupon she was killed on her lawn as the victims of stray bullets shed during gang battles all across this fallen land do. Something that weighed heavily on Eric’s conscience, for you see, Faith was killed while watering the lawn, a chore Eric should have been doing that night. Eric then took to the streets, fearing the government that killed his parents and that had sought to place him in state care.

Eric was on the streets, fighting for his life and his meals for about 3 years, until the age of 14, when he met Shan Liu. Shan Liu was an elderly Chinese man who lived in an isolated home on a far side of Watts. He had a reputation for being a scary old man, and was avoided by most of even the gang members in Watts. Somehow, Eric wandered onto his property while foraging for food and was confronted by Liu. Liu was not the scary old man he had appeared to be, and took Eric into his care. Liu had been a teacher of both martial arts and school until he retired and decided to take refuge in this horrific neighborhood. No reason was ever given by Liu for his choice in home, but one might assume that he felt the most dangerous of areas might also contain the most peace of all as well. Liu taught Eric Kung Fu and a few other forms of self defense as well as homeschooled him until the age of 17, when Eric gained his diploma equivalency from the LA Unified School District and was accepted into Pepperdine University on scholarship.

Unfortunately, it was this year in which Eric discovered why Shan Liu had been living in Watts. He had quit his life and had absconded to Watts for having angered some Triad members in a bad spree of gambling 20 years previous. The Triads found him because Liu’s name had been announced in the newspaper as having been the teacher of Eric Morgan, the young man from Watts who went from the streets to Pepperdine. While Eric was away at school, the Triads came and got their vengeance upon Shan Liu in such ways that could not even be imagined by a true human being. Eric fell into a fit of depression, feeling at fault for Shan Liu’s death as well as Faith Jordan’s, and to a lesser degree, that of his own parents. Eric began to withdraw from the world, in ways that can be best assumed rather than told, and eventually was thrown out of Pepperdine for not achieving well.

For the next 2 years, Eric subsisted by working odd jobs and occasionally foraging for food about Los Angeles. He finally landed a dependable job as a janitor for a semi-pro wrestling federation’s arena of competition. He was paid just enough to eat before sleeping in his beatup non-running Taurus.

The wrestlers in the federation made considerable light of him, because of his hygiene and lack of different clothing. This made his job quite miserable, until one day when a wrestler actually attacked Eric. Eric had been harassed and had ignored it fearing his job being taken, but never physically abused before. He reacted by knocking the wrestler out with a one-inch punch to the solar plexus which made him reel and vomit and cracked two ribs. The other wrestlers cowered in fear and the trainer/owner of the promotion, a former Luchadore by the name of Hector “Chongo” Garcia, saw immediately a gifted pupil in Eric. He began training Eric for free and gave him a place to stay in his own home. Eric became a regular, paid member of the federation, and began to love wrestling very much. He excelled at it, from the level of toughness he gained from being a child of the streets of Modern America and his training by Shan Liu and his enormous amount of heart and courage that just seemed naturally endowed upon him.
