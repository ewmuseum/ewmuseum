Twizzy

Real Name: James Maxwell Stoneking Nickname: "Twisted or Twizz" Also known as: The Co-Dependant, The Lost Soul, The Druggy, The Addict

Born: 7/31/70 Place of Birth: New York City, New York Hometown: New York City, New York

Height: 6 feet 3 inches Weight: 245 lbs. Blood type: A+ Eye color: Dark brown

Team: N/A

Manager: N/A Trainer: Crypt

Theme song: 'Junkhead' - Alice in Chains

Style

Competing style: Reality Fighter/Head Work/Limited Risk Taker

Signature moves:
The Twisted Opinion- Corkscrew Superkick to chest.

Pill Popper- Russian Leg Sweep, but on the downward fall Twizz turns to his opponent, hitting then with a clothesline.

Double Knee Drop- Double Knee Drop from top turnbuckle.

With a Twist- Single Leg Crab and then adds on a Spinning Toehold.

Flying Clothesline- From top rope, dive off with arm across the nose of opponent.

Held-Rope Dropkick- Runs at ropes and with both hands grabs top rope. Bounces off ropes while holding top rope with hands and kicks out feet to perform a variated dropkick.

Tri-Rope Bounce- Facing outside hold top rope. Jump and bounce off bottom rope, followed by bouncing off second rope, let go of top rope and bounce off it finally leaping to the outside for a Double Axe Handle.

No Handed Suicide Dive- A suicide dive without hands in front.

Side Effect- Matt Hardy's Side Effect.

Rebel Yell- Twizzy leans over and yells as loud as possible into opponent's ear.

Samauri Driver II- Attacker tackles opponent and lifts them in the air. They spear them into the turnbuckle and using their momentum and the stopped opponent's leverage flip their legs up while hugging the opponent's waist. The attacker's legs go up to the shoulder of the opponent with their feet often touching the top turnbuckle. Attacker lets go of waist and flips their torso up as quickly as possible so as not to give the dazed opponent time to recuperate. Attacker locks arms around opponent's head and leans back, driving the head of the opponent into the mat.

Finisher: Forward Falling DDT- From a headlock and fall forward to knees or stomach depending on opponent's size. This drives the top of the opponent's head straight into the mat.
Finisher name: Screw Loose

Historical Info

Other/Previous promotions: IWO, CWU, XW, and GCW

Other/Previous teams: GCW: The Lost Soul Militia (w/'The Dark Messiah' Jason O'Neil, Dimensioner, The Grim Reaper, X-Calibur, and Tobias Fang) CWU: New Breed (w/ Vince Reed, Eric 'All That' Allen, and 8-Ball)

Other/Previous managers: N/A

Other titles won: 4x GCW Tag Team Champion and GCW World Champion. Other awards won: Longest Running GCW Champion, Longest Running GCW World Champion, Only GCW Wrestler To Hold Both Tag Titles At One Time, and CWU Number One Contender To Every Title.
