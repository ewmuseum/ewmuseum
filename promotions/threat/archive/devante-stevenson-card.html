Biography

4:03am, September 8th, 1984 a babies cries filled the air in hospital room 418 of Queens Capitol Hospital. After 5hours of pain-staking labour Yasmine Antoinette Stevenson gave birth to her first child, a boy which she named simply… DeVante. The name DeVante wasn’t derived from anything in particular just a seemingly original name which she gave her son; no other names following… simply DeVante Stevenson. After a mere half hour the young DeVante stopped crying and became peaceful… opening his eyes to the world at 4:33am.

Since the moment he exited his mother’s womb and entered the world in that Queens Hospital, his legacy began. Like most children DeVante progressed through his infant years wide-eyed and curious, always touching and examining everything around him and asking a million and one questions. His mother doted over him; every time he asked a question, touched an object or stared at a picture she adored him. As a toddler he kept himself out of trouble, playing hopscotch at the steps of his apartment with the other children in his neighbourhood as his mother worked around the house.

His future was never clear but seeing the way he acted as toddler and seeing his innocent face no one could’ve predicted what he would turn into in his later years. It took 5hours to give birth to this child… and only 4seconds to turn his life upside-down.

Mid-day, July 18th, 1988 the children were playing their usual games of hopscotch and baseball on the street and steps in front of DeVante’s apartment. His mother was inside their home washing his clothes and preparing him for his first day of pre-school the next day when tragedy struck. Certain issues are unclear, why what happened did, all that is known is that the events that occurred re-arranged his fragile mind and made him mature in seconds. A black Impala rolled down his street; the windows of the car as black as its paint. The breeze blew as the laughter and sounds of children having fun wafted in the air… as did cigarette smoke from a group of teenagers sitting on the steps a few meters away from DeVante.

As the car rolled down the street the window of the passenger side wound down that little bit and a shiny object appeared. No one expected a thing but being curious DeVante watched the object glisten in the sunlight as the car rolled past the steps near his game… a few seconds later the sounds of gunshots echoed down the street; cries of young children could be heard as the cars tires screeched as it tore down the street and disappeared. As the bullets left the gun DeVante saw it all… the blaze from the shot and the blood as the bullet thumped into the teenagers chest; the dead look in his eyes as he slumped to the ground lifeless.

Many 5year olds wouldn’t be able to cope with the trauma that accompanies seeing a murder, many children wouldn’t understand… but DeVante was different, he understood what had happened and he accepted it.

For days he would not speak, his mother cried and hugged him for hours until finally everything quietened down and DeVante returned to being a starry eyed young boy; something was different though. A year later from that event Yasmine Stevenson fell pregnant again, a year and several hours after that event she was in the same hospital, same ward giving birth yet again. DeVante was sitting in the waiting room staring silent at a Knicks game on the T.V.

9:00am, March 4th, 1996 the once curious young boy had grown to be a teenager and was beginning his first day of High School. He had moved through Primary School with average grades, showing no sign that what he had witnessed had effected him in the slightest… he was just another face in the crowd. As he attended class his sister, Latisha Eve, was beginning her first day of third year Primary School. All was running well for the Stevenson family apart from the fact his mother was single and living off of one salary.

Yasmine Antoinette Stevenson had always been a heavy smoker, whilst she worked around the house she would casually smoke cigarette after cigarette. Whilst she had breaks during her job as a checkout chick she smoked… finally it all caught up to her. Through both of her pregnancies she had smoked and it had not affected her children or herself, but after becoming ill one morning she decided to visit her local doctor. A few days later after extensive testing she was diagnosed with lung cancer… she didn’t break the news to her children nor did she give up smoking. She lived life like she usually would.

The years passed and the cancer was slowly taking its toll on Yasmine Antoinette, schooling was proving difficult for DeVante but he was sticking it through and Latisha was flying through Primary school. One fateful day during DeVante’s third year of High school something clicked in his mind. Somehow an event triggered something in his mind and he lashed out violently, striking a student in the face and breaking his nose. Before students are punished they are sent to the Principles office… their parents are then called. Whilst the Principle talked with DeVante’s mother about his actions she remained calm. When he arrived home she broke down, confessing her illness and changing DeVante completely.

He had always been one to take things well but when his mother confessed her illness a tear rolled down his cheek; his mother had just told him she was dying. She begged DeVante to complete his schooling and make something of his life and made him promise her that he would always take care of his sister… but it was too late.

6:45am, April 12th, 1999 the sounds of two children getting ready for a day of school and making breakfast filled the apartment of the Stevenson family. Latisha was in the shower when DeVante went to wake his mother as she was working an early shift at the supermarket; his mother was not there. As he pushed open her door his mother lay motionless on the bed, her eyes closed peacefully… immediately he knew what was wrong. Being curious was a trait that had stuck with him throughout the years and he was always very observational; there was no rise or fall in her chest… he knew his mother had passed.

For the third time in his life tears strolled down his cheeks as he bowed his head, went into the kitchen and called an ambulance before returning to his mothers side. His sisters cries filled the apartment as the paramedics shook their head; he knew there was nothing they could do, the cancer had taken her. From that day forward he decided to change his lifestyle and make something of himself, only not in the way his mother had hoped. The only promise he had made to her was to take care of his sister, silently he vowed he would never let anything harm her as he put on his jacket and left his sister crying on the sofa.

As the door of his apartment slowly closed behind him, so did the door of opportunity for him to make something out of himself. Ceasing his schooling that very moment he breathed at the foot of the steps to his apartment. He turned to the left and began to stroll towards the alley near the side of his apartment… he knew which path he was taking and he was old enough to realise the repercussions of his actions; after being through everything he had… the part of him that cared had vanished.

4:03pm, November 6th, 2000 cigarette smoke wafted in the air as the sound of the city bustled around the steps of the apartment. A teenage Latisha dawdled up the stairs carrying a backpack and looking flustered from a hard day at school; glancing to her left she saw her brother dressed in baggy jeans, a puffy black jacket and cream beanie smoking. A faint smile crossed over her lips as she saw her brother, in her eyes he was her world; winking back at her he turned to his side and handed a teenager a small bag to which he exchanged money. Her world tumbled down as she saw her brother dealing drugs; bowing her head she disappeared upstairs as DeVante went back to business.

The elder brother looked after his younger sister as the two survived on child support pay checks; Latisha attended schooling, determined to pull herself up and out of the ghetto whilst DeVante did all kinds of things on the streets. Fighting, Dealing Drugs, Graffiti… it was all a regular part of DeVante’s lifestyle; he felt no need to do anything more than make sure his sister was looked after. It wasn’t until he was arrested for drunken assault that he finally looked at his life in a different perspective.

Everything had taken its toll on his matured mind; the death of that teenager and the death of his mother, he had been left with nothing. But no counsellor, no single person could convince him to change his mind. Most people look at the world in color, seeing many perspectives to a story... DeVante however saw things in black and white, his side and theirs. There was no one that could pull himself up and out, he was in too deep… the life he had he was living through his sister as out of it all he made sure she was safe and well.

11:49pm, December 31st, 2002 a lonesome figure sat with smoke trailing from his mouth in a lounge room. Reaching out he flicked the television set off; the sound of his sister talking in the other room faded as she hung up the telephone. All was quiet in the Stevenson house-hold. A weary look crossed over DeVante’s face as he reached down the sofa beside him; producing a thick book with a manila cover and loose pages floating everywhere. Dumping it on the coffee table in front of him he exhaled and squinted before looking down at the cover.

‘DeVante ‘D.V.S’ Stevenson Court Folder’ the Title was enough to prove that he had made himself known on the streets of New York City. So thick the book was that it looked as heavy as all the cases which resided in it; his whole life of trouble, crime and confusion was in that folder with pages. Some of those pages were fresh whilst others were old, all the same they showed what DeVante had become of himself.

Everything he had lived for, everything he had overcome was reduced to pages in that book. Showing no emotion DeVante Stevenson sat and stared at the cover of the book smoking… inhaling and exhaling slowly; a fresh page of that book was being written everyday as he grew. Nearly 20 years of age DeVante knew that his time would be cut short if he didn’t change his lifestyle, he needed a shield… the income that was being provided was doing little more than pay the rent and bills. Finding a job would be tough for him but he knew if he set his mind to something he could achieve everything, finally he took into account what his mother had said to him.

If he couldn’t finish his schooling he would make sure his sister would, if he couldn’t become a high paid doctor then he would sure as hell make something out of himself. He needed something he had a passion for, something he was actually good at to set his mind onto. As he flicked through the pages of the book the cigarette finally came to an end and the pages finally stopped. Atop the page was the word ‘Assault’ below that were a few paragraphs of witness accounts and legal bull shit when finally his eyes came to rest on a certain quote, ‘vicious fighting’ in those two words his answer and future lied….
