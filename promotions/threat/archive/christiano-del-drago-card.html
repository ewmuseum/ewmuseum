Biography

Christiano's greatest vice in life is that he was born as the bastard child of exiled Mafia heiress Angelina Drago in 1986. Angelina's fiance had died only three weeks before he was born, at his grandmothers plush mansion in Verona, in what appeared to be a poorly planned assassination attempt. Popular myth has it that Sebastian burst into the dining room after a private dinner between Isabella Drago and her two brothers, fired three shots, one hitting Isabella in the head, the other two wounding her two brothers. Quick to react as Mafia men usually are, the two brothers quickly fired back at Sebastian, flooring him before he could fire again. Meanwhile, Angelina heard the return fire on Sebastian and sped off in the planned getaway car to escape. She drove to Milan at blistering pace, before arriving at a friends house and throwing herself upon his mercy. Two weeks earlier than planned, Christiano made his grand entrance - only to become an orphaned bastard child less than a week later, when Sam and Allé Drago caught up with their estranged Niece.

That's the story that has been spoon fed to Christiano since his Great Uncle Sam took him in as his own after finding him crying amongst the aftermath of the Milan showdown. The showdown that watched him suffer his second non-fatal wound and his brother die in the same fashion as his sister.

The Milan showdown made sure that there were only three possibly heirs to the Drago Empire.

Allesandro Drago II. Five year old grandson of Sam's brother. His father had been caught in the crossfire of a bank robbery when he was only three, and his mother was not a pure-blood Drago. Uncle Sam was kind enough to take him under his roof in New York too.

Christiano Drago. The one week old boy that would be king.

And finally, the man that was already king - Salvatore Drago. Better known as Sam.

Sam had no children of his own. He'd been too busy helping his ageing father take the New York underground to have children. That was why he was only too happy to adopt his two Great Nephews at the drop of a hat, after almost every other relative had met their end tragically, taking care of Family business.

For eighteen years, Christiano was brought up like he was Sam's own child. Looked upon more favorably than Allesandro; Christi would always be the favorite.

Drago had been schooled in the ways of the family from a tender age. Sam had taken special care to craft him into the perfect heir to his throne. By Sixteen, Christiano had earned his leather jacket. By seventeen, he had two henchmen by his side at all times. Considered too valuable to risk, Antonio and James were ordered to do what was necessary to ensure Christiano's survival.

On his eighteenth birthday, Sam had offered Christiano anything he could wish for. The answer he received was not what he expected.

You see, Sam had always spoiled Christiano. He bought the most expensive cable packages, every pay-per-view movie and event he ever wanted, any toy or console he could wish for. Anything. If Christi asked, Christi received And he almost always asked for the wrestling pay-per-views. Sam had ignored it at first, wrestling was something boys his age liked. Even Sam himself enjoyed attending any shows Christi dragged him to in New York... but he never expected this...

"I want to be a Wrestler..."

Sam had spent years crafting him to be a hard working, determined, inventive, devious mafia boss... and now Christiano wanted to go into wrestling instead?

Sam wouldn't have it. Instead, Christiano received a Porsche, and $65,000. Not a bad let-down gift, you might say; but Christiano was determined. He'd dreamt of wrestling for years... and he was going to do it. In the company of his Uncle, Christiano stopped his blatant interest in wrestling. He made a down payment on his own flat and flew the nest.

Sam was proud, he'd stopped his heir turning his attention... or so he thought.

After the down payment on the apartment, Christiano still had a sizable amount of money left. With it, he employed some of the most famous technical wrestlers in the world to give him one off lessons in grappling. Coupled with his mental attitude, athletic build and natural agility, most felt Christiano was a natural. Once he'd learned what he needed to know, he went in to as many trials as he could. Trying to get noticed at every twist and turn. It wasn't long before he'd been picked up by one of the smaller Federations, and started to rocket through the ranks as his natural charisma and acquired talent shone through.

Normally, this sort of rise would be accompanied by the adoration of the fans, however Christiano's 'inventive' streak didn't sit well with them. Especially the inventive ways he would bend the rules to obtain victory. Coupled with his built in love for his country of origin, and the US fans were not keen.

With undeniable talent, natural charisma, an unrivalled mental attitude and - of course - his two bodyguards, Christiano now heads towards thReat, hoping rise through the ranks of wrestling and praying that his Uncle never feels the urge to check out any Wrestling he might hear about...
