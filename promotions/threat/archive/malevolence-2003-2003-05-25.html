Previously

Previously, on thReat programming.

---

He was a wrestling fanatic. Ever since he was a kid, Troy was addicted to wrestling. And when Jon Hackett called him out of the blue one day, with a favour to ask for, Mason couldn't possibly refuse. It was like a dream come true for the young man.

Now, here he was. Living the dream. thReat was his now, and Troy was going to make sure he led it right to the top. And had fun while doing so.

"The legacy begins. Tonight."

It certainly would. thReat was back, and looked to pick up where it left off last year. A smashing success, making waves all over. Sure, Jon Hackett was the architect then, and a stellar cast helped thReat gain recognition. But Troy Mason quickly used the files he inherited from his friend Jon, and established a star-studded roster of his own. One that would be unveiled over the course of the night.

---

Tremendous cheers broke out for the new charismatic owner of thReat, who seemed to be brimming with confidence. It was almost as if his exuberence was bursting at the seams, but Troy Mason knew when to keep his head on straight. He wasn't one to exagerrate things and blow them out of proportions. This moment was special for him and he had no intentions whatsoever of mucking it up.

The eyes of everyone were watching him, he couldn't afford to muck up.

"Now, with that little introductory story out of the way, I'm here to lay down some ground rules. The basic of the basics. Bread and butter, black and white. First off... this hybrid concept Hackett had in place? Fighting and wrestling? Scratch that; a NEW thReat means only one orientation. And I'ma choose WRESTLING!"

He grinned, loosening his tie a bit more, soaking in the cheers he was getting. Quite surreal for an owner to get such a reception, but Mason was playing his cards right.

"Pure wrestling, baby. Pinfalls, submissions... all that juicy stuff. I don't have a special place in my heart for senseless fighting and thus, I'm going to throw it out the window."

---

Looking around, Troy Mason finally realised this was his true calling. An owner of a wrestling company. His dream had come to pass and now, he was having the most fun in his life. Not many people actually accomplish their dreams, although Troy had Jon Hackett to thank. Again, all of this was truly... unprecedented.

Satisfied with the fact that he'd completed all of his paperwork and that Flaw 13 had gone off without a hitch, the young man removed his eye and placed it into the right breast pocket of his shirt. It was time to go out and celebrate with friends and colleagues, over a round of beers. He had earned it. So had everyone else who participated today.

But as he got up from his chair and turned off his table-lamp, now looking to pack his things, the door to his office swung open. And there stood a man that looked very familar to Troy.

To all thReat fans.

"Hello, Mr Mason."

Troy had jerked his head to the right to see what the commotion was and his mouth gaped open, as his brain tried to register the face he was looking at and to also come up with a name.

"You're... you are Jakks. Vincent Pembridge!"

---

And with those words uttered, Vincent charged forward, grabbing Troy by the throat and pinning him against the wall. Troy Mason's eyes almost bulged out of the sockets as he began to struggle and choke. The British Degenerate began to laugh like a madman, his voice filling the entire office.

"Listen, twat. Hackett betrayed me. A deal was made but he backstabbed me at the last minute. Now, me felt that Hackett was a wee bit unreasonable but this latest string of developments... I won't stand for it. I'm back to claim what's rightfully mine, and more. You won't stop me. No cunt will stop me. Shite, anybody who even dares to try will get annihilated and their knobber chopped off."

Following that, Vincent rammed his knee into Troy's stomach, before throwing him forcefully against the boss's own table. The impact was thundering but Troy was still conscious. Just in a whole lot of pain and agony. Vincent Pembridge looked down at Mason and spat at him, before the smile and the laugh disappeared. He had one last thing to say...

"The FIGHTERS are coming back. And taking over. That's all there is to it, you stupid cunt."

---

"Here's the juicy bits, cunts. I told Hackett I'd be willing to screw over the fighters. If he, of course, did something in return for me. See, I actually started out as a wrestler. It wouldn't have mattered to me one bit if thReat reverted back to wrestling. I've done both, I've excelled in both. And Hackett began to think that it was a smashing idea. He asked what was it I wanted. I told him I wanted to be the last thReat Fighting Champion. But with the ability to defend it during the new thReat. The wrestling-oriented thReat."

Shocked gasps. Surprised looks on the faces of the fans. In the back, every member of the roster were stunned. Troy Mason's face turned white with aghast. There was more to come.

"Of course, the bloody cunt was sceptical. Said that he had intended for August Monday to win. I told him it would work perfectly. August would beat Quincy, I would beat August and injure him, there'd only be three bleeding fighters taking part. I'd help them get through it, all the other fighters would regard me as a true champion... before, of course, I bugger up the whole thing and at the end, thReat would become wrestling!

And you know what, cunts?

You know what?

JON HACKETT AGREED TO IT ALL."

Absolute silence.

Completely absolute silence.

Troy Mason's eyes almost fell out of the sockets. His heart almost stopped. Hackett would have never done a despicable thing like that, he thought. But he was wrong. Jon Hackett had. He was desperate. Vincent was getting irritating. A pest. Would have been better to simply submit to his demands.

There was, yes, much more yet to be revealed.

"BUT THE CUNT CLOSED THE SODDING COMPANY THE VERY NEXT DAY! ON THE EVE OF THE BLOODY PAY-PER-VIEW!"

At the sound of those melodic words; melodic because it obviously incensed Vincent a hell of a lot; the crowd broke out in a massive round of cheers. Mason punched the air in delight; that was the Jon Hackett he knew and befriended.

---

"You're forgetting one very simple thing for your masterplan. You're still contracted to thReat.

What?

What?

Yes, you are! Jon closed the place down, but in the process, placed some kind of insurance on the contracts. Meaning once a former member of thReat shows up during a future thReat event, he is automatically part of the roster.

Why do you think your name was on the website, Vincent?"

MEGA MEGA POP. Pembridge was stunned to the bone. This revelation was all news to him. And not good news to him.

"This basically means, Vincent, that you're officially a thReat wrestler!"

---

The crowd screamed in joy as they jubilated at Christian Embers’ bold words. The defiance and sincerity moved them…

“YOU SEE… VINCE…”

The crowd instantaneously booed as they heard the beginnings of Vincent Pembridge, aka Jakks. The name made their blood boil… but they knew Christian Embers would stymie that heat with his cool, refreshing sarcastic comments.

Right?

“YOU SEE… VINCENT PEMBRIDGE AND I ARE PARTNERS, ALONG WITH KNICKERSON!!” boasted Christian… indeed this was defiant…

But this defiance the crowd did not like… no… the crowd didn’t like it one bit. They gasped in terror as some lowly few cheered immensely.

Vincent's plan was taking shape at an alarming rate.

"Men, this is it. This is bloody ground zero. Tonight, we shall show Troy Mason we mean business.

Once and for all, we'll bloody show everyone what we're made of.

We're treated as outcasts because we rather fight for a living. Because it's in our blood. Because it's in our nature. Because we're good at it. But is it bloody fair? Was it fair of Hackett to betray me? Sure, backstabbing happens every single moment of every bleeding day. It's inevitable, innit?

All I want is what was rightfully mine. POWER.

Everybody loves power. You want power. You know you do. Every single one of you. Embers, Bob, Ampske. I know you want it.

And here, we have the chance to grab the power. It's our time, men. We've been treated as vagabonds. Outcasts. Troy Mason wishes to exile us because we think differently, and we act differently. Our methods may be crass, yes, but it's in our nature, innit? And the only way to make these foolish people accept the fact that what WE do is simply what we ARE BORN to do is by any means possible."

But hey, there's always a twist, right? Guess who came out next, just when Troy's troops were gaining their second wind and were making it an even fight, somehow?

Well, none other than EL CRACKEROONI if you believe that.

The cheers returned, and Troy turned around, wondering why. Imagine his shock when he saw Crackerooni swagger down the ramp, looking ready for battle. He stopped in front of Mason and did a little dance, before giving him the thumbs up.

Crackerooni rolled into the ring, looked at Fire beating down Embers... and stomped Christian's face. Before clotheslining Fire.

"WHAT THE FUCK?!" Troy screamed.

It wasn't over. Crackerooni helped Embers up, and together, they took Royale out, as Knickerson groggily got up to his feet. The number of lifeless bodies rose, as Ampske threw Disciple X halfway across the ring. West & Semps had knocked each other out, and Voss had taken his fight with Sanders outside the ring, admist the battle between the British teenagers and Troy's security.

Solex? Knocked out by Wolf.

Until El Crackerooni kicked him in the back of the head, as Vincent Pembridge executed a wicked DDT on Dane.

The Vagabonds had won. They were standing tall, amongst a pile of broken bodies, as the teenagers on the outside had finally overpowered the security. El Crackerooni turned to Troy Mason, and rose his arms in the air. Before Vincent P yanked Crakcerooni's mask off.

If you, The Vagabonds, happen to win... thReat will become an actual fighting promotion. No more wrestling matches, unless stated otherwise. The board of directors will appoint Vincent and yourself as the new owners as thReat. I will step down as owner.

The only way there will be a breakeven is if in the event that you get fired.

And the only person who can fire you... is ME."

Fifteen minutes later, with the show truly over and done with, Vincent Pembridge came sprinting out to the arena.

The stadium was absolutely dead empty. The backstage was empty. There was nobody in the arena at all. Not even cleaners, who usually had to stay behind and clean up the mess the fans had made. Empty, except for five people.

The Vagabonds.

3 of which were laying in the cage, face down, in a pool of their own blood. Ty Hughes was outside the cage, bruised... looking inside. Solemn. Reflecting on how easily he was duped, somewhat, by Troy Mason's look of desperation earlier. Before he was jumped from behind, by a middle-aged Eastern European man and a old fossil who was the biggest pervert on the face of the planet.

Noticing Vincent Pembridge at the stage, his fists clenched, his breathing heavy... Hughes shook his head. The eventual score was 3-2 to the Vagabonds on the night, but the 2 points Troy had amassed was now looking as if it was a 100 points. Simply because they were two critical points. Magnified, it all meant one thing for Vincent Pembridge and his posse.

"We were outnumbered."

It seemed that Vincent Pembridge had now gotten near-unaminous support of the thReat board of directors, and the Scorpion Of Manchester single-handedly booked the show, leaving Troy Mason with the chance to make only ONE match. One match; those words kept coming back to hound Mason.

He was boiling with rage, and the intensity in his bloodshot eyes showed it. The battle between himself & The Vagabonds was clearly taking its toll on him, but tonight, Troy wished to continue on the good mini-streak his charges were on. Having outplayed the team of fighters sans Vincent last week, it seemed a weak link had been established.

Ty Hughes wasn't the force he seemed to be. His recent feuds in tA must have left Hughes worse for wear, and now, Troy knew where the focus of his attack would lie. However, something in the back of his mind kept nagging him about the idea. Like a warning, or something.

---

Six members of Troy Mason's resistance, including Darren Anthony in the ring. With an almighty roar, General Sanders let the troops down the ramp, and into the ring. And almost immediately, in accordance with the increasing roars of approval from the crowd... the tide turned. The fighters were now getting overpowered, as Troy Mason shouted instructions.

But then, a new twist in the chapter between The Vagabonds & The Resistance occured.

"Time" by Taproot.

Vincent Pembridge came sprinting out, with a chair in hand, and in one swift motion... he jumped into the ring, and cracked the chair over the head of Jason Semps, before hammering Vertigo. Slowly enough, the fighters began to regroup, with their insipirational leader back in the fray. Troy Mason watched from the outside, as the real preview between the two teams unfolded. It was evenly matched, as Vincent's troops battled his troops tooth and nail.

And as Flaw 17 came to a frenzied end, Troy Mason had his face buried in his hands. His Resistance had faltered in the face of overwhelming physicality in terms of the 6 men that represented his worst nightmare. They were standing tall, arms raised in the air, victorious in this round.

The showdown at Malevolence... would overshadow everything. And by then, either Troy Mason or Vincent Pembridge would have won.

The tA Superstar didn't quite agree with the direction Pembridge was taking, with regardings to the big planned surprise on the night... and was now fighting for it to be scrapped.

However, once the British Degenerate makes up his mind, it's impossible to change it.

The thReat Fighting Title."

SCUM.

Good Idea Or Bad Idea?

Such a wonderful feeling. Finally being back in a place he could really call home. After months of sitting at home, thinking about everything that had happened lately, he was glad to finally be back, and to finally be back on television.

Everything started to go down hill in IOW. After a decent run as Television Champion, Anubis screwed all of that up. Kidnapping one of the few people he could trust, and then costing him his television title. Neither man could be found in a promotion afterwards, until tonight.

While tonight marked a lot of endings for thReat, as it would go to either fully wrestling, or fully fighting, one person saw this as only the beginning. Before he could get back to his business with clearing up his past however, he had one more thing to take care of. That was what brought him to where he was standing tonight, on what could possibly be the most important night in thReat's existance, Levond Daniels was standing in front of the man who could easily be labeled the most important piece of the puzzle tonight.

Vincent Pembridge.

The two had found each other wandering the hallways. Time was ticking down to the fateful moment where everything would change, and Vincent seemed impatient as he stood before Levond Daniels.

"Figured I would run into you back here sooner or later tonight." Levond started what he hoped would lead into a conversation with the leader of The Vagabonds. That didn't seem to be working though, as Vincent just let his eyes drift off, obviously bored with Daniels.

"Ok. Uh. Yea. So I was thinking. I hate wrestling just as much as the rest of you guys do, yea? You've got a couple of weak links on your team here, and ah. I ah, yea. I'll offer my services to replace Knickerson or Embers, since they're obviously the ones holding the group down."

Pembridge just cracked a smirk at Daniels and shrugged, pushing past. Levond turned his head to watch as Vincent walked away, shaking his head in disbelief.

thReat Ain't Over

It was so surreal. I never heard such a deafening explosion of cheers, and the celebrations that followed immediately. It was madness. Xaxon actually did it. The Resistance actually won. Everything was so hazy for me at the moment. I was never prouder to be a thReat fan.

But after Vincent rolled out of the ring, and laid on the ground, his eyes looking at the rafters... Xaxon helped Troy Mason up, and the two men hugged. Xaxon was about to leave the ring, but he stopped, and turned to look at Troy celebrating like a madman. That was funny to watch.

Then, Xaxon got a microphone. And the smile on his face vanished.

The dude went on to say that the meetings the two had proved to be beneficial, but they never discussed about the thReat World Title. Malevolence, yes. World Title, no. Xaxon continued, murmuring something about that since thReat closed due to Hackett, and he was champion when it closed... he was, in actual fact, STILL the champion.

Due to Mason saying whatever contracts that existed in thReat v1 had been carried over to thReat v2.

Now, this confused the heck out of me, and Troy Mason as well. But Xaxon went on to cite an example -- Vincent Pembridge's contract. Meaning he was still a part of thReat, and had to WRESTLE. Applied here, it basically meant Xaxon was still the champion. Suddenly, all of us were stunned. I remember throwing my drink away, and paying real close attention.
