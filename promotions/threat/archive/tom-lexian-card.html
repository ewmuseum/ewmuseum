Biography

Legend Inc. is the leader of the lame, and the self proclaimed source of all lameness, but underneath that shroud of deception is the Executioner, a title dawned on him by the leader of the Vampirical resistance. He wears the Amulet, an artifact that gives its possessor greater than human strength and added agility to be able to leap farther and longer than an average human. Of course, this amulet is not allowed to be used in the ring where it would give Legend Inc. an advantage over other competitors.

History:

It began in the city of San Francisco, California....

...On the date of September 26, 1974 at the University of California, San Francisco...little Tom Lexian was born at approximately 3:45 am. Born to the loving parents of Ryan Lexian and Marlene Lexian, Tom was nicked named by his parents as T.A.M., standing for Tough and Mean. It was often joked that when the doctor spanked Tom when he was born, he splurted out the profanity, "Screw You."

As a child, Tom Lexian found himself in a well off home, where they could afford him the luxuries that other children did not. But while Tom never let the material possessions own him, he realized at a young age that he was different from other kids. He noticed that some children had less than others, and were not as happy as he was. He would carry this image of disproportion with him for the rest of his life.

He attended Saint Mary's Elementary School through the grades of K - 8, and grew up in a rather loving and nurturing household. A private school gave him a chance to interact with other kids in a smaller classroom environment, but it also introduced him to a crueler world. A world where all kids were expected to grow up sooner than expected, and Tom was plunged into constant debates about clothing, material wealth, and money. He kept to himself in grade school, never allowing himself to get caught up in the madness. His attempts to venture into a public school, however, were thwarted when his parents saw nothing wrong with a private high school.

After attending grade school he went to Sacred Heart high school, also in San Francisco, and yet another private school.. There he participated in many sports including swimming, tennis and basketball. But his main concentration was on school work, as his parents wanted him to aspire to eventually major in the medical field. Although the environment improved, Tom didn’t feel that his true talents were being tested, and ventured away from his studies into other areas. After graduating with honors, despite his academic frustrations, Tom accepted his admission bid into the University of San Francisco where he applied as a Biological Sciences major.

In college he pursued schoolwork but also began to work on mat wrestling. He developed an interest in the sport and began pursuing it in a competitive fashion. He was only a walk on for the wrestling team, but it was soon apparent that Tom had an inordinate amount of talent when it came to the sport. He had a mental toughness and will that allowed him to reach a level of competitiveness far greater than anyone could imagine. When he graduated he had the choice of applying for Medical school or applying to wrestling school. He chose the later against his parents' wishes. His parents were upset, but would later forgive Tom, and even urge Tom on and attend his matches later in his career.

Tom Lexian attended Mike McDaniels school of wrestling located in Fresno, CA. It was here where he would be brought under the guidance of armlocks, piledrivers, and suplexes. It was here that he developed into the wrestler he is today. It was here...when Legend Inc. would soon be born. After watching tapes of such classic wrestlers like Ric Flair and Magnum TA, he decided that his style of choice was technical wrestling and submission. He would use ring knowledge to his advantage and always play mind games.

His instructor once told him that the secret to being a superb wrestler was to always keep your opponent off balance, both in the ring and in his mind. “You can’t let them know what you’re thinking,” they told Tom, “or else you’ve already lost.” Tom incorporated this line of thinking into every match, trying to keep his opponents guessing, mixing up his style every time. Although he wasn’t at the top of his wrestling class, he was good enough to graduate, right behind the top student, Marcus Orena, who he would meet again in the future.

But Tom wouldn't be a wrestler immediately. Not a real one, anyways. He began in a backyard promotion, and in his first match, would face off against the World champion. He came in with the moniker, The Legend, and was immediately ostracized for his proclamation of legendary status at such a young age. After being thoroughly thrashed, Tom went back to the basics, trying to improve his non-existent skills. He would head back to the indy circuit, where he would slave and sweat, until finally noticed by fed heads.

Career Timeline

October 5, 1997
Tom Lexian officially entered the ring in the backyard wrestling promotion, Xtreme Wrestling, which was headed up by Brian Winters. His collection of young superstars brought forth a mass of men who were willing to put forth their bodies for the sake of entertaining a crowd. Tom entered simply as “The Legend,” and in his first match, was booked against the World champion, “The Answer,” and was destroyed. In the end, Lexian was placed on a table and the champion landed a five star frog splash on him.

October 6, 1997
Lexian is diagnosed with two broken ribs and a fractured wrist, and deemed out of action for two months. Later that week, Lexian would be released from Xtreme Wrestling.

November 10, 1997
A call is made to Lexin at his home. It is his old trainer from “Mike McDaniel’s school of wrestling.” He tells Lexian that when he gets well, he’s got a gig for him at a local wrestling promotion.

December 19, 1997
All-American Wrestling signs Lexian as “AfterShock,” no longer using the Legend moniker. His first match is against “Shawn Steele,” another young star. They would befriend each other after the match and become good backstage friends, despite Steele taking the victory.

February 2, 1998
Lexian is caught with drugs backstage, given to him by several other wrestlers. He is reprimanded and suspended for a month for the infraction. Lexian was the only man to be punished.

March 16, 1998
After serving two weeks for drug abuse, Lexian returned to a heel role, although he had played the good guy for a majority of his career. The fans responded to the new AfterShock, but Tom couldn’t play the role with his entire heart behind it.

April 1, 1998
Frustrated with Tom’s inability to work within the system, Tom is released from AAW. Steele also wants to leave with Tom, but he convinces him to stay, telling him that he actually has a future there. Steele would end up leaving a month later.

August 1998
After being out of action for several months, Lexian found work again as a wrestler in the ECWF.
He entered EWCF under the name of simply, "Legend." He reused his original name, this time dying his hair grayish to give the appearance of age, but the fans did not buy it. His first match was scheduled to be a one falls match against "Captain Spikey" in which he was victorious. His next match was scheduled to be a "Skywalker" match, but it was never to be.... The ECWF has closed.

It was here that Tom meet Natalie Dischner, a local reporter. Tom asked her out during her coverage of his match, and the two began dating regularly.

September 1998
It was a sight that "Legend" would become all too familiar with. He would soon bounce around federations and promotions like a pin ball until finally he landed in a niche that suited him: TBWL. Here, "Legend" became "AfterShock", a post apocalyptic gimmick. Within a few weeks, AfterShock had established himself a major star and landed a title shot at the U.S. champion Kid Rock which he eventually win. The TBWL would close a few weeks later...

October 1998
The closing of the TBWL was a bit disturbing, but the taste of gold was enough to continue AfterShock in his reign of terror. This time, after searching out many promotions, he landed in the UWF as "Legend" once more and it was in the UWF where he would make a continued impact. At first going solo, "Legend" won his first title in the UWF teaming up with his brother, "Blizzard," and winning the UWF Tag titles. After his brother retired, "Legend" befriended Sean "Rocket" O'Reilly who would join him in the stable soon to be known as "The Legendary Alliance." Soon joining them afterwards were "Amazing" Albert Ambriz and "Relentless" Ryan Roulet. This stable caused chaos throughout the UWF eventually leading them to dominate the scene, eventually enabling "Legend" to obtain the UWF World title - his first title reign. Eventually "Legend" and Sean O'Reilly would have a change of heart and try to change the rampant rulebreakers around, but the other two members would not have it. A breakup ensued causing Sean O'Reilly and Tom Lexian to leave the UWF. Subsequently, Sean retired due to a severe knee injury. Albert Ambriz and Ryan Roulet would eventually be fired from the UWF. All that remained was Tom Lexian, who gazed upon the wreckage he had caused....he would retire soon after....

February 2001
"Headlines: A newcomer has signed a contract with up and coming promotion, RWW. More details to come." The rumors were true, and that newcomer was none other than "Legend Inc." His first match would occur in a battle royal event in the first PPV for the RWW in which he signed a contract on the spot and immediately was placed in the battle royal. He would be eliminated later by Tazz.

In his first match he was involved in a #1 Contenders match for the RWW World title against Blade. After coming out victorious, he would face Triple H in what would be his 2nd World title reign in only his second match in the promotion. After title defenses against contenders such as Big Daddy Bruce and Tazz, he would eventually succumb to injury and pressing concerns and be forced to take a leave of absence from the RWW and had to drop the title to Tazz.

The other pressing concerns turned out to be previous contract engagements with both the oWa and DWF - promotions he had already been signed to. The contracts were signed and nothing else could be done. But his return in the RWW was guaranteed, and it would come sooner than later...

March 2001
In the oWa, Legend Inc. would be able to come out of the gate hard and fast, gardnering wins over Don Mega in the finals of the Pacific Title Number One contenders tournament, getting a shot at the title at the next oWa PPV, Global Dominance. At this same time, Legend Inc. had been very involved with the interfed, IWAPro, signing a dream match against James Malenko of EPW fame. The IWAPro would close soon after, not allowing the fans to see who was the better man. At this point, Legend Inc. also signed a deal to work for the End of Days tournament, and would end up seeded #1 in the West bracket with such wrestlers as Dusty, Syphon Fission, and The Big Shot. However, Lexian would pull out of the tournament in the second round, citing an injury suffered a few days before. It was at this time that Legend Inc. would make his in ring debut for the DWF, gardnering a draw against Jeremy Krayzie Howard.

It was in the oWa that Lexian first meets Miranda Morehouse, a reporter for Pro Wrestling Magazine. She would become fixated on Lexian and his career, continually following him and his career.

The upcoming PPV, Global Dominance over the oWa, would approach faster and faster, with Legend Inc. slated to take on Jason Slade for the Pacific title and also teaming up with Mike Extreme in the tag title tourney. This would be the beginning of a great friendship and partnership. It would not be, however, as the oWa would close at eXstaZe's discretion, vowing to reopen with the PPV, a week later.

It was also this month where Sean O’Reilly, Lexian’s manager and best friend, took up the nickname of the “Impaler,” stealing the name from an oWa wrestler who was summarily defeated by Lexian. It was spawned during a promo that involved a bag boy pushing a shopping cart and tripping over the cart and landing in it.

April 2001
The beginning of April would mark the month where Legend Inc. was completely determined to make it big in the DWF. The draw to Jeremy Howard was a setback, but the next week was already signed to face Howard, Joey T, and Killer Instinct in a fatal four way match for the Novice title. That would end up being Legend Inc.'s first win and his first title in the DWF. His next match would be against The Educator in a Novice title defense. He would come out successful, but at the same time, he would end up giving the title to Joey T, in exchange for T's People title shot against Dusty.

It was at this time that the oWa announced that they would not be coming back until May 1st. Serious questions arose to whether or not Legend Inc. would be returning. Also, Mike Extreme began hinting that he may be interesting in returning to the DWF and possibly continuing their team there as well.

Legend Inc.'s next challenge would come in the form of joining Primetime Championship Wrestling, working under the Dream Team banner alongside Mike Extreme, Caged Explosion and Jeremy Howard. They would surprise assault the PCW World title match involving Frisco and Meanstreak, during the PCW PPV, Spring Fling, and eventually get their first win in a 6 man tag match involving Legend Inc., Caged Explosion and Howard versus Frisco, Meanstreak, and Alan Kriegman.

Legend Inc. would then end his career in PCW (3-0), garnering victories against the Blood Brothers with Mike Extreme, and defeating PCW mainstay, Scott Naket. He would leave with Mike Extreme, who had internal problems with PCW. Soon after, the Dream Team was dismantled, being remembered as one of the worst PCW teams ever.

In the middle of April, the oWa was announced to be returning at May 1st. At this time, Legend Inc. was still undefeated in the DWF, having to settle for a draw versus Doozer in his U.S. title match, after Chainz interfered, causing Legend Inc.'s appearance at the DWF PPV, PrimeTime 3, to be in doubt. But Legend Inc. would be able to compete, garnering victories over Bungle (who had won the People's title from X-Factor, who had defeated Dusty) to win the People's title, and then winning the tag tournament with Mike Extreme to win the DWF tag titles. The month of April ended with Legend Inc. holding a lot of gold.

May 2001
The People’s title in the DWF is disbanded, and Lexian is known as the last title holder. During the May PPV, Lexian would win the DWF U.S. championship in JW Hell Match, defeating the current champion and DWF HOF’er, Doozer. This would be revenge for the draw suffered earlier to Doozer in April.

During this same month, rumors circulated that IWO, one of the famed wrestling organizations in the world, was looking for fresh new talent. With Mike Extreme’s former stint there, he convinced Lexian to send in a resume, and the two would wait to see what would transpire.

The return to oWa was short lived, after garnering the oWa tag titles, the oWa would close by the end of the month.

Later that month, it would be revealed that Lexian had come in contact with the Amulet of Orion, a famed symbol of the Vampire Executioners, who would call upon Lexian to become one of them. The amulet gave Lexian increased strength, heightened awareness, and added agility to jump tall heights when he wore it. He meet Alexandria, a fellow VK (Vampire Killer), as well as Alejandro, a vampire servant to Angelina, the Vampire Master of the City. (Source: Roleplays)

It was announced by [name withheld] that IWO would split into IWO Black and White, and Mike Extreme and Tom Lexian had been placed in IWO Black under the leadership of John Maples. They would capture the IWO Tag Team title by the end of the month, although that would occur after the titles were stolen from them at the PPV.

PainKiller of the FSW announces the Extreme Legends as the FSW Tag Team champions. They would lose the titles a few days later, but Lexian would win them a second time, this time teamed up with Token Weed. He would later leave FSW.

June 2001
Maples decides that Lexian should enter the singles ranks, and does, after gaining a following with his odd promos involving his now cactus manager, Bobby, and his wild adventures through IWO. Before his much hyped up match against Xavier Hall for the IWO Black World title, he IWO Black was merged with IWO White.

Mike Extreme’s win streak was snapped at 18 straight by Canada’s Hero, ending his attempt to break the record held by Cro. He later left the DWF, cutting short the tag team title run with Lexian.

The DWF would close soon after, citing some business issues to attend to. Lexian would be remembered as the number one ranked wrestler in DWF and also undefeated during his DWF run. During the hiatus, Lexian returned to RWW where he would capture the United States title en route to exacting revenge on Tazz who was still the RWW World champion. At the monthly PPV, Inc. would indeed win the World title from Tazz, who would retire at the end of the match and shook Lexian’s hand.

Lexian’s last DWF promo before the shutdown involved the revelation that he was truly in love with Natalie, but was too scared to propose marriage.

July 2001
Lexian is involved in a fued with IWO superstar, Syphon Fission, en route to a North American title match at the monthly PPV.

In RWW, Legend Inc. would lose the U.S. and World title to Bombtrack, thus culminating in his second loss in RWW. He would retire a few days later.

Joey Malone befriends Lexian and Doozer, and they form the Holy Trinity of Lameness, with special member [name withheld].

At the IWO PPV, Lexian is unsuccessful in his bid to win the North American title, being pinned after hit in the head with a porno film.

August 2001
Lexian signs an initial contract with Legends of Wrestling, and awaits for them to complete preparations on the promotion.

DWF reopens, with an initial PPV to crown a new Dream champion. Dissillusioned with the reopening, Lexian’s attention turns back to Natalie, who is drifting more and more away from Lexian.

At the monthly IWO PPV, Lexian would lose the Mall Brawl main event, but did come up with the Television title by the end of the night.

The new Dream champion was Joey T, a man who had never defeated Legend Inc. in their five meetings.

September 2001
In his first match back from Mall Brawl, Lexian retired Harold Hash in a pot on a pole match.

Lexian has his life threatened when a gunman tries to kill him in a local mall. This brings him and Natalie closer together again.

Later in the month, the DWF management team quit, leaving Lexian the option to try to run the promotion that made him. Although he tried, he would later fold as well, handing the reigns over to Doozer, and retired.

Lexian retired as the DWF United States champion, World tag team champion, and IWO Television champion.

“The Crimson” Thinh Alexander joins the initial roster in REALMwrestling, who knows Tom through the vampire killers. (Source: Roleplays)

December 2001
Thinh Alexander wins his first match in REALM over Henry Escher.

After a meeting with REALM executives, it is decided that he will be elevated in the card, and a feud with Providence begins.

January 2002
REALM wrestling closes.

RWW inducts Legend Inc. into their Hall of Fame.

March 2002
After five months, Legend Inc. comes out of retirement and resigns with PCW after a long deliberation with The Phantom Booker. After winning his opening match against Ganker, much hope and expectation was put on Lexian in the coming weeks.

Lexian eliminated Kid Wonder from the March Madness battle royal, soon after being eliminated himself. He later noted that it was one of the crowning achievements in his PCW career. Most thought this was ridiculous.

April 2002
Lexian debuted a series of promos involving his adventures in LameLand, a new world where animals and objects talked and Jello warriors ruled the lands. It was mostly thought that Lexian had been using heavy narcotics at the time. He also set out to produce several promos including Peter Pan and his arch nemesis Preston Pot.

Legend Inc. would win his first PCW title, the World tag team titles, teaming with Frisco against the Divinity (Akiyama and the Pharoah). The win would give Frisco the distinction of being the first Grand Slam champion in PCW. They would lose the titles to Golden Game a few days later.

In the PPV that month, Lexian would lose to Frisco in a single match, sparking his disappearance from PCW for three months.

June 2002
Lexian would enter his name into the RPG3 tournament, and soon after, resign a contract with PCW.

When Lexian arrived in Los Angeles, he was immediately accused of fathering a child out of wedlock and not caring for the boy known as “Ricky.” Lexian would deny the charges, but under duress, realized that the child was his. He began to care for the child.

Lexian would later win the tournament in the month, defeating Snow, a fellow PCW wrestler, in the finals. Also at the end, he found out that Ricky was not his son, and that he had been set up by his former best friend, Sean O’Reilly and Julia Vanders, in a conspiracy to destroy his career. They were both arrested.

Lexian establishes the “Crusaders of Light,” a rag tag band of heroes with “The Crimson” Thinh Alexander and “Night Shard” Robert Yarney.

At the monthly PCW PPV, Lexian would win the Continental title at Gold and Glory, defeating Xavier Kannon, Miyagawa, and Duke Williams. It would be Lexian’s first singles title in PCW.

July 2002
Duke Williams officially declares war on Lexian, sparking the battle by smashing a glass bottle over Lexian’s head during a taping of Tuesday Turmoil. The next week, Lexian would be attacked by the Dark Age, consisting of DarkStar, Syko the Goblin, and Clyde. They would crucify Robert Yarney at the end of the show.

Lexian meets Captain America and Aquaman, both of them telling him to stay true to his hero status and continue to fight for good, not evil.

Fought the Incredible Hulk in a Denver airport, defeating him with a groin kick. Lexian would later say that it was a cheap shot.

Loses the Continental title to DarkStar after Duke Williams betrayed him in a six man tag match.

“The Crimson” Thinh Alexander
Date Of Birth: October 14, 1974
Height: 5’11’’
Weight: 200 lbs
Age: 27 years
Hometown: Saigon, Vietnam
Current Residence: Piedmont, California

He is a young, yet very strong man. He is proud of his heritage, but because he has so many stereotypes against America, he has chosen to wear a mask to conceal himself in the ring. Even when out of the ring, when his appearance and racial ethnicity are plain to see, he wears sunglasses to hide the distinctive asian eyes that he possesses. He continues to try to find his ultimate self through his experience in and out of the ring, and believes becoming the top of the sport is the key to doing so. His secret is that he is a vampire, but one that has his soul and fights for the good of all humanity.

2) Appearance:
Thinh Alexander is average size, with a rather average build. He has very broad shoulders, with short black hair which is parted to the side. He has a clean shaven face, and usually wears dark black shades when he’s in public. His costume includes a dark red mask with black shades around the eyes, and a very dark red body suit. On his chest is a large letter, “C” and he has black boots and gloves.

3) History:
Thinh Alexander was born on October 14, 1974. He was the son of a United States officer, and a very prominent freedom fighter in the South Vietnamese army. It was their love that brought him life, and it was their lifestyles, that drove them apart. Just a year after he was born, his mother died during rogue bombings by the North Vietnamese army. His father was crushed, but unwilling to allow his son to go on without a mother. He would wind up moving his son from the war riddled country of Vietnam over to Japan, where the United States was slowly gaining influence. It was here that Thinh gained most of his knowledge of the martial arts, developed a love for art and music, and even picked up English, would be important later.

As a child, Thinh stayed mostly to himself, although he had a few close friends. There were many stereotypes about Asians in Japan who were not of Japanese descent. Thinh hid his true identity, instead going by Masato Alexander in order to conceal his Vietnamese heritage and create a Japanese persona for himself. He would rise through the ranks of his school adeptly, graduating from high school with secondary honors and going to college at the University of Tokyo. Although he displayed a love for his studies and an energy to succeed, he still had no idea what he wanted to do as a career.

His father worked as an educator. He was a former solider, with a very limited education himself, so going back to the United states would have meant a very poor future for his son and himself. Although he had a background devoid of any real skills, his ability to read and write English fluently proved an incredible asset for a country that was looking to educate their citizens. He would wind up following Thinh to every school he was enrolled in, even up to the University level where he was a professor of American studies.

Thinh actually attended many schools in his scholastic career. Namely, four different elementary schools, three different high schools, and two different universities before he was handed a bachelors degree. The fault was Thinh’s and Thinh’s alone. He never truly found a school he liked, and was constantly badgering his father to move him to another. His father got tired of the constant moving, but did as his son wished. It was mostly Thinh’s fault, who continually was unsocial with the other children. It began as a problem of language, since all of the school children knew Japanese, while Thinh, naturally, only knew Vietnamese. The language gap distanced Thinh in every elementary school he was in. That bridge was eventually gapped, as Thinh learned Japanese, as well as English from his father. Still, his individualistic nature continued to keep him from making close friends.

His university life was rocky at best. He was strangely attracted to the subjects of philosophy and psychology, and that lead him to believe that it would be the career of his future. An incident in one of his philosophy classes Sophomore year, however, would scar that entire idea. He got into a shouting match with his professor about the idea of a perfect family, and Thinh chastised him for being such a narrow minded man. Thinh was reprimanded for his words and disrespect for his instructor, but not expelled. Thinh would do the University one better, and transfer out to a smaller college. There, he strayed away from the subject matter he used to love, and instead went back to music and the arts. He was a casual clarinet player, and he began to hone his skills in music courses, as well as feeding his appreciation for the arts. He soon decided to be an Arts History major, with a minor in music. He graduated three years later with his bachelors degree, and began working on his doctorate degree at the University of Tokyo once more.

It would be his fathers love for the United States, however, that would bring Thinh and his father back to the United States. His father kept Thinh in Asia for the simple fact that he was afraid that Thinh would not be accepted in a country that had warred so much with people of Asian descent. But his father made no bones about it that he had a lot of family there and wanted to return one day. With American feelings towards Asians improving, and his own age catching up with him, he decided to return to the United States and rejoin his family. He returned to much fanfare, as most of his loved ones thought he was dead or had been lost in action. They were equally surprised by his grown up son, who had an amazing array of knowledge and a great working vocabulary with English. Still, Thinh’s nature kept him distant from his father’s family.

Things slowed down for Thinh, who loved the arts, but was soon growing wary. He had learned the martial arts, albeit very little, and wanted to engage in an activity that would spur on both his physical and mental states. He tried martial arts in the states, but found that it was more about flash and dash instead of becoming one with yourself. It was that this time that he met up with several independent wrestling stars, who had come to learn martial arts to try to spruce up their wrestling careers. They invited Thinh to see some of their shows, and so he did. He did, and he fell entranced by it. The action, the story, the drama…it all appealed to him. It was here that he realized that he wanted to wrestle, to be a wrestler, to hopefully find himself in the ring.

His father was disappointed in his decision, and felt partly responsible for taking his son from his higher education and throwing him into a new situation. But he wound up supporting his son, even finding a school for Thinh to train at. As always, Thinh would wind up joining several different schools and leaving, and by the time he was ready to wrestle professionally, he hadn’t even stayed in one place long enough to graduate. Still, he had enough knowledge to wrestle and to fight. He worked several indy feds, with his father as his manager, until they finally got a call that would change Thinh’s fledgling career. He was called over to Realm Wrestling, where he was being given a chance to tell his story all over again. Thinh signed the contract, and brought with him his heart, his soul, and a mask to hide his true identity. But the truth would be revealed someday.


“Night Shard” Robert Yarney
5’5’’
240 lbs
Richmond, California
Face
Hardcore

Description of Character gimmick or personality:

Robert Yarney is a quiet man who lives a relatively boring life. But what he held back was his extraordinary powers. Those powers were his telekinesis, the ability to move objects with his mind, and his ability to cloak himself at night. He is otherwise a very lonely man and has little confidence, but when he becomes Night Shard and is teamed up with the other Crusaders of Light, his self worth becomes apparent.

Character Appearance:
Robert Yarney is fairly overweight man, with a gut that tends to stick out a bit and a receding hair line. He was black hair with a bowl like bald spot. His costume includes an all black body suit, with a yellow utility belt and a yellow lightning bolt on his chest. His face mask is a black strip of material covering his eyes. He often has a bewildered look on his face, but is known to smile every now and then.

Character History:
Robert Yarney was born in Topeka, Kansas, where he was raised by a farmer father and a homemaker mother. He had two older brothers, both who followed in their father’s footsteps and became farmers themselves. John and Harry Yarney were their names, and they were simply men that longed for the laid back life of farming that they had grown up with. Unfortunately for Robert, he didn't share this passion. What was more unfortunate was the fact that farming was becoming more and more commercialized. Family farms could no longer survive under the iron clad rule of many big companies, so the family eventually became workers for larger farming companies.

The family took this in stride and went along with it all.

Robert had other plans, however. He moved out of Kansas and to New York where he wanted to pursue his acting career some more. New York, unfortunately, was not ready for Robert. In a town where good lucks and actual talent was needed, he didn't have either. Every job he went to, he was turned down. Sometimes before he even got the chance to auditon. His only claim to fame was a kitty litter commercial where he was the "hand" that petted the cat. He was ostracized from the business because his lack of good looks and general acting ability, so he moved to California, too embarrassed to go back to Kansas.
He had an affinity for numbers and became an unassuming accountant for the firm, New Wave Incorporated, an industrial company. He lived a rather simple life, going to work, coming home and eating dinner, and then going to sleep. He never had a way with women, and his only female friends came at night and over the phone. They cost him three bucks a minute. The dark secrets of his life, however, was that he was born with the ability to be a telekinetic, meaning he could move objects with his mind. He also could cloak himself in the night so no one could see him. While these powers were genetic, his other family members could not see a reason to use them at all. They were more inclined to hide their true powers, being seen as normal was more important.

Robert, too, didn't have a use for the powers either. That is, until he met Tom Lexian. Lexian was already known as Legend Inc., but his power was not his own. He obtained super human strength due to an amulet he carried. He was an Executioner, a vampire hunter. One day, Robert was assaulted by a gang known as the Fro's, a kick back seventies gang that wanted to bring back national pride by any means necessary. As he was taken away, Legend Inc. was on one of his patrolling runs when he tried to stop the abduction. He was unsuccessful, and at the same time, Robert found himself in the most uncomfortable of situations.
Someone once told me that the deadliest enemy is an enemy that did not care what your name was. That enemy would hunt you down, destroy your name, and stop at nothing to finally end your life. Of course, I wasn’t too concerned about this. In fact, I wanted people to try and hunt me down. After all, what is more flattering than someone trying to kill you?

Don’t stop till you get enough…

So there I was, hip hoppin’, be boppin’, and generally looking fly as I strolled down the business center of San Francisco. I had no cares, no worries, and sure as hell no conception of what I wanted to be doing. I was feeling like cutting loose, hanging ten, and I didn’t care what people thought.

“Whoo!” I shouted as I ran down the sidewalk. “Daddy wants a new pair of shoes!”

Rows and rows of business men and women walked past me with disdainful looks on their faces. They apparently did not like my brass style and my aggressive sounds, but they were living a boring life where they worked 9 to 5 and didn’t get out much. Hey, I’m all for making money, but not at the expense of your life.

One guy in particular caught my eye. He was about five foot three, with a receding hair line that was dangerously reaching the middle of his head, and spectacles large enough to see out into space. He had a grimace on his face and was walking with a slight limp – the poor guy just got off the BART train most likely. So I did what any other fun loving citizen would do – I decided to cheer him up.

“Ahhh!” He screamed as I jumped directly in his way.

“Ahhh!” I shouted back, grabbing his shoulders.

“Please, please!” He screamed, throwing his wallet at me and allowing bills and bills of money to fall to the ground. “Take my wallet, just don’t kill me!”

I just laughed. “Bro, bro. Calm down, I just wanted to cheer you up. I just want to make you happy!”

Sweat glistened off his face as his complexion began to relax. “You want to make me happy? Then just leave me the hell alone.”

I smirked, enjoying this conversation thoroughly. He was about hip deep into this funk that he didn’t even realize what kind of a fool he was being. He must be used to sitting in his house, staring at the boob tube, and warming up those TV dinners. How sad…how pathetic…

No! I won’t let him do this to himself! I will avenge his loss!

“My good man, you will not suffer this intolerable existence anymore!” I shouted, tossing my finger in the air as if calling for some invisible god that no one could see. “I must save you!”

The man just backed away from me, not wanting to get involved with a maniac like myself. That’s around the time that he just turned around and began speeding down the sidewalk, not allowing me to follow. I chased and I chased, but the little bugger is a lot faster than one would think. I ended the pursuit early, not having the strength to continue.

“Dammit,” I muttered under my breath. “This was my chance to really make a difference.” I took a deep breath and shrugged. “Oh well.”

* * *

Robert Yarney struggled to breathe as he ran down the crowded street. He felt like an elephant stampeding through the proverbial china shop, but that was okay, because he was getting away from that maniac. That crazy as maniac guy with the graying hair and that god awful suit. Who did he think he was trying to tell Robert Yarney, the self proclaimed “Night Shard,” what he could and could not do.

So what if he looked unhappy? Shit, half the world was unhappy. Was that pasty faced pansy going to go to every unhappy man, woman and child and try to cheer them up? He highly doubted it and was glad to be rid of such a nuisance. Now he could go back to his apartment and get on with his oh-so boring life.

“Check yo self, man! Check yo self!”

Robert looked up, only to be shocked at what he saw. A large white man with the largest afro he had ever seen was standing before him and drew a gun to Robert’s face.

“Did you hear me, boy?” The man asked again. “You better watch yo self. Don’t get in a fro’s way, or you might find yo self on the ground crying for yo mama!”

“I’m so so so sorry!” Robert said, putting his arms up in the air. “I didn’t mean to get in your way. Please don’t hurt me!”

The man laughed and kicked Robert in the stomach, who doubled over in pain. His face hit the pavement and he tried not to lift his head up for fear of being hit again.

A black man emerged from the store and looked over the scene. “Gandalf, what in fro’s name are you doing?”

Gandalf, the white man with the fro, shrugged. “I’m just having some fun, Rufus. C’mon, can’t I play with the civilians?”

“Heeeeeeeeeell no,” Rufus replied, slapping Gandalf in the back of the head. “We can’t be havin’ not murda’s now! What would Brotha Dubba say? Would he approve of all of this?”

Gandalf shook his head solemnly. “No sir, he would not. He would say that we have to keep our eyes on the prize. That we need to see the…the…uhm, what did he call it?”

Rufus scratched his chin for a moment. “I believe that he called it, ‘The bigger picture.’”

“Yeah, yeah,” Gandalf replied. “The bigger picture.”

“That’s good,” Rufus said. “At least you are learning. Now, see yourself to the Pimp-Mobile and get the ride going. I’ll take care of this scum.”

Gandalf saluted Rufus with his afro pick and ran over to a broken down Camaro which was parked off to the side. Rufus looked over at Robert, who was still sweating and crying, and looked like a complete baby. Rufus hated men that did act like one. He thought they were too weak to be any good to society, and they had to be dealt with.

“So, my good man, what is your name?” Rufus asked with a charming smile.

The smile somewhat comforted Robert, who’s anxiety eased with Rufus’ kindness. “Robert, Mr. Fro, sir. Robert Yarney.”

“My man, Mr. Yarney,” Rufus began to say. “Do you know who I am? Of course you don’t, because the world has not been formerly introduced to us yet. My friend, you are living in a new generation. A generation that will see the formation of the Fro Alliance.”

Robert stared at Rufus intently, not sure if he was joking or being serious.

Please god, let him be joking…Robert thought to himself.

“The Fro Alliance stands for all that is good and right,” Rufus continued. “Now, I know what you’re thinking. You’re thinking that we are nothing but a bunch of no good rascals that just want to take over the world. Let me reassure you that you are wrong.”

Robert breathed a sigh of relief. “Oh good. So you’re not trying to take over the world…”

“No, we are trying to take over the world, but that’s not our only objective.”

“….” Robert wanted to cry. He just wanted to roll over and begin weeping. Why was this maniac telling him about his sinister plans? With this information, they would have to either kill him or bring him along for the ride.

“But we want to change the world for good,” Rufus said, seeing Robert’s apprehension. “This world has been overrun with wannabee men. You know, the kind that pretend that they are masculine but truly they are run by none other than the women that WE should be ruling.”

“But…women are good…” Robert answered back hesitantly.

Rufus slapped Robert, who yelped out in pain. “THAT WAS A BITCH SLAP! NOW DON’T SPEAK UNLESS SPOKEN TO!”

Robert just huddled down into a small ball.

“Women must be held in check, my friend. And the Fro Alliance will do that, once we’ve convinced the entire population that it’s the right thing to do!”

“But…what are you going to do with me?” Robert asked, still unaware of the Camaro pulling up to the curb.

“You’re coming with us, of course,” Rufus said. He took Robert’s hand lovingly. “You’ll either join us, or be tossed aside with the other weaklings. Your choice, of course, but be warned, you don’t want to be tossed aside.”

Robert gulped.

“Let him go!” A voice shouted out.

Rufus turned around and glared menacingly. “Who’s there?”

Suddenly, a man with a shotgun appeared in the distance. “Legend Inc., your worst nightmare.”

Rufus looked at Robert with a confused look. “Who?”

* * *

It’s hard not feeling like a bad ass when you’re waving a shotgun at someone, but hell, we all have to have our moments sometime. I guess my moment was right now.

“You heard me,” I replied to the large fro’d black man. “Legend Inc. is my name, and working for the good people of Frisco is my game. Now, leave that man or do I have to get violent?”

“Brotha, you must be out of your mind,” Rufus replied with a sinister grin. “You do realize that I have twenty of my men surrounding you right now as we speak, don’t you?”

I looked around myself, but I still didn’t see any men with aspiring afros to speak of. “You’re bluffing, man. And I’m not a man to be bluffed with.”

“It’s your choice,” Rufus said with a smile. Just then, the Camaro dashed in front of me, blocking Robert from myself. I tried to get around the car, but Rufus had already thrown him into the car, and it sped away in a cloud of smoke. I was left confused and scared. A large group of people had already begun to amass in the area and were asking too many questions. I hid the gun underneath my coat and ran down the street. I needed backup, and I knew just who to ask.

Robert would eventually be rescued by the tandem of Legend Inc. and "The Crimson" Thinh Alexander, and would discover the courage to use his own powers to help his own rescue. Lexian and Alexander were both incredibly impressed by this turn of events, and took it upon themselves to try and help train Robert in using his powers. His ability to move objects with his mind was unbelievable, and his cloaking powers during the night gave him an advantage against many enemies. He would eventually take the moniker of "Night Shard" from one of his favorite comic book character.

The three men would form the "Crusaders of Light," who would fight injustice and crime wherever they were. It was this world that they would save - it was the life they knew now.
