Biography

The woman now known simply as Geneva spent her entire childhood growing up in the small section of New York City known as College Point. Geneva’s real name is Cynthia Kelley and while growing up she attended St. Johns Lutheran School where the notion of religion was constantly stressed throughout all of their studies. It was perhaps during this time that her mental issues had begun and after reading the bible from start to finish she began to think that she was indeed the person destined to rise and bring peace to the world before violence reigned. It was her own undoing that she could not keep these notions to herself and the teachers at the school brought it to the attention of her parents who immediately tried to take action against it. It was an action that they would live to regret for all their lives.

What Thomas and Angela Kelley thought would be regular doctor sessions for their daughter turned into something that had taken her out of their life. The doctors had decided that Cynthia was not fit to live her life in a public manner and it would be best that she become institutionalized And then it was off to the loony bin for the young Cynthia Kelley where she spent a good deal of her teenage years until she was finally released at the age of sixteen. Nothing about her had really changed though aside from her appearance and the fact that she learned to tell people exactly what it was they wanted to hear. It was through those means that Cynthia had finally managed to earn her release from the hospital and returned to what had been her home but nothing there would ever be the same again.

Cynthia spent all of her time along even when in school. She was not one of the popular girls in high school and kept to herself and out of trouble as much as she could but all the time she was constantly planning. Planning for the opportunity that she knew would one day come to her. It was then that she had met Marcus Escobar. He was a young Puerto Rican teenager that lived two houses down from where the Kelley’s resided in College Point and much like Cynthia he had kept to himself a lot during his younger years but now he had started to come out of the shell he had erected. When he first saw Cynthia he knew that there was something about her and that something had constantly pulled at him.

After months of trying he had finally gotten close enough to her where they begun to become what many would consider friends. Several years later the two were closer than any could have thought and Cynthia even went so far to tell him the truth about her, at least what she conceived to be the truth. To her own shock Marcus did not seem at all repulsed or doubtful of the notion. He had instead pledged his undying loyalty to her and the cause which they believed in. Marcus was only the first of many that Cynthia now calling herself Geneva would convert to her cause and become members of The Faithless. They were all expendable pawns that she would use in her holy quest to save the world.

Now was the time for The Faithless to rise!
