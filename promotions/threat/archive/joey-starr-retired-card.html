Biography

Joey Starr was born in San Diego CAlifornia into a very rich family.His mother was a lawyer and his father was a professional Wrestler.Even though his father was not in the major Wrestling Promotions he was a great wrestler.Joey want to grow up to be just like his father.Then when Joey was about twelve years old his father was killed in a car crash on the way home from a wrestling match.Joey was devastated and prmoised himself that he would grow up to be a wrestler to keep his fathers past in the family.Joey trained as a amatuer wrestler at his San Diego high school all four years and then after winning them a championship his Senior year he just left.Joey got to high headed and dropped out of school his senior year to go to a wrestling school.Joey went to the same school his dad did and went on to wrestle in a very small indy fed called I.W.A. Joey dominated this INDY FED becoming the world champ in only months untill the fed ran out of money and shut down.Joey got a very good deal from a high ranked fed called FINEST and joined up but after the second show Joey Starr won the lightweight title and then the fed ended the same way as the first.

Joey Starr then took some time off for about six months to look for a place he thought would stay.He came across P.i.W,It was said to be one of the best in the country.Joey Starr called up Johnny Cusiamo(Sorry about spelling) the owner of the P.I.W and asked him if he needed anyone.Joey was soon on Telivised provesional Wrestling shows.He had the big time ,can't be beaten ,I am better than you, HAHA ,gimmick.Joey would even hit his fans as he was talking on opponents in Hardcore battles.Joey ended up fueding with a man CHRIS MEZZ.This man claimed to be HArdcore and after Joey won the title Mezz ended up beatin ghim for it after the Record setting Title Reign.Joey and Mezz fought many battles including two over the name King of Hardcore.Joey lost the first match along with his title but then won and was Dubbed King of Hardcore.

Right after this match Joey got himself injurd trying to use his finishing manuver and was out for about a month.There was some P.I.W Internall probmlems and as Joey started to get off the shelf the worste happened.The P.I.W shut down.Joey was astonished and now the P.I.W has brought him out of hiding but only time will tell what is going to happen.

Upon the return of PIW Joey was on winning streak.The fans loved his Face turn and was eating it alive.The thing was Joey had an enemy.Not just anyone though,he had the worste enemy that being the owner of PIW Jim Johnson.Johnson was putting Joey in every kind of match imaginable and he faced top starts as Overdose and LLB.Even winning the World Title from the reigning champion LLB wasnt enough for Johnson eventually LLB won it back after getting a rematch courtset of Johnson.

Then came another Chapter.Over the course of PIW Joey had developed a relationship with the sister of LLB Tammy.Not only that he had also become good friends with anotehr wrestler Sean Cylear a man who Joey respected more than anyone.At a PIW pay per view Johnson has said that Sean Cylear would have to retire after the United States Title match againts the Starr.Joey cames out the winner and the United States Champion but Cylear was to leave only a month later to come back as a heel and align himself once again with the disapointing Team Johnson.They somehow also lured Tammy into the crew too but who knows what they will do next.

Another man Joey has had a few matches with is Reno Davis.Although Davis along with his monkey ZaC has yet to beat the Starr he is a man that has the Starr's respect.There might be somethign in the air with these two guts around but more than less Reno's problems will hold the respect back.

Soon after the huge battle with Reno David Joey Starr went into a state of depression only to return to PIW with a huge World Title Run that lasted a long four days. Joey also captures the US Title before the DEMISE of PIW where it feel because of business reasons.

Joey has searched for a promotion to call his own since the fall of PIW and after a few small run-ins with some smaller organizations Joey thinks thReat might be the place for him. God knows everyone else hopes for the best when it comes to the Starr. But that is what happens when you are a STARR!
