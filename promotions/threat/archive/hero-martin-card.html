Hero Martin

Real Name: Hero Martin Nickname: "the Prodigy" Also known as: the Infamous Hero, the Unrelenting Tyro

Born: Thursday, February 18th, the year Nineteen Hundred and Eighty Place of Birth: Green Bay, Wisconsin Hometown: St. Louis, Missouri

Height: 6'5 Weight: 295 lbs. Blood type: O- Eye color: Blue

Trainer: Adam Wright

Theme song: The instrumental version of "Assfault" as performed by Dry Kill Logic

Style

Competing style: Hardcore Brawler

Signature moves:
The Transfixer A vicous spear aimed at the clueless victim's lower back.
The Spirit of St. Louis Hero picks up his opponent so that they are laying perpendicular to him in a fallaway slam position except they are facing away from him rather than towards him. He then falls backwards as usual throwing them backwards over his head.
The Heroic Transgression Hero grasps his opponent's neck in his huge right hand. He then hoists them high into the air, before slamming them back first as he comes down into a seated position.
The Killswitch Hero locks his opponent in a front face lock. He then reaches under the victim and grabs their opposite arm. From there, he swiftly lifts them from the ground, before he pulls the victim's arm, causing him to rotate 180 degrees. He then drops the opponent in a reverse DDT, all in one quick motion.
The Malicious Intent Hero positions himself behind the opponent and several feet in front of the nearest corner. He then wraps his massive arms around the victim's waste. Next he squats down then sends his opponent flying over his head in a beautiful German suplex into the corner, usually causing the back of the victim's head to crash into the middle turnbuckle with severe force.

Finisher: Hero moves to the side of his opponent where he then places their arm around his neck. He then clasps their wrist in his right (or left) hand. With his free arm he then hooks the opponent's closest leg. Next he sqats down, then completes the move by quickly standing up then falling backwards allowing his opponent to land head/neck first into the mat.
Finisher name: The Butterfly Effect

Historical Info

Other/Previous promotions: wG
