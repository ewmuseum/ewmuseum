The Sodding Truth [Issue 003]
July 13 2003

Hello cunts,

Did you lot really think that the most influential personality in this company wouldn't be present at only the second PPV since the renaissance? Ignorant folk. Of course I would be, and bloody hell, I pulled off one hell of a surprise, didn't I? I felt quite proud at the little stunt Anubis and myself pulled off. Seriously though, why the bleeding hell did you pillocks think that the PPV got pushed forward a day? No, not because of American Idol. It was your favourite British Degenerate, hard at work again.

And you lot have to admit, it was my sheer presence that made the show the mild success that it was. I mean, there were 16 matches in total, and only one of them stood out from the rest. Myself, under the guise of Anubis, taking on Levond Daniels. That was the only bloody match worth watching. Pathetic booking by Troy Mason & Moe Sole & the rest of the Board Of Directors, to be honest. I would have thought that the BOD were a group of educated and brilliant men, after they entrusted me with ownership powers many moons ago. But ever since they stripped me of that power, they have proven to be nonsensical and idiotic.

Now, as I usually do for that other promotion I'm employed with, I shall do a recap of the Payback PPV.

---

Exhibition Fatal 4-Way Opening Match
Bloody boring shite. Four talentless mongrels running around without a bloody clue at all. Ended up as a draw or some other, I believe. Not to worry, you didn't miss much if you failed to catch this match.

Triple-Threat Match: Fire vs Twizzy vs Vertigo
A twat, a drug-obsessed arselicker, and a cunt. Made for a very interesting match. And by interesting, I meant bloody boring. More entertainting than the first match, however, and I was a wee bit surprised to see Fire not pick up the victory. The boy's got a knack of sneaking in victories when people least expect them. This time around, the drug-obsessed arselicker got the win. At least VER-TI-HOE didn't win, which was a massive saving grace.

Dungeon Match: ORB vs Steven Fury
I was in the lavatory during this bout, so I don't know a bleeding thing about it. Sources told me it sucked bollocks. I'll agree with them.

Exhibition Match: Rent-A-Hero/Spiderman vs Jere Casarella
Spiderman versus The Tormented Angel. Bloody excellent match, I have to say. Spiderman isn't really the greatest personality alive, but the lad's got great agility. The Tormented Angel, considering he's a bleeding newcomer to the 'wrestling industry', churned out a variety of impressive manouevres. The bloke's got skill, and I actually enjoy the fact that he's on Rio Nexan's case. I felt a tinge of sadness when Spiderman won, but nonetheless, an exciting match. Made me almost happy that I'm a part of thReat. Almost.

Special Challenge Match: Drake Thanas vs Chris McCluskey
McCluskey is a very interesting bloke, and the fact that he's not an American makes me like him. However, he's from Northern Ireland, so I'm inclined to think he's full of shite sometimes. No matter, he destroyed this Thanas bugger and even won in controversial manner. Not surprising, the Belfast boys always like to win illegaly. But maybe I'll ring Chris up, and talk business over a couple of pints of ale.

Exhibition Match: Ampske vs Rio Nexan
Hmph, Ampske. HMPH, Rio Nexan. Nothing much to say, except that it was a rather interesting contest. I would have expected Mr Sociopathic Arselicker to have won with his apparent superior skills, but Ampske put up a fight. One of the reasons why Ampske was part of the Vagabonds project. Oh well, I liked the ending loads, where The Tormented Angel came out.

Grudge Match: Jason Semps vs Kyle Knowen
BORING. I was on the phone during this match. Sources told me it was more horrendous than Ricky Martin's first concert. I'll agree with them.

Tag Team Titles Rumble Explosion
BORING. At least Born To Die won, so I'm happy over that. The other teams involved in the match are sodding cunts to the two-thousandth degree. None of them are even worthy of being in the same ring as Born To Die, simple as that.

High Stakes Match: Levond Daniels vs Anubis
Oh yes, this was an EXCELLENT match. Truly unparalleled. Which other match had so much emotion, and a major surprise to boot? NONE! Levond, you're one person with a very big mouth. I had told you time and time again, YOU ARE A NOBODY. A cunt like you will never amount to anything. You thought by coming to thReat, after getting a whiff of information, that you'd be able to get your precious Jessica back? Why do you think I signed Anubis in the first place? For fun? To mess with you, cunt. Let me tell you, Anubis knew you were getting close, and needed to find a way to be able to neutralise you. I thought the entire situation was very interesting, so I signed him to thReat, and MADE SURE that word got around. You and your gullible nature fell into the nicely-laid trap, and it all culminated last Saturday. Ah, brilliant stuff. Have fun NOT being able to use your legs, cunt.

Street Fight: DeVante Stevenson vs blind
I'm surprised that DeVante lost this. I was considering taking him under my wing, but what a disgrace he's turned out to be, losing to some blind fecker. Not the most interesting of FIGHTS I've ever seen. I might just consider the possibily of checking further into BLIND's history. Seems to be a interesting chap, with a unique arsenal of moves.

Grudge Match: General David Cuntness vs Candor
Another highly disappointing display. General Cuntness, the rash git, somehow managed to triumph over the eloquent and lethal Candor. I'm deeply saddened by the result of this match, but on the other hand, this fits perfectly into my little plan.

No Holds Barred Match: Rejection vs Dane Manetta
I'd fallen asleep during this match, but I did hear that Rejection won after absolutely smothering Dane with his brilliance. Quite coincidental, Manetta's form gone downhill since he was defeated by YOURS TRULY way back in April. Poor arse.

2-On-1 Handicapped Match: Jay Jaxxen & Stephen Lee vs Ernie French
I was still asleep during this match, but I'm glad that Ernie French actually won. Bloody hell, Jaxxen & Stephen are disgraces to all the 'heels' out there -- the bad guys in this little ongoing soap opera called the wrestling/fighting industry. They've got no charisma, no skill, nothing. I wonder who was the genius who signed them to contracts. Right, Troy Mason. Bravo, Ernie, for ensuring we won't be treated to anything more from those two pillocks.

No Holds Barred Cruiserweight Title Match: Tyler Davidson vs Phil Atken [c]
NO! Atken lost. BLOODY HELL! Not that I'm quite against Davidson winning, but Atken's a bloody smashing character. I should really meet up with the bugger and have discussions on how big a cunt Troy Mason is. Oh well, as long as nobody in Troy's good books has the title, I'm perfectly happy. Solid contest, by the way.

Aggression Title Match: Wolf vs Steve Solex [c]
Hmph, Wolf won. Not the greatest fight in the world, but it certainly was a blast to see the crowd spew abuse at Wolf in the manner that they did. Made for an entertaining lead-up to the final match of the event. Not too thrilled that we have a new champion, the bleedin' Aggression Title's being passed about too easily.

World Title Match: General Cuntness vs Xaxon
Why wasn't I surprised that that lardarse, General Cuntness, was chosen to battle Xaxon? And did you all see the way he ran down to the ring? Like a blue-arsed fly on the verge of emptying his bowels! BWAHAHA! Not a bad match, made superb by the atmosphere and the solitiary fact that I was giving colour commentary. Troy Mason looked worried a ton of times, but truth be told, I knew that Xaxon was going to lose. Hence, our little association has ended. The only reason the two of us had any kind of partnership was for him to gain the World Title. He hasn't done so, and that's the end of the story.

---

So, there you go, laddies and lasses. As I've stated before, a rather decent event, proving why my interest in this company runs so bloody deep. Jon Hackett laid the foundation for a fantastic opportunity for me, and because of that, I'm going to make sure I make full use of it. Some of the highlights include;

* Rio Nexan apparently getting killed. That's bloody fabulous!
* Troy Mason and his desperation to resort to General Sanders. Makes things so much easier for me.
* Levond Daniels taking the piss. Ah, that was bloody wonderful.

This PPV signalled the end of my self-imposed exile from thReat. I'm finally back, and more determined than ever to make Troy Mason's life a living hell. Troy, having disposed of The Vagabonds at Malevolence doesn't mean that you've gotten completely rid of me. And now, I'm ready to wage another war against you. No matter what, thReat WILL become mine.

Be prepared for a rough ride, Troy Mason.

-VP
