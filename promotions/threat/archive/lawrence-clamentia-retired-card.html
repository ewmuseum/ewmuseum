Biography

Lawrence Clamentia came into this world as a saint, for he committed no sins, he was in a family built of tyrants and warriors, he succumbed much pain. . .but at what cost? There were no costs, as he loved his father dearly enough, that he'd even give his life to them.

His father was his heart, his soul, what he wasn't and wanted to be, that was his father. He was at the age of twelve years old, as he was sent to a military training camp by himself, no more father by his dear side. The man who ran the camp was his father's dearest friend.(Place name there.) He was to go to war for his lord, through the ranks of his father's greatest man, for his mere loyalty to the man he loves.

When he is at the camp, he learns the tactics, but he realizes that for the first time, he is learning things without his father by his side, a smile came across his face, only to be wiped away from a slap by his father's best man. For there were to be no smiles lent around at a time of this seriousness.

Betrayal, pure resentment came out of Lawrence Clamentia, for the first time in his life he'd ever been hit. . .and for the first time in his life he really began to thing about the possibilities of things. Maybe his father wasn't the loving man he really was. Maybe he was exactly what his best man was, an angry tyrant, willing to do whatever it took to win a war?

Maybe his love was just a hoax for the future that was to come? Lawrence Clamentia was thinking about what could be. . .what exactly his father was. Maybe what he wasn't in his look, his best man intended to be for him. But deep inside, the hate sufficed, in the deepest sanctum of his body.

That night, Lawrence Clamentia fell asleep, and he dreamt a dream which included his father. It ended with the scene of his father walking out of his vast manor, meeting up with a group of five well-dressed men. The prophecy ended at that weird note, when he woke up, he saw the barracks that he slept in, empty. For he was the only one left. He knew something strange must have happened.

He walked out of the barracks to find a soldier standing by the entrance, watching guard. Lawrence asked why he wasn't told to come along. The soldier on watch told him what happened to his father, and that he had been last seen with a group of five men. After that he'd been found with many bullets, there was nothing left of him.
