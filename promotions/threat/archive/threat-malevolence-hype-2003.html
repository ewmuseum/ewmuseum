Malevolence: The Event 11 Months In The Making

circa May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The Event 11 Months In The Making Malevolence was an event that was supposed to transpire towards the end of June 2002. It was set to be an extravaganza that would decide the fate of the company. And when Troy Mason brought it back from the dead in early April 2003, never could he have expected for the madness that was the Fighters/Wrestlers warfare to return as well. Now, on May 25 2003, matters will finally be resolved. The fate of thReat will be decided, once and for all. The setting for this mammoth event? The Philips Arena in Atlanta, Georgia. An expected crowd of 20,000 people is what will await Troy Mason and crew... with tickets having sold out five hours after they were made available for sale. For those who didn't get the tickets, you can always watch the show at home, on pay-per-view. The show kicks off at 7 PM EST, and is also available to our UK fans on Sky Box Office. This event is also headlined by a theme song that will now be customary for all thReat extravaganzas. And for Malevolence, to fit in with the theme of the show, "Destroy The Opposition" by Dying Fetus is what will set the event towards its climax. The highly-anticipated Wrestlers/Fighters Showdown, which will determine who wins the war at long last. Troy Mason, living the dream of Jon Hackett... or Vincent Pembridge, the Scorpion Of Manchester. Wrestlers/Fighters Showdown The first run of thReat, while not super, left people with many memories. Towards the end of that ill-fated run, a war between the Fighters & the Wrestlers broke out. The owner at the time, Jon Hackett, regretted his decision to bring something new to the world -- a hybrid entity, combining both fighting & wrestling. So, he wanted it to end. The Malevolence PPV at the end of June 2002 was supposed to do that. BUT, Jon Hackett fled the scene, as thReat closed, in the midst of its climb up the ladder. One person at the center of the Wrestlers/Fighters Showdown was Vincent Pembridge, known as Jakks back in the day. He & Hackett were never on good terms, with the Brit's endless requests and Jon thinking that Vincent wasn't good enough to contest for the Fighting Title, after numerous chances to do so had been blown. However, the tricky bit is that Hackett knew the Fighters would win, and was beginning to feel more regretful. Truth be told, he had grown weary of the fighting and wanted thReat to be just pure wrestling. Vincent then went to Hackett, and offered to take a dive in the Wrestlers/Fighters Showdown -- if and only if Jon Hackett did a couple of things in return. The deal was agreed upon, but The Scorpion of Manchester was left with nothing as thReat closed. In April 2003, thReat returned, as a pure wrestling company. Troy Mason, a friend of Hackett's, got all the paperwork and the capital and the enthusiasm in order... and decided to revive thReat. On the first show back, Vincent Pembridge also reappeared. Claiming that the chance for power had been washed down the drain by Jon Hackett... and that he was aiming to finish up unresolved business. The Fighters were returning, and the Showdown that never happened WOULD happen. Over the course of the last few weeks, Vincent Pembridge & Troy Mason have been engaged in an absolutely bitter war. Slowly, Vincent's built his army, called The Vagabonds. In the meantime, Troy's struggled to find able-bodied men to form The Resistance. It's gone back and forth numerous times, Troy's power being equalled by the number of surprises The British Degenerate has managed to pull out of his hat. In this time, Pembridge has managed to turn thReat back into a hybrid organisation, and also had a couple of days in the hotseat, with the power to do whatever he wanted. Of course, Mason managed to retrieve his ownership status, but the damage was done by Vince already. Now, the stage is set. FIVE ON FIVE, Wrestlers/Fighters Showdown. The Vagabonds vs The Resistance. This Sunday, at Malevolence. Vagabonds win, Vincent P and crew take over thReat, which will become a true fighting promotion. Resistance win, thReat return to a pure wrestling status, the Fighters will be binded to the company, and forced to wrestle. The ultimate business decision, and the true fate of thReat... will be decided this Sunday. J. Leslie Voss vs General David Sanders J. Leslie Voss has gotten inside Sanders head, ever since week one of thReat's new revival. There was an immediate tension between the two competitors since their arrival. Everyone sensed it. With all the mind games that Voss used, the General answered back a fraction of the times. Now, Voss has gotten in the General's head and broken his core. He also took a part of the Sanders that only some may understand. He took his pride, his glory... his silver medals. Sanders now looks to prove to Voss and the whole world that he is a worthy competitor in thReat; one who ought to be respected. As for Voss, he seeks to show Troy Mason that he's deserving of a top-tier position within the company. This encounter, which will no doubt be heated, can be summed up in a couple of words. Two men, with seperate goals, on the same playing field! 30-Minute Iron Man Street Fight Wolfgang Kämpfer and Steven Solex had never crossed paths before the re-emergence of thReat. After being paired to fight each other on thReat's inaugural show, Flaw 13, they got to know each other... and apparently the hatred has been growing ever since. After Wolf beat Solex 2 weeks in a row, Solex sought revenge, through a backstage attack, and continued to aggravate the German superstar, costing him two successive matches. Now, Wolf has had enough, and challenged Solex to a Iron Man Match. Solex altered the stipulations to a 30 minute Iron man street fight, and so the battle is on. The odds are stacked in Solex's favour, with Wolf out of his territory, but there is nothing more dangerous than a man with his back to the wall. This battle will test both men to the limits, but will it end their rivalry... or just take it to the next level? -unknown- vs Malik Roland No one knows who he is or where he came from. All they know is that he given himself a mission, and the first step towards completion is getting rid of his first target -- Malik Roland. Over the past few weeks, all this man has done is drive Malik over the edge, attacking him for no justified reason and toying with him when he seeked revenge. This Sunday, Malik Roland hopes to put an end to this man's mission before it starts, and rid thReat of his presence before he gets to comfortable. At Malevolence, these two will go head to head. One for respect. One for God-knows-what. It's set to be an intriguing battle.
