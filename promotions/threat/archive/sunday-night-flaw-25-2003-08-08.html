A Nazi Kidnapping

Screeching… Screaming… Roaring…

Minutes before Flaw 25 began two cars sped, one behind the other, in the direction of the arena. A shining black ford mustang mach 1 gave chase to a red Mazda rx8, two beautiful cars, both vehicles cut through the road, skidding around. The Ford, which was being driven by Wolf, was attempting to avoid the capture from the driver of the Mazda, that being Ernie French.

Wolf’s car spun in to the garage and he slammed his breaks down hard, making the Ford skid a few meters before coming to a halt. Ernie’s car followed a moment later, flying past the car of Wolf and spinning around to a stop. Both cars faced one another and both engines cut out at the same time.

The doors of both cars clicked and swung open at the same time and both of the large thReat superstars stepped from their vehicles and slammed the door shut. Ernie and Wolf paced to the centre of the cars, slamming their faces in to one another, glaring in to one another’s eyes. An intense stare from each man continued for a few moments until Wolf took a step backwards.

The cream brown suits of the nazi regime flickered in the corner of Ernie’s eye, and as he turned to face one of the four men, donned in old Nazi uniforms, a man from behind struck him across the skull with a baseball bat. Ernie collapsed to the floor and all four men mauled him, kicking and punching his unconscious body for a few moments.

“Stop!” Wolf exclaimed. The four men fell back in to a straight line, standing to attention perfectly.

“In the van.” Wolf demanded.

One of the four men ran to the black van parked a few feet inside the car park and opened the door, he removed some duck tape and some rope and tossed it across towards the other men who were guarding Ernie French’s downed body. The men grabbed the rope, two of them bounding Ernie’s arms behind his back and tying his legs together whilst the third man used the duck tape to cover his eyes and mouth. The two men with the rope finished up tying him and picked him up, one man to each arm, and dragged him to the van, loading him in to the back. All four of the Nazi’s climbed in to the rear of the van and slammed the door shut.

Wolf ran over to the van and jumped in the drivers side of the vehicle before turning on the engine, putting it in drive and zooming back out of the arena…

Ernie French had been kidnapped.
