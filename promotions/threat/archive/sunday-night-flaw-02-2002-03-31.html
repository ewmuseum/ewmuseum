03/31/02 Live from the Kiel Center in St. Louis

Introduction

St. Louis, Missouri. The host of Sunday Night Flaw, the second show in thReat's two week exsistence. After last week a few thing were up in the air, some left to be solved and some to be continued. Four matches, booked for the night was sure to be a thriller. The fans at home and in the Keil Center sat down, ready for thReat action.

And it Begins

His music hit. Brandon Blade's. He came from the back with his hands raised in the air, boos being poured on him like a rainy day. He walked down the ramp, flicking a couple fans out as one tried to spit on him. He stepped him and grabbed a microphone. He stood in the center of the ring waiting for the boos to stop. He realised they weren't stopping anytime soon, so he began to speak.

"Shut up."

The crowd booed louder.

"Right. Okay. Last week I showed each and everyone of you what I was capable of doing, beating the little kid you adore to a pulp. I threw him around like a ragdoll. You see Lennox, you made the first mistake. You never said 'Excuse Me.' You wouldn't have suffered the pain of wrestling with me if you would've just been polite. Didn't your mother teach you to use manners?"

The crowd began to chant "Lennox! Lennox! Lennox!

"So you took the intiative Lennox. You set up a match. What a foolish fuck. Did you actually thinky ou could defeat a man of my stature? Heh. Len, I want you to come down here right now, and say to my face, that you can beat me. Come on, hit his damn music."

"21st Century" by Bad Religion played and Lennox Dawson descended the rampway, and soaked in the ovation from the crowd. He got in the ring and snatched the microphone away from Brandon Blade. The crowd instigated throughly by 'OOOOing'.

He swiped his vibrant blonde hair from his face and behind his ear.

"Heh, you're serious? Threw me around like a rag doll...well, I'm a man of little words."

"SMACK!"

Lennox clocked him right in the jaw and knocked him flat on his back and started pummeling him. Lennox ceased the renlentless assult and straddled him and got in his face.

"I can--no I WILL defeat you..."

Vicious Circle VS Riot and Avo Chavez

"The Demented Demons" began to play as Vicious Circle made their way to ringside. As soon as they stepped between the ropes,"Whole" by Flaw hit, and the crowd began to boo. Riot made his way out, calmly, and walked slowly down the ramp. Just as he hit the bottom, Avo Chavez's theme began to play.

But nothing happened.

It played for about a minute, and still, nothing happened. As the music faded out, Vicious Circle left the ring and tried to trap Riot. As Bavin made his way around the right side, Riot sprang into action, leveling Chane with a stiff western lariat. Bavin attacked Riot from behind, and managed to get in a quick back body drop. Riot's head bounced off the floor.

Chane got up, and helped Bavin roll Riot into the ring. Once there, they proceeded to stomp him, until the referee forced Chane onto the apron. Bavin lifted Riot to his feet, whipped him into the ropes, and dropped him with a near-perfect spine buster.

Riot stood up.

He grabbed Bavin's head, and repeatedly punched him in the head and back. When he let go, he kicked Bavin in the stomach and set him up for a powerbomb, but Chane ran in and quickly stopped him with a flying forearm. Riot stumbles back just a little, but it's enough to let Bavin grab him and deliver a powerbomb of his own.

Bavin posed a little. And while he wasn't looking, Riot stood up behind him. Riot ran up, grabbed Bavin around the waist, lifted him as high as he could, and just as he got to the top of the arc, he released him in a German Suplex. Bavin crumpled up on the other side of the ring.

Chane tagged himself in, and ran towards Riot. Riot just stood there.

Chane went for a clothesline, but Riot ducked, turned him around, and lifted him up for a backdrop. But instead of dropping him, he walked over to the ropes and threw him onto the floor below. Bavin got slowly to his feet, ran over, and gave Riot a running low blow. Riot fell to his knees.

Bavin grabbed him by the hair and began tattooing his face with punches. He then kneed Riot in the face several times. He rolled him out of the ring.

Bavin shook Chane awake, and both men began working over Riot. They would take turns kicking him in the ribs and face. Chane then stood Riot up, kicked him in the stomach, and gave him a cross powerbomb on the floor.

Riot stopped moving.

Bavin grabbed him to his feet and threw him into the ring to get the win. Chane calmly walked over and laid down on Riot.

1...

2....

And Riot kicked out.

Chane, in disbelief, argued with the referee over the count, which gave Riot enough time to get to his feet.

He'd gone berserk.

Chane turned around, and Riot leveled him with a hard right to the jaw. Bavin charged in, and Riot powers lamed him onto his partner. Riot screamed out, and grabbed Bavin to his feet. Riot climbed up to the top rope, and brought Bavin with him. He put him into a powerbomb position, but he didn't drop him into the ring.

He powerbombed him to the outside of the ring.

A "Holy Shit" chant started throughout the arena.

Chane, seeing what just happened to his partner, run out of the ring and towards Riot. Riot gave him a til-a-whirl slam for his trouble.

Riot rolls back into the ring, and a few moments later is followed by Chane.

Riot runs right towards Chane again, but this time Chane side steps him, and Riot runs right into the ring post. Chane drops a few quick elbow's on Riots back to keep him down for a moment. He pulls Riot up by his hair and slams his face into the ring post a few times. Riot stands up, but he's groggy. Seeing an opportunity, Chane hits Riot with a devastating heart punch.

Riot just falls backwards.

In the meantime, Bavin has started to get to his feet, but he's in no shape to fight. He somehow managed to get into the ring, where Chane helped him up. He looked down at Riot, looked towards his partner, then called for their finisher:

Torture.

Bavin dragged Riot to the center of the ring, and quickly applied a Texas Cloverleaf. Chane stepped in, and applied a Dragon Sleeper. Riot screamed out in pain, but he refused to tap out. Finally, after what seemed like an eternity, he tapped out. Chane let go of the Dragon Sleeper, and Bavin fell out of the Texas Cloverleaf, barely able to keep himself up.

Once more, "My Demented Demons" begins to play as Vicious Circle walk to the back, victorious.

Just Wondering...

"Mr Jon Hackett, sir?" Jakks asks as he raps his unusually and extremely white knuckles against the oak door which is the gateway to Jon Hackett's office.

Jon Hackett. The esteemed chairman of thReat.

"Come in, Jakks. And you don't have to call me sir. What are you; a Brit?"

"As a matter of fact, I am."

The look of mild shame is evident on Hackett's face, and he quickly attempts to make the awkward moment of silence pass, by offering Jakks to sit down on the black couch in the corner of his office.

"No thanks, this is a rather simple request; should be done with in a minute or so," Jakks replies whilst declining the offer.

The former OFG superstar; wearing a dark blue t-shirt with the word FEAR printed on the fear in small lettering, black denim jeans and black boots; then sighs loudly, before he looks away from Jon.

"I.. um.. I want.. um.. I was just wondering if you could.. um.." Jakks says slowly, struggling to finish his sentence.

Jon eyeballs Jakks suspiciously, and with the former UFF Ultimate Heavyweight Champ flailing his arms about.. looking for the right words to say, Jon sighs in exasperation. One can't blame a man of his stature for being impatient.

"If I could what?"

"What?"

"What?"

"What?"

"What?"

"What?"

"Stop saying what, for Pete's sake!" Hackett shouts, finally, taking Jakks by surprise.

"Sorry, I've been watching more of the television than ever before..." Jakks responds woefully and regretfully.

Hackett sighs and rolls his eyes, before he gets up from his leather chair and walks over to Jakks.

"What is it, Jakks?"

"I was just wondering if you would like... um... ah, forget it. You wouldn't take me up on my offer anyway," Jakks says resignedly, before walking out of the office.

And Hackett is left scratching his head, while cursing mildly at the same time.

"What a f*cking crazy a**hole..." Hackett mutters, in reference to Jakks, before the ringing of his cell phone stops him from going any further.

Never Satisified

He wasn't happy, was there any reason? Yes, he won his first match since he returned to the ring officially, but he knew in the back of his head that he could've done a lot better, and everytime he reviewed the tape, he found a mistake that happened in the match.

Click.

"What's wrong with it now?" Katanya questioned from the magazine VIBE magazine she was reading. She always had a thing for anything american, even though she was born and raised in Japan. Her attire alone showed that, blue jeans, and a white "Pokemon" t-shirt, (probably the only thing japanese about her) that only reached her belly button, maybe because she bought it in the children's section of the store.

"I could've done better," Araknis replied, rewinding the tape once more, "It might've been a win in the record books, but I know if my teachers saw it, they would've thought me to be mediocre." He reached to the back of his head, and untied his hair, as it flowed to his face.

"You need to lay off yourself, you can try and improve yourself tonight," She responded. She was already accostumed to it, he always was this way anyway. "But for now, you need to lend me your wallet."

"What for?" He asked. She gave him that look that women use to get you to do anything for them, and he sighed, reaching in his back pocket and giving her the wallet, "Just don't max the cards out okay? I mean, you're the only one that uses them, but still."

"Me? Max out your credit cards?" She smiled innocently, she reached over and kissed him on the cheek. "Look, don't worry about me, okay? You just deal with Krone, or whatever his name is, and don't be too hard on yourself if you do win."

"You know I can't make that promise," He said in what could be the closet thing to a joke he's ever made in his entire life. She exited the room, as he clicked the remote, and the video rewinded itself once more, and played through the match once again. The scene slowly faded to black.

Retribution

"I WANT CHAVEZ!"

Riot screamed as he flipped over some storage boxes. He had been storming around backstage for the better part of ten minutes in hopes of finding Avo Chavez, a man that had seemingly abandoned him in their tag team match earlier in the evening.

"NO ONE, AND I MEAN NO ONE DITCHES ME AND GETS AWAY WITH IT! I WANT HIS F*CKING HEAD!"

Some of the thReat staffers did there best to avoid Riot, fearful of their safety. Albeit except for one man that was reading over some notes for the show, totally oblivious to the fact that Riot was on a rampage. Riot grabbed him by the shirt color and slammed him against the wall, startling him.

"Chavez! Where is he?!"

Riot sneered as he began to breath heavily on the man.

"I... I don't know! He... he no-showed I think!"

Riot curled his lip and released the man's shirt. The man slid down the down and onto his rear end. As Riot turned and started to walk away, slamming his fists together.

"'No show', huh? That just won't do, no it won't. I will have some retribution. I will have it tonight."

Riot stopped dead in his tracks and raised a finger in the air. With a sly grin he walked back towards the arena as Flaw went elsewhere.

Noah Krone walked around the ring, saluting to the fans, holding his hand up. Noah Krone turned around and was met with a superkick by Araknis. Araknis got in his face and started to yell and scream some harsh words. Araknis picked Noah Krone up, and powerbombed him. He ran to the ropes, bounced off, and dropped an elbow on Noah Krone. He tried it again, but Noah Krone moved. Araknis grabbed his elbow. Noah Krone picked Araknis up, and whipped him to the ropes. Araknis bounced off, and came back. Noah Krone dropped him face first in the middle of the ring. He stomped on the back of Araknis head. And then got him in a figure four.

Noah Krone was in the ring screaming with anger. He then let the move go. Both men were holding their knees. Araknis got up first, and picked Noah Krone up. He started kicking Noah Krone in the knee, so Noah Krone bailed to the outside, and rested along the rail. Araknis got out, and started after Noah Krone. Noah Krone hopped in the ring, and when Araknis got in, Noah Krone kicked him in the back. Noah Krone picked Araknis up, and whipped him into the corner. The ref couldn't move in time, and got caught in between Araknis and the corner. Noah Krone got out the ring, and grabbed a chair. He got back in the ring, and nailed Araknis in the head with the steel. He set the chair down, and DDT'ed Araknis on the chair. Noah Krone then got on the top rope, and nailed an Arabian Facebuster on Araknis using the chair. He covered Araknis but there was no ref to count the three.

Noah Krone was gloating at some fan, when Araknis got up and grabbed the chair. Noah Krone turned around and got nailed right between the eyes. The Return

White. The walls were white. Jon Hackett sat in the dense office in which he got the feeling like it was a work in progress, but it wasn't. He'd been in this boring place a couple of times before, and he frankly couldn't stand its monotony.

Then, the phone rang. He studied the complex phone. Numerous lights were flashing, showing that the phone system was complex. He did the only thing he could think of doing as he picked up the receiver.

"Hackett here," he said sternly.

"Ah, yes, Hackett," a strange voice said with a strange accent. "It's a familiar face speaking, and tonight, I am nearby. I feel it's time to step out of this dark hole I've been hiding in, and I will be there soon." The voice trailed off, and then the end of the phone went silent.

"Wait a minute, who the hell is this?" Hackett had no idea where the person got the number. It could be the disgruntled hot dog salesman for all he knew.

The receiver clicked off. Hackett did not know what to think of the phone call. Was it a prank, or was it something to worry about?

He would soon find out.

Aww, Tommy. Don't be so sentimental. How can you miss me? I bother you every day.
