Biography

Xaxon is old.

He's been trying to change that fact for years. Drugs, supplements, witch-doctors, and psychics are things in which Xaxon has tried over and over.

But nothing has worked.

However, Xaxon went on a trip (which he never does) to Beijing, China.

Something happened, and it changed his life for good.
