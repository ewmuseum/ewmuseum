Extreme Impact

Wrestler 1: CJ Burns Birthday: 6.30.85 Hometown: Minneapolis, Minnesota Weight: 244 LBS

Wrestler 2: Beau Burns Birthday: 8.29.78 Hometown: Minneapolis, Minnesota Weight: 267 LBS

Combined Weight: 511 LBS Entrance Theme: 'One Step Closer' by Linkin Park Wrestling Style: Innovative/All Around Common Spots: Updated Soon. Bio: CJ Burns was born on June 30th, 1980. Since an early age, he was always viewed as a gifted child. He excelled through school all the way through high school. But we won’t go that far yet. CJ was always planning on getting his DVM to become a Veterinarian. All that changed when he turned 16. His father decided that he needed to do something to unite his three boys together. CJ’s father enrolled CJ, who was 16, Beau, who was 18, and Rowdy, who was 14, into Eddie Sharkey’s Training Camp.
CJ and Beau were always hard on Rowdy, because he was never up to par like they were. But, again, CJ excelled like in most things. He was so into it, he opted not to go to college and decided to pursue the world of wrestling. After a few months of training, they all started doing shows for Eddie. Eddie ran an independent promotion by the name of Steel Domain Wrestling. After about a year, Eddie’s SDW was bought out by Ed Hellier. With that, CJ and Beau decided to tour the greater Mid-West, while Rowdy was left in SDW…to claim to his fame.
In late 1999, a new federation by the name of Xtreme Wrestling Federation was starting up in Florida. In early 2000, CJ contacted the XWF for a contract and was signed. In May of 2000, CJ made his debut with child hood friend Adam ‘Vicks’ Vickerman. They were known as The Crew and won their first two tag matches. During their third tag match, Vicks suffered a serious neck injured, which caused him to go into early retirement. With Vicks gone, CJ had to go into singles competition. He was known as Taxi Bandit and got onto a great streak. He won the Intercontinental Title with in one month of his singles career. He went into a real life rivalry with Death Stroke, eventually losing the title. In September, the XWF signed Beau Burns. Grand Theft Auto was born.
CJ changed his Taxi Bandit gimmick to ‘The Bandit’ CJ Burns along with ‘The Mustang’ Beau Burns. Now known as Grand Theft Auto, they were very successfully as a tag team, beating many big name tag teams, Purgatory as one of the biggest. At the New Year’s Eve Bash, ‘Impact’ Rowdy Burns made his debut helping Grand Theft Auto win their tag match.
GTA started doing double duty as they worked for an up-and-coming federation by the name of Tidal Wave Wrestling. They dominated the tag team division and held the TWW Tag Team Championships until TWW’s demise.
In April of 2001, Grand Theft Auto (including Rowdy) made a great success. During the XWF Fallout PPV, Grand Theft Auto finally captured the XWF Tag Team Championships and ‘Impact’ Rowdy Burns captured the XWF Cruiserweight Championship. GTA’s title reign was short as they lost the titles to Ninja K and The Blazer. Also, on the night of Fallout, they announced themselves as Extreme Impact, and not to be known as Grand Theft Auto anymore. ‘Impact’ Rowdy Burn’s was finally being shown as the better Burns as he was always kept down his entire life. Out of nowhere, Rowdy turned on his brothers and he burst into stardom. CJ wasn’t too fond of this and they went into a three-month feud. The majority of their feud was over the XWF Television Championship.
In early 2002, Extreme Impact and Rowdy Burns contract was up and they left the XWF. Rowdy was already working in fEar and then Carnage, but CJ and Beau had nowhere to go. It didn’t take long until they were contacted by F.A.T.E. and were signed to a contract. But, unfortunately FATE didn’t list too long. CJ and Beau took a little vacation for a while until getting in contacts with the IOW. Soon after joining the IOW, they defeated former CWL Tag Team, The Dark Carnival for the Tag Team Titles. Now they look to go one step further by proving themselves in thReat.

Beau Burns was born on August 29th, 1978. Beau was always a big kid and always excelled in sports because of his size. Basketball was always his main goal, but when he was 18, his father enrolled him, and his two younger brothers, CJ and Rowdy, into Eddie Sharkey’s Training Camp for Wrestling.
Beau and CJ were always hard on Rowdy, because he was never up to par like they were. For the most part Beau did it because CJ did. He believes that was right, because CJ was the more intelligent of the two, and Beau knew and understood that. Beau enjoyed wrestling so much; he gave up on going to college on his basketball scholarship and decided to wrestle instead. After a few months of training, they all started doing shows for Eddie. Eddie ran an independent promotion by the name of Steel Domain Wrestling. After about a year, Eddie’s SDW was bought out by Ed Hellier.
