Biography

David was born in Houston, TX in 1982 at 1:45 am. He became a son to David and Jo'anna Johnson, in which they invited him with open arms, or at least his mom did. His father wasn't one-bit excited about his birth, and just after leaving the hospital to go home, his father left him and his mother to fend for themselves. His mother was a middle school teacher so the money wasn't around for him to live the luxurious life most children lived in Allen, TX.

He and his mother moved from Houston to get away from the crime to try and give him the best possibilities for succeeding in life. David would definitely take advantage of this oppertunity, but not until he was in his teens. Growing up as a kid was pretty easy for David because he was a big kid and people didn't dare say anything to hurt his feelings out of fear of being beat up. David wasn't a mean kid, he wouldn't have hurt a fly, except for his father. He's always had a certain rage against his father even though he hardly knew him. His mom did her best job trying to raise him the best she could and mold him into the man he needed to be in this world.

He lived his life as a normal child, the problems started when he got into his teens.. He got caught up in the wrong crowd with drugs and all the bad stuff. He eventually grew out of drugs and began lifting weights at a local gym. This is where David was introduced into wrestling by some guy by the name of Jimmy Deitrich. Jimmy taught David everything he knew about wrestling... Jimmy was no pro, but he knew what he was talking about. David was only 13 at the time and he was learning a lot for being so young. They entered David into a little indy fed and that's where he began his career as a wrestler, at the bright young age of 15.

He was to learn things in this indy fed he never thought he'd be able to pick up, but pick up he did! He quickly jumped to the top 3 of the class and scouts began to notice him. To David this was a huge surprise, for he never thought that'd wrestling would be a career, but things were looking up for him in this profession. He went on to excell in other indy feds, beating one afther the other, and this naturally came easy to him... for he was a man of no pain, until he recieved a call from a doctor.

March 20th, 2003 was a day he'd always remember, the day the one person who stuck by him, never teased him, and took care of him with joyful amounts of love, had a stroke. His mother was rushed to the nearest hospital where she would remain bedridden for the next couple of weeks. David quickly spread the news to his mother about his new career in thReat, and she was so proud of him.

Reguardless of how he was doing in his wrestling, there was one thing that he could never let go of, his mother. He would eventually have to, but right now he is putting every cent into getting his mother the best help and doctors that he can.. Time will only tell how much longer she has.
