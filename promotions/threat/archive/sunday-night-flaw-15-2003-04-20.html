Mounting Stress

Troy Mason sighed, as he swirled around in his chair in the office. His job was getting more stressful by the week. He could have never anticipated such a rollicking start to his ownership reign. Everything had been progressing along just fine, but now, his company was under threat.

Vincent Pembridge wanted to take over. And he was absolutely serious about it.

Having seen his top two challengers falter at the hands of The Scorpion Of Manchester, Mason now knew that Vince had all the power to make his petty threats become a reality. Now only did Troy know the gravity of the situation, and time was running out. Fast. A plan had to be cooked up to fend of the fighters in Pembridge, Embers... and quite shockingly enough, Bobby Knickerson.

Troy knew why Bobby joined forces with Vincent; he had punished him too harshly. Now, the backlash was laced with the insanity of The Shoot Sensation and the raw brutality of The British Degenerate.

As Mason sat in his chair, having received word that the rebel trio had entered the premises of the American West Arena, he wondered whether or not Vincent Pembridge had aces up his sleeves. The Vagabonds had won. They were standing tall, amongst a pile of broken bodies, as the teenagers on the outside had finally overpowered the security. El Crackerooni turned to Troy Mason, and rose his arms in the air. Before Vincent Pembridge yanked Crakcerooni's mask off.

TY HUGHES.

The arena fell silent. Ampske, Embers, Knickerson... laughed. Ty Hughes was El Crackerooni all along. It had been a set up. And he winked at Troy, as a sly grin fell across his face. The Scorpion Of Manchester? Slid out of the ring and traded high-fives with some of the teenagers.

Troy Mason?

Stunned. Absolutely stunned.

Before he noticed Vincent Pembridge advancing towards him, a crazy gleam present in the latter's eyes. Mason was now fearful.

His resistance had faltered. And now, Troy was on the run.

Of course, Vincent Pembridge was no slowpoke. He caught up with the owner and clubbed him across the back, knocking Troy down to the ramp. Vincent quickly turned Mason around and placed his right foot across the owner's throat, the resounding jeers getting louder by the second. But surely, Pembridge had more planned, yes?

....

Yes.

He dug into his jeans pocket, and produced a gun. Everybody was silent.

Troy began pissing himself.

"MASON! ONE CHANCE, YOU'VE GOT! TURN THIS BLOODY PLACE BACK INTO THE HYBRID IT WAS, AND LET'S HAVE THE FIGHTERS VERSUS WRESTLERS MATCH THAT NEVER HAPPENED!"

The owner began trembling, as if he already wasn't. Vincent pointed the gun at Mason's head, as several cries rang out amongst the audience. This was too much, they thought. This was going to far. And Troy wasn't given a chance to answer, what with The British Degenerate's foot across his throat and all. But he had to make up his mind.

Now.

"O-O-OKAY! OKAY! YOU WIN!"

Heads hung low. Vincent laughed. Ty Hughes punched the air. The Vagabonds celebrated. The first phase had been completed. And the challenge for the deciding phase had been laid down. thReat was officially a hybrid federation again. The Scorpion Of Manchester could have gone further, but he knew how deeply Mason felt about thReat becoming a fighter's paradise.

He would have rather been shot.

But as it was, Vincent decided to pull the trigger.

Vincent Pembridge kicked Troy in the face and knocked him out.

See, Vincent could have lost it all. He had no bullets. Troy would have rejected Vince's demand of a change to a fighter's federation, he would have to shoot him, and... The Scorpion would have been embarrased. In any case, however, the objective was to go through this the hard way.

The betrayal of Jon Hackett left a bitter taste in his mouth. And there was only one way to remedy that. To have Hackett sit back and watch, as his brainchild burn.

In the backstage, which was empty... Rio Nexan witnessed everything on a television monitor. And laughed. Before he turned and walked out of the arena, washing his hands off thReat. With one last comment about what he had witnessed, however.

"Amateurs."

And back at ringside, Vincent Pembridge stood tall over Troy Mason's body. Hughes, Ampske, Embers, & Knickerson raised their arms in the air. A crucial victory had been obtained. The mutiny was well underway. Everything was going according to plan. And in a couple of weeks, their vision would be realised. Vincent's vision would be realised. And Troy's worst nightmare would have engulfed him.

"Time to FIGHT."
