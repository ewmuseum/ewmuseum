Chris Collins

Real Name: Christopher Lamar Collins Nickname: "The Macabre Man, The Black Plague, The Ace of Evil, Your Worst Nightmare, The Black Panther, The Immortal"
Also known as: C Dubbed

Born: December 3, 1979 Place of Birth: Brooklyn, New York Hometown: Brooklyn, New York

Height: 6'2 Weight: 235lbs Blood type: A Eye color: Light Brown

Team: none

Manager: none Trainer: Daniel Jackson

Theme song: "Never Scared" by BoneCrusher

Style

Competing style: All Around

Signature moves:
Eternal Pain(Chris kicks the opponent in the mid-section as they bend down, he goes to the back and grabs them by their arms lifting them up into a A Pumphandle hoist, he then brings the opponent back down into a Reverse Tombstone Piledriver as they hitting the mat hard)


The False Assault(Chris lays out the opponent, he then walks over to a nearby turnbuckle where he bends down to wait. Slowly the opponent reaches their feet and when they turn around they are hit with a vicious version of a spear with major impact.)


The Evil Curse (Chris kicks the opponent in the mid-section and lifts them up into a piledriver position. With the opponent still in the position he puts his legs in front of their arms and falls down to the mat making the opponent hit face and body first.)


The Lethal Dosage(Chris lays out the opponent. He goes over to the opponent and rolls them over so they are lying down on their chest. Next he goes to the top of their body near the head and grabs it, locking them into a modified version of the Crossface.)


The Black Out (Chris goes to the back of the opponent to lock in a modified version of the Tazzmission.)

Finisher: Chris grabs the opponent into a reverse ddt position. He then jumps up and flips forward bringing the opponent down into a Ace Crusher. (Reverse DDT Flipped into an Ace Crusher)
Finisher name: The Negative Impact

Historical Info

Other/Previous promotions: NBW, XCWF, GWF, ROW, REW, OSE(Kraig Carter), PURE(Hwao Kwan)

Other/Previous teams: none

Other/Previous managers: Lena Heart, Victoria Vengeance

Other titles won: XCWF Junior Heavyweight Champion, XCWF Extreme Champion (2x), GWF European Champion, GWF North American Champion, GWF Inter-continental Champion (2x), ROW Hardcore Champion (3x), ROW Tag Champion, REW Champion (2x), REW Inter-national Champion (2x)
Other awards won: First NBW Champion, Longest NBW Championship Reign

Biography

Chris Collins was born on December 3, 1981 in Brooklyn, New York. As a child, Chris grew up in the 'ghetto' as you would call it in Harlem, New York. He grew up hustling on the block and fighting in gangs with his 'boyz.' So he struggled in his past. His family was very poor and lived in a very old and run down house. Luckily for Chris he was the only child.

Unlike many blacks in the south, Chris was very well educated. He studied hard whenever he could and he got good grades. But his main focus in life was sports. Sports was everything to him. He wanted to be a professional boxer ad everyone knew that his dream would someday come true.

But there was boxing sport in his high school, so he did the closest thing which was wrestling. He trained everyday and worked very hard to become the best, but he wasn't. He was okay but not the best. But now he knew that he wanted to change his career. He loved wrestling, it had become his life.

Chris went on to Syracuse University and from there he was scouted by his first wrestling promotion. Real Extreme Wrestling, he did very well here capturing the Inter-National and World Titles numerous times.

After his contract was up he went on to other feds winning numerous titles.

Chris' main goal is to become the best wrestler there ever was and to be known by everyone in the world, and he takes one step of accomplishing his dream every single day.
