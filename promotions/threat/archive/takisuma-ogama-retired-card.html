Takisuma Ogama

Born: September 15, 1974 Place of Birth: Tokyo, Japan Hometown: Tokyo, Japan

Height: 6'3 Weight: 233 lbs. Blood type: O Eye color: Green

Manager: "Beautiful" Bobby Blair

Theme song: A Japanese Symphony

Style

Competing style: Extremist / Martial Arts

Signature moves:
1. Asai Moonsault
2. "Ogama Kicks" - With right side kick he takes out the right knee, with a left side kick he takes out the left knee, opponent on both knees he steps back and drills him with a drop kick.
3. Springboarding Back Elbow - When whipped to the ropes, he front hand spring bounce of them and come back with a flying back elbow.
4. Spin Wheel Kick
5. Spinng Heel Kick
6. Superkick
7. "Hatred" - A standard michinoku driver.
8. Top Rope Senton Bomb - Puts his arms out to the side before jumping.
9. Inverted DDT - Sometimes done after a Asai moonsault when the opponent is standing.
10. Tree of Woe - He ties 'em upside down in the tree of woe, then slides into their face with a baseball slide. Sometimes down with a chair.

Finisher: A modified dragon sleeper, with his legs wrapped around the opponent squeezing the life out of them, while choking them out.
Finisher name: Ogamacity

Historical Info

Other/Previous promotions: EIW, LSW, PNW, MLW,

Other/Previous teams: International Incident

Other titles won: LSW Television (3), LSW Tag Team(4), PNW Hardcore, MLW Maple Leaf Champion
Other awards won: #121 on RSPWF's Top 200 of 2002

Biography

Takisuma Ogama was born in the busy city of Tokyo, Japan where he was raised by his father. His father constructed a Martial Arts dojo. Following in his fathers foot steps he trained day after day in that dojo, to perhaps one day take it over.

At the age of 16 he gets introduced to the world of wrestling as he goes to his first ever FMW event. Instantly hooked he lost all sight of his fathers dojo and instead started working out on a daily basis hoping to one day become a pro wrestler.

Age 17, enter "Beautiful" Bobby Blair, a smooth talking wrestling promoter looking for some great talent. Blair was at the time on tour with UWC a promotion he once owned, that was going down hill. Bobby quickly saw talent in the kids blood and decided to work with him, to perhaps one day swith from his current promoting job to managing.

Ogama quickly found success two years later when he, and good friend Bobby Blair signed with EPW. A very big promotion over in the states. Ogama packed his bags and took a big move from Japan to the US. Capturing the Extremist title he showed off what he was capable of, wish his fear nothing attitude. Gathering a cult following of extreme wrestling lovers he made an impact. Several months later he was crowned with the EPW World Championship.

Later that year, he put the title on the line in a no rules, hardcore type matchup. After that match he would never be the same. Ogama was inverted piledriven through two tables, breaking his neck. Facing a career threatening injury he layed on the sideline for a year and a half.

Ongoing monthes or rehabilitation have come through as Ogama has redebuted in many promotions. Most recently PNW where he captured the Hardcore title. Since then he had a rather healthy stint in LSW, where he captured the Television titel on three occasions and the tag titles with "YTLF" Carl Blair on four occasions.

But as of the current time he still searchs for a steady federation where he can quickly make his name well known.
