Let's Get This Show On The Road

Troy Mason sat in his office, reading a wrestling publication, that had covered the Payback PPV. An event that was evolved in his absence, due to the kidnapping. Smiling, he looked at the photos of the event that had been inserted into the magazine, particularly overjoyed at the one taken of General David Sanders & himself hugging, with the thReat World Title having been won by the former.

Troy -- wearing a white long-sleeved shirt with a black tie and dark blue pants -- wasn't completely happy, however. Sure, the Board Of Directors temporary reign of power had ended, but Mason felt that the BOD had done enough damage to thReat already. He had a right to be bitter, too. It was the Board that first gave Vincent Pembridge power many moons ago, during the Vagabonds/Resistance war. A crucial move for the Scorpion, that allowed up to make many decisions ahead of time; insurance policies, they were called.

Troy had no idea what these insurance policies entailed, and thus, couldn't counter them.

There was a knock at the owner's door, before a backstage technician slowly entered the office. Troy looked up from his magazine and nodded his head, as he received the stage report from the technician. Looking over the report, Mason didn't notice that the technician was still in the office, smiling.

"Yep?"

"Oh, nothing, sir. Was just thinking that it's great to be back on the air, after the hoop-la of Payback, yanno? Not that the show wasn't great, just cool to be finally rid of that Xaxon fella. No more kidnappings and stuff!"

Mason chuckled, as he signed the stage report and handed it back to the technician, acknowledging & echoing his sentiments. Troy was relieved that the whole kidnapping incident was over and done with. There were still two unknowns; the kidnapper and the accomplice thought to have been helping to pull the strings.

"Yeah, I agree with you. Things can hopefully return back to normal around here. Although this place has never been normal ever since I took ever. I remember towards the end of Flaw 13, when I was just soaking in all the praise and feedback for the show... that arrogant bastard, Vincent Pembridge, stormed into my office and beat the living crap out of me."

The technician nodded, remembering that day.

"Well, we've got the International Title Tournament kicking off today, right?"

Troy started beaming from ear-to-ear, remembering about the tournament he'd drawn up to crown at International Champion, at long last. The company already had a World Champ, a Aggression Champ, a Cruiserweight Champ and Tag Team Champions. There was just one more title to be passed out, and 16 people would be competing for it.

"Where's our champion, by the way?"

"Getting ready to go down to the ring, to greet the crowd and all that stuff, Sir."

Just then, the door to the office was kicked open. And with an unfamiliar man, stepped in the one & only FIGHTING CHAMPION. You might remember him as the man responsible for the entire Vagabonds/Resistance war. And the man that was at ringside for commentary during the thReat World Title Match at Payback.

"Well, good day to ye too, Mason. I had a feeling that you might have not gotten the memo." Troy & the technician turned to look at Vincent Pembridge, decked out in a blue t-shirt and black jeans with black boots, alongside the aforementioned unfamiliar man, with a smug grin on his face. He was holding a black briefcase and looking around Troy Mason's office, nodding at how nicely decorated it was.

"What memo, Vincent?"

"You know, Troy. The one where the Board Of Director finally agreed upon a person perfect for the plan I devised many months back. And so, I have the pleasure of presenting to you, Troy, thReat's new Commissioner.

Colin Gear."

Mason's heart stopped for a few seconds as he watched Vincent motion for Colin to follow him out of the office, evidently to familiarise the latter with his new work environment. The stage technician blinked, and slowly shuffled out of the office, this being news to him too.

It never ends for ol' Troy Mason, eh?

The Future Is Unwritten

What a waste. That was what was going through their minds as they approached the locker room of the new tag team champions.

What a waste in time, in hard work, and in prestige. They had trained together for a long time. They had both looked forward to the day they could compete against the very best in the world. All that time and energy, in trying to become the best.

And it had all gone to waste. Their time wasted training, their dreams crushed, and the two biggest wastes of all time holding the very titles they had come to thReat to take for themselves.

Zoe Antoinette stopped in front of the door, prepared to knock. She sighed. This was difficult, more so for her then for her partner. She almost felt this was her fault.

"Excuse me! Excuse me, slappio! This is the room of the NEW thReat World Tag Team Champions, pal! You don't just barge right in here an--"

"You --"

"A deal is a deal. Give it to me."

They must have enjoyed some of those pages, and she knew it all too well, as bitter and disgusting as that sounded.

"You think this is funny?! I should just --" Martin took a step forward, crossing over Zoe's leg, almost sliding around to throw himself into a battle he could only lose.

Again, Zoe held her partner back.

"Give me my diary back, NOW." Zoe was through with all of this playing around.

X-Ploder looked at SinFyre, who looked at Johnny O. Olympus sneered condescendingly and turned around. Generative Clash found this snub to be the greatest of all, and the one they were going to make sure was paid back in full.

But that would be later. For now, it had to end.

Personal secrets. Remember that if you EVER think about stepping up to the thReat Tag Team Champions. Ha ha!"

There was always tomorrow for karma to turn around on Born To Die.

But that would be tomorrow. The future.

Zoe patted her diary with the tips of her fingers. The future was unwritten.

And the eventual word to be written would be... payback.
