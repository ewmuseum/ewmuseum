The Sodding Truth [Issue 002]
June 11 2003

Hello cunts,

Flaw 19 was absolutely smashing, I must say. Truly wonderful.

Oh bollocks, who am I kidding? It was shite. Utter shite. No fighting AT ALL. How bloody shocking is that? Okay, sure, The Resistance won, no more hybrid bollocks, no more fighting, blah blah blah. But it was a disgrace. Nobody came close to packing power in their bleeding punches and kicks. It was saddening to see.

Seeing how I am the almighty Scorpion Of Manchester, and I'm still pretty much a part of this company until Troy Mason sees fit to fire me, I figured I might as well entertain you gits with a rundown of what went down on the shitefest that was Flaw 19. Won't that be fun, cunts?

Yes, it would.

Okay, we start off with... well, Ty Hughes and his little speech. I shall not comment on it, since it's purely nonsensical. All I will say to you, Ty, is wait until I decide to take up Joe on his offer and join tA full time. Certain things prevent me from doing so, such as the person you smuggled into the Philips Arena at Malevolence, but it shall not be long.

And then, I will beat your arse to a bloody pulp.

Moving on. That little in-ring show with the bloke who's blind. Boring shite. Then Troy Mason crying. Ah, that was bloody wonderful. Some cunt with big assets showed up to ask Troy about something. Didn't pay attention there, so can't comment on it. Pretty horrendous so far.

Following that, two queers had an altercation. They affect my life in no way imaginable, so I don't bloody care. Another new guy decided to sit down and think after that. WOW, BLOODY EXCITING. Goodness me, what does Troy think about while interviewing these trolls?

After that, General Cuntness met up with a guy who was verbally skilled and used it to his advantage. Candor, I believe he was called. Interesting chap, might ring him up and congratulate him for showing the General a thing or two about speaking eloquently.

Let's see, more boring shite following that. Some cunt being offered drugs in exchange for anal sex, another cunt who approached me at Malevolence about wanting to be in The Vagabonds making an arse of himself, and the cunt whom Troy Mason entrusted with being the veteran of The Resistance losing to nØt after doing some bleeding soul-searching or something of the sort.

Pathetic.

But let me go off on a tangent for a while -- Levond, you are a bleeding NOBODY. You think you're some twat with superior fighting skills and all, but really, you lack a brain. I do hope that the man that I signed, Anubis, rips the twat's throat out. What's her name? Jenkins? Yes, that's it. Should be smashing to watch, aye?

Alright, that's the end of that, ahem. Following that, another cunt was being offered something. Money, this time. She looks like a bloody toothpick in any case, so no bother. And then, my friend Phil Atken had his chance to grace the world with his glorious Cruiserweight Title snatched away by Troy. Feck, that was one of the few bright spots of the show, seeing Atken outspeak Troy. Pure brilliance.

More boredom after that. A match I didn't catch, following which, one of the many new buggers in the company won a match. And then, his old nemesis came back. My, guess who it was? Rio Nexan.

A name I never thought I'd see associated with a company I am a part of.

Didn't he say that everybody in thReat were amateurish or something of the nature? Heh, Rio. I know perfectly well who you are. I know perfectly well that this little split personality thing of yours is just an excuse to piss around with the fans, because they didn't appreciate the ridiculous games you were playing in fEar. Sociopathic? I hardly think so. You, sir, are the amateurish one.

Now, with that out of the way, I can continue. Dane Manetta continued to fight with his sister over something relating to intercourse. I'm not sure on that. Kyle Knowen threw his wedding ring at his wife after that, which was enjoyable to watch.

And that's where I stopped caring. Until the fantastic final segment, of course. Troy Mason, getting the shite kicked out of him, before being kidnapped. Amazing. Xaxon truly is a wonderful person, and he's not disappointed me so far. Headbutt at Malevolence, more arsekicking & a kidnapping on Flaw 19. Incredible.

That's all from me today, I suppose. I've got some people to maim today, no time to sit at my desk and do some mindless typing. Oh, did I mention, Troy, that contrary to popular belief... the Fighting Title is NOT retired? Despite the fighting element in thReat having been completely abolished?

... Oh, how I love those surprises. Have fun with the old bugger.

-VP
