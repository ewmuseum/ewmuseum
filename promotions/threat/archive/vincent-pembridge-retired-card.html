Biography

Greetings, my name is Vincent Pembridge. In the arena of wrestling and fighting, however, I go by the moniker of Jakks. Anyway, you asked me to talk about my life thus far, and I shall. One word of caution, though; I fear I have taken one too many shovel shots to me head, so my memory is rather impaired. I am taking medication for this little problem, but doctors predict it would take months for the matter to be rectified. Even years. Bah. No matter, let's get on with it.

I was born in Manchester, one of the football-crazy towns in England. Two clubs reside in Manchester; City and United. United, of course, being the more successful of the two. But I digress; I was born on February 28 1977. It was a beautiful day in Manchester... save for the blasted rain, of course.

My parents were pretty well-to-do. Our family had our share of monetary issues, especially when me sister; Karen; came along in 1980... but we lived through the hard times with optimism and a few months later, everything was hunky-dory again. I attended school like any other normal boy. I wasn't overly popular, but I wasn't often picked on as well. I was in the middle crowd. But once I hit puberty and adolescence beckoned, I became pretty noticable. Mainly due to how my body developed. The girls liked me, but some of them shyed away due to my aggresive nature. I can't blame them; I don't believe in hurting the fairer sex, be it intentional or accidental.

I also became a part of the in-crowd, although I never really wanted it or cared. I just did my own things and pretty much kept in touch with my friends before my rise to 'stardom'.

Nothing special happened in my life; of course, I may have forgotten a couple of important events due to my failing memory. From what I can recollect, I was just an average guy doing his studies. Eventually, I pursued a graduate's degree in accounting... but seeing how my physicality was quite imposing, I was prodded to try out for some rough sports. Rubgy, football.. even wrestling. I hadn't heard much of wrestling until then, but seeing videos of some of USA's top wrestling promotions lit a fire in my body; I suddenly became very interested in wrestling.

So, it was 1997, and I was 20 years old. I enrolled in a rugby school down in Southampton and also took part in a wrestling academy in Birmingham. You can imagine how hectic my life had become when I started dating, but I lived through all the hardship, and graduated from the rubgy school with flying colours a year later. I was quickly signed on by Broughton Park club under the rugby union. I was deemed an all-rounded and played anywhere I was told to play. And I was pretty good. About nine months later, I graduated from the wrestling school and also got my degree in accounting. My life got better when I received a letter from the US, regarding a job attachment. My parents and my sister gave me their blessings, and I was off to the United States Of America in July of 1997.

My accounting job rather shabbily, though. Some of me colleagues were iffy at the fact that a Briton was working in 'their house'. It didn't matter much to me, though. This job would last me until December that year. Five months was more than enough for me, as the pay was astoundingly good. Of course, I had to work my ass off.. and I vaguely remember an altercation with the supervisor of the department I was working in, which almost got me booted. The whole 'farce' ended in me being able to work from home, and I gave Mr Negris all my thanks.

During the time when I wasn't rounding up numbers for multi-national corporations, I got more involved in wrestling.. and eventually signed a contract with a Supreme Wrestling Federation, owned by a Mr Kamlesh Lilani. A man who, one of my wrestling teachers in the States said, believed in giving chances to everybody who showed signs of brilliance.

And I quickly became a big hit in the SWF; I soon held three titles at once; the Intercontinental, the United-States and the European. Of course, I lost two of those titles at a pay-per-view in November, but I believed I held the Hardcore Title for a few days and went on to become a two-time holder of the United-States Title. Memories of the SWF are blurry, but there were good.

As my job attachment neared its end in December, the SWF folded. On January 6 2000, I was back in Manchester and once again signed on with Broughton Park, as well as securing a job in a nearby industrial zone. My relationship with Katrina also blossomed to new heights. Everything seemed perfect in my life. Of course, perception isn't reality.

About a month later, Katrina got transferred from her workplace to mine, and worked in the Logistics department. The both of us felt that this was incredibly amazing, since I worked in the Finance department, which was always in close conjunction with the Logistics department. A scandal soon rocked the company's boat; money laundering was it, and Katrina was the main suspect for some reason. Not having a good grasp of the legal system, I just stood by Katrina, believing her. After all, we HAD talked about marriage and a family.

Alas, it all crumbled. Actual proof that it was Katrina's doing came into the light. Desperate, Katrina did the only thing she could; she tried to frame me. And I had the evidence to prove it. Seeing her cry and plead, however, made me feel sorry for her. She said she had been tricked. And I believed her. So, with that, I turned myself in. I said I did it.

At the trial that was went down during the early days of March 2000, my life reached an all-time low. I saw Katrina and the manager of the company together. It was all a devious plan by Katrina, who provided doctored video-footage of me and my evil deeds. I was sentenced to twenty years in jail. Enraged to the point of no return when my sentence was read out, I snapped and broke out of my restraints. What ensued in the courthouse remains a blank to me... but by March 10 2001, I was back in the USA, hiding in the apartment of some drug abusers in Chicago. However, something strange happened to me; Kamlesh, the owner of the SWF, found me. How he did, I'll never know. But he must have obviously been impressed with my work in the SWF, for he would not have followed my torrid story in the UK.

'No matter how innocent you are, you are guilty in the eyes of the law. Your little demolition exhibition in the courthouse did nothing good for you. So, I'm gonna offer you some advice; since you're deemed a criminal, start acting like one. Because even the best lawyers in the world can't save you..'

Those words stayed in my mind for the next three days, and they had a profound impact. Mixed with my rage, I decided to follow his advice. I also signed a deal with Kamlesh's newest venture; the Underground Fighting Federation.

For the next year and a half, I fought in the UFF and dabbled in criminal activity. It didn't matter to me what the crime was; as long as it had nothing to do with hurting women. As far as my experience in the UFF goes... it was great. My outlook on life changed when I signed with the UFF, and I became very untrustworthy. I changed alliances pretty frequently, and just when it seemed I finally came to my senses, I went ahead and did something incredibly shocking.

I was Foreigner's Champion a few times, the Gang-Fight Champ twice.. can't remember, really. I do, however, recall winning the Ultimate Heavyweight Title. It was a great night when I won it. A titanic struggle that lasted more than a year. I was one of the first two men to challenge for the title when the federation opened in 2000, but at Revelations 1, I was cheated and missed out on the chance to be the title's first holder. I kept working, however, and finally got what I deserved.

The UFF travelled around the world frequently, and on a particular stop in Australia, I came across the Outback Fighter's Guild. Once I retired from the UFF, I immediately flew to Australia and got myself involved with the OFG. Right from the start, I established myself as a bad guy. It was a blessing, really. I made more connections as far as crime was concerned and the size of Australia meant expansion of business.

The OFG was another great fighting experience for me, and in the six months though, I acheived quite a lot. I won one of the important titles twice... and also won the annual Desert Challenge.

I returned to the United States, mainly because a guy by the name of Sikanah was offering me a very tempting deal. All I have to do is to agree to be a part of his gang, which will take over theFightingZone; a fight club. I then heard about this new arena called thReat.. and well, here I am, telling you; the powers-that-be of thReat; my life story.

I wish to be the best at what I do. I want to dominate. And I'll take on any comers who dare to stand in my way. My mission is simple; to be the best, and to speard the word.

Being bad is good.
