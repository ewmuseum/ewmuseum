J. Leslie Voss

Real Name: Justin Leslie Voss Nickname: "VossMan, Big V, vMan, vMonsta"

Born: 18th of September, 1981 Place of Birth: Preston, Victoria, Australia Hometown: Baton Rouge, Louisiana

Height: 6'6 Weight: 242 lbs Blood type: B + Eye color: Green-blue

Theme song: "Arabian Bustrip" Paprika

Style

Signature moves:
powerbomb, top rope MD fistdrop, Samoan drop, scoop slam, jumping DDT, torture rack

Trademark: Executive Decision -- puts trash can on opponent's head then a spinebuster slam

Finisher: Vertical suplex to sitdown neckbreaker Finisher name: vPlex

Historical Info

Other/Previous promotions: Spinebuster Wrestling (current)

Biography

I was born in Preston, Victoria, Australia to a truck driver and house wife. We moved to Kinglake where I spent three months before mum and dad bought a house in Melton. So we moved there.

I went to kinder, got a baby sister, all that shit kids do. Never really got to see my dad, he worked 40 hour weeks and all we had was mum around. And she started working so we got babysitters. Fucking great. I get to hang around the smelliest, ugliest kids in school coz they lived over the road. It sucked.

1990, five tonnes of steel falls on my dad's legs and fucks them up good. He can't walk for a good year and a half, but he manages to fight and get on his feet. He can walk without any aides or anything. My hero if you will. I guess it sounds kinda selfish, but coz he can't work anymore, he gets to spend more time with me. Listen to me, my disabled father doesn't get to work but he gets to look after his kids. Great son I am.

It's selfish I know. But every kid needs a father.

We move to a country town called Scarsdale, coz dad wanted to go there when he retired. And he hasn't much choice, so we moved.

They move me to fucking hicksville. Nobody around for miles. Out of civilisation and into the stoneage. My free time consisted of wrestling videos and Raw is War. I became quit addicted. I worked hard on this mini-farm, carrying bluestone bricks and cutting wood. I got natural strength. Built it up slowly. I was a city boy in a country town, other kids tried to beat on me.

Some succeeded.

Some.

I broke a lot of heads when I had to, otherwise I didn't use much violence. I was pretty placid.

Average kid at high school, got the job done. Volleyball captain. Actor. School basketball coach and referee. And player. I went to every party there was to be had.

Alcohol. Cigarettes. Bongs.

You name a party then and I was there. But school finished and I just partied harder.

It was called University.

I was supposed to study to be a primary school teacher, elementary for you Yanks. But that sucked, I wanted to party. So I did.

I quit after one year and studied acting. I was top of my class and graduated with an offer to teach. Fuck that. I loved wrestling.

I moved to Sydney and did six months with the AWF wrestling school. Then I moved to Baton Rouge.

Now I'm making history...
