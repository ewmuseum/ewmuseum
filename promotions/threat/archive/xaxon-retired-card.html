Biography

"People age."

Xaxon would've never thought of aging as a child. He had everything. His iron will as a youth was stronger than all the boys in his school. He had everything he wanted, and everything he needed as a child.

His dad had always loved him because he wanted to become a firefighter.

Xaxon's parents were murdered however, which wasn't a big deal at the time to him, because he had never really spoken to his mother, and his father was one of the strict, evil types. Xaxon was with them as the shadow came to life, and right there he saw his parents die on the ground.

The stone eyes developed, his heart darkened, and he aged eight years in only five seconds.

Xaxon went off to live with his uncle Ronnie, who he had heard was a nutcase and ate raw meat in a dark closet. This was just Xaxon's imagination, as Ronnie was a huge, gentle giant.

Ronnie was a wrestler in the late 50's and early 60's, and Xaxon went to his wrestling events-wherever they were-and enjoyed every moment of them. Ronnie was huge with the crowd, and Xaxon realized he wanted to be him one day.

After work, Ronnie brought Xaxon to the car again, where, "the shadow came to life," once again. Ronnie was shot and killed, and Xaxon watched him breathe his final wind into the air.

The next ten years, Xaxon aged, and aged some more. His face looked expressionless, tired, and old. His eyes were boring and gray. His hair was thinning and altering colors.

He strove to become a wrestler for the first few years of his career. He wanted to make his uncle happy.

Xaxon never could get it done. He never could make it into the big leagues. He traveled (what it seemed) every independent in the states. And although he left back memories, no one seemed to care.

Thus, he aged more. His eyes wrinkled, his hair thinned even more, and he was like a jagged stone that had been thrown into a mud puddle.

Xaxon didn't care about Ronnie or his parents. He just wrestled for the sake of wrestling. He began to love the sport more than anything else in the world. No longer would he run the cliché of young man gone bad because his parents and uncle were murdered, now he was just a wrestler who loved wrestling.

An old wrestler.

Xaxon soon realized he wasn't aging because of all the bad things that happened in his life, he was aging because he was getting old. He had just hit the mark of his forty fifth birthday when he stopped wrestling for some time because his knees were weak, and his back was in serious pain.

After the rest, Xaxon came back to a favorite independent of his, AIWF. He had wrestled in it for years, and they told him his time had come. He didn't want to go big now that he was an old man, on the brink of fifty years of age.

They asked him why he didn't want to go big.

He replied, "People age."
