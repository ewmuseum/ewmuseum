The Journey To The Top(Pt 2 "The Long Road Ahead"
Starring: Mat Walton

7/05/2003

Today was the defining day in the career of young Mat Walton. Today was the fifth of July, 2003. Now, for most people this is just a regular day, but for Mat, this would be one of the most exciting days in his life. Today was the day he, along with his cousin Andrew Martin, and trainers Sid Williams and Shaun Cage would head to Calgary, Alberta the site of Mat's first wrestling match in his career.

brrrz..... Good morning everybody it's 5:30 AM right now. I'm Adam Thompson and here's your Kyx 98 weather sponsored by..... The alarm clock radio had just cut in and apparently it's 5:30 outside.

Walton reached for the snooze button but just as he did, his room was invaded by his three companions.

"Wake up sleepy head it's time to get ready for work," Andrew Martin said half sarcastically, half peppy, "Come on sunshine it's a great day to be alive!" He continued as he pulled up the window curtains and sun flooded the room.

"unrgh..wha....who," A groggy Walton said trying the shield his eyes from the bliding sunlight.

"God what are you a vampire or something?" Shaun Cage questioned.

"Haul your lazy ass outta bed!" He barked.

"not yet...." Walton whined.

"Alright then," Martin sighed.

The three men grabbed the matress and flipped it over on to the floor below.

"What the hell was that for?" Walton asked, slightly more awake now.

"You wouldn't get up on your own terms, so we helped you out a little," Martin said. Cage and Williams chuckled.

"Now come on get up and come down for breakfast or else there won't be any there for ya," Martin concluded as he and the other two men left the room.

Mat took a fairly quick shower and hauled on a pair of jeans and a plain white t shirt. He then went and grabbed his bag of things for the weekend and proceeded downstairs to find all the food gone.

"Told you to hurry up," Martin said.

"Snooze ya lose buddy," Williams chimmed in.

A look of angered shock crossed his face as he pulled up a chair and sat down with his arms folded across his chest at the table.

"Awww ickle Matie wanna bottle?" Shaun Cage sarcasticly questioned him with a childish tone to his voice.

"Screw off!" Walton snapped.

"What are you gonna do?" Cage replied.

"Knock it off!" A voice from behind them barked.

The two of them whipped around to see a now very calm looking Andrew Martin.

"You didn't think I'd starve you did you?" Martin questioned his Nephew.

Martin produced a plate of food and placed it down at Walton's place at the table.

"Now eat up," Martin said as he ruffled Walton's hair.

Mat realised that his uncle really was too good to him. After all, he was the only one to really protect him against the semiplayful attacks by Sid and Shaun. He was also the only one that Mat knew that didn't laugh at them when he told them that he wanted to be a professional wrestler. But that didn't matter, his food was getting cold and it was time to eat.

Breakfast was actually quite hearty. Three Eggs, done sunny side up just the way he likes them, several pieces of Bacon and Sausage each, Two very large pieces of Toast with a large assortment of jams to go with it, and a large jug of both Chocolate Milk and Orange Juice were all at his disposal. Mat continued eating even after he was full at the command of his Uncle.

"*You* can never be too full buddy," He said, "Maybe we'll finally put some meat on those bones"

Mat sighed and continued eating until finally everything was gone.

"Time to go to work," He heard his uncle call out from the doorway.

Mat quickly grabbed his bag and headed outside. He had expected for there to be a Limo or some other fancy vehicle to take them to the airport and they would fly to Calgary.

But when he got outside he saw something that was definately not a Limo...

It was an old, run-down, RV that was actually being loaded with things for the trip.

"You're kidding right?" Walton asked Andrew in a bewildered state.

"Nope," Martin replied, "Welcome to your new home on the road,"

The sense of bewilderment hadn't left his face, would he really have to travel in this....this *monstrosity*.

The three other men climbed aboard the Motorhome. Walton was still standing outside.

"You coming or what rookie!" Shaun Cage called out from inside the RV.

Mat swallowed hard, and opened the door to the Motorhome and the steps automatically came down to him. He sighed again and climbed aboard.

"This can't be *that* bad can it?" He thought to himself as he climbed aboard.

"Maybe it can," He thought as he looked at the grungy interior of the RV. Duct tape held together many of the seat, small beds, a cracked dining table, and the only real form of television was a small thirteen incher sitting in a cabinet above the drivers seat. Even that didn't work though.

Mat took a seat on one of the seats and the duct tape clinged to his jeans.

"This is going to be one *long* trip" he said under his breath.
