Honey, This Mirror isn't big enough for the Both of Us
Starring: Kyle Knowen

We could have been perfect... We could be angels under a bright blue sky if I wasn't so damned.

So flawed.

I stared at her from our bed, from the only place we can be in love, as she brushed her hair with a science. One stroke, two strokes, three strokes. She watched me from her mirror, half smiling, but there was a glimmer in her eye, that I couldn't place. Its so strange, when I've been with other woman, I see that face, when I am with her, I see that face, but... I can't stop with the other woman, we're heading for a fork in the road, and I know I'm going to get impaled on the sharp edge of it. Right in the middle.

"What do you think of that Semps kid?"

I asked her, trying to break the excrutiating silence.

"He seem like a punk."

Her reply was right on the money...

"but..."

Oh no, she said but.

"He's cute. Don't hurt him."

And there it was. A bombshell. After all I've done, I felt more then a little jealous. So at that moment, I silently decided to take action. She finished her brushing, and stood up, shutting the light. She rolled into bed, for some reason seeming careful not to make contact with me.

I had never questioned why she attacked me in the ring. I'm afraid to. Just like I won't question this. I rolled over, facing the ceiling in the moonlight. She'll wake up in about two hours, wake me and mount me, and fall asleep in my arms, but for now, she'll hate me.

I almost like it.

And I do love her.

And I will hurt Semps.
