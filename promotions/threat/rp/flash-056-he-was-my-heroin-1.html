He Was My Heroin - 1
Starring: Ernie French

Near death experiences are a much talked about part of the quest to discovering the afterlife. People describe large white rooms or they claim to have seen a bright light that drew them in. Me? My experience was nothing like that. My experience opened my eyes to the world around me; it made me change my ways forever. Two years after I had moved out of France I was living in London, England. Most people would think that London is a marvellous place, a place filled with historic monuments and beautiful buildings, those people are mistaken. London is a dirty place filled with drunks, bums and junkies, and at one stage in my life, I too was a junkie. Sometimes I would wake up in the morning and take my first hit of heroin before setting off to work. I was addicted in the worst way, until this day, the day I looked death in his hollow sockets.



---



I had my lighter warming my golden concoction on the spoon, as it bubbled I knew it was ready to be injected in to my vein, allowing it to flow along with my blood and become a part of my body. I put the lighter down beside me and lifted up my syringe before sucking the heroin up in to my syringe and placed the warm spoon down along side my lighter before using my mouth to tighten the leather belt I had already strapped over my upper arm. I found a spot on my arm that was not already blotched with old track marks and I slowly slid the cold, hard metal needle in to my arm, as I pushed down on the plunger I felt better, I felt as if the whole world was now a better place, and yet, at the same time I had a feeling of self pity, I felt lower than any point in my life. I felt the warm syrup sliding up in to my shoulder area until slowly the warmth subsided, I sighed and lent my head back on the wall, and as I looked up there he stood. His hollow eye sockets bore down on me.



Stand up it commanded, and against
my own free will, I did as I was directed.



Ernie French? it asked me



"Yes." I slurred a little as I felt the effects of the heroin begin to kick in.
I am Death, I have come to take you to your final destiny. It told me, I didn't understand at first, but after a few moments I began to comprehend its words.



Follow me it said, raising its grey finger and beconning me towards it. I stood and walked towards Death, the room faded to black and became hot, hotter than anything I had experienced in my entire life. As the room faded back in I realised that I was no longer in my apartment, I was in fact in a chamber of hell. I looked to my right to see it gazing out across the sea of tortured souls.



This is where you will be staying
it told me, pointing out across the fiery pit.



“No,” I muttered, “No, it can't be.”- I wouldn't believe, I couldn't.



Unless, it said You begin living a better life, immediately.



“Yes.”; I promised, but my word was not good enough.



Prove it. It commanded
