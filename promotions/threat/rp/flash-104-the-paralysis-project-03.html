the.paralysis.project.03
Starring: Vincent Pembridge & the product of destiny

Date: 30 May 2003
Location: Atlanta, GA; USA


Getting up from his seat, Vincent drummed the fingers of his right hand against the table, preparing himself for the confrontation. He'd done so much in his time on God's green planet, but the thought of snapping up a prospective ally to unleash hell on an innocent bystander's life almost made him break out in goosebumps, and freeze in nervousness. He couldn't explain it, but on some odd level, the Scorpion enjoyed the feeling.

Because, in his mind, it served as somewhat of a premonition. That the plan would work out accordingly, and be a huge success. The months of seclusion after the horrors of that fateful stretch of time in November last year made Pembridge advance himself in ways never thought possible. He mingled here and there, and mapped his life out to the smallest detail.

Living a life like a pariah would DOES have its benefits.

Finishing up his order of Pimms No. 5 and wiping his mouth clean with his bare hand, Vincent Pembridge cleared his throat. It was just about time to get down to work, and the British Degenerate was practically trembling from excitement. Standing up, he slowly made his way over to the bar, and toward the man that had just entered. For some reason, that man was just about to embark on a journey he could have never imagined.

How would you like it if your destiny was planned by shady character you don't even know?

You wouldn't, of course. But in the case of Lawrence Neumeyer, he would grow to love it.

Vincent watched as Lawrence got a mug of beer and a bowl of salted almonds from the bartender, who began to engage in a little conversation with other patrons at the bar counter. Lawrence himself didn't bother thanking the 'tender, immediately chomping down on his almonds.

The Scorpion cracked a smile, and readied himself.

"Hey, cunt. You're eating salted almonds in a place like this? Pathetic... must be one of 'em redneck faggots, eh? All talk and no action during the day, and at night, you shove SALTED ALMONDS down your throat!" Vincent started to mumble with a deep voice, attempting to portray a drunk loser, as he stumbled to the counter and sat on the stool next to Lawrence.

Neumeyer looked over his shoulder and at Vincent, staring at the man who'd just slagged off at him. All the other people at the counter, having known Neumeyer as a regular of the pub for the last couple of months, shushed themselves. This was truly unexpected for them; hardly anybody ever caused trouble in the pub. The last time there was anyone who acted stupid, the owner of the establishment -- Jacken -- smashed many a beer bottle over the troublemaker's head and made sure that each & every single patron of the pub on the night got to do the same.

That was ten months ago.

"Barry, I think this fella here is piss-drunk. Better get a cab for him, because from the stories that you've told me, Boss wouldn't be too kind to this fucker if this little situation gets out of trouble."

See, Lawrence used to be in the United States Army, and for a while, was a critical figure in one of the nation's quests to bring peace to a riotous part of the world. But after several problems here and there, he was unceremoniously booted out. Needless to say, Neumeyer was devastated by the entire deal, and lived his life a bitter man from then on.

Barry Hyna, the bartender on duty, chuckled as the other customers at the bar laughed. Lawrence's words went down well with him, and Vincent Pembridge continued to act drunk, swaying from side to side on his stool, ignoring the laughter directed at him. It was only the first course, as far as the British Degenerate was concerned.

Contact had been established. An understanding of how his target's cognitive framework operated was attained.

All systems go, then.

"Come on, ye pussy. I basically compare you to yer mother and all ya got in response is a bloody verbal degrading? Which bloody sucked, by the way. Look at you. Big, strong...

But with a brain obviously as small as some of those almonds."

The only cackle of laughter that could be heard now was that of Vincent's, as he continued to provoke Lawrence. A few of the regulars at the counter moved away, now fearing a fight would follow. Lawrence wasn't one to control his temper, and the fact that his mother had just been insulted for no apparent reason gave rise to the notion that the former Army guy was about to snap.

Neumeyer stopped eating his almonds and stared at his mug of beer. He hadn't touched it yet, and now couldn't. There was an annoying prick sitting next to him, totally intoxicated and barking up the worst alley he'd stumbled upon.

"Listen, fucker. I'm going to give you ten seconds to get your ass out of here. We don't need no drunk Brit idiots in 'ere, begging for an ass-whooping. Quietly leave, and all will be okay."

Vincent sniggered at the response, while doing a little dance inside. Lawrence had played nicely into his trap.

"Pah! I shoulda guessed. Yet another Yank pussy."

Lawrence Neumeyer closed his eyes and clenched his fist. Vincent had just blurted out the magic words, as far as he was concerned. Sure, Lawrence didn't even know the name of the British man sitting next to him and spewing rubbish from his mouth. But proud of his past history in the United States Army, Neumeyer wasn't about to let a drunkard continue spitting out derogatory comments. Not of his mother, not of his adopted homeland.

*CRACK*

Can you say... FIGHT?
