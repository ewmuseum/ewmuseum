Past and Present [5]
Starring: The Cursed One

He stepped up beside her quietly, making sure that his shoulder didn’t nudge her or have him slip on the snow and collide with her back. After carefully walking over, leaving small imprints of his feet to be filled over by the impending snow. The man standing behind him only smirked and raised his eyebrow. No need to pay this stranger any attention.

What does he know?

With his arms wrapped up tightly in his trench coat, he gently crossed his right over his left and sat them down on the surface of the wall. The same wall he found the young lady sitting on next to him. He looked up and saw her curly red hair catching patches of snow, some what glowing in the street light but far too dark to see any real radiance. But, from what he could see, she was quite attractive.

That’s how things were for a few minutes or so. He just stood their calmly, tapping his thumbs together and staring out over the edge, trying to guess the spot she was gazing at. She didn’t move, and, except for occasional blinks of her eyes and her light breathing, you couldn’t tell she was truly alive. The man behind them stared the whole time, but he didn’t move either.

Took him awhile to think of something to say, but when it came out, he didn’t realize that what he just said took no thought at all.

“So what’s your name?”

He could feel the man’s eyes, shooting at his back, staring a hole through him and thinking that he was nothing by an idiot who had no idea of what he was doing. But, he knew that she wasn’t looking at him. Her head never jerked and her eyes didn’t move.

After a moment of silence, he decided that he was going to walk away. He wasn’t needed, and felt kind of weird thinking that he could help. But, just as he lifted his arms to leave and walk back down those stairs, she answered.

“Nicole.”

“So you defeated Twizzy after all. Now, where I’m confused is that you make this sound like it’s a tragedy or something. Like you were going to lose your balls or something if you won this match. Shouldn’t something like that make a man happy?”

From his some what uncomfortable position on the ledge, he glanced over at the man, who was kneeling on the wall with his arms crossed, right above the left. There was no sunlight yet, but it was inching closer towards daylight so that would be changed soon. For now, all the light that was seen was the street lamps below. Just like that day, long ago. Except there was snow.

He realized, ironically, that this was the same position he had been in all those years ago, except with him on the other side.

“Yeah, of course it should. But that was just it – it didn’t make me happy. Because I didn’t know what it intended until now.”

He could feel the man’s eyes, raising an eyebrow and trying to fathom the words of a man he had only met moments ago, who was sitting on the edge of a building he had only seen once before in his life for no particular reason at all. But he didn’t care about what this man thought of him.

What does he know?

“So, who’d you have to face next?” he asked, sounding a little irritated and tired.

He just nodded, and decided to humor the man.

“Name’s Randall Alder-Tangk. But in the fed he’s known simply as R.A.T.”

“Yeah, he’s a professional wrestler all right.” he started, shaking his head and grunting a little. “They always have a weird name like that. Trying to be original and thinking they’re smart, but then making up such a dumb name like that.”

He laughed under his breath, not agreeing to what he had just said but rather preparing himself for the same mild embarrassment. He then realized that it was only a matter of seconds before he asked the same question that he knew was coming. And if he thought R.A.T. was a bad name…

“So hey, what your so-called name in wrestling?”

“blind.”

“blind?” he asked, and looked over at him again. “That’s it?”

“Yep.”

He watched as the man stood there, laughing his ass off and shaking his head, and then had the sudden urge to drive a fist into his face. But he didn’t, and the thought went away after a few minutes, replaced by a small smile and a recurring thought that made it all make sense.

After all, what does he know?
