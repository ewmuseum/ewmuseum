Elijah Upclose: With Phoenix Rose -Pt 1-
Starring: Christian Elijah feat Phoenix Rose

*RING*

"Who the fuck would this be?" wondered Phoenix Rose.

Christian Elijah?

"How's it going man?" asked Elijah. He could sense the smile on Rose's face.

"Hey! I'm doing fine. How are you?"

"Pretty good. Dude, I have a question for you."

"Oh my god! What?!"

Exactly. What could Elijah ask Phoenix Rose? Why would somebody who "has it all" ask somebody who doesn't? Proof: Looks, charisma, wrestling skills, a hot chick that loves him, a nice paycheck from thReat. The other a dumbass with no charisma, nor looks, nor a hot chick that would blow him like an anxious kid blowing balloons.

However, Phoenix had the experience of winning gold. Something that Elijah had never experienced.

Elijah had a match against Chris McCluksey on Flaw25. The winner would take a step closer to the International Title. Elijah felt worried. Would he be able to finally win his first title?

"Spit it out bitch! I know, I know! You're gonna ask me what sex position I would bang Tarah with right?! No problem man, a lot of chicks like the reverse cowboy..."

"I'll keep that in mind," answered Elijah with a chuckle, "but my question is something about titles."

"Titles?"

"About winning one. You've won a handful of titles, while I have never won one yet. Sad ain't it?"

Phoenix Rose smiled. An opportunity to brag. "I'll you some of the major titles I've won, man. LOW Tag title... OWL Outright Title... cW Continental Title... and yeah, I won the EYE Gold Title in a tournament."

There was a pause, before Phoenix Rose continued, "And you're in a tournament, aren't ya? Dude, it's all about determination... just believe in yourself. There is no room for failure. Do-or-die. That's the basis of a tournament."

"Haha, I see you anticipated that question. Ok, here's another: How did it feel when you won the EYE Gold Title? Considering you won it from a tournament, I'm curious."

Another pause. Phoenix Rose scratched his head. This was quite a difficult question. "Erm, of course I was overjoyed. Especially since I won it from a tournament. It made me feel like I was the cream of the crop, and hey, don't worry man. When you strike gold, it gets easier. Trust me."

That was pretty good advice, thought Elijah. It was like what that dude from Rich Dad, Poor Dad said. The rich get richer, while the poor get poorer. It was sort of like a snowball effect. One good thing would lead to another.

"Ok, how about... retaining a title? This one's for the future, if you know what I mean?"

Phoenix Rose laughed. "Retaining a title huh? I dunno. I guess I feel pressured, because every title match, I know I'm putting something on the line while my opponent has nothing to lose. But it feels good when you win, because you're still the champ. But hey, one step a time man! Just win the title first, aye?"

"Alright, thanks for the advice man. I'll see you around."

"Good luck man."

*CLICK*

Elijah pondered on Phoenix's words. It was as if, to put it in another way, it was as if it was like the first orgasm. One of the best feelings to ever experience while subsequent orgasms were still cool, but not as... pleasurable as the first.

Mmm... orgasm.
