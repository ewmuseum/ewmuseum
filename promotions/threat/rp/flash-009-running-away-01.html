running.away.01
Starring: Vincent Pembridge

Location: Madrid, Spain
Date: 17 September 2002

Madrid. The hearbeat of Spain. Madrilenos, as the city's residents are called, seem to spend most of their time eating, drinking and enjoying life. Their long lunches are legendary, they spend their evenings hopping from tapas bar to tapas bar, and eventually; sometimes as late as midnight; they get around to eating dinner.

"Cunts!" Vincent Pembridge muttered under his breath, as he dragged his huddled self through the busy wet market. It was evening, and the wet market was even busier than it was in the morning.

The cynics in Spain think this breezy approach to life has something to do with Madrid's main industry; government. But don't confuse Madrilenos' appreciation for living with a bureaucratic, slothful or superficial nature. They love passion and drama. From El Greco's mystical paintings to the mournful beauty of flamenco to the intense pas de deux of matador and bull. If you stop in at any of the city's countless bars, restaurants and cafes or simply pause to listen to the troubadours as you're walking the narrow streets of old Madrid, you'll see how the city reflects a little bit of each of Spain's varied cultures.

It is truly the heart of Spain.

"Bollocks!" Vincent cursed as he finally got himself out of the wet market and made his way down to the pavement, that would eventually lead to his home at the moment. An apartment with a grouchy Spanish lady. Attractive and young. But VERY grouchy. Vincent's blood boiled at the thought of her, as she would continuously nag for The Scorpion to do various things around the cramped apartment. Even when it was clean.

Oh yes, of course. It had neglected to be mentioned; she was somewhat crazy.

He had been in Madrid for eleven days. Had the apartment for eight days. And was still fearful. Unsure of whether Madrid was THE place for him. He had been in Belgium for the prior two weeks but that wasn't right. There was no way he could have stayed there. Not because of the country itself, or the people who seemed constipated

Sikanah would have caught him.

And truth be told, Vincent knew deep inside that he didn't have what it took to deal with a man like The Thai Warrior. Sikanah was dangerous and very secretive. A man who you would rather have as your ally. Of course, he would decide whether you were HIS enemy or HIS friend. Sikanah determined his own destiny, his own goals. But he couldn't have anticipated how easily his aimed takeover of tFZ crumbled. Only two people could have been blamed for that, although the blame squarely felt on one of the two.

Vincent Pembridge & Karen Pembridge.

And considering the way The British Degenerate handled matters after the big blowoff, he couldn't blame Sikanah for being out for blood. Hence the escape from the USA. Which triggered Sikanah to give up whatever he had been working for during the course of the past few years, simply to get a taste of British blood. Vincent's blood.

Walking slowly, Pembridge began to reflect on the past few months. The rollercoaster ride it was. And how, now, he was on the run from a deranged lunatic. Add to that, the misery of living in Madrid as a poverty-stricken foreigner. He simply had to get some money, but it was tougher than it was expected. The Manchester native would have to rely on the nightlife of Madrid.

There's almost more to do in the wee hours than in the daytime, and traffic jams at 4 AM aren't unusual. Bars tend to shut down around 3 AM, but some discos and clubs are open past breakfast time. During the summer months, terraza bars spring up along busy Calle Castellana in front of Plaza de Colon & Plaza de Cibele,s and are open until early morning. Cafe theaters provide a variety of shows, and you can readily find live music, from flamenco to salsa. Flamenco, especially, is very important in Madrid. In this genre are some 60 classical songs and dances, some of them performed solo, some in groups, some with instrumental accompaniment, some a cappella. The dancing includes much swirling of skirts and stamping of heels. From about 9 pm on, young people flock into the area of Bilbao-Malasana: There's plenty of rock 'n' roll, hip-hop, punk rock and other sounds to delight the independent music fan.

And then, there are the taverns which, on certain nights, turn into a fighter's paradise. Bloodshed, violence, and all that jazz.

Vincent grinned at remembering the travel guide he had read many days ago, approaching his apartment. He'd have to rely on an old trade to make some money. Only thing that stood in his way was the folklore of a legendary bar-fighter who would maim any newcomer to the scene. Most wouldn't stay on after the maiming, some would... only to be degraded and cursed at during every fight.

The legendary fighter's name was Santos Salvatore. Didn't frighten Vincent Pembridge one bit, however. The Scorpion Of Manchester DID defeat him, when they were in the Outback Fighter's Guild together.

"Bloody lovely."
