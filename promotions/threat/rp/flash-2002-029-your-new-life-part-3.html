'Your New Life!' part 3
Starring Riot

Taken from the journal of Timothy Rollins: December 1st, 1992

Greetings Reader,

In what probably the greatest day of my life, I debuted today!

This was what it was all about: learning to take bumps, how to not hurt the other guy in the ring with me, all the little tips and tricks of how to put a good match together... this was it's culmination. Tonight, tonight I would wrestle.

The match went down in some high school in northeast Ohio, Wooster High I believe. Anyway, that's a moot point on any level. My opponent was one of the bigger guys in the company, I'm talking like on a Shawn Michaels or Bret Hart level here. This guy was tops and I had the distinct privilege of debuting against him... yay. If I remember right, his name was Adam Lydon, and I was his bitch. Lydon was huge, he stood at an impressive six foot eight, weighing somewhere around two hundred and eighty pounds. I mean, this guy was a beast, he made me look small and that is an impressive task in any shape or form.

As can be expected, I lost the match but I didn't care. This was my launching pad, this is where I would start the quest for the money, power and women. This was were I would start my pursuit of reaching the next level, and the level after that, and the level after that... until I've gone so far that I am the one that makes the next level. I will be bigger than Flair or Hogan.

Anyway, I guess I did well for a person that was as 'green' as I was. The match really got over with the fans, I may have even earned some respect of the guys in the back. That is, if they even bothered to pay attention to me in the ring. Not that I really care, I was on cloud nine the entire time. The adrenaline you get from wrestling is incredible, like nothing I ever experienced before. It's like your god in the ring and nothing, am I mean nothing can bring you down.

Wrestling is better than drugs.

The fans are something to behold, they understand that this isn't going to be the kind of wrestling they watch on TV, and they know that there aren't going to be big talent names walking out of the locker room and into the gym. And they still don't care, they're here for the wrestling. There here to latch on to ever snap mare or drop toe hold you do. They're here to cling onto every word that you will spew forth so you 'get over'. They're here to elevate you to the big leagues, make you're a household name. The fans are king and they know it, they'll never let you forget.

I am going to be a 'face', or fan favorite, when I get my bearings. Bob says that I'm going to become one of the major players in the company because some of the guys are being shipped off to development federations to get honed for the big leagues. Lucky bastards.

Sadly, though, I must be going now. I know you were probably wishing for more, but I'm aching (as can be expected) from the match and I need to go ice myself up so I'm ready for my match on Friday. I promise more in the next entry. Until next time.

-Tim.
