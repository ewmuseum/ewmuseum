Back To Work.
Starring: Figure it out Yourself

People stood, lined up against the walls of the small hallways inside an apartment complex, thier eyes locked on the person slowly making his way through them towards his destination. The person held a camcorder in one hand, capturing the gazes from many of the people he passed.

Every corner he came around brought on a new wave of people, and into them a new wave of fear. Not so much for him, but more so for the dagger he held in his other hand, dangling it in front of the camcorder.

He turned the final corner of his journey and came to a sudder stop at he stood at a door. He dagger lowered a came back up a moment later, this time with a peice of paper attached to the blade. It was quickly shoved into the door as a slight laugh could be heard behind the camera.

"That should get his attention."

Zoom.

To: Anubis

From: Your friends at thReat

The camcorder then turned off, as the man behind it made his way back through the hallways, and back to his normal early morning routines.
