Outside. Looking In.
Starring: Riot

His life had become shambles. An unsolvable enigma. He was once one of the most recognizable faces on thReat's wrestling circuit, destined for bigger and better things. Timothy Rollins was to become nothing short of a god. To stand tall among all the greats that professional wrestling had ever showcased. It was his dream. It was what he sacrificed his body, mind, and soul to the business for. He had paid his dues time and time again. Every time, with each new federation that he would come under contract with, his work ethic in the ring and backstage attitude had garnered high remarks on the independent and professional wrestling circuits. Everyone wanted a piece of the pie that was Tim Rollins. He chose thReat. An upstart promotion that featured hybrids of fighting and wrestling, the future of the business, he thought. This would be perfect for his image, here he could get his name out with the unique style that was thReat. As with any case, he worked his body to the bone and made his way through the ranks at a breakneck pace. Soon, he would become the forefront of thReat's wrestling division. The pride and envy of any many to ever lace up a pair of boots and climb in the ring to avoid the squabbles of his nine-to-five lifestyle.

That never happened. Several backstage conflicts led to a large number of the talent just walking out on the company. It was left for dead, a sinking ship that Timothy never had the chance to get off of. It seemed that the innovation that was thReat was no more. It was simply "yesterday's news". A passed craze. Timothy Rollins was cast out into the cold, dead world. And what of him now?

Now, he was a shell of his former shelf. The world which he had held so dear had turned it's back on him. Just like all the times before, from day one, Timothy Rollins was not allowed to know what true happiness was. Personal or professional. His family hated him for his career decisions and several rumors about him saw to it that he never secured a stable federation to work in. He had done freelance work for several independent circuits, riding the wave of his thReat status until that wave slammed hard against the shore. Now he had nothing. He had pinched enough of his small paychecks to keep himself clothed and fed, but other than that he had little else. No friends to speak of and no solid home. Outside of the occasional motel, a "perk" as he often referred to it, he spent several nights in his car. Always hoping, above all else, that he would be able to find a temporary job come morning. Sometimes his prayers were answered, other times, fate just laughed it up at the cruel joke that had become Timothy Rollins' life.

He was an outcast to the world that once embraced him. Always spitting on him and ready with the next hurdle to be thrown in his way. He would never wish his life on anyone, not even his most hated enemy. Rollins had little left, he's running dry. Pray for him. Pray with your heart and soul.

Then, one day, Threat came back. The world rejoiced with a triumphant "hooray!" Timothy had somewhere he could finally turn. All was well in the world.

Or was it?

His psyche is in tatters. Ready to snap and rebel against itself at a moments notice. Rollins can't even be safe in the recesses of his mind, it's ready to ruin his life just like all the others did. His mind is a ticking time bomb. What will cause it to go off? Who can cause it to go off? It is already too late?

And what of Threat Wrestling? Can the man they call 'Riot' really trust the same company that left him for dead not that long ago?

Love me.

Hate me.

It doesn't matter anymore.

I am what you want me to be.

I am the end of all things to come.

I have nothing to loose and everything to gain.

Pray for my mercy.
