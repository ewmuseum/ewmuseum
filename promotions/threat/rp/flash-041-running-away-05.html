running.away.05
Starring: Vincent Pembridge

Date: 30 Sep 2002
Location: Madrid; Spain


Vincent dragged himself out of the bathroom, his face having been splashed with water, in an attempt to fully rouse himself from his sleepiness. It had been a tiring night; scratch that, it had been a tiring couple of nights, but The Scorpion was enjoying his life at the moment. Money and his own safety were the biggest concerns to him, and they had been taken care of.

He took a glance at his bed, and grinned. A part of his mind screamed at him for doing what he had done, but really, it wasn't a big deal. Vince didn't initiate things, Rosita & Angique did. He was just mesmerised by the occasion, and went with the flow. Any normal man would have done the same.

As he continued to gaze at the two sisters sleeping peacfully in his bed barenaked, sans for the covers shielding their lower half of their bodies, the Brit recollected the experience he had with both ladies. It was truly remarkable; when he first moved in, Rosita didn't give two shits about him. But after seeing the Scorpion's body in the flesh, and watching him fight... Rosita and sister Angique threw caution to the wind. Both wanted him, both wanted to retain the kinship of each other. No man should come in between their relationship as sisters, was what both ladies agreed upon.

Hence, group action.

The Scorpion snapped himself out of the flashback of his romp with Rosita & Angique, and took a fleeting glance at his bedside clock. It was already eight in the morning, and he had work to do. Grumbling, Pembridge buckled his pants and walked out of his room, into the living room where his laptop was. He wasted no time in sitting down on the couch and booting up his laptop, which he'd recently acquired.

He'd recently managed to buy a plethora of fancy things, due to his new role as Champion at Casa El' Gavriel. Laptop, new clothes, stocks in the European market, emergency airline tickets to wherever he wanted should he have to flee; Vincent Pembridge was making good use of his windfall.

But it wasn't because he was a fighting champion at a local tavern.

No. It was because the tavern was a front for massive illegal activity in Spain, Portugal, and Northern Italy. Bernard Cavalera was a man who prided himself on being immersed in many different cultures, and he had to start small. Portugal & Italy were the two countries he saw as Spain's biggest help, in terms of militiary aid. And seeing how the people he worked for were huge figures in the Spanish Militiary Association, it was only natural to branch out to these two countries.

And in the process, set up his own little business. Once he got bigger than life, he would be able to overthrow the bigwigs he slaved for. And that's where the muscle in Vincent Pembridge came in. The added bonus was that Vinny had brains as well. A frightening combination, but one that would aid Cavalera in his bid to become the most powerful in the land.

After all, that's what everyone wants, yes? Power. The intangible thing that can cause so much harm if in the wrong hands, but bring so much good if in the right hands. It can't be grasped or manifested into something physical, but the ramifications of its usage are as real and tangible as most things in the world.

Vincent sighed as the interface for his laptop finally loaded. He arched forward, and grabbing the mouse, started clicking away. His eyes were focused on the screen; the same kind of intensity that was evident while fighting at the Casa. And for the past eight days, ever since dethroning Santos as champion, Vincent had taken on all challengers to his crown. Altogether, including the fight with Santos, The Scorpion had fought fifteen times.

And won all those times.

"Okay, so now I have to bloody rig some games... this should be easy."

Part of the contract which allowed Vincent to be Cavalera's personal weapon of destruction was for the Manucian to fix soccer games, for the country's top soccer league, La Liga. Having done matchfixing on many occasions before, this was nothing new for Pembridge. Whilst in the UFF, he fixed various NFL & NHL games. And while in the tFZ, he made it a point to screw around with certain clubs in the English Premiership, although his influence wasn't so great then.

Having played the role of a hitman for the last week, this was a refreshing change for Vincent. Something he relished.

---

"So, Mr Cavalera, I realise the plan is in full swing?"

Bernard's office, at Casa El' Gavriel. It was a little too early to be open for business, but deliveries had to be made, and Bernard was there to oversee everything. Sleeves rolled up, a bottle of whiskey already opened for his drinking pleasure. His table was covered with documents, strewn about haphazardly, but Cavalera was too tired to bother about being organised.

He had business to discuss with this stranger. Whom he'd communicated with solely via e-mail over the course of the prior two weeks. Now, he was here, in the flesh... and wanting to see the payoff to his meticulously-planned scheme.

"Yeah, it is. When do you want it to climax?"

Cavalera turned to his right, and towards the man, who was hiding in the shadows. He didn't want to risk being seen, although Bernard wouldn't recognise him in any case. Still, he felt secure if nobody knew he even existed. Until, of course, it was time.

Bernard, despite not being able to fully see the face of the man he was doing business with, saw the sadistic grin that basically spoke volumes, and effectively answered the question. Chuckling, Bernard grabbed his cellphone off his table, and punched in a couple of numbers, before putting the phone to his ear.

"Vincent? Big fight tonight. And by big...

...I mean huge."
