ITT 1: What Will The Pills Say?
Starring: Twizzy

The news had come to him an hour earlier. He’d received a phone call. Twizzy would be in the International Title Tournament for the thReat organization. The winner of said tournament would gain the thReat International Title.

Twizzy stared blankly at the television screen. Just before the phone call he had taken three Twistodium pills. They had been good and sweet and he’d taken them with lemon water. The drink was finished by now. He had been rather high off the pills, not the news, since the phone call and the phone call had been the basis of his thoughts since.

Twizzy was scared.

He was paranoid.

Today the drug made him that way.

Twistodium is a rare kind of breed. It’s harder to classify than alcohol or pot or cocaine. You can’t call it a depressant or a stimulant or whatever. Sometimes it depresses you. Sometimes it excites you. Sometimes it paralyses you. You just never know. It always makes you powerful, though. Twizzy has found ways to create what he wants it to do. Mixing it with thing, chasing it with things, eating before a meal, eating after. They all work their magic into the body and then the pill will do something else.

Today the drug made Twizzy paranoid.

So he sat on his burgundy couch and watched the third episode of the day of Wings on the USA network. It was the one about renting out uncomfortable cold airport floor to masochists. It wasn’t very funny.

He wasn’t focusing on the show though. He was focusing on thReat and thReat’s tournament. Twizzy wanted this. He new he did and he would try, because he was a brave man. But Twizzy was scared. It was mostly the drug. He could do it. He could win. He could do it.

But would he?

The drug would wear off, sure. Then he’d be fine and could do it.

But he would certainly take another pill.

And another.

And another.

And another.

Twizzy was addicted. Remember?

And what would hold for that day? The day of the tournament. What will the pills do to him then? On that day, what will the pills say?
