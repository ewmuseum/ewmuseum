Story Through Theme Music Four: "Show Me How To Live" - Audioslave
Starring: Twizzy

Ahmmm…

One day never came. It would never be here. One day was nothing and Twisted eventually, like everyone else, realized that. One day… was just a dream.

And with the early dawn moving right along
I couldn’t buy an eyeful of sleep.
And with the aching night under satellites
I was not received.
Built with stolen parts, telephone in my heart.
Someone get me a priest
To put my mind to bed, this ringing in my head.
Is this a cure or is this a disease?

‘These creatures! These creatures don’t know what they are doing! I am lost. I know that now. But the rest of them. They are young. They can be saved from this vicious merciless beast!’ Wrestling… wrestling was hell. Wrestling was nothing Twisted wanted to be involved in. He was here and there was no going back. For years he had fallen into the propaganda that a wrestler was able to get somewhere in the business. For years he had failed and proven that theory wrong. Twisted was nothing and had nothing to show for his misfortune. The industry was a demon.

Nail in my head
From my creator!
You gave me life
Now show me how to live.
Nail in my head
from my creator!
You gave me life
Now show me how to live.

He was stuck in wrestling, but others were savable. Other wrestlers still had options. This new generation needed to leave… now. They needed to get away from wrestling as soon as possible. Twisted would preach. He was the perfect example of how this industry was a demon. They all needed to realize what a wrong road they chose. Twisted was stuck here, unsavable, but the rest of them needed to go.

And in the afterbirth on the quiet Earth.
Let the stains remind you.
You thought you made a man, you better think again
Before my role defines you.

No one would listen. No one thought Twisted was right, but there were people who needed to get ahead. There were people who felt the industry was wrong, but these though they could change it. Twisted wanted to save them, but they were equally stuck in this hellhole. They were lost souls and Twisted was the most lost. They became a militia of lost souls. The Lost Soul Militia was in GCW.

Nail in my head from my creator!
You gave me life
Now show me how to live.
Nail in my head
from my creator!
You gave me life
Now show me how to live.

The Lost Soul Militia looked for young guns and Twisted always told them that they must leave at once. He told them he would help them. Trying to coax them out of GCW, out of wrestling. LSM tried what they could and did whatever was necessary, but were never successful. LSM was a group trying to break wrestling’s glass ceilings and trying to take out others in the process for everyone’s own good. LSM did none of those things.

And in your waiting hands I will land and roll out of my skin.
And in your final hours I will stand ready to begin.
Ready to begin.
Ready to begin.
Ready to begin.

“The Dark Messiah” Jason O’Neil and Twisted both did do some good though. They became bigger and more powerful. They won the Tag Team Titles of GCW. They had their differences though and that would be the wedge that destroyed LSM. There was a Tag Team Title match, which saw The Grim Reaper and Jason O’Neil face off against Twisted and Misdirection. Twisted and Misdirection won, but that team lost the titles soon after as well. Twisted was still lost, but now was alone, which was probably better. His misery would not go onto anyone else.

Nail in my head from my creator!
You gave me life
Now show me how to live.
Nail in my head
from my creator!
You gave me life
Now show me how to live.
Show me how to live!
Show me how to live!
Show me how to live!
Show me how to live!

Twisted gave up on his preachings and ended up injured because of his knee. He went to the trainer and that meeting changed his entire life.
