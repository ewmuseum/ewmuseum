RAH Issue#1 Vol.2-"Shrimpin' Ain't Easy"-
Starring: Rent-A-Hero

Our story begins in the midst of a starlit Miami night, the clubs and beaches filled with wild activity as the city ceases to slow down, even as night blankets the city. However, the Miami police are too concerned with what’s going on in those hotspots…leaving everything else a virtual playpen for those of the evil persuasion.

And evil was ready for recess.

It seemed like a normal night for Frank Conners, a security guard at the Miami Shrimping Company for the past 10 years. Pudgy and lazy, the nightshift at Miami Shrimping was perfect, seeing as how no foul fiend usually ranks a seafood company as a target of interest. 8 hour nights of piece and quiet, watching “Cheaters” on the 12 inch TV in the snack lounge were the usual routine and he couldn’t ask for anything more.

Frank took longing breathes as he ached for a soft seat, patrolling the boxing area. His trusty Maglite was right there, chasing the darkness away with it’s regal light before he’d dare to step forward. Accusing Frank of being courageous certainly wouldn’t be a good observation. His patrol was nearing it’s end and it was about time for his “reward”…a bear claw drizzled in icing and an hour of late night Jerry Springer. With his mind on donuts and out of control drag queens, Frank retraced his steps through the catacomb of crates in the boxing area to take his leave.

!~PLINK~!

Frank’s rotund frame span around with surprising quickness, his flashlight moving wherever his eyes roamed. They finally locked upon one of the windows which was apart of a row lining the boxing room, that window now broken and in miniscule shards upon the floor. Frank’s heart raced and sweat crept down the flabby folds of skin on his face. He knew someone was in here and he didn’t know what to do….

Until it was too late. The very turn he made to exit the boxing room lead to his downfall, as a fist emerged from the thick darkness and struck Frank fiercely. The blow sent him crashing to the floor, his Maglite escaping his hand and smashing against the cold floor….his only symbol of safety, lost. The world around him went dark as his senses gave away and he fell into a forced sleep.

Later…

The world started off as a blur, slowly taking shape until Frank found himself awake….and tied up over a vat of boiling hot peanut oil. His eyes jolted wide with shock as the toes of his Dockers dangled dangerously close to the oil, for he knew a swim in that vat meant a certain and agonizing death.

“Don’t take it personal my friend, but even innocent fish are caught up in a sea poacher’s net,” remarked an off-screen voice reeking of a Cajun accent. Frank turned his head to the right, noticing a man decked out in high leather boots, blue tights, a black and white stripe shirt, eye patch, and a bandanna with shrimp imprints upon it. “Your…your….,” the mysterious man took this as his opportunity to introduce himself, not pleased with Frank’s stammering, ”Silly scallop! You have been captured by none other then the PRINCE OF PRAWNS….THE SCOUNDREL OF SHELLFISH …I AM THE CODFATHER!!!”

Some villain-esque plays in the background as both parties look about confused, continuing on shortly. “What do you want here? I mean…it’s a SHRIMPING factory for God sakes?! How low do you set your standards,” desperately asked Frank as is arms grew tense and tired, dangling over the bubbling grease. “Silence you fool! Seafood is an important commodity in today’s world….especially in Miami! When I take control over all the major sources of seafood, city hall will pay BILLIONS if they ever wish to dine on shrimp cocktail or lobster pasta EVER again! No one can stop…THE CODFATHER!! MUAHAHAHA,” manically ranted The Codfather as the aforementioned music plays again.

The Codfather looks about in annoyance yelling off-screen,”WHERE IS THAT DAMNED MUSIC COMING FROM?!” “Blow it our your ass man, I’m paying for college with this gig,” answered the off-screen man, playing the music yet again.

Frank snickered as the scoundrel of shellfish tried to calm himself down, noticing Frank’s snickering as he pulled out a shuriken in the shape of a prawn. “Laugh while you can, for after I have cleaned out Miami Shrimping for all it’s seafood goodness….I shall dip your rotund body into that vat of BOILING peanut oil, and you will make quite the hush puppy indeed. Courtesy of….THE CODFATHER!! MUAHAHA,” his obligatory evil monologue was yet again ruined by the evil music playing in the background, enraging the Codfather into hurtling his shuriken off screen.

!~THWACK~!

“AHH, MY EYE…MY FRICKING EYE,” yelled the music man as the them stopped abruptly. Smiling, the Codfather said, ”Ahh…so much better. NOW, I bid you adieu.” As Codfather’s hand neared a red lever, controlling the winch that Frank was dangling from, a bold voice yelled from nowhere,”TOUCH THAT SWITCH, AND PREPARE TO GET SLAPPED LIKE A BIZNITCH!”

Codfather’s mouth was wide open, he folded back his eye patch so he could confirm his fear with BOTH eyes. Turning around, that fear was confirmed.

A black and white Venom costume….honey blonde hair….aqua blue eyes….a chiseled granite body….a ivory smile…and a pack of Gummi Bears in hand with the seal broken….

He was about to have to answer to Rent-A-Hero.

WILL RENT-A-HERO SAVE FRANK FROM HIS BOILING PIT OF DOOM?! CAN HE STOP THE CODFATHER AND HIS SEAFOOD SCANDAL?! WHERE DOES HE FIND THOSE COSTUMES?! FIND OUT…SAME RENT-A-TIME…

SAME RENT-A-CHANNEL!!
