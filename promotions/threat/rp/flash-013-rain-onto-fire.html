Rain Onto Fire
Starring: Unknown

His body a silhouette against the backdrop of a grey sky, he stood on the rooftop, feeling the kiss of raindrops against his face. A world below him, his body took the position of a statue atop a church, like a gargoyle. No eyes saw this image, nor were any questions asked about his mysterious presence. None witnessed his own stance. And to speak the truth, no one cared.

A setting day already in progress, the remaining minutes of the afternoon drifted away, leaving only the night to keep him company. The rain collected in pools on the rooftop, forming a sea amongst the pebbles and the air vents. His face remained unscathed, no muscles succumbing to the chill of the wind. The only sign of his life was that of his own breath, appearing in shapeless mist around his head and floating into the sky.

Remembrance of the world speaks to his mind, as his hands form fists and his hair drips a mixture of rain and sweat. The sleeves of his trench coat acted as rivers, feeding waterfalls of rain that crashed down to the sidewalk below. This man just stood with his own thoughts, inconsiderate and callous. After all that had happened, all that was, he had been given a task by his God.

The God of reality

The day had died, as twilight stole the sunlight and buried it deep within an evening’s grave. Now he was all that remained of the sky’s image. The cornerstone of despair’s shadow, his eyes looked over the edge of the building, staring down into the crevice that was apart of the disfigured world. He watched as umbrellas walked along the pavement. As cars ravaged the puddles and sent water into the air. As streetlamps gave animation to the falling rain.

All of this through a collection of lies established long ago.

He reached inside his trench coat, making the first distinguishable movement in hours. His hands grasped a book that had silently been resting inside his pocket, virgin to the touch of the rainfall. Extending his hand, he let the book hang over the ten story drop, reading the two words on the cover.

Holy Bible

With drops collecting on the leather binding, he pulled his other hand out of his right pocket, revealing a black lighter. Using the book to shield the sensitive flame from vanishing, the heat of fire sank into the pages of the Bible. He could imagine the ink boiling, finally being turned into ashes like that of so many bodies.

Dreams came truer in those flames then in a million wishes. His soul reached out and laughed into the face of the Bible. He could feel the angels above crying down tears of blood, and reaching out with their arms to hold him and comfort him. He could only wish that he could spit in their faces, and rip off their wings.

All that he could do if he had but three wishes.

Shit on the steps of heaven.

Ice the walls of hell.

And send the Earth into an oblivion from which there was no escape.
When the book was half way through with its demise, he let go of the cover, watching as the fiery pages fell apart and floated to the cement below. He watched as the book dispersed, as the flames were chocked by the air, and as his influence on the world went up just a tad more then it had been before the sun had set.

His manipulation was nothing before. It would be something now.

The wind howled. The rain fell. The eyes watched.

And then, as he jumped down to crush the pebbles and splatter the pools, the world was back to normal.

As blind as it had been only moments ago.
