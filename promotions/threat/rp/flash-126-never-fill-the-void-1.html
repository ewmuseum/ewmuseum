never fill the void [1]
Starring: haTE

Match: haTE versus Rent-A-Hero.

Rent-A-WHAT? Okay seriously, I've got Rent-A-Center in the Quarterfinal of the International Title Tournament. I guess that's fine. So what if he's a little.. out there.

We all have our days, right? Our week? Hell our own fucking career?

Well, I guess it takes a lot to dress up in Halloween costumes that you buy at Wal-Mart and pretend to be a wrestler.

(Buzz)

That snapped him out of it again. haTE reached into his pocket and pulled out his new cellular phone. He turns it on. "Hello?"

Silence on the other end. haTE walks over to the window of his hotel room, trying to get better reception. "Hello?" Sill no answer from the other end. haTE takes it away from his ear, and looks at the number.

Unfimliar.

He shuts it off and shoves it back into his pocket. He keeps gazing out of the window, looking at the people bustling below. No more than a few seconds later-

(Buzz)

"Shit." haTE reaches back into his pocket, looking at the number again. Same one. He answers, this time a little short with the person, "Hello??"

This time, a broken voice can be heard, but the words are not understandable.

"What?" haTE askes. He cannot hear anything, and the other end goes dead again. Cut off. Only one of the greater sides of the technology of today.

haTE desides to try his luck outside. He goes out the door and into the elevator, which is probably slower than the stairs, seeing as how he is only 3 stories in the air.

(DING)

haTE steps off of the elevator. Hardly anyone is inside of the small hotel. Only the receptionist at the front desk, who sits in front of a small portible television that has very poor reception. He walked out of the double doors, and into the street's sidewalk. The sun beat down and the weather was blistering.

haTE walked down the street and past a few furniture stores and the sort. Finally, he came to what he was looking for. He turned right into the little Mini-Mart. Inside, empty besides the man that was sitting behind the desk.

Easy job. Sit behind a counter, selling chips, soda, and cigarettes. A/C all day long. You get to read even. haTE went back to the soda and picked out a 24 Ounce Mountain Dew: Code Red. He looked went up to the desk, and threw in a pack of Bubblelious. Two dollars and ten cents. haTE throws down the exact change and starts to walk out.

"Good day," haTE says as he walks away. "Whatever, man," response from the clerk. He gave haTe a dirty look, after actually looking at his face. Obviously, from the Japanese desent. haTE just sighs and shakes his head, walking out.

Hasn't been the first time since he has joined thReat that he has gotten the racist looks by the pigs that are living in the 1940's. It's not right- but will probably never change.

(BUZZ)

haTE pulls the phone out of his pocket again, the same number.
"Hello??" he says, answering. The reception is a little better this time, but not much.

"Hi-Shi-come-to-Jap-n-yo-da-is-h.."

"Woah, I can't hear you."

"Come-to-japan.. it's your-fath.." cut out. Dead. Fuck.

What a way to leave you hanging. Now, something about haTE's dad, and coming to Japan.

Here we go..

(BUZZ.)
Goes unanswered.. the shock still there.
