The Journey To The Top(Pt. 1 "It All Starts Here)
Starring: Mat Walton

Crack

Another stiff right hand connected with the wide open jaw of one Mat Walton. He came crashing down to the canvas, blooded and battered. Yet amazingly, Walton found a way to pull himself and come charging back at his much larger opponent.

BOOM!

Walton's five foot seven inch, one hundred and ninety-eight pound frame came crashing down on the canvas yet again after a huge clothesline sent him crashing to the ground.

"Get Up!" The larger man yelled at him.

Walton tried to pull himself using his opponent as support only to be kicked in the face and dropped back down to the mat yet again.

The man turned to another man sitting in a metal chair outside the ring.

"I told you I didn't have time for this crap Andy," The man spoke to the other man with a stern tone.


"Watch out," The man from the outside said in a low voice.

"What?' The man in the ring questioned

The man just pointed to the ring, and as this behemoth of a man turned around...

SMACK!

The telltale sound of leather boot meeting the butt of a persons jaw. Walton had just executed an excellent looking, and even better sounding standing side kick. His opposition collapsed to the mat in a heap.

"That's enough for now Matthew," The man from the outside called out as he climbed into the ring.

"So how did I do Uncle Andy?" The young man questioned the man we no know as his Uncle Andy.

The man removed his glasses to reveal a much different, much sterner looking Andrew Martin. He had been one of the top commodities in the World Wrestling Revolution, and to think that he was just nineteen years of age at the time of his greatest success. But he hadn't laced up a pair of boots since the promotion closed two years ago, and Martin was cut down in his prime. Slowly the stern look on Martin's face turned into a bit of a grin, and then into a chuckle.

"Not bad for a first day," Martin said to his Nephew.

Mat Walton stood only five feet seven inches tall and weighed in at one hundred and ninety-eight pound soaking wet with three winter jackets on, a toque, and steel-toed workboots. But what this young eighteen year old lacked in physical stature, he made up with his speed, agility, and amazingly his technical wrestling ability.

The larger man was just pulling himself up off the canvas and Martin turned to him

"Rule number one: Don't turn your back on a poodle because it'll turn into a pit-bull if you don't watch out," Martin said as the man collapsed once again.

Walton and Martin descended the stairs from the basement up into the main living area and quickly made their way into the kitchen and both grabbed a bottle of cold water from the fridge.

"Sit down," Martin instructed his young Nephew while pointing towards the kitchen table, Walton of course did what he was told.

"What's up?" He asked

"You looked good down there today, a little bit of a glutton for punishment but you looked good nonetheless,"

"Really?"

"You bet"

"Do you think I could be as good as you som----" Walton wasn't allowed to finish his sentence before being cut off by his Uncle.

"Whoa whoa whoa I don't know about being as good as me, but I think you could be pretty darn close," Martin said before taking a sip of his cold water.

A large, beaming smile quickly came over the young boy. He was complemented by his Uncle, someone who was a great wrestler just a few years ago and someone who would rarely give out compliments even to some of his closest friends, so to receive one from him would be an honour.

"You know what I like about you?" Martin said as he tossed his Nephew a towel to wipe the sweat from his face, "I like that you're the kind of kid that doesn't give up, even when everybody else thinks that you should and that's why you'll get somewhere in this business.

"What's that?" Mat said pointing to a flyer on the refridgerator door.

Martin turned around and plucked the flyer from the door and turned back to face his beloved Nephew.

"This?" Martin said, "This is our goal, it's the big showcase for up and coming performers," Martin paused for a second seeing the lock of wonder cross his young Nephew's eyes, "It's taking place in Calgary on July the seventh and I think with a couple months of work, I could get a hold of my contacts down in Calgary and hook you up with a match on the card." Martin concluded.

Everyone who was a wrestling fan knew that Calgary was the pinnacle of wrestling in Canada, maybe even the world. So many great technical wrestlers came out of that area, could he really be the next greatest thing to come out of there?

"So what are you waiting for?" Mat was snapped out of his daydream by the sound of his Uncle's voice.

"Huh?" Walton said somewhat bewildered.

"Well don't just sit there staring at me, we've got a lot of work to do so let's go!" Martin exclaimed.

Walton gave a quick nod and got up from his seat at the kitchen table , a sense of shock was still over him though. Was he really as good as his Uncle was making him out to be? Could he really be a big star? Could he really put on a good showing in front of the Calgary fans? Mat gave his head a quick shake before realising that only time would tell just how good he could be.

"This could be the beginning of a beautiful relationship," Walton said allowed to himself as he stop at the head of the stairs leading down to the basement.

"Let's go rookie!" He heard his Uncle call out from below.

Walton sighed, "Time to go to work I guess?"
