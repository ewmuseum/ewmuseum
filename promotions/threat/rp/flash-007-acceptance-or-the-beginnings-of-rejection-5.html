Acceptance? Or the beginnings of Rejection? [5]
Starring: Wolf & Rachel

Wolf had won, the pay check had just come in, and while we know Wolf was riding high, it was apparent that Rachel was just as elated. She paced around the room, some soft rock song playing in the background, as she patiently… or impatiently waited for her man to come home.

She had big news.

I walked through the door… and was met once again by Rachel leaping into my arms. We embraced as she began to shout something, rapidly, in my ear, leaving me… pretty much lost.

“What?”

Rachel: You’re first paycheck… it came through already. Wolfie, you’ve really made it.

I smiled. Not one of those custom smiles to please somebody, but one that came from within and just could not be stopped. Hearing those words, “You’ve made it” coming from the person you love, just made everything seem so worthwhile.

Rachel: You know… I had a crazy idea.

Crazy idea? From Rachel? I wasn’t expecting that.

Rachel: If you win again, this Sunday… let’s get married in Vegas.

“Say what?!”

Rachel: I’m serious. If you win on Sunday, let’s just go ahead and get married. We can easily afford the rings, and… I just don’t want to wait any longer.

I was shocked. Rachel had always been about the hughe wedding with all of our families there, and every friend we’ve had since high school, and the big ballroom, and the dancing, and all of that. Suddenly she wanted to have a rushed marriage? I codulnt help but think that she was putting er dreams away to accommodate my new life… and I couldn’t let her do that. Because I knew she would do the same for me.

“I dunno Rach. It just, it seems rushed. What about that big wedding we had planned. If I keep bringing home the cash, we can easily afford that in a few months time.”

Rachel’s face dropped, and it killed me inside. I hated seeing that look of disappointment on her face, and I usually tried everything to stop it. I’d move the world for this woman, and now I was the one causing her this pain?

Rachel: I guess so… I just… It was…

“Rachel… I just want you to think about it ok? I know how long you’ve dreamt of the perfect wedding, and I don’t want you just throwing it away. IF I win on Sunday, and IF you still wanna go ahead with the marriage… we’ll do it.”

And there it was. That beaming smile on her face.

Rachel: Great! I know you’ll win Wolfie. Submission match? That’s your speciality right?

“Yeah.”

The two hugged again, and during this perfect moment, that hint of doubt and fear crept back into Wolf’s mind. Not only was he wrestling for his pride, he had to win to keep Rachel happy. His mind played back all the gut wrenching moves Solex had brought out before Wolf got the victory and just wondered, was this all a trap by Solex?
