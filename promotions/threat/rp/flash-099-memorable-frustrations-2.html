Memorable frustrations [2]
Starring: Ernie French

Ernie stood, leaning on the plate glass door of the local doctors office. The warmth of the midday sun beamed down upon his back as he considered entering the doctor’s office. His palm became sweaty as he readjusted his grip on the long gold door handle. He considered the possibilities and repercussions that may occur should he go through with the task. He risked being labelled, losing his job at threat amongst many other things and as he tried to think out the possibilities he became frustrated, angry at himself and he took a step back from the door putting himself online with the brick wall that had the entrance door inset in to it. Ernie clenched his fist tightly and his teeth gritted and rubbed against one another as his vision became impaired as he saw nothing but red.

Ernie drove his fist hard n to the brick wall and winced back, grasping at his hand that now had minor cuts across the knuckles. At a later time a bruise would most certainly form, and at that moment Ernie knew that he had to enter the office, no matter what the repercussions would be.

Ernie stepped forwards and yanked the door open, entering a small square room which was around three feet wide by three feet long. A dark blue carpet covered the floor but was in bad condition and came up a little at the edge. Other than the carpet this small room was bare. A plain glass door, mirroring the one he had just entered through, stood directly in front of him and so he continued walking, opening the door and entering the next room. A grey thin carpet lay on the floor; small dirty stains were blotched around from the years of wear and tear it has taken. Along the wall to his right three reception desks lay. They were not the traditional desks, but rather they were a hole in a wall with a shutter above them so that once they wanted to close up they would pull the shutters down and that would be all. Between each ‘desk’ notice boards were nailed up, old pieces of paper dangled off them informing people about weekly lamas classes and alcoholic anonymous meetings. Ernie was here for a class with a very similar concept to those. Ernie was here to go to a group.

Ernie filed in behind a short elderly woman who smelled of something rather fusty. At the desk a single mother stood waiting to take her number before going in to the waiting room. Her young male toddler ran around making incomprehensible noises and yet, somehow, his mother understood him. She left the desk and grabbed his hand, leading him through the painted grey doors at the far end of the room. Now the old lady took her slow steps forwards and enquired about a repeat prescription, which was promptly handed to her and she left.

Ernie took a heavy step in to the desk and planted his large hands firmly in to the desk and leant inwards, crouching a little to get closer to the receptionist.

“I’m looking to take part in the class that is going on about now.” He told her.

The receptionist moved her head away from Ernie a little, she seemed nervous seeing the size of him and knowing the class that he was going to. She punted forwards before motioning to the right.

”T-through the grey doors and to your right, then you will see a sign saying ‘classes’, follow that sir.”

“Thank you miss.” Ernie said as he leant out and turned to his left, walking towards the doors and pulling the grey painted door on the right open before turning left and once more walking forwards, walking past all the seated patients who waited for their numbers to be called. And walked through another set of double doors, pointing up at the multiple signs and reading them in his head he came across the word ‘groups’ and turned right…
