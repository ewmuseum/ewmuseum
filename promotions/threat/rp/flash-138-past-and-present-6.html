Past and Present [6]
Starring: The Cursed One

He stopped walking away before he had even got started. With the hypnotic melody of winter radiance singing around him, he turned around slowly to see her eyes staring back at him. Despite the flooding of white in his eyes, he could still clearly see the color of hers – blue. As blue as the sky in May, clear day and sun burning bright. Curly strands of her red hair feel across them to each side.

As beautiful as she had appeared before, she was so much more breathtaking face to face.

A forced smile formed across her naturally red lips. He rested his arms against the ledge once again, but never took his gaze off of hers. Through some inner voice or past memory that didn’t bother to show up, he felt linked to her, as if he could actually step into her mind and predict every thought before she knew them herself. With this on his side, he looked through her iris and into her soul.

And he tried to read her, as if she were a book picked up off the street without a cover.

“So, Nicole, I think I can understand why you’re doing this.” he spoke, looking up at her and see the disbelief in her stare. She turned away from him, choosing the streets below instead, and sighed dismally in the snowfall.

“What makes you think you could ever know what I’m going through right now? You don’t know me, and I haven’t spoken a word to you yet.”

“Well, I have…”

“And don’t say you’ve been through this before. Because not only do you not know what I’m going through, but you could never go through this yourself.”

“I was going to say ‘I have seen this before’ actually. And, despite the fact that I have gone through similar moments in my life, I wasn’t going to mention that. Not now at least.”

He looked back at her, and noticed that a tear was sliding down her cheek, crawling along the frosty air and slowly freezing against her skin. He thought that he could read her, and in some ways he could still feel her pain as it ravaged her soul, but he could go no deeper then that. Despite what he had thought at first, he now knew that he was going to have to ask instead of interpret.

“I’m sorry. I just thought that I could use what I have seen and what I have been through to help you in your time of need. Seemed sorta selfish now that I think of it.”

“No, it’s not your fault.” she said, another tear filling the corner of her blue eye. “I guess you thought that you could be a hero.”

“I just wanted to help. But I can’t help you if I don’t know what’s wrong, now can I?”

He looked up again, and placed his finger across the second tear, wiping away the salty, freezing drop of water on her face. Her lips formed another smile, this time much truer then before, as she felt the warmth of his touch caressing her cheek. He then put his arm around and over her shoulders and brought himself up to hold her in both his hands as she closed her eyes and interpreted what he had just asked her. He began to softly rub he red hair, and she searched for the right words to his question.

“This is where it all started.” she spoke softly. “And this is where I wanted it to end.”

“So I was to face Randall on Flaw 25, which took place a week or so after my bout with Twizzy.” he said, listening to the stranger’s laughing die down and his breath begin to slow back to normal pace. “Thing was, the day of the event, the match was postponed due to whatever time constraints took place. That would mean that who ever got the win, R.A.T or myself, would not only have to wrestling in the quarterfinal match, but would have to wrestle in his semifinal match as well.”

The stranger coughed as he adjusted his breath to the warm, moist air. He looked up at the man he had just met, and saw nothing but a pathetic, so-called tortured soul sitting on the ledge of his building, developing answers to those unanswerable questions and reliving a moment locked behind a still frame picture resting in his hands.

He leaned over, trying to catch a glimpse of the image giving this man these depressing thoughts, but all he could make out was whiteness and what appeared to be a person standing in the distance. He shrugged, and turned away from him, choosing the streets below instead, and shaking his head pitifully.

“But didn’t you expect any of this shit to be happening. Tournaments and matches and physical challenges and shit like that happen all the time in every sport, so what’s the big deal about having to wrestle two times in one night or anything else?”

He listened to the question, and decided that the conversation was getting more and more futile. He had tried to make him understand what was running through his mind, but he wasn’t doing too good of a job. There were probably no words that could give this man any understanding of his past, and there was no point in even trying at this moment.

He also decided that he had been sitting on the ledge too long.

With his arms, he lifted himself up and easily switched his legs over to the other side of the four foot wall. He jumped down next to the man that had decided to join him uninvited, and looked over at him. An untrue smile formed on his dried lips, and his hand put the picture back inside his coat, locked away in his pocket.

“I guess you could just say that I don’t have a problem with the physical challenges of this so-called sport. What I don’t want to deal with is going through with the unknown, and then having to look back on it afterward.”

“But then again, isn’t that what life is all about?” the man asked, a cocky smirk forming across his face and his left arm leaning on the ledge of the building.

He nodded in agreement, took his hand out of the pocket, and began walking towards the door he had stepped through three times before in his life. The man tossed his own hands into his bathrobe and walked forward with him.

He reached out and gripped the knob. Naturally, it was cold.
