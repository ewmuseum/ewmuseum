Road to Retribution

Many roads can be paved throughout one’s life. Whether it is related to wrestling or not all depends on the person. It the life of the family of Burns; CJ, Beau, and Rowdy, all their roads have been paved based on their surroundings, mainly that there of wrestling. And now, there roads have come to a crossroads, where to go? Rowdy was going back to Carnage in the come days. And now, CJ and Beau have found their selves employed by the highly regarded thReat. What to do? Beau, being absent-minded, could care a less; he goes by what CJ does, as he feels most comfortable that way. He puts all his trust into his younger brother, as it has been that way for most of their lives. That leaves us with CJ, always the leader of the pack, the brains, the operation, the man behind them. With the present day situation, there is a lot of pressure on CJ. He knows that they are looked on to as sort of the new breed, being only one of two tags, they are needed to raise the bar a notch, to provide entertainment for the multi-men matches. So what to do? CJ, for once in his life, is going to take advice from his younger brother Rowdy. He is going to start his own journal.

“Whatcha doin’?” The hollowed words of Beau Burns are heard.

“Just staring at this notebook, thinking about what I should put in it.” The younger, wiser, CJ Burns replies.

“Maybe you should put words or something it in. That’s what it is made for, I think.”

“Yeah, maybe I should write in it. That would be the most commonly used way of a notebook, how ingenious Beau.”

“Hey…what can I say, I try.”

“As I can tell. Now please leave, and let me be, so I can ponder over my thoughts.”

“Make sure you don’t ponder too loud, I don’t want other people getting mad at us.”

Beau makes a quick swift movement in opening their hotel room door and making an exit, he slams the door behind him.

“Ingrate.”

CJ looks down on the notebook that lies on the hotel room desk. It is closed and the chosen color is red. There is black scribbling engraved into it, for texture. In the middle, it reads “Belongs To:” & “Subject:”

Without hesitation, CJ lays his pencil down behind Belongs To, and writes down his name, CJ Burns. He lifts his pencil ever so gently, and pauses.

After a brief hesitation, he lays the point of his pencil back down on the paper. He writes something and after lifting his hand, “My Life” is revealed. He then proceeds to lift the cover up, revealing the first page of the notebook.

He stared blankly at the page. He is reminded of segments of his brother Rowdy revealing his journal on XWF and fEar, but that was the way Rowdy released all his emotions in a sane matter. How is he suppose to take that idea and add his own mix to it, and that is what worried him. He needed to somehow outdo his brother in what he originally started. He knew that his journal would have to be far superior to that of Rowdy’s. That was how CJ was; he wanted to be the best, because he knew he had the ability too. He didn’t think he could be the best, he KNEW he could be the best. All awhile that CJ was thinking this too himself, he realized that he should be writing all this down. With that, he started a new legacy in his life.

5.12.03
7:26 PM

Dear Journal,

First off, I would like to make a note on why I have not entitled this. I feel that a writing shouldn’t be given a title until you’re done with it. The reason for that is this, you never know what you are going to write about and even if you have an idea, something can always branch off that and change the whole meaning of the writing. Therefore, I will refrain from giving a title and the completion of this.

Now, this is a new experience for me. I have always been gifted in whatever I have done, whether it be athletics or academic, but I have never really wrote something in my free time. Although I was the head of my class, I never felt the need to take my personal time to expand something I was already overexposed to in school. Therefore, you would never see me writing if it were not related to my academics. With that aside, forgive me if I ramble, as this is a new experience and it may start of slow for me.

I guess this kind of deal is suppose to help me express my ideas and deepest emotions. To tell you the truth, it bothers me some, the situation I am in currently that is. You see, the thReat hired Beau and myself awhile back. We are one of two tags team in thReat, and that puts a lot of pressure on myself. With Beau, he doesn’t bother him at all, as he lets me deal with all the reality we must face in life, financially and business-wise. That is all fine and dandy, but we need to prove why we belong in thReat. I mean, we have been in TWW, XWF, FATE, and IOW which were great promotions in their own respects, but thReat is a huge accomplishment for us. It proves that all the effort and hard work that we have been putting in the past 6+ years are finally starting to pay off. But with the lack of tag teams, it puts more pressure on us. There is our team and then a team by the name of The Faithless.. I’m sure they are feeling the repercussions as well.

The whole point of this is this; we must raise the bar. All the shit that we did before this means nothing. We must prove why we are established and belong in thReat. With only two tag teams, we need to prove that we are the better of the two and prove that we are needed and wanted. For our efforts, talent, and accomplishments, that we are respected and well liked as peers and associates. Don’t get me wrong; we must have done something right in the first place to get here, but something doesn’t feel right.
A few years back, Beau and I were unstoppable, we were on a natural high that peaked every night we performed, and it would carry us through one hell of a match, each and every time. And now, we are on a road, we are going North, we are making it to the top. But all these crossroads are in the way, trying to persuade us to take a step back and take a rest. But that is not what we need now, we need to keep accelerating. We need to keep going, we need to step up, we need to prove that we belong here. We need to bring back the old days where we were the best and people loved us, for our antics and our ability to please the crowd. It’s time we get our pay back on the business, for what they have taken, for taking what we have earned. It’s a Road, and we are about to break through that final roadblock. For this is, our Road to Retribution.

Title: Road To Retribution Confidently,
CJ Burns
