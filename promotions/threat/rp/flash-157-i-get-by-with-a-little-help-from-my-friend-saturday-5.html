I Get By With A Little Help From My Friend (Saturday) [5]
Starring: Chris "Neverwhere" McCluskey

“This, my friend... is thReat.”

A refreshed Chris “Neverwhere” McCluskey and “The Biggest Joke” Joe Soap had just walked in through the doors and the arena at which Double or Nothing was being held that very night. Chris of course had slept the final part of the journey, and that ties in nicely with the first line of the first of this set of roleplays. They had just completed their extremely long road trip from Cleveland, to California, and then to this state. Sure, Disney World was (hopefully) quite a bit out of the way, but it was worth it for the photograph of Joe running scared stupid away from Mickey Mouse.

Chris and Joe walked through the corridors towards Commissioner Gear’s office. Chris knocked seven times, but it seemed that Colin wasn’t here yet. Chris pushed the door open slightly, and noticed the International championship title belt lying in the trash bin beside Colin’s desk. Must have fallen off the desk or something. He didn’t want to touch it in case he put the scud on himself, so he asked Joe to take it out and set it back on the desk. As they turned to leave, the door swung open and the lights turned on. Colin Gear stood in the doorway, with a large stack of papers in his hands. Obviously the thReat Commissioner was soon to be hard at work. On closer inspection, it turned out that Colin had a large stack of porn.

“Biggly Jibblies Colin? Honestly?”

“That’s my favourite!” screamed Joe.

Joe then remembered that he and Colin had conducted themselves in a rivalry of momentous proportions two and a half years ago, and they gave each other a suspicious look, fearing the worst. Joe knew the injuries a stack of porn used as a weapon could cause. But Colin realised that Chris was his friend, and Joe was Chris’ friend, so obviously, he could trust Joe. The two of them put their differences aside, just as Chris and Colin had done on Colin’s first night on the job.

After brief chit-chat, Chris asked Colin if it was alright if Joe could accompany him to the ring tonight, as his manager. Joe preferred the title of “valet”, but that just sounded gay when applied to a man. Colin had suggested that Joe could wear a tube top to compensate for this, but Chris vetoed the idea immediately.

Chris and Joe then left Colin behind to deal with his “work,” and they made their way to Chris’ locker-room.

“You get a whole room to yourself!”

Joe was obviously flabbergasted at the royalty Chris was getting.

“Of course I do Joe. Why, did you never?”

“I didn’t even get a locker-room! I had to change in the corridor! This one time after a match, I had to shower using a drinking fountain. I’m not allowed in the state of Wyoming anymore.”

Well, I guess Goldberg can’t complain.

Chris checked his watch. It wasn’t long now until the start of the show, and of course, the start of his match with blind. He asked Joe to take a walk while he went to the new thReat Doctor’s office for some medical attention for his hands. But he made sure to warn Joe not to go back to the Commissioner’s office for at least three minutes, or else he may be scarred for life. Joe of course obliged, but he actually had somewhat of a sentimental surprise for Chris, that would only be revealed later, during the results of the show, in the form of a segment.

Good old Joe.
