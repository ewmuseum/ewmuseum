running.away.06
Starring: Vincent Pembridge

Date: 30 Sep 2002
Location: Madrid; Spain


The tavern was packed. At about 10 in the morning, word that a *huge* fight at the Casa would be expected on the night. Finally, a challenger that had the slightest chance of defeating Vincent "The British Degenerate" Pembridge. As opposed to Santos, Pembridge hadn't really endeared himself with the fans of the Casa. The regulars hated him with a passion...

And the stream of walk-in patrons quickly shared the same feeling.

Vincent was just THAT annoying. He insulted everybody, and everything. Especially the capital's most famous football club, Real Madrid. He made sure everyone got the message, regarding his hatred for arguably the most dazzling football club in the world. Packed with international superstars with amazing pedigree, Real was THE club to be in.

But for the Scorpion, only one team mattered to him. Manchester United, the eternal English Premiership greats.

Vincent Pembridge sat in the office of Bernard Cavalera, rubbing medicated oil on his wrists and on his neck. Bernard had told him that the Brit's opponent was someone who claimed he could kill Vinny in under thirty seconds, by any means possible. It was to be the biggest fight in the Casa's history. Vince was the man who derailed the legend, Santos Salvatore. And he did so stylishly. All the challengers that then dared to step up to the plate...

Got mauled. A new era had engulfed Casa El' Gavriel. Santos's maimings of challengers looked like child's play, compared to the easy way in which Pembridge took on all comers. It was a frightening thing to see, the Scorpion kicking the snot out of hapless people who thought they could take out frustrations of their lives out on an imposing British pest.

Wearing blue jeans and black shoes, Vincent waited impatiently for Benard to show up. All the wheelings and dealings that Vincent had done the last couple of days needed to have a payoff, and Pembridge was also curious to know who his mystery opponent was. The whole city was abuzz with news of the big fight at the Casa.

Even the police, as corrupt as you can imagine, had an interest in this fight. It was generating more interest than Real's upcoming home fixture.

"Ah, you're here, Vince. Sorry I'm late!" Bernard Cavalera exclaimed, as the door to the office swung open. Wearing a blue long-sleeved shirt with gray tailored pants and black shoes, Cavalera was looking slightly on edge.

The Scorpion of Manchester was wondering why.

And also, fuming mad that his boss had been more than ten minutes late. It was almost time to step into the Fight Circle.

"Bloody hell, of course I would be here. You did call me in the morning and tell me there was a fight. An important one, possibly the most glittering in this dump. Why else wouldn't I be here? I've been here for the better part of the past hour, getting ready, seeing how you said my challenger...

...Was someone who could actually fight."

Bernard laughed, as he placed his briefcase on his table. He'd remembered what he had told Vince earlier, and still thought that was a golden performance from him. However, he was slightly surprised that for all the Brit's belief that he was a better Scorpio(n) than actual Scorpio(n)s themselves... he had not picked up on the subtle yet foolish clues that Bernard had accidently dropped.

Each of Scorpio's three different emblems contributes distinctive qualities to the signs nature. Agression and instinct dominate the Scorpion guise: the deadly arachnid is destructive, viciously competitive, violent and cruel. The essence of the Eagle on the other hand, is the shrewd insight most Scorpios possess. With their Eagle's eyes, Scorpios see what others miss. They discern hidden motives; they ferret out secret flaws and vulnerabilities. And they are not above exploiting others' weaknesses to their own advantage.

Cavalera smirked, remembering when he'd read up on material pertaining to the zodiac. The night when Vincent disposed of Santos as Champion of the Casa, the two men celebrated their business arrangement with a round of drinks. Inevitably, both men got absolutely hammered.

And the fighter formerly known as Jakks began babbling about what truly characterised a Scorpio(n), with regards of the Zodiac. Truth was, Vincent was a Pisces... but he considered himself the true emblem of the Scorpio.

This amused Bernard Cavalera to no end.

"Hello? Anyone up there?"

Bernard shook himself out of the trance he was in, and nodded, shooting the Manucian a slight smile, before opening his briefcase and tossing a bulky envelope at Vincent. Obviously, it contained Vincent's money which he had rightfully earned from doing what he was told to do.

But the Brit wanted more. Information, regarding his opponent.

"Your opponent is someone I owe money to. He can obviously fight me, and in all probability, kill me. But you should find him to be a piece of cake. He's not even professional... been fighting in the underground for years, gotten lucky a few times.

I just hyped this occasion up for some extra cash."

Vincent Pembridge suddenly felt a wee bit calmer, and he grinned, while counting his money. Cavalera, on the other hand, was bellowing with laughter deep inside. The British Degenerate had bought it.

Hook, line, and sinker.

---

The Masked Warrior, as he was introduced as, was being cheered for his scintillating attack. A roundhouse kick to the face of Vincent Pembridge, following with a couple of side-forearm strikes to the Brit's sternum.

The Scorpion was in deep shite.

He managed to swat away a left-footed sidekick from his opponent, before slugging him with a vicious hook. Before following up with a right-handed cross hook, with immense power packed behind it. It seemed that Pembridge was back in the fight, with those two easy yet effective offensive moves.

But as he went for another hook, The Masked Warrior ducked... before raising his knee into the abdominals of Vincent. Hard.

And repeated the action. Four more times.

Vincent Pembridge was double over in agony, not believing what was going on. It seemed Bernard's evaluation of the challenger was way off the mark, and the apparent underground fighter seemed like a true professional, his moves obviously planned with a lot of purpose.

And then, it all clicked for the Brit. Just seconds... before...

... Vincent was grabbed by his head, and drilled to the ground carelessly, his life flashing before his eyes.

Vincent Pembridge had been dethroned. In his office, Bernard leaned forward in his chair, his face devoid of any emotion. The tavern exploded with a chorus of cheers. Vincent's stranglehold on the Casa, while destructive, was shortlived... and there was to be no more foul-mouthed cursing.

The Masked Warrior, however, simply stood there... fists still clenched, looking down at Vince. Who'd recovered by now, and was in a state of unimaginable shock.

"Now, for the coup de grace."

Vince was puzzled by the new Champion's words. Until he tore his mask off.

"Oh feck."

The tormentor had tracked him down. It had been a ploy all along. Getting Vincent to be picked up by Bernard, having his confidence built up, having his reputation solidified, supplying him with wads of money, getting Santos Salvatore to take the fall to the Brit... it was a trick. A devious trick.

By the man who'd wanted Vincent dead. And hence, followed him from the States, to England, and even to Belgium.

So, it was no surprise to Pembridge's inner child that the Scorpion of Manchester picked himself up and staggered out of the Casa as quickly possible, the fear of God having been instilled into him once again. And yet again, Vincent Pembridge was running away.

From the warrior and avenger known as Sikanah...
