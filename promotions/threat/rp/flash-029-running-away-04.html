running.away.04
Starring: Vincent Pembridge

Date: 21 Sep 2002
Location: Madrid; Spain


"Mr Cavalera?"

Vincent Pembridge was ushered into an office, instead of into the fighting area. This confused him, but the two men who were accompanying him had communicated in Spanish, telling the Brit that the owner of Case El' Gavriel wished to speak to him first. The fighter formerly known as Jakks had spoken to Bernard Cavalera before, and Benard was actually one of the many regulars of the Underground Fighting Federation, before he moved to spain to escape the long arm of the law.

Who doesn't, in this day of age, heh?

Bernard turned around, a glass of port sherry, half consumed, in his hand. He smiled, and motioned for Vincent to sit down, which the Brit did, still perplexed at the whole thing. He hadn't trained and scouted for no reason. He hadn't come to the tavern to drink.

He wanted to fight, to earn money, to get back in the groove.

"So, Mr Pembridge, good to see you again. I was a fan of the UFF, you know?"

The Scorpion's right eyebrow arched up, in surprise, as he leaned back in the black leather couch he was seated in. He figured if there was going to be some discussion about whatever, making himself comfortable was the best thing to do.

"That's interesting. But if I may be crass here for a moment, what the bloody hell is going on? I came here tonight, to fight. Nothing else. All I want to do is beat that piece of shite up, and start earning some money."

Cavalera laughed, just as Vince finished his tirade. Bernard Cavalera was a patient man; hard to find, nowadays, with the pace of life upgrading to breakneck speed. But he always felt that by being patient and waiting things out, the reward will taste much sweeter. Now, as he downed the last of his port sherry, Cavalera put the glass down onto his table and sat on it, arms folded.

Eyes fixated on Pembridge, a sly grin plastered on his face. This was a plan that Benard had been cooking up since he first saw The British Degenerate in his tavern. Recollections of Vinnie fighting in the UFF were once again vivid, and Cavalera remembered how awed he was with all the espionage-like twists and turns unfolded in the organisation.

Vincent Pembridge, or Jakks as he was more commonly known as then, always seemed to be at the center of many things. The focal point from which, pure mayhem enemated. Eventually, underhanded sneak tactics saw the Brit claim the top prize in the UFF, and he held on to it for quite a while, before finally being ousted.

"Vincent. I know you want to fight. I've seen the way you looked at Santos while fighting, and while you signed up, I watched the adrenaline pumping through your veins. You genuinely want to take him out, to prove whatever you have to prove. That's good, yes. But seeing how I know you can beat Santos, I want to enquire if you'd like to make even more money, on the side.

You know, by living the way of the seedy underworld. What do you think this place is, heh?"

The Scorpion was somewhat taken aback, but he didn't show it. He couldn't, it would have been a moment of extreme weakness and with Bernard's eyes watching the Brit like a hawk, there was no room for error. However, the offer sounded enticing. And it wasn't as if Vince was new to immersing himself in the dealings of an illegal nature. He's done it before.

Hell, it was one of the reasons that drove him to signing the contract with the Underground Fighting Federation.

"Sure mate, that sounds bloody interesting. What's on the agenda?"

---

Santos Salvatore stepped into the Fight Circle for the second time in a night, but what had seemed like a millionth time over the course of the past couple of weeks. Day by day, he was regaining his strength and confidence. And the once-famous Brazilian street-fighter, who trekked to Australia in hopes of raising his profile but didn't achieve a high degree of success... was now making waves again. This time, in his adopted homeland, Spain.

He looked around him, at the fans and visitors of the tavern, and smiled. They loved him, he loved them. But standing there, in the Fight Circle, Santos was wondering why his next opponent was hyped up to be a "killer" of sorts.

The Fight Circle of the Casa, actually, is a an area cordoned off by a number of bar stools... arranged to make a circle. The concept was simple; keep an opponent down for the 5-count, or toss him out of the Circle, and victory is attained.

"LADIES AND GENTS!" Santos screamed, raising his arms in the air.

The crowd responded, screaming back in delight, raising their beer mugs to the legend of Casa El' Gavriel.

In his office, Bernard Cavalera signed a purchase requisition order for more tequila and bar stools, before he peeked out of his window and watched intently, as his prized fighter paced within the confines of the Circle.

"Tonight, I fight another challenger! Rumored to be a KILLER of some kind! Some big shot, who apparently thinks he has a chance of beating ME! Well, what I say is BRING IT ON, yeah! I'm always up for a challenge, and so far... I haven't lost!"

Claps and whistles were emitted from the patrons of the tavern, as the atmosphere now mimicked that of a football game, between the country's most illustrious soccer clubs; Real Madrid & Barcelona. Santos nodded his head, grinning, waiting for his challenger.

And right on cue, Vincent Pembridge jumped out of the darkness, and into the circle.

Santos turned around, and his eyes widened in shock. His mouth gaped open, unable to fathom how this ghost from his past had shown up here, of all places. But he didn't have time to tremble with fear, and go weak with queasiness.

A punch to the face, a kick to the groin, the ramming of Vincent's right elbow into his abdomen.

The crowd were stunned. But it wasn't over.

Pembridge smiled as Santos doubled over in pain, gasping for air, watching as his crowd of King in the Casa was slipping away. And as the Scorpion Of Manchester brought down his right elbow onto the back of Salvatore's neck, it was pretty much over. It was the same neck... that Pembridge had broken in the OFG.

All over. Vincent smiled.

The crowd went ballistic, hissing and crying out in anger.

The Scorpion Of Manchester raised his arms in the air, and laughed maniacally. His new life had begun, and he had a feeling that it was going to be loads of fun, with the income to be earned. As for Bernard Cavalera?

He chuckled, smugly. The plan was unravelling perfectly.
