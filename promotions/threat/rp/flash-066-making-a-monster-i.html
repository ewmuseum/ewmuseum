-= Making a Monster =- I
Starring: Wolf

So my prologue’s out the way.

You know my life, you know I’m married, you know that wrestling is my whole life, and you know that am in thReat with only one thing on my mind.

Winning.

But you don’t know anything about the real me. You’ve never been inside my head. You’ve seen me from the outside, but to truly understand anything I do, you need to take a trip… inside.

Steven Solex.

The man has tormented me since my first day in thReat, and doesn’t show any signs of relenting. He’s cost me matches, verbally and physically assaulted me, lied and cheated, and stole a title from within my grasp, and it’s now that I realise that this was my fault. I was the one who didn’t come to Malevolence with my mind in the right place. I was there to outwrestling him, and I did, but he outfought me.

The only problem there is while Solex may be able to outfight me, that’s all he can do.

I outsmarted him on Flaw 19, and I earned the right to fight Solex again at thReat’s next Pay-Per-View, and while I am tempted to sit back and just wait for the day, the fire inside me is telling me I need to take it to him. I need to up my game.

I need to turn up the intensity.

I had liked staying focused, I had liked staying calm, but Sole ha proven to me that to become a champion, those things can’t stay. I need to run on pure emotion, I need to throw myself into every situation with 100%, I need to become the physical embodiment of intensity.

I need to unleash the monster within.

And I will not be responsible for the repercussions.
