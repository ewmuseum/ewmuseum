Past and Present [1]
Starring: The Cursed One

A chilly breeze wrapped the city of Chicago. The night had come, and darkness mixed methodically with snowfall. Flakes drifted down softly from the heavens and came to Earth silently.

A stiff figure emerged from the faintly colored air, stepping out of the snow-covered grass of the park and into the first street he had come to for hours. His body stood wrapped within a brown trench coat, flapping in the wind uncontrollably and slapping his legs like a dog’s tail. He shrugged his muscles together, attempting to trap the warmth within his body. His feet, smothered in fresh snow, lifted up mounds of white and tossed it lowly as he walked.

He plowed through the street slush, a red pick up driving closely behind him as he reached the other side and splashing up strands of the liquid. Small dots of the mixture landed on his back as his right foot penetrated the snow atop the sidewalk, but he didn’t notice. Instead, his attention was paid to the site that caught his interest the moment he stepped into the street.

Before him stood a good sized crowd of people huddled together, looking up towards the grayed sky, their breath rising together in one composed mist.

He knew that there was no reason for such a group of people to stand in one large group, catching snowflakes on their tongues as one. They were looking up at something. And he wasn’t about to just leave something as appealing as this situation alone.

-----

July wasn’t known for its snow, but the thought was cold enough to send a chill through his body as he stood on the rooftop, as the sun’s drab illumination penetrated the summer sky. His eyes reflected early morning light. His mind reflected upon thoughts of his life now, and what events led him this far.

He could remember the evening from which the picture was taken, physically, as well as vividly. The wind still howled in his ear, piercing through the night sky like a dog’s bark. They remind him of the time where he walked, snow blind and waiting for his salvation upon this Earth to take place.

The ideas of what he was and what he had become varied in many ways, but at the same time was quite similar.

Mystery raveled up inside normality, and then hidden behind uncertainty. He had lived a life, eventful and learned through the years, yet there was so much that would take him a lifetime to understand. Thoughts to determine. Ideas to understand. Pictures taken to piece together the past, little by little. He only hoped he had enough time in this waning life to fulfill all those promises he made himself.

A humid breeze slipped through the air, whipping him in the face and building up the sweat in his hair. The day had come, and an azure colored sky mixed with newborn daylight on the horizon. Clouds drifted overhead, but snowflakes were no where to be seen.
