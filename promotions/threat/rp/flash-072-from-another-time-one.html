From Another Time - one
Starring: beforeTHEhatred

Years ago, a few years before Baltimore, there had been Atlanta. Same old city it had always been, give or take a few buildings and people, and it would always be one of the most popular cities in the Unites States.

The smell of the rainy afternoon filled the air, which was moist yet dry in that way only nature could design. The pavement dried slowly, but the sun had already set. Hours ago, in fact. The city lights burned intensely, giving the illusion of day in the dreariness of a city’s night. The nocturnal roamed the streets like ghosts, their faces hidden away behind darkened spaces.

He could picture their eyes burning a hole through him as they walked by, but realized that they were as uninterested in him as any other pedestrian that he briefly encountered on his walks through the city. They were, in their own right, like ants, and nothing more. Traveling on their paths, they may see each other every once and awhile and they might even work together, but they all had their individual roles in this world.

And, in his own mind, he was no longer part of the colony.

Just a rogue, with no home and no ruler. Just a phantom amongst the living dead, roving the bleak evenings mindlessly and vacantly. Alone, walking along a barren path with empty sights and pointless emotions. With a camera in his pocket, waiting patiently, and a box hidden away for his secrets to subsist peacefully.

Turning a corner of the street, the shadowy figure of a park engulfed in nighttime emerged. The leaves upon the trees murmured casually, as the wind stroked through. He listened, but didn’t acknowledge. He was paying far too much attention to those below the tree line, at those courageous enough to venture through such gloomy place at such an unwise time of day.

There weren’t many, but there were enough to give you that feeling. That understanding that there was a world that never fell asleep, even when you slumbered inanely at night. You could look out your window in the gray hours of night, and see this for yourself, but many chose not to. They chose to ignore what they feared, and chose instead to pay attention to the news and television.

This was a world they thought they could understand completely, yet would never bother to truly see with their own eyes.

Blindness comes in all kinds of forms.

He leaned against the gate, watching the park live despite the lateness of the day. Laughter and yelling echoed through the darkness, and it wouldn’t be human to not be a little suspicious of the source of all this. He stared, in full awe of what could be inside the perilous public, like a child would stare at a forest surrounding their rural home. His imagination ran wild, as part of him still had a lot to learn about the world.

And before he knew it, his feet were steadily crossing the street towards the park, despite all the possibilities and all the consequences each chance brought with it.

Shit, what else was he going to do at that time of night?
