From Another Time - five
Starring: beforeTHEhatred

He sat there, a quarter moon shining above his head and lampposts humming in his ear, as he watched the young man step towards the court. His hand gripped the rail edged along the side of the bench, almost restraining himself from seeing the act happen before his very eyes.

His past, a mismatch of emotions thrown together, told him there was no need to witness something of this nature. It was nothing he hadn’t seen before.

A sublime mixture of silence and sound designed the atmosphere of the park. All around him, in the gloom of the weakly lit stars, they grimaced and laughed together. A hundred distinct voices joined together in a calm harmony. A hundred or so glints of cigarettes shined through the darkness. An acrid stench of cigar smoke dragged through the air, mixing with the appealing smell of post rain.

But all this time, through all the distracting elements of this evening, the only sound he heard was the footsteps of a young man, alone and divergent in this miserable world. He could hear only his footsteps, swooshing through the muddy grass, and his heavy breathing, huffing and puffing.

He also thought that, if only for an instant, he could hear the clicking sounds of a handgun.

He passed it off, classifying the thought as naïve and pointless to the bigger picture living before him.

The man, a stranger and uninvited spectator to this little web of events, sat wordlessly, listening to the young man stepping across the mucky park floor before hearing the footsteps no more. Instead, he heard stiff, firm footsteps upon cement echoing through his ear. This for a mere few seconds before someone yelled.

Something about someone having a gun. Unimportant.

A barrage of feet trampled the ground, screams and cries ensuing and people running away as fast as they could. A struggle began, as expected, and the loud crack of a gun shot ripped through the silent resonance of the night. More screams followed, screeching through the trees like a heavy wind. The running slowed down a bit, as another gun shot reached full pitch.

Then peace and quiet. If it had been day, birds would chirp on the trees and in the sky. The wind would hum serenely. The world would still be a happy place, filled with unseen wonders and lively sights.

But instead, the yell of someone’s voice, saying something along the lines of ‘Let’s get the fuck outta here’, was heard. The sound of three or so men running away was next, and the lonely but distinct clang of a gun falling to pavement.

All this time, he never looked up once.

This would remain the same until his head twisted to the side, only to see the same young man lying on the pavement of the basketball court, a puddle of blood flowing down like a makeshift stream surrounding his slowly twitching body, mixing with the muddy grass below.
