All things Fury (Intro)
Starring: Steven Fury

As rays of sunshine beat down on the sweat drenched muscular back of a man swinging his axe against the limb of a huge black oak tree a loud thud is heard on the ground below. He straightens up, runs his fingers through his hair as the wind blows hard against his back. A smallish young woman with red hair and a devious smile walks up to him, gives him a kiss, biting his lower lip before plopping down into a metal lawn chair nearby. She smiles as she says. “Stevie-kins, don’t you think you should be inside getting ready?” Steven Swings his axe with force against the limb splitting it in half, leans against its handle and replies. “NO, M.J I don’t think I should be getting ready.” M.J shifts her weight as she crosses her right leg over her left and begins to frown as she says, “The camera crew will be here any minute though and you’re not even cleaned up.” Steven drops his axe, walks over and sits down on a bench next to M.J and says. “I didn’t ask for this.. I didn’t ask for some camera crew to drop by while I sign copies of my book for little kids.” M.J leans back and begins to talk, “But yo..” only to be cut off as Steven raises his hand and continues. “I didn’t ask to sign any books for little kids either. You know I hate kids. You know I’m not fond of many people at all, so what is this crap! Ozz suddenly decides I should show politeness towards fans now? I’ve climbed to the top of the wrestling mountain many times without the fans so why change now?”

M.J shakes her head and smiles as she asks. “If you didn’t want to do it, then why did you agree to do it for Ozz?” Steven stands, walks back towards the downed limb and answers. “I don’t know? Ozz asked me to do it so I agreed; after all he’s almost like a father to me.. But I’m beginning to think he’s hanging out with that loser cousin of mine too much..” Shaking her head M.J replies. “Why do you hate Sean so much?” A wicked smile creeps over Stevens face as he answers. “My cousin Sean is the biggest loser in the world. Why do you and Ozz continue to protect him and take up for him?” Steven lifts his axe and swings it violently down striking the limb cutting it in half. “I simply don’t understand.. Ever since the attack from Starr that put you in the hospital you’ve changed.. Call it amnesia if you like but I’m beginning to wonder if you ever had it. I want the old M.J back. The M.J that hated the world along with me.” M.J’s face begins to contort into an angry look as she says. “Sean isn’t a loser, he’s a nice guy and he deserves better treatment from you.” Steven shakes his head, looks up in anger as he throws the axe at the trunk of the tree causing it to stick. M.J jumps to her feet, grabs him by the arm and says. “What the hell is your problem, I know you’re angry but what’s causing it? I know you so I don’t believe it has anything to do with this camera crew. It’s something much deeper, so spill it now!”

Steven pulls away and continues to walk towards the house as M.J hollers. “Damn it Steven! Get back here and tell me what’s going on.” Steven continues to walk towards the house as he grumbles. “I don’t have time, your buddy is sending a camera crew over, remember?” M.J seething kicks the metal lawn chair and falls over in pain as she mutters. “That man is incorrigible at times.”

After a nice long shower Steven wraps a towel around his waist and stands looking into a smoky covered mirror covered with condensation from the steamy hot water. He reaches out and wipes away a small band of water and stares deeply into his own eyes as he thinks. “What happened to the good old days, the days when no one questioned what I said and no one ever questioned my authority around this place? Damn it, the sign on the gate say Fury Castle not M.Js castle or castle Ozz.” Steven walks out the door and into the bedroom only to find Ozz seated in a chair across the room. Steven shakes his finger at him as he walks by and into the walk in closet. “What are you doing in mine and M.J’s room Ozz?” Steven says as he pushes the closet door shut and pulls on his gray pinstripe slacks, black silk shirt and walks back out tying his silver tie. He looks down at Ozz and says. “Well, I’m waiting.” Ozz shifts his weight nervously and replies. “M.J said you were upset and wanted me to talk to you.” Steven sighs, sits on the corner of the bed and pulls a sock on as he says. “Talk about what? Shall we talk about you stepping into my business with Sean?” Steven pulls on his gray snakeskin boot and continues as he pulls on his other sock. “Or maybe we could talk about how M.J has changed and how the two of you are making my life a living hell?” M.J storms in and slams the door behind her as Steven finishes pulling on his last boot. “WOW! That’s the most anger I’ve seen out of you in ages. Maybe we’re finally making some progress.”

M.J quickly walks over to the bed, points her finger in his face and says. “What is your problem today, I’d swear you have PMS or something.” Ozz begins to laugh but quickly shuts up as he sees the look in Stevens’s eyes. “My problem? My problem is quite simple. For years I’ve had the support of my two best friends. Now I have Ozz, whom for some awful reason decides it’s time to sell me as some sort of nice guy and you whom seemingly agrees with him. What’s wrong with this picture babe? When are you going to get back to being my M.J, the M.J that hated people? The M.J that hated Sean. The M.J that hated the world just like I do? When… When do I get rid of the M.J who thinks she’s some kind of angel put here to help people?” A shocked M.J looks up at Steven and says. “What are you talking about?” Steven hangs his head and replies. “This amnesia stuff sucks. I want you back. I want you back to being the girl that smiled devilishly as I bloodied up someone. I want you back to normal. I’m going nuts around here trying to figure out what project you’re going to take on next.”

Noticing the confused look on M.J’s face Ozz clears his throat and says. “She cant’ help it she can’t remember those horrid days.” Steven quickly looks up and directly into the eyes of Ozz and mutters “Horrid days?” Ozz turns white as a ghost as he gulps and says. “Yes! I said horrid days. Do you think what you did was right? Do you think we should have stood by and let you continue to control Sean through your mind control techniques?” An evil grin crosses Stevens face as he says. “Why not? I wasn’t hurting him, I just made him a winner for the first time in his life.” Ozz looks to the ground and asks. “How did it make him a winner? No one would go near him because they were all afraid of him.” Steven shakes his head and replies. “He won every match he was in didn’t he? He was so aggressive he couldn’t lose, he was a real monster, MY MONSTER!” Ozz frowns as he says. “You said that right, he really was a monster, you taught him well while he was under your spell.” Ozz stands and begins to walk to the door as he continues. “Aggression is one thing but he was over the top thanks to you.” Steven looks at Ozz and questions. “Over the top? Seems to me he was wining and when you’re winning there is no such thing as over the top.” Ozz shakes his head as he stops at the door and says. “He almost killed himself and many others. He knocked a man out, poured gasoline all over around the ring sat on top of him and set the entire ring on fire. Did he leave? No he sat there laughing as the flames engulfed the ring. If it had not been for the workers both men would have burned to death. And what about the time you had him take torture devises from your freaky little dungeon and use them on that stable that attacked him the week before? That my friend is over the top, torture devices have no place in the wrestling ring.”

Steven chuckles as he says. “Technically Ozz, they weren’t in the ring they were in the basement.” Ozz begins to pull the door shut as he says. “OK, have it your way, I for one am glad you were stopped on that one.” Steven looks around and sees M.J crying in the corner. He walks over to her, places his arms around her as she tries to pull away he whispers. “I’m sorry M.J, I know you can’t help it you’ve changed. Sometimes I shoot off my big mouth but you know I’ll always love you.” Steven turns her around, gives her a kiss and begins to caress her back. M.J bites his lip hard, knees him in the groin causing him to bend over and says. “Sucker! You want the old, M.J back? You want the evil nasty little red head you grew to love and admire back?” Steven stands back up with a smile on his face and says. “OH YEAH!!!” Steven picks M.J up, kisses her and throws her onto the bed. She lies back and asks. “Don’t we have company coming?” Steven smiles and says, “Who cares!” as he buries his face into the side of her neck. .M.J grabs Steven by the hair and pulls as she says. “OH! By the way, I forgot to tell you, I signed you up with thReat because I think it’s time for another violent reign of blood!” Steven stopped, for a minute looks M.J in the eyes and says. “You’ve had your memory back for awhile haven’t you?” M.J laughs as she answers. “Quite awhile as a matter of fact. Sean’s ready for a little violence as well, just say the word, if you know what I mean!” Steven smiles and says, “It’s no wonder I love you and that devious little mind of yours.” They both begin to laugh as Steven pulls the cover over their heads.
