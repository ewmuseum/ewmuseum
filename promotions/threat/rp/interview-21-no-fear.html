No Fear...
Starring: 'The New Disorder' Sean Clayton

Having overcome the fact that he is involved in professional wrestling, and he must now go onto fulfill his dreams, Clayton's mind overtook itself with slight regrets.

Maybe he was ready, maybe he wasn't. Who cares? He had to go for it sooner or later, and as far as Clayton is concerned, the sooner the better.

Having been glancing at his contract almost every minute, Clayton nervously stuffed it back into his pocket and threw himself back on the chair.

After a few seconds of complete silence, Clayton's eyes bulged out, and a slight smile broke out on his face.

"Professional," he muttered to himself. "Professional wrestling."

Clayton slowly nodded to himself. "I can do this, of course I fuckin' can."

He got off his seat and pulled out the confirmation letter of his contract back out of his pocket.

He smiled once again, whilst reading through his contract. "Remember, Dad, when you told me that nobody could be perfect? Nobody could be perfect, Dad?"

Clayton headed towards the window and looked up to the heavens above.

"Wrong," he stated to his father, with his teeth together tightly. "Absolutely wrong. I will be perfect, Dad. I will be the best. And you know why that is, Dad? 'Cause I'm better then you. You knew deep down inside that I had the potential of becoming a better athlete than you. And it burnt a hole right through your heart, 'cause you knew that your only son was on the verge of becoming a better star than you."

The wind brushed through Clayton's hair, as he put his face down, waiting for the wind to lighten.

"You tried everything you could, Dad. Everything you could think of to stop me from progressing towards my destiny. You knew how much it meant to me, but one thing got in the way between you and I. Something that is, ninety-nine percent of the time, the reason why love is broken."

A tear strolled down Clayton's left cheek, as he placed his right hand into one of his back trouser pockets. He pulled out a small photo. A photo of his late father, Henry Clayton, who was also a legendary wrestler back in his days.

"You worked it out yet?" he asked, whilst looking at his father in the photo. "Let me tell you anyway. It's jealousy. Jealousy, Dad. You were jealous of me. You were jealous of my skill. You were jealous of my strength. You were jealous of my speed. You were jealous of my size. Everything about me, you wanted. But you never had it, and you knew you could never gain it."

He looked back up at the sky, placing his photo on the window-sill.

"But most of all, Dad, you were jealous of what I would accomplish. When all your friends told you that I had an unlimited amount of potential, I could feel your pain. No one ever said that to my grandad about you, did they? And you couldn't bare to listen to it. So what did you do? You placed a road block in front of me. Did it work, Dad? Did it work?"

Clayton looked down at the streets. He looked at all the little kids holding onto their father's hands. Some were skipping, some were on their father's shoulders, and some were just kicking a ball while their old man watched on.

"Did it work, my fuckin' ass," he said, whilst continuing to look down at all the children. "Look at me, Dad, look at me. I've turned professional. I'm gonna have the fame, the glory, the houses, the cars, the money, everything. I'm gonna have it all. And you can look down at me, Dad, and tell everyone, 'That's my boy. He will be perfect. He is, without a doubt, The New Disorder.'

Clayton continued to stare down, and then slowly tilted his head up towards the sky, taking one last glance before shutting the window and taking us back...

...to silence.
