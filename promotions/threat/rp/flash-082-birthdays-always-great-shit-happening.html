Birthdays. Always great shit happening.
Starring: Levond and a friend.

"Why the hell do these airports always have to be such a long wait?"

Levond stood in a line, staring towards the beginning of the line, slightly leaning over to his right to try and get a better glimpse of how much longer he would be waiting to get through the metal detector. He took a step forward as the line moved forward a few inches.

Before he could make it any farther, he felt a hand grip his shoulder firmly. Levond jerked around, bringing his arms up into a defensive position as he got to where he could stare the man eye to eye. His angry look quickly wiped away though as he saw who the man standing behind him was.

"You're back home?" Levond spoke, a smile coming across his face.

Golden blonde dyed hair, cut short and spiked up. Ice blue eyes staring into Levond's deep brown eyes, and a matching smile on his face. The man who had been training in Japan since early January.

Shawn Arrows.

"Yea. They let me come home after I seriously screwed up my knee. Seems I'm not supposed to be wrestling asnymore. Atleast thats what my translator over there told me the doctor was saying." Shawn's smile slowly dissapeared as he stared straight ahead, past Levond.

"I know that has got to be getting to you Shawn. Sorry about all the shit back then. You know, the kick to the knee and all." Levond lowered his head.

"I know you are Levond. Thats why I trust you to take care of this, and get Jessica back for me."

"I will. You've got my word on that one."

"Break his knee for me?"

"Sure."

Quick chitter chatter between the two friends, an obvious sign of a long time away from each other.

"So where you heading?"

"Philadelphia. Got plans to talk to someone up there about something, you know, just to keep myself in shape."

"Good idea." Levond's smile came back as he turned back around, staring up at the line, which had grown dramatically shorter now.
