Story Through Theme Music One: "Welcome To The Jungle" - Guns N' Roses
Starring: Twizzy

Oh My God.

Trash was loyal. He was eager to be better and prove himself to the world. He was a man, nay, a wrestler hungry for gold. Trash had never had any true success in a business he had been in for roughly eight years. It’d been his goal to better himself and he would do whatever it took to get there. He would make something of himself.

If you got the money, honey, we got your disease

He entered the Cyber Wrestling Union and immediately joined with a strong group led by the vice president. “The Ringmaster” Marcus Thompson picked out some of the best men to lead the federation to vivacity and strength. These men became known as The New Breed and were made up of Vince Reed, Eric “All That” Allen, “The Ringmaster” Marcus Thompson, and Trash. They were the CWU’s leaders and they were good at what they did.

Welcome to the jungle, we take it day by day
If you want it you're gonna bleed, but it's the price you pay
And you're a very sexy girl that's very hard to please
You can taste the bright lights, but you won't get them for free
In the jungle
Welcome to the jungle
Feel my, my, my serpentine
I, I wanna hear you scream!

Trash was loyal to “The Ringmaster”. He would do what he said. When “Welcome To The Jungle” by Guns N’ Roses played everyone knew what was about to take place. He had smooth success as a hungry animal that simply followed orders to attack and win. Like the rest of The New Breed Trash was good at what he did.

Welcome to the jungle, it gets worse here every day
You learn to live like an animal, in the jungle where we play
If you got a hunger for what you see, you'll take it eventually
You can have anything you want, but you better not take it from me
In the jungle.
Welcome to the jungle.
Watch it bring you to your sha-na-na-na-na-na-na-na knees, knees
I wanna watch you bleed!

There was a tournament held for the three CWU titles. The winner would win the World Title, the second place prize was the International Title, and the man in third place won the US Title. During this tournament Trash was facing a man named 8-Ball. Before the match began the rest of The New Breed demolished Trash and announced that 8-Ball was the newest member to The New Breed and Trash was an ex-member. He was out of the elite group.

And when you're high you never
Ever want to come down. So down. So down. So- Yeah!

Trash made it his mission to take out The New Breed. He formed numerous alliances with others, but those were quickly disbanded and many of those people would up joining with The New Breed. It seemed hopeless for Trash. Completely hopeless.
