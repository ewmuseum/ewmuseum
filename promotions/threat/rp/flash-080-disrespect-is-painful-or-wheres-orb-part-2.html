Disrespect is painful or wheres ORB part 2
Starring: Steven Fury

Steven is seated comfortably in his favorite chair as Mary Jane runs into the room and jumps landing in his lap. He lets out a huge sigh as she wraps her arms around his neck and places her nose against his. “Play time now?” she asks as Steven smiles and twists her beautiful red locks around his fingers and answers. “Anytime you’re ready babe.” MJ whispers, “I’m always ready” as she places her lips next to his and bites his lower lip. Steven quickly grabs her hair and pulls causing her to relax and quit biting. Suddenly she jumps out of his lap, takes his hand and says. “Come on, Play now!” Steven follows her as she runs down a winding staircase dimly lit by torches hung along the gray stone walls. As she approaches the big mahogany door she places her hand on it and slowly pushes the creaking old door open. A shrill squeaking sound pierced through her eardrums causing her to wince in pain.

Mary Jane looks to the left and to the right as she hers voices coming from inside and tries to determine what’s going on. Steven places his hand on the nape of her neck and says. “Why did you stop?” as she recoils in a startled way. She places a finger over her mouth and says. “Shhhhh there’s voices coming from in there.” Steven laughs and says. “Of course they’re voices, Sean and ORB are in there and maybe even Ozz.” MJ turns and quips. “I know that dummy but this sounds like plotting. Like some ones making escape plans.” Steven quickly pulls MJ back and steps through the door. Looking around he sees Sean still mumbling by the table and Ozz talking to ORB. Hastily Steven walks over to Ozz and asks. “Keeping our guest entertained Ozz?” Ozz begins to speak but he’s cut off as Steven grabs him by the throat and continues. “No need to answer that because I know you Ozz. You’ve been like a father to me for years and I know you would never consider a double-cross of me for this pathetic piece of trash.”

Steven releases Ozz and quickly cracks a whip striking ORB causing him to writhe and cry out as the strands of leather with small jagged beads tear into his skin one more time. Steven lifts ORBs jaw with his finger until ORBs eyes meets his and says. “And to think all this could have been avoided if you had only showed a little respect, and hadn’t almost run over MJ.” Steven pauses and begins to rub his chin a moment and continues. “Maybe I could let you go now, let you get on with your life. Maybe you’ve learnt your lesson.” Steven pauses again then says. “Naw, that would be too easy and I’m having too much fun. Only problem is I have to leave for Kentucky in a little bit so I can make it on time for Flaw 21.”

Ozz turns to leave the room and says. “This is sick and depraved behavior you’re showing lately, you need to stop before some one gets maimed.” Steven shrugged his shoulder and says. “Maimed? ORB doesn’t have to worry about that because he wont survive much longer after we leave.” Steven and Sean loosen ORBs chains and help him down from the wall. Laying him on a table they chain his arms and legs stretched, one to each corner. Steven pulls out a small pear shaped device known as the Oral Pear and places it in ORBs mouth turning a screw on the end ever so lightly and watching the prongs begin to open, stretching ORBs mouth and blood begins to trickle out the side. Steven smiles deviously as he walks over to the wall, pulls a lever and watches the anguish and fear mixture in ORBs eyes as he gazes ever so long at the huge pendulum swinging back and fourth above him.

Steven laughs as he says. “I guess your days as a thReat to society are over. You have at the most two hours until this pendulum severs you in half.” Steven, Sean and Mary Jane leave the room closing and locking the door behind them. As they climb the circular staircase they run into Ozz. Steven tosses Ozz the key and says. “Let him out in about half an hour Ozz, we’re off to Lexington, KY. I think he’s learnt his lesson by now.” Ozz smirks and asks. “And exactly what lesson was that?” Steven scowls and answers. “The lesson that disrespecting a Fury brings pain. Anyway, we’re leaving now, see you in a few days.” Ozz shakes his head in disbelief as Steven, Mary Jane and Sean climb into the back of the limo and are whisked away.
