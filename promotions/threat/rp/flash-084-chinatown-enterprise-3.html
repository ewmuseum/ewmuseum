Chinatown Enterprise 3
Starring: Born To Die

The busy streets of Chinatown were bustling this hot sunny afternoon. Weeks of rain showers and bad weather had finally gone away and summer was officially here. And you better believe Born To Die was going to be out and about the streets, looking at the girlies in their tight shorts and low cut tops, looking for some fun, looking for trouble.


Lo and Ricki walked together, laughing and talking as they went up Bond Street toward Canal. Rice Man was the scout today and he was always about a block ahead, keeping an eye out for cops, girls, opportunities, and rival gangs, especially Shadow Dragons. Pong, Davey C, Sunny, Tubbs, and Wangman walked a bit behind the two leaders of the once very powerful Chinatown gang.


Lo grabbed an orange from the fruit stand of a local vendor. When the old man called him out for it, Lo took a big bite out of the orange, spit it in theold man's face, and then threw the orange at him. They all laughed and kept walking, a little faster this time. Born To Die didn't have the juice they once did. There were other gangs and traids and tongs working the streets these days. Juvenile delinquency was about the only thing BTD could do without attracting attention. After all, they were dead as a gang, humiliated, crushed.


Rice Man led them into an alley between Mandarin Lane and Mott Street. The steam from a local restaurant stank up the greasy cobbled street, a small short cut between the busier avenues. A small grocery store stood alone next to the back doors of some other establishments. Huge garbage bins, cardboard, and other refuse were tied up along the street and piled up against the dirty walls. All in all a quiet little street away from thehustle and bustle of the always busy and touristy Chinatown streets.


A few of them lit up cigarettes and smoked as Lo jumped on top of a dumpster and pulled out a little black book from his baggy army green cargo pants. He laughed to himself as he opened the black diary, and started reading from it, taking a puff out of a Marlboro, but intently focused on the subject matter.


"Yo Lo, brother, why you like to read this stupid book so much? Just a little girl and her love story, no? Ha ha ha! I like to read too. Hustler Forum! Ha ha ha!"


"Ricki, man... this shit is better then that bullcrap in Hustler forum, man. This is some real shit. Zoe Antoinette is a fucking dyke bitch, man! And she likes to write in very steamy and filthy details about all her exploits, ha ha! Man, we should ask her for about a gee to get this book back. Unless she wants us to read this shit on the air to all the tHret fans! Ha ha ha! Fucking shit!"


"Yo man, that chick is gonna fuck you guys up, heh heh!"


SinFyre did not appreciate the comment and kicked Pong, who was seated on a milk carton below him, in the back of the head.


"Yo man, what's that for!?"


"Undefeated BITCH! Born To Die, undefeated in fucking tHreat! You think some girl is going to beat us? Kick our asses? We have Generative Clash right where we want them, ya stupid fuck."


"Ya, great, just don't kick me, dick."


"I'll fucking crack your head open mother fucker!"


SinFyre jumps down off the dumpster using his hands to get some bounce, and at the same time heel kicks Pong in the face. His head smacks the steel dumspter behind him, his cigarette getting smashed and soparks flying everywhere.


"Lo! Relax brother!"


"Yo man! What the fuck was that for!?"


SinFyre just punches Pong right in the face, and the smaller man tumbles back, tripping over the milk carton and smashing his head back into the dumpster's side corner.


Immediataly, Ricki grabs his brother before he can bust up Pong any further. Davey C and Wangman run over to check on Pong, while Rice Man and Tubbs just laugh.


"What the fuck is so funny? You think this is funny?"


"Ummm... shit man, take it easy."


"FUCK YOU! Take it easy!?"


"Shit Lo, man!"


"Get the FUCK out of here! NOW! Fucking waste of sperm, you fucking worthless pieces of shit! Fuck off!"


Wangman and Davey C help up Pong, who holds his busted open lip in hand and turns to take a look at SinFyre, the hurt obvious in his eyes. Rice Man slams his cigarette into the ground and walks away. Tubbs has a look of surprise on his face. He wants to say something but he just doesn't know what. He shakes his head and starts to follow his friends.


"Yo, wait up!"


SinFyre shakes off his brother's grip, spits on the asphalt, and kicks the dumpster. The old man from the grocery store comes outside to see what is going on.


"Fuck you looking at old man!? Get the fuck back inside before I torch your fucking place!"


The old man scoots back inside his store. Lo jumps up on the dumpster again, and picks up the diary again.


"Idiots."


Ricki looks at his brother with uncertainty. He thought that signing wrestling contracts and working for good money would have gotten some of that street mentality out of him, but it wasn't working. Ricki had grown up in Hong Kong, and he knew allot about the gang lifestyle and the pride that went along with it. He just didn't want that pride, that anger, that willingness to hurt people maliciously, to become a part of Lo. It was a part of gang life. It didn't have to be a part of his soul.


Ricki was about to say something to his brother when he remembered... Bosnia's Finest... the X-Terminator fireball on BFG... the fireball in Zoe's face, once, twice?


He turned and walked away, leaving his brother behind, who was entranced by Zoe Antoinette's diary.


Maybe getting the gang life out of you was harder then he thought.


As X-Ploder turned the corner and dissapeared out of view, he missed his brother's expression after turning a page in the diary... an expression of sinister proportions.
