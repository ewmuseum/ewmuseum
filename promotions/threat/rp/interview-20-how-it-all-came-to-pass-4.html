How It All Came To Pass [4]
Starring: Jordan X Shabazz

Jordan was retired, not by choice, but by force. He had nowhere to work anymore. Jane Reagan and OSW had screwed him out of a fairly large contract. Crooked promoters was something Jordan knew about from the time he started in the wrestling business, but he didn’t know that when you got to a much higher level, a semi-worldwide level, that crooked promoters continued to exist.

So for the next few weeks Jordan X Shabazz sat home watching his television and thinking of another way to make some money, perhaps he would open his own church and turn out to be a new age Jesse Jackson. “The Reverend” Jordan Copeland, it sounded weird using his real name as opposed to the one he had used for so long while wrestling. He didn’t make any decisions yet, he was waiting to see if any other federation promoters would call him and request his services.

It didn’t take long.

Wednesday, September 25th, 2002
Albany, New York.

As Jordan X Shabazz sat home, getting fat and watching television, his phone finally rang. He knew it was going to be a promoter, he just wasn’t sure which one it was going to be.

“Hello?” Jordan said eagerly into the phone.

“Hello. This is Ethan Winters of ACW.”

Ethan Winters? Jordan X Shabazz thought to himself, he hadn’t heard that name before in the wrestling business. He said he was from ACW, another federation that Jordan hadn’t heard of before.

“How may I help you?”

“Well, I’m opening a federation and I would like you to be a part of it. I can’t promise you great pay, but it’ll be work full time.”

Shabazz smiled to himself. He wanted back in the wrestling business, but he needed to get back in game shape, and he had only been unofficially retired for a few weeks, so getting back into shape wouldn’t be that difficult.

Over the next few weeks, Jordan X Shabazz worked his ass off in the gym, trying to get back into ring shape before the debut of the new promotion for whom he would wrestle for. He even moved away from home and began living in the house of his rather rich friend, “The Reverend” Jesse Kintei. Jesse liked professional wrestling, and insisted that Jordan not train in a regular gym, but train in his specialized gym that was located in his very large home.

Jordan agreed to do so.

At the end of Jordan’s intense training, and just before the opening of the promotion, “The Reverend” suggested himself as a manager for Jordan. He said that Jordan would need a business savvy character like himself to ensure that Jordan didn’t get screwed like he did when he briefly spent some time with OSW.

Shabazz liked the idea, and before he knew it, he and Jesse Kintei were in ACW.

Jordan’s first show at ACW presented Jordan with a new angle partner named ICU, or Ivan Coe Unger. Jordan lost his first match in a United States Championship tournament, and in his first match back after a two month layoff, Jordan injured his right knee.

ACW was just starting off, and it was decided that it couldn’t stand to have an injured roster member and continue to pay him. So they let Jordan X Shabazz go by buying out his 3 month contract.

Shabazz went home that night he had his contract bought out with his bum knee, and he did absolutely nothing. Another fed he was in and out very quickly. “The Reverend” tried to keep Jordan optimistic by telling him that it wasn’t going to be before long when he could wrestle again, but Jordan didn’t care to listen to him.

He wanted to be successful in the wrestling ring, and he was going to do it, by any means necessary.
