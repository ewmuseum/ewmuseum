Story Through Theme Music Five: "Life's Sweet Drug" - Slash's Snakepit
Starring: Twizzy

Yeah! Oh! Yeah! Yeah, yeah, yeah, oh!

His life was changed because of a disease he had. It was called Jobberitis. A newly discovered disease transmitted in mainly sports figures. Twisted had it and he had it bad. There was a new drug developed that he could beta test called Twistodium. He took it and it would be his life force… oh yes.

Hey, what you doin' out there?
Did you tumble down and burn out in the fire?
You ain't going nowhere.
Stay with me now, let's get higher and higher.

Instantly Twisted became great! He was stronger, quicker, better. He shed the name Twisted and simply went by Twizz or Twizzy. He was a new man, if he was even that. Twizzy was great and it was all thanks to Twistodium: The other white pill.

I, dead broke and blue take a look.
No I ain't no rose.
We, just you and me.
Got a little tied up and now I'm indisposed.

Twistodium was a drug that was made out of goat steroids, sugar, non-toxic green paint fumes, and corn. Needless to say the government was very skeptical about this drug. It helped people, yes, but it was highly addictive and caused people to become rather… rambunctious. Twizzy was a perfect example, but like I said, it was highly addictive.

Keep it going, once it's started.
Share a ride with everybody.

The government would not stop Twistodium from being mass-produced. That was for sure. Twizzy did something about that immediately ‘persuading’ various FDA administrators and later congressman into passing the pharmaceutical drug.

Life such a sweet, sweet drug.
Complete my world.
Come and make me numb.

The drug was passed and all was right. Twizzy’s relationship with the U.S. government was on a thin thread though and numerous times they tried to ban the drug again without success. One point had a secret agent take all of Twizzy's Twistodium and stop the production of it for forty-five days. It did not work as Twizzy beat out the secret agent and got his precious Twistodium back.

He had a seven year itch.
Losin' and scratchin' himself.
She was a complicated bitch.
Fuck with your mind.
Put your balls on a shelf.

Twizzy was given an oppurtunity. The makers of Twistodium, Joe’s Drugs N’ Stuff, offered him a position as Twistodium’s spokesman. He battled with Mark McGuire for one week and beat out the home run king to land numerous Commercial spots and special outings as well as all the Twistodium he could handle or not.

Keep it going, once it's started.
Share a ride with everybody.

Life was good.

Life such a sweet, sweet drug.
Complete my world.
Come and make me numb.

Life is good.

Now at this moment
I can feel the ecstasy.
Live the life you're given.
Leave the bag with me, please.

Twizzy won that GCW battle royal he promised everyone he would win one year ago and the prophecy held true. Twizzy was the GCW World Champion and would remain that champion for over one year…

Life such a sweet, sweet drug.
Complete my world.
Come and make me numb.

… because shortly after Global Championship Wrestling closed down.
