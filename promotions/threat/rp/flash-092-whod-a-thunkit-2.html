who'd-a-thunkit? [2]
Starring: haTE

Yeah, where did we leave off?
Oh right, dark alley, punks, knives and old homeless people.

Things couldn't get better.

haTE stood in the alley, no one could see anything. The shitty lights that were placed in the alley had went out, the circut was either faulty, or the lights just sucked plain and simple. Either way, it was dark. He heard screaming, and thunderous footsteps.

haTE brased himself for a stab, or punch, but nothing happend. The footsteps finally got to where they couldn't be heard, and then followed by the scream.

It was the punks lacky friend. Obviously, afraid of the dark. So now, one punk stood. He had not moved in the dark, it wouldn't have mattered, haTE wouldn't have seen it. Hell, the punk wouldn't even have known he moved.

It was now or never, haTE had to do something. He went through things in his head. This guy was just like he had been at one time. He meant well, but he just couldn't put things together to get a job, to buy a home.

Instead, he stood on the streets, scrapping for what he could.

No right for these young little punks to push him around.

haTE came out with a sensable plan, before he turned, threw his arms at what he thought the homeless man should be, and took off in a dead sprint. haTE was a wrestler, and he sure as hell didn't like to be pushed around.

He thought of this as more of deciding to leave on his own.

And as he thought of it, the kid would get his someday. He looked at it like, he would eventually pick on the wrong person and get the living shit beaten out of him. It made no difference to haTE.

He could hear the footsteps behind him, but it was a lost cause. Even with a full grown man on his shoulder, he still ran faster than the stocky little seventeen year old punk. They emerged out of the alley, as a few cars passed on the street. With old man on shoulder, he moved like the wind, down the street. He finally stopped, and layed the man on the concrete below them.

haTE panting hard, tried to speak.

"Whew.. are-.. you o..kay?" he said, inbetween the panting.

"Uuh, that running sure didn't help the old ribs, I think I should be fine."

The man spit three or four times, and the result was a crimson spot on the ground. He man was still bleeding internally.

"You.. need to.. go to a.. hospital?" he choked out, still breathing hard. Hell, he hadn't ran that hard for a LONG time. "No no, I'll be fine," the man assured him. There, he tried to move, but couldn't. He let out a loud groan and grabbed his ribs.

"I can't just leave you like this.. I have to get you to a hospital."

haTE's heavy breathing had stop, and finally he returned to normal. If there is one thing that you need to know about haTE, it's that he doesn't give up easily.

Plus, if he says he's going to do something.. he'll do it- he's fucking ignorant sometimes.. but he'll do it.

haTE picked the poor old man up, on his shoulder again. Its funny, a 6'1" man picking up a man thats about 6'3" like he's nothing. They walked-

and walked- and walked- and walked-

"Fucking hell, where are the clinics in this town?"

He turned the corner, and he had a friend waiting on him.

Okay, I lie, it was the punk. This time?

He had about 4 of his buddies, and by the looks of things, they weren't as cowardly as the other..
and haTE definately wasn't going to tell them that they were cowardly- because they were holding baseball bats.

"Great.."
