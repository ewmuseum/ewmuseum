From Another Time - two
Starring: beforeTHEhatred

He stepped onto the sidewalk surrounding the forbidding park and walked towards the impeding darkness. He was sure that nothing was going to happen to him, but, just like every other instance of life, that fearful anticipation for the future was still there. Nothing could silence that little voice inside the confines of your mind that told you ‘It could happen’. It was just something you had to deal with, whether you wanted to or not.

Sometimes it’s the anticipation that gets you the worst, he figured as he threw his hands into the pockets of his trench coat and walked warily along the park path.

Around him, the fiends of night crept through the patches of darkness, lurking behind the trees and in the distance in little swarms. Paranoia told him that they were staring at him and thinking of all the sinister actions they could do to him if they wished. Common sense told him that this was just exaggeration, and that they barely paid any attention to him at all.

But that didn’t stop him from watching all of them, and studying them all separately, trying to see each of them as individuals and not as stereotypes. Of what he saw, he could tell that, most of them, looked like the same type of people you would come to expect to be in a park at this time night. Guys dressed up to look like rappers or thugs on television. Younger women wearing clothes they weren’t old enough yet. Older women wearing clothes they were too old to be wearing. Young men trying to build an image of themselves, trying to make the world believe they were rebels because they stay out late.

Part of him strived to find a sense of purpose for them, because deep down he always felt that there was some good in all people. But, as he looked around at those in the forest surrounded by such a large city, he could find only scarce amounts of principle. The harder he looked, the more he realized the futility of his search.

He was about to give up on what he thought would be an interesting trip through the park. He began to stop looking at the people around him, and slowly secluded himself from them. But just when he was ready to become a long figure, walking from one end of the park to the other with no reason but to get to the other side like that damn chicken, his eyes caught the attention of a young man, sitting on a bench.

The young man was black, dressed just like the other boys in the park. Same jacket. Same kind of hat. Same baggy jeans and shirt and sneakers and silver necklace dangling from his neck. But, what he had was something very simple, yet so very unique that only a man like the one currently walking through this park could see the awe behind it – a single tear.
