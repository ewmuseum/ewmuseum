How he became [5] - Conclusion
Starring: Two-Face

God knows what we've missed from Vladimir's life. The last time we saw him he was 18, insane and had just beaten his father to a pulp; although no graphic details >were ever released. But today signifies the last tale of Vladimir's beginning...

The beginning of how he became...

The beginning of how HE became Two-Face.

Fuck you people, this man is insane.

-------------

Friday the 11th of October wouldn't sound special to any of you immoral faggots. But to Vladimir Simunek, this day symbolized that he was something 'special'. He wasn't normal...

...and he would have to take it out on someone.

-----------

It's always ironic when the skies turn a dark shade of grey and piss down for hours and hours on end. It’s ironic because whenever the weather turns bleak, some form of harmful activity takes place within the world. Many would call it a sign from God himself, but I would call it Ironic.

I don't know how to really express this day well. It's like a cancer imbedded in my mind, I just cannot forget her or that insane moment that I lashed out. It was like I couldn't control myself, it was like I needed help.

As you should have guessed it was a Friday, 11th of October to be exact. It was raining, and what had originally seemed like a perfect night out with Nicole Smith seemed nothing more then hype.

Back then I was working for a night club. My English pretty well “sucked” so no where else would actually bother to accept me. But being a good fighter I found myself at this place working for the lowest wage possible. Anyway Nicole had met me a few days back, I think the Saturday last to be exact. I didn't know why she liked me, but something must have appealed to her. She made it her business to talk to me, she made it her business to buy me a couple of drinks, and she damn well made it her business to get me in her bed.

Rather embarrassing to say, but I didn't mind being exploited like this for the first time. After everything I had been through I finally had a girl in my life. It felt pretty good and I had enjoyed it immensely.

After that I offered to take her out to dinner on the Friday, which I am heading towards explaining, but I needed to give you some brief background information, otherwise you really would have been confused.

So we've established the rain, but we've not established the scene correctly. Basically I normally had a small shift on Friday. I would be off by 9, and I had promised to take Nicole out to dinner just after I got off my shift. Everything seemed so perfect (apart from the rain), she was there ten minutes early, and the boss let me off 10 minutes early just to allow me to greet her. I greeted her with a hug, brought her a small vodka complimentary of the house, and we left.

I had seen this neat little Italian place around from my motel. It seemed cozy and romantic enough so I thought it was ideal. But the problem was we never made it to that cozy Italian place.

I was and still am to this day emotional about remarks to my mask. This mask symbolizes what my father did to me. I couldn't give a damn if it looks silly, because beneath this fucking mask lies something that no one will ever see. Ever wonder why I had it glued on my face surgically? If you ever want to see my face, then you can look at my left side, because taking off my mask will only tear the skin off my face.

I'm strolling away again, so I better get back to the subject in hand. Anyway we were talking. It’s always good to get talking before you go out with your women. One of my fellow workers had told me that, and it seemed to be working a gem. We both told each other about stuff, I rather reluctantly told her about some of my past, although not into too much detail, and I didn't say what really went on in my family. I concealed it with a cozy lie about a car accident with my father that destroyed my face. But you know women, they always want more. In a way I'm glad that my father never allowed me to take out women, because they're fucking annoying.

So we were approaching this restaurant when she asks "So why don't you ever take the mask off then. I think you would look really cute"

Cute? Who the fuck wants to look cute around here? This world is for fighters. Screw those cute bastards who spend the majority of their life looking for another chick to sleep with. I'm not the kind of man. So anyway I really didn't like the comment about the mask, so I tried to boycott it.

"This Ita-lian place should be really good. I think you will really like it"

Now excuse me for changing subject, but I thought it would be best. But she was a women, she had to continue on like the dumb bitch that she was.

"Why are you trying to change the subject" she asked.

She then had the nerve to stop and give me one of those soppy looks, the look of one of those brown puppies. But I don't like brown puppies, and I don't like emotional women.

"What’s so special about the mask? It doesn't make you look cool if you think that."

And that’s when she did something that I wish to forget, but I find myself being forced to tell you anyway.

She fucking tugged at the mask. No one fucking tugs at MY MASK! Especially AMERICAN women!

And that’s when all hell broke loose. A hard right hand knocked her down and by then I couldn't control myself. My head kept screaming out "Beat her...BEAT HER! YOU HATE AMERICANS...YOU HATE AMERICANS. HAHAHAHA...HAHAHAHA". I couldn't stop, I just kept punching and kicking until her face was a bloody crimson mask. I didn’t stop until she stopped moving…breathing. From then on everything became like a blur.

I ran. I didn't know what else to do. My hands were a fucking mess. Her blood stained my white shirt. And the rain just soaked her, leaving her on her own on the curb.

And that’s when I learnt who I really was on the other side of this mask. A crazy fucking lunatic who needed to fight.

My father had turned me into this, and I’m not particularly proud of my second personality; but I am who I am. Nobody…and that includes you cannot change me. I will not be sorry for my actions…fuck you people!!

--------

End.
