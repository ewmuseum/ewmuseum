He Was My Heroin - 4
Starring: Ernie French

It took me a long while to think of the bout of strength that I would challenge Death with. As I sat at the table in the darkened room I considered many sporting abilities that I had. I was indeed a very sporting young man. Death reached in to his hidden pocket inside his dark robe and pulled out a beautiful hourglass, solid gold topped and based off the hourglass, the sand was reaching the bottom now, the top segment almost empty.



You are running out of time.
Death told me, placing the hourglass down on the table in front of me.



As the final grain of sand fell to the bottom segment of the hourglass Death commanded in a quiet, yet forceful tone, Choose now.



“Okay, okay.” I said whilst simultaneously frantically deciding upon a bout of fitness to challenge Death to.



Now. It decreed a final time



“I challenge you to… To… A fight.”



I knew how quick Death was, I knew how lucky it was, but looking at it you would never think that he would be a worthy opponent inside a ring, and as the thought passed my mind I was stood inside a ring, ropes and turnbuckles created a square barrier, Death stood, his scythe still in its hand and its hollow eyes stared across the ring at me.



Are you ready? It asked me.



“Ready.” I said, wiping the sweat from my brow.



Allow me to explain the rules. The winner will be the first being to knock the other off his feet, if you lose your soul shall be condemned to an eternity in hell, if you win you shall be returned to New Earth on the condition that you live your life according to the commandments set by God almighty in order to fulfil your ultimate destiny. Do you agree to the rules?



“Yes.” I said attempting to hide my nervousness.



Then we shall commence.



Death stepped out, still holding his scythe; it swung the bladed edge at my face, I ducked and launched myself at it, driving my shoulder in to the base of what should be any mans rib cage, but the shape of Death's, for lack of a better word, body was hard like a brick wall, a pain shot down my right arm as I stumbled backwards.



In order to defeat me, you must believe that it is possible. It told me



I understood and cleared my mind - I glared over at it and charged toward Death, I flew through the air and threw my arm across the upper chest of it, this time it felt as if Death was but a normal human being, it stumbled a little and tripped over the wooden end of its scythe, tripping and falling to the floor, I stumbled in to the corner and turned around to see Death simply lift himself to his feet, his body remained perfectly straight, almost as if he was levitating. He turned to face me and held out his hand, his scythe lifted in to his hand and he stomped it down on the rings mat, and in a moments notice we where back inside my apartment.



Congratulations, Ernie it said to me, you passed test three, a bout of strength and now I return you to New Earth as promised. Should you decide to return to your life of sin you shall be perished to an eternity in hell.



As he finished his sentence a feeling of immense exhaustion flooded my body, I lent against the wall and slid down to the floor, my eyes closed and I was fast asleep.



A few hours later I awoke in my bed, an awful headache radiated down my neck and spine, my shoulder ached also and I was in doubt. Was it all a dream, or did it truly happen? The next morning I checked in to a rehabilitation centre and began my recovery from my drug addiction.
