Missing the Show
Starring: 'Superstar' Vince Jacobs

Fade in to see a huge mansion high in the famed Hollywood Hills. This particular area has been seen before by many cameras from around the world but one such camera on this day from the Threat Wrestling has come to these Hills again.

Cameras pan to the front door of one of the most prolific, charismatic, and an all around show stealer in professional wrestling today, ‘Superstar’ Vince Jacobs. We see the camera making it’s way into the home of one of the most sought after commodities in wrestling.

Vince Jacobs is sitting in his study going through some paperwork. Obviously Threat Wrestling wants to sign ‘Superstar’ Vince Jacobs back to a contract.

Vince has been all over the world and he knows that this isn’t the same thReat as before. He knew that those fighters that Jon Hackett thought would make him money was gone and Threat was going to be what SVJ did best and that’s wrestle.

Hackett wanted SVJ back in the fold to solidify the roster of talent that he wanted to have. Hackett had been keeping tabs on SVJ since thReat’s closure. He knew Vince had been in a few feds that had faltered after only a few months of being open.

Hackett wanted Vince back home in Threat Wrestling. Vince knew this. He knew what he could do raise the ante.

But Vince also knew he hadn’t wrestled in months. He also knew he missed the sweat, the tears, the bright lights, and the roaring fans.

The only thing Vince missed the most was the SHOW. He missed the spectacle. He missed performing.

Hackett wanted Vince in Threat Wrestling…

Vince wanted the SHOW back.

There is nothing else left to do but go home…
