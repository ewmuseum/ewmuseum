who'd-a-thunkit? [1]
Starring: haTE

Payback is in the books, and because of haTE-
Steven Fury is victorious.

He, Sean, and MJ all owe it to haTE.

All of the blood that was poured from the skulls of both men, and the ending factor was the special- very special- referee, haTE.

Now, he stood in the streets, right after Payback, and he felt damn good about himself.

Ever since the beginning of haTE's time, he hasn't ever really felt good about himself. I mean, sure, he felt good when he brought home a little extra for his family. It could have been food, a part of shelter, or even money, but that made him feel good.

Then, he would step into the doorway, or whatever they would happen to be living in, and then he would feel the same way- like shit.

Right now? He felt good being a part of something. Making a difference- a big difference. ORB, and Steven together, made a fool of him in his first match.

ORB was paid back for his effort.

--------------

Okay, here I am. thReat.
And it’s not exactly what I thought it was going to be.

No matter though, Payback is in the books. It was my first thReat pay-per-view. The same pay per view that the General David Sanders was made the thReat World Champion, by beating Xaxon.

Nice job, but what is Xaxon anyway? What, is he cool now because he can go and beat the hell out of Troy Mason? Where does that get you in this business? It makes you out to be a gigantic asshole, it doesn't make you someone that I should be scared of.

So, Xaxon is a complete, and utter asshole. Good, I'm glad we got some things established tonight. What else is there. This International title business? I suppose I'll be entered into it. Maybe this time, I won't get fucked over.

Who knows whom I'll be facing. I'm sure Troy has all of his plans made up. I'm sure that he's got everything all cooked up to his liking.

Quite frankly, I don’t give a crap about any kind of title. I'm here to wrestle. If the “powers that be” want to give me a nice title, whatever. I'll take it, and fight with it.

My aim? To kick some ass. Right now, I don't even have to win. I just want to prove a point. The days of pushing me around are over. I don't care whether you think it’s funny. I don't care if you despise me as a man. You will NOT push me around-

or.. you will face the consequences.

--------------

haTE was broke out of his trance when he heard some whimpers, mixed in with some laughter. haTE looked around, but the streets and sidewalks seemed to be, pretty much lifeless. So, he went looking.

He turned off to his left, and down the streets of Cincinnati. The laughter became louder and more intense. The whimper- it stopped all together.

He walked, and came to an alley way between the US Bank Arena, and another building. That is where the sound came from.

He walked down the dimly lit alley, where he found two seventeen-or-so year-old kids. On the ground- a homeless person.

The kids were taking turns kicking the man in the ribs, as he lay on the ground. He was in nothing but rags, and blood was starting to seep from his mouth. The man was seemingly unconscious, but the kids were having fun just beating the crap out of him.

haTE looked on in horror as his whole childhood as a bum flashed through his head. The lights in the alley flickered on and off, so you could only see parts of the beating.

"What in the fuck!" haTE yelled. The kids looked back at the twenty-year-old haTE, who was no bigger than either of them, and laughed again. "What, do you want a nice ass kicking tonight too, kid?" one of the punks yelled at him. "Yeah!" his lackey friend added.

haTE walked up to them, and stepped in between them and the poor man laying on the ground. The guy took all of his strength and rolled over a little, as if to get out of the way. haTE walked right up in the faces of the kids, the glass and grunge on the ground crunching under his feet.

The kids backed off a little bit, but still smiled. One, which seemed to be along just for the fight, swung at haTE's face. haTE, in one swift motion, blocked it and booted the kid in the gut. He fell to the ground, and the brains of the operation stepped up. "What do you fuckin' care? You're a nobody to me, get out of my face and let me finish my business!"

haTE just shook his head. The kid tried to step around him, but haTE stepped back in the way and pushed the kid back. The kid tried to kick haTE but missed, and haTE punched the kid straight in the face. He pulled back holding his mouth.

A little bit of crimson dripped from his lip now, but he didn't fall like the one before him. Instead, he growled and reached into his pocket..

And pulled out a fucking knife.
But- wouldn't you know it..

That’s when the fucking lights went out in the alley.
haTE, stood in a pitch black alley with- a crazy kid with a knife a stupid kid with half a brain and a homeless guy.

Where did things go wrong??
