From Another Time | Pre
Starring: blind

The evening rainfall, blissful and sublime, etched the city through and through. Lightning scorched the sky, the smell atmosphere chocking the air. Soon after, thunder roared and crackled. Twas a night like no other, in a manner of speaking. No other that he cared to remember, at least.

He sat, his eyes glaring down into the object within his grasp, and his mind traveling down memory lane once again. The pain dropped inside, bringing out those agonizing trips through his past and making him feel extra special. He could close his eyes and picture the fear and anger driving adrenaline through his body, and be brought back to all those times. But half the time, the memories had no clear face to go with them.

That was what the box was for.

Face upon face, staring at the ceiling vacantly. He stared down into the black container, all the pictures inside crowded together into one jumble of souvenirs from every worthy time in his long life. All stood out, each for their individual purposes and flashbacks towards a time that made so much sense.

A streak of lightning drew his attention away momentarily, but the idyllic melody of the rain brought him right back in. His hand reached into the forbidden box, and his sweaty fingers gripped one random picture like the claw inside a vending machine full of stuffed animals. But, unlike the silver claw dangling from its wires, his hand actually pulled out what it latched onto.

The follow up crash of thunder split the sky, and his eyes stared down at yet another picture of yet another period of his life. Almost instantly, with his body sitting in the corner and his eyes closed, the single picture transformed entirely into a vision. And the vision turned into a reality, all five senses stimulated at once.

He was no longer trapped inside that room, listening to the rainfall and blindly staring at the storm.

Now he was lost in his own captivating veracity, dancing with his memories, memento in hand.
