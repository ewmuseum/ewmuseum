Fighting for my Country...

18 December 2002

Fighting for my Country...
Starring: General David Sanders

"I will fight for what is right."

A man, probably somewhere in his thirties, is now seen. He's staring straight into the camera, which is lying on the ground. He's doing push-ups.

He's sporting a black muscle shirt along with camouflage pants. His blond hair is laying on his forehead. He then finally stops his push-ups and sits up.

We can now see that he's outside. But it seems he is in something, or he's permitted from leaving, because there are some light stone walls about 8 feet high around this yard.

"Ah, my beautiful home!" says the man.

He takes a deep breath of air raising his head as his dog tags clatter on his neck. He walks up to his doorstep. The house is small, an average sized house.

"However, this is where I shall leave you," the man says, facing the camera. "I'm 'General' David Sanders, and I think I may have been spotted by the enemy."

You can lightly hear the cameraman say, "Enemies?" obviously wondering who the hell the General was talking about.

"Those kids out there, playing basketball. Didn't you hear them? If they see me, they'll think I'm a nice guy, BUT I'M NOT! For Lord's Sake, if they think I'm nice they'll be actually coming to my house for Halloween! This year was a scare, especially after the town made me take down the barbed-wire."

You can easily tell that the cameraman took a big step back, trying to avoid this guy. Was he nuts or something?

"Well, I've got to leave you now." General Sanders says.

He stands up straight, and salutes the camera before walking into the house.

The camera turns around towards the exit. Which is oddly a ladder... The camera then fades out...
