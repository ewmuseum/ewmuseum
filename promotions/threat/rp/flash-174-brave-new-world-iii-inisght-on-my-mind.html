Brave New World III: Inisght on My Mind
Starring: Paul Armstrong

Brave New World III

There I am, sitting on the plane, bored. There I am, sitting, and then she pops back into my head. I hate when I don't do something to keep my mind busy, because if I don't, she'll come back into my mind, haunting me. She comes in the form of day dreams, making me wonder what could've been.

She's the girl that burns my soul everytime I think of her. She's the one girl that I can truly say that I loved and still love.

But what happened?

Just like everything else in this world, she was taken away from me. Was it my fault? Maybe.

But what can I do. I have to live with my choices.

So there I was, sitting on the plane, day dreaming about her. Hell maybe I should give her a call when I come back to the states. Ever since Peter died, she's the only thing that kept me from totally losing control. That...and wrestling of course.

But even if I attempt to call her, she'll probably just leave. She always leaves. It's as if she likes to torment me.

But what the hell, as soon as I land this plane, I'll give her a call. I'll tell her that I might join a new wrestling promotion in the states...

wait...

if I tell her I still wrestle, I might as well say good bye to any chance of a relationship we can have.

Remember Paul, she left you because of wrestling...


well???

Or did she leave you because you couldn't get over your brother?

Which one was it?

Paul, why aren't you answering me...

come on Paul...

you have nothing in this world, all you have is me...


STOP!!!!

so there I was sitting, day dreaming, and partly losing my mind.

I don't think this Threat thing is going to help me out one bit.

Hell, you have to wonder about me don't you.

You're probably wondering whats instore for Threat. Who am I?

Well, I'm Paul Armstrong, there's a lot you don't understand, and you're real confused. But my story will all come together, like pieces of the puzzle, it will come together.

All I can say, is Threat doesn't know what's in store for them.
