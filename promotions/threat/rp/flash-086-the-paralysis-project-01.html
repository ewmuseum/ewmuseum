the.paralysis.project.01
Starring: Vincent Pembridge

Date: 03 May 2003
Location: New Orleans, LA; USA


With the rain intensifying, Vincent Pembridge quickly opened his motel room door and rushed inside, slamming the door behind him. He hadn't bothered to drive to the convenience store to get beer and some tidbits... and whilst walking back, the gates of Heaven opened, unleashing a deluge of great magnitude.

Meaning, it started to rain very heavily.

Vincent placed the paper bag of his 'groceries' down on the ground and removed his black leather trenchcoat, throwing it onto the bed aware of the fact that his trenchcoat was soaking wet. Striding towards the kitchen area, Vince unbuttoned the buttons of his maroon satin shirt which was only slightly wet, and opened the fridge to grab the last bottle of beer.

The Scorpion had been well-dressed, considering he only went to get groceries -- maroon satin shirt which was long-sleeved, black jeans, black boots, and the black leather trenchcoat. Effortlessly opening the bottle of beer, Vince took a swig of it before he walked back towards his bed.

And started to think about, for the first time since arriving in New Orleans the previous day, about his involvment in the national industry known as professional wrestling. Of which, he was a part of two promotions.

All-Star Championship Wrestling, a company with a lengthy history dating back to the early 1990s. And thReat, a company undergoing a successful renaissance as a pure wrestling entity after a previous incarnation as a hybrid promotion under the same name. Two organisations that were also making waves in the industry, and slowly being labelled as two of the standout promotions of the year thus far.

Vincent Pembridge, however, knew one thing that made the two federations very distinct.

The fact that the rulebooks for both had been thrown out. The focus was no longer on sports entertainment, per se. Yes, entertaining the fans meant better ratings, which all led to more money all around. But that was the motivation, money. And to do it, nothing was sanctioned. No more guidelines, no more rules, no more staging matches or storylines or whatever. You did as you pleased. The only thing that was out of bounds was murder -- at least, if nobody found out. All in the spirit of money making, banking on the traditional principle.

The Scorpion of Manchester smiled, remembering a chance meeting with a certain Joe Campbell months ago. Joe Campbell was the owner of the revolutionary fighting promotion, theAsylum. It brought into the mainstream all the realism that professional wrestling sought to hide. And Campbell found it ironic that today's wrestling promotions were trying to emulate HIS company to replace the fWo as the top brand of wrestling. Ironic, because fWo was at the top and remained at the top without veering away from the traditional mould.

Sitting himself down on the bed, Vincent realised that the next day would be a busy day for him. New Orleans was the home of ACW's extravaganza, Pain-or-Pleasure, and also the location for the upcoming edition of thReat's Sunday Night Flaw show. Two shows on one night, with so much to do. The Scorpion took another gulp of his beer and looked at the stack of files on the other side of the bed.

They were files of certain personalities in thReat. Only reserved for top-tier management, of course.

So, how did someone like Vincent Pembridge get access to those files? Simple.

He conned the Board Of Directors into giving him the power to run the company, undermining the owner's own power. Troy Mason was stunned to see how brilliantly Vincent had gotten the near-unaminous of the Board, and realised that he was fighting a losing battle against the British Degenerate.

See, Vincent had an agenda.

You wanted an explanation? Here it is. Last year, the first bleeding run of thReat. Hybrid federation. I was a member of the Fighting Division. Myself & that cunt, Jon Hackett, were never on good terms. Then, the war between the fighters & wrestlers broke out. Obviously, with me representing the fighters, we would have won hands down. But you see, twats, the other buggers in the team were absolute mongrels. Quincy Brown, Steve Solex. August Monday, too. I simply got on his good side, because he was proving to be a decent fighter.

Vincent placed the bottle of beer down on the side-table, recollecting his in-ring rant on Flaw 13 many weeks ago.

But that cunt, August, looked like a total queer and had horrible breath. Add to that the bugger's fetish for needles or some other. What was worse? Jon Hackett, the bloody bastard, decided to give HIM a shot at the Fighting Championship. HIM. Hackett had been against me from the very start but please, Monday? UGH. In any case, I began thinking about the big match at the Malevolence PPV. Wrestlers versus fighters. I began thinking about Auggie fighting Quincy Brown for the Fighting Title. I began thinking... about Hackett. He was obviously wanting to get rid of the Fighting Division. It didn't appeal to him any more. But he knew the fighters would win. He had also taken some sort of shining towards Auggie.

The Manucian Predator's hand reached out to the stack of files, and grabbed the first one in the pile.

So, I put two and two together. Thought about things long and hard. Decided to put forth a facade. A few nights before Malevolence, I met up with Jon. Told him that the Fighting Division were going to absolutely slaughter the wrestlers, and that wasn't what he wanted. The bastard agreed. I then told him that if he wanted to run thReat the way he really wished to, there had to be someone to take the fall for the fighters.

Opening it, Vincent's eyes started to scan the contents of the file, while his memory bank continued rolling...

Here's the juicy bits, cunts. I told Hackett I'd be willing to screw over the fighters. If he, of course, did something in return for me. See, I actually started out as a wrestler. It wouldn't have mattered to me one bit if thReat reverted back to wrestling. I've done both, I've excelled in both. And Hackett began to think that it was a smashing idea. He asked what was it I wanted. I told him I wanted to be the last thReat Fighting Champion. But with the ability to defend it during the new thReat. The wrestling-oriented thReat

Of course, the bloody cunt was sceptical. Said that he had intended for August Monday to win. I told him it would work perfectly. August would beat Quincy, I would beat August and injure him, there'd only be three bleeding fighters taking part. I'd help them get through it, all the other fighters would regard me as a true champion... before, of course, I bugger up the whole thing and at the end, thReat would become wrestling!

Vincent's breathing began to get a little erratic and heavier than usual.

And you know what, cunts?

You know what?

JON HACKETT AGREED TO IT ALL.

His body started to tremble. He always got angry when it got to this part. For obvious reasons.

BUT THE CUNT CLOSED THE SODDING COMPANY THE VERY NEXT DAY! ON THE EVE OF THE BLOODY PAY-PER-VIEW!

Closing his eyes, Vincent expelled the urge to lash out at something, and calmed himself down. He reminded himself that it was all in the past, and the future paved the way for ultimate vengeance.

However, that's the bloody reason I'm back. For revenge. Hackett's not here this time about. No matter, it's still the same thReat. The wrestlers are still strutting about, thinking they're tough shite. When in actual fact, I beat every single one of them in a straight fight, no bleeding questions asked. See, had Hackett not been a cunt, I would have attained total power. Jon would have been in the bloody palm of my hand.

As it is, the twat flushed it down the toilet.

Now, thReat's back. And I want what's rightfully mine.

POWER.

Opening his eyes, Vincent cleared his throat, his body having stopped shaking from the rage that was threatening to erupt from within. He looked back down at the folder, and quickly intrigued by what he had to read, turned to the next page. Nodding his head as he absorbed all of this information in.

Finally, after a few seconds, Vincent closed the folder and placed it back on the stack of pre-existing files, before he completely removed his shirt and tossed it onto the leather trenchcoat of it. Ideas were suddenly forming in his head. Getting up from the bed, the Scorpion grabbed his bottle of beer and took another sip, while walking over to the other side table, where the phone was. Pressing the speaker button, Vincent followed up by punching several numbers on the phone, too excited to sit down.

And after several seconds.

"Vincent? What's up?"

"Norman. Good to see you're awake. Listen, I've got this idea for a side project. It'd go a long way in destroying a man's life, and it's just too good for me to resist not trying out. Also, I know you and your bevy of colleagues would relish undertaking a project like this."

Silence ensued for a few seconds, before a hearty laugh was heard from the speaker on the line.

"Yes, you've got a bloody good point. What does it entail?"

Vincent finished off the remains of his beer and looked at the bottle, contemplating crushing it against his head or smashing it into the face of some innocent bystander somewhere near the motel.

"Just this bugger in thReat. A former member of the American Army. I've fought him before and won. Look, I'll fax you his file and everything tomorrow afternoon. Just one last thing -- we'll call it...

The Paralysis Project."
