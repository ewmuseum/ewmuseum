Thoughts...
Starring: 'The New Disorder' Sean Clayton

A tall, stocky figure appears outside a door, which has a plate hanging off it reading 'Room 136.' Slowly, a key fits into the lock and after a few seconds of twisting and turning, it finally unlocks as the door is swung open.

With a swift motion of the hand onto a switch, a bright light shines across the room as this unknown figure proceeds to enter the room.

With a glance across to his left, he sees a single bed lying there next to a set of drawers. Across to his right, two chairs have been placed opposite a television screen, with a video and a DVD player placed on a glass shelf next to it.

He walks straight towards the window, which is directly opposite the door, and admires the view of the beautiful sea outside.

After a few seconds of enjoying the breeze which flowed into the room, this figure turned around to get a perfect view of his face.

Fair skinned which black hair, a thin black goatee, and two piercings in his left ear; a silver ear ring and a silver ear stud were what stood out the most.

He walked over to the television screen and removed his leather jacket. He carefully placed it on one of the chairs and took a seat on the other.

As he sat there, staring at a blank screen, mixed emotions ran through his head. Happy emotions, sad emotions, painful emotions, and even scared emotions.

He leaned over to his jacket, and pulled out a small, brown envelope from one of the pockets. He pulled out the letter, which, in thick-bold writing, read 'Dear Sean Clayton.'

Obvious as to who this person was now, Clayton just stared at this letter. The letter which confirmed his contract to Threat Wrestling. The letter which confirmed his contract to professional wrestling. Clayton continued to stare, not even reading the letter.

Was he ready to go professional?

Was he ready to put his life on the line at the tender age of 26?

Was he ready to leave his fiancée at home, while she watched him put his life in jeapordy?

All these questions were running through Clayton's head, but he contained the answer to none of them.

Only time will tell if he was ready, or if he made the biggest mistake ever by signing this contract.
