Realisation [1]
Starring: Dane Manetta

Looking around my surroundings taking in everything and everyone I see, I realise that I haven’t lived my life. I haven’t taken advantage of the things that have distinguished me from everyone else I used to know. I have yet to realise that one thing which I am good at, which will give me a newfound perspective… A life I haven’t yet lived.

Dane… Dane… Wake up

Maria said in a soft and slight Italian accent in a vein attempt to wake her me up from the deep and thought provoking sleep I found myself in.

Dane… we are going to miss the tryouts… wake up Dane!

Tryouts? What tryouts I thought to myself as I began to rise from my sleep. What tryouts indeed…

SHIT!!!

I shouted out loud as it hit me… the tryouts of which Maria spoke… wrestling tryouts…

Wrestling had been a life long interest in my family, right though from my great, great Grandfather… Dario Manetta. I once sat down with my father and sister and watch my great, great grandfather compete in what I still consider to be one of the greatest wrestling matches of all time. That match was what earned wrestling my respect and what made me think of becoming a wrestler myself.

As I scrambled to get my tracksuit bottoms on and make it to the tryouts in time, Maria pushed me on the bed as hard as she could. Maria, unlike other young women, still had the great looks and fashion sense… however didn’t really fit in at school because like me… she had a very big interest in the wrestling business. This didn’t go down to well with the other girls, especially when any of them said anything insulting Maria would threaten to bea… you get the picture.

Calm down! We have another four hours before they begin, plus Dad has agreed to lend me the car.

I looked at her as if I didn’t believe her. I turned and looked at the clock by the side of the bed. 7.30 AM. I couldn’t believe she woke me up like this, pulling up my tracksuit bottoms; I brushed passed her trying to show my displeasure as I headed downstairs.

Maria followed trying to explain it was a joke… I didn’t care if it was a joke; this trial is important to me as well as her… plus I’m just not a morning person.
