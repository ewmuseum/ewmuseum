How he became [Part 4]
Starring: Two-Face

I hate my life.

Vladimir glared into the dirty mirror shaking, his fists tightly clenched.

I hate my father.

Vladimir gently felt the newly acquired cut beneath his almost destroyed eye. Blood slowly trickled down the right side of his face.

I’ll kill him one day if I ever see him again.

Vladimir looked down on the floor. His father lay with a huge wound that was covered by a mass of blood. Glass was everywhere shattered from the shot.

Vladimir looked at his hand, the blood his father covered the majority of it. He let go of the glass bottle and the remainder of the bottle shattered next to his father.

I fucking hate you.

Vladimir leapt forward and punched the small mirror shattering it. He took deep menacing breaths and picked up the single suitcase that had originally stood motionless next to the door.

He looked back, his mother smiled.

He waved for possibly the last time.

Fuck you dad, and fuck your politics.

Vladimir opened the door and turned back once more. He conjured up all the contents in his mouth and spat it back at his unconscious father.

Vladimir would be gone by the morning.

New York better greet Vladimir well, because he certainly won’t be a happy customer.
