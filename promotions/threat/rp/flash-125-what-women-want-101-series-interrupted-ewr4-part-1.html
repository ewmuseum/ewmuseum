What Women Want: 101 - Series Interrupted - EWR4 - Part 1
Starring: Randall Alder-Tangk

The laptop was opened up and the sound of Windows chiming in could be heard. Randall Alder-Tangk sat at the table in his hotel room staring at the screen as he waited for his computer to open.

He glanced around the bench at the other stuff around him. The boredom of waiting for your computer to load up always sent your eyes wandering. A little clock sat by the computer and he checked the time.

9:31pm

His attention was then stolen by an envelope marked "thReat Wrestling Incorporated". The wondering and wandering mind of R.A.T had his hands quickly wrapping themselves around the envelope and prying its flap apart so he might get to the awaiting treasure inside. He wasn't sure if he were, so he checked around to see if Sugar might have stopped watching the DVD he'd hired.

No sign of Sugar.

He unfolded the letter and had a read of it...

"Congratulations Randall,

You have advanced into the next round of the International title tournament. Your victory over Sergey Kovhachek ensures that you have a place in the next round of the tournament, the quarter finals.

Not only have we written to congratulate you on your victory on Flaw 24, but we're letting you know who your opponent will be on this upcoming show, Flaw 25. Having also advanced to the next round, blind will be your opponent.

We hope to see a tough battle between you two as you are both up and coming young performers who will ensure that there is excitement for the fans and put on a top calibre performance.

Good luck to you and we hope to see you in the next round,

thReat Board of Directors."

R.A.T put the letter down and grinned as he thought about advancing into the next round. He began to let his mind wander of into dream time when he noticed that his rather slow computer had loaded up and was ready to go.

He sat there looking at the screen of his laptop, peering into nothingness really. He was trying to remember why he had even opened it in the first place. Why did he do it?

He remembered. He clicked on his dial-up and the modem began to kick in, he made sure he had his laptop plugged into the connection that the hotel had for those wanting to get some Internet access.

It was a slow connection, but it was probably just the internal modem on his computer that was slow. But eventually, he was loaded up and ready to rumble. He opened up his Internet Explorer and clicked "Stop" so he didn't have to wait for the homepage to open up.

He thought for a second as he clicked in the location bar, thinking of what he could do online. He began to type an address...

"[detail withheld]" he typed.

The page began to load with excruciating lime green images. He began to scan across the page, he'd heard his favourite wrestling simulator had come out online, but he wanted to be sure. He thought he'd go to the source of the information. To the creator's website. No matter how ghastly the green on the page was.

Latest News
[31 July 2003]
Extreme Warfare moves! As of today, Extreme Warfare has a brand new home. Please see the EW section.

His eyes lit up as he read that message on the site. He looked into the navigation area of the page and looked over the buttons.

"Home". Nope. "EW" was the next one underneath. He clicked it and waited for the page to load up.

The first thing he noticed was a new link. The brand new home for Extreme Warfare Revenge, and part 4 of the game series too. This was becoming quite the mission to getting the latest version of his favourite wrestling sim.

He clicked on the link, it opened up in a new window. Again with the waiting for a page to load up on his screen. The laptop was a little slow, but it did the job. And he had bought it second hand when he first moved away from home.

He immediately put his mouse over "Downloads" and a drop down menu could be seen. He moved across to "Games" and he clicked, and he had to wait for another page to upload onto his laptop.

He scrolled down the page to find what he was looking for. He had found the download of Extreme Warfare Revenge 4.

He clicked.

It was time to download and get thReat in there. That stupid Adam Ryland never put thReat on the game. R.A.T was confused as to why. Half the promotions on that stupid game couldn't get some decent ratings if they tried.

He hadn't even heard of Jersey All Pro Wrestling!

The downloading had begun...
