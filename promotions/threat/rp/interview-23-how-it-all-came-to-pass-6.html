How It All Came To Pass [6]
Starring: Jordan X Shabazz

The day after Sports Entertainment Anarchy 1

Jordan X Shabazz was fuming. How could a big league federation like this make him look like a bitch and know that he wouldn’t get revenge? How could they allow one of their top stars to just pick up and quit?

Jordan didn’t know what was next for him, so instead of continuing on with the tour of SE, Jordan skipped house shows, and flew home to Albany. As soon as he got home, he called Andrew Styles on Styles’ cell phone.

“Andrew, what do you want me to do now? I got rocked in the face and looked like a bitch for not getting revenge, and Maverick’s gone now.” Jordan said angrily into the phone.

Andrew Styles tried his best to calm Jordan down, “Don’t worry about it Jordan, we’ll bring in some new guys and before you know it you’ll have another angle. Look, I’m really busy right now, I’ll talk to you later, okay?”

“Whatever.”

Jordan X Shabazz hung up the phone without saying goodbye, he wasn’t going to head on tour with SE through the house shows or the Anarchy television tapings, he was going to sit his ass home and let the idiots who ran the promotion to scramble with a replacement for him.

Shabazz sat at home through Anarchy 2 and Anarchy 3. Then Shabazz got a call from Andrew Styles.

“Okay, we have an angle for you. We just signed a new guy named Paul Pearson, he’s half African American and half Native American, I’m sure you guys can work something out. Come to the next show, your plane ticket is already in the mail.”

Jordan X Shabazz received his plane ticket the next day, and he was off to Anarchy 4. He worked out a decent two week angle with Paul Pearson that was going to lead to a Pay Per View match at SE’s first Pay Per View, Destiny’s Demise.

Everything went well, they gelled, they worked an excellent Pay Per View match with one another. That was the end of their feud, it was short, just two weeks, but it had put Jordan X Shabazz over as a psychotic, racist, heel, and he loved it. His out of the ring persona wasn’t that far from what he played in character. Secretly he harboured an almost jealous rage against Caucasians and almost every other single race. He even hated a lot of African Americans, his supposed ‘people.’

Like his in ring persona, Jordan X Shabazz did some Civil Rights work in communities around America. He did this to boost his public image, not because he wanted to help out his ‘people.’

Jordan X Shabazz showed up to SE’s Los Angeles office to pick up his pay check, when he arrived there, he realized that he wasn’t the only one. Also there were a few other wrestlers there, including Main Eventer Shawn Jaxxon, Tandem, and Scott Riktor.

Ray Chavez came into the waiting room where they all were, and spoke, “Sorry boys, we’re having some problems right now, you should get your pay checks in the next two weeks.”

Everybody seemed satisfied with this statement, except Jordan X Shabazz, “Are we getting our Pay Per View bonuses as well?”

“That should take an additional two weeks.”

“No, fuck that. I want all of my fucking pay right now. I signed a contract expecting to be paid like anybody else who works for an organization. You and Andrew have continually screwed me out of money for the past few weeks now, and I’m not taking it anymore. I’m not like these honkeys who are gullible and believe every single bullshit lie that you tell them. I know you’re not going to pay us. You’re going to do what you did with CDLL, close up shop, and leave the boys out of a job.” Shabazz said with force, trying to intimidate his boss.

“Would you like to talk to me in my office, Jordan?”

“No, fuck you. I quit.”

Jordan X Shabazz turned around and walked towards the exit, as he reached the door, he turned around and shouted one more thing to Ray, “And if I don’t get my check by Tuesday, I’m suing for it.”

And that was the end of Jordan X Shabazz in Sports Entertainment.
