Story Through Theme Music Two: "Live and Let Die" - Guns N' Roses
Starring: Twizzy

You were young and your heart was an open book.
You used to say, ‘live and let live.’
You know you did, you know you did, you know you did.
How did this ever change in this world in which we live in makes you give and cry.
Say, ‘live and let die.’
Live and let die.

Everything had seemed so hopeless. It was all so hopeless and yet Trash held hope. He knew that one day his time would come. He knew that he could do it. He knew that if everything was set right and the circumstances were just perfect then he would be the greatest and all of his loss and misfortune would pay off. It never did. Instead the CWU went bankrupt after the government found out about a few investors using the fed for various illegal markets. The CWU was bought out by Global Championship Wrestling and the entire roster was given a choice to go to GCW or be fined a small fee and be free to go on their way. Trash decided to go to GCW.

What does it matter to ya when you’ve got a job to do?
You’ve got to do it well.
You’ve got to give yourself another hand!

He began his career in GCW strong while getting into a heated feud with John Reed he eventually decided that John Reed was in fact a good man. Entering a battle royal he got second place in he vowed that next year he would win that same battle royal as he came so close so early in his GCW career. He made his prophecy and began to simply wrestle in GCW. He went day to day, but was not very successful. Trash’s GCW time was like his CWU’s in that things went wrong, but in CWU people were screwing him; in GCW they were just winning.

Used to say, ‘live and let live.’
You know you did, you know you did, you know you did.
How did this ever change in this world in which we live in makes you give in and cry.
Say, ‘live and let die.’
Live and let die.

Trash was losing and things must change. “Live and Let Die” was not going to found playing in GCW ever again. Trash would not wrestle in GCW ever again either. No, things needed to change…
