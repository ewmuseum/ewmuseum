Lurking Death Part 6
Starring: Tyler Davidson

Heather walked slowly along the moonlit streets of New York City without a cloud in the sky as the stars twinkled high above her head. She stopped and took a look right above her and smiled as she stood there just admiring the looks of the dark night sky and not realizing that she was being watched. She continued her walk and made a quick turn around the corner. She had been gone for over half an hour already and knew it was just about time that she get back to the apartment before Jess had an attack of paranoia. Anytime she stayed in New York while Tyler and Dan were off on business Jess had always gone to great lengths to make sure she was okay.

It was almost like having an overprotective and older sister. While Jess was nowhere near being an old woman she had four years on Heather and was already married obviously. Daniel and her, even found out recently that the two of them would be parents in about eight months time. Maybe Jess was just looking to get a head start on the overprotective stuff.

Heather continued walking and made her way back to the apartment but she never did get that far. Just as she walked past an alley a gloved hand reached out and wrapped its hand firmly around her mouth cutting off any possible scream and with the other the person grabbed her by the throat and yanked her into the alley. The person threw Heather down to the ground and she slammed her head hard on the concrete underneath her. Through a shroud of tears filling her eyes she looked up to see who it was that stood above her and standing there of course was Erin Christoph.

“W-w-what do you want?” Heather tried asking as she did her best to move away from the man. Her only answer was a violent kick right to her jaw that sent her facedown to the concrete once again as Christoph moved slowly closer as though he was toying with her. Heather looked up once again and saw the snarling face of Christoph moving closer inch by inch before she quickly raised a hand and raked her nails right across his face opening a large gash.

Heather tried making a break for it knowing that she was in serious danger but her actions were all in vain as Christoph would not be foiled by such an easy prey. He grabbed her by the hair just as she ran past and with all his strength flung her head first into the wall behind them. Heather hit the wall and barely conscious she slipped down to the ground below and lay in a pile of papers and garbage as Christoph stepped to her and she again tried pleading. “W-what do you want?” was all she could barely even whisper as Christoph just smiled.

“To send a message to your dear old Tyler of course,” he answered with a grin. ”And to show you my dear Heather just what I’m capable of. I’ve watched you and that other woman for weeks now and I must say I’m quite curious as to just what things look like under those clothes of yours.”
A look of horror crossed the bruised face of Heather as she suddenly realized just what Christoph had planned. He moved slowly in closer hovering over her before he delivered one final punch down to her face and kneeled down over her unconscious body. With that he slowly began to disrobe her and take one of the perks included in his assignment. She was a beautiful girl after all he thought and smiled as he began.
