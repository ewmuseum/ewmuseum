Return To Grace [Part 2]
Starring: Mike 'Blindside' Jevo

Setting: Airport Station
Location: Nearing my Destination..
Time: 1:15 A.M.

Mike Jevo stood at the entraceway of the airport station, bags in hand, awaiting his limousine.

A limo picking Mike Jevo up, was just one of a million other extroidanary things that would come alongside this joining into Threat Wrestling. Aside the glamour and luxury, he would also have a great chance to showcase his talents once more.. the one thing he longed more than anything.

Mike Jevo stood there for at least 10 minutes, before walking over to a nearby coffee stand in the hussle and bussle of hundreds of airline travelers.

“Excuse me..” Mike said, as a beautiful blonde haired woman about his age turned around.

She smiled at him, notively flurting, before finally answering.

“Yes, sir? What may I do for you?” She said to him.

“I’d like a cappuchino, if that’s alright.” Mike answered her.

She turned around quickly, starting on the cappuchino, and Mike dug deep down into his pockets, pulling out a five dollar bill. The woman turned around, and Mike set the money down on the counter.

“Here you go, sir..” the woman said, handing Mike his cappuchino. “And your total estimates to..... three dollars and fifty cents.”

She gave him his change back, with another quick smile and Mike walked off to his original spot once more.

Half an hour later.

Mike Jevo had been standing in this same spot for over thirty minutes, when finally, a short & stout man approached him.

“Excuse me, sir.. are you Mr. Mike Jevo? The recently signed guy in Threat?” He asked.

“Yeah.. that’s me.” Mike answered, annoyingly.

The man took hold of Mike’s luggage, and began to walk off.

“Follow me, sir.. the limo is here for you.” The little man said, leading the way.

Mike gradually followed, with a beaming smile on his face.

He was nearly there..
