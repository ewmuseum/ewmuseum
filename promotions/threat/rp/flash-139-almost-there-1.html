Almost There [1]
Starring: Chris "Neverwhere" McCluskey

So what’s it like being a thReat superstar? A world renowned icon in the wrestling industry? A hero to both the male and female gender alike? Well, I’ll answer those questions for you, in a random order.

Enrique Iglesias once sang “I can be your hero, baby.” Well, he’s lying. What is a hero? A guy with a ridiculous goatee, who made his career on the back of his father’s success? Absolutely not. Enrique Iglesias isn’t a hero, yet he is adored by many heterosexual women, and the odd man who’s’ sexuality is in question. I am a hero, yet everybody this side of the Atlantic Ocean; and I suppose this side of the Pacific Ocean too; hate me. I find myself constantly asking the question, “why?” It all boils down to jealousy, sadly.

As each week passes, I get booed more and more. The reason for this phenomenon is quite clear: I keep progressing to the next round of the International championship title tournament.

In the qualifying round, I was paired with… I forget who it was. Oh that’s right, Dane Manetta. I’ve had number twos that lasted more than that wrestling bout did.

My next outing was in the quarterfinals this passed week, against Christian Elijah. Or at least it was supposed to be Elijah, until he “dared”, i.e. begged Frankie Focker to take his place in the match, so he wouldn’t have to suffer the consequences. Another opponent, another victory. This seems like a nice little routine that I’ve got going here.

The semi-finals will sure be interesting. Vincent Pembridge to face either R.A.T. or Jere Casarella. Ah Jere, my good friend. You may have pinned me a fortnight ago, but that’s going to be your crowning moment in your career. One to tell the grandchildren about. “Listen Joey, you know that hall of fame wrestler who won all those world championships and then became President of the United States? I beat him once.” Don’t deny it Jere, you can’t wait for that moment to come. As for Vincent Pembridge; well let’s just say that I don’t normally like the English, but since he’s from the red half of Manchester, I can make an exception. As for R.A.T., well he still has difficulty getting into an “R” rated movie, so let’s swiftly move on.

haTE. Something I am very familiar with. Something I deal with every day of my life. You can just feel the hatred every time I step through the curtain into the arena. They love me, they really love me! No wait, they don’t. For the last two months or so, I’ve grown accustomed to the boos. To the jeers. To the hate-mail and death-threats. For them, wrestling is nothing more than a place to go and boo people who they don’t like, and to throw slanderous and defamatory accusations at them. If they were to do it in the street, I would sue them. But they’re too chicken to do it when they’re on their own in “public”. But when they’re surrounded by 19,999 other idiots and “protected” by the crappy guard-rail, then they’ve got balls as big as globes, it seems. Oh boy is there going to be egg on their faces.

And what is my point to all this exactly? There is none. This was all pointless. I just didn’t have anything better to write about, or any brighter ideas. So meh.
