I Am..
Starring: Mike 'Blindside' Jevo

Sin. Swift. Slay.

I am Michael Gregory Vincent. I am known in my wrestling profession as Mike 'Blindside' Jevo, one of the most renounced and rejoiced professional wrestler's in this business. This is just one, of many future Chronicles in my career. This is my life, and this is who I am.

--

Setting: Eastmont Mall; Autograph Signing
Location: Oakland, CA
Time: 4:57 A.M

“Sure,” was Mike Jevo’s response to the woman’s question. “You have a pen and a pad, or anything I can write my signature down on?”

The women fiddled throughout her pockets, finally finding a piece of paper and a pen, and then handed it to the astounded superstar.

“Yes, sir.. I sure do!” The women said, her arms crossed across her chest gleefully.. in utter joy.

The superstar known as Mike Jevo simply smiled, and signed his autograph on the woman’s paper before handing her back the pad and pen.

“And, there ya go.. your very on signature of mines..” said the pro-wrestler cockily, a smirk forming across his face.

The woman began to scream, noticeably, before stopping herself and putting a hand to her mouth, and the other to an outstretched hand of Mike Jevo’s.

“Thank you so much, Mr. Jevo! I mean.. this is a dream come true! I would never imagine.. t-t-his would happen to me..” the woman stammered, as a security guard marched her off, as a young boy stepped up to the table in front of Mike Jevo for the autograph signing.

The child wore dark blue demin jeans, some old Airwalk shoes and a torn yellow sweater that had some Old English letters written across. The boy grimly looked at Mike Jevo, and for a couple of seconds never let a word slip from his mouth, before finally breaking his silence.

“So.. you’re Mike Jevo, huh?” he asked..

The question was extremely stupid, considering this was Mike Jevo’s autograph signing.. but he couldn't just blow off this child like that.

“Uhhh.. yeah,” Mike Jevo said chuckling. “And what can I do for you today, kid?”

The boy stood there a moment, and duck into his pocket, pulling out a piece of paper with some letters jotted on it, and slipped it to Mike.

“I admired your work since, and before the OCW days. It just doesn’t feel right to see a great superstar such as yourself fall deep into a pit of nothingness. Read that paper I just gave you, it could be your sparking return..” The boy said to Mike Jevo, his face expressionless.

Mike Jevo looked down at the paper, and read some of the letters on it.. the letters were to a certain address, with a phone number on it.. at the bottom of the small piece of paper, something caught Mike’s eye quickly, and he looked up quickly..

“What is..” Mike began, looking up to see that the boy was no longer there.

Mike then looked back down at the paper, his eyebrows raising as if questioning himself.

“Threat Wrestling?” Mike Jevo said to himself out loud.
