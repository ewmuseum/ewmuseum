Story Through Theme Music Three: "Paint It Black" - The Rolling Stones
Starring: Twizzy

I see a red door and I want it painted black.
No colors anymore I want them to turn black.
I see the girls walk by dressed in their summer clothes.
I have to turn my head until my darkness goes.

Trash was angry. In GCW all he did was nothing and hate it. He kept blowing his chances and decided that he would change a few simple things to give himself a little push of momentum. This losing streak was certainly nothing to be proud of. A name change to Twisted, a theme music change to The Rolling Stones’ “Paint It Black” and an angrier, darker attitude was what he thought was in order.

I see a line of cars and they're all painted black.
With flowers and my love, both never to come back.
I see people turn their heads and quickly look away.
Like a newborn baby it just happens everyday.

Twisted became the man known as Trash. He thought it was necessary. He thought it was what he needed. He became angrier and more violent. He didn’t care what was going to happen. He was that animal again, only this time that animal was blinded by loyalty. He was no lackey, no hired goon this time. This time he was in command and whoever decided to like his ideals were at his command. He was that animal that had surfaced in The New Breed, but this time would be different. This time he was just Twisted and not Trash.

I look inside myself and see my heart is black.
I see my red door and it has been painted black.
Maybe then I'll fade away and not have to face the facts.
It's not easy facing up when your whole world is black.

Anger did nothing for him. He was just another guy trying to be better than he was. He was nothing and it was quite evident he never would be anything. Everyone saw it. Everyone saw it except Twisted. He would do anything to be the best. ‘One day,’ he said to himself. ‘One day I’ll be the best and one day all this harshness and painful misfortune will pay off.’

No more will my green sea go turn a deeper blue.
I could not foresee this thing happening to you.
If I look hard enough into the setting sun.
My love will laugh with me before the morning comes.

One day would never come and each day seemed worse than the last. Twisted started losing to everyone. Twisted sucked. He hoped though. One day.

I see a red door and I want it painted black.
No colors anymore I want them to turn black.
I see the girls walk by dressed in their summer clothes.
I have to turn my head until my darkness goes.

Twisted kept hoping. Twisted was here and now. This is what he always wanted to be. This is what he had dreamed of doing. He knew he could be a success, but in actuality whatever he tried he failed at. He was losing more than expected. He was too bad to be booked. He was a nothing.

Hmm, hmm, hmm...

‘One day I’ll be the best and one day all this harshness and painful misfortune will pay off.’

I wanna see it painted black, painted black.
Black as night, black as coal.
I wanna see the sun, blotted out from the sky.
I wanna see it painted, painted, painted, painted black.
Yeah.

‘… and I will do anything to get to that day.’
