Past and Present [2]
Starring: The Cursed One

He walked over carefully to the crowd, half expecting some kind of object to fall violently. Something heavy and fat, along the lines of a piano or a scaffold dangling from some wires. He figured that something was about to come crashing down, brought to you by gravity. But, as he stepped closer, he realized that nothing had happened.

At least not yet.

The small sea of people stared up into the colorless sky. All their eyes collectively gazed awkwardly towards the clouds drifting overhead, but they didn’t seem to be staring at that. No one in their right mind would just stand in a crowd staring up at a bunch of drifting million pound pieces of cotton floating up in the sky.

But, as the obstacles that hindered his view began to pass by, including the fire escapes swarming the side of the buildings and the heavy snow fall that had sped up in the last few minutes, he could then see what had all their attention.

Two feet, dressed in matching sneakers, dangled over the edge of one of many parallel apartment buildings. A fresh breath rose vacantly into the sky above those feet, and an arm suddenly shot out for one quick glance before being sucked back in towards whoever sat there. Somewhere, hidden behind that protruding slab of concrete, sat someone whose thoughts had given them something not many would feel. Those thoughts gave them a reason for staring into the eyes of death.

He looked back down at the people standing in the streets, staring blankly as if they could give a shit about this person and their feelings. He laughed, shook his head, and saw the brown door, standing silent in the alcove of the corner of the building.

One last glance at those sons of bitches standing on the street filled his eyes before he stepped inside the doorway. His eyes then adjusted to the dark staircase climbing the wall before him, and his feet almost immediately started up.

And there were no second thoughts.

-----

Two feet, wearing two black boots that matched the August sky, dangled over the edge of the apartment complex standing obscurely amongst the forest of buildings. As the stars twinkled unnoticeably in the city skyline, his head leaned over the edge, eyes gazing down at the black concrete and the ordinary headlights that sparked momentary life in the alleyways and the empty stores below.

But, as the obstacles that hindered his thoughts began to fill his mind, including the distinct memories behind the picture he held in his hand and the events of the passed few weeks, a door swung open behind him and smacked the side of the exposed structure on the rooftop.

“Hey. What the hell are ya doin up here at this time of day?”

His head turned slowly to the side, where the corner of his left eye stared impassively at the somewhat chubby man that stood in the door way. The man carried an angered expression on his slothful face, and he didn’t seem to be too interested in putting up with any shit at that hour.

“Nothing. Something happened to me here once, and I just wanted to come back once and visit the place before I went off to Las Vegas.”

The man, who had been pissed, took on a new look and calmed his expression. His eyes, once wide, were now staring down at the rooftop surface. His mouth, once irate, felt his tongue shot out and lick away the anger that had built up dryness. The stranger then walked up to him, his arms crossed on his chest, and then leaned up against the wall that stood on the edge of the building.

“Well, whatever. You aren’t harming anyone anyway, so what would it hurt if you just sat there and thought things over. I know I wouldn’t want anyone to fuck with me at a time like that.” said that man, his arms resting on the piece of concrete jutting out of the structure.

He nodded, and then brought his eyes back down to the picture resting in his right hand. The man standing next to him tried to look over his shoulder and see what the picture, but the darkness didn’t allow his eyes to observe the image.

“What’s the picture of?” he asked.

“Nothing at all. Just something that reminds me of the time I had on this roof.”

“I see. Any reason you decided to come back?”

“Nothing specific. It’s just been an interesting month to say the least. Found this picture randomly a few days ago, and decided that I’d come pay a visit. Why not?”

“What do you mean by an interesting month? What happened to ya, if you don’t mind me asking?”

“Nothing really. I’m just working for this wrestling promotion, and I was put into this big tournament for a championship. And the outcome has been pretty much been thought provoking. Made me think of some things again, including this incident oddly enough.”

The man nodded his head blankly, telling him that he wasn’t paying real attention but was only pretending to be interested in his words. The stranger then noticed he was looking into his eyes, and sort of panicked a question out of his mouth.

“Well, how’d that tournament thing go?” he said quickly.

“Nothing important happened really. It started for me by facing this man named Twizzy. Tell ya the truth I really didn’t know him that well so I didn’t know what to expect when I faced him. But I went out there and I fought the match. And in the end, I guess you could say the best man won. I have to give Twizzy all the credit in the world. To tell you the truth, he wanted it more then I did.”

He looked at the picture again, his eyes having already forgotten the sight behind the image. There were all his faint memories, trapped in one still frame resting in his hands like a holy cross resting in the hands of a priest. He wanted to keep those memories in his heart, and never forget them. He was going to hold onto that picture forever.

And there were no second thoughts.

“Well what does that have to do with this rooftop?”

“Nothing. Just something that gives me a similar feeling.”

Somewhere above their heads, the roar of a jet engine ripped apart the sky. His thoughts were embedded into his brain, and that was all he could think about now. The past was the past, and, just like this damn picture, there was no changing it. There was nothing he could do about it.

At least not yet.
