Starring: General David Sanders

THE FLASHBACK:

"As you all know, this is the ultimate test for training." The six foot nine-inch mammoth said. He was our trainer. We were to obey his every order. If not, we suffered the consequences. This wasn't a normal summer camp. It was camp hell.

"You will each be set with a look a like M4A1. You are to shoot anything that moves; however, if you shoot a member of your team, you can consider your ass as good as gone."

This guy had it out for me ever since the first week. It was a mishap in orders so he hated me. So he was still out to screw me in any possible way he could.

Even in this training mission - a full combat paintball war - he wanted me to lose. I was the best one out there and he knew it. He tried to stop. Name was Sergeant Charles Hoffman. Biggest son-of-a-bitch America has ever raised.

"This is full-contact. These paintballs that you think you can just take hits from, are to be hitting you at approximately... 175 miles per our."

Some of the rejects around me gasped. What, have they never taken a bullet before? I still wasn't afraid. Screw that. I was just going to go out there and blow up shit. That's all I cared about. And to hopefully some day find my father... God rest his soul.

"Alright, men! The two sides are posted in your cabins. You will study them, you will memorize them, and you will follow them. Move out."

We all started walking away from our uptight position, when an arm stopped me. It was Hoffman.

"Hey, Sanders, you pull anything funny here and I swear you are out of here so fast you won't remember what happened." He inched closer to my face, screaming louder and louder each second. "You hear me? WHAT? DO YOU HEAR ME?!"

I stood there, taking it all in. He wanted to break my surface, and I wasn't going to let that fucking bastard.

"Yes, sir." I replied, with no emotion. I had to pretend nothing happened.

"NOW GET DOWN AND GIVE ME 50 SOLDIER!"

Fucking asshole.
