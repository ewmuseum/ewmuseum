How It All Came To Pass [2]
Starring: Jordan X Shabazz

Saturday April 21st, 2002
Columbus, Ohio

He had almost made it. It was almost time for Jordan to blow up. He was wrestling basically in the minor leagues. New True Intense Championship Wrestling, better known as the NTICW. It was the place where Jordan had spent the past few months honing his skills in preparation of going to the big times, either Primetime Championship Wrestling, or Old School Wrestling. He had his sights set on Primetime Championship Wrestling, where they would appreciate his in ring work rate better than in Old School Wrestling.

He had gotten himself over with the crowd as a mega heel by frequently appearing on the radio bashing white people and anything a white person would do. He made his living off of critiquing people. His run in the NTICW was coming to an end, he was still Extreme Champion, and he was set to take the pin fall victory over his opponent, despite the fact that he was leaving.

Jordan looked out into the crowd, there was no more than 1, 000 people there, but it was going to be broadcasted live on national television, so a lot of people would be watching. He had to tear the house down with his opponent, Amorphis. A young up and comer who was gaining mad respect for his work ethic and stiff in ring work.

“What If I Was White” blared over the PA system as Shabazz looked behind him and his manager, Jake Jackson, came into view. The boos were starting already from the fans. Shabazz loved it. He was over.

Jordan X Shabazz stepped through the curtains with the NTICW Extreme Championship strapped firmly around his waist, and his manager at his side.

Twenty minutes later.

Jordan X Shabazz slowly walked from the ring backstage. He was tired and sweaty from a good fifteen minute match with Amorphis, one of his longest ever. He had picked up the victory and looked like a million bucks.

“The white man can’t hold a good black man down,” Shabazz muttered as he threw the NTICW Extreme Title on the bench beside him. He was leaving the fed that made him a star in the Wrestling Tattler community, and he wasn’t going to miss the place in the least. It was brimming with talent, but the constant running of small arenas and lack of fanfare is what bothered Jordan the most. He wanted to move up.

“The Legacy” Lance Perfection, main event wrestler, United States Champion, and head booker of the NTICW came into Jordan’s locker room without knocking. He was very angry. He ran the NTICW and was lobbying with the head honchos of the Wrestling Tattler organization to give NTICW more television time and allow it to become a big federation, up there with the likes of PCW and OSW, and to do so, he needed all of his top stars, Jordan X Shabazz happened to be one of the people that he wanted to be his top star. When Shabazz told him he was leaving to PCW, Lance Perfection felt as if he had been stabbed in the back by the man he had helped throughout his tenure.

“Jordan, you know I don’t want you to go,” Perfection said calmly, which was hard for him, because he was brimming with anger. Not only was Jordan X Shabazz leaving, but a man whom Shabazz had feuded with in the NTICW, Neo Nazi, Mark Makonie was leaving as well. Two potential big stars for the promotion were bolting.

“I’m going, Lance, I told you that already. Nothing can change my mind.”

“Fine, suit yourself. Give me my belt back.”

Jordan X Shabazz stood up, and dropped the Extreme Title at the feet of Lance Perfection. He grabbed his bags, and he walked towards the parking lot. He was to leave alone, Jake Jackson was going to stay in the NTICW as a road agent and a part of the booking team. Friendship, titles, money, nothing could stop him from reaching his goal.

Jordan X Shabazz was going to the big time.
