Concerns
Starring: 'Superstar' Vince Jacobs

Vince is sitting in a chair in his study with a remote in his hand. He looks to be watching videotapes, as he is fast forward and reversing some of the footage. The footage is of himself and some of his matches he has competed in around the world.

Vince presses a button on the remote again and sighs. The look on his face is not of confidence it is a look of worry. He has never been the type to wear is emotions out on his sleeve unless it was directed to one Sean Stevens.

A lot of questions flooded Vince’s head.

“Will Threat Wrestling last?”

“Will I be able to perform up to my standards?”

“What will the new roster be like?”

“Will Hackett actually use my talents this time around?”

All these questions in Vince’s head had never been there before. These were not questions; they seem to be more like concerns. When did Vince Jacobs ever become concerned about anything having to do with wrestling?

Vince thought for a second before pressing the button to the remote to shut down the tape. He stood up and looked out of his window into the California night.

Vince started to rub his chin. “I have never doubted myself before why start now”

“I am the same ol’ SVJ and no one can deny that.” Vince said to himself still looking out of the window.

“The same cocky son of a bitch” Vince said with a grin as the cameras fade out.
