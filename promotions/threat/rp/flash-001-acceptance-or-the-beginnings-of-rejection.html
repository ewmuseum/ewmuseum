Acceptance? Or the beginnings of Rejection?
Starring: Wolf

Wolf’s dream had been realised. Sure, he’d had an easy start in life, and a lot of things had been gifted to him, but he still worked damn hard to train as a pro wrestler. Some might say even that was gifted to him, because he was born lucky, with the size and natural ability to become any variety of professional sportsman, but to Wolf he’d overcome adversity. Adversity to Wolf was simply something he had to struggle a little with. Rejection… didn’t exist.

People ask me why do I have this dream.

Simple.

It’s the American dream, and I am as American as apple pie. Well, maybe not apple pie, but I’m as American as “Tobacco” or “French Fries”. I’ve dreamt about this day for months now, maybe even years… but it’s only been months since this dream has become somewhat attainable, since it became… real. It’d always been something of a fantasy. It’s every kids dream isn’t it? To become a super hero, known across the world, marvelled at by kids and adults alike? It takes a harsh slap in the face for you to realise that you’re almost there, and that slap in the face came today, when I opened my mail, and found myself signed up to wrestle against Steve Solo, or something, at “Sunday Night Flaw 13”.

Apparently this Steve Solvent had been a part of thReat’s original endeavour, but to be honest, that meant nothing to me. All I knew is that the months of training had paid off, and I was signed to wrestle my first professional match.

The feeling sunk in and I realised it… my dreams were coming true.

There’s no glamour story here, I’m just a guy, about to do what at sometime every guy has wanted to do. I’m gonna be rich, I’m gonna be famous, and the crowd of people are gonna be chanting my name… wolf… Wolf... WOLF!!!

As Wolf let the thReat letter drop to the floor, engulfed in his dreams, his fantasies took over. Dreams of legends of the past, and hoped of becoming a legend of the future. Adversity was about to take on a whole new meaning. Rejection? About to be clearly defined for him.

He had no idea what he was getting in to.
