The Journey To The Top(Pt. 3 Pre Show Jitters)
Starring: Mat Walton

7/07/2003 5:30 PM Ninety minutes from Show Time

Mat Walton ran off of the RV like a kid who was going to Disney World for the first time. He was the most excited he had even been in his life. As the four men entered the Calgary Sun Arena an even greater sense of shock and awe came across Mat.

'You excited or what kiddo?" Andrew Martin asked his obviously excited Nephew.

'You bet,' Walton responded with a huge grin.

The dress for the men was for the most part casual. Williams and Cage were wearing matching red and black track suits with "Team Walton" on the back of the jacket and "Trainer on the right sleeve of the jacket. Mat was wearing a blue University of North Carolina number twenty-three with Jordan written on the back and the same coloured shorts with white and blue sneakers and white socks. The only one who broke this "rule" was Andrew Martin. Martin was decked out in his usual business like attire. Martin was dressed in a navy blue suit with a white dress shirt underneath and a black neck tie to top it all off along with black dress shoes and navy blue socks.

'Always dress to impress," He said, "It doesn't matter the venue because all the world's a stage'

Walton was always amazed at his Uncle's sense of style and him always wanting to wear a suit no matter what the occasion and no matter how big the arena was. This arena was definitely no Madison Square Garden though. It was run-down with poor lighting and stains all over the wall. But it didn't really matter to Mat anyways because this could be his shot at going somewhere. The group of them stopped at and whom Martin got into immediate conversation with.

Martin turned around and looked at Mat 'Mathew this is Jack Armstrong, he is the Promoter of this Promotion and is a very influential man on the Independent wrestling scene,' Martin said, 'Jack, this is my Nephew Mathew,'

'Pleasure to meet you son,' The large man said as he stuck out his hand which Mat shook.

Armstrong was very much like a white Don King. He was a very large man wearing a black pinstripe suit with Gold and Diamonds draped all over him and a goatee. The only real difference between the two men besides their skin colour was that instead of having hair that looks like you got your finger caught in an electrical socket, Jack had no hair atop his head to speak of.

'Pleasure to meet you too Mr. Armstrong,' Mat said back with a smile. The man had the firmest handshake Mat had ever felt . He almost let out a girlish scream because he thought he was going to have his hand crushed.

'Nice to see you again Jack,' Martin said before turning back to the group of three, 'Let's go meet your opponent for tonight'

The group of them continued down the hallways and corridors of the arena, passing various performers and other workers along the way before coming to a halt yet again in front of a door that read 'Patrick Bauer.'

'This is the place,' Martin said aloud to the group.

Martin knocked on the door and after a short wait a man not much taller than Mat came to the door.

'How are you doing son?' Martin questioned the young man.

'Piss off!' The Irish-sounding youngster said and then slammed the door in the face of Martin.

'Crabby little bugger isn't he.' Martin thought to himself. He pounded on the door yet again.

'What the hell do you want!' Bauer yelled as opened the door.

'Hi,' Martin opened up 'I thou....' the young man tried to shut the door on him but Martin wedged himself between the door and forced it open.

'I just thought I'd drop by and make sure you meet your opponent' Martin said.

'Alright, you've got three minutes!' The Irishman said to them.

'Well, this is Matthew and he'll be...' Martin was cut off again.

'Oh did I say three minutes I meant thirty seconds now get the hell out!' The Irishman screamed as he forced them out the door.

'Well that didn't go too well,' Walton said to his now fuming Uncle.

'Listen, you guys head to the dressing room and get ready for tonight I've got to conduct a little business with myself, this prick, and Mister Armstrong'
