Chapter IV
Starring: Ø

"Story of a Strongman"
Page Four (Of Five)

"Cut it all off." Santino instructed the barber, who in turn opened a drawer in search of his trusty blade. A heavily-wrapped Wolfgang Churchill waited patiently in his chair, gawking at his reflection in the mirror. He hardly recognized himself, as over five inches of hair had been eradicated from his head. The arduous task of removing such a large mass of hair seemed to weigh on the barber, as his own curly locks were mopped with sweat. Cutting Churchill's hair took over an hour, and it looked like the mangy beard might take just as long.

"Thanks for all the gifts you've given me, Santa." Wolfgang said with a teasing sneer. Santino wasn't smiling.

"I hated that fucking nickname ever since you gave it to me a few years ago, and I don't want to hear it again."

Churchill nodded as the chair slowly reclined. Moments later, a warm, fluffy shaving lather was carefully applied to his face. He felt its warmth slide down his face as the barber hurriedly sharpened his blade on a long leather strap. He turned back around, and Wolfgang was temporarily blinded by a glare that reflected on the tool's edge. With a magnificent downward stroke, The barber lopped a huge chunk of hair from his cheek. His final transformation had begun.

"What's on your mind?" Santino said as he slowly hovered over Churchill. "Don't tell me 'nothing,' either. It's something...and I want to know what that something is."

"I'm was just thinking."

"About what?"

"Seriously? The old days... when I wrestled. Running into you reminded me of my time in Rival. It's funny how careers turn out. It was my first federation and I was never quite the same when it closed. I had stints--"

"--And I followed those 'stints' in other places," Santino interjected, "I noticed you dropped 'nØt.' You said goodbye to the facepaint and flair. You tried to reinvent yourself by going around and selling yourself as...yourself. What made you think wrestling fans wanted to see you? They wanted your persona. C'mon. Let's be honest, friend. You had a good thing going and you screwed it up."

Churchill took quick offence and his head bobbed, the blade slicing a small cut in his chin. The barber cursed himself and apologized. He pressed a towel to the open wound and looked at Santino like a child who had thrown a baseball through his next door neighbor's window. The barber was let off easy with a mere scoff from Santino.

"See, you wanting to pound my face just then reminded me more of nØt than you. You are you. nØt...nØt is a monster. You gave nØt to us in and out of the ring. Pops may be getting older and more senile, but that's how he remembers you. An unstoppable force that worked in silence. You conducted yourself with a kind of class that no one can ever learn, but only try to imitate. If you find a way to bring that O with a slash back out in the open, I don't doubt that you'll be 'big' again."

"Well, I guess I've learned from my mistakes," Wolfgang's burly voice admitted, "If only I had a second chance..." Wolfgang trailed as his eyes raised up and made contact with Santino, who was already beginning to form a mischievious smile.

"Don't tell me you can get me back in the business..."

"Hey, I'm not telling you anything," Belfonte said defensively with a good-natured smile. He looked at his watch and appeared distressed. "Shit. Um, Wolfgang, I gotta split." He dug in his pockets and produced a crumpled wad of bills and a small gadget. "Here's $500 and Fredo's phone. When you get done here, pay this man and give him a big tip for his troubles. Do whatever you want with the rest. Give me a call tomorrow from whatever hotel you're staying at, and we'll head over to the Estate tomorrow afternoon-- Are you getting all of this?"

"Loud and clear," Wolfgang replied, although he was incredibly confused as to why Fredo's phone was now in his possession. He reached out from under the apron and took the money and phone from Santino's flattened palm. Belfonte smiled.

"All right, Wolf. I'm out of here," Santino said with a smile. He opened the door and was greeted with the inviting sound of colliding bells and glass. Prying the door open with an arm, Santino looked back. "Remember, pops remembers you as that monster... not just some average Joe. Try not and disappoint him, all right?"

"I never disappoint," Wolfgang shot back assuredly. The Belfonte prince sneered and mouthed "No you don't." silently. He then exited the shop, passing the swiveling red, white, and blue peppermint stripes and parking meters to arrive at his sedan parked in its usual spot: next to the sidewalk.

Inside, Wolfgang's chair slowly rose and clicked into an upright position. Churchill felt as if he needed to lift his lower jaw from the floor as he observed his own bald face. The complete absence of facial hair came as quite a shock to him, but he was surprisingly satisfied with the results. He generously paid the barber $80 for his efforts, and unclasped his apron by himself. He handed it over and hastily took to the New York streets.

After all, a countdown had begun. He had 24 hours to find his true self again...to find Ø.
