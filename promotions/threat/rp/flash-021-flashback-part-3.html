Flashback [Part 3]
Starring: General David Sanders

We were all ready. My team - filled with chumps and bums - was as ready as they'd ever be.

The horn sounded. The Battle had begun.

I motioned to my crew to get moving and lay low. We were going stealth and I decided we'd have to pick them off as they ran by.

Three minutes had gone by. No one made a sound.

"AHHH!!"

I looked up from my spot lying in the bushes. One of our men was hit.

"What the fuck?"

How could be have been shot? There's no way they could've covered that much gound in under five minutes. I stayed low, looking for any bastard on the other team.

I saw blue.

Click.

Click.

Click.

Three shots was all it took to take out that bastard. I had to move.

I rolled out into the open and sprinted towards a cement bunker. Shots were flying all around me. I dove into the hole.

In there as well was Private Ryan Johnson. He was scared shit. He was shaking and breathing heavy.

"Listen up," I said, "They're ambushing us. We've got to make a run for their side. We both go on three."

He nodded no.

I slapped him upside the head.

"Understood?!"

He nodded yes. We both went to the ends of the bunker.

"1...

2...

Before three, I went over to his end and pushed his ass out into the open. I sprinted out the other side, trying to give as much cover fire as possible. God only knows if he survived it.

The good thing was I was unseen. I didn't keep running until I made sure I was alone.

The bastards cheated off their side and left early. No one expected it. For all I knew, I could be the only one left.

One lonely soul on the battlefield.
