Acceptance? Or the beginnings of Rejection? [3]
Starring: Wolf

Wolf had pushed all thoughts of vulnerability to the back of his mind, because now something far more ominous was in front of him. The Arco Arena. Wolf had decided to make the trip down, a day early, so that he could check out the place where his dreams would be born… or decimated.

All I can think of is how the place is huge. I had expected thReat to book a small hall for their first return card, out of fear of not receiving enough fans, but it seems they’ve gone the opposite route. Booking a major arena, so that fans will realises that this is a big deal. A very big deal.

I parked the car, and walked up to the main doors. I had received a pass in the mail, which I flashed at the security guard who let me in. It was moments like this that I hoped were soon gonna change. Walking up to an arena, and having guy’s I’ve never met me before greeting me by name and holding the door open for me. That respect… which has to be earned. I walked through the corridors and spied where I thought the locker rooms would be; no name boards on them yet, and as I walked, I found the stage entrance to the arena.

Everything was ready, which I must say shocked me. I stood at the top of the ramp, and looked down to the ring, which set up all but the apron. The immenseness of this place hit me again, as I looked out over the masses of empty seats and realised that this time tomorrow, every one of those seats would have someone in it. Men, women, children, all there to be entertained…by me. I knew I could do it, there wasn’t a doubt about my skill level, it was that first time though. The first time you ride a bike, some people crash, some people sail perfectly, and afterwards of course they never forget, but it was important to me that I didn’t crash. Everyone would probably forget it down the line, but that’s the kind of thing that would eat away at me.

I had a sudden longing for Rachel to be by my side, she always supported me, but I thought it best she stayed at home. She was nervous for me as well, and I fobbed her off with some excuse about her nerves would add to mine, and we’d both be better off if I went alone, but in all honesty? If I went out there and lost, I couldn’t bear to receive the talk about how I tried my best, and I’ll do better next time. I know I should be thinking positive, but you have to be realistic. Solex is an experienced grappler, and I’m as green as they come.

I walked back out of the arena, through the corridors and back to my car. The security guard tipped his hat at me, which brought a smile to my face. I jumped in my car, and drove around looking for the nearest hotel, and while the idea of just sitting there and getting drunk to calm my nerves was might appealing, I decided against it. I needed to be on top of my game tomorrow, mentally and physically.

There would be no excuses.

As Wolf checked in to a Holiday Inn, he had no idea of the torment Rachel was going through, miles away. She was just as nervous as he was, and was totally opposed to not travelling with him, but ultimately it was his big match, not hers, and she respected that. Still… Sunday could not come quick enough for the both of them. Flaw 13... Lucky for some. Would it be lucky for Wolf?
