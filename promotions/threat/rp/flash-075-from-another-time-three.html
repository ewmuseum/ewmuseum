From Another Time - three
Starring: beforeTHEhatred

He stopped walking the path, and he looked over at the young man sitting on the bench. He stopped right there at that moment and just stood there in the darkness of the park. The young man sat alone, an appearance of a lonesome shine living around him. And the trail of that one tear shining like gold on his ebony face.

From where he stood, he pulled the camera out of his pocket, and positioned himself. The flash went off, and the camera took the single image into forever’s grasp. The boy looked up at the stranger standing before him, camera in hand, but then immediately looked back down to the pavement, as if nothing had ever happened.

He caught the picture in his hand, and then put everything away in their appropriate place within the coat. When that was done, he looked back up to see the young man. The tear was gone now, but the same dreary expression was still worn on his face.

With his hands in his pockets, the stranger walked over to him and sat down on the bench.

And the boy never moved to acknowledge his existence.

At first the two sat in silence, listening to the laughter from others and the antics of a nearby basketball court. Cars honked on the distant roads, and every once and awhile someone would walk by the two, who looked to be two complete strangers that just happen to be sharing the same bench. Seconds passed. Seconds turned into minutes. Minutes fell away.

“Why did you take that picture?”

He looked down at the young man, eyes now staring into the darkness ahead of him. The young man wasn’t mad, or scared, or really upset. He was just in a train of thought all his own. Nothing anyone could do about, he supposed.

“I don’t know. I do things like that when I see something that interests me. You just happened to be very interesting.”

“Has anyone ever told you that that’s weird?”

He let out a small little laugh. “Actually, yes. I have heard that before.”

There was silence once again after that. Not as long, but possibly even more apparent then the previous moment. The two seemed to be waiting for the other to say something else. Each sat in anticipation, but nothing happened. Tension rose, and before either of them realized it, one broke the silence yet again.

“What’s wrong?” asked the older of the two, the one that had come into the park to see if there was anything interesting within.

“If I told you, you wouldn’t care.”

“What makes you say that? You don’t even know who I am.”

“Because you don’t even know who I am.”

He couldn’t argue with that, since he had just walked into that trap.

“I just want to understand, that’s all. I see a kid like you sitting in a park, and you just seem to be very…”

“You see those guys playin basketball over there?” said the young man, thrusting his chin towards the left where a few guys had been playing in the court. He nodded when he saw them, and said “Yeah.”

“See that mother fucka over there. He’s in the blue shorts?” He turned his head once again, and glared at the other guy, who just happened to be making a lay up at the time. In the distance, his cocky voice yelled croakily, obviously to try and impress somebody.

“Yeah. I see him.” he answered, realizing that this was leading to something that he might not have wanted to hear.

“I’m going to walk over there, take this gun out of my pocket, and I’m going to kill him.”
