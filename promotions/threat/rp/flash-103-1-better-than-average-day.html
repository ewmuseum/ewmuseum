Thursday ... July 17 2003 ... Undefined Time

10:47 AM
Getting up, Jere wiped the crust from his eyes and yawned. Realising that he needed to shave, the Spainard rolled out of his bed and dragged himself to the bathroom. Switching on the lights, he grabbed his black towel off the laundry basket and proceeded to the sink. He quickly turned on the tap and let the water flow, before placing his hands under the running water.

Lowering his head, Jere splashed the water onto his face, and contemplated shaving only his moustache, leaving the goatee. A new thought popped into his mind -- the possibility of colouring his hair. Blue seemed to be the new colour fad at the moment, he thought.

##

11:12 AM
Having showered, Jere Casarella stepped out of his room and looked at the airplane tickets on the dining table in the main hall. Face devoid of any expression, he walked down the hallway, his hand instinctively reaching up to his face and stroking his own face. Jere decided against shaving, opting to do so on Sunday.

Reaching the main door of his house, the man tormented by his past stopped in his tracks, and did a mental check. His wallet -- taken. His car keys -- taken. Wounds on his body -- tended to. Agenda for the day -- sorted out. Dressing -- appropriate; long sleeved black shirt with the sleeves rolled up to slightly below his elbows, black not-so-baggy pants, black shoes.

Everything was set. He opened the door and took out his shades from the right pocket of his pants, placing them on as he walked towards his car. A black Porsche, purchased rather recently.

Not an entirely busy day set out for him, Casarella thought.

##

11:43 AM
Pulling over, Jere turned off the radio and increased the intensity of the air-conditioning. Picking up a tattoo magazine that was laying on the seat next to him, he leaned back in his seat and unbuckled the seat belt. Before he opened the magazine to begin indulging in it, however, the Spainard set his eyes on the back exit of the restaurant.

Yeap, he was parked in the lot of a restaurant. A rather popular one, too. Out of the way for Jere, but he HAD to be there, so no questions asked. Lunch service was about to commence, and Jere knew that he'd have to pay for waking up late. Shrugging, he figured that he had a good three hours to kill, since the person who he was supposed to meet wasn't out there in the lot.

"If yer ass don't see my ass there, means I'm fucken' busy and you're fucken' late. So cry yerself a river and wait for me to be done. I don't get no breaks, eh. Boss is one fucken' bitch, gets on my case every freakin' second of mah day. Nothin' much I can do ab'ut that, see."

##

12:04 PM
Having finished the magazine, Jere drummed his fingers against the dashboard restlessly. He was somewhat happy that he'd gotten back to the normalacy of his new lease of life, however meanderish and slow-paced it was. No longer was he pining around, consumed with rage.

No longer was revenge on his agenda.

Rio Nexan, or psycho-Rio as the Cuban loved to called himself in an attempt to pass off his facade as a changed man overtaken by some evil entity, was dead. At long last. Vengeance had come, and it had been swift. Unexpected. It didn't matter to Jere, since he had accomplished what he had set out to do.

Time to move on, and continue with life, as was.

Sighing, Casarella turned on the radio and quickly geared into reverse. His stomach was growling, indicating it was time to eat. Ironically enough, he was parked in the lot of a restaurant that he couldn't enter if he wanted to remain alive, and the man was famished. To the extent that he could gobble a horse up.

And still have room for monkey brains.

Chinese food would have to do, the Tormented Angel thought.

##

12:30 PM
Stepping into the main dining hall of the Kwan Jia Hut, Jere nodded at the waitress who had finished tending to a table of four customers. The waitress, decked in a red cheongsam, quickly turned around and slipped inside the kitchen. Jere had become a regular within a short time, and even made friends with Huang Jie Ren, a Malaysian-born Chinese lady who had just celebrated her 22nd birthday not more than a week ago.

So, it was easy for Jie Ren to relay to the kitchen what Jere wanted, since he always ordered the same dish, whenever he visited the Chinese restaurant with a spice of fusion delight, and special Western Asian delicacies.

Casarella quickly found himself a table in one secluded corner of the restaurant and noted that business appeared to be pretty good on the day. Seeing how it was Thursday and lunch-time, it was quite surprising for there to be so many customers. Leaning back, Jere began thinking about the groceries he would have to buy later, after meeting up with Hendry Vozcack back at the Southside Atlantic Restaurant.

At around 2:30 PM. 2 hours left. More than enough time, Jere considered.

##

1:12 PM
Wiping his mouth with a napkin, Jere pushed his plate away, and a waiter immediately came over to clear his empty dishes. As he left, another waiter stopped by, topping up Casarella's helping of herbal tea. He nodded in acknowledgement and let out a silent burp as the second waiter left.

His mind now thinking about one of the newer things in his life; his new career, wrestling. More specifically, the only promotion he was contracted to. thReat.

As he entered the company, the mess with Rio Nexan was brought up. Highly coincidental, Jere figured, but it had to be done for a reason. Jere Casarella had lived as a hitman for most of the 1990s, so it was highly probable that the Almighty wanted him to be rid of one of his unwanted children.

Yes, the Spainard was a religious man. Not in the way most would be, however. His take on religion was messed up and tainted. As a Christian, he'd sinned more times than an antiChrist wannabe. Stroking his goatee, Jere actually cracked a smile, thinking of the vile way he murdered Rio.

It was called for.

Finishing up his herbal tea in one gulp, Jere got up from the chair and nodded at the cashier, who grinned back. A young man he was, but Jere hadn't cared enough to get his name. As long as the kid knew of the special deal -- Jere not paying, ever -- the Spainard was contented. Now outside of the Hut, Casarella coolly rounded the block and proceeded to the back-of-the-house. An alley where the rubbish was thrown.

And where Jie Ren waited, leaning against a wall. She pulled her pink stockings down to her knees and turned around, placing her palms against the brick wall next to the back exit of the hut. Smiling, she stuck her butt out, closing her eyes with Jere placed his hands on her shoulder.

Pulling the back of her cheongsam up, he marvelled at her bare ass. Before he slowly started to kiss the back of Jie Ren's neck, whilst ripping her cheongsam to pieces. That wasn't part of the deal, and Huang contemplated objecting. But there was to be no talking during these sessions.

Completely naked now, except for the pink stockings around her knees, she opened her eyes and looked up at the sky. The roof of the restaurant posed some covering, making the area dark. That didn't stop Jere from opening his pants and quickly easing in his penis into Jie Ren's ass, without any lubrication whatsoever.

He made it a point to remember to pay extra when he was done.

##

1:35 PM
His face expressionless, as per normal, Jere watched and heard as Jie Ren shook violently while moaning & grunting, having reached her climax. Casarella too had climaxed, at the exact same time as his lady friend. The two panted, out of breath, having gone at it like wild bunnies for quite a while.

Jere pulled his member out of Huang and stumbled backwards, wasting no time in pulling his pants up. Jie Ren, meanwhile, collapsed to her knees, her senses returning. Fear had begun to creep into her, as she realised that her cheongsam had been ripped to shreds. She possibly couldn't go inside.

Turning, she looked at Jere, with concern in her eyes.

Casarella snorted and motioned for Jie Ren to get up, as he took out his wallet and produced a few dollar bills. Huang slowly stood up and covered her chest, no longer in the passion zone. All she wanted now was some rest, and an answer.

The Spainard was about to provide both, as he crushed the notes and let it fall to the ground, before grabbing Jie Ren by her right hand and leading her out of the alley, towards his car. Naturally, she was surprised and covered her face, not wanting the passerbys to get a good look at her face.

"Haven't I been humiliated enough? Where are you taking me, and how am I to walk around with no clothes?"

The entire situation seemed so fake to Jere, as he kept quiet and glared at the onlookers lewdly staring at his lady friend. Of course, he didn't mean for the session to pan out the way it did. It wasn't passion that drove him to tear Jie Ren's cheongsam into pieces. It was just...

The emptiness inside of him.

##

2:25 PM
"Well, whud do ye know? We're both early! HAHA, get it? Eh?"

Jere snorted at the lame joke as he received the silver briefcase from Hendry Vozcack, back at the Southside Atlantic Restaurant. The parking lot, more specifically. Leaving the Kwan Jia Hut, Casarella stopped by the convenience store and got his desired groceries, as well as a fresh set of clothing for Jie Ren, who had been sobbing uncontrollably since being ushered into Jere's car, after having consumated. Now, she lay in the back seat, fully clothed.

Sleeping.

Casarella continued to drive around, thinking about the last time he was in a relationship. The names he was called, the tricks she played on him, the fun times they had together. Everything came flooding back to him, but yet, no emotion was shown. Just the acknowledgement that he HAD had so much happen to him in his life, it was quite incredible. And then, with time to spare, the Spainard's mind returned to thReat.

His horrendous record so far was the one thing that stood out. Then, the memory of a tournament for the company's secondary title came back to him, and the Tormented Angel began thinking if the tournament would prove to be a catalyst for a change in fortunes. His fortunes. He'd realised that various personalities in the industry would be keeping an eye on thReat in the coming weeks.

And decided that he could use the tournament to shine. With Rio gone and a huge burden lifted off his shoulders, the focus he once possessed in life would be able to return and flourish. Probably climax with the winning of the International Title.

It had just become his number two priority.

Delving into the left pocket of his pants, Jere handed Hendry a single key, in exchange for the briefcase and its valuable contents. The two men nodded at each other, with their transaction over.

Walking back to the car, Casarella cracked a sadistic grin, and didn't flinch as the Southside Atlantic Restaurant suddenly exploded into a ball of flames the second Hendry stepped into the compounds of his workplace.

The transaction had actually, finally, concluded.

##

3:07 PM
He was back home.

He'd washed up.

The briefcase had been stored in a safe place.

Jie Ren had awoken and her mental state had returned to semi-normal. The day had been a rough one for her, and she was still demanding answers. But as she stood at the doorway of Jere's bedroom, Jie Ren clasped her hand over her mouth, shocked. Casarella was on his knees, holding on tightly to Huang's left hand.

"Marry me."

He spoke for the first time on the day.

"Yes."

She was truly blissful for the first time in her life.

A match made in heaven.

But a certain Cuban begged to differ.

And as psycho-Rio watched Jere Casarella stand up, kissing his new bride, one single thought came to his attention. One single notion popped into his twisted mind.

"Nice ass."

A perfect end to a better than average day? Wouldn't count on it.
