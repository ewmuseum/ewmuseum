He Was My Heroin - 3
Starring: Ernie French

He had defeated me so easily. I had not expected his hands to be so fast, he looked old, decrepit and yet he had the reflexes of a cat. I had underestimated his skills, but not this time I thought… Not this time.



“Damn…” I muttered to myself. “What now?”



Now you must defeat me in a game of skill. It told me.



“Skill… Hmm…” I thought for a while, I needed something original, something that even Death had never heard of. “I know.”



I snapped my fingers, and at that moment I felt brighter than the brightest luminary in the universe.



“Rock… Paper… Scissors…” Yes, Yes I did think it was a good idea at the time, but take in to consideration the amount of Heroin I had pumped in to my body not thirty minutes previous to this moment.



Are you sure? He asked me, and without a moments pause to think about it I replied.



“Yes.” I told him.



His long arm, covered by the sleeves of his black robe reached out, his grey fist was clenched tightly, I outstretched my track marked arm, my palms where clammy as I clenched my fist.



One, two, three. he said, we both shook our fists and I stretched mine out flat. I had chosen paper, Death had chosen scissors, and that was the end of challenge two, although I tried to stretch out this challenge.



“We said best out of three!” I exclaimed, I began to sweat.



No. We did not. He told me in all seriousness. Challenge three is a bout of strength.
