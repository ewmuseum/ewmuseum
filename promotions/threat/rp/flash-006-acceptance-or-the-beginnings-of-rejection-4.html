Acceptance? Or the beginnings of Rejection? [4]
Starring: Wolf

Now he had one week to prepare, and take him on in Wolf’s world. By Wolf’s rules. A submission match.

Wolf sat in that same motel room that he had before Flaw 13. He had done it.

All the fears and reservations were gone, replaced with a subdued jubilation. Wolf was on fire that night. The crowd… the crowd were on their feet applauding his moves, and as for his moves? He executed them almost flawlessly. He’d watched the tapes back, and while he could see the small errors he’d made, and would correct, he was overjoyed by his performance. And of course, his win… So why was he still here?

I had pulled off all the moves that I’d needed to, and it was a great relief. I’d been told by Malenko, that to become a successful professional wrestler, you need to create a move list, and make it your own, and I felt that I was well on my way to achieving this… but before I can unveil my finishing I need to train more. I just don’t know how long I could hold the larger men up in a vertical suplex for, never mind manoeuvre them about in the position. All of this is insignificant now though, I’d beaten Solex. I beat Steve Solex, and left him tapping out in the middle of the ring.

And then that ignoramus had the audacity to claim it was all a fluke. Fluke? I’m surprised he even knows the word’s definition. The Wolf is no fluke… but here I am getting angry when I claimed the upper hand. He wanted me to walk back down the ramp, and fight him? Not gonna happen. I led him straight into my territory, the “Wolf’s den” so to speak… a submission match. I know Solex’s weaknesses know, and after I focused all my energy on his shoulders last week, he won’t even be 100% at Flaw 14. He’s coming in to it looking for revenge? He’s gonna find himself in a world of pain. You see, I know Solex is bigger, stronger, and more experienced than me. In a fight he’d probably tear me apart… so I’m not fighting him. The brawler cannot beat the technical expert in a submission match, I won’t let it happen.

So now the time for preparing is upon us… but I am ready. Solex had better be on his A game, or this will quickly become an embarrassment.

It was clear Wolf was riding high off of his win at Flaw 13, and it seemed his ego was beginning to swell. To most men this would be a problem, but as Wolf left the building and got into his car, he was travelling home to his “support”. His “balance”. Rachel was always there to keep him on the straight and narrow, and while she was it, she had plans of leading him down another path as well.
