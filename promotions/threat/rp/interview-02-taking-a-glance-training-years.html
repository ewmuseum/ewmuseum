Taking A Glance - Training Years
Starring: Tristan Carter


All five of us had always imagined us getting trained together. But things would turn out much differently. Each of us had our own traits that Lincoln focused on. Whether it be size and power or a great mind. Our days were at school and countless nights were spent training hard. Our father demanded that we get some type of schooling and would have always agreed on that fact. Because my uncle had told me - privately - that I would be the best of the bunch. And it would not be because of my size because I wasn't the biggest. Or my bravery or risk taking. For me it would be my ring smarts.

Training was hard. And training without my brothers and sisters may have been even harder. I would have done anything to be able to have attended their endless training sessions. Provide them with the moral support that would be needed. But my uncle put into our heads that training was better one on one and I have no doubts he was correct on most levels with that theory.

In the wrestling world today you find yourself seeing many different types of wrestlers. You have your high flyers and your power wrestlers. Then you have a steady mix of many different styles. But I did not have an opportunity for those. My size was decent but something else was clicking for me. Technical wrestling. An endless mass of moves and holds that would be spit out at me - and then quickly put into use in the ring. But I liked it. Wrestling the way I did gave me a chance to feel in control. And there is nothing better to stand on the canvas and feel in control of your opponent.

Outside of Wrestling.

Believe it or not I did have a life outside of wrestling. Sure I spent a lot of time training and lifting weights. But I managed to be Prom King my senior year. Our high school football team won conference all four years. And I went to the state wrestling tournament each year in high school - managing to get a first place finish my senior year. I spent most of my time with my friends and my girlfriend - who happened to be the best looking girl in our class. I went to the occasional drinking party but rarely did I get so smashed I couldn't walk straight. If I did which to pursue a college, I didn't want my grades slumping off because of drinking. In my other free time, I found time to run for student council and won a land slide election. High school was great.

Back Into Wrestling.

Well my high school days were over. I had made the decision - along with my father and uncle to pursue a career as a professional wrestler. The same direction that my sister had decided to go in. I was finally done with training. My uncle Lincoln sent me on my way - wishing me luck. I would stay in Bloomington. Soon all my brothers and sisters would be gone and my dad needed some companions. But my training days were over. I was now going to work - yes, work, to get paid. To become known. The only way I knew how.
