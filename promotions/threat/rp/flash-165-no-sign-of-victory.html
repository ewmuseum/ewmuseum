"No Sign of Victory"
Starring: Nathan Brady

"Oh... like, wow... aren't you that stupid fuck from thReat?" asked a voice as our picture opened in the comfortable confinements of a Prison Cell in Las Vegas. Nathan Brady, one of the four men that had battled for a prized contract at 'Double or Nothing', had been thrown in a Cell merely minutes after being arrested by Police after a tip from Colin Gear. Both Two-Face and himself had been put in the book for counts of assualt and criminal damage... a couple of items that could have been avoided if a different route had been taken.

He stood with his back against the wall, looking up at the ceiling as his heckler continued to throw all kinds of verbal BS in his direction.

"... What're you here for? Faking your way through some street fight? Come on... don't tell me, you were caught speeding in your flashy little car? No... this can't be all part of some bullshit set-up can it?"

Brady lowered his eyes from the ceiling, his head moving down in a very slow motion. With a stern expression spread across his face, the otherwise 'relaxed' Brady was showing no signs of having to put up with this hecklers comments any more.

"For your information... I'm in here for assualt and criminal damage. The other bastard involved is in another Cell, but to be more than honest with you; I could care less about what happens to that Russian fuck."

Nathan moved away from the wall, strolling into the centre of the room, the heckler seated on the 'bench-like' feature sticking out of the wall. He turned to face the heckler, thrusting both hands down around the collar of his dirty jacket before pulling him up to a vertical status.

"... Woah, hey... what the Hell do you think you're doing? You really want to have more than just those counts of shit attached to your name?" the heckler cried in despair as Brady looked into his eyes, a slow fire beginning to spark into life in his.

"At the moment... I could give a shit about what the Police are wanting to charge me with. You're annoying me, and unless you're looking for a little personal injury... I'd advise you to shut the fuck up. To be honest, I am NOT in the mood for this." Brady explained, the tone of his voice only starting to match the tone of his inner aggression and frustration. "The Professional" dropped the heckler back onto the stone bench and made his way over to the cell bars. Gazing out, the thReat superstar noticed an officer coming in his direction. Eager to secure his own freedom, Brady called over to the gentleman.

"... Yo, Police Dude. What the Hell is happening with me? When the Hell am I going to be able to get out of this place?"

The Officer didn't respond until he came to Nathan's cell. Stopping just a few feet away, he began to respond, "Well, it would appear as though the company that yourself and that Russian fellow claimed to have worked for, are willing to bail you out of this mess. However, stating that... after your court hearing, it is more than likely that the two of you, perhaps even with the help of those other two morons... will have to foot the bill for everything that was lost that night."

"... Everything?" Brady asked, gulping at the thought of having to foot the bill for damages and lost revenue that the establishment could have acquired during the night.

"Thats right Junior... you're more than likely, going to have to foot the bill for everything that was lost during that night alone. Not to mention, you'll probably have to pay out compensation on top... along with the court costs if you're that unlucky. But hey, I wouldn't know what the overall deal is going to be. After all, you might be one of the lucky few that's going to be able to cut some kind of deal with the prosecution." the Officer replied, chuckling to himself at the same time.

'... What sorry bastard is going to be able to foot the bill for the damage and losses of that place?' he asked himself, before pulling out the keys from his belt. The officer, dressed in typical officer attire, unlocked the door with ease and opened it, much to the delight of the thReat superstar.

"So like, I'm totally free to go until I'm required to appear in court?" Brady asked, just making sure that it would be OK for him to leave the Cell and the Station without twenty thousand of these bastards trying to track him down again.

"That sounds about right. You're free to go until you appear in court... but if you pull anything like the shit you pulled at the Casino again, you can be sure that I'll personally be pushing for them to forget about the court case and throw you straight into the Slammer." he answered, an angry tone now taking over his voice. Nathan looked rather 'worried' at the thought and nervously chuckled to himself before walking out of the door and regaining his freedom.

As the officer locked up the cell that Brady had been staying in, our rather athletic and new thReat athlete made his way past the many Police desks to the fore-front of the building. One night here had been more than enough, and the heckler had only made things even worse for him.

Looking past that however, the beginning of his freedom... could ultimately be the time remaining until its taken away from him again. After failing to come out with anything substantial in his encounter with the Russian, Two-face... Nathan felt the desire... felt the need to come out and win at all odds.

Casper and Race had been decimated by the beast... they'd been thrown around like rag dolls like somebody who was 'Ultimate Muscle'.

There's 'No Sign of Victory' tonight... but the war has only just begun.
