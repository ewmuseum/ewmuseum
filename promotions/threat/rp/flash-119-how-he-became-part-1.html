How He Became [Part 1]
Starring: Two-Face

Boris Simunek was regarded as a mentally strong fighter back in the gloomy back streets of Moscow Russia. At his slum of a gymnasium he would accept any tramp off the streets and train them into a full-blooded fighter who would one day venture around the Russian fighting scene stronger than a Russian Lieutenant, but trained harder. Away from work, Boris was another creature altogether. He didn’t respect his wife or his son. He treated them like his stepparents had treated him when he was being brought up. He was cold, angry and willing to batter his family to a pulp to make a point.

Boris wasn’t a tall man, and didn’t look particularly menacing. His eyes were piecing and deceitful, his fists were swollen like a balloon from all the punching, slapping and abuse that he gave his family. He was a man full of hatred, and desire to destroy those who were weak. He was anti-American and brainwashed his family regularly, informing his wife and son that if they ever left him, the Americans would come and kill them one day. He wasn’t your typical father, rather your typical Stalin type character; a man who would persecute those who did not agree with him.

As Boris entered his home once more his family stopped communicating, stopped laughing, and turned into the paranoid, and battered souls that he had turned them into. Vladimir who was only five, ran up the stairs and into his dark room, shaking in the corner, fearing for yet another battering to the already destroyed right side of his face. Maria, Boris’s wife quickly pulled out the scarce food that they had in the fridge and began to frantically chop away, trying to make it look like she had been at hard at work for the past half an hour.

But the problem was Boris wasn’t a stupid man, and as he hung his thick dark brown coat on the hook he took a deep breath in through his nose waiting to inhale the smell of a cooking saucepan, where his wife should have been making him a ‘gourmet’ dinner. However he was greeted by the smell of fresh carrots, un-chopped onions, and raw meet.

“Hunny” he barked, storming down the small corridor into the kitchen where he gave his wife a piercing eye look before checking her progress of the hot dinner that should have been ready within minutes. “What the fuck do you call this?” He yelled looking rather disgusted at the pathetic status of his dinner. “What do you not understand? I go to work every fucking day! You sit around here doing fuck all, and you don’t have the nerve to cook me anything?” He rhetorically asked her, holding his hips with both arms waiting for an answer that he knew he wasn’t going to get.

“Well…umm” She replied, shaking on the spot.

“Wrong answer”

When Boris didn’t get his way at home, he would physically beat it out of his wife or son. He didn’t like a disobedient family.

*SMACK*

Boris dealt Maria a brutal slap to her left cheek, sending Maria to the ground, with a tear in her eye. He soon followed it up with an unforgiving kick to her rib cage sending her across the kitchen winding her temporarily. He motioned forwards to continue on but even the site of his pathetic wife halted him in his attack.

“What do I do wrong?” he asked once more furiously, turning around before slamming his fist on the chopping board, sending the un-chopped onion onto the kitchen floor. “What do I have to expect from you? All I want is a loving wife, and a nice dinner when I get home. Why must you always disobey me!!” he belched at Maria who still lay in the corner weeping at her newly inflicted injuries. “We have a fuck case of a son, and you just do not seem to understand how to love a husband. I don’t know why I let you live with me!!” he concluded before leaving the kitchen and heading into the sitting room where he switched on the small television and slumped on the second hand sofa trying to calm down.

One floor up in his room, the skinny and pathetic site of the brutalised son of Boris Simunek, Vladimir, sat shaking violently in the corner of his room without a single light on. He had grown used to the scream of his formally beautiful mother every night for the past few months and knew that when his father grew angry…

…His time would come later.

For Vladimir this was only the beginning of his life.

End.
