living HATE; the second
Starring: haTE

The shelter. Or lack-there-of? He lived in many different homes in his day as a kid. Use the term home loosely. The first of many homes was just a shithole cubical. They slept in holes in the wall. Sleeping and staying in small places was the trademark of the Japanese..

But this was inhumane. Sometimes the children took turns sleeping in the beds, as there weren't enough to support them all. The rest slept standing or leaning against a wall, as there was not enough room for them all to sit or lay on the floor. Three 'beds' as it was put, or more like boxes with mats shoved in the fucking wall.

To think, they were kicked out because they couldn't keep the rent going. Kicked out of a fucking hole in the wall. It was hard to find enough shelter on the streets for one person, let alone nine. Sometimes, when he knew the kids were safe enough, he would tell the owner of whatever shop he would be working in that day, that he would lock up, and end up just sleeping in there.

Sometimes his sheltering in the shop went un-noticed, but most of the time he would get caught and fired. Back out on the street.

Finally, they found another place to stay. This time, it was totally free. This time, though, there were no beds. Fucking hell, there wasn't even most of the floors. They would find the rotting boards they COULD sleep on, in the abandoned buildings, and sleep there.

Too good to last, they were finally caught and thrown out. Actually, his dad was thrown in the lock up for days, until they could finally scrape up enough money over the week to get him out. That two weeks, they went without food.

Who knew?

Now, he stays in Ramada Inns. A roof over his head every fucking night. A fucking king sized bed. Fresh, cooked, warm food. Spending money and he always knows he will have enough to get by the next day.

Meanwhile, he still tries to support his poor brothers and sisters.

Will it ever fucking end? EVER?
