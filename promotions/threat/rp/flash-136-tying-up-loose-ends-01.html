Starring: Vincent Pembridge

Pro-Wrestling Illustrated September Exclusive - The Apparent Death Of cWf? [1]
Reported by: Andrew Hallaskan

On July 16 2003, the cWf (Championship Wrestling Federation) was supposed to have had their first television event, Avarice, on ESPN. However, it now appears as if the cWf project has collapsed, and the talent contracted to the organisation have found jobs elsewhere. No public notice of cWf's closure has been made available however, but there have been rampant rumours about why cWf closed the way it did. They haven't been substantiated until a few days ago, and we here at PWI are aiming to be the FIRST to spread the news.

Firstly, ESPN network officials had pre-empted the Avarice pilot because of a special sporting event they had wished to show LIVE on the network. cWf co-owners, Andrew Jonah Brooks & Levi Brooks, were only notified of this few hours before the show was supposed to get underway. Nevertheless, the brothers decided to comply with the network and went on to tape the show as for normal, which would actually allow the production team more time in terms of editing and such.

But, something unexpected happened on the show. Read the transcript of the events that transpired as the show approached its climax. What was set to happen was Christian Embers coming out to make an open challenge for the following week, only to have two superstars from the Japan pro-wrestling circut come out and demand a match right then and there. The match was supposed to have helped promote Embers as a high-flying machine, in an effort to make the Shoot Sensation appear to be more versatile and such.

There was a rude shock in plans, however.

--

Water Under The Bridge? Not Bloody Likely, Mate!
So, it had been a night of epic proportions. cWf was back, and with class, it seemed. The first show had proven to be an entertaining one, and the management were eagerly waiting for the ratings to come out the next day. It would be an indication of how well on track the company was. However, no matter what, everybody in the back knew that the job had been done perfectly by every single member of the company.

But the fans were waiting for the main-event.

So, when the lights went out, a buzz of excitement enveloped the audience. Close to 2,000 of them. Nothing amazing... but the atmosphere generated was electric. They were on the edge of their seats, eagerly anticipating a breathtaking finish to a rather splendid night.

Imagine their surprise when the song that played over the speakers was...

"Time" by Taproot.

What? A fucking joke, this is?

Nope, you're not hallucinating. And it ain't a fucking joke. The man known as The Plague From Manchester was appearing as if he was 'continuing' his invasion tour. A blue spotlight now focused on The Callous Fighting Machine himself, as he stepped out onto the stage of the Avarice set and looked out at the sea of devout cWf fans, that were quickly beginning to hurl profanities at him.

Not much of a surprise, the man's been hellbent on making himself absolutely hated.

Time; just a counter-clockwise in motion.
Time; it requires strength, love, and devotion.
Time; a detention of every person.
Time; is used to make us free again.

When we can turn back time, to any time.
By... by moving on inside.
And will we still ask why about the time?
Or be just fine inside of our minds?

Vincent -- decked out in a dark red short-sleeved shirt that was unbuttoned, blue jeans, and black boots -- strolled down the ramp looking like a million dollars. Mainly because of the ACW Scorpion Title that was wrapped around his waist, and the thReat Fighting Title that he was holding in his right hand, along with a microphone. The announce team were certainly surprised to see The Scorpion Of Manchester as well, indicating that this appearance wasn't a pre-meditated publicity stunt. For several hundred fans watching at home, a sense of deja vu swept them off their feet.

Pembridge looked to be everywhere, like a bug planning a global infestation.

Time; grows things older, faster when you find it.
Time; it's wasting away while we spend it.
Time; a reflection of our past with it.
Time; is used to make things right again.

When we can turn back time, to any time.
By... by moving on inside.
And will we still ask why about the time?
Or be just fine inside of our minds?

Having reached the bottom of the ramp, Vincent quickly rolled into the ring, the blue spotlight having followed him throughout. Now in the middle of the ring, the Manucian Predator threw his thReat Fighting Title down onto the canvas, following up by the tapping of the ACW Scorpion Title. The intensity of the hate increased, as Pembridge smiled... revelling in the atmosphere.

I can see this coming over my mind.
Cause you're right.
It's life, my light!

When we can turn back time, to any time.
By... by moving on inside.
And will we still ask why about the time?
Or be just fine inside of our minds?

When we can turn back time, to any time.
By... by moving on inside.
And will we still ask why about the time?
Or be just fine inside of our minds?

Our minds, Our minds, Our minds.
Because you're right, you're right...
Inside our minds.
Minds. Minds. Minds.

Minds.

Vincent raised his left arm in the air to get on the crowd's nerves, as his theme song ended and the house lights returned, prompting them to get even more vocal about their feelings for the man that was standing inside the ring. An intruder, he could be called. To the best of anybody's knowledge, he hadn't been signed to a cWf contract.

So why the fuck was he out there?

"Hello, cunts.

I'm going to make this short and sweet, really. I don't have that much time to educate you tree-shagging arseholes about my greatness and such. Firstly, I have a bloody ticket and clearance from the backstage officials who happen to be Manchester United fans. So, cease your pathetic whining.

Secondly... Christian Embers.

Amazing. I thought you'd retired. Your body wasn't able to continue, you claimed. As a way to escape WRESTLING in thReat, you retired, came to the cWf, recuperated nicely... and now, you're out of retirement, it seems. Bloody fantastic. Truly, it's great to see you back.

Because now, I can kick your arse.

I blame YOU for the fall of The Vagabonds at Malevolence! YOU! The Shoot Sensation. PAH! The only thing that was sensational about you on May 25 2003 was your sorry arse rolling about on the canvas, as some twat beat the shite out of you. Bleeding disgrace, really."

Jeering, several fans started to throw rubbish at him, but were way off the mark. Sniggering, Vincent looked around him at the small audience. Not what he was completely used to, but he figured for his little invasion plan, he had to start small. cWf had a motivation behind it, and its name was Christian Embers.

Former ally of the Scorpion's in thReat -- The Vagabonds. Their objective? To seize control of thReat and turn it into a full-fledged fighting promotion. They were halfway there, and almost managed to go the distance. Certain extuanting circumstances got in the way, however.

And Vincent Pembridge was defeated. Naturally, the sore loser didn't take it well.

"See, you were one of the best soldiers I had. Ty was busy with tA, Ampske was new to the entire situation, Knickerson is only a young lad. Despite the apparent order, YOU were the true second-in-command. But you didn't seize the opportunity. You fecking messed up, lad.

That is why I am here.

For revenge.

You see, Christian, you could have become a mammoth sensation. Together, we would have ruled with an iron fist in thReat. But your incompetenecy let me down, and burned the mission to the ground. All my bloody hard work went down the drain. And fecking hell, you jolly well aren't going to get away with it scot-free.

TONIGHT! IN THIS RING! You and I will do battle, in a Scorpion Fight Rules clash.

For these two lovely titles here!"

The crowd exploded, and suddenly, Christian Embers appeared on the stage, wearing only his black trackpants with sneakers and with a microphone in his hand. No music, no fanfare... nothing. He was fuming mad, as he slowly started down the ramp, staring at Vincent Pembridge, who'd already acknowledged his presence.

Business was about to pick up.

"No pinfalls. No submissions. Either a standing 15-count or a ring-out. Weapons are most welcome, and if you win, you'll be attaining the thReat Fighting Title AND the ACW Scorpion Title. That's just how confident I am of crushing you like a bloody Scouser!"

Embers stopped halfway down the ramp and continued glaring at the man that was once his leader in thReat. What Vincent didn't know was Christian's plan to overthrow the British Degenerate if the battle at the Malevolence PPV was won. It was mega-confusing, but the gist of it was...

"Hey Vincent. You're a stupid fucker. I disappointed you?

YOU DISAPPOINTED US!

And get this, pussy. Me & Ampske was gonna overthrow your sorry ass once WE gained the victory at Malevolence. Where were you when we were getting beaten up illegally and eventually pinned, HUH? Nowhere. YOU LOST THE MATCH FOR US, FUCKER! YOU! YOU WERE THE WEAK LINK!"

Convulsing, the crowd celebratated frantically, with Vincent's smiled evaporating. The Callous Fighting Machine was somewhat stunned by the revelation of The Shoot Sensation's plans, and the venom in his words seemed to agitate the Brit. A plan formed in his head, however.

A plan that would successfully goad Embers into making a very big mistake.

"Well, I should have guessed. When Tito Ortiz trounced your arse, he must have done so with much intensity, that he messed your bloody brains up.

20 seconds. My oh my.

YOU LOST IN 20 SECONDS. FECKING PATHETIC! I'm reconsidering my challenge to you now, actually!"

Grinning, Vincent looked cheekily at Christian Embers, furious by the low-blow. The crowd were now egging him on, forcing him to roll into the ring and take a chunk out of The British Degenerate. The tension was growing, the arena felt as if a nuclear explosion was set to take place. With each passing second, Vincent's arrogance due to the domination of the situation grew.

And then, Christian Embers dropped his microphone.

IT WAS ON.
