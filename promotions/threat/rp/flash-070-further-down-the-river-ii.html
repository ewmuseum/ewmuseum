Further Down the River (II)
Starring: Xaxon

"All aboard the St. Marina, all aboard!"

That 'vas my call to get on the ship and head to Beijing. After reading of the art of 'second becoming', I knew I must go to this place and find these people. I knew I must give my dying body a rebirth. I knew I should start over.

So I got on.

It 'vas a hard ride. I've never been much of a sea p'arson, so I knew it 'vould be uncomfortable. As I slept on the hard bed, I 'vondered if it was hard because of it's true firmness and material composition, or if my body could not hold up to much of anything these days.

That's 'vhat kept me on that boat.

It 'vas a long trip to Beijing. I met no one on the boat and had no desire to. All I did was ponder my past; think of the people I haven't seen in so many decades.

That's 'vhat kept me on that boat.

I thought of 'vhat this destination 'vould lead me to. I 'vorried that it 'vould be the same dead end that everything else I tried had been.

And that too kept me on that boat.

It 'vas an easy decision to make. 'Vhen a car gets old, you replace it. Same 'vith your clothing, your entertainment, your lover. 'Vhen something becomes useless, it must be replaced.

I could actually feel excitement in my stomach. It was a feeling that I had felt only in the years of my childhood, 'vatching my uncle 'vreslte all those wonderful nights.

'Vhat it was like to be a youth...

I stayed on that boat, and in a few short days, we docked.

And as I stepped my first foot in this journey, my excitement grew.
