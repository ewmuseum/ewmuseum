Of One Such Time - Second
Starring: He who once saw

Although a dismal evening, the nightclub was packed and full of life. The techno music sealed the room with pure energy. Men and women danced together on the floor, glowing in the radiance of green and blue lights flashing from the sky. Alone in their pleasure, they danced the hours away, their hearts beating to the music and their minds set on nothing else but their own delight.

He dipped his head back and downed a half a bottle of beer in only a few gulps. The spirit of the dynamic party behind him didn’t catch any of his attention. People danced and lights flashed, but his eyes stayed locked onto the counter and his drink. He could hear the laughter of some people, the whispers of others, and the yells of even more in the back. The music worked the building, and yet he could pay no attention to its almost hypnotic rhythm.

Another sip of his beer and the music was drowned out some more.

Through the night, many people walked by him. Many sat down next to him. Many grabbed some drinks and talked to each other and flirted and hooted. None were of any relevance, and none caught him off guard. His attention came only to himself, and the thoughts that randomly sprung up within his own mind.

He was alone, even though they surrounded him. He was silent, with the world as loud as possible. If it started raining, he felt that he would remain dry.

Some way to live, he supposed.

But, when two men stepped up behind him rather forcefully and sudden, all his attention was converted. His ears caught on to only their voices and he thought only of their words. Awareness of them became the only.

“Paul. Listen fucker. See that group over there?”

“Over where?”

“Over there.” this man said, lifting his arm and nudging his unknown listener’s jacket. “See that bitch with the blue on?”

“Yeah. Yeah I see ‘em. What about ‘em, Skill?”

“They want some of the shit. It’s back in the car, parked around the corner.”

“How much they want?”

“I don’t know, but they want some. That’s all that matters. See that one with the leather jacket on?”

“Yeah, I see him.”

“Take him to the car and give him the shit. Make sure they pay you more then the shits worth this time. I don’t want you coming back without more then we disserve. You got me, fucker?”

“Yeah, Skill, I gotcha. I make sure this time.”

“Fuckin better Paul or it’s your ass.”
He sensed the man unmistakably named Paul step away in one direction, and the other, who had been called ‘Skill’ walk away in the other direction. Suddenly, for the first time sine he had stepped in, he turned around, and saw the back of Paul’s head. Some kid in a leather jacket, probably half way through college, trotted over to him when he raised his hand and called him over.

He turned back towards the counter. Picking up his bottle, he finished off the rest of his beer and put it in his coat pocket. He stood up, and stepped through the crowd, oblivious to what was about to occur in mere seconds. At current, it was only a figment of his own imagination.
