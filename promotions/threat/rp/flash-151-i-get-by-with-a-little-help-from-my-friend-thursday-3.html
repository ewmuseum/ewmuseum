I Get By With A Little Help From My Friend (Thursday) [3]
Starring: Chris "Neverwhere" McCluskey

“So, Chris. Where to?”

“The Biggest Joke” Joe Soap was behind the wheel of the sixty-person luxury tour bus, as he and his good friend Chris McCluskey, or “The Cluck”, as the legend goes, cruised down some highway on their way out of Cleveland. It had been Chris’ intention to drive straight to the arena at which Double or Nothing was to be held, but Joe’s question was a real thinker, and it put some doubt into Chris’ mind.

“What have you got in mind, Joe?

“Well, since we’re here, how about we visit some of our old pals?”

“Since we’re here… in America?

“Yeah!”

“Do you know where Patrick lives?”

“No, but I saw him in New York two years ago. Shall we check there first?”

Poor Joe. So innocent. So humble. So absolutely bloody stupid.

“How about we check on the Internet?”

“What a great idea, Chris!”

So Joe pulled the bus into the side of the road at a lay-by. Luckily for the pair of them, Chris had his laptop that had remote Internet access with him. Otherwise they’d be up a paddle without a creek. Chris loaded up Google.com and typed in “Lowest Common Denominator” and clicked Search. Scrolling down through the listed sites, it was soon clear that they were going to have very little luck:

WLCD >> The News Chicks >> an online comic strip by Bryan M. ...
... Not affiliated with "LCD Lowest Comic Denominator," an adult comic book, nor with
"Lowest Common Denominator," a publication of radio station WFMU-FM, Jersey

2-0: Your Lowest Common Denominator
Have you been repeatedly accused of spoiling the party? Stealing the silver?
Slurping the soup? Are you tired of being compared to a horse's ass? ...

Endless Agony - humorous agony aunt site (Misc)
>> back << lowest common denominator I regularly give advice on this site. ... blokes
snogin, Misc, 13/08/2003. lowest common denominator, Misc, 13/08/2003. ...

“Aww, this is no use Chris! Let’s just look at porn!”

“No Joe! Porn is great, but we are on a mission! This is why the Internet was invented!”

And do you know something? Chris was right. Porn is great.

As Chris and Joe Soap played a two-player game of Solitaire, Chris had another sparkling idea.

“Let’s search for old people from cWo!”

“Why? I was only in cWo for five minutes, then the monkey took over.”

“But if we don’t search for cWo, then I won’t be able to make some hilarious comments about ex-cWo members!”

Joe realised, as always, Chris was correct. They searched old names for half an hour, and boy was it fun. Joe wanted to search for himself, but then realised there wasn’t much point, because they already knew where he was and what he was up to. They did find out some interesting things though:

whyttrash, former cWo World Heavyweight champion, had a UFO website on which he tried to prove that he had been abducted by aliens. He also tried to prove that he was the right choice to have been cWo champion. Visitors of the site weren’t convinced on either topic, according to what Chris and Joe read in the shout-box. Conspiracy theorists, pah, who needs them. Pretending to only be able to see in two-dimensions just so you can wear crappy 3-D glasses isn’t funny at all, especially for those poor people who actually can only see in two-dimensions, such as Phillip J. Fry.

“I always thought his name was a play on the words ‘White Rash,’” said Joe.

Ike Rogers meanwhile had a blog website, on which he posted at least four times a day about why he was a great wrestler, and that he was destined to soon be back at the top of the next company he worked for. It seems that he said that a lot, looking back through the archives. aWc, cWo, aWc… yawn. It seems that was he kidding.

The Superstar seemed to have used to have had a website, however his hosting service took the website offline because of the fraudulent claims he made that he actually had come up with the Christmas Clock idea. Joe agreed with Chris that it was all Chris’ great idea, and that Joe had come up with “Got Milk.”

Phil Atken was still at large in the wrestling world, making an ass of himself in some company with weird capitalisation in its name.

And finally, it seemed that former cWo Television champion Austin Douglas had been signed up by the Slut Records record company, to record his own rap album.

Both men realised it was getting dusk outside, so they decided it was best to move on. They had important issues to deal with tomorrow. So Chris put the laptop away and they once again were cruising down the highway, listening to Neil Diamond’s Ultimate Hits collection.
