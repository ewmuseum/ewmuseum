A Stream of Georgia Consciousness
Starring: Generative Clash

"Every scratch, every beat, every heartbreak..."
~Elvis Costello, "45"

Golden sun rains down the apple blossoms in Georgia, on a Sunday. It's a perfect day for taking the time to relax, grab a lemonade, maybe sit back and have sex with your wife, girlfriend, ex, partner, or... well, even a farm animal, if you stretch to that sick length.

Stephen Martin doesn't rather have anyone to have sex with right now. So, instead, he's going the "lemonade-sipping" route. Walking out of a convenience store, he has in his hands a bottle of pure, fresh-squeezed lemonade.

And he sips it. Enjoys the sweet-yet-sour taste. And continues on his way.

In the car -- no matter of what color, model, size, or year it is, it's a car, transportation, he uses it for that only, not as a fashion accessory -- is Zoe Antoinette.

He doesn't use her for transportation.

Here, he would make a joke to himself: well, only if I were broke.

Nobody told you he was a stand-up comedian. Stephen opened his side's door and got in beside Zoe. She was busy turning over the letter that she had recieved at Flaw 19. Over and over again. She didn't read it anymore -- she had memorized the note. Extortion. That was all it was. Money, it was all these bastards wanted.

If she had money, would she be throwing her body into the selfless form of endangerment that is referred to as professional wrestling? God, no. A woman really doesn't like to have her body tossed around like it is every other day. It makes one feel swept into a place where most women would call for a lawsuit or shoot their husband because of abuse.

But, she does it anyway. It's good money. Sometimes, she feels almost prostituted, though. And now she's being extorted.

Stephen put the key in the ignition. The clik-clack start of the engine... and somewhere under the hood, something internally combusted. Ker-pow. Music, Elvis Costello mixed in a "mash-up" with Eminem ("Less Than Zero" meets "Hailie's Song"), filled the car. It drowned all noise outside the car in decibel force.

For one moment, Zoe felt anxious and afraid. And then Eminem's less-than-stellar voice, singing "it all makes sense when I look into her eyes", calmed her down.

God knew that this song described her to a perfect point. She was such an... attaché. A blank person unless she was "with" someone. Teenage years were such a trial.

She had grown out of it, as she was concerned. She was independent, despite her always being around Stephen. That was a forced thing. She was doing performances with this man every other night in front of ten, twenty thousand people. Close contact tends to be a factor of need in that case.

Fuck.

That was what she said during high school, too.

"Fuckin' posers."

N.E.R.D.'s hit filled the car. "Rock Star". Heavy on the bass, heavy on the innovativeness.

"You can't beat me, I'm a rock star!"

Hmmm... Zoe almost hummed aloud. She kept it deep down, however. A vibration in her throat. Perhaps this would be the way to go... after all, Muhammad Ali stole his now-classic schtick from pro wrestling. She would never have thought of this before, but there was something about this music... it made a whole personality appear in front of her.

Maybe she could try it.

But, at the same time, there was this letter.

Stephen looked over at her. Zoe was deep in thought -- all these years together, and that was her most obvious notion. When she was thinking, you could tell, with the crinkling of the space between her eyes, the slight turn of her lips to the right. She wasn't even looking at the letter anymore.

"Steve..." she said suddenly. At this point, they were out from the town Stephen had stopped to grab a lemonade in. They were on their way to a house show -- following this, they would pull up to Flaw 20.

But there was a span of days between now and then.

"... I have a suggestion."
