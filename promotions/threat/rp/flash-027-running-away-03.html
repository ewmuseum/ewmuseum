running.away.03
Starring: Vincent Pembridge

Date: 21 Sep 2002
Location: Madrid; Spain


Having trained non-stop for the past hours, Vincent Pembridge wiped the sweat of his forehead and took a shower. Rosita was busy talking to her sister in the living room, and they'd been engaged in non-stop conversation for hours. Of course, briefly stopping when Vince walked through the door of the apartment, fresh from his training. The sister, Angique, seemed to have had her breath taken away... and the look on Rosita's face at the true upper-body of the Brit was priceless.

Pembridge smiled, as he stepped out of the dingy shower and wrapped a towel around his waist, before looking himself in the mirror. His sideburns had grown bushier, and his goatee was now reddish-brown. The British Degenerate figured a change would be nice, as it was a minor one. A major change, such as fleeing from Belgium to Spain... something he didn't expect and appreciate.

Not one to waste time, Vincent quickly made his way to the room, quietly. Before he dressed. Tonight was the night, where he'd would begin his job. Income would finally stream in, and a revisitation of an old sport was definitely one that the fighter formerly known as Jakks was relishing. It hadn't been long since The Scorpion Of Manchester was involved in a mainstream hybrid federation in the States, as well as an underground fighting promotion, hoping to retain its exclusivity from the outside world. Not to mention a federation in his homeland, that was slowly gaining cult status.

Three federations at once. But from three, it dwindled to none. And hence, the reason his career at Casa El' Gavriel had to start on the night. Successfully. Either that, or face the indignation of having been beaten by a man he'd defeated thrice before, with relative ease. Something Vincent Pembridge shuddered at the thought at, as he finished dressing and zipped up his black duffel bag.

He was ready.

Stepping out of the room, Pembridge tried to avoid contact with Angique & Rosita, but when both bade him goodbye, it was hard to not look at them. So he turned, smiled and mumbled a parting pleasentary in his broken Spanish, before getting himself out of the cagey tension-filled apartment. It was somewhat un-nerving, the way the two sisters were suddenly acting around him. Angique had been at the apartment before, but at the time, The Scorpion was asleep, worrying about Sikanah.

Suffice to say, it seemed his evasion tactics and false trails leading out of Belgium seemed to have done its job. No word of the Thai Warrior for a couple of days now, and Vincent was completely at ease. As he reached the pavement, he looked at the street, and frowned. It was still crowded, not surprisingly, at about 11 PM. Grumbling, he turned to his left and began a brisk walk towards his destination.

Aware of the fact that tonight was going to be a landmark night.

Sure, Santos Salvatore was obviously a man different from his tenure in the OFG. After November 2001, Santos just didn't seem to have the heart to fight anymore, and plunged into depression. Drinking, aimless brooding; he soon began to pile up defeat after defeat, and finally, a crushing at the hands of Vincent Pembridge in January of 2002 marked the beginning of the end of Salvatore's career in the OFG.

Now, a couple of months later, the Brazilian looked to be in absolutely perfect shape again, his punch once again packing heat, the venom in his attacks even more vicious than ever. The desire was inherent in him again, and Santos was clearly enjoying himself, in the realm of bar-fighting.

The first time Vincent Pembridge & Santos Salvatore met, the latter was picking himself up, after a devastating end to a bloody feud with another South American. Vincent Pembridge was looking for his second victory in as many fights. So, naturally, they were thrown together, to duke it out. See who deserved to get the praise and the money, while the loser would be forced to carry out slave duties for various members of the OFG roster.

Needless to say, Vince destroyed Salvatore.

Second encounter; Vincent Pembridge had become the OFG Submission Champion. His first defense, was against Santos, who'd fended off three other men in a breathtaking fight in New Zealand, as part of a plan by the OFG management to mix things up a bit. Once again, The Scorpion of Manchester triumped rather easily, picking up the victory in about five minutes, on the last event the Guild had in October 2002. One minute more than the first time these two brawled, but there wasn't any real problem for Pembridge.

But the third meeting in early January, was a wash-out. A mere two minutes, and Salvatore was down for the count. True, him being completely intoxicated might have something to with it, but Vincent Pembridge was deadlocked in a malevolent feud of his own. An enemy, who just didn't want to go away. Like cockroaches, who will still be around after a nuclear holocausts, Aristida Goneraiz refused to let his war with the British Degenerate remain unresolved, although from the latter's point of view, there was nothing to be resolved. That was the Outback Fighter's Guild for you, though. Bitter rivals would go to any lengths to end it, and on their terms.

Looking up, Vincent Pembridge crossed the road, and his eyes fell upon the bouncer of the Casa El' Gavriel. It was a new bouncer, and Pembridge started to fear that maybe, he wouldn't be let in. But as he got closer, the bouncer smiled, and opened the door for Vincent Pembridge. Looked as if the owner of the tavern might have mentioned a thing or two to the new man. Pembridge nodded his head in acknowledgement, as he brushed past the bouncer and stepped into the tavern.

Immediately catching sight of Santos Salvatore peform a spinning roundhouse kick, knocking down a much bigger opponent. The bloodthirsty crowd roared in delight, and gulped down their beers, tantalised by the concept of the tavern.

"Guess it's my bloody turn next."
