the.paralysis.project.02
Starring: Vincent Pembridge

Date: 30 May 2003
Location: Atlanta, GA; USA


Vincent Pembridge stepped onto the premises of Jacken's Pub and growled at the sight of all the apparent-rednecks in the pub. Right off, Vince knew that the pub was condemned, but he wasn't there for pleasure. Oh no, it was all about business. He spotted a table with nobody seated there and quickly made his way toward it, whilst several of the regular patrons stared up at the Brit who'd just entered their hangout.

The Scorpion couldn't be bothered in glaring back. He had a lot of things on his mind, but right at the top of the list was his sister. And the way she had cost him immensely a few days prior. Vincent had thought that Karen Pembridge had been tucked away in Manchester, not wanting to return to the land where the relationship between the two siblings soured and hit rock bottom.

He was wrong.

Karen returned to the United States towards the end of January of the year, with a bloodlust that seemed difficult to quench. She wished to return to the fighting industry, but couldn't find a promotion that seemed... good enough. The re-emergence of tFZ didn't interest her at all, but finally, her trainer/agent/friend Takahasi Marinaroj had gotten her involved in a gig with a wrestling company.

Icons Of Wrestling.

Needless to say, Karen wasn't thrilled at first, but slowly got into the swing of things. It didn't take long for her to make a name for herself in the industry... and within weeks, she had gotten her wish. A job in a fighting promotion. THE fighting promotion in the land -- theAsylum.

Ironically enough, Takahasi had almost managed to get Karen into tA before the IOW gig came along. But after knowing that she'd only get in following giving owner Joe Campbell an almighty blowjob, The British Lassie decided to search for greener pastures. Either way, she finally ended up in tA, and was juggling two gigs at one go.

IOW soon closed, and tA became her main focus. tA, where Ty Hughes also plyed his trade in.

Vincent Pembridge -- wearing a long-sleeved black t-shirt, blue jeans, and black boots -- hissed again as he took his seat at the corner table. His memory bank simply HAD to bring up the name of the man that was behind the masterplan. At the Malevolence PPV, Ty Hughes somehow sneaked Karen Pembridge into the building... and the Lassie was the defining factor in the Vagabonds/Resistance classic.

Her involvement allowed The Resistance to win, and put paid to Vinnie's hopes of turning thReat into a fighting promotion for his own greedy need.

"What can I get yer?" a middle-aged lady with the face resembling a cow's rear asked of Vincent, as she finally attended to him.

The Scorpion of Manchester looked up and had the urge to vomit, but managed to keep it all in. It just underlined how ugly the waitress attending to him was. Shaking his head, Pembridge leaned back in his chair and looked back at his clenched fists that rested on his table.

"A shot of Pimms No. 5, and make it bloody snappy."

The waitress grunted as she took down the order and skipped away, off to process it. Vincent, in the meanwhile, returned back to thinking about his sister and Ty Hughes. About how they had conspired to teach the British Degenerate a lesson. He knew what Karen's motives were, but had no clue as to why Ty did what he did.

Did he maybe know of Vincent's plan to cut him out of the picture if The Vagabonds did really get the chance to rule thReat with their collective iron fists? That had to be it, Vincent thought, as he scratched his neck. Over the past few days, Vinnie had disbanded The Vagabonds, seeing no need to continue with the gang. Their mission had failed, and it was time to move on. But the Manucian Predator wasn't ready to give up on thReat.

No.

The man had acquired the virtue of patience, and was loving the new masterplan mapped out not only by the Senior Partners of The Patterson Agency, his newest 'employers'... but by his original boss. Not to mention the advisory plan handed down by Joe Campbell a long while ago. Even if the deranged owner of theAsylum always forgot who Vincent was.

The waitress came back with a highball glass filled with The Scorpion's choice of drink on a round tray and quickly placed it down on Vincent's table. Pembridge quickly fished out a ten dollar bill from the right side-pocket of his jeans and handed it over to the waitress, who skipped away... much to Vincent's relief.

As he takes a sip of his drink, Vincent looks over his shoulder, noting a man about the same size as him entering the pub. Immediately heading to the bar and sitting on one of the stools, making his order confidently. Smiling, the Scorpion decided to discard all thoughts of Malevolence & Ty Hughes & Karen Pembridge. thReat was still under siege, as far as he was concerned, and Ty Hughes... well, he'd get what was coming to him soon enough.

At the moment, business was about to be conducted.

From the left side-pocket, The Manucian Predator pulled out a sheet of paper and unfolded it, revealing it to be a picture. He laid it out on the table and with the back of his hand, straightened it out. Taking one glance at it, Vince looked over his shoulder again, at the man who walked in.

"It's him."

Smiling, he crushed the paper into a ball and took another sip of his drink.

It was time to advance The Paralysis Project.
