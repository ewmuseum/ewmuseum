And So It Begins
Starring: Sacrilege

“GET AWAY FROM ME YOU PSYCHO!!!”

She stepped backwards looking down at her boy.

“GET AWAY FROM ME AND MY FAMILY!!!”

The woman roared at her son in utter disgust while he kneeled down on the grass outside their home. Many of the village people had gathered around the small terraced home, they just like Aidan’s mother, are in complete disarray.

His head hung in his hands.

His clothing ripped and torn almost completely covered in a dark red substance.

He looked up at his mother, the tears streaming down his stained face, “I… I’m so, so sorry Mum. I… I don’t know what happened.”

She stepped forward closer to him and looked down over him. He looked up at her seeing the disgust in her eyes he hung his head down again.

“Get away from me, and get out of this village!!!” she screamed at him, making him cringe slightly and look up at her.

“Forgive me Mum,” He said as his eyes, swelled up, met his mothers. “Forgive me”

She took another step back and looked to be walking away and retiring to her home, but she stopped, looked down on her son again before drawing back her right hand.

SMACK!!!

Her hand crashed against the side of her son’s face forcing his head to turn to his right where he stayed for a few seconds. As he moved his head round again, his mother once again drew her hand back.

“Ouch” she cried, as Aidan grabbed her by the wrist and clutched it tightly, turning his head back round to look up at her. The tears had stopped, and his face was now a dry crimson picture.

“Don’t ever do that again!” He said angrily

A look of fear came over his mothers face, she shook her arm hastily trying to make her son release it, but he continued to clench it as hard as he could.

She looks down at him, wondering what had come over her once loving and caring son, before spurting out “What you did tonight is unforgivable to me, and all these people out here,” pointing towards the crowd with her free arm.

“Go, leave this village! Never show your face here again!”

His grip begins to loosen, as he brings himself to stand up.

“Fine, I’ll go and I’ll never step foot in this crappy little village again!”

Aidan turns and walks away slowly, being jeered by the crowd of people watching his every move. His mother watches on with tears running down her face, wondering what has happened to her little boy.
