What Women Want: 101 - Documentary Interrupted
Starring: R.A.T & Sugar

R.A.T ran down the street after his chubby guinea pig, Jane when a car pulled up alongside him.

"Randy!" shouted a voice.

He stopped in his tracks, watching his tubby friend stroll away in fury. He sighed deeply before turning to see who it was that had interrupted his award winning series.

"What?" he said in an annoyed manner.

He then realised who it was and he grinned.

"Sugar! How are you? You can help, I need a cameraman and it looks like you just got the job," he told his manager.

Sugar leaned out of the window shaking his head looking a little aggravated.

"Randall, we have to get out of town tonight, we're going to Mansfield early so you can train," he told him.

Randall began to say the word "But", but was interrupted by his manager with a stern look and a snappy answer.

"I don't know what you're doing, nor want to know, coz it could only be trouble. But Randy, this is important, you have a match coming up."

R.A.T turned to look down the street. His little heifer had disappeared from his sight, which meant a scowl came across his face when he turned back to Sugar.

He threw his arms in the air as if he gave up.

"Shit, Sugar. You've just lost me my documentary. What match could be so important that my documentary should suffer?" he asked.

His eyes widened in that way your mother says with her own eyes "I'm-waiting-for-you-to-explain-yourself-young-man!" He put a hand on his hip and held his camera with the other.

Sugar sighed as he looked at R.A.T, letting him feel his disappointment.

"This is all some big game to you, isn't it, Randy? You think that things are just gonna fall in your lap coz your cute and can do a back flip, right? You got it all wrong, son. Completely wrong," he began to tell him.

Randall rolled his eyes and looked down at his feet.

"You look at me when I'm talking to you, son. This is important!"

"Sugar" Steve McGowen opened the door and stepped out of the vehicle, killing the engine as he stood up. He moved across to R.A.T and took the camera from his hand, handing it into the car to the driver who put it on the back seat.

He put a hand on each shoulder and bobbed down a little to look in his eye, let him know how damn important his next match was.

"Randall, this next match is a part of the International title tournament."

R.A.T's eyes lit up like a kid at Christmas. He shook his head in disbelief as Sugar continued to tell him the importance of the match that was coming up for him.

"I'm telling you that there are big things in store for you, Randall. There are big things in store. This tournament wasn't my idea. No. It's an invitational tournament, Randy. Invitational. They invite you to compete!"

"They invited me?" he tried to reaffirm.

"Damn straight they did, Randy, and do you know why? It's coz you're fresh and exciting, son. You're money. Money in the bank. For you, for me and for thReat. You could be the back bone of this company, kid-O. You're only 16 years old and you've already been around the circuit for a year and half, and you're here. If you wanted, Randall, this could be the last fed you're ever in."

Randall's gaze began to float skyward as he dreamed about what his career with thReat could be like. What it could be like if he actually won this International title tournament. What it could be like to be the International Champion of thReat!

Sure, there would be some money. He could send some down to his mother and father so they could fix up the H.M.S Alder and do whatever they wanted. Hell, maybe even buy a brand new house boat which would be better and wouldn't leak with a strong current.

There would be fame, too. He would be a household name throughout all of America. Maybe even the world. When people think of a cruiserweight they'll think of the 16 year old who won the thReat International title. He could be the biggest 5'8" kid in the world!

There would be women. So he'd be able to finish off his documentary on what women want. He might be able to sell it to other kids who are trying to get rid of that troublesome cherry and they could elevate themselves from being a boy to being a man.

And then there would be Pedro.

He could prove to Pedro that he made him into the best wrestler there was. He would dedicate the title to Pedro. The person who pushed him into the spotlight. He would dedicate it to Bull Mask, his mentor. And the memory of Bull Mask II.

This is why he and Pedro worked so hard.

This was the pinnacle of what they aimed for.

No, not the pinnacle. It was just the prologue of things to come. It was just the start to great things for Randall Alder-Tangk.

"Whoa!" Sugar said bringing him back down to earth.

R.A.T looked into his eyes to hear what he had to say.

"This ain't gonna be a walk in the park, Randy. You have to beat Sergey Kovhachek. It's not gonna be easy. And if you beat him, you have to beat the winner of blind and Steven Fury. And then someone else, and then someone else. And then it'll come down to Double or Nothing.

"But it's not gonna be easy. No walk in the park, Randy. We gotta think long and hard and work even harder to get the gold 'round your belly. We gotta take this one day at a time, slugger.

"But I think you can do it.

"I think you could be...

"thReat International Champion!"
