who'd-a-thunkit? [3]
Starring: haTE

Baseball bats?

"Shit, you sit around for a few minutes and then you get a mob after you with baseball bats," haTE joked with.. well himself. "Shut the hell up!" one of them yelled. A few laughes were heard in the group. haTE's eyes darted from one direction to another.

"You know how much I hate this, right?"

The punk he met in the alley comes forward. Right into haTE's face, where he spits blood in it. He then swings his baseball bat and connects right in the side of haTE, where he drops to the ground moaning.

"That stung a little bit!"
"oOo-shit.."

haTE's voice as he rolled around on the ground. He finally climbed to one knee. "Why all the hostility?" he asked, as he moaned to get up, "and shit son, is that all you got? My grandma swings a better bat than you do."

haTE smiles. He was always good at this, pissing off his adversaries by use of his mouth. It was the only way to forget the rage that was building inside when he started getting picked on and pushed around.

"We're about to make your life-"

The punk hits the homeless guy laying on the ground with the bat. A loud 'THUD' noise is heard, and then the moan of a grown man as he rolls out onto the side of the street, off of the sidewalk.

"-a living hell!"

At seeing them hit the man on the ground for no reason at all, haTE forgot his pain. Everytime he saw the man get hit, or made fun of by one of the boys tonight, flashes of his childhood went through his head. All of the times that HE himself got picked on and beat up because of his family and where he lived or didn't live.

In rage, haTE stood up and tackled the boy. The rest of them piled on, trying to get at haTE, this went on for only a few minutes when sirens were heard. The boys all looked up, saw the flashes of lights.. and bailed. haTE was left laying on the ground. He was sore, but not really hurt. The baseball bat shot was going to leave a bruise, but nothing serious. He hadn't hit haTE half as hard has he hit the old man, who was laying on the side of the road.

And then the police officers got out of their cars and looked at both of them, lying on the ground.

"Shit-"

Nice, thats something real good to hear from a police officer.

"-they got away."

Oh, thanks for caring.

"We've got two of them down."

Footsteps and then a man was over to haTE.

"Sir, are you okay?" he asked him. Maybe there were people that cared. "I'm fine," haTE responded, as he sat up, "go and check on the man over there, he's in a lot worse shape than I am."

The man ran over there. haTE looked around at all the happenings. He wondered how he could get himself in that mess, and who would have called the police. Two quad cares sat by him, and four police officers were on the scene. One was calling an ambulance for haTE and the homeless man. He didn't plan on sticking around to answer any questions.

Police and questionings just didn't do it for him. He nonchalantly, got up and rounded the corner, gone before anyone noticed he got up.

He never found out how the old man was- but so many feelings were brought up.

Have you ever seen that black and white picture in the back of your head, where you see something happening, and then you put it in the black and white picture of you, like you're watching it on a home video. He had been doing that a lot tonight.

He wondered if he'd ever see any of the boys again. Probabley not, as in a little while he'd be picking up his life, and moving to another place for a week to be wrestling again. This life killed him, but its what he loved to do.

Another chapter was left undone in his life.
That is where his wrestling name came from, I guess,

If he could just go back and do some of the things he left undone in his life, a lot of hate, pain, and fury would be brought back. Things were never done properly.. and it was just continuing..
