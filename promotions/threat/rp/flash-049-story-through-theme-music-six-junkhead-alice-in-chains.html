Starring: Twizzy

Junk, fuck.

GCW was gone, but Twizzy was at ease. He was a World Champion. He would get work easily. Big offers would come from all over the place.

A good night, the best in a long time.
A new friend turned me on to an old favorite.

Twizzy fell into Twistodium. He fell into bottle after bottle after bottle. He took a job here and there with small promotions for a few show appearances. Other than that he didn’t really work. He just lied back and waited for a call from a big company. The big time would eventually call. ‘One day the big time will be here.’ One day…

Seems so sick to the hypocrite norm.
Running their boring drills.
But we are an elite race of our own.
The stoners, junkies, and freaks.

He fell into Twistodium and was on it all the time. He was dependent on it and this led to a subsequent depression. A severe depression. Twizzy was lost from reality; once again he was a lost soul. Twizzy was nothing. This depression and nothingness lasted him over eight months.

Are you happy? I am, man.

At one show he threw up on his opponent before collapsing into the ring ropes.

Content and fully aware.
Money, status, nothing to me.
'Cause your life is empty and bare.

He was banned from wrestling in Minnesota for taking a leak on a wrestling owner. Consequently the man was a Texan people called, “The Alamo” Jack Harrison.

What's my drug of choice?
Well, what have you got?

and I do it a lot.
I do it a lot, yeah.

Twizzy ended up going into a more degrading atmosphere. He started pit fighting every once in awhile and wasn’t half-bad at it. A fighter from a wrestler. Not only that, he was a fighter from a wrestler who was a druggy. Twizz: The Lost Soul.

You can't understand a user's mind.
But try, with your books and degrees.
If you let yourself go and opened your mind
I'll bet you'd be doing like me.
And it ain't so bad.

Today Twizzy finds himself with a job and his depression subsiding. His addiction is worse than ever as he feels excess is goodness. Twizzy enters thReat with skill in fighting and wrestling. He has a superhuman helper and a very twisted persona. Some of the major consequences of Twizz’s Twistodium addiction are that he has some weird fascination with goats, his physical abilities shift constantly, he has some philisophical outlook on the world around him at all times, and he is absolutely crazY.
