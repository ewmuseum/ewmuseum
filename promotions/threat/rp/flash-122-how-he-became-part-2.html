How he became [Part 2]
Starring: Two-Face

At the age of 11, any Russian boy would be looking forward to moving up to the second stage of school, meeting new friends and the challenge that was brought to him by the higher standard of education. But Vladimir Simunek wasn’t a normal kid. For 11 years of his life he had been subjected to brutality that would have shocked children’s charities. He had never made any friends, and was so paranoid that a simple sound of a coin dropping on the floor beside him would make him shiver in his boots.

Vladimir quickly packed a pencil and pen into his plain blue bag and then took a quick glance into his bathroom window. His right eye was dark blue; the skin on the right side of his face was overly pale due to the makeup that he had put on trying to cover the swollen cheek bone, huge cuts and wounds that were still visible to the naked human eye. He quickly wiped a tear from his eye and shook his head frantically trying to re-group himself.

“I won’t let them get to me” he said in his childish voice.

“VLADIMIR” Boris belched up the stairs forcefully encouraging his son to come down the door. “Bus is here!!” he finished opening the front door.

Vladimir quickly threw his bag over his shoulders and ran down the stairs trying not to anger his father. He walked down the small hallway to the door and was greeted by the shallow eyes of his father.

“Remember what I said Vladimir. I’ll be watching” Boris said reminding his son that he was to make no friends. “Don’t want another slap now do you?” he asked, as Vladimir quickly shook his head.

“No sir” Vladimir replied in a gentle childish voice.

“Good” Boris replied, “then I will see you at 4 o’clock!”

Winters in Russia were harsh. Fierce cold winds could pierce through your clothes and could break you down so easily, just like a knife through hot butter. The heavens dropped small icy particles of snow that the wind would forcefully send crashing into your body, and the sun laid in the sky dormant laughing at you because it was nothing more then an attraction. Boris Simunek had sent Vladimir out in what would have been summer clothes for a kid in the Arizona desert. Vladimir wore small, thin pale brown shorts, white socks that he had dragged up to his knee caps to try and protect his legs against the horrible and harsh weather, that would sooner or later fall down due to him walking. Vladimir’s shoes were overly worn, and holes were immerging in the soles. He had a grey T-shirt, and a small blue cap that covered his messy brown hair. His arms carried the tatty looking plain blue bag that his mother had given him. Vladimir had begged that his father give him a jumper the night before, but Boris simply gave him a firm slap on the ass and told him that he had to become a man.

Vladimir walked on ever so slowly down the small front path, and shut the black gate behind him. He noticed the school bus was across the road and the driver that was waving him to hurry up. Vladimir approached the road, took a look to his right and cautiously crossed approaching the bus door, which opened for him.

“You must be Vladimir” said the bus driver in a deep Russian accent. “Amply dressed I see” he sarcastically continued, pointing around the bus, “Sit anywhere kid” he finished shutting the bus doors quickly, trying to avoid letting in too much of the cold weather.

Vladimir nodded and looked down the aisle of seats looking for a single place, where he could look out of the window. He again was met by an orchestra of eyes staring at him, some of the kids pointed, all shocked at the sight of his facial problems. There was the inevitable schoolboy jerk that sat at the back of the bus laughing pointing and shouting names at him.

Vladimir found a seat where he thought that he would be safe from the staring and the name-calling. He threw the bag to the floor below him, and starred out of the window. His mind kept replaying the words of the kids on the bus. Vladimir began to shake and bang on the window, his paranoia running amok.

“Haha, that guy has two different faces!”

“What a freak”

“Ohh my god what the hell happened to him.”

“That’s disgusting!!”

Vladimir took a couple of deep breaths, calming himself down. He knew he was going to get all of this for a very long time. He would have to keep cool.

But could he?

End.
