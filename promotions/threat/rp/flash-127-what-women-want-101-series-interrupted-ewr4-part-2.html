What Women Want: 101 - Series Interrupted - EWR4 - Part 2
Starring: thReat's Teenage Icon

3 hours later...

It was past midnight and R.A.T was still at his laptop. He had been adding every single member of thReat to his EWR roster so he could use them within the game. It had taken him three hours and he had one wrestler left to add to the roster.

blind.

His opponent coming up on Flaw 25.

R.A.T smiled as he looked at the options for creating the wrestler that is blind. If he couldn't beat him in the ring, though he didn't know if he could or not yet, he would beat him in EWR.

He began to fill out the fields.

"Full Name".

"blind" he typed into the space provided.

"Shortened Name".

"poo" he typed into the space.

"Gender" was next.

R.A.T sighed loudly as he thought about what to put into that slot.

'There's no transexual there. I guess I'll have to put "female" for it then,' he thought to himself.

He couldn't wait any longer to fill out all the rest of the info. So he just clicked immediately on "Edit Worker Statistics".

"YOU SUCK BLIND!" he shouted at the top of his voice in a joking way.

He heard something crash and bang in the next room where Sugar was staying. Then someone trodding across the room to open the door. R.A.T slapped his forehead. HE had forgotten Sugar had gone to bed. The door creaked open.

"Randy!" he scolded.

He stood from his chair and shrugged as he looked over to Sugar. He pointed back to his computer.

"I'm just researching, I, er, got a little excited about the quarter finals match for Flaw, ya know?"

A half sleepy smile from Sugar was reassuring enough for R.A.T to tell him he was no longer in trouble. He turned around and sat back down on his chair, his back to Sugar.

"I promise. No more noise, I swear I'll keep it down to a dull roar," he promised.

Sugar nodded as he turned around to head back to bed, scratching his arse on the way back through the door. R.A.T began to type away madly as he heard the door behind him shut.

It was time to edit those statistics.

Brawling: 3

Speed: 7

Technical: 3

Stiffness: 15

Selling: 2

Over: 0

Charisma: 0

Attitude: 0

Behaviour: 0

"Tee hee, I told you that ya suck, blind!" he whispered to himself as he clicked the "Back" button.

He clicked "Back" again.

blind had been created, now for him and blind to face each other inside of EWR. PErhaps this could be the lead in to him defeating blind in the finals of the thReat International title tournament.

But it was more something to set his mind at ease.

He beginning to grow nervous. He had no idea he'd advance. He wanted to, but now he was here he was more nervous than when he hopped into the ring with Sergey.

His career was starting right before his eyes and he didn't know where to hang on. He just knew that he had to somehow. By the skin of his teeth he'd hang on and see where this could take him.

But right now, he had opportunity to beat a strong competitor in blind.

In the ring and on the computer.

R.A.T wanted to be victorious!
