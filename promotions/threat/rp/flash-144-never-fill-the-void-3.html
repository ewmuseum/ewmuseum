never fill the void [3]
Starring: haTE

"His father-
was.. dead."

Dead?

It couldn't happen. But.. it did. haTE turned and looked at the lifeless body of his father. There was nothing that could happen. He was gone.. and as far as he was conserned, he had been ever since that call.

He had the feeling, you know, in the pit of your stomach that you know someone horrible is about to happen.

Yeah, that feeling. As soon as he heard the steady tone of the heart monitor, he felt a piece of him break off. A piece of him that obviously could never be restored.

His face went pale. He turned.. and walked out.
He couldn't take this.

He took the paper that his aunt had gave him, and walked down the hall. She yelled back at him, but, he didn't hear her. He didn't want to hear her. Right now, he was in his "happy place".

And he wouldn't be broke out of it yet.

He returned to the outdoors. It was hot, still. The streets weren't as crowded, as break is now over. He looked at the paper, as he took a seat at a bench. He looked at the paper, and the name of the neighborhood was fimilar to him.

He'd lived there for a long time. Actually, the longest he'd lived at any one place in Japan. It was his last home.

Why she wouldn't be there, was beyond him. He waved down the first cab that came. He still looked to be in a trance as he stepped into the car. He sat in silence for a moment. The driver yelled at him in Spanish, and he finally broke out of it. He told him where to go, and they took off. It took a matter of five minutes. They arrived, and haTE stepped out of the car.

The memories rushed back to him.

He walked for all of ten minutes, she was no where to be found. He covered a long bit in that few minutes.

He stopped, panting. He'd been running the last 5 minutes. From what, he didn't know. He sat at a curb and finally, broke down. He put his head in his hands and sobbed.

And sobbed- sobbed..

Finally, he happen to look over. A fimilar person stood, just a few streets down. He jumped up. He ran.. and ran. All of the sudden, the woman backed up and put her hands in the air. A shot rang out.

Now- she was dead.

He stopped dead in his tracks. He walked the rest of the way, as he didn't want to see what had just happend.

His mother was dead.

He kneeled over her and cried again.. nothing he could do, as he heard the sirens in the distance. She was killed, doing what she only knew how.

Now.. he had no reason to live...

wait.. one more-
The children.
