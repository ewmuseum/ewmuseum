The Journey To The Top(Pt 4 "Backstage Politics")
Starring: Andrew Martin

7/07/2003 forty-five minutes prior to showtime.

Andrew Martin strolled down the hallway with a purpose. His outer demeanor appeared calm. His inner demeanor was that of a caged animal that had been starved for days, however. He had just been shunned by some green punk kid who thought he was better than Martin. He stopped and knocked on the door of the 'office' of Jack Armstrong. It wasn't so much of an office as it was the ladies washroom. Martin angrily pounded on the door and was told to come in by Armstrong from behind the door. When he entered, There sitting in a chair was Patrick Bauer, and standing behind a desk was Jack Armstrong. The latter two men appeared to be surprised by the appearence of Andrew in the doorway.

'Ahhh just the two men I've been wanting to see!' he said half truthfully and half-sarcasicly.

'What do you want?' Bauer sneered at him.

'I'm just here to conduct a little business with the two of you is all,' Martin replied before taking a seat in the wooden chair.

'So how is this all going to go down?' Martin questioned.

'Well we'd like for Mat to win this one,' Jack Armstrong said.

'WHAT!' Bauer yelled and exploded up from his seat, 'You mean to tell me that a bloke like....'

'Hey! That bloke is my Nephew' Martin shot back.

'Take a seat now!' Armstrong said, 'And for god's sake calm down'

'Now, Mat is going to win this by pinfall, Andrew you'll distract him by piulling on his leg from the outside of the ring and Mat runs in and gets the school boy 1, 2, 3 easy as that' Armstrong concluded.

'He can't win if he doesn't have a head left on his shoulders!' Bauer screamed.

He got up from the chair put Martin forced him back down and looked him in the face.

'If you still him I swear to god I'll jump in there and strangle to life out of you,' Martin muttered to him

'Alright, alright I won't kill the poor bastard just get off of me,' Bauer pleaded.

'GET OUT!' Jack Armstrong screamed, 'Andrew get out, you and I will talk later on this mater and your conduct,'

'It's alright, I've got everything I want out of this discussion'

Martin gave a quick wave to Armstrong. Patrick Bauer just snarled at him.

'And a pleasure doing business with you too Mister Bauer,' He said sarcasticly.

He left the room and just as the door had closed Martin said under his breath:

'You prick,'
