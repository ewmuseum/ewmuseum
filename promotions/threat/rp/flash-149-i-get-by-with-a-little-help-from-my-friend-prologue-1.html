I Get By With A Little Help From My Friend (Prologue) [1]
Starring: Chris "Neverwhere" McCluskey

Chris “Neverwhere” McCluskey sat asleep in his tour bus that was on the last leg of its journey – the road to the arena at which Double or Nothing would take place. The bus, nicely entitled the “Cluck Express” by the driver, was empty apart from the Northern Irishman and that same driver, Joe Soap.

After successfully putting away haTE in their semi-final contest last Sunday night at Flaw 25, Chris had realised that he was the underdog in the final. Blind wasn’t a better wrestler than Chris; oh no, but he was superior in his support. The fans seemed to really like him, and that could potentially be an obstacle. So Chris devised the ingenious plan of asking good old Joe to be in his corner for the final, and Joe had somehow managed to drag himself away from the gutter in which he was now living.

It turned out that Joe had read about Chris’ rising success in thReat and had longed to get in touch with his former tag-team partner, but unfortunately had lost contact with him. But since Flaw had been held in Ohio not too long ago, Joe was able to get the required phone number, and Chris was pleasantly surprised to find a familiar voice at the end of the line.

It was a short story, but here’s the long of it: Joe didn’t have a telephone where he lived, and he came up with the plan of committing petty theft so that he would be put in jail overnight. He used his one phone call to ring Chris and ask his buddy to come and pay the fine he was charged with. And being the stand-up, generous, lonely guy Chris is, he kindly obliged. If only Joe had realised the value of the public pay-telephone.

The two of them soon sat inside a local bar, and reminisced about the past. It turned out that Joe now lived in Scandinavia, or at least that’s what Chris thought Joe had said. Actually, he didn’t live in Norway, he was living in a door way. Deciding that Joe had been too long without a job, Chris offered him the almost-royal task of accompanying him to Double or Nothing. Realising they had no transport of any kind; they paid a visit to a rental car company. However, Joe had grander ideas, and begged Chris to rent a tour bus. Always one for the extraordinaire, Chris agreed to Joe’s superb idea, and soon they were on their way.

As they caught up on old times, they both came up with yet another ingenious idea – to pay a visit to all their old buddies from the good old days. Unfortunately, it turned out that Lowest Common Denominator was no longer living in the United States, so that ended that bright idea. They had managed to track down former cWo guys, Ike Rogers, The Superstar, whyttrash, and Austin Douglas. The less said about that the better, but it’s just too good a tale to waste.

The penultimate leg of the journey had led the C-Team to Disney World, but that’s not really that important. What is important is that I begin at the very beginning.
