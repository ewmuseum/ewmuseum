running.away.02
Starring: Vincent Pembridge

Date: 19 Sep 2002
Location: Madrid; Spain


Vincent awoke, as the penetrating rays of the sun focused on his bald head. Cursing silently, he rubbed the crust from his eyes and yawned, before rolling out of bed. It was almost 2 PM, and The Scorpion had actually had one of his better nights of sleep. He smiled, before dragging himself out of his small room, and into the main area of the apartment.

Luckily enough for Pembridge, Rosita wasn't at home. In fact, she'd been out since 10 AM, and by the look of things, Vincent assumed she wouldn't return until it was evening time.

Vince sighed, in relief, and promptly threw himself onto the red leather couch. It was yet another warm day in Madrid, and the fact that roomie Rosita wasn't around made things a little bit better for the Brit. See, Rosita Ornutiz Sallanegro was a young and attractive lady. Legs to die for, really vibrant hair, hypnotic eyes... but one hell of an attitude. Seemed as if she had PMS every day of her life, and was somewhat neurotic.

Ask the Manucian; he'd been ordered on numerous occasions to do various chores around the apartment. Seeing how he shared it with Rosita, Vincent was obviously inclined to help out. It was his place as much as it was hers. But being forced to clean an already-spotless apartment? Something was loose in Rosita's head, no doubt.

Laying there, motionless on the couch, Vincent Pembridge started to flex his biceps, purely out of boredom. He'd gone back to the tavern last night, and watched as Santos Salvatore easily took care of two challenges. No sweat for the Brazilian street-fighter, whose punches could knock out a bull. Of course, back in the days of the Outback Fighter Guild, there was only one person to fear.

The man, the myth, the scorpion. The most dangerous and venomous living thing out there.

Vincent Pembridge. Who was known as Jakks, at the time.

---

Detective Jenna fired her gun again as the truck swung away from the down helicopter. She was hoping to either get Jakks in the left shoulder area or hit an essential part of the engine, but she was unlucky. The truck lumbered down the potholed road. She looked at the crashed helicopter for a fleeting second and cringed, for the pilot wasn't moving. Just when she thought that she would have to risk Jakks getting away, back-up arrived.. and she continued her chase.

It was a sluggish vehicle, taking long periods of time to accelerate. At first, she closed the gap rapidly.. but Jakks changed gear of his own cruiser, and he picked up speed. A sharp right turn proved a little hard for Jakks to navigate, though... and Jenna saw this as an opportunity.

A crazy opportunity, yes. But an opportunity nonetheless.

She jumped out of the jeep, did a half roll and got on her feet quickly enough to see Jakks conquer the turn.. the chase was on. Once again, the truck picked up speed.. and Jenna ran as fast as she could. Her heart pounding, her chest aching, her legs threatening to fall off. She was slowly but surely gaining on Jakks and his cruiser.. but it would take a mammoth effort to catch it.

Either that or some freaky piece of luck.

Like a sudden shift of gears from Jakks, which caused a momentary slowdown?

Being a cop, you had to have amazing reflexes... and Jenna remembered that as she saw the truck slow down right before her very eyes.

Jenna put on a burst of speed and leaped for the tailgate of Jakks's truck.

Somehow, she made it. She got one foot on the bumper and grabbed the spare wheel desperately. For a frightening moment, she thought she would slip and fall...

And an amazingly sharp U-turn by Jakks made her fear come true.

The chase was over.. Jakks had escaped, heading for the Australian Outback. The experience of the Underground Fighting Federation was behind him. Now, a new chapter in his life was beginning. One that would involve even more brutality, and no let-up in the dealing of illegal activity.

---

The prelude to those months in the OFG was certainly interesting. Finally ousted as UFF Ultimate Heavyweight Champion, Vincent took it upon himself to retire from the realm of true underground fighting, and now set his sights on a more unorthodox brand of fighting. Pembridge smiled, remembering that memory of those turbulent couple of days in Australia, with the thoughts of getting to the OFG homebase the only thing in his mind.

"Good ol' days..." he muttered, before getting up off the couch and dragging himself towards the kitchen. His stomach was growling, which meant only one thing.

Time for food.

And the Scorpion Of Manchester needed to have a lot of it. The day was going to be filled with training, and researching, and the scouting of one Santos Salvatore. While in the OFG, Vince & Salvatore fought three times.

The British Degenerate won all three of them.
