Why We Do The Things We Do: Prologue
Starring: Levond Daniels

Sometimes in life, the best thing you can do is sit down and reflect on the past. Sit down and think about what has happened in your life. Some of the things you remember most.

When you don't understand why you are the way you are, or why you say and do the things you're saying and doing, you can usually find those answers in your past, if you search hard enough. Sometimes you even already know the answers. Sometimes they're handed to you on a silver platter every time you wake up. Sometimes you see them in the mirror when you brush your teeth. Sometimes though. Sometimes you can see your answers when you're doing a specific thing.

Knowing your answers, and still reflecting on the past, can be very helpful, however. When you need the drive to move forward. The determination to move forward. Maybe you want to understand why you're trying to protect the loved one of a person you hate more than life itself.

That would be the case for Levond Daniels. Levond sits every night, alone in his hotel room, glancing at the wall. All he can do is think about things. Think about where he's at. What he's doing. For him it's a rather odd feeling. Actually caring for someone who's not immediate family, or a girlfriend.

Levond has spent the past half a year trying to save Jessica Jenkins. He has been beaten relentlessly by Anubis. He has lost a belt thanks to Anubis. He has been tricked, tortured. Then at Payback, when he finally had Anubis defeated, he decided to finally find out the answers to why.

The only thing he found out, was that so far, everything he knew was a lie. The man under the mask, at least at Payback, wasn't even possible to be the man Levond Daniels had been chasing across the country. Vincent Pembridge? No way.

Anubis had found himself an ally, but Levond knew he couldn't give up. Even now, with the option of using his feet for attacks, Levond knew he couldn't give up his plan to save Jessica Jenkins. Sure, it had been side tracked. Not derailed, however.

He still can't help but wonder why. Why he's helping. Everything that had happened between he and Shawn. Thus he reflects on the past, trying to find a common ground he and Shawn shared. Something that could give him a reason to give a damn about Shawn, or how Shawn was feeling. He knew he did, he just couldn't understand why he did.

All he could remember were the bad things.
