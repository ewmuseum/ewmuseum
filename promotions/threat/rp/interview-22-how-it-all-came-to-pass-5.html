How It All Came To Pass [5]
Starring: Jordan X Shabazz

Saturday, October 5th, 2002

Again Jordan X Shabazz was at square one, sitting at home in front of his television, living off of money that he had saved up in the bank from his time in PCW. He hadn’t been in a stable federation since leaving PCW, he was a traveller since then, appearing in a federation for a few shows, and then leaving for one reason or another. This time, he left ACW because of a knee injury. He needed to heal up quickly and get back in the ring, because he couldn’t live off of his PCW money forever.

Jordan X Shabazz was flipping the channels when he saw a familiar face, it was that of PIW superstar, and former head of CDLL, Ray Chavez. Shabazz, wondering what was on the TV, continued to watch.

“That’s right, I, Ray Chavez, along with Andrew Styles and [name withheld] am getting back into the realm of ownership in a wrestling promotion. I’ll be running a federation called Sports Entertainment, or SE. So all you aspiring and accomplished wrestlers, send your promo tapes to…”

Jordan X Shabazz got up, and limped to a table where he quickly jotted down the address for which he could send his promotion tape to Ray Chavez and SE.

He hadn’t been a fan of CDLL, but he had heard great things about it, and assumed with great ownership like the Chavez, Styles, and Clayborn, it would be a great place that would provide a stable, steady pay check to Jordan X Shabazz and satisfy his itch to be in the ring.

Jordan X Shabazz called “The Reverend” Jesse Kintei and he told Jesse to put together a tape of Jordan’s best spots and in ring promos from his time combined in PCW and ACW. Jesse said he would, but he told Jordan that he had to get back into rehab for his knee before they could actually be completely ready.

That’s what Jordan did for the next few weeks. He tried to rehab his knee, and he did so successfully. He knew that at the end of his long hard road, there would be a big money contract and a new stable promotion that would back him and push him to the moon.

After training for a few weeks, Jordan felt that he was ready and told Jesse to send the tape to SE before it was too late.

Three days after the tape was sent, Jordan X Shabazz spoke with 1/3rd owner of the company, Andrew Styles over the phone. Styles said that Ray and he really liked his in ring work rate and his excellent promos, and that they would both be honoured if he would sign the contract.

Jordan laughed to himself and promised that he would. They were to meet in Los Angeles, California for the signing.

That weekend, Jordan signed his contract in SE.

Within a few weeks, the promotion was set to open, and Andrew Styles came up with the idea to have Jordan X Shabazz feud with former IOW World Champion and Action! Wrestling superstar, Maverick Manson. They felt that with the rub that Manson gave Jordan, they could propel him to superstardom. Shabazz liked the idea, and he and Maverick immediately got together to work out their angle.

They taped a segment for the show a few days in advance, it went off without a hitch. Both men looked strong, and Jordan was excited.

The night of the show, Jordan only appeared one other time, when he was arriving to the arena with his manager, “The Reverend” Jesse Kintei.

As soon as the live show was over, Jordan X Shabazz requested a tape of the show, which he received.

He watched the tape, and thought about how great he and Maverick had looked in it.

As Shabazz left the San Diego Sports Arena that night, he was stopped at the entrance by Andrew Styles.

“Have you heard the news, Jordan?”

“What news, Andy?”

“Maverick Manson quit.”

Jordan felt like he had been punched in the gut. In his segment with Maverick Manson, Maverick had slugged him in the face, inducing blood. Then the road agents broke it up. He was supposed to get his revenge the following week, but now he couldn’t because Maverick Manson was gone. He was going to look extremely weak on TV, and he didn’t like that.

But there was nothing he could do… or so he thought.
