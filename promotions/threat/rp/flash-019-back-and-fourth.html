Back and fourth
Starring: 'The Dreamer' Darren Anthony

"It's a beautiful Day."

The harsh, haggard voice streams into the audio, the sarcasm dripping from the unidentified voice.

Poof - - Fade up.

The scene is not what we'd expect, not by a long shot. We fade up alright, onto the overview of a playground, obviously being viewed from an evevated posistion. The set up is usual - - A swing set sitting in the center surrounded by assorted contructions design to please the children, who look pleased.

To the left of the Swing set sits a long stretch of monkeybars, a small boy stretching from bar to bar. To the right sits an steel dome of bars, which is being scaled by numerious children. Every child on the playground wears a an expression of happiness, and content, they have not a care in the world. They spend there days playing, living, and eating PB&J sandwitches, nothing more - - nothing less.

But we're confused, because it is in fact not a beautiful day, as the camera pulls back, revealing the skyline to us. Black clouds make there way across the blue and white sky, consuming ever ray of light in there path, surely promising a storm, if not something worse.

The clouds eat away at the sky, ravaging what was once a nice, cool day. Flashes of light blast through the dark masses, hinting at lightening, which predictably shoots through the clouds, revealing itselfs to us. Thunder follows, of course, and the wins begin to pick up. It's definitly not a beautiful day.

So why the deception? Why the obviously false statement?

"I had a dream last night."

Once again, the voice blasts in over the audio, but we still have yet to see the man himself, but we know who it is. We know who's pulling us in, who's drawing our attention. We know exactly who the voice belongs to - - meanwhile young mothers rush toward the play ground, pulling there crying children into sheltor for there is a storm coming, but they don't want to go. They want to play, they want to have fun, they want to live.

Flash.

And we shoot back two weeks, two long fucking weeks, just after Flawed 14. There he is, bent over the dirty, disgusting sink. Which sits in the dark, dank locker room. Oh, what a good, thick description, right? So he sits, hunched over, hacking up blood. "You're pathetic. You're a fucking waste Dee .. Blood? Some little splash and your hacking up blood?"

Ric Graves pulls himself from the shawdows, Newport in mouth, zippo in hand. *Stich* He pulls to light his cancear stick, the chrome lighter reflecting a distorted image of a beaten and worn body. And the owner of the body speaks. "I didn't ask to be here ....*cough* I didn't want to fucking be here!"

Graves Shrugged. "They exactly why are you here, Dee?"

Flash

Now back to Present day, now back to that voice.

The camera pulls back further, revealing the broken down balcony, the railing rusted through, the conrete filthy. We pull back even further, all the way back, an estimated twenty feet. Ah, now we've got a scene.

A barely functional hotel room. No king size bed, paintings of lavish furniture. No hand carved chairs, desks, and assorted items. Over the years he's moved on - - he doesn't need penthouse, all he needs is what he's got - - Light grey walls running down into the dark red carpeting, a Queen sized bed, and a worn and torn dresser. No TV.

And predictably, on the balcony past the filthy sliding glass door stands Darren Anthony, leaning across the earlier mentioned railing, peering out into the sky. His bare back is all thats visible, aside from his plaid boxers and long dark hair, which falls over his broad shoudlers. He's just standing there. Watching. Waiting. Thinking. He speaks.

"Thats right, I had a dream last night. No, it wasn't the kind of dream your thinking. It wasn't one of those dreams you know is not real. It wasn't one of those dreams walking that thin line between fantasy and reality, you know the ones ... where your running through the jungle, or some other equally shocking setting, fighting to save the word. No, it wasn't one of those dreams - - I wasn't falling, either."

That's right, that's Darren Anthony.

He still doesn't turn around, his back toward us, his breathing is so heavy that its clearly heard. Tattoo's cover his skin, as if a convas. Crosses, words, the face of christ. It's all there.

"It was one of those dreams that you don't expect. It was one of those freaky dreams, the ones you never forget. It was one of those dreams that when you wake up ... you'd swear it was real. It was one of those dreams that leaves in a cold sweat, wide awake, thinking about life. It wasn't even a dream really, more like a vision ... a wake up call."

Darren drops his head, looking down now, peering across the playground we just left.

"In this dream, the world was ending. In this dream, the fucking apocalypse was upon us - - and I was just standing there, right infront of [he lifts his arm from the railing, pointing in the direction of the playground] this very playground, watching the children play, and then it happened. In the distance, I could see the mushroom like cloud lifting into the air through the flashes of light as it begun ... as the world came to an fucking end. "

His tone is mild, smooth. He's serious though, very serious.

"Fire and light rushed across the earth, lifting buildings from there foundation, and ripping them to shreds ... decentigrating before my eyes. And the children? There mothers rushed to them, running toward them - - and me - - there arms open, tears pourning down there faces."

A pause, a short pause.

"But alas, they couldn't make it. The destructive fire rushed from behind, ripping the childrens mothers apart right before there eyes, and seconds later them. And All I could do was stand there and watch .... watch the flesh melt from there bones. I had to sit there and look into the eyes of those women - - I saw the pain and suffering in there eye's, the anguish, the regret."

Flash again, right back to two weeks ago. That same locker room, that same sink, and that same puddle of Blood. Darren drops back, a redlined pressed across his chest from leaning against the edge of the sink. He sniffed, wiping his face as he did, smearing blood across it.

Ric took another drag. "Inquirying minds would like to know, Dee ... Like me, because surely I'm wasting my time if you don't want to be here." He scoffed, turning away from the so called legend, the would be champion.

Darren's dark hair fell over his face, his eyes piercing right through the strands. ".....time....."

Flash

And back to present day. Darren finally turns, looking into the camera. His Emerald green eyes reassure us, they calm us.

"Those are the kind of dreams that make you wonder, those are the kind of dreams that make you question your purpose in life. If I died tommorrow, would I be content? If I died tommorrow, would I die knowing that I did everything I wanted to do - - would I know that my life wasn't a waste?"

The words linger on his soft, cream colored lips.

"... and at the end of this dream, when the carnage finally dissapeared from my mind, one image filled my sight - for what seemed like an enternity, or atleast until I woke up. It was nØt. [a pause] It was nØt, and every unmistakeable feature. His cold eyes, cruel lips, and sinister grin - - it was all there. He didn't say anything, I didn't say anything, we just stood face to face."

Darren walks from the balcony into the hotel room, his steps slow and soft, his tone cold and harsh.

"So I suppose I should ask him. [staring straight into the camera] Would you be content, Wolfgang? If you died tommorrow would you content, would you be happy? Would you die knowing your life wasn't a waste, would you know that you did everything you could possibly do? I don't think so. Yeah, I watched your attack again before I went to sleep, I watched your fucking attack before I had that dream, and I can't believe a thing I saw. Not one fucking thing."

His pauses, but only for a few seconds, continuing in the same harsh tone.

"But by your accounts, if you died tommorrow, you'd be content, right? You'd be at peice, completely satisfied with the life - - the carreer - - you lived through, and you'd die without a regret. If you never had to face me again, you wouldn't care, because your so sure of yourself, your so confident. I'm just another fucked up hack taking up air time, right? I'm just another Joe Schmoe thats be pushing into the spotlight because you need another lamb to slaughter - - nØt needs another helpless soul to infect. "

He sneer's, his eye's narrowing slightly, his lips curling.

And again, Flash, boom. Were back in that dark lockeroom.

"Time? Time?" Ric pulled his newport hard. "Time for what, Dee? Time for the end of your fucking career, because that's what this is beginning to look like." Ric sneered. "Who the fuck is Resch Royal, Dee? Becuase I don't fucking know."

Darren snapped his head back, whipping his head toward the back of his head. His emerald green eyes now perfectly clear. Rage, anger, hate. It was all visible in the stare he directed toward Graves, his voice now louder. "You came and found me Graves!" Darren turned his head and spit. "I didn't search out someone who didn't want to be found, Ric!"

Graves Stacy Adams shoes clicked on the hard concrete as he pressed out the leftovers of his newport. "If you didn't want to be found .... I wouldn't have found you."

He was right.

Flash

And agian present day, and Darren still standing there sneering, his lips still curled. And he goes on, as if never inturrupted.

"And just who the fuck do you think you are, Mr. Churchhill? You just come out and attack me, attack The Dreamer, and that's it? It's just ok, because im nobody because you've never heard of me, right Wolfgang? Or is it because of the money? You're just so damned rich that you don't mind a few thousand in docter bills."

A snicker.

"By all means, Wolfgang, brag and boast to your little hearts content, because your not phasing me one bit. Not one f---ing bit. Talk about your money, your clothes, and how you sucked some shmoe out of his company to make yourself some cash. And keep playing that Game nØt, keep putting up some front, and keep acting as if everything's hunky dory....

...but I know it's not, Churchill. I know you're not content. You're not fucking happy. Becuase if you were, you would have never come out from behind that curtain. If you could die tomorrow happy, you'd still be pissing yourself backstage watching The Dreamer do his job, and wishing you could be him."

He continues circling the room, the camera following him in the endless cycle. And he's in his boxers, for god's sake.

"And if you think you're just going to waltz out of the back, lay into The Dreamer, and get no response you're kidding yourself. It's like smoking crack and trying to sleep, it's just NOT fucking happening. So why'd you come out there Churchill? Why'd you stick your good-for-nothing nose into my business? Do I impress you nØt, do I intimidate you? Do you think you fucking know me?

He's really pissed now.

[nodding]"Thats right, nØt, you don't know me. And as much as you want to know me, you never will. You'll never know Darren Anthony, you'll never know The Dreamer, because in the back of your mindyou really don't want to. As long as I'm just another dron, just another challenger, you'll be satisfied. You'll be calm, cool, and collected as long as you believe in your heart that I'm nothing."

Darren's eye's are burning with anger, and his tone hardens.

"Well f--- you Wolfgang. F--- you and everything you stand for, because I'm not buying your brand. Your not going to get in my head, Wolf, your not going to sike me out. Do you have any idea where I've been and what I've done, Wolf? No, you don't, nor do I think you could possible comprehend it. I've been in this damn business since I was eighteen. That's eleven long pain-driven glory filled years that I've been running around dropping people in there heads, that's eleven years that I've been shattering dreams and living up to the hype."

Darren pauses, running his hands through his hair slowly. His eye's remain thin, his nostrils flairing. His dameanor changed quickly, as his his expression, now wearing a look of disgust.

Flash, and we're back to two weeks ago, and things are heating up.

Graves scoffed, disgust across his face. "Don't play these fucking mind games with me Darren. If you were just going to let a pair of federation hacks run you over, you would have never followed me back into this shit! I know you wanted a second chance...."

He paused, realizing the direction of his words, but it was too late now. Darren snapped back."And what the fuck is that supposed to mean, Graves?" Darren motioned with his hands. "The old washed up Dee call's on the mighty Ric Graves for a ... dare I say it ... second chance?" He sneered. "Don't fucking flatter yourself, you piece of shit."

Graves was on his guard now, Darren didn't even move. "Don't do this Dee!" He pointed at the scared chest of Darren. "You came back by your own free will, I didn't beg you, I didn't ask the great and powerful Darren Anthony to bring fourth the apocalypse." He sneered. "Yeah, I can be sarcastic too."

Darren's eyes were on fire now. "YOU WEREN'T OUR THERE GRAVES! Your best friend didn't jig you right in the back with a knife for gods sakes!" He turned away, throwing his arms up. "You didn't see the hate in there eye's you motherfu--"

"Your still talking about that night...." Graves words silenced Darren, who just stared at him, his long dark hair over most of his face. "So what if they ruin your career, or better yet, your life. Fuck that. Look where you're at now, Darren." Graves looked around. "This is it ... your chance. I know you Dee, you're better than this."

Flash

And where back to reality once again. Darren's eyes relax, his breathing has slowed, hes calm now.

[Relaxed]"I've held the BJWC World Heavy weight title two times. I beat Brandon Lloyd for that title. Do you have any idea who he is? Do you have any idea the kind of men I've faced over there in Minnasota? I ran through the likes of Dante Calavaria and Goliath for the BJWC Hardcore title. I crushed dreams for the North American strap. And all of this in two years time. And I know it doesnt matter to you .... but those guy's that I beat in that federation that-doesnt-matter were real fighters. Not like the pussies we've go infecting this business today. And I handed each and everyone of them there own ass."

He's bragging, how cliche.

"I'm ten times what you are, or will ever be in this business, you no-good hack. So when you came out to that ring, and pissed all over me, you crossed the line .... but you already knew that. But what you didn't know is how I'd react. And let me tell you something .... you NOT going to like the way I react, because when you mess with me you're messing with something you'll never be ... "

[Hissing]"Believe that."


And we're gone.
