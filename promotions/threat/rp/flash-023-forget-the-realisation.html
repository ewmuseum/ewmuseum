Forget the Realisation
Starring: Dane Manetta

The first trial went okay… but in the end it came to nothing. Dane wanted to be a wrestler badly and following in his forefather’s footsteps, but this time it wasn’t to be.


The second trial was something completely different… it went perfectly and Dane found himself with a contract on the table in front of him. Maria was sat in the seat next to him; god knows what was going through her mind. Dane looked at the contract not seeming too eager to take the pen and scribble his name at the bottom.


Was this his realisation?


Was this not the path he really wanted to take?


He turned and looked at Maria who reluctantly nodded her head.


Dane was about to sign away his life for something that could lead to what he wanted to do… but all in all, there was a high risk that it would get him nowhere near becoming a professional wrestler.


Forget the realisation.


He took the pen in his hand, flipped over the page and signed the paper. The man sat opposite him was delighted and gleamed with happiness at signing Dane Manetta.


Would you think Dane would be happy? He just had his second trial and was immediately offered a contract. Perhaps Dane would have been overjoyed had the man sat opposite him not been named Gino Di Maggio.


That’s right… Dane was all set to become the Pariah of TFZ.
