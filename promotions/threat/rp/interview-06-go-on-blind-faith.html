Go On Blind Faith.
Starring: Riot

"Something wasn't right. I could feel it nipping at the tip of my spine. Slowly waiting to rise to the surface and make it's full intentions know. It was an evil the likes of which I had never felt before. And that goes without saying."

A door opened and a lone man walked into the shot, the door slowly coming to a quiet close behind him. He turned on a TV and walked across the small room.

"These days, I look around and all I see is unwelcome and unwanted change. My future --a future that was once so bright-- is now a world so cold and dead. Sadly, nothing ever works the way you want it to. Even the best laid plans can be destroyed without a moments notice. I had so much to look forward to. A future I could be proud of. A future that I wanted. And now it's gone. Not so much gone as it was non-existent. My dreams were filed away under "failures," much like anything else that I tried to do. My life was a sad joke and each day that I woke was just another cruel punch line."

He sat, slouched over on the corner of his sofa bed as his television set illuminated his small apartment room. It had to be two or three in the morning and he was fully dressed. He looked as though he was either getting ready to leave or had just walked in the door from some get-together. Regardless, he sat there, twiddling his thumbs and lost, deep in though.

"I wanted something different for myself. Piles of money. Fast cars. Big houses. Women. I didn't care how I got it, I just wanted it all and I wanted it quickly. I sold my soul for something that I thought would give me what I wanted and as fast as I wanted it. I jumped head first into that game some call life. If were given another chance, I'd take it all back without ever giving it a second thought. It's a shame the Devil never works the way you want him to."

He had finally become used to this new, played-down lifestyle. Aside from his occasional spot on a local wrestling card, he had no consistent job. He had to find a means to support himself, less he would be back out on the streets, living out of the back of his car like when he first started in the business. So he did the best he could, which happened to be bagging groceries for the Food Lion two towns over. The pay wasn't much, but it kept him alive.

"I've seen more in one year's time than any other, normal person would probably see in their lifetime. I hate it. This isn't what I signed on for. I was promised fame and wealth beyond my wildest dreams and all I got was bad luck and misery. It's like I've been cursed for something that I never even did. But, hey, that's the business. Welcome to it."

He looked up at his run down abode. It wasn't much, but it was all he had. As much of a shit hole it might be, he loved it for what it was... home. It was his home. No one could take that away from him. Less he didn't pay rent.

"I hate professional wrestling. I hate it with a passion. It took away my life and my family. Anyone and everyone that I ever loved. It sugarcoated the truth and only told half a story. Never once did it make mention of the hell one went through to "train" for this business. Or how everyone had to "pay their dues" regardless of how greatly talented they might be. It never even slightly made sight of the nights were you made fifty bucks and spent twice that amount just to get to the show to begin with. Wrestling is a bastard. I hope it burns in hell."

He glanced over some old framed photos on his wall. Most were hung to hide cracks and chipped paint, others were out of pride... or spite, it was never really clear. Some of the frames had broken glass, the product of restless nights when he would think back to his "glory days." Days that had come and gone, but still remembered by a small few that really cared.

"Finally, I had enough. I was ready to give in. I had all but given up on the business, anyway. I was in a downward spiral and read to hit the bottom. Wrestling had seemingly ruined my life for the last time."

He glanced over at his answering machine. It was seldom used due to everyone's general lack of interest in him, the screen was flashing, an unplayed message waiting to be heard. He stood, walked over, and pressed play.

"Then one day, just out of the blue, John Hackett came calling.

He offered a second chance. New hopes and bigger dreams.

And I gave in to my demons.

I'm the lamb that's being led to slaughter."
