Past and Present [...and Into Future]
Starring: The Cursed One? Maybe not

An unyielding wind embraced the sky, stepping through each and every snowflake drifting from the heavens and tossing the particles of life around like the seeds of dandelions. He sat on the rooftop, trying to listen to the blissful falling of the snow in his ear, waiting for the early dawn to split apart the night. His eyes, all time, watching the mounds of snow gather in the streets below.

And imagined that he was an angel from above, sent to the planet Earth to watch over those wretched souls with invisible eyes never seen until needed.

The past was buried in five feet of snow. And the present was waking up, drowsy and moody. If you closed your eyes, you could feel it collecting coldly in the palm of your hand.

After that night upon the rooftop, his mind wandered through the warm zones of his memories, heating his bones upon the cozy fires burning within. By those welcoming flames, he traveled, trying to comprehend what little bit of understanding of what had just happened. After hours of sitting alone on the rooftop, the night building stronger, all he could feel was a sense of satisfaction and contentment. Each minute of the passing night as important and as irrelevant as the next.

The moon, serenely shining from the invisible gray sky, remained hidden and unforeseen by clouds drifting along the sky. For a moment, as his eyes looked up to the sky and his eyelashes collected small flakes, he wished that he could see and understand the light of the moon, but, for that night, the moon would never come out for him.

Maybe he could see it again on some other evening in the future.

-----

That was all a few years ago. Time had passed by, slowly but surely, and now he found himself standing in the same spot he had been on those many years ago in the middle of Chicago. His eyes scanned the water as he stood beside that same lake, waiting impatiently for the sun to rise over the horizon. It would be a magnificent site to see, but just another he could add to his pile of magnificent memories.

As minute waves slapped the shoreline softly, glimmering in the early August dawn, his mind wandered through the corridors of his vindictive mind. His eyes gazed, but his mind didn’t see the awe inspiring image clearly, as the thoughts of a menacing future bled into his brain. Very few things could distract him from such a sight as this, but the upcoming event to take place in a matter of days was doing just that. The day he had been waiting to live for quite awhile, but of which he never realized.

He was confused, and at the same time open-minded and ready to face the impending future. He figured before that he didn’t care about such things as a wrestling title and its tournament, but now that his one chance was just around the corner, he knew that he did. After all that time of not realizing his true feelings, he didn’t know if there was anytime left for him to turn it all around. For him to have another chance. For him to open his eyes and desperately try to steal the future.

Then, in the distance of the clear sky, a glint of orange sprang forth from the blue and reached over the edge of the world. His once invisible eyes could now see clearly, and his mind could realize just what he needed to know. It may have seemed hopeless, that night was there to stay and it was too late to turn around. But just when you think its over, the sun returns. A second chance is upon us, and we are given the opportunity to open and see what we have been given.

Before long, he found himself staring up at the sunrise, slowly coming to life like a newborn. He could see the water of the crystal clear lake now, beautiful and silently brushing the shore. The fears he carried before that morning were now buried under a new day’s warmth, like fires burning in the middle of a winter storm. As he threw his hands in his pockets, he could feel two pictures, resting comfortably, and the pages of a novel without a cover, waiting to be deciphered, all within his trench coat.

In front of him stood a grand spectacle of life, one that people around him probably saw everyday but never took the time to savor. He could turn around and see the life of the windy city, but he would much rather stare at this site and remember what had happened to him, what was happening to him, and what would happen to him soon enough. The mystery of life, he figured as he dreamt consciously.

For hours upon hours, with nothing but his memories and his few material possessions to keep him company, he stood on the shoreline, watching closely as the sun climbed the sky, fearless and unstoppable. In a few days, he would be ready. Ready to take on his enemies for pride and glory, but also to take on himself and the fears he held for so long. But for now, all he needed was to feel the warmth of this summer day, while remembering the cold of that winter night.

That was all that matter right now – the past and the present.

Little did he know what the future had in store for him.
