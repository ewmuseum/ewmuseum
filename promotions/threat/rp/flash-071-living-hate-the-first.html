living HATE; the first
Starring: haTE

Wrestling. It was something that he used to do to pass the time. Somehow, it became a main stay in his life. He used it to make money, to get him an image and respect.

Probably the most important, he used it to have fun, and get away from the horrible life of poverty that he used to live in Japan. It worked sometimes.

Then he would sit down and think about looking at all of the fans. The Japanese fans, packed in the arena, screaming their heads off whenever a fucking C4 would explode and almost take a man's leg off. Or whenever someone was gashed open and blood spilled from the man's head.

They would jump up, and cheer for more.

That's when the thoughts of the life he led rushed back into his head. That killed him, thinking of his family. They barely lived. His siblings cried. He knew what it COULD be like. He lived in the States. Why couldn't it be like that here?

-----------

He was still just a boy when he started wrestling, but he had seen things that even men shouldn't need to see. He had lived on the streets more than once, and even the little shelter that he could provide, with the help of his parents, it wasn't much, just a roof over his head.

"It'll are turn out soon, Mia," he would speak to his sister in Japanese. She was the youngest, and the one that he would worry about the most. She wasn't old enough to do anything herself, and the fears of what could happen to her by herself racked his brain.

"This- this horrible life!" he would say, in his broken English. His siblings would just stare at him with the look of curiosity in their eyes. Then, he would go off to work.

Now, the fact that he was a very talent wrestler hardly crossed the minds of anyone in his family. Where he wrestled, though, it didn't matter if you were a Japanese Ric Flair, the more blood you spilled into the company, the more popular you became.

He had no problem doing just that.

Banged and bruised up beyond belief most of the time, he found it hard to get up in the morning, let alone go in and do another hard days work of wrestling, just for the blood hungry fans, but with the mix of family… and even his own adrenaline, he kept pushing himself.

Maybe too far. Now, people were starting to look at him. He was starting to turn heads in the Sates now. His death defying stunts and no hesitation when he did it blew scouts minds. One by one, people started to notice. That is when he started to get calls. From some State's Indy leagues. For people in the US, the pay for wrestling in the Indies was really nothing... just something you could get by on. To him-

It was everything.

But he could not leave his family again.

The first scout called:
Broken Japanese was all you could hear.

"I speak some English," he told the scout. He was getting annoyed already, because the mans Japanese was almost embarrassing to his people.

"Oh, good. I'm calling from the IFC. It’s a wrestling promotion in the United States. I saw you wrestle this evening, I have to tell you, you're good."

He nodded; this wasn't the first time he had heard that, he just had never heard it from someone that actually knew anything about it. Hearing the silence, the man continued.

"I'm calling to offer you a job, all you would have to do is move over to the United States and live in Utah. Your trip and apartment would be paid for, call it a gift of the company."

"Is that right?" he paused for a moment. "Here is what you can do with your job in the 'United States'," he said it very sarcastically.

"You take it, and you shove it straight up your ass!"
