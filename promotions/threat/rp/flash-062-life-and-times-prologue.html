Life and Times - Prologue
Starring: Tyler Davidson

A great many things have happened in the life of one, Tyler Davidson.

Tyler had at one time in his short life thus far sat in the lap of luxury. He lived the life of a millionaire with his fiancée Heather McDonald thanks to the riches that were made by both his parents in their careers as New York City based lawyers. Even Tyler himself had put some time into studying to be a lawyer until the profession that he truly loved came calling for him and he stepped into the squared circle for the first time under the tutelage of the man known as Daniel [name withheld]. A man who in recent years retired from the ring, due to a stipulation in a match he lost to Tyler, and has since run two very successful promotions.

For all the good things that has happened to Tyler in his life however there have been many pitfalls and quite a few tragedies. His brother had died in one of the worst tragedies to ever strike the United States of America while he was working as a firefighter in New York City. His mother had died several years earlier at a rather young age as a result of cancer. And had that not been enough his fiancée Heather was murdered by a man who was in actuality an employee of Tyler’s Davidson Enterprises.

The horror of the entire situation had brought Tyler’s entire life crashing down around him and sent him spiraling into an endless vortex of depression that Tyler didn’t seem likely to come out of. All of it had happened right when things were looking up the most for Tyler in his life and career. He was just days away from his huge ladder match against Mr. Showtime for the $W Global Championship. He was focused only on making the most of the given opportunity and because of that he wasn’t there for Heather when she needed him the most.

For months Tyler had simply vanished from the sight and minds of wrestling fans everywhere with those who did remember about him wondered if he would ever return again and make it back to the level of greatness that he had once achieved. Much to the chagrin of wrestling fans everywhere however Tyler Davidson did indeed return to his old stomping grounds in grand fashion and made an impression that would remain with the people forever when he turned his back on the fans and placed the blame for his fiancée’s death squarely on the shoulders of wrestling fans around the world.

From that point on however Tyler’s career seemed to have stalled in a very bad fashion. He was vindictive and vicious in the ring and the fans didn’t take any time in turning on the former highly popular star. Tyler didn’t care at all though but apparently the promoters did. He had never again achieved the high level of success that he had achieved in $W even when he jumped ship to FSW for a highly lucrative contract which quickly fluttered out almost upon his arrival in a game of constant politics throughout the promotion.

From there it was IOW where Tyler had finally it seemed begun to regain a little bit of his former self. It seemed for months like he had come to his senses and “forgiven” the fans for what he had blamed them for and began once again to do what he can to satisfy them and fight the good fight. It was a fight that had eventually earned him the new nickname of The Career Assassin as he ended the careers of both Impact and his former tag team partner De’Andre Walker. IOW came to an end though and Tyler made sure it ended on a very different note than it started. He again turned his back on the fans and it catapulted him into further success this time as he defeated Evan Maxim and captured the IOW Extreme Title. Tyler’s first championship in over a year.

That brings us now to the present day and Tyler’s existence in thReat where he has tried to pick up where he left off in IOW. With his IOW Extreme Title in his grasps at all times Tyler has made it known to everyone that he is the future of the promotion and will not stand for anyone to get in his way any longer as he fulfills his destiny.

The life and times of Tyler Davidson has been quite a roller coaster ride so far and the ride it seems will be continuing for quite a long time.
