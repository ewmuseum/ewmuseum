History [3]
Starring: Chris 'Neverwhere' McCluskey

So I may have made some anti-American comment or two during my short time here in thReat. So what? I speak from the heart; I speak the truth.

Just because certain people, namely the fans who I direct my “negativity” towards, don’t like what I have to say doesn’t mean I’m a bad guy.

The days of the blonde haired, steroid enhanced, baby kissing do-gooder are long gone. I’m not saying I was one of those, but being nice to people isn’t going to get you anywhere in today’s world. Why would it? Being a nice guy doesn’t give you an advantage. In fact, it makes you weak.

Seriously, what good does the fans’ cheers, chants and encouragement really do? Make you impervious to pain? Give you the strength of ten men? It fills you with false hope, that’s what it does. It makes you feel as if you’re invincible. Then you go and get your ass handed to you because you thought you were God’s gift to wrestling.

Compassion versus hardness. Gentlemen versus cavemen. There really isn’t a contest. To be a success, you’ve can’t be soft. You’ve got to do all you can to get ahead. Professional wrestling isn’t a fair game. You snooze and you lose. Even if that means that you’ve got to do things that people don’t appreciate or find offensive.

Maybe I “turned” on the crowd because I wanted them to turn on me. There’s already enough pressure to go out and win your match each night, without having the pressure of the fans wanting you to be their “hero” on your shoulders. Not that there are any real heroes anymore, though.

There’s one thing that I will never lose, and that’s my integrity. As long as I wrestle in this company, and in this business, I won’t do anything that will degrade myself in my eyes. Because when you fail yourself, then you really are a failure.

That said, there’s nothing I won’t do to win the International championship. Well, within reason of course. To be honest, I know very little about my opponent in the quarterfinals. I was never one for homework when I was at school, and the same will apply this weekend. Just like the other seven men in the International championship tournament, Christian Elijah wants to win it. Dream on, dreamer.

Dream on.
