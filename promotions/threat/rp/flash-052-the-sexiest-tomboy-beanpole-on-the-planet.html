The Sexiest Tomboy Beanpole On The Planet.

The Sexiest Tomboy Beanpole On The Planet.
Starring: Generative Clash

Zoe Antoinette is the sexiest tomboy beanpole on the planet.

Sez who?

Sez... Zoe Antoinette.

And it is Zoe whom stands in front of the mirror at this very moment, checking out her features. She was once a gymnast, once a ballerina... but now, there are bruises and scars along points in her body -- points that she had always wanted to keep young, tender, and smooth.

Her eyes, too, have aged. She may only be drinking age. But inside, her body feels and senses things that are far beyond that simple landmark in life.

To forget, Zoe reaches over to a small boombox -- one of the new-age ones, shaped as though an ostritch had a retarded egg in it's nesting -- and lets some music take her away from the depths of subconscious pain and conscious turmoil. She falls back on the bed sheets -- not caring about the discolored stains that have slowly overtaken the hotel room -- and runs her hands against the aged satin. The smell of the room is that of a recently vacuumed shithouse.

But it's closer to home than the empty arena is. Not that she the whole idea of travelling, city to city, to make her way through life with some cash in her pocket and a purpose to making it to the next day. It would just be that...

... things can get boring.

During these thoughts, Stephen Martin enters the hotel room. Zoe had barely heard the buzzing sound as the hotel keycard was recognized by the "advanced" locking system the man at the front desk had mentioned. She could remember exactly what he had said. "We have the most advanced security system in the city. It won't let you down."

All it took was a few hole punches in the right places on an index card, and you could get into these hotel rooms. Zoe knew that because she saw a maid with a stack of holed-up index cards.

Go figure.

"How was shopping?" Zoe asked.

"Not too bad. They were wondering about the permanent markers, though." Stephen turned over the plastic bag he had carried into the room. Three or four bundles of black permanent markers spilled onto the bed next to Zoe.

"I don't know where you thought of this, Zoe... and I'm pretty sure this is illegal." Stephen remarked.

"Shoosh. C'mon, have some fun in your life, Steve."

Zoe opened one of the bundles, took a marker out of it, and uncapped the thing. She turned on her stomach, and -- with her tongue sticking oh-so-far out of the right side of her mouth, poking between two warm-flesh brown lips like the pink bud of a flower, she began to scrawl out the semen stains on her bedsheet. They wouldn't be sleeping here tonight -- they had spent the night before here, honestly, so they would have to move soon -- but you have to leave your mark somehow.

Because, if you don't leave a mark of some kind -- how do you even bother with the idea of living?

Zoe supposed this to be the reason she had listened to Stephen in the first place. Between this and teaching English, she had chosen this. And she wasn't totally sure how big a mark she would leave as an English teacher.

And besides, this paid better -- even despite the bruises, scars, and that damnation that was a sense of loneliness.

You had to look on the bright side.

Zoe finished up her bedsheet. Less "cumstains" here than in most hotel rooms, she noticed. She slipped off the bed, and scribbled a signature.

"The sexiest tomboy beanpole on the planet."

Across from her, Stephen was sketching a little scene on the wall -- of what, Zoe didn't know and didn't care. It might have been two stick figures performing sodomy. It might have been a tree being planted by a plain girl.

Whatever.

They did what they wanted. Otherwise, there would be no generating of a clash.

And the name would be a waste, wouldn't it?

And you can't waste a name like Generative Clash.

Or an award like "sexiest tomboy beanpole on the planet".

Zoe smiled at that.

She smiled at a lot of things.

She was smiling also... because they had a steady job now.

And a big moment to come soon. Pay-Per-View. Some amount of people would watch them on television.

So maybe she could buy something nice for herself, for once. Or maybe she could pay off her parent's house.

Or maybe... well, no. That was something that would cost more than money to deal with.

Zoe got up, and walked over to Stephen. She looked, peering over his shoulder, and saw what he was drawing.

She watched him draw for another half an hour, then left for the hotel's "gym" -- a Stairmaster and a few free weights -- while Stephen kept on drawing.

He only had a spare hour to work on this.

Then they would have to attend Malevolence.

And be involved in a major way.

So says the clock... and Stephen knows, the clock always wins.

And it will never give up.

But there's still an hour. And damn the man, he will not stop until that hour is up.

So things go.

So how life goes.

Time, and rebellion. Two credos.

The rules of Generative Clash.
