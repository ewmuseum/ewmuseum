Acceptance? Or the beginnings of Rejection? [2]
Starring: Wolf & Rachel

While Wolf was a strong person, no one in this world was truly strong by his or her self, and a combination of inner strength and close support creates each person’s might. Moreover, every wolf runs in a pack, so to speak. This wolf? He had his family willing to support him in any way he could possibly ask, and while he had turned down any more financial aid, the fact that the offer would still be made if need be was a subconscious comfort. However, his real inner strength came from without, from that one person that everyone hopes to find in their life, it just happened that Wolf had found her working for his father’s company. Rachel. She was everything Wolf could ask for in a person, best friend, and fiancée; and for the moment it seemed, she was just as happy as Wolf was with his job at thReat wrestling.

Rachel: Oh my god! Wolf! You’re on the card Wolf, have you read this?!”

Oh, man. I’d been so excited with finding out I was on the first thReat card I’d forgotten to show it to Rachel. Ah well, she didn’t seem to be upset about it.

“Yeah, I know, It’s great! I couldn’t ask for anything else.”

As I walked into the bedroom, Rachel ran over and jumped on me hugging me, as I fell back onto the bed.

Rachel (giggling): You’re gonna have to be a bit tougher than that, Wolfman!

I laughed as I rolled her over, and we play fought before I kissed her. She was everything to me, and the only reason we weren’t married were financial ones. Her family had to use all their savings on restoring their house after it burnt down last year, and I had moved out of my house. While I was on great terms with my parents, I was on a mission to prove that I could survive in this world without them, and that meant without them forking out cash to pay for the wedding. So, for now we have deal with just engagement, with the wedding date up in the air until I can make enough money to pay for it. That’s where thReat came in. Rachel had always been supportive of everything I did, but she was cautious when it came to wrestling. It seemed like every other day you would hear of wrestlers receiving injuries, and she couldn’t even bear to think of me lying in some hospital bed. Partially because of the pain, I would be in, but I think the main reason was she hated feeling helpless, and there’s no greater feeling of helplessness than when a loved on is in pain and you can do nothing to help.

Rachel: Whatcha thinking about?

I hadn’t noticed that I’d drifted off into thought.

“The future. Our future. I love you Rachel, and I know that that fire was nobody‘s fault, but sometimes I just feel like I can’t wait any longer to get married.”

Rachel smiled, as she looked right through my eyes into my soul.

Rachel: There’s no rush… unless you’re going somewhere…

I smiled, but it was one of those moments where I needed a serious answer, not dismissal with humour.

Rachel: I don’t mean to just joke about, but you know I feel the same as you do, but you have to understand, I’ve dreamt of that day from when I was little girl. This is the biggest day of our lives, and the start of our life together… and I just want everything to be perfect….

She turned away, looking awkward after exposing her feelings to me. She was always like that, never willing to give away any vulnerability, but I knew her too well now. I always knew what she was feeling, whether she wanted me to or not, and right now she was scared, masking it with awkwardness.

“I know Rach, I know, and it will be I promise. I don’t mean to be so impatient, but ya know. I’ve known you were the one from the first day I met you, and know it’s just a matter of sharing that with friends and family. I’ve made it in the business world, I’ve just made it into the world of wrestling, and you are the final, and most important piece of the puzzle. It all means nothing without someone to share it with.”

Rachel got up and hugged me. Not just the hug when you greet family and such, but an embrace full of emotion, one that showed the bond we had was true. She suddenly let go, her face almost lighting the room she looked so excited.

Rachel: I just can’t believe you’re going to be on the first show Wolf, this is great!

“I know!”

As Wolf and Rachel embraced there was one small thing niggling at the back of Wolf’s mind. Although the two were similar, they were always there to ground the other one. When Rachel was excited, Wolf remained grounded, and Rachel did the opposite, it was like a defence mechanism they had developed, to protect each other from disappointment. As the two hugged so jovially, Wolf couldn’t help but feel strangely vulnerable. He pushed it the back of his mind for now, but he knew he’d have to confront it at some point. Some point soon.
