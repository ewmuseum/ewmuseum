Who let Phil near a PC?
Starring: Phil Atken

Ladies and Gentlemen, wait, what hell am I talking about, you lot are thReat fans, how the hell can you be called Ladies and Gentlemen. Let's re-start shall we.

Hicks and Shemales, it is my duty to inform you that starting from today you shall be seeing a new era for both thReat and Phil Atken. You see the idiots that run this place noticed that they had a left over title they hadn't awarded yet and lord knows we don't have enough titles kicking around this place already. Especially not after last night, no siree bob, Troy Mason won't be satisfied till everyone in this place has a damn belt around their waists but me. Damn arsehole wouldn't know true talent if it smacked him in the face, which it's planning to do very soon if he doesn't brighten up his damn ideas.

Not only that but the shit, arse, cunt, feck (T-Shirts coming soon folks, there will be a range of four, with a family pack containing all four. You know you want to pre-order them, oh yes you do) Mason is added me to this 16-Man, whatever the feck it is for this damn "Interdumbass" title. I mean, what the hell is he thinking? Is this his way of saying sorry so him and Sole screwing me out of the title? Is this some sort of damn joke? I mean does he really think I'd like fighting in this tournament, straight after being forced out of my title? If this is an apology what Mason should really do is just hand over the damn title to me, or better yet hand over that world title to me. I mean it's not like David Sanders is going to bring respect to that belt. Heck, I went out before Payback and asked people their thoughts on David Sanders, the majority answered:

"What? That KFC guy?"

But hey, you know what's right for the company Mason, and its angles that revolve around your fat ass. Yeah, they REALLY bring in the ratings. I'm sure the Internet community loves your constant booking yourself into angles. Must be fun.

But that's not the point right now, the point is that I'm now stuck in this freaking 16-Man "We're not good enough to Main Event, but we're not Mid-Card!" Title Tournament. Can't help but wonder why I'm stuck in it, everyone knows that I'm the damn reason everyone watches the freaking craphole of a company. Mason your ass better believe they'll be a backlash after you screwed my arse, not just by me, but by the fans too.

Maybe I won't take part in your pathetic tournament; maybe I'll just no-show, why should I bother showing up to this crap hole after you screwed me out of my title? Do you really deserve my presence Mason?

Ah, screw it, I'll show up and embarrass your ass by defeating, who is it you drew me against? Your big fat ugly mother? Oh, no it was haTE. Seriously, that guy needs to learn the basics of the English language, that or he needs to fix his shift key. I wonder if he writes his name like that too, that'd be a pain in the arse. Not only that, but I think he pissed off our web team, I mean no one can be that big of an idiot as our web team makes him look. I'll admit I've never met Hate backstage, but I'm curious to know what member of staff he pissed off to get stuck with Special Guest Referee duty. I mean they only pull out that card when they have no other use for the idiot on the show, and with 16 matches you'd think he might of been able to be slotted in SOMEWHERE. Ho-hum, who gives a flying shit?

I'm being totally honest here; who gives a flying crap about a guy like haTE? Not the fans, not the staff and I sure as hell don't. Everyone knows that I only care about myself...and maybe my good friend Vincent Pembridge (Hi Vincey! Thanks for getting me in that title match! Give me a call some time, you never seem to be in when I phone round). To be honest here, I could sit here all day and shoot off my mouth about the patheticness that is the thReat talent, but that's not my style. My style is to shoot from the hip, be honest, and let you the fans know what the hell's going on with thReat backstage. So I will:

When the participants of the 16-Man International title match were announced, backstage after Payback, Troy Mason gathered all the hopefuls around, apart from me, oh no, he didn't want to give me the information I needed. He didn't want to show me the brackets, why? Because I do this, I tell you what's happening, and how much of an idiot Mason is. It's obvious he doesn't want me with another title around my waist, and so I guess I WILL enter into this pile of crap.

But I won't stop there, the backlash will come, and none of you will see it coming. You'd never expect that screwing around with talent would ever push a man just that little bit more over the edge would you? Well, I'm sick of being screwed around by you arseholes, you don't even have a freaking clue what the fans want. I do, and I’m going to give it to them, while giving you the proverbial finger.

Fans, enjoy Flaw, I know I sure as hell will.

Mason, you enjoy Flaw too, well…if you can.

And haTE… you’re standing in the way of greatness you little fuck, move out the way.
