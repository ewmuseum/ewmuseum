living HATE; the final
Starring: haTE

The parents. His parents were just like your parents. They worked to support there children. They worried about there safety, and stressed just like the rest of them. Only, you can take all of your parents stress, and multiply that by five hundred.

The father. Named Robu Shiga.

What did he do for a living? He worked in an office. For a living, he worked in a cubical. He didn't have to go out and get his hands dirty to support his family, at least not during the day. No, all he did during the day was type on a computer.

Night? He was the fucking JANITOR. He was the night janitor of the office building he worked at during the day. Few times did he come home at night. Sometimes he would go WEEKS without even coming out of the office.

But when he did, they were sure glad to see him.

Robu wasn't a big man. Smaller than haTE, he was only made of like 5'9", 150lbs. The low weight just because he would go so long without eating, sometimes only getting the stuff he could get out of vending machines in the office.

To support his family, he never left one building. He typed, and cleaned.

The mother. Named Sheri Shiga.

What did she do for a living? She was a fucking stripper. During the day, she did nothing, but help out the kids, like every other mother you would know of. Her nightlife was a wild one. Her looks didn't get her anywhere though. She just had to wait for some horny 'mother' fucker to come by, and she might get lucky.

By letting him get lucky.

There were many a morning where she would come home, empty handed. She stripped, at least that was her story. He knew different, actually, all of them that were old enough, knew different. She DID strip, but it was for some horny old man in his bedroom.

To support her family, she was a fucking 2 yen whore.
