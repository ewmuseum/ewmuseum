never fill the void [2]
Starring: haTE

Japan.

He'd arrived back in Japan. The plethera of memories came rushing back into his head. He was fresh off another International Title Tournament Win, and he was feeling pretty good. He still had never gotten back ahold of the person that called him, and he had no idea why he was here. He knew it had something to do with his father.

He was scared. He almost couldn't be happy to knock off another person in the tournament, as his family was everything to him. That was why he was doing what he was doing in thReat.

Now..

He rode, slowly, in a Japanese cab. The roads were jammed. It didn't suprise him at all. He pulled out a wad of money from his pocket, and pretty much threw it at the driver. Getting out of the non-moving vehical. He walks through a dense crowd of people.

It was always crowded.

He walked, dodging running into people. He saw children in the rodes, walking around and through the cars that are stopped. He thought back, to him as a child, doing the same in almost the same conditions. It was hot. The children were walking barefoot.

He shook his head, and went on. Japan wasn't nessisarily a poor country, but it had its weaknesses. He walked across the street himself, dodging the cars that seemingly started moving as soon as he stepped into the street.

He mummbled something Japanese under his breath and dodged on. He finally got back to the side walk, and then something across the road caught his eyes.

He ran through the traffic on the other side, and walked up to a huge hospital building. He sighed and went in.

He spoke something to the receptionist at the desk. Of course, in Japanese. She looked at a chart, and motioned with her hands as she spoke. He nodded and walked to an elevator. Pressing a floor button, the doors close without anyone else getting on. A few seconds pass of haTE looking at the floor. A pleasent "ding" is heard, and haTE steps off of the elevator, walking down the corridor.

He comes to a room marked 54B. He knocks, but does't wait for an answer before he steps in. They're is a man, laying in the hospital bed, looking very grim. He is hooked up to all of these machines, and a woman comes walking over.

The woman is not haTE's mom, but rather his aunt from the States. "He's not looking good, Derek." Derek- haTE's name in english.

"Something terrible has happend to your mother, she hasn't been heard from. As well as the rest of the kids."

"Mia?" haTE gasped.

The woman- just shook her head.

"Go to this address." She handed him a piece of paper. "Go there, and look for her, that was the area she was last seen.

Just as haTE turned, he heard a high pitched beeping noice- that was steady.

The heart monitor.

His father was dead.
