looking up
Starring: haTE

Well, give me some time here. I'm not exactly what they call 'computer literate'.

well, here I am in thReat.. still.

I got to thinking about my win over Phil Atkin last week on Sunday Night Flaw 23. Yes, I read his little report that he did on me and the rest of thReat. After I beat him.. I got to thinking.

This guy has to have the biggest ego that I have ever seen. The guy isn't even from the States, how can he be so damn egotistic?

Then, when you see words like these.. you start to think about what they can REALLY do. They say the ones you should be scared of are the ones that rarely talk. The shut-ins. Phil Atkin was all but one of these.

I like to think I deflated his ego a little bit. Probably not.. they also say people like that are absolutly hard headed, that you never get through to them. That wouldn't suprise me at all about Phil.

That's enough on that. I'm going to Flaw 25 next week to compete again in the thReat International Championship Tournament. Phil is not. All good and well by then. He says he was embarassed. I don't think so. Showed up, maybe. Embarassed, no. He just underestimated me.

That was his first mistake.. of many. Fucking around with Sergy was probably another. I won't go there. That is not my business. I have worries of my own, and that is farthest from Phil Atkin. My business lies with Rent-A-What?, Chris McClucks, and Jere Casseralle-a-er-something. I don't really care what their names are, it's not important.

Rent-A-Hero is one of them people that kind of make you feel a lot better about yourself. I mean, you know how when you are really starting to lose faith in yourself, and your self-esteem is in the shitter.. then you look at someone like this and realize that your not all that weird or messed up at all. Look at this guy, a guy that thinks every day is Halloween. Or worse, he actually thinks that he is some sort of a super hero. Really, its the funniest thing that I've seen in a long time.

He shows up at Flaws in petty Spiderman costumes and flies around like he is someone. Hah, its funny really. Kind of makes you want to point and laugh. Then you realize, the games are over and you put all of the crappy gimmicks behind you.

That is when I go out and kick some ass. In the name of my family. Good luck, Rent-A-Hero. You keep trying to save the world, when you really need to think about being in the ring with three other guys, that are ready to kick your ass.

At least the other two are halfway normal. Well.. kind of. Jere is a little on the psycho side. I'm not intimidated. At least.. I don't think I am. Hell, I dont care. Chris is hated, but hell.. it's all good. I'm just ready to fight. I'm ready to go with the guys that did the same deed that I did at Flaw 23. They beat the people they had to beat to move on.

Let's face it, they weren't near as underestimated as I was.
But.. its all good.. because I'm going on.

Right now, everything is looking up for me, and I plan on keeping it going.

That's all for now.. I think I'll do this thing more often.

Signing off,
-hateZILLA
