Taking A Glance - Childhood Years
Starring: Tristan Carter


My childhood resembled what many other children did. I ran through swamps to catch frogs. I sat around with my dad hoping that Indiana would finally win a college football game. I had no idea who I was cheering for but all I cared about what that my dad was rooting for them to win. As a child there seemed to be so many things to do in Bloomington. But running around with my brothers and sisters was usually enough to do.

I guess I first became interested in wrestling when my father bought a wrestling gym and designated my uncle, Lincoln, as the head trainer. I remember visiting on the hot summer days. My siblings and I would walk in - and you could feel the heat. All around you young men and the occasional woman would be working with each other. My uncle would be walking around, telling them what they were doing wrong. Dad said he was a great wrestler in his prime. And by watching him teach, I could tell that he had been.

After the gym would close up, my uncle would take us all out to get ice cream. We would constantly ask him about wrestling and he would only chuckle at our questions. But one thing he did say was that he was going to train us all to become wrestlers. Maybe he said it but didn't mean it. That would be a handful. Training five brothers and sisters. I'm sure he regrets the day he ever promised to train us all together.
