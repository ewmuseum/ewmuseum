It's Official..
Starring: Mike 'Blindside' Jevo

Setting: Home
Location: Hollywood, CA
Time: 1:22 P.M.

As usual, the home of Mike Jevo was cluttered, and noisy.

The sound of loud children running around, playing with toys and screaming at the top of their lungs infiltrated the home.. alongside the sounds of women chatting it up, and in the room across.. business as usual.

Across one end of the long, fine glossed wooden table sat Mike “Blindside” Jevo, a former-retired professional wrestler, in terms of a possible return. On the other end of the table, a recruiter of the profit that the man was actually considering joining, bringing his huge return into the pro-scene back.

“Well, sir..” said the nervous recruiter, fumbling papers in his hand on the other end of the table.. staring at his hopeful employee. “Threat Wrestling offers a lot.. including great roster members, great storylines to follow by.. and so on..”

The recruiter slides Mike Jevo a few papers, and Mike stops them.. reading each paper over thoroughly.

“Okay.. name a few talents on the roster as well.” Mike asked the recruiter, adjusting his sunglasses.

“Well,” the man said. “We have a lot of new talent, including such wrestlers as Jordan X Shabazz, Riot, and returning superstars such as Rejection.. and so on..”

Mike Jevo sat there with a finger to his temple, obviously pondering the offer. Finally, Mike Jevo looked back at the recruiter.. with his decision.

“Well, this crap might as well do for a new beginning for my wrestling career. I'll sign.” Mike finally answered.

The smiling recruiter stood up gradually in rejoice, and walked over to Mike with the contract, and pen.

“You do not know how much of an accomplishment this is for Threat, sir. If you would just sign right here on the dotted line.. then this deal is set.” The recruiter finished.

Mike Jevo held the contract in hand.. reading over all the tiny print, and anything else on the paper, before finally signing his signature onto the dotted line. Mike Jevo stood to his feet, an extended hand out from the recruiter, Mr. Chris Burks. Mike Jevo gradually shook.

And so it was set.. Mike Jevo was apart of Threat Wrestling.
