Why We Do The Things We Do
Starring: Levond Daniels

Living in someone else's shadow can really be the worst thing that can happen to a person. Especially a cocky person, who truly believes they're better than the ones that hide them from the spotlight.

Be it when you're young, and you got around your friends. There was always that one person who got all of the attention from everyone, like they were the one who mattered most. Maybe it is when you go to work, and everyone always asks you how that one person is, because every time they look at you, they remember him.

Every time I try and do anything for myself, I have to try and fight out of Shawn Arrows' shadow. It seems he's been everywhere, and done everything in the world of wrestling, because no matter how different I try to be, people still remember him, and could give a crap less about me.

The day I began to fight out of the shadows was fun though. It was a while before my career started, but I can still remember it like it was yesterday. The day after I turned 18, Shawn brought me into his families private gym. He told me he would help me "live my dream." He even told me he forgave me for the things I had done lately. That was the only reason I went.

At the end of day one I realized something was wrong. Shawn was a quick teacher, and I a quick learner, so we meshed well there. The problem was, he was training me to be him. To be a show off and a technician in the ring. To master the ropes and learn how to work on a single body part until the opponent can't stand to have it touched anymore. I had no desire to be a wrestler.

I had already found my calling by then, and it was something Shawn couldn't help me with. I loved fighting. I wanted to be a fighter. I wanted to bring back the true sport side of this business, by giving people a real challenge.

The second day of training couldn't have come sooner. I had already had enough of the training, and knew it would be my last day. Only two days in, and I'd rather shoot myself than ever go through it again. I came in on time, dressed to fight that day, not to wrestle as we had planned.

Shawn noticed this right away, and wound up sending a confused stare in my direction my entire way to the ring. He would learn soon enough why I was dressed this way.

He had told me that day would be the day we got in the ring and went at it, to see what I had learnt from him the day before, and from just watching the shows live and on television. That day was going to be fun. I was going to make sure of that.

I slid into the ring and bounced back up onto my feet, immediately locking up with Shawn. Shawn easily started backing me up towards the corner, more used to the lock up than I was, and having more power than I did. I knew his routine. He'd get me into the corner, and then go to work with chops. He'd done the same at the end of the day before's training.

He slowly did so, but today I was ready. As I felt my side brush against the ropes I smiled, confusing him for a second, and distracting him long enough for me to bring a knee up into his stomach. I followed the knee with a roundhouse to the face, which caused Shawn to stumble back a bit. I was surprised it hadn't taken him off his feet, but this was even better.

The slight stumbling back gave me the room I needed to do what I wanted, and before he knew it he was on the ground, courtesy of a reverse tornado kick. I slowly got back out of the ring, staring at Shawn from the floor as I back peddled, turning around just in time to side step a bench and make my way out of the gym.

I couldn't even say a word to Shawn that day. I didn't know how to explain to him why I did what I did, I just know I did it because I had to. I couldn't keep training under him. Had I done that, I'd still be Shawn Arrows Junior. That was a chance I couldn't take, even if I did want to wrestle.

Now here I am, years later, and everything has come back to Shawn once again. Now I'm fighting for him, to save his precious girlfriend Jessica from some freak Anubis. Now I'm fighting to save him and his life from crumbling for a second time this year.

I was finally fighting into my own career. I was finally becoming my own man, and now I'm stuck back inside the shadow of Shawn Arrows. I just wish I could understand why.
