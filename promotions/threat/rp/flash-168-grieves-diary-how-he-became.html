Grieve's Diary: How He Became
Starring: Grieve

I bet you’re wondering were James Edward Grieve is? He is no longer around to write his thoughts and feelings, he died several years ago, leaving me with you. You are now my only friend, my only family. We have one thing in common, we both have lost the only thing we loved and it’s fate that we have emerged together.

If you’re wondering who I am, then let me introduce myself.

My name is John Edward Grieve, the son of James Edward Grieve. You will realize by the words I create that I am a man concealed by hate and triggered by rage due to my Grief and the past.

“What happened to James?” you ask.

He and mother was a victim of God. He stole their life away from them; he stole them away from me.

It was 1999, October 31st. My twenty-first birthday.

The morning sun had finally awakened from its slumber and its burning rays peaked through the tall, thick oak trees and crept upon the maroon brick wall. Birds chirped, engines roared, and I moaned as my eyes ached open. My heart and soul had yet to be tattered by God and I was living the normal life.

As you can tell from the previous pages, my father and I were close. We had been since mother gave birth to me and therefore I still lived with the two in a nice, cozy house just at the corner of Jay St. We were all on-going Christians and had a nice peaceful life.

In college, I participated in two sports; college football and wrestling. Father wanted me to be the best I could be at anything but mostly, wrestling.

He enjoyed it very much; he enjoyed watching me . He had a dream of becoming a professional wrestler but due to an injury he sustained to his spine during college football, he lost that dream. Dad was paralyzed from his waist down; it was a miracle that he regained his feelings from his waist up.

The day of October 31st grew older and a big night was ahead for me. We had a match against our rivals that night and father was anxious as well as I.

Light evaded, and darkness advanced. People crowded the gymnasium followed my chants and rants. Fans from both teams were cursing at one another, some even fighting with each other. It was hell on earth.

I finished lacing up my boots and stretched until it was time for the big match. My fans cheered enormously as I pushed myself through the door and appeared before them as the other side tossed cups and popcorn in my direction. I remember making eye contact with a few, gracing them with fear. Believe it or not, Gimmicks played a big roll in this match. I was the big silent monster and my opponent was the cocky prick. It’s hard to believe that this much shit happened in college but it did.

The match only proceeded for a minute or so before I easily obtained the victory. I had the advantage against the poor foe. He lacked what I had; strength, size, and speed. One side roared with boo’s and the other roared with cheers.

And that’s when it happened.

One miserable little sorry ass loser stepped from the opposing side and pulled a pistil from his inner jacket.

Commits like, “Aw…” and “He’s got a gun!” Were shouted as everyone stood there with a thumb in their ass.

But me, I tried being the hero. I tried saving innocent people from their demise, I asked God for help but did he comply? No, instead he let the idiot pierce my chest with a bullet. But that was the least of it. I was helpless and drenched with pain as I watched him proceed over to father; put the barrel in his mouth, and watched him put a bullet through his head.

Tears formed and tears fell down the side of my face. Mother screamed and the killer laughed, putting a bullet through her skull. The pain that had ached by body was gone and my rage was triggered by that shot.

I stood to my feet with his back turned and advance behind the deprived soul. He turned around before I could clutch my hands around his neck and shot another bullet into my arm. I grunted, knocked the gun from his grasp, and locked my hands around his throat, lifting him off the ground until he was lifeless. My teeth grinded against each other, veins exploded through my skin, and my hatred for everyone was formed.

After the event occurred, no one cared for me. No one said anything to me. I saved them all and they didn’t even once, greet me with a thank you or I’m sorry. Instead, they stayed away from me. They thought I was a monster, a murderer, but in fact I was their savior.

But fuck every last one of them.

You see now diary, why I am the way I am. You see why I am concealed by hatred, you see why my rage is triggered so easily. It’s God and his followers, I would kill every last one of them but father would despise me for doing so, he’d want me to ignore their sorry ways and conquer my dreams.

And that their diary, is what I am doing now, just for him, just for James Edward Grieve.

I am now a professional wrestler at thReat.

And I will do anything to be known, anything.
