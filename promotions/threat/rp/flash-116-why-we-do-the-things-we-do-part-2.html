Why We Do The Things We Do: Part 2
Starring: Levond Daniels

Friendship can be such an odd thing. Most friendships only last as long as there is something to gain from the person you call a friend. Of course, there are always those that are true friends. Those special bonds you can find between two people who just care about each other. The two would honestly enjoy each others company, and want nothing more than the friendship they have.

The friendship Shawn and I had was obviously the first kind. We were always using each other for something, wether we knew it or not. There was always something. Shawn always had the money I needed to get by on. He always had the new games, the new computers, the new everything. Things I took advantage of every time I got the chance. I had the easy girls. When he felt like having something new, he would come to me and ask me to help him out and keep it a secret.

Yea. Shawn never could be committed. He wasn’t to Brit. He wasn’t to Samantha. I don’t see why he’s with Jessica. Looking back on things now and thinking about it. He’ll just wind up hurting her, and she wont stand for it like everyone else did.

I remember the one time I did wish Shawn and I had a strong friendship. It was a couple of months after I started selling drugs. My sister’s boyfriend made the call for me, and got a guy to buy a quarter bag. I usually just stuck to people I knew when I sold, but I was desperate for money and my so called friends were all broke. They couldn’t afford to buy anymore.

I remember going over to his house that night. It was just starting to get dark, which was normal. That was when I usually went out and sold, after dark, but before it got so late it was obvious. This night was different though. Something unexpected happened.

As I walked in the door, I could feel something was wrong. The way he looked at me was odd, and the fact that he was at least six feet five didn’t set well either. He was almost as dark as I am, and had on a tank top that looked small on him, but would’ve covered my whole body probably, and some dark pants. As soon as I sat down the bag, I knew something was wrong.

“So you’re charging me thirty dollars, for this?” The tone in his voice as he stared towards the bag worried me. He didn’t seem very pleased at all. It was great weed, probably the best you could find in Greensboro, but he didn’t seem happy with the price.

“I usually charge a bit more for first time customers. The more you buy over time, the more I’ll lower it. I’ve got some people that only pay fifteen now.” I responded, hoping I could make him a permanent buyer. What I didn’t expect was the hand crashing into the side of my face and causing me to turn sideways. I quickly span back around to face him, only to catch a fist just below my eye, causing me to stumble back into the door.

He was slowly coming towards me, so I did the only thing I could think to do at the time. I froze. Or so he thought. Just as he got close enough to reach down and grab me, I swung a leg out, catching the back of his and tripping him up, causing him to fall onto his back. All I could think about at the time was how badly he was going to kick my ass when he got up now, after doing something that stupid.

He got back to his feet after a couple of seconds and cracked his knuckles as he made his way towards me again. He charged at me, but before he could get to me I jumped, spinning around and catching him with the inside of my foot to the side of his head, knocking him to the side and onto the table the bag of weed lay on.

That was the first time I had ever hit the Reverse Tornado Kick right. Every other time I had tried that move practicing, I wound up on my ass. When there was an actual threat, my body just seemed to push itself to it’s limit.

Had Shawn been there, that never would’ve happened. Shawn could’ve taken care of the guy easily, and just held him off while we got out of there. Had he just understood why I had to resort to selling, I never would’ve had to hurt that guy.

I never would’ve realized I was a fighter.

The reason I’m a fighter now is because of that night. Once I realized I could actually take care of myself, my practices became three times as grueling on my body. It wasn’t just about having fun anymore. I fought to protect myself during deals gone wrong like this one. I never would’ve become a fighter if not for Shawn abandoning me.

It’s all his fault.
