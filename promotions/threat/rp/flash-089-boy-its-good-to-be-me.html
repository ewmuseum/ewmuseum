Boy it's good to be me.

Boy it's good to be me.
Starring: General David Sanders

Cue the General.

He was in his small house in Virginia. He opened the large white door and took a step outside. It was a sunny day. Rain hailed upon earlier in the week, so it was a nice change. He took a deep breath in with a smile on his face. His high grass in his yard was a sign he wasn’t much of a homeworker. He had seven-foot stone wall topped with barbed-wire surrounding his yard. I guess he enjoyed his privacy.

After a quick stretch, he walked back into his home. Stepping over clothes, he sat down on an old couch. He turned on the television, and in the corner of the screen read “> PLAY.”

He was playing, for the zillionth time, Payback’s Main Event.

It showed him getting battered and beaten to a pulp. But he was the man that won.

General David Sanders gripped the mesh of the cage tightly and raised his body, before.... well, doing something sensational. Something almost undescribable. You had to be there to see it.

A spinning heel kick with his left leg -- the leg which was on the inside of the cage. Which basically meant that the General's left leg had traveled from the inside of the cage, around to the outside, and caught Xaxon squarely in the face.

Sending him hurtling down to the canvas.

*CRAAAAAAAAAAAAAASH*

And through the fucking ring, which had been worn out throughout the course of the night!

The General smiled at this part, as the entire crowd raged.

"HOLY FUCK!"

"HOLY FUCK!"

"HOLY FUCK!"

"HOLY FUCK!"

General Sanders, meanwhile, was holding on to the top of the cage by one hand... following his crazy stunt. Troy Mason was in shock at the sight of his lovely ring collapsing before his very eyes, as Xaxon fell down from the top of the structure. The ring in shambles, it was almost certain that Sanders was victorious.

And as he pulled his entire body upwards, like he was doing a pull-up, General Sanders managed to get himself over the top of the cage and on the other side of it in one fluent motion... something you'd normally see out of an action movie. The crowd exploded in a round of jubilant cheers at this point, as David quickly descended down the wall...

With only a few feet left, General David Sanders decided to just let go and drop to the ground. So, he did.

And the bell rang almost instantly, sending the entire audience into a joyous rapture.

General David Sanders... had become... the thReat World Champion.”

By now, the General had gotten up and walked to his refrigerator. He was filling a cup of water via the sink. As he watched the ending minutes of Payback, he said...

“Boy, it’s good to be me.”

Result: GENERAL MOTHERFUCKING DAVID SANDERS VIA CLIMB-OUT TO WIN THE MOTHERFUCKING THREAT WORLD CHAMPIONSHIP
