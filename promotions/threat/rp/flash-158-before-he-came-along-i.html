Before He Came Along: I
Starring: A Really pissed-off Son

From the desk of Shane Edward Fawcett:

Why, oh, dear God why, did we have to take our chances with this stupid goof?

You know, sometimes, I think my father is a grade-A crackpot. Since Fawcett And Son Agencies closed down, I’ve seen changes in my father. A man, once with a brilliant mind like a new-age Machiavelli, has collapsed over a long period of time into a basic pile of steaming, stinking, putrid pile of dog shit. He’s just gone insane now. Is he right to take our chances with that fucking goof! We literally plucked him off the street!

Or the harbor, if you want to be precise…

But that retard…oh, God. Is my father so insane that he is willing to take all the money that we have left and invest it so that way he could send the bum to a wrestling school? It doesn’t matter if he is good! It doesn’t matter if he, for some reason that the holy deities of the heavens refuse to divulge to me, bested every other student at the school! My father took nearly all of our money, our savings, and invested it in some fucking hobo to wrestle!

“Oh, don’t worry,” he says. “I know what I’m doing! I know it! This man is going to be our meal ticket!”

Yeah. Sure, Dad. Theodore Edward Fawcett, my father, or Ted as he likes to be called, has just gone off the deep end and is drowning in an endless ocean of madness without any signs of a fucking life preserver. How many “meal tickets” did we go through before Fawcett and Son closed down?

112.

Exactly the number of “meal tickets” our business had gone through before it ended 3 years ago. We tried to take in so many clients: bands, pro-wrestlers, movie stars, B-list actors, basically anyone with a fucking wallet, we tried to leech off their money and for what? To wallow in the depths of obscurity for three years just to finally put all our stock in a fucking hobo.

Well, here we are now. We barely make ends meet as is. We live in a really shitty neighborhood where people rotate their tires: from our car to theirs.

We’re lucky that some federation actually had so many brain cells rotted out of it that they picked him up. Some place called thReat. Well, for Christmas this year, if we don’t make money off this raving, smelly derelict, we’re gonna be lighting a mistletoe on fire to stay warm.

And if we aren’t successful with this one…

It’s gonna be a sad holiday…

Merry FUCKING Christmas to you, too, Dad!
