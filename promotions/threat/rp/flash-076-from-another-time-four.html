From Another Time - four
Starring: beforeTHEhatred

Now this was a phrase a man would hear about a million times in his lifetime. You could always find someone that said ‘I’m going to kill you’ or ‘If he does it, I’ll kill him’. The words would eventually come to mean nothing unless someone was holding a knife to your throat or pointing a loaded shotgun at your head.

But, when the voice of the young man spoke, there was no lie behind the expression. There was no exaggeration, but rather a true and serious realization. This was the truth. This was what would happen, no ifs, ands or buts.

“Gun in your pocket, huh?” he said, nodding almost sardonically but also understandably. “Let me ask you something. What has this boy done to you to disserve death? Huh?

“What’s it to you? You know you don’t give a shit.”

“No, fuck that. Did he call you a pussy in front of your friends? Did he say you have no balls? Was it that meaningless and fucking stupid? You know, I’ve seen shit like this before. Killing each other over this stupid macho shit you guys seem to have. Fucking pointless.”

“No, man. Not this time. This time he needs to die. He has to die for what he did to…”

“To what? What did he fucking do that was so…”

“He raped my fucking girlfriend!”

He just sat back, bewildered by the response and taking a second to contemplate the words. Going from impromptu rage to sudden awareness, he laid on the bench, and looked back over to the other young man, playing basketball, wearing blue shorts and talking shit on the court.

“When I was away in Florida, he thought it would be a pretty cool thing to sleep with my fucking girl. And when she didn’t buy into all his bullshit, he got some of his buddies to help him later that evening. In the alleyway behind my house no less. Everyone heard her screamin, and fucking beggin for help, but no one gave a shit. No one cared. He thought he’d gotten away with dat shit. That’s why I’m going to walk over there and shot him in the head.”

His eyes were still locked onto to this so-called rapist, who just happened to be laughing along with his friends. A man who had the audacity to rape a girl because he couldn’t get her otherwise, and there he was, living life as if he hadn’t done a thing. The thought was too infuriating to truly realize, but then again, there he was, standing right in front of his eyes.

“I don’t know you, so I guess I can’t stop you.”

“No you can’t. I wish ya could man, but… You can’t stop me from what I’m about to do.”

All this time, the young man’s eyes never left the pavement, and the other man’s eyes remained on the man whose body would be lifeless in only a few moments. Their souls basked in the light of a streetlamp, surrounded by a forest of darkness and both waiting for something to happen.

What was that thought I had before?

Sometimes it’s the anticipation that gets you the worst.

Shit, how true that is.

He finally turned back to look at the young man when he heard the gun’s clip go in and the safety being pulled. The sound reminded him of the rattle of a snake before a strike, or the glare of a lion just before the pounce. Except this was more like the prey taking offensive. This wasn’t the vicious predator about to kill for food. This was a young man who was about to do something that only pure hatred could drive him to do, and that was all there was to it.

The young man stood up, and asked on last question.

“What are you going to do with that picture? You won’t, you know, give it to the police after…”

“No. No, I hold on to all of my pictures. And yours will be no different.”
