I Get By With A Little Help From My Friend (Friday) [4]
Starring: Chris "Neverwhere" McCluskey

“I really shouldn’t have eaten all that ice-cream before I went on that roller-coaster. That lady sure didn’t look pleased when I vomited over all her children.”

Unfortunately for Joe Soap and Chris McCluskey, their brief stop at Disney World hadn’t gone as well as they had hoped. The aforementioned incident happened on top of the calamity of all of Chris’ loose change falling out of his pockets and maiming the people queuing below, when he and Joe were upside down on one of the rides.

Chris and Joe were now back in the tour bus, less than a hundred miles outside of the city at which Double or Nothing was to be held. It was then that Joe chose to ask the question that had been on his lips for the past two days. Chris knew it was coming.

“Can we stop soon? I’ve got to pee.”

“Sure Joe.”

Joe pulled over into the next gas station, and got out to stretch his legs. Chris did the same, and went into the station and paid for the gas he was about to put into the bus. As he pushed the door open, one of those little door chimes chimed, and Chris went towards the drinks refrigerator, after deciding to pick up some of the splendour that is Mountain Dew. Pepsi don’t sell it in Britain, which is the most heinous and downright outrageous thing any drinks company has ever done. Bastards. It was then that he noticed the three men dressed in black, wearing balaclavas. Gee, the guy at the cash register sure was sweating a lot. Oh well, he was kind of fat, and those people do that a lot. Wait a minute!

Meanwhile, Joe had finished up in the bathroom, but not before he scared the bejesus out of the woman who walked in as he was taking a leak in the sink. The silly Billy had gone into the wrong toilets. “Hmmm, funny urinals,” was what he had said before relieving himself. As Joe walked into the station to look for his buddy, he saw Chris standing beside the drinks machine, with a strange cheesy smile on his face. Joe smiled back. He had seen the Mountain Dew. He then also noticed the three men.

“What are you guys dressed like that for, it’s a little hot outside for skiing, don’t you think?”

The three men turned and looked at Joe, who just stood there smiling.

“Tic Tac?”

The men clearly didn’t take kindly to Joe’s offer, and the one in the middle pulled his gun out of his pocket and aimed it at Joe. Then, two things happened.

Number one, there was the sound of breaking glass.

Number two… no, Stone Cold Steve Austin didn’t run down from the back with a chair and lay everybody out. Joe felt the breeze of somebody charging passed him at top speed, and Joe decided to do the same.

The glass shattering had been Chris dropping a bottle of Coca Cola, and the breeze of somebody charging passed him at top speed… well, I’ll leave that up to your intuition.

Chris threw the plastic bottles of Mountain Dew onto the passenger seat and fired up the engine and Joe lunged through the doors. Shots fired off around the bus as it pulled out of the station. Two of them men in black (no, not Will Smith and Tommy Lee Jones) chased after them, slapping the side of the bus. Chris put the pedal to the metal and they were out of there and gone.

“Quick! Give me your cell phone!”

Chris fumbled around in his pocket and pulled out his Nokia 3330 (check out the continuity baby!) and handed it to Joe.

“How do you get the keypad lock off this thing?! What’s the number for 911?!? And what’s your high-score at Snake II?!?!?!” screamed a horrified Joe.

Chris just looked at Joe, who soon realised what a fool he was. He didn’t have his seat-belt on. Chris took the phone back off Joe, dialled the required number, and handed it back to Joe, who informed the police. Despite the law most-likely stating that since Chris and Joe were witnesses to armed robbery, they would normally have to go to the police station to make a statement, Joe explained on the phone that they didn’t have the time for that, and luckily for them, and once again luckily for this story which is filled to the brim with plot holes and inconsistencies, the police officer obliged to let Chris continue on his way to Double or Nothing.

It was five minutes before either of them spoke

“Mountain Dew, Joe?” offered Chris.

“I can’t, I suffer from vertigo.”

And so, Chris swapped seats with Joe, and it was the Northern Irishman who drank the fruits of his labour, while Joe drove on.
