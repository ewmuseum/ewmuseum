Life and Times Part 1 - Return To Magic Kingdom

Life and Times Part 1 - Return To Magic Kingdom
Starring: Tyler Davidson

The Introduction

Time has been passing by throughout the life of Tyler quite quickly as of late and the events of his hectic life have left him feeling the pressure of being a superstar. With that in mind it was again the idea of longtime friend Daniel [name withheld] and girlfriend Heather that the trio should take another trip to help relieve some of the stress with which Tyler has suffered from as of late. Who knew that such a pleasant idea of stress relief could go so wrong for Tyler a second time? Obviously his two friends who put so much time and effort into their little plan for his own relaxation had not thought of just how wrong it could go. It’s because of this that Heather and [name withheld] had insisted on a short break before the match would take place in order to let Tyler get some relaxation in.

Now Tyler along with Heather and [name withheld] make their long awaited return to Disney World in Orlando, Florida. Many people could still remember the first trip which had been taken to the magical world of Disney but for those who don’t things did not go quite as planned for the trio. Instead of having a fun and relaxing time among the dozens of attractions in the Magic Kingdom things became a bit hectic when an already grumpy and irritated Tyler had somehow managed to get himself into a fight with one of the Disney characters in Goofy. Needless to say such an event did not go over very well with the workers in Disney World or the kids nearby who witnessed the whole thing. Tyler had been branded a villain among the Disney World populace on that day with good reason.

Time heals all wounds they say however and now it is time to find out if that is indeed the truth.

End Intro

The scene begins with a wide overview of a parking lot jammed full of cars. In the distance you can see the first images of Disney World’s Magic Kingdom as the castle looms high against the afternoon sky. The blazing Floridian sun shines down upon the ground below it as thousands of people scurry through the immense heat of the city. As the people pass by the shot focuses in on one specific black Chevrolet Avalanche parked in the lot. The doors to the truck stand open and outside of the truck are two people standing looking into the truck at something or someone else. As you get closer you can make out the features belonging to Heather and Daniel [name withheld] as they look into the car at Tyler Davidson as he sits there.

“You have to get out of the car eventually,” Heather began while giving Tyler a firm look. “It does get quite hot in there especially when in Florida.”

“That’s why it comes equipped with air conditioning,” Tyler fired back not showing any signs of budging from his seat.

[name withheld] and Heather both exchange looks as they try to deal with the pouting of Tyler in the car. Neither of the two had ever had much luck convincing Tyler to do anything when he had himself set against it but they knew they would have to find some way of doing it here tonight.

“What do you have against Disney World?” [name withheld] asked. “Just don’t start a fight with Goofy again and everything should be fine.”

“How do you know that?” Tyler argued trying anything to keep from having to get out of the truck. The heat however was obviously beginning to get to him as beads of sweat began dripping down his forehead.

“Trust me,” [name withheld] replied. “I already talked to some of the workers here. So long as you don’t start a fight again everything will be fine.”

“Trust you? HA!” Tyler yelled. “I know better than that. You two tricked me into coming here the first time to begin with.”

“So?” Heather responded. “At least we told you about it this time.”

“Yeah after you got me into the car and locked the doors,” Tyler argued back.

Both [name withheld] and Heather look about ready to give up on their pleading before [name withheld], a much larger man than Tyler, suddenly reaches into the car and grabbing him by the shirt manages to yank him out of his seat and onto the ground beneath him. Tyler lands hard on his back and lays there for a minute with his eyes closed before opening them again to see [name withheld] and Heather both looking down at him and the door to the truck closed and locked.

“Now that was uncalled for,” Tyler said as he began lifting himself off of the ground. He got back to his feet and brushed off the back of his pants and shirt before giving an angry look at [name withheld] and Heather who stood there chuckling. “Find that funny huh?”

“Slightly,” Heather answered back quickly. “You’re just pouting too much. Just let’s head into the park and try to have a good time. What’s the worst that could happen?”

“Gee I wonder,” Tyler responded though obviously sounding quite defeated. “I guess I don’t have much choice. It doesn’t seem you two are going to let me out of this no matter what I say.”

“No we’re not,” [name withheld] agreed with a grin. “Now get moving.”

“Alright, alright,” Tyler replied. “I’m going already.”

Together all three of them began their walk finally from the truck to the gates of the Magic Kingdom. All the while Tyler tried lingering in the back of the group but [name withheld] was there to constantly keep him in the front of the pack. Before getting to the front gate though [name withheld] stopped Tyler and Heather again from pulling out from a bag at Heather’s side a hat and pair of sunglasses.

“Maybe you should wear these just in case,” [name withheld] said as he handed the hat and glasses to Tyler who took them.

“Just in case of what?” Tyler asked quizzically and obviously not too happy.

“Well the sun is really hot,” [name withheld] replied. “Wouldn’t want you to get sunburn or anything before the big match.”

“You lying sack of……” Tyler began about to take a step towards [name withheld].

“Now, now,” Heather interrupted before he could finish. “No reason to get all upset Tyler. He’s just looking out for your best interests. We are in Florida right now and you know how hot the sun can get down here.”

“Yeah I’m sure that’s it,” Tyler said as he turned back to the park gate and put the hat and glasses on.

The group again continued their walk to the gate and passed by the crowds of people with little trouble till they got to the turnstiles. As they passed through the turnstiles Tyler drew stares from some of the people and workers surrounding the area but was allowed to pass by without any questions being asked. The three of them all made it through the gates and now inside the park it was time to decide what their first stop could be.

“Ok so where do we go first?” [name withheld] began asking looking more to Heather than he was Tyler.

“How about Small World?” Heather answered. “That’s always a fun stop.”

“NO!” Tyler yelled. “Anywhere but there! That place is evil. Evil I tell you damn it!”

“Oh stop it,” Heather admonished him. “There is nothing wrong with Small World now let’s go.”

“B-b-but…..” Tyler began but quickly died off as he realized little attention was being paid to him as [name withheld] and Heather both began walking away. Tyler realizing he was defeated slowly began to follow them before they finally came to the ride itself. One last time he decided he would try and save himself from the shear horrors which would certainly await them on the inside of the ride.

“Do we really have to go on this one again?” Tyler pleaded. “We went on it last time we were here.”

“Yes and we will be going on it again,” Heather answered without even looking at Tyler. “We should try and relive the whole experience all over again.”

“Well maybe not the whole experience,” [name withheld] added in. “No reason to get a fight started again.”

”True,” Heather agreed.

“Oh fine,” Tyler said giving up on his quest as they got to the front of the line.

The three of them all tried loading themselves onto the next boat which came through. Tyler and Heather both fit with ease but [name withheld] had to take up a couple of seats himself due to his larger frame. After a short wait the boat sailed off and they began their trip through the Small World.

“See now this isn’t so bad,” Heather said before looking to Tyler. Tyler though heard little if anything she said as he sat there with his ears and eyes covered. “Oh knock it off already you.”

Heather reached over to Tyler and pulled his arms down forcing him to sit back and watch and hear everything that went before them.

“No that’s not right,” Tyler argued as their boat slid off into the first of many scenes on the ride. “I can’t relive this horror again!”

The boat now slowly disappeared into the attraction as the scene faded out to black. It wasn’t long again before the shot reopened and their boat had come sailing back to the front of the ride to a complete stop allowing them all to get off. Tyler still sat in the same spot on the boat with his eyes again covered but to late to have done him any good. [name withheld] gives him a helping hand as he grabs hold of him again and lifts him to his feet before Tyler steps out of the boat himself.

“She that wasn’t to bad now was it Tyler?” Heather asked looking at the stricken Tyler as he steps onto the solid ground again.

“Evil, horror,” he replied not even looking at her. “Can we get away from this thing now please?”

“Alright we’ll find another ride,” Heather responded. “We’ll let you pick this one even.”

“Well in that case how about a ride out of here?” Tyler said looking between [name withheld] and Heather hoping that they would agree.

“Not a chance Ty,” [name withheld] put in before Heather could respond. “Since you don’t want to choose, I’ll choose one for you. Let’s head out to the Pirates of the Caribbean. They’re even making a new movie based off of this ride now.”

“Oh,” Tyler answered as he thought about it. “That doesn’t sound to bad actually.”

“Good let’s get moving then,” Heather replied quickly not giving Tyler the chance to change his mind.

Tyler actually leads the way this time as the group makes their way through the park and head for the next stop. On the way they stop at one of the refreshment booths and order a lunch consisting of hot dogs and ice cream. Before they could get very far on their quest though the group was stopped by the arrival of some familiar faces.

“Hehe look Pluto,” Mickey began pointing in the direction of the group as Tyler tried sliding to the back and out of sight. “It’s them again.”

“Arf, grrrrrr,” Pluto responded eyeing the three.

“Be nice Pluto,” Mickey said not recognizing Tyler in the back covered by the hat and sun glasses. “They didn’t bring the mean guy with them this time.”

“Uh yeah we left him at home this trip,” [name withheld] responded casting a quick glance back at Tyler. “Didn’t think it would be good to have a round two between him and Goofy.”

“Goofy would win again anyway,” Mickey replied laughing as Tyler restrained himself in the back. “You people enjoy your stay and keep out of trouble this time.”

“We’ll try,” Heather yelled back to him as they quickly moved away from them laughing the whole time.

“That was not funny,” Tyler mumbled under his breath as he now continued following [name withheld] and Heather who took the lead paying little attention to his grumbling.

Before long the day had quickly passed by and despite his exterior appearance Tyler had actually begun to enjoy most of the stay. From the Pirates of the Caribbean to the Haunted Mansion to Space Mountain they quickly passed through the park with little incident. Upon leaving the Splash Mountain ride they had again run into another group of characters from their last trip as Chip and Dale greeted them at the exit. Tyler though never had the chance to put his hat and glasses back on following the ride and was quickly spotted by the two of them.

“It’s him again Chip,” Dale yelled out pointing at them and noticing Tyler most of all.

“Oh no,” Chip said. “Maybe we should go get Goofy to beat him up again.”

Both Chip and Dale began laughing at the comment as did [name withheld] and Heather much to the dismay of Tyler.

“Aren’t you two supposed to be on my side here?” Tyler asked looking at the two of them.

“Yeah but you got to admit that was funny,” [name withheld] answered.

“Yeah we loved it,” Chip said laughing and pointing at Tyler.

“Listen furball….” Tyler began before being cut off again by Heather.

“Calm down Tyler,” Heather said. “They’re only having a little fun.”

“Yeah,” Dale agreed still laughing himself. “Don’t get to worked up about it. We won’t beat you up or anything.”

“I bet you won’t,” Tyler said before turning to [name withheld] and Heather. “Can’t we keep moving now please?”

“Why?” Chip asked. “Don’t you want to wait to see Goofy again?”

“Shut it furball,” Tyler fired back. “I got better things to do than get run out of an amusement park by a bunch of costumed freaks.”

“Freaks?” Chip and Dale responded in unison. “Who are you calling freaks?”

[name withheld] and Heather both grabbed Tyler by an arm each and began pulling him away as Dale tried chasing after him only to be held back by Chip.

“We’ll be going now guys,” [name withheld] shouted back to the two chipmunks as Dale continued shouting at Tyler and calling him names.

“You always got to start trouble don’t you Tyler?” Heather reprimanded him. “We can’t even have just one enjoyable day at Disney World.”

“Hey that wasn’t my fault,” Tyler pleaded. “They were the ones who started it.”

“Oh listen to you,” Heather continued. “They were just having a little fun is all.”

“At my expense of course,” Tyler argued.

“Well you did give them reason to,” [name withheld] put in. “It’s not everyday someone starts a fight with Goofy in Disney World.”

“Ah shut it,” Tyler responded irritably. “Obviously my disguise is done for now so can we get out of here now?”

“I guess we might as well,” Heather agreed. “Can’t risk you getting beat up again.”

“HEY!” Tyler shouted after hearing her last comment. “I did not get beat up by Goofy.”

“Yeah you did actually,” [name withheld] put in with a grin. “But who’s counting right?”

Tyler could think of nothing else to say and fell silent casting a harsh glare in the direction of [name withheld] for his little joke.

“Okay,” Heather said. “That’s enough from you too. We’ve spent quite a few hours here already so maybe we should get some dinner before leaving.”

“That sounds like a good plan,” [name withheld] agreed casting a look at Tyler to hear his thoughts.

“Sure why not,” Tyler agreed. “How about we just stay away from anywhere these annoying little characters are likely to go?”

“That could be tough considering where we are,” Heather replied. “But we’ll try at least.”

“Let’s try and head over to Epcot,” [name withheld] suggested. “They have a nice seafood restaurant there that could work well for us.”

“Sounds good,” Tyler quickly agreed in a hurry to get out of the Magic Kingdom itself. “Let’s get moving.”

The trio quickly made their way towards the entrance to Epcot Center as they passed through many more of the people now recognizing Tyler as well though not for the same reason as Chip and Dale. They finally made it almost to the exit of Magic Kingdom when they were stopped by a voice from behind. It was a voice which was all too familiar to Tyler.

“Hyuk, hyuk,” came the voice from behind as it stopped Tyler dead in his tracks. He slowly turned around only to see standing behind him Goofy along with an army of Disney characters. “Look’s who back for more.”

“Oh crap,” Tyler responded. “I think it’s time to leave.”

All at once [name withheld], Heather, and Tyler turn to run as the army of Disney characters charges at them quickly from behind with Goofy in the lead flanked by Chip and Dale. The army chased after the three of them running them completely out of the park but the chase continued far after that. Never tiring the Disney army continued well on into Epcot Center throwing all sorts of items out at them trying to slow them down. Now Goofy was finally within reach of Tyler and went to grab him……..

Shooting up in his seat Tyler slammed his head against the overhead compartment of the plane before looking around puzzled and rubbing his head. It took a few minutes for him to realize that everything had just been a nightmare and that he was still on the plane flying into town for the next Flaw show. Tyler looked around noticing the concerned looks of the passengers about him before sitting back down in his seat spotting [name withheld] at his side also staring at him.

“Problems?” [name withheld] asked showing a little concern over his partner.

“No, not at all,” Tyler answered. “Just a dream.”

“You and your damn dreams,” [name withheld] replied. “It’s a wonder you even go back to sleep.”

“I wish I didn’t,” Tyler agreed. “Are we almost there yet?”

“No we’re not,” [name withheld] responded. “Now go back to sleep. It was actually peaceful then.”

“Ah screw you,” Tyler replied turning his attention to the window as the scene fades to black.
