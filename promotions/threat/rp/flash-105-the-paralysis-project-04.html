the.paralysis.project.04
Starring: Vincent Pembridge & the product of destiny

Date: 31 May 2003
Location: Atlanta, GA; USA


It was the wee hours of the morning of 31 May 2003. Lawrence Neumeyer shifted unfortunately in the seat, his heavy eyelids clamped shut but his mind still operating as per normal. He was immensely tired but couldn't sleep. It always happened. Too exhausted to stay awake, yet too exhausted to sleep. A serious problem that many people in the world currently experienced.

For Lawrence, it developed right after he was booted out of the Army. A side-effect of extreme disappointment and anger.

It was his life, the Army. He'd risked everything when he was shipped off to North Korea, to help put an end to the growing unrest amongst demonstrators arguing over possible nuclear disarmament. Most of them being university students. He'd heard that North Korea was a hostile land, cut off from the rest of the world. The day he got there, mass riots break out. Naturally, Lawrence was called into action.

And naturally, the riots get out of control. Brutality had to be utilised by the American soldiers, but the university students had strength in numbers and the element of surprise. The temporary American Embassy in the country was trampled upon, and the situation got ugly. Neumeyer was one of the unlucky American soldiers to get shipped back to the US of A weeks later, with a horrid leg injury and a dislocated shoulder.

On one hand, it was terrifying for the Swedish native to go through that.

On the other hand, it was the proudest moment of his entire existence. He'd gone off to a hostile country and fought for his adopted homeland. For a good cause, too.

But his life went downhill from there.

Grunting as the car he was in made a sharp turn, Lawrence began to think about the utterly weird night he had. Firsty, he was in Jacken's Pub. Minding his own business, as usual. Having his regular order of beer and salted almonds. A loudmouth, drunk British guy came stumbling to the bar and began to blabber semi-incoherently, provoking Lawrence for no logical reason. Neumeyer remained calm, and as he now remembered, he wanted nothing bad to arise from the situation.

But a few derogatory comments later, and he'd punched the snot out of the British prick. Sent him staggered backwards, falling over a chair. There was no stopping the ex-Army enthusiast by that time, as he lifted the prick up and slammed him through the table. Scaring the patrons of the pub away. Thankfully, Jacken had passed out in his office, intoxicated beyond your wildest dreams.

He'd just been seperated from his wife.

Back to the point; Lawrence continued to unload some of his fiercest blows upon the drunk Brit, who showed no signs of fighting back. That aggravated Neumeyer even more, and he slammed the prick into a wall, causing a huge hole in it. By then, the entire pub had emptied. Even service staff and the regulars had taken off.

A amazingly brutal & one-sided asswhooping was unfolding, and it was something new in the pub. Within seconds of having completely mutiliated the prick, however, police had raided the pub. Not because some cowardly idiot had called the cops to dissolve the fight. No, it was a MEDITATED raid. For months, Jacken had been using his establishment to push some of the sickest drugs to the shady underworld of Atlanta. These included school kids, depressed due to family problems and whatnot. Proved to be innocent victims and Jacken's best buyers.

Lawrence Neumeyer was in the wrong place at the wrong time.

And he got taken downtown.

Rotting inside a jail cell, with several overweight bikers as his cell mates. They were part of a new biker gang, aiming to be cool by riding all over the city on their crappy motorbikes, hollering out obscenities at anyone they passed. Brutus Jen, the leader of the gang, had unknowingly cursed a police officer's rather unattractive daughter.

And the rest, as they say, is history.

Neumeyer fumed in the cell, furious that he'd been arrested. Initial investigations showed that the Stockholm-born character that stood admist a pile of debris wasn't a part of the drug-pushing activities of the pub. But there was blood on his hands, despite no body being around. That was enough to warrant an arrest.

What Lawrence didn't understand was why the British punk he'd bashed up wasn't found. After all, he had been badly beaten by the ex-Army man.

Another thing he didn't understand was who exactly the person who bailed him was.

The car finally pulled over, and Neumeyer's eyes shot open. The door opened after a few seconds preceding the engine being turned off, and there stood Norman Ilijah on the outside, smiling at Lawrence. The latter frowned as he stepped out of the car and his feet instantly hit wet gravel. It had been raining while he was transported from the jailhouse to this unknown part of Atlanta.

"Where the fuck am I?"

"Calm down. Come, follow me."

Norman Ilijah's appareance had altered from when Lawrence first saw him, as he was allowed to leave his cell and headed off to the administration counter to sign some forms. His pink tie had gone, and his eyes were even more bloodshot now. That was the one thing about Norman that freaked Neumeyer out -- his bloodshot eyes.

As the two left the jailhouse, Ilijah offered Lawrence an opportunity of a lifetime, if he'd play along with the baldie's requests. Something so dark inside of Neumeyer forced him to agree. And an hour later, there he was. Somewhere unknown, standing in the driveway of a huge mansion.

Following Ilijah, Lawrence Neumeyer's curiousity grew. The hallways of the mansion appeared to be neverending, but within seconds, he was led into a hall with exquisite finishes and jaw-dropping furniture. Amongst which, included a plush-looking armchair.

Which had familiar looking man sitting on it, tending to his facial bruises.

"Hello, Lawrence."

Neumeyer's heart almost stopped.

"You? The fuck? I just... didn't I?"

"Yes, you did. You kicked my arse pretty bloody well, I've got to say."

It was all so surreal to Neumeyer. In front of him, was the man he'd annihilated back at Jacken's Pub. Sitting smugly in the armchair, tending to his bruises, like it was no big deal. Norman Ilijah retreated out of the hall, after emitting a chuckle.

"Who the fuck are you?"

A snort, before the British man stood up from the chair.

"Vincent Pembridge. And I've got a bloody interesting proposition for you.

Involves a bugger by the initials of... DS?"

Lawrence Neumeyer's memory framework immediately came up with a match.

"David."
