Disrespect brings pain or wheres ORB!!!
Starring: Steven Fury

Torchlight flames burn bright supplying the only light in a shadowy room. Shadows of the flames dance across the gray stone walls creating an image of sinister happenings as the silhouettes of a two tall men talking, no conspiring look on. Devious laughter heard in the background leads one to believe something ghoulish is going on, the hair on the back of your neck begins to stand on end as screams are heard which leads you to think of something out of an old Boris Karloff or Vincent Price movie. What’s going on here you question? Why the screams? Is this a movie? Nay, the answer to these questions is it is reality of disrespect, or rather the reality of disrespecting a Fury!!

Upon another look a body, yes a body is dangling, held up only by the crude metal bracelets around the wrists held against the wall by chains. Steven walks over to the body, places the handle of a whip under his chin forcing the head upwards looking into his eyes. The glassy looking eyes of the man and the beads of sweat mixed with blood show the man has been under severe duress. An excruciating look of pain crosses the mans face as Stevens devious smile grows wider and questions. “Do you know what this is all about ORB?” ORBs head falls forward with his chin against his chest as Steven removes the handle of the whip from its place under his chin. “It’s about respect ORB. You made two fatal mistakes at Flaw last week. First you almost ran over my manager Mary Jane and secondly you disrespected me when I was trying to talk to you afterwards.”

Steven paces slowly and angrily around the dungeon as he continues. “If there’s one thing you will learn it is that showing disrespect towards the wrong persons can cause severe pain.” Steven laughs, looks at his cousin Sean and says. ”Tighten it a little more Sean.” Sean smiles as he tightens the brass band that is placed around ORB’s waist and small drops of blood begin to trickle over the spikes inside the band. Steven sighs as he walks up to a small table and rests against it. He places one hand to his chin and strokes his jaw as he says. “Tell me ORB, how do you like St. Elmo’s Belt?” ORB clinches his teeth tight as a grimace of pain washes across his now white as a ghost face. Steven smiles a little more as he continues. “Feeling a little sensation of burning?” ORB grimaces some more and lets out a huge gasp for breath as Steven looks on and says. “You know that’s where the term St. Elmo’s fire came from? It really is, those spikes digging into your gut make an extreme burning sensation don’t they?” Steven motions towards Sean and Sean loosens the belt, takes some of the blood on his finger and begins to paint his face with it.

Steven laughs loudly as the door opens and in walks a smallish man. He quickly runs over to ORB and begins to try and help him. The sinister laughter from Steven is quickly replaced with a frown as he angrily yells. “Back off Ozz!!” Ozz turns to Steven, shakes his head in disgust and says. “What has gotten into you? This is disgusting the way you’re doing things. I see you’ve undone all the work I did getting Sean away from your devious ways and have him under control again.” Steven snaps his fingers and says. “Not really, Sean’s just finally understands that listening to me he can be a winner, isn’t that right Sean?” Sean quickly comes out of his trance and looks around puzzled as Steven continues. “See Ozz, he’s still his foolish loser self that can barely even remember where he is.”

Ozz grabs a towel and begins to clean the wound marks where the spikes had pressed into ORBs flesh and watched Steven as Mary Jane comes skipping into the room. MJ smiles as she places her arm around Stevens’s waist, looks adoringly up into his eyes and says. “Playtime now?” Steven began to smile again as she reached up and nipped at his ear. Ozz looks on in surprise and asks. “MJ, I thought you were against all these horrible, despicable things Stevens been doing, why are you smiling so big?” MJ walks slowly towards ORB and Ozz with a sinister look on her face as she sticks a fingernail into one of ORBs open wounds and laughs as he grimaces and writhes in pain. “Well, that’s what you get for thinking Ozz, you of all people must know my thirst for blood revenge is second to none, excepting maybe Steven.” She continues to dig until ORB cries out in pain and continues. “This moron almost ran me down so he deserves everything he gets.”

Ozz pushes her away and continues to tend to ORBs wounds and says. “This man is human being and deserves to be treated as such, what gives any of you the right to do this to him?” Steven laughs as he stands, walks over to Sean and whispers in his ear and answers. “Because I can Ozz, because I can.. Steven takes MJ by the hand and starts to the door and continues. “Sean, watch over our guest and see that Ozz doesn’t help him escape.” Steven and MJ walk slowly out the door as Steven yells, “ORB, we shall return for our next lesson on respect later.” Steven and MJ laugh a sinister laugh as they walk away.
