Starring: Wolf & Rachel

Wolf had done it.

He’d beaten Steve Solex in the submission match, and according to his agreement with Rachel, if she still wanted to, they would get married as soon as possible.

The night’s event spun round Wolf’s head as he walked through the front door.

Sure, I won the match, but even at the time, that wasn’t the main thing on my mind. All during that match, I was thinking about how if I won, I’d be a married man, and yes, I was fighting to win. This woman is my everything, she’s the reason I got into the business in the first place, so I could get money to get married. It just hit me when the referee raised my hand. That could very well be the last time I get in the ring as a “free” man. Every week I go out there from now on, I’d have to look after myself better. Sure, the situation hasn’t really changed, but the ring on my finger that is a token of our everlasting bond? That’s’ pretty significant.

Rachel: Wolf… is that you?

“Yeah, I’m home. Did you watch the match Rach?”

Out she ran, and stood in front of me, trying to look a serious as possible, but he eyes, wild with excitement.

Rachel: Yeah. I watched it.

“And…”

Rachel: And what?

She loved playing games, even if she was terrible at them. She couldn’t keep that grin under the surface for less than a second.

Rachel: Wolfie… I know what we talked about before the match… but I have a question…

Second thoughts? Maybe she didn’t want to rush into this after all. I’d kind of put the pressure on her when I won my side of the match, but I couldn’t throw it. To be fair, I had to let her make the choice. And then she got down on one knee…

Rachel: Wolfgang, will you make the happiest gi…woman in the world?

She was still a girl. Just 22 years old, and we were planning the rest of our lives together. Sure it was a nervous time… but there was no chance I would ever say no. I got down on one knee with her, she looked tense, the pressure of putting herself out like that with the risk of being shot down.

“Of course”

We kissed, and hugged, and eventually ended up in the bedroom, but I don’t need to tell you about that.


Time Lapse

We arrived in Las Vegas, and that was it.

I’m not joking, you literally get married and thrown back out into the world of casino’s and bars. They only care about your money, and the weddings seemed more of a tourist attraction than any holy sacrament… but that wasn’t the point. We were now Mr. & Mrs. Kämpfer, and nothing could change that, and as I drove back, and Rachel slept on my shoulder, I felt at peace… and yet entirely uneasy.

The drive was going to be a long one, with old thoughts and new flying around Wolf’s head. His journey with Rachel as his girlfriend, had come to an end… and a new chapter of his life had begun. With thReat a huge part of their lives, lives that were now eternally bonded, Wolf couldn’t help but feel overwhelmed.

Everything was going perfectly…

Everything was going too perfectly.
