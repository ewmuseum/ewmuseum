Elijah Upclose: With Alias -Pt 2-
Starring: Christian Elijah feat Alias

"What would you gents like?" asked the bartender.

"Budweiser please."

"Orange juice."

Alias turned and glared at Christian Elijah, and they both laughed. "You're shitting me, orange juice?!"

The bartender eyed Elijah, wanting him to confirm his order. Elijah nodded, and Alias laughed again.

"So, what brings you to Cleveland?" asked Alias.

"Flaw25 man. I have a match against Chris McCluksey. And well, since you're still in Cleveland after the latest Courage, thought we could catch up on old times."

"$7.50 please." That was the bartender, who had returned with the drinks. Elijah slapped a $10 bill, and told the bartender to keep the change. He didn't want anymore interruptions. He had something important to ask Alias.

"I need to ask you something."

"Shoot."

After thinking about what Phoenix Rose had said, Elijah felt he needed to ask something else. Like what? Surely, most people would feel the same after they won their first title and when they were defending their title.

"Put yourself in my shoes, and tell me how you would feel about the upcoming match. That's right, be Christian Elijah, somebody who's never won a fucken title and is facing Chris McCluksey for a semi-final spot in the International Title Tournament. Don't tell me you dunno shit all about him man, because neither do I. All I know is, he must be pretty good if he's in the quarter-finals."

Alias looked at Elijah, who looked serious. He seemed he wouldn't be happy if he didn't get a good answer. He took a sip from his can of beer, and then started to speak.

"Dude, I've been in many feuds before, and recently, one with "Superstar" Vince Jacobs. I beat him to end a what seemed to be a never-ending feud, and I went in, with the mentality to prove I was better than him. Dude, use that mentality against that Chris..."

"McCluksey."

"Fuck that, I'm not bothered about his name. I'm Christian Elijah, and I'm better than him," retorted Alias. "And wassup with that Elijah? Why are you asking me all this? Nervous?"

It was like a slap to Elijah's face. But it wasn't one that was meant to insult. It was the slap that your dad would give you to shake your thinking. Elijah didn't expect Alias to be so blunt, but deep down, he knew he was nervous. But why?

"I think so."

"Don't dude, you were fucking excellent against Ernie French! Carry on like that and you'll fucking fry Chris Mac."

"But... I shouldn't have won. If Wolf didn't interfere, Ernie would have nailed me like a sack of potatoes. And I'm already feeling the pressure. How can I ever become a champion if I can't cope with the pressure of this match?!"

What must I say to him to make him understand? What has caused him to change so much? This isn't Christian Elijah! These thoughts ran through Alias' mind. He was getting frustrated.

"I saw you in SE. You won something more valuble than a title. You won Tarah from Jason Reaves. Think about that. I got to go man, I'll see you around."

And with that, Alias got up and left, after muttering "Good luck." Elijah was still stunned by Alias' words. He was right. He had beaten a tough competitor in the "Animal" Jason Reaves to win Tarah, who was more valuble than all the titles in the world put together. I mean, could you have sex with a title?

To compare Jason Reaves and Chris McCluksey was like comparing a lion and a kitten. If I could tame a lion, Elijah told himself, I can eat this kitten, fur and all. Elijah smiled. He was gonna fry Chris Mac, like what Alias said.

Don't waste the good beer, Elijah told himself, as he picked up the half-empty Budweiser can and stood up, walking away.

The orange juice remained untouched.
