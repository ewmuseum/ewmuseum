the.paralysis.project.05
Starring: Vincent Pembridge & the product of destiny

Date: 01 June 2003
Location: Atlanta, GA; USA


A limousine ride wasn't what he expected, but Lawrence Neumeyer continued smiling nonetheless. Forget the fact that it was about seven o'clock in the morning, and that he was going to Japan for a month. Forget the fact that his stomach hurt so bad, he decided against having breakfast and the hunger pangs were killing him now.

Lawrence was on the highway to ultimate revenge.

He wouldn't have guessed so a few days ago. June was meant to be a month where he would get a stable job, after finding out that servicing and maintaining cars wasn't his niche. Yes, Lawrence was 32 years of age, and had no stable job. The difficulty of surviving without being part of the American Army destroyed him.

So for months on end following that, Neumeyer worked out. Made himself into a tough machine. Then went out on the streets and picked fights with everyone that looked bigger than him. At first, he got his ass handed to him. Striking fear into the hearts of innocent bystanders seemed harder than he thought it was. But all he had to do, Neumeyer realised, was remember WHO exactly caused him to get kicked out of the Army.

And with motivation, Lawrence carved himself out to be quite unstoppable.

There had to be more to life than just releasing his frustrations upon innocent people, he thought. So, for a few months, he underwent training to become a police officer. The Academy were willing to overlook his past in the Army and gave him a chance. His mutated aggressiveness proved to be both a blessing and a problem at times, but after months of training, he finally graduated, a certiable police officer.

First day on the job, Lawrence was rounded up by the precinct he was assigned to for a drug bust. Neumeyer, still plagued by the sudden exodus from the Army after dedicating four years of his life to it, recognised one of the men in the warehouse they were raiding as the same man who'd driven him out of the Army.

It was but an illusion, of course. The bust failed, Lawrence's head came under the knife, and he was released.

Following that, all that was left for the Stockholm-born maniac to do was either rot or find a dead-end job. And as he retreated to Atlanta, a place he'd been informed was a complete change of pace from the madness that was Manhattan, the only job up for offer was that of being a car mechanic. Desperate, Lawrence caved in and began his career as a keeper & servicer of automobiles.

It was better than doing nothing the whole day long. It would have driven him crazy.

Blinking, Lawrence breathed heavily, all the thoughts of his past leading up to this moment overwhelming him. He wasn't one who was fond of thinking about the past, but he couldn't help it. His mind wouldn't quit remembering the assinine way he got kicked out of the Army. It was like a tape recorder, with the replay button being taped down. Neumeyer himself got physically sick about thinking of the past. So much so that he felt like committing suicide on several occasions.

And then, he realised HE would have won. Something Lawrence wouldn't stand for.

His cerebral cortex brought up the memory of the previous day. The meeting with Vincent Pembridge after getting bailed out of jailed by the latter's attorney, specifically. It seemed Vincent knew of HIM and Lawrence Neumeyer. The shared history between the two was stored in the Brit's mind as well. What the Swede wanted to know was why he was being recruited for a vengeance project.

"You're the only man fit for this job."

"You're the only man deserving for this job."

"If he suffers, it should be because of you."

"His blood has to be on your hands."

"He wrecked your life, it's only fair that you return the favour."

Lawrence remembering thinking those were valid points, but knew that Vincent had his own agenda. Or else, going to the extent of tracking Neumeyer down wouldn't have been considered by the British Degenerate and his attorney, Norman Ilijah.

"What does that bald fucker have to do with this?"

"He'll guarantee that your arse doesn't get into any trouble. And if you do, he'll get your arse out of hot water. That's what he does. That's what The Patterson Agency does."

The limousine made a right turn, and almost jolted Neumeyer out of his flashback sequence. He had no intention of remembering all the details of the meeting, but since it ending in him agreeing to take part in The Paralysis Project, Lawrence had to make sure that he'd actually done the right thing. A reassurance of sorts, to ensure there would be no major reprecaussions from his future actions.

"So, what do I have to do?"

"Bloody simple. Go to Japan and train under a mate of mine. He's a bloody animal, I'll tell you that now. And with your already superior skills, he's going to shape you into a truly invincible machine. He's probably going to take you to the mountain side and all that bollocks, to train under extreme conditions. You'll be there for a month.

Other than that, whatever else you do there is of your own agenda."

On those cold, lonely nights where Lawrence Neumeyer couldn't sleep... he'd fire up his computer and indulge in a little research. Seeing how HE had become a big part of his life, it was only natural that Neumeyer find out about HIS entire history. As luck would have it, a complete life biography was found, and Lawrence would re-read it every night before dinner.

It enhanced his appetitie. In more ways than one.

So, when the opportunity's handed to you, grab it.

"When the time comes, you have to bloody promise me... to show him no mercy."

The limousine came to a slow crawl, having reached the airport. The product of destiny was about to embark on the first phase of something so dark, so malevolent, so meticulously plotted by Vincent Pembridge and his friends over at The Patterson Agency. The driver behind the wheel was one with a motive, and that was the final piece of the puzzle.

"Count on it."

Whoever David was, he'd better prepare for the rudest shock of his life.

Lawrence Neumeyer was going to show no mercy.
