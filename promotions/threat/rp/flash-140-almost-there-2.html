Almost There [2]
Starring: Chris "Neverwhere" McCluskey

So what’s it like being a thReat superstar?

I would normally answer that question with “it sucks”, but it’s not really true. When I entered this company, I knew nobody. What do you mean I knew Phil Atken? I just told you I knew a bunch of nobodies. Everybody seemed pleased to see me. That first night in Tio Pepe Hall in Kentucky was quite humorous. I didn’t sneak into the building round the V.I.P. entrance like the fans had some sort of contagious disease (let’s face it, stupidity, moronity and retardedness aren’t contagious). Nope, I walked into the building through the front door, and boy oh boy were the fans elated. “Chris, you rock!” Or “Chris, you’re the greatest!” Yeah, okay. Bum lickers the lot of them.

Well all know what happened next. I went out, they cheered for a while until they realised I had been insulting them all night, and they’ve haTED me ever since. Which is absolutely fine by me of course, because I don’t need them.

I may not drive around in a fancy limousine or sleep in the nicest hotel room, but I’m the most popular superstar in thReat. Just ask me, I’ll tell you. I’m buddies with the Commissioner, which is great. It’s like being the Pope, only when I talk to God, he actually does grant me my wishes. Good old Colin.

I even have my own dressing room. The only problem is that it doesn’t have a toilet, but that doesn’t make much of a difference, that’s what cleaning ladies are for.

And now, I’m just two steps away from winning the International championship. The fans will really like that. Just imagine it, say it out loud in a Howard Finkle voice: “Ladies and Gentlemen (ha!), making his way to the ring, from Belfast, Northern Ireland (dumbasses), weighing two-hundred-twenty-nine pounds, the thReat International champion, Chris “Neverwhere” McCluskey!”

Sounds sweet, doesn’t it? Nice.
