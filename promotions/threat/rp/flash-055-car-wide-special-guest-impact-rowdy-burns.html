Car Wide(Special Guest - Impact Rowdy Burns!)
Starring: Extreme Impact

“McDonald’s or Wendy’s?” A voice comes in the play as black nothingness brings us into light.

“It doesn’t matter, whatever you feel like.” Another voice, different replies.

There is a slight pause.

“Should we wake Rowdy?”

“Nah, he had a rough night with lap dance, he wasn’t ready for it, let him sleep.”

A muffle voice belts out from the back seat of this ’69 Mustang. “…Fuck you guys.”

“Hey sport, you’re up.” CJ replies to his younger brother.

“Wow, surprised you aren’t more worn out, I remember my first lap dance.” The eldest brother, Beau puts his two cents in.

“Really? You do, well jeez, you have so many of them, and I’m surprised.” Rowdy shoots back cockily. “I usually don’t have to pay to get a little pie, but it was a nice Christmas gift nonetheless.”

CJ turns around from the passenger seat and looks at Rowdy.

“Yeah, we couldn’t think of anything better to get you. You have everything you wanted, so we thought we get you a little ass. Being the big man now, 20 years old. No longer a teenager! My little brother, all grown up…how sweet.”

“I’ll show you something sweet.” Rowdy follows by flipping around and quickly dropping his shorts revealing his posterior. He brings his shorts back up. “Sweet enough for you there?”

“Perfectly fine…I’d recommend shaving that thing, you ever get a girl, she will be afraid.” Raffled off by CJ.

Beau thinks to himself shortly, than decides to say what’s on his mind. “Yeah man, she won’t be able to find you’re A-Spot!”

CJ and Rowdy share a glance shortly, more of a puzzled look.

“What the fuck are you talking about?” Rowdy, the freer with his language exclaims at this notion of an A-Spot.

“You know…the A-Spot…CJ, you must know?”

“The hell if I do, that is one thing I may not know.”

Beau goes on. “Well, I was watching the Sunday Night Sex Show…and…” He was cut off.

“Gross, the show with the old lady talking about how to have good sex, that’s disgusting bro, even for you.” Rowdy, freely expressing his feelings.

“Anyway…” Beau continued.

“No, really stop. We are about to eat and I want to hold this down.” CJ smartly stops this.

“Fine…see if you guys ever get any pleasure.” Beau quickly fires back.

Fade to black.
