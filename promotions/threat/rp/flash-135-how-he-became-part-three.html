How he became [Part three]
Starring: Two-Face

“Son, you’ll love what I’m about to show you. It’s a present that you’ll not forget” a sniggering Boris Simunek spoke while taking a left into a back street alleyway.

Vladimir simply glared out of the window blankly, quite frankly knowing that something was not right.

Today was Vladimir’s 15th Birthday. The past 14 birthdays Boris Simunek had never shown any joy towards his son. He had never given Vladimir a present, instead a simple pat on the back and a cenacle while sarcastic smile. His mother had always been the one who secretly snuck out of the house while Boris was away at his gym. She would spend three hours a day, earning below the minimum wage to simply scrap up enough money to by her son something that he wanted. He never got the toys that the other kids would boast about in school, he would get something simple, nonetheless he knew that the thought and effort was what counted.

Today his mother had cooked Vladimir his favourite breakfast; a fried egg, beans, a rash of bacon, and a piece of toast. It was a rarity in the Simunek family, thus it was that more special. After, she had taken Vladimir into the living room, told him to close his eyes and handed him a used shoebox, which had been filled with a cosy blue fleece. Vladimir had leapt at his mother, and hugged her with all his life, before ‘he’ had entered the room and demanded that Vladimir leave.

Vladimir never spoke a word, never asked where he would go, fearful that his father would call him an “ungrateful brat” and give him another slap. But Vladimir had a gut feeling that this ‘birthday present’ was going to be something of a chore, rather then a present. And as the rusty blue ford slowed down in front of a blue “Fighting club” sign things became so apparent.

“This is your gym dad” Vladimir said looking across to his dad who smiled back and opened the rusted door.

“Come Vladimir” he said in his deep Russian voice, stepping out of the car and walking towards the front door, which had been chained shut for the evening to stop the drunks walking in and using it to refuge for the night. “Your present is in here!” Boris smirked, trying to hurry the uneasy Vladimir towards the door.

“O..O-kay” Vladimir replied, knowing 100 percent that this was now a chore for his father rather then the present that Boris had promised.

Vladimir had been to his fathers gym only twice before in his life. The First time he was carrying sweat buckets back to the loo, the second time he cleared the toilets that had been sabotaged the night before. For Vladimir, this was not a kind place. He had been spat at by the thugs, punched by his father and had been all-round treated like shit.

Still, with those thoughts plaguing his mind, Vladimir stepped out of the car cautiously and slammed the door behind him. He then squeezed through the narrow gap of the car bonnet and a trashcan that had been awkwardly placed cutting the route through the alley way in half. He glanced over to his father once more who encouraged him with a number of hand gestures but Vladimir felt uneasy. Slowly, he walked around the car and his father unlocked the chain on the paint free door. Boris let Vladimir enter first, into the dusty gymnasium. Vladimir took a couple of paces into the gym, avoiding a bucket that had been used to catch the rain water that dripped through a large hole in the roof. On the right corner of the gym lay a standard sized boxing ring, which was tatty and worn. Everywhere else fighting equipment and weights lay poorly laid out around the large room where Boris would usually take in any junkie and train him up.

“Your present son, is in the ring” Boris said gently, pointing his son towards the tatty ring.

Once again, Vladimir took a couple of paces forward, and stood face to face with the ring, trying to spot any item that lay inside. But there was nothing, the grey canvas had nothing but a wooden chair lodged in two corners of the ring.

“I don’t see anything dad” Vladimir said cautiously, trying to avoid sparking any anger from his father.

“What do you mean you can’t see anything” Boris moved over to where Vladimir stood and swivelled around sniffing in a deep breath of the sweaty indecent air. “This is your present Vladimir, a chance to become a real man…Like your father” Boris proudly stood up in a proud fashion, trying to prove a point.

“Be a man?” Vladimir asked, wondering what his insane father was talking about.

“Learn to fight Vladimir. Against all these morons who enter this gym everyday; To earn the respect of your father, which to be frank you have not earned.” Boris sighed and took an aggressive stance towards Vladimir who stood in his fragile state, looking around the gym.

“But I don-“

“But?” Boris barked taking another two paces forward in a lieutenant type fashion, “But what!! This is a chance Vladimir! A Chance to be a man. To stand up for yourself, instead of being that piece of shit that lays shivering in his bedroom corner every evening because he’s not a man. Your 15, not 10 boy!!”

Vladimir took a deep breath and gulped, he had managed to anger his father yet again. He turned around, to face his father eye to eye. A single tear dropped down the right side of his face, the side that had been ‘decorated’ with scars, and he looked his father in the eye, trying to emotionally break the man down.

That however was never going to work with Boris Simunek. He could never stand crybabies, and ‘that’ tear had just broken Boris’s patience.

Slap. Followed by another, then with his anger at its peak, a right hand that would have knocked down many a man. And Vladimir did fall, head first into the side of the ring to his father’s disgust.

“YOU PATHETIC PIECE OF TRASH!! GET OUT OF MY SITE!!” Boris screamed turning around towards his office. “You’ll fight here boy, don’t you think I’m going to let you turn into your mother!!”

And Vladimir had lost again, like he had lost every year of his life. He would be back in the gymnasium come Saturday morning, and he was about to realise that his life wasn’t as bad as it was about to get.

He could forget the school bullying, Boris made a promise that Vladimir was going to be a man. Whether he liked he or not.

What a loser.

End.
