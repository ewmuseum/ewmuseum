Lurking Death Part 5
Starring: Tyler Davidson

Opening where things had left off Erin Christoph slowly lowered the phone with a smile plastered across his arrogant face before he finally hung it up. The moment he had been spending weeks waiting for had finally arrived thanks to that one phone call and now he would finally be able to finish his job and to more importantly finish her. Christoph turned and quickly ran down the hall of his small New York apartment and began the preparations.

Meanwhile In A Building Not To Far Away…….

The sun had already set over the city of New York and the lights of the buildings were lit bright illuminating most of the sky and giving an incredible view to those who were currently flying back into the city. Right across the street from where Erin Christoph was staked out, Heather and Jessica [name withheld], the wife of Daniel [name withheld], sat at a table eating a late dinner. Scattered across the table were several different boxes and plates of Chinese food that both Heather and Jess were picking at for different tastes.

“So how long is it now until Tyler gets back Heather?” Jess asked her good friend and the fiancée of Tyler Davidson.

“A couple more days now,” Heather answered before taking a spoonful of cereal into her mouth. “He has a match out in San Diego tomorrow night and shortly after that he’ll be on his way back.”

“It’s about time I’d say,” Jess continued. “It seems he and Dan are gone longer and longer each time.”

“Yeah a west coast trip and it last about two weeks,” Heather agreed. “I’ll just be glad when it’s all over and they are both home.”

“Yeah tell me about it,” Jess agreed. “It’s not much of a home life with Dan gone all the time.”

“Guess it’s something we had to get used to,” Heather solemnly said.

Heather pushed the plate of food sitting in front of her out of the way and got back to her feet. She raised her arms in the air and stretched back letting out a yawn before looking down at Jess again.

“I’m going to go for a bit of a walk,” Heather told her. “Want to come?”

“No thanks,” Jess answered. “It’s gotten quite cold out there tonight and I’d rather stay in here.”

“Ok I’ll be back in a bit,” Heather told her before she turned and grabbed her brown leather jacket. She put the jacket on and made her way out of the apartment.
