Starring Araknis

]["Where are you going now?"][

"Home."

]["Why?"][

"I've failed."

Noah flew backwards, and was helped down by another shot from Riot. Riot continued to slam the chair down on Noah. Araknis once more got to his feet, and Riot turned around and slammed his chair so hard on Araknis' head that the seat popped off. Araknis slumped over.

Riot lifted him to his feet, and grabbed him for a reverse DDT. He spun him around, and dropped him with an Ace Crusher. Crowd Control.

Riot ran over and awoke the ref then laid over top of his nearly dead enemy, and the referee made the count.
