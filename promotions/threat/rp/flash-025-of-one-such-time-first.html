Of One Such Time - First
Starring: He who once saw

The trying years melted away.

The day was bright and sunny, a warm mixture of air filling the world. A good day. As good as any could be, he figured as he walked down the streets of Baltimore, one of many stops in his quest.

The crowd of the usual people walked with him, a blend of traffic as he stepped along the sidewalk towards the distant blue sky. A melody of birds and horns sang in the wind, catching all that motioned through the streets. A barrage of voices and cigarette smoke filled the air, but rarely deafened the train of thought and hardly ever cut the breath off.

The city was his haven, yet he walked through them with little or no emotion. They were merely ants, looking for food and nothing else. The food that fed their ignorance and their greed and their arrogance and their abhorrence. His eyes saw only this, like a disease spreading through them, infecting them in their adolescent lives and exacting on them in their adult years.

With all this in mind, he walked the streets of Baltimore.

He walked for hours on end, with no patience for time.

Golden streaks of sunlight etched the horizon as the sun set. Night was quickly taking the city under, leaving only the blackness of evening and of the hearts of the routine nocturnal. He didn’t mind living amongst their kind. He had dealt with them many times before. He would deal with them many times to come.

The presence from others slowly dwindled. They crawled inside, seeking safety from the night and from the world they claimed to love, yet secretly feared. He only walked, letting the chill of the wind tweak his bones and tickle his skin. With his hands sweating a river in his pockets, he looked to the side, and saw a nightclub’s lonesome sign, glowing in the growing darkness and crackling with the flowing electricity.

His eyes caught the wink of the sun, before it was covered with an oncoming car.

Night was official now.

Where else to go?

What better to do?

Fuck it.

Feeling one last snippet of the heavy breeze, he stepped inside the nightclub, music already blaring in the background, voiced already laughing and celebrating, and the future waiting for him to catch up.
