Starring: Vincent Pembridge

Pro-Wrestling Illustrated September Exclusive - The Apparent Death Of cWf? [2]
Reported by: Andrew Hallaskan

The transcriber, Jonathan Bayden-Shinawi, went on to note down the ensuing battle between the two. At the time, security officials were already being mobilised, but it seemed Andrew Jonah Brooks actually WANTED the match/fight to pan out, as he believed that it was better than the original plan.

This led to the two Japanese workers getting mighty furious that they'd been weaseled out of their deal, and Levi Brooks was apparently forced to pay a huge amount in compensation, a factor which also led to the downfall of the cWf. The match itself was deemed to be 'very extreme' and hence, the reason Avarice never actually aired.

--

ACW Scorpion Title/thReat Fighting Title Fight -- Christian Embers vs Vincent Pembridge [c]
“FUCK YOU!” screamed Christian Embers as he roared down the ramp, towards the awaiting Vincent Pembridge. Christian Embers could not take it any longer… he could not stand the barrage of insults that Vincent Pembridge was dishing out. “SHUT THE FUCK UP!” hollered Christian Embers as the 1,849 fans roared behind Christian.

He quickly slid inside the ring and felt the blow of Vincent’s elbow in the small of his back. The main event was now starting… and it involved a person that wasn’t even supposed to be in the newly revived federation!

Vincent quickly began to work on the chronically weak back of Christian and began a torrent of stomps, rendering Christian Embers nearly paralyzed on the canvas. Vincent laughed as he kneeled down grabbed Christian’s long shoulder length hair and picked him up. “FECKER! YOU ARE A SODDING TWAT!” he screamed as he gave a roaring forearm to the back of Christian’s neck, sending his face flattening onto the mat. Vincent got up and took his ACW Scorpion Championship off his waist and gave it to the referee and picked up his thReat Fighting Championship. The fans booed as they saw him clutching it with malicious intent and saw Christian Embers rising.

*CRACK*

Christian Embers dodged the belt’s blow and ducked while sneaking in a knee into Vincent’s gut. Regaining his stance, he gave a quick low kick to Vincent’s calf followed by a thundering knee once more to Vincent’s gut. Nailing Vincent in the back with a forearm, he lifted him by his waist and drove him twisting to the mat like a Greco-roman suplex, without the bridge. Embers stood up and punched the air, while the crowd began the count in unison with the referee.

Vincent Pembridge shook his head and saw his thReat Fighting Championship on the mat beside him. Christian Embers, unbeknownst of this fact, picked up Vincent Pembridge only aiming to inflict more damage... and got clobbered in the face with the title! Sending him down...

And allowing the Scorpion to recover, as the count started up again;

ONE!

Vincent, surprised at Christian Embers’ tenacity, took up a mounted position and began to attack Christian’s face with mighty fists, not intending to show any mercy. He rose once again, waving his arms in the air, gloating at the fans in the packed cWf arena.

“LOOK AT YOUR HERO NOW TWATS!” he screamed as he turned around and picked up Christian.

Left jab.

Right jab.

Left jab.

DISCUS PUNCH.

Christian fell to the mat, dazed and unaware of the atmosphere. He clutched his hair as he tried to regain an ounce of reality… only to be swatted in the face again by the 'reality'... Vincent Pembridge. He could hear Vincent’s shrill voice mocking the fans. His blood boiled, but he could not rise.

“YOU ARE A CUNT! YOU WERE A CUNT IN THREAT AND YOU’RE A CUNT HERE IN THIS SHITE HOLE!” screamed Vincent Pembridge as he picked up Christian once more by his hair. Vincent then threw Christian to the ropes, his eyes gleaming.

“GET READY, FECKER!” he screamed as Christian came off the ropes...

YAKUZA KICK!

A DUCK UNDERNEATH THE FEET!

OFF THE PARALLEL SET OF ROPES... SPEAR! SPEAR! SPEAR!

Christian Embers dove his shoulders sinking into the back of Vincent and felt Vincent fall down in pain. “FECK!” screamed Vincent as he landed on the mat.

“AHHHHH!” screamed Christian as he pounded in his chest with his two fists, beating them red and full of fury. He picked up Vincent and swiftly struck him with a palm shot to the face sending his wobbling to the ropes. He followed suit and whipped him off and slammed the returning Vincent Pembridge with a tremendous returning arm slam. He began to pounce on his weakened foe and began to kick him with his heel meticulously, aiming for all the weak spots in the human body.

Solarplexes.

Kidneys.

Neck.

The nose.

Christian could feel why he loved fighting all this time… it was the lust to make his opponent, especially a cocky son of a bitch like Tito Ortiz… or Vincent Pembridge, just be in…

PAIN.

Christian Embers salivated liked a mad man, his mouth foaming with the unmentionables from within his stomach, his eyes popping out like Jack Nicholson from Psycho, his veins emerging from his neck from what only appeared possible from cartoons.

Christian Embers was in THE ZONE. The Shoot Sensation was getting into the groove.

Fuck that, Embers was THE zone.

Christian ran to the ropes and bounced back to Vincent’s felled body and gave him a swift kick to the head and bounced off the opposite rope and came back with a shattering double knee drop to Vincent’s gut. He picked Vincent up and gave a vicious forearm shot to his face sending the British Degenerate to the turnbuckle. Christian lifted his fists like a boxer, tucking in his chin to protect his neck. He approached Vincent and gave him a body shot.

Right hook to the face!

Right forearm to the chin!

Right elbow to the cheeks!

Left hook to the kidneys!

Christian Embers was a machine. He could not be stopped. He reared back and lifted his right fist and lunged.

BLOCK!

Vincent Pembridge blocked Christian’s punch and then began a whirl of his own.

Right hook to the kidneys!

Left hook to the chin!

Knee to the gut!

Vincent sent Christian all the way to the ropes and whipped to the opposite.

SMASH!

Vincent screamed to the crowd as blood trickled from the side of his mouth, following Embers flying fist. He looked at the recovering Christian and scowled as Christian looked back with the same hatred. Christian spit on the ground and lifted his arms. “I’M OPEN MOTHER FUCKER! COME GET ME!” Vincent relented a furious, animalistic scream and began approaching strategically towards Christian, and vice-versa.

They were going for an ol'-fashioned tie-up.

Christian and Vincent grunted as they tested each other’s power. Christian then saw his opening… he dropped to the floor and jabbed his shoulder into Vincent then lifted him in a northern lights suplex… but the Shoot Sensation rolled through; flipping backwards and held onto Vincent’s left arm and head… he sat directly in front of Vincent’s body and began to torque Vincent’s neck and back using the arm and head like a half belly-to-belly suplex.

But this was Scorpion Rules Fight… Christian couldn’t win by submission! Christian finally realized his weakness in this match! No matter what, his submission skills wouldn’t win him the match! Christian let go and screamed in frustration and ran to the ropes and came back, shooting his shin and knee area to Vincent’s chest. He screamed in frustration once more as he saw his predicament in the match.

What did he have?

Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu taught the fundamentals of ground submissions.

About 30% of his skills had just been taken away.

And he knew that Vincent Pembridge knew.

He went back to Vincent and lifted his arm and began to kick the back of Vincent like a whirlwind. Kick by kick, he put more force and more emphasis into each, making the Manucian Predator’s back redder by the moment.

“TAKE THAT, MOTHERFUCKER!” screamed Christian Embers as he gave a final buzzsaw like kick to the head of Vincent Pembridge. His strategy for fighting Vincent Pembridge in this match had been just cut away. Christian Embers had to find another way to win.

Suddenly, he finally realized… this was not just a “no submissions counted” Fight. But also a “fuck him up with a weapon” Fight. Christian Embers had found his way of winning, and the former IOW superstar smiled.

He was going to bash Vincent’s brain into his ass.

Rolling out of the ring, Embers got himself a chair, with the crowd going wild. They were looking forward to their hero destroying the invader and the epitome of pure hatred, Vincent 'sodding' Pembridge, on his own terms. Scorpion Rules Fight? Christian was up, absolutely, up for it.

"FUCK HIS BRAINS OUT!"

"FUCK HIS BRAINS OUT!"

"FUCK HIS BRAINS OUT!"

Rolling back inside, the former Vagabond noticed Vincent slowly getting up to his feet and raised the chair above his head, getting ready to strike. However, The Plague From Manchester suddenly sprung into life, dishing out a Capoeira-esque move, courtesy of a tripod spinning sweep kick, knocking Embers off his feet and the chair down to the canvas.

Now, it was Pembridge's turn to get physical.

He sniggered as he pulled Christian up and headbutted him, instantly causing blood to flow from his opponent's nose. The crowd jeered, but watched as Vincent started to slug Embers, driving him closer towards the ropes. Appeared as if the Manucian Predator was gonna win the fight via ring-out... maybe he came to the conclusion that keeping Embers down for the 15-count would be quite impossible.

But as the two got closer to the ropes, and Vince reared back for the knockout blow, Christian jolted back to life. Blocked the punch, and started firing away with hooks of his own! Driving the crowd into a frenzy, which died as soon as six punches later, for The British Degenerate managed to block one of the punches and sent his right foot into Christian's gut, before elbowing him in the back of the head, sending him to his knees.

Christian Embers wasn't going to go down, however.

That didn't matter to Vincent.

*SMASH*

The crowd cringed collectively, as the Scorpion viciously kicked Christian in the face, as if he was kicking a football. Embers flew across the ring and his head slammed down onto the canvas with some impact, as a chant started up...

The Scorpion ducked easily and wrapped his hand around Christian's throat, the crowd growing uneasy. They had an idea of what was to come, and they didn't like it. Immediately breaking out into huge garbled cheering, the audience attempted to help their hero stir back to life and fight back.

It wasn't to be.

He had thought that enough was done to keep the animal known as The Shoot Sensation down, but Embers was obviously much tougher. On his feet, Christian turned around and found Pembridge charging at him, with the thReat Fighting Title looking to play a part.

Embers ducked and waited for Vincent to come back off the ropes, before superkicking him in the jaw. Didn't really knock the Scorpion off his feet, but at least it got the title out of his hands. Grinning, Christian kicked his rival in the abdominals, forcing him to double over... before underhooking the arms...

With a sadistic smile that spoke volumes of his intentions, the Shoot Sensation tossed the chair at Vincent, expecting him to catch it. That duly happened, allowing Embers to spring into action...

With a utterly wicked rolling cartwheel kick! Pembridge fell to the canvas, stunned, as a small cut forced itself open on his temple... how could it not, considering the velocity of Christian's kick, rebounding the chair into the Scorpion's face? Both men were up very quickly though, Vince worse for wear obviously. It was about to go downhill for him, as Christian retrieved his chair and with a fleeting glance at his staggering opponent...

So much for holding on to his leg; Scopes fell down to the ground, following a chairshot from the maniacal Shoot Sensation. Nobody was going to deny him the shot at ultimate revenge, and with that taken care of, Christian turned around to resume the annihilation of Vincent Pembridge, raising his chair up, to get ready to crack it over the Scorpion's cranium.

Scorpion Sting, ladies and gentlemen.

Magnified, because...

IT KNOCKED CHRISTIAN EMBERS OVER THE ROPES... AND OUT OF THE FUCKING RING! IT WAS ALL OVER! VINCENT PEMBRIDGE HAD STOLEN THE VICTORY VIA THE CHEAPEST WAY POSSIBLE, AND DEFENDED BOTH HIS FUCKING TITLES!

*television feed cut off here*

-----

Mr Vincent Pembridge has claimed that he bears no responsibility for the apparent eventual failure of cWf to even see the light of day, as network officials -- as stated earlier -- deemed the first Avaraice main event too 'gory' for their liking and wouldn't allow it to air on television. The show cropped up on the Net a few days later as a webcast, but by then, internal strife in the company had reached new heights and it seems cWf is all but dead.

Truly a shame, as cWf was touted to be a new force in the industry.

RIP.
