FTW: One Man's Random Thoughts
Starring: Rune Winters

Fuck PIW.

Fuck aWc. Fuck Jim Johnson. Fuck Jonathan Conspiracy. Fuck Sylo. Fuck KroW. Fuck Sharc. Fuck Jamal Wilson. Fuck Chris Mezz. Skullfuck L.i.T. Fuck Nathan Daniels. Fuck everyone who loves PIW. Fuck everyone who can't let the place die.

I'm not done yet.

Fuck Graphic Violence. Fuck him in his ass without telling him first. He thinks he's a better character than I am. Motherfucker, I'm not fucking make believe. This is my fucking life. This is how I am. I don't have an on-camera persona you little bitch, and if you 've got something you wanna say...

Do something.

Call me out. Somebody, fuck, anybody go for it. I'm asking. I'm fucking begging one of you shitheads. Step up to the plate and swing for the motherfucking fences. But you had better make sure I don't get up, because if I do I'm gonna fuck your day up.

I'm tired of playing along. I'm tired of assholes telling me what I'm going to do and where I'm going to do it. I'm really fucking tired of the douche bags that think they can run a company just because they saw someone do it on T.V. You sons of bitches aren't Vinny Mac, so come off your fucking high horse and let me smack you back into reality. You overconfident shits keep putting me on the unemployment line. You fucks feed me your lines off bullshit and your plans for the future. I buy in and a few months later you close up shop when a challenge comes up. Why I should I wish you good luck in life when you destroy mine? Oh yeah...it was fun while it lasted right? Fuck you.

That brings me to the fans. The people that I always fight for. I put my life on the line for so they can cheer and clap and buy my fucking t-shirts. Here, have a foam finger directly up your asses.

Rune's #1!

You want me to sing and dance for you too? For a price I guess I'm willing to do anything. That used to be me. I'm Jerry Macguire screaming "show me the fucking money!" Want to know what you can do with your money now? Where's the last place you saw your foam finger before it disappeared?

Bottom line...fuck the world and I hate all of you.
