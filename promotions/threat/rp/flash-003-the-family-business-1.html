The Family Business [1]
Starring: Resch Royale

The golden embers of a fire shimmered across the walls of a well lit room. It was late, very late. It was also cold outside; snow crept down from the heavens and spread itself across the vast fields of rural Germany. This was the countryside, the getaway. After all, the Royales had many houses. They were one of the most successful families in German history. There were trophies lining the walls, but the biggest one was spread out across the top of the fireplace's mantlepiece. It was a golden title belt reading "Papa Royale: German Wrestling Confederation Hall of Famer". This was their pride and glory: wrestling.

There was a nice, beaten chair sitting infront of the fire. In it sat the next heir to the Royale throne, Resch Royale. Resch had already wrestled in small German federations, gaining lots of underground respect. Resch had just broken through to the big time...this was his shot to leave the family name untarnished and prove himself. The Royale family, after all was the innovation of popular wrestling in Europe.

Resch stood up out of the chair and stood idle for almost 10 seconds, staring into the white falling snow outside the house. He pondered for moments, almost predicting his future.

"I must." He said quietly to himself.

"Vat vas zat?" comes a call from the kitchen.

"Oh nothing, Mama. I vas just speaking vith myself." Resch replied with a sense of embarrasment.

"Some-sing your faja never did do enough." She chuckled.

The snow left them trapped in the mountain retreat for just a few days, this was alright with Mama Royale, but Resch had business to attend to.

"Ven vill ze pass become open Mama?" Resch asked.

"Be patient Resch, ze snow vill die down soon" she was frustrated with his anxiousness.

Nothing would stop Resch Royale from achieving his dream, he needed to get to America.

"America, Mama. America. Just think about it." He said quietly.

"I know." she called. "You never cease to amaze your Mama."
