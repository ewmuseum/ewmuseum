Flashback [Part 2]
Starring: General David Sanders

It was go time.

I was on team red - which was arguably the more visible color in wooded areas - but I wasn't going to put every Hoffman chose against me. But, what was against me was that I clearly had the shittier team. You wouldn't call many of them my allies, more like blatant enemies. Plus two of them had sprained ankles.

We needed strategy.

I partially elected my self captain of the team. The Serg. probably wanted it that way because if we lost he'd blame it all on me.

Our team met.

"Alright," I spoke, leading the pack, "I know none of you like me, and I don't like you."

"Actually, I don't have anything against you." The soldier spoke.

"Wrong place to bring this shit up. Anyway, I'm no fan of pep-talks, but here's my best shot...

Work in unison. More fast and swift. If I see you flailing behind, I might fucking shoot your ass. What's that supposed to mean? Stay ahead of the game. If your taken fire, stay down and wait for assistance or blast your way out the there."

Sergeant Charles Hoffman heard us talking strategy; which was perfectly legal. He wanted to stop it.

"Alright Sanders, get your crew out on the battle field. It's time."

I looked up at him as we were all huddled down. We were each wearing a big red cover-shirt with our typical camou pants. "Yes, sir."

We all got up and started walking to our base on the other end of the two mile training field.

One of my teammates, the soldier that apparently didn't have anything against me, approached me.

"Hey private, did you really mean that stuff back there?"

"Fuck it." I said.

I cocked the the gun back.

"Every man for himself."
