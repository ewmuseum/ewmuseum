Chapter V
Starring: Ø

"Story of a Strongman"
Page Five (Of Five)

"I know you made the right decision," Salvatore said, continuing his congratulations by shaking Churchill's hand. His cherubic face gleamed with satisfaction, and Wolfgang seemed touched by the moment as well. However, his well of emotion quickly ran dry. The moment was losing its effect. He let go and dug his hands in his pockets, not knowing what to do next. Sal easily translated his discomfort and soothed him with a smile.

"Have a seat, because what I'm about to say has the power to sweep you off your feet."

Wolfgang Churchill obeyed, taking a seat near the desk. Sal, too, sat down and folded his hands in his lap.

"Because you are a Belfonte now, I must give you three gifts. We usually call them "The Gifts That Keep On Giving," but I don't really give a shit what you care to call them. We choose each gift according to your life status at the time of joining. Since you are not a blood relative, you won't get the same quality or quantity of things I give to my immediate family. I'm sure you understand, right?"

Wolfgang nodded and gave Belfonte a glare that said "Get on with it." Feeling a bit more rushed, Sal hurriedly went back to business. He quickly pulled out a yellow tablet, which was covered with writing and sat it on his lap. He dug some reading glasses from his suit coat pocket and steadied them on his large round nose. He began to read.

"First, I entitle you, Wolfgang Churchill, the sum of --- Oh, before I go on I must tell you that these gifts are non-negotiable, and you're basically stuck with what you get. I'm sorry, but that has always been a personal guideline of mine."

Churchill buried his forehead in his hand; the suspense was killing him.

"First, I entitle you a sum of 65,000 U.S. dollars to help you get back on your feet."

"With that kinda dough, he can do a helluva lot more than that," Santino muttered beneath his breath. He glanced over and playfully sneered at Wolfgang, who returned a similar expression. Salvatore slapped his note pad with scorn, making Santino jump in the process and wish he had not been so disrespectful.

"More will come later, of course, as spoils for a job well done. You must accept the second gift with my deepest apologies. The construction of your house is taking a little longer than we had expected. I hope that our guest room will be a satisfying place of rest for the time being."

Wolfgang choked mid-swallow and began hacking on the liquor that remained in his throat. He quickly lifted a cocktail napkin and wiped away the residue that had formed on the corners of his lips.

"Did you say house?" Wolfgang asked hoarsely, as if the 65 thou was only chump-change. To him it was, because he knew Sal was worth a couple thousand times that. Whatever the case, Churchill sat in utter amazement at the Don's generosity. His choosing to join the clan would have been a lot easier had he known about these 'gifts' beforehand.

"Oh, and there's one last thing," Salvatore Belfonte said with an excited smirk. "Santino has told me you wanted back in the wrestling business. I've talked to Troy Mason over at thReat Wrestling, one of these outfits, and he has agreed to give 'O-with-a-slash' a home."

Churchill was ecstatic, and he burst from his seat. Not knowing what to do next, he sat right back down and anxiously tapped the table. He couldn't remember a time when he was more grateful and excited. He had a chance for redemption in the industry, and he wasn't going to take it for granted. He immediately began thinking about training regimens and other things that would give him the same kind of edge and freshness he displayed in Rival. He looked up and quickly noticed that the Don's facial expression had dimmed considerably.

"Perhaps you shouldn't get so excited yet," Belfonte suggested grimly, "Because I already have a job for you and Santino lined up. But in this time of celebration, I'm willing to allow you a week or two to settle in. Does that sound reasonable?"

"More than reasonable," Wolfgang said while standing up. Sal got up from his plush chair and the two embraced tightly. "Thank you again, Sal."

Sal read the affection in his voice and smiled. It was only a matter of time before the Bertoulli family recognized their error in downplaying the Belfonte Family.

"Go on, get out of here."

Wolfgang Churchill did just that, his jubilation refusing to fade. Father and son remained, taking a seat and planning out 'what would happen in two weeks.'

"So you really think we oughta unleash the animal on Bertoulli's boy this soon?" Santino asked before taking a long quaff of his drink.

"Oh, most definately. What better way to welcome back the Prodigal son than to throw him a punching party? They have disgraced us by running their dirty mouths like faucets. Such careless flow has lead to an enormous debt, a debt which we must now collect." Sal declared plainly while staring blankly into the distance.
