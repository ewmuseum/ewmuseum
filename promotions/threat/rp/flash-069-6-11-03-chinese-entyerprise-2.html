6-11-03 Chinese Entyerprise 2
Starring: Born To Die

Knock, knock!


"Herro? Chinese food derivery!"
Nothing.


"Herro?! Pork fly lice!"
Still no answer.
"Chinese food derivery fo' Stephen a-Martin! Sum yung fuk an' side ordah fuk em yung!"


SinFyre couldn't help as a short blast of laughter escaped his lips.


"Go, go, go! Nobody home!" ordered X-Ploder, one half of Born To Die.


His younger brother SinFyre, born in the states, quickly started picking the lock. He inserted long, thin silver or iron wire rods into the lock, one at a time, but he didn't seem to be trying to jiggle the handle open or gig the lock.


"Hully up, dummy!", whispered X-Ploder in a harsh, hurried tone. "We get busted, we outta dis place, FAST!"


"Chill out big bro, " said SinFyre as he leaned over a door knob that just happened to be connected to the dressing room of Generative Clash. "One, two three, POOF!"


They both turned their heads at the same time as the magnesium strips that Sin had inserted into the lock sparkled and blazed with a maddening fury. And just like that, with a long wisp of blue smoke rising from the now melted door knob, the job was done.


X-Ploder kicked the door in quickly and entered followed by SinFyre, holding a butterfly knife in his hand. When they looked around and realized no one was in, X started scouting out the place while Sin put away his blade. With expert precision he twirled the knife around in his hand, too fast for the eye to follow, closed it and inserted it into the inside pocket of his jeans jacket. Sin ran his hand through his black and orange dyed spiky hair.


"What we lookin' for, bro?"


"Anyting, eh? That cow Zoe don' wanna pay up, we gonna show here BTD don' mess aroun', and NO ONE mess aroun' wit' da BTD!"


"Fuck that bitch!", said SinFyre, flailing his arms in a macho show of aggression. "She slapped me in the face on FLAW, man! I don't want no money, no nothing from here.... except," Sin whipped out the butterfly knife again and twirled it open. "Heh heh heh, a little fun!"


"Money first, fun later, eh? Look fo' anyting worth money, fuck up da rest. You bling da paint?"


Sin reaches into his left jacket pocket and pulls out a can of spray paint. He shakes it and the clacking noise of the paint mixing seems almost ominous, like the rattle of a snake.


"They don't call me Vincent Van Chow for nothing, huh? Ha ha!"


The chaos begins as X-Ploder and SinFyre rip through the lockeroom, destroying everything that isn't bolted to the floors. They rip up clothing, smash the chairs to bits, empty out all the lockers and spray everything with paint. X-Ploder takes a piss in the corner while Sin tags up on everything.


"BTD! Born to Die!", yelled SinFyre as he threw up a quick piece on the wall in red.


"Ey! Lo! Look at this, huh?", said X-Ploder as he zipped his fly up.


Standing in the middle of a room completely destroyed and vandalized, X and in looked down at a little black book, thrown in with the trashed mess that had come out of Zoe Antoinette's locker. SinFyre picked it up and opened it and read a line or two, then flipped through the the book, scanning pages quickly.


"Ha ha ha! Hee hee hee! That bitch is gonna get hers now! Ha ha!"


X-Ploder looked at his brother, excited but unsure. "What it is, huh? Tell me!"


"Aaaahahaha! Secrets Ricki baby, secrets!"


Sin dashed out of the room laughing with glee. X followed him, making sure no one had seen them leave. The room left behind them was all that was left of their presence. And it would be a presence felt soon enough.
