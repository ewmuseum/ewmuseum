The Long Screw Job: Part 1
Starring: Jesse Ramey

We have dreams and admirations, which we would love to live up to one day. I had dreams like those once before, but the hands of one man, of whom I will mention later on in this story, shattered them.

I’m not your typical athlete, actually quite the extraordinary one. I’ve been trained in every aspect of the ring and my psychology is the best in the game. You could easily put me up against anyone from any wrestling promotion that was in the so-called “Main Event” and I could hang on their every move, nipping at their heels with every turn.

There is no doubt in my mind that I would have been one of those that are talked about from far and wide, if I had only used my head a little bit and not stepped foot into the arms of the enemy that I have come to hate so dearly.

My career has only grown over the past ten years of my life, and my fan base has continued to grow tremendously. Only that man, held me down in the rankings, so to speak.

Cyberspace Championship Wrestling, a time period in which I hate to look back on. This was a time when I was to the point of just not caring anymore and experimented a massive amount with a certain thing that we in the business would call “gimmicks”. We’ve all got one in this game, well, unless you’re just a loser and have nothing better to do than job matches.

My time in CCW was great in the beginning, I was taking on the names and the guys in the blue suits didn’t care. I had come in looking to take names and kick some butt, right off of a great run in a former rival of the CCW in Just Electrifying Wrestling, and they were hyped to have some of my unlimited potential.

Then came the day that a man who I had helped train came in and was willing to do anything to screw me over to take my spot. Who was this man, you may ask? A youngster by the name Cianan, he stills wrestles in a place called Old School Wrestling; a place I once did as well, and the place where the dark memories begin.

This on the other hand was the beginning of the long three years that I would call my “Screw Job Era”. Cianan had worked himself up in the rankings of CCW you see, and was offered a position as the Commissioner of the place, which we all know can go to our heads when we have the power of the company sitting on the tips of our fingers.

My last match in CCW ended in a way that was far beyond my comprehension, as I had just finished the Compton Street Fight with another CCW worker of whom I had much respect for, Blayze. When out of nowhere came the enraged Cianan, and pushed me off a bridge.

Now, we all know that anything that happens in this business like that is completely fake and scripted. I wasn’t hurt at all; in fact, there was the stinking balloon mattress thingy at the bottom of the bridge waiting for me. Now, the real question behind all of this is why did it happen?

I was pushed out of the scene because Cianan didn’t want me getting to the top before he did and he made the upper room sign some stinking contract saying that I was unable to wrestle anymore and that I needed to be taken off of the CCW network shows. Which indeed came about and I was left sitting out the duration of my contract with CCW at my home, then in Calgary, Alberta, Canada.

Long story made short. That was the beginning of the Long Screw Job.
