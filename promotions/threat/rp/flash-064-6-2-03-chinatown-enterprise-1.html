6-2-03: Chinatown Enterprise 1
Starring: Born To Die w/Johnny O

The rain came down hard and fast on this stormy night, as two figures stood under a big black umbrella, trying desperately to stay dry. They had crowded in under the awning of a small Italian restaurant, whose rose tinted windows let out a mysterious light. The candles on the dinner tables inside added to the small eateries ambiance. But the rain fell short of making the night anything but miserable.


As the two figures huddled under their umbrella, looking very dissatisfied with their situation, a black Lincoln Towncar rolled over to the curb. The driver ran out and opened the door to the back passenger side, and a man exited, dressed in a black rain cap and long black trench coat The driver popped open a large umbrella, taking his cues from the other man's movements. He took one step toward the restaurant and stopped short, the driver walking ahead of him by mistake.
He then ran back to recover the man with the umbrella. Under the rain hat, Johnny
O smiled mischievously, although there was a little bit of nastiness in that grin.
He liked playing games with people. He was about to play another, it seemed. But on who?


Johnny O walked over to the two young men standing grimly under the awning. They apparently hadn't dressed for the weather.


"What are you two guys doing? Why aren't you fucking inside? It's shitty as hell out here!"


"They don't let us in, boss.", proclaimed X-Ploder.


"Fucking Italian asshole waiter said we weren't dressed appropriately. Mother fucker. I should go in there and blast his ass!", yelled SinFyre with scorn written in his eyes.


Johnny O smiled. He liked the fact he was in control. It wasn't a power trip. More control, less problems.


"C'mon, let's go inside. I hate fucking rain."


"Me too, boss.", said X-Ploder. They walked in, Johnny O snapped his fingers, and before you knew it, they were seated at a nice table. A waiter was pouring out a bottle of red wine. Olympus tasted in, nodded, and the waiter filled Sin and X's glasses. The young Chinese didn't look right inside the fancy restaurant, with orange streaks in their hair and dressed down. SinFyre picked up the candle holder and played with it, the heat coming off the hot glass holder not seeming to bother him in the least bit.


Johnny O raised his glass to the two young men.


"Gentlemen, congratulations. At MALEVOLENCE, you made people look up and take notice. You got talent, you got style, you got a great gimmick... the shit looks good. Here's to the future."


They all raise their glasses in a toast.


"When we get paid, boss?", asked X-Ploder.


"Ha ha ha! Paid? Slow down Ricky, slow down. You'll get paid. Don't worry about that at all."


"Hey man, we NEED the money.", said Lo Ting-yi aka SinFyre. "We got debts to pay off and shit."


"Guys, you just worked a pro-wrestling PPV! Pay-per-View! You're gonna get a nice cut! And so am I. Just wait till the numbers come in and we'll all see a nice check from Mason, or Pembridge, or whoever, I dunno, I don't care. That's not the point anyway. The point is, you guys got a SHOT! This is tHreat! You know what that means?"


The two young Chinese men shrugged their shoulders.


"Guys, this is the BIG TIME! Well, is some people's eyes it is. But you're working in a whole different territory then what I have worked in before. Fertile ground for CYDONIA Incorporated, my wrestling agency. I picked you, Born To Die, as the premier tag team of the company, to represent CYDONIA Inc. in tHreat, to a whole new audience."


"Yeah, but when do we see the green, man?", asked SinFyre, hell-bent on his payoff.


"Man... what the fuck? Didn't you hear me? I said, you're gonna get paid as soon as the numbers come in. Is that all you think about, the money? Smarten up Lo,
FAST! I know all about your past, running against some Chinese street gang, the
Shadow Dragons or whatever. I know that you got some fucking stupid ideas in your head. All I am telling you guys is this. No more gang shit. No more bullshit petty robberies and extortion and heroin and opium and all that crap. This is BIG TIME!
Real fucking money! Fame! Fame equates to power equates to money! You dig? DON'T...
fuck this up for me. I picked you, myself, and I am here MYSELF. I could have sent another manager. I could have picked a more experienced tag team. I chose
YOU! You don't let my ass down, dig it slappio's? Or you're gonna end up back on the fucking streets where your buddies the Shadow Dragons are gonna be waiting."


Johnny O snaps his fingers and a waiter comes over.


"I'm leaving. I assume you guys are hungry? If so, put it on my tab. Gentlemen... enjoy."


Olympus leaves. Sin sits back in his chair and folds his arms at his chest. The waiter serves both men menus. X-Ploder picks up his menu, opens it, acts like he is looking at it until the waiter leaves.


"Lo, order food."


"I'm not hungry."


"I AM hungry. Order food. No worry about money. We get our money. And I have idea. Little money on side, eh? Old school. Born To Die style. Ha ha!"


SinFyre smiles at his brother and sits up in his chair.


"Oh yeah? BTD style? Heh heh... now we're talking big brother."


Sin takes the lit candle on the table and with his index finger and then presses down tightly on the wick. The sound of sizzling flesh doesn't seem to bother Sin much at all. Neither does the smoke rising from his blackened finger tips. The scene fades as the candle light diminishes.


"Born To Die style. heh."
