Getting Out The Dark
Starring: Torrance Smith

“ Monsters come and go, they find a pray have there way with it then move on, you can’t stop them, you cant talk to them, you just go along with them. You just want to stay alive…” The voiced whimpered from the darkness of a corner. The street lamp seemed to avoid this corner, true hatred was in that corner and no one knew it, the world was in an abyss to the hatred lurking around one corner, they would find out soon enough.
“An unpleasant sensation occurring in varying degrees of severity as a consequence of injury, disease, or emotional disorder. That’s pain, injury pain will slowly decrease, and disease will be drugged out your system. Emotion, Emotion pain you can’t get away from it, you can’t forget about it, and it haunts you. Wherever you go the pain follows you, you sleep it there, you run its there you fight it’s there. You cannot lose emotion pain!” The voice roared out the corner, it seemed to change emotions every sentence, could you blame it? For what it has said is true, yet people can be so self-indulged and cause this pain.
The streets were cold; the icy-wind flew through every crack every gap. “He's in my dreams, he is in my mind, and yet he always has someone else in is mind. Why do I waste so much time with him in my mind! He is like the wind, he gets every where you don’t want him to, you cant get rid of him and you can’t stop. I know, I have tried and failed.” So much pain is stored in this thing, so many emotions locked up inside her, all her emotions seem to be released in her words. Each word has a hidden message, each sentence a hidden meaning, each paragraph a piece of her life is revealed.
It seemed her voice, her emotional voice she was a lady. A lady who has gone through a lot, you can just tell. Her words, show her fear and anger, and her hidden appearance shows she is ashamed of herself, it isn’t hard to release all of that, its just simple breaking down her actions and what you can tell from them. “ Men, the chauvinistic Lucifer’s of this world. They deserve to be rotting in hell, as there skin melts down there body and there blood evaporates into the damp moist air, that what the deserve. It will teach them show respect and learn when to stop!” She has deep emotional trauma you can tell by that sentence, she has thought about that before. Men don’t all deserve that, she may have met the bad one or two but there all different no man is the same, but she has this tuck in her mind and I don’t think she is going to let it go. If you feel someone so strong why stop?
“Hatred, a natural emotion, a feeling of dislike so strong that it demands action. Action how can you act against someone when you’re being pinned down? How do you fight back when they’re on top of you? How do you act to them when you’re groping you everywhere? How do you act when you have just been raped?” The hatred has no been revealed, the hatred comes from deep inside her. She was raped; the love for men has been devoured by the hatred for men, all because one man couldn’t keep his hands to himself.

People don’t know the effects of rape. The rapists do it for the thrill, they don’t have the looks or the emotions to make the girl fall in love with him, so they take it in there own hands, they decide I will have my way with her and then go. All men want is sex they get so desperate they rape and these are the normal ones. Some people do it because they enjoy the lady being in pain hitting them fight screaming they enjoy the woman going through pain. They’re sick people around, no wonder this lady has such hard feelings towards men, if I had had gone though all that I would hate them too.

“He had no right, I hadn’t provoked him, I hadn’t spoken to him and I hadn’t even seen him. Yet some how he thought he had the right to grab me put his grubby nails in my arms, rips my clothes he had no right. I cant sleep, I have the dreams of him coming back running after me, each time I run away he just gets closer and closer I cant get away from him!” We have all had chase dreams but chase dreams mixed with reality is something I don’t want but she has to live with.

” I have hid in the shadows for long enough. It is time… Time for me to show my face to the world and get my face know to the world for what happened and all the anger inside me because of it!” It was her time, she was going to show herself to the world…
