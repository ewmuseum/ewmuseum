WWF News

2002

WWF News WWF by Ditch 6/7/2002 Smackdown did just a 3.3. Looks like Edge killed himself for nothing. On ByteThis, Vince McMahon essentially said that the WWF's focus right now is on finding the next 'innovation' or 'direction', that will increase ratings and interest long-term. Scott Steiner will work an entire upcoming New Japan tour, which will be his first extended time in the ring since last year. The Ultimate Warrior is quite entertainingly insane. Here's his first commentary in two years: http://www.ultimatewarrior.com/ww.06.06.02.htm Edge might be able to go without surgery, but he's out at least a month regardless. Mike Awesome and Kanyon are healthy. After they work off the ring rust, they'll be back. Kane is still 2 months away from returning. King of the Ring card is shaping up thusly: HHH vs Undertaker, WWF title Austin vs Guerrero Angle vs Hogan X-Pac, Booker T, Jericho and Val Venis are in the King of the Ring semis; probably going to be X-Pac vs Booker and Jericho vs Venis Hurricane vs Jamie Knoble, Cruiserweight title 6/6/2002 In a recent interview, Joey Styles explained about the offer he rejected from the WWF. Since he lives close to WWF headquarters, he was willing to do voice-overs and pre-taped segments as a part time job. They wanted him to tour full time, and do things 'the WWF way'. Since he's currently earning more than they offered him, he turned them down. The new NWA promotion run by the Jarretts is steadily scooping up the vast majority of old WCW stars, as well as indy phenoms like AJ Styles and Low Ki. It appears that they're going for broke on signing talent, despite the highly risky concept of doing weekly PPVs. Vince McMahon will do a 'State of the Business' address tomorrow afternoon on the WWF ByteThis webcast. 6/5/2002 It appears that things will finally go to how they were originally planned for the split; Raw will focus on 'entertainment' (looks like more puke on the way) while Smackdown wil focus on 'wrestling'. Goldberg has once again stated that he's not interested in signing with the WWF. 6/4/2002 No plans for Shawn Michaels to wrestle. Shawn appears to have 'calmed down' backstage. The last time he was at a TV taping, he upset many with his attitude, so now he's going to try and tone down his flamboyancies. Based on the building feud with Austin, it looks like Benoit will stay on Raw once he returns to action. Raw jumped up to a 4.1. Yay! Some in AOL/Time-Warner are saying that Goldberg ISN'T free to sign with a wrestling company. Hmmmm. 6/3/2002 The Tough Enough finalie landed a 2.8 rating. Take THAT, Ozzy! King of the Ring will be like normal, only with wrestlers from both feds. Qualifiers start on Raw. 5/31/2002 Raw, despite being the best in AGES, only got a 3.7. Hopefully next week's rating will be improved. Also, the holiday might have damaged the rating. Smackdown, which was ALSO really good, only did a 3.3. COME ON PEOPLE, they had two **** matches this week! Watch or we'll never see Edge vs RVD for the WWF title! While busting his ass and bleeding in the cage, Edge popped a shoulder out of its socket. This may require surgery, and will probably bring an early end to his push (see also: Kane). The two women (Linda and Jackie) won Tough Enough. In other news, Vince McMahon had been sniffing markers for several hours a day when he decided against taking Jake. The WWF is pushing hard to sign Goldberg, but he only wants to talk to Vince so that he can try and work the best deal possible while ratings are down. Talks with Scott Steiner have slowed, since they're afraid of giving him a long-term deal given his health. It now appears that his back is bad after only having done a few matches this year. In indy news, on June 15th a promotion called MLW (www.mlw.com) will debut, and hold an 8-man tournament for the promotion's world title at the ECW Arena. The participants are La Parka, Vampiro, Bam-Bam Bigelow, Taiyo Kea (of All Japan), Steve Corino, Shane Douglas, Jerry Lynn and Chrisopher Daniels. Joey Styles will commentate the show, and no less than myself will be in attendance. Good ticket prices, too. The next few Heats will see several tryouts for announcing. I think this week is Jackie. In other news, the Heat that will air on Sunday will get a 0.1. Fit Finlay, having turned Trish into a watchable wrestler, will turn his sights on Mark Henry. Good luck on THAT uphill battle, Fit. On the list of those scheduled to work house shows in preparation for a spot on TV: Jamie Knoble, Shannon Moore, Shelton Benjamin, Rob Conway, John (Prototype) Cena, Ron Waterman, Sean O'Haire and Victoria. Works for me. Finally, to close out this monster of a news update (how come it all waits for Friday?), Jim Ross says that soon enough, Smackdown is Mysterio. Amen to that. 5/29/2002 The new Velocity/Confidential combo, which has replaced Excess, did about double Excess' average rating. Whooo! RVD is very happy, saying that the ladder match was the first "ECW" match he's gotten to wrestle in the WWF. Maven broke his leg at Smackdown tapings, but finished the match. He'll be out around 2 months. The WWF will run house shows in Hawaii, and while there will meet with All Japan. All Japan is currently looking to cross-promote, as the other three major Japanese federations (New Japan, Noah, Zero-One) are all cross-promoting heavily. Don't expect much, since All Japan doesn't have much useful talent at the moment; more likely, the WWF would send some developmental wrestlers there for experience. 5/28/2002 The WWF has finally split the writing team into two distinct groups, one writing Raw and one writing Smackdown. The reason for the move is to try and make each show somewhat unique. Brian Gewertz will be the head of the Raw writing team while Paul Heyman and Michael Hayes will head the Smackdown team. Stephanie and Vince will oversee the overall product. Fit Finlay can be thanked for the improved wrestling in the women's division. Apparently, Tommy Dreamer did in fact ingest Undertaker's tobacco juice. In a related story, Mick Foley was (probably) heard to say, "Man, I'm glad I never had to do anything insane to put over Undertaker." WWF security is taking heat for allowing a fan to run in during the RVD/Guerrero match. No word on how the Benoit turn will be played out. 5/26/2002 Smackdown got a 3.6. Given that the season finalies are over, and Hogan's "retirement speech", that's a disappointing number. Tough Enough 2 wraps up next week. GO JAKE~! Velocity, which airs in the former first hour of Excess, had a 15+ minute Kidman vs Tajiri match. This qualifies it as the best hour of WWF programming this year. Let us pray that this marks the start of a trend. 5/23/2002 Despite getting the feud with Guerrero that he wanted, Austin isn't that happy with how he's being used. X-Pac went as far as to quit before Nash talked writers at Raw into re-writing the nWo segment. Undertaker suffered a hip injury in the opening segment, but still insisted on working the main event. There finally seems to be significant momentum behind getting a new head booker to replace Stephanie. Vince Russo will soon be doing a book about his time behind the scenes in the WWF. Raven will stop being an announcer because he wants to wrestle while he still can. Bradshaw's push is over, in case you couldn't tell. Chyna got squashed by Joey Buttafuoco in their Celebrity Boxing match. 5/21/2002 Raw got a VERY low 3.7, and Heat got an even worse 0.9, both of which point to a drop in overall fan interest. Austin wants good wrestling matches, so he pushed for a feud with Guerrero. Attaboy, Steve! RVD's false title win was done to judge fan reaction to him winning the WWF title. This will determine if he has a PPV feud with Undertaker. Nash and X-Pac threatened to quit due to how the nWo angle was being booked. This led to the segment on Raw, where Nash 'addressed the troops'. 5/20/2002 "British Bulldog" Davey Boy Smith has died at age 39. It is believed that he died of a heart attack. 5/13/2002 Ken Shamrock has apparently signed a 1 year deal with the new Jarrett family run NWA promotion. 4/3/2002 "I've read where some people say I jumped over the entire talent roster into a top spot. That's a crock too. I've been in this business 15 years. I've been a main-event guy and I was kind of damaged goods for a while, but I took six months off and I'm back, and that's all there is to it. I'm putting my money where my month is. A lot of guys think they know what they're doing, and the fact of the matter is, they don't really. They don't know how to draw money. And until they get there, they need to shut up." - X-Pac, on WWF.com, reminiscing about all those times he drew money and people wanted to see him. That is to say, that one week back in 1997. 4/1/2002 "I don't condone its use. But I understand its place. I don't have a problem with people who take steroids. It's a great medicine for people who need it, and it has a place in the world just like any other medication. If a doctor feels that's what you need to improve your health or life and puts you on it, then that's great. However, if you take it without a prescription, then it's just a drug like any other drug, and you shouldn't take it. It should only be given by a doctor." - HHH on steroid use.
