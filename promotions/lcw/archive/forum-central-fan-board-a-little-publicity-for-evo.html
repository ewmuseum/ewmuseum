LCW Central Forums LCW Central Forums Home | Register | Help | Search | Profile Announcement: 0 LCW Central Forums : Central Fan Board : A little publicity for Evo () Poster Message Wyatt Level: Head Booker Registered: 04-15-2002 Posts: 53 IP: Logged A little publicity for Evo As you may or may not know, the fans Wrestling organization (fWo), which is largely considered by many people in e-wrestling to be the best e-fed on the 'net, has been around for five years, and is largely considered to be the first big angle fed out there, has been running house shows of late. In-character, the story is that Fox dropped the Sunday Slaughter program due to waning ratings, and the fWo went on the road between then and when their new television contract with USA begins (June 10th). As such, they've been traveling the country and putting on three to five house shows a week. The May 28th show came from Philadelphia, and Jeremy, the guy who runs the fWo, and I had discussed possibly using some Evo talent as "local talent" to get the crowd hot for the fWo portion of the show. Last night, that became a reality, and nine members of the Evo roster appeared on the house show.
