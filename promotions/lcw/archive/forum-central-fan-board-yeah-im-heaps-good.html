Still I'll take what is given. 05-15-2002 at 08:37 AM | Quote Reply Dirge Level: Merc Wrestler Registered: 05-09-2002 Posts: 12 IP: Logged Re: Yeah I'm heaps good EVEN THOUGH I LOST TO DIRGE? Give yourself some credit, man. I think you're a great rp'er. Its not all about wins and losses. You put in an awesome effort, and people recognize that. Besides, I doubt if those punks even know who Dirge is. Consensus: Me and Brimstone both enjoy your character, and both think you're kickin' ass. If this helps, I don't think Dirge won a match between November and February that wasn't a tag match. I'm just now starting to make a name for myself besides being "that goofy evil guy". Keep up the intensity and quality level of your promos, take wins and losses in stride, and you'll get what's due. Although I'm a hypocrite, Dirge didn't have a win for so long that when I started to get them I gloated like MAD. Working on that. But NEVER, E-E-E-EVER base the value of your character on win/loss ratio. Hell, in the lifetime of DIRGE (many years, ask Brimstone, he keeps better track) Dirge loses WAY more than he wins. But he manages to get the few important matches, and I'm very glad of that. Its all about developing the character and caring about it when the chips are up or down. Not like you needed a lecture, just that sometimes it helps to know other people go through similar experiences. Keep it up, man. Ya got nothing but respect from me.
