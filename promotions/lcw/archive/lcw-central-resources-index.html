LCW Central:: Where It All Comes Together Resources Menu Move Database: Not sure what a move from one of our shows is? Just looking for more moves to complete your application? Here is the place, where you can find finishers for current wrestlers, as well as sections dedicated to: general moves, submission holds, pinning combinations, suplexes, and powerbombs and drivers. Roleplay Tips: Stuck in the mid- or low-card in this or another fed? Tired of losing all your matches and being the laughing stock of your fed? You've come to the right place! In our exclusive Roleplay Tips section, let the cream of the crop here in LCW offer you some constructive pointers on improving your roleplays and you'll be at the top of the card in no time! Title Histories: Here you can find every champion to ever hold a title in LCW, from its inception in November of 2000 until the split in September of 2001.
