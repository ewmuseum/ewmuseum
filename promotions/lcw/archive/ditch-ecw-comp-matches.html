ECW comp matches

2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
-Rey Mysterio vs Juventud Guerrera, ECW '96 (ripped from DVD), 2 of 3 falls. This is only slightly better than the average Undertaker match. Heh. Flawless video and audio, 140 megs, AVI format. -Tajiri & Whipwreck vs FBI vs Crazy & Kash, Guilty As Charged 2001. Best match on ECW's last PPV. Very fast pace, tons of neat spots and high-end moves. And kicks to the face, can't forget that. AWESOME quality (thus the large size), 124 megs, AVI format with DIVX codec. -RVD vs Jerry Lynn, Hardcore Heaven '99. This is THE match they've had, at least in terms of historical value. A king-sized spotfest, both of them take an insane amount of big bumps and have some killer sequences. Warning: there's a LOT of 'nothing' between some of the sequences and spots. Excellent quality but tinny audio, 69 megs, WMV format. -Malenko vs Eddie Guerrero, ECW Hostile City Showdown. One of the better matches in a classic series that helped define ECW. Some slow parts, but when they're going it's great. Very good quality (it's '95 ECW, can you expect more?), 61 megs, ASF format. -Benoit vs Snow, from ECW's Double Tables. The best Al Snow match ever, by a mile. See just why he's running Tough Enough. A tiny bit below very good quality, but still watchable; 60 megs, WMV format. -Michinoku Pro 6-man tag, ECW Barely Legal. Spectacularly fun match, much like a smaller-scale version of the 10-man tag listed above. Most of the guys in this match had WWF contracts within six months. Gotta love ECW fans popping for workers they've never seen before. Excellent quality, 58 megs, ASF format. -Mike Awesome vs Masato Tanaka, November to Remember '99. These two CAN NOT have a bad match. Awesome ruled when he had the mullet. Excellent quality, 54 megs, WMV format. -RVD vs Jerry Lynn, Guilty As Charged 2001. The last ever televised ECW match. Takes a while to get going, is spotty, and isn't as good as their '99 matches. You still need to see it. Also features the finish of the last ever ECW title match at the start. Excellent quality, 53 megs, ASF format. -RVD vs Bam Bam Bigelow, 4/4/98. The start of RVD's push, and Bigelow's best match ever. RVD busts out some phenominal dives from the ring into the crowd. Excellent quality, 44 megs, ASF format. -Sandman vs Sabu, Stairway To Hell, House Party '98. A grueling ordeal. Sick spots, blood, Sabu wrestling half the match with a broken jaw. Both men are *on* here, hitting everything and piling layers of intensity on as the match progresses. Excellent quality, 46 megs, ASF format. -Tanaka & Lynn vs Awesome & Credible, 7/29/98. Plenty good tag action, with lots of cool rrademark moves and ECW's first Awesomebomb from the ring through a table (I think). Excellent quality (taken from DVD), 46 megs, WMV format. -Tajiri vs Psicosis, ECW 8/20/00, ripped from DVD. THIS MATCH RULES ALL! Tajiri's best match ever, and Psi's best match from a technical standpoint. Killer, innovative mat work; crisp, athletic sequences; stiffness; nearfalls; great crowd; pacing without being slow. Yes, it's all that and more. GET THIS MATCH! Great quality (the ECW DVDs are amazing), 42 megs, WMV format. -Cactus Jack vs Mikey Whipwreck, Foley's ECW Farewell. Not really that great from a technical standpoint, but both guys bump like there's no tomorrow. Excellent quality (ripped from DVD), 38 megs, WMV format. -RVD & Jerry Lynn vs The Impact Players, Heatwave '99. Credible gets out of the way, and thusly this match RULES YOU. Excellent quality, 37 megs, WMV format. -Mike Awesome vs Masato Tanaka, 12/31/99. Yet another great match from these two. I wish the WWF would bring in Tanaka. Excellent quality, 34 megs, WMV format. -Taz vs Tajiri, Heatwave '99. Lots of suplexes, and lots of kicks to the head. You figure out who does which. I don't understand the finish, but it's still a nice match. 32 megs, ASF format. -Mike Awesome vs Masato Tanaka, 12/24/99. You know the score. Excellent quality, 30 megs, WMV format. -Mike Awesome vs Masato Tanaka, Heatwave '98. The first of their five matches in ECW, and it set a great precedent. Excellent quality, if grainy at times. 28 megs, WMV format. -Rey Mysterio vs Psicosis, ECW 9/16/95. Their first time on US TV. Not the blowaway match that they had at Bash at the Beach '96, but still, it's Rey with his original knees so you know it's good. Excellent quality, 27 megs, ASF format. -Super Crazy vs Ikuto Hidaka, 12/24/99. Hidaka is a hidden gem, and he and Crazy mesh very well. I miss ECW so much. A great match-to-megs ratio. Excellent quality, 27 megs, WMV format. -RVD vs Taz, November to Remember '99. Taz was on his way to the WWF, but before leaving he put over RVD. Not spectacular, but some nice suplexes from Taz to go along with the usual goodness from RVD. A touch below excellent quality, 24 megs, WMV format. -Tajiri vs Jerry Lynn, 1/14/00. You know exactly what you're getting, and if you don't then you need to get it that much more. Excellent quality, 23 megs, WMV format. -Tajiri vs Super Crazy, 12/31/99. Just about a squash for Tajiri, as he kicks Crazy in the head about a dozen times, has time to taunt the crowd, and still wins. Works for me! Excellent quality, 21 megs, WMV format. -Tajiri vs Jerry Lynn vs Super Crazy, November to Remember '99. Not as awesome as it COULD be, but come on, can you possibly go wrong here? Excellent quality, 20 megs, WMV format. -Super Crazy vs Ikuto Hidaka, 12/3/99. Hidaka wins over the ECW crowd very quickly. I'd love to see what these two could do given five to ten more minutes to work with. Yet another superb match-to-megs ratio. Excellent quality, 19 megs, WMV format. -"Superstar" Steve Austin promo, from '95. This is Austin's entry into ECW after getting canned by Bischoff. Hearing him say that he's "the biggest potential superstar in wrestling" is uncanny, and helps cement just how badly WCW screwed up in getting rid of him. Steve's still got hair here, along with all the charisma and intensity one expects from his serious promos. Excellent quality, 15 megs, ASF format. -Mikey Whipwreck vs Tony Mamaluke, 6/2/00. Tony lands headfirst on the guardrail, then takes a crucifix into a spinning sit-out pedigree (see: really really nasty). Mamaluke has a death wish. Excellent quality, small screen size; 6 megs, RealPlayer format.
