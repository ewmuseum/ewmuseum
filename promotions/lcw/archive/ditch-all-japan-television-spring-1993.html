All Japan Television Spring 1993 Specs: 6 hours 30 minutes, Excellent quality. Ditch's Description: First off, I can only get in 6 hours. You can choose to take it out of the start or the end. Thankfully, the best isn't in those portions. Next, THIS TAPE ROCKS! There's lots to love in here. Japanese commercials, **** matches, RVD, an intense beating, and perhaps the stiffest match ever. I can't recommend this tape enough. What's On The Tape: -Lots of clipped/meaningless matches. None of them suck, some are worth pointing out. -Misawa & Kawada vs Steve Williams & Terry Gordy, Tag Titles. Misawa and Kawada's last big match as a team. This is the meat of what you'll miss if you opt to skip the start. Match is dominated by Williams and Gordy, whose offense I really don't care for. Match is above *** , but not by a lot. -Misawa/Kikuchi/Asako vs Akiyama/Taue/Ogawa. Plenty good 6-man tag. Kikuchi is great throughout the tape, and really brings a spark here that isn't normally seen in All Japan (namely, face-in-peril). -Kawada & Kobashi vs Stan Hansen & R...V...D! Rob Van Dam, incredibly green, is still amazingly athletic. He busts out some moves that I've never seen before from him, and does a REALLY KILLER lariat sell. One wonders how fast RVD could have progressed had Heyman not constantly suck him in spotfests with or against Sabu. -Hansen & Spivey vs The Blackhearts (in masks). One of the short matches worth highlighting. Hansen lobs a chair at one of the jobbers, and busts him open hardway in the back of the head. You can see blood pooling under the mask! -Kikuchi vs Fuchi, lightweight title. I LOVE THIS MATCH! Fuchi methodically crushes Kikuchi, while Kikuchi keeps refusing to stay down. There are 9 backdrop drivers, some with a lower angle than others, but that's still more head drops than I've ever seen anyone take in a match. Awesome. -Kawada vs Hansen. Another awesome beating, but this goes both ways. Not much technically, but Kawada and Hansen just pound the crap out of each other more than I've ever seen anyone else do. Watch as they totally haul off with kicks to the back. Lots to love. Now we move into matches from Champions Carnival. -Williams vs Gordy. Solid power match between the tag partners. -Kawada vs Taue. Lots of face kicking. -Misawa vs Kobashi. ****+ , match of the tape. -Kobashi vs Kawada. **** , give or take 1/4*. You can't go wrong with these two. -Misawa vs Gordy. Shock finish. This is the main thing you'll miss if you opt to skip the last half hour.
