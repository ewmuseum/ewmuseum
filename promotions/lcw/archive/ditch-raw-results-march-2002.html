RAW RESULTS by Squiggy 3/25/02 -- State College, Pennsylvania Linda McMahon starts the show by talking to all the WWF superstars from Stanford, via satellite, and says tonight's plans on the draft. Tonight, only 20 picks will be made. The rest of the wrestlers' places will be chosen in a lottery on WWF.com after Raw. Because of the WWF Title match tonight, neither Triple H, Jericho, nor Stephanie can be picked as these first 20, and Stone Cold is a free agent because of a contract stipulation. Match #1 -- Mr. Perfect vs. Tazz This match got the Lance Storm-treatment.... Perfect scores with a Perfect Plex early on, but Tazz is able to hook his free arm around the ropes to stop the count. Later on, Tazz locks in the Tazz-Mission. Perfect almost grabs the bottom rope twice, but just an inch to far away and taps. Winner -- Tazz -- McMahon's First Pick -- Wow, they got a podium for this... Vince chooses the Rock, being as he is the best non-free agent available right now. Rock doesn't seem to happy at this and leaves the back to come to the ramp across from Vince. Vince gives Rock some new rules: Rock can't touch Vince, no more "shoving things up people's asses" phrases, and no more saying "It Doesn't Matter." (Just for the record, I actually LIKE what Vince is saying, for once.) Vince is about to leave, Rock starts his shtick. Since this is going to be Rock's last Raw, he wants to go out with a bang. Rock then has one side of the crowd say, "You are," and the other say, "an asshole." -- Flair's First Pick -- Flair actually picks the Undertaker, and the dead man flips out in the back, showing that he was obviously bluffing about wanting to be with Flair. In Vince's "War Room," Angle comes in and bitches about Vince not picking him first. Undertaker then barges in and bitches that Vince promised that Flair would not pick him. Taker then threatens Vince. Match #2 -- Edge & Diamond Dallas Page vs. Christian & Booker T Page scores with a Diamond Cutter to Booker after dodging an Axe Kick, but Christian stops Page from covering and gives Page a falling reverse DDT. Edge takes his brother to the outside, and the two fight as Booker connects with an Axe Kick and wins. Winners -- Booker & Christian -- The Next Few Picks -- In Vince's War Room, Angle convinces Vince that Flair doesn't want the pick the nWo, so Vince makes his second pick Kurt Angle. Flair comes out to the podium again, and makes his second pick the nWo!!! He gets them all as a collective unit, meaning he gets Nash, Hall, and X-Pac all in one pick. Back in the War Room, Vince is pissed at Angle now, but Angle tries to calm Vince down and tell him to throw Flair off his game now. Angle tells Vince to pick a man that he knows Flair has been talking to and a man that is about to come back. Vince thinks this is a good idea and makes Chris Benoit his third pick! In Flair's War Room, Flair explains to the nWo that he picked them so he can boss them around, and not Vince. Flair then makes his third pick Kane. Match #3 -- Trish vs. Ivory Trish connects with a handstand-to-top rope hurricanrana, and then scores with the Stratusfaction for the win. Winner -- Trish Vince comes out the podium and makes Hulk Hogan his forth pick. Then he does some Hulk poses... After the commercial, Flair comes out and makes his next pick Rob Van Dam... Vince is pissed that Flair now has the I.C. Title. Angle says he can get it tonight. All Vince has to do is make a match between Angle and Van Dam. Vince likes the idea. In the back, Hogan walks up to Rock and soon they decide they should face the nWo in a 3 on 2 handicap match since it's their last Raw. Vince comes out and makes his next pick the Tag Team champions, the Chuck'n'Suck'wit'Rico Connection. Match #4 -- 3 vs. 2 Handicap Match -- nWo vs. Rock & Hulk Hogan Rock scores with the Rock Bottom to Nash. He tag Hogan and goes to lock Hall in a Sharpshooter as Hogan leg drops Nash, but X-Pac breaks up the count. The nWo start to beat down on Rock and Hogan, but when X-Pac breaks out the nunchuks, Kane's pyros go off and the Big Red Machine head to the ring. The nWo are soon forced from the ring, but since no weapons were used by them, the nWo wins from Kane's interference. Winners via Disqualification -- Hall, Nash, & X-Pac In Flair's War Room, Vince walks in and the two start to fight about stealing people from each other. Flair picks Booker T, and Vince picks Edge. Flair picks Big Show, and Vince picks Rikishi. Flair comments that Rikishi should stick his ass in Vince's face again, and Vince pushes Flair and leaves. Match #5 -- Jeff Hardy /w Matt & Lita vs. Billy /w Chuck & Rico Jeff scores with a 360 degree tornado DDT and goes up top. Chuck tries to stop Jeff, but gets kicked away. Billy dodges the Swanton. As Matt and Chuck are fighting on the outside, Lita gives Rico a Litarana off the apron. Billy yells at Lita, and Jeff comes from behind him and rolls him up for the win. Winner -- Jeff Hardy -- 3D Divided -- In Flair's Room, Flair picks Bubba Ray, and Vince picks D-Von from his War Room. The half-brothers shake hands and hug for maybe the last time Match #6 -- European Title -- Rikishi vs. William Regal(C) Before Regal can even get down the ramp, Brock Lesnar runs in the ring and completely destroys Rikishi! First with a clothesline. Then, with a Spinebuster-ish slam, and lastly with that fireman's carry-to-reverse Russian leg sweep! THAT WAS COOL! Squiggy's NEW Favorite Wrestler/Badass -- Brock Lesnar -- Last Picks -- Vince comes out to make his next pick.... and he picks Brock Lesnar!... BUT it's not Vince's pick... so Flair picks Brock! GOOD PICK :). So Vince picks a different powerhouse, Mark Henry. Flair picks the European champion Regal, so Vince makes his last pick the Hardcore champion, Maven. Flair makes his last pick Lita. Van Dam chases him, smashes Angle's head in the stairs, and tosses him back in the ring. Van Dam goes up top again, but when he goes for a jumping martial arts kick, Angle pulls the referee in the way. Angle scores with an Olympic Slam and locks in the Ankle Lock, but the referee calls for the bell because of that top rope kick. Angle looks for an Olympic Slam, but Edge flips over him and Spears Angle. Edge punches Kurt until referees come to break it up. Winner via Disqualification and STILL I.C. Champ -- Rob Van Dam Main Event -- WWF Title -- Chris Jericho vs. Stephanie vs. Triple H(C) During the match, Jericho and Stephanie start fighting, making this an actual triple threat and not just a handicap in disguise...Jericho applies the Walls on Triple H, but Stephanie breaks it up. Maybe he could of spent time with Stephanie so their marriage wouldn't have fallen apart, and maybe he would of been man enough to really impregnate her. Stephanie doesn't know about that. She says Trips always came up short in that department. Triple H's music hits and The Game comes down to the ring. Triple H comments on his 'shortness' by saying, "Even a 747 appears short when you fly it into the Grand Canyon." Triple H challenges Jericho to a non-title match since he has nothing else to do this night. Kurt Angle walks to the ramp way and says Vince McMahon has just given him a shot for the Undisputed Championship for later tonight, since he was 'robbed' of his title shot last Monday. Match #4 -- Women's Title -- Mighty Molly vs. Jazz(C) Molly catapults Jazz into the ropes. Molly goes for a cross body block, but Jazz ducks and scores with her Fisherman's Brain Buster. After the match, Jazz beats on Molly more, locks in a STF, and then gives Molly a DDT. EMTs and other officials, including Arn Anderson, rush to help Molly. Winner and STILL Women's Champ -- Jazz After the commercial, Molly is still getting helped as Undertaker's music hits. Taker punches down Anderson. Taker continues to beat him down until he's bloody. Taker tosses him into the ring and gets a mic. Taker says this is all Flair's fault because he rejected Taker's offer for a match at Wrestlemania. Taker punches Anderson some more, and then boots him the face before leaving. Match #5 -- Undisputed Championship -- Kurt Angle vs. Chris Jericho(C) /w Stephanie Jericho connects with an enziguri that knocks Angle to the outside. Kane appears from the crowd. Kane press slams Angle back into the ring as Stephanie has the referee distracted. Jericho scores the Breakdown and wins. Right after the match, Kane enters the ring and clotheslines Angle over the top rope. Outside the ring, Kane tries to nail Angle with a chair, but Kurt takes refuge by fleeing through the crowd with Kane chasing him. Winner and STILL Undisputed Champ -- Jericho During the Undisputed Championship match, Flair arrived. Earl Hebner soon told him that Anderson had been taken to the hospital, and Flair gets back in his limo, probably to go to the hospital to see Anderson. In the back, Hulk Hogan makes more of a fool of himself by talking to a cardboard cut out of Rock. He basically tells the cut out that he's going to win at Wrestlemania. -- Another Visit to The Friendly Tap -- For those that don't know about the Friendly Tap, it's a bar owned by Earl Hebner (or at least one of the Hebners. I can't remember which one owns it.) that the Acolytes have been doing skits in for the past couple years whenever the WWF would be at Providence for either Raw or Smackdown. This time, it's "Men's Night." Faarooq and Bradshaw think that just means that drinks are cheap. It turns out that "Men's Night" means it's a night that men party in drag. One of the drag queens wants to dance with one of them, but they refuse. Billy and Chuck sneak behind them. Bradshaw gets tossed into a pinball machine and beaten with a pool stick as Faarooq gets tossed over a rail and has a beer bottle smashed over his head. Main Event -- Mr. Perfect vs. Steve Austin (Perfect FINALLY gets a main event...) It's pretty much one sided at the end, with Austin giving all his moves to Perfect and ending it with the Stunner for the 1-2-3. Who cares, though, because Perfect actually got to main event for once! After the match, the nWo beat on Austin. They tie his legs around a ring post. Hall gets one of the cinder blocks that he has been carrying around in that wheelbarrow and nails Austin in the knee with it. Winner -- Steve Austin
