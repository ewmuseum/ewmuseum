"You Need This Tape" by Blue Thunder

2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"You Need This Tape" by Blue Thunder Specs: 6 hours, VG-EX quality; some of it is just how backwater several of the federations involved are. Ditch's Description: There's a whole lot of stuff from a whole lot of places; something for everyone, and then some. What's On The Tape: -Honma vs Yamakawa, 6/20/99 & 1/2/00, Big Japan Deathmatches. Some really insane stuff. First match is sub-par video quality, but the violence still gets through. -2 matches from Revolution Pro, which is a C-level US indy fed. Cool moves used at times, including an inverted fireman's carry into a spinning inverted michinoku driver. -Misawa & Akiyama vs Kawada & Taue. ***** tag match from '96. -Whipwreck & Tajiri vs FBI, 8/26/00. One of the most innovative and advanced tag matches I've ever seen; maybe the best ECW tag match ever from a technical standpoint. -Japanese streetfight. This is VERY VERY VERY long, and the most bizarre wrestling-related thing you'll ever see. Five no-name Japanese indy workers wander around beating each other up in a shopping plaza. There are cheerleaders, a marching band, onlookers, several parts where you'll THINK it's overy but it isn't, a totally random match in a crappy ring at the end... trust me, you won't see this anywhere else. -Christopher Daniels vs Michael Modest, three matches. The camera work isn't the greatest, but these two are good enough to make up for it. All the matches have length, build, big moves, nearfalls, etc. -Crazy Max (Cima/Suwa/Fuji) vs Dragon Kid, Tokyo Magnum & Saito, 1/31/99 elimination match. One of the defining matches from Toryumon, this really shows a lot of what is expected from the federation. Lots of cool moves, slight amounts of US-style interference (awh), and plenty of crowd heat. -La Parka & Silver King vs Damien & Ciclope, Mexican Hardcore Match on Nitro. Quite insane. Damien takes a SICK SICK SICK powerbomb onto assembled chairs that don't give for the finish. And he manages to not die. Wow. -Lucha libre singles match. Eh, it ain't bad. -Mutoh vs Tenryu, Triple Crown, 6/8/01. These two are WAY up there in age, yet bust out a killer match. Mutoh bumps like a madman, including a head-drop snap German suplex off the top. ****+ . -Minoru Tanaka vs. Takehiro Murahama, 4/20/01. A lightweight shoot-style match, for those of you who enjoy such things.
