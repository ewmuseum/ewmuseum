Mission Statement

LCW - Lethal Championship Wrestling LCW Mission Statement The stated goal of Lethal Championship Wrestling, an electronic wrestling federation under the joint control of the individuals named at the end of this document, can be most simply expressed through the following tenets: :: We at LCW stand, first and foremost, for a commitment to quality. Not only do we aspire to bring you the best-written and best-wrestled shows on the Internet, we constantly seek to incorporate the freshest storylines in order to keep the content on the cutting edge. :: Our second goal is to bring you the highest-quality shows without sacrificing timeliness. In other words, when a show is scheduled, it will take place within twenty-four hours of the scheduled day and time. At the same time, should a situation arise that prevents us from finishing and putting out a show on time, we will be honest with you, only asking that you remain patient with us in return. :: Our third goal is, outside of shows, the most important to the continued success of LCW. We strive to create an honest relationship with each one of our members, whether it is via email, instant messenger, or some other means of interpersonal contact. Through experience, we've found that honesty and professionalism go hand-in-hand with creating a successful, handler-friendly environment, which is necessary in order to promote growth and loyalty in any relationship, and especially important in the world of e-wrestling. :: Our final goal is to fairly judge and provide criticism on all roleplays, regardless of the wrestler's position in the LCW hierarchy and/or card. Should a member of LCW ask for feedback, the entire booking committee will respond promptly, honestly, and without bias in order to help our roster develop into the elite group that we know that we can cultivate. By achieving these four objectives, we feel that we can reach our ultimate aim: to become the premiere e-federation on the Internet today. We can honestly say that we consistently present the best-written shows in the world of e-wrestling, both in terms of grammar and diction and also in terms of wrestling content. A dedication to learning move names and studying world-wide styles has allowed us to perfect match writing, and by distributing the workload amongst our many talented writers, we can confidently maintain, and yes, even improve, the quality of our shows. All we ask from you in return is that you maintain your commitment to us, meet our roleplay guidelines faithfully, and hang on and enjoy the ride! Thank you, The LCW Booking Committee [name withheld] David Ditch [name withheld] [name withheld] [name withheld] [name withheld] All Images and content © 2000-1 LCW Entertainment, Inc.
