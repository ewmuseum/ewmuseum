J Cup 2000 Specs: 3 hours, Excellent quality. Ditch's Description: Not much that's awesome, but loads and loads of goodness. What's On The Tape: -I'm skipping the lame matches. You'll know them when you see them. There aren't many. -CIMA vs Ricky Marvin. Start off with the good stuff! Marvin does a move called the Muscle Buster, which those in LCW will know as the Purge. Short but sweet. -Sano vs Suwa. Sano does a HUMONGOUS German suplex on Suwa, then a flawless tiger suplex to finish. -Men's Teioh vs Usuda. Some intense, stiff stuff from these two. -Curry Man (Christopher Daniels) vs Onryo. Good, heavily carried by Daniels. -Great Sasuke vs Kaz Hayashi. Kaz does a great tribute to Benoit, and they get close to **** . -Liger vs Tiger Mask IV. Very intense, lots of crowd heat, right around **** . -CIMA vs Onryo. Onryo finally holds his own by firing off a TIGER DRIVER 91. -Gran Hamada vs Ricky Fuji. This match is all about the finish, a super tornado DDT. -Great Sasuke vs Sano. Sasuke does some nice acrobatics and then takes a HUGE ass-kicking. -CIMA vs Sano. Quality match with quality nearfalls. -Liger vs Gran Hamada. Very heated, as Hamada goes into 'badass' mode for the first time that I've ever seen. -Liger vs CIMA, finals. **** , built nicely off of previous matches. Good build and pacing.
