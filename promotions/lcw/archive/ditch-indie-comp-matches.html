indie comp matches

2002

-AJ Styles vs Donovan Morgan, ECWA Super 8 2002 Finals. There's a lot to love about this match, but I'll just run down a list of some of the cooler moves. Shooting star plancha, kryptonite krunch, twisting swanton (!!!), release northern lights suplex into a corner, fisherman's spinning neckbreaker, second rope short powerbomb, and five of the coolest faceplants ever. Whooooo! Excellent quality, 58 megs, WMV format. -Low Ki vs Homicide, JAPW 7/7/01. Very grueling, hard-fought match. All kinds of coolness from Low, including a reverse swanton and a camel clutch/dragon sleeper combo. Match is a few minutes too long based on how little Homicide does towards the end, but it's still good. Excellent quality (for an indy fed), 56 megs, WMV format. -Low Ki vs Homicide vs Xavier, 8/18/01. Highly innovative 3-way spots to start, then it gets kinda slow in the middle before they start busting out the finishers. How cool is this match? The run-in is a BURNING F'N HAMMER~! Yeah, that's the stuff. Excellent quality, 41 megs, WMV format. -Low Ki vs Sabu vs Billy Reil, 3-way dance, 4/7/00. Reil doesn't get in the way for too long. Spotty but good. Excellent quality, 40 megs, WMV format. -WWA 6-man Cruiserweight Elimination, 2/24/02. This is an awesome match! Low Ki brings stiff kicks and a great off-the-top move, Shark Boy brings diamond dust, AJ Styles brings the world's coolest facebuster, Nova brings a snap head-and-arm suplex off the top, Mamaluke bleeds, and Christopher Daniels helps out on some phenominal spots. Oh, and Mark Madden is still fat; not on-camera, but you can hear it. Cameramen miss some spots on the outside, but the rest is all action. Low Ki looks so, so great. Excellent quality, 40 megs, WMV format. -Doug Williams vs Jody Fleish, 2/9/02. I know what you're thinking; "who?" But this match is KILLER! An easy ****, and it's the best match I've seen of the year outside Japan. Great, innovative psychology; great announcing to sell it; great highspots and bumps from Jody, including a shooting star plancha, an apron-to-floor ligerbomb, and a SUPER GERMAN SUPLEX. Get this match, you won't be disappointed! Excellent quality, 36 megs, WMV format. -Jayson Reign vs Tony Kozina, ECWA Super 8 round 1, 2/24/01. Lots and lots of indie coolness. Rana off the top to the outside, iconoclasm, perfect driver, and an inverted bulldog into last rites (aka roll the dice) are among the highlights. Most of the cool stuff is from Reign, in the shorts. Excellent quality but low tech, 32 megs, WMV format. -Reckless Youth vs Mike Sullivan, ECWA Super 8 round 1, 2/24/01. Lots of cool moves, including a SICKETY SICK SICK snap DVD by Youth. Only knock is that it's sometimes not so crisp, but what can ya do. Excellent quality but low tech, 31 megs, WMV format. -Low Ki vs Crowbar, 7/6/01. Crowbar brings some killer chops, Low Ki brings the usual bucket full o' goodness. Ki even busts out a PHOENIX SPLASH~, which is the single best top rope move ever. It's fairly clear by the end which of the two is more likely to wind up with a WWF contract in the future. Excellent quality (for indy), 30 megs, WMV format. -Eddie Guerrero vs Doug Williams, 2/9/02. Eddie can still go with the best of them, and here's the proof. A meaty technical match from England, with flawless transitions and just about every pinning combination known to man. Very, very much like his ECW stuff with Malenko. Good announcing too. Excellent quality, 28 megs, WMV format. -Christopher Daniels vs Suicide Kid, 8/7/98. Great little indy match. Highlight to me was the spectacular springboard tornado ace crusher (ace crusher = diamond cutter) by Kid on Daniels. Some odd crowd reactions, but they're all in good fun. Very good quality, 25 megs, WMV format. -American Dragon vs Spanky, ECWA Super 8 round 1, 2/24/01. You haven't heard of these two? Well, you should, especially Dragon (in the red). He busts out some MINDBLOWING stuff based on a double underhook. One blown spot early, the rest is quality. Check this out! Excellent quality (though low-tech), 25 megs, WMV format. -Low Ki vs Billy Fives, ECWA Super 8 round 1, 2/24/01. More ECWA indy lovin'. This time, it's all about Low Ki busting out a can of KICK IN THE FACE goodness that even Tajiri can't match, as well as the best rollup ever, the Gannosuke clutch. Fives, well, he doesn't suck. Low needs to be signed by the WWF yesterday. Excellent quality, 24 megs, WMV format. -Tajiri vs Low Ki, 1/26/01. Tajiri is fresh out of ECW. These two are the lords of KICKING PEOPLE IN THE HEAD, and this match is no exception. Lots of their usually kickass offense, including the PERFECT DRIVER~ from Ki. The announcing sucks, but the wrestling more than makes up for it. Slightly grainy quality, 23 megs, WMV format. -Donovan Morgan vs Jamie Knoble, ECWA Super 8 2002 Semis. Solid in every way possible. Lots of WRESTLING packed into 6 minutes, including a BACKDROP DRIVER at the 4 minute mark. Yeah baby. Excellent quality, 22 megs, WMV format. -Matrix vs Billy Kim vs Pinoy Boy vs Drunken Irishman, 10/23/01. This match is all about two moves from Kim. First is a HUUUUGE release German suplex on Matrix. Second is right around the finish, where he RIPS Irishman's head RIGHT OFF. Seriously, it's one of the coolest things I've seen in a while. A touch below excellent quality, 21 megs, WMV format. -Low Ki vs Minoru Fujita vs Xavier, 7/20/01. Low goes nuts, destroying both of them thoroughly. Geez, I have a lot of Low Ki don't I? Well, this is more of the same from him, plus the PERFECT DRIVER~! Excellent quality, 20 megs, WMV format.
