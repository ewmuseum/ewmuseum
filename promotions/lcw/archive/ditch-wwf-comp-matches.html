WWF comp matches

2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
-Bret Hart vs Shawn Michaels, Wrestlemania 12 Ironman Match. A bit on the slow side (by necessity), but both work in pretty much every move they've ever used in a match. This is the FULL match, along with Shawn's famous zipline entrance. Excellent quality, 143 megs, WMV format. -The 1992 Royal Rumble. 42 minutes of the match, 6 post-match. GREAT stuff; there's always action, lots of big names (Piper, Savage, Hogan, Flair, Sid, Slaughter, Sheik, Undertaker, Shawn Michaels), and the performance of a lifetime from Flair, who just never stops. That's even MORE emphasized by Flair's energetic promo after he wins. This match holds up well, and I think it's even more exciting than the most recent Rumble. Excellent quality, 127 megs, WMV format. -Steve Austin vs HHH, No Way Out 2001, 2 of 3 falls. Just about everyone calls this a ***** match of the year. I wouldn't go that far, but I will say that it's damn good. One file for each fall. Excellent quality, 124 megs, ASF format. -Shawn Michaels vs Mankind, Mind Games. Mick Foley's best match ever from a technical standpoint, something which he himself has attested to. Intense psychology from Shawn, including some nasty stomps to poor Mick's hands. Just a shade below excellent quality, 112 megs, RealPlayer format. -Steve Austin vs Bret Hart, Survivor Series '96. Austin's best match before the big face turn. If he'd worked half as heelish when he turned at Wrestlemania 17 as he does here, he could have actually gotten over. Opening is solid technical stuff, then it slows waaaay down. Last six minutes are all good to redeem it. Watch Austin bust out a Texas Cloverleaf. Watch it be called properly by the German announcers (yes, it's in German; no, I don't know why). Excellent quality, 101 megs, AVI format with DIVX codec. -The Rock vs Steve Austin, Wrestlemania 17. Outside the finish, this is a phenominal match. If you're a fan of either, and haven't seen it yet, by all means go for it. Even if you're not, still go for it. Excellent quality, 94 megs, WMV format. -Dean Malenko vs Scotty 2 Hotty, Backlash 2000. A lot of bests here; best WWF lightweight title match, best Scotty match, and best Dean-in-WWF match. KILLER finish, and way more than Scotty has ever done before. Sadly, the WWF never gave the division another chance. Excellent quality, 91 megs, RealPlayer format. -TLC 3, Smackdown 5/22/01. Throw in Benoit and Jericho to the usual three teams, and you've got a winner. Nothing groundbreaking, but come on it's TLC! Definitely better than TLC 2, and the Wrestlemania 2000 semi-TLC match. Excellent quality, 87 megs, WMV format. -RVD vs Jeff Hardy, Hardcore match from Invasion. Jeff's best match ever, and RVD's first WWF match (not counting '97). Lots of cool spots and RVD getting spiked on his head. Watch RVD get over in a single match and thus force the WWF to push him. Excellent quality, 80 megs, WMV format. -Benoit vs Jericho, Ladder match from Royal Rumble 2001. I don't think anything really needs to be said here. Excellent quality, 75 megs, ASF format. -Steve Austin vs Shawn Michaels, King of the Ring '97. Everything they do here is seamless after some oddness at the start. No real historical value, crappy finish, but still a meaty wrestling match from before these two got hurt. Their Wrestlemania 14 match was crap in comparison. Excellent quality, 69 megs, ASF format. -Shawn Michaels vs The Undertaker, Hell in a Cell, from Badd Blood. Best HIAC match ever by a lot, carried by Shawn but 'taker was trying too. Excellent quality, 68 megs, WMV format. -TLC 2, Wrestlemania 17. This is the one with the sidekicks running in to add new spots. Not as good as the Summerslam match because most of the big spots are repeats. That said, it's a TLC match, so you're legally required to love it. Excellent quality, 57 megs, ASF format. -Benoit & Jericho vs Austin & HHH, Raw 5/22/01. Best old-school style tag match I've ever seen. HHH blows out his leg but keeps going anyway. Excellent quality, 56 megs, WMV format. -Bret Hart vs Mr. Perfect, Summerslam '91. Classic technical match. Perfect is always at his best against Hart. Excellent quality, 54 megs, WMV format. -Undertaker vs Kane, Wrestlemania 14. A war of attrition, as Undertaker has to fight smart and wear down Kane rather than simply go through his usual match. Undertaker does an AMAZING no-hands tope suicida. File includes Pete Rose getting tombstoned, Undertaker's awesome entrance, and the post-match beatdown, as well as the match. Excellent quality, 46 megs, WMV format. -Taka Michinoku vs Great Sasuke, WWF Canadian Stampede. Both men's debuts in the WWF. Taka gets way over with the Canadians. A tad bit below excellent quality, 44 megs, RealPlayer format. -Mr. Perfect vs "Rocker" Shawn Michaels, 3/91. Well, gee, I wonder if this match is any good? Use your friggin' imaginations, people. Only flaw is a weak finish. Excellent quality, 42 megs, WMV format. -Mankind vs Hunter Hearst Helmsley, Cage Match, Summerslam '97. Good cage match, with some huge bumps from both and a great 'Foley lives his dream' story. Chyna interference gets annoying, but then again, it's pre-badass HHH so it's assumed. Excellent quality, 41 megs, WMV format. -Steve Austin vs Kurt Angle, Raw 1/8/01. The first of their 73 or so matches in 2001, and (in my opinion) the second best. At least better than two of their PPV matches. Weak ending, very hot crowd. Excellent quality, 40 megs, WMV format. -The Hardys vs Edge & Christian, Ladder match, first Raw on TNN. Total bumpfest that surpasses their first ladder match in '99. Why are the Hardys faces if Lita interferes in all their matches? Features THE SPEAR. Excellent quality, 40 megs, ASF format. -Steve Austin vs Owen Hart, Summerslam '97. Match is REALLY GOOD for the first ten minutes, then REALLY BAD up until the big historical moment. Owen does a fire thunder driver on Austin, nearly paralyzing Steve. After the match, Austin valiantly tries to walk to the back. Excellent quality, 39 megs, WMV format. -The Hardyz vs Edge & Christian, Ladder Match, No Mercy '99. The match that made these teams famous, and segued the tag division from the New Age Outlaws era to the TLC era. More cool spots and big bumps than you can shake a rainbow-dyed stick at. Again, excellent but slightly grainy quality; 32 megs, WMV format. -Benoit vs Austin, Raw 11/20/00. The first match that Austin actually *wrestled* in after getting his neck broken at Summerslam '97. Solid stuff from both. Amazing what Austin's surgery did for him. Excellent quality, 27 megs, WMV format. -Bret Hart vs Steve Austin, "I Quit" at Wrestlemania 13. Huge historical importance, and a great match to boot. Must-see stuff. Excellent quality, small screen size; 26 megs, MPEG format. -Rhino vs Raven, Backlash 2001. One of the top five WWF hardcore matches ever, as well as Raven's best WWF match ever. Not that either says a lot. Features the 'Rhino gores the shopping cart' spot, and some fun stuff with the steel steps. Excellent but slightly grainy quality, 26 megs, WMV format. -Bret Hart vs Mr. Perfect, KoTR '93. A little better technically than the Summerslam match, but not as hot a finish. These two do the little things so well as to make ** offense into a **** match. Excellent quality, small screen size; 25 megs, RealPlayer format. -Taka Michinoku vs Aguila (Essa Rios), Wrestlemania 14. One of the top WWF cruiserweight matches ever. Very, very spotty. Essa busts out a breathtaking CORKSCREW TOPE CON HILO that was probably banned for being cool immediately thereafter. Listening to the announcing, you'd think that there hadn't been two years of Rey Mysterio before it. Lawler insists on putting over Grandmaster Sexay the whole time to piss me off. Excellent quality, 23 megs, AVI format. -HHH vs Ultimate Warrior, Wrestlemania 12. HHH's punishment for the 'MSG Incident' starts here. Warrior does a hilarious sell of the pedigree, then runs through his usual offense for the win. Warrior looks like he has a George Hamilton level tan; that can't be healthy. I'd love to see HHH get jobbed like this today to Booker T. Hell, I'd love to see ANYONE job like this to Booker, I'm not picky. Excellent quality, 4 megs, MPEG format.
