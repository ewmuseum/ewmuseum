"Advice is like snow -- the softer it falls, the longer it dwells upon, and the deeper in sinks into the mind." -Samuel Taylor Coleridge Advice can come in many forms. Some good, some bad, some indifferent. To me, advice has always been a dirty word. I usually ignore any advice given to me. Mostly because I usually think I'm right. Heh. Point is that this is one of the few times I wish I'd taken some advice. I over-stepped my bounds on a few occasions in the last few months. First with Wyatt... then with the CCW thing... then with Dave. I wish I had listened to Dave when he told me I needed to be quiet. Or to Brock when he warned me to cool my tongue a little. Or even the huge warning Ben gave me when he walked out on us (for good reason). But I didn't listen to their advice... and spouted off even more. And I wish I hadn't. Not because I feel bad about bad-mouthing Tim Hall. He was being a jerk. Tim, you knew that post was going to cause problems... I'm sorry I fell into the trap you set. Not because I feel bad about an attack on Adrienne... because as I pointed out, that was not an attack. As illustrated by my apology for that. Not because I feel bad about telling Wyatt what everyone else was afraid to tell him. No... I feel bad because I'm no longer a member of LCW-Mercury. Mercury is easily the most competitive, and hard-working federation I've ever been in. Much love to everyone involved on a professional level. I loved every minute I spent there... every second I spent there. Except when I was getting yelled at. Which, I suppose, is my fault for not listening to advice. Not the point, though. My apologies to the men who are making The Oblivion what it is. All the hard work you guys have put in really shows... I've really loved my time here. But, as they say, all good things must come to an end. I said some things to Dave tonight that I probably shouldn't have. He was finally fed up with me. And so... off I go. Into the wild blue of wrestling federations. I'm going to go out on a limb and predict that no one in PTC is going to hire me... but I'll try to get on somewhere. Maybe I'll meet back up with some of you at some point. No hard feelings are harbored for anyone because, really, this e-fed thing isn't as important as it once was. So, bring on the comments about me leaving the LCW because I couldn't hack it. I've heard them all before, though, so be creative. I hope that despite what your characters say, you realize my devotion to Mercury, and my talents as a writer. But, if you don't, oh well. At least I've got my dignity. Goodbye everyone. Enjoy your e-wrestling careers. My AIM is [detail withheld] if you want to keep in touch. e-mail is [detail withheld] . Good luck to you all... and Slate... you frickin rule. ____________________________ I'm the guy you wish you were. That's right, Enrique Iglasius. Mmmmm...
