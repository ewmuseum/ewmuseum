All Japan Fans Appreciation Day 1992 Specs: 1 hour 45 minutes, Excellent quality Ditch's Description: Well, this is basically a one-match show for all intents and purposes. Said match is very unique, but I doubt that many of you will enjoy the entirety of it. This show is more of an oddity than anything else; worth a look once you've gone through most of the other All Japan shows. What's On The Tape: -Undercard stuff. Nothing to see here, move along. -8-man tag, four at a time, once three are eliminated from a team the match is over. The teams are SOOOOO lopsided, as there's Misawa, Kawada, Kobashi, and Kikichi against the already devalued Tsuruta, the ancient Kimura, the worthless Ogawa, and the underappreciated Fuchi. The format of the match is cool, but the execution leaves much to be desired. That said, there is something very special about the match. The first fall features Kikuchi taking the single most intense stretching I've ever seen in my life, mostly from Fuchi. There's a brutal STF variat, and some across-the-turnbuckle stuff that I doubt anyone else would be willing to take. To reiterate, I've never seen anything quite like it before in my life, and it's clearly THE selling point of the tape.
