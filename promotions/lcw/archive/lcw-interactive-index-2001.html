LCW - Lethal Championship Wrestling Interactive Menu Roleplay Board: This is the focal point of the LCW Universe, where careers are either made or broken. Because we base our storylines around the quality of our members' roleplays, our roleplay board is the lifeblood of LCW, and it's also an excellent place for new members and fans to get acquainted with LCW. We welcome fans to read the roleplays that are contained within this board, but you must be a member of LCW to post here. Out-Of-Character Board: This is where our members can cut loose and be themselves. Here is the best place for direct wrestler-to-administration feedback. Fan Board: This is the board in which fans and potential members can voice their opinions and ask questions, and they will most likely be responded to in a timely manner by the LCW Staff or other LCW wrestlers. Lethal Chat: This is the Official LCW Lethal Chat, where we will be holding regularly scheduled chat sessions (usually before and/or after shows), and it also makes a great place for members of a faction to get together and talk strategy. Fans are also welcome here, but your best bet at catching one of roster for a chat would definitely be during the scheduled chats. Alternate Roleplay Board: This board is to be used only in the event of the main board (hosted by EZBoard) going down. All Images and content © 2000-1 LCW Entertainment, Inc.
