LCW front page, July 2001

4 July 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
LCW - Lethal Championship Wrestling Most Lethal Wrestler Coming Soon! Affiliates Ditch Wrestling EWF Top 1000 IWA Top 1000 eWrestling - Top 100 eFeds Latest News [07.04.01] Tempest Tempest is up. Sorry for the slight lateness, but I was away at relatives' houses from 2:30 pm until 11 pm, so I hadn't had much time to work the HTML coding into it until now. [07.03.01] News! LCW Weekly is up, and in the effort to get the LCW website ready content-wise for LCW Version 3.0, we're making an effort to update all the sections on the site now in preparation for the new format. The Regulations section has now been updated, and everyone, including those who have recently applied to LCW, should read over them here . Also, we've posted the LCW Mission Statement, which is the standard by which LCW will be run from here on out, and you can find that here . Look for a new, easier-to-use Archive system for the shows either later today or tomorrow. [07.01.01] Roster Updates and Awards Scott Tibor and Jerry Cane have been added to the roster, and if you've been in LCW long enough to receive a nominations form, make sure you send it back to the fed mail as soon as possible, so that we can begin voting on the "First Half of 2001" Awards. [06.28.01] Tempest Card and Roster Updates The card for next week's Tempest is up, and you can find it to the right, or here . Also, we're completely moved to www.lcw-wrestling.com, and we've added Matt Henstock and Scott Wolfe to the roster. All Images and content © 2000-1 LCW Entertainment, Inc.
