All Japan New Years' Giant Series 2000 Specs: 2 hours 20 minutes, Excellent quality Ditch's Description: Not the best All Japan show ever, but you'll still get more goodness here than you would in any given month of WWF TV. What's On The Tape: -Vader vs Akiyama, Triple Crown. A match that any wrestling fan can enjoy. These two throw everything at each other, and you'll be blown away at the suplexes Vader gives and takes, including Vader's always killer release German suplex that launches Akiyama like a rag doll. ***1/2 , but a bit above that for sheer entertainment value. -Misawa vs Hase. If you don't like submissions, stay away. If you do, then you'll love it up to the finish. Hase assaults Misawa's right arm with a mindblowing level of tenacity and creativity. Then, Hase fires off a string of lovely head drop suplexes for nearfalls, and Misawa decides to ignore the arm damage. About *** , all things considered, but you could either love or hate the match. -The Burning (Kobashi & Akiyama) vs Vader & Smith. Pretty lackluster. -Kawada vs Kobashi. This is the stuff! Head drops, stiffness, crowd heat, and two different types of badass all in one nice package. One of the most vicious lariats you'll ever see is the finish. ***3/4 -Misawa & Akiyama vs Vader & Taue. Lackluster. -Team No Fear vs Kawada & Taue. ANNOYING.
