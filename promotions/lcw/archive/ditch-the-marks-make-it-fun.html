Make It Fun for the week of March 17th Disclaimer: The following opinions are [name withheld]’s and [name withheld]’s only, and he begs you to make up your own, and not make his opinions yours. WrestleMania X8 So I watched WrestleMania, as did many others from what I’ve gathered. And my sweet lord alive, I became a Hulkamaniac. Again. It happened in 99 shortly before Road Wild 99. I was there, front row, marking out for the red and yellow like I still do. Am I a sheep for Hogan? No. I’m a man that is respectful of the amazing legend that Hogan has created. Toronto, I thank you for not only taking us on an incredible journey during that match, but also being respectful of Hogan’s legend. Nostalgia trip my ass. Hulk made grown men Little Hulksters again, and Hulkamania was running wild. It was a moment that will always live in wrestling history, as it rightly should. As for the rest of WrestleMania? It wasn’t one of the best, but it was one of my favorites. Solid matches, just lacking the buildup. Am I mad at the WWF for this? No. They had one month, compared to near two months last year I believe. And of course, my favorite, HHH, winning the gold surely put a nice cherry on top for me. HHH. My all time favorite, hands down. No man was able to make me stand up and pose like them since Hogan until the former Terra Ryzing came along. It was a great match, don’t fool yourself. Great psychology, and the payoff with the Pedigree on Steph and Jericho was just awesome. If you don’t get why the Steph Pedigree was such a payoff, go back and watch WWF TV for the last two weeks. Some stories weren’t really told and I think that is why this one lacked the WrestleMania atmosphere we all got accustomed to last year. But remember, up until XVII, many WrestleMania’s didn’t have that atmosphere we all beg for now. It had the same look, and even more amazing actually. But I think things would have felt a lot more WrestleMania like if they had just given the stories more time to develop. But in the end, I still loved it. Saliva and Drowning Pool were lackluster, but you can’t have a WrestleMania without some kind of celebrities. The hardcore skits were a little too comical for a WrestleMania, and the crowd was spent after Hogan/Rock, making them kind of dead for HHH/Jericho. Many factors came into play as why it didn’t have the "feel" we all love, but don’t pass the buck to the wrestlers. They went out and gave their all. When was the last time you saw Hall move like that? Or Hogan take those kind of moves, with a broken rib no less? You want to be entertained, watch WrestleMania X8, and remember to tell your grandkids where you were at when Hulkamania ran wild, you were a Hulkamaniac again, and everyone was a mark. Except for Scott Keith. Brock Lesnar What can I say about this monster of a man. I’ve been looking forward to his debut since my gym teacher told me he got signed to the WWF. You see, I’m from North Dakota, and Brock has somewhat of a legend in this state. He is former state champ, and a state wide recognized name in high school and college wrestling. Many friends of mine are in awe of him, even the ones who don’t watch wrestling and admit to it anymore. I saw him at a SmackDown! back in June. I was amazed. The guy is a freaking monster like everyone says, and many got to see this for the first time. He picked up Rico Constantino and threw him ACROSS the ring from one corner to the other without breaking a sweat. After the show I went to Taco Bell with my friends. As we were ordering, a large man and a former Olympian made their way into the fine taco joint as well. That’s right, I ate tacos with Kurt Angle and Brock Lesnar. The man...is gigantic, and very gentle from what I could tell. But gentle in the ring, no. Watch out, WWF and wrestling fans, you have THE next big thing on your hands. And their is absolutely no way you can mess it up, trust me. Brock Lesnar is destined to be a star in the world of wrestling, and is nothing short of a phenom in the making. On RAW, many of you got a good look at the raw muscle displayed by the big man. When was the last time you saw Spike Dudley tossed like that? I swear, he died, TWO TIMES. The fans - in shock. Everyone was just jaw dropped. The select few in the crowd that knew of him were happy, and giddy. All I can say is thank god for OVW, so he didn’t become another Bill Goldberg. He has heart, and he has skill. He has everything he needs to be to become a future WWF World Champion, and most important of all, he has legit training and experience behind him. He is taking the same path as Kurt Angle, a WWF made product, from the ground up. And we all know how great Kurt Angle is... Until Next Time, this is [name withheld], Marking off. And don’t forget, Goldberg + talent + charisma + heart - ego = Brock Lesnar Make It Fun: Don’t Listen To Wrestling "Journalists" I’m not exactly new to this column writing thing, or this wrestling thing. I’ve been cheering on my favorites and booing the heels since 1989, when I was old enough to actually understand what I saw on the TV. And I still do cheer, but now I sometimes cheer the heels, and sometimes boo the faces. But I’m still a fan, even if I do happen to like the guy with a better workrate more than the guy who knows the kick, punch, powerbomb sequence that led Kevin Nash to multiple WCW World Titles. Or was that the booker? So are you a fan? Do you ever find yourself sitting on the edge of your seat when your favorite wrestler is on his back, one second away from a loss? Do you find yourself shouting at the TV, telling your wrestler to "Turn around! He has a chair!"? I find that happening every Monday, and one Sunday a month. Not Thursdays because my town doesn’t get UPN, so thus no SmackDown!. But what I’m getting to, eef you wheel, is that I am a wrestling fan. Not an insider, not a critic, but a fan. I’m the guy that you see biting his nails, front row every Monday. I’m that kid that got in fights in 4th grade because other kids said wrestling was fake. I don’t sit around and review ratings with my calculator, I sit around and watch Austin/HHH from No Way Out 2001, Benoit/Malenko from Hog Wild ‘96 (I was there, front row, I’m on the tape!), and Benoit/Angle from WMX7. Why? Because I love to watch good wrestling. Sure the angles are a little whacky at times, but aren’t we wrestling fans? I like marking out. It’s fun. It makes me feel all warm and fuzzy inside, because I know that I haven’t become jaded like some *coughSCOTTKEITHcough*. I hate people who try to tell me what I should and shouldn’t watch, and I hate people on the Internet that use their websites and opinion columns to attack the thing that is putting money in their pockets. Think about it, Keith and Ryder. And to those of you that claim to be a fan no matter what, quit bitching and appreciate that wrestling is on your TV. And to those of you who claim to barely stomach the shows, pick up the remote and change the channel, because I can barely stomach you. As you can tell, I don’t like Internet opinionists that harf on the WWF because they don’t cater to their needs. But I do like people who can get into the show and have a helluva time watching Kane/Big Show for the tenth time in one month. Once in a while I get kind of mad at the booking, but I realize that the people who do the booking are doing it because they know how to do it better than you and me. So I have a challenge for you. Watch Raw, or SmackDown!, and attempt to have fun. Get into the matches, and hang on every wrestling move like they are throwing around your mother. Until next time, this is [name withheld], Marking Off. Next Column: How To Have Fun Watching A 2000 Nitro
