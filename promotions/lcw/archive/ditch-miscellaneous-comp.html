miscellaneous comp

2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
290 megs of spots, highlight reels, and Ludvig Borga rapping. You want all of it, trust me. 99% is AVI and MPEG format, and excellent quality. Comes in two portions, divided by a file size limit: -161 megs of primarily spots, most of which are from ECW -129 megs of longer spots, hightlights, and Ludvig doing his thing 57 megs of RealPlayer files, almost all match highlights from WWF and WCW in 2001. Features the ECW formation at Atlanta; the best stuff from the Raw/Nitro simulcast shows; the recently done WWF history video package; Benoit vs Austin on Smackdown and Benoit vs Angle in a cage, both of which rock; MatRats doing a bunch of killer spots. Mostly small screen size, but you get a lot. 211 megs of MP3s of Japanese wrestlers' entrance music. Generally rock-based instrumentals, not nearly as generic or angsty as the stuff you hear in the WWF or the former WCW. Split into New Japan (99 megs) and Others (112 megs). 24 megs of US-related MP3s. Mostly what I consider to be cool entrance songs. Also some comedy, from a kazoo theme song, to a Stevie Ray interview, to a Joel Gertner monologue. 46 megs of backyard wrestling goodness. These people kill themselves for no money; I say, watching them kill themselves is the best way to make people not start backyard feds of their own. Features lots of mindblowing stuff from Dakahn, who clearly has no regard for his own body, and is an amazing athlete. Also plenty of sick powerbombs. Not for the faint of heart. Is that all? No! I'm not limited to wrestling. There's also comedy... 472 megs, covering what I consider to be the best seven episodes of the animated Tick series. All AVI format, all high quality. -Tick vs Europe. Tick goes to Euorpe, whackiness ensues. More odd villains than you can shake a spoon at. 92 megs. -Alone Together. Tick goes to space and meets a planet-eating giant, who is pretty much a parody of a certain Marvel comics villain. Arthur becomes DieFladermous' sidekick in the meantime. 92 megs. -Tick vs Science. Body swapping = ratings. Features the utterly bizarre Tongue-Tongue. 69 megs. -Evil Sits Down. DieFladermous falls for the villain, a furniture-based megalomaniac. The World's Most Comfortable Chair comes into play. Hilarious monologue at the end from Tick. 65 megs. -That Moustache Feeling. Tick must deal with an evil hair growth. Lots of great 70's-style parodies, and a subtle parody of yet another b-level Marvel Comics character. 58 megs. -Tick vs The Idea Men. The series debut. Come for the comedy, stay for the Blowfish Avenger. 57 megs. -Tick vs The Tick. The Evil Midnight Bomber rules. So does The Doorman. DieFladermous is at his lecherous best. You've got to have it. 52 megs. Cowboy Bebop episode 20, unedited, in Japanese with English subtitles. The coolest episode of all, thanks to the unstoppable badass known as Mad Pierrot. 104 megs, AVI format. Two episodes of Mr. Show, a sketch comedy series that ran on HBO. There's swearing and stuff, but that's not where the funny comes from. Some sketches might be offensive, so if you think you might be offended by humor, by all means stay away. Both are in RealPlayer format. -Episode 304, with sketches ranging from a violent East Coast vs West Coast ventriloquism feud, to an off-the-wall lie detector session. 66 megs. -Episode 305, with too much to cover, but it's mostly the sort of stuff I haven't seen anywhere else. 143 megs of sketches from The State, which ran on MTV in the early 90's. Like Mr. Show, it's out there and on the adult side of comedy, but there's some great stuff that results. And pancakes. All are in QuickTime format. 35 megs of stuff from SNL, for those of you who haven't seen it all a dozen times on Comedy Central. Most of it is in RealPlayer format. 38 megs of 'other stuff', including Family Guy, Animaniacs, Indian pop music, Monty Python, and a man in a kung fu fight with a bear.
