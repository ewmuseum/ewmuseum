by Squiggy Judgment Day -- 5/19/02 -- Nashville, Tennessee Match #1 -- I.C. Title -- Rob Van Dam vs. Eddie Guerrero(C) After both men miss attempts at their respective Frogsplashes, they charge at each other with cross body blocks, and Van Dam falls on top, 1-2-shoulder up. Eddie rakes Van Dam's eyes and uppercuts him twice before Van Dam retaliates with a backslide, 1-2-kick out. Eddie then delivers a backslide of his own and gets his feet on ropes without the referee seeing, 1-2-3. Winner and STILL I.C. Champion -- Eddie Guerrero Match #2 -- Women's Title -- Stacy /w D-Von & Deacon Bautista vs. Trish(C) /w Bubba Ray Stacy pulls Trish's hair and Irish whips her, but Trish kicks Stacy and baseball slides the Deacon. Trish connects with the StratusFaction to Stacy, 1-2-3.... D-Von tries to attack Trish, but she ducks and leaves, and now it's just Bubba and D-Von in the ring. D-Von goes to shake his hand, but the Deacon charges at Bubba from behind. Bubba ducks the Deacon and clotheslines him to the outside, but D-Von then knocks Bubba down. D-Von seems to regret he hit his own brother, and actually stops Bautista, who had gotten back in the ring and started to stomp on Bubba. D-Von then shows he was just faking and he, too, gets a cheap shot on Bubba. D-Von tells Bautista to get a table. The Deacon attempts to powerbomb Bubba, but Bubba back body drops out of it. Bubba starts to beat the crap out of D-Von, but soon Bautista gets back up and the "Holy Team" put Bubba through the table Winner and STILL Women's Champion -- Trish Stratus -- Just One Month Later... -- Vince McMahon enters Ric Flair's office. Vince and Flair shake hands, and Flair says he finally understands Vince. Flair didn't know back then that Austin would be this much of a pain, but tonight he's going to do what Vince couldn't: take Austin down. Vince and Flair hug, but while Flair can't see Vince's face, Vince gets a disgusted look on his face. Match #3 -- Hardy Boys vs. Brock Lesnar & Paul Heyman Jeff accidentally knocks Matt off the apron and walks into a Brock Bottom from, who else, Brock. Paul screams at Brock to tag him in. Brock does so, and Jeff becomes the first man to be able to say he got pinned by Paul Heyman in a wrestling match. Winners -- Brock Lesnar & Paul Heyman In the back, the newbie interviewer Marc Loyd asks Booker T how he feels being the new nWo member. Booker begins to say he likes it when a beautiful lady catches his eye. Booker asks her if she wants an autograph. She whispers something in his ear and gives him her hotel key. Match #4 -- Handicap Match -- Steve Austin vs. Big Show & Ric Flair Big Show gets tagged in and charges at Austin, who is in the corner, but Austin boots Show in the face and connects with a top rope Thesz Press. Flair gets in the ring only to receive a Thesz Press of his own and a spinebuster, 1-2-stopped by Big Show. Show grabs Austin by the neck, but a Stunner counters it. X-Pac runs down the ring. Austin goes for another Stunner to Show as X-Pac tries to kick Austin. The result is Show getting kicked by X-Pac and X-Pac getting a Stunner from Austin. Austin then Stunners Flair, and covers the Nature Boy, 1-2-3. Winner -- Steve Austin Match #5 -- Hair vs. Hair -- Kurt Angle vs. Edge Edge goes for a Spear, but Kurt kicks him in the head. Angle then tries an Olympic Slam, but Edge slips out of it and Spears Kurt, 1-2-kick out! Edge tries to Edge-ecute Kurt, but Angle breaks out of it and Spears Edge. Kurt delivers an Olympic Slam, 1-2-kick out! Kurt applies the Ankle Lock, but Edge soon enziguris out of it. Kurt applies the Ankle Lock again, but Edge counters it with a roll up, 1-2-3! Kurt tries to attack Edge right after the match, but Edge comes back with an Edge-ecution. Edge carries Kurt all the way to the barber's chair before Kurt breaks free and runs away with Edge in pursuit.Winner -- Edge -- At the Hotel... -- We cut the local Marriot Hotel, and then to a room inside. Booker is in a bed, and soon the women from before joins him. She says she wants to do it in the dark, so Booker turns off the light. They continue to talk, but then Goldust's voice is heard saying, "I want you to leave the nWo and come back to Goldust." Booker hits the lights, and Goldust is in the bed with them. Booker leaves in disgust. Match #6 -- Hell in a Cell -- Triple H vs. Chris Jericho As the two are fighting on the outside, referee Tim White yells at them to get back in the ring. They are about to do so, but Triple H tosses a charging Jericho into White, causing him to fly into the cage! Jericho regains control of the match and seems to go back to wake White back up. Instead, Jericho throws him back into the cage, bloodying defenseless referee. Other officials come down the ring, one with bolt cutters to open the door. They get White out of there, but have given Jericho and Triple H access to the outside if they choose to do so. Back in the ring, Triple H counters an attempted chairshot with a boot, knocking the chair right back in Jericho's face. Triple H searches for, and finds, his sledgehammer. He clocks Jericho with it, but there is no referee. Triple H goes after the referee, and Jericho heads for the open door. Triple H attempts to follow, but Jericho shuts the door in his face. The two then fight toward the announce tables. Jericho clears off the Spanish table. He attempts to Pedigree Hunter, but Triple H breaks free and DDTs Jericho throw the table! Triple H finds a barbed wire 2x4, but Jericho books it up to the top of the Cell. Triple H throws the 2x4 up and scales to the top of the cage, also, but is met with two 2x4 shots from Jericho. Jericho locks in the Walls, and referee Mike Chioda climbs up to check if Triple H is tapping. (So has this been a rule of the match all this time? That you can win on top? I never remember Jim Ross telling us about this back in October '97.) Jericho lets go of the hold and goes to grab the 2x4 again, but Triple H low blows him. Hunter tries to Pedigree Jericho, but Chris back body drops out of it. Triple H hits Jericho with the 2x4, 1-2-kick out! Triple H Pedigrees Jericho and covers him, 1-2-3! Winner -- Triple H Match #7 -- Tag Titles -- Rikishi & Rico vs. Billy(C) & Chuck(C) Just as I thought, Rico was McMahon's choice... Rikishi fights off both the tag champion for most of the match. Rikishi sits on Chuck, 1-2-broken by Billy. Rico gets in the ring as Chuck holds Rikishi. Rikishi ducks, and Rico spin kicks Chuck on accident. Rikishi punches Rico and pins Chuck, meaning Rikishi and Rico are the new champions. (Either the writers are trying to kill the tag division or they just don't know what the Hell they're doing.) Winners and NEW Tag Team Champions -- Rikishi & Rico -- Red, White, and BALD -- In the back, Edge is still chasing Kurt Angle. Angle cleverly hits Edge with a trashcan lid. They fight their way up to the barber chair. Angle says that Edge is going to be bald and calls for the barber to come back out. Kurt tries to Olympic Slam Edge on the platform, but Edge slips out and locks in a sleeper! Once Angle is out cold, Edge shaves Angle bald! He wakes Kurt back up, and Angle is outraged. Edge tells the crowd to chant "You're Bald" instead of "You Suck." Kurt's music hits, and the fans do as Edge suggested. Main Event -- Undisputed Championship -- Undertaker vs. Hulk Hogan(C) (Hooray! after about a year and a half, we FINALLY don't have to hear "Rolling" anymore!) Hogan hulks up, and soon connects with the Boot and Legdrop combo, 1-2-kick out! Hogan goes for a back body drop, but Taker counters with a guillotine DDT, 1-2-kick out. Vince McMahon makes his way to ringside as Hogan kicks a chair back into Taker's face. Hogan connects with another Legdrop, but Vince distracts the referee. Hogan drags Vince in the ring and Legdrops him. The referee pushes Vince out of the ring as Taker smashes Hogan with the chair. Taker scores with a Chokeslam, 1-2-3! After the match, Taker gives Hogan the "place the chair on the throat and slam on the ground" move. Winner and NEW Undispued Champion -- Undertaker Backlash -- 4/21/02 -- Kansas City, Missouri Match #1 -- Cruiserweight Title -- Tajiri /w Torrie vs. Billy Kidman(C) A powerbomb-to-facebuster from Kidman gets a two count. Kidman goes up top for a Shooting Star, but Tajiri moves and Death Kicks Kidman, 1-2-kick out! Tajiri works Kidman in the corner, but when Tajiri looks for a turnbuckle move, Kidman fights back and powerbombs Tajiri off, 1-2-kick out! Kidman goes for another on, but Tajiri spits the Mist! Tajiri hooks Kidman's legs for the three count... After the match, Michael Cole tries to get a word with Tajiri, but he just yells some more Japanese, grabs Torrie by the hand, and walks away. Winner and NEW Cruiserweight Champ -- Tajiri Match #2 -- Scott Hall /w X-Pac vs. Bradshaw /w Faarooq After lots of punching and kicking from both men, Bradshaw connects with the Clothesline from Hell, 1-2-Pac puts Hall's foot on the ropes. Faarooq chases X-Pac around the ring, and soon throws him into the steel steps as Hall low blows and rolls up Bradshaw for the win. Winner -- Scott Hall In Flair's office, Vince McMahon walks in. Vince says he's proud of Flair, because he's learning how to be an owner. He's learning that no matter what you do for your employees, it's never enough. Flair says that he will never be like Vince, but Vince says he could try as he extends a handshake. Flair says, "Never." Match #3 -- Women's Title -- Trish vs. Jazz(C) Trish comes out first, but then Molly Holly comes out. She talks more about being a pure and wholesome virgin, and that the fans want someone like that as the Women's Champion. Molly then starts to attack Trish with the mic. Molly tosses Trish outside the ring, but Trish then whips Molly into the steps. Jazz then comes out and mostly takes advantage of a weekend Trish.... Trish dodges an attempted corner splash and goes for the Stratusfaction, but Jazz counters with a belly-to-back suplex. Jazz then locks in a Boston Crab, but Trish gets to the ropes. Jazz drags her back to the center of the ring and applies an STF, and Trish eventually taps. Winner and STILL Women's Champ -- Jazz Match #4 -- Brock Lesnar /w Paul Heyman vs. Jeff Hardy /w Lita Brock mostly kicked Jeff's ass during this one, but Jeff did hit a few moves... Jeff connects with a Whisper in the Wind, a neckbreaker, and a Swanton, but Brock throws Jeff off like a pillow. Frustrated, Jeff goes outside the ring and grabs a chair, but when he goes to use it, Brock scoops Jeff in that fireman position and gives Jeff the Brock Bottom! Heyman tells Brock to not pin Jeff, but to hurt him more. Brock powerbombs Jeff, picks him back up, and powerbombs him again! Brock then gives Jeff a spinning powerbomb. The referee then just calls for the bell, seeing as Jeff wouldn't not live through much more of a beating like that. During this entire match, Paul was either yelling at Brock to get back up or yelling at Lita saying that this is all her fault. Winner and STILL The Man -- Brock Match #5 -- Kurt Angle vs. Edge Edge goes for an Edge-a-Cution, but Angle reverses it into an Olympic Slam, 1-2-kick out! Angle applies the Ankle Lock. Edge goes for a Spear, but Angle kicks Edge in the face to block it and scores with an Olympic Slam, 1-2-3. Winner -- Kurt Angle -- One Angry Canadian -- Chris Jericho comes out and gets a mic. Title -- Eddie Guerrero vs. Rob Van Dam(C) Eddie connects with two suplexes, 1-2-3. Eddie goes up top, but Van Dam hops up and kicks Eddie down. Eddie hits a powerbomb, but can't make a cover. Title and brings it in the ring only for him to get kicked by Van Dam, but the referee got caught in this and is down.
