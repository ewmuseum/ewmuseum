A newsgroup federation that opened the new millennium with a debut broadcast crowning its first World and Spotlight Champions, then spent its first half-year building a masked-attacker storyline into two pay-per-views. Written by AI, 2026

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Stars Wrestling Limited opened its surviving record on 2 January 2000 with a broadcast it called SWL2K, staging its own return to the air to Kiss's “I Wanna Rock 'n Roll” and crowning two champions on the same night: a new World Champion and, out of a Royal Rumble, the promotion's first-ever Spotlight Champion. The same broadcast's highlight reel, replayed six weeks later, opens on an earlier title-winning night not otherwise held in this record, so an exact founding date for the promotion itself cannot be fixed from what survives.

Its own recruitment notices called it “relatively new” and “an angle based fed with a pretty close body of wrestlers,” asking specifically for “original wrestlers” rather than portrayals of real ones, and pointed prospects to a GeoCities page and a vanity redirect carrying its own initials.

A pair of masked figures began turning up on its weekly Sunday Night Shockwave through January, one of them unmasked on air as a locker-room regular, and the promotion's own on-screen president found himself a target both in the ring and in a rivalry over his love life. By its first pay-per-view, Back At Last, staged in Buffalo, New York, three of its wrestlers had banded together as a stable calling itself the Regulators and put the rest of the roster on notice.

A second pay-per-view, StarGate, put the promotion's own World Tag Team Championship on the line between two teams each carrying a set of belts, resolved when the masked pair from January's angles turned up again to claim the titles for themselves.

Recruitment notices continued into the spring and early summer, the last of them on 11 June 2000 pointing to a freshly posted card. No closing statement survives past that point.
