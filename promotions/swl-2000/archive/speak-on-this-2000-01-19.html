Stars Wrestling Limited: Speak On This (19 January 2000)

19 January 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Note: The Insiders Report is up on the main page. Check it out! ------------------ (The scene opens to Micheal Hudson alone in the studio.) Micheal: Its a day late but we are still here for speak on this. I had last week off and Lucas has tonight off. So without further ado lets get into the action. Starting with Micheal Cage.
1. Micheal Cage 2. MOB Squad 3. The Vindicator 4. "HeartThrob" Tommy Havock 5. Overcast 6. "Brutal Youth" Brad Williams Micheal Cage********************************** ********************************************** (The scene opens up with Micheal Cage sitting in the dimly lit room again) Micheal: This week I will team up with Brad Williams in this Quasar Tag Tournament situation. We will go up aganst this X team. I respect this Brad Williams a lot. It will be an honor to get the better of this X team with you.
(Micheal like in the last interview stands up and walks towards the window.) Micheal: Don’t think winning this Quasar title will shut me up. Bret Real has something that belongs to me. And thats my world title. Grinnin you are in for some trouble. Real is going to tear you apart. And if you somehow get past Real I will tear you apart.
Bottom line is this. I want to be the world champion. (Cage then pauses for about 10 seconds and then continues) Micheal: The Franchise has spoken. ************************************** MOB SQUAD **************************** ************************************** (Screen fades in to M.O.B Squad looking at his locker, and looking really pissed off, as the crownd starts to chant his name)
You know what Havock, I bet your real happy you beat me in my own challenge. You know what, you shouldn't be, for 2 reasons. 1, i kicked your ass pretty good, and 2, it wasn't the match I challenged you to. The guys in charge must screwed up or something, because ot my best recollection, I had challenged you to a ladder match with weapons for the belt. Did I get a ladder match with weapons for the belt........NO!! I want that match, and I will prove that I can beat your ass in any day when i meet you in the ring for the spotlight belt at Back at Last. I will kick your candy ass left, right, up, and eventually down in the ring.
Now, to my next problem, Overcast. Now, I know it has been a while since you became a cocky son of a bitch, but I've still got my problems with you, and I'm gonna be bitching my ass off until we get in the ring. In the ring, I will be kicking your candy ass left, right,
up, and eventually down in the ring. I will then proceed to fall on you, with you, if you are still conscious after this beat down, will hear
1.......2.......3. And finally, to my last problem, named X-Rated Scott Boyle. This man is a big man, no doubt about that man. But, anybody that is 7 feet and taller always have something in common, and that is an extremely large, fat, candy ass. When I meet you in the ring, I plan on kicking your candy ass left, right, up, and eventually down in the ring. I will then pin you for the 1........2.........3 pinfall.
And when I'm through with you, all I've got to say, is that your faat, candy ass will be needing a "face lift"
(Screen fades out as M.O.B Squad yells the camera to get out of his fu**ing locker) ********************************** The Vindicator******************** ********************************** (Scene opens in a back alley somewhere. It is night and the alley is dimly lit and is even less visible from the fog that is hanging close to the ground.
Footsteps can be heard in the distance. As they grow louder a shadow can be seen in the fog. The fog rolls out of the way as the figure approaches. You now can recognize the man as the Vindicator. He is walking slowly toward the camera and carrying his patented crowbar. He speaks, never looking directly into the camera.)
The Vindicator: I'm not really sure what just went down this past Sunday night. First I'm teamed up with some guy who thinks he Superman. You know a lot of people have been institutionalized for making those kinds of claims.
(He begins to pace.) The Vindicator: I made the choice that I was going to stay in this tournament whether he was sane or not. I figured that I would be able to carry the weight of out team if I had to. Then without notice, Captain
Amazing sends out some peon that I've never heard of to take his place. That's OK. I handled that and that guy didn't do too bad. Then Captain Amazing comes limping down to the ring. I'm not one for unnecessary breaking of the rules as most people know but I thought maybe Amazing was coming down to help.
But after watching that tape I'm not so sure that his intentions were that pure. (He stops pacing and becomes increasingly serious.) Amazing, I might be imagining things but it almost looked like you wanted to swing that crutch at me!
Now I'm gonna give you the benefit of the doubt on this one. We got match coming up against Michael Cage and Brad Williams. These guys don't play around so you better give me some indication of whether or not you are in this for the long haul. Whatever you're up to, mark my words.
(He slaps the crowbar against his palm with each point.) I WILL win this weeks match with or without you. I WILL be in the finals of this Quasar tourney. I WILL destroy you and whatever other two guys are in that ring in the finals. I WILL be the Quasar Champ at
Back at Last. Got it? The full details of my SWL mission will be known soon. (He turns around and walks back in to the fog and the darkness. The scene fades to black.)
***************************************************** Tommy Havoc********************** ************************************************ ::Our scene begins in the locker room of the SWL superstar "HeartThrob" Tommy Havock. It is post-match at Sunday Night Shockwave after Tommy Havock has retained his SpotLight Championship during his match against MOB Squad. Havock remains in his long blue pants and his hair appears as if it has been sitting in water soaking. His valet, Erica, is resting the title on the counter and getting towels for Havock.
Havock sees the camera and begins to speak:: "HeartThrob" Tommy Havock: MOB Squad, I've been telling you all along...you simply do not mess with the best. And Tommy Havock is the very best that the
SWL has to offer. You see MOB Squad, I have prophecized all along that I would beat your *** from pillar to post and that is exactly what I did. Tell him about it, Erica.
Erica: That's right, Tommy. MOB Squad, you ran your mouth for weeks and then when it came time to put up, you couldn't do it. You wouldn't even be able to beat
ME in a match, a woman one quarter of your size. "HeartThrob" Tommy Havock: You see, MOB Squad, I'll give you a little iota of credit...you showed up. A lot of other SWL wrestling superstars wouldn't even have the kahoneys to walk down to the squared circle when they know that I, The HeartThrob, await them.
They soon realize that doom is all that awaits them. But you, my friend, came down to the ring and put up a gallant effort. Yes, that you did indeed. And for that, MOB Squad, you have earned my respect.
Erica: All this talk about respect, Tommy, it brings me to mind about these rookies entering the SWL. Some of them really make me sick...
"HeartThrob" Tommy Havock: I agree with you wholeheartedly on that one, Erica. I mean, where do some of these guys get off? They come into the SWL and it's as if they deserve some kind of special respect or something. I'm talking about the
GlassVoices...the Rampages...the Overcasts...Captain Amazing...The Vindicator... I mean, what's the deal? They have to earn their respect the way "The HeartThrob" did...the hard way. Now I'm letting each and every one of these SWL superstars this fair warning...if you ever want to see the way you earn the respect in the SWL that you need, step into the ring with Tommy Havock. If you can walk out after the match, you'll have even my respect. Now let's get out of here, I need to shower...
::The scene ends with Tommy Havock walking out of the locker room towards the shower area::
********************************** Overcast************************** ********************************** (Fade in on Overcast) Overcast: Well, a few things to say. First, M.O.B. Squad, if I were you, I would not be worrying about my training schedule, because I can tell you that it is going just fine. Instead, may I suggest this; worry about yourself. Because you are going to go into the ring with me, and I may be cocky, but I am also good.
Moving on though, Rampage, you are still mad about that Spotlight Rumble thing. Look, you are a punk and you got thrown over that top rope by none other then myself. So, you think that beating on a little kid like Glassvoice is going to impress me? My four year old cousin could beat Glassvoice. Oh, I forgot, you are going to annihilate Glassvoice. That is almost like me saying I am going to kill this fly. It just isn't that impressive. So, I'll tell you what, after I beat M.O.B. Squad, I will take you on at the Pay Per
View, but with a condition. The kid you are going to beat up, Glassvoice, is going to be the guest ref.
Take it or leave it, but either way, you will fear your own shadow! (Fade out) ************************************* Brad Williams************************ ************************************* (The lights flicker around an unknown warehouse. The sound of hungry guard dogs can be heard in the distance. The place is damp and bleak and looks to be not fit for humans to be near. From out of the darkness, "Brutal Youth" Brad Williams steps out. He has long, shoulder length black hair and has a teardrop painted underneath his left eye. He is wearing a tight, see-through black t-shirt, jeans and boots. He is holding a dented steel chair with "Brutal
Youth" spraypainted on it. A small trickle of blood is running down his face). "Slaves beginning from demanding justice and end by wanting to wear a crown. A quote from Albert Camus. So truly spoken. From small acorns giant trees will grow.
Michael Cage, you think by stealing the limelight in our tag team match that you can hide away my genius.
My pure wrestling ability, I promise you, Cage will shine on long after you are gone. Cage, you and my good self are tag team partners once more next week, and I know that we will win for we are the better team".
(Walks closer to the camera, as fire breaks out behind him). "But after next week, Cage watch your back, every step of the way, because I promise you thar I will bury this chair (points chair at camera) into your so-called beautiful skull. On another subject, Bret
Real, Champ. I know that being the Champion that you are that you would have been watching me last week say how I want you to come down at Back At Last and hand me the Quasar Championship belt. You are nothing but a shell of a man who has no spine. You have not got the guts to face me in the ring and hand me the belt. You haven't even gut the guts to mention my name in the ring. You think that you have got problems with
Grinnin. He is just the first obstactle. I am the mountain that you will never climb". (Wipes the blood across his face and grins manically at the camera). "Both Cage and Real, It will be your last night in Heaven and...your...first...day...in...hell". __________________________________________________ *********************************************** (the scene fads back to Micheal Hudson) Micheal: Well some powerful words from our superstars tonight. There getting into it going into the back at
Last PPV. Of course to be headlined by none other than Real Grinnin II. And that is what we will leave you with.
