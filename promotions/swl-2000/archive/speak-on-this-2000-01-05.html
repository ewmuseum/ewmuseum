Stars Wrestling Limited: Speak On This (5 January 2000)

5 January 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
http://www.geocities.com/SouthBeach/Cove/5832/SWL.html SWL SPEAK ON THIS (The scene fades from black into the following scene with the words: "The following opinions expressed on Speak on this are of the wrestlers themselves and not the host, Brian
Williams or anyone else in SWL, just the wrestlers.") (The scene than switches to the host) Lucas Lawler: Hello, and welcome to Speak On This. I'm Lucas Lawler. And he is Micheal Hudson. Micheal Hudson: Yes I am. We are days removed from SWL2K where we crowned two new champs. Lucas: Yes Bret "The Regulator" Real is the new World Champ and we crowned a first ever Spotlight Champion. Micheal: Yes with Real being the new champ, that left the Quaser Championship vacant. Hopefully we will be able to get the ruling on it from the president, shortly.
Lucas: Yes but now we have to let our superstars speak whats on there mind. Micheal: Yes, tonight we have tapes from our champs. Bret Real and Tommy Havoc. As well as Overcast, Grinnin, and GlassVoice. (With that the following videos roll) I.GlassVoice II. Grinnin III. "HeartThrob" Tommy Havock IV. Bret "The Regulator" Real V. Overcast. ************GLASSVOICE*************** ************************************* (GlassVoice is in a meeting with his manager, Bill Buthony, who has a heavy Brooklyn accent.) Mr.Buthony: What happened out there GlassVoice? Ya got creamed like a tissue in a dishwasher! What's da matter wit ya?
GlassVoice: Give me a break. It was Rampage that threw me out of the ring in that Royal Rumble. He's even got the favor of half the audience. Even my father was cheering for him.
Mr.Buthony: Well, it's no wonder. Yer always takin' his liquor. It's probably the reason you lost, cause you looked wasted when they brought you from the ring.
GlassVoice: Well, actually, I lost my bottle of Coke and drank some rubbing alchol by mistake.
Mr.Buthony: You are the youngest wrestler here. You are kinda young-lookin' for a 23 year-old.
GlassVoice: Heh-heh, you thought I was 23? (Mr Buthony glares at GlassVoice in amazement.) Mr.Buthony: .... huh? GlassVoice: Well, I guess I am old-looking for a 17 year-old. Mr.Buthony: And that why you live with your parents! Do they know that you're 17? GlassVoice: They ARE my parents. Everyone knows my age. (Mr.Buthony hangs his head down.) Mr.Buthony: I'm always the last to know. GlassVoice: You know what gotta do now, don't ya? Mr.Buthony: Make sure the fed manager doesn't know? GlassVoice: He knows. Mr.Buthony: Holy hell. GlassVoice: That's not what I have to do. I just need to challenge Rampage to a rematch. Mr.Buthony: I'll alert the fed manager. GlassVoice: He'll know once I send this roleplay in. Mr.Buthony: What!? You were recording this? (GlassVoice leaves Bill Buthony's Office.) ****************GRINNIN************** ************************************* [The camera opens to a snow covered field with a large cabin taking up the rear of it. There is a small line of smoke coming out the top of the cabin indicating that it is indeed inhabited. The camera pans around first taking in a rather battered looking pickup truck, then a large woodpile with what looks to be fresh woodchips in the snow nearby. The camera man begins to walk towards the house where it stops and turns to the right. When it stops we see Lucas Lawler who is bundled in a large parka, huge mittens which are holding a mic with the SWL logo on it. Lucas has a very pained expression on his face as he clears his throat and begins to speak]
Lucas Lawler: Hello SWL fans. We're here today in front of this cabin by a man who has already left his mark here in the SWL. He notched his first win of the year 2000 at SWL2K. He actually caused some fear to some of my commentating co-workers. He is the new number one contender for the SWL World Championship belt. I am of course speaking of the Grinman himself,
Grinnin. Lets see if he is home. [Lucas Lawler knocks on the door and then waits for about seven seconds. Just as he raises his arm to knock again the door opens and there is all 6'3", 283 lbs of well muscled without being bulky, Grinnin.
Grinnin has a scowl on his face as he looks first at Lucas and then at the camera] Grinnin: What?!? Lucas Lawler: Umm...hello Grinnin. Lucas Lawler of the SWL here and i was just wondering if i could get a bit of a word with you.
Grinnin: Lemme guess, you want to talk to me about next Sunday, about this Sunday past, and blah blah blah?
Lucas Lawler: I suppose you could say that. Would that be alright? Grinnin: Go ahead. Lucas Lawler: Can we come in? Grinnin: Is it hot out there? Lucas Lawler: No. Grinnin: Then there's your answer. [Lawler sighs, looks briefly past the camera to the camera man then looks back at Grinnin] Lucas Lawler: This past Sunday you had a match with Eric Nomad that you won by count out. Do you think that winnin like that justifies you getting a title shot next week?
Grinnin: Your damn right i do! That little lost puppy came into the ring looking like something a farmer takes out of a horses stall after he is done cleaning.
For those idiots that dont know what i am talking about i am referring to a big bag of sh ... manure.
He came down and i wrestled him my way. In and out of the ring until i had had quite enough of him. The count hit eight, i hit the Gobsmacker and rolled back into the ring. Do you really think that he would have kicked out of a pinfall after the Gobsmacker anyways?
I dont think so. Lucas Lawler: Fair enough i suppose. But what about your antics at the announcers table when you assaulted one of them and then took that cheap shot at Bret
Real? Grinnin: First off the announcer i sposedly assaulted was merely in my way and i removed him from that predicament. As far as Real goes that was nothing more then a little tap. A reminder that while he beat
Cage on Sunday, he is a long way from being comfortable with that belt. And an even longer way from keeping that belt for more then a week.
Lucas Lawler: So what are your expectations about next week? Grinnin: I am going to win? Are you stupid or something? What do you think my expectations are?!?
Lucas Lawler: And what have you done to prepare for this match? Grinnin: You are stupid! Do you really expect me to answer a question like that and let my opponent actually know what i have done to prepare for him?
Give me a bloody break! I aint answering that on either. Lucas Lawler: Bret Real beat you the last time you two gentlemen faced off in the ring. Do you think that will hamper you in any way?
Grinnin: Wow, a real question. For that you get a real answer there Luc. No i dont think that will hamper me in anyway. When he beat me before it was only for the Quasar title and i was injured just before the event took place so i wasnt even close to being 100% for that match. This time its different.
This time its for the big daddy of all the belts and there aint a damn thing that Metamucil Real is going to do that will help him to keep it.
Lucas Lawler: Metamucil Real? What the hell is that? Grinnin: That is our champion. The man that likes to stay regulated and as such is regular and so therfore uses metamucil. Are you really this stupid Lawler?
Lucas Lawler: Apparantly so Grinnin. Well, any final thoughts before we leave Grinnin? Grinnin: Just this. Metamucil Man i want you to stay regular. I want you to stay just like you are, quavering in your boots at the thought of having to face the Grinman next week. Knowing that at any moment i can hit the Gobsmacker and that it can be over just that quick. You best bring your "A" game
Bret because i sure as hell am bringing everything i got including a few surprises for you. Hell, i was wondering if you could do me a favour Real. Could you count your teeth before and after the match, for you protection of course because regardless of what you do or try the end result is still going to be the same.
I WILL WIN the SWL World Title but even more important then that Bret is this .... YOU WILL GET SMACKED!!!!!
Lucas Lawler: Thanks Grin .... [Grinnin slams the door closed, cutting off Lucas in midsentence] Lucas Lawler: Nin. Well fans ... [Turns to the camera] ... there we have it straight from the man himself. Remember the date will be January the 9th and this will be live on television so do yourself a favour and set the recorder as we're about to witness a war. For the SWL this has been Lucas Lawler.
Good-bye. [Fade back to the studio] ************Tommy Havoc*********** ********************************** ::Our scene begins in the Havock household. Tommy is sitting on his Laz-E-Boy recliner. He is wearing a pair of blue jeans and a gray sweater and has his hair slicked back in a ponytail. His lovely valet, Erica, is sitting on the arm of the recliner wearing a short black skirt and a blue tube top. She is playing with
Havock's hair as Havock begins to speak:: "HeartThrob" Tommy Havock: SWL, welcome to the Havock-Mania! Welcome to the reign of the Havock-Wrecker. Going into Sunday, I guaranteed that I would become the new SpotLight Champion at the SuperCard and, by golly, that's exactly what I did. Look up on that mantle...there's no longer an open spot. Every spot has been filled with the acquisition of the
SpotLight Title. Erica: Mod Squad, didn't I tell you? If you mess with "The HeartThrob," you're going to face havock.
And now my man, Tommy Havock, is the new SpotLight Champion. "HeartThrob" Tommy Havock: The force is with...me! President Williams, make your list of worthy adversaries for "The HeartThrob." But be careful...whomever you throw in there against me will never EVER be the same again. Take a look at Mod
Squad...take a look at Mod Squad, take a look at the Assassin. They all went down and so will everyone else in the league.
Erica: SWL, this is the dawn of a new destiny. It's the Havock Days. We warned you from the onset that you might not be able to handle what we are. I guess now we're going to see who the men are and who the boys are. President Williams, get this man some competition.
"HeartThrob" Tommy Havock: Guys, this is it. I'm a man of my words. I promised a SpotLight Title reign and now I'm promising even greater success for
"HeartThrob" Tommy Havock. Just how high will this star rise??? **************BRET REAL************** ************************************* SWL Announcer: “… that’s at Back At Last next week. Don’t you dare miss it!” (The camera fades the announcer out and starts to fade in a promo for Back At Last… Anna Dea’s voice is heard introducing Bret “The Regulator” Real and Grinnin from their SWL Quasar Title match held at Prevailing Justice.) Anna Dea: “On his way to the ring at this time, weighing in at 285, Bret "The Regulator" Real!!! And his opponent, weighing in at 283 from Toronto,
Canada, Grinnin!!! (Terry Hogan’s voice is heard on the screen as a clip of the wrestlers staring each other down is shown…)
Terry Hogan: “These two won the right to be here in this match. It's for all the glory.” (A few clips of the match are shown until the final clip… The Regulator piledrives Grinnin. Bret "The Regulator" Real leg drops the throat of Grinnin. Grinnin climbs to his feet. The Regulator goes for the Suplex. Oh, he
DDT's him. It's his Judgement Day! The same finishing move is shown back to back three times from three different angles with Charlie Watkins being heard yelling out “Judgement Day!” each time it is shown)
Charlie Watkins: “Real has won the match!” Anna Dea: “The winner of this match, and new SWL Quasar champion, Bret "The Regulator" Real!!!” (There is a bright flash on the screen and this time we hear Anna Dea’s voice announcing the combatants from the title for title match at the last SWL card…)
Anna Dea: “This match is a Standard Match for the SWL World title. On his way to the ring at this time, weighing in at 285 pounds, he holds the SWL Quasar title belt, Bret "The Regulator" Real!!! And his opponent, weighing in at 256 pounds, from Seymor,
Indiana he holds the SWL World title belt, Micheal Cage!!!” (Clips of the two superstars going toe-to-toe are shown while we hear the commentating at ringside…)
Charlie Watkins: “This should be quite a fight. What do you think Grinnin” Grinnin: “Yeah, it's for the championship, but ya gotta remember, the belt is going to be mine next
Sunday” (The next clip shows Real and Cage going to the outside of the ring during their match and Grinnin getting up from the announcer’s table…)
Charlie Watkins: “What are you doing?” Grinnin: “I'm doing what I want. What are you going to do about it, Chuck?” (Grinnin pushes Charlie Watkins to the ground.) Terry Hogan: “Go get em Grinnin!” (Grinnin takes a cheap shot at The Regulator while his back was turned and returns to the announcers table)
(A few more clips of the match are shown until the final clip… Bret Real punches Micheal repeatedly. Real delivers a spine buster to Micheal. The Regulator goes for the
Suplex. Oh, he DDT's him. It's his Judgement Day Bret "The Regulator" Real covers Micheal hooking the leg. Kevin Mills counts. ...1 ...2 ...3 ) Terry Hogan: “Bret has won the match!” (The announcers’ table is shown…) Charlie Watkins: “Looks like you are going to be taking on Bret Real on Sunday.” Grinnin: “Yep, it does. Looks like I'll see you guys next week after I win the World Championship.”
(As Grinnin leaves ringside and heads to the back, Anna Dea’s voice is heard as “The Real Deal” is shown standing on the second rope with both arms raised in victory.)
Anna Dea: “The winner of this match, and new SWL World champion, Bret "The Regulator" Real!!!” **(Bret Real’s music can now be heard being faded in to the promo and slowly getting louder and louder)** (Still standing on the ropes with his new World Championship belt in his right hand and his prized Quasar belt in his left hand The Regulator is shown staring directly at Grinnin with that patented smirk on his face. Grinnin doesn’t look back – he either didn’t see the World Champion staring him down or
PRETENDED not to see the World Champion staring him down. What the viewers believe depends on who they are a fan of.)
**(COME WIT IT NOW!! yells the lead singer of Rage Against The Machine)** (A close-up shot of Real’s face while staring down Grinnin begins to fade to black but the music still continues… Silver lettering fade in on to the screen… Sunday January 9, 2000 As they fade off the screen and new words fade in, RATM’s lead singer yells once more “COME WIT IT NOW!!” REAL GRINNIN II The screen and the music fades to black) *************OVERCAST************* ********************************** Fade in on Overcast in the back of the arena after Saturday Night Shockwave: SWL2K. He looks well rested after competing in the Royal Rummble and then watching the rest of the show in the back. He looks up to the camera and begins to speak.)
Overcast: First, congratulations are in order. I would like to say congrats to Bret Real. While Mike Cage was talking about how much he respects you and how he thought that you were a great wrestler, you were preparing, and it showed. When it came down to it you proved you were the better wrestler. So, now you are the "man", and everyone is going to gun for you, including me! Second, congrats are in order for Tommy
Havock. You won the Royal Rummble and are now the Spotlight champ. I should be insulting you now saying that I should be the Spotlight cahmp, I am not going to. Now, this brings me to my next point. Now that
Real is the World champion, the Quasar title is vacant. Here is my thought on the subject. How about we take the last three people to be eliminated from the Royal Rummble, M.O.B. Squad, Rampage, and myself.
Then, we throw in Eric Nomad, the loser of the number one contender's match and we have ourselves either a tournament or a round robin tournament. SO,
M.O.B.SQUAD, RAMPAGE, ERIC NOMAD, WHAT DO YOU BOYS THINK?!? I promise you only one thing, if this happens, and you face me, you will come out of the match fearing your own shadow!
(Fade Out) ************************************ (The scene than fads into the announcers) Lucas: Well there you have it. Five of the top SWL superstars speaking whats on there mind.
Micheal: That’s gonna be all the time we got for this show. So this is me speaking for lucas and myself.
Saying Goodbye. Lucas: And this is me adding that the next show will be Friday. Get Flashin. (The closing credits roll with the SWL 2000 copyright information.) Next Show: Friday January 4, 2000. Sent via Deja.com http://www.deja.com/ Before you buy.
