"HeartThrob" Tommy Havock************** ****************************************** ****************************************** ::The scene begins in the SWL locker room area. The camera pans over to a black, steel door with the name of the SWL Spotlight Champion engraved in it. The door opens and we see Tommy Havock pouring water over his head and talking to his valet, Erica.
Tommy is still in his wrestling attire, which consists of red and blue long trunks and he has thrown a
"HeartThrob" t-shirt on to cover his bare chest. From all impressions, this appears to have been an SWL house show and it looks as if Tommy has won his match, since his Spotlight Championship Title Belt is sitting on his desk in the corner. Tommy notices the camera and begins to speak::
"HeartThrob" Tommy Havock: After a week with incredibly low ratings, it looks as if the SWL brass has gotten smart and decided to put "The HeartThrob"
back in action. And let's see who his opponent is... Why, it looks as if it is "M.O.B. Squad." We've got a long past. Mr. Squad, here, once thought it would be wise to run his mouth about "The HeartThrob" and where did that get him? Well, it did not get him the SWL
SpotLight Championship, that's for sure. Erica: MOB Squad, you want to come back for some more of Tommy Havock? Didn't you get enough the first time?
How many times does Tommy have to beat you from pillar to pillar before you get the clue? MOB Squad, you're nothing. You never were and never will be.
You went so far as to challenge me, a lady, MOB Squad. And you think you're going to be able to defeat Tommy Havock? "HeartThrob" Tommy Havock: You know MOB Squad, in my spare time, I like to listen to the radio. And while
I was driving to the arena tonight, they were playing a song which will soon become your new theme song once
"HeartThrob" Tommy Havock is through with you. It's called "I Feel Like A Woman." No, no...I take that back. You won't even be able to feel anything, MOB
Squad. They haven't created the medicine that will be needed to revive your carcass. Erica: MOB Squad, I highly suggest you call all of your family...all of your loved ones...and anyone else you can think of this week because your days are numbered...
"HeartThrob" Tommy Havock: Now let's move on to other subjects. There seems to be a lot of new talent coming into the SWL that likes to run their mouth.
They like to talk trash about "The HeartThrob." They think he's not a worthy enough champion. Well, Tommy
Havock thinks that that is a lot of bull. This championship title means a lot. But most importantly, it means that I'm better than all of you. You see, I am the SpotLight Champion. You may be the Champion of
Missing the Toilet or of taking a crap in your pants when you step into the ring, but the titles don't compare. One of these days, each and everyone of you will realize this. But Mr. MOB Squad, this next warning is for you: Don't show up this Sunday at
ShockWave...because, either way, you won't be leaving on your own. ::The scene ends as Tommy Havock shoves the camera man outside of his locker room and slams the door shut::

Lighting shoots down the Titantron and Red flames shoot up from the ramp area. Rampage steps out as his music begins to play as he holds a microphone, on the Titantron the writing 'the evil has returned'
in blood writing appears] "Some of you losers out there may be GlassVoice fans, I thought I better show you his performance so far in SWL" spat Rampage as he pointed to the crowd. [The crowd boos as a the royal rumble at SWL2K is showing on the Titantron, as it shows Rampage throwing
GlassVoice over the top rope to be eliminated by Rampage] "To be honest folks he looks pretty crap to me, but I have some more footage," joked Rampage.
[The scene changes on the Titantron, the Quasar tournament match at Sunday Night Shockwave vs. Rampage and The Enforcer] [The Enforcer tags in Rampage. GlassVoice punches Rampage repeatedly. The Enforcer tagged in by Rampage. GlassVoice slaps The Enforcer in the face. GlassVoice gets back to his feet. GlassVoice is going to try to make the tag to Drew Archer.]
Charlie Watkins - Archer doesn't want the tag! What is going on?! [The Enforcer applies a boston crab to GlassVoice. The referee is checking the situation. ... ... The
Enforcer breaks the hold. The Enforcer chokes GlassVoice. The Enforcer chants start. ] Terry Hogan - He won't be able to stand much more. He NEEDS to make the tag with Archer. Charlie Watkins - But Archer won't make the tag! What is he to do? [Now "The Classic" is getting in the ring.. "The Classic" Drew Archer takes GlassVoice off his feet with a clothesline ] Charlie Watkins - WHAT?! That's his teammate? [Rampage tagged in by The Enforcer. The Enforcer executes a reverse DDT on GlassVoice. The Classic grabs a chair and hits GlassVoice in the head. ring, ring, ring! Kevin Mills calls for the bell. The
Classic was disqualified.] Terry Hogan - Usually, I am all for getting ahead. But what was the point of that? Anna Dea - The winners of this match, Rampage & The Enforcer!!! "Even his own tag partner wanted to beat the crap out of him, but to be fair GlassVoice is lucky Drew Archer cost him the match cos if I lost, I have to whip both you and Archer piece of shit asses all the way to the moon," spat Rampage.
"Also Overcast, you better run cos after I have beaten GlassVoice's ass black and blue, I'm coming after you, you will pay for you actions at SWL2K," spat Rampage.
[Rampage's music "Sweet Disorder" by the strawpeople plays as Rampage walks up the ramp and out of the arena]

4. Vindicator*********************************** ************************************************ ************************************************ (Lucas Lawler is backstage at the 1/9 Shockwave. Several wrestlers can be seen milling around and some security guards are leaning against a wall eating doughnuts. Lucas Lawler appears to be looking for someone.)
LL: We are trying to catch up with the Vindicator. He just took his first win in the SWL against Captain Amazing. I've got some inside news I'm going to break to the Vindicator and you folks watching Speak on This will see his reaction first hand when I give him this major announcement.
(The Vindicator walks in sweaty and still a little winded from the nights match. LL approaches him.)
LL: Excuse me, Vindicator. That was an impressive victory you pulled off over Captain Amazing.
The Vindicator: Yeah well there's a heckuva whole lot more on the way. LL: What's next on your agenda? The Vindicator: Well as I've stated before, my signing with this company wasn't an accident. I've got a few things I need to accomplish here.
LL: Well what if I told you I just received word that you will have your first opportunity to work toward some gold?
The Vindicator: What do you mean? LL: You've just been entered into the Quasar Tournament. The Vindicator: Who's my partner? LL: The man you just beat, Captain Amazing! The Vindicator: You're kidding me right? (he shakes his head and laughs) In my 4 years of pro wrestling
I've come across some seriously whacked out people. I've wrestled with and against ape men, wannbe vampires, clowns, and even a guy that was anxious to give everybody in the business an anal probe. Fact is this guy ain't bad in the ring, he's just got a few screws loose. As long as he doesn't blow it for us we'll be in those finals.
LL: Those are pretty confident words. The Vindicator: Lemme put it this way - I've got a mission here in this company and if I have that chance to get a little gold along the way I'm not gonna pass up the opportunity.
LL: You keep talking about this mission. Give us some insight. The Vindicator: Well I could tell you but then I'd have to kill you. Everyone will know soon enough. To tell you the truth I don't even know the full extent of my mission yet. Let's just say you'll know it when it happens.
(The Vindicator turns and walks off and LL turns back to the camera.) LL: Cryptic words from the "V" man. I guess we won't get anymore on this story until it happens.

Brad Williams****************************** ********************************************** ********************************************** (The screen fades from black to a disused warehouse. The hissing and churning of machinary can be heard all around. From out of the shadows walks "Brutal Youth"
Brad Williams, carrying a wrench in his hand. He is slapping the wrench repeatedly into his open palm.
Sparks fly from unknown machinary as Williams prepares to speak.He speaks with little emotion in his voice and his eyes do not flicker an inch.)
"Welcome adults, children and fellow competitors to your worst nightmare. You see, my namesake Brian showed a lot of sense when he signed me up to compete in the SWL. The rest of you nobodies, never wases and has beens better pack up your bags and hit dodge pretty quickly for when I step into the ring on Sunday
16th January for my debut appearance on Shockwave, it will be the turning point in the history of the SWL.
As a speak to you today, I do not yet know who my tag team partner is or who my opponents are but, then again, I don't care . A word of warning to my partner though, (points threateningly to the camera) if you screw me, cross me or cause our team to lose, I promise that I will break you in half in an instant. I have no sense of friendship, loyalty or comradeship in my bones, I am in the tournament for one reason and one reason only, to step through the ropes at Back At
Last, win the SWL Quaser Championship, and get that no-mark Bret Real to bring his stinking carcass down to MY ring and hand my MY championship belt. Believe me, Bret, Tommy and whoever wins the tag titles next week, I am gunning for each and everyone at you and believe me when I say that I will get you, one way or the other, I WILL GET YOU. It will be your last night in heaven (laughs manically)
and...your...first...day...in...HELL. (Williams turns his back to the camera and hurls the wrench to the ground. The camera zooms in to his hand which is bleeding because of the force administered by the wrench being slammed into it. The camera fades to black on his hand as blood drips to the floor.)
6. Overcast ****************************** ****************************************** ****************************************** (Fade in on Overcast sitting in a chair with his head down. He looks into the camera.) Overcast: Okay, I am about to commit one of the biggest mistakes you can in the wrestling business.
M.O.B. Squad, I am sorry. I was cocky, I wanted the win, and now not only have I cost you a shot at the
Quasar Title, but I also cost myself that chance. The point is this though, now that I've said that, getover it. I'll tell you what, I will even give you a chance at me in the ring. At the next show M.O.B. Squad, you and me in that ring! This time I won't be so cocky, this time I am going to prepare hard, and if you don't believe me, meet me in the ring and see if you leave fearing your own shadow!
(fade out) 7. Vindicator***************************** ****************************************** ****************************************** [Before the previous interview on "Speak On This" even finishes a black and red "V" appears and flickers on the screen. A woman's voice is then heard.]
Voice: You are not experiencing technical difficulties. We have taken over your airwaves to bring you this message from the Vindicator.
[The scene flashes over to a dark room where the Vindicator is sitting in large wooden chair. His elbows are resting on the arms of the chair and his fingers are touching. He is wearing a black shirt with a red "V" on it, black jeans, and a black leather jacket. His face paint is in the original "Vindicator"
design and has colors of black, white and red. His patented crowbar rest on one of the arms of the chair
(he had it way before that Flair guy did). He takes a deep breath and begins.] The Vindicator: So much to cover. I've only been with the SWL for a few days and it seems that I've already a hot item. First let me address my opponent
this Sunday, Captain Amazing. I have nothing against you other than the fact that you're a few fries short of Happy Meal. If you're really what you claim to be then step up and realize that I'm right there with you. Your mission might a little different from mine but we are both after the same thing: JUSTICE! You just wear your underwear on the outside of your clothes and have magical powers. I just carry this
(picks up the crowbar) as my magical powers. [He sets the crowbar back down.] The Vindicator: So this Sunday I won't have any pity in you as a mental patient. I've beaten guys a whole lot more mental than you in other feds. But I will respect you for striving toward what is right. By the way, props to ya for rescuing that kitty. So it is with regret that I inform you that I will take my first victory this Sunday.
[He leans forward in his chair.] The Vindicator: And on to another item of business. I knew it wouldn't take long but the Grin man is already running his mouth about the past. Well Grinnin, believe it or not I have a mission here and it's not focused on you: YET! So let me clarify the past as it seems to have blurred your memory. In that crowbar match, I took that belt from you and I walked out the champ. Justice was served up to on a harsh platter.
For right now you stay outta my way and I'll stay outta yours. I'd hate to have to open up some old wounds. I know that our paths will cross soon enough.
Just leave it at that. [He stands up out of the chair.] The Vindicator: To the rest of the SWL, Grinnin said it best when he said there's a new sheriff in town and you can be the rest of the posse's not far behind.
Keep wrestling true and you have nothing to worry about. All you thugs out there might as well leave before things get too ugly. My mission will be known in full soon.
SWL thugs: JUSTICE IS COMING FOR YOU!! [Screen goes black to the black and red flickering "V". The woman's voice is heard again.] Voice: We now return you to your regularly scheduled programming.
