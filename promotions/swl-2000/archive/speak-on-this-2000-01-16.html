Lawrence Rhodes********************************* *************************************************** [Scene: We pans back into the sparsely accommodated bar at an archetypal Holiday Inn Hotel somewhere near the SWL Headquarters. The same middle aged well tanned truly American couple sit in silence drinking beer -
(must be a plant). The camera moves further into the bar area picking out the same working girls as the previous week and then moves smoothly into a side on shot of 'Luscious' Lawrence Rhodes. Again Rhodes is dressed in dark blue G-Star jeans and matching jacket.
He is sat on the same two seater couch. Rhodes is half speaking, half shouting into a mobile phone.]
RHODES: I just ain't ready to do TV interviews yet. I know ... I know [pause] I just aint ready. Look if it's in my contract, there ain't anyfing I can do. But
I tell ya this I ain't to happy about it. Anyway ... I'm gonna get me a workout ... I'll ring ya later. [Scene: Cut to the interior of the hotel gymnasium. There are a few individuals using the numerous running and rowing machines, as the camera pans around picking up a few older buffed men/ women working on the weight machines. The camera moves in to focus on the Luscious
One doing some reps on a multi gym machine. The camera moves closer in, so that we can actually see the sweat on Rhodes forehead. Rhodes is clad in a Krypt Gym sweat top and grey sweat pants. The camera moves in closer as the Luscious One grimaces straight at it.]
RHODES: What the <bleep>? Who let these guys in 'ere? I can't do my weights without the damn camera guy distressing the situation. I've got my bleedin'
training to complete, turn that thing off. [The camera moves back slightly as Rhodes begins to rise from his sitting position. At that moment the picture fuzzes over ... then to black.]
[Scene: Cut to the interior of a locker room. There are a series of lockers, some open some closed. A few individuals stand around in differing states of undress. The camera moves through the locker room towards the sound of running water. A large man with a closely cropped crew cut stands opposite a mirror, adjusting a tie. The camera moves past him and then turns a corner into a shower room. A few men are showering, including 'Luscious' Lawrence Rhodes. The camera moves in as the Luscious One moves towards it, towel in hand to cover his watershed bits]
RHODES: How many god damn times [pause] wait a minute [pause] get your sorry ass out of this room. You guys never give up, do you. I've got a pile of faxes, the tape on my voicemail is working overboard, and you still don't know ... do you. Just get out, at least leave the Luscious One time to shower and make myself look presentable and then ... and only then will I contemplate giving the world what it wants.
[Again the camera moves back, as Rhodes moves towards it then the picture fuzzes over into black.]
[Scene: From black the shot fuzzes and then cuts to 'Luscious' Lawrence Rhodes standing outside the Holiday Inn Hotel. The camera moves closer as the Jewel begins to talk] RHODES: So this is it then. The Luscious One can finally meet his adoring public. But what I want to know is what the SWL actually want me to do. Stand
'ere and chat about how I'm gonna do this and do that. Dress my ass in black and go onto the roof tops and chat about being mysterious or somefing. [Rhodes smiles] I could ... but that <bleep> has already been done. I could go and get my sorry ass into a hot tub with a few of the SWL's paid ho's. Not really my scene, but then again ... that <bleep> has already been done.
[The grin seems to be getting bigger as Rhodes seems to at last rise to the occasion. He seemingly rubs his hands together with some glee.]
RHODES: How about donning a United States bandanna [pause] or should that be banana and then claim that I want to embrace the whole of the US, throwing away my
European roots in the process. [The grin is getting bigger] RHODES: Hey ain't that <bleep> already been done [The Luscious One shakes his head, knowingly] or how about putting myself up as a rolemodel for all the little people out there. Hey, I think I might 'ave to get it the queue for that one [Rhodes is now beaming, on the verge of laughter. He composes himself] No, I've got it, the Luscious One is going to live on the edge, friendless, volatile ... you get the picture, have a reputation for numerous assaults, drink a lot, hey ...
even say that I only wrestle for money and so I can hurt a few people. [He is now chuckling to himself, as he pulls some gum out of his pocket and starts to unwrap it.]
FRANCIS: No ... that sucks. [Scene fades to black as the Rhodes turns and heads off] 3. Captain Amazing********************************* *************************************************** [The screen cuts immediately and the Channel 7 news logo appears. A quick fade later, and we see the petite brunette Lois Laime stands in front of the
Stars Wrestling Limited headquarters, getting her make-up adjusted before her story. After receiving a couple brushes of blush, she adjusts her outfit, raising her short yet professional skirt and fluffing up her more than ample bosom.]
Charlie the Cameraman (CC): We're ready to roll, Ms Laime. Lois Laime (LL): 3... 2... The SWL. One of the leaders of this new wave of adult entertainment sweeping across America: professional wrestling. I'm
Lois Laime, reporting for Channel 7 news in the first of a new series on the phenomenon of sports
entertainment- I'm sorry, cut, cut! CC: What's wrong? LL: I can't believe we're doing this. I should be covering the big news. I went to Harvard! I want to be the next Barbara Walters, or the next Diane Sawyer.
But what do I get? I have to cover some little wrestling fed, just to please a bunch of redneck wrestling fans who spend all their time on watching big ugly men roll around in tights!
CC: Hey! There's nothing wrong with men in tights! LL: That's not my point. My talents are wasted here! CC: Let's just get this over with, shall we? All we have to do is find one wrestler and follow him around, and that's it! LL: Fine. Anything to get us out of this. Who are we supposed to spotlight? CC: Some new guy named Captain Amazing. He thinks he's some kind of superhero or something. LL: Great, another nutball. Okay, let's get this over with. [Clears throat] 3... 2... The SWL. One of the leaders of this new wave of adult entertainment sweeping across America: professional wrestling. I'm
Lois Laime, reporting for Channel 7 news in the first of a new series on the phenomenon of sports entertainment. We will follow the exploits of one particular wrestler and hopefully take an in depth look into the world of professional wrestling. The subject of our analysis? A young up-and-coming wrestler who goes by the name Captain Amazing.
[In the background, we see the glass doors of the building open and out steps the Amazing One, Captain
Amazing, dressed in a fancy Italian suit. On his face, however, is his omnipresent red leather half mask that conceals his identity from the outside world.] LL: And here he comes now... [turns to look at Captain Amazing, but she can't seem to take her eyes off of him.] [Captain Amazing walks up to the camera's foreground and the awaiting (and drooling) Lois Laime. She eyes him up and down, admiring his physique, not to mention the flowing brown locks leaking from under the mask.
Her eyes bulge noticeably when looking at his large muscular hands and again as she passes his midsection.
A couple of awkward milliseconds pass before she realizes the camera is still rolling.] LL: Captain Amazing, I'm Lois Laime, Channel 7 news. I'm here to follow your career and show our audience a glimpse of the life of a pro wrestler.
Captain Amazing (CA): Really? Well, it will be a pleasure working with you. LL: The pleasure's all mine... CA: What was that? LL: Er, nothing. So tell me. How are you doing so far in your tenure in SWL? CA: Well I just had my first match, and unfortunately I lost to a valiant wrestler by the name of the Vindicator. I wasn't sure about him at first, but after losing to him in a very exciting back-and-forth match, I have a new respect for him.
You see, he too values the tenets of truth and justice. LL: And speaking of which, how is the super hero business going? CA: Well, you know, same ole, same ole. Rescue cats from trees, pushing old ladies across the street. No big crimes yet, but every bit of good that I do will go far in making our society a peaceful law abiding one.
LL: Fascinating. [Flashes her long lashes] Now, back to the Vindicator. I see by the SWL schedule that your next match will be a tag team match with him as your partner against the team of the Assassin and Eric Nomad. What are your thoughts going into this Quasarntournament match?
CA: After the last match I had, I think it would be a privilege to partner up with another upholder of justice, especially one like the Vindicator. I have complete confidence in him, so my main concerns involve our opponents, Eric Gonads and this Ass-Ass man character. I don't like the cut of their jib.
Going into a match-up against these two evil-doers, I just have some words for the Vindicator: Watch your back. Those two nogoodniks might try to resort to illegal methods to gain an advantage over us. You have to be careful about who you should trust, and these two should definitely not be trusted.
LL: Interesting. Now, can I ask you a private question? Why are you still wearing that mask? Don't you think that a handsome man such as yourself is depriving the world of a natural wonder by wearing that little mask? CA: Well I get asked that question a lot, you see, and I've decided that it is better that no one, (no, not even you, Lois) should know my true identity. Not only would it put those I know in danger being associated with a super hero, but it would also break the hearts of many young men whose girlfriends will swoon over me.
LL [Completely love struck]: *Sigh* Really? CA [Completely unaware]: Yeppers. LL: *cough, cough* Well, I guess that some secrets are meant to stay secret. But, I guess I'll leave you to your crime fighting duties now. We will have to keep in touch. Here's my number. [Reaches into her bosom and retrieves a card]
CA: Thank you, Ms Laime. And to all you little Amazing Ones at home watching: Drink Milk! [With a flourish, Captain Amazing leaps and takes off... into a waiting taxi. A flustered Lois Laime remains and watches him vanish into traffic.]
LL: *Sigh* I'll find out what's under that mask someday. And what's under all those clothes too! CC: Um, Ms Laime? The camera's still rolling. LL (embarrassed): And there you have it folks, the Amazing One, Captain Amazing. Catch him, and all the other wrestlers of the SWL at an arena near you. Once again, this is Lois Laime for Channel 7 news, reporting.
CC: And... fade to black! We're done! Okay, now we can go back to the editors and tell them we want a different assignment. LL: No, no! I don't think that will be necessary. I expect big things to come from Captain Amazing. I mean, big things about wrestling, that is.
CC: Yeah, sure. Whatever. It's your career. LL: *Sigh* [Fade to black] 4.
