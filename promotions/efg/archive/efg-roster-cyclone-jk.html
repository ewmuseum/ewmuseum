Battlemania Signup: "The Cyclone" JK

22 February 2015
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
CycloneJK — Feb 22, 2015 10:28 AM APPLICATION - Current EFed (if any): N/A Character Name: 'The Cyclone' JK Height: 6'3" Weight: 219lbs Hometown: Townsville, North Queensland Australia Theme Song: What a Great Night - the Hilltop Hoods Alignment: Face Wrestling Style: High Flyer/Hardcore 10 Favorite Move(s): Ensiguri, Hurricanrana, flying forearm, missile drop kick, Suicide dive, Shining Wizard, tornado DDT, cross body, jawbreaker, corkscrew dive
5 Signature Move(s): Pele Kick, 'Stand Trial' (Undertaker's Hell's Gate), 'Eye of the Storm' (Styles Clash), 'Flying Debris' (Orton Punt from the top rope), Curb Stomp
Set-up Move: a punch to the gut Finishing Move: 'The Cyclone' Finishing Maneuver Description: starts off as an F5 before swinging out into a neck breaker.
Character Details: mid-length shaggy brown hair, he has a very laid-back Aussie kind of look and attitude. He is the youngest wrestler to lace a pair of boots, having debuted for the Global Impact Wrestling company at the age of 15. While he was not really a big name, main event star during his tenure with the UGWC, he was considered to be a well respected high card player, with most of his matches taking place right before the main event.
Anything else you think we’d need to know about a character in order to write them into the Battlemania event? He is the youngest champion in both GIW and UGWC history, winning the GIW.com and UGWC Cross-Hemisphere titles at 15 and 16 respectively. Currently, he is 22 years of age.
