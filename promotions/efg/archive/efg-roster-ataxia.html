Battlemania Signup: Ataxia

22 February 2015
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Jorden Snow — Feb 22, 2015 10:00 PM APPLICATION - Current EFed (if any): Boardwalk Wrestling Character Name: Ataxia. Nickname: "The Messiah Pariah" Height: 5'11" Weight: 180 lbs Hometown: Parts Unknown Theme Song: "Die, Die, Die My Darling" by Metallica Alignment: Heel Wrestling Style: Unpredictable Hardcore Lucha 10 Favorite Move(s): 1. running drop kick. 2. through the ropes kick (619) 3. run to the ropes moonsault 4. running splash to the corner 5. drop to the ground and slap his opponent in the face (counter from being thrown to the ropes)
6. running shoulder to the gut or head 7. hip toss 8. running spinning neckbreaker 9. bulldog 10. crescent flip kick to the jaw (bicycle kick in soccer to the jaw) 5 Signature Move(s): 1. No Bitch: German Suplexs opponent into the turnbuckle post 2. Enzuguri: Enzuguri. 3. Facepalm: Ataxia slaps his opponent into "The Claw" submission but forces them into the corner and smashes the back of their head into the post while holding onto the temple hold.
4. The Claw: Ataxia leaps up onto the opponent and latches on his thumb and fingers in pressure point areas on the face of the opponent.
5. Peaceful Tolerance. From the top rope; 360 kick to the face. Set-up Move: Sternum Shot: Ataxia hits his opponent in the sternum. Finishing Move: N.T.F.U. (Now That's Fucked Up) Finishing Maneuver Description: After hitting the sternum shot Ataxia runs to the ropes and bounces off. He leaps into the air bring his knees up and hitting them into the collarbone area of his opponent knocking them to the ground. As they fall Ataxia lands crotch first on their neck and then goes for the pin.
Character Details: (Gimmick, History, Look) Ataxia is a mystery in the world of professional wrestling. Some call him a federation terrorist and some call him a hero of the fans. Ataxia wears a brown sack like mask. He has red pupils, red teeth, white and black facepaint on under the mask where you can see his face slightly. His outfit consists of a black tuxedo and tie, white button up shirt, black boots, and black opera gloves. Ataxia is pretty much insane. In intense pain he will laugh at his opponent. He is a psychological opponent often making "advances" at his opponents to unnerve them. He will do anything to get on their nerves and mess with them. Often times if he is "unmasked" in a match he is wearing a white micheal myers like face to cover his real face. The white mask however is made to look like an opponent and Ataxia is a master of verbal mimicry. He will speak in their own voice just to screw with them. He doesn't care about titles or victories, but just to make you learn your lesson!
