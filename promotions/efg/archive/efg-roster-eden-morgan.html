Battlemania Signup: Eden Morgan

21 February 2015
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
FallofEdenUGWC — Feb 21, 2015 4:58 PM APPLICATION Current Efed: UGWC (Unified GlobalWrestling Coalition) Character Name: Eden Morgan Age: 22 Height: 5'3 Weight: 116 lbs Hometown: Slaughter Swamp, SC Currently Residing: Los Angeles Affiliates: The Syndicate: 'Vain' Alan Wallace, Killian King, and Zane Scott Theme Song: Porcelain and the Tramps “King of the World” Entrance: The entire arena falls to black as the almost industrial bass sounds of Porcelain and the Tramp's "King of the World" begin to play. The video on the screen turns to solid black, a thin purple line moving across the screen, elevating and descending with each hit of the bass. More lines join as the voice of Porcelain Black wails out, until the words"Do Not Test Me" flash one at a time on the screen with the song. As the full force of the song hits, a shattering graphic goes across the screen, followed by scenes of Eden Morgan in the ring, her hand being raised in victory, looking down at Donovan Hastings' still form, Travis Pierce dropping to his knees in disbelief, Eden planting the World Title in the face of Jet Somers. The scenes mix with scenes of her during various press conferences and board meetings, circling back to the beginning. As the lights begin to rise, we see Eden Morgan standing on the stage, a confident half-smile turning up one side of her lips. She surveys the crowd, some cheers still heard, but most of the fervor has turned to boos and chants of "You sold out!" Eden makes an obvious show of yawning and then smiling in a deceptively lazy way as she slinks down the ramp. As she goes along, she turns to the crowd on each side, pointing to her heart with one hand and pointing out to all of them mockingly. Finally ignoring the screaming, she makes her way into the ring, taking her time to climb each step and slip between the ropes.
Alignment: Heel Wrestling Style: Hi-flyer. Technical.She’s very fast which makes up for her lack of size, but can sometimes get her into situations she isn’t ready for. Reckless yet methodical, she's an oxymoron.
10 Favorite Moves: - Superkick - Pelekick - Hesitation drop kick - Standing shooting star press - Double knee facebreaker - Jawbreaker - Springboard Enzuigiri - Running corkscrew neckbreaker - Springboard knee strike - Moonsault 5 Signature Moves: Ouroboros: Sharpshooter where Eden takes it all the way back until her shoulders almost touch the mat.
The Hard Way: Inverted facelock transitioned into a double-knee backbreaker (Angelina Love's “Break A Bitch”)
Entropy: Backward jump into a hurricanrana High Maintenance: Suicide dive tornado DDT Hostile Takeover: Ringpost Figure Four. Eden holds this until the last possible second and is likely to push her luck by performing it repeatedly (if possible), even by dragging her opponent to another ringpost for another count.
Primary Finisher: Fall of Eden-Inverted Stomp Facebreaker (Gail Kim's “Eat Defeat”) Secondary Finisher: Severance Package-Slingshot guillotine leg drop Alternate Finisher: Beautiful Disaster- Hurricanrana Driver Character Details: Appearance- Pic base of Eva Green (Pic below) In-ring attire: Black boy shorts with a silvery glitter fabric overlay and a black bra top with the same overlay. A wide band of the same black fabric lies beneath her breasts and runs across her bare abdomen, a vertical line of the fabric intersecting the other and descending from the middle of the bra down to connect with the shorts. A large diamond-like broach sits at her cleavage, two bands of fabric coming out from the top of it and going in a "v" shape over each shoulder. She wears fishnets and black knee-high leather boots with a high heel, and black leather wrist bands. Think the black outfits the Bella twins wore at one time.
Out-of-ring-attire: Just about everything Eden wears is tailored to fit her specifically, so generally very form-fitting but nothing pornographic or completely tasteless. Her style is sexy and edgy and she's very fond of shoes. The higher the heels, the better. She's also known to wear well-tailored suits and loves designer brands.
Taunts: Eden loves a good taunt. She'll do whatever she can to draw her opponent off his/her game whether that be verbal taunts, a middle finger or two, or a good slap across the face. She may be small but she packs a nasty bitch slap.
Personality: Eden at one time was a very sarcastic, impulsive, fly-by-the-seat-of-her-pants kinda girl. She tended to say things without thinking, and while sometimes she did mean to be rude, generally she didn't. She had a kind heart and a fun, wild spirit, all of which occasionally combined to get her into trouble. After the events of her time in the UGWC, Eden has changed. Now, Eden still possesses that sarcastic, impulsive nature, but she tends to keep it buried unless she needs it or is around someone she doesn't need to hide it from. Gone is the youthful exuberance and left behind, a cold and calculating exterior. She can act and lie with the best of them, her delivery smooth, and she frequently tests her skills on unwitting participants, just to see "if she's still got it." Eden is extremely manipulative with a lust for revenge. She loves to watch people squirm, looks for ways to learn what it is they most want, what they most like and she dangles it in front of them before snatching it away. In some ways, she's almost become a sociopath, or she's teetering very closely on that edge. When angered, Eden explodes, committing intense acts of violence be they on a person or an object, and these acts tend to rage uncontrollably. This invariably bothers her even more later because if there's one thing she craves now it's complete and utter control in all things. She will use her looks and her body as a lure, may even play the innocent or attempt to tug on the heartstrings of those who knew her before, playing them perfectly if they're not careful. Sometimes she continues the charade and sometimes she does it just to laugh in their face.
