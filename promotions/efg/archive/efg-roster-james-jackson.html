Battlemania Signup: James "Red Duke" Jackson

15 March 2015

Parkera — Mar 15, 2015 5:44 AM APPLICATION - Current EFed (if any): ANZAC Pro Wrestling APW Character Name: James "Red Duke" Jackson Height: 6'9 Weight: 315 lbs Hometown: Clarksburg, WV Theme Song: “Keep your hands to yourself” by Georgia Satellites Alignment: Face Wrestling Style: Brawler 10 Favorite Move(s): Military press slam Clawhold Sidewalk slam Spinebuster Clothesline Bear Hug Eye rake Cross Body Block Backbreaker DDT 5 Signature Move(s): • Body avalanche • Camel clutch • Cobra clutch slam • Front powerslam • Leg drop Set-up Move: Irish Whip to Ropes Finishing Move: Jackson Bootbomb Finishing Maneuver Description: Big Boot followed by a Powerbomb Character Details: (Gimmick, History, Look) ATTIRE: James comes to the ring wearing black boots and blue jeans. He wears the shirt of the local city, state team depending on what sport is in season at the time and on his hands he wears black finger less gloves.
ENTRANCE: He appears out from the curtain and clapping his hands to his music. As he makes his way to the ring he will acknowledge the fans especially the kids. He will circle the ring once and then climb over the top rope into the ring. He will then proceed to climb the nearest turnbuckle where he will sit and wait until his opponent arrives.
** If his opponent is already in the ring he will not climb the turnbuckle instead he will pace from one cornr to the other watching his opponent,. **
BIO: The son of Indie wrestler Stonewall Jackson born in Jackson, Alabama. Growing up he idolised his father with all the stories he told of life on the road, but as he got older their relationship become distant and strained as the young man realised his father wasted his talents travelling around hicksville towns never getting anywhere even when chances came his way he would remain loyal to no name promotions, just because they shook hands on it.
Unlike his father, James wants to actually win something and make a name for himself within the wrestling world and is determined that nothing will get in his way.
James is a menacing brawler who resides in Clarksburg, WV and who is noted for being very agile for a man his size. A powerhouse, James prefers to fist-fight, blitzing his opponents with hammering punches and clotheslines and other powerful moves. He has the look and manner of a face champion, he just needs to polish up his rough edges and sometimes his temper that boils to the surface.
