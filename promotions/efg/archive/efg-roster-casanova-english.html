Battlemania Signup: Casanova English

English — Feb 23, 2015 5:03 AM APPLICATION - Current EFed: VOW Character Name: Casanova English Height: 5'11 Weight: 170lbs Hometown: Prince Edward Island Canada Theme Song: "Real Solution Number 9" by White Zombie Alignment: Heel (slight cult following) Wrestling Style: Technical/Brawler 10 Favorite Move(s): Suplex, DDT, Back Drop, arm bar (usually leaping to ground bigger wrestlers quicker), Hurricanrana, Crossface, Boston Crab, Russian Leg Sweep, German Suplex, Spinning Heel Kick,
5 Signature Move(s): Lionsault Diving Headbunt Guillotine Choke Eblows to the face of downed oppoent Inverted DDT Set-up Move: English Lesson (Fameasser) Finishing Move: Silence of the Lamb Finishing Maneuver Description: A leaping variation of a triangle choke. More of a leaping hells gate where Casanova drags his opponent down with him like the codebreaker and locks in the choke.
Character Details: STRENGTHS Resilient never gives up no matter what, like a fucking cockroach. Intense Intelligent with surroundings WEAKNESSES Puts the body on the line Not a powerhouse Lack of experience PERSONALITY Casanova English is a existentialist, a philosopher, and an asshole. His main goal is to fight for the the equality of mankind, the way in which he plans to obtain that is off the ball and insane. He plans on convincing the people through his wrestling career that they too can get off their fucking asses and change the world. They too can take the power back. Think Rage Against the Machine. This is his stage to ignite a revolution. In other words he likes his own opinion too much.
BIOGRAPHY Casanova English (born Casanova Jones) had a rough up bringing. He raised himself and his other brother amongst the mist of his mothers drug addiction. A lengthy court battle landed Cass and his brother in the hands of their aunt and uncle. These where some of the kindest and caring people on the planet they owned a farm and allowed Cass to finally be at peace, unfortunately he was already 17 and his childhood already destroyed. Casanova went on to Carlton University where he studied journalism and started a career in that field until his Uncle fell ill with Alzheimer's disease. Casanova feeling like he must repay the sacrifices that man has made for him was the one with him on his death bed. This experience changed something in Casanova, he needed something bigger to fuel his desire. He no longer wants life to pass him by, he sets sale to inspire. . . though probably not in the most conventional, ethical, or moral ways.
