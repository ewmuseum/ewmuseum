Battlemania Signup: Austen Impact

21 February 2015

Austintatious26 — Feb 21, 2015 9:40 AM APPLICATION - Current EFed (if any): New Edge Wrestling Character Name: Austen Impact Height: 6'2" Weight: 240 Hometown: Minneapolis, MN Theme Song: Bring The Noise by Public Enemy and Anthrax Alignment: Face Wrestling Style: Technical 10 Favorite Move(s): German Suplex, Belly to Belly Suplex, DDT, Hammerlock, Powerslam, Heel Hook, High Angle Half Boston Crab, Fisherman's Gutbuster, Release German Suplex, Cloverleaf
5 Signature Move(s): Inverted DDT, Exploder Suplex, Regal Cutter, Double Underhook Powerbomb (Regal Bomb), Release German Suplex
Set-up Move: Package Piledriver Finishing Move: Impact Wrench (Regal Stretch) Finishing Maneuver Description: Just a simple Regal Stretch no modifications Character Details: (Gimmick, History, Look) Look is the typical All American Midwest boy (jack swagger is my pic base) grew up a state champion amateur wrestler, but shocked his home state of Minnesota when he committed to wrestling at the University only to shun it after graduation instead signing with NEW and taking a big contract. Very early in his run with NEW he won the now Defunct NEW Youngblood Championship off of Custard Creamer. During that run, he was inadvertently thrust into a political race for Governor of the state of Pennsylvania, which started as a gimmick but then gained ground, and due to that, had to retire from wrestling for a brief time, just recently returning.
