What Is BATTLEMANIA?

20 February 2015
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Jorden Snow — Feb 20, 2015 11:06 PM The EFed Guerilla Radio (Podcast) is proud to present to the eWrestling world; BATTLEMANIA!
What is BATTLEMANIA? Well, it is the brainchild of EFed Guerilla Radio founding father, El Gringo Loco. It is an interactive way to bring multiple people from all around the internet and from every eWrestling place we can find, together.
Specifically though? The BATTLEMANIA Event, is specifically one match. It is a 30 man, over the top rope, timed entrance battle royal (I.E. - The Royal Rumble).
So, 30 people only? Correct. We are taking the first 30 people to signup to the event. If the total number of signups has been reached, feel free to still submit the application. If there are any "no-shows," on a first come, first serve basis we will use those extra applicants as alternates.
Cool, hows it work? - Must be a registered member of fWrestling to take part in BATTLEMANIA, if you’re not a registered member, please register at http://www.fwrestling.com
- Application must be filled out completely in order to be included in the BATTLEMANIA event.
- There is a ONE roleplay maximum for BATTLEMANIA. - Roleplays should be no longer than 3,000 words. - Anyone who has been a host/co-host of EFG at anytime is not eligible to participate. When is the deadline to register? The registrations are open tentatively for 8 more days, meaning as of midnight on February 28th, 2015 (1am EST / 12am CST) the application process will be closed.
How long will I have to roleplay if I join? The roleplay process will be for 2 weeks (Saturday March 14, 2015 @ 1am EST / 12am CST). If at that time there are no-shows, the alternates will be given 1 week of extra time to roleplay.
When are the results going to come out? Friday April 3rd, 2015 on a live edition of the EFed Guerilla Radio (Podcast). We will both posts the results and release the winners name on that show.
Is there any prize for winning BATTLEMANIA? There currently is. Though, we are working on even more prizes.
1st Place - $150.00, An entire EFG show dedicated to their character, premium advertisement spot on the EFG website, a custom graphic representing your character and the recognition of being the winner of the first annual BATTLEMANIA! *Potential Prize - A custom made poser of your character*
2nd Place - $25.00 and an advertisement on the EFG website. 3rd Place - A custom graphic representing your character. - $75.00 donated by EFG BATTLEMANIA sponsor Dusty Shull (EWZine & The nbW) and $100.00 donated by EFG Colin ([detail withheld])
Who is judging this event? El Gringo Loco, Pete Russo, Keegan Carrahar and Jorden Snow of the EFed Guerilla Radio (Podcast). If other EFG hosts step up, their names will be added to this later.
NOW GO JOIN, BBAAAAATTTTTTLLLLLEEEEEEEEMMMMMAAAANNNNNIIIIIIAAAAA!!!
