APPLICATION - Current EFed (if any): ACE, GPW (fedhead), VoW Character Name: Jason Orion Height: 5ft6 Weight: 150lbs Hometown: Gary, Indiana Theme Song: 'Everybody's Got Something To Hide (Except For Me And My Monkey)', The Beatles Alignment: The most babyface of babyfaces Wrestling Style: Aerial 10 Favorite Move(s): 1. Shooting Star Press (the 'sixth signature') 2. 450 Splash 3. Seated senton 4. Enzuigiri 5. Slingshot crossbody 6. Diving forearm smash 7. Headscissors takedown 8. Hurricanrana 9. Arm trap wristlock 10. Frogsplash 5 Signature Move(s): 1. Small boot (get it?) 2. International Destroyer (because he's not Canadian) 3. Corner slingshot splash 4. 720 corkscrew plancha 5. 540 corkscrew moonsault Set-up Move: Nebula Kick (Superkick - also used as finisher) Finishing Move: Short But Sweet Finishing Maneuver Description: Front Flip DDT Character Details: Picbase: JoJo Bravo Gimmick: None. Just a genuine, no-frills nice guy. The most distinctive thing about him is his pet monkey, Spot Monkey, who usually accompanies him to ringside.
History: Jason Orion is a rookie just breaking through to the big leagues after several years wrestling for handshakes and ham sandwiches in Indiana. In his first big-leagues experience, he was able to capture the UWL American Championship, which he successfully defended last January, on his fourth-ever televised match. That was the moment when he shed his previous nickname, 'The Vanilla Midget', in favour of his (stylised) real name. He then joined Awesome Championship Entertainment, one of the promotions affiliated to the UWL, which he is representing at Battlemania.
Anything else you think we’d need to know about a character in order to write them into the Battlemania event? Jason is uncomfortable hitting women and will avoid it whenever possible.
