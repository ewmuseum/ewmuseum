Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

WFW's only surviving document is an open recruitment card, posted 2 December 1996: “JOIN THE WFW TO DAY... WITH YOUR WRETSLER NAME AND 5 MOVES AND TELL HIM IF HE IS BAD OR GOOD.” It called itself “A HARD CORE LEAGUE” that “DO ALL TYPS OF MATCHES,” and closed with a standing offer: “IF YOU WOULD LIKE TITLE MATCHES, PLEASE E-MAIL ME.”

The sample card it printed to make its case ran four matches. Evil Janitor beat Gold Warrior with a Mop Drop; Gunner won the U.S. Title from Tom Deathman with a Texas Cloverleaf submission; Chris Austin beat Shawn Michaels in a Backlot Brawl, closing on a bat shot and a powerbomb the announcer called for a broken back; and Ric Flair beat Zorpo in a footrace back to the ring in a Van Match — though, as the card itself notes, “ZORPO STILL HOLDS THE CHAMPIONSHIP,” the Intercontinental Title unmoved by the race's outcome.

It previewed a Monday Madness card next, headlined by a tag match pitting Rowdytirak's team of the Old Man and Chris the Man against Chris Austin and the Asswuper, and promised a house-show pay-per-view, “In Your House,” for 10 December 1996. Whether that show ran isn't in the surviving record — no further document survives beyond this one sample card.
