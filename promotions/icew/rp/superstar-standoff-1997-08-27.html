[ICEW] Superstar Standoff August 27, 1997

28 August 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
########### ######## ######## #### #### ### #### #### #### #### #### ### #### ###### #### #### ### #### #### #### #### #### #### ########### ######## ######## ##### ##### =============================================== *International Championship Extreme Wrestling* =============================================== S U P E R S T A R S T A N D O F F =============================================== [One lights comes on and it illuminates the blue desk in which "Uncensored" Jason Keller sits with his feet propped up on the desk. Keller is dressed in jeans and a white T-Shirt that reads "Totally Uncensored" in red. Keller realizes he is on and removes his feet from the desk and speaks.] JK: Welcome morons, peons, and peasants to ICEW Superstar Standoff. I am the most uncensored commenator on television, the great Jason Keller. Anyway we are here for one reason is to hear these freaking superstars speak. The only people in my opinion who are worth listening to are Poe and "Lucky Man" Dylan, but if you want to listen to these other guys dribble then go ahead. Let's start off for the man who claims to be the ICEW Cruiserweight Champion, but we all saw the screwy stuff he pulled with his sex toy Onslaught. Here is Jeff "Madfox" Matthews. =============================================== Jeff "Madfox" Matthews =============================================== [Jeff "Madfox" Matthews stands facing the camera. He's wearing a black t-shirt and his wrestling pants and boots. The ICEW Cruiserweight Belt is tightly secured around his waist. His face still shows a little scar tissue from when he was burnt but he has recovered. He pulls a black mask from his pocket and throws it into the garbage can.] JM: So...who's the fucking Cruiserweight Champion? [smiles] That's me...not Mr. Bungle...not Jason Polo....but Jeff "Madfox" Matthews. And it doesn't come as a surprise to me...because I am the man who deserves to hold this belt. Polo...you called my win foul. Look at the fucking contract you signed...it says " International Championship EXTREME Wrestling." You got that...if you can't handle being hit by a fucking chair then get the hell out of my league. It is MY league. Not Immorality's...seeing as I beat that man already in my career. But I will soon get the challenge I have been searching for. [sits down on a stool off to the left] JM: Suicide...you've had my number ever since I entered this league. Two meetings and two losses for me. Many might say that you have the advantage going into the match. [shakes head] Suicide, I am a changed man. I am the mother fucking MAN! Do you understand that? Do your little butt buddies understand that? I _AM_ and will _ALWAYS_ be the ICEW Cruiserweight champion. Not because I am perfect...but because you can not stop this juggernaut. I am on a roll...and frankly, I don't intend on losing to you. Bring the fucking CIA or whatever the fuck you want, and I'll take them all out my god damn self!! And that's a promise as good as gold. [pats the belt] JM: Suicide...it's not that you aren't good. It's just that I am better than you will ever see me. I am at the top of my game. I actually care about winning now. [laughs] I'm not out to hurt anyone anymore...I'm out to win. And that in all apects is scarier than waiting for my nexy scheme. Believe it or not Suicide.....you will be the next victim to fall into the Foxs Lair. But before I leave I want to say something to Mr. Dice. What the fuck is your problem you adolescent punk? Do you think that you can scare me with your bully tactics? I got news for you pal...I don't lower myself to your third grade level. I'm too much of a man ot degrade myself to the level of Dylan Dice. You're just another side of beef walking around the ICEW. No one in here..is man enough to step up to your so called "King of Hardcore" ass...except for Jeff "Madfox" Matthews. You want to do this Dice....you want to lay your fucking stubborn mule ass on the line? SO FUCKING BE IT!!! Bring your Lucky 7...your Boy Scouts....National Guard...because you're going to need all the help you can get to take me out. And it's not because I say so...it's because I know so. =============================================== [The shot fades back to the studio.] JK: Yes, Matthews, Suicide has kicked your ass twice already and he is going to do it for a third time this coming Saturday night, but this time he is going to take your strap. You got that! You call yourself a juggernaught, well, I will just have to get Immorality to light you on fire again! Then you will need that fucking mask to hide your disfigured face, not that it isn't already. [Keller takes a deep breath following his bashing of Matthews.] JK: This next guy is another whack job, I mean the dude dresses like he is some mid-evil executioner. He even acts as if the Guillotine is the only form of execution. Well, Executioner I got news for you, but now-a-days we fry your ass in the chair, gas your ass in the chamber, or inject with that lethal shit. Plus, we don't have some guy with a mask on do that shit no more. We have this fat ass warden who flips the switch. Got that? =============================================== The Executioner =============================================== [Scene opens inside an abandoned warehouse. The place looks very dim and empty. Executioner appears from out the darkness and starts walking towards the camera. He stops when he was 10 feet away from the camera. Then he stares at the camera, his big muscles and framed body shows as sweat drips down his biceps. He clenches his fist then begins to speak.] EX: Mr. Goat...I told your stupid ass that you were gonna get executed and you did. How did it feel when you got Guillotined? You wasn't worth my sweat. I didn't even break a sweat, I sweated more in Antarctica. But I still respect your courage for actually not forfeiting the match. Now....I have to wrestle in a Battle Royal for the Intercontinental Title on Saturday. [Executioner laughs.] EX: This is too easy. I have won most of my battle royal matches. I only lost one or two times. But you see...I don't want the IC Title, I want the World Title but I will win the Battle Royal and capture the IC belt. My name will feared by every wrestlers in ICEW. This bitch who calls himself Jason Starr thinks he's good but when I step in the ring, he will see why I am called the Executioner. I will try my best to make sure that IC Belt will be MINE. [Executioner turns around and walks out the warehouse. Camera fades to black.] =============================================== [The shot fades back to the studio.] JK: So Mr. Executioner man, you think Jason Starr is your bitch, well, when it comes battle royal time "The Hollywood Kid" is gonna make you his bitch, you pathetic excuse for, well, anything! Finally, we get to someone who is worth listening to, so for all you little kiddies sit down and listen to the gospel that this man will speak. =============================================== "Lucky Man" Dylan Dice =============================================== [Scene opens up to "Lucky Man" Dylan Dice who is sitting on a log in the desert in his home state of Arizona. Dice has a fire burning near him where he has decided to roast some marshmallows. Dice also has a cooler filled with "Lucky 7" beer and is smoking his traditional cigar. Dice is wearing a pair of blue jeans and a short sleeve shirt with the number "7" on it. Dice will now speak.] DD: This is the fucking place to be! Last week, I was in the shithole they like to call New Jersey and the place smelled so bad because of all the garbage thrown there from New York. Well, I guess it was a trade, New York's Garbage for New Jersey's Statue of liberty. I had the privelage of heading down to the old NIWF Arena to conduct some business. The only thing I don't like about that place is that it is an abandoned Pez Factory in Newark and all the niggers smell like shit. Well, that is one thing that bothers me but now I am back in my home state of Arizona. A place where the air is clean and the weather stays the same all the time and it doesn't fucking snow. I have a few days left to think about what the fuck I am going to do. Well, I must say this to start things off. It's about fucking time the president got off his lazy ass and did something! I mean, I was getting sick of hearing his excuses like: "I'm going to the beach" or some bullshit like that. We all know he is going to look at the guys is speedo's so it's too fucking bad! But what the fuck happens here? I look and I am not even on the fucking house show! It's a house show, God Damn it! I want to wrestle whenever the fuck I can get a chance to wrestle but Storm calls me up and tells me to take a rest! Does he not realize that if I take a rest, this cuts off some money from my paycheck? I have bills to pay, asshole and I want you to put me on a fucking house show next time so I can make some fucking money! But enough about the president. [Dice takes a puff on his cigar and he puts a marshmallow on a stick and begins to roast it over the fire. The marshmallow goes into flames and burns to be brown, just like Dice likes it. Dice shoves the whole marshmallow in his mouth.] DD: So, Poe daddy thinks I'm his bitch boy? That's too bad Poe! I told you I will have a surprise for you and that surprise may come soon enough. Who knows, I may have the surprise for you on the next card or I may have the surprise for you at the Ruler of the E-world tournament. Maybe I will save this big fucking suprise for a later date just to toy with your mind. You see, I could give two flying fucks about not winning that world title! The world title means jack shit to me! So fucking what if I have one loss under my skin? It's less then any of these other assholes who have like 6 losses or some bullshit like that. But Poe seems to want to make a big deal about it. Poe, neither I nor the rest of the ICEW give a fuck about our feud! You see, I know a little secret about the DIA and how it is not YOUR DIA! Isn't that a fucking shame that Poe really doesn't control the DIA? You want me to break kayfabe, then I'll break it just like I'll break your fucking neck! But Poe, you never know when the fuck I will come down and "help" you in your matches. You better watch out because one of these days I'm going to rip your fucking neck off and shit down your throat! But I have had enough of your sorry ass here in the ICEW! I think maybe it's time for a new challenge somwhere else for the both of us, and you know where to go and that is at hardcore hell! [Dice grabs a cold one out of his cooler and pops it open and chugs his can of "Lucky 7" beer. Dice then takes the can and throws it in the blazing fire.] DD: There is nothing like a cold fresh beer early in the morning! You see, that is the breakfeast of champions and that is what I am and what I soon will be! The champion. You know, I know I am capable of winning a fucking title here in the ICEW but it was just a matter of which title I was going to win in the ICEW. However, I want all of you to know that the world title tournament was fixed. Those fucking num-nut president's rigged it so I would lose due to outside interference. A day before the fucking thing, they came up to me and said: "Dice, you know you are the champion in all of our minds, why not give somebody else a chance?" Well, look who they gave the fucking title to! They gave the fucking title to Immortality, their best friend! Now, I see all of these stupid new faces coming into this fed. What is the first fucking thing these assholes want to talk about? ME!! All I hear is Dice this and Dice that! I even hear it from people who have never even fucking heard of me! It sickens me to see that people come in here and expect to have a match with a big star such as myself! Why the fuck would I want to face some new guy who just walked into this fucking fed? I'll tell you why, because I know I can beat the living shit out of any of the assholes. As a matter of fact, I already know who the mystery guy is but I ain't going to fucking tell you. You can thank me for bringing this guy into the ICEW! [Dice takes another puff on his cigar.] DD: But let me talk about one of the newcomers here in the ICEW! This is the man that goes by the name of Killa! I remember your punk ass back from the NIWF. You thought you were all hot shit because you brought your pussy cane to the ring! Boy, you can't do jack shit! You're a little pussy cat as far as I am concerned and I am the fucking lion! But now, you came into MY den and it's time to destroy your sorry ass. You think you have seen extreme? You have seen nothing yet, you fucking WIGGER! I sit back and watch you trying to be black and preach all this shit and talk ebonics. When the fuck are you going to learn that we speak English in this fucking country? Killa, you sure as hell know that you have to thank me for a lot of the shit that you have been thru. You seemed to develop a fucking reputation around here. But what people don't know is that I got your wife pregnant while you were wrestling in Japan the past few months! She is bearing my fucking child! I know what you're probably going to say: "I'm not married" or some shit like that. Well, it's a load of shit and you know it! So, if you go home tonight and your whore....er, I mean wife, tells you she is pregnant, then know that you are going to have a baby Dice! I've had enough of this bullshit! I'm going to win the fucking Battle Royal and walk out as new champion, I guran fuckin' tee it! Now, GET THE FUCK OUT OF MY FACE!!!! [Dice takes the camera and throws it into the fire and then stomps it out breaking it into pieces.] =============================================== [The shot fades back to the studio where Jason Keller is clapping.] JK: Bravo! Bravo! Now that is what I call a speech! Although I like Poe, it seems Dice has his number. Speaking of the Master of Mischief here he is. =============================================== Poe =============================================== [Fade in to a rather small room somewhere deep within the D.I.A. FORTRESS, which coincidentally makes the D.I.A. Mansion look like a barbie fun house... It is rather modern looking, with a full entertainment station against the wall behind Poe who is sitting at a nice wooden table staring at a fork. In the background, not too loud but understandable, "Regardless" by Shootyz Groove is playing. It slowly gains volume as Poe's face starts to tighten up and strain. He is still staring at the fork, the music is growing louder, staring, louder, STARING, LOUDER until...] POE: AUUUUUUGGGGGHHHHHHHH!!!! [The music stops as Poe stops screaming, and the fork is slightly bent. Poe looks at it in utter amazement and disbelief.] I DID IT! HOLY SHIT! I...oh. [Poe notices the camera is on and he thus turns to look into it. His face is slightly bruised and has some cuts, as do his visible arms because he is wearing one of LD's FACE YOUR DOOM t-shirts with the eyes of ZeX set eerily behind the words. <By the way this t-shirt is now available for $20.00 + shipping and handling, as a tribute to one hell of a match between LD and The Black King!] Hmmm, for once this wasn't a prepared flash. I thought my scheduled time was later tonight, but hell I wouldn't want to upset all the TRILLIONS of fans out there who hang on every word from Poe Daddy's mouth! HA ha ha... But I do have a lot to say and right now seems as good a time as ever to get it off my chest, before I do my business in the battle royal and take my time off. [Poe turns a little more and after pointing the remote at the stereo to allow "Don't Fight the Feelin'" by Too Short to be perfectly audible, he begins his little talk session.] POE: Ok, where to start? There is so much. Well let's start with the good stuff. I, The Master of Mischief Poe, not only recently won the HCW's Triangle title by pinning then champion Chris Hunter and HCW's new Heavyweight Champion Cyanide, but as of press time I have won the Ruler of the E-World Tournament title! That's right! Yours truly went through 73 other men starting with my first match against that piece of human garbage Cardiac, all the way up to my finals match where I pinned Mr. Bungle for the 1,2,3!!! Bungle flipped his shit man, he pulled out some document stating that he was supposed to win the whole tournament but imagine his surprise when I hit him with the RAVEN and put his whiny ass down for the three count! HA HA HA! I Love it! So there will be no more "who is Poe" or "what the hell has Poe done to deserve a title shot" BULLSHIT! Hell it pissed me off when that Hollywood Kid claimed he had never heard of me. But worse things have happened and I'm sure he'll know who I am and then some very soon. [Poe picks up the remote and switches the CD to Eazy E's "Boyz in tha hood", and continues] POE: The boys in tha hood are always hard, come talkin' that trash I'll pull your card... Knowin' nothing in life, but to be legit, don't quote me BOY cause I ain't said shit! Gotta LUV E-Z! But you gotta hate "Deuces Wild" Dan Williams, that little bitch. Why in the world did you think attacking me would accomplish anything? What is it, you want in on me and Dice's little dance? You want it to be a three way dance now? Hell Dice will sooner set you up for 7th Heaven before he would let anyone besides him take me out, and I want to be the first man here to pin Dice without any interference. Oh the bragging rights I'd have. His head would explode! But "Deuces Suck" Dan Williams, your attack won't be forgotten and mark my words, when it comes to Poe Daddy, the JOKER's WILD! And that spells trouble for you my friend! One last man to discuss before I go on about the battle royal and what I plan on doing afterwards. Rob Magnum P.I., or whatever the hell his gimmick is. Don't you cuss at me you third grade flunkie! Some people are so touchy around here it makes me sick. Here's the deal Robbie, don't talk about me again until you either wash that dirty little mouth of yours out with soap or you learn a new cussword, cause I'm predicting some repetativeness on your part if you don't. Ok big guy? GOOD! Next issue! [Poe jumps up on the wooden table for no reason, smiling and remaining in a crouched position, as if ready to pounce. He continues his tirade] POE: HA ha ha...scared ya didn't I? Don't worry, you're all safe for now, but come battle royal time it's going to be all Poe Daddy! Now I won't be like some of the complete morons and monkey boys around here and claim that I will beat ALL the competitors around until such time as I see fit to win the thing. No, no, no, I'm too smart for that method of attack because I know it will get you tossed right over the top and down onto the concrete floor. Another thing I refuse to do is to claim that this is MY match, and that I have never or rarely lost a battle royal...that ain't me either. Doesn't seem like I have much confidence does it? Well you are of course wrong, just like your parents were on that dark day in the back of the volvo when sperm met egg...but I won't go there! I have something special in mind for this one, and you will all get to see it soon enough. And I don't plan on letting nayone stop me from doing this something special because in all honesty, I don't think you can...it's SIMPLE! HA ha ha...I can't wait this is gonna be sweet, just plain sweet... And yes the rumors are true, right after the royal, Poe Daddy will be leaving you, BUT FOR A SHORT TIME ONLY! Hell I may only be gone for a week, or as long a a month, I just don't know yet. So if I win that Intercontinental Title I will have already cheapened it for the next man who holds it because I will obviously have to relinquish it right away! That man will be carrying Poe's title and there's nothing he can say or do about it until I come back...at which point a few people I previously mentioned will receive a proper dose of SingaPOE cane, washed down with their own blood. Bad 4 Life! Can you handle it?!!! [Fade as Poe takes a long and deep chug of that wonderful cola in the green can, Surge.] =============================================== [The shot fades back to the studio where Jason Keller appears to be fighting with himself.] JK: Poe.....Dice......Poe......Dice.....ARGHHHHHHHHHHHHH. [Keller slams his fists on the desk.] JK: Who do I choose? [Keller pauses for a moment and then regains his opponent.] JK: First, I would like to say Dan Williams, what the fuck were you thinking? When I first saw you, I said this kid might turn out to be something, then on Tuesday you go and attack Poe Daddy with a fucking slot machine. You just wrote your own death wish pal! Who the hell is next? Oh yeah, it is another bad ass! =============================================== Joel Gutman =============================================== [Gutman is seated on the sofa in his wood-paneled living room. He's wearing jeans and a black t-shirt that says, "House of Horror's, ICEW Branch Open for Business 8/26/97."] JG: The first customer for the House of Horror's checked in on Tuesday night. Mailbomber had his ticket to hell punched, so now there's a line formin', beginning with the battle royal for the IC belt. Step right up boys, there's always room for another victim. That belt was made for me and I don't give a rat's a$$ if I have to drive every stinkin' one of your heads through concrete to get it. Blood's gonna flow. Bones are gonna snap. I'm just gettin' warmed up and I'm gonna enjoy hearin' all the screams of anguish and torment as I stomp my way to title history. Now get outta my house, 'cause this tour is over! =============================================== [The shot fades back to the studio.] JK: Short, but sweet. This guy is going places! Before we go, I have one thing to say, "Iron Man" Mike Madland fuck you! Plus, Deathbringer, fuck you too! That is the truth, and I am Totally Uncensored, bitch! [The light turns off and only Keller and the desk's shadows can be seen as the credits roll.] +===============================================+ | International Championship Extreme Wrestling | | President: [name withheld] | | [detail withheld] | +========== http://icew.home.ml.org ==============+
