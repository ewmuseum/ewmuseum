[ICEW] Superstar Standoff August 24, 1997 (2/2)

24 August 1997

=============================================== Vile Vince Viper =============================================== [The camera slowly fades in on Vile Vince Viper he stands against a black backdrop. He wears his black snakeskin suit with orange New Breed tie. Greasing his hair back he coughs and wih a sneer addresses the camera.] V.V.V: Well, well...ICEW isss ssstill alive. I for one am ssshocked. I would like to offer my congratulationsss to Immorality for hisss fantassstic disssplay that got him the heavy weight title. For thossse of you who are wondering why I, Vile Vince Viper, am not the heavy weight champion I will make it quite sssimple. When that no talent Dice wasss kicked out of the tourney I didn't feel like fighting my partnersss in crime ssso I went out and ssshowed the officialsss that I don't fight friendsss. Besssidesss, I wasss paid more for that one appearance than any other wressstle got paid for wressstling the whole night! HaHaHa..think I'm lying? Mr.Bungle got ssso angry becaussse of it he quit! HaHaHaHa! [Vile Vince Viper lights up a cigarette.] Camera Person: You can't smoke in here. V.V.V: Watch me! HaHaHaHa...now, Bungle hasss gone and when I tried to fight with Dice the fucking officialsss told me that they had other plansss. I don't know what ssshit head wressstler wantsss a piece of me but bring it on, I WILL BEAT YOU DOWN!! Why? I'm the King of Sssnakesss...why elssse? Oh asss for thisss battle royal the officialsss got me sssigned up for? Real ssscary! I'm going to eliminate Dice unlesssss POE wantsss him then I am just going to let the ressst of you sssuckersss fight it out. Your looking at the firssst ICEW International Champion the only thing that can ssstop me from becoming champion isss if I find sssomething better and I let Loco win. Another thing, am I the only perssson in the battle royal that doesssn't have a gimmick? Heh heh...I mean we got "The Player", "The Sssexyboy", "No Worriesss", "Lucky Man", "MadMan" I mean maybe I ssshould change my name to "No Gimmicksss Needed" Vile Vince Viper "The King of Sssnakesss" "not to mention" "Icon of Extreme"! Wow....I would be unbeatable. Lisssten here punksss, I don't care which one of you I am going to eliminate to become international champion but mark my wordsss I am going to be the next champion in ICEW! HaHaHaHa [The camera fades out as Vile Vince Viper goes into hysterics.] =============================================== [The shot fades back to the studio.] J.K: He is the "Icon of Extreme" it was truely extreme when he and Dice fought it out on a bed of glass. That was cool, that was bloody, and that was truely hardcore! Next up is Jason Polo. =============================================== "The Player" Jason Polo =============================================== [The camera focuses on "The Player" Jason Polo sitting on a couch in his hotel penthouse. He is wearing grey baggy slacks and a black white silk shirt. He has been watching a replay of Hardcore saturday night. He takes a sip from a glass of wine and looks at the camera.] J.P: Well after a week in Hawaii "The Player" is back to continue giving out wrestling lessons. For all the new members I am the sexiest man alive "The Player " Jason Polo. Now my last match was in the semis of the cruiserweight title tournament and I lost to Jeff "Madfox" Mathews. Well Jeff we both know the shit you pulled to win that match and that it was foul. We'll meet later on down the road, but first I have to take care of some other business. Suicide, you had your little friend jump me after I beat you. I never forgive and never forget. I'm going to be coming for you soon. Count on it." [Polo calls for a woman in the kitchen to bring him another drink. She brings it to him and he pats her behind and shoos her away.] J.P: Now I've been thinking about my style and after losing my last match I've decided that i'm going to have to get hardcore. You fools in the fed think your extreme? You haven't seen anything yet. I'm going to let loose on anyone in the ring with me, and I'm going to start on Saturday in that battle royal. You all better get ready for a new side of Jason Polo, because i'm going to take out whoever steps to me. No more games! =============================================== [The studio comes into the shot once again.] J.K: This guy actually thinks he can take any member of the Triple Threat. Who does this guy think he is? I mean if he were fucking Superman he might be able to do, but he can't do it when he is just "The Player." That's it for tonight and where did I put the Awesome Alliance's phone number.
