[ICEW] Hardcore Saturday Night (5/5)

17 August 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Skiba cinches Immorality for the move, but Immorality blocks it and throws Skiba off the top rope. Skiba hits the mat and slowly gets back to his feet. Immorality decides to greet Skiba with a flying clothesline that sends the Plastic Surgen into a 360 before hitting the mat hard. Immorality covers 1-- 2-- Skiba kicks up. Immorality holds Skiba on the mat with his left arm and smashes Skiba's nose with repeated fore arms from his right arm. Skiba can be heard mumbling obsceneties after each blow. Immorality continues with the fore arms, Skiba takes them for a moment before grabbing Immorality's arm and head butting the Plastic Surgeon. Immorality falls backwards onto his butt and Skiba gets to his knees and clothesline the seated Immorality to the mat, he then covers 1-- 2-- Immorality kicks up. Skiba then works Immmorality's legs into a figure four, but Immorality is able to extend his 6'11" body enough so that he grabs the ropes and Skiba is forced to break the hold.] S.S: Back and forth, back and forth, would somebody get an advantage. D.F: I figured you would say would somebody get a damn chair. S.S: I was going to say that, but you cut me off jack ass. [The Plastic Surgeon backs up and motions with his hands for Immorality to get up. Immorality starts to get up, but Skiba takes advantage of Immorality's slow movement and side kicks the Immoral One in the jaw. Immorality hits the ropes and stumbles back into the center into a clothesline by the Plastic Surgeon. Skiba pulls Immorality up and hooks him for a belly to belly suplex. Skiba falls back driving Immorality's back into the mat, he then bridges up for the pin 1-- 2-- Immorality kicks free. Skiba gets up and stomps on Immorality's spine before yanking the Immoral One up to his feet by his long black hair.] D.F: Glenn "Plastic Surgeon" Skiba is definitely in control now. He has been pounding on Immorality for the last couple minutes. [Glenn Skiba head butts Immorality, but holds onto Immorality's arm to prevent the big man from falling. Skiba Irish whips Immorality into the ropes and wraps Immorality up in a sleeperhold. Immorality swings his arms around frantically trying to break the hold.] S.S: A sleeper by Skiba this could be fatal. D.F: If Skiba can hold onto this hold and put Immorality away he could be the very first ICEW World Heavyweight Champion. [Immorality's arm movements begin to slow down. Skiba begins to beam a smile as he feels Immorality's body start to go limp. Immorality's knees give way as Skiba slowly drops down with Immorality. Skiba allows Immorality to become seated and Skiba puts a knee in Immorality's back to keep him seated. The referee realizes that Immorality is out and raises his arm in the air, it falls, he raises Immorality's arm a second time and it falls. The ref raises Immorality's arm for a third and final time.....] D.F: It stays up! Immorality is still in the game. [Immorality's body suddenly starts moving as the Immoral One climbs to his feet, he starts jamming elbows into the mid-section of Skiba causing the Plastic Surgeon to break the hold. Immorality hits the ropes and leaps at Skiba with a crossbody block, but the Plastic Surgeon catches him and delivers a back breaker on the Immoral One. Skiba then covers the very tired Immorality 1-- 2-- Immorality kicks up.] D.F: Immorality was coming back, but Skiba halted his parade. S.S: Hey, I know how Immorality could get back on the offense pretty quickly.... D.F: We know use a chair.... S.S: No, no, knee him in the balls. [Skiba pulls Immorality up and kicks him in the mid-section causing the Immoral One to bend over and allowing Skiba to execute a reverse neck breaker. Skiba covers quickly after the move 1-- 2-- Immorality kicks up. Skiba pulls Immorality up to his knees, he pulls Immorality's hair so that the Immoral One is looking into this eyes he then yells something at Immorality and Immorality punches the Plastic Surgeon in the mid-section. Skiba backs up and Immorality gets to his feet he then runs at the Plastic Surgeon and clotheslines him over the top rope. Immorality decides to rest a bit as his pack of wolves on the outside go to work on the Plastic Surgeon. Viper, Suicide, and Yuko all put the boots on Skiba while Immorality chats with the referee. Suicide then tosses Immorality back into the ring and Immorality rolls the Plastic Surgeon over and covers 1-- 2-- Skiba kicks up.] S.S: The ICEW Triple Threat went to work on Skiba and that bought Immorality time to rest. D.F: Well, that is cheating, but it did work. S.S: Cheating that was just bending the rules Fitzgerald. [Immorality drags Skiba to his feet and whips him into the ropes, he then delivers a boot to the face of the Plastic Surgeon. Immorality picks Skiba up again and Hot Shots him onto the top rope. Skiba's neck gets caught on the top rope and Immorality lifts Skiba's legs up in the air. This move causes a lot of weight to be placed on Skiba's neck and starts to cut off Skiba's path of breath. The ref asks Immorality to break the hold and Immorality does so. He then pulls Skiba to the center of the ring and presses him high in the air. He walks around the ring holding the Plastic Surgeon like a baby, he then throws Skiba in the air and Skiba drops hard to the mat. Immorality covers 1-- 2-- Skiba kicks up. Immorality pulls the Plastic Surgeon up and plants him in the mat with a front layout suplex. Immorality then lifts Skiba up onto his shoulders.] D.F: Euthanasia coming up. [Skiba however shifts his weight and rolls Immorality up in a crucifix pinning combination 1-- 2-- Immorality breaks free. Immorality rolls to his feet, but Skiba dropkicks him to the mat. Skiba then hits the ropes and splashes Immorality and then covers 1-- 2-- Immorality kicks up. Skiba then climbs to the top rope, he waits for Immorality to get to his feet he leaps for a crossbody block, but Immorality catches him and throws him up on his shoulders, he then drops back in the....] D.F: Euthanasia by Immorality! S.S: This could be it as Immorality covers 1-------2--------3!!!!!! D.F: He won it. [Ding! Ding! Ding!] R.A: The winner of this match and the very first ICEW World Heavyweight Champion Immorality! [Sugar Ray's "RPM" plays over the P.A. as the fans go nuts and the referee hands Immorality the belt. Immorality holds the belt high in the air and the cameras start flashing again. Immorality then straps the belt around his waist and his stablemates celebrate with him. Over this celebration Scott Storm's voice can be heard.] S.S: That is it we got to get out of here! +=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= I * C * E W =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=+ | | | President: [name withheld] | | [detail withheld] | | | +=-=-=-=-=-=-=- http://www.geocities.com/Colosseum/7407=-=-=-=-=-=-=-+ (WEB SITE NOT OPERATIONAL)
