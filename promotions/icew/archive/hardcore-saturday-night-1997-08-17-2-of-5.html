=============================================== Cruiserweight Tournament 1st Round: Mr. Bungle w/Mr. Johnson Vs. "The One Man Crime Spree" Adam Kuhl =============================================== WRITER: ZS [Chris Haley for once does not have the distraction of any young ladies at ringside as he simply steps to the center of the ring and raises his mic.] R.A: The following contest is a 1st Round match-up in the Cruiserweight Tournament! [Mr. Bungle walks through the curtain decked out in black wrestling attire. He strokes his infamous goatee with one hand and twirls his pine walking stick as he waits for Mr. Johnson to appear at his side. Johnson does and Bungle stops his stroking of his goatee and his twirling of his cane as he walks to the ring with a smirk on his face.] S.S: What is he so happy about? [The scruffy Adam Kuhl walks down the isle hitting his baseball bat against the palm of his hand. He screams obsceneties at the fans as he walks the isle. He then stops before the steps and points the bat at Bungle. Bungle simply gives a short laugh, but Kuhl then swings the bat and it hits the ringpost. Kuhl then begins to laugh chaotically as he steps through the ropes and waves his bat around.] D.F: Are you going to allow him to use that bat in the ring? S.S: Yeah! [Ding! Ding! Ding!] D.F: He still has that bat this is not going to be good at all. [Kuhl charges at Bungle with the bat pulled back behind his bat. Kuhl swings at Bungle, but Bungle ducks down and sweeps Kuhl's legs out from under him. Kuhl hits the mat and drops the bat, Bungle quickly follows up his move with a leg drop. He then pulls Kuhl up and positions him for...] S.S: Inverted DDT by Bungle! Beautiful move. [Bungle however decides not to cover and instead pulls Kuhl up once again, he whips "The One Man Crime Spree" into the ropes and Bungle hits the opposite ropes. Bungle launches himself at Kuhl and takes him out with a flying forearm. Bungle then covers.] [_1_] [_2_] S.S: Kick by the Crime Spree. This Kuhl is being stunned by Bungle early. However, that does not stop Kuhl as he grabs his bat and climbs to the top rope he then leaps and executes a double axe handle from the top rope, but instead of his fists hitting Bungle in the head it is the baseball bat that connects with Bungle's forehead. Bungle falls on his face and Kuhl drives the bat into the back of Bungle. Kuhl for some odd reason tosses the bat aside and Hot Shots Bungle onto the guard rail causing Bungle to bite his tongue. Kuhl turns Bungle around and delivers a punch to the bloody mouth of Mr. Bungle. Bungle flips backwards into the fans as a result of this punch. S.S: Well, yeah. D.F: Why isn't the ref counting? S.S: I specifically told the refs no countouts for tonight's tournament. [Kuhl has by this time put a serious dent in the frying pan. He then discards the pan and pulls Bungle up. He leads Bungle by his head and leads him through the tunnel leaving the fans. Kuhl then Irish whips Bungle into the counter of a nearby consession stand. Bungle's back hits the counter hard and Kuhl charges at him. Bungle ducks and back body drops Kuhl over the counter. Kuhl flies over the counter almost takes out a cook and ends up falling onto the stove knocking it over. However, Bungle can not keep his attack going as the punishment Kuhl has dished out leaves Bungle out of breath and energy. Kuhl isn't in good shape either as he finally sits up and brushes off some hamburges and hot dogs before stumbling to his feet. He searches around for Bungle. By this time, Mr. Johnson has made his way through the fans and out into the consession area. He gives Bungle a sip of water, but by this time Kuhl has found Bungle. Johnson backs up as Kuhl leaps over the counter. He delivers a kick to the face of Bungle as suddenly Poe runs by them and a few feet behind him is Dylan Dice.] S.S: Where did they come from? D.F: Is Dice still chasing Poe? This is amazing. [Poe and Dice run off the screen as this seems to draw the attention of Kuhl. However, the Crime Spree shrugs his shoulders and picks Bungle up. He sets him up for a suplex, but Bungle blocks it and suplexes Kuhl onto the concrete floor. Bungle stands up and stares at Kuhl with pure rage. He then backs up runs and leaps for a splash, but Kuhl moves at the last minute. Bungle ends up doing a belly flop on some concrete. Kuhl staggers to his feet and pulls Bungle up, he then leads Mr. Bungle down the hall and up the ramp towards the second level.] S.S: They are going to the second level this could get ugly. Bungle runs and frankensteiners Adam Kuhl onto the concrete. Bungle catches his breath once again before pulling Adam up. Bungle then takes him to the nearest tunnel leading to the second level fans. Bungle and Kuhl come through the tunnel and stand at the top of a flight of stairs. Bungle picks Kuhl up in the air and tosses him down the stairs. Kuhl tumbles down the stairs hitting his head on just about every cement step on is way down. Mr. Bungle then pulls Kuhl up and goes for suplex, but Kuhl somehow manages to block it. Kuhl then lifts Bungle up in the air and holds him for a verticle suplex. Kuhl then lets Bungle drop over the edge.] D.F: Oh my GOD! Bungle has fallen from the second level. [The shot catches Mr. Bungle falling some one hundred feet down into the first level fans who manage to cushion the fall of Mr. Bungle by trying to catch him as if he were a stage diver, but the force behind Bungle's fall causes him to drop through the hands of the fans and fall not so hard to the floor. Kuhl looks over the edge and laughs at Bungle who lays probably unconcious on the floor. He then goes to walk up the stairs, but he is halted by Mr. Johnson. Kuhl takes a swing at Johnson, but Johnson ducks and lifts Kuhl into the air and throws him off the second level.] S.S: Kuhl goes off the edge, Kuhl goes off the edge. [Kuhl however does not land anywhere near the fans, he falls into a large trash bin that is behind the ring level fans. Kuhl disappears from the camera's sight as Bungle is now being helped up by the fans.] D.F: I don't know if Kuhl had a good fall or not he landed in that huge trash bin. S.S: Wait look that is Kuhl climbing over the top. [Kuhl indeed climbs over the top of the trash bin and starts to walk throught he fans. The fan are trying to help Bungle up, but Kuhl pushes his way through them and clotheslines Bungle to the floor. Kuhl takes Bungle and leads him through the fans and throws him over the guard rail. The seemingly unphased Adam Kuhl then climbs over the guard rail and hits a double stomp on Mr. Bungle. Adam Kuhl throws Mr. Bungle into the ring and then crawls in and covers.] [_1_] [_2_] S.S: Foot on the ropes, foot on the ropes. Bungle stays alive with a foot on the ropes. [Kuhl now drags Bungle towards the center of the ring and covers him.] [_1_] [_2_] S.S: Kick up. [Adam Kuhl now completely irate with referee Kyle Hickman gets in the small referee's face. Kuhl then shoves Hickman down to the mat and points at him and yells "Count fucking faster." Hickman backs down from no man as he stands up and reads Kuhl the riot act. Bungle now gets to his feet, he climbs to the top rope as the arguement continues.
