[ICEW] Hardcore Saturday Night 3/3

31 August 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
RA: "Madman" Matt Howard! [Matt Howard comes jogging through the curtain wearing his usual black and gold tights plus a "Madman" Matt Howard T-Shirt.] RA: The Executioner! [The Executioner walks through the curtain wearing all black, the thing that comes out the most is the red "X" on his mask. He steps into the ring and waits for the competition to begin.] JK: Where the hell is this mystery man? KT: How would I know? RA: And finally our mystery man in this competition, here is Deathbringer! [Deathbringer walks through the curtain wearing his usual cowl and holding his scythe. Deathbringer however stops at the ring steps and removes the cowl. As he drops it to the mat it reveals the tatooed arms of....] KT: "Iron Man" Mike Madland! JK: Mike Madland is Deathbringer. KT: No it was a hoax the whole time, Madland, has been pretending to be Deathbringer to fool everybody. [Mike Madland slides into the ring, but immediately recieves stomps from both the Executioner and Joel Gutman. The duo then pulls the "Iron Man" up and whips him into the ropes they then catch him on the rebound with a double axe handle. Soon all of the men are brawling, Poe and Dice go at it in one corner while Killa struggles to get at Dice, but Adam Kuhl and Jason Starr push him towards the rope and they try to force him out of the ring. LOCO and Vile Vince Viper double team "Madman" Matt Howard in the corner, but there brawl is soon interupted by Joel Gutman crashing into the corner after a Mike Madland clothesline. Madland then scoops up the Executioner and plants him in the mat with a powerslam.] KT: Mike Madland was getting double teamed, but now he just took out both the Executioner and Joel Gutman. JK: Uh-oh Dice just leveled Poe with that hickory stick. [Dice indeed does take Poe out with a hickory stick and he walks over to a corner where Jason Starr and Adam Kuhl are holding Killa still. Dice winds up and nails Killa square in the face with his hickory stick and then starts laughing as Killa struggles to get free. However, Poe smacks Dice in the back with his SingaPOE cane, but Dice isn't phased due to the armor covering his body.] JK: Dice is brilliant he is wearing armor on his body, so no one can phase him. KT: The only way they can get to him is if they all team up and throw him over the top rope. [By this time LOCO and Vile Vince Viper have gotten Matt Howard close to elimination. Mike Madland continues hammering on the Executioner as Joel Gutman stands around watching the brawls going around him. He then sees how close Matt Howard is to elimination and charges the corner clotheslining both Viper and LOCO causing Howard to falls over the top rope and to the floor.] RA: "Madman" Matt Howard has been eliminated! [Viper and LOCO then turn their attention to the man who clotheslined them that being Joel Gutman, the duo hits the soul propeter of the House of Horrors with vicious double axe handles to the forehead. Poe continues the viciously swing his SingaPOE cane at Dice, but to no avail, as Dice simply looks down and picks up a television that came from his garbage can. He then smashes Poe over the head with it, the Master of Mischief then stumbles into the corner.] JK: Dice is invincible. KT: I dunno, but Killa seems to be breaking free of the beating he is recieving by Jason Starr and Adam Kuhl. [Killa begins to swing his arms around and knocks Adam Kuhl to the mat, but Starr halts Killa's movement with a knee to the stomach. Kuhl then gets to his feet and jabs the baseball bat into this mid-section. Mike Madland powerbombs the Executioner to the mat and then picks up the empty garbage can and smashes it over his foe's head. Joel Gutman continues getting pounded on in the corner, but he then charges out and hits a double clothesline taking both Viper and LOCO down.] KT: This is full fledged war. JK: You got that right. We have LOCO and Viper going after Joel Gutman, Mike Madland battling the Executioner, Poe trying to take out Dylan Dice, Jason Starr and Adam Kuhl beating the daylights out of Killa. What more can you ask for? We got 10 men beating the hell out of each other. [Joel Gutman tries to catch a breather, but LOCO connects with a British bulldog onto a kitchen sink that just happened to be laying on the mat. Obviously coming from Dylan Dice's garbage can. Dice now piledrives Poe into a pile of pots and pans, he then pops open a can of Lucky 7 beer and begins drinking it in the corner as Poe lies there in abosolute pain. Mike Madland presses Executioner over his head, but does not dump him over the top rope as Vile Vince Viper kicks the "Iron Man" in the mid-section causing Madland to drop the Executioner. Viper then grabs the now smashed up kitchen sink and levels Madland with it.] KT: Viper is going after Madland. Here comes LOCO to join in. [LOCO and Vile Vince Viper stomp away on the fallen "Iron Man" as Joel Gutman lays motionless on the mat. Killa finally breaks free of Jason Starr and Adam Kuhl. He runs into the center of the ring and grabs a hockey stick. Starr and Kuhl run at Killa and Killa levels them both with the stick. Starr falls to the mat and rolls under the bottom rope. Kuhl meanwhile is scooped up and pressed in the air by Killa, Killa walks over to the ropes and dumps the "One Man Crime Spree" over the top.] RA: Adam Kuhl has been eliminated! [Joel Gutman finally gets up and gets a hold of a cheese grater and smashes it on the back of LOCO's head. This little incident allows Mike Madland to clothesline Vile Vince Viper to the mat, however, Madland is then ambushed by the Executioner. Killa goes into the corner after Dylan Dice who still drinks his beer, but he is tackled from behind by Jason Starr. Dice finishes off his beer only to drop the television on Poe once again.] KT: This is wrong Dylan Dice should not be allowed to wear that armor in the ring. I mean look at him he is the most rested man in there. He has already finished off three beers in that six pack while Poe just lays there on the mat. JK: Isn't it great. [Jason Starr DDTs Killa into the mat. "The Hollywood Kid" then begins to flaunt his accomplishment. Joel Gutman picks LOCO up and tries to throw him over the top rope, but LOCO locks onto the top rope, so Gutman starts to pound on LOCO's back. Viper meanwhile lounges in a corner as the Executioner powerbombs Mike Madland into the mat. Dice finishes off his sixth and final beer, he stands up and drags Poe to his feet to try to toss the Master of Mischief over the top rope, but Poe somehow stops him. Jason Starr now takes the hockey stick in which Killa used on him and breaks it over Killa's head. Vile Vince Viper sees his partner LOCO close to elimination as he charges the corner, but it is too late as Gutman dumps LOCO over the top rope.] RA: LOCO has been eliminated. [Out of revenge Viper sneaks up behind Joel Gutman and dumps him over the top rope and to the floor. This allows LOCO to get some more revenge by pounding away at the cranium of Gutman on the outside. Soon a clutter of referees push the two brawling men back to the locker room.] RA: Joel Gutman has been eliminated. [The Executioner whips Mike Madland into the ropes and ducks down for a back body drop, but Mike Madland halts and kicks the Executioner square in the face. Madland then rebounds off the ropes and splashes him. However, Vile Vince Viper charges out of the corner wielding Killa's Singapore cane, he smashes the Iron Man with it and once again Madland finds himself on the mat.] KT: Madland has been getting beaten down since this match began. JK: Look Dice has removed his helmet and is smashing Poe with it. [Dice indeed begins smashing Poe with his helmet causing the Master of Mischief to be busted wide open. However, Dice is slammed into the corner when Mike Madland tosses Vile Vince Viper into him. Madland then scoops up the Executioner and tosses him over the top rope.] RA: The Executioner has been eliminated. [Madland finally gets a breather in a corner while Dice removes his armor that was covering his legs and smashes Viper over the head with it. Viper stumbles into the center of the ring and Dice hits him with the armor once again.] JK: Uh-oh Jason Starr has the Starrlock on Killa in the corner. KT: This is bad for Killa. [Killa slowly starts to go to sleep, but Mike Madland charges the corner sending the "Hollywood Kid" falling backwards. Madland shoves Jason Starr in the corner and throws vicious right hands at the face of Starr. Killa slowly crawls to his feet after the vicious attack from Jason Starr. Dice meanwhile removes his torso armor and smacks Vile Vince Viper over the head with that too. By this time, both Killa and Poe have made it to their feet. They both simultaneausly charge the corner and pin Dice against the turnbuckles as both of them kick at the mid-section of Dice.] KT: Dice is recieving his first beating of the night this is simply amazing. JK: Well, I guess he should not have removed any of his armor. [Vile Vince Viper stumbles to his feet and picks up the dented garbage can only to smash it over Mike Madland's head buying Starr time to catch a breather in the corner. The King of Snakes continues to further dent the can with the "Iron Man's" head. Viper then tosses the can aside and Irish whips Madland into the ropes and he hits the Satan Strut on the "Iron Man." However, instead of attempting to eliminate the "Iron Man," Viper decides to charge the corner to give Jason Starr a pounding, but the rested Starr ducks and sends Viper flying over the top rope.] RA: Vile Vince Viper has been eliminated! KT: We are down to five men, "Iron Man" Mike Madland, Killa, Poe, "Hollywood Kid" Jason Starr, and "Lucky Man" Dylan Dice. [Soon a fight erupts between Killa and Poe who were double teaming the King of Hardcore. Killa slaps Poe in the face and the Master of Mischief knees Killa below the belt and then DDTs Killa into the mat. This little time allows Dylan Dice to slide to the outside and duck under the ring. A group of referees try to pull Dice out, but Dice fights them off. Meanwhile in the ring, Jason Starr sees Madland laying on the mat not moving, he then decides to apply the Starrlock on the "Iron Man" who quickly begins to fall asleep.] KT: If Jason Starr keeps that hold on Mike Madland longer than usual this could be fatal to the "Iron Man's" career. JK: I think that is what he is planning on doing. [Poe whips Killa into the ropes and clotheslines the violent one on the rebound. Poe then scales to the top rope.] KT: What is Poe thinking? Doesn't he realize that it is dangerous to climb to the top rope during a battle royal. [Poe leaps high in the air and spreads his wings like a raven and then plummets towards the mat with his infamous splash properly named the Raven. However, Killa sees Poe coming and rolls out of the way. Poe smacks the mat hard as Killa stands up and drops an elbow. Starr continues to hold Mike Madland in the Starrlock. Killa then takes Poe by the throat and steps up on the second rope he then chokeslams him down to the mat on the very dented garbage can.] KT: Chokashmoka by Killa! JK: Chokamokashokakoka, what the hell are you talking about? KT: Chokashmoka it is Killa's infamous second rope chokeslam. JK: Taylor, are you speaking dumb ass or something? [Starr finally releases the Starrlock on the now unconsious Mike Madland, he then proceeds to lift Madland up on his shoulder and toss him over the top rope.] RA: "Iron Man" Mike Madland has been eliminated! KT: Madland is gone we are down to four, Killa, Poe, Starr, and Dice who still who is still under the ring. [Dice now creeps out from under the ring and attacks Killa from behind with his own Singapore cane. Killa drops to the mat fast as Dice continues to lash out with the cane on Killa's back. Poe still lays motionless and Jason Starr takes the oppurtunity to lock the Starrlock on the Master of Mischief.] JK: That's like the fifth guy Starr has put that move on. I think he ether is running out. KT: Ether, what ether? JK: Umm, ether did I say ether, I mean the effect must be running out, yeah that's what I said. [Dice sees Star applying the hold on Poe and decides to smack his old nemesis over the head with the long bamboo stick that is the Singapore cane. Starr quickly hits the mat as Poe collapses on the mat seemingly out of it. Dice then pulls Killa up on his knees and begins to choke him with the Singapore cane.] JK: This is great Dylan Dice is beating on three people at a time. KT: Wow, just great. [Starr finally stumbles to his feet and he picks up Poe's SingaPOE cane and hits Dice over the head with it. Dice's forehead begins to bleed as he falls backwards on the mat. Killa and Poe don't move, but Starr continues caning Dice in the face, causing more cuts and more blood to seep out of the "Lucky Man's" forehead. By this time, Poe begins to crawl towards the ropes to attempt to pull himself up, he gets to his feet and focuses his eyes to see Jason Starr using his SingaPOE cane, he then picks Dice's kitchen sink up and smashes Starr in the back of the head with it causing the "Hollywood Kid" to go tumbling over the top rope.] RA: "The Hollywood Kid" Jason Starr has been eliminated! KT: Dice is in trouble now because it is Poe and Killa in there against him both men want to tear his face off. JK: Come on Poe Daddy. KT: A minute ago you were cheering for Dice. JK: Ah shut-up. [Poe continues using the kitchen sink smashing Dice in the back of the head with it. Dice drops to all fours, but this doesn't stop Poe who simply drives the sharp ends of sink into the back of the neck of Dice causing grave pain for the "Lucky Man." Killa now gets to this feet with help from the ropes he picks up his Singapore cane and measures a shot for Poe, who doesn't see Killa at all, Killa swings and connects, hitting Poe in the back of the head causing Poe's blonde hair turn a blood red. Poe drops to both knees and Killa raises his cane in the air to smack Poe square in the forehead. Killa leaves Poe alone and starts caning Dice in the back. Bloody welts form on Dice's back with each cane shot, but Dice still struggles to get up. However, each time the cane hits Dice falls back down a bloody mess. Poe starts crawl on his stomach towards the corner to attempt to get up once again.] KT: What do you think of Dice and Poe now? Killa is completely beating the hell out of the both of them. JK: Did you say hell? I think you said hell, your not supposed to use those kinds of words on television, because I am supposed too. Dammit! [Poe struggles to his feet, but he sees Killa standing a little close to the ropes, Poe hits the ropes and launches himself towards Killa hitting him in the side of the head with a flying forearm, but the impact behind Poe's dive sends Killa flying over the top rope and down to the floor.] RA: Killa has been eliminated! KT: We have Dice vs. Poe! This could get real ugly. JK: Your damn right it can. [However, Poe can not attack Dice as he collapses on the mat from the beatings he has recieved through out the match. Dice doesn't fare to well either as he still struggles to try to get up, but each time he gets up the pain kicks in and he drops back to the mat.] KT: This is amazing these two bloody athletes hate each other so much, yet they can not get to their feet to settle their differences. JK: Hey, why isn't that freak Killa leaving ringside? KT: I don't know. [Dice finally seems to get a hold of the ropes and starts slowly moving up to his feet, but as does Poe who stands up Dice's dented garbage can and pulls himself up. The two men then see each other and stand toe to toe in the center of the ring. Soon a slugfest emerges both men firing hard punches at the others face, however, Poe starts to get an advantage hitting Dice repeatively with hard right hands, then he Irish whips Dice into the ropes, Killa pulls down the top rope, but Dice reverses the Irish whip and Poe goes towards the ropes and falls over the top rope with the top rope lowered. Dice drops to the mat a bloody mess as the bell rings.] RA: Poe has been eliminated! [The fans begin to pop as Poe and Killa begin to trade blows on the outside.] RA: So the winner of this match and the very first ICEW Intercontinental Champion "Lucky Man" Dylan Dice! [Metallica's "Holier Than Thou" plays over the P.A. system as the fans pop loudly and Dice struggles to his feet his body now a color of bright red due to the large amount of gaping wounds on his body. Dice stands as President Schooley climbs in the ring to give Dice, the belt, Dice snatches it away and shoves the President down, he then flips him off and proceeds to tell him off. Schooley says something back to him and then exits the ring.] KT: Dice is the champion, but he is going to need a hell of a lot of stitches. JK: You got that right, but I love this song, it is jive turkey! KT: Well, this has been a card of epical proportions, good night everybody! [Keller starts to dance around the ring as a bloody Dice thrusts the belt high in the air to a loud pop as the shot fades to the credits.] +===============================================+ | International Championship Extreme Wrestling | | President: [name withheld] | | [detail withheld] | +========== http://icew.home.ml.org ==============+
