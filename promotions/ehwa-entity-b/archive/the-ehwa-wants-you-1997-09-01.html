The EHWA Wants YOU!!!!!

1 September 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Are you a good roleplayer that likes fantasy wrestling and craves the real stuff that goes on in REAL wrestling, like contract signings, match bonuses, "CASH", bribery, stables, and much, much more? Then the Extreme Hardcore Wrestling Alliance WANTS YOU!!!!!! If you would like to join, please e-mail [detail withheld] and request an EHWA application. Or cut and paste this one and send it to me. You must also agree to these rules as well: Extreme Hardcore Wrestling Alliance (EHWA) Do you seek REAL wrestling action? Like all the stuff you hear about through the WWF, WCW, and ECW? Well, how fortunate, since the EHWA is here!!! This is the elite league of ALL e-leagues. Contract signings, match commissions, bonuses, injuries, abilities, "money", bribery, everything you see in real wrestling!!! So, if you're ready for the blast of e-wrestling that you need, join the EHWA now!!! Titles: EHWA World Champion EHWA Television Champion EHWA Tag Champions EHWA Hardcore Champion Regular Events: Monday Stampede Thursday Night Trashing The Danger Zone PPV's: Jan. - Snowball Brawl Feb. - Ground Zero Mar. - King of the Mountain Apr. - Smash Bash May - MDK June - Red Alert July - Summer Smash Aug. - BBQ Brawl Sept. - Killer KO's Oct. - Halloween Scream Nov. - Deadly Conflict Dec. - Christmas Havok Rules: I. Signing. 1. Each wrestler will be subject to a contract agreement, based on the creativity and our interest in the wrestler created. 2. All contract offerings are final by either [detail withheld] or [detail withheld]. 3. If you are unhappy with your contract; tough luck, then quit. II. Endorsements/Signing Bonus/Match Bonus 1a. Do not ask for endorsements - we will ask you if we need one. 1. For every new contract that that a wrestler signs with either the EHWA Roster or Endorsements, they will receive bonus money for doing so. For each match wrestled, each of the participating wrestlers shall receive a commission of what the match is worth to us. III. Contracts. 1. Contracts are only valid for how long you signed them for. 2. In order to resign, you either must be contacted by us, or if you think we've slacked off, you contact us. 3. We reserve the right to terminate any contract on bias that: ( i ) You do not roleplay good enough or often enough. ( ii ) You offend us or the league or harrass any other wrestler [personally]. ( iii ) We don't like your performance. ( iv ) There are many variables that are subject to change. IV. Gaining Cash. 1 You may gain cash by: signing endorsements, contracts, wrestling matches, and helping us (The fed officials) write matches (We'll give you a hefty commission if we're in terrible need of writers and you are a GOOD writer). V. Using Cash. 1. You can use cash you earn in many ways. Here they are: Gaining abilities. If you want to gain an ability point, you will pay us to use our gym here and we will give you the ability point you seek, granted over a period of "real" time. Paying fines that we might administer (such as skipping a house show, harrassing the fed heads, and the list goes on, bribing someone and getting caught by security [about a 10% chance of this happening - the fed official or wrestler will keep the cash you offer even if you get caught]). Bribery of Fed Officials (I.E. To win a match)* Bribery of Fed Wrestlers (I.E. To help you win a match)* ** *** *If you get caught, you will pay a fine and more than likely get suspended for a few days. **This only works if you aren't rivals and you are on the same fan appeal (face, tweener, or heel) ***In all bribes of other wrestlers, it will be between me (fed head), you, and the other wrestler. Don't worry about the 10% chance of getting caught - it's not that I don't know, it's completely and totally random - it's like rolling a 10 sided die - I pick a number and see what happens. VI. Injuries. 1. If you are injured during a match, then you will have to sit out the time allotted to heal completely, or else you risk traumatizing your wrestler, and creating something worse. VII. Roleplaying. 1. In order to win, you can't put out crusty little 1 liners. We expect a lot of time and effort put into your roleplays. If they aren't worth my time, neither will the match, and your part of the commission goes down. 2. You will send all roleplays to [detail withheld]. They will come out in flashes. 3. We don't mind swearing, but please, don't be excessive. 4. Matches are decided by quality of roleplays by [detail withheld] and [detail withheld]. We have the right to reject ANYONE based on their application. Application: Personal Info Name: Email: Wrestler Info (Nobody Real PLEASE) Name: Height: Weight: Gimmick (Face, heel, tweener): Description (tell me EVERYTHING): Characteristics (Habits, what he does in the ring, etc): 15 - 50 Favorite Moves: Finisher & Description: Backup finishers & Description: Manager (Optional, Nobody Real): Please divide up 90 points between these categories: Strength: Speed: Aerial: Technical: Hardcore: Cheat: Agility: Aggressiveness: Brains: Stamina: Sample Roleplay (NOT OPTIONAL)(MAKE IT GOOD!!!! - We have the right to reject any application because the roleplay isn't good enough, so give an example and set a good slate for your wrestler!!!!!!!!)
