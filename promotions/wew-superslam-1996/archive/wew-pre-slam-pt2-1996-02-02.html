WEW — Weekly Card

"WEW Pre-Slam (Part 2 of 2)"

2 February 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
SKLOK McM: Hellraiser, charging at the Rock and Roller, but the Roller ducks and comes in with a go-behind. The
Roller's trying to get Hellraiser up!!! Mick: 600 pounds??? I don't think so... SKLOK McM: Hellraiser just elbows the Rock and Roller!!! Hellraiser, with the Rock and Roller... He's got him up and... SUPLEX!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! Layne: Yeah, that's REAL tough. Picking on a man 1/3 of your weight. SKLOK McM: Hellraiser, going into the ropes and... ELBOW
DROP!!! The cover: 1..2..No!!! Maniac Mike Skull comes in and stomps on Hellraiser's head. Wait a minute!!! Hellraiser's down!!!
Mick: Me thinks that something was on the bottom of that boot. SKLOK McM: I'm beginning to think that too. Okay, the refferee is counting both men down on the mat: 1..2..3..
4..Wait! The Roller's going for the cover!!! 1..2..NO!!! Wideload comes in and stomps the Rock and Roller on the head!!! Right now, the refferee is distracted and talking to Wideload in the corner as... Wait a minute!!! The
Samoan Headhunters are in the ring now!!! The Master of Pain throws his skull to Maniac Mike Skull.... The Samoan Headhunters holding Hellraiser, much like this past Tuesday Night... Vixen Vicki and Sexy Cindy are pointing for the refferee to turn around. NO!!! The
Skull gets Hellraiser right in the forehead!!! Mick: Ouch! A skull to the forehead. Layne: Don't worry, Mick. You're skull would just destroy the other one. SKLOK McM: The ref's too distracted talking to Vixen Vicki and Sexy Cindy on the ring apron!!! The Headhunters have gone under the ring!!! Mike Skull puts the Rock and
Roller on Hellraiser!!! The ref turns around. The count:
1..2..3!!! DING! DING! DING! ANNOUNCER: Ladies and Gentlemen, here are your winners: the team of Maniac Mike Skull and the WEW World Champion: The Rock and Roller!!!! Mick:... and the Master of Pain and the Samoan Headhunters. Layne: Well, you can add Sexy Cindy and Vixen Vicki to the list, because if they weren't on the ring apron and were at home cleaning the house like they were supposed to be, nothing would have happened.
SKLOK McM: This is a travesty of justice. What kind of condition will Hellraiser be in this Sunday when he goes for the WEW World Championship?
Mick: Not very good condition. SKLOK McM: We'd like for you all to join us this Sunday for WEW Super Slam I only on Pay-Per-View. So, long, everybody!!!
