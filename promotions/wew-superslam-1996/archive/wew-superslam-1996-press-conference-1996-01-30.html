WEW (1996) — News

"WEW Press Conference"

30 January 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Recorded on Tuesday, January 30, 1996 (The sounds of cameras flashing and papers flipping can be heard. Along with a lot of talking) SKLOK McM: Thank you, everyone for coming on such a cold day. This is the WEW Super Slam I press conference, and I thank you all for attending. No, may I present to you, the first participant in the first main event, for the #1 contendorship here in the WEW. Ladies and Gentlemen, please welcome former WEW World Heavyweight Champion: Arcin.... (Arcin takes the podium) Arcin: It's Super Slam and once again Arcin, the Main Man is in the Spotlight. You know, I am a former WEW Champion and in my eyes I am still the champion. The Uncrowned Champion thats me. Now a lot of people are scared of Hellshock. I'm not taking anything away from his, well I mean look at him. Hey Hellshock stand up. Come on, don't be a party pooper, stand up. {Hellshock stands up and Arcin puts his arm around him} I mean, he is in great shape, a good wrestler, not too handsome but hey, you don't need to be in wrestling. Alright you can sit down now. {Hellshock sits} So at Super Slam, I am going in the Uncrowned Champion, and sorry Hellshock, but I am going out as the number 1 contender. And, I don't care if you like me, I don't care if you hate me because when I become the champion, you will have to deal with it. Prepare to perish in flames. I don't know if you have meet him before , but my best friend Iceman would like to say a few words. Iceman: Hellshock, HAHAHA!!!! The Main Man is not afraid of you and neither am I. Hellshock, HAHAHA!!!!! I hope you are ready to lose. As for me. I want a shot at the International title. Thats right, I don't know who has it, but I want it. So bring it on. Arcin: Alright, any questions? Reporter#1: Yes, Mister Arcin, isn't it true that you lost to Robin? Arcin: Well, yes but that match never should have happened. Next. Reporter#2: Isn't it true that in the FASW, Hellshock is number one and you are no were near. Arcin: Now, now, he may be but thats a different fed. Reporter#2: But doesn't that mean he is better than you? Arcin: I will take no more questions at this time. Thank you. SKLOK McM: Thank you, Mr. Arcin. Now, his opponent: Hellshock... (The cross seems to glow brighter on Hellshock's face as each question he answers complete) Hellshock - (Sitting in chair) Ok, lets get this crappy thing over with. WEW....geeeez! I got better things to do..... (Hellraiser goes to the podium) Reporter1 - Ok Hellshock, here you.... Hellshock - CEASE! You will call me MR. Shock. Reporter1 - Mr. Shock, why did you come to the WEW? Hellshock - To make friends and pick daisies. What kind of question is that? I came here to win the WEW title, plain and simple. And it will be done. Next. Reporter2 - You are facing Arcin at Super Slam. You have never faced him before, and i have yet to hear a comment about him from you. He is an excellent wrestler, and his track record in many federations have yet.... Hellshock - To be matched? Reporter2 - Yes... Hellshock - Well......i guess it will be then. Any more intelligent questions? I have important things to do....i mean, i need to wash the clothes, mow the lawn.. (giggles are heard) Reporter3 - Ok, Mr. Shock, i have a question for you. Why the HELL does your DAMN CROSS GLOW??? (Suddenly, Hellshock gets a look on his face that silences the entire room. You could hear a pin drop) Hellshock - My cross? (silence, as the Cross glows with a constant color of red) My cross....is....me. It is one with me. It contains.....me. My cross, ladies and gentlemen, is what i am. Without it......Hellshock no longer exists. And my cross will lead me to victory. Arcin! Bring on your flames, bring on your fire, bring on your career. Because at the end of Super Slam, it wont make a bit of difference to me. Feel....THE SHOCK.... (Hellshock leaves the conference with the audience still in awe of the future champion) SKLOK McM: Thank you.... Mr. Shock. Now, on to the other main event. For the WEW World Heavyweight Championship. Apparently, Hellshock got snowed in today, so he couldn't make it. So, I bring to you at this time: The current WEW World Heavyweight Champion: The Rock and Roller..... (The cameras REALLY start flashing as the Rock and Roller takes the stand) Reporter 1:ROCK AND ROLLER, HOW DO YOU FEEL ABOUT YOUR UPCOMING TITLE DEFENSE. R&R:I FEEL CONFIDENT THAT I CAN OVER COME THE POWER OF HIM AND THAT I'LL KEEP MY WORLD TITLE STATUS. Reporter 2:R&R DO YOU THINK THAT YOU ARE OUTNUMBERED IN THE POWER AND STRENGTH DEPARTMENT? R&R: WELL OF COURSE, I'M THE UNDERDOG IN ALL MY MATCHES. IT WAS A WONDER TO THE WORLD THAT I CAPTURED THE BELT. Reporter 3:WHEN THE MATCH COMES, WILL YOU BE A GENTLEMAN AND SHAKE HIS HAND? R&R:IF HE DESIRES TO SHAKE MY HAND I'LL DO THE SAME BACK. Reporter 3:THANK YOU FOR YOUR TIME, ONE LAST QUESTION, IF YOU RETAIN YOUR TITLE WHO WILL BE YOUR NEXT CHALLENGER? R&R:IF I DO, AND I WILL RETAIN MY TITLE, I'LL TAKE ANY CHALLENGES. I'M CONFIDENT THAT THE POWERS OF ROCK&ROLLERS, PAST PRESENT AND FUTURE ARE ON MY SIDE AND THE ROCK&ROLLER WILL PREVAIL AND REIGN AS THE SUPREME CHAMPION OF ROCK AND ROLL AND OF WRESTLING. SKLOK McM: Thank you, Mr. Roller. That's about all for today. I'd like to reccomend that you all buy some of that great WEW Merchandise and... WAIT A MINUTE!!! Hellraiser's not snowed in!!!! (The camera goes to the parking lot outside. Maniac Mike Skull and the Samoan Headhunters are beating the crap out of Hellraiser. Maskio slams Hellraiser's head into the front of a car and throws it down. Meanwhile, coming around the corner, the Master of Pain [manager of the Roller, Skull, and the Headhunters] drives the car into Hellraiser causing serious damage) SKLOK McM: No!!! In this kind of weather what are they thinking? Get some security out there!!! (They all escape, leaving Hellraiser in -30 degrees weather.) http://206.26.158.13/free/wew.html
