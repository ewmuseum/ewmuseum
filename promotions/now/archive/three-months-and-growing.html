Three Months and Growing

4 July 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
On April 4th, 1997, Network Online Wrestling was formed by myself ([name withheld]) and [name withheld]. When Mike had to leave due to time constraints, [name withheld] took over the Co-Presidency. Since May 12th, NOW has been putting on action as best as our staff can possibly do so. So far, I'd like to think it's been a success. :) Most new feds these days die out in a month or less -- yet NOW has stood the test of time in the beginning, which is usually the roughest time. With twelve writers, our staff is pretty solid, but we make sure to not overbook things. So far, Saturday Night HeatZone has become NOW's backbone, but in late July, Jen will be launching her card, too. NOW is gearing up for it's second pay per view, Survival of the Beast, set to rock the e-wrestling world on July 19th. How good will this be? In my mind, I think it should be pretty good, but then again, it's an opinion that everyone must decide upon. NOW isn't `hardcore` or `extreme`, but rather an all around mix of styles with less notion on the violence and more on the excitement and entertainment. Yes, there _has_ been a bit of blood shed, and yes, one announce booth was broken ONCE, but when it comes down to the most bitter feud, even that sort of thing happens in the WWF and/or WCW. :) Of course, we're not McMahon or Bischoff. (thank god!) NOW's been able to gather quite a good roster, including top names such as Dan Kauffman, [name withheld], [name withheld], [name withheld], [name withheld], and many others. From home grown talent standouts to a few bigger names thrown into the mix, NOW is becomming a damn fine fed, in my eyes, and in many others, too. Starting on the 19th, [name withheld] will begin to post all NOW thingies on RSPWF. For those who want to see it, enjoy, and for those who don't, simply pass it by. Simply because I think other people's opinions might help, here's a few quotes I've got from NOW people ... "This is the first league I've been in where there's quality, but it's on time as well." -- [name withheld], expirienced e-wrestler, handler of Edain Spears in NOW "I just want to say how much I'm enjoying this. Kauffman and I couldn't have joined up at the better time. It appears that our war of words have really spiced up the tag team scene. I for one am glad to have an opponent like him to work with. Thanks for letting me in." -- [name withheld], established e-wrestler, handler of Brian Knight and Mark Destructo in "You have been doing an excellent job with the fed. It really looks like you, and the handlers are having a good deal of fun with all of it. That's what its all about, and you've been doing great in the fun category." --[name withheld], EWA Co-President "Just like to say, awesome card. One of the best I have ever read. I am greatly impressed." --[name withheld], handler of Bishop, winner of the 1996 WCeW, speaking on HeatZone #6 "<__DAN__> NOW kicks ass, Zach. It really does" --Dan Kauffman, handler of Dan Kauffman and Craig Anderson, handler in NOW Not too shabby, eh? :) Bottom line is, Jen and I are in it for the fun -- and running NOW has been just that. We've had a chance to go `bigtime`, becomming a regional of a loop fed, but we declined it. We're quite happy where we are -- climbing the road to the top. It's been one hell of a fun time climbing here, and I know there's still a ways to go. I can't wait ... can you? :) Network Online Wrestling's Survival of the Beast ... July 19th. Right here on RSPWF, for your viewing pleasure. Like it or not ... I think we're here to stay. -[name withheld] NOW Co-President Handler of J.P. Steele[WEW], The Outcast[GFWA], and the Nightriders[IIWF]
