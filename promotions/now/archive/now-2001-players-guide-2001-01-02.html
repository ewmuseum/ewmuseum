NOW Player's Guide (2001)

2 January 2001
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
NOW Player's Guide "NOW 2001 -- The Future of Wrestling is Now" Premise (IC) ************ In the spring of 1997, Network Online Wrestling was born. The indepedent Arizona based promotion grew and expanded, becomming one of the hottest new promotions in the business. Run by fan-friendly Garrett A. Michaels, NOW sought to deliver the highest quality of sports entertainment to the public. Such notable ring legends as Chris Stonebreaker, Otto Verhoeven, Dan Kauffman, Ronnie D., and more called NOW home, as well as home grown talents like the Phantom, the Cherokee Chief, Edain Spears, and Chris Childress. In early 1998, NOW shut its doors. It was bought out and turned into a national promotion, which quickly flatlined. The finacial strain of a false sexual harrassment suit filed by former co-owner Jennifer Sweet drained GAM of his resources. He was forced out of the business he loved. Now, nearly three years after its final show, NOW is back. Thanks to the finacial help of former A1 Promotions CEO Steve Nelson, Network Online Wrestling lives again in 2001. GAM has even enlisted the help of a close friend and wrestling mainstay in need of a job to help man the booking duties. Last seen in N.E.W., Jeremy Steele has been given the job as Commishioner of NOW in hopes of bringing his expertise to the table. It is the dawn of a new era. The future is NOW! Premise (OOC) ************* NOW will be run in what I believe to be an original format. The weekly shows will be written in the style of an online recap as seen on TV or tape. Those of you familiar with the pro wrestling recaps of Scott Keith will have a good idea of what I'm going for -- some play by play, some analyzation, some smarmy asides that really have no purpose other than to make the show read better. The line-ups for these cards will be half booked in advance and half booked on the fly (or at least appear to be.) Impromtu matches will be set up by both myself and the handlers. The handlers will always know of their matches, but those not involved in the match may or may not know of the match ahead of time. This gives things a more live, realistic feel. It also allows flexibility. It's easy to do thanks to the style of writing. Handler participation will be different than the typical e-fed: short pre-card flashes will appear in packets. The rest of the involvement will come in the form of on-card activities that will be outlined later. The supercards, which will be held every two months or more, will be done in regular action block format. This will make them "special" again, a quality that's been lacking for a long time, in my opinion. As far as the talent goes, it will be divided into two catagories: regulars and touring. The regulars are just that: mainstays of the promotion. The touring are the established stars coming through for an angle or two, or just checking the fed out. The rest of the details are explained in their sections. Shows ***** There will be two weekly shows, each featuring 8-10 matches, among other things. Sunday Night Heat Zone, held on (obviously) Sunday is the flagship show. It is hosted by former NOW broadcasters Toby James and Rob Scatterson. The show will always emit from the NOW Colosseum in Phoenix, Arizona unless otherwise noted. The secondary show, Wednesday Night Dynamite, will be hosted by former A1 Pro commentators Tommy Vaughn and Mitch Decker. It will be held in the newly christened NOW Auditorium (formerly the A1 Arena) in Redondo Beach, California. Titles ****** NOW Heavyweight title -- the main title, open to all wrestlers of every weight class. NOW Arizona State title -- the secondary title, open to all wrestlers of every weight class. NOW Television title -- defended weekly, open to all wrestlers of every weight class. Should a tag team division form in NOW, a title will be made available. Staff (IC) ********** Steve Nelson, CEO. Formerly financed A1 Promotions for his niece, Meredith Riley. Following its closure, was left with a heavy investment in a business he knew little about. As a result, he offered his services and finacial help to any who were in need of it. NOW was the project he decided to help fund. Has little involvement in the fed, but everything must be approved by him. Garrett A. Michaels, former owner, now reduced to owning 40% of the stock and mainly the head promoter. Early 30s, loves the business, always trying to give the fans what they want. Willing to try anything to gain interest. Enjoys the new, edgier sports entertainment style. Jeremy Steele, old school wrestler with Midwest values. Retired to have a family, but was forced to return to the ring in N.E.W. to provide for his family. After a few months, left after a dispute with the front office. Still needing work, GAM hired him to work as a head promoter and someone who could bring in the talent and work with them. Toby James, the old voice of NOW. The Tobster is in his 40s and a bit on the rotund side, but he is a cherished figure from NOW's past. Loves the business, knows it well, and is easily excited by the action. Rob Scatterson, the heel counterpart of Toby James. Simply there to make life hell for Toby. In his mid-30's now, he acts much younger and immature. Blood and t & a excite him. Mitch Decker, the young DJ-turned-announcer from Redondo Beach. A lifetime wrestling fan with a knack for pumping up a crowd, the beach bum proved himself worthy of another go behind the desk in A1 Pro, calling the action and providing analysis with the younger generation's POV. Tommy Vaughn, the sarcastic East Coast commentator believes himself to be "above" whatever his job is. The sardonic African American is always complaining or ranting about something, but truth be told, he loves his job -- even if he'll never admit it.
