* * * Network Online Wrestling * * * My name is [name withheld], some of you have seen my promoting feds like ERWI and WAeW. Well, let me give you some background. ERWI lived from June 18th-late Febuary, dieing under ICE's banner in a merge. WAeW lasted from January 8th-March 20th. Neither fed was known as a huge success, but they were fun. The only reasons for folding were lack of help. [name withheld] had the same problem with ICE, circa May 18th-August 30th; October 25th-late Febuary. Both of our reigns as fed heds weren't long, but somewhat succesful. Knowing each other since June, we decided to put together a fed and make SURE it lasted. And thus, we decided to end the bad taste of previous outings and run a fed we could be happy with. What would it be? NOW. Yes, NOW, Network Online Wrestling. The staff?
