Network Online Wrestling (NOW) is an e-wrestling league run by Garrett A. Michaels, fictionally, and [name withheld] in reality. It is your basic e-fed around for the handlers and staff's own entertainment and to provide the world of e-wrestling another solid, fun organazation. It was created on April 4th, 1997, by [name withheld] and [name withheld] (of ICE fame) and ran until October 30th, 1997. There were staff problems at the time, but on November 22nd, NOW officially returned and has been running strong since, as well as before. B) Who runs NOW? To maintain a good fed, there must be a solid staff of reliable and commited writers and bookers. The Network's staff is just that. There are currently three singles and one tag team title in the Network. The main one is the NOW World heavyweight title [the 'World' may be dropped in the future as NOW is primarily a Southwestern fed based in Phoenix]. The secondary title is the NOW Southwest Heritage title, formerly the Midwest title. The finals singles title is the NOW Junior Heavyweight title, contested between those wrestlers weighing 245lbs and under. The NOW World tag team titles are the only tag team prize. ========================================== SECTION TWO: Rules and Regulations A) How are matches determined? Matches are booked by Zach at his discretion with help from the Vice Presidents. Roleplays alone are not the only factor taken into account. Angles, heat, effort, and other things of the like are also considered. Aproximetly 75%-80% of the time, the wrestler who RPed better wins. Sometimes, the 'better' wrestler will lose to advance an angle, due to a strat or a feud by someone not in the match, or to just keep things interesting. EVERYBODY will lose, so when it happens, don't freak out. It doesn't nessacarily mean you were out roleplayed, just that the situations in NOW worked best with the other guy winning. B) What are the roleplay rules? Each individual wrestler or tag team is allowed NO MORE than 3 roleplays over the course of the week, beginnining on Saturday/Sunday and ending on Friday/Saturday. THREE roleplay packets will be sent during the week, on Monday, Wednesday, and Friday. In ring interviews and strats do NOT count towards this limit, but remember, it's quality, not quanity. Don't try to spam to win, but if you're really into the match and have stuff to say, you have enough things available to do it. C) How do I get matches? Normally, the active people will be scheduled for matches. With a decently large roster, though, sometimes you might accidently go unnoticed. If you EVER feel you're being slighted in the number of matches you're getting, feel free to e-mail Zach [ [detail withheld]@aol.com ] and ask about this. You will get matches by being active, mainly. Sometimes you will face people who have challenged you or vice versa, sometimes you face someone you have no connection for just for a good match. D) Are sneak attacks allowed? Yes, to a degree. Not every strat can be used to the last word, but hopefully as much of it as possible will be worked in. Don't strat for anything extreme, though, because NOW is not that type of fed. Occasionally, in a bitter feud or an act of getting over to a large end, something over the top will occur, but not regularly. ========================================== SECTION THREE: Events and shows A) What weekly shows are there? NOW produces two weekly shows, one completely live, one completely dark. Saturday Night HeatZone is the live show, featuring 6 matches plus any in rings and special announcements. Tuesday Night Crunchtime is the dark show, featuring four dark matches. Both shows normally commence from the specially built NOW Auditorium in Phoenix, but occasionally there are tours. For the most part, they stay in the southwestern area. B) What PPV/supercards are there? In the past, NOW ran supercards on a two month + one week basis, but for the '98 calender year, that has changed. Here is the tenative line-up. January 3rd, Survival of the Beast 2 Febuary 28th, SNHZ #25 Silver Edition Supercard April 4th, Spring Madness 2 - the Anniversary Early July, Survival of the Beast 3 Mid October, Deuces Wylde 2 C) What deadlines do I need to know for them? NO strats/in rings are accepted for Crunchtime, because there isn't really reason for them. SPECIAL strats are accepted, but NO in rings are, ever. It's just not nessacary. Wednesday, 7EST is the FINAL deadline for any HeatZone in rings or strats. ========================================== SECTION FOUR: Application A) What do I need to do to join?
