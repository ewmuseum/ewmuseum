Legendary Status Is Achieved Not Granted Tony Stecchino CCWA Promos #124 Date: February 11, 2005 Location: Seattle, WA =============== Tony’s House Seattle, Washington Time: 9:32 PM =============== Tony’s hand grasps the shower door and he pulls it open with a click. The sound of handles turning, the spurts of water and then the water crashes down on the floor. Tony’s reaches his hand in and feels the water; he adjusts the handles and sticks his hand back down in the streams of water. Tony’s legs step into the shower and the door closes behind him. He lets out a long sigh and dips his head under the hot water; it sprays off and runs down his body. His golden hair turns dark from the water as it mats it down to his head. His hands rest on the wall just above the handles, supporting his leaning body. ’Did I do the right thing?’ He thinks to himself. ‘Was it the right call?’ The phone rings, Tony lifts his head. His eyes lift over the shower door. Tony: Melissa? Tony shakes his head, ’She’d never call here, she’d never call me. The sound of Stevie Nickel’s voice is faintly heard on the answering machine a few rooms down the hall, through all the open doors. Tony: I’ll have to call him back. Tony dumps some shampoo in his hands and rubs it together, then rubs it through his hair. ’I know I made the right decision, I have left the company in good hands, the person I have chosen should do an excellent job at running everything. Yeah. I feel good about what I have done. I am a businessman and a wrestler. Well just a wrestler more or less now.’ Tony pushes his hair under the streams of water. Tony: Yeah, I am the best damn wrestler that CCWA has now or will ever have. Tony picks up a bottle of body wash and puts it into a wash clothes and begins to lather is body with the substance. This is one of the best decisions of my life. Tony turns off the water and pushes the shower door open. He grabs a towel and dries off. He wraps the towel around his waist. He slowly walks out of the bathroom, rubbing his ear, trying to get the water out. Walking over to his dresser he pulls out a pair of silk boxers and slides them on. The towel drops to the floor. He stretches out and flips water from his hair. He walks over to his closet and looks through. He steps in and grabs a suit. He looks it over and smiles. Tony: It’s gonna be a wild night. =============== Doc Maynard’s Seattle, Washington Time: 12:06 AM =============== Tony sits up stairs in a casual suit, no tie, his shirt unbuttoned at the top, about two buttons down, showing off his chest a little bit. Stevie Nickels walks up the steps in a t-shirt and jeans, two beers in hand. He sets down the beers on the table and slides in the booth, just a few feet from Tony. The horrible and very untalented Seattle band The Fakes finish up their weekly Friday night set here at Doc Maynard’s. Tony looks over his shoulder and down at the people just outside the entrance to the bar, smoking, talking having a good time. Tony: You know Stevie… Tony looks back from over his shoulder and over at Stevie who is taking a sip of his beer. Tony: I’ve been thinking way too much about Melissa lately. Stevie looks over at his friend, swallowing his beer, setting the glass down on the table. He licks his lips and thinks over his words carefully. Tony takes a large drink of beer and looks forward and the horrible, horrible band that is playing some really, really shit song on the stage. ”Christ, someone should shoot those guys and put them out of their misery.” Tony thinks to himself. He sets his glass down on the table. And smacks his lips. Tony: This is good beer. What kind is it? Stevie: It’s Skagit Red. Tony looks at the concern that is all over his friend’s face, he knows what Stevie is going to say to him. They’ve been here and done this whole spiel a million times before. Always the same thing, nearly the same words in the same order they have left Stevie’s mouth a million times before. Tony: I know what you are going to say. Tony hangs his head. A very happy couple walks by hand in hand, the guy nibbles on the girls ear playfully, she tosses her long brown hair to let him have better access to her ear and neck. They sit down in a booth just down the way. They begin kissing passionately. Tony looks over at them. She’s very pretty like Melissa. Stevie takes another drink of beer. He sets his glass down. Tony looks up and over at Stevie. Tony rubs his index finger of his right hand along the rim of the glass that contains his ice cold, golden-red beer. Stevie: You know I have told you, it’s probably time to file a divorce, the papers will reach her one way or another, just send her a check for a few million dollars and just be done with her. Start seeing some other people, maybe find another girl to settle down with… Stevie pauses. ’I can’t believe I am going to tell him to do this.’ Stevie’s mind races with conflict. ’I really shouldn’t do this, but I will.’ Tony looks at the couple down the way. The man is getting pretty grabby under her shirt. Tony is a bit disgusted and looks down at his beer. Stevie: Find her. Tony’s gaze is removed from his beer glass and is locked on the eyes of Stevie Nickels. Tony: Wha? What did you just say? ’He didn’t just tell me to go find Melissa did he?’ Stevie: I’ve been thinking about this over the past few days and you should do it. You have to do it. Last time you did it, you were nearly killed, but… You have to do this. This is what’s going to free your mind and allow to be you again. Sure this whole thing with CCWA has weighed heavily on you over the past few weeks, but now, you have a full chance to clear your mind. You should take the opportunity and do it. Tony looks over at the booth just down the way from them. The man sits with his hands behind his head and glazed over look on his face. The head of the girl raises and lowers from the level of the table. Tony: Looks like those two are having fun. Stevie looks over his shoulder at the two having a little fun in the near privacy of their own booth on the second floor of Doc Maynard’s. Stevie: You know, what is society coming to when you see stuff like that? I mean it’s a Friday night and in Pioneer Square, any one of thousands could walk up and see this, it’s nearly disgusting. Stevie turns his head back around and looks over at Tony, who is looking down at The Fakes as he finishes off his beer. Tony: Yeah, I hear that, you know what else is bad? Stevie: What? Tony: That band down there. How can they come here every week and just suck like this. You think they would practice enough, always off key, out of time, out of tune. YOU GUYS SUCK!!! The lead singer flips Tony off. They both laugh. Stevie: So you ready for your match next week? Tony: Yeah I guess, it’s not like I have to do a whole lot of homework. Don’t have to scout this guy out. He couldn’t even win the number one contendership for the Television Title. So there is no way he can just jump into my league and come close to beating me. Won’t happen. Stevie: True, he wasn’t very impressive at Sensation. Tony: Yeah I don’t even know his name, nor do I want to. It won’t matter. Stevie: You could piss him off. Tony reaches around pulls out his wallet and flips through some bills. Tony: You know I have pissed off a lot more people than some piece of shit upstart that thinks he is the funniest and wittiest guy ever. This guy is no Silent Wind or Maverick Reno. I fear him not, but he should fear me. Tony tosses some money down on the table. Tony: Give that to the waitress. Tony begins to stand. Stevie: Where you going? Tony takes a couple steps away from the table and turns around. A look of confusion comes over his face and looks back over at Stevie. He slowly licks his lips and then looks over at the couple down the way. All he see is the girls naked back and the man’s arm wrapped her, her hips thrusting into his. Her head lies on his shoulder, his face covered by her long brown hair. Hair just like Melissa’s. He looks back down at Stevie. Tony: I don’t know. He looks back up at the couple having their good time in the booth down the way. ’She looks like Melissa from behind, the skin color, the hair, the physique, God I miss her.’ Tony turns around and walks down the steps. Stevie watches him disappear below the balcony and into the sea of people and horrible music by The Fakes. =============== Pier 71 Seattle, Washington Time: 2:31 AM =============== Tony sits on a bench, the night cold and damp. A mist of rain has just finished and the wood of the pier is shiny with the backdrop of the lights from the massive buildings. Tony huddles, pulling his jacket tightly over him. Melissa pops in and out of his mind. Each image brings a smile to his face. Flashback =============== Casa De Stecchino Rome, Italy December 25th, 2000 Time: 9:12 AM =============== Melissa reaches over and kisses Tony on the ear. Tony rolls over in the whiteness and softness of his and Melissa’s bed. He opens his eyes and looks up at his beautiful wife. Her brown eyes look down on him, her soft brown hair tickles his forehead, her naked body hidden by just enough of the blankets. She’s perfect. Melissa plants a firm kiss with her dark red lips on to Tony’s. Tony melts. Melissa: I love you. Tony looks up at her, her mouth inches away, he kisses her. Tony: I love you to. Melissa pounces out of bed and quickly puts on a robe. She jumps back up on the bed and kneels next to Tony a radiant smile blasted over her face. A smile so infectious that Tony has no other choice but to smile. Melissa: You ready to open the presents that I got you? Tony sits up and takes her in his arms. Tony: Are you ready to open the presents that I got for you? Like a little girl, excited for her first real Christmas morning she shakes her head agreeingly. She leaps out of his arms and dives to the floor and runs out of the bedroom. She disappears down the hallway. Tony ties his robe as he comes down the stairs, a huge Christmas tree sits next to a huge bay window, decorated with lights, ornaments and other knick knacks. Two stocking hang above the lit fire place. Tony and Melissa are written in cursive on the top of them. Melissa holds a present on her lap and she looks at Tony, that same smile on her face. Melissa: I am so excited for our first Christmas together. Tony walks across the living room and kneels next to Melissa. He kisses her again, she sets the present down in his lap, its heavy, Tony falls backwards. The present rolls on to his chest. A look of surprise fills his eyes. A smile still on his face. Melissa: Open it! Tony sits back up and adjusts the present. It’s not that heavy on second count. He begins to tear into the paper. Tony: Can’t wait to see what present you got me nearly killed me. Tony finishes the paper and opens the box. He pulls out a giant plaque with a huge golden championship belt on it. It reads, “TONY STECCHINO THE WORLD’S CHAMPION” He looks over at Melissa. A smile on his face. Tony: What is this? Melissa: I had all the gold melted down from all the belts that you have held. You said it took up too much room and you wanted something done with it. So there is sixty-five pounds of gold in that belt. Tony: Really? Melissa: Yeah. He leans over and kiss her again. Melissa: And I had a support built on the wall that would hold it. It’s right across from your desk, so you can look up and see it whenever you want. He kisses Melissa again. Tony: I never expected this. Thank you so much. Melissa: You are so welcome. Tony: My present to you is no where near as good as this, since I haven’t had a whole lot of time recently. Melissa reaches over and grabs a present from under the tree with her name on it. She tears into it. Melissa: It doesn’t matter, it’s the thought that counts more than anything. She rips through the paper and opens up the box. Her smiles fades quickly fades and she almost looks as if she is ready to cry, she brings her hand to her mouth as she is taken aback. She looks over at Tony and then back in the box. She pulls out a picture frame, it’s oak and smooth, hand crafted, the design on it fabulous. Inside is a picture of Tony and Melissa kissing at the top of the space needle. Their very first kiss. She places the picture back into the box and dives on top of Tony and starts kissing him. Melissa: This… Kisses Tony. Melissa: Is the most… Kisses Tony. Melissa: Romantic present… Kisses Tony. Melissa: EVER!!! Melissa slips Tony the tongue and continues to kiss him passionately. She opens up her robe ready to make love to her husband right there on the floor. =============== Pier 71 Seattle, Washington Time: 3:00 AM =============== The rain falls hard and steady, Tony leans up against the rail looking over the cold choppy water of the Puget Sound. A solemn look over his face. His body shivers from the cold, he doesn’t notice. His eyes are closed and he is in a completely different world at that moment.
