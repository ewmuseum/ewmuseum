CCWA — Roleplay

Discovery

17 January 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
This is a roleplay I did in CCWA my first roleplay in a long time. I take criticism very well so please any feedback would be appreciated. Rummaging through Friday’s Journal Sentinel, Wisconsin’s most popular newspaper, we see a picture of a man who is fighting over in Iraq to help rebuild the troubled country. The title of the story tells the importance of why we’re staring at this particular story. "American Hero Comes Home to Wisconsin". We look upon the face of the reader and see that it’s none other than Shane Michael Stevens. A tear drips down from his eye as he remembers back to when he fought for the men and woman back home to have the freedom and choices that the men and women don’t have in Iraq. But most important he cries for the fact that he witnessed the death of his battle buddy because his buddy wanted to charge and because of his careless intentions didn’t see the sniper on top of a 8 story building that gave him the perfect target to shoot at. Stevens: Brian!!!! You see Stevens and his battle buddy have been through everything together. They went to Basic Training together and even went to the same base for their MOS Training. Destiny wanted them to be friends. But destiny also took their friendship away at the expense of a bullet. War is a terrible thing. All it does is make things worse. But Brian would have wanted Shane to move on with his life and try and make the rest of it. You see both Brian and Shane were young about 20 or so. So they were young and still had plenty of time to experience some of the finer things in life. Growing up Shane had pretty much a mediocre life with a mediocre job. It was the same routine for him everyday. Getting up every morning and going to work then after work coming home eating dinner and going to bed. Nothing was different. Shane didn’t have to a wife or girlfriend so he didn’t have to worry about any emotional attachment that he had to spend time with but yet he was lonely and tired of the same thing which was a big reason why he went into the military to begin with. He needed a change. He needed a challenge. In high school Shane wasn’t popular or hated he was yes in the middle class clique. He didn’t get drunk or get high on the weekends. He was smart he didn’t want to fuck his life up because he’s seen what drugs can do and to your mind. He always believed strongly in the mind is a terrible thing to waste. He was a good student. Always keeping up his 3.4 GPA and he was on the Varsity basketball team but just not quite good enough to earn himself a scholarship to any premier college so he had to concentrate more on his studies because his parents were having financial troubled because they were being audited and owed the federal government money and couldn’t afford to put him through college. The story in the paper tells about their Wisconsin hero coming home from a war that had died out and they were to award him a key to the city in the next couple of days. This came as a shock to Shane because this was the first time he was hearing about this. Nobody had called him to welcome him home let alone to say he was being offered a key to the city. He places the paper to the side and sheds a few more tears because of the memory of the times he and his buddy had together. Brian was a brother to him and he missed him dearly. But then his life begins to change as there is a ring of the phone… Later that night while having a lunch freshly prepared by himself. You see he has always had the skill of being a great cook but didn’t want to go to culinary school. He sat down and began to taste it but before he could the phone. Not sure if he should answer it because of his lack of eating anything for the past 24 hours he went ahead and did anyway. Curiosity killed the cat and killed him. He had to know who was calling him. Stevens: Hello? Voice: Is this Shane Michael Stevens? Stevens: Yes it is. Who am I speaking to? Voice: Well Stevens I’ve got a great opportunity for you. Something that could change your life. Stevens: I’m not interested. About to hang the phone up he hesitated. He thought the caller was trying to sell him something or it was a scam artist trying to con him out of money. Voice: Can I get just 5 minutes of your time? Stevens: Well I was just about to sit down for lunch. Can this call wait? Voice: Actually no it can’t. I’m a little pressed for time. Stevens: Okay I’ll give you 5 minutes. Voice: Okay thank you. Let me first off introduce myself. I am Stone Fox the owner of a company called CCWA which stands for Continental Championship Wrestling Alliance. Stevens: Okay well what do they want with me. Fox: Well Mr. Stevens I’ve heard about about your heroic and brave times you spent over in Iraq fighting for our country and for theirs. So I did a little research on you and found out that you used to wrestler in high school and thought that since you have wrestled before and you being one of militaries finest that I could offer you a spot in my company. Stevens: That sounds nice but I don’t know I haven’t wrestled in some time. I think I’m all rusted up. I think I’ve lost the heart to wrestle anymore anyway. Fox: Well I am not getting off this phone until we work something out how about you come in and we can talk about some details. I’m willing to off you a nice amount of money here. I’ll even pay for your flight out here to Los Angeles. And a limo to pick you up and bring you by my corporate office. How can you turn it down and even if you don’t want the job I’ll pay for your plan ticket back home. How can you not like that deal? Stevens: Sounds pretty good to me. When do you want me to come out. Fox: Actually I was wondering if we can do this today. Because if so I’d like you to make an appearance on our weekly show called "The Show". I’ll have you take the next plane out to LA. Stevens: Alright Fox: I’ll call back with the time of your flight and everything else you need to know. Stevens: Sounds good. And that marked the end of the conversation. With Stevens at this point already cold he put it in the microwave to heat up. And not but 10 minutes later he received another call but he had just finished his lunch and was happy now since he got to eat it. Stevens: Hello? Fox: I have your flight information. You ticket is waiting for you at your airport Mitchell Field. And it leaves in 2 hours. Stevens: Alright. Let me go pack then. Fox: Alright see you in a couple of hours. They both hang up the phone and Stevens begins to get frantic and begins packing wildly so that he can make his plane. He gets everything packed and ready and heads to the airport where he makes it in time and boards the plane and is on his way to Los Angeles.
