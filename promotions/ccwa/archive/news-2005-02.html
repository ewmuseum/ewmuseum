News — February–March 2005

27 Feb – 14 Mar 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is A later rolling news capture from the same front page — signings, title challenges, and the fed's own recap of its Flash Championship tournament card.
Monday, March 14, 2005: After two weeks, CCWA is coming back to you live from Osh Kosh, Wisconsin! It has been announced that the CCWA Television Title no longer exists. Now a new title is out to be won and that title is the new, CCWA Flash Championship! 8 men will compete in a tournament for the title. The bracket has been announced and early on are Justin Warner vs. Dan Blitz, Freezer Burn vs. Mercenary, Painkiller vs. Chris Night and Randy Taylor vs. Kris Krimzon. Also on the card is new comer and legend Sylkk making his CCWA in ring debut against the Continental Champion, Danielle Northsky. Then in the main event, the CCWA World Heavyweight Championship will be on the line when the champion Ronnie McNeil takes on the challenger and the cockiest son of a bitch in Continental Championship Wrestling Alliance... Lyger. Sunday, February28, 2005: We here at CCWA are pleased to announce the signing of Dan Blitz. He is currently on a temporary contract as he works for another promotion in the form of GTWF. He is a multi time champion in GTWF, holding every single belt there and currently is their GTu Champion which is the highest belt that won in that prestigious federation. Welcome to CCWA Dan. Sunday, February27, 2005: CCWA would like to announce the signings of Chris Night and former 3x time CCWA Tag Champion and 4x  CCWA Hardcore Champion, The Mercenary. We would like to welcome both you to and back to CCWA. Chris Night will be making his in ring debut very soon, while The Mercenary will make his debut this Friday night at WildSide against Randy Taylor. Also this week, a new match has been signed and added to the card. With other hot matches such as PainKiller going against Jay Robson, Kris Krimzon fighting the President of CCWA, Chris Sanchez and our main event, Psyno taking on the North American Champion, the lovely and dangerous Danielle NorthSky. Scott Monroe will now be taking on the returning Justin Warner and Freezer Burn in a triple threat match. This week Continental Championship Wrestling Alliance delivered another superb performance and maintains it's reputation of a fed that anything can happen in. And anything and everything happened almost all in one night. Scott Monroe made his comeback against the crafty and wiley veteran in the form of "The Rocket" Randy Taylor. In a tough fought match, was Monroe able to come out on top or was it Taylor who managed to win by cheating once again, maintaining his reputation. "The Ladies Man" Jay Robson made his debut against the very tough Lyger. Tony Stecchino promised revenge from what transpired last week and he was at ring side to help the boys call the match, did Stecchino get involved. Speaking of Stecchino, he was in the main event and wrestling for the CCWA World Championship against the man himself, Ronnie McNeil. Who came out on top in what could have been the main event of any pay-per-view anywhere, tune in to find out. Also Tony Stecchino made a huge announcement, he claims to be from the mouth of Owner Stone Fox himself. Though Fox is off in Iraq does his hand of power still reach into CCWA. Were the two fired by Stecchino, actually fired from CCWA. FIND OUT NOW!
