Interview in the Home of the dead

7 Feb 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is Posted to CCWA's own Feedback Forum by Furious — the poster's own subtitle names it “A r/p I did in IWX for a triple threat,” a promo written for a different fed (IWX) and shared here for feedback, not a CCWA storyline.

Raw, the scene is in darkened room, nobody knowing exactly what room it is. In the room there are carcasses hanging from the ceiling and blood dripping down. As the camera man looks up, a drop of blood drops down onto the lens of the camera as the crowd lets out a loud ewww sound. As the camera man slowly continues on, constantly thinking about whether he should or not, but keeps going do to the fact he may loose his job if he doesn't. As he continues, he looks to the right to what looks like a huge wood built device, a noose hanging from it. As he continues, he catches glimpse of another wooden device, an extremely sharp blade attached to it. He walks up to the device to get a better look and catches the concept that it is a Guillotine. As shutters at the blood on the blade and steps back, tripping over something on the floor. As he tries to catch his balance, he sees what it is that he tripped over, and it was a skull. The camera man screams at the site of it and picks up the camera and hurries along away from it as the crowd watches, not one of them making a sound. As he leeches forward, he comes upon another death causing device, but there wasn't much to this one. He found a small rope, an axe, and a wooden stand with a grove in it, he then realized that it was used for beheadings. As he raised the camera, he noticed a spear behind the wooden stand and on top of it, was a human head. The entire crowd let out a gasp of shock as the camera man quickly turned the camera away and continued down the dark path. As he goes on, he comes about 20 feet from a door. The door looks as if it is brand new, made of Mahogany wood, very fancy door. As the camera man continues, two hooded figures halt him and stand in his way.- Man #1: You shall not pass...... Man #2: Leave now.... or pay the dire consequences..... Camera man: I don't want any trouble, I just want through..... Man #1: no one shall pass. -A loud noise is heard from within the door and with that, both of the men move aside. The door creeks loudly as the camera man leeches through the door, shaking intensely and not being able to keep the camera still. As he continues, he sees a chair, finely crafted and looking as if it was a mid evil times throne. As the camera man progresses forward slowly, the man in the chair speaks, and the crowd doesn't quite recognize the voice.- Man: Welcome to my lair....... I am Griffon, as most of you probably know, and you have found your way into here, all to keep your job, what foolishness. -The camera man is constantly trying to hold the camera still but having much trouble with it.- Griffon: Young man........ tell me, are you frightened? No need to answer, I understand that you are. I also understand that you came in here to see Furious didn't you.... how terrible for you that he only comes out now when he feels like it.... I'm sure you come to see what he has to say about this Intercontinental Championship Number 1 contenders match...... well my boy has not the least bit of doubt in his mind that he will win the shot, and then go on to win the championship... I mean, when he puts his mind to doing something he does it....... for example.... his mind was set on beating the X-Ecutioner..... and he did so, he trained day in and day out until the day came he faced him, and won. So I can tell you now he isn't worried about his competition... *Just then Ace the interviewer and new owner busts into the room and seems out of breath* Ace: Goddamn, that's some freaky shit back there *The crowd gets a laugh out of the statement by Ace.* Griffon: Barging into this place is not wise of you..... Ace: Well I had a few questions, mind if I asked them? Griffon: Not at all, go on ahead and ask anything.. Ace: ok then, can you tell me what your thoughts are on Furious going into this match? Griffon: There is nothing to think about it..... he walk into that ring and dominate on both of the men in there..... because that demon back their training is ready for that title, and will not let them stand in his way, that's my thoughts on it.... Ace: I see..... what are your thoughts on his opponents? Griffon: They are both very skilled athletes...... but none of them compare to my boy.... they don't compare to him in strength, speed, endurance, technical ability, they are of no match. Furious is stronger then and man that has ever wrestled, quicker then any cruiserweight that has sped through the ring, can take more hits then anyone in the world, and has more technical ability then even the greatest wrestlers of all time, live and dead..... Ace: I see...... but yet Furious was beaten by the Highway dog? Griffon: It was an unfortunate accident indeed..... if he wanted to win, he would have. Ace: right, and where is Furious right now, I have a few questions for him Griffon: How many times must I repeat myself.... he is training for his match Ace: Could I get a few questions with him? Griffon: Why don't you ask him? -As Griffon finishes his last statement, a door is flung open in the right corner of the room. In the doorway stands a shadow, nobody can make it out do to the little light in the room. As the figure stands their, not moving at all, the crowd begins to let out little whispers, guessing at who the man could be. As they try to figure it out, they agree that it must be Furious. As the crowd awaits to find out who it is, the man takes a step forward into the fireplaces light. The crowd gets a glimpse of his face to see a young man, in his early 20's, around 6'6, short white hair and a red cape and clothes on. As the crowd looks at the man, very few know who the man is. As the man takes another step forward, he looks at Ace, who is also trying to figure out who the man is. The man looks at Griffen and Griffen nods, the man takes another step towards Ace- Ace: J-J-Just who in the hell are you? Man: My name is not important, but if you must know, my name is Dante. Ace: Dante? well just what are you doing here? Dante: What kind of question was that? I am here to aid Furious, I am an old partner of his, and I am sure that once the master finds out your here, you shall have hell to pay. Ace: master? who is your master Dante: Furious is my master you twit...... how retarded you normal people can be. Ace: interesting.... so what part do you play in all of this? Dante: I am here to help Furious train..... I'm helping him make sure he is at 100 % for his challengers..... because when he is at 100 %, nothing even comes close to beating him. Ace: You make it sound as if the man is indestructible, despite the fact he has been beaten here in the IWX 3 times Dante: 3 losses..... three unfortunate mishaps... they do not linger in his mind, he simply forgets about them and moves on, as do we. Ace: Well what are your thoughts on the man he just defeated on Malice, The X-Ecutioner Dante: The X-Ecutioner was a challenge that Furious himself wanted more then anything. He considered the X-Ecutioner a high mountain to climb and worked to beat him. I believe that the X-Ecutioner was a tough challenge for Furious and pushed him to his limits, but he ended up like most people do, beaten by the warlord himself. Ace: So you are obviously very confident in Furious... Dante: O yes, very confident... as a matter a fact, he is in a stable mind as of now, centering himself in his mind.. quite a weird method how he does it..... come, I'll let you have a look -Dante turns around and heads through the door in which he came. Ace wastes no time following him and neither does the camera man. The 3 men continue down the hallway and as they draw nearer and nearer to the door at the end, a noise is heard. The noise sounds like that of an instrument. As they grow closer, the sound becomes heard quite well, and is recognized as an Organ. As they reach the door, Dante opens it and walks into the extremely large room, torches lit all over the place. As Ace and the camera man walks through, Ace makes a comment- Ace: I wonder how much he will sell this room for? -The crowd easies up a bit and lets out a laugh at Ace's comment. As the camera man looks around the room, he focuses the camera on the organ on the right side of the room. The organ is a very old one, and humungous at that. As the organ lets out a creepy tune the camera lowers from the top of it, and slowly creeps down until it catches a glimpse of the person playing it. As the camera focuses on the person playing it, the crowd realizes who it is. The man, with his long white hair hanging down in front of his face and down to the lower part of his back, his black cape hanging to the floor, his monstrous face starring down at the keys of the organ, is Furious. Dante stands for a brief moment as Ace wonders what's going on. As Furious continues to play the organ, Dantespeaks- Dante: Furious...... -Furious pauses for a moment, the organs sound dies off and Furious turns his head slightly to the right- Dante: I am sorry to disturb you my lordship but this scoundrel needs to speak with you......... Furious: Very well.... you may leave Dante -Dante nods and exits the room, closing the doors behind him- Furious: now...... what do you need? Ace: Furious, I am trying to get some questions from you about your match next Monday.... Furious: Well then ask them...... Ace: Ok, what are your thoughts on facing a man you have never faced before, and a man who beat you just last week? Furious: What are MY thoughts you say........ well.. -Furious stands up from the organ and turns around slowly to stares into Ace's eyes, Ace lets out a gulp as he does this.- Furious: my thoughts are quite simple........ CWebb is but another competitor in this tournament.... he is a man who plans to win the tournament and become the IWX Intercontinental Championship. He is a man of skill.... but unfortunately he can not stand my power..... as for The Highway Dog..... he has another thing coming to him... indeed he got the victory over me on Malice last week, but I also had a pumpkin thrown at me by a fan in the crowd if I am not mistaken.... and despite the fact that he did defeat me, I am ready for him now.... I understand now that I took him to lightly and therefore lost the match.... I should have came prepared, just like I was for the X-Ecutioner.... Ace: About the X-Ecutioner.... what are your thoughts on the match that went down this week between the two of you? Furious: Well.... I must admit that he did take me to my limits... but I knew from the start that he would. I also knew from the start that he would be an example of what the ending of this tournament was going to be, me being on top and winning the title shot at the PPV.... and I could have beaten him, with or without Stir Crazy's help.. Ace: I see, well on Malice, you and the Champion had quite the moment of silence after you won your match, what was that about? Furious: It was a warning...... I warned him that I was coming for him, and him, being the champion that he is, did not back down, that makes him an admirable champion, but I will be there to face him at the PPV.... be their to go toe to toe with him.... be their to go the distance with him, to defeat him and take what's mine from him, the Intercontinental Championship...... and there is nothing he can do to stop me, because if he tries to interfere with my match and attack me like he did X, then I will destroy him then and there..... and that's all there is to it, because one way or another, I will be facing Stir Crazy in the ring at the PPV, no matter what the costs......... Ace: Wow, you do realize the seriousness of this Triple Threat match.... I mean at any moment, The Highway Dog could pin CWebb or CWebb could pin the Highway Dog, you aren't necessarily the man who needs pinned you know Furious: I have thought about this you see....... and come to a fine conclusion..... I shall just have to take both of these men at the same time Ace: I highly doubt that to be possible, not to be disrespectful..... Furious: O but it is possible Ace....... I just need to train hard and do it.... I will start the match on top, and finish the match on top..... that's all there is to it... Ace: Wow I see.... well do you have any last words for the other to men you will be facing? Furious: yea..... Webb and Highway Dog..... fates hand has been dealt and you two boys are the ones up for the sentence to death..... next Monday.... we will all beat the living hell out of each other until we are bruises, battered, and beaten to a bloody pulp, but mark my words, I will rise victorious...... because I am the lord of all evil..... the only man who can tempt fate... and you boys are nothing but normal people...... so with this being said... I expect you both to be at your best, I don't want no squash match this time, I want to destroy you both at your best.... the reaper is coming boys.... look into these eyes and you will see eternity... -As Furious mutters those last words, Ace and the camera man begin to back up as Furious turns around and slowly takes a step back towards his organ. As Furious continues, Ace and the camera man edge closer and closer to the door. As Furious sits down at his organ and begins playing it once more, Ace and the Camera man start out the door as IWX takes a commercial break- Part 2, (much shorter) -As IWX comes back from the commercial break, Ace and the Camera man are at the door across the hallway from Furious's room. As they get to the door, it is flung open as Dante stands there, blocking their way out.- Ace- umm hey their pal *Ace gives Dante a light punch on the shoulder* -The crowd finds this humorous and lets out a laugh once again but then go back to being quiet.- Dante: Have you done what you needed with Furious? Ace: Y-Yep, all rapped up Dante: Good, now leave -Dante steps aside as Ace and the camera man walk through the doorway and to the other side of the room- Griffon: Let them two boys know if they aren't watching this, Furious is ready for them, and their is no turning back once they step into that ring...... -Ace nods his head and then heads out of the door in which he first entered the room.
