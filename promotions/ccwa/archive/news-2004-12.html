News — December 2004

1–9 Dec 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is A rolling news capture from CCWA's own front page, opening on a hack that wiped nearly everything the site had, then the fed's own December 2004 roster and staff signings.
12/09/04 We have signed a few new guys, which means! Our first card is now announced! The Show from Staples Center in LALA Land, Monday December 20th. Get roleplaying! 12/06/04 Nathaniel Hawke just signed on to the active roster, he is a former multiple time champion in other federations and should be a great addition to the roster. Also Colin Lundgren has been announced as Commissioner. We have our backstage account set up and ready to go. Though it's temporary, please get me your login in formation, the information is on the boards. Also former CCWA World Champion Maverick Reno will have to us a new website in early  January and his site designs are phenomenal. 12/03/04: Like to announce the signing of Jacob Briggs to the active roster welcome! And also Damien Payne has signed on as Vice President. Welcome to CCWA guys! 12/01/04: Unfortunately some time in the middle of the night while I was sleeping we got hacked. We lost everything almost. Though I am extremely sad that we lost around 20 results from the past and it makes me angry that some schmuck would do a thing like this. Dude even hacked my email and changed the password. But everything is set up again and we shall be moving to a new secure server very soon. So if you applied and haven't heard anything from me. This is why, so just refill out the application. We should have new boards up hopefully very very soon. Stone 11/30/04: CCWA would like to announce the signing of Furious. Congrats on being the first member of the roster and we hope you have a long, successful stay. This just in! Resigning with CCWA is none other than former NCW and GW champion Tony Stecchino! Welcome back home Tony! Application is now fixed! Sign up! 11/29/04: CCWA is now open and taking applications. Please bear with me as I continue to update the site. The applications page is a bit messed up. Use the tab button to get from field to field. I would love to see a huge turnout and maybe even get a couple of old CCWA heroes back here in CCWA in the for of Tony Stecchino(who designed this site) and Maverick Reno the greatest CCWA champion of all time.
