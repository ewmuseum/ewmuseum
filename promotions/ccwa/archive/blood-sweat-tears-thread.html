Blood, sweat, tears

7 Feb 2005
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is Posted to CCWA's own Feedback Forum by a member writing as “Kenji Lee” — the poster's own subtitle names it “Old GW rp,” a promo written for a different, separately held promotion (Global Wrestling) and shared here for feedback, not a CCWA storyline. Served as CCWA's own forum content; the promo's own opponent, Seymour Almasy, is a memorial-linked character elsewhere in this museum — see this accession's own digest.

Track this topic | Email this topic | Print this topic Kenji Lee Feb 7 2005, 01:08 PM Post #1 Curtain Jerker Group: Members Posts: 7 Joined: 7-February 05 Member No.: 33 Blood, sweat, tears For years, Kenji Lee fought with the Kendo to protect his soul, sprit and pride. This week in Global Wrestling he is expected to do that all again, He now knows he will Face Seymour Almasy in a Kendo Match on this weeks Evolution. In Front of millions Kenji Lee will show all the power he has and the damage he can do with a Kendo Stick. We all know that Kenji is ready, But does he? Blood, sweat, tears; pain and pride. They are the five things that came into my head as I woke from my sleep on Friday morning, I was still in shock that I Kenji lee had been chosen to de-rail a superstar who went by the name of Seymour Almasy. Now I have known and heard of Almasy for a long time now, his dominance was a major force over in UWF for many months, he even was able to capture the world title and make it into their prestigious hall of fame. Once in a lifetime are people inducted into hall of fames. That’s the highlight of anyone’s career. Most people retire after the Hall of fame, but not Almasy, no. He carried on his success and won another title; this one being the primetime global Championship. Now I know this week Seymour has to put that on the line also. Now I am not going to say that he cares for that match more than this one in Global wrestling, as I wouldn’t be speaking the truth. The fact of the matter is that Seymour has bad luck. Not only will he lose his primetime central title. But he will also lose to me, the Asian sensation you know as Kenji Lee. Now I have heard that the match we shall be competing in is my special match. The kendo or Singapore cane match. Now Seymour the downside again for you is, you have never ever competed in a match such as this in the past, where as I. I have competed in more than one thousand of these. And I will be honest with you; I know more tricks with that kendo than I do with anything else in life. Many years ago the traditional people of Japan made a vow. They swore upon the mighty gods above that they would protect the land that they now live upon. Where as the English would use guns, the French would use swords, my native countrymen would use the power of wood. In shape of a sword and call it a kendo stick or a boa-shaft. Now Seymour it seams you was hurt after that so called woman hit you with a kendo, well I can tell you something champ, that kendo stick is nothing compared to the power and energy that runs through my body when I am fighting for my pride. You see that kendo stick is a hard weapon and the main use of it is to tolerate people. But after I am swinging that around hitting you in all your major arteries the match is going to be cut short. Because quite frankly champ, you wont be able to stop me, it’s a simple fact and I am sure you know you have a very slim chance of winning this match, I guess at the end of the day if you do somehow win, your still going to be hurt in a way that your body will be unable to think of how to heal itself. Your body Seymour has never had this type of pain before, you call a few hits by the woman punishment?, then your living a lie. And a lie in my country is equalled with a death sentence. So Seymour, are you living a lie, are you portraying the truth that does not exist? Will you be beaten, hung, and then killed with the sick of kendo on Monday? Only you hold the answer to that one my friend. Now I remember after saying this passage I shifted my body to the right hand side and got out of bed, instead of taking a shower like normal I walked down the corridor and looked into my sister’s bedroom. She was sleeping and not wearing a lot, I could tell this as I saw her nipple slip from under the sheet, I took my head out of her bedroom and carried on walking down the hall. I walked a few steps past the door before stopping and questioning myself on why I was looking at my sister, I then shrugged and carried on walking towards the grand steps which are the main focus of the house here in Albany New York. I remember looking up at the clock as I began to walk down the stairs, the clock read six fifteen, I remember this was quite late in Kenji’s standards, as I walked down I could see the television was on a local station and it had a interview with some-new wrestler on the scene talking about his current federations he would like to work for. Once again global wrestling was on top of that list; it now went on to talk about the highly rated and hyped return of Seymour Almasy. This got my attention as I sat down and turned the volume up and listened in on it. “So you think Seymour has this match won?” one of the commentators said to the other one with a sarcastic tone in his voice. “It’s in the bag already, Kenji lee is good, and it’s his own match, but come on. We are talking about Seymour Almasy here. Most people wouldn’t even show up for the match” the other guy responded. “So the prediction is Seymour Almasy all the way then?” “Yes, and Kenji if your watching this morning I am sorry, its just not your time, you’re the underdog here and you did very well against Max Roswell, but its just not your time” With those comments I threw the remote at the television and it sparked then turned off to a loud bang. I hate it when others voice their options and it’s nothing which you want to hear. Its like a right kick in your teeth, after all the work you put in trying to work and all the people try to put you down its like it never ends. I will beat Seymour and then a lot of people will owe me a apology. Why don’t they think I can do it? Have not I proved to them that I, Kenji lee is here to stay and will not be driven out by others hype. Yes Seymour is the champ, but he is also a man, a man that can and will be defeated, and after I beat Seymour and people take notice of me then I should be in line for a title. As I want to be the champ, and I wont be denied for that title. I don’t care if its Holmes, Double J, or even Rokku, I want my rightfully earn belt, if need be I will find myself a partner and win those tag titles. I won’t be beat, I cannot be beat, I am the Asian sensation. I cannot be beat, and I won’t again, and Seymour I am sorry but I will do everything in this match not to be beat. I will be hardcore, I will cheat, I will go all rules under the sun to get that one…two…three and mark my name in the history books once again, then the elders gods above and the writers in the back, the people watching at home and Bubba McCoy can look around and say that Kenji Lee is ready. He is a Force to be Redound with. Then the sales will go up, the demand for Kenji lee will double and after all that’s happened they wont be chanting the name for Seymour Almasy, oh no!, they will be chanting for the Self Proclaimed Sensation. Kenji Lee. And on Monday it’s the first challenge to make sure that happens Seymour. And it will happen. On a lighter note it was around twelve and I was in my car once again, as I had to meet up with Juvian Ramorez to work on that song that will be used as my new entrance theme. Now Juvian and I had gone back a while, since he quit the ring his music came into play a lot and well we decided it would be better to make one ourselves and ditch the crappy music that Global Wrestling created for me. I wanted a theme at as soon as the few bars hit you knew who it was. That gives the crowd chance to start chanting my name and cheering as I begin to get ready and appear out of the curtain. I arrived at the studio around two, it was a long and stressful drive. But one good thing that comes out of stress is that the mind just writes, and it carries on writing until there is nothing left to say. So in that sense it was successful. Juvian welcomed me with open arms, and we walked into a room, this isn’t the first time I’ve been in a studio but it’s the first time that I have co-written a song. So I have a brief layout of how I want it to sound. “Shall we get started?” he spoke to me. “No time like the present” I said back to him. One thing I especially liked about Juvian is that his will to work was so high, no stopping, he just got on with it. “So what have you got down for the first couple of lines then?” he said to me. I got out my little black book and read him what I had so far. “I am coming- to the end Like a story, but not pretend I'm like the pages, in the book Slowly turning to take a look.” I said to him, I think he liked what he heard as he praised me a lot on them, also in his eyes I could see his mind working to the music playing in the background. “Who’s that in there?” I asked Juvian with a puzzled look on my face. “That’s a local band from Sarasota, Fl called Random Xaos. I do a lot of work with them, they shall be providing the music layout for the track is that ok?” whoa he asked me to make a decision. “Yea, that’s cool with me, anything just to make it sound the best in Global Wrestling will do for me.” I replied with a stern and yet evil look on my face. For the next three hours and after so many cups of tea to keep me awake we had finished just under four hours and we had made Kenji’ Lee’s new entrance theme for Global Wrestling. I was absolutely over the moon, but then my luck was about to change. As the band was leaving the recording booth, I heard one of them says to the other one, “Who is this Kenji Lee guy anyway? everybody knows Seymour is going to wipe his ass with him when they meet on Monday.” “Sure thing that my friend” The other replied to him. I just stood in front of them as they walked my way; they suddenly saw it was me, Kenji lee in there way as they cut into a false sentence of communication. “WHAT THE HELL DID YOU JUST SAY!” I turned to look at him and his friend; I had a evil star and couldn’t help shouting at them. “Sorry Kenji, I never knew you were there” He replied. It’s funny because all my life I have had people say that to me. Whether it is my family hiding the truth from me, or its people talking to others about my progress it always happens, and quite frankly I want it to stop. Right now! Stop talking about me when I am not there. I always find out in the end what you have said. Wherever Kenji lee seems to go, it’s like he is going to lose to Seymour, is there anyone in the world that actually thinks that I can beat him apart from myself? Maybe I am living a dream? Maybe I cannot beat Seymour; maybe it’s all a false part of reality… What the hell am I thinking, Of course I can beat Seymour, I will do as well. Seymour, its nearly time for the clash of the kendo’s and you have to think, Can you really Beat This?, Can you Beat Kenji Lee?!
