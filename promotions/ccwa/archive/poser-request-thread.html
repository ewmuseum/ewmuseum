Johnny Depp is the body type for the character. Thin, lanky and slightly ripped, but more like someone who's lived and gotten in shape, rather than worked out and gotten in shape. Face: Would be a mix between Johnny Depp and Baek Do San from Tekken 5. Long silver and black hair, silver eyes.. the eyes are important. Thin face, obviously, and a thin trimmed mustache with a small line of hair down his chin. I can't think of what that's called. Outfit: Black leather jeans, black combat boots, a black cotten hook shirt (You see these in films where there are S&M scenes or intended S&M props, or also at Hot Topic. ^^) and shackles on his wrist with a couple links of broken chain hanging. If he's bare chested then it's important to have scars, specifically beneath his shoulder blades. Otherwise he has a cigarette burns bracelet on his right arm and numerous scars from scarification on his stomach, upper and lower arms, and chest. On his back are just scars from whips and the ones beneath his shoulder blades, where the hooks were inserted and caught on the bone. Hmmm.. As of this moment I think that's about it.
