Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

By the fall of 2000, NAWA was several years into a run its own president dated, in an aside during a newsgroup exchange, to two years and five months — an origin around early 1998 that no surviving document confirms directly. Its weekly broadcast, Tuesday Night Rage, aired from arenas across the continent under a rotating pair of commentators, and its own promotional teasers traced an annual November pay-per-view tradition back through Seattle Shockwave in 1998 and Seattle Shakedown in 1999 to that fall's Seattle Showdown.

Frank “The Falcon” Peregrine wrestled on screen as the promotion's own owner and president, a heel authority figure who was double-choked-slammed by his own roster on one Tuesday Night Rage and, at that November's Soul Survivor 2000, fielded a translator's question in a Mexican press conference about stepping down to regain custody of his son.

Its North American Championship changed hands at least once on screen, decided in a Tuesday Night Rage main event before a raucous Salt Lake City crowd, and its Soul Survivor 2000 pay-per-view crowned a new titleholder amid a ring invasion the broadcast's own announce team could not talk over.

No roster sheet or title history survives outside these broadcasts; the last document on file is dated 16 November 2000.
