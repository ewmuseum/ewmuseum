6- Deadline Announcements
==========================================================
It was stated a bit earlier, but we still want to make sure everyone
gets the point about the deadlines.
FLASHES...
You are required to flash at least once per week.  No more than once
per packet, which is a max of twice per week.  The packets will go
out on Monday Night and Friday night every week.
The deadlines for flashes are 7 pm EST on both Monday and Friday.  If
you send a flash for Monday and it does not make the deadline, it
will go out in the next packet.  Get the picture there?  Now remember
that Flashes account for 40-50% of the decision when booking your
matches, so send in the RP's as often as possible.  It will help you
in the long run.
STRATS...
Since only one of our shows requires a long strat and one requires a
very short strat, we have decided to have them both be due on the
same night.  Strats for the Sat. and next Tuesday's shows are due at
7 pm EST (US) on Wednesday night!
If you do not send in a strat, you will lose.  There are no
exceptions unless something really out of the ordinary has happened,
but for the most part only death should keep you from stratting.
Strats count for about 40% of the booking process, so get them in.
