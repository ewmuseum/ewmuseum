Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Rampage World Wrestling described itself, in RSPWF's own 2002 community fed directory, as offering "3 years of history, fair results, on time results, no out of character fighting" — "great times, great creative rivalries, Rampage World Wrestling has it all," putting its own founding at roughly 1999. Its own hosted GeoCities splash page survives from a later point in its run: a single banner image reading "Rampage World Wrestling--Where Only The Strong Survive," linked onward to a further address, rww.newerawrestling.com, that was never itself captured.

No roster, card, or title of its own survives in either holding. Its listed address, efedplanet.com/rampage, no longer resolves.
