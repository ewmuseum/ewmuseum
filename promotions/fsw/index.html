Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

On New Year's Eve 1997 the Federation of Supreme Wrestling crowned its first named World Heavyweight Champion live from the Hollywood Park Casino in Inglewood, California, in a multi-man caged brawl it called the New Year's Massacre Match, where elimination came by blood loss rather than pinfall.

Down to the final two, Tommy Van Dam slapped “Ice Man” Chris Winters, walked away from a one-on-one fight, and asked officials to let him out of the cage; Winters was awarded the vacant title by forfeit. As officials handed him the belt inside the blood-soaked ring, “Total Eclipse” Eric Black and “Studly” Steven Black appeared on a rooftop looking down at the new champion, and the broadcast ended with the newly-eliminated Ruiner charging back into the cage to attack him.

This is the third part of a three-part New Year's Eve special; the other two parts, and the promotion's own three earlier Information Packet versions from that November and December, survive but are not individually recapped here. A follow-up document confirms the promotion still running a year later; no closing statement survives.
