[GSW] Golden State Wrestling - Announcement Do you want to make a name for yourself in e-wrestling, or do you feel you all ready have and want to make it even better? Are you shooting to become #1 on the RSPWF Top 200 singles or tag-team list, well than the [GSW] is the place for you. From high flying non stop action to getting dirty in the backstage locker rooms the [GSW] offers the widest variety of wrestling at its best. Trying to bring back some tradition we strive to be the best on the net. Don't ask me come check it out for yourself, all though the page may not look to impressive the quality is, and remember, it's the size of the flash/roleplay, its the QUALITY. That's one of our modos in the [GSW]. THe homepage is http://www.geocities.com/Colosseum/Sideline/4285/index.html The next card is not this saturday but the next and a few openings are still available. Want to be the best in the business, well, your going to have to go through the [GSW] first, if you can survive that, well, than you might as well just retire now.
