Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Golden State Wrestling introduced itself to rec.sport.pro-wrestling.fantasy on 26 February 1998 with a single recruitment post, run from a GeoCities homepage and pitching itself in its own words as a fed offering “high flying non stop action” alongside “getting dirty in the backstage locker rooms.”

Its own pitch aimed squarely at the RSPWF Top 200 rankings, asking would-be handlers “Are you shooting to become #1 on the RSPWF Top 200 singles or tag-team list” and promising that, whatever the homepage looked like, “the quality is” there — a line it called one of its own “modos.” The post named a card the following Saturday with openings still open on the roster.

How that card, or the promotion itself, played out isn't in the surviving record. This one announcement is Golden State Wrestling's own surviving document.
