SJPW: New Ownership (the ownership-change pair)

20-21 April 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
----- SJPW: New ownership (Apr 20, 1997, 12:00:00 AM) ----- Super Japan Pro Wrestling is back in business! [name withheld] was kind enough to let me take over his great idea of a japanese based efed! SJPW is the first official league of the GIWA that I and some others are trying to start. I sent out email to all the SJPW handlers, but some emails came back with bad addresses (or my server can't connect to it). Would the following people get in touch with me please: Jyurai ( [detail withheld] ) [detail withheld] (The tag team Supreme Court) Thanks, [name withheld] SJPW new president ----- SJPW: Offical Announcement of new ownership (Apr 21, 1997, 12:00:00 AM) ----- (The setting is a conference room in the SJPW corporate headquarters in Tokyo, Japan. A large group of reporters, japanese and otherwise are in attendance. [name withheld], CEO of SJPW steps up to the podium, and prepares to speak) [name withheld]: Thank you all for coming today. As you all know. Super Japan Pro Wrestling was supposed to start holding matches a few weeks ago. Due to some dealings in the upper echelons of the federation's corporate structure, the SJPW debut in the Tokyo dome was put on indefinite hold. This has led to the departure of some of the talent SJPW signed before the start date was set up. (pauses to look through his papers) From this moment on, I am no longer CEO of SJPW. A consortium of investors did a leverage buyout of the federation. As a result of this, I was released from my duties as SJPW CEO. I part company with SJPW on the best of terms, and wish the federation well. Now, I'd like to introduce the new CEO of SJPW, Shinsaku Yamashita (Mr Yamashita steps up to the podium. He is a well built japanese man in his early 50's. He is wearing a grey armani suit. [name withheld] sits down) Shinsaku Yamashita: Thank you mark. I am Shinsaku Yamashita. I am a board member for a new alliance of wrestling federations called the Global Internet Wrestling Alliance, or GIWA. I took over SJPW to add it to the GIWA's roster of regional federations that we are building. Along with the European E-wrestling Federation, EEF, SJPW is one of the first federatiosn to become a part of the GIWA. Our goal is to have an alliance of regional federations that will not only have their own championships, but will recognize one world champion. With new ownership comes new changes. [name withheld] is no longer CEO of SJPW, and [name withheld] will no longer be with us. Akira Ono shall remain with SJPW, as will Miss Akiyama. As SJPW will be allied with american federations in the GIWA, we wil have Ring Wars televised on cable in the US. Therfore, we have hired a GIWA play by play man to co-commentate with mr Ono. His name is Eddie Rose, and he was specifically hired by the GIWA committe to work for SJPW. Mr. Misawa is still in charge of ringside security for SJPW. As for the title belts, there are some minor changes. The Junior heavyweight title has been redesignated as the SJPW Cruiserweight title. We may add a tv title, and a special title for more extreme style matches, like our competitors in FMW and W*ing participate in. We are in the process of contacting the remainging wrestlers in SJPW who have yet to commit to staying with us. Super Tengu is the first wrestler to commit to staying, and we thank him for his support. The SJPW has gone through some torrents of late, but calmer waters should now prevail. We look forward to getting SJPW back in action as soon as possible. I thank you all for coming. We will not be taking questions today. Good Day
