SJPW Card Archive

Metro, 2/2

13 October 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Suddenly, the sound of Japanese war drums resounds through the arena. The audience turn their heads towards the entrance way, attempting to catch a glimpse of what is happening. The curtains to the entrance way are thrown aside and a column of marching Japanese men in kimonos, carrying drums and flutes, enter the arena in an orderly fashion. They do not look at the crowd, but only make their way somberly towards the entrance] JA: What the ?!*& [In the center of this column of men is Yukio "The Warbird" Murakami, a stocky, muscular man with a shaved head and dressed in a red and white kimono. He ignores the crowd and silently guides his column of men into the ring] ER: It's Yukio Murakami! He's not scheduled to be here tonight! JA: It looks like he's gonna say something... [Inside the ring, Yukio grabs the microphone away from the ring announcer. His men stand around him, playing their instruments. Yukio waves a hand and the men stop playing their instruments. Yukio looks at the audience.] Yukio "The Warbird" Murakami: When I look out at this audience, all I see are the remains of a once great empire. You are all corrupted by the decadence of the West! With your rock and roll and dance music and Hollywood movies! You have forgotten what it means to be a proud culture, a culture of warriors and emperors!" [Yukio ignores the boos and insults which rise from the crowd.] Yukio: I have returned to Japan from the West after beating every wrestler the gaijin had to throw at me. My crusade continues here, in the SJPW! I will not rest until I have unified all the titles available in the SJPW and proven to the people of my fallen country that the Way of the Warrior is the RIGHT WAY! The Warbird's way is the RIGHT WAY! Honor, righteousness, pride, and merciless ability. [Yukio points his finger at the camera. The camera man zooms in for a close- up.] Yukio: Like I've said before, Angel Taboo, you are but a gnat which troubles the sleep of a great lion. On Tsunami, I will use you to demonstrate the devastating skill and fortitude of the last son of the Murakami clan. Now, I address the other wrestlers of the SJPW - watch carefully how I dismantle Angel Taboo on Tsunami...for you are NEXT! [Yukio throws the microphone down to the mat amidst a chorus of boos. His men resume playing their instruments, following Yukio out of the ring and towards the exit. The camera pans back to Eddie & Jonnie...] ER: It seems that Yukio Murakami has decided that he's the only man worthy of holding all the titles in SJPW. JA: The man has definite talent Eddie, but his attitude, that's something else. He's not the only man in SJPW to follow the path of the warrior. I can think of quite a few others who do. ER: So can I Jonnie, but Yukio doesn't believe they follow the same path he does. It will be interesting to see him and Angel Taboo face off this coming weekend. JA: I can't wait! Angel Taboo claims to be from a long line of warriors like Murakami, so we'll have to see whose bloodline is stronger. ER: That we will. Up next though, we have none other than The Banshee, facing off against Yataro Hori, who has just returned from a big tour of the U.S. JA: Hatori's a tough customer Eddie. Banshee better not underestimate him, or he'll find himself going down in defeat. He's got a big match this weekend against Kabuki Kid, so I'm not sure how smart it was for him to take this match tonight. ER: Banshee really feels the need to prove himself. He's been in a slump over in rival Gunyro Pro, and feels his tenure in SJPW could be cut short if he isn't careful. A win tonight could convince the powers that be that he's able to compete in SJPW... __ ___ _____________________________________________________ / |/ /__ / /________ / / /|_/ / -_) __/ __/ _ \ Yataro Hori vs The Banshee /_/ /_/\__/\__/_/ \___/_______________________________________/ [The scene changes to the ring where The Banshee bows toward Hori who returns the gesture.] JA: Yataro Hori has just returned to Japan from an extensive tour of the United States while The Banshee is a well-known gaijin who has been wrestling in Japan for some time. ER: He’s got a good reputation but we’ll have to see if it’s deserved as Hori is a wily veteran of the squared circle. JA: They lock up and Hori with an Irish whip... no, reversal by Banshee sends Hori into the ropes. He rebounds back and Banshee sends him face-first into the canvas with a drop toehold. Banshee takes a moment to tilt his head back and issue that ululating warcry of his. ER: An interesting psychological weapon that serves to agitate his opponents. And the crowd here tonight is certainly behind him as they respond with a cheer. Banshee now sending Hori for the ride with an Irish whip and knocks him down on the rebound with a flying shoulderblock. JA: Hori back on his feet quickly but Banshee maintains the pressure with an atomic drop... and then an ENZUIGIRI to the back of the head! [crowd applauds] ER: So far, Hori has been able to mount little offense as Banshee climbs up onto the top turnbuckle... FLYING SOMERSAULT SPLASH... no! Hori rolls out of the way at the last moment and Banshee hits nothing but canvas! JA: One of the disadvantage of a largely aerial arsenal is that it puts you at some risk when you miss high impact maneuvers like that. Hori now backing up as Banshee struggles to his feet... HANDSPRING INTO A FRANKENSTEINER by Hori! [crowd "Ooohhs" and applauds] ER: Banshee took a hard shot to the top of his head as Hori goes for the pin... ...1... ...2... ...KICKOUT!!!! JA: Incredible resilience shown by the masked man as he manages to escape being pinned. Hori pulls him to his feet... HURRICANRANA... NO! Banshee counters with a power bomb! [crowd cheers] ER: These two really taking it to each other as now The Banshee goes for a pin... JA: Banshee with that devastating finisher and he hooks the leg... ...1... ...2... ...shoulder up! ER: The Banshee has the support of the fans tonight as he lets out another of his warcries but Hori is putting up a good fight. Banshee pulls Hori to his feet and attempts a DDT... NO! Hori counters with a backdrop. JA: Hori making a good showing here and it would look very good on his record if he upset The Banshee tonight. He’s up on the second turnbuckle... flying fistdrop and he goes for another pin... ...1... ...2... ...KICKOUT!!! ER: The Banshee’s not ready to give up yet as Hori watches him while he regains his feet. Hori with a superkick... no, Banshee ducks it and comes up beside him to connect with a series of kicks to the ribs. Banshee runs into the ropes and rebounds back with a diving shoulderblock. JA: The Banshee heading up top once more as Hori slowly rises... flying spinning leg lariat knocks Hori down once again! ER: An incredible move especially with the way The Banshee flies through the air like that! JA: Hori is down on the mat as Banshee runs towards the ropes... springboard somersault legdrop! [crowd "Oooohhs" and applauds] Hori is definitely in trouble here as Banshee pulls him upright and sends him into the ropes. Banshee with a backdrop attempt but Hori floats over... and pulls him down with a sunset flip! Here’s the count... ...1... ...2... ...KICKOUT!!!! ER: Both men pulling out all the stops as they roll to their feet... and Banshee flings Hori over with a belly-to-belly suplex. Hori dazed for a moment as Banshee climbs up onto the top turnbuckle once more... leaps off... senton! [crowd applauds] JA: The Banshee definitely has the advantage now as he picks Hori up in a fireman’s carry and carries him over into the corner. He’s setting him up on the top turnbuckle and climbing up there with him... oh! Standing piledriver from the top rope! [crowd "Ooohs" and applauds] ER: Banshee with that devastating finisher he calls The Atomic Sky Spike and he hooks the leg... ...1... ...2... ...3!!!! **ding** **ding** **ding** Nishi Toju: The winner of this match.... THE BANSHEE!!! [crowd applauds] [Camera cuts to the broadcast table...] JA: Very impressive as Banshee demonstrates why he’s one of the finest cruiserweights competing in Japan today. ER: And he’s got his work cut out for him as he’s set his sights on The Kabuki Kid. But tonight’s performance was certainly impressive. JA: We now take you to comments made by Super Tengu after the big six man match on Tsunami last sunday. Needless to say, Tengu was in a fowl mood. ER: I'd have to agree Jonnie, since I was there! ____ _____ _ _____________________________________________ / __/_ / / _ \ | /| / / / _\ \/ // / ___/ |/ |/ / Super Tengu /___/\___/_/ |__/|__/__________________________________________/ [open camera shot in the locker room following Tsunami, Eddie Rose stands ready to interview Super Tengu] Eddie Rose: Fans Edie Rose here and what a night it was!! Yes My team was victorious, but the real story is yet to come!! Kobayashi are you listening to me? Does Tengu have your undivided attention? Let me make this very clear to you... Super Tengu will NOT set foot in an SJPW ring again unless across the ring from me is Jensei Tenru. Forget the petty ignorance of the Mandarin. Tengu is being gracious and giving that pathetic manchild a chance to get over by riding Tengu's magnificent coat tails!! Do you hear me Jumbo? Is everything sinking into that oversized freak head of yours? Tengu will not wrestle till Tenru is in my sight across the ring ready to take the beating he rightfully deserves!! [Tengu tosses the mic at Rose who catches it nervously] Eddie Rose: W-what? Tengu you can't be serious!! Super Tengu: [turning to Rose angrily] DID I STUTTER BOY? TAKE THIS TO KOBAYASHI AND LET HIM KNOW WHO TRULY MAKES POLICY!! [voices drops and eyes narrow] Oh Tenru, you have done what no other man has yet to do... YOU'VE STIRRED MY ANGER!! SET ABLAZE MY WRATH!! THIS IS MY ULTIMATUM!! NO TENRU.... [voice lowers to an almost icy tone] means no Tengu... that is all Tengu has to say to you... [tosses the mic on the floor and exits] [fade out] ER: See what I mean? Tengu refuses to wrestle! JA: I hope whoever Jumbo sent can get this situation cleared up fast! Otherwise, we'll lose our main event! ER: I hope not. JA: Neither do I. In the meantime though, we have another match in the tournament, as "Flying Dragon" Jushin Kojima, part of Ryu A. Taka's stable, takes on Tasogare no Prince. ER: This should be a good match Jonnie. Both men have been competing in the U.S. of late, with Twilight Prince having held cruiserweight titles in at least two U.S. promotions. As for Kojima, he's newer to the sport, but he's a top contendor for the light-heavyweight title in AAPW, one of our allied promotions in the U.S. JA: Let's not waste any more time then; let's get to ringside, and get things underway! __ ___ _____________________________________________________ / |/ /__ / /________ Welterweight Tournament / / /|_/ / -_) __/ __/ _ \ Jushin Kojima vs Tasogare no Prince /_/ /_/\__/\__/_/ \___/_______________________________________/ [The scene changes to the ring where Kojima and Tasogare no Prince square off against each other.] JA: This should be a very interesting match as while Tasogare no Prince may have an advantage in terms of experience, Kojima has Ryu A. Taka at ringside to offer his advice. ER: Tasogare no Prince will have to watch out for that putter though as Taka may involve himself more in the match than he should. SPRINGBOARD HURRICANRANA by the Twilight Prince! [crowd "Ooohhhs" then applauds] ER: A very impressive start as Tasogare no Prince pulls Kojima back to his feet... but is knocked down by a dropkick from Kojima that caught him by surprise. Kojima lines him up... and nails Tasogare no Prince with a standing moonsault! [crowd applauds] JA: Both men pulling out hard-hitting moves very quickly as they both seek to take the other man down. Kojima in control at the moment as he sets up... SNAP SUPLEX sends Tasogare no Prince up and over. ER: Ryu A. Taka at ringside, yelling encouragement to his protégée. Kojima pulls Tasogare no Prince up once more... ENZUIGIRI by Kojima but it misses as Tasogare no Prince ducks out of the way. Kojima goes down hard and Tasogare no Prince pounces, slapping on a Lion Tamer submission hold. JA: A very painful variation of the camel clutch and Kojima is in trouble here. He’s struggling to reach the ropes... and he makes it! Tasogare no Prince not pleased to have to release the hold as he pulls Kojima to his feet. ER: The advantage has changed hands several times in this match but it’s Tasogare no Prince who’s in control right now. He’s behind Kojima, grabs him around the waist... release German suplex! [crowd applauds] JA: Both men demonstrating some very impressive moves but neither attempting a pinfall as of yet. Tasogare no Prince now demonstrating his quickness as he climbs up onto the top turnbuckle. Kojima struggles to his feet... MISSILE DROPKICK sends him flying! [crowd "oooohs", then applauds] ER: The Twilight Prince showing off his impressive arsenal of maneuvers as he pours the pressure on. Taka making his presence felt... but look! Misawa is right there to grab him and take him away! ER: Word has come down that Kobayashi will not tolerate interference and Ryu A. Taka is being escorted back to the locker rooms. But the damage has been done as Tasogare no Prince has been hurt and Jushin Kojima is back in control. JA: Kojima picks Tasogare no Prince up... shoulderbreaker and that looked like it hurt! A devastating move as Kojima follows that up with a vertical suplex. [crowd applauds] Kojima not letting up for a moment as he climbs up in the corner while the Twilight Prince is down on the mat... FLYING LEGDROP from the top rope... but Tasogare no Prince rolled out of the way! [crowd "oooohs", then applauds] ER: Kojima in trouble as he came down hard. Tasogare no Prince shaking out the cobwebs as he pulls Kojima up and whips him into the ropes... Springboard moonsault by Tasogare no Prince OVER TOP of Kojima as he lands behind him... and nails the TWILIGHT SUPLEX! JA: Tasogare no Prince executes his finisher and this may be it as he’s got the pin... ...1... ...2... ...3!!!! **ding** **ding** **ding** Nishi Toju: The winner of this match.... TASOGARE NO PRINCE!!! [crowd applauds] [Camera cuts to the broadcast table...] JA: An excellent showing by both men although that interference by Ryu A. Taka almost proved a crucial moment in derailing Tasogare no Prince’s momentum. ER: Yes, and despite some devastating moves by Jushin Kojima, the Twilight Prince managed to pull off a win. JA[holding his headset]: Something's going backstage. Let's get a camera back there ASAP! [cut to the locker room area. Super Tengu is in the background seen arguing with an SJPW official. Riki: Whatever... anyway he came barging in here, busting up my interview and told Tengu that if he refused to wrestle that the Super one's license would be suspended for 90 day. That meant missing the heavyweight tourney. since then They've been jawjackin' back there!! [Suddenly Matsumoto points to the door and angrily yells at Tengu. Tengu pounds his fist violently against the wall in protest but Matsumoto waves a paper in front of him. finally Tengu storms off to his locker. Matsumotot approaches the camera crew] Matsumoto: What is this I authorized no camera crew here? Riki: Yo, Kenshiro.. what's the scoop? Matsumoto: [looking at Fudo strangely] Well it seems my beloved nephew attempted to dictate policy here in SJPW but I reminded him of the fact that Kobayashi-san is calling the shots. Kobayashi-san has hired me as assistant head of operations and it is my job to make sure the talent that is booked wretles. Tetsuo... Riki: Don't you mean Tengu? Matsumoto: [gruffly] He is Tetsuo no matter what he thinks. He may think he can hide behind a mask but he is still my broter's son. anyway, I told him that if he didn't wrestle I would enforce a 90 day suspension. as we speak he is changing into his wrestling gear.. I will NOT tolerate any disobedience from the talent. I am Kobayashi's enforcer.. AND ENFORCE I WILL!! [Matsumoto exits from the locker room as Fudo watches in beilderment and whistels softly] Camera man: Kuso!! Things sure got interesting all of a sudden... Riki: You can say that again brother... [camera cuts back to the broadcast position] ER: Wow! Kenshiro Matsumoto was the man Jumbo sent to get Tengu into the ring! Amazing! JA: Jumbo did it again. He constantly proves why he was chosen to be CEO of this promotion! This is a unique twist to this situation! ER: I'll say. I'm really surprised Kenshiro Matsumoto was here. We were never informed that he was hired! JA: Well fans its time for our main event, and what a match it should be as two SJPW vetrans, Super Tengu and the Mandarin, will meet in the first round of the Welterweight Tournament. It almost didn't happen, but thanks to Kenshiro Matsumoto, things are back on track. I have to say Eddie, looking at the rest of the field, based on experince, the winner of this match has to be considered a strong favorite to take the whole tournament. ER: Your right on the money. The rest of the field is composed of newcomers, at least to Super Japan. These two vets may very well be deciding the Welterweight champion right here in the first round of the tourny! That is Mandarin can win, since Tengu will probably pull this stunt again until he and Tenru face off in the ring! JA: On that note lets go to the ring!
