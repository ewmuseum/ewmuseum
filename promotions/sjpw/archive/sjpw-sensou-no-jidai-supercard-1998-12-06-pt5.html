Sensou no Jidai Supercard, Part Five

6 December 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Sensou no Jidai concludes...] TK: The ring is clear, and it's time for our main event. Sixteen men started this tournament, and it's down to two of the. Tasogare no Prince, the Twilight Prince, faces Yukio "The Warbird" Murakami to determine who will be the first welterweight champion... AO: Both men are excellant welterweights, but did this have to be the main event? Surely Hamada or Matsumoto would have been better off headlining... TK: Akira! Hamada is in America in the TCI tournament, and Tetsuo only just returned. This was the scheduled main event for this card. AO: I know, but... TK: But nothing! Let's go backstage, and check in on Yukio Murakami... [CAMERA fades in on a dressing room deep in the bowels of the arena. The dressing room is decorated with traditional Japanese silk screens, carefully arranged chrysanthemums, calligraphy scrolls, and ornate fans. Incense burns in a vase sitting on a low table. Yukio "The Warbird" Murakami is kneeling at the table, his eyes closed. He is dressed in his blue kimono and his right arm is heavily bandaged. He breathes deeply in a series of exercises. He then unsheathes a wakizsashi sword and cuts the forefinger on his right hand. He allows the droplets of blood to wash into a cup of steaming tea. The Warbird drinks the tea, being very careful to turn the appropriate number of cycles. He then pours himself a small drink of saki. [The Warbird falls deep into meditation. The CAMERA fades out.] AO: Drinking Saki? Is he crazy? TK: It's part of the meditation technique. That small amount will have no effect on him. AO: I have my doubts... TK: You always do! Let's go to the ring, and crown a champion! ____ _____ _ _____________________________________________ / __/_ / / _ \ | /| / / Welterweight Tournament Final: / _\ \/ // / ___/ |/ |/ / Tasogare no Prince vs Yukio Murakami /___/\___/_/ |__/|__/__________________________________________/ [The crowd pans to Nishi Toju & Goto Kyoji standing in the ring. The crowd is cheering wildly. Nishi motions for them to quiet down, so he can make his announcement. They stop cheering, and he raises the microphone to speak...] Nishi: This is our MAIN EVENT for the evening! [crowd cheers]. It is one fall, with a one hour time limit. It is the finals of the Welterweight title tournament! [crowd applauds] [Calm, sombre, yet somehow soothing cello music plays over the PA] Nishi: Introducing first, hailing from The Twilight, he weighs 190 pounds....TAS-O-GAR-E no PRINCE!!! [The crowd applauds as Tasogare no Prince steps out from backstage, and calmly heads to ringside. He is carrying a bouquet of white roses, and stops along the runway to pass them out to various women in the audience. He steps into the ring, and goes to his corner, then bows to the referee...] AO: This man is a strange one! He's the palest nipponese I've ever seen. Must be Ainu! TK: Knock it off Ono! [Soft Japanese flutes begin to play over the PA, then erupt into banging War Drums] Nishi: His opponent, hailing from Osaka, and weighing 225 pounds...YU-KI-O "THE WARBIRD" MUR-A-KA-MI!!! [Murakami steps out on the runway, and the crowd cheers. Two men follow behind him, bearing the Murakami family crest. He strides to the ring confidently. His right arm is still bandaged...] AO: Murakmi's arm could be his weak link. Miller did cause injury to it in their match. TK: Miller was wrestling smart, and this could cost Murakami tonight! [Goto gives both men instructions, and they nod. Murakami offers his hand to Tasogare, and he shakes it, causing the crowd to applaud. The bells sounds, and both men circle each other looking for an opening. They lock up, and Murakami maneuvers Prince into a wristlock. He twists the arm slowly, and Prince doubles over. Murakami attempts to twist the arm again, but Prince drives his shoulder into Murakami's gut, then reverses the hold on his injured arm. Muramki winces in pain, but grits his teeth. Prince drives his shoulder into Murakami's arm three times, then turns the arm into a hammerlock. Murakami attempts a back elbow, but Prince ducks it. Murakami attempts a reversal, but Prince blocks it. Murakami reaches back with his free arm, and locks it around the back of Prince's head. He jumps up, and snap mare's Prince over as he comes down! Prince rolls to his feet, and both men stare at each other as the crowd applauds...] AO: Good work by both men so far. They are testing each other, seeking weak links in their opponent's armor... TK: It seems even now, but time will tell who has more weak links... [They lock up again, and Prince again goes for the hammerlock. Murakami catches him this time with the back elbow, and nails a second for good measure. Prince releases the hold, and Murakami runs to the ropes. He comes off, and Prince catches him with a dropkick. Murakami staggers back, and Prince gets to his feet quickly, and hits a second dropkick. Murakami falls back to the ropes, and Prince charges. He attempts a hurricanrana, but Murakami drops down, slamming him to the mat with a powerbomb! The crowd "ooohs" loudly, and Murakami attempts a cover, but Prince kicks out at one. Murakami sits Prince up, and attempts the Magistral Cradle, but Prince kicks out at one and half. The crowd applauds again as both men roll to their feet...] AO: Smart move by Murakami. He went for the pin again quickly, as Prince was still a bit dazed. TK: But Prince was aware enough to kick out! [Both men start to circle each other again, then move to lock up. Prince catches Murakami coming in with a kick to the gut, then drives his knee into his gut. Murakami doubles over, and Prince grabs him by the back of the hair, and drops down into a sitting position, driving Murkami's face into the mat! The crowd "oooohs", while Prince gets to his feet, and runs to the ropes, hitting a snap legdrop. He runs to the ropes again, and hits a second legdrop. He does this a third time, then rolls Murakami over, and covers. Goto drops to count, and Murakami kicks out at two. The crowd applauds. Prince drags Murakami to his feet, then snapmares him over. He runs to the ropes, coming off with a low dropkick to Murakami's face! The crowd "oooohs" as Prince blows a kiss to a woman in the crowd...] AO: Prince is showing everyone that he does have a little mean streak! He can be *both* a lover and a figher! TK: These two men are like opposite sides of a coin! [Prince drags Murakami to his feet, and attempts an irish whip, but Murakami reverses the move, and pulls Prince towards him, sending him to the mat with a short-arm clothesline. Prince goes down hard, and Murakami, still holding his arm, drags him to his feet. He locks his right arm under Prince's left arm, creating a half nelson, then takes him over with an Exploder Suplex, dumping Prince on his head! The crowd "oooohs" loudly, and Murakami drags Prince back up, and lifts him up, then falls back, dropping him hard to the mat! Murakami rolls on top for a cover, but Goto only counts to two before Prince kicks out. Murakami heads to the corner, and hops up to the second rope. He pats his right arm, then dives off for an elbowsmash, but Prince moves! The crowd "ooohs" as Murakami lands elbow first on the mat, then rolls around, holding his elbow in pain...] AO: This is the mistake that could decide the match... TK: I fear you are right... [Prince rolls to the ropes, and using them for leverage, gets to his feet. He holds the back of his head, then looks over at Murakami, who is sitting up, holding his elbow. Prince walks over to him, grabs his right arm, and extends it outward. He drops down, driving his elbow onto Murakami's shoulder, and Murakami grunts in pain. Prince stands up, still holding the arm, and locks his legs around Murakami's neck. He twists his body, falling to the mat, and pulls on the arm. Murakami grunts in pain again, and Goto asks him if he wants to submit. Murakami shakes his head now, then tries to sit up. Prince pulls back on the arm again, and Murakami stops moving. He tries again, and is able to sit up, putting Prince off balance. He shifts his body over, so that he is leaning on top of Prince. Prince tries to cince the headscissors, but it's obvious that he's lost his leverage. Murakami reaches down, and locks his hand around the back of Prince's neck. Then, showing surprising strength, lifts himself off the mat, so that he has Prince's shoulders to the mat! Goto drops to count, and Prince breaks the armbar/headscissors at two, and sits up. Murakami rolls away from him, and clutches his arm. Prince stares at Murakami with admiration in his eyes, then gets to his feet...] AO: What a move! he was literally vertical on top of Prince! TK: But it cost his arm more damage! [Murakami gets to his feet, favoring his arm. Prince moves towards him, and Murakami catches him with a left-handed chop across his chest. The crowd "Whooos" as Prince staggers back a moment, then fires back with a right-handed chop of his own! The crowd "Whooos" again, and Murakami staggers back, then leaps forward, catching Prince with a Yakuza kick to the face! Prince goes down hard, and Murakami leaps up, and lands a stomp to his gut. Prince sits up in pain, and Murakami leaps again, landing an axe kick on top of Prince's head! Prince collapses on the mat, and the crowd applauds loudly. Murakami draws his thumb across his throat, then drags Prince to his feet. He whips him chest first to the corner, and follows in behind him with a lariat to the back of the head. Murakami gets on the top rope, and locks Prince in a front facelock. He signals for the Divine Wind, but Prince grabs Murakami's tights, and lifts him off the turnbuckle! The crowd "ooohs" as Prince turns towards the ropes, and drops Murakami gut first onto the ropes! Murkami sags there, and Prince goes to the corner, climbs to the top, then comes off with a Rocker Dropper than sends Murakami flying into the ring, and on his back....] AO: Murakami had Prince where he wanted him, and he took too long! TK: And now he's paying for his mistake... [Prince drags Murakami to his feet, then whips him to the ropes. He starts a tilt o'whirl, but instead of a backbreaker of slam, drives Murakami's right shoulder down onto his knee! The crowd "oooohs" as Murakami screams in pain. Prince drags Murakami to his feet, places him into a pumphandle, then hoists him over his shoulder, and drives him down into a shoulderbreaker! The crowd "oooohs" again, and Murakami screams in pain once more! Prince drags Murakami up again, and sticks his head between his legs He lifts Murakami up for a powerbomb, but instead drives him down into a form of shoulderbreaker! Murakami screams again, and the crowd "oooohs" in shock. Goto asks Murakami if he wishes to submit, but he refuses. Prince stands Murakami up, extends the arm, and applies the Crippler Crossface. Murakami lays on the mat in pain, but when Goto asks him, he still refuses to submit! The crowd starts to chant "Murakami! Murakami!", while Prince pulls back hard on the crossface. Murakami reaches out with his left arm, and somehow grabs the ropes! The crowd applauds loudly as Prince reluctantly breaks the hold...] AO: I thought it was all over for Murakami! He's taken so much punishment on his right shoulder, I was sure he would submit! TK: Tasogare no Prince has wrestled an excellant match. He knows Murakami's weak link, and he's focusing on it! [Prince backs off, and allows Murakami to get to his feet. He waits until Murakami nods his readiness to continue. Prince moves in, and Murakami catches him with a left handed chop to his throat. Prince stops short, and Murakami chops him across the chest. He kicks Prince in the gut, doubling him over, then double underhooks his arms, lifting him up, spinning around, then dropping down into a DDT! The crowd applauds, but Murakami doesn't cover. He sits up, holding his elbow in pain. When he does attempt a cover, Prince kicks out at two. Murakami slams his fist to the mat in frustration, then drags his opponent to his feet. He attempts an irish whip, but Prince reverses. Prince runs to the corner nearest him, and as Murakami comes off the ropes, springboard moonsaults over Murakami's head, landing behind him. Murakami had stopped short, hoping to catch Prince, and before he can turn around, Prince waistlocks him, and executes a German suplex, driving Murakami's shoulder onto the mat hard. Prince bridges back, and Goto drops to count. Though Murakami tries to kick out, he is unable to, and Prince gets the three count. The crowd applauds as the bell rings...] AO: Prince got him with the Twilight Suplex! TK: That move is so devestating! Nishi: The winner of this match, and NEW Welterweight champion... TASOGARE no PRINCE!!! [crowd applauds again] [Goto raises Prince's hand, and some women enter the ring, and start to hand Prince flowers. Jumbo Kobabayashi enters the ring, and hands Prince his trophy. Nishi enters the ring with the title belt, but Murakami takes the belt from him, and confronts Prince. The crowd quiets down as the two men face off, then Murakami places the belt around Prince's waist, and raises his arm in victory. The crowd erupts into applause, and Prince raises Murakami's left arm then embraces his opponent. Jumbo them takes Murakami, and leads him to the ropes, where the medical team waits to check out his arm. Prince gets up on the turnbuckle, and raises his arms to the crowd, and white rose petals fall from the rafters, enveloping the arena. The crowd gives Prince a standing ovation, and fireworks go off. The camera pans back to Kikuchi & Ono...] TK: What a night this has been! Tasogare no Prince is now the Welterweight champion! That was an incredible match! AO: Yukio Murakami gave his best, but with his injured arm, was not up to the task. I'm sure he will want a rematch, and I'm sure Prince will give it to him. TK: As am I. We are out of time. We hope you all enjoyed Sensou no Jidai. We will see you again soon on Tsunami. For Akira Ono, I am Tiger Kikuchi. Goodnight everyone! [The credits start to roll, with Tasogare no Prince in the ring, showing his title to the fans.
