Battlecade '97 Control Center Special

6 June 1997

There are two high back chairs with a large "V" shaped table in the middle of the set. The table has a large SJPW Logo on the front of it in Red Black and White. There are stacks of papers on top of the table. There is a video wall covering the entire back side of the set. Currently only a SJPW logo is shown on the screen. A caucasian man in his mid 20's walks in from the left. He's has red hair and is wearing blue jeans, a black T-Shirt with "SJPW" written in Red on it, and a black leather jacket. An asian man in his mid 30s with long black hair put into a pony tail and a goatee walks in from the right side. He's wearing a black suit with a white chinese shirt and black Oakley shades. They both sit in their respective chairs.] Eddie Rose: Hello Everyone, I'm Eddie Rose, and this is Akira Ono. Tonight, we take a special look at our first PPV, Battlecade '97. At Battlecade '97, we will see two champions crowned, as Shinji "Live" Hamada & Super Tengu vie for the Cruiserweight title, and 20 men step into two rings surrounded by barbed wire, with little landmine bomblets attatched to the wire, to decide who will be the Battlecade champion! Akira Ono: We shall also have the semi-finals of our woman's title tournament, as Baby Hotta battles "Heartbreaker" Robin Blaze, and Pizzazz takes on Terra! We will also see the finals of the NACW tag team tournament, as Mafioso battle the Space Warriors. ER: It's good to see other members of the GIWA showcasing their talent at our event. AO: Speaking of the GIWA Eddie-san, Mr. Yamashita has a special announcement concerning that. ER: Really? I didn't know that! AO: Once again, it is because you are not in "the loop". ER: Great. Let's start our rundown of Battlecade. ------------------------------------------------------------------- "Dark Angel" Dawn Rose vs "Lovely Angel" Mimi Honda ------------------------------------------------------------------- ER: Both women were eliminated from round one of the women's title tournament. Rose's defeat was considered by many to be a major upset by some, a fluke by others. AO: Both women have alot to prove, and it will be a hard fought match. I am going with Dawn Rose on this one for one simple reason: Victor Creed. This man also works with Steel Serpent, and we know how tough that guy is! ER: But Mimi has her "Lovely Legion" behind her as well. I also understand that her mentor, "Desperado" Ric Rivas, has sent someone to SJPW to watch her back. Who this person is, we have yet to discover. AO: Unless he is Derek Rage, or Little Joe, I doubt he will do much good against Victor Creed. We have some comments from "The Lovely Angel"... ------------------------------------------------------------------- [Taped 5/31/97] [Mimi Honda is dressed in a very nice business suit. She is leaving the Super Japan offices, she's holding her checkbook in her hand and is very upset.] MH:(Walking down the hall) Well I never, fined. I can't believe it. I was fined for what little Fifi did to me. Well Fifi you better give your soul to God because your @$$ is mine. MH: (walks to the stairs) If you can follow me go ahead. Fifi your 'hand of hate' was nothing compared to what I've been though. I was disowned by my family, they gave me a same remittance to never set foot in their house again. Only because they didn't want their little girl wrestling. MH: (stops walking and looks out a window) See that tower? My parents own that. They paid people in the business not to train me. So I grab a cedit card and headed to America. Changed my name and I met the greatest man ever, Desperado Ric Rivas, he took me in, trained me and paid him back by leaving. Now I found out he may have to quit wrestling because of illness. MH: (Starts down the stairs again):You never know where the fans are waiting for you. Life stinks, sometimes. But don't think that whatever happens in the ring really affects me outside. It's just a sport, Fifi. But it doesn't mean you get off the hook. I still hurt you. That's a bit insulting than a slap. [Stops on a landing does a roundhouse kick and then a spin kick] MH:(starts down) Nothing like a nice spin kick, huh? Dawn Rose, don't ever call me a loser. Just by the fact what I went though just so that I can wrestle makes me a winner. Just because you have a guy trying to help you don't mean diddy, I'm going to find a way to win. Because that is what winners do find a way to win. [Mimi reaches the door, opens it and finds 50 members for her fan club outside. She shuts the door.] MH: The light out in the darkness, my fans. Rose I don't care if you got sixty guys with you. Just tell him stay out of the match. I let me do what I have to do. Because Fifi you didn't beat me, your male slut did. Desperado told me on the phone I don't any man helping me. But you know, he got a point, but I got a plan. Hee, Hee. [Mimi opens the door and goes out to meet her fans.] ------------------------------------------------------------------- ER: What is this plan Mimi has? We'll find out soon enough I guess. Let us see our next match: ------------------------------------------------------------------- Jet Jaguar vs Takao Nakami ------------------------------------------------------------------- ER: Jet Jaguar is a newcomer to SJPW, and a rookie. He made an impressive debut, but now he faces a man second only to Super Tengu in the ego department: Takao Nakami. Nakami is riding high following last week's tag team victory with Tengu over Shinji Hamada and Rhiyoto Makada. Add in Sean O'Meara, his new trainer, and Nakami seems to hold all the cards. AO: I agree. Jet Jaguar holds promise, but Nakami is a formidable grappler for his size. He has suffered a minor setback when Hamada beat him in the cruiserweight tournament, but he went and found a new trainer, and now he is back on top of his game. Expect Nakami to take the win in this one. ER: Last night, we heard from Takao Nakami; tomorrow night we'll try to get some words from his opponent. AO: Our next match has been dubbed a "young lions" match: ------------------------------------------------------------------- "Black Tiger" J.T. Ruckus vs "Too Sexy" Lance Smith ------------------------------------------------------------------- ER: Ruckus has found direction and inspiration from Tiger Kikuchi, while Smith has turned from the fans, and taken the advice of his father. Ruckus appreciates Japan, while Smith accuses SJPW of bias against it's non-asian competitors. AO: Smith has allowed his father to poison his mind. Ruckus on the other hand, has come to understand my people more. Both have support from the fans, but Ruckus is more poplular since he became Kikuchi's student. ER: Both men will have their mentors at ringside. Who's presence will make the most difference? I go with Terry Smith. We saw what he did to Suicide during the Cruiserweight tournament. AO: Yes, and then we saw what he tried to do to Super Tengu. I was able to handle the braggart, so I know Kikuchi will be able to negate his attempts to interfere. We have comments from both wrestlers. First is J.T. Ruckus: ------------------------------------------------------------------- [Taped 5/31/97] (J.T. Ruckus is sitting in front of the television watching SJPW Control Center. He seems to be particularly interested in what is being said on the television. By the looks of his surrounding, he is in a hotel room. The room is very tidy. Ruckus is sitting on his bed while he watches the tape.) ER: Is J.T. Ruckus the most dangerous wrestler in SJPW now that he's spent the last few weeks learning all he could from Tiger Kikuchi, one of Japan's greatest wrestlers? At Battlecade '97, J.T. battles the hottest rookie in the fed, "Too Sexy" Lance Smith. This will be a classic matchup: Lance's aerial tactics against Ruckus' technical skill. With Terry Smith and Tiger Kikuchi at ringside, men who have fought in the past, this match could turn volatile in a flash. While Lance seems to be going the way of his father, and insinuating that american wrestlers are getting the shaft, Ruckus has become more involved in the japanese culture, and is widely supported by the fans. Besides Akira Hashimoto and Shinji Hamada, Ruckus is SJPW's top fan favorite. All ready sales of the new "Black Tiger" T-shirt are going through the roof! (Ruckus cocks an eyebrow as he hears the last comments. He sits up and turns the VCR off and addresses the camera.) J.T. Ruckus: At last I now know who my opponent will be at Battlecade. It seems that Mr. Rose has some facts mixed up. I, too, am a rookie to this sport even though it doesn't seem like it at times. I will give the devil his due, Smith is a gifted wrester and one of the best aerial wrestlers in Super Japan. It's too bad that he can't cut the apron strings from his precious daddy. I know Tiger Kikuchi and your father have had their battles, but you must concern yourself with me in the ring. What are you going to do with your aerial skills when I have you down on the mat tied up in the CAtskills Clutch, my friend. That's right. Nothing, but submit. The Black Tiger will hunt his prey down, and take home a victory. I will truly earn the respect of the Japanese fans at Battlecade. ------------------------------------------------------------------- ER: Ruckus is focused and ready for this match. I still feel Terry Smith being at ringside will give Lance the edge though. AO: I disagree; Kikuchi can handle him. Let us see what the Father and Son have to say: ------------------------------------------------------------------- [Taped 6/2/97] [Scene opens on Terry Smith and Lance Smith. Both are pictured wearing a "Gaijin...and DAMN PROUD!" T-Shirt... ] TS: Yeah... were gaijin's...adn damn proud of it too! Sure, Shinsaku Yamashita might screw us over all of the time.. but were not going to take his $#!+ anymore. My son is here to win... and i'm not going to let some Racist screw him over. LS: No... no one is going to screw me over. Ruckus... you will feel my wrath... the Smith family wrath. The skills that have been passed down from generation to generation... from my father, to me. But, J.T., you have to realize one thing. The only reason your where you are is because you got Tiger Kickuchi as your mentor. No.. he sure as hell is no Terry Smith, but the reason why he is such as good mentor is because he is a Japanese Man. If he wasn't japanese... you would've lost every match. In all of my matches.. the ref's count slow for me, but if i'm facing a Japanese Man, Sheesh! They count to three faster than most people count to one! TS: I'm sick of this... My son is sick of this... and a lot of others have to be sick of this! Lance comes out, put's on a great show for you all, and what happens? He gets screwed because he is born in Japan. This really aggrovates me, and has to aggrovate some of the other Gaijin's in this fed. LS: I came to this fed to be a winner... i came to show everyone that i had what it takes to succeed in wrestling... to prove that my father is the best damn trainer in the world! But i am always brought down by the Man... the Japanese Man. They've made sure none of the Gaijin's are in the Crusierweight Finals, and most of there special matches are occupied by Japanese men... can't they give us a fair shake? TS: No... they cannot. Well... i'm sick and tired of this... i'm going to chage this...and i don't give a damn if no one likes it! [Scene fades] ------------------------------------------------------------------- AO: These men are taking this too far. They are making a situation out of nothing. They claim bias against non-japanese wrestlers; this is simply an excuse to cover the fact that they lost some matches, and their pride refuses to let them accept this. So they blame us. ER: I understand Mr. Yamashita will be joining us later in the show to address this issue, as well as make some statements concerning SJPW, and our parent organization, the GIWA. AO: That is correct Eddie-San. After the Rukcus/Smith match, we will then shift to our second broadcast position as our guests from the EEF take over to handle the play by play of a match bewtween two EEF superstars, Sir Alfred Cartwright, and Skidmark Harris. ER: From there, we take control over at our position, and continue the show. The next match will be a tag team confrontation between: ------------------------------------------------------------------- The Toyota Brothers vs Keiji Asamiya & Kazuya Asakino ------------------------------------------------------------------- ER: You have to give the edge to the Toyota Brothers. They are a well oiled machine, and are undefeated in SJPW. AO: I agree, but Asamiya and Asakino are no pushover. Yes they fight amongst themselves, but they work well together. Still, the Toyota's don't have the friction between them that Asamiya and Asakino do, which is why they will prevail over them tomorrow night! ER: We have comments from Keiji Asamiya & Kazuya Asakino about their match tomorrow night.... ------------------------------------------------------------------- [Taped 6/4/97] [open camera on Keiji Asamiya, he stands in a pool of his own sweat and looks extremely peeved!!] Keiji:[muttering under his breath] Damn you Kazuya!! Where have you vanished to this time!! [Kazuya Asakino stumbles in, followed by a host of photographers and reporters] Kazuya: [drunk as al heck!!] Now lisshen all you guysh... me and [looks over at Keiji] m'cushin are gunna wreck thosh Toyota Bruthersh like they wash a cheap Honda Shivic!! Keiji: [hands over his face] not again, this is the 3 time this week he's done this!! Reporter#1: Is this how you always train for big matches Asamiya? [the whole press junket laughs] [Keiji picks up Kazuya by the collar of his shirt] Keiji: Must you heap such dishonor upon me? Have you no shame? WHY DO I TAG WITH YOU? Kazuya: [looks at Keiji and sobers up a little] BECAUSE EVERYONEELSE REFUSES TO BE YOUR PARTNER!! I'm the only pershun on this planet that shtand to be around you... and I barely can shtand that!! Keiji: [throws his arms up in the air in frustration] LESS THAN 3 DAYS TILL OUR BIGGEST MATCH YET AND YOU ARE NOT EVEN TRAINING!! [throws a chair at the press] GET OUT OF HERE YOU VERMIN!! [the press scramble out of the gym] Kazuya: [singing drunkedly] Dare da, dare da, dare da Sora no kanata ni odoru kage Shiroi tsubasa no Gatchaman Inochi wo kakete tobidaseba Kagaku Nimpo Hinotori da Keiji: [bitterly]Been to Shinjuku have you? Went to the Karoake bars singing anime songs again? WHY DO YOU PUT ME THROUGH THIS? HOW DO YOU EXPECT TO WIN AT BATTLECADE!! KAZUYA NO BAKA!! [fade out as Kazuya continues to sing the theme to 'Kagaku Ninja Tai Gatchaman'] ------------------------------------------------------------------- AO [chuckling]: I think this proves us both right Eddie-san. They have absolutely no focus! ER: You're right. Heaven help them both. We also have comments from their opponents, not just on this match, but also about Dragon Special and a certain woman named Terra.... ------------------------------------------------------------------- [Taped 6/5/97] (fade in on Kyu Kabuki with the $500,000 he won in a bag over his left shoulder. He's in the Japan AC offices, with Hakurana and the Toyota Brothers, but Hiroshima is training against some guy they found looking for a gas station. He's got a huge smile on his face, and begins the flash) Kyu: Well. Everyone said I wasn't good enough to even step into the ring with Chris D. Everyone said he's one of the best wrestlers in the world. Well, when I kicked his sorry Canadian ass last Tuesday, I proved that *I* am the better man! And, I loved doing it, because now, I have $500,000 to do as I want with it. But, you see, now, it's on to another challenge. A spiked nail death match. Well, I haven't fought in this kind of a match. But you know, this could be fun. When I put Chris D.'s head into that cage and made him bleed, I enjoyed it. When I slammed Chris D. into the cage, I enjoyed it. AND WHEN I PUT HIM THROUGH A TABLE, I ENJOYED IT! And at Battlecade, I'll enjoy slamming the man who cost me my Cruiserweight Title shot into a bed of nails! Dragon Special, your doom is near! Kensauke: As for Keiji Asamiya & Kazuya Asakino, you two seemingly can't even get along. How are you gonna do against a team that the experts are calling the next SJPW World Tag Team Champions? Huh? NOT VERY WELL! You see, team work is a must here in Japan. And if you don't have it, then, you're gonna find out the hardway what happens in handicap matches. (Kensauke sees what's going on in the ring, screams AC RUN IN, and charges the ring to help attack the guy) Hakurana: Terra. You made a HUGE mistake hitting my Kyu.... Kyu: I'm not your Kyu.... Hakurana: Oh..that's so sweet. Your playing hard to get. Kyu: I'm not playing hard...... Hakurana: Anyways. Terra, you hit him. Now, you've crossed the AC. OHHH...you called us Awfully Crappy. Good, even losers like that HCW punk Michael Kegonite can do that. But, the one thing is EVERYONE who has called us Awfully Crappy have gone down, and proven who is really Awfully Crappy. And, you'll be just like the rest Terra. Flat on your back....not that you normally aren't already! Kyu: The Japan version of the Annihilation Corporation.....you'd better hate us already, because if you don't, you'll learn the HARDWAY how to! (all 4 laugh as we fade to black) ------------------------------------------------------------------- ER: The Toyota Brothers are more than ready for this one! AO: And Kabuki seems prepared for his opponent in our next match: ------------------------------------------------------------------- Dragon Special vs Kyu Kabuki [Spiked Nail Deathmatch] ------------------------------------------------------------------- ER: Kabuki is a top contendor for the cruiserweight title, and many say he's also a top contendor for the heavyweight belt. Dragon Special is a former juniorheavyweight champion in another federation. He has also wrestled in extreme style matches before. I'd have to say the edge goes to him in this one. AO: I agree he has more experience in this type of match, but with the DQ rules suspended at the ppv, that gives the AC free reign to do as they please. I expect them to intercede on Kabuki's behalf, and that will give him the edge needed to win. ER: We all ready heard from Kabuki, so let's see what Dragon Special has to say about his match.... ------------------------------------------------------------------- [Taped 6/2/97] (Camera opens to Dragon Special. He is reading the card lineup for Battlecade `97. Suddenly he begins to smile and laugh when he reads what kind of match he is in...) Kyu Kabuki vs Dragon Special [Spiked Nail Deathmatch] (Ring is surrounded by wooden planks with long nails in them. Match is won when one wrestler is forced out of the ring, and onto the spikes, then cannot return to the ring to answer a 10 count) Dragon Special (DS): Kabuki. You're entering my territory now. The Death Match. The match where I, Dragon Special, won the SIW Jr. Heavyweight Title. The death match. Where I, Dragon Special, destroyed all of my enemies and made them respect my athletic ability. Respect isn't given, it's earned and Kabuki, after I beat the living daylights out of you, you're going to earn a whole LOT of respect! I'm bringing my "friend" with me to the ring - so, in case you get out of hand, my "friend" will be there to stop that. My "friend" will help me win, as he OR she has in other promotions and in other stadiums. So, Kabuki, I've already got your room at the hospital reserved for you - maybe you'll share a hospital room with Myst..who knows..but, Kabuki, you've messed with the wrong wrestler and now you're going to PAY!!! See you at Battlecade, loser! HAHAHAHAHAHAHA!! (Camera fades to black as Dragon Special continues laughing...) ------------------------------------------------------------------- ER: It seems that Dragon Special has anticipated the AC's interference, and has gotten help to neutralize this. AO: If that is the case, then Dragon Special has the advantage. This is his kind of match. Kabuki had better be ready, because blood will flow!
