Control Center (7 May 1997)

6-7 May 1997

There are two high back chairs with a large "V" shaped table in the middle of the set. The table has a large SJPW Logo on the front of it in Red Black and White. There are stacks of papers on top of the table. There is a video wall covering the entire back side of the set. Currently only a SJPW logo is shown on the screen. A caucasian man in his mid 20's walks in from the left. He's has red hair and is wearing blue jeans, a black T-Shirt with "SJPW" written in Red on it, and a black leather jacket. An asian man in his mid 30s with long black hair put into a pony tail and a goatee walks in from the right side. He's wearing a black suit with a white chinese shirt and black Oakley shades. They both sit in their respective chairs.] Eddie Rose: Hello Everyone, I'm Eddie Rose, and this is Akira Ono. Tonight on the Control Center, we recap this past weekend's Ring Wars, and rundown the lineup for the upcoming Ring Wars this weekend. Akira Ono: We had some incredible matches this weekend! Many surpased the first weeks outstanding show. ER: That's correct Akira. We saw some debuts this past weekend, as SJPW keeps signing new superstars to compete here in Japan. Our first match saw "Tsunami" Rhiyoto Makada bounce back from his loss to Takao Nakami, and defeat the veteran Sho Nakatoni AO: He got lucky. Nakatoni had a bad night. He promises me that next time he faces Makada, he will defeat him! ER: I'm sure we all look forward to that matchup. Our second match saw the mysterious newcomer, Omega, defeat another veteran, Takashi Fujimoto. This match was pretty wild, and spilled into the crowd at least twice! AO: But in the end, Omega defeated the veteran with a cradle frankensteiner. Then we had the debut of the nutcase, Patty Babes Carter! ER: Akira! He's not a nutcase. He's simply going through some problems right now. AO: Tell that to Gregor Davinsky, who he beat senseless by ramming his head into the turnbuckle while trapped in the Baby Face Destroyer! ER: Yes Carter lost control, but Suicide's presence had something to do with it. Anyway, we were given official word shortly after Ring Wars that Carter is taking some time off to get his head together. AO: You mean he is in a mental institution? ER: basically, yes. We'll have progress reports as they become available. I'm sure I speak for everyone here at the Control Center when I say we all wish Patty Babes Carter a speedy recovery, so he can once again grace the Tokyo Dome, and give the SJPW fans a chance to see him wrestle again. AO: Speak for yourself! I want him to stay crazy, and hurt more wrestlers! I like him better this way! ER: Your're almost as bad as Super Tengu! AO: I'll take that as a compliment! The next match was the first of 3 first round matchups in the cruiserweight tournament. Shinya Sasaki and Ace Killcannon faced off, and it was a classic matchup! ER: Let's see some clips from this matchup (both men turn to the screen) [The screen shows scenes of Shinya Sasaki doing a moonsault somersault senton splash from the top rope to the floor on Killcannon; Killcannon executing a twisting flipsplash legdrop from the top rope on the apron to the floor on Sasaki; Sasaki lifitng Killcannon high in a vertical suplex position, then dropping him straight down into a tiger bomb; Killcannon executing a corkscrew senton splash on Sasaki. The screen goes blank, and both men turn to the camera] ER: That was one hell of a match! AO: One of the best on the card. The next matchup had Kyu Kabuki of the Japanese AC defeat newcomer Jumbii by almost breaking his arm. ER: The newcomer put up a hell of a fight, but in the end, the damage to his arm was just too much, and Kabuki got the submission with the Sasake Power Special. And after his comments on Trash Talk, it seems that Kabuki has some kind of grudge for Akira Hashimoto. AO: Yes, which could be an unwise move. I like Kabuki and the Toyota Brothers; they remind me of me when I was active. But Akira Hashimoto is considered one of the best in all of Japan. I don't know what is going on in America, but here in SJPW, that would be one hell of a match! ER: It definitely would be. The third of the first round matchups this past weekend saw another japanese legend, Tiger Kikuchi, wrestler the rookie J.T. Ruckus to a draw. let's see some highlights (both men turn to the screen) [The screen shows scenes of Ruckus countering a hiptoss with a bulldog; Kikuchi nailing Ruckus with a running powerbomb; Kikuchi hitting the Tiger Driver on Ruckus; Ruckus putting the JT Tieup on Kikuchi. The screen goes blank, and both men turn to the camera] ER: A great matchup, and the start of a new alliance Akira. AO: That's correct Eddie-san. Ruckus asked Kikuchi to help him become the best, and Kikuchi agreed. Both men have withdrawn themselves temporarily from competition, so it will be interesting to see what Ruckus learns from Kikuchi while he is away. With the right guidance, Ruckus could come back stronger than ever! ER: It will be interesting to see how this turns out. The next matchup saw the Toyota Brothers totally maul former All Japan tag team champions the Golden Boys. Jerry & Keith Golden are seasoned veterans, and have held numerous titles over the course of their career. But last saturday night, they wrestled like a pair of jobbers against the Toyota Brothers. AO: It was definitely a one-sided affair! I have never seen the Golden Boys man-handled like they were that time! ER: Rumor around the Control Center is that a return match is being requested by the Golden Boys, once they are cleared to return to the ring. For now, Jerry Golden remains in the hospital after that vicious AC Bomb he took. AO: I hope next time, they put up a better fight. That match just wasn't like them. Now we get to talk about the main event! ER: That's right Akira. Two legends of our sport made their SJPW debuts this past saturday. "The Perfectionist" Nik Nyce, a former 3 time BAHW TV champion, and former US champion in both the AWA and NWA took on former 3 time World champion, Akira Hashimoto. This match took on more importance as the day before, Hashimoto took the TAEWF TV title, and dumped it in the trash! He was then named by Antonio Inoki as his successor. AO: It was an incredible turn of events that led up to this matchup. Let us view highlights of this incredible matchup (Both men turn to the screen) [From SJPW Ring Wars 5/3/97] AO: Akira locks on another armbar..hammerlock slam. Akira now stomping on Nyce's arm! He's trying to take out his arm! Akira has him up..He rams Nyc shoulder first to the corner..two..three..four..five..six! He goes for it again, but Nyce Blocks it, and rams Akira headfirst to the corner. He locks him with a reverse headlock..inverted DDT! (fans applaud) Good counter by Nyce. (scene changes) ER: Hashimoto now elbowing Nyce in his gut...Nyce lets go of the sleeper...Hashimoto goes to the ropes...Nyce grabs him for a suplex, but Hashimoto blocks..He's locking himself under Nyce's arm...Tazplex by Hashimoto dumps Nyce on his arm! (Fans applaud) (scene changes) ER: Nyce goes to the ropes, and drops a Ric Flair like kneedrop to Akira's head, rolling to his feet. He sits Akira up, gets behind him...Nyce flips over Akira, and snaps his upper body forwar by the neck! Akira is holding his neck again! (scene changes) AO: He is honorable man. Nyce is off the ropes, and Akira is on him, and kneelifts his gut..two..three..four! Akira now with an open hand shot to the head! He's signalling the crowd...He's going for the Kataha-jime! ER: He's locking it one Nyce! He has him..He's going to wrap his legs around Nyce's body...OH MY GOD! Nyce dropped down, and caught him with the "Nyce Finisher"! (fans oooh, then appluad) Both men are down in the ring! What's the ref doing? (The scenes end, and both men turn back to the camera) ER: The match ended in controversy, as we couldn't see why the ref counted the pinfall. On closer examination of the tapes, and enlargements, we discovered that Hashimoto had his arm draped over Nyce, which is why the ref counted the pinfall. However, what we saw that the ref missed was that one of Nyce's shoulders was not firmly on the mat, as Hashimoto's other arm was under it. Nik Nyce sent us this tape of comments, which he says will shed light on the whole situation..... (both men turn to the screen) [Taped May 5, 1997] (Nik Nyce is in the SJPW locker room after the match. He looks a little exhausted but he has a little smile on his face) NN: You know I know that the rules over here are a little different but I didn't know when I pin someone for the 1-2-3, that I LOSE THE MATCH! NN: You know Hashimoto, you thought you were just going to come into that match and win it in five minutes, well as far as I am concerned I was robbed. Its real simple actually. I want another match with you and I want a different ref. (Nik opens up one of the lockers and see's all kinds of Hashimotto posters,shirts,and other SJPW merchandise) NN: You see this locker, this locker is the ref's locker. This really makes me mad and making The "Perfectionist" Nik Nyce is a bad thing. I came to this league because I was told that it wasn't like one of these leagues where you have to watch your back all during the match and everything is on the up and up. Well looks like this is just like everywhere else. NN: Hashimoto, your old,washed up, and a cheater. I will admit that I have cheated and will cheat in many matches, but Hashimoto comes over here acting like a GOD and everyone thinks he can do no wrong. (Nik points to the locker) NN: Well as we can see, since your half the man you used to be, you have to pay off a ref and a very stupid ref to make me get the lose. NN: Hashimoto atleast if your going to cheat me you could do a good job of it. You had this ref give you the 1-2-3 infront of the sold out crowd and TV and still the stupid ref said you were the winner. NN: Hashimoto, I'm not going to jump you from behind. I'll leave that stuff in The States where I still wrestle and unlike you I'm in my prime but Hashimoto if you think your going to just forget about me then you'll have a big surprise very soon. (Nik Nyce walks out of the lockeroom in disgust and the screen fades; both men turn back to the camera) AO: How dare he accuse an SJPW official of favoritism! ER: Calm down Akira. Nik Nyce did send a copy of this tape to the SJPW championship committee as well as to us. Shinsaku Yamashita, CEO of SJPW, has an official statement (holds up a piece of paper). "As CEO of SJPW, I will not tolerate any acts of favoritism on the part of any referees. I promise to fully investigate this matter, and if so warranted, will take actions against said referee. However, If Mr. Nyce, or any other competitor in SJPW has problems with the officiating here, they need to bring it to my attention, and not in a public forum. Furthermore, it is my decision that Nik Nyce and Akira Hashimoto shall have a rematch at the Battlecade Pay Per View, with a referee hand picked by me for his unbiasness. I will brook no further public complaints on this matter, subject to fines, and if necessary, suspension. This is not an American federation, where every decadent sin can be exposed to the masses for ratings points. Shinsaku Yamashita, CEO, SJPW" AO: Good for Yamashita! And we get to have a rematch between Hashimoto and Nyce! Battlecade will be an incredible ppv! ER: True. Now we take a look at the upcoming Ring Wars. Let's look at the lineup for this weekend (both men look at the screen) [SJPW Ring Wars 5/10/97] SJPW Cruiserweight tournament, round one Takao Nakami vs Shinji "Live" Hamada (main event) Jiraiya vs Omega Super Tengu vs Masa Kokura "Tsunami" Rhiyoto Makada vs "Sweet" Stan Jackson Suicide vs "Too Sexy" Lance Smith Kensuki Mishawa vs Frankie "Thumper" Douglas (j) "Japeater" Jarred Jones vs Sho Nakatoni (j) Sung "The Catman" Kim vs Takashi Fujimoto (j) (both men turn back to the camera) ER: We have comments from two of the participants in the cruiserweight tournament: "Sweet" Stan Jackson, and Masa Kokura, who will introduce the Tokyo Express' new manager, and another newcomer to SJPW. AO: Both men have tough challenges this weekend: Jackson faces Makada, and Kokura faces the one, the only, Super Tengu! ER: Definitely a challenge for anyone! Let's see what these men have to say (both men turn to the screen) [Taped May 7, 1997] (Deja-Vu are in the gym working out with their manager, "Fabulous" Frankie Funk. "Jaded" Jake Jones is doing bench presses as "Sweet" Stan Jackson spots him.) Funk: Hey boys...look! It's one of those SJPW camera men. Well, I suppose you want a little something to put on that trash talk show of yours, eh? (camera nods) Ok, well then, "Tsunami" Rhiyoto Makada, this weekend at Ring Wars, in the cruiserweight tournament, you will get a chance to have a brush with greatness first hand when you take on "Sweet" Stan Jackson!! And I can guarentee you that my man will be poised and ready for action! Jackson: That's right Makada. I've been watching the films on you, and I must admit, you are impressive and you will not be taken lightly. This weekend at Ring Wars, you fans are in for a real treat when you get to see the debut of "Sweetness"! Jones: And the next weekend after that, you get to see both of us in action together, as Deja-Vu!!! And we are getting prepped for that match as we speak now! Funk: That's right...SJPW....we came here for a reason, to be successful and to win some gold and we're planning on getting those goal accomplished REAL soon!!! (the screen goes blank for a moment, then starts up again) [Taped May 7, 1997] (The SJPW camera crew with Sharon Akiyama and several others from the Japanese Press were invited to Masa Kokura's house as they are having a news conference to introduce their new manager and new stable mate...) Masa: Well it's nice to see you again Sharon... SA: Pleasure's all mine Mr. Kokura... Masa: Please call me Masa... SA: OK, Masa... You've called all of us here for a reason what news do you have for us? Masa: Riki and myself would like to introduce our new manager Ryu Taka and our new stablemate "The Modern Day Samurai" Hiro Kanemoto... (Taka and Kanemoto both enter the room as several flashbulbs go off) RT: Greetings and salutations... I am Ryu Taka the new manager of the Tokyo Express and Hiro Kanemoto... I had seen the Tokyo Express wrestle Badd Intentions at Ring Wars to a time limit draw, and I said to myself these boys are talented the only thing they are missing is a guiding light... I got in touch with Masa and Riki and told them what I could do for them and they agreed to let me manage them... Reporter: Do you think they have what it takes to someday be the SJPW Tag Team Champions? RT: Of course I do... They are one of the most talented teams in the world... SA: Do you think with your help they will beat Badd Intentions when they have a rematch? RT: Yes, I do... They almost had Badd Intentions beat if they had a few more minutes they would have won... Reporter: So, you're saying that they are better then Badd Intentions? RT: Yes, they are better... Those men were bigger and stronger then Masa and Riki but they couldn't put them away... SA: Masa this question is for you... You have a match up coming against Super Tengu what are your thoughts about the match? MK: It'll be tough no doubt about it... I've been training hard and planning out my strategy... Ryu has helped me alot with the planning of my strategy... He is a great mentor... Reporter: Mr. Taka what about your new wrestler "The Modern Day Samurai"? What can you tell us about him? RT: Why don't you just ask him yourself? Reporter: OK, Mr. Kanemoto what are you thoughts about being in the SJPW? HK: It is an honor and a pleasure to be in a league with so many great competators... It's a tough league but I plan on wearing the gold very shortly... Reporter: You sound a bit cocky you might even say arrogant? HK: I'm not arrogant or cocky I'm just confident in my ability... I know what I can do and I know my ability will lead me to the gold... SA: Riki what do you think about all of this? RK: I like the whole idea of having a manager... Ryu will guide us all the way to the tag straps... We're honored that he approached us and asked us if he could manage us... That made Masa and myself feel very good... Like I said before it is an honor... RT: (Turns around to model his jacket on the back there is a picture of the Japanese flag and in the middle of the sun there is an R and an S embroidered on it...) SA: What does the R and S stand for? RT: Rising Sons... My stable will be known as the Rising Sons... On that note we would like to thank you for coming here today now if you don't mind we have some planning to do... Once again thank you... (The men all leave at the same time but before Masa leaves he blows a kiss to Sharon, which she pretends to catch and then blows one back to him... Fade to black...both men turn back to the camera) ER: It looks like both men are prepared to face their opponents this coming weekend. AO: Maybe, but Masa Kokura will have a major task ahead of him when he faces Super Tengu. ER: What about the Tokyo Express now having a manager? Will it help them in further matches? AO: It could. I hope so, since Badd Intentions have insulted my country! ER: And what do you think of Hiro Kanemoto? AO: I'm not sure. I'll reserve judgement until I see him wrestle. ER: Sounds good. That's all we have for tonight. For Akira Ono, I'm Eddie Rose. I'll see you again at the next Trash Talk!
