Control Center

There are two high back chairs with a large "V" shaped table in the middle of the set. The table has a large SJPW Logo on the front of it in Red Black and White. There are stacks of papers on top of the table. There is a video wall covering the entire back side of the set. Currently only a SJPW logo is shown on the screen. A caucasian man in his mid 20's walks in from the left. He's has red hair and is wearing blue jeans, a black T-Shirt with "SJPW" written in Red on it, and a black leather jacket. An asian man in his mid 30s with long black hair put into a pony tail and a goatee walks in from the right side. He's wearing a black suit with a white chinese shirt and black Oakley shades. They both sit in their respective chairs.] Eddie Rose: Hello Everyone, I'm Eddie Rose, and this is Akira Ono. Tonight on the Control Center, we recap this past weekend's Ring Wars, and rundown the lineup for the upcoming Ring Wars this weekend. Akira Ono: And we cannot forget that SJPW is now a month old! ER: That's correct Akira. This past Ring Wars was our 4th weekly show, making us a month old. This card was mired with some problems, due to the fact that our mini-division staff were out of touch, and the wrestlers scheduled no-showed. We also lost Deja-Vu a few days before the card, and had to replace them. But as always, SJPW prevailed, and we reorganized the card. AO: And gave the fans one great main event! ER: That's an understatement. Our first match saw Steel Serpent defeat Ryu Fujimoto, younger brother of Takashi Fujimoto, a former champion. Afterwards, Steel Serpent challenged Sung Kim, just as Akira Ono predicted. Sung Kim heard about the challenge, and made these comments (both men turn to the screen) ------------------------------------------------------------------------------- [Taped 5/19/97] "Catman" Sung Kim: Steel Serpent, I'm ready for you whenever the SJPW officials want to sign this match. Don't worry about my wrestling ability Steel Serpent, worry about your own. I know that I have the ability to defeat you in the center of the ring. You say you'll take me on anywhere? Well let's do it at Battlecade 97! When we step in the ring, you will be destroyed! DESTROYED! Do you understand that term Serpent? Do you? I want a match for next weeks Ring Wars so that I can start getting ready for Steel Serpent. It's not going to be easy, it's going to be the fight of my life, but he'll still be DESTROYED! -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- AO: I have official word that these two men will meet at Battlecade 97! It is the perfect place for these two martial artists to combat each other! ER: It certainly does. Our second match saw Babby Hotta defeat Kyoko Shibata in after an intense battle. Baby Hotta recorded these comments for us recently (both men turn to the screen) -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- [Taped 5/20/97] (Camera focuses in on a playground. Seated on a swingset, mobbed by children, is BABY HOTTA. She wears a bonnet on her head with little animal ears and red long-johns. A group of kids sit cross-legged in front of her, as if she is telling a story...) Baby Hotta: ...and then that wicked Kyoko-girly was sent straight to Hell by the beautiful princess, and everyone lived happily ever after. Kid #7: Tell us another story, Miss Baby!! Baby Hotta: Welllll... (the kids all start yelling and begging, some pull at her leg) Baby Hotta: Okay! Kid #4: Tell us about that nasty Dawn Rose! Baby Hotta: I don't have a story about her, but I do have a rhyme. Would you like to hear it? Kid #4: Yes, please! Baby Hotta: Okay, here goes... Ugly girly Dawnie Rose Couldn't beat Baby even if Hell froze Ugly face, ugly hair, ugly body, ugly clothes Wouldn't make a difference if I broke her nose (The kids cheer) Baby Hotta: Oh, you liked that? Thank you. Kid #3: My dad says that Mimi Honda wants to fight you! My dad says that she's gonna beat you! Baby Hotta: Oh he did, did he? Kid #3: Yep! Baby Hotta: And do you think she will? All the kids: NOOOOOO!!! Baby Hotta: You tell your daddy that Princess Baby will clip his Lovely Angel's wings and maybe, MAYBE, if he's nice enough, I'll put them under his pillow while he's asleep. (The kids look at each other while Baby laughs to herself) Baby Hotta: Okay, beautiful children, your princess must go now. I'll see you all tommorrow, until then don't talk to strangers...especially Mimi Honda. -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- ER: Mimi Honda is one of our newest competitors, and it seems Baby Hotta wants a piece of her! AO: Unless Baby Hotta can focus more, it won't matter. Mimi will destroy her. She acts like spoiled little girl! ER: On that we can both agree! The next match saw "Burning Cherry Blossom" Rekka Sakura defeat Sumie Odaka, in a wild match. Both competitors gave it their all, but in the end, Rekka prevailed. We caught Rekka while working out the other day, and recorded this for the fans (both men turn to the screen) -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- [Taped 5/21/97] [open camera on a tiny gym. One would expect a wrestler of Rekka Sakura's stature to have the most modern gym at her disposal but all that lies here is a set of mats on the floor, a sandbag hangs from a chain and a speed bag dangles not very far from there. The closest thing to modern about this gym is the shinning bicycle that sits in the corner. On the mat doing situps is Rekka Sakura with her trainer Yukio Otonashi. Rekka looks spent as the beads of sweat drop from her head and hit the mat] Yukio: [intense] C'MON REKKA!! YOU WANT THAT TITLE DON'T YOU? WELL IT'LL TAKE EFFORT AND SACRIFICE TO GET IT!! THE QUESTION IS REKKA, WHAT ARE YOU WILLING TO SACRIFICE? Rekka Sakura: [tires] M-my body!! EVERY OUNCE OF MY BODY!! [finishes her last sit-up and kip-ups. She immeadiately heads for the sand bag and begins to strike it with a series of kicks and palm thrusts] Yukio: [angrily] YOU THINK THAT'S ALL IT WILL TAKE? ARE YOU STUPID? WHY AM I BOTHERING WITH YOU? IS THIS TRULY YOUR BEST? Rekka Sakura: [continuing to strike the sandbag] NO!! I CAN DO BETTER!! I AM WORTHY TO BE CHAMPION!! [the strikes get feircer with each blow] IT MATTERS NOT WHO MY OPPONENT IS, "THE BURNING CHERRY BLOSSOM" SHALL... Yukio: [cuts Rekka off by Slapping her] NO!! FORGET THIS "BURNING CHERRY BLOSSOM" CRAP!! [grabs Rekka by collar of her sweatshirt and hisses evilly] Do you think I took you under my wing because of your marketability? Are you that stupid? You've had limited success before but not REAL success because of your lack of dedication!! If you want me to train you to be THE BEST then stop acting like a whinning little bitch AND TRAIN AS I TELL YOU!! [let's go of Rekka and shoves her to the mat] Rekaa Sakura: [shocked] But... Yukio I am dedicated!! I'm giving you everything I have!! Yukio: [reaches over and Gives Rekka a HARD slap] THEN GET OUT!! YOU ARE NOT THE COMPETITOR I BELIEVED YOU TO BE!! BE GONE FROM MY SIGHT!! [Rekka slowly rises to her feet, grabs her gym bag and walks to the door, she looks back to Yukio who refuses to acknowledge her] Rekka Sakura: [sadly] You are a hard woman Yukio, I returned to Tokyo in the hopes to fulfill my dream and win the women's title. But now you, mu one hope I have to perfect my skills cast me out... [she drops her gym bag defiantly and looks with tears staining her cheeks] I AM DEDICATED!! YOU CAST ME OUT AND YOU THROW AWAY THE GREATEST POTENTIAL TO EVER ENTER YOUR HUMBLE GYM!! [she hangs her head in shame] I commit myself to your training regimen... I am your clay to mold into the perfect wrestler [fade out as Yukio smiles triumphantly] -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- ER: Rekka is constantly pushing herself. She really wants the SJPW woman's title bad! AO: Yes, and with such dedication, she could be hard to beat. However, too much training could be detrimental. ER: The next match pitted newcomer Jet Jaguar against the Tsutomu Kobayashi, son of a man you know VERY well. AO: Jumbo Kobayashi was one of the all time greats here in Japan. I fought him many times, and we teamed together may times. His son is a good wrestler, and with more experience, should be a champion like his father was. ER: Maybe, but he didn't do too well against Jet Jaguar. he put up a game fight, but in the end, Jaguar prevailed. AO: The next match was part of our Cruiserweight tournament. Kyu Kabuki, a member of the Japanese AC, made his return to the Tokyo Dome after the insidious attack on him and the Toyota Brothers by Chris D and the rest of the AC. Omega gave him a run for his money, but Kabuki prevailed with some incredible power moves, stunning Omega long enough for Kabuki to pin him with a side roll cradle. ER: It was impressive. The second Cruiserweight tournament match saw Ace Killcannon and Shinji "Live" Hamada, two former friends and tag team partners, face each other. Last week, Ace and Badd Intentions jumped Shinji prior to his match with Takao Nakami, but Shinji took everything Nakami had, and defeated him. This match was a classic, and if it weren't for the caliber of the 8 men in the main event, this match would have been the main event for the evening. AO: But it almost ended early for both men, as Super Tengu appeared out of the crowd, and claimed he had found money Shinji used to bribe Killcannon to lose to him. Our CEO came to ringside, and almost dq'd both men. Instead, he ordered an investigation of Shinji and Killcannon, but let the match continue. ER: At that point, Shinji had the match close to won, but while he argued with Tengu and Mr Yamashita, Ace had time to recover. Ace caught Shinji by surprise when the match restarted. Killcannon then took it to Hamada, and hit him with the Skytwister Press. As an added insult, Ace threw Shinji to floor, and Super Tengu showed up to insult Shinji. AO: While Shinji was distracted by Tengu, Killcannon was going for a plancha to the outside, but for reasons yet to be explained, Tengu pushed Hamada out of the way, and Ace busted his head open when he hit the railing. Hamada then argued with Tengu for interfering, while Tengu berated Shinji for wasting time, when he could be defeating Ace. Hamada then went crazy of Ace, and it looked like he may have been trying to end his career, if not his life. ER: At this point, Master Chono came to ringside, and tried to talk Hamada out of this course of action. While the two argued, Ace once again had time to recover, and he dropkicked Hamada into his mentor, causing Master Chono to hit the railing, and be taken away by medical personel. Ace took it to Hamada again, and was going for his new finisher, "The Kill", which is a Dragon Superplex, when Hamada miraculously used his legs to hold onto the ropes. AO: Hamada held on for dear life, and when he sensed Killcannon was off balance, he let go of the ropes, and caused the both of them to crash to the mat, where Killcannon landed on his head. To add insult to injury, Hamada then locked on a full nelson on the mat, along with body scissors, and before he could injure Killcannon seriously, the referee ruled Ace unable to continue due to loss of blood. So Hamada got a measure of revenge, and he advances to the next round of the tournament ER: We recently taped a press conference, where Shinji Hamada was asked about these allegations, as well as the Cruiserweight tournament (both men turn to the screen) -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- [Taped 5/19/97] [int: The conference room in SJPW Headquarters. The room is filled with reporters. All are talking among themselves, but not loudly enough that any one conversation can be made out. From the side entrance, SHINJI HAMADA enters. He is wearing jeans, a SJPW t-shirt, and a dark blue sport jacket. The reporters anxiously begin shouting questions to him as he takes his spot in front of the podium.] SHINJI: One at a time please. You... in the back. Reporter #1: Shinji, how does it feel having defeated Ace Kilcannon in the Cruiserweight tournament? SHINJI: It was a tainted victory. Kilcannon has always been a tough opponent, even when we were just sparring partners. I don't know what would have happened if Super Tengu hadn't pushed me out of the way when Kilcannon went for that dive, so I will be petitioning for a rematch against Ace Kilcannon at the earliest possible time. Reporter #2: Why DID Super Tengu help you? SHINJI: You'd have to ask him. My assumption is that he wants to face me in the finals of the Cruiserweight tournament. Reporter #3: What's Master Chono's condition? SHINJI: Master Chono is fine. He recieved a nasty bump, but he is a strong man. He is saddened by the way Ace has changed, but he accepts that everyone must walk their own path. Reporter #4: What about the money Super Tengu found in your locker? Are you bribing your opponents to take dives? [SHINJI looks at the reporter angrilly] SHINJI: How DARE you! From the moment Super Japan Pro-Wrestling opened it's doors, I have devoted my career to bringing honor and prestige to not only the federation but to my homeland of Japan. I have promised to stop people like Ace Kilcannon and his RoT compatriots, as well as the depravity of people like Super Tengu. Now he makes these wild accusations of my bribing people and you are ready to believe scum like HIM? [SHINJI glares at the reporter] NO, I do NOT bribe my opponents. I beat them because I am the better man. And when I finally meet Super Tengu in the ring and I drive him through the mat with the Live Crush Bomb I will pin him in the center of the ring 1-2-3. Then afterwards you can ask HIM how much money it cost to make HIM throw the match, becuase I will GUARANTEE I will beat Super Tengu and become the first SJPW Cruiserweight Champion. [Reporters continue to throw out questions, but Shinji Hamada turns and walks from the room.] -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- AO: As you can see, these allegations have made Hamada very upset. Even I find Tengu's accusations hard to swallow, but I still feel Tengu is the better wrestler. However, Shinji is an honorable man, and that is why I doubt that he would bribe someone. Especially Killcannon. These two men HATE each other, and no amount of money can change that. Then again, I could be wrong! ER: Two-face. Speaking of Ace Killcannon, we caught him in the locker room, just before Badd Intentions went to the ring for their matchup (both men turn to the screen) -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- [Taped 5/17/97] [Fade in to the Reign of Terror locker room, after the just finished Ring Wars. There is a trail of blood leading from the doorway to the bathroom. After a few moments Ace Kilcannon comes out with a blood soaked towel around is neck and blood still streaming down his face, in his blonde hair, and on his chest. Ace walks over to some of the locker room benches and sits down. As he looks up at the camera, the locker room door whips open and in come Badd Intentions with Donny "Ice" Berg. They are are angry, yet happy about their win in the 8-man tag-team match.] The Big Chill: Yeah!! We killed those damn nips!! Nick Patera: Damn right, and we didn't even need those other losers...screw 'em! [Donny "Ice" Berg notices Ace sitting on the bench and bleeding.] Donny "Ice" Berg: Guys, quiet down....Ace is hurt. The Big Chill: Damn...Ace, you... [Ace waves his hand in the air and The Big Chill stops in mid-sentence. Ace calls Berg over to him, whispers in his ear, and Berg nods the whole time Ace is speaking. Ace finishes and Berg turns to Badd Intentions.] Donny "Ice" Berg: Let's go, boys, we gotta couple of slant-eyed bastards to mangle... [Patera and Chill both pounds their fists into open hands and then storm out of the locker room, with Berg close behind. After they're gone, Ace looks back at the camera.] Ace Kilcannon: "Shinji....you KNOW that this is not over by far, my old friend...I never gave up...you KNOW that I don't know the meaning of quit...I busted my head on that guardrail and lost a lot of blood, son...that's why I passed out....it wasn't because of that sissy ass submission hold you slapped on me...no way in hell...I have not and will not ever say the words I quit in a professional wrestling match...that is the bottom line and you know it. For along time I have had to live in YOUR damn shadow, Shinji, you STOLE my glory back when we were partners and now you have cost me the chance at the coveted SJPW Cruiserweight title...the belt that I WANT and NEED so badly. I'll tell you one thing, boy, and that's that I will make sure that gold NEVER reaches your waist..that's a guarantee. I WILL plot out and seek my revenge upon you Shinji....and you never know when I may just pop up and beat the living hell outta you, son, because these days I do what I want and when I want to...that's the power of being in the Reign of Terror...we are THE alliance in wrestling not only here in Nipville, but in the States and all over the world...and that's a FACT. And as far as us being a nWo rip-off....nothing could be further from the truth. You see, we're not on the outside, trying to get in and take over...we're already on the inside...it's just a matter of time before we hold the gold that belongs to us. Sninji, tonight you got a HOLLOW victory over me...savor it, because it will NEVER happen again. We will meet again, whether it be on the street, in a bar, or in the ring, I really don't give a damn. I will take your discolored, slant-eyed, nip body and twist it into ways that you never thought were possible...then...hm...then I'm gonna drop you right on your flat head and claim my revenge upon you. You STOLE my glory, Shinji, and now you'll be lucky to walk out of this with your LIFE!" [Ace takes the blood soaked towel and tosses it across the room. He sighs heavily and looks back up at the camera.] Ace Kilcannon: "And you, Super Tengu, I had NO beef with you, but now you've done stepped foot into something you're not ready for. When you knocked Hamada out of the way of my plancha and I busted my head open on the railing, you sealed your fate, Tengu. Make sure you have your will in order, because myself and Badd Intentions don't take kindly to others sticking their noses into Reign of Terror business. You can come up with all the childish names and insults you want, but it won't help you when it comes down to stepping in the ring, Tengu. You sure are a badass when it comes to flapping your gums and making jackass out of yourself, but can you hang with the big boys in the ring? Namely me and the RoT? I SERIOUSLY doubt it. Just remember, when I drop you on your skull and everything goes white...you brought this upon yourself, Tengu...you brought it ALL upon yourself." [Ace wipes his face with his right hand and it's covered in blood. He smears it on the lens, leaving a bloody handprint on it.] Ace Kilcannon: "Hamada...Tengu...and anyone else...prepare to be ACED!" [Fade to black.] -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- ER: This man is on a mission, and instead of one man, he now has two men he targets. Super Tengu did something he may soon regret, all for the chance to beat Hamada in the finals of the Crusierweight tournament. AO: I don't like the Rot at all, but Ace is an accomplished wrestler, and he does have the ability to beat Hamada, and maybe beat Tengu. Watching this develop of the next few weeks will be very interesting! ER: It definitely will. That brings us to our main event. An 8 man tag match pitting Kenriki Ogami, Survival & The Tokyo Express on one side, and Elijah, "The Perfectionist" Nik Nyce & Badd Intentions on the other. Before the bell even rang, the match degenerated into a brawl, with Badd Intentions and the Express battling all the way backstage, Elijah and Survival slugging it out at ringside, while Ogami and Nyce actually tried to wrestle in the ring! AO: What made this worse was the fact that most of security, as well as the referee, went to seperate Badd Intentions and the Tokyo Express from each other. This gave Nik Nyce carte blanche to humiliate Kenriki Ogami. When Nyce saw that there was no referee after he tapped Ogami out with a sleeperhold, he continued to punish Ogami, even hitting the Nyce Finisher on him at one point ER: Survival escaped from Elijah long enough to even the score, but Elijah's recuperative abilities payed off, as he was able to blindside Survival when he might have won the match, and help Nyce further punish Ogami. Then, after posing for the crowd, and insulting Survival, Elijah finsshed of Ogami by pressing him over his head, then dropping him down into another Diamond cutter! AO: Luckily, Ogami suffered only minor injury to his neck, and will be back in action in a week. We sent a camera crew to his dojo the day after Ring Wars, and recorded these comments from him (both men turn to the screen) -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- [Taped 5/18/97] (The scene opens in Kenriki Ogami's dojo. Sad Japanese music is playing as the camera pans through the dojo. We see pcitures of Kenriki Ogami and Sumo wrestlers we find Kenriki, dressed in a white kimono, sitting in front of an old shrine. He's bruised and has a black eye as a result from his match. He looks very sad but struggeles to keep a proud face. The music fades away as he starts to speak.) KO: I was defeated. I cost my teampartners the match. I'm very ashamed of this fact. I would like to appologize to the Tokyo Express and Survival for costing them the title. And I would like to appologize to all of my fans for letting them down. This only proves that I must train even harder. So train I will. As a reminder of my misstakes I'm going to shave my head, and not let the hair grow back until the fans has forgiven me. (Picks up a tanto and begins to shave of his short black hair.) This was a wake up call for me. I will now dedicate my life in perfecting my skills so that such a disgrace will never happen again. I will be ready for my next match. I may have been defeated this time but the next time I will be ready. Survival and Tokyo Express I owe you! I will help you, if you ever get in trouble I will be there for you! That's the only way I can get my selfrespect back. (Scene fades away as Kenriki continues to shave his head.) -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- ER: Ogami took his loss to heart. He intends on being better prepared next time. AO: I don't think Ogami should blame himself for what happened. Nik Nyce is a phenomenal wrestler, which is why we signed him! Look at the match he had with Akira Hashimoto. He is one of the top contendors for the vacant heavyweight title, and may even be the first champion! ER: I can agree with that. This man is so focused, and didn't let the chaos around him distract him from the important objective, which was winning the match. Our camera crew caught Nyce at the airport, before he caught a flight back to the US, so he could oblige committments he had there (both men turn to the screen) -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- [Taped 5/20/97] (Nik Nyce is shown in an airport getting ready to take a plane home to America) NN: Well some guys like to spend their Sundays BBQing or washing the family car but "The Perfectionist" Nik Nyce spends his Sundays winning matches in the SJPW. NN: I expected a bigger challenge but I guess not everyone can be on the same level as me. The one thing that I really didn't like was the fact that I had to be on the same team as Bad Intentions. These boys are complete losers. A polar bear has more brains than these two boys combined. NN: They seem to think that they are one of the better teams here in the SJPW. Well the only reason they think that is becasue the tag team scene here is kinda weak and there isn't a team to shut these guys up. NN: I don't have any intentions of becomming a tag team wrestler but I want to put these boys in there place. Of course I need another person to tag with. There's one guy I have in mind. NN: Elijah how about you and me teaming up one time and putting these punks in their place. Elijah I don't want to become your best friend I just want to team with you for one more match. I think if we teamed together, Badd Intentions won't have a chance against us. NN: The tag match is just to kill some time. I really want to get back in the ring aginst Hashimoto. He seems to be hiding and doing a hell of a job at it. I guess I am a little impatient but I don't like being robbed of a win. At the PPV I will answer all questions of who is the best wrestler in the SJPW and that will be "The Perfectionist" Nik Nyce (screen fades) -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- ER: That is a bombshell! Nik Nyce asking Elijah to team with him against Badd Intentions! What's your take on this Akira? AO: I'm shocked, but also pleased. I don't like Badd Intentions, and it seems that Nik Nyce doesn't either. But what of Elijah? No one has heard from him since Saturday night! Will he accept? We have to wait and see how this turns out. ER: What about his comments concerning Hashimoto? AO: Akira Hashimoto is a great man, and I look forward to them having their rematch. These two men could be the two who truly decide who will be the first SJPW champion! I admire both men, as they are two of the best in the world! ER: On that note, we're out of time. I'll see all of you soon on Trash Talk. For Akira Ono, I'm Eddie Rose. Goodbye [Camera fades out]
