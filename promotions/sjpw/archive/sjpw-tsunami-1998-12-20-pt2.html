Eddie Rose: Do you feel that Murkami's injury made it easier for you to defeat him? Unlike your other matches, you seemed very focused on moves that took advantage of that injury.... Tasogare no Prince: Admittedly, that is not my usual style, however, I am sure that Murakami-san would agree with the fact that a true warrior takes advantage of any and all of an opponent's weaknesses in order to emerge triumphant. I could do no less to insure the coming of my destiny. *A slight smile crosses his lips...* Eddie Rose: Murakami made a recent statement asking you for a rematch when he's healed. Will you grant him a rematch? Tasogare no Prince: When he is healed, then I will not hesitate to give him a rematch, so long as the executive committee of SJPW deems him worthy. I will not refuse any worthy competitor the opportunity to challenge me. I am nothing if not a fighting champion. However, I feel I must tell Murakami-san, as well as any and all others who would challenge me that it will be their destiny to fall before me. *He takes another whiff of the orchid...* Eddie Rose: During the semi-finals, you fought Jet Jaguar II of Chiimu Tora. He was injured after you defeated him. The Banshee came to ringside, and the two of you had a staredown. Is it true that the two of you have a past history? Tasogare no Prince: I have never encountered the Banshee before. However, I can understand that he was concerned about the welfare of his friend and teammate in that meeting, perhaps that was the reason for his looking at me in that manner. But, I could not allow Jet Jaquar to stand between myself and my destiny, nor can I allow Banshee to do the same. Eddie Rose: Banshee has made comments that your paths will cross here in SJPW. Your reaction? Tasogare no Prince: If he feels that is his destiny, then by all means, I wish him well. I too, know a thing about destiny... *He smells the white orchid in his hand and smiles...* And I know that if we were to meet, then it will be the destiny of the Banshee to fall, like all the rest will. Eddie Rose: What about Jensei Tenru? He constantly claims that he "Is It!" He also makes the limit for the Welterweight Division. Many feel he is the next Super Tengu, and we all know how successful Tengu was! Tasogare no Prince: Jensei Tenru? Hmph. I sincerely doubt if he would take the time to compete for the Welterweight title out of his busy schedule of self-promotion and hocking those poorly made trinkets which bear his likeness. But, if he should express a serious desire to face me for my championship, then I will welcome the challenge, and prove to him that it is also his destiny to fall before the Prince of Twilight. Eddie Rose: Now that you're currently on top of the Welterweight ranks, would you consider going after a heavyweight title? How about the Koubousen Geijutsu division? Tasogare no Prince: As for the Koubousen Geijutsu division, though I respect the athletes who compete in it, I would never participate in it. That is not the style of wrestling which I have trained myself for. Although, I feel that a man well-trained in puroresu could defeat a pure martial artist. The Heavyweight title? Eventually the time will come when I am destined to prove myself not only the greatest Welterweight wrestler in SJPW, but the greatest wrestler period in SJPW. If part of that destiny is winning the Heavyweight title, then so be it. For now, however, I am more concerned with keeping my Welterweight title until such time as destiny calls me to something else. Eddie Rose: Have you decided when you will first defend your title, and who you've chosen to defend your title against first? Tasogare no Prince: I am ready and willing to defend my belt at any time. As for my choice of opponent, obviously, I feel that Murakami-san is more than deserving of the opportunity, however I realize that his injury may prevent that from occuring too soon. There is another man that I would be interested in facing, though. Banshee, you seem to think that facing me is a matter of honor for what occured to your teammate Jet Jaquar. Whatever your reasoning, if you truly wish to enter into the Twilight, I will be more than happy to oblige you. But I feel I must warn you, that your destiny will be the same as your friend's was. You will fall before the Prince of Twilight. Eddie Rose: Congratulations again, and best of luck holding onto it against the kind of competition only SJPW can throw against you! Tasogare no Prince: I look forward to it. The title is only as g reat as the men who compete for it. That is why I can always be proud of winning the SJPW Welterweight championship. I know that I did it by defeating the very best competition in the world, and that makes me a true champion. Arigatoo. [As the lights in the arena dim once more, and calming cello music plays, Tasogare no Prince leaps nimbly over the top rope out of the ring. He goes over to the group of young girls at ringside who are screaming loudly for him, and presents one of them with the white orchid in his hand, stroking her cheek as he does. He then turns and walks back up the aisle, exiting the arena...]
