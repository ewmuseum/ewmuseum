SJPW House Show — Battle Sphere Tokyo (6/8/04)

Jun 8, 2004, 12:00:00 AM
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
June 8th, 2004 Battle Sphere Tokyo: 300 (sellout) 1) Jet Jaguar III def Dragon Mask (8:19) after a Fisherman's Falcon Arrow. 2) Frankie "The Thumper" Douglas & Doug Thomas def Dynamite Boy & Eiji Kid Shimura (10:32) after they hit the Mauler Plex on Shimura. 3) Tetsuo Matsumoto def "Kyoto Tiger" Takayoshi Asakura (14:07) after a half-nelson suplex. 4) "All Star" Joey Darren & Tsunami defeated El Santa Azteca & Toramusha (7:28) after Tsunami pinned Toramusha following a Tsunami Driver #2. 5) "Ecchi Kid" Kentaro "H-bomb" Kawasaki def Wolf Jager (12:45) with the Ecchi Driver. - Dragon Mask's solo debut was fairly even paced, with him keeping up well with the slightly more experienced Jaguar III. Both men shook hands after the match, which got a nice ovation from the crowd. - The Nagasaki Bulldogs were unable to make lightning strike twice in a row, as the Maulers were able to stop them cold with their Mauler-Plex (Douglas Superplexing Thomas on top of their opponent) - Super Japan's #1 son evened the odds with the rookie sensation. Both men fought tooth and nail, with Asakura going to the air, and Matsumoto sticking to his hard hitting ground attack. Asakura looked to be ready to win the match, when out of nowhere, Matsumoto dumped him on his head with a half-nelson suplex, stunning him long enough to score a three count. - While they may be rivals currently, Darren & Tsunami showed they can coexist together as a tag team. They showed some excellent teamwork, hitting quite a few double team moves on their opponents. Try as they might, Azteca & Toramusha couldn't get it together, and after hitting the Tsunami Driver #2, Tsunami pinned Toramusha (with Darren assisting by preventing Azteca from breaking up the pin attempt). - In a strange twist of fate, Jager found himself pitted against Kawasaki. Jager took it to the Baba Cup finalist, working the arm with a variety of moves. Kawasaki kept to using some stiff strikes when he did so, and would followup with some impact moves. The end came as Jager was attempting an over the shoulder armbreaker, and Kawasaki shoved him off. Jager came off the ropes, and Kawsaki scooped him up, hit the Ecchi Driver, and quickly covered for the pin. After the match, both men had a stare down, and a man came out of the stands and into the ring, grabbing Jager. Jager started to struggle with him, but as the man spoke to him, he ceased struggling, and allowed himself to be escorted from the ring. Kawasaki basically ignored Jager as he left, exiting the ring, and heading to the back with Mysterious Warrior.
