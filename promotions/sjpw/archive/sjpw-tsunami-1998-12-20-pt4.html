SJPW Card Archive

Tsunami, Part Four

20 December 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Tsunami continues...] [The camera pans to Eddie Rose standing at the foot of the runway. He waits a moment for the crowd to die down, then starts to speak...] Eddie Rose: Ladies & Gentlemen, allow me to introduce to you my guest at this time...."THE WARBIRD" YUKIO MURAKAMI!!! [Yukio "The Warbird" Murakami, dressed in a sharp three-piece midnight blue suit, enters the arena and makes his way down the runway. The audience applauds and calls out his name, but he ignores them. His face is very serious, and he occasionally runs his calloused hand across his bald head. He stops next to Eddie Rose, staring at the interviewer with intense eyes.] Eddie Rose: Yukio, let's get right to it: How is the arm doing? You took alot of punishment at the hands of first Greg Miller, then Tasogare no Prince. Yukio Murakami: You don't know the meaning of the word "punishment," little man. Punishment is watching your culture disappear in flames, flushed down the toilet of history. Punishment is watching your proud people parade around like little western puppets. When I look around this arena tonight, all I see is a decadent, washed-up excuse for a once great nation. My arm? It means nothing. The Warbird is willing to give his soul for Japan. [The audience doesn't know how to respond to this. A few begin to boo, but mostly the audience remains quiet. There is tension in the air.] Eddie Rose: As you know, earlier tonight, I conducted an interview with Tasogare no Prince. During that interview, he agreed to your request for a rematch. How does that make you feel? Yukio Murakami: I expected as much. The Twilight Prince is an honorable man, albeit on the girlish side, don't you think? What's with all his flowers and quiet smiles? When my arm heals, Tasogare no Prince will end his reign as the welterweight champion of the SJPW. I guarantee that. Eddie Rose: Speaking of Greg Miller, I've heard reports that he wants a rematch with you. Would you be willing to face him in the near future? Yukio Murakami: Greg Miller? Hah, don't make me laugh! That gaijin wouldn't even begin to know how to wrestle me. But if he wants a rematch, I'd be more than happy to put him down again. This time for good! [Again, a few audience members cheer, but mostly the rest of the audience is a bit taken aback by Yukio's sudden display of arrogance and anger.] Eddie Rose: Do you still intend to wage war on the Gaijin competitors of SJPW? Your early statements when you came to SJPW was that you wished to see them all driven from the promotion... [Yukio grabs the microphone from Eddie's hand. He jabs his finger into Eddie's face.] Yukio Murakami: What are you implying, little man? Are you saying that I'm the kind of man who would want to prevent an honest wrestler from making a living? Are you saying that just because I feel that the west has contaminated my great country that I would support isolationism? Do you really think the Warbird, the greatest wrestler this nation has ever produced, would shy away from wrestling the dregs of the West? [Yukio thrusts the microphone back into the hand of the stunned Eddie Rose.] Yukio Murakami: Damn right, little man. The Warbird is here to shape the SJPW in his image! When the dust settles, the SJPW will begin to reflect the true dignity and honor of Japan! If you are not with me, my fellow wrestlers, you are against me. And for my enemies, there is only one path - the path of the Divine Wind! [Rose, still a little flabergasted at Murakami's tirade, gulps a quick breath of air] Eddie Rose: If you're so dead-set on driving all the Gaijin out, why settle for the Welterweight title? Why not go after the All-Asian title, which is the primary title in SJPW? Even though considered a welterweight, you can comepete for that belt... Yukio Murakami: I'll do what I desire to do, little man. All in good time. I've already announced my intention of unifying the belts in the SJPW. When I feel I am ready to pursue the All-Asian title, then I can guaranty you, it will be mine. But for now, Tasagore no Prince...enjoy that welterweight title. Take many photographs of it. Because very soon they will be the only rememberances you will have of it. Eddie Rose: I see. What are your opinions of Chiimu Tora then? They've allowed two Gaijin, Banshee & Onslaught to become members. They are the most powerful stable in all of SJPW, and honor the teachings of their mentor, Tiger Kikuchi. Are they so wrong to practice tolerance? Yukio Murakami: Wrong? No, there is no right or wrong. There is just weak and strong. Chiimu Tora, you have weakened yourself by allowing those foul- mouthed, undisciplined, arrogant heathens to enter your once-honerable clan of wrestlers. Shishi Fujiwara - you are an up and coming young lion. I can see promise in your style and skills. And Jet Jaguar II - your fluid fighting skills are signs of a well-trained and gifted warrior. Why pollute yourself with garbage? Eddie Rose: Well, thank you for joining us this evening. Ladies & Gentlemen, "The Warbird" Yukio Murakami! [Yukio grabs the microphone away from Eddie once again. He stares intensely into the camera.] Yukio Murakami: SJPW, you have awakened a sleeping dragon. The reign of the Warbird begins now! [Yukio throws the microphone to the ground and stalks off backstage. The camera view shifts back to Kikuchi & Ono...] TK: That young man is very angry. At least he will get his rematch with Tasogare no Prince... AO: Murakami is bitter. Why, we can only guess. His mindset is much like the recent attitude of the Daimyo, ne? TK: Very true. Could an alliance be in the works? AO: Up next we begin the Semi-final matches in the All-Asian Juuryou Tournament as Kikuchi-san's favorite, Shinji Hamada, faces Scorpio. TK: Akira, I do not have favorites. I do believe Hamada has a good chance of winning this tourament, but I am unbiased. AO: As you say, as you say. TK: Hamada has trained harder for this tournament than he has ever trained before, and it is showing. He defeated the always dangerous Kensei Tokugawa earlier tonight. AO: Hai, and Scorpio defeated the luchadore Metamorphico to advance. TK: Both men facing very differant styles as they move on in the tournament. ____ _____ _ _____________________________________________ / __/_ / / _ \ | /| / /All-Asian Juuryou Tournament, Round Three:/ _\ \/ // / ___/ |/ |/ / Shinji Hamada vs. Scorpio /___/\___/_/ |__/|__/__________________________________________/ [The camera cuts to the ring, where ring announcer Nishi Toju stands.] Nishi: The following contest is a Semi-final match in the All-Asian Juuryou Tournament![fans applaud and cheer]. It is scheduled for one fall with a fifteen minute time-limit, your offical is Goto Kyoji! ["Carry On" by Angra plays] Nishi: Introducing first...Hailing from Sao Paulo, Brazil and weighing in at 246 pounds....SCORPIO!!! [Scorpio heads down to the ring as the fans cheer. Scorpio wears long yellow tights and green boots with the Brazillian flag on the sides. He climbs on to the apron, and then executes a slingshot backflip into the ring to the cheers of the fans. The then jumps to the second rope and raises his hands as green and yellow fireworks explode over the ring.] AO: Scorpio a fine athlete, and a bit more of a high-flyer than Hamada. He will have to use his speed and avoid being taken to the mat by the submission specialist. I am still curious where Erika Lima is... TK: Hai. Hamada will have to counter that with his own aerial skills, but he should use his strength to ground Scorpio and then use his mat and submission skills. As for Ms. Lima, that is none of our concern... ["Phaedra" by Tangerine Dream plays] Nishi: His opponent, he is the leader of Chiimu Tora! Hailing from Osaka and weighing in at 235 pounds...SHIN-JI HA-MA-DA!!! [The crowd explodes with cheers as Hamada strides down the aisle slapping hands. He wears long gold tiger-stripped tights, white boots, white elbowpads and black kneepads. He enters the ring, and stares down Scorpio, who nods in his direction.] AO: Hamada looking as focused as ever. He wants the All-Asian title, and he wants it tonight. TK: He must face a great competitor in Scorpio first. You cannot put one match before the other, not if you want to win. [Goto brings both men together, and calls for them to shake hands. They do, and the fans applaud. Goto sends both men back to their corners and then signals for the bell.] AO: We are underway, and Hamada moves in, and looks to lock-up, but Scorpio explodes with European Uppercuts and now...Standing Dropkick puts Hamada down. TK: Scorpio showing that quickness, and he is up to his feet as Hamada rises, looking a bit shocked. [Scorpio smiles and nods to Hamada, and Hamada stares back, and then moves in. They lock-up, and Hamada suddenly lays in with straight forearms to the side of Scorpio's head, and then leaps up and connects with a standing dropkick of his own. The fans applaud as Hamada gets to his feet and Scorpio stands up, and nods his head acknowledging Hamada's moves.] AO: Both men circling now, and they lock-up, Scorpio with a side-headlock, but Hamada quick to send him to the ropes. Lariat attempted by Hamada, but Scorpio ducks under it. Scorpio back off the ropes...Powerslam by Hamada![fans "ohhhh" and then applaud] TK: Hamada immediately attempts a Dragon Sleeper, but Scorpio is able to squirm away, and he gets to his feet. [Scorpio shoots off the rope, but Hamada catches him for a side-slam, but Scorpio is able to counter with a quick spinning headscissors takedown and the fans applaud. Both men get to their feet, and Scorpio lays in with quick European Uppercuts sending Hamada reeling back to the corner.] AO: Scorpio pulls Hamada out of the corner and lifts him up and over with a Vertical Suplex. Neither man showing much wear and tear from their earlier matches, but we might see some effects and fatigue showing later. TK: Both men are in top condition, ideal for this type of tournament. [Scorpio quickly climbs to the top, but Hamada is up, and he cuts him off. Scorpio batters Hamada with forearms however, and Hamada goes crashing back to the mat. Scorpio leaps off with a Somaursault Senton and hits his mark, crushing Hamada.] ...1... ...2... AO: Kickout by Hamada, and Scorpio is back up quickly. He pulls Hamada up...Hamada battles back with a forearm and now a kick to the midsection. Gutwrench Suplex by Hamada. TK: Hamada showing his power there, and now he is up. Scorpio rising as Hamada comes off the ropes...LARAITO! [Hamada levels Scorpio with a running clothesline as the fans applaud. Hamada quickly pulls Scorpio up and attempts a Vertical Suplex, but Scorpio counters with a small package.] ...1... ...2... AO: Hamada able to escape, and Scorpio has been close on two occasions. [Both men rise, and Hamada explodes into Scorpio with a clothesline that puts both men down. Hamada rises, and runs to the ropes, Scorpio scrambles to his feet, and leapfrogs over Hamada. Hamada bouces back, and Scorpio leaps up for a Frankesteiner.] TK: POWERBOMB BY HAMADA! [The fans cheer as Hamada counters Scorpio's move, and then quickly spins him into a Boston Crab. Scorpio crys out, and frantically reaches for the ropes. Erika Lima claps and cheers for Scorpio as he reaches out, but Hamada grinds his knee into the back, and tightens the pressure.] AO: Hamada has the hold locked in tight, and Scorpio is in trouble, this may be the end. TK: Hamada showing his submission skills learned from his father before him. [Goto checks in, looking for the tap out, but Scorpio refuses to submit. Hamada releases the hold, and stomps down across the back of Scorpio. He pulls the Brazilian up and sets him.] AO: Another Powerbomb? No, Hamada lifts Scorpio into a Hanging Backbreaker. [The fans "ohhhh" and "ahhhh" and applaud as Goto checks in as Scorpio crys out in pain, but refuses to submit. Hamada applies more pressure, pulling down, bending Scorpio across his shoulder and Scorpio crys out once more, but shows his heart by not quitting.] TK: Scorpio trying to force Hamada's hands apart, but they are like a vise. [Scorpio, realizing the futility of his attempt to pry apart Hamada's hands, begins to kick in the air with his legs, and Hamada stumbles back a bit. This allows Scorpio to kick off the top turnbuckle and he escapes the Backbreaker, and simultaneously sends Hamada over with a back body drop. The fans applaud as Scorpio staggers to the corner, holding his back. Hamada gets to his feet, and Scorpio rushes him and connects with a dropkick to the chin.] AO: Pinpoint dropkick by Scorpio, but he is still favoring his back. TK: Hai. Hamada is the first man up, and he must press his advantage. [Hamada drives a forearm to the small of Scorpio's back as he rises, and then locks on a Full Nelson. He lifts Scorpio of his feet into a Swinging Full Nelson and the fans applaud the display of pure strength. Goto checks in for a submission, but Hamada doesn't give him a chance as he drops Scorpio down with a Full Nelson Slam.] AO: Cover by Hamada. ...1... ...2... TK: Scorpio just able to kick-out. [crowd applauds] AO: Hamada up, and he is signalling for the Tiger Driver 2000! [The fans cheer as Hamada pulls Scorpio up and sends him to the ropes. He presses Scorpio up as the fans "ohhh" and "ahhh", but Scorpio suddenly shifts his weight and manages to catch Hamada and take him over with a Frankensteiner.] AO: Great counter by Scorpio, and he drives Hamada headfirst into the mat. TK: Hai. Hamada showed Scorpio he was attempting his big move, and Scorpio was ready. [Scorpio gets to his feet, holding his back, but looking determined to fight. Hamada gets up slowly, shaking his head. Scorpio quickly scoops Hamada up, and slams him to the mat. Scorpio grimaces in pain, clutching his back, but then quickly goes to the apron and climbs to the top rope.] AO: Scorpio leaps off...FROG SPLASH! ...1... ...2... ...3 AO: NO![Fans "ohhhh" and then applaud] TK: Hamada powers out in time, and Scorpio pounds the mat in frustration. [Scorpio gets to his feet, and he signals for his Tidal Wave finisher before pulling Hamada up. Scorpio lifts Hamada up and slams him hard to the mat. Scorpio shrugs off the pain in his back, and pulls Hamada up again, executing a second slam.] AO: Scorpio hits two, and now he goes for the third to finish off the Tidal Wave...He has him up... TK: Scorpio's back gave out. [The fans "ohhh" as Scorpio releases Hamada and grabs his back, his face etched in agony. Hamada quickly gets a waistlock and lifts Scorpio up.] AO: INVERTED LIGER BOMB!! TK: Hamada now locking in the STF. [The fans cheer as Hamada levers back on the chinlock and Scorpio cries out. Goto drops down and checks in.] AO: Scorpio still refusing to submit. TK: This manuver effects so many points, and it has to be working over his already weakened back. Hamada now pulls back with even more force. AO: Scorpio taps out! Hamada has won it! **ding** **ding** **ding** ["Phaedra" by Tangerine Dream blares over the PA as the crowd explodes with cheers] Nishi: Your winner advancing to the finals of the All-Asian Juuryou Tournament...SHIN-JI HA-MA-DA!!!! [The crowd countinues to applaud as Goto raises Hamada's hand. Several colored streamers fly into the ring as Hamada raises his hands into the air and the fans cheer] AO: Hamada secures the victory using his submission skills to great affect, and he is one step closer to winning the All-Asian Title. TK: Hai. A great effort on both men's parts, and Hamada showing tremendous skill and determination. We shall see if it can guide him to his ultimate goal. [Hamada helps Scorpio to his feet, and raises his hand and the fans applaud again. Scorpio nods to Hamada and then he stands up straight fighting the pain, and shakes Hamada's hand. Scorpio then leaves the ring, and heads to the back. Hamada pumps his fist in the air, and then raises his index finger indicating "1" more match to the title and then exits the ring] AO: Hamada-sama only one match away from holding a title he once held before. Can he become a two-time All-Asian champion? TK: Good question Ono-sama. Time will tell, and I can't wait to see... [The jumbotron suddenly comes on, and opens on... darkness? Yes, the camera is focused on apparently black space] Voice: From darkness was the legend borne, thus to darkness it returned... the spirit possesed one man and made him a god... a god of the grappling arts... *a light chuckle escapes* now the "God" has thrown the mantle down beleiving that what light in him may banish away the darkness... poor, poor boy, the darkness will lways be within you... [there is a long pause, the voice wheezes slowly, laughing between deep breaths] Voice: The mantle has been picked up by another... another now embraces the darkness and holds it close to his soul... But A "God" is not what I seek to become... no, nothing as grand as that... rather this little bird would be master over everything he surveys... like the raven I covet, like the raven I shall take... like a demon I see the evil in men's hearts, like a devil I consume it and make myself stronger... [the voice laughs once more, who is this guy?] Voice: Jaguar, Miller, Murakami, Prince... simply men, simply foolish men who have no idea what is about to sweep through them... I am the demon bird, I am the darkness that you knew for a year as... "Puroresu No Kami-sama"... you may simply call me... [a click is heard and a lone lighbulb emanates a bright white light. Sitting under this light is... Super Tengu? But that's impossible!! In't it?] Super Tengu: You may burn me, but the spirit finds another, you may hold a victory over me but you will never BEAT me... The Demon you though gone lives again... Fear me Kobayashi, know that your beloved prize, your Super Japan Puroresingu is the pearl at the center of the oyster the Tengu will pry open... [pauses for a moment and smiles evilly, shaking his head] No, not pry open... rather smash to bits with a hammer... Tengu lives, There is no Super Japan without Tengu, remember this and all will be well... Let my magnificence in the ring shine like a beacon once more, let the fans know that it is Tengu, the Demon-Bird that will hear their calls... Let them no longer be forced to worship false heroes... let them instead plead to an anti-hero... TO THE ANTI-HERO!! SUPER TENGU RETURNS!! SUPER TENU BRINGS YOU GLORY!! SUPER TENGU BRINGS YOU LOVE... [voice lowers and lets off a malicious smile] for who knows more of love than Tengu-sama? Many have known and all have loved me... Let my insanity begin... anew... [slowly Tengu's hand reaches up and turns the light off returning the room to darkness. The jumbotron shuts off, and the fans murmur over what they have seen...] TK: What is going on here? AO: First Jumbo makes accusations about Tetsuo Matsumoto, then Super Tengu, who Tetsuo once was, or is he once again? [Suddenly, Jumbo Kobayashi comes out from the back, a microphone in his hands. The crowd goes silent, as Jumbo's face is a mask of rage...] Jumbo Kobayashi: MATSUMOTO! Once more you show how you have lied to us all! You swore an oath to no longer use the identity of Super Tengu! Then you do this? You are a man WITHOUT honor! [the crowd "oooohs' at the accusation] Jumbo Kobayashi: Does your treachery know no bounds? You dissapoint me Matsumoto. Your father must be turning in his grave over your acts of late. You disgust me Matsumoto. You truly disgust me... [Kobayashi walks backstage, leaving the crowd in stunned silence] TK: Kobayashi-sama is very angry. He should be less judgemental, and find out if that was Matsumoto in the attire of Super Tengu. AO: It looked like him to me... TK: He looked like Tengu did before Tetsuo added 20 pounds of weight. It could be someone else in the guise of Tengu... AO: Maybe, but can this all just be coincidence? TK: That is not for us to speculate. We still have two more matches to go, so let us worry about this situation later. AO: I still think it's Tetsuo... TK: Enough Ono-sama! AO: Oh, all right. Ed Dean faces another challenge next, as he faces Ace Kilcannon. This should be an interesting match, as neither man has faced each other before. TK: Their former factions, the Gaijin and Reign of Terror, while similar, were opposed at one time. This should prove to be an interesting match. AO: I'll say! I hope they both beat each other senseless! ____ _____ _ _____________________________________________ / __/_ / / _ \ | /| / /All-Asian Juuryou Tournament, Round Three:/ _\ \/ // / ___/ |/ |/ / Ace Kilcannon vs Ed Dean /___/\___/_/ |__/|__/__________________________________________/ [The camera cuts to Nishi Toju in the ring...] Nishi: This next contest is a semi-final match in the All-Asian Juuryou Tournament! [crowd cheers] It is one fall, fifteen minute time limt. Introducing first, from Columbus, Ohio, USA, and weighing in at 245 pounds...ACE KIL-CAN-NON!!! ["Judgement Night" by Biohazard & Onyx blares over the PA as Ace comes out from the back, and walks confidently to the ring...] TK: Ace looks confident about this match. AO: Good! I hope he defeats the "Ugly American"! Nishi: His opponent, accompanied to the ring by Hadi Moditan, from Stone Mountain, Georgia, USA, and weighing 355 pounds...ED DEAN!!! [Dean comes out from the back, and strides to the ring, with Moditan in tow. He jumps up on the apron, then looks out at the crowd before holding the ropes open for Moditan...] TK: Dean seems very confident himself... AO: And why not? He's made it this far; if he gets past Ace, it's up to Hamada to stop him! [Dean enters the ring, and Ace immediately charges him, landing a dropkick to his knee. The crowd "oooohs" as Moditan makes a quick exit, and Botan signals for the bell...] TK: Kilcannon wasting no time catching Dean off guard! AO: Good! Break his legs! [Ace gets back to his feet, and starts to stomp on Dean's leg. Dean rolls to the ropes, and out of the ring. He starts to walk around, rubbing his leg. Botan starts to count, while Ace yells at Dean to get back in the ring. Dean flips Ace off, and Ace grabs the ropes, and slingshots over the top rope, cross bodyblocking Dean on the floor! The crowd "oooohs" as Dean hits the railing. Ace gets to his feet, hops up on the apron, then executes an Asai moonsault onto Dean. Deans slumps to the floor, and Ace works the crowd, who applaud his offense so far....] TK: Kilcannon making a good showing of himself so far. AO: But he needs to ignore the crowd, and take Dean out! TK: He's doing a good job so far. AO: Until Dean is defeated, he is not doing anything! [Ace whips Dean across the floor, and he crashes into the railing a few feet away. Ace charges him, but Dean ducks down, and Ace misses the spinwheel kick, and crotches himself on the railing! The crowd "oooohs" as Ace is draped over the top rope. Dean gets to his feet, grabs Ace from behind, then takes him backwards, German Suplexing him onto the floor! The crowd "oooohs" as Ace's head hits the floor mat. Dean gets to his feet, and looks at the crowd, a vicious smile coming over his face. He drags Ace to his feet, then presses him over his head, and throws him through the ropes into the ring. He hops up on the apron, and enters the ring. He walks over to Ace, and drags him to his feet. He picks him up off the mat, then drops down into a side suplex. He gets back up, dragging Ace with him, then hits a gutwrench suplex. Dean makes a cover, but Ace kicks out at one...] TK: Dean recovered quickly from Kilcannon's early offense, and is now dismantling him! AO: I told you he had to be more careful! Foolish Gaijin! [Dean rains a series of elbowdrops across Ace's chest, then drags his dazed opponent to his feet. He irish whips him across the ring, then attempts a lariat. Ace ducks under, stops short, and turns around. Dean comes off the ropes, and Ace catches him with a superkick flush on the chin. The crowd "ooohs" as Dean stops dead in his tracks, then collapses in a heap. Ace runs to the corner, steppladders to the top, and moonsaults off, twisting in mid-air so that he lands on Dean with a senton splash. The crowd "oooohs" as Ace gets back to his feet, then grabs Dean's leg, and turns him over into a single-leg boston crab. Ace's face betrays his confidence, but Dean grabs the ropes, and Botan makes him break. Ace releases the hold, and stomps the back of Dean's knee for good measure. He drags Dean to his feet, drives a knee into his gut, doubling him over. Ace runs to the ropes, and comes off, landing a Rocker Dropper to the back of Dean's neck, driving his face into the mat. Dean rolls around, clutching his nose, while Ace puts the boots to him...] TK: Kilcannon once more in control, and showing the world why he is one of the most versatile competitors out there. AO: He's looking good so far, but Dean is far from beaten! [Ace stands Dean up, then whips him to the ropes. As Dean comes off, Ace attempts a hurricanrana, but Dean keeps himself from going over. He runs towards the ropes, slingshots Ace off them, turns, then runs across the ring, dropping down, and slamming Ace down with a vicious sit down powerbomb! The crowd "ooohs" as Botan drops to count, then applaud as Ace kicks out at two and a half. Dean complains of a slow count, then slowly gets to his feet. Dean sits Ace up, then slaps on a full nelson. He drags Ace to his feet, and Ace somehow slips out of the full nelson, goes behind Dean, and applies a waistlock. Dean attempts a back elbow, but Ace is able to keep his head low, then using his leverage, takes Dean over into a German Suplex! The crowd "ooohs" as he gets the bigger man over. Moditan looks worried on the outside, and Ace gets to his feet, looking a little winded. He goes to the corner, and starts to climb to the top rope. He steadies himself, then crouches low, waiting for Dean to get up. Moditan urges Dean on, and he slowly rises to his feet, He looks around for Ace, and as he turns towards him, Ace comes off the top with a missle dropkick that connects to Dean's face, sending him flying across the ring! The crowd "oooohs" as Ace kip-ups to his feet, then runs towards Dean, and lands on top of him for a cover. Botan drops to count, and makes it to two and three quarters before Dean kicks out! The crowd applauds, while Ace looks shocked that Dean kicked out...] TK: I thought for sure that Dean was finished there! AO: This man can take alot more punishment than that! He's a monster! [Ace drags Dean to his feet, Dean's nose bleeding again. Ace lands a european uppercut, and Dean fires back with a punch. Ace hits another uppercut, and Dean grabs him by the hair, and lands a headbutt to his face. Dean then drives a knee into Ace's gut, doubling him over. He double underhooks Ace's arms, then lifts him up. He holds him vertical, letting the blood rush to his head, and causing the crowd to "ooooh". Dean then moves forward, and brings Ace down gut first across the top rope! The crowd "oooohs" again, while Dean, who still has Ace double underhooked, lifts him back up, and takes him over for a suplex. Ace hits the mat hard, and sits up holding his back. Dean gets behind him, then lands a stiff kick to his back, the crowd "oooohing" at the impact. Dean grabs Ace from behind, and drags him to his feet. He locks Ace in a double armbar from behind, then falls back, driving Ace's head & neck into the mat with a release Tiger Suplex! The crowd "ooohs" as Ace hits the mat, and lays there. Dean gets to his feet, and moves to cover, but Moditan stops him. He motions for Dean to do it again, and Dean smiles malevolently, and nods. He drags the dazed Ace to his feet again, locks his arms behind him, then once again executes a release Tiger Suplex. Ace hits the mat hard, and this time Dean makes a cover, and Botan drops down, making the three count. The crowd applauds half-heartedly as Moditan enters the ring, smiling...] TK: Dean once again wins, thus making it to the finals. AO: Where he will face Hamada! Nishi: The winner of this match, advancing to the finals...ED DEAN!!! [crowd applauds] TK: So now it is down to Dean & Hamada. Two men, one title. Who will win. AO: For all our sakes, let's hope it will be Hamada!
