Ring Wars Preview and Control Center #1 (23 April 1997)

There are two high back chairs with a large "V" shaped table in the middle of the set. The table has a large SJPW Logo on the front of it in Red Black and White. There are stacks of papers on top of the table. There is a video wall covering the entire back side of the set. Currently only a SJPW logo is shown on the screen. A caucasian man in his mid 20's walks in from the left. He's has red hair and is wearing blue jeans, a black T-Shirt with "SJPW" written in Red on it, and a black leather jacket. An asian man in his mid 30s with long black hair put into a pony tail and a goatee walks in from the right side. He's wearing a black suit with a white chinese shirt and black Oakley shades. They both sit in their respective chairs.] Eddie Rose: Hello Everyone. I'm Eddie Rose, and my partner is the japanese legend, Akira Ono. Akira Ono: Thank you Eddie-san. ER: Tonight we preview the first ever Super Japan Pro Wrestling card, Ring Wars, live from the Tokyo dome, this saturday night! AO: It will be a grand event to start Super Japan! ER: That's right Akira. Though the control center listed a different set of matches, we have an updated set of matches for the card. This is going to be a spectacular card, and the main event will be exciting. AO: That is true Eddie-san. Elijah, the Ultimate Phyiscal Speciman, vs the World reknowned Survival! Both men posess great strength and power. ER: That's true Akira, buy Survival is a veteran. He's wrestled all over the world, and holds belts in other feds. He's got to have the edge. AO: Maybe, but all these other federations take up his time. He can't focus on Elijah, as he has other matches to contend with. And he will suffer from Jet Lag. I am sure of this. I say Elijah will prevail ER: Maybe. We'll find out this weekend. Did you hear the rumor that Patty Babes Carter may soon compete in SJPW? AO: What? That man is another legend! Although I have never faced him myself, I know Carter is a tremedous wrestler. His arrival will bring great honor to Super Japan! ER: Definitely. Let's look at the Super J monitor, and see the updated card listing (both men turn to the large screen behind them. The SJPW logo materializes, then flashes the Ring Wars logo, with the date for this saturday underneath. The match list comes up next) Kenriki Ogami vs Sho Nakatoni (j) "Tsunami" Rhiyoto Makada vs Gregor Davinsky (j) MPW tag team match: The Blood Merchantitos vs ??? Super Tengu vs Shinya Sasaki The Tokyo Express vs Badd Intentions Main Event!!!!! Elijah vs Survival ('j' denotes jobber) (The screen fades) ER: Wow! Super Tengu vs Shinya Sasaki. I guess they set that up after the comments Super Tengu recorded for this show at that hospital. AO: I like Super Tengu. He is not afraid to speak his mind. ER: You like him? The man is vile! AO: But he has style! And he is a great wrestler. ER: I realize that. But he's such a jerk! The way he uspet those kids was horrible! AO: He was simply teaching them a lesson in how the real world is. Now they are prepared. ER: I see. Well, why don't we let the fans see what we're talking about. This was recorded yesterday by our camera crew following Super Tengu (both men turn to the large screen again as it turns on....) [open camera at the emergency ward of a large metropolitan hospital in downtown Tokyo. The scene is hectic with people moaning in pain and some actually bleeding.] Doctor: We need some AB negative blood and get me some disinfectant!! Looks like we're pulling an all-nighter fols!! Nurse: But Doctor you've been working 23 hours straight!! YOU CAN'T KEEP THIS UP!! [Coming in through the doors is the all too familiar face of Super Tengu, brandishing a microphone and strutting] Super Tengu: Yes all you miserable sick people, marvel at the awesome glory that us Super Tengu!! To know me is to love me and to love me is only natural!! [kicks a gurney, with a suffering man hooked up to an IV out of his way] Ah silly sick person, do you not know better than to get in the way of the uncrowned SJPW Cruiserweight champion? [turns over the gurney] AARRRGGHH! Now that's better, the silly sick man is now out of my and I can go about my business and visit the children's wing. It is only right that the children have a role model like me... SUPER TENGU!! [a few minutes later Super Tengu arrives at the children's ward] Super Tengu: BEHOLD CHILDREN!! SUPER TENGU HAS ARRIVED!! Child#1: Look the Nurse brought Batman!! Child#2: COOL!! BATMAN!! Super Tengu: [rolls with it] Ah small feeble minded child!! I am not this western dog Batman, I am the legendary... SUPER TENGU!! To know is to love me and to love me is only natural!! [points to a kid in the corner] you boy, do I not know you? Child#3: Um... No? Super Tengu: Wait I do know you... Yes your mother showed me a picture of you while I was making love to her the other night!! She referred to you as 'the brat that my bastard husband made me have!!' Child#3: Huh? Super Tengu: [smiling] Don't you understand child? YOUR MOMMY DOESN'T LOVE YOU!! I mean how could she with you being the genetic freak that you are? You are more deformed than that pathetic Shinya Sasaki!! The man cannot only not wrestle but is truly a mongrel as far as looks go!! he cannot compare to my stunning good looks and natural charm and talent!! Child#1: Mr. Tengu... Super Tengu: THAT'S SUPER TENGU!! Child#1: Super Tengu, if you're so good lookin' why do you where a mask? Super Tengu: [laughs non-chalantly] A Child... If you were to see me without the mask you would become jealous of my beauty and kill yourself!! You see, no one is as beautiful as Super Tengu!! NO ONE!! [composes himself] Now who wants to see a puppy? All the children: YEAH!!! Super Tengu: [whips out some wallet sized photos] Here is my dog Jiro, he was a nice dog, I loved him very much... Child#2: Loved? What Hasppened to him Super Tengu? Super Tengu: [whips out another picture] THIS!! [the picture is of a mutilated dog, bloody as heck with it's ears bitten off] The mongrel tried to eat my dinner!! I SIMPLY COULD NOT TOLERATE SUCH DISOBEDIENCE!! [all the children start crying as they see the mutilated dog, Super Tengu just stands there smiling] Super Tengu: Well, my work here is done!! Remember children, when you are old and smell like mothballs you can tell your children that you met the Legendary Super Tengu!! The Uncrowned SJPW Cruiserweight Champion!! [Fade out as Super Tengu waves and smiles to all the crying children] (Both men turn back to face the camera) AO (chuckling): Super Tengu is funny! ER: I can't believe you like this man. He's despicable. How about "Tsunami" Rhiyoto Makada making his debut against Gregor Davinsky? The russian guy's pretty tough. AO: I hope Davinsky beats him good! I don't like pretty boy japanese wrestlers. Leave that to the americans! ER: Hookeey. Let's see what the "pretty boy" has to say for himself (both men turn to the screen again..) (The camera opens on a training gym. A large, black dragon mural is on the wall behind a wrestling ring, nautilus machine and various punching bags. Some men are being trained and the grunts as they rigorously go through their training are heard around the gym. The camera zooms in on a man in the corner who is doing gymnastics excercises on rings. He performs a twirl in the air before landing and walking toward the camera, a smile eminent on his face.) Young Man: Ah yes. The men from the wrestling promotion are here. I'm Rhiyoto Makada, a Tsunami. I am training now in the great city of Tokyo at the Black Dragon gym. Some of my countries top athletes have trained here and now I seek guidance for my ordeal. The ordeal is great and shall be a supreme test of my ability as a wrestler. I specialise in my aerial skills and love to throw my opponents off balance with a kick. Larger men heed my words, I may be small but then a flea can bite the emperor. I fight for honour and respect and for the fans. The great fans shall bear witness to my ability and will not be dissapointed. Now may you excuse me for I must go back to my excercises. (The young man walks off to a punching bag and starts to kick it as he leaps with extreme agility around it, dodging it as it swings back. The camera fades as it goes back to the Black Dragon mural.) ER: He seems like a nice guy. AO: Nice guys finish last. ER: Ahem. What do you think about the MPW match we set up Akira? Do you know anything about these Blood Merchantitos? AO: Yes, I do. These two are the most ruthless min-wrestlers to come out of Mexico. The are the mini versions of Canadaian Vampyre & El Luchador Violento, the Blood Merchants. These two men have terrorized CPW, the old UWA, and recently, HCW! The Merhcantitos have recently been seen in RFHW, where they beat up Mini-Ghost. I feel sorry for their opponents! ER: I understand they are very popular here in Japan. AO: Yes, they are. They have fought many battles here, so expect them to show no mercy to their opponents! ER: You really like people who show no mercy, don't you Akira? AO: Why not? Merciless men win more matches. ER: And speaking of merciless men, here are pre-recorded comments from Elijah, the Ultimate Physcial Specimen, about his upcoming match with the veteran Survival (both men turn once more to the screen..) the scene slowly begins to fade in from black. The camera is angled, and focused on a shot of an empty wrestling ring. On the outside of this ring, lays a small masked wrestler, motionless. Two men wearing black sweat suits that say Trainer on the back, grab him and lift him up and carry him off. The camera raises up and on the other side of the ring, climbing back in, is a large man, rippling with muscles. He wears some small, black, speedo-like tights, with black kneepads and white boots. He is well tanned, and appears to not even be sweating. He has short black hair, with a small black goatee on his chin. He appears to be very unhappy, and climbs into the ring and looks down at the men carrying off the masked wrestler] Elijah: Take that piece of trash back to Mexico where you found him and bring me some REAL athletes in here!!! [he looks around as a small Japanese man in a black suit with a collarless, grey shirt underneath climbs onto the ring apron. These two men are evident. The one in the ring is the self proclaimed "ULTIMATE" Physical Specimen, and SJPW Superstar Elijah, while the one on the apron is his manager and Japanese buisness man, Damon Onaki.] Damon: Ochitsuite! Calm down, Elijah. Ochitsuite! Ochitsuite! Elijah: Calm down? Damon, I'm the Ultimate physical specimen, a god amongst men, a shrine amongst so many ruins, a king amongst peasents! I was made to be FEARED, to be WORSHIPPED, and to be RESPECTED!!! But you bring me to so many places, that don't realize my greatness. One being this gym, I know you own it Damon, but there's no talent here. Damon: I've bought the best from Japan, Mexico, Europe, and the U.S. to train with you. I know of your greatness, but I can't find any worthy to train with you! Wakarimasu-ka? Do you understand? Elijah: Yes, I understand, perfectly! I understand that there is NO ONE in the world, that can compete with me! No one from Australia, where I've dominated for years, no one from the states, from Mexico, and surely no one from Japan. [he turns to face the camera] SJPW, you've realized my greatness and placed me in your first Main Event match, but against who? Survival? They say you are a legend, and that you are great. But I, the ULTIMATE Physical Specimen, Perfection in a human form, have NEVER heard of you! And after our match on Saturday, no one will EVER hear of, or from you AGAIN!! You'll know perfection, when you see me, and you'll know pain when you meet me in the ring! Damon: [turns to the camera as well] Survival, gaigin haku-jin!! Nani-temė, chikusho? Geso! [laughs] Why do you want to throw you're career away, by stepping into the ring with Elijah, the ULTIMATE physical specimen? KICHIGAI!!!! Baka-da! Because your career is now OVER!! [Damon turns around suddenly as he sees another victim coming toward the ring, wearing some bright colored tights and a blue mask, he hops down off the apron as teh other man enters in and charges in at Elijah only to be dropped by a hard ring and lifted up and sent into the ropes] [he bounces off and comes back fast and is leveled by a Spinebuster Slam from Elijah and is hoisted up again as Elijah presses him over his head, and drops him, catching him in mid fall and falling down with him, using the man's own momentum in a powerslam type maneuver. He then stands and walks over to the camera and leans down] Elijah: Soon Survival, soon, you will know the true meaning of PERFECTION!!! [he turns around and walks back across the ring as the camera fades to black] ER: No wonder he can't keep sparing partners! He treats them like dirt! AO: That is all they are good for. ER: It figures you'd say that. Well, Survival will have a chance at a rebuttall when Trash Talk comes out before we go live to Ring Wars. That's all the time we have for now. I'll see all of you before Ring Wars with a special edition of Trash Talk. I'm Eddie Rose. AO: And I am Akira Ono. ER: And we'll see you at Ring Wars! There are stacks of papers on top of the table. There is a video wall covering the entire back side of the set. Currently only a SJPW logo is shown on the screen. A caucasian man in his mid 20's walks in from the left. He's has red hair and is wearing blue jeans, a black T-Shirt with "SJPW" written in Red on it, and a black leather jacket. An asian man in his mid 30s with long black hair put into a pony tail and a goatee walks in from the right side. He's wearing a black suit with a white chinese shirt and black Oakley shades. They both sit in their respective chairs.] Eddie Rose: Hello everyone, and welcome to the First Edition of Super Japan Pro Wrestling's Control Center. I'm your co-host Eddie Rose. My partner in the Control Center is none other than legendary japanese superstar, Akira Ono! Akira Ono: Thank you very much Eddie-san. I am honored to be here once again, and to be a part of Super Japan as it recovers from it's recent troubles. ER: That's right Akira. Our first story involves SJPW directly. As you know, SJPW was supposed to debut with Ring Wars over a month ago. Everything got put on hold, as SJPW was bought out in a leverage buy out by Shinsaku Yasmashita, the new CEO. Mr Yamashita is the asian representative to a new federation called the Global Internet Werestling Alliance, or GIWA. They are acquiring regional promotions world-wide, and are subsidizing them under the GIWA banner. Super Japan was the first, and the European E-wrestling Federation, EEF, was the second AO: So what does all this mean, Eddie-san? ER: It means that SJPW is now part of a group of inter-connected efeds around the world. Each fed is independant of each other, and have their own championships. However, as part of the GIWA, all these feds recognize one World Title: The GIWA world heavyweight title (GIWA title belt appears on the video wall behind Rose and Ono) This title will be given to the winner of a world spanning tournament held in September, and wrestlers from all over the world, including SJPW, will compete for it. AO: And in the meantime, SJPW is in the process of crowning our own champions. The SJPW title tournament should start it's first round in two weeks at Ring Wars. Also this month is Battlecade 97, where our Battlecade champion will be crowned. ER: What is a Battlecade champion anyway Akira? AO: The Battlecade title is for the more extreme forms of wrestling, much like the kind of wrestling you see in FMW, W*ing, and ECW in the US. The matches have no disqualifications, and no countouts. Other stipulations are added in different matches. One match may be a strap match; the next a barbed wire landmine deathmatch. You never know with this belt! ER: Sounds extreme all right! Exactly how will the title be crowned? AO: The title will be crowned to the winner in a tournament. 8 men will vie for the title, so let the SJPW officials know who wants to enter! ER: And coming up this weekend is Ring Wars. We'll be doing the play by play live for you at this match. A little later, we will have a tentative lineup for the card. AO: Now we have a more important announcement to make! SJPW is considering dropping the stipulation that Gaijin wrestlers wrestle exclusively with SJPW. I think this is a bad thing, but I do not make the decisions here. ER: Well, it gives them a chance to grow. I mean, Survival, who has held world titles in 4 federations, as well as numerous other titles, has applied. He also wrestles in HCW/IFW. Should we deny him entry because of his commitments to them? AO: Maybe, maybe not. We will see how committed he is to SJPW before I decide how to respond. At least he has not decided to leave. Many wrestlers originally sighned to SJPW were released from their contracts. It is a long list Eddie-san. ER: Your're right. So far, SJPW has lost alot of talent. Masashi Lanther decided to stick to his TV and movie career. The Jade Dragons signed to another GIWA sponsered promotion in the US. Falcon, Hiroshi Tsunami, Chief Gray Wolf, The Rising Suns, and Badd Intentions have all left. Supreme Court and Jyurai canntot be reached. Mekaniac is on the inactive roster at this point. May others have not yet responded to our calls on whether or not they will stay. However, we do have some who are still here: Kenriki Ogami, Jarred Jones, Elijah, Shinya Sasaki, and Super Tengu. Along with Survival, and any others who are accepted soon, may wrestle on the first Ring Wars! AO: I like Super Tengu and Shinya Sasaki; both men know how to fight! ER: you mean cheat, don't you Akira? AO: Japanese wrestlers do not cheat. We are too honorable for that! ER: If you say so Akira. AO: I do! Now we have the tentative lineup for this weekend's first Ring Wars! ER: Excellant! Let's see what we have (both men turn to the screen. A lineup comes up shortly thereafter) Ring Wars, April 26th, 1997 Shinya Sasaki vs Takashi Fujimoto (j) Kenriki Ogami vs Sho Nakatoni (j) Super Tengu vs Gregor Davinsky (j) Main Event: Elijah vs Survival! Possible Midget Pro Wrestling match(es) ('j' denotes jobber) (Screen fades out) ER: WOW! Elijah, th eultimate physical speciman, vs the world reknown Survival. That is a main eventer anywhere in the world! AO: Elijah will win. He is in the best shape any wrestler in SJPW could be. ER: What do you mean? Survival is a multi-world champion! Elijah is newer to the sport of pro-wrestling, You gotta give the edge to Survival. AO: Elijah is hungrier; Survival has been to the top; Elijah has not. He want it more. Elijah will win. ER: I don't know where you get this logic from Akira, but you are the expert! AO: Of course I am. ER: Well, that's all the time we have for now. If the card is updated, expect a flash before saturday. And expect the next Trash Talk next monday or tuesday, so wrestlers, get in touch with us to set up interviews! For SJPW, I'm Eddie Rose AO: And I am Akira Ono ER: This has been the SJPW Control Center. Good Night. (the camera takes a long zoom at both men as the lights go dim, and the camera fades out)
