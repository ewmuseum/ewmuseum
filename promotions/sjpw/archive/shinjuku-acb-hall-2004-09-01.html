SJPW House Show — Shinjuku ACB Hall (9/1/04)

Sep 1, 2004, 12:00:00 AM
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Wednesday, September 1st, 2004 Shinjuku ACB Hall: 220 fans (sellout) Another small show before the big Friday show at the Shinjuku Island Hall prior to J*STAR! 1) Mr. Smiley Mask def Mascara Negra (9:29) after Sh*t Happens 2) Tsunami def Sangre Guererro (10:51) following a Fisherman's Falcon Arrow. 3) Despair def El Gato Loco (11:04) after a top rope Inverted DDT. 4) Masashi "Lightning" Lanther, Jiraiya & Takahiro KEOKI def "All-Star" Joey Darren, "Firefly" Horo Atashi & "Kyoto Tiger" Takayoshi Asakura (15:23) after KEOKI hit the High Impact Driver on Atashi. - Mr. Smiley Mask drove his Smileymobile into the ACB Hall to take on Mascara Negra. Smiley wowed the crowd with his aerial ability, but lost control of the match to break up a scuffle in the crowd between a Despair fan and a Blood Merchants fan, both of whom threw their drinks in Smiley's masked face! The crowd roared with laughter, but Mascara Negra used the opportunity to hit a corkscrew tope from the top rope onto Smiley outside the ring. He then laid Smiley's chest red with a series of blistering chops, then whipping him hard into the railing. He charged Smiley, only to catch a huge boot to the face, followed by Smiley taking him down with a lariat. Smiley rolled Negra into the ring, and executed a text book suplex on his opponent, then went for Sh*t Happens, taking his opponent down with his version of the Shooting Star Press. - Tsunami continued his comeback trail, taking down Sangre Guererro with a Fisherman's Falcon Arrow after avoiding the Sangre Mist Attack. - In the second confrontation between the Blood Merchants & the Disciples of Despair, the DoD proved victorious. Despair, angry at being pinned by Gato Loco, went on a high impact attack, hitting a few nasty suplexes on the crazy cat. Loco was able to come back with some hiptosses and finally a superkick for a two count. After hitting his version of the Tombstone (which is illegal in Mexico), Gato Loco went to the top for his Tornillo Gato moonsault, only for Despair to get up and nail him from behind. Despair went to the top rope with Gato Loco, and hit a devastating Inverted DDT from the top rope, allowing Despair to get the three count. Despair attempted to do more damage after the match, but Sangre Guererro made the save, blinding Despair with his Sangre Mist Attack. - The main event saw KEOKI & Team Raiden take on the trio of Asakura, Atashi & Darren. Asakura proved to be as aggressive as he was on the last The Zone!, taking the fight to Lanther with a series of stiff kicks. Atashi too hit some stiff kicks on KEOKI, who was able to catch his leg and hit a Dragonscrew. Jiraiaya then scored a two count after a top rope Senton on Atashi. Darren hit the ring, and scored a two count with his All-Star Frogsplash, but Lanther made the save. Lanther scored a two count on Darren with a Lanther Bomb, but Asakura made the save. Asakura then hit a sick Yakuza kick on Lanther, only to have KEOKI break up the pinfall. Atashi came back in, and nailed KEOKI with a spinwheel kick from behind. He went for an Irish whip, but KEOKI reversed it, catching Atashi in a Sleeper hold. Atashi tried to fight it, and KEOKI turned it into his High Impact Driver, pinned Atashi for the win. The fans were buzzing after the show. They discussed Smiley Mask's hilarious out of ring antics, Despair's devastating DDT on Gato Loco, and KEOKI taking out Atashi with the High Impact Driver. Fans were really excited about Friday's show at the Shinjuku Island Hall, expecting it to be a big event before the J*STAR Festival in America.
