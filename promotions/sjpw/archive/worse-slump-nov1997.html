No one is is a worse e-wrestling slump than?...

17 November 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[name withheld] wrote: > > This is the OPPOSITE for me. "Playboy" only works when I feel UP, not > suicidal... Strange, isn't it? This is definitely interesting. My theory: > Emotional characters need very emotional times whereas not so emotional > characters need less deep emotion to work... I don't think so myself. Since Dan has come into SJPW, he's been playing top notch. He's fine emotionally [imo], so I don't seem him needing emotional times to portray the character properly. Dan, you hit a slump. A long one, yes, but one nonetheless. In the short time you've been in SJPW, you've caused me to recieve so much feedback on your arrival. All of it positive. On top of that, you have handlers outside the orgainzation literally begging me to let them have just one match with you. To say that your reputation seems to remain untarnished after these low times is a wonder, to say the least. I'll be honest: Dan, I know very little of your days prior to the cage match in NOW. I just know what I read, and your retrospective in SJPW. I saw your comeback in IIWF, and I understand how you felt frustrated by the events that happened during that time. However, coming from the viewpoint of someone who wasn't there to see your glory days, I will say this: As a fedhead, you're one of the best damn signings I've ever made. You wanted only mid-card status, you INSISTED on that. You asked for no title shots, and for no spotlight on you. The only reason I hyped the contract signing at Invasion, USA was to help re-integrate Kyu Kabuki into SJPW. Sure, it hyped you too, but regardless, I needed a big way to bring Kyu back into the fold. This wrestler WAS going to be the first world champion in the fed, then [name withheld] left e-wrestling [thank god he is back!] prior to me starting work on the Super J Crown ppv. Lance Smith has more than proven worthy of his title, but Kabuki is the hero of the scenario, so to speak I don't consider Dan Kauffman, the wrestler to be a fossil. I find alot of enjoyment in writing matches for Kauffman, both because of the detailed sheet you gave me, the competance of your upcoming competition, and because you have busted your ass since before becoming an offical member of SJPW. One thing I've realized is that as a fedhead, I've been controversial myself at times. It's my stubborn Italian streak. But it paid off for me, by giving SJPW a reputation. I did it myself, and after months of busting my ass, I know have a staff. Sure, I can't match Cutter's ability to have every card out on time [and my hat is off to Cutter for being able to do that], or to make such a realistic card like IIWF Saturday Night is [like it or hate it, IIWF earned best fed last year] Anyway, my point is that you went through a phase, and now a new phase is beginning. You've got Anderson in GFWA again, and the rp's I've seen are good. Kauffman's tour started off well, and I've got more challenges for you than your tour schedule can handle. I was going to save this for off the ng, but what the fuck, I'll make it official: Dan Kauffman, I'm gonna make you an offer only Ed Dean and Blackline got as tour wrestlers: A full time offer to be in SJPW. I'm doing this cause you are one of the best roleplayers in my booked fed, and I want to keep you around. Why? Cause I can see your feuds with some of the people coming up would be more interesting if you stay more than 2-4 months. If you still wish to stay mid-card, we can do that too. If you still wish no title shots for awhile, then no problem with me. I know the current champions will breathe a sigh of relief over that! :) So Dan, your slump is all ready over. It ended at Invasion, USA. Get over it, move on, and go back to what you do best: being a good competitor :) [name withheld] SJPW Fedhead, and general pain in the ass
