Metro

17 October 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

The arena is dark and the place is packed. The ring is setup in the middle of it all. The camera pans up the luxury boxes where a broadcast booth has been set-up. Tania Goodwin and William Mercker can be seen and then the camera cuts to a rear shot of the two of them in the booth. Goodiwn is wearing a Seattle Mariners playoff jacket and her usual white pantsuit while Merkcer is wearing a brown suit with a white silk undershirt beneath.] Goodwin: Hello SJPW fans and welcome to Super Japan Pro Wrestling Metro coming to from the beautiful city of Seattle, Washington here on the SJPW American tour! We're glad to have you here with us as we start the big tour of my homeland, the USA. Tonight on Metro, we will have Nik Nyce in action, as well as Baby Hotta, and Onslaught all here with you tonight. Plus, we have EXCLUSIVE footage and news concerning the big Escape from Earth pay per view, coming next month from Los Angeles. Mercker: I hear 50,000 tickets have already sold. I wonder how Jinsei Tsumaru managed to scam his corporation into thinking that this was family day? Goodwin: Manditory family day. Mercker: Oh yes. I remember. Goodwin: [laughs] Let's go to the ring. There is a match or something. =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= Onslaught v. X-Treme (j) =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= SJPW Metro made its American debut before 20,000 fans and the first match was a good one. The big Onslaught took on the smaller, lighter, and faster man from Harlem, NY. Onslaught had trouble catching up to his opponent early on and X-Treme managed to get in a few good shots, including a dropkick combo and a flurry of flying forearms and punches. Onslaught took control however and put the match away after about 5 and a half minutes, winning with his finisher by kicking X-Treme in the stomach and jacknife powerbombing him almost all the way through the ring. Winner: Onslaught (w/ the Onslaught Bomb) Time: 5:48 [The camera cuts from the exit of Onslaught back to the booth. Goodwin is filing her nails and Mercker is talking on his cell phone. When the camerman signals that they are back, Goodwin puts down the file and slaps on her showbiz smile, while Merkcer closes the phone and, meaning to toss it on the table, mistakenly throws it out of the booth and into the crowd. He gets up and looks pissed off as he watches someone below take it. Goodwin sees this and cracks up momentarily before beginning to talk.] Goodwin: Whoops. Well, fans. We are just about a month away from the big pay per view taking place down in the City of Angels, Los Angeles, California. And on that pay per view, we will surely have THE match that no one else has ever had. This match will be a monstrosity. You want to see a horrendous cash cow, you tune in to Escape From Earth. Thanksgiving weekend, 1997. Be there and puke up your Turkey. [takes a deep breath] And, boy are we glad to be here in Seattle. You could see The Mariner's ERA from the plane! HA! Mercker: I take offense. Im a former baseball player and we're the only ones allowed to rag on them. Something else I'd like to comment upon. This situation with Alex Akira. Now, myself being a former pro wrestler, Ive been in several "supergroups" in my time. Ive been both a member and Ive ran one or two before, as well. And what I'll tell you right now that in all cases, the major downfall has been EGO. EGO tears apart the best teams in all pro sports. You tell me this, where would the 1980's Mets be if Darryl Strawberry had realized whom he owed for his career. But anyway, a note to the RoT, don't let your own goals get in the way of the team. If you're going to call yourselves a "unit", then act like one. Thats all I have to say. Goodwin: And he didn't swear. Thats an accomplishment. As always, after the leadoff batter, you have to send the power hitter to the plate. And stepping up the bat right now is Jay Estes in the Escape from Earth control center. Go to it, Jay. [The screen cuts from the booth to a black screen. Eventually, an electronic picture of the earth swirls into view and you hear a sound similar to the slash of an axe as the earth is incased in chain link fence and the words "Escape from Earth" appear on the screen as if they are scrawled in blood. The lettering begins to drip as the scene fades away to Jay Estes who is sitting behind a black wood desk in front of a black screen. He is wearing a dark blue suit with a white shirt and a black tie.] Estes: Thank you, Tania. Try and keep it in your pants while I get the people up to date on Escape from Earth. [Estes turns to another camera, this time from a side shot and flashes a showbiz smile.] Estes: Just signed for this colossal event is a heavyweight contenders match, pitting Ed Dean v. Mr. Big. This match is sure to be one hell of a fight to the finish with both men in it...to win it. Also on the horizon for this big event, is a big 10 man tagteam match. No word on that, however. [Jay turns back to another camera and flashes the smile once again.] Estes: What we do have word on now is that there is some EXCLUSIVE footage of the Escape from Earth cage being constructed. Let's take you now to the man who has got to be THE coolest broadcaster in SJPW. Go to it! [The camera cuts to Jay Estes himself who is standing in a large wherehouse. He is holding the microphone.] Estes: Thank you, Jay. What we have right now for all of you fans is a Metro original. You won't see anything this cool on Control Center or anything like that. This is a Jinsei Tsumaru brainchild and he's paying, or so I hear, over two MILLION dollars to build this cage. Let's take you on a little tour right now. [Jay walks over to a large piece of chain link fence. The fence has razor wire woven in between the links and the whole thing looks like a trellis that has been overgrown with vines. The light from the camera glints off of the wire and the razor sharp edges gleam.] Estes: [barely above a whipser] How'd you like to rub your face across that a couple times? [chuckles to himself] [Estes walks away from the fencing and over to a large steel box that has about 50 red and white wires running out of it. The wires are connected to a larger wire which leads to another section of fence. This fence is about halfway adorned with the razor wire and the whole thing is suspended off the ground. Estes picks up an empty beer can from the ground and throws it against the cage. The can hits the fence and sparks fly in every direction and Estes has to jump to get out of the way. Estes: [laughing in sort of a bewildered way] Would you look at that? Would you look at that? Where in the hell are they going to find four poeple who are either insane, stupid, or have no fear at all to get in that thing and actually fight a match? I say it can't be done. Back to you, Tania. [The camera cuts back to the broadcast booth where Goodwin is in a state of shock. She shakes her head and laughs to herself before she speaks.] Goodwin: Good GOD! And it isn't even finished yet. That will be a match for the ages, I'll tell you that. Jinsei Tsumaru knows how to get it done, let me tell you that much. Mercker: We're going to need ponchos and umbrellas for that match. For those fans sitting in the front 15 or so rows, don't wear white that day! Goodwin: I can NOT wait for that. That is going to be the social event of the year, in more ways than one. We can only hope that SJPW manages to sign the best Battlearts superstars to make this match the best that we all know that it can be. With the stars in the SJPW Battlearts league, this will sure to be an excellent match. Could one of the competitors in our next contest be on their way to the triple decker cage this Thanksgiving? Who knows? I'm going to stop talking and we're going to have a match. Go to it. =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= "Catman" Sung Kim v. The Black Fog -Bunkhouse match- =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= These two athletes locked up in a match where the one rule is that there are no rules. Both athletes were allowed the bring one weapon to the ring with them. Sung Kim brought along a baseball bat while Black Fog brought a steel curtain rod with him. Fog began the match by whipping Kim to the mat, but the Catman fought back and leveled Fog with a barrage of hits with the bat and took him down with a mean spike powerbomb. A spinning heel kick knocked Fog facefirst into the steel railing, which opened up a gash on his forehead. Sung Kim had the match well in hand when he pinned Black Fog with an Arabian Facebuster with a steel chair from ringside. Winner: Sung "The Catman" Kim (w/ Arabian Facebuster) Time: 14:26 [The camera cuts from the exit of Sung Kim back to the broadcast booth.] Goodwin: There ya go. Sung Kim with a hard fought Battlearts victory over the man from the deep sea. We wish Sung Kim all the luck in the world should he secure a spot in the big Escape from Earth matchup. And lord knows he needs the support because this match isn't for the faint of heart. So remember that we said that when you try and petition the FCC, or whatever about how he show was too violent. Remember that. Mercker: Remember that. Goodwin: Now that we have that said, we will now take you back down to the ring where Nik Nyce, The Perfectionist, is going to lock horns with the man from the UFC, Doug Thomas. Go to it. =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= "Perfectionist" Nik Nyce v. Doug Thomas (j) =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= Thomas, fighting for his first win in SJPW, brought all his skill to the ring and fought Nik Nyce with everything he had. The match went back and forth for over 10 minutes as both men pulled out every move they had and tried to out-do the other one with their best maneuvers. Thomas fought with every submission hold in the book and quite possibly made up a few new ones to add as well, while Nyce used the ring like his own personal classroom, launching submission attack after submission attack. Thomas battled and used every method possible to try and win the match, but Nik Nyce was just too tough for him. Nik Nyce put the match away shortly after 13 minutes with his version of the Texas Cloverleaf. Winner: Nik Nyce (w/ the Texas Cloverleaf) Time: 13:27 [The camera cuts from Nik Nyce exiting to the booth.] Goodwin: A big win for Nik Nyce. Mercker: Apparently his MAJESTY is too good to wrestle on the number one raited syndycated 1 hour wrestling show in the WORLD. And apparently Akira Ono is too stupid to acknowledge that. Goodwin: We won't get into that now. What we will get into right now is the subject of Escape from Earth. Just this morning, Jinsei Tsumaru sent down a memo that says we have to officially warn the viewing and attending public who will be viewing Escape from Earth either live or on PPV, that this PPV will contain mature content and should not be viewed by those who find excessive violence and gore objectionable. Parents should strongly consider wether or not this event will be suitable for children of a impressionable age. Thats about all it said right there. Mercker: [looking EXTREMELY disgusted] What the....? Why is this even necessary? If parents are going to let their little children watch that pay per view, then they need to look at themselves as parents! If your kids are watching barbed wire death matches, then think about why they are doing so. Is it because their mother or father is too damned FAT to get up off the couch and actually GET INVOLVED with their kids? Its people like that that ruin the good television shows and make people like Dennis Rodman feel they have to tone down their "volatile personalities". If your kids are looking up to boneheads who go into the ring and hit each other with barbed wire base- ball bats, then you need to think aboout what YOU the PARENT is teaching your kids. I swear to-- Goodwin: Ok, Rush. Let's calm down now. Are you going to let your kids watch the pay per view? Merkcer: No. They don't want too because they have a father who is involved with their lives. My kids don't need to look up to garbage match fighters. Goodwin: What is your point? Mercker: That if your kids are watching matches like Escape from Earth, it's your own damn fault. Thats my point. Goodwin: He makes alot of sense....I hope. What we have right now is the Metro main event. Baby Hotta v. Celine Amaury. We'll be back after the match with a final word. =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= Baby Hotta v. Celine Amaury (j) =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= Hotta went to the ring to take on the newcomer from Montreal, the aerialist Celine Amaury. Both women had alot to proove in this match and they both showed it early on. Hotta took control and never let up as she punished Amaury with a series of power moves and brutal kicks and punches. Hotta got back on the serious winning track by defeating her opponent her with a spike powerbomb. Winner: Baby Hotta (w/ the spike powerbomb) Time: 4:58 [The camera cuts from Hotta exiting to the booth.] Goodwin: Baby Hotta gets a big win here on SJPW Metro over Celine Amaury. Congrats Ms. Hotta and I can only hope that you have nothing but sucess against today's subpar womens wrestling roster. Good luck. [gives a rather fake "thumbs up" to the camera] Mercker: The final word for Metro is this.......to all of you SJPW fans who feel that we aren't "worthy" because we "just came in from the street" and got "nepotistic jobs", you need to get your heads examined. Tania Goodwin isn't just some shmuck off the street, she is some shmuck who busted her ass in the barbed wire rings of FMW for 10 years. Goodwin: He's right. Merkcer: And I'm not some loudmouth who came in from the bars in New York City. I busted MY ass for over 20 years in pro sports, and I do know my way around the wrestling ring. So all of you armchair critics might want to think about things. We've been there, done that, and beat the hell out of everyone. You THINK you know the sport better than anyone else. But really, you don't know a damn thing. Goodwin: He's right. Maybe you guys at After Dark should listen to him.
