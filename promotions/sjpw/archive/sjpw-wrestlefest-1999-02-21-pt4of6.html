SJPW Cards

Wrestlefest

21 February 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Wrestlefest Continues...] ER: We now take you to Jonnie Adonis, who will conduct an interview with the MLWO Japan senshuken, Jensei Tenru... [Jonnie Adonis is in the ring] Jonnie Adonis: Please welcome... [The pounding fury of Kodo drum music begins and the fans erupt into a *huge* explosion. After a few seconds, the grinding guitars begin the music becomes "Kamaitachi" by Sepultura.] JA: JEN-SEI TEN-RU! [If the ovation could get louder, it does when Jensei Tenru walks out wearing a black Matsuoko Dojo International shirt, Black jeans, boots and an arrogant smile. The crowd begins to chant "TENRU! TENRU! TENRU!" Jensei pauses and pumps his fists in the air in unison with the chants. He finally makes his way into the ring and slyly smiles at Rose] JA: Jensei... [Tenru cuts him off, grabs the mic and pushes Rose out of the way] JT: I think that each and every one of you out there, the guys in the back and everybody else have a huge problem. Look at the stellar card that Super Japan is putting on tonight. Up and down, the card is stacked. With the lone exception of... _ME!_ Now, I wasn't on the first Super Card and now I am not on this one. I don't know how anyone expects anyone else to make money when I am not in the ring. This isn't smart business. I am the _NUMBER ONE_ contender to everything here in Super Japan. Dean's belt, Takada's belt, Prince's belt even that phony Golden Orchid Belt. So, why is someone of my rank and caliber not on the card? Simple. Politics. The Team Corporate crew fears me, as they should. The bogus CEO neglects to put me on the card because I would, if I wanted to, take each and every Team Corporate head for my wall collection. Pathetic. Tetsuo Matsumoto needs a partner, I volunteered. He didn't accept. I volunteered to help Team Super Japan but they said I wasn't needed. I even volunteered to team with Takada and Extreme but they said they had "someone else" in mind. Sad. Pathetic. Economic Suicide. Mental Retardation. I don't know. All that I am gonna say is that Jensei Tenru is about to make his mark on Super Japan. If you think that my dominance was bad before, it will be awe inspiring and crushing. Do you know why? [Tenru doesn't even say it, the crowd in a deafening response does] Crowd: I AM _IT!_ ["Kamaitachi" begins again and Tenru plays to the crowd, tosses the mic back to Adonis, pats him on the belly and gets out of the ring. As he plays to the crowd, his theme music stops, and is replaced with the blaring sound of "Rising" by Stuck Mojo. A man dressed in a navy blue full bodysuitm with white Tsunami waves on his legs. His mask is also navy blue, with Tsunami waves on the sides. The eye slits are covered. The man has black elbowpads & boots, as well as a black kneebrace over his left leg. He has a title belt around his waist, and another one over his left shoulder. He glares out at the crowd, who start chanting "TSUNAMI! TSUNAMI!". He soaks in the cheers of the crowd, then raises a microphone to speak...] Tsunami: Hey Tenru! I'm sick and tired of your whining! You are it? What are we playing a game of tag? [crowd laughs]. Let me tell you something punk: You are a nobody. You think you're some kind of megastar cause you've been lucky to date? Well let me tell you something: You're a wannabe! [crowd goes silent] Tsunami: I came over here with Wild West Wrestling on tour, and since we're all one big happy family now in the NCWF, I figured I'd come by and say hello to some of my good friends. What do I see backstage? A pompous, megalomaniac spouting off at the mouth! Maybe my friends here in Super Japan have learned to ignore you by now, but I tend to be the impulsive type. [The crowd starts to murmur, not sure what to expect] Tsunami: So I tell you what Tenru, how about you and me tussle right now? You see, you *may* be the MLWO Japan senshuken, but the MLWO died not too long ago. Your title means nothing anymore. On the other hand, I am the IICW world cruiserweight champion, and more importantly, I'm the WWW cruiserweight champion, which makes me a top contendor for Lanther's belt. So if you got the guts, come swim through the unforgiving waves of the Tsunami! [The crowd "oooohs" at Tsunami's challege, then cheers as Tenru enters the ring again. Tsunami heads to the ring. As Tsunami nears the ring, Tenru runs to the corner, stepladders to the top rope, then flies off, landing on Tsunami with a corkscrew plancha! The crowd reacts loudly as both men land hard onto the runway. A referee runs to the ring, and signals for the bell...] ____ _____ _ _____________________________________________ / __/_ / / _ \ | /| / / / _\ \/ // / ___/ |/ |/ / Jensei Tenru vs Tsunami /___/\___/_/ |__/|__/__________________________________________/ ER: Holy! We've got an impromptu match here between Tenru & Tsunami! AO: Tenru may be biting off more than he can chew with Tsunami! The man once took Lance Smith to the limit! ER: Both men slowly getting to their feet, and Tsunami pulls off the IICW title. Tenru attempts a spinwheel kick, but Tsunami ducks under, turns, and nails Tenru with the title belt! [crowd "ooohs"] AO: Tenru goes down hard, and Tsunami drops his belt, and drives an elbowdrop onto Tenru's chest! He kip-ups to his feet, and stands Tenru up. He whips him into the side of the ring, charges, and dropkicks Tenru in the face! [crowd "ooooh"] [Tsunami hops up on the ring apron, and ascends to the top rope. He sets himself, perched on the top rope, while Tenru, shakes the cobwebs out, Tenru turns to look for Tsunami, who comes off the top rope, nailing Tenru with a spectacular spinwheel kick! The crowd rises to it's feet, cheering, and Tsunami gets up, and raises his arms up in victory...] ER: What a move! Tsunami is taking it to Tenru tonight! AO: Tenru may have finally met his match! [Tsunami stands Tenru up, then rolls him into the ring. He hops up on the apron, springboards over the top rope, but Tenru moves, and Tsunami crashes to the mat! Tenru rolls to the corner, and using the ropes for leverage, rises to his feet. He sags there, while Tsunami slowly makes it to his feet. Tenru charges, and catches Tsunami with a flying forearm smash that sends him staggering back. Tenru runs to the ropes, and comes of....] ER: TOKYO FIST! [crowd "oooohs"] AO: He caught him this early? Tenru drops on top of Tsunami for the cover... ...1... ...2... KICKOUT!!! [crowd applauds] ER: Tsunami kicked out of one of Tenru's most devestating holds! [Tenru gets up, a look of disbelief on his face. He drags Tsunami to his feet, hoists him up over his shoulders, then hits a samoan drop. He runs to the ropes, and comes off, hitting a snap legdrop across Tsunami's chest. He drops to cover once more, but Tsunami kicks out before the one count. Tenru looks up at the referee again, and complains that he is counting slow. The referee ignores him, and checks to see if Tsunami is knocked out. Satisfied that he isn't, he motions for Tenru to continue. Tenru drags Tsunami to his feet, and whips him to the ropes. Tsunami comes off, and Tenru hoists him up for a move, but Tsunami shifts his weight, locks his legs around Tenru's head, and takes him over with a rana! The crowd pops, and both men roll to their feet. Tenru charges, but Tsunami catches him, and spins him around, before dropping down into a vicious backbreaker! The crowd "oooohs" as Tenru flops around on the mat. Tsunami grabs Tenru by the arm, sits him up, then applies a magistral cradle. The referee drops to count, but Tenru kicks out at one and a half...] ER: So far, Tenru has held his own, but Tsunami keeps coming back whenever he gets momentum going! AO: Tsunami is a seasoned veteran. Tenru truly has his work cut out for him! [Tsunami drags Tenru to his feet, and hits a snap suplex. He gets to his feet, falls forward into a headbut, gets back to his feet, then hits a kneedrop to Tenru's forehead. Tenru sits up in pain, and Tsunami flips over him, snapping his neck forward! Tenru's body snaps back, and he rolls on the mat, holding his neck in pain. Tsunami gets to his feet, runs to the ropes, and hits a corkscrew quebrada! He gets right back to his feet, and lands a senton splash. He gets to his feet once more, turns away from Tenru, then hits a standing corkscrew moonsault! He hooks the leg, but Tenru kicks out at two and a quarter...] ER: Tsunami is hitting all these combinations, and Tenru barely can kick out! It could be over for him... AO: Even so, he's given Tsunami a run for his money... [Tsunami goes to the top rope, and signals that it's over. He turns around, and leaps off for a moonsault, but Tenru rolls out of the way! The crowd "ooohs" as Tsunami hits the mat hard, and rolls around in pain. Tenru gets to his feet, sucking wind, and looks out at the crowd. His many fans start a "TENRU!" chant, and he smiles. He turns towards Tsunami, who is starting to rise, and charges, catching him with an axe kick that sends him back to the mat. He drags Tsunami to his feet, sets him up in a pumphandle, then lifts him, and slams him down across his knee. He drags Tsunami back to his feet, double underhooks his arms, and hits another backbreaker. He makes a quick cover, but Tsunami kicks out at two...] ER: Tenru seems to have recovered from the beating he took earlier. His fans have energized him! AO: This man is so much like Tetsuo was when he was Super Tengu! He's truly becoming a phenom! [Tenru drags Tsunami to his feet, and hits an exploder suplex. He grabs Tsunami again, sets him up, then hits a snap powerbomb. He goes to the ropes, springboarding off into a back elbowdrop onto Tsunami's chest. He makes another cover, but Tsunami again kicks out at two. He stands Tsunami up, and whips him into the corner. He charges in right behind him, catching him with a lariat. He whips Tsunami to the opposite corner, charging with another lariat that crushes Tsunami in the corner. Tsunami staggers out of the corner, and Tenru grabs him, and spins him around. He kicks Tsunami in the gut, then spins him upside down for a tombstone piledriver. The crowd "oooohs", but Tsunami shakes his legs, and is able to make Tenru go off balance. He gets to his feet, and reverses the hold! The crowd "oooohs" as Tenru's head spikes into the mat. Tsunami makes a cover, but Tenru kicks out at two and a half...] ER: Once again, Tsunami has broken Tenru's momentum! AO: This doesn't look good for him at all... [Tsunami drags Tenru to his feet, and double underhooks his arms. He lifts him up, holding him vertical for a few moments, then dropping forward, driving him face first into the mat. The crowd "oooohs" loudly. Tsunami signals that it's over, and drags Tenru to his feet again. Tenru catches Tsunami with a european uppercut, and both men stagger on unsteady feet. Tsunami recovers first, and bounces off the ropes. Tenru catches him with a kick to the gut, and Tsunami doubles over. Tenru yells out "I AM IT!", and sets Tsunami up, He lifts him up in the air, and the crowd "oooohs", expecting the S.T.D. Tsunami flips off of Tenru's shoulders, and runs to the ropes. Tenru turns around, and Tsunami springboards off the ropes, moonsaulting behind Tenru. As he comes down, Tsunami catches Tenru, and drives him to the mat with a spectacular Inverted DDT! The crowd goes wild...] ER: TSUNAMI DRIVER III!!! AO: It's all over now! Tsunami makes a cover... ...1... ...2... ...3...!!!!! [crowd applauds] Nishi: The winner of this match....TSUNAMI!!!! ["Rising" comes over the pa, and the crowd applauds again. Tsunami wearily makes it to his feet, and walks over to Nishi. He grabs the microphone from him, and motions for the crowd to quiet down...] Tsunami: CUT THE MUSIC! Tenru, How does it feel to go down in defeat? [Tenru, only now recovering from the Tsunami Driver, sits up in a dazed state, and shakes his head.] Tsunami: At a loss for words? Well get used to it punk! This is the first of many losses for you. Maybe now you're realize that you're not it. No matter how good you are, there is always someone better than you. [Tenru still doesn't respond] Tsunami: Learn the lesson well, or we may just have to do this again. I've beaten better men than you, so doing this again will be a snap! [Tsunami throws the microphone down, and exits the ring. The crowd surprisingly gives him a mixed reaction, with slightly more boos...] ER: Tsunami seems to have some serious problem with Jensei Tenru. AO: I'll say! [The referee helps Tenru to his feet. A medical crewmember enters the ring, but Tenru motions that he isn't in need of assistance. Tenru looks out at the crowd, who start to applaud. He leans on the ropes, and shakes his head in dissapointment. The crowd starts to chant his name, and streamers start to fly into the ring. Tenru looks out at the crowd, and slowly smiles. He stands in the center of the ring, and bows to each side of the ring. The crowd cheers louder, and Tenru exits the ring, and slowly walks to the back, soaking in the applause of the crowd....] ER: Even in defeat, this crowd loves Jensei Tenru. AO: The man has acquired a cult-like following, much like Matsumoto did as Super Tengu. Can he rebound from this loss though? Did he truly learn a lesson tonight, or will he remain the same arrogant young man he has been until now? That is the question that needs to be answered now...
