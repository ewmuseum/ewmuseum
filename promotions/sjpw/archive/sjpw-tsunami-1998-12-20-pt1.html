Tsunami, Part One

20 December 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

Tiger Kikuchi & Akira Ono are seated at the broadcast table...] TK: Greetings everyone! I am Tiger Kikuchi, and this is Akira Ono. Tonight we have for you... (The crowd suddenly starts to give a loud but mixed pop as "Afterhours" by The Sisters of Mercy plays over the public address system. The lights dim, and aisle and ring are highlighted by white katekana symbols reading "Cleansed in Darkness") TK: "Oh no..." (The pop becomes bigger as a man steps through the curtains. He is slender, yet tall and athletic, muscular yet not muscle-bound. His long jet-black hair is flowing gently down to his shoulders. He is wearing a black satin shirt showing eleven white stars in an ecliptic pattern around on the front, with the twelth star being a golden shooting star on his forehead. In the middle is the crimson red katekana symbol for "giri". His silk pants are black and go down to his ankles, where they meet high emerald-green snakeskin boots. On his back is a long flowing golden robe, engulfing his entire upper body in a gentle hug. The robe is ornamented with tiny Japanese lettering, obviously hand-stitched and highly expensive. On his back are sheated katana and wakizashi, traditional long- and shortsword of the samurai. The most distinguishing fact about this man though is the strange belt around his waist, a golden belt with the image of a golden orchid in the middle and "Strength through purpose" written along the sides in Japanese lettering. This man is Chandrasekhar Yoshida) AO: "What's that belt? It what promotion is Yoshida a champion?" TK: "None that I know of..." (Yoshida majestically walks the aisle to the ring, then enters. He waits for the crowd to calm down somewhat, then grabs the house microphone) Yoshida: "I..." (Yoshida stops again as the boos and hisses become deafening. He shakes his head in deep contempt, almost sadly, then attempts to address the crowd again) Yoshida: "I came here tonight because..." (His voice drowns in the loud boos of the fans once more! Yoshida snarls in disdain, then climbs one of the turnbuckles and cockily sits down on the top rope, being careful not to crease his expensive robe) Yoshida: "I came here tonight for two reasons. First of all, I wanted it came to my attention that, despite being unquestionably the top drawing card on SJPW's pathetic roster, I have not been scheduled for any matches. Let me give you a warning to Jumbo and the other overweight bigwigs, I walked out on you once, I walked out of Gunyro-[BLEEP]ing-Pro, and if you guys don't finally treat me with the respect I deserve I'm going to walk out on you a second time. And we all remember what happened with SJPW when I left the promotion - it didn't." (Loud boos! Yoshida walks around the ring, grimly nodding to himself, then pats on the strange belt around his waist) TK: "What a shameless liar!" Yoshida: "I would have even put my new tsuihoonin championship on the line the Golden Orchid society and their oyabun have bestowed on me." (Crowd gives a baffled pop) TK: "Tsuihoonin? Outcast? What is he talking about?" AO: "Wouldn't surprise me if the Golden Orchid are one of those West coast Yakuza branches, it sounds vaguely familiar..." Yoshida: "Yes, that's right. I am the proud holder of a championship again. And unlike my last reign, the SJPW All-Asian Tag Title, this belt is a belt you cannot win by cheating, backstabbing, bribing, skullduggery or gerrymandering. This is a title I have been granted for my continuing efforts to promote budokhan and puroresu against all odds, for taking pride in being a tsuihoonin, a boumeishka, a man who is not afraid to step up and defend his values... and tonight, yes, right here tonight in this very ring I will prove that I am a fighting champion, and despite SJPW not wanting me to wrestle since Jumbo is stuck so far up Jaguar's [BLEEP] he can see what he had for breakfast, I will defend the Golden Orchid Society Tsuihoonin championship!" (Crowd pops, somewhat baffled) TK: "He wants to defend that bogus belt of his? Give me a break!" AO: "But against who?" (Yoshida, nodding to himself in self-satisfaction, walks around the ring and smiles slyly) Yoshida: "And being the champion that I am, I will defend this title against a true samurai. I am not talking about Super Tengu who should have remained buried when SJPW died the first time. I am not talking about old and grey Dan Kauffman. I am not talking about one of those pubertating Team Tiger boys with a cute name and a fuzzy mask. Fans, please rise from your seats as I introduce to you, the challenger! Representing the Wild Jamaicans, this is... RAS-TA-MAN!!" (Fans boo! Bob Marley's "Buffalo Soldier" plays over the public address system! The curtains part, and Rastaman steps out from the back. He is a dark-skinned, fairly well-cut Jamaican, his head in dreads. He is wearing a one-piece spandex suit in orange with the Jamaican flag on the back and matching boots. Rastaman cheers and pumps his fists into the air. Some fans cheer him, others boo, but most seem indifferent and proceed to yell at Chandy Yoshida in the ring) TK: "Rastaman? RASTAMAN??? For Buddha's sake, Yoshida can't be serious..." (Rastaman walks down the aisle, and Yoshida chalantly holds the ropes open for him to enter. He then bows to Rastaman, and Rastaman responds by bowing himself. Yoshida slowly extends his hand, and Rastaman eagerly shakes it as the fans respond with boos. Yoshida then raises the mike to his lips again) Yoshida: "I sense you are baffled, but that just shows your limited precognition. All you see is a man who is obviously of non-Japanese heritage but from the other side of the globe. A man from a country known for dreadlocks and reggae and a really crappy bobsleigh team. Yet what you fail to realize is that, despite all this, despite his heritage, Rastaman is training hard to one day become a samurai and has more honor in his little finger than most of the other SJPW roster combined." (The crowd is somewhat stunned. Some fans still boo while Rastaman pumps his fists) Yoshida: "Let me ask you something, Rastaman, how long have you been under contract with SJPW?" (Yoshida holds the mike for Rastaman to speak) Rastaman: "Yo, fer a little o'er two years naw, bro, know what ah'm sayin'?" Yoshida: "Two years! Two years of hard work, driving across Japan in a beat-up old Toyota mini-van while Jet Jaguar, Shinja Hamada and Super Tengu got the learjets. Two years, and how much airtime did SJPW grant you to express yourself in those two years?" Rastaman: "None at all, bro, zey never let me do an'thin but wrestle the undahcards, know what ah'm sayin'?" Yoshida: "In two years SJPW never let this up-and-coming samurai a chance to express his feelings, never once expressed gratitude for his hard work! NEVER! All they cared about was the sales numbers of their Jet Jaguar masks and that Thunder and Verhoeven's hotel room had a large enough whirlpool. Two years! Just imagine!" (The fans continue to boo!) TK: "Oh come on! This is sickening!" Yoshida: "But being the tsuihoonin champion, the champion of the outcasts, the exiled, the expatriated, the unjustly treated, I am giving you the opportunity of a lifetime! Rastaman, how would it feel if, just once, you could wrestle in an SJPW Main Event?" (Rastaman's eyes lighten up) Rastaman: "Zat... zat'd be... oh man brudda I don't know what to..." Yoshida: "Yes, Rastaman, you have heard right! Although it is not the last match on the card, _EVERY_ match with Chandrasekhar Yoshida in it should be a main event, and RIGHT HERE, RIGHT NOW, I will put the Golden Orchid Tsuihoonin championship on the line against you, Rastaman! This is your chance!" (Loud, mixed pop! Rastaman starts to jump on the top turnbuckle and throws his fists into the air while Yoshida unbuckles his bogus belt and hands it to the timekeeper. The referee calls for the bell) TK: "They are actually going to wrestle for that bogus belt? Give me a break!" AO: I can't believe this! Nishi Toju is making announcements right now! I can't believe Kobayashi is allowing this...this....MOCKERY to take place! ____ _____ _ _____________________________________________ / __/_ / / _ \ | /| / / Golden Orchid Society Tsuihoonin Title / _\ \/ // / ___/ |/ |/ / Chandrasekhar Yoshida vs. Rastaman /___/\___/_/ |__/|__/__________________________________________/ **ding** **ding** **ding** AO: "Alright! It looks like we have a bonus match, and what a great fighting champion Yoshida seems to be!" TK: "Oh come on! You can't be serious! Yoshida and Rastaman assuming neutral position... lock-up... ooooh!! Rastaman with a hiptoss, sending Yoshida down!" AO: Of course I'm not serious! (Crowd applauds, some fans cheer as Yoshida goes to the mat. Rastaman smiles and extends his hand, helping Yoshida back to his feet) TK: "What great sportsmanship!" AO: "Yeah, makes me want to throw up!" TK: "Yoshida and Rastaman with another lock-up, and the bigger and stronger Rastaman can push Yoshida into the ropes... the referee with a break! Rastaman immediately grabs Yoshida's arm and whips him into the ropes... no! Reversal by Yoshida! Rastaman goes into the ropes and comes back... Yoshida charges... Clothesl... no! Rastaman ducks it! Yoshida turns around... Short Running Shoulderblock by Rastaman sends Yoshida down!!" (The crowd applauds again as the unpopular Yoshida goes down once more) TK: "I have never seen Rastaman so focused before!" AO: "That's cause it's for a highly prestigious title, didn't you hear what Yoshida said?" TK: "Oh come on! Rastaman gives Yoshida time to get back up, and the two men circle each other again... Yoshida steps forward and grabs Rastaman in a collar-and-elbow tie-up! He can throw Rastaman into the ropes, Rastaman comes back... Dragonscrew Legwhip by Yoshida *crowd applauds* Yoshida immediately grabs Rastaman's legs and steps through for a Scorpion D... no... Rastaman counters with a Small Package!" ...1... ...2... (The crowd goes: "Ooooh!!" as Yoshida can barely kick out before the count of three! Rastaman slaps the mat in frustration of not having gained the three count, but jumps back up and cheers to the crowd!) TK: "Oh boy! Yoshida having more problems with Rastaman than I would have thought... Rastaman pulls Yoshida back up... high knee to the gut by Yoshida! Rastaman stumbles back, and Yoshida with a hard Muy Thai kick into the midsection! Another one, and Rastaman doubling over, Yoshida goes into the ropes and comes back and grabs Rastaman's head from behind... oooooh!! Plants him right into the mat with a face-first Bulldog!" (The fans go: "Ooooh!!") TK: "Yoshdia using one opening to bring Rastaman down, showing his killer instinct. This could be over right there! Yoshida grabs Rastaman's legs and turns the Jamaican onto his stomach... Cloverleaf Leglock applied! That's the Genyosha Deathlock, although I belief Yoshida may have given this move a different name since breaking from the Genyosha, we have to hear from that... Rastaman trying to reach the ropes, but Yoshida with great positioning in the middle of the ring... that's it!! Rastaman taps!" (The crowd lets out a disappointed groan! The referee immediately calls for the bell, yet Yoshida doesn't release, and the ring announcer grabs the house mike) **ding** **ding** **ding** Nishi Toju: "Your winner in 2:36, as a result of a submission... and still the Golden Orchid Society Tsuihoonin Champion... CHAN-DRA-SEK-HAR YO-SHI-DA!!" (Yoshida still does not release the cloverleaf hold! The fans start to boo, and Rastaman frantically taps the mat, visibly in pain. The referee starts to yell at Yoshida, who doesn't seem to care, or to notice for that matter) TK: "Rastaman gave it all but the more experienced Yoshida capitalizing on his rookie mistakes and... Yoshida won't release! Oh this is sickening and totally uncalled for..." (The referee is grabbing Yoshida's arm and tries to pull him away from Rastaman and to break up the hold but Yoshida is firmly keeping Rastaman locked into the hold! The Jamaican has buried his face in the canvas to suppress the pain, and his movements are getting slower) **ding** **ding** **ding** **ding** **ding** **ding** **ding** TK: "Oh please! First he is talking about honor and bushido and all this, and now this gross display of unsportsmanlike conduct! Look at this! I think Rastaman has passed out... this is so sickening! Someone has to do something!" (The fans boo and yell! Some debris is sailing in the ring, a very uncharacteristical move from the normally more reserved Japanese fans. The referee is gesturing to the timekeeper, who rings the bell anew) **ding** **ding** **ding** **ding** **ding** **ding** **ding** (Finally, Yoshida releases, dropping Rastaman's broken body carelessly to the mat. Yoshida grabs his Tsuihoonin title and holds it high into the air, being greeted by a thunderous concert of boos and yells. He points at the belt and himself, shouting something which is made incomprehensible by the booing crowd) Nishi Toju: "Ladies and gentlemen, since Yoshida-san did not follow the official's call and refused to release his submission hold, the referee had no choice but to reverse the decision and award this match, as a result of a disqualification, to... RAS-TA-MAN!!" (Some fans start to cheer but the majority still boos Yoshida, who seems absolutely unmoved by the referees' post-match decision. He buckles his belt around his waist again and seems to bathe in the crowds' boos, to drink them in) AO: "Hee hee, he doesn't look much like a winner here, does he?" TK: "Akira stop it!" (Yoshida shoves the referee out of the ring, then grabs the microphone again and drops down over the motionless Rastaman, yelling in his face) Yoshida: "You are pathetic! You're a f[BLEEP]ing loser! I gave you a chance of a lifetime, and you little gutless piece of Gaijin trash blew it! You make me sick, you make me as sick as all the other wannabe samurai pieces of [BLEEP] like Suzuki and Banshee!" (Yoshida, on all fours over the unconcious Rastaman, is slowly calming down, still heavily breathing. He slowly gets back up, then grins sardonically and addresses the crowd again) Yoshida: "I apologize. Yeah, I apologize for wasting your time with a piece of garbage like this clown. But I didn't come along tonight. No, I brought someone who has a little story to tell about this lousy promotion and the spineless pitworm running it along with his spoiled bratty Team Tiger teddies." (Crowd pop, loud boos) TK: "Who... who can he mean now?" Yoshida: "I want you all to rise from your seats and welcome a former SJPW superstar, you all used to cheer for her before you sucked her dry and cast her aside, I'm talking about the one, the only, the leading force in the former SJPW women's division... the Wildcat, SUSAN K'UANG-YIN!!" (The fans immediately start to pop as Guns N'Roses' "Welcome to the Jungle" plays over the PA! Rastaman still lies motionless in the middle of the ring. Some "Wild Girls" chants start up, and the curtains part. Not having been seen in SJPW for over half a year, Susan K'uang-yin finally steps out. She is wearing her traditional furry, tigerstriped two-piece she used to wear in the ring during her SJPW career. She is also wearing a pair of golden high-heeled Roman sandals. Her blonde hair is freely hanging down her shoulders. Around her left calf though is a soft bandage) TK: "Oh Dear Buddha! It's Susan K'uang-yin! We haven't seen her since her terrible injuries, first at SJPW and then during her WSIW career... what on Earth..." (Susan smiles warmly and slaps a hands as she walks down the aisle, clearly enjoying the contact with the fans. Chandy Yoshida looks at her, somewhat amused, somewhat displeased. Susan is limping slightly and is having visible problems of climbing into the ring, but Yoshida isn't making any moves to help her, earning him some further boos. Yoshida waits until the crowd has died down a little, then raises the mike to his lips) Yoshida: "Fans, I bring to you, once again, Susan-sama. Now, Susan, tell me. If my memory serves me right, once upon a time you used to tag with Jet Jaguar's retarded little sister, Misae... - " (Loud boos!!) AO: "Rose-sama will be very angry to hear his woman so insulted..." TK: "Shut up!" Yoshida: " - and since you carried their team, the future seemed to be wide open, yet it was not to be. Tell me what happened." (Yoshida holds the mike up to Susan's lips. Susan is swallowing hard, having visible problems to regain her composure, before she finally talks under Yoshida's merciless stare) Susan: "I... I got injured." (Crowd boos) Yoshida: "Yes, I remember. Not once, but twice. Susan K'uang-yin, the rising star, got injured. Now who was there by your side when it happened? When Astroman powerbombed you into oblivion? When Hitomi Yasuda wrecked several tendants and nerves in your lower back and left calf? Surely, your partner Misae Suzuki, the sister of the oh-so-great superstar Shoji Suzuki, Jet Jaguar... surely she was there to aid you?" (Yoshida's voice is dripping with sarcasm. Susan's eyes slowly fill with tears as she continues. The crowd is silent, somewhat shocked at the grotesque scene) Susan: "N... no... no, she wasn't." (Some boos from the crowd) Yoshida: "No. No, Misae Suzuki wasn't there. Where was she when you were in hospital, not knowing if your career could ever continue?" (Yoshida looks to the crowd) Yoshida: "Do you know where she was? I'll tell you where she was, her big brother Shoji pulled a few strings and got her bratty little sister a position as top drop in the RINGST*RS promotion, a position you should have had! Everyone saw that you had more talent, more charisma, more skills than that boring little tart, but when you have outlived your usefulness for Misae, she simpy dropped you. Cast dry and sucked aside!" (The crowd boos louder and louder! Susan can hardly suppress her tears and is swallowing heavily) Yoshida: "Yeah, go ahead and boo me! You just believe what you want to believe anyways! Come on, Susan, tell them! Tell them it's true!" (The fans continue to boo! Susan slowly starts to nod weakly under Yoshida's words, and the fans seem to be more and more in shocked disbelief. Yoshida grins in morbid self-satisfaction) Yoshida: "Hai. Shoji and Misae Suzuki. You call them great heroes. I call them great actors. Tell me, Susan, while you were lying in hospital, repeatedly being operated on your torn and broken body, who came there to visit you and care for you, was it Misae and Shoji Suzuki?" (Susan slowly bows and shakes her head) Yoshida: "Of course not! Shoji was selling Jaguar masks and making sure Misae could suck herself up the RINGST*RS ladder while holding teddybears and making cute interviews, pretending to be a teenie idol. So who else would have come? SJPW officials, whose women's division you carried?" (Once again, Susan sadly shakes her head) Yoshida: "No, they didn't come either! They terminated your contract, and you were left with a bunch of hospital bills and no one, nothing left in your life! A shattered career, a broken body, a heap of bills, while your former tag team partner sucked and f[BLEEP]ed herself to a championship. Now, why don't you tell those hippocritical fans who came and helped you out when all seemed lost? It wasn't Misae, it wasn't fat Jumbo... who was it?" (Susan slowly looks up with tear-filled eyes, then slowly grabs the mike) Susan: "It... it was you..." (The fans give a small pop, stunned in disbelief. Yoshida smiles and nods) Yoshida: "Yes, it was me. Yes, it was me who paid for your bills and made sure you got the best possible treatment. Yes, it was me who gave that pathetic moron Rastaman a chance he didn't use. Yes, it is me, Chandrasekhar Yoshida, the leader of the Golden Orchid, the champion of the exiled, the hero of the outcasts. It is me who stands up for those treated with injustice. This is why the Golden Orchid exists, we seek the truth and expose frauds such as the Suzukis and that rotten Team Tiger Circus. We promote bushido in it's purest form, not putrified by ridiculous names and stupid masks. Susan K'uang-yin, you are a true warrior and burned soul, you are an outcast, a samurai without a master. You are a ronin seeking guidance. I ask you, Susan, will you accept me as your master and join the Golden Orchid?" (Utter silence. For a long moment Yoshida's stare meets Susan's before the young woman finally bows, and a faint smile on her lips) Susan: "Hai. Hai, Yoshida-sensai, I accept." (Thunderous boos! Yoshida smiles grimly, then produces a golden orchid from his robe and hands it to Susan) Yoshida: "Then you know what must be done." (Susan nods, obediently, and kneels down over the fallen Rastaman. She rips his shirt open with her long fingernails, then places the flower on the bared chest of the humiliated Jamaican. The fans' boos are deafening now, and trash is sailing into the ring. Susan bows to Yoshida) Yoshida: "Rastaman shall be the first name in a long list of fallen enemies who oppose the Golden Orchid. You shall serve me well, Susan. However, there is one more thing. You are no Wildcat." (Susan looks at him, baffled, seemingly not understanding) Yoshida: "You are no circus clown, hiding behind a name. You are a warrior. I don't want to see any more masks or gimmicks. I don't want to see any more tigerstriped bikinis. Is that clear?" (The fans boo. Susan slowly nods) Yoshida: "Hai. Then take it off." (Shocked crowd pop! Susan's eyes widen as she realizes his intentions) Face: "He... he can't... or Dear Buddha, he isn't going to..." (Susan hesitates, then reluctantly removes first her bra top, then her bottom piece. The camera gives breaks out in a shocked turmoil, and the camera immediately visually distorts her crotch and her chest. Yoshida starts to smile, his domination completed, while the fans seem to be close to rioting) AO: "OH BUDDHA!! I DON'T BELIEVE THIS!! THIS ISN'T HAPPENING!! I... I... I can't believe... They're gonna fire him! Jumbo has to kick him out for this... this is... COMMERCIALS!! WE HAVE TO GO TO COMMERCIALS!! Hurry, get off the air! GET OFF THE AIR..."
