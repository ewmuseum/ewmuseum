Calling all former members and fans of SJPW: I'm back! (reply, 28 November 2001, 12:34 PM)

2001-11-28
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
*The sun sinks below the horizon, coloring the sky overhead with purple, pink and orange -- the colors of the Twilight. A slight wind blows across the top of the tall grass that covers the rolling hills, causing it to ripple like the waters of some vast lake. Upon a small rise, standing amongst the waist-high grass is a lone figure, familiar to some. He wears a long purple trenchcoat, which flaps open in the strong breeze, revealing his thin, yet muscular body clad entirely in black. His silvery hair is extremely long, and flows all around him, almost like a living thing. In his right hand, he holds a solitary white orchid, which he lifts upwards to his pale, handsome face -- his eyes closed in apparent bliss as he inhales deeply of its fragrance. After a few moments, he lowers the flower from in front of his face, and his large, violet eyes open and stare directly into the camera as a slight smile plays upon his thin lips...* Can you imagine a day without Twilight? A horrifying thought, ne? And yet, for some months now, the wrestling world has been devoid of the Twilight. For months, I have been waiting, wondering, what Destiny held for me. And for months, there was no answer. I had begun to wonder if perhaps I had been abandoned by the one thing I had been able to count upon my entire life. I had begun to feel as if Destiny had forsaken the Prince of Twilight... *He pauses, taking another sniff of the white orchid in his grasp...* But today, borne upon the wind, I have at long last heard the faint whisper of Destiny calling out to me once more. And along with it, I have heard mentioned the names of some of the men whom I most admire and respect. Names like Dean, Hamada, Suzuki, Asakino, and of course, Super Tengu. *The Prince smiles at the mention of this last name...* Naturally, I cannot help but be curious as to why I have, as of yet, not heard my own name mentioned. But, I can overlook what is obviously merely an oversight The siren song of Destiny is far too tempting to let such a petty thing prevent me from answering. *He takes another whiff of the flower in his hand...* At long last, Twilight will come. And perhaps, I will recieve yet another opportunity to achieve the Destiny that has been denied me so many times. Rest assured, if even so much as a glimmer of that destiny holds itself out to me, I will follow it to the ends of this, and any other world. Kore wa sakete, toore me, anata no unmei. Once again the world will see what it is like to truly enter into the Twilight, and perhaps once more the world will find itself... *His eyes close and the corners of his mouth curl upwards in a knowing smile as smells his orchid once more...* Forever changed... -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- Sorry!! I just couldn't resist!!! I've been having Prince withdrawal for a long time now! You can count me in!! That is -- if I'm invited along for the ride!! ja, ne -Alan-
