SJPW Card Archive

Tsunami, Part Five

20 December 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Tsunami concludes...] [The camera opens on Eddie Rose, standing at the runway entrance...] Eddie Rose: Ladies & Gentlemen, the CEO of SJPW... JUMBO KOBAYASHI!!! [The crowd applauds as Jumbo walks out, followed by Jonnie Adonis. Adonis has two title belts over his shoulders, and one more in his hands...] Eddie Rose: Kobayashi-sama, we understand that you have an announcement to make. Jumbo: That is correct Rose-san. As everyone knows, Hadi Moditan once ran Pro Japan Wrestling. PJW started off much like SJPW, then went to an all cruiserweight promotion in short order. At Moditan's request, a deal was struck that allowed then PJW International Heavyweight champion Sunjinski to compete in SJPW, and for his title to be recognized as an official SJPW title. Eddie Rose: Yes, we all know that. That's old news though. PJW no longer exists. Jumbo: Ah, but it does. You see, when Moditan-san signed that deal with Yamashita, there was a clause that stated if PJW folded, all of it's titles would revert to SJPW. Therefore, I have decided to re-activate the PJW titles as of this moment! [crowd "oooohs"] The winners of the Frank A Marano tournament will be awarded the PJW Tag Team championship, with these belts that Adonis-sama found in of all places, a PAWN SHOP! I guess Moditan-san was desperate for money to fund SHVF, ne? [crowd laughs]. As for the International Title, it will be decided in a special match between two men... [Suddenly, "Fly" by SUGAR RAY begins playing over the P.A. system and Kobayashi looks up in surprise. The audience cheers as a familiar green-and-white garbed figure appears in the entrance. The Banshee pauses at the top of the aisle for a moment, holding a large cloth bag in one hand as he waves at the fans with the other. He then jogs quickly down the aisle, walking over to bow toward Kobayashi. Extending a hand, he asks for the microphone and Kobayashi gives it to him with a puzzled expression on his face.] Banshee: Pardon the intrusion, Kobayashi-san, but I could not help but be interested in your announcement. You see, I was once a member of the PJW roster and I know something that you may not be aware of... [Reaching into the bag with one hand, The Banshee pulls out a shiny title belt with the PJW logo emblazoned on the front.] Banshee: ...there were *TWO* International titles in that organization! [The crowd murmurs in surprise as The Banshee hands the title belt to Kobayashi. Jumbo holds the belt in his hands and examines it closely for a moment.] Banshee: Sunjinski-san was indeed the PJW International Heavyweight Champion as you well know. But *I* won the PJW International Championship and have held that title ever since. If you would, Kobayashi-san, I would request that if you re-activate the PJW International Heavyweight Championship... that you do the same for my title. A Champion can only wear a belt if he deserves to... and I would be honored to defend this title against *ALL* comers! [Handing the microphone back to Kobayashi, The Banshee waves at the crowd, then heads up the aisle towards the back. Watching him go, Kobayashi has a thoughtful look on his face as he continues holding the PJW International title belt in one hand.] Eddie Rose: Well, Kobayashi-san? Will you recognize Banshee's title as well? Jumbo: This is an unforseen circumstance. Originally, I was going to announce a special match between two men for this title [points to the one Adonis is holding]. but if there are two of them, then I must carefully consider this. The Tag titles will still be awarded to the winners of the Marano tournament, but as for the International title, I....will have to think more on this. [Jumbo & Adonis head to the back] Eddie Rose: Well folks, it seems that PJW is back in action, under the direct supervision of SJPW! We'll keep you posted on the International title situation as it develops. Back to you Kikuchi & Ono! [camera pans to Kikuchi & Ono...] TK: Interesting announcement by Jumbo. AO: Hah! He gave it to Moditan there! About time someone did! TK: How can you say that? AO: Becuase I enjoy doing so? TK: You are incorrigible! AO: It is one of my best qualities! [Kikuchi shakes his head in disbelief] TK: And now we come down to the final round of the Juuryou Tournament and I can not believe that it involves Ed Dean! AO: The big man has carved a path through the competition and there's only Hamada to stop him from taking the gold. TK: Hadama-san can do it. Chiimu Tora will defend SJPW's honor! ____ _____ _ _____________________________________________ / __/_ / / _ \ | /| / / All-Asian Juuryou Tournament, Finals: / _\ \/ // / ___/ |/ |/ / Ed Dean vs Shinji Hamada /___/\___/_/ |__/|__/__________________________________________/ [The camera cuts to a shot of Nishi Toju standing in the ring with his microphone in hand.] Nishi: This contest is the final match of the All-Asian Juuryou Tournament and is scheduled for one fall and a time limit of thirty minutes. Introducing first, accompanied to the ring by Hadi Moditian, from New York, New York... weighing in at 355 pounds... ED DEAN! [Hissing and booing from the crowd greets the immense form of Dean as he paces through the entrance curtains with Hadi Moditian in his wake. No music plays and the arena is filled with the disapproving sounds of the fans as they express their displeasure. Ignoring them, Dean stalks down to the ring and climbs in, performing some last minute stretching as he prepares for the match. Dean once more is wearing a bandage over his nose...] AO: Dean is coming off a bruising victory over the mighty Onslaught as well as overcoming Ace Kilcannon only a few brief moments ago. He must be feeling the effects of the energy he's expended already. TK: As much as I dislike this traitorous animal, I must admit that his strength and power is prodigious. But he is not immune to the effects of exhaustion which will likely affect his performance in this match. Nishi: His opponent... from Osaka, Japan... weighing in at 235 pounds... SHINJI HAMADA! [The fans cheer loudly and throw streamers into the aisle as "Phaedra' by Tangerine Dream begins playing over the P.A. system. Hamada, wearing his gold, tiger-striped wrestling pants, white boots, white elbowpads, and black kneepads, pauses at the top of the aisle to wave to the crowd. Walking down to the ring, he slaps hands with fans along the way, all the while never taking his eyes off Dean in the ring.] AO: Possibly the strongest member of Chiimu Tora, Hamada will need to use his mat expertise to overcome the force of nature known as Ed Dean. TK: I have every confidence in him. And you can see both men are being cautious as they circle each other when the bell rings. Collar-and-elbow tie-up... no, Dean pushes out of it and runs into the ropes. Dean bounces back and Hamada hits him with an elbow across the chest. AO: Now Hamada runs into the ropes but gets doubled over on the rebound as Dean hits him with a kneelift. Dean with a headbutt followed by another kneelift. Now he grabs an arm and sends Hamada into the ropes with an Irish whip. TK: Hamada comes back with a running football tackle... oh! Dean staggers backward... but he doesn't go down! Dean traps Hamada with a front facelock... DDT! [crowd "ooohhs"] AO: Dean with an early advantage and Hamada cannot compete with him in the strength arena! TK: Hai! Hamada-san must use his submission techniques to wear the bigger man down. But he went down hard onto the top of his head and Dean is in control as he yanks Hamada upright with one arm. Dean now attempting to execute a dragon suplex... but Hamada ducks out it and performs a go-behind... AND HAMADA PICKS UP DEAN AND NAILS HIM WITH AN ATOMIC DROP! [crowd applauds] AO: Well Hamada is not as strong as Dean but he's no weakling either. Dean's tailbone was crunched down onto Hamada's knee and now Hamada trips him up so that the bigger man goes down on one knee. Hamada from behind... AND HE LOCKS DEAN IN A COBRA CLUTCH! [crowd applauds very loudly] TK: This is what Hamada-san must do. Wear down Dean's strength until such time as he is vulnerable to the Tiger Driver 2000. AO: Could he possibly lift a man as big as Dean over his head? TK: It remains to be seen but right now, Dean is desperately struggling to break out of the hold. Hamada really has the cobra clutch locked in tightly and you can see the difficulty Dean is in... but the big man manages to lunge forward, reaching the ropes to force Hamada to break the hold. AO: Hamada staying right on top of him as he turns Dean around and buries a fist into the midsection that drives the air out of Dean's lungs. Hamada with a front facelock... AND HE HITS WITH A DDT OF HIS OWN! [crowd "ooohhs" and applauds] TK: Hard-hitting action from both wrestlers as they both want the gold. Dean started off this match very strongly but Hamada has come back and taken him off his feet which is what he needs to do. AO: Dean is down on the canvas and Hamada grabs his arm, locking in a wakigatame. Dean gritting his teeth in pain as Hamada goes to work on him. TK: Both these men are known for their expertise with submission holds but while Dean is definitely the stronger, Hamada-san might have the edge on him in technical ability. AO: Dean in a lot of pain as he tries to reach for the ropes but Hamada pulls him back, maintaining a lot of pressure on that arm. The crowd is behind Hamada as they cheer him on. TK: Dean gives up trying for the ropes and instead rolls toward Hamada and twists his way out of that wakigatame. AO: Good defensive maneuver by Dean but Hamada was able to inflict a lot of punishment on that arm. Hamada continuing to get a good reaction from the crowd as he runs into the ropes while Dean staggers to his feet. TK: Hamada comes racing back... and he knocks Dean down with a tremendous flying lariat! AO: Powerful move by Hamada as he goes for the pin. The referee is there to count: ...1... ...2... ...shoulder up! [crowd "ooohhs"] TK: Hamada almost had him there as he's cheered on by an openly partisan crowd. Hamada a little frustrated but he remains on the offensive as Dean rolls onto his stomach. Hamada grabs one of Dean's legs... and traps him with an STF! AO: Dean's great strength and energy reserves are being sapped as Hamada applies different holds on him... but he's no slouch in the mat wrestling department either as Dean again manages to twist himself out of the hold and kick Hamada away. TK: Hamada right on top of him as he nails Dean on top of the head with a forearm smash. Hamada runs into the ropes while Dean struggles to his feet, bounces back... oh! AO: Dean catching Hamada by surprise as he charges forward and stops Hamada in his tracks with a clothesline! Dean now steps up beside Hamada and grabs him... and takes him down with a sidewalk slam! TK: Hamada may have lost control of the match right there as Dean hammered him into the canvas. But Dean looks both tired and sore as he takes his time pulling Hamada to his feet. AO: Dean tries to take Hamada down with a snap suplex but Hamada hooks the ankle and blocks it. Dean with a go-behind, grabs Hamada and takes him off his feet with a waistlock takedown. TK: Hamada now in trouble as Dean slaps on an armbar. And Dean needs a chance to recover from some of the punishment that Hamada has inflicted on him so this is a wise strategy. AO: You are complimenting Ed Dean? TK: I am giving credit where it is due, Akira-san. I may dislike the man, personally, but I respect his ability. AO: Hamada grimacing from that armbar but he manages to swivel around on his side and hook his foot over the bottom rope. The referee forces Dean to release the hold and back away as Hamada regains his feet by pulling himself up with the ropes. TK: The two surge together for a lockup but Dean grabs an arm and sends Hamada for a ride with an Irish whip. Hamada bounces back off the ropes and is taken off his feet by a hiptoss from Dean. AO: Dean now finding his second wind as he steps up behind Hamada as he sits up on the mat and pummels him over the head with a series of forearm strikes. Dean grabs Hamada from behind and pulls him to his feet, locking on a full nelson. TK: And Dean's size and strength make this hold a painful submission maneuver as you can see Hamada valiantly trying to break the hold. Hamada struggling to reach the ropes but Dean too strong to let him get close enough. AO: Hamada fighting the pain as he reaches up... grabs the sides of Dean's head... JAWBREAKER! [crowd "ooohhs"] TK: And that counter-move got Hamada out of trouble as he dropped to the mat and pulled Dean's jaw down onto the top of his skull. Hamada back up quickly, pivoting in place to catch Dean in the face with a back elbow. AO: Hamada now going after that arm he worked on earlier as he slaps on a standing wristlock. Dean scowling, looking to reach the ropes but he's too far away. Hamada avoids a counter by Dean as he releases the arm but immediately traps him in a side headlock. TK: Hamada not allowing Dean enough time to gather any momentum as he ducks underneath and picks the bigger man up... POWERSLAM! AO: Now *THAT* was an impressive display of strength by Hamada as he goes for another pin: ...1... ...2... ...kickout!!! [crowd "ooohhs"] TK: Dean not having an easy time of it with Shinji Hamada who has come close to winning this bout twice now! AO: But it's not over yet, Kikuchi-san! Hamada is looking good, however, as he spins around behind Dean... and catches him in a Dragon sleeper! TK: This could be the end of it as Dean is struggling to reach the ropes. But he can't make it as Hamada pours on the pressure. AO: The crowd is cheering Hamada on as this just might be it! The referee checking on Dean who's refusing to submit but you can tell that he's weakening. The referee picks up Dean's arm as it looks like the big man is out... TK: And the arm drops! Hamada still applying pressure with that sleeper and Dean may be out of it! The referee picks up his arm... and it drops a second time! AO: We may have a new champion here if Hamada can keep this up. The referee picks up Dean's arm for the third time... but it stays up! TK: Hamada can't believe it as Dean is fighting back again! Dean starts rocking back and forth and Hamada is still trying to keep that sleeper hold on... and Dean breaks loose! AO: Amazing! Dean's strength reserves seem to know no bounds as he was able to fight his way out of that hold. But it took an awful lot of him as he's helpless to prevent Hamada from slapping on another side headlock. TK: Dean pushes him off, however, and sends Hamada running into the ropes. Hamada rebounds back... OH! AO: Unbelievable power on the part of Dean as he nearly took Hamada's head off with a running lariat! TK: Hamada is down but Dean is unable to follow up as he staggers into the ropes, fighting to remain upright. AO: Both men have been through exhausting matches already and they've really taken it to each other. Thus far, I'd say that Hamada has had the better go of it thus far but Dean has shown that he's still in this one. TK: The crowd is definitely behind Hamada all the way but he's in trouble as Dean grabs a handful of hair and pulls him to his feet. Dean steps up behind him... and executes a release German suplex on Hamada! AO: Beautiful move by Dean and it flung Hamada down onto the back of his head. Dean moving slowly but he's methodically demolishing Hamada as he pulls him up once again and flings him over onto his back with a gutwrench suplex. TK: Hamada being pummeled as Dean pulls him up once more and sends him into the ropes with an Irish whip. Dean drops his head for a backdrop... too early! AO: Dean showing how tired he is by making a classic error as Hamada stops short and clobbers him across the back of the head with a forearm smash. And just like that, the tide turns once more Hamada pivots around behind Dean... AND NAILS HIM WITH A FULL NELSON SLAM! [crowd applauds] TK: The crowd is going into a frenzy as Hamada hooks the leg: ...1... ...2... ...3... shoulder up! [crowd "ooohhs"] AO: Very close call as Hamada takes control of the match once again. But he's moving very slowly now as he's still feeling the effects of that release German suplex and the gutwrench suplex. TK: An extraordinary match so far as both men are taken to the very limit. Both men back on their feet, looking the worse for wear, and Hamada dives forward, using a single-leg takedown to knock Dean off his feet. AO: And Hamada is going for an anklelock... he's got it! Dean is roaring in agony as Hamada twists his foot around in ways it was never meant to go. The crowd is cheering loudly... but Dean manages to pull himself over and grab the bottom rope, forcing Hamada to release the hold. TK: The pace of the match has really dropped off now as both men are wrestling hurt. Dean shaking out his ankle as he pulls himself upright with the aid of the ropes. Hamada doesn't give him much time to recover as he whips Dean into the ropes... AO: No! Dean reverses it, catches Hamada on the rebound... and hammers him into the canvas with a devastating powerslam! TK: Dean with his first pin attempt of the match: ...1... ...2... ...kickout!!! [crowd "ooohhs"] AO: The momentum swings back in the other direction as Dean is visibly limping but he pulls Hamada to his feet and stuns him with a series of forearm strikes to the head. He follows that up with a headbutt on Hamada, grabs him... BELLY-TO-BELLY SUPLEX! [crowd "ooohhs"] TK: We are starting to run out of time and this could go to a draw if neither man scores a pin. But Dean hit with a lot of impact on Hamada with that last move as he grabs the arm... AND SLAPS ON THE JUJI GATAME CROSS ARMBREAKER! AO: Hamada is in agony as Dean has trapped him in his signature submission hold! Hamada valiantly resisting but he's too far from the ropes to escape. TK: This does not look good as Hamada continues trying to break the hold but his arm could suffer serious damage. He summons one last burst of energy to try and break free... but he can't do it! AO: The referee is checking on Hamada and he may be seriously injured if he's not careful... Hamada submits! **ding** **ding** **ding** Nishi: Here is your winner and new All-Asian Juuryou Champion... ED DEAN!!! [a disappointed groan rises from the crowd] TK: I can't believe it! Dean is the champion!! AO: And it came right down to the wire, Kikuchi-san, as the watch was stopped at 29:02. Hamada came very close several times but the victory belongs to Dean. [Jumbo Kobayashi & Jonnie Adonis come out from the back, and head to the ring. Moditan enters the ring, and he & Dean high five each other. Jumbo enters the ring, his head held low. Adonis enters behind him, holding the new All-Asian title belt. The ring attendants bring in the trophy. Jumbo takes the trophy, and hands it to Dean. Dean smiles at Jumbo, then sets it aside as Jumbo reluctantly hands him the All-Asian title belt. The rest of Chiimu Tora come out, and check on Hamada. Banshee & Takizawa help Hamada to his feet, then hand him out to Nishida & Onslaught. Dean makes some comment towards Hamada, and Fujiwara makes a move towards Dean, But Jet Jaguar II restrains him. Dean bows mockingly towards Fujiwara, and says "You're next kid. Better save that for next week!" Jaguar II gets Fuiwara out of the ring, and all of Chiimu Tora head towards the back, the crowd applauding Hamada for his efforts. Dean holds the All-Asian title over his head, then hands it to Moditan, who grinning at Kobayashi, places it around Dean's waist. Dean hoits Moditan up onto his shoulders, then hands him the trophy. Moditan holds it over his head, and the two men parade around the ring, celebrating their victory...] TK: This is a sad night for Super Japan. Tonight, a traitor wins the most prestigious belt in this promotion, and will defend it in the name of a rival federation! AO: Hopefully next week, Shishi Fujiwara can defeat Dean, and bring the title back to Chiimu Tora! TK: Maybe. I hope so. We are out of time. For Akira Ono, I am Tiger Kikuchi. We will see you again soon. [As the credits start to roll, the camera zooms in on Dean & Moditan celebrating in the ring, then fades out...] _____ _____ _____ __ __ /\ _ `\ /\___ \ /\ _ `\ /\ \ __/\ \ \ \ \_\_\ \/__/\ \ \ \ \_\ \ \ \ \/\ \ \ \ \/` ____\ _\ \ \ \ \ ,__/ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ ` /\ \_\ \ /\ \_\ \ \ \ \/ \ \ \_/ \_\ \ \ \_____\ \ \____/ \ \_\ \ `\___ ___/ \/_____/ \/___/ \/_/ '\/__//__/ © 1998 http://www.calnan.com/sjpw/
