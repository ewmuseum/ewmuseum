SJPW Card Archive

Control Center, Part One

5 December 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The scene dissolves from black to reveal a small room with three empty stools in the center of the floor. On the back wall, a large, tiger striped curtain has been hung with the Japanese kanji characters spelling "CHIIMU TORA" prominently displayed across its surface. From the left side of the room, three men walk into view and take seats on each of the stools. The men are in their wrestling gear complete with masks and are recognizable as Shishi "Lion" Fujiwara, Jet Jaguar II and The Banshee. Fujiwara and Jaguar settle themselves on their stools while The Banshee stands and takes a step toward the camera. As he begins speaking in English, instant translation is provided with subtitles in both Japanese and Spanish.] Banshee: Greetings from members of the Chiimu Tora. My comrades and I have decided that considering Sensou no Jidai is fast approaching, we should address the matter of our trios match against The Daimyo [He pauses for a moment, gathering his thoughts.] Banshee: My feelings toward The Kabuki Kid are well known and they are the same in reference to The Daimyo as a whole. I have always respected the technical ability of all of its members but their attitude and lack of honor disgusts me. I have faced The Kabuki Kid not only in this organization but in other federations and I have overcome him every time. How could that be? Am I such a superior wrestler that I can beat him easily? No... the truth is that every time The Kabuki Kid and I step into the ring, it could go either way. He is one of the best cruiserweights in the business and defeating him is not easy... until now. You see, his attitude change has undermined him... weakened him... taken away the technical greatness that he once had. This is a lesson that Kensei Tokugawa and Kagemusha would do well to learn. [The Banshee steps back and sits on the empty stool while Jet Jaguar II rises and steps forward. As he begins speaking in Japanese, subtitles appear in English and Spanish translating his words.] Jaguar II: Daimyo... the struggles between you and the Chiimu Tora have been great. Intense clashes that have taken their toll on all involved. However, now the fight continues, at an even higher level. The battle will be fierce and none of us shall be the same when it is done. Like the clashes of old, warriors on both sides unwilling to budge, to surrender, driven to the breaking point. So it will be at Sensou no Jidai. Only the brave of heart, the strong of body, and the calm of spirit will stand victorious in the end. We are all these things... Are you? [Jet Jaguar II gives a shallow bow toward the camera and steps back as Fujiwara steps forward. Again, he speaks in Japanese with English and Spanish subtitles appearing at the bottom of the screen.] Fujiwara: Kagemusha... The mist you blow doesn't blind me to the truth. The truth is you hide behind your illusions and backfighting because you aren't man enough to face me man to man. At Sensou no Jidai you would be well advised to come to the ring void of your tricks and filled with what little courage and honor you possess. [Fujiwara pauses to brush the hair out of his face...] Fujiwara: Always remember that mind games are meant for two and turn about is fair play. Tiger Kikuchi & Akira Ono are sitting at the table, talking...] Tiger Kikuchi: Good evening everyone. I am Tiger Kikuchi, and this is Akira Ono. Tonight, we take a look at Sensou no Jidai, the first supercard for Super Japan Pro Wrestling. This supercard should prove to be an exciting event. Akira Ono: I'll say, though it will be strange not having Super Tengu there. TK: Why is that? AO: Because SJPW has always had Tengu at most of it's supercards. TK: Then I guess we break with tradition, ne? [Smiles]. Sensou no Jidai is tomorrow night in Yokohama Arena. AO: It should be an interesting card, and the main event pits two men who are new to SJPW to decide the first Welterweight champion: Tasogare no Prince, and Yukio "The Warbird" Murakami! TK: Let's take a look at the lineup for [Both men turn to face the screen behind them, which has the SJPW logo on it. The logo dissapears, replaced by a blank screen for a moment, which is then replaced with: SJPW Sensou no Jidai [Sunday, December 6th], Yokohama Arena, Yokohama, Japan Koubousen Geijutsu mini-tournament, Round One: The Mandarin vs Steel Serpent Chiimu Tora [Banshee, Shishi Fujiwara & Jet Jaguar II] vs The Daiymo [Kagemusha, Kabuki Kid & Kensei Tokugawa] Rupan Loco & The Young Warriors vs Ace Kilcannon & The Outlaws Koubousen Geijutsu Title Match: Mitsunori Takizawa vs Toshiaki "The Pyro" Takada [champion] 8 Man Tag Team Match: The Bonecrushers/Tokyo Express vs Ishida/Usami & The Yakuza Main Event: Welterweight Title Tournament finals: Tasogare no Prince vs Yukio "The Warbird" Murakami. [The screen goes blank once more, and both men turn back to face the camera...] AO: This should be a strong show for SJPW. I mean, look at the undercard! Mandarin vs Steel Serpent! That match should be as thrilling as Mandarin vs Tiger Claw was! TK: I agree. Then we have the two Trios matches. The Chiimu Tora/Daiymo match should be interesting, since we are still unsure if Jet Jaguar II or Kabuki Kid will be able to compete for their teams. AO: I think the Trios match between Rupan Loco & The Young Warriors against Ace & The Outlaws should be interesting. The Young Warriors are on a roll, and Rupan Loco seems destined for glory! TK: Then your old partner, Mitsunori Takizawa, battles Toshiaki Takada for the Koubousen Geijutsu title. AO: If Takizawa wins, Chiimu Tora will finally hold some gold, eh Kikuchi-sama? TK: True, though it really isn't the first title I'd like to see them hold... AO: Of course, this eight man tag team match should be interesting, as both sides don't fully trust each other. TK: Very true. The Tokyo Express seem to have changed their ways, and this alliance with The Bonecrushers is tense at best. Ishida & Usami are not trusted by The Yakuza, who took this match only to get their hands on The Bonecrushers at long last. AO: Last, but far from least, our main event: Tasogare no Prince & Yukio "The Warbird" Murakami. Two men, one title. Who will be crowned as our first Welterweight champion? TK: That one is hard to pick, but I feel Tasogare no Prince will prevail. AO: I lean more towards Murakami myself. TK: But he's slightly injured. AO: True, but that will make him that more determined to win. TK: An interesting point. We have some excellant matches tonight, as well as two pre-taped matches, which feature Shinji Hamada & Greg "Millionaire" Miller in the first round of the Twin Cities Invitational tournament! AO: And Jumbo has a special announcement to make later... TK: I wonder what it concerns? AO: I've heard rumours it concerns filling the Juuryou tournament slot vacated when Tenru, Tenru & Tengu had that three way battle a few weeks ago. I also heard it involves someone who is in Chiimu Tora... TK: Interesting. I wonder who it could be? AO: I would like to know that as well. TK: Our first contest should be a truly great one, Ono-san. "Flying Dragon" Jushin Kojima versus Hori Yatoro. AO: Hoonto desu ne? Both are promising young athletes here in SJPW. Although, I must admit, I know little of Yatoro-san. TK: Soo. Kojima made an impressive showing in the first round of the Welterweight tournament against one of the men who will be competing in the final match of that tournament tomorrow evening at Sensou no Jidai, Tasogare no Prince. However, since that time, he's been relatively quiet in SJPW. Yatoro has also been rather quiet ever since Kobayashi-san re-opened SJPW. I'm looking forward to seeing these two athletes show us what they are capable of here tonight. AO: Hai! Hajimemashoo, ne? TK: Hai! Let's go on up to Nishi Toju and referee Owada Botan in the ring!! ____ _____ _ _____________________________________________ / __/_ / / _ \ | /| / / / _\ \/ // / ___/ |/ |/ /"Flying Dragon" Jushi Kojima vs Hori Yatoro /___/\___/_/ |__/|__/__________________________________________/ [The camera pans to the ring, where Nishi Toju & Owada Botan are standing. The crowd is cheering. Nishi waits for the crowd to die down, then raises his mike to speak...] Nishi: Minna-san, our next match is scheduled for one fall with a ten minute time limit. Your referee for this contest is Owada Botan... [Botan takes a quick bow as the fans politely applaud...] NT: Introducing first... [Prodigy's "Firestarter" plays loudly over the PA] NT: From Yokohama prefecture, standing 5 feet 10 inches tall, and weighing 223 pounds... "THE FLYING DRAGON" JUSHIIIIIINNNN KOOOOOOJJJJIIIIIIIIIIIIMMMAAAAAAAA!!!!!!! [The arena lights go out and Jushin steps out from behind the curtain and hold his flaming wand up in the air black and green strobe lights begin to go off around the arena. He wears a green jacket with scales airbrushed on them, and on the back of the jacket the words "Flying Dragon" are airbrushed in gold. His long wrestling tights are black trimmed in green, on the left leg the word "Flying" is airbrushed in green, and on the right leg "Dragon" is airbrushed in green as well. Jushin wears knee length black boots with shin pads, on each shin pad the word "Dragon" is written. He wears a mask similar to the one Juventud Guererra wore, which covers his face only letting his hair flow out from the top. On his mask a picture of a dragon wraps itself around the entire mask. Jushin takes his time coming down to the ring, once he gets to the ring the strobe lights stop flashing, and Jushin climbs the top rope and begins to spit fire.] TK: Quite an impressive entrance by Jushin Kojima, eh, Ono-san? AO:Hmph. I suppose. I prefer to be impressed with in-ring ability. These fancy entrances are becoming ridiculous! NT: And his opponent.... ["Let It Go" by Loudness blares through the PA] NT: From the City of Hiroshima... Standing 5 feet, 7 inches tall and weighing in at 228 pounds... HOOOOOOOOOOOOOORRRIIII YAAAAATTOOOOORRROOOOOOOOO!!!!!!!!!!! [Walks calmly to the ring, slapping hands with the fans. He betrays no emotion, and keeps his eyes on his opponent at all times. Yatoro wears blue trunks, red boots & kneepads. He climbs up into the ring as the thousands in attendance politely applaud both competitors...] AO: Ah! A very simple, straightforward entrance from Yatoro! Perhaps he plans on letting his ability in the ring speak for him? TK: Perhaps. I'm sure both men will do their very best here in this bout this evening. Now Nishi Toju has left the ring, and Botan-san is signalling for the bell to be rung. *ding-ding-ding* TK: Kojima starts off the match aggresively by CHARGING at Yatoro... JAPANESE ARM-DRAG by Yatoro!!!! AO: Now Yatoro quickly back up to his feet! He SCOOPS up Kojima... SLAMS him hard to the mat!!! Now Yatoro comes off the ropes... ELBOW DROP!!! TK: Hori Yatoro is off to a very quick start!! But Kojima is managing to get back up to his feet... IRISH WHIP by Yatoro... REVERSED by Jushin Kojima!!! AO: Yatoro comes off the ropes...Kojima catches him with a BOOT to the midsection!!! TK: Now Kojima with a SNAP MARE takes Yatoro down to the mat! Kojima is back to his feet... Yatoro sitting up now as Jushin Kojima comes off the opposite rope with a DROPKICK to the face of Hori Yatoro!!!! AO: And now Kojima tops it off with a STANDING MOONSAULT!!! Impressive chain wrestling by the Flying Dragon!!! TK: Kojima goes for the pin... ...1... ...2... KICKOUT!!! [crowd applauds] AO: Yatoro kicks out!! Hmph! That shows the inexperience of Kojima!! If he had hooked Yatoro's leg, he could have easily just won!!! TK: I disagree, Ono-san! It will take more than that to beat Hori Yatoro!!! Both men getting back up to their feet... AO: Now Yatoro with another IRISH WHIP... Kojima comes off the far ropes... SPINNING BACK FIST by Hori Yatoro!!! NO!!! DUCKED by Jushin Kojima!!! TK: Kojima stopped short and ducked that fist by Yatoro, now Kojima with a SIDE RUSSIAN LEG SWEEP takes Yatoro back to the mat!!! AO: Now Kojima back to his feet... SNAP ELBOW!!!!!! TK:Right across the sternum of Yatoro!!! That seems to have taken the wind right out of him!! AO: Now Kojima is heading for the corner. Hmph! if he had covered Yatoro, he could have easily beaten him, but instead he is choosing to showboat. That is typical of these young athletes!! TK: The only way for him to learn, Ono-san, is to try. AO: *grumble* I suppose you are right... TK: Now Kojima is on the top turnbuckle... He leaps... FROGSPLASH!!!!!!!! [The crowd OH's in unison...] AO: But Yatoro moved out of the way!!! I knew it was a mistake for him to try that move!! Now he's paying for it!!! TK: Missing that splash took a lot out of Jushin Kojima!!! Now Yatoro is standing up over him... AO: Yatoro is picking up Kojima... He has him... SPINNING BACKBREAKER!!! TK: Not it's Kojima who's lying on the mat!! AO: But the more experienced Yatoro is going for the cover!! ...1... ...2... KICKOUT!!! [crowd applauds] TK: KICK OUT by Jushin Kojima!!! He certainly has a lot of endurance, eh, Ono-san? AO: Hmph! I suppose so... TK: Kojima is on his hands and knees now, but Yatoro is already to his feet!! AO: Yatoro heads over to the corner now... He's climbing the turnbuckles... TK: Kojima is staggering to his feet... He seems confused... AO: I believe Yatoro is going for his Assaiki finisher!! He leaps backwards... MOONSAULT... TK: Kojima moves forward!! Yatoro goes over his head... LANDS ON HIS FEET!! Hori Yatoro landed on his feet on the mat behind Kojima!!! AO: But Jushin Kojima with a THRUST KICK to the jaw of Yatoro!!!! Yatoro goes down to the mat!!!! TK: Now Jushin Kojima to the top turnbuckle... DRAGON DROP!!!! That 450 splash is his finishing maneuver!!! AO: Kojima covers Yatoro... ...1... ...2... ...3...!!! **ding** **ding** **ding** [Crowd applauds as "Firestarter" cranks on the PA] TK: An impressive victory for Jushin Kojima!!! AO: Yatoro's experience wasn't enough to overcome the skills of the Flying Dragon!! TK: But both men should be congratulated on a hard fought match!!! AO: I guess. All the men who compete in SJPW fight hard! TK: Before we get to our next match, I've been informed that Mitsunori Takizawa has a special announcement to make. AO: What about? TK: It seems that he has a new tag team partner, and he wishes to present him to the audience tonight. That, and comment on his match with Toshiaki Takada tomorrow night! AO: I wonder who his new partner could be... TK: Let us go to the ring and find out! ____ _____ _ _____________________________________________ / __/_ / / _ \ | /| / / / _\ \/ // / ___/ |/ |/ / Mitsunori Takizawa /___/\___/_/ |__/|__/__________________________________________/ [The camera pans to the ring, where Nishi Toju stands. He waits until the crowd quiets down, then makes his introduction...] Nishi: At this time fans, welcome Chiimu Tora member...Mistunori Takizawa!!! [The camera pans from Nishi down the aisle to the raised platform at the entrance way. Out through the curtains comes Takizawa and the fans applaud and cheer. Cut to another, closer camera angle. Takizawa holds a microphone in his hands, he surveys the audience, and then raises the microphone to his lips and speaks.] Mitsunori Takizawa: The time is counting down for you Takada-san. Your time as Super Japan Pro Wrestling's Battle Arts champion is drawing to a close. If you listen very carefully perhaps you can even hear the seconds as they tick away... as rapid as your heartbeat as you realize that you're facing a man with two decades of experience inside the ring. A man who, unlike you, _does_ care about the honor of the titles established here in Super Japan Pro. A man who is unimpressed by your feeble antics. Feel free to bring a sharp x-acto knife with you to the ring when we meet, Takada-san... I will have no problem in taking it from you and using it to carve my name in your skull and let your blood spill over the ring. [Takizawa grins and teases the ends of his mustache.] Mitsunori Takizawa: However, I am not here to talk about my impending match. No, rather I am here tonight to address a different concern. Not too long ago my good friend Shinji Hamada conducted an interview with PN Magazine and admitted that he and I had ended our tag team combination. That's true. He also mentioned that I had already found a new partner, and that's why I'm here tonight.... to announce the newest member of Chiimu Tora and my new tag team partner. Therefore at this time I'd like to introduce.... [The fans let out shocked gasp as Noboru Nishida bursts through the curtains. Takizawa turns and he too is surprised.] AO [from his broadcast postion]: What? Nishida is his new part... [Nishida suddenly rushes the surprised Takizawa and catches him with a stiff running lariat. Nishida then collapses on top of Takizawa and begins to lay in with punches.] TK: Nishida attacking Takizawa...This is terrible. [The fans suddenly roar with cheers as Onslaught explodes through the curtains, and pulls Nishida off of Takizawa. Onslaught begins to rain blows down on the young man as Takizawa gains his feet. Surprisingly, Takizawa pulls Onslaught off of Nishida.] Mitsunori Takizawa: Enough! ENOUGH! You listen to me now, Nishida.... don't blame me for the fact that Ed Dean is a cowardly fool. I carried him when we formed a tag team.... I did everything I could to assist him in defeating Mr. Big. The fact is that even after week after week of attacking Big, Dean _still_ lost cleanly to him. Is that the kind of man you want to emulate? A man who can't even win over a cripple? A coward who ran from SJPW when he realized that his path would cross mine in the ring? A man who pledged to be a partner to YOU and then abandoned you? [Nishida gets to his feet, and Onslaught moves forward, but Takizawa keeps him at bay. Nishida breaths heavily, and stares wild eyed at Takizawa as he speaks.] Mitsunori Takizawa: You're still young Nishida. You have skills. What you need is focus. Ed Dean abandoned you, but I'm willing to put the past behind us. I have a formidable tag team partner in Onslaught... another man with a reason to detest Ed Dean. I'm offering you my hand in friendship... accept it, and I'll do my best to help you learn the focus you need. [Nishida looks out at the crowd, is face furrowed in confusion. He looks back to Takizawa, and then looks down at the ground.] AO: Takizawa making an offer to the young man. He would be a fool to refuse. [Takizawa continues to stare at Nishida, his eyebrow cocked, w aiting for his answer. Nishida finally looks up the fire in his eyes having burned out, and nods. The fans cheer as Takizawa smacks Nishida on top of the head as if scorning a child and then all three men walk back through the curtains.] AO: An amazing development here. Takizawa's new partner is Onslaught, and not only that, it appears he has turned a hated opponent into an ally. I have to commend my former partner. He is almost as wise as me. TK: I am proud to see Takizawa taking such an active role in Chiimu Tora as they continue to grow stronger with each day. AO: I just realized something: Onslaught is the newest member of Chiimu Tora! I never thought I would see the day when he would become one of the "good guys"! TK: Ono-sama, there is much more to Onslaught than meets the eye. Yes, he is a powerful man, and he brawls. But he has the heart of a Dragon, and the other members of Chiimu Tora wholeheartedly approved of him joining. AO: I still have my doubts... TK: You always do. AO: I am to stubborn in my old age [grins] TK: This next match should prove to be an exciting one, eh Ono-san? AO: Exciting? Why's that? TK: This match will feature two outstanding athletes who have studied the Lucha Libre school of wrestling from Mexico. AO: Hmph! I prefer our own puroresu. Besides, I thought Jiraiya was supposed to be wrestling next? TK: He was unable to be here tonight, due to personal reasons. AO: So they replaced him with another luchador? I guess that is ok. TK: Ono-sama, you must admit they are impressive athletes! AO: I guess. At least they are better trained than American wrestlers. TK: I don't think that statement was necesarrily true, Ono-san! Let's go on up to Nishi Toju and Owada Botan in the ring!!! ____ _____ _ _____________________________________________ / __/_ / / _ \ | /| / / / _\ \/ // / ___/ |/ |/ / El Gato Diablo vs Juventud Sagrada /___/\___/_/ |__/|__/__________________________________________/ [The camera pans to the ring, where Nishi Toju & Owada Botan stand. The crowd is again cheering. Nishi waits for the crowd to die down, then proceeds to make his introductions...] Nishi: Minna-san, our next match will be for one fall with a 10 minute time limit. The referee for this contest is Owada Botan... [Botan makes a slight bow...] NT: Introducing first... ["Muevete by DLG plays over the PA] NT: From Orocovis, Puerto Rico... Standing 6 feet tall and weighing 225 pounds... JUVENTUD SAGRADA!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! [Sagrada steps from behind the curtains and raises his arms in the air. He wears a t-shirt to the ring which reads "Es que yo soy Boricua, Puertoriqueno de corazon!" He takes this off when he gets to the ring. His pant length tights, are of a vinyl fabric and are a dark crimson. Black wrestling boots and black medical tape make up the rest of his attire. His wrestling mask is in fact painted in the colors of the Puerto Rican flag: red,white and blue with the lone star on the forehead. He starts to dance a bit of salsa, as the song happens to be a salsa song. He slowly makes his way to the ring, giving out high fives and pumping his fists in the air. He jumps on the apron, slingshots over the ropes and climbs to the top rope, throws his shirt to the crowd and awaits the match.] NT: And his opponent... [Metallica's "For Whom the Bell Tolls" blares through the PA] NT: From Monterrey, Mexico... Standing 5 feet 9 inches tall and weighing 224 pounds... EL GATO DIABLO!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! [El Gato Diablo enters the arena. He wears black tights with red and yellow flames on the legs and yellow "Spanish Cat" designs on the hips and red boots. He wears a black mask with red around the eyes, and nose, "devil horns" on the forehead & yellow "Spanish Cat" designs throughout. His long black hair sticks out of the top of the mask, and his lower jaw is totally exposed. He wears a black cloak with red and yellow fire designs on it. He walks swiftly to the ring, and removes the cloak once he's in the ring...] TK: Looks like both men are ready to begin... *ding-ding-ding* TK: And the bell has sounded starting the match!! AO: Both men are circling each other, slapping each others hands... TK: Now Gato Diablo WHIPS Sagrada into the ropes... DROPKICK to the jaw of Sagrada!! AO: Diablo being very aggressive right from the very beginning! He helps up Sagrada... another IRISH WHIP... DROPKICK!!! TK: But Sagrada managed to hold onto the ropes! Diablo crashes to the mat flat on his back!!! AO: Now Sagrada comes off the near rope...FORWARD SOMERSAULT into a GUILLOTINE LEGDROP!!!! *the crowd applauds politely...* TK: Very impressive!! Wouldn't you agree Ono-san? AO: He appears to posses some skill. TK: Ono-san, I hope you are more generous with your money than with your compliments! AO: I give both only to those who earn them. TK: Now Sagrada tries to SCOOP UP Diablo... ROLL UP INTO A SMALL PACKAGE by Gato Diablo!!! He's going for a quick pin!!! ...1... ...2... KICKOUT!!! [crowd applauds] AO: Hmph! It was far too soon to attempt a pinfall like that! TK: Not necessarily, Ono-san! Diablo almost caught Sagrada completely by surprise that time!! AO: Now both men are back to their feet! TK: Sagrada grabs Diablo by the wrist... IRISH WHIP into the ropes... Diablo comes back off the ropes... CLOTHESLINE by Sagrada... DUCKED by Diablo!! Both men lying there in the corner now!! AO: These Luchadores always seem to hurt themselves worse than their opponents!! TK: That's because these men always take great risks in order to win!!! But it looks like both men are getting back up now!!! AO: Both wrestlers are almost back to their feet...they're facing one another... TK: LA MAJISTRAL CRADLE by Juventud Sagrada!!! This could be it!!! ...1... ...2... KICKOUT!!! [crowd applauds] AO: Diablo managed to kick out at the last possible moment!!! TK: Both men quickly scrambling up to their feet... AO: Diablo executes a RUSSIAN LEG SWEEP!!! Now he FLOATS OVER for the pin... ...1... ...2... KICKOUT!!! [crowd applauds] TK: Another kick-out!! This has certainly been a quick paced match!! AO: This time, Sagrada manages to get up quicker!! He SCOOPS up Diablo... MICHINOKU DRIVER!!! TK: This could be it for Diablo!! But wait! Sagrada's picking him up! He has him in a FRONT FACE LOCK!! AO: Now Sagrada's backing into the corner... He's climbing the ropes, and he still has the Face lock on... TK: Sagrada to the top... LA SAGRADA MISION!! Juventud Sagrada's patented TORNADO DDT!! AO: Now Sagrada goes for the pin... ...1... ...2... ...3...!!! **ding** **ding** **ding** [Crowd applauds as "Muevete by DLG plays over the PA] TK: Juventud Sagrada has emerged the victor in another exciting contest!! What a great exhibition of the Lucha Libre style!! AO: I _still_ prefer Puroresu... TK: We need to take a short break, then we will be right back!
