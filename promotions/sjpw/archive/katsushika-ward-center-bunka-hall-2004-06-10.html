SJPW House Show — Katsushika Ward Center Bunka Hall (6/10/04)

Jun 11, 2004, 12:00:00 AM
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
June 10th, 2004 Katsushika Ward Center Bunka Hall: 350 Fans (Sellout) 1) Pramoj Ananda & Se-hong Kwan def Wolf Jager & Sergei Baranov (7:02) when Ananda pinned Baranov after the Ananda Special. 2) "Firefly" Horo Atashi def Yatoro Hori (8:37) after a superkick 3) El Santo Azteca & MAMMOTH Kagawa def Tsutomu Kobayashi & Botan Nakatoni (12:19) after Azteca made Nakatoni submit to a hanging armbar. 4) Despair retained the Gunyro title over Kenichi Kubota (9:38) after a belly to belly moonsault through a table. 5) "Ecchi Kid" Kentaro "H-Bomb" Kawasaki, "The Living Legend" Tommy Fury & Super Bakemono def Tsunami, Takahiro KEOKI & Tasogare no Prince (15:41) after Fury pinned KEOKI after hitting The Legend Killer. - The returning Kwan and his partner Ananda scored a big upset over Jager and the debuting Baranov, who supposedly is Davinsky's nephew. Baranov proved to be capable of holding his own against his opponents, but like his uncle, fell to Ananda's devastating springboard spinning roundhouse kick. - Horo Atashi defeated the veteran Yatoro Hori in a short, but faced paced bout. Both men shook hands after the match. - Continuing his quest to face Despair, El Santo Azteca teamed up with the rookie powerhouse of Team Tiger to take on the DoD's Crush Crew. Kagawa & Kobayashi hit some stiff moves, but Azteca was smart enough to target Nakatoni, who was the smaller member of the CC. Nakatoni's arm was almost pulled out of it's socket by the hanging armbar, and he had to get it checked out at the hospital. - Despair made his first official title defense, taking on the veteran Kubota. The two used a variety of weapons, including Kubota nailed Despair with a flaming chair! The end came when Despair hit is belly to belly moonsault through a table in the ring. Both men had to be helped to the back, but Despair stopped where his fans were seated, and flicked blood from his face on them, his fans literally moaning in what appeared to be ecstacy! - Tommy Fury made his SJPW debut, substituting for Joey Darren, who had to fly back to American to defend his ICW Network title. Fury proved a good partner for Kawasaki & Bakemono, and scored the win by hitting his Legend Killer on the rookie KEOKI.
