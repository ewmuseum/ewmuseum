Thanks, but no thanks. Jonnie is retiring, after a long career stretching back to 1991 in the UWA play by mail wrestling fed. The only people I let in recently was the original NorthPac Coalition [who were in HCW], because the handler is an old ally from the UWA, and the NorthPac are even older than Jonnie Adonis is. Add in the 10 people currently on the waiting list, and SJPW is booked full, most likely for the rest of the year. Sorry
