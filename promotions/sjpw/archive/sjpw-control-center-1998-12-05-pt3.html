SJPW Card Archive

Control Center, Part Three

5 December 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Control Center continues...] TK: Welcome back everyone... [The House lights go out, the arena is now filled in utter darkness. A red spotlight falls on the ring where Tenryu stands. suddenly the Jumbotron flashes on evealing an image of a burning fire] Voice of Super Tengu: They called me Tengu... they called me a daredevil... they called me a man without fear...The myth of Tengu was just that... a myth...tell me Jinsei Tenru, what made you think you could attack me, try to cripple me and get away with it? Well now it is time to pay the heavy toll for that transgression!! [the fire flickers and burns. the camera does not leave the fire, there is no shot of Super Tengu, only the burning logs of the fire] Voice of Super Tengu: I have laid the curse of Tengu on many and all those that have feltits touch have suffered... but I was a child then, a child playing men as if they were toys... You prided yourself on my humiliation and took away EVERYTHING that made Tengu powerful... But now that you have taken away Tengu... [a mask falls on the fire, the familiar black mask of Super Tengu is consumed by the flame] I see no need to return him to Super Japan... Now I am a man with nothing to lose... and it is said a man with nothing to lose truly is... a man without fear, a man... OF IRON!! [The mask burns away, the camera stays focused on the fire] Voice of Super Tengu: I was a child, but now I am a man and it is time to put aside childish things... Tell me Jinsei... are you ready to face a "Man of Iron"? [The voice breaks into harsh chuckle] Voice of Super Tengu: The Tengu is gone... You will see the evolution of tha which was intothat which IS!! a Man of Iron will teach pain, a Man of Iron will show... REVENGE!! [the fire blazes up, the logs collapse as the image fades fromthe screen] AO: What the? Tengu no more? How can this be? TK: Calm down Akira! He said he would no longer wrestle as Tengu! You know what that means, don't you? AO: That he will wrestle without a mask? TK: Yes! That means he will wrestle as Tetsuo Matsumoto, and the world will finally see him without his mask! AO: I will miss Tengu. TK: It's the same person. AO: Yes, but, Tengu was....well Tengu! TK: You make no sense Ono! [a man comes to the broadcast table, and hands Kikuchi a piece of paper...] TK [looking up]: What? Thank you... [Kikuchi reads the paper] AO: Well? TK: It says here that Akira Hashimoto will not be here tonight! It seems he was detained in America by their immigration officials! AO: What? TK: It says he had some problems with his Visa! Kobayashi says he will have a replacement for him come match time! AO: This is getting ridiculous! Can't Jumbo keep people from missing events, or jumping to another promotion? TK: Ono-sama! Stop that! Jumbo is our friend! AO: Still, he's not been a very good CEO lately... TK: Enough! We have a show to do. Up next is that tag tournament qualifier match between The Maulers & The Tokyo Express. The Maulers have been on a losing streak of late, while the Express have a big eight man match tomorrow night. AO: Another of Jumbo's smart moves... TK: Ono! Let's get to ringside, as your irritablity is starting to annoy me... ____ _____ _ _____________________________________________ / __/_ / / _ \ | /| / /Marano Tag Tournament Qualifier Match: / _\ \/ // / ___/ |/ |/ / The Maulers vs The Tokyo Express /___/\___/_/ |__/|__/__________________________________________/ [The camera pans to Nishi Toju & Goto Kyoji standing in the ring. Nishi waits for the crowd to die down, then starts to make his introductions...] Nishi: This next contest is a QUALIFIER match for the Frank A. Maran Memorial Tag Team Tournament! [crowd cheers] The winner of this match will fill the second round slot vacated by Black Sun Rising! The referee for this match is Goto Kyoji! [crowd applauds as Goto bows] ["Fall" by Click blares on the PA] Nishi: Introducing first, at a combined weight of 545 pounds... FRAN-KIE "THE THUMPER" DOUG-LAS & DOUG THOM-AS....THE MAULERS!!!! [The crowd gives a mixed pop as The Maulers come out, wearing SHVF t-shirts. Douglas jawjacks to the crowd, while Thomas ignores them. Both men enter the ring, and go to their corner...] TK: Both these teams recently fought in America.. AO: Really? Where? TK: In Wild West Wrestling. The Express came out on top. AO: As they will tonight! ["Back In Black" by AC/DC blares over the PA] Nishi: Their opponents, accompanied to the ring by Ryua A. Taka, at a combined weight of 513 pounds..."PRETTY BOY" MA-SA KO-KUR-A & RIK-I "THUNDER" KA-NO...THE TOKYO EXPRESS!!!! [Pyrotechnics go off at the runway entrance, and RAT steps out, followed close behind by Kano. Kokura steps out a moment later. RAT & Kano head directly to the ring, while Kokura stops to jawjack with the fans, flirt with some women in the crowd, and strut around. Once they are all in the ring, the lights go out, and golden strobe lights begin to go off throughout the arena, and the sounds of a runaway train can be heard in the background...] AO: The Express always make an impressive entrance, eh? I love these guys! TK: You used to hate RAT... AO: I did not! He and I had a misunderstanding. That's all. TK: If you say so. AO: I do. [The strobes go off, and the house lights come back on. Kokura takes off his robe, and hands it to a ring attendant, while RAT exits the ring...] TK: The Maulers wast no time, and attack the Express! [crowd "ooohs"] Douglas & Kano are fighting toe-to toe, while Thomas caught Kokura from behind with an enzulariato! [crowd "oooohs"] AO: These Maulers have no honor! Thomas now draping Kokura over the ropes, and hammering his back & shoulder with forearm shots, while Kano & Douglas are still battering each other with punches! [Thomas grabs Kokura's arm in a hammerlock, then takes him backwards into a belly to back suplex on his arm, while Kano ducks under one of Douglas' punches, kicks him in the gut, then double underhooks his arms, and takes him over with a butterfly suplex! The crowd applauds both men, who get to their feet, and see each other. Kano charges with a lariat, but Thomas grabs the arm, spins around Kano, then spikes him to the mat with a DDT! The crowd "oooohs" as Thomas helps Douglas to his feet...] TK: Impressive DDT by Thomas. He ususally sticks with submission holds. AO: Maybe he is trying to become more than one dimensional... [The Maulers drag Kano to his feet, and Douglas presses him over his head, then drops him gut first on Thomas' knee! The crowd "oooohs" loudly as Kano rolls on the mat, clutching his gut. The Maulers grab Kokura, and whip him to the ropes, then send him to the mat with a double shoulderblock. Thomas slaps on an armbar, and drags Kokura to his feet, while Douglas goes to the second rope. Thomas forces Kokura to one knee, and Douglas comes off with a double axe handle to his exposed shoulder! The crowd "oooohs" as Kokura winces in pain. Douglas scoops Kokura up, slams him to the mat, then drives an elbowdrop onto his chest...] TK: The Maulers are very precise tonight, while The Express are not... AO: They have tomorrow's match on their mind! Damn Jumbo! [Douglas drags Kokura to his feet, and attempts an irish whip, but Kokura reverses. Kokura leap frogs Douglas, and goes to the ropes. He slides under Douglas's legs, and comes up behind him. Douglas comes off the ropes, and Kokura catches him with a standing dropkick to the face! The crowd cheers as Douglas staggers back to the ropes, and Kokura runs to the corner, springboarding off into a rana that takes Douglas over. Kano is finally standing in his corner, though he is still clutching his chest somewhat. Kokura nails Douglas with an enzugiri, and The Mauler stumbles to his corner, and tags his partner...] TK: The Express seem to have recovered from their early setback... AO: About time! [Thomas enters the ring, and Kokura dropkicks him, knocking him into the corner. Kokura charges Thomas, then takes him out with a monkey flip. Kokura kip ups to his feet, and attempts a spinning heel kick, but Thomas rolls under it, and tackles Kokura's other leg, taking him to the mat. Thomas grabs the leg, and immediately turns Kokura over into a single leg boston crab. Kokura winces in pain, and Douglas roots his partner on. Kokura attempts to reach the ropes, but is unable to. Kano enters the ring, and kicks Thomas in the back of the head, forcing him to break the hold. Goto makes Kano go back to his corner, while Thomas, still holding the leg, tags in Douglas. Douglas enters the ring, and takes the leg from Thomas. He drags Kokura into the middle of the ring, and grabs his other leg. He holds them wide apart, then stomps Kokura low in the midsection. The crowd "ooohs" as Goto warns Douglas...] TK: That was almost a low blow. AO: Goto should disqualify him on principle... [Douglas drags Kokura to his feet, and "The Pretty Boy" thumbs his eye! The crowd "ooohs" as Goto warns Kokura, who tags in Kano! The big man enters the ring, and hammers the blinded Douglas in the back with double axe handle blows. Douglas drops to his knees, and Kano slaps on a full nelson, dragging Douglas to his feet, then falling back into a Dragon Suplex! The crowd applauds as Douglas lands on his head, and flips over onto his chest. RAT applauds his man's strength. Kano walks over to Douglas, and legdrops him across the back of the head. Douglas rolls over in pain, and Kano grabs his legs, and starts to swing him around in a 360. He spins four times, then lets Douglas go, sending him crashing to the mat. Kano staggers for a moment, dizzy from spinning around. Douglas lays on the mat, unmoving...] TK: What strength by Kano! He did four rotations of the Giant Swing! AO: Only Jumbo has ever done more! [Kano staggers to his corner, and tags in Kokura. Kokura goes to the top rope, then leaps off with a frog splas across Douglas' chest! The crowd applauds as Kokura makes the cover, but Thomas stops the count with a kick to Kokura's back. Goto makes him go back to his corner, and Kokura drags Douglas to his feet. He attempts an Irish whip, but Douglas reverses. He kicks Kokura in the gut coming off, and as Kokura doubles over, Douglas grabs him around the waist, hoists him up in the air, runs forward, and slams him hard to the mat with a powerbomb! The crowd "oooohs" loudly as Douglas rolls to his corner, and tags in Thomas!] TK: Douglas is a powerful man too. Did you see the force behind that running powerbomb? AO: I saw. It wasn't bad. [Thomas enters the ring, and proceeds to stomp on Kokura's shoulder. Goto warns him off, and he backs off for a moment, then grabs Kokura by the hair, and drags him to his feet. He hammerlocks Kokura, then rams him shoulderfirst to the corner. He rams him two more times, then spins him around, and starts to lay into him with european uppercuts. Kokura sags in the corner from this punishment. Goto makes Thomas back off, but he comes right back in with a front kick to Kokura's gut. Kokura doubles over, and Thomas sets him up, then snap suplexes him out of the corner. He rolls on top for a cover, but Kokura kicks out at two. The crowd applauds, while Thomas argues with the referee...] TK: Kokura hasn't given up yet! AO: Thank Buddha! [Thomas drags Kokura to his feet, and Kokura leaps up, taking Thomas over with a snap hurricanrana! The crowd applauds as Thomas rolls to his feet and charges, Kokura catching him with a spinning backbreaker! Kokura drops Thomas to the mat, and leaps over him to tag Kano! The crowd "oooohs" as Kano enters the ring, his face a mask of rage. Thomas slowly makes it to his feet and turns, only to have Kano tackle him with a football spear that sends him across the ring! The crowd "oooohs" as Kano gets to his feet, and roars, then grabs Thomas, presses him over his head, and drops him face first to the mat. Kano attempts another press slam, but Douglas enters the ring, and kicks him in the gut. As Kano doubles over, Thomas drops behind him, and applies an oriental sleeper! Kano tries to fight it, while Kokura goes to the top rope. Douglas sees him, and he charges the corner, forcing Kokura to crotch himself! The crowd "ooooohs" as Kokura sits on the top rope in pain...] TK: The Maulers seem to have gotten the upperhand again. AO: I hope RAT does something soon! [Thomas takes Kano over with a belly to back with the sleeper still on, then rolls to his feet. Douglas climbs to the top rope, and superplexes Kokura to the mat. RAT jumps up on the apron, but Thomas rushes him, and knocks him off with a forearm shot. RAT slams into the railing, and the crowd "awwwws" loudly. Douglas grabs Kokura, and presses him over his head. The crowd "oooohs" as he walks towards the ropes, and throws him out of the ring on top of RAT!] TK: This doesn't look too good. AO: No, it doesn't. TK: Douglas has Kano set up for a powerbomb...he lifts him up, and Thomas grabs his neck...Combination Neckbreaker/Powerbomb! [crowd "ooohs"] They call that "The Power Wrecker"! Thomas rolls on top for a cover... ...1... ...2... ...3...!!! **ding** **ding** **ding** [Crowd applauds as "Fall" cranks on the PA] Nishi: The winners of this match, and advancing to round two of the Marano tournament....THE MAULERS!!! [crowd applauds again] TK: What an upset! AO: I can't believe it myself! The Maulers have been on a losing streak of late. They just scored a major coup here tonight! [Douglas takes the microphone from Nishi...] Frankie Douglas: There lie your fallen heroes! Face it SJPW; The Maulers rule! Yakuza, you're next! We're gonna win this tournament, then we're gonna kick the crap outta The Bonecrushers, and become the next All-Asian tag team champions! [Thomas takes the mike as the crowd booos] Doug Thomas: One more thing: SHVF Rules! That's where the REAL competition is! Tenru, Sterling, we haven't forgotten you two! If you have the guts, we challenge the two of you to a tag team match. We're gonna finish the ass-kicking we started on you two last weekend! [Thomas drops the mike, and he and Douglas head toward the back, the crowd booing loudly] TK: The Maulers making some bold statements, as well as a challenge to Jinsei Tenru & Alan Sterling. AO: Sterling & Tenru will destroy them, just like they did last weekend. And the Yakuza, well, they will do even worse! TK: We have to take a short break, then we'll be right back! [Control Center continues...]
