[detail withheld] (The Blade) wrote in message news:< [detail withheld] >... > GERRY! Good to see you back in e-wrestling. Glad to be back! > Hmmmm...TOP was the last fed I was in full-time and it is dead. So there are only women efeds from Japan now, eh? > Geez. No love for Ed Dean, Shoji Suzuki, "Demon Boy" Ishrinku, or Jet > Jaguar II. Man, that's cold.:) Please! I only mentioned people I saw on rspwf. I can never forget any of those 4 guys. Ishrinku was to Battearts what Tengu was to the Cruiserweights. And Ed Dean was everyones favorite "Ugly American". Shoji was the best rookie I saw played in a fed, back when he was Jet Jaguar (still love the gimmick from a Godzilla flick). Jet II was just coming into his own when SJPW went under. I still fondly remember the feud between Ishrinku and Steel Serpent. It rivaled Tengu/Hamada, but was far more brutal. I even remember the Tengu/Ishrinku match from HCW, where Tengu learned what it meant to be hardcore first hand from a master (Tengu did win, but man, it was a good match). And their teamup was very cool too. And don't forget Dean/Miyazaki in Adam's fed; I still remember losing by knockout when Dean dumped Eiji on his head with a german suplex! > Hey I would be on board for the reunion show and HOW. Coolnes. > SJPW was perhaps the most fun I've had in E-wrestling and it would be > great to revist the old characters and maybe finish some careers with > a bang. Hope it happens. If I can find enough old handlers, it hopefully will. I may also want to see if wrestlers from the more recent feds would like to appear, showing the old and the new in one show.
