Introducing first, led to the ring by Grandmaster Xu... hailing from Ji Zhu, Japan... weighing in at 131 pounds... XIAMEN! ["Ana Ng" by THEY MIGHT BE GIANTS begins playing over the P.A. system as a dark-skinned Japanese man, smallish and skinny by wrestling standards, steps through the curtains. He is accompanied by Grandmaster Xu, a Tanzanian wearing loose fitting white cotton pants and a colorful print shirt. Xiamen has dark black hair down to his chin as well as a faint mustache, sideburns, and beard. He smiles and waves at the cheering crowd as he takes off down the aisle toward the ring at full tilt. Grandmaster Xu follows in his wake as Xiamen dives into the ring underneath the bottom rope, clambers up onto a corner turnbuckle and jumps off to perform a 450 degree somersault into the center of the ring.] Nishi: And his opponent... from Chicago, Illinois... weighing in at 260 pounds... DOUG THOMAS! ["Fall" by CLICK begins playing over the P.A. system as a well-built, well-tanned Caucasian man with brown hair steps through the curtains. The crowd cheers politely as Thomas stares at the ring, dressed in red trunks, black boots, black elbow pads, black knee pads and black shootfighting gloves. His eyes focused on the ring, Thomas stalks down the aisle, never taking his eye off Xiamen as he climbs into the ring.] ER: The bell rings and Thomas quickly grabs an arm and traps Xiamen with a wristlock. Xiamen strains to break free and manages to twist out of the hold by somersaulting forward and then kipping up to his feet. But Thomas immediately nails him with a clothesline and Xiamen goes down! AO: Thomas in control at the beginning as he pulls Xiamen to his feet and tries to hit him with a European uppercut. But Xiamen ducks underneath it... AND COUNTERS WITH A BACKSLIDE! ER: He's got Thomas' shoulders on the mat as the referee counts: ...1... ...2... kickout!! [crowd "ooohhhs" then applauds] AO: Fast action on the part of both men as Thomas tries to dominate physically but Xiamen uses his quickness and agility. Xiamen sends Thomas crashing into the turnbuckle with an Irish whip and immediately charges in afterward... oh! But Thomas lifts his knee and Xiamen crashes right into it! ER: Thomas charges out of the corner and staggers Xiamen backward with a back elbow to the jaw. An Irish whip sends Xiamen into the ropes and Thomas flattens him on the rebound with a thrust kick that connects right to the head of Xiamen. AO: I'm impressed with Thomas' performance as a singles wrestler since his focus in SJPW so far has been on the tag team division. But he's looking very good as he goes to work on Xiamen's shoulder as he slaps on a hammerlock. ER: This is where Thomas's submission expertise comes into play as he tries to wear down a limb or joint. Xiamen is in trouble and he knows it as he valiantly tries to break out of that hammerlock. And he does it! AO: A quick duck-down and a go-behind reverses the hammerlock but Xiamen releases the hold quickly, spinning Thomas around... AND NAILS HIM WITH THE POKE-BOX! [crowd "ooohhhs"] ER: A deadly nerve strike as Xiamen uses two fingers on each hand to quickly jab Thomas in the ribs, back, side, stomach, and neck. More of Xiamen's unusual fighting style but highly effective as Thomas reels backward. AO: Xiamen follows right in with a crescent kick... no! Thomas catches his foot, blocking the kick and... oh! Xiamen just swung his free leg over his trapped one, touching it briefly to the ground before using his hands and leg strength to swing the leg back and kick Thomas in the head! ER: Another impressive move which he calls the Korean Take-down as Xiamen lands lightly on his feet. He then runs towards the ropes, leaping up and bouncing off the top rope to come back and nail Thomas with a springboard shoulderblock that knocks him to the mat. AO: And now the smaller man is in control of the match as Xiamen steps out between the ropes onto the apron. He grabs the top rope in both hands and propels himself up and over... AND CONNECTS WITH A SLINGSHOT SOMERSAULT LEG DROP! [crowd applauds] ER: Xiamen going for another pin and here's the count: ...1... ...2... ...shoulder up!! [crowd "ooohhhs"] AO: A very close call but Thomas is not about to give up just yet. Xiamen runs into the ropes as Thomas staggers to his feet. Xiamen rebounds back... but is sent face-first into the canvas as Thomas catches him with a drop toehold! ER: Excellent timing by Thomas as he immediately grabs a leg and applies a half Boston. But Xiamen is too close to the ropes and he quickly grabs the bottom rope, forcing Thomas to release his hold. Xiamen back on his feet quickly but Thomas retains the advantage with a kick to the abdomen. Thomas sends Xiamen into the ropes with an Irish whip and as Xiamen rebounds back, he tries for a flying headscissors... no! AO: Thomas showing his strength advantage as he just throws Xiamen off, sending the smaller man crashing to the mat. Thomas pulls Xiamen to his feet but then immediately slams him back down onto the canvas as he nails him with a belly-to-belly suplex. Thomas yanks Xiamen to his feet once more and applies a hammerlock... ER: But he's not looking for a submission here as Thomas picks the smaller man up and drives him down to the mat with a hammerlock slam! Thomas is going right back after that shoulder again as he tries to soften Xiamen up and take away the use of that arm. AO: Xiamen is grimacing in pain as Thomas grabs the injured arm and yanks him to his feet, hyper-extending that shoulder joint. Thomas maintaining his momentum as he grabs Xiamen's head in a front facelock and begins pounding away on that injured shoulder with a series of forearm smashes. ER: Xiamen manages to push Thomas into the ropes but that might have been a mistake as Thomas immediately grabs his arm and wraps it around the top rope, using the ropes for extra leverage in a painful arm wringer. AO: The referee counts for the break and Thomas lets go in time to avoid disqualification. But he has been methodically targeting that arm throughout this match and you can tell that his strategy is having its effect. ER: Xiamen in a great deal of pain as he massages that injured shoulder. But Thomas is right back on top of him, slapping on another hammerlock. Xiamen yelling now as that arm is definitely hurt. But he manages to break free from the hold... running into the ropes... AND HE TAKES THOMAS DOWN WITH A SPRINGBOARD LARIAT! [crowd applauds] AO: The fans really appreciating this interesting match-up pitting the submission specialist against the unconventional high flyer. Xiamen's arm is still giving him some trouble but he's put Thomas off his game plan temporarily at a time when he desperately needs to turn things around. ER: Thomas back up quickly, looking to regain the advantage. But Xiamen throws his body forward into a rolling somersault, crashing into Thomas's legs and knocking him down. AO: Xiamen calls that the 8-pin as he literally turns himself into a bowling ball. Thomas doesn't quite know how to react to these unconventional maneuvers as Xiamen gets very low to the ground, dropping his arms and bending his knees almost like a monkey. ER: We've seen this before... and there it is! Thomas struggled back to his feet only to get hammered in the forehead with a Tanzanian Kick! Xiamen's version of a drop kick began from that crouched position from which he sprang straight upward, kicking out vertically right at Thomas' head. AO: More of Xiamen's ancient African fighting style as taught to him by Grandmaster Xu. Thomas took a hard shot with that Tanzanian Kick and now Xiamen hits him with a crescent kick followed by a legsweep kick. ER: The crowd is cheering Xiamen on as he puts on a spectacular display of his unusual fighting style. Xiamen climbs up in one corner as Thomas lays on his back in the middle of the ring. Now Xiamen leaps off... AND CONNECTS WITH A FLYING HEADBUTT! AO: That really knocked the wind out of Thomas' sails as Xiamen goes for his third pin attempt of this match: ...1... ...2... ...kickout!! [crowd "ooohhhs"] ER: Xiamen just can't seem to put Thomas away and you can see he's getting a little frustrated by this. Thomas demonstrating tremendous resiliency as he's absorbed a lot of punishment from the smaller man. AO: Xiamen now standing behind Thomas who is sitting up. Now Xiamen sets him up... and he's going for his Don't Jeer A Prophet finisher! ER: But he doesn't get it on! Thomas grabs his head and sends him over with a snap mare before Xiamen can lock on his painful submission hold. Now Xiamen leaps to his feet and charges... AO: Armdrag takedown by Thomas... right into an armbar! And just like that, the tide turns once more as Thomas summons up a burst of energy to turn things around. ER: And Xiamen is in trouble once again as Thomas is exerting tremendous pressure on the same arm he's been working on all along. A very sound strategy by the submission specialist as Xiamen's arm can only have been weakened by the constant punishment he's suffered thus far. AO: Xiamen manages to squirm his way over to the ropes and the referee forces Thomas to release his hold. Xiamen uses the ropes to pull himself upright but Thomas is on him once again as he catches Xiamen in a standing wristlock. Keeping a grip on that injured arm, Thomas sends Xiamen into the ropes with an Irish whip. ER: Xiamen rebounds back... oh! And Thomas catches him right in the face with a spinning leg lariat! AO: Now it's Thomas turn as he goes for the pin: ...1... ...2... ...shoulder up!! [crowd "ooohhhs"] ER: And Thomas very nearly had him there as he uses his size and strength advantage to dominate the smaller man. Thomas pulls Xiamen up, lifts him into the air and crunches his tailbone with an atomic drop. He follows that up with a thrust kick that knocks Xiamen back into the ropes... AND CATCHES HIM ON THE REBOUND WITH A PUNISHING CLOTHESLINE!! AO: Another pinfall attempt by Thomas: ...1... ...2... ...3-no! Kickout!! [crowd applauds] ER: Another very close call and Xiamen is struggling to keep up at the moment. Thomas still in control as he pulls Xiamen up from behind, wrenching his arm up between his shoulder blades with a hammerlock. AO: What's this? Ooh! And Thomas drives Xiamen forward, ramming his injured shoulder into the turnbuckle with tremendous force! Xiamen slumps forward in the corner, grimacing in pain as Thomas wraps his damaged arm around the top rope and applies an arm wringer. ER: Again, the referee counts for the break and Thomas releases the arm before being disqualified. But he's still got a grip on that arm as he drives Xiamen down to the mat, locking on an armbar. AO: This is not where Xiamen wants to be as down on the canvas is precisely where Thomas has the advantage. Xiamen needs to regain his mobility but right now he's in agony as Thomas bears down on that wounded arm and shoulder. ER: Despite the punishment he's taken from Xiamen's irregular moves, Thomas has done an excellent job of wearing him down and consistently targeting that shoulder. But Xiamen manages to hook his foot over the bottom rope and Thomas is forced to release the armbar. AO: Thomas pulls Xiamen to his feet once more and sends him into the ropes with an Irish whip. Xiamen rebounds back and Thomas goes for a thrust kick... no! Xiamen ducks out of the way!! ER: Xiamen bounces off the ropes on the opposite side as Thomas turns to face him. And Xiamen nails him with a tremendous crescent kick that catches Thomas in the side of the head! AO: Thomas goes down and Xiamen immediately circles around behind him... AND HE SLAPS ON THE DON'T JEER A PROPHET! [crowd applauds] ER: Once again, the tide shifts at lightning speed as the submission expert finds himself caught in a tremendously painful hold. Xiamen's variation of a Stump Puller has Thomas writhing in pain and the referee is checking with him closely to see if he submits. AO: Thomas is in great difficulty but he's fighting off the pain. He's shown incredible determination as Xiamen has been unable to put him away so far but... **ding** **ding** **ding** ER: What's this? Did Thomas submit?? AO: No, he did not! But Xiamen thinks he did as he's released the hold and is celebrating what he believes to be a victory!! ER: Thomas is arguing with the referee as he clearly did not submit but I'm not sure what happened. [In the ring, Nishi Toju steps between the ropes, holding his microphone, as he confers briefly with the referee.] Nishi: Ladies and gentlemen. The twenty minute time limit for this match has expired and the result of this contest is... A DRAW! [The crowd applauds and throws paper streamers into the ring as Thomas shakes his head in frustration.] ER: What a hard-fought match but what a disappointment not to have a clear winner. AO: I agree. Xiamen showed some amazing moves in that ring but Thomas was able to take him off his feet on a number of occasions and cause some serious damage to that arm and shoulder joint. ER: [looking at Akira Ono] What are you doing Akira? Sharon: Now please allow me to introduce to you the man you have come to know for the past 2 years, please allow me to give to you... "THE IRON MAN" TETSUO MATSUMOTO! Atsukute, tsurai jibun wo kakushite, mijikai toki wo ikiteru [Trans: Hiding myself, heated and irritated; living only a short time.] Hitori de wa, tooi ashita wo Yoake no mama de, koesou de [Trans: When I'm alone, tomorrow feels far away. I won't be able to sleep at all tonight either.] Nando nankai, kurikaeshitemo Modottekichau, ai dakara [Trans: However many times it's repeated,. It revives again and again--because it's love.] Butsukatteiku, kesunu omoi wo Semeru hou ga, suji chigai Trans: [You can't blame my emotion, Because you should know it will never fade away.] [Finally tetsuo climbs up on the ring apron and raises both his hands over his head to a huge pop. he blows kisses to the crowd and climbs through the ropes] Hitori de wa, tooi ashita wo Yoake no mama de koeteyuku Trans: [When I'm alone, tomorrow feels far away. And even though I'm in the darkness of dawn, I have to go.] Aishou yori mo, fukai futari wa Sure chigatte kamawanai [Tetsuo mounts the top turnbuckle and again raises his arms over his head and then backflips off, landing on his feet as the cheers become more than deafening] Trans: [I don't care about "bad affinity". Even if our love is not doing well, Nevertheless we have deep ties.] [Finally he walks over to Sharon Akiyama and gives her a gentle kiss on the cheek as the music dies] Sharon: Tetsuo let me be the first to say to you... WELCOME BACK TO SUPER JAPAN PRO-WRESTLING!! [The audience reaction is a deafening cheer] Tetsuo: Domo [bows slightly] Dome.... Tell me Jumbo, who has had the last laugh? [jerks a thumb to his chest] I have!! You think that by putting me in your little "garbage" division that you'll deny me my chance to become the All Asian Champion? [cracks a thin smile] Oooooh no, I relish it!! Now that Yamashita is in power the executive committee thinks they can disregard me? Toss me aside like a piece of trash? Well I say this... [fixes his gaze on the camera in front of him] THAT WILL NEVER HAPPEN!! Up next is the debut of Kevin "The Cobra" Richards, as he takes on Toshiro Ukemo. AO: Richards is an accomplished technical wrestler, but he seems determined to compete in garbage matches. Such a waste of talent! ____ _____ _ _____________________________________________ / __/_ / / _ \ | /| / / / _\ \/ // / ___/ |/ |/ /Kevin "The Cobra" Richards vs Toshiro Ukemo /___/\___/_/ |__/|__/__________________________________________/ Both men started off with a lockup, and Richards went for a go behind, then executed a kneebreaker. Richards then grabbed Ukemo's leg, and rained three elbowdrops onto the inner thigh. Richards went for a spinning toehold, but Ukemo countered with a small package for a one count. Ukemo got to his feet, and caught Richards with an uppercut, then hit a butterfly suplex. Ukemo started to choke Richards, who was able to lock his legs around Ukemo's waist, and apply enough pressure to make Ukemo break the choke. Ukemo started to pummel Richards with his fist, until Richards again applied pressure with the body scissors. Richards released the hold, then kicked Ukemo off him. He rolled to his feet, and as Ukemo started to rise, tackled his legs. Richards stomped on Ukemo's knee a few times, then applied a leglock grapevine. Ukemo tried to reverse it, but to no avail. Richards broke the hold, and dragged Ukemo to his feet. He hit another kneebreaker, then applied his Poison's Pleasure figure four leglock in the middle of the ring. Ukemo attempted to reverse, but Richards was able to block that. Ukemo then tried to grab the ropes, but was too far from them, so he quickly submitted. Winner: Kevin "The Cobra" Richards by submission ER: Richards looked impressive tonight. AO: He even fought without resorting to his garbage tactics. That is a change. Maybe he is trying to prove he can do more than brawl! ER: He definitely has the technical ability. AO: Without a doubt. I hope he wrestles like this more often! ER: Our next match features Rupan Loco against Yo-sub Gwang, a korean martial arts expert. Rupan charged him, and Gwang sprang up to the top rope, and hit a springboard spinwheel kick that sent Rupan staggering back into the corner. Rupan then hit a Tiger Driver, then applied the Wolfe Bite Sleeper. Gwang tapped out immediately! Winner: Rupan Loco by submission ER: Gwang didn't look too bad. AO: But Rupan Loco looked better.
