After Dark, Hour 2 (SJPW Hour)

5 October 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

The crowd is cheering. Adonis waits for the crowd to die down, then raises the microphone to speak...] Jonnie Adonis: Ladies and Gentlemen, at this time I would like to introduce to you your All Asian Champion "The Modern Day Samurai" Hiro Kanemoto! (Voodoo Child begins to play and the spotlight hits the top of the runway but Kanemoto is nowhere to be seen....) Hiro Kanemoto: Hey Jonnie, I'm up here! (Adoins looks into the crowd and once the spotlight is turned around to shine on the crowd we see Hiro Kanemoto standing in the crowd surrounded by his fans... Huge crowd pop once they realize where Hiro is... As he makes his way to the interview stage he stops and slaps hands with almost everyone... Once he gets onto the stage, he shakes hands with Jonnie Adonis and they both wait until the crowd dies down to begin the interview...) JA: First of all I would like to congratulate you on becoming SJPW's All Asian Champ... (Huge crowd pop... Hiro turns to the crowd and and holds the belt up high over his head...) HK: Thank you very much Jonnie... This belt means alot to me... It wasn't easy getting it but all the hardwork paid off... JA: Were you shocked that you made it all the way to the final's of the tournament? HK: Shocked is an understatement... Who'd of though I would make it? Here I was on a horrible losing streak then all of a sudden my luck seemed to change over night... I can't explain it... JA: What are your thought's on the PPV? HK: I thought it was great... Alot of hard fought matches, championships changing hands and new champions being crowned, what else could you want? The fans sure did get their money's worth... JA: How do you feel losing to Lance Smith but gaining the All Asian Title? HK: I'm not happy at all with my loss to Lance Smith (Crowd boos') I'm not going to take anything away from him; he took the fight to me and he won... On that night he was the better man... When I get the chance to face him again the outcome will be different I can promise you that! JA: How do you feel about Lance Smith as World Champion? HK: I think he disgraces the belt(Crowd cheers)... He's a punk who doesn't deserve to wear the belt... JA: So you're saying that you'd make a better World Champ then Lance Smith? HK: Yes, I am saying that I would make a better World Champ then Lance Smith! Anyone who disgraces our Country the way he has doesn't deserve to wear such a prestigious belt... (Huge crowd pop) JA: Have you decided who your first title defense will be against? HK: Yes, I have... JA: Well are you going to let us know? (Crowd begins to cheer wildly...) HK: (Looks out to the crowd) Should I tell you? (Crowd begins to cheer louder, breaking into chants of Hiro, Hiro!) HK: I have decided to give the first title shot to none other then the fat, ugly, out of shape gaijin known as the "Japeater" Jarred Jones... (Crowd begins to boo lustfully...) JA: Why Jones? HK: Afterall he did pin me once before... And I've been trying for several month's now to get a rematch with him so I can slap the taste out of his mouth but he keeps ducking me... So I figured maybe a chance at gold will entice that slob and make him come out of hiding... JA: What is your status with Ryu Taka? He normally accompanies you to ringside but at the PPV you came to the ring by yourself... Why is that? HK: I've decided to go on my own... I'm better off that way... Mr. Taka is a good man but he could only do so much for me... I can do alot more for myself... Besides in the past few weeks Mr. Taka, the Tokyo Express and Terra have seperated them selves from me... I felt like I was a 5th wheel with no room to grow... That's why I made the decision to let Mr. Taka go... JA: Well Hiro I'd like to thank you for taking time out of your busy schedule to stop by and talking with us... HK: It's been my pleasure... One last thing before I go I would just like thank the fans of SJPW for supporting me it's because of you that I now have this belt! (Hiro holds the belt high over his head once again and the crowd erupts into the chant of Hiro, Hiro, Hiro! The camera moves over to the Control Center, where Akira Ono is sitting...) Akira Ono: Hello. I am Akira Ono. Along with Jonnie Adonis, I'll be doing play by play for After Dark! Our first hour saw the long awaited debut of Kobayashi Pro Wrestling, or K-Pro. We hope you enjoyed the action K-Pro put on for you in Nagoya tonight. [Jonnie Adonis enters the camera view, sits down, and puts on his headset] JA: That man is pumped! AO: Yes. He's a good man. JA: Are we ready to get this show on the road or what? AO: Yes Jonnie. We're ready to start. Our first match is the debut of Conrad James, who will face Gregor Davinsky. Let's go to our ring announcer... [Camera cuts to the ring. The ring announcer, the referee, Gregor Davinsky, and another man are all standing in the ring...] Ring Announcer: Welcome to SJPW After Dark! [crowd cheers] This first contest is one fall, 15 minute time limit. Introducing first, to my right, GRE-GOR DA-VIN-SKY!!! [crowd booos] His opponent, to my left, making his SJPW DEBUT, CON-RAD JAMES!!! [crowd applauds] JA: This should be a good match. James is one tough SOB. AO: Davinsky is too. He offers James his hand, and James takes his hand, and levels Davinsky with a short-arm clothesline! [crowd oooohs] He fistdrop's Gregor's forehead, then drags him to his feet. He headbutts the russian, then whips him to the ropes...tilt o' whirl backbreaker! [crowd oooohs] JA: James gets to his feet, and runs to the ropes...big legdrop across Davinsky's chest. He covers...1...2...kickout! [crowd applauds] James gets to his feet, and drags Gregor up by his hair. He starts to hammer the side of his head with punches, but Gregor rakes him in the eyes. James now staggers around blinded, and Davinsky clotheslines him to the mat! [crowd oooohs] AO: Davinsky now stomping away at Conrad's chest & head; He's vicious! He drags James to his feet, and takes him over with a snap suplex. He lifts James up, and whips him to the ropes...back bodydrop! James is sitting up holding his back...Davinsky with a swift kick to his back! [crowd oooohs] JA: Davinsky drags James to his feet, and whips him to the ropes...James ducks the yakuza kick...kick to the guy by James; he double underhooks Gregor's arms...Powerbomb! [crowd oooohs] James drags Davinsky to his feet, and sets him up..Gutwrench Powerbomb! [crowd oooohs] James now with another fistdrop to Davinsky's head! AO: James drags Gregor to his feet, and hoists him over his shoulders...Stomachbreaker! [crowd oooohs] Davinsky is rolling around in pain! James lifts Davinsky up in his arms again... Fallaway Slam! [crowd ooohs] James looks ready to end it now! JA: James drags Davinsky to his feet, and headbutts him. He doubles him over, then elbowsmashes the back of his head. He runs to the ropes...SWINGING DDT! [crowd ooooohs] He covers...1...2...3!!! [crowd applauds] Ring Announcer: Your winner, CONRAD JAMES!!! [crowd applauds] AO: James is a powerful man. Davinsky is no lightweight! JA: He's a good worker. And unlike alot of other american wrestlers in SJPW currently, he has no anti-Japan bias. AO: Good. At least he isn't an idiot. The ring attendants have carried Davinsky out of the ring, and our next two competitors are in the ring... Ring Announcer: This next contest is one fall, 15 minute time limit. Introducing first, to my left, HA-RU-KI SHI-O-MI!! [crowd applauds] His opponent, to my right, making his return to SJPW, the man formerly known as Jiraiya, YAZ!! [crowd applauds] JA: What's with the name change Akira? AO: He is going back to his Mexican roots, or so he claims. JA: I see. Shiomi starts fast, whipping Yaz to the ropes, then nailing him with a spinwheel kick. Yaz falls back onto the ropes, and Shiomi catches him coming off with an armdrag. Yaz rolls to his feet, but Shiomi catches him with a rana, and takes him over again! [crowd applaud] AO: Yaz gets to his feet once more, and runs to the ropes... springboard rana takes Shiomi over. He gets to his feet, and Yaz catches him with a springboard dropkick! [crowd applauds] Shiomi hits the mat hard, and sits up. Yaz runs towards him, and nails him with a jumping neck snap! [crowd applauds] He runs to the ropes again...springboard legdrop! [crowd applauds] JA: Yaz gets to his feet, turns around, and..standing moonsault! He covers...1...2...kickout! [crowd applauds] He drags Shiomi to his feet, and plants him with a belly to back suplex. Yaz going to the top rope now...Frog Splash! [crowd oooohs] He grabs Shiomi by the legs...Double Leg Hook...1...2...kickout! [crowd applauds] AO: Yaz drags Shiomi to his feet, and takes him over with a Saito suplex. He goes to the top rope once more...flying somersault headbutt! [crowd ooohs] Yaz drags Shimoi to his feet, and applies a full nelson...Shiomi slips out of it, and takes Yaz over...1...2...kickout! [crowd applauds] JA: Both men on their feet, and Shiomi lays a chop across Yaz's chest [crowd "whooos"] He nails him with a forearm uppercut, then whips him to the ropes...Yaz ducks the spinwheel kick...jumping side kick levels Shiomi! [crowd ooooohs] Yaz is going to the top rope one more time....SHOOTING STAR!! [crowd oooohs] Cover...1...2...3!!! [crowd applauds] Ring Announcer: Your winner, YAZ!!! [crowd applauds] AO: It seems that Yaz still has all the skills he had as Jiraiya. JA: It seems that way. Yaz now helping Shiomi to his feet, and helps the man out of the ring. I must say that things are moving pretty fast tonight! AO: Good! I can't wait for Fujinami vs Hashimoto! Then our main event is going to be incredible! JA [holding his headset]: I just got word from the back; it seems Jim Mongol vs Steve Sampson has been cancelled. AO: What? Why? JA: It seems Mongol missed his flight. It happens. We've got Valentine & Kubota in the ring, so let's go to ringside... Ring Announcer: This next match is a Battle Arts Division match! Introducing first, from Nagoya, weighing 245lbs, KEN-I-CHI "THE CRUSHER" KU-BO-TA!! [crowd boooos] AO: Kubota is almost as bad as Ishrinku! Ring Announcer: His opponent, from Palm Springs, making his SJPW debut, "HARDKORE" JON-NIE VAL-EN-TINE!!! [crowd booos again as "7 Words" comes over the pa] JA: Both men are decent wrestlers. Valentine and I were both in the UWA; I teamed with one of his brothers in Texas. AO: As if anyone really cares about you or Valentine's brother! JA: One of these days Ono... AO: SHHHHHH! There's the bell, and Kubota rakes Valentine in the eyes, then whips him to the ropes...clothesline sends Valentine to the mat. Kubota now stomping away at Valentine's head and chest! JA: Kubota now legdropping Valentine, then covers...1...2..kickout! [crowd applauds] Kubota gets up, and drags Valentine to his feet. Jonnie drives a knee into Kubota's gut, then rams his head onto the turnbuckle. He gets up onto the second rope, then locks Kubota in a front facelock...spinning DDT! [crowd oooohs] AO: Valentine gets to his feet, and drags Kubota to his feet. He punches Kubota in the head, then sets him up for a T-Bone... Slingshot T-Bone suplex! [crowd ooooohs] Kubota landed right on his head! JA: Awesome T is heading down to ringside; I wonder why he's here? Valentine sees him, then smiles. T picks up a chair, and slides it into the ring. Valentine picks it up, and lays it across the top rope. He picks Kubota up, and whips him to the ropes...HOTSHOT onto the chair! [crowd ooooohs] Kubota went flying! AO: Valentine drags Kubota to his feet, scoops him up...Michinoku Driver! [crowd oooohs] He is destroying Kubota! I like it! Here come the Down Boys now; what do they want? JA: Jonnie drags Kubota to his feet, and Kubota rakes his eyes again, then takes him over with a swinging neckbreaker! [crowd oooohs] Where did Kubota get the energy to do that? Kubota gets to his feet, blood trickling from his forhead. He drags Jonnie to his feet, and bits his forehead! [crowd ooohs] Kubota scoops Jonnie up, then slams him to the mat. AO: Kubota with a fistdrop, then drags Valentine to his feet again. He sets him up...power-Hurricanrana by Valentine! [crowd ooohs] Both men get to their feet, and Valentine kicks Kubota in the gut. He picks up the chair, and throws it to Kubota, who catches it... DROPKICK by Valentine onto the chair! [crowd ooooohs] JA: Kubota falls back onto the ropes, and comes off with a forearm uppercut. Valentine staggers back, then comes back with a punch! Kubota grabs Valentine, then whips him to the ropes...springboard hurricanrana by Jonnie! [crowd oooohs] He hooks the legs... 1...2...3!!! [crowd applauds] Ring Announcer: Your winner, "HARDKORE" JONNIE VALENTINE!!! [crowd applauds again] AO: This Valentine is a decent wrestler. Too bad he wastes his time with Battlearts. This man could be a serious threat in the cruiserweight division. JA: Who says he won't compete in both Akira? Valentine walks off with the Down Boys and Awesome T. I wonder what is going on with them? AO: Who cares? Here comes Akira Saito! He's accompanying Tom Turner to the ring. I hope Turner has gotten his killer instinct out now! JA: I see he is wrestling Tsutomu Kobayashi. Didn't he beat Kikuchi recently? AO: Shut up Adonis. Ring Announcer: This match is one fall, 20 minute time limit. Introducing first, to my right, weighing 235 lbs, TSU-TO-MU KO-BAY-ASH-I!!! [crowd applauds] JA: This kid could go places if he continues to win. Ring Announcer: His opponent, to my left, weighing 230 lbs, accompanied by AKIRA SAITO, he is "CONSTABLE" TOM TUR-NER!! ["Give Me The Simple Life" comes on the pa, and the crowd cheers] AO: Both men lock up, and Tsutomu whips Turner to the ropes, and takes him over with a big back bodydrop! Turner lands hard, and Tsutomu dropkicks him in the back of the head! [crowd oooohs] JA: Akira Saito is on the apron, and he is arguing with the ref! Meanwhile, Kobayashi lands on Turner with a senton splash! Here come the Down Boys again! They are wearing IIWF t-shirts! Awesome T is coming down too! Akira Saito sees them, and starts to yell at T. T is trying to assure Saito that they aren't here to cause trouble, but he's not buying it! AO: Tsutomu scoops Turner up, then slams him to the mat. He goes to the top rope, and Awesome T jumps up on the apron. He starts to argue with the ref! The Adam Peterson is on the apron too, and he crotches Kobayashi! [crowd oooohs] Oliver up now, and both Down Boys double enzugiri Kobayashi off the top rope! [crowd oooohs] JA: The Down Boys jump down, and T gets down too. Saito doesn't know what to make of this! Turner gets to his feet, looking groggy. He sees Tsutomu on the mat, and grabs his leg... Figure Four Leglock! [crowd ooooohs] AO: Kobayashi is out from the Down Boys attack. The ref counts his shoulders...1...2...3!!! [crowd applauds, though some booo] Ring Announcer: Your winner, "CONSTABLE" TOM TURNER!!! [crowd applauds] JA: The Down Boys are in the ring now, and are patting Turner on the back! Saito is in the ring, and he pushes Peterson back! Awesome T is in the ring, and he is trying to assauge Saito. Oliver is behind Saito...LARIAT! [crowd ooooohs] Turner turns to Oliver, and punches him! [crowd cheers] Peterson hits Turner from behind. AO: The Down Boys now putting the boots to Turner, while T scoops up Saito...NORTHERN LIGHTS BOMB! [crowd oooohs] T now going over to the corner; it looks like he is pulling off the top turnbuckle. He rips it off, and calls the Down Boys over...DOUBLE HOTSHOT onto the corner! [crowd oooohs] JA: The Down Boys lift Turner up again, and DOUBLE HOTSHOT him again! [crowd oooooohs] [Awesome T grabs the microphone, and screams "IS THIS WHAT YOU PEOPLE WANT?", causing the crowd to booo] AO: Security has hit the ring, and the Down Boys & T exit the ring. Turner is bleeding! Saito is up, holding the back of his head. This isn't over between these two teams! I can promise you that! JA: The ring Attendants help the NorthPac backstage, the crowd cheering them all the way. [The camera shows the NorthPac heading backstage. It then cuts to Sharon Akiyama at the interview area...] Sharon Akiyama: Ladies and Gentlemen, at this time, please welcome SJPW Middleweight Champion ISHIKAWA!!! (Ishikawa enters the ring and grabs the microphone.) Ishikawa: I don't need someone to hold my microphone. I don't need someone to ask me moronic questions. This is not America! In Japan, we are educated people. We don't sit at home watching some Atlanta billionaire's fantasy. (Crowd pops as Akiyama looks shocked) Ishikawa: You can just stand there holding your microphone. I can do this myself. First off I want to thank my partner, the Great Oni, for his moral support. He will have his chance to shine but right now he understands and accepts this as my time. Japan needed a champion such as myself. A man that can wrestle any style. Technical, aerial, hardcore. You name it and I can do it. I maybe undersized in the wrestling world ...I lack true agility ...I admit that. I am not a power house. But to be honest most Japanese men fall into this category. I am nearly an every man. An average Japanese man. Except for 1 thing that separates me from all the others ... and that is this gold belt around my waist. (Crowd claps politely.) Ishikawa: September 26 I took care of "Tsunami" Rhyioto Makada. How many "Tsunami"s are there in the wrestling world? Is there no originality among Nihonjins? I guess not. That is until I step into the ring and dazzle the crowd with my skills. NOW, I am looking forward ... the future is a dark road with many curves and turns. However, the SJPW Middleweight title will remain in good hands for a lond time. I am not going to challenge people to step up to the plate. Why should I? I am the champion ... the hunted. Set-up the matches ... set-up the opponents and I will knock them down. This interview is over. (Ishikawa throws down the microphone and leaves. Akiyama picks it up) SA: Thank you Ishikawa. Back to you Akira & Jonnie. [camera cuts back to Adonis & Ono] AO: I like Ishikawa's attitude! JA: He definitely has self confidence. Well, it's main event time! Let's go to our ring announcer... Ring Announcer: This is our MAIN EVENT!!! [crowd cheers] It is one fall, 30 minute time limit. Introducing first, to my left, weighing 281 lbs, KI-A-TO FU-JI-NA-MI!! [crowd applauds as "Forever I may Roam" plays] AO: This will be a classic matchup! Ring Announcer: His opponent, to my right, weighing 260 lbs, A-KI-RA HASH-I-MO-TO!! [crowd erupts into cheers as "Honoo-No-Fighter" comes on the pa] JA: This will be a classic. Hashimoto has held titles around the world, and Fujinami is a former 3 time SJPW champion in it's previous incarnations. AO: Both men shake hands [crowd cheers] and start to circle each other. They lock up, and Fujinami backs Hashimoto to the ropes, and nails Akira with a european uppercut! [crowd ooohs] He nails another uppercut, then whips Hashimoto to the other side...Hashimoto ducks the clothesline, and comes off the ropes... Spinwheel kick! [crowd applauds] JA: Fujinami falls back onto the ropes, and Hashimoto chops Kiato across the chest [crowd "whooos"], then locks on a half nelson... T-Bone Suplex! [crowd ooooohs] Hashimoto drags Kiato to his feet, and whips him to the ropes...Fujinami blocks the hiptoss, then reverses! Akira gets to his feet, and Kiato levels him with a clothesline! [crowd oooohs] AO: Kiato drags Hashimoto to his feet, and takes him down with a russian legsweep. He covers...1...2...kickout! [crowd applauds] Kiato drags Hashimoto up, and nails him with a backbreaker. He gets to his feet, and drops the elbow. He drops another elbow, then goes to the corner. He goes up to the top rope, and sets himself, waiting. The ref starts to count, as Hashimoto gets to his feet... JA: Kiato comes off the top rope, but Hashimoto side steps, and Kiato crashes to the mat! [crowd ooooohs] Hashimoto falls back onto the ropes, and lays there. Kiato gets to his feet, and Hashimoto leaps, nailing Kiato in the shoulder with a vicious Axe kick! [crowd oooohs] Kiato drops to one knee, and Hashimoto lays into the side of his head with forearm shots. AO: Tommy Chang and Dustin Bones are coming to the ring now. Chang is up on the apron, and he starts to yell at the referee. Hashimoto scoops Fujinami up, and plants him with Northern Lights Bomb! [crowd oooohs] JA: Dustin Bones, and nails Hashimoto from behind with a clothesline. Hashimoto goes down, and Bones drags him to his feet, locking his hand around his throat...CHOKESLAM!!! [crowd oooohs] He drags Hashimoto to his feet again...HEART PUNCH!! [crowd oooohs] AO: Bones drops Hashimoto, and rolls out of the ring. Chang keeps the referee occupied, giving Fujinami time to recover. Fujinami gets to his feet, holding the back of his head. He sees Hashimoto laying there, then sees Bones and Chang. He goes over to the referee, and points to Hashimoto. The referee is shaking his head, and Fujinami now arguing with Chang! [crowd oooohs] JA: Fujinami turns to the ref, and says something. The ref is calling for the bell! What is going on? Ring Announcer: The referee has informed me that Kiato Fujinami has forfeited this match [crowd oooohs]. Therefore your winner, AKIRA HASHIMOTO!!! [crowd cheers] AO: I don't believe this! Fujinami turning his back on the House of Hate? Chang is in the ring now, and he is yelling at Fujinami; Kiato drives a knee into Chang's gut, and powerbombs him! [crowd oooohs] Bones is in the ring, and he clotheslines Kiato into the corner, where he lays into him with knee smashes! [crowd oooohs] JA: Hashimoto is getting to his feet, and he sees Bones beating on Fujinami. He asks the ref what happened; Hashimoto now going to the corner. He grabs Bones from behind...GERMAN SUPLEX! [crowd oooohs] Bones hit the back of his head, but somehow gets to his feet. Hashimoto locks him in a half nelson...T-Bone Suplex! [crowd oooohs] Bones now rolling out of the ring! AO: Hashimoto now checking on Fujinami, who seems to be telling him he is ok. Hashimoto helps Fujinami to the center of the ring, and offers him his hand [crowd starts chanting "Kiato! Kiato!"] Fujinami shakes! [crowd cheers] Hashimoto now raising his arm in victory! It looks like Fujinami has turned his back on the House of Hate, and has gone back to his old ways! Maybe these two men will join forces? JA: I guess we'll find that out in the weeks to come. We are out of time now. For Akira Ono, I'm Jonnie Adonis. We'll see you again, AFTER DARK!!!
