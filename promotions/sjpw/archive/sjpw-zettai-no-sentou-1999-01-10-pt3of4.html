[Zettai no Sentou continues...] ER: Our next segment is a special interview Akira Ono conducted with Kensei Tokugawa, Kabuki Kid's partner in the Ronin. With Kabuki Kid's potential retirement, Tokugawa has seemed directionless of late. AO: This interview will shed some new light on "The Samurai". Just past the gate is a bridge that goes over a small mote that leads to the front area of the castle yard and castle itself. A brick road leads to the front gate with light poles and trees lining it. There, standing in front of the gate is the legendary Akira Ono.] AO: Konnichiwa, everyone. I’m standing out front of Nagoya Castle in Nagoya Japan. I’m sure your asking, why is Ono-san there? To be frank, I’m not really sure myself, other than to say that Kensei Tokugawa-san invited me to interview him, and that’s what I’m going to do. [The scene goes black, then cuts back on. AO: Domo arigato for inviting me. It’s very nice. [Kensei whips the sweat off his body with a towel and sits down on the edge of the ring apron.] KT: Hai, it is. Only the best when it comes to Tsuburaya Enterprises. Mr. Tsuburaya demands it. AO: Shall we get started? KT: Hai. [The scene goes black once again. When the camera cuts back on. Akira is siting on a beautiful black leather couch. Kensei is now in a red robe, still with his wrestling boots on sticking out the bottom. He’s sitting in a black leather love seat.] AO: Kensei-kun, exactly, what are your plans in Super Japan Pro-Wrestling? This question may be typical, but it isn’t in any way. I ask it because of the recent situation yourself and Kabuki Kid-san have been put in. KT: I understand where you’re coming from. First of all, we had a situation of being over booked. Not a bad thing, if you’re talking about a career, but it did wear Kabuki-kun and I out. So, we took a bit of a break. Unfortunately, my partner, and Daimyoite, Kabuki had a mandatory leave of absence. AO: You’re referring to his broken neck? KT: Hai. The warrior’s soul was more than his body could handle. Kid-san went a little too far after going a little too uncontrolled for so long. AO: You can’t mean you’re upset at his wrestling style? KT: Iie, of course not. It’s how he got to where he’s at. However, for those around him like Ms. Miki and myself, you hate to see him push his body further than it’s limits. But, let it be known that what the Kid does in the ring is special. He’s a one of a kind that cannot be matched. AO: In someways I agree, but in others I must disagree. Kabuki Kid’s in ring abilities are incredible I do grant you, but some of his extracurricular tactics are questionable. KT: A warrior does what he has to get the battle over with. If it takes extra, non wrestling tactics to get it done, so be it. If that’s what he chooses to do, who am I to question it. In the past you’ve questioned my tactics, my words. Would you not say that I’ve backed my statements up, even when you disapproved? AO: Hai. KT: In the tradition of a true warrior, if you say it or do it, don’t be surprised if it gets done to you. Kabuki Kid and myself cross the line on occasion, but we fully realize the consequences. Unlike some others who do it for the wrong reasons. AO: Are there ever right reasons? KT: Sometimes yes. Sometimes it’s the only way to send a message, to end the situation. AO: You’re saying the end justifies the means? KT: Hai, in most cases. AO: How does that go with your code as a Samurai? KT: Good question Ono-san. I knew you would be a good interviewer. AO: [Nods his head] Arigato. KT: [Now with an intense expression] I often refer to the squared circle as the battlefield on which two warriors go toe to toe using their particular skills to out do their rival and gain a victory. The "Sensou no geijutsu" is the bible that I follow. Once I’m in battle, anything goes. Hai, I agree some things aren’t as honorable as others, but they are nothing more than what the other warrior can use to help himself reach his goal. If I choose to use my kendo stick in battle, it’s just as useful to me as it is my opponent, should he be able to get it from my grasp, and know what to do with it afterwards. AO: You said earlier that there are people who use illegal tactics for the wrong reasons. Who were you referring too? KT: The SHVF and that kuso Hai Moditan-san. He comes in and thinks he’s going to take over. He forces his thoughts and antics on all of us. You heard what I said in the ring a few weeks ago, and I meant every word of it. The wrong tactics for the wrong reason. If Moditan-san wants something, I say it should be settled in the ring. That’s the battlefield that this entire sport is focused upon. Do it like a true warrior, put your best in the ring with the best of the SJPW and may the strong survive. That is the only honorable why to solve this situation, the only respectable way. AO: Speaking of that speech you gave a few weeks ago. I must say it was very inspiring. You really spoke from the heart. I was very much impressed. KT: Arigato. It felt good to say what I felt for a change. I had been quiet for so long, it just had to be. AO: How about we play the name game? KT: Hai. AO: Shinji Hamada. KT: My recent defeat to him doesn’t sit well with me. I know I can beat him. It’s a shame it had to come at such a bad time for me. My mind has been in other places. That’s not an excuse though. The better man won the match. Hamada-san, I wont forget it. We will face each other again, and trust me the results wont be the same. AO: Joey Rappaport? KT: What can I say? I gave him a beating once, and he took it in stride. He didn’t flinch and just moved on. The Senshi spirit in that kid is great. Most guys when faced with a set back get stuck there. They can’t move on. Joey-kun not only moved on, he surpassed it. He’s a true warrior. One guy I wish I could face again. AO: Jet Jaguar? KT: Suzuki-san, well that’s a situation I don’t need to get into right now. Let him and Banshee-san have their cake and eat it, for now. Kabuki Kid, Kagemusha and myself will get back to them in time. When it’s right. But, Suzuki is a great athlete. AO: Tokyo Express? KT: Two very honorable men. In the past the Ronin did them a favor, and what did they do in return? They paid us back. Very surprising to say the least. he favor we did for them didn’t come with string attached. They repaid us anyway, out of respect. I’ve got nothing but good things to say about those two. AO: How about the Yakuza? [Kensei glances at Akira with a smirk.] KT: Is that a set up question? You know how I feel about them. They stand for all that’s wrong with Japanese history. They stand for scum beneath my feet. They take pride in a blemish of Japanese society. Yakuza, the Japanese mafia. A mafia built off the backs of good people. The mafia is nothing more than the ninja mushi that infested this great country in the past. Like ninja, they take the good and exploit it. Same goes for the Yakuza. Those to inu need to be taught a lesson. [Kensei takes a deep breath.] But that again is a bees nest best left alone until my partner returns. AO: That brings up the last name on my list, the Kabuki Kid? KT: He’s my friend. I can honestly say that I respect him more than any other person on the face of this earth. Always been there for me and I’ve always tried to be there or him. In the recent past, I failed in that task. For that, I am ashamed. AO: How so? KT: He was doing battle in a few leagues that I wasn’t in. I wasn’t around to watch his back. It comes back to the art of war, what can be used against you will be. Kabuki Kid went into G-Pro blind. He went against three men and fell short. Three on one aren’t good odds. AO: Wasn’t Satsuma and Nakajima there? KT: Hai, they were. But you’ve got to realize that when the Fabs are around, everyone else is going to be gunning for them. They did their best, but they were spread too thin as well. When I broke into the sport, it was Kabuki Kid who found me and gave me a chance. Sure, it was Tsuburaya who had the money and the connections that Ms. Miki perfectly manipulated to get he Daimyo where it is day, but it was Kabuki Kid who introduced me to it. When I came aboard, I accepted my role as the enforcer of the Daimyo. I accepted the situation. The spotlight wouldn’t be on me, and I like it like that. I also accepted the role of protector. You mess with the Daimyo, you answer to me. That’s the bottom line. The Fabs did what they could, but I failed. AO: Would that be the reason behind your change in wrestling style? KT: I believe so. Unconsciously, I feel lost a bit. I know what I’m good at and yet, I wander at times. I’m a wear you done kind of wrestler. Take you apart limb by limb until there’s nothing left to do but hit you with the heart punch and slap on the hangman...till you say I quit! AO: Well, I’d say we’ve got enough for an interview. KT: Domo arigato for stopping by. But, before you leave, I’ve got a hold I’ve been working on. It’s a submission hold you might know?
