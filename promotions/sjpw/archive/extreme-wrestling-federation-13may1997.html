I think we should go back to that Just my 2 cents > > [name withheld] My fed, SJPW is quality old wrestling too. It's less than a month old, but the handlers in the fed (at least one has a toop 200, as far as I know) think it's great that people are not interfering every match, and that 95% of the time, there is no controversy over the win. THat is why I chose to run an efed in Japan. Quality is the most important thing in Japan (at least it was; I don't know how they are handling the NWO situation there since that started). I also keep extreme matches (and the Extreme title, or Battlecade Championship in SJPW) limited to ppv's, and some house shows, but not on Ring Wars, the weekly TV taping. I think there are quality efeds out there. You just need to look for them. I'm in at least one or two that I can thing of (NAEC and Wild West Wrestling) where interference doesn't happen every match, or even every card. IIWF is the best damn fed I've seen, and is an inspiration for me when I do my SJPW cards. Quality feds are out there; you just have to sort through the rest to find them! [name withheld] SJPW
