This upcoming match, as he sees it, is simply a chance for him to get his hands on a nice belt and a golden trophy. He' cares nothing for the symbolism behind any of it. He cares not if he's the best, so long as he has a fat wallet.....It is uncharacteristic of me to say such things about a person, but I could find nothing complimentary to say about him..." [Reaching for his cup of tea, Takada-san continues to speak....] Toshiaki Takada:"...And what of my feelings on the corporate team?? Thugs. Worthless, talentless, and ultimately faceless. To take in such a wrestler as Kubota means to partner yourself with a diseased man....Perhaps his downfall at my hands could lead to the downfall of the entire group.....This metaphysical 'disease' I'm referring to is quite catching.....Perhaps, they will all be infected....." [Takada-san raises his cup to his lips and drinks, the contents being slurped rapidly into his mouth. Johnny, content with the answer given, asks him yet another question....] Jonnie Adonis:"...Takada-san, with the above answered, I would like to ask you, how will you counteract the methods of the corporate team, that is, of course, assuming that they do as they did with Tiger Extreme....." Toshiaki Takada:"..Adonis-san, I believe that question is best left unanswered. I do, of course, have a strategy, and I would certainly hope that if I needed any help it would be given to me, but......Let's just say that a certain friend of mine may be returning to Super Japan puroresu....." Jonnie Adonis:"..Might you be referring to Matt Nakamoto, the man that has been absent thus far in your return here in SJPW? Or are you referring to someone else?" [Takada-san giggles, and answers his question.] Toshiaki Takada:"..I believe that you've hit the nail on the head Adonis-san. Nakamoto-san, conspicious by his absence, shall soon return with me to SJPW. I cannot say when, for to do so would be to give too much away..." [Jonnie Adonis, content with his answer, scratches his chin and continues the interview....] Jonnie Adonis:"...Takada-san, I believe that I should ask you about a certain incident that has created lots of controversy as of late. As I'm sure you know, a familiar face here in the SJPW has said some rather important things that pertain to you....Tetsuo Matsumoto, as we all know him, has challenged you with the hope that, if he defeats you, he will become the number one contender for Ed Dean's All-Asian title....You care to give some comments??....." [With the question posed, Takada-san's face begins to turn bright red, his emmotions obviously becoming involved in the conversation.] Toshiaki Takada:"..Tetsuo Matsumoto, the man named after the rather questionable movie known as 'Iron Man,' is obviously quite upset... This I can see. However, I will not question his reasons. Simply put, he is disgruntled. As he sees it, Tetsuo is a technical wrestler stranded in a world of cannibalistic, barbwire maniacs.....I will, however, question his ability to handle his emmotions when placed in such a situation....Matsumoto-san has no respect for me because he considers me to be a no-talent 'Garbage' wrestler. If that is what he believes, however, he knows nothing of my past...But, I needn't explain myself to him....As far as a signed match goes, I would truly enjoy stepping into the ring with him. He plans to use me as a stepping stone; he thinks that I am merely a speedbump in his quest to attain the All-Asian title...." [Takada-san pauses briefly to pour some more tea for Jonnie Adonis, and finishing this task, continues his speech.] Toshiaki Takada:".....I have but one stipulation to lay down if such a match is to be signed....I would simply ask that the winner of this match be declared the number one contender for the All-Asian title, whether it be myself, or the 'Iron Man'...." [Jonnie Adonis, having heard this remark, nearly spits a mouthfull of green tea all over his host. Gathering himself together, he follows this point up...] Jonnie Adonis:"...Takada-san, are you saying that should you win in this hypothetical situation, you should become the contender for the All-Asian title? Please tell me, what about your Kobousen Gaijutsu championship?.." Toshiaki Takada:"Johnny-san, you know I cherish this title. However, when the chance to become the 'Greatest pound-for-pound wrestler in the world' as I earlier put it, should present itself, I would hope that I'd be sane enough to capitalize on such an opportunity.....I would hope that others in the world of puroresu could see me as being worthy of such an honor....I've worked towards it all my life. My martial arts credentials certainly speak for themselves....But, we must first focus on matters in the near future...." [Arising to his feet, Jonnie bows to his host and utters his goodbyes...] Jonnie Adonis:"...Takada-san, I appreciate you answering all my questions. I would also like to thank you for being such a gracious host..Arigato, and goodbye......" [Toshiaki utters his last goodbyes to the man. As the camera moves to a close- up of Jonnie Adonis. He speaks one last time before the scene fades out....] Johnny Adonis:"...Now, where the hell are my shoes??......" [The scene fades out.....]
