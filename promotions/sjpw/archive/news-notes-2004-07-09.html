News & Notes 7/9/04

Jul 11, 2004, 12:00:00 AM
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Both Takahiro KEOKI and GPH champion "Ecchi Kid" Kentaro "H-Bomb" Kawasaki have been confirmed for the Super 8 Tournament in the United States. They square off in the first round, to be held at the MGM Grand on July 17th. Due to the fact they are facing that night, SJPW officials are rebooking The Zone! show for July 17th. The new lineup will be listed below. Speaking of Takahiro KEOKI, Noboru "Raging Bull" Nishida, a fellow member of Team Tiger, took issue with some comments KEOKI made in his interview with Ken Otaku on July 1st: "KEOKI-san claims he does not "blame" anyone for not stepping up to face Despair for his actions. Does KEOKI not recall that it was Despair who helped cost Lanther his NCWF title? You took it upon yourself to challenge Despair without allowing Lanther-san to do so. You chose to stoop to Despair's level, and fight in matches without honor. You have much to learn KEOKI-san, the first of which is not to insult the honor of your team mates. You may not have intended on doing so intentionally, but insult us you have. An apology is in order, and if you are not man enough to make one yourself, then perhaps one of us should force you to....in the ring!" When asked about the situation with the Dokyu Dojo, Nishida said: "This is a troubling situation. I have teamed with Toroyama-san before, and I believe him to be a man of honor. As Kikuchi-san has said, this is not a matter between Team Tiger and the Dokyu Dojo. However, if the Dokyu Dojo wishes to start a war, they had best beware. While Kikuchi-san does not want war with Dokyu, if they continue to insult us, they will see that the claws of Team Tiger are very sharp, and may well mark them for life!" Nishida then commented on his new tag team partner, MAMMOTH Kagawa, and their upcoming match in Pro Wrestling Glory with The Crush Crew: "Kagawa is a powerful young man, one who has a bright future ahead of him. He is a little dejected about losing to Toroyama at Zenmen Sensou, but I have been working with him to hone his limitless energy into a focused weapon. We've defeated the Crush Crew once, and will do so again at Legends are Born! The Disciples of Despair will not prevail against the fury of Team Tiger!" Speaking of Masashi "Lightning" Lanther, he too was asked his thoughts on the situation with the Dokyu Dojo: "As Kikuchi-san stated, there is no "war" between Team Tiger and Dokyu, unless it is one of their own making. While Kikuchi-san does not wish for conflict between our schools, I will not allow our school's honor to be questioned. If Super Mask, whom I have fought before, wishes it, I will gladly face him for my NCWF title. Failing that, let his protege, Super Mask NEO challenge me. I will gladly defeat him, as I did Super Mask when I beat him for the Old Japan Lightheavyweight championship for the first time" When asked about his challenge to Kentaro Kawasaki, Lanther said: "The man disgraced a tournament honoring the greatest wrestler of our country! What he did was dishonorable, and worse, there were those who applauded his actions! It is a sad day for Nippon when such an act garners applause. Much like Tasogare no Prince, I have stayed out of the Sedei-X tournament to prepare to challenger Kawasaki. Yamashita-san has agreed to let me face him first, and if I fail, then it falls to Tasogare no Prince to uphold the honor of Super Japan against this cretin. We cannot allow such a man to represent our promotion as it's champion. Bad enough we have Despair as Gunryo champion!" When asked about Nishida's comments regarding KEOKI, Lanther only said: "KEOKI-san came to me about facing Despair, and I saw the hunger in his eyes. As much as I wanted satisfaction from him, I did not want to disappoint such eagerness in a young lion. I do agree with Nishida that the rest of Team Tiger did nothing because KEOKI wanted this, and we allowed him to follow the path he chose. Both men need to settle their differences though, as we have enough enemies from without to fend off, so internal dissension will just disrupt our unified harmony" Pramoj Ananda has returned to Thailand after his defeat at the hands of Wolf Jager. Ananda's departure is only temporary, as the Thai sensation plans on getting back into his Muay Thai roots, and prepare himself for a return to Super Japan. "Wolf Jager proved to be a better opponent than I had imagined. Although he wanted to make me submit, my warrior's spirit would not allow that to happen. I lost, but at least did so with my honor intact. My issues with Jager are over, but his fellow EuroPack members are a different matter. I know Baranov and Davinsky still have issues with me over Davinky's concussion. When I return, I will be more than happy to settle those issues with them. Add Se-hong Kwan to the list of people gearing up to challenge Despair for his Gunryo title. The veteran martial artist from Korea has long been a staple of the Jisatsu wars in various versions of SJPW, and this time, he's ready to take the gold: "I feel the time is right for me to finally achieve my destiny of winning a title in Super Japan. Too many times I've come close, only to fail to grasp the gold. Despair is one of the best champions since Ishrinku or Steel Serpent, but his match schedule is grinding him down. If Azteca does not take the gold, then I may finally have the chance to win what has eluded me for so long." Of all the people in Super Japan, no one expected Super Bakemono to come to the defense of KEOKI for the comments he made regarding his fellow Team Tiger members. In a statement released by SJPW, Bakemono is quoted as saying: "I applaud KEOKI for pointing out that no one else from Team Tiger had the courage to face Despair. They talk so much about honor and the like, yet when KEOKI points out an obvious fact, he is taken to task for it. Pitiful" Bakemono also expressed support for his fellow Team Tiger graduate, Junya Toroyama: "Toroyama-san did not betray Team Tiger, any more than I have. We simply don't buy into the mystique of Tiger Kikuchi. He expects all graduates to do as he says, when he says it. Toroyama-san has his reasons for aligning with the Dokyu Dojo, and instead of simply asking him what those reasons were, he was immediately branded a traitor, and blamed for the loss in that trios match. I don't know what everyone else saw, but I saw someone else cost Team Tiger that match, not Toroyama. If Toroyama-san ever needs assistance, he can be assured that I will gladly be there to help out. We trained at Chiimu Tora together, and he knows when I give my word, I keep it. This is not an offer from the Disciples of Despair either; this is simply from one graduate of Chiimu Tora to another graduate. If you need my help Toroyama-san, ask, and I will be there to fight by your side." The Maulers are gearing up for the GPH tag team league, and one team they look forward to facing are Van Wild & Scott Starring: Frankie "The Thumper" Douglas: "We heard a lot of good things about these guys, and we've seen a few matches. They look to be the team to beat in this league, so we look forward to facing them. It should be a great match up!" Doug Thomas: "I think we have the edge in experience, but we won't underestimate a team with their credentials. That would be foolish, and could cost us in the end. Like Frankie said, this should be a great contest between our respective teams, and we're really looking forward to facing some new competition" The revised lineup for the July 17th Show is below. Block A: "All Star" Joey Darren vs Mr. Smiley Mask Junior Struggle! Block B: "Kyoto Tiger" Takayoshi Asakura vs Super Bakemono Incredible Teams Tag Match: Despair & El Santa Azteca vs "GAIJIN Extreme" Jun Suzuki & "Lone Wolf" Brody Thunder
