SA: I like it too, but what about your reactions to that announcement by Lance Smith? PBC: Well, what can I say? I've been asking for some response, only Jonnie would do that in passing, and I get one, wasn't expecting a Champion to respond by telling me he wants to defend his title against me. Listen, Lance, I'll tell ya straight, all you had to do was ask nicely, man, I'd accept on the spot. I heard the coments, and when you told me we were gonna face. I thought, what the hell, if you can't be polite, I'll pass the opportunity by, but you know me, or should do, I'd love to try and prove you wrong. SA: What about Hiro Kanemoto? PBC: What about him? I've just been sliding by in the SJPW, he hasn't really SHONE as such, but then everyone knows my opinions on who has shone, Super Tengu. SA: Is it a copincidence or curse that you are BOTH Ranked 8? CW: Erm, who knows? Are we alone? Are we in a parrellel universe? Wh knows? In fact, Who cares if we are both ranked the same. Anyway, I'm ranked fifth Sharon! <A small ripple of laughter as Sharon acknowledges the mistake by blushing.> SA: So you are. What are both your thoughts on the top ten contenders? PBC: It is not the other contenders you have to be impressed by, in awe of, even have opinions on, we are in the same boat, Titleless, and struggling to get our shots. We depend on Lance giving us shots, accepting our challenges and what have you. The Title Holders are the ones to worry about. Of course, you must keep your eye on your Zero Gravity's, who I have known for years, and have fought and lost too. Your Nik Nyce's who can't belive you don't mind losing, or your Elijah's. I know most of the guys above me, can, have and could beat the hell out of me, I also know I could do the same to them. Wrestling is a dangerous, and intriguing sport, it's like most sports, you have your giant killers. A Title Holder can lose every non-title match to someone half their stature, yet face top contenders and obliterate them. You ook at them all individually, treat them the same, with RESPECT!! CW: Hell, I'm unbeaten Sharon, check it on your paper if you want, but I just wanna stay that ways as longa s possible. Patty, darling, you're right about everything, you could and probably can, be beaten and beate everyone above you, I could say the same. What more can I say? SA: OK then, say that tomorrow Mr. Yamashita asked you to face the champions and you both won, what you do? PBC: Sharon, the hypothetical is all well and good, but is it possible? NO, but I'll answer the question, I'd go to Yamashita and ask him why we were granted the shots and then I'd pinch myself, if I wake up, I know I was dreaming. SA: Cherry? CW: Let's face it, it ain't gonna happen is it? SA: OK, Patty, we saw you mention this Wrestling University. What is this Uni'? PBC: The University is to help people who want to enter wrestling for whatever reason, anyw alk of life. Rich, poor, back, white, oriental, and for whatever reason, and whatever skill and strength level, you don't have to be able to lift a feather, just be ready to work. CW: it's a fun place. SA: Well, thank you for your time and good luck with your tite shot at Lance. PBC: Thank you. <"Gimme all your Lovin'" by ZZ Top plays a again and the two exit to a rousing chant of "PBC! PBC!" and some whsitles directed toward Cherry by the male fans.> [camera cuts to the Control Center, where Eddie Rose and Akira Ono sit] Eddie Rose; Welcome back to After Dark everyone. As you just saw Patty Babes Carter has accepted the challenge by WWGP World Heavyweight champion "Too Sexy" Lance Smith to face him for the World Title. Akira Ono: Picking Carter was a good choice. After PBC takes the belt, the title will once again have honor! ER: Now we just need to confirm when this match will take place. Will it be here on After Dark? Will it be on Ring Wars, or will it be at Invasion, USA? AO: I hope it will be at Invasion, USA. Think of it: A Japanese wrestling promotion hosting a ppv in America. A British wrestler beat some punk American for the World Title. Classic! [chuckles] ER[sighing]: You never give up, do you? AO: Of course not. I enjoy it too much.
