Mr. Yamashita: Shinji Hamada, what Eddie Rose tells you is true. Super Tengu set me up, then blackmailed me to keep the evidence concealed, so you would always be branded! Yamashita: He also blames you for what Robin Blaze did to him. He suspects that you turned her against you. He even blames you for
Jet Jaguar beating him for the cruiserweight title! Everytime something goes against him, he blames YOU! Please forgive me

He takes Rekka by the hand, then exits the ring, his head still lowered in shame. The crowd starts to cheer Hamada as he walks to the back, but he doesn't acknowledge them. his head still held low in shame. Ring Announcer: Ladies & Gentlemen, the referee has determined this match to be a NO CONTEST!!!!
