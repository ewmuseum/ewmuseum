Eddie Rose and Akira Ono are sitting in their respective seats] Eddie Rose: Welcome everyone to the SJPW Control Center. On this addition, we'll see the remaining matches the SJPW contingent recently wrestled while on tour in HCW in the US. Akira Ono. We also have interviews with Patty Babes Carter, "Demon Boy" Ishrinku, and a video montage from Misae Suzuki! ER: Our first match pitted Keiji Asamiya & Kazuya Asakino against Kyu Kabuki of the Japan AC, and "Too Sexy" Lance Smith of the Gaijin. For some reason, The announcers had Kabuki listed as the Cruiserweight champion, but to reassure you, Super Tengu did not lose the title while in America. He had not title defenses while in HCW. Sharon Akiyama was helping with play by play. Let's see what happened.... ------------------------------------------------------------------- [From HCW Tuesday Night-Mare 8/5/97] [Cut to Sharon Akiyama, sitting at a ringside table, smiling] Sharon: Thanks guys, now we have the hailed Lance Smith and Kyu Kabuki facing the TAG Team of Keiji Asamiya and Kazuya Asakino. *Cut to aisle*** [DING DING DING!!] [Fans ROAR] [Keiji Asamiya and Kazuya Asakino walk down the aisle, in pure black padded tights, drawing some heat from the fans] David: FROM THE SJPW....KAZUYA ASAKINO AND KEIJI ASAMIYA!!!!!!!!!!! ...AND THEIR OPPONENTS, SJPW's CRUISERWEIGHT CHAMPION...KYU KABUKI AND "TOO SEXY" LANCE SMITH!!! [FANS CHEER] Sharon: Here comes Too Sexy Lance Smith, in the green and pink, and Kyu Kabuki, in the AC T-Shirt and baseball cap...No sign of the Degaus...that's weird. [Keiji and Kazuya wait in the ring until Kyu and Lance turn to the fans, and then Keiji and Kazuya grab the ropes, and SPRINGBOARD over onto Their Opponents!] Sharon: Wow! [FANS CHEER!!!] Sharon: How about that! Lance is down, Kyu is on his back, Keiji and Kazuya in control early! Kieji has taken a fan's chair, and Kazuya WHIPS Lance towards him, and Keiji throws the chair IN THE AIR and Dropkicks it into LANCE!!] [FANS CHEER] [Keiji and Kazuya pull Kyu up, and they Double Atomic Drop him on the rail-Keiji hops to the apron, and as Kazuya holds Kyu on the railing, Keiji JUMPS to the Second rope, and FLIPPING back, ASAI Moonsaults INTO KYU!] [FANS CHANT SJPW!! SJPW!! SJPW!!] Sharon: GREAT Reception tonight! Keiji and Kazuya came to FIGHT tonight! Wow. Keiji has a chair -Lance is up- KEIJI THROWS it INTO his head!! [FANS CHEER!] Sharon: Lance is down. [Kazuya picks up Lance by the hair, and Drags him to his feet, and Suplexes him into the ROPES, and SLINGSHOTS him Gutfirst into the guardrail!! And Kazuya hops to the railing, LEGDROP to the back of the head takes Lance over!!!] Sharon: These guys are pulling moves I'VE never seen!! Wow, turning out all the stops to [Kyu hops over the railing, and slides into the ring- and a fan hands him a Schwala® Mop! Keiji climbs in, and and KYU SWINGS- BREAKING the mop on his head!- Keiji Asamiya goes DOWN to the mat...Kyu grabs Ref Janet Hill, she turns and Pushes him over Keiji, who has the sense to ROLL HIM UP!!] [FANS CHEER!!!] [Janet counts] 1 2 3!! [DING DING DING!!] [A bloody Keiji Asamiya crawls around, and Kyu is FURIOUS] Sharon: !! Keiji is KICKED- he tumbles to the floor!!! NO!! [Kazuya grabs a Chair- going to take revenge out on Lance- BEN TOUSSI runs down and gets in the way!!] [FANS ROAR] Sharon: Ben Toussi of HCW here, PROTECTING Lance Smith?! [FANS CHEER] [Kazuya drops the chair, and goes to help his partner, as Kyu stumbles past him, in a huff....] [Ben leaves to the back, slapping a few hands, and eventually Lance gets up, and leaves...The Tag Team of Keiji and Kazuya celebrate in the ring, Keiji holding his head where the mop broke] ------------------------------------------------------------------- AO: What a short, but bloody match. Before we go to Super Tengu vs "Demon Boy" Ishrinku, we have some comments from the NorthPac Coalition, responding to some remarks made by "The Perfectionist" Nik Nyce.... ------------------------------------------------------------------- [Taped 8/9/97] [Constable Tom Turner and Akira Saito are sitting ina gloomy gym when the cameraman approaches them. Turner looks a little sad.] CTT: Ever since I was a little kid 've been fighting Timothy's battles. He would always mouth off to the wrong person and I would have to bail him out. It looks like that is happening again. Mr.Nyce I... AS: Don't worry about it, Tom. This guy is nothing. We can... CTT: (interrupting): No, Akira. That was the old way. That was T im's way. We walk the path of justice and if Mr.Nyce wants to settle old scores I will be happy to join him in the ring. You go after that title you have been seeking. I will face Mr.Nyce and show him what true justice really is. ------------------------------------------------------------------- ER: "Constable" Tom Turner accepting the challenge of Nik Nyce. That should be an interesting match. AO: Nyce will slaughter that good two shoes! ER: Ahem. Before we talk about the next match, here are some recent comments made by All Asian Woman's champion, Baby Hotta, in response to comments from her #1 contendor, Pizzazz.... ------------------------------------------------------------------- [Taped 8/9/97] (SCENE: BABY HOTTA, playing with her hair as she gives an interview through a mirror...) Baby Hotta: Pizz...azz. I tried to listen to what you had to say, believe me I did, but I couldn't understand a word of it. I wish someone would've taught me French... (Hotta winces as she looks like she's about to cry. She sniffles and her face goes back to a smile) Baby Hotta: I'm assuming you were talking about me, but I can't be certain. Maybe you said "otter." I like to think of myself as a leopard, personally. Wily, strong, fast...y'know, Kyoto is a lot like...umm...the jungle? (Hotta turns and faces the camera directly, looking for validation for what she just said. She gets none, and just turns back into the mirror smiling) Baby Hotta: Yep, a leopard. Leopards have a pretty strict diet; it's necessary to keep them lean. We like ice cream and chocolate and FROGS. We like frogs the most. It's funny, I once heard it said that French people are called "frogs." Why is that? I've never seen a frog smoke before. And a frog cetainly couldn't wear the SJPW Women's All-Asian belt...their hips are very wide and there is only a certain number of notches on the strap. (Hotta stops playing with her hair and picks up the All-Asian belt, playing with the studs on the strap. She gets a very sad look in her eye) Baby Hotta: My Tamagotchi baby died because I spent too much time on the road and not enough time with it. It's hard being a champion... (Hotta sniffles and then smiles again. She turns and looks directly in the camera again) Baby Hotta: Maybe, Pizzazz? Maybe you could come and live with me after I beat you? I could get a nice big glass tank and put rocks and water in it for you? Kyoto is like a jungle, and I know that frogs like the jungle. Some even live there! I've been awfuilly lonely since my Tamagotchi died. (Hotta looks up) Baby Hotta: Yep, she's up in Heaven now. Playing Pachinko with Rekka Sakura's dad. I hope she's keeping track of the scores, 'cause I know that no-good vampire will try and cheat her. Like father, like daughter, I always say. (Hotta turns back to the mirror and pulls her hair into a pony-tail on the top of her head) Baby Hotta: Rrrrrrriiibbit!! Rrrrrrrriiibbit!! (She winks and whispers) Baby Hotta: For all of you at home...I just passed along a very important message to Pizzazz. Words from the great Zen Master Sozan. "Hide your good deeds and keep your functioning secret. Look like a simpleton or a fool." Take that as a gray cloud for your silver lining, Pizzazz. Rrrrrrriiibbit!! (Baby Hotta breaks out in laughter) Baby Hotta: Wow! French is such a simple language!! (Scene fades to black as Hotta watches herself ribbit over and over again in the mirror) ------------------------------------------------------------------- AO [shaking his head]: That woman is very, very disturbed! I much prefer Robin Blaze! ER: That's because she flirst with you. Up next, Akira Ono's favorite wrestler, Super Tengu, as he battles the most vicious man in SJPW, "Demon Boy" Ishrinku, in HCW.... ------------------------------------------------------------------- [From HCW Tuesday Night-Mare 8/5/97] (Scene returns to Sharon at ringside, whilst the fans surround the railing, acting obnoxious, and blocking the fans behind them with 6 foot Signs that say stuff like "EAT at MOE'S" and "Nerdman 3:16") Sharon Akiyama: Well hey, it's good to see you all back, and right now, Super Tengu is making his entrance, the Violent "Demon Boy" Ishrinku already circling the ring, tossing chairs into the ring, Super Tengu sets one up, RUNS, JUMPS off the chair over the-Clears the top rope, SUICIDE DIVE- [Super Tengu Sails into A CHAIR!!!] [FANS CHEER!!!!!!! Super Tengu collapses, rolling around clutching his mouth, DEMON BOY drives the chair into his neck, and promptly runs to Sharon's table, Sharon gets up, leaves quickly,Demon Boy lays Super Tengu out, with a chair] [Sounds of headset being dropped] [FANS CHEER!!] [Camera goes to Demon boy, with a chair, climbing up to the buckles across the ring area, and MOONSAULTING with the chair- Super Tengu MOVES!! And Demon Boy CRASHES through the table, and is out facefirst...Super Tengu rolls up, staggers into the ring, and He collapses on the mat...and tells Lou Davidson to count Demon Boy out, [FANS CHEER!!] [Lou starts counting and waving...] (Demon boy gets us, Bleeding somehow...and he staggers to ringside, SUPER TENGU baseball kicks him back to the railing! Super Tengu to the buckles, Demon Boy turns- SUPER TENGU flies off and SPINS crashing his shoulder into Demon Boy, HITS the Skytwister Press on Demon boy, Demon Boy is out...Tengu has a CD from a fan, and CRACKS it Into Ishrinku's bloody head, spraying blood on his HCW Shirt, and Tengu kicks, and ENZUGIRI's Demon Boy to the ground... Tengu drags Ishrinku up, rolls him in, to the apron, and grabbing the ropes, hits a somersault legdrop, and covers, Ref Lou counts....) (1) (2) (Feet on the ropes, Tengu is up, slapping his hands for a quicker count, and Demon Boy Rolls him up from behind, Ref Louis dives over, for the count) (1) (2) (Kickout, Tengu is mad, Demon boy is up, Tengu SUPERKICKS him to the mat, covers,) (1) (2) (BLOODY kickout by Demon Boy, Demon Boy stumbles, DROPS, and FLOORS Tengu with a crotchshot, and sets him on the buckles, for a Superplex, TENGU headbutts him, and DEMON BOY falls, runs up, and HEADBUTTS Tengu and HITS A SECOND ROPE Suplex to the MAT! TENGU rolls over on DEMON BOY!!) (1) (2) (3!!) FANS ROAR!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! FANS CHANT: TENGU!! TENGU!! TENGU! FANS CHANT: HCW!! HCW!!-SJPW!! SJPW!! (A weary Super Tengu has his Strap, and strapping the belt on, he holds Demon Boy's arm up, Demon boy's face is a Crimson Red, they stand and pant as the HCW fans stand and Cheer!) ------------------------------------------------------------------- AO: Super Tengu once again showing why he is a great champion! Even in Ishrinku's garbage wrestling style, Tengu prevailed! ER: Speaking of Tengu, we have comments from him as he was at Kennedy Airport in New York City, before he hopped a flight back to Japan and SJPW.... ------------------------------------------------------------------- [Taped 8/7/97] [open camera on Super Tengu in John F. Kennedy International Airport. as usual the media is swamping the Japanese superstar] Super Tengu: [smiling as he walks, having the press follow him] Please ladies and gentlemen I'll answer all your questions... Reporter#1: Tengu many are saying that your less than impressive tour in HCW is going to affect your J Crown title defense against Ace Killcanon. any thought? Super Tengu: [chuckling] So I lost in some non-title and tag matches in America. It is nothing to be upset about, It does prove that SJPW does have the best talent in the world and that Super Tengu is among that talent!! Reporter#2: What about recent claims by Shinji Hamada and Killcanon saying that you are over rated because fo your loss to Kyu Kabuki? Super Tengu: Kyu Kabuki beat me in the J Crown tournament, Well good for him!! He earned that victory and Tengu gives him much credit in that win!! If Hamada wishes to boast how I was beaten then perhaps someone should remind him that he has been pinned cleanly by the very man he is seeking to mock and not only has this person pinned him but he has KNOCKED HAMADA OUT!! Reporter#2: And this person is? Super Tengu: You are talking to him!! As for Killcanon, we shall see at J Crown who the better man is. We wrestled to a draw once so this time THERE MUST BE A WINNER!! Reporter#3: Still losing to Kabuki must have been a blow Super Tengu: I restate what was said before, Kyu Kabuki is a great wrestler and intense competitor. He was the better man that night and I applaud him!! Reporter#2: What about your [cough] advances to Robin Blaze? Super Tengu: [smiling] My offer to 'wrestle' with Ms. Blaze stands... I have but 2 goals right now, to defend this SJPW Cruiserweight title and to 'win' Robin Blaze!! Reporter#3: Any chance we'll see you and Hamada in the ring again? Super Tengu: If by some miracle Hamada and I wrestle again I WILL defeat him just as I did before. Besides, poor Shinji doesn't even seem to be Shinji these days. Last I checked he was acting very much like some young hoodlum I see polluting the streets of New York or some stupid Yakuza or Triad from a John Woo movie... [the press corps laugh] Super Tengu: Let "Hot Bluster" Hamada threaten and insult me all he wants, the fact remains It was I who beat HIM at Battlecade and not the other way around [looks at a TV camera] Do you understand Shinji? I BEAT YOU!! Nothing, and I mean NOTHING, will change that fact!! The fact that I did it on my own while you seek to get help from your little croney Killcanon just proves my point about you being a fraud and a cheat!! The U.S. fans have come to love what the Japanese fans already love... SUPER TENGU!! [fade out as Super Tengu boards a JAL flight] ------------------------------------------------------------------- AO [chuckling]: This is why I love Tengu so much! He is more than a match for Ace & Shinji together! Ha Ha Ha! ER: Tengu needs to stop taking Ace so lightly They have this non-title match prior to their meeting at the ppv, so Tengu needs to stay focused. Challenging someone as talented as Jet Jaguar seems foolish... AO: To you! Tengu KNOWS exactly what he is doing! First, he will beat Ace in a non-title match, thus proving he doesn't deserve a shot anymore than Hamada does, then he will defeat Jet Jaguar, the so-called 'best' Middleweight in SJPW! ER: We'll see. It could explode in his face. Right now, we have an interview Sharon Akiyama did at a recent house show with "Demon Boy" Ishrinku, who will soon be facing Steel Serpent for the Battlecade title..... ------------------------------------------------------------------- [Taped 6/8/97] (Camera opens on Sharon Akiyama standing on a platform/stage. Behind her is the SJPW banner.) Sharon: At this time it is my...uhm...pleasure to welcome "Demon Boy" Ishrinku. (Crowd boos as Ishrinku walks up onto the stage beside Sharon. She moves a little further away from him with a look for fear and discomfort on her face. Ishrinku is dressed in black military style pants, black army boots, a SJPW T-shirt, black tape on his forearms and he has red face paint on. He looks wild-eyed as usual and he yells out to the fans and flips them off.) Sharon: Mr...Ishrinku. You and I have just returned from a tour of the American-based HCW. How do you think you fared on this tour? (Ishrinku ignores Sharon and continues to scream out at the crowd.) Sharon: Excuse me..."Demon Boy"? (Ishrinku suddenly whirls around and faces Sharon, causing her to gasp and back away.) Ishrinku: HCW! HCW!! HA-HA-HA! Sharon: Yes, HCW. How did you enjoy the tour. Ishrinku: HCW! HCW! I went to America yes? Sharon: Yes. Ishrinku: And I bled in America yes? Sharon: Umm...yes. Ishrinku: I caused much pain to others yes? Sharon: Yes. Ishrinku: Then I had good time! HA-HA-HA! Sharon: How do you feel you were treated by the American fans and wrestlers. Ishrinku: They hate me! I love it!! Ha-ha-ha! (He flips off the fans again.) Sharon: Did you have any difficulty teaming with the other SJPW stars. (Ishrinku suddenly gets a very serious look on his face. His sudden change of mood is startling.) Ishrinku(In a low somber tone): I am an outsider here in SJPW. A freak. People say I am a "garbage wrestler". People like you and Akira Ono. (He points to Sharon and she backs up further. She is almost on tip-toes as she holds the microphone out toward Ishrinku.) Ishrinku: I don't belong here. (He tears off his SJPW T-shirt to reveal a HCW T-shirt.) Ishrinku: I thought maybe here, but no. Too far from home. (He tears off the HCW T-shirt to reveal a PMW T-shirt.) Ishrinku(Pointing to the shirt): This was my home. It is gone now. Maybe I should just go away too. (Ishrinku lowers his head and the crowd grows quiet. He stands with his head lowered for nearly a minute. Sharon holds the microphone and now appears to have a sorrowful look on her face. She looks to be feeling some pity for Ishrinku. Ishrinku suddenly raises his head to the camera, his eyes wide and glaring with madness.) Ishrinku: NOOOOOOOOOO!! I won't go! Not for you! (He points at Sharon and she lets out a little yelp and then drops the microphone and leaves the stage. Ishrinku picks up the microphone.) Ishrinku: Not for Akira Ono! Not for fans! Not for anyone!!! I will stay and do what I do! Bleed! And make others bleed! I will not be happy until all of you are sick from the sight of blood! Steel Serpant! You are the Battlecade champion! People think you are tough! You are not tough! (He rams the microphone into his forehead several times.) Ishrinku: I can beat you! I can make you bleed! Ha-ha-ha! I will be a champion! Then what will they call me? Garbage Champion! Ha-ha-ha! (He throws down the microphone and takes a martial arts stance. He grabs his throat and then sprays red mist into the are. He sticks his red tongue out and shouts out to the fans again.) ------------------------------------------------------------------- AO: That man is seriously disturbed. Thank Buddha that he was the only wrestler from PMW to join SJPW! He is deranged! ER: He is right about one thing: He is an outsider in SJPW. He has no friends. And he has many enemies. Not only has he made himself a target by Steel Serpent, he also ticked of Sung "The Catman" Kim, who is still recovering from his attack, and Zero Gravity, one of the top contendors in the Super J Crown tournament. AO: And Zero Gravity must face his long time friend and ally, Patty Babes Carter in the 2nd round of the tournament. Speaking of Carter, Sharon Akiyama also spoke to him recently... ------------------------------------------------------------------- [Taped 8/8/97] Sharon Akiyama: SJPW fans, please welcome my guest, PATTY BABES CARTER!!!! [crowd cheers as PBC comes out, and heads to the interview area] Mr. Carter, it seems to me that you haven't been focused on the SJPW recently, why is that? Patty Babes Carter: Well, I have already spoken with the President on this matter, but in the past week, the NAWF has taken over, hopefully that will calm down after next Sunday. You see, On Tuesday, I became the NAWF World Champion. To be honest, as your current Champion will acknowledge, it isn't easy when your a marked man. SA: Ok, then, when you do get back into the SJPW again with new focus, what do you hope to do? PBC: Pick up where I left off, trying to make my way into a great fed. I have been in so many feds, it's unbelievable, I have coped before with more than one fed, I'll do it again, but until next Sunday, you will hear very little from me. SA: Of what we have heard, you have been continually berating Nik Nyce and inviting El perfecto into this Rainbow Squad, any reason why? PBC: Well, El Perfecto is a great pal, and with Jonnie Adonis, has seemingly had a bad time with friends. I am offering him a loyal group of wrestlers to back him up, me and Zero Gravity here, and many other elsewhere. As for Nik Nyce? Well he just refuses to accept that I accept he beat me, The Enforcer had the same problem in the HCW. SA: Finally, anyone you fancy facing here in the SJPW when your back for real? PBC: Definitely, Super Tengu, an imprssive wrestler. I'd sure like to pit my wits against him. SA[blushes at hearing Tengu's name]: Ahem, well thank you for your time. ------------------------------------------------------------------- ER: That about wraps up this addition of the Control Center. Before we go, we leave you with a training montage of Misae Suzuki, who is undergoing a rigourous regimen under the strict eye of the veteran Sade Edo. We'll see you next time, goodnight! ------------------------------------------------------------------- [Taped 8/9/97] (Camera opens on a training dojo. Misae Suzuki is standing in the ring with another women who is dressed in a green bodysuit. The woman with Suzuki is much taller and heavier than Suzuki. Sake Edo is standing at ringside. She claps her hands and the women in green and Suzuki bow to her and then to each other. Suzuki gets in a fighting stance and the other women begins to fire kick after kick to Suzki's legs, stomach and chest. Suzuki blocks some of the shots, but most get through and the impact is brutal. The woman in green suddenly hits a spinning backfist that puts Suzuki on the mat. She rises back up and resumes the fighting stance. The woman fires in again and this time catches Suzuki behind the head with a stiff kick and Suzuki goes down again. She stays down for a moment and when she raises her head, her eyes are filled with tears of pain. Sake Edo lowers her head and shakes it sadly. Suzuki sees this and screams out. She is red in the face as she rises to her feet and takes the fighting stance. She screams at the other woman to continue and her opponent looks over to Sake Edo. Edo nods and the woman continues a brutal barrage of kicks. Suzuki takes them all and while she sometimes crumples to the mat in pain, she always comes up red faced and with eyes filled with tears screaming for more. Sake finally barks out a command an d Suzuki and her opponent stop. Sake barks another command and both Suzuki and the woman in green bow to her and then to each other. Suzuki's opponent takes Suzuki down to the mat and ties her up into a STF. Suzuki screams in pain and Sake asks her to quit. Suzuki screams no and she tries to make the ropes. Her opponent pulls back on the chinlock causing Suzuki to scream some more, but she finally makes the ropes. This sequence continues for several more minutes as several scenes are edited together. Suzuki bent painfully into a submission move, only to fight her way out. Then the scene cuts to a series of clips of Suzuki being hit with a variety of brutal suplexes by the woman in green. On one release German suplex, it appears Suzuki is knocked out, but after few moments she groggily rises to her feet. The camera cuts to a series of grueling exercises being performed by Suzuki. Then it cuts to Suzuki doing many minial chores around the dojo. Cleaning, doing laundry, perparing meals for the other trainees, etc. Finally it cuts to a shot of Suzuki's face. Gone is the smile and shin in her eyes. They are replaced by a grim determination and a gleam of toughness.)
