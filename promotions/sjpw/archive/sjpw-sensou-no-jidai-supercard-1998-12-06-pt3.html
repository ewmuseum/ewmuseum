Sensou no Jidai Supercard, Part Three

6 December 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Sensou no Jidai continues...] TK: This next match is the first of two title bouts tonight. Mitsunori Takizawa, a close friend of ours, will battle Toshiaki Takada for the Koubousen Geijutsu title. AO: Knowing Mitsunori, and knowing Takada, this will be a brawl! TK: We can expect no less from this division, ne? AO: Very true. I hate this division myself, but I fully understand why we have it... ____ _____ _ _____________________________________________ / __/_ / / _ \ | /| / / Koubousen Geijutsu Shimeijiai: / _\ \/ // / ___/ |/ |/ /Mitsunori Takizawa vs Toshiaki Takada /___/\___/_/ |__/|__/__________________________________________/ [The camera pans to the ring, where Nishi Toju & Goto Kyoji stand. The crowd goes silent as Nishi prepares to speak...] Nishi: This next contest is for the Koubousen Geijutsu championship! [crowd cheers] It is set for one fall, with a one hour time limit! Introducing first, the challenger! Representing Chiimu Tora, from Tokyo, and weighing in at 300 pounds.... MIT-SU-NOR-I TA-KI-ZA-WA!!! ["Too Old To Rock-n-Roll, Too Young To Die" by Jethro Tull blares over the PA. The house lights dim, and a spotlight highlights the runway entrance. Mitsunori Takizawa steps out from the back, and the crowd gives him a standing ovation. Takizawa is wearing jeans & a t-shirt, and black biker boots. He has on elbow & kneepads. He is carrying a gym bag that looks full. He stops for a moment, and looks out at the crowd, then heads to the ring. He enters the ring, and goes to his corner, and sets the bag down...] TK: I wonder what is in the bag? AO: We'll find out soon enough! ["The Hunted" by Kodo blares over the PA] Nishi: His opponent is the current Koubousen Geijutsu Champion! He hails from Sapporo, and weighs in at 235 pounds...he is TOSH-I-A-KI "THE PYRO" TA-KA-DA!!! [The lights go dim again, and Takada steps out to a mixed pop from the crowd. The Koubousen Geijutsu title is around his waist. He is wearing black sweatpants and boots. He has bullrope with a cowbell in his left hand. He strides to the ring, and enters. He goes towards Nishi, and motions for him to hand him the microphone...] TK: Takada has something he wants to say... AO: Wonderful. I wonder if he can still pronounce complete sentances... Toshiaki Takada: "Recently Takizawa, you said some pretty dis-heartening things. You mentioned that you no longer respect me. You placed emhasis on your silly belief that I am a man not worthy of respect!!..." [Toshiaki begins to scratch his forehead, giggles shaking his head back and forth. Suddenly, with a look of pure determination crossing his battle-scarred features, Takada returns to his speech.] Toshiaki Takada: "You know, I was never out for respect.....Those who have watched my antics over the past year can attest to that. The bottom line, however, is that my behavior at times has not always been deserving of it either. Apparently, comic relief is simply not such a great way to present the situation at hand.....Apparently, I should simply keep my mouth shut until it is time to present my argument in the ring!!" [Takada, his face growing more flushed by the second, begins to scream into the microphone. Tiny beads of spit fly across the ring as he presents his points.] Toshiaki Takada: "...You say that I am nothing more than a pale imitation of all of the garbage wrestlers that have preceded me?....Well, of course I am!! That is all anyone can be...Once history has developed it's base, what's left to invent? What have I yet to show these good people that they have not already seen a thousand times?....I'll tell you, that is a subject that I often ponder. I think to myself- What is it that I can show these people that they have not already seen?...I will now present to you my answer..." [Toshiaki turns his back on Takizawa, ascending the nearest turnbuckle.] Toshiaki Takada: "...PAIN!!!..Whether it be blood spilled, organs ruptured, or bodies charred, I always deliver violence that will make you cringe.....Whether it be my body or yours, blood WILL be spilled!!!...." [The crowd erupts into applause as Toshiaki drops the microphone] TK: Takizawa charges Takada from behind, and nails him with a forearm shot! [crowd "oooohs"] He grabs him by the waist...German Suplex off the second rope! [crowd "ooohs"] AO: Takizawa pulls the title belt off his opponent's waist, and smashes it over Takada's head! [crowd "oooohs"] He bashes him again, and Takada is bleeding! [Takizawa hits him with the belt again, then holds it over his head. The crowd gives him a mixed pop, then he throws the belt in the corner, and turns towards the fallen champion, and starts to put the boots to him. Takada attempts to block the blows, but is still stunned from the sneak attack...] TK: Takizawa is waisting no time exploiting his advantage. He drags Takada to his feet, and sends him back down with a short-arm clothesline. He measures him, then drops an elbow on his chest! AO: Mitsunori rolls to his feet, then kneedrops Takada across the forehead! Takada sits up, holding his head in pain, and Takizawa applies a rear chinlock with his left arm, while pummeling his forehead with his right fist! [crowd "ooohs"] [Takizawa flails away on Takada, his hand covered in his blood. He drags Takada to his feet, then headbutts him. Takada drops back into the corner, and sags. Takizawa looks out at the crowd, then charges in, but Takada catches him with a back elbow coming in. Takizawa stops short, and Takada explodes on him with rapid palm strikes to his face and chest. Takizawa staggers back from this assault, and Takada leaps, landing a stiff lariat that sends Takizawa to the mat...] TK: Takada now proving why he is the champion! He gets to his feet, then lands a vicious stomp to Takizawa's groin! [crowd "oooohs"] AO: Takada's face is a mask of blood, but he loves that! It motivates him! He drags Takizawa to his feet, then lays into him with three european uppercuts! Takizawa staggers back...Jumping spinwheel kick sends Takizawa back to the mat! [crowd "oooohs"] [Takada reaches into his sweatpants, and pulls out his trademark fork. He shows it to the crowd, who "ooooh" loudly. Takada straddles Takizawa, and grabs him by the back of the head. He lifts his head up, then jabs the fork onto his forehead! The crowd "oooohs" as Takada rips Takizawa open with the fork. Takizawa squirms on the mat, screaming in pain. Takada releases his foe, and throws the fork into the crowd. He grabs his title belt, and brings it to the middle of the ring. He drops it, then grabs Takizawa, drags him to his feet, then DDT's him onto the belt! The crowd "ooohs" loudly, and Takada makes a cover, and Goto counts to two before Takizawa kicks out...] TK: Takada is a vicious individual! That fork did alot of damage! Takada drags Takizawa to his feet, and sets him up for a suplex. Takizawa blocks it, then lifts Takada up in the air, spins him around, and drops forward into a gourdbuster! [crowd "ooohs"] AO: That's the sneakiness of my old partner! He's always pulling counters like that out of the hat! Takizawa stumbles over to his gym bag, and unzips it. He looks around inside, then smiles. He pulls out...a metal glove wrapped in barbed wire! [crowd "oooohs"] [Takizawa turns towards Takada, and smiles. He waits for the champion to get to his feet, then charges, nailing him with the glove on the forehead. Takada falls to the mat, and Takizawa fistdrops him with the glove! Takada rolls around in pain, and Takizawa grabs him by the back of the hair, and drags him to his feet. He throws him into the corner, then proceeds to pummel him with the glove, causing cuts on his face & chest, as well as causing his forehead to bleed more profusely. Takizawa smiles at his opponent, then starts to kick away at Takada's gut...] TK: Takizawa is showing the champion he can be just as vicious. This isn't the Takizawa I once knew! He swings that glove at Takada again, but Takada blocks it! His arm is cut open from blocking the blow, but he just smiles, and kicks Takizawa low in the gut. Takizawa doubles over, and Takada hops up on the second rope. He grabs Takizawa around the neck...Tornado DDT! [crowd "ooohs"] AO: He spiked Takizawa hard! Takada now taking the glove off Takizawa, then puts it on himself! Takada now flailing away at Takizawa's forehead with the glove! [crowd "oooohs"] [Takada releases Takizawa, then takes the glove off, and throws it out of the ring. He walks over to where his bullrope is, and picks it up. He shakes the cowbell, and the crowd applauds approvingly. He walks over to Takizawa, who is rising to his feet. He slams the cowbell on Takizawa's head, and Mitsunori falls back to the ropes. Takada whips Takizawa to the ropes, and as he comes off, Takada clotheslines him with the bullrope! The crowd "oooohs" as Takizawa falls to the mat. Takada sits Takizawa up, and wraps the bullrope around his neck, and starts to choke him! The crowd "oooohs" loudly as Takizawa's face turns red, then purple. His cheeks expand as he tries to find air to breathe. Takada puts his right foot on Takizawa's back, using it for leverage as he pulls back on the bullrope. Takizawa's arms fall to the mat, and his head lowers. Takada, sensing victory, loosens the bullrope, and removes his foot. He pulls the bullrope off of Takizawa, and let's him fall to the mat. He executes a legdrop, then rolls on top to cover him. Goto drops to count, and gets to two and three quarters before Takizawa kicks out! The crowd applauds loudly as Takizawa sits up, his face a mask of rage. Takada looks at him in shock. Takizawa gets to his feet, and Takada attempts a lariat, but Takizawa stops him short with a Uraken! Takada staggers back, and Takizawa grabs him around the throat, lifts him up, then drives him down with a chokeslam! The crowd "oooohs" loudly, while Takizawa calls for Goto to count. He drops down, but Takada kicks out at two...] TK: I thought Takizawa was done for, but somehow he found a resevoir of strength! AO: He's always had it Kikuchi! That's why he's the workhorse of SJPW! TK: Takizawa drags Takada up, sets his head between his legs.... PILEDRIVER!!! [crowd "oooohs"] Takizawa with the cover... ...1... ...2... KICKOUT!!! [crowd applauds] AO: Takada, for all his bad points, which are too many to count, is a hard man to beat! Takizawa drags Takada to his feet, and attempts an irish whip, but Takada reverses...he catches Takizawa coming off...EXPLODER SUPLEX!!! [crowd "oooohs"] TK: Takizawa gets to his feet, and Takada grabs him...ANOTHER EXPLODER SUPLEX! [crowd "oooohs" again] He dumped Takizawa on his head this time! [Takada screams in the air, and some fans scream back, reminded of "Demon Boy" Ishrinku. He pulls something out of his tights and moves to the corner. He kneels down, and waits. Takizawa slowly gets to his feet, shaking his head. He turns and sees Takada, and staggers over to him. Takada rises, turns, and throws a fireball in Takizawa's face! The crowd "oooohs" loudly as Takizawa falls to the mat, rolling around and screaming in pain. Takada walks over to Takizawa's gym bag, and reaches inside. He pulls out a frying pan, and shows it to the crowd. He grabs Takizawa by the back of his head, and drags him to his feet. He slams the pan over Takizawa's head, and he collapses in a heap...] TK: This doesn't look good for Takizawa. What's Takada doing? He's going outside the ring? AO: He's looking under the ring...he's got a table! [crowd "ooohs"] Takada slides it into the ring, along with a bottle of... lighter fluid!? TK: He's pouring the fluid over the table. He pulls a lighter from his sweatpants, and lights the table on fire! [crowd "oooohs"] AO: This doesn't look good for Takizawa... [Takada looks askance of the crowd, and they applaud their approval. Takada drags Takizawa to his feet, and rams his head into the turnbuckle. Takizawa fires back with a back elbow, then grabs Takda by the back of the head, and rams him headfirst into the buckle. He turns Takada around, then sits him up on the top rope. He lands a punch to his jaw, then climbs up on the corner. He stands Takada up, and ascends to the top himself. He starts to set Takda up for a superplex, but Takada headbutts Takizawa, then sticks his head between his legs. Takizawa attempts to backdrop Takada, but he hammers Takizawa's back with forearm shots. Both men almost fall, but somehow, Takada keeps them both from falling. He locks his arms around Takizawa's waist, then lifts him up in the air, and flies off the top rope, powerbombing him THROUGH the flaming table! The crowd gets to it's feet, looking to see how both men fared...] TK: What a move! Takizawa could be seriously hurt! AO: Takada doesn't look too good himself! He sits up, shaking his head, and sees Takizawa! He crawls over to him, and lays over him for a cover... ...1... ...2... ...3...!!! **ding** **ding** **ding** [Crowd applauds as "The Hunted" cranks on the PA] Nishi: The winner of this match, and STILL Koubousen Geijutsu Champion....TOSHIAKI "THE PYRO" TAKADA!!!! [crowd applauds again] TK: What a match! Both men showed how violent they could be! AO: It was a wild brawl. I hope Mitsunori is all right. He took a nasty one through that table! [The camera pans to the ring, where Goto is holding Takada up while handing him his title. He guides Takada through the ropes, and some ring attendants help him out of the ring, and start to take him to the back. Another attendant enters the ring with a fire extinguisher, and puts out the fire. The medical team enters the ring, and helps Takizawa to his feet. He shoves them off, and under his own power, steps out onto the apron. The crowd gives him a standing ovation, and he bows, then jumps off the apron, and heads up the runway to the back...] TK: At least Takizawa is all right. He could have been seriously injured by that powerbomb! AO: He's a tough old bastard. One day though, his luck will run out! TK: The attendants are clearing the remains of the table from the ring. We have two matches left now. Our next one will be a war, as Chiimu Tora members Banshee, Jet Jaguar II & Shishi "Lion" Fujiwara face Kagemusha, Kabuki Kid & Kensei Tokugawa of The Daimyo! AO: We still don't really know if Kabuki Kid is here though. Someone saw Jet Jaguar II in the back, so it seems that he is able to compete. TK: Very true. Before we go to the ring, we have these comments from Kagemusha... [The camera opens on the elderly man known only as Kagemusha's mentor sits quietly in the Japanese style garden at their home, drinking a cup of tea in a decorated china cup.] Old Man: "Gottagaesu"... confusion, turmoil. This is the state of mind young Kagemusha finds himself in. His soul has been torn asunder by that fateful day 4 years ago.... [He slowly drinks some of his tea, then nods slowly and sadly.] Old Man: And now he has come under the influence of The Daimyo, has been exposed to the spotlight that comes with wrestling in Japan's greatest organization, SJPW, and is faced with fame and money like he has never known before. He has fallen into a dark hole, from which there appears no way out. Why do I stay with him? I am asked this many times... I stay because I know that young man that I taught respect, and honour... "giri" - is still in there somewhere, and I know that someday he will return. Someday... [The Old Man goes back to drinking his tea as the scene fades.]
