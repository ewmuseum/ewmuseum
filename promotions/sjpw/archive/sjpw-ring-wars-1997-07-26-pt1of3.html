There are two high back chairs with a large "V" shaped table in the middle of the set. The table has a large SJPW Logo on the front of it in Red Black and White. There are stacks of papers on top of the table. There is a video wall covering the entire back side of the set. Currently only a SJPW logo is shown on the screen. A caucasian man in his mid 20's walks in from the left. He's has red hair and is wearing blue jeans, a black T-Shirt with "SJPW" written in Red on it, and a black leather jacket. An asian man in his mid 30s with long black hair put into a pony tail and a goatee walks in from the right side. He's wearing a black suit with a white chinese shirt and black Oakley shades. They both sit in their respective chairs.] Eddie Rose: Good evening everyone. I'm Eddie Rose, and this is my partner, the legendary Akira Ono. Welcome to another addition of Super Japan Pro Wrestling Ring Wars, LIVE from the Tokyo Dome, in Tokyo, Japan. Tonight, we continue Round One of both the Middleweight title tournament, and the Super J Crown Tournament. Akira Ono: Before we went on the air, we had two things happen; the first was that Scorp no-showed, so Alex Akira has been given an automatic bye into round two. I was really hoping to see him debut tonight; I have much to discuss with this man. The 6 woman tag has been pushed back, so our main event now is the match between Super Tengu & Kyu Kabuki! ER: That's correct Akira. It should prove to be a volatile situation. I also understand we will have live interviews with both Badd Intentions, who will challenge the Toyota Brothers at the Super J Crown ppv, and Shinji "Blockbuster" Hamada, the undisputed #1 contendor for the Cruiserweight title. AO: That is not true Eddie-san. Super Tengu disputes Hamada's position as #1 contendor. ER: Tengu disputes anyone's claim as #1 contendor! Let's cut to ringside, and get the show underway. [The camera pans to the Tokyo Dome, where thousands of japanese wrestling fans await the start of the show. As the announcement that the show is about to begin, the crowd erupts with loud hand clapping, and some throw streamers into the ring. The Ring Announcer steps into the ring, and waits for the clapping to die down] Ring Announcer: Greetings SJPW fans! [crowd cheers] This first contest is a first round match in the SJPW Middleweight title tournament! [crowd cheers] Introducing first, from Osaka, weighing 220lbs, KO-BI-A-SHI ME-RU!!! [Kobiashi Meru comes out to moderate support from the fans] ER: The fans seem unsure of Meru, even after his debut match. Ring Announcer: His opponent, from Vancouver, Canada, weighing 180lbs, he is one half of the NEW JAPAN CREW, ISH-I-KA-WA!!! [The crowd cheers as "You Could Be Mine" plays, and Ishikawa and the Great Oni head to the ring] AO: I like Ishikawa. He beat Watterson on the last show in the J Crown tournament, and he will beat Meru as easily as Hamada did! ER: How can you be so sure? AO: Because Meru doesn't have the killer instinct, and Ishikawa does. ER: There's the bell, and Ishikawa charges Meru, and starts flailing away with kicks & punches! [crowd oooohs] He has Meru backed up on the ropes, and he is flailing away! The ref moves in to seperate them, and Ishikawa nails Meru with a dropkick right over the ref's head! [crowd oooohs] AO: The referee is out of the way now, and Ishikawa grabs Meru in a headlock, and takes him down with a bulldog [crowd ooohs] He drags Meru up, and takes him down with that Flair-like kneebreaker! [crowd oooohs] Ishikawa now dropping repeated elbows to his leg; Meru is screaming in pain! [crowd oooohs] ER: Ishikawa now holding up the leg, and laying in kicks to the inner thigh. Meru looks unable to mount any type of comeback; what is wrong with him? Ishikawa now turns him over into a single leg boston crab; this man is truly vicious! AO: He's sending a message to all his future opponents. The NJC have been largely overlooked by promoters, and they don't like that. They are showing why they deserve the spotlight! Ishikawa breaks the hold, and stands Meru up.... football knee clip sends Meru back down, holding his leg! [crowd ooohs] ER: He's tearing Meru apart! He now locks on a Texas Cloverleaf, and Meru looks ready to tap out, but Ishikawa breaks the hold before he can! He's doing this on purpose! AO: Ishikawa with another kneebreaker on Meru, then slaps on his figure four leglock! [crowd oooohs] Meru is screaming in pain; he gives up! He couldn't take it! And Ishikawa still has the hold on! The Great Oni goes to the top rope... Kamikaze! [crowd ooooohs] ER: Oni now stomping on Meru's knee! The ref can't get them to stop! [crowd starts booing]. Security is starting to move in... Wait! Here comes Jet Jaguar, Black Ninja, Jiraiya, Hanashi Tanaka & Kendo Masao! [crowd cheers]. Ishikawa breaks the hold, and the New Japan Crew bolt from the ring! You can see them arguing with Jet Jaguar and Tanaka. If Tanaka wins tonight, he will face Ishikawa in round two. AO: I love it! These two now have some real tension between them. I hope Jaguar and Ishikawa make the finals; what a match that will be! The medical team is attending to Meru; it looks like he's going out on a stretcher. At least we all ready have the wrestlers for the next match in the ring! ER: They've taken Meru away, so let's go back to the ring announcer, and get this next match started. Ring Announcer: This next match is a first round match in the SJPW Middleweight Title Tournament! [crowd cheers]. Introducing first, from Higo, Japan, weighing 213lbs, JIR-AI-YA!!! [crowd cheers loudly for Jiraiya] ER: Jiraiya has really made waves of late since he got involved in the situation involving the Cruiserweight title. Ring Announcer: His opponent, from Osaka, and weighing 220lbs, "DRAGON" HA-NASH-I TA-NA-KA!! [crowd cheers for Tanaka] AO: Tanaka was lucky that Bob Thurmond attacked him in front of Mr. Yamashita, or he would not have gotten this second chance to go for the Middleweight title! ER: Very true. There's the bell, and Jiraiya offers his hand to Tanaka.... They shake, and we are underway! Both men lock up, and Jiraiya with a hiptoss on Tanaka. Tanaka is up, and Jiraiya now with an armdrag! Tanaka up once more... He blocks the hiptoss, and counters with a backslide! Cover...one...two..Kickout! [crowd applauds] AO: Both men up, and Tanaka goes for a sunset flip! Cover... one...tw-kickout! [crowd applauds] Tanaka is going for the pin early, keeping Jiraiya off guard! Both men up, and Tanaka takes Jiraiya down with a rana, then hits a dropkick as he gets to his feet! [crowd applauds] ER: Tanaka's really taking charge here! He whips Jiraiya to the ropes... Tanaka misses with the clothesline... Springboard rana by Jiraiya takes Tanaka down! [crowd ooohs]. Tanaka up, and he charages in... spinning backbreaker by Jiraiya! [crowd ooohs, then applauds] AO: Jiraiya hits a standing moonsault, runs to the ropes... springboard legdrop! [crowd ooohs] He sits Tanaka up, and runs to the ropes... jumping neck snap! [crowd ooohs] Cover ...one...two...Kickout! [crowd applauds] ER: Jiraiya whips Tanaka to the ropes... hurrican- Powerbomb by Tanaka! [crowd ooohs]. He's still holding onto his legs, and lifts him up again... another Powerbomb! [crowd ooohs] Tanaka flips over into a bridge pin...one...two...thr-kickout! [crowd applauds] AO: These men seem evenly matched, though it is obvious that Jiraiya is more of a high flyer, and Tanaka more of a technician. Tanaka now with a reverse chinlock on Jiraiya; this makes a good rest hold, allowing Tanaka to catch his breath, while forcing Jiraiya to expend his. ER: Tanaka now dragging Jiraiya to his feet... Slingshot suplex! [crowd ooohs] He runs to the ropes... elbowdrop! Tanaka is going to the top rope [crowd starts cheering].... Moonsault! [crowd applauds] Cover...one...two...thr-kickout! AO: Jiraiya is taking alot of punishment, and Tanaka looks shocked that he hasn't put him away yet! He whips Jiraiya to the ropes; he goes for a back drop, but Jiraiya rolls over his back... Tanaka turns around... spinwheel kick! [crowd ooohs] Jiraiya charges Tanaka, who is laying on the ropes... flying bodypress sends both of them to the floor! [crowd oooohs] Ref: One! ER: Jiraiya is first to his feet, and he scoop slams Tanaka on the floor. Ref: Two! AO: Jiraiya goes to the apron, springboards off the second rops, and lands with a senton splash onto Tanaka! [crowd oooohs] Ref: Three! ER: Jiraiya rolls Tanaka into the ring. He drags him to his feet.. Belly to back suplex. He's going to the top rope; he might be going for the Shooting Star! He sets himself on the top... He's going for it! Tanaka gets his knees up! [crowd oooohs] Jiraiya is rolling on the mat in pain! AO: Tanaka grabs Jiraiya, and locks on a front facelock with his left arm, and puts on a hammerlock with his right... DDT! [crowd oooohs] Cover...one...two...THREE!!!! [crowd cheers] Ring Announcer: Your winner, "DRAGON" HANASHI TANAKA!!! [crowd cheers again, and streamers fly into the ring] ER: So Tanaka advances this time, after losing to Black Ninja the last time, thanks to Bob Thurmond. AO: Tanaka gave Ninja a great fight, and now he gets to advance, and battle Ishikawa in round two. That will be an interesting match! ER: It should be; did you see the look Ishikawa gave him and Jet Jaguar? There was hatred in his eyes! Now we come to the second half of the Super J Crown tournament first round matches, and we start with a doozy: Mr. Big vs Goro Honda. Both of these men have something in common: They don't like the Gaijin. Honda has a problem with Jarred Jones, whose leg was injured at the hands of both the House of Hate and the Sampson Brothers, and Mr. Big doesn't like Survival or the Smiths. Survival was suspended for a short time due to his actions at our last show, but I'm sure he will turn up again soon. AO: I'm glad he's suspended; he has been nothing but trouble since he got here! The ring announcer is ready, so let us go to him to start the rest of round one! Ring Announcer: This next contest is a round one match in the SUPER J CROWN TOURNAMENT! [crowd cheers] It is one fall, 15 minute time limit. Introducing first, from Kagoshima, weighing 295lbs, GO-RO HON-DA!!! [crowd cheers as Goro Honda heads down the runway to the ring] ER: Goro Honda faces his toughest challenge since arriving in SJPW! Ring Announcer: His opponent, from Winnipeg, Canada, weighing 400lbs, MR. BIG!! [crowd also cheers as Mr. Big heads to the ring] AO: This man is massive; I don't know if Goro can match up to his strength. ER: There's the bell, and Mr. Big goes right after Goro, hammering away with that huge forearm of his. Goro is staggering under this onslaught; Mr. Big now forcing him to one knee! [crowd oooohs] AO: Mr. Big is just too strong! Goro gets back to his feet, but Mr. Big still has him doubled over from those forearm shots. Mr. Big goes to the ropes... Rocker Dropper legdrop! [crowd oooohs] Goro's face landed hard on the mat! ER: Mr. Big drags Goro to his feet, and Goro nails him in the throat with a quick uppercut. Mr. Big staggers back, cluthing his throat, while Goro leans on the ropes, trying to shake the cobwebs out. Mr. Big is double over on the ropes, and Little Joe is yelling at the ref! AO: Goro now on his feet, and he grabs Mr. Big by the hair, and starts laying into him with a series of headbutts to his forehead. It looks like Big has a cut on his head now. Goro now ramming knees into his face! [crowd oooohs]. He now nails him with a chop [crowd "Whoos"], but Mr. Big stands up! [crowd oooohs] ER: Mr. Big grabs Goro... Inverted Atomic Drop! [crowd ooohs]. Did you see how high he got him? Mr. Big whips Goro to the ropes, and catches him with a big powerslam! Cover...one...two...th-kickout! [crowd applauds] AO: Mr. Big goes to the ropes...Splas-Goro got his knees up! [crowd ooohs] Mr. Big is rolling on the mat in pain. Goro slowly rises to his feet; he goes over to Mr. Big, turns him over, and slaps on an STF! [crowd ooohs] This is how he beat Terry Smith! ER: Mr. Big was too close to the ropes; the ref is making Goro break the hold [crowd applauds] Goro backs away from Mr. Big, and stands there, waiting, as he slowly gets to his feet. Mr. Big shaking his head, and slowly turns around... Goro goes for a Yakuza kick, but Mr. Big caught his leg! [crowd ooohs]. He locks hir right arm under Goro's left arm... modified T-bone suplex! [crowd applauds] AO: That was a great move by Mr. Big! Goro is flopping around on the mat. Mr. Big grabs Goro by the throat, and lifts him up. He sets him up for a chokeslam, and lifts him up high... Goro locks his legs around Mr. Big's neck in desperation! [crowd ooohs]. That's a headscissor submission hold! Mr. Big goes for the chokeslam anyway, but Goro is able to flip him over, lessening the impact! [crowd applauds] ER: These two men are really showing why they are two of the top stars in SJPW! Both men slowly get to their feet, and Mr. Big charges Goro, who ducks the clothesline, and grabs him from behind... GERMAN SUPLEX! [crowd ooohs, then applauds] He got him over! I can't believe it! AO: Goro is a very strong man, and Mr. Big only outweighs him by 105lbs. If that had been a cruiserweight, then I would have been impressed more. Goro now locking on an armbar on the prone Mr. Big. I'm not sure this was the wisest thing to do. ER: I agree. Mr. Big is trying to get to his feet. He's only a 3 inches taller than Goro, who is extremely large for a japanese wrestler. Mr. Big reaches his feet, and he is punching away at Goro, who is doing his best to keep the pressure on the arm [crowd starts cheering] Goro is loosening the armbar under this onslaught... Mr. Big nails Goro with an Asian Spike! [crowd oooohs]. They both have a submission hold on each other! AO: Goro pulls Big back towards the ropes, and the referee makes them both break their holds [crowd applauds]. Mr. Big is showing that he has more talent than we gave him credit for. Mr. Big locks up with Goro, and backs him into the corner... He whips him to the opposite corner... Avalanche by Mr. Big! [crowd ooooohs] Goro was squashed! ER: Mr. Big whips him back to the other corner... he goes for another avalanche, but Goro moves! [crowd ooohs]. Big hit the corner hard. Goro is up on the turnbuckles, and he locks a headlock on Big... Spinning DDT! [crowd oooohs]. If Goro wasn't so big himself, he could never get Mr. Big over. AO: True Eddie-san. This is truly a battle between titans here! Goro sets Mr. Big up...piledriver! Cover...one...two..KICKOUT with authority by Mr. Big! [crowd ooohs, then applauds] Goro went up in the air on that one! Mr. Big is up, and he looks mad! ER: Goro now chopping away at Mr. Big, who responds with punches [crowd oooohs] They don't care about wrestling now; it's an all out brawl! Goro now grabs Big by the hair, and starts laying into his bloody forhead with those headbutts again! [crowd oooohs] AO: Mr. Big now with a big knee into Goro's gut, stopping his momentum. He whips Goro to the ropes... Goro ducks the big boot... Flying Forearm by Goro! [crowd applauds] Mr. Big staggers back! ER: Goro sets Mr. Big up... Heart Punch! Mr. Big goes down! Cover...one...two...thr-kickout! [crowd applauds]. Mr. Big is getting up! [crowd cheers] I can't believe he got up so quick after a heart punch! AO: He kicks Goro in the gut, and sets him up.... Heart Punch by Mr. Big! [crowd oooohs] [*DING* *DING* *DING*] ER: What happened? Did they run out of time? AO: It would seem so. Ring Announcer: The referee informs me that the time limit has expired. He rules this contest... A DRAW! [crowd boooos] ER: That was too bad; I thought Mr. Big could have had him there. AO: It was a might contest for both men. They both showed more skill than they have in the past. Mr. Big has alot more in his arsenal besides his size and power. ER: It also means that someone from group A gets a bye to round three! Let's go to ringside for our next match. Ring Announcer: This match is one fall, 15 minute time limit, and is a first round match in the Super J Crown Tournament [crowd cheers] Introducing first, from Osaka, weighing 228lbs, "ANGEL OF DESTINY" SHIN-ZI UR-I-KA-MA!!! [crowd applauds as Shinzi heads to the ring] AO: Urikama faces a strong test tonight. Ruckus is very determined. Ring Announcer: His opponent, from the Catskills, USA, weighing 225lbs, "BLACK TIGER" J.T. RUC-KUS!! [crowd cheers are JT and Tiger Kikuchi head to the ring; Ruckus is wearing his mask, which he takes off after he enters the ring] ER: There's the bell. They circle each other, looking for an opening. JT moves to lock up, and Shinzi takes him down with a quick armdrag. JT gets up, and moves in again, but Shinzi catches him in a scoop slam! JT looks a little frustrated as he gets up this time. AO: JT moves in again, and this time he catches Urikama with a European uppercut. He backs Urikama to the ropes, and whips him to the other side... Urikama ducks the spinwheel kick, continues to the ropes, comes off, and takes JT over with a springboard rana! [crowd applauds] JT slides under the ropes to the outside, shaking his head in disbelief. ER: Urikama wastes no time though; he comes off the ropes, and charges, but JT moves out of the way. Urikama goes to the top rope with amazine speed, and as JT turns around... Flying bodypress to the floor! [crowd ooohs, then applauds] Ref: One! AO: Urikama is up, and he drops an elbow on JT's chest, then picks him up, and slams him back down to the floor. Ref: Two! ER: Urikama goes up on the apron, and climbs to the top rop... Splash from the top to the floor! [crowd ooooohs] JT looks hurt! Ref: Three! AO: Urikama rolls JT into the ring, and goes for the cover... one...two..kickout! [crowd applauds] Urikama drags JT to his feet, and whips him into the corner. He sets himself in the opposite corner... Handspring Elbow- Ruckus comes out of the corner with a lariat, catching Urikama as he was coming up from the handspring! [crowd oooohs] He did a 360 in the air! ER: JT with a snap suplex now, and a cover... one...two..kickout [crowd applauds] JT drags Urikama up... swinging neckbreaker! [crowd ooohs] He drops the leg across the chest, and covers... one...two...th-kickout! [crowd applauds] AO: JT is being a little more aggressive than we've seen him be before. He drags Urikama up again; he goes for a belly to back suplex. but Urikama flips out of it! Ruckus rolls to his feet.. superkick by Urikama! [crowd ooohs] JT staggers to the ropes... Urikama catches him as he comes off, and sends him up high with a back bodydrop! [crowd applauds] ER: JT sits up, and Urikama runs to the ropes... springboard leg lariat decapitates JT! [crowd ooohs] Standing moonsault by Urikama; cover...one...two...thr-kickout! [crowd applauds] Both men are giving this their all! AO: Look! [camera pans to top of runway] Lance Smith is there watching the match! Urikama comes off the ropes, and hits JT across the chest with an elbowdrop. He drags JT up, and flips him down with a rana, then dropkicks him in the back of the head [crowd applauds] ER: Nik Nyce is at the top of the runway now. He and Smith look at each other, nod, then go back to watching the two men in the ring. Urikama has JT up, and is setting him up for a belly to belly, but JT block it, headbutts Urikama, turns to his side, locking on a headlock... Bulldog by JT! [crowd applauds] AO: JT is shaking his head; those headbutts can sometimes disorient you! He sets Urikama up... Face first Piledriver! [crowd applauds] He rolls Urikama over... one...two...th-kickout! [crowd applauds] These two men have alot of fortitude. Both men's chests turning red from the force of the chops. Urikama is starting to stagger back, as JT seems to have more raw power behind his chops. Enzugiri by Urikama! If JT and Smith lock up again, it will be interesting to see which hold will prevail: The Catskills Clutch, or the Too Sexy Submission. AO: The Clutch is the better hold Eddie-san. That is very obvious. Now we have a highly anticipated match: Akira Hashimoto vs Hiro Kanemoto. Hiro has been on a losing streak of late, losing to Jarred Jones, and in IWF/WOW, to Battlecade champion Steel Serpent. Hashimoto lost to Nik Nyce at Battlecade under special circumstances, and then went to a time limit draw with Tiger Kikuchi. Akira is a top seed to win the GWC world title tournament, so this match will be interesting!
