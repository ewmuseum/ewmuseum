Metro, 1/2

13 October 1998

Jonnie Adonis is wearing a leather jacket, white shirt, black tie, slacks and shoes. He has green eyes. Eddie Rose has red hair and is wearing blue jeans, a black T-Shirt with "SJPW" written in Red on it, and a black leather jacket. Both men smile at the camera..] Jonnie Adonis: Welcome to SJPW Metro everyone! I'm Jonnie Adonis, and this is Eddie Rose. Welcome to the Met Studio in downtown Tokyo! We are now in our official timeslot, and ready for action! Eddie Rose: That's right Jonnie. So, what did you think of what happened on Tsunami sunday night? JA: Awesome. We saw some great matches the other night. The start of the Welterweight tournament saw some fantastic action, the tag team battle between The Outlaws & The Tokyo Express was great, and the main event, well, I can see Tengu & Tenru heading for a showdown after what happened! ER: I agree. These two men have one thing in common: Their egos. I expect them to butt heads down the line, possible even in the heavyweight tournament! I understand is Tengu is supposed to face Mandarin in the Welterweight tournament tonight in our main event, but as we will see later, he's refusing to wrestle! JA: Mandarin & Super Tengu are two world class Welterweights. I can't believe he'd let his problems with Jensei Tenru get in the way of a shot at being Welterweight champion! ER: Don't be too sure Jonnie! Tengu is here tonight, but I understand he's still refusing to wrestle. CEO Kobayashi has sent one of his new assistants to talk some sense into Tengu, but I don't know of anyone who can! JA: Neither can I. Any idea who it is? ER: None. JA: Well, we'll just have to keep everyone posted as this situation develops. First up, we have some comments from Jiraiya, the protege of Masashi "Lightning" Lanther. This past sunday, he faced Jet Jaguar II in the first round of the Welterweight tournament, and was defeated by the young rookie. He then had this to say... ____ _____ _ _____________________________________________ / __/_ / / _ \ | /| / / / _\ \/ // / ___/ |/ |/ / Jiraiya /___/\___/_/ |__/|__/__________________________________________/ [The camera opens backstage at Tsunami, where Jiraiya is standing in the post match interview area...] Jiraiya: Jet Jaguar II. You defeated me. You took all I gave you, and still you prevailed. You may have won this first battle, but this war is far from over. Chiimu Lanther has declared war on Chiimu Tora, and it is a war that we intend on winning! [glares into the camera] Jiraiya: Shinji Hamada, I haven't forgotten what you did to me last week on Metro either. You will also pay for your transgressions. Chiimu Lanther always pays it's debts. Remember that Hamada-sama, and Jaguar-san. Both of you will PAY! [Jiraiya storms off the interview set as the camera fades...] ER: That young man has a severe temper. Between Jet Jaguar & Shinji Hamada, this young man will have his hands full! JA: I agree. As both Hamada & Jaguar are part of the newly reformed Chiimu Tora, or Team Tiger for our english speaking fans, this situation between them and Chiimu Lanther, or Team Lanther, is sure to escalate. ER: My question is this: How does Chiimu Lanther expect to prevail, when they have two members, and Chiimu Tora has five? JA: Good question. I'm sure Lanther is looking for more recruits as we speak! ER: For his sake, I hope so. Otherwise, I'd be worried his ego is becoming much like his nephew, Super Tengu! JA: You never know Eddie. ER: Our first match sees the debut of a tag team that came into the promotion under a storm of controversy. Tony Starks is a world reknown athlete, but his partner, Oleg Vladikoff, is another story. He once killed a man in the ring, and that makes him a risk. He was almost turned down for SJPW! JA: But he made it in, and tonight, he and Starks take on two very tough individuals in Frankie "The Thumper" Douglas & Doug Thomas, a former UFC competitor. ER: Both men are capable veterans, but can they stand up to the fury of Starks & Vladikoff? Let's go to the ring and find out... __ ___ _____________________________________________________ / |/ /__ / /________ / / /|_/ / -_) __/ __/ _ \ Starks/Vladikoff vs Douglas/Thomas /_/ /_/\__/\__/_/ \___/________________________________________/ Douglas & Thomas attacked Starks & Vladikoff as soon as the bell rang. Douglas overpowered Starks, while Thomas tried to choke out Vladikoff, then threw him outside the ring. Both men double-teamed Starks in the corner, pummelling him with elbow & kneesmashes. Vladikoff recovered, and when he hit the ring, he nailed Thomas with a german release suplex. Douglas turned to clothesline Vladikoff, but he ducked under, and nailed Douglas with a release belly to belly suplex. As both men rose to their feet, Starks charged out of the corner, double clotheslining them both to the mat. Starks & Vladikoff then spike piledrove both men into the mat. Starks put Douglas in the Kata-ha-jime, while Vladikoff put Thomas in the Vladikoff submission [a modified Dragon Sleeper]. Both men tried to fight it, but soon went under, the referee ruling both of them KO'd. Winners: Starks & Vladikoff by Knockout/Submission. [The camera cuts back to the broadcast table as Starks & Vladikoff head back to the dressing room...] JA: I like this team! They are fast, brutal, and talented! Douglas & Thomas never stood a chance! ER: I agree about Douglas & Thomas not standing a chance. They had them going early on, but severely underestimated Oleg Vladikoff, and it cost them dearly. JA: Speaking of tag teams, we now take you to comments from The Outlaws, who scored a big win over SJPW veterans The Tokyo Express... ____ _____ _ _____________________________________________ / __/_ / / _ \ | /| / / / _\ \/ // / ___/ |/ |/ / The Outlaws /___/\___/_/ |__/|__/__________________________________________/ (SCENE: A backstage room in the Tokyo Dome, set aside for interviews. The grey brick wall is covered on one side by a black SJPW drapery backdrop. The floor is covered with a dark rug, with the SJPW emblazoned on it in scarlet. And other than that, two chairs, and the camera, the room is nondescript and empty. Sitting in the chairs are Brent Maverick and Doc Holliday, The Outlaws. The two men are still in their wrestling gear... this is RIGHT after the tough win over the Tokyo Express, in fact, we hear oohs and ahs in the background as the next match goes on. Both men are sweaty, bruised, exhausted... and happy.) Brent Maverick: Wall, one down, many ta go. If this's th' compy-tishin whut SJPW gots ta offer, ah lak it. They wrassle purty tough fer yahoos whut eats nothin' but rice an' squids. Doc Holliday: Yup, it was fun. Jus' havin' two teams out thar, no waves o' pumped-up tinhorns comin' runnin-in, it's a nice change. But, ya know, they's still some issues ta deal with. Seems lak ya got some varmints whut thinks us Gai-jins ain't upta par... Brent: Gai-whuts? Ah thought they was talkin' 'bout Americans! Doc: Uh... them too, Brent. Anyway, it's lak this. We don' keer whut anyone says 'bout America... hell, the damn Yankee lawyers ruined th' whole country! Times used ta be whar a man could stand by his principles... whutevah his principles was. Them times is long gome, but fer a damn few places... an' right heah in SJPW is one o' them places. Brent: So, ya git ta sayin' American wrasslers is second-rate... mebbe ninety percent of 'em is, but Th' Outlaws is top-shelf in this heah sport, an' don' you fergit it! Doc: So lissen up! Any one o' you tag teams gits ta thinkin' thet yer all high an' mighty... sign on th' dotted line! On account o' this... We's down-low an' dirty, We take guff from none, We don't do it purty, We JUST GIT IT DONE! Ain't that the truth? Brent: THAT'S THE _DAMN_ TRUTH! (The Outlaws get up, and head for the showers... fade to black.) ER: The Outlaws definitely proved themselves sunday night, defeating The Tokyo Express in a match that saw the Express once more being embraced by the fans, something which hasn't happened in a long time. JA: That's right Eddie. The Express were surprised that the fans were actually on their side, since they had stopped seeking their support months ago. This goes back to the match with Kenji Ishida & Shinji Usami a few months ago, before the WWGP shut us down temporarily. ER: And interestingly enough, Ishida & Usami have their sites set on The Bonecrushers, who are wrestling later tonight. JA: Our next match pits Eizo Usami against a newcomer to SJPW, Rupan Loco. The "Crazy Wolf" comes in with a reputation as an excellant technical wrestler, which is interesting for a luchador to have. ER: I agree. Then again, he's alot bigger than most luchadors, so he must be one of those exceptions to the rule. __ ___ _____________________________________________________ / |/ /__ / /________ / / /|_/ / -_) __/ __/ _ \ Rupan Loco vs Eizo Usami /_/ /_/\__/\__/_/ \___/_______________________________________/ Eizo surprised Rupan early with a series of armdrags, finally settling into an armbar. Rupan made it to the ropes, forcing a break. As Eizo backed off, Rupan caught him with a european uppercut, then whipped him to the ropes. He took him down with a fireman's carry, and slapped on an armbar of his own. He turned the hold into a hammerlock, then slammed Usami on his arm. He hit two more hammerlock slams, then applied a short-armscissors. Usami was able to grab the ropes, and Rupan broke cleanly. Rupan then attempted a suplex, but Usami reversed it, then hit three dropkicks as Rupan tried to recover from the reversal. Usami tried a fourth dropkick, but Rupan backed off, and Usami hit the mat. Rupan quickly turned Usami over into a boston crab. He punished Usami's back for a few minuted, then broke the hold, and proceeded to stomp on his injured arm. He dragged the weakened Usami to his feet, hit an arm DDT, then applied a Cross Arm Breaker, which caused Usami to submit. Winner: Rupan Loco by submission. [The camera cuts back to the broadcast table as Rupan Loco heads back to the dressing room...] JA: Impressive debut from the man from Mexico. He's definitely a master of submission holds. ER: That much is evident. He never took to the air once. That's rare for a luchador. JA: We now have comments from former Koubousen Geijutsu champion Steel Serpent, who makes his return to SJPW this coming weekend, when he faces Ronin member "Samurai" Kensei Tokugawa in a Koubousen Geijutsu match. ER: Serpent is one of the toughest, most prolific competitors in SJPW. He's tended to focus on the Koubousen Geijutsu division, as it appeals to his warrior's mentality. JA: I think Serpent could be heavyweight champion if he set his mind to it. He has all the tools to be the man if he wanted to. ER: Well, you have to admire the fact that he prefers Koubousen Geijutsu over other divisions, and gives it his all every time he's in that ring. JA: Believe me Eddie, I know what Serpent is capable of. Remember that I recruited most of the original SJPW Koubousen Geijutsu division! I'm just saying if Serpent went for it, he could take the heavyweight title. He's as good as alot of other competitors in the promotion. ____ _____ _ _____________________________________________ / __/_ / / _ \ | /| / / / _\ \/ // / ___/ |/ |/ / Steel Serpent /___/\___/_/ |__/|__/__________________________________________/ [Scene opens down a darkened passageway. The camera travels the length of the corridor, the dim light revealing the dirty, crumbling catacomb walls. Traveling the full length, the corridor opens into a large room; crumbled debris, carrion, and large, thick, concrete support pillars seem to be the only occupants. A glow of light seems to come from the western part of the large room. Turning, moving slowly between the debris and pillars, the camera advances towards the source of the light. Getting closer, the sound of a repeated thumping seems to get louder. Moving around the final pillar reveals a small alcove, with rows upon rows of lit candles. The source of the noise stands in the center. Standing in the center of the alcove, is a man. His body bears many scars, as does his well muscled arms and legs. Barefoot, his ankles are taped solidly. On his chest, mild burns are visible. Over his heart, seven drops of blood have been tattooed in a small circle. On his head, the man wears a metallic gray mask. The eye holes are covered with a black mesh, making his eyes invisible. He's also wearing black trunks, with an emblem of a silver cobra, coiled to strike, on the back. This man is making the thumping noise. How? He's standing in the center of the alcove, repeatedly kicking one of the concrete support pillars with his shin. With every strike of his shin, dust falls from the top of the pillar, and pieces of cement chip off. He seems oblivious to the blood trickling down his shin. He is Thai. He is Steel Serpent. And he's back is SJPW.] (Serpent) Before SJPW closed it's doors, the Thai Warrior was regarded as the Greatest Battle Arts competitor, and its greatest Champion. That is more than an idle statement; a Warrior has no need to boast of his conquests. The honor of Greatest was well recorded, and well earned. Many times I was carried from the ring, but my arm was held high in victory. I held the Battle Arts Title twice, a feat yet unmatched. I defeated men the likes of "Hardkore" Jonnie Valentine, and Demon Boy Ishrinku. And how did I accomplish this? I am not the strongest man, nor the fastest. But I have never made it a mystery as to how I have overcome so much, to become great. I am more than just a wrestler. I am a Fighter. I am a Warrior. I possess a Warrior's Spirit. That is what drives me. That is how I can look my opponent in the eye, see into his very soul, and know he is beaten before any blood is spilled. [The kicks become faster, harder, and larger concrete pieces snap off, flying across the alcove.] (Serpent) Since the closure of SJPW, I have continued to fight. For Honor. For Survival. After being abandoned by those who brought me here, I was left alone to prosper... or perish. So I fought: in alleys, warehouses, and even catacombs such as this. Food, clothing, and shelter, all provided by the strength given to me by my Fighter's Spirit. I never gave up. [Steel Serpent halts his attack on the pillar, and unconsciously puts a hand to his eye.] (Serpent) Even when the Devil took my eye.... I never gave up. [The attack renews on the support pillar, harder than before. The THUMP THUMP fills the room, and echoes down the corridor.] (Serpent) Soon, I will be facing a man who calls himself Samurai. Samurai, honored Warrior Class of the Noblemen, facing Steel Serpent, in a Koubousen Geijutsu match. It is a match you've already lost, Samurai. [Walking deep into the dimly lit room, Steel Serpent pulls off his mask. The light is too dim to make out any facial features, but he can still be seen. He steps between three cinder blocks; one hangs at chest level, one is suspended at waist level, and the third hangs behind him at head level.] (Serpent) My Spirit is unmatched, Samurai. Every day, I grow stronger. Every morning, as I look at my reflection, I am reminded of the consequences when one does not maintain his focus. You may have many thoughts as you enter the ring: strategies, thoughts of your family, or maybe a woman in the front row catches your fancy. I enter the ring empty, devoid of all feelings and thoughts save one: To completely and utterly destroy you. [Steel Serpent steps forward quickly, a Thai elbow shooting out and splitting the cinder block in half. Then, a knee crumbles the block positioned at waist level. The third cinder block, positioned behind him, lay parallel with his head. But smoothly he spins, ending his deadly ballet by striking the cinder block with a high kick. Shin hits cinder block... and the cinder block explodes with such force, that the largest remaining pieces are no bigger than a marble. Slowly, deliberately, Steel Serpent lowers his leg.] (Serpent) I have given you another thought to consider, Samurai. If that kick could decimate a cinder block, what could it do to a human skull? I know seven men who could be able to tell you... if you could speak to the "Spirit World." The Thai Warrior is Back. Long May He Reign... [camera fades out...] JA: I'm telling you, this guy has that killer instinct! ER: I agree. He's got everything it takes. We now take you to comments from Shishi Fujiwara, who was eliminated from the Welterweight tournament by Greg Miller, with an assist from one Kagemusha.... ____ _____ _ _____________________________________________ / __/_ / / _ \ | /| / / / _\ \/ // / ___/ |/ |/ / Shish "Lion" Fujiwara /___/\___/_/ |__/|__/__________________________________________/ [The scene opens in what appears to be a lockerroom. Shishi Fujiwara sits in a folding chair, still wearing his wrestling gear. His left knee is wrapped in an ace bandage and he holds an ice pack to the back of his head. Cameras can be heard snapping in the background. Several TV lights focus on Fujiwara as he sits in the chair with several microphones in front of his face. Reporters can be heard talking off camera...] Fujiwara: [with his head bowed...] From the high of joining the most honorable faction in all of wrestling to being eliminated from the Welterweight Tournament because of the interfearance of a shadow warrior.. KAGEMUSHA! [Fujiwara raises his head and brushes his hair out of his face.] Fujiwara: I could have accepted a loss to Greg Miller in a fair fight.. but to lose due to the interfearance of back fighter... a coward who involves himself from behind. I can't accept that, but I must live with it. Reporter # 1: Do you have any idea why Kagemusha attacked you? Fujiwara: I know nothing about him. We had never met prior to tonight, so your guess is as good as mine. Kagemusha, you can rest assured that just as no good deed goes un-rewarded, no bad deed goes unpunished.. [Fujiwara takes the ice from his neck and throws it in the floor] Fujiwara: Tonight you cost me my shot at becoming the first Welterweight Champion. You placed a blimish on an otherwise perfect night... My first match as a member of Team Tiger will forever be a loss.. For every action there is a re-action... For every choice, there are consequences... Kagemusha, you will soon suffer the consequences for your actions! Mindgames are meant to be played by two... [Shot fades to black] JA: Fujiwara is definitely going to want payback, and Kagemusha better be prepared to reap what he has sown. ER: Very tru Jonnie. Kagemusha is part of the Daiymo, and they seem to have issues with certain members of Chiimu Tora. it's bizarre! Our next comments are from the man Fujiwara lost to, Greg "Millionaire" Miller... ____ _____ _ _____________________________________________ / __/_ / / _ \ | /| / / / _\ \/ // / ___/ |/ |/ / Greg "Millionaire" Miller /___/\___/_/ |__/|__/__________________________________________/ (Scene opens up in a large room. The room is decroated with nice furniture, there is a grandfather clock in one corner of the room, and a big-screen TV in the other. A couch, and a couple of chairs fill up the middle of the room. And oriental rug is on the floor, and many elaborate draperies line the four large windows in the room. In one of the chairs sits Greg Miller. He has a white Chaps shirt, and a pair of light brown Khakis on. Miller turns on the TV, and he begins to talk.) Fujiwara Sunday evening was HELL for you. And for that fluke of a manager you have. Watch this!!! (Smiles...As he picks up the remote control, and presses the play button. A shot of Greg Miller behind Fujiwara landing the inverted DDT is seen. Then Miller jumps down on Fujiwara, and goes for the pin. He gets the three count, and the referee raises his Miller's hand in victory.) Sunday evening I beat you. Kagemusha came out, and....and....You should have seen the look on your face. (Laughs) I could not believe it. For a minute there during the match I thought you were gonna get me, but then I said to myself "Greg Miller being beaten? It just can't happen" (Pauses) Fujiwara...I do not doubt your wrestling skills at all. I just doubt that they are as good as mine. It was quite a tough break for you, but I cannot be blamed. Anyone in the same situation, would have done the same thing. At least I know I would have..... (Laughs) Well...enough about the PAST!!!! (Pauses) Let's talk about the FUTURE!!!! (Pauses Again) The future is Greg Miller going on to the second round of the Welterweight Tourney. As of yet I do not know who my opponent is, and I don't really care who my opponent is. It could be Super Tengu...I could care less. I can beat Super Tengu, and I can beat Mandarin. I can beat anyone who steps into the ring with me, and I am gonna prove it to you all. I will find out who my opponent is, after Metro on Tuesday. My opponent could be any of four different people. Regardless of who my opponent is...The match will end with the same outcome. (Pauses, and then smiles) Greg Miller will advance to the THIRD ROUND of the Welterweight Tourney. (Scene Fades) JA: Very cocky man. ER: With good reason to be. Miller's original tenure in SJPW wasn't stellar, but he's proven himself able to bounce back. He's gotten past the first round, and depending on who wins tonight's main event, will go on to face two of the toughest Welterweights in the entire world! JA: I'm not sure Miller is up to the task of defeating Mandarin or Super Tengu though. These two men are former multiple champions. That says alot for them, while Miller has yet to establish himself fully. ER: On that we both agree. Our next contest should be interesting, as All-Asian tag team champions The Bonecrushers take on Jerry & Keith, The Golden Boys... __ ___ _____________________________________________________ / |/ /__ / /________ / / /|_/ / -_) __/ __/ _ \ The Bonecrushers vs The Golden Boys /_/ /_/\__/\__/_/ \___/_______________________________________/ The All-Asian tag team champions found Jerry & Keith Golden fired up tonight, as the Golden Boys started the match strong. Jerry easily countered every move Jake Reed attempted, and finally took him down with knee to the gut. He tagged in Keith, and they proceeded to double team Reed until Lankford stormed the ring, sending both Golden Boys to the mat with a double lariat. Lankford took over on Keith, punishing him with slams & suplexes, until Keith reversed an irish whip, and planted Lankford with a side suplex. The Golden Boys then went for their Golden Superbomb, but Reed pushed Keith off the top rope. Aaron Lankford recovered enough to stun Jerry, and the Bonecrushers set him up for the Facesmasher when Kenji Ishida & Shinji Usami hit the ring, and nailed the Bonecrushers with chairs. They hit the Dragon Strike on Lankford, and were about to do the same to Reed, when the Tokyo Express stormed the ring, and drove Ishida & Usami off to the cheers of the fans. As the Express helped The Bonecrushers to the back, The Yakuza blocked their path. A few tense moments passed as the Bonecrushers & Yakuza had a staredown, then the Yakuza stepped aside, and let them and the Express pass.] Winners: The Bonecrushers by Disqualification. [The camera cuts back to the broadcast table...] ER: That was an odd situation. Ishida & Usami feel they deserve the tag titles, as do The Yakuza. The Tokyo Express dislike Ishida & Usami, so they come to the aid of the Bonecrushers, only to have the Yakuza confront both teams! JA: This situation is going to complicate the title picture here in SJPW. With the Nippon tag team tournament taking place, there is no set number one contendor. Last week, Ishida & Usami had their shot, and were disqualified. I have a feeling they are going to hound the Bonecrushers, and the Tokyo Express will hound them. As for The Yakuza, they just sit back, waiting for the right moment to strike. The Bonecrushers are marked men right now! ER: I have to agree there Jonnie.
