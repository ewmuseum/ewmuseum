SJPW House Show — Fukaya City Shiba Central Park (8/16/04)

Aug 20, 2004, 12:00:00 AM
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
August 16th, 2004 Fukaya City Shiba Central Park: 600 fans (Sellout) Super Japan started a three show run in Saitama with a show at the Fukaya City Shiba Central Park. Fans sold out the venue in a day, eager to see the Super Japan stars in action... 1) Scott Starring def El Gato Loco (5:20) after hitting The Rookie Mistake. 2) MAMMOTH Kagawa & Noboru "Raging Bull" Nishida def "The Wolverine" Masuhiro Hayatashi & Kenichi Kubota (10:50) after Kagawa hit the MAMMOTH Charge! on Hayatashi 3) Junya Toroyama def "Living Legend" Tommy Fury (13:08) after a high angle powerbomb. 4) Masashi "Lightning" Lanther & Jiraiya def Despair & Super Bakemono (17:24) after Lanther pinned Bakemono following a Lanther Bomb. 5) IICW Cruiserweight Title Match: "All-Star" Joey Darren (champion) def Fanta Boy (challenger) (14:00) after the All-Star Frog Splash - Scott Starring, continuing his singles push by Super Japan, defeated SMLL's El Gato Loco in short, but fast paced match. Loco tried to cheat his way to victory, taking the match outside, where he tried to blast Starring with a chair, only to have Starring nail him with a superkick, then hit an X-factor onto the chair. Starring rolled the dazed Loco into the ring, hit his Rookie Mistake Shooting Star Press, and scored the pinfall. - Kagawa & Nishida continue to rack up tag team wins, defeating the odd pairing of Hayatashi & Kubota. Kubota looked good against both men, and Hayatashi was fairly dominant, especially after a near fall Chokeslam from Hell on Nishida. However, Nishida fought his way back, and after hitting a series of three stiff lariats, tagged in Kagawa, who hit the MAMMOTH Charge! on Hayatashi, scoring the pinfall. - In a classic encounter, Junya Toroyama took on Tommy Fury, and scored an upset victory. Fury dominated the match early on, working over Toroyama's leg with a variety of moves, even getting him into a figure four leglock at one point. Toroyama fought his way back from near defeat, showing that even with his recent troubles, he still follows the fighting spirit he was taught. After keeping Fury off balance following a series of lariats, Toroyama hit a corner avalanche, then bulldogged Fury out of the corner. Without missing a beat, he set Fury up, and hit his high angle powerbomb, scoring the pinfall. - Team Tiger and the Disciples of Despair clashed again as Team Raiden took on the Gunryo Champion & Super Bakemono. Both teams really pulled out all the stops, hitting a variety of stiff and high flying moves. Neither side would give an inch, with Lanther being especially vicious with his former student Bakemono. Bakemono seemed hesitant to take on his former mentor for some reason, which lead to him taking too long when he attempted the Bakemono Bomb, allowing Lanther to roll out of the way. After nailing Despair with a koppo kick that sent him from the apron to the floor, Lanther hit his Lanther bomb on Bakemono, and scored the win for his team. - The main event saw Joey Darren defending his IICW cruiserweight title against the flamboyant Fanta Boy. Darren was a bit flustered by some of Fanta Boy's antics, such as when he hit a gutbuster on Darren, then proceeded to spank him. Fanta also rubbed his crotch in Darren's face in the corner, which Darren countered with a Manhattan Drop. After that, Darren was relentless, hitting Fanta Boy from everywhere, finally taking him down with a tornado DDT, and finishing him off with his All Star Frog Splash. The crowd was extremely pleased with the matches at this event, and may were discussing Bakemono's reluctance to face his former mentor. Some wonder if his recent partnership with Junya Toroyama has softened Bakemono's attitude towards Team Tiger.
