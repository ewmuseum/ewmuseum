Welcome to another edition of SJPW After Dark. This week, we have matches from a house show at the Osaka Dome, in Osaka, Japan. Tonight we'll see a return to action for both Badd Intentions, and SJPW Battlecade Champion Steel Serpent. We will also see matches featuring the superstars of HCW, as they make a return to Japan since the Battlecade '97 ppv. [pauses to look at his notes] JA: Up first, Zero Gravity, as he takes on Gregor Davinsky. [camera shifts to the big screen] ------------------------------------------------------------------- [Taped 8/16/97] ER: Zero doesn't seem very impressed with Davinsky's flexing. AO: Of course not! Gregor is nowhere near as defined as Zero is! Gregor moves to lock up, and Zero kicks him in the gut, locks his arms up, and takes him over with a butterfly suplex! So much for Gregor being the stronger of the two! ER: Zero gets up quickly, and stomps Gregor in the head. He drags him to his feet, whips him to the ropes, and sends him in the air high with a back bodydrop! He runs to the ropes, and nails Gregor in the back of the head with a dropkick! [crowd ooohs] AO: He's quick for someone his size! Gravity now with a swinging neckbreaker on Davinsky, jumps to his feet, then snaps an elbow on his throat! ER: Gravity looks to the crowd, then signals for the Gravity Bomb! [crowd cheers] He sets Davinsky up... GRAVITY BOMB! [crowd ooohs] Cover...one...two...THREE!!!! [crowd cheers] Ring Announcer: Your winner, ZERO GRAVITY!!! AO: That was a quick victory for Zero Gravity. ER: He totally dominated Davinsky, didn't he? ------------------------------------------------------------------- [the camera cuts back to Adonis in the Control Center] JA: Zero Gravity with a fast win over Gregor Davinsky. That's why he's one of the top stars in SJPW. [pauses to look at his notes] JA: Up next: Ladyhawke. This fascinating woman is slowly working her way up the ladder in SJPW. Tonight, we see her in action against Sumie Odaka. [camera shifts to the big screen] ------------------------------------------------------------------- [Taped 8/16/97] ER: Odaka rushes Ladyhawke, but she stops her short with a superkick! [crowd ooohs] Odaka staggers back, and Ladyhawke goes to the ropes...high knee! [crowd oooohs] AO: Ladyhawke drags Odaka up, and takes her back down with a belly to back suplex. She goes to the top rope... moonsault! [crowd oooohs] Cover...one...two...kickout! [crowd applauds] ER: Ladyhawke drags Odaka up, who rams a knee into her gut. Odaka hits her with a european uppercut, then hiptosses her to the mat. Odaka drops the elbow, gets to her feet, and flips onto Ladyhawke with a senton! [crowd applauds] AO: Odaka drags Ladyhawke to her feet, and whips her to the ropes... she locks Ladyhawke in an abdominal stretch, but Ladyhawke hiptosses Odaka over. Odaka gets to her feet...Ladyhawke leaps on her, and flips her over with a monkeyflip! [crowd applauds] ER: Odaka gets to her feet, and Ladyhawke whips her to the ropes...flying rana takes Odaka down, but she gets up when Ladyhawke does. She rushes Ladyhawke, who catches her with a powerslam! [crowd ooohs] AO: Ladyhawke drags Odaka to her feet, and whips her into the corner, following her in with an enzilariato. She signals to the crowd, then locks on a full nelson...BIRD OF PREY!!! [crowd applauds] One...two...THREE!!! [crowd applauds] Ring Announcer: Your winner, LADYHAWKE!!!! [crowd applauds] ER: Ladyhawke with an impressive win over Sumie Odaka. AO: This woman is headed for the top ranks in SJPW! ------------------------------------------------------------------- [camera cuts back to Adonis in the Control Center] JA: Ladyhawke with another impressive victory. If she keeps this up, I guarantee we'll be seeing her challenging for titles very, very soon. [pauses to look at his notes] JA: Our next match sees the return of Badd Intentions to SJPW, tuning up for their title shot at the Toyota Brothers. On this show, they faced the Wild Jamaicans, a strange team indeed... [camera shifts to the large screen] ------------------------------------------------------------------- [Taped 8/16/97] ER: Badd Intentions wasting no time attacking the Wild Jamaicans! Chill clotheslines Rastaman over the top rope, while Nick Patera whips Shakka to the ropes, and catches him, spinebusting him to the mat! [crowd ooohs] AO: Chill renters the ring after whipping Rastaman into the railing. He goes to the top rope, as Patera lifts Shakka up on his shoulders... DOOMSDAY DEVICE!!! [crowd oooooohs] ER: They don't even bother to cover him! Patera locks Shakka in his swinging full nelson....Shakka wastes no time submitting! Patera swings him around one more time, then lets go, sending him flying across the ring! [crowd oooohs] Ring Announcer: Your winners, by SUBMISSION, BIG CHILL & "NECKBREAKER" NICK PATERA, BADD INTENTIONS!!!! [crowd applauds] AO: They dominated the Wild Jamaicans. This match was a joke! ------------------------------------------------------------------- [camera cuts back to Adonis in the Control Center] JA: Badd Intentions simply slaughtered the Wild Jamaicans. I guess too much ganja does make you mellow [smiles]. The Toyota Brothers will have their hands full keeping their titles from these two determined men. [pauses to look at his notes] JA: Our next match see the return of Steel Serpent, who has been busy representing SJPW in the IWF/WOW Ultimate Wrestling Challenge tournament. He has made it to the third round, and will soon appear on the next IWF/WOW ppv. Tonight, the Battecade Champion faces Frankie "The Thumper" Douglas in a non-title match. [camera shifts to the large screen] ------------------------------------------------------------------- [Taped 8/16/97] ER: Douglas clotheslines Serpent as he enters the ring! [crowd ooohs] He starts to stomp away on his head! He drags Serpent to his feet, and pulls the title off his waist, then whips him to the ropes. Douglas smashes Serpent in the head with his own title belt! [crowd ooohs] AO: Douglas needs this if he expects to win. Serpent is just too good. The referee makes Douglas give up the title belt, and gives it to the ring announcer as Douglas presses Serpent over his head... Serpent rolls off, and lands behind Douglas. He turns... Thai elbowstrike! [crowd ooohs] Roundhouse kick! [crowd ooohs] Jumping spinwheel kick! [crowd ooohs] ER: Just like that, Steel Serpent shows why he IS the Battlecade Champion! Serpent locks Douglas in an armbar, and starts to smash elbows onto the joint. He twists the armbar into a hammerlock, hitches his tights, and pulls Douglas over with a belly to back suplex! [crowd ooohs] AO: Serpent gets to his feet, and leaps up, landing a stomp on Douglas' chest [crowd ooohs] He applies an anklelock, twisting hard on his leg. Douglas screams as Serpent slowly twists his leg. He reaches out, and just barely grabs the ropes. ER: Serpent breaks the hold, and waits as Douglas slowly gets to his feet. Serpent moves in, and Crescent kicks Douglas [crowd ooohs] He whips him to the ropes, and catches him as he comes off with a spinning backfist. Douglas staggers back, and Serpent goes for a wristlock, but Douglas counters with a short arm clothesline! [crowd ooohs] AO: Douglas stomps on Serpent's head, then drops a big elbow onto his chest. He covers...one...two..kickout! [crowd applauds] Douglas looks infuriated. He scoops Serpent up, and slams him down to the mat. He runs to the ropes...big legdrop! [crowd oooohs] He sits Serpent up, then locks on a reverse chinlock, sticking his knee into his back. ER: Serpent looks to be bleeding under his mask. He tries to get to his feet, but Douglas jams his knee into his back again. The ref asks him if he wants to give up, but he says no. He tries again to get up [crowd chants "Serpent! Serpent!"] He gets to one knee, and you can see that Douglas is losing his grip on the chinlock. Serpent gets to his feet, and rams an elbow into Douglas' gut. [crowd oooohs] He then jumps up, and locks a headscissors submission around his neck. Douglas tries to get to his feet, but Serpent has his legs locked on tight. Serpent usually relies on his martial arts skills, but tonight we're seeing that he is also an excellant grappler. Douglas was unprepared for this! I hope "Demon Boy" is watching! ER: Serpent shifts his body as Douglas gets to his knees. He lifts himself to his feet, but Serpent flips him over with a rana. Serpent kips up to his feet. Douglas gets to his feet, and charges Serpent. He swings at Serpent, who blocks, and counters with a jab of his own. Douglas staggers back, and Serpent grabs him by the hair, and smashes him in the face with a vicious headbutt! [crowd ooohs] AO: Douglas pokes Serpent in the eye, causing him to release Douglas. He grabs him by the throat...CHOKESLAM!!! [crowd oooohs] Cover...one...two...kickout! [crowd applauds] This is why Douglas is so dangerous; just when you think you have him, he makes a comeback! ER: Douglas looks pretty smug now. He whips Serpent to the ropes, and catches him...side suplex! [crowd ooohs] He gets up, and goes to the ropes...Corkscrew elbowdrop! What agility for a big man! AO: Douglas senses victory now. He drags Serpent to his feet, and lifts his arm up. He must be going for the "Thumper" He cocks his arm back...Serpent blocks it with his knee! [crowd ooohs] Douglas lets go of Serpent, holding onto his hand. Serpent's knees are like steel. Douglas shakes his hand, then looks up...DRAGON'S TAIL!!! [crowd cheers] Douglas flies across the ring! ER: Serpent isn't done yet. He drags Douglas to the center of the ring by his leg, then locks him in a cross face chickenwing! [crowd cheers] Douglas submits! [crowd cheers] Ring Announcer: Your winner, by SUBMISSION, STEEL SERPENT!!!! [crowd cheers, then throw streamers into the ring] AO: Serpent has to be sending a message to Ishrinku. The two of them will meet soon enough, and when they do, Serpent is going to cause him some serious pain! ------------------------------------------------------------------- [camera cuts back to Adonis in the Control Center] JA: Steel Serpent is a man many people in SJPW underestimate. I should know, since I've worked out with him. When he finally does face "Demon Boy" Ishrinku, it's going to be a wild and bloody match, most likely more extreme than anything I ever competed in!
