RSPWF 200 and SJPW (the operator's year-end review)

16-18 December 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
----- RSPWF 200 and SJPW (Dec 16, 1997, 12:00:00 AM) ----- Hell, if everyone else is going to do this, then I might as well too! :) As the newcomer on the block, I have to say that SJPW has acquired a strong reputation since I took over from [name withheld] in April. Even I had doubts that a promotion based in Japan, and compromised of at least half it's talent as japanese would last as it has. Faces have come and gone, but the fed moves on. I know many people feel my booking style unorthodox, as many of the champions in the promotion have been lesser known people, but believe me, everyone who has held a title in SJPW has earned it! Anyway, here is a list of those people who compete in SJPW [among other feds] that ranked: 169. Patty Babes Carter Patty has had a bit of a bad year in SJPW, with a slightly lackluster record. However, he seems to be picking up momentum following his loss to WWGP world champion Lance Smith in a controversial ending. The reason for this momentum? Saving Dan Kauffman from the next man on my list... 77. Survival Here is a guy who tied with Caleb Temple in the half year awards, then quit e-wrestling shortly thereafter. Now he's back, and SJPW has him. He was on indefinite suspension, but Dan Kauffman just *HAD* to offer him a contract. This past monday, Dan may have learned that he may have made one of the biggest mistakes of his SJPW career. This guy only cares right now about causing mayhem and if it wasn't for Patty Babes Carter, there may not even be a match between the two 68. Blackline Very underrated cruiserweight. Here is a guy who's wrestled Steve Sampson to a draw [yes, THE Steve Sampson], and beaten Derek Mota in a wild match [Derek's only loss in SJPW to date] Currently co-holder of the All Asian tag team titles, Blackline has now drawn the attention of not only Nik Nyce, but World Champion Lance Smith, all because he is partners with Alex Akira, a rookie who has made waves. 1998 looks like a good year for the angst ridden Blackline 64. Derek Mota He lost a loser leaves town match in NLWP to Requiem. He shows up for a tour of SJPW, and in his debut match gets the crap kicked out of him by Requiem AND Soultaker, and STILL wins his match! What else needs saying. Sure, he lost the IIWF cruiserweight title. Everyone loses eventually. Now Derek has a chance to earn more glory when he faces not only SJPW cruiserweight champion Ace Kilcannon for the belt in a cage match, but he also faces the next man on the list... 51. Super Tengu The most controversial wrestler in SJPW! Super Tengu is the only man on this list [to my knowledge] who wrestles exclusively in SJPW, so his ranking here shows the power one fed's handlers can have in a vote. The man has an almost cult-like following of the younger fans in Japan, and was recently considered a fan favorite following his loss to Ace Kilcannon at The Super J Crown tournament. Following a recent loss to Masashi "Lightning" Lanther, his own uncle, Tengu has gone back to being the most despised man in SJPW after Survival. His year long feud with Shinji Hamada, his supposed relations with Rekka Sakura [his sister], and his 'affair' with "Heartbreaker" Robin Blaze are the stuff epics are written about [well, maybe not <G>] If anyone can be called "The Franchise" in SJPW, Super Tengu is that man. He may not be the best man to represent what SJPW stands for with his attitude, but his ring ability more than makes up for it 50. Nik Nyce Although a certain other fedhead has insinuated that the handler of this wrestler kisses my ass [think again bucko!], Nik Nyce has proven himself to be one of the most reliable handlers in SJPW. Sure, he has yet to win a title. Sure, he's lost to some people he shouldn't have. However, he did beat 5 men in one night, and not because he kissed ass either :) Seriously though, Nik has drawn the ire of Dan Kauffman with his recent mindgames, World Champion Lance Smith, and even the young Alex Akira. With the forced retirement of Hiro Kanemoto, Nik faces his next big challenge when he faces Tengu's nemesis, Shinji Hamada, for the now-vacant All Asian heavyweight title. Win or lose, Nik Nyce will make this a match to remember Last, and far from least, we have... 19. Dan Kauffman Dan came to SJPW for a tour. However, the response to his arrival caused such a numerous amount of challenges for him, both from within the fed, and without, Dan has been offered a full time contract. Although he hasn't said yes yet, the attack monday night by Survival should be prime motivation for him to stay. With Nik Nyce and Lance Smith looming on the horizon, Dan needs to stay focused, or else Survival will put a hurt on him worse than he did this past monday night. Plus, rumours of a certain "Butcher" possibly touring SJPW next year could add to the troubles Dan has seen. 1997 has looked a bit sour for "The Flash", but if he gets a win over Survival, the year won't end with a bitter taste left in his mouth. That's what's up with SJPW's superstars who made the top 200. Congrats to all of them, and everyone who won! Yes, I'm being humble. Last year I scored in the top 200 with Nova Storm and now this year Super Tengu falls one spot short of the top 50. Would I have placed higher with Tengu if he were multi-fed? Possibly... will I go Multi-fed to expand Tengu's appeal? HELL NO!! I already spread myself out thin with Mike Malone (though not nearly as much as I used to. Right now he's in 2 feds as opposed to the 6 he was in back in June). If I take Tengu to other places I fear that my creative juices for his flashing would be strained. Through Tengu I've managed to create a whole diverese family of wrestlers. Almost all of mywrestlers are of blood relation in one way or another (save for my team the Mystic Beast Kings). I've used Tengu as a springboard to help develop the characters of other characters (especially Rekka Sakura, who was struggling in the GDWA. But by making the exact opposite of Tengu I've turned her into the most humble wrestler in the fed). The coming year looks bright for Tengu. With the cage match coming up against Killcanon and Mota I forsee Tengu with gold. Then Shinji Hamada and I have unfinished business. Who knows... perhaps a run at the Super J Crown title and the WWGP strap are in the future. Whatever Tengu goes after though, you can bet both excitement and controversy will follow. After all he is "Puroresu No Kamisama". Just fair warning people, don't even ask for Tengu to sign with another fed. The top 200 tends to generate email from new fed heads who want to recruit people to get their fed over fast. Tengu STAYS in SJPW and will only wrestle somehwere else only by special arrangement through myself and [name withheld]. I'm not trying to flash an ego here but I remembver last year when Nova storm made the list (after 1 month of e-wrestling) I got about 10 guys begging me to join their fed. Tengu started in SJPW and that's where he stays!! J.H. Handler of a very largew family of Japanese wrestlers J.H.
