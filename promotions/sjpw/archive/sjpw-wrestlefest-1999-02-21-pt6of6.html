SJPW Cards

Wrestlefest

21 February 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Wrestlefest Concludes...] [Banshee gets to his feet, and walks over to Yoshida. Moditan gets in his way, and attempts to beg him off. Banshee looks out at the crowd, then kicks Moditan in the gut, and throws him over the security railing! The crowd cheers loudly, and Banshee drags Yoshida to his feet, and rams his head into the railing. Moditan gets to his feet with the aid of some ring attendants, and heads up the runway, shaking his fist at Banshee. Banshee whips Yoshida hard into the railing, then charges him. He jumps up, and takes Yoshida over into a monkey flip, but Yoshida lands on his feet! Banshee turns around, and Yoshida catches him with a spinning heel kick...] ER: Banshee gave Moditan what for, didn't he? AO: Thank Buddha! That man is a pest! [Yoshida rams Banshee's head into the ring apron, then slides into the ring. He runs to the ropes on the opposite side, and comes off, sending Banshee slamming into the railing with a baseball slide! He steps out onto the ring apron, leaps, and lands a stiff elbowsmash to the top of Banshee's masked head. Grabbing Banshee by the mask, he rolls him into the ring. He slides in after him, and drags Banshee to his feet. He double underhooks the arms, and takes him over into a butterfly suplex. He drags Banshee back to his feet, and takes him over into a gutwrench powerbomb. Yoshida grapevines the leg, but Banhsee manages to kick out at two and a half. Yoshida gets to his feet, and moves towards the corner. He climbs to the top rope, and perches there. Banshee slowly gets to his feet, and looks around for Yoshida. He turns towards the corner, and Yoshida leaps, almost decapitating Banshee with a flying clothesline! The crowd "ooohs" loudly, sensing the match may soon be over. Yoshida grabs Banshee by the legs, and starts to apply The Genyosha Deathlock, but Banshee is able to grab the ropes. Yoshida attempts to power him over, but Banshee grabs him by the hair, and pulls him into a cradle position! Botan drops to count, and Yoshida kicks out at one...] ER: Banshee is very lucky! Yoshida almost finished him off there! AO: I'm beginning to wonder if Yoshida's new attitude has given him the edge to win this match... [Yoshida drags Banshee to his feet, and tries to set him up for a DDT but Banshee picks him up and counters with an atomic drop. Maintaining his grip on Yoshida, Banshee lifts him up in a vertical suplex position, but then drops forward into a gourdbuster! As Yoshida lays facedown on the mat, Banshee steps out onto the apron, grabs the top rope and flings himself back into the ring with a slingshot legdrop onto the back of Yoshida's neck! The crowd "oooohs" loudly, then applauds. Banshee rolls Yoshida over, and makes a cover, but Yoshida kicks out at two and a half! The crowd "awwws" in frustration, though some people start to chant "YOSHIDA! YOSHIDA!"...] ER: Some of the crowd are actually getting behind Yoshida now... AO: He has wrestled an excellant match, and has fought honorably. Even I can admire that, even if his attitude disturbs me... [Banshee, shaking his head in frustration. gets to his feet. He drags Yoshida to his feet, then sends him to the mat with a enzugiri! Banshee immediately drags Yoshida up, scoops him up, and slams him to the mat. He runs to the corner, and climbs to the top rope. Steadying himself. Banshee leaps off, landing a flying elbowdrop onto Yoshida's chest! He goes out on the apron, grabs the ropes, and slingshots into the ring, landing on top of Yoshida with a splash! As the crowd "ooohs", Banshee grapevines the leg, and Botan drops to count. Yoshida kicks out at two and three quarters, and the crowd "awwws", then applauds. Banshee slaps the mat in frustration, and gets to his feet. He scoops Yoshida up once more, and slams him to the mat. He heads to the corner, and starts to climb to the top rope. Yoshida gets to his feet, and in a daze, falls backward to the ropes, causing Banshee to crotch himself! The crowd "oooohs" as Banshee falls onto the top rope, and sits there, dazed. Yoshida leans on the ropes, shaking his head, then sees Banshee sitting on the top rope. He moves towards the corner, then grabs the ropes, jumps to the top rope next to the corner, and springboards off, catching Banshee with a spinning leg lariat that sends him crashing to the floor! The crowd "ooohs", as Yoshida himself lands hard on the mat, and lays dazed...] ER: Yoshida pulled that move out of nowhere! But it cost him too! AO: It looks like he may be unconcious... [While Botan checks on Yoshida, two masked men leap over the security railing, Hadi Moditan behind them. One of them grabs Banshee, and holds him, while the other man starts to lay into him with side kicks to his ribs. Some of the ring attendants move towards them, and the men release Banshee, and attack them! The crowd starts to boo, and Hadi Moditan takes a cheap shot, kicking Banshee in the groin! Jessica Starbird moves towards him, and he gets in her face! The masked men dispose of the ring attendants, and once more start to pummel Banshee! Suddenly, the crowd cheers as Kagemusha comes charging down the runway, and attacks the masked men from behind! The two masked men double team him, and when Hadi Moditan turns to see what is going on, Jessica Starbird shoves him to the floor. She helps Banshee to his feet, and in his dazed state, shoves her off....] ER: What the hell is going on here? Who are these masked men, and what are they doing with Hadi Moditan? AO: And why is Kagemusha helping Banshee? [In the ring, Botan sees the fight outside the ring, and motions for security to come out. The masked men beat Kagemusha down, then help Moditan to his feet. Yoshida also gets to his feet, and leans on the ropes. He sucks in quick breaths. Banshee, still slightly dazed, climbs up on the apron, and enters the ring. He staggers around, looking for Yoshida. Yoshida sees him, and lunges off the ropes, and nails Banshee with a superkick! The crowd "ooohs" as Banshee collapses in a heap, and Yoshida falls on him for a cover. Botan drops to count, and before Banshee can kick out, makes the three count. The crowd gives a mixed pop at this, and Moditan and his masked cronies laugh on the runway...] ER: What a travesty of justice! AO: Yoshida didn't know what happened outside the ring though. He did the right thing. He saw an opening, and he took it... Nishi: The winner of this match, as a result of a pinfall... SHIRYO NO YOSHIDA! [Jessica Starbird helps Kagemusha to his feet, and the two of them enter the ring. Yoshida gets to his feet, and Botan raises his hands. He falls back to the ropes, still slightly dazed from the beating he took. He exits the ring, and many people start a "Yoshida!" chant. Kagemusha & Starbird check on Banshee, then help him to his feet. On the runway, Moditan produces a microphone, and starts to speak...] Hadi Moditan: "Banshee, you, the rest of Team Tiger, and everyone else in SJPW will pay for your trepasses into the tomb of Pro Japan Wrestling. I'm sure your all asking who are these men. These are my men, my shooters. While the rest of this industry, my friends, have turned on me, these men remain loyal. Tonight is just the beginning, in time all of the debts owed to Hadi Moditian by this orgization and its so called star "wrestlers" will be paid. Prepare yourself SJPW... these men are no professional wrestlers, they are true fighters, the last of a dying breed... They are shooters!" AO: That weasel! I'll strangle him myself! ER: Akira! Control yourself! [Moditan and his shooters walk to the back, security surrounding them. In the ring, Banshee is on his hands and knees, and he slaps the mat in frustration. He gets to his feet, nods to Kagemusha, and exits the ring. Starbird follows him, trying to console him. Banshee's eyes betray his dissapointment, and he waves off Starbird, and heads to the back. Kagemusha, a sad look on his face, watches the two of them head to the back, then exits the ring, and walks to the back himself...] ER: This situation is really bizarre. Who are Moditan's masked me, and what, if any, connection do they have to Shiryo no Yoshida? AO: And why did Kagemusha, a member of The Daiymo, come to the aid of The Banshee, a member of their rivals in Chiimu Tora? ER: These are questions we will definitely have to look at in the coming weeks! AO: I'll say! Banshee is too good of a man to be cheated like this! ER: I totally agree. Yamashita needs to do his damn job! AO: Hah! As if that will ever happen! ER: Well Akira, we're finally here. The main event. The match we've all been waiting for. The battle to end all battles. AO: The war to end all wars you mean! For the first, time, and possibly the last and only time, Mitsunori Takizawa will face his former tag team partner, Ed Dean. This has been a long time in coming. These two men have one goal: To destroy each other! ER: We all know Dean's contempt for Takizawa, as well as the rest of SJPW. He feels he is above all of SJPW, and that his "Strong Style" is superior to puroresu. So far, he's proven to be better than the rest of Chiimu Tora. AO: But tonight! Tonight, he faces a man who many underestimate. Tonight, he faces Mitsunori Takizawa, the one man who may know Ed Dean better than anyone in this world. For Dean, it is the chance to finally pay Takizawa back for his supposed betrayal. For Takizawa, it is a chance to restore honor to himself, Chiimu Tora, and all of SJPW! He is our last, best, and only hope for that restoration. If he loses tonight, SJPW will be so dishonored, the company may have to close down! ER: Right now, we take you to Takizawa, who is waiting backstage... [Camera opens in the locker room of Mitsunori Takizawa. The middle-aged warrior is dressed for his match with the SJPW champion, Ed Dean, and he looks calm and at peace as he looks up at the camera. He lightly tugs at the end of his mustache as he looks solemnly at the camera] MT: Well Dean, the time is almost here. I hope you've enjoyed having that gold title around your waist, because after tonight that's over. Tonight that title comes back to the Japanese wrestlers who deserve to have someone they can respect hold it. The wrestlers of SJPW may not like me, they may not even want to see me with the title... but I'm sure the majority of them are tired of seeing an arrogant Gaijin walking around with it, disrespect it... using it to bolster claims that the "strong style" of the dead SHVF was better." [Takizawa chuckles lightly at the mention of SHVF.] MT: "How is your precious Strong Style doing , Dean? It looks to me like it died a slow, painful death. Your SHVF champion was defeated by some unknown... the promotion crumbled... and now you get to be the final dirt thrown on it's coffin. Rest In Peace SHVF... the biggest extoller of your virtues is on his way to meet you in disgrace." [Takizawa stands up and punches his right hand into his left fist, grinning into the lens.] MT: I will beat you tonight Dean. I will bloody you and you will bloody me, but in the end I will prevail and SJPW will again have a champion to represent IT'S wrestlers, and not the followers of another promotion. For too long you have been holding the most prestigious title in SJPW and you have treated it with no respect. Tonight, you will LEARN about respect Dean, because I will beat you down and you will have to respect the fact that you lost ... TWICE ... to the man you will NEVER be able to beat. [Takizawa turns to exit the lockeroom, then stops and looks back at the camera for a moment.] MT: Enjoy your last hour as champion Dean. It all ends shortly. [Takizawa exits the dressing room, to start his long journey to the ring...] ____ _____ _ _____________________________________________ / __/_ / / _ \ | /| / / All-Asian Juuryou Shimeijiai: / _\ \/ // / ___/ |/ |/ / Mitsuonori Takizawa vs Ed Dean /___/\___/_/ |__/|__/__________________________________________/ [The camera switches from ringside to the ring itself where Nishi Toju is standing with his microphone in hand.] Nishi: This contest is scheduled for one fall and is for the All-Asian Juuryou Championship! Introducing first, the challenger... Nishi: ...hailing from Tokyo, Japan...weighing in at 300 pounds... MIT-SU-NOR-I TA-KI-ZA-WA! [The crowd gives a loud cheer as "Too Old To Rock and Roll, Too Young to Die" by Jethro Tull begins playing over the P.A. system. Takizawa steps out through the entrance curtains and pauses as the fans greet him with a standing ovation.] AO: You can feel the emotion in this arena tonight! These fans *WANT* Takizawa to bring Dean down!! ER: You're right! Normally, puroresuring fans are more restrained than this but tonight, *EVERYONE* wants to see Takizawa succeed. [Takizawa walks down the aisle, waving at the fans while wearing blue baggy wrestling tights, black wrestling boots and blue elbow pads on both arms. His long, scraggly black hair is down to his shoulders, and a Fu-Manchu style mustache and beard decorates his chin. His forehead, chest, arms, and back are covered with scars from his years in the squared circle and he steps into the ring, faces the corner and smacks salt between his hands for good luck.] Nishi: And his opponent... fighting out of New York, New York but currently residing in Stone Mountain, Georgia... weighing in at 355 pounds... the undisputed All-Asian Juuryou Champion... ED DEAN! [A few American-style boo's can be heard but mostly the crowd responds with a polite cheer a lone figure appears in the entranceway without any accompanying music. The huge Ed Dean is wearing his usual red singlet, red kneepads and shin guards over blue Asics, white wrist tape and a black T-shirt that reads "G-MAX DOJO" on the front in red. Over Dean's massive shoulder is the All-Asian title belt which he taps possessively as he starts walking down to the ring.] AO: This is unusual. Normally Dean has an entire entourage accompanying him to the ring. ER: In one of his recent interviews, he distanced himself from Hadi Moditian and said he was doing things on his own so perhaps this is what we can expect. AO: An Ed Dean match without ringside backup... who would have guessed?!? [Dean ignores the crowd as he stalks down to the ring, staring at Takizawa intently. At ringside, Dean climbs in, performing some last minute stretching in preparation for the match after handing the title belt to the referee.] ER: Takizawa eager to get things underway as he walks right over and gets into Dean's face, standing nose to nose with him. AO: We can't hear what the former tag team partners are saying to each other but from their expressions, I don't expect it's very nice. ER: The bell has rung and the match is officially underway but neither man has moved yet as they continue to verbally berate each other at close distance... OH! HEADBUTT BY TAKIZAWA SENDS DEAN REELING! AO: And the action begins as Takizawa immediately rushes forward, scoops Dean up... and plants him into the canvas with a bodyslam! ER: Dean off his feet very quickly as Takizawa unleashes a barrage of stomps to his head and shoulders. A lot of emotion in this contest as both men bear a lot of hatred towards the other. AO: Takizawa brings Dean to his feet... and is doubled over as Dean catches him with a kneelift that drives the wind from his lungs. Dean looking furious as he drives repeated knee strikes into Takizawa's ribs and stomach. ER: Two very large, very powerful men in that ring and it's almost like watching two bulls go after each other. Dean now firing away on Takizawa as he lays in a couple of forearm shots to the head. Takizawa back-pedaling as he's feeling those strikes... AO: But Dean snags an arm, sends him into the ropes with an Irish whip... AND KNOCKS TAKIZAWA DOWN TO THE MAT WITH A MIGHTY LARIAT! [the crowd "Ooohhs" and applauds] ER: Some high-power maneuvers from both men as Dean goes for an elbowdrop... but Takizawa moves! Dean in pain as his elbow hit the canvas as Takizawa manages to roll to his feet. AO: Takizawa regaining control as he drives an elbowsmash into the top of Dean's head. He now pulls the champion to his feet... and sends him crashing into the corner turnbuckle with authority! ER: A lot of strength behind that Irish whip as Takizawa charges in... OOOH! And a mighty splash in the corner squashes Dean against the turnbuckle! AO: You said these two were like two bulls earlier?!? More like bull elephants... and mad ones at that! ER: Neither man holding back as Takizawa puts on an impressive strength display as he lifts Dean up out of the corner and places him on his shoulder. He starts running across the ring... RUNNING POWERSLAM! [the crowd "Ooohhs"] AO: I think the entire ring shifted just now! Takizawa now going for the pin: ...1... ...2... ...shoulder up! [the crowd applauds] ER: Tremendous impact from that running powerslam and Dean is having a hard time with Takizawa so far. Takizawa now holding his hand in the air and I think he's going for the stomach claw! AO: He's got a lot of stomach to work with as he tries to slap it on... but Dean manages to roll out of reach and avoids the claw. Takizawa right on top of him... OWCH! Thumb to the eye and Takizawa is blinded! ER: The referee was out of position to see that as Dean once again demonstrates he's willing to do anything to win a match. But in this case, I think he hates Takizawa so much that he just wants to hurt him. AO: No doubt. Dean now up beside Takizawa and he drives a fist into Takizawa's belly. Sets him up... AND HURLS HIM OVER WITH A TREMENDOUS GUTWRENCH SUPLEX! [the crowd applauds] ER: A great move by the champion as he takes Takizawa down and now he's punishing him with stiff kicks to the legs and body. Takizawa manages to roll away and climb to his feet but Dean immediately slaps on a side headlock and has him trapped. AO: It's almost a chokehold there as Dean squeezes tightly on Takizawa's head and neck. Takizawa manages to push him off and Dean goes into the ropes, rebounding back... and he bowls Takizawa over with a powerful shoulderblock! ER: No subtlety there, just raw power... AND DEAN CATCHES TAKIZAWA IN A FUJIWARA ARMBAR! AO: He might be trying to soften up the arm for his Juji Gatame/Cross Armbreaker finisher. And Takizawa is in a lot of pain but he's refusing to submit. Both men are ring veterans but I'd say Takizawa has the slight edge in ring experience and he uses that to advantage as he manages to hook his foot over the bottom rope. ER: The referee forcing Dean to release the hold... but he immediately nails an elbowdrop to that injured shoulder. AO: I'd guess that softening up that limb is definitely part of Dean's game plan as he brings Takizawa to his feet with a standing wristlock. Dean keeping up the pressure as he pounds away on Takizawa's arm with a series of forearm shots. ER: You're right in that neither of these men are strangers to the ring. And while Takizawa is more of a brawler, he can apply a few submission holds of his own. AO: Right now, though, Dean has been hammering away on him and now Takizawa slumps down to one knee... OH! Dean connects with a mighty kick to the side of the head!! ER: That really hurt as Takizawa's head just snapped around from the force of that blow! AO: Dean really being aggressive as he goes after Takizawa's arm once more... sets him up... OVER THE SHOULDER ARMBREAKER! [the crowd "Ooohhs"] ER: You can see the pain etched on Takizawa's face as Dean is really causing a lot of damage to that arm. AO: Dean's momentum rapidly building as he steps forward... thrust kick by Takizawa drives Dean backward! [the crowd applauds] ER: Takizawa caught Dean by surprise with that as he grabs the champion's head... DDT! AO: Wow! Dean's head just bounced right off the canvas and Takizawa gets himself out of a lot of trouble as Dean was really wearing down that arm. ER: Unfortunately the damage has been done as Takizawa is nursing that injured limb instead of going after Dean while he's down. AO: That's funny. Dean looks like he's out cold but he's taken worse hits before. That DDT looked nasty but... OOH! DEAN WAS PLAYING POSSUM AND HE JUST SUCKERED IN TAKIZAWA!! ER: Yikes! A painful knife-edge chop to the throat caught Takizawa by surprise as he was pulling Dean to his feet. And the champion displays a little cunning there as he pretended that DDT hurt him worse than it really did. AO: It's a sign of his frustration level that Takizawa even fell for such a ploy. But it was effective nonetheless as Dean steps up behind Takizawa... HALF-NELSON/HAMMERLOCK SUPLEX! [the crowd "Ooohhs" and applauds] ER: What a tremendous move as Dean locked Takizawa into a hammerlock and a half-nelson, then falling backwards to drive Takizawa's head and neck into the mat! AO: Notice that Dean isn't going for a pin, though? He doesn't want to just win this match, he wants to deliberately injure Takizawa! ER: And he could very easily do that as he's locked on a facelock/armlock submission hold and Takizawa is flailing away, trying to fight his way out of it. AO: Again, Dean targeting that arm he's been working on throughout the match and you can't fault his strategy. He's picked his focus and all of his attacks have been concentrating on that one area. ER: You can't tell me you admire him?!? AO: No, he's baka! But he's *DANGEROUS* baka, that's all. ER: Too true. Takizawa is in deep trouble as Dean has that painful hold locked in and it's inflicting a lot of pain on that arm. The referee is checking on him... AO: Has Takizawa passed out?!? ER: It's hard to tell. His eyes are closed and the referee is raising his arm... and it falls to the mat! AO: This is not a good sign! The referee raising that arm for a second time... and it falls again! ER: Dean may very well have won this match by driving Takizawa into unconsciousness! The referee raises the arm for a third and final time... IT STAYS UP! TAKIZAWA IS STILL IN THIS MATCH!! [the crowd cheers loudly] AO: And look at this! Takizawa is somehow finding reserves of strength to power himself to his knees. He's trying to fight his way out of that hold... OOOFF! Dean knocks him back down again with a nasty kneestrike to the side of the head. ER: Just when it looked like Takizawa might be making a comeback, Dean hammers him and retains control of the match. Dean now pulling a limp Takizawa to his feet, setting him up for a snap suplex... NO! TAKIZAWA BLOCKS IT, GRABS DEAN AROUND THE WAIST AND FLINGS HIM OVERHEAD WITH AN INCREDIBLE BELLY-TO-BELLY SUPLEX! [the crowd cheers loudly] AO: Where did he get that from?!? Dean was pounding away on him, giving Takizawa the beating of his life when suddenly he pulled that out of a hat! ER: Takizawa still weakened by the punishment he's absorbed but he's earned himself a breather with that tremendous suplex. Both men slow to regain their feet but Dean is still the fresher man. He comes in low for a fireman's carry takeover... but Takizawa counters with a kneelift! AO: Dean still bent over as Takizawa grabs him and lifts him up... PILEDRIVER! [the crowd "Ooohhs"] ER: And again, Takizawa drives the top of Dean's skull into the canvas! First a DDT a little while ago and now a *VERY* punishing piledriver! AO: At 355 pounds, Dean has a *LOT* of weight coming down onto the top of his head! Takizawa making a comeback late in this match and he's really taking it to Dean. He picks the larger man up over his shoulders... BRAINBUSTER! [the crowd applauds] ER: Once more, Dean's head is the physical target of choice for Takizawa and I think he's setting him up for the Cradle DDT. AO: Dean is very woozy as Takizawa pulls him up and sends him into the ropes with an Irish whip. I don't think Dean is fully conscious of where he is right now as he rebounds back... Takizawa catches him... CRADLE DDT! HE DID IT!! HE DID IT!!! ER: Dean is out of it as Takizawa hooks the leg: ...1... ...2... ...3!!!!! [crowd erupts into cheers] **ding** **ding** **ding** Nishi: Here is your winner and *NEW* All-Asian Juuryou Champion... MITSUNORI TAKIZAWA!!! [a loud cheer rises from the crowd as paper streamers descend into the ring] ER: YES! Finally Chiimu Tora regains SJPW's honor!!! AO: SJPW regains it's honor! [From the back, the rest of Chiimu Tora, sans Fujiwara, come out from the back, and head to the ring. The members of The Daimyo come out as well, as do many other competitors from SJPW. Hamada & Takizawa embrace, then Takizawa embraces Matsumoto. Nishida & Onslaught hoist Takizawa onto their shoulder, and parade him around the ring. Tiger Kikuchi enters the ring, the All-Asian title in his hands. He hands the title up to Takizawa, who motions to be let down. He takes the title from Kikuchi, then bows to him. Kikuchi smiles, and returns the bow, and the crowd erupts into a new round of cheers. Nishida & Onslaught hoist Takizawa up onto their shoulders again, and a second wave of streamers hit the ring...] ER: What a tremendous night for SJPW! History has been made! AO: This night will long be remembered! ER: I'll say! We are out of time everyone. We will see you all again real soon! [The camera shifts back to the ring, where the wrestlers are still celebrating. The credits start to roll, and the camera view shifts to Ed Dean being helped out of the ring by Doug Thomas. He helps Dean up the runway, and as the camera fades out, Dean stops, turns around, and looks out at the ring, With a look of dissapointment on his face, he shakes his head, and head hung low, turns around and allows Thomas to help him to the back....] ____ _____ ____ __ __ /\ _`\ /\___ \/\ _`\ /\ \ __/\ \ \ \,\L\_\\/__/\ \ \ \L\ \ \ \/\ \ \ \ \/_\__ \ _\ \ \ \ ,__/\ \ \ \ \ \ \ /\ \L\ \/\ \_\ \ \ \/ \ \ \_/ \_\ \ \ `\____\ \____/\ \_\ \ `\___x___/ \/_____/\/___/ \/_/ '\/__//__/ © 1999 http://www.[name withheld].com/sjpw/
