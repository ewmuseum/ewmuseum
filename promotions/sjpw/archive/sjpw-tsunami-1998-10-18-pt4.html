Tsunami, Hour Two 2/2

18 October 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
TK: Combattler II drags Retsudo back to his feet, and starts to rain elbowsmashes to his shoulder...Retsudo drops to one knee, his face a mask of pain. Combattler II with another armbar, and he drags Retsudo to his corner, and tags in Kazuya. Kazuya carefully goes to the top rope, then comes off with a stomp to Retsudo's shoulder! [crowd "ooohs"] AO: He could have disclocated it with that move! Kazuya now raining chops on the shoulder, and Retsudo's face is turning red from biting back a possible scream! Kazuya now with an armbar himself, and he stands Retsudo up...DDT on the arm! [crowd "oooohs"] He rolls Retsudo over for a cover.... ...1... ...2... TK: KICKOUT WITH AUTHORITY! [crowd applauds] Retsudo sits up, his face a mask of rage! Now we see why he is called "The Raging Bull"! Retsudo is on his feet, and he sets himself as Kazuya gets up... SPEAR by Retsudo sends Kazuya backwards, cracking the back of his head on the top turnbuckle! [crowd "oooohs"] He might have snapped Kazuya's neck there! AO: Retsudo gets to his feet, and he starts to kick away at Kazuya! [crowd "ooohs"] He's kicking his chest, his face, and his knee! He's lost it now! Combattler II comes in, and he nails Retsudo from behind with a forearm shot, but it had no effect! [crowd "ooohs"] Retsudo turns around, and Combattler II is backing off, waving his hands in front of him, asking for mercy! Retsudo roars, and SPEARS him as well! [crowd "ooooohs"] TK: Retsudo gets to his feet, and screams to the rafters! He tags in Huan, then grabs Combattler II. He drags him to his feet, and hoists him over his shoulder. He runs towards the corner, and drops Combattler II face first onto the top turnbuckle! [crowd "oooohs"] Combattler II sags in the corner, and Huan charges, dropkicking him in the back of the head! [crowd "ooohs"] He backflips off, landing on his feet. Retsudo grabs Combattler II from behind, and pulls him out of the corner. Huan goes to the top rope...German Suplex by Retsudo, while Huan nails him with a thrust kick off the top rope! [crowd "oooohs"] AO: Lady Mai Tai is beside herself! She's on the apron now, screaming at Botan to do something about this double teaming! Botan makes Retsudo go to his corner, while Huan rolls Combattler II to the floor. He charges Kazuya in the corner, but Kazuya catches him with a spinwheel kick coming in! [crowd "ooohs"] Huan staggers back, and Kazuya with a superkick to the chin! [crowd "oooohs"] TK: But Kazuya's other knee gave out on him! He's laying on the mat in pain! Huan is laying dazed on the mat, while Kazuya is holding his knee! [The referee starts to put the count on both men] AO: Kazuya rolls to the ropes, and attempts to rise to his feet. Huan is still laying there... [Botan continues to count both men out] TK: Kazuya is getting up now, and Huan is starting to sit up! [Botan continues to count...] AO: Huan sits up, shaking his head, while Kazuya makes it to his feet! Kazuya starts to walk off the pain, while Huan keeps shaking his head... TK: Huan tries to rise, and Kazuya charges...Kazuya grabs Huan, and sets him up...snap suplex, rolls over, and brings them both up ...another snap suplex, rolls over, and again brings them both to their feet..another snap suplex! [crowd applauds] Kazuya with a cover... ...1... ...2... AO: Kickout! [crowd applauds] Kazuya drags Huan up...URAKEN! Cover... ...1... ...2... TK: KICKOUT! [crowd applauds] Kazuya is getting frustrated! He gets to his feet, and he starts to kick away at Huan! [crowd "oooohs"] Retsudo enters the ring, and he charges, clipping Kazuya in the knee! [crowd "ooohs"] Combattler II comes in, and he slams into Retsudo, and both men fall through the ropes to the floor, brawling! [crowd "oooohs"] AO: Huan is sitting up again, shaking his head. He's taken quite a beating, but his partner laid Kazuya out with that knee clip! Kazuya rolls to the corner, and attempts to get to his feet. Huan rolls to the ropes, and starts to pull himself up as well... [Both men rise to their feet simultaneously, and the crowd applauds both men. Outside the ring, security is attempting to seperate Retsudo & Combattler II from each other...] TK: Both men move to the center of the ring, and Kazuya attempts a cross bodyblock, but Huan ducks, and Kazuya crashes to the mat! [crowd "oooohs"] Huan gets to his feet, and he waits patiently while Kazuya gets to his feet...Dropkick to the knee by Huan! [crowd "ooohs"] Huan kip-ups to his feet, goes to the ropes, comes off...high kneedrop to Kazuya's leg! [crowd "oooohs"] AO: Security has Combattler II & Retsudo seperated, while Huan applies a leglock grapevine! Kazuya is screaming in pain! Combattler II attempts to enter the ring, but Retsudo intercepts him, and they fall through the ropes again! [crowd "oooohs"] Lady Mai Tai is on the apron, and Botan orders her to get down! Kazuya's leg will be broken if she doesn't stop distracting the referee! TK: Botan gets Mai Tai down, and he checks on Kazuya. He can't take much more of this...Botan asks him...Kazuya submits! **ding** **ding** **ding** [crowd applauds] Nishi: The winners of this match, as a result of a submission... THE YOUNG WARRIORS!!! [crowd applauds again] AO: I knew he had to give up. Kazuya's knees have always been a weak spot with him. [Security seperates Retsudo & Combattler II, and Retsudo enters the ring, and hoists his partner onto his shoulder. Combattler II enters the ring, and checks on Kazuya] TK: Kazuya gave a valiant effort, but it just wasn't enough. So we now have three teams advancing to round two of the tag team tournament. [The Young Warriors head to the back, slapping hands with the fans, while Combattler II helps Kazuya out of the ring, and he refuses to be placed on a stretcher. The crowd cheers as he limps to the back with the aid of Combattler II & Lady Mai Tai...] AO: I hope that Kazuya will be all right. TK: I'm sure he will Ono-sama. AO: That won't stop me from worrying though. TK: Steel Serpent and Kensai Tokugawa meet in our first Koubousen Geijutsu main event, Akira. AO: This division seems to grow stronger with each day Kikuchi-san. The resigning of Tokugawa, Steel Serpent, the Pyro Toshi Takeda, the Mandarin, and such new worthies as the gaijin Steve Houston, means that this is the premiere division in the world for this style of fight. What more could the wrestling public wish for? TK: Our fighters are being introduced, let's shift to the ring! ______ ___________________________________ /_ __/__ __ _____ ___ ___ _ (_) Koubousen Geijutsu Main Event / / / (_-</ // / _ \/ _ `/ ' \/ /Kensei Tokugawa vs Steel Serpent /_/ /___/\_,_/_//_/\_,_/_/_/_/_/________________________________/ [The camera focuses on the ring where Nishi Toju is calling out the introductions.] Nishi: Ladies and gentlemen, this match is adjudicated by the KOUBOUSEN GEIJUTSU division [CROWD POP]. Introducing first, from Bangkok, Thailand, weighing 240 pounds, the *FIRST* Battlecade Champion...here is STEEL SER-PENT!!! ["Unforgiven" by Metallica blares over the PA as Steel Serpent comes to the ring. He is a tall Asian man in black trunks, wearing a black mask with a metallic serpent on it. There is a medium crowd reaction to him] TK: What's this happening here? AO: It seems that the competition in the Battle Arts division is greater than I had anticipated! [The camera pans to the runway where Steel Serpent is walking to the ring. The Mandarin steps from the crowd, blocking his way. The crowd goes quiet as both men lock eyes. The Steel Serpent shakes his arms to loosen the muscles, while the Mandarin is completely still.] TK: Not another display like Super Tengu! He lost face by trying to dictate who he would and would not face in the ring, and Kobayashi-san taught him the error of his ways!! AO: I would hardly expect someone to try and emulate Tengu on that point. [SJPW security comes down as Steel Serpent and the Mandarin continue their staredown. The Mandarin lets himself be led away, still staring at the Steel Serpent. The Steel Serpent points a finger at him. The Mandarin takes a seat at ringside.] TK: An odd exchange between the Steel Serpent and the Mandarin, neh? AO: Somehow I think they were communicating, even if the rest of us are unaware. Nishi: His opponent, from Nagoya Japan [HUGE CROWD POP], representing The Daiymo, weighing in at 265 pounds, KEN-SAI TO-KU-GA-WA!!! ["Mr. Scary" by Dokken plays over the PA as Tokugawa comes to the ring wearing replica samurai armor and with a bo-ken in his obi. The crowd is solidly behind him, but he stares at Steel Serpent in the ring] *DING*DING* [As the bell sounds, Kensai Tokugawa wades into the ring, while Steel Serpent hangs back in his corner. As Tokugawa gets closer, Steel Serpent explodes with a jumping side kick to the shoulder, rocking Tokugawa. Serpent recovers and closes with a spinning backfist, which Tokugawa ducks under. Tokugawa makes a grab and stomps on Steel Serpent's foot, then hoists for a Belly to Belly suplex.] TK: An even fight here, as both Steel Serpent and Kensai Tokugawa land some important blows here in the early going! AO: This is home country for Tokugawa though, and that is a factor that the Serpent will have to overcome, along with the extra 10 kilos of power behind each blow! [Tokugawa gets to his feet rapidly and pulls the Steel Serpent up with him. he whips him to the ropes before laying him out with a textbook chokeslam. The crowd cheers wildly as he pulls the Steel Serpent up and throws him back to the ropes. On the rebound Tokugawa grabs him and plants him with a spinebuster.] TK: COVER! ...1.... ...2.... ...KICKOUT! [crowd applauds] AO: An instinctive response from the Steel Serpent, and one that saved him for the moment! [Tokugawa gets up and pulls the Steel Serpent up to him. He whips the Serpent to the ropes and runs to the opposite ropes. On the rebound he leaps and slams the Serpent with a Springboard Clothesline.] AO: The hour approaches for the Steel Serpent as Tokugawa continues his relentless assault with *another* belly to belly suplex! Tokugawa plays to his fans here in Nagoya as the Steel Serpent climbs to his feet. TK: It looks as though the Serpent may be cut, but it's hard to say. Tokugawa has some blood on him, but he hasn't been hurt. AO: Tokugawa is pumping up the crowd...not wise, considering that each second he takes here is one that might save the Steel Serpent. TK: Tokugawa heads to the second turnbuckle...Flying Double Axehandle...MISSED!! [Tokugawa leaps for the Axehandle, but the Steel Serpent sidesteps and smashes him with a roundhouse kick to the back! Tokugawa rolls to his feet, surprised, as the Steel Serpent wades in with an elbow smash to the back of his head as the crowd "ohh" 's.] TK: The Steel Serpent has taken damage from the likes of Demon-Boy Ishrinku... Tokugawa made a mistake by not talking a pin when he could! [The Steel Serpent grabs Kensai Tokugawa and knees him twice in the chest before sending him to the ropes. Tokugawa controls the rebound and collides heavily with the Serpent, sending both men to the outside.] AO: The action comes outside, and I wonder how long it will be before the fans get in on the fight! TK: I hope for their sake that it is a long time! [As Steel Serpent gets up, Kensai Tokugawa charges him, shoulderblocking him into the guardrail. The Serpent delivers a double axehandle to him as the guardrail catches him, and both fighters fall again.] TK: The Serpent seems to be recovering, even as the bout intensifies outside the ring! What endurance!! [The Steel Serpent is up first and hauls Kensai Tokugawa up, smashing him with punches to the face and chest. With each hit he walks Tokugawa backward, closer to Tokugawa's corner. The Serpent launches a kick to the thigh, knocking Tokugawa off balance and into his gear at ringside.] AO: The Steel Serpent has made another mistake by giving Tokugawa a weapon!! TK: I have to agree...he hasn't had much of an advantage this fight, and this is a lucky break for Tokugawa!! Tokugawa now has his bo-ken and SMASHES the Steel Serpent with it! [The Steel Serpent wobbles from being hit with the bo-ken, and Kensai Tokugawa pounds the butt end into his shoulder, dropping the Serpent. Tokugawa rolls the Serpent back into the ring.] AO: And finally, Tokugawa with the cover! ...1... ...2... ...KICKOUT BY STEEL SERPENT!!! [The crowd applauds] TK: The Steel Serpent is definitely showing his endurance here by managing to stay alive, and Kensai Tokugawa is frustrated and furious!! AO: His temper is renowned, Tiger, and I think the Steel Serpent will discover a new level of pain. [The Steel Serpent is on his feet for mere seconds before Kensai charges him. Incredibly, the Steel Serpent vaults Tokugawa, delivering a back-kick as he goes by. Tokugawa hits the turnbuckles with force, but charges back out completely enraged. The Steel Serpent clinches him and starts working over his abdomen and chest with vicious knee lifts and body punches. Tokugawa rips the Serpent off of him and throws him to the ropes, but the Serpent controls the rebound and shoulderblocks Tokugawa.] TK: Both men are on the mat again!! Tokugawa has definitely opened up the Steel Serpent, and despite the massive damage done to him by Tokugawa, the Serpent still hangs on! AO: He is a tenacious one, isn't he. Makes you sad that he is gaijin. [Both men get up again and Tokugawa charges into a haymaker by the Steel Serpent. The Serpent lands a crescent kick to the head of Tokugawa, and the crowd "ohh" 's as Tokugawa stumbles for the first time.] AO: Tokugawa has to be surprised by that sudden attack by the Serpent!! TK: He seems stunned!! The Steel Serpent steps in and HEADBUTT!! Tokugawa is definitely stunned here!! The Steel Serpent locks on the TORTURE GRIP and the crowd has quieted down as the momentum shifts... [The Steel Serpent locks his hand around Kensai Tokugawa's throat, pressing down hard on the carotid arteries (like a sleeperhold). Tokugawa likewise wraps his hands around the Steel Serpent's throat, trying to choke him out.] AO: Double choke there in the ring!! Both men are trying to choke the other out!! A complete test of endurance!! [The crowd is quiet as both the Steel Serpent and Tokugawa shuffle together in the ring, chokeholds set. They hold together for a few tense seconds, then Kensai Tokugawa's eyes flutter, and he releases his hold.] TK: The Steel Serpent HELD ON!! Tokugawa used every technique but the legendary endurance of the Steel Serpent sees him through a match where he was out-powered!! Let's get the official announcement!! Announcer: Ladies and gentlemen, your winner VIA KNOCKOUT... THE STEEL SERPENT!!! [The crowd claps politely for the Serpent, who stands in the middle of the ring. Then he strides over to a corner and points to someone in the crowd.] AO: He caught sight of the Mandarin again... [The Mandarin stands and invites the Steel Serpent to come into the crowd, while the Serpent points at him. Both men are locked in a staredown again.] AO: I wish that Security could keep better control of these gaijin ruffians!! TK: I think they are only making their wishes for a match known to Kobayashi-san. AO: Maybe so, but still, there is a time and place for these things! TK: Speaking of time, we are out of ours. For Akira Ono, I am Tiger Kikuchi. We will see you next week on Tsunami!
