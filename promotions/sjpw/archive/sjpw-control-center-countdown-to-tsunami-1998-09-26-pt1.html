For those of you who do not know us, we are both veterans in the Japanese wrestling scene, having competed in Old Japan, the UWA, and Kikuchi most recently competed in SJPW before it was temporarily closed down. He has since joined me here, and we will call play by play for SJPW's live show, now called TSUNAMI! It's a pleasure to have you here Kikuchi-san. Tiger Kikuchi: Thank you Ono-san. Together, you and I will do play by play, and it should prove to be an interesting experience. Along with Eddie Rose & Jonnie Adonis, who handle interviews, the SJPW broadcast team is now stronger than ever. Before we begin to discuss the return of SJPW, Akira will inform everyone of the events that led to SJPW closing down last April, and how it's return came about. AO: Thank you Kikuchi-san. Earlier this year, Shinsaku Yamashita, the CEO of SJPW, was fired because of questionable conduct. He was replaced by Jumbo Kobayashi, the current CEO, a man whose honor cannot be questioned. Yamashita took offense at this, and besides disrupting SJPW's activities with his lackey team of Kenji Ishida & Shinji Usami, filed a complaint with the board of the World Wrestling Grand Prix, who had taken over SJPW when the company went international. The WWGP sided with Yamashita, and ordered Kobayashi to step down. He refused, so the WWGP withdrew it's support of SJPW, and ordered all WWGP world titles to be vacated. Kobabayashi & the SJPW fought back in court, and after a protracted battle, SJPW has finally terminated all business dealings with the WWGP, with one exception: Shinsaku Yamashita has managed to acquire a managerial license, and Kenji Ishida & Shinji Usami are on the list of wrestlers in SJPW. What trouble they plan to cause, only time will tell.... TK: Thank you Ono-sama for that report. SJPW plans on airing it's weekly programming starting October 10th, 1998, with the debut of TSUNAMI, the show that replaces Ring Wars as it's main show. This show will take place at the Tokyo Dome, home of SJPW. A lineup of matches has all ready been determined, and we will look at the video screen behind us for the lineup... [Both men turn to face the screen, which still has the SJPW logo on it. The logo dissapears, replaced by a blank screen for a moment, which is then replaced with: SJPW Tsunami: Return to the Tokyo Dome! [Sunday, October 10th], Tokyo Dome, Tokyo, Japan SJPW Welterweight Title Tournament, Round One: Note: This is only *HALF* of round one. Super Tengu & Shinji Hamada have had a long-standing emnity, and Ace Kilcannon has been both men's enemy in the past, and was once even Shinji's tag team partner! Add in Akira Hashimoto, a man who reminds me very much of....well me, and my old partner Mitsunori Takizawa, a formidable man in his own right, and then the behemoth Onslaught, and you have an explosive match! We all ready have comments made by Super Tengu, a man you once dubbed the "icon" of SJPW. AO: That was a long time ago Kikuchi-sama. Tengu and I are no longer close, for he has proven that he lacks the honor his father had. It is a shame that he has fallen so low, yet Hamada-sama has risen above his personal demons. TK: At least we have all resolved our differences, ne? For now, let us show Super Tengu's press conferance, which caught our friend Eddie Rose totally by surprise... [both men turn to the screen...open camera in a packed media room. Eddie Rose sits up front with a huge grin on his face. The SJPW logo is well lit behind gim as he steps upto the podium to speak] Eddie Rose: Ladies and gentlemen I am Eddie Rose and I am here to announce the most exciting news to hit our sport in quite some time [pauses as he looks over the press corps, who see rather uninterested] I am proud to announce the RETURN of Super Japan Pro-Wrestling!! Reporter#1: We know Rose!! We got the Fax yesterday.. heck Gong already has SJPW on the front cover!! Eddie: They Do? Reporter#2: Get with it Rose!! We're here to find out WHO you got!! Eddie: Um well... Voice: [off stage] Silence your foolish blathering simpleton!! Do not insult my DEAR friend Edward insuch fashion!! Eddie: Oh no... not him... dear lord, NOT HIM!! [Suddenly "Battle Hymn of the Republic" blares out across the room and slowly, from the back a marching band and choir start thumping in] Choir: Oh, mine eyes have seen the glory of coming the Lord, his name i Super Tengu so please do not be bored!! He is an awesome stud with his incredibly large sword... His greatness marches on!!! Eddie: [in horror] Oh dear heaven no!! [Slowly as the band and choir march forward a man is seen crawling on his knees with a cross across his back. Stage blood is paintedacross his face and arms as he drags a cross with the letters "SJPW" on his back] Choir: Glory, glory Halleujah!! Glory, Glory Halleujah... Glory Glory Halleujah... he has a real big sword!! [behind the man bearing the cross is Super Tengu, with mask on but dressed as a Roman Centurion. He cracks a whip over the man's head and laughs] Super Tengu: FORWARD TO THE STAGE!! I MUST GREET MY PUBLIC!! [Tengu continues to crack the whip over the man's head as he approaches the stage. He quickly kicks the cross of the man's back and props it against the podium] Super Tengu: Now You!! [points to the man] Go pick up your check from my secretary!! [turns to the audience] SJPW HAS RETURNED!! WITHOUT TENGU THERE IS NO SJPW!! WITHOUT TENGU THERE IS NO TRUE PURORESU!! THUS TENGU RETURNS TO YOU MY HUDDLED MASSES!! Eddie: [frantic] NO!! NYET!! NEIN!! NO!! No Tengu!! Super Tengu is NOT!! I REPEAT NOT a part of the SJPW!! [Tengu reaches in his tights and produces a a folded piece of paper and smiles a triumphant smile] Super Tengu: Ah dear Edward!! But Tengu IS a part of SJPW!! If you would please read this wonderful contract the front office sent me you'll see... SJPW is my DOMAIN once again!! [points to the contract and then turns to the cameras and reporters] Yes!! It is true "Puroresu no Kamisama" has indeed returned!! Let those who revel in sin and debauchery... take a seat for you are all but poseurs in the shadow of the true master of all things carnal!! Let all those who claim to be "Wrestlers" step forward so they can feel my MIGHTY HAND [raises his open left hand] as it STRIKES [slams his hand against the podium] them down like Davd smiting the mighty Goliath!! (Suddenly, Jerry Jones steps out of the same door the Outlaws used... he has a black eye! And a whole host of security guards and offensive linemen, which he's pointing right at the Outlaws!) Doc: Time ta skidaddle! SHOTGUN! Brent: DAGNAB IT! AH AIN'T A-DRIVIN'! (The Outlaws both run for the passenger side of the van... Doc gets there first, and sits in the seat, prompting Brent to just shove him over to the driver's side. Doc manages to pull out JUST in time, and all the people chasing them take to their cars and follow in hot pursuit! Meantime, back at the press conference... Tex is fielding questions.) Tex: Thet's right, ah done signed mah boys to a long-term deal with Supah Japan Pro Wrasslin'. Reporter #1: Even though they compete in two federations state-side? That sounds like a rough travel schedule! Tex: Wall, they only wrassle once in a blue moon as it is! In UEW, they ain't had but two title defenses since winnin' the gold thar, an' in th' IWC, they fight once ever' two weeks tops. An whenevah they's a lull inna schedule, they lak ta take tours in Japan anyway, on account of th' compy-tishin is so good. Not ta mention th' cash! So... it was jus' good, soun' biz-ness. Reporter #2: How do The Outlaws stack up against the other teams in SJPW? Certainly, the competition there is as good as any they've faced. What teams do you foresee as possible threats? Tex: Now, ah ain't at liberty ta discuss much straty-gy or none o' thet! Only team we's a-gonna focus on is th' Tokyo Express... on account of thet's who we's knucklin' up with first, on Octobah 10! Reporter #3: Where ARE The Outlaws, anyway?! Tex: Uh... thet's a good question... but ah sent them out ta train! Yep, thet's right... jus' goes ta show how serious we is 'bout alla our compy-tishin, thet we's in trainin' right outta th' gate... Voice: COMIN' THRU! Not far behind them is a group of police, security guards, angry football players, and a little old lady they cut off in traffic! The Outlaws bolt right through the assembly of reporters, sending reporters, pads, chairs and helmets flying! Their pursuers follow, slowed up by the cluttered area... Brent and Doc slip through a side door, and into the bowels of the building. The police, guards, linemen, and lady all follow! Tex holds his hat on, and looks around the mess that was made of the room in mere seconds... the reporters all look at him, as if for an answer...) Tex: Um, in fact, their trainin'... it's part o' Tex James' new fitness program! EVER'BODY'S doin' it! We're keepin' th' Outlaws, th' Dallas Cowboys, th' Police Department, an' th' AARP in shape! But thet's all th' time we got fer taday, an make sure y'all tune inta SJPW's firs' card back on Octobah 10. Th' Outlaws'll be thar... y'all be thar too! (Tex hurriedly waddles off... the scene changes (and time passes)... SCENE: A small, dark office, somewhere on the upper floors of the building. A typical desk and typical chairs are the only things in this typical office... the lights in this room are out, but the window leading to the hallway outside shows the light is on out there. All is silent... Voice of Brent Maverick: Ya think we ditched 'em? Voice of Doc Holliday: They won't NEVAH look in heah fer us... ah put a sign on th' door thet said "New Tofu Product Taste Testing Office", so won't nobody come in heah. In Pro Japan I was rushed into the spotlight.. Here in SJPW I will earn it... [Removes his sunglasses] Fujiwara: Jensei Tenru our paths will cross at the Tokyo Dome in the first round of the Super Welterweight Title Tournament. I can't guarentee a win but I can guarentee my best effort and a clean match.. at least on my part. [scratches his chin] Fujiwara: Tenru-san the word in the locker room is that you and your adviser have been tempted before to bend the rules that govern our sport... I'd advise that on the 17th of October you and Mr. Matsuoko walk the fine line between righteousness and wickedness in the interest of the fans, AND for the sake of your well being... [Fujiwara puts his sunglasses back on and walks out of the picture as the shot cuts to show the Golden Lion Mask floating on the water. The screen fades out, and Kikuchi turns to face the camera] TK: Shishi Fujiwara feels he has alot to prove to the fans. His opponent, Jensei Tenru, on the other hand, does not. The protege of Hakiro Matsuoko, a former member of Syndicate Japan, and IIWF superstar, has come to SJPW to prove his superiority. We recently caught the two of them as they arrived at the airport... [Kikuchi turns to face the screen, which opens on The international airport in Tokyo, Japan. A flight is arriving from the United States at Gate Seven. A multitude of people start to come out of the gate. Two men stand out among them and once they come into the shot, the mix of Kodo drum music and the insanity of Sepultura combine for "Kaimatafhi" slowly fills the audio. The first man is dressed in an all white suit and has his hair pulled back. It is Hakiro Matsuoko. His companion is dressed in an all black three piece suit, his hair flowing down to his shoulders while wearing sun glasses. They walk slowly to the customs guard. Guard: Welcome back, Mister Matsuoko. [Matsuoko nods] May I see your passports? HM: Of course, here they... [Hakiro is interrupted by his companion] Man: Passports? The name is Jensei Tenru. I am the heir to the Matsuoko throne. You can actually call yourself Japanese and _NOT_ know who I am? I mean, come on... I know that you dont have much of a life outside of this job but you must be a wrestling fan and therefore, you must know who I am. [The guard smirks] Guard: I see. Your pupil is very confident. HM: Yes...he is. JT: Why shouldnt I be? I am the number one commodity in the business today. The most innovative, athletic and awe inspiring wrestler in the history of creation. HM: Jensei, you must be humble. Have I taught you nothing? JT: Look, I thought that there was going to be a press conference or something. I mean, the biggest story of the year. Jensei Tenru, the worlds greatest self made superstar comes home to Super Japan from gaining world status in America. The son and protege of Hakiro Matsuoko. Come on, this is sad. HM: -- JT: The only thing that will top this story will be the fact that I am going walk through my foes to the Welterwieght Title. Super Japan has some real talent to be sure. [Jensei breaks sentance and sees a small family reunion happening beside their party. A young man is taking photos of his reuntied family. Just as the picture is taken, Jensei darts into the shot and has his picture taken with the family. They looked shocked and stand in amazement. I knew my fans would get the word out. [Matsuoko shakes his head.] Japan, I AM HOME! The student of the best...well, that really doesnt matter, I would have made it to the top anyway, I mean, I have God given talent! Come on... [Tenru looks himself over] really, I AM IT!
