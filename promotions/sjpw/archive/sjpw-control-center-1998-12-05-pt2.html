Control Center, Part Two

5 December 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Control Center continues...] [The camera opens on the broadcast position. Jumbo Kobayashi is sitting next to Kikuchi...] TK: Welcome back everyone. With us now is CEO Jumbo Kobayashi, who has an important announcement to make. Jumbo? Jumbo Kobayashi: Domo, Kikuchi-sama. As you know, The Tengu-Tenru match was pulled from the tournament when it became a three way match. Tengu is injured now, and Jensei Tenru wants to go after Jinsei Tenru, so they are out of the tournament as well. Therefore, tonight, we will fill that last bracket with two other men. AO: Who will that be? JK: First is Ed Dean. TK: Ed Dean? But he went to SHVF! JK: Yes, he did. However, he was still contractually obligagted to SJPW for at least the end of the year. He has reluctantly agreed to return to wrestle tonight. AO: Who is his opponent? JK: His opponent is none other than.....MITSUNORI TAKIZAWA!!! [crowd goes crazy when they realize that Dean will face his former tag team partner] TK: Takizawa? But he has a match tomorrow against Takada! JK: I realize that. However, Takizawa-san is the only man qualified to take this slot. He wasn't in the tournament at all, and as Chris K & Kyle Lee have left the promotion, I had no other choice. AO: This is a major announcement! Takizawa will fight two hard matches in two nights! Even I can't say that he can handle that! JK: I regret putting him in this position, but he is the best choice to face Dean... TK: Thank you Kobayashi-sama! [Jumbo leaves the broadcast position] AO: I can't believe this! TK: It does seem a bit unfair... AO: A bit? Takizawa may be tough, but he is not invincible! He can't face Dean tonight, then challenge Takada tomorrow night! He will risk serious injury! Dean HATES him! Dean will try and take him out! TK: Very true Ono-sama. However. Kobayashi-sama has made his choice. We have a real treat for the fans next. Shinji Hamada & Greg "Millionaire" Miller have both been inactive of late in SJPW. That is because both men are competing in the Twin Cities Invitational, an internation tournament of some of the best wrestlers on the planet! AO: Hamada-san should win it then! TK: Very funny... AO: I am not joking. The way he has matured over the past year, he is truly one of the greatest wrestlers Nippon has ever produced! TK [eyeing Ono skeptically]: Be that as it may, the tournament has only finished it's first round, and with the kind permission of the TCI promoters, we have permission to show you the taped matches both men faced in. First up, Shinji Hamada... [The camera pans to the Jumbotron...] ____ _____ _ _____________________________________________ / __/_ / / _ \ | /| / /Twin Cities Invitational Tournament, RD 1 / _\ \/ // / ___/ |/ |/ /Shinji Hamada vs "The Legend Sean Darling /___/\___/_/ |__/|__/__________________________________________/ Len Larsen: Ladies and gentlemen, the following match is scheduled for one fall... introducing first, from Osaka, Japan, weighing 235 pounds, representing Super Japan Pro Wrestling.... SHINJI HAMADA!!!!!! ["Phaedra" by Tangerine Dream plays as the focused but friendly Hamada makes his way down the aisle to a rousing cheer, slapping hands with the fans along the way.] Ryan Anderson: Apparently we have quite a few SJPW fans in attendance tonight, Lar... they love this guy! Larry Franken: I've always said Minneapolis wrestling fans watch waaaaaay too much wrestling! They know everything for crying out loud! RA: Unlike you, who knows nothing. LF: HEY! LL: And his opponent, weighing 240 pounds, representing POW.... "THE LEGEND" SEAN DARRING!!!! [Big heel pop as Darring makes his way to the ring, getting into it with the fans along the way.] LF: Now this guy I like! RA: Big surprise... both men are in the ring, the ref has checked them over... there's the bell!!!! Hamada and Darring lock up..... go behind by Darring into an armbar... back elbow to the head by Hamada!!!! And another!!! Darring breaks the hold... Hamada spins, grabs Darring, whips him into the ropes.... drop toe hold!!!! Hamada follows up with an elbow drop to the back of Darring's head! LF: Hey Andj, since this is such a special occasion tonight, I'm only drinking imported beer during the broadcast! RA: That's real good of you, Lar. I'm sure the fans appreciate your sacrifice. Hamada picks Darring up..... DARRING WITH THE THUMB TO THE EYE!!! LF: One of The Neck's favourite manouvers! RA: Darring with some big chops to the chest! Crowd: WHOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO!!!! RA: Irish whip by Darring..... BELLY TO BELLY SUPLEX!!!!! And a cover! 1......... RA: Only a one count. Darring picks Hamada up off the canvas, locks him up for a double underhook DD.... BACKBODY DROP BY HAMADA!!! Shinji Hamada on the offensive now, picks Darring up.... gutwrench suplex into a Doctor Bomb!!!!! LF: I felt that all the way down here, Andj. RA: Hamada is heading up to the top turnbuckle.... ASAI MOONSAULT!!!!!! LF: He nailed it! RA: But he doesn't go for the cover! He's signalling to the crowd... I think he's calling for the Tiger Driver 2000! He picks Darring up, hoists him up overhead into a Gorilla Press........ TOMBSTONE PILEDRIVER!!!! LF: That's the Tiger Driver 2000??? All I can say Andj is... OUCH! RA: Hamada covers! 1.......... 2.......... 3!!!!!!!!! <DING DING> RA: Let's go to Len Larson for the official decision. LL: In a time of 4:43, the winner of this match.... SHINJI HAMADA!!!!!! RA: Hamada totally dominated Sean Darring, but I predict a tougher go of it in Round 2 when he meets veteran "Tiger" Tong Po! LF: Yeah, it'll be a good match, if you're into all the technical wrestling crap. [The camera pans back to the broadcast position as the jumbotron fades...] TK: What an exciting match! AO: Hamada clearly dominated his opponent. That man had no right being in the ring with him! TK: Ono-san, you are "too much!" AO [smiling]: That I am. That I am. TK: Before we show you the Greg Miller match, we are going to cut to Eddie Rose in the ring, where he is going to conduct an interview with one of the finalists in our Welterweight tournament. Take it away Eddie! *The camera pans to Eddie Rose, who is standing in the middle of the ring, a microphone in his hand...* Eddie Rose: Minna-san, please join me in welcoming at this time, one of the men who will be competing for the SJPW Welterweight title at Sensou no Jidai... TASOGARE NO PRINCE!!!!!!!! *The lights in the arena dim, as orange, pink and purple spotlights come on, giving the inside of the jam-packed arena the appearance of the sky at Twilight. Calm, soothing, yet somehow sombre cello music fills the auditorium, as Tasogare no Prince walks through the curtains leading to the locker room area, and begins walking slowly towards the ring. In his hand is a white orchid, which he smells as he walks to the ring. His eyes seem to be almost closed, and there is a slight, knowing smile upon his face. He climbs the steps to the ring, and steps through the top and middle ropes. The music fades and the house lights come back on as he heads towards the center of the ring and stands next to the man already in the ring...* ER: Welcome Tasogare no Prince. TnP: Arigatoo, Rose-san. *he gives a slight bow...* ER: Prince, tomorrow on Sensou no Jidai, you will face "The Warbird" Yukio Murakami for the SJPW Welterweight title in the main event!! Can you tell me, and all the fans here, what are your feelings about being a part of the headlining match on the first SJPW Supercard since it's rebirth? TnP: Obviously, I am quite honored. I have worked very long and hard for this opportunity. For all the effort and time I put into my career in America, I was never given the chance to headline an event of such magnitude. Now that I have earned just such a chance, I will not waste it. ER: And what of your opponent in this match? Yukio Murakami? *The Prince smiles and smells the white orchid in his hand...* TnP: Ah, yes. I am once again honored to be able to pit myself against such a worthy competitor. I am truly looking forward to it. I have been watching Murakami-san with some interest since my debut here in SJPW. And although I have been impressed with what I have seen, and I am aware that I will be in for a monumental battle tomorrow evening, I am more than prepared for "The Warbird." ER: So, are you saying that you will become the SJPW Welterweight champion? TnP: Of course. After all, it is my destiny to do so. *He pauses and smiles, smelling the white orchid in his hand...* ER: If you don't mind my saying so, that could easily be mistaken for overconfidence... TnP: Soo? Ah, in that case perhaps I should elaborate as to the reason why, ne? Murakami-san, it seems that you spend a great deal of time speaking of the past. Of Samurai warriors and Kamikaze pilots. All of these things are history. And while there is a great deal we can, and should, learn from the past, a man who looks only to the past cannot see his destiny before him. Sensou no Jidai. The War of Generations. Murakami-san, it is a fact that the two of us entered into the SJPW at the same time, but we are not of the same generation. You are a man from, and dwelling in, the past. You represent the old ways. The old ways of thinking, and of wrestling. I, on the other hand, represent the future of SJPW. A future that _will_ come to pass, for that is destiny. Mine, and the SJPW's. Kore wa sakete toore me, anata no unmei. You cannot avoid your destiny. Yukio Murakami, tomorrow night, at Sensou no Jidai, it will be your destiny to enter into the Twilight, and you, and the entire SJPW, will leave it forever changed... *Tasogare no Prince hands the microphone back to Eddie Rose, as the cello music once more fills the arena and the spotlights come on, again giving the arena's interior the appearance of Twilight. The Prince exits the ring, and goes over to a young girl standing at ringside and presents her with the orchid he has been holding. She smiles and clutches the flower to her chest, as Tasogare no Prince walks down the aisle and exits the arena...* AO: That man is very strange... TK: He feels he has a Manifest Destiny, and the Welterweight title is part of it! AO: In some ways, he reminds me of Super Tengu. Both of them feel the old ways are of the past, and that a new line of thinking are in order. It worked for Tengu; it might just work for Tasogare no Prince! TK: Is that a prediction? AO: Maybe. Normally I would go with Murakami, but he *has* been injured slightly, thanks to Greg Miller... TK: Speaking of Miller, he is in our second TCI showcase... AO: Just what I need! To see that obnoxious, overbearing, filthy rich gaijin... He climbs into the ring, takes off his Trevor Hartman shirt and tosses it out into the crowd.] LL: And his opponent, from Sunset Blvd., California, weighing 220 pounds, here is... GREG "THE MILLIONAIRE" MILLER!!!! [C.R.E.A.M. by the Wu Tang Clan starts to play, and Greg Miller walks out from backstage, and he has a look of determination on his face. He receives boos from many of the fans in the arena, but he pays no attention to them at all. The lighting stays the same, and as he approaches the ramp, he stops. Fireworks go off on either side of him, and then he looks around, and looks at Hartman, and starts to walk towards him. He takes his time going to the ring, never taking his eyes off his opponent at any point in time.] RA: Miller is putting the bad mouth on Trevor Hartman, Lar.! LF: He's insulting his women!!! I like this guy! RA: You would! Miller is really getting in the face of Hartman, saying all kinds of things that can't be repeated... Hartman takes a swing at him, Miller ducks under and kicks him in the midsection!!! LF: Hartman lost his temper and it cost him... GOOD! RA: Miller with a couple of big chops! Now an Irish whip into the ropes..... knee to the midsection and Hartman does a midair 360 and hits the canvas! LF: Hey, Hartman's an acrobat! RA: Stop it! Miller follows up with a legdrop across the throat and covers! 1........ RA: Just a one... Miller hauls Hartman to his feet.... SMALL PACKAGE!!! 1........ 2........ RA: NO!!! Miller almost got caught sleeping there, but it's only a two count. Both men getting to their feet... Miller swings wildly at Hartman, Hartman ducks under, locks him up for a belly to belly... LF: NICE BLOCK!!! RA: Miller wrapped his leg around Hartman, blocking the suplex attempt!!!!! Miller drops to his knees...... HEADBUTT TO THE GROIN!!!!! LF: Oooooh, that's gotta hurt! RA: The official is warning Greg Miller about the lowblow, but Miller ignores him and picks Trevor up off the canvas. He hoists him up into the air.... FISHERMAN'S BUSTER!!!! A COVER!!!! 1........ 2........ HARTMAN KICKS OUT!!!! LF: HOW?!?!?! RA: Will power and determination? LF: Nah, that can't be it. RA: Miller getting a little frustrated, but he stays on his opponent, hauling him to his feet by the hair. HARTMAN WITH THE ELBOW... MILLER DUCKS, GRABS THE ARM.... FUJIWARA ARMBAR!!!!!!! LF: Submit, you pretty boy punk! Submit!!!! RA: The ref is checking on Hartman, but Hartman is not giving in! He's inching his way towards the bottom rope as Miller really cranks on his arm!!!! And....... he............ REACHES THE ROPE!!!!! LF: DAMNIT!!! RA: Greg Miller has to release the arm bar, but the damage has been done. Hartman slowly gets to his feet, but Greg Miller is right there to meet him. Irish whip by Miller..... VICTORY ROLL BY HARTMAN!!!!! 1........ 2........ KICKOUT!!!! LF: THAT WAS TOO DAMN CLOSE!!!! RA: Hartman almost got the duke!!!! Both men slowly struggle to their feet.... MILLER WITH THE KICK TO THE GUT!!!! Hartman doubles over....... ROCKER DROPPER BY MILLER!!!!!!!! LF: Hey, DT lost... Glitter lost... I need a new best friend with a whole lotta cash! [The camera pans back to Kikuchi & Ono as the Jumbotron fades...] TK: Miller looked very impressive. AO: Of course he did! He's been competing here, with the highest quality of competition! Hartman hasn't, and that is why he lost! TK: You won't give Miller any credit, will you? AO: Never. Not even if he paid me! TK: We must take another break, then we will return with more action!
