[Camera opens on Mr. Big standing in front of an SJPW banner, the
WWGP World Heavyweight Title over his shoulder. He looks right into the camera, and prepares to speak...]
Mr. Big: I'm not usually the type of person to say I told you so but, Lance Smith old buddy, I TOLD YOU SO. There was never any doubt in my mind that I would win the championship belt. I told you that I would take your belt, why didn't you believe me? Well, now it's too late. I must give you some credit however. You are my toughest opponent to date. I do hope you don't get too upset over me taking the title I deserved from you. After all, now that you are just a challenger, maybe now you'll give some top heavyweights a chance against you. Then again, HA HA HA, maybe not. The one time you did, you lost, TO ME. Maybe that's why you never took on anyone like me or Nik Nyce until you knew you had to. You knew you would lose. Hopefully now you've learned to be a little more humble. But it doesn't matter. After all, I am a man that keeps my word. And I promised Nik Nyce that I would give him the first shot at me after I won the title, and I am going to.
Nik, just name the time and place, I'll be there with the title. You see, I don't avoid the top competition like Lance Smith did. The difference between me and Smith is that I won't lose the first time I take on the someone in the top five heavyweight contenders.
Nik, your a great wrestler, I'm not denying it. I think you could give me a great match. I respect your talent. However, I just won the world title and I don't plan on giving it up right away. And if things go the way I plan, I won't give it up either. Count on it.

I was successfull for quite some time here in SJPW, winning nearly all of my matches. Then I came upon my biggest test,
Jet Jaguar. He defeated me, and I couldn't believe it, I was shocked. I presumed i was invincable. I challenged him, and lost again... and that's when I lost it. I was depressed. I felt like
I failed. I hit rock bottom, and i looked for a quick fix, and that's where the alochol and narcotics came into the picture. I'd use them whenever i wasn't feeling good... which was pretty much all of the time. Sure, it helped early, but as soon as the "high"
began to get lower and lower, I became more depressed and more depressed. I contemplated suicide many times, sticking a gun in my mouth and thinking " Let's end this pain...". Luckily, I didn't do it. But that wasn't it. I continued wrestling in SJPW. None of the athelete's knew what was wrong with me, neither did the officials. I had one more matchup with Jaguar, and that's when the heart attack occured. Jet Jaguar, being the true friend that he was, didn't care about the matchup any longer. He rushed me out of there, and to the back, not wasting any time to finish the matchup. I was then taken to the hospital, and treated for a mild heart attack, which could have been fatal if action had not been taken so soon.

I didn't need any help. When Jaguar saved me, he really helped me.
Not only did he take me from that matchup, but he got me involved with a few drug rehab programs, as well as being there for me when
I needed someone to talk to. He's a great friend.
JA: Takao, there have been many rumors as far as you joining Tiger
Kikuchi's "Team Tiger". Is there any truth behind these rumors?
TN: Somewhat. Jet Jaguar is attempting to get me a spot in Team
Tiger, but i believe that i have to earn my spot, prove to those great athelete's that i am worthy to join that stable, prove to them that I am a changed man. I hope to start off proving that to them tonight, when i face El Gato Diablo.
JA: Speaking of El Gato Diable, what do you think of him? And what do you think your chances of success are against him?
TN: Well, to be truthfull, i really haven't seen this guy in action much, if at all. But if he's here competing in the SJPW, he's got to be a great wrestler. As far as my chances of victory against him go, i'm just going to give it my best, 110%, and hope that's enough to win the matchup.
