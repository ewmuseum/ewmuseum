The History of Super Tengu: The Feud Part 1 Part 1 – Opening Shots Even before Super Tengu debuted on “Ring Wars” I had people asking [name withheld] if they could work a program with me. I contribute this to my early flashes, which were so off the wall they made Tengu seem like a super cool anti-hero. I had Tengu visiting children’s hospitals to show them a picture of his bludgeoned dog, Tengu in “Toys’R’Us” showing kids pornography (that flash caught me more flak than any other, Gerry told me I had to tone it down or else people were going to leave en masse). Gerry put me in touch with [name withheld], the handler of Shinji “LIVE” Hamada and together we cooked up an angle involving Tengu accusing Hamada of paying off other wrestlers “to take a dive”. No the irony of “taking a dive” in a fixed sport was not lost on Scott nor myself. First we had to get some face heat for Hamada. That was easy enough as Scott was already a damn good flash writer: ------------------------------------------------------------------------- From the 4/28/97 “SJPW Control Center” Posted to RSPW/SJPW ML 4/27/97 Excerpt of Shinji “LIVE” Hamada Interview Written by [name withheld] Shinji Hamada: Super Tengu, you are a disgrace to yourself, SJPW, and to the country of Japan. You and I most definitely WILL face each other Super Tengu, and I promise the fans that YOU will learn humility at the hands of Shinji Hamada. To know you, Super Tengu, is to despise you and to despite you is only natural. [Pause] The fans of SJPW need a champion they can respect and look up to, and I made a vow that they WOULD get that champion. Super Tengu, I promise you this... you will NEVER represent SJPW as it's champion. Right now, though, I'm asking Shinya Sasaki to form a tag team with me to face YOU, Super Tengu and a partner of your choice... IF you can find someone low enough to form an alliance with a worm such as yourself! ------------------------------------------------------------------------- The beauty of this angle was that it was taking off during the All Asian Cruiserweight Title Tournament. The idea was to build up the heat between Hamada and Tengu until the tourney finals. Gerry made us no promises that we’d be in the finals but did promise a match at the first SJPW Pay-Per-View, Battlecade, between Tengu and Hamada. Round 1 of the Tournament saw Tengu face Masa Kokura. ------------------------------------------------------------------------- From the 5/10/97 edition of “SJPW Ring Wars” Posted RSPWF/SJPW ML 5/12/97 Excerpt of Super Tengu v. Ring Announcer: Your winner, SUPER TENGU!!!!!!!!!!! (Fans applaud, and throw streamers into the ring. Tengu gets up on one corner, and holds his arms up to the crowd) ------------------------------------------------------------------------- With Tengu advancing we had to make Shinji Hamada seem like the guy who might be able to beat Tengu. Of course a new wrinkle was added to this equation as Hamada’s former tag team partner Ace Killcannon arrived in Super Japan wanting to settle some old scores. Thus the Hamada/Tengu feud added Ace Killcannon and a little bit of Sports Entertainment as Ace attempted burning down the Dojo of Shinji Hamada’s sensei, with the sensei still inside. Was it wacky? You bet it was but it gave us a great new dynamic by having Hamada as the uber-babyface determined to get revenge on Ace and stop the ultra-over Super Tengu. [name withheld] did a great job of getting Hamada over in his flashes. One of the key elements was that in every interview Hamada always would say that he would make it to the Cruiserweight Tournament finals just so he could stop Tengu from winning the title. Of course Hamada had to get past the first round and the challenge of Takao Nakami: ------------------------------------------------------------------------- From the 5/10/97 edition of “SJPW Ring Wars” Posted to RSPWF/SJPW ML on 5/12/97 Excerpt of Takao Nakami v. Streamers hit the ring as his theme music starts to play) ------------------------------------------------------------------------- So with Round 1 of the tournament down Tengu and Hamada are both a step closer to the finals. Trouble looms on the horizon though as Ace Killcannon also entered the tournament and beat Shinya Sasaki. Ace and Hamada were in the same bracket and fated to meet on the 5/17/97 Ring Wars show. Neither man was ready for the bizarre set of circumstances the unfolded... ------------------------------------------------------------------------- From the 5/17/97 edition of “SJPW” Ring Wars” Posted 5/18/97 to RPWF/SJPW ML Excerpt from Ace Killcannon v. I have found the yen you used to pay off Quiltcannon! ER: Where is he? What is he talking about? AO: There! (The camera shows a spotlight where Ono points to. Your winner, SHINJI "LIVE" HAMADA!!!! ------------------------------------------------------------------------- Shinji Hamada got revenge for his sensei but could he have done it without Super Tengu’s help? As for Ace Killcannon, his memory is long. He will come back for revenge against Tengu. The end to the cruiserweight title is on the horizon but Tengu and Hamada meet before then in tag team action… ------------------------------------------------------------------------- End Notes: It was amazing what Scott, Gerry and me were doing at this time. While SJPW had a healthy heavyweight roster, the cruisers were getting all the hype. It's not like we had bad handlers in the Heavyweight division, far frim it. Some the guys who were in sJPW were guys like [name withheld] (Akira Hashimoto), [name withheld] (Ed Dean) and well established characters like Patty Babes Carter, Dan "Flash" Kaufmann and Survival. But the angle that got the play with a lot of guys was the Hamada/Tengu/Killcannon stuff. I even got people complimenting Tengu in character during slashes. It was weird. I'd only been in e-wrestling for 6 months and Gerry, well it was his first time running a fed. Scott seemed to be the old hand at this stuff but even he was suprised at how our little angle had taken center stage. Almost everyone wanted a piece of Tengu and I got several offers from other feds to bring hi mi. I turned all those offers down, I kept Tengu exclusive to SJPW (save one cross promotional tour of Hardcore Championship Wrestling, THE HCW, the one that had Survival and Flash Flood and tons of really great roleplayers). Me and Scott were starting to hit creative highs with Hamada/Tengu and it was only getting better. ------------------------------------------------------------------------- Next Installment: Tengu teams with Takao Nakami against Hamada and Rhiyoto Makada. “Too Sexy” Lance Smith meets Tengu in the semi-finals of the cruiserweight tourney and… BATTLECADE!
