SJPW Card Archive

Metro, 2/3

20 October 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
ER: Our next match is another Welterweight tournament match, as Tasogare no Prince takes on Xiamen in a second round match. I like Tasogare no Prince. He's an excellant competitor, and an honorable man to boot. JA: I like Xiamen. This guy's unorthodox, but he has conviction. This should be an excellant match! __ ___ _____________________________________________________ / |/ /__ / /________ Welterweight Tournament, Round Two:/ / /|_/ / -_) __/ __/ _ \ Xiamen vs Tasogare no Prince /_/ /_/\__/\__/_/ \___/_______________________________________/ [The scene changes to the ring where Xiamen and Tasogare no Prince stand facing each other, Grandmaster Xu standing at ringside in Xiamen’s corner.] JA: This will definitely be a very interesting match as Xiamen is highly conventional in his wrestling style and has his mentor on hand to provide him with advice and support. ER: Yes but Tasogare no Prince will have what will be an unusual advantage for him in terms of size and weight... Xiamen’s practically a waif! JA: Yes, he’s much smaller than most of the SJPW roster but he more than makes up what he lacks in raw power with unconventional ability. ER: Attempted collar-and-elbow lockup after the bell rings but Xiamen ducks out of it and runs into the ropes. He rebounds back and... HANDSPRING BODYBLOCK takes Tasogare no Prince down! [crowd "Ooohhhs" then applauds] JA: He’s quite the gymnast and his mobility is his greatest asset. But if Tasogare no Prince can slow him down, Xiamen might very well be in trouble. He has the advantage for the moment as he brings the Prince to his feet and whips him into the turnbuckle... NO! Tasogare no Prince reverses it and it’s Xiamen who’s back goes crashing into the corner! ER: The Prince demonstrating his strength advantage as he charges shoulder-first into the corner and drives into Xiamen’s stomach, knocking the wind out of him. The Prince backs out of the corner and attempts a jumping side kick but Xiamen ducks out of the way. JA: Xiamen grabs The Prince and flings him into the corner, hops up and... monkey flip hurls The Prince out into the center of the mat! [crowd applauds] ER: The Prince back up on his feet quickly but you can tell he’s learning not to underestimate Xiamen. Xiamen steps forward and grabs The Prince’s arm, sending him into the ropes with an Irish whip... no, reversal by Tasogare no Prince. Xiamen comes rebounding back... oh! Back elbow to the jaw and Xiamen goes down hard. JA: Tasogare no Prince keeping up the pressure as he pulls Xiamen up and rocks him back on his heels with a short-arm clothesline. He then whips him into the ropes... HURRICANRANA by Tasogare no Prince! [crowd "Ooohhhs"] ER: The Prince hooks the leg... ...1... ...2... ...shoulder up!! [crowd applauds] JA: A lot of hard-hitting action early on in this match as both men exchange a series of impressive moves. This contest may come down to which man can endure the worst punishment. ER: You’re right and right now, it’s Tasogare no Prince with the definite advantage as he pulls Xiamen to his feet. And I think he’s going for his Twilight Suplex finisher as he sends Xiamen into the ropes with an Irish whip and then performs a springboard moonsault over top of him as Xiamen bounces back... oh! The Prince landed on his feet behind Xiamen to execute a German Suplex to finish the move but Xiamen ducked out of it and rolled out of the way! JA: He came very close to losing this match for if The Prince had been able to hit his Twilight Suplex, it would have been the end for Xiamen! ER: Xiamen comes back with an incredible move he calls "The 8-pin" as he throws his body in a rolling fashion at Tasogare no Prince’s legs, knocking him down. Tasogare no Prince lunges to his feet quickly but Xiamen executes his "Humbler" submission hold, a variation of a sleeperhold where Xiamen has hopped up onto The Prince's back and wrapped his legs around his chest, crushing together to constrict his breathing. JA: This is another example of how innovative and unusual Xiamen can be. His arsenal consists of highly unusual moves and it can be difficult to counter them. ER: Tasogare no Prince is in trouble here as he’s beginning to fade... oh! What a brilliant counter as The Prince simply jumped up into the air and came slamming down onto his back, crushing Xiamen between him and the canvas! JA: Very smart thinking by The Twilight Prince as Xiamen’s Humbler is effectively broken. Both men a little slow in getting to their feet as this match is exacting a toll on the two of them. Tasogare no Prince runs towards the ropes as Xiamen struggles to his feet... SPRINGBOARD DROPKICK! [crowd "Ooohhhs" then applauds] ER: Xiamen goes down hard and Tasogare no Prince makes another pin attempt... ...1... ...2... ...kickout!! [crowd appaluds] JA: So far, Tasogare no Prince is the only one to get near falls in this match. But Xiamen is taking him to task as neither man has been clearly superior to the other. ER: The true winners so far are the fans as this has turned out to be an excellent match thus far. Tasogare no Prince climbing up onto the top turnbuckle as Xiamen is still down on the mat. He launches himself into the air... 450 SPLASH! JA: He missed! Xiamen rolled out of the way at the last second!! [crowd "Ohhs"] ER: The Prince in real trouble as he came down hard right onto his front. Xiamen not in much better shape as he’s taken a bit of a beating himself. He grabs one of The Prince’s legs... SPINNING TOE HOLD! Tasogare no Prince is squirming and trying to break out of this painful hold but Xiamen has it locked in tight. JA: The Prince fighting the pain and the referee is asking if he submits but Tasogare no Prince shakes his head. He definitely has a lot of fighting spirit as Xiamen is exerting tremendous pressure on his foot. ER: Look at this! Tasogare no Prince is fighting his way towards the ropes! He’s inching closer... closer... he’s got them! [crowd applauds] Xiamen immediately releases the hold but you’ve got to wonder if the damage has been done. How mobile will Tasogare no Prince be if his foot is in such pain? JA: A good point and one of the greatest advantages of submission holds. Even if the opponent doesn’t give up, they still inflict damage which can give you an advantage. Xiamen now in control as he catches Tasogare no Prince as he stands with a knifehand chop across the chest. ER: Xiamen keeping up a furious assault and... what was that?!? JA: He calls that "The Poke-Box"! It’s Xiamen's version of a nerve strike where he uses two fingers on each hand to quickly jab Tasogare no Prince in the ribs, back, side, stomach, and neck. ER: Another example of Xiamen’s unusual African fighting style and a highly effective one as Tasogare no Prince reels backwards. Xiamen drives him further back into the corner with a spinwheel kick and then picks him up and sets him up on the top turnbuckle. JA: I think he’s going for a Super DDT here as he climbs up in front of Tasogare no Prince... no! The Prince pushes Xiamen off and Xiamen falls backward into the ring, coming down hard onto his back!! ER: His back is a weakness as he’s prone to injuries there. And The Prince is looking a little wobbly as he climbs up onto the top turnbuckle. I think he’s going for a shooting star press. JA: Oh! But Xiamen surges to his feet and dives toward the ropes, shaking them enough to dislodge Tasogare no Prince’s balance. ER: Ouch! The Prince slipped and fell so that he straddled the top turnbuckle!! JA: That’ll hurt and from the expression on his face, it looks like The Prince really felt that! He topples forward into the ring where a winded Xiamen is trying to catch his breath. ER: I don’t think Xiamen was looking for that particular result when he tried to disturb The Prince’s balance. But he pulls him upright and sends him into the ropes... JA: Ouch! What a collision!! Tasogare no Prince came off the ropes with what looked like a back elbow attempt just as Xiamen tried to hit him with body block. But the two men’s heads cracked together and they both go down! ER: That did not look good as both men are down on the mat and neither is moving. JA: The fortunes of war can be fickle and this time, both men suffered badly as their skulls collided. They’re both on their back, neither man fully conscious but Tasogare no Prince’s arm happens to be draped over Xiamen. ER: The referee drops down beside the two and begins counting... ...1... ...2... ...3!!!! **ding** **ding** **ding** Ring Announcer: The winner of this match.... TASOGARE NO PRINCE!!! [crowd applauds] [Grandmaster Xu and several medical technicians swarm into the ring as the camera cuts to the broadcast table.] JA: What a match! Tremendous action on the part of both men and it certainly came down to an unusual ending. ER: Tasogare no Prince surrounds himself with mystery and Xiamen is certainly unorthodox so it’s not surprising that the match ends in an unusual fashion. But at what cost? Both men will be lucky if they walk away from this one without concussions! JA: So no Tasogare no Prince advances to round three, along with Greg Miller. Miller will face the winner of Murakami/Kurasawa, while Prince faces the winner of Kabuki Kid/Jet Jaguar II! ER: Those matches themselves should be awesome. Up next though, we have the team of Kenji Ishida & Shinji Usami. These two men are talented, but have such an attitude since coming under the influence of Shinsaku Yamashita. JA: I'll say! They lost their first title match to the Bonecrushers, but don't count them out yet! __ ___ _____________________________________________________ / |/ /__ / /________ / / /|_/ / -_) __/ __/ _ \ Ishida/Usami vs The Golden Boys /_/ /_/\__/\__/_/ \___/_______________________________________/ Before the match, Shinsaku Yamashita ranted on the mircophone about The Bonecrushers cheating in their title match. He also berated The Tokyo Express for sticking their nose in his business, and challenged both teams to face his men & The Yakuza at the supercard. As soon as the bell rang, Ishida nailed Keith Golden with a thrust kick to the throat, while Usami caught Jerry Golden with an enzugiri. Ishida clotheslined Keith over the ropes to the floor, and then proceeded to beat on Jerry with Usami. They nailed him with a drop toehold/legdrop combo, then Usami punished Jerry with 3 spinning backbreakers in a row. He tagged in Ishida, and they did did the Tokyo Express camel clutch/dropkick to the face combo, mocking the Express as they did it. Ishida then applied the Ishida Agonizier, while Usami dropkicked Keith off the apron. Ishida would release the hold, then reapply it, preventing Jerry from submitting. Finally, the Tokyo Express stormed the ring, and started a brawl with Ishida & Usami. Security rushed to seperate them, and after pulling them apart, Riki Kano carried Jerry to the back for medical attention, the crowd applauding him for his actions. Winners: Ishida & Usami by DQ. ER: This situation continues to heat up. Ishida & Usami provoked the Express by mimicking them! JA: And it worked. Lucky for Jerry Golden too, cause that hold is painful! ER: I wonder if the Bonecrushers & Express will agree to the challenge Yamashita laid down. JA: More importantly, will the Yakuza agree to team with them? ER: Another good question! JA: Speaking of tag teams, we now take you to comments from The Young Warriors, a team that caused quite a stir this weekend, overcoming the team of Kazuya Asakino & Combattler II. ER: I was shocked to see Combattler I unmask as Kazuya, but I don't think Combattler II was Keiji Asamiya. He looked too big. JA: I agree. Lady Mai Tai was quite upset with her team's performance, and rumors that she may fire both of them has been floating around the locker room... ____ _____ _ _____________________________________________ / __/_ / / _ \ | /| / / / _\ \/ // / ___/ |/ |/ / The Young Warriors /___/\___/_/ |__/|__/__________________________________________/ [The scene opens to Huan Feng Sho and Yagyu Retsudo standing in front of a black background with the red "Young Warriors" logo scripted across it. Huan is wearing a pair of tan pants and a plain, white short-sleeved shirt while Retsudo is wearing a pair of blue jeans and a white T-shirt with the SJPW logo on the chest in red. Both men are smiling widely.] Retsudo: First match over! Young Warriors walk away with victory! This is sign of what to come, SJPW! We advance in tournament, and not going to stop until we reach finals. That all there is to it. [He gives a wide grin.] Retsudo: Now people know us. Now noone underestimate us! We paying dues... We fighting some of best teams in world here in SJPW! Is best way to start career! Huan: Team Combattler, we wish to thank you for a good fight. You proved yourselves to be worthy opponents, and you proved my doubts about your willingness to fight fairly to be groundless. We were honored to enter the field of battle against you. Retsudo: HAI! Combattler-san... Asakino-san... You give good as you get. You prove selves in Young Warriors' eyes. Most honored to have fought you... but now we look to future... To next opponents. We not know who this is, but going to find out soon... And when we do... Huan: They are going to find out just what Team Combattler found out... We back down from noone... We refuse no challenge... We _never_ give up. That is the way of the honorable warrior. That is the way of the Young Warriors! [Fade to black] ER: This team caused quite a stir last sunday. The new blood SJPW has brought in has really revitalized this promotion. This team could go very far. JA: I expect they will. I just hope they don't get too cocky. It was only one match after all! ER: Speaking of one match, Shishi "Lion" Fujiwara will never forget his first SJPW bout, as he lost thanks to the distraction of Kagemusha! JA: What makes that so humiliating is that he had just been accepted as a member of Chiimu Tora. That has to weigh heavily on his mind. ER: Fujiwara has claimed he will avenge this affront, and we saw him earlier when Kagemusha wrestled. Now, we get to see Fujiwara himself in the ring... __ ___ _____________________________________________________ / |/ /__ / /________ / / /|_/ / -_) __/ __/ _ \ Shishi Fujiwara vs Haruki Shiomi /_/ /_/\__/\__/_/ \___/_______________________________________/ Fujiwara came out totally ignoring the fans, and an intense look on his face. When the bell rang, he went right for Shiomi, locking up, and forcing him to the ropes. He whipped him across the ring, caught him, and hit an Exploder Suplex. Shiomi landed on his head, and Fujiwara wasted no time in hitting his patented Lionplex, rolled through, hit a face first powerbomb, then hit a second Lionplex. Fujiwara dragged the dazed Shiomi to his feet, double underhooked his arms, and executed the Fujiwara Spike for the win. Winner: Shishi Fujiwara by pinfall. JA: That was a short match! Fujiwara is in a volatile mood. Poor Shiomi never had a chance! ER: Plus he got carried out on a stretcher! That Fujiwara Spike is a dangerous move! JA: No arguement there. I'd hate to be on the recieving end of it, and I'm sure Kagemusha is thinking the same thing!
