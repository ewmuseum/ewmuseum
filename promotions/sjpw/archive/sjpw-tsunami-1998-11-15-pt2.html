Tsunami, Part Two

15 November 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
AO: A lot of guts being shown by the kid as he’s taken a beating to that injured back but he still manages to stay in the match. TK: The Prince not showing any frustration, however, as he is remaining calm while he climbs up to the top turnbuckle once more. AO: He’s being rather methodical about working over Jet Jaguar II’s back and the youngster is powerless to stop him at this point. The Prince is... wait... what’s he doing? TK: I’m not sure... there seems to be an attractive young woman in the front row who has caught his eye! And now the Twilight Prince is reaching into a fold in his leggings... what’s this? AO: It’s a flower! A white orchid! Tasogare no Prince is wasting valuable time as he inhales some of the flower’s fragrance... and then gently tosses it to the girl in the front row! TK: He seems to have a fondness for the ladies but it might cost him... yes! Jet Jaguar II has managed to recover and has rolled to his feet... and he’s caught the Prince up on that top rope! AO: This looks bad for the Prince... Jaguar throws him down to the mat! The Prince spent too much time flirting with that girl and it gave Jaguar just the opening he needed! The youngster bounces off the ropes and the Prince drops down onto his stomach. Jaguar hops over him and races toward the ropes on the opposite side. The Prince hops to his feet as Jaguar bounces back... oh! And Jaguar connects with a flying dropkick that sends The Prince soaring across the ring! TK: Jet Jaguar II fighting through the pain as he’s really making a comeback here. He rushes over and traps the Prince in a front facelock. He’s setting him up... and lifts him up the air for a vertical suplex! And down comes the Prince with tremendous force! AO: The advantage has been traded back a few times in this match so far but it’s Jet Jaguar in control as he slaps on an armbar submission. The Prince is in trouble as Jaguar leans right into that arm with all his weight. The Prince struggling to reach the ropes and he’s almost got them... and he grabs the bottom rope, forcing Jaguar to break the hold. TK: Jaguar slowing down the pace of the match for a moment as he wears the Prince down for a little bit. Both men back on their feet and Jaguar sends The Prince into the ropes with an Irish whip, knocking him down with a shoulderblock on the way back. Jaguar now runs into the ropes and hops over the Prince to rebound off the opposite side... HIGH CROSS BODY BLOCK! AO: But the Prince rolls through with Jaguar’s momentum and ends up on top! Here’s Goto Kyoji with the count: ...1... ...2... ...kickout!!! [crowd cheers] TK: A close call for Jet Jaguar II as the Twilight Prince demonstrates his ring savvy by using the youngster’s momentum against him. The fans are really getting a treat as this match has seen some tremendous moves already. Jaguar is against the ropes, pulling himself upright as the Prince charges toward him. High cross body... oh no! Both men go tumbling over the top rope and down to the outside! AO: Uh oh! That definitely hurt Jet Jaguar II as he came down on the edge of the ring apron on his injured back! And he’s clutching his spine, yelling in pain as Tasogare no Prince struggles to his feet beside him. TK: One of the dangers of a high-flying match like this is that it takes a lot out of both men! AO: Jaguar struggling to pull himself upright and you can tell that his back is really aching badly. The Prince has climbed up on the outside and he’s on the top turnbuckle. TK: This looks really dangerous... CORKSCREW PLANCHA! [crowd "ooohhs"] AO: BUT JAGUAR MOVED! Tasogare no Prince tried for a high-risk maneuver and it backfired on him as he went crashing down from the top rope to land with horrendous velocity on the floor! TK: Both of these men are going to be feeling the effects of this match for some time as they’ve shared equally in the damaging bruises and scrapes that they’ve suffered. It looks like Tasogare no Prince is just about out of it and Jet Jaguar II is in little better shape. Jaguar slowly moving over to the Prince and is having a hard time pulling him to his feet. AO: The Prince is out of it and Jaguar has taken so much punishment that he can barely lift him up. The crowd is cheering him on as he slowly hauls the Prince upright and rolls him back into the ring. Jaguar climbing up on the outside now and the Prince is still down on the canvas. TK: It looks like the youngster is going for another aerial maneuver but that might be a mistake. AO: He’s making the sign for the Jaguar Special! His trademark finisher, learned from the original Jet Jaguar, is a twisting moonsault legdrop and it’s lethal. He flips himself backward off the top rope... NO!! TK: Oh no! The Prince rolled out of the way just in time and Jaguar crashed down onto the canvas with tremendous velocity! AO: Both men have tried high-risk maneuvers and both men have suffered the consequences. It looks like Jet Jaguar II came right down on his tailbone and he’s limping badly as he uses the ropes to pull himself to his feet. The Prince is up and he’s not looking in great shape either as he staggers forward. TK: The Prince with an Irish whip into the ropes, no, reversal by Jaguar. The Prince comes rebounding back... and Jaguar slaps on a sleeperhold! An unusual move for him as his repertoire normally consists of aerial maneuvers but he’s got the sleeper on pretty tightly! AO: The Prince is in trouble as he’s flailing away with his arms. But he’s backpedalling... oh! And he forces Jaguar backward into the corner where he crushes his damaged spine against the turnbuckle! Great ring presence from the Prince as he manages to escape that sleeperhold and go after Jet Jaguar II’s injured back once again. TK: Jaguar is wincing in pain as he steps slowly out of the corner. The Prince ducks down, grabs his legs and lifts him up... spinebuster slam! Jaguar is in absolute agony as Tasogare no Prince has managed to regain the advantage after nearly being knocked out cold. AO: But he’s back to taking chances once again as he’s climbing up in the corner. Jaguar has rolled onto his front and is really feeling the effects of all the pounding his back has taken. The Prince looks remarkably well-balanced on top of the turnbuckle despite all the punishment he’s suffered in this match. TK: He’s lining up Jaguar... leaps off... 450 SPLASH! [crowd "ooohhs"] AO: Right down onto Jaguar’s injured back and I think I heard the youngster scream just then! The Prince rolls him over and is going for the pin: ...1... ...2... ...3!!!! **ding** **ding** **ding** [Soothing cello music begins playing over the P.A. as the crowd cheers loudly.] Nishi: The winner of this contest... TASOGARE NO PRINCE!!!! [crowd applauds again, and streamers fly into the ring] TK: What a spectacular showing by both men. And look! The crowd is giving the two of them a standing ovation! AO: Yes but I don’t think they’re in any condition to appreciate it. Neither of them is able to stand and... wait! Here comes The Banshee! [Down the aisle, The Banshee comes jogging with a concerned expression visible beneath his green-and-white mask.] TK: He looks worried about his tag team partner as Jet Jaguar II is obviously still in a lot of pain. AO: Or perhaps he’s looking to attack Tasogare no Prince? He’s had some things to say about him in the past... TK: Please! This is The Banshee you’re talking about! Chiimu Tora does not do such things. AO: Oh really? Didn’t Shishi Fujiwara cost Kagemusha a match recently by interfering? [The Banshee slides into the ring and a hush grows over the crowd as they’re uncertain as to what he’ll do. He immediately rushes over to the fallen Jet Jaguar II who is being attended to by the referee and some medical personnel.] TK: This does not look good for the youngster. He may have suffered some sort of serious back injury. AO: They’re bringing a gurney down to the ring... and now The Banshee has turned to face Tasogare no Prince! TK: I hope he does not do anything foolish. The shape the Twilight Prince is in, he’s in no condition to defend himself. [The Banshee glares at Tasogare no Prince but makes no move toward him as he turns back to his injured partner and helps lift him onto the gurney.] AO: Well it doesn’t look like there’ll be any further conflict after all but I can’t imagine The Banshee is happy about Tasogare no Prince injuring his partner. TK: Give the youngster credit, he very nearly won this match and the Prince got the victory by a very slim margin indeed. AO: But he advances in the tournament and at Sensou no Jidai, Tasogare no Prince will face off with Yukio Murakami for the Welterweight title! TK: My question is this: Will Jet Jaguar II be able to stand beside his Chiimu Tora members against The Daiymo at Sensou no Jidai? AO: Good question. [The camera shows the medical team, along with Banshee, escort Jet Jaguar II to the back. The crowd applauds as they carry him to the back...] TK: Our next match should be interesting. It is a tag team match in the Frank Marano tournament, featuring native sons White Angel & Soultaker, known as Heaven & Hell, against Los Guererra de Pantera, Pantera Guererra's Uno & Dos. AO: I've heard alot about the Panthers of War. They are supposed to be a very talented tag team. I understand they were trained by TNT & Dynamite, The Detonators! You remember them, don't you? TK: Vividly. Lanther & I lost quite a few matches to them. I also remember you and Takizawa losing to them on many occassions. AO: You had to remind me, didn't you? TK: Someone must keep you honest... AO: Let's go to the ring! ______ ___________________________________ /_ __/__ __ _____ ___ ___ _ (_)Frank Marano Tournament, Round 1/ / / (_-</ // / _ \/ _ `/ ' \/ /Heaven & Hell vs Panthers of War /_/ /___/\_,_/_//_/\_,_/_/_/_/_/________________________________/ [The camera pans to the ring, where Nishi Toju & Owada Botan wait...] Nishi: This next contest is a first round match in the Frank Marano Memorial Tag Team Tournament! [crowd cheers] [Theme from "Mortal Kombat: The Movie" blares on the PA] Nishi: First, at a combined weight of 467 pounds... WHITE AN-GEL & SOUL TA-KER....HEAVEN & HELL!!! [White Angel & Soul Taker come out on the runway to a applause. White Angel wears a white robe, along with a white headwrap. His wrestling attire consists of white tights, with "Heaven" written down the left and "Angel" down the right. Soul Taker appears dressed in a black robe and a black headwrap. When he gets to the ring, he reveals black tights with "Hell" down the left leg and "Taker" down the right. Both men acknowledge the fans as they head to the ring] TK: The fans seem to like this team. AO: I would say so! ["Black Cat" by Janet Jackson blares on the PA] Nishi: Their opponents, hailing from Mexico, and weighing a combined weight of 456 pounds...PAN-TER-A GUER-ER-RA U-NO & PAN-TER-A GUER-ER-RA DOS....LOS GUERERRA DE PANTERA!!! [The Panthers of War come out on the runway, ignoring the fans. They are both wearing a Black mask with a panther-like face in gold on the front of the mask. Wears a full wrestling outfit (ie from shoulders to legs) in black, with silver slashes on the legs (looks like claw marks). Wears gold boots, red elbow and knee pads. Is muscular for his size, but not rock solid; kind of lithe. The thing that tells the two apart is the Roman Numeral 'I' on Unos costume, and the 'II' on Dos' costume] TK: This team looks all business. AO: They didn't come to socialize; they came to fight! [Botan gives last minute instructions, then signals for the bell] TK: The bell sounds, and it looks like it will be The White Angel starting out for Heaven & Hell, and Pantera Guererra Uno for Los Pantera De Guererra. The only way to tell with Pantera is in the ring is my the roman numerals on their tights. AO:If they were more willing to bend the rules, that would be a great a dvantage for them. Just get rid of the numerals on the tights, and the poor referee would have a hard time telling which Pantera was in the ring. Hmm... even _with_ the numbers, they could probably still get away with it. TK: Thank you for that insight into how to break the rules, Akira. Pantera Uno and White Angel starting off cautious, circling each other... just trying to feel each other out... into a collar and elbow tie-up... both men jockeying for position... Uno goes for a hiptoss, but it's blocked by the White Angel. Another attempt by Uno, another block by Angel... Angel down with an attempt of his own... Uno goes over, but lands on his feet. [Crowd pops] Uno now bounces off the ropes... White Angel drops down and Uno goes over him into the far ropes, rebounds off ... leapfrog by The White Angel.... another rebound by Uno... met by a DROPKICK from The White Angel! [another pop from the crowd.] AO: Both men moving at a fast pace early in the match. Pantera Guererra Uno rolls away from White Angel and gets back to his feet, The White Angel wisely not charging in. Uno moves back in on The White Angel... they go for another lock-up... and Angel with a driving knee to the mid-section... goes for a DDT... blocked by Pantera Uno who takes Angel over with a bridging suplex.... 1... one count only. [Both men get back to their feet and immediately lock up again. The two men jockey for position and the crowd pops as Pantera Guererra Uno takes The White Angel over with a textbook Japanese Armdrag. The White Angel quickly kips up and as Pantera Uno moves in, The White Angel ducks down and lunges forward, driving his head into Uno's mid-section, sending him to the mat, gasping for breath. The White Angel rushes to the ropes, tagging in Soul Taker and bouncing off the ropes at the same time, leaping hig h into the air and dropping a hard legdrop across the chest of Pantera Guererra Uno.] TK: Heaven and Hell now beginning to gain the advantage on Los Pantera de Guererra, and Pantera Uno needs to make the tag... if he gets the opportunity. Heaven & Hell pulls Pantera Uno off the mat and send him for the ride.... Uno comes back and is met with a double clothesline. The referee sends The White Angel out of the ring, and that leaves Pantera Uno in there with the "Hell" half of Heaven & Hell. Soul Taker wastes no time in bringing Pantera Uno back to his feet... scoops him up and... BACKBREAKER! [crowd "ooohs"] Pantera Uno holding his lower back, rolling on the mat and Soul Taker drops a knee right across the fallen Pantera's back to add more damage. [Soul Taker stands over Pantera Uno a moment, watching him writhe... thenbrings him back to his feet and throws him into the ropes. Soul Taker goes for a backdrop, but the crowds pops as Pantera manages to land on his feet. Pantera Guererra Uno quickly drives a knee into the back of Soul Taker before he turns around, grabs him in a reverse headlock, and receives another pop from the crowd as he drives Soul Taker to the mat with a REVERSE DDT. ] AO: Great agility shown by Pantera Guererra Uno! He caught Soul Taker completely by surprise, and now if he's smart he'll... and there he goes, making the tag to Pantera Guererra Dos. Pantera Dos climbs the turnbuckle as Soul Taker staggers to his feet and ... MISSLE DROPKICK! Soul Taker just went head over heels backwards, and now it's Heaven & Hell who are in trouble. [Pantera Guererra Dos pulls Soul Taker up to his feet and whips him into the ropes. Dos runs to the opposite ropes and bounces off with a Springboard Flying Headscissors, causing a crowd pop... but Soul Taker quickly converts it into a SPINNING POWERBOMB and the crowd erupts.] TK: Pantera Dos had the right idea, but unfortunately for him, Soul Taker had the presence of mind to counter it. Soul Taker grabs Dos by the ankle and pulls him towards Heaven & Hell's corner, making the tag to The White Angel. Soul Taker now scoops up Pantera Dos while the White Angel climbs the ropes.... RUNNING SHOULDERBREAKER on Pantera Guererra Dos.. .. GUILLOTINE LEGDROP BY WHITE ANGEL! [crowd "ooohs"] AO: Now that's what tag team wrestling is all about. Great double team strategy on the part of Heaven & Hell, and Los Pantera de Guererra are in trouble now. The White Angel now pulling Pantera Dos back towards the corner yet again.... climbs the ropes... 450 SPLASH! ...1... ...2... TK: Pantera Uno breaks the pin! [crowd applauds] The ring official now trying to send Pantera Uno out of the ring, but in comes Soul Taker behind his back. Soul Taker pulls Pantera Dos up, White Angel heads right back up to the top rope and.... BRAINBUSTER BY SOUL TAKER... MOONSAULT BY WHITE ANGEL! They call that The Afterlife... the referee turns around finally as Soul Taker slides out of the ring... ...1... ...2... ...3!!!! **ding** **ding** **ding** Nishi: The winners of this match...HEAVEN & HELL!!! [crowd applauds] AO: So another team advances to the second round. TK: That was an excellant win by Heaven & Hell. This team has much potential.
